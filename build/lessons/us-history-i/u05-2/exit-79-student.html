Source I. The Pacific Railway Act, July 1, 1862, section 3. Public domain (United States government work). 12 Stat. 489.

there be, and is hereby, granted to the said company, for the purpose of aiding in the construction of said railroad and telegraph line, ... every alternate section of public land, designated by odd numbers, to the amount of five alternate sections per mile on each side of said railroad, on the line thereof, and within the limits of ten miles on each side of said road, not sold, reserved, or otherwise disposed of by the United States

Gloss: alternate section, every other square mile; disposed of, already given or sold.
