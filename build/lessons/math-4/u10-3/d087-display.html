Pia says: "Lena has 40 dollars. Omar has 2 times as much. Together they have 80 dollars."

Their working

2 × 40 = 80.

The right working

d is all their money. d = 40 + 2 × 40. 2 × 40 = 80, and 40 + 80 = 120 dollars.

Kim has 4 sheets of 6 stickers and 9 more stickers. Kim wrote only "33".

Their working

33, with no letter and no equation.

The right working

s is all the stickers. s = 4 × 6 + 9. 24 + 9 = 33, so s = 33.

Ed has 10 marbles. He gets 3 bags of 3 marbles. Ed wrote m = 10 + 3 × 3 and got 39.

Their working

10 + 3 = 13, then 13 × 3 = 39.

The right working

multiply first. 3 × 3 = 9 marbles in the bags. 10 + 9 = 19, so m = 19.
