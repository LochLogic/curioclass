Day 88: Show what I know

Teacher page

Day88, unit 10.3, check day
GoalI can show what I know about "times as many" and two-step problems
Standard parts4.OA.A.1 (a) a multiplication equation as a comparison, both ways; 4.OA.A.1 (b) a comparison statement as a multiplication equation; 4.OA.A.2 (c) to solve a comparison word problem, the larger amount unknown; 4.OA.A.2 (d1) to solve a comparison word problem, the smaller amount unknown; 4.OA.A.2 (d2) to solve a comparison word problem, the number of times unknown; 4.OA.A.2 (e) "times as many" (multiplicative comparison) from "more than" (additive comparison); 4.OA.A.3 (f) two-step word problems with whole-number answers, no remainders (this stretch's share); 4.OA.A.3 (g) a two-step problem with an equation with a letter for the unknown; 4.OA.A.3 (h) whether an answer is reasonable, by estimating
ToolNone

Before class

Print everything on the print list. Open the display; show the answers only at the marking. Put strip sheets out for the groups. Cover the comparison frame on the wall.

The day

30 min. End check

Keep the day 87 slips until pencils go down. Alone, unaided: strips may be drawn in the work boxes, no strip sheets, cubes or times table. Read each item aloud once. At 30 minutes, pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

Mark, 5 minutes. Pencils away; hand back the day 87 slips (and any day 86 slips); show the answers to 1 to 6. Students tick or circle each in pen. You judge 7 and 8 after class; students answer the two questions on the F to H screen, and a "no" joins that group.

Groups, 15 minutes. Hands up sets the groups; a student in two joins the earlier one. Each group fixes its items in pen. Tasks are on the display.

  • A and D (13 or 48 on 3, 28 on 4, 24 on 5, an addition on 1 or 2, Lee and Kim alike or swapped, or day 86's slip: melon 20 or squash 96): draw the cat as an 8-strip, the dog as copies under it. Count copies aloud, the first as one. Beside it, draw 5 more than 8.
  • B and C (128 on 4, 81 on 5, roles swapped on 1 or 2, or day 86's slip: melon 96): sort items 3, 4 and 5 by what is unknown. Ask "who has more?" and mark the longer strip first. C: say the bigger amount first and write it alone: 210 = 7 × 30.
  • F to H (7, 8, or a day 87 look-for missed, as error G). Underline the question. Is your answer what it asks? Did your letter say what it stands for? Write the two steps as two equations, then join them, multiplying first.

Students who missed nothing do A white kite string is 9 yards long. A red string is 8 times as long. A blue string is 15 yards shorter than the red one. How long is the blue string? Write one equation with a letter. [b = 8 × 9 − 15; 57 yards], then A tall tower is 8 times as tall as a short tower. It is 63 blocks taller. How tall is each tower? Draw strips. [Short 9 blocks, tall 72 blocks: 7 copies make the 63]. Collect the checks.

10 min. Stretch 11's readiness check

Alone, with grid paper for item 4. Read each item aloud once.

Other rooms

  • No projector. Answers and group tasks on a covered part of the board.
  • One child at home. The defaults, then the readiness check.

Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups ten minutes, never the check, marking or readiness check.

Words and supports

The defaults hold; items 1, 7 and 8 may be answered aloud.

Tomorrow

After class, about 40 minutes: record 1 to 6 from the pencil first try, judge 7 and 8 from the pencil first try, each look-for alone, and mark R1 to R4 on stretch 11's sheet tonight; day 89's plan depends on them.

Item 7:

  1. writes one equation, or two steps joined, with a letter that stands for all the beads (part g)
  2. does both steps: 4 × 20 = 80, then 20 + 80; or 1 + 4 = 5 jars of 20, then 5 × 20 (part f)
  3. finds 100 beads

Item 8:

  1. estimates with easy numbers from the story (about 50, about 20)
  2. compares the estimate with 175 and says why 175 cannot be right
  3. finds s = 67

Day 89 hands 7 and 8 back with the meets answers. Many 80 on 7 (F): there, underline the question and ask "is 80 what it asked?" Many 175 on 8 (H): there, write Ravi's steps as two equations. Many with no estimate on 8: days 91 and 92 start each warm-up sum with "about ". If more than a third missed readiness item 1 or 2, day 90's warm-up is the reteach; otherwise day 89 builds one rectangle.
