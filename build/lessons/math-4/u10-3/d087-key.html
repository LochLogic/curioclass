challenges10-C87-14.OA.A.3 (f, g), MP.4Write a two-step story for n = 6 × 20 − 45. Then find n.A story with 6 groups of 20, or 6 times as many as 20, then 45 taken away; n = 75
Look-fors, each ticked alone:
  1. the story has 6 equal groups of 20, or 6 times as many as 20
  2. 45 is taken away after the 6 × 20 is found
  3. n is named in words and n = 75
Samples (ours, untested):
  • meets: Lu has 20 shells. Max has 6 times as many. Max gives away 45. How many does Max have left? n is Max's shells left, n = 75.
  • partly: 6 bags of 20 apples, and 45 more apples. n = 165. (Look-for 2 missing: 45 is added, not taken away.)
  • not: 6 × 20 = 120, 120 − 45 = 75. (No story and no letter named.)

Look-fors, each ticked alone:
  1. one equation with a letter that stands for the pencils left (part g)
  2. both steps: 3 × 30 = 90, then 158 − 90 = 68 (part f)
  3. an estimate from the story's numbers that lands near 68 (part h)
Samples (ours, untested):

  • partly: 158 − 90 = 68 pencils. 68 is about 70. (Look-for 2 only: no letter, and the estimate comes from the answer, not the story.)
  • not: 3 × 30 = 90 pencils. (One step only, error F.)
