Their working

13, 16 and 19 take turns, so it keeps going. Three more numbers say nothing about the ones not written yet.

The right working

13 is pairs and 1 left over. Add 3: the 1 left over gets a partner from it, and 2 more make a pair. Draw 16 in pairs: none left over. Add 3 again and 1 is left over. Every jump of 3 does this, so odd and even take turns for ever.
