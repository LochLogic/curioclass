Item 4: build step 1 with squares, one on a row of 5, and write 6. Say step 6 in words first, "the first shape and how many jumps?": 6 + 5 × 5 = 31. Item 6: say "times 3" at each jump. Written task: count by threes to 30.
  • C and D (item 2: "It goes up by 11" or "They are all even"; item 6: "They triple"): cover the rule. Write odd or even under every number of item 2 before saying anything, then list what is true of the numbers themselves.

Challenge. Start at any number and add 6. When are all the numbers even? When are they all odd? Can even and odd ever take turns? Why?
