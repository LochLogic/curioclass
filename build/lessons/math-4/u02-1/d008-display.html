    No: 3 × 32 = 96 and 3 × 33 = 99, so 97 falls between them
  3. Is 78 a multiple of 6? Show how you know.
    Yes: 6 × 13 = 78 (6 × 10 = 60, and 3 more sixes are 18)

Jo drew ten squares in a row and counted every stick, one by one. 31 is right, but it took a long time, and one slip spoils it.

The right working

Step 1 has 4 sticks. Each new square shares a side, so it adds 3 sticks. From step 1 to step 10 is 9 jumps. 4 + 9 × 3 = 4 + 27 = 31.
