Day 8: Shape patterns, and the 10th step without drawing: key

Each answer is worked out in full. The last column names the wrong answers to expect and the error each one shows.

A feature is right if it is true of every number and is not the rule. A reason says what one jump does to the feature; more numbers are not a reason.
