Day 9: Show what I know: key

Each answer is worked out in full. The last column names the wrong answers to expect and the error each one shows.

A feature is right if it is true of every number and is not the rule. A reason says what one jump does to the feature; more numbers are not a reason.

challenges02-C9-14.OA.C.5 (c, d), MP.3Start at any number and add 6. When are all the numbers even? When are they all odd? Can even and odd ever take turns? Why?All even when the start is even; all odd when the start is odd. They never take turns: 6 is three pairs, so a jump of 6 never changes odd or even
challenges02-C9-24.OA.C.5 (a, d), MP.7Make a rule whose numbers all end in 3 or 8. Write the start, the rule and six numbers. Why does it keep going?For example start at 8, add 5: 8, 13, 18, 23, 28, 33. Any start ending in 3 or 8 works with an add that ends in 5 (they take turns) or in 0 (they all end the same). Adding a number ending in 5 is a jump of 10 every two jumps, and 10 leaves the ones digit alone
