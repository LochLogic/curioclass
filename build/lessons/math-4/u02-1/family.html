Unit 2 family note: follow the rule, then notice more

English

This week your child follows a number rule, like "start at 1, add 3", and looks for something the rule never said.

What it looks like. Start at 1 and add 3: 1, 4, 7, 10, 13. The rule said nothing about odd and even, but the numbers take turns: odd, even, odd, even. Your child explains why: an odd number is pairs and one left over, and adding 3 adds a pair and one more, so odd and even swap.

Try this at home, five minutes. Give your child a start and a rule, such as "start at 2, add 5". Ask them to write six numbers. Then ask: "What do you notice that I did not say? How do you know it will keep going?" If they give you more numbers as the reason, ask about the ones not written yet.
