Unit 2 record sheet: number patterns

One row a student, for days 6 to 10; print two for a class over 18. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to E (the section opener); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for not reached. R1 to R3: the readiness check, day 6. E7 and E8: the exit tickets, judged after class, one tick a look-for (listed under the table). W8: day 8's written task, judged after class and handed back on day 9, one tick a look-for (listed under the table). End 1 to 6: the end-of-unit assessment, day 9; items 3 and 5 take one tick a look-for, marked after class and answered in day 10's first five minutes. Each head names the parts of the standard it records.
