Stretch 1 record sheet: factors and multiples

One row a student, for days 1 to 7; print two for a class over 17. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to G (the unit openers); s for a slip (a fact slip with the right search); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for not reached. R1 to R3: the readiness items, day 1 (R1 is the number right out of 20). E1 to E5: the exits; on day 2 a tick means all three look-fors, on days 3 and 4 both parts. W3 and W5: the written tasks of days 3 and 5, one tick for each look-for on the row (listed under the table). End 1 to 6: the end check, day 6; items 5 and 6 take one tick for each look-for, marked after class and handed back on day 7. Each head names the standard's part it records.
