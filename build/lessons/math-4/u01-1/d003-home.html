Day 3: Factor pairs with no tiles

At home

GoalI can find all the factor pairs of any number to 100 with no tiles, even pairs that are not in my times tables
Time34 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: read the examples from side 1. The 9 by 9 table: print one, or write out the times table the child asks for.

Skip. The facts check, 90, the practice sheet and the challenge. No coins from today.

  • 10 min. Two examples. Example (side 1) together, writing the list as it grows. Test 3 and 4 from ten times: 3 × 10 = 30, 18 more is 3 × 6, so 3 × 16. Then Then (side 1).
  • 8 min. Every shape: 72. Alone, in the 72 column of the side 1 table: "fits, with " or "no" for each row from 1, the turn marked, then the pairs. Check: 1 × 72, 2 × 36, 3 × 24, 4 × 18, 6 × 12, 8 × 9.

  • 5 min. Fix it. Fix it 1 (side 2).

    Say. "How can 3 be a factor of 42 when 42 is not in the 3 times table?" Expect: 3 × 14 = 42; 3 × 10 is 30, then 12 more.

  • 6 min. When to stop. Write 2 (side 2), alone. The child may say it while you write it down.

  • 5 min. Exit ticket. The child answers ticket question 1 and question 2 alone.

Write 2 (side 2)Write how you know when to stop looking for factor pairs of 64.Try 1, 2, 3 and on, in order. At 8, 8 × 8 = 64. Any number after 8 goes with a number under 8, which was already tried, so stop at 8
