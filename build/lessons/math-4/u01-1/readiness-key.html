Ready for factor pairs: key

These three items belong in the start-of-year check. Until that check is written, this sheet is day 1's fallback: it takes 15 of the first 30 minutes, and the other 15 go to the work by hand (the day 1 teacher page). Alone, on paper, with grid paper for item 3. Read each item aloud once; item 1 may be read as a list. Mark after class, before day 2, and write each result on the unit 1 record sheet (R1 to R3): a tick, x for a wrong answer, or the error letter where the key gives one. Item 1 gets the number right out of 20.

"Many" means more than a third of the class. Day 2's first five minutes after the warm-up are the reteach slot:

  • Item 1, facts (both sections). No reteach slot. It is the first reading for the facts checks on days 3 and 4; note the facts each student missed. A student who missed many keeps a 9 by 9 table out on days 3 and 4.
  • Item 2, division as a missing factor. The sign: 24 × 3 = ? (72), 21, or 24 ÷ 3 = 8 with no multiplication. Reteach on day 2: three rows of 8 dots, read as 3 × 8 = 24 and 24 ÷ 3 = 8 (section 1.1 opener). Whole class if many missed it; otherwise a small group in the warm-up.
  • Item 3, every rectangle for an area. The signs: only 3 by 4 (A), a turned copy drawn as a new rectangle (C), or rectangles with perimeter 12. Day 1 builds rectangles with tiles before this is marked, so read item 3 beside E1: a student who missed item 3 but got E1 right needs nothing more. Reteach on day 2 only for students who missed both, the same way as item 2.

When no whole-class reteach is needed, the five minutes answer E1. If both items need one, both share the five minutes and E1's errors are taken inside day 2's worked search.
