Day 2: Search in order: key

Each answer is worked out in full. The last column names the wrong answers to expect and the error each one shows.

Any equal form of an equation is right: the two factors in either order, either side of the equals sign, any letter, or the matching division (n × 9 = 36, 9 × n = 36, 36 = 9 × n and 36 ÷ 9 = n all say the same).

After 5, neither 6 nor 7 works, and 8 × 8 = 64 is already more than 50, so any new pair would be one of these turned. Also right: "the next number that works is 10, and I have it."
Look-fors, each ticked alone:
  1. lists 1 × 50, 2 × 25 and 5 × 10, with no turned copies
  2. says the numbers were tried in order
  3. says why it stops: the next pair would be one already found, or 8 × 8 is more than 50
