Mia says: "The factor pairs of 20 are 2 × 10 and 4 × 5. Done."

Their working

2 × 10 and 4 × 5, facts she knew, in no order. Done.

The right working

Try the rows in order from 1. 1 row: 1 × 20, fits. 2 rows: 2 × 10, fits. 3 rows: 3 rows of 6 is 18, 2 left, no. 4 rows: 4 × 5, fits. 5 rows: 5 × 4 is 4 × 5 turned, so stop. Mia missed 1 × 20.

Kai says: "28 has five factor pairs: 1 × 28, 2 × 14, 4 × 7, 7 × 4, 14 × 2."

Their working

Five pairs. He kept going after 4 × 7 and counted 7 × 4 and 14 × 2 as new.

The right working

7 × 4 is 4 × 7 turned: the same rectangle, standing up. 14 × 2 is 2 × 14 turned. Cross both out. 6 × 6 = 36 is past 28, so the search stops at 5. 28 has 3 factor pairs.
