Mia says: "The factor pairs of 20 are 2 × 10 and 4 × 5. Done."

Their working

2 × 10 and 4 × 5, facts she knew, in no order. Done.

The right working

try the rows in order from 1. 1 row: 1 × 20, fits. 2 rows: 2 × 10, fits. 3 rows: 3 rows of 6 is 18, 2 left, no. 4 rows: 4 × 5, fits. 5 rows: 5 × 4 is 4 × 5 turned, so stop. Mia missed 1 × 20.

Kai says: "36 has seven factor pairs: 1 × 36, 2 × 18, 3 × 12, 4 × 9, 6 × 6, 9 × 4, 12 × 3."

Their working

seven pairs. He kept going after 6 × 6 and counted 9 × 4 and 12 × 3 as new.

The right working

9 × 4 is 4 × 9 turned: the same rectangle, standing up. 12 × 3 is 3 × 12 turned. Cross both out. After 6 × 6, every fit is a pair already found. 36 has 5 factor pairs.
