Facts check, part 1: products: key

The 36 products of the facts from 2 × 2 to 9 × 9, each pair once (stretch 1, s01.md). Given in a warm-up on paper: part 1 on day 3 and again on day 89, part 2 on day 4 and again on day 90. No clock is shown. Collect at 5 minutes; an item not reached is left blank, not marked wrong. The mark (ours, untested): 32 of 36. Students under 32 keep a 9 by 9 table out for that day's lesson. Fact help goes into the warm-ups, not the lessons.
