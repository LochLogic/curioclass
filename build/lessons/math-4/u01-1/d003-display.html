Rosa says the factor pairs of 42 are 1 × 42, 2 × 21 and 6 × 7. She says she is done.

Their working

1 × 42, 2 × 21, 6 × 7. She skipped 3, because 42 is not in her 3 times table.

The right working

Test 3 from ten times. 3 × 10 = 30, and 12 more is 3 × 4, so 3 × 14 = 42. 4 and 5 do not fit. 6 × 7 = 42. 7 goes with 6, so stop: 1 × 42, 2 × 21, 3 × 14, 6 × 7.
