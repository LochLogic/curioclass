Rae says: "912 is more than 56, because 9 and 12 are bigger."

Their working

9 is more than 5 and 12 is more than 6, so 9/12 > 5/6.

The right working

12 = 6 × 2, so cut each sixth into 2. 5/6 = 10/12, and 10/12 > 9/12, so 5/6 > 9/12.

Ivy says: "45 = 410, so 45 < 710."

Their working

4/5 = 4/10, and 4/10 < 7/10, so 4/5 < 7/10.

The right working

10 = 5 × 2, so cut each fifth into 2. The 4 fifths become 8 tenths: 4/5 = 8/10. 8/10 > 7/10, so 4/5 > 7/10.
