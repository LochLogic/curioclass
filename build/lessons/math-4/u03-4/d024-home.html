Day 24: Show what I know

At home

GoalI can show what I know and fix what I missed
Time45 minutes, one parent and one child (an assessment day)
Printthe student page; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: mark from the key. No strip envelope: fold paper strips.

Skip. The groups, the challenges, and unit 4's readiness check (give it the next morning).

  • 30 min. End-of-unit assessment. Items 1 to 7, alone, with no strips. Read each item aloud once. Not reached is not wrong.

  • 15 min. Go over it, one to one. Mark items 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 together from the key; judge 2 and 5 from its look-fors. Fix each missed one in pen beside the first try: item 1 (side 1) by folding a thirds strip into sixths and counting the parts from 0; item 4 (side 1) by asking how many parts each part was cut into, top and bottom alike.

    Say. For item 7 (side 2): "Read each answer aloud as a sentence. Does the open side face the greater fraction?" Expect: 5/6 is greater than 7/10; 3/10 is less than 1/3; 2/6 equals 4/12.
