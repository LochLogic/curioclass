Their working

She looked for one number that turns the fourths into sixths. Nothing times 4 is 6, so she said they are not equal.

The right working

Give each one a name with smaller numbers. Group the fourths in twos: 6/4 = (6 ÷ 2)/(4 ÷ 2) = 3/2. Group the sixths in threes: 9/6 = (9 ÷ 3)/(6 ÷ 3) = 3/2. Both are 3/2, the same name, so 6/4 = 9/6. On a line from 0 to 2 both are at one point, halfway from 1 to 2.
