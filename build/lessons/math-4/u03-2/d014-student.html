  1. The strip is cut in tenths. 4 tenths are shaded. Group the tenths in twos. Write the new name.

    ■ ■ ■ ■

    4/10 = (4 ÷ )/(10 ÷ ) = /

  2. The strip is cut in eighths. 6 eighths are shaded. Group the eighths in twos. Write the new name.

    ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■

    6/8 = (6 ÷ )/(8 ÷ ) = /

  3. 40 hundredths are shaded. Group the hundredths in tens. Then group the tenths to make fifths. Write each name. Each box is 10 hundredths.
