Day 18: Closer to 1

At home

GoalI can use 1 to compare, and tell which fraction is closer to 1
Time35 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: point at side 1.

Skip. The warm-up, Each side of 1 questions 2 and 4, One part short 4, and the challenge.

  • 8 min. Two examples. Read Example 1 (side 1) together, then Example 2 (side 1), on the printed lines; the child fills the frames.

    Say. "Which one is missing the bigger part, 4/5 or 5/6?" Expect: 4/5; a fifth is bigger than a sixth.

  • 8 min. Fix it. Fix it 1 (side 2), then 2. The child writes what went wrong.

    Say. "5/6 and 7/8 are each one part short of 1. Are they equal?" Expect: no; an eighth is smaller, so 7/8 is closer to 1.

  • 7 min. Each side of 1. Each side of 1, question 1 (side 1) and question 3, then One part short, or one part more 3 (side 2).

  • 7 min. Dee's claim. Dee's claim (side 2): the child marks both and writes why.
  • 5 min. Exit ticket. The child answers the ticket question alone.

The questions

Example 1 (side 1)Mark 56 and 65 on the line from 0 to 2. Write >, = or <: 56 __ 65.<: 5/6 is less than 1 and 6/5 is more than 1
  1. The top line is in sixths: 1 is 6 sixths. The lower line is in fifths: 1 is 5 fifths.
  2. 5/6 is 5 of the 6 parts from 0 to 1. It stops one sixth before 1.
  3. 6/5 is 5 fifths, which make 1, and 1 fifth more. It is past 1.
  4. 5/6 is left of 1 and 6/5 is right of 1. So 5/6 < 1 and 1 < 6/5.
  5. So 5/6 < 6/5. The top number tells us: less than the bottom number, less than 1; more than the bottom number, more than 1.
Example 2 (side 1)45 and 56 are each one part short of 1. Which is closer to 1? Write 45 __ 56.5/6, because a sixth is smaller than a fifth; <
  1. 4/5 is on the fifths line, one fifth short of 1. 5/6 is on the sixths line, one sixth short of 1.
  2. Look at the gap from each point up to 1: a fifth for 4/5, a sixth for 5/6.
  3. A sixth is smaller than a fifth: the whole is cut into more parts.
  4. 5/6 is missing the smaller part, so it is closer to 1.
  5. So 4/5 < 5/6.
