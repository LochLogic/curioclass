half of what?

Rio says: "Half a large pizza is more food than half a small pizza. So this 12 is greater than that 12."

Their working

The large half is more food, so 1/2 > 1/2.

The right working

Half of what? Each half is 1/2 of its own pizza, so as numbers 1/2 = 1/2. The large half is more food because the large pizza is the bigger whole. Two fractions compare only when they are of the same whole.

Kim says: "410 is more than 12, because 4 and 10 are bigger."

Their working

4 is more than 1 and 10 is more than 2, so 4/10 > 1/2.

The right working

On the line, 1/2 is also 5/10. 4/10 is one tenth left of 5/10. So 4/10 < 1/2.
