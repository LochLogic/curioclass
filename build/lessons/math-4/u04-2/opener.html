Section 4.2: Joining and separating like parts (days 29 to 31)

The math, for the adult

Standard 4.NF.B.3a: "Understand addition and subtraction of fractions as joining and separating parts referring to the same whole." Part 3d: "Solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions referring to the same whole and having like denominators." This section is the first adding and subtracting, under and past 1, with no mixed numbers yet.

Joining keeps the part size. 2/8 + 3/8 joins a count of two eighths and a count of three eighths: five eighths. The tops add because they are counts; the bottom stays because it is the size of each part. On a line in eighths this is a hop of two parts, then a hop of three: the student draws the hops, says the sum, then writes it.

Separating takes a count away. 11/8 − 4/8 is eleven eighths with four taken away: seven eighths, as hops back on the line. Adding and subtracting undo each other, so every difference is checked by adding: 7/8 + 4/8 = 11/8.

Only parts of one whole. Half a small cup and half a big cup are not "one cup": the halves are different sizes. Two strips of different lengths, each in fourths, laid together show it. Fractions are added only when they are parts of the same whole, and every story in the unit says what the whole is.

Stories. On day 31 each story is read twice and a tape is drawn before any equation: a long bar for the whole, pieces for the parts, a box for the unknown. A "how much farther" story is drawn before it is solved.

Limits. Like denominators only. A sum past 1 may stay one fraction or be written as 1 and a part; both are right. No mixed-number subtraction yet (section 4.3).

The line and the tape

  • Day 29. A line in the day's part size; hops drawn first, then the equation. The day the line is tied to adding.
  • Day 30. Hops back for subtracting, each checked by adding. Two paper strips of different lengths for the same-whole talk. Recipe Scaler's mug and pan rounds (light, show why, 5 min) where the class has devices; the two strips carry it otherwise.
  • Day 31. Stories: the tape diagram enters, drawn by the student. The line is drawn by hand from today.

Use the number line pages from section 4.1 (lines 1 to 6, from 0 to 2).

Why this model

A hop is one count of one part size, so the line shows why the bottom number does not change. Hops back make subtracting the same picture run the other way, which is why a difference is checked by adding. The tape shows a story's action (join, separate, compare) before any number is chosen, so the operation comes from the story, not from a word in it. Recipe Scaler (spec only) shows two unequal halves side by side after the student commits; that is light help for day 30 only.

The errors to expect, in plain words

What students do What it shows What to do when many do it
A Add the tops and add the bottoms: 2/8 + 3/8 = 5/16 A fraction read as two whole numbers Say the sum in words; hop it on one line
E Add parts of different wholes: "1/4 of a short ribbon and 2/4 of a long ribbon make 3/4" A fraction read as an amount of stuff, not a part of one whole Lay the two strips together; ask "fourths of what?"
H Use the story's numbers without reading what is asked The operation picked from a word or from habit Draw the tape first, box the unknown, then write the equation

Wrong examples: A again on day 29, E on day 30. H is shown in full on day 36; here a story is drawn before it is solved.

Supplies for the section

  • Per student: the number line pages; two paper strips of different lengths (day 30); a pencil.
  • Per class: the projector for the display pages; a board line in eighths.
  • A room with nothing: lines ruled by hand; two strips cut from scrap paper.

Words

Join, separate, difference, the same whole. Frame, to take or leave: " parts of size and parts of size make parts of size ." Stories on day 31 are read aloud first.

How the unit is checked

Exits on days 29 to 31, on paper; day 30's written task, judged with look-fors and handed back on day 31; items 4.2a and 4.2b on the end-of-unit assessment, day 38. All of it goes on the unit's record sheet (u04-1/record.md).
