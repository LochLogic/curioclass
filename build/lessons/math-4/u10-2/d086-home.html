Day 86: Which kind of comparison?

At home

GoalI can decide what kind of comparison a problem is, then solve it
Time33 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: point at side 1.

Skip. The warm-up, sort cards 4 and 6, solving cards 4 and 5, and the two-stories task.

  • 6 min. Two that look alike. Example 1 (side 1), then 2. Ask "Copies or extra?" before any working.

    Say. "Both use 8 and 6. Why are the answers so different?" Expect: 6 copies of 8 is 48; 8 and 6 extra is 14.

  • 10 min. Sort the cards. Read aloud Sort cards 1 (side 1), 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8. For each, the child says who has more, the kind, and what the strip would look like. No solving yet. Then read the kinds from the key.

    Say. On card 7: "Does it ask how many more, or how many times?" Expect: how many more, so "more than".

  • 10 min. Solve two. Cards 3 and 7, each with a letter equation and a sentence. The child rereads the story with the answer in it.

  • 7 min. Exit ticket. The child answers the ticket question alone.

The questions

The child works on the printed page; the answers are for you.

WhereQuestionAnswer
Example 1 (side 1)Nora has 8 marbles. Owen has 6 times as many marbles as Nora. How many marbles does Owen have?Times as many; o = 6 × 8; Owen has 48 marbles
  1. Read it. Who has more? Owen.
  2. Copies or extra? 6 times as many means 6 copies of Nora's 8.
  3. Stuck? Draw an 8-strip for Nora and 6 copies of it for Owen. Put o under Owen's strips.
  4. Equation: o = 6 × 8.
  5. o = 48. Owen has 48 marbles.
  6. Reread: is 48 six copies of 8? 6 × 8 = 48. Yes.
Example 2 (side 1)Nora has 8 marbles. Owen has 6 more marbles than Nora. How many marbles does Owen have?More than; o = 8 + 6; Owen has 14 marbles
  1. Read it. Who has more? Owen.
  2. Copies or extra? 6 more than means Nora's 8 and 6 extra.
  3. Stuck? Draw an 8-strip for Nora. For Owen, the same 8-strip and a short piece of 6. Put o under Owen's strip.
  4. Equation: o = 8 + 6.
  5. o = 14. Owen has 14 marbles.
  6. Reread: is 14 six more than 8? Yes.
Sort cards 1 (side 1)Leo has 5 stickers. Mia has 7 times as many stickers as Leo. How many does Mia have?Times as many; m = 7 × 5; 35 stickers
Sort cards 2 (side 1)A rope is 9 feet long. A hose is 7 feet longer than the rope. How long is the hose?More than; h = 9 + 7; 16 feet
Sort cards 3 (side 1)A big box holds 36 crayons. That is 4 times as many as a small box holds. How many does the small box hold?Times as many; 4 × s = 36, or s = 36 ÷ 4; 9 crayons
Sort cards 5 (side 1)Ivy read 43 pages. That is 9 more pages than Sam read. How many pages did Sam read?More than; s + 9 = 43, or s = 43 − 9; 34 pages
Sort cards 7 (side 1)Tia has 49 beads. Max has 7 beads. How many more beads does Tia have than Max?More than; 7 + b = 49, or b = 49 − 7; 42 beads
Sort cards 8 (side 1)Kim picked 45 apples. Gus picked 5 apples. Kim picked how many times as many apples as Gus?Times as many; n × 5 = 45, or n = 45 ÷ 5; 9 times
the ticket questionA pumpkin weighs 24 pounds. That is 4 times as much as a melon weighs. A squash weighs 4 pounds more than the pumpkin. How much does the melon weigh? How much does the squash weigh?Melon 6 pounds; squash 28 pounds
