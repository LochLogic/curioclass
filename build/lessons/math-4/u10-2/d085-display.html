  1. Rosa has 8 marbles. Teo has 7 times as many marbles as Rosa. How many marbles does Teo have?
    t = 7 × 8; 56 marbles
  2. Pia has 42 stickers. That is 6 times as many stickers as Sam has. How many stickers does Sam have?
    6 × s = 42, or s = 42 ÷ 6; 7 stickers
  3. A tall jar holds 48 buttons. A small jar holds 8 buttons. The tall jar holds how many times as many buttons as the small jar?
    n × 8 = 48, or n = 48 ÷ 8; 6 times
  4. A whale is 72 feet long. That is 9 times as long as a dolphin. How long is the dolphin?
    9 × d = 72, or d = 72 ÷ 9; 8 feet
  5. A puppy weighs 9 pounds. A big dog weighs 10 times as much as the puppy. How much does the big dog weigh?
    d = 10 × 9; 90 pounds
  6. A tower is 45 feet tall. A shed is 9 feet tall. The tower is how many times as tall as the shed?
    n × 9 = 45, or n = 45 ÷ 9; 5 times

Ann has 24 pens. Raj has 3 pens. Ali says: "Raj has 8 times as many pens as Ann."

Their working

Ali's sentence, drawn: Raj would have 8 copies of Ann's 24.

The right working

Ann has more, so Ann's strip is the long one, 8 copies of Raj's 3. Ann has 8 times as many pens as Raj. 24 = 8 × 3.
