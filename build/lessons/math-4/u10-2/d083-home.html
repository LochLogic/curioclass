Day 83: More than, or times as many?

At home

GoalI can tell "times as many" from "more than" and draw each
Time35 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: point at side 1. No grid paper: the strip sheet, or squares drawn on plain paper.

Skip. The warm-up, Bo's row for 2, the talk about "times more", stories 3 and 4, and the challenge.

  • 8 min. One story, two ways. Set out 6 beans for Ada. Example 1 (side 1): add 2 more. Example 2 (side 1): make 2 copies of the 6. The child draws both on side 1.

    Say. "In which story does the 2 tell how many copies?" Expect: the times story; in the other, 2 is a short piece on the end.

  • 8 min. The Bo table. On side 1, the rows for 1, 4 and 10 stamps: both answers in each row. Expect: 4 and 3; 7 and 12; 13 and 30.

    Say. "If Bo had 10, which gives more? If Bo had 1?" Expect: at 10, times as many; at 1, 3 more.

  • 9 min. Two stories. Both ways 1 (side 2) and 2: all three strips first, then the numbers.

  • 5 min. Fix it. Fix it (side 2): the 10 is how many copies, not a piece to add.
  • 5 min. Exit ticket. The child answers the ticket question alone.

The questions

The child works on the printed page; the answers are for you.

WhereQuestionAnswer
Example 1 (side 1)Ada has 6 shells. Eli has 2 more shells than Ada. How many shells does Eli have?6 + 2 = 8; 8 shells
  1. Draw Ada's strip: 6 shells long.
  2. Draw Eli's strip under it, starting at the same place. Eli has all that Ada has.
  3. Add a short piece to Eli's strip for the 2 more. That piece sticks out.
  4. 6 + 2 = 8. Eli has 8 shells.
  5. Say it: 8 is 2 more than 6.
Example 2 (side 1)Ada has 6 shells. Eli has 2 times as many shells as Ada. How many shells does Eli have?2 × 6 = 12; 12 shells
  1. Draw Ada's strip again: 6 shells long.
  2. Eli's strip is 2 copies of Ada's strip, end to end. Count the copies: one, two.
  3. The 2 tells how many copies, not how many extra shells.
  4. 2 × 6 = 12. Eli has 12 shells.
  5. Say it: 12 is 2 times as many as 6.
Both ways 1 (side 2)Kit has 8 marbles. Rue has 3 more marbles than Kit. Sol has 3 times as many marbles as Kit. How many do Rue and Sol each have?Rue 11 (8 + 3); Sol 24 (3 × 8)
Both ways 2 (side 2)Max has 9 cards. Nia has 5 more cards than Max. Oz has 5 times as many cards as Max. How many do Nia and Oz each have?Nia 14 (9 + 5); Oz 45 (5 × 9)
Fix it (side 2)Bo has 4 stamps. Cy has 10 times as many stamps as Bo. Lu says Cy has 14.10 × 4 = 40; Cy has 40 stamps. Lu added
