Both ways. Draw each story on your strip sheet, one strip for each one. Then write both answers.

  1. Kit has 8 marbles. Rue has 3 more marbles than Kit. Sol has 3 times as many marbles as Kit. How many do Rue and Sol each have?

    Rue has marbles. Sol has marbles.

  2. Max has 9 cards. Nia has 5 more cards than Max. Oz has 5 times as many cards as Max. How many do Nia and Oz each have?

    Nia has cards. Oz has cards.

  3. A green ribbon is 6 inches long. A red ribbon is 7 inches longer than the green one. A blue ribbon is 7 times as long as the green one. How long are the red and blue ribbons?

    The red ribbon is inches. The blue ribbon is inches.

  4. Ida has 70 buttons. Jo has 2 more buttons than Ida. Kai has 2 times as many buttons as Ida. How many do Jo and Kai each have?

    Jo has buttons. Kai has buttons.
