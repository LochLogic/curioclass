Lu says: "Jo has 12 shells. That is 2 times as many as Max has. So Max has 24 shells."

Their working

12 × 2 = 24.

The right working

Who has more? Jo. So Jo's strip is the longer one: 2 copies of Max's strip, m. 2 × m = 12, and 2 × 6 = 12, so Max has 6 shells. Max cannot have 24: that is more than Jo.
