Day 86: Which kind of comparison?

Name ______________________ Date __________

I can decide what kind of comparison a problem is, then solve it.

Example 1. Nora has 8 marbles. Owen has 6 times as many marbles as Nora. How many marbles does Owen have?

Copies or extra? Equation: Owen has marbles.

Example 2. Nora has 8 marbles. Owen has 6 more marbles than Nora. How many marbles does Owen have?

Copies or extra? Equation: Owen has marbles.

Sort cards. Who has more? Copies or extra? Stuck? Use your strip sheet.

  1. Leo has 5 stickers. Mia has 7 times as many stickers as Leo. How many does Mia have?
  2. A rope is 9 feet long. A hose is 7 feet longer than the rope. How long is the hose?
  3. A big box holds 36 crayons. That is 4 times as many as a small box holds. How many does the small box hold?
  4. A dog weighs 56 pounds. A cat weighs 7 pounds. The dog weighs how many times as much as the cat?
  5. Ivy read 43 pages. That is 9 more pages than Sam read. How many pages did Sam read?
  6. A short ribbon is 8 inches long. A long ribbon is 9 times as long. How long is the long ribbon?
  7. Tia has 49 beads. Max has 7 beads. How many more beads does Tia have than Max?
  8. Kim picked 45 apples. Gus picked 5 apples. Kim picked how many times as many apples as Gus?
