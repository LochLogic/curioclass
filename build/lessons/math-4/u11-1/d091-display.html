Lia says: "A hall floor is 60 feet long and 9 feet wide. Its area is 138 square feet."

Their working

60 + 9 + 60 + 9 = 138. That is the edge, the perimeter.

The right working

the area covers the floor. 60 × 9 = 540, so the area is 540 square feet.
