Ava says: "A rug is 6 feet long and 5 feet wide. Its area is 22 square feet."

Their working

6 + 5 + 6 + 5 = 22.

The right working

area covers the inside, rows times columns. A = l × w, so 6 × 5 = 30 square feet. Her 22 is the edge: the perimeter, in feet.

Ben says: "A card is 10 inches long and 7 inches wide. Its perimeter is 17 inches."

Their working

10 + 7 = 17.

The right working

the edge goes all the way around, along four sides. 10 + 7 + 10 + 7 = 34 inches, or 2 × (10 + 7) = 34. His 17 is only halfway around.

Is 2 × (l + w) always the same as l + w + l + w? How do you know?
