Day 91: the generator sheet

Make and print it: https://curioclass.org/closet/worksheets/make/measure-shapes/?grade=4&skill=formulas&count=12&fixes=off. Each press of New sheet gives a fresh sheet and its key; print one sheet per student and the key for you.

What it holds

Twelve problems in words, with no drawings, and no fixes: day 91 runs its own five fixes first. The twelve mix both formulas: an area from two sides, two areas with a side that is a multiple of 10, a missing side from an area, a missing side from a perimeter, a perimeter with two- and three-digit sides, and a perimeter with fraction sides (like denominators). The order is shuffled, so the words have to say which measure is asked for. The sheet says 18 minutes on its head.

The generator never uses a fact that the unit's lessons, exits or checks use: two sides with their area or their perimeter, in any order. So the sheet cannot hand students an answer they will meet on the end check. Each story thing comes in units and sizes it really has.

Making it

Open the link above, press New sheet once, and print one copy per student and the key for yourself. Every student gets the same sheet, so pairs can compare. The sheet code printed in its foot (five letters) reprints exactly the same sheet; write it on the day 91 teacher page if you want it again next year.

Using it on day 91

Alone for 18 minutes, then 4 minutes in pairs checking against each other; where partners disagree, both redo the problem. Then put the key up. Most students finish 8 to 10; the pairs check what is done.

Without a printer or a device

Write these six on the board; students work them in their notebooks. None uses a fact from the unit's lessons or checks (they are rows in the plan, so the gate checks them). The answers are in brackets.

  1. A patio is 11 yards long and 6 yards wide. How much ground does it cover? [11 × 6 = 66; 66 square yards]
  2. A poster covers 84 square inches. It is 7 inches wide. How long is it? [7 × ? = 84; 12 inches]
  3. A goat pen uses 46 feet of fence. It is 15 feet long. How wide is it? [15 + ? + 15 + ? = 46; 8 feet]
  4. A field is 236 meters long and 118 meters wide. How far is it around the edge? [236 + 118 = 354; 354 + 354 = 708; 708 meters]
  5. A card is 4 1/8 inches long and 2 5/8 inches wide. Find its perimeter. [13 4/8 inches; 12 12/8 is also right]
  6. A garden is 40 yards long and 8 yards wide. How much ground does it cover? [40 × 8 = 320; 320 square yards]
