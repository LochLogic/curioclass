Day 91: the generator sheet

Make and print it: https://curioclass.org/closet/worksheets/make/measure-shapes/?grade=4&skill=formulas&count=12. Each press of New sheet gives a fresh sheet and its key; print one sheet per student and the key for you.

What it holds

Twelve problems in words, with no drawings, then five wrong answers to fix, one for each error A to E. The twelve mix both formulas: an area from two sides, an area with a side that is a multiple of 10, a missing side from an area, a missing side from a perimeter, a perimeter with two- and three-digit sides, a perimeter with fraction sides (like denominators), and a side that is "times as long" as the other. The order is shuffled, so the words have to say which measure is asked for.

The generator never uses a pair of numbers that the unit's lessons, exits or checks use, so the sheet cannot hand students an answer they will meet on the end check.

Making it

Open the link above, press New sheet once, and print one copy per student and the key for yourself. Every student gets the same sheet, so pairs can compare. The sheet code printed in its foot (five letters) reprints exactly the same sheet; write it on the day 91 teacher page if you want it again next year.

Using it on day 91

Alone for 18 minutes, then 4 minutes in pairs checking against each other; where partners disagree, both redo the problem. Then put the key up. The fixes at the end are the same five errors the lesson just named, so a student who gets a fix wrong is showing which error to watch on the exit.

Without a printer or a device

Write these six on the board; students work them in their notebooks. None uses the unit's lesson or check numbers.

  1. A patio is 8 yards long and 6 yards wide. How much ground does it cover? (48 square yards)
  2. A poster covers 54 square inches. It is 6 inches wide. How long is it? (9 inches)
  3. A pen uses 46 feet of fence. It is 15 feet long. How wide is it? (8 feet)
  4. A field is 236 meters long and 118 meters wide. How far is it around the edge? (708 meters)
  5. A card is 3 2/6 inches long and 2 1/6 inches wide. Find its perimeter. (11 inches, or 10 6/6)
  6. A garden bed is 4 feet wide and 2 times as long as it is wide. Find its area. (32 square feet)
