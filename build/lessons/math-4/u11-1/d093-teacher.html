Creditours

Before class

Print the end check both sides, the key, the record sheet and stretch 12's readiness check. Write the answers to items 1 to 4 and 7 on a covered part of the board, or ready them on the projector. Put grid paper and string out for the groups.

The day

30 min. End check

Alone, unaided: no grid paper, tiles or times table. Read each item aloud once. Early finishers check each unit. At 30 minutes, pencils down. An item not reached is recorded as not reached, not wrong.

20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

Mark, 5 minutes. Pencils away; uncover the answers to 1 to 4 and 7. Students tick or circle each in pen. Items 5 and 6 come back tomorrow.

Groups, 15 minutes. Hands up by item sets the groups. A student who missed several joins the group for the first one missed. Each group fixes its item in pen beside the first try, then checks it in the equation.

  • Item 1 (error C): rows of 4 on grid paper until the 28 squares are used.
  • Item 2 (error D): fill in 16 + + 16 + = 50 together.
  • Items 3 and 4 (error B): trace and name all four sides, then write l + w + l + w. A right setup with a wrong sum is an adding slip: redo the sum only.
  • Item 7 (error A or E): sketch it, shade the inside, trace the edge in another colour. Area is the shaded part, in square centimeters.

Students who missed nothing do the challenge on the board (A rectangle has an area of 60 square feet and a perimeter of 32 feet. How long are its sides? [6 feet and 10 feet]). Collect the checks; record each keyed item as right or its error letter on the record sheet.

10 min. Next: stretch 12's readiness check

Alone, written, collected for marking before day 94. Stretch 12 is frozen and its check may not exist yet. If not, the groups keep fixing for these ten minutes, and the readiness check moves to stretch 12's first day.

Other rooms

  • No projector. The covered board does the job.
  • One child at home. Give the check, then go over each missed item together with paper squares or string.

Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the groups to ten minutes, never the check or the marking. Longer: the challenge, then every rectangle with area 60 and its perimeter.

Words and supports

The defaults hold: items read aloud once, large print offered, a scribe or an oral answer on items 5 and 6, a sketch drawn from a student's words. Nothing else: the check is unaided.

Tomorrow

Judge items 5 and 6 after class, ticking each look-for alone.

  • Item 5. Finds the length, 20; uses area for the sheet and perimeter for the border; writes square feet and feet.
  • Item 6. Two sides that add to 19 and are not 11 and 8; a reason from length plus width or half the perimeter. 22 by 4 (the same area) does not meet it.

Day 94 opens with both, five minutes. A length of 9 ("4 more") goes to stretch 10's help. "20 by 5" for (a) is asked again: "How much plastic is that?" Area misses on item 7 need care in stretch 12, where areas grow.
