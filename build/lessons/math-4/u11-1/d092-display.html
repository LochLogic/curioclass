  1. A kitchen floor is 9 feet long and 6 feet wide. Each tile is 1 foot by 1 foot. How many tiles does the floor need?
    9 × 6 = 54 tiles (area, 54 square feet)
  2. Zoe makes a rectangle out of tape on the gym floor. It is 14 feet long and 9 feet wide. How much tape does she use?
    14 + 9 + 14 + 9 = 46 feet (perimeter)
  3. A bench seat is 2 feet wide. It is 6 times as long as it is wide. Mom paints the top of the seat. How much does she paint?
    Length 6 × 2 = 12 feet; 12 × 2 = 24 square feet (area)
  4. A dog yard is 6 feet wide. It has 42 square feet of ground. How much fence closes it in?
    6 × ? = 42, so 7 feet long; fence 7 + 6 + 7 + 6 = 26 feet
  5. A flag is 9 inches wide. It is 2 times as long as it is wide. Gold trim makes a border on the flag. How much trim is used?
    Length 2 × 9 = 18 inches; 18 + 9 + 18 + 9 = 54 inches (perimeter)
  6. Kai bends 24 inches of wire into a rectangle. It is 7 inches long. How wide is it?
    7 + ? + 7 + ? = 24; 5 inches (the wire is the perimeter)
