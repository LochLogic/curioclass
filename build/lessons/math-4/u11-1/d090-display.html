Their working

35 − 5 = 30.

The right working

5 × ? = 35. 5 × 7 = 35, so the rug is 7 feet long.

Max says: "A goat pen has 44 feet of fence all the way around. It is 14 feet long, so it is 30 feet wide."

Their working

44 − 14 = 30.

The right working

14 + ? + 14 + ? = 44. 44 − 28 = 16, and half of 16 is 8. The pen is 8 feet wide.
