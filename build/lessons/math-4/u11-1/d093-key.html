Day 93: Show what I know: key

Each answer is worked out in full. The last column names the wrong answers to expect and the error each one shows.

Any equal form of an equation is right: the two factors in either order, either side of the equals sign, any letter, or the matching division (n × 9 = 36, 9 × n = 36, 36 = 9 × n and 36 ÷ 9 = n all say the same).

end 5s11-E54.MD.A.3 (e), MP.4A sandbox is 5 feet wide. It is 4 times as long as it is wide. (a) A plastic sheet lies flat on top and just fits. How much plastic is that? (b) A wooden border runs along all four sides. How long is the border? Write the units.100 square feet; 50 feet
Look-fors, each ticked alone:
  1. finds the length, 4 × 5 = 20
  2. uses area for the sheet and perimeter for the border (part e)
  3. writes square feet for the sheet and feet for the border
Samples (ours, untested):
  • meets: Length 4 × 5 = 20. Sheet 5 × 20 = 100 square feet. Border 20 + 5 + 20 + 5 = 50 feet.
  • partly: 5 + 4 = 9. Sheet 45 square feet, border 28 feet. (Look-for 2 is met; the length goes to the comparing help: 4 bars of 5.)
  • not: Length 20. Sheet 20 + 5 + 20 + 5 = 50 feet. Border 20 × 5 = 100 feet.
Length 9, giving 45 and 28: "4 more", unit 10; swapped: error A, uses one formula for the other measure; 25: error B, adds one length and one width; "100 feet": error E, wrong unit: "40 feet" for an area
