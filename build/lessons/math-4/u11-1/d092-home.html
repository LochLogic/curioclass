Day 92: Area or perimeter?

At home

GoalI can decide which measure a real problem needs
Time38 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. String loop: a 36-inch string on a table, an inch for each foot. Grid paper: plain paper for sketches.

Skip. The warm-up, problems 1, 4 and 6, and the challenge.

  • 10 min. The dog pen. Read The dog pen (side 1) aloud. Make a long thin pen with the string, then a square one, and fill the table on side 1 together.

    Say. "Every pen used 36 feet of fence. What is the same about length plus width?" Expect: 18 every time, half of 36. Then: "Same fence, same room?" Expect: no; 9 by 9 is the most.

  • 8 min. Two examples. Example 1 (side 1), then 2. In each step ask: "Inside or edge?"

  • 10 min. Three problems. Six problems 2 (side 2), 3 and 5, alone, area or perimeter first.
  • 5 min. Tell me. Tell me (side 2). The child may say it while you write it down.
  • 5 min. Exit ticket. Read the ticket question aloud once; the child answers alone.

The questions

The child works on the printed page; the answers are for you.

WhereQuestionAnswer
The dog pen (side 1)A dog pen is a rectangle. It uses all 36 feet of fence. Which pen gives the dog the most room?9 feet by 9 feet: 81 square feet. Every pen's length plus width is 18
Example 1 (side 1)A rug is 7 feet wide. Its length is 2 times its width. How much floor is under the rug?Length 2 × 7 = 14 feet; area 14 × 7 = 98 square feet
Example 2 (side 1)A chicken yard has 72 square feet of ground. It is 8 feet wide. How much fence does it need?8 × ? = 72, so 9 feet long; fence 9 + 8 + 9 + 8 = 34 feet
Six problems 2 (side 2)Zoe makes a rectangle out of tape on the gym floor. It is 14 feet long and 9 feet wide. How much tape does she use?14 + 9 + 14 + 9 = 46 feet (perimeter)
Six problems 3 (side 2)A bench seat is 2 feet wide. It is 6 times as long as it is wide. Mom paints the top of the seat. How much does she paint?Length 6 × 2 = 12 feet; 12 × 2 = 24 square feet (area)
Six problems 5 (side 2)A flag is 9 inches wide. It is 2 times as long as it is wide. Gold trim makes a border on the flag. How much trim is used?Length 2 × 9 = 18 inches; 18 + 9 + 18 + 9 = 54 inches (perimeter)
Tell me (side 2)Look at problem 2. Did it need area or perimeter? How did you know?Perimeter, 46 feet: the tape makes the sides, not the floor inside
the ticket questionA patio is 4 yards wide. Its length is 3 times its width. (a) Ana paints the patio. How much does she paint? (b) She fences in the patio. How much fence does she need? Write the units.48 square yards; 32 yards
