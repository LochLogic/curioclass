Day 90: Find the missing side

At home

Not ready to teach: tools/community-plot-planner/community-plot-planner.html missing.

GoalI can find a missing side from the area or the perimeter
Time40 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No device: the Plot notes table on side 2 is the whole job. Grid paper: 27 paper squares. No projector: point at side 1.

Skip. The tool, the facts check, Plot notes rows 4 and 5, and the challenge.

  • 10 min. Two examples. Read Example 1 (side 1) together; the child fills the frame. Then Example 2 (side 1). Write the four-side equation first, every time.

  • 8 min. Fix two. Fix it 1 (side 1), then 2.

    Say. "Jo subtracted. Area is rows times columns: 5 times what is 35?" Expect: 7, so 7 feet.

  • 10 min. Plot notes. Plot notes, row 1 (side 2), row 2 and row 3: the equation, the missing side, the fence.

  • 7 min. On your own. On your own 1 (side 2) with the 27 squares in rows of 3, then On your own 2 (side 2). The child checks each answer.

    Say. "How can you check a missing side with no one to tell you?" Expect: put it back in the equation; 3 × 9 gives 27.

  • 5 min. Exit ticket. The child answers ticket question 1 and question 2 alone.
