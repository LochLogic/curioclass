Ray says: "8 times as many as 3 is 11." He drew a 3-strip and 8 more squares.

Their working

a 3-strip, then 8 more squares on the end. 3 + 8 = 11.

The right working

8 times as many as 3 is 8 copies of the 3-strip. Count by threes: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24. 24 is 8 times as many as 3.

Lin drew 4 times as many as 3. She drew a 3-strip, then 4 more 3-strips. She got 15.

Their working

the 3-strip, then 4 more copies after it. 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 15.

The right working

in the row for 4 times as many, the first 3-strip is copy one. Count the copies aloud: one, two, three, four. 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12. 12 is 4 times as many as 3.
