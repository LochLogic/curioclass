Day 80: Times as many

At home

GoalI can build and draw "4 times as many" and say it in a sentence
Time30 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No cubes: dried beans, coins or paper squares. No projector: point at side 1. No grid paper: the strip sheet, or squares drawn on plain paper.

Skip. The counting warm-up, comparisons 3 and 6, and the challenge.

  • 8 min. Two builds. Example 1 (side 1): you set out a row of 3 beans; the child sets out 4 copies of that row under it. Then Example 2 (side 1): your 3, then 4 single beans on the end. Leave both out; the child fills the two frames on side 1.

    Say. "Where is the 4 in each one?" Expect: 4 copies of the row of 3; 4 single beans on the end.

  • 7 min. Fix two. Fix it 1 (side 1), then 2. The child says what went wrong and draws it right.

    Say. "Which row is copy one? How many copies are there?" Expect: the first row of 3; 4 copies, so 12.

  • 10 min. Build, draw, say. Build, draw, say 1 (side 2) and 2 with beans, drawn on the strip sheet. Then Build, draw, say 4 (side 2) and 5, drawn only: each copy a box with its number inside.

  • 5 min. Exit ticket. The child answers the ticket question alone.
