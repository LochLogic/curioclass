Ready for times as many: key

The last ten minutes of day 79, the respond day after Check 2. Alone, on paper, all written. Read each item aloud once. Mark before day 80 and write each result on the unit 10 record sheet (R1 to R3): a tick, x for a wrong answer, or the error letter where the key gives one. Item 3 needs both the equation and m = 12 for a tick; 12 with no equation is G.

"Many" means more than a third of the class. Each item serves one section, and its reteach is in that section's opener:

  • Item 1, equal groups (section 10.1). Reteach on day 81, in the warm-up and the first five minutes: whole class if many missed it, otherwise a small group in the warm-up. The sign: 25, 35 or 150, an answer that does not fit equal rows.
  • Item 2, how many more (section 10.2). Reteach on day 83, the same way. The sign: 23 (added) or one of the two amounts given back.
  • Item 3, a two-step problem with a letter (section 10.3). Reteach on day 87, the same way. The signs: 18 (one step), 48 (added), 252 (worked left to right), or 12 with no equation. Days 85 and 86 also put one grade 3 two-step problem in the warm-up for everyone.

The results are in hand a week before sections 10.2 and 10.3 open. Keep the record sheet where you will see it on days 83 and 87.
