Warm-up. Count by 3s to 30. Count by 4s to 40.

Build, draw, say. Draw each pair of strips on your strip sheet. No sheet: use grid paper.

  1. Build a strip of 2 cubes. Build 3 times as many cubes. How many is that?

    is times as many as .

  2. Build a strip of 1 cube. Build 7 times as many cubes. How many is that?

    is times as many as .

From 3 on, draw each copy as a box with its number inside.

  1. Build a strip of 5 cubes. Build 3 times as many cubes. How many is that?

    is times as many as .

Put the cubes away.

  1. Draw a strip for 5. Draw 7 times as many. How many is that?

    is times as many as .

  2. Draw a strip for 6. Draw 8 times as many. How many is that?

    is times as many as .

  3. Draw a strip for 4. Draw 9 times as many. How many is that?

    is times as many as .

Challenge. A strip has 7 squares. Draw 8 times as many squares. How many is that? Draw it in the box.

is times as many as .
