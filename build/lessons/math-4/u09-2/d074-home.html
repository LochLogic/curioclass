Day 74: Ungrouping one place

At home

GoalI can subtract in columns when one place needs ungrouping, and say what I ungrouped
Time38 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: point at side 1. Grid paper: lined paper turned sideways, one digit a space.

Skip. The warm-up, readiness items 3 and 4, problems 4 and 7, and the challenge.

  • 10 min. One ten ungrouped. Example (side 1) together. To ungroup is to trade one of a place for ten of the place to its right. The child fills both halves of the side 1 table.

    Say. "What did 7 tens and 2 ones become?" Expect: 6 tens and 12 ones; still 9,372.

  • 10 min. Both ways. Both ways, with a partner 1 (side 1) and 2 (side 2). On the first, you say each expanded-form step and the child says the matching column step. On the second, swap.

  • 8 min. One ungrouping each. One ungrouping each 3 (side 2), 5, 6, 8. After each, the child says what the crossed-out digit became, as a number, and writes the place in the table.

  • 5 min. Fix it. Fix it 9 (side 2), then 10.

    Say. "Mia made 14 ones. What should the 9 tens be now?" Expect: 8 tens, so 4,226.

  • 5 min. Exit ticket. The child answers the ticket question alone.

The questions

The child works on the printed page; the answers are for you.

Example (side 1)9,372 − 4,1565,216; 7 tens and 2 ones became 6 tens and 12 ones
  1. Say both numbers aloud. Line them up under the place names: thousands, hundreds, tens, ones.
  2. Expanded form: 9,372 is 9,000 + 300 + 70 + 2. 4,156 is 4,000 + 100 + 50 + 6.
  3. Start with the ones. Can I take 6 ones from 2 ones? No. I need more ones.
  4. Ungroup one ten. 7 tens and 2 ones become 6 tens and 12 ones. In expanded form, 70 + 2 becomes 60 + 12.
  5. Nothing was taken away yet. 9,000 + 300 + 60 + 12 is still 9,372. Ungrouping is carrying run backwards.
  6. In the columns: cross out the 7 and write 6 above it. Write a small 1 by the 2: 12 ones.
  7. Ones: 12 − 6 = 6. Tens: 6 − 5 = 1. Hundreds: 3 − 1 = 2. Thousands: 9 − 4 = 5.
  8. Expanded form gives 5,000 + 200 + 10 + 6. The columns give 5,216. Both ways, the difference is 5,216.
