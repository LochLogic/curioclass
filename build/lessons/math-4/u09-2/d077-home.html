Day 77: Show what I know

At home

GoalI can add and subtract big numbers without help
Time45 minutes, one parent and one child (an assessment day)
Printthe student page; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: mark from the key in this view. Grid paper: lined paper turned sideways.

Skip. The mixed set, the groups and the challenge.

  • 30 min. End-of-unit assessment. Items 1 to 6, alone, with no help. Read an item aloud once if asked. Not reached is not wrong.

  • 15 min. Go over it, one to one. Mark items 1, 2, 4 and 5 together from the key. Fix each missed one in pen beside the first try: item 1 (side 1) with each small 1 said by its worth; item 5 (side 2) by renaming the whole top number in one line first, then adding back.

    Say. For item 6 (side 2): "Add Rosa's answer to 2,536. Do you get 8,004?" Expect: no, 9,068, so she is wrong; 5,468.

    Read items 3 and 6 after, ticking each look-for on the key alone.

item 6 (side 2)Rosa found 8,004 − 2,536 = 6,532. Use adding to show Rosa is wrong. Then find the right difference.6,532 + 2,536 = 9,068, not 8,004; 5,468
Look-fors:
  1. adds 2,536 to Rosa's difference and says it does not come back to 8,004

Samples (ours, untested):
  • meets: 6,532 + 2,536 = 9,068. That should be 8,004, so Rosa is wrong. It is 5,468, and 5,468 + 2,536 = 8,004.
  • partly: Rosa took the small digit from the big one. It is 5,468. (Look-for 2 only: true, but no adding. If many: every warm-up difference is checked by adding for a week.)
  • not: It is 5,578. (No adding, and each zero left as 10: error I.)
