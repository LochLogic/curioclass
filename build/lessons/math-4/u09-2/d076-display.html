Their working

The right working

5,002 is 4 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 12 ones. 12 − 6 = 6, 9 − 3 = 6, 9 − 8 = 1, 4 − 1 = 3, so 3,166. Check: 3,166 + 1,836 = 5,002.
