I can subtract in columns when one place needs ungrouping, and say what I ungrouped.

Say both numbers. Line up the places. Can I take away? If not, ungroup. Subtract.

Th is thousands, H is hundreds, T is tens, O is ones. First four boxes: expanded form. Last four: columns.

Example. 9,372 − 4,156
