Ty says: "45,206 − 3,874 = 6,466."

Their working

45,206 written over 38,740, lined up from the left. 45,206 − 38,740 = 6,466.

The right working

Estimate first. About 45,000 − 4,000 = 41,000, so 6,466 is far too small. Line up the ones: 3,874 has no ten thousands. 45,206 − 3,874 = 41,332. Check: 41,332 + 3,874 = 45,206.

Nia says: "82,415 − 36,278 = 56,137."

Their working

She ungrouped 1 ten thousand to make 12 thousands, then took 3 from 8 in the ten thousands. 82,415 − 36,278 = 56,137.

The right working

Check by adding. 56,137 + 36,278 = 92,415, not 82,415. The 8 ten thousands became 7 when one was ungrouped. 7 − 3 = 4, so 82,415 − 36,278 = 46,137. Check: 46,137 + 36,278 = 82,415.
