Samples (ours, untested):
  • meets: There are no hundreds or tens, so I ungroup one thousand. It goes to 10 hundreds, then 10 tens, then 10 ones, and each place gives one away and keeps 9.
  • partly: You cross out the 6 and make it 5, and the zeros turn into 9s. (Look-for 1 only, said as marks: ask what the thousand became.)
  • not: The zeros become 10s because you borrow. (Error I.)
"the zeros become 10s": error I, across zeros, turns each zero into 10 and leaves it 10
