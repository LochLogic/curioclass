Before class

Print everything on the print list. Mark the day 76 tickets; judge the written tasks. Open the display; show the check's answers only at the marking. Take down the place names.

The day

10 min. Mixed set (Road Trip Logbook when it exists)

Alone, on the day 77 mixed set: Add: 37,295 + 48,617 [85,912], Add: 5,846 + 7,379 [13,225], Add: 263,580 + 94,763 [358,343], Add: 708,469 + 186,754 [895,223], Add: 49,387 + 6,958 [56,345], Subtract: 8,153 − 3,627 [4,526], Subtract: 61,742 − 25,386 [36,356], Subtract: 437,215 − 168,549 [268,666], Subtract: 7,003 − 2,465 [4,538], Subtract: 500,060 − 236,847 [263,213]. When it exists: Road Trip Logbook's Trip ledger, the same ten, stopped at 10 minutes. Until then, paper, worked on grid paper: each answer is checked against the display key. Not finished is fine.

Check: Right on the first try? Tick it. If not, fix it in pen.

30 min. End-of-unit assessment

Keep the day 76 tickets and written tasks until pencils go down. Alone, unaided. At 30 minutes, pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

Mark, 5 minutes. Hand back the day 76 tickets and written tasks. Show the answers screen; students tick or circle each unit assessment item in pen. You judge 3 and 6 after class. A ticket with 23,776 or 22,675 (I) or 37,324 (F), or a written task with zeros as 10s, joins that group.

Groups, 15 minutes. Students find their wrong answer on the groups screen and go to that group; a student in two goes to the subtracting one. A wrong answer on no list: bring it to me. Each group fixes its items in pen on the column sheet.

  • A and D (short by 10, 100, 1,000 or 10,000 on 1 or 2, 111,225, or two digits in one column): item 1 in expanded form beside the algorithm, each carry said by its value: "15 ones is 1 ten and 5 ones." Then fix 1 and 2.
  • E (22,335 or 11,225 on 1): estimate first; it must pass 100,000. Fix 1.
  • B (1,253,595 on 2, 127,625 on 5, or Dev's 15,998 let stand on 3): place names over the columns; say both numbers aloud, estimate, then redo 2 and 5.
  • F to H (55,254, 55,854, 44,854, 45,754 or 54,754 on 4, 647,163 on 5, Rosa's 6,532 let stand on 6): in item 4, ask "can I take 6 from 1?"; ungroup everything first, then subtract and add back.
  • I (563,047 or 552,037 on 5, 5,578 on 6): say 8,004 as 7 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 14 ones before any column. Fix 6, then 5, each checked by adding.

Students who missed nothing do Add the three numbers: 148,276 + 39,508 + 205,617. [393,401], then Find 504,030 − 268,457. Show the adding that checks it. [235,573; 235,573 + 268,457 = 504,030]. Collect the checks.

Other rooms

  • No projector. The mixed set's key, the answers and the group tasks on a covered part of the board.

Shorter or longer

Shorter: the mixed set to six problems, three of each kind, and groups ten minutes. Never the check or the marking.

Words and supports

The defaults hold, plus large-square grid paper and typed columns; items 3 and 6 may be answered aloud.

Tomorrow

After class: record 1, 2, 4 and 5 from the pencil first try on unit 9's record sheet; judge 3 and 6 the same way, each look-for alone.

Item 3:

  1. an estimate, or a place value reason, for why 15,998 cannot be right, given without the exact sum
  2. finds 8,321

Item 6:

  1. adds 2,536 to Rosa's difference and says it does not come back to 8,004
  2. finds 5,468

Day 78 is Check 2, with no respond half. Items 3 and 6 go back on day 79 with their "meets" answers; no estimate on 3 sends a student to day 79's estimate-first group, no adding on 6 to its check-by-adding group.
