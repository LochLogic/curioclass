Before class

Print everything on the print list. Mark the day 76 slips. Open the display; show the check's answers only at the marking. Take down the place names.

The day

10 min. Mixed set (Road Trip Logbook when it exists)

Alone, on the day 77 mixed set: Add: 37,295 + 48,617 [85,912], Add: 5,846 + 7,379 [13,225], Add: 263,580 + 94,763 [358,343], Add: 708,469 + 186,754 [895,223], Add: 49,387 + 6,958 [56,345], Subtract: 8,153 − 3,627 [4,526], Subtract: 61,742 − 25,386 [36,356], Subtract: 437,215 − 168,549 [268,666], Subtract: 7,003 − 2,465 [4,538], Subtract: 500,060 − 236,847 [263,213]. It is the warm-up. When it exists: Road Trip Logbook's Trip ledger, the same ten, stopped at 10 minutes. Until then, paper: each answer is checked against the key on the display once done, ticked or fixed in pen. Students write a right-first-try count, compared only with their own last one. Not finished is fine; sheets stay with students.

Check: Right on the first try? Tick it. If not, fix it in pen.

30 min. End check

Keep the day 76 slips and the day 76 written task until pencils go down. Alone, unaided. At 30 minutes, pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

Mark, 5 minutes. Hand back the day 76 slips; show the answers to 1, 2, 4 and 5. Students tick or circle each in pen. You judge 3 and 6 after class. A day 76 slip with 23,776 (I) or 37,324 (F) joins that group.

Groups, 15 minutes. Hands up sets the groups; a student in two goes to the subtracting one. Each group fixes its items in pen on the column sheet. Tasks are on the display.

  • A and D (short by 10, 100, 1,000 or 10,000 on 1 or 2, or two digits in one column): item 1 in expanded form beside the algorithm, each carry said by its value: "15 ones is 1 ten and 5 ones." Then fix 1 and 2.
  • E (22,335 on 1): estimate first; it must pass 100,000. Fix 1.
  • B (1,253,595 on 2, 127,625 on 5, or Dev's 15,998 let stand on 3): place names over the columns; say both numbers aloud, estimate, then redo 2 and 5.
  • F to H (55,254, 55,854, 44,854, 45,754 or 54,754 on 4, 647,163 on 5, Rosa's 6,532 let stand on 6): in item 4, ask "can I take 6 from 1?" Ungroup everything first, saying what each place became, then subtract. Check by adding.
  • I (563,047 on 5, 5,578 on 6): say 8,004 as 7 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 14 ones before any column. Fix 6, then 5, each checked by adding.

Students who missed nothing do Add the three numbers: 148,276 + 39,508 + 205,617. [393,401], then Find 504,030 − 268,457. Show the adding that checks it. [235,573; 235,573 + 268,457 = 504,030]. Collect the checks.

Other rooms

  • No projector. The mixed set's key, the answers and the group tasks on a covered part of the board.
  • One child at home. The defaults.

Shorter or longer

Shorter: the mixed set to six problems, three of each kind, and groups ten minutes. Never the check or the marking.

Words and supports

The defaults hold, plus large-square grid paper and typed columns; items 3 and 6 may be answered aloud.

Tomorrow

After class: record 1, 2, 4 and 5 from the pencil first try on stretch 9's record sheet; judge 3 and 6 the same way, each look-for alone.

Item 3:

  1. an estimate, or a place value reason, for why 15,998 cannot be right, given without the exact sum
  2. finds 8,321

Item 6:

  1. adds 2,536 to Rosa's difference and says it does not come back to 8,004
  2. finds 5,468

Day 78 is Check 2, with no respond half. Items 3 and 6 go back on day 79 with their "meets" answers; no estimate on 3 sends a student to day 79's estimate-first group, no adding on 6 to its check-by-adding group.
