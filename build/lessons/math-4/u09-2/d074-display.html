Kai says 5,842 − 1,718 = 4,136.

Their working

Ones 8 − 2 = 6, tens 4 − 1 = 3, hundreds 8 − 7 = 1, thousands 5 − 1 = 4. He got 4,136.

The right working

Can I take 8 ones from 2 ones? No. Ungroup one ten: 4 tens and 2 ones become 3 tens and 12 ones. 12 − 8 = 4, 3 − 1 = 2, 8 − 7 = 1, 5 − 1 = 4. The difference is 4,124.

Mia says 6,594 − 2,368 = 4,236.

Their working

She wrote a small 1 to make 14 ones, but left the 9 tens as 9. 14 − 8 = 6, 9 − 6 = 3, 5 − 3 = 2, 6 − 2 = 4. She got 4,236.

The right working

Ungroup one ten. 9 tens and 4 ones become 8 tens and 14 ones. 14 − 8 = 6, 8 − 6 = 2, 5 − 3 = 2, 6 − 2 = 4. The difference is 4,226.
