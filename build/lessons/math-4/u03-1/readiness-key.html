Ready for equivalent fractions: key

The last ten minutes of day 9, after unit 2's end-of-unit assessment and its respond half. Alone, on paper, all written; the line and the two strips are printed at the top of the sheet. Read each item aloud once. Mark before day 10 and write each result on the unit 3 record sheet (R1 to R4): a tick, the error letter where the key gives one, or x for any other wrong answer. Item 1 needs both points for a tick, and item 4 both fractions.

"Many" means more than a third of the class. All four items serve section 3.1, and their reteach is in its opener. It runs in day 11's warm-up and first five minutes: for the whole class if many missed an item, otherwise for the students who missed it, during the warm-up.

  • Item 1, fractions on a line, past 1. The signs: 5/4 at the fifth tick when 0 is counted as "one" (error C), or 5/4 at five eighths of the way from 0 to 2 (error K). Reteach: lay a one-fourth strip from 0 again and again, counting to "five fourths"; four fourths land on 1.
  • Item 2, the same amount in fourths. The sign: 1 (one number kept, error A). Reteach: fold a strip in half, shade one half, fold again, and count the shaded fourths.
  • Item 3, which unit fraction is larger. The sign: 1/8 (bigger numbers, bigger fraction, error D). Reteach: a strip in thirds beside one in eighths: more parts, each smaller. Item 3 is asked again with new numbers at the start of day 19.
  • Item 4, whole numbers as fractions. The sign: 1/3 for 3, a whole number not yet written as a fraction. Reteach with item 1: count fourths along the line to 1, then whole steps to 3, and write 3 as 3/1.

The results are in hand the night before day 10. Day 10 does not wait for the reteach: it opens by folding strips, the same move as item 2. Keep the record sheet where you will see it on days 11 and 19.
