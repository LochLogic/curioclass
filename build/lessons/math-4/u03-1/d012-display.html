Kai says 25 and 410 are different amounts, because 4 and 10 are bigger.

Their working

4 is more than 2 and 10 is more than 5, so 4/10 is more.

The right working

Cut each fifth into 2 equal parts. 5 × 2 = 10 parts in the whole, and 2 × 2 = 4 parts in 2/5. Twice as many parts, each half the size: 2/5 = 4/10, one point.
