Unit 3 record sheet: equivalent fractions and comparing

One row a student, for days 9 to 25; print two for a class over 24. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to K (the section openers); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for not reached. R1 to R4: the readiness check, day 9. E10 to E23: the exits; a tick means every part of the ticket. W12 to W23: the written tasks, judged after class and handed back the next day (W23 when pencils go down on day 24): one tick a look-for (listed under the table). End 3.1a to 3.4a: the end-of-unit assessment, day 24; 3.1b and 3.3a take one tick a look-for, marked after class and handed back on day 25. Each head names the parts of the standard it records.

W12 look-fors: 1. says how each part was cut; 2. says there are more parts and each is smaller; 3. says the length or the point is the same

W16 look-fors: 1. each third was cut into 2; 2. twice as many parts in the whole and in the fraction; 3. the point did not move

W18 look-fors: 1. names the missing parts, 1/4 and 1/6; 2. says a sixth is smaller than a fourth, so 5/6 is closer to 1; 3. writes 3/4 < 5/6

W22 look-fors: 1. each renaming is right (5/20 and 4/20; 2/8 and 2/10); 2. both ways give 1/4 > 2/10; 3. a reason for the choice

W23 look-fors: 1. 5/6 < 9/10, with the sign; 2. each way is complete; 3. says, or shows on one line, that both are of the same whole

End 3.1b look-fors: 1. says how each part was cut; 2. says there are more parts and each is smaller; 3. says the length is the same

End 3.3a look-fors: 1. compares each fraction with 1/2 correctly; 2. gives a reason: half of the denominator, or an equal name for 1/2; 3. writes 48/100 < 3/5
