Lu shaded 13 of a strip. She cut each third into 2 equal parts. She wrote 13 = 16.

Their working

6 parts in the whole, and 1 shaded. 1/3 = 1/6.

The right working

The shaded third was cut too, so it is now 2 parts. 6 parts in the whole, 2 shaded: 1/3 = 2/6. Lay 1/6 beside 1/3: it is plainly shorter, so it cannot be the same amount.

Mo shaded 36 of a strip. He cut each sixth into 2 equal parts. He wrote 36 = 66.

Their working

6 shaded parts, and he kept 6 for the whole. 3/6 = 6/6.

The right working

Every sixth was cut, so the whole has 12 parts now, and 6 are shaded. 3/6 = 6/12. 6/6 would be the whole strip, but only half of it is shaded.
