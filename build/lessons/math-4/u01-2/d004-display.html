Ravi says: "68 is not a multiple of 4. The 4s stop at 40."

Their working

The 4s table stops at 4 × 10 = 40, so he said no.

The right working

The 4s keep going past 40. 4 × 10 = 40 and 4 × 7 = 28. 40 + 28 = 68, so 4 × 17 = 68, and 68 is a multiple of 4.
