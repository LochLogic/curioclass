Day 6: Show what I know

At home

GoalI can show what I know and fix what I missed
Time45 minutes, one parent and one child (a assessment day)
Printthe student page; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: mark from the answers below.

Skip. The groups and the challenge.

  • 30 min. End-of-unit assessment. Items 1 to 6, alone, with no coins or chart; a child who has used the 9 by 9 table keeps it. Read each item aloud once. Not reached is not wrong.

  • 12 min. Go over it, one to one. Mark items 1 to 4 together. The child fixes each missed one in pen beside the first try: item 1 (side 1), search 80 in order from 1 to the turn; item 2 (side 1), read each pair both ways; item 3 (side 1), start from 4 × 10 and 6 × 10; item 4 (side 2), a pair for each composite.

    Say. For item 6 (side 2): "Why can Maya stop?" Expect: 7 goes with 5, which she has; every pair after is one she has, turned.

    Read item 5 (side 2) and item 6 after, against the answers.

  • 3 min. Ready for unit 2. Ask aloud: the next four numbers after 6, 12, 18; is 4 times a number always even, and why; which of 3, 8, 11, 14, 15, 17, 20 are even. Write down what the child says.

The questions

The child works on the printed page; the answers are for you.

WhereQuestionAnswer
item 1 (side 1)Find all the factor pairs of 80.1 × 80, 2 × 40, 4 × 20, 5 × 16, 8 × 10
item 2 (side 1)The factor pairs of 52 are 1 × 52, 2 × 26 and 4 × 13. Write every number that 52 is a multiple of.1, 2, 4, 13, 26, 52
item 3 (side 1)Is 76 a multiple of 4? Is 76 a multiple of 6? Show how you know.Yes: 4 × 19 = 76. No: 6 × 12 = 72 and 6 × 13 = 78. Any working that lands on 76 for 4, or shows 76 between two 6s, is right
item 4 (side 2)Write prime, composite or neither for each number: 71, 93, 1, 94, 53. Prove each one. For a composite, write a factor pair. For a prime, write the numbers you tested.71 prime, tested 2, 3, 5 and 7; 93 composite, 3 × 31 = 93; 1 neither, its only factor is 1; 94 composite, 2 × 47 = 94; 53 prime, tested 2, 3, 5 and 7 (a search in order to the turn is also right)
item 6 (side 2)Maya is finding the factor pairs of 35. She tries 1, 2, 3 and so on, in order. She has tried 1 to 6 and found 1 × 35 and 5 × 7. She says she can stop before she tries 7. Is Maya right? Explain.Right: 7 goes with 5, and she has 5 × 7 = 35; every pair after that is one she has, turned
item 5 (side 2)Ana says 99 is prime. It is odd, and she cannot think of a fact for it. Is Ana right? Explain how you know.Not right: 9 × 11 = 99 (or 3 × 33 = 99), so 99 has a factor pair other than 1 × 99
