Ready for adding and subtracting big numbers: key

One sheet a student, given in two halves. Items 1 and 2 are the last ten minutes of day 71 (the last day of stretch 8): alone, on paper, each item read aloud once. Collect the sheets and mark them that night, before day 72. Items 3 and 4 are the first five minutes of day 74, on the same sheet: hand the sheets back, read both items aloud, collect them, and mark them that night. Write each result on the stretch 9 record sheet (R1 to R4): a tick, the error letter where the key gives one, or x for any other wrong answer.

"Many" means more than a third of the class. Items 1 and 2 are reteached in day 73's warm-up and first five minutes; items 3 and 4 in day 75's.

  • Item 1, adding within 1,000. The signs: 642 (every carry lost, error A) or 61412 (a two-digit sum written in one column, error D). Reteach on day 73: 467 + 285 in expanded form on a place value chart, then in columns, each carry said by its value. Day 72 shows A and D as wrong examples first, so read item 1 beside E72: a student who missed item 1 but got E72 right needs nothing more.
  • Item 2, a number in digits. The signs: 36,042 or 306,42 (an empty place dropped), or 306,000,042 (the words written down one after another). Reteach on day 73: read the number by its comma groups, "three hundred six thousand" first, then "forty-two" in the last three places, a zero in each empty place. A student who misreads the places will line numbers up wrongly on day 73 (error B).
  • Item 3, subtracting within 1,000. The signs: 524 (the smaller digit taken from the larger, error F) or 586 (the small 1s written, nothing taken away, error G). Reteach on day 75: 734 − 258 with 700 + 30 + 4 rewritten as 600 + 120 + 14, then in columns. Day 74 teaches the same move, so read item 3 beside E74.
  • Item 4, subtracting across a zero. The signs: 376 (F) or 234 (the zero made 10 and left 10, error I). Reteach on day 75: 402 said as 3 hundreds, 9 tens, 12 ones before anything is written. Every student who missed item 4 also sits with the teacher for day 76's teaching (both examples), whatever E75 shows.

When no whole-class reteach is needed, the five minutes answer the previous day's exit (E72 on day 73, E74 on day 75). If both are needed, the reteach goes first and the exit's error is talked through inside the worked sum or difference.
