Stretch 9 record sheet: adding and subtracting to a million

One row a student, for days 71 to 79; print two for a class over 17. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to I (the unit openers); s for a slip (a fact slip with the right method); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for not reached. R1 and R2: day 71; R3 and R4: day 74. E72 to E76 and E79: the exits; a tick means both parts (the answer and the worth, estimate or check). W73 and W76: the written tasks, and End 9.1c and 9.2c, judged on day 77 and back on day 79: one tick a look-for (listed under the table). C2.1 and C2.2: Check 2, day 78.
