Day 72: What is the small 1 worth?

At home

GoalI can add big numbers in columns and say what each small 1 is worth
Time38 minutes, one parent and one child (a teaching day)
Printthe student page and one exit ticket; this page and its answers are for you

Swap. No projector: point at side 1. No column sheet: lined paper turned sideways, so the lines make columns.

Skip. The warm-up, items 6, 10 and 11, and the challenge.

  • 12 min. Two ways. Read Example 1 (side 1) together: expanded form (3,000 + 700 + 40 + 6) on the left, columns on the right. The child fills the frame on side 1. Then Example 2 (side 1).

    Say. Point at a small 1: "One what?" Expect: one ten (or hundred, or thousand), never "a 1".

  • 8 min. Both ways. Both ways 5 (side 2): you write expanded form, the child writes columns. Compare the sums.

  • 8 min. Columns alone. Columns alone 7 (side 2), 8, 9. Beside each, the child writes what every small 1 is worth.

  • 5 min. Fix it. Fix it 12 (side 2), then 13.

    Say. "Kai got 6,274. Where did the ten from 14 ones go?" Expect: he lost it; it goes over the tens, so 6,284.

  • 5 min. Exit ticket. The child answers the ticket question alone.
