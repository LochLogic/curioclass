Their working

6 + 8 = 14, write 4. 4 + 3 = 7. 7 + 5 = 12, write 2, carry 1. 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. Sum 6,274.

The right working

14 ones is 1 ten and 4 ones, so a small 1 goes over the tens. Tens: 4 + 3 + 1 = 8 tens. The sum is 6,284.
