practices09-W734.NBT.B.4 (a), MP.6Tell a third grader, in two sentences, what the small 1 means.For example: when a column makes ten or more, ten of that place are traded for one of the next place. The small 1 is that one, so over the tens it is one ten.
Look-fors, each ticked alone:
  1. names the unit the small 1 stands for (a ten, a hundred)
  2. says ten of one unit were traded for one of the next
Samples (ours, untested):
  • meets: When a column makes ten or more, ten of that place make one of the next place. So the small 1 over the tens is one ten.
  • partly: The small 1 is a ten. (Look-for 1 only: no trade said.)
  • not: You carry the 1 because the number is too big. (Neither look-for: error C.)
"it is a 1": error C, calls every carry "one"
