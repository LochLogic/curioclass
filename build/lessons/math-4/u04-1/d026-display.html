  1. Lay five sixth-pieces end to end on line 3, from 0. Say: one sixth, two sixths, up to five sixths.

  4. Both ways use the same five sixth-pieces. Grouping adds no piece, takes none away and does not change their size. So each way makes 5/6, and every bottom stays 6.

Kai wrote 58 two ways: 28 + 38 = 58 and 38 + 28 = 58.

Their working

2/8 + 3/8 = 5/8, then 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8. Both times the rings hold 2 pieces and 3 pieces.

The right working

Ring the five eighth-pieces in groups of new sizes, 1 piece and 4 pieces: 1/8 + 4/8 = 5/8.

Or ring 1, 1 and 3 pieces: 1/8 + 1/8 + 3/8 = 5/8. A new way needs a new set of ring sizes, not the same sizes in a new order.
