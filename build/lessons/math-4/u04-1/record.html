Unit 4 record sheet: adding and subtracting fractions

One row a student, for days 24 to 39; print two for a class over 24. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to H (the section openers); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for not reached. R1 to R4: the readiness check, day 24. E25 to E37: the exits; a tick means every part of the ticket. W27, W30, W34 and W37: the written tasks, judged after class and handed back the next day: one tick a look-for (listed under the table). End 4.1a to 4.4b: the end-of-unit assessment, day 38; 4.1c takes one tick a look-for, marked after class and handed back in day 39's first five minutes. A sum written 5 15/12 on 4.3b gets a tick and the letter G beside it. Each head names the parts of the standard it records.
