Bo's row is 4 tenth-pieces. Is 4/40 that long?

Bo laid 4 tenth-pieces in a row. He wrote 110 + 110 + 110 + 110 = 440.

Their working

1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 = 4/40. He added the tops to get 4 and the bottoms to get 40.

The right working

Each piece is one tenth. Four pieces of size one tenth are four tenths: 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 = 4/10.

4/40 is 4 parts of a whole cut into 40: far shorter than Bo's row of 4 tenth-pieces. The size of the pieces did not change, so the bottom stays 10.
