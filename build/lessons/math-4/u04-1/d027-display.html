Their working

2 1/3 = 3/3. Jo added the 2 and the 1 to make the top.

The right working

Each whole is three thirds, 3/3. Two wholes and one third more: 3/3 + 3/3 + 1/3.

Count the thirds: 3 and 3 is 6, and 1 more is 7. So 2 1/3 = 7/3.

Jo's 3/3 covers one whole strip. 2 1/3 is more than 2 wholes, so 3/3 is far too small.
