Ready for adding fractions: key

The last ten minutes of day 24, after unit 3's end-of-unit assessment and its respond half. Alone, on paper, all written; no strips. Read each item aloud once. Mark before day 25: a tick, the sign below where it shows, or x for any other wrong answer. Item 1 needs all three for a tick; item 4 needs eight of ten. Enter the marks on unit 4's record sheet (record.md, columns R1 to R4); section 4.1's opener has the reteach. The four items are the ones the grade 3 to 5 progression names for unit 4.

  • Item 1, counting on by fourths, past 1. The signs: 4/5, 5/6, 6/7 (both numbers counted up, the start of adding tops and adding bottoms); stopping at 4/4 (a fraction thought to end at one whole).
  • Item 2, a whole number as fourths. The sign: 2/4 (the whole number written on top).
  • Item 3, an equal name, from unit 3. Any equal name is right. The sign: 3/4 (the same number added to both, unit 3 error B); the unit 3 record sheet says who still shows it.
  • Item 4, sums and differences within 20. Fewer than eight right: the warm-ups carry the facts; unit 4's sums need them quick.
