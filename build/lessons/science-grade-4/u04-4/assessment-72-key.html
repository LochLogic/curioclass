24-ESS1-1 (history)Invented for practice. Across the valley and far away, the distinctive P-Q-R order and fossils repeat. Another exposure shares their colors but reverses the order and lacks rooted plants. Judge the proposed matches, including uncertainty.Order and fossils support near and far matches, with uncertainty. Other colors alone cannot establish a match; the different order and missing rooted plants weaken it.Matching colors prove all exposures are the same.: error G, color alone proves matching layers. (Error G: Matching colors prove all exposures are the same.)

The record does not order those two changes.The break happened before any layer existed.: error x, any other wrong answer: no group (Error x: Unsupported or incomplete evidence; silence alone is not a misconception.)
44-ESS1-1 (history)Invented for practice. Bent, displaced layers end at a river notch. Flat gravel lies on the notch floor. Explain the relative order of cutting and displacement.The notch truncates already shifted layers, so cutting came later. Floor gravel is later than the surface beneath it.Today's river always occupied this notch.: error H, a river always occupied its present position. (Error H: Today's river always occupied this notch.)
54-ESS1-1 (history)Use the same invented valley record.
