Choose one evidence repair

Teacher only: ten-minute Targeted repair, four simultaneous paper groups. Spend about two minutes at each, with two minutes for transitions. Return day 70 notes within this part, not as extra minutes. Measurement support may replace a child's assigned paper task. Nothing calls for a new practical round.

  1. Breakage/carrying: compare a split but stationary rock sketch with unchanged grains moved on a line. A separates movement from breaking. B annotates actual changed edges; a failed short trial does not establish impossibility.
  2. Controls and land/life: revisit one familiar practice card from day 64. C fixes one uncontrolled condition. D connects an organism action to changed material. E links water to a plant response. Do only the child's recorded gap.
  3. Layer order: retrace day 68's original moderate bend through labeled bands. F applies only to an explicitly inverted-layer rule claim.
  4. Historical evidence: revisit the day 69 lake sequence. G uses fossil and order patterns instead of color alone. H puts cutting after its host material.

Ruler diagram for a measurement gap, invented for practice: a starting patch at 2 centimeters reaches 8 centimeters. Travel is 6 centimeters. Align the same ruler with both marks. Check that the child subtracts start from end. These are teacher-only repair numbers, unrelated to a returned item's key.
