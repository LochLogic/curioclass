Day 117: familiar repair moves

Use your assigned group. These are familiar teaching records, not fresh-response cases.

Light group

First five minutes: compare Light L source-on and source-off rows. Trace the missing source link with one arrow.

Next five: compare a blocked segment, or remove flash from the familiar cat model. Use the contrast named in your feedback.

Sensing group

First five minutes: place receiving, processing and action roles separately, then connect them. Next five: use Signal S to add remembered meaning to the class model.

Plant group

First five minutes: join source card U's roots with an internal transport role. Next five: separate rootless Plant P from uptake, or order familiar reproductive cards. Use the contrast named in your feedback.

Animal group

First five minutes: join intake with a separate internal digestion card. Next five: join one familiar reproductive structure with its supplied job.

Teacher: groups run simultaneously for ten minutes. Lead the largest-need group throughout. During twenty-minute revision, verify other three groups for five minutes each, then unresolved papers for five. Keep original answers and marks. Assign one fresh routed item after revision.
