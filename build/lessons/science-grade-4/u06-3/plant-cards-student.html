Separate plant jobs

Source-backed facts. Original schematic drawings, not to scale.

Card C

Prickly pear has shallow roots that collect rainfall. Its pads store water. Spines help deter animals from eating it.

Card T

Thorns can reduce damage from plant-eating animals. A thorn is a modified branch. Not every sharp plant part is a thorn.

Card D

Dandelion seeds can move with the wind.

Card B

A shoot can bend toward light as it grows. Plants do not need an animal brain for this response.
