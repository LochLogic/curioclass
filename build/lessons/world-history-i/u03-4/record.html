E42 look-fors: 1. connection marked from producers to crossing to market; 2. political meaning: revenue and control; 3. economic meaning: whose labour and which goods

E43 look-fors: 1. two separate dated bands; 2. overlap given; 3. the ceramics read as exchange, not founders

E44 look-fors: 1. the forced-labour detail chosen; 2. who carried the cost named; 3. the claim judged, not repeated

E46 look-fors: 1. the missed component corrected with evidence; 2. two developments placed in the same years; 3. the overlap explained

W44 look-fors: 1. claim weighs the three, not one alone; 2. two details explained from named sources; 3. a counterclaim answered with evidence; 4. a limit of the sources named; 5. Chinggis to Khubilai: conquest to Yuan government; 6. a Silk Roads consequence, with conquest's harms kept

End 1 look-fors: 1. three words defined accurately; 2. one applied to the case; 3. a second word shown to fit too; 4. why the words help compare power

End 2 look-fors: 1. map: dated, key, scale, routes; 2. politics from a source; 3. economy with labour and costs; 4. culture with contributors beyond the ruler; 5. trade; 6. alliance, or a note that none is recorded; 7. war; 8. exchange; 9. two named sources; 10. evidence kept apart from inference

End 3 look-fors: 1. Ghana, Mali, Songhai in order; 2. gold and salt exchange taxed or controlled; 3. enslaved people named as people forced; 4. Timbuktu linking trade and learning

End 4 look-fors: 1. Axum placed earlier, by the Red Sea; 2. Swahili towns placed later, on the Indian Ocean coast; 3. a political contrast; 4. an economic contrast

End 5 look-fors: 1. European contact named and explained; 2. a Yuan achievement; 3. an early Ming achievement, after the Yuan; 4. from the claim: Chinggis to Khubilai and a Silk Roads consequence

End 6 look-fors: 1. five goods each with origin and direction; 2. goods moving in several directions; 3. relays or regional exchange explained; 4. grain as regional

End 7 look-fors: 1. what P1 added; 2. what P2 added; 3. a user of each; 4. a limit of each tied to how it was made

End 8 look-fors: 1. first half traced to P1; 2. second half traced to P2; 3. one inference explained; 4. one unsupported claim named

End 9 look-fors: 1. a question that needs both sources; 2. a reasoned choice of card; 3. a detail from the card; 4. the question revised or answered; 5. what is still unresolved
