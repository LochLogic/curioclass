Read the completed network, day 17

This original diagram simplifies a network. It is not to scale. Actual voyages varied. Not every ship sailed all three links.

Goods: Europe → Africa: manufactured goods. People forced: Africa → Americas: enslaved Africans, the Middle Passage. Goods: Americas → Europe: plantation goods.

Crowded: little space. Confined: held within. Ventilation: fresh air moving through. Identify conditions separately during practice. Do not write a cause paragraph.
