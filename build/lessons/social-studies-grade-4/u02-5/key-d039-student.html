Keep this sheet separate from every student packet. Use one scale per component: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Access support is separate from content help. No points or totals.

Label features 1, 2, and 3 using the index.

1 Colorado River; 2 Sierra Nevada; 3 Great Basin Desert. All three match the keyed features.

Teaching sequence: Look for: 1 Colorado River; 2 Sierra Nevada; 3 Great Basin Desert. All three match the keyed features.

Wrong: 1 Sierra Nevada; 2 Colorado River; 3 Great Basin Desert. Error A. Compare keyed feature marks.

C1-E39-2

Explain how Map A distinguishes rivers from mountain ranges.

The river is a thick line; the range has triangle marks. Use both marks from this key.

Teaching sequence: Look for: The river is a thick line; the range has triangle marks. Use both marks from this key.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: Thick line means river; triangles mark mountains. | Thin boundary lines mean rivers.

Wrong: Thin boundary lines mean rivers. Error A. The thick line is the river mark.

C1-E39-4

Shade Newfoundland and Labrador, Chiapas, and Haiti on Map B.

Shade a Newfoundland and Labrador within Canada, b Chiapas within Mexico, and c Haiti, the western country on the island shown. Do not shade the whole containing country or the eastern neighbor.

Teaching sequence: Look for: Shade a Newfoundland and Labrador within Canada, b Chiapas within Mexico, and c Haiti, the western country on the island shown. Do not shade the whole containing country or the eastern neighbor.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: Each target outline shaded separately. | Whole Canada shaded for Newfoundland and Labrador.

Wrong: The whole country of Canada is shaded. Error E. The index target is a division within Canada.

C1-E39-5

Explain province and state using the Canadian and Mexican places you marked.

Newfoundland and Labrador is a province, a division within Canada. Chiapas is a state, a division within Mexico. Neither is itself an independent country. Accept equivalent wording.

Teaching sequence: Look for: Newfoundland and Labrador is a province, a division within Canada. Chiapas is a state, a division within Mexico. Neither is itself an independent country. Accept equivalent wording.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: The province is part of Canada; Chiapas is a state within Mexico. | A province is a separate country.

Wrong: A province is a separate country. Error E. Use containment, without treating a division as independent.
