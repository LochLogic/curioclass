Day 41 teacher-only answer key

Keep this sheet separate from every student packet. Use one scale per component: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Access support is separate from content help. No points or totals.

Record columns: assigned route; repair components; exit supported claim; exit limit; access/help; speaker names; next step. Only the assigned route is marked.

C1-K41-1

Repair this made-up key record: triangles mean water.

Triangles mark hills in the supplied teaching key.

Teaching sequence: Read this key: triangles mark hills; waves mark water.; Match the record to the key.; Replace water with hills.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: Triangles mark hills in the supplied teaching key. | Triangles mean water.

Wrong: Triangles mean water. Error A. The supplied teaching key says hills.

C1-K41-2

Repair this made-up bar record: 3 bars mean 3 kilometers.

Each bar is 50 kilometers; 3 bars represent 150 kilometers.

Teaching sequence: Read 50 kilometers on each bar.; Add 50+50+50.; Write 150 kilometers.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: Each bar is 50 kilometers; 3 bars represent 150 kilometers. | 3 kilometers.

Wrong: 3 kilometers. Error M. Count represented units, not bars.

C1-K41-3

Repair this made-up grid record: latitude first means a column number.

Latitude is read from the north/south margin; longitude from the east/west margin. The point is their crossing.

Teaching sequence: Read the north/south label first.; Read the east/west label second.; Follow both lines to their crossing.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: Latitude is read from the north/south margin; longitude from the east/west margin. The point is their crossing. | Latitude is the column number.

Wrong: Latitude is the column number. Error G. Use the labeled margin, not a letter-grid habit.

C1-K41-4

Repair this made-up claim: a path map proves the garden smells nicest.

A path map shows paths; it cannot establish nicest smell.

Teaching sequence: Identify what the map records: paths.; Identify the claim: a preference about smell.; Keep the claim separate from the map evidence.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: A path map shows paths; it cannot establish nicest smell. | A path map proves nicest smell.

Wrong: A path map proves nicest smell. Error C. The source does not record that preference.

C1-P41-A

Repair the made-up Harbor Map caption using its key and north arrow.

The dotted area is reeds. The square is west of the circle: north points down, so page-right is west. Both caption repairs needed.

Teaching sequence: Look for: The dotted area is reeds. The square is west of the circle: north points down, so page-right is west. Both caption repairs needed.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: Dots mark reeds; the square is west of the circle. | Dots are water; the square is east.

Wrong: The square is east of the circle. Error B. Use the downward north arrow.

C1-P41-B

Measure U to V. Find its distance using this new scale bar.

315 kilometers: U-V is 3 centimeters, bar 2 centimeters represents 210 kilometers; one bar plus half: 210+105. Accept 294 to 336 for 2.8 to 3.2 centimeters.

Wrong: 210 kilometers Error M. Omits the half-bar portion.

C1-P41-C

Repair the two marked points using the coordinate labels.

Move A from 60 N, 50 W to 50 N, 60 W. Move B from its cell to the 40 N, 70 W crossing. Each correction uses the printed target record.

Teaching sequence: Look for: Move A from 60 N, 50 W to 50 N, 60 W. Move B from its cell to the 40 N, 70 W crossing. Each correction uses the printed target record.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: A and B correctly moved to their target crossings. | A remains at the reversed crossing.

Wrong: A stays at 60 N, 50 W. Error G. Read target latitude then longitude.

C1-P41-D

Judge the made-up shelter leaflet using the separate grounds map.

The shelter is east of the pond as claimed: map places it to the right with north up. The map cannot show that it is the quietest place. Both the location support and the preference limit are needed.

Teaching sequence: Look for: The shelter is east of the pond as claimed: map places it to the right with north up. The map cannot show that it is the quietest place. Both the location support and the preference limit are needed.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: East is supported by the shelter right of the pond; quietest is not mapped. | The right location proves quietest.

Wrong: The right location proves quietest. Error C. A location detail cannot establish a preference.

C1-P41-CP

Repair the division card using its reference.

New Brunswick is a province within Canada; Durango is a state within Mexico. Neither is an independent country.

Teaching sequence: Look for: New Brunswick is a province within Canada; Durango is a state within Mexico. Neither is an independent country.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: Both are divisions within their named countries. | Both are independent countries.

Wrong: Both are independent countries. Error E. Distinguish a division from its containing country.

C1-X41

Use the new map to judge the card. Explain one supported claim and one limit.

Supported: the footbridge is north of the reeds, using the upward north arrow and the bridge above reeds. Limit: the map cannot show that the bridge is everyone's favorite place. Accept equivalent supported reasoning.

Teaching sequence: Look for: Supported: the footbridge is north of the reeds, using the upward north arrow and the bridge above reeds. Limit: the map cannot show that the bridge is everyone's favorite place. Accept equivalent supported reasoning.

Samples, first shows the component, second needs work: The bridge is above the reeds with north up; favorite is not mapped. | The map proves everyone likes the bridge.

Wrong: The map proves everyone likes the bridge. Error C. Separate a mapped location from an unmapped preference.
