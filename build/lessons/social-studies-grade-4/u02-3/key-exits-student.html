Section 2.3 teacher keys

Teacher only. Read before class; keep out of student packets. One scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Access support is not content help.

s02-X27

Most fun is preference; 3 conflicts with 2. Check measurement date/method/place; neither automatically true.

Look for: Separates preference; acknowledges unresolved conflict.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Most fun is preference; the widths disagree, so I must check them. | The leaflet is printed, so 3 is right.

s02-X28

Say inland, citing marker away from coast.

Look for: Source-grounded correction for listener.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: I will say inland because the marker is away from the coast. | Coastal sounds better.

s02-X29

Two stops supported by map. Replace one tram with three, citing depot list.

Look for: Supported strength and sourced repair.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Two stops fits the map; the depot lists three trams, so replace one with three. | It is good because it is short.

s02-E7

Individual six-topic folder: people, languages, government, cities, land, industries. Log map, atlas, article and printed digital page with maker/date/kind/context/relevance. Include scoped dated population/language information, offices/functions, city locator, natural resource, landmark, analyzed resource/work relationship and coherent order. Score revised components separately.

Look for: Four source kinds; six accurate sourced topics; dated scope; map/resource/landmark; analyzed relationship; organization.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: A six-topic folder links findings to sources and explains water supporting agriculture. | Favorite places without source links or coverage.

s02-E8

Supported finding naming source; relevant evidence-based reply. Thirty 45-second teacher-heard finding slots in 23 minutes. Paired replies: 45 seconds each within revision's last two minutes; retain question/reply slips. Same scale for private/signed/aided response.

Look for: Source-linked finding; relevant reply on retained slip.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: The office card names lawmakers' job; my reply points to that detail. | Best because I like it.

s02-P27

Practice six-topic draft with map, four kinds and analyzed relationship. Feedback only; score revision on 29.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Topic-ordered report links findings to sources. | A list without sources.

s02-P28

Practice finding; thirty 45-second teacher-heard slots in 23 minutes. Feedback before 29.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: My card explains courts' job. | I like it.

s02-P29

Criterion-linked order improvement; no accuracy rehearsal.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Put the city heading before its details. | I like it.

s02-P29b

Relevant missing sourced detail, with topic.

Look for: Fresh component evidence; record support and next step.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: City note needs a source. | Longer is always better.

s02-P28q

Retained paired exchange; feedback only.

Look for: Fresh component evidence; record support and next step.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Which source? The office card supports it. | No source needed.

s02-P29q

Retained evidence for E8, scored once.

Look for: Fresh component evidence; record support and next step.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: This card supports the office's job. | I like it.
