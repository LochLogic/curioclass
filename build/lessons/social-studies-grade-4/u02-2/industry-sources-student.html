Industry research cards

Canada, 2024 survey description

Canada's statistical agency studies lumber produced and shipped by Canadian manufacturers. Sawmills are part of the wood industry. Wood supplies material for lumber production. This source describes an industry, not every resident's work.

Original summary: Sawmills survey, Statistics Canada.

Mexico, 2021 report

Mexico's water agency records water allocated to agriculture. Agriculture is work growing crops and raising animals. Water supplies a resource needed for growing crops. This does not show every resident's job.

Original summary: water report, chapter 3, CONAGUA.
