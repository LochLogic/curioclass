Government office cards

Canada

Form: parliamentary democracy within a constitutional monarchy. Head of government: prime minister; the government needs the elected House's confidence. Lawmakers: Parliament makes laws. Courts: interpret laws and resolve disputes.

Original factual summary: democratic institutions and court system.

Mexico

Form: federal presidential republic. Head of government: president leads the executive branch. Lawmakers: Congress makes laws. Courts: interpret laws and administer justice.

Original factual summary: political system.

United States

Form: federal presidential republic. Head of government: president leads the executive branch. Lawmakers: Congress makes laws. Courts: interpret and apply laws.

Original factual summary: government branches.

Confidence means support to continue governing. Executive means carrying out laws. Read each office separately. No current leaders or judge-selection details are needed.
