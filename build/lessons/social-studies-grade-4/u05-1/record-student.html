Unit 5 component record

Teacher only. Copy a grid for each day or assessment block. Shown: the child demonstrates the component independently. Shown with help: content prompting was needed. Not yet seen: no usable evidence or a contradiction after access support. Leave a blank for work not observed. Neutral reading, a scribe or home-language rehearsal alone is access, not content help. Never average components.

Record each named look-for separately. Use the item id plus a short component label. Keep date, content help and next move beside the evidence.

Public-turn roster

Assign six children per day across each five-day cycle: 80 to 84, 85 to 89, 90 to 94, 95 to 99. On 100 and 101 continue the rotation. A pass is always available. Each single ask allows about 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes for six children. Every child submits paper; return written evidence the next day. Day 103 hears at most four children per five-minute group, with written moves and same-day paper feedback for all others.
