Section 2.4 teacher keys

Teacher only. Read before class; keep out of student packets. One scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Access support is not content help.

s02-X30

Complementary parts; jar makers need more imported lids, lid sellers need those purchases.

Look for: Specialization and increased deliveries/purchases.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Jar makers need more lids; lid sellers need those purchases. | Each country makes everything alone.

s02-X31

Canada exports oats; Mexico imports. Growing flows increase buyers' reliance on supplies and sellers' reliance on purchases.

Look for: Correct arrow viewpoints and growth mechanism.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Canada exports, Mexico imports; growing deliveries link buyers and sellers more. | Mexico exports oats to Canada.

s02-X32

Bakers gain reliable inputs; redirected purchases reduce local millers' orders.

Look for: Both mechanisms within country.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Bakers keep baking; millers sell less because purchases moved abroad. | Everyone wins.

s02-X33

Repairers gain customers as costs fall; replacement sellers lose purchases through substitution.

Look for: Both effects explained through changed purchases.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Repairers gain work; replacement sellers lose orders as customers choose cheaper repairs. | All sellers gain.

s02-P30

Paper input; notebook customers buyers. Component labels only.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Paper: input. Customers: buyers. | Paper is the buyer.

s02-P31

Wheat: Canada; vegetables: Mexico. Origins only.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Wheat starts in Canada. | Arrowhead marks origin.

s02-P32

One supplied group's order/price change; not paired gain/loss.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: A printing shop gets another order. | All change alike.

s02-P33

Factory gains packaging orders after buyer expansion, or farm loses orders after buyer switch. Correct one supplied link.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Farm sells less after buyer switches. | National total proves this farm lost its buyer.

s02-P30a

Goods: cocoa. Retrieval only, no real route asserted.

Look for: Fresh component evidence; record support and next step.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Cocoa crosses. | Nothing crosses.

s02-P30b

Pencil, the next-best alternative forgone, not price.

Look for: Fresh component evidence; record support and next step.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: The pencil. | The cocoa.
