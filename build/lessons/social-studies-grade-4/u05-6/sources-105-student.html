Day 105 sources

Source C: royal permission

Henry VII, March 5, 1496. Primary legal record, summarized in our words. The king allows John Cabot to sail under his banners. The grant calls for part of the profits to go to the king. This is royal permission, not residents' agreement. Rights: original factual summary. Record: https://avalon.law.yale.edu/15th_century/cabot01.asp.

Source D: later voyage history

Parks Canada, public historical plaque account. Publication date not stated. This later interpretation summarizes Cabot's voyage for visitors. In 1497, Cabot sails from Bristol seeking a western route to Asia. He reaches North America's east coast. The precise landing place remains uncertain. Rights: original factual summary; no plaque wording or image copied. Record: https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=1036.
