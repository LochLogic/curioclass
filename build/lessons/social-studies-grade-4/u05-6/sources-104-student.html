Source A: dated tool evidence

Research team: Jessi J. Halligan and colleagues, 2016. Place: Page-Ladson, Florida. Researchers found stone tools in undisturbed sediment. Nearby samples date the tool layer to about 14,550 years ago. This card summarizes their finding in our words.

Proposed claim to test, made up: All people arrived through an inland passage after 13,000 years ago.

Rights: original factual summary. Research report: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4928949/.

Source B: a voyage letter

Original writer: Christopher Columbus. Document date: February 15, 1493. Immediate recipient: Luis de Santangel. This is our factual summary, not the letter's words.

Columbus reports on his voyage. He promises gold to the rulers if they give him more help.

The original letter is a primary source about his report and request. A university now hosts a later translation. Its hosting date is not the letter's date.

Rights: factual summary only; no translation wording copied. Record: https://ems.kcl.ac.uk/content/etext/e022.html.
