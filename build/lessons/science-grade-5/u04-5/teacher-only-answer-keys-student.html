s04-Q3

Answer: Ten points: sun is star (1); sun much nearer and R/S differently distant (2); controlled trial evidence (2); spreading paths explain less light per fixed patch farther away (3); disk supports larger apparent size, lamp supports greater apparent brightness nearby (2). No R/S brightness ranking from distance alone or equation.

Look-fors: category /1 | distances /2 | controlled trial /2 | spreading and reason /3 | apparent size/brightness /2

Meets: Sun is a star much nearer: minutes versus R six/S eleven years. Lamp output, patch and method match: 9 units at 70 cm versus 1 at 210 cm. My diverging paths show less light per fixed patch farther away. Equal disk covers more image nearby. These support the nearer sun appearing larger and brighter, without ranking R/S. | Not yet: The sun is not a star. (error E)
