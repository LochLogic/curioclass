Day 79 independent sky records: key

#PartQuestionAnswerWrong answers and what they mean
185-ESS1-2 (shadows)Invented for practice: new northern site, fixed stick, local solar time. At 09:00, noon and 15:00, lengths are 54, 18 and 54 cm. Directions are northwest, north and northeast. Graph lengths with time-keyed direction arrows. Explain the daily pattern using your model.Five points: bars 54,18,54 cm (2); NW,N,NE arrows keyed by time (1); middle shortest and changing direction (1); spin changes apparent sun position (1). Zero baseline, time and centimeter labels. At 6 cm per interval, bar heights are 9,3,9 intervals.Shadow direction never changes.: error B, a fixed stick's shadow never changes direction through a day. (error B: Misses timed direction changes.)
195-ESS1-2 (daylight)Invented for practice. January, April, July and October daylight lasts 10, 13, 15 and 11 hours. Darkness lasts 14, 11, 9 and 13 hours. Graph both series. Explain their seasonal comparison and daily light-dark cycling.Five points: paired bars 10/14,13/11,15/9,11/13 (2); month/series/hour labels (1); July five more daylight and five fewer dark hours than January (1); spin takes site into/out of sunlight daily (1). Pairs total 24. No seasonal mechanism required.Every month has equal daylight duration.: error C, every month has equal daylight duration. (error C: Replaces evidence with a fixed twelve-hour rule.); Earth's yearly orbit causes the daily light-dark cycle.: error G, earth's yearly orbit causes the daily light-dark cycle. (error G: Uses yearly motion for a daily pattern.)
205-ESS1-2 (stars)Invented for practice. Four clear nights per month share place, clock time and unobstructed horizon. January, April, July and October counts for A are 4, 4, 0 and 0. Counts for B are 0, 0, 4 and 4. Graph both groups' visibility. Explain the seasonal contrast using your model.Five points: two-series graph A=4,4,0,0 and B=0,0,4,4 (2); month/group/key labels (1); contrast with counts (1); Earth orbit changes outward matched night view (1). Zero means below this horizon then, not nonexistent. One symbol per observed night permitted.Daily spin alone explains these seasonal records.: error D, daily Earth spin alone explains different seasonal night-star records. (error D: Confuses daily and yearly changes.)
215-ESS1-2 (moon)Invented for practice. The moon changes position against background stars across a week. It returns near the first group about a month later. Explain this record using your model.One point: moon own orbit around Earth changes background position and permits approximate monthly return. Earth also spins/orbits, but Earth orbit alone insufficient. No phases.Earth orbit alone makes moon circle Earth.: error H, earth's orbit alone makes the moon circle Earth. (error H: Omits the moon own orbit.)
