Teacher practical-action checklist

Teacher only. Initial personally observed actions; keep actual failed readings. Supplied numbers earn no practical credit. Record missing coverage separately.

Each day: one minute directions, six four-minute visits, two final-wash minutes, one collection minute. Earlier groups wash during later visits, one group per two-minute slot at two sinks. Then seventeen independent paper minutes.

59: five 45-second personal turns, fifteen reset seconds per visit. Observe tare/weigh, length and magnet actions.

60: three paired trials, 15 mL water per cup, 1 g salt per treatment. Fix temperature. Stir twenty seconds; observe thirty seconds afterward. Stagger starts. Three children mix; two perform named control-measuring or recording actions. All independently plan and record starting/resulting properties.
