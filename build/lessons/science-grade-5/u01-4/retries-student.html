Day 17: one assigned fresh retry

Print only the assigned route for each student.

Route A

Invented for practice. A sealed empty-looking float keeps water outside. Draw what occupies it and explain its unseen scale.

Route B

Invented for practice. A sealed sand mixer and cradle read 126 g. After turning, the mixer reads 118 g; its cradle reads 8 g. Graph comparable totals and explain.

Route C

Invented for practice. A sealed gel pack changes appearance while cooling. Complete readings are 113 g twice. Graph the evidence with a conservation caption.

Route D

Invented for practice. A supplied record shows clear cleaner liquid drying to solid. Draw its earlier clear stage with an evidence caption.

Route E

Invented for practice. A closed liquid kit reads 108 g, then 105 g. No spill, leak or scale checks were recorded. Explain why the drop alone cannot identify escaping gas.
