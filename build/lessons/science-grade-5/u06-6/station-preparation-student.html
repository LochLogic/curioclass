Teacher preparation for day 116

Teacher only. Keep the problem, raw records and all catcher images concealed before day 116. Prepare two identical shallow channels inside the 16 cm by 8 cm footprint. Change only barrier height. Edges must be blunt, covered and stable on a collection tray. Use only classroom clean soil and water. Do not collect environmental runoff. Prepare six freshly stirred 20 mL portions per group, an unused 1 mL measuring cup and timer. Prewet/drain both equally; establish one pour position and repeatable reset. Rehearse six cycles safely inside four minutes before offering the practical path. If turnaround, supervision, protective equipment or washing cannot fit, select the held backup record. No desired result is supplied during actual testing.

Four-minute station: 45 seconds simultaneous paper planning; six 25-second cycles; thirty seconds fault notes; fifteen seconds finish. Each child pours once in the first five cycles; sixth repeats the first. Teacher records actual named actions on the separate action roster. No feedback or points during sitting. Two minutes initial safety and four distributed transition minutes are outside observed turns. Only the active group handles. Waiting students work independently and wash at supervised nearby places afterward. Each child has individual goggles and nonlatex gloves. Never share goggles between station turns. Last-group cleanup: one waste/surface, one parallel goggle care, three soap/water washing minutes. Keep sediment out of sinks. Stop for missing protection, damage, uncontrolled spills or failed timing.
