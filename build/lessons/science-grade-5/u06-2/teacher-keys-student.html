Section 6.2: teacher keys

Teacher only. Keep separate before copying. Equivalent supported answers count. Language support does not establish a science error.

s06-X103

Invented for practice. A display filter removes visible paper flecks from classroom water. It may use one mesh square and one frame. Show a measurable goal and the material limit in a design-cycle drawing.

Key: Example: Fewer counted flecks after filtering equal samples. At most one mesh square and one frame. Link plan, build, test, compare, change and retest to the measured goal and limits. Accept another workable visible-fleck or volume criterion.

Look-fors: Measurable goal; material limit; test-to-change-and-retest route | Shape: measurable goal and limit within a cycle drawing

Accept another workable visible-fleck or volume criterion. | Not yet: Clear output proves safe drinking water. Error D.

  • Clear output proves safe drinking water. Error D: Appearance does not establish drinking safety.

s06-X105

Invented for practice. Two model litter baskets differ in hole spacing. They receive stirred water containing equal-sized paper scraps. Write a fair repeated-test plan explaining your measurements and reason for repetition.

Key: Change hole spacing only. Fix input volume, scrap amount/size, mixture, pour position, duration and reset. Alternate at least three trials each. Record volume in a stated unit and escaping scrap counts. Repeats reveal variation; retain every failure.

Look-fors: Changed feature; workable controls; three trials each; volume units and scrap counts; variation rationale | Shape: complete repeated-test specification with measures and rationale

Samples: Meets: Change hole spacing only. Fix input volume, scrap amount/size, mixture, pour position, duration and reset. Alternate at least three trials each. Record volume in a stated unit and escaping scrap counts. Repeats reveal variation; retain every failure. | Not yet: Give baskets different water amounts. Error E.

  • Give baskets different water amounts. Error E: A second changing condition confounds comparison.
  • One good trial proves the best basket. Error F: One result does not establish repeated performance.

s06-X104-1

Invented for practice. A model screen must retain at least four floating squares. It must collect at least 100 milliliters. Both proposals use the allowed frame and mesh. Predicted R: five squares retained, 80 milliliters collected. Predicted S: four squares retained, 120 milliliters collected. Compare both predictions with every goal and limit.

Key: R meets retention/material limits but misses volume. S predicts meeting every stated goal and limit.

Look-fors: Compare both supplied forecasts with every goal and limit | Shape: Compare both supplied forecasts with every goal and limit

Samples: Meets: R meets retention/material limits but misses volume. S predicts meeting every stated goal and limit. | Not yet: One forecast proves S always works. Error F.

  • One forecast proves S always works. Error F: One result does not establish repeated performance.
