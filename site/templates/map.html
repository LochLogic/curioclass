About this order

The order is our own suggestion for arranging our own modules. It is not a pacing guide or a requirement, and it is not endorsed by the authors of any standards or curriculum named here. Move stops to fit your school's calendar. The map shows standard codes and our own module titles only, never the standards' text.

Each region page says where its order came from:

  • Math K-11: the unit order of Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, first edition, by Illustrative Mathematics, licensed CC BY 4.0, used as a reference for which topics come first. "Builds on" links follow each math domain from one grade to the next, as described in the Progressions for the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics (Common Core Standards Writing Team, 2023 edition, licensed CC BY 4.0). Grade 12 courses follow their framework's unit order.
  • Science 6-8: based on the grade placements in NGSS Appendix K (Conceptual Progressions model). Kindergarten to grade 5 and high school follow what each topic needs first.
  • English language arts: foundational skills early, then reading modules, each followed by the writing or speaking module that uses it, with language modules spread through the year.
  • Social studies: expanding horizons in the early grades (self, family, school, community, state), then chronological order, with skills, civics and economics modules next to the era or topic they serve.
  • Every other subject: the framework's own order, course by course.
