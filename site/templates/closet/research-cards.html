How to use a research card
  1. Read page 1 together. Point to each picture word and talk about the words to know.
  2. On page 2, the child finds each answer on page 1 and writes or draws it. That is research: finding facts in a source.
  3. Finish with "I wonder" and where to look next: a library book, a grown-up, a museum or a trusted website.
