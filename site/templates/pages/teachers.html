Using Curio Class

Four ways to get a tool in front of students.

On the big screen

Open any tool and press Play full screen. Every K-2 tool is designed for you to drive at the front of the room while the class calls out answers.

On student devices

Share the tool's link. It runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks, with nothing to install and no student accounts.

In your LMS

Every tool page has an Embed tab with a code you can paste into Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology or a class website.

Offline

Press Download to save the tool as one HTML file. Put it on a shared drive or a USB stick and it works with no internet at all.
