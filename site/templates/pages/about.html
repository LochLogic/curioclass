About Curio Class

A cabinet of free, tactile digital tools for K-12 classrooms.

The Maker

Hi, I'm Cory. I build Curio Class in my spare time for the educators doing the heavy lifting in our classrooms every day. The project is dedicated to my mom, brother, and sister, teachers who proved to me that real learning starts with curiosity and kindness is priceless.

That's me in the photo with my wife, Sara, and our daughter, Emily, on a recent trip to Tokyo. Sara competes at the highest level in international roller derby, and Emily spends her days diving into music, learning, and pouring her whole heart into her friends.

I've always liked building things: community websites, laser-engraved work, physical prototypes, and lightweight software designed to do one specific job really well. Curio Class is the biggest thing I've built, and easily the one I care most about. Every single resource starts with one standard: What would make an entire room of kids lean forward and ask to do it again?

Why It's Free (For Real)

Education shouldn't have a paywall! Teachers already burn through their own evenings and empty their own pockets just to keep their rooms supplied. Quality interactive material shouldn't be another item on your credit card statement or one more password to manage.

  • No Paywalls: No premium tiers, "freemium" trials, or district billing gates.
  • Zero Student Tracking: No user accounts, cookies, ads, or background telemetry. There is literally no student data to collect, harvest, or lose.
  • True Open Access: Content is open under CC BY 4.0 and underlying code is MIT licensed. Copy it, modify it, and share it, just include an attribution back to the project.

Built for the Actual Classroom

School tech environments are chaotic: patchy Wi-Fi, locked-down Chromebooks, temperamental projectors, and strict 45-minute blocks. Curio Class tools are built to thrive in that reality.

  • Single-File, Works Offline: Every tool lives inside a single, self-contained HTML file. Launch it in any browser, throw it on a smartboard, or distribute it on a USB drive without needing an active internet connection.
  • Exploration Over Drills: No sterile quiz banks or multiple-choice treadmills. These are manipulative environments, like rotatable 3D periodic tables, tactile counters, and dynamic balance scales.
  • Grade-Targeted Design: Kindergarten through 2nd-grade modules are teacher-led with high-contrast touch targets and clean read-aloud support. Upper grades get sandbox-style investigations mapped directly to national standards frameworks.
  • Transparent Craft: Each module is designed, vetted, and built with modern developer workflows, openly documenting the source of every asset, audio file, and dataset used.
