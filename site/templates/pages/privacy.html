Privacy

The short version: we don't collect anything about students or teachers. The only thing we keep is a message you choose to send us.

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no logins.
  • No ads, no analytics scripts, no tracking cookies.
  • Tools never send what a student types, draws or records anywhere. Anything a tool saves (a setting, a drawing) stays in that browser on that device.
  • Our fonts and files are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn't contact Google or other third parties.
  • If you email cory@curioclass.org, your message and address are used only to reply and to improve the tools. They're never shared or added to a mailing list.
  • The message box on the home page stores only what you send: the message, what it's about, and, if you choose, who you are and an email for a reply. It doesn't store your IP address or anything about your device. Messages are read by the Curio Class team, used only to fix and improve the site, and never published or shared. Students shouldn't add an email.
  • Our web host keeps standard server logs for security and counts page requests in aggregate. We don't use them to identify anyone.

Because nothing is collected, schools don't need a data agreement to use Curio Class.
