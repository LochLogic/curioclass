## 4. Media arts Cn10 and Cn11: a labeling note

The 2021 NCAS media arts "at a glance" handout prints the two Connecting anchors swapped against every other
discipline:
- Its MA:Cn10 holds the societal, cultural and online safety indicators.
- Its MA:Cn11 holds "use personal experiences and choices".

I followed the NCAS anchor definitions instead, which are the same in all five disciplines:
- **Anchor 10** synthesizes and relates knowledge and personal experiences to make art.
- **Anchor 11** relates art to societal, cultural and historical context.

So in this folder, MA:Cn10 means personal connection (Field Trip Slideshow, Cartoon Soundtrack Mixer, Morning Show
Crew, Mini Documentary Crew). MA:Cn11 means society, culture, ethics and media safety (Snack Ad Questions, Toy Ad
Reality Check, Viral Video Breakdown, fair-use-review). If Cory reads the handout literally, they would swap those
two codes on these modules. The cell counts would not change, because every band then has both.
