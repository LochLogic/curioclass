# Lens: mathematics

Pre-work P5a (docs/PRODUCTION-PATH.md), written 2026-11-13. The expert reads this before any math progression,
course frame, scope and sequence, unit plan or day. Math already has the most on file, so this lens points there
and adds only what is missing. The standard is the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics
(data/standards/ccss-math-standards.txt, ccss-math-clusters.txt, ccss-math-focus.csv). Illustrative Mathematics
K-12, 1st edition (CC BY 4.0) is the base text through Algebra 2; past Algebra 2 is Curio Class's own.

## What good teaching of math looks like, in one paragraph

Most of the year goes to the grade's major work. Each topic grows from the grade before along its progression,
inside the standards' limits. Understanding, fluency and application are each built where the standards call for
them. The eight practices are developed on purpose, tied to content. New ideas come with a model that is true to
the mathematics and later dropped, and are taught clearly before they are practised. Errors are expected,
named in advance and used.

## Errors reviewers catch

**Progression**
- A skill owned by two grades at the same depth, or a handoff that skips a grade.
- A model (tape diagram, number line, area model) used before the grade that builds it.
- A standard algorithm expected before its fluency grade (add and subtract grade 4, multiply grade 5, divide 6).
- A limit broken (grade 4 fraction denominators, grade 3 products within 100).

**Course frame**
- Major work under the line, counted by units, by days or by time.
- A supporting cluster that does not practise major work.
- A source map row with no keep, split, merge, cut or ours decision.

**Scope and sequence**
- A standard taught in part, with no later day that finishes it.
- No problems that join units on purpose.
- Fluency named but never given spaced, mixed practice.

**Unit**
- No practice named, or a practice with no task where students do it.
- Understanding, fluency and application unbalanced with no reason.
- An assessment item with no standard, or a unit assessment that misses part of a standard.

**Day**
- The exit ticket misses the day's goal.
- A manipulative with no tie to the written method.
- A countdown timer shown to a student.

## Gaps this lens sees beyond the rubric

- **No high school bar.** RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md covers K to 8 only. Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2 and the courses past
  them need the high school criteria (the same reviewer's high school tool, the publishers' criteria for high
  school, the modelling standards) before their frames are read.
- **Progression pages exist only for grades 3 to 5.** K to 2, 6 to 8 and high school need the same handoff work;
  the Progressions documents cover every domain from counting and cardinality to high school modelling.
- **Past Algebra 2 has no base text and no progression.** Precalculus, statistics and calculus need a named
  source for order and limits before their frames are written.
- **The K to 2 fluency and counting work** (counting and cardinality, add and subtract within 5, 10, 20) has no
  page on how it is checked without a timer.

## What this lens does not cover

Scoring (the rubric). Arithmetic in keys and items (lesson.py check, BASELINE rule 27). Coverage and day counts
(plan.py check). Tool design (the teaching test).
