# Lens: career education

Subject pre-work, 2026-11-16, in the shape of the core lenses (P5a, docs/PRODUCTION-PATH.md). The expert reads this
before any career education frame, scope and sequence, unit plan or day. It is a way of seeing, not a bar: the bar is
part 7 of docs/ADOPTION-BAR-SUBJECTS.md. The standard is in docs/courses/career-education/STANDARD.md. Two courses:
Career Exploration (6 to 8) and Career Pathways (9 to 12).

## What good teaching of career education looks like

**Self, then world of work, then plan, then action.** Students learn about their own interests, strengths, values and
work styles; then about real jobs and the fields they sit in; then they make a plan that links the two; then they do
something with it (a task from a field, an interview, a portfolio piece). Each unit moves along that line, and the
plan is revised through the year, not filled in once.

**Real jobs, real figures, with dates.** Pay, training, outlook and daily tasks come from public labour data with a
year on every figure. Students compare jobs on more than pay: training time and cost, hours, conditions, local
demand. Variation by place is shown.

**Doing the work of a field.** A job study is not a poster. Students try a task the field does (read a blueprint,
plan a menu at a cost, triage a case on paper, test a design) and defend a decision with data.

**Employability skills taught directly, and in context.** Communication, teamwork, reliability and problem solving are
modelled, practised in tasks and judged with criteria, as the 2024 career ready practices frame them; never a list on
a wall.

**Many paths, no ranking of people.** Apprenticeship, the military, two-year and four-year study, certificates and
starting work are presented as real routes with their costs and returns. No path is shown as for "other people".
Gender and background stereotypes about jobs are named and countered with real workers from many groups.

**Honest about what a school adds.** Work-based learning, counselors, local employers and equipment matter most and
are outside the page; the course says what to add and gives a paper path when a school cannot.

## Errors reviewers catch

**Course frame and scope and sequence**
- Clusters listed and toured with no personal plan made or revised.
- All 14 clusters given equal one-day slots with no depth anywhere (Exploration), or one cluster only (Pathways).
- No applied task from a field in a unit; no data source named.
- A practice claimed with no task that practises it.

**Unit**
- A wage or outlook figure with no source or year; old figures; national figures shown as local.
- The end-of-unit assessment is vocabulary or a quiz about clusters, with no plan, demonstration or portfolio piece.
- An interest quiz that tells a student what job to do, or a scored instrument adapted without its licence's
  conditions.
- A company, platform, credential vendor or job board named or promoted.
- One path (usually a four-year degree) treated as the default; a job shown only through one gender or group.

**Day**
- The goal is "learn about the health cluster" rather than a skill or decision.
- A resume or interview task with no model, no criteria and no feedback round.
- A student asked to share private family income, immigration status or similar in class.

## What this lens does not cover

The scored bar. Personal finance (Economics). Technical courses in one field (not on the list). Whether data or a
source may be adapted (BASE-TEXTS.md and the scout).
