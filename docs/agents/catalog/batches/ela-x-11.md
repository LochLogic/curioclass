## side-by-side-reader
- title: Side by Side Reader | format: sort | family: none | stage: spec
- module: ela-3-5-comparing-texts-side-by-side (ela 3-5, Reading Literature): Comparing Texts Side by Side. Compare books in a series, two texts on one topic, and how texts are structured
- module standards: RL.3.9;RI.3.9;RL.4.9;RI.4.9;RL.5.5;RI.5.5
- standard texts (filed wording; a bare cluster is spelled out):
  - RL.3.9: Compare and contrast the themes, settings, and plots of stories written by the same author about the same or similar characters (e.g., in books from a series).
  - RI.3.9: Compare and contrast the most important points and key details presented in two texts on the same topic.
  - RL.4.9: Compare and contrast the treatment of similar themes and topics (e.g., opposition of good and evil) and patterns of events (e.g., the quest) in stories, myths, and traditional literature from different cultures.
  - RI.4.9: Integrate information from two texts on the same topic in order to write or speak about the subject knowledgeably.
  - RL.5.5: Explain how a series of chapters, scenes, or stanzas fits together to provide the overall structure of a particular story, drama, or poem.
  - RI.5.5: Compare and contrast the overall structure (e.g., chronology, comparison, cause/effect, problem/solution) of events, ideas, concepts, or information in two or more texts.
- concept line: Two texts open side by side (two stories in a series, two tellings of one tale from different cultures, two articles on one topic); drag shared details to the middle and differences to each side, then name what each structure does.
