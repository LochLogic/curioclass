## Second tools for major work

1. **Operations with Whole Numbers and Decimals (Desk).** Grocery Run carries fluent multi-digit multiplication,
   division by two-digit divisors, and all four operations on decimals, with three, three and six items, plus a
   personal finance code, in 30 minutes. That is most of a fifth grader's computing year in one sitting. Shape:
   Grocery Run keeps decimals and spending (5.NBT.B.7, NSPFE.Spending), which is what a grocery setting is for; a
   new lesson tool takes whole-number multiplication and division (5.NBT.B.5, 5.NBT.B.6).
2. **Powers of Ten and Decimals (Desk).** Zoom by Tens carries five codes in 25 minutes and two of them only show:
   the tool does the place-value shift and the rounding. Shape: Zoom by Tens keeps place value and powers of ten
   (5.NBT.A.1, 5.NBT.A.2); a new lesson tool takes reading, writing, comparing and rounding decimals (5.NBT.A.3,
   5.NBT.A.4).
3. **Multiplying fractions.** Shrink Ray carries five codes in 25 minutes, and one part (5.NF.B.4a) has one item.
   It is one topic, so it holds for now. If Sara's fix cannot fit every part at five items, area with fractional
   sides (5.NF.B.4b) is the piece to move to a second tool. Not asked now.

Fraction Kitchen, Camp Cook Share Out and Box Packer each hold one topic and stay single.

## What this course taught about shaping

- The same two patterns as grade 4: clusters shared between modules, and one tool carrying a whole computing
  cluster. Both can be listed by script for every math course before anyone reads a spec.
- Shaping before fixing saves real work here: Sara would otherwise have rebuilt Grocery Run and Zoom by Tens
  around loads they are about to lose.
