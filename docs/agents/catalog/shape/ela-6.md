## The finding that matters

Grade 6 ELA is not nine tools. It is 23: nine in grade 6 modules and fourteen in grade 6 to 8 band modules that
each carry a grade 6 standard. Sara's batch fixed the nine. The fourteen band tools are untouched, and every large
gap left in the course sits on one of them. An earlier note in the roadmap said the gap was "too few tools"; that
was wrong. Every one of the 43 standards has a tool that at least practises it. The gap is fourteen unfixed specs.

Shape: a band module belongs to each grade's course it carries a standard for. Its spec must give grade 6 its own
path: grade 6 texts, the grade 6 wording of the standard, five or more items, so a sixth grader is taught the
grade 6 standard and not an average of three grades. These fourteen specs are Sara's next batch, ahead of any
other course.

## What this course taught about shaping

- A course is its own modules plus every band module that carries one of its standards. The fix list and Sara's
  batches should be cut that way, not by the grade on the module.
- Check where a gap sits before calling it a missing tool. Here it looked like too few tools and was unfixed specs.
