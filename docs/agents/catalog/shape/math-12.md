## Modules with two topics

The rule: one module, two tools when the topics sit in separate modes or cannot both be taught in one sitting of about 35 minutes; otherwise one tool. Separate modes need a spec read, so only the time test is applied here.

- Functions and Their Graphs: the weaker topic is F-IF.C.7, F-IF.C.9. Giving it a model and five items puts Function Family Reunion at about 40 minutes: two tools.
- Matrices: the weaker topic is A-REI.C.9. Giving it a model and five items puts Image Transformer at about 42 minutes: two tools.
