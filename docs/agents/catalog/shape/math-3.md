## Second tools for major work (Desk, all four)

Each is a second lesson tool in the same module. No module is split.

1. **Meaning of Multiplication and Division.** Equal Groups Zoo carries six codes. The two about properties
   (3.OA.B.5) and division as an unknown factor (3.OA.B.6) are only shown, with three items and one. Shape: Equal
   Groups Zoo keeps what multiplying and dividing mean and the word problems (3.OA.A.1 to 3); a new tool takes
   unknowns, properties and unknown-factor division (3.OA.A.4, 3.OA.B.5, 3.OA.B.6). This is the centre of the
   grade and the first place a reviewer looks.
2. **Fractions as Numbers.** Fraction Trail carries seven codes: unit fractions, the number line, and all of
   equivalence and comparing (3.NF.A.3 a to d), which is practice only or shown at four to six items. Shape:
   Fraction Trail keeps unit fractions and the number line (3.NF.A.1, 3.NF.A.2); a new tool takes equivalence and
   comparing (3.NF.A.3). Grade 4 has the same pair of topics in two modules.
3. **Area.** Tile the Floor carries eight codes. Counting unit squares is taught; relating area to multiplication
   (3.MD.C.7 a to c) is shown, with one to four items. Shape: Tile the Floor keeps the idea of area and measuring it
   by counting (3.MD.C.5, 3.MD.C.6) and partitioning shapes (3.G.A.2); a new tool takes area as multiplication and
   addition (3.MD.C.7 a to d).
4. **Time, Volume and Mass.** Two unrelated topics, both major. Kitchen Lab gives mass and volume thirteen items and
   telling time three, with no model. Shape: Kitchen Lab keeps volume and mass (3.MD.A.2); a new tool takes time to
   the minute and elapsed time (3.MD.A.1). See the rule below.

## The rule for a module with two topics

Set after Cory's answer on the grade 4 card (2026-11-10). A module that holds two unrelated standards keeps one
module and gets two tools when either is true, and otherwise keeps one tool:

- the two topics live in separate modes of the tool, so the tool is already two activities under one roof; or
- giving each topic a model and five differing items would push the sitting past about 35 minutes.

By that rule: Time, Volume and Mass gets a second tool (above). Scaled Graphs holds scaled picture and bar graphs
(3.MD.B.3) and measuring to make a line plot (3.MD.B.4): one tool, two codes, one activity (collect, then graph), so
it stays one tool, and the line plot part is a small addition for Sara.
