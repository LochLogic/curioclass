# Pilots: what real people found

Set up 2026-11-11 (Cory accepted the cold strategic review, recommendation 5). Nothing is filed here yet.

This folder holds what happens when a finished unit meets a teacher and a student. One file per test, named
`<course>-u<unit>-<test>-<date>.md`. Cory finds the people. Nothing is sent to anyone without his yes, and no
student's name is written down.

## The three tests on a pilot unit

1. **A teacher of the grade band reads it.** For grade 4 math unit 11.1, a grade 3 to 5 teacher. They get the five
   days, the print and the checks, and the five questions below.
2. **Someone teaches it.** One teacher or one parent runs the days in order and writes down the minutes each part
   really took.
3. **Three students sit the end check.** The adult times it on a watch the student does not see. No countdown is
   shown to a student, ever.

## The five questions for the reading teacher (draft, ours)

1. Could you teach day 1 tomorrow from these pages alone? If not, what is missing?
2. Which day holds more than its minutes? Which holds less?
3. Where would your class get stuck, and does the plan say what to do there?
4. Is anything on the checks unfair, unclear, or not what the days taught?
5. What would you cut, and what would you never cut?

## What each file records

Who (role and grade only), what they were given, the date, the minutes, what they said in their own words, and
what was changed because of it. A finding that changes a template goes to docs/HANDOFF.md the same day.

## The cost sheet

`COST.md` in this folder records, for each pilot unit and each piece of it: tokens, wall hours, owner minutes,
and defects found per read. It is the base of the plan with sizes and dates for all 105 courses.
