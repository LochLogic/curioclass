# Base texts and references: the record

Written 2026-11-13. Decisions: DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-12 (math base text, social studies anchor, science and ELA base
texts). How these become Curio Class days: [INTEGRATION-PLAN.md](INTEGRATION-PLAN.md).

The copies live outside the repository in `S:\CurioClass-sources\` (not committed, not on the site). Each source folder
has `NOTES.md` (what it is, licence, gaps) and `MANIFEST.csv` (file, url, date, bytes, sha256, licence line). The
whole store is catalogued in `S:\CurioClass-sources\_catalog\` (`CATALOG.csv`, `ZIP-CONTENTS.csv`, `STRUCTURE.md`,
`build_catalog.py`), with two analyses: `analysis-math.md` and `analysis-science-ela-ss.md`.

## The two kinds

- **Base text** (top level of the store): CC BY 4.0. We may adapt it into our days, in our voice and format, with a
  credit line on the day. Its name and logo are trademarks and are never used except inside the credit line.
- **Reference** (`reference-only-do-not-copy\`): non-commercial, share-alike, no-derivatives or unclear. For checking
  ideas, order, models and pacing only. Never copy its problems, wording or images into a Curio Class page.

## Credit lines

Long form (teacher page, one per source lesson used):

> Adapted from IM(R) K-12 Math, grade 4, unit 3, lesson 7, (c) 2019-2021 Illustrative Mathematics, [lesson link],
> licensed under CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Changed by Curio Class: [one line on what
> changed].

> Adapted from OpenSciEd Unit [X.Y], [lesson N] (openscied.org), CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
> Changed by Curio Class: [one line].

> Adapted from EL Education K-5 Language Arts Curriculum (2017), grade [G], module [M], unit [U], lesson [L],
> CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Changed by Curio Class: [one line].

Short form (student pages, tickets, foot lines): "Based on [source] by [owner], CC BY 4.0. Changed by Curio Class. Full
credit on the teacher page."

No wording that implies endorsement or a relationship with any source.

## Never carried over from a base text

- Photos and images unless their own line says CC BY or public domain (Pixabay, Pexels, Shutterstock, CC BY-SA, NC and
  all-rights-reserved items are dropped). Check images by eye: uncaptioned ones escape a text scan.
- Videos, slides, posters, logos, platform text and links, sign-in stubs, vendor kit links.
- Third-party items named in `analysis-science-ela-ss.md` section 1.4 (OpenSciEd) and `analysis-math.md` section 2 (IM).
- Recurring character names and routine labels: keep the move, use our words and names.
- Trade books (EL K to 5): replaced by our texts, with their questions and models rewritten.
