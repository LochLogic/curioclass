# Health: the 2007 standards mapped onto the 2024 set

Written 2026-11-11. **This mapping is ours.** It was made by Curio Class for its own use. No standards body or
government agency wrote it, checked it or approved it. All descriptions below are in our own words; for the exact
wording, read the publisher's pages listed at the end.

## Why this note exists

- Curio Class loads the SHAPE America National Health Education Standards, 2024 (`data/standards/nhes-2024.txt`,
  codes NHES.S1 to NHES.S8). Always name that set as "SHAPE America, 2024".
- The federal health curriculum review tool, CDC's HECAT (2021 edition, `data/standards/hecat-topics.txt`), builds
  its knowledge and skill expectations on the older National Health Education Standards, 2nd edition (2007).
- So any time a health unit is looked at through HECAT, it is being read against 2007 expectations. Say both
  editions by name wherever HECAT is used.

## The mapping

Both editions have eight standards in the same order, so the numbers map one to one. No code in
`data/modules.csv` moves.

| 2007 standard | 2024 standard (our code) | Same skill? | What was reworded (our words) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NHES.S1 | Partly. This is the one that moved | 2007 asks students to understand health concepts. 2024 asks them to use health information that is practical enough to act on. The shift is from knowing to using. |
| 2 | NHES.S2 | Yes | 2007 lists the kinds of influence by name (family, peers, culture, media, technology and others). 2024 drops the list and speaks of influences in general. |
| 3 | NHES.S3 | Yes | 2007 speaks of valid information, products and services. 2024 speaks of resources that are both valid and reliable. |
| 4 | NHES.S4 | Yes | 2007 ties communication to improving health and to avoiding or lowering risk. 2024 drops the risk phrase. |
| 5 | NHES.S5 | Yes | 2007 speaks of decision-making skills. 2024 speaks of using a decision-making process. |
| 6 | NHES.S6 | Yes | 2007 speaks of goal-setting skills. 2024 speaks of using a goal-setting process. |
| 7 | NHES.S7 | Yes | 2007 is about practicing healthy behaviors and avoiding or lowering risk. 2024 is about showing healthy practices and behaviors, without the risk phrase. |
| 8 | NHES.S8 | Yes | 2007 names personal, family and community health. 2024 speaks of promoting the health of self and others. |

Changes that run through all eight:

- 2007 states the purpose of each standard as improving health. 2024 states it as supporting the health and
  well-being of the student and of other people. Well-being and "others" are new in every standard.
- 2007 opens most standards by saying students will show an ability. 2024 opens with the action itself.
- The grade spans are the same four (through grade 2, 3 to 5, 6 to 8, 9 to 12), but the indicators under each
  standard were rewritten and renumbered. An indicator number from 2007 does not point to the same indicator in
  2024. This note maps standards only, not indicators.

## How to use it

- Standards 2 to 8: a HECAT skill finding carries straight across to the 2024 standard with the same number.
- Standard 1: do not carry a HECAT knowledge finding across as it stands. HECAT checks whether the right
  concepts are covered. The 2024 standard also asks whether students put the information to use. Reread the
  unit against NHES.S1 itself.
- HECAT's Healthy Behavior Outcomes and topic modules do not depend on the edition and can be used as they are.

## What was checked and what was not

- Checked 2026-11-11 on SHAPE America's own pages: the eight 2007 standards and the eight 2024 standards, read
  side by side. The rewording column comes from that reading.
- Taken from `docs/reviews/standards-currency-2026-11-11.md`, not reopened today: that the HECAT overview names
  the 2007 edition as its source.
- Not done: an indicator-by-indicator comparison of the two editions.
- A different set with the same title exists (National Consensus for School Health Education, 3rd edition,
  2022). It is not loaded and is not part of this mapping.
