# PE: the 2014 five standards mapped onto the 2024 four

Written 2026-11-11. **This mapping is ours.** It was made by Curio Class for its own use. No standards body or
government agency wrote it, checked it or approved it. All descriptions below are in our own words; for the exact
wording, read the publisher's pages listed at the end.

## Why this note exists

- Curio Class loads the SHAPE America National Physical Education Standards, 2024
  (`data/standards/shape-pe-2024.txt`, codes SHAPE.S1 to SHAPE.S4).
- The federal PE curriculum review tool, CDC's PECAT (2019), scores a curriculum against SHAPE America's earlier
  five standards. SHAPE America dates those standards 2013; the book that carries them with grade-level outcomes
  is dated 2014, which is why both years are seen. They are the same five.
- So a PECAT-style look at a PE unit reads it against the older five. Say both editions by name wherever PECAT
  is used.

## The older five, in our words

1. Motor skills and movement patterns: being able to do a range of them well.
2. Movement knowledge: using concepts, principles, strategies and tactics about movement and performance.
3. Fitness: the knowledge and skills to reach and keep a healthy level of activity and fitness.
4. Responsible behavior, personal and social, that respects oneself and others.
5. Valuing physical activity: for health, enjoyment, challenge, self-expression and time with others.

## The 2024 four, in our words

- SHAPE.S1: building a range of motor skills.
- SHAPE.S2: using knowledge about movement and about fitness.
- SHAPE.S3: building social skills through movement.
- SHAPE.S4: building personal skills, seeing what movement gives you, and choosing to be active.

## The mapping

| Older standard | Goes to (2024) | Fit | Notes (our words) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Motor skills | SHAPE.S1 | Direct | 2024 speaks of developing skills; the older wording set a bar of competency. |
| 2 Movement knowledge | SHAPE.S2 | Direct | Carries across whole. |
| 3 Fitness | SHAPE.S2 for what students know about fitness; SHAPE.S4 for choosing to be active and looking after one's own activity | Split | Fitness no longer has a standard of its own. The 2024 wording does not set a fitness level to reach and keep. |
| 4 Responsible behavior | SHAPE.S3 for the social half (working with others, respect, fair play); SHAPE.S4 for the personal half (self-management, effort, safety for oneself) | Split | One older standard became parts of two. |
| 5 Valuing activity | SHAPE.S4 for health, enjoyment, challenge and self-expression; SHAPE.S3 for the time-with-others part | Mostly SHAPE.S4 | 2024 turns valuing into naming personal benefits and making the choice to take part. |

Read the other way:

| 2024 standard | Draws on older standards |
|---|---|
| SHAPE.S1 | 1 |
| SHAPE.S2 | 2, and the knowledge side of 3 |
| SHAPE.S3 | the social half of 4, and the social part of 5 |
| SHAPE.S4 | the personal half of 4, most of 5, and the choosing-to-be-active side of 3 |

## How to use it

- A PECAT finding on older standards 1 or 2 carries straight to SHAPE.S1 or SHAPE.S2.
- A PECAT finding on older standards 3, 4 or 5 has to be split by hand using the table. Do not report it under
  a single 2024 standard.
- Where the split is a judgment call (for example, whether a fitness-planning task is knowledge or a personal
  skill), say which way it was read.
- This note maps standards only. The grade-level outcomes of the older edition and the grade-span indicators of
  the 2024 edition are different lists and are not mapped here.
- The older edition framed every standard around a "physically literate individual"; the 2024 standards do not
  open that way.

## What was checked and what was not

- Checked 2026-11-11 on SHAPE America's own pages: the five older standards and the four 2024 standards, read
  side by side.
- The split of older standards 3, 4 and 5 is our reading of the standard sentences. We did not find a published
  crosswalk from SHAPE America to check it against, and did not compare the indicators one by one.
- Not reopened today: PECAT itself (the CDC addresses we tried returned "not found"). That PECAT 2019 is keyed
  to the five standards is taken from `docs/reviews/standards-currency-2026-11-11.md`.
