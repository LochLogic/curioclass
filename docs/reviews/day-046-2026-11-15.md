## Verdict

The repair half is honest and the content is right: every move matches the unit's error register, the source
details it points to are on the sheets and in Gibb, and the day keeps harms and gains apart, keeps marks private
and names coercion plainly. The biggest risk is that the day does not do the two jobs its plan gives it: the
"review for course-level check 1" practises one skill on unit 3 content while days 47 and 48 test units 1 and 2,
and the response groups cannot be formed from what day 45 records, because the template's marking step is the math
one (self-marked short items, hands up by item) and every item here is teacher-judged by component. The one change
that matters most is to rebuild the review and the exit around a unit 1 or 2 development beside a unit 3 one,
with the correction kept as practice.

### 1. The review does not prepare students for the check it is named for

d046's 15-minute review and its only item, s03-P46-2 ("name two developments that happened at the same time in
different regions, and one network"), practise T1.5b simultaneity, and its sample right answer is unit 3 only
("Mali and the Yuan ruled at the same time; the Indian Ocean routes linked Kilwa to Asia"). Its look-fors (two
developments in the same years, different regions, a network) never ask for a unit 1 or 2 entry, though the
teacher page says "first scan entries from each of units 1 and 2". Course-level check 1 is: day 47, a sourced claim
paragraph from two unseen sources on an interaction drawn from units 1 and 2 (T1.4, D3.1); day 48, a map, parallel
timeline and comparison chart (T1.5a to c) on a unit 1 or 2 network, plus knowledge items across units 1 to 3
(days.csv). Day 46 is the only day between the unit 3 assessment and that check; units 1 and 2 were last taught in
weeks 1 to 6.

- Why it matters: a student meets a units 1 and 2 claim paragraph cold after a "review" that never touched that
  content or that skill; a teacher reading the item's sample will run a unit 3 recap.
- Fix: s03-P46-2 becomes "On the parallel timeline, pick one development from unit 1 or 2 and one from unit 3 in
  different regions. Give each its dated band, say whether they overlap, and name the network (if any) that linked
  them, or say none is recorded." Look-fors: one unit 1 or 2 entry and one unit 3 entry, both dated from the
  timeline | overlap stated or ruled out | network named or its absence stated. Sample right: "Han China
  (206 BCE to 220 CE) and Rome both on the silk routes in the first century CE; Mali's Musa in the 1320s is a
  thousand years later, so they do not overlap." Sample wrong: "Rome and Mali were at the same time." (H, already
  the item's). In d046's review part add one sentence: "Day 47 asks a sourced claim on units 1 and 2: close by
  naming the five checks on one unit 1 source on the timeline." Section plan field 6, day 46: "review units 1 and
  2 entries beside unit 3, for days 47 and 48".
- LAYER day. CATCH expert. REPEAT new. CHEAP missed. ORIGIN unit (items.csv P46-2 and the section plan's day 46 block).
- Confidence: firm (days.csv 47 and 48; the item row).

### 2. The response groups cannot be formed from what day 45 records

d046 says "Sort response groups by the most frequent missed component and error" and lists fifteen moves by error
letter. But day 45 scores each component 0, 1 or 2 (d045, "Mark"; s03 field 5 "Scoring"); the record sheet has one
column a look-for and no error letters (record.html); and s03 field 7 copies the template's marking step from
UNIT-PLAN.md field 7: "Students mark the short items against the key on the screen ... Hands up by item sets the
groups." That is the math shape (self-marked short items with a computed key). In this unit every item is
teacher-judged, nothing is self-marked except the three survey items circled on day 45, and an error letter has to
be read off each answer by the teacher overnight, which no one has budgeted beyond day 45's 40 minutes of scoring.

- Why it matters: a no-prep teacher reading thirty papers cannot sort them into fifteen letter groups between
  day 45 and day 46; in practice the groups will be formed by item, and the move list will go unread.
- Fix (unit): s03 field 7 and the section opener carry a component-to-move table, so the record sheet's 0 or 1
  cells sort students directly: A1 to A; A2 map to B, economy to I, culture to N, politics or limit to L, links
  to K (option b) or D (option d); A2a to H, J; A2b to H, K; A2d to H, D; A3 to C, O; A4 to E; A5a to F; A5b to G;
  X44 to I. Cap the day at four groups (the four most missed components); others work in pairs from the written
  move (this last part is the cheap read's finding 5). Fix (template): UNIT-PLAN.md field 7's marking step gains
  a subject line: "Social studies: items are teacher-judged; the groups come from the record sheet's 0 and 1
  cells by component, through the component-to-move table in this field."
- LAYER process. CATCH expert. REPEAT yes (the first probe's day 79 finding 3, a math rule sending a social studies
  repair where it does not fit). CHEAP missed. ORIGIN template.
- Confidence: firm (UNIT-PLAN.md field 7; d045; record.html).

  ORIGIN unit.
- Confidence: likely (one rule plus reasoning).
