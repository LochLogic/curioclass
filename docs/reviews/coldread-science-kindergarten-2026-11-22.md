## Verdict

Would not pass yet. The course is a real, coherent kindergarten science year with strong models that carry forward (pattern sheet, claim strip, system chart, fair-pair, design cycle). Four things stop it:

1. Unit 2 does not answer its own anchor. s02.md:15 promises a day 17 "closing explanation" that dictates the coat-pile explanation, but day 17 does a chain revision with Denver and Seattle records and never returns to the coat pile. EQuIP I and II for unit 2 land at 2, so the unit totals 6, under the 8 line.
2. S5 (a door) is not clean. The plan-level safety note (s05.md:229, repeated u05-1.md:158, u05-2.md:210) calls for splash goggles at sand, soil and water stations, but the unit 5 day files do not carry it, while unit 3 day 20 does list fitted eye protection. Safety handling is inconsistent across days.
3. Key plant-light evidence (K-LS1-1, unit 4 day 33) rests on telling plus an invented second pot record, and day 35 and the water-source evidence are invented sets, while the plan and days.csv say local dated pictures.
4. Assessment keys that cannot exist before use (unit 5 day 52 real sun and shade cards, unit 2 live records) and S11 has no element-level count of practices and crosscutting concepts across the band.
