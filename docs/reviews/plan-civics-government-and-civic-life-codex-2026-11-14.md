## Unit 3: The Constitution and the three branches

### 3. Moderate: The armed-services hierarchy is inaccurate.

**Location:** `s03.md`, field 5, item 4b’s answer, and U3, exit 39. **Confidence: firm.**

The answer says, “Armed services operate within Defense,” and exit 39 repeats that placement. The Coast Guard is an armed service ordinarily within Homeland Security. This would produce an incorrect executive-branch diagram and answer key. A January 2026 federal report confirms the distinction. [GAO report](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108639).

**Exact fix:**

- **Old:** “Armed services operate within Defense under civilian authority.”
- **New:** “The Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force and Space Force are within Defense. The Coast Guard is ordinarily within Homeland Security and can operate as a service in the Navy under law. All are under civilian authority.”

Make the same correction in exit 39 and day 39’s hierarchy. [14 U.S.C. § 101](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t14/s101/).

Day 46 can then use five minutes directions, five packet reading, thirty-eight for the remaining items and two for checking completeness. Update field 5, field 7 and the day 46 data note to identify the additional retained evidence.

### 6. Moderate: The political-courage task supplies no evidence of political risk.

**Location:** `s05.md`, field 5, item 11 and source N. **Confidence: likely.**

The plan correctly says that an executive order alone cannot establish courage. However, its assessment then stops at that insufficiency. Students never receive the contextual evidence needed to discuss a documented example of political courage under MA.8.T4.11.

**Exact fix:** Add a short, attributed context note to N explaining the threatened Southern Senate filibuster against Truman’s civil-rights proposals and the military resistance to integration. ## Unit 6: Rights over time

### 7. Serious: Congressional protection against sex discrimination drops out of the standard.

**Location:** `s06.md`, field 2, field 5 item 3 and packet Q, and U6 day 82. **Confidence: firm.**

The original MA.8.T5.4 includes congressional civil-rights protections concerning race, gender and disability. The local paraphrase loses that distinction. The assessment then specifies racial public-accommodation protection, voting protection and disability protection, without a sex-discrimination provision. The suffrage amendment and marriage ruling do not demonstrate this part concerning legislation enacted by Congress. [Massachusetts framework, printed page 108](https://www.doe.mass.edu/frameworks/hss/2018-12.pdf).

**Exact fix:** Restore the three categories in field 2. On day 82, distinguish Title II’s public-accommodation protections from Title VII’s employment protections, including sex discrimination.

Add a short reserved Title VII section 703(a)(1) selection to Q. In item 3, require students to explain both protections within the 1964 Act. Extend the answer guide with:

> “The Act also prohibited sex discrimination in covered employment; enforcement was needed to make that protection effective.”

The statutory provision is traceable in the [official Title VII text](https://www.eeoc.gov/statutes/title-vii-civil-rights-act-1964). Use historically appropriate wording when preparing the 1964 packet.

## Unit 9: A free press and the news

### 11. Moderate: Literal question marks replace quotation marks and apostrophes.

**Location:** `s09.md`, throughout the authored prose and exits. **Confidence: firm.**

Examples include `Boston?s`, `publisher?s`, `unit 8?s`, `Riders? Transportation Access Group`, and question-mark delimiters around complete quotations. These are literal characters in the file, not a display problem. They obscure quotation boundaries and would propagate into classroom materials.

**Exact fix:** Restore apostrophes in possessives and paired quotation marks around quoted text. For example:

- `Boston?s evaluation` becomes `Boston's evaluation`.
- `publisher?s indicator` becomes `publisher's indicator`.
- `?This establishes ___, but leaves ___ uncertain.?` becomes `"This establishes ___, but leaves ___ uncertain."`

Preserve genuine question-ending punctuation and URL query strings. Review every occurrence individually.

## Unit 10: Civic action

### 13. Serious: A paper storyboard cannot demonstrate the digital part of D4.3.

**Location:** `s10.md`, field 5 item 3 and individual performance evidence; field 8; U6 days 156 to 158. **Confidence: firm.**

The plan awards equivalent coverage for a paper storyboard when no device is available. D4.3.6-8 explicitly includes presentation through digital technologies as well as print and oral forms. Planning a digital version on paper does not demonstrate presenting one. [C3 Framework, printed page 60](https://www.socialstudies.org/sites/default/files/c3/c3-framework-for-social-studies-rev0617.pdf).

**Exact fix:** Replace:

> “Score audience, wording, layout and source decisions in either path, not equipment access.”

With:

> “Score the print and oral components from either path. Record the digital component separately. A paper storyboard prepares that component but does not demonstrate digital presentation.”

For the device route, use one shared offline digital version with documented student decisions about audience, wording, layout and sources. Retain the paper route and record its digital component as awaiting demonstration. The resulting paper-only coverage question belongs to Cory, as recorded below.
