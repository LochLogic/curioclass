### 1. Likely phantom duplicate id `MU:Re9.1.Kb` and `MU:Cn10.1`/`Cn11.1` vs official `MU:Cn10.0`/`Cn11.0` in the standards files

- **Where:** `data/standards/ncas-performance-standards.txt` lines 698 to 728; `data/standards/ncas-performance-standards-words.txt` lines 635 to 662 and line 381; `docs/courses/arts/STANDARD.md` section 1 and section 3.2 count table.
- **What is wrong:**
  1. `MU:Re9.1.Kb` appears in `ncas-performance-standards.txt` (line 700), making kindergarten the only grade in PK to 8 with two `Re9.1` statements (PK and grades 1 to 8 all have `a` only). In `ncas-performance-standards-words.txt` (lines 635 to 636), `MU:Re9.1.Ka` and `MU:Re9.1.Kb` carry the exact same wording ("With help, judge music by taste and expression."). Web search against the published National Core Arts Standards for Kindergarten Music (`nationalartsstandards.org` and NAfME) shows only `MU:Re9.1.Ka` ("With guidance, apply personal and expressive preferences in the evaluation of music."). Because header line 15 of `ncas-performance-standards.txt` notes that letters were derived by parsing the PDF cell layout rather than copied from a published list, `MU:Re9.1.Kb` is almost certainly a parsing artifact. If removed after checking the 2021-11 PDF, music K drops from 19 to 18, taught music ids from 185 to 184, taught total from 1,114 to 1,113, and file total from 1,202 to 1,201.
  2. Official 2014 NCAS Music Connecting standards are coded `MU:Cn10.0` and `MU:Cn11.0` (for example `MU:Cn10.0.Ka`, `MU:Cn10.0.8a`, `MU:Cn10.0.C.Ia`, `MU:Cn11.0.Ka`), whereas the other four disciplines use `.1`. Curio Class's files use `MU:Cn10.1.*` and `MU:Cn11.1.*` (20 ids across PK to 8) without recording a `.0 -> .1` normalization in header line 18 of `ncas-performance-standards.txt`. Also, in `ncas-performance-standards-words.txt`, all nine grades K to 8 carry the exact same sentence for `MU:Cn10.1` and the exact same sentence for `MU:Cn11.1` (reflecting NCAS's overarching connecting statements), giving writers no grade-by-grade depth ladder for music connecting.
  3. Line 381 of `ncas-performance-standards-words.txt` (`MA:Pr6.1.IIIb`) is 15 words ("Independently judge, compare, and fold in improvements, weighing personal-to-global effects on artist and audience."), exceeding the file's own 14-word cap in header line 9.
- **Cost:** A kindergarten music scope and sequence will plan and assess a non-existent standard (`MU:Re9.1.Kb`), or fail a future id-existence check against the official framework. External reviewers checking music connecting codes against official NCAS tables will see `.1` where NCAS publishes `.0`.
- **Fix:**
  1. Re-open the 2021-11 `Music at a Glance.pdf` cell for Kindergarten `Re9.1`. If only `a.` exists, delete `MU:Re9.1.Kb` from both `ncas-performance-standards.txt` and `ncas-performance-standards-words.txt`, and update the counts in `STANDARD.md` section 1 and section 3.2 (1,201 total, 1,113 taught, music 201 total / 184 taught, music K to 2 = `18 + 20 + 20 = 58`).
  2. Check `MU:Cn10.0` / `MU:Cn11.0` in the same PDF. Either change the 20 codes in both files to `.0` (both `scripts/standards.py` line 107 and `scripts/progression.py` line 121 already accept `\.\d\.`) or add `MU:Cn10.0/Cn11.0 -> Cn10.1/Cn11.1` to header line 18 of `ncas-performance-standards.txt`. Add one line to `STANDARD.md` section 3.1 noting that NCAS Music `Cn10` and `Cn11` share one overarching wording across K to 8, so each grade teaches them at that grade's repertoire and vocabulary depth.
  3. Shorten line 381 (`MA:Pr6.1.IIIb`) in `ncas-performance-standards-words.txt` to at most 14 words:
     > `MA:Pr6.1.IIIb	Independently judge and apply presentation improvements, weighing personal through global effects.`
