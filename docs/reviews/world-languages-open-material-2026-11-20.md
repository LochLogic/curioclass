# World languages: open material scout (2026-11-20)

**World languages are SHELVED (Cory, 2026-11-11; restated 2026-11-20). This scout is pre-work only and adopts
nothing.** Base-text scout for Spanish and American Sign Language, in the shape of docs/reviews/eld-open-material-2026-11-16.md.
The bar is docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md: CC BY 4.0, public domain, or UK OGL v3.0. Share-alike (SA), non-commercial (NC)
and no-derivatives (ND) licences do not meet it. Licences are as stated on the page read 2026-11-20; "search only"
means seen in a search summary, not opened. Nothing was downloaded. Standard: docs/courses/world-languages/STANDARD.md.

## The short answer

**No openly licensed full K to 12 course was found for either language.** For Spanish there are several CC BY college
level books and one CC BY heritage learner set for grades 6 to 12, all useful as reference and partial bases. For
American Sign Language **nothing that meets the bar was found**: the open courses are non-commercial, share-alike or
carry no open licence, and they rely on video, which Curio Class does not use. The courses are written by us.
