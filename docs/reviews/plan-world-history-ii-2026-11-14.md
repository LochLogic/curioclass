## Course verdict

These are strong plans. The history is careful and mostly current:
- Herero and Nama, Armenia, the Holocaust, 1948 as both independence and Nakba, Rwanda and Srebrenica are named plainly and with the right legal precision.
- Codex verified sources more than in the earlier courses, and the plans already carry several dated lines (Goodall's 2025 death, Gambia v. Myanmar, Sudan, Syria after Assad).

The plans are not ready for section writers until three course-wide problems are fixed:
- Ten of the eleven end-of-unit assessments are over their minutes on the settled sizing rule.
- Exit tickets run to three or more parts.
- Units 8 and 9 teach Israel and Palestine and the Middle East "since 1980" with no line on the two largest events of the last three years: the Gaza war since 7 October 2023, and the 2026 war on Iran with Khamenei's death.

Unit 1 handles the start-of-year check correctly. No source is invented.
