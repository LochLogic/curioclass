## Findings (worst first)

1. **Day 12 record sheet cannot hold the two cycles E1 and E2 score.** courses/science-grade-3/u01-3/changed-pendulum-record.md has a 6-row table (Turn 1 to 6) and says "Tick L, C, R for at least two cycles". Two cycles of L, C, R, C is 8 turns, and the student page in d012.md has 8 rows. E1 asks for "at least two cycles" and E2 for "two earlier cycles"; a child on the 6-row sheet cannot earn them. Fix: make the changed-pendulum-record.md table 8 rows, as in d012.md.

2. **E2 "last turn" is ambiguous.** Stem and key: "predicts the side opposite the last turn recorded". The record's rows include center crossings (column "Side (L, C, R)"), and the keyed example record "L C R C L C R C" ends on C, which has no opposite. The key intends the last far turn (R there, so next is L). Fix: in E2, P12-5 and P14-2 write "the next turning side after the last left or right turn you recorded", and in the key "opposite the last left or right turn recorded".

3. **UK spellings print to children.** E1 and E2 stems ("centre crossings", "the centre"), the K9-1 stem ("Centre, 0 cm"), the answers and samples, changed-pendulum-record.md and position-table.md ("centre", "Colour one cycle") use UK spelling while the day pages use "center". Fix to "center" and "Color" (AGENTS.md: fix when next edited).

4. **R1, R2, R3 and K6-1 name pictures that no piece prints.** R1 "Picture 1 / Picture 2", R2 "Three dated rows", R3 "This chart", K6-1 "the first drawing / second drawing" have no figure (figure column empty; readiness.md and feature-menu.md carry text only). All four are fully answerable from the words, so no key is wrong, but a child reading "Picture 2" sees no picture. Fix: supply the small pictures, or reword to words ("First, a gentle push. Second, a stronger push.") and drop "picture" and "drawing".

5. **E3 key fixes the change to direction, but the day 7 plan lets groups choose the unbalanced pair** (feature-menu.md: "two from the front, or one from the front and one from the side"; group-test-record.md has "Our unbalanced pair"). A child who validly changes only strength, or only the number of pushes, with everything else kept, is not described by the key ("The plan changes only one thing, the direction of one push"). Fix: the key reads "one change, direction expected; any single change with the rest kept earns the 2 points".

6. **K11-1 stem leaves out what 12, 11 and 10 cm measure, and uses a flat "baking pan".** Stem: "A marble rolls in a baking pan ... left, 12 cm". The key and the page table call them heights; the stem never says "height". A flat-bottomed baking pan will not rock a marble with shrinking heights. Fix: "A marble rolls in a curved bowl ... left, 12 cm high" (the class uses a bowl), and "high" in each entry.

7. **K8-1 asks about two moments with one sketch and one blank.** The student page (d008.md) gives one {sketch} and one "They cancel: ____" for parts (a) and (b); the key needs a different answer for each. Part (b) also needs "the moment after it is let go". Fix: two sketches and "(a) They cancel: ___ (b) They cancel: ___", and change "is held at the side and let go" to "is held at the side, then let go. Draw its two arrows the moment after it is let go".

8. **R2 and the day 2 repair pictures disagree on direction.** R2: morning sun low on the left. weather-and-sky-pictures.md (day 2 repair): morning low on the right. Not wrong (different invented rows), but a child retaught on day 2 after R2 may carry "left" over. Fix: make the day 2 repair match R2, or say aloud "this time it is on the right".
