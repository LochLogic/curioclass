## Verdict

The seven units form a sound year: routines open on Frankenstein, each later unit names what it keeps from the one before, and the handoffs into grade 11 (the perceive family, evidence, seminar and syntax rows) are all met. The most serious finding is unit 5's field 4, which put W.9-10.9a (literature) on the rhetoric day 108 and W.9-10.9b (nonfiction) on the novel day 110. That is the reverse of days.csv and field 5, so a writer could build the wrong evidence day. Unit 5 had also swapped the frame's compelling question for Pope's poem title. The rest is minor drift: unit 6's word-list week label, unit 2's unnamed seminars, and source and supply lines the old cap had pushed out.
