# Ladder sample, science grade 1, set A (2026-11-19)

Reader: a fresh read-only reader of the kindergarten-to-grade-3 science ladder run (later.csv L81). Nothing was edited but this report. Each day was read cold, with the day before it (the hand-off), its section plan block, DEFAULTS.md, its days.csv, items.csv and wrongs.csv rows, the unit's errors.csv and handbacks.csv rows, and every shared piece named in `leans_on`. The ladder ledger (`ledger.py show science --before science-grade-1`) was saved to a temp file and grepped.

## Days read

- courses/science-grade-1/u02-1/d014.md (Fluency): teachable and kind to the clock, but the paper class model it revises is never defined, and the exit tests the chain again rather than the comparison.
- courses/science-grade-1/u03-1/d025.md (Application): sound routine, but the exit re-uses day 24's cat markings and the day's own blade routine, and several parts run to the second.
- courses/science-grade-1/u04-1/d028.md (Understanding): a good hook, but the exit asks for a test plan that day 29 and day 31 teach, and does not measure noticing the blur or proposing a cause.
- courses/science-grade-1/u04-1/d031.md (Application): the stopping test runs in the time, but the exit stem (Pip's handle claim) does not match its key, and the exit repeats day 28's.
- courses/science-grade-1/u04-2/d034.md (Respond): the repair and feedback parts are sound, but the fresh-evidence exit is built from cases already taught or repaired that day, its keys assume a positive result, and it names two chart arrows where the chart has three.

## How the keys and claims were checked

Every exit and end item key was reworked by hand from its stem and card description (items.csv, cards-exits files), including the arithmetic on blade lengths (30, 60, 75 mm order; 15 mm gaps) and the timing sums.

- Checked against a source in the repository: keys against the cards files, wrongs.csv and errors.csv letters; blade sizes against cards-m22.md and cards-exits-22-27.md; timing against each day's own seconds; safety lines against unit plan field 8 (u04-1.md) and materials.csv.
- Could only judge (no outside source consulted): that a hare, dog, duck and fish do what the day says; that a held spoon, bell, band and drum go much quieter; that a hanging paper fringe moves at classroom-level speaker volume (this one is doubtful and drives two findings); that a rubber-band guitar plucked low with eye protection is safe; every real recording (none exists yet, so no recorded result could be checked).
- Gates run, filtered as the job allows: `lesson.py check` (all five days: 0 failures, word counts inside the limits) and `plan.py check` (no failure names any of the five days; the only FAILs are section ids missing from the units registry and day-number gaps in units 8 and C, none about these days).

## Ladder

What the grade gap caused:

- Kindergarten's three-box strip (before, touch, after), "fair test", "I think ___, because I saw ___" and the system model each returned in grade 1 under a new name: "I did, I saw, I heard" (day 28), "fair check" (days 31 and 34), "we think" and "parts that work together" (day 14). Each is the same idea in new words and none is named as review in the lesson text, though the ledger entries say review.
- The older ladder pieces were used well where the words stayed identical: "keep, change, watch" (days 29 to 31 name it as kindergarten taught it) and "I saw ___, so ___" and the evidence arrow from u02-1 (day 31's rug).
- Within the grade, the failures are mostly unit-internal re-use: day 28 pre-empts day 31's stopping test, day 31 repeats day 28's exit, day 34 re-uses days 31 and 32 as its "fresh" case, and day 25 re-uses day 24's cat markings. The grade gap is not the cause; the single pass without a read after it is.
- Nothing in these five days assumes something a lower grade or an earlier unit did not teach, except "blade" (never taught as a word) on day 25 and "need" for ears and legs on day 14 (kindergarten's needs were water, light and food only).
- No exit reuses a kindergarten case or card; the fox appears only in kindergarten's unit 4.3 assessment case, and the duck, spoon and bell are new.

Neighbouring days seen while reading, not counted: day 13's stems say "sees" and "carries" (the taught chant) and so give away the key, and its sort key does not allow nostrils (smell) under sense.
