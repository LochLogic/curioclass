## Mismatches

No answer of mine differed from a key on what the correct claim is. Mismatches are in whether the item gives a child what the key asks for.

1. **E1 and E2: item ambiguous (fix the item).** Key E1 wants "reaching water could help" (also in the P68-3 answer). Nothing on the barley card, in days 64 to 68 or in the opener says what deeper roots do; barley appears only on the card and in d068. A child who writes only "eight not three" gets the count point but cannot show the "could help" cause the step names; a child who guesses a cause is rewarded for outside knowledge. E2 has the reverse problem: the meadow-bird card never says plumes matter to mates, and the E2 key asks for counts only, so a bare count passes as an explanation (3-LS4-2 says "explain"), while the fresh cards (waxier leaves "lose less water", calls "are mating displays") do supply the function. Fix: add one plain line to each source card, for example barley "Deeper roots reach water farther down" and meadow bird "Males show their plumes to females", and let E2 step read "uses seven against two and says the plume could help find a mate".
2. **E10: item ambiguous, a fair alternative is keyed wrong.** The pond card says "The edges are waterlogged" and "no dry ground", and the marsh flower "needs wet soil". A child who says the flower does well at the waterlogged edges is right by the card, but the key says poorly "under water". The card never places the flower fully under water in this pond. Fix: in the pond card add "The flower is planted in the deep middle, fully under water", or cut the edges line.
3. **E5: item mixes two things.** The meerkat record says trips "end with food and a safe return", so eight against four serves both E5 (food) and E6 (safety); E6's key then uses the call and retreat only. A child who splits the record the other way (eight of ten safe for E6) is arguable. Fix: say in the E5 key that eight against four counts toward food and accept it also as safety evidence, or make the card give trips with food and returns separately.
4. **E8: key asks for the huddle's "jobs" that the card does not give.** The penguin card says only "neighbors shelter one another" and "birds change places". The key sample says "take turns on the edge", a detail not on the card. Fix: accept "no separate jobs named, birds change places" as the huddle side and drop "on the edge" from the E8 sample and the P62-5 answer.
