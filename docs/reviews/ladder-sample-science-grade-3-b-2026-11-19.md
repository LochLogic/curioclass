## Ladder

The grade gap caused five things in these days.

1. New words for an old idea: "many-year record" and "one year" (d103, d106) restate "short record, long record" from u05-1 day 85, and the ledger lists the old term as review on the plan but not in the days' words (d106 finding).
2. Things assumed that no lower grade or earlier unit taught: "below freezing" has no number (grade 2 u04-1 says freeze, never measured), and the millimeter appears first as a 20 mm threshold on day 104 (d105 finding). Both are small, one-line fixes.
3. K safety not reconnected: kindergarten u02-2 (thunder: go in; a tree is not shelter) is the only earlier lightning content, and d111 does not link it (d111 finding).
4. Lower grades used well: d102's second-colour revision and written feedback are from u03-4 and u05-3 (stated as review, same words); scale and key from math 3.MD.B.3 are retrieval; "goal, limit, token, merit claim, trial table" in d111 and d118 are the u02-2 and u05-2 words, unchanged, which is the best fit in the sample; map key, equator and poles are grade 2 words kept in d103.
5. No lower grade's case is reused as an exit. Where an exit reuses a case it is this grade's own: d102 and d111 reuse their own days' cases, and d118's dry basin echoes day 94 and day 86.

Verdict: the grade used the lower grades well for tools and words (goal, limit, token, merit, key, second colour) and badly for the two assumed measures (freezing, millimeters) and for the one idea that got a new name (short/long record became one year/many years).
