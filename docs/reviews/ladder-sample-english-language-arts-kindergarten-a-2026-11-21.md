## Ladder

Kindergarten is the first grade, so the grade gap caused nothing here; the ladder is inside the grade. The grade used its own earlier days well: reviews are labeled and almost all of them point to a real earlier day (day 9 parts of a book to day 2; day 37 "What does ___ mean?" to day 4; day 66 detail to day 9, character to day 49, who and where to day 43; day 101 source sticker, tracking, was and his; day 134 nursery to day 131, ask what a word means to day 123). No day teaches as new something an earlier day already taught, with these exceptions: day 66 extends "detail" to people without naming it, and day 37's ledger line for the unknown-word routine does not match the day. Nothing assumes a lower-grade skill; the words stay consistent, except "he" read as a naming word on day 37. Exit cases are never a lower day's case (moose, cloak, Zia, Wen, stacking toys are all new), with the one soft overlap of the scarf in day 66's later verse.
