# Fixes: social studies course frames, grades 3 to 5, after the two cold reads (2026-11-13)

Applies the expert reads (`frame-social-studies-<course>-2026-11-13.md`) and the Codex reads
(`frame-social-studies-<course>-codex-2026-11-13.md`) for grades 3, 4 and 5 to the three frames,
`data/progressions/social-studies.csv`, `data/plans/later.csv` (L5) and the seam table of the grade 6 frame. Every
expert finding and every "Settled by the expert" row is applied; each Codex finding was weighed on its merits. The
K to 2 pass's changes stand (ss1-group-roles hands on to ss3-taking-part).

Gates after the fixes: `scripts/frame.py sync` (15 courses); `scripts/stale.py check` 19 files, 0 failures;
`scripts/progression.py check social-studies` 384 rows, 0 failures; `scripts/plan.py check` exit 0, every frame 0
failures. No unit or skill moved between units; every course keeps its unit count and total days (grade 3 7 and
160, grade 4 7 and 160, grade 5 10 and 160). Inside grade 5, two days moved from unit 10 to unit 9.

Files outside this pass (`docs/courses/social-studies/grade-3-outline.md`, `data/standards/ma-hss-2018.txt`,
`docs/ROADMAP.md`, earlier review reports) were not edited; what the readers found there is under "For the lead".
