## Verdict

Day 3 is correct mathematics and a sound plan: one worked search, two searches with feedback, two practice sets
checked after each pair, the register's error B fixed on purpose, and a written task that asks for the stopping
reason the unit is built on. The biggest risk is that the day models only one jump of ten (3 × 10 = 30, then a
table fact), while nearly every 3 the students must test (63, 66, 72, 75, 90) needs two or three tens, and the
teacher page's own prompts ("what is 3 × 10? How far is that from 72?") lead to 42, which is not a 3s fact either.
The one change that matters most is finding 1: add a short second example, 3 on 81, that takes another ten, and
fix the two prompts.

## Findings, most serious first

### 1. The day teaches one jump of ten, and the practice needs two or three

- **What is wrong.** The worked example tests 3 and 4 on 48 from ten times, and both land after one ten (3 × 10
  = 30, 18 more is 3 × 6; 4 × 10 = 40, 8 more is 4 × 2). Every 3 tested later needs more: 3 × 24 (72), 3 × 30
  (90), 3 × 21 (63), 3 × 25 (75), 3 × 22 (66). After "3 × 10 = 30" a student is left with 42, 60, 33, 45 or 36
  more, none of which a grade 4 child reads as a 3s fact on day 3. The teacher page's watch prompt for 72 ("what
  is 3 × 10? How far is that from 72?") gives 42, and its reteach after set 1 ("test 3 on 63 together from ten
  times") gives 33: both lead back to the same wall. Day 4 then names "past ten times" as its own new teaching
  (its heading, and error E "stops at ten times"), so the stretch's order says day 3 should not need it.
- **Evidence.** items.csv s01-K3-1 steps; s01-T3-1, T3-2, P3-2, P3-3, P3-4 answers; d003.md "Every shape" and
  "Practice set 1"; d004.md "Teaching first: ... then past ten times". Grade 3 hands over one-digit times
  multiples of 10 (3.NBT.A.3) and the distributive property (3.OA.B.5): docs/courses/math-3-5-progression.md,
  stretch 11 hand-off line, so 3 × 20 = 60 is in reach; it is just never shown.
- **Cost.** Students who follow the routine exactly stall on the first 3 of the first search, which is most of the
  class's first independent work with no tiles. They fall back to "3 is not in my table, so no", which is error
  B, the error the day exists to remove, and the check screens then mark them down for following the model.
- **Change.** Add a second worked example, `s01-K3-2`, "Is 3 a factor of 81?", shown after 48 (rows and steps
  under "Settled by the expert"). Teacher page, after "Leave 'start from ten times' on the board": "If what is
  left is still past your table, take another ten." Watch prompt for 72 becomes: "Count 3 by tens: 30, 60. How
  far is 60 from 72?" The set 1 reteach becomes: "test 3 on 63 together: 3 × 20 = 60, 3 more is 3 × 1, so 3 × 21
  = 63." Student page side 1, under the 48 frame: "**Then.** [[s01-K3-2]]" with one answer line. Minutes: Teaching
  first 8 to 9, Pull it together 4 to 3 with one **Ask.** (the plan's planned question, on 42); the second Ask
  (stopping at 7) is what the written task has just asked, so it goes.
- **Confidence.** Firm (the rows themselves, and day 4's heading).
- **LAYER:** day. **CATCH:** expert. **REPEAT:** yes (day 91 finding 4, used a day before it is taught). **CHEAP:** missed.

## What is right and should not be touched

- The search in order with "fits, with __" or "no" for each row, and the stop said as a reason ("7 × 7 is already
  past 48, so any new pair would be one we have, turned"), not a rule to recite. This is the Progressions'
  system (Prog. p. 45 (39), via the progression page).
- "A factor fits with none left; it need not be a fact I know": the day's real idea, said plainly.
- Fix it is the register's error B example, on the day the register puts it, with the right working shown beside.
- The written task (64, a square) asks for the stop before the class discussion, so it is evidence, not an echo.
- The exit's 98 is new, reserved for E3, and needs a pair from past the tables (7 × 14).
- Hand-off to day 4 is complete: B (7 on 98 from ten times), E ("98 is even"), the turn in the written task, and
  the written tasks go back on day 4. Day 4's Before class sorts the same three piles.
