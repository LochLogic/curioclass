# Open ELA material for Curio Class: is there an Illustrative Mathematics for reading?

Written 2026-11-12. Research only: nothing in the repository was changed. This is not legal advice. Every licence
below was read on the publisher's own page or in a file it printed, on 2026-11-12, unless marked "unverified".
Licence lines are "found, not yet second-read" in the sense of FOUNDATION section 7.

## The short answer

- **Only one full ELA program is CC BY: EL Education K-5 Language Arts, 2017 edition** (CC BY 4.0, rated Meets
  Expectations in every gateway by EdReports, 2018). It is the nearest thing to Illustrative Mathematics for
  reading, but only for K-5, and its newer 2025 edition is CC BY-NC. Use the 2017 copy, and keep a dated copy of
  its licence page.
- **Its texts do not come with it.** EL K-5 is built around trade books and third-party passages, which its own
  licence excludes. Adapting EL K-5 means keeping its structure, routines, questions and writing tasks, and
  swapping in texts we have rights to. That is far more rework than math.
- **Everything else is NC, NC-SA or closed:** CKLA (CC BY-NC-SA), Bookworms (CC BY-NC), EL 6-8 (CC BY-NC),
  EngageNY ELA (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0), Fishtank (CC BY-NC-SA), CommonLit 360 (CC BY-NC-SA, texts excluded, no AI use),
  UFLI (all rights reserved), Louisiana Guidebooks and Odell (no open licence found). These are reference only.
- **Grades 6 to 12 have no CC BY base.** Write our own on public-domain and CC BY texts, as the grade 6 pilot
  already does.
- **Phonics:** no CC BY phonics program with independent ratings was found apart from the EL K-2 Skills Block,
  and whether the 2017 Skills Block files carry CC BY is still unverified (the 2024 Skills Block is CC BY-NC).
  Settle that one file check before choosing a K-2 base.

## Traps

1. **NC and SA do not mix with CC BY.** Copying any CKLA, Bookworms, EL 6-8, EL 2025, Fishtank, CommonLit or
   EngageNY text, question or worksheet into a Curio Class page would force NC or SA terms on it. Reading for ideas
   and order is fine; copied wording is not. Keep the same line we drew for Eureka.
2. **Edition drift.** EL K-5 is CC BY only in the 2017 edition. Never mix in a 2025 file. Save the licence page and
   the file's own licence line at the time of use. (Creative Commons licences cannot be withdrawn from what was
   already released, but we must be able to show which edition we used.)
3. **Excluded third-party content inside a CC BY program.** EL marks some content as third party; each item marked
   that way must be removed or replaced, not adapted.
4. **Decodable texts.** UFLI's, CKLA's and EL 2024's decodables are not CC BY. Even word chains from UFLI's lesson
   plans may not be reused. Our decodables must be written by us.
5. **Names in credit lines.** Credit the source without implying endorsement: "Adapted from EL Education K-5
   Language Arts (2017), CC BY 4.0. Not endorsed by EL Education." Do not use "CKLA", "Core Knowledge", "UFLI",
   "Achieve the Core", or Amplify marks as labels on our pages ("UFLI-aligned" is expressly forbidden). Do not call
   a Curio Class course "EL" anything. Logos never.
6. **Lexile.** "Lexile" is a registered trademark of MetaMetrics. Its permitted use policy says use of its measures
   "requires a license" and trademark use "also requires a license". Quoting the Common Core band table with
   credit is likely fine (unverified); publishing our own Lexile measures for our texts is not, without a licence.
   APPROACH-ELA already uses a Flesch-Kincaid script for that; keep it, and name it as such.
7. **CommonLit and AI.** Its terms forbid putting its materials into AI tools. Keep it out of any model prompt.
8. **Project Gutenberg boilerplate.** Remove the header and licence block and the name, or follow its trademark
   rules.
9. **Public-domain status is country-specific.** Gutenberg's US status does not hold everywhere; Curio Class is a
   US site, so say "public domain in the United States".
