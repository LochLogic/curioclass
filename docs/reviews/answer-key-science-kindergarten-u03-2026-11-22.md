## Findings

1. **M1b (and M1a): "your change" has no referent.** M1b asks "Did your change make the ball start faster?" but M1a's stem now reads "Push the ball gently from the line, then push it harder from the same line." It never says the harder push is the change. The unit plan's M1 stem named it ("Push so it starts faster than your first push"). Fix M1a: "Push the ball gently from the line. Now change your push: push it harder from the same line. Watch both starts..." and M1b: "You pushed harder. Did that make the ball start faster? Touch yes or no."

2. **M3a presupposes a turn the key says may not exist.** Stem: "What made the ball change the way it went?" The key accepts a straight path ("the bumper did not touch it"), but the stem tells that child the ball changed. Fix the stem: "Look at the path you drew. Did the ball change the way it went? What made it?" (answer as keyed; a straight path answers "no, the bumper did not touch it").

3. **P2c key is narrow.** "Why do we find out what people want before we build?" The answer line is "So we know what to test, or what the people want." Fair child answers such as "so we make the right thing", "so it works", "so it helps the people" fit the plan's goal, but a teacher reading the key literally may mark them not seen. Fix the answer: "So we make what helps: we know what is wanted, or what to test. Any answer that says we need to know the want first counts." Keep "build first and find out after" as error A.

4. **P1a: answer and look-fors disagree, and one keyed card is not on the day 27 list.** The answer says a question no card answers is kept and marked shown, but the second look-for says "the question is about what is wanted, where, or what can be used", so a fair observation question ("Why did it stop?", "Can we push harder?") fails that look-for. Fix the look-for: "the question is about the problem shown". Also the answer cites "what can we use", but day 27's materials list and cards-lane.md hold no materials card, and the not-on-this-page card is not in cards-lane.md (both live in u03-1/cards-goal-materials.md). Add the materials card and the not-on-this-page card to day 27's per-class list and print line, or drop "what can we use" from the key.

5. **P1b: the mat cannot show "short of the bay".** The mat box is blank ("where the push stopped") and the look-for only needs "a mark at the stopping place". The key says "short of the bay", which cannot be read from a mark in an empty box. Fix: have the child draw the bay and the mark (stem: "Draw the bay, and draw where the gentle push stopped"), or print a small lane and bay outline in that box.

## Other checks

- **Stem and picture show what the key needs.** P1c: the goal card picture (ball in the bay) matches the spoken goal and one of the two P1c cards, so P1c is a listening and matching item, fair for K but easy. P2b: the distractors (tall, red, shiny) are clean. The plan listed "stays low" as a third keyed card; items.csv replaced it with "is shiny" and keys two. That is consistent inside the items, but it drops the plan's "stays low" look-for, and "is tall" now stands alone against nothing the children saw. Either restore "stays low" as a keyed card or note the change in the plan.

M2: a bumper placed where the ball misses it is a valid trial, but the look-for "places the bumper where the ball will touch it" would then be unmet; the answer text already calls a miss a true result, so reword the look-for to "places a bumper in the lane".
