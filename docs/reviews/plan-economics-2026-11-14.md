## Course verdict

The course is strong and close to the bar: nine units cover every Massachusetts economics and personal finance code the frame lists, items are carefully keyed with lettered errors, and the hard-coded 2026 data in units 5 and 6 match the federal releases exactly. Two things keep it from the bar. First, current law moved in 2025 and 2026 and three units do not say so: the Supreme Court's February 20, 2026 tariff ruling and the copper tariffs (unit 7), tax year 2026 forms and the 2025 law (units 4 and 8), and the consumer finance agency's funding fight, student loan changes and new child accounts (units 8 and 9). Second, unit 1 still invents a start-of-year sitting, and several days put reteach blocks or too many standards into one 50-minute period. All of it is fixable inside the existing 80 days; nothing needs Cory.

## Course-wide findings

### CW1. There is no start-of-year sitting; Economics begins on school day 81 (moderate, plan, data and process, firm)

**What is wrong.** s01 field 4 says the readiness check is "the start-of-year check, before day 1, within a few days of opening". days.csv day 1 says it "is served by the start-of-year check, given before this day"; days 2, 29 and 69 say "the start-of-year check flagged"; day 6 says "the start-of-year check's readiness items". scope-and-sequence.md lines 9, 13, 14, 27, 51, 87 and 95 say the same. Under the lead rule of 2026-11-14 there is no sitting before day 1. Economics is a spring half-year course that opens on school day 81: the school year started 80 days earlier, but in a different course with a different teacher, so "start of year" names nothing this teacher gives.

**Why it matters.** A teacher looking for the check finds no time for it, and the reteach on day 2 then has no evidence behind it.

**Fix.** Unit 1 has readiness rows (ss8-public-budget, the whole row, and wh2-soviet-collapse), so the check is the **last ten minutes of day 1, after the hook and the day's teaching**, with the reteach on days 2 and 3 (see 1.2). Wording everywhere: "the day 1 readiness check". How school day 81 fits: the course's day 1 is its own first day whatever the school calendar says; the check sits inside that period, so nothing runs before the course opens and nothing depends on the fall course's records. LAYER plan (s01, s04, s08, s09 text), data (days.csv notes on days 1, 2, 6, 29, 59, 69), process (scope-and-sequence lines above). REPEAT: same fault as grade 4 (its 1.1); the lead rule now settles it.
