## Findings

**1. s01-E5: the stem and student page never ask the child to show the route, but the key and wrongs score it.**
Stem: "The caption says that rainwater runs along the valley toward the river mouth. Use your model to say why the water goes that way." The steps require "shows the water route running along the valley toward the mouth, not over the hills", and wrongs.csv scores "The arrow runs up and across the hills" as error E. The day 9 student page, side 2, has only the lines "The water goes ___ because ___", and the model is drawn on side 1 before any arrow is mentioned. A child who draws no arrow cannot show error E and loses a component for something the stem did not ask.
Fix: change the stem to "Use your model to show the way the water goes, with an arrow, and say why it goes that way", and put the arrow instruction on the student page above side 1's sketch box (d009 teacher step 3 already says "show and say").
Also widen the key: the stem does not say "lower", so "because it goes downhill" or "because the valley is low" is a fair, true answer. Change "because the valley is lower than the hills" to "because the valley is lower (downhill) than the hills".

**2. s01-E1 and s01-E2: the packet picture notes do not say that each record's heading and the water labels are printed.**
packet-day8.md, record A: "A keyed map of a coast seen from above... The ocean and river each carry a filled dot." Records A and B give no heading text in the picture note, only in the section title ("Record A: Pine Coast survey"). Only record C says "headed Other Cove". The packet's own rule is "Children read only labels and the key" and "The headings on the records carry the evidence", yet record A names no printed labels for ocean, river or ice. If the headings or labels are not printed, E1 (choose by place) and E2 (ocean, river, ice from A) cannot be solved by a second grader.
Fix: in record A add "Headed: Pine Coast survey. Labels: Ocean, River, Ice"; in record B add "Headed: Pine Coast observations". Keep C as is.

**3. s01-K1-1: picture 3 does not show the high ground or a hollow the steps ask for.**
Steps: "puts a finger on the high ground first | follows the finger downhill... | stops at the low end and draws the hollow there". pictures-day1.md page 3 is "A close view of the curb at the low end of the yard. A thin stream... ends in a puddle against a drain cover." There is no high ground in picture 3 and no hollow. The puddle ends at a drain cover, so "it went down the drain" is a fair child answer, but d001 expects only "the puddle in the hollow", and d010 later teaches the drain as an unseen route. The sample answer ("The finger starts on the grass") needs picture 2.
Fix: change the stem to "Look at pictures 2 and 3" (picture 2 shows the grass slope, the low corner and the stream by the path), or add a strip of slope above the curb to picture 3. Add to the key: "along the curb to the puddle at the drain cover" is accepted as seen, and "down the drain" is marked as not seen.

**4. s01-E3 and the plan: the unit plan's day 8 pattern (liquid lower, solid higher) is not an item component.**
The plan's day 8 row and look-fors end with "states a location-and-state pattern supported by both sources". The key says "A child who says 'low' for the liquid and 'high' for the solid is also right; the pattern is not required", and the day's six components replace the pattern with "Says where the liquid and the solid water are for Pine Coast only". This is a defensible narrowing for grade 2 and the items still test 2-ESS2-3 fully (E1 source choice, E2 states and places, E3 combine). But patterns is the unit's named crosscutting concept, and plan and items disagree.
Fix: record the narrowing in docs/courses/science-grade-2/s01.md (day 8 look-fors), or add one optional step to E3 ("says liquid is lower and solid is higher here, from these records, not for every place"). Item-level choice for the lead; I made none.

**5. s01-E6: a generic answer is not separated from the keyed one.**
Stem: "Say how a map could show a different area." Frame: "A map of a different area would show ___." A child who writes "hills and a river" is true but is not "that area's own shapes and places", the only step. Fix: add a two-tier note to the key: "its own hills and water in their own places" earns the point; "hills and water" alone gets the prompt "Are they the same as Willow Bay?".

**6. s01-K2-1: pour count and key box do not match the day and the sheet.**
Steps: "pours once at the top of the hill" while d002 has each group pour twice; "draws the key box with arrow, hill, hollow and water" (four entries) while pour-record-sheet.md prints a key box with two lines ("This mark means" twice). The build also shapes "two hills and a valley", so "hollow" here means the valley pool. Fix: "pours from the top of a hill"; "key box with at least the arrow and the hill", or print four lines on the sheet.

**7. Spelling (low).** d009.md teacher page: "recognisable shapes" should be "recognizable". Fix when next edited; I did not edit.

**8. Out of scope, noted.** The day 10 retry packet has ice on a small pond low beside the stream, while the unit plan describes a second observer's close-up of snow on the ridge. The key's note "(low, not high)" fits the packet as printed, so I marked no mismatch, but plan and packet should be reconciled.
