## Findings

**1. E1 (item ambiguous): nothing on the mat or lane for the cards to be placed on.**
Stem: "put the cards on your mat for what you change, what you keep and what you watch." Key steps: "puts the change card on how hard the push is", "puts the keep card on the same ball, box, start and way", "puts the watch card on the ball and the box". The mat (response-mat.md) already has three boxes printed "change", "keep", "watch", so a child can put each card on its own-named box and satisfy the stem without showing understanding. The cards file says "for end item 1 add a small picture of a ball and a box so the child can place each card on the part it is about", but neither the mat nor d008 prints or lays out that picture, and "how hard the push is" has no object at all.
Fix: give the child targets. In the lane, the child lays the keep card by the real ball and box, the change card by the push 1 and push 2 boxes (or a drawn long and short arrow), and the watch card by the box. Reword the stem to "put each card where it belongs for this test", and say in d008 that a child who tells it aloud while pointing at the real objects scores the same (the item's path is spoken).

**2. E1 (key too narrow): "watch".**
Key step 3: "the watch card on the ball and the box before and after the touch". The item's own right sample says "I will watch the bump and what moves", which is fair and would fail a literal reading. Fix: accept the bump, the box, how the box moves, or the ball and box before and after the touch.

**3. E2 (item ambiguous): two keyed steps are not asked.**
Stem: "Mark what the ball and the box do each time." Key step 1 requires putting the box back between tries; step 3 requires marking how the box started under the harder push compared with the gentle one. The stem asks for neither the reset nor a comparison, and the mat does not say where a comparison goes. A child who marks both tries correctly and says nothing about "faster" would be scored not yet seen on step 3.
Fix: add "and tell me if the harder push did anything different" to the stem (it does not give the answer; the key already allows "slower or about the same"), and say in d008 that step 1 is scored by watching, not asking. Say too that push 1 and push 2 hold the two tries.

**4. E5 (key too narrow): the ball "slowing or stopping at the touch".**
A large soft ball rolled slowly into a light box may keep going while pushing the box, slow a little, stop, or bounce back. Key step 1 would fail a child who truthfully marks the ball bouncing back or going on pushing. Fix: "marks the ball changing at the touch (slowing, stopping, bouncing back or going on more slowly)". Steps 2 and 3 are correct.
Note: E5's first half repeats what E2 already marks (ball before the touch, box starting at the touch). Fine for the plan; the only new thing tested is pass by.

**5. E6 (item ambiguous): step 3 is never elicited.**
Stem: "Look at your marks. Tell me what they show about the ball and the box, and point to the mark that shows it." Key step 3: "keeps every mark, without rubbing out one that looks wrong". The stem never asks about keeping marks, and nothing in d008 sets it up. The listed wrongs ("Throw away the marks that look wrong", "I will rub out the mark that looks wrong") are not answers the stem invites, so error E can only be recorded if a child rubs out a mark earlier. The handback sends the group to error E repair on a result the item did not test.
Fix: either say in d008 that the teacher watches items 2 to 5 for a child rubbing out a mark and scores step 3 from that (lighter on time, the slot is one minute for five children), or add a spoken follow-up: "One try did not look like the others. What will you do with its mark?" (key: keeps it and shows it).
Also the Answer column and the day 8 home line ("Expect: the ball touched the box and pushed it") give only the push. A child who points to a pull mark and says the pull moved the box is giving a true, evidence-based answer. Add "or the pull moved it" to the Answer column; step 1 ("says the touch moved the box") already allows it.

**6. E4 (small ambiguity): "tapped from the side" versus "toward each place".**
The right sample says "Taps the same ball from each side and marks it turning toward each place." A tap from the left turns the ball to the right, away from the tapping side. A child who taps from the same side as the place and marks that the ball did not turn toward it is still testing correctly. Fix: add to the key that the tap may come from either side; what is scored is the same ball, a similar slow tap and the marked path. The key already accepts "or close to it".

**7. E3 (small): "toward its delivery place".**
The box sits beside two delivery places (E1), but E3 says "its delivery place". Fix: "toward a delivery place". Key and samples are correct.

**8. Wrongs and handbacks do not line up for E2.**
wrongs.csv lists "Swaps to a different ball for the stronger push" for s01-E2 with error A, but handbacks.csv sends E2 to repair D only, and d008 and d009 name only D for items 2 and 5. A child who swaps the ball in E2 is repaired with the wrong cards. Fix: add repair A to s01-E2 in handbacks.csv and write "error A (items 1 and 2)" in d008 and d009.

**9. Steps that can fail with no wrong listed.**
E2 step 1, E3 step 1 (same box, same start) and E4 step 1 (same ball) can fail without a matching wrongs row for that step. If repair is by error letter, such a child receives none. Low weight; add A rows to wrongs.csv if repair should reach them.

## Does each item test the plan goal it names?

- E1, E2, E3, E4: yes, K-PS2-1 (plan and carry out one-change comparisons: strength by push, strength by pull, direction). The set keeps to strength or direction, never both, with no numbers or force words, as the plan's limits require.
- E5: tests contact (PS2.B, "contact can start, stop... an object"), not a fair test itself, which s01.md lists in the unit. The pass-by half checks no touch, no change, without magnets, which the standard leaves out.
- E6: tests cause and effect from evidence and keeping all data. Finding 5 is the one place the key tests something the item does not ask.

## The three worst

1. E1: no target for the cards, so the item can be passed or failed without showing understanding (finding 1).
2. E6: step 3 (keep every mark) and the error E repair rest on a question the item does not ask (finding 5).
3. E2 and E5: a keyed comparison and a keyed ball outcome that the stem does not ask or a fair child may not show (findings 3 and 4).

## Could not settle

Nothing that changes what the grade teaches. For the lead to see with real equipment when the unit is taught: whether a large soft ball moves a light cardboard box at a gentle roll (E2) and whether the harder push visibly changes how the box starts. The keys allow "about the same", so either result is honest.
