## Verdict

The assessment is well conceived: every unit standard has an item, the T3.2 product is scored only after written
feedback and revision, and A4 to A5b are done from unseen, checked primary sources, which is what an adoption
reviewer looks for first. It is not yet usable as printed: the built source packet puts the reserved further
cards where students will see them, the record sheet cannot hold the 0, 1, 2 component scores the day asks for, and
the "about 40 minutes" of marking is a fraction of the real load. Most of what is wrong was set in the unit plan and
its data (items, record, materials), not in the day; the one change that matters most is to print F1 and F2 as
separate cards and the goods cards apart from them.

## Findings, most serious first

### 1. The built packet shows the reserved further cards before students choose

The built sources-45-student.pdf runs P1, P2, S, F1, F2, goods cards in one flow. F2's title and first lines sit at
the foot of page 1 and its body runs on at the top of page 2; the goods cards students need for A3 (minute 15 or so)
sit directly under F2's body on page 2. A student doing A3 reads F2 before A5b; F1 sits mid-page 1 between P1 to S
and F2, so no fold can hide it while leaving the "titles visible" the print line asks for. A5b's look-fors ("a
reasoned choice of card", "a detail from the card") then measure nothing, because the student has seen both.
D2.His.12 is the item most likely to be lost.

Fix: print the packet in three pieces: sheet 1 P1, P2, S and the goods cards; F1 and F2 each on its own half-sheet
card, title on the outside, folded once, handed out folded. Print line and "Before class": "Cut and fold F1 and F2;
hand out both cards closed." If the build cannot force a page break in a `piece: blank`, that is a gate or build
change (template).

LAYER day. ORIGIN section. CATCH cheap read (CHECK 4, the built PDF, was skipped). REPEAT yes (day 82 #4, unseen
sources on a sheet students hold). CHEAP missed. Confidence: firm (seen in the built PDF).

### 2. "About 40 minutes after class" is not a credible marking estimate, and day 46 needs it overnight

After class the teacher scores, per student, A1 (4 look-fors), A2 (10 components, from a map and two-part
explanation), two of three survey items (4 each), A2d's claim part, A3 (4), A4 (4), A5a (4) and A5b (5): about 40
judged components, each 0, 1 or 2, plus reading a claim and a map. At even 6 minutes a student that is 3 hours for
30 students; 40 minutes is 80 seconds a student. Day 46 opens by returning everything "by component" with groups
sorted from the marks, so all of it must be done the same night, the third marking evening in a row (map feedback
after day 43, claim feedback after day 44). A teacher who believes the 40 minutes will either skim or arrive at day
46 without groups.

Fix: s03 field 7 and u03-4 Day 45 say "about 3 hours for 30 students, an untested estimate; score A2 from the
folders on the evening of day 44, when they are already collected (finding 7), and the sitting items after day
45". Day 45's Mark part repeats the honest number. If that is too much for one night, day 46's groups can form
from A1 to A5b only, with A2 returned on day 47 (a section change).

LAYER day. ORIGIN unit. CATCH expert. REPEAT new. CHEAP missed. Confidence: likely (reasoning from the counts).

### 3. The record sheet cannot hold what the day tells the teacher to record

The day says "Use row look-fors and record sheet A1 to A5b columns ... Enter 0/1/2 instead of ticks." The built
record.html has one column per end item, headed "End 1" to "End 9" (not A1 to A5b), and its rule line is "One tick a
look-for met". A2 alone has ten components, each to be scored 0, 1 or 2 (s03 field 5: "Record each named component
separately"); one cell cannot hold ten scores, and day 46 sorts groups by component. The teacher must build a second
sheet.

Fix: the record generator gives an end item one column a look-for (as it already does for exits on days 42, 43, 44
and 46), heads end columns by item id (A1 ... A5b), and prints the 2, 1, 0 rule for end items. Day 45's line then
reads "record sheet, End columns, one cell a component, 2, 1 or 0".

LAYER process. ORIGIN template (the record piece's generator treats end items as one tick, a math shape).
CATCH expert. REPEAT yes (math record sheet that could not hold what the day asked). CHEAP missed. Confidence:
firm (seen in the built page).

### 4. Skipping the mapped option's survey leaves that option's named content unchecked

"Skip only the mapped option's survey" rests on "A2 already scores it". It does not: A2's ten look-fors are generic
(politics, economy, culture, four links, sources, evidence apart from inference). The option's named content in the
standard is scored only in the survey: Ghana, Mali, Songhai in order and Timbuktu (T3.2a); Axum against the Swahili
coast (T3.2b); a European contact, a Yuan and an early Ming achievement (T3.2d). A student who mapped the Mongol
Empire up to the Yuan is never asked about the early Ming; one who mapped Mali alone is never asked for the
sequence. The students who studied an option most are the only ones not checked on its named parts.

Fix (pick one): (a) no skip: everyone answers A2a, A2b and A2d, and A2 gives back its minutes by scoring the map
from the folder; the three survey items take 9 minutes, which the skip does not save anyway once the class is
mixed; or (b) A2's steps gain one option-specific look-for per option ("a: the three states in order and Timbuktu;
b: Axum earlier, Swahili towns later; d: European contact, a Yuan and an early Ming achievement"). (a) is simpler
and gives every student the same paper.

LAYER day. ORIGIN unit (items.csv s03-A2 steps, or the skip rule in s03 field 5 and u03-4 Day 45). CATCH expert.
REPEAT new. CHEAP missed. Confidence: likely.

### 12. A5a's statement S was written to match P1 and P2, so tracing it is matching, and the key misses the best evidence

S ("Saharan wealth depended on connections between places, and distant mapmakers associated Mali with gold") was
written for the assessment from P1 and P2, and its second half nearly names P2 ("distant mapmakers"). D2.His.9
("how historical sources underpin secondary interpretations") is better tested with an interpretation that reaches
past its sources, so a student must judge how far each source carries it. The key also names only the imported
provisions for the first half; P1's salt price, 8 to 10 mithqals at Walata and 20 to 40 in Mali, is the direct
evidence that wealth came from moving goods between places, and a student who cites it should not lose the
component.

Fix: S gains one clause the sources only partly carry, for example "... and Mali's rulers grew rich by controlling
that trade" (P1 and P2 suggest it, neither shows who collected the gain), and the look-for "one unsupported claim
named" then has a planted answer. Key, first half: "the provisions brought from far off, or the salt price rising
from Walata to Mali". The key change is settled below; the clause is a unit choice for the fix pass.

LAYER day. ORIGIN unit (items.csv s03-A5a; sources.csv 45-S). CATCH expert. REPEAT new. CHEAP missed. Confidence:
judgment call (S), firm (key).

### 15. F2 is dated 1353; the passage describes two festivals across 1352 and 1353

F2 is headed "a festival at the court of Mali, 1353" (sources-45.md; sources.csv 45-F2). Ibn Battuta reached Mali
on 28 June 1352 and left on 27 February 1353 (the Fordham text's own dates); the passage opens "I was at Malli
during the two festivals of the sacrifice and the fast-breaking", which in AH 753 fell in late 1352 and early 1353,
and describes them together. Source A on day 42 dates the same court "1352 to 1353". The elided middle (marked "...")
is also where the women and girls sing with Dugha; keeping one clause of it would add women's part at court, which
the sensitivity read asks for elsewhere.

Fix: "F2. Ibn Battuta, festivals at the court of Mali, 1352 to 1353" in the sheet and sources.csv. Optional: keep
"The women and girls sing along with him" before the ellipsis.

LAYER day. ORIGIN unit (sources.csv; the sheet is section). CATCH cheap read (CHECK 7). REPEAT yes (day 78 and 80,
dates on source lines). CHEAP missed (sensitivity #18 is F2's page numbers, not its date). Confidence: firm.
