## Verdict

The eight units make a sound kindergarten arc: one-to-one counting, then cardinality and comparison to ten, a
geometry and measurement unit that keeps the daily count, operations meaning, decomposition and partners of ten,
teens as ten ones and more, solids, then a gathering unit that closes counting to 100 and fluency within five, and
the hand-off into grade 1's retrieval on day 1 matches what kindergarten owns. The biggest risk across units was
drift between the bulk-written Goal map rows (2026-11-22) and the U3 exits and limits: day 146's practice named a
hundred chart the course forbids, day 136's exit compared groups to twenty against the ten limit, and days 115 and
116 had exits that missed half of their goals. Those are fixed; what remains is days.csv and generator work for the
structure pass.
