## Course verdict

The year is well built underneath: the frame's own-place design holds in the teaching days, the C3 arc runs in every unit, sensitivity limits are careful and specific, error registers are usable, and every unit hands on cleanly to the next with the readiness and retrieval slots where scope-and-sequence.md puts them. It is not yet at the bar, for two reasons that repeat in every unit. First, every end-of-unit assessment asks far more than a grade 3 child can do in 30 minutes. Second, the assessed items drift back to Massachusetts and Boston even where the frame and bar item 13 say the anchor is the class's own place: units 5 and 7 answer their compelling questions from Plymouth and Boston alone. Both are fixable in the plans without changing a day, and neither needs Cory.

## Course-wide findings

### C1. Every end-of-unit assessment overruns 30 minutes (serious)

LAYER plan and process. REPEAT yes (grade 1 3.1, grade 2 C2).
- **What.** Day 23: fourteen items, ten in the 30-minute block, including drawing two maps and labelling up to twelve states. Day 47: Q1 to Q15 plus Q16 to Q18 over nine source packets. Day 67: ten items plus two saved, most multi-part. Day 88: eight items plus one saved over four source sets. Day 120: about twenty lettered parts (Q1 to Q7 with Q2a, Q2b, Q4a to Q4d, Q5a, Q5b, Q6a and more). Day 135: five items, but Q2 alone asks six things. Day 159: five items timed to 30 minutes, but Q1 asks for every group, a population comparison and four sources in eight minutes.
- **Why.** C14 (runs in an ordinary room) and the settled spread rule: a paper this long measures reading stamina and speed, not the standards. Children will leave the last third blank and the teacher will read that as a gap.
- **Fix.** Cap each 30-minute paper at about eight prompts, each asking one thing, with at most three source packets read aloud. Where days.csv says "every part of each listed code asked once", meet it by judging parts from products already made in the unit (the settled rule from the kindergarten and grade 1 reads: spoken and observed evidence is spread across the unit, not loaded onto the assessment day). Units 6 and 7 already do this for their timelines and products; copy that pattern to units 1 to 5. Unit-level edits are listed per unit below.
