## Verdict: Pass with changes

The structure is sound: ids sit in sensible threads, owned counts run 1 to 4, grade 12 rows contribute only (`~`
ids), every row names its assessment, and no brand names or em dashes appear. The problems are text-level and mostly
systematic: uneven privacy and sacred-work wording, filler "What changes" lines (worst in media arts), and notes that
track the words file very closely.

## Findings

1. **Firm. All media arts rows that capture or show people.** The recording note says no faces or names are
   "published" without permission. APPROACH.md is stricter: no filming a student without the family's yes. Only
   ma-k-craft and ma-g1-craft say "no one is recorded without a yes". The same two sentences are pasted word for
   word into about 40 rows, while ma-k-make and ma-g1 to g5 make say "record" or "capture" with no note at all.
   Fix, one note for every capturing row: "Teacher note: no student is recorded, photographed or filmed without a
   yes from the student and the family; a face or voice is never required; kept work stays on the school's or
   family's own device."

2. **Firm. Sacred or ceremonial wording is uneven** (rule: studied only when the source says it is open to be taught,
   never an exercise). Looser, with no open-to-be-taught condition: va-g2-museum ("looking only"), mu-g6-respond,
   da-k-respond, da-g9-respond, da-g12-criticism, th-g12-research. Stricter and inconsistent: da-g6-form says
   "never on sacred or ceremonial dance" while da-g9-respond allows watching. Absent where cultural content is
   named: va-g5, g8 and g10 museum, va-g11-museum (only a sensitivity read), mu-g1 to g5 read and respond,
   ma-g8-sources, ma-g9-society. Fix, one sentence everywhere: "Sacred or ceremonial work is studied by looking and
   listening only, and only when the community's own source says it is open to be taught; it is never used as an
   exercise, a costume or an imitation."

3. **Firm. Media arts "What changes" lines are topic fragments, not changes.** 53 of 96 media arts rows have a line
   of five words or fewer: ma-g5-read "telling pieces apart", ma-g6-read "analysis of meaning", ma-g7-judge "own
   criteria", ma-g5-make "meaning and clarity", ma-g1-sources "interests and examples", ma-g3-society "values and
   online behavior". A planner cannot tell grade 5 read from grade 6 read. Fix: rewrite each as a contrast with the
   year before, for example ma-g6-read: "from naming parts to explaining how a choice steers a viewer, with a reason
   from the piece". Ma-*-judge and ma-*-present also repeat their assessment sentence in 5 or 6 of 12 grades.

4. **Likely. Dance, visual arts and music have the same fragments in places:** da-g2-respond "clues point to
   meaning", da-g5-respond "interpretation with stated traits", da-g10-elements "purposeful space and unpredictable
   timing", va-g3-craft, va-g6-show, mu-g2-respond. Same fix. Also va-g9-ideas, craft and design carry no "What
   changes" line at all.

5. **Firm. mu-choose (K to 8) and mu-respond: Cn10.1 and Cn11.1 have the same words line in every grade** (nine
   identical lines each, checked by script). Every choose row repeats "show how tastes and skills shape choices", so
   that part reads the same each year and the real change comes from Pr4.1 and Re7.1. Fix: add to each mu-choose row
   "Cn10.1 is the same statement every grade; the change is in what the student must justify", and keep the one
   true change line (for example mu-g5: claims come with evidence from the music).

6. **Likely. Notes sit very close to the words file.** Mean token overlap of a row's first sentence with its owned
   words lines is 86 percent; 75 percent of rows are above 80. The notes are in effect the words file strung
   together, and some words lines still read near the standard (va-g5-museum "cite proof", th-g12 rows). That risks
   the "never quote the standards" rule at one remove. th-g12-concept and th-g12-role ("weave research, script
   study, history and culture into one believable performance") are closest. Fix: the second reader of the words
   file compares it with the handouts before any plan cites it, and notes state the unit action ("The student
   shows...") instead of copying the line.

7. **Likely. Ids in a thread where they fit poorly.** va-g4-design owns Cr2.3.4a (record built places) with
   Cn10.1.4a (art showing a community's traditions), two unrelated actions: move Cn10.1.4a to va-g4-museum.
   mu-g5-read owns Pr4.2.5c (how history and culture shape performance): it belongs with mu-g5-respond, leaving
   read for notation. da-g8-technique and da-g9-technique own Pr5.1 health habits with no health lean: add one.
   ma-g1-present and ma-g5-present own Pr4.1 (mixing media into one piece), which is making, not showing.

8. **Firm. Level 1 bridge note is inconsistent.** It is on va-g9-ideas, th-g9-imagine and da-g9-ideas only, but on
   all eight ma-g9 rows, identical. The Level 2 and 3 review unit (STANDARD.md 3.2) appears in no row. Fix: bridge
   sentence on the first thread of each discipline only; add "Level 2 opens with a review unit of at most 6 days
   that serves as placement" (and the same for Level 3) to the first row of g10 and g11 in each discipline.

9. **Likely. Theater grade 12 differs from the other disciplines.** Five rows (others have four), written as
   imperatives that echo the standards' actions ("Lead a production concept: blend..."), with no "The Level 3 ids
   are owned in grade 11" line and no family-consent note for a showing. Fix: rewrite in the va-g12 shape with
   "The student..." and the owned-in-grade-11 sentence.

10. **Judgment call. Grade 12 audience and consent.** The exhibit and perform rows say a showing needs each family's
    yes, but a Level 4 student may be 18. Add: "A student of 18 or over gives their own yes to be shown; otherwise
    the family does." Otherwise the grade 12 rows are meaningful: a proposal, a series, a showing, a critique.

11. **Likely. Dance safety is thin and one row conflicts.** da-g9-elements says "balances are partner-supported with a
    spotter"; APPROACH.md says no lifts and partner contact only by agreement and never required. Only
    da-g6-elements carries "no lifts"; da-g4-elements has "land jumps softly" with no note. Fix: put "no lifts;
    partner contact only by agreement and never required; a solo version is always offered" on every elements and
    technique row from grade 4, and rewrite g9 as "balances are low to the floor, by agreement, with a solo version".

12. **Judgment call. Leans are few and uneven.** 41 in all: da 14, th 12, mu 7, va 6, ma 2. All are real and at or
    before the row's grade; dance PE leans exist for K to 9 technique and point at real PE rows. Against STANDARD.md
    section 6, media arts has no computer science lean and no art has a social studies lean (works taught with
    time and place is a bar criterion). Too few, not too many. Suggest one social studies lean on va-museum and
    da-connect at grades 6, 8 and 11, and one computer science lean on ma-society at grade 8 (or more, as that
    progression allows).

13. **Judgment call. "Fair use" in ma-sources and ma-society.** The note says use others' media "only as fair use
    allows (a small part, for study or comment, with credit)". That is a legal rule stated as fact, and APPROACH.md
    says remix uses public domain or openly licensed sources only. Fix: "use only public domain or openly licensed
    media, named and credited; fair use is explained, not relied on."

14. **Judgment call. Music 9 to 12 has no rows.** The file holds mu K to 8 (the strand is not loaded), so
    mu-g8-invent says nothing hands on. One of the 20 courses therefore has no progression, and the check cannot
    see it. Confirm this is intended until the 63 ids load.
