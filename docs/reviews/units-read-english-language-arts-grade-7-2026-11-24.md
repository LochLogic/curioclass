## Verdict

The six units form a sound arc: one long novella first, then informative, research, argument, poetry and drama, and a
return to the same river with evaluation and a second research project, with retrieval at every seam and a formal
discussion at least every ten days. The most serious cross-unit fault was unit 6's seam: its field 3 never named the
river knowledge and bounded-evidence habits unit 1 built, nor unit 2's first teaching of relevance and sufficiency,
and it credited unit 5 with an argument handoff unit 5 does not make. Four of six plans also left the field 5 Owner
column at the unit's first section for every item. Both are fixed; what remains is mostly days.csv and source work
for the lead.
