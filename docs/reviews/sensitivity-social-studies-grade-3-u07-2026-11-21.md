## Must-fix (3)
**M1. Q1b key says neighbors "kept a synagogue"; Card C says it is gone, and "work" is asked of ethnic groups.**
File: data/plans/social-studies-grade-3/items.csv, row s07-Q1b answer; u07-5/key-day159.md line 13; u07-5/packet-day159.md line 28 (Card C).
Text: "neighbors who kept homes and a synagogue (C)" (items.csv, same in key line 13). Card C: "The synagogue is gone and is now a parking lot."
Problem: the key contradicts the card it scores, and "kept homes" is not in U7S's status (neighborhood was Jewish, Italian and Irish; synagogue is a parking lot; Latino and Asian people came later). Q1b ("Name the work one of your groups did") also asks a child to name the "work" of Irish, Jewish or Italian neighbors, which Card C never gives, so the only way to answer is to guess a trait for a group. A child who answers from the card could be marked wrong.
Fix: reword Q1b to ask what the card says about the neighborhood (who lived there, what changed); key answers only from the three sentences in Card C.

**M2. Enslaved people are scored as one "group" and one kind of "work" in a list with a store and neighbors; "Caribbean" is unsupported.**
File: items.csv rows s07-Q1, s07-Q1b; u07-5/key-day159.md lines 12 and 13; packet-day159.md line 24 (Card B).
Text: Q1 answer "enslaved people forced to labor in the Caribbean trade (B), business owners such as Hayden (B), neighbors of Irish, Jewish and Italian families (C)". Q1b answer "Forced labor in the sugar trade (B), a clothing store and housing freedom seekers (B), or neighbors who kept homes and a synagogue (C)." Stem: "Name the work one of your groups did."
Problem: the stem calls what enslaved people did "work" and the key places "forced labor" as one pick beside "a clothing store" and neighbors, which is the people-beside-trades pattern the unit forbids. Card B says only "Enslaved people were forced to make the sugar and molasses"; it says nothing of the Caribbean, and U7F's status does not either, so the item adds a place no checked row supports.
Fix: write the stem as "what happened to one of your groups" or split enslaved people out of the "work" list; say "forced to make sugar" and drop "Caribbean" unless U7F's page is re-read and the row updated.

**M3. Note on map A teaches that the sugar trade "gave merchants and dock workers jobs"; no row supports it, and the note leaves out the forced labor.**
File: u07-5/packet-day159.md line 16; items.csv row s07-Q2b answer; key-day159.md line 16.
Text: "Ships carried cod and grain out and brought sugar and molasses in. The trade gave merchants and dock workers jobs."
Problem: U7F's status supports "trade and forced labor in the sugar trade", not jobs for merchants or dock workers. The note that explains why Boston grew names sugar and molasses as cargo and credits jobs, while the people forced to make them appear only on Card B. A child can earn full marks on Q2b with a growth story that has no enslaved people in it.
Fix: cut "merchants and dock workers jobs" (or add a checked source for it) and add to the note: "Enslaved people were forced to make the sugar and molasses."

## Should-fix (10)
**S1. Native nations in past tense.**
File: u07-1/d143.md line 28.
Text: "If the land then was home to Native nations or part of another country, say so from a page you have read, naming each nation by its own name."
Problem: "was home to" puts living nations in the past; the day teaches the opposite.
Fix: "If Native nations lived there then, say so, and say the nation lives on today where its own page says."

**S2. "Here today" can be wrong for a nation that was removed.**
File: u07-1/d142.md line 28; u07-1/d144.md line 31; items.csv rows s07-P141r2 and s07-P142c.
Text: "Say aloud that the nation is here today"; "say that the nation is here today"; answer "No. The nation is still here; a boundary does not end it."
Problem: true for the Massachusett (U7N), but d144 sends the teacher to another city (Denver is named at line 17) with no nation row. For nations forced from a place, "still here" is a claim about the town, not the nation.
Fix: "say that the nation is a living nation today, and where its page says its people live."

**S3. The Boston "town record" has no checked row except Wikipedia.**

**S7. "Move" and "came here" for people taken by force.**
File: u07-1/d145.md line 12 (title "Who came here, and how work changed"); u07-1/record.md line 26; u07-1/cards-day145.md lines 18 to 21; items.csv rows s07-E145, s07-P145d.

**S10. Day 147's tradition has no guard against a sacred or religious observance.**
File: u07-2/d147.md line 17.
