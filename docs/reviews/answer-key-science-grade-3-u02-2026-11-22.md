## Findings

1. **E2 does not test the plan's interaction points (key vs plan).** s02.md section 5 gives the interaction score as four question points, plus "one for a cause-and-effect reason from the new static record; one for explaining the new magnet evidence with revised arrows" (arrows before and after one magnet turns). E2 scores 2 points: the pull arrow across the gap, and gravity plus thread. The printed interaction-record.md has no static record and no turned-magnet arrow; it asks only "Draw every arrow on the tack" and "Why the arrow starts on the tack". So no E item scores the cause-and-effect reason, and turning arrows are never asked. Fix: add a turning-arrow line and a static-record reason line to interaction-record.md and rewrite E2 as three points (pull arrow with why it starts on the tack; gravity and thread; arrow reversed after a turn), or edit s02.md to the 2-point arrow score actually printed. The key's wrong sample ("No arrow across the gap, because the magnet does not touch the tack") correctly maps to error A.

2. **E3 is 3 points; the plan gives measurement 1 point.** Plan: "Measurement score: one additional point for all three ruler look-fors", 15 points in all, 14 of 15 on asking and defining (about 93 percent). The keys total 17 (4+2+3+2+3+3). E3 is also coded part "ask" under 3-PS2-3, but ruler reading is m2-measure-with-tools, not asking questions, so counting it toward the 93 percent is inaccurate. Fix: score E3 as 1 point (zero at one edge, the other edge read, "3 centimeters" with its unit), part "measure", and keep 15 in all; or change the plan to 17 and restate the percent (14 of 17 is about 82 percent). The key's wrong sample ("4 cm, read from the 1 mark") is right.

3. **Lid allowance differs between plan and printed days; E4 does not need interacting materials.** s02.md lid brief: four encased magnets, two cardboard pieces, three tape lengths, ten making minutes, six tokens, "at the prices in item 3" (one token each). d029.md and lid-record.md: two magnets, six making minutes, six tokens, magnet 2 tokens, tape length 1. E6's sample ("At most two magnets. Six making minutes. Six tokens.") matches the days, not the plan, and has no cardboard limit although the plan's materials limit counts cardboard. The days are what children use, so edit s02.md to match them. For E4, the plan awards one point for appropriate interacting materials and one for using distance and orientation evidence; the key's point 1 is only "names the materials the latch uses", so "wood and tape" would score. Fix: point 1 reads "names materials that interact (two magnets, or a magnet and steel)"; keep "Any metal will do" as the B-wrong sample. E4 is also coded 3-PS2-4 "name", but it tests a latch arrangement, not naming a design problem; rename the part to "match materials, gap and ends".

4. **E5's third point replaces the plan's judging points.** Plan definition score: need, several checkable criteria, materials limit, time limit, cost limit, and "a changed-user criterion that could judge a proposal". E5 point 3 is "one part named in the day 29 note was revised". d030.md lets the child revise any one thing the note names (it could be a limit or the gap), so the revision may belong to E4 or E6, and a child whose note named the limits cannot earn E5 point 3 from the need or criteria the stem tells them to check. The changed-user criterion appears only on P32-5, which is not an E item. Fix: make point 3 "a criterion revised or added for a changed user, still checkable" and add a line for it to lid-record.md, or state in E5's key that P32-5 supplies it.

5. **E1 key checks "one change" only.** The plan asks for fixed conditions and observable evidence, but the record sheet asks "I change, I keep, I watch" for one question only, so E1 cannot check it for all four. Acceptable as scored. The first sample (static distance) is a magnet question ("Does the clip lean more at a smaller gap?"). Fix: give the static sample with the comb or straw ("Do the paper bits cling more at a smaller gap?") so the four samples cover the four named variables. The wrong sample (two changes, error D) is right.

6. **K18-1 stem is ambiguous.** "A wonder: does the gap change how far the clip leans? Say what we change, keep the same and watch. Can we test the wonder, why is a magnet magic?" "The wonder" first means the gap question, then "why is a magnet magic" reads as the same wonder, so a child who answers "yes, we can test it" about the gap is marked against the key's "No". Fix: "A wonder: does the gap change how far the clip leans? Say what we change, keep the same and watch. A second wonder: why is a magnet magic? Can we test that one? Why or why not?" The key's last sentence then stands.

7. **K17-2 asks children to name arrows that card 3 already labels.** cards-gap-arrows.md card 3 prints the labels "magnet pull, gravity, thread pull", so the item tests copying. It is a worked example and unscored, so low priority. Fix: print the arrows unlabeled, or ask "Which arrow is the thread's, and how can you tell?".

8. **Scoring statements disagree across days.** d031.md and d032.md say the E records are scored after class, which fits the plan. d032.md also says the day 31 question sheets (s02-P31-1 to P31-4) "are scored after class too", but the plan says the day 31 and 32 fresh work is unscored ("eligible earlier work supplies its score"), the P31 and P32 rows are practice, and d031.md says the sheets go forward unchanged to day 35. Fix: change d032.md's launch to say the day 31 question sheets are not scored and go back on day 33. This sits outside the nine items but decides which items count.

9. **wrongs.csv code for E3.** The E3 row uses error code "x", outside the register A to F. Fine if the gate allows it; otherwise the note belongs under s02.md's "missing measurement evidence, no letter".
