**algebra-ii s03, R1 (field 4, gap response):** "Each card below takes ten minutes: three to reconstruct the earlier model, four to work with feedback and three for a fresh independent retry." There are five cards and day 46's slot holds one. The plan spreads the rest over days 47, 57 and 64 but never says what the slot holds when three or more gaps exceed a third.

**algebra-ii s07, R1, weak (field 4):** "run their ten-minute repairs consecutively within that day's 45 minutes, not as additional time." Day 141 then holds 20 repair minutes, a retrieval and the 45-minute modelling task. Weak because only two of the four gaps ever meet on one day.

## Which kinds look like one rule, and which look like unit rewrites

**One rule for the section-write step (covers R1 and S1, 6 of the 6 marked units):**
- R1: before writing a day's repair or retry slot, add up the repair minutes in the error register and the number of errors and items routed to it. If they do not fit, the plan must say which are cut, deferred to a named later slot, or done by a named subgroup. Do not accept "ten minutes for five cards".
- S1: for each shared item (scale, stick, tray, scope), the section-write step should state sets per group of about five and minutes per group, or switch to stations with a named adult.

**Small unit-plan edits, not rewrites:** algebra-ii s02, s03 and s07 need their gap-response paragraph in field 4 rewritten to match the rule. Science s02, s03 and s04 need one or two lines in field 8 (more sticks or scales, or a station plan). Nothing found needs a unit rewrite.

**No unit change needed from this measure:** M1, M2, S3 (unit-level evidence absent), R2. S2 and S4 on science s02 are single-line fixes if wanted (add a scale to day 23 or label the numbers as the backup, and add a mask and damp-towel note for dried soil).
