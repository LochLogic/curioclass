  opposite. Fix: leave "2 km: the leftover tenths dropped" untagged. G stays measured by 4.3b and E33, E35.
- **E28 (b), "1/6 or 3/6" tagged B.** Not seeing 1 as 6/6 is error C's "what it shows" (a whole not yet seen as
  n parts of size 1/n), not B's (a fraction stops at one). Fix: tag C.
