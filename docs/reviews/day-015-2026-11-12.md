## Verdict

The mathematics is right, the day keeps its promise of "no drawing" with a sketch always allowed, and the
missing-number sets, marked at once with a count of first tries and no clock, are the right shape for a fluency
day. The biggest risk is in the display and the reteaches rather than the items: the one "check" screen shows all
twelve answers at once, so after set 1 every student can see sets 2 to 4; and the E14 reteach fixes yesterday's
grouping error with a cutting example, while the error B reteach puts 7/5 and 12/10 on a board line that stops
at 1. The one change that matters most is finding 1: give each set its own `Check:` line, as day 16 does, so the
display shows one set's key at a time.
