# Ladder sample, science grade 3, set A (2026-11-19)

Read-only sample of the science ladder run (later.csv L81). Fresh reader, cold. Nothing was fixed. Gates run only with the two given filters.

Sources checked: each day file, the day before, the section plan block, DEFAULTS.md, the day's rows in days.csv, items.csv, wrongs.csv, the unit's errors.csv rows, handbacks.csv, every shared card the day names, and the ledger (`ledger.py show science --before science-grade-3`, saved to a temp file and grepped for course plus term). Keys were reworked by hand. Every number in the keys was checked against its own card or record (all arithmetic holds). Facts I could only judge, not check against a source: life-stage timings (zinnia, lady beetle), pool and fish temperature thresholds (14 and 21 degrees), cup-on-a-sill temperature difference, wolf and heron success pattern (all invented for practice, plausible).

## Ladder section

What the grade gap caused:
- Day 98 treats "season" as new although grade 1 taught it with its own local months and the word "fall"; the plan's own term note already says "adds summer and autumn" but the day does not. The gap (grade 1 to grade 3, with no season work in grade 2) is what the day should recall, in the same words.
- Day 90 assumes kindergarten sun and shade (sunlight warms water, shade keeps it off) without naming it; the cups are a fresh test of it, so the assumption stands unnamed.
- Day 61 uses grade 2's squared-paper bar graph (bars from the bottom line, labelled) with a new scale (one square a trip) and does not say it is a review.
- Day 56 uses "lady beetle" where grade 2 (u07-1 day 68, u07-2 day 70) used "ladybug" for the same animal (cards only, never an exit, so no rule 4 problem). Offspring, pupa, birth and reproduce all trace to grade 3 u03-1 (days 38, 39) and are used correctly. Use one word for one animal across grades.
- No day assumes something no lower grade or earlier unit taught. No day reuses a lower grade's case as an exit. Day 97 uses a pond-and-reed shape and a low-layer, high-layer fossil shape that earlier days of this grade already used (rule 4 findings above).

Use of the lower grades: mostly well. The grade 3 dosing of review is thin on day 98 and day 90; days 56 and 97 name their earlier units explicitly (u03-1 records, unit 5.1 layers and extinct). Day 61 and day 98 both call the claim, evidence and reason frame by name without saying it dates to grade 2 (u04-2 day 44); that frame is fresh in grade 3 unit 3.3, so it is fine here.
