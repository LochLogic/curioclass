## Ladder

Kindergarten is the first grade, so there is no lower grade to reuse or contradict. The ladder is inside the course.

- Day 11 teaches job and responsible as new, and the ledger lists them as new; unit 2 section 3 reviews "responsible" in the same words. It assumes only unit 1's charter and talk rules (days 5 to 10 taught them). It does not say why the charter is being retrieved (finding above).
- Day 24 teaches photograph, symbol and key as new; u04-2 and u05-2 later review photograph and symbol in the same words. It reviews "it stands for" (day 22), "next to" (day 20) and the claim frame (unit 1) in the same words, apart from the exit's "beside" in place of the taught "next to".
- Day 33 teaches nothing new and uses words from days 20, 21 and 29 (between, left, right, next to, far). Its one gap is that day 23's middle-of-map idea (item 8) has no fresh card.
- Day 50 teaches nothing; its "I think ___, because ___" is called a review from unit 1, in the same words. The grade-gap cost is in E1 and E11/E12, where the assessment's "fresh" cases are the taught cases of days 44, 48 and 49.
- Day 63 uses unit 6's words in the unit's wording (want: "stay well without"; trade: both say yes) and draws content from units 2 and 4 as the scope intends. The cost is rule 4: a farm bucket and a fence service were already case material.
- The course used its own earlier units well. The leaks are in assessments and respond days, which reuse earlier cases for the sake of retrieval, and in one word (beside for next to).
