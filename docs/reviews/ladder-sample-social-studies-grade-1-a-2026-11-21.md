## Ladder

- **What the grade gap caused.** Kindergarten taught the tools (wonder, rule, map from above, symbol and key, before and after, timeline, holiday, four look-back questions, claim frame) and the grade 1 days mostly name them as review in kindergarten's own words (d002 wonder and rule, d045 symbol and key, d023 President, d059 holiday and timeline). Three places where the gap bit: d078 relabels kindergarten's look-back frame ("It helped, but ___ remains" for "It helped (or did not) because ___") and still calls it review; d023 reviews Lincoln from Presidents' Day without reconciling kindergarten's "helped end slavery" with the day's limit on the paper; d045 maps carry the direction indicator that day 47 teaches as new.
- **New words for old ideas, old words for new.** "Closing" has two frames in the grade (day 28 "This matters because", day 78 "so"). The unit 6 claim frame is cited as a three-part "I think" frame it is not.
- **Lower-grade cases as exits.** None reused outright. d002's old clock beside a new clock is the same move as kindergarten's before and after chart; d078's dated pictures are the day's own model.
- **Overall.** Grade 1 used kindergarten well: reviews are named, nothing assumed that no lower grade taught, new vocabulary (history, geography, civics, economics; proclamation; united, law; region) is tagged new in the ledger. The weakness is not the ladder but exits that check the review skill or the day's own worked example rather than the new idea (d045, d023, d078, d059).
