## Unverified

These facts are not written as fact in the plans, or are written only in hedged form:

- **Black Hills award.** "More than $1.3 billion" was reported in 2023 by the Christian Science Monitor, not a wire service, and the $2 billion figure could not be confirmed. s06 says "now reported at more than $1 billion".
- **Tulsa agreement.** The Oklahoma Supreme Court reportedly ruled the June 2025 agreement invalid, 8 to 1, around May 29, 2026. The source is KGOU, not a wire service or the court's opinion. s03 says only "since challenged in Oklahoma's courts, so check its status".
- **Colfax upper bound.** The "about 150" end of the range comes from secondary reviews and was not checked independently. The 62 to 80 count (Ballantyne and Lane, July 28, 2026) and the 54 burials were confirmed.
- **The Archivist's replacement.** The February 7, 2025 removal is well reported, but who served as acting Archivist and when comes from secondary sources. s09 says only "an acting Archivist named".
- **Two older dates.** The Jones Act (March 2, 1917) and Philippine independence (July 4, 1946) are standard facts but were not checked against a primary source this pass.
