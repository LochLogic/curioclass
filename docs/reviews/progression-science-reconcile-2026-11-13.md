## Verdict

The blind grade 4 rows and the old scope and sequence agree on what grade 4 teaches: no expectation moves grade
and the bones (energy first, waves, life and light, Earth processes, hazards last) match. Of 25 differences the
old work won 19 and the new rows 6; 17 of the 19 old wins rest on a named source (mostly core idea elements and
articulation lines the blind rows left out) and 2 are judgment calls on order inside the life and light block and
the hazards row. The old work did not steer: its wins add detail the standard text asks for, and three of its
claims (engineering owned in full by grade 4, Life Cycles not a prerequisite, Weather and Climate not a
prerequisite) lost to the standard text.
