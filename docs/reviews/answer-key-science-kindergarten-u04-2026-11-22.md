## Findings

1. **M2a/M2b key contradicts the map (key error).** The map says the grassy bank "carries a swirl and a brown patch only (it is dry)", with no drop and no grass mark. Key M2b requires lines "to the bank (shelter), the grass (food), the pond (water) and the air". The pond is a different place on the map, and "grass" is not a drawn thing separate from the bank, so two required lines (shelter, food) go to the same object. M2b's stem says "in its place", so a child who stays on the bank and draws to the bank, the brown patch and the swirl is correct by the stem and marked short by the key. Key M2a also says an animal "where its resources are missing" is error E, yet the bank itself has no water mark. Fix: add a grass mark (green tufts) to the bank in the map; change M2b to "Draw lines from the animal to what it uses, in its place or beside it"; key M2b accepts a line to the bank or burrow (shelter), the grass (food), the pond (water, it sits beside the bank) and the swirl (air), and says four lines are the full mark. Note for a teacher line in the key: real prairie dogs get most water from plants, so the pond line is the map's rule, not a fact about prairie dogs.

2. **C3b has no picture (item cannot be answered as printed).** Stem: "Two routes for the channel bring the same water. One crosses a bare strip. The other crosses a patch of plants. Touch the route..." cards-assessment-argument's sequence C3 lists three pictures (dry garden, digging, green garden) and no route picture, and the pieces say no printed words. A child has nothing to touch. Fix: add a fourth panel, "C3 routes": the garden and stream drawn twice, route 1 across a bare strip, route 2 across a patch of plants, same line length. Key and d043 unchanged.

3. **N4a stem is ambiguous (fair alternative keyed wrong).** "Match each tag to who ate it" does not tie to the meal records. A child eats lettuce and seeds, and a turtle could eat fish, so "lettuce to the child" is a true answer. Also d041 asks N4a before N3a shows the records. Fix: "Use the meal records. Match each tag to the one who ate it there." Order the N3 panel face up before N4a, or ask N3a first.

4. **C2a stem ambiguous.** "Touch what the animal changed." The soil pile (middle picture) and the bank with a den both fit; the key accepts only the den picture. Fix: "Touch the picture that shows the change the animal made." and key accepts the finished den picture.

5. **C3a stem ambiguous.** "Touch the land the people changed." The green garden is land the people changed too, and wrongs.csv itself lists "Touches the stream" as a wrong that is really a misreading. Fix: "Touch what the people made." Key: the channel.

6. **N1a drops have nowhere to go.** The stem asks the child to put a drop on eight pictures, but the panel is "one copy for the front table" and mat box 1 holds only the caption "the water drops". d041 says "all five mark their own mat at once". Fix: "What new thing did the plants in the window get that helps them?" C1b "What does that help the plant get?" could be answered "room to grow"; fix: "What does the plant reach?" Key: water, or the damp ground.
