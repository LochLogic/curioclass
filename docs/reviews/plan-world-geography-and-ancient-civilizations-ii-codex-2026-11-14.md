## Course verdict

The course has a coherent sequence, substantial source work, careful treatment of uncertainty, and useful handoffs, but it needs revision before lesson writing. The most consequential defects are a missing clause of the original anchor standard, comparison assessed before its explicit instruction, missing daily evidence in unit 5, and several assessments whose response demands exceed their convincingly planned formats. ## Unit 2: Faiths and empires of ancient India

### 2. Serious: Ashoka’s missionary geography is missing from teaching and assessment

**Location:** [s02.md, fields 2 and 5](S:/CurioClass/docs/courses/world-geography-and-ancient-civilizations-ii/s02.md:28), day 19 and assessment item 4; also unit 5’s I-d comparison.  
**Confidence:** Firm.

The original MA.7.T1b.2d names missionary support involving North Africa, Central Asia, Southeast Asia and Mediterranean Europe. The local paraphrase drops those destinations. The plan consequently assesses missionary support and conversion uncertainty without assessing that geographical reach. Naming Sri Lanka and deferring “wider routes” to day 44 does not supply the missing evidence. See the [Massachusetts framework, printed page 97](https://www.doe.mass.edu/frameworks/hss/2018-12.pdf).

**Exact fix:**

- Add those four regions to the unit’s content-parts checklist.
- On day 19, compare a map of destinations named in Ashoka’s declarations with destinations in later Buddhist mission traditions.
- Add this requirement to assessment item 4:

> Identify the four regions named by the standard. Distinguish a ruler’s claim of moral influence, a later account of a mission, and independently supported travel. Explain why none supplies a conversion count.

Use [Rock Edict XIII](https://accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/dhammika/wheel386.html) and [Mahavamsa XII](https://lakdiva.org.lk/mahavamsa/chap012.html) with their different dates and purposes. Do not convert the standard’s wording into an unsupported claim that every proposed journey is established. Add the same geographical distinction to unit 5’s I-d reference card.
