# Ladder sample, science grade 2, reader B (2026-11-19)

Read-only sample of the science ladder run (later.csv L81), Curio Class. Fresh reader; nothing edited but this report. Days: d040, d051, d053, d057, d061. Each was read cold with the day before in full, its plan block, DEFAULTS.md, the days.csv row, the unit's errors.csv rows, the handbacks rows and every shared piece in `leans_on`. items.csv and wrongs.csv hold no rows for these days except d057 (c1-M57a to c1-H57c); the other four days keep their exits in the days.csv `exit` column and in the cards-exits files, so those were the keys reworked.

What was checked against a source: every number in the cards and keys (heights, centimeters, ruler marks, minute sums) was recomputed by hand; terms were checked against `ledger.py show science --before science-grade-2` (saved in my own temp folder and grepped); keys were checked against the card files and the unit plan. What I could only judge: the plant biology (dark-grown seedlings tall and pale, light and leaning), hook-and-loop seed carrying, pollen, snowball re-freezing, cooked noodle staying soft. All read as true to me; I looked nothing up outside the repository.

Gates run (filtered as instructed): `plan.py check` and `lesson.py check`. Output saved in my temp folder. No gate failure names a content fault in these five days; the failures that touch them are template ones (see TEMPLATE rows).

## Ladder

The grade gap caused word drift, not wrong content. Kindergarten and grade 1 gave fair test (keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing), the design cycle ("problem, sketch, build, test") and shape and job. Grade 2 uses all three, but renames them: "what changed, stayed the same, is watched" (d053), "define" for "problem" (d060 and d061), and "use" for "job" (d040). Each is the same idea under a new word, so a child who learned the earlier word has to relearn it. "Condition" is reused for two meanings inside one unit (d050 treatment, d051 to d053 how it looks).

This grade used the lower grades well in four places: d057's check reuses unit 1 words (model, key, caption, source, map, not to scale) and the unit 2 model limit; d061 retrieves ruler zero from unit 2 day 16 and the seed and powder routes from d060; d051 and d053 retrieve the "taller is not healthier" idea in the unit's own sequence; d040 builds on d039 without leaning on a lower grade. None of the five days assumes something no lower grade or earlier unit taught. No exit reuses a lower grade's case: the one risk was the ramp in the unit 4 plan's draft E40 (kindergarten unit 3), which the card (stage floor) already avoids, and the exit sets use new plants (sunflower, basil, chive, mitten).

Where days teach an old idea as new without naming it: d040 (shape and job, ledger "first taught day 40"), d051 (fair test), d061 (the model limit). Each needs one spoken line naming the earlier lesson in its own words.
