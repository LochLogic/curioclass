# Open science material for Curio Class: is there an Illustrative Mathematics for science?

Read 2026-11-12. Research notes, not legal advice. Every licence below was read on the publisher's own page on that
date unless marked "unverified". Nothing in the repo was changed.

## The short answer

- **Only for grades 6 to 8.** OpenSciEd Middle School is CC BY 4.0 (OpenSciEd's own licensing page) and rated
  all-green by EdReports (February 2023, per OpenSciEd and BSCS announcements). It is the one science program that
  plays the role Illustrative Mathematics plays for math: a top-rated, complete, CC BY base.
- **Not for K to 5.** OpenSciEd Elementary is complete (all 24 units, K to 5, marked Released) but every unit page
  reads "Licensed under OpenSciEd's Creative Commons NonCommercial Plus 4.0 International License". That is
  CC BY-NC plus a paid commercial agreement. Treat it like Eureka: reference only. No CC BY K to 5 program was found.
- **Not for high school, mostly.** OpenSciEd Biology, Chemistry and Physics moved to CC BY-NC on 2023-10-18 (rated
  all-green by EdReports, 2025). OpenStax switched its live science books to CC BY-NC-SA. The one CC BY exception
  is OpenStax **Physics** (high school, Texas Education Agency), and even there OpenStax's own images, activities and
  assessments are CC BY-NC-SA.
- **The trend is closing, not opening.** OpenSciEd (2023), OpenStax, CK-12 (2021), Concord Consortium and PhET (2026)
  have all moved to tighter terms. Whatever CC BY copy Curio Class uses, save a dated copy with its licence page now.
- **Public domain federal material (NASA, NOAA, USGS) and pre-2026 PhET simulations (CC BY 4.0) are safe assets** for
  every course, but they are pictures, data and simulations, not a course. K to 5, Biology, Chemistry, Earth and
  Space and Environmental Science are "write our own, with references".

## Traps

1. **NC and SA cannot sit inside a CC BY page.** Copying NC text or NC-SA text into Curio Class content would make that
   part NC or NC-SA, which breaks the LICENSE-CONTENT.md promise. Reference means: read for order, phenomenon, time,
   misconceptions; write every word, figure and question ourselves.
2. **Two OpenSciEd licences on one site.** The site's own knowledge base still says "all" units are CC BY, while
   elementary and high school unit pages say NonCommercial Plus. Check the licence on each unit page before use,
   and keep the dated page.
3. **Middle School 2.0 may change terms.** If Curio Class adapts from the current CC BY version, save that version
   (with its licence statement) now; the CC BY grant on copies already received does not expire.
4. **Third-party assets inside open units.** Phenomenon videos, photos, readings and data sets inside OpenSciEd units
   are likely owned by others and not covered (unverified). Replace each with a public domain or our own asset.
5. **Login gate.** Full OpenSciEd unit downloads need an account, which means accepting OpenSciEd's terms of use.
   Someone should read those terms in full before an account is made.
6. **AI clauses.** OpenStax Physics forbids LLM training without permission; OpenSciEd's commercial agreement and
   CK-12 restrict AI use. Curio Class builds with AI agents. Feeding a document to a model to adapt it is not the
   same as training, but the wording should be read by a person before agents work from these files.
7. **Credit lines and trademarks.** Name the source (for example "Adapted from OpenSciEd Unit 6.1, CC BY 4.0, link,
   changes made"), but do not use OpenSciEd, OpenStax, PhET, NASA or USGS logos, and do not word credit to imply
   endorsement. PhET requires its exact credit line and visible logo. NASA insignia is not public domain.
8. **"NGSS" is a trademark** with its own rules (already noted in APPROACH-SOURCES.md).
9. **Kit costs.** OpenSciEd assumes a kit per unit or a materials list. Prices not read (unverified). A free site's
   teachers will need a household stand-in list per investigation, which NEXT-SPINES.md item 3 already asks for.
   Middle and high school units also assume probes, sensors and lab ware.
10. **Licences are tightening.** OpenSciEd 2023, OpenStax (date not read), CK-12 2021, PhET 2026, Concord now NC.
    Snapshot every source used, with its licence page and the date.
