## Verdict

Would not pass yet. The course is strong on safety, cost, models that carry forward, bilingual family notes and respond days. It falls short on two things the rubric counts: (1) several core ideas are told or shown by the teacher rather than figured out by children (unit 6 most of all, and unit 1's data are invented, not the class's own place), and (2) scoring does not cover every element the plan claims (unit 6 never scores pollination; unit 1 never scores the pattern; unit 4 pairs two unrelated expectations under one question). That gives five part passes (S1, S2, S3, S9, S11) against a limit of two, and EQuIP totals below 8 on all three sampled units. S5 safety and S10 accuracy, the two doors, hold on what I read. Every finding below is fixable inside the units; none needs a redesign.
