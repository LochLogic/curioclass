## Findings

### 1. Key error: a fair alternative is keyed wrong (E7, with E8, E10; wrongs.csv E7)
E7 answer: "A matching plant in the sun that gets two spoonfuls of water every day, the healthy plant." The items.csv wrong sample and the wrongs.csv row (error A) are "A plant in the dark with no water." That plant is a fair control: against P (dark, watered) it differs only in water; against Q (sun, unwatered) it differs only in light. The unit limit is "Each test changes only one thing at a time", and both pairs meet it. Error A in the register is the dark-to-dry comparison (P against Q, two changes at once), which this child did not draw. A child with a correct design is marked wrong.
Fix: accept either added plant. E7 answer: "A matching plant that makes both comparisons fair: sun with two spoonfuls a day (the healthy plant), or dark with no water." Change the E7 wrong sample to "A plant in the sun with no water" (it copies Q, so it adds no comparison) and the wrongs.csv shown text to match. E8 answer: "Lines from P and from Q to the added plant, each pair differing in one thing: with the healthy plant, P against it for light and Q against it for water; with the dark, dry plant, P against it for water and Q against it for light." E10 answer: "P gets two spoonfuls every day and Q none; the added plant gets two spoonfuls, on the same days as P, if it is sunny and watered, or none if it is dark and dry." The s05.md look-for "adds a watered sunlight control" should be reworded the same way (a plan doc line, so the lead edits it).

### 2. Item ambiguous: the picture shows the start, the stem asks about the end (E5 to E12)
plan-sheet-day54.md: "Both are drawn at 4 centimeters with three green leaves." E5 says "P is tall and pale. Q wilted." Nothing the child can look at shows tall, pale or wilted, and the paragraph is read once with no repeat (12 minutes, 8 boxes). E9 also needs "same temperature, matching cups and soil, 4 centimeters", all heard once. P's darkness rests on the word "opaque" ("stands behind an opaque screen"), which is not in the day's word list and is not a grade 2 word.
Fix: add a second picture "after two weeks" (P tall, pale, leaning; Q small, limp), with "invented for practice" under both; draw the screen as a dark box or shadow and say "a dark screen that blocks the light" in the read-aloud; print the matched facts (same cups, soil, room, 4 cm start) as a short picture strip so E9 tests knowing what to keep alike, not remembering a sentence.

### 3. Item cannot be marked as printed (E14)
E14 stem: "Look at your record from day 52. Say or write what you did with your plant and what you recorded." Key answer: a first-person account. d055 never prompts it (the student page holds only E13, E15, E16), and its marking section scores the two conducting points "from the day 52 record with its appended steps", a written table of date, condition, height, leaves. A table line cannot hold "I measured our watered dark plant from the soil", and the "undated group number" wrong sample cannot be told from a record. Pick one: score the record (stem: "Your day 52 record and the steps you were seen doing are marked"; key: date, condition, height and look present, and the appended steps show the ruler's zero at the soil), or add the account as a spoken prompt on day 55.

### 4. Item gives away what it tests (E1)
"Put the zero of the ruler where the soil is, and tell us how tall..." hands the child the first look-for ("puts the ruler's zero at the soil"). d054 says "no hint". E13 does not do this. Fix: "Read your class plant. Show where you put your ruler and tell us how tall it is to the whole centimeter." Also E1 and E13 have no tolerance: add "within 1 centimeter of the adult's own reading" to the key, since no number is keyed.

### 5. Look-for too loose (E12)
Answer: leaves, their color, how many, the stem. Step: "includes the leaves and the stem or condition beside height". "Or condition" lets a bare "how it looks" pass. Fix the step to "names the leaves or the stem (color, number or firmness) beside height", matching the plan's "leaves and condition".

### 6. Data caution (E15, E16)
The printed card says P is 15 cm and the added plant is 10 cm with five leaves; s05.md's own invented record says 13 cm and 10 cm with seven leaves. Card, plan record and key should agree (the card is what prints, so update the plan line). More important: P grew more than the added plant, so wrong answer C ("P got water, so a plant does not need light") is half supported by height. The key already says "grow healthy", which settles it, but the stem should too: "Tell us what the comparison shows about light and how healthy the plant is." The stems also name the pair and say "same water" and "same light", which supplies the first look-for; acceptable at grade 2, but drop it if the lead wants the item to test more.
