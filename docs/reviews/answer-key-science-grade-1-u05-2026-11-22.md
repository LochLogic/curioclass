## Findings

1. **Fair answer keyed wrong (s05-E46b, error D).** wrongs.csv: "The mirror needs a fourth group." It is marked error D, "two sorting questions mixed", and the key repeats it: "the mirror needs no fourth group (D)". The standard sorts materials four ways. s05.md line 20 reads: "clear, partly clear, blocking or mirror-like". The NGSS statement also lists redirecting light as a fourth outcome. So a child who says the mirror is its own kind because it sends light to the side has given the standard's answer, but the course's own frame (mirror in the no-light-through group, plus a separate yes-or-no question about redirecting) would score it wrong. Fix: error D fires only when the child loses the blocking, for example "the mirror lets light through a new way" or "the mirror is not in the no-light group". In wrongs.csv, change the s05-E46b D row's shown to "The mirror lets the light through to the side, so it is not a no-light piece." In the item answer, change "the mirror needs no fourth group (D)" to "putting the mirror in the no-light group is the course's way; calling mirror-like its own kind is also right if the child says no light went through to the screen behind; D is only saying light went through the mirror". The plan row in s05.md line 95 needs the same change.

2. **The picture cannot show what the key needs (s05-E46a).** cards-day46.md asks for "plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading". It also calls each card "a real, unlabelled record made by an adult". In a line drawing with no shading, the cupboard with outside light absent looks the same as with outside light present, so the child cannot see that the label was not seen. The item depends on that look. The rule also clashes with "real record" (a drawing is not a recording), and d046.md says "Recordings are called recordings". Fix, in cards-day46.md: "Item 1 records are dated photographs taken by an adult, printed as they are. The dark looks are printed dark. The page is not lightened, and no result is written on it. The thick-line, no-shading rule applies to the pieces' pictures on the response sheet, not to these records."

3. **The sheet gives one answer box where the item needs two (s05-E46a).** sheet-response.md has one pair of boxes, "the thing we saw" and "why we saw it", and d046.md says children "draw in the boxes the thing we saw and why we saw it". The steps score the label and the little light separately ("marks the label's paired looks", "marks the little light's paired looks"). A child who draws only one object loses a step because of the sheet, not because of what they know. Fix: make it two rows, "the label: when we saw it, why" and "the little light: when we saw it, why". In d046.md, change the line to "draws in both rows when we saw the label and the little light, and why".

4. **The item claims parts the stem never asks for (s05-E46b).** The item's part column is "plan-fair,three-groups,turns-light,revise-model", and the plan prompt ends "Record evidence and revise your model". But the stem only says "Place your piece in the beam and say what the two screens showed", and none of the five steps asks the child to sort into the three groups or change the chart. As written, three-groups and revise-model are never checked on day 46. Fix: add a step, "says which group the piece goes in on our chart (all, some or no light through) and adds or changes one thing on the chart". Add to the stem: "Then say which group your piece goes in on our chart." Or drop those two parts from the item and let day 47's revision carry them, if the plan agrees.

5. **The polarity of the error labels is mixed (both items).** E46a's answer ends "Waiting alone makes the unlit label visible (A); the little light needs another lamp (B)." These are the wrong ideas, stated as plain facts. E46b's answer states the correct ideas with the error letter: "Moving the light between pieces is not fair (E)". A teacher reading E46a quickly could take "the little light needs another lamp" as part of the key. Fix: put "Errors:" before each list and state each one as the wrong idea. In E46a: "Errors: A, thinks waiting alone makes the unlit label visible; B, thinks the little light needs another lamp." In E46b: "Errors: C, thinks the side patch is light through the mirror; D (see finding 1); E, moves the light between pieces."

6. **Minor (s05-E46b).** The guess row on sheet-response.md offers only "bright / dim / shadow" under the mirror picture, so a child who rightly predicts the side patch has nowhere to ring it. Fix: under the mirror only, add "side patch: yes / no". This matches day 44's separate yes-or-no question. d046.md and the response sheet also disagree on who fills the "what I saw" row. The day says "score all four effects from each child's what I saw row", while the sheet says "you mark the what I saw row". Fix: the child draws in the row during the waiting turns, and the teacher writes dictated words.
