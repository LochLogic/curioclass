## Mismatches

Key error: none.

Item ambiguous (fix the item or key):

1. **s03-E15, key too narrow, premise untaught.** Key: "People can test the bamboo for the property the job needs ... before they choose it." The steps require the child to say people test the bamboo. My cold answer, "the pole stands straight and does not bend, so use something like it for the rail", is a fair reading of "how could knowing about bamboo help", and it would fail the steps line "says people test the bamboo". Bamboo appears nowhere in unit 3 (only the opener's "wood from a tree is tested for bend or dent"), and the packet's picture is only one sentence. Fix: add to steps and key "or says the pole's property, such as standing straight without bending, is what the rail needs, and links it to the rail job". Keep "Any rail will do" as the wrong sample.
2. **s03-E5, evidence comes from a caption.** The record shows only "Picture: ice cubes sit in a tray beside a bathtub of warm water." There is no drawing. The steps require "keeps its shape" and "flows". Fair answers such as "ice is hard and stays in cubes" and "the bath water splashes or pours, and takes the tub's shape" would not match those words. Fix: draw the picture (cubes with sharp corners, the tub with a level surface), and accept hard, stays in cubes, pours, splashes or fills the tub.
3. **s03-E6, chart-bound.** The key says "checked against the answer chart". A true shared property that the chart does not list, such as "all three are solid" or "all three are 10 centimeters square", would be marked wrong. Fix: "checked against the inspected samples or the chart", and accept any property true of both samples.
4. **s03-E3, two chart cells are soft.** The cotton line reads "lets a little light through" but the box is a binary "light through?". The cotton "dents when pressed" is a gentle blunt-stick press on fluffy cloth that may spring back. A child's honest "yes" or "no" on the light box, or "no dent" on the cotton, is a fair observation. Fix: in the key, "credit what the sample showed, even where the chart cell says otherwise", and add that the teacher fixes the chart line from the real sample (the chart already says this, the item key does not).
5. **s03-E1, feel is a gentle test too.** Steps list "press, bend or light through". "I will rub it to see if it is rough or smooth, the same way each time" is a valid test and steady condition and is not named. Fix: add "feel" to the steps.
6. **s03-E4, bad wrong-sample wording.** The sample "The wood and the cotton ... are both light, and the plastic ... is shiny" reads as light (weight) against "lets light through", the box name on the record. The fault is two rules, not the word light. Reword to "both light in weight" or swap the property.
