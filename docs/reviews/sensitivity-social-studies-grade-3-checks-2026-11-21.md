## Must-fix (1)

**M1. Day 139 practice map answer is wrong ("west"); the arrow points left, so the dock is north.**
File: u06-4/key-exits-139.md line 12; u06-4/cards-day139.md line 14; item sc3-P139a (answer and sample "West").
Text: "P139a: west. The arrow points to the left edge, so the left of the page is the arrow's way." Card: "The arrow points to the left edge. The dock is on the left side of the page from the mill."
Problem: the course convention (d069/packet-check1 line 14: arrow to the left edge, market on the right, answer "south"; cards-day71 line 14: arrow to the right edge, pond on the left, answer "south") makes the arrow's edge north. Dock on the arrow's side of the mill is north. "West" teaches a wrong reading of the arrow and would mark a correct child wrong. The key's own reasoning ("the arrow's way") says north.
Fix: change the key line and the item answer and sample to "north", and keep "Not 'up'".

## Should-fix (6)

**S1. Day 123 key reasoning for the claim is not in any checked row.**
File: u05-8/key-check2.md line 21; item sc2-E4 (answer, stem).
Text: "E4: a claim that answers: fishing and whaling are part of Makah ways of life, and the Makah wanted to keep them." Stem: "Write a claim: why did the Makah keep fishing and whaling rights?"
Problem: C2A cuts "the reasons for the treaty (the text states none)". C2B supports only "retain whaling rights" and "protect health, education and welfare", and "ways of life" appears there only for what agents tried to change. "Part of Makah ways of life" is our inference, and the stem asks the child to give a reason the sources do not state. Fishing is also not in C2B.
Fix: reword the stem to "Write a claim: what did the Makah want to keep in the treaty?" and the key to "the right to fish and hunt whales and seals, and their whaling rights", the reason resting on C2B's stated purpose only.

**S2. Day 123 teacher text calls a reworded card "the treaty text".**
File: u05-8/d123.md lines 16 and 24; u05-8/key-check2.md line 17; u05-8/packet-check2.md line 32.
Text: "Source A is the treaty text from 1855; Source B is the Makah Tribe's own page" (d123 line 24); card line 32: "In our words:".
Problem: line 16 and the card say the sources are printed in our words; lines 24 and key line 17 say Source A is the treaty text. Both rows say "Printed in our words" (C2A) and "paraphrase only" (C2B). A child could think the words are the treaty's or the Tribe's own.
Fix: "Source A is our wording of the 1855 treaty; Source B is our wording of the Makah Tribe's own page."

**S3. Card 4 adds a sequence and an audience no row states.**
File: u05-8/packet-check2.md lines 32 and 34.
Text: "Written then, for both sides." and "After that, government agents, missionaries and teachers tried to change Makah ways of life."
Problem: C2A records no audience. C2B lists agents, missionaries and teachers without saying "After that". Line 34 also softens C2B's "sought to assimilate", which is the unsupported-in-both-directions risk on a sensitive point.
Fix: drop "Written then, for both sides." and "After that"; write "Government agents, missionaries and teachers tried to change Makah ways of life, to make them assimilate." only if "assimilate" is kept in the checked wording, otherwise leave the current phrase.

**S4. Mayor items let a child credit a mayor with policy that U2H gives to the Select Board.**

Fix: stem "Name one job a mayor or Select Board does for a town." or limit the key to "proposes the budget".
