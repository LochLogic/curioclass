## Findings

1. **E22 versus day 46 marking note (item ambiguous).** d046.md Marking says: "A card with no cooling record is not enough yet, never no." The item E22 asks "Say yes or no", the key says "No. We need to see what happens when it cools", the step requires "says no", and the student page says "Yes or no." A teacher who reads the marking note will mark a correct "No" as wrong, or will not know whether "no" is the credited word. Fix: replace the marking sentence with "On butter two, 'No, it is not enough yet' earns the point when the child says a cooled result is needed. Never credit 'no, it will not return'." Keep the item and key as they are.

2. **E20 stem leads (item ambiguous).** "Say how the cold changed the cucumber, even after it thawed." The phrase "even after it thawed" tells the child the change stayed, which is the answer to E19 (remained), asked just before on the same sheet. Fix stem: "Look at the cucumber record again. Say how the cold changed the cucumber." Key wording is also clumsy: "Cooling changed the firm slice, so that it was soft after thawing." Fix key: "Cooling changed the firm slice to soft, and it stayed soft after thawing."

3. **E5 and E11 identical pieces (fair alternative).** The rocket has two rectangles and two triangles, the bus two rectangles and two circles. A child may draw lines from either rectangle to either rectangle in the new whole. The key says "one from each old piece to its place" and does not say that either pairing of identical pieces counts. Fix: add to the E5 and E11 answers "Either pairing of two matching shapes is correct."

4. **E4 and E5 draw from a covered whole (procedure).** d045.md "Moves and pack up" says to "cover it with the record", then E4 asks the child to draw the new object and E5 to match pieces. The child cannot see the whole if it is covered. d046.md repeats it. Fix: say "cover it with the record only while moving; lift it to draw."

5. **Key coverage for cause items (gap).** key-argument-records-day46.md gives returned or remained for each record and the butter two result but not the answers to E14, E16, E18, E20 (heating made butter liquid, heating made dough firm, cooling froze the juice, cooling made the cucumber soft). The answers exist only in items.csv. Fix: add one line per record to the key file.

6. **wrongs.csv gaps (low).** No rows for E14, E16, E18, E20, although items.csv samples name a wrong answer for each ("The butter just changed by itself", "The cookie was always firm", "The juice stayed liquid", "The cucumber did not change"). The sheet then cannot say which error to file. Add rows with a note like those on E1.

7. **E17 wrong tag (low).** "Returned, because it froze, so it went back" is tagged D (before and after alone prove reversal). It fits roughly: the child counts the freezing, the first change, as the return. Keep, but the D repair (add a third observation) should be read with this case.

8. **E21 asks less than the plan's look-for (low).** The plan point says the child connects both kinds of result to the general claim; the E21 step only requires "some changes go back and some stay." The key sample names the four cases, which meets the plan. Consider adding "names one that went back and one that stayed" to the step.

9. **Picture dependence (watch).** E15, E17, E19 depend on the pictures showing firm versus soft and frozen versus flowing in plain line drawing. The captions are read aloud, so the key holds, but the artist's note in argument-records-day46.md should ask for visible droop on the thawed cucumber and a visible block on the frozen juice.

10. **Sample inconsistency (cosmetic).** E9's sample whole is a robot; E12's sample reason uses a wheel. Both are only examples, but a robot with a wheel reads oddly. Change E12 sample to "The rectangle is flat, so it makes a robot's body" or leave.
