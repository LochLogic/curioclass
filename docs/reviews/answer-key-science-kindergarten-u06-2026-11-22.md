## Verdict
Pass with fixes. The keys are right and each item tests part (a), (b) or (c) of K-ESS3-3 as the plan says. The steps for T2a and T2b are too narrow for the "any feasible action" the key promises, and the T1 touch items have no picture in front of each child.

## Findings

1. **T1a and T1b have nothing to touch on the mat (item ambiguous, fix the piece).** d060 says "all touch together" and "mats in front", but mat-response.md has two empty boxes labeled "what people do here" and "what changes", and the scene is a single copy "at the front table". Five children touching one picture together will copy each other, and the teacher cannot tell whose touch is whose. Fix: say in d060 that each child gets the scene (a copy per child, or the group sitting so each can reach it) and touches it before talking, and that the two mat boxes are for the child to draw in if they want, not the touch. If copies are not wanted, ask T1a and T1b one child at a time in the 12-second hearing.

2. **T1a and T1b each have a second fair touch (item ambiguous).** The scene also has a school door and a gate, so "what people do here" can fairly be answered by touching the door or gate (people go through them), and "what changes" can fairly be answered by touching the footprints (the ground changed) or the upright plants as the contrast. Key text: "Touching the bent stems instead is shown with help and has no letter" scores a child who touches the stems for T1a as help, which is right, but a child touching the door is not covered. Fix, in the T1a stem: "Touch what people do to the bed of plants." In the T1b key add: "Touching the footprints and saying the ground changed is accepted with a follow-up: which part bent?"

3. **T2a steps reject a fair answer (key too narrow).** The key says "Any feasible action is accepted" but the steps are "draws a changed way for feet to go | the new way does not cross the plants". A child who draws a sign, a rope or a fence at the bed, or who draws the gate at the bed, changes where feet go only indirectly and would miss step 1. The scene lists a sign and a gate among the four action cards, so these are expected answers. Fix: steps become "draws a change that keeps feet off the plants (a path beside the bed, a way around, a fence, a rope or a sign) | the change does not send feet across other plants". Add a sample "Draws a fence along the bed."

4. **T2b steps follow the narrow T2a (key too narrow, small).** "draws feet on the new way" fails the child who drew a fence or sign. Fix: "draws feet on the new way, or draws feet kept off the plants". Also the key says "Plants standing upright where the footprints were". Flattened stems may not stand up again, and a K child may fairly draw the bed with new plants, or just no new footprints. Fix: "plants that are not bent (upright or no new footprints on them)".

5. **Error D is invited by the picture on purpose, and the scene is fine.** The scene draws a plain border strip, T3 sample 2 is "A pretty border tells people what to do", and error D fires from it. This is a good trap and the key's rule (an action plus a part spared) is clear. No change. One note: the scene says the gate sits on the right and the action cards include "a gate", so a child may draw a gate at the bed or a gate at the school. Both are feasible; finding 3's wording covers it.

6. **T1b sample and steps name a spoken error on a touch item (minor).** Step 2 "does not say walking cannot change the plants" and error G are spoken, while the stem says touch. The d060 teacher hears each child for 12 seconds, so it works, but the stem should invite a word: "Touch what changes, and tell me what changed it."

7. **Plan goal (parts a, b, c).** T1 checks that the child sees the impact (part c's premise), T2a and T2b check draw and lessen harm, T3 checks talk. All three parts are covered. Unit limits are honored (no decomposition, no cause claim from counts). No change.

## Three worst

2. Finding 1: no per-child picture for the touch items, so T1 results are copied.
3. Finding 2: a second fair touch for T1a and T1b is not covered.
