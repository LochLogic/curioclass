## Findings

### 1. c1-P35a, key error (worst)

Key: "The roots take in water." Step: "says the roots take in water". Day 10 taught: "Expect: hold the plant and take in water. Say 'roots take in water and hold'", and day 11 repeats it. A child who says "the roots hold the plant in the ground" names a true root job the course taught, and is marked not yet seen.
Fix: answer "The roots take in water and hold the plant in the soil. Either job counts." Step: "says the roots take in water, or hold the plant in the soil". Sample shown: add "Says the roots hold the plant in the ground."

### 2. c1-C35c, item ambiguous (worst)

Stem: "Circle yes or no for each: touched the dish, a gap above, one open side." It does not say what touched the dish (the cover, or the sliding marker). C35b's key says the cover stands "without resting on it", so "touched the dish" can be read either way. The step "circles, for each of the three, what the slide showed" is also off: a gap above and an open side are features of the cover, not results of the slide.
Fix: stem "Slide the marker once toward your cover from the taped line. Circle yes or no for each: the marker touched the dish; my cover has a gap above the dish; my cover has one open side." Same three lines on the d035 student page ("What the marker showed" becomes "What the marker showed and what my cover has"). Step: "circles yes or no for the marker line from what the slide showed, and for the two cover lines from the cover, as the adult watched".

### 3. c2-M67a, step the day never asks for (worst)

Stem: "Touch what the friend marked at each mark." Step: "points to the friend's actual mark at each place". d067 Group turns says: "Nothing else is asked at the lane." No other part of the day asks it, so the second step can never be seen.
Fix in d067 Group turns: replace "Nothing else is asked at the lane." with "Then **Ask.** 'Touch what the friend marked at each mark.' Nothing else is asked at the lane."

### 4. c1-L36a, step needs a label the card does not carry

Step: "points to the tips on the tape: first young shortest, then second young, then the parent". The card has "no printed words" and only says "the parent's longest, one young blade shortest, the other between"; nothing tells a child which young blade is "first" or which blade is the parent. A child who points to the different tips and says "not the same" has shown the skill.
Fix: step "points to the tips on the tape and says they are not the same length". Card: put a small parent-plant picture at the base of the parent blade so the parent can be found.

### 5. c2-V66a, "dark" is not dark enough

Stem: "Look 1 is taken at night with the door open and every lamp in the house off." At night with lamps off a room still gets window, street or moon light, and eyes adapt, so a child who says "if I wait, I can see it" (wrong M) is describing something true. 1-PS4-2 needs a case with no light at all.
Fix (stem and cards-check-66-68.md): "Look 1 is taken at night with the door open, every lamp off and no light at all reaching the shelf."

### 6. c2-M67b, the card row gives the order away

Card: "Sender, device and receiver picture cards in a row." If they lie in that row, touching "in order" is just left to right.
Fix (cards-check-66-68.md): "Sender, device and receiver picture cards, laid out mixed, never in path order."

### 7. c1-S35b, moon speed (minor science)

Stem: moon "low above the chimney" at 7 and "high above the water tower" at 9. The real moon takes about six hours to climb from low to its highest, not two. The keyed answer (farther along the path, lower) is the only option that follows the path, so the key stands, but the times model a moon about three times too fast.
Fix: "at 7 o'clock low above the chimney, and at 9 o'clock higher above the water tower ... or higher still above the radio mast"; key "Higher still above the radio mast: the moon moves along its path." Wrongs: the B row stays; the x row "back at the start" stays. Also cards-check-35-37.md says "Three place cards to touch" while d035 has children mark the page: change to "three places marked on the student page".

### 8. Minor wording, not scored

- d035 At home ("what a plant did when its window moved") and d037 At home ("the window moved and the leaves turned"): the pot turned, the window did not. Write "when its pot was turned" and "the pot turned and the leaves turned back to the window".
