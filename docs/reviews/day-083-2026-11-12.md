## Verdict

Day 83 is correct mathematics in a good order: one story told both ways with the same numbers, the extra piece
against the copies, a table that shows the two comparisons growing apart, four stories each drawn both ways, then
the "times more than" talk the Progressions ask for, and a fix. The biggest risk is that its paper does not hold its
pictures: the practice boxes are about three quarters of an inch for three strips and no answers, the exit asks for

## Findings, most serious first

### 1. Error A is used for the opposite mistake, and the key prints a definition that contradicts it

- **What is wrong.** The register defines A one way: "Reads 'times as many' as 'more than'". Day 83 is the first
  day both comparisons sit side by side, and its likeliest new mistake is the reverse: multiplying in a "more than"
  story. The rows label that reverse mistake A too: `s10-K83-1` wrong 12 ("multiplied: the 2 read as copies"); the
  practice rows' "both 24", "both 45" and so on; the exit's "both 8" and "Mo 8". The key then prints, beside Eli's
  12 in the "more than" story, "error A, reads 'times as many' as 'more than': 5 times as many as 3 is 8", which is
  the other direction. Day 84's response for many A on the slip ("ask which one the words for Di say") only treats
  Di, not a Mo who was multiplied.
- **Evidence.** d083-key.html, row `s10-K83-1`; errors.csv stretch 10 row A; d084.md "E83 back". From memory:
  using multiplication where an additive comparison is meant, once multiplication has been taught, is a well
  documented over-use (the "illusion of linearity" studies of Van Dooren and colleagues, for older students).
- **Cost.** A teacher reading the key at night is told the 12 means the opposite of what it means; the record
  sheet's A column mixes two different mistakes with one response that fits only one of them.
- **Change.** errors.csv, stretch 10 A, error text becomes: "Mixes the two comparisons: reads 'times as many' as
  'more than' (5 times as many as 3 is 8), or multiplies in a 'more than' story (3 more than 5 is 15)". `shows`
  becomes "The two kinds of comparison are not yet told apart". d084 "If day 83's slips showed many A" adds: "ask
  which picture the words for Mo say, then which for Di." The opener's error table row A says the same.
- **Confidence.** Firm for the mislabel (read in the built key); likely for how common the reverse is.
- **LAYER:** process (the register's one-way wording is printed by the key on every A row in the stretch).
  **CATCH:** cheap read. **REPEAT:** yes (day 89 finding 5, an error misnamed; day 91 finding 11, wrong-answer rows).
