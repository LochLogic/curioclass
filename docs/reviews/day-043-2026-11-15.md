## Verdict

The day does the standard's own task honestly: the map product is finished, shared and sent for written feedback
before any score, alliances a source does not record are reported as absent, and conquest and slavery are named
plainly. Its biggest risk is that a third to two thirds of the class cannot meet the map's culture look-for from
their sheets, because the option a and d sheets hold no source the unit's own A2 samples rely on (manuscripts,
Yuan patronage). The one change that matters most is to put one checked culture passage on each of those sheets
(the Gibb poets passage for Mali is free and fits) and to fix the exit stem that tells students the answer to error
K. Five minutes of "Teaching first" also carries three days' worth of reminders and should be cut to the day's one
model.

### 1. The unit's A2 culture samples rest on sources no option sheet holds (options a and d)

s03 field 5 A2 samples a meeting answer for Mali as "manuscripts show learned networks" and for the Mongols as
"Yuan cultural patronage"; P43-2's first look-for is "culture explained with a named contributor beyond the ruler".
The option a sheet names Timbuktu as a "learning town" and "books came south" on the fact card, but no source shows
a scholar, a manuscript or anyone teaching; the option d sheet has no culture source at all. The cheap read found
the option d half (its finding 5); the option a half and the mismatch with the unit's own samples are new. Cost: on
day 45 the teacher scores culture 0 to 2 against samples the student could not have written from the sheet, so the
score measures what the sheet lacks. Fix: (a) option a, add to sources-42a.md the Gibb passage in which the poets
recite past kings' deeds to the sultan ("This pempi which you occupy was that whereon sat this king and that
king..."; Fordham copy, Mali court section, pp. 323 to 335), with a gloss that these were the griots (jeliw) who kept
Mali's history, and that Ibn Battuta called their dress "ridiculous" (a sourcing point). It also answers the
sensitivity read's finding 1 for option a. (b) option d, move Source F (Mongke, Rubruck) onto the option d sheet as
the cheap read says, and change A2's Mongol sample from "Yuan cultural patronage" to what F shows (a court of many
faiths). (c) sources.csv rows for the new passage and F's `days`. **ORIGIN unit** (sources.csv and the s03 A2
samples; the sheet change follows). LAYER day. CATCH expert. REPEAT yes. CHEAP missed (option d half found).
Confidence: firm (sheets and s03 read side by side).

### 2. The exit stem gives away the error it tests

s03-X43: "...and explain one link to another region without claiming foreign founders." The wrong answer filed under
K is "The ceramics show foreigners founded R." The stem forbids that answer, so a student who holds error K is told
not to write it, and the K look-for ("the ceramics read as exchange, not founders") cannot catch anyone. Day 44's
first block and the H/K branch in Tomorrow then act on a signal the ticket cannot give. Fix: stem ends "...and
explain what the ceramics show about R's links to another region." Keep the answer, look-fors and both wrong
answers. **ORIGIN unit** (items.csv). LAYER day. CATCH cheap read. REPEAT yes. CHEAP missed (the cheap read's
finding 1 says K is not taught to all options; it does not see the stem). Confidence: firm.

### 3. The "scale" look-for cannot be met as the days tell students to draw

P42-2's look-fors require "dated map: years in the title, key, scale"; the section plan field 5 says students draw
"from the fact card's coordinates" and the teacher accepts "a sketch map with a key and scale". The cards give no
coordinates; day 42 and day 43 tell students to label every sketch *not to scale*; day 43's feedback line is "dated
geography". A teacher scoring A2 from the look-fors marks every map down for a missing scale the day forbade. The
sheets do hold honest distance: Source C gives "a fortnight's journey" Kilwa to Sufala and "a month's journey" to
Yufi. Fix: look-for becomes "dated map: years in the title, key, and either a *not to scale* label or the journey
times a source gives"; section plan field 5 drops "coordinates"; day 43's map part says "where your source gives a
journey time, write it on the route". **ORIGIN unit** (items.csv look-for; section plan follows). LAYER day. CATCH
expert. REPEAT yes. CHEAP missed. Confidence: firm.

### 6. Source D is said to show something the printed excerpt cuts

"Source D's merchants using written currency, an inference about material culture." The printed excerpt shows the
Khan paying "on his own account" in paper and merchants forbidden to sell to anyone but him. That merchants were
paid in, and then bought with, the paper is in Polo's text, but in the part the sheet cut ("the Emperor then pays a
liberal price for them in those pieces of paper", Gutenberg, same chapter). A student told to point to the evidence
cannot find it. Paper money is also economy and power, not culture; modelling it as culture blurs the look-for.
Fix: drop Source D from the culture model; if the teacher wants it for exchange, say "Source D shows the Khan forced
merchants to sell to him; the sheet does not show what they were paid in." (Restoring the cut sentence to the sheet
is the alternative, a section piece change.) **ORIGIN day.** LAYER day. CATCH expert. REPEAT yes. CHEAP missed.
Confidence: firm (checked against the Gutenberg text).

### 7. The unit's supporting question for this day is gone

show, and whose does it leave out? Expect: rulers and routes are drawn; producers, carriers and enslaved people

### 12. "Shona builders" modelled as Swahili culture

Shona builders of Great Zimbabwe are a link inland." **ORIGIN day.** LAYER day. CATCH expert. REPEAT new. CHEAP missed.
Confidence: likely.
