## Findings

### 1. Item 1 drops two of the plan's eight components (worst)

s07.md field 5: "Item 1 earns eight: ... compare one agreement or difference in source coverage; describe the repeated protective relationship across the two episodes; ..." record.md agrees ("for item 1, one cell for each of the eight components"; lookfors 1=9). But the s07-E60a steps list six components and the stem never asks for either missing one. The stem ends "Then point to the words and the pictures that show how the ducks stay safe." and d060 has each child "point to a marked place and gives one survival reason". Nothing asks a child to say how the words and pictures differ, or what repeats across the two sequences, yet the part column names `compare-sources` and `survival-pattern` and the teacher has two record cells per child with no step to fill them. The key and samples do carry the comparison ("the pictures show them together, but the words tell why").

Fix (items.csv s07-E60a): add to the stem, after the last sentence: "Say one thing the words tell that the pictures do not. Say what happens again in both picture sequences." Add two steps before the support step: "says one agreement or difference between the words and the pictures (for example, only the words say why the calls help)" and "says the pattern in both sequences: the adult calls and the young respond, and that keeps them together or safe". In d060's group turn, change "gives one survival reason" to "gives a survival reason and says one thing the words tell that the pictures do not"; the 30-second turn is tight for this, so the group turn could take 36 seconds by trimming the waiting-group change-over, the writer's call.

### 2. Item 2: sun or rain drawn onto the young plant could be scored as error D

held-sunflower key: "A food arrow drawn by the child is error D". A grade 1 child who draws the sun or rain helping the young sunflower grow has drawn a fair, true picture, and in a picture it looks like a "food arrow". Error D is meant only for the grown plant feeding its young (wrongs.csv note: "a plant is taken to feed its young like an animal").

Fix (held-sunflower.md key and d060 plant case): "An arrow from the grown sunflower giving food to the young one is error D. Sun, rain or soil drawn around the young plant is not an error; credit the seed link if it is there."

### 3. Item 3 has no cell on the record sheet

d060 says for s07-E60c "mark each child", but record.md gives End cells only for item 1 and item 2 (lookfors 1=9;2=3), and s07.md field 5 counts ten points with no item 3. The teacher has nowhere to write the thumbs answer, and error B cannot be routed to its day 61 table.

Fix: either add one End cell for item 3 in record.md ("item 3, one cell: no, not every animal calls"), unscored toward the ten points, or fold the question into item 1 as the plan's day 60 row does ("Do all animals need to call?") and drop the separate mark. The first is the smaller change.

### 4. Item 3 key implies every animal keeps its young safe

Key: "other animals keep their young safe in other ways." Day 57 teaches "Many animals care for young, not all." A child who says "some animals don't look after their babies at all" is right and should be credited.

Fix (items.csv s07-E60c answer and samples): "No. Calling is how these ducks do it. Other animals help their young in other ways, and some do not care for their young at all. Every animal must use these calls is B." Add to the steps: "a child who says some animals give no care at all is also credited."

### 5. Sequence 1, frame 2 can read as the young giving the adult's call

held-ducks.md: "The young, hidden among the plants, with a different call symbol (two small marks) by each. The adult's arc mark is still there." The adult is not said to be in the frame, so a lone arc mark among hidden young can be read as a young duck's call, which blurs the young-answer evidence the key needs.

Fix: "The adult at the edge of the frame, its arc mark still by its beak. The young, hidden among the plants, each with two small marks by its beak."

### 6. One re-reading or none

The stem says "Words read aloud once", held-ducks says "The text is read once", but d060's launch says "Nobody helps today, except one neutral re-reading of the words", and record.md says to write H "when you ... read a card aloud once more". A teacher cannot tell whether the allowed re-reading earns S or H.

Fix (d060 launch): "Nobody helps today. If a child asks, read the words once more, neutrally, and write H for that child."
