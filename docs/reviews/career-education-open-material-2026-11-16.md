# Open career education material for Curio Class: is there a base text we may adapt?

Read 2026-11-16 (subject pre-work, the career education counterpart of P2). Research notes, not legal advice. Each
licence was read on the publisher's own page that day unless marked "search only" (seen in a search summary, page
not opened) or "not read, blocked". The bar is docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md: CC BY 4.0, public domain or the UK Open
Government Licence v3.0 is a base; NC, SA, ND or unclear is reference only.

## The short answer

- **No openly licensed career exploration or pathways course clears the bar.** The programs found are
  NonCommercial-ShareAlike, unlicensed on their pages, or sold.
- **The data does clear it, and the data is what the bar most asks for** (real labour market figures, criterion 3):
  the Bureau of Labor Statistics publications are public domain, and the O*NET database is CC BY 4.0.
- **Verdict:** none fits as a course base. Both courses are our own, built on public domain and CC BY data.

## Traps

- O*NET's required attribution includes a line that the agency has not approved or tested the changes. That is a
  credit line, not a claim of review, and it stays in the credit only.
- Figures date fast. Every wage or outlook figure carries its source and year (bar C3), and the scope and sequence
  names a yearly refresh day for the course's data sheets.
- No company, job board or credential vendor is named on student pages.
