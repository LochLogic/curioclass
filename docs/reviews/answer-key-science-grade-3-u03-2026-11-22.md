## Findings, most serious first

1. **K42-2 key contradicts the unit's own birth rule (key error).** Stem: "Card B shows a split pod with seeds. Card C shows a seed beginning to grow. Which shows a new plant?" Key: "Card C ... Card B shows seeds the plant made", steps "A pod with seeds is not yet a new plant"; wrong answer "Card B. It has seeds in it" (error B). But K38-2 steps teach "Birth is the start of a new living thing: in a flowering plant, in a seed", and the unit's own records say "Day 0: a seed containing a new plant" (portfolio 2, item 2 cards, day 58). A child answering B by that rule is right by our own teaching. Fix: stem "Which card shows a new plant that has begun to grow?"; key "Card C. The seed has begun to grow. The seeds in the pod are what the plant made."; delete the step "A pod with seeds is not yet a new plant"; in cards-day-42 change "Only a seed that begins to grow shows a new plant" to "Only a seed that begins to grow shows a new plant growing."

2. **Pea record contradicts itself on day 49 (item ambiguous, E5/E6).** Portfolio record 2, first card: "Day 49: closed pods." Second card: "On day 49, the opened pod of P holds new seeds." A careful child can say the pod is closed and open on the same day. Fix: first card "Day 45: closed pods" (the 7 days from seed to seedling still holds: growing day 49, seedling day 56). Also the wrongs.csv wrong answer for E6, "The pods show the pea reproduced.", is not clearly wrong, since seeds in a pod are what reproduction made; use the items.csv wording, "The pods show the pea reproduced, and the dead pea starts again."

3. **Wrong-answer text differs between items.csv and wrongs.csv.** E5: items.csv "Peas and bees are alive, so they are the same." vs wrongs.csv "Peas and bees are the same, so I cannot predict." E6: see 2. K42-2: "Card B. It has seeds in it." vs "Card B, because the pod has seeds." Make each pair identical.

4. **E4 and E6 score the same act.** E4 needs reproduction in the plant model, but the first pea card shows only closed pods (the zinnia card only a dried head), so reproduction enters the plant model only through the second-card revision, which E6 scores again. A child who misses the revision loses both points. Fix: say in E4 that it is scored on the revised model, or let a dotted reproduction box count (as d056's look-for already says) and keep E6 for the link.

5. **K39-1 asks "Which stage is dotted for us" but the stem never says which stages the class saw (item ambiguous).** Key names only the egg; steps hedge "a pupa or adult is solid only if the class saw one". Fix the stem: "The class saw the larva, the pupa and the adult through the lid. Which stage is dotted for us, and why?" and drop the hedge.

6. **K40-1 accepts only caterpillar and chrysalis (fair alternative keyed wrong).** The stem also asks "Which stage is a pupa?" and the unit teaches larva and pupa beside the beetle. Add to the key: "egg, larva (caterpillar), pupa (chrysalis), adult is also right."

7. **K43-2 "All three kids" (item ambiguous).** The stem does not name goats, and "kids" can read as children; the standard uses living things other than people. Fix: "All three goat kids".

8. **K38-1 key says "The day tells when the class saw that stage" but the pod box (day 45) is dotted and unseen.** Fix: "The day tells when that stage was seen or pictured." A petunia flowering at day 30 is early for the real plant; it is marked invented, but a fast plant such as radish or bean would not raise the question.
