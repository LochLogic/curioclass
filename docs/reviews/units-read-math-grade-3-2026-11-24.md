## Verdict

The eight units make a sound arc: graphs open multiplication, area gives the array a second meaning, unit 3 maintains addition
and subtraction and starts two-step work, units 4 and 5 finish the operations, unit 6 builds fractions on the number line, and
units 7 and 8 apply them to measurement, shape and perimeter. Day counts, kind counts and focus minutes in every header and
Balance line match days.csv, and the seam with grade 2 (retrieval only, every assumed row last used in grade 2) holds. The one
serious fault was a cross-unit contradiction: unit 5 told teachers to replace unit 4's property, tiling and fact evidence and
unit 3's computation evidence, against units 4 and 8, the scope and sequence and days.csv. That is fixed, along with a set of
exits that had dropped the explain half of their goal. All three gates pass.
