COURSE-RUN stage 4 "Reads" for `physics` (grade 11 science). This read looked at all ten unit plans side by side, after the single-unit reads, the 2026-11-17 lens pass and the 2026-11-22 Goal map pass.

## What was read

AGENTS.md; docs/COURSE-RUN.md stage 4; docs/agents/EXPERT.md; docs/agents/lenses/science.md; docs/courses/UNIT-PLAN.md; docs/GLOSSARY.md (the locked words). Grade above: Earth and Space Science and Environmental Science (grade 12) have frames and scopes and sequences but no unit plans, so no unit 1 plan exists to read; the Earth and Space Science frame says its unit 1 needs no readiness check because Physics used every row it assumes. No day skeletons or written day files exist for Physics, so this read logged no day faults.

## Verdict

The course holds together. The arc runs from ground motion to orbits, energy, fields, waves, light, stars, the solid Earth and deep time. Every readiness hand-back has both ends: the item is on the giving unit's last day and the repair on the receiving unit's second day. Law, theory and model are built on purpose across units 1, 3, 8 and 10, and the photoelectric hand-off from unit 6 (the evidence) to unit 7 (the photon model) is deliberate. The most serious fault was in the joins between units: a retrieval prompt given twice on back-to-back days across a unit boundary, and a review key that used the photon idea three days before it is taught. Five tools the plans call "does not exist" also had no heading in the course's tool-asks file. All three faults are fixed, and the gates pass.
