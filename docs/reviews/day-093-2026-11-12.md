## Verdict

The mathematics is right: every key reworked by hand is correct, and the seven items cover the seven parts of
4.MD.A.3 one each, unaided, in fair grade 4 numbers. The respond half is the weak part: it uses the keyed results
at once and well, but the two reasoning parts (items 5 and 6, choosing the measure and the structure of the
perimeter) get no response on the day and wait on a day 94 that cannot be planned while stretch 12 is frozen,
and the record sheet cannot hold three of the marks the day asks for. A grade 4 teacher could run the check in
60 minutes with no prep; the groups would stall without their tasks on the screen.

## Findings, most serious first

### 1. The respond half leaves parts (e) and (f) with no response, and its last ten minutes rest on a piece that cannot exist yet

- **What is wrong.** Items 5 and 6 are the only evidence of choosing the measure (e) and of length plus width as
  half the perimeter (f). The day sends both to "day 94's first five minutes", which belongs to stretch 12,
  frozen until the pilot is read; nothing on day 93 touches them. The last ten minutes lean on
  courses/math-4/u12-1/readiness.md, which cannot exist yet, so the day stays "not ready" for a reason outside
  its unit, and its fallback ("the groups keep fixing") turns one fix into 25 minutes for most students.
- **Why it matters.** The parts where reasoning shows are the ones left with no answer, possibly for weeks. The
  same will happen on the last check day of every stretch until the next stretch is planned: a check day leans on
  the next stretch's readiness by the plan's own rule (days.csv: "next readiness 10").
- **Fix, meanwhile (day).** Rewrite the 10-minute block as: "**10 min. Items 5 and 6 together.** Hand back the
  checks. Pairs read each other's items 5 and 6 and ask the two questions on the display: 'Which part asked for
  the inside, and which for the edge?' and 'Do your two sides add to 19? Why does that make the perimeter the
  same?' Students add to their own answers in pen; you judge the pencil first try after class. When stretch 12's
  readiness check exists, it takes these ten minutes and items 5 and 6 return on day 94 instead." Remove the
  stretch 12 readiness from `print` and "Before class" until it exists.
- **Fix (process).** `leans_on` gains a conditional form, `when it exists: courses/math-4/u12-1/readiness.md`,
  which the gate accepts only when the day's prose names the fallback; the build prints "Uses when it exists:
  ..." instead of "Not ready to teach". Then day 93 can be ready now. Add one line to s11.md section 7 so stretch
  12's planners inherit the duty: "Hand-off to stretch 12: day 94 opens with items 5 and 6 returned (5 min);
  item 7 misses go to stretch 12's first area warm-up; stretch 12's readiness check takes day 93's last ten
  minutes."
- **Confidence.** Firm on the gap; the meanwhile design is a judgment call.
- **LAYER:** process. **CATCH:** expert.

### 2. The record sheet cannot hold what the day asks it to record

- **What is wrong.** (a) Day 93 says "an item not reached is recorded as not reached, not wrong"; the sheet
  allows only a tick or an error letter. (b) Five of the end check's wrong answers have no letter by design (783
  on item 4, "56 or 5,600" on item 7, fraction and adding slips sent to the warm-up, the three item 6 misses);
  the sheet has no mark for them, so a teacher will force a letter or tick. (c) The columns are "End 1" to "End
  7" with no part, so the sheet cannot be read by part (who has (b) across E90 and End 2), which is the point of
  one column a part. (d) The item 6 look-fors are paraphrased and cut ("a reason from length plus width"; the
  plan also accepts half the perimeter and "one side 1 longer, the other 1 shorter"). (e) "A column with more
  than a third letters is the next day's opening" points at day 94, which is unplanned (finding 1). (f) 22 rows;
  many grade 4 classes are larger (from memory).
- **Why it matters.** The record sheet is the one place results live (rule 7). Wrong marks send children to
  the wrong help in stretch 12.
- **Fix.** record.md legend: "Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to E; s for a slip (a fact, adding,
  fraction or zero slip with the right setup); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for not reached. Items 5
  and 6 take one tick for each look-for on the key." Column heads print the part: "End 1 (a)", "End 2 (b)", ...
  "E91 (c, d, g)". Generate the look-for wording from the `steps` cells (`lookfors: 5=3; 6=2` already ties the
  counts), not prose. Rows: 30, or a line "print two for a class over 22".
- **Confidence.** Firm on (a) to (e); (f) from memory.
- **LAYER:** process (the record sheet is built once a unit from this template). **CATCH:** cheap read.
