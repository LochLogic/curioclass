# Fixes: social studies course frames, grades 9 to 11, after the two cold reads (2026-11-13)

Applies the expert reads (`frame-<course>-2026-11-13.md`) and the Codex reads (`frame-<course>-codex-2026-11-13.md`)
for World History I (grade 9), World History II (grade 10) and US History I (grade 11) to the three frames and
`data/progressions/social-studies.csv` (33 rows). Every expert finding and every "Settled by the expert" row is applied
or, where its file is outside this pass, listed for the lead; each Codex finding was weighed on its merits. The K to 2,
3 to 5, 6 to 8 and grade 12 passes' changes stand. The 6 to 8 report's grade 9 items and the grade 12 report's "For the
grade 9 to 11 fixer" list are applied (below). The lead's decision on World History II's days is applied: units 8 and
10 gain 3 days from units 1 to 3, the course stays at 160.

Gates after the fixes: `scripts/frame.py sync` (17 courses); `scripts/frame.py check` world-history-i, world-history-ii,
us-history-i, civics-government-and-civic-life, us-history-ii, us-government-and-politics and economics, 0 failures
each; `scripts/stale.py check` 21 files, 0 failures; `scripts/progression.py check social-studies` 384 rows, 0
failures; `scripts/plan.py check` exit 0. Every course keeps its unit count and total days (World History I 10 and 160,
World History II 11 and 160, US History I 9 and 160). Days moved only in World History II: unit 1 16 to 15, unit 2 12 to
11, unit 3 14 to 13, unit 8 13 to 14, unit 10 9 to 11; its week ranges and same-year calendar were recomputed (unit 10
now weeks 27 to 29; the hard weeks for ELA 10 are 15 to 18 and 27 to 29). No skill moved between units. No seam row in
the civics or grade 12 frames needed a change: no handoff to or from them moved.

Files outside this pass (`data/standards/ma-hss-2018.txt`, `data/plans/flow-status.csv`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`,
`docs/APPROACH.md`, `docs/PRODUCTION-PATH.md`, `docs/ADOPTION-BAR-SUBJECTS.md`, earlier review reports) were not edited;
what the readers found there is under "For the lead".

All three frames now carry a "Sensitivity reads" bullet and a "Reads:" line in every unit's last column (lesson 12 of
frame-write.md), and every outside communication in an informed action goes through the teacher or parent with adult
contact details, so a student learning at home has a route (Codex World History I 6, carried to the other two frames).
