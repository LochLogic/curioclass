## Must-fix (2)

**M1. Elizabeth Freeman is said to be born in Massachusetts; no U6J text supports it and it is probably wrong.**
File: u06-3/cards-day130.md line 32; u06-3/d130.md line 34; u06-3/opener.md line 20.
Text: "Elizabeth Freeman was born enslaved in Massachusetts about 1744."
Problem: U6J records only "born enslaved about 1744" and the 1781 Massachusetts jury. No checked row names her birthplace. I believe the NPS page places her birth at Claverack, New York, and that she came to Massachusetts while enslaved. A wrong birthplace for an enslaved woman, taught as fact, is a plain accuracy error on a sensitivity day.
Fix: "born enslaved about 1744. She was enslaved in Massachusetts when, in 1781, she and Brom went to court." Confirm against the NPS page before any place of birth is printed.

**M2. Oneida exit card adds a date and wording that U6H cuts.**
File: u06-3/cards-exits-130-132.md line 14.
Text: "In December 1777 Congress sent the Oneidas a message. It promised to protect them and to treat their welfare as its own."
Problem: U6H says "a 1777 message from Congress promised protection and called the Oneidas friends" and lists "the 1777 message wording" as CUT, to be summarized "as a promise of protection". "December" and "treat their welfare as its own" are not in the row. This is a real Native nation card, so the claim must sit on a checked row.
Fix: "In 1777 Congress sent the Oneidas a message. It promised them protection." Add "friends" only if wanted (it is in the row). Check E130 (key line 12 in u06-3/key-exits-130-132.md) still reads the same.

## Should-fix (6)

**S1. The made-up New Jersey cards do not meet the rule they are said to meet.**
File: u06-3/cards-day130.md lines 25 to 26; u06-3/d130.md line 31; u06-3/d131.md line 22.
Text: "Isaac, a free Black man, 1802. He is of full age, owns land and has lived in his county for two years." and d130 "1802 is inside the band, so Isaac met the rule."
Problem: U6F's rule needs property worth fifty pounds with a clear estate, and twelve months in the county. "Owns land" does not show that. The cards (and Cato/Ruth on d131 line 22, "owns land") teach that any land owner voted, which overstates who could vote and drops the poor-men limit the lesson is about.
Fix: add "land worth more than fifty pounds" (or "enough property") to Isaac, Hannah, Cato and Ruth, and keep "made up, for practice".

**S2. The Oneida card is called "own words" but is an original summary.**
File: u06-3/d131.md line 31; u06-3/d131.md line 85; u06-3/cards-day131.md line 21.
Text: "the fourth is the Oneida Indian Nation's own words, there today." and student page "Oneida Indian Nation, from its own page."
Problem: cards-day131.md line 21 labels it "(real, original summary)". "Own words" attributes our paraphrase to the nation.
Fix: "the fourth is our summary of what the Oneida Indian Nation says on its own page." Same on the student page.

**S3. The Oneida case is the "none of the three" card.**
File: u06-3/cards-day131.md lines 21 and 23; u06-3/d131.md line 85; item s06-P131a answer.
Text: "it answers none of the three."
Problem: the only real Native nation in the sort is also the only odd one out, beside three made-up town rules. It can read as "Native nations do not fit" and children may carry a vague "does not count" to the nation. The teacher line (d131 line 34) does not say why.
Fix: add one sentence on the card and the teacher page: "A nation choosing its own path is a decision made by that nation, a fourth kind of question." Keep the nation named and in the present tense.

**S4. Freeman: "bought her own home" and "worked for pay" over-read U6J.**
File: u06-3/cards-day130.md line 32.
Text: "later worked for pay and bought her own home."
Problem: U6J says "later paid work and her own home"; "bought" is not in the row.
Fix: "later did paid work and had her own home."

**S5. The enslaved-people definition is about work only.**
File: u06-3/d130.md line 28.
Text: "you learned the words in grade 2: people who were forced to work and could not leave."
Problem: reads as a labor description. The standing rule is people who were forced and held in slavery, never "workers".
Fix: "people who were held in slavery: forced to work for others, not paid, and not free to leave." (The grade 2 claim is also an unlisted review claim; check the ledger.)

**S6. State caption names a year no U6R row states.**
File: u06-2/cards-day128.md line 36; u06-2/d128.md line 89.
Text: "a convention met in 1779 and 1780."
Problem: U6R gives only "convention form agreed 2 March 1780, accepted by the people, first General Court met 25 October 1780". 1779 is not in the row. Also "The people accepted it" is stated with no note on who voted, in a unit about who was left out.
Fix: "A convention agreed the plan on 2 March 1780. The people accepted it." (add "the voters" only if a checked row says so).
