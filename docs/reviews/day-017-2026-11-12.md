## Findings, most serious first

### 1. The planned question comes after the worked example has answered it, and the pair fold repeats the demo

- **What is wrong.** Example 1's steps end "Are both one half? Yes. Each is 1/2 of its own strip ... Ask: half of
  what?", and the teacher then says "the big half is more food, and both are 1/2". The next part, 6 minutes, has each
  pair fold the same kind of strips and answer "are they the same length? Are they both one half?". Every student has
  just heard the answer. The plan's key moment 1 says "two real wholes of different size that the student halves and
  lays together"; as written, the student does it after the idea is told, as a copy.
- **Why it matters.** The same whole is the one idea on day 17 that cannot be told into a child (the opener says so:
  "felt rather than told"). A question asked after its answer earns nothing on the exit's part (a). Same kind as day
  92's finding 3.
- **Evidence.** d017.md, "Teaching first" and "Fold and lay together"; s03-K17-1 steps; u03-3.md field 5. Judgment
  from memory (teaching practice), likely.
- **Fix.** Hand out the strips before Example 1. In Example 1, students fold their own as the teacher folds (steps 1
  to 3). After step 3, put the planned question to pairs, 1 minute, as an **Ask.**, then steps 4 to 6 and the pizza
  talk. Delete the separate "Fold and lay together" part. Minutes become: warm-up 5, question aloud 5, teaching 14,
  Fix it 10, sort 9, On your own 8, pull together 4, exit 5 (60). days.csv row 17: `opens_min` 19, by-hand 31. The
  extra 2 minutes on Fix it and On your own answer the cheap read's CHECK 4.
- **Tags.** LAYER day; CATCH expert; REPEAT yes; CHEAP missed.

- **Why it matters.** E17 part (a) is the only evidence for 4.NF.A.2 part (e) in the section before the end check;
