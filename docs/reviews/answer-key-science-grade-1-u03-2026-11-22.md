## Findings

1. **Rabbit card: the child cannot tell which rabbits are the young and which is the other adult (E26h, E26i, E26j).** cards-day26.md: "All rabbits and the fish are drawn in side view at the same size." and "Captions: \"The rabbit parent and its two young.\"" The item stems say only "with a caption naming the parent". RY1, RY2 and RA are all gray, same size, with one white marking each, and no seed-path equivalent links the young to RP. The oak card captions OA "Same kind, another adult"; the rabbit card gives RA no caption. A child asked to ring a difference "on the two young rabbits" or "the two adult rabbits" has no picture evidence for which is which, so a correct comparison of the wrong pair would be scored wrong. Fix in cards-day26.md, rabbit table: give each rabbit a label under it, "parent", "young", "young", "Same kind, another adult", and add a drawn line from RP to each young (as the oak seed path); keep all at the same size, as s03.md field 5 requires. Change the item stems of E26g to E26l to "with labels naming the parent, its two young and another adult".

2. **E26c rests on a drawn size the materials say is never scored, and the card spec does not fix it.** cards-day26.md says only "The young blades are drawn smaller than the parent's"; OY1 and OY2 are otherwise identical ("three side lobes on each side"). key-cards-day26.md says "widths and drawn portrait sizes are never scored", and s03.md line 101 says "No size comparison between organism portraits is scored." Yet E26c keys "one young blade is shorter than the other", sample "Rings the shorter blade of the smaller young oak". If the artist draws OY1 and OY2 at one size, the item has no right answer on the card. Fix, in cards-day26.md oak paragraph, add: "On the card, OY1's labeled blade is drawn shorter than OY2's, in the same ratio as the loose blades (50 and 70 mm)." In key-cards-day26.md, change the sentence to "widths and whole-plant portrait sizes are never scored; the labeled blade lengths on the oak card are", and add to the E26c key: "a child who points to the loose OY1 and OY2 blades instead of ringing is shown." (Alternative fix if size on the card must stay unscored: give OY2 one drawn, labeled non-size difference, such as a small notch on one lobe, and key that.)

3. **E26b key is narrower than the card's shared features; E26g leaves out gray.** Key: "the rounded lobes, or the blade shape." The card spec states OP and both young also share three side lobes a side, a rounded end lobe and a flat base, and every whole plant has roots and a stem. Three side lobes a side is arguably the best answer, since it is what separates the family from OA. Fix in key-cards-day26.md and the items.csv answer: "the rounded lobes, three side lobes a side, the rounded end lobe, the flat base or the blade shape; roots or a stem also counts as a shared feature." For E26g add "or gray fur" to the key list (cards-day26.md: every rabbit is gray).

4. **Small: record.md asks for more than the turn has time to hear.** "credit a paper component only if the child can point to the mark and say the observation", but each child gets 30 seconds at the turn, which d026.md fills with E26f and E26l. Ten paper marks cannot all be pointed to and spoken in that time. Fix in record.md: "credit a paper component from the mark itself; at the turn, ask about one unclear mark only (\"Tell me about this mark\"), as d026.md says."
