## Findings

1. s06-E12 (ambiguous, key weak). Stem: "Check today's weather and climate." Card task: "how a climate is different from one day's weather." The key sample, "Both vary between years, and tomorrow is uncertain.", never separates climate from one day, so a child who writes only that scores a point the step ("one day's weather kept apart from climate") does not earn. The stem says "today" but the cards hold no today record. Fix the stem to "Check one day's weather and climate." and the sample to: "Climate is the usual weather over many years. One rainy day does not set it, and tomorrow can differ." Also the wrong "because both had rain today" has no matching data on the card; reword to "because both had rain on one day".

2. s06-E18 (key narrower than its answer). Steps say "heavier rain named as untested", but the answer says "one thing not tested, such as heavier rain". A fair alternative (the ramp was never tried with a wheelchair, a real house, a longer storm) could be marked 0 by a scorer who follows the steps. Fix steps to: "names one thing not tested (for example heavier rain, a real house, a wheelchair on the ramp), with no claim that storms stop".

3. s06-E14 (minor). Key: "with the doorway ... without it". The card's trials are "with the doorway and the ramp" against "without them". Fix to "with the doorway and ramp 1, 2 and 1 wet; without them 6, 5 and 6". E16's ramp claim is stated by the card, not trialed, which is fine for a limit check.

4. s06-E10 (note only). Ranges 12 to 24 and 2 to 10 do not overlap, so "usually wetter" is safe, and "always in this record" would also be true. Accept both. No change.
