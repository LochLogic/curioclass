## Ladder

Course plus term were searched in the saved ledger.

- Used well: keep, change, watch (K u01-1 day 2, grade 1 days 43 to 51); the pattern-from-records habit (K u02-1); the K forecast question habit (K u02-2) and question board (K u05-1 day 44 and grade 1 u01-1); the message and signal words in day 56's cheep (unit 6); "light shines on it" and "makes its own light" in day 66 (unit 5); the day 22 blade-tip "shorter, longer" taught in unit 3 reused for light parts on day 62.
- What the grade gap caused: day 56's "adult" and "grown plant" for unit 3's "parent" (new words for an old idea); day 62's "spring is March" built from the strips the class happens to have, not the K to grade 1 meaning of spring; day 51's "near mark" beside K's "nearer"; day 66's "dark shadow" for the day 43 word "shadow".
- Taught as new what a lower grade or earlier unit taught: day 56 lists "young" as taught new though unit 3 taught it; day 51 lists "understood, not understood" as new though day 48 taught it.
- Assumed something not taught: day 62 exit assumes April is spring; day 56 uses "staying alive" with no row; day 56 retrieval assumes a K forecast picture is still on the wall.
- Lower-grade cases reused as an exit: day 66 cardboard shadow (K u05-1 day 47 cardboard cover and shadow, and day 43); day 66 toy-in-a-container with a flap (day 38 shoebox).
- Overall: the grade 1 sample reuses K and earlier units for routines (turns, keep-change-watch, question board) well. It leaks through new words for old ideas and through exits that restate the day's own finding.
