## Verdict

The four plans hang together well: the arc climbs from teacher-modeled quotation and a whole novel in unit 1 to
student-chosen organizers, independent paired-story comparison and community research in unit 4, every "comes
back" claim but one lands on a day that does the work, and unit loads match days.csv by kind. The most serious
cross-unit fault was in unit 3: its goal map had days 97 to 99 hearing and rereading "the testimony" while field 10
replaces Robinson's words with our own explanation (02), so a day writer would have built read-alouds of words the
plan forbids quoting. The other faults were small: a wrong return day, a new form first met on an assessment day,
and an unbudgeted 700 to 1,000 word reading. The biggest open risk is in days.csv, not the plans: several codes the
plans practise or score are still missing from their day rows (listed for the structure pass).
