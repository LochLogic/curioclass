## Findings

1. **c1-F58d (ambiguous).** Stem: "Use any two of the cards to say how a fast change and a slow change are different." The key needs one fast card and one slow card, but "any two" allows gate and field, both fast, which cannot answer. Fix: "Use one fast card and one slow card to say how a fast change and a slow change are different."
2. **c1-F58b (ambiguous).** Stem: "Touch the card that shows a slow change." Side 1 of the sheet also holds the sandbar card, which is also a slow change (older photo against today). A child who touches the sandbar is keyed wrong. Fix: "Touch the one card of the gate, ridge and field that shows a slow change."
3. **c1-S57c (sample wording).** The right sample says "in the valley by the blue patch", but the map shows the pond as the blue patch, and the wrong sample says "The pond is the blue patch" as if it were an error. "In the valley" is on no card. Fix the right sample: "The pond is the blue patch on the map, and its top is ice in winter."
4. **c2-X76h as retry for groups C, D and E (skill mismatch).** Day 76 sends groups C (one thing differs), D (sun-only and water-only plants) and E (measured against supplied records) all to X76h, the sun against dark healthier-seedling item. That item tests only height against health (error S). It does not test group C's plan, group D's need for both light and water, or group E's strength of evidence. Fix: give C a short plan retry like X76c, D an item like X76d, and E an item that sets a measured record beside a supplied one (error V), or relabel X76h as the retry for D only.
5. **c1-B58d and c1-B58b (fair alternative keyed wrong).** The steps require naming a limit. A child who says "J lost more soil than H both times" (6 against 1, 3 against 2) is true and a fair weakness, but it is not keyed. Fix: add to the key "or J lost more soil than H in both runs".
6. **c1-W58a (stem and test do not quite match).** The stem says the cover "must let water soak in", but the test measures how far water climbed along a strip. Fix: "must let water climb along it" or "must wick water". The answer is unaffected.
