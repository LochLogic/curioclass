## Findings

1. **s06-E6 (worst): a working carry is keyed wrong.** Stem: "Show how your model carries the acorn." Key and steps: "closes the beak halves around the card acorn | moves the model with the acorn held." The day 63 set holds hook-and-loop pieces, felt and a handle (d063 materials), children used hooks on days 61 and 62, and the plan's component 6 reads "carries material on the animal representation." A child who sticks the acorn to the beak with a hook patch carries it and fits the stem, yet fails the step, and wrongs.csv has no row for this. Fix: change the answer and first step to "carries the card acorn on the beak part (closing halves, or any attachment that holds it while the model moves)", and leave the hook-versus-beak reasoning to E8, where error D already sits. Or remove the fasteners from the jay set, which the jay card already says are not needed ("Fasteners are not needed for the jay").

2. **s06-E4: key and steps disagree, and a keyed wrong cannot occur.** Answer lists three lines (halves to beak, acorn to acorn, outline to oak); steps require two (the oak line is not a step). Pick one: add "joins the outline to the oak" as a third step, or cut the oak line from the answer. The wrongs row "Lines to the jay as a whole, with no parts named" cannot happen on the printed record: model-record-days-63-64 lists only words ("the jay's beak, the acorn, the oak") and has no jay picture. Fix: replace that wrong with "Lines all go to one word", or add three small real-part pictures to the record, which also lets a child who cannot yet read do the item.

3. **s06-E10: key narrows a free answer.** Answer says "Whether the acorn would grow, or what a real jay does, such as eating. Paper cannot eat." The step ("names one thing the model cannot show") is right and accepts more. Fair true limits not named in the answer line: it cannot fly, it is not alive, the acorn is card, one tray only. Fix: answer line "any true limit, for example growth, eating, flying, size of the real oak." Unit limits (s06.md: models show movement, not growth) are consistent.

4. **s06-E3: "seed" is half the key.** Steps 2 says "says it stands for an acorn, a seed." The teacher says aloud on day 63 that the acorn is a seed, but the item's own stem asks only "say what it stands for." A child who says "the acorn" is correct on material and loses the step. Fix: step 2 "says it stands for the acorn (a seed is credited if said, not required)". Component 3 is "correct transported material", and acorn is that.

5. **s06-E11: direction rests on one clue.** The picture has dots on all three parts and no arrows. The source is told by "dots at its middle" against "two dots" on the receiver, and by the receiver's "patch." A child can fairly read the right-hand flower as source if the dots look like the "more dots" side. Fix: the card text should say "dots start on the first flower" in the picture description, or print an unmarked start dot on the left flower. Unscored, so low risk.

6. **s06-E12: color is the only cue in the stem.** "One brown, one yellow." The piece shapes differ (seed piece, dot), which the card says, but a child with a color vision difference gets only the shape. Fix: key and stem accept the shape (large seed piece, small round dot) and the card prints the word "seed" shape mark in black outline as well. Unscored.

7. **s06-E8 (minor): the stem gives the answer.** "Tell how the beak halves hold the acorn and let it go" leaves "close" and "open" as the only content. This matches component 8 for grade 2 and is fair; note for the lead that component 8 can be met by repeating the stem, so the stronger test is the E9 trial record. The key line "hooks are not needed" is not asked by the stem and is not a step, so it is correctly not required.

8. **Print note (E1 to E3, E5 to E7):** day 63 Side 2 prints these six teacher asks as numbered student items, with no answer lines on the model record for them. They are observation asks. Say so on the page or give each a "show it" box so a child does not think they must write.
