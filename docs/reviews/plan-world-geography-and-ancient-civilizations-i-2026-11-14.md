## Course verdict

The content is accurate and careful. Its 2026 background is current: the INPE estimate and Brooklyn Rivera's death check out. The sources are mostly exact, with honest limits. The living-peoples handling in units 9 to 11 is strong.

It is not yet to the bar, for four reasons:
- Unit 1 sets a sitting before day 1, against the lead rule.
- Unit 8's research project has collapsed into one alphabet packet that repeats unit 6.
- Unit 6's frame and day 79 state enslaved rowers as fact, which the plan's own source does not support.
- The settled assessment-sizing rule is broken again in eight units.

Every fix is an edit inside existing days. Nothing needs Cory.

Unit 3's split (days 26 to 38 plus day 55) is named in the header, field 2, field 3, the exit table, field 6 and unit 4's fields 3 and 7. It needs one teacher line (3.2).

## Course-wide findings

### CW1. Assessments overloaded for their minutes (moderate, plan, REPEAT of grade 4 Settled sizing and WG-II CW1)
The grade 4 table sets the rule:
- about 1 minute per locate or date item;
- 2 minutes per one-sentence explanation;
- 4 to 5 minutes per constructed map or line;
- 8 minutes per paragraph claim;
- cut instances, never parts.

Units s01, s02, s04, s05, s06, s07, s09 and s10 break it, and s11 task 2 is borderline. The fixes are per unit below.

### CW2. Ten-minute repairs and bridges in first-teaching minutes (moderate or minor, plan, REPEAT of WG-II Settled 6 and the K and grade 1 rule)
The error-register footers in s02, s03, s04 and s09 say "next day's first ten minutes". Several retrieval bridges (s02 day 12, s04 day 41) take ten minutes from the only teaching day of a new skill. The settled rule:
- observations never take the only teaching minutes of a new skill;
- reteach goes in the warm-up of the next practice day, or in supported work.

s10 and s11 already word it correctly ("inside supported work... Protect the day's new teaching"). Use their wording as the model.

### CW3. Empty or dangling sensitivity flags (minor, skeleton, REPEAT of WG-II CW3)
Field 10 "Days flagged" reads:

- "Day 143: " in s11.

The skeleton writes a flag whenever the days.csv notes column is non-empty, even when that column holds only a base note.

### CW4. Pipeline notes in teacher text (minor, process, REPEAT of WG-II Settled 7)
