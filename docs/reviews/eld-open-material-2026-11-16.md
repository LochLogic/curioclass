# English language development: open material scout (2026-11-16)

Base-text scout for the ELD courses, in the shape of the social studies scout (2026-11-13). The bar is
docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md: CC BY 4.0, public domain, or UK OGL v3.0. Licences were read at the source page on
2026-11-16 unless marked "search only". Standard: WIDA 2020 (docs/courses/eld/STANDARD.md). Building waits until C11
is proven in the core courses, so this scout records options; it adopts nothing.

## The short answer

**None fits as a whole-course base text.** No open K to 12 ELD curriculum was found; an education policy review
(New America) also notes that open ELD material is thin. Two partial bases exist: EL Education K to 5 (CC BY,
already in the store) for its language lessons in K to 5, and VOA Learning English (public domain, item by item)
for secondary newcomers. The courses are written by us, with content taken from the core units (STANDARD section 3).

## For the frames (after the gate)

- K to 5: pair each ELD unit with the EL Education module the ELA course uses that month; adapt its Language Dive
  routine with credit.
- 6 to 12 newcomers: pick VOA items by topic and level; record source URL, date, credit line and grade check.
- No program or service name in student, teacher or family pages beyond the credit line a licence asks for.
