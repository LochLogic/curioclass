## Ladder

Kindergarten is the bottom grade, so the ledger "before science-kindergarten" holds only the course's own units; no lower-grade case, word or exit was reused or assumed. The ladder here is inside the course, and it is mostly used well: day 5 hands off cleanly from day 4 and back to day 6 (order cards, error D), day 24 hands off from day 23 (error F) and to day 25 (error D), day 40 opens with day 39's error I and sets up day 41's J and I, and the terms the ledger calls review (touch, three-box strip, claim, place, link, resource) are used in the same words.

What the missing grade below caused: nothing is assumed from outside. But four days teach more than their kind allows. Day 24 and day 40 are typed Application ("nothing is taught as new") while the ledger gives bumper, turn, "less harm", tick counts and a new sense of "plan" as first taught there. Day 5 and day 24 each re-ask something unit 1 already showed (a touch changes a moving ball; a touch, not nearness, turns the path) without saying so, and day 24 builds a new repair for error D instead of reusing unit 1's order cards, which exist for that exact error. Cases are reused as exits across days (the cushion on days 1 and 5), and day 42's people lines are day 37's worked case on a new picture. The plan and gate are not tuned for a three-day assessment window (the day 42 TEMPLATE row).
