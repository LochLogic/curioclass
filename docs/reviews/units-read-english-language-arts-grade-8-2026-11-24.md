## Verdict

The five units read as one sound year: the arc climbs from a shared whole novella to supported primary sources, a
research cycle, a literary unit with staging and a second, shorter research cycle ending in a talk to a real
audience, and every hand-back between units has both ends. Day loads in the headers match days.csv exactly, and
the grade 7 and grade 9 seams agree with what those courses expect. The faults a single-unit read missed were all
about the year: two thread words of the founding-documents thread were on no unit's word list, the year's argument
rubric was never named as one rubric across units 2 and 5, and grammar from unit 1 vanished for ninety days. All
three are fixed in the plans; one text record change goes to the structure pass.
