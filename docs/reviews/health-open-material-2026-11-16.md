# Health: open material scout

Read 2026-11-16 (pre-work for health). Research notes, not legal advice. Every licence read on the publisher's own
page unless marked "search only". The bar is docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md: CC BY 4.0, CC BY 3.0 or public domain can be
a base; NC, SA, ND or unclear terms are reference only.

## The short answer

**No openly licensed K to 12 health curriculum fits as a base.** The free comprehensive curricula found are
copyrighted with no open licence, or free only in part. Health is written fresh, under
docs/courses/health/STANDARD.md and the approach page.

Two US government sets are **possible partial bases for single topics** (nutrition in grades 1 to 6, drug
science in middle school), because federal works are usually public domain. Neither was verified at source today:
the nutrition page refused the fetch. Before either is adapted, its own page and every embedded item (songs,
photos, third-party pieces) are checked, and the result is recorded here.

## What the scout does not settle

- Figures go stale. Wherever a base or reference gives a number (servings, prevalence, guideline minutes), the unit
  takes the current figure from the current federal source and dates it.
- The sensitive units have no open base. They are written fresh under STANDARD.md section 5, and they are the
  first place a qualified reader's check matters (bar criterion 7).
