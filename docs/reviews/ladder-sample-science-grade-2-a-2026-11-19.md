# Ladder sample, science grade 2, sample A (2026-11-19)

Read-only sample of the science ladder run (later.csv L81). A fresh reader, cold, one day at a time. Nothing was fixed. Each day was read in full with the day before it, its section plan block, DEFAULTS.md, its rows of days.csv, items.csv, wrongs.csv, the unit's errors.csv rows, handbacks.csv and every piece its leans_on names. The ladder file was saved to a temp folder and grepped for kindergarten, grade 1 and grade 2 earlier units. Both gates were run, filtered as instructed.

How the checks were made. Every exit and end key was reworked by hand against the packet, card or data it points at (day 8 six marks and record letters, day 16 yes/no, day 21 bars 9/8, 3/4, 2/6 and path 3 and 5, day 27 felt and foam, the day 10 retry key). Facts that are judged only, not checked against a source (no source was consulted for any of them): that ice and snow are solid water, that damp sand moves under a low fan, that sealed ice cups are safe held 20 seconds, that handwashing for 30 children on two sinks takes about 20 seconds a child, and the safety of the six sample types.

## Ladder section

What the grade gap caused:

- Days 8 and 10 lean on grade 2's own unit 1 only; liquid and solid are first taught on day 7 (not in kindergarten or grade 1) and are named as new there. That is right. Day 8's new word "record" and its untaught pattern (liquid low) are the gap-style faults.
- Day 16's retrieval is where the lower grades were used worst. The design cycle is grade 1 (u02-2 day 16) but the card says "unit 1"; the daylight card tests sun height while grade 1 taught length of daylight; the signal caption contradicts grade 1's "a signal is not a message".
- Day 27's retrieval reads a unit 2 strip that unit 2 never made. Its cards 1 and 2 (daylight bars, spoon shape and job) are correct uses of grade 1; card 3 (lets light through) is a correct review of grade 1 u05-2.
- Day 27 teaches property words (rough, smooth, hard, soft, bends, stiff) as new; the ledger shows no lower grade taught them, so there is no clash. "Job" is a new sense next to grade 1's "shape and job", and the ledger says so.
- Day 10 first teaches "uncertain" and the dotted arrow, new on a Respond day; day 11, day 21 and day 26 then review it correctly.
- Rule 4 against lower grades: no exit reuses a kindergarten or grade 1 case. Rule 4 inside the grade: X21 re-skins X17; the day 10 retry re-skins day 8's Pine Coast; X27 re-skins the day's own blue pair.

Whether this grade used the lower grades well: where it names them (day 16 design cycle, day 27 daylight bars and shape and job) the idea is mostly right but the wording slips in the retrieval cards and wall strips. The assessment routines (neutral read-aloud, one packet, one scale) reuse day 8's pattern consistently across days 9, 23 to 25 and 45.
