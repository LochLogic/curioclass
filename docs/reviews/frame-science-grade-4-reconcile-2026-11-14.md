## Verdict

The frame and the old work agree on what grade 4 teaches and in what row order; the differences are in packaging,
picks and detail. Of 32 differences the old work won 18 (12 on a named source, 6 judgment calls) and the frame 14.
The old work did not steer: its sourced wins are core idea elements, connection boxes and evidence the frame left
out, its judgment wins are small additions (a tub not a tray, a layer model, a contour model, "watch for" lines),
and it lost on unit order, unit edges, engineering ownership, the device pick and deep time.
