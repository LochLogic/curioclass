# Answer-key read: social-studies-grade-3, unit 5 (days 90 to 121)

Kind: answer-key (docs/pipeline/briefs/sensitivity-read.md, sections 1 to 3). Read by a fresh agent.

## What I studied

Nothing outside; the study step is to solve. I solved every exit and end item cold from the card text (courses/social-studies-grade-3/u05-*/cards-*.md, the two packets, written-moves-day121.md) before opening any key file, items.csv answer column or wrongs.csv. Then I compared, then checked each fact against its sources.csv row (U5A to U5AC, U5R1 to U5R7) and each case against the unit's shared pieces and the lower-grade ledger (filtered terms only).

## Verdict

Pass with fixes. 78 exit and end items read (plus the 12 practice rows for context only); my cold answers matched the key on nearly every item, and the faults were in item design: two asks, answers given by the stem, cases repeated from shared pieces, a dossier with no Native evidence for a Native-nations question, and keys that needed facts the card never gave. All are fixed in the data and every changed item was answered cold again.
