## Unit 1: How we study the past

### 1. Serious: The assessment presents an unidentified lake record as empirical evidence.

**Location:** `s01.md`, field 5, item 5 and its source pack.  
**LAYER:** plan. **Confidence:** firm.

The card states that “one Saharan lake record” shows reduced rainfall and vegetation 4,700 years ago, but it names neither the lake nor the underlying record. The linked 2024 paper principally investigates climate-model experiments. The linked federal summary discusses offshore sediment evidence around 5,000 years ago, rather than the particular lake record described here. A lesson writer could turn this unspecified card into an apparently authentic scientific observation. [Climate-model study](https://cp.copernicus.org/articles/20/1595/2024/), [End of the African Humid Period](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2021-11/5%20End%20of%20the%20Africian%20Humid%20Period%20-Final_OCT%202021.pdf).

**Exact fix:** Replace:

> “T, one Saharan lake record shows less rainfall and vegetation, 4700 years ago.”

With:

> “T, a modern summary of offshore sediment records reports increased dust from northern Africa around 5,000 years ago, interpreted as evidence of reduced vegetation.”

Identify the federal summary and its underlying sediment studies on the card. Change the timeline placement to approximately 5,000 years ago. Revise the answer and sample to distinguish measured dust from inferred vegetation and rainfall. Label any invented pollen or dust values used on day 8 as constructed practice data.
