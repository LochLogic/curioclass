# Physical education: open material scout

Read 2026-11-16 (pre-work for PE). Research notes, not legal advice. Every licence read on the publisher's own page
unless marked "search only". The bar is docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md: CC BY 4.0, CC BY 3.0 or public domain can be a
base; NC, SA, ND or unclear terms are reference only.

## The short answer

**No openly licensed K to 12 PE curriculum fits as a base.** The best-known free PE curriculum is free to use
after registration but carries no open licence, and it is owned by a company. Other open pieces found are
NC-licensed single lessons. PE is written fresh, under docs/courses/physical-education/STANDARD.md and the
approach page.

## What the scout does not settle

- Skill cue videos. None openly licensed at scale was found. Cue pictures and any video are made fresh or linked,
  never copied.
- Games and dances from many cultures: rules of traditional games are not owned and can be described in our own
  words; a specific published version or recording is checked on its own terms.
