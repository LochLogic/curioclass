## Verdict

The check is sound mathematics and a fair stretch 10 check: one item a part, in the stretch's numbers, keys right,
a clear respond half in 60 minutes that a teacher can run from the page. The biggest risks are around the check
rather than in it: the day 87 slips, marked two-step work with estimates, sit on desks during an unaided check
whose items 7 and 8 test exactly that; and "Tomorrow" sends errors F and H to an estimate warm-up that answers
neither and does not exist in days 89 to 92. The one change that matters most is to hand the slips back at
pencils down (finding 1); the next is to make item 8's Ravi show the error the key names (finding 3).
