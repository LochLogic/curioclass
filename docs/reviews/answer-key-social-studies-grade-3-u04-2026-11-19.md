Kind: answer-key. Read by a fresh reader. ## Verdict

Pass with fixes. Most keys agree with my cold answers; the faults were answers or keys that ask for a fact the card does not give, one wrong example key (Q7), a case that repeats a taught case (E74), wrongs rows filed under the wrong ask, two stems with an unstated "answer", one stem with two asks, one unsupported card detail, and a locator picture that disagrees with its source.
