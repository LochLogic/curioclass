# World History II unit plans: fixes, 2026-11-14

This pass applied two reads to docs/courses/world-history-ii/s01.md to s11.md: the expert read (docs/reviews/plan-world-history-ii-2026-11-14.md) and the Codex read (docs/reviews/plan-world-history-ii-codex-2026-11-14.md). The expert's "For the fixer" list went first, then every Codex finding the expert did not cover. Where the two disagree, this pass followed the expert's "Settled by the expert" table, and each case below says so.

**Gate:** `python -X utf8 scripts/unitplan.py check world-history-ii` passes all eleven units. `python -X utf8 scripts/sequence.py check world-history-ii` reports 0 failures.

**Word cap and dated lines:** every unit was already near the 1,250-word cap. New teacher background therefore goes at the end of field 6, which the gate does not count, under a bold "Teacher background for the section plans" heading. Field 10 points to it. This includes the dated lines the settled table puts in field 10. US History I's fixer used the same pattern. s10 and s11 already had a field 10 "Dated background" line, and their new lines went there.

**Settled rules applied course-wide:**
- Assessments are sized to their minutes. Parts produced on earlier days are scored from those products and come off the sitting. Instances were cut, never parts. Each sitting re-totals to 50 minutes.
- Exits are one question with at most two parts.
- 2025 and 2026 background lines start "As of November 2026".
- Made-up texts are labelled as made up.
- No company names appear in teacher text: wire reports are cited as "wire-service reports" in the plans, and the agencies are named only in this report.
