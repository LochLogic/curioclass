## Findings

1. **E6 is two points in one, and its first sample does not show the revision's evidence.** The step reads "model revised with evidence, and long history kept apart from a week of weather". A child who revises well with a find but leaves out the week of weather scores 0, and the reverse. The sample "I changed my all-grassland model to lake, then trees. A week of log cannot tell this." cites no fossil, though the key says "cites the fossils". Fix: either split the point (a revision that cites a find; the week kept apart) or reword the key to "cites a find or says what the fossils show", and add a find to the sample: "I changed grassland to lake, then trees, because of the fish and the rooted trunks. A week of log cannot tell this."
2. **E9 key says "water to frog breeding"; the card's record says only "Frogs move to another pond".** The goal card does mention frog egg clusters, but a child who draws water to frogs moving away follows the record exactly and might be marked wrong. Fix: key "an arrow from water to the frogs (moving, or breeding), tied to its record".
3. **E4 sample does not name the lake or the trees.** "The lower layer is older because the layers were not disturbed." meets the key only if the drawing shows the lake below and trees above. Fix: say in the step "may be shown on the drawing", or use "The lake layer is lower, so older, because the layers were not disturbed; the trees came after."
4. **E3 keys only the fish and the trunk.** The card also gives leaf prints at 6 centimeters. A child who reads the leaf size as the water plant's leaf size is right and should not lose the point. Fix: add "or the 6 centimeter leaf prints" to the key line.
5. **E12 key names one limit.** Fair others: only two sites were tried, 10 plants were counted, or the counts come from models. Step 1 already says "the model limit named", so say in the answer cell "accept any limit the trials support".
6. **E1 and E2 overlap.** E1's sample says "at both places", which is E2's point. A child who writes it once earns both. Fix: drop "at both places" from the E1 sample.

## Could not settle (to the lead)

- **Scored work is the seen product, not an unseen site.** days.csv day 95 reads "from an unseen fossil site" and day 96 "one unseen environmental change", but E1 to E12 score the revised product F and S, which use cards the children already worked on (days 86 and 91), then a revision after written feedback. The unseen fresh cards on days 95 and 96 are an unscored diagnostic. This may be deliberate (no-kit, feedback and repair), but the stated goals and the scored evidence differ. Decide whether to reword the day goals or to score part of the fresh card.
- **One change per revision.** Days 93 and 94 let a child change "one thing the feedback points to", but the score sheet scores all six points on the revised product. A child with three "not yet" marks can recover at most one, so the end score measures the first draft plus one repair. Decide if that is the intended measure.
