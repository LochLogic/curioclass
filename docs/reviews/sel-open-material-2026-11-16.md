# Social and emotional learning: open material scout (2026-11-16)

Base-text scout for the SEL courses, in the shape of the social studies scout (2026-11-13). The bar is
docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md: CC BY 4.0, public domain, or UK OGL v3.0 (accepted by Cory 2026-11-13). Licences were
read at the source page on 2026-11-16 unless marked "search only". Standard: CASEL 5 (docs/courses/sel/STANDARD.md).

## The short answer

**No open material fits as a whole-course base text.** The best partial base is Oak National Academy's RSHE (PSHE)
units under OGL v3.0: their feelings, friendship and kindness lessons fit SEL units in grades 1 to 6 and some in 7
to 10, lesson by lesson, after the sensitivity read. Everything else is reference only. The SEL courses are written
by us from the CASEL sub-skills, using Oak lessons where a unit matches.

## Searched and found nothing that fits

Open K to 12 SEL courses under CC BY or public domain: none found. Several state SEL pages publish standards or
adult guidance, not student lessons. Some lesson sites state "free" without a licence, which is not open under the
bar.
