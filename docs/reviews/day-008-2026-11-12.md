## Verdict

Day 8 is mathematically sound and a teacher could run most of it from the page: every key is right, the sticks
and the plus sign are well chosen to show that "10 jumps" fails when step 1 is not one jump, and every 4.OA.C.5
item that claims parts (c) and (d) really asks for a feature and a reason. The biggest risk is that the exit ticket
does not ask for a later step at all, so the day's headline goal goes unmeasured and the error B signal it hands to
day 9's groups ("all five steps drawn") is not an error on a five-step ticket. The display adds a second risk: both
planned questions are answered on the screen before they are asked, and no shape is drawn anywhere on a shape day.
Fix the exit first (one item row, settled below).

## Findings, most serious first

### 1. The exit ticket never asks for a later step, so the day's goal is unmeasured and its B signal is void

- **What is wrong.** The goal is "find a later step without drawing them all". Every taught item asks for the 10th
  step (K8-1, T8-1, T8-2, F8-2, P8-1). The exit, X8, asks only for steps 1 to 5 and to draw steps 2 and 3. Its
  error B row is "all five steps drawn and counted one by one", but drawing five small steps is a sensible way to
  answer a five-step question, not error B. Tomorrow then sends "all five steps drawn" to day 9's A and B group,
  and day 9's group list repeats it.
- **Evidence.** items.csv s02-X8; wrongs.csv s02-X8 (B row); d008 Tomorrow; d009 Groups; s02.md U5 key moment 2 ("a
  later step comes from the rule, not from drawing").
- **Cost.** The record sheet's E8 column says nothing about the day's key moment, and students who drew steps they
  were asked to draw are put in a reteach group. The unit assessment does not test a far step either (E1 and E4 stop
  at the 6th), so after day 8 nothing in the unit ever checks it.
- **Change.** Replace X8 with s02-X8v2 (see "Settled by the expert"): steps 1 to 4 and the 10th step, step 2 drawn in
  a box. Tomorrow: "the 10th as 30 (error A) or all ten steps drawn (error B)". Day 9's group list: replace "B (all
  five steps drawn)" with "B (all ten steps drawn)".
- **Confidence.** Firm.
- **LAYER:** day. **CATCH:** expert. **REPEAT:** yes (day 90's tool exit "tested nothing on the day's goal",
  LESSON-DAY "What day 90 showed" 7). **CHEAP:** missed (the cheap read found the missing drawing box, not this).

### 4. A shape-pattern day draws no shape anywhere

- **LAYER:** process. **CATCH:** expert. **REPEAT:** yes (day 1 finding 2, "the key idea is drawn nowhere"; day 90,
