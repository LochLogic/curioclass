Science K to 5 is Curio Class's own (no base text; docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md, "Our own"); grades 6 to 8 adapt OpenSciEd Middle School (CC BY 4.0, held in S:\CurioClass-sources\openscied-ms) lesson by lesson in our own words, with one short credit line on its day in the BASE-TEXTS.md form, never its name or logo outside that line; the course frame's base-text line says which applies. Teach in three dimensions as the unit plan's Goal map and field 5 set them: a practice, a core idea and a crosscutting concept used together on a phenomenon the class can see or a record it can read. The class measures where it can; an invented record is the named fallback, printed and said aloud as invented for practice, and the plan says what happens when a reading fails. A reading sits inside its instrument's step. Every key, sample answer and worked case is answered cold before it is written; every listed wrong answer names its error-register letter. The exit is one ask on a new case in the Goal map row's exit shape, never the day's practice with the names swapped; kindergarten to grade 2, every child marks a picture on paper while the teacher reads the prompt aloud, and a spoken comparison or reason counts as produce. Safety: the course safety page (courses/COURSE/safety.md, where it exists) holds the year's room rules; a day names only what differs and the equipment it actually uses; eye protection where anything can splash or fly; no tasting; allergies asked for before foods, plants, soil or latex. Science comes first where a lesson meets a belief (Earth's age, evolution, climate): teach the evidence plainly, with at most one respectful teacher line ("People bring different ideas. In science class we look at what the evidence shows."), never hedging the science (Cory, 2026-11-23). No child discloses home, family, illness or a disaster they lived through; hazards are taught as places and events, never as a child's story. A real place, species, event or number rests on a checked source row, or is labelled invented for practice. For shape, look at courses/science-grade-3/ (a finished, signed-off grade: copy shape, not content).
