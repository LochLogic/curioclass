English language arts adapts its base text, EL Education K-5 Language Arts 2017 (CC BY 4.0; held in S:\CurioClass-sources\el-education-k5-2017, rules in docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md and the frame's base-text line), module lessons plus the K-2 Reading Foundations Skills Block (2017 edition only). Adapt in our own words with the day credit BASE-TEXTS.md gives; never use the 2024 or 2025 editions, trade books, logos, videos or anything EL marks as third party. Every text a day reads is a row of data/plans/ela-texts.csv that is `on file` or `written: <path>`: no day may need a trade book or any book the class may not have, and the day writer never writes a core text. Reading fits the minutes (about 120 words a minute in class); a cold check reads a new text, never a taught one. Foundations (letters, sounds, decoding, spelling, handwriting) follow the Skills Block cycle order the source map gives; decodable passages are adapted with the credit or written by us to the cycle's taught letters only (no word a child must decode may use an untaught pattern, apart from the cycle's named high-frequency words). End items (A ids) are never practised with their own words, even by plan design; dictations print numbered blank lines only. A child's own draft (essay, close, revision) stays unjudged folder work when it would share an exit's form; any judged practice uses a different task (pick the better of two, fix a weak one). For shape, look at courses/english-language-arts-grade-11/u01-1/ and data/plans/english-language-arts-grade-11/ (a finished ELA probe, a much older grade: copy shape, not content, length or tone) and courses/social-studies-kindergarten/u01-1/ (a finished kindergarten day).
