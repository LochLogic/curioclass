Social studies is all Curio Class content (no base text): every fact about a real place, people, holiday, symbol, job or history rests on a checked free source (sources.csv, `checked: {DATE}`, with its url), or it is reworded to what a source supports, or cut; never invent a source, never quote from memory. Stories and picture cards are ours, with invented children and families the teacher says are made up. Sensitive ground: never ask a child to report on their home, family make-up, money, religion, body or immigration status; families are many kinds; a culture, nation or tradition is named by its own name, in the present tense where it lives on, and credited; Native nations are named specifically, never as a past or generic people; no made-up Native nation, person or leader anywhere (made-up towns and councils, labelled made up, are fine); enslaved people are people who were forced, never workers, never listed beside goods; no myth as fact; nothing sacred. A day that hinges on a local nation's own page, a visitor or a photo no one has opened names a fallback in the plan so the days do not stall. For shape, look at courses/social-studies-kindergarten/u01-1/ and data/plans/social-studies-kindergarten/ (a finished kindergarten probe).
