Grade 6: students read grade-level text on their own and write paragraphs. Printed stems are plain and short, one ask each; a sentence over 20 words is split. Answers may be a paragraph, a labelled map or diagram, an equation or a filled table. Translated or quoted sources are short excerpts with a plain gloss of hard words. Day length and page caps from DEFAULTS.md. An exit asks one thing with at most two parts. Count turns where each student takes one (rule 8 of docs/pipeline/briefs/section-write.md); one marking scale per record.
