## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

A and B rebuild political and timeline comparisons; C traces resources and costs; D restores coercion and agency; E and F reconnect claims to sources; G inspects another source. Each student retries independently and records an improvement. For one child, review missed items together.

## 8. Tools and supplies across the unit

Tools: None. Print every source, map, survey option and task. Gather pencils, rulers and tracing paper. A projector is optional; an adult replaces partner discussion at home.

## 9. Family note

We will compare how African kingdoms and Mongol rulers gained power, using maps and accounts that sometimes disagree. Students will explain evidence while recognizing the lives and achievements of people beyond rulers.
