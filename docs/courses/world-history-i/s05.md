## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Who carried the knowledge of the classical world forward, and what did they change as they carried it?"
- **Hook, day 64:** Compare Arabic and Latin manuscript records for al-Khwarizmi's work. What might survive translation, and what might change? Open the compelling question before teaching the carriers.
- **Supporting questions, drafted together:** Who preserved and extended learning (day 66)? How did technologies travel (day 68)? How did farming and cloth reshape places (day 71)?
- **Closing claim, day 72:** Use al-Khwarizmi's own preface to the Algebra (Rosen's 1831 translation, public domain) and its manuscript history, Benedict's Rule and Bologna's institutional history. Explain continuity and innovation, answer one counterclaim and name a source limit. Share with a partner or adult.
- **Teaching:** Model sourcing on day 64 and a continuity comparison on day 65; students complete one on day 66. Model a causal chain on day 67; withdraw completed examples by day 72. Teach translation, manuscript, monastery, university, irrigation, surplus, textile and demand through evidence.
