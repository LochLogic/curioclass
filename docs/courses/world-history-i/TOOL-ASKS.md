# World History I: tool asks

## Network map

Unit 1 asks for an offline geographic layer editor. No spec or tool was available for this plan. The section plan must establish the final job before a build starts.

- Day 10: construction, key, check unaided, twenty minutes. Students select supplied geographic data, construct physical, political and network layers, and explain a pattern. Use coordinates or registered base-map locations, not a decorative drawing surface.
- Day 14: construction, key, check unaided, four minutes within A4. Load a separate assessment data set with fresh places and features. Preserve the student's constructed layers and explanation as a printable artifact. Give no hints, automatic corrections or answer overlay during assessment.
- Support keyboard operation, readable labels, patterns distinguishable without colour, and text descriptions of physical features. Work as one offline file, without an account, network, tracking or a student timer. An adult records the evidence.
- Supply matching printed base maps and tracing layers. Paper construction shows mapping and explanation; record geographic technology use as pending when the student has only that route. Watching a projector demonstration does not establish individual technology use.

The editor does not rank whole historical networks from incomplete data or grade open historical claims. Its job is constructing and explaining maps.
