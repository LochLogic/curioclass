## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Did belief follow power, or did power follow belief?"
- **Hook, day 16:** Show Kanishka's coin beside a Gandharan Buddha. What can rulers borrow from belief, and what can belief gain from rulers? Separate observation from inference.
