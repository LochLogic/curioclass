## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Did belief follow power, or did power follow belief?"
- **Hook, day 16:** Show Kanishka's coin beside a Gandharan Buddha. What can rulers borrow from belief, and what can belief gain from rulers? Separate observation from inference.
- **Supporting questions, drafted together on day 16:** What do followers teach (days 17 to 20)? What changes or survives locally (days 21 and 22)? Who carries beliefs, and why there (days 23 to 25)? How does patronage work (days 26 to 28)?
- **Closing claim, day 28:** Answer the compelling question using the chosen empire packet and Faxian's Khotan account. Cite two details, explain a connection, answer one counterclaim and name a limit. Share with a partner or adult before submitting.
- **Teaching:** Model attributed belief descriptions on day 17, perspective on day 21 and contextual explanation on day 26. Partners test explanations on day 27; students write independently on day 28. Teach covenant, dharma, karma, filial piety, diffusion and patronage through sources.
