<!-- skel -->
- **5-PS2-1:** Argue with evidence that Earth's gravity pulls objects down. Emphasis: down means toward the center of the round Earth, wherever you stand. Limit: Gravity is not described with math.
- **5-ESS1-2:** Graph data to show daily patterns in shadows, the cycle of daylight and darkness, and the seasonal patterns of which stars can be seen at night. Limit: What causes the seasons is left out.
- **5-ESS1-1:** Argue that the sun looks brighter than other stars because it is so much closer to Earth. Limit: Only distance, not star size; other causes of brightness, such as a star's mass, age or stage, are left out.
