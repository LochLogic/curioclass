## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **5-PS1-3** (days 59, 61): Observe and measure properties to tell what a material is, such as a powder, metal, mineral or liquid.
- **5-PS1-4** (days 60, 61): Investigate whether mixing two or more substances makes a new substance.
- skill s5-identify-materials (grade 5)
- skill s5-new-substances (grade 5)
