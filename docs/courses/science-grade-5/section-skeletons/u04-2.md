## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **5-ESS1-2** (days 67, 68, 69, 70): Graph data to show daily patterns in shadows, the cycle of daylight and darkness, and the seasonal patterns of which stars can be seen at night.
- skill s5-shadows-sky (grade 5)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Question board: Why does down differ on the globe (62 to 66)? Why do shadows change (67 to 69)? Does daylight change too (70)? Do the same stars return (71 to 74)? Why is one star so bright (75 to 77)? Join answers on day 78. Each answer raises the next question. Transfer: unfamiliar observation-site records and an observatory display.
> Three-dimensional goals: argue center-directed pull as a cause (62 to 66); graph matching and differing sky patterns using spin and orbit (67 to 74); argue from equal-light evidence and relative distances across very different scales (75 to 77).
> Carried: cause-effect, force-diagram and claim-evidence-reasoning begin on day 62; plumb evidence revises them on 64, with consensus on 66. Data-graph and patterns return on 67; hourly and monthly records revise them on 68, daylight on 70 and stars on 71. Earth-sun-moon begins on 67; lamp evidence revises spin on 69, star records add orbit on 72, and moon records add its orbit on 73; sky consensus is 74. Light-ray and scale-proportion begin on 75, revised by controlled light evidence on 76 and travel-time comparisons on 77. Day 78 joins all eight; assessments use them.
