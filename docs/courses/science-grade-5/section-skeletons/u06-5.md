Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> On day 113 children explain the anchor using community sources, trial graphs and failure records: protection can reduce material entering streams; classroom filters change cloudiness, without establishing drinking safety.

Assessments introduce Philadelphia street runoff and unfamiliar runoff catchers.

Keep data-graph: initial trial table on 105, revisions from measured repeats on 108 and 111. Design-cycle begins on 103, adds alternative predictions on 104, failure evidence on 108, a proposed change on 109 and retests on 110. All reach consensus on 113 and support the transfer tasks on 115 and 116. Unit 5's scale-proportion model rests.

Part 1: use both cards to explain a land-and-water interaction and one limit on the claim. Part 2: two proposed catchers receive different water amounts; identify the flaw and repair the plan. Answer: the planted hollow slows runoff and can retain some material; neither card establishes drinking safety. Use equal inputs to isolate the catcher difference. Wrong: separate facts (A), disconnected parts (B), drinking safety inferred (D), unequal amounts accepted (E). Inspect both parts before planning day 117's response; do not reteach on 115 or 116.

>
> Day 103's criteria, ours, untested: improve cloudiness by at least one category, collect at least half a cup in 60 seconds, avoid leaks, and use no more than one cup of media. Compare lower cloudiness with slower flow; no single measurement decides success. Day 104 proposes two designs differing in one feature, such as cloth-layer count, with the same sand and gravel portions. Day 105 specifies one cup of input per trial, equal initial wetting and draining, the same pour position, fresh stirring before each aliquot and the same collection interval. Alternate three trials of each design and reset cups identically. Compare cloudiness at equal depth, lighting and settling time. Check the half-cup criterion at the fill mark before rounding collected volumes to half cups for plots; use a spoon for positioning only, not unit conversion. Keep cloudiness ratings separate. Mark between-tick volumes as estimates. Day 110 repeats the same conditions after the chosen change; every trial stays in the record, with anomalies annotated.
