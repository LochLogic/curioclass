## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **5-ESS1-2** (days 79, 81): Graph data to show daily patterns in shadows, the cycle of daylight and darkness, and the seasonal patterns of which stars can be seen at night.
- **5-PS2-1** (days 80, 81): Argue with evidence that Earth's gravity pulls objects down.
- **5-ESS1-1** (days 80, 81): Argue that the sun looks brighter than other stars because it is so much closer to Earth.
