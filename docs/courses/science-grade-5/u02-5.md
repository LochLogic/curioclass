**Shares.** 5-PS3-1: "Use models" connects sunlight, plant food and animal functions. 5-LS1-1: "Argue with evidence" interprets plant/soil records within limits. 5-LS2-1: "Make a model" connects environmental returns. Modeling/argument join LS1.C/LS2.A/LS2.B/PS3.D with energy and matter through growing-plant records and ecosystem changes.

**Adult explanation.** Food supplies growth/repair matter and energy for growth, repair, movement and warmth. Plants make food using air/water matter and sunlight energy. Minerals matter but are not energy-supplying food. Energy transfers outward as warmth, never cycling back into plant food. Fresh plant weight includes water; do not call its gain dry biomass. Drying, handling losses and scale steps limit conclusions. No gas names, molecules or energy calculations.

**Connections.** SL.5.5 display support is modeled here; no assumed ELA mastery. Keep grams on one scale, without conversion.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: transfer, recipient, material, energy, tare, warmth. Frame: "This reading measures ___, not ___." At most 210 independent words daily; records read aloud.
