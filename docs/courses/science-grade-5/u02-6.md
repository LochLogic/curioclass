**Day 41 fresh retry R41.** Each child receives one assigned route, ten minutes plus five independent checking. All invented cases remain unused until retry; separate formative record uses only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Maximum two-part ask.

- **R41-S, sources.** Record: "Dry whole-plant weight rises from 6 g to 38 g. Dry soil falls from 900 g to 898 g. Soil-free plants gain dry weight in mineral water. Matched plants receiving different air grow differently. All other conditions match." Ask: "Argue for the chief incoming matter sources using these records. Explain sunlight's different role."
  Key: 32 g gain exceeds 2 g soil loss; soil-free growth with minerals and controlled air evidence support chiefly air/water; sunlight energy. Wrong: "Mainly soil" (A); "Water alone" (B); "Minerals unnecessary" (C); "Sunlight is material" (D); "Any growth alone proves its source" (I).
  Shape: integrated source argument. Prior repair sorts single comparisons; no combined argument or these numbers.
- **R41-R, returns.** Record: "A fern-bed model shows plant to leaf eater. Dead parts and wastes stop at an empty box." Ask: "Complete routes through decomposers and the environment for plant reuse. Include gas and water exchanges."
  Key: remains/wastes to decomposers, outputs to air/water/soil pools, uptake into plants; gas/water arrows cross organism boundaries. Wrong: "Dead parts vanish" (E); "No gas exchange occurs" (J).
  Shape: complete a reuse model with exchanges. Prior repair points to separate correct edges, never completes this fern-bed model.
- **R41-E, energy.** Record: "A goat eats grass, grows, heals, moves and stays warm." Ask: "Model food energy's origin, uses and destination. Show food material used for growth and repair."
  Key: sunlight to grass food to goat, all four uses, outward warmth; food material builds/repairs. Wrong: "Energy cycles through soil" (F); "Food gives no building material" (G); "Sunlight feeds the goat directly" (H).
  Shape: construct food-energy path plus material uses. Prior repair matches isolated labels; no full goat model.
- **R41-K, interactions.** Record: "In a clearing, two plant eaters share shoots. A new shoot eater arrives. No other food is available." Ask: "Predict one effect on an existing eater. Explain the shared-resource link."
  Key: fewer available shoots may reduce food for an existing eater, not a guaranteed outcome. Wrong: "The newcomer cannot affect existing organisms" (K).
  Shape: predict/justify a new-eater resource effect. Prior repair locates a supplied dependency, never predicts for this clearing.
