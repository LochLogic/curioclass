- **R41-R, returns.** Record: "A fern-bed model shows plant to leaf eater. Dead parts and wastes stop at an empty box." Ask: "Complete routes through decomposers and the environment for plant reuse. Include gas and water exchanges."
  Key: remains/wastes to decomposers, outputs to air/water/soil pools, uptake into plants; gas/water arrows cross organism boundaries. Wrong: "Dead parts vanish" (E); "No gas exchange occurs" (J).

- **R41-E, energy.** Record: "A goat eats grass, grows, heals, moves and stays warm." Ask: "Model food energy's origin, uses and destination. Show food material used for growth and repair."

  Shape: construct food-energy path plus material uses. Prior repair matches isolated labels; no full goat model.
- **R41-K, interactions.** Record: "In a clearing, two plant eaters share shoots. A new shoot eater arrives. No other food is available." Ask: "Predict one effect on an existing eater. Explain the shared-resource link."
  Key: fewer available shoots may reduce food for an existing eater, not a guaranteed outcome. Wrong: "The newcomer cannot affect existing organisms" (K).
  Shape: predict/justify a new-eater resource effect. Prior repair locates a supplied dependency, never predicts for this clearing.
