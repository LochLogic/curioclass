# Section 2.3: Following departing matter

## Header

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 5. Unit 2, Where a tree comes from. "Where does a growing tree's matter come from, and where does it go when the tree dies?" Anchor: the tree-in-a-pot experiment Jan Baptist van Helmont ran in Brussels in the 1600s: a willow grew from 5 pounds to about 169 in five years while its soil lost less than a pound (days 18 to 41): see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 27 to 31.** Days by kind: understanding 3 (27, 28, 31), application 2 (29, 30). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 3 of 6 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.

- **E30.** Item: "Invented for practice. A gutter screen holds torn leaf pieces after a windy afternoon. An earlier drawing shows the whole leaf on the roof. No weights or gas readings were taken.
