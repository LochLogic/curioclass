# Section 1.4: Explain the changed reading

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 5. Unit 1, Matter too small to see. "What is everything made of, if we cannot see the pieces, and does any of it vanish when it changes?" Anchor: a flat ball weighed, pumped up with air, and weighed again: it is heavier, though all we added was air we cannot see (days 1 to 17): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 12 to 17.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (12), fluency 1 (15), application 2 (13, 14), check 1 (16), respond 1 (17). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 4 of 4 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.

- **Share.** 5-PS1-2: "Measure and graph" through mixing, then integrate heating/cooling. 5-PS1-1: "Make a model" joining unseen matter and totals. Mathematics and model development connect PS1.A/PS1.B retained matter to scale, proportion and quantity. Cause and effect guides testing explanations, not assuming one from a lower number.
- **Limits/connections.** Whole grams, no conversion, particle names, reaction equations, buoyancy lesson or microscopic evaporation mechanism. Use 3.MD.A.2 readings. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing (science-grade-3 u01-2 day 6).
- **Teacher explanation.** The weighed boundary includes apparatus and retained gas. Tiny open trials may lose too little to resolve. Larger balloons can distort readings; never increase amounts to force a difference. An anomaly calls for checking conditions.
