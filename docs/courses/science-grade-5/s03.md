- **5-PS1-3:** Observe and measure properties to tell what a material is, such as a powder, metal, mineral or liquid. (The "such as" list gives examples, not required parts.) Emphasis: Properties include color, hardness, shine, conducting electricity or heat, response to magnets, and dissolving; density is not one of them. Limit: Density is left out, and mass and weight are not told apart.
- **5-PS1-4:** Investigate whether mixing two or more substances makes a new substance.
