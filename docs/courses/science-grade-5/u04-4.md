- **E75.** "Invented for practice. Both comparisons keep receiving area and method fixed. Ash keeps lamp output fixed. At 100 and 200 cm, readings are 8 and 2. Birch raises output at 200 cm and reads 8. Explain which record tests distance alone. Trace the light path that makes its screen visible." Key: Ash isolates distance, supporting dimmer patch farther away. Birch changes output, confounding comparison. Light travels source to screen to eye. Wrong: Changing distance cannot change a source's apparent brightness. (error F).
  Shape: evaluate competing controls plus trace seeing path. Practice observes one controlled demonstration; paths reviewed separately, never competing-record selection.
- **E76.** "Invented for practice. Wide screens catch the same total light nearby and far away. Nearby light covers four equal patches; farther away, sixteen. Explain why one fixed patch is dimmer farther away. Revise spreading paths to match this record." Key: Same total is distributed across more equal patches, so each receives less. Draw diverging paths across larger area, not light wearing out or disappearing. Wrong: Changing distance cannot change a source's apparent brightness. (error F).
  Shape: reconcile constant total/dimmer patch and revise spreading model. Practice audits controls/observes footprints, never equal-total arrays or ray revision.
- **E77.** "Invented for practice. Fern and Lake receive the same sun's light after eight and sixteen minutes. Identical fixed-exposure instruments show image widths eight and four squares. Brightness readings are eight and two. Other stars' light takes five and nine years. Explain which observer sees a larger, brighter sun. Use the records to support distance as the explanation." Key: Fern nearer: shorter travel, wider image, brighter under same-source controlled conditions. Sun is a star; others much farther and differently distant. Do not rank unlike stars from distance alone. Wrong: The sun is not a star. (error E).
