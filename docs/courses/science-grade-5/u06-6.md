**Administration and ownership.** Q1a/Q1b/Q1c on 115 and Q2a/Q2b on 116, whole in s06 field 5; last teaching is section 6.5. No extra exit on check days. R117 is fresh, unscored response evidence. Hide all assessment cards until their sitting. Read prompts/units without hints. Every child submits independent work.

**Q1a Shape:** combine two unfamiliar authentic sources into a short report and labelled causal system drawing. No practice card/model bundles that complete report/drawing or uses Philadelphia. Prompt: "Use both sources to explain how Philadelphia changes runoff routes. Include a labelled system drawing and a short report." Ten points as s06: four source use, four combined route explanation, two connected drawing. Cold key: the community account describes management before sewers; the scientific descriptions explain passage through soil/gravel or storage below paving. Connect land surfaces, ground/underdrain paths, sewers and receiving water; do not claim every planter infiltrates. Meets: a traceable combined route explanation with both sources. Wrong: separate facts (A); disconnected labels (B).

**Q1b Shape:** produce a system part, protection action and causal connection for each of six new impacts. To prevent elimination, omit the unit table's choice columns. Print only its six invented impact cards. Prompt: "For each card, name the affected system part. Explain an action that could reduce that impact." Six points, one per complete part/action connection. Cold key: bare cultivated land, retain soil with vegetation; removed tree habitat, preserve/replant habitat; stream runoff, intercept before entry; litter moving oceanward, prevent entry to waterways; engine pollution, reduce emissions at source; discarded orbiting equipment, prevent new orbital debris. Accept another supported action targeting that impact. Wrong: protecting a different disconnected part (I). Practice never pairs all six cards/actions.

**Q1c Shape:** distinguish reported completed work from a projection, locate remaining uncertainty and bound the claim. Prompt: "Which line reports completed work, and which anticipates a benefit? Use another line to explain remaining uncertainty and one protection limit." Four points as s06. Cold key: A1 completed projects; A3 expected overflow reduction; A4 says completion/evaluation need time; neither source tests drinking safety. The completed project count does not itself measure water safety or prove every expected benefit. Wrong: projection already measured (C); clear means drinkable (D). Practice never rehearses this whole line-selection/uncertainty/limit bundle.

Source-card precision: preserve s06's A1 to A4 and B1/B2, metadata and headings. The omitted-word excerpts were compared with opened pages on 2026-11-24. A4 omits construction words, so retain its FAQ heading/context. Expand the 16-word B2 excerpt onto two lines with an omission if needed; student sentences still require at most fifteen words. Prefer the exact shortened fragment "Stormwater soaks in at the surface ... stored in underlying layers of soil and gravel." Keep its meaning, ellipsis and B2 identifier. Do not replace infiltration/underdrain alternatives with universal soaking-in. Required source rows are still to make; see field 11.

**Q2a Shape:** collaborative planning and controlled alternating trials of two unseen catchers, with individual evidence. No earlier practice uses catchers or a two-design six-trial assessment bundle. Eight points per child: two each planning, observed controlled action, complete units/results, failure/limit. Cold-check the s06 problem: 20 mL input; collect outlet to five seconds; no more than 10 mL escapes; no side leaks; both inside the footprint. Six alternating trials; 1 mL cup steps and one-second timer, not fractional readings. Teacher supplies freshly stirred portions, equal prewet/drain/reset and protected edges. Planning is simultaneous paper, not five spoken asks in 35 seconds: 45 seconds mark one distinct control/feature decision each; six 25-second pour/read/reset cycles (150 seconds); thirty seconds individual fault notes; fifteen seconds finish. Total four minutes. First five pours distribute one controlled action to every child, sixth repeats one. Identify actions by name; no group score substitutes for observation.

Cold no-kit key: A 18/17/18 mL, B 8/9/8 mL. All A trials exceed 10; all B trials meet it. Both fit/no side leakage; A overtops. Baseline model numbers are invented for practice, never promised live results. Missing/obscured reading: one safe reread within cycle; then unresolved/reason. Keep it; do not invent a replacement. Wrong: unequal inputs (E); first trial alone proves a winner (F); delete a result (G). No-kit conducting stays unobserved.

**Q2b Shape:** individual all-criteria comparison plus failure-based improvement on a revised system drawing. No practice bundles this whole product for the assessment case. Four points as s06: numerical comparison, footprint/side leaks, repeated failure evidence, justified improvement/predicted retest. Key for fallback: A repeatedly overtops and exceeds escape limit; B meets it, both meet other limits. Raise A's barrier within the footprint/material limits and repeat matching trials. Accept another feasible failure-targeted change and actual-data conclusions. Wrong: single-trial proof (F); an unrelated change (H); erased inconvenient repeat (G). Practical evidence remains exactly what was observed.

**R117, ten minutes.** Entire new case is invented for practice. Card K1: "A crew covers a sand pile before rain." K2: "A covered model loses fewer grains into its drain than an uncovered model." Inset: two proposed covers are tested with equal inputs but different slopes. Ask: "Explain this protection plan using both cards and a labelled water route. Identify the trial flaw and draw one repair." Key: covering can reduce sand carried from pile to drain, supported separately by action and model record; model evidence cannot establish community effectiveness or drinking safety. Match slopes while holding other conditions fixed. Wrong: separate facts (A); disconnected parts (B); drinkable runoff (D); change unrelated to the flaw (H).
Shape: fresh source/model explanation plus control repair. Repair packets teach individual components only, never this composite product or covered-pile case.
