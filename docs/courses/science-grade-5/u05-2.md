## 9. Supports and extension

Watershed means land draining to a common outlet, distinct from a built water-storage reservoir. Offer raised terrain, high-contrast overlays and a dry observer role with equal evidence responsibility. Neutral reading/scribing preserves child choices. Independent records total at most 120 words daily. Extend by naming a limit of tray-to-landscape inference.
