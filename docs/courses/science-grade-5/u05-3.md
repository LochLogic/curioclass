# Section 5.3: Where water is and what each whole means

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 5. Unit 5, Earth's systems and its water. "How do water, air, land and life shape each other, and how much of Earth's water can we use?" Anchor: from space Earth looks blue, yet towns run short of water to drink; and one mountain range (the Cascades of Washington) has rain forest on its west side and dry land on its east (days 85 to 99): see [s05.md](s05.md). **Days 92 to 95.** Days by kind: understanding 2 (92, 95), application 2 (93, 94). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 3 of 4 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Using mathematics and computational thinking joins ESS2.C with scale, proportion and quantity. Models represent water amounts, not map areas. Connections: 4.NF.C.6, day 93 equal shares; decimal reading on 95 directly explained if math teaching is unavailable.

Groundwater occupies spaces in earth materials, not a universal underground lake. Fresh-only whole enlarges a subset for inspection. No percentages, huge volumes or atmospheric quantities. Teach graph units/nested-whole conventions on 92 before application days.

## 9. Supports and extension

Teach store/reservoir, groundwater, glacier, polar ice, salt/fresh, share, whole and inset with drawings/strips. Frame: "These units represent ___ water." Fade before exits. Large/tactile grids and identical raised axes preserve construction; neutral scribe follows child choices. Independent records/labels at most 130 words daily; read directions aloud. Extend by explaining one amount on two valid scales, no percent calculation.
