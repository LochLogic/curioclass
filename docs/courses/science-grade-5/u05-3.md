# Section 5.3: Where water is and what each whole means



## 1. Standards and practices


**Share:** 5-ESS2-2, "Describe and graph": locate named stores, distinguish salt/fresh, change the whole and compare small stores on identical axes. Using mathematics and computational thinking joins ESS2.C with scale, proportion and quantity. Models represent water amounts, not map areas. Connections: 4.NF.C.6, day 93 equal shares; decimal reading on 95 directly explained if math teaching is unavailable.
Teacher distinguishes salty groundwater/lakes from ocean, combined ice caps/glaciers/permanent snow from sea ice, and surface-and-other remainder from lakes/rivers alone. Groundwater occupies spaces in earth materials, not a universal underground lake. Fresh-only whole enlarges a subset for inspection. No percentages, huge volumes or atmospheric quantities. Teach graph units/nested-whole conventions on 92 before application days.
