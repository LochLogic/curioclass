# Section 5.4: Available water, joined explanations and response

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 5. Unit 5, Earth's systems and its water. "How do water, air, land and life shape each other, and how much of Earth's water can we use?" Anchor: from space Earth looks blue, yet towns run short of water to drink; and one mountain range (the Cascades of Washington) has rain forest on its west side and dry land on its east (days 85 to 99): see [s05.md](s05.md). **Days 96 to 99.** Days by kind: fluency 1 (97), application 1 (96), check 1 (98), respond 1 (99). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 4 of 4 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.
