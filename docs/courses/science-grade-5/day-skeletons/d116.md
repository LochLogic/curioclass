## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Assess fair trials, failure analysis and improvement of unfamiliar runoff catchers.
- **Note:** Every part of 3-5-ETS1-3 asked once: each child contributes to the collaborative plan, controlled trials and failure analysis; run three quick trials per prepared design, compare against criteria and constraints and justify an improvement. Six groups of five at four observed minutes each, six minutes set-up/transitions, ten minutes independent test-plan/failure explanations, five cleanup. Waiting students work on that independent task. One per-child record, no duplicate collection on day 119. Timer measures flow over an equal interval. No-kit: conducting not yet observed. Response day 117. Six small fixed-interval pours per group permit every child to conduct a test action; pre-portioned water and one prepared catcher pair keep the station within four minutes. Data route: full 45-minute meeting; exit: half. Paper replaces unbuilt tool; zero digital minutes.
