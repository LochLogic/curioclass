## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Assess an unseen material change with particle models and measured-weight graphs.
- **Note:** Full 5-PS1-1 and 5-PS1-2: gas and dissolved matter at unseen scale; measure and graph heating, cooling and mixing in grams; explain complete-system differences. Observed weighing plus new records, not class cases. For 30: six groups of five at four observed minutes per group plus four transition minutes; every child weighs once while others independently complete the model/graph records. Seventeen independent minutes and overlapping waiting time hold the paper evidence. No-kit: weighing not yet observed. Every part of each listed code asked once; response day 17.

- **5-PS1-1:** Make a model showing that matter is made of bits too tiny to see.
