> Day 89, each group of five: one shallow plastic tray, two cups clean damp sand, one cup water, pouring cup, towels, five splash goggles and five nonlatex glove pairs. Reuse kitchen trays and cups. The day before, the teacher wets sand, checks eyewash access, stabilizes trays and rehearses a small pour. Dust, splashes, ingestion and slippery spills are hazards: no blowing or tasting; goggles and gloves stay on through cleanup; contain pours and wipe spills. Stop for unsafe handling or missing protection. Only the teacher portions dry sand and disposes of sediment in solid waste. Reserve seven minutes to collect sand, wipe, remove gloves and wash hands; never pour sediment down sinks.
