- **Compelling question (frame):** "Where does a growing tree's matter come from, and where does it go when the tree dies?"

<!-- skel -->
- **5-LS1-1:** Argue with evidence that plants get the material they grow from mostly from air and water. Emphasis: most of a plant's matter is built from air and water; soil supplies very little of it.
- **5-LS2-1:** Make a model of how matter passes between the surroundings and plants, animals and decomposers. Emphasis: plants change matter that is not food, such as air, water and broken-down soil material, into food. Limit: No explanations at the level of molecules.
- **5-PS3-1:** Use models to show that the energy in animals' food, used to grow, repair, move and stay warm, first came from the sun.
