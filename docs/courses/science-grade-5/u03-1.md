## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none; section 3.4 owns Q1 to Q4 teaching, 3.5 administers them. Notebook evidence E45/E46/E47 is individual paper, four minutes each. One formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. More than ten misses triggers the named next-day repair. An unsupported answer gets a follow-up before assigning a letter.

- **E45.** Invented for practice: Reference A is white and dissolves fully. Reference B is white and leaves grains. Unknown C is white and dissolves fully. Powder amounts were not recorded. Water amounts, stirring and observation times matched. Which identification claim has support? What measurement must the next comparison include?
  Key: tentative A match; measure starting powder weights in grams and use matched amounts before confirming.
  Wrong: "White proves A" (A); "Record starting weight without a unit" (B)
  Shape: interpret an incomplete reference record and supply its missing measurement. Practice uses separate components, never this held case or full response.

- **E46.** Invented for practice: Sheets L and M look equally shiny. Under equal loads, L bends and M stays stiff. A shield must stay stiff. Choose the sheet for this shield. Propose one recorded property test against its lookalike.
  Key: M from the bending record; accept a safe fair further test, such as matched scratch observations, with labelled results/source location. Multiple valid test proposals.
  Wrong: "L, because shine proves stiffness" (A)
  Shape: choose for a purpose AND propose a further recorded test. Practice uses separate components, never this held case or full response.

- **E47.** Invented for practice: Powder T leaves grains in 10 mL water. Its comparison clears after equal stirring and waiting. Starting powder weights are missing. Record the dissolving difference. Name a further measurement needed before identifying T.
  Key: T leaves solid while comparison clears; obtain starting powder weights in grams under matched water, stirring and interval. No unique identity follows.
  Wrong: "Weigh starting portions but omit grams" (B); "White means salt" (A)
  Shape: contrast dissolution AND identify missing measurement. Practice uses separate components, never this held case or full response.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


Children hold the property-evidence table; add test/condition/source columns on 46 and measured dissolving observations on 47. Review grade 2 u03-1: property, something we can see or feel about a material, and a test can show more; color tells color and nothing else. Review its sorting chart and pattern chart: different materials share a property. Use US spelling in new copy.

Review grade 4 u01-1: fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing; question board: notices become questions. Review u02-1: rotating six heard, record sheet names who; shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Each public contribution takes 45 seconds, at most six within five minutes; pairs work simultaneously. All thirty return paper. Grade 4 u02-4's two-column notes: source statement and source location returns on 46.

Unit 1 u01-3 data-graph and u02-5 tare are planned only: explicitly demonstrate labelled readings, empty-cup zeroing and retaining originals. Ideal instruments: six portion sets, 1 g scale station, 1 mL marked measure and 1 cm ruler. Physical apparatus fits; no digital/key tool. Practice quantities are teacher-selected within instrument steps, not predicted outcomes. One ambiguous reading gets one retry; retain both, then label fallback separately.

Ask allergies before powders/foods. Goggles, labelled cups, trays, soap, towels and approved wipes ready. No tasting, sniffing or cooking. Two sinks need ten minutes for thirty 40-second washes, plus two for collection/goggle care. Without this provision, adult handles samples and children use records; practical evidence stays unobserved. Fallback P47: 2 g powder, 20 mL water, 30 seconds stirring, grains visible at 60 seconds, invented for practice. No-kit: labelled photos/records, never firsthand credit.

## 6. Days

## Day 45


- **7 min. Opening.** Inspect coded cups and jar drawing; collect all thirty notices. Hear rotating six within five minutes. Pose the unit question; lab powders never enter food.
- **6 min. Teaching first.** Name observable properties, grams (g) and milliliters (mL); distinguish a test from an identity. Use unrelated colored counters for the component example, not reference matching.
- **12 min. Observation and planning.** Six groups of five inspect powder pinches with lenses. Children list tests/measurements, not unknown identifications. Rotate one-minute lens turns; everyone notes observations.
- **4 min. Model.** Each child starts a property table; teacher posts unanswered questions.
- **4 min. Notebook evidence.** E45 independently on paper.
- **12 min. Cleanup.** Stagger six groups through two sinks; washing, collection and goggles as field 5. Dry waiting children file papers.

Tool: none. Actual observations remain beside any labelled photographic fallback. E45 returns on 46.

- **5 min. Recording model.** Demonstrate source statement/location and labelled whole-unit readings, not an E-style choice/test.

## Day 47


- **5 min. Return and repair.** E46 A; single-property lookalikes, not material choice plus test design.
- **14 min. Measuring investigation.** Six parallel groups compare four equal powder portions with fixed water, stirring and interval. One shared scale: six one-minute group visits within this block; teacher weighs demonstration portions while all record. Children personally measure water with 1 mL marks and dimensions where relevant to 1 cm; a spoon is not a ruler target. Use complete records; obtain actual outcomes, no promised dissolution.

- **4 min. Notebook evidence.** E47 independently on paper.
- **12 min. Cleanup.** Thirty staggered washes at two sinks, cups collected and goggles cleaned.

Tool: none. Children retain table; E47 A/B goes to 48's ten-minute Return and repair.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: property, stiff, dissolve, reflectivity, hardness, conduction. Frame: "I recorded ___ using ___." Read all directions aloud once; independently read at most 220 words daily, 350 total printable words maximum. Use tactile sealed samples, enlarged marks, stabilized trays or a scribe; preserve individual decisions. Dictated exits get the same key. Extension: choose a second safe distinguishing property, without an exit-shaped combined response.

## 10. Two rooms

One child: adult portions samples, reads neutrally and records the child's own measurements/actions. Use records if protection/washing is unavailable. One projector: display demonstration readings; every child has their own table and reserved paper. No home kitchen identification task.
