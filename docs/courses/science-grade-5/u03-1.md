**Share:** 5-PS1-3, "Observe and measure": plan and conduct powder observations with labelled quantities. Planning investigations joins PS1.A with scale, proportion and quantity; patterns support distinguishing lookalikes. No density, particle names or mass/weight distinction.

Adult explanation: appearance supports a candidate, not certainty. A fair comparison fixes portions, water, stirring and observation interval. Dissolving depends on conditions; an unresolved result stays unresolved. Introduce hardness, reflectivity, magnetism and electrical/heat conduction through separate demonstrations on 46, before application days. A mineral here is a solid specimen, unlike unit 2's nutritional use.

One formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. More than ten misses triggers the named next-day repair. An unsupported answer gets a follow-up before assigning a letter.

- **E45.** Invented records: "Reference A is white and dissolves fully. Reference B is white and leaves grains. Unknown C is white and dissolves fully. Powder amounts were not recorded. Water amounts, stirring and observation times matched." Ask: "Which identification claim has support? What measurement must the next comparison include?"
  Key: tentative A match; measure starting powder weights in grams and use matched amounts before confirming. Wrong: "White proves A" (A); "Record starting weight without a unit" (B).
  Shape: interpret an incomplete reference record and supply its missing measurement. Avoid reference-to-unknown reasoning and missing-measurement cards in practice; observe real powders and list possible tests instead.
- **E46.** Invented records: "Sheets L and M look equally shiny. Under equal loads, L bends and M stays stiff. A shield must stay stiff." Ask: "Choose the sheet for this shield. Propose one recorded property test against its lookalike."
  Key: M from the bending record; accept a safe fair further test, such as matched scratch observations, with labelled results/source location. Multiple valid test proposals. Wrong: "L, because shine proves stiffness" (A).
  Shape: choose for a purpose AND propose a further recorded test. Repair practice sorts or chooses from supplied tests separately; never joins choice with designing another test.
- **E47.** Invented records: "Powder T leaves grains in 10 mL water. Its comparison clears after equal stirring and waiting. Starting powder weights are missing." Ask: "Record the dissolving difference. Name a further measurement needed before identifying T."
  Key: T leaves solid while comparison clears; obtain starting powder weights in grams under matched water, stirring and interval. No unique identity follows. Wrong: "Weigh starting portions but omit grams" (B); "White means salt" (A).
  Shape: contrast dissolution AND identify missing measurement. Practice records complete quantities and single observations; never repairs incomplete paired dissolution records.
