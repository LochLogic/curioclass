- **E52.** Invented for practice: A colored solid spreads through water. After drying, the solid is recovered. These records do not identify it. Model what remained when the water looked clear. Propose a controlled test of a new-substance claim.
  Key: show material remaining as unseen parts, not vanishing; propose equal portions under matched water, stirring, temperature and interval, with a comparison testing a resulting property against starting material. Accept any safe fair discriminating test, paper plan only. Recovery alone is insufficient proof either way.
  Wrong: "Nothing remained while clear" (F); "Change amounts and temperature together to isolate mixing" (I)
  Shape: model recovered material AND propose a test. Practice uses separate components, never this held case or full response.

- **E53.** Invented for practice: A plan changes powder amount and liquid type, then runs once. New shadow records use different sticks and observation times. Revise the mixing plan for fair repeated evidence. Explain how the shadow records could become comparable.
  Key: vary one chosen factor, fix other quantities/stirring/temperature/interval, plan three paired trials with starting/resulting properties; shadow comparison needs same stick, location, sun time and length unit. Multiple fair plans accepted.
  Wrong: "Keep both mixing changes to identify one cause" (I)
  Shape: repair a confounded mixing plan AND transfer fair-comparison rules to shadows. Practice uses separate components, never this held case or full response.

- **E54.** Invented for practice: Three unfamiliar mixtures keep bubbling after stirring stops. Matched single-material controls stop bubbling promptly. Starting and resulting observations were recorded. Explain what this supports and what remains unidentified.
  Key: consistent treatment/control contrast supports gas production from interacting materials; it does not identify the gas or every resulting substance. A sign supports investigation, not certain identity.
  Wrong: "Any pouring bubble proves a new substance" (G)
  Shape: infer interaction from persistent bubbling AND state an identification limit. Practice uses separate components, never this held case or full response.

- **E55.** Invented for practice: three coating-and-additive cups form clumps; water controls do not. Treatment cups were warm; controls were chilled. A separate dampness test changes both dampness and container size. Judge both comparisons, including repairs that isolate their intended causes.
  Key: Neither comparison isolates its intended cause. Match starting temperatures while varying only additive versus water. Match container sizes while varying only dampness. Retain matched quantities, observation times and repeats. Clumps alone do not establish new material.
  Wrong: "The additive alone caused clumps despite different starting temperatures." (I); "All mixing must create new material" (E)
  Shape: judge two flawed comparisons and repair their controls. Practice uses separate components, never this held case or full response.
