## 5. What is carried, and instruments


Teacher carries the control sheet, question board and system arrows. Groups keep versioned A/B proposals, serial T107 slips and plots; children keep individual records. Directly explain the planned grade-5 graph conventions. Day 110's V transfer supplies additional baseline/retest evidence for 111, without pretending it tested A/B's unrun improvements.

Ideal: six protected stations, each with a half-cup-step measuring cup, one-second timer and matched category 0 to 3 photographs. Physical apparatus partly fits: coarse volume marks and appearance cannot establish fine differences or drinking safety. Check the half-cup threshold at its mark before rounding for a plot. Between marks is an estimate; unclear readings get one safe reread, then unresolved with reason, never zero.

Teacher prepares covered edges, rinsed/portioned media, stirred clean classroom soil/water and stable spill trays. Ask allergies before soil/cloth handling; use nonlatex gloves and splash goggles. No tasting, environmental runoff or dust blowing. Stop for damaged edges, missing protection or uncontrolled spills. Seven cleanup minutes: two for sediment waste/surfaces, one for parallel goggle wiping/bagging, four for soap/water washing. Thirty 45-second turns need six existing washing places (3.75 minutes); fewer selects no-kit before starting. Name towels, waste containers, soap and goggle wipes in the day.

Fallback Archive V110, invented for practice, baseline as E110; revised volumes 0.5/0.5/0.5 cups, categories 2/2/3; no side bypass. Retain third failure: volume/leak limits pass; improvement is inconsistent. Children choose the adjustment before reveal. Never merge backup into actual readings. No-kit records cannot show conducting.

Digital tool: None; no key tool. The [filter-test specification](TOOL-ASKS.md#unit-6-filter-test-light-spec-only) is light, spec only, does not exist and partly fits analysis; it cannot replace observed handling. Keep the paper path.
