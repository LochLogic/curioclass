- **MS-LS1-1:** Investigate to gather evidence that all living things are built of cells, whether one cell or many cells of many kinds. Emphasis: evidence that life is made of cells, telling living from nonliving things, and that organisms may have one cell or many varied cells.
- **~MS-LS1-3:** (begun here; the full expectation is owned and assessed later) Argue from evidence that the body is made of subsystems that interact, each built from groups of cells. Emphasis: cells make tissues and tissues make organs suited to particular body jobs. Limit: Only six body systems (circulatory, excretory, digestive, respiratory, muscle and nerve), and not how one system works on its own apart from the others.
- **~MS-LS1-8:** (begun here; the full expectation is owned and assessed later) Collect and combine information showing that sense receptors react to stimuli by signaling the brain, leading to quick behavior or stored memories. Limit: Leave out how the signals are carried.
- **~MS-LS1-2:** (begun here; the full expectation is owned and assessed later) Build and use a model of how a whole cell works and how its parts help it work. Emphasis: the cell as one working system, and the main job of five named parts: nucleus, chloroplasts, mitochondria, the membrane and the wall. Limit: Structure and function links only for the cell wall and membrane; other parts only in relation to the whole cell; no biochemistry of cells or their parts.
