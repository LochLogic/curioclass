<!-- skel -->
- **MS-ESS2-1:** Build a model of how Earth materials cycle and what energy drives that cycling. Emphasis: melting, crystallizing, weathering, deforming and settling of sediment working together to form rocks and minerals. Limit: Leave out identifying and naming minerals.
- **MS-ESS2-3:** Use data on rocks, fossils, continent shapes and sea floor features as evidence that plates moved in the past. Limit: Magnetic striping (paleomagnetic patterns) in the crust is left out.
- **MS-ESS2-2:** Explain from evidence how geoscience processes have reshaped Earth's surface over small and large spans of time and space. Emphasis: changes both slow and huge and fast and tiny, gradual processes broken by sudden catastrophes, and local landforms where possible.
- **MS-ESS1-4:** Explain from rock layer evidence how the geologic time scale organizes the 4.6 billion year history of Earth. Emphasis: rock formations and their fossils give the relative ages of major events, from very recent ones to the oldest.
