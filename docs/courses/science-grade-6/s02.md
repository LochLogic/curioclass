## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Cold is a substance that flows between objects. | Treats a temperature condition as transported matter. Repair: trace energy from warmer to cooler matter. | Base storyline, lessons 7 and 14. |
| B | Energy disappears when a sample cools. | Omits the receiving system. Repair: pair the cooling and warming records in one energy account. | MS-PS3-5 core idea on file. |
| C | A lid blocks all energy transfer. | Confuses a closed matter boundary with insulation. Repair: compare a closed cup's changing temperature. | Base storyline, lesson 7. |
| D | Temperature alone determines a sample's total thermal energy. | Omits amount, kind and state. Repair: compare equal-temperature samples with different amounts. | MS-PS3-4 core idea on file. |
| E | Particles must cross an intact wall for conduction to occur. | Confuses energy transfer with matter transfer. Repair: model neighboring interactions across a fixed boundary. | Base storyline, lesson 13. |
| F | Equal exposure times guarantee equal energy transfers. | Confuses time control with an energy measurement. Repair: contrast matched time with a separately controlled input. | MS-PS3-4 core idea on file. |
| G | Outside droplets prove that liquid leaked through the cup. | Ignores the surrounding air as a matter source. Repair: compare a sealed empty chilled cup. | Base storyline, lesson 5. |
