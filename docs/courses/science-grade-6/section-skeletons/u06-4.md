## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **~MS-LS1-2** (days 153, 155), begun here and owned later: Build and use a model of how a whole cell works and how its parts help it work.
- **~MS-LS1-3** (days 154, 155), begun here and owned later: Argue from evidence that the body is made of subsystems that interact, each built from groups of cells.
- **~MS-LS1-8** (days 154, 155), begun here and owned later: Collect and combine information showing that sense receptors react to stimuli by signaling the brain, leading to quick behavior or stored memories.
- **MS-LS1-1** (days 155): Investigate to gather evidence that all living things are built of cells, whether one cell or many cells of many kinds.
- skill s6-cells-body-systems (grade 6)
