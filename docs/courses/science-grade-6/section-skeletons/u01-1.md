## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **~MS-PS4-2** (days 1, 2, 3, 4), begun here and owned later: Build and use a model showing that materials reflect, absorb or let waves pass through them.
- skill s6-light-paths (grade 6)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 1 hook: peers describe opposite views through a teacher-prepared mirror-film box. Record both accounts without explaining the difference. Read sensor observations aloud and offer tactile box diagrams.
>
> - **Earlier-grade rows in the days.csv skills column (retrieval or readiness, not owned; field 4):** s4-senses-brain (grade 4).
>
> Question board: group notices into viewing position, illumination, material and observer. Days 1 to 4 ask what both accounts require; days 5 to 7 test illumination; days 8 to 10 ask where light goes; day 11 connects material structure to function; day 12 raises the weak-image puzzle; days 13 to 14 connect receptors and boundary bending; days 15 to 16 test the explanation elsewhere. Attach evidence to answered questions and retain unresolved ones. Reserve the viewing-tube display in field 5 for individual transfer work.
>
> Carried: both models start day 1 and remain available. The light-ray model gains illumination evidence on day 7, material interactions on day 10, a two-direction consensus on day 12, receptor inputs on day 13 and bending on day 14. Day 15 consolidates these into the final model used on days 16 to 17. The cause-effect model changes from competing causes on day 4 to controlled-light evidence on day 7 and material evidence on day 10; day 15 links relative inputs to visibility.
