## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-LS1-1** (days 147, 148, 151, 152): Investigate to gather evidence that all living things are built of cells, whether one cell or many cells of many kinds.
- **~MS-LS1-3** (days 149, 150), begun here and owned later: Argue from evidence that the body is made of subsystems that interact, each built from groups of cells.
- skill s6-cells-body-systems (grade 6)

> Question board: What stopped working (139)? What else connects the parts (140)? What are tissues made of (143 and 146)? Why do nerves branch (145)? Are all samples cellular (147)? Where do replacement cells come from (150), and what do they need (152)? How do materials cross cell boundaries (153)? How do these parts interact (154)? Whose access needs shape environments (157 and 158)? Transfer uses unfamiliar rabbit-ear growth and a pond-cell boundary problem.

Joint evidence revises interactions on 139; microscopy revises nested cellular structures on 143 and 146; nerve evidence revises communication on 145. Growth records revise cell replacement on 150 and resource supply on 152. Boundary observations revise intake on 153. Consolidate both models on 154, use them on 155, and revise access assumptions on 158. Retain unit 5's cause-effect and evidence displays; retrieve access reasoning on 158.
