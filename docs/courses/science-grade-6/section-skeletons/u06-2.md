## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-LS1-1** (days 141, 142, 143, 146): Investigate to gather evidence that all living things are built of cells, whether one cell or many cells of many kinds.
- **~MS-LS1-3** (days 143, 145), begun here and owned later: Argue from evidence that the body is made of subsystems that interact, each built from groups of cells.
- **~MS-LS1-8** (days 144, 145), begun here and owned later: Collect and combine information showing that sense receptors react to stimuli by signaling the brain, leading to quick behavior or stored memories.
- skill s6-cells-body-systems (grade 6)
