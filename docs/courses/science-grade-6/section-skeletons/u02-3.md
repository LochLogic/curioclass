## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-PS3-3** (days 29, 31): Use science ideas to design, build and try out a device that slows the movement of thermal energy or one that speeds it up.
- **MS-PS3-4** (days 31, 32, 33, 34): Plan an investigation into how energy transferred, kind of matter, mass and temperature change of a sample relate to one another.
- **~MS-PS4-2** (days 32, 33), begun here and owned later: Build and use a model showing that materials reflect, absorb or let waves pass through them.
- **MS-PS3-5** (days 34): Build, use and present arguments that whenever an object's kinetic energy changes, energy has moved into or out of it.
- skill s6-insulator-design (grade 6)
- skill s6-particle-temperature (grade 6)
- skill s6-light-paths (grade 6)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

Days 18 to 22 compare features; days 23 to 30 ask where matter goes; days 31 to 34 ask why closed cups change; days 35 to 44 explain change through particles; days 45 to 49 test improvements. Attach evidence day 50; reserve E1 and E2's containers for transfer.
> Limits: ~MS-PS4-2 samples reflected, transmitted and absorbed light on days 32 to 33; wave mechanisms wait. ~MS-PS1-4 samples motion and temperature on day 37, with days 38 and 39 on MS-PS3-4; state-change proficiency waits for unit 3. Only slowing is built; speeding up is reasoned and checked in E1 and E2. ~MS-ETS1-4 samples predictions before redesign on days 47 to 49; optimization remains later-owned. Day 45 compares trapped air with moving-fluid transfer. No energy calculations or formal statistics. Day 38 compares particle-speed distributions; day 39 practises average versus total energy with feedback.
> Carried: system and design models begin day 18; feature, matter and trial evidence revise them on days 24, 27 and 49. Graphs begin day 19 and gain paired records day 24. Rays gain warming evidence day 33. Particle and energy models begin day 18; closed-cup, dye, speed and collision evidence revise them on days 31, 36, 39 and 41. Arguments develop on days 34 and 43. Day 50 consolidates these models for transfer.
