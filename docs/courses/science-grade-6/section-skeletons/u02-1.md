## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-PS3-4** (days 18, 19): Plan an investigation into how energy transferred, kind of matter, mass and temperature change of a sample relate to one another.
- **MS-PS3-3** (days 18, 20, 21, 22): Use science ideas to design, build and try out a device that slows the movement of thermal energy or one that speeds it up.
- skill s6-particle-temperature (grade 6)
- skill s6-insulator-design (grade 6)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 18 hook: compare equal amounts of equally cold water in ordinary and nested, covered cups. Record the difference without explaining it.
>
> Group question-board notices into features, matter and energy. Days 18 to 22 compare features; days 23 to 30 ask where matter goes; days 31 to 34 ask why closed cups change; days 35 to 44 explain change through particles; days 45 to 49 test improvements. Attach evidence day 50; reserve E1 and E2's containers for transfer.
> Carried: system and design models begin day 18; feature, matter and trial evidence revise them on days 24, 27 and 49. Graphs begin day 19 and gain paired records day 24. Rays gain warming evidence day 33. Particle and energy models begin day 18; closed-cup, dye, speed and collision evidence revise them on days 31, 36, 39 and 41. Arguments develop on days 34 and 43. Day 50 consolidates these models for transfer.
