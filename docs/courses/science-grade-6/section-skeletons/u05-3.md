## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-ESS3-2** (days 124, 125, 126, 127, 128, 129): Study data about natural hazards to forecast future disasters and guide technologies that lessen their harm.
- **~MS-ETS1-1** (days 124), begun here and owned later: State a design problem's criteria and constraints clearly enough to lead to a good solution, considering science ideas and effects on people and nature.
- **~MS-ETS1-2** (days 125, 126), begun here and owned later: Judge competing design solutions step by step against the problem's criteria and constraints.
- **MS-PS4-3** (days 127, 128, 129): Pull together descriptive science and technical information to argue that digital signals carry and store information more reliably than analog ones.
- skill s6-hazard-forecast (grade 6)
- skill s6-digital-signals (grade 6)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Model histogram reading on 119 and evidence integration on 129. Step back for trials on 125 and guided arguments on 131.
>
> Group the question board: Where might this happen (119)? How does displacement reach shore (122)? What can we forecast (123)? What reduces harm when barriers fail (126)? Who receives warnings (129)? Which messages survive interference (131)? How do subsystems depend on each other (132)? Each answer raises the next question. Transfer uses withheld storm and river-valley cases.
> Exclude earthquake dates predicted in advance, wave-energy equations, binary arithmetic and device internals. Exact verbs: "Analyze and interpret"; "Integrate"; "Define"; "Evaluate". The engineering starts define measurable criteria and environmental constraints on 124, test two options on 125, improve on 126 and compare systematically on 134. Full ownership remains later.
> Carried: initialize cause-effect, data-graph, design-cycle, plate-model and tentative wave-model on 116. Wave observations revise height and spacing on 117; mapped events revise plate causes and graphs on 118 to 119. Displacement evidence revises wave causes on 122. Barrier trials revise design-cycle on 126; message-copying evidence revises wave-model on 131. On 132, consolidate all five into a consensus hazard system, retaining them for transfer.
