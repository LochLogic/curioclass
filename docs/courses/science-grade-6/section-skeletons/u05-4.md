<!-- skel -->
- **MS-PS4-3** (days 130, 131, 132): Pull together descriptive science and technical information to argue that digital signals carry and store information more reliably than analog ones.
- **MS-ESS3-2** (days 132): Study data about natural hazards to forecast future disasters and guide technologies that lessen their harm.
- **~MS-ETS1-1** (days 132), begun here and owned later: State a design problem's criteria and constraints clearly enough to lead to a good solution, considering science ideas and effects on people and nature.
