## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-ESS2-1** (days 108, 112): Build a model of how Earth materials cycle and what energy drives that cycling.
- **MS-ESS2-3** (days 108, 109, 110, 112): Use data on rocks, fossils, continent shapes and sea floor features as evidence that plates moved in the past.
- **MS-ESS2-2** (days 108, 112): Explain from evidence how geoscience processes have reshaped Earth's surface over small and large spans of time and space.
- **MS-ESS1-4** (days 109, 110, 111, 112): Explain from rock layer evidence how the geologic time scale organizes the 4.6 billion year history of Earth.
- skill s6-rock-cycle (grade 6)
- skill s6-plate-evidence (grade 6)
- skill s6-surface-change-scales (grade 6)
- skill s6-geologic-time (grade 6)
