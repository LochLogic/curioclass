## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-ESS2-6** (days 87, 88, 89): Build and use a model of how uneven heating and Earth's spin set up air and ocean circulation patterns that shape regional climates.
- **MS-ESS2-4** (days 87, 88, 89): Build a model of water cycling through Earth's systems, powered by sunlight and gravity.
- **MS-PS1-4** (days 89): Build a model that predicts and explains how a pure substance's particle motion, temperature and state change as thermal energy is added or taken away.
- **MS-ESS2-5** (days 89): Collect data as evidence of how moving and colliding air masses change the weather.
- skill s6-climate-circulation (grade 6)
- skill s6-water-cycle-energy (grade 6)
