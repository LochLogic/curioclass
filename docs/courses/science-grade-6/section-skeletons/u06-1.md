## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-LS1-1** (days 136): Investigate to gather evidence that all living things are built of cells, whether one cell or many cells of many kinds.
- **~MS-LS1-3** (days 136, 137, 138, 139, 140), begun here and owned later: Argue from evidence that the body is made of subsystems that interact, each built from groups of cells.
- **~MS-LS1-8** (days 140), begun here and owned later: Collect and combine information showing that sense receptors react to stimuli by signaling the brain, leading to quick behavior or stored memories.
- skill s6-cells-body-systems (grade 6)
- skill s4-structures-function (grade 4, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill s4-senses-brain (grade 4, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 136 opens with an original recovery timeline and non-graphic diagrams, invented for practice: two broken foot bones, interrupted movement and later walking. Students ask what repaired the gap and draw an explanation.
> The teacher models focusing on 141 and comparing text with images on 146, then steps back on 147; on 138 students choose the observation separating two explanations.
>
> Question board: What stopped working (139)? What else connects the parts (140)? What are tissues made of (143 and 146)? Why do nerves branch (145)? Are all samples cellular (147)? Where do replacement cells come from (150), and what do they need (152)? How do materials cross cell boundaries (153)? How do these parts interact (154)? Whose access needs shape environments (157 and 158)? Transfer uses unfamiliar rabbit-ear growth and a pond-cell boundary problem.
> Carried: initialize system-model and structure-function on 136. Joint evidence revises interactions on 139; microscopy revises nested cellular structures on 143 and 146; nerve evidence revises communication on 145. Growth records revise cell replacement on 150 and resource supply on 152. Boundary observations revise intake on 153. Consolidate both models on 154, use them on 155, and revise access assumptions on 158. Retain unit 5's cause-effect and evidence displays; retrieve access reasoning on 158.
