## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-PS3-3** (days 45, 46, 47, 48, 49): Use science ideas to design, build and try out a device that slows the movement of thermal energy or one that speeds it up.
- **MS-PS3-4** (days 45): Plan an investigation into how energy transferred, kind of matter, mass and temperature change of a sample relate to one another.
- **~MS-ETS1-4** (days 46, 47, 49), begun here and owned later: Build a model that produces data for repeated testing and changing of an object, tool or process until the design is as good as it can be.
- skill s6-insulator-design (grade 6)
- skill s6-particle-temperature (grade 6)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Group question-board notices into features, matter and energy. Days 18 to 22 compare features; days 23 to 30 ask where matter goes; days 31 to 34 ask why closed cups change; days 35 to 44 explain change through particles; days 45 to 49 test improvements. Attach evidence day 50; reserve E1 and E2's containers for transfer.
> Limits: ~MS-PS4-2 samples reflected, transmitted and absorbed light on days 32 to 33; wave mechanisms wait. ~MS-PS1-4 samples motion and temperature on day 37, with days 38 and 39 on MS-PS3-4; state-change proficiency waits for unit 3. Only slowing is built; speeding up is reasoned and checked in E1 and E2. ~MS-ETS1-4 samples predictions before redesign on days 47 to 49; optimization remains later-owned. Day 45 compares trapped air with moving-fluid transfer. No energy calculations or formal statistics. Day 38 compares particle-speed distributions; day 39 practises average versus total energy with feedback.
