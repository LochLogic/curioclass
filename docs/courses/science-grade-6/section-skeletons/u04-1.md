## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MS-ESS2-1** (days 92): Build a model of how Earth materials cycle and what energy drives that cycling.
- **MS-ESS2-3** (days 92, 94, 95, 96): Use data on rocks, fossils, continent shapes and sea floor features as evidence that plates moved in the past.
- **MS-ESS2-2** (days 92, 94, 96): Explain from evidence how geoscience processes have reshaped Earth's surface over small and large spans of time and space.
- **MS-ESS1-4** (days 92): Explain from rock layer evidence how the geologic time scale organizes the 4.6 billion year history of Earth.
- skill s6-rock-cycle (grade 6)
- skill s6-plate-evidence (grade 6)
- skill s6-surface-change-scales (grade 6)
- skill s6-geologic-time (grade 6)
- skill s4-rock-layers (grade 4, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)

Timeline evidence revises scale on 111; erosion revises rock and stability on 113. Consolidate every model on 114 and use them in transfer; retain, never withdraw.
