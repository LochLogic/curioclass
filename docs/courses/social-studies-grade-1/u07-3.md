- **MA.1.T1.10, this section's share (days 75, 76).** Children "evaluate the qualities of a good citizen or community member using examples from history, texts, news, and personal experience". Day 75 takes texts (an invented story), news (a real dated school bulletin) and personal experience (the class's own action log); day 76 takes history (a library founder) and collects the supported-work judgments. 7.4 finishes the standard.
- **D2.Civ.14.K-2, this section's share (day 76).** Children "describe how people have worked to make their communities better over time": a long ago and a now, and what stays the same.
- **D4.6.K-2, this section's share (day 77).** Children "name and explain problems near and far, and some ways people are working on them": the class's problem placed near, in the region and far away, with people already at work at each.
- **Limits that bite here.** Citizenship is a legal tie, not a reward for helping; a good member may or may not be a citizen, and a quality is judged by what a person does, never by money, fame or status. Judge deeds and the class's action, never a classmate; no child is rated. The class's action is not said to have fixed or caused everything. Other places are named by their own names, in the present tense where people live there now, with their own adults and groups as the actors; no place is shown as helpless, and no child is asked to act abroad. No invented news report or country fact: invented cases say "invented for practice", and real ones come from a named, dated page.
- **Connections.** Unit 6's evidence (what a source shows) and the claim frame, as review on day 75. Kindergarten's then-and-now chart and unit 2's then and now return on day 76 as review. Kindergarten's near and far, and the unit 5 map, return on day 77. Qualities (caring, fair, helpful, respectful, responsible) are review from kindergarten; honest and cooperate are new on day 75.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to judge a quality from a detail ("what did the person do?") before liking it. The difference between a plan or invitation and a dated record of what happened (error J, taught on day 77). Why the real sources must be checked and printed before the days: the school bulletin (day 75), the founder text and a school helper account (day 76), and the packets for the class's problem (day 77). The Pratt facts rest on the page named in unit field 10 and are source to check until the page is re-read; if it cannot be checked, the day uses the class's helper account alone.

- **Key moment 3, day 77: three scales.** Must be seen: each card is placed by where its problem is, and the actors at each are named. Ideal instrument: a three-part floor strip. Spec: paper fits.

## 6. Days

## Day 75

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Application. Applies taught work to a new task or case (an inquiry, a lab, a draft, a revision or edit); nothing is taught as new.
- goal: What makes a good citizen or community member, from history, texts, news and our own lives; is what we did what a good community member does?
- note: Read beside the class's own action, which is still running. First-day quality judgments are practice only, never scored
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **3 min. Action routine.** The action keeps running: one line in the action log, with the date (hand: a child draws it).
- **4 min. Warm-up and repair.** If many made error H on day 74, sort safe and unsafe step cards here (4 minutes).
- **8 min. Teaching first: a quality needs a detail.** An invented story, one actor a scene: Mo carries a younger child's bag; later Mo tells the truth about a spilled paint cup. Teach quality, honest and cooperate with pictures (caring, fair, helpful, respectful are review). Model matching: "I think Mo is caring because Mo carries the bag" against two detail cards that do not fit. A good member is judged by what a person does, not by what a person has.
- **6 min. News.** The teacher reads an invented, dated record about pupils helping (no real bulletin was found and checked), different from the exit record. Fifteen pairs match a quality card to a detail card (about 50 seconds a pair), and the teacher hears a rotating six pairs.
- **4 min. Our own action.** Point to one log line and say what it shows. "Is what we did what a good community member does?" The class judges the action, not a child. Practice, not scored.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E75: two marks (2 minutes); the rotating six heard (3 minutes) while the others draw the quality they chose.

Who holds it: the bulletin and the story hold the evidence; the teacher holds the cards. Tool: None. By hand: quality and detail cards, printed bulletin. Evidence: E75 (item 2, practice).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: quality, honest, cooperate, long ago, region, far. Frames: "I think ___ is ___ because ___." "Long ago ___; now ___; the same is ___." "At ___, people ___."
- **Access.** Cards are large and movable; an adult marks the strip as a child points; the sources are read aloud twice; dictation.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a token, drawing, dictation, a familiar-language helper; a private place for the spoken part.
- **Reading load.** None beyond a word label; every source is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Judge a quality of someone in a source and name a different quality with its detail.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read one short true story about a helper, ask what the person did and what quality it shows, then ask what changed and what stayed. A grown-up helps with the dated source and its address.
- **One projector.** The strip and cards show on screen; the child points to a place and says who is helping there.
