## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.1.T1.8** (days 38, 40, 41): Explain that people in a town, city, or nation are citizens whose rights and duties include electing leaders for set terms and judging whether to re-elect them.
- **MA.1.T1.7** (days 38, 40): Name some leaders chosen by elections, such as the President, the Governor of Massachusetts, or a team captain, and explain what they do.
- **D4.1.K-2** (days 38, 39): Make an argument and give reasons for it.
- **MA.1.T1.5** (days 39, 40): Give examples of why groups with differing views need ways to decide, and explain how voting can make fair decisions or choose leaders.
- **D2.Civ.8.K-2** (days 39, 40, 41): Describe democratic ideas such as equality, fairness and respect for fair authority and rules.
- **MA.1.T1.6** (days 40): Explain that an election is a kind of voting in which people choose their leaders.
- **D2.Civ.5.K-2** (days 40): Explain what a government is and some things it does.
- **D2.Eco.12.K-2** (days 40): Give examples of goods and services that governments provide.

- **MA.1.T1.8, this section's share (days 38, 40, 41).** Children "explain" that citizens' duties include electing leaders for set terms and judging whether to re-elect them: day 38 judges a made-up leader's term from the record; day 40 shows it unaided (item 4); day 41 responds.
- **MA.1.T1.7 (days 38, 40).** Children "name" an elected leader and "explain what they do", here a captain's job on a term record and two real offices on day 40 (item 3).
- **D4.1.K-2 (days 38, 39).** Children "make an argument and give reasons for it", with a claim, a reason and a closing. This is review of unit 3's frame, not new ownership.
- **MA.1.T1.5 and D2.Civ.8.K-2 (days 39 to 41).** Day 39 answers the unit question for a new situation; day 40 explains how voting can make fair decisions and describes equality, fairness and accepting a fair result.
- **MA.1.T1.6, D2.Civ.5.K-2, D2.Eco.12.K-2 (day 40).** Election versus a vote on a matter; what a government does; a good and a service.
- **Limits that bite here.** Every case is invented, never a classmate, a real candidate or a party, and is said aloud to be made up. A child's day 38 judgment may go either way when a deed supports it. No one is asked how they voted. The record shows deeds and what the record explains; "everybody says so" and "my friend" are not evidence. The teacher never announces a child's choice.
- **Connections.** Unit 3's claim, reason and closing, and its sourcing questions (who made it, when, how it helps), days 38 and 39. Unit 1's class garden team, which day 42 uses.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to read a term record (a deed on the record, a reason the record gives for a slip). That two judgments can both be sound. That a closing says why the reason matters (F, H). How the day 40 spoken groups run, five groups of six, and why a part neither marked nor heard is "not yet seen". How day 41 repairs by letter.

## 3. Checks, written first

<!-- skel -->
- Day 40: assessment "observe" (Check day).
<!-- /skel -->

**Unit assessment items owned:** 1, 2, 3 and 4 (on the unit plan, field 5; day 40, response day 41). Items 1 to 4 are today's evidence; nothing is added here, and their withheld cases are not taught or rehearsed.

**Day 38 judgment** (feedback written after class, revised on day 39, practice). Look-fors, each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen: a deed named from the record; a reason that links the deed to the judgment; a closing that says why it matters.

**Exit questions.** Each has a mark every child makes and a spoken why heard from a rotating six. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E38.** Case: an invented equipment captain's term is ending; the record says the captain repaired the bags and missed some meetings. Mark: circle the reason card that says what the captain did, from three cards: "The captain repaired the bags" (key); "The captain is my friend" (F); "Everybody says the captain is great" (F). Spoken: "Would you choose this captain again? Tell one thing the captain did." Answer: either judgment, with a deed from the record (the bags or the missed meetings). Wrong: "Yes, because the captain is my friend" (F); "Yes, because everybody says so" (F).
- **E39.** Case: a hiking club chose its route leader; its rule card and tally are shown as pictures. Mark: look at the card and tally and circle how this club chose, from three cards: each member made one private ballot, counted the same (key); the loudest member decided (Q); the leader's friends voted twice (I). Spoken: "Why did this club vote and not talk until all agreed?" Answer: two members wanted the job and they were still apart after talking, so equal votes decide. Wrong: "Voting is always better than talking" (H); "Talking never works" (R).
- **E41.** Case: an invented reading club's count looks wrong. Mark: circle what to do, from two cards: tell the counters so they check (key); keep quiet (J). Spoken: "The club leader's term is ending after sharing turns fairly. Would you re-elect? Say one reason." Answer: either judgment, citing the shared turns. Wrong: "Keep the leader, because the leader is nice" (O).
- **Day 40.** Items 1 to 4 supply the evidence; gaps are recorded by part.

## 6. Days

## Day 38

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Application. Applies taught work to a new task or case (an inquiry, a lab, a draft, a revision or edit); nothing is taught as new.
- goal: Should we re-elect? Judge a made-up class leader's term from what they did, with a written claim and reason
- note: Return written feedback on the term deed, linked reason and closing; revise during five minutes of day 39 supported work before scoring
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** If many missed O on E37, use its field 4 repair. Read the unit question and the blank claim frame: "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___."
- **6 min. Model.** Source questions first for a plant monitor's term record: who made it (the group's recorder), when (the end of the term), how it helps (it lists what he did). Then a say-so card, "everybody likes him", beside the record. The teacher models re-electing Ivo, who watered every week, with the frame (F).
- **10 min. Judge.** The class's invented library captain, Rosa: the record shows returned books shelved every week, one request for the group's ideas, and a reading-corner sign that was never painted, with no reason on the record. The teacher reads the record aloud twice. Each child chooses re-elect or not, points to a deed card, draws it and fills the frame by pointing. The teacher takes dictation from the rotating six (30 seconds each, 3 minutes).
- **4 min. Partners.** Two judgments can both be sound: partners tell their reasons.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E38: mark for everyone (2 minutes); the rotating six say why (30 seconds each, 3 minutes) while the rest draw one way they weighed a deed.

After class the teacher writes feedback on the term deed, linked reason and closing, about one minute a child (30 minutes of teacher time, outside the day). Who holds it: the child's own judgment. Tool: None. By hand: record, deed cards, frame. Evidence: E38; the written judgment.

## Day 39

- goal: Answer the compelling question "What is the fairest way for a group to decide?" with a written claim, reason and closing: choose a fair way (talk to agreement, a vote, an election) for a given class situation, with the reason from the cases and the ballot
- note: Use five supported-work minutes to revise day 38 judgments before scoring. The main closing claim is practice; the exit uses the fresh invented hiking-club rule and tally card
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **3 min. Warm-up and repair.** If many missed F on E38, use its field 4 repair.
- **5 min. Return and revise.** Written feedback returned; each child revises the day 38 judgment once, adding a missing deed or closing. Teacher notes revised work; it fills a part only if day 40 does not show it.
- **4 min. Two records.** Reread Our Decision Record: day 32's reading corner and storybook, and day 34's garden election. Cover completed models; leave blank frames.
- **10 min. Answer the unit question.** Three invented situation cards, one for each of three tables: four children want different buildings and the blocks fit both; one song must be chosen for a visitor's day; a club needs a leader to hand out supplies all season. Each child picks talk, a vote or an election, and completes "The fairest way is ___ because ___. That is why ___." by drawing, pointing or dictating. The teacher hears the rotating six (3 minutes inside).
- **3 min. Share.** Another method may be fair when the reason fits.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E39, as day 38.

Who holds it: the child holds the claim. Tool: None. By hand: situation cards, frame, records. Evidence: E39; the claim is practice.

The spoken parts run alongside, five groups of six, 3 minutes a group (15 minutes), while the others work on paper: group 1 item 1, group 2 item 2, group 3 item 3, group 4 item 4, group 5 item 2. ## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: re-elect, term, deed, record, reason, closing. Frame: "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___." Familiar-language speech is accepted.
- **Access.** Large-print records; an adult can mark a ballot or write as the child directs, privately; pointing, drawing and dictation count; a child may pass.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all sources and prompts aloud; accept pointing, drawing, dictation, familiar-language speech and supported communication; never supply a choice, explanation or reason; judge content apart from reading and handwriting.
- **Reading load.** None; label words at most.
- **Extension, same standard.** Defend two different judgments of the same term record.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read a made-up term record, mark a ballot privately in a cup, say a claim, reason and closing. On the last day of the unit, show the child's record and re-teach one missed part, then a fresh case; no one is asked about family voting or citizenship.
- **One projector.** Show the same prints; no student timer.
