## 1. Standards and practices


- **D4.2.K-2, this section's share (days 27, 30, 31).** "Explain something using the right order and information that fits": all three parts. Day 27 teaches the order words first, then, so and the fact that matters; day 30 item 3 checks them on an unseen case; day 31 retries.
- **D4.1.K-2, this section's share (days 28 to 30).** "Make an argument and give reasons for it": review of kindergarten's spoken "I think ... because ...", now written or dictated as a claim, a reason and a closing (the unit plan's frame: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___.").
- **D4.5.K-2, this section's share (days 28 to 30).** "Ask and answer questions about an explanation": a listener asks one question about a partner's explanation; the speaker answers the question asked.
- **MA.1.T1.4, this section's share (days 29 to 31).** "Analyze examples of leaders ... and describe the qualities of a good leader": the claim names a quality; the reason is an action from a record. Day 29 uses two real leaders from the chart; day 30 uses three unseen cases.
- **MA.1.T1.3, D2.Civ.1.K-2, D2.His.3.K-2 (day 30 only).** Their shares were taught in 3.1 and 3.2; day 30 checks them in items 1 and 4.
- **Limits that bite here.** Children write at most a word label; claims, reasons and closings are spoken or dictated, with a frame on the page of at most three short blanks. Day 30 uses an original summary of a government page about Jane Addams and two invented cases, none used in teaching; Addams and Hull House are not named before day 30. No child is asked to share about family or home. Heard evidence is counted in turns: see the day rows. The frame never supplies the child's content (P).
- **Connections.** ELA unit 4's opinion writing (W.1.1), later: shared writing here is its bridge. The unscaled timeline from 3.2 returns on day 27 (review). Kindergarten's claim frame (day 28, review).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The difference between a claim (what we say a leader is), a reason (an action that shows it) and a closing (why it matters, back to the claim). That a good answer can name courage, listening, organizing or another quality, if the action supports it. That the models are covered on day 29 and blank frames stay. How to hear pairs and write only what the child says.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** 2, 3 and 4 (day 30; on the unit plan). Item 1 is owned by section 3.1 and given on day 30. Day 30 is "observe": the evidence is items 1 to 4, marked by part with the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

**Exit questions.** Mark by every child; one spoken ask heard from the rotating six. Invented, never taught or reused. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's repair; day 29's goes to day 31 (day 30 teaches nothing).
- **E27.** Mark: an invented repair group notices a broken bench, plans the repair, then fixes it. Place the three pictures in order. Spoken: "Tell it in order: what did they do first, then, and so what happened?" Answer: noticed, planned, fixed, so the bench could be used. Wrong: fixing before noticing (C); adds an unrelated detail such as the color of a hat (J).
- **E28.** Mark: an invented choir leader asks quieter members for ideas before choosing a song. Circle the reason that supports "She listens": "She asks quieter members for ideas," or "She is a good leader." Spoken: "Give one reason that supports 'She listens.'" Answer: she asks quieter members for ideas. Wrong: "She is a good leader" repeated as the reason (H).
- **E29.** Mark (the other twenty-four): a new invented case; a school club leader draws names from a hat so every member gets one turn. Circle the action card that supports "The leader was fair": "gave each member a turn," or "is famous." Spoken (the six, s03-X29b, taken in the dictated piece): "Say a closing that goes back to The leader was fair." Answer: a closing in the child's own words, such as "So fair leaders make sure no one is left out." Wrong: a reason with no closing (I); the covered model's closing copied (P); fame (B).
- **E31.** Mark: in a new invented story a leader hears a member's worry, changes a walking route, then checks everyone can use it. Place the three pictures in order. Spoken: "Tell it in order with first, then, so." Answer: heard the worry, changed the route, checked it; so everyone could use it. Wrong: order wrong (C); an unrelated detail told as a step (J).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

## 6. Days

## Day 27


- **5 min. Reopen and repair.** Day 26's miss (N) with a new pair of jobs, five minutes at most.
- **2 min. Words with pictures.** first, then, so (review of kindergarten's "first, next, last"); a fact that matters (it helps explain what happened).
- **7 min. Model.** The Lost Coats, an invented picture story (to make): Ms. Dunn notices coats piling up, asks helpers to sort them, so owners find them. Model the explanation in order, with one fact that matters and one that does not.
- **5 min. Timeline.** Set the four leaders' cards from 3.2 in order on the strip and say which was before another.
- **7 min. Pairs, and hearing the six.** Each pair gets three action cards and one extra card; they cross out the extra one and say the order. The six are heard one minute each at the table with the exit card.
- **4 min. Exit.** E27 mark.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the story and strip; pairs hold the cards. Tool: None. By hand: The Lost Coats cards, strip cards, pair cards. Evidence: E27.

## Day 28


- **5 min. Reopen and repair.** Day 27's miss (C or J) with a new case.
- **2 min. Words with pictures.** claim (what we say a leader is), reason (an action that shows it), closing (why it matters). Rehearse "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___."
- **7 min. Model.** The teacher writes, with children pointing to the chart: "A good leader is brave. The source tells she led others to freedom. This matters because brave leaders help others reach safety" (Harriet Tubman; source to check against the day 22 summary). Think aloud: the claim, an action as the reason, the closing back to the claim.
- **7 min. Shared writing.** The class writes a second piece about Dolores Huerta together (organizing), the teacher holding the pencil, children choosing the action card from the chart.
- **5 min. Pairs ask and answer, and hearing the six.** Each child asks one question about the partner's shared-writing sentence ("What did she do?") and answers one, in a word from the question (D4.5). The six are heard one minute each, with the exit card.
- **4 min. Exit.** E28 mark.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the pencil; children hold the action cards. Tool: None. By hand: the three-box frame, chart cards, pair question cards. Evidence: E28. Practice, not scored.

## Day 29


- **5 min. Reopen and repair.** Day 28's miss (H or M) with a new case, five minutes at most. Cover the day 28 models; blank frames and the chart stay.
- **2 min. Frame.** Say the three parts aloud once with blank frames.
- **11 min. Write or dictate.** Each child chooses two leaders from the chart, one claim, one action from each as the reason, and a closing, by drawing the action and writing a word label. The six heard this day dictate their whole piece to the teacher, one and a half minutes each (nine minutes), while the others draw.
- **8 min. Pairs ask and answer.** Each child tells a partner's piece back in order, asks one question and answers the partner's (D4.5); the teacher moves among pairs.
- **4 min. Exit.** E29 mark; the six's spoken closing is taken during the dictating above.

Who holds it: children hold the frame and chart cards; the teacher holds the table of six. Tool: None. By hand: blank frames, chart cards, pair question cards. Evidence: E29. The pieces are practice, not scored; the teacher writes one line of feedback on each piece after class (about 30 minutes for 30 pieces) and returns them on day 31.

## Day 30


- **5 min. Source read-aloud and directions.** Read the Addams summary, the bus-monitor case and the puppet-club case aloud (about 150 words), each labeled a modern summary or "made up". Models and charts stay covered; blank frames and rereading stay available. Nothing taught is on the wall.
- **14 min. Paper parts, items 1 to 3.** Each child marks, draws or writes a word label for items 1 to 3. A child who needs dictation gives it to the teacher in turn at the table, three minutes each, at most four children; any further child is scribed by a helper or finishes on day 31's retry, marked shown with help.
- **11 min. Item 4, and the exchange.** Two minutes: every child draws a question about Addams or her group. Nine minutes: pairs of children exchange ordered explanations and questions, and the teacher hears one pair at a time at one and a half minutes, so six pairs (twelve children) are heard on day 30. The other pairs repeat the exchange, unheard, as practice.

Who holds it: children hold the paper and their drawn question; the teacher holds the record sheet. Tool: None. By hand: the three read-alouds, three item sheets, the record sheet with a "heard" column. Evidence: items 1 to 4, scored on day 30 only from unaided answers on unseen cases; day 28 and 29 pieces are not scored.

## Day 31

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 27, "What happened first, then, so?"; day 28, "What did she do? How do you know?" and "What does the reason show?"; day 29, "What question could you ask about your partner's piece?"

- Judged by the teacher with four look-fors: a claim that names a quality; a reason that is an action from the chart; a closing that returns to the claim; an ordered, relevant explanation. Practice only; day 30 is scored apart.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: claim, reason, closing, first, then, so, quality, evidence. Frames: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___." Familiar-language explanations are accepted before the English frame; content is judged apart from English.
- **Access.** Raised-line cards; all sources read aloud and again on request; pointing, a token, dictation, a scribe, a communication aid; an adult or communication partner may be the discussion partner on day 30. The teacher does not supply the child's question, reason or sequence.
- **Check accommodations.** Enlarged print, tactile sequence cards, a private hearing, extra read-throughs, neutral descriptions of any picture.
- **Reading load.** None; labels are at most one word.
- **Extension, same standard.** Support two different qualities from the same actions.
