- **D4.1.K-2, this section's share (day 78).** Children "make an argument and give reasons for it": a claim, a reason from the class's own records, and a closing, answering the compelling question. The day 78 local writing is practice; item 6 is scored only on the day 79 packet.
- **D4.7.K-2, MA.1.T1.10 (days 78, 79), D2.Civ.14.K-2, MA.1.T1.9, D4.6.K-2 (day 79; D4.6.K-2 and MA.1.T1.10 again on day 80).** Day 78 looks back at the class's action ("name ways to take action": what we did and what remains) and judges a quality from the log. Day 79 samples every part of every code with fresh cases (unit field 5), and day 80 teaches again what it showed.
- **Limits that bite here.** The class's action is not said to have caused every change; limited or no improvement is an acceptable conclusion if the dated records say so. Day 77 comparisons stay practice. Day 79 asks nothing about any child or family, and no person in the packet has a status stated or implied; the three made-up cases are the only citizenship cases. No child's name is rated. Never ask for home, family or status information; if a child volunteers it, do not question or record it.
- **Connections.** Kindergarten's before-and-after chart, "Before, ___. After, ___. It helped (or did not) because ___" and the four look-back questions, and unit 2's then and now, are review on day 78. Unit 6's blank claim frame and source routine (kind, maker, date, use) are review on days 78 and 79.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to compare the day 70 baseline with the day 78 observation taken the same way, and what a dated record can and cannot show (a single picture cannot show lasting change). Why feedback comes before a score. How day 79 runs in 30 minutes (below) and how the rotating spoken groups are recorded. How day 80 uses the written moves by item and how to teach a shared error first.

## 6. Days

## Day 78

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Application. Applies taught work to a new task or case (an inquiry, a lab, a draft, a revision or edit); nothing is taught as new.
- goal: Look back: what changed after we acted; answer the compelling question with a written claim, reason and closing
- note: Continuity-change as review. Six school days after the action (day 74). Revise the day 76 judgments within five writing minutes. Give the local claim written feedback on evidence, reason and closing, with five further writing minutes for revision before scoring
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **3 min. Observe and action routine.** Repeat the day 70 observation the same way (the same place, a drawing, the same count), dated, and add a log line.
- **3 min. Warm-up and repair.** If many made errors C, O, J on day 77, put the helper at each scale on the strip and relabel plan and record here (3 minutes).
- **5 min. Return and revise.** Each child gets the day 76 judgment sheet with the teacher's written note and changes or adds one thing (quality card or detail); the revised sheet is what counts for item 2.
- **3 min. Model a look back.** A different invented class (a water-bottle shelf) with two dated pictures: "Before, ___. After, ___. It helped, but ___ remains." Say that dated pictures show change, not that the action caused it.
- **5 min. Our look back.** Baseline beside today's observation, the staff account and the log; fill two columns, what changed and what remains. Review of kindergarten's four look-back questions. The class says our claim aloud once, then the completed example is covered.
- **5 min. Claim, reason, closing.** Each child writes or draws or dictates three boxes using the blank frame: "I think ___ because ___ so ___". The rotating six are heard (about 30 seconds each, 3 minutes) while others work.
- **2 min. Pair check and revise.** Partners tick claim, reason and closing on a picture checklist and add one thing. Practice, not scored; the teacher's written note goes back after day 80.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** E78: the mark (1 minute); the rotating six heard (3 minutes) while the others draw one thing that remains.

Who holds it: the dated records hold the evidence; the teacher holds the frame. Tool: None. By hand: drawing sheet, columns, frame, checklist. Evidence: E78.

## Day 79

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Check; assessment: observe. An assessment day: its items sample only what earlier days taught.
- goal: Observation task with a short drawn or dictated piece: from a short read-aloud not seen before about a community helper, say what the problem was, where else the same problem is found (a nearby town, another country), how the helper worked on it, and whether that is what a good community member does, with a reason; name two ways a person can be a citizen and one way someone who is not a citizen still belongs, using made-up people
- note: Never about a child or a family; the read-aloud's helper may be a newcomer. Day 77 comparisons are practice, not scored. Score the new packet and only earlier records with written feedback and revision. Response on day 80
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **5 min. Hear the packet.** The teacher reads Source A (a short history account) and Source B (an invitation), then three comparison cards, each with a pictured vocabulary card; the printed copies stay in view. No discussion. Parts of the packet are named in unit field 5.
- **3 min. The six items.** The teacher reads each paper item once aloud, without hints; the packet has a picture and one word label for each.
- **20 min. Paper parts and spoken groups.** Every child does the six paper parts at an own pace (about three minutes each, items 4 and 6 four; 20 minutes in all). Meanwhile five groups of six are called, one at a time, to the teacher for 3 minutes (30 seconds a child, one ask each; asks 1, 2, 4, 5 and 1 again across the groups, so each ask is heard from five or six children, and the others are heard in the rotation on day 80). Children who finish early draw. Counted: each child is away 3 of the 20 minutes; ours, untested.
- **2 min. Hand in.** Packets collected.

Who holds it: each child holds their own answers; the teacher holds the spoken record. Tool: None. By hand: printed packet, picture cards, record sheet. Evidence: items 1 to 6 (unit field 5). Marking: about 15 teacher minutes after the day.

## Day 80

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Respond. Acts on an assessment: reteach and revise what the checks showed, from the work students handed in.
- goal: Respond: small groups re-teach what the observation showed, then a brief fresh try
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Each child sees their packet with marks (shown, shown with help, not yet seen) and a picture of the move for the part missed. Up to four groups form by most-missed item. If more than a third missed one error, teach it first to everyone (errors E, K, B repairs from day 78 go here).
- **10 min. Reteach.** The teacher meets each group about 2 minutes with the written move for the item (item 1: show a made-up noncitizen member; item 2: link a quality to a deed; item 3: lay the then-and-now pictures; item 4: sort plan and record, and a problem at each scale; item 5: one small, safe part; item 6: claim, reason from the source, closing). Others pair and work from the written move, then an unused case; the assessment cases are not rehearsed.
- **10 min. Fresh try.** Only the missed parts, on changed cases (field 3). Each child does a paper part; the teacher hears spoken parts for up to 20 children (30 seconds each); a child not heard is heard in the next rotation, never by a whole-class wait.
- **5 min. Review.** Each group says its move aloud once; the record sheet is updated.

Who holds it: the groups and their written moves; the teacher holds the record. Tool: None. By hand: marked packets, move cards, fresh cards. Evidence: the day 80 fresh try (unit field 5).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: claim, reason, closing, change, remain, keep. Frames: "I think ___ because ___ so ___." "Before ___; after ___; still ___." Familiar-language rehearsal and answers are allowed.
- **Access.** Large print and read-aloud; pointing and dictation for any item; a quiet place for the spoken part; seated recording.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all sources and prompts aloud without hinting; allow drawing, pointing with explanation, dictation, familiar-language responses and adapted writing tools; assess civic understanding apart from English and handwriting.
- **Reading load.** None beyond a word label; every source is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Defend a different action with a detail from the record, and say what would show it worked.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Look back at a small thing done at home or school: compare a dated before drawing with a today drawing, say what changed and what remains, then write a claim, reason and closing in three boxes. On the last day of the unit, a grown-up reads a short fresh source aloud and asks the unit's questions one at a time.
- **One projector.** Show the packet and cards on screen while the child works on paper; the spoken parts are heard one at a time.
