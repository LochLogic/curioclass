## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.1.T1.1** (days 3, 4, 5): Explain the benefits and meaning of belonging to a group, follow a group's rules and duties, and explain the reasons for those rules.
- **D1.3.K-2** (days 5): Name the facts and concepts that connect to a smaller supporting question.
- skill ss1-group-rules (grade 1)
- skill ss1-supporting-questions (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions.** Co-write on day 1: What does belonging give members (answered day 3)? Why do groups need rules and duties (day 5)? How does each role help a shared goal (day 8)? On day 2, connect each to civics and list needed facts and words. Contrast history, geography and economics questions using the mural's past, location and limited supplies.
> - **Teaching.** Model a supporting question on day 1 and one claim from a picture, "I think ... because ...", on day 4, which prepares item 6. Day 7 opens with original pictures of firefighters and a librarian with a helper, then the volunteer account (motives, not pictures). Source kind and maker wait for unit 2. Step back during day 8's task. Teach group, member, belong, rule, duty, role, goal and community through pictures and actions.
>
> - **Carried.** No formal model is added. Keep the wonder wall and spoken claim routine from kindergarten. Tie supporting questions to facts and words on day 2; return on days 5 and 8. Withdraw completed examples on day 9, retaining accessibility supports. Carry the question board forward.
