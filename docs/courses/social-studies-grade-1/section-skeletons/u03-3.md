Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Teaching.** Model questioning on day 21, ordered explanation on day 27 and shared writing on day 28; step back on day 29. Before reading, identify source kind, maker, old or new, and usefulness. Compare accounts of each episode. Teach leader, member, responsibility, organize, guide, quality, evidence and reason through actions.
>
> - **Limits.** Study actions, not flawless heroes. Slavery meant people were treated as property and forced to work; it was wrong. Teach Tubman's agency and helpers. Lincoln's proclamation did not free everyone or end slavery alone. Workers organized with Huerta; Cherokee citizens shaped their nation with Mankiller. As of November 2026: Huerta died on October 28, 2026; teach her in the past tense (1930 to 2026). She and Cesar Chavez started the farm workers' union together; if a child names him, the teacher says so in one sentence and that this class studies Huerta's work. The 2026 statements are not taught at this age. No oppression simulations. Elections wait for unit 4; action for unit 7.
> - **Carried.** Keep the question board, calendar and source routine. On days 22 to 25, return to an unscaled timeline, extending beyond one year. Keep unit 2's change chart for comparing actions. Tie spoken reasons to writing on day 28; remove completed models on day 29, retaining blank frames and access supports through assessment.
