## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.1.T1.3** (days 20): Explain that a leader is also a group member who takes on extra responsibility, such as inspiring others, organizing tasks, and guiding decisions.
- **D2.Civ.1.K-2** (days 20): Describe what people in charge are responsible for.
- **D2.His.3.K-2** (days 21): Ask questions about the people and groups who brought about an important change.
- **MA.1.T1.4** (days 21): Analyze examples of leaders from history, daily life, and texts, and describe the qualities of a good leader.
- skill ss1-leaders (grade 1)
- skill ss1-supporting-questions (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions.** Co-write on day 21: What responsibility does a leader take (answered day 20, revisited day 26)? What did each historical leader and their group change (days 22 to 25)? Which actions show good leadership (day 26)? List needed facts and words beside each.
> - **Teaching.** Model questioning on day 21, ordered explanation on day 27 and shared writing on day 28; step back on day 29. Before reading, identify source kind, maker, old or new, and usefulness. Compare accounts of each episode. Teach leader, member, responsibility, organize, guide, quality, evidence and reason through actions.
