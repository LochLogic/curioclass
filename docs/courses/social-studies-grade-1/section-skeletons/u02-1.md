## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.1.T3.3** (days 11, 12, 13): Record patterns in seasonal events or personal experiences using a calendar and words and verb tenses about past, present, and future.
- **D2.His.1.K-2** (days 13): Put several events in time order.
- skill ss1-calendar-time (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions.** Co-write: How do calendars keep track of events (answered day 13)? What kinds of clues survive (day 14)? Which would help our questions (day 15)? What changed and stayed in these examples (day 16)?
> - **Teaching.** Model dating a calendar entry on day 11 and choosing a source on day 15. Before each reading, name kind, known maker, old or new, and usefulness. Model a claim with evidence on day 16; step back on day 17. Teach calendar, month, year, season, past, present, future, source, photograph and object through pictures and actions.
>
> - **Carried.** Keep unit 1's question board and spoken claim routine. On day 13, connect calendar dates to a January-to-December month strip, including events earlier and later than today. Separate future plans from recorded observations; dates do not guarantee weather. Day 16 revises kindergarten's then-and-now chart with source details and an unknown column. Withdraw completed examples on day 18, retaining blank models and access supports. Keep the calendar through spring, noting local seasonal changes such as rainfall or plant growth.
