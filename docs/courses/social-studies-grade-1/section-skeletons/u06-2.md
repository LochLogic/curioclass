## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Print photographs, maps, source cards, song words and music, a timeline, blank comparison charts, claim frames and record sheets. Supply paper and crayons. An adult can hum tunes from printed music; no recording or device is required. One shared screen may display the same prints; no student timers.
>
> **Access.** Read everything aloud; require no independent reading. Rehearse "The source shows ___. This supports ___ because ___." Provide familiar-language rehearsal, neutral descriptions, large print and adapted marking tools. Distinguish language gaps from conceptual errors. Extension defends two interpretations of one symbol. At home, the adult takes the discussion partner's role and follows day 68's fresh attempt.
