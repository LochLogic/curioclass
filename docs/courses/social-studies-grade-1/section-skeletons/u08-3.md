## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.1.T4.9** (days 88, 90): Compare reasons people save money, such as a future purchase, a donation, or an emergency.
- **D2.Eco.10.K-2** (days 88, 90): Explain why people save.
- **D2.Eco.9.K-2** (days 89): Describe what banks do in an economy.
- skill ss1-saving (grade 1)
- skill ss1-choices (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions.** Co-write: How do people earn (81 and 82)? What do buyers pay for (83 and 84)? What does choosing give and give up (85 to 87)? Why and where save (88 to 90)?
> - **Teaching.** Model pay vocabulary on 81, source comparisons on 84, forgone alternatives on 86 and a reasoned claim on 87. On 82 trace bread from baker through driver to seller, naming skills and tools. On 89 explain deposits, savings interest and repayable loans. Step back on 90 and 91. Ask who made each source, when, its kind and usefulness. Fictional shop statements are not proof about actual businesses.
>
> - **Carried.** On 86 build a choose and give-up mat; on 88 tie it to saving instead of spending now. Introduce three paper jars on 90 and link their total to available money. Cover worked examples on 91; retain blank mats and jar outlines. Sharing may receive zero. These are optional planning categories, not a required family budget.
