## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.1.T4.7** (days 91, 92, 93): Give examples of choices people make when buying goods and services, and explain that limited money forces those choices.
- **MA.1.T4.9** (days 91, 92, 93): Compare reasons people save money, such as a future purchase, a donation, or an emergency.
- **D4.1.K-2** (days 91): Make an argument and give reasons for it.
- **MA.1.T4.4** (days 92): Explain what being employed means and define income, wages, and salary.
- **D2.Eco.6.K-2** (days 92): Explain how people earn income.
- **MA.1.T4.5** (days 92): Give examples of goods that people buy and use.
- **MA.1.T4.6** (days 92): Give examples of services people do for one another.
- **D2.Eco.5.K-2** (days 92): Identify the prices of products in a local market.
- **MA.1.T4.8** (days 92): Analyze examples of voluntary buying choices, such as picking a company that treats workers well or protects the environment.
- **D2.Eco.2.K-2** (days 92): Identify the benefits and costs of personal decisions.
- **D2.Eco.9.K-2** (days 92): Describe what banks do in an economy.
- **D2.Eco.10.K-2** (days 92): Explain why people save.
