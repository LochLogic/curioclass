## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.1.T1.8** (days 38, 40, 41): Explain that people in a town, city, or nation are citizens whose rights and duties include electing leaders for set terms and judging whether to re-elect them.
- **MA.1.T1.7** (days 38, 40): Name some leaders chosen by elections, such as the President, the Governor of Massachusetts, or a team captain, and explain what they do.
- **D4.1.K-2** (days 38, 39): Make an argument and give reasons for it.
- **MA.1.T1.5** (days 39, 40): Give examples of why groups with differing views need ways to decide, and explain how voting can make fair decisions or choose leaders.
- **D2.Civ.8.K-2** (days 39, 40, 41): Describe democratic ideas such as equality, fairness and respect for fair authority and rules.
- **MA.1.T1.6** (days 40): Explain that an election is a kind of voting in which people choose their leaders.
- **D2.Civ.5.K-2** (days 40): Explain what a government is and some things it does.
- **D2.Eco.12.K-2** (days 40): Give examples of goods and services that governments provide.

- skill ss1-voting (grade 1)

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Use printed source summaries, invented case drawings, candidate deed cards, folded paper ballots, a covered box, tally sheets, government cards, blank claim frames, paper and crayons. One optional shared screen shows the same prints; no student timer. At home, use supplied fictional ballots for counting while the child makes a private choice; explain that this models a larger group's election.
>
> **Access.** No independent reading is required. Rehearse "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___." Offer familiar-language rehearsal, high-contrast print, spoken descriptions and adapted marking tools; ballot assistance preserves the child's choice and privacy. For extension, defend different judgments from the same term record.
