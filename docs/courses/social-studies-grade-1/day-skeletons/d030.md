## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observation task with a short drawn or dictated piece: from a short read-aloud about a leader not studied, tell in order what the leader did, what extra responsibility they took on as a member of their group, and one quality it shows and why (a written or dictated claim, reason and closing); ask one question about the leader and one about a classmate's answer, and answer a classmate's question
- **Note:** The teacher records asking and answering during the exchange. Score earlier records only after written feedback and day 29 revision. Sensitivity read: Hull House immigrant neighbors, including Jewish experience, from Polacheck's own memoir. Response on day 31

- ss1-claim-reasons (grade 1): taught or practised here
