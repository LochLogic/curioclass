## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observation task with a short drawn or dictated piece: read a ballot tally not seen before and say the result; tell whether a vote was an election (choosing a leader) or a vote on a matter; give one reason groups need a way to decide and explain why a secret ballot is fair; name an elected leader, what they do, and one service government provides; from a short made-up term record, say whether to re-elect the leader and why
- **Note:** The teacher also notes each child's day 38 re-election judgment. Response on day 41

**Skills:**

- ss1-voting (grade 1): taught or practised here
- ss1-elected-leaders (grade 1): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **MA.1.T1.5:** Give examples of why groups with differing views need ways to decide, and explain how voting can make fair decisions or choose leaders.
- **MA.1.T1.6:** Explain that an election is a kind of voting in which people choose their leaders.
- **D2.Civ.8.K-2:** Describe democratic ideas such as equality, fairness and respect for fair authority and rules.
- **MA.1.T1.7:** Name some leaders chosen by elections, such as the President, the Governor of Massachusetts, or a team captain, and explain what they do.
- **MA.1.T1.8:** Explain that people in a town, city, or nation are citizens whose rights and duties include electing leaders for set terms and judging whether to re-elect them.
- **D2.Civ.5.K-2:** Explain what a government is and some things it does.
- **D2.Eco.12.K-2:** Give examples of goods and services that governments provide.
