## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** A secret-ballot class election: choose a leader for a pretend class job (garden captain) from two made-up candidates whose deeds are on cards, weighing unit 3's qualities; paper ballots, a tally, the result read together
- **Note:** Lean 1.MD.C.4: math unit 1 (weeks 1 to 4), already taught. The candidates are made up, never classmates, a real candidate or a party, so no child loses a vote. An election chooses a leader; a vote on a class matter is a vote, not an election
