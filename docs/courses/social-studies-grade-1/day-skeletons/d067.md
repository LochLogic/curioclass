## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observation task with a short drawn or dictated piece: from a photograph and a short text not seen before, one way people there live differently and how the land or weather explains it; the meaning of a symbol, of a phrase (the motto or the Pledge) and of a song, one from the new source and two from picture cards, each with its evidence; a claim with evidence about how Americans are both united and diverse

- **MA.1.T2.5:** Explain that people in different parts of the world can have different ways of living, customs, and languages.
- **D2.Geo.6.K-2:** Identify some cultural and environmental features of particular places.
- **MA.1.T3.1:** Use evidence to explain how people in the United States are united, such as by a shared national history, and how they are diverse in backgrounds, beliefs, and traditions.
- **MA.1.T3.2:** Explain the meaning of symbols, phrases, and songs that show pride in belonging to the United States.
- **MA.1.T3.2a:** Explain the meaning of national symbols such as the flag, the bald eagle, the White House, and the Statue of Liberty.
- **MA.1.T3.2b:** Explain the meaning of national words and mottoes, such as "E pluribus unum" ("Out of Many, One") and the "Pledge of Allegiance."
- **MA.1.T3.2c:** Recognize the tunes and words of patriotic songs such as "America the Beautiful" and "The Star-Spangled Banner," and explain what they mean.
