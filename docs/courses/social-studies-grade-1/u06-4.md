# Section 6.4: What holds a country together: the claim, the check and the response


## 1. Standards and practices


- **MA.1.T3.1, days 66 to 68.** Children "use evidence to explain how people in the United States are united, such as by a shared national history, and how they are diverse in backgrounds, beliefs, and traditions". 66: a claim with evidence from the Constitution, the Cherokee Nation's account and one national symbol source, with a reason and a closing. 67 and 68: checked and answered.
- **MA.1.T3.2, days 66 to 68.** Children "explain the meaning of symbols, phrases, and songs that show pride in belonging to the United States", here as one piece of evidence. Parts a, b and c are checked on 67 (6.3 taught them).
- **MA.1.T2.5 and D2.Geo.6.K-2, day 67.** Taught in 6.1 and checked in items 1 and 2.
- **Limits that bite here.** Defensible answers may stress shared government, shared history or shared ideals. Shared ideals do not prove equal treatment: people were enslaved, and Native people were forced from their homelands. Assess understanding, never patriotism, and never saying, standing or singing. Fiction (the invented Willow Town) shows an idea and is not evidence about a real town. A name or a tune match alone is not meaning.
- **Connections.** 6.2's united and diverse, and the frame "The source shows ___. This supports ___ because ___" (carried; the unit plan says unit 3 and unit 5, not in the ledger yet); 6.3's symbols; 6.1's places and map.
- **What the teacher page must explain.**
  - Item-by-item scoring is in the unit plan's field 5 and is not repeated; here it is run by component: a child's record has six rows, each "shown", "shown with help" or "not yet seen".
  - On 66 the teacher covers completed class models and supplied reasons: the three source cards, a blank frame and the compelling question remain. No new model is shown.
  - The fresh packet for 67 uses unseen photographs, captions, a new Constitution excerpt from the same transcript (S: National Archives, not the opening words used on 59), a new seal view, and held-out phrases ("amber waves of grain", "our flag was still there").
  - A child who cannot hear a tune is scored on title and lyric-card matching and meaning; the tune part is recorded as inaccessible, not as an error.

## 6. Days

Every day is 30 minutes (frame). Parts are the lines in bold; the day skeleton reads them. No timer is shown to children. Stems, captions and passages are read aloud; children point, circle, place a token, draw or say. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and said aloud to be made up. On days 66 and 68 the rotating six are heard 40 seconds each (about 4 minutes). Nobody is asked to say the Pledge, stand or sing.

## Day 66


- **5 min. Return and repair.** One repair: E65, error D, if many missed (5 minutes): the class finds the detail behind a meaning; retry with a new phrase.
- **3 min. Reopen.** The compelling question, read aloud: "What holds a country of so many different people together?" Three source cards on the board: the Constitution, the Cherokee Nation's account, one symbol source (the Great Seal's motto). The teacher says that shared government, shared history or shared ideals are all good answers, and each needs a detail.
- **4 min. Step back.** The teacher covers the class's completed charts and the supplied reasons, and shows the blank frame: claim, evidence, reason, closing. No model is shown.
- **12 min. Independent work.** Each child draws and labels, or dictates to a partner who listens and says it back, a claim, one piece of evidence (a card pointed to), a reason ("because") and a closing ("So ___"). Turns: the teacher hears the rotating six dictate, 40 seconds each (4 minutes); the rest draw. The six are heard once each in 5 days.
- **3 min. Exit.** E66: photograph read aloud, every child circles.
- **3 min. Look back.** Children tell their partner their closing. "Tomorrow: new pictures and a new story. Nothing new is taught. Nobody is asked to say or sing."

Holds: pairs hold their own frame; the teacher holds the three cards. Tool: None. By hand: source cards, frame, crayons. Evidence: E66; the drawing for item 6.

## Day 67


- **5 min. Orientation.** Oral: today is a check; children point, circle, draw and say; no one is asked to say the Pledge, stand or sing; a child may listen quietly. Practice words are not used as answers.
- **5 min. Item 6.** Willow Town record, a new Constitution passage and a new seal view: the claim, evidence for unity and for diversity, a symbol link and a closing. Drawn or dictated; closing heard.
- **5 min. Item 3.** The four symbols with fresh views and captions, marked with meaning and evidence. One symbol heard.
- **4 min. Item 1.** Sources A and B: a difference in daily life, a school custom, a language clue, what the sources leave unknown. The teacher hears the first group in these minutes.
- **3 min. Item 2.** The two places on the map; a cultural and an environmental feature in each; how weather might affect daily life. Hearings continue.
- **4 min. Item 4.** A new seal view and phrase cards: "Out of many, one" and the Pledge's belonging; whether someone must say it to belong. Hearings continue.
- **4 min. Item 5.** The adult hums short passages of each tune; matches of tune, title and the held-out phrases, with meaning. Done by the whole class together.

Holds: the teacher holds the record sheet. Tool: None. By hand: packet, tokens, crayons. Evidence: items 1 to 6, recorded by component; no practice work is scored. If a child's evidence is missing, the record says "not yet seen" and day 68 picks it up.

## Day 68


- **5 min. Return evidence.** Each child gets their own sheet back with one component marked "shown", "shown with help" or "not yet seen" and one next step; the sheet names no other child.
- **10 min. Reteach by component.** Groups come from day 67's record sheet by component, at most four, the most shared error first (the repairs table gives the move); other children work in pairs from the written move on a card. One child follows this cycle with an adult. The teacher also hears any child not heard on day 67, one symbol or the closing, about 35 seconds each.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Mark E68 (three minutes, every child circles). Then each child tries again the component they missed, on a new card; the teacher hears the unresolved children, 35 seconds each (up to six at a time in this 7 minutes); others do a new pair task from the move card.
- **5 min. Compare work.** Pairs set their day 66 frame beside their new try and say one thing that improved. "Tomorrow starts the next unit: who is a citizen."

Holds: groups hold the written move. Tool: None. By hand: record sheet, new cards, tokens. Evidence: E68; the fresh attempt recorded by component.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: 66, "What holds a country together? What shows it?"; 68, "What changed in your answer? What is your detail?"
- Written task, day 66: a drawn or dictated claim, evidence, reason and closing. The teacher scribes.
- Judged by the teacher with five look-fors: a claim that answers the question; evidence pointed to in one of the three sources; a reason that links them; a closing; understanding, not a fixed answer (government, history and ideals are all defensible). Drawing, pointing and dictation count.

- **Day 68.** The component each child missed, on new cards.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: claim, evidence, reason, united, diverse, symbol, motto, song. Frames: "The source shows ___." "This supports ___ because ___." "So ___." Familiar-language speech is accepted and scored for the idea.
- **Access.** Large print, raised-line maps and seal, neutral picture descriptions, tokens in place of drawing, a private hearing; for a child who cannot hear a tune, title and lyric-card matching.
- **Check accommodations.** Read everything aloud; accept a point, a token, a drawing, dictation, familiar-language speech or a private turn. Do not score reading, handwriting, belief or taking part in patriotic acts. A child not heard stays "not yet seen", never guessed.
- **Reading load.** None.
- **Extension, same standard.** Defend two answers to the compelling question, each with its own detail.
