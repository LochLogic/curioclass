# Section 7.2: Hearing views, choosing and acting

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-1/7/s/7.2), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-1 u07-2.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## 1. Standards and practices


- **D4.7.K-2, this section's share (days 72, 73, 74).** Children "name ways to take action on local, regional and global problems": here the local problem only. Day 72, hear two views before choosing; day 73, list ways the class could help and choose one; day 74, take the first step. Regional and global action belongs to 7.3 (day 77) and the unit assessment (item 5, day 79), which asks for another way and a small, safe part for children.
- **Limits that bite here.** A vote chooses a plan; it is not an election, and no leader is chosen. Safety, rights and access are never put to a vote. Every proposal is checked by the teacher before the ballot: inside the school, an adult can help, nothing unknown to touch (no unknown waste, road, water or sharp thing), no money, no leaving school. Children act only under adult supervision; any child who finds something unknown stops and tells an adult. Offer seated, drawing and recording roles. The course takes no side on the problem. The unchosen proposal and both views stay on the board and return on day 95.
- **Connections.** Unit 4's secret ballot (day 33, from the unit 4 plan; not in the ledger yet) and kindergarten's block-in-a-cup count and "a shared revision before any vote" return as review. Unit 6's perspective (day 57) returns on day 72. Kindergarten's Our Decision Record is reused for the class's own record.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Why the two views are kept after the vote and nobody's reason is ranked. How a secret ballot stays private yet counted: a folded slip, a box, and counters who read the marks aloud with no name on any slip. What to do with a tie (a runoff between the top two, then both in turn). Why a vote on a class plan is open to every child in the class, and why safety is never voted. The two views on day 72 are real adults' words, not the teacher's guess; where none exists, the fallback is said aloud (day 72).

## 2. Where it sits

Results go to the record sheet and the action log, and feed item 5 (day 79) and the record of listening, ballots and acting.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** item 5 (another way people could help; one safe part for children), scored on day 79 with 7.4, plus the day 74 row above. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every case is "invented for practice" and never returns in teaching; if the class's own action matches an exit, use the reserved alternate named on the day.

- **E72.** Mark: two made-up children, Hana and Bo, want the same table, one to paint and one to build. Circle an arrangement that helps both: turns or two spaces, or the painter's way only. Answer: turns or two spaces. Wrong: the painter's way only (D). Spoken (rotating six): "Tell me both reasons." Answer: paint needs room to dry and building needs room to spread. Wrong: "The builder is wrong because he wants something different" (D).
- **E73.** Mark: an invented group chooses between two ways to help, and one box stands on the table. Circle the fair secret ballot: everyone puts one folded slip in the box, or only one side puts slips in. Answer: one slip each. Wrong: only one side votes (I). Spoken (rotating six): "How can the ballot be fair?" Answer: one slip each, no names, counted together. Wrong: "The side that wins gets the votes" (I). Teaching covers the difference between a vote and an election; it is not in the exit (plan point).
- **E74.** Mark: an invented class chose to help younger pupils find a room. Circle a safe first step: making signs with the teacher, or leaving school alone to put signs outside. Answer: signs with the teacher. Wrong: leaving school alone (H). Spoken (rotating six): "Why is that step safe?" Answer: a grown-up is with us and we stay inside the approved route. Wrong: "Children can do anything on their own" (H). Reserved alternate if the class's action is signs: an invented class helps the lost-and-found with a staff helper, or carries the box alone down a street.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The question board and helper web (7.1); the dated baseline (day 70) stays on the wall for day 78. Unit 6's perspective becomes "two views, each with what we saw", on day 72 and kept after the vote. Our Decision Record (kindergarten) holds date, problem, proposals, result and reasons from day 73; the action log (new) holds the dated steps. Both views and the unchosen proposal remain visible and return on day 95.
- **Key moment 1, day 72: two real views.** Must be seen: each view has a reason and a thing we saw, and neither is called bad. Ideal instrument: two adults who see the problem differently, in person or in a dated note. Spec: print and a short visit fit; no tool. If only one adult view exists, the teacher says so and reads a second view labeled "possible view, invented for practice".
- **Key moment 2, day 73: the ballot.** Must be seen: one folded slip each, nobody's mark shown, counted aloud by counters. Ideal instrument: paper slips with three pictures, a box with a slot. Spec: print fits. A room with nothing: scrap paper slips, a hat or box, a crayon.
- **Key moment 3, day 74: the first step.** Must be seen: an adult is present and a safe-step check was said aloud. Ideal instrument: the real place. A room with nothing needs no more than the classroom.

## 6. Days

## Day 72


- **4 min. Reopen.** Post "Why do views differ?" Retrieval prompt on a picture card of two made-up friends (one wants the quiet corner, one wants to talk about a book): "Tell your partner each friend's reason." Partners speak 20 seconds each; the teacher hears a rotating six (2 minutes).
- **5 min. Return and repair.** If many made errors J, C on day 71, repair here with a fresh note on the shows and does-not-show card; otherwise a small group meets. A child who named one side only joins the two-sides sort here.
- **6 min. Teaching first.** Two invented picture figures, Pim and Dov, want the new class rug in different places (by the window to read by; by the shelf to reach books). Fill two columns: a view, its reason, and what we saw. Teach "turns" and "two spaces" as ways both can be helped. A view that differs is not a bad view.
- **6 min. Two real views of our problem.** Two adults give about a minute each, with a role card (no faces needed). The teacher says what each said and when.
- **3 min. Fill the columns.** Class and teacher write both views, reasons and what we saw (a baseline picture or a count) in the columns. Each column is read back aloud together.
- **3 min. Both helped?** Each pair offers one way that helps both views (about 40 seconds); the teacher notes two. They stay on the board.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** E72: the mark (1 minute); the rotating six heard (6 children, about 30 seconds, 3 minutes) while the others draw one reason.

Who holds it: the two adults' words hold the views; the teacher holds the columns. Tool: None. By hand: picture figures, two-column sheet, role cards. Evidence: E72.

## Day 73


- **2 min. Reopen.** Say the ballot rules aloud (review of unit 4): one slip each, no names, counted together.
- **3 min. Warm-up and repair.** If many made error D on day 72, put each reason beside its view and say it back here (3 minutes).
- **6 min. Ideas, then a filter.** Children offer ways to help (kindergarten's "idea"); the teacher writes them. Filter aloud: small enough for us, inside school, an adult can help, nothing unknown, no money. Those left become three proposal cards with a reason each. Unsafe or too-big ideas go to the board with the reason.
- **3 min. A vote, not an election.** This vote picks a plan, not a leader. The teacher models a slip: three pictures, mark one, fold, drop.
- **5 min. Cast the ballots.** Each child marks, folds and drops one slip, a row at a time (30 children, about 10 seconds each); a child who cannot mark points and an adult marks.
- **4 min. Count.** Four counters read marks aloud while the class counts the three piles together. A tie: a runoff between the top two; if still tied, try both in turn, as in kindergarten.
- **2 min. Record.** Our Decision Record: date, problem, three proposals, result, reasons. Keep the unchosen proposals and both views visible.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E73: the mark (1 minute); the rotating six heard (3 minutes) while the others draw the folded slip going into the box.

Who holds it: the class holds the decision; the counters read, the teacher records. Tool: None. By hand: slips, box, crayons, record. Evidence: E73.

## Day 74


- **3 min. Warm-up and repair.** Read the Decision Record. If many made error I on day 73, rehearse one slip each with two paper boxes here.
- **6 min. Plan the steps.** Three steps on picture cards: who does what, with which adult, in which place. Say the safe-step check aloud: inside the school, an adult is with us, nothing unknown to touch, stop and tell an adult if we find something unknown. Sort any child idea that fails it, with the reason (error H); sort what we do and what adults do (error P).
- **14 min. Carry out the first step.** Three groups of ten each take one pictured job, with an adult nearby; seated, drawing and recording roles are offered. The teacher moves between groups for the safety check and hears no one in this part.
- **2 min. Action log.** Date, what we did, which groups, what is next; a picture added.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E74: the mark (1 minute); the rotating six heard (3 minutes) while the others draw their own part of the job.

Who holds it: the class holds the action; adults hold the safety checks. Tool: None. By hand: step cards, action log, supplies the chosen action needs. Evidence: E74 (the day 74 row).

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 72, "What does each person want, and why?"; day 73, "Which way could we try, and what makes the vote fair?"; day 74, "What is our part, and who is helping?"
- **Written task (day 74).** Draw or dictate the child's own part in the action, with the date. Look-fors: a real part the child did; it matches what the teacher saw; it follows the vote.
- Recorded shown, shown with help, not yet seen; spoken evidence in its own column.

## 8. Practice that returns

- Day 72: kindergarten's talk rules and two views; unit 6's perspective. Day 73: unit 4's secret ballot; kindergarten's idea, count and a shared revision. Day 74: kindergarten's "help (make a problem smaller)"; the 7.1 helper web (who else is working on it).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: view, reason, proposal, ballot, action, safe. Frames: "I see ___. I think ___ because ___." "Pim wants ___ because ___." "Our part is ___."
- **Access.** Cards move; an adult marks the slip as the child points; a token or sound-count for a child who cannot see slips. Seated jobs on day 74.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a token, drawing, dictation or an aid; a private place for the spoken part.
- **Reading load.** None; everything is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Offer a way to help that neither view has said, and one safe first step for it.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Pick a small shared-space problem at home or school. Hear a reason from each person at the table, find a way that helps both, then vote with one slip each. Act the same day, with a grown-up, inside the home or school.
- **One projector.** The two columns and three proposal cards show on screen; the ballot stays on paper.
