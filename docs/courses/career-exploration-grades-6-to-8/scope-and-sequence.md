# Scope and sequence: Career Exploration, grades 6 to 8

Course `career-exploration-grades-6-to-8` (catalog id `cte-explore`), placed in grade 8 (open to grades 6 to 8).
Draft, written 2026-11-26 from docs/courses/career-exploration-grades-6-to-8/frame.md and fixed the same day from
the expert read (docs/reviews/sequence-career-exploration-grades-6-to-8-fixes-2026-11-26.md). The days live in
data/plans/career-exploration-grades-6-to-8/days.csv; this page holds the judgment. The frame's 7 units, their order,
owned rows and day counts are unchanged.

## The year at a glance

- **Days.** 96 course days: unit 1 days 1 to 16 (16), unit 2 days 17 to 30 (14), unit 3 days 31 to 45 (15),
  course-level check 1 days 46 and 47, unit 4 days 48 to 58 (11), unit 5 days 59 to 68 (10), unit 6 days 69 to 84
  (16), unit 7 days 85 to 94 (10), course-level check 2 days 95 and 96. No unit runs over 25 days, so none has a
  mid-unit assessment.
- **One year, one grade.** The course is one year, not a band: all its standards fall in its one year, placed in
  grade 8 (a school may run it in grade 7 with the swaps the frame's "Placement and grade" bullet names; readiness and
  retrieval below are counted from grade 8).
- **Meeting days and `school_day`.** Three 45-minute sessions a week for 32 weeks. Formula, stated once: course day n
  falls on school day ceil(n x 160 / 96), the frame's even spread; week = (school_day - 1) // 5 + 1. Every unit and
  check sits on the frame's school days: unit 1 school days 2 to 27 (weeks 1 to 6), unit 2 29 to 50 (weeks 6 to 10),
  unit 3 52 to 75 (weeks 11 to 15), check 1 77 and 79 (week 16), unit 4 80 to 97 (weeks 16 to 20), unit 5 99 to 114
  (weeks 20 to 23), unit 6 115 to 140 (weeks 23 to 28), unit 7 142 to 157 (weeks 29 to 32), check 2 159 and 160
  (week 32). No pin is needed.
- **The shape of a unit** (APPROACH.md): each cluster gets two explore days (Understanding) and a field task day
  (Application) that drafts the piece and the plan's changed line, collected in the last five minutes; the unit's
  learner-goal days sit among them; the unit closes with a plan day (Application: written feedback on the pieces and
  plan lines returned, one piece revised, the evidence attachments revised), the assessment day (Check, `end`) and a
  respond day (Respond). The response time is the respond day, counted once in every unit. No day is Fluency: the
  rereading of a pay table or outlook figure on a new field takes a few minutes inside explore days (frame "Day
  kinds").
- **The plan and the portfolio** are carried all year; every plan day returns them and the goals are reread from unit 2
  on (CD1.R3, first taught day 28, reread on days 43, 56, 66, 82 and 92 through `retrieves`). The plan is private,
  read by the teacher only, and scored on its reasoning, never on the path chosen.
- **Devices.** None per student. Day 10 holds practice 5's three-minute turn per pair at one shared device with a free
  offline spreadsheet and a privacy step (Cory 2026-11-26 kept it); every other tool day has a paper path, named in
  its note.
- **Safety** (frame's "days.csv words", copied into the notes): days 33 and 34 (scissors, the production line), day 50
  (the optional seed tray), day 78 (the menu task, paper only). The safety agent's read of units 3, 4 and 6 comes
  before the course is called finished.
- **Sensitivity.** Every unit day 1 note says the family note goes home (English and Spanish; the work question may go
  to any trusted adult or an invented or public worker). Every unit 6 day carries health's "If a student tells you
  something" steps in its note (the pay-gap unit), as do day 51 (Resource Extraction on the Energy map) and any unit 7
  packet on a sensitive sub-cluster (day 87). The military route (day 41) carries its teacher-page line.
- **Sources.** No base text, so no source map. Every data sheet, job card, field task sheet, case and invented data set
  is Curio Class's to write; none is written yet, so every explore and field task day leans on a sheet not yet made
  (built with the unit plans). Public sources the days name: the federal outlook handbook and wage tables, the federal
  occupational database (credit line only), the federal employment-by-demographic table (day 75), a state extension
  service's planting calendar (day 50), a town's adopted budget (day 81), the young worker safety curriculum (day 33)
  and, for day 90, the federal young workers page (a safe workplace and who to ask, https://www.osha.gov/young-workers)
with the federal Wage and Hour Division's YouthRules and child labor pages (the hours and task limits under 16 and
under 18, https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/youthrules and https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/child-labor; a stricter
state law applies where there is one). None is loaded into the repository; the unit plans date each figure.

## Unit 1. Me, the plan and the tools (days 1 to 16, weeks 1 to 6)

**Arc.** The hook's three invented workers open the question; the self comes first (inventory day 1, values day 2),
then the cluster map (3), the plan's parts and goals (4) and the first plan (5); then career information: why old facts
mislead (6), the job study taught (7) and done again (8), four sources read laterally (9), the table and the device
turn (10); then deciding: how I decide and the decision steps card (11), facts used and a changed plan line (12). The
field task (13) uses every model to choose between two jobs. Plan day 14 introduces the portfolio (practice 12).
**Why this order.** It follows the lens (self, then world of work, then plan, then action) and the frame's "In this
order" list; each model is introduced before a later day uses it (the job card before the table, the table and the
decision steps card before the field task). Days follow load: the job study and the deciding rows get two days each,
the map and goals one.
**Assessments.** Readiness: day 1's last ten minutes (the two APPROACH items only). End: day 15 scores the revised
piece (practices 5 and 10, CD2.A2, CD2.A3), the plan lines (CD1.A2, CD1.K3, ALL1.K1, CD3.A1), the plan page with each
part labeled (CD1.K2) and the portfolio choice (practice 12), with a one-line prompt per part for the other knowing and
doing codes (CD3.K4, CD3.A4, CD2.K5 answered privately, CD3.K1, CD3.K2, CD3.K3, CD3.A3, CD2.K1, CD2.A1, CD2.K2 with
four shuffled decision steps put in order, CD2.K3 with two invented students' choices compared) and three to five data
items, one of them CD3.A2 (three kinds of information read from a new 150-word job profile, each with its source and
year). The device part of practice 5 is the teacher's day 10 record. Respond:
day 16.
**Candidates.** Kept: the unseen dated hourly wage read as its yearly figure (a data item on day 15, no day added).
Dropped from the unit: the decision steps card run on an invented student's choice, because check 1 (day 46) runs
exactly that task and the field task already shows the card on the student's own choice.
