## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe each child planning and carrying out a new contact-motion comparison and explaining the result.
- **Note:** End-of-unit observation, first group rotation; new objects and layout. Each child compares strong and gentle pushes AND pulls, and changed direction in separate one-variable trials, describes starting, stopping, speed and turning, and explains collision evidence with the revised chart. Complete remaining children on day 9. Six groups of five for 30 children: four groups at seven minutes today, two groups at seven minutes next day; two minutes set-up today, six minutes set-up/record completion next day, then ten-minute response. Each child has an own picture mat and checklist entry; parallel card/drawing responses cover every part and each gives one brief spoken why per code. Teacher observes each child's planning/action where required, not only a spokesperson. Others independently draw the rug-ball anchor and mark before/after positions.

**Skills:**

- sk-push-pull (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K-PS2-1:** Plan and carry out a fair test comparing how harder or softer pushes and pulls, or pushes in different directions, change how an object moves.
