## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Change a bumper to turn the ball and use results from ball collisions and falling targets to revise the design.
- **Note:** Speed and direction are both addressed across separate trials; ball hits ball and ball knocks down blocks. Revised design-cycle chart connects each change to evidence. For 20-minute periods only, this is one of the unit's four observation-window days (24/25/27/28): ten minutes essential teaching, ten new-case evidence today; closing days add twenty and ten evidence minutes, then ten response minutes. Split group parts at day boundaries, one per-child record; full parts are in the closing rows.

- sk-ramp-design (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K-PS2-2:** Use test results to decide whether a design, such as a ramp or a turning wall, changes an object's speed or direction the way it was meant to.
