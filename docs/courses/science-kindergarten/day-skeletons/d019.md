## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe every child asking for forecast information and explaining prediction, preparation and response.
- **Note:** End-of-unit forecast observation for every child: ask a question, obtain the answer from supplied calm media, connect regional hazard likelihood and observed weather patterns to prediction, then community preparation AND response. Six groups at three minutes, two minutes set-up and ten response minutes once, responding also to day 18 weather gaps. Others independently sort familiar weather pictures. Read the live two-month chart together without collecting day 18 evidence again; school day 44 provides about two months. Only a late start needs another observer's labelled dated picture records; no invented categories or private disaster accounts. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code. Record each child, never just a spokesperson. No second collection of an already completed part.
