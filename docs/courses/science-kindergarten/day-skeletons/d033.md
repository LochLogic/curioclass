## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe plants and animal media to find the repeated need for water and the plant need for light.
- **Note:** Read dated plant records as evidence, not a controlled experiment proving the needs. No photosynthesis; humane care. Sorting/counting K.MD.B.3 in math unit 2 ended school day 44, before this unit.

- sk-living-needs (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K-LS1-1:** Describe, from observations, the patterns in what plants and animals, people included, need to stay alive.
