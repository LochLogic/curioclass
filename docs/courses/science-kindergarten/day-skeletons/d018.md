## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe every child sharing counted daily and monthly weather patterns from an unfamiliar record.
- **Note:** End-of-unit weather observation for every child: observe/interpret and share daily AND monthly patterns, count a selected whole-number weather group up to ten with support, compare monthly picture rows and actual morning/afternoon relative warmth. Six groups at four minutes, six minutes set-up/transitions. Others independently sort familiar weather pictures. Forecast performance is day 19, not another full performance today. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code. Record each child, never just a spokesperson. No second collection of an already completed part.
