## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe every child asking, observing and gathering information to define an unfamiliar motion problem.
- **Note:** End-of-unit problem-definition observation: every child asks questions, observes and gathers information, states a simple desired change an object/tool could solve BEFORE proposing a solution, and explains why the problem must be understood first. Six groups at four minutes, six minutes set-up/transitions. Others independently draw the familiar ramp and target. Results-based speed/direction performance is on day 28. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code. Record each child, never just a spokesperson. No second collection of an already completed part.
