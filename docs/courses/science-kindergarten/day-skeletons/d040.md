## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Add people clearing land for houses and compare ways to meet needs while doing less harm to the system.
- **Note:** People change surroundings to meet needs, stated without blame. Connect land, water, air and living things; use plant care with less wasted water/paper as an earlier context for the year-end solution. No judgments of families' resource use. For 20-minute periods only, this is one of the unit's four observation-window days (40/41/42/43): ten minutes essential teaching, ten new-case evidence today; closing days add twenty and ten evidence minutes, then ten response minutes. Split group parts at day boundaries, one per-child record; full parts are in the closing rows.

**Skills:**

- sk-change-environment (grade K): taught or practised here
- sk-needs-places (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K-ESS2-2:** Argue, using evidence, that plants and animals, people included, change their surroundings to get what they need.
- **K-ESS3-1:** Use a model to show how what a plant or animal, people included, needs is linked to where it lives.
