## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Compare covered and uncovered patches, finish each child's shade explanation and respond to the record.
- **Note:** Use the covers set out on day 53 after a shared exposure interval before the lesson or in the afternoon routine look. Six groups at three minutes: each child compares covered/uncovered warming and gives one brief why pointing to the own build. Two minutes set-up and final ten minutes response once, using day 52 warming and days 53/54 shade records. Others independently draw the familiar slide-and-shade explanation. Continue the same shade record, no second design performance. Label dated thermal media if sunlight is unsuitable; retain the physical child-built cover. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code. Record each child, never just a spokesperson. No second collection of an already completed part.
