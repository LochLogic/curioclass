## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe every child using test results to judge a motion design and respond to the unit record.
- **Note:** End-of-unit design/results observation: every child uses the day 27 new layout, tries a solution and analyses separate speed AND direction outcomes against the stated intention, explaining the contact cause from results. Six groups at three minutes, two minutes set-up, final ten minutes unit response once using both days' records. Others independently draw the familiar ramp and target. No friction explanation; no repeat of problem-definition evidence. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code. Record each child, never just a spokesperson. No second collection of an already completed part.
