- **Note:** Year-end week 29. All 30 children participate today in six groups of five at four minutes each, six minutes set-up/transitions. K-ESS2-2: use unfamiliar plant-root, woodpecker-hole and human-building evidence to argue how plants, animals AND people change surroundings for needs; connect system parts and a choice reducing harm.
