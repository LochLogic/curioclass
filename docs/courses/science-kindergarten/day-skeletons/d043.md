## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe every child arguing from environmental-change evidence, then respond to the three-part unit record.
- **Note:** End-of-unit argument for every child: use unfamiliar plant, animal AND human change evidence to back a claim about meeting needs; connect changed system parts and a lower-impact choice. Six groups at three minutes, two minutes set-up, final ten minutes response once using days 41/42/43 records. Others independently sort familiar needs pictures. Teacher transcribes where needed; no independent spelling or decoding scored. Do not repeat needs or model performances. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code. Record each child, never just a spokesperson. No second collection of an already completed part.
