## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe every child using a new place model to link living needs with resources.
- **Note:** End-of-unit model performance for every child: make/use a relational picture model linking plants, animals INCLUDING humans to places with air, water and land resources; explain how connected parts meet needs. Six groups at four minutes, six minutes set-up/transitions. Others independently sort familiar needs pictures. Carry the day 41 needs record unchanged; the argument performance is on day 43. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code.
