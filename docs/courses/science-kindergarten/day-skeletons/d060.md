## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe each child drawing and explaining a solution for an unfamiliar schoolyard impact and communicating why it helps.
- **Note:** End-of-unit new-case rotation: each child communicates a detailed solution in drawing/model and speech, connects the human action to an effect on one or more of land, water, air and living things; the class model holds all four, and explains how the proposed change lessens harm. Actual audience is a partner or class group here; optional family/other-class sharing is teacher-arranged. Complete remaining children on day 61. Six groups of five for 30 children: four groups at seven minutes today, two groups at seven minutes next day; two minutes set-up today, six minutes set-up/record completion next day, then ten-minute response. Each child has an own picture mat and checklist entry; parallel card/drawing responses cover every part and each gives one brief spoken why per code. Teacher observes each child's planning/action where required, not only a spokesperson. Others independently draw the already-studied school solution.

- sk-reduce-impact (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**
