## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Revise the class model and observe every child explaining four living-needs patterns from new evidence.
- **Note:** First end-of-unit performance: twelve minutes revise the consensus model and explain the weed/squirrel anchors; eighteen minutes for six groups at three minutes. Every child uses unfamiliar observation cards for all-water, plant-light, animals-food/plants-no-food and different animal diets; describe patterns and their evidence. Others independently sort familiar needs pictures. Place modelling and environmental-change arguments follow on days 42 and 43. For 20-minute periods use the four-day window 40/41/42/43 described in the scope and sequence. Each of 30 children has an own picture mat and checklist entry; six groups of five, parallel pointing/card/drawing responses and one brief spoken why per code. Record each child, never just a spokesperson. No second collection of an already completed part.
