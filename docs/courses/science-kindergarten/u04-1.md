# Section 4.1: Which needs repeat?

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#science/science-kindergarten/4/s/4.1), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py science-kindergarten u04-1.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **K-LS1-1, this section's share (days 32 to 35).** "Describe, from observations, the patterns in what plants and animals, people included, need to stay alive." Three patterns, each seen again and again in different records: every living thing in the records takes in water; the plant records show light; animals eat and plants do not eat meals, and different animals eat different foods. Day 35 asks whether a second place shows the same patterns.
- **K-ESS3-1, day 32 (a start only).** "Use a model to show how what a plant or animal, people included, needs is linked to where it lives." Day 32 begins the first needs-and-places drawing; the links are built in section 4.2.
- **Limits that bite here.** No photosynthesis, food chains, gases, growth measurements or deprivation test. Say "animals eat; a plant is not fed a meal; we saw it take in water and use light." Never say plants need no food. The two class plants are dated records of these two plants over these weeks, not a test that proves a rule. No plant is left to suffer: the shelf plant is back in light before day 32. Species names are not assessed. No child reports on a home, a meal or a pet.
- **Connections.** Math K.MD.B.3 (sort and count each group), days 33 to 35, by touching each picture once; the last number says how many. ELA SL.K.5 (add drawings to a description), day 32: the child tells the drawing and the teacher labels it.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** An observation is what we saw; a pattern is what we saw again and again in different records. One plant shows one plant, so days 33 to 35 gather several living things before any pattern is named. The claim "I think ..., because I saw ..." is not taught until day 38; here the stem is "I saw ___."

## 2. Where it sits


Before: unit 3 and, for habits, unit 2's pattern sheet and picture rows (review below). After: section 4.2 opens on day 36 with the pattern sheet and the needs rows on the wall. No readiness check; the only before-teaching evidence is the unscored pattern retrieval on day 32 (unit plan field 4). Results go to the record sheet; N1 to N4 are run on day 41.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** N1 to N4 (K-LS1-1, run on day 41; unit plan field 5). They are asked aloud and marked on the child's own mat on the one scale (shown, shown with help, not yet seen). The panels (a caretaker's delivery log, a recovery sequence, meal tags) are withheld until day 41 and use other animals, plants and scenes than days 32 to 35. Teacher keys and look-fors are in unit plan field 5. Spoken forms, one ask each (the unit plan's two-part stems are split):
- **N1a (mat).** "Put a water drop on every picture where something is taking in water." Answer: the plant, the mouse, the rabbit and the child, in both rounds. Wrong: no drop on the plant (A). **N1b (heard).** "What did all of these need?" Answer: water.
- **N2a (cards).** "The watering stayed the same. Touch what changed after unpacking." Answer: the light was there. **N2b (heard).** "What else does the plant need?" Answer: light. Wrong: "water is enough" (B).
- **N3a (cards).** "Touch every picture where something eats a meal." Answer: mouse, rabbit, child. Wrong: touches the plant (C). **N3b (heard).** "The cart label says soil is the plant's meal. Is it right? Touch yes or no." Answer: no. Wrong: yes (C).
- **N4a (cards).** "Match each meal tag to who ate it." **N4b (heard).** "Do they all eat the same? Touch yes or no." Answer: no, and the day's one brief reason for the code: points to two meal tags that differ and says why. Wrong: yes (D). N1b to N3b are answered by pointing or touching, all at once; any child may also say the word.

**Evidence form.** Asked aloud, picture cards, a rotating six heard on exit days, a "heard" column. Exit panels are marked "invented for practice" and withheld until the exit. Exits are unscored.

**Exit questions.** If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example (field 4).
- **E32a (day 32).** Invented: a roof garden with pots and two birds, before and after rain. "Point to something in the roof garden that is like our plant by the window." Answer: any potted plant. No letter: first ideas, kept for the question board.
- **E32b (day 32).** Same pictures. "Say one thing you wonder about the plants in the pots." Answer: any wondering; it goes on the question board. A child who says plants need nothing is logged under A.
- **E33a (day 33).** Invented cards: a sunflower being watered and a goat drinking at a trough. "Tell me one thing both of them need." Answer: water, or a drink, for both. Wrong: only the goat needs water, "plants do not drink" (A).
- **E33b (day 33).** Two sunflower pots, both watered; one stands in a closed box. "Touch the pot that looks well." Answer: the pot in the light. Wrong: the boxed pot or "both", "water is enough" (B).
- **E34a (day 34).** Invented cards: a goat eating grass and a sunflower being watered. "Touch the one that eats." Answer: the goat. Wrong: touches the sunflower, "it is eating the water" (C).
- **E34b (day 34).** Invented: a cow with hay, a cat with fish. "Do the cow and the cat eat the same? Touch yes or no." Answer: no. Wrong: yes (D).
- **E35a (day 35, section exit).** Invented: two bushes on a hillside, one in the open sun and one under a thick roof of branches. "Touch the bush that gets the light it needs." Answer: the bush in the open sun. A miss is "shown with help", no letter.
- **E35b (day 35).** Same picture. "The chipmunk eats seeds. Tell me what the hiker eats." Answer: a sandwich. Wrong: seeds, the same (D).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Unit 2's pattern sheet and picture rows, and unit 1's keep, change, watch habit, stay on the wall (review). Day 32 extends the class picture chart into the first needs-and-places drawing. Day 33 revises the pattern sheet with a water row and a light mark; day 34 adds the eats row; day 35 checks the three rows against a second place and ticks them. The chart and sheet stay up for 4.2.
- **Key moment 1, day 33: the two class plants in dated photographs.** Ideal instrument: the plants themselves, watched for weeks by the teacher. Partly fits: spider plants change slowly, so the difference may be small. Say what the photographs show. If nothing clear shows, say so aloud ("we do not see a clear difference; that is a result too") and add the invented second pot record, said aloud to be invented. Never keep a plant in the dark longer to get a result. The plants were set up before the unit (a named set-up with weeks of lead time); the teacher alone handles pots and watering.
- **Key moment 2, day 34: animals eating.** Ideal instrument: the animals themselves. Fits as pictures only: no class animal, no feeding, no food in the room.
- **Key moment 3, day 35: a second place.** Ideal instrument: a walk to the water. Fits as dated pictures. If the local pictures lack a recorded date, place, creator and permission, use the invented "park pond" set, said aloud to be invented. Names no child.
- **Equipment and minutes.** Six table sets (picture cards, crayons, one sheet). The plants stay at the front. Turns: a rotating six, about one minute each, inside the sort or share part; the rest of the class draws or sorts. Wipes: one each from the table tub, counted in the pack minutes.
- **A room with nothing.** The dated plant photographs and the picture cards, with the teacher saying aloud that no plant is in the room.

## 6. Days

## Day 32


- **5 min. Hook.** The saved fall photographs (the weed in the sidewalk crack, the squirrel's hole) stand beside the two plants. Ask the compelling question aloud before saying anything about the crack or the hole. Children say what they see; no explanation yet.
- **3 min. Retrieval, unscored.** Three retained weather rows from unit 2. Each child marks on paper the picture that repeats; "What makes it a pattern?" (review: a pattern is what shows up again and again). A miss: line up matching pictures during the chart work, then offer another row.
- **5 min. Notice.** In pairs children point to what they notice (green leaves, a crack, a hole). The teacher writes each notice as a question on the question board. A rotating six is heard.
- **5 min. Two plants.** The teacher shows the dated photographs of the window plant and the shelf plant, both watered, and holds each pot up for a look; children stay seated. Children say which looks different. The teacher says what the photographs show, no more.
- **6 min. First drawing.** Each child draws the weed, the squirrel or a plant and one thing they guess it needs, and tells it; the teacher labels what each child says. These go on the class picture chart as the first needs-and-places drawing. A guess is not marked right or wrong.
- **3 min. Exit.** E32a and E32b.
- **3 min. Pack.** Cards into envelopes; one wipe each from the table tub.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice circles the picture that repeats in three weather rows and draws a living thing with a guessed need; the exit is a new roof garden pictured before and after rain, with one pointing ask and one spoken wonder, and no circling and no drawing.

Held by: teacher (photographs, chart, question board); children (drawing). Tool: None. By hand: drawing, question board. Evidence: E32a, E32b (unscored). Hand-backs out: none; first ideas stay as questions.

## Day 33


- **3 min. Warm-up.** Review: touch the pattern sheet from unit 2 and say aloud, with a hand motion, "a pattern is what shows up again and again."
- **4 min. Guess.** Pip (one line at a time) asks "What does a plant need?" Each child draws one thing as a guess (review: a guess is what we think before we look). First ideas about water and light are heard here.
- **6 min. Two plants again.** The dated photographs of the window plant and the shelf plant laid side by side, as rows are in unit 2. Both were watered. Children point to which is greener. The teacher says "the shelf plant had no light for a while and is back in the light now." Said aloud: this is these two plants over these weeks, not every plant.
- **6 min. Water records.** Pip's forgotten mint pot (invented, said aloud: Pip forgets the water, the mint droops, Pip waters it, it stands up), the squirrel drinking photograph (credit on the card) and a robin at a birdbath (original drawing). Each pair puts a water-drop mark on every picture where a living thing takes in water.
- **5 min. Pattern sheet.** Drop-marked pictures go in a water row; a sun mark goes beside the two plant photographs. Count the water row, touching each once. "What do all of these need?" The sheet now reads: water for plants and animals; light for plants. Each table says one line aloud to the teacher.
- **3 min. Exit.** E33a and E33b.
- **3 min. Pack.** Cards into envelopes; one wipe each.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice marks a drop on every picture where a living thing takes in water, sorted by touching; the exit gives two living things and asks the child to say the one need they share, a spoken general statement with no marking and no sorting.

Held by: teacher (sheet, plants); children (drop marks). Tool: None. By hand: drop and sun marks. Evidence: E33a, E33b. Hand-backs out: A and B to day 34.

## Day 34


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 33 exit, errors A and B. Pictures side by side (the pot drooping then watered; the two class plants). If fewer than a third missed, a small group with the teacher while the rest do a second sort.
- **3 min. Hook.** Pip asks "Does the squirrel eat like the plant?" Children say what they think.
- **8 min. Sort.** Six tables sort picture cards into eats and does not eat: a squirrel with an acorn, a cottontail with clover (original drawings if the park photograph is not credited), a person with apple slices at snack (the teacher's own snack, never a child's lunch), the class plant being watered, the shelf plant in light. Count each row, touching each once. The teacher says "animals eat; a plant is not fed a meal; it took in water and light." No food is in the room.
- **6 min. Different animals.** Match each animal to its food card. "Do they all eat the same?" Rows laid side by side. A rotating six says which two differ.
- **3 min. Chart.** The eats row goes on the pattern sheet; the class picture chart gets the pictures.
- **3 min. Exit.** E34a and E34b.
- **2 min. Pack.** Wipes; cards into envelopes.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice sorts many cards into eats and does not eat and draws a bite on each; the exit gives only two new cards and asks which one eats, a single choice between an animal and a plant, with no sort and no marks.

Held by: teacher (sheet); children (sorts). Tool: None. By hand: sort cards. Evidence: E34a, E34b. Hand-backs out: C and D to day 35.

## Day 35


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 34 exit, errors C and D. Two pictures side by side each; the larger error group with the teacher, the others use repair cards.
- **3 min. Warm-up.** Touch the three rows on the pattern sheet and say each aloud: water, light for plants, animals eat.
- **4 min. Look at the place.** Dated pictures of the water near town (plants at the edge, birds, a person with a bottle), each labeled with date and credit, as another observer's record from unit 2. No home or child is pictured. If these are not recorded, the invented "park pond" set is used and said aloud to be invented.
- **9 min. Sort.** Pairs sort the observation cards onto the three rows: water, light for plants, food for animals. They tick a row when the place supplies it. A rotating six explains one card aloud.
- **4 min. Check the sheet.** Do the same patterns hold in this second place? The teacher ticks the pattern sheet. One thing the place does not show is put on the question board.
- **3 min. Exit.** E35a and E35b (section exit).
- **2 min. Pack.** Wipes; cards.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice sorts a park pond's cards onto the water, light and food rows and draws a drop, a sun and a bite; the exit touches the bush that gets the light it needs and says what a hiker eats, for a hillside the sorts never used, with no row to sort onto.

Held by: teacher (sheet, ticks); children (sorts). Tool: None. By hand: sort cards. Evidence: E35a, E35b. Hand-backs out: D to day 36's warm-up.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 33, "What do all of these need?"; day 34, "Do they all eat the same?"; day 35, "Does this place have what they need?"
- Written task: none. The pattern sheet is scribed by the teacher; on day 32 the teacher labels each child's spoken drawing.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: the child names a need and points to the picture that shows it; says plants take in water as animals do; says animals eat and a plant is not fed a meal.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 32 retrieves unit 2's pattern. Day 33 pulls the pattern sheet, side-by-side rows and touch-each-once counting from unit 2, and the guess from unit 1. Day 34 pulls counting and sorting rows. Day 35 pulls all three patterns, the labeled, credited picture record (another observer's record) from unit 2, and the exit routine from unit 1. Every day uses the rotating six and the one-wipe clean-up from earlier units.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: water, light, eat, need, plant, animal, same, different. Frames: "It needs ___." "I saw ___." Home-language answers are accepted on every item.
- **Access.** Tactile drop, sun and bite marks; a partner may place a mark, but the child decides where. Pointing and communication symbols replace speaking or drawing.
- **Check accommodations.** Read every prompt aloud; enlarge cards; accept pointing, dictation and home-language reasons; record help as "shown with help".
- **Reading load.** None. All words are spoken.
- **Extension, same standard.** Sort a new card onto the right row and say which picture shows it.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 32: look at a plant near a window and a picture of a weed or a hole outside and ask "What do you think it needs?" Day 33: look at any plant in the room and a bird or squirrel through a window; do not move or deprive any plant. Day 34: sort four picture cards into eats and does not eat. Day 35: look at a puddle or pond picture and ask which needs it could meet. Use only what is on hand; no purchase.
- **One projector.** Show the photographs and sort cards large on days 32 to 35; the marks and sorts are on paper.
