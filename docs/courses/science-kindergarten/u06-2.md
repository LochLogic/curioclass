**Unit assessment items owned:** T1 to T3 (days 60 and 61), unit plan field 5. Each child has an own mat and marks with the group; the teacher hears one brief reason per child and sees every mark. Stems are split so one ask is one item. The scene is invented, said aloud to be invented, and not taught before any group's turn.

**Days 60 and 61, T1 to T3 (held back).** Invented: a schoolyard picture where people take a shortcut across small plants; footprints and bent stems cross the bed; no route is drawn. Each group has 7 minutes: T1 1.5, T2 2.5, T3 2, feedback and revision 1.
- **T1a (mat).** "Touch what people do here." Answer: the footprints, walking across the plants.
- **T1b (mat).** "Touch what changes." Answer: the bent or flattened plants. Wrong: "walking cannot change the plants" (G).
- **T2a (mat).** "Draw a solution for this place." **T2b (mat).** "Draw what it would change." Answer: a different way for feet to go (a hard path, a way around) with the plants left upright. Wrong: the way moved across another part of the same plants, "now they are safe" (H).
- **T3 (heard, partner).** "Tell your partner why your solution helps. Use your drawing." Answer: the action and the part it spares, "Use this path so feet do not bend the plants." Wrong: only decoration, "a pretty border tells people what to do" (D). Any feasible solution with a defensible link is accepted, not only the example.

**Exits.**
- **E59a (day 59).** Invented: two areas with before and after counts, five then two where a reminder was added, seven then four where none was. "Touch more or fewer wrappers, for the first area and then the second." Answer: fewer in both. The teacher checks the card's numbers against the class tallies and changes the card if they match.
- **E59b.** Pip says: "Only the reminder made litter go down." "Change what Pip says, using both areas." Answer: "Both went down, even without a reminder; we cannot tell it was only the reminder." Wrong: "the reminder did it, the numbers went down" (E).
- **E61a (day 61, section exit, not scored).** Invented: an open bin spilling paper toward water. "Draw a change that keeps the paper in." Answer: a feasible action, such as an adult closing the lid. Wrong: paper tucked under the shrubs (C).
- **E61b.** "Tell why it helps." Answer: "If we ___, then less paper reaches the water." Wrong: "a nice border shows people what to do" (D).

## 7. Talk and writing

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: before, after, fewer, more, path, protect. Frames: "Before there were ___, after there were ___." "We cannot tell yet, because ___." "Use this path so ___."
- **Access.** Large action cards; a child may direct an adult to draw; the teacher records the child's choices and the help. A child who cannot go outside on day 59 uses the photograph set.
- **Check accommodations.** Read every prompt aloud; accept pointing, home-language speech, dictation and a partner's gesture; never score reading, spelling, speech or neatness; record help as "shown with help".
- **Reading load.** None.
- **Extension, same standard.** Plan two solutions for the same place and say which spares more.
