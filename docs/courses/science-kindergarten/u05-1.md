# Section 5.1: Sunlight warms surfaces, and a cover keeps it off

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **K-PS3-1, this section's share (days 44 to 46, 48).** "Observe how sunlight affects surfaces on Earth, such as sand, soil, rock and water." Children touch matched sun-and-shade pairs of sand, soil, rock and water with the back of a hand and say warmer, cooler or about the same.
- **K-PS3-2, day 47.** "Design and build, with given tools and materials, a structure such as a shade or tent that keeps sunlight from warming a spot as much." Day 47 only defines the problem and the cover's job and tries cardboard shadows. The build is in 5.2.
- **Limits that bite here.** No degrees, particles or insulation. Each material is compared with itself, never one material with another. A tie is a result and is written "about the same". Afternoon is not always warmer. Shade is where sunlight is blocked, not where it must feel cold. Never look at the sun. The teacher alone fills trays and checks every surface; children touch only cleared surfaces, briefly.
- **Connections.** Math K.MD.A.2 (compare two things: warmer or cooler), days 44 to 46 and 48. Unit 4's "I think ___, because I saw ___", days 44 to 48.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Why a pair must be alike in material and in time (a fair test, said with keep, change, watch); that the hand can tell warmer from cooler but not how much; that sunlight reaching a surface warms it, and a roof between the sun and the spot stops that.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none. W1 to W3 and D1 to D3 belong to 5.2. Every exit below is spoken, with picture cards that are invented for practice (said aloud); each child marks and points at the same time while the teacher hears a rotating six. Rule 4: none of these panels is a taught case.

- **E44a (day 44).** Invented: a patch of soil by a wall, first in the wall's shade, then in the sun with a warm mark. "Touch the picture that shows what made the soil warmer." Cards: sunlight on the soil, the wall, a clock. Answer: sunlight on the soil. Wrong: "nothing, soil gets warm by itself" (A); touches the clock, "it was later" (H).
- **E44b.** Same pictures. "If the wall shade came back over the soil, what would the soil feel like?" Answer: cooler. Wrong: "the same because it is later" (H); "the sun cannot change soil" (A).
- **E45a (day 45).** Invented flower-bed cards. Card 1: two pots of the same soil put out at the same moment, one sunny, one shaded. Card 2: a pot of soil in the sun and a pot of sand in the shade. Replaced: the card shows two bowls and a heap of wood chips. "Say what Pip keeps the same." Answer: the same chips and the same time; only sun or shade changes. Wrong: sand in one bowl (C); one put out earlier (G).
- **E45b.** Card 1: two bowls of sand put out at the same moment. Card 2: sand that has sat in the sun all morning beside sand put in the shade a minute ago. "Touch the pair that is fair." Answer: card 1. Wrong: card 2, "the long one shows it best" (G).
- **E46a (day 46).** Invented rooftop cards read aloud: water in a tub in the sun and in the shade; the caption says the sunny water was warmer. "Say the sentence: The ___ tub was warmer, so the sun warms ___ too." Answer: the sunny tub; the sun warms water too. Wrong: says "water never gets warm in the sun" (B).
- **E46b.** Rock card: a sunny rock and a shaded rock at an early look and a later look; both are warmer later, and sunny is warmer than shaded at each look. "Touch the two rocks to compare to see what the sun did." Answer: sunny and shaded at the same look. Wrong: the same rock early and later, "later makes it warm" (H).
- **E47a (day 47).** Invented: a striped awning standing beside a clay path bench in the sun. "Tell me what to do with the awning to keep the sun off the bench." Answer: move it over the bench. Wrong: leave it beside, "it is nearer" (D); paint it, "it is prettier" (E).
- **E47b.** Same picture, awning now over the bench. "Point to the part of the awning that keeps the sunlight off." Answer: the roof overhead. Wrong: points to the stripes, "the colours do it" (E).
- **E48a (day 48, section exit).** Invented: a wooden garden step in the sun and a matching step in the shade, set out together, no marks. "Touch the one you think feels warmer." Answer: the sunny step. A wrong touch is "shown with help", no letter.
- **E48b.** Two tubs of water, one sunny and one shaded, the same warm mark over each. "Touch the card that says what the marks show." Cards: sunny warmer, shaded warmer, about the same. Answer: about the same. Wrong: says "water cannot warm in the sun" (B); touching a warmer card is shown with help.
- **E48c.** A wooden fence post in the sun with a warm mark and a matching post in the shade with none. "Finish the sentence: the sun ___ the post." Answer: warms. Wrong: "does not warm" (A).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The sunlight-to-surface picture model and the evidence sentence start on day 44, take sand and soil on day 45, rock and water on day 46 and are checked at a second place on day 48. Cause and effect (sunlight causes warming) is the thread. The design-cycle chart is brought back on day 47 and gets the shade problem. All stay on the wall into 5.2.
- **Review, in the ledger's words.** Keep, change, watch (unit 1); problem, try an idea, test it (unit 3); "I think ___, because I saw ___" (unit 4); about the same; rotating six heard; exit with picture cards; return and repair; picture-caption stickers. None is taught as new.
- **Key moment 1, days 44 to 48: feeling a matched pair.** Ideal instrument: a thermometer would give degrees, which the limits forbid. The back of a hand tells warmer, cooler or about the same only: a difference too small to feel is recorded "about the same". Fits. Fallback with no sun or a surface too hot: dated matched photographs with the observer's recorded comparison, said aloud to be weaker; never infer warmth from brightness.
- **Key moment 2, day 47: seeing where the sunlight is blocked.** Ideal instrument: the sun and a cardboard roof. Fits. With no sun, the teacher shows one lamp shadow as a labelled demonstration.
- **Equipment and minutes.** Days 45, 46 and 48: six groups at once, each with its own pairs, so no queue; five children touch four samples at about 8 seconds each, about 3 minutes with marking. Day 44: two lines of fifteen at two slides, about 2.5 minutes. Day 47: one sunny spot, six turns of 1.5 minutes. Hand-washing for thirty children at two sinks takes about 5 minutes, so tables use wipes and the sink wash joins the next routine wash.
- **A room with nothing.** The dated photograph records; the toy-animal shadow done with a lamp.

## 6. Days

## Day 44


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Hand-back: unit 4's argument captions, held for this first meeting after day 43. The teacher reads the three caption strips aloud once; each child revises their own argument mat from the caption that fits it, at most two, alone. No new interview.
- **3 min. Retrieval.** Three questions, one each, with retained charts: "What changed the ball's motion?" "After trying the ramp, what did we do?" "Say: I think ___, because I saw ___." Hear a rotating six. A miss is pointed back to that chart during the touch.
- **10 min. Out and touch the pair.** Before class the teacher checks both slides (or benches) with the back of a hand and clears them; a surface too hot is replaced by the dated photograph record. Two minutes out; two lines of fifteen, one at each slide; each child touches the sunny and the shaded surface for a count of three. The teacher asks: "Why is the slide in the sun warmer than the one under the tree?" Never look at the sun; water break. Two minutes back.
- **4 min. Question board.** Notices become questions: what is different, how can we compare fairly (45), does water do the same (46).
- **5 min. Start the picture model.** Children direct the teacher: sun, an arrow to the sunny slide, none to the shaded one. Each child draws it on their mat. The class sentence is tentative: "I think the sunny slide is warmer, because the sun is on it and I felt it."
- **3 min. Exit.** E44a, E44b.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice has each child touch a real sunny and shaded surface for a count of three and draw the sun with an arrow; the exit is two invented pictures of soil by a wall, a card touch about what made it warmer and a spoken answer about what shade would do, with no touching of surfaces and no drawing.

Held by: teacher (chart); children (own mat). Tool: None. By hand: mat, touch. Evidence: E44a, E44b. Hand-backs out: A and H to day 45.

## Day 45


- **3 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 44 exit, A and H. In pairs, 1.5 minutes each: trace the sunlight to the warmer picture; compare sun and shade at one look.
- **2 min. Warm-up.** Say "I think ___, because I saw ___" about the slides (review).
- **6 min. Fair pairs.** Review keep, change, watch. New words: pair (two samples alike except sun or shade) and look (a time we touch and record). Three pictured pairs of a sandpit and a garden bed: same material and time (fair); sand beside soil (unfair); one set out long before the other (unfair). Children point to the fair one. "Keep the material. Keep the time. Change only sun or shade. Watch which feels warmer."
- **3 min. Plan the afternoon look.** The class tells the teacher what to keep. The teacher alone sets out the afternoon pairs and photographs them with a recorded comparison (a set-up, out of the lesson).
- **8 min. Touch the pairs.** Each group of five has a sand pair and a soil pair, set out at the morning look and sitting at least 30 minutes (ours), cleared by the teacher. Each child touches the four samples and marks warm, cool or equal on the mat.
- **2 min. Add to the model.** Sand and soil rows join the picture model; the sentence names what was seen.
- **3 min. Exit.** E45a, E45b.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice points to the fair card and touches the four sand and soil samples; the exit says what to keep the same for a new pair of bowls, aloud and cold, then touches a pair.
- **3 min. Clean-up.** Samples to tubs, hand wipes at tables.

Held by: children (own mat). Tool: None. By hand: touch and marks. Evidence: E45a, E45b. Hand-backs out: C and G to day 46.

## Day 46


- **4 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 45 exit, C and G, 2 minutes each: swap one sample for the same material; match the time.
- **3 min. Afternoon record.** The teacher shows day 45's afternoon-look photographs with the recorded comparison read aloud. Children point to the warmer one; "about the same" is a fine answer.
- **9 min. Touch rock and water.** Each group has a rock pair and a shallow water pair, cleared by the teacher. No pouring. Each child touches four samples.
- **3 min. Mark and say.** Marks on the mat; hear a rotating six give one reason.
- **5 min. Revise the model.** Rock and water rows join sand and soil. The class dictates a revised sentence only as far as the records show; a water tie stays "about the same, look again".
- **3 min. Exit.** E46a, E46b.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice points to the warmer sample and marks the rock and water pairs the child touched; the exit says a sentence that adds water to what the sun warms, for a card no one touched, aloud.
- **3 min. Clean-up.** Table cloths for spills; the teacher empties the trays; hand wipes.

Held by: children (own mat). Tool: None. By hand: touch, marks. Evidence: E46a, E46b. Hand-backs out: B and H to day 47.

## Day 47


- **4 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 46 exit, B and H, 2 minutes each: put the two water records side by side; sun and shade at one look.
- **3 min. Warm-up.** The design-cycle chart, said with hand motions: what is the problem, try an idea, test it (review).
- **4 min. The problem.** The teacher sets the large toy animal on a rock in the sun. What do people want to be different? Less sunlight on this spot. A goal card goes up: "keep the sun off this spot". Shade need not feel cold.
- **12 min. Umbrella, canopy, tent.** The teacher has checked card edges and small pieces. At the front sunny spot each group in turn (1.5 minutes) holds a cardboard cover beside, then over, the toy animal in a taped square and watches where the shadow falls. The other groups sort umbrella, canopy and tent cards into "over the top" and "beside" and say which keeps the sun off.
- **2 min. Add to the chart.** The problem, and "a useful shade is over the spot, between the sun and the spot". The roof is the part that does the job; spoken only.
- **3 min. Exit.** E47a, E47b.
- **2 min. Clean-up.** Cardboard and toys to the tub.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice sorts nine cards into over the top and beside and holds a cover beside, then over, a toy animal; the exit is spoken and gives one new picture of an awning beside a bench and asks what to do with it, with no sorting and no holding.

Held by: teacher (chart); children (turns). Tool: None. By hand: cards, cardboard. Evidence: E47a, E47b. Hand-backs out: D and E to day 48.

## Day 48


- **4 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 47 exit, D and E, 2 minutes each: trace the shadow edge onto the patch; choose the covered-and-open comparison over the decoration.
- **2 min. Warm-up.** The class reads its sentence aloud and asks: does it hold at another spot?
- **11 min. Second spot.** Two minutes out, to a second safe sunny edge with matched shade. The teacher has set and cleared two pairs for each group (three groups sand and soil, three groups rock and water). Each child touches both pairs. Two minutes back.
- **4 min. Mark and say.** The group says each pair in relative words; a tie is marked "about the same".
- **3 min. Revise.** If the pattern differs, the class revises the sentence; if not, it says so.
- **3 min. Exit.** E48a, E48b, E48c. Section exit.
- **3 min. Clean-up.** Trays to the teacher; hand wipes.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice has each child touch two real pairs at a second spot and say each pair in relative words; the exit uses invented steps, tubs and posts in pictures and asks for a prediction from the class pattern, a card touch, a tie card and a finished sentence, with no surface touched.

Held by: children (own mat). Tool: None. By hand: touch, marks. Evidence: E48a to E48c. Hand-backs out: A and B to day 49, which has the slot (5.2).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 44, "Why is the slide in the sun warmer than the one under the tree?"; day 45, "What do we keep the same?"; day 47, "What must a shade do?"
- Written task, day 46: none by hand; the class dictates the revised evidence sentence and each child draws it.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: the pair is alike in material and time; the child says warmer, cooler or about the same from what they felt; the reason names sunlight, not the clock.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 44 pulls units 1, 3 and 4 (push or pull, the test, the evidence sentence). Day 45 pulls the evidence sentence and keep, change, watch. Day 46 pulls day 45's fair pair. Day 47 pulls the design cycle. Day 48 pulls the whole sentence.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: sun, shade, warmer, cooler, same, pair. Frames: "The ___ one is warmer." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Home-language answers count.
- **Access.** A child who should not touch a sample points to the cards and uses a partner's report; no child is asked to touch. Enlarged pictures; pointing for speech.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each prompt aloud; accept pointing; record help as "shown with help".
- **Reading load.** None.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a place at school where the sun reaches and one it does not, and say which you expect to be warmer.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Check a step or a bench in sun and in shade yourself first. The child touches both with the back of a hand and says which is warmer and why. Use only what is on hand; no report on the home.
- **One projector.** Show the pair cards large; each child still marks their own paper.
