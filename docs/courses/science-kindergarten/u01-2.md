# Section 1.2: When objects meet, and our own tests

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#science/science-kindergarten/1/s/1.2), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py science-kindergarten u01-2.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## 1. Standards and practices


- **K-PS2-1, this section's share.** "Plan and carry out a fair test comparing how harder or softer pushes and pulls, or pushes in different directions, change how an object moves." Here: contact as the cause of a change (day 5), the children's own one-change test (day 6), a claim qualified to its objects (day 7), and the unit observation of each child planning, carrying out and explaining a new case (days 8 and 9).
- **Limits that bite here.** Strength or which way, never both. Contact only; pushes and pulls without touching (magnets) are left out. No numbers, speed figures, force, friction or energy words. Claims name their objects: "this box on our floor". Farther does not always mean faster.
- **Connections.** Cause and effect (crosscutting), days 5 and 6: a touch is the cause, a change is the effect. K.MD.A.2 (math): side-by-side tape strips, day 7.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** In a bump the objects push on each other, so the moving ball slows or stops as the other starts; children say "it pushed it" and that is enough. A loaded box may not start under a gentle pull: that result is kept and qualifies the claim. Day 8 scores only each child's first unaided record; day 9 feedback and revision never change it. Replace the points in s01 field 5 with the one scale per component (plan point 2).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** items 1 to 6 (s01 field 5), observed on day 8 for four groups and day 9 for two. Each item is one ask, spoken, answered with picture cards or a mark on the mat; item 1 is read as one task, placing the three cards (change, keep, watch) on the mat. Each component is recorded on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Wrong answers by letter are as s01 field 5: item 1 A; 2 D; 3 B; 4 F; 5 D; 6 E.

**Evidence form.** As section 1.1: asked aloud with picture cards, "invented for practice" said first, one ask a stem, the teacher hears a rotating six, and the record sheet has a "heard" column.

**Exit questions.** If many miss one, the next day's warm-up repairs it (day 7's error goes to day 9).
- **E5 (day 5).** "A rolling toy bus rolled toward a toy bus sitting still, and they touched. Say what happened to the bus that was sitting still." Cards: before, the buses apart; touch, the buses touching; after, an empty box. Answer: it began to roll when it was touched. Wrong: "it rolled when it saw the first" (D) or "nothing happened".
- **E6 (day 6).** "Pip guessed that only a strong pull starts a sled. We tried it, and a gentle pull started the sled too, so Pip will test again. Say Pip's next test: I change one thing, ___. I keep ___ the same." Card: a sled on a start tape. Answer: one change, the sled and start kept, the first try kept. Wrong: two changes (A) or "throw that try away" (E).
- **E7 (day 7).** "Pip pulled an empty cart, gently and then harder, and it moved both times. Pip then pulled the same cart with a bag of beans in it. Tell me what happened to the loaded cart on the gentle pull." Cards: the empty cart's two stops; the loaded cart's two stops, the gentle one still at the start tape. Answer: it did not move; the gentle pull left it at the start tape. Wrong: "it moved the same way" (E) or a different rope (A).

**Day 9 response case, invented for practice.** Two spoken items, three minutes. R1: "Touch the two pictures that make a fair check of a harder push on a puck." Pair 1, the same puck, surface, start and way, a gentle then a harder push; pair 2, a soft puck pushed gently and a heavy puck pushed hard. Answer: pair 1. Wrong: pair 2 (A). R2: "Touch the picture where something made the cloth move." Cards: the puck touching the folded cloth; the puck beside it with a gap. Answer: touching. Wrong: the gap card (D). Not scored.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The class picture chart is revised: day 5 adds "a bump can slow, stop or start things, and it only works when they touch"; day 6 keeps the chart's entries the children's tests back and changes the ones they contradict; day 7 narrows the strength claim to the box, the load and the floor tested. From day 8 the chart stays on the wall, and children use it without copying its words. The question board is answered with the children's marks, not told.
- **Key moment, day 5: the order of approach, touch and change.** Ideal instrument: slow-motion replay so each child sees the order. Spec: does not exist in this unit (tools: None). Stand-in: three picture cards (come close, touch, changed) put in order, and slow rolls watched in turn. Fits well enough for kindergarten.
- **Key moment, day 7: the loaded box.** Ideal: the same box and string, the load fixed. Fits. Reading step: one tape piece. If a gentle pull fails to start the box, that is the reading; keep it.
- **Key moments, days 8 and 9: each child's own action.** Ideal: one lane a child, watched by the teacher. Five adjacent lanes with five balls, boxes and strings. Fits. Fallback: the day 1 to 4 photo records are not a stand-in for carrying out; a child who cannot be seen acting is recorded "not yet seen".
- **Equipment and minutes.** Days 5 to 7: three lanes at a time, halves of 6 minutes, five children at two tries of about 35 seconds. Days 8 and 9: seven minutes a group at five lanes (items 1 to 6 take 1 + 1.5 + 1 + 1.5 + 1 + 1 minutes); the teacher hears all five at once. Others draw at the rug within sight.
- **Clean-up.** Days 5 to 7: 2 minutes as in section 1.1. Days 8 and 9: half a minute a group inside the group's seven (balls to bins, one wipe each); the teacher collects the eye protection.
- **A room with nothing.** As section 1.1. Paper balls and scrap boxes; with no eye protection run no trials and say the record is of photographs, which cannot show carrying out.

## 6. Days

## Day 5


- **5 min. Warm-up.** Hand-back: day 4 exit, error F (same ball pushed two ways, tape marks).
- **5 min. Teaching first.** The teacher stops a rolling ball with a flat hand and rolls one ball into another resting ball. Pip asks "Can touching change something that is already moving?" The class says what changed.
- **12 min. Stop it, bump it, pass it, in halves (6 + 6).** Each child rolls one ball into a resting ball, then rolls it past a resting ball with a gap, about 35 seconds a try; the waiting half draws the touch and the change.
- **3 min. Rug.** Order cards: come close, touch, changed. Revise the chart: a bump can slow, stop or start.
- **2 min. Clean-up.**
- **3 min. Exit.** E5.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice draws before, touch and after for the child's own balls; the exit gives the first two boxes for new buses and asks the child to say the empty third, which no practice part asks.

Held by: teacher (chart, order cards); children (strips). Tool: None. By hand: touch strip. Evidence: E5.

## Day 6


- **5 min. Warm-up.** Hand-back: day 5 exit, error D (order cards).
- **4 min. Plan.** Application: each group picks one change by pointing at a card, how hard or which way, never both, and sets what to keep and watch. Scientists find out by looking and testing; groups may choose different tests.
- **12 min. The group's own test, in halves (6 + 6).** Each child makes the two settings of the group's change, about 35 seconds a try; the waiting half marks the picture chart.
- **4 min. Rug.** Each group tells one mark. If marks disagree, repeat once and keep both. The chart keeps what the tests back.
- **2 min. Clean-up.**
- **3 min. Exit.** E6.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice points to one change on the group card for the child's own ball; the exit says a whole one-change plan for a sled the group never tested, aloud.

Held by: teacher (chart); children (group card). Tool: None. By hand: group card and strip. Evidence: E6.

## Day 7


- **5 min. Warm-up.** Hand-back: day 6 exit, error E (repeat one pull, keep both).
- **3 min. Guess.** Practice, nothing new: would a box carrying one large soft block respond to gentle and stronger pulls as the empty box did? Each child draws a guess.
- **12 min. Loaded box pulls, in halves (6 + 6).** Each child pulls the loaded box gently then harder, about 35 seconds a try, and marks the stop; the waiting half compares the strips with day 3's.
- **5 min. Rug.** Strips side by side. The class says the claim for this box and floor; a gentle pull that did not start it stays on the chart.
- **2 min. Clean-up.** The block stays in the box.
- **3 min. Exit.** E7.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice pulls the real loaded box gently and harder and lays the strips side by side to compare them; the exit gives two finished pairs of stops for a cart the class never pulled and asks what happened on one pull, said aloud, with no strips to lay beside each other.

Held by: teacher (chart, day 3 strips); children (guess, strip). Tool: None. By hand: guess drawing, strip. Evidence: E7. Hand-back out: error E to day 9.

## Day 8


- **2 min. Set-up.** Five lanes, five balls, boxes and strings, mats; fitted eye protection on everyone present. A new layout and objects.
- **7 min. Group 1 observed.** Five children at five lanes. The teacher observes planning (item 1), then the actions of items 2 to 6, and hears one reason from each child. No coaching.
- **7 min. Group 2 observed.** As group 1.
- **7 min. Group 3 observed.** As group 1.
- **7 min. Group 4 observed.** As group 1.

Others draw the rug ball before and after at the rug, within sight; a second adult supervises if there is one. No exit, no repair today. Held by: children (own mat). Tool: None. By hand: mat and cards. Evidence: items 1 to 6 for 20 children. The teacher records each component after each group.

## Day 9


- **3 min. Set-up.** As day 8.
- **7 min. Group 5 observed.** As day 8 groups.
- **7 min. Group 6 observed.** As day 8 groups.
- **3 min. Record completion.** The teacher completes each record; the others finish their mats.
- **2 min. Read feedback.** The teacher says aloud, with the child's own marks, what was seen.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** The largest error group works with the teacher on its move; the others use repair cards. Hand-back: day 7 exit, error E.
- **3 min. Fresh case.** R1 and R2 (field 3).
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice is an observed group turn and a completion of the record on the child's own mat; the exit is two invented picture pairs on cards about a puck and a cloth, a choice of which pair is a fair check and which picture shows a touch, with no observed turn and no record.

Held by: teacher (record); children (own mat). Tool: None. By hand: mat. Evidence: items 1 to 6 for 10 children; R1 and R2 are response, not scored.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 5, "What changed when they touched?"; day 6, "Which marks do we keep, and why?"; day 7, "Is it true for this box and this floor?" Days 8 and 9 hear one reason per child.
- Written task: none; day 6, the group card marked by pointing, with a dictated line.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: the child changes one thing; the child's marks show what touched what; the child's reason points to a mark.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 5 warm-up: the day 4 push and its tape marks. Day 6 warm-up: order cards. Day 7 warm-up: matched pulls from day 3. Days 8 and 9 sample days 2 to 7 on new objects. Each day's evidence is spoken or pointed; the record sheet notes "heard".

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: bump, stop, start, touch, gentle, harder, same, change, watch. Frame: "It moved when ___ touched it." Home-language reasons and pointing are accepted on days 8 and 9.
- **Access.** Lanes within reach; adults stabilize equipment but never supply the plan or the action; physical help is recorded separately as "shown with help". A child may direct a partner for the push; the choice remains the child's.
- **Check accommodations.** Read aloud, enlarge pictures, accept pointing, dictated words and home-language reasons.
- **Reading load.** None. All words are spoken.
- **Extension, same standard.** Plan a one-change test with a new object from the box and say what you will keep.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Roll a soft ball into a second ball and pass it by; ask "Which one touched?". Last day of the unit: the child plans a gentle then harder roll and says what stays the same. Use the unit's safety limits.
- **One projector.** Show the order cards and the day's strips; days 8 and 9 are done on the floor and the mat.
