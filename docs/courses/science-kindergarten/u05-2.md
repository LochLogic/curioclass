# Section 5.2: Build a cover and test it

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **K-PS3-2, this section's share (days 49 to 51, 53, 54).** "Design and build, with given tools and materials, a structure such as a shade or tent that keeps sunlight from warming a spot as much." Day 49 builds a group cover, day 50 improves it from evidence, day 53 each child builds their own for a new patch, day 54 judges it.
- **K-PS3-1, days 50 and 52.** "Observe how sunlight affects surfaces on Earth, such as sand, soil, rock and water." Day 50 compares covered and open matches; day 52 observes every child on sand, soil, rock and water from unfamiliar dated cards.
- **Limits that bite here.** Compare a covered patch only with its open match of the same material, started alike and left out for the same time. No degrees; a cover need not make a spot cool, only warm it less; a tie is "no clear difference" and is not turned into a result. Afternoon is not always warmer. Structure and function is spoken only: the roof is the part that blocks sunlight. No roof template. Scissors are blunt; the teacher pre-cuts hard pieces. Never look at the sun.
- **Connections.** Math K.MD.A.2 (compare covered and open: warmer or cooler), days 50, 51, 54. Unit 4's "I think ___, because I saw ___", days 49 to 54.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to read a covered-and-open pair as a fair test; why "it works because it is nice" is not evidence; how to give feedback with a picture sticker before any mark counts; why no one is scored on work made and marked in one period.

## 2. Where it sits


Before: 5.1 ends on day 48 with a hand-back that day 49 repairs (a slot is named there). After: the end of unit 5; day 55 follows. No readiness check. Results go to the record sheet on the one scale; points are assigned later in teacher planning, as unit plan field 5 sets.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** W1 to W3 (day 52), D1 and D2 (day 53), D3 (day 54), unit plan field 5. Each child has a mat and marks at the same time as the group; the teacher hears one brief reason from each child per code and sees every other mark. Stems are split so one ask is one item. Cards are invented for practice and said so aloud; the dated sun-and-shade captions on day 52 are real records and are never changed to match an answer. Teacher keys, look-fors and points are in unit plan field 5.

**Day 52, W1 to W3 (held back, never taught from).** Each group has 4 minutes: W1 0.25, W2 1.25, W3 1.5, feedback and revision 0.75 (group 1's minutes also hold the opening line). Each heard reason gets about 15 seconds.
- **W1a (cards).** "Touch the fair pair." Two equal sand samples, sunny and shaded, same interval, beside an unmatched sunny rock and shaded water. Answer: the sand pair. Wrong: the rock and water pair, "they prove it" (C).
- **W1b (mat).** "Mark which sand sample felt warmer." Answer: the mark the record gives, or "about the same".
- **W2a to W2d (heard, one reason per child, rotated).** Number the children 1 to 30 down the record sheet; each child gives one W2 reason, on the material that count sets (sand, soil, rock, water, then sand again), so every material is heard about seven or eight times across the class. Every child still compares all four materials by touch in W3a. The child touches that card. "Tell me one reason the sunny sand (or soil, rock, water) and the shaded one felt the way the cards show." Answer: a reason that fits the dated captions: the sun reached the sunny one and the shade blocked it, or, for a tie at both looks, "we need to look again". Wrong: "the sun cannot warm it" (A; for water, B, only when the child says so); "it was warmer because it was the afternoon" (H).
- **W3a (mat).** "Touch the earlier or later look for each sunny sample." Answer: as the records say; no claim that later always wins.
- **W3b (heard).** "Which pair shows the sun did it?" Answer: a sunny and a shaded match at one look. Wrong: "the sun does not warm things" (A); "the later one is warmer, so sunlight did it" (H). If every pair is a tie, the answer is "we need to look again".

**Day 53, D1 and D2 (held back).** Four minutes set-up, six for D1, twenty for the build.
- **D1a (mat).** "Touch the cover that keeps the sun off your patch." Two pictured covers: on posts over the patch, and standing beside it. Answer: over. Wrong: beside (D).
- **D1b (mat).** "Touch two pieces from the pile you will use, and leave the side open to look." Answer: two pieces, a roof and a support.
- **D2a (build).** "Build your cover over your patch." **D2b (heard).** "Show how your cover helps this patch. Will it be warmer or less warm?" Answer: the roof blocks sunlight so it should warm less. Wrong: "the colors make it work" (E); "it will be cool as soon as it is on" (F).

**Day 54, D3 (held back).** Three minutes a group.
- **D3a (mat).** "Touch your covered patch and your open patch. Mark which felt warmer." **D3b (heard).** "Why? Point to the part of your cover." Answer: the open patch was warmer; the roof kept sunlight off the other. Wrong: "it works because it is the nicest" (E).

**Exits.** Spoken, invented, same rules as 5.1.
- **E49a (day 49).** Invented: a seed tray with its plants in a box; one cover on posts over the whole tray, one on posts over the empty path beside it, one leaning against its side. Replaced: one picture, a flat board on posts over a box of seedlings. "Draw the sun and the shadow the board makes." Answer: the sun above, the shadow under the board on the seedlings. Wrong: the shadow beside the box (D).
- **E49b.** A crate under a roof and a matching crate in the open, put out together and left in the sun the same time. "Which crate will feel less warm? Say: I think the ___ crate, because ___." Answer: the crate under the roof, because the roof keeps the sun off it. Wrong: "it was cool the moment the roof went on" (F).
- **E50a (day 50).** Invented: a balcony cover that leaves half its target in the sun; the card says the open half was warmer. "Touch the change that fixes it." Cards: stretch the roof over the open half; paint flowers on the roof; stand the cover farther away. Answer: stretch the roof. Wrong: flowers, "it will look better so it works" (E); the farther one (D).
- **E50b.** "Say: I think ___, because I saw ___." Answer: the open half needs cover, because the card says it was warmer. Wrong: "because it is nicest" (E).
- **E51a (day 51).** Invented: a canopy whose shadow misses a stone seat; the caption says the open seat was warmer than its covered match. "Touch yes or no: is the seat that the shadow misses shaded?" Answer: no. Wrong: yes, "the canopy is close by" (D).
- **E51b.** "Why does the card say the open seat was warmer?" Answer: the sun reached it. Wrong: "because it was later in the day" (H).
- **E54a (day 54, section exit, not scored).** Invented: a doll's umbrella leaning beside a doll's blanket in the open; the caption says the open blanket was warmer than its shaded match. Cards: move the umbrella over the blanket; add a ribbon; stand the umbrella farther away. "Touch the move that fixes it." Answer: move it over the blanket. Wrong: add decoration (E); farther away (D).
- **E54b.** "Why does moving the umbrella help?" Answer: it keeps the sun off the blanket. Wrong: "the umbrella looks nice" (E).
- **Days 52 and 53 have no separate exit.** W1 to W3 and D1 and D2 are their evidence.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The picture model and evidence sentence from 5.1 are revised on day 50 from the covered-patch results and checked elsewhere on day 51; day 52 agrees the explanation of the slide in the sun from the records. The design-cycle chart gets a trial, a comparison and an improvement on days 49 and 50; day 54's response agrees it. Review in the ledger's words: problem, try an idea, test it; worked, did not work; one change, then retest; keep, change, watch; "I think ___, because I saw ___"; picture-caption sticker; observation sitting with own mat, six groups in turn; return and repair; the independent familiar drawing while one group is observed.
- **Key moment 1, days 50, 51 and 54: a covered patch against its open match.** Ideal instrument: matched samples started alike, left out the same time, touched with the back of a hand. Fits. The step is warmer, cooler or about the same; a smaller difference is "no clear difference". Samples start alike (the teacher touches both before they go out) and the open control sits beyond the cover's shadow. Fallback, no sun: the retained dated records, labeled as media; the child still builds and checks coverage, and the inference is written as media-based, not a test of that child's cover.
- **Key moment 2, day 53: each child's own build.** Ideal instrument: the teacher at each child's side. Partly fits. One teacher, thirty children: 30 seconds each is 15 minutes, so a three-look-for checklist is used and the photograph of each revised build is taken in teacher planning time before the cover is set out. A second adult, if present, takes half the room.
- **Equipment and minutes.** Day 49: six covers, one per group, five turns of 2 minutes. Two scissors per group; one cutter at a time. Day 53: sixty sample containers (thirty targets, thirty open matches) are filled by the teacher the day before, outside lesson time. Day 52: paper only.
- **Set-ups.** Each is under 5 minutes and named: the matched rocks at the morning look for days 50 and 51; the day 53 covers set out for day 54.
- **A room with nothing.** Reused cardboard and tape; photograph sets with recorded comparisons in place of outdoor samples, labeled.

## 6. Days

## Day 49


- **4 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 48 exit, A and B, 2 minutes each: trace the sunlight to the warmer picture; put the paired water records side by side.
- **2 min. Warm-up.** The goal card "keep the sun off this spot" and the design-cycle chart, said aloud (review).
- **3 min. The problem and the pile.** A rock with the large toy animal is the spot. The pile: cardboard, paper, tape. No shape is shown.
- **11 min. Build the group cover.** Each group of five makes one cover over its rock. Each child takes a 2-minute turn adding a piece (10 minutes, one scissor in use at a time); one minute to steady it. Hard cuts were done the day before. Work on a low table; rocks kept low.
- **4 min. Guess.** Each child draws the sun and the shadow on their mat and marks where less warming is guessed, saying "I think ___, because ___". Hear a rotating six.
- **3 min. Exit.** E49a, E49b: E49a drawn by all (60 seconds); E49b whispered by all (30 seconds), then the rotating six heard, 10 seconds each.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice builds the group cover and draws the sun and shadow of it; the exit draws them for a given board on posts the group never built, then says which crate will feel less warm.
- **3 min. Clean-up.** Pieces and tape to the tub; covers labeled and shelved. The teacher sets the covered and the open rock out at the next morning look, started alike (a set-up).

Held by: children (the cover). Tool: None. By hand: build, mat. Evidence: E49a, E49b. Hand-backs out: D and F to day 50.

## Day 50


- **4 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 49 exit, D and F, 2 minutes each: trace the shadow edge; compare after a shared time.
- **2 min. Warm-up.** Read the day 49 guesses aloud.
- **8 min. Compare.** Each group has its covered rock and its open match, and a covered and an open shallow water tray, all out the same time (at least 30 minutes, ours) and cleared by the teacher. Each child touches four samples and marks covered cooler, open cooler or no clear difference. The teacher shows a pair out for different times as an unfair example and fixes it at once.
- **5 min. Improve.** Where did the sun still reach? One change, then retest: each group adds or moves one piece, then checks the shadow edge on the patch. Warming is retested on day 51.
- **4 min. Revise the model.** Blocking sunlight causes less warming is added to the chart; the evidence sentence is revised from the touches. A tie stays a tie.
- **3 min. Exit.** E50a, E50b.
- **4 min. Clean-up.** Trays emptied by the teacher, spills wiped, hand wipes.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice has each child touch four real samples that stood out the same time and mark covered cooler, open cooler or no clear difference, then move one piece to improve; the exit gives a balcony card with a caption and asks the child to touch the fix and say a claim from the card, with no sample touched and no piece moved.

Held by: teacher (chart); children (covers, mats). Tool: None. By hand: touch, change. Evidence: E50a, E50b. Hand-backs out: D and E to day 51.

## Day 51


- **4 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 50 exit, D and E, 2 minutes each.
- **4 min. Out and back.** Two minutes each way to a second small patch.
- **9 min. Second patch.** The teacher has set a matched open patch and moved each group's cover over its own patch, started alike, out the same time, at the morning or afternoon look as the timetable falls (afternoon is not always warmer). Each child touches both and marks.
- **4 min. Mark and say.** The group says covered or open, and why, in relative words.
- **3 min. Check the sentence.** Does the class sentence hold at a second place? Revise or say it holds.
- **3 min. Exit.** E51a, E51b.
- **3 min. Clean-up.** Rocks and covers to the tub; hand wipes.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice has each child touch both real patches and mark which is warmer, then the group says covered or open and why; the exit gives a canopy card and asks a yes or no about whether a seat is shaded, then why the card says the open seat was warmer, with no patch touched and no marking.

Held by: children (cover, mat). Tool: None. By hand: touch. Evidence: E51a, E51b. Hand-backs out: D and H to day 52.

## Day 52


- **3 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs: day 51 exit, D and H, 1.5 minutes each. Nothing else is reteached today.
- **3 min. Closing explanation.** From the wall records the class dictates why the slide in the sun is warmer than the one under the tree: the sunlight reaches it, the tree blocks the sunlight, shade need not feel cold. The teacher scribes. The question board is read back and answered questions are marked.
- **Set-up (inside group 1's minutes).** The front table has the unfamiliar dated cards, mats and crayons. The five other groups draw the familiar slide-and-shade picture alone.
- **24 min. Six groups observed, 4 minutes each.** W1 to W3 as in field 3. Five children mark at once. The teacher hears each child's one W2 reason on the rotated material and one W3 reason, about 15 seconds each, and reads each a picture sticker at the end (about 9 seconds each), read aloud once; each child may revise their own mat alone before the group leaves. No coaching.

Held by: children (own mat). Tool: None. By hand: mat, cards. Evidence: W1 to W3 for all thirty children, held back. Sticker feedback now; marks assigned after class and before day 53.

## Day 53


- **4 min. Set-up.** The patches, mats and the pile are out. Each child has their own marked patch (sand, soil, rock or water) and its open match, filled by the teacher. The new problem: shade this patch and keep the side open to look.
- **6 min. Choose.** D1a and D1b on the mat, all at once. Others who finish draw the familiar slide-and-shade picture.
- **20 min. Build.** D2a. Thirty builds at once, five to a group, two scissors a group. 15 minutes of building, with the teacher visiting each child for about 30 seconds, a three-look-for checklist and a sticker; 3 minutes for each child to revise alone; 2 minutes packing, the cover set over its patch. D2b is heard at the visit.

Held by: children (own cover). Tool: None. By hand: build. Evidence: D1 and D2 for all thirty children, held back. The teacher photographs each revised build in planning time; the teacher sets each cover with its open match in the sun at the routine look, started alike, so they have at least 30 minutes (ours) before day 54. Points assigned before day 54.

## Day 54


- **2 min. Set-up.** The covers and their open matches have been out at least 30 minutes; the teacher has checked them.
- **18 min. Six groups observed, 3 minutes each.** D3a and D3b. Each child touches their own covered and open patch at once (about 30 seconds), marks (30 seconds), and gives a brief why (about 20 seconds each); a sticker is placed in about 5 seconds a child. The other groups draw the familiar slide-and-shade picture alone.
- **2 min. Feedback.** The teacher reads the sticker meanings aloud once, so every child hears their own comment.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Independent revision of the day 54 mat and, from the day 52 and 53 stickers, of earlier work. The two largest error groups from the record (at most two) work with the teacher, 2 minutes each: A and B together (paired records), C (swap a sample), H (same look), D (placement), or E or F (a separate evidence repair); everyone else revises the mat from the sticker alone. G is read in the feedback only. No reteaching.
- **3 min. Section exit.** E54a, E54b. Not scored.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice draws the familiar slide-and-shade picture and revises the child's own mat from a sticker; the exit touches the move that fixes a doll's umbrella beside a blanket and says why moving it helps, a new object and a spoken reason the practice never asks.

Held by: children (own cover, mat). Tool: None. By hand: touch, mat. Evidence: D3 for all thirty, held back; E54 is response. The unit's response is counted once, here. D3 points are assigned after the revision, in teacher planning on the next school day.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 49, "What will the roof keep off the spot?"; day 50, "Which patch felt warmer, and why?"; day 54, "How does your cover help your patch?"
- Written task, day 54: none; the child points to their cover and says why; the teacher scribes.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: the roof is over the target; the child compares the covered patch with its open match; the reason names blocked sunlight, not looks or the clock.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 49 pulls the problem and the design cycle. Day 50 pulls the fair pair (keep, change, watch). Day 51 pulls one change, then retest. Day 52 pulls all four materials and the slide anchor. Day 53 pulls day 47's cover job and day 49's build. Day 54 pulls the evidence sentence.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: cover, roof, block, warm less, open, covered. Frames: "The roof keeps the sun off the ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___."
- **Access.** Large easy-fold pieces and adapted scissors; a child directs an adult's help and the teacher records the child's choices and the help. A child who should not touch a sample points and uses a partner's report.
- **Check accommodations.** Read every prompt aloud; accept pointing, home-language speech and dictation; never score neatness; record help as "shown with help".
- **Reading load.** None.
- **Extension, same standard.** Plan a cover for a second material and say which feature blocks the sunlight.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Use a paper roof and one rock or a toy in a sunny window. Hold it beside, then over, and watch the shadow. Compare a covered and an open object after the same time with the back of a hand; check surfaces first. Last day: ask the child to show how their cover helps. Use what is on hand; no report on the home.
- **One projector.** Show the cards large; every child still marks and builds their own.
