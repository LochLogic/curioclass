- **K-ESS2-1:** Observe the local weather, share the observations, and describe the patterns that show up over days and months. Emphasis: Includes describing weather in words and counting sunny, windy or rainy days; patterns such as cooler mornings than afternoons. Limit: Counts use whole numbers only, and temperature uses relative words such as warmer and cooler.
- **K-ESS3-2:** Ask questions to find out why people forecast the weather and how forecasts help a community get ready for and handle dangerous storms. Emphasis: the kinds of severe weather that happen where students live.
