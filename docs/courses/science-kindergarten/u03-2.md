# Section 3.2: Judging a design on a new target

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#science/science-kindergarten/3/s/3.2), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py science-kindergarten u03-2.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header




## 1. Standards and practices


- **K-2-ETS1-1, this section's share (days 25, 27).** "Ask questions, observe and gather information about something people want to change, to name a simple problem a new or better object or tool could solve." Day 25: a problem the class chooses from its own room, defined before any fix. Day 27: each child does it alone on an unfamiliar layout.
- **K-PS2-2, this section's share (days 26, 28).** "Use test results to decide whether a design, such as a ramp or a turning wall, changes an object's speed or direction the way it was meant to." Day 26: a new target the class agrees on, one change, judged from results. Day 28: each child judges a speed result and a direction result on the new layout.
- **Limits that bite here.** As section 3.1: no friction, gravity, measured speed or force numbers; speed is watched, never inferred from where a ball stops. No building a second device on day 25. Assessment days teach nothing new and reteach nothing; the delivery lane and its cards stay hidden until day 27. A design that misses is valid evidence.
- **Connections.** ELA SL.K.3 (ask and answer to get information), days 25 and 27, with the spoken "I want to find out ___". Math K.MD.A.2 (compare distances and speeds directly), day 26, by tape marks and side-by-side watching, no numbers. Cause and effect, days 26 and 28, as review of unit 1.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The same two moves as 3.1, now alone: say what people want to be different, then judge a result against it. A child who defines a problem by naming a favorite tool has not yet separated the want from the fix. Judge each component on its own; a good-looking design that missed the bay does not earn the judging component. Rehearse every group's four or three minutes with a timer the day before; the teacher must hear every child.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** P1 and P2 (day 27), M1 to M3 (day 28), unit plan field 5. The unit plan scores by points; here each component is marked on the one scale (shown, shown with help, not yet seen). Each child has an own mat and marks pictures at the same time as the group; the teacher hears each child. Stems are split so one ask is one item. The layout is a pretend delivery lane, said aloud to be invented; the rolls are real.

**Evidence form.** Asked aloud, picture cards, a rotating six heard on exit days, "heard" column. Teacher keys and look-fors are in unit plan field 5.

**Day 27, P1 and P2 (held back, never taught from).** Each group of five has four minutes: P1 2, P2 2. The teacher shows the lane and a low-block bay beside it, with a taped pretend walking strip: people want balls delivered into the bay without chasing them into the strip.
- **P1a (heard).** The teacher rolls a gentle push that stops short, then a roll that misses the bay. "What would you ask?" Each child asks aloud, words or symbols recorded. Answer: any question a card can answer (what do people want, where should it go, what can we use). Wrong: "build a wall now" (A). A question no card answers is "shown", never an error.
- **P1b (mat).** "Draw where the ball stopped." Answer: a mark at the stop. No register letter; wrong place is "shown with help".
- **P1c (cards).** The child asks the goal card; the teacher answers. "Touch what the goal card said." Answer: the ball in the bay, not in the strip.
- **P2a (mat).** "Draw what people want to be different." Answer: the ball ending in the bay. Wrong: draws a favorite ramp or tool instead (B).
- **P2b (cards).** "Touch each thing a helper must do." Cards: starts faster; turns into the bay; stays low; is tall; is red. Answer: the first three. Wrong: no register letter, "shown with help".
- **P2c (heard).** "Why do we find out first?" Answer: so we know what to test or what people want. Wrong: "build first, find out after" (A).

**Day 28, M1 to M3 (held back).** Each group has three minutes: M1 1.25, M2 0.75, M3 1. Same lane, a fixed low ramp, a pre-cut bumper.
- **M1a (roll, mat).** "Push so it starts faster than your first push." Each child pushes gently, then harder, watched; marks the start-picture cards (slow, faster). Answer: marks match what was seen.
- **M1b (cards).** "Did your change make it start faster? Touch yes or no." Answer: matches the watched start. Wrong: yes, "it went farther" with no start watched (C).
- **M2 (roll, mat).** "Put the bumper where it must touch the ball to send it into the bay. Roll it and draw the path." Answer: a path drawn before and after the touch. Wrong: marks the turn before the touch, near the bumper (D).
- **M3a (heard).** "What made it turn?" Answer: the bumper touched it. Wrong: "the bumper was close" (D).
- **M3b (cards).** "Did your design do what you wanted? Touch yes or no." Answer: matches the recorded path, a miss is "no". Wrong: yes while the mat shows a miss, "it looks nice" (E).

**Exit questions.** If many miss one, the next day's Return and repair covers it; day 26's go to day 28's Return and revise.
- **E25a (day 25).** Invented: an empty supply box tips over on a narrow shelf. "Touch the picture of what the people want to be different." Cards: the box standing on the shelf; a wide new shelf. Answer: the box standing. Wrong: the wide shelf, a fix instead of the want (B).
- **E25b (day 25).** Same picture. "Tell me one thing you could look at to find out why it tips." Answer: any relevant thing to watch (how far it hangs over, how wide the shelf is, what is in the box). Wrong: "build a shelf now" (A). An unrelated question is "shown with help".
- **E26a (day 26).** Invented: a toy tractor must stop in a barn square beside its path; a card guide touches it, and the result picture shows it ends beside the barn. "Say your answer: The guide did or did not do what Pip wanted, because the picture shows ___." Answer: did not, because the tractor ended outside the square. Wrong: yes, "the guide is neat and strong" (E).
- **E26b (day 26).** Same pictures. "Touch the place on the path where it began to turn." Answer: the touch. Wrong: a spot before the touch, near the guide (D).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Unit 1's chart and strip, unit 2's rows, and 3.1's design-cycle chart, path model and cause-effect arrows stay on the wall. Day 25 revises the problem step with the question cards (what is wanted comes first). Day 26 reaches a class consensus on the design-cycle chart from the new-target results. Days 27 and 28 use all of them, unchanged; the records of day 27 are carried unchanged into day 28.
- **Key moment 1, day 25: gathering information about a room problem.** Ideal instrument: the real classroom event, watched and asked about. Fits, as a teacher demonstration (the door, if chosen, only by the teacher, children back; no pinch points handled).
- **Key moment 2, days 27 and 28: each child's own marks.** Ideal: one mat for each child, watched by the teacher. Fits. A component not seen is recorded "not yet seen". Tools are None.
- **Equipment and minutes.** Day 25: six table sets of photograph cards. Day 26: six sets as 3.1, three groups testing, three drawing (4 minutes a half). Days 27 and 28: one supervised lane, six groups in turn, 4 minutes (day 27) and 3 minutes (day 28) each. Day 27's four minutes hold 5 asks, 5 reasons and the marking at the same time as the group. Day 28's three minutes hold 15 short rolls (about 8 seconds each) and 10 spoken answers; this is tight, so rehearse the day before. The independent group draws the familiar ramp and target and touches no ball.
- **Clean-up.** Day 26: wipes from table tubs, bags, eye protection if worn; 3 minutes. Day 27: 4 minutes of change-overs hold packing. Day 28: 2 minutes set-up and packing; the lane is cleared by the teacher.
- **A room with nothing.** Photographs of the layout, the stops and the paths, with the teacher's single ball and card ramp. Children mark the photographs; this cannot show a child rolling.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Hand-back: day 24 exit, error D. Roll past a bumper with a gap, then into it; each child puts a finger on the touch on a drawn path.
- **3 min. Warm-up.** The three steps with a hand motion each (problem, idea, test); the goal and materials cards (review of day 21).
- **5 min. Choose a problem.** The teacher shows three photograph cards of the room: crayons rolling off a tilted tray, a door that closes fast, a chair that slides. The class chooses one (hands, the teacher touches each hand once). Only room things; nothing about homes.
- **8 min. Ask, observe, gather.** The teacher shows the chosen problem (the door only by the teacher, children back). In pairs each child asks one "I want to find out ___" question; the partner points to the card that could answer. A rotating six is heard; a question no card answers goes on the "not on this page" card.
- **4 min. Say the problem.** "People want ___." The teacher scribes it on the problem step of the chart. No building.
- **3 min. Exit.** E25a and E25b.

## Day 26

- **3 min. Agree on the new target.** Two target cards: a line farther down the rug, a square to the side. The class chooses by hands; the teacher touches each hand once. The goal is said first.
- **9 min. Test one change.** Three groups test while three draw, then swap (4 minutes each, 1 to swap). The teacher checks each build. Each child picks one change card (a book more, a harder start, a moved bumper), rolls once and marks reached or not and the path. The target stays inside the cleared area.
- **3 min. Rug: did it do what we wanted?** The rows are read; each group says which wanted change its result shows, speed or direction. Consensus goes on the design-cycle chart.
- **3 min. Exit.** E26a and E26b.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice picks a change card, rolls once and moves a square for the child's own result; the exit says whether a finished result for a tractor and a guide shows the wanted turn, with the reason, aloud.

Held by: teacher (chart); children (result marks). Tool: None. By hand: result marks. Evidence: E26a, E26b. Hand-backs out: errors D and E to day 28 (day 27 has no repair).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 25, "What do you want to find out?"; day 26, "Did our change do what we wanted?"; days 27 and 28, one reason heard from every child.
- Written task: none. Mats are marked by drawing and pointing; the teacher writes a dictated caption on day 27 and day 28.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: the problem names what people want to be different; the judgment matches the recorded result; the touch is named as the cause of a turn.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 25 uses the question cards and the three steps. Day 26 pulls speed-watching and bumper paths from days 22 to 24 and the hands-touch-once vote from unit 2. Days 27 and 28 sample days 20 to 26 on a new layout. Each day's evidence is drawn, pointed or spoken; the record sheet notes "heard".

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: problem, want, idea, test, bay, bumper, turn, worked. Frames: "I want to find out ___." "People want ___." "It worked because ___." Home-language answers are accepted on every item.
- **Access.** Raised or tactile lane and bay edges; a partner may roll for a child who cannot, but the child decides the placement, the mark and the answer. Pointing and communication symbols replace speaking or drawing.
- **Check accommodations.** Read every prompt aloud; enlarge cards; accept pointing, dictation and home-language reasons; record any help given as "shown with help".
- **Reading load.** None. All words are spoken.
- **Extension, same standard.** Name a second helpful feature for the bay and say how a test would show it works.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 25: look at one thing in the house or room that annoys someone (a box that tips) and ask "What do people want to be different?" Last day of the unit: roll a ball toward a cup, a new spot, ask "What do you want?", change one thing, roll, and ask "Did it do what you wanted?". Use only what is on hand; no purchase.
- **One projector.** Show the layout and the photograph cards large on days 25 and 27; the rolling is real and the mats are paper.
