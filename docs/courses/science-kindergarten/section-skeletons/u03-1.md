## 1. Standards and practices

Transfer: a ball-delivery lane with a sideways collection bay, unseen until day 27.

Day 22 revises cause-effect arrows and reached pictures from trials; day 23 adds second-ball evidence. Day 24 adds bumper contact and collision evidence, then an improvement and retest. Day 25 revises the problem step from information questions; day 26 reaches consensus from new-target tests. Use these models in both assessment parts. Judge speed by watching two pushes side by side.
