## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K-PS3-2** (days 49, 50, 51, 53, 54): Design and build, with given tools and materials, a structure such as a shade or tent that keeps sunlight from warming a spot as much.
- **K-PS3-1** (days 50, 52): Observe how sunlight affects surfaces on Earth, such as sand, soil, rock and water.
- skill sk-shade-design (grade K)
- skill sk-sun-warms (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Question board.** Group notices into warmth and covering questions: what differs (44), how can we compare fairly (45), does water behave similarly (46)? If sunlight matters, what could block it (47)? Does the pattern persist elsewhere (48)? Can our cover work (49), and what should change after testing (50)? Does it work elsewhere later (51)? Close answered questions on day 52. Transfer: an unfamiliar outdoor Earth-materials exhibit needing shade, not the playground or toy shelter.
> - **Carried.** Day 44 starts a sunlight-to-surface picture model and tentative evidence sentence. Day 45 adds sand and soil evidence; day 46 revises both from rock and water, day 48 from another location. Day 47 retrieves the design cycle and adds the shade problem and limits; days 49 and 50 add a trial, comparison and improvement. Day 50 revises the sunlight model and evidence sentence from covered-patch results; day 51 checks them elsewhere. Day 52 agrees the warming explanation from these records; day 54's response agrees the design cycle. Keep these for transfer.
