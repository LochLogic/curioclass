## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K-ESS3-2** (days 15, 16, 17, 19): Ask questions to find out why people forecast the weather and how forecasts help a community get ready for and handle dangerous storms.
- **K-ESS2-1** (days 18): Observe the local weather, share the observations, and describe the patterns that show up over days and months.
- skill sk-forecast-severe (grade K)
- skill sk-weather-watch (grade K)

Days 13 and 14 practise matching two rows before day 18; day 19's two-month reading is review.
