## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K-ESS2-2** (days 40, 43): Argue, using evidence, that plants and animals, people included, change their surroundings to get what they need.
- **K-ESS3-1** (days 40, 42): Use a model to show how what a plant or animal, people included, needs is linked to where it lives.
- **K-LS1-1** (days 41): Describe, from observations, the patterns in what plants and animals, people included, need to stay alive.
- skill sk-change-environment (grade K)
- skill sk-needs-places (grade K)
- skill sk-living-needs (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Question board.** Group notices about green leaves, cracks and holes into questions. Which needs repeat (days 33 and 34)? Can another place meet them (35)? That raises: how do place parts supply needs (36 and 37)? If needs depend on places, why change places (38 and 39)? Can people meet needs with less disturbance (40)? Return to unanswered anchor questions on day 41. Transfer: an unfamiliar garden beside a meeting room, reserved for assessment.
> - **Carried.** Day 32 extends the weather-pattern chart into an initial needs-and-places drawing. Days 33 and 34 revise patterns from plant records and food observations; day 35 checks another place. Day 36 links needs to resources; day 37 adds people from the local map. Day 38 starts the evidence sentence from change sequences; day 39 revises a system link using a second sequence. Day 40 adds human changes and a less harmful choice. Day 41 reaches consensus from the collected evidence. Keep all three models available for transfer.
