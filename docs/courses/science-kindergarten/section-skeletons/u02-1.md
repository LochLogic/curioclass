## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K-ESS2-1** (days 10, 11, 12, 13, 14): Observe the local weather, share the observations, and describe the patterns that show up over days and months.
- skill sk-weather-watch (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Teaching moves.** Model one dated entry on day 10 and a question on day 15. Then let children choose evidence, ask questions and revise claims; repeat unclear observations.
>
> - **Question board.** What changed between recesses (days 10 to 12)? Does it repeat (day 13)? What does a whole month show (day 14)? If patterns vary, how can we plan ahead (days 15 and 16)? How do regional patterns and today's information help predict danger (day 17)? Transfer: unfamiliar dated weather records, then a new school outing forecast.
> - **Limits.** No degrees, averages, probability or atmospheric mechanisms. Surface warmth is not air temperature; day 12 cannot establish an air-weather pattern. Categories overlap. A forecast describes possibilities; a warning does not cause a storm. No engineering expectation is owned.
> - **Carried.** Retain unit 1's cause-effect chart. Day 10 begins weather-pattern pictures and a picture graph. Day 12 revises the warmth claim from repeat looks; day 14 adds counted monthly evidence. Day 15 extends cause-effect to forecast information causing preparation. Day 17 revises this chain from regional records and reaches consensus: observations support patterns and predictions, which inform actions. Retain all three representations for assessment. Days 13 and 14 practise matching two rows before day 18; day 19's two-month reading is review.
