# Grade 4 math: what holds on every day unless a plan says otherwise

Set up 2026-11-11. A unit plan or a section plan writes a support, an accommodation or a room note only where
it differs from this page. Draft: taken from unit 11 and the scope and sequence only, to be checked against the other
units as each is next opened.

- **The day.** 60 minutes: warm-up 5, lesson about 48, exit 5 to 7 minutes, as the day's exit needs. No countdown is shown to a student.
- **Check accommodations.** Items and exit questions are read aloud once. Large print is offered. A scribe or
  an oral answer is allowed on teacher-judged items. A sketch may be drawn for the student from their words. Extra time and repeated reading as a student's plan
  says: the student keeps working through the respond half and joins a group after.
- **"Many"** means more than a third of the class.
- **The respond half.** Students mark keyed items against the key on the screen, pen down, five minutes. Hands
  up by item sets the groups; on a check whose groups are by error, students match their wrong answer to the
  group list on the screen; each group does its error's "if many" action. Teacher-judged items are read after
  class and answered in the next day's first five minutes. When the next day is a check, judged work goes back
  when pencils go down, never before the check, and feeds that day's respond groups.
- **Teaching one child.** Give the check items, then go over the missed ones together.
- **No document camera.** Draw it on the board; the display page carries every figure and step.
- **Lesson day sizes (docs/courses/LESSON-DAY.md).** Teacher page 900 words of prose (15 a minute of a 60-minute
  day), Before class 80. Student page 350 words a student reads, questions included (ours, untested), with no
  sentence over 15 words; two sides of US Letter; 14 point.
- **One projector.** The teacher runs the tool; the class writes each answer on paper before it is shown.
- **A room with nothing.** Every day has a paper path; hand-ruled grids and paper squares stand in for supplies.
