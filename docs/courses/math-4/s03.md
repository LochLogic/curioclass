# Unit 3: Equivalent Fractions and Comparing

Decided, Cory, 2026-11-11: benchmarks first. Benchmarks and the same whole (section 3.3, days 17 to 18) are taught before common denominators and numerators (section 3.4, days 19 to 24). The expert's reasons are in docs/reviews/unit-plans-4d-2026-11-11.md.

Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 10 to 24.** Sections: [3.1 Same amount, different parts](u03-1.md) (10 to 12);
[3.2 Why multiplying top and bottom works, in both directions](u03-2.md) (13 to 16); [3.3 Comparing to one
half and one; the same whole](u03-3.md) (17 to 18); [3.4 Comparing with a common denominator or
numerator](u03-4.md) (19 to 24, with the choose-a-way day, 23, and the assessment day, 24).
All four are planned. Days by kind: understanding 10, fluency 3, application 1, check 1, as the scope and sequence asks. A plan,
not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md). **The
tables on this page and in the four section plans are built from `data/plans/math-4/`** (since 2026-11-12): change a
row there and run `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py tables`, never the table here. A section plan's days and exits
are its own section's rows.

- **Limits.** Denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100 in tasks. No simplest form. Explain by cutting
  each part into n, never by "multiplying by 1". A common denominator found while working may fall outside
  the list; no least common denominator is asked.
- **Carried.** The number line, with paper strips: tied on day 11, strips withdrawn on day 12. Section 3.2: the
  line is tied to the written rule on day 13 and the drawn line is dropped on day 15. Section 3.3: a line from 0 to
  2 with 1/2 and 1 marked, tied to the written comparison on day 17 and printed on day 18. Section 3.4: both
  fractions on one line, tied to the written comparison on day 19. Both lines are withdrawn on day 22. After
  each withdrawal a student may still sketch a line to explain.
