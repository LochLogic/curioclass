## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **4.NF.A.1.** Parts, by verb: (a) **explain why** two fractions are equivalent, "by using visual fraction
  models"; (b) **recognize** and (c) **generate** equivalent fractions.
- **4.NF.A.2.** Parts: (d) **compare** fractions with different numerators and denominators; (e)
  **recognize** that a comparison needs the same whole; (f) **record** with >, = or <; (g) **justify**.
- **Limits.** Denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100 in tasks. No simplest form. Explain by cutting
  each part into n, never by "multiplying by 1". A common denominator found while working may fall outside
  the list; no least common denominator is asked.
- **Carried.** The number line, with paper strips: tied on day 11, strips withdrawn on day 12. Section 3.2: the
  line is tied to the written rule on day 13 and the drawn line is dropped on day 15. Section 3.3: a line from 0 to
  2 with 1/2 and 1 marked, tied to the written comparison on day 17 and printed on day 18. Section 3.4: both
  fractions on one line, tied to the written comparison on day 19. Both lines are withdrawn on day 22. After
  each withdrawal a student may still sketch a line to explain.
