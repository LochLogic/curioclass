# Stretch 3: Equivalent Fractions and Comparing

Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 10 to 24.** Units: [3.1 Same amount, different parts](u03-1.md) (10 to 12,
planned); 3.2 (13 to 16), 3.3 (17 to 20) and 3.4 (21 to 24), not yet planned. Days by kind: understanding 3
so far, from unit 3.1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow
[UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md), second draft; its fields wait for Cory's yes.

## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **4.NF.A.1.** Parts, by verb: (a) **explain why** two fractions are equivalent, "by using visual fraction
  models"; (b) **recognize** and (c) **generate** equivalent fractions.
- **4.NF.A.2.** Parts: (d) **compare** fractions with different numerators and denominators; (e)
  **recognize** that a comparison needs the same whole; (f) **record** with >, = or <; (g) **justify**.
- **Limits.** Denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100 in tasks. No simplest form. Explain by cutting
  each part into n, never by "multiplying by 1".
- **Carried.** The number line, with paper strips: tied on day 11, strips withdrawn on day 12. Unit 3.2
  names the day the drawn line is dropped.
