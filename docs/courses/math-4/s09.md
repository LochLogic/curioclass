Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 72 to 77.** Units: [9.1 The standard algorithm for
adding](u09-1.md) (72 to 73, planned); 9.2, subtracting (74 to 77, not yet planned). Days by kind, as
proposed: understanding 3, fluency 2, use 0, check 1 (the spine has 1, 4, 1). A plan, not a lesson: nothing

ungroupings, to six digits. 76: across zeros. 77: mixed, then Check (end check 30, respond 20, next readiness 10). Why:

## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **4.NBT.B.4** (the grade's one fluency standard). Parts, by verb: (a) **add**; (b) **subtract**; both
  **fluently**, "using the standard algorithm".
- **Limits.** Whole numbers to 1,000,000.
- **Carried.** Expanded form beside the standard algorithm, under place names. Adding: tied on day 72,
  withdrawn on day 73. Subtracting: unit 9.2 names its days. No blocks.

- **Item 9.1c.** Look-fors: an estimate, or a place value reason, for why 15,998 cannot be right; finds
  8,321. Meets (ours, untested): "7,468 is about 7,500 and 853 is about 900. That is about 8,400, so 15,998 is

  before every warm-up sum for a week.
- **Accommodations.** Large-square grid paper; numbers read aloud; a scribe or typed columns; 9.1c may be
  answered aloud.
