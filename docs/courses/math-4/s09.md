# Stretch 9: Adding and Subtracting Multi-Digit Numbers

**Decided, Cory, 2026-11-11: unit 9.1 on days 72 to 73 and unit 9.2 on days 74 to 77. The spine says the same.**

Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 72 to 77, then Check 2 and its respond day (78 to 79).** Units: [9.1 The
standard algorithm for adding](u09-1.md) (72 to 73); [9.2 The standard algorithm for subtracting](u09-2.md) (74
to 77). Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 2, use 0, check 1, as the spine has them; Check 2 (day 78) and
its respond day (79) sit in this stretch's rows because nothing else holds them. A plan, not a lesson: nothing
here is built or tried with children. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md). **The tables on this page
and in the two unit plans are built from `data/plans/math-4/`** (since 2026-11-12): change a row there and run
`python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py tables`, never the table here. A unit plan's days and exits are its own unit's
rows; days 78 and 79 are in the table below only.

**The days.** 72 and 73: adding (unit 9.1). 74: subtracting, one ungrouping. 75: several ungroupings, to six
digits. 76: across zeros. 77: Check (mixed set 10, end check 30, respond 20). Day 77 gives no readiness check
for the next stretch: Check 2 follows on day 78, and stretch 10's readiness check is given in the last ten
minutes of day 79 ([courses/math-4/u10-1/readiness.md](../../../courses/math-4/u10-1/readiness.md)). The ten
minutes go to mixed adding and subtracting before the check.

- **4.NBT.B.4** is the grade's one fluency standard: both parts **fluently**, "using the standard algorithm".
- **Limits.** Whole numbers to 1,000,000; a difference is never below zero.
- **Carried.** Expanded form beside the standard algorithm, under place names. Adding: tied on day 72,
  withdrawn on day 73. Subtracting: tied on day 74, withdrawn on day 75, back for ten minutes on day 76 for
  zeros only. No blocks.

## 7. The respond half, Check 2 and the respond day

Day 77. The marking step is as in DEFAULTS.md. Groups for errors A to E do their "if many" action; groups for B
and F to I do theirs. A student in two groups goes to the subtracting one. E76's slips go back at the door and
join the I and F groups, so day 76's exit is answered on day 77.

**Check 2, day 78.** One period, alone. It covers stretches 6 to 9 and fractions again (spine), so it is more
than this stretch: a part from the generators and two fixed tasks that ask for an explanation or a model.
Stretch 9's share is items C2.1 and C2.2. Stretches 6 to 8 are not yet planned; their items and the two fixed
tasks are added when they are, and Check 2 is not ready to give until then. No respond half on the day: it is
marked and judged after class.

**Day 79, the respond day.** Items 9.1c and 9.2c come back first, five minutes, with their "meets" answers;
students fix their own in pen. (DEFAULTS.md sends judged work back at the door when the next day is a check,
to feed that day's respond groups; Check 2 has none, so the work waits a day.) Then Check 2's
keyed items are marked against the key, five minutes, and groups by error work for thirty: stretch 9's groups
use the register's "if many" actions with new numbers. Then E79, then stretch 10's readiness check in the last
ten minutes, as s10.md field 4 has it. E79 is read that night and goes to the warm-up, not to day 80's lesson.
For one child: go over the missed items together.
