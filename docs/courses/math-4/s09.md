# Stretch 9: Adding and Subtracting Multi-Digit Numbers

**Decided, Cory, 2026-11-11: unit 9.1 on days 72 to 73 and unit 9.2 on days 74 to 77. The spine says the same.**

Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 72 to 77.** Units: [9.1 The standard algorithm for
adding](u09-1.md) (72 to 73); [9.2 The standard algorithm for subtracting](u09-2.md) (74 to 77). Days by
kind: understanding 3, fluency 2, use 0, check 1, as the spine has them. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is
built or tried with children. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md).

**The days.** 72 and 73: adding (unit 9.1). 74: subtracting, one ungrouping. 75: several ungroupings, to six
digits. 76: across zeros. 77: Check (mixed practice 10, end check 30, respond 20). Day 77 gives no readiness
check for the next stretch: Check 2 follows on day 78, and stretch 10's readiness check is given on day 79.
The ten minutes go to mixed adding and subtracting before the check.

## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **4.NBT.B.4** (the grade's one fluency standard). Parts, by verb: (a) **add**; (b) **subtract**; both
  **fluently**, "using the standard algorithm".
- **Limits.** Whole numbers to 1,000,000.
- **Carried.** Expanded form beside the standard algorithm, under place names. Adding: tied on day 72,
  withdrawn on day 73. Subtracting: tied on day 74, withdrawn on day 75, back for ten minutes on day 76 for
  zeros only. No blocks.

| 9.2a | 4.NBT.B.4, MP.6 | (b) subtract | "Subtract: 73,518 − 28,764", set in columns | 44,754 | 55,254 (F); too big by exactly 100, 1,000 or 10,000 (one ungrouping not taken away, G or H); 55,854 (none taken away, G) | 9.2 |
| 9.2b | 4.NBT.B.4, MP.6 | (b) subtract, across zeros | "Subtract: 600,305 − 47,268", written across the page | 553,037 | 127,625 (B); 563,047 (I); 647,163 (F) | 9.2 |
| 9.2c | 4.NBT.B.4, MP.3, teacher judged | (b) subtract, across zeros | "Rosa found 8,004 − 2,536 = 6,532. Use adding to show Rosa is wrong. Then find the right difference." | 6,532 + 2,536 = 9,068, not 8,004; 5,468 | Finds 5,468 but shows no adding; says "she subtracted wrong"; 5,578 (I) | 9.2 |

- **Item 9.1c.** Look-fors: an estimate, or a place value reason, for why 15,998 cannot be right; finds
  8,321. Meets (ours, untested): "7,468 is about 7,500 and 853 is about 900. That is about 8,400, so 15,998 is
  way too big. The sum is 8,321." Does not: "Dev added wrong. It is 8,321." If many gave no reason: estimate
  before every warm-up sum for a week.
- **Item 9.2c.** Look-fors: adds 2,536 to a difference to test it; finds 5,468. Meets (ours, untested):
  "6,532 + 2,536 = 9,068. That should be 8,004, so Rosa is wrong. It is 5,468, and 5,468 + 2,536 = 8,004."
  Does not: "Rosa took the small digit from the big one. It is 5,468." (True, but no adding.) If many did not
  add: every warm-up difference is checked by adding for a week.
- **Accommodations.** Large-square grid paper; numbers read aloud; a scribe or typed columns; 9.1c and 9.2c
  may be answered aloud.

## 6. Error register

"IM": the open course in Sources, unit 4. "Prog.": the Progressions, PDF page (printed page). A to E belong
to unit 9.1; B returns in unit 9.2; F to I belong to unit 9.2.

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Drops a carry: usually one, rarely all | Each column is added as its own small sum | Partly IM lesson 19; the rest from memory |
| B | Lines numbers up from the left | Digits are matched, not places | IM lessons 18 and 20 |
| C | Calls every carry "one" | The place value is gone | From memory |
| D | Writes a two-digit sum in one column | A place is not seen as holding one digit | From memory |
| E | Loses the last carry into a new place | Stops at the longer number's last digit | From memory |
| F | In each column, takes the smaller digit from the larger, whichever is on top; or subtracts from the left with no ungrouping | Each column is its own small problem | Prog. pp. 63 (57), 66 (60); IM lesson 20 |
| G | Writes the small 1 to make 12 ones, but leaves the 7 tens as 7 | The marks are copied; nothing was traded | IM lesson 20 |
| H | Ungroups rightly, then forgets a place was already made smaller | Lost track while switching between ungrouping and subtracting | Prog. p. 66 (60) |
| I | Across zeros, turns each zero into 10 and leaves it 10 | The zero place passed nothing on | IM lesson 21 names zeros as the hard case; this form of the error is from memory |
