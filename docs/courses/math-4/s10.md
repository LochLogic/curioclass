# Unit 10: Multiplicative Comparison

Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 80 to 88.** Sections: [10.1 Times as many](u10-1.md) (80 to 82); [10.2
Comparison word problems: times as many or more than?](u10-2.md) (83 to 86); [10.3 Two-step problems: a letter
for the unknown, an estimate to check](u10-3.md) (87 to 88). Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 1, application 4,
check 1, as the scope and sequence has them. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields
follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md). **The tables on this page and in the three section plans are built from
`data/plans/math-4/`** (since 2026-11-12): change a row there and run `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py tables`,
never the table here. A section plan's days and exits are its own section's rows.

- **4.OA.A.3, this unit's share.** Parts (f) to (h) with two steps. Remainders, and problems of more than
  two steps, wait for units 13 and 14.
- **Limits.** Whole numbers and whole-number answers; two steps; no remainders (scope and sequence). "Times as many" and
  "times as much" only. "Times more than" is talked about once, on day 83, and is never asked (Progressions); a word rule in
  `words.csv` now fails any row whose stem uses it. "One third as much" is grade 5.
- **The numbers rule. Decided, Cory, 2026-11-11, and now in the scope and sequence** (the scope and sequence was silent, and multi-digit multiplying
  starts on day 94). Comparing uses only: products of two one-digit numbers and the matching divisions; a
  one-digit number times a multiple of 10 up to 90, multiplying only; and 10 times a one- or two-digit number,
  multiplying only. The adding or subtracting step in a two-step problem stays within 1,000. Money is in whole
  dollars or whole cents; measures are whole units with no conversion.
- **Carried.** The strip picture (tape diagram): one strip for the smaller amount, and a row of equal copies
  of it for the larger. Cubes or counters on day 80 only. The strip is tied to the equation on day 81 and to
  the letter on day 84. It is not withdrawn in this unit: from day 86 a student draws it only when stuck,
  and may draw it in the end-of-unit assessment.

## 3. Where it sits

- **Builds on (weak handoff).** 3.OA.A.1 and 3.OA.A.3 (equal groups and arrays), 3.OA.D.8 (two-step problems
  with a letter). Grade 3 has no "times as many" problems; the nearest base is grade 2 "how many more"
  (2.OA.A.1). So the readiness check matters here (progression page).
- **Leads to.** Units 12 to 15. Grade 5: multiplying as scaling (5.NF.B.5), writing expressions (5.OA.A.2).
- **Comes back.** Word problems in units 12 and 13; "times as long" on day 92; unit conversions in
  unit 15; 4.OA.A.3 in full in unit 14; Checks 3 and 4.

## 7. The respond half

Day 88. The marking step is as in DEFAULTS.md. Groups: A and D rebuild one comparison with strips (section 10.1,
field 4); B and C sort three problems by which amount is unknown (section 10.2, field 4); F to H do section 10.3's "if

and nothing of it waits for unit 11. A student in two groups goes to the earlier one. Then the last ten
minutes: unit 11's readiness check (s11.md, field 4). For one child: go over the missed items together.

**Hand-off to unit 11.** Items 7 and 8 are judged after class. Day 89 (courses/math-4/u11-1/d089.md) hands them
back in its five minutes after the warm-up, shows the "meets" answers of both, and students fix their own in pen;
the marks go on unit 10's record sheet. Day 89 leans on this file for those answers until a unit 10 key is
built.

## 9. Family note

"Your child is learning what '3 times as many' means, and how it differs from '3 more'. Put out 2 spoons. Ask
for 3 times as many, then for 3 more. Ask which is bigger, and why."
