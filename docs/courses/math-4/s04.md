# Unit 4: Adding and Subtracting Fractions

Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 25 to 38.** Sections: [4.1 A fraction as a sum; taking it apart more than
one way](u04-1.md) (25 to 28); [4.2 Joining and separating like parts](u04-2.md) (29 to 31); [4.3 Mixed
numbers](u04-3.md) (32 to 35); [4.4 Fraction word problems](u04-4.md) (36 to 38, with the assessment day, 38).
A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md).
The tables here and in the section plans are built from `data/plans/math-4/` by `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py
tables`: change a row there, never a table here.

## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **4.NF.B.3:** "Understand a fraction a/b with a > 1 as a sum of fractions 1/b." With 3a to 3d (the standards
  file holds their words). Parts, by verb:

| Code | Part | Verb | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.NF.B.3 | (a) | understand | a fraction a/b, a > 1 too, as a sum of unit fractions 1/b |
| 4.NF.B.3a | (b) | understand | adding as joining and subtracting as separating parts of the same whole; parts of different wholes are not added (two unit assessment items) |
| 4.NF.B.3b | (c) | decompose | a fraction into a sum with the same denominator in more than one way, each recorded by an equation, past 1 too |
| 4.NF.B.3b | (d) | justify | a decomposition, with a visual fraction model |
| 4.NF.B.3c | (e) | add | mixed numbers with like denominators, by renaming as fractions and by wholes and parts |
| 4.NF.B.3c | (f) | subtract | mixed numbers with like denominators, by both ways, breaking a one, checked by adding |
| 4.NF.B.3d | (g) | solve | word problems that add and that subtract fractions of the same whole, with a model and an equation (two unit assessment items, one each way); first stories in section 4.2 |
<!-- /gen -->

- **Limits.** Like denominators only, from the list 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100 (tenths plus hundredths
  waits for unit 7). No simplest form. A sum such as 4 9/8 is right, not yet written as a mixed number (renaming
  is measured by item 4.3a (ii)). A mixed number is written as a sum first, 2 + 3/4, never by a "times the
  bottom, add the top" rule.
- **Carried.** Unit-fraction pieces and the number line from unit 3. Pieces: tied to the written sum on day 25;
  withdrawn on day 28; back as wholes to break or build on days 30, 32 and 34. The line: hops of one part size,
  tied to the equation on day 29, counting up on day 30; it runs to 6 for mixed numbers on days 32 to 34 and is
  withdrawn on day 35. A tape diagram carries the stories from day 31 to 38 and is not withdrawn: 4.NF.B.3d names
  a visual model.
