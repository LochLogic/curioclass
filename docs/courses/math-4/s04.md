# Unit 4: Adding and Subtracting Fractions

Grade 4 math, major work. **Days 25 to 38.** Sections: [4.1 A fraction as a sum; taking it apart more than
one way](u04-1.md) (25 to 28); [4.2 Joining and separating like parts](u04-2.md) (29 to 31); [4.3 Mixed
numbers](u04-3.md) (32 to 35); [4.4 Fraction word problems](u04-4.md) (36 to 38, with the assessment day, 38).
Days by kind, summed from the sections: understanding 7, fluency 3, application 3, check 1. The scope and sequence's first
cut is 6, 4, 3, 1; day 33 is marked understanding here because the standard's two ways of adding and
subtracting mixed numbers are both new (field 10). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with
children. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md). No expert read yet. Tables are by hand until the plan-data job
moves them into `data/plans/math-4/`; the readiness table is built from data.

## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **4.NF.B.3:** "Understand a fraction a/b with a > 1 as a sum of fractions 1/b." With 3a to 3d (the standards
  file holds their words). Parts, by verb:

| Code | Part | Verb | What it is | Taught |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.NF.B.3 | (a) | understand | a fraction a/b, a > 1 too, as a sum of unit fractions 1/b | 4.1, day 25 |
| 4.NF.B.3a | (b) | understand | adding as joining and subtracting as separating parts of the same whole; parts of different wholes are not added (two unit assessment items) | 4.2, days 29 and 30 |
| 4.NF.B.3b | (c) | decompose | a fraction into a sum with the same denominator in more than one way, each recorded by an equation, past 1 too | 4.1, days 26 to 28 |
| 4.NF.B.3b | (d) | justify | a decomposition, with a visual fraction model | 4.1, days 26 and 27 |
| 4.NF.B.3c | (e) | add | mixed numbers with like denominators, by renaming as fractions and by wholes and parts | 4.3, days 32, 33, 35 |
| 4.NF.B.3c | (f) | subtract | mixed numbers with like denominators, by both ways, breaking a one, checked by adding | 4.3, days 33 to 35 |
| 4.NF.B.3d | (g) | solve | word problems that add and that subtract fractions of the same whole, with a model and an equation (two unit assessment items, one each way) | 4.2 day 31; 4.4 |

- **Limits.** Like denominators only, from the list 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100. No unlike denominators
  (grade 5; tenths plus hundredths waits for unit 7). No simplest form: 2 5/10 and 2 1/2 are both right. An
  answer such as 5 15/12 is right and noted "not regrouped". A mixed number is written as a sum first, 2 + 3/4, never by a "times the bottom, add the top" rule
  (progression page, unit 4).
- **Carried.** Unit-fraction pieces (paper strips cut into single parts) and the number line from unit 3.
  Pieces: tied to the written sum on day 25, one addend a piece; withdrawn on day 28 (sketch only). The line:
  joining and separating are hops of one part size, tied to the equation on day 29; it runs on to 5 for mixed
  numbers on days 32 to 34 and is withdrawn on day 35. A tape diagram (a drawn strip) carries the stories from
  day 31 to 38 and is not withdrawn: 4.NF.B.3d names a visual model.
