# Stretch 1: Factors and Multiples

Grade 4 math, supporting work. **Days 1 to 6.** Units: [1.1 Rectangles and factor pairs](u01-1.md) (days 1 to
3); [1.2 Multiples, prime and composite](u01-2.md) (days 4 to 6). Days by kind, summed from the units:
understanding 4, fluency 1, use 0, check 1 (the spine's first cut has 3, 2, 0, 1; see field 10). A plan, not a
lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md). **The
tables on this page and in the two unit plans are built from `data/plans/math-4/`** (since 2026-11-12): change a
row there and run `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py tables`, never the table here. A unit plan's days and exits
are its own unit's rows.

## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

<!-- gen:parts (from data/plans/math-4; do not edit here) -->

|---|---|---|---|
| 4.OA.B.4 | (a) | find | all factor pairs for a whole number in the range 1 to 100 |
| 4.OA.B.4 | (b) | recognize | that a whole number is a multiple of each of its factors |
| 4.OA.B.4 | (c) | determine | whether a whole number in the range 1 to 100 is a multiple of a given one-digit number |
| 4.OA.B.4 | (d) | determine | whether a whole number in the range 1 to 100 is prime or composite |
<!-- /gen -->

- **Limits.** Whole numbers 1 to 100 only. "A multiple of" is tested against one-digit numbers. No greatest
  common factor, no least common multiple, no prime factorization (grade 6). Students "need not be fluent" in
  finding all pairs (Progressions).
- **Carried.** The rectangle of unit squares: in hand on days 1 and 2, tied to its equation on day 1 and
  to the ordered list on day 2, withdrawn on day 3. Unit 1.2 uses a sketch only.
