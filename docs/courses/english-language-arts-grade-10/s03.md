## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What does each work show about how its people live with power held by others, at home and from abroad, and how can a reader tell from the text?"
**Hook, day 46:** Read the indigo-factory opening of The Postmaster. Whose needs does its arrival serve? Open the compelling question.
**Supporting questions:** What does Ratan hope the lessons mean (47)? How does the handprint change Mini's father's view (48)? How does Rizal make dinner etiquette reveal authority (51)? How does Mhudi's own account complicate Ra-Thaga's assumptions (56)?
**Closing claim:** Day 59's short literary argument compares exclusion in The Renunciation with The Cabuliwallah, using two details from each. Defend a claim about who controls belonging without treating either work as all India.
Model evidence versus contextual inference on day 46; model a comparison paragraph on 47. Step back for seminar 49 and Rizal comparison 51. Guide Plaatje's hardest passages on 54 to 55; students lead comparison on 56.

## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | One fictional experience represents an entire people. | Overgeneralization. Repair: compare two different choices within the named text before comparing regions. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| B | A remembered impression substitutes for located textual evidence. | The inference is unsupported. Repair: locate a detail and explain its link to the claim. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| C | Teacher-assigned rules count as student decision-making. | No student negotiation is shown. Repair: students agree and record one changed rule. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| D | Taking a turn alone counts as advancing discussion. | No connecting exchange occurs. Repair: ask a text-linked question and invite a reply. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| E | Repetition counts as responding to disagreement. | Another view is not considered. Repair: summarize it before qualifying the claim. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| F | A comma alone can join independent clauses. | Clause boundaries are missed. Repair: test each clause as a sentence, then join with a semicolon. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| G | A colon may split a verb from its object. | The lead-in is incomplete. Repair: complete the introductory clause before the colon. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| H | A plausible sound guarantees spelling. | Word spelling is unchecked. Repair: compare with the reference, cover and rewrite. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| I | One short task proves a range of writing. | Time frames and purposes are conflated. Repair: identify the retained extended product and a distinct short task. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| J | Related word forms have the same grammatical job. | Suffix function is missed. Repair: rebuild the sentence with a noun, verb and adjective. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| K | The 1913 law caused events set in the 1830s. | Publication and story time are conflated. Repair: place the two dates on separate timeline rows. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| L | A character has only one motive. | Interaction and change are overlooked. Repair: trace two impulses and the action they produce. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| M | Accurate retelling alone counts as analysis. | Evidence is not connected to a claim. Repair: add a because sentence explaining how one retold detail supports the comparison. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| N | A sentence opening needs no capital. | Sentence boundaries are not marked. Repair: mark each sentence boundary and capitalize its first word. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| O | An unrelated word meaning fits the context. | Meaning is mismatched. Repair: choose the reference sense supported by the scene. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| P | An inferred meaning needs no verification. | The inference is unchecked. Repair: compare it with a reference entry and confirm or revise it. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| Q | A teacher-selected word shows independent word gathering. | Word choice is dependent. Repair: select an important word independently and explain the choice. | Anticipated, from memory. |
