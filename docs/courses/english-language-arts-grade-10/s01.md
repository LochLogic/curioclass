## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What does Victor owe the being he made, and does the novel blame him, the creature or the world that turns them away?"

**Hook, day 1:** Read letter 1's promised benefits. What do those promises suggest Walton owes others? Reserve letter 2's friendship paragraph for the exit.

**Supporting questions:** What does Walton want (day 1)? How does Victor direct sympathy (day 7)? How does the embedded account change that judgment (day 16)? How does Walton's return qualify responsibility (day 22)? What can Shelley's recollection establish (day 23)?

**Closing claim:** On days 20 to 25, defend a qualified answer using Victor's, the creature's and Walton's accounts.
Model evidence on day 1, sentence unpacking on day 6 and competing readings on day 11. Withdraw charts by day 12; students lead day 16's reread.
