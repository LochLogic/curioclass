## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Was Brutus right to kill Caesar, and how did Shakespeare change Plutarch's story to make us ask?"
**Hook, day 61:** Read I.1. Why does celebrating Caesar trouble Marullus? Open the compelling question.
**Questions:** What conflicts in Brutus's response to Cassius (62)? How does the serpent analogy justify preventive violence (66)? What does the painting leave unheard (68)? How does the ending complicate Brutus's judgment (77)?
**Closing claim:** Day 81 judges Brutus through Shakespeare's transformation of North, citing both Lives and three play scenes.
Model paired evidence on 64 and counterclaims on 66. Students lead seminars on 65 and 75: prepare citations, set roles and rules, invite challenges, then write a qualified synthesis. Partners discuss daily; teacher-led rereading targets difficult syntax.
