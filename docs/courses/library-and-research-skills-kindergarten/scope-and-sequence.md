# Scope and sequence: Library and Research Skills, kindergarten

Course `library-and-research-skills-kindergarten` (catalog id `library-k`), kindergarten, draft, written 2026-11-26
from the frame (docs/courses/library-and-research-skills-kindergarten/frame.md). The days live in
data/plans/library-and-research-skills-kindergarten/days.csv.

## The course on one page

- **Units and days.** 4 units, 30 course days, as the frame sets them: units of 6, 6, 5 and 7 days (24), the
  mid-year check (day 13) and its respond day (14), the year-end check over two sessions (days 27 and 28) and its two
  respond days (29 and 30). `stretch` is the frame's unit number; `unit` is `<unit>.1` (no unit is split into
  sections at this stage). Per unit: show-me (Understanding), practice (Fluency), inquiry (Application), the
  end-of-unit assessment (Check) and a respond day.
- **How school_day is counted.** One session of about 25 minutes a week for 30 weeks, a specials slot (library does
  not count in the K to 5 stagger, CALENDAR.md rule 1). Course day n falls in week n: school_day = 5n (the last
  school day of week n, the slot the even spread already gave), so week = (school_day - 1) // 5 + 1 = n. A school
  picks its own day of the week. Every unit and check falls in the frame's weeks: unit 1 weeks 1 to 6, unit 2 7 to
  12, the mid-year check week 13 (respond 14), unit 3 15 to 19, unit 4 20 to 26, the year-end check weeks 27 and 28
  (respond 29 and 30).
- **A session's minutes.** About 19 working minutes: about 3 minutes at each end for getting to the carpet and
  putting books back. From unit 2 on, show-me and practice days open with the five-minute retrieval task, and the
  `retrieves` column names the row; inquiry days open with the cycle card; day 7, unit 2's first day, opens with its
  hook and compelling question and no retrieval (four new routines). Check days open with no retrieval of a move they score.
- **Readiness and retrieval (rule 2).** No course comes before kindergarten and no kindergarten row has an `assumes`
  cell, so no unit has readiness rows and no day carries a readiness check. Every retrieval is of a row taught
  earlier this year: k-wonder (days 9, 22), k-class-inquiry (8, 30), k-picture-book-labels (15, 21),
  k-library-visit (16), k-listen-and-retell (20) and k-who-made-book (30). The cycle card comes back for one day in
  each later unit (days 10, 17, 23), as the frame's Models carried line asks; there the cycle run is the day's own
  skill, so those days carry no `retrieves`.
- **Evidence.** The teacher's class checklist, one named move per child, thrown away at unit end, plus the drawing or
  spoken answer (APPROACH.md, K to 2). Every end-of-unit assessment uses one of the frame's costed forms on a case not
  seen before, fits the 19 working minutes for a class of 30, and never spills into its respond day. Each prompt asks
  every part of the rows and codes the day lists. Nothing is scored on a show-me day. Each unit's response time is
  its respond day, counted once. No unit is over 25 days, so there is no mid-unit assessment.
- **Codes.** Kindergarten owns one id, AASL.V.C.1 (k-wonder), taught on days 1 to 4, assessed on day 5 and sampled
  on both course-level checks (days 13 and 27). The nine carried ids are written with a `~` on the days that begin
  them with help; each has its later owner in the frame's What students learn.
- **Sources not loaded.** No day of this course leans on the sources later.csv L149 is waiting for (the
  online-reasoning lessons, the web-literacy book, the continuum's 3 to 5 band file): the PK to 2 band file is in the
  source store and was read cell by cell for the frame. Day 7's stop rule leans on the listing of a free K to 2
  online-safety presentation only; its kit is not opened or loaded. No base text; every day is Curio Class's own; no
  source-map.csv this run.
- **Paper path and supplies.** The whole course runs on print, crayons and the class book corner: no device, no
  internet, no library visit. Supplies and safe use of space are named in each day's note.

## Unit 1. I wonder (days 1 to 6, weeks 1 to 6)

*What do we wonder about, and how can we find out?* The class looks closely at whole seeds and says "I wonder..." questions (days 1
and 2), meets the cycle's four pictures and runs the ask step on one board question (day 3), then finds and draws one
fact (day 4), where on the stand-alone path the teacher first cuts a seed open beside the read-aloud, so the find
step has something new to find. The wonder comes first because every later unit's question comes from the board; the cycle card comes
on day 3, once children can ask, so the ask step has a real question to run on. Host: a push-pull wonder from
science kindergarten unit 1 (sk-push-pull, weeks 1 to 5, in step with days 3 and 4); stand-alone: "What is inside a
seed?". The board is shared with social studies kindergarten unit 1's wonder wall, running the same weeks.

- **Readiness:** none (no earlier row).
- **End-of-unit assessment:** day 5, circle round, about 15 minutes for 30: the four cycle pictures in order with
  each step's word (k-class-inquiry) and one question about an unseen picture (k-wonder, AASL.V.C.1). The day 4
  drawing (one found fact, labeled or dictated) is k-class-inquiry's observed performance for the find and use
  steps, marked on the checklist on day 4 (an inquiry day), so the cycle is done, not only named (sequence-write
  rule 2: a product or observed performance may stand for a code where the day says so).
- **Respond:** day 6 opens with the share circle of the day 4 drawings (each child says one thing a friend's drawing
  taught them, begun AASL.I.C.1) and the pointing self-check, then regrouped reteach. The class count of each move goes on the mid-year sheet before the checklist is thrown
  away.
- **Candidates:** the cover-guess cell, kept as one 3-minute practice prompt on day 2 (the teacher shows one guess
  first); the connect cell (one or two
  new ideas from a read-aloud), kept as the share prompt on day 6. Neither adds a day.
- **Leans:** ELA kindergarten meets the same cycle later (ek-shared-research, weeks 16 to 24): day 3 carries the
  bridge and teaches the four pictures itself, with no writing.

## Unit 2. Our book corner (days 7 to 12, weeks 7 to 12)

*How do we find a book that answers our question, and look after it?* Two show-me days split the load the frame read
found too heavy for one: the hook and compelling question, then book care, borrowing and the stop rule (day 7, no
opening retrieval), then the bins, their labels and the story
or fact sort (day 8). Day 9 practices both, and day 10 runs the whole cycle on the rain question, with the fact-book
pick as the judge step at K depth; it ends at the drawing, and the partner share opens day 12. Host: a weather fact book in ELA kindergarten unit 2 (weeks 7 to 16, in step), or
the science weather watch taught in weeks 5 to 9; stand-alone: "What happens when rain falls?".

- **Readiness:** none.
- **End-of-unit assessment:** day 11, about 17 minutes for 30: a station of five (fetch from an unseen bin, story or
  fact, "Which one would help us find out about ___?" for a named question, put back; k-picture-book-labels with
  the ~AASL.IV.A.2 slice, and k-library-visit) while the others sort, then the stop strip scanned
  across the circle (k-library-visit).
- **Respond:** day 12, partner shares and self-check, then regrouped reteach. The class count is written on the
  mid-year sheet before the checklist is thrown away.
- **Candidates:** true or not true picture books and library tools that help find materials, kept inside days 8 and
  10's sort and bins; the personal-interest choice, kept as the borrowing-day choice on day 9, not scored. No day
  added.
- **Sensitivity:** borrowing from the class corner only, no question about books at home, no blame for a lost book;
  the stop rule on pretend cards, with the adult's steps on the teacher page (day 7).

## Course-level check 1, mid-year (day 13, week 13; respond day 14, week 14)

A circle round on an unseen picture, about 15 minutes for 30: the four cycle pictures in order and one "I wonder".
It samples unit 1 (k-wonder, k-class-inquiry, AASL.V.C.1) on a new case. Unit 2, assessed on day 11, is not
re-tested: its class count is reused unscored. The circle-round form was last used in week 5. Week 13 holds no
kindergarten core check (core weeks 14 to 17). Day 14 reteaches the missed step in small groups from the sheet.

## Unit 3. Listening for the answer (days 15 to 19, weeks 15 to 19)

*What does a rabbit eat?* Day 15 opens on the rabbit question as the hook, then the teacher models the move on a
parallel case, holding "What does a tortoise eat?" aloud over a tortoise passage and stopping at the answering
sentence; the class practices on three short passages about other plant eaters (day 16), then runs the cycle on its
animal question, hears the rabbit read-aloud for the first time and draws the answering fact (day 17), so the
inquiry keeps something to find. One row, five days. Host: sk-needs-places in science kindergarten unit 4 (weeks 15 to 20, in step), or
ELA's trees research once it starts in week 16; stand-alone: the rabbit question.

- **Readiness:** none (the row assumes nothing).
- **End-of-unit assessment:** day 18, whisper walk, about 10 minutes for 30: an unseen passage about a different
  plant eater, each child whispering the answering fact (k-listen-and-retell, begun AASL.I.B.1).
- **Respond:** day 19, share circle of the drawings and self-check, then regrouped reteach.
- **Candidates:** taking notes on facts by drawing, kept as day 17's drawing; telling the main idea of a read-aloud,
  dropped, because it is ELA's (STANDARD.md section 4) and pulls against this unit's move (the part that answers, not
  every part). No day added.
- **Passages for the unit plan** (Curio Class writes them, all plant eaters): the rabbit read-aloud (day 17), the
  day 15 tortoise model passage, three practice passages (day 16), unseen passages for days 18 and 27, a fresh-try
  passage (day 19), and day 20's two-sentence retrieval passage. The frame's Tools cell now lists the same set.
- **Sensitivity:** plant eaters only, so no passage describes a hunt.

## Unit 4. Sharing what we found (days 20 to 26, weeks 20 to 26)

*What lives in and around a tree, and who told us?* The group turn with a talking object (day 20, on a card that opens with SEL's talk turn
card, "eyes look, wait my turn", then adds library's steps) and the maker's
name (day 21) each get a show-me day, day 22 practices both, and two inquiry days run the find (day 23) and the group
share, the year's last product (day 24). It comes last because a child brings the question, the fact book and the
answering fact to the group. Host: ELA kindergarten unit 3's trees research (ek-shared-research), in step (ELA days 80 to
118, weeks 16 to 24, so days 20 to 24 fall inside it): the class brings its ELA tree facts in as found facts. Stand-alone: "What
lives in and around a tree?".

- **Readiness:** none.
- **End-of-unit assessment:** day 25, about 17 minutes for 30: groups of three with an unseen picture set (one fact
  each, what a friend added: k-talk-in-group), then the maker's name pointed to on a cover card, scanned
  (k-who-made-book).
- **Respond:** day 26, self-check and regrouped reteach; the class count goes on the year-end sheet.
- **Candidates:** listening and raising a hand before speaking, kept inside day 20's talking-object turn; what
  feedback is and why it helps, kept as 3 minutes after day 24's shares. Telling one's own idea from a found fact is
  taught on day 21 inside k-who-made-book. No day added.
- **Leans:** ELA unit 1 (ek-books-and-authors, ek-discussion) and social studies unit 1 (ssk-talk-together, weeks
  3 to 4, and again in week 23) come before this unit; SEL kindergarten unit 1 teaches the talk turn card's first two
  pictures, which day 20 teaches itself where a school does not run SEL; days 20 and 21 still teach the turn and the maker's names where a class has not had them.
- **Sensitivity:** a child who calls a found fact "mine" is helped, never corrected in front of the group; groups
  mixed by the teacher; a child may hand the talking object on.

## Course-level check 2, year-end (days 27 and 28, weeks 27 and 28; respond days 29 and 30)

Two sessions, because its two forms each fill about 15 of the 19 working minutes. Day 27, a whisper walk on an
unseen passage: the answering fact (unit 3) and one "I wonder" (unit 1, AASL.V.C.1). Day 28, a station of five: fetch, story or fact, "Which one would help us find out about ___?", put back (unit 2). Unit 4, assessed on day 25, is reused unscored as its class count. Neither
form was used in the two weeks before (whisper walk week 18, station week 11). Day 29 reteaches gaps from the sheet;
day 30 is the year's reflect step on the wonder board and the class-level note to grade 1. Weeks 27 and 28 hold no
kindergarten core check.

**Codes sampled.** AASL.V.C.1, the one owned code, is sampled on days 13 and 27. Every carried id that a check day
lists is sampled there by its row's move; I.B.3, I.C.1, III.D.1, II.A.1 and VI.B.2 (units 1 and 4's begun ids) are
not owned here and sit only in the end-of-unit assessments and the reused class counts.

## Shorter path

- **A shorter period (about 20 minutes, about 14 working minutes).** Teaching days drop the opening retrieval to 3
  minutes and run shares in pairs instead of the full circle. The five assessment days (5, 11, 25, 27, 28) and the
  mid-year check (13) need 15 to 17 working minutes for 30, so on those days the school gives the full 25-minute slot,
  or the classroom teacher runs the station groups during the class's own book-corner time that week with the same
  items; no item and no row is cut.
- **A year about 10 school days short (2 sessions at one a week).** Fold-in days, nothing assessed and no owned part
  cut: day 30 into day 29 (the wonder-board look-back takes the last 9 minutes after a 10-minute reteach; saves about
  19 minutes); day 24 into day 26 (the circle share of the group drawings, "We found that... in the book by...", and the
  3-minute feedback candidate take day 26's opening 10 minutes, the pattern units 1 and 3 use, with the self-check
  and the reteach after; the whole class stays the audience for the year's last product; saves about 19 minutes); and, if a third is needed, day 22 into days 21 and 23 (the maker-naming practice
  runs inside day 23's group work; saves about 19 minutes). Two fold-ins save about 38 working minutes, two sessions,
  so the year runs in 28 of the frame's 30 days; three give 27.
- **A leave-out unit:** the frame names none. Every unit carries a row that grade 1 assumes.
