# Section 6.3: The class store, our claim, and the check

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-kindergarten/6/s/6.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-kindergarten u06-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## 1. Standards and practices


- **MA.K.T4.3 and MA.K.T4.2, days 57, 58, 60, 61.** Children "give examples of goods and services people pay for with money they earn": the store's drawings are goods, the paper-folding is a service, and tokens stand for money from work. They "ask and answer questions about buying, selling, and trading" and "explain how people choose among things they need and want" (the choice on 58). All parts, with help recorded.
- **D2.Eco.1.K-2, days 58, 60.** The limit is named aloud: five tokens cannot pay for both.
- **MA.K.T4.1 and D2.Eco.3.K-2, days 59, 60.** The closing claim describes work from a named source. When a claim names a worker, the teacher asks what that worker knows or can do.
- **Limits that bite.** No coin values, change, wages, budgets or opportunity cost. Prices are one to five identical tokens, each worth one (math K.CC.B.5, counting; already taught). Everyone starts with five tokens, never earned by good behavior or taken away. Trade is voluntary and a child may decline. Saving is allowed and never better. No question about what any family has or earns. Tokens model exchange, not worth.
- **Connections.** Unit 3's day 26 map and key (57, 58, review). Unit 5's hearing why people choose differently (58). Unit 1's "I think ..., because ..." (59, the claim frame). 6.1's know, do, use icons (59).
- **What the teacher page must explain.**
  - Every child has one roster turn on 58, 59 or 60 for the spoken parts (items 1, 4 and 7). A turn is a first try, one spoken next step, and a revised try; only the revised try is scored. Day 60's group also gives short reasons for the paper items. A spoken part not heard stays "not yet seen", never inferred.
  - The marking scale is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
  - Marks on paper are by every child; the teacher reads all prompts and judges reasoning apart from speech or handwriting.
  - A saved token is a choice, not a lesson; "not all money comes from work" is said aloud on 58.

## 2. Where it sits


Two unmarked reviews: R57 (the day 26 map and key, unit 3) and R59 (Our Store Record, unit 1's "because"). Results go to the record sheet and day 61's groups. No readiness item for day 62 is reserved.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** 5 (ask and answer about buying, selling, trading) and 7 (the closing claim with a named source), on the unit plan. Day 60 hosts all seven items; items 1 to 4 and 6 are owned by 6.1 and 6.2. Day 60's evidence is the seven items, so it has no extra exit.

**Record sheet.** One row per child and one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Columns: E57 mark, E57 heard, E58 marks, then a column for each unit item. Spoken parts are heard on the child's one roster turn, ten children a day on 58, 59 and 60, fixed on the sheet before 58. Cards are new, printed "invented for practice".

**E57.** Cards: a bucket; a person cleaning shoes; a plant pot. Scene card: a girl gives tokens to a man who hands her a bucket.
- Mark 1 (all): "Circle the service." Answer: the shoe cleaning. Wrong: the bucket, "because someone carried it" (F).
- Mark 2 (all): "Put a dot on the buyer." Answer: the girl. Wrong: the man (E).
- Spoken (rotating six, about 25 seconds): "What does the buyer get when her shoes are cleaned?" Answer: clean shoes, work done for her. Wrong: "the tokens" (E).

**E58 (all mark at the store door; the day's ten also speak).** A card pair: a paper windmill and a bookmark, each three tokens; a picture of five tokens.
- Mark 1 (all): "Circle the one you choose, or the envelope to save." Any choice is right.
- Mark 2 (all): "Put a dot on what stops you buying both." Answer: the five tokens. Wrong: a dot on one item, "because it is best" (D).
- Spoken (the day's ten, in the turn): "You can have only one. Why did you choose that one?" Answer: names the limit (three tokens, then two are left) and a reason; saving counts. Wrong: "the windmill is best" (D).

**E59 (no extra exit).** The roster turns are the evidence (items 1 and 7). When a claim names a worker the teacher asks one stem: "You said Davi helps riders. What does Davi know how to do?" Answer: the way, or when to stop. Wrong: "a bus" (B); "because people work" with no source (H).

**Day 60 is the unit plan's items 1 to 7, by their numbers.** Item 2's photograph and caption are held (field 11).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.**
  - Unit 3's day 26 map and key: restored on 57 as the model for a store map, revised on 58 after the shelf check, kept blank from 59 to 61.
  - The "I think ..., because ..." frame: modeled by the teacher until 58, withdrawn on 59. Completed charts (the need and want hoops, money chains, the washing chart) come down on 59; blank maps, picture supports and the frame stay.
  - Know, do, use icons: blank, available on 59 to 61.
- **Key moment 1, 57: the store map.** Ideal instrument: a large floor-plan map with movable shelf symbols and a key box. Spec: print fits.
- **Key moment 2, 58: the store.** Ideal instrument: six stations (a shelf and a folding counter each), price tags with dots, 150 paper tokens, named envelopes, and a dated transaction record. Spec: print fits; each station's record keeper marks a stroke per sale, and the teacher joins the strokes into Our Store Record after 58. The six stations are one per table, so no station waits for equipment.
- **Key moment 3, 60: paper response rows.** Ideal instrument: picture rows and the record sheet. Spec: print fits.
- **A room with nothing.** Boxes for shelves, scrap-paper tokens, a hand-drawn map.

## 6. Days

Every day is 20 to 30 minutes (frame). Parts below add to 20; on a 25-minute day the 5 extra minutes go to the store on 58 and to roster turns on 59 and 60, and a 30-minute day 10. No timer is shown to children.

## Day 57


- **5 min. Reopen.** Hand-back: E56 (F, G), 3 minutes: an object card beside the action done for someone, then a three-picture chain on a new worker. Then 2 minutes, R57: show the saved day 26 map and key and ask "Find a symbol. What does this picture stand for?" (the unit plan's "paper shelf" only if that map has one; if the map is missing, redraw a key). If many missed (F, G), 4 minutes are the repair and the map check is 1.
- **5 min. Teaching first.** The teacher sells and a child buys: a reusable drawing for two tokens, then the paper-folding service for three. "What did the buyer give? What did the seller get? What did the buyer get?" Roles swap. Words with pictures: buy, sell (named on 56). Puppet Pip says error E.
- **7 min. Supported work.** Six tables, one station each. 3 min: lay the dots-and-numeral price tags (1 to 5 across the class) by counting tokens beside each item. 4 min: the table's shelf symbol goes on the large map and in the key, and a child says what it stands for. The teacher checks each table about 30 seconds and hears the six.
- **3 min. Exit.** E57: stems read, all mark, the six heard.

Holds: the teacher holds the key box. Tool: None. By hand: tokens, price tags, shelf symbols. Evidence: E57.

## Day 58


- **3 min. Reopen.** Hand-back: E57 (E, F), 3 minutes: who pays and who is paid, a new exchange by pairs. If many missed, the 3 minutes are the repair.
- **3 min. Organize.** Five tokens each in a named envelope; the rules (a child may say no; return everything; saving is fine). The teacher reads item 1's two scenes aloud before the first turn: a grown-up plants seeds in a window box at their own home without pay, and a gardener tends a public garden for pay, as examples, never as models. "Not all money comes from work."
- **12 min. Store work.** Six stations with rotating buyers, sellers and trades. The teacher moves to the day's ten children, about 70 seconds each: first try, one spoken next step, revised try, for items 1, 4 and 7. Each station's shelf checker checks the shelf against the map and moves the symbol if needed; each station's record keeper strokes each sale. Exchange questions today are practice.
- **2 min. Exit.** E58 at the store door, all mark; tokens return to the bank except a saved one in the envelope.

Holds: stations hold the roles. Tool: None. By hand: tokens, envelopes, price tags, map. Evidence: E58; the ten turns (practice, revised try recorded).

## Day 59


- **2 min. Saved tokens.** A child with a saved token buys one item from the store; others watch.
- **3 min. Reopen.** Hand-back: E58 (D, with K) and R59 share these 3 minutes. R59: "Point to something Our Store Record shows. How does it support your answer?" Completed charts and supplied reasons come down.
- **11 min. Hearing.** The day's ten, about 66 seconds each: first try, next step, revised try, for items 1 and 7, and the E59 stem (what Ms. Idris needs to know to steer the ferry) is heard from the same ten. The other children draw "Why do people work?" with one source card of Work Around Us, Jobs Book or Our Store Record, and tell a partner "I think ..., because ...". Helping, caring and earning can each support a claim.
- **4 min. Close the wonder wall.** Read the wonders, mark the ones the unit answered.

Holds: children hold the claim frame. Tool: None. By hand: source cards, crayons. Evidence: items 1 and 7 (revised tries); the E59 stem.

## Day 60


- **2 min. Directions.** Showing what we know with new cards; marks on your own paper; pointing counts. No completed example is visible.
- **15 min. Fresh tasks.** Five blocks of three minutes, in the unit table's order of items 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6. In each, the teacher reads the prompt aloud once, all children mark, and the teacher hears two of the day's ten, about 90 seconds each, for items 1, 4 and 7 and the short reasons for items 2, 3, 5 and 6, pointing allowed. Item 5's scored ask and answer are new scenes, not day 58's.
- **3 min. Close.** Papers collected; the wonder wall read; no result is said aloud.

Marking: about fifteen teacher minutes after day 60 combine marks, revised tries and dated notes by item (ours, untested). Holds: children hold their papers. Tool: None. Evidence: items 1 to 7.

## Day 61


- **2 min. Reconnect.** Hand back the record by part; each child sees one cell, privately.
- **8 min. Three groups, and pairs.** From the record sheet's not-yet-seen cells: Work (items 1, 2, 7; A, I, B, H), Choice (items 3, 4; C, J, D), Exchange (items 5, 6; E, K, F, G). The teacher leads each for about 2 minutes in turn, and the others use the written move with a partner on a new card. Fewer than four children missing a part work in pairs.
- **10 min. Fresh tries.** Each child marks one fresh card for the part missed (the unit plan's field 6 list), and the teacher hears up to eight whose explanation is still unheard, about 70 seconds each. Unheard parts stay "not yet seen" in the year-end record. Close: "Next time we look back at maps and time."

Holds: groups hold their move. Tool: None. Evidence: the fresh tries; right only after help is "shown with help".

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: 57, "What did the buyer give? What did the seller get?"; 58, "Why did you choose that one, or save?"; 59, "Why do people work? Which source helped you?"
- Written task: the day 59 drawing of why people work with one source card, and the teacher's scribing of the claim in the child's words.
- Look-fors: a claim; a named source detail; a reason joined by "because". Buying and selling placed on the right sides; the limit named on 58. Helping, caring or earning each meet.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 57.** Map-key (unit 3), goods and services (56). **Day 58.** Choosing (55), buying and selling (57). **Day 59.** The "because" frame, workers' knowledge (53). **Day 60.** All, unsupplied. **Day 61.** The missed part only.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: buy, sell, trade, token, save, choose, source. Frames: "I buy ___. I give ___ tokens." "I think ___, because ___." "I choose ___ because ___."
- **Access.** Raised tokens and price dots, large symbols, an adult placing pieces or handing tokens as the child directs. A child may decline any trade or buy.
- **Check accommodations.** Read every prompt aloud; accept home language, signs, pointing, symbols, a private turn or longer time for communicators. Reasoning is judged apart from speech, English and handwriting.
- **Reading load.** None.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain how one repair uses a good, such as a part, while the repair stays a service.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Make a table store of six items with dots for prices and five tokens; swap buyer and seller; save a token and say why; on 59 ask for a claim with one source card. On the last day, 61, redo the one missed part with a new card. Never real money.
- **One projector.** Show the map and cards large; children mark paper rows. Hear the ten at a table while stations or drawing go on.
