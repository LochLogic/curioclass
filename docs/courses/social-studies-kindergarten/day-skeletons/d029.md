## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observation task: on a picture map not seen before each child says where two things are with position words and the key and what is next to what; tells one way a map and a globe are alike and one way they differ, and with help one thing that differs between the two world maps of day 23; names the parts of the school's address and points to the school's town on the state map of day 28; "I think ..., because ..." to the compelling question
- **Note:** Score only revised model and graph records from day 26 and the redrawn map from day 28 after written feedback; day 28 location work is practice, with fresh location responses scored on day 29. Response on day 30.

**Skills:**

- ssk-position-words (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-maps-globes (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-local-maps (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-my-address (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **MA.K.T2.1:** Describe where people, objects, and places are using position words such as near, far, left, right, behind, and between.
- **MA.K.T2.2:** With help, explain how maps and globes are alike and different, including maps of the world designed in different ways.
- **MA.K.T2.5:** Use maps, photographs, or drawings to show and explain where important places are near home or school and how they relate.
- **MA.K.T2.6:** Make maps, drawings, and models that show the physical features of familiar places.
- **D2.Geo.1.K-2:** Make maps, graphs and other pictures of familiar places.
- **D2.Geo.2.K-2:** Use maps, graphs, photographs and similar sources to describe places and the connections that shape them.
- **MA.K.T2.3:** Name the parts of an address: street name and number, city or town, state (Massachusetts), and country (United States).
- **MA.K.T2.4:** With help, find the school's city or town on a state map and the school itself on a street map of that town.
