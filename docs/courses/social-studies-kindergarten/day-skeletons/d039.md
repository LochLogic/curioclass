## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observation task: each child orders picture cards not seen before from a school day, from nature and from a read-aloud with first, next, last; places one event with a word from morning, afternoon, night or yesterday, today, tomorrow and one with long ago, last week or next year; says how an old photograph helps us know about long ago; then answers the compelling question by picking one change, pointing to a named photograph or object, giving a reason and naming one thing that stayed the same
- **Note:** The teacher notes each child's time words over days 36 to 39; day 39 completes what is missing. The answer stays inside the evidence; no judgment of a whole era. Response on day 40 Earlier time-word evidence counts only from revised records after written feedback; initial records remain practice.

**Skills:**

- ssk-sequence-time (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-long-ago-today (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-i-think-because (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **MA.K.T3.3:** Put events from personal life, nature, and texts in time order using words about sequence and time.
- **MA.K.T3.3a:** Use sequence words such as first, next, and last to order actions.
- **MA.K.T3.3b:** Use time words such as now, then, long ago, before, after, today, tomorrow, yesterday, morning, afternoon, night, last or next week, month, and year to place events in time.
- **D2.His.1.K-2:** Put several events in time order.
- **D2.His.2.K-2:** Compare life in the past with life today.
- **D2.His.10.K-2:** Explain how sources can be used to study the past.
