## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Traditions: how families from different backgrounds celebrate (foods, songs, special days, ways of greeting); what is alike and what is different
- **Note:** Religious traditions are described, never practised; no child is asked to share a tradition; families who do not celebrate a holiday are respected.
