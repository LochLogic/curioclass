## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observation task: in the talk circle each child says why a rule on a picture card not seen before exists (one at school, one outside it), says what the class could do if a rule does not work, tells what they think about a class question and what a friend thinks and why, answers the compelling question with "I think ..., because ...", and asks a classmate "Why do you think that?"; wonder wall look back: why our question mattered to us
- **Note:** The teacher notes each child over days 8 and 9, and through the unit how each child follows class rules and talk rules with little reminding (MA.K.T1.1, D2.Civ.9); response on day 10 First-teaching notes are practice; score the later day 7 to 9 rule and discussion attempts after written feedback and revision, and the day 9 personal explanation.

**Skills:**

- ssk-asking-questions (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-rules-why (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-talk-together (grade K): taught or practised here
- ssk-i-think-because (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **D1.1.K-2:** Say why a big inquiry question matters to you personally.
- **MA.K.T1.1:** Follow rules and expectations with little reminding, and with help ask and answer questions about why rules exist.
- **D2.Civ.3.K-2:** Explain why rules are needed and what they are for, at school and elsewhere.
- **D2.Civ.12.K-2:** Identify and explain how rules work in the classroom and school.
- **D2.Civ.9.K-2:** Follow agreed discussion rules, listen to others and decide things as a group.
- **D2.Civ.10.K-2:** Compare your own point of view with other people's.
- **D4.1.K-2:** Make an argument and give reasons for it.
- **D4.4.K-2:** Ask and answer questions about an argument.
