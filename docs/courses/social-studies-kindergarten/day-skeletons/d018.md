## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observation task: each child says what they do in their class job and who counts on it, names what a character in a picture story not seen before did that was fair, caring, responsible or in charge, and tells how the class decided and whether it helped; "I think ..., because ..." to the compelling question
- **Note:** The teacher notes each child over days 17 and 18, including each child doing a class job through to the end, helpfully and respectfully (MA.K.T1.2, D2.Civ.7); response on day 19 Earlier job and decision evidence counts only from later dated attempts after written feedback and revision; first-teaching observations remain practice.
