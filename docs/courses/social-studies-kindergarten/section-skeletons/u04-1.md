## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.K.T3.3** (days 34, 35, 36): Put events from personal life, nature, and texts in time order using words about sequence and time.
- **MA.K.T3.3a** (days 34, 36): Use sequence words such as first, next, and last to order actions.
- **MA.K.T3.3b** (days 34, 35, 36): Use time words such as now, then, long ago, before, after, today, tomorrow, yesterday, morning, afternoon, night, last or next week, month, and year to place events in time.
- **D2.His.1.K-2** (days 34, 36): Put several events in time order.
- skill ssk-sequence-time (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Hook, day 34:** show original drawings of a washboard and a washing machine. Both help wash clothes. What might change about the work? Post the compelling question and children's wonders, then order familiar school actions.
> - **Co-write supporting questions:** How do we show what happened first (34 and 36)? How do time words place events (35)? What can old photographs and objects tell us (37)? What changed and stayed in everyday work (38)?
> - **Teaching:** model ordering on 34, calendar anchors on 35 and left-to-right strips on 36. On 37 model noticing before guessing, then a claim and because. Children compare on 38; withdraw supplied reasons on 39. Teach sequence, photograph, object, clue, changed and stayed with pictures. Ask who made each source, when and why; read known information and label unknown motives unknown. Compare photograph, object and secondary drawing; distinguish visible evidence from inference.
>
> - **Carried:** prepare ordered pictures on 34 to 35 (strip on 35); name the timeline on 36, tying left-to-right order to time words. Introduce continuity-change on 37; revise its two columns with a shared-purpose sentence on 38. Withdraw completed examples on 38, keeping blank strips and charts. Retain unit 3's source comparison and claim-and-because routine; set aside its map-key. Retain perspective when children defend different changes. All talk supports continue.
