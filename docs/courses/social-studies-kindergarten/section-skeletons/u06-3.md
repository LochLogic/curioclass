## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.K.T4.3** (days 57, 58, 60, 61): With help, give examples of goods and services people pay for with money they earn.
- **MA.K.T4.2** (days 57, 58, 60, 61): With help, ask and answer questions about buying, selling, and trading, and explain how people choose among things they need and want.
- **D2.Eco.1.K-2** (days 58, 60): Explain that scarcity forces people to make choices.
- **MA.K.T4.1** (days 59, 60): With help, describe things people do when they work inside and outside the home, using experience and texts.
- **D2.Eco.3.K-2** (days 59, 60): Describe the skills and knowledge needed to make certain goods and services.

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Co-write supporting questions:** What does work require (52 to 53)? Why must people choose (54 to 55)? How do goods, services and paying help people get things (56 to 58)?
> - **Closing, day 59:** the class returns to the question; scribe each child's claim, revised and recorded on 60, and named evidence from Work Around Us, Jobs Book or Our Store Record. Helping, caring and earning can support different answers; paid work is not the only work.
> - **Teaching:** model a driver's knowledge, skill and tools on 53, a limited choice on 55 and a source-based because on 56. On 57 the teacher sells, a child buys, the class asks what was got and given. Step back on 58 to 59. Name each source's maker, time and purpose; compare a book description with an observed action. A photograph shows actions, not everything a worker knows. Teach work, skill, tool, need, want, choose, buy, sell, trade, goods, services, earn and pay with pictures.
>
> - **Carried:** restore unit 4's continuity-change chart on 53 for washing work, naming change and continuity without ranking eras. Restore unit 3's day 26 map and key on 57 and revise it after checking shelf locations on 58. Keep unit 5's perspective and spoken-reason routines for differing choices. Remove completed charts and supplied reasons on 59; retain blank maps, picture supports and the claim frame through 61.
