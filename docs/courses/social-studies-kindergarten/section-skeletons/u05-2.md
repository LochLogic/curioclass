## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.K.T1.4** (days 45, 46, 47): Ask and answer questions and use books to learn about national symbols, songs, and texts of the United States.
- **MA.K.T1.4a** (days 45): Explain why the United States flag has its colors, stars, and stripes.
- **MA.K.T1.4d** (days 45, 46, 47): Explain what the words of the "Pledge of Allegiance" mean.
- **MA.K.T1.4b** (days 46): Explain why the bald eagle is the national emblem of the United States.
- **MA.K.T1.4c** (days 47): Explain why "The Star-Spangled Banner" is the national anthem.
- skill ssk-national-symbols (grade K)

- kind: Understanding. Meets new content: the goal is taught here, from a hook and a worked example.
- goal: The flag: its colors, thirteen stripes for the first thirteen states and one star for each state, fifty today, and why (the 1777 flag law for the stripes, a star for each new state since 1818); "the record says" or "people say it means" spoken sort; the Pledge begun: a promise to be loyal to the country the flag stands for
- note: Lean RI.K.1: ELA unit 1, already taught. At most two statements per symbol, two picture bins; the teacher says the dates, and no date or statement goes on the student page First-teaching symbol responses remain practice; written feedback supports revised explanations during days 48 and 49.
