## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D2.Civ.11.K-2** (days 13, 14): Explain how people can decide things together in the classroom.
- **D4.8.K-2** (days 14): Use listening, consensus and voting to decide on and take action in the classroom.
- skill ssk-class-decision (grade K)
- skill ssk-i-think-because (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Co-write supporting questions:** Who counts on a job (11 to 12)? How can we choose together (13 to 14)? What do story characters show us (15 to 16)? Did our decision help (17)?
> - **Teaching:** model finishing a job on 12, listening and seeking agreement on 13, and pointing to a story action on 15. On 14 children choose and act; on 17 they judge results. Teach job, responsible, respect, fair, caring, just (putting an unfair thing right), in charge, agree and vote with pictures. Name each source's maker, occasion and purpose; distinguish stories from records of our actions. Compare the dated chart with children's observations, including disagreement.
>
> - **Carried:** keep unit 1's perspective columns on 13 to 14, then revisit dissent on 17. Keep its spoken claim and because routine on 14 and 18 as review, not new ownership of D4.1 or D4.4. Withdraw supplied reasons on 18; retain picture and communication supports. Both models continue.
>
> On 14 offer two safe, accessible proposals from children's ideas. Seek a shared revision before voting, even when confirming agreement. Each child directs one identical block into a choice tower. Compare and count together. For a tie, revise and vote again; if still tied, agree to try both safely in turn. Record dissent without penalty.
