## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.K.T1.1** (days 3, 5): Follow rules and expectations with little reminding, and with help ask and answer questions about why rules exist.
- **D2.Civ.3.K-2** (days 3, 4): Explain why rules are needed and what they are for, at school and elsewhere.
- **D2.Civ.12.K-2** (days 4, 5): Identify and explain how rules work in the classroom and school.
- skill ssk-rules-why (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Co-write supporting questions:** What do different classmates share (day 2)? Why have rules here and elsewhere (3 to 4)? How can we improve a rule (5)? How can people disagree and still listen (6 to 7)? What supports our answer (8 to 9)?
> - **Teaching:** name each source's maker, setting and purpose before use. Distinguish a made-up illustration from our real charter. Model rule and consequence on day 3, claim and reason on 5, and listening on 6. On days 3 and 5 the teacher models a child asking "Why do we have this rule?" and an answer; children echo both. On day 8 the class reopens one charter rule and chooses with the talk rules (item 5). On 7 compare two views without ranking people; on 8 children choose reasons. Teach rule, fair, safe, reason and point of view through pictures and actions.
>
> - **Carried:** perspective becomes two picture-and-reason columns on day 7, revisited on 9. Claim-evidence-reasoning is tied to the spoken because on 5 and 8. Withdraw teacher-supplied reasons on 9; keep picture references and communication supports. Retain both models for later units.
