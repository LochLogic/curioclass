## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.K.T2.3** (days 27, 28, 29): Name the parts of an address: street name and number, city or town, state (Massachusetts), and country (United States).
- **MA.K.T2.4** (days 28, 29): With help, find the school's city or town on a state map and the school itself on a street map of that town.
- **MA.K.T2.1** (days 29, 30): Describe where people, objects, and places are using position words such as near, far, left, right, behind, and between.
- **MA.K.T2.2** (days 29): With help, explain how maps and globes are alike and different, including maps of the world designed in different ways.
- **MA.K.T2.5** (days 29, 30): Use maps, photographs, or drawings to show and explain where important places are near home or school and how they relate.
- **MA.K.T2.6** (days 29): Make maps, drawings, and models that show the physical features of familiar places.
- **D2.Geo.1.K-2** (days 29): Make maps, graphs and other pictures of familiar places.
- **D2.Geo.2.K-2** (days 29): Use maps, graphs, photographs and similar sources to describe places and the connections that shape them.
- skill ssk-my-address (grade K)
- skill ssk-position-words (grade K)
- skill ssk-maps-globes (grade K)
- skill ssk-local-maps (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Co-write supporting questions:** Which words help us locate things (20 to 21)? What can maps and globes show (22 to 23)? How can pictures stand for places (24 to 26)? How does an address help locate a school (27 to 28)?
> - **Closing, day 29:** each child claims how to help someone locate things, citing Our Place Record, Two World Maps or School Garden. The teacher writes the child's words and named evidence. Words, models and maps can support different answers.
>
> - **Limits:** no scale calculations, compass directions, projection terminology or home addresses. World-map design is supported exposure. Locating our state within the country on 28 is unassessed extension. Unit 5 owns the interleaved holiday lesson.
> - **Carried:** tie map-key to observed features on 24, use it in models on 25, revise unclear symbols on 26, and withdraw supplied symbol meanings on 29. Keep the key and access supports. Retain unit 2's perspective routine for differing map choices on 23 and its claim-and-because routine on 26 and 29; supply no closing reason.
