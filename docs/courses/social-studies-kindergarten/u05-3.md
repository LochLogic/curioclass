No tradition is called strange, wrong or better. No greeting custom is named without a community page that describes it; say only that people greet in many ways and no one greeting is universal. River Town and Hill Town stand for no real group.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: tradition, celebrate, alike, different, reason, special, the page says. Frames: "Both ___, but ___." "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." Home-language words are welcome; a name is said as its owners say it.
- **Access.** Large print and high contrast; raised-line pictures; descriptions of each picture's visible detail. Pointing linked to a dictated explanation, signs, communication aids or a private exchange all count.
- **Check accommodations.** Read every source and label aloud; accept a point, a token, a home language or a private conversation; extra time. Judge content apart from English, speech, vision, handwriting and motor skill.
- **Reading load.** None; labels read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain why two different supported reasons for remembering may both be defensible.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Use the two food pictures and a chart with two columns: say what is alike, then what differs, and why each family or community might do it its way, only where the page says.
