# Scope and sequence: Kindergarten: Living and Learning Together

Course `social-studies-kindergarten`, kindergarten, draft 2026-11-14. 64 course days (58 in six units, 3 course-level
check days, 3 respond days), about two lessons a week of 20 to 30 minutes over 30 weeks. The days are in
data/plans/social-studies-kindergarten/days.csv; this page gives the judgment behind them. No base text, so no
source map.

**How the year sits on the calendar.** The course meets about twice a week, so `school_day` is filled. Unit 5's three
on-the-day holiday lessons (course days 41 to 43) are taught on their dates, inside units 2, 3 and 4 (about weeks
7, 12 and 19), so their school days run ahead of their course-day numbers; every other day is in course-day order.
Before the mid-year check the course runs a little over two lessons a week (32 lessons in weeks 1 to 14), after it
a little under two (26 lessons in weeks 17 to 29). The course-level checks sit on the target weeks, 15 and 30.

**What holds every day.** Every source is a printed photograph, object, picture card or read-aloud: a day assumes at
most one shared classroom device and always has a paper path. Every unit opens on the wonder wall with its
compelling question and closes with a spoken "I think ..., because ..." answer. The end-of-unit assessment in every
unit is an observation task (assess `observe`) over the unit's last lessons, with a task on a picture, map or card
not seen before, and the response is one respond day after it (counted once). Kindergarten has no earlier grade,
so no unit opens with a readiness check.

## Unit 1. Learning together (days 1 to 10, weeks 1 to 5)

*What makes our class a good place to learn?* Anchor MA.K.T1.1; C3 D1.1, D2.Civ.3, D2.Civ.9, D2.Civ.10, D2.Civ.12,
D4.1, D4.4 (all K-2).

The class opens its wonder wall, notices that everyone in it is different and still shares one room, then learns why
rules exist, inside and outside school, and makes its own rules with a reason for each (days 1 to 5). Talk rules and
two points of view side by side come next (days 6 and 7), because the "because" sentence (day 8) needs a circle that
listens. Rules come before talk because the talk rules are themselves rules with reasons. Load: ssk-rules-why carries
three days (it holds three standards and the class's own rules); the two talk rows two days and one day.

- Assessment: observation task on days 8 and 9 (the reason for a rule on a picture card not seen before, the
  compelling question answered with "I think ..., because ...", a "Why do you think that?" asked); respond day 10.
- Candidate settled: **kept**, the "all about our class" piece (New York kindergarten, self and others), inside
  day 2 at no added day. No child is pushed to share about themselves.

## Unit 2. Doing our part (days 11 to 19, weeks 5 to 9)

*How can each of us help our class?* Anchor MA.K.T1.2, MA.K.T1.3; C3 D2.Civ.7, D2.Civ.11, D4.8.

Class jobs and following through (days 11 and 12) open the unit, with unit 1's rules as a retrieval task. The class
decision comes early (days 13 and 14: a warm-up plant vote, then a real classroom problem the class names, reasons,
agreement, a vote by blocks or hands, and a plan), so that the read-aloud days on fair, caring and in-charge
characters (days 15 and 16) fill the week before the look back (day 17), which the frame asks for "after a week".
This is the year's informed action; the class picks the problem.

- Assessment: observation task on days 17 and 18 (class job, a character in a picture story not seen before, how the
  class decided and whether it helped); respond day 19.
- Candidate settled: **kept**, an open folktale of honesty or courage beside our read-aloud (California K.1.2),
  inside day 16 at no added day.
- Leans: RL.K.3 (ELA unit 2 starts about week 7, so days 15 and 16 carry the frame's bridge); K.CC.B.5 (the vote is
  kept to two block towers, compared by height and counted with the teacher).
- The Indigenous Peoples' Day or Columbus Day lesson (day 41) is taught between days 14 and 15.

## Unit 3. Where things are (days 20 to 30, weeks 10 to 15)

*How can we show someone where things are?* Anchor MA.K.T2.1 to MA.K.T2.6; C3 D2.Geo.1, D2.Geo.2.

Position words first (days 20 and 21), because every map task uses them; then globe and map, with the two
differently designed world maps as exposure only (days 22 and 23); then picture maps of places the children know,
with the key, a block model and a drawn map (days 24 to 26, the unit's made product, so the most days); the
address last (days 27 and 28), with the school's address and the student's own state worked through two or three
example states, never a child's own address.

- Assessment: observation task on day 29 (position words and the key on a picture map not seen before, map or globe,
  the parts of the school's address), with the maps and models of days 25 and 26; respond day 30.
- Candidates settled: **kept**, map symbols (California K.4.3) and locating familiar places (New York K.6), inside days
  24 to 26 at no added day, since they reinforce the owned rows. **Kept as an extension**, finding our state on a
  United States map, a few minutes of day 28, not assessed and not owned.
- Lean: K.G.A.1 (math unit 3, the same weeks); the unit teaches the words itself.
- The Veterans Day and Thanksgiving lesson (day 42) is taught between days 24 and 25.

## Unit 4. Long ago and today (days 34 to 40, weeks 17 to 20)

*Which change from long ago made the biggest difference to everyday work?* Anchor MA.K.T3.3, MA.K.T3.3a,
MA.K.T3.3b; C3 D2.His.1, D2.His.2, D2.His.10.

Sequence words, then every time word of MA.K.T3.3b, then the picture-strip timeline from a story and an
informational read-aloud (days 34 to 36), so that the then-and-now days (37 and 38) can place old photographs and
objects in time. Sources are open archive photographs, each with "who made it, is it old or new".

- Assessment: observation task on day 39 (picture cards not seen before ordered with sequence and time words; one
  change picked, pointed to in a named photograph, with a reason and one thing that stayed); respond day 40.
- Candidate settled: **kept**, how people got water, food, clothing and travel long ago (California kindergarten),
  inside day 38 at no added day.
- Lean: RI.K.3 (ELA unit 3, the same weeks): picture cards ordered before any text.
- The Martin Luther King Jr. Day lesson (day 43) is taught between days 38 and 39.

## Unit 5. Days we remember (days 41 to 51; days 41 to 43 in weeks 7, 12 and 19, days 44 to 51 in weeks 21 to 24)

*Why do we remember some days and some people?* Anchor MA.K.T3.1, MA.K.T1.4 with MA.K.T1.4a to d, MA.K.T3.2.

The three on-the-day lessons come first in the unit's numbering and are taught on their dates; day 44 gathers them
and the rest of the year's civic holidays on the school-year strip (unit 4's timeline) about the time of Presidents'
Day. Then one day each for the flag, the bald eagle, and the anthem with the Pledge (days 45 to 47), each with the
spoken "the record says" or "people say it means" sort, and two days on traditions (48 and 49) using unit 1's
perspective. Every lettered part of MA.K.T1.4 has its own day.

- Assessment: observation task on day 50 (a holiday and who it remembers; one symbol's why, from a photograph not
  seen before; two traditions alike and different); respond day 51.
- Candidate settled: **kept**, Washington and Lincoln named with Presidents' Day (California kindergarten), inside
  day 44 at no added day.
- Sensitivity: days 41 to 44 (Native content, Thanksgiving from the record and from the Wampanoag nations today, hard
  history for Martin Luther King Jr. Day) and days 48 and 49 (traditions) are flagged for the sensitivity read.
- Lean: RI.K.1 (ELA unit 1, already taught).

## Unit 6. Work and trade (days 52 to 61, weeks 25 to 29)

*Why do people work?* Anchor MA.K.T4.1 to MA.K.T4.3; C3 D2.Eco.1, D2.Eco.3.

Work and its tools (days 52 and 53), needs and wants and choosing because there is not enough (54 and 55), goods and
services (56), then the class store with its picture-map shelves and token prices (57 and 58), and the spoken answer
to the compelling question (59). Personal finance stays a touch inside day 58 (a child may save a token), no added
day.

- Assessment: observation task on day 60 (needs and wants, goods and services sorted from cards not seen before, a
  worker's work and tools), with the store and closing days; respond day 61.
- Candidate settled: **kept**, matching jobs to work and tools with community helpers (California and New York
  kindergarten), inside day 53 at no added day.
- Lean: K.CC.B.5 (math units 2 and 6, already taught); tokens worth one each, prices 1 to 5, no printed coins.

## Course-level checks

| Check | Course days | Week | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, mid-year | 31 and 32, respond 33 | 15 (respond in week 16) | Why a rule exists; an "I think ..., because ..." answer; position words on a picture map not seen before; map or globe |
| 2, year-end | 62 and 63, respond 64 | 30 | Time order; then and now from photographs not seen before; a picture map with its key; a claim about a photograph; needs, wants, goods and services; a holiday |

32, math 17 and 31, science 14 and 29).
