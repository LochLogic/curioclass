# Section 6.1: Work, and what a worker knows


## 1. Standards and practices


- **MA.K.T4.1, days 52 and 53, whole standard.** Children "describe things people do when they work inside and outside the home", with help, "using experience and texts". Describe is spoken, pointed or drawn. Inside is a home; outside is anywhere else (a clinic, a field, a road). Experience is the work children do at school, never at home. Texts are Work Around Us and Jobs Book, read aloud. Help is recorded.
- **D2.Eco.3.K-2, day 53, both parts.** "Describe the knowledge and abilities needed to make goods or provide services": what a worker knows, and what a worker can do because they learned it. A tool supports the description and never replaces it. The words goods and services are taught on 56, so these days say only "work".
- **Limits that bite.** No question about who works in a child's home, and no child reports what any grown-up does. No pay amounts, no "best job", no job tied to a kind of body, age or family. Work at home counts whether or not it is paid.
- **Connections.** Unit 2's Our Jobs Chart (review, 52). Unit 4's continuity-change chart (review, 53, no ranking of then and now). The spoken "I think ..., because ..." from unit 1 (review, the reasons on both days).
- **What the teacher page must explain.**
  - Work is the wider word; a job is a regular piece of work others count on (unit 2).
  - On 52 "paid" is a plain spoken contrast: someone gives the worker money for the work. No amounts. Earn and pay are taught on 56.
  - A worker knows (kept in mind), does (a skill, learned by practice) and uses (a tool). A picture shows doing and tools; it hides knowing, so the book's words tell it.
  - Every worker in Work Around Us and Jobs Book is invented and the teacher says so. The facts in those stories are story facts, not claims about every real worker, and none depends on a source outside Curio Class.
  - Families differ: nobody is praised, pitied or asked.

## 2. Where it sits


Two unmarked reviews from unit plan field 4 are used: R52 (Our Jobs Chart, unit 2) inside the hook, and R53 (the washing chart, unit 4) in the opening of 53. A miss gets the plan's repair, and results go to the record sheet as notes, not scores. The exits go to 6.2's opening.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** 1 (MA.K.T4.1, work inside and outside the home, from experience and text) and 2 (D2.Eco.3.K-2 with MA.K.T4.1, the bakery photograph). Both are on the unit plan and are sat on days 58 to 60 in section 6.3. This section teaches their parts with cases that never return: item 2's photograph is not shown here. Its title, date and place are source to check (the archive record gave an access error on 2026-11-19); the fallback is another public-domain federal worker photograph, or an original drawing said to be a drawing.

**Record sheet (the section's record).** One row per child, with one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Columns: E52 mark, E52 heard, E53 know, E53 do, E53 use, E53 heard. A rotating six are heard each day, so each child is heard about once a week.

**E52, in the last three minutes.** Cards, new and printed "invented for practice"; the teacher says "this is a pretend town". The teacher tells aloud: "The shop pays the window cleaner. Nobody pays the grown-up who sweeps." Card 1: a grown-up sweeping the floor in a pretend home. Card 2: a window cleaner at a pretend shop. Card 3: a child on a swing.
- Mark (all): "Circle each picture where someone is doing work." Answer: cards 1 and 2. Wrong: circles card 2 only, because that one is paid (A). Circling the swing is no register letter: ask "who does it help?" and look again.
- Spoken (rotating six, about 15 seconds each): "Is sweeping the floor work? Tell me why." Answer: yes, it makes the floor clean for people, whether or not anyone pays. Wrong: "No, nobody pays" (A).

**E53, in the last three minutes.** The teacher reads a fresh Jobs Book page, "invented for practice": "In Pine Hollow, Ms. Okonkwo is the crossing helper at Birch Corner. She learned the safe times to cross and the rules for cars. She holds up a stop sign and walks beside the children as they cross." Paper: the same row of five pictures printed three times, marked only with one, two and three dots: a head with cars stopped and a child crossing; Ms. Okonkwo walking beside children; a stop sign; a hammer; a loaf of bread.
- Mark (all), three stems, one each: "Row one: point to something she knows." Answer: the head picture. "Row two: point to something she does." Answer: walking beside children. "Row three: point to something she uses." Answer: the stop sign. Wrong: the sign or the walking picture for what she knows (B). Choosing the hammer or the bread is a guess, no letter: read the page again.
- Spoken (rotating six, about 25 seconds each): "Tell me one thing Ms. Okonkwo has to know to do her work." Answer: the safe times to cross, or the rules for cars. Wrong: "a stop sign" or "she is a crossing helper" (B).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.**
  - Unit 2's Our Jobs Chart (review, 52): brought out, read, never edited.
  - Unit 4's two-column continuity-change chart (review, 53): washboard and washing machine drawings, and the washing photograph only if unit 4 cleared it. Changed and stayed are named, no era ranked.
  - The know, do, use frame (new, 53): three icons, a head, a hand and a tool. Taught by the teacher on 53, held by pairs the same day, kept through 61.
  - The "I think ..., because ..." frame (unit 1, review): used for the spoken reasons.
- **Key moment 1, 52: inside and outside the home.** Ideal instrument: two floor hoops, one drawn as a house, with movable work cards. Spec: no tool; objects and print fit.
- **Key moment 2, 53: know, do, use.** Ideal instrument: three icon cards beside one worker's picture, moved into place. Spec: print fits.
- **A room with nothing.** Scrap-paper cards, a chalk circle for the home, a hand-drawn icon strip.

## 6. Days

Every day is 20 to 30 minutes (frame). Parts below add to 20; a 25-minute day adds its 5 to the supported work, a 30-minute day 10. No timer is shown to children.

## Day 52


- **2 min. Hook and question.** The teacher sets out paper at each table while Work Around Us's first picture shows a grown-up cooking in a pretend home. The set-out is one row of Our Jobs Chart (R52): "What work was that? Who did it help?" Post "Why do people work?"; scribe three wonders.
- **5 min. Teaching first.** Work Around Us, made by Curio Class in 2026 to think with, invented people. Three pictures: cooking at home (error A), painting the inside of a home (error I), building a wall outside. Words with pictures: work, inside, outside, paid, not paid. Puppet Pip says each wrong sentence of field 4, then is corrected.
- **10 min. Supported work.** Tables of five, one set of seven cards each (cooking, caring for a small child, painting, building, farming, driving a bus, nursing) and two hoops, home and not home. 4 min: each child places a card, says what the worker does and who it helps (the sixth and seventh cards are done together). 4 min: each child draws a work they do at school; the teacher hears the six, about 40 seconds each. 2 min: tables set their drawings beside Our Jobs Chart.
- **3 min. Exit.** E52: stems read, all mark, the six heard.

Holds: the teacher holds the hoops and the cards. Tool: None. By hand: cards, hoops, crayons. Evidence: E52; the school-work drawing for item 1 (experience).

## Day 53


- **4 min. Reopen.** Hand-back: E52 (A), 2 minutes: the pretend café cook beside the home cook. Then 2 minutes: unit 4's washing chart (R53): "What changed? What stayed? Which clue helps?" Post "What does work need?" If many missed E52 (A), the four minutes are the repair and the washing chart moves into the then-and-now part, which drops its farm strip.
- **5 min. Teaching first.** Jobs Book (Curio Class, 2026, drawings and read-aloud words): Davi drives a bus in Pine Hollow. The teacher models know (the way, when to stop), do (steer and stop gently, a skill) and use (the bus, the mirror), icon by icon. "A picture shows what Davi does; the book tells what he knows." Words with pictures: skill, tool, know (error B).
- **6 min. Supported work.** Jobs Book: Mina grows beans on a pretend farm. Pairs place the head, hand and tool icons beside her picture and say each aloud with "I think ..., because ..." (the page names what Mina knows, does and uses). Two school helpers are shown as drawings; a real helper's photograph or visit is optional, with their agreement, and never needed. The teacher hears pairs, noting help.
- **2 min. Then and now.** Two drawings of one pretend farm, planting by hand and with a machine; each pair names one change and one thing that stayed (the work of growing food).
- **3 min. Exit.** E53: stems read, all mark, the six heard; the last mark is a new two-picture strip of digging a flower bed, circle what changed.

Holds: pairs hold the three icons. Tool: None. By hand: icon cards, Jobs Book pages, paper rows. Evidence: E53; the hearing for items 1 and 2.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: 52, "What is the worker doing? Who does it help?"; 53, "What does the worker know, do and use?"
- Written task, 52: a drawing of a work done at school, with one dictated line for who it helps. The teacher scribes.
- Look-fors: a worker doing something; who it helps; inside or outside the home placed right; for 53, a know, a do and a use told apart. Drawing, pointing or an aid counts.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 52.** Unit 2's jobs and the "who does it help?" question.
- **Day 53.** Work counts paid or not (day 52); unit 4's changed and stayed; the "because" frame.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: work, helps, inside, outside, know, do, use, tool. Frames: "___ is working. It helps ___." "She knows ___. She does ___. She uses ___." Home-language words welcome.
- **Access.** Raised icons, large cards, an adult placing cards as the child directs, a drawing in place of speech. Judge the idea, not the English or the motor control.
- **Check accommodations.** All text read aloud; point, sign, symbols or a private turn. Unheard children stay "not yet seen", never guessed.
- **Reading load.** None.
- **Extension, same standard.** A second worker on the farm page: what does another worker there know?

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Use four pictures on the table and two paper loops. Ask what the worker does and who it helps. On 53 read the driver page and set the three icons. Never ask about the child's home. On 53, ask E53 aloud.
- **One projector.** Show a picture at a time; children mark paper rows. The class marks before the teacher says an answer.
