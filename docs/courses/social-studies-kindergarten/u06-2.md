# Section 6.2: Needs, wants, choices, goods and services


## 1. Standards and practices


- **MA.K.T4.2, days 54 and 55.** Two parts here. Children "explain how people choose among things they need and want": need or want on 54, and choosing when there is not enough for both on 55. They "ask and answer questions about ... trading" in the sticker trade on 55 ("Would you trade?", yes or no). Buying and selling are asked in 6.3.
- **D2.Eco.1.K-2, days 54 and 55.** "Explain why not having enough for everything requires a choice": the connection between not enough and deciding, with the limit named aloud.
- **MA.K.T4.3, day 56, both halves.** "Give examples of goods and services people pay for with money they earn": a good (a thing you can hold), a service (something a person does for you), and the money coming from work. Help is recorded.
- **Limits that bite.** No coin values, change, wages, budgets or formal opportunity cost. No question about what a family has or buys. Needs are never rewards. Trade is voluntary. Money comes from work and in other ways; this unit follows one way, said so aloud. No printed coins or bills.
- **Connections.** Unit 2's choosing together and unit 5's "hear why someone chose differently" (day 55, two children choose differently, both fine). Davi and Mina from 6.1 return on 56 as workers who earn (review of 53).
- **What the teacher page must explain.**
  - Need means something people must have to stay well: food, water, shelter, clothing. A want is something we like and can stay well without. Context matters: a coat is a need in snow and a hat may be one in sun, and a person's access needs (a ramp) are needs.
  - Not enough is shown only by classroom facts (one space, one sticker spot), never as a fact about any family.
  - A good is a thing you can hold; a service is work done for you. The thing a service uses is still a good.
  - Why the "because" is modeled from a picture on 56: a reason names what the source shows.
  - Wants are fine to have; nothing here says a want is wrong.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** 3 (needs and wants), 4 (choosing, scarcity) and 6 (goods, services, money from work), on the unit plan, sat on day 60. This section's exit cards never reuse their cases (mittens, clean water, pinwheel, crown; paper maze, finger puppet; stool, bicycle repair).

**Record sheet.** One row per child; one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A rotating six are heard each day. Every card is new, printed "invented for practice", and the teacher says "this is a pretend class".

**E54.** Cards: a bed under a roof; the same bed with a ribbon; a paper lantern.
- Mark (all): "Circle the one a person must have to stay well." Answer: the bed under a roof. Wrong: the ribbon or the lantern (C).
- Spoken (six, about 25 seconds): "Why is that one a need?" Answer: you need a safe place to sleep to stay well; the others are for looking nice. Wrong: "because I want it" (C); "everybody needs the same kind of bed" (J).

**E55.** A pretend class has one empty space on the wall and two posters, a bird and a fish. Cards: the empty space, the bird poster, the fish poster.
- Mark 1 (all): "Circle the poster you would put up." Any choice is right.
- Mark 2 (all): "Put a dot on what means you can only pick one." Answer: the empty space. Wrong: a dot on a poster, because it is "best" (D).
- Spoken (six, about 25 seconds): "Why can you put up only one?" Answer: there is only one space. Wrong: "the bird is best" (D).

**E56.** Paper: two rows of three pictures, marked with one and two dots. Row one: a blanket on a bed; a tuner at a piano; a pencil box. Row two: a basket; a dog on a leash held by a child; a singing lesson.
- Mark (all): "Row one: point to the one that is a service." Answer: the tuner. Wrong: the blanket, "because someone touched it" (F). "Row two: point to the one that is a good." Answer: the basket. Wrong: the dog on the leash, "because the child holds the leash" (F).
- Spoken (six, about 30 seconds): the teacher then tells "A pretend girl pays a piano tuner to tune her piano." and asks "Where could the girl get the money to pay the tuner?" Answer: from work someone did; some money comes from work. Wrong: "It was just there" (G).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.**
  - The know, do, use icons (6.1): kept on the wall; Davi and Mina return on 56 as earners.
  - The "I think ..., because ..." frame: spoken on all three days; on 56 the teacher models a reason that names a source; supplied reasons withdraw from 59.
  - The two floor hoops: need and want on 54, goods and services on 56.
- **Key moment 1, 55: one space, two stickers.** Ideal instrument: a card with one paper space and two paper stickers per child. Spec: no tool; print fits.
- **Key moment 2, 56: work, earn, pay.** Ideal instrument: a three-picture chain on movable cards. Spec: print fits. The picture of a token is only a stand-in for money; tokens are first handled on 57.
- **A room with nothing.** Scrap-paper stickers, a drawn space, a chalk hoop.

## 6. Days

Every day is 20 to 30 minutes (frame). Parts below add to 20; a 25-minute day adds its 5 to the supported work, a 30-minute day 10. No timer is shown to children.

## Day 54


- **5 min. Reopen.** Hand-back: E53 (B): a worker's tool-only label beside know, do and use pictures, 3 minutes. Then 2 minutes: the hook, an apple and a kite on the rug, "Which would you pick to stay well?" Post "Why must people choose?" and scribe three wonders. If many missed E53 (B), 4 minutes are the repair and the hook is 1.
- **5 min. Teaching first.** Work Around Us, Curio Class, 2026, invented children. Four needs on picture cards: an apple, a cup of water, a roof, a coat on a snowy day. Two wants: a kite, a toy boat. The teacher models each with "I think it is a need, because ..." Words with pictures: need, want. Puppet Pip says C and J, then is corrected (field 4).
- **7 min. Supported work.** Tables of five, eight cards and two hoops each. 4 min: each child sorts a card or two with the frame (pair help allowed). 3 min: each child draws one thing to look at or play with and tells a partner "need or want?"; the teacher hears the six, about 30 seconds each. No child is asked what they have.
- **3 min. Exit.** E54: stems read, all mark, the six heard.

Holds: the teacher holds the two hoops. Tool: None. By hand: cards, hoops, crayons. Evidence: E54.

## Day 55


- **4 min. Reopen.** Hand-back: E54 (C, J), 3 minutes: one snow card and one sun card sorted into need and want. Post "Why must people choose?" If many missed, the 4 minutes are the repair.
- **5 min. Teaching first.** The teacher has the kite and the toy boat and one place on the table. "I can only pick one. I choose the kite, because ..." A child picks the boat with a different reason; both are fine (unit 5's hearing why). Words: choose, not enough, trade. Puppet Pip says D and K.
- **8 min. Supported work.** Each child has a card with one paper space and two different paper stickers, the same for everyone. 2 min: choose one for the space and tell a partner why; the teacher hears the six here. 4 min: trade round. A child asks "Would you trade my moon for your star?" and the partner says yes or no; trade only if both say yes, and a no is a fine answer. 2 min: tables tell who traded and who did not, no one ranked. Stickers go back to the box.
- **3 min. Exit.** E55: stems read, all mark, the six heard.

Holds: children hold the one-space card. Tool: None. By hand: paper stickers, space cards, crayons for the exit dot. Evidence: E55; asks and answers in the trade round (MA.K.T4.2, trading part), noted for the six.

## Day 56


- **4 min. Reopen.** Hand-back: E55 (D), 2 minutes: "I like it" beside "only enough for one, so I choose this because ..." Then 2 minutes: post "How do goods, services and paying help people get things?" and show Mina's and Davi's pictures from 6.1. If many missed (D), 3 minutes are the repair.
- **6 min. Teaching first.** Jobs Book, Curio Class, 2026: Mina sells a bag of beans at her stall and earns money. She pays Davi for a bus ride. Goods: the beans, a shirt. Services: the ride, a haircut. Money from work is followed through the three pictures; some money comes in other ways. Buy and sell are named for the one who pays and the one who is paid. The teacher models a source-based because: "I think the ride is a service, because the Jobs Book picture shows Davi driving for his rider." Words with pictures: goods, services, earn, pay, buy, sell. Puppet Pip says F and G.
- **7 min. Supported work.** Tables of five, eight cards (four goods, four services) and two hoops. 4 min: each child sorts a card and says "I think ... because the picture shows ..." 3 min: each table follows one money chain picture, work then earning then paying, and says who did the work; the teacher hears the six, about 30 seconds each.
- **4 min. Exit.** E56: stems read, all mark, the six heard.

Holds: the teacher holds the chain pictures. Tool: None. By hand: cards, hoops, chain pictures. Evidence: E56.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: 54, "Is it a need or a want? How do you know?"; 55, "Why can you not have both? Why did you choose that one?"; 56, "Is it a thing you can hold or something someone does for you? Where did the money come from?"
- Written task, 54: a drawing of one thing to look at or play with; the teacher scribes "I want ...". Never a family's thing.
- Look-fors: a need told apart from a want with a reason; the limit named on 55 ("only one"); a good and a service told apart on 56, with work as the source of money. A point, a sign or a picture counts.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 54.** Know, do, use and the workers of 53 (the farmer grows food, a need). **Day 55.** Need and want from 54. **Day 56.** Choosing from 55; workers and tools from 6.1.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: need, want, choose, only one, trade, goods, services, earn, pay. Frames: "I think it is a need, because ___." "I choose ___ because ___." "It is a service because someone ___."
- **Access.** Raised hoops, large cards, an adult placing pieces as the child directs, pointing for speech. A child who finds trading hard may only choose.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all aloud; accept pointing, symbols, signs or a private turn. Unheard children stay "not yet seen".
- **Reading load.** None.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find one good and one service in the same picture of a shop.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Use eight picture cards and two paper loops. On 55 give two stickers and one space on a card; the grown-up offers a swap and says no once. On 56 follow Mina's three-picture chain. Never ask what the home has. Ask E54 to E56 aloud.
- **One projector.** Show one card at a time; children mark paper rows before the teacher says an answer.
