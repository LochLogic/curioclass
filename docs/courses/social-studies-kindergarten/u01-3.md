# Section 1.3: Talking, listening and giving reasons

<!-- skel -->
Kindergarten: Living and Learning Together. Unit 1, Learning together. *What makes our class a good place to learn?* (days 1 to 10): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 6 to 10.** Days by kind: understanding 3 (6, 7, 8), check 1 (9), respond 1 (10). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 3 of 3 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 20 to 30 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children do not read yet. Talk, pictures and marks are the evidence. Each day runs 20 minutes; the 30-minute route adds time for communication and recording.

## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D2.Civ.9.K-2** (days 6, 9): Follow agreed discussion rules, listen to others and decide things as a group.
- **D2.Civ.10.K-2** (days 7, 9): Compare your own point of view with other people's.
- **D4.1.K-2** (days 8, 9, 10): Make an argument and give reasons for it.
- **D4.4.K-2** (days 8, 9): Ask and answer questions about an argument.
- **D1.1.K-2** (days 9): Say why a big inquiry question matters to you personally.
- **MA.K.T1.1** (days 9, 10): Follow rules and expectations with little reminding, and with help ask and answer questions about why rules exist.
- **D2.Civ.3.K-2** (days 9): Explain why rules are needed and what they are for, at school and elsewhere.
- **D2.Civ.12.K-2** (days 9): Identify and explain how rules work in the classroom and school.

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Co-write supporting questions:** What do different classmates share (day 2)? Why have rules here and elsewhere (3 to 4)? How can we improve a rule (5)? How can people disagree and still listen (6 to 7)? What supports our answer (8 to 9)?
> - **Closing, day 9:** children answer the compelling question with a reason from Our Class Charter, Two Drawing Tables or the assessment cards. The teacher writes their spoken claims verbatim. Safety, fairness and listening can support different answers.
> - **Teaching:** name each source's maker, setting and purpose before use. Distinguish a made-up illustration from our real charter. Model rule and consequence on day 3, claim and reason on 5, and listening on 6. On days 3 and 5 the teacher models a child asking "Why do we have this rule?" and an answer; children echo both. On day 8 the class reopens one charter rule and chooses with the talk rules (item 5). On 7 compare two views without ranking people; on 8 children choose reasons. Teach rule, fair, safe, reason and point of view through pictures and actions.
>
> - **Carried:** perspective becomes two picture-and-reason columns on day 7, revisited on 9. Claim-evidence-reasoning is tied to the spoken because on 5 and 8. Withdraw teacher-supplied reasons on 9; keep picture references and communication supports. Retain both models for later units.
<!-- /skel -->

- **D2.Civ.9.K-2, this section's share.** "Follow" agreed discussion rules and "attend and respond" to others in a group idea and a decision. Taught on day 6 (listen to the speaker, wait for a turn, answer what was said); first notes on days 5 and 6 are practice; scored on days 7 to 9, including the shared choice on day 8.
- **D2.Civ.10.K-2.** "Compare" one's own view with another person's: day 7 puts two views side by side, with a reason for each, without deciding who is right.
- **D4.1.K-2 and D4.4.K-2.** "Make an argument supported by reasons" (the spoken "I think ..., because ...", day 8) and "ask and answer" a why-question about it (day 8). Scored on day 9.
- **Day 9 and 10 codes.** D1.1.K-2, MA.K.T1.1, D2.Civ.3.K-2 and D2.Civ.12.K-2 are sampled on fresh cases on day 9 and retried on day 10. They are not retaught here.
- **Limits that bite here.** Spoken reasoning only. Stillness and eye contact are not look-fors; a child may pass in the circle and show evidence privately. Disagreeing is shown as normal and safe; no view is ranked and no child is assigned one. Pictured facts still limit a reason ("because it is best" is not one). An unheard response is "not yet observed", never a failure.
- **Connections.** SL.K.1 (day 6): ELA uses the same talk rules. W.K.1 (day 8): spoken here; ELA teaches the drawn opinion. SL.K.3 (day 8): asking a why-question.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** That "listening" here means answering what was said, not sitting still, and that a child using a communication aid, signing or moving is listening. That the "because" is the point of a claim, and that neutral prompts ("Why do you think that?") are allowed without supplying content. How the record sheet keeps practice and scored attempts apart.

## 3. Checks, written first

<!-- skel -->
- Day 9: assessment "observe" (Check day).
<!-- /skel -->

**Unit assessment items owned:** items 5 to 8 (D2.Civ.9, D2.Civ.10, D4.1, D4.4). Items 1 to 4 are sampled on day 9 too, as their owners' fresh tasks (s01 field 5). Items 2 and 5 come from the dated records of days 7 to 9, after written feedback and a revised attempt: the practice note is taken on the day among days 3 to 7 the child is in the rotating group, and the revised attempt is the child's own day 9 group (s01 field 5).

**Exit questions.** Each has a picture mark by every child and a spoken part heard from the day's rotating group of six; others are "not yet heard". Cases print "invented for practice". If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E6.** Mark: point to the picture where the second child answers the idea. Show me the picture where a child is talking about something else. The teacher reads a new statement: "I want the scarves hung lower so we can reach them." Spoken: "Show how to answer that idea after your turn comes." Answer: restate or agree or disagree with a reason ("You want to reach them. I agree, because ..."). Record the turn and the response separately. Wrong: marks the still-sitting picture as answering (C); answers with a new idea about something else (I).
- **E7.** Mark: draw a line from each puppet to its reason picture. Two puppets disagree about what to do first: one wants drawing first because the paper is ready, the other wants listening first because the room is quiet. Spoken: "How are their ideas alike or different?" Answer: both choose a first thing, but the things and the reasons differ. Wrong: "The listening puppet is wrong" (D); "Drawing is best because it is best" (E).
- **E8.** Mark: circle the rule picture that helps. A new sand-table card shows two children reaching for one bucket with a turn-card beside it. Spoken: "Which rule helps this group? Why do you think so?" Then a partner asks a why-question and the child answers. Answer: take turns with the bucket so both can use it; the partner asks "Why do you think that?" and the answer gives a because. Wrong: "It is best because it is best" (E); the partner asks "What color is the bucket?" (F); asked why, the child repeats "Take turns" (L).
- **E10.** (Day 10 fresh try, not a teaching day.) Mark: circle what the rule protects. A pretend museum asks visitors to leave display objects in their cases; the picture shows everyone looking. Spoken: "Why this rule?" Answer: the objects stay whole and others can still see them. Wrong: "It is a rule" (B); "It is good because it is good" (E).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** Perspective: two views side by side from day 7 (two picture-and-reason columns), revisited on 9; the teacher holds the columns, children hold their own picture and reason. Claim-evidence-reasoning: "I think ..., because ..." tied on day 8, with the teacher's supplied reasons withdrawn on day 9, keeping picture references and communication supports. The talk rules from day 6 are held by the class on a poster and by the teacher's notes. Both models are kept for unit 2's retrieval and later units.
- **Key moment 1, day 7: the two columns.** Ideal instrument: movable picture cards under two headings (I think; my friend thinks), each with its reason picture. Spec: fits; paper and tape. No tool is used.
- **Key moment 2, days 8 and 9: the record sheet.** Ideal instrument: a sheet with a row for each child and a cell for each item, dated and marked practice or scored. Spec: fits; print or hand-ruled. Gaps and asks: none.
- **A room with nothing.** Draw the cards on scrap paper. The class poster of talk rules is chart paper with three pictures.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 6, "Which reply answers what was said?"; day 7, "How are the two ideas alike or different?"; day 8, "Why do you think that?" (children ask and answer).
- Written task: none. Children do not write. The teacher writes each spoken claim and reason verbatim on the record sheet.
- Judged by the teacher with four look-fors: (1) a claim with a because that uses a visible detail; (2) a why-question aimed at the claim; (3) an answer that explains, not repeats; (4) a reply that matches the idea just heard. Samples and unit assessment look-fors are in s01 field 5.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: listen, wait, turn, answer, think, because, agree, different. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." and "Why do you think that?" Home language, signs and drawing with dictation are accepted.
- **Access.** Large and tactile picture outlines; a communication aid, a private exchange or a pass is allowed; movement and lack of eye contact are not errors. A scribe records without supplying ideas.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all prompts aloud; accept a point, a mark or a drawing; score civic reasoning apart from English, speech and handwriting. The 30-minute route adds ten minutes for communication and recording.
- **Reading load.** None; the teacher reads every card aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Ask a child who is secure to give a second reason for a claim, or to ask a why-question of a fresh claim.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** At the table: practise answering what was said, then show two views of one choice, then "I think ..., because ..." with a pictured reason. On the last day of the unit (day 9), ask a fresh why-question on a rule picture, ask the child what they think and what another person might think, and listen for a because; day 10 retries any missed part.
- **One projector.** Show the cards and poster on one screen; children use paper for marks and for the day 9 independent drawing. The teacher keeps the record sheet.
