## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Test the effect of stopping the story at a different point.
- **Note:** Reread Owl Creek Bridge; teacher-led passage discussion and analytic paragraph using inference, ambiguity and resolution; consolidation of day 7. Week 2 word-family slot: three minutes within existing word work on conceive / conception / conceivable, retrieval of grade 10's Latin cap/cept (perceive) with con-, not a new root; form a related word, explain its changed meaning and part of speech, then verify and use it in the current text's context. Later encounters apply the family, not another introduction. On assessment/respond days do this after independent evidence, using a previously taught text, never previewing cold text.
