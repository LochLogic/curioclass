## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Verify meanings with context, related forms and historical references.
- **Note:** Reread Young Goodman Brown and Mather; retrieve e10-l-word-strategies, context-clues and word-parts; infer then verify awful and presently, related forms, pronunciation, part of speech, etymology and usage; independently add a word. Week 1 word-family slot: three minutes within existing word work on conceive / conception / conceivable, retrieval of grade 10's Latin cap/cept (perceive) with con-, not a new root; form a related word, explain its changed meaning and part of speech, then verify and use it in the current text's context. Later encounters apply the family, not another introduction. On assessment/respond days do this after independent evidence, using a previously taught text, never previewing cold text. Two-minute retrieval of grade 9's recorded families: analyze/analysis/analytical, derive/derivation/derivative, infer/inference/inferential, constitute/constitution/constitutional, vary/variation/variable, synthesize/synthesis/synthetic. These are retrieval only; new grade 11 families are spread across units.
