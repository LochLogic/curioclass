## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Treating a writer's theological claim as established classroom truth. | Confuses analysis with assent; attribute the claim to its writer. | From memory. |
| B | Treating an asserted premise as its own proof. | Misses the warrant; draw and test one premise-to-conclusion arrow. | From memory. |
| C | Naming a structural device without evaluating its effect. | Misses effectiveness; compare actual and reversed order for the three effects. | From memory. |
