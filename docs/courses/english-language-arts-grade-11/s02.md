## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What did Emerson and Thoreau ask a person to trust, and what did they say it costs?"
Day 17 opens on Emerson's iron-string image beside his society paragraph. Locate what is trusted and surrendered before opening the compelling question.
Questions: How does conformity limit self-trust (day 18)? What work does paradox do (day 19)? How does conscience qualify government (day 23)? Whose labor sustains Alcott's experiment (day 25)?
The day 31 explanation answers the compelling question from all three texts: define self-trust, explain its civic obligations, and test its practical costs through Alcott.
Model interacting ideas day 17, a term trail day 21 and explanatory organization day 24. Students reread hard clauses before speaking; withdraw the model during drafting.
