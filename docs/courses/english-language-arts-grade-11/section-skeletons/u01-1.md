Context-clues and word-parts connect all four texts, days 4 to 16. Discussion-protocol returns on days 5, 8, 12 and 15. Each exchange uses written preparation, chosen roles, an agreed goal and deadline, evidence questions, invitations to dissent and a synthesis naming further research.
