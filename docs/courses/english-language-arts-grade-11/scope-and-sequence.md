## Reading, writing and talk through the year

The text record is `data/plans/ela-texts.csv`. Every required anchor and paired text has a reading placement in the CSV. The Course text record below distinguishes scheduled reading from source preparation still owed, including the complete prefatory materials and Appendix of Douglass, The Custom-House, all six Dickinson poems, the full Whitman elegy and the entire one-act play. Song of Myself uses sections 1, 2, 5, 6, 24 and 52. Riis uses chapters IV and IX complete with their photographs. Sui Sin Far uses the 448-word opening through the childhood-party episode. Remaining unverified excerpt boundaries are named in the course text record and block final text preparation, rather than being called settled. Texts are never replaced by a simpler summary. Unseen assessment selections and withheld interpretations are chosen in the unit plans and kept out of instruction; their identity, length, rights and demands must be recorded before use.

Demand moves from short but difficult older stories, through aphoristic essays and founding argument, to independently managed research and whole long prose works, then condensed poetry and cross-format judgment. This is a rise in the reader's work and decreasing routine scaffolding, not a claim that every successive text has a higher numerical measure. Poems and drama need qualitative judgments; Douglass's lower reported measure does not remove the demand of narrative framing, argument and historical language. Before reading, supply necessary context briefly; during reading, guide the hardest sentence or passage; after reading, reread for a purpose. Begin fading routine supports in unit 6 according to evidence. Keep access supports and record supported proficiency separately from any independent extension.

The long works use 20 to 30 minutes of home reading on school nights as the frame authorizes. Douglass is distributed across days 82 to 89, with the prefatory documents in quarters on days 82 to 85 and chapter 10 spread across days 86 to 89. The novel's chapters run across days 96 to 111, with The Custom-House in ten equal word portions on days 96 to 105 and the paired tale on days 106 to 108. Reading endpoints include advance home reading; they are not whole-class read-aloud assignments. The provisional load budget below includes concurrent discussion, writing, vocabulary and feedback. Required missing book components must be restored before unit planning; no summary substitutes for them.

A seminar is explicit on day 62 and a teacher-led source-integration discussion on day 64 inside unit 4. Each teaching day has partner evidence exchange and a written exit ticket about the text. A weekly one-sitting analytic paragraph occurs on the fifth available teaching meeting, using that day's text and folding into an already scheduled response or draft when appropriate. Every unit has both an explicit seminar and a shorter teacher-led text discussion in its day notes. Students prepare evidence, choose or create roles, set a goal and deadline, practise civil decisions and a hearing for all positions, probe reasoning, and synthesize contradictions and further research. Those routines begin in unit 1 and recur without becoming new owned rows in every unit.

Teach about 8 to 10 words per week from the current texts before, during and after reading, with later reuse and historical-reference checks. This follows the frame's weekly rate rather than treating the progression's approximate fifteen words per unit as a cap on a long unit. Students also add three words per unit from independent reading, with sentence and source. Unit plans select the complete weekly lists. The CSV names initial examples and recurring word work, not a fabricated complete list. Unpack and recombine one anchor sentence weekly and apply a choice to the student's writing.

The five process pieces are due on days 31, 51, 75, 114 and 150. Each has a course-written model, a rubric for its type and a named peer response plus teacher conference. Three cold one-sitting essays fall on days 80 (informative), 118 (public argument) and 157 (literary analysis). Day 150 supplies the narrative record once; the year-end check reuses it. The independent American book is chosen from the frame's shelf on day 1, with progress in reading logs throughout, a conference on day 13 and the year-end conference on day 158. No single book is silently made compulsory in place of that shelf.

Assessment rows list parent codes and their taught lettered parts. Every listed part needs a short prompt or the product or observed performance explicitly named as its evidence. Full-length writing and individual observations are collected once in a window. End days and nearby course-level checks reuse the record, rather than assigning another full piece or conference. Unit-plan task specifications must keep the bounded components within the period. Grade 12 retains Shakespeare in RL.4 and RL.7, legal reasoning in RI.8, early twentieth-century completion of RL.9 and independent high-band proficiency. The grade 11 marks are partial-band teaching, not claims to finish those grade 12 expectations.
