- **HS-PS1-5:** Use science principles and evidence to explain how changing temperature or concentration of reacting particles changes how fast a reaction goes. Emphasis: reasoning about how many collisions happen between molecules and how much energy they carry. Limit: Only simple two-reactant reactions; evidence is temperature, concentration and rate data; rate and temperature linked in words only.
- **HS-PS1-6:** Refine a chemical system's design by naming one change in conditions that would raise the amount of product present at equilibrium. Emphasis: applying Le Chatelier's principle and linking a change made at the visible scale to what molecules do; examples: adding reactant or taking product away. Limit: One variable changed at a time; no calculating equilibrium constants or concentrations.
