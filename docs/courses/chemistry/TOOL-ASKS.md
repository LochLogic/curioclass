## Particle attraction

Requested by [unit 2](s02.md), fields 8 and U6. Mark: **key**. Status: **spec only; does not exist**. Verdict: **does not exist**, because no specification or built instrument is on file.

The ideal instrument connects qualitative electrical interactions and supplied particle structures with bulk patterns. It supports an evidence-based explanation, never automatic grading of an open explanation.

1. Day 23 uses the solid-structure part to show why for eight minutes. Compare atomic, ionic, molecular, metallic and network arrangements with supplied property evidence. Include graphite sheets and distinguish weak inter-sheet interactions from strong connections within sheets. Contrast intact molecules separating with extended structures changing. No numerical force law, force-type taxonomy or vapor-pressure calculation.
2. Show attraction and repulsion separately. Electric interactions resist close approach before nuclei touch. Diagrams must label particles and charges and state where a simplified image cannot show electron distribution accurately.
3. Day 25 uses the mobile-charge part to show why for eight minutes. Under the same applied electrical influence, compare mobile electrons in a supplied metal structure with charges fixed in a supplied solid. Keep ion cores in place. Distinguish electrical attraction holding a material together from charge motion producing current.
4. Require a written prediction before the evidence reveal. Supply a printable sequence of equivalent frames and data for every screen case. With one projector, pause for each student's written reasoning. Keep individual model revisions on paper and store no student information.
5. Label synthetic cases "invented for practice" and link factual records to unit 2 field 10. Clearly separate simulated model behavior from measured data. Do not imply a simulation completes the practical investigation in HS-PS1-3.

7. Reserve assessment liquids L and M, seal structures P and Q and every associated value from field 5. Do not reuse the independent prompts from U3 as worked examples or feedback exercises.
8. Use original Curio Class wording and diagrams. No outside lesson, image or textbook diagram is carried over. The reference links guide factual checks only.
