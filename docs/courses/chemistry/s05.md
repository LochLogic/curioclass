<!-- skel -->
- **HS-PS1-4:** Model how a reacting system takes in or gives off energy depending on how its total bond energy changes. Emphasis: the reaction treated as a system that sets the energy change; models might be particle drawings, energy level graphs of reactants and products, or diagrams showing energy is conserved. Limit: No computing the overall bond energy change from listed bond energy values.
- **HS-PS3-4:** Plan and carry out an investigation showing that when parts at different temperatures mix in a closed system, energy spreads more evenly among them. Emphasis: analyzing students' own data and describing energy changes in numbers and in concepts; examples: mixing liquids of different temperatures, or placing warm objects in water. Limit: Investigations use only the materials and tools students are given.
- **HS-PS3-1:** Build a computational model that finds the energy change of one part of a system when the other parts' changes and the energy flowing in and out are known. Emphasis: explaining what the math in the model means. Limit: Basic algebra only; two or three parts; only thermal energy, kinetic energy, and energy stored in a gravitational, magnetic or electric field.
