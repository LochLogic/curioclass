<!-- skel -->
- **HS-ESS2-4:** Use a model to explain how shifts in the energy entering and leaving Earth's systems change climate. Emphasis: Causes differ by time span: eruptions and ocean currents over years, human activity and solar output over centuries, orbit and tilt over many millennia, air makeup over millions of years. Limit: Climate change results limited to surface temperature, precipitation patterns, glacier ice volume, sea level and where living things are found.
- **HS-ESS2-2:** Analyze geoscience data to claim that one change to Earth's surface can set off feedbacks that change other Earth systems. Emphasis: Include at least one climate feedback, such as melting ice reflecting less sunlight; others may involve lost plant cover, dams, or shrinking wetlands.
- **HS-ESS3-5:** Analyze geoscience data and climate model output to forecast, with evidence, how fast climate is changing globally or in a region and what that will do next. Limit: Only one climate change and the effects that follow from it.
