## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | An observed pattern alone proves its cause. | Evidence does not yet establish mechanism. Day 117 contrasts correlation with mechanism plus independent evidence; redraw the claim with its limit. | From memory. |
| B | Greenhouse gases produce the extra energy. | The energy source is misplaced. Day 115 contrasts an incoming sunlight arrow with infrared absorption and re-emission; trace the source. | From memory. |
| C | Ocean circulation creates energy for the whole Earth. | A transfer is counted as a source. Day 116 contrasts two regional budgets with their combined boundary; cancel the internal arrows. | From memory. |
| D | A slow orbital cycle explains any present warming trend. | Timescale evidence is missing. Day 119 contrasts a thirty-year record with millennial orbital evidence; justify a matching cause. | From memory. |
| E | Positive feedback means a beneficial effect. | Feedback sign is confused with value. Day 122 contrasts ice loss amplifying warming with increased radiation opposing warming; follow the initial change. | From memory. |
| F | A projection range makes the forecast useless. | Uncertainty is confused with no knowledge. Day 126 contrasts a bounded conditional forecast with no forecast; state what both limits support. | From memory. |
| G | Two endpoints determine the exact future climate rate forever. | Window and extrapolation limits are ignored. Day 126 contrasts endpoint slopes and whole-series fits; explain the conditional window. | From memory. |
| H | Melting floating sea ice is the main direct source of rising sea level. | Floating ice is confused with added land water. Day 127 contrasts displacement with land-ice runoff and expansion; revise the source arrows. | From memory. |
| I | Every one-way consequence is a feedback. | No returning influence is required. Day 123 contrasts a sediment-loss chain with a closed vegetation-soil loop; identify the return evidence. | From memory. |
| J | Slow plate-linked weathering establishes a rapid present cause. | Slow carbon removal is applied at the wrong timescale. Day 120 contrasts million-year weathering with a thirty-year change; match mechanism and elapsed time. | From memory. |
