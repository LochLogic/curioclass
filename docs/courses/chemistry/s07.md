- **HS-PS3-3:** Design, build and improve a device that converts energy from one form to another while staying within set constraints. Emphasis: judging devices both in words and in numbers; examples: chain-reaction machines, wind turbines, solar cells or ovens, generators. Limit: Quantitative judging only compares total output with a given input; devices are built from materials provided.
- **HS-ETS1-2:** Design a way to solve a large real-world problem by splitting it into smaller problems that engineering can handle.
