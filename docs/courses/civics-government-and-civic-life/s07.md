**Teacher background:** The Tenth Amendment reserves powers to states or people, within federal limits. Supremacy concerns valid federal law. State constitutions constrain broad powers differently from enumerated federal powers. Local authority derives from state law; tribal sovereignty predates the United States. As of November 2026 (checked 2026-11-14): a March 20, 2025 executive order directed steps toward closing the federal Department of Education, and on July 14, 2025 the Supreme Court, in McMahon v. New York, let its layoffs proceed while the case continues, without deciding their legality. The July 4, 2025 budget law raises states' share of food-aid (SNAP) administrative costs from half to three quarters from October 2026 and adds a state share of benefits tied to payment error rates. State these as facts; students contrast responsibilities at each level, not parties. **Limits:** No financial disclosures or reenactment. Detailed jurisdictional litigation waits for Government.
**Carried:** Keep blank organizers; park unit 6's timeline and continuity chart for unit 8. Revise branches on 108 to 110. Return budget and opportunity cost on 112, tied to allocations on 114. Revise supply and demand on 113. Withdraw examples on 117.
