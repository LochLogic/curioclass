- **Share of each code.** W.K.7 "Participate in shared research and writing projects": the class owns one question, "What does our tree need, and who needs it?", and each child owns one part (a question, a source-labelled note, a picture). W.K.8 "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": one fact at a time from 03, 05b, 06 and 05a, always with an adult and always with its source label. RI.K.3 (102, 103, 106) "describe the connection between two ... pieces of information": a need and a living thing that uses the tree, joined by an arrow and a so or because. W.K.2 (104, 105) "name what they are writing about and supply some information": a shared opening that names the tree, then one fact; drawing and dictation count. Cycle 16, short o (RF.K.3b, 3d, L.K.2d, RF.K.4, RF.K.2d, 2e, RF.K.3c): the letter o for its short sound only; one-letter changes with the changed sound named; spelling by sounds; the cycle reader; middle-sound isolation; add or swap one sound; the mapped words or and for.
- **Limits that bite here.** Two facts at a time. Adult-provided sources only, no search. Drawing and dictation accepted; no mark down for spelling by sound in content work. Short o only: or and for are mapped whole words (the o-r sound is one part to remember), never a pattern; long o and r-controlled spellings are not taught here (parent RF.K.3 closes on days 158 and 159). Acorns are tree nuts: pictures only, nothing handled or tasted on any day.
- **Connections.** RF.K.1a to c (record P1): days 102, 103. RF.K.1d, L.K.1a (P2): days 102 to 105. RF.K.3a, L.K.2c (P3): day 105. RF.K.2d, 2e (P4): isolation 102 to 104, manipulation 105 and 106. RF.K.3c (P5): day 106. W.K.2 with RI.K.3: day 104, where the shared opening names the tree and then joins a need to a dependent.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A need is something the tree takes in (water, air, light); a dependent is a living thing that gets food or shelter from the tree. Two arrows, two directions: need in, dependent out. Never say a tree wants or likes. (2) The research cycle at five: ask a question, find a fact in a provided text, note it with its source label, share it; the label is a picture sticker for the text, so another child can find the line again. (3) Why the sugar maple profile can stand in a school with no maple: it is the class's provided source, and a real tree is compared only as a picture or a safe look. (4) or and for are not short o words; the day says so aloud. (5) The words held for the fresh checks in 3.7 and 3.8 (F1): the pair pen and pin, the dictation words bed and sun, and the whole F1 passage are never used in teaching or exits.
- **One word, one sense.** support: 05b may say branches support a nest (hold it up); on day 106 the word means back up a note with a source. Say which sense aloud each time. check means look at the source again, never a test.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview research, focus, describe, observe, select, evidence with a gesture and a picture; accept home-language rehearsal before the English talk. Frame: "My tree is ___. It needs ___. ___ uses it." (Offered only to children who ask; the class starters faded after day 96.)
- **Access.** Enlarge or describe the pictures; accept pointing, dictation and usual communication supports; counters are large and tactile. Keep the cards at table height.
- **Check accommodations.** Repeat the ask without giving the link; offer a described or tactile picture set; accept a spoken or pointed answer; record support used. A scribe may write the label, never replace the sound-to-letter act on days 105 and 106.
- **Reading load.** Children read only the cycle reader (the day writer keeps each day's printed text to 60 words or fewer) and short labels; all tree texts are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain a second fact from another source with its sticker.
