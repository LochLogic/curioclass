## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of each code.** W.K.7 "Participate in shared research and writing projects": the class owns one question, "What does our tree need, and who needs it?", and each child owns one part (a question, a source-labeled note, a picture). W.K.8 "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": one fact at a time from 03, 05b, 06 and 05a, always with an adult and always with its source label. RI.K.3 (102, 103, 106) "describe the connection between two ... pieces of information": a need and a living thing that uses the tree, joined by an arrow and a so or because. W.K.2 (104, 105) "name what they are writing about and supply some information": a shared opening that names the tree, then one fact; drawing and dictation count. Cycle 16, short o (RF.K.3b, 3d, L.K.2d, RF.K.4, RF.K.2d, 2e, RF.K.3c): the letter o for its short sound only; one-letter changes with the changed sound named; spelling by sounds; the cycle reader; middle-sound isolation; add or swap one sound; the mapped words or and for.
- **Limits that bite here.** Two facts at a time. Adult-provided sources only, no search. Drawing and dictation accepted; no mark down for spelling by sound in content work. Short o only: or and for are mapped whole words (the o-r sound is one part to remember), never a pattern; long o and r-controlled spellings are not taught here (parent RF.K.3 closes on days 158 and 159). Acorns are tree nuts: pictures only, nothing handled or tasted on any day.
- **Connections.** RF.K.1a to c (record P1): days 102, 103. RF.K.1d, L.K.1a (P2): days 102 to 105. RF.K.3a, L.K.2c (P3): day 105. RF.K.2d, 2e (P4): isolation 102 to 104, manipulation 105 and 106. RF.K.3c (P5): day 106. W.K.2 with RI.K.3: day 104, where the shared opening names the tree and then joins a need to a dependent.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A need is something the tree takes in (water, air, light); a dependent is a living thing that gets food or shelter from the tree. Two arrows, two directions: need in, dependent out. Never say a tree wants or likes. (2) The research cycle at five: ask a question, find a fact in a provided text, note it with its source label, share it; the label is a picture sticker for the text, so another child can find the line again. (3) Why the sugar maple profile can stand in a school with no maple: it is the class's provided source, and a real tree is compared only as a picture or a safe look. (4) or and for are not short o words; the day says so aloud. (5) The words held for the fresh checks in 3.7 and 3.8 (F1): the pair pen and pin, the dictation words bed and sun, and the whole F1 passage are never used in teaching or exits.
- **One word, one sense.** support: 05b may say branches support a nest (hold it up); on day 106 the word means back up a note with a source. Say which sense aloud each time. check means look at the source again, never a test.

## Day 102

- **15 min. Questions and talk.** Q1 "What does the chant explain?" Q2 "Which new question about a particular tree can we investigate?" Turn and talk with the token, one turn each; every child may pass. Teacher models two question starters ("What does the tree need?" "Who needs the tree?") and the class chooses the working question: "What does our tree need, and who needs it?"
- **20 min. Draw and dictate.** 8 min: retrieve the research cycle on a four-picture chart (ask, find, note, share) by pointing to the step the class took on the animal question; read the collage prompt aloud, "Name your tree. Show what it needs and one living thing that depends on it. Use details from two readings.", and say the collage is made later. 12 min: notebook page one, "I wonder": each child draws one thing they wonder about trees, tells a partner, and dictates it to the teacher or writes by sounds. No child reports on a tree at home.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E102, covered until now; six children heard.
- **7 min. Sound warm-up.** Whole class, oral, no print: the middle sound of spoken words with counters (hop, job, rock), chorally. Six rotating children each answer one item alone (P4 isolation, one minute each, names written on the record sheet column).

- **10 min. Spell and print.** 5 min: dictate log, mop, dog; children push a counter a sound and write plausible letters. 5 min: P2 handwriting, the third date for set O to T, revising forms the written feedback flagged.
- **5 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up.** Hear verse five (adult reads; no new decoding pattern), find the two rhyming words and clap the two-syllable referent; tracking line by line left to right. Cards and counters collected, 2 min.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the question and chart; each child holds one "I wonder" page and, from today, the notebook that will hold labeled notes. Tool: None. ## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview research, focus, describe, observe, select, evidence with a gesture and a picture; accept home-language rehearsal before the English talk. Frame: "My tree is ___. It needs ___. ___ uses it." (Offered only to children who ask; the class starters faded after day 96.)
- **Access.** Enlarge or describe the pictures; accept pointing, dictation and usual communication supports; counters are large and tactile. Keep the cards at table height.
- **Check accommodations.** Repeat the ask without giving the link; offer a described or tactile picture set; accept a spoken or pointed answer; record support used. A scribe may write the label, never replace the sound-to-letter act on days 105 and 106.
- **Reading load.** Children read only the cycle reader (the day writer keeps each day's printed text to 60 words or fewer) and short labels; all tree texts are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain a second fact from another source with its sticker.
