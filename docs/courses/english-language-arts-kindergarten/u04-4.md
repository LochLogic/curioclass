## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **Section share, by standard.** W.K.1 (use a combination of drawing, dictating, and writing to compose opinion pieces) is taught again on day 133 with a planting place and a preference, and an optional reason labeled as the writer's. RI.K.8 (the reasons an author gives) is returned to on days 133 and 135. RL.K.1, RI.K.1 and SL.K.2 (ask and answer questions about key details, and ask for clarification) are taught on days 134 to 136 on the true account Wangari and the Tree Planters; RL.K.10 and RI.K.10 (actively engage in group reading with purpose) on day 134; RI.K.3 (the connection between two pieces of information) on day 136. RI.K.2 and RI.K.7 are retrieved on day 135. Foundations: cycle 21 (days 133 and 134), its check F3 on day 134, cycle 22 (days 135 and 136).
- **Limits that bite here.** Children give an opinion only about a class pot or the school grounds, never a yard or garden. The true account is read aloud and its facts are used as the text states them: Kenya is located on a map, Wangari Maathai organized with women who shared concerns, the women set up nurseries and planted together. No word of dialogue, no feeling and no act is added; no child is told she acted alone; nothing ranks one place or culture over another. The number in the book's subtitle is said in words only and never worked with.
- **Connections.** SL.K.2 asking what a word means (day 134); RF.K.1c spaces each day.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A fact the text states against one it does not (V); an action and its result in the order the text gives (AE); how to say Kenya's place on the map without describing the country as poor or far away; that one page of the book says Wangari Maathai died in 2011 and the teacher says it plainly and briefly; that the sensitivity read named by the frame is to be done before the day is taught.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none (no check day in this section). The foundations cycle check F3 runs on day 134 inside the foundations block; its prompts are in s04 field 5.

**How the exits run.** As in section 4.1: the teacher reads the named page (days 135 and 136) or the fresh card (days 133 and 134, invented for practice and said so) twice; ask 1 is a mark on one of three pictures by every child; ask 2 is the spoken evidence from the day's rotating six at 30 seconds each; a mark on the near-miss picture is a screen, not a verdict.

**Exit questions.** If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E133.** Card: "The writer says a tree can shade the class bench. Two possible places are beside that bench and beside a storage shed." Ask 1: "Point to the place you would choose." Ask 2: "Say your plan: I would plant it ___ and I prefer ___." (a reason the writer gave may be added, labeled "the writer says"). Right: a named place with "I prefer ___", the bench shade labeled as the writer's reason; a child may choose the shed and give an own reason labeled as their own. Wrong: "The bench" with no preference (F); "The writer says I like the shed" (C).
- **E134.** Card: "Neighbors plant young trees together beside their school. A teacher holds the water can." Ask 1: "Mark the picture that shows the people and the place." Ask 2: "Ask me one question that would help us know more about their work." Right: neighbors planting beside a school; a question about how they planted together or who holds the can. Wrong: the picture of the neighbors beside a house (Y); "I do not know" with no question asked (W). The pictures are original and show no costume or stereotype.
- **E135.** On the text, not an invented card: 02b (Planting Together), page 8, read aloud again. Ask 1: "Which card is the reason Planting Together gives?" Right: The roots grow strong (people keep watering until the roots grow strong). Wrong: points to I like watering cans (C); points to Trees give birds shelter (Y).
- **E136.** On the text, not an invented card: 03 (Wangari and the Tree Planters), page 2, read aloud again. Ask 1: "How are studying science and becoming a scientist connected?" Right: She studied science at schools and universities, so she became a scientist. Wrong: says Being a scientist made her go to school (AE); says She planted trees at school (V).

## 6. Days

## Day 133


- **10 min. Return and repair.** Hand-back: day 132 check C2, errors B, C and E, and the section 4.3 cycle evidence; small groups by error with the rest rereading, whole class for any error more than a third made. Fresh retries only.
- **10 min. Reread.** Trees Help Our Community, pages 3 to 4, aloud. "What place could receive shade?" and "Which stated reason might help our choice?" One ask each.
- **10 min. Model an opinion about where to plant.** The teacher models a topic and a preference with the class pot and the school grounds as the only choices (no home yard): "I would plant our tree ___. I prefer it there." An optional reason is added only if the text states it, and labeled "the writer says". Error F is shown with a right example.
- **25 min. Draw and dictate the opinion.** Each child draws the planting place chosen and says the sentence; the teacher takes dictation from the rotating six (about 3 minutes each), others write the letters they know and copy the frame "I would plant it ___." Any child may pass.
- **5 min. Exit.** E133.

- **20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 21, lesson 109: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only).** Pattern teaching and joint reading of cycle-21 sentences. Sound change jet to jut, the changed sound named. Ending sounds ank and ink are heard only. Retrieve the mapped words so far, each read in text before it is read alone.
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** A Hen in a Tub, reread with a new question each time. Assignments 1 to 6 only, with the saved time given to more reading; no standalone isolated tasks today. The teacher hears the day's rotating six for 10 minutes (about 100 seconds a child: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task) and names them in the record column; the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards, with no timer shown.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** Reread Tree Care Words, verse 3 by echo; point to the line that gives the order. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, a space between words, and each child writes the day's spelled word by its sounds.
- **5 min. More reading.** The saved check minutes go to a second reading of the reader for meaning (assignments 1 to 6 only).

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the box; children hold their own frame. Tool: none. By hand: drawing and writing. Exit: E133. Out: errors F and C to day 134's Vocabulary and return.

## Day 134


- **5 min. Vocabulary and return.** With pictures and the map: Kenya, nursery, seedling. Hand-back: day 133 exit, errors F and C (one repair each, in one five-minute round).
- **20 min. Read-aloud, the true account.** Wangari and the Tree Planters, whole (375 words), the map first. Content: a real woman; the account says she won a prize and died in 2011 (said plainly, once); the account names her prize and that she was the first African woman to win it; the account names no violence.
- **10 min. Questions and talk.** "Who organizes with whom?" and "Which place and which actions does the account name?" One ask each.
- **10 min. Group reading with a purpose.** 5 minutes: join in a repeated phrase ("side by side", page 6) with a purpose card "listen for who plants". 5 minutes: a brief return to Reading Under the Tree (page 7) with a purpose card "find who comes to the quiet place"; every child holds a purpose card.
- **5 min. Ask what a word means.** The class asks "What does ___ mean?" for one new word (the teacher chooses nursery or seedling) and answers from the picture.
- **5 min. Draw and dictate.** Draw the people and the place; the rotating six say "___ plant trees in ___."
- **5 min. Exit.** E134.

- **15 min. Shared lesson (cycle 21, lesson 110: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only).** Explicit pattern teaching and one joint reading of cycle-21 sentences (the last lesson of the cycle). Retrieve the mapped words so far, each read in text before it is read alone. Reread Tree Care Words, verse 3 by echo; point to the line that gives the order.
- **45 min. Cycle check F3 (prompts in s04 field 5), in the rotations.** The teacher observes one child at a time for the oral parts (45 minutes for thirty children is 90 seconds each; a child not finished in 90 seconds is marked shown with help on that component and finishes in the next day's response). The written lists are completed together on response sheets. The other children do familiar reader work: partner reading of A Hen in a Tub, reread with a new question each time and assignment cards. No help is given during an independent probe.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the map; children hold purpose cards. Tool: none. By hand: map, cards. Exit: E134. Out: error W to day 135's Return and repair.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 134, "Who organizes with whom?"; day 135, "What concerns are heard?" and "Which reason does each source state?"; day 136, "What do people do together?" and "How does the account connect growing and planting?"

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview: Kenya, nursery, seedling, organize (with gestures and the map). Frames: "I would plant ___. I prefer ___." "The writer says ___." "First ___, then ___." Home-language rehearsal first is welcome; a child may point to the map.
- **Access.** The map in large print, picture cards for the five-picture strip, tactile cards for a child who cannot see them; dictation for a child who cannot write.
- **Check accommodations.** Ask 1 by pointing; ask 2 by speaking or by placing cards in order; no time limit for a child.
- **Reading load.** Children read no text of the account; the teacher reads 375 words aloud. Alone, they read only the cycle reader and mapped words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second place the writer's reason would suit, and say it with "the writer says".

## 10. Two rooms

- **One projector.** Show the map as a picture only; the cards are held at the table.
