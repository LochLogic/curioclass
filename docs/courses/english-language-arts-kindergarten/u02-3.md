# Section 2.3: Clouds, rainbows, where things are, and the mid-unit check





## 1. Standards and practices


- **L.K.1d, SL.K.3, this section's share.** Day 43 teaches "understand and use question words (interrogatives)": who, what, where, when, why, how, each with a picture card. Children "ask and answer questions in order to ... get information" with them.
- **RL.K.4, RI.K.4, L.K.4.** Day 43 continues "ask and answer questions about unknown words" (cloud words, with the three-card routine). Day 44 teaches L.K.4a: "identify new meanings for familiar words and apply them accurately", for the one word 01e uses in two senses, clear.
- **L.K.1e, L.K.1b, L.K.1c.** Day 44 teaches "the most frequently occurring prepositions": on, under, beside, in. Day 45 applies them to a pictured scene. Nouns, verbs and plurals are review in speech.
- **W.K.2.** Day 45 drafts, and day 46 revises and scores, an information piece that "name[s] what they are writing about and supply[ies] some information about the topic" in "drawing, dictating, and writing".
- **SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6.** Days 45 and 46: "describe familiar people, places, things, and events", "add drawings ... to provide additional detail", "speak audibly and express thoughts, feelings, and ideas clearly". Day 46 collects each as its own record.
- **Foundations** (RF.K.1, RF.K.2, RF.K.3a, RF.K.3c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c): cycle 6, lessons 33 to 35 (g and i) on days 43 to 45, with the mapped words on and to, the cycle 6 check on day 45, and a response block on day 46. Phonemes: day 43 changing the first sound, day 44 changing the last sound, day 45 the check.
- **Limits that bite here.** Day 46 is a check: nothing new is taught and no earlier error is repaired on it. Day 45's exit error goes to day 47's warm-up. L.K.4b (affixes) stays on day 53. Read-alouds are listening texts. The independent piece uses a fact from 01 and is judged on topic and fact, never on spelling or letter form beyond the taught letters. Only weather pictures from the texts or the class journal are used; no child describes a storm or a home. Phoneme words never end in /l/, /r/ or /x/. "Clear" is checked in the two senses the text itself writes (no clouds; easy to see through); no other word is treated as having two meanings here.
- **Connections.** RI.K.1 and SL.K.2: the question words are the detail questions of 2.1 with names. L.K.1c with RF.K.2b: day 45 description uses plural nouns. W.K.2 with the class journal of day 41.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Question words ask for a kind of answer: who for a person, what for a thing or event, where for a place, when for a time, why for a reason, how for a way. "Clear" is one word with two senses in 01e: the sky is clear (no clouds) and a raindrop is clear (you can see through it); the first sense is reviewed from day 36. Prepositions are taught by moving cards and objects, and "in front of" is heard, not taught. The check is observed performance with the day 45 feedback already given; the scoring marks and look-fors are in field 3.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** the day 46 rows of unit 2 field 5, here named I1 (W.K.2) and O1 (SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6), plus the retained independent piece F1. Items are teacher-judged; the marks are shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Accommodations: dictation, pointing and a communication aid count for I1, and a drawing counts as the child's writing.

**Day's evidence.** Every card below is invented for practice, Curio Class, 2026, ours, said aloud to be invented, read twice with its picture, and never used in teaching. Spoken parts are heard from the day's six; a child marks or points on paper. No wrong answer is scored on days 43 to 45.
- **E43.** Card C43: "In the evening, a farmer looks at the sky over her barn. Dark clouds gather. She brings the cows in." Spoken, item a: "When does the farmer look at the sky?" Answer: in the evening. Wrong: "the farmer" or "at the barn" (Z, answers the wrong question word). Spoken, item b: "Ask me a question that starts with who." Answer: any question about a person: "Who looks at the sky?" "Who brings the cows in?" Wrong: "Who is in the evening?" (Z).
- **E44.** Card C44: "The window is clear. I can see a bird." Spoken, item a: "What does clear mean here?" Answer: easy to see through. Wrong: "no clouds" (AC, only the familiar sense). Picture P44: a boot under a bench. Spoken, item b: "Tell me where the boot is." Answer: under the bench. Wrong: "on the bench" (AA, reversed).
- **E45.** Picture P45: a cat on a chair, a ball under the chair, a bird beside the chair. Spoken: "Tell me where the ball is." Answer: under the chair. Wrong: "on the chair" (AA, reversed). Mark: place a counter on the ball.
- **I1 (day 46).** The day 45 draft, revised after its teacher marks, then judged. Look-fors: (1) a topic is named (a picture with a label, or a word the child says or dictates); (2) one true fact about the topic that 01a to 01e says, with its page found when asked; (3) the fact belongs to the topic. Meets: a drawing of a cloud, the word "cloud" dictated, and "Clouds are made of tiny drops of water." Does not meet: a drawing of the child's pet and "I like it" (C, preference alone) or a rainbow drawing with no word said (B, no topic). Retained: F1, a new 15-minute independent piece, read beside I1 and not scored again.
- **O1 (day 46).** Each pair takes a page from the class picture journal (days 41 to 45) and describes the weather on it; the teacher prompts one more detail ("What else did you see?"). Look-fors: SL.K.4, tells what is on the page in an order with one added detail; SL.K.5, the drawing shows the detail told; SL.K.6, heard from where the partner sits and the idea can be said back. Meets: "It was raining at the playground. (points) The puddles were big." Does not meet: "It was a thing and the other thing" (F, unclear idea); a drawing of something else (D); a whisper (E, recorded apart); "I felt happy" when asked for a feeling, with no idea clear to the listener (AX).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** sentence-combining (from 2.2) returns on day 45 when children join two position sentences with "and". main-idea-details returns on day 46 (the independent piece has a topic on top and a fact below). The class picture journal (day 41) supplies the pages for O1 on day 46. discussion-protocol and the three-card routine are used each pairs part. New here: six question-picture cards (who, what, where, when, why, how), carried through unit 2.
- **Key moment 1, day 44: two meanings of one word.** Must be seen: a child uses clear in the second sense with a real thing. Ideal instrument: a clear cup of water, a cup of milk (not clear), a window. Spec: no tool; real things fit. Gaps and asks: none (TOOL-ASKS.md names only the text editor).
- **Key moment 2, day 46: the pair talk.** Must be heard: a description with a detail and a drawing that shows it. Ideal instrument: the teacher listening, with a mark for each child on the record sheet. A recorder would let the six-minute tally be checked later; it is not planned. Spec: none needed.
- **A room with nothing.** A cup, a window, picture cards on scrap paper, a pencil, a clipboard record sheet and a quiet corner.

## 6. Days

## Day 43


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 42 (AR or AS) with the token and puppets, then one fresh two-turn talk in pairs.
- **6 min. Words.** With gestures and pictures: cloud, sky, because (a reason), clarify (ask again so it is clear).
- **10 min. Reading 01d.** First reading of 01d, Clouds, whole, about 2.5 minutes with its five pictures; then one question: "What is a cloud made of?"
- **14 min. Model: the six question words.** Show six picture cards (who: a person; what: a thing; where: a map pin; when: sun and moon; why: an arrow to a reason; how: a hand showing a way). Reread page 5, whose six questions each have a different kind of answer. Model asking and answering one of each about page 4.
- **12 min. Sort the answers.** The teacher reads three sentences of 01d (page 3 and page 4) and pairs place each under the question card it answers. The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **8 min. Partners ask.** Pairs: one asks a who, where or when question about a picture of 01d, the other answers, then swap. The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E43, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Articulatory gestures verse: say /g/ and /i/ with the mouth pictured on the card; point to g and i on verse V3.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Mystery word on: clues in the verse, map the letters (o says /o/, n says /n/; no part to remember), find on in the verse. Source: cycle 6, lesson 33.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Change the first sound: say sat, change /s/ to /m/ to make mat; change the first sound of sit; each child says the new word. Writing place: Write g and i; trace on on a card (o is taught in cycle 10). Sound and verse place: Pairs change the first sound with counters; find on in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the six cards; children hold the questions and answers. Tool: None. By hand: 01d pictures, six question cards, three sentence strips (read aloud), paper. Evidence: E43, foundations heard column.

## Day 44


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 43 (Z) with the question cards, then one fresh question-and-answer pair.
- **5 min. Words.** With gestures: rainbow, light, bend, sunshine, raindrop.
- **10 min. Reading 01e.** First reading of 01e, Rainbows, whole, about 2 minutes with its four pictures; then one question: "What does a rainbow need?"
- **10 min. Reread the explanation.** Reread pages 2 and 3. Pairs find the two things a rainbow needs and where the sun must be; the teacher points to the matching picture.
- **12 min. Two meanings of clear.** The day 36 sky sense is reviewed ("the sky is clear: no clouds"). Then a clear cup of water beside a cup of milk, and a clear window beside a window with cardboard: "clear can also mean you can see through it", as 01e says of a raindrop. One word, two senses; the day checks only these two. Children say a sentence for each sense.
- **13 min. Where things are.** Using the scene on page 4 (the girl, the boy, the dog, the bag, the boat, the bridge), move picture cards to show on, under, beside and in; children say where each is. The teacher notes that "in front of" is heard in the text and is not taught.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E44, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Letter-sound chant of twelve letters (a, t, h, p, c, n, m, r, v, s, g, i).
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Feel the beats: clap and count the syllables (rain-bow has two; sun has one), blend and segment two-beat words. Map to beside on and he. Source: cycle 6, lesson 34.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Change the last sound: say sat, change /t/ to /p/ to make sap; each child says the new word. Writing place: Trace to on a card (t is known; o is taught in cycle 10); write g and i from memory. Sound and verse place: Clap beats of three picture words; match two words that rhyme. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the two cups and the moving cards; children hold the sentences and the positions. Tool: None. By hand: a clear cup of water, a cup of milk, window card, picture cards, scene page. Evidence: E44, foundations heard column.

## Day 45


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 44 (AC or AA) with the two cups and a moving picture, then one fresh sentence.
- **12 min. Describe a scene.** Reread page 4 of 01e, children listening for where each thing is. Pairs describe a scene card of their own (a weather scene with three objects, the teacher's card for each pair): "The ___ is ___ the ___." They join two position sentences with "and" (sentence-combining, review). The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **8 min. Pick a topic and a fact.** Each child picks a weather topic from 01a to 01e (rain, snow, clouds, rainbows, warmer and cooler, wind), says one fact to a partner, and finds the page with the teacher's help.
- **5 min. The teacher's marks.** Show the three marks the teacher will use on a draft tomorrow: a big box (add the topic picture), a book (add one fact from the page), a pencil (add a label or a letter). Children try each on a model draft; no draft is marked in class.
- **25 min. Draft an information piece.** Each child draws the topic, then says or dictates one fact from the page; the teacher writes it, and the child adds labels and any letters of taught sounds. Drafts are practice. The teacher moves through the room and hears six.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E45, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Letter-sound chant of the twelve letters; reread verse V3 with the pointer.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Rhyme Time: find the rhymes in the verse and say new rhymes. Cycle 6 check on paper: write the letter for the sounds /g/ and /i/ (all children, 5 minutes). Source: cycle 6, lesson 35.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Cycle 6 check, one child at a time: name g, name i, say the sound of each (30 children, about 40 seconds each, 20 minutes in all across the three turns). Writing place: Finish the sound-writing check; write g and i in lines. Sound and verse place: Rhyme cards; find the words with g or i in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: children hold the draft and the fact; the teacher holds the marks and writes. Tool: None. By hand: scene cards, drafts, crayons, topic pictures. Evidence: E45, the draft (marked after class; practice), the cycle 6 check.

## Day 46

- **15 min. Independent piece (F1).** A new piece, drawn and said or dictated or written: a weather topic and one fact from 01a to 01e. Retained with I1, not scored a second time.
- **10 min. Prepare a drawing.** Each child opens the class picture journal to one page and draws the one detail to tell (SL.K.5).
- **20 min. Describe a weather page (O1).** Fifteen pairs at one table with the teacher, one pair at a time for about 75 seconds (about 35 seconds for each child); the teacher prompts one more detail. The other pairs finish F1 or colour their drawing quietly.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Reread verse V3 and the earlier verses with the pointer; find on and he.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Cycle 6 response, ten minutes, in groups by the day 45 check (field 4: U); chant the twelve letters for the rest. No new pattern. Source: ours (foundations response), after cycle 6.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Small groups by the cycle 6 check: sort name cards from sound cards for g and i; change one sound (sat to mat). Writing place: Write g and i; trace on and to. Sound and verse place: Beats and rhyme cards; blend and segment the spoken words sit and sap. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds only the marks and the record sheet; children hold their own work. Tool: None. By hand: day 45 drafts, journal pages, crayons, record sheet. Evidence: I1, F1 (retained), O1.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: cloud, sky, because, clarify, rainbow, light, on, under, beside. Frames: "The ___ is ___ the ___." "I learned that ___." "Who ___? Where ___?" A home-language label may go on the draft.
- **Access.** Large picture cards and a real cup; an adult moves the cards as the child directs; a communication aid or dictation counts for the draft; a child who cannot hold a crayon chooses between picture cards. A pair talk may happen away from the group.
- **Check accommodations.** Dictation, a pointing drawing and an aid count for I1; O1 may be given in a quiet corner; repeating the prompt once is allowed. Volume (E) and clarity (F, AX) are recorded apart.
- **Reading load.** None; everything is read aloud. Children recognise only taught letters and the mapped words he, we, on and to.
- **Extension, same standard.** A second fact on the draft, or a "why" question to a partner (heard, not required).

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 01d and 01e aloud on different days. Ask the six question words about one picture. Show a clear cup and a milk cup and say both senses of clear. Draft one information piece, set it aside, then revise it by the three marks and tell it aloud with a drawing.
- **One projector.** One screen shows the pages and the question cards; the cups and the drafts are real; O1 is one pair at a time with the teacher, so only one screen is needed.
