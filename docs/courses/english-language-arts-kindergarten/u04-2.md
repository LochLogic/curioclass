## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **Section share, by standard.** RL.K.9 (compare and contrast the adventures and experiences of characters in familiar stories) is applied on day 125 on the rain and noise scenes already taught, observed on days 126 and 127 on two scenes never worked in teaching (check C1), and repaired on day 128. SL.K.1, SL.K.1a and SL.K.1b (collaborative conversation, agreed turns, more than one exchange) are rehearsed on day 125 and observed on day 127; SL.K.3 (ask a question to clarify) is rehearsed on day 125. RI.K.9 (two texts on one topic) is only retrieved here, on two invented picture cards on days 125 and 128; its first teaching with real texts is day 131. Foundations: cycle 20 (-an and -am), days 125 to 127, a retrieval and response day on 128.
- **Limits that bite here.** C1 uses only the reserved scenes: page 3 of A Tree Beside Our Door (Lina invites a neighbor to smell the blossoms) and page 3 of Reading Under the Tree (Ben invites a classmate to feel the bark); the second pair, page 9 of each, is kept for the later check C3 (unit plan field 5). Nothing in days 125 or 128 may use them. Children answer by speaking and pointing; none writes an answer. One group of five at a time is heard on the check days; the rest do familiar work and are not helped.
- **Connections.** SL.K.1a and SL.K.1b on day 127 with the comparison; RF.K.1, RF.K.2a to 2c and L.K.1a in the day 128 retrieval.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The three groups of five and what ten minutes holds; what the teacher says and does not say (one prompt, never the answer); how a carried record from day 125 stands for the first fifteen children's talk on day 127.

## 2. Where it sits


Day 125 uses the chart and frame from day 123 and the errors left from day 124 (X, Y, Z, AA, AB). Results of 126 and 127 go to the record sheet by child and to day 128, which is the respond day.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned.** C1 on day 126 (RL.K.9), and C1 on day 127 (RL.K.9, SL.K.1, SL.K.1a, SL.K.1b), as in the table above (items are on the unit plan).

**How C1 runs.** Groups of five sit with both books open to page 3, 126 for the first fifteen children and 127 for the other fifteen. The teacher rereads the two pages aloud (about 90 seconds). Stem 1, to each child in turn: "What is the same on these two pages?" Stem 2, to each in turn: "What is different?" Five children times two stems at about 30 seconds is five minutes; with the reading and one prompt each, ten minutes is enough. The one allowed prompt is "Who does Lina invite? Who does Ben invite?" Marks: shown (names a shared event and a difference unprompted), shown with help (after the one prompt), not yet seen. On day 127, after the stems, the group has a three-minute exchange of questions among themselves: SL.K.1a and SL.K.1b are marked from it at no added minutes, using the item's wording. For the first fifteen children the day 125 talk record stands, with no second score.

**Exit questions.** No separate exit on days 126 and 127. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E125.** Card: "Kim reads under a tree. Len sings under a tree. Both move when a bee buzzes close." Ask 1: "Mark the two-column picture that fits." Ask 2: "Have a three-turn talk with your partner about what Kim and Len did that is the same: ask a question, hear the answer, then ask one more." Right: the picture with the shared move on a bee, reading and singing apart; a related question, an answer and a follow-up. Wrong: the picture with each child in a different hat (A); a partner talk that stops after one turn (I).
- **E128 (fresh retry).** On the text, not an invented card: 01a page 5 and 01b page 5, read aloud again. Ask 1: "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about Lina and Ben." Right: Both find a way when there is a problem at their tree, but Lina asks Mr. Dube for help and Ben walks around the trunk himself. Wrong: says Both ... but Lina has braids and Ben has glasses (A).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

## 6. Days

## Day 125


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Hand-back: day 124 exit, error A; and day 124's errors X, Y, Z, AA and AB (five minutes, two small groups: A in one, the story-element errors in the other; any over a third get the whole class).
- **15 min. Reread both scenes.** The rain scene (01a pages 6 and 7) and the noise scene (01b pages 4 to 6) twice each. "Which details belong in the shared column?" and "Which details tell the experiences apart?"
- **15 min. Make the comparison chart.** Partner pairs build the two columns with picture cards, shared column first (both enjoy a tree; both have a plan that changes) and the differences second (rain, noise; Lina waits, Ben moves).
- **15 min. Rehearse discussion exchanges.** Three groups of five are watched for five minutes each (the first fifteen children; their talk record is carried to day 127); the other fifteen practice in pairs with the turn card and the follow-up question card. Then the groups swap sides for rehearsal only, unwatched. Errors H and I are shown with right examples; SL.K.3 clarification question: "What does ___ mean?"
- **5 min. Two invented cards.** RI.K.9 retrieval only: "A branch holds leaves." and "A branch holds a nest." (invented for practice). "Say a Both ... but ... sentence."
- **5 min. Exit.** E125.

- **20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 20, lesson 102: -an and -am).** Pattern teaching and joint reading of cycle-20 sentences. Sound change ham to him, the changed sound named. Retrieve the mapped words so far, each read in text before it is read alone.
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** Sam and Pam, reread with a new question each time. Assignments 1 to 6 only, with the saved time given to more reading; no standalone isolated tasks today. The teacher hears the day's rotating six for 10 minutes (about 100 seconds a child: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task) and names them in the record column; the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards, with no timer shown.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** Reread Tree Care Words, verse 2 by echo; point to the line that gives the order. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, a space between words, and each child writes the day's spelled word by its sounds.
- **5 min. More reading.** The saved check minutes go to a second reading of the reader for meaning (assignments 1 to 6 only).

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the chart. Tool: none. By hand: chart and cards. Exit: E125. Out: errors A and I to day 126, and a note in the record that fifteen children's talk is already carried.

## Day 126

Assignments 1 to 6 use the reader and the verse; 7 and 8 are the two isolated tasks (sounds without a text; one mapped word read and one taught word spelled). ## Day 128


- **15 min. Return and repair, with a fresh retry.** Ten minutes: hand-back from C1 on days 126 and 127, errors A, H and I. A group of up to six who made one error gets a small-group repair with the turn card and the follow-up card; if more than ten children made one error, the whole class gets that repair and the others reread. Then 5 minutes: E128 as the fresh retry, on a new card.
- **10 min. Reread new passages.** 01a page 8 and 01b page 8 (the changed plan and the calm after it; not the reserved pages). "Which event does the comparison describe?" "Where does each story show it?" (pointing to the page).
- **15 min. Compare the two passages.** Pairs say two Both ... but ... sentences from the chart and the pages, one about an event and one about a feeling; the teacher hears the rotating six.
- **10 min. Draw and dictate.** One Both ... but ... sentence for the passage pair, the rotating six dictate to the teacher, and the rest copy-trace the frame.
- **5 min. Two invented cards.** RI.K.9 retrieval only: "A stem holds a flower." and "A stem holds a leaf." (invented for practice); one Both ... but ... sentence.
- **5 min. Partner share and tidy.**
- **15 min. Foundations: retrieval of print and sounds.** No new pattern. Retrieve print direction, one spoken word to one printed word, spaces (T), both letter cases (S), letter formation, consonant sounds, rhyme, syllables, and onset and rime, in one rotating routine at five stations of six children; the teacher hears the rotating six at one station.
- **20 min. Foundations: response and fresh retry.** Hand-back: the cycle-20 practice evidence of days 124 to 127 and any day 123 check component still not seen after the day 124 response (errors S, T, AG, AH, AI, AJ and AK first). Cycle 20's own check is day 129, so only taught text and taught words are used. Whole class for any error that more than a third made, others in small groups; fresh cases only.
- **15 min. Foundations: reread taught readers.** Sam and Pam and A Hen Can Nap, taught text only; the rotating six are heard.
- **10 min. Foundations: shared print and writing.** Tree Care Words, verse 2, reread by echo; one source sentence written together, spaces marked.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the chart. Tool: none. By hand: chart, cards. Exit: E128 is the fresh retry. Out: day 129 needs nothing from this day except records.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 125, "Which details belong in the shared column?" and "Which distinguish the experiences?"; day 128, "Which event does the comparison describe?" and "Where does each story show it?"
- Written task, day 128: draw and dictate one Both ... but ... sentence for the passage pair.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: the sentence names something both characters do or feel; it names one thing that differs; the drawing matches the two parts. Marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Frame "Both ___. But ___." and the question "What does ___ mean?" Preview: both, same, different. A home-language word may start the sentence; the teacher repeats it in English.
- **Access.** On check days a child may point to the pages, use picture cards of the two scenes, or answer through a partner who speaks for them only if the child asks; large-print page cards.
- **Check accommodations.** C1 is oral and never timed for the child; the one prompt is allowed for everyone; a scribe for any drawing.
- **Reading load.** Children read no story; they read only the cycle-20 reader and mapped words. The teacher reads every page used in C1.
- **Extension, same standard.** A second shared event and a second difference for the same two pages.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 125: the chart and one three-turn talk with the grown-up. Days 126 and 127: the grown-up reads page 3 of each story and asks the two stems; one prompt, not the answer; days 126 and 127 are one sitting. Day 128: repeat the missed idea with a new card.
- **One projector.** The two pages of C1 can be shown side by side as pictures only if the books are not held; groups of five work at a table with the books.
