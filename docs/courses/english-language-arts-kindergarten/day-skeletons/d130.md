## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Reread the whole reasons text and model a box with arrows for its stated reasons.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-02 Informational texts on why trees matter to a community and how to plant one. Topic base M4 U2 L2, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 21 L106: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-21 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words we all one; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Reserved text not yet on file: write/choose, measure where meaningful, clear rights and complete text read before use. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: school grounds or a class pot; no yard assumption; cards and identities remain in class. Read gk-u4-02-a from the why-trees/planting pair; author-reason prompts use -a, and comparison uses both.

- **RF.K.2:** Demonstrate understanding of spoken words, syllables, and sounds (phonemes).
