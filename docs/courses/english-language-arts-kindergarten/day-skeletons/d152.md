## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Explain the author point and the reasons heard in the information text.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-02 Informational texts on why trees matter to a community and how to plant one; gk-u4-03 A true account of Wangari Maathai, who led people in Kenya to plant millions of trees. Topic base M4 U3 L13, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 25 L127: r-controlled vowels heard only; long e heard and linked to printed e. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-25 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words she her like; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Independent text stays within taught short vowels, digraphs and doubled finals; no CVCe, vowel teams, printed -ank/-ink, clusters or r-controlled patterns. The long sound is linked to the printed vowel alone. Reserved text not yet on file: write/choose, measure where meaningful, clear rights and complete text read before use. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: school grounds or a class pot; no yard assumption; cards and identities remain in class. Sensitivity flag, Wangari Maathai: sensitivity read after the frame-specified Kenyan and movement source study; show Kenya and people with agency, no poverty/distance shorthand. Millions is said in words only. Text facts await verification during writing. Embedded end-of-unit observation. Six groups of five, five minutes each: point to the author point and every reason stated in the selected short gk-u4-02 passage; accept prompting. Do not substitute the child's preference or judge the reasons. The codes cell names observed or explicitly retained evidence only; concurrent foundations/topic instruction continues as described, without assessing every practice code. Read gk-u4-02-a from the why-trees/planting pair; author-reason prompts use -a, and comparison uses both.
