## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Model a shared focus statement and one factual detail from the sources.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u3-05 Tree Texts; gk-u3-03 A Tree Is a Living Thing. Topic base M3 U3 L3, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 16 L83: short o. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-16 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words or for; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: no graphic predation; drawing and dictation accepted without a spelling penalty. Read Tree Texts selections gk-u3-05-b; verify the final held selection titles when adapting the set.
