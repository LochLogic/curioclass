## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

- **Note:** Read: gk-u3-05 Tree Texts; gk-u3-06 Who Depends on a Tree?. Topic base M3 U2 L6, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 14 L74: short i. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-14 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound.
