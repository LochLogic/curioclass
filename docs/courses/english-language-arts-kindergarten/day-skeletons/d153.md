## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Share cards with a clear explanation and helpful drawing.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-01 Stories of characters who enjoy and care for a tree (one: a child who looks after a young tree outside her apartment building); gk-u4-02 Informational texts on why trees matter to a community and how to plant one. Topic base M4 U3 L14, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 25 L128: r-controlled vowels heard only; long e heard and linked to printed e. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-25 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words she her like; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Independent text stays within taught short vowels, digraphs and doubled finals; no CVCe, vowel teams, printed -ank/-ink, clusters or r-controlled patterns. The long sound is linked to the printed vowel alone. Reserved text not yet on file: write/choose, measure where meaningful, clear rights and complete text read before use. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: school grounds or a class pot; no yard assumption; cards and identities remain in class. Embedded end-of-unit observation. First 15 children, three groups of five, five minutes each: describe the familiar subject, add prompted detail, clarify with the drawing and speak audibly with clear thoughts/feelings. The codes cell names observed or explicitly retained evidence only; concurrent foundations/topic instruction continues as described, without assessing every practice code. Read gk-u4-01-a and gk-u4-01-b from the tree-care story pair; both were read whole before comparison. Read gk-u4-02-a and gk-u4-02-b from the why-trees/planting pair; author-reason prompts use -a, and comparison uses both.
