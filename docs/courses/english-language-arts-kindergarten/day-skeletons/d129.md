## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Read why trees matter to a community and identify the author's point.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-02 Informational texts on why trees matter to a community and how to plant one. Topic base M4 U2 L1, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 20 L105: -an and -am. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-20 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words there they where; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Keep the cycle assessment inside foundations small-group rotations; next foundations block begins with 10 minutes of response and a fresh retry, counted only there.
