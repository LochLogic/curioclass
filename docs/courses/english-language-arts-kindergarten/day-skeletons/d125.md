## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Prepare a two-column comparison and rehearse several discussion exchanges.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-01 Stories of characters who enjoy and care for a tree (one: a child who looks after a young tree outside her apartment building). Topic base M4 U1 L7, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 20 L102: -an and -am. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-20 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words there they where; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Reserved text not yet on file: write/choose, measure where meaningful, clear rights and complete text read before use. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: school grounds or a class pot; no yard assumption; cards and identities remain in class. Literary comparison uses familiar gk-u4-01 stories A and B, both similarities and differences in the characters adventures/experiences. Read gk-u4-01-a and gk-u4-01-b from the tree-care story pair; both were read whole before comparison.
