## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Read A Tree Is a Living Thing whole and ask who needs trees.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u3-03 A Tree Is a Living Thing. Topic base M3 U2 L1, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 13 L69: short a. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-13 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words at has; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: no graphic predation; drawing and dictation accepted without a spelling penalty.
