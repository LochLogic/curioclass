## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Reread the planting text and compare its steps with the earlier reasons text.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-02 Informational texts on why trees matter to a community and how to plant one. Topic base M4 U3 L8, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 24 L122: long o and u heard, linked to printed o and u. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-24 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words you said were; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Independent text stays within taught short vowels, digraphs and doubled finals; no CVCe, vowel teams, printed -ank/-ink, clusters or r-controlled patterns. The long sound is linked to the printed vowel alone. Reserved text not yet on file: write/choose, measure where meaningful, clear rights and complete text read before use. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: school grounds or a class pot; no yard assumption; cards and identities remain in class. Informational comparison uses the two familiar gk-u4-02 selections: why trees matter and how to plant one, including what their words and illustrations show alike and differently. Read gk-u4-02-a and gk-u4-02-b from the why-trees/planting pair; author-reason prompts use -a, and comparison uses both.
