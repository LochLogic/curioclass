## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Use the living-things reading to explain one connection between facts.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u3-02 What's Alive and What's Not?. Topic base M3 U1 L2, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 12 L62: sh th ch. Teach digraphs directly; continue oral onset-rime and three-phoneme work before printed CVC reading. Map high-frequency words to do; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: no graphic predation; drawing and dictation accepted without a spelling penalty.
