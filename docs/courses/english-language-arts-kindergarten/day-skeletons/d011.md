## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Use the text to group toys by one attribute and explain the categories.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u1-05 Attributes of Toys. Topic base M1 U2 L3, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 1 L9: a t. Name and form upper/lowercase letters; map sounds in both directions. Oral rhyme and syllables first; onset-rime continued from cycle 5. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: classroom toys only; no home possessions or real-child conflict; names stay in class. Bridge, 10 minutes inside topic work: math unit 2 runs school days 19 to 44, after this day. Sort real toys by one attribute and compare groups by one-to-one matching, without needing counted totals. Additional mapped-word instruction for this cycle: I my. These original supplements support the class writing/shared text; they add no new decoding pattern and source lesson order is unchanged.
