## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

- **Note:** Read: gk-u3-05 Tree Texts; gk-u3-06 Who Depends on a Tree?. Topic base M3 U3 L11, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 18 L91: all short vowels. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-18 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words are with will; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: no graphic predation; drawing and dictation accepted without a spelling penalty. The codes cell names observed or explicitly retained evidence only; concurrent foundations/topic instruction continues as described, without assessing every practice code. Use matching cycle evidence once, never another interview. Day 117 reuses this record. Day 114 holds practice only and remains available for the shorter-year fold.
