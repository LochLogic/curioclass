## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Make the card opinion clear through words and drawing.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-01 Stories of characters who enjoy and care for a tree (one: a child who looks after a young tree outside her apartment building). Topic base M4 U3 L3, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 23 L117: long a and i heard, linked to printed a and i. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-23 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words what when then; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Independent text stays within taught short vowels, digraphs and doubled finals; no CVCe, vowel teams, printed -ank/-ink, clusters or r-controlled patterns. The long sound is linked to the printed vowel alone. Reserved text not yet on file: write/choose, measure where meaningful, clear rights and complete text read before use. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: school grounds or a class pot; no yard assumption; cards and identities remain in class. Base end-of-unit assessment slot: each child's opinion product names its subject and preference; use the drawing and composition already made, without a second comparison performance. Reading and foundations teaching continue. The earlier comparison record is not re-scored; days 150 to 152 provide the closing comparison/reason window. Read selection gk-u4-02-a for the card discussion; any return to gk-u4-01 uses the two already familiar stories, with no additional comparison observation today.
