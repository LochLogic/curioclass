## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

- **Goal:** Read the planting text whole and compare its information and pictures with the reasons text.
- **Note:** Read: gk-u4-02 Informational texts on why trees matter to a community and how to plant one. Topic base M4 U2 L3, adapted; trade-book wording and images replaced. Foundations base cycle 21 L107: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only. After explicit pattern teaching, read and reread cycle-21 decodable sentences in small groups and with a partner; say what happened or was learned; spell and change one letter, naming the changed sound. Map high-frequency words we all one; mark regular spellings and the part to remember, then read in text. Review earlier mapped words. Reserved text not yet on file: write/choose, measure where meaningful, clear rights and complete text read before use. Turn and talk on the text; teacher models early and fades starters. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading. Sensitivity: school grounds or a class pot; no yard assumption; cards and identities remain in class. Informational comparison uses the two familiar gk-u4-02 selections: why trees matter and how to plant one, including what their words and illustrations show alike and differently. Read gk-u4-02-b from the why-trees/planting pair; author-reason prompts use -a, and comparison uses both.
