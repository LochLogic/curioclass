# Section 2.1: Weather information: what observers notice

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-kindergarten/2/s/2.1), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-kindergarten u02-1.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.K.1 and RI.K.1, this section's share.** Children "ask and answer questions about key details in a text", with prompting and support. Day 35 models it on 01a (information) and on one retrieved Magic Bow detail (story); day 37 adds a four-sentence story card for RL.K.1; day 38 practices both kinds.
- **SL.K.2.** Children "confirm understanding of a text read aloud ... by asking and answering questions about key details and requesting clarification if something is not understood". Day 35 models the request ("Can you say that again?"); days 37 and 38 practice it.
- **RI.K.2.** Day 35 only: "identify the main topic" of 01a, with its details around it (the unit 1 main-idea-details model, review). Retelling key details is spoken in pairs on day 35 and returns on day 41.
- **SL.K.1, SL.K.3.** Day 35: "follow agreed-upon rules for discussions", "continue a conversation through multiple exchanges", and "ask and answer questions in order to seek help, get information, or clarify something that is not understood". The unit 1 discussion protocol is review.
- **RL.K.4, RI.K.4, L.K.4.** Days 36 and 37: children "ask and answer questions about unknown words": they ask "What does ___ mean?" and look in the page or picture for the meaning.
- **L.K.1b, SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6.** Day 36 teaches "frequently occurring nouns and verbs" (naming words and action words). Days 36 and 38 practice "describe familiar ... events", with a drawing, "audibly and clearly".
- **Foundations** (RF.K.1, RF.K.2, RF.K.3a, RF.K.3c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c): cycle 5, lessons 26 to 29. The letters v and s (name, sound, form), the mapped words he and we, syllable beats (review from unit 1), and first, last and blended sounds in spoken three-sound words.
- **Limits that bite here.** Read-alouds are listening texts; no child reads a passage. Words children recognize are only the letters and mapped words taught so far. Weather is words and pictures: "temperature" is a word for how warm or cool, felt with hands, never a thermometer or a number. No child is asked what they wear or own. No storm scene is acted. The day skeletons list L.K.1c (plurals), L.K.1d (question words) and L.K.1e (prepositions) on days 36 and 38; the unit plan teaches them on days 39, 43 and 44, so here the teacher uses them in speech and no exit asks for them. L.K.4a and L.K.4b (a new meaning; affixes) are not taught here: "clear" for the sky is first met on day 36 and its second meaning is day 44; word parts start day 53.
- **Connections.** SL.K.5 with SL.K.4 on days 36 and 38 (a drawing added to a description). Unit 1's Magic Bow, day 35 (retrieval). Science weather (K-ESS2-1), day 35: its picture record is the bridge if it is on the wall.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** At five, the main topic is what a whole text is mostly about, and a detail is one thing it says about the topic. A question about a key detail is asked about something the text said, not about the child's own home. Asking for clarification is a skill, so "Can you say that again?" is praised, never treated as a miss. "Weather observer" is a real job; children are invited to act as observers at the window and never asked to report anything from home. "Clear" has two senses (day 44 teaches the second); on day 36 only the sky sense is used.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none. The day 46 items (section 2.3) sample what these days teach. The day skeletons' exit rows were rewritten here as one-ask spoken items with new cases (rule 4).

**Day's evidence.** Every card below is invented for practice, Curio Class, 2026, ours, and the teacher says so aloud. A card is read aloud twice, with its picture, and never returns in teaching. Each child marks a picture on paper; the spoken part is heard from the day's six (two from each of three groups of ten, so every child is heard about once in five days) and recorded shown, shown with help or not yet seen. A wrong answer below is what a five-year-old says; none is scored and no child is named in public.
- **R35 (retrieval, no score).** After the hook, in pairs: "Tell your partner one thing The Magic Bow said." The teacher listens at five pairs. Meets: a detail the story said (for example the bow, the woman, what she said). A miss is repaired inside today's model (error A).
- **E35.** Weather Changes, page 8, read again with its picture. Spoken, item a: "What is this page mostly about?" (strip: a windy mountaintop, a sunny yard, a rainy playground). Answer: the very windy mountaintop. Wrong: "a flag" or "thick coats" (B, names a detail, not the topic). Spoken, item b: "What do the weather observers wear when they go outside?" Answer: thick coats. Wrong: "sun hats" or "boots and umbrellas" (A, not on the page).
- **E36.** Picture P36: snow falls on a snowman in a yard. Mark: point to the picture that matches, from three. Spoken: "Tell me about this picture in one sentence." Answer: names the thing and what it does ("Snow falls." "Snow falls on the snowman."). Wrong: "Snow" or "snowman" alone (AE, a fragment); "It is cold and white and nice" with no naming and doing (F, unclear). A voice too quiet is E, noted apart.
- **E37.** Warmer and Cooler, page 4, read again with its picture; say mittens once more. Spoken: "Ask me a question about the word mittens." Answer: a question that names the word: "What are mittens?" Wrong: "Why is it cold there?" or "Where is the town?" (AB, not about a word). A child with no words may point to a hand in the picture.
- **E38.** Two-picture strip S38: at sunrise a fox sits on a hill under a gray sky; at noon the same fox lies in bright sun. Spoken, item a: "Tell me what changed between the two pictures." Answer: gray sky then sun, or cooler then warmer, with a detail from each picture. Wrong: "The fox" or "a fox on a hill" (I, names an object, misses the change); one picture only (F). Spoken, item b: the teacher says, "The fox went to the thing by the hill. What could you say to me?" Answer: "What thing?" or "Can you say that again?" Wrong: nothing said, or "OK" (AT).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The unit 1 models are reviewed and used, none withdrawn: main-idea-details (day 35 topic with details around it; again on days 41 and 46), discussion-protocol (listen, take a turn, add one more; every pairs part), sound-spelling-map (the letter-sound cards gain v and s on days 35 and 36, the mapped word he on day 37 and we on day 38). New here: a three-card routine for asking ("Tell me about a detail", "What does ___ mean?", "Can you say that again?"), used in 2.1 and carried through the unit.
- **Key moment 1, day 35: finding a detail on the page.** Must be seen: a child points to the part of the picture that answers a question, and the teacher reads the matching words. Ideal instrument: a large picture page every child can point to. Spec: no tool; print or the big picture fits. Gaps and asks: none (the unit names only a text editor, from day 69; see TOOL-ASKS.md).
- **Key moment 2, days 36 and 38: the real window.** Must be seen: children look at today's weather and say what they see. Ideal instrument: a window, or a drawn scene card when the sky shows nothing new (said aloud to be invented). Spec: none needed.
- **A room with nothing.** A window or a hand-drawn scene on scrap paper, a pointing finger, three hand-drawn question cards and crayons.

## 6. Days

## Day 35


- **4 min. Hook.** Show the first picture of 01a, a yard in sun and the same yard in rain. Read page 1 aloud, then post the compelling question: "How does the weather change what people wear and do?" Children point to what is different; no one is asked about their own clothes.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** R35 (field 3): partners tell one detail from The Magic Bow; the teacher listens at five pairs and says today's next step is "ask and answer about a detail".
- **5 min. Words.** With gestures and pictures: weather, observe, notice, detail. Sun and wind are named by gesture only.
- **14 min. Reading 01a.** First reading of 01a, Weather Changes, whole, about 4 minutes with its eight pictures and no stops except to show a picture. Then reread pages 1 to 4 with one question a page ("What do children wear on the rainy day?", "What do they do?"), children pointing to the picture detail and the teacher saying the words that match.
- **8 min. Model: ask and answer, and the main topic.** The teacher asks a detail question about page 3, points to the evidence, and asks for clarification once ("Can you say that again?"). Then the main-idea-details picture: topic card "weather" on top, two detail cards under it (sun hats; boots and umbrellas), and the topic named aloud as "what the whole page is mostly about".
- **8 min. Partners.** Pairs take turns: one asks about a detail on the page the teacher names (page 2 or 3), the other points and answers, then they swap. Discussion protocol review: one speaks, one listens, a token passes. The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **5 min. Science bridge.** If the science weather picture record is on the wall, read it together; otherwise look at page 1 again. Weather is words and pictures only.
- **6 min. Draw a detail.** Each child draws one detail from page 2 or 3 and tells a partner "I noticed ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E35, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Shared reading of verse V1 (the first Weather Words verse, with s and v words; to write): track the line left to right with a pointer, locate the opening word, name its first letter in both cases.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Getting to Know Letters, part 1: reveal v and s (shape, name, sound /v/ and /s/, keyword from the verse); letter-sound chant for the eight taught letters plus v and s. Source: cycle 5, lesson 26.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Spoken words that start with /s/ or /v/ (sun, sit, van, vet): say the first sound; review sound-and-letter cards a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r. Writing place: Trace v and s in the air, then on paper with the arrow path (L.K.1a); say each sound while writing. Sound and verse place: Sort picture cards by first sound (sun, van, sock, vest, hat, pan); find v and s in the verse with a pointer. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the model and the main-idea-details cards; children hold the pointing and the partner talk. Tool: None. By hand: 01a pictures, topic and detail cards, token, paper, crayons, v and s cards. Evidence: R35, E35, foundations heard column.

## Day 36


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 35 (field 4, A or B) with puppets, then one fresh detail question about page 3 of 01a.
- **5 min. Words.** With gestures: rain, snow, describe, clear (the sky is clear: no clouds), and "naming word" and "action word" for noun and verb.
- **15 min. Reading 01a again.** Reread pages 1 to 4 of 01a. Before reading, children listen for one naming word and one action word. After each page, point to the picture and say who or what and what it does.
- **12 min. Model: naming words and action words.** Sort picture cards of rain, children, sky, drops, puddles (naming words) and tap, fall, run, splash (action words). Build one sentence from a naming card and an action card: "Rain taps." A puppet says only "Taps." and the class helps add the naming word.
- **18 min. Describe today's weather.** Look through the real window at today's weather (or a scene card the teacher says is invented for practice). Draw it in 8 minutes; then pairs say one sentence with a naming word and an action word, adding a detail (10 minutes). The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E36, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Shared reading of verse V2 (the second Weather Words verse; to write): find the letters v and s with a pointer and a finger, say the sound each time.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Getting to Know Letters, part 2: the verbal path for forming v and s, the keyword, and writing each letter on paper. Source: cycle 5, lesson 27.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Last sounds: say bus, yes, glass, then the last sound; first and last sounds in sat and van. Compare with the first-sound turns of day 35. Writing place: Write v and s three times each, then write the letter for a sound the teacher says (L.K.2c). Sound and verse place: Pairs find the same sound at the start of two pictures; point to v or s in the verse and name it. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the sentence cards and the window; children hold the pictures and the sentences. Tool: None. By hand: naming and action picture cards, window or scene card, paper, crayons. Evidence: E36, foundations heard column.

## Day 37


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 36 (field 4, AE or F), then one fresh sentence about a picture card.
- **6 min. Words.** With hands and gestures: temperature (how warm or cool something feels; no numbers), warmer, cooler, sunlight. Warm one hand with the other and cool it on the table.
- **9 min. Reading 01b.** First reading of 01b, Warmer and Cooler, whole, about 2 minutes with its four pictures; then one question: "What is the air like at noon, and when the sun goes down?"
- **15 min. Reread and ask about a word.** Reread pages 2 and 3. Model "What does cooler mean?" and find the answer in the page ("Cooler means less warm"). Then model asking about snug, which the page does not say: look at the picture, ask a person, or ask the teacher. The three-card routine is practiced once as a class.
- **4 min. A short story card (RL.K.1).** The teacher reads aloud and shows story card ST37, invented: "Ravi's hat blows away. He runs and catches it." Children ask one detail question (who? what?) and point to the picture that answers.
- **16 min. Partners.** Pairs: one asks about a detail on page 2 or page 3 of 01b, one answers with a picture; then swap; then each requests clarification once ("Can you say that again?"). The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E37, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Articulatory gestures verse: say /v/ and /s/ with the teeth and lips pictured on the card; point to v and s on the verse.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Mystery word he: clues in the verse, map the letters (h says /h/; e is the part to remember), find he in the verse. Source: cycle 5, lesson 28.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Blend sounds: say /s/ /a/ /t/ and sweep a finger; blend van, sat, vat (spoken words, with counters). Isolate the last sound. Writing place: Trace he on a card (only h is written from memory; e is taught in cycle 11); write v and s from memory. Sound and verse place: Find he on the verse; blend and say two spoken words with a partner and counters. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the model and the unknown-word routine; children hold the questions. Tool: None. By hand: 01b pictures, three-card routine, story card, paper. Evidence: E37, foundations heard column.

## Day 38


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 37 (field 4, AB or AT), then one fresh "What does ___ mean?" with a picture card.
- **8 min. Reread 01b.** Reread pages 2 and 3 of 01b (noon and dusk), children listening for what changes between the two times and pointing to the picture detail.
- **8 min. Two times.** Look at the window now and say what it looked like at the start of the day; if the sky has not changed, use the teacher's two scene cards for morning and afternoon (said aloud to be invented). Name one weather word for each time.
- **10 min. Draw two times.** Each child folds paper into two boxes and draws the weather at the first time and the second time. The teacher writes one dictated word for a child who asks.
- **12 min. Describe with a drawing.** Pairs: one describes the two pictures in clear speech with the drawing in hand, the other asks one question and requests clarification once; then swap. The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **12 min. Questions about both kinds of text.** A detail question about 01b page 3 (information), then a spoken story strip told by the teacher with two pictures (invented; the day and its weather): children ask one detail question and one clarification request, then answer one.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E38, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Letter-sound chant of the ten taught letters (a, t, h, p, c, n, m, r, v, s); point to each card.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Feel the beats: clap and count the syllables of weather words (sun-ny, weath-er, ob-serv-er); blend and segment two-beat words. Review from unit 1. Source: cycle 5, lesson 29.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Segment spoken words with one counter for each sound (van, sat, sit); map the second mapped word we beside he. Writing place: Trace we on a card (w is taught in cycle 10); write v and s from memory. Sound and verse place: Clap the beats of three picture words and place counters for the sounds of one of them. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: children hold the drawing and the questions; the teacher holds the two scene cards. Tool: None. By hand: two-box paper, scene cards, crayons, 01b pictures. Evidence: E38, the two-time drawing (practice), foundations heard column.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 35, "What is different in the two pictures? Which words tell you?"; day 36, "Which word names it, and which word tells what it does?"; day 37, "What does cooler mean, and how do you know?"; day 38, "What changed between the two times?"
- Written task, day 38: a two-box drawing of the weather at two times, with one word the teacher writes from the child's dictation or one letter the child writes for a taught sound. Practice only.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: shows two different times with one weather detail each; says what changed in clear speech; asks or answers one detail question about the text.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: weather, observe, notice, detail, warmer, cooler. Frames: "I noticed ___." "The ___ is ___." "What does ___ mean?" Children may answer in a home language with a picture, and the teacher records what was understood.
- **Access.** Raised-line or large cards; the real window or a seat near it; an adult points as the child directs; a communication aid or private exchange counts. A child who cannot hold a crayon dictates or chooses between picture cards.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a token on a picture, drawing or dictation count for every exit; the spoken ask is repeated once on request. Weather content is recorded apart from speech.
- **Reading load.** None. Everything is read or told aloud; children read only the letters and mapped words already taught.
- **Extension, same standard.** A second detail for the same page, or a question about a detail that a partner has not noticed.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Look out the window together and ask "What do you notice?" Read one page of the weather picture book aloud, point to the picture, and ask one detail question. Draw two times of the day in two boxes. No question about clothes or home.
- **One projector.** One screen shows each picture page and the sorting cards; the cards are drawn or printed once and passed; pairs work on paper.
