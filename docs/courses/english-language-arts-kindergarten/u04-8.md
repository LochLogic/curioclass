- **RL.K.9, RI.K.9** (day 157): retry comparisons of two familiar kinds of text.
- **RF.K.3b** "associate the long and short sounds with common spellings", with RF.K.3, 3c, 3d, RF.K.4, L.K.2d, RF.K.2d, 2e, RF.K.2: cycle 25 (days 152 to 155) links the heard long e to the printed e, r-controlled sounds heard only; day 156 responds to F7; day 157 retrieves RF.K.1a to d, L.K.1a, RF.K.3a, L.K.2c, RF.K.2a to c with fresh cases.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- Carried: the finished cards (they stay in the class), each child's plan sheet (wording, place in the order, who chose), the written picture-and-word feedback, the day-155 recording, the retained day-75 typed story, and the S1, C5 and P1 records. The recording stays on the teacher-held recorder; no uploads, no accounts.
- Instrument: a teacher-held audio recorder (light), day 155 (make the version), job practise with feedback. Judged against: every child's 45-second turn is audible and kept in its place in the order; the recorder is tested before class and the room is quiet. A room with no recorder cannot complete W.K.6 by paper or narration alone: the audio part stays open for the course window and the paper plan sheet is kept.
- Day 155 plan: 30 turns at 45 seconds is 22.5 minutes, plus 30 turn changes at about 5 seconds is 2.5 minutes; the teacher ticks W (wording chosen by the child) and O (order chosen by the child) on the class list as each child decides.
