**Unit assessment items owned:** O1 (day 74); N1, V1, D1, E1 and the retained S1, C1, G1, H1 (day 75); P1 (fifth six, day 75); the cycle 11 pattern check (day 75). Day 74 and day 75 are check days; there is no exit ticket on either, and day 76 collects no new record (a fresh retry informs support only). Marking scale for every record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Cards are invented for practice, Curio Class, 2026, ours.

- **O1, day 74: six groups of five children, 5 minutes a group, 30 topic minutes.** Each child, one at a time (about 60 seconds): "Tell us about your weather piece from day 46: who was there and what happened?" (about 40 seconds); the teacher gives one prompt ("Where was it?" or "What did it look like?") and the child adds a detail; then the child shows the revised day 46 drawing and points to the detail it shows (about 20 seconds). Meets: names who and what happened ("Mia ran in the rain."), the drawing shows the same detail, the voice carries to the back of the group. Wrong: "There," with no identifiable idea (F); a drawing unrelated to what is said (D); too quiet to hear (E); a thought or feeling that cannot be identified (AX). Each part is its own cell. Family alternative: a person you know, no private details.
- **N1, day 75, zero new child minutes:** the day 72 draft revised on day 73 and edited on day 74 after written feedback. Meets: events in order and a reaction, with a weather fact from reading ("Rain started. I waited inside. Later I walked. I felt glad."). Wrong: events without a reaction (AG); events in reverse (AH). First-teaching work is never scored.
- **V1, day 75, zero new minutes:** the day 73 peer-question addition after feedback and the day 74 revision. Meets: a relevant added detail ("Where?" leads to "beside the gate"). Wrong: draft unchanged (AI).
- **D1, day 75, 23 topic minutes (30 children, 45 seconds each):** each child chooses wording or order, or types with an adult, for the shared class piece. Meets: any contribution the child decides. Wrong: watching without contributing (AN). The record keeps contributions; the variety of tools completes on day 156.
- **E1, day 75, 15 topic minutes (30 children, 30 seconds each):** (1) zero new minutes: look at the edited captions for a capital on the first word and on I; (2) three brief oral prompts on unused strips: "Point to the period." "Point to the question mark." "Say the name of this mark" (the exclamation mark). Three sets of strips rotate by thirds of the class; each strip is a short invented sentence the teacher does not read in a tone that gives the mark. Meets: a capital first word and I; period, question mark and exclamation mark recognised and named. Wrong: a lowercase first word (AJ); a lowercase i (AK); a mark misnamed (AL). Each is its own cell.
- **S1, C1, G1, H1, retained, zero new minutes.** S1 from day 63: characters, setting and major events (wrong: object as character G, wrong setting H, a minor object as event I). C1 from days 72 and 73: a complete expanded sentence (wrong: fragment AE, no added information AF). G1 from days 70 and 71: "Ari carries"; "cups, boxes"; question words; locations (wrong: unclear description F, missing s X, missing es Y, a time for where Z, a reversed location AA). H1 from days 67 and 68 after written feedback and the day 69 retry: formation (unrecognizable form AM), rhyme (shared first sound alone K), syllables (phonemes counted as syllables L), onset and rime (every phoneme segmented M). Only parts actually heard are marked; missing parts are completed on days 77 and 78.
- **P1, day 75 (the fifth six children, 2 minutes each, 12 foundations minutes):** isolate map (m, short a, p); blend and segment sun; add s before at; change m in mat to s. Meets: m, short a, p; sun, three phonemes, sat, sat. Wrong: N, O, P (first, middle, last sound wrong); Q (cannot blend); R (omits a sound); S (adds in the wrong place); T (changes the wrong sound). Keep for days 77 and 78.
- **Cycle 11 pattern check, day 75 (10 foundations minutes).** Hold-up cards for both cases of j, e, z (3 minutes, all children, a rotating six say names aloud); each child says the primary sound of the taught consonants j and z, and the short sound of e (3 minutes in groups of five); every child writes j, e, z for dictated sounds on a slip (4 minutes, whole class). Meets: names; taught sounds; the right letter. Wrong: a sound given for a name (U); a wrong consonant sound (V); a wrong letter written (W). Completion of the whole alphabet is days 77 and 78.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 74: "Who was there, and what did you see?" Day 76: "How did the weather change what the character did?" and, after a share, "What would you ask the writer to learn one more detail?"
- **Written task, day 74:** the child's edited caption: a capital first word, a capital I, an end mark, with taught letters.
- **Judged by the teacher with three look-fors:** a capital first letter; a capital I; an end mark that fits the sentence. Samples that meet: "I see rain." "Where is the kite?" Does not meet: "i see rain" (AK); no first capital (AJ).

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 74: no retrieval beyond the feedback hand-back (check day); day 75: one look at 02a's ending; day 76: the most missed idea from the unit.
- **Fluency target.** None; the standard sets none. Rereading a poem or strip only.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: weather, draw, question, mark. Frames: "My weather piece is about ___." "This shows ___." "The mark at the end is a ___."
- **Access.** A child who cannot speak aloud points to the drawing or uses a card; a child who cannot type may choose by pointing; enlarged strips; a quiet corner for the group turn.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each prompt twice; a seat nearer the teacher for volume; dictation allowed.
- **Reading load.** No child reads; 02b and 03 are read aloud; strips hold one short sentence.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second detail to the drawing and say it.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 02b, hear the child describe a weather piece, check capital, I and end mark in one caption, share the finished piece aloud, and keep the retry card with the portfolio.
- **Last day of the unit.** Day 76 closes the unit: the adult reads the child's piece aloud, asks one question, and files the piece with the record sheet for the mid-year check.
- **One projector.** Show the typed class piece on one screen; drawings stay on paper.
