# Section 4.3: An author's point and reasons, and two texts on one topic

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **Section share, by standard.** RI.K.8 (identify the reasons an author gives to support points in a text) is taught on days 129 and 130 on Trees Help Our Community, a point and two stated reasons, and observed on day 132 (check C2) on an unseen card. RI.K.2 (identify the main topic and retell key details) is taught on day 129 only. RL.K.9 and RI.K.9 (compare two texts on one topic) are taught for informational text on day 131, on the reasons text and the planting text, and RI.K.9 is observed on day 132. RI.K.3 (describe the connection between two pieces of information) is first taught on day 131 with a planting step and its result, and returns in 4.4. Foundations: cycle 20's last lesson and its check on day 129, cycle 21 on days 130 to 132.
- **Limits that bite here.** The author's reasons are only the sentences the text states: children find them, never invent them, and a preference of their own is never an author reason. The point is a stated sentence, not a topic. A comparison names what the two texts say alike and what each adds; it is not a ranking. Planting is shown as a study of steps; no digging activity is planned. The reasons text is Trees Help Our Community; the planting text is Planting Together.
- **Connections.** RF.K.1, RF.K.3c and L.K.2d each day in the foundations block. RI.K.7 (pictures and words) in the day 131 questions.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The difference between a topic (what a text is about), a point (what the writer wants us to believe) and a reason (why, stated in the text); why a "because" sentence helps; how to point to a stated sentence; that the check cards of day 132 stay unseen until then.

## 2. Where it sits


Section 4.2 ended with the day 128 respond day; this section's evidence feeds C2 on day 132. C2 results (and the cycle 21 evidence) go to day 133's Return and repair in section 4.4, which holds the slot.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned.** C2 on day 132 (RI.K.8, RI.K.9), in the table above (items are on the unit plan). Days 129 to 131 each run the exit below; day 132 has no separate exit.

**How C2 runs.** Six groups of five, all thirty children, 5 minutes each. Page 4 of Wangari and the Tree Planters (read aloud once, then again): RI.K.8, 3 minutes: the five children place the point and the reasons on a mat with picture cards at once (1 minute, watched), then each child in turn says the writer's point and then one reason (about 10 seconds a stem, 100 seconds for five), with the 20-second reading it is 3 minutes. Page 7: RI.K.9, 2 minutes: the teacher reads page 7 (15 seconds), each child says one way pages 4 and 7 are alike and one way they differ (about 20 seconds each). The teacher gives one prompt per child and never an answer. Marks: shown, shown with help (after the one prompt), not yet seen.

**Exit questions.** If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E129.** On the text, not an invented card: 02a (Trees Help Our Community), page 6, read aloud again. Ask 1: "What is the writer's point on this page?" Right: A tree with birds makes our community a lively place. Wrong: says We can hear them sing (B); says We can watch them hop (B).
- **E130.** On the text, not an invented card: 02a (Trees Help Our Community), page 6, read aloud again. Ask 1: "Which two reasons does the writer give?" Right: We can watch them hop from branch to branch; we can hear them sing in the morning. Wrong: puts I like birds on an arrow (C); puts The bird is brown on an arrow (C).
- **E131.** On the text, not an invented card: 02b (Planting Together), page 1, and 03 (Wangari and the Tree Planters), page 5, read aloud again. Ask 1: "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about the two pages." Right: Both say a nursery is a place where young trees are grown, but the school tree comes from a nursery in a big pot and the women in Kenya grow seedlings from seeds. Wrong: says They say the same thing (E); says Both say the tree comes in a big pot (E).

## 6. Days

## Day 129


- **5 min. Vocabulary and return.** Words with pictures: community, shade, shelter. Hand-back: day 128 retry, error A, only for children who again chose a coat or hat (a small group, five minutes).
- **15 min. Read-aloud, the reasons text.** Trees Help Our Community, whole (282 words) with picture pauses. Content: birds, nests and baby birds; no harm to any animal.
- **15 min. Questions and talk.** "What is the main topic?" and "What point does the writer make about trees?" The teacher models the topic (trees in our community) on a card and the point as its own sentence (page 1 and again page 7), one ask each.
- **20 min. Topic, details and the point.** Children sort four picture cards of details (a shaded bench, a nest in a fork, children reading under leaves, a bird in the rain) from the point card, then retell two details in order to a partner; the teacher hears the rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E129.

- **20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 20, lesson 105: -an and -am).** Explicit pattern teaching and one joint reading of cycle-20 sentences (the last lesson of the cycle). Sound change ran to rat. Retrieve the mapped words so far, each read in text before it is read alone.
- **30 min. Cycle check F2 (prompts in s04 field 5), in the rotations.** About 30 teacher minutes for thirty children, one child at a time at about 60 seconds each for the oral parts; the written lists are completed together. The other children do familiar reader work. A child not finished is marked shown with help and finishes in the next day's response.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** Reread Tree Care Words, verse 2 by echo; point to the line that gives the order. Write one source sentence together, spaces marked.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the point card; children hold detail cards. Tool: none. By hand: sorting cards. Exit: E129. Out: error B to day 130's Vocabulary and return.

## Day 130


- **5 min. Vocabulary and return.** The words point and reason, said with the card in hand. Hand-back: day 129 exit, error B (one card: the pictures in the notebook are a detail, not the point).
- **10 min. Reread, the reasons and the point.** Pages 3 to 7. "Which sentence states the point?" (the first sentence of page 7) and "Which two reasons explain it?" (page 3, page 5).
- **20 min. Model the box with arrows.** On chart paper, the whole help claim in the box; one arrow to "shaded places to rest" (page 3), one to "birds shelter" (page 5), each said with "because"; then the class finds the point again on page 1 and sets the arrows on page 3 and 5 picture cards.
- **20 min. Partners try a new card.** "Put a rug by the reading corner. It makes the floor soft. It keeps the corner quiet." (invented for practice). Each pair builds a box with two arrows from five cards: the point, two stated reasons, and two cards that are not stated (an own like, a picture only); the teacher hears the rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E130.

- **20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 21, lesson 106: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only).** Explicit teaching of short e and short u from the source lesson, with the contrast heard and then printed (net, nut). The teacher says bank and sink and the children listen for the ending; no printed ank or ink word is read or spelled. Map we, all and one the same way; keep the earlier words on the wall.
- **15 min. Small-group reading.** A Hen in a Tub (first use; title adult-read, body uses only taught sounds and mapped words). Assignments 1 to 6; isolated assignments 7 and 8 are replaced by the response below. The teacher hears the day's rotating six for 8 minutes (about 80 seconds a child: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task) and names them in the record column; the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards, with no timer shown.
- **10 min. Response and fresh retry.** Ten foundations minutes for the F2 check (day 129), counted only here: the whole class repeats the repair for any error that more than a third made, the others retry one at a time with a fresh case; the retry replaces the matching probe. Hand-back: day 129 cycle check F2, errors S and AN.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** The teacher reads Tree Care Words, verse 3 (first heard today; adults read its rich language). Write one source sentence together, spaces marked.
- **5 min. Foundations check.** Say the first, middle and last sound of a new word; six children heard; unscored.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher builds the box; pairs build their own. Tool: none. By hand: cards, box. Exit: E130. Out: errors B and C to day 131's Vocabulary and return.

## Day 131


- **5 min. Vocabulary and return.** Words with pictures: nursery, mulch, pot. Hand-back: day 130 exit, error C (point to the writer's sentence first).
- **15 min. Read-aloud, the planting text.** Planting Together, whole (286 words), pages shown. No digging activity is done.
- **10 min. Questions and talk.** "What happens first in planting?" and then "How are the words and pictures different from the reasons text?" One ask each.
- **15 min. Compare two texts.** The teacher models a two-column chart for the two texts (Both are about trees, but one tells why and one tells how); partners then choose one likeness and one difference with cards; error E is shown with a right example.
- **10 min. A step and its result.** RI.K.3: link one planting step to its result with the "so" card ("the hole is wide, so the roots have room"); children say what the picture on page 3 shows about the step (the adult digs and the children stand back); the teacher hears the rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E131.

- **20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 21, lesson 107: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only).** Pattern teaching and joint reading of cycle-21 sentences. Sound change pen to peg, the changed sound named. Ending sounds ank and ink are heard only. Retrieve the mapped words so far, each read in text before it is read alone.
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** A Hen in a Tub, reread with a new question each time. Assignments 1 to 6 only, with the saved time given to more reading; no standalone isolated tasks today. The teacher hears the day's rotating six for 10 minutes (about 100 seconds a child: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task) and names them in the record column; the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards, with no timer shown.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** Reread Tree Care Words, verse 3 by echo; point to the line that gives the order. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, a space between words, and each child writes the day's spelled word by its sounds.
- **5 min. More reading.** The saved check minutes go to a second reading of the reader for meaning (assignments 1 to 6 only).

Who holds what is carried: the chart is the teacher's; pairs hold cards. Tool: none. By hand: cards, chart. Exit: E131. Out: error E to day 133's Return and repair (section 4.4).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview: community, shade, shelter, nursery, mulch. Frames: "The writer says ___." "One reason is ___." "Both ___, but ___." A home-language rehearsal first is welcome.
- **Access.** Large picture cards for the text's pages; a child who cannot place cards may point and a helper places them; sound counters of any size.
- **Check accommodations.** C2 is oral, untimed for the child, with one prompt; a scribe for any drawing.
- **Reading load.** Children read no text; the teacher reads 282 and 286 words aloud (a few minutes of reading with picture stops). Alone, they read only the cycle reader and mapped words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second sentence in the reasons text that supports the same point.
