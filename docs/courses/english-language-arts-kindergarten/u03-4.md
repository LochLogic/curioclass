# Section 3.4: People and animals depend on trees: the first animal piece

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Kindergarten. Unit 3, Trees are alive. *What do trees need, and who needs trees?* (days 80 to 118): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 95 to 99.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (95), application 1 (96), check 3 (97, 98, 99). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 4 of 9 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children still read nothing but short decodable words. Day 95 teaches how a person's need meets a tree resource, on 04. Day 96 plans and drafts the first animal piece (R1) from two read-aloud sources and three supplied cards, with no model of the answer. Days 97 to 99 are check days: ten topic minutes of revision, then thirty children heard in three groups of ten, one day each, with no reteaching. The foundations block closes short i on day 95 (the cycle 14 check and P5) and starts short u on day 96 (cycle 15). Each day is 120 minutes: 60 topic, 60 foundations.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: source, detail, information, check, person. Frames: "The ___ needs ___ from the ___." and "I found it on card ___." Home-language rehearsal with a partner first; then English.
- **Access.** Enlarged, tactile or described cards; a pointed card is an answer. A child who cannot write dictates; dictation does not replace the sound-to-letter act on foundations tasks.
- **Check accommodations.** The R1 cards are read aloud twice; the child may point, draw or answer privately; a scribe records without adding ideas. Use an agreed access method rather than a default "not yet seen".
- **Reading load.** Words of taught letters only; the teacher reads every direction and every card.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child who is secure adds a second fact from a second card and shows both cards.
