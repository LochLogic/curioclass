# Section 1.7: Explain, join in and close the unit


## 1. Standards and practices


Children do not read these texts. Stories, information pages and poems are heard, with pictures, and answered by pointing, marking, drawing, acting or saying. Each day is 60 minutes of topic and 60 of foundations.

- **Shares.** RI.K.2, RI.K.1, RI.K.7 and SL.K.2 on day 31, observed as T1 to T4: "identify the main topic and retell key details", "ask and answer questions about key details", "describe the relationship between illustrations and the text", "requesting clarification if something is not understood". L.K.5a to d and L.K.6 on day 32, observed as V1 to V5: "sort common objects into categories", "relating them to their opposites", "identify real-life connections between words and their use", "distinguish shades of meaning among verbs ... by acting out the meanings", "use words and phrases acquired". RL.K.10 and RI.K.10 on day 32 (V6, V7): "actively engage in group reading activities with purpose and understanding". W.K.1 on day 33 is a retained record only. Day 34 is one response and a fresh unscored retry. Foundations: cycle 4, m and r, lessons 24 and 25 (days 31, 32); the last battery group on day 31; C4 and C5 on day 32; response on days 33 and 34.
- **Limits that bite here.** Classroom toys only; no home possessions; names stay in the room. Quiet attention alone is not evidence of joining a reading. A fresh word is never taught or practiced first: the held-back list (pat, tap, hat, bat, pal, mat, cat, puppet, wagon, sun, mop, cup, stop, wet, dry, loud, quiet, shiny, smooth, hop, creep, stomp, long, short, fox, box, fish, log, bounce, spring, rattle, gallop, glide, full, empty) is kept out of practice until its check, and day 34 uses bat, mop, log and cup only for the repair. Seated finger movement is the same evidence as moving. Letters in unit 1 are a t h p n c m r only; the full alphabet closes on days 77 and 78.
- **Connections.** Discussion-protocol, main-idea-details and the sound-spelling-map are used, not extended.
- **Teacher page explains.** Why the heard folio is new on day 31; why an unfinished component stays blank and is not guessed; why day 33 scores nothing new; what "purposeful" means in a shared reading (a child joins to find or say a named word).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned (field 5 of the unit plan):** the day 32 RF.K.3c row, which is no new item: name the child's dated Mapped words component from the day 28, 29, 30 or 31 battery as the record; no second list, text reading or score. T1 belongs to section 1.6 (table above); T2 to T4 and V1 to V7 belong to section 1.5; this section runs them. C4 (cycle 4 components) appears in neither table; it is planned on day 32. Day 33 has no exit: O4 is retained evidence and nothing new is scored.

**Exit questions (days 31 and 32).** Cards are invented for practice and the teacher says so. Every child marks a picture; the spoken part is heard from a rotating six (a column names who was heard). Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Wrong answers carry one letter.
- **E31 (RI.K.2, RF.K.2b).** A Toy Can Turn, page 3, read again. Mark the picture that explains the page with a detail (a top spinning upright, then leaning, then lying on its side; a top in a toy box; a drum). Spoken: "Clap the parts of over." Answer: the top spinning and falling; o-ver, two. Wrong: the drum (B); the top in the box with no action (B); the separate letters or sounds counted as parts (R).
- **E32 (L.K.5, L.K.6, RL.K.10, RI.K.10).** Card: "The bag is full of leaves. The tub is empty. We gallop like horses, then we glide like boats." Mark the strip that shows full against empty and gallop against glide. Spoken: "Say a word that is the opposite of full." Answer: the strip with a full bag, an empty tub, a springy horse and a smooth boat; "empty". Wrong: both movements the same (W); a tub with two leaves called empty (V); heavy given as the opposite of full (U).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Discussion-protocol: partners hold turns and the teacher listens, from day 31. Main-idea-details: the 03 and 04 chart stays on the wall for day 31. Sound-spelling-map: m and r are on it from day 28 and stay for every foundations block. The teacher holds only the observation sheet.
- **Key moment 1, days 31 and 32: the observation groups.** Ideal instrument: one record sheet, a column for each T or V item and for who was heard. Spec: fits, hand-ruled. Count: day 31, six groups of five at 4 minutes: all five mark the topic and picture cards (40 seconds) and each child is heard on two of the four spoken items, about ten hearings of 20 seconds a group. Day 32, the table items V1 to V4 take 3 minutes a group, 18 minutes; V6 and V7 are observed during the shared readings at 30 seconds a group. Spoken items a child was not heard on are "not yet seen" and are filled on day 33 for the children with the most blanks, about 15 minutes at 1 minute each. A blank stays blank.
- **Key moment 2, day 32: C4 and C5.** Ideal instrument: the component record sheet. Spec: fits. Count: C4 at most 1 minute a child, 30 minutes for thirty children; C5 half a minute each, 15 minutes; the other children rotate through stations.
- **Key moment 3, day 34: the response.** Ideal instrument: the child's own recorded error on an unused strip. Spec: fits. Count: 15 minutes of topic in small groups by target.
- **A room with nothing.** Draw the cards on scrap paper; use finger movements for the verbs; chalk or a finger in a tray for letters.

## 6. Days

## Day 31


- **5 min. Reopen and repair.** Day 30 portrait patterns (M, X): name the toy first, then a fact, beside "I like it".
- **8 min. Return to 03 and 04.** "Which details belong together?" Sort detail cards (a cart's wheels roll; a top spins) under the two topics; "How do pictures extend the heard words?" Partners point to one picture and say what it adds.
- **4 min. Hear a new information booklet.** Folio B (Bouncing Toys, invented for practice), whole and once, with its front cover, title page and back cover shown.
- **24 min. Observation groups.** Six groups of five, 4 minutes each, T1 to T4 as the field 5 count. The other twenty-five draw and label two details from 03 or 04 and tell a partner which page each came from.
- **8 min. Parts of words.** Clap the parts of toy names off the held-back list; pairs clap and say.
- **6 min. Turn and talk.** "How did a picture tell you more than the words?" 3 minutes each way.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E31.
- **10 min. Oral work.** Rhyme and syllables with off-list words.
- **10 min. Letters m and r.** Cycle 4 lesson 24: name M m R r and their sounds, map both ways; the part to remember in in.
- **28 min. Common battery, final seven.** Four minutes a child at the teacher table; the other twenty-three rotate through four stations of 7 minutes (letter cards, printing, oral picture games, pointing in shared print).

- **5 min. Check and feedback.** Write m and r for two dictated sounds; practice only.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 31, "Which details belong together? How do pictures extend the heard words?"; day 32, "Which learned words describe different play? Which attribute categories stay consistent?"; day 33 has no new question set.
- Written task: none new. Day 33 children share the earlier toy-choice drawing with a partner in two exchanges and may add a label; day 34 gives one fresh unscored informative drawing and label.
- Look-fors: (1) the child points to or names the word or picture that answers; (2) the answer matches the question asked; (3) the child continues to a second exchange; (4) a learned word is used with its real object. Each is recorded shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: topic, detail, opposite, full, empty, riddle, clue. Frames: "This page is about ___." "The opposite of ___ is ___." "Please read ___ again." A gesture, a home-language word or a pointed card is an answer.
- **Access.** Enlarge and describe each picture; seated finger movement for gallop, glide and the others; tactile letter cards; a communication aid is an answer; each prompt is said twice.
- **Check accommodations.** A private answer or a scribe is allowed; a spoken part may be pointed; an unfinished component stays blank and is not guessed.
- **Reading load.** No child reads a text. Read aloud: Folio B (about 40 words), a story page, the poem p07, one page of 05 and the riddles; at most 150 heard words at once, with a picture.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find one more opposite pair in the room and say where it is.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** At the table: read Folio B or a page of 03 once; ask the child to say the topic and two details, to ask you one question and to point to the picture that matches one sentence. Words: say the opposite of a word and let the child act it. Letters: say a sound, the child writes the letter. Day 34: keep the fresh retry with the child's folder.
- **One projector.** Show one page at a time, large; children mark on printed cards; a screen never replaces a child's own mark or spoken answer.
