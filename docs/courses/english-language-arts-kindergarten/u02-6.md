## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.K.3, this section's share:** "identify characters, settings, and major events in a story", with prompting and support. Day 54 teaches it on a new story (gk-u2-02-b, Priya at the Window); day 57 practices it on a familiar one.
- **RL.K.4, RI.K.4, L.K.4a, L.K.4b:** "ask and answer questions about unknown words" and use an ending or prefix as a clue. Day 54 asks and answers about one word; day 58 collects the first-half record (fifteen children) for M2, which section 2.9 owns and finishes on day 73.
- **RL.K.2:** "retell familiar stories, including key details", day 57 (The Magic Bow from unit 1, then pages 3 and 4 of The Picnic Plan read again).
- **W.K.2, L.K.1f, L.K.2a, L.K.2b:** a drawn and dictated information piece (days 55, 58); "produce and expand complete sentences" in shared talk (days 55, 56); first-word and I capitals, and "recognize and name end punctuation" (day 56).
- **SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6, RI.K.1:** a drawing-supported weather report from the weather information, day 58.
- **Foundations (RF.K.1, RF.K.2, RF.K.3a, RF.K.3c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c):** cycle 8 (k and y; mapped words up and do) on days 55 to 58, the cycle check inside rotations on day 58. Day 54 is the response day for the cycle 7 check. Letters taught so far: a t h p n c m r v s g i d f l, and k y from day 53.
- **Limits that bite here.** Children do not read the stories. Everything is heard and pictured. Sentence strips and marks are printed for the child to look at; no child is asked to name a letter or read a word that is not already taught. Names of marks (period, question mark, exclamation mark) are said by the teacher and the rotating six. L.K.1b to L.K.1e are only used in the sentence talk; G1 on days 70 and 71 assesses them.
- **Connections.** RF.K.1 to RF.K.3 and L.K.1a run every day in the foundations block. SL.K.1 turn and talk runs every day (discussion-protocol, carried).
- **Content notes.** gk-u2-02-b: rain, a kitchen window, an older brother, an observer's notebook; no danger, no loss. gk-u2-02-a (day 57 retell, day 58 words): a spoiled picnic and a mild sad moment, a safe indoor ending. The Magic Bow: as unit 1 handled it (a toy that seems magic, effort and asking for help). No death, violence, religion or family inventory. Quiet alternative: a child may pass or point.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A story-map is who, where and what happened; a "major event" is a change the story turns on, not an object (G, H, I are the three confusions). A complete sentence names who and what happened; to expand it, add where, when or what kind. Capital letters mark the first word and I; the end mark shows what the sentence is doing. Observed weather (the window, the journal) is not the same as an event in a story. A one-word sense note: "drop" is a falling bit of water in 02b and 01c; the verb "to drop" is not used in this section.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none. Day 58 collects the first-half M2 word record (scored with section 2.9, day 73). Marking scale for every record below: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. All cards are invented for practice, Curio Class, 2026, ours, and are never taught. Each exit takes five topic minutes; the teacher reads the card twice, children mark pictures on paper, and the teacher hears reasons from a rotating six (a column on the record sheet names who was heard).

**Exit questions.** If many (more than a third) miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example (field 4).
- **E54** (RL.K.3). The Picnic Plan, page 6, read again with its picture. One ask, spoken: "Put each card in its box on your map." Three boxes, who, where, what happened; six cards (who: Tomas and Grandma Rosa, the toy duck; where: the blanket on the living room floor, the park by the pond; what happened: Tomas sets the duck on the blanket, the lemonade jug alone). Meets: Tomas and Grandma Rosa; the blanket on the floor; Tomas sets the duck on the blanket. Wrong: the toy duck in the who box (G); the park in the where box (H); the jug alone as the happening (I).
- **E55** (W.K.2, L.K.1f). Card: "Nell draws a calm pond. Later wind comes and little waves cover the pond. Nell draws the waves." Item a, spoken: "Which picture did Nell draw first?" (two pictures; paper mark). Item b, spoken, six children: "Tell me what happened in a sentence." Meets: the calm pond first; a sentence with who, what and a detail ("Wind makes little waves on the pond."). Wrong: a fragment "wind pond" (AE); the same short sentence repeated with nothing added when asked for more (AF). The other 24 children's sentences are taken from their journal dictation of the day and the C1 record on days 72 and 73.
- **E56** (L.K.2a, L.K.2b). Strips printed by the teacher, read aloud, no child reads them. Item a: "Circle the strip that starts the right way." ("the puddle is big." / "The puddle is big."). Item b: "Circle the strip with the I written the right way." ("Now i hop." / "Now I hop."). Item c: "Circle the question mark." (three mark cards; the rotating six also say each mark's name). Meets: capital on the first word; capital I; question mark circled, names period, question mark, exclamation mark. Wrong: the lowercase start (AJ); lowercase i (AK); a wrong name or wrong circle (AL).
- **E57** (RL.K.2). The Picnic Plan, pages 3 and 4, read again with their pictures. One ask: "Put the four pictures in order and tell what happens." Four pictures plus one extra (the picnic on the blanket). Meets: cloud over the sun, drop on Tomas's nose, hurry home, Tomas wet and sad; told first, then, last. Wrong: the hurry home before the drop (J); tells only that it rained (AV). Rotating six tell it aloud; the rest order the pictures.
- **E58** (SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6; L.K.4b). Information card, read twice: "Hail is ice that falls from clouds. It bounces on the path." (in no text; the teacher says it is a practice card; the hail fact: source to check). Item a, one ask: "Tell one fact from the card and show your drawing." Meets: names the topic hail and one fact (ice falls from clouds or bounces), drawing shows that fact, voice reaches the partner. Wrong: a drawing of something else (D); speech too quiet to hear (E); score the two separately. Item b, spoken, with a word card: "Jo rehangs the wet coat on the peg." One ask: "Mark the picture that shows what rehangs means." (hangs it again; takes it down; a different coat). Wrong: marks the picture that ignores the prefix (AD). The word question (AB) is heard in the fifteen conferences, not on paper.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Story-map (2.5 day 49): taught on a new text on 54, set beside 02a and The Magic Bow on 57. Word-parts (2.5 day 53): re- and -er on 54 and 58. Sentence-combining (day 40): the sentence-expansion move on 55 and 56. Five-place chart (day 49): reused on day 58 for the report. Discussion-protocol and sound-spelling-map: every day.
- **Key moment 1, day 54: a story seen in three boxes.** Ideal instrument: movable picture cards on a large three-box chart that children can hold and swap. Spec: none; paper cards and a chart fit completely (no tool is used in this section, so nothing is key or light).
- **Key moment 2, day 56: a mark and a capital seen in print.** Ideal: large teacher-made strips in a pocket chart with a color for capitals. Paper strips fit.
- **A room with nothing.** The same cards drawn by hand; a window or one printed scene stands in for today's weather (never an outdoor storm walk; a closed window or a picture).

## Day 54


- **Day 54 carried:** teacher holds the story-map and word-parts; children hold their own three-box maps. Tool: None. Evidence: E54.
- **5 min. Warm-up.** Review (a reminder, not a test): the three boxes on The Picnic Plan (2.5), said aloud with the old cards.
- **5 min. Words before reading.** window, room (gesture and point); observer (observe, from day 35, plus -er: a person who observes) and meaning.
- **15 min. First hearing of gk-u2-02-b, Priya at the Window.** Whole, unabridged, once straight through (about 3 minutes), then pages 1 to 5 again with the pictures and one pause a page. Reading fits: 313 words.

- **15 min. Model and map.** 6 min teacher models the three boxes on 02b (Priya and Dev; the kitchen window, a rainy afternoon; rain falls, rain gets lighter, rain stops and the sun comes out). 9 min pairs fill their own map from picture cards; the teacher confers with the rotating six.
- **5 min. Class weather journal.** Today's entry by the window, teacher draws, children say what to add.
- **5 min. Exit E54.**
- **10 min. Foundations: return and repair.** Response and a fresh retry to the cycle 7 check (day 52), small groups, counted only here; the others partner-practice with letter cards.
- **10 min. Foundations: letters and sounds.** k and y review from day 53 (names, sounds, sky-writing, sound-to-letter writing).
- **10 min. Foundations: rhyme, syllables, onset and rime.** Oral.

- **10 min. Foundations: shared print.** The class journal strip: left to right, spaces, first letter in both cases.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 54: "Who watches the rain, and what place and happening make this story different from The Picnic Plan?" Day 56: "What does the mark at the end tell a reader?" Day 58: "What weather fact will your report share, and which text or window told you?"
- **Written task, day 58 (and the day 55 entry):** a drawn and dictated report: the topic, one true fact, and what people do or wear in the text; the child may write any taught letters.
- **Judged by the teacher with four look-fors:** names the topic; the fact is true to the text or the window; the drawing shows that fact; the voice is audible. Samples that meet: "Rain. Rain fills a puddle." with a puddle drawn. "Snow. Flakes are ice." with flakes drawn. Does not meet: "I like boots." (C, no fact).

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 54 the 02a map (2.5); 55 the 02b map; 56 the journal sentence (2.5 sentence work, day 40); 57 the marks and capitals (day 56); 58 the retell cards.
- **Fluency target.** None; the standard sets none. Reading fluency is built by shared rereading of one printed strip, never timed (the teacher holds any watch, for rotation pacing only).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: window, room, observer, capital, period. Frames: "Priya is in the ___." "The rain ___." "My sentence starts with a capital." Gesture and picture first.
- **Access.** Enlarged strips and cards; a child who cannot see points to a described card; a child who cannot hold a crayon dictates; a child who cannot speak points or uses a communication card.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each card twice; pointing, dictation and a quiet corner allowed; preserve speech and handwriting where they are the target.
- **Reading load.** No child reads alone. About 313 words of 02b and the Magic Bow are read aloud; the student page holds at most 60 words, mostly labels.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second key detail or a second event in 02b and put it on the map.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 02b aloud, fill the three boxes together, draw today's weather, say one sentence and expand it, print the capital. Skip the pair parts; the exits are the same.
- **One projector.** Show each page of 02b and the cards on one screen; every child's marking stays on paper.
