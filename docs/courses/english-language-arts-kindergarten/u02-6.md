- **Limits that bite here.** Children do not read the stories. Everything is heard and pictured. Sentence strips and marks are printed for the child to look at; no child is asked to name a letter or read a word that is not already taught. Names of marks (period, question mark, exclamation mark) are said by the teacher and the rotating six. L.K.1b to L.K.1e are only used in the sentence talk; G1 on days 70 and 71 assesses them.

The Magic Bow: as unit 1 handled it (a toy that seems magic, effort and asking for help). No death, violence, religion or family inventory. Quiet alternative: a child may pass or point.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A story-map is who, where and what happened; a "major event" is a change the story turns on, not an object (G, H, I are the three confusions). A complete sentence names who and what happened; to expand it, add where, when or what kind. Capital letters mark the first word and I; the end mark shows what the sentence is doing. Observed weather (the window, the journal) is not the same as an event in a story. A one-word sense note: "drop" is a falling bit of water in 02b and 01c; the verb "to drop" is not used in this section.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: window, room, observer, capital, period. Frames: "Priya is in the ___." "The rain ___." "My sentence starts with a capital." Gesture and picture first.
- **Access.** Enlarged strips and cards; a child who cannot see points to a described card; a child who cannot hold a crayon dictates; a child who cannot speak points or uses a communication card.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each card twice; pointing, dictation and a quiet corner allowed; preserve speech and handwriting where they are the target.
- **Reading load.** No child reads alone. About 313 words of 02b and the Magic Bow are read aloud; the student page holds at most 60 words, mostly labels.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second key detail or a second event in 02b and put it on the map.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 02b aloud, fill the three boxes together, draw today's weather, say one sentence and expand it, print the capital. Skip the pair parts; the exits are the same.
- **One projector.** Show each page of 02b and the cards on one screen; every child's marking stays on paper.
