- **Content notes.** gk-u2-03 (Yusuf and the Parcel, our title; the unit plan's working title was A Parcel for Home): a snowstorm, strong wind, deep snow; a six-year-old's worry (stomach tightens, hot wriggling feeling in the chest, face falls) that his grandfather names as all right; a phone call to a parent; a plow; a safe ending in a warm kitchen. No injury, no loss, no danger comes to pass, no death or violence. Baba chooses to wait; danger is not a test of courage. Quiet alternative: a child may pass, point or look at the picture.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A story-map that grows: character, place and each linked event stay visible on the wall from day 59. Sources for the weather facts in the story are on the text's own sources list (NOAA and National Weather Service pages, checked 2026-11-19); the teacher need not add more. "Fell" is checked in the sense "his face fell" (looked disappointed); the snow "falls" in 01c and 03 is a different sense, said aloud on day 62.
