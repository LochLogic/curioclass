## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.K.3, this section's share:** "identify characters, settings, and major events in a story", with prompting and support. Days 59 to 62 teach it on one growing map; day 63 (S1) checks it; day 57 and day 64 practice it.
- **RL.K.4, RI.K.4, L.K.4a, L.K.4b:** "ask and answer questions about unknown words". Day 59 (parcel; -ed) and day 62 (face fell, a new sense of a familiar word; -ing). Day 62 asks one information word question from 01c.
- **RL.K.2:** "retell familiar stories, including key details", taught day 61 (linked events retold with first, next, last); the fresh record is R1 on day 64, with The Picnic Plan (gk-u2-02-a).
- **L.K.1b to L.K.1e:** used on day 60 only inside the story questions (who, what, where, when; in, by, on). They are not assessed here (G1, days 70 and 71, section 2.9).
- **SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6:** day 62, describe a picture detail with a drawing.
- **Foundations (RF.K.1, RF.K.2, RF.K.3a, RF.K.3c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c):** cycle 9 (q, u, x; mapped words a and my) on days 59, 61, 62 and 63; response and practice only on days 60 and 64 (no new pattern). Isolation words never end in /l/, /r/ or /x/. Letters taught so far: a t h p n c m r v s g i d f l k y, and q u x from day 59.
- **Limits that bite here.** The story is read aloud whole, never abridged, one fifth a day (212, 279, 228, 264, 309 words, about 2 minutes aloud each, the rest is talk and pictures). No child reads it or decodes it. No guessing the story from pictures before it is read. No outdoor storm activity, no snow-clothing inventory, no personal storm retelling.
- **Content notes.** gk-u2-03 (Yusuf and the Parcel, our title; the unit plan's working title was A Parcel for Home): a snowstorm, strong wind, deep snow; a six-year-old's worry (stomach tightens, hot wriggling feeling in the chest, face falls) that his grandfather names as all right; a phone call to a parent; a plow; a safe ending in a warm kitchen. No injury, no loss, no danger comes to pass, no death or violence. Baba chooses to wait; danger is not a test of courage. Quiet alternative: a child may pass, point or look at the picture.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A story-map that grows: character, place and each linked event stay visible on the wall from day 59. A major event is a change that moves the story on (the snow thickens, they go in, the plow comes), not an object (G, H, I). Sources for the weather facts in the story are on the text's own sources list (NOAA and National Weather Service pages, checked 2026-11-19); the teacher need not add more. "Fell" is checked in the sense "his face fell" (looked disappointed); the snow "falls" in 01c and 03 is a different sense, said aloud on day 62.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** S1 (RL.K.3, day 63; section 2.7 per the unit plan). R1 (RL.K.2, day 64) is the response retell the unit plan places here; the unit plan lists RL.K.2 as owned on no assessment day, so R1 is its one record. Marking scale for every record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Cards are invented for practice, Curio Class, 2026, ours. Each exit takes five topic minutes: the teacher reads the card twice, every child marks pictures on paper, six children (a rotating six, named on the record) give reasons aloud.

**Exit questions.** If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example (field 4).
- **E59** (RL.K.3). The Picnic Plan, page 2, read again with its picture. One ask: "Put each card in its box." Cards: who (Tomas and Grandma Rosa; the wagon), where (the path to the park by the pond; the school), what happened (they walk to the park and the sky begins to change; the jug alone). Meets: Tomas and Grandma Rosa, the path to the park, the walk as the sky changes. Wrong: the wagon as who (G); the school as where (H); the jug alone as the happening (I).
- **E60** (RL.K.3; the map keeps the old cards). Priya at the Window, page 4, read again with its picture. Item a: "Which picture shows where Priya is now?" (the kitchen window; a park by a pond; a bakery). Item b: "Which picture shows the big change?" Meets: the kitchen window; the rain stops and the sun shines on the wet leaves. Wrong: the park or bakery (H); Dev alone or the notebook alone (I).
- **E61** (RL.K.2). Priya at the Window, page 2, read again with its picture. One ask: "Put the pictures in order and tell the story with first, next, last." Meets: looks at the drops, listens to the tapping, writes and draws, told in order with all three. Wrong: the writing before the looking (J); tells only the writing (AV).
- **E62** (RL.K.3, SL.K.4). Priya at the Window, page 5, read again with its picture. Item a: "Which face shows how Priya feels at the end?" (four faces). Item b, six children: "Tell me what Priya did or said that shows it." Meets: the pleased face; "she grinned", "Can we go out now?". Wrong: a face the page does not support (A); a feeling word with nothing from the page (F).
- **S1, day 63** (RL.K.3, owned here). Each child gets three spoken asks, a minute in all, from the teacher, with the class map turned to the wall and prompting allowed. One ask is about the section heard today (section 5), by the child's roster number: 1, 4, 7 character; 2, 5, 8 setting; 3, 6, 9 event; the other two asks are about the whole of 03. Character: "Who is the story about?" Meets: Yusuf (also Baba, Mama, Mrs. Chen). Wrong: "the parcel" (G). Setting: "Where were they while the snow was too thick to walk?" Meets: the bakery. Wrong: the post office or the school (H). Event: "Tell me one big thing that happens." Meets: they wait in the bakery; the plow comes; they get home safe; the parcel is kept dry in a bag. Wrong: "snow" or "a parcel" alone (I). Look-fors: names the person or place; says what changed. Samples that meet: "A boy, Yusuf." "They waited inside the bakery." "The plow came and then they walked home." Prompting allowed ("Who carried it?"); a prompted answer is marked shown with help.
- **R1, day 64** (RL.K.2). "Retell The Picnic Plan with important details in order." Meets: the beginning (Tomas and Grandma Rosa pack and go to the park), the change (rain starts, they hurry home, Tomas is sad), the ending (the picnic on the living room floor, Tomas is happy), in that order. Wrong: only a feeling or the ending with no events (AV); order mixed (J). Record R1 first for children who did not need the repair; mark "after repair" for the others, in a separate cell.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Story-map (2.5, day 49; 2.6, days 54 and 57): grows on one wall across 59 to 63 and is turned to the wall for S1. Word-parts (day 53): -ed (59), -ing (62). Sentence-combining (day 40): an expansion on day 60. Five-place chart (day 49): not used here. Discussion-protocol: daily.
- **Key moment 1, days 59 to 63: one story, one map.** Ideal instrument: a wall map with movable cards that stays up for five days. Spec: none; paper cards and tape fit; no tool is used in this section.
- **Key moment 2, day 63: S1 and day 64: R1, thirty one-minute turns.** Counted: 30 children times 1 minute is 30 minutes of the teacher's time, so 29 children work at stations meanwhile (a drawing of any favourite part, partner picture sort, journal) that neither teach nor test the answer; the class map is turned to the wall. A room with nothing: the same, with cards on a desk.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 60: "Where are Yusuf and Baba now, and what changed?" Day 62: "Which words show how Yusuf feels, and which fact about wind and snow explains what Baba does?" Day 64: "What happened first, next, last in The Picnic Plan?"
- **Written task.** None in this section; a drawing on day 62 and the S1 and R1 responses are spoken. The class weather journal lines are teacher-written.
- **Judged by the teacher with three look-fors:** names the person or place; says what changed; keeps events in order. Samples that meet: "Yusuf waited in the bakery because it was too snowy." "First they packed, next it rained, last they ate on the floor."

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: parcel, path, shelter, pause, safe, plow. Frames: "Yusuf is in the ___." "First ___, next ___, last ___." "His face fell. He feels ___."
- **Access.** Enlarged cards; a child who cannot see touches described cards; a child who cannot speak points or uses cards; wall map at child height.
- **Check accommodations.** For S1 and R1: pointing, a picture-card answer, a quiet space, extra think time; the three asks may be spread over two short turns.
- **Reading load.** No child reads; 212 to 309 words a day are read aloud; the student page holds at most 60 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add the second adult or the second place to the map.
