- **Limits that bite here.** Children draft by drawing, saying and dictating; a child prints only taught letters (names, labels). The weather fact comes from 01 (a to e) or the class observation, never from home. No isolation word ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/. No home clothing inventory, no disaster retelling. No new teaching: every day applies taught work (days 65 to 69) to a new case.
- **Content notes.** gk-u2-02-a (the model story) has a mild sad moment and an indoor ending; Avi, Ari, Gran, Dad and Jo are invented. The F cards are invented for practice. Quiet alternative on every task: pass, point or draw.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The drafting order (character and place, then events in order, then ending and reaction) and why the order of boxes is kept. A "useful question" asks about something missing from the writer's own draft. A conference is two minutes of one child at the table; the rest work on their own drafts.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 70: "Which words tell us who is in the story, and where?" Day 72: "How does the ending show how the character feels?" Day 73: "What would you ask a writer to learn one more detail?"
- **Written task, day 73:** one added detail by drawing and dictation, printed by the teacher; the child labels it with a taught letter.
- **Judged by the teacher with three look-fors:** the detail answers the partner's question; it names a place or thing; it was added beside the earlier draft. Samples that meet: "I waited inside." "I waited by the gate." Does not meet: the earlier draft with nothing added (AI).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: character, setting, draft, duck, question. Frames: "My story is about ___." "It happens at ___." "What does ___ mean here?"
- **Access.** Enlarged cards and boxes; a child who cannot draw points to cards or dictates; a child who cannot speak points or uses a communication card; large print for the cards.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each card twice; pointing, dictation and a quiet corner allowed; G1, P1, M1, M2 and C1 can be done at the child's own table.
- **Reading load.** No child reads; 02a (314 words) is read aloud in parts; every card holds at most 40 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second reaction or a second detail.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the opening, events and ending of 02a in turn; the child draws and dictates each part; the adult does G1, P1, M1, C1 as a short talk with counters and cards. The partner question comes from the adult.
- **One projector.** Show the arc chart on one screen; children's drafts stay on paper.
