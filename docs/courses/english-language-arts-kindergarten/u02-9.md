## 1. Standards and practices


- **W.K.3, drafting share:** "narrate a single event or several loosely linked events, tell about the events in the order in which they occurred, and provide a reaction to what happened". Days 70 to 72 draft the character and setting, the events, then the ending and reaction, in drawing, dictation and taught letters only.
- **W.K.5, this section's share:** "respond to questions and suggestions from peers and add details to strengthen writing as needed" (day 73, partners ask, writers add; scored V1 on day 75 after written feedback and the day 74 revision).
- **L.K.1b, L.K.1c, L.K.1d, L.K.1e:** "use frequently occurring nouns and verbs", "form regular plural nouns orally by adding /s/ or /es/", "understand and use question words", "use the most frequently occurring prepositions" (G1, days 70 and 71, spoken only).
- **L.K.1f:** "produce and expand complete sentences in shared language activities" (C1, days 72 and 73).
- **RL.K.4, L.K.4a, RI.K.4, L.K.4b, L.K.4:** ask and answer about an unknown word; a new meaning for a familiar word (duck); an affix as a clue (M1 day 72 and 73; M2 day 73).
- **RF.K.2d, RF.K.2e:** isolate first, middle and last sounds in a three-sound word; add or substitute a sound (P1, five dates, six children a date). **RF.K.1d, RF.K.3a, L.K.2c:** cycle 10 pattern check on day 70 (b, o, w); retained for days 77 and 78.
- **Foundations (RF.K.1 to RF.K.3, L.K.2c):** cycle 10 lesson 55 on day 70 (the check, run inside rotations); cycle 11 (j, e, z; mapped words see and does) lessons 56 to 58 on days 71 to 73. Letters taught by day 70: a t h p n c m r v s g i d f l k y q u x b o w. Taught later in this section: j e z.
- **Limits that bite here.** Children draft by drawing, saying and dictating; a child prints only taught letters (names, labels). The weather fact comes from 01 (a to e) or the class observation, never from home. No isolation word ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/. No home clothing inventory, no disaster retelling. No new teaching: every day applies taught work (days 65 to 69) to a new case.
- **Content notes.** gk-u2-02-a (the model story) has a mild sad moment and an indoor ending; Avi, Ari, Gran, Dad and Jo are invented. The F cards are invented for practice. Quiet alternative on every task: pass, point or draw.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The drafting order (character and place, then events in order, then ending and reaction) and why the order of boxes is kept. A "useful question" asks about something missing from the writer's own draft. A conference is two minutes of one child at the table; the rest work on their own drafts.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** G1 (days 70 and 71), P1 (days 70 to 73, five dates with day 75 in section 2.10), M1 (days 72 and 73), M2 (day 73), C1 (days 72 and 73), V1 (day 73, scored day 75), and the day 70 cycle 10 pattern check. Marking scale for every record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Each exit takes five topic minutes (card read twice, paper marks, a rotating six give reasons; the record names who was heard). Cards are invented for practice, Curio Class, 2026, ours.

**Exit questions.** If more than a third miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example beside a right one (field 4).
- **E70** (W.K.3, RL.K.3). Card: "At dawn, Avi and Dad wait at a garden gate in rain." Item a: "Which drawing begins a story from these words?" Three drawings: Avi and Dad at a garden gate in rain; the gate and rain with no people; Avi and Dad at a pond in rain. Item b, dictated beside it: "Say the words that go with it." Meets: Avi and Dad, the garden gate ("Avi and Dad wait at the garden gate."). Wrong: the gate without people, the gate named as the character (G); the pond (H).
- **E71** (W.K.3, L.K.1c). Card: "First Avi packs a hat. Next rain begins. Then Avi takes the bag inside." Item a: "Put these pictures in the order of the words." Three picture cards (hat packed, rain begins, bag inside), shown mixed. Item b: "Say it for more than one: a hat, a bag. Two ___." Meets: packs a hat, rain begins, bag inside, in that order; hats, bags. Wrong: the order reversed or mixed (AH); "hat" or "bag" said again for two (X).
- **E72** (W.K.3, W.K.5). Card: "Avi waits inside. Rain ends. Avi smiles and says, 'Now we can go together.'" Item a: "Which two pictures finish the story?" Four cards: rain ends; Avi smiles; Avi waits with rain still falling; the gate alone. Item b, spoken: "Ask the writer one question to learn more." Meets: rain ends and Avi smiles; a question about a missing detail ("Where do they go?", "Who goes with Avi?"). Wrong: the rain still falling and the gate alone, no reaction (AG); "What is for lunch?" (AW).
- **E73** (W.K.5). Card: earlier draft "Rain started. I waited." Partner asks "Where did you wait?" Item a: "Which picture adds an answer to the draft?" Two pictures: a child waiting inside by a window with rain outside; the first rain picture again. Item b, dictated: "Say the words you will add." Meets: names a place ("I waited inside.", "I waited by the gate."). Wrong: the first picture again, or no words added (AI). The dictation is the evidence; the picture alone is not enough.
- **G1, days 70 (first fifteen children) and 71 (the other fifteen), 2 minutes each, 30 minutes a day.** Teacher reads F2 aloud once to all (30 seconds, inside the 30), then calls one child at a time. Child describes with a noun and action card ("Ari carries a bag"); forms plurals from object cards of cup and box (cups, boxes); answers and asks six printed questions with who, what, where, when, why and how (about 6 seconds a turn); follows two placements and describes two new ones from the eight prepositions to, from, in, out of, on, off, by, with, drawn so that all eight are heard across the fifteen. Meets: "Ari carries"; "cups, boxes"; appropriate information for each question word; correct relative position. Wrong: unclear description (F); missing s (X); missing es (Y); a time offered for where (Z); reversed location (AA). Record only what is heard; an unfinished component is finished in spare topic minutes rather than inferred.
- **P1, days 70, 71, 72, 73 and 75 (six children each date, 2 minutes each, 12 foundations minutes).** Isolate map (m, short a, p); blend s, u, n into sun; segment sun; add s before at (sat); change m in mat to s (sat). Meets: m, short a, p; sun; three phonemes; sat; sat. Wrong: first sound wrong (N), middle (O), final (P); cannot blend (Q); omits a sound (R); adds in the wrong place (S); changes the wrong sound (T). No isolation word ends in l, r or x. Keep the record for days 77 and 78.
- **Day 70 cycle 10 pattern check (10 minutes, inside rotations; no new item).** Hold-up cards for both cases of b, o, w (all children, teacher sees every card); a rotating six say names and sounds aloud; every child writes b, o, w for dictated sounds on a slip. Meets: names; taught sounds; the right letter for each sound. Wrong: a sound instead of a name (U); wrong consonant sound (V); wrong letter written (W). Naming aloud for all thirty is completed on days 77 and 78 (plan point).
- **M1, day 72 (first fifteen, 20 of the 30 conference minutes) and day 73 (the other fifteen, 10 topic minutes).** Item a: the teacher reads page 3 of The Picnic Plan once, words only; the child asks "What does dropped mean here?" and answers "His shoulders went down, because he felt sad" (wrong: a question not about the word, AB; "a drop of rain fell", AC). Item b: the teacher reads F3 twice: "The duck rests beside the pond. Gran says, 'Duck under this low branch.' Ari bends down." The child says a new bending-down sentence ("Duck under the table."). Meets: relevant question, meaning, new correct use, scored separately. Wrong: a question not about the word (AB); only the bird meaning (AC).
- **C1, day 72 (first fifteen, 10 minutes) and day 73 (the other fifteen, 5 minutes).** Teacher says F8 "Nia sweeps the path." Child makes a complete sentence and expands it with where, when or a quality. Meets: "Nia sweeps the wet path at noon." "Nia sweeps the path near the gate." Wrong: "Nia path" (AE); the same sentence again (AF).
- **M2, day 73 (the other fifteen children, 20 of the 30 conference minutes).** Teacher reads F4 beside 01c: "Rain falls on the path. Dad repaints the faded sign. Jo unrolls a mat. Two coats hang beside three benches. Yesterday Jo rested. Now Jo is resting. The careful child carries a spotless sheet. Before the walk Jo attends preschool." Item a: the child asks and answers a question about a word on page 3 of Rainbows, read twice, words only, such as "What does arch mean?" (8 minutes for the fifteen; wrong: a question not about a word, AB). Item b: the child explains two affix words drawn by rotation so all eight are heard across the fifteen (12 minutes): again, reverse, more than one, past, ongoing, full of, without, before. Meets: repaints means paints again; careful means full of care. Wrong: a question unrelated to the word (AB); "a different sign", ignoring the affix (AD).
- **V1, day 73 (15 minutes, all children).** Pairs: the writer reads or points through the draft; the partner asks one question; the writer adds one detail by drawing and dictation. The relevant detail (for example "beside the gate" for "Where?") meets; no change after a relevant question is wrong (AI). Written feedback follows class; scored on day 75.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 70: "Which words tell us who is in the story, and where?" Day 72: "How does the ending show how the character feels?" Day 73: "What would you ask a writer to learn one more detail?"
- **Written task, day 73:** one added detail by drawing and dictation, printed by the teacher; the child labels it with a taught letter.
- **Judged by the teacher with three look-fors:** the detail answers the partner's question; it names a place or thing; it was added beside the earlier draft. Samples that meet: "I waited inside." "I waited by the gate." Does not meet: the earlier draft with nothing added (AI).

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 70: the three boxes (day 69 errors); 71: character and place (day 70); 72: first, next, then (day 71); 73: the reaction (day 72).
- **Fluency target.** None; the standard sets none. Shared rereading of a poem or strip, no speed target.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: character, setting, draft, duck, question. Frames: "My story is about ___." "It happens at ___." "What does ___ mean here?"
- **Access.** Enlarged cards and boxes; a child who cannot draw points to cards or dictates; a child who cannot speak points or uses a communication card; large print for the cards.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each card twice; pointing, dictation and a quiet corner allowed; G1, P1, M1, M2 and C1 can be done at the child's own table.
- **Reading load.** No child reads; 02a (314 words) is read aloud in parts; every card holds at most 40 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second reaction or a second detail.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the opening, events and ending of 02a in turn; the child draws and dictates each part; the adult does G1, P1, M1, C1 as a short talk with counters and cards. The partner question comes from the adult.
- **One projector.** Show the arc chart on one screen; children's drafts stay on paper.
