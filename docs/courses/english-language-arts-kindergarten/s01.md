# Unit 1: Toys and play

Course `english-language-arts-kindergarten` (English Language Arts, Kindergarten). **Days 1 to 34** (34 course days; school days 1 to 34, weeks 1 to 7). Sections: 1.1 (days 1 to 5); 1.2 (days 6 to 8); 1.3 (days 9 to 14); 1.4 (days 15 to 19); 1.5 (days 20 to 25); 1.6 (days 26 to 30); 1.7 (days 31 to 34). Days by kind: understanding 14, fluency 3, application 9, check 5, respond 3. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

The phases are sharing, describing toys, different play choices, and reporting a partner's preference.

## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What makes a toy fun, and how do we share it?"
On day 1, hear the first weekly poem beside two classroom toys. Notice its invitation to join in, then open the compelling question. Model listening before asking children to respond.
Supporting questions: What do the friends do (day 3)? How does the picture show the words' moment (day 4)? Which details fit the topic (day 9)? What reveals the child's enjoyment (day 22)? Reread before comparing choices on day 24.
The closing piece names a classmate's classroom toy preference and gives information from the interview and moving-toys texts. Draft on day 27 and score the revised earlier piece on day 30.
Model pointing back to words on days 3 and 9, and an opinion on day 15. Partners take over on days 16 and 24; model interviewing on day 26, then step back on day 27.
