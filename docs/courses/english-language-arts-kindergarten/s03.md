## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What do trees need, and who needs trees?"
Open day 80 with the tree and rolling toy in Living Things, selection a. Read their contrasting descriptions, then pose the compelling question. Retrieve weather details after this hook.
Ask which two facts establish a need (day 82), how repeated lines explain growth (day 89), and how two sources explain an animal connection (day 96). U6 names the rereading.
On day 114, children finish a collage explaining a tree need and who depends on that tree, using Tree Texts and Who Depends on Trees? They explain it on day 116.
Model a because link on day 81, research notes on day 92 and information writing on day 93. Partners select evidence on day 96; children choose collage details by day 108.

## U7. Talk and writing

Use Read, Point, Talk, Draw daily, at least twice weekly formally: reread a difficult because line, point to evidence, exchange several turns, then draw and dictate. Feedback names one source link. Use the same writing rows for class and home.

## U8. Practice that returns

## U9. Supports and extension
