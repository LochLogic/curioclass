## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What do trees need, and who needs trees?"
Open day 80 with the tree and rolling toy in Living Things, selection a. Read their contrasting descriptions, then pose the compelling question. Retrieve weather details after this hook.
Ask which two facts establish a need (day 82), how repeated lines explain a need (day 89), and how two sources explain an animal connection (day 96). U6 names the rereading.
On day 114, children finish a collage explaining a tree need and who depends on that tree, using Tree Texts and Who Depends on a Tree? They explain it on day 116.
Model a connection arrow on day 81, a because link on day 82, research notes on day 92 and information writing on day 93. Partners select evidence on day 96; children choose collage details by day 108.


Keep seven text-record identifiers. Intended titles are Living Things (01a and b), the four adapted short works (02, 03, 04 and 06), Tree Texts: Oak and Sugar Maple (05a and b), and The Tree Beside the Path (07). Replace the image-based 05 readings with original prose and art. Field 10 records editions; U6 gives first readings and returns.
Measures of finished adaptations are unavailable. For anchor 01, causal meaning, linked explanations, because clauses and unfamiliar plant needs require gestures before reading, whole read-alouds, then matching two heard facts. Anchor 03 is a short chant: repetition supports participation, but figurative stretching and hidden processes need literal diagrams. Read 01 beside it. Both precede independent source selection; neither is a decoding test.
