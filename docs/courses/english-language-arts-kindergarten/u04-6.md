# Section 4.6: The appreciation card: opinion, detail and a partner's question


## 1. Standards and practices


This section teaches the card and gets its first draft. Shares, with the verb quoted:

- **W.K.1** (days 140, 141, 142, 144, 145): "compose opinion pieces" by drawing, dictating and writing. Taught on 140 (model) and 144; day 141 is the initial card, day 142 reads it as made, day 145 drafts the final card. Reasons enrich but are not required.
- **RI.K.8** (day 140): "identify the reasons an author gives", used to choose a card detail from 02a. Taught in this section only as a model; observation is elsewhere.
- **SL.K.4, 5, 6** (days 141, 145): "describe", "add drawings" and "speak audibly", practiced with the card.
- **SL.K.1, 1a, 1b** (day 143): "follow agreed-upon rules" and "continue a conversation through multiple exchanges", observed once per child (D1).
- **RL.K.1, RI.K.1, SL.K.2, SL.K.3** (day 143): key-detail questions asked and answered, and clarification (T1).
- **W.K.5** (day 144): "respond to questions and suggestions from peers and add details". Taught here; its observation (R1) is on day 148 in section 4.7.
- **W.K.6** (day 144): "explore a variety of digital tools". Shown only (the teacher-held recorder); nothing scored.
- **RF.K.3b** "associate the long and short sounds with common spellings", with RF.K.3, 3c, 3d, RF.K.4, L.K.2d, RF.K.2d, 2e, RF.K.2 (days 140, 141, 144, 145). Cycle 22 ends on day 140; cycle 23 teaches long a and i by ear and links them to the printed a and i only.

Limits that bite: no child decodes a long-vowel word; every stem is spoken with a picture card; the card is a process product (initial, final, color, revision, share) with written feedback after day 141, and day 142 reads it as made, with no interview. Judged by the teacher on the three-point scale (shown, shown with help, not yet seen).

The teacher page must say that D1 and T1 share thirty minutes, that the long sound is hearing and pointing only, and that R1 sits on day 148.

## 9. Supports and extension

- English learners: appreciate, subject, detail, question; frame 'I like ___. I like ___.' and 'What do you ___?' Home-language rehearsal before D1.
- Access: dictation, a pointed answer, enlarged cards; a quiet seat for D1.
- Check accommodations: pointing counts for a and i matching; extra time for the oral list.
- Reading load: no child reads the cards; the only print decoded is taught.
- Extension (same standard): ask a second question of the partner and add two details.

## 10. Two rooms

A parent teaching one child: reread 02a, then draw a tree or tree activity and say 'I like ___. I like ___.' Add one thing the book told. On the days of the partner's question, you are the partner: ask 'What do you do there?' and wait for the answer. A pencil and paper are enough. With one projector, show a card and read it aloud.
