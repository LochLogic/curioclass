# Section 1.3: How toys move, and how we describe them

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-kindergarten/1/s/1.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-kindergarten u01-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## 1. Standards and practices


Children still do not read. Everything is heard, pointed to, sorted, drawn or acted out; the teacher writes. Each day is 120 minutes: topic block 60, foundations block 60. One scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The two information texts are read aloud only; the pictures carry the examples.

- **RI.K.2, days 9 and 14.** "With prompting and support, identify the main topic and retell key details of a text." Day 9 models a topic box with detail boxes on Push, Pull, Roll; day 14 adds a new detail from A Toy Can Turn to the same chart.
- **RL.K.5, RL.K.6, RI.K.5, RI.K.6, day 9.** The kinds of text and the book parts, now on an information book: front cover, back cover, title page, author (words), illustrator (pictures).
- **L.K.5, L.K.5a to 5d and L.K.6, days 10 to 13.** "With guidance and support from adults, explore word relationships and nuances in word meanings"; sort objects into categories (5a); opposites (5b); real-life connections between words and their use (5c); act out shades of meaning among verbs (5d: walk, march, tiptoe); "use words and phrases acquired through conversations, reading and being read to".
- **RL.K.1, RI.K.7 (days 13 and 14).** Riddle clues as key details; "describe the relationship between illustrations and the text" (the day 14 picture explains a movement sentence).
- **RL.K.10, RI.K.10 (day 12).** Shared reading of poem p03, Toy Clues.
- **Foundations, days 9 to 14.** Day 9 keyword and printing for a; day 10 finding a and t in a poem; day 11 chant and writing for the sound; day 12 the cycle assessment inside the rotations; day 13 repair and retry; day 14 introduces h and p. RF.K.1a to 1d, RF.K.3a, L.K.1a, L.K.2c, RF.K.2a, 2b practiced; RF.K.3c: I, my and from day 14 me.
- **Limits that bite here.** Classroom toys only; no home possessions; riddles and acting use a puppet or toy, never a named child as a mistake. Movement games keep paths clear and stop for crowding. A child may pass.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A topic is what the whole text is about; a detail is one fact under it (a detail alone is not the topic). Opposites are two words that mean far apart, which is not the same as a stronger word. That the sound is shown with the letter and keyword, never guessed from a picture.

## 2. Where it sits


Uses the 1.1 and 1.2 record sheet: the book-part errors (I, AC) and the exit errors set day 9's opening repair. Its own results (E9 to E14, the cycle assessment notes of day 12) go to the record sheet; the day 12 notes select the day 13 foundations response.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none. Each teaching day has one exit; day 12 carries the cycle 1 assessment inside the foundations rotations, recorded as notes.

**Exit questions.** Five minutes at the end of the topic block, read aloud once and again on request. All cards are invented for practice and say so. Item (a) is answered by every child on paper; item (b) is asked aloud of the day's rotating six, and a child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". One ask in each item.
- **E9 (day 9; RI.K.2).** Read page 6 of Push, Pull, Roll again. (a) "Circle the picture that tells what the whole page is about." Pictures: a toy car that goes, stops and turns; one car standing still; a pole alone. Answer: the car that goes, stops and turns. Wrong: the car standing still (K). (b) "Tell me one detail the page gives." Answer: a push or a pull can make a toy stop, go faster or turn. Wrong: "Toys" (K) or an idea not on the page (A).
- **E10 (day 10; L.K.5, L.K.6).** Read: "The tiny drum is white. The gigantic drum is black." (a) "Circle the two drums that fit these words." Pictures: tiny white and gigantic black; tiny black and gigantic white; two drums the same size. Answer: tiny white and gigantic black. Wrong: tiny black and gigantic white (V). (b) "Which word tells you which drum is the big one?" Answer: gigantic. Wrong: "black" (V).
- **E11 (day 11; L.K.5a).** Read: "The page says the pillow and the sock are soft. The shell is hard. The shell goes in another group." (a) "Circle the picture of the groups that follows the page." Pictures: pillow and sock | shell; pillow and shell | sock; all three together. Answer: pillow and sock | shell. Wrong: pillow and shell | sock (T). (b) "What is the rule for the group with the pillow and the sock?" Answer: soft. Wrong: "They are both red" (T).
- **E12 (day 12; L.K.5b, L.K.5c).** Read: "The light is on. Now the light is off." (a) "Circle the pair of pictures that shows opposites." Pairs: on and off; on and on; off and off. Answer: on and off. Wrong: on and on (AE). (b) Three objects on a tray: sandpaper, smooth paper, soft cloth. "Show me the rough one." Answer: sandpaper. Wrong: smooth paper (V).
- **E13 (day 13; L.K.5d).** Read: "The toy band marches into the hall. The toy cat tiptoes past the dog." (a) "Circle the picture that shows how the cat moves." Pictures: the cat tiptoeing; the cat stomping; the cat marching in a line. Answer: tiptoeing. Wrong: marching (W). (b) "Show me how the band moves." Answer: firm, rhythmic marching steps in place. Wrong: tiptoeing (W).
- **E14 (day 14; RI.K.2, RI.K.7).** Read page 7 of A Toy Can Turn again. (a) "Circle the picture that shows what the whole page is about." Pictures: a top and a cart side by side, each turning; a top alone; a cart alone. Answer: both. Wrong: the top alone (K). (b) "Point to the picture that shows rolls from place to place." Pictures: a cart rolling across the floor; a top spinning on a table. Answer: the cart. Wrong: the top (B).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The topic chart (a topic box on top, detail boxes beneath) made on day 9 and extended on day 14 and in 1.4; the sound-spelling-map (t, a, and from day 14 h, p); the opposites pair cards from day 12; the book parts labeled on the class copy of Push, Pull, Roll. Drawings go to the portfolio.
- **Key moment, day 9: a topic box with details.** Ideal instrument: a large chart at the rug beside the real toys, so each detail is a picture and a sentence the teacher can point back to. Spec: fits; paper and a pointer. No tool is used; no gaps or asks.
- **Key moment, day 13: contrasting movements.** Ideal instrument: the children's own bodies and a puppet. Spec: fits; clear floor paths.
- **A room with nothing.** Chart paper from scrap, objects from the room for tiny and gigantic and for soft and hard, a tray with sandpaper, smooth paper and cloth.

## 6. Days

## Day 9


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair I or AC if many missed them on day 2. Gesture "push", "pull", "roll".
- **4 min. The information book.** Show Push, Pull, Roll closed: front cover, back cover, title page; author words, illustrator pictures. "This book tells facts. The poem and the story were different."
- **8 min. First hearing, whole.** The teacher reads gk-u1-03 (205 words, seven pages) once, holding up each picture. The pictures carry the toys.
- **6 min. Talk.** "What is the whole book about?" Partners tell each other; the teacher hears three pairs.
- **12 min. Model the topic box.** The teacher draws a topic box on chart paper ("Toys move") and two detail boxes under it, pointing back to page 2 ("a push moves a toy away") and page 3 ("a pull brings a toy toward you"). A detail alone is not the topic.
- **12 min. Pairs choose details.** Pairs get picture cards from pages 2 to 4; each pair places two cards under the topic box and says the sentence each shows. The teacher hears three pairs.
- **9 min. Draw one detail.** Each child draws one detail under a topic box on paper and tells a partner.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E9.
- **3 min. Opening.** ABC song along the strip, then the chant for t and a.
- **8 min. Keyword and printing a.** Review apple and the sound /a/; print lowercase a, then uppercase A, on handwriting paper (L.K.1a).
- **6 min. Sounds both ways.** Say /a/ and find the letter; see the letter and say the sound. Add the entry to the map in both directions.
- **3 min. Closing.** Chant "a, a, /a/, apple."
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six, five 8-minute turns. Table: a and t, their sounds and a toy's beats for the six; stations: finger a in the sand tray, draw and label, name-card match, big-book browse.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the chart and the book parts; pairs hold the cards. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, placing cards. Evidence: E9; foundations notes for the six.

## Day 10


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair K if many missed E9. Words: color, size, tiny, gigantic (real objects: a bead and a large ball).
- **12 min. Reread Attributes of Toys, pages 1 to 4.** Color words and size words; after each page the teacher asks "What color is it?" and "Is it tiny or gigantic?" and points back to words and picture.
- **8 min. Model opposites.** The teacher shows a tiny and a gigantic classroom object, acts each (small hands, wide arms) and says "The ___ is tiny. The ___ is gigantic." Tiny and gigantic are far apart; big is not as far (error U).
- **12 min. Pairs describe.** Pairs hold two picture cards of one toy, one tiny and one gigantic, and say a sentence for each with a color. The teacher hears three pairs.
- **12 min. Draw.** Draw one tiny and one gigantic toy from the room; color them; tell a partner.
- **7 min. Talk.** "Which words helped you choose?" Children point back to words and picture.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E10.
- **3 min. Opening.** Chant for a and t.
- **10 min. Poem letter search.** The teacher rereads poem p02 in large print; children find a and t in the shared print, lowercase and uppercase, name them and say the sounds (S: no guessing from the picture).
- **4 min. Printing.** Print a and t once on handwriting paper.
- **3 min. Closing.** Chant for a and t.
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six. Table: find a and t in poem lines for the six; stations as day 9.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher models the opposite pair; pairs hold the cards. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, tracing. Evidence: E10; foundations notes for the six.

## Day 11


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair V or T if many missed E10. Words: texture, soft, hard, smooth.
- **10 min. Reread Attributes of Toys, pages 5 to 9.** Two questions: "What does texture mean here?" and "Which words let us sort by texture alone?" Point back to the words.
- **8 min. Model a sort.** Four real toys; the teacher puts a rule card (soft) above one group, sorts, and says the rule. Color is a different rule (error T).
- **10 min. Bridge: sort and match.** In six table groups, children sort six real toys by one attribute and match one group to the other group one for one, saying "one for each"; no totals are counted.
- **14 min. Explain the groups.** Each table says its rule ("These are all ___."); each child draws the two groups on paper with the rule word. The teacher hears the day's six.
- **9 min. Talk.** Which toy did not fit and why? Pairs tell.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E11.
- **3 min. Opening.** Chant for a and t.
- **10 min. Writing for a sound.** Say /a/ or /t/; children choose the letter card, then print it on a strip (errors O, P).
- **4 min. Map.** Find the letter or the keyword on the map, both directions.
- **3 min. Closing.** Chant "a, t, /a/, /t/."
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six. Table: sound to letter and letter to sound for the six; stations as day 9.

Who holds what is carried: the rule card is kept above each group; partners hold the pointing back. Tool: None. By hand: sorting, drawing. Evidence: E11; foundations notes for the six.

## Day 12


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair T if many missed E11. Words: opposite, same.
- **10 min. Model opposites.** The teacher acts open and close, up and down, soft and hard; the class acts each (L.K.5b). On a fluency day this is a brief model of a standard with no earlier teaching day; see field 11.
- **8 min. Shared reading, p03.** The teacher reads Toy Clues in large print, pointing under each line, twice more with the class joining; the picture pairs show the opposites.
- **10 min. Word and object.** Pairs hold word cards (smooth, rough, soft, hard) and find a classroom object for each ("The ___ is rough."); the teacher hears three pairs (L.K.5c).
- **12 min. Reread Attributes of Toys.** "Which attribute opposites occur?" and "Which real toy fits a word you heard?" Point back to the words.
- **11 min. Draw.** Fold a paper; draw one opposite pair, each side labeled by the teacher or with the first letter.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E12.
- **3 min. Opening.** Chant for a and t.
- **10 min. Whole group.** Writing a and t for dictated sounds, then a poem letter search in p03 (a and t, both cases).
- **4 min. Mapped words.** Read I and my in the shared line pointed to.
- **3 min. Closing.** Chant for a and t.
- **40 min. Rotations (cycle 1 assessment).** Five groups of six. At the table the teacher hears each of the six name and sound a and t, print both cases and write a letter for a heard sound, one minute each; notes only, nothing reteached. Stations as day 9.

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the word cards. Tool: None. By hand: acting, pairing, drawing. Evidence: E12; cycle 1 notes for the six, which select the day 13 response.

## Day 13


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair AE or V if many missed E12. Words: riddle, clue, walk, march, tiptoe.
- **10 min. Toy Riddles.** The teacher reads two riddles aloud; after each, "Which clues tell what the toy is?" ("Which clues identify the wooden blocks? How do smooth and bumpy both describe the building bricks?") Point back to the clue words.
- **8 min. Model walk, march, tiptoe.** The teacher says "I walk to the cart. I march with firm steps. I tiptoe quietly past the bear." and acts each; the class acts each in place beside the seat, then tells the difference (firm and rhythmic against light and quiet).
- **12 min. Act and guess.** In pairs, one acts one of the three, the other says which; swap. The teacher hears three pairs. Paths clear, stop for crowding.
- **10 min. Riddle talk.** One new clue is read; children tell which toy fits and why; one question each.
- **11 min. Draw.** Draw a puppet that marches or tiptoes; tell a partner which one and how it moves.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E13.
- **3 min. Opening.** Chant for a and t.
- **10 min. Foundations response.** The most missed of the cycle assessment (a name, a sound, a print or a letter written for a sound): wrong example beside right, then a fresh retry on a new strip; this replaces the usual prompt.
- **4 min. Mapped words.** Read I and my in a dictated sentence ("I march with my toy."), whole words pointed to.
- **3 min. Closing.** Chant for a and t.
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six. Table: the retry for the day's six; stations as day 9.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the movement model; partners hold the clue cards. Tool: None. By hand: acting, drawing. Evidence: E13; retry notes for the six.

## Day 14


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair W if many missed E13. Words: turn, spin, circle, label.
- **8 min. First hearing, whole.** The teacher reads gk-u1-04 (159 words, eight pages) once, holding up each picture. Label page: wheel, handle, box.
- **8 min. Compare.** Show the day 9 topic chart. "Which detail is new beside Push, Pull, Roll?" The teacher adds a detail box: "A top spins in one place."
- **12 min. Reread pages 2 to 5.** "How does its picture explain the movement sentence?" Point to the curved arrows and the straight arrow on page 4.
- **12 min. Pairs add a detail.** Pairs choose one new detail card (a top, a wheel, a steered car), place it under the topic and say its sentence; the teacher hears three pairs.
- **11 min. Draw.** Add a new detail picture to a topic box on paper and tell a partner, with one dictated sentence for the six.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E14.
- **3 min. Opening.** ABC song along the strip.
- **10 min. Letters h and p.** Show the cards, say the names, then the sounds /h/ (a breath on the hand) and /p/ (a puff); keyword pictures hat and parrot; add both to the map; the chant "h, h, /h/, hat; p, p, /p/, parrot."
- **4 min. Chant.** Letter-sound chant for h, p, a and t.
- **3 min. Closing.** The mapped word me, pointed to in "Look at me," as a whole word, not decoded.
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six. Table: name and sound of h and p for the six; stations: finger h and p in the sand tray, draw and label, name-card match, big-book browse.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the chart; pairs hold the detail cards. Tool: None. By hand: placing cards, drawing, tracing. Evidence: E14; foundations notes for the six.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 9, "What is the whole book about?"; day 10, "Which words helped you choose?"; day 13, "Which clues tell what the toy is?"; day 14, "How does its picture explain the movement sentence?".
- Written task, day 14: add a new detail picture to a topic box and dictate one sentence under it. Children do not write words.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) the picture goes under the named topic; (2) the detail is new beside day 9's; (3) the sentence matches the picture. Samples: shown, a top picture with "A top spins in one place"; shown with help, the detail placed after a prompt; not yet seen, the detail named as the whole topic (K).

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Each day opens with the repair of the previous exit's most common error (five minutes, one letter) when more than a third missed it; day 9 repairs I or AC from the earlier book-part work.
- **Foundations frame, once.** Opening, whole-group work, closing, then five 8-minute rotations: the table (six children, one minute each, two minutes for moves) and four stations. Letter prompts use t, a and from day 14 h, p; rhyme and a toy's beats run every day. Mapped words: I, my, and me on day 14.
- **Fluency target.** None; no rate is scored.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: tiny, gigantic, soft, hard, smooth, rough, march, tiptoe, turn. Frames: "The ___ is ___." "A ___ turns." "This group is ___." Home language, signs and acting count as answers; the teacher says the English back.
- **Access.** Large print and a pointer; real objects to feel for texture; a child who cannot march may show firm and light with the hands; a communication aid or pointed card is an answer.
- **Check accommodations.** Every exit is read aloud; a child may point, circle, draw, act or answer privately; a scribe records without supplying ideas. A child who passes is "not yet heard", not missed.
- **Reading load.** None alone. The teacher reads the information books, riddles and poem aloud; the child sees large-print lines and the letters t, a, h, p.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child who is secure adds a second new detail under the topic or a second object for a word.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read each text aloud and ask the same two questions with pointing; keep the topic chart on one page. Sort five objects from around the room (a spoon, a cloth, a block) by one attribute, and act walk, march and tiptoe together.
- **One projector.** Show the book covers, the topic chart and the map on one screen; children answer on cards and point on the screen in turn.
