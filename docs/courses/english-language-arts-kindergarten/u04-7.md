## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- Carried: each child's final-draft card with sketch (kept), the written note, the revised card beside the original (day 147), the partner's question strip, the R1 record and the shared typed caption (a few words, saved on the classroom device and printed). Paint is not kept; cards dry flat overnight.
- Instruments: a classroom device (light), days 147 and 148, a shared typed caption, unscored; judged by whether a child chooses and enters a word. A room with none writes the caption on a paper strip with six children each adding a word.
- Paint: watercolor labeled "Conforms to ASTM D-4236" with no cautionary label, small amounts, supervised (CPSC Pub. 5015 page 5); no food; wipe spills; wash hands. A room without paint uses colored pencil and the same rule: words stay readable.
- Day 146 plan: 15 palettes (one per pair), 15 water cups and cloths set out before the block; 25 minutes of painting; 10 minutes of clean-up: two sinks, about 20 seconds a child, 15 children a sink in about 5 minutes.

## 6. Days

## Day 146


- **5 min. Words return.** readable (words you can still read), wash (a thin layer of paint), brush, palette: picture and gesture, one sense each.
- **10 min. Reread 02a and talk.** 5 min: the teacher rereads 02a pages 3 to 5 aloud. 5 min: paired turn, 'Which benefit goes with your card detail?' (shade or bird shelter); six children share.
- **5 min. Model the wash.** The teacher paints a thin wash around the words on a sample card, leaving the words dry, one color for each part.
- **25 min. Paint the card.** Each child paints the sketch with a thin wash; words stay readable; pairs share a palette. Written feedback follows class (a one-line note each, about 30 minutes).
- **10 min. Clean-up and handwashing.** Tables wiped, brushes in cups, cards flat on the rack; two children at a time to two sinks, about 20 seconds each.
- **5 min. Exit E146.** Card and one ask from field 3.
- **10 min. Foundations response to F5.** 3 min model the commonest of AO or AP, wrong beside right; 3 min table repair; 4 min fresh retry: point to a or i for cave, mail, mine, light; read what, when, then in a new sentence.
- **10 min. Long o and u by ear.** The first lesson: the teacher says home and cube slowly; children hold the card o or u for the vowel that says its name. Four words (home, rope, cube, mule); spoken only, no word read.
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** Five stations with A Pup in a Hut (title adult-read) and verse 24, both first used today; tasks 1 to 6.
- **10 min. Map you, said, were.** Letter tiles: the regular parts (y /y/ and the d in said; w and r in were) and the part to remember (ou, ai, e); read them in 'You said they were in a hut' on the chart.
- **5 min. Check.** The teacher says note and huge (new words); children hold the o or u card; six are marked.

Held by: the teacher for the wash and the exit; the children paint. Carried: the final cards and the notes. Rule 1: one repair in 10 minutes. Tool: None. By hand: paper, watercolor, brushes, cups, palettes, cloths, a and i cards, o and u cards, letter tiles. Evidence: E146; the card goes to written feedback; the foundations check is recorded for six.
