Children still read nothing but short decodable words. Day 100 is a sharing day: pairs compare each statement in the animal piece with the source sentence that supports it, ask each other questions, and add one missing detail; it also holds the T3 and P5 records and the cycle 15 check. Each day is 120 minutes: 60 topic, 60 foundations.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: match, adds, source, detail, question. Frames: "The source says ___. The draft says ___." and "Which card did you use?" Home-language rehearsal with a partner first; then English.
- **Access.** Enlarged, tactile or described cards; a pointed card is an answer. A child who cannot write dictates; dictation does not replace the sound-to-letter act on foundations tasks.
- **Check accommodations.** The cycle 15 words are read aloud twice; the child may mark, point or answer privately; a scribe records without adding ideas. Use an agreed access method rather than a default "not yet seen".
- **Reading load.** Words of taught letters only; the teacher reads every direction and every text.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child who is secure compares a second statement with its source and says whether it matches.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 05a page 3 and one R1 card; the child says one statement, the adult points to a source sentence together with the child, and they say match or adds. For foundations, counters, tiles and the reader; the adult hears the records once.
- **One projector.** Show the source sentence and the draft side by side on one screen; children draw and write on paper.
