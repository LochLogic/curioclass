# Section 1.1: Listening, talking and the sharing story

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-kindergarten/1/s/1.1), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-kindergarten u01-1.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## 1. Standards and practices


Children do not read yet. Everything is heard, pointed to, drawn, acted out or marked on a picture card; the only print a child meets is a name card, a poem or big-book page read aloud and pointed to, and the letter t on day 5. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Records use one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- **SL.K.1 and SL.K.3, this section's share.** "Participate in collaborative conversations ... follow agreed-upon rules ... continue a conversation through multiple exchanges" and "Ask and answer questions in order to seek help, get information, or clarify". Day 1 teaches the four talk rules (look, listen, wait, take a turn) and the question that asks for help; days 3 to 5 practice two-turn exchanges. Day 5 observes both (A3 to A5).
- **RL.K.10 and RI.K.10, "Actively engage in group reading activities".** Day 1 shared poem; days 2 to 5 the story heard whole and returned to. No child decodes any text.
- **RL.K.5, RL.K.6 and RI.K.5, RI.K.6 (day 2).** "Recognize common types of texts" (a poem on day 1, a storybook on day 2); "name the author and illustrator ... define the role of each"; "Identify the front cover, back cover, and title page". Day 9 repeats all of it on an information book.
- **RL.K.1, RL.K.7, SL.K.2 (days 2 to 5).** "With prompting and support, ask and answer questions about key details"; "describe the relationship between illustrations and the story". RI.K.1 and RI.K.7 have no information text before day 9: the same question and picture routines run on the story, and the information versions are first taught on days 10 and 11.
- **Foundations (cycle 1, days 1 to 5).** Words and names (RF.K.1b), print direction and spaces (RF.K.1a, 1c), straight and curved lines, then the letter t: name, sound, upper and lower case, printed (RF.K.1d, RF.K.3a, L.K.1a, L.K.2c). I and my are met as whole words (RF.K.3c). Rhyme and syllables are oral practice from day 1 (RF.K.2a, 2b).
- **Limits that bite here.** Classroom toys only: no child is asked what they own, shares about home, or stands as the example of a mistake; wrong examples are voiced by a puppet. A child may pass. Onset and rime (RF.K.2c) is taught from cycle 5, not here. Only t is a taught letter by day 5.
- **Connections.** SL.K.1 and SL.K.3 are used in social studies from school day 3 (one shared talk-rules poster). W.K.2 starts on day 6.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A question asks; a statement tells (why a statement given to a question is not wrong, only not yet a question). That the word we hear is the word we see, and every letter is a part of that word. That a keyword picture recalls a sound and is never used to guess a word. That "I heard" and "This shows" are sentence starters faded by day 24.

## 2. Where it sits


Section 1.1 opens the year and the unit; there is no readiness check (s01 field 4). The notes taken on days 1 to 4 (a child's name, a letter, a sound, how they like to answer) set the order of the five foundations groups. Day 5's A1 to A6 go to the record sheet and select the repairs of day 8; day 6 opens with a five-minute repair of any exit error.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** A1 to A6 (day 5; the items are in the skeleton table above and in s01 field 5). Days 1 to 4 are practice only.

**Exit questions.** Five minutes at the end of the topic block. All cards are invented for practice and say so. Item (a) is answered by every child on paper (circle the dots or the picture); item (b) is asked aloud of the day's rotating six. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". One ask in each item. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E1 (day 1; SL.K.1, SL.K.3).** Read: "Lu cannot get the lid off the paint jar. Dan sits at the same table." (a) "Listen to three things Lu could say. Circle the dots for the one that asks Dan for help." One dot: "This lid is stuck." Two dots: "Dan, can you help me open it?" Three dots: "I do not like paint." Answer: two dots. Wrong: one dot (E); three dots (A). (b) "Dan tells Lu how to twist the lid. What does Lu do while Dan talks?" Answer: listens, waits, looks at Dan. Wrong: "I say my idea too" (C).
- **E2 (day 2; RL.K.6, RI.K.5).** Push, Pull, Roll, a unit book not yet heard: show its front cover, page 2 and back cover, then read the two credit lines on its title page. (a) "Circle the back cover" (front cover, page 2, back cover). Answer: back cover. Wrong: page 2 (AC). (b) "Who made the pictures in this book?" Answer: the Curio Class art team, the illustrator. Wrong: the writing team (I). RL.K.5 is heard in talk on day 2 and first checked in the day 28 battery.
- **E3 (day 3; RL.K.1, SL.K.2).** Read page 7 of One Hoop, Two Friends again. (a) "Circle how the friends will share the hoop" (counting to ten; pulling the hoop between them; Noor walking off with it). Answer: counting to ten. Wrong: pulling the hoop (A). (b) "Ask me one question about what Teo says on this page." Answer: "What does Teo say?" or "Is it Teo's turn next?" Wrong: "Teo is happy" (E).
- **E4 (day 4; RL.K.7, SL.K.2).** Read page 2 of One Hoop, Two Friends again, its picture covered. (a) "Circle the picture that goes with the last sentence" (Noor running after the rolling hoop; the two friends at the basket; Teo asking for a turn). Answer: Noor running after the hoop. Wrong: the two friends at the basket (B). (b) "What could you ask if one word on this page was new to you?" Answer: "What does edge mean?" Wrong: "I do not know that word" (E).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The talk rules (discussion-protocol): the teacher models them on day 1 and holds the poster through day 3; partners hold the turn card from day 3; day 5 observes them with no prompt. Pointing back to the words and the picture that tell: modeled by the teacher on days 3 and 4, tried by partners on day 4, then returned to in 1.2 and 1.3. The sound-spelling-map (a class wall chart: letter, keyword picture, sound): hung empty on day 3 with its use said aloud, filled with t and its keyword on day 4, kept through the unit. The story One Hoop, Two Friends is the shared case for days 2 to 4, then reread in 1.2.
- **Key moment, day 4: a picture matched to the words.** Ideal instrument: large print beside a real toy, the story page, and two picture cards to hold against the reread sentence. Spec: fits; paper and a pointer. No tool is used; no gaps or asks.
- **A room with nothing.** Paper hoop and basket from scrap; names on torn paper; a drawn chart for the map; two real classroom toys. The poem and story are read from large-print pages or copied by hand.

## 6. Days

## Day 1


- **5 min. Hook and first words.** Two large classroom toys on the rug (no detachable parts). Name each with the children; show "toy" and "turn" with gestures.
- **12 min. Poem p01, Our Play, beside the toys.** Large print, teacher reads it once pointing under each line, then twice with the class joining the repeated line, holding up a toy at "roll it, spin it". Ask "What does the poem invite us to do?" (come and play) and "Which words tell whose turn comes next?" (you take a turn, then I take mine).
- **10 min. Model listening and taking turns.** The teacher and a puppet show listening and not listening (the puppet speaks one line at a time and talks over once). Post four rules: look, listen, wait, take a turn. Children do each move. One poster for ELA and social studies.
- **8 min. Ask for help.** The teacher cannot find where the toy cart lives and says it two ways: "I cannot find the place" and "Where does the cart go, please?" Children say the question back and ask a partner one question about a toy on the rug.
- **10 min. Turn and talk.** Pairs of neighbors, two exchanges: "Which toy would you like to play with here?", answer, swap, one thing said back. The teacher hears three pairs. No home toys.
- **10 min. Draw a toy from our room.** Each child draws one classroom toy; the teacher writes its word on request, and the rest are labeled in the foundations stations.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E1.
- **5 min. Word game: What's the word?** Show an object or an action (ball, cart, roll, push); the class says the one word.
- **8 min. Name game: the word we heard, the word we see.** Name cards face down; up to six volunteers say their name, the card is turned, "We heard it, now we see it, and it looks the same every time." Names stay in the room.
- **4 min. Words on paper.** The teacher writes two toy words and one action word the class said, left to right, finger under each.
- **3 min. Closing.** "Where can you find a written word in our room?"
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six, five 8-minute turns. Table: the six say a toy word, clap its beats, find the rhyme in "play, today"; stations: draw and label, name-card match, big-book copy browse, finger lines in the sand tray.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the rules and the question; each pair holds a turn card. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, drawing, circling dots. Evidence: E1; the record sheet notes (practice only).

## Day 2


- **5 min. Reopen and words.** If many missed E1, repair E or C (five minutes, one of them). Gesture "share", "ask", "answer".
- **3 min. The closed book.** Show One Hoop, Two Friends: front cover, back cover, spine. "This is a storybook; yesterday's was a poem."
- **12 min. First hearing, whole.** The teacher reads gk-u1-01 (nine pages) once without stopping, holding up each picture. Noor and Teo are invented; say so. Content note: a child waits and feels left out; no injury, death, violence or home life.
- **5 min. Talk.** "What does the title name?" (a hoop, two friends) and "Which toy did they have?" Partners tell each other one thing.
- **10 min. Title page and covers.** Pairs share a paper booklet of the story: find the front cover, back cover and title page, hold up, name each.
- **8 min. Who made it.** Author (words) and illustrator (pictures): two label cards under a pencil picture and a brush picture; point to the credit labels the teacher reads aloud.
- **12 min. Draw one thing from the story.** Draw, then tell a partner what the picture shows, pointing to its part.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E2.
- **6 min. The word we heard, again.** Name cards: the finger moves under the whole name, left to right; "a name is one word made of letters."
- **11 min. Letters and words.** Pairs sort name-card letter tiles and whole name cards: "one word, many letters." Finger under each space-bounded word of big-book page 1 ("We have blocks."), left to right, top to bottom; the teacher covers the picture (error S).
- **3 min. Closing.** "Show me one word and one letter."
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six. Table: rhyme in p01 and the beats of a toy name for the six; stations as day 1.

Who holds what is carried: teacher and pairs hold the book parts; the teacher holds the maker labels. Tool: None. By hand: handling a booklet, sorting labels, drawing, circling the back cover. Evidence: E2; foundations notes for the six.

## Day 3


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair I or AC if many missed E2 (four minutes, in place of the words). Gesture "turn", "wait".
- **14 min. Return to pages 3 to 6.** Read each page again. After page 3 the teacher asks "What does Teo ask?", then points back: finds the words and the picture that tell. Same for pages 4 to 6 (Noor's reply, what Teo does while waiting).
- **8 min. Model asking and answering.** The teacher asks a question about page 5 (Who stopped smiling?) and answers by pointing; then a child asks one and the class answers. Starters: Who? What?
- **12 min. Partner questions.** Pairs with picture cards of pages 3 to 6: one asks, the other answers by pointing and saying, then swap. A two-turn exchange (question, answer, a related question, answer) is modeled first. The teacher hears three pairs.
- **10 min. Question and answer cards.** Four spoken questions; children point to the picture card with the answer and say it ("Teo asks for a turn").
- **7 min. Talk rules check.** Which rule did Teo follow? Which did Noor forget? Partners tell.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E3.
- **4 min. The word I.** Find the one-letter word "I" in the shared print of poem p01 ("then I take mine"); it is a word and a letter at once (mapped word, read as a whole).
- **7 min. Skywriting.** Straight lines and curved lines in the air and on backs, large arm motions, then fingers on the table (no letter names yet).
- **6 min. Handwriting paper.** Top line, middle line, bottom line; trace two straight and two curved lines with a crayon, left to right and top down.
- **3 min. Closing and the map.** Hang the empty sound-spelling-map; say how it will work: say the keyword, say the sound, point to the letter.
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six. Table: a toy's beats and the mapped word I for the six; stations: line making in the sand tray, draw and label, name-card match, big-book copy browse.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher models pointing back; pairs hold the picture cards. Tool: None. By hand: pointing, matching cards, tracing lines. Evidence: E3; notes for the six.

## Day 4


- **4 min. Reopen and words.** Repair D or E if many missed E3 (four minutes, in place of the words). Words: picture, moment, "What does ___ mean?".
- **12 min. One page, two pictures.** Read page 4 aloud with the picture covered, then show two pictures (page 4 and page 6). "Which picture fits these words? Which words tell?" Point back to the words, say why the other picture does not fit (error B).
- **10 min. Ask what a word means.** Model: "I do not know scuffed. I ask, What does scuffed mean?" A child acts it. Pairs choose a word card (scuffed, wobble, tight) and ask the partner what it means; the teacher hears three pairs.
- **14 min. Match a different moment.** Pairs get four picture cards from the story; the teacher reads a different sentence (page 8: "Teo's whole face lit up") and the pair points to the matching picture and says why.
- **11 min. Draw the moment.** Reread page 9; each child draws one moment from it and tells a partner which words it shows.
- **4 min. Reflect.** "How does a picture help the words?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E4.
- **5 min. Showing the sound t.** Say /t/ with a tongue tap, act it (tap, tap); no letter story is read, only the sound and its action.
- **8 min. The letter t and its keyword.** Show the card t with the keyword picture top; say the name "tee", then the sound /t/ (error N). Add the entry to the sound-spelling-map. The keyword picture recalls the sound; it never guesses a word.
- **4 min. Lines for t.** Straight down, then across, in the air, on a partner's back and on paper.
- **3 min. Closing.** Chant: "t, t, /t/, top."
- **40 min. Rotations.** Five groups of six. Table: letter t for the six (name, sound, find t on the map); stations: finger t in the sand tray, draw and label, name-card match, big-book copy browse.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the pointing back; the class holds the map entry. Tool: None. By hand: matching, drawing, tracing. Evidence: E4; notes for the six.

## Day 5


- **6 min. Set-up.** Read Folio A (The Puppet, invented for practice) once to the whole class with its three pictures; explain the groups; children not at the table draw two children sharing a toy (invented, not from home) at a seat.
- **48 min. Six groups of five, 8 minutes each.** A1 2, A2 1, A5 3, A6 2 minutes at the table; A3 and A4 observed throughout. The groups come in the same order as day 1's six, never named as missed. Pencils down, no reteach. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
- **6 min. Closing.** "Tell one thing about asking for help"; collect drawings. No exit ticket (check day).
- **3 min. ABC song.** Sing it once, pointing along an alphabet strip, left to right.
- **5 min. Keyword t.** Review the keyword picture and the sound; the map entry is read aloud.
- **8 min. Printing t and T.** Down, then across; lowercase, then uppercase, on handwriting paper (L.K.1a).
- **2 min. The word my.** Shown as a whole word on drawing labels the teacher wrote ("my toy"); read together, not decoded.
- **2 min. Closing.** Chant for t.
- **40 min. Rotations.** Familiar practice only, no reteaching: the table hears the day's six write t and T and say /t/ (O, P); stations as day 3.

Who holds what is carried: no one prompts; the poster stays up. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, writing t, speaking. Evidence: A1 to A6 on the record sheet; t and T notes for the six.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 1, "What does the poem invite us to do?"; day 3, "What does each friend ask?"; day 4, "Which words tell this moment?".
- Written task, day 5: print t and T on handwriting paper. Children do not write words; the teacher writes every label a child asks for.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) starts at the top and goes down; (2) the cross goes left to right at the middle line; (3) says /t/ while writing it. Samples: shown, a clear t with its cross and the sound said; not yet seen, a vertical line with no cross, or "tee" for the sound (N).

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 1 none. Days 2 to 4 open with the previous exit's repair (error letter, five minutes) when more than a third missed it. Day 5 no repair (check day).
- **Foundations frame, once.** Opening, whole-group work, closing, then five 8-minute rotations: the table (six children, one minute each, two minutes for moves) and four stations. Five groups of six; the table's six is the day's rotating group on the record sheet. Rhyme and a toy's beats run from day 1 on poem and picture cards; letter prompts begin with t on day 5. Mapped words: I (day 3), my (day 5).
- **Fluency target.** None; no rate is scored.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: toy, turn, share, ask, answer, title. Frames: "I ask ___." "You answer ___." "My toy is the ___." Home language, signs and pointing count as answers; the teacher says the English back.
- **Access.** Large print and a pointer for every page; a tactile letter t; a child who cannot sit still may stand at the rug edge; a communication aid or pointed card is an answer.
- **Check accommodations.** Every exit is read aloud; a child may point, circle, draw or answer privately; a scribe records without supplying ideas. A child who passes is "not yet heard", not missed.
- **Reading load.** None alone. The teacher reads the poem, story and cards aloud; the child sees a name card, the poem in large print and the letter t.
- **Extension, same standard.** Ask a child who is secure for a second question about the same page.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the poem aloud, look at two toys and ask which one to play with; read the story pages aloud over days 2 to 4, ask one question and let the child answer by pointing. Day 5: read the short puppet story and ask the same three questions.
- **One projector.** Show the poem, the story pages and the sound-spelling-map on one screen; children answer on printed cards and point on the screen only in turn.
