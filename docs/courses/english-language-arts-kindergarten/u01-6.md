# Section 1.6: Ask a classmate: interview and toy portrait

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Kindergarten. Unit 1, Toys and play. *What makes a toy fun, and how do we share it?* (days 1 to 34): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 26 to 30.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (26), application 2 (27, 30), check 2 (28, 29). Children do not read these texts. Stories and information pages are heard with their pictures; answers are said, pointed to, marked, drawn or labelled with taught letters (a t h p n c, plus m and r only as practice). Each day is 60 minutes of topic and 60 of foundations.

- **Shares.** SL.K.3, "ask and answer questions in order to seek help, get information, or clarify something that is not understood": taught day 26, observed days 26 to 29 as interviews. SL.K.1a and b, "follow agreed-upon rules" and "continue a conversation through multiple exchanges": the same days. W.K.2, "name what they are writing about and supply some information about the topic": taught on day 26 (joint portrait), drafted day 27, revised day 29, new unscored edition day 30. RI.K.2, "identify the main topic and retell key details": 03 and 04 on days 26, 27 and 30, scored in section 1.7 (day 31). RL.K.5, RL.K.6, RI.K.5, RI.K.6: practised only (battery, days 28 to 30). Foundations (RF.K.1a to d, RF.K.2a to c, RF.K.3a and c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c): cycle 3, n and c, ends on day 26; cycle 4, m and r, starts on day 28 as instruction only.
- **Limits that bite here.** Interviews are about classroom toys only; no home toy, no family and no child's own belongings; a child may say "pass". Names stay in the room. One actor a scene: the teacher and a sock puppet, Pip, speak one line at a time. The word "turn": in 04 it means spin or change direction; "take turns" and "turn and talk" are the talk routine; the interview item may ask "What do you mean by turn?" so the day 27 reading says which sense. Practice words for rhyme, syllables and onset and rime stay off the held-back list (pat, tap, hat, bat, pal, mat, cat, puppet, wagon, sun, mop, cup, stop, wet, dry, shiny, hop, creep, stomp, loud, quiet, long, short, rattle, bounce, spring, gallop, glide, full, empty, fox, box, fish, log) except box and fox, which the day 30 exit uses.
- **Connections.** Discussion-protocol and the routine "Read, Point, Talk, Draw" carry from section 1.5; main-idea-details returns on 04 (day 27).
- **Teacher page explains.** The interview is a game of asking a real question to get a fact you do not have; a statement is not a question; the portrait is scored for the named topic and the information separately; the battery pacing is about one child at a time, not speed.
