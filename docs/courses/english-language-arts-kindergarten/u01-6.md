## 1. Standards and practices


Children do not read these texts. Stories and information pages are heard with their pictures; answers are said, pointed to, marked, drawn or labeled with taught letters (a t h p n c, plus m and r only as practice). Each day is 60 minutes of topic and 60 of foundations.

- **Shares.** SL.K.3, "ask and answer questions in order to seek help, get information, or clarify something that is not understood": taught day 26, observed days 26 to 29 as interviews. SL.K.1a and b, "follow agreed-upon rules" and "continue a conversation through multiple exchanges": the same days. W.K.2, "name what they are writing about and supply some information about the topic": taught on day 26 (joint portrait), drafted day 27, revised day 29, new unscored edition day 30. RI.K.2, "identify the main topic and retell key details": 03 and 04 on days 26, 27 and 30, scored in section 1.7 (day 31). RL.K.5, RL.K.6, RI.K.5, RI.K.6: practiced only (battery, days 28 to 30). Foundations (RF.K.1a to d, RF.K.2a to c, RF.K.3a and c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c): cycle 3, n and c, ends on day 26; cycle 4, m and r, starts on day 28 as instruction only.
- **Limits that bite here.** Interviews are about classroom toys only; no home toy, no family and no child's own belongings; a child may say "pass". Names stay in the room. One actor a scene: the teacher and a sock puppet, Pip, speak one line at a time. The word "turn": in 04 it means spin or change direction; "take turns" and "turn and talk" are the talk routine; the interview item may ask "What do you mean by turn?" so the day 27 reading says which sense. Practice words for rhyme, syllables and onset and rime stay off the held-back list (pat, tap, hat, bat, pal, mat, cat, puppet, wagon, sun, mop, cup, stop, wet, dry, shiny, hop, creep, stomp, loud, quiet, long, short, rattle, bounce, spring, gallop, glide, full, empty, fox, box, fish, log) except box and fox, which the day 30 exit uses.
- **Connections.** Discussion-protocol and the routine "Read, Point, Talk, Draw" carry from section 1.5; main-idea-details returns on 04 (day 27).
- **Teacher page explains.** The interview is a game of asking a real question to get a fact you do not have; a statement is not a question; the portrait is scored for the named topic and the information separately; the battery pacing is about one child at a time, not speed.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned (field 5 of the unit plan):** I1, I2, I3 (days 28 and 29, SL.K.3, SL.K.1a, SL.K.1b); the battery components for letter names, track, letter sequence, spaces, formation, sound writing, consonant sounds, rhyme, syllables and mapped words on days 28 to 31 (RF.K.1a to d, L.K.1a, L.K.2c, RF.K.3a, RF.K.2a, RF.K.2b, RF.K.3c); P1 on day 30 (W.K.2); T1 on day 31 (RI.K.2); C5 on day 32 (RF.K.2c). The rows are in the table above; the book-kind, story-maker, book-part and information-maker components belong to section 1.5. Days 28 and 29 are checks: no exit ticket.

**Exit questions (days 26, 27 and 30).** Cards are invented for practice and the teacher says so. The adult says the card once, repeats it on request, then shows the picture choices. Every child marks a picture; the spoken part is heard from a rotating six (a column names who was heard; one not heard is "not yet seen"). Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Wrong answers are tagged with one letter.
- **E26 (SL.K.1, SL.K.3, W.K.2).** Puppet Pip says: "My pick is the xylophone. I like how it chimes." Mark the picture of Pip's pick (xylophone, toy telephone, tricycle). Spoken: "What could you ask before you label it?" Answer: the xylophone; "What do you mean by chimes?" or "Can you show me how it chimes?" Wrong: marks the toy telephone (B); "I like the xylophone too" (E); "I have a xylophone at home" (A).
- **E27 (SL.K.1, W.K.2, RI.K.2).** Card: "The red bead is round. The blue bead is round too. The green bead is round." Pip says: "I pick the blue one." Mark the labeled picture that tells the page's topic and Pip's pick. Spoken: "What fact will you tell for the label?" Answer: the blue bead, labeled beads; "It is round." Wrong: marks the red bead (B); a label "blue" for the topic (K); "beads" and no fact (M); "I like it" (X).
- **E30 (W.K.2, RF.K.2a), unscored.** Card: "The toy fox sits in its box. Its tail is soft." Mark the labeled drawing that teaches a fact about the toy. Spoken: "Say a word that rhymes with box." Answer: fox with a soft tail, labeled; fox or another correct rhyme. Wrong: a drawing labeled only "I like it" (X); fish, because of the first sound (Q).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Discussion-protocol and "Read, Point, Talk, Draw": the teacher holds the model on day 26 and steps back on day 27; partners hold the interview from day 27. Sound-spelling-map: n and c (review), m and r added on day 28; it stays on the wall. Main-idea-details: 03 and 04, held by the teacher on day 27 and by partners on day 30. The new routine "Ask a classmate" is the interview: a question for a fact, a clarifying question for a word, then a follow-up.
- **Key moment 1, days 26 to 29: the interview.** Ideal instrument: a record sheet, one row a child, a mark for each exchange. Spec: fits, hand-ruled; no tool. Count: a group of five takes a chain, each child asking once and answering once in about 3 minutes, then a second round of about 3 minutes with a follow-up; fifteen children are seen in three six-minute groups, 18 minutes.
- **Key moment 2, days 28 to 31: the battery.** Ideal instrument: one component record a child. Spec: fits, hand-ruled. Count: at most 4 minutes a child, so 32 minutes for eight, 28 for seven; the other children rotate through four stations of 8 minutes (7 minutes on day 30). A component not reached stays blank.
- **Foundations, days 26 and 27.** Rotation as in 1.5, five turns of six children; the day 26 teacher-table turn is the cycle 3 check, 50 seconds a child.
- **A room with nothing.** Draw the toys on scrap paper, use a sock as Pip, a chalk line or a finger in a tray for letters.

## 6. Days

## Day 26


- **5 min. Reopen and repair.** E25 errors (I, J, AC): the credit lines, poem and back cover of three books beside their right answers.
- **5 min. Words first.** interview, question, prefer, with the puppet asking the class.
- **10 min. Return and read.** Mira Makes a Road (02): one page, "which picture goes with these words?" (3 minutes). Then Push, Pull, Roll (03) whole, about 2 minutes plus pictures, and the two questions: "Which story detail does its illustration show?" "Which movement fact should an interviewer check?"
- **10 min. Model the interview.** The teacher asks Pip three questions on a classroom bus: "Which toy do you pick?" "What can it do?" "What do you mean by zoom?" Pip answers. The teacher draws and labels the bus.
- **12 min. Practice.** Fifteen pairs at tables with real shelf toys, 5 minutes each way, 2 minutes to change; the teacher hears the rotating six (three pairs, 2 minutes a pair).
- **8 min. Joint portrait.** The class helps compose one portrait of Pip's pick: choose the picture, label it, find one fact on a page of 03 and point to it; the teacher writes "Pip picks the bus. A bus has wheels." and the class points along (RF.K.1).
- **5 min. Draw.** Each child draws a partner's pick on a half sheet; labels continue tomorrow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E26.
- **10 min. Oral work.** Rhyme and syllables with toy words off the held-back list.
- **10 min. Letters n and c.** Cycle 3 lesson 20: name N n C c and their sounds, map both ways; the (review, mapped on day 21).
- **10 min. Formation and mapped words.** Print N n C c; read the and a in a list and in the poem line.
- **25 min. Rotation.** Five turns of 5 minutes; the teacher-table turn is the cycle 3 check (names and sounds of n and c).
- **5 min. Check and feedback.** Write n and c for two dictated sounds on a card, collected. Response is the first 10 minutes of day 27's foundations.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the interview model; pairs hold the practice. Tool: None. By hand: asking, drawing, labelling, the exit mark. Evidence: E26; the record sheet; the cycle 3 check.

## Day 27


- **5 min. Reopen and repair.** E26 (A, B, E): questions beside statements, the right picture beside the wrong.
- **3 min. Words first.** turn (spin or change direction, not "take turns"), spin, label.
- **12 min. Hear 04 and name the topic.** A Toy Can Turn whole (159 words, about 1.5 minutes plus pictures), then topic and details on the chart: topic, toys that turn; details, a top spins, a cart's wheels spin. "What is its topic? Which detail could a partner confirm about a toy?"
- **13 min. Rehearse the exchanges.** New partners, 5 minutes each way and 3 to change; the teacher hears the rotating six.
- **17 min. Draft the portrait.** Draw the new partner's toy (8 minutes); label with taught letters and dictate one fact from 03, 04 or the interview, the teacher scribing (9 minutes: the rotating six, 1.5 minutes a pair; others label). Written feedback is attached after class (30 teacher minutes).
- **5 min. Read, Point, Talk, Draw.** Show the portrait to a partner and point to the page that holds its fact.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E27.
- **10 min. Response and fresh retry.** The day 26 cycle 3 check: small groups by missed component, fresh retry of that component.
- **5 min. Oral work.** Rhyme and syllables.
- **5 min. Onset and rime.** Blend and segment d-oll, k-ite and t-ent (none held back); six children are heard.
- **10 min. Reread.** Already taught poem lines, pointing; find n and c.
- **25 min. Rotation.** Five turns of 5 minutes.
- **5 min. Check and feedback.** Print one capital and one small letter from memory.

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the exchange; the teacher holds the topic chart. Tool: None. By hand: asking, drawing, dictating, the exit mark. Evidence: E27; the portrait for P1.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 26, "Which story detail does its illustration show? Which movement fact should an interviewer check?"; day 27, "What is its topic? Which detail could a partner confirm about a toy?"; day 30, "Which fact matches this labelled picture? Which second detail would help its reader?"
- Written task: the informative toy portrait: a drawing of a classmate's pick with a label, and a sentence (dictated; labels in the child's own letters): "Name your partner's classroom toy preference and teach a reader something about that toy." Drafted day 27, revised day 29, scored day 30 (P1); the day 30 edition is unscored.
- Look-fors: (1) it names the toy; (2) it is a classmate's pick, not the child's; (3) it gives a fact from 03, 04 or the interview; (4) the drawing matches. Topic and information are scored separately: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: interview, question, prefer, turn, spin, label. Frames: "What do you mean by ___?" "Can you show me?" "My pick is ___." Home language, gesture or a pointed card is an answer.
- **Access.** Enlarge and describe each picture; tactile letter cards; a communication aid or a pointed card is an answer; a child may say "pass"; interview seating faces the speaker.
- **Check accommodations.** The prompt is said twice; pointing counts; a scribe may write the label; a quiet corner for the interview; an unfinished battery component stays blank.
- **Reading load.** No child reads a text. Read aloud: Push, Pull, Roll (205 words), A Toy Can Turn (159 words), one story page and a poem; at most 160 heard words at once, each with a picture.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second fact to the portrait and point to where it was found.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** At the table: you are Pip and the child asks the questions; the child draws what you pick and you label it; ask for a fact before the label. Read 03 and 04 once each; the battery is four minutes of the same prompts, one at a time. Day 30: the child makes a new portrait of your pick.
- **One projector.** Show one page at a time, large; children mark on printed cards; a screen never replaces a child's own mark or spoken answer.
