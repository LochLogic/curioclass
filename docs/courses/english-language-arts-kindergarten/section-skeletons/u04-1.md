> **Text commissions.** These are specifications for original manuscripts, not reports of existing books. Write 01a as a whole short story about Lina, who enjoys a young tree beside her apartment building, sees dry soil, plans to water with an adult, and changes that plan when rain arrives. Neighbors enjoy the tree with her. Write 01b as a distinct whole story about Ben reading under a school tree, moving when nearby work grows noisy, and finding a quieter shaded place with classmates. Include two reserved scene pairs (scenes kept out of teaching): Lina invites a neighbor to smell blossoms, Ben invites a classmate to feel bark; in the endings Lina gives that neighbor a fallen leaf beside the apartment tree, Ben gives that classmate a drawing of the trunk beside the school tree.
