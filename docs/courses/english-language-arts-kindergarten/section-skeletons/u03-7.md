>
> Provide six original cycle readers, 20 to 80 words each, one sentence per page: Ants at a Mat (short a), A Pig in a Pit (short i), Gus in a Tub (short u), A Log in a Pond (short o), A Hen for Meg (short e), and A Pet at Home (mixed vowels; title adult-read). Do not infer a digraph from the picture.
>
> Tree Words supplies seven complete short verses for shared foundations reading, first heard on days 80, 85, 91, 96, 102, 107 and 112; on assessment dates the adult reads without introducing a new decoding pattern. Commission a particular tree-related action sequence, two rhyming words and one two-syllable referent per verse. Use them for group reading with purpose and understanding and for print retrieval. Never treat the measured demand of an adult-read verse as independent decoding difficulty.
>
> On days outside the six F1 dates, use six text-dependent assignments and two isolated assignments within sixty minutes. From day 85, the first three use the current reader; before then use the adult-read verse. The remaining three use shared verse print:
> 1. Read or listen to find the particular actor and action answering the stated purpose, then point to its words.
> 2. Reread to distinguish two plausible accounts of what happens in the actual selection.
> 3. Match its stated event or fact to a picture and explain the text link.
> 4. Locate the verse's exact spoken opening in print and track its separate words, line and page.
> 5. Identify the two actual rhyming words in the verse; show where their sounds agree. On alternating days identify the two-syllable object from its description and clap its name instead.
> 6. Point to the verse line supporting the partner's description, then add another actual line detail.
> 7. Without print, perform the day's oral sound task. Rotate rhyme production, syllable blending and segmentation, onset-rime blending and segmentation, isolation of three phonemes and a one-sound addition or substitution. These count against text dependence even when words came from a reading.
> 8. Read one mapped word in isolation and write its taught spelling or one current sound-spelling. Count this isolated assignment against. On pattern-check days replace it with the current letter-sound and spelling probe, preserving the same task count; separate extra probes must be added to the denominator.
>
> The two isolated assignments are explicitly instructed, jointly tried, then practised independently. Model CVC blending on day 85 and chaining on day 89, practise day 91; model short-vowel spelling day 89 and practise day 94.
