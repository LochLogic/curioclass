Write 01a as a whole short story about Lina, who enjoys a young tree beside her apartment building, sees dry soil, plans to water with an adult, and changes that plan when rain arrives. > (shared across the unit) Use Read, Point, Talk, Draw most days, formally twice weekly: reread the hard line, point, exchange questions and replies, then draw and dictate. Judge accurate source use separately from opinion preference. Use identical rows at home.
<!-- /skel -->

### U8. Practice that returns

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Retrieve yesterday's mapping and a prior text word. Reread for accurate meaning; no speed target. Return to opinion topic and preference throughout card writing.
<!-- /skel -->

### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Preview words with gestures; allow home-language rehearsal, enlarged print, described pictures and dictation. Preserve independent sound-letter work when judging spelling. Extend by comparing a second source-supported experience, using the same standards.
<!-- /skel -->

### U10. Two rooms

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) At home the parent reads, partners and records the same evidence in shorter sessions. One shared classroom device suffices. Keep cards and recordings within the learning setting; no accounts or uploads.
<!-- /skel -->
