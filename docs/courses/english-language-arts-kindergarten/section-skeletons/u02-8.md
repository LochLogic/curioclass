> On each of the 37 days outside the five P1 dates, use these nine separate practice prompts in foundations:
> 1. Locate the day's verse's opening word, track its line and next page, then name the initial letter in both cases.
> 2. Hear a named word from its line; choose the exact printed letter sequence and explain the speech-to-print match.
> 3. Locate the space between two named verse words and frame the separate words.
> 4. Name the person or object answering a clue from the heard verse, then isolate its initial, medial and final sounds where it is a permitted three-phoneme word.
> 5. Find the two words that rhyme in the heard verse and produce another rhyme.
> 6. Identify the two-syllable object described in the verse; count and pronounce its syllables, blend its teacher-spoken syllables, then segment them.
> 7. Identify two named one-syllable verse words and blend and segment their onset and rime, then add or substitute the specified sound to find another word actually used in the verse.
> 8. Read mapped words in isolation and the actual shared line, then indicate the detail that line conveys.
> 9. Write a letter for a dictated taught sound, without text.
