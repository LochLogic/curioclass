> On each of the 37 days outside the five P1 dates, use these nine separate practice prompts in foundations:

> 3. Locate the space between two named verse words and frame the separate words.
> 4. Name the person or object answering a clue from the heard verse, then isolate its initial, medial and final sounds where it is a permitted three-phoneme word.

> 9. Write a letter for a dictated taught sound, without text.
