# Section 3.7: Research talks about the tree

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of each code.** W.K.7 "Participate in shared research and writing projects": the research talks (R2), each child bringing the one part they own (a question, a source-labeled note, a picture). W.K.8 "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": the child's own source-labeled notes from 3.6 and the readings 05a, 05b, 06 and 03, always with an adult. RI.K.3 "describe the connection between two ... pieces of information in a text": a need and its dependent joined by an arrow and so or because, said in the talk. RF.K.3b, 3d, 3, L.K.2d, RF.K.4, RF.K.2d, 2e (cycle 17, short e) and RF.K.3c (be, by): short e for its short sound only; a one-letter change with the changed sound named; spelling by sounds; reading the cycle reader for what happened; middle sound and add or swap one sound; be and by mapped as words to remember.
- **Limits that bite here.** The talks are observation days (107, 109, 110): each child is observed once for each missing part and the R1 and C1 records are retained, never redone (zero new minutes). Six groups of five across the three talk days, at most ten minutes a group; the rest follow the day's reading and drawing. Two facts at a time; adult-provided sources only; drawing and dictation accepted. Short e only. Reserved for F1 (days 110 to 113, 115, 116): the word pair pen and pin, the spelling words bed and sun, and the F1 decodable; none appears in teaching or exits here.
- **Connections.** RI.K.3 with W.K.7 and W.K.8: the talk asks the child to say a need, a dependent and the arrow, with the source label. RF.K.3d with L.K.2d: the one-letter chain (ten, tan, tin) names the changed sound. RF.K.3c: be and by, with the regular and remembered parts marked.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) What a research talk is at five: three turns, "my question", "my line from the card", "so ...", about two minutes a child. (2) How the six groups of five are chosen and rotated across the three talk days so that no child is observed twice for the same part. (3) A need is something the tree takes in; a dependent is a living thing that gets food or shelter from it. (4) Why 109 and 110 reuse records: the day 97 to 99 and 86 to 87 records already hold the evidence, so the talk fills only a missing part. (5) The short e lesson names the sound of e in a word, never the letter name in a sound task.
- **One word, one sense.** check means look at the source again, never a test. support means back up a note with a source line.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** days 109 and 110 W.K.7, W.K.8 and RI.K.3 (retained R1 and C1 records, no new item); day 110 RF.K.3d (F1 item: read the pair, name the changed letter and its sound), L.K.2d (retained F1 spelling record) and RF.K.4 (F1 fresh decodable: say what it tells and point to its words); day 111 repeats the three F1 rows for the next roster five (owned by 3.8's roster). The F1 decodable and the pair are held back by the unit plan; this section does not write or use them. Day 110 serves roster children 1 to 5.

**Exit questions.** Only day 108 is a teaching day with an exit; days 107, 109 and 110 are observation days with no exit (their evidence is the talk record and the F1 sheet). Fresh cards are covered until the exit. The case is **invented for practice**, the teacher says so aloud; the facts are from memory (checked 2026-11-20, see sources.csv).
- **E108.** Card: "A holly tree has red berries in winter. Waxwings eat the berries. Holly leaves are green and sharp." Ask once: "Which line fills the gap?" The teacher says: "Our collage shows the holly tree but does not show who depends on it." Three strips, each with a picture: (a) "A holly tree has red berries" with a holly branch; (b) "Waxwings eat the berries" with a bird at berries; (c) "Holly leaves are green and sharp" with a leaf. Key: Strip (b), "Waxwings eat the berries", with the bird at berries. Wrong: Strip (a), the red berries on the holly branch (E); Strip (c), the sharp leaf (E). Heard: "Where does it go on the collage?" Meets: Beside the berries, with an arrow, from the card.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** research-cycle: ask, find, note, share, used as the talk's frame (review of unit 3, introduced day 80). text-structure (order and because): the arrow and so link (review of unit 3, taught in 3.1 to 3.3). main-idea-details: the talk gives a topic and one detail (review of unit 3). discussion-protocol: the talking token and one turn each, with passing allowed (review of unit 3). sound-spelling-map: short e is added on day 107; short o, a, i, u remain on the map. Notes keep their source label and arrow; no sentence starter is offered.
- **Key moment 1, days 107, 109, 110: the research talk.** Ideal instrument: a record sheet with each child's name and three boxes (question, line from the card, so), ticked by the teacher while a partner listens. Spec: a paper sheet and a pencil; the three boxes are marked shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Gaps: none for a spec; nothing needs a device.
- **Key moment 2, day 108: the gap on the collage.** Ideal instrument: movable picture strips and a gap card. Spec: paper strips and a card. Judged against it: the child can place the line without help.
- **A room with nothing.** A paper roster, a pencil, chart paper and six picture strips; the day writer draws the strips.

## 6. Days

## Day 107


- **5 min. Words and warm-up.** Preview research and share by gesture (share is hands out, open); question, source, line are known. Retrieve the talk frame by holding up three fingers: my question, my line, so.
- **15 min. Read 05a and 05b.** Read the oak profile 05a and the maple profile 05b aloud, then reread the dependent lines. Name the source of the reread line aloud.
- **15 min. Prepare the talk.** In pairs with the token: each child says their question and points to their labeled note; the partner finds the matching line on the card. One turn each, about a minute a child, the teacher circulating.
- **20 min. Observed research talks.** Two groups of five, ten minutes each at the teacher table, about two minutes a child; the other children draw a collage plan sheet (the tree, one need, one living thing) in two columns. The teacher marks R2 for the ten children heard.
- **5 min. Close.** Name one thing a friend said that taught you something; no exit on a check day.
- **7 min. Sound warm-up.** Oral middle sounds with counters (hen, net, red, leg), chorally; no print.
- **12 min. Short e lesson (new).** Say /e/ and trace e on the map; the keyword picture (an egg) recalls the sound and never gives a word. Sort picture cards by middle sound: /e/ against a, i, o, u. Blend e with known letters (ten, net) and chain ten, tan, tin, naming each changed sound (RF.K.3d). One counter set per pair, 15 sets.
- **6 min. Map be and by.** Mark b plus e and b plus y as the parts to remember (the long sound of e and y); read each in a sentence strip; review earlier words (to, do, at, has, is, it, was, his, or, for) by pointing as the teacher says them.
- **20 min. Partner and small-group reading.** The cycle 17 reader page one is read aloud by the teacher, then the 15 pairs reread and say what happened. The teacher listens to partners and notes who needs a repair slot on day 108.
- **10 min. Spell and print.** Dictate ten, net, leg; children push a counter a sound and write plausible letters.
- **5 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up.** Hear the verse, find the two rhyming words, clap the referent; counters collected.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the roster and the frame; each child holds one labeled note and one arrow. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, drawing, counters, printing. Evidence: R2 observation for ten children (talks 1 and 2); the day 97 to 99 R1 and C1 records are retained, not redone.

## Day 108


- **5 min. Words and warm-up.** Preview collect and gap by gesture (collect is hands gathering, gap is hands apart). Retrieve the need and dependent words from 01b. Repair at most one letter group from day 107 (A is likely).
- **15 min. Read 03 and reread 05b.** Read the chant 03 whole, then reread the dependent lines of 05b, listening for a living thing that is missing from the class collage chart.
- **15 min. Questions and talk.** Q1 "What does our collage not show yet?" Q2 "Which line in the reading fills the gap?" Turn and talk with the token, one turn each; every child may pass.
- **20 min. Draw and dictate.** 5 min: teacher models finding a missing dependent with a gap card and one line, then placing it with an arrow and a source sticker. 15 min: each child checks their collage plan sheet from day 107, finds one gap, draws a note to fill it and dictates its so sentence; the teacher takes six children to name the need, the dependent and the link.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E108, covered until now; six children heard.
- **5 min. Sound warm-up.** Oral middle-sound task with new words (jet, wet, men), chorally; six children alone.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** 3 min: each child rereads one sentence they stumbled on from day 107 with a partner. 4 min: groups by letter (K, R, L, AH, M, AG, V) with the teacher or a helper. 3 min: a fresh retry with a new word pair.
- **10 min. Short e review.** Tiles and counters: chain ten, tan, tin and set, sat, sit; name the changed sound each time (RF.K.3d); isolate the middle sound alone and add or swap a sound (RF.K.2d, 2e).
- **5 min. Map be and by.** Read both in new sentence strips; map the remembered part again (RF.K.3c).
- **20 min. Partner and small-group reading.** Partners reread the reader and say what happened; the teacher listens to five children read a sentence and say what it means, two minutes each (observation only; the F1 battery begins day 110).
- **5 min. Spell and print.** Dictate three short e words from a new set (men, red, wet); children write by sounds.
- **5 min. Tree Words verse.** Reread the verse; point to the first word of each line.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the gap card; each child holds a collage plan sheet and one new note. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, dictating, tiles, printing. Evidence: E108; the repair groups by letter (practice only).

## Day 109


- **5 min. Words and warm-up.** Review source, line and share by gesture; repair at most one: if many made E on E108, lay a new card and a gap picture and place the line that names who uses the tree.
- **15 min. Read 05b and 06.** Read 06 whole and reread the dependent lines of 05b. Name the source of each line aloud.
- **15 min. Prepare the talk.** Pairs rehearse the three turns (my question, my line, so) with a new partner, one turn each; the teacher circulates.
- **20 min. Observed research talks.** Two more groups of five, ten minutes each; the rest finish their collage plan sheet. The teacher fills only a missing part of the R1 and C1 records, never a second conference.
- **5 min. Close.** Choose one friend line to keep for the collage; no exit on a check day.
- **7 min. Sound warm-up.** Oral task for all with counters; six rotating children alone.
- **10 min. Short e, application.** One-letter chains on tiles (hen, men, ten; ten, tin; set, sat); name each changed sound.
- **5 min. Map be and by.** Reread in text; review earlier words.
- **20 min. Partner and small-group reading.** Reread the cycle 17 reader for what was learned; partners point to one line and say it in their own words.
- **8 min. Spell and print.** Dictate three words (jet, leg, net); children write by sounds and the teacher notes letters used.
- **10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up.** Choral read; echo the two rhyming words; clap the referent; materials away.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the roster; each child holds their part. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, drawing, tiles, printing. Evidence: R2 observation for ten more children; R1 and C1 retained records (days 97 to 99, 86 to 87).

## Day 110


- **5 min. Words and warm-up.** Review gap, collect and share by gesture. No repair is routed here; the day 109 evidence stays.
- **15 min. Read 05b again.** Reread the maple selection 05b for the dependent lines, as a listening task. No new text.
- **15 min. Prepare the talk.** Pairs rehearse the last turn, so, joining a need and its dependent; the teacher circulates.
- **20 min. Observed research talks.** The last two groups of five, ten minutes each; each child is checked against the roster so that every part of the R1 and C1 records exists once. Missing parts are filled from the day 107 and 109 observations or from this talk.
- **5 min. Close.** Verify each child has a note, an arrow and a source label for the collage; no exit on a check day.
- **5 min. Sound warm-up.** Oral task with new words; six children alone.
- **10 min. Short e review.** Chain tiles with two new triples; name each changed sound; blend and read one new line aloud.
- **5 min. Map be and by.** Read in text strips; review all mapped words.
- **20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery.** Five children (the first five on the roster) each take three minutes at the teacher table: read the fresh F1 decodable for what it tells and point to its words; spell by sounds; read the F1 pair and say the changed sound (15 min in all). The other twenty-five read the reader in pairs, use tiles, and print verse words and letter cards in station groups.
- **10 min. Spell and print.** Dictate three words from a new set; children write by sounds.
- **10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up.** Choral read; tracking line by line; collect materials.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the roster and the F1 sheet; each child holds a collage plan sheet. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, drawing, tiles, printing. Evidence: R2 observation for the last ten children; F1 for children 1 to 5 (RF.K.3d, L.K.2d, RF.K.4); R1 and C1 retained records.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 108: "What does our collage not show yet? Which line in the reading fills the gap?" Day 109: "Which line of the card is your fact from?" Day 110: "How do your need and your dependent go together?"
- **Written task, day 108:** a drawn note that fills one gap on the collage plan sheet, with an arrow and a source label; drawing and dictation accepted.
- **Judged by the teacher with four look-fors:** (1) the need or the dependent is named; (2) the line is from the card; (3) the source label names the right text; (4) an arrow joins them. Unscored practice for the collage; the talks fill R2 only.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview research, share, collect, gap, check with a gesture and a picture; accept home-language rehearsal before the English talk. Frame: "My tree is ___. It needs ___. ___ uses it, so ___." (Offered only to children who ask.)
- **Access.** Enlarge or describe the pictures; accept pointing, dictation and usual communication supports; counters are large and tactile.
- **Check accommodations.** Repeat the ask without giving the link; offer a described or tactile picture set; accept a spoken or pointed answer; a partner may speak first; record support used. For the F1 battery, a scribe may write for a child, never choosing the letters.
- **Reading load.** Children read only the cycle reader and short labels (the day writer keeps each printed page to 60 words or fewer); every tree text is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain a second dependent from another source with its sticker.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the day's text aloud, ask the two questions, talk through the three turns once, sound out two short e words with coins as counters. The F1 battery is three minutes: the adult hears the child read the fresh passage and records the answer, no scoring on a first try.
- **One projector.** Hold the roster on paper; project one picture strip at a time. Everything else is paper.
