W.K.8 "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": the child's own source-labelled notes from 3.6 and the readings 05a, 05b, 06 and 03, always with an adult. - **Limits that bite here.** The talks are observation days (107, 109, 110): each child is observed once for each missing part and the R1 and C1 records are retained, never redone (zero new minutes). Six groups of five across the three talk days, at most ten minutes a group; the rest follow the day's reading and drawing. Two facts at a time; adult-provided sources only; drawing and dictation accepted. Short e only. Reserved for F1 (days 110 to 113, 115, 116): the word pair pen and pin, the spelling words bed and sun, and the F1 decodable; none appears in teaching or exits here.
- **Connections.** RI.K.3 with W.K.7 and W.K.8: the talk asks the child to say a need, a dependent and the arrow, with the source label. RF.K.3d with L.K.2d: the one-letter chain (ten, tan, tin) names the changed sound. RF.K.3c: be and by, with the regular and remembered parts marked.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) What a research talk is at five: three turns, "my question", "my line from the card", "so ...", about two minutes a child. (2) How the six groups of five are chosen and rotated across the three talk days so that no child is observed twice for the same part. (3) A need is something the tree takes in; a dependent is a living thing that gets food or shelter from it. (4) Why 109 and 110 reuse records: the day 97 to 99 and 86 to 87 records already hold the evidence, so the talk fills only a missing part. (5) The short e lesson names the sound of e in a word, never the letter name in a sound task.
- **One word, one sense.** check means look at the source again, never a test. support means back up a note with a source line.
