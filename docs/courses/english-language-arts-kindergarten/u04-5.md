## 1. Standards and practices


This section gives the planting-place opinion its draft, its first observation and its reteach. Shares, with the verb quoted:

- **W.K.1** (days 137, 138): "compose opinion pieces" by drawing, dictating and writing, naming the topic and a preference. A reason enriches but is not required. Day 137 plans and drafts; day 138 observes the revised draft (item O1). Nothing composed on day 138 itself is scored.
- **RI.K.8** (days 137 to 139): "identify the reasons an author gives". Taught on 129, 130, 133 and 140 elsewhere; here it is practiced on 02a and observed once on day 138. It is scored apart from the preference.
- **RL.K.9, RI.K.9** (day 139): "compare and contrast" and "identify basic similarities in and differences between" two texts, as retry only.
- **RF.K.1a to d, L.K.1a, RF.K.3a, L.K.2c, RF.K.2a to c** (day 139): retrieval with fresh cases; no new teaching.
- **RF.K.3b, 3c, 3d, RF.K.3, RF.K.4, L.K.2d, RF.K.2d, 2e, RF.K.2** (days 137, 139): cycle 22 practice; the pattern was taught on day 135.

Limits that bite: children read only taught letters and words, so every stem is spoken with a picture card and every answer is spoken, pointed, drawn or dictated. The class pot or a classroom place is the planting site, never a yard. The one marking scale is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Judged by the teacher against the look-fors in field 3.

The teacher page must say that the opinion and the author's reason are two records, that O1 reads the draft as revised and never scores same-day work, and that the day-139 retry uses new cases only.

## 6. Days

## Day 137


- **5 min. Words return.** shade, place, helper, reach: a picture and a gesture for each, one sense each (place is where something goes; reach is to stretch and touch). Fifteen pairs say each word in a sentence, about 20 seconds a word; the teacher models the first two.
- **15 min. Reread with a purpose card.** 4 min: the teacher rereads 02a pages 3 to 5 aloud (about 100 words), pointing at the pictures. 6 min: five groups of six each hold one purpose card (a bench, a bird, a resting child) and point to the sentence that answers it, then say it back, about 1 minute a group. The teacher says one unfamiliar word on purpose (shelter) so a child can ask what it means (prompted clarification). 5 min: whole class, 01b pages 5 and 6 (Ben looks for a quieter place) with the purpose card taught on day 134, 'Where does Ben go?', answered by pointing then saying 'behind the tree'. Never the reserved pages 3 or 9 of 01a or 01b.
- **15 min. Questions and talk.** Three paired turns of about 2 minutes (fifteen pairs): 'Which reason in the page helps you choose a place?' then 'Where could our class pot go: by the window or by the door?' then 'Say: I would put our tree ___. I like it ___.' Six children share, 30 seconds each (3 min); the teacher models one share (3 min) and holds 3 minutes for moves.
- **20 min. Plan and draft the opinion.** 5 min: the teacher models the topic and preference boxes with a different place (the library shelf) and one drawing. 12 min: each child draws their chosen place in the topic box and a liking in the preference box; the teacher takes dictation from about eight children while the rest label by themselves or tell a partner. A reason is welcome, not required, and is labeled 'the writer says' when it comes from 02a and 'my reason' when it is the child's own (the wording of day 133). 3 min: three children show. All drafts are collected for written feedback after class (a one-line note each, about 30 minutes after school).
- **5 min. Exit E137.** Card, two pictures and one ask from field 3. Hear about six rotating children; the rest mark.
- **20 min. Sounds and spelling.** Doubled finals ll, zz, ss, ff recalled and practiced, no new pattern: 3 min recall with letter tiles (bell, buzz, mess, puff: two letters, one sound); 6 min sort word cards by final sound and blend each (hill, doll, jazz, fuzz, miss, moss, huff, cuff); 4 min counters, isolating first, middle and last sounds of three words; 5 min change one letter and name the sound (bell to bill, miss to moss, huff to puff); 2 min mapped words but, not, no on tiles.
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. Tasks 7 and 8 are omitted today, so the time goes to reading Moss on a Log again (title adult-read). Teacher table, about 50 seconds a child: find the event that answers the purpose card, then choose which of two event descriptions matches the hard sentence (tasks 1 and 2). Station 2, task 3: match the sentence to a picture and say why. Station 3, task 4: copy the sentence that answers a new question, spaces and letter shapes. Station 4, tasks 5 and 6: the cycle-22 verse heard on day 135, find the line that fixes the order, say what it adds to the reader, dictate one sentence. Station 5: partner reread.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** The class writes one reader sentence on chart paper (say it, count the words, leave a space); children point left to right as it is read; two children each add a doubled-final word.
- **5 min. Check.** Whiteboards: the teacher says bell, then buzz; each child writes both; the six children of the day's rotating group are marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

Held by: the teacher for the plan, the table and the exit; the children hold their own draft and purpose card. Carried: the plan sheet. The reserved pages of 01a and 01b stay closed. Tool: None. By hand: purpose cards, plan sheet, letter tiles, counters, whiteboards, word cards. Evidence: E137 (field 3); the draft goes to written feedback; the foundations check is recorded for six children.

## Day 138


- **5 min. Words return.** place, helper, shade, reach, water: gesture and picture, one sense each. 'Reach' was taught on day 137, so the card on day 138 does not test it cold.
- **10 min. Reread 02a.** The teacher reads 02a whole (about 280 words, 2.5 minutes) with the pictures; no new questions.
- **10 min. Revise the day-137 plan.** Each child gets their written note, hears it from a partner or the teacher, and changes the draft: names the place if missing, adds a liking. Drafts stay as made beside the revision.
- **30 min. Observation windows (O1).** Six groups of five, 5 minutes each at the teacher's table: 2.5 minutes W.K.1 and 2.5 minutes RI.K.8, both from field 3, each child about 25 seconds a part with one 15-second prompt allowed. The other children read and draw tree helpers with a partner, from familiar books; no reteaching occurs during the windows.
- **5 min. Partner share.** Each child tells a partner the place they chose and what they like. Spoken, not scored.
- **20 min. Sounds and spelling.** L114 practice, no new pattern: 4 min recall ll zz ss ff; 6 min read word cards (fell, fill, hiss, jazz, doll, dull) with a one-letter change named by sound (hill to hall, doll to dull, fuzz to fizz); 5 min isolate three sounds with counters; 5 min spell two words on whiteboards; 2 min but, not, no.
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** The same five stations with Moss on a Log, tasks 1 to 6, and tasks 7 and 8 in their normal place (isolated: three-sound isolation without text, one mapped word read and one taught word spelled). The teacher table sees six children.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** The class and the teacher write the cycle-22 verse line that says what to do first, point to each word, and hear a doubled-final word in it.
- **5 min. Check.** Dictate bell, then fuzz; the next six children of the rotating group are marked on the three-point scale.

Held by: the teacher at the table; the others work from familiar books. Carried: the revised plan and the O1 record; unfinished plans stay in folders. Tool: None. By hand: observation sheet, picture card, Planting Together page 2, chart paper. Evidence: O1 W.K.1 and O1 RI.K.8, recorded apart (field 3). No exit today; nothing composed today is scored.

## Day 139


- **5 min. Words return.** reason (a sentence the writer states to support the point, as taught on day 130), step (one thing done in order), shade, place, helper: a card for each. These return from earlier days, so this is a check by showing, not new teaching.
- **15 min. Topic response (E139).** Model 5 min (whole class): the teacher shows a wrong answer beside a right one, with new cases: 'A bench' beside 'A bench. I like it by the fence'; 'I like the sign' beside 'Then visitors can find us' (card: Put the sign by the door); one bean card beside another that tell different things (a pole, a pot). Repair 5 min in two parts of 2.5 minutes each: the teacher works with one table of children from day 138's F, G, C and B results while the others sort; then the second table. Retry 5 min: E139 (field 3). The 15 minutes hold one repair, so no 'Return and repair' part is needed; only the day-138 results are routed here.
- **15 min. Sort reasons and steps.** 02a pages 3 and 5 and 02b pages 3, 5 and 6 are read aloud (about 200 words, 2 minutes); children sort five sentence cards, spoken and pictured, into a reason pile and a step pile with a partner, then hang each reason on an arrow of the box-with-arrows model (the point in the box, each stated reason on an arrow tied to a page), and say one sentence back; six pairs share.
- **15 min. Questions and talk.** Compare 02a and 02b (RI.K.9) with the frame 'Both ___. But ___.' from day 131: 'What do the words of 02a tell us that 02b does not?' then 'What do the pictures of 02b show?' Paired turns of 2 minutes; six children share.
- **10 min. Draw and dictate.** Each child draws one reason and one step on a strip and dictates one sentence; the teacher takes about eight.
- **20 min. Retrieval, sounds and print.** 10 min each. First: print direction and spaces (point left to right, top to bottom, page by page; find the spaces) and letter names, upper and lower case, with letter cards G K J Q Y E U L in pairs; the teacher listens to the six children of the day's group. Second: print three of those letters and write a letter for the first sound of net, jet, jam, dig; rhyme (bell and sell, hill and fill: which two rhyme, then one of your own); syllables (clap and count basket and jam); onset and rime (say j-am, d-ig and blend).
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** Five stations with Moss on a Log and the cycle-22 verse. Only already-taught text is read; no new pattern. Teacher table: tasks 1 and 2; stations 2 to 4: tasks 3 to 6; partner reread.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** Write one sentence together from the verse; each child points to the spaces; two children print a doubled-final word.
- **5 min. Check.** Pairs of spoken words: bell and bill, buzz and fuzz, mess and miss; children say which sound changed; the rotating group's six are marked.

Held by: the teacher for the model and the tables; the children sort and share. Carried: the day-138 results and the sort cards. Tool: None. By hand: sentence cards, picture cards, letter cards, counters, chart paper. Evidence: E139 (field 3); the retrieval check, kept apart from the exit; a one-line record for each child on the three-point scale.

## 9. Supports and extension

- English learners: the words place, helper, reach, reason, step. Frame: 'I would put our tree ___. I like it ___.' Home-language rehearsal with a partner before the observation windows.
- Access: enlarged print on the sentence cards; dictation for any child; pointing counts as an answer; a seat near the table.
- Check accommodations: a pointed picture or a drawn answer is accepted for O1 and the exits; extra time on the 15-second prompt.
- Reading load: no child reads the cards; the teacher does. The only print a child decodes is taught.
- Extension (same standard): add a second reason from 02a to the plan and say which page it came from.

## 10. Two rooms

A parent teaching one child: reread 02a with a purpose ('find the sentence that says who the shade helps'), then draw a place and say 'I would put our tree ___. I like it ___.' Use a pot or a window as the place. On day 138, ask the child for the reason in a sentence you read: 'Place the pot where you can reach it. Then you can water it.' With one projector, show the picture card and keep the sentences spoken.
