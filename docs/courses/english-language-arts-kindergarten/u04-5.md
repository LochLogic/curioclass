First: print direction and spaces (point left to right, top to bottom, page by page; find the spaces) and letter names, upper and lower case, with letter cards G K J Q Y E U L in pairs; the teacher listens to the six children of the day's group. Second: print three of those letters and write a letter for the first sound of net, jet, jam, dig; rhyme (bell and sell, hill and fill: which two rhyme, then one of your own); syllables (clap and count basket and jam); onset and rime (say j-am, d-ig and blend).
- **25 min. Small-group reading.** Five stations with Moss on a Log and the cycle-22 verse. Only already-taught text is read; no new pattern. Teacher table: tasks 1 and 2; stations 2 to 4: tasks 3 to 6; partner reread.
- **10 min. Shared print and writing.** Write one sentence together from the verse; each child points to the spaces; two children print a doubled-final word.
- **5 min. Check.** Pairs of spoken words: bell and bill, buzz and fuzz, mess and miss; children say which sound changed; the rotating group's six are marked.

Held by: the teacher for the model and the tables; the children sort and share. Carried: the day-138 results and the sort cards. Tool: None. By hand: sentence cards, picture cards, letter cards, counters, chart paper. Evidence: E139 (field 3); the retrieval check, kept apart from the exit; a one-line record for each child on the three-point scale.

## 9. Supports and extension

- English learners: the words place, helper, reach, reason, step. Frame: 'I would put our tree ___. I like it ___.' Home-language rehearsal with a partner before the observation windows.
- Access: enlarged print on the sentence cards; dictation for any child; pointing counts as an answer; a seat near the table.
- Check accommodations: a pointed picture or a drawn answer is accepted for O1 and the exits; extra time on the 15-second prompt.
- Reading load: no child reads the cards; the teacher does. The only print a child decodes is taught.
- Extension (same standard): add a second reason from 02a to the plan and say which page it came from.

## 10. Two rooms

A parent teaching one child: reread 02a with a purpose ('find the sentence that says who the shade helps'), then draw a place and say 'I would put our tree ___. I like it ___.' Use a pot or a window as the place. On day 138, ask the child for the reason in a sentence you read: 'Place the pot where you can reach it. Then you can water it.' With one projector, show the picture card and keep the sentences spoken.
