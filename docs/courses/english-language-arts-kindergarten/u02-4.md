# Section 2.4: Sharing the weather piece, and a response to the evidence





## 1. Standards and practices


- **SL.K.1a, SL.K.1b, SL.K.3, day 47.** Children "follow agreed-upon rules for discussions" and "continue a conversation through multiple exchanges"; they "ask and answer questions in order to ... clarify something that is not understood". All are practiced, none newly taught: the token and the question words come from days 42 and 43.
- **SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6, days 47 and 48.** "Describe familiar people, places, things, and events", "add drawings ... to provide additional detail", "speak audibly". Practiced from the day 46 evidence.
- **W.K.2, day 48.** "Name what they are writing about and supply some information": one new fact in drawing and dictation, a practice piece not scored.
- **Foundations** (RF.K.1, RF.K.2, RF.K.3a, RF.K.3c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c): cycle 7, lesson 36 (d, f, l) on day 47, with the mapped word and; day 48 is the cycle 7 response, no new pattern. Phonemes: day 47 isolating and segmenting three sounds; day 48 adding a sound. No word ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.
- **Limits that bite here.** Nothing is taught as new on day 47 apart from the cycle 7 foundations lesson. Day 48 repairs only what the day 46 records and the day 45 exit showed. The response retry is unscored. Texts are read aloud; children recognize only taught letters and mapped words.
- **Connections.** The question words (day 43) are the clarifying questions of day 47. The three marks (day 45) return in day 48's retry.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A clarifying question asks for one more detail from the text or the picture. A conversation continues when each child answers and then asks or adds one thing. The response is built from the groups the day 46 records make (field 4).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none; I1 and O1 belong to section 2.3. Day 48 is a response: its card is a retry and is not scored. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

**E47 (spoken, from the day's six).** Invented card C47, said aloud to be invented: "Wind pushes the gate. Mai holds the gate still." Spoken: "A friend asks, who held the gate? Tell your friend one thing from the card." Answer: Mai held the gate (a detail from the card, said to a partner who then asks one more thing and the child answers or says "I do not know yet"). Wrong: "A gate" or "wind" with no answer to who (F); no reply to the partner's second question (AS).

Answer: fog sat on the harbour (or boats waited). Wrong: "boats" alone as an answer (F); a drawing of something other than the detail told (D).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The three question cards, the discussion token, the class picture journal and the three marks, all from 2.3, are reused. Nothing new is introduced.
- **Key moment, day 47: the second exchange.** Must be heard: a child answers and the partner asks again. Ideal instrument: the teacher at six pairs with a mark in the heard column. A recorder is not planned. Spec: none.
- **A room with nothing.** Paper, pencils, crayons, a clipboard record sheet and the child's own pieces.

## 6. Days

## Day 47


- **10 min. Return and repair.** Day 45 and day 46 errors for the groups: AA (picture moved under), B and C (topic card on top, fact below). The teacher takes the groups in turn, about 3 minutes each.
- **5 min. Words.** With gestures: share, clarify, detail, question.
- **10 min. Reread 01a.** Reread 01a pages 1 to 4 (kinds of weather) aloud, children pointing to a detail they will share about.
- **5 min. Model.** A puppet shares a weather piece; the teacher asks one clarifying question; the puppet answers from the page and asks one back (two turns).
- **15 min. Share the weather piece.** Pairs share their day 46 piece, then ask each other one question with a question card and answer from the page or picture (two turns each). The teacher hears six pairs in about 2 minutes each.
- **10 min. Add a detail.** Each child adds one drawn or dictated detail that answers the partner's question (SL.K.5).
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E47, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Poem launch for cycle 7: point to d, f and l in verse V4; clap the beats.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Mystery word and: clues in the verse, map the letters (a, n, d), mark the part to remember, find and in the verse. Source: cycle 7, lesson 36.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Isolate and segment: say fan, say its three sounds with a counter each; say sad the same way. Writing place: Write d, f and l; trace and on a card. Sound and verse place: Pairs match picture cards to the first sound; find and in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: children hold the pieces; the teacher holds the cards and the record. Tool: None. By hand: day 46 pieces, journal pages, question cards, token. Evidence: E47, foundations heard column.

## Day 48


- **15 min. Response.** Groups by the day 46 records (field 4: D, E, F, AX, AS): redraw one told detail; rehearse toward a partner; recast an unclear sentence; practice a second turn. The teacher visits each group about 3 minutes; the other groups practice a pair talk.
- **5 min. Words.** Return: detail, clear, question.
- **15 min. Reading 01a.** Whole reading of 01a (gk-u2-01-a) aloud, children pointing to a detail; one question: "What did you hear about the weather?"
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs tell one detail from 01a, with a partner question and an answer (two exchanges).
- **10 min. Draw and dictate.** Each child draws a topic and says or dictates one new fact from 01a; practice, not scored (W.K.2).
- **5 min. Fresh card.** E48, from the day's six; unscored retry.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Reread verses V3 and V4 with the pointer.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Cycle 7 response, ten minutes, in groups by the cycle 7 evidence; chant the letters taught so far. No new pattern. Source: ours (foundations response).
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Add a sound: say at, add /s/ to make sat; say it, add /p/ to make pat; each child says the new word. Writing place: Write d, f and l in lines; trace and. Sound and verse place: Beats and rhyme cards; blend and segment sat and pat. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: children hold their work; the teacher holds the groups and the record. Tool: None. By hand: day 46 records, drafts, journal pages, 01a. Evidence: E48 (unscored), heard column.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 47, "Who held the gate? What did you see on your page?"; day 48, "What did you hear about the weather?"
- Written task, day 48: one drawn or dictated fact about a topic from 01a; practice, not scored.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: a topic is named; one fact from the text; the fact is about the topic.

## 8. Practice that returns

- Day 47: question cards (day 43), token (day 42), the day 46 piece. Day 48: the three marks (day 45), the class journal (day 41). Foundations: he, we, on, to and and.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: share, clarify, detail, question. Frames: "I saw ___." "Can you say more about ___?" A home-language label may go on the piece.
- **Access.** Dictation, pointing and a communication aid count; a pair talk may happen away from the group.
- **Check accommodations.** None needed: nothing is scored on these days.
- **Reading load.** None; everything is read aloud. Children recognize only taught letters and the mapped words he, we, on, to and and.
- **Extension, same standard.** A second question to a partner, said and heard.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Share the piece, ask and answer two questions about it, add one drawn detail. On day 48 retell one fact and try the retry card.
- **One projector.** One screen shows 01a; everything else is paper.
