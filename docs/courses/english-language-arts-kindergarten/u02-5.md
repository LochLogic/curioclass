# Section 2.5: Weather in five places, and a weather story: where, when, first, next, last





## 1. Standards and practices


- **RI.K.1, RI.K.2, day 49.** Children "ask and answer questions about key details in a text" and "identify the main topic and retell key details" of 01a pages 5 to 8 (three places). The main topic is weather in different places.
- **RL.K.3, days 49 to 51.** "Identify characters, settings, and major events in a story": the story-map of 02a (who, where, what happens). Day 49 models it, day 50 setting (where and when), day 51 events.
- **RL.K.2, days 51 and 52.** "Retell familiar stories, including key details": first, next, last with three picture cards (modelled day 51, partner-held day 52).
- **SL.K.1, SL.K.3, day 51.** Collaborative talk with "multiple exchanges"; questions to "get information, or clarify" using the question words of day 43.
- **SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6, days 50 and 52.** Describing a weather place aloud with a drawing, heard and clear.
- **L.K.1b to e, days 50 and 53.** Nouns, verbs, plural nouns, question words and prepositions are used in talk about the five places.
- **RL.K.4, RI.K.4, L.K.4, L.K.4b, days 49 and 53.** One unknown word a day by the three-card routine (cloudless, day 49; unpacked, day 53). Day 53 teaches un-, re- and -ed from 02a (unpacked, refilled, packed); pre-, -ful, -less and -ing are only heard (precut, colorful, cloudless, tapping) and return later. L.K.4a was taught on day 44 (clear); no new multiple-meaning word is taught here.
- **W.K.2, day 53.** The piece "name[s] what they are writing about and supply[ies] some information": the place and one fact from the chart; drawn, dictated or written with taught letters.
- **Foundations** (RF.K.1, RF.K.2, RF.K.3a, RF.K.3c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c): cycle 7 lessons 37 to 40 (d, f, l; and, of) on days 49 to 52, the cycle 7 check on day 52, then on day 53 a 10-minute response followed by cycle 8 lesson 41 (k, y; up). Phonemes: blend (49), substitute the first sound (50), substitute the last sound (51), the check (52), add a sound (53). No word ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.
- **Limits that bite here.** The text read whole is 02a (about 314 words, read in 8 minutes with its pictures); its pictures and the places of 01a are never read by children. Day 52 teaches nothing new. Each exit takes one new card (invented, unused in teaching). The word "clear" is not used in any card (it has two senses already; a third would confuse).
- **Connections.** The five-place chart is the unit's evidence chart and carries to days 58 and beyond. The story-map is carried to day 54.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A story-map has three boxes: who, where and when, and what happens. The change is the event that makes the weather matter. A retell keeps the first, next and last with their key details. Un- means not or the opposite (unpack); re- means again (refill).

Each teaching day has an exit of two spoken items (information, then story), each one ask, answered by pointing, saying or putting cards in order; hear six children a day; marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Days 49 to 51 and item b of day 52 read a named page of Weather Changes, Clouds or The Picnic Plan aloud again, with its picture; the other cards are invented for practice, said aloud to be invented, read twice, never used in teaching.

- **E49.** Weather Changes, page 2. Item a: "Point to the picture where children sit when they feel hot." Answer: the shade by the bench. Wrong: the swings or the sunny path (A). The Picnic Plan, page 5. Item b: "What happens on this page that changes the picnic again?" Answer: Grandma Rosa's idea, a picnic on the living room floor. Wrong: "the blanket" or "the grapes" (I).
- **E50.** Clouds, page 2. Item a: "Where is some water that we cannot see?" Answer: in the air. Wrong: "in the pond" (A). The Picnic Plan, page 4. Item b: "Tell me where Tomas and Grandma Rosa are going on this page." Answer: back home. Wrong: "to the park" (H).
- **E51.** Clouds, page 3. Item a: "Ask me a question that this page answers." Answer: "What colors can clouds be?" "Are all clouds the same?" Wrong: a question the page does not answer, "How high is the sky?" (A). The Picnic Plan, page 5. Item b: three pictures, mixed; "Put the pictures in the order they happen." Answer: unpacks the basket, spreads the blanket, puts out the plates and cups. Wrong: any other order (J).
- **E52 (fluency day, practice only).** Information card: "Wind moves the flag by the gate. The ground below is wet." Item a: "Tell me one thing about the weather on this card, in a sentence." Answer: "The wind moves the flag." or "The ground is wet." Wrong: a whisper (E); "wet ground" alone (AE). The Picnic Plan, page 1. Item b: "Tell me what happens last on this page." - **E53.** Information card: "Snow covers the grass beside the pond. The path by the school is bare." Item a: "What will you draw to show the weather on this card? Say it in a sentence." Answer: names a place and its weather: "Snow covers the grass." Wrong: no place or weather (B); "I like snow" (C). Word card: "Mara unties the knot. Then Mara reties it." ## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The five-place chart is begun day 49 and kept. The three-card routine and the question cards (day 43) return daily. New here: the story-map (three boxes: who, where and when, what happens; held by the teacher on day 49 and by partners on day 52), and three retell cards (first, next, last).
- **Key moment 1, day 49: the story-map model.** Must be seen: a child names a character, a place and the event that changes the plan. Ideal instrument: the teacher's chart, with children pointing. Spec: none.
- **Key moment 2, day 51: the retell.** Must be heard: first, next and last in order with a key detail. Ideal instrument: the teacher at six pairs with a mark in the heard column. A recorder would let a retell be checked later; it is not planned. Spec: none.
- **A room with nothing.** Chart paper, three index cards a pair, pencils, crayons, a clipboard record sheet and objects (cup, box, bag).

## 6. Days

## Day 49


- **5 min. Warm-up and words.** Retrieve one letter-sound from cycle 6 with its letter card; then with pictures: pond, hill, field, compare.
- **10 min. Reread 01a.** Reread 01a pages 5 to 8 aloud with the pictures (three places: Mawsynram, Atacama, Mount Washington), children pointing to the weather in each.
- **15 min. Begin the five-place chart.** Chart paper with a column for each place; the teacher models one place: name, weather, one detail. Pairs choose another of the three and say it; the teacher writes. Main topic: weather in different places. The teacher hears six pairs.
- **3 min. Unknown word.** Before 02a: cloudless, with the three-card routine (word, picture of the sky, a sentence).
- **8 min. Read 02a whole.** First reading of 02a, The Picnic Plan, aloud whole with its six pictures, no stops except one: "What happens when the sky changes?"
- **12 min. Model the story-map.** Three boxes: who, where and when, what happens. The teacher models, then pairs point: who acts (Tomas and Grandma Rosa), where (the park, then the living room), what changes the plan (rain starts). One actor named at a time.
- **2 min. Move.** Children move to the exit places.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E49, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Letter-sound chant; reread verse V4; find d, f and l.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Letter search and formation: find d, f and l in the verse and write each in the air, on a card and in lines. Source: cycle 7, lesson 37.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Blend: say d-i-g slowly, children blend to dig; do the same for fan and lip. Words never end in /l/, /r/ or /x/. Writing place: Write d, f and l; trace and; write and from a card. Sound and verse place: Beats and rhyme cards; find and in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the chart and the map; children hold the pointing and the saying. Tool: None. By hand: 01a pictures, chart paper, map boxes, card word. Evidence: E49, foundations heard column.

## Day 50


- **5 min. Warm-up and words.** Repair for G, H or I (one, 5 minutes): match the person to the character box; then fog, sunshine, bridge, garden with pictures.
- **10 min. Reread two places.** Reread the Utqiagvik page of 01b and the San Francisco fog page of 01d aloud with pictures; pairs point to the weather and say one detail.
- **15 min. Add two places.** Add both to the five-place chart. Each pair describes one place with a drawing (SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6): the teacher models a sentence with a plural noun and a place word ("Fog covers the bridges."); the teacher hears six pairs.
- **5 min. Reread 02a.** Reread 02a pages 1 to 3, children listening for where and when.
- **12 min. Where and when.** Cards for where (park, pond, home) and when (morning, afternoon); the teacher reads a sentence, pairs choose the card. Model first, then two sentences each pair. Where does the plan change? On page 3, in the park.
- **8 min. Pairs locate.** Pairs place the where and when cards under the three boxes of the story-map; the teacher hears six pairs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E50, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Letter-sound chant; reread verse V4; find and.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Articulatory gestures: say /d/, /f/ and /l/ with the mouth pictured; mystery word of: clues in the verse, map the letters (o says /o/ here, the part to remember is f), find of. Source: cycle 7, lesson 38.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Substitute the first sound: fan to can to pan; dip to lip; each child says the new word. Writing place: Write d, f and l; trace of; write and. Sound and verse place: Pairs change the first sound with counters; find and and of in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the chart and cards; children hold the places and the descriptions. Tool: None. By hand: 01b and 01d pictures, chart, where and when cards, map. Evidence: E50, foundations heard column.

- **8 min. Reread 02a.** Reread 02a whole, children listening for first, next and last.

- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E52, items a and b, from the day's six; practice only.

- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. ## Day 53


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Repair for E or AE or J (one, 5 minutes); then reread the five-place chart.
- **5 min. Model the piece.** The teacher models a journal piece: draw a place, say its weather, dictate one fact from the chart; then asks "Where? What?".
- **20 min. Write the piece.** Each child draws one place from the chart, says or dictates one fact, and writes any taught letters or the mapped words he, we, on, to, and; practice with feedback after class on the three marks. The teacher hears six.
- **5 min. Words.** Un- and re- with real things: pack and unpack a box, fill and refill a cup, tie and retie a bow; -ed as in packed (it happened). Ask what the changed word tells about the action.
- **8 min. Heard in 02a.** Reread the sentences of 02a page 5 (unpacked, spread, refilled) aloud; children point to the base word and the changed word. Colorful, precut and cloudless are heard only.
- **7 min. Act and say.** Pairs act pack, unpack; fill, refill; then say what each word tells (two turns each); the teacher hears six pairs.
- **5 min. Sort.** Pairs sort six action cards under un-, re-, and -ed (a base-and-changed card each, read aloud).
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E53, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Cycle 8 opening: reread verse V5 and the earlier verses (verse V6 comes on day 56); point to k and y; clap the beats.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Cycle 7 response, ten minutes, in groups by the day 52 check (field 4: U); chant the twelve letters, then five minutes: mystery word up with the clues, map the letters (u, p). Source: ours (foundations response), then cycle 8, lesson 41.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Add a sound: say at, add /s/ to make sat; say it, add /p/ for pat; each child says the new word. Writing place: Write k and y; trace up on a card. Sound and verse place: Beats and rhyme cards; find up in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: children hold the pieces and the cards; the teacher holds the marks and the record. Tool: None. By hand: chart, journal, action cards, real objects (cup, box, bag). Evidence: E53, the journal piece (practice), foundations heard column.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: pond, hill, compare, retell, first, next, last, unpack, refill. Frames: "The ___ is in ___." "First ___, next ___, last ___." A home-language label may go on the journal piece.
- **Access.** Large picture cards and real objects; an adult moves cards as the child directs; dictation and a communication aid count; a pair talk may happen away from the group.
- **Check accommodations.** None needed: no day here is scored. Dictation, pointing and an aid count for every exit; repeating a prompt once is allowed.
- **Reading load.** Texts are read aloud: 02a is about 314 words (about 3 minutes read; 8 minutes with pictures) and 01a pages 5 to 8 about 2 minutes. Children recognise only taught letters and mapped words.
- **Extension, same standard.** A second place on the journal piece, or a second question to a partner.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 01a pages 5 to 8 and 02a aloud on different days. Make the five-place chart on one sheet, build the story-map with three boxes, retell with three cards, and write one journal piece.
- **One projector.** One screen shows the chart and the story-map; everything else is paper and real objects.
