Wrong: points to the pond or field (A). Story card: "At breakfast, Sol sits by the window. Rain starts. Sol moves the basket inside." Item b: "Tell me what happens that changes Sol's morning." Answer: rain starts. Wrong: "the basket" or "the window" (I).
- **E50.** Information card: "Fog covers the bridge. Sunshine reaches the garden." Item a: "Tell me where the fog is." Answer: on the bridge. Wrong: "in the garden" (A). Story card: "In the morning, Ana reaches the park gate. Wind shuts the gate. Ana waits beside it." Item b: "Tell me where Ana waits." Answer: beside the gate (at the park). Wrong: "in the garden" or "on the bridge" from the other card (H).
- **E51.** Information card: "Rain falls by the school. People open umbrellas to stay dry." Item a: "Ask me a question that this card answers." Answer: any question the card answers: "What do people open?" "Why do they open umbrellas?" Wrong: a question the card does not answer, "What color are the umbrellas?" (A). Story card: "Bo packs a snack. Rain begins. Bo eats beside the classroom window." Item b: three pictures, shuffled; "Put the pictures in the order they happen." Answer: packs, rain begins, eats at the window. Wrong: any other order (J).
- **E52 (fluency day, practice only).** Information card: "Wind moves the flag by the gate. The ground below is wet." Item a: "Tell me one thing about the weather on this card, in a sentence." Answer: "The wind moves the flag." or "The ground is wet." Wrong: a whisper (E); "wet ground" alone (AE). Story card: "Jin holds a paper boat. Rain fills a puddle. Jin watches from the dry porch." Item b: "Tell me what happens last." Answer: Jin watches from the porch. Wrong: says the first event (J).
- **E53.** Information card: "Snow covers the grass beside the pond. The path by the school is bare." Item a: "What will you draw to show the weather on this card? Say it in a sentence." Answer: names a place and its weather: "Snow covers the grass." Wrong: no place or weather (B); "I like snow" (C). Word card: "Mara unties the knot. Then Mara reties it." Item b: with three pictures (a knot tied, a knot undone, tied again): "Point to the picture that shows reties." Answer: tied again. Wrong: points to the undone knot (AD).
