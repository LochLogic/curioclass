# Section 2.2: Rain and snow: plurals, sentences and a weather talk





## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.K.4, RI.K.4, L.K.4, this section's share.** Day 39 continues "ask and answer questions about unknown words" with the three-card routine from 2.1, on 01c (puddle, snowflake). L.K.4a and L.K.4b stay later (day 44 and day 53); the day skeletons list them on day 39, and no exit here asks for them.
- **L.K.1c.** Day 39 teaches "form regular plural nouns orally by adding /s/ or /es/": drops, flakes, puddles, then dishes. L.K.1b (nouns and verbs) is review. L.K.1d and L.K.1e are listed by the skeleton and taught on days 43 and 44.
- **L.K.1f.** Day 40: "produce and expand complete sentences in shared language activities": add where or when to a short sentence, and join two with "and". L.K.2a and L.K.2b (capital, end mark) are listed on day 40; children only watch the teacher write the sentence, and the words capital and period are taught on day 54.
- **RI.K.2 and W.K.2.** Day 41: "identify the main topic and retell key details", and a joint "drawing, dictating, and writing" model of an information entry that "name[s] what they are writing about and suppl[ies] some information". The class picture journal begins; each child's own begins day 55.
- **SL.K.1, SL.K.3.** Day 42: "participate in collaborative conversations ... in small and larger groups" and "continue a conversation through multiple exchanges"; ask and answer to clarify.
- **SL.K.4, SL.K.5, SL.K.6.** Days 40 to 42: describe with a drawing and speak audibly and clearly; practiced in pairs, not taught new.
- **Foundations** (RF.K.1, RF.K.2, RF.K.3a, RF.K.3c, L.K.1a, L.K.2c): cycle 5, lesson 30 (rhyme time) and the cycle 5 check on day 39; cycle 5 response on day 40; cycle 6, lessons 31 and 32 (g and i; the mapped word on arrives on day 43) on days 41 and 42. Phonemes: day 39 blending, day 40 response, day 41 middle sounds, day 42 adding a sound.
- **Limits that bite here.** Plural words are said, never read. The -es ending is heard in dishes and buses (it makes a new beat), and irregular plurals (children, feet) are left to talk and never corrected as errors. The weather is rain, snow and ice only, with no storm and no thermometer number. Phoneme words are three sounds and never end in /l/, /r/ or /x/. No child is asked about weather at home; the journal holds what the class sees.
- **Connections.** RF.K.2 with L.K.1c: the extra beat in "dishes" is a syllable (day 39). W.K.2 with RI.K.2 (day 41). SL.K.1 with the unit 1 discussion protocol (day 42).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Plurals at five are heard: one drop, many drops; two sounds for the ending (/s/ in cats, /z/ in drops, /iz/ in dishes) are not taught as rules. A sentence is a naming part and a doing part; "expanding" adds where or when. Information writing at five is a topic picture with details; the teacher writes the sentence the child says. A talk has several exchanges: say, ask, answer, add. Rain and snow facts rest on 01c, whose facts are checked in its sources.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** main-idea-details (topic on top, details below) is used on day 41 with the journal entry and again on day 46. discussion-protocol grows into the science talk on day 42 (say, ask, answer, add; several exchanges); both stay on every pairs part. New here: sentence-combining (day 40: join two short spoken sentences with "and"), carried through both short stories and day 76; and the class picture journal (day 41), carried to the child's own journal on day 55. The three-card question routine (2.1) is used on day 39.
- **Key moment 1, day 40: a sentence grows.** Must be seen: the same sentence said again with a place or time added. Ideal instrument: movable picture-and-word cards on a pocket chart. Spec: no tool; cards on a table fit. Gaps and asks: none (TOOL-ASKS.md names only the text editor).
- **Key moment 2, day 42: several exchanges.** Must be heard: a pair or group going past one question and answer. Ideal instrument: the teacher listening and a tally on the record sheet; a recorder would help but is not planned. Spec: none needed; print and ears fit.
- **A room with nothing.** Word and picture cards on scrap paper, a pocket made from a folded sheet, a token, paper and crayons.

## 6. Days

## Day 39


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 38 (field 4 of 2.1: I, F or AT) with puppets, then one fresh two-time sentence about a picture card.
- **5 min. Words.** With gestures: wet, dry, protect, explain, record (draw or say what you see). The word moisture is not taught: 01c never uses it.
- **10 min. Reading 01c.** First reading of 01c, Rain and Snow, whole, about 2 minutes with its four pictures; then one question: "What falls from the clouds?"
- **12 min. Reread and ask about a word.** Reread pages 2 and 3. Model asking "What does puddle mean?" and find the answer in the picture and the line "Many drops make a puddle". Children ask about snowflake with the three-card routine. Remind: rain is wet drops; snow is flakes of ice.
- **13 min. Model: more than one.** Show one drop and many drops, one flake and many flakes, one puddle and many puddles (counting objects and picture cards), then one dish and two dishes: say the ending, clap the beats (dish has one, dishes has two). Children echo, then say the plural before the teacher does.
- **10 min. Partners.** Pairs take turns: the teacher shows a picture card with one object (boot, hat, mitten, dish, glass), the partner says "one ___, two ___" with a pointer; swap. The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E39, items a and b, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Verse V2 reread with a pointer: track the lines, find the words with v or s.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Rhyme Time: find the rhyming words in the verse and say a new rhyme for a word with a new first sound. Cycle 5 check: on paper, write the letter for the sound /v/ and the sound /s/ (all children, 5 minutes). Source: cycle 5, lesson 30.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Cycle 5 check, one child at a time: name v, name s, say the sound of each (30 children, about 40 seconds each, 20 minutes in all across the three turns; the other places run without the teacher). Writing place: Finish the sound-writing check on paper, then practice writing v and s in lines. Sound and verse place: Rhyme cards: match pairs that rhyme and say one more; find rhyming words in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the plural model and the check; children hold the objects and the saying. Tool: None. By hand: object and picture cards, token, paper, crayons. Evidence: E39, the cycle 5 check (foundations record).

## Day 40


- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** One repair from day 39 (X or Y) with picture cards, then one fresh plural said aloud.
- **10 min. Reread 01b.** Reread pages 2 and 3 of 01b; children listen for when the air feels warmer and when it feels cooler, and say it as a sentence.
- **15 min. Model: a sentence grows.** The teacher says a short sentence ("The air feels cool.") and asks where or when to make it longer ("The air feels cool at night."). Then two short sentences become one: "The sun goes down. The air gets cooler." becomes "The sun goes down and the air gets cooler." Word and picture cards on a pocket chart show each addition. This is the sentence-combining model, introduced here.
- **5 min. Shared writing.** The teacher writes one grown sentence on chart paper as children say it; children watch where it starts and where it stops. No term for the capital or the end mark is taught yet.
- **20 min. Pairs.** Pairs take a picture card (a dog under a roof, a boy at a window, a girl with a bag), say a short sentence, then add where or when, then join two short sentences with "and". Each child makes three sentences. The teacher listens at six pairs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E40, from the day's six.
- **5 min. Foundations opening.** Verses V1 and V2 reread with a pointer; track the lines and find he.
- **15 min. Foundations work time.** Cycle 5 response, ten minutes, in groups by the day 39 check (field 4: U and W); the rest of the time, chant the ten letters and reread only the taught verses and words. No new pattern. Source: ours (foundations response), after cycle 5.
- **2 min. Foundations closing.** Reflect aloud, "Today I practised ..."; three children finish the sentence.
- **38 min. Foundations rotations.** Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns (teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place) and 2 minutes to move; the teacher is at the table for each turn, so about one minute for each child. Table: Small groups by the cycle 5 check: sort name cards from sound cards for v and s; blend and segment the spoken words sat, van, vat. Writing place: Write v and s; write the letter for a dictated sound; trace he and we. Sound and verse place: Beat and rhyme cards: clap the beats of weather words; match two rhyming pictures. The day's six (two from each group) go in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Who holds it: the teacher holds the pocket chart and the sentence; children hold the cards and the added words. Tool: None. By hand: pocket chart or folded paper, picture and word cards, chart paper. Evidence: E40, the response notes.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: wet, dry, protect, explain, record, journal. Frames: "I see ___." "The ___ is ___ at ___." "I think ___. What do you think?" A home-language word may name the topic if a picture shows it.
- **Access.** Large picture cards; objects to hold for plurals; an adult moves the cards as the child points; a communication aid counts. A child who finds group talk hard may talk in the pair round only.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a token on a picture and dictation count for every exit; the spoken ask is repeated once on request. Plural endings are recorded apart from loudness and clarity.
- **Reading load.** None; everything is read aloud. The only print children recognize is taught letters and he and we.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second detail to the grown sentence, or ask a question that begins with a word like "why" (heard, not required).

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 01c aloud on the first day, then count objects for plurals (one spoon, two spoons; one dish, two dishes). Add where or when to a sentence together. Make one journal page with a topic picture and one detail. Take three turns of a talk.
- **One projector.** One screen shows the pictures and the journal page; the pocket chart is drawn once; the group talk circle is a floor circle for six, not a screen task.
