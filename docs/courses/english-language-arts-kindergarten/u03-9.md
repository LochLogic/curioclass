# Section 3.9: Sharing the tree collage

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of each code.** W.K.7 "Participate in shared research and writing projects": the finished collage is the shared project's product, shown on day 116; retained R1 record closes day 117. W.K.8 "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": each fact on the collage has its source sticker; retained R1 record. SL.K.4 "Describe familiar people, places, things, and events and, with prompting and support, provide additional detail": the child describes the tree and one detail with a prompt. SL.K.5 "Add drawings or other visual displays to descriptions": the collage is the display. SL.K.6 "Speak audibly and express thoughts, feelings, and ideas clearly": the child speaks to a table of five. L.K.2 "Demonstrate command of the conventions ... when writing": a record from earlier evidence only (day 75 capitals and marks, day 77 or 78 sound-to-letter spelling, the F1 spelling). L.K.2d "Spell simple words phonetically": F1 spelling, children 21 to 30. L.K.1a "Print many upper- and lowercase letters" and L.K.2c "Write a letter or letters for most consonant and short-vowel sounds": day 118 dictation and printing, retained with the P2 and P3 records.
- **Limits that bite here.** Day 115 is rehearse and edit, day 116 is share (scored inside the existing S1 minute, no new time), day 117 is records only with no new pattern and no repeat conference, day 118 is one targeted fifteen-minute response. Reserved for F1 (115, 116 only): pen and pin, bed, sun and the F1 decodable; none appears in teaching or exits here. Acorns are tree nuts: pictures only. Short vowels only; RF.K.3b and parent RF.K.3 finish on days 158 and 159, not here.
- **Connections.** SL.K.4 to 6 with W.K.2 (scored day 116): the child shows the collage and says the tree, a fact and the link. L.K.2 with L.K.2d: one record from earlier evidence. L.K.1a with L.K.2c: printing and sound-to-letter writing on day 118.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) How the share is run: six tables of five children, one child speaks at a time to the table, the teacher visits each table once and hears the child who is speaking; the collage pages themselves are scored for all thirty after class. (2) The four collage rows (tree named, fact supported, detail from the right reading, picture adds information) and what meets, partly meets and does not (2/1/0). (3) Why day 117 gathers nothing new. (4) The bridge to day 119: the class will ask how people can care for trees. (5) The last-day response is one block of fifteen minutes: reteach the one error most children made, then a fresh retry on a new passage.

- **E115.** On the text, not an invented card: selection b (The Sugar Maple), page 6, first part, read aloud again. Say three explanations, each with a picture: (a) "People boil its sap, so it gives them syrup." (b) "It has sap. It has people." (c) "People boil the sap." - **E116.** Card: "A persimmon tree has orange fruit. Opossums eat the fruit." A child shows a picture of a persimmon and an opossum. Ask once: "Which sentence uses the card to say who depends on the tree?" Three strips read aloud: (a) "Opossums eat the fruit." (b) "Persimmons are orange." (c) "Opossums like to climb." Key: Strip (a), "Opossums eat the fruit." Wrong: Strip (b), "Persimmons are orange." (E); Strip (c), "Opossums like to climb." (D). Heard: "Show me the line." Meets: Points to the fruit sentence about opossums.
- **E117.** On the text, not an invented card: selection b (The Sugar Maple), page 6, last part, read aloud again. Ask 1: "Which drawing joins what the tree needs and who uses it? (strip E117: the maple with water, light and soil coming in, and a squirrel at its seeds with an arrow out; the maple with water and light and no squirrel; a squirrel with seeds and no maple)" Right: The maple with water, light and soil coming in, and the squirrel at its seeds with an arrow out. Wrong: The maple with water and light and no squirrel (A); A squirrel with seeds and no maple (A). Ask 2: "Who depends on the maple on this page?" Right: Squirrels need its seeds; birds need its shelter; people get syrup (one is enough). Wrong: names only what the maple needs, not who depends on it (E).
- **E118.** Card: A cold card, new today: "A mangrove tree grows in salty water. Young fish hide among its roots." Ask once: "Which picture shows who depends on the tree?" Three pictures: young fish among the mangrove roots with an arrow from the roots to the fish; the mangrove with no fish; a fish far out in open water. Key: Young fish among the roots, with an arrow from the roots to the fish. Wrong: The mangrove with no fish (A); A fish far out in open water (D). Heard: "Which words tell us?" Meets: "Young fish hide among its roots."

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** writing-process: edit and share the finished collage (review of unit 3, taught in 3.3 and 3.4). text-structure (order and because): the arrow and so link joins need and dependent (review of unit 3). research-cycle: ask, find, note, share, whose last step is the share on day 116 (review of unit 3). main-idea-details: the opening names the tree (review of unit 3). discussion-protocol: speaking to a table with the token (review of unit 3). sound-spelling-map: all five short vowels on one map (review of earlier cycles of this grade). The finished collages are kept for day 119 and for the portfolio.
- **Key moment 1, day 116: the share.** Ideal instrument: each child's collage held up, a table of five, the teacher's record sheet with the four 2/1/0 rows and three SL boxes. Spec: paper and a pencil; the SL boxes are marked shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Gaps: none for a spec; the page itself is the display.
- **Key moment 2, day 118: the fresh retry.** Ideal instrument: the cold card with its picture choices covered. Spec: paper cards and a pencil; the child marks a picture.
- **A room with nothing.** The collage pages, a paper record sheet, a talking token, chart paper and crayons; the day writer draws the exit cards.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview explain, share, display, care with a gesture and a picture; accept home-language rehearsal before English. Frame: "My tree is ___. It needs ___. ___ uses it, so ___." (Offered only to children who ask.)
- **Access.** Enlarge the picture cards; accept dictation and pointing; a child who does not speak aloud may share by pointing to the collage while a partner reads the caption; counters are large and tactile.
- **Check accommodations.** Repeat the ask without giving the link; a described or tactile picture set; a spoken or pointed answer; a partner may speak first; record support used. A scribe may write the label, never choose the letters on a spelling day.
- **Reading load.** Children read only the cycle reader and short labels (each printed page 60 words or fewer); all tree texts are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain a second dependent, or show how two sources agree.
