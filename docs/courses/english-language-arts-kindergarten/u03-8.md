# Section 3.8: Making the tree collage

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of each code.** W.K.2 "Use a combination of drawing, dictating, and writing to compose informative/explanatory texts in which they name what they are writing about and supply some information": the tree collage, begun day 111, a detail drafted day 112, a second added day 113, finished day 114; scored day 116 inside S1. W.K.7 "Participate in shared research and writing projects": each child makes their own collage from the class's shared notes and sources; partners ask one question. W.K.8 "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": each detail is checked against a named reading on day 114. RI.K.3 "describe the connection between two ... pieces of information in a text": the need and the living thing joined by an arrow. RF.K.3d, L.K.2d, RF.K.4 (days 111 to 113 by F1, 114 by practice): a one-letter change with the changed sound named; spelling by sounds; the cycle reader read for what happened. RF.K.3b, 3, 3c, RF.K.2d, 2e (day 114): all short vowels, mapped words are and will, middle sound, add or swap one sound.
- **Limits that bite here.** Two facts a day, one picture, one arrow. Provided sources only; no search. Drawing and dictation accepted, no mark against spelling by sounds in content work. Written feedback after days 112 and 113 is four short notes (tree named, fact supported, source right, picture adds information), never a rewrite. Short vowels a, e, i, o, u only; no long vowels. Reserved for F1 (days 110 to 113, 115, 116): pen and pin, bed, sun and the F1 decodable; none appears in teaching or exits here, and the cycle 18 reader must not use them. Acorns are tree nuts: pictures only.
- **Connections.** W.K.2 with RI.K.3: the collage names the tree and joins a need to a dependent. W.K.8 with W.K.2: each detail carries its source sticker. RF.K.3d with L.K.2d: the one-letter chain names the changed sound. RF.K.4 with RF.K.3c: the reader uses are and will as words to remember.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) What the collage is: a page with the tree in the middle, a need coming in, one living thing going out, labels by sounds or dictated, and a source sticker; it is a picture first. (2) The four feedback notes after days 112 and 113 and what each tells the child to do next. (3) How the F1 battery runs inside the foundations rotation, three minutes a child at the teacher table, five children a day, with no extra interview. (4) Why day 114 is practice only: the unit plan keeps it free for a shorter-year fold. (5) Short vowels are compared by their middle sounds, not by the names of the letters.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** day 112 and day 113 F1 rows for roster children 11 to 15 and 16 to 20 (RF.K.3d pair, L.K.2d spelling, RF.K.4 fresh decodable; day 111 is the same battery for children 6 to 10, owned by 3.7's table); day 115 F1 for children 21 to 25; day 116 W.K.7, W.K.8 and RI.K.3 (retained R1 and C1, plus a current S1 cell for need and dependent with a link) and W.K.2 (the four 2/1/0 rows scored on the day 116 collage). The F1 decodable and pair are held by the unit plan and are not used here. This section produces the collage that day 116 scores.

**Exit questions.** One spoken ask each; the child points to or marks a picture, and six children (a rotating group named on the record sheet) are heard saying why. Fresh cards are covered until the exit. Day 111 rereads a named page not yet worked; the other cases are **invented for practice**, the teacher says so aloud; the facts are from memory (checked 2026-11-20, see sources.csv). If many miss one, the next day's warm-up opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E111.** On the text, not an invented card: selection a (The Oak), page 5, read aloud again. Ask 1: "Which picture goes next, to show who depends on the tree? (strip E111: a songbird on its nest in the oak's branches; the oak alone in summer; a bird at a birdhouse far from any tree)" Right: The songbird on its nest in the oak's branches. Wrong: The oak alone in summer (J); The bird at a birdhouse far from the tree (D). Ask 2: "Which words on the page tell us?" Right: "Some birds build nests in the oak." Wrong: says words about the oak alone, not the birds (J).
- **E112.** Card: "A beech tree grows tall. Wild turkeys eat its nuts." Ask once: "Which strip is a fact from the card for the collage?" Three strips read aloud, each with a picture: (a) "Wild turkeys eat the nuts." with a turkey under a beech; (b) "Beeches are the best trees." with a beech; (c) "Wild turkeys gobble loudly." with a turkey. Key: Strip (a), "Wild turkeys eat the nuts." Wrong: Strip (b), "Beeches are the best trees." (D); Strip (c), "Wild turkeys gobble loudly." (D). Heard: "Show me the line." Meets: Points to the nuts sentence.
- **E113.** Card: "An aspen has smooth pale bark. Its leaves flutter in the wind. Beavers eat aspen bark." A partner's question card, read aloud: "Where does the beaver get its food?" Ask once: "How should the drawing change to answer the question?" Three drawing changes: a beaver at the trunk with an arrow from the bark to the beaver; a bigger aspen; a beaver asleep in a lodge with no link to the tree. Key: Add the beaver at the trunk, with an arrow from the bark to the beaver. Wrong: Draw a bigger aspen (J); A beaver asleep in a lodge with no link (A). Heard: "Which words tell us?" Meets: "Beavers eat aspen bark."
- **E114.** Card: The source card, with sticker "ash card": "An ash tree has seeds with wings. Evening grosbeaks eat the seeds." Ask once: "Which note does the card support?" Three notes read aloud, each with a sticker "ash card": (1) "Ash seeds have no wings." (2) "Rabbits eat ash leaves." (3) "Evening grosbeaks eat ash seeds." Key: Note (3), "Evening grosbeaks eat ash seeds." Wrong: Note (1), "Ash seeds have no wings." (D); Note (2), "Rabbits eat ash leaves." (D). Heard: "Show me the line." Meets: Points to the grosbeaks sentence.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** writing-process: draft, revise by one partner question, check against a source (review of unit 3, taught in 3.3 and 3.4). text-structure (order and because): the arrow and so link (review of unit 3). research-cycle: ask, find, note, share, now ending in the share on day 116 (review of unit 3). main-idea-details: the opening names the tree and gives details (review of unit 3). sound-spelling-map: short e and all short vowels now on one map (review of earlier cycles of this grade). discussion-protocol: the partner question with the talking token (review of unit 3). Sentence starters are not offered.
- **Key moment 1, day 113: the partner question.** Ideal instrument: a question card the partner holds up while the child points to the drawing. Spec: paper cards and a pencil; the child marks the change with an arrow. Gaps: none for a spec.
- **Key moment 2, day 114: the source check.** Ideal instrument: the printed card with each sentence on its own strip. Spec: paper strips and a source sticker for each reading.
- **A room with nothing.** Chart paper, paper strips, a marker, crayons, glue sticks and precut paper (the collage); the day writer draws the exit cards.

## 6. Days

## Day 111


- **5 min. Words and warm-up.** Preview collage and gap by gesture; retrieve tree and dependent from 3.7. No repair is routed here; day 110 held its own.
- **15 min. Read 05b.** Reread the maple selection 05b for what the tree needs and who uses it; name the source out loud.
- **15 min. Questions and talk.** Q1 "Which tree is our collage about?" Q2 "What does it need, and who uses it?" Turn and talk with the token, one turn each; every child may pass.
- **20 min. Collage composition begun.** 3 min: teacher models placing the tree in the middle of the page, naming it with a label strip, and one need coming in. 17 min: each child composes the tree and one need on the collage page with crayons, glue and precut paper; names the tree by sounds or dictation. Unscored practice.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E111, covered until now; six children heard.
- **5 min. Sound warm-up.** Oral middle sounds with counters; six children alone.
- **10 min. Short e review.** Tiles and counters: chain ten, tan, tin; name each changed sound (RF.K.3d).
- **5 min. Map be and by.** Read in sentence strips; review earlier words.
- **20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery.** Roster children 6 to 10, three minutes each at the teacher table (15 min): read the fresh F1 decodable for what it tells and point to its words; spell by sounds; read the F1 pair and name the changed letter and its sound. The other twenty-five read the cycle 17 reader in pairs, use tiles, print verse words and letter cards in station groups.
- **10 min. Spell and print.** Dictate three words from a new short e set; children write by sounds.
- **10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up.** Choral read; tracking line by line; materials away.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the roster and the F1 sheet; each child holds one collage page with the tree and a need. Tool: None. By hand: collage, drawing, tiles, printing. Evidence: E111; F1 for children 6 to 10 (RF.K.3d, L.K.2d, RF.K.4).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview collage, draft, revise, check, source with a gesture and a picture; accept home-language rehearsal before English. Frame: "My tree is ___. It needs ___. ___ uses it. It says so in ___." (Offered only to children who ask.)
- **Access.** Provide precut shapes and a pre-glued tree if fine motor is hard; enlarge the picture cards; accept dictation and pointing; counters are large and tactile.
- **Check accommodations.** Repeat the ask without giving the link; a described or tactile picture set; a spoken or pointed answer; a partner may speak first; record support used. A scribe may write the label but never choose the letters on a spelling day.
- **Reading load.** Children read only the cycle readers and short labels (each printed page 60 words or fewer); all tree texts are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a third fact with its source sticker, or explain why one fact belongs beside another.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the day's card aloud, ask the two questions, make the collage together with a tree, one need and one living thing; the adult takes a photo or tells the source. The F1 battery is three minutes: read the fresh passage for what it says, spell two words by sounds, read the pair. The adult scribes and never corrects spelling in content work.
- **One projector.** Hold a page of the collage on a document camera or on paper; project one exit card at a time. Everything else is paper.
