## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T5a.4** (days 137, 139, 141): Describe the culture and way of life of the region's indigenous peoples, such as the Carib, Taino, Lenca and Miskito.
- **D2.Geo.6.6-8** (days 137, 138, 139, 140, 141): Explain how the physical and human features of places and regions connect to human identities and cultures.
- **MA.6.T5a.1** (days 141): Use map tools to locate Central America and the Caribbean Sea, and identify features like the Gulf of Mexico, the Yucatan Peninsula and the Panama Canal.
- **MA.6.T5a.2** (days 141): Locate the present-day countries and major cities of Central America and the Caribbean on a political map, using maps to add to text.
- **MA.6.T5a.3** (days 141): Explain how location, climate, landforms and natural resources shaped settlement, population size and economies in Central America and the Caribbean.
- **D2.Geo.1.6-8** (days 141): Make maps that show and explain spatial patterns of culture and environment.
