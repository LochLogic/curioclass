## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T3b.1** (days 39): Explain how the Tigris and Euphrates Rivers supported farming and early complex societies, and why the surrounding region is called the Fertile Crescent.
- **MA.6.T3b.2** (days 40): Use a map of archaeological sites to identify where and when the Sumerians, Babylonians and Assyrians rose as successive states and empires.
- **MA.6.T3b.3** (days 41): Describe how irrigation, mining, metalworking, farming, animal domestication and inventions like the wheel, sail and plow helped Mesopotamian settlements grow.
- **D2.Eco.1.6-8** (days 41, 42): Explain how economic decisions affect the well-being of people, businesses and society.
- **D2.Eco.13.6-8** (days 42): Explain why living standards rise as productivity improves.
- skill ss6-mesopotamia-states (grade 6)
- skill ss6-surplus-specialization (grade 6)
