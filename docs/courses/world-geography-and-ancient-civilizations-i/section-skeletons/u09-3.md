## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T4b.1** (days 124, 125): Identify where the Kingdom of Axum was, what made it wealthy and important, and its role in Ethiopia's adoption of Christianity, and explain how East African societies helped spread Islam across the Indian Ocean to South Asia.
- **MA.6.T4b.2** (days 125): Explain how Swahili coastal societies linked inland Africa to Indian Ocean trade routes reaching South Asia and China, and name key goods traded.
- **MA.6.T4b.3** (days 126, 127): Identify the locations, sources of wealth and importance of West African cities and empires, including Timbuktu and ancient Ghana.
- **MA.6.T4b.4** (days 127): Explain these societies' key role in trans-Saharan trade, the spread of Islam and trade in gold, ivory and enslaved people, and their contributions to the modern world.
- skill ss6-axum-swahili (grade 6)
- skill ss6-west-africa-trade (grade 6)
