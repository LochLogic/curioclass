## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T3a.3** (days 30, 31, 32): Explain how location, landforms, climate and natural resources shaped where people settled, how large populations grew and what economies developed in the region.
- **D2.Geo.2.6-8** (days 30, 33): Use maps, satellite images and photographs to explain how the location of places and regions relates to changes in their environmental features.
- **D2.Geo.8.6-8** (days 32, 33): Analyze how the human relationship with environments widens or narrows patterns of settlement and movement.
- skill ss6-mena-settlement (grade 6)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions, drafted together.** How do maps locate people and water (days 26 to 29)? Why do settlements cluster (days 30 to 32)? How can people change those patterns (day 33)? What should we do about water where we live (days 34 to 36 and 55)?
> **Teaching.** Model keys on 26, coordinates on 28, scale on 29 and the regional frame on 30. On 31 separate climate axes; different observation periods prohibit precise city rankings. On 32 explain oil, aquifers, irrigation and desalination. Model evidence and reasoning; withdraw the example on 33. Compare community accounts with satellite evidence. Days 34 to 36 and 55 keep dated action records: question, public facts, alternatives, chosen step, audience, result and revision. Students choose; no promised savings or household disclosures.
>
> - **MA.6.T3a.1:** Use map tools to locate the region's key seas, rivers, straits and peninsulas, such as the Nile, Tigris, Euphrates, Red Sea and Suez Canal, and read other map types. Parts, by verb: Use map tools to locate waterways and peninsulas (teach 26, practise 27 to 29, exits 26 and 29, item 1); read physical, political, population and climate maps or graphs (teach 26, 27, 30 and 31, practise 32 and 33, exits 27, 30 and 31, items 1 and 3).
> - **MA.6.T3a.3:** Explain how location, landforms, climate and natural resources shaped where people settled, how large populations grew and what economies developed in the region. Parts, by verb: (a) Explain settlement location; (b) explain population growth; (c) explain economic development, each through location, landforms, climate and resources. Teach all three on 30 to 32, practise on 33, check through exits 30 to 32 and item 3.
>
> **Carried.** Keep unit 2's timeline, sourcing, continuity and change, and independent claims; retain its trait checklist for unit 4. Compass directions and map keys return on 26; tie scale to distance on 29 and latitude and longitude to hemispheres on 28. Withdraw worked map answers on 29, retaining reference keys and grids. Tie region to the settlement frame on 30; withdraw its completed example on 33. Add image dates to the timeline.
