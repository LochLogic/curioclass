## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T3b.4** (days 43): Analyze the main features and achievements of early Mesopotamian civilization.
- **MA.6.T3b.4a** (days 43): A complex society with rulers, priests, soldiers, craftspeople, farmers and enslaved people.
- **MA.6.T3b.4b** (days 44): A polytheistic religion, the belief in many gods.
- **MA.6.T3b.4c** (days 44): Monumental building such as the ziggurat, and developed art including relief sculpture, mosaics and carved cylinder seals.
- **MA.6.T3b.4d** (days 45): Cuneiform writing, used for keeping records, collecting taxes, recording laws and writing literature.
- **MA.6.T3b.4e** (days 46): The first epic, the Epic of Gilgamesh, and the Code of Hammurabi, an early surviving written law collection (the framework calls it the first written law code), including its "eye for an eye" principle.
- skill ss6-mesopotamia-society (grade 6)
- skill ss6-hammurabi-code (grade 6)
