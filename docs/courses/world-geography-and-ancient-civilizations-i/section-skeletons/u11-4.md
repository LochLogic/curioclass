## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T5b.1** (days 155): Use map tools to locate South America and the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans, then features like the Isthmus of Panama, the Andes, Cape Horn and the Amazon River.
- **MA.6.T5b.2** (days 155): Read a political map to tell countries, capitals and cities apart and describe their absolute location (coordinates) and relative location, using maps to add to text.
- **MA.6.T5b.3** (days 155): Explain how location, landforms, climate and natural resources shaped settlement patterns, population size and economies in South America.
- **D2.Geo.4.6-8** (days 155): Explain how cultural patterns and economic choices affect environments and daily life, both nearby and far away.
- **MA.6.T5c.1** (days 155): Research and report on one major ancient Central American society (such as the Maya) or Andean civilization (such as Moche), covering its location and cultural traits.
- **D2.His.12.6-8** (days 155): Use questions about several sources to find more to investigate and more sources to consult.
- **D3.3.6-8** (days 155): Pick out evidence from several sources that supports claims, and note its limits.
- **D4.2.6-8** (days 155): Explain something using reasoning, the right order, examples, details and fitting information, admitting the explanation's strengths and weaknesses.
- skill ss6-south-america-geo (grade 6)
- skill ss6-maya-andes-research (grade 6)
