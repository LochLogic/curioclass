## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T3g.2** (days 105): Use primary and secondary sources to research one ancient Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Phoenician, Israelite, Islamic or Eurasian society's contribution to the modern world.
- **D1.3.6-8** (days 105): Explain where experts agree about how the concepts behind a supporting question are read and applied.
- **D1.4.6-8** (days 105, 106): Explain how supporting and big questions strengthen each other as the inquiry goes on.
- **D1.5.6-8** (days 106, 107): Decide which kinds of sources would help answer the questions, allowing for the several points of view the sources show.
- **D3.1.6-8** (days 107): Gather useful information from several sources, using origin, authority, structure, context and how well they back each other up to choose.
- skill ss6-research-contribution (grade 6)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions, drafted together.** What moved besides goods (100)? How did roads and conquest connect people, at whose cost (101 to 103)? How can we trace a contribution without claiming sole invention (105 to 108)? What makes it significant today (109 to 111)?
> **Teaching.** Section 8.1 teaches exchange and contextual causation; model routes on 100 and causation on 101. Section 8.2 teaches research questions, source selection and testing; model questions on 105 and selection on 106. Students hold records on 107. Section 8.3 develops significance, question revision and the report; model a paragraph on 109. Support RH.6-8.1 and WHST.6-8.7 to WHST.6-8.9.
