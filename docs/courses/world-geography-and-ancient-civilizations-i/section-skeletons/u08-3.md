## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D2.His.3.6-8** (days 108, 111, 112): Use questions about people and groups to analyze why they and what they shaped are seen as historically significant.
- **D1.3.6-8** (days 108, 112): Explain where experts agree about how the concepts behind a supporting question are read and applied.
- **MA.6.T3g.2** (days 109, 110, 111, 112): Use primary and secondary sources to research one ancient Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Phoenician, Israelite, Islamic or Eurasian society's contribution to the modern world.
- **D3.1.6-8** (days 109, 112): Gather useful information from several sources, using origin, authority, structure, context and how well they back each other up to choose.
- **MA.6.T3g.1** (days 112): Describe the effects of trade, cultural exchange and conquest among the region's societies, especially along the Silk Roads, and the goods, languages and cultural motifs exchanged.
- **D2.His.1.6-8** (days 112): Analyze how events and developments connect within broader historical settings.
- **D1.4.6-8** (days 112): Explain how supporting and big questions strengthen each other as the inquiry goes on.
- **D1.5.6-8** (days 112): Decide which kinds of sources would help answer the questions, allowing for the several points of view the sources show.
