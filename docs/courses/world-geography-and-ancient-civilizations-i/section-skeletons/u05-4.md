## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.6.T3c.2** (days 68, 69): Describe why the Nile River mattered so much to ancient Egyptians.
- **D2.His.10.6-8** (days 68, 69): Spot gaps or weak points in the historical record using evidence from different kinds of sources.
- **MA.6.T3c.1** (days 69): Locate Upper Egypt, Lower Egypt and Nubia, and explain what "Upper" and "Lower" mean in this setting.
- **MA.6.T3c.4** (days 69): Analyze the pharaoh's role as god and king, how pharaohs appeared in painting and sculpture, what dynasties were, and what at least one pharaoh or queen did.
- **MA.6.T3c.5** (days 69): Describe how Egypt's social classes related to one another, from the pharaoh and priests to scribes, artisans, farmers and enslaved people.
- **MA.6.T3c.6** (days 69): Describe Egypt's polytheistic religion, including beliefs about death, proper behavior, the afterlife, mummification and the roles of the gods.
- **MA.6.T3c.7** (days 69): Summarize key achievements of the Old, Middle and New Kingdoms, such as farming systems, mathematics, astronomy, the calendar, papyrus, hieroglyphs and the Giza pyramids.
- **MA.6.T3c.3** (days 69): Analyze the evidence archaeologists and historians use to understand ancient Nubia (the Kingdom of Kush), and compare its society and economy with Egypt's.
