**Teacher notes for sensitive content.** Name slavery as ownership and forced labor; captives had lives and agency beyond their captivity. Giza's organized workforce challenges the all-enslaved story without proving every worker freely chose service. Teach religion as history, with no declarations recited by students, devotional practice, personal faith disclosures, mummy making or victim and conqueror role play. Egyptian and Kushite communities were African and internally diverse; painted skin is not a modern racial census. Nubian peoples live today, and their languages are not interchangeable with Meroitic. Attribute modern claims to their particular community, without asking a child to represent it. If a stereotype or faith disagreement arises, stop the generalization, return to the dated evidence and allow a private written response. Discuss human remains with dignity through object descriptions rather than graphic images.
