**Sensitive-content note.** Slavery meant ownership and forced labor, not paid employment. Enslaved people had families, skills and agency beyond imposed work. Do not reenact capture, sale or forced rowing. A person's job does not establish legal status. Tsirkin distinguishes dependent service from enslavement and finds sailors' and oarsmen's status uncertain. Day 79 must teach that uncertainty, alongside slavery's documented presence; a specific enslaved crew requires dated evidence before publication. Do not infer that all rowers were enslaved or that all workers shared merchants' profits. Treat ancient colonies as settlements among existing peoples, not empty land. No student represents a modern community. Stop stereotypes and return to evidence; offer private written responses.
