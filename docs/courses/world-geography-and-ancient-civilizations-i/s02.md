## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Did the turn to farming make life better?"
**Hook, day 11.** Show the flint sickle insert, dated 3100 to 2650 BCE, beside an original grain-store drawing. Could stored food mean a better life? Keep answers provisional.
**Supporting questions, drafted together.** How do interpretations change (days 11 to 14)? What changed with farming, and for whom (days 15 to 18)? What makes societies complex (days 19 to 21)? How do societies meet (day 22)?
**Closing claim, day 23.** Answer from the Çatalhöyük settlement account and Larsen study summary. Explain a benefit, a cost and a continuity; name a competing claim. Different judgments about better are defensible.
**Teaching.** Source accounts before comparison. Model classification on day 12, comparison on day 13 and claim, evidence and reasoning before writing on day 14. Compare Irhoud's 2007 estimate around 160,000 years with 2017's roughly 315,000 years. Explain dating without calculations. Teach tools, fire, hunting, gathering, painting and sculpture on day 15. Day 16 locates independent farming origins in Southwest Asia, China, New Guinea, Africa and the Americas; names Jericho; distinguishes domestication from taming; and places later copper working before bronze and iron. Days 19 and 20 teach every trait. Day 21 tests supported, uncertain and absent evidence. On day 23 identify agreement between the settlement and health accounts before judging better; withdraw worked answers. Section 2.1 owns all parts.

- **Account B, reporting a revision:** "In 2018, researchers reported a human jaw from Misliya Cave in present-day Israel, associated with stone tools. Both accounts support modern human presence outside Africa; the exact age and route require more evidence." Source notes: Hershkovitz and colleagues, 2018; Sharp and Paces, 2018, abstracts. - **Accounts C and D, reporting farming research:** C: "Zeder's 2011 review describes domestication of crops and livestock in different parts of Southwest Asia, challenging one small region as the origin of everything."
