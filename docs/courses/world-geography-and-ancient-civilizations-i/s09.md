**Task 1 location key.** Africa straddles the equator; Atlantic west, Indian east, Mediterranean north. North Africa borders the Mediterranean; West occupies the western bulge, Central includes the Congo basin, East includes the Horn and Great Lakes, Southern lies below these. Sahara spans the northern interior; Sahel lies immediately south; savannas extend beyond equatorial rainforest belts. Kilimanjaro lies in northeastern Tanzania, Cape of Good Hope on South Africa's southwestern peninsula, Great Rift Valley through eastern Africa, and Lake Victoria between Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania. Accept reasonable regional boundaries; regions overlap and vegetation has local variation.
