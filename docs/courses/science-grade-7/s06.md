- **MS-ESS3-5:** Ask questions that make clear the evidence for what has caused global temperatures to rise over the last hundred years. Emphasis: the major part human activities play in the warming, weighed beside natural factors.
- **MS-ESS3-3:** Use science ideas to design a way to track and reduce one human impact on the environment.
- **MS-ESS3-4:** Argue from evidence how rising human population and resource use per person affect Earth's systems. Emphasis: science describes the results of population and consumption growth, but society, not science, decides what to do.
- **MS-ESS3-1:** Explain from evidence how past and present geoscience processes leave minerals, energy sources and groundwater unevenly spread across Earth. Emphasis: these resources are limited, mostly not renewable, and shifting as people remove them; examples are oil, metal ores and soil.
