- **MS-PS1-6:** Design, build, test and improve a device that gives off or takes in thermal energy through a chemical process. Emphasis: designing and changing the device, controlling energy transfer to the surroundings, using factors such as the kind and concentration of a substance (for example dissolving calcium chloride). Limit: Testing criteria are limited to the amount of substance, the time and the temperature.
- **MS-ETS1-4:** Build a model that produces data for repeated testing and changing of an object, tool or process until the design is as good as it can be.
