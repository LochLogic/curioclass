<!-- skel -->
- **MS-LS2-1:** Use data as evidence of how the amount of resources available affects single organisms and whole populations in an ecosystem. Emphasis: cause and effect between resources and the growth of organisms and population size when resources are plentiful or scarce.
- **MS-LS2-4:** Argue from evidence that changes to living or nonliving parts of an ecosystem change its populations. Emphasis: spotting patterns in data, drawing justified conclusions about population change, and judging the evidence behind arguments about ecosystem change.
- **MS-LS2-2:** Explain and predict patterns in how organisms interact, across many different ecosystems. Emphasis: predicting the same patterns (competition, predation, mutual benefit) among organisms and nonliving parts in different ecosystems.
- **MS-LS2-5:** Judge competing designs for protecting biodiversity and the services ecosystems give people.
- **MS-ETS1-1:** State a design problem's criteria and constraints clearly enough to lead to a good solution, considering science ideas and effects on people and nature.
