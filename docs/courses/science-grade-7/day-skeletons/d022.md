Stop for missing protection, irritation or spills. The teacher collects waste for locally permitted disposal, rinses equipment and supervises handwashing. No live closed-container trials.

Card-model days use one printed card set per group; mats are one for each student on day 2, three for each group on day 4 and one for each group on other card days; teacher-cut cards beforehand, avoid loose small counters and allow two minutes to collect paper. Paper work needs no eye protection.
