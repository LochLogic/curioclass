## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.2.T5.1** (days 37): Explain how natural resources in a place connect to local industries and jobs such as fishing, farming, or mining.
- **D2.Geo.9.K-2** (days 37): Describe how a place's physical environment connects to the economic activity found there.
- **MA.2.T5.2** (days 38): Tell the difference between renewable and non-renewable resources.
- **D2.Eco.4.K-2** (days 39, 40): Describe what goods and services the local community makes and what other communities make.
- **D2.Geo.8.K-2** (days 39): Compare how people in different kinds of communities use nearby and faraway environments to meet daily needs.
- **D2.Eco.7.K-2** (days 40): Describe examples of what it costs to produce something.
- **MA.2.T5.3** (days 41): Explain that people are a resource too, since skills and knowledge from school and work lead to new inventions and more goods and services.
- **D2.Eco.13.K-2** (days 41): Give examples of capital goods and human capital.
- skill ss2-resources-industries (grade 2)
- skill ss2-people-resource (grade 2)
