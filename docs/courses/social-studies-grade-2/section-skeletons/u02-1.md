## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.2.T2.1** (days 17, 18, 19): Locate all the continents and some major physical features on each one on a world map and a globe.
- **MA.2.T2.2** (days 20): Locate the world's oceans on a map and globe, and explain why oceans matter and help make the world livable.
- skill ss2-continents-features (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions.** Shape students' questions into: Where are these places (days 17 to 20)? How do land and water shape life (days 21 to 23)? What changes over time (days 24 and 25)? How can sources support our choice (days 26 and 27)?
> **Teaching.** Model locating on day 17, climate on day 21, comparison on day 24, and sourced reasoning on day 26. Step back on day 27. Revisit the question board daily. Say claims before writing; retain the frame until independent reasoning is demonstrated.
>
> **Carried.** Retain unit 1's key (read on the Atlas), directions and sourcing card. Read directions on day 17; locate independently by day 20. Ask who made a source, when and where on days 18, 19, 24, 25 and 26. Tie region to shared conditions on day 23; revise boundaries as features change. Revisit continuity and change on days 24 and 25. Add a named source to claim, evidence and reasoning on day 26; withdraw teacher composition on day 27, retaining transcription support.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Supply printed maps, feature labels, Place Cards, dated pairs, a self-check strip per child, pencils and paper. A class globe is required; with none, use a ball and record the shortfall. Globe locations are heard at the class globe, a rotating group at a time, across days 17 to 27; every child marks the flat map; paper globes are optional. All sources and tasks print. Spread day 19's rehearsal across days 17 to 20. At home, use the same cards and a globe or ball map; the adult records explanations. Show no student timers.
>
> **Language and extension.** Teach continent, ocean, feature, weather, climate, region and source with pictures and gestures. Rehearse "The source shows ... , so ... ." Extend by defending two different choices from the same evidence.
