## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D2.His.6.K-2** (days 68, 69, 70, 71): Compare different accounts of the same event from the past.
- **MA.2.T3.1** (days 69, 70): Investigate why people move to new places around the world, recognizing that some migration is chosen and some is forced.
- **D2.Geo.7.K-2** (days 70): Explain why and how people, goods and ideas travel from place to place.
- **D2.His.14.K-2** (days 70): Suggest possible reasons for a past event or development.
- **D2.His.16.K-2** (days 70, 71): Pick which reasons are more likely than others to explain a past event.
- **MA.2.T3.2** (days 70): Give examples of why the United States is called "a nation of immigrants."
- **D2.His.4.K-2** (days 70): Compare the views of people in the past with the views of people today.
- skill ss2-two-accounts (grade 2)
- skill ss2-claim-sources (grade 2)
- skill ss2-why-people-move (grade 2)
- skill ss2-nation-of-immigrants (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions.** Shape students' questions into: What reasons explain a move (58 and 59)? Who could stay, and who could not (60 to 62)? Whose history does the immigrant phrase describe or miss (63 and 64)? Did arrival feel the same to everyone (65 to 67)? Does agreement mean two witnesses (68 and 69)?
> **Closing claim.** On 69, children answer the question using Smith Life, Cherokee Today and Arrival Voices. They may argue that safety, family or several reasons matter most, with evidence and reasoning, while distinguishing choice from force.
> **Teaching.** Model the chain on 59, perspective comparison on 67 and the two-column account chart with a copied retelling on 68. On 69 model a sourced claim on a separate example before students compose independently. Revisit questions daily; source each text before using it: who, when, where, and why it was made.
>
> **Carried.** Keep unit 4's maps, sourcing card and claim frame. Tie the chain to because, moved and afterward on 59; withdraw filled examples on 62. Revise perspective and continuity-change charts on 67. Tie sourcing to account comparison on 68 and independence on 69. Retain these models. On 65 bridge grade 1's before-and-after ordering into dated cards; unit 6 develops the timeline. Withdraw teacher composition on 69, retaining transcription.
