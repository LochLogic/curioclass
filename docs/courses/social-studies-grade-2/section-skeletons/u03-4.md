## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.2.T2.4** (days 42, 43, 44, 45): Explain and describe how people interact with the physical environment, including why people settle near particular bodies of water and landforms.
- **D2.Geo.12.K-2** (days 42, 44): Identify ways a disaster can affect the people living in a place.
- **D2.Geo.5.K-2** (days 43, 44): Describe how what people do changes the cultural and environmental character of places.
- **D4.1.K-2** (days 43): Make an argument and give reasons for it.
- **D2.Geo.8.K-2** (days 44): Compare how people in different kinds of communities use nearby and faraway environments to meet daily needs.
- **MA.2.T5.1** (days 44): Explain how natural resources in a place connect to local industries and jobs such as fishing, farming, or mining.
- **MA.2.T5.2** (days 44, 45): Tell the difference between renewable and non-renewable resources.
- **D2.Eco.4.K-2** (days 44): Describe what goods and services the local community makes and what other communities make.
- **D2.Eco.7.K-2** (days 44): Describe examples of what it costs to produce something.
- **D2.Geo.9.K-2** (days 44): Describe how a place's physical environment connects to the economic activity found there.
- **MA.2.T5.3** (days 44): Explain that people are a resource too, since skills and knowledge from school and work lead to new inventions and more goods and services.
- **D2.Eco.13.K-2** (days 44): Give examples of capital goods and human capital.

- skill ss2-claim-sources (grade 2)
- skill ss2-resources-industries (grade 2)
- skill ss2-people-resource (grade 2)
