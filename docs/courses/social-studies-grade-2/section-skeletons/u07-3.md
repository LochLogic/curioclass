Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions.** Write together: What land and water are here (85)? What resources support people's work (86)? How do people live today, and what varies (87)? Which answers best support our claim (88)?
> **Closing claim, day 89.** Each child answers the compelling question using their country's Map, Land and Work page, and People Today page, naming two supporting facts and explaining the connection. Revise on 90; present on 91. Different claims can fit the evidence.
> **Teaching.** Model question connections on 83, source selection on 84 and claim writing on 88, using Japan. Step back during gathering and drafting. Teach before observing; retain prompts but remove completed examples on 89.
>
> **Carried.** Keep unit 6's sourcing card and claim frame. Revisit map-key and region on 85; tie map symbols to notes. Revisit opportunity-cost on 86 with an invented trade choice, without a new assessment. Review corroboration on 87: compare independently made records of one place; a copied caption is not another witness. Tie claim-evidence-reasoning to source labels on 88; withdraw filled examples on 89, retaining the frame only where needed. Other models remain available.
