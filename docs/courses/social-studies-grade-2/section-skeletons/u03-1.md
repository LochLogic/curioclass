## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.2.T2.4** (days 30, 31): Explain and describe how people interact with the physical environment, including why people settle near particular bodies of water and landforms.
- **D2.Geo.5.K-2** (days 30, 31): Describe how what people do changes the cultural and environmental character of places.
- **D2.Geo.8.K-2** (days 32): Compare how people in different kinds of communities use nearby and faraway environments to meet daily needs.
- **D2.Geo.12.K-2** (days 33): Identify ways a disaster can affect the people living in a place.
- skill ss2-people-environment (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions.** Shape students' questions into: Why live here (day 30)? How do communities meet needs (day 32)? How can we help our school prepare (days 34 to 36)? How do resources and learning support work (days 37 to 41)? What did our action accomplish (day 42)?
> **Teaching.** Model reading maps and captions on day 30, checking advice on day 34, resource chains on day 37, and sourced reasoning at the start of day 43. Step back during action and day 43. Source each document: who, when, where and why. Revisit the question board.
>
> **Carried.** Keep unit 2's map supports, sourcing card and claim frame. Revise region on day 31: similar conditions allow varied adaptations. Return on day 37 through regional work. Retain the region model; withdraw teacher composition on day 43, keeping transcription. The teacher page explains adaptation, modification and their overlap.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No specifications or asks. Supply printed sources, original diagrams, advice excerpt cards, Action Record, record sheet, poster paper, pencils and voting counters. All tasks print; one shared display is optional. Each 30-minute teaching core keeps five minutes to open, eight to teach, twelve to work and five for evidence; optional ten minutes extend discussion. Show no timers.
>
> **Action.** Days 34 to 36 use class-proposed options, a fair vote and school procedures; day 42 checks delivery and response. No drills or cleanup. At home, one child and adult compare options and deliver a household message in the advice card's own words; record the response.
>
> **Language.** Teach adapt, change, region, resource, renewable, industry, skill and source with pictures. Rehearse "The source shows ... , so ... ." Adults read captions; children read labels. Extend by defending a different choice.
