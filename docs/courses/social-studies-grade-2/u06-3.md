# Section 6.3: The closing claim, the check and the response

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


Grade 2 children read short labels; the teacher reads every card, passage and prompt aloud, and the whole day 80 packet. Answers are circled, boxed, tokened, said, drawn or a few written words; the claim is said first, then recorded with a frame, with transcription support only. Days run 30 to 35 minutes. Pip says each wrong idea, one line at a time. The Orsa family, Odel and the Eddyford-style cards are invented and printed "invented for practice". Real sources appear only as Curio Class summaries in our words with one credit line. No sources.csv rows exist for this course yet, so every real fact is marked "source to check". No child reports on home, family, money, religion or how they came here; any child may pass.

- **MA.2.T3.4 (days 79, 80, 81).** "Identify what people bring when they move, the effects of migration, and the elements that define a culture, and explain how all of a community's people enrich it." Day 79: the closing claim in pairs and the exit. Day 80: item 3. Day 81: reteach and a fresh task.
- **MA.2.T3.3 (days 79, 80).** "Interview family, neighbors, friends, or school staff ..." Day 79: revise the interview record from the written cue. Day 80: item 1, scored from the retained day 75 observation and the day 79 revised record, not from a first attempt.
- **D2.His.12.K-2 (days 80, 81).** "Ask questions about a source as it relates to a particular event or development." Day 80: item 2 on Memory M and Photograph P. Day 81: a fresh memory.
- **Limits that bite here.** A claim may stress that the newcomers changed more, that the place did, or that these sources cannot measure which; it names a source and never ranks cultures. Unknown or declined answers are accurate recording, never a gap to fill. A photograph shows what is visible, not what was said or felt. Native nations are living governments. Scores are kept apart: question, can, cannot; each topic; each element; each change.
- **Connections.** The claim frame of unit 5 (day 69) and unit 4: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." The self-check strip, the sitting and the respond day (unit 5, days 70 and 71). Item 1 draws on the interview record from 6.1; item 3's community part on 6.2's day 78 explanation, revised on day 79.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How the three parts of the record are scored apart; the held-back packet (Memory M, a Curio Class retelling of a 1912 memoir, and Photograph P, a 1912 school-group photograph; each fact matched to the original before layout, any not found cut, source to check); that the day 79 claim is practice and the day 80 claim alone is scored; how to read the packet aloud neutrally; the four groups on day 81.

## 2. Where it sits


Follows 6.2, whose day 78 explanation and day 75 and 76 records come back with written cues on day 79. Day 80 is the unit check; day 81 responds. After it, unit 7 opens on day 82 with retrieval only (the next unit reserves no readiness check). Results go on the record sheet.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** item 1 (MA.2.T3.3) and item 3 (MA.2.T3.4). Item 2 (D2.His.12.K-2) is owned by 6.2; the day 80 sitting holds items 2 and 3 on paper. Item 1 is scored from retained records, with no paper minutes. The key and wrong answers of items 1 and 3 are in the unit assessment rows above; item 2's are in unit plan field 5.

**Sitting, day 80.** 25 minutes of individual work, the packet read aloud inside each item (item 2, 8 minutes; item 3, 17 minutes), then 5 minutes to collect and reflect. Each part is scored shown, shown with help or not yet seen: item 1 as four topic parts (where, how, why, when), asking and a follow-up, a faithful record and a dated event; item 2 as a question, one thing the source tells and one it cannot; item 3 as two elements, what was brought, one change to the newcomers, one to community life and a claim with a named source. A child who cannot mark says the line to the teacher, at most 4 children at about 1 minute each while the rest work, named on the record sheet.

**Exit questions.** Each child marks once in the last 3 minutes; the teacher reads each ask aloud, one ask at a time. A "(six)" item is heard from the rotating six, about 45 seconds each, in the pairs work. Rule 4 holds.
- **X79 (day 79).** Mark, paper: Invented for practice. The Orsa family moves to Hollis Lane. They keep singing their own songs. They learn the street's words for things. They teach the neighbors a new song. Three pictures: the Orsas saying the street's words, the neighbors singing the new song, the Orsas singing their own songs. Put a token on the one picture that shows something that did not change. Answer: The Orsas singing their own songs. Wrong: The token is on the Orsas saying the street's words (F); The token is on the neighbors singing the new song (F).
- **X79b (day 79).** Spoken, the six: Invented for practice. On the Orsa card, say one thing that changed, starting: The Orsa card shows ___. Answer: A change the card gives, such as The Orsa card shows the neighbors learned a new song, or The Orsa card shows the Orsas learned the street's words. Wrong: I think everything changed because I like it (H); The Orsa card shows the Orsas were sad (H).
- **X81 (day 81).** Mark, paper: Invented for practice. A memory says: I wove cloth in my old town. Here I learned my neighbors' words and taught them to weave. Two pictures: weaving, the neighbors' words. Put a token on the one the writer brought. Answer: Weaving. Wrong: The token is on the neighbors' words (F).
- **X81b (day 81).** Spoken, the six: Invented for practice. On the weaving memory, ask one question that helps us learn about life after the move. Answer: A question about the memory, such as How did you learn those words? Wrong: What is your favorite color? (D); It shows everyone learned weaving (I).

## 6. Days

## Day 79


- **3 min. Return and repair.** The letter most missed from day 78 (F, G, K, L or M) with a fresh card from the repair cards, day 79 (unit 6); any other letter goes to a small group at the supported work.
- **6 min. A claim, modeled.** A separate invented move: Odel moves to a cold town, bakes with the town's berries and teaches neighbors her bread (the Odel cards). The teacher says and records "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." The model says both changed and that these cards cannot measure which changed more. Pip says H.
- **12 min. The closing claim.** Each pair answers "When people move to a new place, do they change the place more, or does the place change them more?" from the Posadas Card (Music Here), Language Today and a line of the day 74 account or a record a teller allowed to share, using the claim frame from unit 5. The model is covered. A pair may stress either change, or say the sources cannot measure which; it names a source; it never ranks cultures. A child may use Language Today instead of Music Here. Said first, then recorded with transcription support only. The rotating six are heard on X79b (about 4.5 minutes): "On the Orsa card, say one thing that changed." Practice, not scored.
- **8 min. Supported work.** The written cues come back. 3 minutes: revise the interview record and the dated event on the dated strip (unit 6) from the cue. 5 minutes: revise the day 78 community explanation. Both versions are kept and only the revision is scored. A small group of at most 6 meets the teacher for a repair letter.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** X79 on paper; X79b was heard while the pairs worked.
- **3 min. Question board.** The unit's question is answered aloud once and the board is closed.

- **Print and before class.** Print: Odel cards, repair cards, day 79 (unit 6), dated strip (unit 6), culture chart, seven parts (kept up), exit cards, days 79 to 81 (unit 6) (the day 79 card only), question board, unit 6, record sheet (unit 6). Before class: the returned pages with written cues; the claim frame on the wall as a blank.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The modeled claim shares X79's shape, a family that keeps something and changes something. Changed so it does not copy the exit: Odel's cards give berries and bread, never songs, a street or words for things, and the model's filled claim is withdrawn before the pairs work. Change the practice, never the exit.

Who holds what is carried: each pair holds the frame and three source cards; each child holds a returned page; the teacher holds the model claim. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, recording, tokens. Evidence: X79, X79b; the claim and revisions are practice and then scored on day 80.

## Day 80


- **8 min. Item 2, the memory and the photograph.** Both read aloud, neutrally, once; the photograph shown in large print. Each child asks one question about a source of their choice (item 8), then says one thing it can tell (item 9) and one it cannot (item 10), on paper, by pointing, drawing or dictation.
- **17 min. Item 3.** The memory read again. Each child names two different parts of culture in it (items 11 and 12), says one part of the family's culture that they kept in their new place (item 13), one change to the newcomers (item 14) and one to community life (item 15), says one thing newcomers add (item 16) and one thing long-time families add (item 17), uses the nation card for what a Native nation adds today (item 18), says one thing a newcomer family and a long-time family could do together (item 19), and writes a claim that names the memory or the photograph (item 20), said first, then recorded with a frame and transcription support only. No timers are shown.
- **5 min. Collection and self-check.** Papers collected; each child marks the self-check strip with one piece of work that supports the mark.

- **Print and before class.** Print: day 80 packet (unit 6) (large-print copies for any child who needs them), self-check strip (unit 6), culture chart, seven parts (sampled, kept up), record sheet (unit 6). Before class: the packet stays held back until this day; the photograph needs a person to look at the image and describe it plainly before layout (planned only, not yet written).
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The sitting samples the unit's shapes on new cases: a question and what a source can and cannot tell (the day 76 line), parts of culture (day 72), what was brought and two changes (days 77 and 78) and a claim (day 79). No piece of the unit prints the packet's cases. Change the practice, never the exit.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the packet and the frame. Tool: None. By hand: pointing, circling, boxing, short writing. Evidence: items 8 to 20 here; items 1 to 7 from the retained day 75 round and the day 79 revised record. Items 1 to 7 and the community part of item 3 take no day 80 minutes.

## Day 81


- **5 min. Feedback.** Each child's day 80 work and day 79 claim come back with a written cue and a picture of the part to try next.
- **15 min. Grouped reteaching.** Four groups, about 3.5 minutes each (4, 4, 4 and 3), in the order of most missed: items 1 to 7, items 8 to 10, items 11 to 15 and 20, and items 16 to 19, as grouped in field 4. - **Print and before class.** Print: fresh cards, day 81 (unit 6), written move strip, day 81 (unit 6), exit cards, days 79 to 81 (unit 6) (the day 81 card only), self-check strip (unit 6), record sheet (unit 6). Before class: order the four groups from the day 80 record.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** Group 3's fresh memory shares X81's shape, something brought and something learned. Changed so it does not copy the exit: it gives a flatbread recipe and a cheese, never weaving or the neighbors' words. Change the practice, never the exit.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds returned work; the teacher holds the groups. Tool: None. By hand: marking, pairs work. Evidence: X81, X81b.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: "Which source shows that, and what does it say?" (day 79); "Can these two sources measure which changed more?" (day 79); "What can this memory not tell us?" (days 80 and 81).
- Written task, day 79: the closing claim, said in pairs and recorded with the frame, with transcription support only; practice. Day 80's claim is made alone.
- Judged by the teacher with four look-fors: answers the question or says it cannot be measured; names a source; says one change to the newcomers and one to the place; does not rank a culture. Marked shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Meets: "Music Here shows gatherings were shorter after the move, and Language Today shows a nation teaching its language today. Both changed in some way. These two sources cannot measure which changed more." Does not: "Moving is good because I say so."

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with a picture: culture, claim, evidence, source, changed, kept, teller, memory. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "The ___ shows ___, made by ___." "The source says ___; it cannot tell ___." Home-language rehearsal allowed; the item may be said, drawn or dictated in any language.
- **Access.** Large print; described pictures and photograph; a child may point, draw or dictate; a child who cannot hold a pencil places tokens and speaks.
- **Check accommodations.** The packet is read aloud, neutrally; pointing, drawing, dictation and home-language answers are accepted; historical reasoning is scored apart from English production; a child who passes in public answers to the teacher alone; no timers are shown.
- **Reading load.** Labels only, about 60 words; the teacher reads the cards and the packet (about 150 words on day 80).
- **Extension, same standard.** "What other source would check the memory, and who could tell it for themselves?"

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 79: model Odel's claim, then the child says a claim from the cards; revise the record from the cue. Day 80: read the packet at the table and mark together without hints. Day 81, the last day of the unit: talk through each miss, try the same move on a fresh card, and end by saying the answer to the unit's question in the child's own words.
- **One projector.** Show one card at a time enlarged; the child marks on paper. The frame can be drawn on the board.
