# Section 3.2: Checking advice and helping our school

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-2/3/s/3.2), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-2 u03-2.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 2 children read short labels; the teacher reads every card, message, credit and prompt aloud. Answers are circled, pointed to, said, drawn or a few written words. Days run 36 to 37 minutes. The Advice Cards are ours: each is at most three short sentences written from a published town or state page, with the page name, date and link as its source line (source to check; the wording follows the checked page and no rule is ever invented). The school's own town or state advice is found by the teacher before day 34 and used first if it exists; the Vermont page named in the unit plan is the fallback example, said aloud to be another state's. Practice cases are invented and printed "invented for practice". Pip the puppet says each wrong idea, one line at a time. The class chooses its way to help; the course picks no cause.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **D2.Geo.12.K-2, this section's share (days 34, 35, 36).** "Identify ways a disaster can affect the people living in a place." Here the child explains, from a card, one effect on people and one help, then revises it after written feedback. The revised version is item 4's evidence.
- **MA.2.T2.4 (days 34, 35, 36).** "Explain and describe how people interact with the physical environment, including why people settle near particular bodies of water and landforms." Here people get ready for a flood by checking what they say against published advice, then act through the school.
- **Limits that bite here.** No drills, no cleanup, no engineering. Every line of a message comes from a checked Advice Card; the class never makes up a safety rule. The action goes through school procedures and the principal's yes before day 34's options are shown, and the receiving class is asked first. A child who has lived through a flood is never asked; the quiet task (matching helper pictures) is always open. At home nothing is reported.
- **Connections.** Day 33's practice explanation (3.1). Grade 1: an action step and the decision record (review); kindergarten's fair vote (review). Unit 2: the three questions (who made it, when, where) on each card, and "The source shows ___, so ___". D4.1 returns on day 43.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** What published advice is, where to find the town's or state's page, and why a poster says what a checked page says and names the page. How the two feedback loops run: a cue on day 33's practice returned on day 34, and written feedback on day 34's explanation returned before day 35 (about 20 minutes each evening for 30 children, one picture cue or one line each). A vote chooses; it does not show the action worked.

## 2. Where it sits


Follows 3.1 (day 33's practice explanations come back with a cue on day 34) and leads to 3.3, then to day 42's look-back (3.4), about two weeks after day 36. Item 4's revised explanations are kept here and read on day 44. Results go on the record sheet.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** item 4 (D2.Geo.12.K-2) with section 3.1, and the two daily rows above. Item 4 scores only the revised explanation of day 35 or 36, in three parts (an effect on people, a help, the card named), each shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Both earlier versions are kept. No written disaster item is asked on day 44. A missing part stays not yet seen.

**Exit questions.** Every child marks one picture in the last two minutes; the teacher reads the prompt aloud. The day's rotating six (about 45 seconds each, one spoken ask) are heard in the supported-work block. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example. Every case is new (rule 4).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The three questions on a card (who made it, when, where made) are asked of each Advice Card. "The source shows ___, so ___" says why a message is checked. The fair-vote model from kindergarten and grade 1 (listen, ask why, look for a change both accept, vote only if still apart; a counter in a closed cup or a pass) runs on day 35. The Action Record starts on day 36, is filled with delivery and response on day 42, and is read on day 43. The question board gets "How can we help our school prepare?" on day 34.
- **Key moment 1, day 34: matching a message to a line of the card.** Ideal instrument: the card on a screen with a pointer. Spec: fits; a printed card and a pencil are enough.
- **Key moment 2, day 35: a fair vote.** Ideal instrument: a closed cup for each option and a token for each child. Spec: fits; paper cups, counters (30 tokens).
- **A room with nothing.** Printed cards, paper cups, counters, poster paper, markers, pencils. Tools: None. Supplies for the poster plan: six sets of markers for six groups of five.

## 6. Days

## Day 34


- **5 min. Return and repair.** D (from X33): Creek Road (invented) linked to a daily need.
- **3 min. Cue return.** Each child receives the one-line picture cue on the day 33 practice explanation and adds or says one thing.
- **9 min. Reading published advice.** Advice Card A, read aloud with its page name, date and link (the school's own town or state page if it exists; source to check). Model the check: say a message, find the line that says the same, name the card. Pip's made-up rule beside it: Pip's wrong idea K. The board gets "How can we help our school prepare?"
- **6 min. Ways to help.** The class names ways to help the school get ready; the teacher sorts them onto at most three option cards the school allows, for example a poster or a short announcement to another class. A way the school cannot allow is said kindly and set aside; the course picks no cause.
- **12 min. Supported work.** First 4 minutes, all children: a reserved Recovery Card 2 caption (ours, from the city recovery account; source to check) is read aloud; each child draws or labels one effect on people, one help and the card's name. The teacher keeps these and writes feedback before day 35. Next 8 minutes: the six heard (about 5 minutes); the small group (5 minutes); the rest, in pairs, match message cards to lines on Advice Card B.
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** X34 mark; X34b spoken to the six.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The day 34 match of message cards to lines on Advice Card B shares X34b's shape, find the line that says the same. Changed so they do not copy it: practice uses Card B and four messages (one with no line), and the exit uses Card C, Poster 1 and two say-so posters that are shown nowhere in practice. Pip's made-up rule (clap three times) is a say-so with no card, not the exit's Mr. Ray. Creek Road shares X33b's shape (a closed road, who is affected); it follows that exit as a repair.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the check steps; children say "the card says". Tool: None. By hand: drawing, circling. Evidence: X34 and X34b, and the day 34 explanation for items E9 to E11.

## Day 35


- **5 min. Return and repair.** K (from X34): three message cards, a say-so, a liking and a card-checked line; the checked one is found.
- **4 min. A fair choice.** Retrieval of kindergarten's and grade 1's vote: listen, ask why, look for a change both accept; a counter in a closed cup or a pass.
- **8 min. Check each option.** In pairs, for each option card the pair reads the lines on Advice Cards A and B and says "a line says this" or "no line says this". An option no line supports is reworded to what a line says or set aside; nothing is invented.
- **6 min. Vote.** Each child puts one counter in the cup of one option or passes (30 children, about 3 minutes in a line, 1 minute to count). Two children say a reason for an option that did not win. The result goes on the Action Record. Pip's wrong idea E.
- **11 min. Supported work.** First 4 minutes, all children: revise the day 34 explanation from the written feedback. Then the six heard (about 5 minutes); the small group (5 minutes); the rest continue the revision or draw their job for tomorrow.
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** X35 mark; X35b spoken to the six.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** Checking each option against Advice Cards A and B shares X35's shape, find the line that says the same. Changed so they do not copy it: practice uses Cards A and B only; Card D, and its lines on barriers, officials, radio and clean water, are printed on the exit and nowhere before. The three message cards of the day 35 repair (a say-so, a liking, a checked line) share X34's shape and come after that exit as a repair. The vote step shares X35b's idea (a vote chooses); the exit asks what to check later and the taught step does not.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the fair-vote steps; each child holds a counter. Tool: None. By hand: counters, revising, circling. Evidence: X35 and X35b, and the revised explanation (items E9 to E11).

## Day 36


- **5 min. Return and repair.** E (from X35): three Action Record pictures sorted into "we chose", "we did" and "people answered".
- **4 min. Jobs.** Each child picks a job picture (make a panel, say a line, hold the card, keep the record); the teacher keeps a list so all 30 have one.
- **10 min. Make or rehearse.** The chosen way, using only lines from Advice Cards A and B. Poster plan: six groups of five each make one panel (a drawing, one checked line and the card's name). Announcement plan: six children say one checked line each (about 20 seconds each) while the others rehearse listening and hold the card.
- **6 min. Do it.** The panels are hung where the school allows, or the announcement is said in the other class. The Response Box (an envelope and picture slips for "I read it", "I heard it" and a few words) stays with that class's teacher, who returns it by day 42.
- **10 min. Supported work.** The six heard (about 5 minutes); everyone completes the revised explanation (4 minutes, overlapping). The small group (5 minutes).
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** X36 mark; X36b spoken to the six.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The job step shares X36's shape, a picture of a job. The exit asks what happened, so the Action Record job pictures are the exit's own tool; practice, such as the three Action Record pictures of the day 36 repair (we chose, we did, people answered), uses other pictures and the exit has no wrong answer. The two-week check shares X36b's shape; the repair sorts pictures and names no check.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds a job; the teacher holds the Response Box. Tool: None. By hand: panels, speaking, recording. Evidence: X36 and X36b, and the revised explanation (items E9 to E11). A vote alone is not completion.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 34: "Which line of the card says that, and who made the page?" Day 35: "How did we hear everyone?" Day 36: "What can we check later to see whether people read it?"
- **Written task, days 34 to 36.** The explanation of the Recovery Card 2 caption: "The ___ shows ___ (an effect on people). It shows ___ (a help)." First version day 34, revised day 35 or 36. A child who cannot write dictates.
- **Judged by the teacher with three look-fors:** (1) an effect on people, not only a damaged thing; (2) a help; (3) the card named. Meets (form only; the facts follow the checked card): "Roads were closed, so people could not get to shops. Neighbors cleaned up together. Recovery Card 2 shows this." Does not: "The flood was bad."

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 34.** Day 33's effects and helps; the three questions. **Day 35.** Day 34's check steps; kindergarten's fair vote. **Day 36.** Day 35's vote and the advice lines; the Action Record.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: advice, message, check, vote, source, help. Frames: "The card says ___." "I vote for ___ because ___." "Our message is checked because ___." Pointing first; judge reasoning apart from English.
- **Access.** Large counters; a partner reads a card line for a child who cannot see it; a child may pass in every vote; any job picture can be done with support. A child who cannot speak aloud holds the card or keeps the record.
- **Check accommodations.** Read prompts neutrally; accept pointing, speech or a scribe; enlarge cards. Do not assess handwriting. Record the support used.
- **Reading load.** An Advice Card is at most three short sentences read aloud; children read labels; at most 150 words a child reads in a day.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child who has revised early checks a second message against Advice Card B and says which line supports it.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 34: read one advice card together and point to a line. Day 35: pick one of two ways to help and say which line supports it. Day 36: make one small panel of a checked line, naming the card. At home (15 to 30 minutes, any day skippable): one child and one adult compare two ways to tell the household about a flood and deliver a message in the card's own words; the adult records any response aloud. Nothing about the home is reported to the class.
- **One projector.** Show each card on the screen; each child still marks a paper copy.
