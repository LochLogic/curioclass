# Section 3.3: Resources, work and learning

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-2/3/s/3.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-2 u03-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 2 children read short labels; the teacher reads every card, caption and prompt aloud. Answers are circled, lined, sorted, said, drawn or a few written words. Days run 36 minutes. Every place, worker, firm and chain is ours and invented (Gull Cove, Wide Plain, Iron Hill, Tall Pine and the rest), printed "invented for practice" and said aloud to be made up; no real firm, brand or figure appears. The only real lines are the plain facts about renewal and the local profile line (source to check: the federal energy and agriculture pages named in the unit plan, not yet checked). No child reports a family job, money or shopping. Pip the puppet says each wrong idea, one line at a time.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **MA.2.T5.1 (day 37).** "Explain how natural resources in a place connect to local industries and jobs such as fishing, farming, or mining." A resource, the industry and a job, for four invented places and our own region. **D2.Geo.9.K-2 (day 37).** "Describe how a place's physical environment connects to the economic activity found there."
- **MA.2.T5.2 (day 38).** "Tell the difference between renewable and non-renewable resources." Trees, wind and river water against oil, coal and iron.
- **D2.Eco.4.K-2 (days 39, 40).** "Describe what goods and services the local community makes and what other communities make." **D2.Geo.8.K-2 (day 39).** "Compare how people in different kinds of communities use nearby and faraway environments to meet daily needs." Day 39 follows one food, near and far; day 32's scoring stays with 3.1.
- **D2.Eco.7.K-2 (day 40).** "Describe examples of what it costs to produce something." Materials, equipment and workers' time.
- **MA.2.T5.3 (day 41).** "Explain that people are a resource too, since skills and knowledge from school and work lead to new inventions and more goods and services." **D2.Eco.13.K-2 (day 41).** "Give examples of capital goods and human capital." In grade 2 words: equipment and learned skills.
- **Limits that bite here.** A place offers possibilities; not everyone there does one job. Renewable does not mean unlimited: trees renew when planted at the pace they are cut. Metals can be reused without making new ore. A human resource is what people know and can do, never a person's worth. No household purchases; no brand names.
- **Connections.** Kindergarten K-ESS3-1 (people use what the land gives), retrieved day 37. Grade 1: job, income, tool, skill and goods and services (review, days 37, 40, 41). Unit 2's region (day 37); 3.1's near and far (day 39).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Natural resource, industry (all the work that makes or gets one kind of thing), renewable and non-renewable; producer and consumer; good and service; cost; equipment and learned skill, plain for adults; why geography is a possibility and not a fate; why melting an old metal object makes no new ore.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: resource, job, industry, renew, good, service, cost, tool, skill. Frames: "___ is a resource. It supports the ___ job." "___ can grow again." "A maker pays for ___." Pointing first; judge reasoning apart from English.
- **Access.** Tactile strips and raised-line chains for a child who cannot see; a partner moves cards for a child who cannot; a scribe writes the child's own words.
- **Check accommodations.** Read prompts neutrally; accept pointing, lines, speech or a scribe; enlarge pictures. Record the support used.
- **Reading load.** Card labels of one to three words; at most 150 words a child reads in a day; every caption is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child secure on day 38 explains why a forest can be renewable and still run low.
