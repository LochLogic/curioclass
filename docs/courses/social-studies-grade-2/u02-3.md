# Section 2.3: One region at two dates

<!-- skel -->
Grade 2: Places and Peoples of the World. Unit 2, Land, water and climate. *Where on Earth would you choose to live, and why?* (days 17 to 29): see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 24 to 25.** Days by kind: application 2 (24, 25). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 3 of 4 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 30 to 40 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Grade 2 children read short labels; the teacher reads every caption, credit and prompt aloud. Answers are circled, boxed, pointed to or a few written words. Days run 39 to 40 minutes, each with a 12-minute supported-work block. The Aletsch pair is real and credited (NASA Earth Observatory); every other card is ours, invented, and printed "invented for practice". Pip the puppet says each wrong idea, one line at a time. Change is told at grade level without alarm.

## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->

- skill ss2-climate-regions (grade 2)

> **Supporting questions.** Shape students' questions into: Where are these places (days 17 to 20)? How do land and water shape life (days 21 to 23)? What changes over time (days 24 and 25)? How can sources support our choice (days 26 and 27)?

>
> **Carried.** Retain unit 1's key (read on the Atlas), directions and sourcing card. Read directions on day 17; locate independently by day 20. <!-- /skel -->

- **D2.Geo.10.K-2, this section's share (days 24, 25).** "Describe changes in the physical and cultural features of world regions." Day 24: one region at two dates, a physical change (a glacier that shrank), what changed, what stayed and one reason from a named source. Day 25: another world region, a town street at two dates, a cultural change (what is built, how people travel, what signs say), with what stayed. Item 5 (day 28) is owned here.
- **Limits that bite here.** A physical feature and a cultural feature are told apart by what made the change (land, water, ice, plants; or what people built, did or wrote). A cause is taken from a printed source, never from two pictures. A photograph shows one moment, and each card prints its date. No alarm: the Aletsch change is slow, real and the reason is told once; children are not asked to feel guilty or to fix it, and no student is asked about their own place. A street changed is not a street made worse or better, and no region is ranked above another. Old and new views of a town show people living and working there, never a place "stuck in the past".
- **Connections.** Grade 1: the dated source and "changed, stayed the same" with the then-and-now chart (review, days 24 and 25), and kindergarten's before and after. Unit 1: the three questions on a card (days 24 and 25). Section 2.2: a region (day 24 places Europe, day 25 another region) and F (day 24).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Aletsch Glacier, in the Alps of Europe: NASA's two images are 2 September 1984 and 6 August 2024, a month apart, so children compare ice, not snow (NASA page read, 2026-11-20: credit Michala Garrison and United States Geological Survey data). The reason card is our wording from the Swiss Glacier Bulletin 2025, page 10: summers in the Alps have grown warmer, and warm summers melt more ice than winter snow adds (page 10 of the bulletin, read). Day 25's street pair is ours and invented (see day 25); the real Main Street, Brattleboro, photographs are not printed, so no list of their changes is given. A page of a town's own or its nation's own words is added only if a source is read.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 24: "What changed, what stayed, and how do we know why?" Day 25: "Which change is physical and which is cultural?"
- **Written task, day 25.** On the then-and-now chart, write or draw one change, one thing that stayed and a date for each picture, a few words. A child who cannot write dictates it.
- **Judged by the teacher with three look-fors:** (1) a change and a continuity are both on the chart and fit the pictures; (2) each picture carries its date and no old picture is called today; (3) a reason is taken from a card, never guessed from the pictures.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 24.** Grade 1's dated source and the then-and-now chart; F's repair; unit 1's three questions. **Day 25.** Day 24's chart; section 2.2's region; the globe for the continent.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: changed, stayed the same, then, now, date, glacier, ice, street, bridge. Frames: "It changed from ___ to ___." "It stayed the same: ___." "The card says ___." Pointing and home-language answers first.
- **Access.** Describe each image in words for a child who cannot see; tactile outlines of the street; the teacher boxes or circles for a child who cannot.
- **Check accommodations.** Prompts read aloud; circle, point, scribe; captions read aloud, never explained; record the support used.
- **Reading load.** Labels of one to three words and dates; captions of at most three short sentences read aloud; at most 150 words a child reads in a day.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child secure on day 25 says how two different changes could share one reason or have two.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Two printed dated pictures of one place. Say what changed, what stayed and what the card says about why. Then the same with a street. The wrong ideas are said by a toy.
- **One projector.** Show the pair side by side with the dates; each child still marks their own paper copy.
