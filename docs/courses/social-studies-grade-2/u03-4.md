# Section 3.4: Looking back, a claim and the check

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-2/3/s/3.4), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-2 u03-4.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 2 children read short labels; the teacher reads every card, caption, prompt and the whole day 44 packet aloud. Answers are circled, pointed to, said, drawn or a few written words; the claim is said first, then recorded with a frame, with transcription support only. Days run 34 to 36 minutes. The Action Record is the class's own; Room 6's record, the Hill Path cases and every fresh card are ours and invented, printed "invented for practice". The Cedar Inlet packet is held back and unseen until day 44; Day 43's sources are the Terraced Field photograph (source to check) and the Changing Land Cards of 3.1. Pip the puppet says each wrong idea, one line at a time.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **MA.2.T2.4, this section's share (days 42 to 45).** "Explain and describe how people interact with the physical environment, including why people settle near particular bodies of water and landforms." Day 42: what happened after the class acted. Day 43: whether to change land, adapt or both. Day 44: item 1. Day 45: a fresh try.
- **D2.Geo.12.K-2 (days 42, 44).** "Identify ways a disaster can affect the people living in a place." Day 42 reads the action; day 44 reads only the revised explanations of 3.2.
- **D2.Geo.5.K-2 (days 43, 44).** "Describe how what people do changes the cultural and environmental character of places." Change, adapt or both for a stated place; item 2.
- **D4.1.K-2 (day 43).** "Make an argument and give reasons for it." The reason names its source; item 8. The other codes on days 44 and 45 are sampled from 3.1 and 3.3 and owned there.
- **Limits that bite here.** No claim that the poster or announcement prevented flooding. A claim is a choice among change, adapt or both with a reason; every supported choice meets. No child is asked what their family does. Day 44 is a check: nothing is taught and no repair is made on it.
- **Connections.** Unit 2's claim frame "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___" (day 43, review). Grade 1: a reason for a choice (retrieval, day 43). 3.1's Changing Land Cards; 3.2's Action Record. Unit 4 reuses region, resources and skills.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Looking back is not scoring the class: an action that got no answer is still a record. A tradeoff is one good and one hard thing that come together. Marking is shown, shown with help or not yet seen by part, never averaged; day 44's thirds; the day 45 groups come from the record sheet. Day 46 and 47 take what is still not shown.

- **Judged by the teacher with four look-fors:** (1) a choice of change, adapt or both for a stated place; (2) a reason about land, water, weather or how people live; (3) a named source and its maker; (4) one good and one hard thing. Meets: "I think people at Stilt Bay should adapt because the water rises. The Place Card shows houses on posts, made by Curio Class. A good thing is dry floors; a hard thing is climbing steps." Does not: "People should change the land because I like it."

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: action, record, slip, choose, adapt, change, tradeoff, source. Frames: "I think people should ___ because ___." "The ___ shows ___." "One good thing is ___; one hard thing is ___." Pointing and home-language answers first; judge reasoning apart from English.
- **Access.** A scribe writes the child's own words; raised-line and described cards; a partner holds a card for a child who cannot. Day 44 may be sat in two short parts for a child who tires.
- **Check accommodations.** Read prompts and the packet neutrally; accept pointing, speech, drawing or a scribe; enlarge packet pictures. Do not assess handwriting or spelling. Record the support used.
- **Reading load.** Labels of one to three words; frames of at most nine words; at most 150 words a child reads in a day.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child secure on day 43 defends a second choice from the same two sources.
