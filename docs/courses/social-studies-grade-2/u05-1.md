# Section 5.1: Why people move, and the chain


<!-- skel -->
Grade 2: Places and Peoples of the World. Unit 5, People on the move. *Why do people leave home for a new place?* (days 58 to 71): see [s05.md](s05.md). **Days 58 to 59.** Days by kind: understanding 2 (58, 59). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 1 of 4 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 30 to 40 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Grade 2 children read short labels; the teacher reads every card, caption and story aloud. Answers are circled, pointed to, sorted, said or a few written words. Days run 35 minutes. Pip the puppet says each wrong idea, one line at a time; one actor in each story scene. Teo, Dara, Juno, Imre, Pia, Ravi, Hale, Nell, Orla, Bram, Sela, Tavi, Mina and every place in a story card are invented: the teacher says so aloud and each card is printed "invented for practice". Real people and places appear only as Curio Class summaries in our words with one credit line each; every real fact is marked "source to check" until its page is opened in full (this course has no sources.csv yet). No child is asked about their own family, home, money, religion or how they came here; any child may pass.

- **MA.2.T3.1, this section's share (days 58, 59).** "Investigate why people move to new places around the world, recognizing that some migration is chosen and some is forced." Day 58 gives the possible reasons (work, family, safety, land, freedom). Day 59 gives chosen and forced, with one test: "Could they have stayed?" Harder cases come in 5.2.
- **D2.His.14.K-2 (day 58).** "Suggest possible reasons for a past event or development." A reason is possible when a card gives a line for it; it is not certain unless the teller says so.
- **D2.His.16.K-2 (day 59).** "Pick which reasons are more likely than others to explain a past event." One better-supported reason, with the line that supports it.
- **Limits that bite here.** No child tells a family story. No legal status, policy or numbers. A reason is a possible one, never "everyone moves for ___". Fleeing an unsafe place is forced even when the family picks the road (5.2 returns to it). One real person, Anna, appears, as a summary.
- **Connections.** Unit 4: the origin-to-destination arrow (day 58; review of u04-2) and the three questions on a card (day 58; review of u01-2). Unit 2: the continents (day 58). Grade 1: first, then, so (day 59). Unit 2's claim frame "I think ___ because ___" (day 59).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** "Move" is the children's word; "migrate" is the standard's word, said and not tested. People move for several reasons at once; some push people from a place, some pull them to another (adult words only). A source can show a reason without showing the whole story: Anna's page tells of meeting family and does not say that was her only reason (source to check). The teacher says "the card does not say" aloud when it does not. Chosen does not mean free of pressure; forced does not mean no choice of road.

## 3. Checks, written first

<!-- skel -->
<!-- /skel -->

**Unit assessment items owned:** none. 5.1 teaches toward items 1, 3 and 4, which 5.2 and 5.4 own.

**Exit questions.** Each child marks once in the last 3 minutes; the teacher reads each ask aloud, one ask at a time. The rotating six (about 45 seconds each, one spoken ask) are heard in the pairs work. Every case is new (rule 4).
- **X58 (day 58).** Mark, paper: Invented for practice. On the Weaver Card, Sela hears that a far town needs weavers. Her sister lives in that town too. Sela moves there. Circle a reason the card gives for Sela's move: work, a storm, a visit. Answer: Work. Wrong: A storm is circled (K); A visit is circled (K).
- **X58b (day 58).** Spoken, the six: Invented for practice. The Weaver Card gives work and family as reasons. Say one reason a person might move that this card does not give. Answer: Any sensible reason, such as a storm, war, land, a school or freedom. Wrong: Everyone moves for work (A); Work, because the card says work (K).
- **X59 (day 59).** Mark, paper: Invented for practice. Card 1: Mina's family sells their stall and moves to a bigger market town to sell more. Card 2: A river rises over Wren Village and the water is not safe. The families must leave tonight. Circle the card that shows a chosen move. Answer: Card 1. Wrong: Card 2 is circled (C).
- **X59b (day 59).** Spoken, the six: Invented for practice. On the Land Card, Tavi's cousin visits each summer. Tavi says, My soil is thin; I want deep soil to grow food. Tavi moves to a river plain and plants a field. Say which reason the card supports better: deep soil to grow food, or the cousin's visits. Answer: Deep soil to grow food. Wrong: The cousin's visits (K).
- **X59c (day 59).** Spoken, the six: Invented for practice. On the Land Card, tell what happened in order: first the reason, then the move, then what happened next. Answer: Tavi wanted deep soil to grow food; Tavi moved to a river plain; Tavi planted a field. Wrong: Tavi planted a field, so Tavi moved (B); Tavi wanted to be near the sea (K).

## 6. Days

## Day 58

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Understanding. Meets new content: the goal is taught here, from a hook and a worked example.
- goal: Why people move to new places: work, family, safety, land, freedom; list the possible reasons for a move
- note: No child is asked to tell a family story. Sensitivity read (docs/pipeline/briefs/sensitivity-read.md) with the unit plan: immigration
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **4 min. Hook.** Anna Arrival, a Curio Class summary in our words with its credit line (Anna Zagar Klarich, interview EI-89, 12 September 1991, National Park Service Ellis Island oral histories): she crossed the sea and was met by family, with hugs and tears. The teacher asks the three questions of the card and draws the route arrow from where she left to where she arrived on the world map (places and year read off the page at plan data; the page's later country name is a teacher note for day 63). The food memory on the same page is held for day 65.
- **3 min. Question board.** The compelling question, "Why do people leave home for a new place?", goes on the board. The class gives possible reasons in its own words on cards. Nobody is asked about their own family; "I wonder" cards and passing are fine.
- **3 min. Retrieval, unit 2.** In pairs, each with a small world map, point to the continent the teacher names: Europe, North America, Africa, Asia. The teacher scans; a miss goes to day 59's slot.
- **9 min. Five reasons.** Five story cards, one actor each, all invented: work (Teo, a boat builder, has no boats to build at home, so a shipyard in the next bay gives him a bench); family (Dara goes to live in the town where her older brother lives); safety (after a hillside slides onto the road, Juno's family looks for a safer town); land (Imre leads his goats to a valley with more grass); freedom (Pia is a printer; her town says nobody may print newspapers; she moves to a town where printers are free). For each: "A possible reason is ___. The card says ___." Terms: move, reason, possible (reason here means why a person moved, not unit 2's why you think it). Pip says A, then K.
- **11 min. Pairs work.** Each pair sorts three new story cards (Nell, Orla, Bram) under the five reason pictures and says for one: "A possible reason is ___. The card says ___." The six are heard (about 4.5 minutes). A small group of at most 6 meets the teacher for a retrieval miss.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** X58; X58b was heard in the pairs work.
- **2 min. Question board.** Reasons are added. "Could Anna's be one of these? The page tells us one; the card does not say the rest." Tomorrow: who decided to move.

- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The sorting of Nell, Orla and Bram under the five reason pictures shares X58's shape, a reason named from a card. Changed so they do not copy it: the practice cards give a bakery, wet fields and a grandmother, never a loom, a sister or a weaver; the spoken ask on day 58 is the exit's ask for a reason the card does not give, heard on the exit.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the route arrow and the three-question card; each pair holds its five reason pictures; the class holds the board. Tool: None. By hand: sorting, circling. Evidence: X58, X58b.

## Day 59

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Understanding. Meets new content: the goal is taught here, from a hook and a worked example.
- goal: Chosen and forced moves; the cause-effect chain: a reason, the move, what happened next
- note: Cause-effect-chain model introduced
- credit: ours (no base lesson is mapped to this day)
<!-- /skel -->

- **5 min. Return and repair.** The letter most missed from day 58 (A or K, usually A): two fresh invented motives, each with "Which line of the card supports it?" The other letter goes to a small group in the pairs work.
- **3 min. Retrieval, grade 1.** Three pictured steps of baking (mix, bake, eat), put in order with first, then, so: the bridge to reason, move, afterward.
- **8 min. The chain, and who decided.** Two story cards, one actor each, invented. Ravi's town has one small school; a big school opens in a town by the sea; Ravi's family decides to move there; afterward Ravi learns at the big school. The company that owns every house in Hale's town sells the land and the new owner tells every family to leave by the end of the month; afterward they rent rooms in the next town and the neighbors help. The cards go on the strip: "Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___." The test question: "Could they have stayed?" Chosen: they decided. Forced: they could not stay safely or freely. Pip says C, then B.
- **6 min. Which reason is better supported?** Anna again (day 58's card): two possible reasons on cards, to be with family, or to see a big city. Which line of the card supports one? "I think ___ because the card says ___." Not certain, better supported. Review: unit 2's claim frame.
- **8 min. Pairs work.** Each pair has cut cards from three new stories (names set at plan data: Fenn, Cass and Dov) (Fenn takes a shop job a cousin offers, chosen; the only well in Cass's town runs dry, forced; Dov's family moves to a lake town where a house costs less, chosen), orders each strip and says "Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___.", with chosen or forced. The six are heard (about 4.5 minutes). A small group of at most 6 meets the teacher for the other repair letter.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** X59 and X59b; X59c was heard in the pairs work.
- **2 min. Question board.** "Who decided to move?" is added. Tomorrow: a person whose move was forced in the plainest way.

- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The chain strips for Fenn, Cass and Dov share X59's shape, chosen or forced, and X59c's order, reason then move then afterward. Changed so they do not copy them: the practice cards use a cousin's shop job, a dry well and a lake house, never a market stall, a river over a village or deep soil; Anna's two reasons share X59b's shape, the better-supported reason, and use a big city against a mother and aunt who met her.

Who holds what is carried: each pair holds its strips; the teacher holds the filled example on the wall. Tool: None. By hand: ordering, circling, one label a box. Evidence: X59, X59b, X59c.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with a picture: move, reason, because, afterward, chosen, forced. Frames: "A possible reason is ___." "Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___." Home-language rehearsal allowed.
- **Access.** Tactile arrow and cut cards; described pictures; large print; any child may point, draw or dictate.
- **Check accommodations.** The ask is repeated aloud; a word, a label, a point or a drawing is a mark; a child who passes in public answers to the teacher alone.
- **Reading load.** No child reads alone beyond labels (move, work, family; about 40 words in all); the teacher reads every card (about 150 words a day).
- **Extension, same standard.** "What is another possible reason the card does not give, and what source would show it?"

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Use the same cards at the table; the child puts the three chain cards in order and answers "Could they have stayed?"; the adult asks one question at a time.
- **One projector.** Show Anna's route and the story cards enlarged; the pairs work uses printed cards; the strip can be drawn on the board.
