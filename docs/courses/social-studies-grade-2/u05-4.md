# Section 5.4: Two accounts, one claim, and the check



Grade 2 children read short labels; the teacher reads every card, caption, story and prompt aloud, and the whole day 70 packet. Answers are circled, boxed, pointed to, drawn, said or a few written words. The claim on day 69 is said first, then recorded with a frame, with transcription support only. Days run 30 to 35 minutes. Pip the puppet says each wrong idea, one line at a time; one actor in each story scene. Rin, Sol, Ada and every observer and event in a practice card are invented and printed "invented for practice"; the teacher says so aloud. Real tellers (Anna, Catherine, Mary Antin, Rahaf) appear only as Curio Class summaries in our words with one credit line each. Every real fact is marked "source to check" until its page is opened in full (this course has no sources.csv yet). No child is asked about their own family or how they came here; any child may pass.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **D2.His.6.K-2, this section's share (days 68, 69, 70, 71).** "Compare different accounts of the same event from the past." Day 68: what both say and what only one says, and why a shorter account differs. Day 69: whether each teller had their own way of knowing, so that two accounts agree only when they are two tellers. Day 70: item 7. Day 71: reteach.
- **MA.2.T3.1 (days 69, 70).** "Investigate why people move to new places around the world, recognizing that some migration is chosen and some is forced." Day 69: the closing claim; day 70: item 1 (chosen or forced, with evidence).
- **D2.Geo.7.K-2, D2.His.14.K-2 (day 70); D2.His.16.K-2 (days 70, 71); MA.2.T3.2, D2.His.4.K-2 (day 70).** Sat on day 70 on cards not seen before; nothing new is taught. Day 71 reteaches what the sitting shows.
- **Limits that bite here.** A shorter account that leaves a detail out has not lied. Two accounts agree usefully only when each teller had their own way of knowing; a retelling is not a second witness. Antin's family moved by choice under pressure; no legal talk. In this unit children meet independent tellers only as invented observers (day 69); no pair of independent real tellers is compared (none was found for the Antins' arrival; Antin C is the fallback). The day 69 claim is practice with written feedback and is not scored.
- **Connections.** Unit 1: the three questions on a card. Unit 2 (day 27): say a claim to a partner, record it with a frame, transcription support only. Unit 4: the closing claim frame with a named source. 5.1: the chain and the test question. 5.2: Smith Life and Cherokee Today. 5.3: Arrival Voices; perspective and alike and different.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** An account is what one person tells or writes about an event. A retelling takes its facts from an account, so it depends on it: it has no way of knowing for itself. Agreement of two tellers who each saw it makes that part more trustworthy; agreement of an account and its copy adds nothing. Different names alone do not make independent tellers. "Checkable" and "opinion" are unit 1's words. The Antin cards (A, B, C, D) and the Forced Case are held back; the packet's label rules are in the unit plan, field 5.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** items 1 (MA.2.T3.1) and 7 (D2.His.6.K-2). The day 70 sitting also holds items 2 to 6, owned by 5.2 and 5.3. Sitting: 25 minutes of individual work, packet read aloud inside each item (item 1, 3 minutes; 2, 4; 3, 2; 4, 3; 5, 4; 6, 3; 7, 6), then 5 minutes to collect and reflect. Each part is scored shown, shown with help or not yet seen; no part is averaged away. A child who cannot mark says the line to the teacher, at most 4 children at about 1 minute each while the rest work, named on the record sheet.

**Exit questions.** Each child marks once in the last 3 minutes; the teacher reads each ask aloud, one ask at a time. The rotating six (about 45 seconds each, one spoken ask) are heard in the pairs work. Day 70 is a check, with no exit. Every case is new (rule 4).
- **X68 (day 68).** Mark, paper: On the Ribbons Card and the Retelling Card, circle a detail both cards say: ribbons in their hair, or no uniforms. Answer: Ribbons in their hair. Wrong: No uniforms is circled (K); Nothing is circled (K).
- **X68b (day 68).** Spoken, the six: On the Ribbons Card and the Retelling Card, say one detail that only the memory tells. Answer: That the children did not wear uniforms. Wrong: Both say ribbons (K).
- **X68c (day 68).** Spoken, the six: The Retelling Card is shorter than the Ribbons Card. Did it lie, yes or no? Answer: No. It is shorter; leaving out is not disagreeing. Wrong: Yes, it lied (F).
- **X69 (day 69).** Mark, paper: Invented for practice. A writer on the dock says the bell rang. A writer on the ship says the bell rang. A third writer copies the dock writer. How many tellers? Circle one, two or three. Answer: Two. Wrong: Three is circled (G); One is circled (G).
- **X69b (day 69).** Spoken, the six: Invented for practice. The bell rang. The bell was lovely. Say which one we can check. Answer: The bell rang; we can ask someone who heard it. Lovely is what someone thinks. Wrong: Both are true because I like them (H).
- **X71 (day 71).** Mark, paper: Invented for practice. A watcher on a hill writes about a sandcastle contest. A watcher on the beach writes about it. A poster copies the hill watcher. How many tellers? Circle one, two or three. Answer: Two. Wrong: Three is circled (G).
- **X71b (day 71).** Spoken, the six: Invented for practice. A picture shows a fountain with no water. The caption says the prettiest fountain in town. Say the part we can check on the picture. Answer: The fountain has no water. Wrong: It is the prettiest (H).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The two-column chart "Both say ___; only ___ says ___.", new on day 68, kept on the wall through day 70's item 7. Corroboration, new on day 69: each teller's own way of knowing, then agreement. The claim frame with a named card, unit 2's and unit 4's: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." The teacher's composing is withdrawn on day 69 and transcription support is kept, child by child. Perspective and alike and different (5.3) are sampled on day 70, item 6. The question board is closed on day 69.
- **Key moment 1, day 68: the both-say chart.** Ideal instrument: a two-column chart on which a pair places detail cards. Spec: none (Tools: None); a paper chart and cut detail cards fit.
- **Key moment 2, day 69: who saw it.** Ideal instrument: three labeled cards laid out with a line from each to its teller. Spec: none; three paper cards and a pencil fit.
- **A room with nothing.** Printed cards, charts, pencils, the packet on paper. Tools: None; the packet needs no screen.

## 6. Days

## Day 69


- **5 min. Return and repair.** F: a complete invented notice beside its accurate short version, both-say and only-one-says filled. The other letter goes to a small group.
- **6 min. Two tellers, or one.** Two invented observers of one arrival: Sol on the pier and Ada on the boat each write that the boat came in at noon and a band played; a poster copies Sol. Each has their own way of knowing: Sol saw it from the pier, Ada from the boat, the poster did not see it. Agreement of two tellers who each saw it makes that part more trustworthy; the poster adds nothing. Different names alone do not make two tellers. Pip says G. Terms: teller, own way of knowing, copy.
- **4 min. A sourced claim, modeled.** A separate invented move for a hospital job (a nurse takes a job at a hospital in a new town): the teacher says and records "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Not a historical claim.
- **12 min. The closing claim.** Each child answers "Why do people leave home for a new place?" using Smith Life, Cherokee Today and Arrival Voices: says it to a partner, then records it with the frame, with transcription support only. A claim may stress safety, family or several reasons, must say chosen or forced, and must name a source. A card that is invented is marked practice. The six are heard (about 4.5 minutes). Practice with written feedback returned on day 71; not scored.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** X69; X69b was heard in the pairs work.
- **3 min. Question board.** "Does agreement mean two witnesses?" is answered; the board is closed.

- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The Sol, Ada and poster cards share X69's shape, counting the witnesses. Changed so they do not copy it: the practice is a boat at noon and a band, never a bell, a dock or a ship's writer.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the frame and three source cards; the teacher holds the model claim. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, circling, recording. Evidence: X69, X69b; the claim is practice.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with a picture: account, retelling, teller, copy, both say, only one says. Frames: "Both say ___; only ___ says ___." "___ saw it for themselves." "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___." Home-language rehearsal allowed.
- **Access.** Large print; detail cards cut so a child can touch them; described pictures; any child may point, draw or dictate.
- **Check accommodations.** The packet is read aloud, neutrally; pointing, drawing, dictation, large print and home-language rehearsal are allowed; a child who passes in public answers to the teacher alone; no timers are shown.
- **Reading load.** Labels only (about 60 words); the teacher reads every card and the packet (about 250 words on day 70).
- **Extension, same standard.** "What record would be a second independent teller of the Antins' arrival, and where could we look?"
