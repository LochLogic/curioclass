# Section 7.2: Gathering facts from the country packet

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-2/7/s/7.2), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-2 u07-2.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


Grade 2 children read short labels and sentences. Each packet page is about sixty words; country groups of one to four read it in pairs, a sentence each, and a child who needs it read aloud sits with the teacher or a helper (at most 6 children, about 1 minute each, named on the sheet). Every stem is spoken, one ask at a time. Pip says each wrong idea. Packet facts are Curio Class summaries in our own words with a credit line; the real facts in each country's set are "source to check" until their sources.csv rows exist (none yet for this course). Reedmark, Fennel Valley, Oakfield and every practice case are invented and printed "invented for practice". No child reports on home, family, money, religion, body or immigration status; any child may pass.

- **D3.1.K-2 (days 85 to 87).** "Gather useful information from one or two sources, using who made them and how they are built to choose." Each day the child picks a source for the day's supporting question by maker and useful part, takes two facts and stamps each with its source label. Source choices are scored only after written feedback and revision on day 89.
- **MA.2.T4.3 (days 85 to 87).** "Gather information ... on the physical features, resources, and people of a country." Day 85: land and water (physical features). Day 86: resources and work. Day 87: people today. The report is made in 7.3.
- **Limits that bite here.** Study examples of life, not one national lifestyle. No rankings, costume shows or family disclosure. A custom is compared as two practices, never ours as the norm. The local example is a school practice the teacher supplies, never a child's home. No new action cycle or finance lesson (unit 3 holds the year's action).
- **Connections.** Map key and compass rose (unit 1) and region (units 2 to 4), day 85. Natural resource, industry, job (unit 3) and opportunity cost (unit 4), day 86. Corroboration (unit 5) and "Both ___, but ___" (unit 1), day 87.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The packet design (a map, Land and Work, People Today, an original illustration; each source names maker, date, place, purpose and rights); that a page made from one office's records is one teller, so two pages from the same set never corroborate; resource versus product; why "some" and "many" are accurate where "everyone" is not; the written feedback after 87.

## 2. Where it sits


Follows 7.1, whose three supporting questions and sourcing card the days use. Precedes 7.3, which turns the notes into a claim. The record sheet keeps each child's chosen source, maker, helping part, fact and question; day 84 choices are practice. A miss on 84 (B) is repaired on 85. Written feedback on source origin, helping part and relevance is returned for revision on day 89.

## 3. Checks, written first


Unit assessment item owned: item 3 (D3.1.K-2, on the unit plan). Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A mark alone is recorded; "shown" needs the reason heard from the rotating six, so a child not yet heard is "not yet seen" until their turn.

**Exit questions.** Each child marks once in the last 3 minutes; the teacher reads each ask aloud, one at a time. "(six)" is heard from the day's six, 45 seconds each, during supported work. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E85a (day 85).** Mark, an unseen invented map, "Fennel Valley, invented for practice", with a key: a row of dashes is a river; a wavy line is a road; a triangle is a town. "Put a token on the river." Answer: the row of dashes. Wrong: the wavy line (G).
- **E85b (day 85, six).** Spoken: "Say how the key told you where the river is." Answer: the child says the key tells that a row of dashes is a river, pointing to the key box and then the dashes. Wrong: "a river is always wavy" (G).
- **E85c (day 85, six).** Spoken: "Read the sticker on one fact in your land box. Say where the fact came from." Answer: the page name and Curio Class, as the sticker says. Wrong: "I just wrote it" (H). The land note page itself is gathered work, read after class for a fact in the right box with its sticker; a note about animals only is C (marked in end item 1).
- **E86a (day 86).** Mark, a fresh caption, "invented for practice": "Workers use wood to make chairs." Pictures: a log; a finished chair. "Put a token on the resource." Answer: the log. Wrong: the chair (I). Renamed from clay: unit 3's exit already used clay as a resource.
- **E86b (day 86, six).** Spoken: "Say what work the wood helps with." Answer: making chairs. Wrong: "the chair is the resource" (I).
- **E87a (day 87).** Mark, a fresh caption, "invented for practice": "Some neighbors meet to tell stories." A school card: "Our class hears a story at the rug each Friday." "Finish the line: There, some neighbors ___; here, our class ___." Answer: some neighbors meet to tell stories; our class hears a story on Fridays; neither ranked. Wrong: everyone (E).
- **E87b (day 87, six).** Spoken: "Say what we can say about the people there." Answer: some neighbors meet to tell stories; we cannot say everyone does. Wrong: "everyone there tells stories" (E).

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 85: "How did the key tell you?" Day 86: "What is the resource, and what is made from it?" Day 87: "What can we say, and what can we not say?"
- **Written task, day 87.** The finished note page: two facts in each of the three boxes, each with its sticker, a few written words or a drawing. Dictation allowed.
- **Judged by the teacher with 4 look-fors.** Each fact is in the right box; each has its sticker; a fact is the child's own words, not a copied sentence; a custom is said as "some" or "many", not "everyone".

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 85.** The key and compass rose (unit 1); region (units 2 to 4); B repair.
- **Day 86.** Natural resource, industry, job (unit 3); opportunity cost (unit 4); G, H or C repair.
- **Day 87.** Corroboration (unit 5); "Both ___, but ___" (unit 1); I repair.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Cards: river, mountain, key, resource, work, language, city, some, many. Frames: "The key says ___." and "The page says some ___, so I can say ___." Bilingual rehearsal allowed.
- **Access.** Page read aloud; large print; stickers pre-cut; a scribe; pointing at the key.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each stem aloud; accept a point, a drawing or dictation; the reason may be said in any language the teacher can record.
- **Reading load.** About 120 words a child reads (two pages of about sixty words plus labels) with a partner; at most 6 children a day have the page read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second page in the set for the same fact and say whether it is another teller or a copy.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the page together, two sentences at a time. Find two facts, put a source sticker on each. On day 87 compare one practice on the page with one at the table, with "Both ___, but ___". The grown-up never asks about the child's family. Any day may be skipped.
- **One projector.** Show the packet page and key on the screen while the child works on paper.
