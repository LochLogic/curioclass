## Supplies and safety limits (unit plan field 8)

<!-- skel -->
> **Tools: None.** No specifications or asks. Supply printed sources, original diagrams, advice excerpt cards, Action Record, record sheet, poster paper, pencils and voting counters. All tasks print; one shared display is optional. Each 30-minute teaching core keeps five minutes to open, eight to teach, twelve to work and five for evidence; optional ten minutes extend discussion. Show no timers.
>
> **Action.** Days 34 to 36 use class-proposed options, a fair vote and school procedures; day 42 checks delivery and response. No drills or cleanup. At home, one child and adult compare options and deliver a household message in the advice card's own words; record the response.
>
> **Language.** Teach adapt, change, region, resource, renewable, industry, skill and source with pictures. Rehearse "The source shows ... , so ... ." Adults read captions; children read labels. Extend by defending a different choice.
<!-- /skel -->

Every investigation, build or task stays inside these limits and their cost line. A task the limits forbid is a data error to report, never a writer's guess.
