## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** End-of-unit assessment: on a map not seen before, name what the title, key, compass rose and scale tell and what the map shows about how people use the land there; find who made it, when and where; tell a fact from an opinion in its caption; say which projection a world map uses and what it bends; a spoken claim answering the compelling question: which map is best for a stated purpose, and why

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **MA.2.T1.1:** Explain what map parts such as the compass rose, scale, key, and title tell us, and give examples of maps showing how people relate to their environment.
- **MA.2.T1.2:** Compare map projections such as Mercator and Peters and explain how each shows the world differently.
- **MA.2.T1.3:** Make a map of a familiar place such as the school, neighborhood, or a park.
- **D2.Geo.1.K-2:** Make maps, graphs and other pictures of familiar places.
- **D2.His.11.K-2:** Identify who made a source, when and where, using clues in the source itself.
