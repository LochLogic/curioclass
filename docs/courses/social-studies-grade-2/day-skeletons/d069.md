## Supplies and safety limits (unit plan field 8)

<!-- skel -->
> **Tools: None.** Paper serves every day. Supply printed source cards, world and regional maps, dated cards, blank chains, comparison charts, pencils and record sheets. One shared device may display enlarged copies; nothing requires it. Read aloud two or three sentences per card. Teach migrate, chosen, forced, refugee, immigrant, account and evidence with examples; rehearse "Both say ...; only ... says ... ." Offer tactile arrows, described pictures and dictation. Extend by asking what further source would help.
