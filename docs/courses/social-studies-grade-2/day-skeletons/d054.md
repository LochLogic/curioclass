## Supplies and safety limits (unit plan field 8)

<!-- skel -->
> **Tools: None.** No specifications or asks. Supply printed maps, country and trade cards, sourcing cards, unbranded label transcripts, paper and pencils. Every source and task prints; one shared display is optional. One imported classroom object with a readable origin label, or a Curio Class label photograph marked "invented for practice." Nothing is bought or brought from home.
> **Core path.** Keep five minutes opening, eight teaching, twelve working and five gathering evidence. Optional ten minutes extend comparisons. Show no timers. At home, the adult replaces partner talk and records the child's own reasons.
> **Access and language.** Teach country, continent, border, government, trade, import, export and opportunity cost with pictures. Rehearse "Made in ... , used in ... , so ... ." Adults read captions; children read labels. Accept home-language reasoning. Extend by reversing the trading viewpoint.
<!-- /skel -->

Every investigation, build or task stays inside these limits and their cost line. A task the limits forbid is a data error to report, never a writer's guess.
