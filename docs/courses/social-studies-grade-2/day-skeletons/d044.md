## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** End-of-unit assessment: from a short source set not seen before about a pictured place, say how people there changed or adapted to the land and why they settled there; name one thing they get from nearby and one from far away; name one resource, the industry and jobs it supports and whether it is renewable; name one good or service made there and one made elsewhere, and one cost of making a good; name one tool and one learned skill people use; write a claim naming its source
