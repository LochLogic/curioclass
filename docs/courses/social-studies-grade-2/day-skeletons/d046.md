## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Course-level check 1, mid-year: on a map not seen before, name what the title, key, compass rose and scale tell and what the map shows about how people use the land; place the seven continents and five oceans from a word bank, name a feature of two continents and one reason oceans matter; answer who made the map and when from the map itself; then, for a pictured place in one of unit 2's regions, write where you would choose to live there and how you would live with its land, with a reason that names the source
- **Note:** Week 14 (CALENDAR.md target; course day 46 pinned to school day 68 in scripts/frame.py). The check falls right after unit 3's assessment, so its living-with-the-land item uses a unit 2 region, not unit 3's source set. Writing bridge as on day 27

**Skills:**

- ss2-map-parts (grade 2): taught or practised here
- ss2-continents-features (grade 2): taught or practised here
- ss2-source-maker (grade 2): taught or practised here
- ss2-claim-sources (grade 2): taught or practised here
- ss2-people-environment (grade 2): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **MA.2.T1.1:** Explain what map parts such as the compass rose, scale, key, and title tell us, and give examples of maps showing how people relate to their environment.
- **MA.2.T2.1:** Locate all the continents and some major physical features on each one on a world map and a globe.
- **MA.2.T2.2:** Locate the world's oceans on a map and globe, and explain why oceans matter and help make the world livable.
- **D2.His.11.K-2:** Identify who made a source, when and where, using clues in the source itself.
- **D4.1.K-2:** Make an argument and give reasons for it.
- **MA.2.T2.4:** Explain and describe how people interact with the physical environment, including why people settle near particular bodies of water and landforms.
