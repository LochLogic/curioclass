## U9. Supports and extension

Language grows from quantity sentences on day 19, to conditional proof on day 32, to intersecting constraints on day 41. Use "If both original equations hold, then..." and "This point fails because..." Students explain to a partner, listen and restate, then write. Offer home-language rehearsal, labeled diagrams or a short word bank as needed; fade from observed language evidence, not fixed groups. Record mathematical understanding separately from connective words and units.

Read contexts aloud; independent reading is one short paragraph. Large or tactile grids, raised line boundaries, spoken coordinate pairs and a scribe preserve the goal. Keep these accommodations on exits and assessments. Extend the same standards by choosing a different retained equation and proving the resulting system equivalent.
