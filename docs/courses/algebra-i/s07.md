## U7. Talk and writing

Ask on day 134, "What changes if the product is not zero?" On day 149 ask, "Which operation needs a nonzero coefficient?" Day 151's written argument uses field 5's general-proof look-fors.

## U8. Practice that returns

Open with two earlier strands: linear solving and expression structure, then notation, domains and growth comparisons. Revisit a repaired error after two days and a week. Days 145 and 146 replace retrieval with response. Independent rewriting and hand graphs continue toward day 158's direct fluency sample.

## U9. Supports and extension

Build language from naming unknowns to explaining equivalence and qualifying a claim: "These steps preserve solutions because __." Preview factor, root, discriminant and viable with diagrams; permit home-language rehearsal. Read contexts aloud and keep independent reading to short prompts. Enlarge grids, describe coordinates, and accept dictated explanations. Preserve these supports on assessments and record use of because and nonzero separately. Extend by comparing two methods' efficiency on the same quadratic.

## U10. Two rooms

At home, alternate solver and skeptic, then have the child complete the exit alone. On day 157 repair one error and retain the retry. With one projector, everyone predicts and plots on paper before comparing the displayed graph.
