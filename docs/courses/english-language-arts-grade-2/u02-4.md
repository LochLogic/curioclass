Item (b): the day's one ask, answered aloud by the rotating six (about 20 seconds each); the rest tell a partner. Cards are printed "invented for practice" and read aloud twice. None reuses an article sentence or a worked example.
- **E46** (day 46). Card: "Paragraph 1: A leaf print can stay in rock. Paragraph 2: A burrow can show where an animal dug." Ask: "Tell me one topic that fits both paragraphs." Answer: Clues about past life in rock (fossil clues): a leaf print and a burrow are both clues.
- **E47** (day 47). Card: "Subheading: A Track. Sentence: A track is a line of **prints** made by feet. Caption: These prints show one step. A separate subheading: Shell Shapes." Ask: "Which feature on this card leads you to the track part fastest?" Answer: The subheading A Track.
- **E48** (day 48). Card: "Why draw a fossil? A drawing records details that a reader can compare with the find." Ask: "Tell me what the author wants to explain, using words from the card." Answer: Why drawing a fossil helps: a drawing records details a reader can compare with the find.
- **E49** (day 49). Card: "Index: labels 2; tracks 4. Menu: bag icon, Labels; paw icon, Tracks. Glossary: label, a note naming something." Ask: "Which tool sends you to a page number for track facts?" Answer: The index: tracks, page 4.

**Cycle 8 written check** (day 47, 15 minutes, unscored). The teacher reads each sentence twice and says its missing word; children write the word in a printed gap. Four independent spellings; none is a day 43 to 46 word. (1) "The soup is ____, not hot." mild. (2) "She was ____ and not shy." bold. (3) "We ____ the corn into flour." grind. After the check is marked, dictate "behind" and "told" as unscored practice.
