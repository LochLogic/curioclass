## Course text record and the work across the year

The topic texts, rather than isolated standards, organize the work. Day notes name the exact text ids in `data/plans/ela-texts.csv`; those ids refer to planned texts, not books already on file. Reading comes before discussing or writing about the passage. A brief written response to the day's reading accompanies teaching days, alongside the longer products named below. The unit plan must make at least 80 percent of its questions and tasks depend on the texts, including rereading difficult parts; this sequence reserves the work but writes no questions or items.

The school story is read whole on days 1 to 10, with rereading after completion. Early rich read-alouds can exceed students' decoding ability, while daily child-read school and fossil selections establish grade 2 reading. In units 3 and 4 students themselves read harder texts within the grades 2 to 3 band, with support at its high end. Teacher reading never substitutes for this opportunity. Records distinguish independent understanding from supported understanding. Grade 3 receives that distinction, rather than an unsupported claim of independent reading at the band's high end.

The named discussion routine is **turn, pair, link, clarify**. It begins with gaining the floor and taking turns, adds linking comments, and asks speakers for clarification, information, and deeper explanation. It runs briefly on most teaching days and at least twice each week, including weeks with assessments. Frames fade; they do not replace text evidence. Topic vocabulary is met before, during, and after reading; general words such as respond, describe, explain, and support return across topics.

## Unit 1. Schools and community

**Calendar:** course days 1 to 32, weeks 1 to 7. The question is how schools around the world are alike and different. The chapter story first makes character response and belonging concrete; school articles then supply facts, definitions, and images for an informational book. Reading and talk precede the book draft, and its conclusion follows its factual pages. Day 17 is Understanding: model the informative paragraph with a topic, facts, definitions and conclusion before the paragraph assessment and later book drafting. Ten days sustain the whole story instead of treating a long text as disconnected excerpts.
