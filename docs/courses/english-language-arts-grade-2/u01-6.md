- **Limits that bite here.** Every fact in the book comes from one of the two articles the child names; no outside fact. A definition is told in the article's meaning. The dictionary page is a short printed page of Curio Class entries, not a library book. Day 27 teaches with a different page from the one used on day 30. The reserved cards (Pattern, Spelling, Dictionary and the retry cards) are not taught from. No claim of completed year-end reading range is made.
- **One word, one sense.** A "source" is the article a fact comes from. An "entry" is one word and its meaning on the dictionary page. To "correct" is to fix a spelling after checking the entry. "Evidence" is a line of the text that supports an answer. A "pattern" is the letters that make a sound, as in the map.
- **Connections.** Section 1.5 gave the notes sheet and the reading record; section 1.4 gave the frame and the four-row feedback. Unit 2 owns explicit revising and editing; today's feedback is the grade 1 routine.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A book needs a topic that answers the book question (D), facts from two named articles (E), a meaning for each hard word (F) and a conclusion that follows (G). To consult a dictionary is to find the entry, compare the letters and fix the draft; copying a peer's spelling is a different act (R). The Reading Cards are parallel so no child hears a card before their turn.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: arrange, consult, correct, source, strengthen, evidence, practice, pattern, entry. Frames: "The article says ___." "A ___ is ___." "My picture shows ___, so ___." "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___."
