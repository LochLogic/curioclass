# Section 1.6: Making and sharing the school book

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-2/1/s/1.6), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-2 u01-6.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children draft, revise and publish a short book from their notes, use a beginning dictionary page, and read the Reading Cards in short conferences. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cards are invented and the teacher says so aloud. This section holds the last day of the unit.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **W.2.2: "Write informative/explanatory texts in which they introduce a topic, use facts and definitions to develop points, and provide a concluding statement or section."** Day 27 drafts the topic page and the first fact page; day 28 adds the second fact page with a definition and a clarifying picture; day 29 writes the conclusion; day 30 publishes. Written feedback follows days 27, 28 and 29 and revision comes before scoring.
- **L.2.2d and L.2.2e.** L.2.2d is practiced in the draft (a spelling pattern carried to a new word). L.2.2e, "Consult reference materials, including beginning dictionaries, as needed to check and correct spellings", is taught on day 27 (10 minutes), guided on day 28 (10), independent on day 29 (5) and performed on day 30 (7).
- **RF.2.4a to RF.2.4c.** Four-minute reading conferences on days 26 to 30; this section holds days 27 to 30.
- **RF.2.3a, RF.2.3b (day 29).** A 10-minute written transfer on long and short vowels and the vowel teams met so far; no later team is scored.
- **RI.2.7 (days 28, 31).** Day 28 asks what the child's own picture shows that the words do not; day 31 asks it of the 02-e diagram.
- **Day 31 items:** RI.2.1, SL.2.2, RI.2.7, RL.2.3 and RL.2.7, each in field 3. **SL.2.1a to c and SL.2.3** run on days 27, 28 and 32 in partner talk.
- **Limits that bite here.** Every fact in the book comes from one of the two articles the child names; no outside fact. A definition is told in the article's meaning. The dictionary page is a short printed page of Curio Class entries, not a library book. Day 27 teaches with a different page from the one used on day 30. The reserved cards (Pattern, Spelling, Dictionary and the retry cards) are not taught from. No claim of completed year-end reading range is made.
- **One word, one sense.** A "source" is the article a fact comes from. An "entry" is one word and its meaning on the dictionary page. To "correct" is to fix a spelling after checking the entry. "Evidence" is a line of the text that supports an answer. A "pattern" is the letters that make a sound, as in the map.
- **Connections.** Section 1.5 gave the notes sheet and the reading record; section 1.4 gave the frame and the four-row feedback. Unit 2 owns explicit revising and editing; today's feedback is the grade 1 routine.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A book needs a topic that answers the book question (D), facts from two named articles (E), a meaning for each hard word (F) and a conclusion that follows (G). To consult a dictionary is to find the entry, compare the letters and fix the draft; copying a peer's spelling is a different act (R). The Reading Cards are parallel so no child hears a card before their turn.

**Day 30 how it runs.** The finished book is scored once after class, after the day 29 conclusion is revised in the first 10 minutes from written feedback. Four rows: introduces the topic (D), develops with facts traceable to two named articles (E), defines (F), concludes (G), each shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The 8-minute spelling item and the 7-minute dictionary item are unscored practice after reference instruction.

**Reading conferences, days 27 to 30.** As in section 1.5: six different children a day, four minutes each, 24 teacher minutes. Day 27 uses Reading Card 2, day 28 card 3, day 29 card 4, day 30 card 5. The days 29 and 30 reading rows reuse this record with no extra minutes. Across days 26 to 30 all 30 children are heard once.

**Exit questions.** Each exit is two spoken items, each one ask: (a) "Point to the picture that goes with the card I read." (A or B hold-up); (b) one reason from the rotating six, the rest tell a partner. Shown means the right picture and a reason from the card; shown with help means the right picture and a reason after one prompt; not yet seen means the other picture or no reason. Cards are invented, read twice and printed "invented for practice". None reuses an article scene.
- **E27** (day 27). Card (02-a insert): "Pupils use a whiteboard to practise letter shapes. They wipe it clean before writing another letter. Each pupil has a marker on a string." Page: "A marker on a string stays with its pupil." One ask: "Say the sentence of the card that this page comes from." Answer: the third sentence, about the marker on a string. Wrong: "The first sentence", the page is not supported by that line (E). items.csv is the truth over this line.
- **E28** (day 28). Card (02-c insert): "Pupils use a stencil to trace letters. A stencil is a thin sheet with a letter shape cut out of it." One ask: "Say the caption for a picture of a stencil, using the card." Answer: "A stencil: a thin sheet with a letter shape cut out." Wrong: "A stencil: a bucket with a handle", contradicts the card (E).
- **E29** (day 29). Draft (invented): "Pupils stand wet umbrellas open by the door. Open umbrellas dry faster." Peer suggestion: "Explain what opening them helps." One ask: "Say an ending that follows from the draft." Answer: "Opening umbrellas helps them dry faster." Wrong: "The umbrellas are all red", adds a fact the draft does not give (E).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** From section 1.5: the notes sheet, the Both/But sheet, the reading record and Reading Cards 1 to 5. From section 1.4: the four-row feedback. From sections 1.1 to 1.3: the question words card, the picture card, the link card and the talk routine. New here: the book pages (folded sheets: topic, two fact pages, conclusion, cover), the beginning dictionary pages, and the peer suggestion strip ("Explain why ___", grade 1).
- **Standard rotation (foundations).** Ended on day 25; days 26 to 32 use the flexible block with conferences.
- **Key moment 1, day 27: using an entry.** Ideal instrument: a printed alphabetical page of six entries (bag, light, map, rain, road, tray) at each table. Spec: fits; paper. Entries for the book's own definition words (cooperate, library, volunteer, shift, schedule, diagram, caption) go on a second page.
- **Key moment 2, day 31: the cold selections.** Ideal instrument: printed copies and the 02-e diagram. Spec: fits; paper.
- **A room with nothing.** The dictionary page is written on the board and copied on index cards; the books are folded scrap sheets.

## 6. Days

## Day 27


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E25 (J), practice "Which part do you mean?" with a partner; otherwise retrieve the notes sheet.
- **4 min. Words.** Arrange, consult, correct and source, with a sentence each.
- **8 min. Model.** One topic statement ("Schools help children ___") and a first fact page, with a source tag, from an invented page of notes.
- **6 min. A second article.** Pairs reread a part of the article their partner chose and add one row to their own notes sheet with its source tag.
- **20 min. Draft.** Each child writes the topic page and the first fact page from the notes.
- **8 min. Partner source check.** The partner finds the fact's line in the article and says "found" or "which line?".
- **4 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which line in the article does this fact come from?" Hear the six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E27.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Repair the commonest cycle 5 check miss (Q): compare with the word wall.
- **10 min. Dictionary teaching.** On the printed page (bag, light, map, rain, road, tray): find an entry by its first letter, compare a draft spelling with it, and correct the draft; model three.
- **24 min. Reading conferences.** Six different children, Reading Card 2; the others read a matched cycle 5 text, spell with tiles and sort.
- **18 min. Flexible groups.** Two 9-minute teacher groups from the cycle 5 evidence: map, blend, read, spell, reread.
- **3 min. Close.**

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the notes sheet and a dictionary page. Tool: None. By hand: writing, finding entries. Evidence: E27; reading record. After class, written feedback on the day 27 pages.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 27, "Which line in the article does this fact come from?"; day 28, "What does your picture show that your words do not?"; day 32, "What did your new card help you fix?".

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: arrange, consult, correct, source, strengthen, evidence, practice, pattern, entry. Frames: "The article says ___." "A ___ is ___." "My picture shows ___, so ___." "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___."
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer. Large print pages. A child who cannot write dictates to the teacher or an approved scribe; a child who finds the dictionary page hard works with a partner who reads the entries aloud.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards and stems are read aloud; extra time on days 30 and 31; the Spelling Card can be answered with tiles.
- **Reading load.** A child reads the Reading Card, 01-check31 (79 words), 02-e (72 words), the dictionary page and the book; 02-f is heard.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a third fact page from a third article, or a Both/But page, with sources.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Make a one-page book: a topic sentence, two facts with where each came from, one meaning and an ending. Use the dictionary page to check one word. On the last day, hear the child read it aloud and tell what they would change.
- **One projector.** Show the dictionary page and the Reading Card's picture; mark on printed copies.
