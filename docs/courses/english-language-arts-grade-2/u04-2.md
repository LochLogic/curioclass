Topic block (60 minutes).
- **10 min. Cultural introduction and credit.** The teacher shows where the tale 03 is told on a map, in the credit's own words: Maathai in Kenya told it as a hummingbird doing her best; a film in Hatxa Kuĩ, a language of the Amazon forest in Brazil, shared by a language-heritage group at a university in Spain. - **12 min. Read 03.** The teacher reads it whole (3 minutes: 280 words); pairs read it again by turns, a paragraph each (6 minutes); the class rereads the hummingbird's lines together (3 minutes). Words previewed: flames, perched, scooped, part.

- **15 min. Pairs.** Each pair fills the story-map for 03 and puts a dot on the event that shows the message; the teacher confers with the six heard.
- **5 min. Exit.** E123.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The credit talk on the map asks who told the tale, as the exit does. Instead: sort three given sentences about a tale into "the book says" and "nobody knows".

Foundations block (60 minutes).
