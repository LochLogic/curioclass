# Section 3.5: Whole-group and small-group research on four pollinators

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-2/3/s/3.5), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-2 u03-5.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read the research articles 01-b to 01-e (each about 110 to 170 words, read aloud first) and the cycle 16 reader, each article read whole for the first time here (some paragraphs and headings were first heard as exits on days 78 to 91); they read at most 150 printed words a day on their own and the teacher reads every stem aloud. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: a bee, butterfly, bat and hummingbird; the bat is a nectar-drinking bat that feeds at night and is never shown as frightening; no animal is handled.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **W.2.7**, "Participate in shared research and writing projects (e.g., read a number of books on a single topic to produce a report; record science observations)." Day 92 gathers as a whole class, day 93 checks notes against the source, day 94 organizes notes in a chart, day 95 works in groups of five, each child adding a fact to the joint report.
- **W.2.8**, "Recall information from experiences or gather information from provided sources to answer a question." Every day: the child finds the fact in a provided article without the teacher selecting it, writes a note in a few words and tags the source. Day 93 adds the check that a note matches its line (C).
- **RI.2.10** (day 94): the child reads the first paragraph of 01-d alone, then the teacher reads and the pairs reread with support; the two records stay apart and feed the range record, not a verdict. **RI.2.3** and **RI.2.5** are practiced in the notes and chart.
- **RF.2.3c, e, f; RF.2.3d; RF.2.4, 4a to 4c; L.2.2d, 2e; SL.2.1a to 1c, SL.2.3** (cycle 15 check on day 92, cycle 16 days 93 to 95): -dge and -ge spellings, reading to confirm meaning, spelling by pattern, and the talk routine.
- **Limits that bite.** 01-b to 01-e are given whole; no line is highlighted (AD). Seed dispersal is told apart from pollination in the day 92 plan. The bat article is read as an animal that feeds on nectar; "more than 500 kinds of flowering plants depend on bats" is read as stated and not compared with other counts. The words ledge, wedge, ridge, judge, lodge, stage, plunge and change are reserved for the day 97 and 101 checks and are never used in teaching. Bee Visit and Butterfly Visit (R2) are never shown.
- **Connections.** Grade 1 research-cycle chart, source label and verifying a note in a second source return as review. Unit 1: note slip, notes sheet, Both/But sheet (day 23), discussion in groups of five with a talk card and a tally child (day 25). Unit 2: feature route card, topic and focus chart.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Bees look for pollen on purpose; butterflies, bats and hummingbirds move it by accident while they feed. A source tag names the article (01-b to 01-e), not the animal. A note that disagrees with its line is changed to match the line.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned.** None are scored here. This section prepares W.2.7 and W.2.8 (R1 and R2, day 99, section 3.7), and never uses the Bee Visit or Butterfly Visit cards, their how-each-carries-pollen question, or the paragraph-writing checks as examples.

**Cycle 15 written check** (day 92, 15 minutes, unscored). The teacher reads each sentence twice and says its missing word; children write the word in a printed gap. Five independent spellings, none a day 88 to 91 word. (1) "She keeps her lunch in a ____ box." plastic. (2) "The ____ zoomed into the sky." rocket. (3) "Please ____ the bag." unzip. (4) "We will ____ the suitcase." repack. (5) "Pour some ____ in the cup." milk. Wrong: "plastik" and "plasick" miss the -ic ending (Y); "rokit" and "rocit" omit the ck (Y); "mil" and "milc" miss the k (Y); "unzip" read or written as "onzip" shows the prefix not seen (AL); "repack" written "repak" omits the ck (Y); "plastic" read "plaster" from the picture (S). After marking, dictate "sock" and "unlock" as unscored practice.

**Exit question.**
- **E93** (day 93). Spoken, one ask per stem, answered by the rotating six (about 20 seconds each); the rest tell a partner and the teacher notes hands. Card, invented for practice: Source: "Some flowers open early in the morning. Bees visit them." Note: "Bees visit flowers late in the evening." Item (two asks): "Does the note match the source?" then "Which word would you change in the note?" Key: it does not match; change "evening" to "morning"; the finger is on the source line. Shown is the right answer in the child's words; shown with help is right after one prompt ("Read the source line again"); not yet seen is "it matches" or no line. Wrong: "it matches" (C); a changed note with no line shown (AE); "bees visit them" given as the fix with no change to the wrong word (E).
- Days 92, 94 and 95 have no exit; their evidence is the slips on the joint chart and the group record, plus the oral card and the heard column.

## 6. Days

## Day 92


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed D, B or AE on day 91, cover the card and say a fact in four words; or lay an arrow card between two slips and say the link.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Today the whole class asks "Why do bees visit flowers?" The answer is in 01-b. I give you the whole page and I do not point to the answer. Pollination moves pollen; seeds travelling is a different thing.
- **8 min. Read 01-b.** Teacher reads 01-b, A Bee Visits (about 170 words), aloud once; pairs reread it with a finger and star any word that stops them. No line is highlighted.
- **14 min. Pairs ask, find and note.** Pairs write two slips with tags (01-b, or the photograph caption of the mining bee) and say them aloud. The teacher hears six pairs (1 minute each); the others work in pairs.
- **10 min. Trade and trace.** Each pair trades with another; the reader traces one slip back to its line with a finger and says "This matches because ___".
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which fact is the most important for the question, and why?" Children say "I think ___ because ___". Hear the six.
- **8 min. Joint chart.** Each pair puts a slip on the joint chart; the class sorts them into answers the question and does not answer (E) and reads two tags aloud.
- **2 min. The photograph.** Children look at the mining bee photograph and caption (public domain) and say one thing it shows that the words do not.
- **15 min. Written check (foundations).** Cycle 15 written check (field 3), unscored.
- **5 min. Reader.** Reread The Attic Trunk in pairs for expression.
- **25 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation; the six read the oral card for the whole cycle (12 minutes), the rest in stations. Group 5 of five.
- **15 min. Fresh response.** Day 88's six read cycle 15 words to the teacher (12 minutes, 2 minutes each) while the others partner-read a pattern card and spell two dictated words; no new teaching.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the research-cycle chart and joint chart; pairs hold the article and slips. Tool: None. By hand: finding, tagging, writing slips, spelling five words. Evidence: the slips; the cycle 15 check; the oral card records (group 5 of five, the last of the cycle); the heard column.

## Day 93


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed D, E or AD on day 92, cover the article and say a fact in four words; or hold up the question card and ask whether the note answers it.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Today we ask "Which flowers do butterflies like?" We read 01-c and check every note against its line.
- **8 min. Read 01-c.** Teacher reads 01-c, A Butterfly Visits (about 135 words), aloud once; pairs reread with a finger and star words that stop them.
- **14 min. Pairs ask, find and note.** Pairs write two slips with tags and say them aloud. The teacher hears six pairs (1 minute each).
- **12 min. Check against the source.** Teacher adds two invented slips with an error ("butterflies like dark blue tall flowers"; "butterflies move more pollen than bees"). The class finds the line for each and fixes the slip. Then pairs trade slips and check each other's notes the same way.
- **8 min. Exit.** E93 (field 3): the bees card, one ask at a time; the six answer aloud, the rest tell a partner.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** "Check against the source" (find the line for a slip and fix it) is the exit's shape exactly. Instead: sort given slips into "matches the card" and "does not match" under source cards, and keep the fix and point-to-the-line for E93.
- **10 min. Both and But.** Both/But sheet (unit 1): pairs write one fact the bee and the butterfly share and one that differs ("Both visit for food. But the butterfly moves less pollen"), with two tags. The teacher hears the six.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Cycle 15 check repair: the commonest miss of the day 92 check (Y, AL, S or T), the wrong spelling beside the right one, written on the board.
- **15 min. Teach: -dge and -ge.** Retrieve -ck and -k endings. Teach -dge after a short vowel in a one-syllable word (badge, edge, fudge, bridge, dodge) and -ge after a long vowel or consonant (cage, huge, page, large, hinge); the g says /j/ when e follows. Model, map, blend and spell. Tricky words (from memory, source to check): their, whole. Dictate a sentence with edge.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 16 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 1 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which ending has the d, in bridge or in cage?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the article and slips; the teacher holds the joint chart. Tool: None. By hand: finding, writing, checking by finger, spelling. Evidence: E93; the slips and checked notes; the Both/But sheet; the heard column (heard-six and oral card).

## Day 94


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed C or AE on day 93, reread the line and put a finger on it, then fix the note; or trace the right tag.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Today we read about a bat and a hummingbird. First you read a little alone; then I read and you read again with help. We ask "How does each animal reach the nectar?"
- **14 min. Read with support.** Each child reads the first paragraph of 01-d (about 35 words) alone with a finger; record: read alone. Teacher reads 01-d and 01-e aloud (about 220 words); pairs reread 01-e with a finger and a partner; record: with help.
- **14 min. Pairs ask, find and note.** Pairs write one slip for the bat and one for the hummingbird with tags (01-d, 01-e) and say them. The teacher hears six pairs (1 minute each).
- **10 min. Organize.** A two-column chart, Bat and Hummingbird, with three rows: when it feeds; how it reaches the nectar; flowers it likes. Pairs place their slips in the rows; the class reads two rows.
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "What is one way a bat and a hummingbird are alike, and one way they differ?" Children say "Both ___. But ___". Hear the six.
- **4 min. Share.** Each pair names one tag and reads one slip aloud.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Yesterday's commonest miss (S, T or Y) written beside the right word and read aloud.
- **15 min. Read A Package at the Bridge.** First reading after pattern teaching: the teacher reads page 1 aloud, pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4 (words with -dge, -ge and the tricky words their and whole). Confirm meaning after decoding. Spell badge and cage. Text to make: a Curio Class reader, A Package at the Bridge (field 11).
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 16 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 2 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "What does the picture not tell you that the letters did?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the articles and slips; the teacher holds the bat and hummingbird chart. Tool: None. By hand: reading, writing slips, placing them in rows, spelling. Evidence: the RI.2.10 alone and with-help record; the slips and chart; the oral card; the heard column.

## Day 95


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed D, E or C on day 94, cover the article and say a fact in four words; or hold up the question card.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Today six groups of five do their own research. Each group chooses two articles and asks "Which flowers does each animal visit, and why?" Roles: reader, finder, noter, checker, sharer; the tally child counts who spoke (unit 1).
- **6 min. Choose a pair.** Each group chooses two of 01-b to 01-e; the teacher records the pairs on the group record sheet and chooses none. A pair may repeat across groups.
- **20 min. Gather and note.** Groups share the two articles (the reader reads a paragraph aloud, the finder finds the line, the noter writes the slip, the checker traces it, the sharer keeps the sheet). Each child writes at least one slip with a tag. The teacher visits all six groups and hears one child in each for 1 minute.
- **10 min. Check and repair.** The checker in each group traces each slip back; the group fixes any D, E, C or AE.
- **10 min. Share.** Each group gives the class one fact and its tag (1 minute each, 6 minutes); the class says whether it answers the question (4 minutes).
- **6 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which article gave your group the surest fact, and how do you know?" Children say "The article ___ was surest because ___". Hear the six.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Yesterday's commonest miss (S, T or Y) written beside the right word and read aloud.
- **15 min. Reread and sort.** Reread A Package at the Bridge in pairs for accuracy. Sort twelve cards by ending: -dge (badge, edge, fudge, bridge, hedge, dodge) and -ge (cage, huge, page, large, hinge). Spell hedge and page on paper and use each in a sentence.

- **3 min. Close.** "Which two letters come before e in badge?"

Who holds what is carried: each group holds its two articles and its record sheet; the teacher holds the pairs list. Tool: None. By hand: sharing a page, writing slips, tracing, sorting. Evidence: the group record sheet; one slip per child; the oral card; the heard column.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: article, question, source, note, tag, match, butterfly, bat, hummingbird, nectar, both, differ. Frames: "I found it in ___." "The note matches the line because ___." "Both ___. But ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer; articles are large print and read aloud twice; a child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes; a tag may be a drawn symbol of the article.
- **Check accommodations.** The cycle 15 check is read twice; a child may say the spelling aloud and the teacher writes it, recorded as help.
- **Reading load.** At most 150 printed words a day: one article is read aloud first and only a paragraph (about 35 words) is read alone; the cycle 16 card is about 40 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a third article to the group's question, or find a fact in one article that the other article leaves out and say why it matters.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read each article aloud, then let the child find and tag the fact. Use real paper slips on the table as the joint chart. Spell three words with letter cards.
- **One projector.** Show the article and the chart side by side; the child finds the line and the teacher writes the tag.
