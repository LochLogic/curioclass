## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: story map, beginning, ending, introduce, conclude, because, line, choose. Frames: "The beginning introduces ___." "The ending concludes ___." "I know because the text says ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer. Large print cards; the A and B pictures differ in color and shape. A child who cannot write the sentence draws and the teacher scribes, and the scribe is named on the check strip.
- **Check accommodations.** The cold text and every question are read aloud; the six-box strip can be answered by pointing at picture cards for the child to name; the spelling check can be answered with tiles. Extra time is allowed on days 40 and 41.
- **Reading load.** A child reads at most 150 printed words a day (a story page of at most 96 words, cards, the cycle reader). The cold text is read aloud while children follow.
- **Extension, same standard.** Compose a question that needs two lines of the story to answer, or draw the beginning and ending of a different story and say what each does.
