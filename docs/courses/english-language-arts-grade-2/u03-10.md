## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: rehearse, listener, detail, careful, friend, plan. Frames: "Where does it ___?" "I will tell you about ___." "I see ___ in my head." A child may rehearse in the home language first; the home language is never ranked.
- **Access.** Pointing to a card is an answer; a scribe; large print; a child who prefers not to speak aloud may rehearse to one listener and be heard live later; a child may record in a pair.
- **Check accommodations.** The check is read aloud after a first independent try (recorded as help); extra time inside the same part.
- **Reading load.** The poems (about 150 words) are read together on 110, never alone; cards of 111 are short lines read aloud; no day asks a child to read more than 150 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second detail from the senses to the plan and say what a listener should see.
