# Section 1.5: Choosing facts and talking together

## Header


Children read the cycle 5 reader, the poem lines, the notes sheet and the Both/But sheet; the teacher reads the articles aloud again and children follow the words to know. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cards are invented and the teacher says so aloud.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.2.10 (day 21).** Children reread the poem Our School Day aloud, in groups, for a listener. Its year-end demand stays open in the unit plan; no claim is made here.
- **RI.2.1: "Ask and answer such questions as who, what, where, when, why, and how to demonstrate understanding of key details in a text."** Day 22 teaches choosing the facts that answer the book question; days 23 and 24 apply it.
- **RI.2.7: "Explain how specific images (e.g., a diagram showing how a machine works) contribute to and clarify a text."** Day 22 teaches choosing a picture that clarifies a fact (the 02-c diagram); day 23 applies it to the 02-a and 02-b photographs.
- **W.2.2 (day 24).** Notes for the book: fact, source tag, meaning and a picture idea. Drafting is section 1.6.
- **SL.2.1a to c and SL.2.3.** Run on days 21 to 25 through the talk routine, then are observed on day 26 in small groups and a whole-class exchange.
- **Foundations.** RF.2.3a, RF.2.3b and L.2.2d: cycle 5 (oa and ow as long o), new in grade 2, on days 21 to 25 with the written check on day 25; no word with ow as in cow is read. RF.2.4 to RF.2.4c run daily; the day 26 reading conference opens the unit reading record (field 3).
- **Limits that bite here.** The book answers one question from two articles; no fact comes from outside them. No country is ranked, and the book's similar and different are about routines, not about better or worse. No child is asked about their own school, meals or home. Present-day claims in 02-a to 02-c need the recheck noted in section 1.3. Day 25 rehearses on a different question (the poem and 02-c) from the one observed on day 26 (02-a and 02-b).
- **One word, one sense.** A "refrain" is the repeated lines of the poem. An "audience" is the listeners. To "select" is to choose the facts that answer the book question. "Similar" and "different" are said as Both ___. But ___. (review of kindergarten and grade 1). "Light" in the poem and in cycle 4 is one word.
- **Connections.** Section 1.4 gave the note slip, the source tag, the definition and the frame; day 24 withdraws the frame. Section 1.6 drafts the book from the day 24 notes.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A fact belongs if it answers the book question; an unrelated fact is crossed out. A picture clarifies when the child says what it shows that the words do not (C). A listener who nods at "those" has not asked which things (J). A reader who runs two sentences together hides the meaning (O).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** day 26 SL.2.1a, SL.2.1b, SL.2.1c, SL.2.3 (and SL.2.1 through them), RF.2.4a, RF.2.4b, RF.2.4c (and RF.2.4 through them). The day 29, 30 and 31 rows in the table belong to section 1.6.

**Day 26 discussion, how it runs (48 + 12 minutes).** Six groups of five, set from the heard column so each group mixes talkers. The teacher sits with each group for 8 minutes; the five other groups write fact cards (fact, source tag) from their day 24 notes and draw a picture idea. Question card, read aloud twice: "What do 02-a and 02-b show that schools help children do?" The teacher says nothing for the first 2 minutes, then may ask once "Which part do you mean?" (marked shown with help if used). Each child has four cells: H (waits for the signal), I (links to a peer's remark), J (asks which part when unclear), K (asks or answers about the speaker's idea). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The 12-minute return: each group's reporter gives one fact (1 minute each), then the class links or asks a clarifying question (6 minutes); the teacher records a larger-group cell for every child who speaks.

**Day 26 reading conference, how it runs (4 minutes a child, 24 teacher minutes).** The day's six sit at the teacher's corner in turn. 1 minute purpose and understanding (the two questions of the RF.2.4a row), 2 minutes the first four sentences read aloud twice (RF.2.4b), 1 minute confirm or correct "beds" and reread (RF.2.4c). Unscored; no adult supplies a word or its meaning. The Reading Card in the unit plan is card 1. Cards 2 to 5 are parallel (same length, same teams, a different garden or tap scene) and are used on days 27 to 30, so no child hears a card before their own turn. Look-fors and samples are shared across the five cards.

**Exit questions.** Each exit is two spoken items, each one ask: (a) "Point to the picture that goes with the card I read." (A or B hold-up); (b) one reason or one reading from the rotating six, the rest tell a partner. Shown means the right picture and a reason from the card; shown with help means the right picture and a reason after one prompt; not yet seen means the other picture or no reason. Cards are invented, read twice and printed "invented for practice", except the reading exits of days 22 and 23, which reread article parts named in their lines.
- **E21** (day 21). Stanza (invented, not from the poem): "The rain taps soft upon the glass. / We watch the little drops slide past. / We curl up with a book and stay, / And wait for clouds to drift away." One ask: "Read the stanza aloud so that a listener understands." Answer: a pause at each line end and an even, unhurried voice. Wrong: a rushed reading that loses the meaning (O). items.csv is the truth over this line.
- **E22** (day 22). Reread aloud twice the 02-c paragraph that begins "Why does a school serve meals?" Say: "Where is part of the food bought?" Answer: From family farms. Wrong: From a shop (A); The pupils grow it (A).
- **E23** (day 23). Reread aloud twice the 02-b sentence about a free meal and the 02-c sentence that begins "The full-day pupil." Show the 02-c diagram. Say: "Tell me one way the meals are different, using the diagram." Answer: 02-b says every pupil gets a free meal each school day; the 02-c diagram shows the full-day pupil has four meals: breakfast, lunch and two snacks. Wrong: Both articles say pupils have four meals (A); The diagram shows no meals (A).
- **E24** (day 24). Card (02-b insert): "Pupils carry their reading books in a satchel. A satchel is a bag with a long strap." One ask: "Tell me what a satchel is, using the card." Answer: a bag with a long strap. Wrong: "A hard box with a lid", the meaning contradicts the card (E).
- **E25** (day 25). Lee says, "They carried those to the back before the bell." One ask: "Lee's words are not clear. Say what you would ask Lee." Answer: a question that links to the speaker and asks which things, such as "You said they carried those to the back. Which things?" Wrong: nodding or "OK", leaves the unclear word unasked (J); "I like the bell", an unrelated comment (I).

**Cycle 5 written check** (day 25, 15 minutes, unscored). The teacher reads each sentence twice and says its missing word; children write it in a printed gap. None is a teaching word. (1) "I put jam on my ____." toast. (2) "A frog can ____ at night." croak. (3) "Please ____ me the ball." throw. (4) "I have a ____ of fruit." bowl. Wrong spellings tagged: "tost", "croke", "thro", "bol" (Q, spelled by sound, pattern not carried over). Changed from the first plan, whose float and blow are words of the reader.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 21, "How did the reading help the audience understand?"; day 22, "Which fact belongs in a book about what schools help children do?"; day 23, "Which details make the two schools similar and different?"; day 24, "Which fact helps explain what schools help children do?".
- Written task: the day 24 notes sheet, two facts with source tags, one meaning and one picture idea.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) each fact answers the book question; (2) each carries its source tag; (3) the meaning matches the article.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: audience, refrain, gather, select, caption, careful, similar, different, connect, complete, revise. Frames: "Both ___. But ___." "The article says ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer. Large print slips; the stanzas on cards. A child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes. A child may pass a talk turn and join on the next.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards and stems are read aloud; the cycle check can be answered with tiles; the reading conference can be held in a quiet corner.
- **Reading load.** A child reads the reader (about 60 words), the poem lines, slips and sheets; articles are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second similar and a second different detail, each with its source tag.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read one article and the poem aloud, then fill the Both/But sheet together; the child tells one fact and one meaning.
- **One projector.** Show the photographs side by side and the Both/But sheet; mark on printed copies.
