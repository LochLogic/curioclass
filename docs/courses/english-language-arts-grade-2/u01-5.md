- **Limits that bite here.** The book answers one question from two articles; no fact comes from outside them. No country is ranked, and the book's similar and different are about routines, not about better or worse. No child is asked about their own school, meals or home. Present-day claims in 02-a to 02-c need the recheck noted in section 1.3. Day 25 rehearses on a different question (the poem and 02-c) from the one observed on day 26 (02-a and 02-b).
- **One word, one sense.** A "refrain" is the repeated lines of the poem. An "audience" is the listeners. To "select" is to choose the facts that answer the book question. "Similar" and "different" are said as Both ___. But ___. (review of kindergarten and grade 1). "Light" in the poem and in cycle 4 is one word.
- **Connections.** Section 1.4 gave the note slip, the source tag, the definition and the frame; day 24 withdraws the frame. Section 1.6 drafts the book from the day 24 notes.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A fact belongs if it answers the book question; an unrelated fact is crossed out. A picture clarifies when the child says what it shows that the words do not (C). A listener who nods at "those" has not asked which things (J). A reader who runs two sentences together hides the meaning (O).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: audience, refrain, gather, select, caption, careful, similar, different, connect, complete, revise. Frames: "Both ___. But ___." "The article says ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer. Large print slips; the stanzas on cards. A child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes. A child may pass a talk turn and join on the next.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards and stems are read aloud; the cycle check can be answered with tiles; the reading conference can be held in a quiet corner.
- **Reading load.** A child reads the reader (about 60 words), the poem lines, slips and sheets; articles are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second similar and a second different detail, each with its source tag.
