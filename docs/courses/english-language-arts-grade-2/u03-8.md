## 1. Standards and practices


Day 102 publishes the paragraph and shares it (application; nothing new is taught). Day 103 is the unit's one response day for days 99 to 101 and opens cycle 18. Each day is 120 minutes: topic block 60, foundations block 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Children read at most 150 printed words: on 102 their own paragraph, a caption and a strip; on 103 one fresh source of about 30 words; cycle readers are in the foundations block. Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: bees, beetles and flowers only; no sting, injury, death or illness; no insect is touched.

- **W.2.7**, "Participate in shared research and writing projects (e.g., read a number of books on a single topic to produce a report; record science observations)." Day 102: each child names the sources that answered the question. Day 103: a fresh question and source.
- **W.2.8**, "Recall information from experiences or gather information from provided sources to answer a question." Repaired and retried on 103 from a new source.
- **L.2.2d and L.2.2e**: "Generalize learned spelling patterns when writing words"; "Consult reference materials, including beginning dictionaries, as needed to check and correct spellings." Day 102 holds the cycle 17 written check; the R4 and R5 correction is offered on 102 and scored on 103.
- **RF.2.3c to 3f, RF.2.4a to 4c**: cycle 17 check on 102; cycle 18 (-ible, -able) opens on 103.
- **SL.2.1a to 1c, SL.2.3**: sharing in fives on 102; the talk group on 103. **RI.2.3** (103): "Describe the connection between a series of historical events, scientific ideas or concepts, or steps in technical procedures in a text."
- **Limits that bite here.** The cycle 17 check words and the day 102 reference entry are reserved (field 3); the fresh day 103 source and the cycle 18 check words (washable, bendable) are never used in teaching. Written feedback on the check comes before 103; 103 repairs, it does not re-score the first marks (the R1 and R2 marks stand; revised work is marked shown with help).
- **Connections.** Section 3.7 (the paper trail of days 99 to 101); unit 1 booklet sharing; the talk routine; standard rotation (1.1).
- **The teacher page must explain.** How the 103 groups are made from the record (largest letter first, field 4) and why no child sits through repair they do not need: they work on a named task at their table.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Items owned:** R4 and R5 correction (offered 102, scored 103); W.2.5 editing and R3 (read on 102 and 103; scored cells stay as marked); R6 (scored 103 from the three observations). The cycle 17 written check is held on 102 (15 minutes: spelling 5, passage 5, reference correction 5); its feedback comes after 102, correction on 103, score on 104 (section 3.9).

**Cycle 17 check card (day 102 only, invented for practice, ours).** Dictation, two words and a sentence: "ranch, sweeter. Put the peach by the kitchen." Printed key: ranch, sweeter, kitchen. Passage, read on paper: "Jess is the smallest child on the team. She cannot reach the high shelf, but she can catch a ball better than the rest. What can Jess do better than the rest?" Key: catch a ball. Reference correction: draft "strech"; entry "stretch: to pull out long"; key stretch, compared letter by letter. Wrong: "strech" kept (Y); "ranche" or "kitchin" (AN: ending letters out of order or replaced); a passage answer that contradicts the words, "reach the shelf" (C); stretch copied from a neighbor (J). Data note: the end items of the first data job use stitch, crunch, clutch, darker and loudest on day 102; they overrule the words printed here.

**Exit questions.**
- **E102.** Card, read aloud and said to be invented: "Card X says: a beetle walks on a flower and pollen sticks to its body. Card Y says: the beetle walks to a new flower and the pollen falls off there." The question on the board: "Where does the pollen end up?" Two asks. Ask 1: "Say which card answered the question." Key: Card Y. Wrong: Card X (AE). Ask 2: "Say what that card told." Key: the pollen falls off at the new flower. Wrong: a fact from neither card, "It goes in the nest." (E); the other card's fact (AE). The rotating six answer aloud; the rest tell a partner.
- **E103.** This card is invented for practice. Show the question: "How does a moth get pollen to the next flower?" Show the fresh source line: "A moth rests on a pale flower. Pollen sticks to its body. When it rests on the next flower, some of the pollen rubs off." Say: "Say one sentence that answers the question, using the line." Key: One sentence that joins the sticking to the next flower, such as Pollen sticks to its body, and some rubs off when it rests on the next flower. Wrong: Pollen sticks to its body (B); It drops it in a hole (C).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The research slips, drafts with written feedback and record sheets of 99 to 101 go back to each child on 102 and 103; the chart of four jobs, talk card and tally child stay up. Words: pollen, carry, topic, fact, definition, ending (unit words, field 9 of 3.7).
- **Shared keyboard (day 102).** One text editor on one keyboard; 15 pairs at 2 minutes each is 30 minutes, the whole station window. A pair types one sentence of the class paragraph with an adult beside it; the other pairs publish by hand or draw. Written collaboration feedback follows 102 and is revised on 105.
- **Key moment 1, day 102: sharing.** Ideal: a listener who has not read the paragraph. Spec fits: groups of five, listeners in the group. Key moment 2, day 103: a fresh source and a new sentence. Ideal: paper source line and pencil. Spec fits.
- **Tools.** Shared word processor (light), 102, practice with feedback; the spec fits. A room with nothing: the class paragraph is written on chart paper, a sentence a pair, by hand.

## 6. Days

## Day 102


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **10 min. Return and correct.** Hand back the written feedback on the paragraph, R4 and R5. Each child corrects one thing and finishes the final copy.
- **30 min. Publishing stations.** The shared keyboard: 15 pairs, 2 minutes each, one sentence of the class paragraph, an adult beside. The rest publish: copy the final paragraph, draw and label, rehearse reading aloud.
- **12 min. Share in fives.** Six groups, each child reads the paragraph aloud, about 2 minutes each, while the listeners write on a slip the letter of the card each part came from; then the listeners link or ask. The teacher visits and hears the rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E102.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The share in fives was changed so the listeners write on a slip the letter of the card each part came from while the reader reads (label); the exit asks the child to say which card answered a question.
- **3 min. Transitions.**

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** The commonest miss of day 101's foundations exit.
- **15 min. Cycle 17 written check.** Spelling 5, passage 5, reference correction 5, from the card in field 3.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Round 1, 13 minutes: six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: tiles, sort and reread.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which ending means the most of all?"

Who holds what: each child holds the paragraph and the check sheet; the pair at the keyboard holds the sentence. Tool: shared word processor, 30 minutes, 15 pairs. By hand: publishing, speaking, spelling. Evidence: R4 and R5 correction; share; E102; the cycle 17 check; the oral card.

## Day 103


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Hand-back.** Records are returned; each child learns the table to go to, named by their commonest letter.
- **38 min. Repair in groups.** Four teacher turns while the others work a named task at their table: sources 10 (C, D, E, AD, AE), writing 10 (F, G, H, I, AF, B), talk 5 (K, L, M, N), references 5 (J, Y, AN) and 8 for a second pass for a child with two misses. Group sizes come from the record; the largest is seen first. No child sits through repair they do not need.
- **10 min. Fresh source.** Everyone finds and draws a line under the words that answer "How does the pollen get to the next daisy?" in a fresh strip about a fly on a daisy (piece in this section), then draws what happens. The strip is a different case from E103. Scored as retried.
- **5 min. Exit.** E103.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The fresh source is changed to finding and underlining the answering words in a strip about a fly and a daisy (find and mark); the exit asks for a spoken sentence about a different case.
- **2 min. Transitions.**

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** The commonest miss of the cycle 17 check, beside the right word.
- **15 min. Cycle 18 pattern.** Model -ible and -able, map and blend, then read the cycle 18 reader (A Label for the Bin) and spell five words.
- **37 min. Rotation.** As day 99; six at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "What does -able mean at the end of a word?"

Who holds what: the teacher holds the groups; each child holds a repair task. Tool: None. By hand: repair, writing, spelling. Evidence: the retry (shown with help); E103; the oral card.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 102, "Which card told where the pollen goes?"; day 103, "What does your new sentence add?"
- Written task, day 103: one sentence answering the fresh source question (a drawing and a few words allowed).
- Look-fors: (1) the answer fits the question; (2) the fact is in the source; (3) two ideas are joined by "so" or "because".

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 102.** Source tags (97), the four jobs (98), revision (100), the entry strip (101).
- **Day 103.** The cycle 17 words (ch, tch, er, est); the talk routine; source work from 99.
- The oral card is heard for six children a day; the heard column is kept.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: beetle, sticks, falls off, card, source. Frames: "Card ___ told that ___." "The source says ___, so ___." A child may answer in the home language first.
- **Access.** Pointing to the card is an answer; scribe for writing; large print on request; the keyboard turn may be shared by one child pointing and one typing.
- **Check accommodations.** The check is read aloud after the first independent attempt (recorded as help); oral dictation answers are not accepted for spelling; extra time inside the same part.
- **Reading load.** Their own paragraph, a caption, a strip and one fresh 30-word line; no reading beyond.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write a second sentence linking a second source to the first.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Share the paragraph with a parent who has not read it; the parent asks "Which card said that?" On 103 give the fresh line on a strip and ask for one sentence, then write it together beside the feedback.
- **One projector.** Show the paragraph strips; the keyboard window is the same 2-minute pair turns at the projector.
