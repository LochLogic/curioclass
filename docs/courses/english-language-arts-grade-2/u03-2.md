# Section 3.2: Choosing a strategy, the mid-unit check and its response

## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 2. Unit 3, The secret world of pollination. *How do plants and pollinators help each other?* (days 78 to 118): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 82 to 84.** Days by kind: application 1 (82), check 1 (83), respond 1 (84). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 2 of 12 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read an invented practice paragraph on day 82, the new check card Next Spring on day 83 and a fruit-tree retry card on day 84; the teacher reads every stem aloud and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen on practice days; check items are scored 0 or 1 by component on the later day the unit plan names. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: plants, bees, pollen, nectar and seeds only; no sting or injury; a bee is never handled.

- **14 min. Choose and use.** Pairs pick a strategy card for each starred word, use it and say "I used ___ because ___". ## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: strategy, clue, process, step, connect, explain, retry, pod, careless. Frames: "I used ___ because ___." "First ___. So ___." "This word means ___. The clue is ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer on the heading and diagram items; cards are large print; a child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes; the dictionary strip is read aloud.
- **Check accommodations.** Every stem is read aloud twice; the child may point or draw; extra time within the 20 minutes; a child may read the card aloud to the teacher instead of silently, recorded as independent only if no help is given.
- **Reading load.** At most 150 printed words a day: Mira's Beans (about 60), Next Spring (about 70 with labels), the word card (about 40), the fruit-tree card (about 45).
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a fourth link to the chain from the text (the nectar feeds a later visitor) and explain it with because, or explain why seed dispersal is not the same as pollination.
