# Section 3.2: Choosing a strategy, the mid-unit check and its response

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-2/3/s/3.2), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-2 u03-2.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read an invented practice paragraph on day 82, the new check card Next Spring on day 83 and a retry on a paragraph of article 01-b (A Bee Visits) on day 84; the teacher reads every stem aloud and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen on practice days; check items are scored 0 or 1 by component on the later day the unit plan names. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: plants, bees, pollen, nectar and seeds only; no sting or injury; a bee is never handled.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RI.2.3**, "Describe the connection between a series of historical events, scientific ideas or concepts, or steps in technical procedures in a text." This section uses it three ways: day 82 explains the process in a new paragraph, day 83 checks it on a new article (Next Spring, A1), day 84 repairs and retries it. No new content is taught; days 78 to 81 taught it. The historical-events part is not met here.
- **RI.2.4, L.2.4** (practiced, never taught here): children choose a strategy (sentence clue, word parts, dictionary strip) for a hard word and name the clue. Unit 2 taught them; day 82 is their first use in this topic and does not count as evidence (unit plan, field 5).
- **RI.2.10**: day 83 only. The child reads Next Spring alone first, then again with support; the two records stay apart and feed the range record, not a verdict. **RI.2.7, RI.2.5, RI.2.2** each have one small C1 item on day 83 (diagram, heading, paragraph focus), taught in unit 1 and 2.
- **L.2.5a** on day 84: a retrieval of the grade 1 words tiny, look, peek and stare with a real use. Practice only.
- **RF.2.3c, e, f; RF.2.4, 4a to 4c; L.2.2d, 2e** (cycle 13 check on day 82, cycle 14 days 83 and 84): consonant-le and two-syllable long vowels, the cycle's tricky words, accuracy on rereading, and spelling by pattern checked against the word wall. **SL.2.1a to 1c, SL.2.3**: turn, pair, link, clarify every day.
- **Limits that bite.** Facts rest on 01-a and the cards. A check card never reuses a taught case: Next Spring, the word card, Bee Visit and Butterfly Visit are reserved for checks and never appear in teaching or exits. Seed dispersal is told apart from pollination aloud on day 83 before reading.
- **Connections.** The grade 2 unit 2 strategy cards (sentence clue and root card, flip card, compound cards, dictionary strip), the unit 1 diagram card and the grade 1 connection arrow return here. Written feedback by row, then revision, then one scoring (unit 1, days 18 to 20) is the scoring path for A1 and C1.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Pollination moves pollen; seeds travelling is a different thing. Next Spring's chain crosses seasons and does not follow one insect. A wrong answer is marked by what it shows (letters A to AP).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned.** All seven day 83 rows of the unit plan (RI.2.3, RI.2.10, RI.2.7, RI.2.4, RI.2.5, RI.2.2, L.2.4) are administered here; scored 0 or 1 by component on day 85. Time: A1 25 of the 60 topic minutes, exit 5, C1 20 (RI.2.7 3, RI.2.4 2, RI.2.5 2, RI.2.2 3, L.2.4 10), quiet independent rereading 10.

**Check cards (Curio Class originals, invented for practice; print that on each card).**
- **Next Spring (A1).** The card of the unit plan, field 5, with its diagram: "New plants. Successful pollination can help a flower make seeds. A seed can grow into a new plant. The new plant can make flowers. New food. These flowers can provide nectar, a sweet liquid that feeds visiting pollinators. The visitors can help these flowers make seeds in turn." Diagram: pollinated flower to seed to new flowering plant to nectar for a later visitor.
- **C1 word card.** Unit plan, field 5: stored, replant, useful, sunlight, and the separate dictionary strip for tendril.

**Scoring.** A1 explanation: three or more steps connected by cause (because, so, then because of that). Key: pollination can help seeds form; seeds can grow into new plants; their flowers can feed later visitors. Wrong: "the bee carries the seeds to the next flower" (A); the facts said as a list (B). Both attempts are kept: independent in pencil, supported in a second color.
- **RI.2.10 (A1).** Independent attempt and supported attempt kept apart; a source contradiction is C; understanding after hearing the passage is supported evidence.
- **RI.2.7.** "What does the diagram show that the words do not?" Key: arrows show a chain across generations, ending in food for a later visitor. Wrong: one bee flies along every arrow (C).
- **RI.2.4.** "What is nectar here? Which words tell you?" Key: a sweet liquid that feeds visitors, from "a sweet liquid that feeds visiting pollinators". Wrong: a seed (C).
- **RI.2.5.** "Which heading takes you straight to what the visitors eat? Point there." Key: New food. Wrong: starts at the top and reads on, ignoring the heading (AB).
- **RI.2.2.** "Say the whole topic and each paragraph's focus." Key: pollination supports later plants and visitors; first, new plants; second, new food. Wrong: names only new food as the topic (AC).
- **L.2.4.** Five cells (stored: put away safely, sentence; replant: plant again, prefix; useful: helpful, known root plus suffix; sunlight: light from the sun, compound; tendril: thin curling stem, dictionary strip). Wrong: replant means throw away (AP); prefix treated as meaningless (AL); a tendril guessed without the strip (AB).

**Cycle 13 written check** (day 82, 15 minutes, unscored). The teacher reads each sentence twice and says its missing word; children write the word in a printed gap. Five independent spellings, none a day 78 to 81 word. (1) "The ____ is hot on the stove." kettle. (2) "She tied a ____ of sticks." bundle. (3) "I write on ____." paper. (4) "A ____ lives in the zoo." tiger. (5) "We will play ____." tonight. Wrong: "ketl" and "bundl" omit the final syllable (Y); "kettel" and "bundel" swap the last two letters (AN); "tigr" and "papr" leave out the vowel of the second syllable (T); "tonite" writes the odd part as if regular (AM). After marking, dictate "paddle" and "silent" as unscored practice.

**Exit questions.** Spoken, one ask per stem, answered by the rotating six (about 20 seconds each); the rest tell a partner and the teacher notes hands. Shown is the right answer in the child's words; shown with help is right after one prompt ("What does the card say?"); not yet seen is another answer or none. None reuses a check case or a taught worked example.
- **E82** (day 82). Card: "A moth lands on a pale flower at night. Her long tongue uncurls and reaches the sweet juice deep inside. Pollen dusts her tongue. She flies away, and some pollen brushes onto the next flower." Item (a): "Pick a word that stops you. Which strategy card do you use, and what does the word mean?" Key (any one): uncurls, word parts, "un- undoes curl: the tongue unrolls"; dusts, sentence clue, "covers lightly, from the pollen on her tongue"; brushes, sentence clue, "touches lightly and leaves some". Items (c) and (d) are on article 01-b, paragraph 3 (A special way to shake a flower), read twice: "Say the steps in order." and "Why does the pollen fall out?" Key: the bumblebee holds on, buzzes, and the shaking makes pollen fall out; because the buzzing shakes it out. Wrong: "uncurls means curls" (AL); a strategy named with no clue (AB); "because the bee drinks nectar" (C); steps as a list (B).
- **E84** (day 84, the retry). Article 01-b, paragraph 2 (How a bee moves pollen), read twice. Item: "Say how the steps connect, with because or so." Key: some pollen stays on her fur, so when she lands on the next flower some brushes onto the stigma. Then: "Where does the pollen brush off?" Key: onto the stigma of the next flower. Wrong: "the bee carries the seeds" (A); steps as a list (B); "in her nest" (C). Scored with A1 on day 85 as the revision.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The strategy fan (sentence clue, word parts, dictionary strip) is three cards on a ring; the teacher holds it on day 82 and each pair holds one set from day 82 to day 84; it was built from the unit 2 sentence-clue and root card, flip card and dictionary practice card. The step chain and arrow cards (day 79) and the diagram card (unit 1, day 15) stay on the wall. Marked papers go home only after day 85 scoring; the pair of colors (pencil for independent, a second color for supported) is the record. The sound-spelling-map, the syllable-types chart, the word wall, the talk routine and the heard-six column stay up.
- **Standard rotation (foundations).** Three stations, tiles, sort and reread, ten children each; the day's six sit at the teacher table for round 1, two minutes each, then take the other stations. Check days use the 25-minute form.
- **Key moment 1, day 83: independent, then supported reading of one card.** Ideal instrument: two pencil colors on one printed card. Spec: fits; paper. Gaps and asks: none.
- **Key moment 2, day 84: group time by recorded letter.** Ideal instrument: the day 83 feedback row and a color-coded group list. Spec: fits; paper.
- **Tools.** None. No account is needed.
- **A room with nothing.** Three strategy cards drawn on card; a dictionary strip written on a slip; two pencils or one pencil and one crayon; tiles are letters on card.

## 6. Days

## Day 82


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E81 (arrow 2, A or C), show the flower picture and have pairs trace the arrow from one flower to the next and say what moves; otherwise retrieve the step chain from memory.
- **5 min. Say the plan.** Today you read a new paragraph and choose your own strategy for a hard word. The strategy fan has three cards: sentence clue, word parts, dictionary strip. You say what you chose and what clue it gave. Nothing is new; you choose.
- **8 min. Listen and read.** Teacher reads the invented card "Mira's Beans" aloud twice, then children read it once with a finger and star a word that stops them. Card, about 60 words: Mira's garden has a bean plant. Its flowers are small and white. A bee lands on one and drinks the sweet juice inside. Dusty pollen sticks to her fuzzy legs. The bee is careless about the dust; she does not mind it. She flies to the next bean flower, and some pollen falls onto its sticky top. Soon that flower starts a bean pod.
- **14 min. Choose and use.** Pairs pick a strategy card for each starred word, use it and say "I used ___ because ___". Planned words: careless (word parts), mind (sentence clue), pod (dictionary strip, which the teacher gives: pod, a case that holds seeds). The teacher hears six pairs (1 minute each); the others work in pairs.
- **12 min. Explain the process.** Pairs retell the paragraph as a chain with the step chain cards: "First ___. Then ___. That is why ___." Each pair gives the class one link. The teacher hears the six (1 minute each).
- **11 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which strategy helped you most, and what did the word turn out to mean?" Children say "I used ___ because ___"; the next child links with "You said ___; I would add ___". Hear the six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E82 (field 3).
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** "Choose and use" (a strategy for a starred word) and "Explain the process" (the steps, then why) are the exit's shape. Instead: sort the starred words into strategy piles by the clue they carry, and put a scrambled step chain in order, or judge a given strategy claim.
- **15 min. Written check (foundations).** Cycle 13 written check (field 3), unscored.
- **5 min. Reader.** Reread A Little Table in pairs for expression.
- **25 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation; the six read the oral card for the whole cycle (12 minutes), the rest in stations. Group 5 of five.
- **15 min. Fresh response.** Day 78's six read cycle 13 words to the teacher (12 minutes, 2 minutes each) while the others partner-read a pattern card and spell two dictated words; no new teaching.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the strategy fan; the teacher holds the step chain. Tool: None. By hand: the star, the strategy choice, spelling five words. Evidence: E82; the cycle 13 check; the oral card records (group 5 of five, the last of the cycle); the heard column.

## Day 83


- **5 min. A1: read alone.** One minute to say the plan: today is a check, not a lesson; pollination moves pollen, and seeds travelling is a different thing; I do not help on the first read. Then each child reads the Next Spring card (about 70 words and its diagram) alone with a finger. Record: read alone.
- **10 min. A1: explain in pencil.** Each child explains how three or more steps connect on the printed organizer: boxes for the steps, arrows, and a few words on each arrow (because, so). Teacher scribes for a child who asks. No other help.
- **7 min. A1: supported reread.** Teacher reads the card aloud once; children reread it with a finger and add to or change their explanation in the second color. Record: supported.
- **3 min. Collect.** Papers in; the teacher gives written feedback by row after class (A1, RI.2.10 and C1), not today.
- **5 min. Exit.** No new case: each child tells a partner, then writes one word, which strategy helped most in the C1 cells and why. Unscored; the teacher notes hands.
- **3 min. C1: the diagram.** RI.2.7: "What does the diagram show that the words do not?" answered in a few words or a drawing. The teacher reads the stem twice.
- **2 min. C1: a topic word.** RI.2.4: "What is nectar here? Which words tell you?" Written, a few words.
- **2 min. C1: a heading.** RI.2.5: "Which heading takes you straight to what the visitors eat? Point there." Pointing counts.
- **3 min. C1: topic and focus.** RI.2.2: "Say the whole topic and each paragraph's focus." Written or drawn on a blank topic and focus chart.
- **10 min. C1: the word card.** L.2.4: the five words, one ask each ("What does ___ mean? Which clue or card did you use?"), on the printed word card with the separate dictionary strip. The teacher reads each stem twice; answers written in a few words.
- **10 min. Quiet reread.** Independent quiet rereading of 01-a; no teaching; the teacher scores nothing and confers with no one.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Cycle 13 check repair: the commonest miss of field 3 (Y, AN, T or AM), the wrong spelling beside the right one, written on the board.
- **15 min. Teach: C-le continued.** Retrieve the -le syllable (table, kettle). Teach -ble, -dle, -kle and -zle after a short vowel (ankle, puzzle); model, map, blend and spell each. Two-syllable long vowels: robot, spider. Tricky words (from memory, source to check): again, said. Dictate a sentence with puzzle.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 14 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 1 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "What are the last two letters of ankle?"

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the printed cards and two pencil colors. Tool: None. By hand: reading, boxes and arrows, writing a few words. Evidence: A1 in two colors; RI.2.10 independent and supported records; the C1 cells; the heard column (heard-six and oral card).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 82, "Which strategy helped you most, and what did the word turn out to mean?"; day 83, none (check, no talk questions); day 84, "How does one step make the next possible?"

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: strategy, clue, process, step, connect, explain, retry, pod, careless. Frames: "I used ___ because ___." "First ___. So ___." "This word means ___. The clue is ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer on the heading and diagram items; cards are large print; a child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes; the dictionary strip is read aloud.
- **Check accommodations.** Every stem is read aloud twice; the child may point or draw; extra time within the 20 minutes; a child may read the card aloud to the teacher instead of silently, recorded as independent only if no help is given.
- **Reading load.** At most 150 printed words a day: Mira's Beans (about 60), Next Spring (about 70 with labels), the word card (about 40); the 01-b paragraphs are read aloud, not printed.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a fourth link to the chain from the text (the nectar feeds a later visitor) and explain it with because, or explain why seed dispersal is not the same as pollination.
