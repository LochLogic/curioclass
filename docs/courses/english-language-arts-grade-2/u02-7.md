# Section 2.7: Planning, drafting and finishing a fossil narrative

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-2/2/s/2.7), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-2 u02-7.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read the story pages they were read before, short cards, phrase strips and their own draft; the teacher reads every stem aloud, and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. This section builds one practice narrative: an illustrated story of one event, about a find at a fossil park (a curved line, a small bump, a ridge, a hole in a shell). The practice problem is a find and what to check about it; the problems of the day 69 prompt (a label that falls, a shadow, a smudge) are not used. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud.

- **L.2.1f**, "Produce, expand, and rearrange complete simple and compound sentences." Day 60, with movable phrase strips and sentences about finds. The day 70 item is owned here.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned** (rows above; names): 68-RL.2.5 and 70-L.2.1f. Both sit on cold texts (03-check68 Dev's Puzzle; the day 70 sentence card is about 03-check69), so nothing here uses either. Teaching avoids the day 68 answer (a missing piece found in a box lid, a finished puzzle) and the day 70 series (carry the box, wait for the teacher, open the glass case); the sentence models use Wren, finds and beaches instead. Keys and error letters are as the table gives them; marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

**Exit questions.** A reading exit marked Passage asks on a named part of the unit's own texts, read aloud twice, never on a card and never on the passage the day works as its example; for those exits, "the card" below means that passage. Each exit is one ask on an invented card, with two items on the days that test two things (a and b, 54, 55, 61, 66 and 67). The teacher reads each card twice (days 65 to 67 and 71 use a printed card), asks the one question, and hears the rotating six (about 20 seconds each); the rest tell a partner. Shown means the answer is right alone; shown with help means it is right after one prompt ("What does the card say?"); not yet seen means no right answer. There are no hold-up pictures: the ask alone carries the check. No card reuses an article sentence, a worked example, a shared piece or an end item. Items.csv holds the keys.
- **E59** (day 59, one ask). Passage: Rob Is Skipping, pages 1 to 6, read aloud twice: the beginning (can Rob beat Ann?) and the ending (Rob has won) (g2-u2-cr9). Read Rob Is Skipping aloud twice. Ask: "Tell how the end of the story closes what its start began." Answer: The beginning starts a race: can Rob beat Ann? The ending answers it: finally Rob has won, and Ann claps. Wrong: "Rob is skipping" (P); "Rob trips on a rock" (P).
- **E60** (day 60, one ask). Card: "Pru holds the lamp. Her friend sorts the stones." Ask: "Say both jobs as one sentence that uses the word and." Answer: Pru holds the lamp, and her friend sorts the stones. Wrong: "While Pru holds the lamp" (AD).
- **E61** (day 61, two items, each one ask). Item (a) Card: "Ana lifted the lid of an old box. Was something moving inside? She felt scared, and then she felt glad when it was a wind-up frog." Ask: "Tell me what Ana thought." Answer: She wondered whether something was moving inside the box. Wrong: "She lifted the lid" (M). Item (b) Card: "Ana lifted the lid of an old box. Was something moving inside? She felt scared, and then she felt glad when it was a wind-up frog." Ask: "Tell me how Ana felt." Answer: Scared, and then glad. Wrong: "She lifted the lid" (N).
- **E62** (day 62, one ask). Card: "Zuri needed a clean edge on her drawing. She checked the rock. She traced the line. Last she shaded the edge." Ask: "Say the first two actions using First and Next." Answer: First she checked the rock. Next she traced the line. Wrong: "She checked the rock and she traced the line" (O).
- **E63** (day 63, one ask). Card: "First Ivo saw a shiny coin in the sand. Next he brushed it off." Ask: "Write one closing sentence that finishes the story." Answer: A closing that ties to the coin and uses a time word, such as "At last he saw it was an old button.". Wrong: "Ivo had a sandwich for lunch" (P); "The coin was an old button" (O).

**Cycle 11 written check** (day 62, 15 minutes, unscored). The teacher reads each sentence twice and says its missing word; children write the word in a printed gap. Four independent spellings; none is a day 58 to 61 word, and none is moon, soup, fruit, blue, flew, juice, pool or spoon (juice, pool and spoon are in the reader). (1) "She swept with a ____." broom. (2) "Our ____ sat down." group. (3) "We go on a ____." cruise. (4) "Put on the ____." glue. Wrong: "brume" or "brum", "grup", "cruze" or "gloo" put a different spelling on the vowel sound (Y); a plain "brm" or "grp" tag the letter that fits. After marking, dictate "cool" and "new" as unscored practice.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The five-box event sheet (time word, action, thought, feeling, closing) from day 45 is held by each child on days 59 to 63; the plan picture cards (a curved line, a small bump, a ridge, a groove, a hole) by pairs on day 59; the phrase strips by pairs on day 60; the feelings word bank on day 61; the draft page (an opening, First, Next, Then, At last) on days 62 and 63. The story is a read-aloud set of pages kept on the wall. The word wall holds says, once, would and the cycle 11 words.
- **Standard rotation (foundations).** Three stations, tiles (spell the day's words), sort (card sort by pattern) and reread (partner reread of the reader or phrase cards), ten children each. The day's six sit at the teacher table for round 1, two minutes each, then take the other stations.
- **Key moment 1, day 60: phrase strips.** Ideal instrument: movable strips for who, what and a detail, on a pocket card. Spec: fits; paper.
- **Key moment 2, day 62: the first draft.** Ideal instrument: a draft page with five labeled lines and a space for a picture. Spec: fits; paper. A shared text editor (grade 1, day 67) comes on day 66 and not here.
- **Tools.** None in this section.
- **A room with nothing.** The five boxes are a folded sheet; the strips are cut from a page; the feelings bank is written on the wall; the draft is on lined paper with a drawn frame.

## 6. Days

## Day 59


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E58 (H), flip the prefix card and say not; otherwise retrieve the word-part cards.
- **5 min. Words.** Narrative, event, beginning, ending: each said once, shown on the story-map and used in a sentence about the story.
- **10 min. Reread the story.** The teacher rereads the first page (the class arrives at the fossil park) and the last page (the bus ride home); children point to who is there at the start and what Amara knows at the end. They say how the beginning sets up the story and how the ending finishes it.
- **10 min. Model: plan one event.** The teacher plans a find for Cal, an invented child: a ridge on a river stone. Fill the five-box event sheet: First (time word), brushed (action), "Is it a root?" (thought), curious (feeling), drew it (closing). Say each box aloud and that one event stays in the boxes.
- **12 min. Pairs plan.** Each child takes one find card and fills the five-box sheet with a few words and a picture, saying each box to a partner; the partner checks that the last box answers the first.
- **7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "How does your ending finish what your beginning started?" Hear the six.
- **6 min. Draw and label.** Each child draws the event in the last box and labels it with a few words.
- **5 min. Exit.** E59.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Reread six oo and ou cards; reblend any wrong vowel team (Y).
- **15 min. Teach: ui and ue, then the first reading.** Retrieve oo and ou; model mapping suit, bruise (ui) and clue, Sue (ue); blend, then spell suit and clue; map says. Pairs read the first part of the cycle 11 reader (a baby animals at the zoo story, adapted or written to the cycle's patterns); never guess a word from its picture.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 2 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which two letters make the sound in clue?"

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the five-box sheet; pairs hold the find cards. Tool: None. By hand: filling the boxes, drawing, writing a few words, tiles. Evidence: E59; the plan sheets; the heard column.

## Day 60


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E59 (P), put the first box beside the last box and ask "Did the end answer the start?"; otherwise retrieve the plan sheets.
- **5 min. Words.** Produce, expand, rearrange, and: each said once, with a sentence on the wall.
- **8 min. Reread.** The teacher rereads the story page where Amara brushes the stone; children say the sentence and point to the detail words (gently, dust, careful).
- **12 min. Model: make it, make it longer, move it.** Simple: "Wren found a shell." Expand: "Wren found a small shell on the beach." Rearrange, keeping the meaning: "On the beach, Wren found a small shell." Compound: "Wren found a shell, and her friend found a bone." Expand and rearrange it too. Each time ask "Is it a whole sentence? Does it say the same thing?"
- **12 min. Pairs build with strips.** Pairs build a simple and a compound sentence from phrase strips about their own find, add one detail strip, and rearrange one.
- **7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Does the sentence still mean the same thing after you moved the strip?" Hear the six.
- **6 min. Write.** Each child writes one simple and one compound sentence about their plan, with a few words.
- **5 min. Exit.** E60.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Reread six ui and ue cards; reblend any wrong vowel team (Y).
- **15 min. Teach: ew, then reread and sort.** Model mapping new, grew and crew (ew); blend, then spell crew. Reread the cycle 11 reader with a partner; sort 12 cards into oo, ou, ui, ue and ew; map once.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 3 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which spellings can make the sound in new?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the phrase strips. Tool: None. By hand: building and moving strips, writing a few words, sorting, tiles. Evidence: E60; the written sentences; the heard column.

## Day 61


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E60 (AD), read the strip aloud and ask "Does it tell a whole thing?"; otherwise retrieve the phrase strips.
- **5 min. Words.** Elaborate, action, thought, feeling, rehearse: each said and shown on the five-box sheet.
- **8 min. Reread.** The teacher rereads the story pages where Amara wonders and where she feels disappointed and excited; children point to a thought and a feeling.
- **12 min. Model: rehearse before you write.** The teacher stands for Cal and acts the action (kneels, brushes), says the thought aloud ("Is it a root?") and the feeling ("I felt curious"), then says "Now I can write each one." One actor performs; the class watches and names each box.
- **12 min. Pairs rehearse.** In each pair one child performs their plan (action, thought, feeling) while the partner holds the five-box sheet and ticks each box; then they swap.
- **7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which words showed the thought, and which showed the feeling?" Hear the six.
- **6 min. Write.** Each child adds a thought and a feeling to the plan sheet, using the feelings word bank, with a few words.
- **5 min. Exit.** E61.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Reread six ew and mixed vowel team cards; reblend any wrong vowel team (Y).
- **15 min. Transfer to a fresh passage.** Pairs read a short fresh passage that uses only cycle 11 patterns and the mapped word would; confirm meaning after decoding; the teacher dictates one sentence.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 4 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which word did you decode, then check?"

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the five-box sheet and the feelings word bank. Tool: None. By hand: acting, ticking boxes, writing a few words, tiles. Evidence: E61; the plan sheets; the heard column.

## Day 62


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E61 (M, N), put a thought bubble beside the boxes and point to the feelings word bank; otherwise retrieve the plan sheets.
- **5 min. Words.** Opening, first, next, then, at last: each said, with the time-word card.
- **8 min. Reread.** The teacher rereads the story pages that begin First, Next, Then and At last; children point to each time word and say what came in that part.
- **10 min. Model: draft an opening and two ordered events.** The teacher writes for Cal on a large page: "On a cool morning, Cal went to the river." "First he brushed the ridge gently." "Next he wondered, Is it a root?" Read each line aloud and ask "What does the time word tell us?"
- **7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Why does First go before Next?" Hear the six.
- **20 min. Draft.** Each child drafts an opening, First and Next on the draft page with a picture, using their plan sheet; the teacher moves and gives no wording.
- **5 min. Exit.** E62.
- **15 min. Written check (foundations).** Cycle 11 written check (field 3), unscored.
- **5 min. Reader.** Reread the cycle 11 reader for expression.
- **25 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation; the six read the oral card for the whole cycle (12 minutes), the rest in stations. Group 5 of five.
- **15 min. Fresh response.** Day 58's six read the cycle words to the teacher (12 minutes, 2 minutes each) while the others partner-read a pattern card and spell two dictated words; no new teaching.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the plan sheet and the draft page. Tool: None. By hand: drafting, drawing, writing a few words, spelling on paper. Evidence: E62; the draft opening and details; the check notes.

## Day 63


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E62 (O), slide a time-word card before each action; otherwise retrieve the draft pages.
- **5 min. Words.** Closure, finish, conclude: each said once and used about a story's end.
- **8 min. Reread and role-play.** The teacher rereads the last page, where Amara tells Ben; one child acts Amara, the teacher reads the words, and the class says how the ending closes the story. One actor performs.
- **8 min. Model: Then, At last and a closing.** The teacher writes for Cal: "Then he drew the lines." "At last his drawing showed the ridge clearly." Ask "Does the end answer the start?"
- **18 min. Write.** Each child finishes the draft with Then, At last and a closing that ties to the find, using a time word and the plan sheet.
- **5 min. Illustrate.** Each child adds a picture that shows the event's end.
- **6 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** Partners read drafts aloud; the listener points to the time words and the closing. Hear the six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E63.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Reread six cycle 11 words and the wrong-spelling misses from the day 62 check (Y, AA).
- **15 min. Teach: -tion.** Review from unit 1 that endings are read as chunks; model mapping nation, section and collection with -tion saying /shun/; blend by chunk, then spell nation and section; map said.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 1 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which letters say /shun/ at the end of nation?"

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the draft page and the plan sheet. Tool: None. By hand: writing a few lines, drawing, reading aloud, tiles. Evidence: E63; the finished draft (kept for feedback after class); the heard column.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 59, "How does your ending finish what your beginning started?"; day 60, "Does the sentence still mean the same thing after you moved the strip?"; day 61, "Which words showed the thought, and which showed the feeling?"; day 62, "Why does First go before Next?"; day 63, "Does the end answer the start?"

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Earlier work returning.** The five-box event sheet (day 45) and the story-map with a beginning and an ending (days 34, 36) return on day 59; the time words First, Next, Then and At last come from grade 1; the sentence skill from grade 1 returns on day 60; the day 57 cycle 10 spellings return in dictation.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: narrative, event, beginning, ending, expand, rearrange, thought, feeling, closing. Frames: "First I ___. Then I ___." "I wondered, ___?" "I felt ___." "At last ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer; the boxes can be filled by drawing; the strips are large print. A child who cannot write the sentence dictates it and the teacher scribes.
- **Check accommodations.** Day 62's spelling check can be answered with tiles; cards are read aloud twice.
- **Reading load.** At most 150 printed words a day: the strips, the story pages and the child's own draft.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write a second ending for the same find that still finishes the event, and say which fits better.

- **One projector.** Show the five-box sheet while the child speaks each box; show the draft page for the model on day 62.
