The following check texts are Curio Class originals, 2026 plan edition, ours. M, N, Q and R state facts checked against the background sources in field 10; they are not quotations. Keep them out of examples and practice until their assigned assessment. The wording below is the complete check text.

**M, Plants That Fit:** Include suitable native plants in a pollinator garden. These plants are adapted to the local climate and soil. Choosing plants suited to the place helps us plan a garden for local pollinators.

**N, Flowers After Sunset:** Include suitable night-blooming flowers in a pollinator garden. Some moths and bats visit flowers at night. Flowers that open at night can offer food when these visitors are active.

**Q, Flowers Together:** Plant suitable flowers in clumps. A clump is a group close together. Pollinators can find and use a group of flowers more easily than single flowers far apart. A clump helps make food easier to find.

**R, Leaves for Caterpillars:** A butterfly garden needs suitable host plants. Caterpillars eat the leaves of their host plants. Flowers can feed adult butterflies, but nectar alone cannot feed their caterpillars. Providing food for the young helps the next generation grow.
