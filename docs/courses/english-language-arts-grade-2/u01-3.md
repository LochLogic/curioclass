# Section 1.3: Reading schools

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-2/1/s/1.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-2 u01-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read the cycle 3 reader, labels, captions and the words to know; the teacher reads each article aloud and then partners reread parts. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cards are invented and the teacher says so aloud.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RI.2.1: "Ask and answer such questions as who, what, where, when, why, and how to demonstrate understanding of key details in a text."** Day 13 models one text-dependent question and an answer with a located detail, and partners practice both roles; days 14 and 15 apply it to the second and third articles.
- **RI.2.7: "Explain how specific images (e.g., a diagram showing how a machine works) contribute to and clarify a text."** Day 14 teaches the photograph (what it adds to the words); day 15 teaches the diagram, its labels and its order.
- **SL.2.2: "Recount or describe key ideas or details from a text read aloud or information presented orally or through other media."** Taught on day 13; practiced on day 14 when partners recount the new read-aloud before explaining its photograph. The day 31 item (field 3) is owned by section 1.6.
- **Foundations.** RF.2.3a, RF.2.3b and L.2.2d: cycle 3 (ee, ea, y) on days 13 to 15, with the written check on day 15. RF.2.4 to RF.2.4c and SL.2.1a to c, SL.2.3 run daily through the cycle reader and the talk routine.
- **Limits that bite here.** Articles are read aloud; children read the cycle reader, captions and the words to know. The articles say "many" and "some" where practices vary; read them so. No country is ranked; no child is asked about their own school, meals or home. Teacher lines at the foot of each article are said aloud before reading. Present-day claims in 02-b and 02-c were checked on 2026-11-19 and need a recheck before launch. No photograph shows a child; pictures and the diagram are made by Curio Class.
- **One word, one sense.** A "shift" is a part of the day a group is at school; a "diagram" is a labeled drawing that explains; a "photograph" is a picture of something real. "Turn" and "share" as before; the pupils "take turns" as helpers in 02-a (name it once as the grade 1 sense).
- **Connections.** Section 1.2 gave the picture card and the link card; this section applies the picture card to a photograph and a diagram. Section 1.4 begins informative writing from these articles.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A recount tells the key ideas in order and drops the small ones (error N). A photograph or diagram clarifies when the child says what it shows that the words do not (error C). A diagram's order comes from its arrows and labels, not from the left-to-right habit alone (error A).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none on days 13 to 15. The day 31 row (SL.2.2) in the table above belongs to section 1.6.

**Exit questions.** Each reading exit is one spoken ask on a part of the day's text that the day does not work through, reread aloud twice (named in each line); a talk or fluency exit stays on an invented card printed "invented for practice". Nothing in the question gives the answer, and a child cannot guess it from a choice. The day's rotating six answer aloud, about 20 seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Shown means the answer is right and uses a word of the card; shown with help means it is right after one prompt ("What does the card say?"); not yet seen means a wrong answer or none. The cards and the answers are in items.csv (items.csv is truth where this list differs). If many miss one, the next day's opening repair shows its wrong example beside the right one.
- **E13** (day 13). Read aloud once, then once more: "Pupils put question slips in a bag. The teacher reads a slip. Partners find its answer in a book." Say: "Tell me the key ideas of the card in order." Answer: Pupils collect questions, the teacher reads one, and partners find its answer in a book. Wrong: It is about bags (N).
- **E14** (day 14). Reread aloud twice the 02-a paragraph that begins "Look at lunchtime." Show the 02-a photograph. Say: "Tell me one thing the photograph shows that the words do not say." Answer: A cart by the door holds a covered pot, a stack of bowls and a ladle, so you see how the lunch is carried in and served. Wrong: It has a classroom (C); The pupils are eating (A).
- **E15** (day 15). Reread aloud twice the 02-c paragraph that begins "Some schools run in parts of the day." Show the diagram. Say: "Tell me how the diagram shows that one building holds two shifts." Answer: There is one school under the bar for the day; the morning shift bracket covers the left half and the afternoon shift bracket the right half, so two groups use one building at different times. Wrong: There are two buildings (A); The full day has four meals (A).

**Cycle 3 written check** (day 15, 15 minutes, unscored). The teacher reads each sentence twice and says its missing word; children write it in a printed gap. None is a day 11 to 14 word. (1) "Put the ____ of paper on the desk." sheet. (2) "A ____ fell from the tree." seed. (3) "It is a ____ day." windy. (4) "The ____ is on the shelf." teapot. Wrong spellings tagged: "shet" or "sead" (Q, spelled by sound, pattern not carried over); "wundy" (M); "windee" (Q, y at the end of a word).

## 6. Days

## Day 13


- **5 min. Opening repair.** Repair the commonest day 12 retry letter, or retrieve the link card.
- **4 min. Words.** Cooperate and helpers, acted out.
- **8 min. Read article 02-a, Learning Together.** Teacher reads aloud with the map and the photograph; say the teacher line first. About 214 words.
- **12 min. Ask and answer with a located detail.** Model: "Why do schools do this?" Point to the line that answers it. Then model the partner role: the partner finds the line and says "The article says ___".
- **12 min. Recount.** Teach the recount strip with the text covered: first, then, last. Model three key ideas of 02-a and one small detail that is left out.
- **14 min. Partners practice both roles.** One asks a question of the article and the other answers with a line; swap; then each recounts to a partner. Hear the six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E13.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Retrieve ee and ea: map need and beak, blend, spell.
- **15 min. Reread and sort.** Reread The Green Beanbag; sort twelve words into ee, ea and y; spell two sentences; find the one wrong word in a printed sentence.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation; the day's six read the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.**

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the recount strip; the teacher holds the article. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, tiles. Evidence: E13; heard column.

## Day 14


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E13 (N), cover the text and tell three ideas in order; otherwise retrieve the recount strip.
- **4 min. Words.** Library and volunteer, acted out.
- **10 min. Read article 02-b, A Place to Read.** Teacher reads aloud with the map, covering the photograph; say the teacher line first and say "in 2025" as the article does. About 242 words.
- **8 min. Partners recount.** Each recounts the article to a partner from the strip before looking at the photograph. Hear the six.

- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation; the six read the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.**

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the picture card. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, tiles. Evidence: E14; heard column.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 13, "Why do many Japanese schools have pupils clean?"; day 14, "What does the photograph show about a place to read?"; day 15, "What does the diagram explain?".
- Written task: none (writing begins on day 16); the day 15 check is the spelling check.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) answers with a located line; (2) tells the ideas in order; (3) says what the photograph or diagram adds.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Days 13 to 15 open with one repair of at most 5 minutes, for the commonest exit miss; if none, a retrieval of the day before.
- **Oral card.** The rotating six read six words and one sentence, using patterns taught earlier. The record sheet has a heard column.
- **Fluency target.** A smooth second reading of the cycle reader by day 15, accuracy first; no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: cooperate, library, volunteer, shift, schedule, diagram. Frames: "The article says ___." "First ___, then ___, last ___." "The diagram shows ___."
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer. Large print captions; labels in contrasting colors. A child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards are read aloud; the spelling check can be answered with tiles.
- **Reading load.** A child reads the reader (about 60 words), captions and words to know; the articles are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Recount in order and name a second thing the picture adds.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read one article aloud and ask one question; let the child point to the line and tell it back in order with three paper boxes.
- **One projector.** Show the photograph and diagram beside the lines; mark on printed copies.
