# Section 3.1: Pollination as a chain of steps

## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 2. Unit 3, The secret world of pollination. *How do plants and pollinators help each other?* (days 78 to 118): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 78 to 81.** Days by kind: understanding 4 (78, 79, 80, 81). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 1 of 12 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read short labels, the five steps of article 01-a and the cycle 13 reader; the teacher reads 01-a first and every stem aloud, and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: 01-a holds plants, bees, pollen, nectar and one food fact; no sting, injury, death or home-life fact appears. A bee is never handled.

Each step's "because" needs the step before it to have happened. Facts rest on the Forest Service and Park Service pages named in text 01 (checked 2026-11-19).

- **E79** (day 79). Card, a true idea: "Pollen on the Wind. A corn plant has a tassel at the top. The wind shakes the tassel and pollen floats out. Some pollen lands on the silk of another corn plant. Then that plant can start making kernels." Pictures in order. Item (a): "Say the steps in order with First, Then, Next, Finally."
