# Section 3.1: Pollination as a chain of steps

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-2/3/s/3.1), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-2 u03-1.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read short labels, the five steps of article 01-a and the cycle 13 reader; the teacher reads 01-a first and every stem aloud, and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: 01-a holds plants, bees, pollen, nectar and one food fact; no sting, injury, death or home-life fact appears. A bee is never handled.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RI.2.3**, "Describe the connection between a series of historical events, scientific ideas or concepts, or steps in technical procedures in a text." This section carries scientific ideas and steps: day 78 hears the whole series in 01-a, day 79 teaches that each step makes the next possible (the step chain), day 80 reads the steps alone then with help, day 81 joins the steps to a diagram. The historical-events part is not met here (unit 2 and the grade 3 hand-off hold it).
- **RI.2.7**, "Explain how specific images (e.g., a diagram showing how a machine works) contribute to and clarify a text." Day 81 only: what the arrows show that the words do not.
- **RI.2.10**, "read and comprehend informational texts ... in the grades 2-3 text complexity band". Day 80: the child reads the steps page alone first, then with support; the two records stay apart and feed the range record, not a verdict.
- **RF.2.3c, e, f; RF.2.4, 4a to 4c; L.2.2d, 2e** (cycle 13, days 78 to 81): consonant-le words and two-syllable long vowels, the cycle's tricky words, reading the reader accurately and rereading to confirm meaning, spelling by pattern and checking with the word wall or a beginning dictionary. **SL.2.1a to 1c, SL.2.3**: turn, pair, link, clarify, every day.
- **Limits that bite.** 01-a is read aloud first; a child reads only the steps page (about 95 words) and labels. Say aloud on day 78 that pollination moves pollen and that seeds travelling is a different thing. The diagram shows a chain, not one bee's whole flight. No live insect is brought in; photographs and pictures are the close looks. One word, one sense: land means come down onto, plant means a living thing, visit means a short stay by a creature.
- **Connections.** Grade 1 connection arrow (a fact, an arrow, because or so) and syllable division are retrieved on day 78 (practice only). Unit 1 gives the talk routine, the heard-six column and the diagram card; unit 2 gives the sentence-clue card and the syllable-types chart. Science 2-LS2-2 comes later (weeks 23 to 25), so every plant fact is taught from 01-a now.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The anther makes pollen; pollen on the sticky stigma helps the plant start seeds. A bee does not mean to move pollen; some rubs off. Each step's "because" needs the step before it to have happened. Facts rest on the Forest Service and Park Service pages named in text 01 (checked 2026-11-19).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned.** None are scored here. This section prepares A1 and the RI.2.7 item of day 83 (section 3.2), and never uses Next Spring, its diagram, its nectar question or its seed chain as an example.

**Exit questions.** Each exit is spoken: a paragraph of an article of The Secret World of Pollination (g2-u3-01) not yet read in class (01-c, 01-d), read aloud twice or printed, then one or more items, each one ask, answered aloud by the rotating six (about 20 seconds each); the rest tell a partner and the teacher notes hands. Shown means the right answer in the child's words; shown with help means right after one prompt ("What does it say?"); not yet seen means another answer or none. None uses 01-a, the worked text, or a check case. Day 81's card is invented and printed "invented for practice". If many miss one, the next day's opening repair shows the wrong example beside the right one.
- **E78** (day 78). Article 01-c, paragraph 1, read twice ("probes" means pokes in to look). Item (a): "Join what the butterfly does and why, with so or because." Answer: "A butterfly probes a flower for nectar because nectar is its energy food." Wrong: two separate statements with no joining word (B); the link run backwards (C). Item (b), on 01-c paragraph 4: "Say the word for an animal like the butterfly that moves pollen between plants." Answer: pollinator.
- **E79** (day 79). Article 01-c, paragraph 3 (How pollen moves), read twice, then printed for each child. Item (a): "Say the steps in order with First, Then, Next, Finally." Item (b): "Why can the last step happen? Say because." Item (c): "Point to the sentence that says where some pollen comes off." Answer: (a) sips nectar or rests, pollen sticks to its body, flies to the next flower, some pollen comes off; (b) because pollen stuck to its body; (c) the last sentence. Wrong: "because the butterfly carries seeds" (A); the steps said as a list (B); the sentence about pollen sticking (C).
- **E80** (day 80). Article 01-d, paragraph 1 (Some flowers open after dark), printed; the child reads it. Item (a): the child reads it aloud; record alone, with help or not yet seen. Item (b): "Why do bats visit these flowers at night?" Answer: because the flowers open after dark, when the bats feed. Wrong: "seeds" (A); "because they are pale" (C).
- **E81** (day 81). A flower picture with three arrows and the card: "A bumblebee reaches into a bell-shaped flower. Pollen sticks to her. Then she flies to a second bell-shaped flower. Pollen touches its sticky top." Item: "Point to the arrow that matches 'Then she flies to a second bell-shaped flower' and say what moves along it." Answer: arrow 2, between the two flowers; pollen moves. Wrong: "the bee flies home" (C); "seeds fly" (A).
- Foundations has no exit; the close question and the oral card are the evidence (Field 6).

The step chain is new on day 79: five strip cards (First, Then, Next, After that, Finally) with arrow cards between; the teacher holds it on day 79, pairs on day 80, and a wall copy stays up to day 118. The diagram card with movable labels (unit 1, day 15) returns on day 81 with the flower diagram of 01-a. The sound-spelling-map, the syllable-types chart (now with a sixth type, consonant-le, added on day 78), the word wall, the talk routine and the heard-six column stay up.

- **Key moment 1, day 79: the step chain with one card taken away.** Ideal instrument: a wall chain of movable step cards and arrow cards. Spec: fits; paper. Gaps and asks: none.
- **Key moment 2, day 81: the flower diagram with its arrows.** Ideal instrument: a large flower picture with removable arrows. Spec: fits; paper.
- **Tools.** None. No account is needed.
- **A room with nothing.** The step chain is five strips of paper; arrows are drawn on torn card; tiles are letters on card; the word wall is a sheet on the board.

## 6. Days

## Day 78


- **5 min. Hook.** Three picture cards on the board: a flower, a bee on it, a bowl of fruit. "Who helps a plant make the fruit we eat?" Each child tells a partner a guess; the teacher takes three. Say aloud that 01-a is true and every practice card is invented.
- **7 min. Words before reading.** Pollen, pollination, nectar, pollinator, transfer, connect, sequence, cling, carry: each said by the class and shown on a picture card or acted out (cling: fingers on a sleeve) once, about 45 seconds a word.
- **10 min. Read aloud: Pollen on the Move.** Teacher reads 01-a (about 330 words) with the pictures, pausing at each numbered step. Say aloud that pollination moves pollen and that seeds travelling is a different thing, met later in science.
- **4 min. Retrieval (grade 1).** The connection arrow on a familiar pair from unit 2 (a bone is buried; then rock closes round it): "___ so ___." Unscored; a miss gets the two-card model on day 79.
- **15 min. Bridge: plant facts from the text.** Teacher models labelling a flower from 01-a (petal, anther, stigma, nectar) in 5 minutes. Pairs then sort nine picture cards into three piles, "makes or holds pollen", "gets pollen", "carries pollen" (8 minutes), and the class shares one card from each pile (2 minutes). All facts come from 01-a.
- **5 min. The compelling question.** Post "How do plants and pollinators help each other?" Each child draws one thing the bee gets and one thing the flower gets, a first idea kept unscored for day 118.
- **9 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "What does the bee get from the flower, and what does the flower get from the bee?" Children say "The bee gets ___". Hear the six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E78.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** Retrieval (grade 1) asks the child to join a familiar pair with "___ so ___", the same ask as the exit (join two things with so or because). Instead: lay two linked sentences, one with a wrong link, and have the pair judge and fix the given one.
- **5 min. Retrieval (foundations).** In 4 minutes divide and read sunshine and picnic (grade 1 syllables); in 1 minute explain the oral card for cycle 13. Unscored.
- **15 min. Teach: consonant-le.** The last of the six syllable types: a consonant plus le makes the last syllable. Model handle: map /h/ /a/ /n/, then the syllable dle (han-dle), blend, spell with tiles. Then giggle and waddle (the cycle overview words) and table (open first syllable, long a). Tricky words one and guess are starred on the odd part, never guessed from the picture.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 13 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 1 of five at the oral card. Oral card: grade 1 and unit 1 and 2 words only today.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which letters make the last syllable of handle?"

Who holds what is carried: the teacher (connection arrow, syllable chart). Tool: None. By hand: sorting cards, drawing, tiles. Evidence: E78; the retrieval notes; the first-idea drawings; the heard column (heard-six and oral card).

## Day 79


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E78 (B), lay the arrow card because between the two butterfly facts and read the link aloud; otherwise retrieve the connection arrow on one new pair.
- **8 min. Model connected rereading.** Teacher rereads steps 1 to 3 of 01-a aloud, then rereads step 1 before step 2 and says "Step 2 can happen because step 1 happened." Children chorus the because for steps 3 and 4.
- **12 min. Build the step chain.** Five strip cards, First, Then, Next, After that, Finally, each with a three-word picture label (lands, reaches in, flies on, rubs off, seeds start). Teacher builds arrows 1 and 2 with arrow cards (so); the 15 pairs build arrows 3 and 4.
- **5 min. Read the cards.** Pairs read the five labels and the order words aloud in turn (about 25 printed words).
- **5 min. Take one away.** Teacher takes the card "lands" off the chain: "Can the next step still happen?" The class reads the text lines "If the bee never lands, the pollen never sticks." Each step makes the next possible.
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "How does one step make the next possible?" Children say "Step ___ happens because ___". Hear the six.
- **10 min. Write: two linked steps.** Each child writes "First ___. Because of that, ___." using two neighbouring cards, a few words each, and draws the two steps; teacher scribes for a child who asks.
- **5 min. Exit.** E79.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The "because" chorus on steps 3 and 4, "Take one away" and the written two linked steps all ask for steps in order and a because, the exit's shape. Instead: pairs put a scrambled step chain in order with the order-word cards, or judge a given chain that has one card swapped.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** If many missed a syllable division on day 78 (grade 1 retrieval), map han-dle and sun-shine again; otherwise reread yesterday's -le words from cards.
- **15 min. Read: A Little Table.** First reading after pattern teaching: the teacher reads page 1 aloud, pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4 (words use only -le, unit 1 and 2 patterns and the tricky words I've, we've, guess, one, tonight). Check meaning after decoding. Text to make: a Curio Class reader, A Little Table (field 11).
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 13 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 2 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "What did the picture not tell you that the letters did?"

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the step chain; pairs hold the step cards. Tool: None. By hand: moving cards, writing two linked sentences, tiles. Evidence: E79; the written sentences; the heard column (heard-six and oral card).

## Day 80


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E79 (A), show a pollen-dust card and a seed card and have pairs match each to its caption; otherwise retrieve the step chain from memory.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Today you read the steps page alone first. Then I help, and you say it again. Both are marked, apart.
- **10 min. Read alone.** Each child reads the steps page of 01-a (steps 1 to 5, about 95 words) alone with a finger and the diagram beside it, and puts a star by a word that stopped them. Pencil down at the end.
- **10 min. Explain to a partner.** Partners take turns saying, without the page, how step 3 follows from step 2 and step 5 from step 4. The teacher hears the six (1 minute each) and records alone, with help or not yet seen; the other 24 partner-talk.
- **12 min. Supported reread.** Teacher reads aloud each starred word with its sentence and the class rereads the steps in two voices, stopping at each arrow card and reading the step before it (causal rereading). Pairs then explain the same two links again; this second explanation is recorded as with help.
- **8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which words tell you the order of the steps?" (First, Then, Next, After that, Finally). Children say "The word ___ tells me ___". Hear the six.
- **7 min. Write.** Each child writes "Because ___, ___." for one pair of steps, a few words each.
- **5 min. Exit.** E80.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** "Read alone" and the supported reread are the exit's shape (read a card aloud, say what moves). Instead: find and mark the line on the steps page that says what the bee carries, or judge a given partner answer, so reading is practiced without repeating the read-aloud ask.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Read six -le words from cards; remap the first syllable of any missed (T).
- **15 min. Reread and sort.** Reread A Little Table in pairs for accuracy. Then sort -le word cards by the first syllable: open and says its name (table, maple, title) or closed and short (little, bubble, puddle). Spell candle and apple on paper and use each in a sentence.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 13 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 3 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Say the ending of table: which two letters?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the step chain; each child holds the steps page. Tool: None. By hand: a finger on the line, a star by a stuck word, a written sentence, sorting cards. Evidence: E80; the alone and with-help record; the heard column (heard-six and oral card).

## Day 81


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E80 (C), reread the bat paragraph and have the child put a finger on the line that says when the flowers open; otherwise retrieve the step order.
- **7 min. Words for the diagram.** Anther, stigma, diagram, label, arrow, explain, cause, result, adheres: each said, shown on the flower picture or acted, about 45 seconds a word.
- **8 min. Look at the diagram.** Title, labels and the caption "Each arrow shows one step." Teacher models arrow 1 (anther to bee): "The arrow shows pollen moving." Pairs find arrows 2 to 4 and say what each shows.
- **12 min. Match steps to arrows.** Pairs lay the five step cards beside the four arrows of the diagram card and find the step with no arrow (step 1, the bee lands) and say why (words tell why the bee comes; the diagram shows where the pollen goes).
- **8 min. Words say, diagram shows.** Two chart columns. Model one of each (words: the bee smells the flower; diagram: pollen moves from the anther to a second flower). Pairs add one of each in turn.
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "What does the diagram clarify?" Children say "The diagram shows ___ that the words ___". Hear the six.
- **5 min. Write.** Each child writes one label (anther, stigma, nectar or pollen) and one sentence with cause and result: "Because ___, ___."
- **5 min. Exit.** E81.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** "Look at the diagram" and "Match steps to arrows" ask the child to point to an arrow and say what it shows, the exit's shape. Instead: label a blank diagram with the arrow cards, or judge a teacher statement about an arrow ("this arrow shows the bee flying home") and fix it.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Spell candle from memory; check it on the word wall (AN, Y).
- **15 min. Fresh passage and dictation.** Read the cycle 13 card (a fresh decodable passage of about 40 words, cycle 13 patterns only); confirm meaning after decoding. Dictate two -le words (gentle and bubble) and one sentence with guess or one. Text to make: the cycle 13 card (field 11).
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 13 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 4 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which letter in one is the odd part?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the diagram card and the step chain. Tool: None. By hand: moving arrow cards, writing one sentence, dictation. Evidence: E81; the written sentence; the dictation; the heard column (heard-six and oral card).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 79, "How does one step make the next possible?"; day 81, "What does the diagram clarify?"; day 78, "What does the bee get, and what does the flower get?"
- Written task, day 79: "First ___. Because of that, ___." with a drawing of the two steps, a few words each.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) the two steps are neighbours in 01-a and in its order; (2) a joining word (because, because of that, so) links them; (3) the fact is about pollen, not seeds.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Days 79 to 81 open with one repair of at most 5 minutes for the commonest exit miss; the foundations opening repairs the day before's commonest reading or spelling miss.
- **Oral card.** The rotating six read six words and one sentence each day, using only patterns taught on an earlier day; the record sheet has a heard column.
- **Fluency target.** A smooth second reading of A Little Table by day 80; accuracy first; no timer.
- **Earlier work returning.** The connection arrow (grade 1) on days 78 and 79; the sentence-clue card (unit 2) on day 80 for starred words; the diagram card (unit 1) on day 81; the question words card on day 78.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: pollen, nectar, pollinator, anther, stigma, arrow, cause, result, because, so. Frames: "First ___. Then ___." "Step 2 happens because ___." "The arrow shows ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer; step and arrow cards are large print and the arrows differ in color and shape; a child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes.
- **Check accommodations.** Paragraphs and cards are read aloud twice; the child may point to the arrow, the line or the picture; E80 may be answered after the teacher reads the paragraph.
- **Reading load.** A child reads at most 150 printed words a day: the steps page (about 95 words), five labels, the E79 paragraph (about 45 words) or the E80 paragraph (31 words). 01-a is read aloud first.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a sixth step to the chain from the text ("Seeds can grow into new plants") and say its link, or say what would happen to the chain if step 3 never happened.
