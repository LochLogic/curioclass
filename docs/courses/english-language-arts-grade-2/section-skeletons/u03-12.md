<!-- skel -->
- kind: Check; assessment: observe. An assessment day: its items sample only what earlier days taught.
- goal: Record the first groups' pollinator stories with clarifying drawings.
- note: Topic reading: g2-u3-02, g2-u3-01. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Audio tool on a teacher-held or shared device, second tool kind after unit 2 typing; record each child's detailed coherent story, drawing, and collaborative contribution. Class-only; partner or group recording offered. Live telling alone leaves SL.2.5 unfinished. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 19 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Catch-up group: map, blend, matched decodable, spell, reread, regroup; keep child-read band texts in the topic block. Sensitivity: no bee contact; allergies checked and supervision for outdoor looks; indoor photographs are complete. Audio stays in class; partner recording offered. Recording window for 30 children: day 117 records 20 at two minutes each plus ten minutes for changeovers, 50 topic minutes; day 118 records the remaining ten at two minutes each plus five minutes for changeovers, all done by topic minute 25. Each child tells an audible, coherent story with facts, descriptive details and complete sentences, and connects a drawing to the audio. Partner recording still identifies each contribution. RF.2.3c closing sample in foundations: six different children, two minutes each, 12 minutes today, including two-syllable long-vowel and consonant-le words. All 30 are heard once across days 113 to 117; day 118 retains that record. The complete g2-u3-02 bee-view story is an anchor here, read before the child's own story work. Each two-minute story turn includes a recorded coherent telling, relevant facts and descriptive details in an audible voice, complete sentences, and a drawing linked to the audio; retain the individual collaboration record across typing and audio.
