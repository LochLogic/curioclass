## 6. Days

## Day 43

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Application. Applies taught work to a new task or case (an inquiry, a lab, a draft, a revision or edit); nothing is taught as new.
- goal: Recount an Anning discovery with actions, thoughts, feelings, and time words.
- note: Topic reading: g2-u2-02. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision.  Topic base: M2 U1 L10. Foundations: base M2 C8 L36, 60 minutes, ild, old, ind, and ost; is-contractions. Retrieve known patterns; explicitly model, map sounds, blend, then spell. Decodable: adapt WheresGoldie for cycle 8 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Earlier-owned foundations skills, including e2-spelling and e2-fluency, are continued, not reassigned. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Sensitivity: fossil time is very long ago, no numerical scale or family-faith comparison; Anning avoids dwelling on loss; invented product names only. Parent RF.2.3 is partial through cycle 12; two-syllable and later prefix patterns continue in units 3 and 4. Cycle 8 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 1 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn.
