Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Catch-up group: map, blend, matched decodable, spell, reread, regroup; keep child-read band texts in the topic block. Sensitivity: no bee contact; allergies checked and supervision for outdoor looks; indoor photographs are complete. Audio stays in class; partner recording offered. Cycle 15 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 1 of five covers 30 once across the cycle.
