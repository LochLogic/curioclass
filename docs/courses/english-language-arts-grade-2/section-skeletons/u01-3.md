- **RF.2.4** (days 13, 14, 15): Read with sufficient accuracy and fluency to support comprehension.
- **RF.2.4a** (days 13, 14, 15): Read grade-level text with purpose and understanding.
- **RF.2.4b** (days 13, 14, 15): Read grade-level text orally with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings.
- **RF.2.4c** (days 13, 14, 15): Use context to confirm or self-correct word recognition and understanding, rereading as necessary.
- **RF.2.3a** (days 13, 14, 15): Distinguish long and short vowels when reading regularly spelled one-syllable words.
- **RF.2.3b** (days 13, 14, 15): Know spelling-sound correspondences for additional common vowel teams.
- **SL.2.1a** (days 13, 14, 15): Follow agreed-upon rules for discussions (e.g., gaining the floor in respectful ways, listening to others with care, speaking one at a time about the topics and texts under discussion).
- **SL.2.1b** (days 13, 14, 15): Build on others’ talk in conversations by linking their comments to the remarks of others.
- **SL.2.1c** (days 13, 14, 15): Ask for clarification and further explanation as needed about the topics and texts under discussion.
- **SL.2.3** (days 13, 14, 15): Ask and answer questions about what a speaker says in order to clarify comprehension, gather additional information, or deepen understanding of a topic or issue.
