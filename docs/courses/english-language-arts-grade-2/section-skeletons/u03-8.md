Foundations: base M3 C17 L85, 60 minutes, -ch and -tch; -er and -est. Cycle written check, 15 minutes, on only the completed cycle patterns and spelling; retain oral evidence from the five-day rotation, then use the final 15 minutes for a fresh response. Decodable: adapt WateringYourGarden for cycle 17 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Catch-up group: map, blend, matched decodable, spell, reread, regroup; keep child-read band texts in the topic block. Sensitivity: no bee contact; allergies checked and supervision for outdoor looks; indoor photographs are complete. Audio stays in class; partner recording offered. Cycle 17 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 5 of five covers 30 once across the cycle.
