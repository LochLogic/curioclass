# Section 4.7: The wildflower packet: steps, facts, feedback and editing

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **W.2.1, days 143 to 146:** "Write opinion pieces in which they introduce the topic or book they are writing about, state an opinion, supply reasons that support the opinion, use linking words (e.g., because, and, also) to connect opinion and reasons, and provide a concluding statement or section." One product over four days: plan (143), draft (144), revise (145), edit (146). The question is "Why should someone plant a suitable wildflower?" with two facts from 05.
- **RI.2.3, days 143 and 146:** "Describe the connection between a series of historical events, scientific ideas or concepts, or steps in technical procedures in a text." The text is 05-d, Planning a Flower Pot. Taught on 143 (why one step comes before the next); practiced on 146 on the class planting steps.
- **W.2.8, day 144:** "Recall information from experiences or gather information from provided sources to answer a question." Provided sources only: 05-a and 05-c. Each fact on the card is tagged with the letter of its article.
- **W.2.5, day 145:** "With guidance and support from adults and peers, focus on a topic and strengthen writing as needed by revising and editing." Revise on 145 after written feedback, edit on 146 in its own step.
- **L.2.5b and L.2.6, day 144:** exact and describing words are practiced in the draft (taught 138; review of section 4.6). **L.2.2a to c, day 146:** capitals for names, end marks, apostrophes and the greeting comma (review of unit 2, section u02-8).
- **Limits that bite here.** Planting is a class pot, a window box, a school bed or a park; no home yard, no sprays, no insects handled; paper seeds only in the packet, no planting or outdoor visit. The draft uses two facts from 05 and no other fact. Written feedback is one line a child, after class on 144.
- **The teacher page must explain.** Why a step must come before the next (the connection, not just the order); that a fact helps the opinion only if it speaks to the question; how revising (145) differs from editing (146); and that a right retry never replaces the first mark.

## 2. Where it sits

No readiness check. Exit marks, the draft before and after revision, and the edited packet go to the record sheet; the packet is scored later as W3 on 154.

Key: "Let us make a suggestion box." Spoken (E145b): "Say the draft again, joining the reason to the opinion with a link word." - **E146.** Passage: 05-b Room Without Sprays, paragraph 3 ("Garden experts give some ideas"), reread aloud twice; not 05-d, which the day rereads. Reread aloud twice 05-b, Room Without Sprays, paragraph 3, about what garden experts say to do instead. Show three step strips: A "Find out what the pest is." B "Change what drew the pest to the plant." C "Accept a few holes in the leaves." Say: "Mark the step that comes right before changing what drew the pest." Key: step A (find out what the pest is). Wrong: step C (accept a few holes in the leaves) (Z). Second ask, spoken, heard from the six: Same paragraph. Say: "Say why finding out the pest comes before changing what drew it." ## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Models carried in by children: the opinion parts and the exact-word ladder from 4.6, the claim-evidence-reasoning arrow, the editing checklist, the talk routine (turn, pair, link, clarify) and the heard-six column. The paragraph-frame stays withdrawn. Carried out to 4.8: the packet in its envelope, the drawing and the edited card.
- **Key moment 1, days 143 and 146: an ordered set of step strips.** Ideal: loose strips and a pocket chart. Spec: fits (paper strips on a wall). **Key moment 2, day 145: a draft and a one-line note side by side.** Ideal: the page with note attached. Spec: fits.
- **A room with nothing.** Steps copied on the board and numbered; children use folded paper for the envelope; pens in two colors.

## 6. Days

## Day 143


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening.** Retrieve the five parts of an opinion (4.6) by pointing to them on the wall card; pairs say one part to each other.
- **17 min. Read 05-d.** Preview tier 3 words (native, drain, thinly, regularly); the teacher reads 05-d aloud (about 190 words, 2 minutes); pairs reread by turns, finger under the words (about 8 minutes for 15 pairs); the whole class rereads the first and last paragraphs together.
- **15 min. Steps and why.** The teacher models how one step connects to the next (holes first so water drains, stones then soil) on strips; 15 pairs order four cut-up steps from 05-d and say a because for two joins (RI.2.3); the teacher circulates.
- **8 min. Talk.** Turn, pair, link, clarify on "Why must the steps come in this order?" Each of 30 children has a 30-second turn in pairs, then three pairs share to the class.
- **10 min. Plan the packet.** Each child fills the plan strip: question, my opinion, two facts to find in 05 (not yet chosen), link words, ending. Packet parts shown: envelope, drawing, opinion card, the class steps. No drafting today.
- **5 min. Exit.** E143.
- Shared shape (note, not timed): The strips model asks why one step comes before the next, as E143 does. Use a different kind of task: have pairs act the steps in the wrong order and show what breaks, with no reason sentence required.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest foundations error of the day before, at most 5 minutes.
- **15 min. Written check, cycle 24.** Every child writes four dictated words, then the sentence, then corrects the shown misspelling with the printed dictionary entry (5 minutes each); the reserved check words stay unseen.
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (25 to 30) read the cycle 24 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); the rest at stations. New material is modeled before a child's turn; the reserved check words stay unseen.
- **15 min. A fresh response.** Each child writes or draws a sentence using a cycle word, then reads it to a partner.
- **13 min. Close.** Pairs read the cycle 24 reader aloud once more for ease.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the step strips and the wall card of five parts; pairs hold the ordering. Tool: None. By hand: strips, plan strip, the written check. Evidence: E143; the plan strip (for feedback); the cycle 24 written check; the oral card, children 25 to 30.

## Day 144


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** X on the sprinkle, pour, dump order; then Y with "Water the pot gently".
- **12 min. Find facts.** Pairs reread 05-a and 05-c and mark two facts each with sticky strips lettered a or c (W.2.8); each fact answers "Why should someone plant a suitable wildflower?" 15 pairs, the teacher visits each pair for about 40 seconds.
- **23 min. Draft.** Each child writes the card in the packet: "Dear planter,", the topic, the opinion, two facts tagged with their article letters, a link word for each and an ending. Exact verbs and describing words from the wall ladder (practice). The teacher confers with six children (6 x 1 minute) inside the 23.
- **10 min. Draw.** A wildflower and one pollinator visitor, watched from a little way off, in pencil on the drawing sheet.
- **5 min. Pairs.** Pairs read the card aloud; the listener names the fact they heard.
- **5 min. Exit.** E144.
- Shared shape (note, not timed): Find facts and the draft ask which fact helps the opinion, as E144 does. Use a different kind of task: sort four strips from 05 under "helps" and "does not help" for a different opinion, with an arrow drawn from each fact.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Retrieve.** Cycle 24 words (compound words).
- **15 min. Model, map, blend (cycle 25).** The -cal ending says /kul/ (vertical, tropical, comical) and the -cle ending also says /kul/ (article, miracle, vehicle); the c says /k/ and the e is silent. Tiles on the table: tap the syllables, blend the whole, then spell. Each word is checked in a sentence.
- **15 min. First reading of the cycle 25 reader.** A pattern-matched replacement written by us (the base reader on recycling is used only where text and picture rights clear); read after the pattern is taught; pairs echo-read.
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (1 to 6, first-day samples, diagnostic) read the cycle 25 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); the rest at stations. New material is modeled before a child's turn; the reserved check words stay unseen.
- **13 min. Spell.** Words and a sentence from dictation; map the cycle's irregular words with no guessing from pictures or first letters.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the exact-word ladder and the arrow from reason to point. Tool: None. By hand: sticky strips, the card, the drawing, tiles. Evidence: E144; the draft card (for written feedback after class); the oral card, children 1 to 6.

## Day 145


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Read the note.** Each child reads the written note on the card and tells a partner what it asks.
- **10 min. Pair feedback.** Pairs swap cards; each says one kind, specific and helpful thing and asks one question (review of grade 1 and unit 2): 15 pairs, 2 minutes each way (4 minutes) and 6 minutes to write the notes.
- **10 min. Group critique.** Groups of five set the card and the drawing out together; each child hears one comment about the writing and one about the drawing from the group, 2 minutes a child (5 x 2 = 10 minutes). The teacher sits with two groups in turn.
- **25 min. Revise.** Each child revises reasons, links and the ending after the notes (15 minutes), and the teacher takes five tables by recorded letter (H and I, J, K, L, M), 5 minutes each (25 minutes in all); every child takes one table turn.
- **5 min. Share.** Pairs read the revised card aloud and say one thing they changed.
- **5 min. Exit.** E145.
- Shared shape (note, not timed): The revise task adds a link word and an ending, as E145 does. Use a different kind of task: tell the group which part of a model is missing and point to it on the parts wall card.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest foundations error of the day before, at most 5 minutes.
- **8 min. Revise the cycle 24 record.** Written check (143) and any remaining oral turn, corrected after the note; scored afterward on 146.
- **12 min. Read the matched decodable.** After pattern teaching; meaning confirmed from print and sentence (RF.2.4a, c).
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (7 to 12) read the cycle 25 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); the rest at stations. New material is modeled before a child's turn; the reserved check words stay unseen.
- **16 min. Spell.** Words and sentences with -cal and -cle.
- **7 min. Map irregular words.** No guessing from pictures or first letters.

Who holds what is carried: the child holds the revised card; the group holds the critique strip. Tool: None. By hand: notes, revision, spelling. Evidence: E145; the card before and after revision; the cycle 24 revision; the oral card, children 7 to 12.

## Day 146


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening.** Retrieve the sequence words first, next, then, after that, last on a wall strip.
- **10 min. Reread 05-d.** Pairs reread 05-d by turns and mark each sequence word.
- **15 min. Shared writing.** The teacher writes five class planting steps on chart paper; children supply the because for each in a complete sentence (for example, "We put stones first so extra water can drain out"). Pairs say their because to each other first (30 seconds each), then 8 children share.
- **20 min. Edit the packet.** Each child edits the card in a separate pass with the checklist: a capital for a name, an end mark, an apostrophe, the comma after "Dear planter" and before the closing, and a spelling checked in the printed dictionary (L.2.2a to c, e). Color a different pen for each change. Envelope and drawing are finished.
- **5 min. Pairs check.** Pairs check each other's card against the checklist.
- **5 min. Exit.** E146.
- Shared shape (note, not timed): Shared writing gives a "because" for each step, as E146 does. Use a different kind of task: order the shuffled strips of 05-d and say what each step changes, one word each.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest foundations error of the day before, at most 5 minutes.
- **12 min. Reread the cycle 25 reader.** For accuracy, then smoothly (RF.2.4b).
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (13 to 18) read the cycle 25 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); the rest at stations. New material is modeled before a child's turn; the reserved check words stay unseen.
- **15 min. Sort.** Twelve -cal and -cle words by ending; spell four on paper.
- **11 min. Use the pattern in sentences.** Each child writes one sentence with a cycle word.
- **5 min. Close.** Pairs read one sentence aloud.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the checklist and the sequence strip. Tool: None. By hand: editing, shared writing, sorting. Evidence: E146; the edited card (for W3 on 154); the cycle 24 score (written and oral, after class); the oral card, children 13 to 18.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 143, "Why must the steps come in this order?" Day 145, "What is one change that would help a reader?"
- **Written task, days 143 to 146.** The packet: an opinion card with two tagged facts, a drawing and the class steps, planned, drafted, revised and edited.
- **Judged by the teacher with five look-fors** (the W3 parts): (1) the topic is named; (2) the opinion is stated; (3) each reason is a fact from 05 that answers the question; (4) a link word joins each reason; (5) an ending completes the position. Editing is judged on the packet: names, end marks, apostrophes and the greeting comma, changed in their own pass.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 143.** The parts of an opinion (4.6), the compound-word cycle. **144.** Exact words and describing words (138). **145.** The note and the retry on the card (142); kind, specific, helpful feedback. **146.** Capitals, end marks, apostrophes and the greeting comma (unit 2); the printed dictionary.
- **Fluency target.** A smooth reading of the cycle 25 reader by 146; accuracy first, no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: step, because, native, drain, scatter. Frames: "___ comes before ___ because ___." "I think ___ because ___." "Let us ___." Pairs may talk in the home language first.
- **Access.** Enlarge print, read 05-d aloud, accept a scribe or a labeled drawing for a reason; steps can be acted.
- **Check accommodations.** Extra time; the card read again; a scribe for content, recorded. Conventions are not scribed in the editing pass.
- **Reading load.** 05-d is about 190 words and is read aloud once, then in pairs; the cards are short; exit cards are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a third fact from 05-c and say why it fits, or choose the strongest of three facts and say why.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read 05-d together and order the four steps with a reason for each join; find two facts in 05-a or 05-c; write the card; read the grown-up's note and change one thing; edit with the checklist. Take one oral card a day aloud.
- **One projector.** Show the steps and the checklist; the card and drawing stay on paper.
