# Section 2.6: Describing finds with grammar, and the check on articles

## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 2. Unit 2, Fossils tell of Earth's changes. *What can fossils tell us about the past?* (days 33 to 74): see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 54 to 58.** Days by kind: understanding 3 (54, 55, 56), check 1 (57), respond 1 (58). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 6 of 9 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read short labels, picture cards, a few printed lines of an article at a time and the cycle reader; the teacher reads the articles and every stem aloud, and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Days 54 to 56 teach the grammar of unit 2 on the fossil articles; day 57 is the mid-unit check on a cold article; day 58 answers it.
