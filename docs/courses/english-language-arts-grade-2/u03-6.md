# Section 3.6: Photographs, partner talk and a model paragraph

## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 2. Unit 3, The secret world of pollination. *How do plants and pollinators help each other?* (days 78 to 118): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 96 to 98.** Days by kind: application 3 (96, 97, 98). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 6 of 12 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read the pictures and captions of the research articles, their own slips, and a model paragraph of about 55 words read aloud first; they read at most 150 printed words a day and the teacher reads every stem aloud. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: photographs of a bee and of pollen only; no sting or injury. The indoor path is complete. Any outdoor look is optional (field 5) and no child handles an insect.
