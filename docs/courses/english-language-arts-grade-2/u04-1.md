- **Limits that bite here.** The fables are read; the class does not act out hunger or cold. Hunger never proves a person is lazy, and no child is cast as the grasshopper or the ant. No child reports on home food or money. The moral is worked out from events, never copied from a printed line (01 and 02 print none). Neither check card of the unit (F1, V1 to V4, F2) is used on these days.
- **Connections.** RL.2.6, voices for characters, day 122 (the ant's two answers read aloud once); W.2.1 is not yet taught (section 4.5).
