# Section 4.3: Beats, rhymes and repeated lines

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.2.4, this section's share:** "Describe how words and phrases (e.g., regular beats, alliteration, rhymes, repeated lines) supply rhythm and meaning in a story, poem, or song." Day 126 takes the beat and the repeated line in the poem 06-a, Room for Wings; day 127 takes rhyme and alliteration in the song 06-b, The Garden Refrain, and what repeating a sound emphasizes. Describe means say what the device does, not only name it.
- **RL.2.9 and RL.2.6, day 128 only:** "Compare and contrast two or more versions of the same story" and "speaking in a different voice for each character when reading dialogue aloud." Day 128 rehearses both with 01 and 02, not with new content.
- **RL.2.2 in independent work, days 126 and 127:** a child recounts 01 or 02 aloud on 126 with a moral that rests on an event, and writes it on 127 (a few words and a drawing).
- **L.2.2d** and **L.2.2e**: cycle 21 (schwa spelled a; de- and dis-) runs through 128, with the cycle's written check on day 128. **RF.2.3c to f, RF.2.4a to c, SL.2.1a to c and SL.2.3** are carried every day.
- **Limits that bite here.** The poem and the song are read, chanted and sung by the class together; no solo is required and no child is made to sing alone. No tune is fixed; any steady four-beat tune will do. The unit plan names the song's effect (a steady beat like wings), but the children say it in their own words. Neither the poem nor the song asks a child to report on a home garden.
- **The teacher page must explain.** What a beat is (a clapped steady pulse); why a repeated line carries meaning (here, the request for room for wings); how rhyme and alliteration differ (end sounds against beginning sounds); and the poem and song are Curio Class originals with no source needed.

## 6. Days

## Day 126


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest wrong answer on E125 (C).
- **12 min. Read 06-a.** The teacher reads the poem aloud (1 minute: 96 words), then the class claps the beat with the first stanza (3 minutes), chants the poem in four rounds with the repeated line said together (4 minutes), and pairs read the stanzas by turns (4 minutes). Words previewed: bloom, petals, stem, visit.
- **10 min. Model.** The teacher shows the beat (four claps a line) and finds the repeated line, "Room for wings, room for wings." She says what it does: it asks again and again for room, and the steady beat sounds like wings. Describe means say what the sound adds.
- **8 min. Talk.** Turn, pair, link, clarify on "What does the repeated line ask for?"
- **20 min. Independent: recount.** Each pair takes 01 or 02. One partner recounts in order with the story-map (4 minutes), the other partner does the same (4 minutes); then both name the event that shows the moral (4 minutes). The teacher confers with the six heard during the remaining 8 minutes.
- **5 min. Exit.** E126.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The model and the talk ask what the repeated line adds, the same as the spoken exit. Instead: find and mark every repeated line on the printed poem, then judge a given answer ("it repeats") and fix it.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.**
- **12 min. Reread the cycle 21 reader** for accuracy, then smoothly (RF.2.4b).
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (13 to 18) read the oral card, 2 minutes each; the rest at stations.
- **15 min. Sort** twelve words by schwa a or de-/dis-; spell four on paper.
- **11 min. Use the pattern in sentences:** each child writes one sentence with a cycle word.
- **5 min. Close.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the beat; pairs hold the story-map. Tool: None. By hand: clapping, recount, sorting. Evidence: E126; the oral card, children 13 to 18; the independent recount heard from the six.

## Day 127


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest answer on E126 (O).
- **12 min. Read 06-b.** The teacher sings or reads the song once through (2 minutes), the class sings the chorus (2 minutes), and pairs read the verses by turns (5 minutes); then the class reads the whole with one clap a beat (3 minutes). Words previewed: pollen, petals, pollinators, refrain.
- **10 min. Model.** On the printed song the teacher marks two rhyme pairs (grow, glow; white, twilight) and the beginning sounds in "Bright bees buzz by the blossoms". She says what it adds: rhyme ties the ends of lines together; repeated beginning sounds make the bees buzz.
- **8 min. Talk.** Turn, pair, link, clarify on "What does repeating a sound emphasize?"
- **20 min. Independent: write the recount.** On the strip from 126, each child writes or draws first, next and last for 01 or 02 and one sentence frame, "The lesson is ___ because ___." The teacher confers with the six heard.
- **5 min. Exit.** E127.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The model and the talk ask what a rhyme or a repeated sound adds, the same as the exit. Instead: sort word pairs into rhyme and same first sound, or judge a given answer and fix it.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.**
- **15 min. A fresh decodable passage** (not the reader), read and meaning confirmed.
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (19 to 24) read the oral card, 2 minutes each; the rest at stations.
- **15 min. Dictation** of words and a sentence, then compare with the pattern.
- **8 min. Dictionary.** Each pair finds one cycle word in the printed beginning dictionary and checks its spelling (L.2.2e).
- **5 min. Close.**

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the beat and the story-map; the teacher holds the sound chart. Tool: None. By hand: marking rhymes, writing the recount, dictation. Evidence: E127; the oral card, children 19 to 24; the written recount.

## Day 128


Topic block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest answer on E127 (O).
- **8 min. Reread.** Pairs reread the ant's two requests in 01 and 02 aloud from the side-by-side print.
- **12 min. Compare.** Pairs sort six strips into only in 01, only in 02 and in both, then name one shared event and one difference; a sentence frame, "Both ___. But ___." is on the board.
- **10 min. Talk.** Turn, pair, link, clarify on "How does the ant sound different in the two tellings?"
- **20 min. Voices.** Six groups of five each read a short piece of the dialogue in the two tellings (2 minutes each: 12 minutes), changing voice for each speaker; after each group the class names one difference; the last 8 minutes pairs revise their chart.
- **5 min. Exit.** E128.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The compare step ("Both ___. But ___.") is the exit's ask. Instead: give a comparison that leaves out a difference, and have the pair find and mark what is missing.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.**
- **15 min. Written check, cycle 21.** Every child writes the cycle's words from the spoken words on the check sheet (taught patterns only), with the printed dictionary for one word; the reserved check words stay unseen.
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (25 to 30) read the oral card, 2 minutes each; the rest at stations.
- **15 min. A fresh response.** Each child writes or draws a sentence using a cycle word, then reads it to a partner.
- **13 min. Close.** The class reads the cycle 21 reader once more for ease.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the side-by-side print, the story-map and the beliefs table. Tool: None. By hand: sorting, reading in voices, writing. Evidence: E128; the written check; the oral card, children 25 to 30.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: beat, rhyme, repeat, bloom, voice. Frames: "The repeated line asks for ___." "The sound ___ is repeated, so ___." "In the second telling, the ant ___." A child may say the effect in the home language first.
- **Access.** Enlarge print, read directions aloud, accept clapping or pointing for the beat; the exit pictures are described aloud for a child who cannot see them.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing for the picture; the reason may be said to a scribe or drawn; extra time within the part; a quiet corner for the oral card.
- **Reading load.** 06-a and 06-b (192 words in all) are chanted and sung with teacher support; the exits are read aloud; the reader, the check sheet and the strips are the only other print.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second repeated line or a pair of rhyming words to the poem and say what it adds; or compare the poem and the song for what each repeats.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Clap the beat together, chant the repeated line, and ask what it asks for. On 127 read the song and find two rhyming words. On 128 read both tellings and find one thing the same and one different. The grown-up hears the cycle words aloud.
- **One projector.** Show the poem and the song with the repeated lines and rhyming words in two colors; the class claps along and the teacher marks as the class names.
