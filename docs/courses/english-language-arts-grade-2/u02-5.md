## Header


Children read short labels, cards, one printed page of an article at a time and the cycle reader; the teacher reads the articles and every stem aloud, and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **L.2.4b**, "Determine the meaning of the new word formed when a known prefix is added to a known word (e.g., happy/unhappy, tell/retell)." Day 50, on the prefixes un- and re- with readable familiar words; this is word meaning, not prefix decoding (that comes later in the Skills Block).
- **L.2.4d**, "Use knowledge of the meaning of individual words to predict the meaning of compound words." Day 51, on compound words from article C.
- **L.2.4e**, "Use glossaries and beginning dictionaries, both print and digital, to determine or clarify the meaning of words and phrases." Day 51 (print and screen), after day 49's glossary work in section 2.4.
- **RI.2.2**, "Identify the main topic of a multiparagraph text as well as the focus of specific paragraphs within the text." Day 52 practices it on article A with the model withdrawn.
- **RI.2.5** and **RI.2.6**: "Know and use various text features... to locate key facts" and "Identify the main purpose of a text." Day 53 practices them together on article C.
- **RF.2.3d, 2.3e, 2.3f; RF.2.4, 4a to 4c; L.2.2d, 2e** (days 50 to 53): cycle 9 doubling before a vowel suffix, even, finally, wrong, won; on day 53 the three sounds of -ed and would-contractions (he'd, I'd, you'd) read and spelled as whole words, and o'clock. **SL.2.1a to 1c, SL.2.3**: the talk routine on days 50 to 53.
- Skills carried: e2-word-strategies, e2-prefixes-suffixes, e2-main-topic-paragraphs, e2-text-features, e2-author-purpose, e2-spelling, e2-fluency, e2-tricky-spellings, e2-discussion.
- **Limits that bite.** Day 51 uses a shared device that shows the same dictionary entry as the print; no search engine and no account. A child reads no more than 150 printed words a day. Of the prefix and compound words, only uncover (article B) and seashell, footprint, riverbed, sandstone, hillside, notebook (article C) are in the texts; the others (unlock, replay and so on) are spoken or on cards. Unmarked, notebook and the day 57 dictionary entries (trace, cast) are never worked examples here.
- **Connections.** Section 2.4 supplied the topic chart, the feature route and the purpose cards; section 2.1 the sentence-clue card. Section 2.6 opens on day 54 with grammar.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Day 52 has no model: the blank topic chart is the only support. Day 53 uses article C, whose features are labeled in "Using This Article".

**Exit questions.** A reading exit marked Passage asks on a named part of the unit's own texts, read aloud twice, never on a card and never on the passage the day works as its example; for those exits, "the card" below means that passage. Each exit is two spoken items, each one ask, except as noted. Item (a): "Point to the picture that goes with the card I read." All children show A or B on a two-card hold-up. Item (b): the day's one ask, answered aloud by the rotating six (about 20 seconds each); the rest tell a partner. Shown means the right picture and an answer using a word from the card; shown with help means the right picture and an answer after one prompt ("What does the card say?"); not yet seen means the other picture or no answer. Cards are printed "invented for practice" and read aloud twice. None reuses an article sentence or a worked example.
- **E50** (day 50). Card: "The fossil sketch is unfinished. Finished means complete. Mateo adds the missing edge." Ask: "Use un- and finished to tell me what unfinished means." Answer: Not finished (not complete).
- **E51** (day 51). Card: "The worker keeps a fieldbook. Dictionary: field, open land; an area of study. Here the worker writes about an outdoor site." Ask: "Put the two parts together and tell me what fieldbook means here." Answer: A book of notes about an outdoor site (field plus book).
- **E52** (day 52). Passage: article C, page 3, Scientists at Work, paragraphs 1 and 2, read aloud twice; not studied for focus (g2-u2-01). Read aloud the first two paragraphs of Scientists at Work, on page 3 of article C, twice. Ask: "Say the whole topic that both paragraphs share." Answer: How scientists study fossils: what they do with a find and the questions they ask. Then s02-X52bv2: "Say what the second paragraph is about." Answer: The questions scientists ask about a fossil and the clues that answer them, such as teeth for what an animal ate.
- **E53** (day 53). Passage: article A, page 1, the part Why Fossils Are Rare, read aloud twice; studied on day 52 for focus, not for purpose (g2-u2-01). Read aloud the part of article A called Why Fossils Are Rare twice. Ask: "Tell me what this part is meant to do." Answer: It explains why few living things become fossils: hard parts last longest, and a quick cover of sediment keeps a body safe. Then s02-X53bv2: "A child wants to read about the fossils Mary found. Which feature should the child use?" Answer: The subheading Big Finds.

**Cycle 9 written check** (day 52, 15 minutes, unscored). The teacher reads each sentence twice and says its missing word; children write the word in a printed gap. Four independent spellings; none is a day 48 to 51 word. (1) "The cat is ____ on the mat." sitting. (2) "She ____ her baby." hugged. (3) "We are ____ a hole." digging. (4) "We are ____ a trip." planning. Wrong: "siting", "huged", "diging" and "planing" each omit the doubling (AB); "hug" or "plan" for the whole word omits the ending (Z); tag the letter that fits. After the check is marked, dictate "making" and "hopped" as unscored practice (drop the e against double).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The blank topic chart (from section 2.4) is held by partners on day 52 with no worked example; the feature route cards (from day 47) and the purpose cards (from day 48) return on day 53. The prefix and compound cards are made on days 50 and 51 and held by pairs. The word wall holds even, finally, wrong, won and, from day 53, o'clock. The beginning dictionary card (original practice cards) is shared by pairs from day 51. The talk routine, the heard-six column and the six question words card stay up.
- **Standard rotation (foundations).** Three stations, tiles (spell the day's words), sort (card sort by pattern) and reread (partner reread of the reader or phrase cards), ten children each. The day's six sit at the teacher table for round 1, two minutes each, then take the other stations.
- **Key moment 1, day 50: the prefix card.** Ideal instrument: a flip card with the prefix on a flap over the word. Spec: fits; paper.
- **Key moment 2, day 51: dictionary entry in print and on a screen.** Ideal instrument: a teacher-held device showing the same entry as the print card, children asking for the entry by its label. Spec: does not exist (the unit plan names reference screens as light use; the ask goes in TOOL-ASKS.md and this plan does not edit it). Gaps: the labeled links. The print dictionary card does the whole job on paper; the screen is shown, not graded.
- **Tools.** Reference screen, day 51 only, in the dictionary part (5 of 10 minutes), to show why a screen entry matches the print. No search engine and no account.
- **A room with nothing.** The flip card is a folded slip; the dictionary page is a hand-drawn entry; tiles are letters on torn card; the topic chart is two rows on folded paper.

## 6. Days

## Day 50


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E49 (E), set the question beside the route cards and walk the finger from subheading to bold word to caption; otherwise retrieve the purpose cards.
- **6 min. Words.** Locate, feature, mineral, purpose: each said, shown on a picture card and used in a sentence about the articles.
- **8 min. Reread Uncovered With Care.** The teacher rereads the 55-word part of article B; children point to the word uncover and say what the scientists do.
- **12 min. Model: a prefix and a base word.** Flip card: cover, then uncover. Un- means not or the opposite of the action; re- means again. Model uncover (not covered), unlock, then replay (play again) and rebuild. Say aloud only uncover is in the article; the others are spoken practice words.
- **12 min. Pairs sort and explain.** Pairs sort 8 word cards into un- and re- words (unwrap, unfold, redo, refill, unpack, reread, unlock, rebuild) and say each meaning with "not" or "again".
- **7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "How does the small word help you with the whole word?" Hear the six.
- **5 min. Write one sentence.** Each child writes "___ means ___ because un- (or re-) means ___" with a few words.
- **5 min. Exit.** E50.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Reread six doubling word cards; reblend any missed ending (Z, AB).
- **15 min. Reread, sort and spell.** Reread the cycle 9 reader with a partner; sort 12 cards into doubled and not doubled; spell two words and a sentence; check one spelling in the beginning dictionary.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 3 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Why is it stopped and not stoped?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the flip cards. Tool: None. By hand: sorting, flipping cards, writing a few words, tiles. Evidence: E50; the sorted cards; the heard column.

## Day 51


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E50 (H), flip the word card finished / unfinished and say not finished; otherwise retrieve the prefix cards.
- **6 min. Words.** Mold, cast, clarify, reference: each said once with the article C glossary entry for mold and cast read aloud, and used in a sentence for clarify and reference.
- **8 min. Reread parts of article C.** The teacher rereads the Tracks and Traces part and the glossary; children point to footprint, riverbed and hillside.
- **12 min. Model: compound words.** Decode first: read each part, then the whole; say each part's meaning; predict the whole; check the meaning against the sentence. Model footprint (foot and print) and riverbed (river and bed). Context never replaces decoding.
- **10 min. Pairs predict and check.** Pairs break and predict seashell, sandstone, hillside (from article C) and two spoken words (raincoat, sunlight); notebook is read but not used as an example. They check each against the sentence in the article.
- **10 min. Print and screen dictionary.** The teacher shows the same entry on a print dictionary card and on a shared screen (the labeled link requested orally); pairs use the print card with the words bark and wave to pick the sense that fits a given sentence. Neither word is cast, trace or collector.
- **4 min. Write one sentence.** Each child writes "I think ___ means ___ because ___ and ___" with a few words.
- **5 min. Exit.** E51.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Reread six doubling cards; map any missed even or finally.
- **15 min. Transfer to a fresh passage.** Pairs read a short fresh passage that uses only cycle 9 patterns and the words wrong and won (to make); confirm meaning after decoding; the teacher dictates one sentence.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 4 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which word did you decode, then check?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the compound cards and the dictionary card. Tool: reference screen (light), dictionary part, 5 of the 10 minutes, to show why a screen entry matches the print; the practice is on paper. By hand: sorting, writing a few words, tiles. Evidence: E51; the predictions; the heard column.

## Day 52


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E51 (J), predict, read the sentence and check that the meaning fits; otherwise retrieve the compound cards.
- **5 min. Read aloud: article A.** The teacher reads What Is a Fossil? (237 words) with its four subheadings; children follow the print.
- **15 min. Pairs name the topic and each focus, no model.** Each pair gets article A with a blank topic chart and fills in the whole topic and the focus of each of the four subheadings in a few words. The wall chart and the worked example from day 46 are covered. The teacher moves between pairs and gives no answers.
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** Pairs compare charts with another pair: "Your topic is ___; my focus for ___ is ___". Hear the six.
- **10 min. Write on your own.** Each child writes "The topic is ___." and two focuses ("___ is about ___."), with a few words.
- **5 min. Check the writing.** Each child checks a capital, an end mark and one spelling against the word wall or the beginning dictionary.
- **5 min. Partner fix.** Partners swap and mend one focus with "Your focus fits because ___".
- **5 min. Exit.** E52.
- **15 min. Written check (foundations).** Cycle 9 written check (field 3), unscored.
- **5 min. Reader.** Reread the cycle 9 reader for expression.
- **25 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation; the six read the oral card for the whole cycle (12 minutes), the rest in stations. Group 5 of five.
- **15 min. Fresh response.** Day 48's six read the cycle words to the teacher (12 minutes, 2 minutes each) while the others partner-read a pattern card and spell two dictated words; no new teaching.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the blank topic chart. Tool: None. By hand: writing a few words, filling a chart, spelling on paper. Evidence: E52; the charts and sentences; the check notes.

## Day 53


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E52 (C), read the title, name the topic and check each paragraph fits it; otherwise retrieve the route cards.
- **8 min. Reread Using This Article.** The teacher rereads the Scientists at Work and Using This Article parts of article C; children follow the print.
- **15 min. Pairs choose the most useful feature.** Pairs take three question cards about article C (for example "Where do scientists write the place a specimen was found?"), name the most useful feature among the subheading, bold word, caption, glossary, index and menu icon, and find the answer on the print.
- **12 min. Explain the purpose.** Pairs say what the author of article C wants to explain and point to the line that shows it, in the part called Using This Article. Contrast with the day 48 blurbs.
- **8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which feature found the answer fastest, and why?" Hear the six.
- **7 min. Write one sentence.** Each child writes "I used the ___ because ___. The author wants to ___." with a few words.
- **5 min. Exit.** E53.
- **5 min. Retrieval (foundations).** Read the cycle 9 check misses (AB, Z); retrieve jumped, rubbed, wanted.
- **15 min. Teach: the three sounds of -ed.** Review from grade 1: -ed says /t/ (jumped), /d/ (rubbed) or /id/ (wanted). Model: map /w/ /o/ /n/ /t/ /id/, blend, spell. Read he'd, I'd, you'd as whole words (the apostrophe stands in for letters; not scored). O'clock is a mapped irregular word.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 1 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which sound does -ed make in wanted?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the question cards; partners hold the route cards. Tool: None. By hand: pointing, finding the line, writing a few words, tiles. Evidence: E53; the written sentence; the heard column.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 50, "How does the small word help you with the whole word?"; day 52, "How do the paragraph focuses fit the topic?"; day 53, "Which feature found the answer fastest, and why?"
- Written task, day 52: "The topic is ___" and two focuses, a few written words each, with no model.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) names the topic and then separate focuses; (2) a focus is smaller than the topic and fits it; (3) points to a word, subheading or line as the reason.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Days 51 to 53 open with one repair of at most 5 minutes, for the commonest exit miss; day 50 takes E49. The foundations opening repairs the day before's commonest spelling or reading miss.
- **Oral card.** The rotating six read six words and one sentence each day, using only patterns taught on an earlier day. The record sheet has a heard column.
- **Fluency target.** A smooth second reading of the cycle 9 reader by day 52; accuracy first; no timer.
- **Earlier work returning.** The topic chart (day 46) returns blank on day 52; the feature route and purpose cards return on day 53; the sentence-clue card (day 37) returns on day 51.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: locate, feature, mineral, purpose, uncover, compound, dictionary, reference, topic, focus. Frames: "Un- means ___." "I think ___ means ___ because ___ and ___." "The topic is ___." "I used the ___ because ___." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer. Large print cards; the A and B pictures differ in color and shape. A child who cannot write the sentence draws and the teacher scribes.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards are read aloud twice; the spelling check can be answered with tiles; the dictionary card is read aloud on request.
- **Reading load.** A child reads at most 150 printed words a day (a subheading part, cards, the dictionary card); the articles are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Make three new compound words from article C parts and write a one-line meaning for each, or name the topic and focus of a second article.
