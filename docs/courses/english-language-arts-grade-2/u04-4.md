# Section 4.4: Check the tales, then retry

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-2/4/s/4.4), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-2 u04-4.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.2.2, days 129 and 131:** "Recount stories, including fables and folktales from diverse cultures, and determine their central message, lesson, or moral." Day 129 reads each child's recount of a new card and its lesson from events; day 131 retries what missed.
- **RL.2.6, days 129 and 131:** "Acknowledge differences in the points of view of characters, including by speaking in a different voice for each character when reading dialogue aloud." Day 129 reads two speakers in two voices; day 131 retries.
- **RL.2.9, days 130 and 131:** "Compare and contrast two or more versions of the same story." Day 130 asks one thing the same and one different in two new versions; day 131 retries.
- **Day 131 only:** L.2.2d and L.2.2e (cycle 22: schwa spelled e and o; im- and in-), RF.2.3c to f, RF.2.4a to c are carried; cycle 22 runs 129 to 131 and beyond (the cycle's remaining days are in section 4.5).
- **Limits that bite here.** The checks read new cards, never a tale already taught; a card is used once and kept unseen until its day. No child is scored on voice quality, only on whether two views differ in the voice. The made-up cards are labeled "invented for practice". Nobody acts out fire or hunger; no accents or costumes. An unheard reason is never marked as shown.
- **The teacher page must explain.** The three marks and how an exit or check mark differs from a judgement of the child; that the sampling is of the taught work only (a recount with a moral, two voices, a similarity and a difference); how to read the hand-back letters A to E; and what the retry on 131 can and cannot change in the record.

## 2. Where it sits

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items read in this section.** The unit plan places T2 (RL.2.6) and T3 (RL.2.9) with sections 4.3 and 4.1; the section that last taught each owns it. This section schedules T1 to T3 on days 129 and 130 and gives the cards; nothing from them appears in any example before its day.

- **T1, day 129, RL.2.2.** Recount card F1 (unseen) and explain a lesson using its events. Six groups of five, 3 minutes a group (36 seconds a child), 18 minutes. Card F1, Small Hops (to print, invented for practice): "Sparrow wanted the cherries on the high branch. 'It is too far,' she said, and she sat down. Finch said, 'Try one hop.' Sparrow hopped to a low branch. She hopped again, and again. At last she reached the cherries. 'Small hops carried me,' she said." Key: Sparrow says it is too far, Finch says to try one hop, Sparrow hops again and again and reaches the cherries; small hops can carry you to a far goal. A recount that reaches the cherries first is A; an unrelated moral is B.
- **T2, day 129, RL.2.6.** Read the two speakers in F1 with different voices and explain the different views. Two minutes a group (24 seconds a child), 12 minutes, completing the same 30-minute rotation. Key: Sparrow gives up ("It is too far"); Finch encourages one try ("Try one hop"). Identical voices or views are C.
- **T3, day 130, RL.2.9.** Versions V1 and V2 (to print, unseen, invented for practice). V1: "Hen found one seed. 'I will plant it,' she said. She planted it and watered it each day. A tall sunflower grew, and Hen shared its seeds with every bird." V2: "Hen found one seed. 'I will plant it,' she said. She planted it and watered it each day. A tall sunflower grew, and Hen kept its seeds for herself." Twenty topic minutes with rereading. Each child answers on a response sheet with two boxes, Both and Only one, in a drawing and a few words, and the six heard give the reason aloud. Key: in both, Hen finds a seed, plants it, waters it and a sunflower grows; V1 ends with Hen sharing the seeds with every bird, V2 with Hen keeping them for herself. A comparison without a difference is D; without a similarity is E.

**Record sheet.** One row a child; T1, T2 and T3 each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen; the heard column names the six asked "How do you know?" on 130; the foundations column holds the cycle 22 oral record (children 1 to 6 on 129, 7 to 12 on 130, 13 to 18 on 131). The sheet goes to the repair groups on 131 and to the second sitting on 157.

**Exit question, day 131** (a new case, invented for practice, said so aloud; "How do you know?" goes to the six). Read aloud twice: "Hare left a basket in the rain. Turtle covered Turtle's basket. Turtle's food stayed dry." Show two picture cards: a covered basket; an uncovered basket. Say: "Mark the picture of the event that keeps Turtle's food dry." Key: the picture of the covered basket. Wrong: the picture of the uncovered basket (B). Second ask, spoken, heard from the six: Say: "Say the lesson that event shows." Key: If you get ready before trouble, what you have stays safe. Wrong: Turtles are slow (B).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The story-map, the beliefs table, the talk routine and the heard-six column are held by pairs; the side-by-side print and the sound chart are brought back for the retry on 131. The teacher holds nothing new. Cards F1, V1, V2 and the response sheet are single-use and kept unseen until the day.
- **Key moment 1, day 129: the six groups of five.** Ideal: a printed card per child so each reads along. Spec: fits (paper). Gaps and asks: none. **Key moment 2, day 130: a response sheet.** Ideal: a two-box sheet printed once per child. Spec: fits (paper). No digital tool is needed.
- **A room with nothing.** Cards read aloud twice; the response sheet is a folded paper; the six groups of five stand at the teacher table in turn.

## 6. Days

## Day 129


Topic block (60 minutes). Check day (assessment mid).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest wrong answer on E128 (D or C).
- **5 min. Briefing.** The teacher says what the check is (a new card, a recount, a lesson, two voices), says it is invented for practice, and sets the stations.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Six groups of five come to the teacher table in turn. T1: 3 minutes a group (36 seconds a child, 18 minutes). T2: 2 minutes a group (24 seconds a child, 12 minutes). The other children at stations reread 03 or 04 in pairs and draw their favorite event.
- **15 min. Pairs.** Pairs ask each other one question about the card they heard others read (SL.2.3); the teacher hears the six.
- **5 min. Close.** The class names one thing that goes well in a recount.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Retrieve.** Cycle 21 words (schwa a, de-, dis-).
- **15 min. Model, map, blend (cycle 22).** The schwa spelled e and o (taken, lemon, today), and the prefixes im- and in- (impossible, inside). Tiles on the table.
- **15 min. First reading of the cycle 22 reader** (a pattern-matched replacement, unless rights clear); pairs echo-read.
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (1 to 6) read the oral card, 2 minutes each; the rest at stations.
- **13 min. Spell** words and a sentence from dictation; map the cycle's irregular words with no guessing from pictures.

Who holds what is carried: nothing new is held; the teacher hears the groups. Tool: None. By hand: card, drawing, spelling. Evidence: T1 and T2; the oral card, children 1 to 6.

## Day 130


Topic block (60 minutes). Check day (assessment mid).
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest answer on T1 and T2 from 129 (A or B).
- **10 min. Retrieval.** Pairs recount 01 and 02 in three events, reading the printed strips (practice only).
- **20 min. T3.** Rereading V1 and V2 (6 minutes, pairs read both aloud); each child fills the two boxes (10 minutes); the teacher hears six give the reason aloud (4 minutes: 40 seconds a child).
- **15 min. Pairs.** Pairs read their boxes to each other and ask one question each (practice, not marked).
- **10 min. Talk.** Turn, pair, link, clarify on "What was hard about finding one thing different?"

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Retrieve.** The previous day's words.
- **12 min. Read the cycle 22 reader** again, now for meaning and accuracy.
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (7 to 12) read the oral card, 2 minutes each; the rest at stations.
- **16 min. Spell** words and sentences with the schwa e and o, im- and in-.
- **10 min. Map irregular words** again with no guessing from pictures or first letters.
- **5 min. Close.**

Who holds what is carried: nothing new is held. Tool: None. By hand: response sheet, spelling. Evidence: T3 (the response sheet and the six reasons); the oral card, children 7 to 12.

## Day 131


Topic block (60 minutes). Respond day.
- **5 min. Opening repair.** The hand-backs from 129 and 130, read from the record: A, B, C, D, E by group.
- **10 min. Reread.** Pairs reread 03 and 04 with a partner, now finding the line that fits the missed item.
- **15 min. Teacher repair rotation.** Three groups of up to ten children by need (A and B; C; D and E), 5 minutes each (30 seconds a child); the others pair-practice with strips.
- **5 min. Talk.** Turn, pair, link, clarify on "Which event shows the lesson?"
- **20 min. Retry.** Each child retries the missed task with a fresh short card, to a partner and then to the teacher for the six heard; the retry is recorded as a second sitting, not a replacement.
- **5 min. Exit.** The day 131 exit question.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The talk "Which event shows the lesson?" and the retry repeat the exit's ask. Instead: pairs judge a given lesson against two events and fix the given answer.

Foundations block (60 minutes).
- **5 min. Opening repair.**
- **12 min. Reread the cycle 22 reader** for accuracy, then smoothly (RF.2.4b).
- **12 min. Rotation.** Six children (13 to 18) read the oral card, 2 minutes each; the rest at stations.
- **15 min. Sort** twelve words by schwa e or o, and im- or in-; spell four on paper.
- **11 min. Use the pattern in sentences:** each child writes one sentence with a cycle word.
- **5 min. Close.**

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the story-map, the beliefs table and the sound chart. Tool: None. By hand: strips, cards, spelling. Evidence: the retry; the day 131 exit; the oral card, children 13 to 18.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 130, "What was hard about finding one thing different?" Day 131, "Which event shows the lesson?"
- **Written task, day 130.** The two-box response sheet, Both and Only one, a drawing and a few words. Short writing, one task for the section.
- **Judged by the teacher with four look-fors.** (1) a real shared event from the two versions; (2) a real difference; (3) the children's own words; (4) an ending named for the difference. Marked shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 129.** Recount and moral (4.1, 4.2); voices (4.2). **130.** Versions (4.1, 4.3). **131.** Whatever the record shows missed; cycle 22 words each day, with one earlier cycle word, in foundations.
- **Fluency target.** A smooth second reading of the cycle 22 reader by day 131; accuracy first, no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: recount, lesson, version, both, only. Frames: "In both, ___." "Only in V2, ___." "Sparrow thinks ___." A child may answer in the home language first with a helper.
- **Access.** Enlarge print, read each card twice, accept pointing, drawing or a scribe; pictures are described aloud.
- **Check accommodations.** Extra time within the part; the reason may be given to a scribe; a quiet corner for the oral card; the card read aloud again on request. The answer is marked the same.
- **Reading load.** F1 (about 55 words) and V1 and V2 (about 25 words each) are read aloud by the teacher or in pairs; the exit is read aloud; the response sheet has two box labels.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second lesson for F1 and the event that supports it; or explain what V1 and V2 say about Hen.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the card, ask the child to say what happens first, next and last, then ask for the lesson and the event. On day 130 read the two versions and ask for one thing the same and one different. On day 131 retry whatever was hard. The grown-up hears the cycle words aloud.
- **One projector.** Show the card or the two versions side by side and read aloud; the child answers on paper. The class sheet is gathered at the end.
