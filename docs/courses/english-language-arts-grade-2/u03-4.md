# Section 3.4: Shared research: find, note and join facts

## Header


Children read short invented cards, the features and labels of 01-a, and the cycle 15 reader; the teacher reads every text first and every stem aloud, and children read at most 150 printed words a day. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice cases are invented and the teacher says so aloud. Content note: plants, pollen, wind, seeds and bees; no sting or injury; a bee is never handled.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **W.2.7**, "Participate in shared research and writing projects (e.g., read a number of books on a single topic to produce a report; record science observations)." Day 88 teaches the shared cycle (ask, find, note, share) on one class question; days 89 to 91 each add a note to a joint chart. This section carries the first half; the second half is sections 3.5 to 3.7.
- **W.2.8**, "Recall information from experiences or gather information from provided sources to answer a question." Day 88 gathers from two provided cards with a source tag on every note; day 89 from the features of 01-a; day 90 from one paragraph; day 91 joins them.
- **RI.2.5, RI.2.7, RI.2.2, RI.2.3** (practiced, taught in units 1 and 2 and days 78 to 81): the feature route card (day 89), the diagram (day 89), gist and the topic and focus chart (day 90), the step chain (day 91). Nothing is taught as new except the shared cycle on day 88.
- **RF.2.3d** (cycle 15, un- and re-), **RF.2.3c, e, f; RF.2.4, 4a to 4c; L.2.2d, 2e; SL.2.1a to 1c, SL.2.3**: -k, -ck and -ic spellings, decoding the prefixes un- and re-, reading to confirm meaning, spelling by pattern checked against the word wall, and turn, pair, link, clarify.
- **Limits that bite.** The teacher never highlights the answering line: the pine cards, 01-a and the cards of E88 are given whole, so a child finds the fact (AD). A note is a few words, never a copied sentence (D). The words plastic, rocket, unzip, repack and milk are reserved for the day 92 check and are never used in teaching. Bee Visit and Butterfly Visit (R2) are never shown.
- **Connections.** Grade 1 research-cycle chart (ask, find, note, share), connection arrow and shared research return as review. Unit 1: note slip (day 16), notes sheet (day 24), source tag. Unit 2: topic and focus chart (day 46), feature route card (day 47).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A source tag says where a fact came from (card 1, the picture, 01-a, the heading name). The pine cards are invented for practice: pine pollen is light and wind carries it. Wind is one carrier; the unit is mostly about animals.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned.** None are scored here. This section prepares W.2.7 and W.2.8 (R1 and R2, day 99, section 3.7) and RI.2.3 (day 115, section 3.11), and never uses the Bee Visit, the Butterfly Visit or the paragraph-writing checks as examples.

**Exit question.**
- **E88** (day 88). Spoken and written, one ask per stem. Question: "What does a bean seed need to start growing?" Two invented cards, "invented for practice": Card 1 (text): "A bean seed sleeps in a dry jar. When it gets wet, it swells. Then a tiny root pushes out." Card 2 (picture with caption): a bean seed in damp soil; caption: "Water and warmth help a seed wake up." Item (a): "Write one note that answers the question, in a few words, and add where you found it." Key: "water, card 1" or "water and warmth, picture" (any one fact the cards give that answers the question, with its source tag). Item (b): "Say where you found it." Key: the tag, said aloud. Shown means a short fact that answers plus the right tag; shown with help means right after one prompt ("Does it answer the question?"). Wrong: "a seed sleeps in a jar" (E); the right fact tagged with the wrong card (AE); the whole sentence copied word for word (D).
- Days 89 to 91 have no exit; their evidence is the note slips on the joint chart and the notes sheet (field 6), plus the oral card and the heard column.

**Reserved for the day 92 check.** plastic, rocket, unzip, repack, milk, and any sentence built on them.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The research-cycle chart (grade 1: ask, find, note, share) is set up on day 88 by the teacher and stays on the wall to day 118. The note slip (unit 1) holds a fact in a few words and its source tag; the joint chart holds the class's slips under the question; the notes sheet (unit 1) holds a child's slips. The feature route card (unit 2) returns on day 89, the topic and focus chart (unit 2) on day 90, the step chain and arrow cards (day 79) on day 91. The sound-spelling-map, syllable-types chart (now un- and re- added on day 88), word wall, talk routine and heard-six column stay up.
- **Standard rotation (foundations).** Three stations, tiles, sort and reread, ten children each; the day's six sit at the teacher table for round 1, two minutes each, then take the other stations.
- **Key moment 1, day 88: the first note slip.** Ideal instrument: a printed slip with a box for the fact and a box for the tag. Spec: fits; paper.
- **Key moment 2, day 91: the joint chart read as a chain.** Ideal instrument: slips on the wall with arrow cards. Spec: fits; paper.
- **Tools.** None. No account is needed.
- **A room with nothing.** Slips are torn paper; the chart is a sheet on the board; the cards are copied by hand; tiles are letters on card.

## 6. Days

## Day 88


- **4 min. Retrieve.** e1-shared-research (practice only): find one answer together in two familiar fossil sources and name its source. A miss gets today's question-to-source demonstration. If the class has no fossil sources, use the first two pine cards below.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** The research cycle chart: ask, find, note, share. Today the class asks one question, finds the answer in two cards, writes a note with a source tag and shares it. The question is "How does the wind help a pine tree?"
- **8 min. Hear two cards.** Teacher reads aloud, twice, the invented card 1 ("Some trees, such as pine trees, do not need an animal to move their pollen. Their pollen is light and dry. The wind blows it from tree to tree.") and describes card 2, a picture of a pine in spring with a yellow cloud and the caption "Wind carries yellow pollen away from a pine tree." Cards are given whole; no line is marked.
- **12 min. Model ask, find, note.** Teacher models one find (wind blows the pollen from tree to tree, card 1) and writes the note slip in four words with the tag "card 1"; models the picture note (yellow pollen blows away, picture) and puts both on the joint chart. Children join the sentence "The note answers the question because ___".
- **12 min. Pairs find and note.** Pairs take the same two cards and a question card, find the line or picture that answers, write one note slip each with its tag, and read the tag aloud to the partner. The teacher hears six pairs (1 minute each); the others work in pairs.
- **8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Where did you find your fact, and does it answer the question?" Children say "I found it in ___. It answers the question because ___". Hear the six.
- **8 min. Exit.** E88 (field 3): each child reads two new cards and writes one note with its tag; the teacher hears the six say the tag aloud. Cards collected.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** "Model ask, find, note" and "Pairs find and note" ask the child to write a note with a tag, the exit's shape. Instead: judge given slips (does it answer the question, is the tag right) and find and mark the line each slip came from.
- **5 min. Share.** Each pair puts one slip on the joint chart; the class reads them in order and says whether each answers the question.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Cycle 14 check repair: the commonest miss of the day 87 check (Y, AN, T or AM), the wrong spelling beside the right one, written on the board.
- **15 min. Teach: -ck, -k, -ic and un-, re-.** Retrieve the short-vowel patterns (cat, duck). Teach -ck after a short vowel in a one-syllable word (pack, kick, duck), -k after a consonant (desk, mask), -ic at the end of a longer word (attic, magic, comic, picnic). Teach the prefixes un- and re- (unlock, unpack, replay, rewind): cover the prefix, read the base, then add the prefix and say what it adds. Model, map, blend and spell. Tricky words (from memory, source to check): would, could. Dictate a sentence with pack.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 15 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 1 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "What two letters end pack?"

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the research-cycle chart and joint chart; pairs hold the cards and slips. Tool: None. By hand: finding, writing a four-word note and a tag, sorting, spelling. Evidence: E88; the slips; the heard column (heard-six and oral card).

## Day 89


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed E88 (D or E), cover the card, say the fact in four words, then write it; or hold up the question card and ask whether the note answers it.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Today the class asks "What do the anther and the stigma do?" The answer is in 01-a. We use its features to find the fact fast: the subheading, the bold words and the diagram labels.
- **10 min. Find with features.** Feature route card (unit 2): subheading, bold word, caption and labels. Teacher models one route for anther: the subheading "The parts of a flower that matter", then the bold word anther. Children say "The feature that led me was ___".
- **14 min. Pairs gather and tag.** Pairs find the line for the stigma and the line for the anther, write two slips with tags (the heading or the bold word that led them), and say what each part is for, not only what it is (AF). The teacher hears six pairs (1 minute each).
- **10 min. Add from the diagram.** Pairs read arrows 3 and 4 of the diagram and write one slip with the tag "diagram" saying what the arrow adds to the words. The teacher models one.
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which feature led you to your fact fastest, and how do you know?" Children say "The ___ led me because ___". Hear the six.
- **8 min. Share on the chart.** Each pair puts a slip on the joint chart; the class sorts them into answers the question and does not answer (E), and reads one tag aloud.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Yesterday's commonest miss (S, T or Y) written beside the right word and read aloud.
- **15 min. Read The Attic Trunk.** First reading after pattern teaching: the teacher reads page 1 aloud, pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4 (words with -ck, -k, -ic, un- and re- and the tricky words would and could). Confirm meaning after decoding. Spell kick and unpack. Text to make: a Curio Class reader, The Attic Trunk (field 11).
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 15 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 2 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "What does the picture not tell you that the letters did?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the route card and slips; the teacher holds the joint chart. Tool: None. By hand: finding, tagging, writing slips, spelling. Evidence: the slips on the chart; the oral card; the heard column.

## Day 90


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed AE or AF on day 89, trace one fact back to its line with a finger and read the tag; or join the word anther to the step it helps explain.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Gist means what a text is mostly about. Today we find the gist of 01-a first, then take two notes from one paragraph in a few words.
- **10 min. Read for gist.** Teacher reads all of 01-a aloud once (about 330 words). Partners say "It is mostly about ___" using the topic and focus chart (unit 2). Hear the six.
- **12 min. Ask and find.** Question: "Why do pollinators matter to people?" Pairs find the paragraph under the heading "Why does it matter?", read it with a finger, write two slips in a few words with the tag (the heading) and say them aloud. The teacher hears six pairs.
- **10 min. Trade and check.** Each pair trades slips with another pair; the reader says "Does it answer the question? Which source?" and the owner fixes or defends. The teacher hears the six.
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Which note answers the question best, and why?" Children say "I think ___ because ___". Hear the six.
- **10 min. Notes sheet.** Each child sets two slips on the notes sheet (unit 1): fact, source tag, what the fact means and a picture idea. Teacher scribes for a child who asks.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Yesterday's commonest miss (S, T or Y) written beside the right word and read aloud.
- **15 min. Reread and sort.** Reread The Attic Trunk in pairs for accuracy. Sort twelve cards by the ending: -ck (pack, kick, duck, deck), -k (desk, mask, tusk), -ic (attic, magic, comic, picnic) and by prefix (unlock, unpack, replay, rewind). Spell deck and magic on paper and use each in a sentence.
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 15 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 3 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which prefix means again?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the slips; each child holds a notes sheet; the teacher holds the topic and focus chart. Tool: None. By hand: finding, writing slips, filling a sheet, sorting. Evidence: the slips and notes sheet; the oral card; the heard column.

## Day 91


- **5 min. Opening repair.** If many missed D, B or AE on day 90, cover the card and say a fact in four words; or lay an arrow card (so, because) between two slips and say the link.
- **3 min. Say the plan.** Today we join notes into steps. A good explanation says why each step happens. We use the step chain and arrow cards.
- **12 min. Model the chain.** Teacher takes three slips from the joint chart (the anther makes pollen; the bee brushes the anther; pollen reaches the stigma) and lays them with two arrow cards, reading each link: "So pollen sticks to the bee. Because of that, some pollen reaches the stigma." Class echoes.
- **15 min. Pairs build a chain.** Pairs pick three of their own slips about how pollen moves from the anther to a second flower, order them with two arrow cards, and say each link with so or because. Then each writes one sentence per arrow, a few words each. The teacher hears six pairs.
- **10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify.** On "Does each arrow say why the next step happens?" Children say "The arrow shows ___ because ___". Hear the six.
- **10 min. Write.** Each child writes the explanation on the draft strip: First ___. Then ___. So ___. A few words each; teacher scribes for a child who asks.
- **5 min. Share.** Partners read the strips to each other and put a tick on the link they believe. The teacher records the strips; no score.
- **5 min. Opening repair (foundations).** Yesterday's commonest miss (S, T or Y) written beside the right word and read aloud.
- **15 min. Fresh passage and dictation.** Read the cycle 15 card, a fresh decodable passage of about 40 words (cycle 15 patterns only); confirm meaning after decoding. Dictate two words (stick and unfold) and one sentence with would or could. Text to make: the cycle 15 passage (field 11).
- **37 min. Rotation.** Standard rotation (section 2.1, field 5): round 1, 13 minutes, six at the teacher table for the cycle 15 oral card, 2 minutes each (12 teacher minutes); rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each at tiles, sort and reread. Group 4 of five at the oral card.
- **3 min. Close.** "Which part of would is the odd part?"

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the arrow cards and slips; each child holds a draft strip. Tool: None. By hand: ordering slips, writing a short explanation, dictation. Evidence: the draft strip; the dictation; the oral card; the heard column.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 88, "Where did you find your fact, and does it answer the question?"; day 89, "Which feature led you to your fact fastest, and how do you know?"; day 91, "Does each arrow say why the next step happens?"
- Written task, day 91: First, Then, So on the draft strip, a few words each.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) each note answers the question and is in the child's words; (2) each note has a right tag; (3) the arrows say why the next step happens, not only what comes next.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Each day opens with one repair of at most 5 minutes for the commonest miss of the day before; day 88 opens with the cycle 14 check repair.
- **Oral card.** The rotating six read six words and one sentence each day; the record sheet has a heard column. Day 88 begins cycle 15 with group 1 of five.
- **Fluency target.** A smooth second reading of The Attic Trunk by day 90; accuracy first; no timer.
- **Earlier work returning.** Research cycle chart (grade 1); note slip and notes sheet (unit 1); feature route card and topic and focus chart (unit 2); the step chain and arrow cards (day 79); the talk routine (unit 1).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: question, source, note, tag, answer, feature, gist, wind, pine, anther, stigma. Frames: "I found it in ___." "It answers the question because ___." "The ___ led me to it." "Which part do you mean?"
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer; cards are large print; a child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes; a source tag may be a drawn symbol (a card, a picture, a heading box).
- **Check accommodations.** No check here; the exit is read twice and the child may say the note aloud for the teacher to write, recorded as help.
- **Reading load.** At most 150 printed words a day: the two pine cards (about 45), the E88 cards (about 40), 01-a features (about 60), the cycle 15 reader.
- **Extension, same standard.** Ask a second question of the same cards ("What does pine pollen look like?") and tag the notes, or find where two cards say the same thing.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the cards aloud, then let the child find and tag the fact. Use real paper slips on the table as the joint chart. Spell three words with letter cards.
- **One projector.** Show the cards and the slips side by side; the child answers aloud and the teacher writes the tag.
