## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Model a school paragraph with a topic, facts, definitions, and conclusion.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-02. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision.  Topic base: M1 U2 L5. Foundations: base M1 C4 L17, 60 minutes, igh, ie, and y. Read the matched decodable after pattern teaching; spell words and sentences. Decodable: adapt StuckUpHigh for cycle 4 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Cycle 4 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 2 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn.
