## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Finish stories, compare speech registers, then respond to the complete record.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u3-02, g2-u3-01. The last ten two-minute recordings and five minutes of changeovers end by topic minute 25; day 117 records the first 20. Six groups of five compare a formal and an informal story line for three minutes each, 18 minutes, ending by minute 43. Use the final 15 topic minutes for feedback and short rerecordings after every child has evidence. The drawing clarifies the recorded story, which is audible, detailed, coherent and in complete sentences. In foundations, six children read new regularly spelled two-syllable long-vowel and consonant-le words daily on days 113 to 117, two minutes each; retain that record here, no repeat decoding. Retain the cycle check on day 112 (fresh oral cards on days 108 to 112) to complete RF.2.3b; an insecure pattern gets a fresh word during this response, never a whole new test. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 19 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Catch-up group: map, blend, matched decodable, spell, reread, regroup; keep child-read band texts in the topic block. Sensitivity: no bee contact; allergies checked and supervision for outdoor looks; indoor photographs are complete. Audio stays in class; partner recording offered. Recording window for 30 children: day 117 records 20 at two minutes each plus ten minutes for changeovers, 50 topic minutes; day 118 records the remaining ten at two minutes each plus five minutes for changeovers, all done by topic minute 25. Each child tells an audible, coherent story with facts, descriptive details and complete sentences, and connects a drawing to the audio. Partner recording still identifies each contribution. The complete g2-u3-02 bee-view story is an anchor here, read before the child's own story work.
