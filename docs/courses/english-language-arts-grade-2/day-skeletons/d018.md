## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Read another school article and begin a paragraph from text-based answers.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-02. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Base M1 U2 L6 assessment, part 1; questions and paragraph topic with facts and definitions. Topic base: M1 U2 L6. Foundations: base M1 C4 L18, 60 minutes, igh, ie, and y. Reread the decodable; sort, spell, and use the pattern in sentences. Decodable: adapt StuckUpHigh for cycle 4 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Reading evidence includes a question composed by the child and a text-supported answer, as well as answers across who, what, where, when, why and how. For a two-day product, complete-code judgment waits for the finished product. Cycle 4 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 3 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn. Begin one informative product; day 19 completes its topic, facts, definitions and conclusion before the whole-code judgment.
