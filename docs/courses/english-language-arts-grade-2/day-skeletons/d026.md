## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe a school discussion using facts, linked comments, and clarifying questions.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-01, g2-u1-02. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Base M1 U3 L5 retained; observe gaining the floor, linking, clarification, and questions that gather or deepen information. Topic base: M1 U3 L5. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 5 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Unit 1 reading record: six different children today, four minutes each in the foundations groups, 24 teacher minutes. Each has an initial and successive short oral reading, purpose and understanding questions, and a confirmation or correction after decoding. Across days 26 to 30 this covers all 30 children once. Other children read matched texts and spell. No second conference on day 31. Discussion record includes peer and adult talk in a small group and a brief whole-class exchange; the teacher observes respectful floor-taking, linking, clarification and a question/answer that gathers or deepens information.
