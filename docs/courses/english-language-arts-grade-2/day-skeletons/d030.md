## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe the finished school book and its spelling corrections.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-01, g2-u1-02. The book supplies a topic, facts, definitions and a conclusion. A short written transfer uses learned spellings; the child checks and corrects a draft word with a beginning dictionary. The last six children complete the four-minute reading conferences in foundations, 24 minutes; retain the other 24 records from days 26 to 29. Reading questions and images are on day 31. Topic base: M1 U3 L9. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 5 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Unit 1 reading record: six different children today, four minutes each in the foundations groups, 24 teacher minutes. Each has an initial and successive short oral reading, purpose and understanding questions, and a confirmation or correction after decoding. Across days 26 to 30 this covers all 30 children once. Other children read matched texts and spell. No second conference on day 31.
