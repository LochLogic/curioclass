## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Edit the research paragraph after its separate revision.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u3-01. Complete the days 99 to 101 shared-project record: independent gathering from provided sources answers the research question; the paragraph has a topic, facts, definitions and conclusion. Editing follows day 100 revision with peer and adult help. Spell a new word by a learned pattern, then check and correct one draft word in a beginning dictionary. Discussion evidence stays on days 99 to 100. Foundations lesson 84 teaches; decoding is sampled by the cycle check ending on day 102. Topic base: M3 U2 L14. Foundations: base M3 C17 L84, 60 minutes, -ch and -tch; -er and -est. Transfer taught patterns to a fresh decodable passage and dictation; confirm meaning after decoding. Decodable: adapt WateringYourGarden for cycle 17 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Earlier-owned foundations skills, including e2-spelling and e2-fluency, are continued, not reassigned. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Catch-up group: map, blend, matched decodable, spell, reread, regroup; keep child-read band texts in the topic block. Sensitivity: no bee contact; allergies checked and supervision for outdoor looks; indoor photographs are complete. Audio stays in class; partner recording offered. Base M3 U2 L14 completes the same research-paragraph assessment: editing after the separate day 100 revision, with adult and peer support. This completes both parts of W.2.5 before response on day 103. Cycle 17 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 4 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn.
