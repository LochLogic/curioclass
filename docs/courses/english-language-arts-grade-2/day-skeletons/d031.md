## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Explain school reading through questions, character responses and images.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-01, g2-u1-02, g2-u1-03. Short article responses ask and answer a child-generated question and cover the six question words; recount key ideas after hearing a short passage and explain what its diagram adds. Explain the story character response using words and an illustration. Retain day 11 literary asking-and-answering evidence, day 29 reviewed-pattern evidence and days 26 to 30 reading records without another conference. No claim of completed year-end reading range here. Topic base: M1 U3 L10. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 5 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class.
