## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Write a fossil letter using commas in its greeting and closing.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u2-03. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. W.2.6 first teaching, 10 minutes inside the writing time: on a shared device the teacher types the letter's greeting and one sentence while partners choose the words and their order, with adult guidance; day 72 publishes the narrative this way. Topic base: M2 U3 L8. Foundations: base M2 C12 L59, 60 minutes, -tion and -sion; module review. Transfer taught patterns to a fresh decodable passage and dictation; confirm meaning after decoding. Decodable: adapt TooManyOptions for cycle 12 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Earlier-owned foundations skills, including e2-spelling and e2-fluency, are continued, not reassigned. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Sensitivity: fossil time is very long ago, no numerical scale or family-faith comparison; Anning avoids dwelling on loss; invented product names only. Cycle 12 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 4 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn.
