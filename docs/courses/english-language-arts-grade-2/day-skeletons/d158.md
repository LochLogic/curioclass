## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Begin the year-end reading and word rotation on fresh school texts.
- **Note:** Year-end rotation: 15 different children today at five minutes each, 75 teacher minutes; finish the other 15 on day 159. Each child reads a short story and an informational school selection independently, rereads an oral excerpt, explains purpose and understanding and confirms or corrects after decoding; harder band support is recorded separately. Across the two text kinds include a poem, history/social-studies, science and a technical procedure in the reading portfolio. Fresh words sample long/short vowels, additional teams, two-syllable long vowels, prefixes and earlier suffixes, inconsistent patterns and irregular words. Retain day 153 cycle 26 -ous/-us reading evidence; do not hear those same words or patterns again. A 15-minute class written station asks spelling-pattern transfer, checking and correction with a dictionary. No article-pair comparison or reason-support task, those forms were just collected on day 156.  Reserved selection ids: g2-check2-story/poem/article/procedure.
