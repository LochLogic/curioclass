- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u2-01. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Foundations: base M2 C10 L50, 60 minutes, three sounds of -ed; would-contractions. Cycle written check, 15 minutes, on only the completed cycle patterns and spelling; retain oral evidence from the five-day rotation, then use the final 15 minutes for a fresh response. Decodable: adapt TheMarchingBand for cycle 10 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Earlier-owned foundations skills, including e2-spelling and e2-fluency, are continued, not reassigned. Sensitivity: fossil time is very long ago, no numerical scale or family-faith comparison; Anning avoids dwelling on loss; invented product names only. Cycle 10 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 5 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn. Use short responses: article topic and each paragraph focus; author purpose as answering, explaining or describing; locate facts using captions, bold print, subheadings, glossary, index, electronic menu and icon. Give one word/phrase prompt per strategy: sentence context, known prefix, root, compound parts, and glossary/beginning dictionary, with print and digital examples. The child chooses and checks the strategy on an unknown or multiple-meaning word; these are short parts on one article, not separate full products.
