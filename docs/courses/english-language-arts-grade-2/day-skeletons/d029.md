## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Write the conclusion and strengthen the book after peer suggestions.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-02. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Grade 1 feedback routine; explicit grade 2 revising and editing is owned in unit 2. Topic base: M1 U3 L8. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 5 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Dictionary independent practice, 5 minutes: check a draft spelling and correct it using the entry. Written pattern transfer, 10 minutes, samples long/short vowels and reviewed teams through cycle 5; no later team is scored. Unit 1 reading record: six different children today, four minutes each in the foundations groups, 24 teacher minutes. Each has an initial and successive short oral reading, purpose and understanding questions, and a confirmation or correction after decoding. Across days 26 to 30 this covers all 30 children once. Other children read matched texts and spell. No second conference on day 31.
