## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Revise the narrative with peer and adult feedback and expanded or rearranged sentences.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u2-03. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Base M2 U3 L12 split: revising first; simple and compound sentences, expansion and rearrangement. Topic base: M2 U3 L12 (split across days 70 and 71). Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 12 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson.
