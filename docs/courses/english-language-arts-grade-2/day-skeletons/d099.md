- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u3-01. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Catch-up group: map, blend, matched decodable, spell, reread, regroup; keep child-read band texts in the topic block. Sensitivity: no bee contact; allergies checked and supervision for outdoor looks; indoor photographs are complete. Audio stays in class; partner recording offered. Observe one half of the class in three groups of five, six minutes per group: respectful turns, linked comments, a request for clarification, and asking and answering a speaker to gather information or deepen understanding. Cycle 17 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 2 of five covers 30 once across the cycle.
