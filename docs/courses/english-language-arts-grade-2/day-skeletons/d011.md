## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Check school-story questions, character response, and illustrations in a short written response.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-01. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Base M1 U1 L11 assessment adapted; all six question words, a major-event response, and words with illustrations. Topic base: M1 U1 L11. Foundations: base M1 C3 L11, 60 minutes, ee, ea, and y. Retrieve known patterns; explicitly model, map sounds, blend, then spell. Decodable: adapt DoFishEatCheese for cycle 3 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Reading evidence includes a question composed by the child and a text-supported answer, as well as answers across who, what, where, when, why and how. For a two-day product, complete-code judgment waits for the finished product. Cycle 3 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 1 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn. The child explains a response to a major event, using both an illustration and words; the image response may concern character, setting or plot.
