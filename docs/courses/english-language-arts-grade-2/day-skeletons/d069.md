## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Draft a new discovery narrative with event details, time words, and closure.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u2-03. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Base M2 U3 L11 retained; product plus brief sentence performances supply all five word-form parts where the story does not elicit them naturally. Topic base: M2 U3 L11. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 12 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Sensitivity: fossil time is very long ago, no numerical scale or family-faith comparison; Anning avoids dwelling on loss; invented product names only. Score the elaborated narrative for actions, thoughts, feelings, time words and closure. Where absent, use one short sentence each for a collective noun, an irregular plural, a reflexive pronoun, an irregular past verb, and adjective/adverb choice by the word modified. These are short items, not extra writing products.

**Skills:**

- e2-narrative (grade 2): taught or practised here
- e2-grammar (grade 2): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **W.2.3:** Write narratives in which they recount a well-elaborated event or short sequence of events, include details to describe actions, thoughts, and feelings, use temporal words to signal event order, and provide a sense of closure.
- **L.2.1a:** Use collective nouns (e.g., group).
- **L.2.1b:** Form and use frequently occurring irregular plural nouns (e.g., feet, children, teeth, mice, fish).
- **L.2.1c:** Use reflexive pronouns (e.g., myself, ourselves).
- **L.2.1d:** Form and use the past tense of frequently occurring irregular verbs (e.g., sat, hid, told).
- **L.2.1e:** Use adjectives and adverbs, and choose between them depending on what is to be modified.
