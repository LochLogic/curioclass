## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Add facts, definitions, and clarifying pictures to the school book.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-02. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision.  Topic base: M1 U3 L7. Foundations: 60-minute flexible group block through cycle 5 only; map, blend, read matching decodable, spell, reread, and regroup from evidence. No new source lesson. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Dictionary guided practice, 10 minutes: locate, compare, check and correct a draft spelling with the teacher, then explain the correction. Unit 1 reading record: six different children today, four minutes each in the foundations groups, 24 teacher minutes. Each has an initial and successive short oral reading, purpose and understanding questions, and a confirmation or correction after decoding. Across days 26 to 30 this covers all 30 children once. Other children read matched texts and spell. No second conference on day 31. If day 27 folds here, run its 10-minute dictionary model before this day's guided practice; fold repeated rereading, not this first teaching.
