## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Reread in groups and try a fresh response to the character's challenge.
- **Note:** Topic reading: g2-u1-01. Reread relevant source passages before writing or speaking; the child draft is an additional text during revision. Response to day 11 with a fresh short task. Foundations: base M1 C3 L12, 60 minutes, ee, ea, and y. Read the matched decodable after pattern teaching; spell words and sentences. Decodable: adapt DoFishEatCheese for cycle 3 only where text and picture rights are cleared, otherwise write a pattern-matched replacement. First reading after pattern teaching; reread through the cycle. Map irregular words, no guessing from pictures or first letters. Text discussion: turn, pair, link, clarify; gain the floor, link comments, and ask or answer the speaker to clarify, gather information, or deepen understanding. Brief protocol most days, at least twice weekly. Sensitivity: schools and countries are everyday places, no ranking or exotic framing; no probing moves, shelter, or home life; no records or photographs leave class. Cycle 3 oral record: six different children for two minutes each in today's foundations group, 12 teacher minutes; group 2 of five covers 30 once across the cycle. The final lesson retains these records, not another oral queue. This samples only this cycle's taught patterns; new material is modelled before that child's turn.
