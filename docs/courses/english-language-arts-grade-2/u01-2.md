- **What the teacher page must explain.** A picture adds to a response only when the child says what in the picture shows it (a hand position, a face, a path), not when the child names the picture (error C). A comment links when it uses a word of the last speaker (error I). An ai or ay word is read by its team before it is checked against the picture (error P).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 8, "How does Mira respond to each event?"; day 9, "Which words in the difficult exchange change its meaning?"; day 12, "What does the challenge make Mira do?".
- Written task, day 11: "Mira ___ because ___" with a picture detail named.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) names the event; (2) says what Mira did, not what she is like; (3) names the picture detail that shows it.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Days 7 to 12 open with one repair of at most 5 minutes, for the commonest exit miss; if none, a retrieval of the day before.
- **Oral card.** The rotating six read six words and one sentence, using patterns taught earlier. The record sheet has a heard column.
- **Fluency target.** A smooth second reading of the cycle reader and the day 10 passage, accuracy first; no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: approach, offer, accept, hesitate, return, mean. Frames: "The event is ___, so Mira ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "The picture shows ___."
- **Access.** Pointing is an answer. Pictures differ in color and shape. A child who cannot write draws and the teacher scribes.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards are read aloud; the spelling check can be answered with tiles.
- **Reading load.** A child reads the reader (about 60 words), labels, line cards and the 75-word passage; the chapters and the check text are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain a second picture detail and its link to the response.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read one chapter, ask "What happens?" and "What does Mira do?" and let the child point. Use paper cards for the event and the action.
- **One projector.** Show the picture beside the lines; mark on printed cards.
