## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: opinion, reason, because, also, exact, tiny, sip. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "Also, ___." "Let us ___." A child may say a reason in the home language first and have it scribed.
- **Access.** Enlarge print, read cards aloud, accept a scribe or a drawn picture with a labelled reason; the exit strips are described aloud.
- **Check accommodations.** Extra time within the sitting; the card read again on request; a scribe for content, with the support recorded. Conventions are not scribed in the editing pass.
- **Reading load.** Children read the model opinion (about 60 words) and cards M and N (about 40 words each) with a partner; the exit cards are read aloud; the sheets have a few labels.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second reason that a source card supports and say why it fits; or choose an exact word for the ending and say what it shows.
