## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How do plants and pollinators help each other?"
Day 78 opens on *Pollen on the Move*, words beside diagram, then the compelling question. The 15-minute bridge supplies plant facts.
Day 79: How does one step make the next possible? Day 81: What does the diagram clarify? Day 87: Why does the poem choose clings? Day 97: Which two sources explain this pollinator? Day 112: Which story details agree with the articles?
The poster and talk on 113 to 115 are built from 01 on 105 with own notes.
Model connected rereading on 79 and research on 88; withdraw selected answers on 92. Most teaching days, at least twice weekly, use turn, pair, link, clarify: mark evidence, take turns, link remarks, ask clarifying questions, write a supported sentence.


In 01, read *Pollen on the Move* from 78, *A Bee Visits* on 92, *A Butterfly Visits* on 93, and *A Bat Visits at Night* and *A Hummingbird Visits* on 94. Read 02, *One Bee, Two Flowers*, on 104 and 112. Poems 03-a, *Land, Cling, Carry*, and 03-b, *A Dusty Visitor*, enter on 85 and 87.
Target child-read prose in the grades 2 to 3 band. In 01, linked steps, process structure, domain words and flower knowledge require label preview, causal rereading and independent explanation. In 02, separate imagined thoughts from factual actions; map before retelling. Its narrator prepares children's stories. Verse has no usable measure; compare precise words in 03.
Preview these words on the named day; reuse during and after reading. Tier 1: carry, land, small, tiny, visit, share. Tier 3: pollen, pollination, nectar, anther, stigma, pollinator. Others are Tier 2.
