## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What did the missing letter say, and why did someone want it to stay lost?"
**Hook, day 1.** Read the commissioned opening at the empty letter file. Separate what the file establishes from what the volunteers suspect; open the compelling question without revealing its answer.

**Inquiry questions.** Q1, day 3: Which incident changes the search, and what dialogue explains the decision? Q2, day 10: Where does knowing more than the volunteers create suspense or humour? Q3, day 18: How do the archive readings change which clues deserve confidence?

**Closing explanation, days 21 to 27.** Explain how knowledge gaps and consequential decisions build suspense. Use beginning, turning point and ending evidence, including the letter's contents and concealment motive. The manuscript must supply these; this plan invents neither.

**Teaching.** Model evidence on 1, theme on 2, plot effects on 3, reader knowledge on 5 and nonfiction reasoning on 6. Withdraw completed models on 7; students lead the final reread on 18.

## U7. Talk and writing

On 10 teach: prepare a citation and question; agree roles and goal; invite, connect and answer; restate new evidence; qualify or justify. Run formally on 10, 20, 29 and 30, informally in pairs daily. Record one observation and give a specific next move. The teacher judges the essay using field 5's components.

**Model form, Curio Class, 2026, ours, invented for practice:** "Before the solo, readers learn that the accompanist must leave early. The singer, unaware of this deadline, keeps delaying her entrance. Suspense grows with each delay because the reader can foresee a performance without accompaniment. Her leisurely preparation therefore has consequences she cannot yet imagine." Form demonstration only; the final day 19 model requires checked novella quotations.

## U8. Practice that returns

Retrieve the scheduled skill, then the week's words in context. Weekly partner rereading builds accuracy and expression without a rate target. Choice reading keeps its listed 15-minute blocks; log pages and confer using the course roster.

## U9. Supports and extension

Preview essential words; mark finite verbs and clause boundaries in hard sentences. Offer “This line is stronger because…” and fade it after 7. Audio accompanies the same text during learning. Extend by testing a rival inference. Finish 35,000 to 45,000 words within existing class and home reading; check feasibility against the manuscript.

## U10. Two rooms

At home, the parent models one text-based position and supplies another for the child to connect, using the same criteria. With one projector, keep individual print copies and paper evidence. Do not claim unobserved discussion contexts. Curio Class collects no student work.
