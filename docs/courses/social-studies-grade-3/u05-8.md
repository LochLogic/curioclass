# Section 5.8 skeleton: First meetings and early colonies

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-3/5/s/5.8), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-3 u05-8.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Grade 3 children are early readers and writers. Printed stems are short and plain, no sentence over 15 words; the teacher reads every card, source and stem aloud once. Answers may be a sentence, a filled table, a labeled map or words said at the side table. Each day runs 40 minutes. Exits and records are marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen (one scale for each record); the unit plan's 2, 1, 0 scores belong to the teacher's scoring of day 120 only, from the record sheet, never self-marked. Every made-up card is said aloud to be made up and printed "invented for practice". Native nations are named by their own names and live today.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **D3.4.3-5 and D4.1.3-5, days 117, 118 and 120.** "Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question" and "Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources." Day 117 gathers evidence from two sources; day 118 writes one claim with a detail and the maker from each, and a limit; day 120 asks it again on new sources (Q8).
- **D1.4.3-5, day 117.** "Explain how answering supporting questions helps answer the big question of an inquiry." Taught as the link: what the answer shows about the unit question.
- **D2.His.1.3-5, days 119 and 120.** "Make and use a time-ordered list of related events to compare developments that happened at the same time." Practiced on a fresh timeline with a written cue and a revision.
- **The first-half and later codes, day 120.** Every code the unit teaches is asked once on day 120, from revised records (see the unit plan, field 5); no teaching day here teaches them new.
- **Limits that bite here.** The claim must keep to what the sources show: a possibility and a limit; evidence cannot prove what would have happened. Wishes are not evidence. Native nations are named by name, in the present tense where they live on. No reenactment, no simulation.
- **Connections.** Unit 4 corroboration (two tellers who each saw it; an account and its copy are one teller) and claim, evidence, because (day 117); the claim frame and the supporting-question record (days 90 and 118); source-sourcing on two papers (day 99); the timeline strip (days 91 to 104); ELA grade 3's opinion writing, now running (day 118).
- **New here.** None; day 117 and 118 apply, and 119 and 121 practice and repair.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Why two details from one source are one source; how a supporting question links to the big one; how the claim names a limit; how to give a criterion cue in ten seconds; how to read the dossiers aloud without leading; how scoring works on day 120 (2 accurate and connected, 1 partial, 0 missing or contradicted; never a total that hides a missing part).

## 6. Days

## Day 117


- **10 min. Return and repair (W from E116).** If many missed W: ten minutes, harbor, labor and trade connected on a fresh invented port record. If few: five minutes at the side table while the rest read the question board.
- **4 min. Retrieval.** The unit question and the question board. Unit 4: two tellers who each saw it agree; an account and its copy are one teller. The claim frame from grade 3 unit 1: claim, evidence, because.
- **10 min. Gather.** Two sources: John Smith's 1616 account, a visitor's telling, and the Massachusett Tribe's own page (sources.csv U5N, U5H; Mourt's Relation is cut). Teacher reads each, original summary beside the short primary words. Pairs fill a table: source, maker, detail, what it shows about sharing the land. Both tellings of the harvest, Mourt's and the Wampanoag page, are a reminder of day 103.
- **6 min. Pairs: two sources.** Cards of four details, two from each maker (fresh). Each pair names the maker of each detail and says how many sources there are (AG). The teacher hears a rotating six pairs.
- **8 min. The link.** The supporting-question record (day 90) is reread: the skaters and walkers path. The class says how one answer helps the big question (AH). Pairs build one claim from two fresh details, ending "this does not prove ___" (J). Practice record; written cue after class; revised on day 119.
- **2 min. Exit.** E117.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher the question board; each pair a table. Tool: None. By hand: the table, the sort. Evidence: E117; the link record. Hand-off: tomorrow, write your claim.

## Day 118


- **10 min. Return and repair (J, AG or AH from E117 and 117).** The most-missed letter gets the part: J ten minutes, AG or AH five. The other letters go to a small group of six during the write part. Fresh invented details, no dossier text.
- **3 min. Step back.** The teacher reads the blank claim frame once. It is not modeled. The unit question is read aloud.
- **14 min. Write.** Each child writes a short claim on the unit question, with the two sources named, one detail and the maker of each, how each supports the claim, and a limit ("this does not prove ___"). A sentence frame is offered. A claim may be longer than one sentence (ELA grade 3 opinion writing is running). The teacher walks and hears a rotating six, named on the record.
- **8 min. Share.** Pairs read to each other; the partner names the two sources and the details. Three children read aloud, each a different supported claim; the class hears them before revising.
- **4 min. Revise.** Each child adds to or fixes the claim in pencil, below the first.
- **1 min. Collect.** The sheets go to the folder.

Who holds what is carried: each child a claim sheet. Tool: None. By hand: the claim, the revision. Evidence: the claim sheet (retained, not an exit). Hand-off: tomorrow, review and fix your earlier records.

## Day 119


- **5 min. Written feedback.** Each child gets the folder: days 90, 106, 107, 108, 110, 113, 116 and 117 records, each with a one-line criterion cue; read silently or with a partner.
- **20 min. Revisions.** Each child revises the records whose cue says "not yet", and finishes any missing record, in coloured pencil below the first answer, about 2 to 3 minutes each. No new teaching. A child who scored 0 on a first-half part answers one spoken prompt while the teacher moves from child to child (a rotating six, named on the record).
- **5 min. Fresh timeline.** Fresh dated trade records (invented for practice) placed on the paper strip with ten-year marks; two developments that overlap are compared.
- **5 min. Timeline cue and revision.** The teacher circles one missed criterion on each strip (spacing or overlap), about ten seconds a child, starting with the first finished (ours, untested); each child then revises the strip alone. Only the revision is scored.
- **3 min. Review.** The strip with the colonies, then the sea routes: spoken, one question each.
- **2 min. Exit.** E119. The strip, cause chart and claim frame are taken down, leaving the blank frames.

Who holds what is carried: each child a folder and a strip. Tool: None. By hand: the revisions, the strip. Evidence: E119; the revised records. Hand-off: tomorrow, the unit check.

## Day 121


- **5 min. Feedback.** Each child gets the teacher's note on the day 120 sheet: one missed idea, circled, with one sentence.
- **20 min. Groups.** Four groups of the 0 and 1 cells, largest gaps first, meet the teacher in turn for five minutes (K and AI repairs are inside the group moves; fresh passages and maps, not dossier text); the others work in pairs from the written move for that component. The teacher hears each child in a group once.
- **10 min. Fresh task.** Each child fixes the missed idea on a fresh map or passage and writes why the fix is right. E121.
- **5 min. Reflection.** Each child says to a partner what they fixed and why; the folder is closed for the unit.

Who holds what is carried: each child the note and the fresh task. Tool: None. By hand: the fix. Evidence: E121. Hand-off: day 122 is course check 2 (primary and secondary, and a short timeline).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 117, "Who made this, and what does it show about sharing?"; day 118, "What does this not prove?"; day 121, "What did you fix, and why is it right?"
- Written task, day 118: one claim with two named sources, a detail and the maker of each, and a limit.
- Judged by the teacher with look-fors: (1) two sources named and different; (2) an accurate detail from each; (3) how each supports the claim; (4) a limit stated; (5) no wish as evidence. Shown, shown with help or not yet seen on days 117 to 119; 2, 1, 0 on day 120.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: source, maker, detail, claim, limit, prove, evidence. Frames: "I think ___ because the first source says ___ and the second source says ___." "This does not prove ___." A home-language word beside each is welcome.
- **Access.** Large-print dossiers, a color for each source, speech or a scribe for claims, sheets read aloud, extra time on 119 and 120.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all directions, sources and stems aloud; accept a spoken, pointed or dictated answer without prompting reasoning; the six heard may include any child who needs to say it.
- **Reading load.** Source lines are one to three short sentences, read aloud; the claim frame is a short list.
- **Content notes.** The dossiers name land loss, war and slavery plainly. No graphic detail, no slurs, no role play. A stop card and the right to pass hold.
- **Extension, same standard.** A claim that uses a third colony from the strip, or a second limit.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** The adult reads each source aloud, helps fill the table, and lets the child write or say the claim. On day 119 the adult marks one missed criterion on the strip. On 120 the adult reads each task and writes the child's words. On 121, the last day of the unit, the adult goes over the day 120 sheet with the child, picks the one missed idea and listens as the child explains the fix.
- **One projector.** Project the claim frame and one source at a time; each child has a paper copy. On day 120 only the card named is shown.
