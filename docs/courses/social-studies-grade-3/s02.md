## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Who should make the rules in our town?"
- **Hook, day 25:** compare a classroom turn-taking rule with the local government record. Who can change each, and whose needs should count?
- **Co-write on day 26:** Why have governments (27)? Who decides locally (32)? What do rules protect (34)? How can people participate (37)? How can we decide fairly (40)? What limits government (43)?
- **Closing, day 45:** answer the compelling question using Local Government Record and Class Decision Record. Defend a balance between authorized decision-makers and public participation; different balances need evidence.
- **Teaching:** model source maker, date and purpose on 27; compare records on 32; model restating and reasoning on 38. Students select evidence on 44 and revise after hearing another claim on 45.

**Teacher notes:** distinguish elected and appointed roles, school governance and classroom management. On day 33, discuss a proportionate consequence such as removing an obstruction, without voting away anyone's rights. Property taxes are one school funding source, not the only source. Fees do not purchase civic rights. Rights are not rewards for completing duties; procedures must respect them. Do not request citizenship, affiliation, family finances or tax payments.

Teach tribal sovereignty as a nation's right to govern itself, not a lower rung under a state. Describe nearby nations from their own records. Menominee is one worked case. If restoration appears, explain plainly that federal policy tried to end recognition and members worked to restore it; the people and their governance did not begin with recognition. No identity simulation, costumes, sacred material or student spokesperson roles. Constitutions change; historic promises did not provide equal rights in practice. "Oldest functioning written constitution" is a worked-example fact about Massachusetts; San Marino's older statutes are often named as a rival claim, so say "often called". Historic faith requirements are not current student duties; no religious performance.

**Real class decision:** on day 38 invite feasible questions about shared classroom life. Exclude anyone's rights, identity, access or punishment.
