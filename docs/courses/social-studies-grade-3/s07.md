## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Who has made our community what it is?"
- **Hook, 141:** compare an old image of our own town with a current public-place photograph; the Long Wharf engraving is the fallback. Ask whose work shaped this place and whose story the images cannot tell.
- **Co-write on 141:** Who lived here first (142)? Why did towns grow (143 to 145)? What do memories and photographs reveal (146 to 148)? Who shaped change (149 to 152)? How can we contribute (153 to 157)? List needed evidence; discuss two meanings of contribution.
- **Closing, 157:** answer the compelling question from Local Nation Account (142) and Local Interview (146), checked against Labor History Card (145) and the action record. Defend which contributions mattered; hear another supported answer, then revise.
- **Teaching:** model sourcing and conflicting accounts on 142, map limits on 143, interviews on 146, overlapping timelines on 148 and explanatory writing on 150. Step back on 151 and 157. Teach founder, immigrant, population, continuity, biography, evidence, contribution and prediction in context.
