## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Who has made our community what it is?"
- **Hook, 141:** compare the Long Wharf engraving with a current public-place photograph. Ask whose work shaped this place and whose story the images cannot tell.
- **Co-write on 141:** Who lived here first (142)? Why did towns grow (143 to 145)? What do memories and photographs reveal (146 to 148)? Who shaped change (149 to 152)? How can we contribute (153 to 157)? List needed evidence; discuss two meanings of contribution.

Teach founder, immigrant, population, continuity, biography, evidence, contribution and prediction in context.

Observe immediate use on 157; record longer effects as unknown. At home, deliberate with the adult and share with another family adult.
