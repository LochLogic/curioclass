# Course frame: Grade 3: Communities Then and Now

Course id ss-k5 (grade 3 row), grade 3, draft frame, written 2026-11-13; the scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 7, as in Curio Class's own grade 3 year (docs/courses/social-studies/grade-3-outline.md).
- **Days.** 160 teaching days: 150 in units and 10 at course level (6 for three course-level checks, 4 respond
  days). It assumes one social studies lesson of about 40 minutes every school day. The year owns 27 progression
  rows, more than any other elementary grade (grade 2 has 18, grade 4 has 17), and runs seven inquiry arcs, a
  community project and the year's informed action, so it takes the full-year yardstick; it does not share the
  day the way kindergarten to grade 2 may.
- **Standard.** C3 for skills (the 3 to 5 band, data/standards/c3-indicators.txt); Massachusetts 2018 is the
  content anchor, but grade 3 is the year the anchor's own-state content is replaced (STANDARD.md section 2,
  adoption bar item 13). Each unit cites the anchor codes its rows hold, taught in general form: the student's own
  town, state, region and Native nations in place of Massachusetts's, with worked examples from several states.
  MA.3.T6.2 is excluded on purpose (data/progressions/social-studies-excluded.csv): its early 1770s events are
  taught in grade 5. The check set (California, Tennessee, New York) is read unit by unit at the unit plan; this
  frame names each unit's candidate additions with their source. The values rule (DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-12)
  governs every unit.
- **Base text.** None: every complete open social studies program is NonCommercial, ShareAlike or all rights
  reserved (adoption bar section 1), so every day is Curio Class's own. Open pieces are adapted with a credit
  line: Library of Congress and National Archives primary sources, National Park Service pages, US Census Bureau
  data for any town, USGS maps, state constitutions and legislature pages, public-domain images. Native nations'
  own websites and published histories are read and linked, and quoted only with permission. Massachusetts's
  Investigating History grade 3 and the New York toolkit's grade 3 inquiries are reference only, never copied.
- **The inquiry arc.** Every unit runs the full C3 arc at grade 3 depth: a compelling question set by the
  teacher, supporting questions written together as a class, sources the unit supplies, a short claim with one
  or two pieces of evidence from named sources, and sharing it. Unit 1 teaches the arc (ss3-inquiry-questions,
  ss3-claim-evidence); units 2 to 7 use it.
- **The own-place method.** Every unit has a local thread (a short guided task about the class's own town or
  state) and worked examples rotated across US regions so no region carries every example. The teacher guide
  links each state's own standards page and official state and tribal sites. Curio Class does not claim to meet
  any one state's grade 3 standards.
- **Threads through the year.** A timeline in every history unit from unit 5 on (taught in unit 5, read in years,
  decades and centuries); unit 4 places events as before and after, then and now. Telling a primary from a
  secondary source (taught in unit 4, used from then on). A short biography read in most units from unit 1, whose
  first biography read carries the readiness check on grade 1's ss1-leaders, with the written biography in unit 7.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent sensitivity read (bar item 12). Units 1, 4, 5 and 7 carry
  Native content, and units 5, 6 and 7 hard history; those units also need the qualified person's read
  (FOUNDATION section 7) when one is found.
- **Informed action.** Once, in unit 7: the class names a local problem from its project, weighs ways to help,
  decides by a fair process and makes one contribution. The class chooses; the course never picks the cause.
- **Reading level.** Sources are read aloud, adapted or excerpted to a grade 2 to 3 band; primary sources are
  short (a few lines, a map, a photograph, an object) with a sourcing step before use.
