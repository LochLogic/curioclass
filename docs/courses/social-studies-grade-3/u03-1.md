# Section 3.1: What it takes to meet a need

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-3/3/s/3.1), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-3 u03-1.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 3 children are early readers and writers. Printed stems are short and plain, no sentence over 15 words; the teacher reads every card, source and prompt aloud once. An answer may be a sentence, a labeled drawing, a filled table or spoken at the side table. Each day runs 40 minutes. Teaching cases are invented and said aloud to be made up (a bakery, a lawn service, a boat workshop, Pinecrest library); exit-only cases are the clinic, the potter and window cleaner, the bicycle repairer and the book team. The School Needs Record is the class's own and real. Exits are marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **D1.1.3-5, this section's share.** "Explain why the inquiry matters to other people." Part: explain importance (day 49): name a group besides the people directly served, and a reason. The day 49 exit is practice; it is revised on day 66 and scored on day 67.
- **D2.Eco.3.3-5, this section's share.** "Identify natural resources, workers' knowledge and skills, and equipment used for goods and services." Part: identify each resource type: natural, human and capital together on day 51, after day 50 sorts goods from services and names producers. Day 52 shows how work is shared. Day 55 applies the type sort to local businesses.
- **D1.2.3-5, this section's share.** "Identify economic ideas in the question that people can interpret differently." Part: identify interpretable ideas (day 50): "needs" can mean supplies for today or saving for later.
- **Limits that bite here.** Capital means equipment people made and use to make things, never money. Wheat is natural; flour is made from it. A human resource is learned knowledge and skill, never a person's worth, and practising a skill decides no one's future. A person can produce and consume. No job belongs to a group of people. Day 52 (specialization, division of labor) is the frame's settled extension and has no day 67 item. No child reports on home, family jobs or money.
- **Connections.** D3.4.3-5: day 49 asks who made it, when and why of the School Needs Record, the unit's first source (the closing claim is day 65). Day 50's reteach of grade 1 choices and saving feeds days 53 and 56.
- **Review, not new (the ledger's words).** Goods and services (review of social-studies-grade-1 u08-1); natural resource, human resource, producer and consumer (review of social-studies-grade-2 u03-3); choose and give up, save and bank (grade 1 u08-2 and u08-3); big question, supporting question, matters to others, two readings and the wonder card (review of social-studies-grade-3 u01-1); who made it, when and why (u01-5); the question board, exit ticket and Return and repair (u02-1, u02-3); need (review of social-studies-kindergarten u06-2).
- **New here.** Supplier; capital resource; division of labor; specialize.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Capital at an adult's level: physical capital is equipment and buildings used to make goods and services; human capital is the knowledge and skill workers have learned (from memory, source to check). Why flour is traced back to wheat. How to run Return and repair when two readiness gaps appear. How to write the cue on each day 49 exit and day 50 record by day 62: about 3 minutes a child for both records, about 90 minutes (ours, untested).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none numbered here. D1.1 is judged from the day 49 exit and D1.2 from the day 50 record, each after a written cue and revision on day 66 (unit plan field 5). Q3 (D2.Eco.3) is first taught here and is listed in 3.2, where its code was taught last.

**Exit questions.** Each is a quarter page read aloud once, answered in a word, a sentence, a drawing or aloud, and marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example. Every exit case is new and kept out of teaching.
- **E49 (New Clinic Needs Card, invented for practice).** "Clinic Needs Card, invented. Who besides patients needs to know how we meet needs?" "Clinic Needs Card. Why does that group need to know?" Answer: A named group other than the patients, such as cleaners, drivers or nurses. A reason tied to the clinic need, such as cleaners need clean sheets or drivers need a plan for on-time delivery. Wrong: "Only the patients" (T); "Because I like clinics" (x). Kept as a practice record, revised on day 66.
- **E50 (Potter and Plumber Card, invented for practice).** "Potter and Plumber Card, invented. Which one sells a good?" "Potter and Plumber Card. Which one does a service?" Answer: The potter: a mug is a thing you can hold. The plumber: fixing a sink is work done for someone. Wrong: "The plumber, because people pay" (V); "Both are goods" (V).
- **E51 (Repairer Card, invented for practice).** "Repairer Card, invented. Which one is the capital resource?" "Repairer Card. Which one is the natural resource?" Answer: The wrench: a tool people made and use to make things. The water. Wrong: "The water" (A); "The wrench, because it is metal" (U). Exit box extra (practice): "Repairer Card. Which one is the human resource?" Answer: Her skill at finding faults: knowledge she learned.
- **E52 (New Book Team Card, invented for practice).** "Book Team Card, invented. Which one divides the work of one book?" "Book Team Card. Which one specializes?" Answer: The workshop: a writer, an illustrator and a binder each do one task on the same book. The repair shop, which does mainly one kind of work apart from other businesses. Wrong: "The repair shop" (S); "The workshop" (S).

**Day 50 supporting-question record (practice).** "My question: ___. A fact it needs: ___. People may read the word ___ two ways: ___ or ___. This helps answer our big question because ___." Read, cued and revised on day 66.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The question board opens on day 50 with its six answers dated (52, 53, 54, 58, 60, 64) and stays up all unit. The three baskets (natural, human, capital) begin on day 51 and return on days 55 and 56, then fade after the unit. Who made it, when and why is asked of every record, starting with the School Needs Record. The cue-then-revise routine starts here with the day 49 and 50 records (revised day 66).
- **Key moment 1, day 51: the three baskets.** Ideal instrument: real tools and picture cards sorted into three baskets, an oven set beside a coin. Spec: print and real objects fit; no tool is needed.
- **Key moment 2, day 50: the board and the record.** Ideal instrument: chart paper and a record strip. Spec: print fits.
- **A room with nothing.** Chart paper, index cards, three boxes or envelopes as baskets, ten counters a pair; the teacher draws the cards.

## 6. Days

## Day 49


- **3 min. Hand back.** The day 48 response is handed back: each child gets the F48a to F48h redo note, and the teacher names the day 48 readiness gaps (choices and saving) that day 50 repairs.
- **6 min. The School Needs Record.** The teacher shows the dated record (paper, water, cleaning) and reads it aloud once. The class asks who made it (our teacher, with the school office's help), when (the date on it) and why (to see what our school needs each day). No school worker is named or questioned in class unless they agreed beforehand.
- **10 min. Trace who supplies it.** Pairs each get one need strip (five pairs for each need): the need, who supplies it, who uses it. Draw or write one arrow each. The teacher visits a rotating six pairs, about 1 minute each. Children the readiness check named for oral support sit nearest the teacher.
- **7 min. Who else needs the answer?** The teacher models "matters to others" for paper: the office staff and the art teacher need to know too. Pairs name one other group and a reason for one need (1 minute each way). The teacher hears a rotating six.
- **5 min. Wonder card.** Each child writes or draws one question about how our community gets what it needs, and one source that could answer it. Cards go on a wonder strip.
- **6 min. The big question and two words.** The teacher posts "How does our community get what it needs?". Need is said again from kindergarten; supplier is new, with pictures. The question board opens; its six supporting questions come on day 50.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** E49.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the board and the record; pairs hold their strips. Tool: None. By hand: the strip, the wonder card, exit. Evidence: E49 (practice, a cue before day 66); the wonder cards are not scored.

## Day 50


- **5 min. Return and repair.** The more-missed readiness gap, whole class, in one sequence that links today's choice to tomorrow's goal: model 2 minutes, guide 2, a fresh choice 1 (unit plan field 4). The grade 2 retrieval (a fishing crew uses fish, a boat and fishing knowledge; which comes from nature?) is said to a partner inside the guide minutes. If only one gap, a named group of up to 6 takes it at the Pairs sort side table instead.
- **5 min. Goods, services, producers and consumers.** The teacher sorts four picture cards of local kinds of business (a shoe shop, a window washer, a bus company, a bakery) into two hoops and marks who makes and who uses each. Review, not new.
- **10 min. Pairs sort.** Pairs sort 12 picture cards of kinds of local business (invented; real local pages later, source to check) into goods and services and mark producer and consumer. The teacher visits a rotating six pairs. A side table of up to 6 meets the teacher 5 minutes for the other readiness gap or for T from E49.
- **8 min. Co-write the board.** The class reads the big question and co-writes its six supporting questions with the teacher's list: who produces what, why use money, what shapes what sellers make, what rewards and penalties push choices, what banks and other places do with money, how places depend on trade. Each gets the day it is answered (52, 53, 54, 58, 60, 64).
- **10 min. Supporting-question record.** The teacher models one line on Pinecrest library ("How does the library get what it needs?"): needs now (books to lend) or later (money to fix the roof). Each child fills the record on one board question; spoken, drawn or dictated is allowed. The teacher visits a rotating eight.
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** E50.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the board; each child holds the record. Tool: None. By hand: the sort, the record, exit. Evidence: the day 50 record (practice, a cue before day 66), E50.

## Day 51


- **5 min. Return and repair.** V from E50, five-minute form, whole class: sort fresh product and repair pictures.
- **8 min. Trace a bakery.** The teacher traces a bakery (invented) back: bread, flour, wheat from a farm. Three baskets go up: natural (wheat), human (what the bakers know and can do), capital (the oven, the mixer). Flour is said to be made from wheat, so it is traced back, not placed in natural.
- **10 min. Sort the cards.** Pairs sort 12 resource cards from the bakery and the farm into the three baskets. The teacher visits a rotating six. A side table of up to 6 takes whichever of A or U ends up more missed on the sort (five minutes).
- **6 min. A tool is not money.** The teacher sets an oven card and a coin card side by side: one is used to make bread, one is used to pay. Pairs sort six cards into "used to make things" and "used to pay" (A, U).
- **8 min. A service needs resources too.** The teacher works a lawn service (invented): the grass is natural, the owner's skill is human, the mower is capital. Pairs name the three kinds for a fresh service card (L).
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** E51.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the three baskets; pairs hold the cards. Tool: None. By hand: the sorts, exit. Evidence: E51.

## Day 52


- **5 min. Return and repair.** A and U from E51: the more-missed one whole class, five minutes; the other at the side table during Pairs sort.
- **7 min. One workshop, many tasks.** The teacher shows a boat workshop (invented): three workers cut, sand and paint one toy boat. The class says what each does. Division of labor: the team divides the tasks of one product.
- **8 min. Two businesses, one kind of work.** The teacher shows a sail shop that only makes sails and a boat shop that only makes hulls (invented). Specialize: make mostly one kind of thing. Each needs what the other makes. Every worker still uses learned skill, which is a human resource; practice builds knowledge and does not decide anyone's worth.
- **10 min. Pairs sort.** Pairs sort 8 job cards into "inside one workshop" and "between businesses" (S). The teacher visits a rotating six. A side table of up to 6 takes the second letter from E51, five minutes.
- **7 min. Our town street.** On a made-up street picture (Pinecrest Main Street), pairs find two specialist businesses and say what each must get from somewhere else. The teacher hears a rotating six. Ends on the hand-off: the sail shop has sails and needs a hull, so how does it get one? Day 53 gives money.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** E52.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the three baskets and the board; pairs hold the cards. Tool: None. By hand: the sort, the street picture, exit. Evidence: E52.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 49, "Who else needs to know how our school gets paper, water and cleaning, and why?"; day 50, "Which word in our big question could mean two things?"; day 51, "How is an oven different from the money used to buy it?"; day 52, "What does the sail shop need from the boat shop?"
- Written task, day 50: the supporting-question record; spoken, drawn or dictated counts. Practice.
- Look-fors (judged on the record after class and again on day 66): (1) a supporting question from the board; (2) one fact it needs; (3) "needs" read two ways, each with a reason; (4) a link to the big question. Present, partly or missing.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 49 returns who made it, when and why (grade 3, unit 1) and matters to others. Day 50 returns grade 1 choices and saving and grade 2 resources and learned skill. Day 51 returns goods and services from day 50. Day 52 returns the three baskets.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: need, supplier, good, service, resource, capital, divide, specialize. Frames: "The ___ is a ___ resource because ___." "___ needs this answer because ___." Accept home language, drawing with dictation or a pointed card.
- **Access.** Read aloud, enlarge type, real objects for the baskets, speech, pointing or dictation, extra processing time inside an existing block. Score reasoning, not handwriting.
- **Check accommodations.** Read aloud once; a scribe writes the child's words; pointing at the baskets counts.
- **Reading load.** The teacher reads every card and prompt; printed pages at most 350 words, no sentence over 15 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Trace one capital resource back to the natural resource it was made from (an oven to clay or metal) and say who learned how to make it.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** The adult reads the made-up cards and the child traces who supplies paper at school using the School Needs Record copy or a made-up one. Sort the three baskets with the cards, not the home's own tools or jobs. Do not ask the child about the family's work or money.
- **One projector.** Show the board, the record and the cards enlarged; children use paper copies.
