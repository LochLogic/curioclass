# Section 2.6: Our answer to "Who should make the rules in our town?"

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-3/2/s/2.6), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-3 u02-6.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 3 children are early readers and writers. Printed stems are short and plain, no sentence over 15 words; the teacher reads every card, source and prompt aloud once. An answer may be a sentence, a labeled drawing, a filled table or spoken at the side table. Each day runs 40 minutes. Teaching cases are invented and said aloud to be made up (Brindle; redo cards: Wexley, Garnet Club, Class Pet, Club Plan); exit-only places are Fennmoor, Ferndale and Cedarbank (the dock, the ice rink, the dog park). The unit's own records are real: the Local Government Record (teaching edition), the Class Decision Record and the children's records.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **D3.4.3-5, this section's share.** "Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question." Parts: use evidence (days 44, 45); develop a claim (45). One claim, one or two details, from the unit's own records. Day 47's Q8 asks it unaided.
- **D1.4.3-5, this section's share.** "Explain how answering supporting questions helps answer the big question of an inquiry." Day 44: one supporting answer and how it helps (the link), recorded and revised on day 46.
- **D4.1.3-5, this section's share.** "Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources." Day 45: a claim naming two sources, each adding a different detail.
- **Day 47 and 48.** Every other code above is asked once on day 47, from the unit plan's table or a revised record (field 3 and unit plan field 5); the exact verbs are in the lines above. Day 46 revises the records. Nothing new is taught after day 45.
- **Limits that bite here.** Elected leaders, a direct vote or a supported balance can all be defended; the claim needs a detail, not a liking. No party preference; no real officeholder is judged. The Local record has a maker, a date and a link. The Class Decision Record has no names. The day 47 sources are not the ones taught.
- **Connections.** Bridge for ELA grade 3 W.3.1 (its unit 3, days 81 to 119, later): the claim is spoken first, then one written sentence; this course does not assume that ELA unit. The norms card from day 38 hears each claim (restate first). The question board's six answers (days 27, 32, 34, 37, 40, 43) are the evidence list. Unit 3's readiness check (days.csv day 48) uses grade 1 choices and saving; it is not taught here.
- **Review, not new (the ledger's words).** Claim, evidence, because, a named source and its maker (review of social-studies-grade-2 u03-4); closing claim and reasoning; the claim frame "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___" (review of social-studies-grade-3 u01-6, shown again on day 44 and withdrawn on day 45); big question, supporting question and matters to others (u01-1); who made it, when and why (u01-5); the question board, Return and repair, written cue then revision, exit ticket (u02-3); the norms and restating (review of u02-4).
- **New here.** Link (how a supporting answer helps the big question); people a rule touches (the people who use or follow it); final say (the person or group that makes the decision); public voice (people telling leaders what they think).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A supporting answer is not the final answer; it earns its place by helping. A title is not evidence; a detail is (J). Final say belongs to those who are allowed to decide; public voice tells them what people need, and speaking is not voting (K). How to give a written cue on eight records for thirty children, and how to mark day 47: about three minutes a child after class (ours, untested), so about ninety minutes.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** Q8 (a claim from two sources, D3.4 and D4.1) and the day 44 inquiry record (D1.4), asked or judged on day 47. The whole of day 47 (eight unaided prompts, the packet and the record rows) is in unit plan field 5; this section owns how it runs and how it is answered. The day 44 record is practice, with a written cue before day 46.

**Exit questions.** Each is a quarter page read aloud once, marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. If many miss, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E44 (Cedarbank Card, invented for practice).** "Cedarbank Card, invented. How does this supporting answer help the big question?" Answer: It tells who uses the dock, so deciders know whose needs to hear. Wrong: "The dock has many boards." (W); "I like the dock, so children should decide." (V).
- **E45 (Rule Exit Pair, invented for practice).** "Write one sentence: who decides the rink's rules? Use a detail from each card." Answer: The council decides, as Card A says, after hearing residents. Card B shows a need: the door is too narrow for wheelchairs. Wrong: "Card A and Card B say it." (J); "I think the mayor, because I like him." (V).
- **E46 (Ferndale Card, invented for practice).** "Ferndale Card, invented. Who has the final say on the dog park?" Answer: The council, which votes. Neighbors speak to it. Wrong: "Everyone who spoke." (K).

**Day 44 record (practice).** "A supporting question: ___. A record that answers it: ___. The answer: ___. This helps decide who should make town rules because ___." Look-fors: a supporting question from the board; a named record and its answer; a clear link. **Day 45 claim (practice).** One written sentence on the frame, naming the Local record and the Class record, each with a detail. **Day 47 (check).** Eight prompts, 30 minutes: Q1 4, Q2 4, Q3 3, Q4 3, Q5 3, Q6 3, Q7 3, Q8 7 (unit plan field 5). **Day 48 fresh redo (one item for each group, new cases, not on day 47; not scored on the record sheet).**
- **Authority and funding.** "In Wexley, the council decided to charge a small fee to use the roller rink. The mayor's staff open it each morning. Who decided to charge the fee? Who pays it?" Answer: the council; the people who use the rink (the daily running is a separate ask in the data). Wrong: "The mayor decided everything" (A).
- **Rights and taking part.** "A club, the Garnet Club, says only members over twelve may speak at meetings. Does this protect everyone's freedom to speak? What could change?" Answer: no; let every member speak, each with the duty to wait a turn. Wrong: "Only grown-ups can take part" (D).
- **Deciding.** "Two children disagree about a class pet's name. One says the other's idea is silly. What should that child do next? How should the class decide?" Answer: say the other's reason back, give their own, then one equal, private ballot each. Wrong: "The loudest child's name wins" (F).
- **Constitutions and claims.** "A made-up club plan says: members may speak at meetings; the president may not spend over ten dollars alone. Name one power and one limit." Answer: members may speak (power or right); the president may not spend over ten dollars alone (limit). Wrong: "The club can do anything it wants" (AB).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: question, claim, evidence, record, link, final say, public voice, council, vote. Frames: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." "This helps because ___." Accept home language, drawing with dictation or a pointed card.
- **Access.** Read aloud, enlarge type, accept pointing, speech or dictation, and allow extra processing time inside an existing work block. Sources stay available; worked answers do not. Score reasoning, not handwriting or English fluency.
- **Check accommodations.** Read aloud once; a scribe writes the child's words; no spelling or handwriting marks.
- **Reading load.** The teacher reads every record and prompt; printed pages at most 350 words, no sentence over 15 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Compare two defensible balances of deciders and public voice, each with a detail.

On day 47 the adult reads the packet and prompts aloud and records the child's answers; the Class record is the home's own two-person decision record. On day 48, go over the missed items together and redo one orally before the readiness items.
