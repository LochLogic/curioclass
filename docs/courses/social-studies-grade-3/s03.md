**Teacher notes:** human capital means learned knowledge and skills, not a person's worth. A person can produce and consume. Physical capital is equipment used in production, not money. Wheat is a natural resource; flour is a processed material. Keep the day 51 bakery example, but trace flour to wheat rather than calling flour an unprocessed natural resource. Specialization between producers differs from division of tasks within one workplace. Practising skills at school can develop knowledge without determining anyone's future worth or earnings.

Profit is sales income left after costs, not a moral measure of a person. Profit expectations can shape products and prices but do not guarantee sales. Scarcity applies to time and resources in wealthy and poor places. Poverty is not proof of poor choices; access, pay, costs and events can limit options. Use fictional amounts only. Never ask pupils to disclose accounts, income, debts, purchases or insurance, or to act as representatives of their families.

Banks may pay interest on savings and charge it on loans; not every account pays interest and terms differ. Charging borrowers more than the bank pays savers can contribute to earnings, before other costs. A credit union belongs to members. Insurance pays losses covered by its agreement, not every expense. The class model omits fees and uses one fixed year; no real offer is recommended. Grade 3 compares dollars on each $100 without percentage notation. Teach the arithmetic here if its parallel math instruction is not yet available. On 54, compare a price customers will pay with an unsold higher-priced offering. On 62, connect available goods and buyers' orders to possible price choices, without graphs or a claim that sellers can charge anything.

On 54, Maggie Lena Walker's Richmond business decisions are a worked biography, not a claim that she is local to every class. The own-place packet replaces it with a documented local owner's actions where needed. Name racist exclusion plainly in the Walker context: Black residents faced unfair barriers, and Walker helped build banking services and opportunities. Do not imply profit was her only purpose or invent her prices. No segregation reenactment, identity assignment or religious performance. Her disability is not needed for this short economic account; any later inclusion needs appropriate study rather than an overcoming-disability story.

On 63 to 65, name Techiman as a present-day urban market center, not a timeless village. A dated report supports only its period and observations. Present Ghana is not the medieval empire called Ghana, which lay farther northwest. Show traders, producers, transport workers and buyers with agency. Include Jennifer Owusu-Mensah's account of customers alongside Kusi Appiah's concerns about trading conditions, using only what the Daily Graphic article of 24 June 2023 reports (her words are quoted there; his concerns about waste, food sold on bare ground, broken streetlights and security are reported, not quoted). Compare the assembly's later response without deciding that all traders agree. Do not rank countries as advanced or backward, assume universal barter, assign ethnic occupations or ask a Ghanaian child to authenticate the account. Different emphases about local production and trade need evidence, not a vote on facts. No Since Time Immemorial content is used.
