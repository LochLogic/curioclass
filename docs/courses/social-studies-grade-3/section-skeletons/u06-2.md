## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.3.T6.4** (days 128, 129): Explain that the new nation wrote the Constitution as a plan of government, that the Bill of Rights spells out citizens' rights, and that full citizenship was then limited, with voting set by each state and in most states held by white male property owners over 21 (New Jersey's voters included some women and free Black men until 1807).
- **D2.Civ.5.3-5** (days 128): Explain where different systems of government came from, what they do and how they are built, including those in the U.S. and state constitutions.
