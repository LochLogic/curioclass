## 1. Standards and practices

- **MA.3.T2.3c** (days 76): Explain how physical features influenced where traditional settlements were located.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No key tool, spec or TOOL-ASKS.md needed. Print every map, source and task. Gather pencils, paper location markers, pattern-coded maps, source cards, enlarged copies, tactile markers, the day 80 schematic. One projector enlarges those sheets.
