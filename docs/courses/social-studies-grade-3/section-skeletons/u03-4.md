## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Paper records, maps and counters fit the jobs; no spec or TOOL-ASKS.md needed. Print every source and task. Gather pencils, record sheets, world and West Africa locator maps, source cards, origin-label cards, two ledger sheets per child and ten counters per pair. One projector enlarges the same sheets.
>
> Teach resource, profit, opportunity cost, interest and interdependence with pictures. Frame: "The source shows __, so __." Read aloud grade 2 to 3 summaries; independent reading stays to short captions. Accept speech and dictation. Extend by defending a different supported choice. At home the adult reads and takes the partner's role.
