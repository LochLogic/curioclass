## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 133, 135): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 133, 135): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **D2.His.1.3-5** (days 134): Make and use a time-ordered list of related events to compare developments that happened at the same time.
- **MA.3.T6.3** (days 135): Analyze how colonists' sense of injustice led them to declare independence, and what the Declaration of Independence shows about its writers' beliefs.
- **MA.3.T6.4** (days 135): Explain that the new nation wrote the Constitution as a plan of government, that the Bill of Rights spells out citizens' rights, and that full citizenship was then limited, with voting set by each state and in most states held by white male property owners over 21 (New Jersey's voters included some women and free Black men until 1807).
- **D2.Civ.8.3-5** (days 135): Identify the main civic virtues and democratic principles that guide government, society and communities.
- **D2.Civ.5.3-5** (days 135): Explain where different systems of government came from, what they do and how they are built, including those in the U.S. and state constitutions.
- skill ss3-claim-evidence (grade 3)
- skill ss3-founding-ideas (grade 3)
- skill ss3-timeline (grade 3)
