## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 86, 88): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 86, 88): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.3.T2.3** (days 88): Explain the diversity of Native Peoples, past and present, in Massachusetts and New England.
- **MA.3.T2.3a** (days 88): Name at least three Native groups of the region, such as the Massachusett, Narragansett, Nipmuc, and Wampanoag.
- **MA.3.T2.3b** (days 88): Locate the tribal territories within Massachusetts.
- **MA.3.T2.3c** (days 88): Explain how physical features influenced where traditional settlements were located.
- **MA.3.T2.3d** (days 88): Describe contributions of a tribal group near the school, such as foods, crafts, words, place names, and useful knowledge.
- **D2.Geo.6.3-5** (days 88): Describe how environmental and cultural features affect where people live in particular places or regions.
- **D2.His.12.3-5** (days 88): Ask questions about several sources and how they relate to particular events and developments.
- skill ss3-claim-evidence (grade 3)
- skill ss3-first-nations-region (grade 3)
- skill ss3-primary-secondary (grade 3)

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No key tool, spec or TOOL-ASKS.md needed. Print every map, source and task. Gather pencils, paper location markers, pattern-coded maps, source cards, enlarged copies, tactile markers, the day 80 schematic. One projector enlarges those sheets.
>
> Teach nation, homeland, sovereignty, primary, secondary and evidence with pictures and examples. Frame: "The source shows __, so __."
