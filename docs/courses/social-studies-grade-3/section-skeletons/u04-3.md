## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.3.T2.3** (days 77): Explain the diversity of Native Peoples, past and present, in Massachusetts and New England.
- **MA.3.T2.3a** (days 77, 78, 79, 80, 81): Name at least three Native groups of the region, such as the Massachusett, Narragansett, Nipmuc, and Wampanoag.
- **MA.3.T2.3c** (days 77, 79, 80, 81, 82): Explain how physical features influenced where traditional settlements were located.
- **MA.3.T2.3b** (days 78): Locate the tribal territories within Massachusetts.
- **D2.Geo.6.3-5** (days 82): Describe how environmental and cultural features affect where people live in particular places or regions.
- skill ss3-first-nations-region (grade 3)
