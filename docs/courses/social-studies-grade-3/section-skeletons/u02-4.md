Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Printed role charts, maps, source cards and ballots fit the jobs; no spec or TOOL-ASKS.md needed. Gather pencils, counters, paper, a ballot box and ballot slips. Print every source and task; one projector only enlarges them.
>
