## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Paper maps, source cards, timeline strips, interview sheets, notes frames and action records fit; no tool asks. Supply pencils, rulers, scissors, tape, markers, index cards and reused paper. The supplied interview is a transcript. Print every source and task. One projector enlarges them; at home an adult reads and joins deliberation. No student timers. Read adult originals aloud beside original grade 2 to 3 explanations. Use large print, image descriptions, a scribe and translated vocabulary. Extend by testing another source against a claim.
>
> **Contribution:** on 154 students nominate problems from project evidence. The teacher screens options for completion in day 156's forty minutes using existing materials, then students weigh two or three feasible approaches. On 155 hear minority reasons before compromise and a vote. On 156 assign accessible roles and make the chosen contribution. No travel, fundraising, hazardous cleanup or required public contact (ours, untested). Observe immediate use on 157; record longer effects as unknown. At home, deliberate with the adult and share with another family adult.
