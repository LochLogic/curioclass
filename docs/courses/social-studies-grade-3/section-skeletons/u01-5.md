Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: none.** A globe, printed maps and evidence cards do every job; no TOOL-ASKS.md entry. Supplies: a globe, world, physical and political maps, the local map pair (a sourced worked pair stands in on days 18 and 21 if no historical map exists), a satellite view, outline maps, grids, photographs, the Landmark Exit Caption, source cards, answer overlays, tactile copies, string, rulers, pencils.
