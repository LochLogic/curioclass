Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Hook, day 72:** compare a local physical map with the local nation's account of its homeland and government today. Ask what each reveals about who shapes this place.
> - **Co-write on 72:** Whose homelands are here (75)? Why settle here (82)? What contributions continue (83)? Who governs today (84)? How can sources help us know (85)?
> - **Teaching:** model sourcing on 73, settlement reasoning on 76 and corroboration on 85. On 75 locate the edition's three nations on teaching maps; compare again on 82 (field 5). On 86 students select evidence, hear another supported emphasis and revise.
>
> - **Models:** retain unit 3's claim-evidence-reasoning; use unit 2's perspective on 86, then withdraw it. Return to map-key on 75; revise region on 82 because homelands cross state boundaries. Tie source-sourcing to maker, date and question on 73; tie corroboration to agreement and difference on 85. Withdraw filled organizers on 87; retain the blank sentence frame until unit 5. Before-and-after cards prepare the timeline on 85.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No key tool, spec or TOOL-ASKS.md needed. Print every map, source and task. Gather pencils, paper location markers, pattern-coded maps, source cards, enlarged copies, tactile markers, the day 80 schematic. One projector enlarges those sheets.
>
> Teach nation, homeland, sovereignty, primary, secondary and evidence with pictures and examples. Frame: "The source shows __, so __." Read sources aloud; independent reading stays to grade 2 to 3 captions. Accept speech and labelled pointing. Extend by testing a different supported claim. At home the adult takes the partner's role.
