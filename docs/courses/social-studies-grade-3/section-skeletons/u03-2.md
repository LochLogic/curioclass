Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Paper records, maps and counters fit the jobs; no spec or TOOL-ASKS.md needed. Print every source and task. Gather pencils, record sheets, world and West Africa locator maps, source cards, origin-label cards, two ledger sheets per child and ten counters per pair. One projector enlarges the same sheets.

Read aloud grade 2 to 3 summaries; independent reading stays to short captions. Accept speech and dictation. Extend by defending a different supported choice.
