## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What does it mean that government needs the consent of the governed?"
- **Hook, day 125:** show the Declaration's title and read its consent phrase beside an original explanation. Ask whose agreement a government needs.
- **Co-write on 125:** Why independence (126)? What did the writers believe (127)? What makes a government plan (128)? What rights were listed (129)? Whose voices counted (130 to 131)? What do symbols promise (132)?
- **Closing, day 133:** answer the compelling question from the Declaration and Constitution, checked against the Black freedom petition. Defend what consent requires and explain a gap between promise and practice; hear another supported answer before revising.
- **Teaching:** model maker, date, audience and purpose on 126; connect evidence to a claim on 127. Compare constitutions on 128; step back on 133. Teach independence, consent, constitution, right, citizenship and symbol in context. Day 132 gives twenty minutes to five brief symbol cards, with discussion and an exit ticket afterward.
