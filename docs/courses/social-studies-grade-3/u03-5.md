# Section 3.5: Our answer, a closing record and the assessment

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-3/3/s/3.5), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-3 u03-5.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 3 children are early readers and writers. Printed stems are short and plain, no sentence over 15 words; the teacher reads every card, source and prompt aloud once. An answer may be a sentence, a labeled drawing, a filled table or spoken at the side table. Each day runs 40 minutes. Day 65 writes one claim sentence; day 66 revises the inquiry records and the savings ledger; day 67 is the unit assessment, read aloud; day 68 returns feedback and reteaches in groups. Teaching cases are the ones already used in the unit; the exit case (a greengrocer and a bus depot) and the redo bank are invented and said aloud to be made up. Exits are marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **D3.4.3-5, this section's share.** "Use evidence to develop an answer to the inquiry." Part: develop a claim (day 65): one sentence from the frame, with a detail from each of the two records.
- **D3.1 and D3.2 revision, day 66 and the assessment.** Revise the day 49 and 50 inquiry records and the savings ledger; the assessment judges D2.Eco.10, D1.1 and D1.2 from the revised records.
- **Limits that bite here.** The answer is about two sources: our town's Local Production Record and the Techiman Traders Record. One market is not all of Ghana; a dated report supports only its period; no ranking of places; no percent. The claim may stress two supported things, not one winner. Nothing on the assessment names the family, origin or work of any child. The sources are labeled "source to check" until the grade 3 sources file exists.
- **Connections.** The claim frame comes from unit 1 and unit 2 and is withdrawn on day 65 (read once, then taken down). The savings ledger closes on day 66 with the fourth $20 (Kept $80). Day 64's tables feed the sentence.
- **Review, not new (the ledger's words).** Claim, evidence, because and the claim frame (review of social-studies-grade-3 u02-6); shows, suggests, cannot tell (u01-3); the class savings ledger and interest (u03-2, u03-3); opportunity cost, incentives, goods and services, trade (this unit); grouped reteach and the assessment packet read aloud (u02-6 days 47 and 48).
- **New here.** None. Written cue then revision is a routine, not a term.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The assessment has eight prompts; each is read aloud once and measured against one component. The redo bank has one fresh item per component, so day 68 tests the same move on new content.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The claim frame is read once on day 65 and taken down. Cues written on days 49, 50 and 60 are read on day 66. The savings ledger closes on day 66. The assessment packet is read aloud on day 67.
- **Key moment, day 67: the assessment.** Ideal instrument: a printed packet with one prompt per page. Spec: print fits.
- **A room with nothing.** The teacher reads each prompt aloud; children answer on paper or at the side table.

## 6. Days

## Day 65


- **5 min. Return and repair.** The more-missed of J and K (from E64), five-minute form, whole class.
- **4 min. The frame, read once.** The teacher reads "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___" once, then takes the card down. No teacher example.
- **8 min. Say it first.** Pairs say their claim aloud twice using the day 64 tables. The teacher hears a rotating six pairs.
- **10 min. Write one sentence.** Each child writes one sentence with a detail from each record, or dictates it.
- **6 min. Share and revise.** Pairs read their sentence, name the detail from each record, and revise one word.
- **4 min. Our answer.** The class names two supported emphases for the big question; one market is not all of Ghana.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** E65.

Who holds what is carried: children hold the tables and a pencil; the teacher holds no example. Tool: None. By hand: the sentence, exit. Evidence: the sentence, E65.

## Day 66


- **5 min. Cues.** Children read the cues they wrote on days 49, 50 and 60.
- **10 min. Inquiry revisions.** Each child revises the day 49 and day 50 records with what the unit taught, in a different color.
- **5 min. A fresh savings try.** A made-up ledger: four $15 deposits toward $60; then two offers, $8 and $5 on each $100. Children say which offer gives more and why.
- **5 min. Teacher written check.** The teacher checks three look-fors: ledger closed; interest meaning; amount and time compared.
- **10 min. Independent savings revision.** Each child revises their own class savings ledger entry and interest sentence.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** The final $20 goes in: Kept $80 against the $80 goal; then the review list read aloud.

There is no exit ticket. Who holds what is carried: each child holds a ledger and the records. Tool: None. By hand: the revisions. Evidence: the revised records and ledger.

## Day 67


- **5 min. Packet.** The teacher hands out the packet and reads the directions.
- **30 min. Eight unaided prompts.** Q1 3, Q2 4, Q3 3, Q4 3, Q5 4, Q6 4, Q7 3, Q8 6 minutes. Each prompt is read aloud once.
- **5 min. Reread and Q3 marking.** Children reread their answers; the teacher marks Q3 with the class.

D2.Eco.10, D1.1 and D1.2 are judged from the day 66 revised records. Tool: None. By hand: the packet. Evidence: the packet.

## Day 68


- **5 min. Feedback.** Each child gets a short written note and a letter if there is one.
- **25 min. Grouped reteach.** Four groups of at most eight, six minutes each, one minute to move. Groups form by the component to move table.
- **10 min. Fresh redo.** Each child does one new item from the redo bank on the component they missed.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the groups and the bank. Tool: None. By hand: the redo. Evidence: the redo.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 65 returns day 64's tables and the claim frame. Day 66 returns the cues from days 49, 50 and 60 and the class ledger. Day 67 returns the whole unit, unaided. Day 68 returns the packet feedback.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: claim, because, detail, interest, deposit. Frames: "The ___ shows ___." Accept home language, drawing with dictation or a pointed card.
- **Access.** Read aloud, enlarge print, speech or dictation, extra processing time inside an existing block. Score reasoning, not handwriting.
- **Check accommodations.** Read aloud once; a scribe; pointing counts.
- **Reading load.** The teacher reads every prompt aloud; printed pages at most 350 words, no sentence over 15 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write a second sentence naming what the sources cannot tell.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** The adult reads the packet aloud and the child answers on paper; no group is needed, and the redo is the child's own. Do not ask about the family's own savings or work.
- **A parent on days 67 and 68.** A parent may sit in; nothing on the packet names the family, origin or work of any child.
- **One projector.** Show the prompts one at a time; children use paper copies.
