# Section 3.5: Our answer, a closing record and the assessment

<!-- skel -->
Grade 3: Communities Then and Now. Unit 3, Making a living here (days 49 to 68): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 65 to 68.** Days by kind: fluency 1 (66), application 1 (65), check 1 (67), respond 1 (68). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Compelling question (frame): "How does our community get what it needs?"
- Section 5 of 5 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 40 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Grade 3 children are early readers and writers. Printed stems are short and plain, no sentence over 15 words; the teacher reads every card, source and prompt aloud once. An answer may be a sentence, a labelled drawing, a filled table or spoken at the side table. Each day runs 40 minutes. Day 65 writes one claim sentence; day 66 revises the inquiry records and the savings ledger; day 67 is the unit assessment, read aloud; day 68 returns feedback and reteaches in groups. Teaching cases are the ones already used in the unit; the exit case (a greengrocer and a bus depot) and the redo bank are invented and said aloud to be made up. Exits are marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: claim, because, detail, interest, deposit. Frames: "The ___ shows ___." Accept home language, drawing with dictation or a pointed card.
- **Access.** Read aloud, enlarge print, speech or dictation, extra processing time inside an existing block. Score reasoning, not handwriting.
- **Check accommodations.** Read aloud once; a scribe; pointing counts.
- **Reading load.** The teacher reads every prompt aloud; printed pages at most 350 words, no sentence over 15 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write a second sentence naming what the sources cannot tell.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** The adult reads the packet aloud and the child answers on paper; no group is needed, and the redo is the child's own. Do not ask about the family's own savings or work.
- **A parent on days 67 and 68.** A parent may sit in; nothing on the packet names the family, origin or work of any child.
- **One projector.** Show the prompts one at a time; children use paper copies.
