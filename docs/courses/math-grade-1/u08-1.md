## Day 151

**Fluency.** I can use what I know to add within ten.

- **5 min. Hook.** Return outstanding papers. Offer familiar 2 + 5 = 7 and 8 + 1 = 9 cards for a private easy-or-still-working mark. Ask “What do we know now about numbers and stories?”
- **5 min. Warm-up.** Retrieve 6 + 3 = 9 and 8 − 3 = 5. Point to one ten on a drawing. Each child marks the retrieval, and partners share which fact they knew.
- **10 min. Strategy review.** Hold five and add two. Place both new marks outside the cover. Connect the frame to 5 + 2 = 7 and show the A contrast. Show the sign + as putting groups together, and = as the same amount on both sides. Name the completed number sentence a fact.
- **30 min. Practice.** Play Partner turns with two-group cards. Mark a frame, turn over a handwritten key, then retry. Select the deck from the response notes. For all three days, keep the exit groupings, helper pictures and removal story out of practice, repairs and warm-ups.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Partners point to the held group. Compare two brief examples with the class.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E151. Shape: make one fact from a three-group flap picture. Keep this stock out of two-group rounds.

## Day 152

**Fluency.** I can use addition to help me subtract.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E151. Mix both signs and a part-whole sketch.
- **10 min. Related facts.** Lift five from eight and see three left. Restore five and three. Revoice 8 − 5 = 3 and 5 + 3 = 8.
- **35 min. Practice.** Partners match completed fact cards and check with individual frame marks. Hold ten for A and a separate ten for a distinct 151 retrieval miss. Classmates continue matching and checking while the teacher repairs.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Ask which addition undoes the modeled removal. Two brief reasons, then all point to both restored parts.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E152. Shape: restore two separately moved parts and the covered remainder. Keep the cup-and-bag picture out of matching.

## Day 153

**Fluency.** I can choose known facts to solve within twenty.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E152. Mix both signs and a tens sketch.
- **10 min. Strategy review.** Start with 7 + 4 + 3. Switch the four and three, then group the seven and three: (7 + 3) + 4 = 14. Point to each unchanged group. Show B with separate stock. Use a matching addition to check a removal.
- **35 min. Practice.** Sort completed workings by strategy. Rebuild one already-marked route, including a subtraction that steps back to ten or uses related addition. Give ten minutes to lingering A if needed.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Point to where each piece ended up. Cover the shared examples.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E153a, then E153b. Shape: choose a known fact for the remaining dots and a count for the two-row removal. Read one slip at a time. Return both on 154.

## 7. Talk and writing

152: “Which addition undoes this removal?” 153: “Where did each piece go?” In 153 practice children mark a completed route with its known fact. Look for a true equation and each addend used once. Hear up to two 45-second examples in synthesis, with parallel marks from the rest.

## 8. Practice that returns

Five-minute warm-ups mix addition and subtraction within 10 plus one earlier place-value or story idea. The within-10 fluency target uses spaced rounds, immediate private feedback and retries. Within 20, children select a familiar strategy. Review Kindergarten's Partner turns (1.1), one handles or counts while one watches, then switch. Review partner of ten (5.3), the other part that completes ten, and the paired frames from Kindergarten 6.2, where all ten ones stay visible. Grade 1 sections 1.1, 3.1, 3.4, 3.6, 5.1 and 5.3 are planned only. Show the covered start and the partner check and retry on 151. On 152 put both parts back to recover the starting group. Use actual pieces before the cards.

## 9. Supports and extension

On 151 point out a fact as a true number relationship and a number sentence as numbers and signs telling that relationship. Review equals, “is the same quantity as,” from Kindergarten 4.4. Name the sum as the total and the double as a pair of equal groups. To count on, start with the number already known and name the next number for each new piece. On 153 point to a helpful pair that is easy to put together first. To add on, keep one group fixed and bring in the other a piece at a time or in chunks. “I know ___, so ___.” Read aloud, offer tactile marks, pointing or neutral dictation, and keep the same cue and quantities. Extend by comparing two helpers on practice stock.

## 10. Two rooms

At one table the adult and child switch roles, then the child marks the exit independently. At home, read the cue aloud and let the child say or point to the fact. A projector may enlarge practice. Each child still works on paper.
