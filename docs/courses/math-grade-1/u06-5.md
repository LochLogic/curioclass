Grade 1 Math. Unit 6, Length to 120 units (days 111 to 128), see [s06.md](s06.md). **Days 126 to 128.** Days by kind: fluency 1, application 1, check 1. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.MD.A.1.** “Order” three lengths and “compare” indirectly using the same third length. Complete L on 126.
- **1.MD.A.2.** “Express” length in whole-number units and “understand” what touching equal units measure. Collect M on 127 and corroborate it with F on 128.
- **1.NBT.A.1.** “Count to 120,” “read and write numerals” and “represent” collections. Complete N on 126 and 127.
- **1.OA.A.1.** “Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems.” Complete the twelve cells and six Compare variants. Keep supported start-unknown and opposite-language evidence descriptive. MP.4, 127: draw matching bars and place the difference and unknown.
- **Teacher explanation.** A measurement has a unit size and count. A bar represents the whole compared length and shows which part is the difference. Match each retained original to written feedback and a later revision before closing its judgment.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned.** F, the single fresh transfer on 128, [unit field 5](s06.md). This section also collects L, M, N and S10 to S15. Use sections [6.1](u06-1.md), [6.2](u06-2.md), [6.3](u06-3.md) and [6.4](u06-4.md) for their prompts, keys and reservations. IDs below are planned Data ids. Read each stem aloud. Every child marks paper independently. Print story slips “invented for practice.”

- **E126a (s06-X126a), 1.NBT.A.1.** Show unnumbered loose stock at a paper supply desk: eleven tied tens with visible unit marks and three singles. “What numeral belongs on the tag?” Key: mark the groups and write 113. Wrong: 114 from counting a single twice (G). 123 from counting a tied group twice (G).
  - Shape: reconstruct the count from a loose stock picture with its tag missing. Practice adds a bundle to a known quantity. Hold this picture apart from N's three inventories. Data id: s06-X126a.
- **E126b (s06-X126b), 1.MD.A.2.** Give an unmarked strip exactly four identical units long and extra equal pieces. “How would you mark this strip so someone can tell its length?” Key: four equal intervals meeting end to end and spanning the strip. Wrong: three with spaces (C). Five overlapping (D).
  - Shape: make interval marks from scratch. Practice moves one supplied faulty joint. Reserve this span and its stock. Data id: s06-X126b.
- **E127a (s06-X127a), 1.MD.A.2.** Give a straight unmarked paper puppy path exactly nineteen equal units long. Supply visibly segmented tens and loose singles of that same unit, with spare stock. “What length belongs on the puppy's path tag?” Key: 19 units, for example one strip of ten units and nine singles touching and covering the path. Wrong: 18 with open spaces (C). 20 with an overlap (D).
  - Shape: label a path by accounting for grouped and single units. Practice uses stated lengths. M uses a rectangle and loose single units. Reserve the path and stock. Data id: s06-X127a.
- **E127b (s06-X127b), 1.OA.A.1.** Collect the measurement slip. Give everyone the actual length as a new given and supply a blank bar mat. Show a separate row of two two-unit pieces and one three-unit piece, with the unit marks visible. “The puppy path is nineteen units long. The plant path is shorter by the length of this row. How would your bars show the plant path?” Key: the difference is 2 + 2 + 3 = 7. The bars of 19 and 12 share a start. The unknown spans the shorter bar, and 19 − 7 = 12. Wrong: 7 as the whole shorter bar (J). Draws the shorter bar as 19 with a separate 7 (J).
  - Shape: combine three difference pieces and create a missing bar. Practice reasons from stated differences. Reserve the three-piece difference and the paired paths. Data id: s06-X127b.

**F on 128, 1.MD.A.2 and 1.OA.A.1.** Supply two unnumbered strips exactly eleven and sixteen of the actual paper units long, with identical units to lay on them. Read each of the three items separately and use separate paper response spaces. Use two minutes, three minutes and three minutes respectively, including cues. Set aside the first two answers before reading both true lengths aloud for the extension. The child marks boundaries or draws from their arrangement.

- **F-short (s06-AF-short).** “What length belongs on the short strip's tag?” Key: 11 units with eleven touching equal lengths. Wrong: ten pieces with spaces (C).
  - Shape: independently measure the shorter member of a pair before comparing. Reserve both strips. Practice repairs a supplied seam on unrelated objects. Data id: s06-AF-short.
- **F-long (s06-AF-long).** “What length belongs on the long strip's tag?” Key: 16 units with sixteen touching equal lengths. Wrong: 17 with overlap (D).
  - Shape: measure the longer member independently and leave an inspectable record. Reserve its span and stock. Practice uses already counted pieces or other stock. Data id: s06-AF-long.
- **F-extend (s06-AF-extend).** “How many more units does the short strip need to reach the end of the long strip?” Key: 5. Mark the extension between aligned strips and connect it to 11 + 5 = 16 or 16 − 11 = 5. Wrong: 16 as the added part (J).
  - Shape: determine the extension from the two physically measured lengths, with a model linked to an equation. Reserve this strip pair, marks and question. Practice places given lengths into other bars or checks supplied labels. Data id: s06-AF-extend.

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. On 128, L/M/N/S are retained work. Missing components stay open. Any oral revision is on paper with the original observation attached.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Dated originals, written feedback and later revisions stay together in the private folder.

## Day 126

**Fluency.** I can check counts and measurements and keep a record.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Return E125. Use this opening for one priority repair if needed. Otherwise mix familiar facts. Children keep their own blank bars. Read N's paper prompts at the start of the inventory block.
- **15 min. Story record.** S10 to S15, six separate read-aloud prompts at two and a half minutes each. All mark independently.
- **16 min. Length record.** Run the six-child group (25 to 30) first, then 17 to 24, eight minutes each. In minute one read the three short N paper cues and the two L prompts aloud with each card in view. All except the first six start N in this first eight-minute block. The six begin L on their own mats, then each gets one private minute. In the second block those six do N while 17 to 24 take L. Hear each child show a comparison, starting with the physical placement if their drawing is unfinished. Children finish their own traces. Log 19 to 24's L turns. Each N block lasts eight minutes including the oral cues. Finish the three written labels on each child's own paper.
- **15 min. Number record.** N for children 1 to 15, one minute each. Others check familiar arrangements, add one bundle to a known count, or finish L drawings. Their own tape diagrams (light) give feedback on the E125 practice comparison for up to five minutes. Give five minutes of E125 paper repair if routed, before or after each child's scheduled N turn.
- **4 min. Revision.** Return early L originals with written feedback for independent revision.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E126a/b, separate slips. Return on 127. Review late L, N and S work after class and give written feedback.

## Day 127

**Application.** I can show a measurement and model an invented story.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mix familiar facts and return E126 and late L/N/S feedback. Open the priority E126 repair when needed.
- **3 min. Launch.** Say the stories are invented. Read the four short M prompts with their picture cues. Let the first group start placing units. Read the two practice cards aloud: “Rin adds two paper leaves to six. How many are there now?” and “A paper fox trail is nine units long. A paper hare trail is two units shorter. How long is the hare trail?” The teacher key gives eight leaves and seven units. Children choose one scene to draw. Keep the reserved path covered.
- **32 min. Measurement record.** M with four eight-minute groups of 8, 8, 8 and 6. Group members lay and draw concurrently. Hear one child each minute privately on their arrangement. Use paper for the validity judgments. Peers draw on blank story mats or independently revise. Set five minutes of paper repair outside each child's eight-minute M group when routed. For the combined M and N turn day, serve the six-child M group first if extra setup time is needed.
- **15 min. Number record.** N for 16 to 30, one minute each. Reuse N's written inventory from 126. Log 25 to 30 without a second interview. Others use their own blank bars and feedback, tape diagram (light), practice with feedback up to 15 minutes.
- **5 min. Close.** E127a/b, about two and a half minutes each. Read the second given after collecting the first slip. After class give written feedback on M and remaining oral evidence.

## 7. Talk and writing

Ask “Which length does your number name?” and “What does the uncovered part of your bar tell you?” on 127. Children mark one practice bar. Look for matched units, a common start and labels attached to the right quantities.

## 8. Practice that returns

Mix within-ten facts with a familiar place-value or story idea. On 126 rehearse counting and measurement without a timer. Grade 1 section 1.4's Counting observation, half the class a day and Written feedback and one revision in a second color are planned only. Show the turn cue and use a spare practice slip to model dating a later revision. No owned standard sets a new fluency target.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review unit, difference, unknown and tag from the earlier sections with objects. “The difference is ___ units.” Read aloud and use raised joints, large pieces or neutral dictation. Extend in practice by finding a difference in two ways from the same bar, within 20.

## 10. Two rooms

For one child, collect L on 125. On 126 give 15 minutes for S10 to S15, eight for the written N, two for L feedback and revision, and five for the spoken exits. On 127 use three to launch, eight for M, one for oral N, eight for the story, five for revision and five for exits. On 128 give ten minutes for revision, eight for F and five for filing and feedback. Keep the original oral record. With one screen show practice only.
