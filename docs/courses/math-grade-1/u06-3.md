# Section 6.3: Count and record lengths across 100

Grade 1 Math. Unit 6, Length to 120 units (days 111 to 128), see [s06.md](s06.md). **Days 118 to 120.** Days by kind: understanding 1, application 1, fluency 1. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.NBT.A.1.** “Count to 120, starting at any number less than 120,” “read and write numerals” and “represent a number of objects with a written numeral.” Reach 110 on 118 and 120 on 119.
- **Connections.** 1.MD.A.2, “Express” equal whole-unit lengths through 120 on 118 to 120. On 120 maintain 1.NBT.C.4, “Add within 100” and “relate the strategy to a written method.”
- **Strategies and teacher explanation.** Count each loose piece once, then group tens and label the same total. MP.7, day 118: ten tens are a hundred, and a new one changes the ones place. After 109, the next one completes a ten. Ten more than a nineties number changes the tens and hundreds places while its ones stay the same. For addition, combine like units and make a ten where needed.

## Day 118

**Understanding.** I can connect my count to a numeral.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Return E117a/b. Count eighteen paper squares once each and write 18. Start at 78 and continue through 82. Use a familiar fact orally as pieces are passed out.
- **5 min. Opening repair or retrieval practice.** Begin the E117 priority move here when routed. Otherwise move and count practice squares once.
- **10 min. Model.** Ten tens make 100. Add one square at a time and explicitly write 101 through 110, placing each numeral beside the count. Base-ten drawing (light), show why, 10 minutes. Keep the exit's joined layout covered.
- **20 min. Practice.** Partners count loose pieces once before grouping and building a paper row. Reserve the first five for a new E117 retry when routed.
- **10 min. Record.** Rebuild 105 from a spoken label with ruled bundles or towers and singles. Children draw their own boundaries and write the count.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear children 1 to 6. Peers mark where a group ends.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E118, independent. Return on 119.

## Day 119

**Application.** I can read, count and write through 120.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Return E118 and begin the priority move when routed.
- **10 min. Model.** Start at 114 and continue to 120 as pieces join. Add one ten-strip to 94 to get 104, then connect each place to the grouped stock. Base-ten drawing (light), show why, 10 minutes.
- **25 min. Practice.** Extend unfinished rows starting at different counts. Use five minutes for the E118 repair and ten separate minutes for retrieval misses: move each loose square once. Pairs exchange counter and recorder roles.
- **10 min. Rehearsal.** Read a partner's drawn groups and rehearse checking and retrying a paper measure before 120. Use runs that start at different numerals below 120.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear children 7 to 12. Peers connect one numeral to visible units.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E119a/b, independent on separate slips. Return on 120.

## Day 120

**Fluency.** I can check my measures, counts and sums.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Return E119a/b and mix familiar facts.
- **5 min. Recall.** Return marked unit 5 drawings. Recall the paper check, then put shared examples away. If needed, start the E119 priority repair here.
- **30 min. Practice.** Use child-made drawings. Adjust a partial row, verify 61 + 27 = 88 and use one extra ten to check that ten more than 93 is 103. Include a familiar caption with a trade from the unit 5 folder. First five continues any E119 repair. Base-ten drawing (light), practice with feedback, up to 30 minutes.
- **10 min. Retry.** Partners check one marked place or seam, then retry a familiar task individually.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear children 13 to 18. Peers mark a matching quantity on practice paper.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E120a/b, independent on separate slips. Return on 121.

## 7. Talk and writing

Ask “Where did you count this square?” on 118 and “What does the zero hold?” on 119 when a numeral such as 104 represents no extra tens beyond its hundred. Children mark where one loose square enters a bundle record. Look for one mark per piece and a numeral consistent with the groups.

## 8. Practice that returns

Mix untimed within-ten facts with one familiar place-value idea. On 118 recall kindergarten 1.1 counting and Partner turns and kindergarten 6.1's Echo supplied start then continue. Kindergarten 6.2 gives ten ones before grouping. Grade 1 section 1.1 Counting collections and 5.2 Predict and test the ones boundary are planned only in the ledger. Show moving one object once and sorting by whether another ten forms before using the routines. Day 120 uses the practiced paper check and retry. No owned standard sets a new fluency target.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review numeral from kindergarten 2.4 as the written form of a spoken number. Review ten ones, then show a ten as their group and the leftover ones as singles. Use bundle for the held group of ten. “I counted ___ tens and ___ ones.” Offer raised joints and neutral dictation for numerals. Extend by making ten more in two ways, with loose pieces and a whole strip, within 120.

## 10. Two rooms

Use folded paper strips and loose squares with one child. On 118 say the count together once, then let the child mark their own record. One projector magnifies practice joints.
