## 1. Standards and practices

**1.NBT.C.4.** "Add within 100," "relate the strategy to a written method" and "explain the reasoning used." Add two numbers: a two-digit number plus ones, whole tens, or another two-digit number with fewer than ten ones in the combined ones group. Use 1.NBT.B.2 on day 96 to read tens and ones in grouped quantities. MP.7, day 97: Like-unit pairs, sorting by unit before finding a total.

The teacher page distinguishes the value of a marked ten-stick from the number of sticks. Adding singles below the exchange threshold leaves the tens unchanged. Adding whole tens leaves the ones unchanged. A method record uses a true equation at each step.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** none. Section 5.4 owns P1 to P5 and F. Use only the named teaching cases for cards and hand-backs. Reserve the exit and assessment pairs in both orders, including pictures of the finished work. Read aloud. Everyone marks paper. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Assign a letter only when the child's marks or words show that error.

**E96a, 1.NBT.C.4.** Show a card labeled 53 squares with space for the child's own sketch and a separate open packet with three loose squares. Leave the new total tag blank. "How many squares will the tag show when we put these in?" Key: 53 + 3 = 56. Five tens stay and the six loose squares come from three plus three. Wrong: 11 after counting the five sticks as five singles (D).
Shape: fill a new total tag from a start card and loose pieces. Practice moves pieces on a mat or follows steps on a chart. Data id: `s05-X96a`.

**E96b, 1.NBT.C.4.** Give the other tag to everyone. Start with 34 squares. This packet holds five strips, ten squares on each. "How many squares will this tag show when we put the strips in?" Key: 34 + 50 = 84. Eight tens and the original four ones. Wrong: 39 after adding five ones in place of five tens (C).
Shape: fill a total tag from a packet of whole strips. Practice moves pieces or follows a chart. Read the panels separately and keep completed total tags out of practice. Data id: `s05-X96b`.

**E97, 1.NBT.C.4.** Read 22 + 44. "How many are there when you put these groups together?" Give a blank mat for the child's answer. Key: two tens join four tens, two ones join four ones, 66. Wrong: 12 after counting the six sticks as singles (D), or 30 after adding four singles for the forty in 44 (C).
Shape: make one combined picture from two number cards. Practice sorts pieces or checks a supplied arrangement. Data id: `s05-X97`.

**E98, 1.NBT.C.4.** Give separate drawings of 62 and 34 and a writing space. "What number sentences tell how you put these groups together?" Key: 60 + 30 = 90, 2 + 4 = 6, 90 + 6 = 96. Accept other true steps that match the drawing. Wrong: 62 + 34 = 92 = 96 (E), or 15 after counting nine sticks as nine singles (D).
Shape: write a method for a new pair of drawings. Practice writes a missing step in a partner's record. Data id: `s05-X98`.

**E99, 1.NBT.C.4.** Read 61 + 7. "How did you work out the total?" Give space for each child to record a method before uncovering two pictures, six sticks with eight ones and one stick with four ones. Children may mark the picture that supports their method. Key: 1 + 7 = 8, 60 + 8 = 68. Wrong: 14 from counting six sticks as six singles (D), or 61 + 7 = 60 = 68 on the child's own record (E). A picture choice alone gives no letter.
Shape: produce a sum before seeing possible totals, then use a picture to check the work. Practice uses movement cards. Data id: `s05-X99`.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Kindergarten u06-2 day 108 review: **ten ones** and **further ones**, individually visible. The paired-frame drawing keeps ten individual squares visible. For equal, point out that the two amounts have the same number. Grade 1 section 4.2's grouped place-value columns and section 4.5's twenty-two trade are planned only. Rebuild from visible pieces. The lesson uses the same amount after opening a rod, then puts it back before teaching addition.

Ideal instrument: a strip whose ten equal cells can each be matched to a single, next to a column for singles. Marked base-ten drawings (light) fit the handoff from towers to writing on day 96. The teacher records links on day 97, children write them on day 98 and shared towers go away on day 99. Individual drawings remain. No key tool is needed.

Adult-precut strips and large paper squares replace cubes, and a folded sheet with two columns replaces a mat. Draw strips with ten cells and singles as dots if loose pieces are unsuitable. Handwrite cards and recording sheets. Keep pieces out of mouths and count them back in during the last practice turn.

## 6. Days

Tool: None digitally. Use fifteen pairs. Both partners try the problem and check the other's work. For the exit, read aloud and let everyone start privately. On an eight-minute exit, give four minutes for private work first. On a five-minute exit, give one. Then hear six children who have a mark to explain, up to 30 seconds each plus 10 seconds per turn for moving and notes. If a child needs more time, keep the paper and put their name first at the next private turn. Record the names heard. For the other children, judge the paper and leave the spoken reason open. Return all thirty papers with one mark or comment. Keep a later explanation dated separately.

## Day 96

**Understanding.** I can add ones or tens without making a new ten. Base-ten drawing (light), Model, show why, 10 min.

- **5 min. Hook.** Use 24 grouped paper squares. Point to its filled count label and remind children that a tag is a paper label that tells how many are in a collection. Add three singles to make 27, reset, then add three sticks to make 54. Ask, "When do we make a new ten?" Save the question for day 100.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** Show sixteen squares. Read, "Show how sixteen makes a ten with ones left." Look for a group of ten and the six remaining squares. Separately show four marked strips and two ones. Read, "What number do these pieces show?" Key: 42. Mix in one familiar fact and its inverse orally. Keep the two practice slips for day 97.
- **10 min. Model.** Move actual units in contrasts C and D, draw them in grouped columns and review the word equation. It is a number sentence with an equals sign. Point to the same amount on each side as you record the move.
- **22 min. Practice.** Add the hook's singles and strips in columns. Rebuild the section 4.3 paper chart before using it (planned only). Move right one square for one more or down one row for ten more. Let partners check each move against the pieces.
- **5 min. Explain.** Pairs take turns pointing to the place that stayed put. Mark it on their own papers.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Trace the ten squares behind one drawn stick.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** E96a and E96b. Give one half slip with both printed stems and read them separately. All mark both tags. Children 19 to 24 explain the whole-tens panel only.

- **s05-X96a, Shape:** Fill a new total tag from a start card and a loose packet.
- **s05-X96b, Shape:** Fill a total tag from a packet of whole strips.
- **s05-R96a, Shape:** Make a ten in a scattered collection of sixteen visible squares.
- **s05-R96b, Shape:** Read a picture of four marked strips and two singles.

**Shared shape.** Exit fills two new total tags from start cards and incoming packets. Practice moves actual pieces or follows a chart.

## Day 97

**Understanding.** I can combine tens with tens and ones with ones. Base-ten drawing (light), Model, show why, 10 min.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mix familiar facts, make-ten and a tens-and-ones picture.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** E96a and E96b, choosing C or D as evidenced.
- **10 min. Model.** Like-unit pairs: sort mixed pieces for 32 + 15 = 47. Teacher writes 30 + 10 = 40, 2 + 5 = 7, 40 + 7 = 47 alongside the sorted trays.

- **15 min. Practice.** Each partner sorts, then swaps with the checker. Compare supplied groupings against the pieces.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Identify the unit that joined each group.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E97. Children 25 to 30 explain one like-unit connection.

## Day 99

**Fluency.** I can add ones or tens and show how I checked my sum. Base-ten drawing (light), Rounds, practice with feedback, 25 min.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mixed facts, make-ten and place value.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** E98, label units or repair equality.
- **5 min. Recall.** Withdraw shared towers. Rehearse the day 98 card roles with a known card.
- **25 min. Rounds.** Students choose a previously taught action card and use a personal sketch, including 63 + 20 = 83. Each partner tries and checks.
- **5 min. Retry.** Switch roles and revise one mark on an attempted card. Tidy pieces.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Point to the unchanged place.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E99. Children 7 to 12 justify one mark that supports the choice.

- **s05-X99, Shape:** Work out a new sum before seeing candidate pictures, then use a picture to check the record.

**Shared shape.** Exit records a method before seeing picture choices. Practice uses movement cards without a picture-choice bank.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 97, "What unit did you add?" Day 98, "Where is that amount on the mat?" Look for a true step written by the child and a mark linking it to the tens or ones it describes.

## 8. Practice that returns

Mix familiar within-ten addition and subtraction with one earlier idea in each retrieval. Days 97 to 99 rehearse a known make-ten case, such as 8 + 5 = 13, with 8 + 2 = 10 and 10 + 3 = 13. For the planned-only section 4.1 missing-groups case, use Rin's paper supplies as an oral practice cue. Restore the scene with physical pieces before naming joining, take away, parts or comparing as appropriate. Retire the 60 change panels, torn-stick 17, 46/81/81/69 strip and 49-square scatter. No standard here sets a fluency target. Day 99 uses untimed attempts, feedback and retries.

## 9. Supports and extension

Pair tens, ones and equal with marked pictures. "I added __. The __ stayed the same." Use enlarged tactile cells and neutral scribing, as for the exits. Read all stems and let children mark or gesture on paper. Extend by finding two taught-type changes with the same total, then identifying the place that stayed.

## 10. Two rooms

For one child, use 25 minutes with paper strips. The grown-up takes one checking turn while the child completes both total tags on day 96. A single screen can show the practice work while everyone uses paper.
