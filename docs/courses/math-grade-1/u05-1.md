## 1. Standards and practices

**1.NBT.C.4.** "Add within 100," "relate the strategy to a written method" and "explain the reasoning used." Add two numbers: a two-digit number plus ones, whole tens, or another two-digit number with fewer than ten ones in the combined ones group. Use 1.NBT.B.2 on day 96 to read tens and ones in grouped quantities. MP.7, day 97: Like-unit pairs, sorting by unit before finding a total.

The teacher page distinguishes the value of a marked ten-stick from the number of sticks. Adding singles below the exchange threshold leaves the tens unchanged. Adding whole tens leaves the ones unchanged. A method record uses a true equation at each step.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** none. Section 5.4 owns unit plan field 5, P1 to P5 and F. Keep their cases and reversals off practice, models and repairs. Read each stem aloud. Every child marks a paper picture. Provide one response region per item, with space for labels, arrows or numbers. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A result alone earns no link or error judgment. Read any judged exit filed as practice and update this field with the exact stem when data changes.

**E96a, 1.NBT.C.4.** Show separate before-and-after mats labeled 53 and 56, with five marked tens and three singles before, and five marked tens and six singles after. Provide a change mat as the response space. "What did we add to get from 53 to 56?" Key: three singles, 53 + 3 = 56. Wrong: draws three sticks as three ones (D).
Shape: infer and draw the missing ones from the before-and-after pair. Practice starts with the extra pieces visible and moves forward.

**E96b, 1.NBT.C.4.** Show mats for 34 and 84, in different arrangements, plus a change mat. "What did we add to get from 34 to 84?" Key: five sticks, 34 + 50 = 84. Wrong: labels those five sticks as an addend of five ones (C). Make the numeric label part of the change mat; use no separate equation box.
Shape: infer and draw the missing whole tens while preserving the ones. Practice gives the pieces to be added; avoid before-and-after inference cards. Give both panels to every child.

**E97, 1.NBT.C.4.** Read 31 + 26 with one blank mat. "What would the two groups look like together on this mat?" Key: five marked tens and seven ones, 57. Links or grouping marks show three tens with two tens and one one with six ones. Wrong: 12 after counting the five sticks as singles (D), or 39 after adding two singles in place of the twenty (C).
Shape: produce a combined, annotated mat from a new pair. Practice sorts mixed pieces or verifies a supplied arrangement.

**E98, 1.NBT.C.4.** Give an unsummed drawing of 42 and 15 with one writing space beside it. "What number sentences show how these groups add together?" Key: 40 + 10 = 50, 2 + 5 = 7, 50 + 7 = 57. Children connect their equations to the picture. Accept other true linked steps. Wrong: 42 + 15 = 52 = 57 (E), or 12 after counting five ten-sticks as five singles (D).
Shape: author and link an original equation record to a supplied unsummed drawing. Practice repairs or places supplied captions on completed records.

**E99, 1.NBT.C.4.** Give three unlabeled, equally styled drawings: six sticks and eight ones, one stick and four ones, and six sticks with no ones. Read 61 + 7. "Which picture can you mark to show your way of adding?" Key: six tens and eight ones, 68, with a total label and marks linking the unchanged sticks and the joined singles to the addends. Wrong: marks fourteen after counting each starting stick as one (D), or chooses the six-tens picture and records 61 + 7 = 60 = 68 (E).
Shape: solve and justify a picture choice through marks tied to both quantities. A choice alone is incomplete. Practice uses movement cards and feedback without a choice bank.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Kindergarten u06-2 day 108 review: **ten ones** and **further ones**, individually visible. The paired-frame drawing keeps ten individual squares visible. For equal, point out that the two amounts have the same number. Grade 1 section 4.2's grouped place-value columns and section 4.5's twenty-two trade are planned only. Rebuild from visible pieces. The lesson uses the same amount after opening a rod, then puts it back before teaching addition.

Ideal instrument: a strip whose ten equal cells can each be matched to a single, next to a column for singles. Marked base-ten drawings (light) fit the handoff from towers to writing on day 96. The teacher records links on day 97, children write them on day 98 and shared towers go away on day 99. Individual drawings remain. No key tool is needed.

Adult-precut strips and large paper squares replace cubes, and a folded sheet with two columns replaces a mat. Draw strips with ten cells and singles as dots if loose pieces are unsuitable. Handwrite cards and recording sheets. Keep pieces out of mouths and count them back in during the last practice turn.

## Day 96

**Understanding.** I can add ones or tens without making a new ten. Base-ten drawing (light), Model, show why, 10 min.

- **5 min. Hook.** Use 24 grouped paper squares. Add three singles to make 27, reset, then add three sticks to make 54.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** Circle ten of sixteen, label the six left, then label four strips and two singles as 42. Include one familiar fact and its inverse orally. Keep retrieval papers for day 97.
- **10 min. Model.** Move actual units in contrasts C and D, draw them in grouped columns and connect each move to its equation.
- **25 min. Practice.** Add supplied singles or whole strips in columns. On a supplied hundred chart use lateral one-steps or vertical ten-steps, stopping below the next decade for the ones move. Check each move against the model.
- **5 min. Explain.** Pairs take turns pointing to the place that stayed put. Mark it on their own papers.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Trace the ten squares behind one drawn stick.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E96a and E96b. Children 19 to 24 explain the whole-tens panel only. All mark both.

## Day 97

**Understanding.** I can combine tens with tens and ones with ones. Base-ten drawing (light), Model, show why, 10 min.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mixed facts and make-ten.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** E96a and E96b, choosing C or D as evidenced.
- **10 min. Model.** Like-unit pairs: sort mixed pieces for 32 + 15 = 47. Teacher writes 30 + 10 = 40, 2 + 5 = 7, 40 + 7 = 47 alongside the sorted trays.
- **10 min. Rebuild bundles.** Serve day 96 retrieval misses. Peers sort or verify supplied groups in pairs.
- **15 min. Practice.** Each partner sorts, then swaps with the checker. Compare supplied groupings against the pieces.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Identify the unit that joined each group.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E97. Children 25 to 30 explain one like-unit connection.

## Day 98

## Day 99

**Fluency.** I can strengthen my methods without making a ten. Base-ten drawing (light), Rounds, practice with feedback, 25 min.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mixed facts, make-ten and place value.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** E98, label units or repair equality.
- **5 min. Recall.** Withdraw shared towers. Rehearse the day 98 card roles with a known card.
- **25 min. Rounds.** Students choose a previously taught action card and use a personal sketch, including 63 + 20 = 83. Each partner tries and checks.
- **5 min. Retry.** Switch roles and revise one mark on an attempted card. Tidy pieces.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Point to the unchanged place.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E99. Children 7 to 12 justify one mark that supports the choice.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 97, "What unit did you add?" Day 98, "Where is that amount on the mat?" The day 98 repair task links a corrected caption to its group. Look for equal amounts on the two sides and an identifiable tens or ones group.

## 8. Practice that returns

Mix familiar within-ten addition and subtraction with one earlier idea in each retrieval. Days 97 to 99 rehearse a known make-ten case, such as 8 + 5 = 13, with 8 + 2 = 10 and 10 + 3 = 13. For the planned-only section 4.1 missing-groups case, use Rin's paper supplies as an oral practice cue. Restore the scene with physical pieces before naming joining, take away, parts or comparing as appropriate. Retire the 60 change panels, torn-stick 17, 46/81/81/69 strip and 49-square scatter. No standard here sets a fluency target. Day 99 uses untimed attempts, feedback and retries.

## 9. Supports and extension

Pair tens, ones and equal with marked pictures. "I added __. The __ stayed the same." Use enlarged tactile cells and neutral scribing, as for the exits. Read all stems and let children mark or gesture on paper. Extend by finding two taught-type changes with the same total, then identifying the place that stayed.
