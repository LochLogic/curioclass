## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.G.A.1.** "Distinguish" a feature that decides shape membership from color, size or turn. MP.6, day 129: report an outside feature a partner can feel.
- **1.G.A.2.** "Compose" solids and "compose new shapes from the composite shape." MP.7, day 130: move a joined build as one piece before adding to it.
- A cube is a special rectangular prism. A face is a flat surface. A cube has flat faces and occupies space. A right circular cone has one flat circular base and a curved surface. A right circular cylinder has two flat circular ends and a curved surface. Use right rectangular prisms, right circular cones and right circular cylinders. Keep angle measures out.
- Revisit 1.NBT.B.2 on 130 and 1.OA.D.8 on 131 by linking tens sketches and story bars to the written number or unknown.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Show the correct case first, then the wrong one. On 129 for A, contrast one tube's curved side and flat ends with a folded paper puppet's claim that blue makes it a cylinder. On 130 for D, slide a low paper roof and base together before showing a detached roof. On 131 for A, trace one circle's curved boundary beside a claim that red makes it a circle. Keep these pieces and poses separate from exits.

Return each slip privately at the next opening. With 11 or more misses, use five opening and five practice minutes for a new repair and retry (130 A, 131 D, 132 A). With fewer, use that space with a small group while pairs work. Keep the ten-minute kindergarten retrieval repairs and unit 6 Centers separate.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Children handle solid objects before sketching. Tie a kept-together build to a labeled sketch on 130. Use the sketch through 134 and withdraw shared examples on 135. Children retain unit 6's labeled bars for number work and unit 8. The ideal instrument lets children turn a real solid, feel a face and move joined pieces. Clean empty boxes and tubes, adult-folded sturdy paper solids and removable paper tabs fit. Substitute folded boxes and rolled paper for kit solids, and adult-cut scrap paper for flat cards. Flat silhouettes record solids after handling. Digital tools: None.

## Day 129

**Understanding.** I can group solids and explain my rule.

- **5 min. Hook.** Put a cone on one tray and a cube and cylinder on another. Invite a rule about having a point and hear another possible sort. Ask the unit question.
- **15 min. Number work.** Use field 8.
- **10 min. Model.** Review flat, curved, solid and face by touch. Feel a cone's base and curved surface. Name a noncube rectangular prism using a plain box.
- **15 min. Practice.** Partners guess a rule disclosed one solid at a time. Retrieve the flat triangle among the triangle, cube and cone and mark a difference from one solid.
- **10 min. Centers.** Return F and the dated unit 6 paper to every child, two minutes. Choose one priority per child, up to two teacher groups with parallel paper cues. Repair for five minutes, then allow three for a fresh individual retry. For a gap priority, repair a new strip with one open joint. For an equality priority, use different stock for the repair and the unseen retry 6 + 3 = 8 + 1. Each side is nine. Keep unit 6 letters on the separate unit 6 record.
- **5 min. Exit.** E129.

## Day 130

**Understanding.** I can use my solid build as one piece.

- **15 min. Number work.** Use field 8.
- **5 min. Geometry opening.** Return E129. Turn a low cylinder-and-cone build intact or use it in the A repair. Say "joined" for pieces held together and "whole" for the entire build.
- **25 min. Building practice.** Reserve five for E129 retry and ten for matching solids to face outlines when the kindergarten retrieval calls for it. Use five for the supported kindergarten join: provide a faint rectangle target and fresh nonsquare paper triangles. Start with opposite right corners. Use five to add a second cylinder beside the model base and sketch. Reallocate unused repair time to partner builds.
- **10 min. Centers.** Use two minutes to return remaining unit 6 priorities, five for one repair and three for a private fresh retry. For a tens priority, demonstrate adding tens with different stock. Use 41 + 20 = 61 as the unseen retry. File the original with the retry.
- **5 min. Exit.** E130.

## Day 131

**Understanding.** I can group flat shapes by their features.

- **15 min. Number work.** Use field 8.
- **10 min. Geometry opening.** Return E130. If needed, spend five preserving a low build before adding. Trace a half-circle to test the supplied rule "has a curved edge." Contrast a side on a triangle with a corner where sides meet.
- **25 min. Partner practice.** Five for E130 practice repair if needed. Hold ten for the kindergarten join repair by fitting triangles to a different outline. Test one piece at a time and revise a supplied label. Use drawn pieces when cutting supplies are unavailable.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Compare two rules on one practice shape. Record the six named practice turns once.
- **5 min. Exit.** E131.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review kindergarten flat, solid, cube, cylinder, cone, face, whole, side and curved with real pieces and gestures. Show cube faces, a cone with one flat base and a cylinder with two flat ends. To show Partner turns, one child handles or counts while one watches, then they exchange roles. Name rule as what all the pieces in a group have in common. Use "It belongs because ___." Let a child trace a tactile outline and indicate a marked surface or use neutral dictation. On 130 show how a small picture or one word can tell which shape a mark names. Extend by testing a second rule on a practice object.

## 10. Two rooms
