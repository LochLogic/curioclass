## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.G.A.1.** "Distinguish" a feature that decides shape membership from color, size or turn. MP.6, day 129: report an outside feature a partner can feel.
- **1.G.A.2.** "Compose" solids and "compose new shapes from the composite shape." MP.7, day 130: move a joined build as one piece before adding to it.
- A cube is a special rectangular prism. A face is a flat surface. A cube has flat faces and occupies space. A right circular cone has one flat circular base and a curved surface. A right circular cylinder has two flat circular ends and a curved surface. Use right rectangular prisms, right circular cones and right circular cylinders. Keep angle measures out.

The triangle is flat beside cube and cone. For the 130 retrieval, ask “Can you make a larger triangle with these two pieces?” Cut one wide, symmetrical triangle straight from its tip to the middle of its base. Give the child the two pieces, mixed up. Joining the matching upright legs with the right angles side by side gives a straight base. After collecting the first try, review the longest-side join from kindergarten section 3.5. Rotate the second congruent right triangle a half-turn around the midpoint of its longest side and join the longest sides. The result is a rectangle, or a square for equal legs. Simply flipping one piece across the join can give a kite.

## Day 129

**Understanding.** I can group solids and explain my rule.

- **5 min. Hook.** Put a cone on one tray and a cube and cylinder on another. Ask what rule fits. Accept “one round base with a tip opposite it.” A cube has corners that a child may also call points. Invite another sort. Ask “What makes a shape that shape, and what time is it?”
- **5 min. Warm-up.** Mixed within-ten facts, using the earlier skills selected by the unit retrieval rule. Allow untimed retries.
- **10 min. Number work.** Use the story or place-value work in field 8.
- **10 min. Model.** Review flat, curved, solid and face by touch. Turn the cube. Ask which way it faces now. The turn leaves its faces unchanged. Feel a cone's base and curved surface. Name a noncube rectangular prism using a plain box.
- **15 min. Practice.** Retrieve positions with “Put the cube beside the cylinder.” Show a triangle, cube and cone. Ask “Which is flat?” Then ask “How is it different from this cube?” One lies flat, the other takes up space. Partners guess a rule one solid at a time, then put two solids together. Hear the six using the practice stock.
- **10 min. Centers.** Return F and the dated unit 6 paper to every child, two minutes. Choose one priority per child, one teacher group at a time. If two groups need help, give each two minutes and use one minute to switch. Both groups keep working on their own paper cue for the full five minutes. Allow three minutes for a fresh individual retry. For a gap priority, repair a new strip with one open joint. For an equality priority, use different stock for the repair and the unseen retry 6 + 3 = 8 + 1. Each side is nine. Keep unit 6 letters on the separate unit 6 record.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E129.

**Shared shape.** Practice infers a partner’s rule by handling one object at a time. Exit selects and marks a feature group on four new solid pictures.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review kindergarten flat, solid, cube, cylinder, cone, face, whole, side and curved with real pieces and gestures. Show cube faces, a cone with one flat base and a cylinder with two flat ends. To show Partner turns, one child handles or counts while one watches, then they exchange roles. Review the kindergarten word “rule” by showing what every piece in a group has in common. Use "It belongs because ___." Let a child trace a tactile outline and indicate a marked surface or use neutral dictation. On 130 show how a small picture or one word can tell which shape a mark names. Extend by testing a second rule on a practice object.
