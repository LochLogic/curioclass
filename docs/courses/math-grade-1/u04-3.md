Read with [the unit plan](s04.md) and [course defaults](DEFAULTS.md). Grade 1 Math, Unit 4, Numbers to 99. **Days 79 to 81.** Days by kind: understanding 1, fluency 2. A plan, not a lesson.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.NBT.C.5.** "Mentally find" ten more or ten less and "explain the reasoning used," without counting. For ten more, start below 90. Include ten less from teens and the nineties. Use one or two repeats of ten more on day 79, staying below 100.
- **1.NBT.B.2 and B.2c.** "Understand" what each digit counts. Whole-ten numerals "refer to" tens with zero ones. On day 80 revisit 1.NBT.C.6: "Subtract" whole tens, "relate" a drawing to the equation and "explain" the difference. Maintain taught 1.OA.C.6 strategies on day 81.
- **MP.7, day 79.** Track the extra ten on a cropped hundred chart beside the actual tower. The ones digit stays because the ones did not change. In a teen, the last ten can leave and the ones remain in a single-digit result.

## Day 79

**Understanding.** I can change tens while keeping ones. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Identify a digit's unit. Review counting up from a held amount, marking only new counts.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Return E78 and trace chosen labels to their units.
- **10 min. Model.** Add a tower to 22 to make 32, then another to make 42. Tie each move to one step down the chart. Show E beside the second move.
- **20 min. Practice.** Pairs supply an intermediate chart step, then check the preserved ones with strips.
- **10 min. Mental rehearsal.** Cover the model, predict then uncover. Introduce direction rounds. Model ten less from 96 (86) and from 15 (5), explaining what happens to the ones.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E79. Hear children 25 to 30.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 80 "What happens to the last ten in fourteen?" Day 81 "How did you keep track of the ones?" On day 81 mark a numeral beside a worked picture. Look for a changed count of tens with the same ones.

## 8. Practice that returns

Use the named retrieval with a brief taught within-ten warm-up fact. Days 80 and 81 rehearse known direction and fact routines with feedback and retry. No owned standard sets a fluency target. Use units to reason before checking with materials.

## 9. Supports and extension

Ten more means one additional ten; ten less means one fewer ten. Use mentally for thinking before using objects or marks. Offer "__ tens became __ tens. The ones stayed __." Read aloud. A child may sign or dictate privately before the confirming mat appears. Provide raised cell outlines and large numeral slips. Extend with two inverse ten moves and a reason the result returns to its start.

## 10. Two rooms

At home, cover the strips for the child's prediction, then uncover and confirm. Use one screen for the practice chart if available and blank it for mental turns.
