## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.MD.C.4** (days 7, 8, 9, 10): Organize, represent, and interpret data with up to three categories; ask and answer questions about the total number of data points, how many in each category, and how many more or less are in one category than in another.
- **1.OA.C.5** (days 10): Relate counting to addition and subtraction (e.g., by counting on 2 to add 2).
- skill m1-data-three-categories (grade 1)
- skill mk-sort-count (grade K, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m1-count-on (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions:** What stays counted when we add (day 3)? How can a display keep every response (day 8)? Which questions can our display answer (day 13)?
>
> - **Practices:** MP.7, day 3: cover the first group in the partner addition game and explain why it remains counted. MP.2, days 12 to 13: connect each tally to one response, then pose and answer total and difference questions. Supporting data work practises major counting work here and on days 10, 14 and 15.
> - **Fluency:** Neither owned standard sets a fluency expectation. Days 6, 10 and 14 rehearse taught counting methods through partner rounds, immediate checking and another attempt, without time pressure or new content. Record counting strategy separately from later mental fluency.
> - **Limits:** Addition and subtraction stay within 10 initially, then within 20 on days 12 to 15. Data have at most three categories. Formal graphs, four categories, number lines and standard algorithms wait. Full story-type coverage belongs to later units.
> - **Carried:** Tie the ten-frame to an addition expression on day 1 and to the written sum on day 2. Children move counters, sketch, then count without a frame on day 6; restore it for access. Withdraw it as the shared display on day 7, retaining it for unit 2. Tie object groups to labelled tallies on days 7 to 8. On day 16 remove the shared tally display while each child makes a paper representation.

### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Read all prompts aloud; no independent reading is required. Pair picture labels with group, total, more and fewer. Offer "I kept __ and counted __ more." Use large, high-contrast outlines and tactile paper mats; a child may point while an adult scribes. Say counting words rather than requiring finger movements. Extend by representing the same three-category data another way and explaining why totals and differences agree.
<!-- /skel -->
