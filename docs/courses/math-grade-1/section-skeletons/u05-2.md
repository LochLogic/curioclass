## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.NBT.C.4** (days 100, 101, 102, 103): Add within 100, including adding a two-digit number and a one-digit number, and adding a two-digit number and a multiple of 10, using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction; relate the strategy to a written method and explain the reasoning used. Understand that in adding two-digit numbers, one adds tens and tens, ones and ones; and sometimes it is necessary to compose a ten.
- skill m1-add-within-100 (grade 1)
- skill m1-add-no-new-ten (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions:** Which units combine (day 97)? Where does the new ten come from (day 100)? Can we predict an exchange (day 102)? Why do different methods agree (day 106)?
> - **Teaching:** Model exchanges without changing quantity. Connect each drawing to equalities, then step back on days 102 and 106. Teacher pages explain like units, the value of the new ten and why either order of combining units works. Section 5.1 carries adding ones or tens with no new ten; 5.2 composing a ten; 5.3 two-digit pairs; 5.4 the explained written record and application.
