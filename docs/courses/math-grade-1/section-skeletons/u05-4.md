## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.NBT.C.4** (days 108, 109, 110): Add within 100, including adding a two-digit number and a one-digit number, and adding a two-digit number and a multiple of 10, using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction; relate the strategy to a written method and explain the reasoning used. Understand that in adding two-digit numbers, one adds tens and tens, ones and ones; and sometimes it is necessary to compose a ten.
- skill m1-add-no-new-ten (grade 1)
- skill m1-add-within-100 (grade 1)

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Read every prompt aloud; require no independent reading. Pair tens, ones, exchange and equal with pictures. Offer "These ten ones become __; the amount stays __." Extend within the same standard by finding two addend pairs with the same sum, one requiring an exchange and one not, and explaining why.
