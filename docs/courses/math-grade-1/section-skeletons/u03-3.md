## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.NBT.B.2a** (days 50, 51, 53): 10 can be thought of as a bundle of ten ones - called a “ten.”
- **1.NBT.B.2b** (days 50, 51, 53): The numbers from 11 to 19 are composed of a ten and one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, or nine ones.
- **1.OA.C.6** (days 50, 51, 52, 53): Add and subtract within 20, demonstrating fluency for addition and subtraction within 10. Use strategies such as counting on; making ten (e.g., 8 + 6 = 8 + 2 + 4 = 10 + 4 = 14); decomposing a number leading to a ten (e.g., 13 – 4 = 13 – 3 – 1 = 10 – 1 = 9); using the relationship between addition and subtraction (e.g., knowing that 8 + 4 = 12, one knows 12 – 8 = 4); and creating equivalent but easier or known sums (e.g., adding 6 + 7 by creating the known equivalent 6 + 6 + 1 = 12 + 1 = 13).
- **1.OA.A.1** (days 52): Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- skill m1-ten-as-unit (grade 1)
- skill m1-add-sub-within-20 (grade 1)
- skill m1-add-take-problems (grade 1)

### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Read all text aloud; no independent reading is required. Pair ten, ones, remaining and total with pictures. Offer "I moved __; __ stayed." Make groups differ by shape as well as color. Use large tactile mats and neutral dictation; never supply a strategy during assessment. Extend by showing two valid methods and explaining which fits the same numbers better, within twenty.
