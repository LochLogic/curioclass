## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.OA.A.1** (days 45): Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **1.OA.C.6** (days 45, 46, 47, 48, 49): Add and subtract within 20, demonstrating fluency for addition and subtraction within 10. Use strategies such as counting on; making ten (e.g., 8 + 6 = 8 + 2 + 4 = 10 + 4 = 14); decomposing a number leading to a ten (e.g., 13 – 4 = 13 – 3 – 1 = 10 – 1 = 9); using the relationship between addition and subtraction (e.g., knowing that 8 + 4 = 12, one knows 12 – 8 = 4); and creating equivalent but easier or known sums (e.g., adding 6 + 7 by creating the known equivalent 6 + 6 + 1 = 12 + 1 = 13).
- **1.NBT.B.2a** (days 47, 48, 49): 10 can be thought of as a bundle of ten ones - called a “ten.”
- **1.NBT.B.2b** (days 47, 48, 49): The numbers from 11 to 19 are composed of a ten and one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, or nine ones.
- skill m1-add-take-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-fluency-within-10 (grade 1)
- skill m1-ten-as-unit (grade 1)
- skill m1-add-sub-within-20 (grade 1)
- skill mk-make-ten (grade K, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill mk-teen-ten-ones (grade K, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
