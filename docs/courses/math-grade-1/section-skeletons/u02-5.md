## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.OA.A.1** (days 37, 38, 39): Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **1.OA.B.4** (days 37, 38, 39): Understand subtraction as an unknown-addend problem. For example, subtract 10 – 8 by finding the number that makes 10 when added to 8.
- **1.OA.D.7** (days 37, 38, 39): Understand the meaning of the equal sign, and determine if equations involving addition and subtraction are true or false. For example, which of the following equations are true and which are false? 6 = 6, 7 = 8 – 1, 5 + 2 = 2 + 5, 4 + 1 = 5 + 2.
- **1.OA.D.8** (days 37, 38, 39): Determine the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation relating three whole numbers. For example, determine the unknown number that makes the equation true in each of the equations 8 + ? = 11, 5 = _ – 3, 6 + 6 = _.
- skill m1-add-take-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-part-whole-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-compare-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-equal-sign (grade 1)
- skill m1-unknown-in-equation (grade 1)
