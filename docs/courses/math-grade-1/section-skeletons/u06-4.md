### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Read all prompts aloud; require no independent reading. Pair longer, shorter, unit, whole and unknown with pictures. Offer "This is longer because __" and "The unknown is __". Use raised endpoints, large pieces, high contrast and neutral dictation. Accept pointing to a labelled picture. Regroup from current errors. Extend by proving two different correct arrangements measure the same whole length; keep equal units and whole-number spans.
