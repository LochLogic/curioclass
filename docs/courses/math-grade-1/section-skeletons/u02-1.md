## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.OA.A.1** (days 17, 18, 19, 20, 21): Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **1.OA.D.7** (days 18, 20): Understand the meaning of the equal sign, and determine if equations involving addition and subtraction are true or false. For example, which of the following equations are true and which are false? 6 = 6, 7 = 8 – 1, 5 + 2 = 2 + 5, 4 + 1 = 5 + 2.
- **1.OA.D.8** (days 19, 20): Determine the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation relating three whole numbers. For example, determine the unknown number that makes the equation true in each of the equations 8 + ? = 11, 5 = _ – 3, 6 + 6 = _.

- skill mk-add-sub-expressions (grade K, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m1-equal-sign (grade 1)
- skill m1-unknown-in-equation (grade 1)

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Error register rows (unit plan field 6) that name one of this section's days:

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Rejects equality when the total is written first. | Treats equality as an instruction to produce an answer. Day 18: show false rejection of 7 = 5 + 2 beside its true picture match. Read "is the same amount as" in either direction. | From memory. |
| C | Reports the ending quantity as the unknown change. | Locates the wrong story quantity. Day 20: for 7 + __ = 12, contrast wrong 12 with right 5. Circle only the arriving group. | From memory. |
| D | Adds a known whole and part to find a missing part. | Treats the whole as another part. Day 24: contrast wrong 12 - 5 = 17 with right 7. Cover four within nine and count the uncovered part. | From memory. |
| H | Counts the starting word as the first added step. | Makes an off-by-one count. Day 19: contrast wrong 4 + 3 = 6 with right 7. Hold four still and mark three new steps. | From memory. |
| I | Adds the removed amount when solving a removal story. | Reverses the action. Day 18: contrast wrong 8 - 3 = 11 with right 5. Cross out the three leaving pieces before counting what remains. | From memory. |

<!-- skel -->
