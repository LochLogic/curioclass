## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.OA.A.1** (days 22, 23, 24, 25, 26): Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **1.OA.B.4** (days 22, 23, 24, 25, 26): Understand subtraction as an unknown-addend problem. For example, subtract 10 – 8 by finding the number that makes 10 when added to 8.
- **1.OA.D.7** (days 22, 23, 25): Understand the meaning of the equal sign, and determine if equations involving addition and subtraction are true or false. For example, which of the following equations are true and which are false? 6 = 6, 7 = 8 – 1, 5 + 2 = 2 + 5, 4 + 1 = 5 + 2.
- **1.OA.D.8** (days 24): Determine the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation relating three whole numbers. For example, determine the unknown number that makes the equation true in each of the equations 8 + ? = 11, 5 = _ – 3, 6 + 6 = _.
- skill m1-part-whole-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-equal-sign (grade 1)
- skill m1-unknown-in-equation (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions:** Where is the missing change (day 20)? How can a whole help find a part (day 24)? Where is the difference in a drawing (day 30)? Does an equation tell the same story (day 35)?
> - **Teaching:** Model actions before expressions, then connect every number to its quantity. Compare a situation equation with a solution equation. Step back as children choose representations on days 25 and 31 to 35. Teacher pages explain why subtraction finds an unknown addend and why equality compares quantities on both sides.
>
> - **Fluency and balance:** No owned standard sets a fluency expectation. Keep five understanding, four fluency and thirteen application days, plus one check. Days 21, 26, 32 and 37 repeat taught facts within 10 through partner attempts, checking and retrying, without time pressure or new content. Unit 3 owns mental fluency within 10.
> - **Focus:** This is one major-work unit: 22 of 22 teaching lessons, 100 percent; 1,270 of 1,270 instructional minutes, 100 percent. Exclude day 39, thirty observation minutes and twenty minutes responding to unit 1. Data on days 29 and 38 supplies quantities for major story and equation work.
> - **Carried:** Keep unit 1's ten-frame. Tie counters to expressions on day 17 and equations on day 18; move from counters to sketches during days 22 to 30. Withdraw the shared frame on day 33, retaining individual access. Matching rows begin on day 27, connect to equations that day and become optional on day 35. Unit 3 receives the ten-frame and labelled drawings.
