## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.OA.A.1** (days 154, 155, 156): Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **1.NBT.A.1** (days 157): Count to 120, starting at any number less than 120. In this range, read and write numerals and represent a number of objects with a written numeral.
- skill m1-all-problem-types (grade 1)
- skill m1-count-to-120 (grade 1)
