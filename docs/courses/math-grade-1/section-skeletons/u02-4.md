## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.OA.A.1** (days 33, 34, 35, 36): Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **1.OA.B.4** (days 33, 34, 35, 36): Understand subtraction as an unknown-addend problem. For example, subtract 10 – 8 by finding the number that makes 10 when added to 8.
- **1.OA.D.7** (days 33): Understand the meaning of the equal sign, and determine if equations involving addition and subtraction are true or false. For example, which of the following equations are true and which are false? 6 = 6, 7 = 8 – 1, 5 + 2 = 2 + 5, 4 + 1 = 5 + 2.
- **1.OA.D.8** (days 34, 35, 36): Determine the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation relating three whole numbers. For example, determine the unknown number that makes the equation true in each of the equations 8 + ? = 11, 5 = _ – 3, 6 + 6 = _.
- skill m1-add-take-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-part-whole-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-compare-problems (grade 1)
- skill m1-equal-sign (grade 1)
- skill m1-unknown-in-equation (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Supporting questions:** Where is the missing change (day 20)? How can a whole help find a part (day 24)? Where is the difference in a drawing (day 30)? Does an equation tell the same story (day 35)?
>
> - **Practices:** MP.4, day 27: match labelled rows and mark the unmatched part; day 31: connect a comparison drawing to addition and subtraction equations. MP.6, day 33: defend true and false equations with reversed and two-expression layouts, evaluating both sides.
> - **Limits:** Teach and sample joining and removing with result or change unknown, all three part-whole cases, and comparison with difference unknown, within 20. These contribute to ~1.OA.A.1; unit 6 completes it. Day 36 discusses start-unknown stories with support, without a proficiency score. Larger- and smaller-unknown comparisons wait. No number lines, tape diagrams, algorithms or two-step stories. An unknown in a bare equation is assessed independently of difficult story language.
> - **Carried:** Keep unit 1's ten-frame. Tie counters to expressions on day 17 and equations on day 18; move from counters to sketches during days 22 to 30. Withdraw the shared frame on day 33, retaining individual access. Matching rows begin on day 27, connect to equations that day and become optional on day 35. Unit 3 receives the ten-frame and labelled drawings.
