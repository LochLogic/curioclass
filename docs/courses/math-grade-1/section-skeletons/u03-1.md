## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1.OA.C.6** (days 40, 41, 42, 43, 44): Add and subtract within 20, demonstrating fluency for addition and subtraction within 10. Use strategies such as counting on; making ten (e.g., 8 + 6 = 8 + 2 + 4 = 10 + 4 = 14); decomposing a number leading to a ten (e.g., 13 – 4 = 13 – 3 – 1 = 10 – 1 = 9); using the relationship between addition and subtraction (e.g., knowing that 8 + 4 = 12, one knows 12 – 8 = 4); and creating equivalent but easier or known sums (e.g., adding 6 + 7 by creating the known equivalent 6 + 6 + 1 = 12 + 1 = 13).
- **1.OA.B.3** (days 41, 42): Apply properties of operations as strategies to add and subtract. Examples: If 8 + 3 = 11 is known, then 3 + 8 = 11 is also known. (Commutative property of addition.) To add 2 + 6 + 4, the second two numbers can be added to make a ten, so 2 + 6 + 4 = 2 + 10 = 12. (Associative property of addition.)
- **1.OA.C.5** (days 41): Relate counting to addition and subtraction (e.g., by counting on 2 to add 2).
- skill m1-fluency-within-10 (grade 1)
- skill mk-decompose-to-10 (grade K, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill mk-fluency-within-5 (grade K, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m1-properties-strategies (grade 1)
- skill m1-count-on (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Hook, day 40:** Show an unfinished addition table. Children mark a familiar sum and explain what makes it familiar. Ask the compelling question. Adapted from the opening named in field 10.
> - **Supporting questions:** Why start with the larger addend (day 41)? How can ten things be one unit (day 47)? Which pair helps first (day 55)? When does counting up help subtraction (day 63)?
>
> - **Practices:** MP.7, day 55: rearrange three paper groups, bracket the pair making ten, and explain why nothing was added or lost. MP.8, day 43: explain doubles and plus-one patterns; day 59: compare 9 + 3 + 7, 8 + 4 + 7 and 7 + 5 + 7, predict 6 + 6 + 7, and explain the constant total of nineteen.
> - **Carried:** Keep unit 2's ten-frame and labelled drawings. Tie frames to equations on day 40, double frames to teen numerals on day 47, and moving counters to equivalent expressions on day 56. Introduce cube towers and drawn ten-sticks beside numerals on day 47. Children progress from objects to drawings. Withdraw shared frames on day 58 and shared towers on day 64; individual access remains during learning. Mental fact observations use neither. Unit 4 receives towers and ten-sticks.
