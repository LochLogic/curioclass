Rin sets out seven tiles, adds some, and now has twelve.” Show three spaces for before, change and after. “How many did Rin add?” Five. Accept a drawing. ## 9. Supports and extension

On 154 review change as what joins or leaves, Kindergarten 4.3, with a number sentence that uses an equals sign for the same amount. The unknown is the amount to find. The before, move and end panels and the box from Grade 1 section 2.1, the unknown from 2.4 and the number sentence from 5.1 are planned only. Show each with pieces before using it. On 155 review part, one group within the whole, and whole, every piece in the collection (Kindergarten 5.1). On 156 a label tells which bar holds which group, and difference is what sticks out after matching. Section 1.2's label and section 2.3's difference plans are planned only. Review more and fewer (Kindergarten 2.2) with matched pieces. Say “___ has ___ fewer than ___.” On 157 review numeral as the written form of a number, Kindergarten 2.4. A ten-stick holds ten visible ones. Read aloud and offer tactile bars, large marks, pointing or neutral dictation. Extend on 156 by rewording a practice comparison and keeping the same labeled difference.
