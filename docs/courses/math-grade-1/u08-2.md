## Day 154

**Application.** I can find what changed in a story.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return both E153 slips. Mix a known addition and subtraction. Say “Invented for practice. Rin sets out seven tiles, adds some, and now has twelve.” Show three spaces for before, change and after. “How many did Rin add?” Five. Accept a drawing. Recall the box for a missing amount on a separate example before children use it in equations. Route a miss to change practice.
- **10 min. Opening.** Rebuild section 2.1's before, move and end panels with fresh counters. Name the start, the change and the end. Show C. Draw added pieces outside the covered start. Say how the number sentence names equal amounts and the box marks the unknown.
- **35 min. Practice.** Ten for result unknown. Rin sets out eight tiles and adds five. Move two of the five to fill ten. Three remain, so there are thirteen. Show the B repair here, then try a fresh joining card. Also act out Rin taking three from fourteen, leaving eleven. Fifteen for change unknown. Act out joining from six to ten and removing from ten to six, both changes of four. Use ten minutes for a recurring A. Children who missed retrieval mark before, change and after while acting. Check those pictures in the last five minutes. Ten for start unknown. Rin added three and ended with eight, or took three away and ended with eight. The starts were five and eleven. Partners draw each story and put the box where its unknown belongs.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Ask “What changed?” Connect one acted story to its situation equation and a solution equation.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** s08-X154 s08-X154b.
  - Shape: Recover a joining change from a grouped ending picture and a single starting tag. Practice acts out changes with counters.
  - Shape: Recover a removal from two equal starting strips and the last pin still attached. Practice acts out changes with counters.

**Shared shape.** Acting out a change precedes rebuilding it from static pictures with either a grouped end or equal starting strips.

## Day 156

**Application.** I can show the difference with labeled bars.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E155. Mixed facts and a story sketch.
- **10 min. Opening.** Match cubes and draw the two labeled bars from the D example. Attach the box to the unmatched length. Read both 6 + □ = 11 and 11 − 6 = 5 against the bars. The extra length stays five when the wording changes to fewer.
- **35 min. Practice.** Fifteen for difference from supplied bars, including ten for A if needed. Ten each for larger and smaller unknown. Use both wordings: “Eight is two fewer than what number?” Ten. “Eight is two more than what number?” Six. Reverse the wording using the same quantities and relation. Work in pairs with supported discussion and a drawn mark from each child.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Ask “Where is the extra part?” Trace the common start and gap. Cover the shared bar.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** s08-X156 s08-X156b. Use student-page Exit box s08-P156-fewer after saving the first marks.

## Day 157

## 9. Supports and extension

On 154 review change as what joins or leaves, Kindergarten 4.3, with a number sentence that uses an equals sign for the same amount. The unknown is the amount to find. The before, move and end panels and the box from Grade 1 section 2.1, the unknown from 2.4 and the number sentence from 5.1 are planned only. Show each with pieces before using it. On 155 review part, one group within the whole, and whole, every piece in the collection (Kindergarten 5.1). On 156 a label tells which bar holds which group, and difference is what sticks out after matching. Section 1.2's label and section 2.3's difference plans are planned only. Review more and fewer (Kindergarten 2.2) with matched pieces. Say “___ has ___ fewer than ___.” On 157 review numeral as the written form of a number, Kindergarten 2.4. A ten-stick holds ten visible ones. Read aloud and offer tactile bars, large marks, pointing or neutral dictation. Extend on 156 by rewording a practice comparison and keeping the same labeled gap.
