## 6. Days

Fifteen pairs work in parallel, with up to 45 seconds per partner per spoken turn, then both mark. On 154 to 156 the exit rotation is names 19 to 24, 25 to 30 and 1 to 6, with overdue names first. Six turns of up to 40 seconds leave one minute to cue and collect. Return every paper at the next opening, including those from unheard children, and date later hearings when they occur. On 157 use only parallel pairs. Put the credit once on each day page.

## Day 154

**Application.** I can find what changed in a story.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E153. Mixed facts and the retrieval story, 7 + □ = 12. Mark before, change and after. Five join. Route a miss to the change block.
- **10 min. Opening.** Revisit before as the start, after as the end and the change as what joins or leaves. Show C. Draw added pieces outside the covered start. Say how the number sentence names equal amounts and the box marks the unknown.
- **35 min. Practice.** Ten for result unknown: join 10 and 3 to get 13, then remove 3 from 14 to leave 11. Use this block for the routed B repair, with a result question inside the repair. Fifteen for change unknown, acting 6 + □ = 10 and 10 − □ = 6, both four. Give ten of these minutes to A as needed. Partners with a retrieval miss rebuild their acted changes in the three picture spaces during this block. The teacher checks their papers in its last five minutes. Ten for start unknown, □ + 3 = 8 and □ − 3 = 8, starts five and eleven. Pairs draw each situation and mark its unknown.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Ask “What changed?” Connect one acted story to its situation equation and a solution equation.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E154. Shape: reconstruct a missing change from a grouped ending picture. Reserve this packet from acted stories.

## Day 155

**Application.** I can find a missing part and explain my equation.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E154. Mixed facts and a tens sketch.
- **10 min. Opening.** Show a whole of twelve with eight visible. Revisit part and whole. Compare 8 + □ = 12 with 12 − 8 = 4. Show the A contrast on separate stock.
- **35 min. Practice.** Ten for total unknown, such as 3 + 8 = 11. Use this block for C when needed, with the joining picture before a task that asks for the fixed whole. Fifteen for one part unknown with a supplied open-and-cover bar. Ten for both parts unknown: split six, including zero and six. Keep the whole labeled and check the parts against it.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Reopen parts and check the whole. Ask two children to show which number stayed fixed. Everyone else points on paper.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E155. Shape: combine two clues about excluded cards. Reserve the clipped and banded groups from bar practice.

## Day 156

**Application.** I can show the difference with labeled bars.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E155. Mixed facts and a story sketch.
- **10 min. Opening.** On separate stock, match cubes, draw aligned bars and place their labels and unknown. Show D. Explain difference as the unmatched amount.
- **35 min. Practice.** Fifteen for difference from supplied bars, including ten for A if needed. Ten each for larger and smaller unknown. Use both wordings: “Eight is two fewer than what number?” Ten. “Eight is two more than what number?” Six. Reverse the wording using the same quantities and relation. Work in pairs with supported discussion and a drawn mark from each child.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Ask “Where is the extra part?” Trace the common start and gap. Cover the shared bar.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E156. Shape: generate bars for a fresh pair of sleeves with equal hidden starts. Practice supplied bars remain covered.

## Day 157

**Fluency.** I can connect counts, pictures and numerals to 120.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E156. Mixed facts and a tens picture.
- **10 min. Opening.** Echo supplied starts of 87 and 99, then continue across the next decade or 100. Revoice numeral and ten-stick with visible units. Show E.
- **35 min. Practice.** Partners count loose collections, bundle by tens and match completed tens drawings to numerals. Include counting from 114 through 120. Embed ten for D and a separate ten for A if necessary. Switch roles with immediate feedback.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Partners read a numeral from their practice pile, then link its label to that count. Both partners get a turn.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E157. Shape: produce a numeral from a mixed stock card, then check in pairs. Allow each partner up to 45 seconds to read their own mark. Sheets go home. Anonymous retry slips can flag a class repair for 158.

## 7. Talk and writing

154: “What changed?” 156: “Where is the extra part?” Take two 45-second contributions inside synthesis and let classmates point on their pages. In 154 practice look for a box where the story leaves an amount unknown. In 156 mark a supplied bar gap. Look for matching units, common starts and a label attached to the correct bar.

## 9. Supports and extension

On 154 review change as what joins or leaves, Kindergarten 4.3, with a number sentence that uses an equals sign for the same amount. The unknown is the amount to find. Grade 1 sections 5.1 and 2.4 are planned only. On 155 review part, one group within the whole, and whole, every piece in the collection (Kindergarten 5.1). On 156 a label tells which bar holds which group, and difference is what sticks out after matching. Section 1.2's label and section 2.3's difference plans are planned only. Review more and fewer (Kindergarten 2.2) with matched pieces. Say “___ has ___ fewer than ___.” On 157 review numeral as the written form of a number, Kindergarten 2.4. A ten-stick holds ten visible ones. Read aloud and offer tactile bars, large marks, pointing or neutral dictation. Extend on 156 by rewording a practice comparison and keeping the same labeled gap.
