# Section 6.4: Where does the unknown belong?

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-grade-1/6/s/6.4), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-grade-1 u06-4.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 1 Math. Unit 6, Length to 120 units (days 111 to 128), see [s06.md](s06.md). **Days 121 to 125.** Days by kind: understanding 1, application 4. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.OA.A.1.** “Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems.” Cover the three positions for each of Add To, Take From, part-whole and Compare. For Compare use both more and fewer for each position. Keep descriptive evidence for start unknown and opposite-language comparisons.
- **Connection.** On 125, 1.MD.A.1 “Order” and “compare” return in retained record L from section 6.1.
- **MP.4, day 122.** Match equal units first, then draw bars with aligned starts, quantity labels and the unknown. Check an equation against those quantities.
- **Teacher explanation.** A situation equation follows the story's quantities and places the unknown where it belongs. To find a removing story's start, a related addition can restore the amount taken away. In comparisons the words more and fewer tell how quantities relate. The unknown's place determines a useful operation. Use paper pieces, then drawings, then equations. All story quantities and answers stay within 20.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Return E120. In an invented joining scene start with seven paper squares and add three. Children mark 7 + 3 = 10. Give the familiar facts orally.

## 7. Talk and writing

Ask “What changed?” on 121 and “Which bar holds the unknown?” on 122. Children mark a bar and connect it to an equation. Look for aligned starts, relevant quantity labels and an unknown placed over the intended length.

## 8. Practice that returns

Mix untimed within-ten facts with one earlier story meaning. On 121 reuse Rin from grade 1 section 2.1 in new single-actor scenes with classroom strips, without recalling the badge cases. On 122 use section 2.3's Check a supplied match. Both are planned only in the ledger, so demonstrate the action and correction. On 123 distinguish a situation equation from a related solution equation, using the purpose of each; grade 1 section 2.4 wording is planned only and gets a concrete demonstration. No owned standard sets a fluency target.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review whole from kindergarten 5.1, every piece in the collection. Show unknown as the number to find and difference as how much more or fewer. Name a tape diagram as bars that show amounts. Show a tag as a little paper label naming an amount or piece. Grade 1 section 4.1 has tag at planned only; demonstrate it here. “___ has ___ fewer than ___.” Read aloud, offer raised pieces or neutral dictation and keep directions accessible without naming the operation. Children mark their own paper. Extend by matching two true equations to one story, within 20.
