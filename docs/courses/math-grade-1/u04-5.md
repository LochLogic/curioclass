Read with [the unit plan](s04.md) and [course defaults](DEFAULTS.md). Grade 1 Math, Unit 4, Numbers to 99. **Days 87 to 92.** Days by kind: understanding 2, fluency 1, application 2, check 1. A plan, not a lesson.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.NBT.B.2 and B.2a, b, c.** "Understand" the values of both places. Ten ones "can be thought of" as one bundle. Teens "are composed of" a ten and further ones. Whole-ten numerals "refer to" tens and zero ones. Use these relationships when opening a ten or making one again, below 100.
- **1.NBT.B.3.** "Compare" and record >, = and <. Compare the value after exchanging as needed. **1.NBT.C.5.** "Mentally find" one more or fewer ten and "explain" the reasoning. **1.NBT.C.6.** "Subtract" whole tens from whole tens, "relate" drawing and equation and "explain" a positive or zero difference.
- **MP.7, day 88.** Trace the ten singles that become one strip. The teacher page must show both directions of the trade, the conservation of quantity, and how a comparison can use regrouped amounts. The closing evidence draws on strategies already taught.

## 9. Supports and extension

Show that to exchange is to trade one form for another of the same amount. Review equal, the same number, and estimate, a sensible first guess before counting. Offer "I traded __ for __." Read aloud, enlarge pictures and use tactile cell lines or neutral scribing. Keep both private mental directions free of pictures and cues. Extend by finding another equal two-digit form and tracing which ten moved.
