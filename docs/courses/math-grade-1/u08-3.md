## Day 158

**Understanding.** I can explain why different clues name one number.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Mixed facts. Retrieve 82 with eight tens worth 80 and two ones worth 2. E157 papers went home.
- **10 min. Opening.** A clue is a hint the number has to fit. Show F. Trade ten loose ones for a ten-stick and connect the new grouping to the unchanged amount.
- **20 min. Practice.** Shade one clue at a time on a hundred chart. Include four tens with zero ones, 40. Put ten minutes here for E157 or 82 retrieval misses, by opening and rebuilding a marked stick. Keep chart and repair stock away from R1 and E158.
- **15 min. R1.** Hide the shared drawing. Everyone marks a trade and then gives a reason. Save the trade before reading the objection. Give up to six 45-second reason turns and retain paper or neutral dictation from everyone else.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** “What stayed the same in the trade?” Draw a mark from loose pieces to a new stick on separate stock.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E158. Shape: fix the faulty second picture with an exchange mark. Return the slip on 159.

## Day 160

**Application.** I can write a riddle and explain my learning.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Return E159. Mix familiar facts and a tens sketch.
- **15 min. Riddle writing.** Pick a private numeral card, for example 51. Draw or dictate clues for five tens and one one. Keep the answer on the back. Leave exit, product and response stock covered.
- **10 min. Partner testing.** Each partner gets a turn reading or showing clues and a turn testing the other riddle. Embed the five-minute G repair and use both marks to check a practice candidate.
- **5 min. R2 revision.** Return feedback to every child. File the revision with the original.
- **10 min. Record closure.** Children do familiar paper puzzles. Teacher closes from the presorted R1 and R2 record, thirty children at about twenty seconds each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E160. Shape: author two clues from the reserved unlabeled picture. Keep the original before any discussion. The riddle is unscored.
- **10 min. Response and handoff.** Four minutes for paired F/G repairs on familiar stock. Four minutes for fresh unscored pictures of five tens and three ones beside four tens and thirteen ones. “Mark the trade that makes these pictures match.” Save the marks. ## 9. Supports and extension

On 158, point to tens as joined groups of ten and ones as single pieces. Review zero from Kindergarten 2.4 as none, here no loose ones. Show a trade by crossing out ten singles and joining a stick of the same amount. Name a clue as a hint the number must fit. Use “Both clues fit because ___.” Read aloud and offer high contrast, tactile strips, a pointed mark or neutral dictation. Extend by finding a second clue pair for a practice number within 99.
