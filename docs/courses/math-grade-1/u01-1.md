# Section 1.1: Counting on keeps a group

Grade 1 Math. Unit 1, Adding, subtracting and working with data (days 1 to 16): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 1 to 6.** Days by kind: understanding 5, fluency 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.OA.C.5, this section's share.** "Relate counting to addition and subtraction": count on to add (days 1 to 3), count back to subtract (day 4), and count the steps when counting up to find what is left (days 4 to 6). The whole standard is met inside 10.
- **Limits that bite here.** Starts and totals are at most 10. Days 3 and 4 add or remove 1 or 2; the decks of days 5 and 6 go up to 3. No number line, no written algorithm, no story types, no make-ten. A child who starts from the larger number is accepted and not taught.
- **Connections.** None claimed. The warm-ups review the kindergarten skills in the ledger's words: count on from, one more, how many, facts within 5.
- **Strategies.** Counting strategies only; the standard sets no algorithm.
- **Practices.** MP.7, day 3: the child covers the first group and says why it stays counted.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The start is already counted, so it is held and never a step: "six (tap the cover), seven, eight". In counting back the first word after the start is the first thing taken away. When a child counts up to find what is left, the answer is how many steps were taken, not the word the counting stopped on.

## 2. Where it sits

This is the start of the unit and of grade 1. No readiness item is owned. Exit repairs go to the next day's Warm-up and repair; day 6 repairs go to day 7 (section 1.2).

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** none. The counting check C1 is owned by section 1.4; its facts (11 + 3 and 16 - 12) never appear here.

**Exit questions.** Each is read aloud, one ask at a time; every child marks a paper picture. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Counting words are written on the picture. Before class print each day's exit-NN.md at half-sheet size and keep key-exits.md teacher only. Timing for about 30 children: reading the two asks and marking takes 3 minutes of the 5; the rotating six are heard in the Synthesis, 45 seconds each (4.5 minutes), names recorded. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example (field 4). Each listed wrong answer carries one letter; an answer that matches no letter is described on the record (s01 field 6).

- **E1.** Picture: 5 counters, then 4 more with blank marks. (a) "Write a counting word on each added counter." (b) "Circle the total after four more." Key: 6, 7, 8, 9; total 9. Wrong: 5, 6, 7, 8 and total 8 (A); 1 to 9 written on every counter (C). Shape: practice uses real squares under a cover and spoken counts; no printed blank-mark sheets and no 5 + 4 or 4 + 5.
- **E2.** Picture: 2 dots and 5 dots. (a) "Write a plus expression for this picture." (b) "Mark a count that finds the total." Key: 2 + 5 (5 + 2 accepted); total 7. Any count that holds one group and counts on is accepted, from either group. Wrong: marks that count the start as a step, total 6 (A); every dot numbered 1 to 7 (C). Shape: practice pictures show counters in frames, never loose dots.
- **E3.** Card 8 + 1 beside a covered group. (a) "Mark the new counting word." (b) "Circle the total after one more." Key: 9; total 9. Wrong: 8 on the added counter and total 8 (A). Shape: the new added-counter word and total are marked on a reserved card; no 8 + 1 or 1 + 8 in any deck or warm-up. The former 7 + 2 case is in the kindergarten ledger, so data uses 8 + 1.
- **E4.** Picture: 9 dots with 2 crossed out. "Mark a count that shows how many are left." Key: 8, 7, so 7 are left; or counting up from 2, seven steps. Wrong: "nine, eight", 8 (A); 9, the last word of counting up (B). Shape: practice removes real counters, with no printed dot pictures; no 9 - 2.
- **E5.** Cards 5 + 3 and 8 - 1 beside two pile pictures, a forward arrow (count on) and a back arrow (count back). (a) "Draw a line from each card to the way you count it." Key: 5 + 3 to count on; 8 - 1 to count back. A swapped line matches no register letter and is described; it marks the pick not yet seen. (b) "Mark how you count 5 + 3." Key: 8 ("six, seven, eight"; from 3, "four, five, six, seven, eight" is also accepted). Wrong: 7, "five" counted as a step (A); every counter numbered 1 to 8 (C). Shape: practice sorts real cards onto mats and solves aloud with no marks; no 5 + 3, 3 + 5 or 8 - 1 in any deck or warm-up.
- **E6.** (a) "Mark how you count 6 + 3." Key: 9 (seven, eight, nine). Wrong: 8, "six" counted as a step (A). (b) "Mark how you count 7 - 2." Key: 5 (six, five). Wrong: 6, "seven, six" (A); 7, counted up from 2 and the last word given (B). Shape: the partner record holds an answer and a tick or retry, never method marks; no 6 + 3 or 7 - 2 in the deck.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Shown with one folded-paper puppet, and only after the right count is known: the right count first, then the puppet's wrong count beside it, and the teacher asks "Is the cover still counted?" These are invented for practice and said so aloud.

| | Wrong example beside a right one; day | If many made it |
|---|---|---|
| A | Day 3: 6 + 2, puppet says "six, seven", answers 7; right: "six (tap the cover), seven, eight", 8. Day 4: 10 - 2, puppet says "ten, nine", answers 9; right: "nine, eight", 8. | Next day Warm-up and repair, 5 min, whole class, in place of retrieval: tap the cover, say the start, one counter per new word (4 + 2, then 9 - 1). |
| B | Day 5 (own example; the register's is day 12), after day 4 has shown counting up right: 6 - 2 counted up from 2, puppet says "three, four, five, six" and answers 6; right: the same words are 4 steps, so 4 are left. | Day 5 Warm-up and repair, 5 min: one dot per step, then count the dots (5 - 3 up from 3: four, five; 2 dots). |
| C | Day 5: 4 + 4, puppet starts again at one; right: name 4, cover it, "five, six, seven, eight". | Day 6 Warm-up and repair, 5 min: name, cover, count on (4 + 3). |

Over a third (more than 10 of 30) is "many": the 5-minute Warm-up and repair is for the whole class and takes one letter, the earliest obstructing one; the other letters wait for the group. Fewer: a 5-minute teacher group during the next practice while the rest work. Day 6 misses go to day 7.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** The paper ten-frame (light). Day 1 it is tied to an expression, 3 + 2 written beside it. Day 2 it is tied to the written sum, 6 + 4 = 10. Day 3 adds step boxes, one per word after the start. Day 4 adds the back arrow. Day 6 children move counters, sketch, then count without the frame; the frame is restored for any child who needs it. It leaves the shared display on day 7 and returns in unit 2.
- **Key moment 1, day 3: the start stays counted.** Ideal: real counters under a card, step boxes beside them. Spec: the paper ten-frame with a cover card fits; no digital tool is needed.
- **Key moment 2, day 4: counting back or counting up gives the same answer.** Ideal: counters crossed out in order with the back arrow. Spec: paper fits.
- **A room with nothing.** Squares cut from scrap paper, one side marked with a pencil for the second color; a 2 by 5 frame drawn on a sheet or the board; a folded sheet as the cover; step boxes drawn in a row. Squares are larger than a small-parts tube, with no food or sharp edges, and an adult is present throughout.

## 6. Days

Every part names its turns: pairs work at once (15 pairs of about 30 children), the teacher hears the rotating six in the Synthesis (45 seconds each), and every child may pass in public talk. Credit line on each day's page only.

## Day 1

**Understanding.** I can keep a counted group in my head and count on to find the total.

- **5 min. Hook.** Four paper squares are counted aloud, covered by a card, then three more are set beside the cover. "How can we find how many without starting again?" Take a few answers; children may pass. Show: "four (tap the cover), five, six, seven."
- **5 min. Warm-up.** Retrieval of the kindergarten skills on a paper strip, five boxes, each read aloud once, no timer: write the next three numbers after 5 (6, 7, 8); draw one more than 3 dots (4); count five scattered squares and write how many (5); 2 + 2 (4); 4 - 1 (3). The teacher collects the strips and notes each row separately; a child who misses a row joins the day 2 group.
- **10 min. Model.** 3 + 2 on the frame: three counters of one color, tap, two of the other color, "three (hold), four, five". Write 3 + 2 beside the frame and name it: "This is a plus expression: three plus two." Partners copy it with their own squares on a paper frame, all pairs at once.
- **20 min. Counting collections.** Fifteen pairs, all at once. Each pair gets a bag of 6 to 10 small classroom objects or paper squares, counts the collection together, and shows how many on paper: a drawing, numbers or the frame. Pairs that finish trade bags and count again. The teacher moves between pairs and notes how each counts (one word per object, the last word as how many); no child is singled out.
- **10 min. Cover and add.** Pairs turn six cards, one partner shows the start and covers it, the other counts on aloud: 6 + 1 = 7, 3 + 3 = 6, 4 + 2 = 6, 2 + 3 = 5, 5 + 1 = 6, 7 + 1 = 8.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six say the total of a new covered pile and how they found it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E1: s01-X1a, s01-X1b.
- **Carried and tool.** The teacher holds the frame in the model; children hold their own copy. Ten-frame (light): show why, 10 min; practice with feedback, 20 min.
- **By hand.** Squares, frames, cover cards, pencils. Credit: adapted from IM G1 U1 L1, Count and Add.

## Day 2

**Understanding.** I can write a plus expression for a picture and find the total.

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 1 exit repair, one letter, if many missed; otherwise retrieval from the pool in field 8.
- **10 min. Model.** 6 + 4 = 10 on a full frame, counted on: "six (hold), seven, eight, nine, ten"; the sum is written beside the frame. Then 2 + 4 = 6: "two (hold), three, four, five, six".
- **25 min. Match and record.** Pairs get six picture cards of counters in frames, match each to its frame, write the expression and sum and count on: 5 + 1 = 6, 3 + 3 = 6, 7 + 3 = 10, 1 + 6 = 7, 4 + 2 = 6, 8 + 2 = 10. For up to 10 of these minutes the teacher takes a group who did not hold the day 1 retrieval; the rest work in pairs.
- **10 min. Compare counts.** Two pairs set cards beside each other and tell how each count went (3 + 3 and 4 + 2 both end at 6). Three volunteers share, about 45 seconds each; others may pass.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six name the expression for a pictured frame.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E2: s01-X2a, s01-X2b.
- **Carried and tool.** Children hold the written sum beside the frame. Ten-frame (light): show why, 10 min; practice with feedback, 25 min.
- **By hand.** Picture cards, pencils. Credit: adapted from IM G1 U1 L2, Explore Expressions and Sums.

## Day 3

**Understanding.** I can count on one or two more, one word for each step.

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 2 exit repair, or retrieval.
- **10 min. Model.** Step boxes, one per word after the start. 5 + 1: "five (hold), six", one box. 8 + 2: "eight (hold), nine, ten", two boxes. Then the puppet pair: 6 + 2 as "six, seven" beside "six (tap the cover), seven, eight" (error A).
- **25 min. Covered-group addition.** Pairs cover the start, add 1 or 2, complete a supplied first step in the boxes, then count on aloud, then uncover to check: 3 + 1 = 4, 6 + 1 = 7, 9 + 1 = 10, 2 + 1 = 3, 3 + 2 = 5, 4 + 2 = 6, 6 + 1 = 7, 1 + 2 = 3. Each pair plays eight cards, partners alternating.
- **10 min. Explain the kept group (MP.7).** Pairs answer "What have we already counted?" with the frame "I kept __ and counted __ more." Three volunteers share, about 45 seconds each.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E3: s01-X3a, s01-X3b.
- **Carried and tool.** Children hold the cover and the step boxes. Ten-frame (light): show why, 10 min; practice with feedback, 25 min.

## Day 4

**Understanding.** I can find how many are left when one or two are taken away.

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 3 exit repair (error A), or retrieval.
- **10 min. Model.** The minus sign and the back arrow. 6 - 1, read "six take away one": "six (hold), five", 5 left. 10 - 2: "ten (hold), nine, eight", 8 left. Counting up, shown right: 6 - 2 from 2, "three, four, five, six", four steps, so 4 are left. Puppet pair: 10 - 2 as "ten, nine" beside "nine, eight" (A).
- **25 min. Remove and say.** Pairs remove 1 or 2 counters from a frame and say what is left: 10 - 1 = 9, 5 - 1 = 4, 7 - 1 = 6, 5 - 2 = 3, 4 - 2 = 2, 3 - 1 = 2, 9 - 1 = 8, 8 - 2 = 6.
- **10 min. Compare drawings.** Pairs set their sketches beside each other and ask "Does the last word tell what is left, or the steps?" Three volunteers share.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E4: s01-X4.
- **Carried and tool.** Children hold the back arrow. Ten-frame (light): show why, 10 min; practice with feedback, 25 min.

## Day 5

**Understanding.** I can choose to count on or count back.

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 4 exit repair (A or B, one letter), or retrieval.
- **10 min. Model.** Choose a method. 7 + 3: "seven (hold), eight, nine, ten". 10 - 3: "ten (hold), nine, eight, seven". Puppet pairs: 4 + 4 started again at one beside "four (tap), five, six, seven, eight" (C); 6 - 2 counted up and answered 6 beside the same words as 4 steps (B).
- **25 min. Sort and solve.** Pairs sort eight cards into a count-on pile and a count-back pile, then solve each aloud: 6 + 1 = 7, 9 + 1 = 10, 7 - 1 = 6, 4 + 3 = 7, 8 + 2 = 10, 6 - 1 = 5, 5 - 2 = 3, 10 - 1 = 9. The teacher takes a 5-minute group for any day 4 repair left over.
- **10 min. Compare methods.** Pairs say which pile a card went to and how they know.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E5: s01-X5a, s01-X5b.
- **Carried and tool.** Children choose the frame, step boxes or the arrow. Ten-frame (light): practice with feedback, 25 min.

## Day 6

**Fluency.** I can practice counting on and counting back with a partner and check with counters.

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 5 exit repair (error C), or retrieval.
- **5 min. Recall the rules.** Say together: keep the start, count on to add, count back to take away.
- **30 min. Partner rounds.** Fifteen pairs. Cards are turned, answered, checked with counters, and retried if missed: 5 + 1 = 6, 3 + 2 = 5, 8 + 2 = 10, 4 + 2 = 6, 9 + 1 = 10, 7 + 3 = 10, 10 - 2 = 8, 6 - 1 = 5, 5 - 2 = 3, 9 - 1 = 8, 4 - 2 = 2, 10 - 3 = 7. Children first try without the frame; it comes back for any child who asks. At 12 cards in 30 minutes each pair has about 2.5 minutes a card. The record holds the answer and a tick or a retry mark.
- **10 min. Retry missed cards.** Each pair retries only its marked cards, the other partner checking with counters. The teacher takes a group.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E6: s01-X6a, s01-X6b.
- **Carried and tool.** The frame is optional from today and leaves the shared display tomorrow. Ten-frame (light): practice with feedback, 30 min.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 3, "What have we already counted?" Day 4, "Does the last word tell what is left, or the steps?"
- Written task, day 5: on the exit marks, the child writes the counting words for 5 + 3 (E5 b).
- Judged by the teacher with two look-fors: the start is held (not written as a step), and there is one word per step.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Five minutes daily, spoken, no timer. Pool: count on 9 to 13 and 14 to 17; one more than 8 and than 15; 1 + 3, 2 + 2, 5 - 1, 5 - 3, 3 - 2. Never 4 + 1. No exit fact appears in a warm-up.
- **Fluency target.** None; the standard sets none.
- **How fluency is built without time pressure.** Day 6 partner rounds: answer, check with counters, retry, with no clock and no scores; each child compares only with their own last record.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: count on, count back, keep, cover, step, plus expression; total and altogether are review from kindergarten. Frame: "I kept __ and counted __ more."
- **Access.** Large outlines, tactile paper mats, an adult scribe; a child may point instead of writing counting words, and need not move fingers.
- **Check accommodations.** The stem is read aloud and may be repeated; counters may be moved before marks are made; a scribe writes the child's words.
- **Reading load.** None. Every stem is read aloud and the pages hold pictures and boxes.
- **Extension, same standard.** The child makes a cover-and-add card with a start up to 7 and an added group up to 3, trades it, and explains both counts.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** A 35-minute path: warm-up 5, model 5, practice 15, talk 5, exit 5. The grown-up is the partner and hears the child's counts.
- **One projector.** It may show the frame; every child works on paper. The hook uses real squares and needs no screen.
