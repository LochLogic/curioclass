## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What makes a shape that shape, and what time is it?"
- **Hook, day 129:** A cube, cylinder and cone have been mixed into two groups. What rule could explain the groups? Children suggest another sort, then hear the compelling question. Adapt the sorting opening named in field 10.
- **Supporting questions:** Which features describe a solid (129)? What stays true when a triangle turns (133)? Can a whole construction become one piece (135)? What makes shares equal (137)? Where does each hand go at half past (142)?
- **Closing claim:** "I can explain shape attributes, reuse a composite, partition equal shares and read and write hours and half-hours." Records R1, R2, R3 and R4, with short supplements S1 and S2, establish this on day 146.
- **Teaching:** Model feature language after children sort, then release descriptions on 132. Demonstrate preserving a composite before independent construction. Overlay equal shares before naming them. Move both clock hands together before releasing clock drawings. Build a specified triangle from three paper strips on 133 and a specified rectangle from paper strips on 134, so the day 135 build is not its first practice. Draw analog hands for hours on 141, for half-hours from words on 142, and for both times from digital times on 144. Teacher pages explain square corners, squares as rectangles, equal area rather than piece count, and continuous hour-hand movement.
