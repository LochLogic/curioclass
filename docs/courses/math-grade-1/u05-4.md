Grade 1 Math. Unit 5, Adding within 100: see [s05.md](s05.md). **Days 108 to 110.** Days by kind: fluency 1, application 1, check 1. A plan, not a lesson. Read with [course defaults](DEFAULTS.md).

## 1. Standards and practices

**1.NBT.C.4.** "Add within 100," "relate the strategy to a written method" and "explain the reasoning used." Show two-digit plus one-digit, two-digit plus whole tens, and two-digit pairs, with and without an exchange where possible. Use two addends and show 100 as ten tens. Use 1.NBT.B.2 on day 108 to identify the formed unit. MP.7, day 109: match the parts of a book inventory to the contribution of each place.

The teacher page distinguishes finding a sum, representing the quantities and explaining the written steps. Accept place value, properties or a valid related addition/subtraction method. The closing window uses methods already taught.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment item owned:** the sole day 110 row in field 5, including P1 to P5 and F. Section 5.4 collects and closes this record. Give everyone an individual paper response area for each item. Read each stem aloud as a single ask. An annotated picture can include the equations and the reason in the same record. Keep cases, reversals, response sheets and wrong records out of demonstrations, rounds and repairs. Read any judged exit filed as practice and quote a changed data stem in this field.

**E108, 1.NBT.C.4.** Give a start card labeled 49 and a separate collection of 23 with a loose square shown beginning to move from that collection. Read the first move aloud as a statement. "How does that move help you show 49 plus 23?" Key: 49 + 1 = 50, 23 = 1 + 22 and 50 + 22 = 72. Mark the moved one once in the filled ten, with two tens and two singles still coming. Wrong: 62 after dropping the newly formed ten (A), or 82 after counting the completed group as well as its replacement (B).
Shape: finish a partly made move and account for the remaining addend. Practice checks completed labels. Keep the retired six-tens/twelve-singles choice picture and the 49-square scatter out.

**E109, 1.NBT.C.4.** Title the slip "Invented for practice." Read, "Mina counts 38 books on one shelf and 29 on another. How many books are on the two shelves together?" Give an outline of two shelves with the two source labels only. Mark the shelves and record the method in one space. Key: 30 + 20 = 50, 8 + 9 = 17, 50 + 17 = 67 books. Marks link the new strip to ten of the seventeen loose ones. Wrong: 57 after dropping the ten (A), or 77 after counting it twice (B).
Shape: generate the total for two shelves from a story and support it with marks on an uncompleted shelf record. Practice audits a supplied total for a running cart inventory. Reserve the shelf layout and the independent record.

Day 110 uses F and the retained Addition record, with no extra exit.

**P1, 1.NBT.C.4, day 108.** "How would you show your way to add 54 and 3?" Key: 4 + 3 = 7, 54 + 3 = 57. Five tens unchanged. Wrong: 12, counting the five sticks as singles (D).
Shape: independently document an unchanged tens group beside a written singles method. Keep this blank independent panel off practice cards; practice verifies completed labels.

**P2, 1.NBT.C.4, day 108.** "How would you show your way to add 36 and 20?" Key: 30 + 20 = 50, 50 + 6 = 56. Preserve six ones. Wrong: 38 from adding two singles (C).
Shape: independently mark the addition of whole tens and the preservation of ones in a blank record. Practice starts with supplied pieces and checks their movement.

**P3, 1.NBT.C.4, day 108.** "How would you show your way to add 48 and 5?" Key: 48 + 2 = 50, 50 + 3 = 53. Circle ten of the thirteen ones for the trade. Wrong: 43 after losing the filled ten (A).
Shape: independently generate a written single-digit trade with a drawn trace of ten. Keep the blank trade record and its circled completed version off cards; practice repairs already supplied stages.

**P4, 1.NBT.C.4, day 108.** "How would you show your way to add 23 and 34?" Key: 20 + 30 = 50, 3 + 4 = 7, 50 + 7 = 57. Both like-unit groups accounted for. Wrong: 12 from counting each strip as one (D).
Shape: record both like-unit groups with no exchange on an independent page. Practice sorts supplies or checks a completed drawing; keep packet facsimiles out.

**P5, 1.NBT.C.4, day 108, with one private oral link on day 108 or 109.** "How would you show your way to add 56 and 27?" On the paper key, 50 + 20 = 70, 6 + 7 = 13, 70 + 13 = 83. Seven old sticks join one new stick and three ones remain. Wrong: 73 after dropping ten (A), 93 after leaving both copies (B), or 56 + 27 = 70 = 83 (E).
Shape: independently generate the full two-digit exchange record for a later private link. Keep the blank packet and independent record format off practice paper.

**P5-link, 1.NBT.C.4, private evidence for P5.** In the child's one oral turn ask, "How does your writing show the ten you made?" Accept any link that locates the trade in the child-made drawing and links it to a true step. Sample: "I made a ten from the thirteen ones. That's the ten in 70 plus 13." Sample for adding on: "I used four of the seven to get to eighty. The rest are the three that make eighty-three," with 56 + 20 = 76, 76 + 4 = 80 and 80 + 3 = 83. Wrong: says the writing counts the new stick and also keeps all thirteen loose ones (B).
Shape: explain one exchange-to-equation link in the original P5 record. No interview rehearsal or practice packet.

**F, 1.NBT.C.4, day 110.** "How would you show your way to add 58 and 17?" Give a blank mat. Key: 50 + 10 = 60, 8 + 7 = 15, 60 + 15 = 75. One new ten joins six tens and five ones stay loose. Accept any true linked record with the same model-to-writing reasoning. Another sample: "68 plus 2 gets to 70, then 5 more gets to 75," with 58 + 10 = 68 and the two moved ones accounted for. Wrong: 65 after dropping ten (A), or 58 + 17 = 60 = 75 (E).
Shape: fresh independent transfer on a blank mat, with child-chosen reasoning throughout. Practice audits supplied quantities or records. Supply neither partial steps nor a worked display.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

The ideal instrument is a private paper record with room to track both kinds of units and a dated copy of revisions. Blank marked-unit mats (light) fit the evidence. Keep shared models covered throughout independent work. In practice, replace towers and singles with adult-precut strips and squares, or draw strips and dots. Draw the two shelves when art is missing and handwrite cards when needed. Keep loose pieces out of mouths and count them during the last practice turn. No key tool is needed.

Grade 1 section 4.5's retained-record assembly is planned only. Demonstrate filing the original, dated feedback and any revised work with a separate practice sheet. Review kindergarten section 4.1 day 64 **altogether**, how many in both groups.

## Day 109

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 108, "Which pieces changed form?" Day 109, "What does your total count?" On day 109 annotate a checked inventory label. Look for units kept apart in the steps, with a link from the quantities or the traded group to the written method.

## 8. Practice that returns

Mix familiar within-ten addition and subtraction with the day's named retrieval. Repeat taught card rounds on day 108, with untimed feedback and retries. The familiar paper work on day 110 reinforces matching models and captions. No standard here sets a fluency target.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review exchange, equals and altogether with pictures. "My new ten is __ in my picture and __ in my writing." Read aloud, enlarge or raise picture cells, and accept a neutral scribe, directed mark or signed link. Use the same support during the record and F. Extend by comparing two taught-type additions with the same total, one with an exchange.

## 10. Two rooms

For one child, use the eighteen-minute record and one private P5 turn on day 108. Give feedback for a short revision on day 109. On day 110 use five minutes for directions and return, eight for F and five for closure. Use one screen for practice only.
