# Section 3.6: Choose a subtraction strategy and close the record

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-grade-1/3/s/3.6), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-grade-1 u03-6.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 1 Math. Unit 3, Adding and subtracting within 20 (days 40 to 68), [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 64 to 68.** Days by kind: understanding 1, fluency 1, application 2, check 1. A plan, not a lesson. Tasks are ours, untested.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.OA.C.6.** “Add and subtract” within twenty using ten, a related sum or difference, or a count from the smaller quantity. “Demonstrate fluency” within ten by recall or an efficient mental method.
- **1.OA.A.2 and 1.OA.B.3.** “Solve word problems” with three whole-number addends totaling at most twenty. “Apply properties” to rearrange a sum and preserve every group.
- **1.NBT.B.2a and 1.NBT.B.2b.** Ten “can be thought of as a bundle of ten ones.” Teens “are composed of a ten” and further ones.
- **~1.OA.A.1 and 1.OA.D.8.** “Use addition and subtraction” in stories with unknown quantities. Keep difficult story starts supported and unscored. “Determine” an unknown number in each of the six bare-equation positions already taught in unit 2.
- **Connection.** 1.OA.C.5, “Relate counting” to subtraction in S64. **Practice.** MP.4 on day 67, locate the three groups and the unknown in the scene, picture and equation.
- **Teacher explanation.** Method choice depends on the numbers. In a take away story the blank names the quantity the question leaves missing, and restoring the pieces that left can recover a start.

## 3. Checks, written first

Read each question aloud. Every child marks an individual picture. Say and print “invented for practice” on fictional scenes. Use shown, shown with help or not yet seen. A bare wrong numeral gets a descriptive note if the work gives no evidence of a register error.

- **E64 (1.OA.C.6).** Show eighteen pieces and the removal target nine. Offer the familiar double, ten and count-up icons. “How would you find eighteen take away nine?” Key: mark a suitable way and show nine, for example split the eighteen into two equal nines and connect 9 + 9 = 18 to 18 − 9 = 9. Accept another correct method with matching marks. Wrong: claims ten steps by including nine (A), or keeps eight when copying the result of the supplied fact in S64 for a different start (H).  
  Shape: select and justify a strategy on an unworked picture. Practice checks the accuracy of supplied methods.
- **E65a (~1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.8, 1.OA.C.6).** Show a finished display of twenty paper kites. Rin has just put up a bunch with two striped kites and three plain ones. Show the bunch of arriving kites in the addition position, with an empty box for the start and 20 on the other side of the equal sign. “How many kites did Rin have up before the bunch arrived?” Key: fifteen. Mark the original group or put fifteen in the first box. Wrong: writes sixteen after counting five as the first new step up to twenty (A). Record any help. This difficult story stays unscored.  
  Shape: Find the start after identifying which pieces arrived together. Practice fixes supplied labels on acted scenes. Reserve the twenty-kite display and the two-part arrival.
- **E65b (~1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.8, 1.OA.C.6).** On a separate take away picture, read “Rin had twenty paper wheels. She put some away. One stayed. How many wheels did she put away?” Give 20 − □ = 1 and space to mark the group that left. Key: nineteen in the middle box or nineteen departures marked. Wrong: writes one by using the survivor as the change (F).  
  Shape: Find the missing change and show which quantity belongs in its box. Practice fixes a supplied story label.
- **E66a (1.OA.C.6).** Provide a paper copy of two flaps that were given the same answer, twelve. Read the first flap, 18 − 18, with a picture of eighteen and room to change it. “What marks fix this answer?” Key: cross out all eighteen once and record zero. Wrong: says seventeen because the child uses the result of taking only one away (H).  
  Shape: correct a shared answer label with a removal picture. Practice checks one supplied move at a time.
- **E66b (1.OA.C.6).** On the other flap, read 17 + 0. Provide seventeen pictured marks and room for additions. “How would you show the score on this flap?” Key: leave all seventeen and show no additions, putting seventeen on the corrected label. Wrong: keeps the previous sixteen from 16 + 0 despite the extra mark in the start (H).  
  Shape: independently repair the same incorrect label using the pictured zero change. Keep shared-answer flaps out of partner rounds.
- **E67 (1.OA.A.2, 1.OA.B.3, 1.NBT.B.2a, 1.NBT.B.2b, 1.OA.C.6).** Give a blank three-scene strip and read 3 + 6 + 7 = □. “What story could go with this sum?” Key: any invented story with one actor and all three groups joined. For example, Kit puts three shells, six pebbles and seven seeds on a tray. Show the total box on the strip. Accept a drawing or dictation that gives sixteen, with three and seven circled for the ten and six outside. Wrong: ends at nine after dropping the seven (D), or ten after dropping the six (D).  
  Shape: Invent a whole story from a bare equation. Practice matches supplied fragments to groups. S67 starts with a different, fully specified tile story on labeled mats.
