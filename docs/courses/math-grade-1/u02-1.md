## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.OA.A.1.** “Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems” about joining or removing, with the result or change missing. Unit 6 completes story coverage.
- **1.OA.D.7.** “Understand” the equal sign. Day 18 connects it to the same quantity on both sides, including with the result first.
- **1.OA.D.8.** “Determine” the unknown whole number. Days 19 and 20 place the box at the change.
- **Connection.** 1.OA.C.5 on days 17 to 21: keep the counted start and track only later moves. MP.4 on day 17: connect each term of a paper expression to an acted story.
- **Teacher explanation.** The first count names the group already there. Each later count matches one moved piece. A result names the group at the end. A change names what joined or left.

**Exit questions**

- **E17 (1.OA.A.1).** Ari sets twelve paper kites on a mat and adds seven. Leave room for paper pieces and a sketch. “What expression shows Ari's move?” Children act the move on their papers and label it. Key: 12 + 7 (also 7 + 12 with the groups identified), nineteen in the acted picture. Wrong: 12 + 7 with eighteen pictured, counting twelve as the first new step (H).
  Shape: Produce an expression from an independently acted scene. In practice show the move or supply the sign first.
- **E18 (1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.7).** On the paper branch, fourteen birds are there first and three join. Print 17 = 14 + 3. “How does this equation tell the story?” Children show the links with marks. Key: 14 links to the start, 3 to the arrivals, + to joining, 17 to the whole and = to the shared quantity seventeen. Wrong: crosses out the equation because seventeen comes first (A).
  Shape: Interpret a complete total-first equation on a new picture. Practice starts with actions and builds the equation piece by piece.
- **E19 (1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.8).** Jo has eleven paper stars on display. Jo puts up more. Now there are nineteen. Give start and end panels and an empty arrivals panel, with 11 + □ = 19. “What goes in the box?” Mark the group in the empty panel. Key: eight stars. Wrong: nineteen copied from the ending group (C), or nine new marks including the spoken starting number (H).
  Shape: Reconstruct an unshown arriving group from pictured states. Practice moves pieces into a frame.
- **E20 (1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.8).** Sam has eighteen paper flags. Sam takes some down. Twelve stay up. Show before and after rows on separate panels with a blank change patch. “What goes on the patch?” Mark the missing quantity on paper. Key: six flags taken down, 18 − 6 = 12. Wrong: twelve copied from the row that stays up (C).
  Shape: Infer and mark a removal change between static states. Practice uses a live action and a movable label.
- **E21 (1.OA.A.1).** Print 16 + 2 = 18 and give a blank scene frame. “What joining story fits this equation?” Children draw or act on paper and may tell their partner. Key: any single-actor joining story with sixteen at first, two joining and eighteen afterward. Example: Kit puts sixteen bells on a tray and adds two. Wrong: a scene with sixteen bells losing two and ending with eighteen by adding the departing bells (I).
  Shape: Invent an entire scene from a supplied equation. Practice finishes a teacher-started scene and checks a partner answer with pieces.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

| Letter | Wrong next to right, with first day | If many made it |
|---|---|---|
| A | Day 18: puppet rejects 7 = 5 + 2. Draw five and two opposite seven and read the true equation. | Read both ways and match quantities in the next Practice return, 5 min. |
| C | Day 20: in 7 + □ = 17 the puppet puts 17. Ten new pieces fit the box. | Keep the start still and circle just the moved group in the next Practice return, 5 min. |
| H | Day 19: 2 + 3 shown as 4 when the puppet includes two as a step. Track three new moves to get 5. | Touch one new piece per new word in the next Practice return, 5 min. |
| I | Day 18: six on a shelf and one removed, puppet moves one in and says seven. Cross out one and count five. | Cross out only departing marks in the next Practice return, 5 min. |

Show the right example before the puppet's wrong version. If more than a third miss, use the next listed slot for repair plus one fresh paper retry. With fewer, pull a small group. If two letters occur, run mats in parallel with picture cues. Keep the ten-minute day 19 retrieval repair separate.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** Review kindergarten's joined 5-frames, with one five-cell row full before overflow, as a ten-frame record, with a first and second frame when needed. Children hold the marks. Tie the start and moves to expressions on day 17 and to equations on day 18. The shared frame is withdrawn on day 33.
- **Day 18 instrument.** Ideal: equal-sized movable pieces and two paper workspaces to compare the quantities named on the two sides. Paper frames fit. The teacher can move each piece while all children see which symbol it represents.
- **Day 20 instrument.** Ideal: a movable change marker across start and end views. A folded paper flap fits and keeps the moved group separate.
- **No-kit path.** Draw frames and write cards and recording sheets on scrap paper. Replace two-color counters with patterned paper squares, the folded-paper puppet with a sketched puppet face, and story props with drawn symbols. Digital tool: None.

## 6. Days

Use fifteen simultaneous pairs, with both roles and at least one marked paper from each child. Each five-minute Synthesis hears six named children at 45 seconds each on the preceding work. Record names and help. Every child then marks the exit in parallel. Collect all papers and return feedback in the next named slot. A day can run with hand-drawn cards throughout.

## Day 17

**Application.** I can show what joined or left. **Carried:** Children hold their own paper representations and pieces. **Digital tool:** None.

- **5 min. Library hook.** Use separate scenes with one actor each. A reader adds one to four books (five on the shelf). A reader takes one from five (four left). Ask what changed before introducing the unit question.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** On separate mats, show five birds gaining two (5 + 2, seven) and six shelf tabs losing one (6 − 1, five). Check action, expression and count from five (six, seven) on each child's paper. Hear retells from the rotating six during Synthesis.
- **5 min. Read twice.** Read a short workroom picture story twice. On the second read point to who, how many and what happened. Children point along on paper.
- **10 min. Model expressions.** Move the pieces first. Draw the start and movement, then attach the supplied sign to the move. Use the hook's books to show how a picture remembers the action.
- **15 min. Practice.** Pairs alternate acting an adult-supplied move and labeling a sketch with the given sign. Each hands back one marked sketch.
- **10 min. Unit 1 response centers.** First five: return days 15 and 16 papers, including unfinished E15c. Repair one priority unit 1 A, B or C in a teacher group while peers use partner marks. Second five: fresh transfer. Schedule a separate sitting for a full absent-child D2. Keep any partial pages.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear today's six on the retrieval retell, 45 seconds each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E17, paper.

- **Shape:** Fresh acted story to expression. Show the movement or supply the sign in practice.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 18: “What does equals say?” Day 20: “Which group changed?” On day 20 draw an action and put a numeral or one word by the moved group. Look for an unchanged start mark, the correct moved amount and a label attached to that group.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Review the meanings from K u04-4 day 78. An expression is numbers and a sign naming a quantity. Plus means put together. Minus means take away. An equation is a statement that two quantities are the same. Equals means is the same quantity as. Review K u04-3 change, what joins or leaves a group. Use Partner turns from K u01-1: one handles or counts, one watches, then exchange.
- **Unit 1 review, planned only.** See u01-1 for count on, count back, step, ten-frame, the folded-paper puppet and the partner and paper-exit routines. Re-establish their moves with the new drawings. The u01-4 C1 counting card (11 + 3 and 16 − 12) stays in the returned packet. Recall Rin moving badges from K u04-4 as the same single-actor action. Keep Rin's badge pictures outside new evidence.
- **Fluency target.** The owned standards set no fluency target. Rehearse taught counting and facts within ten, with immediate feedback and a retry on day 21. Work at the child's pace.

## 9. Supports and extension

Say and show start (what is there first) and review change. Show left (what stays after some go) with fresh removal pictures. Teach the missing number as how many the box stands for. Use “I started with __.” Read every prompt and label aloud. Use raised marks or large outlined panels, and let a child point or direct a scribe to mark their paper. Record mathematical help. Invite children to depict a different classroom object for the same action and expression.

## 10. Two rooms

Use 30 minutes with one child: retrieval or hook 5, model 5, paper practice 10, discussion 5 and exit 5. The adult trades roles with the child. Repeat a needed repair in place of extra practice. One projector may enlarge the picture. Give everyone an individual copy or hand-drawn equivalent.
