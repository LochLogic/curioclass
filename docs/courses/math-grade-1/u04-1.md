## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.NBT.B.2, B.2a and B.2c.** "Understand" the two digits of whole-ten numerals. Ten ones "can be thought of" as one bundle. Numerals 10 through 90 in steps of ten "refer to" one through nine tens and zero ones.
- **1.NBT.C.5, day 71.** "Mentally find" one more or fewer ten from a whole-ten start and "explain" the change. Stay below 100.
- **1.NBT.C.6, days 71 to 73.** "Subtract" a multiple of ten from another in 10 to 90, "relate" the drawing to the equation and "explain" the difference, including zero. Use place value and related addition. Keep taught 1.OA.C.6 strategies in day 73 practice.
- **MP.7, day 72.** Match the number of crossed-out towers to the tens removed. Explain why the counted unit stays ten even when the result is zero. The teacher connects unit counts to object counts before asking for a numeral or equation.

## 3. Checks, written first

Read the stems aloud. Each child keeps a paper answer. The six named for that day explain one marked part privately. When two panels are given, hear three children on each. Record only the direction and reason heard. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen, and apply a letter only where the work or explanation shows that error. Use invented counts and tell children so. Keep the reserved totals, number families, pictures and sample mistakes out of teaching, cards and repairs. For open-ended collection and card rounds use the worked practice quantities named in field 6.

**Unit assessment items owned:** field 5 rows 2 (P decade) and 5 (P differences and Fresh trace).

- **P decade.** Read “How would you draw eighty so I can see what the zero means?” Key: eight drawn tens, zero leftover ones for 80. Wrong: eight ones for the eight rods (A).
- **P differences.** Give a blank picture-and-equation panel for each problem. Read the panels separately. “How would your drawing explain ninety take away thirty?” Key: 90 - 30 = 60. Wrong: six ones from counting rods as singles (A). Then ask “How would your drawing explain fifty take away fifty?” Key: 50 - 50 = 0. Wrong: keeps fifty after subtracting all fifty (F).
- **Fresh subtraction trace.** Read “How would your drawing explain eighty take away thirty?” Leave a line for the equation. Key: 80 - 30 = 50, five tens. Wrong: five ones from counting rods as singles (A).

**E69, 1.NBT.B.2 and 1.NBT.B.2c.** Fresh scatter of 70 ungrouped squares with a blank total tag. "How would you show the count on this tag using groups of ten?" Key: seven groups of ten, total 70. Wrong: 7 from counting each group as one one (A), or 61 from counting the last group as one one (A).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Review kindergarten u01-1 Connecting cubes and u06-2 ten ones and further ones. The unit 3 section 3.2 model Full frame to tower to drawn ten-stick is planned only. Rebuild it from loose pieces before connecting the tower to the numeral on day 69. Show a ten as one complete group and say tens for more than one group. Partners move pieces and each child holds a private paper record.

Ideal for day 69: a full tower that opens to show all ten equal pieces. Ideal for day 72: marked strips children can cross out with the original group visible. Cubes and paper fit; use no digital spec or key tool. Ten-cell strips and pencil dots replace cubes and counters, drawn trays replace bins, and handwritten slips replace cards. Use large pieces on trays. Keep them out of mouths. An adult counts them back in during the last partner turn. The whole class can use paper.

## Day 69

**Understanding.** I can count a collection in tens. Base-ten drawing (light), Model, show why, 10 min.

- **5 min. Hook.** Put forty loose paper squares on the class mat. Ask how to avoid losing count, then point to the two written marks in 40 as you ask what each tells us.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** On the retrieval picture of twelve squares, circle ten and mark the two further ones. Log a miss for day 70. Use three minutes for the picture and two for familiar within-ten facts.
- **10 min. Unit 3 centers.** Return all closing papers and E67. Repair for five, then retry for five. If a group was lost, Rin has three paper brushes, three pens and eight chalk sticks, fourteen in all. Mark each group once. Print and say "Invented for practice."
- **10 min. Model.** Make four connecting-cube towers, draw the marked sticks and tie them to 40. Unfold the separate sticks in A to check what each is worth.
- **20 min. Group and compare.** Pairs count a movable collection by ones and again by tens. Both keep a tally of which pieces were counted.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Point from each bundle to its numeral contribution. Say the total in tens and in ones.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E69. Hear children 25 to 30.

## 9. Supports and extension

Say "One ten is ten ones" as the child unfolds a group. Pair decade words with drawings; offer "__ tens are __ ones." The teacher reads all words. Enlarge cell lines, supply tactile group boundaries, or mark the child's indicated place. For extension, show the same positive tens difference with a removal and a related addition.

## 10. Two rooms

One child and an adult alternate roles, keep the child's paper and revisit one correction before the next attempt. Project one practice picture if available. Children mark their own sheets.
