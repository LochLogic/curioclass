**Unit assessment items owned:** Section 2.1 owns O, Q, N and S. Assemble on day 39. Give written feedback before each revision and keep both original and revised marks. Mark each component shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Oa.** With the child's own S30 open, read: “How does eight plus something help find twelve minus eight?” Key: the four unmatched brushes are the missing addend in 8 + 4 = 12 and the difference in 12 − 8 = 4. Wrong: names twelve, the larger count, as the difference (E), or adds twelve and eight and gives twenty as missing (D).
  Shape: One explanation on a dated child-made comparison page. Keep this conference pairing of page and prompt out of practice.
- **Ob.** Point to an equation on the same page. “What does the equal sign tell us here?” Key: both sides show the same amount. For example, eight and four show twelve, the same as twelve. Wrong: “It means the answer comes next” (A). Record the child's words or indicated marks.
  Shape: Explain equality from the child's completed related pair. Practice traces a teacher-supplied relationship on another case. Pre-open pages and time Oa and Ob at about thirty seconds each. Show spaces for the child to mark the unmatched group and the value on each side.

  Shape: Six bare unknown placements with pictures on the retained strip. Closing puzzles use previously taught cards.

**Exit questions**

- **E37 (1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.8).** Print 13 + 1 = □ above a blank acting picture. “What small story makes this fact true?” Key: a single actor starts with thirteen, adds one and the picture marks fourteen. For example, Tia adds one ticket to the thirteen on her tray. Accept any such scene. Wrong: thirteen pictured after counting the start as the added step (H).
  Shape: Generate a story that completes a new small fact. Practice checks an oral, taught card with loose pieces and no invented story stem.
- **E38 (1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.B.4, 1.OA.D.7, 1.OA.D.8).** Print a pretend three-category display: fourteen triangle choices, four circle choices and two square choices. Give one paper link from the difference to the boxes in 4 + □ = 14, 14 − 4 = □ and 14 = 4 + □. Read each equation. “What difference fits all three boxes?” Children ring the extra in the picture and transfer its value across the linked boxes. Key: ten unmatched triangles, ten in every box. Both sides match in each equation. Wrong: fourteen, circling the larger group (E), or crosses out the total-first equality because fourteen comes first (A).
  Shape: Select two relevant categories and transfer one marked extra to three layouts. Practice fixes labels or traces one supplied relation on a different display.

## Day 38

## Day 39

**Check.** I can show my story and equation work in one record. **Carried:** Each child holds the retained paper record. **Digital tool:** None.

- **5 min. Settle.** Distribute retained pages, strips and the record sheet.
- **5 min. Return O.** Half B revises from written feedback. Half A opens its packet.
- **10 min. Final revisions.** Children independently revise existing booklet pages or strips.
- **5 min. Numerical check.** Read the key aloud with pencils down. Mark provisional groups from hands up by item.
- **25 min. Familiar puzzles.** Children solve earlier paper equation puzzles without scoring while the teacher assembles O, Q, N and S.
- **10 min. Close.** Return to the unit question. Score the retained evidence after class, allowing about forty teacher minutes.
