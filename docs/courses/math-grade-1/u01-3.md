# Section 1.3: Questions and claims from data

Grade 1 Math. Unit 1, Adding, subtracting and working with data (days 1 to 16): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 11 to 13.** Days by kind: understanding 1, application 2. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.MD.C.4, this section's share.** "Organize, represent, and interpret data with up to three categories; ask and answer questions about the total number of data points, how many in each category, and how many more or less": all three question types, asked and answered, with "more" and "fewer" both ways.
- **1.OA.C.5, days 12 and 13.** "Relate counting to addition and subtraction": count on to a teen total, and count up the steps to find a difference.
- **Limits that bite here.** Day 11 stays within 10; days 12 and 13 go to 20. Three categories; no number line, algorithm or graph axes.
- **Connections.** 1.OA.C.5 and 1.MD.C.4 meet on days 12 and 13: the total of a display is found by counting on.
- **Strategies.** Counting on and counting up; no algorithm. New notation, day 11: the tally bundle of five (the fifth mark across four). Exits may show bundles.
- **Practices.** MP.2, days 12 and 13: tie each tally to one response, then pose and answer questions.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** One tally is one response, so the total counts responses and not groups. A difference counts up from the smaller count and the answer is the number of steps. The word "difference" is for teachers only; children say "how many more" and "how many fewer".

## 2. Where it sits

Section 1.2 ends on day 10; its misses go to the day 11 Warm-up and repair. Day 13 misses go to day 14. No readiness item is owned.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** none. 11 + 3, 16 - 12 and cloud creatures 6, 2, 3 are reserved for section 1.4 and never appear here. Picture question frames are taught here; they are never used as a whole task like D2.

**Exit questions.** Read aloud, one ask at a time; every child marks paper. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Timing for about 30 children: 3 minutes to mark, rotating six heard in the Synthesis at 45 seconds each, names recorded. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.

- **E11.** Imagined-pet display: turtles 2, fish 5 (one bundle), snails 1. (a) "Which is true: 3 choices altogether, or 8 choices altogether? Mark it, and touch-mark every choice." Key: 8. Wrong: 3, counted the groups (H). (b) "Which is true: 5 more fish than turtles, or 3 more fish than turtles? Mark it, and draw match lines." Key: 3. Wrong: 5, the larger count (F). The claims are split so one wrong pick shows one letter. A pick without its marks is not yet seen. Shape: practice sorts claim cards into two piles, never a two-way pick; no 2, 5, 1; no turtles, fish or snails.
- **E12.** Display: stars 7, moons 5, suns 1. (a) "Write each group's count." Key: 7, 5, 1. (b) "Mark how you count on to find how many altogether." Key: 13 (keep 7, then eight to twelve, thirteen). Wrong: 12, seven counted as a step (A); 3, counted the groups (H). Shape: no 7, 5, 1 and no sky pictures in practice.
- **E13.** Pretend game choices: hopping 6, drawing 4, building 3. (a) "Ask a question about how many altogether, and answer it." Key: 13. Wrong: 3 (H). (b) "Ask a question about how many more or fewer, and answer it." Key: hopping and drawing 2; hopping and building 3; drawing and building 1; "fewer" wording accepted. Wrong: the larger count, such as 6 (F); 6 with step marks counted up from 4, the last word given (B, read from the marks). Shape: practice composes only on displays already used; no 6, 4, 3; never cloud creatures.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Puppet pairs, right before wrong, invented for practice and said so.

| | Wrong example beside a right one; day | If many made it |
|---|---|---|
| H | Day 11: rabbits 5, hamsters 3, birds 1; puppet says "3 altogether" (three groups) beside touching each response once, 9. | Day 12 Warm-up and repair, 5 min: touch each response once (4, 2, 1: 7). |
| B | Day 12: 15 - 11 counted up from 11, puppet says "15" beside the steps "twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen", 4. | Day 13 Warm-up and repair, 5 min: one dot per step, count the dots (13 - 10: 3). |

Over a third (more than 10 of 30) is "many": a 5-minute whole-class repair of one letter in the Warm-up and repair; fewer go to a 5-minute teacher group in the next practice.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** The tally display (light), paper. Single marks from section 1.2 become bundles of five on day 11. Counting on from section 1.1 and step boxes return on day 12. By day 13 children use displays and frames without teacher support.
- **Key moment 1, day 11: one mark stands for one response.** Ideal: each tally touched and matched to one response card. Spec: the paper display fits.
- **Key moment 2, day 12: the last word of counting up is not the answer.** Ideal: one dot per step in step boxes. Spec: paper fits.
- **A room with nothing.** Hand-drawn displays and tally sticks on paper; picture frames drawn with blanks for categories and a question mark. Sort household objects, not food.

## 6. Days

Pairs work at once; every child may pass in public talk. The class pet is imagined: no child owns a pet or reports on home.

## Day 11

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 10 exit repair, one letter.
- **10 min. Model tallies and claims.** Imagined pets: rabbits 5 (a bundle), hamsters 3, birds 1. Total 9, 2 more rabbits than hamsters, 2 fewer birds than hamsters. Puppet pair for H.
- **20 min. Judge claims.** Pairs get puppies 3, kittens 4, frogs 3 and five cards: "10 altogether" (true), "1 more kitten than puppies" (true), "same puppies as frogs" (true), "3 altogether" (H), "4 more kittens than frogs" (F; true is 1). They sort the cards into "shows it" and "does not" and say each false card's true number.
- **15 min. Draw evidence.** Each child touch-marks every choice and draws match lines for two claims.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six: "What does one tally mark stand for?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E11.
- **Carried and tool.** Tally display (light): show why, 10 min; practice with feedback, 20 min. Credit: adapted from IM G1 U1 L11, Class Pet Surveys.

## Day 12

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 11 exit repair (error H).
- **15 min. Model across ten and counting steps.** Invented games: tag 9, jump rope 6, ball 2. Total: "nine (hold), ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen", 17. Tag than jump rope: up from 6, "seven, eight, nine", 3 steps. Puppet pair for B.
- **20 min. Teen displays in pairs.** Pair 1: sun hats 8, rain hats 4, caps 2 (total 14; 8 than 4 is 4). Pair 2: blocks 9, beads 5, puzzles 1 (total 15; 9 than 5 is 4). One card, 14 - 9: up from 9, "ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen", 5 steps. Steps are recorded in step boxes.
- **10 min. Label each count.** On their own copy of display 1 or 2, children label each group's count.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six: "Does the last number tell the total or the steps?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E12.
- **Carried and tool.** Tally display (light): show why, 15 min; practice with feedback, 20 min. Credit: adapted from IM G1 U1 L12, How Many?

## Day 13

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 12 exit repair (error B).
- **10 min. Model question types.** The day 11 pet display (5, 3, 1): "How many altogether?" 9. "How many hamsters?" 3. "How many more rabbits than hamsters?" 2. "How many fewer birds than rabbits?" 4. "Which pet is softest?" cannot be answered by the display. Puppet pair for F with rug, beanbag and table.
- **20 min. Compose questions.** Picture question frames (category blanks and a question mark) on displays 1 and 2 and the puppies display. Each child makes a total question and a more-or-fewer question, one in their own words or drawing.
- **15 min. Answer and check.** Partners trade, answer, and check with counters.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six: "Can this display answer your question?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E13.
- **Carried and tool.** Tally display (light): practice with feedback, 35 min. Credit: adapted from IM G1 U1 L13, Questions About Data.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 11, "What does one tally mark stand for?" Day 12, "Does the last number tell the total or the steps?" Day 13, "Can this display answer your question?"
- Written task, day 13: the child's own two questions.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: the question names its categories, the display can answer it, the answer matches the marks.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Daily, 5 minutes, no timer. Pool: count on 10 to 15 and 13 to 18; 9 + 2, 8 + 3, 6 - 2, 10 - 2. No exit facts and no 11 + 3 or 16 - 12.
- **Fluency target.** None; the standard sets none.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: tally, altogether, more, fewer, true. Frame: "There are __ more __ than __."
- **Access.** Paper tally sticks, enlarged displays, an adult scribe who writes the child's dictated question.
- **Check accommodations.** Read each stem aloud; a child may say the question and an adult marks it.
- **Reading load.** None; claims on day 11 are read aloud with their pictures.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write a claim that a display does not support and say why.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** A 35-minute path (5, 5, 15, 5, 5) with a display of household objects.
- **One projector.** It may show a display; children mark paper copies.
