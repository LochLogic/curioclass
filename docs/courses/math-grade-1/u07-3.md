# Section 7.3: Make equal shares and compare their sizes

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-grade-1/7/s/7.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-grade-1 u07-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 1 Math. Unit 7, Geometry and time, [s07.md](s07.md). **Days 137 to 140.** Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 1. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

## 1. Standards and practices

**1.G.A.3.** "Partition" circles and rectangles into two and four equal shares, "describe" the shares and "Describe the whole" in terms of two halves or four fourths. "Understand" why more equal shares of matching wholes make smaller shares. MP.7 on 139: show that a half can be covered by two fourths of the same whole. Equal shares have equal area and cover the original exactly once. Stacked matching pieces are sufficient evidence of equal area. Some equal-area pieces have different shapes. Keep the work within matching wholes, halves and fourths, in words and pictures. Retrieve 1.OA.C.6 on 137 to 140.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Show the correct case first. For E on 137, overlay matching pieces from guided folds before comparing two uneven pieces described as equal. For I on 139, cover one paper half with two matching fourths before presenting the claim that more pieces means larger shares. Use other outlines and orientations than the checks.

Return each exit the next day. For 11 or more misses, use five opening and five practice minutes for one priority repair with a fresh retry. Use the same space with a small group for fewer misses. Route by date from 137 to 141, including E136 on 137. Keep 137's R1/R2 revisions, 140's partition revisions and 141's share-language revisions distinct from the exit repair slot.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Start with physical folds and overlays, then use picture marks and tie the share to spoken and written words on 138. Children keep their own records. Withdraw shared worked samples on 140. Ideal instruments allow pieces to fit over one another and preserve the original whole. Precut paper and overlays fit. Stack paper copies in place of a clear overlay and use adult-cut scraps for share pieces. Digital tools: None.

## 7. Talk and writing

Ask "How could we check both pieces?" on 137 and "Why do the wholes need to match?" on 139. For the 139 paper reason, look for a cut that divides a half into two equal areas or a neutral transcription linked to the child's paper mark.

## 8. Practice that returns

Five minutes of mixed within-ten facts and ten of alternating story bars and tens drawings. Example on 137: 9 minus 4 is 5, with 5 plus 4 checking the amount. Example on 138: two tens and seven ones make 27. On 139 revisit the 6.5 invented leaf scene, six plus two is eight, using a labeled bar. On 140 show three tens and seven ones make 37. Demonstrate the 6.4 bar and 6.5 story, both planned only. Use untimed feedback and retries. No new fluency target.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review kindergarten circle and rectangle. On 137, teach equal shares as pieces with the same amount of space that together cover the whole. For 138, say half of for one of two equal shares, fourth of or quarter of for one of four. Tie the plural names to rebuilding: "The whole has ___ equal shares." Offer large tactile edges and let children trace or dictate single-word labels. Extend by making halves of the same square in two ways and testing their equality.
