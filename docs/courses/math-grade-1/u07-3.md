# Section 7.3: Make equal shares and compare their sizes

Grade 1 Math. Unit 7, Geometry and time, [s07.md](s07.md). **Days 137 to 140.** Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 1. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

## 1. Standards and practices

**1.G.A.3.** "Partition" circles and rectangles into two and four equal shares, "describe" the shares and "Describe the whole" in terms of two halves or four fourths. "Understand" why more equal shares of matching wholes make smaller shares. MP.7 on 139: show that a half can be covered by two fourths of the same whole. Equal shares have equal area and cover the original exactly once. Stacked matching pieces are sufficient evidence of equal area. Some equal-area pieces have different shapes. Keep the work within matching wholes, halves and fourths, in words and pictures. Retrieve 1.OA.C.6 on 137 to 140.

## 7. Talk and writing

## 8. Practice that returns

Five minutes of mixed within-ten facts and ten of alternating story bars and tens drawings. On 137 say “Invented for practice. Val has nine paper flags and puts four away.” Draw the bar. Five remain, and five plus four checks nine. Example on 138: two tens and seven ones make 27. On 139 revisit the 6.5 invented leaf scene, six plus two is eight, using a labeled bar. ## 9. Supports and extension

Review kindergarten circle and rectangle. On the nonsquare rectangle in S1, the four diagonal triangles have equal area even though all four need not match when stacked. Each diagonal halves the whole and the second cuts each half into two equal areas. On 137, teach equal shares as pieces with the same amount of space that together cover the whole. For 138, say half of for one of two equal shares, fourth of or quarter of for one of four. Say “Two halves make this whole. Four fourths make this whole. Four quarters do too.” Tie the plural names to rebuilding: "The whole has ___ equal shares." Offer large tactile edges and let children trace or dictate single-word labels. Extend by making halves of the same square in two ways and testing their equality.
