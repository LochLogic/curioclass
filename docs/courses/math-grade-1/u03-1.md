Grade 1 Math. Unit 3, Adding and subtracting within 20 (days 40 to 68), [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 40 to 44.** Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 2, application 0, check 0. A plan, not a lesson. Tasks are ours, untested.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.OA.C.6.** “Add and subtract” and “Use strategies.” Here, work within ten. Use plus zero, plus one, doubles, partners of ten and related addition to find a difference.
- **1.OA.B.3.** “Apply” reordering as an addition strategy, days 41 and 42. **1.OA.C.5.** “Relate” each new count to one added object, day 41.
- **Limits.** Totals and differences within ten. Children use the properties through objects and pictures. Mental strategies and recall are the fluency target.
- **Practices.** MP.7, day 41: hold the larger addend and show why both group orders preserve both quantities. MP.8, day 43: use changes of one in doubles and partners-of-ten sums.
- **Teacher explanation.** The held group is already counted. When the groups swap, the objects in them stay the same. In a related subtraction the addition shows how the part taken away and the part left fill the same whole.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** None. Read prompts aloud over individual picture sheets. Each child marks a paper. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen and note strategy only when the work shows it. Say and print “invented for practice” with fictional scenes.

- **E40 (1.OA.C.6).** Print four fact cards on the sheet beside two picture pockets labeled “Know” and “Practice.” Read 3 + 0, 8 − 0, 4 + 1 and 0 + 0. “Where would you put each fact?” Key: any one pocket per fact. Adult reference values are 3, 8, 5 and 0. Either label is valid. Record missing or double placement descriptively.
  
  Shape: A private four-fact inventory. Practice sorts by mathematical pattern and keeps these four cards and confidence pockets out.
- **E41 (1.OA.C.6, 1.OA.B.3, 1.OA.C.5).** Print one square and nine circles, then the same groups in the other order, with a blank record beside them. “How do these two pictures keep the same total?” Children connect the unchanged groups and show their held start and count with numeral or step marks. Key: same nine and same one, hold nine, count ten once. Nine after saying nine for the added step is A.
  
  Shape: Explain a swap from two still pictures. Practice physically moves groups behind a cover and reserves this pair and picture layout.
- **E42 (1.OA.C.6, 1.OA.B.3).** Print eight ungrouped marks and the cards 3 + 1, 7 + 1 and 5 + 1. Read the cards. “Which card fits these marks?” Children link the card and allocate its two quantities to the marks. Key: 7 + 1, seven marks and one. Wrong: links 5 + 1 to eight by copying the total of 5 + 3 (H).
  
  Shape: Select a matching expression for a fixed ungrouped quantity. All choices share the final addend. Practice builds one quantity two ways from loose pieces and keeps these cards out.
- **E43 (1.OA.C.6, MP.8).** Show a card with ten dots inside a frame and an empty pocket beside it. Read the known ten plus zero, then reveal a picture with one dot crossed out. “What sum does this changed picture show?” Children mark a total on the changed picture with the removed dot as evidence. Key: 9 + 0 = 9, one fewer than ten. Wrong: writes ten using the original sum unchanged (H).
  
  Shape: Infer a nearby sum from a static change to a known partner of ten. Practice rebuilds physical frames, keeping the zero-part before and after case and crossed-dot card out.
- **E44 (1.OA.C.6).** Draw a tray with room for ten clips. A flap is labeled with four hidden clips, and the exposed part is blank. Read the capacity and hidden amount. “What addition fact tells how many clips are showing?” Children draw the showing part and mark the addition fact beside it. They may dictate the numerals for the adult to record. Key: 4 + 6 = 10 or 6 + 4 = 10, six showing, 10 − 4 = 6. Wrong: seven showing after counting four through ten as seven new steps (A). Five after copying the number left when five are hidden (H).
  
  Shape: Recover the exposed part from a fixed capacity and hidden part. Practice covers a supplied part and rebuilds a visible whole. Reserve the flap, numbers and missing exposed part layout.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** Re-establish personal frames and labeled drawings from unit 2. On day 40 tie one occupied cell per object to each expression and equation. Children hold the marks even when they can recall a fact. Shared frames are withdrawn on day 58.
- **Day 41 ideal instrument.** A cover holds a known group still while separate moves make the new counts visible. A drawn ten-frame and scrap-paper flap fit. No key tool or digital spec is needed.
- **No-kit path.** Draw frames, use patterned scraps in place of counters and handwrite the fact cards and records. Fold a scrap for the puppet.

## 6. Days

Fifteen simultaneous pairs exchange roles. Each synthesis hears the next six on the roster, one 45-second practice turn apiece. The other children show marks at once. Record names and help. Collect all exit papers for individual feedback in the next Practice return.

## Day 40

**Understanding.** I can group familiar facts and find ones to practice. Children hold a frame and sketches. Digital tool: None.

- **5 min. Hook.** Mark a familiar cell in an unfinished addition table. Ask “What makes a sum easy to find?”
- **5 min. Retrieval.** Read 1 + 3 and 5 − 2, keys four and three. Show two different splits of eight. Accept six and two, and eight and zero, or other distinct splits. Mark each skill separately.
- **10 min. Model.** Use the ten-frame (light) to show why. Build the double and changed-dot case in field 4. Show 1 + 0 = 1. Trace each piece into its picture and equation.
- **20 min. Fact rounds.** Sort by plus zero, plus one, doubles, partners of ten or other. With overlapping patterns, accept either mathematically valid placement and have the child point to what it shows. Reinstate partner check and retry.
- **10 min. Unit 2 centers.** First five for E38 equality or difference, or day 39 missing-start priorities. Next five for fresh individual retries. Other groups mark their assigned paper mats.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear six children name a fact pattern.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E40, paper.

## Day 41

**Understanding.** I can count on from the larger group. Children hold a frame and cover. Digital tool: None.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Cover five. Add one and then, on a reset mat, two. Six and seven, with only six and seven as the two new counts on the second mat. Mix one taught subtraction into the opening.
- **10 min. Model.** Ten-frame (light), show why. Use field 4 A. Swap the actual groups and trace the two groups to their expressions.
- **25 min. Practice return.** First five for E40 feedback and a fresh pattern check or a short individual retry. Partners then check a puppet's held start by moving just the new counters, including taught plus-one and plus-two cases.
- **10 min. Unit 2 centers.** Five for the assigned change, part, difference or step repair and five for fresh paper retries. This is the final unit 2 center slot.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear six on the held group.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E41, paper.

## Day 42

**Understanding.** I can match expressions with equal amounts. Children hold paper and counters. Digital tool: None.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mix taught facts and a held starting group.
- **10 min. Model.** Ten-frame (light), show why. Build seven with three and four, then four and three. Draw both and write 3 + 4 = 4 + 3.
- **25 min. Practice return.** Reserve ten for day 40 retrieval, five for day 41 retrieval and five for E41. Rebuild a split, track new steps and retry on fresh paper in their respective slots. Use at least five for independent builds and expression labels. If a repair is unneeded, use its minutes for builds.
- **10 min. Compare.** Compare the previously shown double and plus-zero example. Trace each side to the amount it names.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear six compare their built amounts.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E42, paper.

## Day 43

**Fluency.** I can use ten and familiar fact patterns. Children hold individual frames and known cards. Digital tool: None.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mix taught facts and a split of a fixed whole.
- **5 min. Recall.** Return to 1 + 1 = 2 and 1 + 2 = 3 from day 40.
- **30 min. Practice return.** First five for E42 and a fresh retry. Ten-frame (light), practice with feedback for the remaining 25. Build eight and two to fill ten. Set one of the two counters aside and confirm that eight and one make nine. Return it and rebuild another taught split.
- **10 min. Explain.** Compare the double with its one-more neighbor and point to the extra piece.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear six on what changed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E43, paper.

## Day 44

**Fluency.** I can use addition to find a difference. Children hold covers and individual records. Digital tool: None.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mix known facts and point to parts of a known whole.
- **5 min. Recall.** Use the known relationship 4 + 2 = 6 to find 6 − 2 = 4.
- **30 min. Practice return.** First five for E43 and a retry. For the remaining 25, use the ten-frame (light) to practice with feedback. Cover a supplied part, restore it to check the whole and record the related fact on a handwritten slip.
- **10 min. Compare and retry.** Show how a piece restored in addition is the one removed in subtraction. Revise a practice mark.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear six point to the part that stays.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E44, paper. Send results to day 45.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 41, “Which group stayed the same?” Day 43, “Where did the extra dot go?” On day 42, children mark two builds and put an expression beside each. Look for one mark per object and equal totals despite different group orders.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Kindergarten review.** Equals “is the same quantity as” (u04-4 day 78). A partner of ten completes ten (u05-3 day 97). Retrieve within-five facts and splits on day 40, and a held start on day 41.
- **Earlier grade 1, planned only.** Re-establish count on, “Partner rounds with a counters check and retry,” “Puppet wrong example beside the right one,” and “Paper exit with the rotating six heard” from u01-1. Re-establish “Practice return” from u02-1. Use u02-5's “E38 three-category shape choice display” only with the returned paper. Supply a fresh mat for the retry.
- **Fluency.** Within ten, practice recalling an answer or finding it with a brief mental strategy. Attempt, check with pieces, and retry without time pressure. Keep the checking method separate from the first attempt.

## 9. Supports and extension

Use hold, same and step with gestures. Day 40 introduce double, two groups with the same number, and sum, how many are in the groups together. Say “I held __ and counted __ more.” Offer large tactile marks, a child-directed scribe and pointing. Read the cards and labels aloud. Extend by explaining why a chosen check works for another taught fact.

## 10. Two rooms

At home, use five minutes of retrieval, fifteen of model, practice and needed return, five of talk and five for the exit. The adult takes the partner role. One screen can enlarge the model while children keep their individual sheets.
