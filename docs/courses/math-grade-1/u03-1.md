Grade 1 Math. Unit 3, Adding and subtracting within 20 (days 40 to 68), [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 40 to 44.** Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 2, application 0, check 0. A plan, not a lesson. Tasks are ours, untested.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.OA.C.6.** “Add and subtract” and “Use strategies.” Here, work within ten. Use plus zero, plus one, doubles, partners of ten and related addition to find a difference.
- **1.OA.B.3.** “Apply” reordering as an addition strategy, days 41 and 42. **1.OA.C.5.** “Relate” each new count to one added object, day 41.
- **Limits.** Totals and differences within ten. Children use the properties through objects and pictures. Mental strategies and recall are the fluency target.
- **Practices.** MP.7, day 41: hold the larger addend and show why both group orders preserve both quantities. MP.8, day 43: use changes of one in doubles and partners-of-ten sums.
- **Teacher explanation.** The held group is already counted. When the groups swap, the objects in them stay the same. In a related subtraction the addition shows how the part taken away and the part left fill the same whole.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** None. Read prompts aloud over individual picture sheets. Each child marks a paper. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen and note strategy only when the work shows it. Say and print “invented for practice” with fictional scenes.

- **E40 (1.OA.C.6).** Give each child four cards: 0 + 7, 8 − 0, 9 − 9 and 0 + 0. Read each card and both pocket labels, “Know” and “Practice.” Read one prompt per card: “0 + 7. Which pocket fits this fact?” Repeat with 8 − 0, 9 − 9 and 0 + 0. Each child marks the first two on the half slip and the last two in the Exit box. Key: either pocket for each card. The values for the teacher are seven, eight, zero and zero. Record the choices privately. Leave the answers off the cards. On the return, practice the chosen pattern on a different fact.  
  Shape: Four private choices across addition and subtraction. A correct arithmetic answer is not required. Reserve this set and the pockets from fact rounds.
- **E41 (1.OA.C.6, 1.OA.B.3, 1.OA.C.5).** Two sealed packets hold counters, fewer in the striped one. Both groups together fit on one ten-frame. The second picture shows the very same packets in reverse order. Only the striped packet opens, showing two counters. “Why does counting on from the big packet work both ways?” Key: point to the larger packet as the amount already counted, then mark a step for each of the two striped counters. The same packets are there both times. Accept matching arrows between the pictures. Wrong: taps the large packet for the first step and adds only one of the striped counters (A).  
  Shape: Explain a swap even though the larger count is hidden. Practice swaps groups with known counts. Keep both packet pictures out of practice.
- **E42 (1.OA.C.6, 1.OA.B.3).** Put at most six buttons in a spotted bag and keep it closed. The drawing shows that bag and three loose buttons. Each card shows the loose buttons first and then the same unopened bag, so the order differs from the drawing. Read the picture cards as “two and the bag,” “three and the bag,” and “four and the bag.” “Which card shows just as many buttons as this picture?” Key: the card with three and the bag. Connect the matching bag and the three loose marks. Wrong: chooses two after tapping the bag as the first of three added buttons (A).  
  Shape: Match two expressions for a collection whose bag count stays hidden. Practice builds known amounts from pieces.
- **E43 (1.OA.C.6, MP.8).** Show a pair of pockets with dots. Print the two moves as separate arrows and leave both pocket counts unnumbered. “One arrow moves a dot to the other pocket. One arrow moves a dot onto the floor. Which move changes how many dots are in the pockets?” Key: Mark only the arrow to the floor. A move from one pocket to the other keeps the same total. Wrong: Marks neither arrow and says the total stays the same even after seeing one fall out (H).
  Shape: Compare two moves with the starting counts hidden. Practice changes a known pair on a frame. Reserve the pocket picture and both arrows.
- **E44 (1.OA.C.6).** The first picture shows a closed sleeve of clips in a small tray. Four spaces beside it are empty. The next picture shows Rin filling those spaces with four clips. In the last picture the sleeve is lifted out under a flap. “How does the adding picture show how many clips stay?” Key: four. The whole tray was the sleeve plus four. Taking out that same sleeve leaves the four added clips. Accept a link from the four arrivals to the four left. Keep the sleeve closed until the child answers. A child who opens it afterward can check the related addition with the actual clips. Wrong: gives five by tapping the held sleeve as a first new count and then counting all four arrivals (A).  
  Shape: Use the addition to undo a removal without knowing how many are inside the sleeve. Practice starts with a known total and rebuilds a visible whole. The tray holds at most ten clips.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** Re-establish personal frames and labeled drawings from unit 2. On day 40 tie one occupied cell per object to each expression and equation. Children can point from a mark to the group it represents. Shared frames are withdrawn on day 58.
- **Day 41 ideal instrument.** A cover holds a known group still while separate moves make the new counts visible. A drawn ten-frame and scrap-paper flap fit. No key tool or digital spec is needed.
- **No-kit path.** Draw frames, use patterned scraps in place of counters and handwrite the fact cards and records. Fold a scrap for the puppet.

## 6. Days

Fifteen pairs work at once and swap roles. Continue one roster across sections: on days 40 to 44 hear children 1 to 6, 7 to 12, 13 to 18, 19 to 24, then 25 to 30. Repeat every five days. In Synthesis or Close, six turns of 45 seconds leave 30 seconds to switch. The other 24 mark their own answer to the same question. Record who was heard and any help. Check those marks after class and return them with each child's exit in the next named return. Use the child's next 45-second turn for any explanation still needed. A later answer has its own date. In each teaching day's five-minute retrieval, use a familiar addition and subtraction alongside the named earlier idea. Draw from worked practice only. Keep field 3 cards and their number combinations out of models, repairs and decks.

## Day 40

**Understanding.** I can group familiar facts and find ones to practice. Children hold a frame and sketches. Digital tool: None.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Read 1 + 3 and 5 − 2, keys four and three. Each child marks both answers and two different splits of eight on a strip. - **10 min. Model.** Use the ten-frame (light) to show why. Build four and four, then add one dot to one group. Eight becomes nine. A puppet that keeps eight shows H. Name a double and point to the two equal groups. Read the sum before sorting by pattern. Show 1 + 0 = 1. Trace each piece into its picture and equation.

## Day 44

**Fluency.** I can use addition to find a difference. Children hold covers and individual records. Digital tool: None.

- **5 min. Retrieval.** Mix known facts and point to parts of a known whole.
- **5 min. Recall.** Use the known relationship 4 + 2 = 6 to find 6 − 2 = 4.
- **30 min. Practice return.** First five for E43 and a retry. For the remaining 25, use the ten-frame (light) to practice with feedback. Cover a supplied part, restore it to check the whole and record the related fact on a handwritten slip.
- **10 min. Compare and retry.** Show how a piece restored in addition is the one removed in subtraction. Revise a practice mark.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Hear six point to the part that stays.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E44, paper. Send results to day 45.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 41, “Which group stayed the same?” Day 43, “Which move changed the total?” On day 42, children mark two builds and put an expression beside each. Look for one mark per object and equal totals despite different group orders.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Kindergarten review.** Equals “is the same quantity as” (u04-4 day 78). A partner of ten completes ten (u05-3 day 97). Retrieve within-five facts and splits on day 40, and a held start on day 41.
- **Earlier grade 1, planned only.** Re-establish count on, “Partner rounds with a counters check and retry,” “Puppet wrong example beside the right one,” and “Paper exit with the rotating six heard” from u01-1. Re-establish “Practice return” from u02-1. Use u02-5's “E38 three-category shape choice display” only with the returned paper. Supply a fresh mat for the retry.
- **Fluency.** Within ten, practice recalling an answer or finding it with a brief mental strategy. Attempt, check with pieces, and retry without time pressure. Keep the checking method separate from the first attempt.

## 9. Supports and extension

Use hold, same and step with gestures. Day 40, before the sort, show a double, two groups with the same number. Recall sum, how many are in the groups together. Point to the whole when you say it. Say “I held __ and counted __ more.” Offer large tactile marks, a child-directed scribe and pointing. Read the cards and labels aloud. Extend by explaining why a chosen check works for another taught fact.

## 10. Two rooms

At home, use five minutes of retrieval, fifteen of model, practice and needed return, five of talk and five for the exit. The adult takes the partner role. One screen can enlarge the model while children keep their individual sheets.
