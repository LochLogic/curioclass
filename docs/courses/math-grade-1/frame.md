# Course frame: Grade 1 Math

Course id math-k8, grade 1. Draft frame, 2026-11-13; the scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8, Illustrative Mathematics Grade 1's eight units in its own order, with no unit moved: the base text's
  order already teaches each model and skill before its first use (the reasons are in the Units table).
- **Days.** 160: 154 in units (each unit's days include one end-of-unit assessment day), 4 for course-level checks
  (2 after unit 4, 2 after unit 8) and 2 respond days (one after each check). Math is daily: one lesson of 60
  minutes a day, five days a week, 32 weeks, the base text's own lesson length (warm-up, activities, lesson
  synthesis and exit ticket; center days are whole lessons of practice games). The base text's 146 lessons, all
  kept, and eight assessment days make the 154. Days follow the base text's own weight by unit: unit 3 (five rows,
  the year's core fluency and make-ten work) is the longest; unit 8 is spaced review. The base text budgets two
  assessment days a unit; Curio Class keeps one, because its own end-of-unit assessment (the base text's is behind a
  login) is one period of short items, and the one-to-one evidence grade 1 needs (fluency within 10 watched in a
  game, an explanation said aloud, work with objects) is gathered during the unit's center days and lessons. Each unit
  plan names what is gathered on the assessment day, what is gathered in the lessons before it, the observation
  minutes and how every child is reached, and that every owned standard is covered. If a unit's evidence cannot fit,
  the extra day goes to Cory before the scope and sequence fixes the year; no standard is cut to keep the total.
- **Major work.** A provisional allocation, with the 75 percent line for K to 2 (docs/RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md). By days,
  133 of 160 (83 percent, an allocation estimate) sit outside unit 7 and unit 1's data lessons, the only additional
  and supporting work (1.MD.B, 1.MD.C, 1.G), check and respond days counted with what they check; by units, 7 of 8
  (unit 8 reviews major work only); by base lessons (a tag inventory, not a time count), 137 of 146 carry a
  major-cluster standard. By time, at 60 minutes a day the line is 7,200 of 9,600 minutes; a mixed lesson (a
  geometry lesson with a number warm-up) is not all one kind, so each unit plan records minutes by lesson part and
  which supporting work practises major work (the evidence guide counts it with major work), and the scope and
  sequence confirms the line from those minutes. Length measurement (1.MD.A) is major work in grade 1 and unit 6 is
  counted as such. The sequence is not changed to raise either percentage.
- **Standard.** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 1: every grade 1 standard and every lettered part
  is owned by a progression row and placed in a unit below (1.NBT.B.2a and b in unit 3, 1.NBT.B.2c in unit 4).
  1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.C.6 and 1.NBT.C.4 are each begun by a contributing row and finished by their owning row, in a later
  unit or the same one, as the Units table says. Skills: the eight Standards for Mathematical Practice, one or two
  named per unit (candidates in the Check set adds column; the unit plan settles them with the task where students
  do them). Check set: each unit plan checks its order, limits and models against the Progressions documents (CC BY
  4.0) and the Eureka Math grade 1 reference (sequence and models only, never its problems or wording), and records
  any addition with its source.
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12, 1st edition, Grade 1 (CC BY 4.0), adapted into Curio Class days with
  a credit line on each day; the source map at the end places every base unit and lesson. Only the free public pages
  are used: cool-downs, keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken, so every exit ticket and
  assessment is Curio Class's own. The second edition (CC BY-NC) is never used.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 1.OA.C.6 asks for fluency within 10. It is built with short spaced practice in
  warm-ups and center games all year and checked by watching and listening (a game, a one-to-one card check): the
  teacher records whether a child just knows, uses a quick strategy or counts all. No timer is ever shown and no
  sheet is timed. Within 20 is practised with strategies; fluency within 20 is grade 2's.
- **Concrete to written.** Ten-frames, connecting-cube towers and quick drawings come first and are tied to the
  written equation in the same lesson; "fluently" never means with objects. No number line in grade 1 (it comes in
  grade 2, after rulers), and no standard algorithm (grade 4).
- **Readiness.** Unit 1's first two lessons open with a short readiness check on kindergarten counting on and facts
  within 5; units 3, 4 and 6 open with one on the kindergarten piece they rest on (partners of 10 and teen numbers;
  teen numbers; comparing two objects directly).
- **Paper path.** Every lesson runs with objects, printed frames and paper; any Curio Class tool is an extra, with no
  student account.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent read. No unit needs a community reader. Teacher notes in the
  last column cover class surveys, pets and belongings in story problems, the food drive lesson and home routines in
  time-telling.

## Course-level checks

- **After unit 4 (2 days, about week 19).** Short written items with a one-to-one part: facts within 10 (watched in a
  card game, recorded as just knows, quick strategy or counts all, never timed); four story problems of different
  types within 20 with a drawing or equation; true and false equations; an unknown in an equation; a make-ten sum
  within 20; two-digit numbers as tens and ones, compared with a symbol; 10 more and 10 less; a multiple of 10
  subtracted.
- **1 respond day after the mid-year check.** Re-teach the gaps (most often Compare problems and the meaning of =), in
  small groups, ending with a short fresh task.
- **After unit 8 (2 days, end of year).** Fluency within 10 (watched, untimed); add within 100 with and without a new
  ten, with a written explanation or drawing; all problem types, start unknown and Compare included (solved and
  discussed; proficiency on those two is grade 2's); count and write from a given number past 100; order three
  lengths and measure one in units; name a shape's defining attributes; halves and fourths; time to the half hour.
- **1 respond day after the end-of-year check.** Re-teach the gaps the check shows; the record goes on to the grade 2
  teacher with what the respond day changed.
