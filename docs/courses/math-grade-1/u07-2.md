## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.G.A.1.** "Distinguish" features necessary for membership and "build and draw" shapes with those features. MP.6, Triangle detective on 133: test for a closed boundary of three straight sides.
- **1.G.A.2.** "Compose" flat and solid shapes and "compose new shapes from the composite shape." MP.7, Reusable motif on 135: use an outlined whole as a piece of a larger design.
- A rectangle closes with four straight sides and four square corners. A square also has four equal sides and belongs to the rectangle group. A tilted shape keeps its corners. Explain that two square corners alone leave the other corners untested. Use a folded corner to verify, with no angle measures. In 1.OA.C.6 retrieval, materials can show a strategy after an independent try.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Teach the right case first. Model this contrast routine with a folded puppet, planned only in Grade 1 section 1.1. For A on 132, trace the sides of a qualifying shape before presenting a claim that blue decides membership. On 133 trace an unbroken triangle first. Calling the open figure a triangle is (B). Calling the bowed edge a straight triangle side is (C). For J on 134, verify all four corners on one rectangle before testing a four-sided figure with no square corners. Use a non-parallelogram here. Keep the two-square-corner trapezoid for R1 and the oblique parallelogram for E134. For D on 135, move a traced whole before contrasting scattered pieces. B, C and J are predictions from memory.

On each following day, return the preceding exit at the opening. At 11 or more misses use five opening and five practice minutes for the priority repair and a fresh retry. For fewer, use a small group in those minutes. Days 135 and 136 protect observations by taking the second five from practice. Day 137 receives E136. Where two letters compete, assign one priority per child and use partner cues for the other group.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Use paper pieces and a sketch to carry closure, straight edges and complete first outlines. Tie side and corner marks to a sketch on 132. Introduce the intact outline with a teacher-started flat build on 134. Withdraw shared worked builds before independent records on 135. The ideal instrument exposes every edge and lets a build slide while touching pieces stay put. Paper strips, traced faces and removable tabs fit. Adult-cut scrap paper substitutes for tiles and strips, folded paper for a corner tool, and clean boxes, rolled tubes and folded cones for kit solids. Children handle large pieces. Digital tools: None.
