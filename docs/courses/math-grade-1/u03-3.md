# Section 3.3: Change the ones and keep the ten

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-grade-1/3/s/3.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-grade-1 u03-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 1 Math. Unit 3, Adding and subtracting within 20 (days 40 to 68), [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 50 to 53.** Days by kind: understanding 2, fluency 1, application 1, check 0. A plan, not a lesson. Tasks are ours, untested.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.NBT.B.2a and 1.NBT.B.2b.** Ten “can be thought of as a bundle of ten ones.” Eleven through nineteen “are composed of a ten” and one to nine further ones. Keep the ten visible while further ones change.
- **1.OA.C.6.** “Add and subtract” using the ten and further ones, “demonstrating fluency” within ten. Change only the loose ones in the teen calculations here, with totals within twenty.
- **~1.OA.A.1.** “Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems.” Day 52 applies the take away cases with the result or change missing.
- **Practices.** MP.7, days 50 and 51: trace the unchanged ten to the same part in the picture and equation. MP.4, day 52: use a picture to locate the missing quantity.
- **Teacher explanation.** A folded ten has the value of ten ones. Before and after pictures preserve the same ten while the loose group changes. The same take away action can leave the number taken or the number remaining unknown.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

| Letter | Wrong beside right, with day | Response and retry |
|---|---|---|
| B | Day 50: one strip and eight further ones called nine. Right: open the strip to see ten and add eight, so eighteen. | Open, account for every one, refold and retry with a fresh amount. |
| A | Day 50: counts fourteen, fifteen, sixteen for three additions to fourteen. Right: fifteen, sixteen, seventeen are the new steps. | Hold the start and mark only new steps before retrying. |
| G | Day 51: takes two from nineteen and then all four again, gives thirteen. Right: remove exactly four to leave fifteen. | Partition a removal and cross off each part once. |
| F | Day 52: for 17 − □ = 12, puppet labels twelve as taken. Right: five moved and twelve stayed. | Trace the leaving pieces to the missing amount and retry. |
| H | Day 53: copies eleven from 12 − 1 as the result for 12 − 0. Right: twelve stays when zero is removed. | Mark the changed removal beside the known fact and retry. |

Show the correct example first. In the next Practice return, reserve five minutes for the exit's feedback, one priority contrast and a fresh paper retry. More than a third triggers a class repair. Otherwise hear a targeted group while peers work in parallel. Day 54's Practice return receives E53.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** Keep the section 3.2 ten and numeral connection. On day 50 link the unchanged bundle to before and after expressions. Children handle pieces and shift to drawings that show what changed. Shared frames and towers withdraw on days 58 and 64 respectively.
- **Ideal instruments.** A double frame and a foldable ten fit because they make the same ten and the moving singles visible. For the mental sample use private bare cards and a roster with strategy and recording-help columns. Children mark the separate frames after the oral observation.
- **No-kit path.** Draw frames. Substitute folded ten-mark strips for towers, patterned scraps or pencil marks for singles and handwritten cards for printed cards. No key tool or digital spec is needed.

## 6. Days

## 9. Supports and extension

Use “__ left and __ stayed,” with separate gestures. Read prompts and labels aloud. Offer large tactile marks and child-directed scribing for pictures. In M1, keep the oral questions private and free of supplied quantities or steps, and accept a spoken or signed response. Extend by explaining a different valid method for a taught teen case.

## 10. Two rooms

At home use five minutes of retrieval, fifteen of work, five discussion and five exit. On day 53 include the two short T53 parts and one private observation in the work time, with a later paper revision. A screen may enlarge a model. If a master is missing, draw the reserved layout on paper.
