# Section 1.4: Our survey and the unit check

Grade 1 Math. Unit 1, Adding, subtracting and working with data (days 1 to 16): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 14 to 16.** Days by kind: fluency 1, application 1, check 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **1.OA.C.5, this section's share.** "Relate counting to addition and subtraction", the whole standard, rehearsed and closed by C1.
- **1.MD.C.4, this section's share.** "Organize, represent, and interpret data with up to three categories; ask and answer questions about the total number of data points, how many in each category, and how many more or less": applied on days 14 and 15 and closed by D1 and D2, all three question types.
- **Limits that bite here.** Within 20; three categories; survey groups of at most 20, so two groups of 15. No new teaching.
- **Connections.** The two standards meet in every day: totals by counting on, differences by counting up.
- **Strategies.** Counting on and counting up.
- **Practices.** MP.7 (C1, counting keeps a group) and MP.2 (D1 and D2, each mark is one response, and questions name their quantities).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Each child's count is heard once (days 14 and 15) and revised once in writing; D1 is scored after its day 16 revision; D2 is the only fresh product; there is no reteaching or observing on day 16. The marking scale is shown, shown with help, not yet seen (DEFAULTS.md).

## 2. Where it sits

Section 1.3 ends on day 13; its misses go to the day 14 Warm-up and repair. Unit 2 starts on day 17 with response centers: results of days 15 and 16 go to day 17 (errors A, B, C) and day 18 (D to H), 10 minutes each.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** C1, D1 and D2 (unit plan field 5), described below.

- **C1 (1.OA.C.5, MP.7), heard days 14 and 15.** A card shows 11 + 3 with a pictured start group and blank step boxes, and 16 - 12. Key: keep 11, "twelve, thirteen, fourteen", 14; start at 12, "thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen", 4 steps. Wrong: 13 or five steps, the start counted as a step (A); 16 as the subtraction answer (B); counting from one (C). The card is pre-marked so each count takes under 45 seconds a part; one minute for the child. Keep the card and the teacher's verbatim count. Shape: no 11 + 3, 3 + 11, 16 - 12, 16 - 4 or 12 + 4, and no start of 11 or 12, in any deck or warm-up.
- **D1 (1.MD.C.4, MP.2), day 15.** Each group of 15 picks three of six pictured imaginary animals; each child drops one anonymous slip; each child makes a labeled display of the group's raw list, one mark per response. Key: counts match the raw list, total 15. Wrong: a slip in two groups (D); one left out (E); no labels (G). Written feedback is given after day 15, five minutes of revision on day 16, then scoring. Sample counts 7, 6, 2 are for scoring only.
- **D2 (1.MD.C.4, MP.2), day 16, 25 minutes, "invented for practice".** Cloud creatures: 6 kites, 2 puffs, 3 spirals. One ask at a time: (a) a labeled display (6, 2, 3); (b) ask and answer a total question (11); (c) to (e) how many kites, puffs, spirals (6, 2, 3); (f) how many more kites than puffs (4); (g) how many fewer spirals than kites (3). Frames are offered; one question in the child's own words or drawing, else asking is not yet seen. Wrong: total 3, counted groups (H); 6 more (F); D, E or G on the display. The word is "labeled".

**Exit questions.** Read aloud; every child marks paper. Timing: 3 minutes to mark, rotating six heard in the Synthesis at 45 seconds each, names recorded. Day 16 has no exit; D2 is its check.

- **E14.** Display: strips 5, circles 2, triangles 1. (a) "Mark a count for how many altogether." Key: 8 (keep 5, "six, seven, eight"). Wrong: 7, five counted as a step (A); one to eight (C); 3, counted groups (H). (b) "Mark how many fewer circles than strips." Key: 3 (up from 2: "three, four, five"; or 3 unmatched). Wrong: 5, no step marks (F); 5, step marks ending at 5 (B). Shape: day 14 cards use displays already seen; no 5, 2, 1 and no strips, circles or triangles.
- **E15.** Mixed pretend slips: 8 winged, 3 finned, 1 furry. (a) "Make a labeled display." Key: 8, 3, 1, labeled. Wrong: a slip in two groups (D); one left out (E); no labels (G). (b) "Mark how many altogether." Key: 12 (keep 8, "nine, ten, eleven, twelve"). Wrong: 11 (A); 3 (H). (c) "Mark how many more winged than finned." Key: 5 (up from 3: "four" to "eight"). Wrong: 8 (F); 8 with step marks ending at 8 (B). Shape: D1 options are never winged, finned or furry; never kites, puffs or spirals. An unfinished (c) goes to the day 17 center.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** The tally display (light), paper, tied to labeled marks on days 7 and 8; the shared display is withdrawn on day 16, when each child makes a paper representation alone. Step boxes may be used on day 16.
- **Key moment, day 15: one slip, one mark.** Ideal: slips laid on a mat in view, each touched once. Spec: paper fits; no digital tool.
- **A room with nothing.** Slips are cut paper; the display is a folded sheet; counters are paper squares. No child is asked about home.

## 6. Days

Each child's count is heard in 1 minute, half the class on day 14 and half on day 15 (15 children in 15 minutes); others work in a quiet known center. Counts are pre-marked so each part takes under 45 seconds.

## Day 14

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 13 exit repair (error F), or retrieval from the pool in field 8.
- **5 min. Recall routines.** Partner turns, the cover, step boxes, matching pairs.
- **25 min. Partner question and fact cards.** Pairs trade question cards on puppies 3, kittens 4, frogs 3 and on 8, 4, 2, and facts: 9 + 3 = 12, 10 + 4 = 14, 15 + 3 = 18, 8 + 5 = 13, 14 + 1 = 15, 17 - 15 = 2, 13 - 10 = 3, 18 - 14 = 4, 20 - 17 = 3, 19 - 17 = 2. Counters check each answer; no timer.
- **15 min. Counting observation, half A.** The teacher hears C1 from each of 15 children for 1 minute; the rest practice in a quiet known center.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six: "How did you count, and how do you know?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E14.
- **Carried and tool.** Tally display (light): practice with feedback, 25 min. Credit: adapted from IM G1 U1 L14, Center Day 3.

## Day 15

- **5 min. Warm-up and repair.** Day 14 exit repair, one letter.
- **5 min. Launch imaginary choices.** Groups pick three of six imaginary animals; choosing is optional and anonymous.
- **20 min. Collect and show.** Slips are dropped (5 min); then each child makes a D1 display. If fewer than 15 are present, pretend slips fill and are said aloud to be invented; the raw list is the key.
- **5 min. Half A revises.** Half A corrects the C1 picture in a second color after the teacher's written note; half B writes questions.
- **15 min. Counting observation, half B.** 15 children, 1 minute each; the others answer survey questions.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Rotating six: "What question can our survey answer?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E15.
- **Carried and tool.** Tally display (light): practice with feedback, 20 min. Credit: adapted from IM G1 U1 L15, Animals in the Jungle.

## Day 16

- **5 min. Settle and hand out.** Cards, pencils, no talk of answers.
- **5 min. Half B revises C1.** Half A has an unscored picture puzzle.
- **5 min. Revise D1.** Each child corrects the day 15 display in a second color after the written note.
- **25 min. D2.** One ask at a time, read aloud; the teacher does not teach or observe.
- **5 min. Check answers.** The key is read aloud.
- **10 min. Picture puzzles.** Unscored sheets from days 10 and 14.
- **5 min. Close.** The class says what it can now do.
- **Carried and tool.** None (paper). Absent children revise and take D2 in the day 17 or 18 centers. Credit: original Curio Class closing record.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 14, "How did you count, and how do you know?" Day 15, "What question can our survey answer?"
- Written task, day 15: the display and one question, judged as D1.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: one mark for each response, labels, counts that match the raw list.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Daily, 5 minutes, no timer. Pool: count on 13 to 17; 9 + 4, 7 + 3, 15 - 13, 10 - 1. No start of 11 or 12.
- **Fluency target.** None; the standard sets none. Day 14 rehearses taught methods with counters checking, no clock.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: survey, choice, slip, total, more, fewer. Frame: "I kept __ and counted __ more."
- **Access.** Prompts read aloud, large outlines, tactile counters, pointing or a scribed question; the teacher never makes the math choice.
- **Check accommodations.** An adult scribes dictated answers and reads each ask once more on request.
- **Reading load.** None; everything is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Ask a question about the survey that the display cannot answer, and say what would be needed.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** The adult hears C1 once, with pretend slips for the survey. Day 16 at home: revise 5, D2 20, check 5, close 5.
- **One projector.** It may show the D2 slips; every child works on paper.
