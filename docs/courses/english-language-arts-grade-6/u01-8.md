## 6. Days

## Day 28

Fluency. “I can reread in phrases and explain how Mara changes.” Pairs hold the complete novella and earlier maps.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return E27 privately. Locate the opening situation for the turning-point reread.
- **10 min. Oral reread.** Model grouping a clause, then reread untimed. Fifteen pairs alternate 45-second turns and give a phrasing or accuracy cue before retrying.
- **30 min. Change map.** Use released selections from chapters 1, 10 and 16. Compare drawings and trace the choices the final one carries forward. Lift the prompts for individual annotation.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E28. In parallel pairs take up to 45 seconds per reader, then complete each map privately before feedback. Teacher hears six in 30-second turns including notes while the other pupils work. Collect every first map and listener slip. Date pending teacher observations and give each pupil a cue.

## Day 29

Application. “I can use each source for the claim it supports.” Students hold four released selections and independent source notes.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return E28 and link one action or order cue to the map.
- **5 min. P4 retry.** Return written day 27 cues. Give only the matched held language retry lines from section 1.7. Save independent responses. If a child's matched work exceeds five minutes, record the remaining parts for private makeup. A spoken gap can replace a scheduled conference turn or receive a named makeup.
- **15 min. Conferences.** Eight 90-second turns leave three minutes for records. Reuse eligible records. Others read independently.
- **3 min. Sentences.** Keep the source attached to its statement while combining two clauses.
- **17 min. Synthesis.** Reread selected lines from the novella, Yu and both explainers. Sort literary choices and scientific explanations by what each contributes. Draft a cited paragraph about what the town preserves through change. Keep each first claim before a comparison.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E29 on paper, alone.

## Day 30

Application. “I can discuss a choice and locate evidence in my folder.” Students hold N1 and the teacher holds earlier observations.

- **5 min. Submit earlier work.** Collect existing P1 to P7 artifacts and reading records. Return E29 cues.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Inside the discussion allocation, match E29's evidence or source cue to released reading.
- **25 min. Discussion.** Each child marks a claim before talk. In six concurrent groups of five, take one 45-second contribution, one 45-second question and one 45-second response per child. Agree on roles, goal and deadline, and confirm paraphrases. Discuss why the drawer remains. Teacher observation today guides practice. Record the scored P7 cohorts from days 10 and 20 with their earlier feedback and preparation revisions.
- **10 min. Annotate.** Locate an earlier decision, comment or change in the portfolio. Date that annotation as practice.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E30. Allow two minutes to save an individual cited answer, 45 seconds per partner for response and paraphrase, and 90 seconds for an individual mark. The new annotation and discussion remain practice.

## Day 32

Respond. “I can correct an answer and choose a reading goal.” Students hold their own marked C work.

- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E32 on paper, alone. Send the goal forward separately.

## 9. Supports and extension

Rebuild means build again. A community is the people connected to the place in these texts. A decision is a choice someone makes. Meet the words in the anchor and return to them in the evidence note. “My evidence supports ___ within ___.” For day 28 use up to 300 words per selected reread. For day 29 keep the four excerpts together under 400 words. Complete independent day 31 reading uses the cold story, excerpt and map. Read directions aloud if needed. Preserve identical wording and labels in large print, braille or an ordered map description. Record passage audio as supported reading and an oral or scribed response in its access condition.
