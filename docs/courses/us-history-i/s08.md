**Teacher background for the section plans (dated lines checked 2026-11-14):**

- **Fact-checking today, day 125.** As of November 2026: in 2025 some large platforms ended third-party fact-checking in the United States and moved to user-written notes. Name the practice, not the company.
- **Income tax, days 126 and 127.** The 1913 income tax reached only a few percent of households, and wage withholding began in 1943. The pay stub in E127 is made up for practice and shows a present-day stub, not how 1913 workers paid.
- **Voting rights today, day 132.** As of November 2026: in Louisiana v. Callais (decided April 29, 2026) the Supreme Court, 6 to 3, held that Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act did not require Louisiana's second majority-Black congressional district, which narrows how Section 2 can be used against district maps. Not projected backward onto 1920; stated as the law, not argued by party.
- **Native citizenship, day 132.** Many Native people were citizens before 1924, through allotment, military service or treaty. The Indian Citizenship Act of 1924 covered the rest, and states still blocked Native voting for decades after.
