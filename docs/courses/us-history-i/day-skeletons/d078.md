- [[s05-K78-1]] (example, judged by teacher): key: His public aim, his official duty, is saving the Union, by freeing all, some or none; his closing sentence keeps his personal wish that all men everywhere could be free; he did not say he had already drafted an emancipation order.; samples: Lincoln says his official aim is the Union and he would free all, some or none to save it, but he ends by saying his personal wish is that all men everywhere could be free; the letter was public, written a month after he had shown his cabinet a draft order, so it shows his public position, not his plan. / Lincoln did not care about slavery at all.
