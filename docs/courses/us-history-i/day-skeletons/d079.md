Antietam turned back Lee's invasion at once; at Vicksburg the city and its army surrendered on July 4. In Source I Congress grants the company every other section of public land along the line, so the federal government decided who would own that land. The Pacific Railway Act gave the railroad companies land and a right of way and promised to extinguish the "Indian titles", so the federal government planned to take land that the Cheyenne, Arapaho and Shoshone still held, as Source H and its gloss show. / All three battles ended the war; the government gave away empty land.
