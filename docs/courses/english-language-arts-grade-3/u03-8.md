Letters this section meets; if many made it, 5 to 10 minutes of repair.
- **S, no situation.** Wrong: "Ada said she was done." Right: "At lunch, in the garden, Ada said she was done." Shown 108 and 109. Repair: name who is where when the problem opens; 5 minutes.
- **T, a feeling without a response.** Wrong: "Ben felt nervous." Right: "Ben rubbed his folder. Was he too late? 'May I look?' he asked." Shown 110 and 111. Repair: add an action, a thought or a spoken line; 6 minutes.
- **U, a reversed time link.** Wrong: "After she erased it, she checked." (the check came first) Right: "Before she erased it, she checked." Shown 112. Repair: place two events on an arrow, then choose the word; 5 minutes.
- **V, a new problem at the end.** Wrong: the scene ends with a new question about lunch. Right: the ending closes the consequence of the scene's action. Shown 110 and 113. Repair: reread the last action and close it; 5 minutes.
- **W, a word whose effect clashes.** Wrong: "stomped" for a gentle walk. Right: "tiptoed". Shown 108 and 112. Repair: say two verbs aloud against the feeling; 5 minutes.
- **X, a class named, no function.** Wrong: "careful is an adjective." Right: "careful describes the readers." Shown 112. Repair: say what the word does here and in general; 5 minutes.
- **Y and Z, agreement.** Wrong: The readers shares his plan. Right: The readers share their plan. Shown 110 and 112. Repair: mark the subject and the referent, then choose the form; 6 minutes.
- **AE, a direction spoken as dialogue.** Wrong: reading "pause before Ben answers" aloud. Right: the voice pauses and the words stay unsaid. Shown 108. Repair: bracket the directions before the line is read; 5 minutes.
- **AF, a fragment for a clarification.** Wrong: "Her worry." Right: "I want you to see her worry in an action." Shown 108 and 111. Repair: rehearse with a subject and a verb; 5 minutes.
- **AL, speech without marks.** Wrong: Ben said Let us try. Right: Ben said, "Let us try." Shown 110 and 111 (retrieval from unit 2). Repair: mark the spoken words, then place the marks and the comma; 6 minutes.
- **L, recopying as revision.** Wrong: the same lines in neater writing. Right: one added gesture after the note, original beside. Shown 111, 112 and 113. Repair: model one margin change; 5 minutes.
- **O, an unrelated question.** Wrong: What did you have for lunch? Right: Which line shows how Ada feels? Shown 111. Repair: build the question from the writer's own line; 5 minutes.
- **AN, a report too fast.** Wrong: all four sentences in one breath. Right: a pause between each sentence for the listener. Shown 108. Repair: mark pause points and say it once for the listener; 5 minutes.
- **AH and AJ, word check.** Wrong: runing; becuase. Right: running; because. Shown in the skills block. Repair: in the next skills session; 5 minutes.

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: preview situation, closure, effect and stage direction with a picture of a stage and a script; frames "Ada is in ___ at ___", "First ___, then ___" and "I chose ___ because it sounds ___". Access: audio beside print for the script; large print; oral planning before writing; a child who cannot write much may draw the plan card and say each box. Check accommodations: read the audit card aloud (not the folder), extra time inside the 90 minutes, answers by mouth. Reading load: the script is read aloud by the teacher and volunteers; children read alone only a marked 150 to 250 words. Extension, same standard: change a second action and say how the ending changes.
