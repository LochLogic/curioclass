## 4. Expected errors and first ideas


- **S** Wrong: "Pipe is the abstract noun." Right: "Safety is the abstract noun; you cannot touch it." Shown 150 (worked example). If many: 6 minutes, sort ten nouns into things I can touch and ideas I cannot, then use one idea noun in a sentence.
- **R** Wrong: "leafs." Right: "leaves." Shown 150 exit, 152 audit. If many: 5 minutes, say the singular and irregular plural together for leaf, man, woman, then write each.
- **Y** Wrong: "Lake Bend MT 59000." Right: "Lake Bend, MT 59000." Shown 150 (worked example) and 151. If many: 5 minutes, mark the boundary between city and state with a slash before inserting the comma; no comma between state and number.
- **U** Wrong: a filter fact, an invitation, then another filter fact. Right: the filter facts together, then the invitation. Shown 151. If many: 6 minutes, sort six sentences by topic into two groups, then order them.
- **V** Wrong: "Filters are important." Right: "A filter is a layer that catches particles; sand and gravel are two layers." Shown 151 and 152 feedback. If many: 6 minutes, add a definition and a source detail to two vague sentences.
- **W** Wrong: invites a question before saying what the step does. Right: states the purpose first. Shown 152 exit. If many: a written move on the feedback; no reteaching on a check day.
- **N** Wrong: joins a treatment fact to an invitation with "because". Right: joins two treatment facts with "another". Shown 151 (feedback). If many: 6 minutes, name the relationship, then pick the link word.
- **O** Wrong: stops after an unfinished example. Right: a closing sentence that returns to the issue and invites a question. Shown 151. If many: 5 minutes, add a close to two letters.
- **T** Wrong: only a clean copy of the letter. Right: one grouped change made after the feedback. Shown 152. If many: written move, no reteaching.
- **X** Wrong: offers one sitting as evidence of sustained writing. Right: the folder's dated plan, draft and revision. Shown 152. If many: written move on the audit sheet.
- **AK** Wrong: says river for remembering. Right: reads every syllable, then checks the sentence. Shown 150 (Long words regroup). If many: 5 minutes, break two long words from the 149 script into syllables and blend each.
- **AS** Wrong: rhymes said with paid. Right: said as it is spoken. Shown 150. If many: 5 minutes, map the spelling to the spoken word, then reread in a sentence.
- **AR** Wrong: siting. Right: sitting. Shown 150 and 151. If many: 5 minutes, sit plus ing, smile plus ed, happy to happiness.
