- **N, interrupting.** Wrong: speaking before the speaker ends. Right: wait for the end, then use the turn cue. Shown 105. Repair: rehearse the cue in the group, one pass; 5 minutes.
- **O, an unrelated question.** Wrong: What is your favorite food? Right: Which page shows that? Shown 105. Repair: build the question from the speaker's last word; 5 minutes.
- **P, repeating the first idea.** Wrong: "I still think the same." Right: "Your page helped me see why she changes." Shown 105 and 106. Repair: name one peer detail that strengthened the idea; 5 minutes.
- **Q, a vague answer.** Wrong: Stuff happens. Right: She unfolds the plan, so Ben can check it. Shown 105 and 107. Repair: replace stuff with the action and why it matters; 5 minutes.
- **L, recopying as revision.** Wrong: the same sentences in neater writing. Right: one added explanation from the note, original beside. Shown 105 and 107. Repair: model one margin change; 5 minutes.
- **I and J, reasons and links.** Wrong: I recommend it because it is good; Therefore the beans are green. Right: I recommend it because Ada asks the readers; so the plan improves. Shown 106 (audit). Repair: read both ideas and choose the link; 7 minutes.

## Day 105

- kind: Application. Applies taught work to a new task or case (an inquiry, a lab, a draft, a revision or edit); nothing is taught as new.
- goal: Prepare an evidence-based series discussion and rehearse building on another speaker.
- note: Reads: g3-u3-01 (Chapter-book series, book 1 (read whole)); g3-u3-02 (Chapter-book series, book 2 (at least three connected chapters, or whole as the independent book)). Base 2017 G3 M3 U2 L13; adapted on these texts. Skills block: independent reading log, conferences and small-group teaching. Partner talk and a written exit ticket require the listed text. Daily skills block: use the week list and pattern sequence in the scope and sequence. Decode words, never guess them. Sensitivity: gentle contemporary family or school conflict; teacher previews the optional independent classic. Formal text-based discussion: passage preparation, norms, linking to a peer, asking for detail, explaining after listening; teacher records individual evidence, models roles and fades sentence frames. Product O02: Book review; opinion, process; count once across 101-106.
- credit: adapted from EL G3 M3 U2 L13, Preparing for and Participating in a Text-Based Discussion (keep)

- **10 min. Word check.** Thirty children at twenty seconds each, called one at a time to the teacher's table (10 minutes); the class meanwhile writes six dictated words (said, because, sitting, smiled, cries, happiness), writes stopped, hoping and happiness from stop, hope and happy and names each base change, reads a short passage silently, and answers two affix-meaning questions. At the table each child reads four irregular words (said, does, were, their) and one longer word; the teacher records each reading.
- **20 min. Independent reading and conferences.** Log day: four 4-minute conferences (16) and two log lines read aloud at 2 minutes each (4); the heard-six column is filled. No new teaching.
- **5 min. Read the note.** E104 comes back with its commonest letter beside the correction (2 minutes). The draft comes back with the written note from 104; each child marks the one change to make (3 minutes).
- **5 min. Roles and preparation.** The discussion moves chart is read (mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer). Teacher models one linked question (2); children choose a question frame (3).
- **10 min. Prepare.** Children mark one passage in each book (150 to 250 words each, reread in 3 minutes), then write a prepared claim that answers "Would you recommend this series to a friend, and why?" with the detail and its page, and one linked question for a neighbor (7).
