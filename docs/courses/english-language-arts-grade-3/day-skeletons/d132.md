## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Use comparison feedback and discuss access, demand and pollution from the texts.
- **Note:** Reads: g3-u4-01 (Informational book on water on Earth); g3-u4-02 (How far people in one named region walk for water today); g3-u4-03 (How a US town cleans its water); g3-u4-04 (Population Growth (excerpt of "The Effects of Urbanization on Water Quality: Population Growth")). Base 2017 G3 M4 U2 L1; adapted on these texts. Skills block: partner rereading with teacher feedback, then grammar retrieval. Partner talk and a written exit ticket require the listed text. Daily skills block: use the week list and pattern sequence in the scope and sequence. Decode words, never guess them. Sensitivity: water scarcity and disease plainly named; community solutions and real place names; no personal disclosures. Formal text-based discussion: passage preparation, norms, linking to a peer, asking for detail, explaining after listening; teacher records individual evidence, models roles and fades sentence frames. Existing exit response becomes which issue the class should explain first. It replaces the usual response and adds no task. Give opinion, organized reasons, linking words and closing. Product O09: Choose an issue to explain first; opinion, one sitting; count once across 132.
