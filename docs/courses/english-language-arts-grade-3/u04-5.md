## 1. Standards and practices


This section's share: W.3.7 and W.3.8 "short research projects ... brief notes on sources and sort evidence into provided categories" are applied on 137 (attributed notes in the four categories), then audited on 144; RI.3.5 "use text features and search tools (key words, sidebars, hyperlinks) to locate information relevant to a given topic efficiently" on 137 (heading and keyword search on print and a device) and scored on 154; W.3.1a to W.3.1d "introduce the topic ... state an opinion ... reasons ... linking words ... concluding statement" for the O04 water essay on 138 (plan), 139 (opening and first reason), 140 and 141 (reasons); W.3.5 "with guidance and support from peers and adults, develop and strengthen writing" on 138 and 140 as written feedback revision; W.3.4 on 140 and 141; L.3.1b and L.3.1c on 139 (plurals and abstract nouns in the draft); SL.3.1a to SL.3.1d on 141 (a prepared passage and a discussion, evidence recorded by the teacher). Fifteen devices serve thirty children in two ten-minute turns on 137; the other fifteen annotate print. Each draft day ends on one finished piece, not a whole essay.

## 3. Checks, written first


Items owned here are scored later: RI.3.5 on 154 (Show a heading and search keyword that locate channel information), W.3.1a to W.3.1b on 144 and 145, W.3.7 and W.3.8 on 144 (the research folder started on 137), SL.3.1a and SL.3.1d on 156 (taken from the 141 discussion record). The table above holds each Meets and Does not. Nothing is scored in 137 to 141; exits are single asks on unseen cases, marked 2, 1, 0.

**Exit 137.** Unseen cards. Heading: Wells. "A cap keeps dirt out of a well." Heading: Taps. "A tap is turned off when it is not used." Ask: "Write a brief note for solutions from the Wells card. Name its source." Answer: "cap keeps dirt out of well (Wells card)" or similar. Wrong: copies the whole card (Q); files under 'demand' (AQ); an invented episode taken as a source fact (AP).

**Exit 138.** Unseen: "A sign at the dock asks boaters to keep fuel out of the river." Ask: "Which should a class explain first: stopping fuel before it enters, or testing after? Write one opinion sentence." Answer: any sentence that picks one, such as "Our class should explain stopping fuel first." Wrong: only a topic, "fuel and water" (L); one that names a choice but the plan's link word misstates, "so" for "but" (N).

**Exit 139.** Unseen: "Plan: Explain why care matters. Source: A helper stacked one clean dish on top of another dish." Ask: "Write a first reason sentence from the source. Use the plural of dish." Answer: for example "Care matters because the helper stacked clean dishes." Wrong: dishs or dishies (AO, M if no source fact); "I like care" (M).

**Exit 140.** Unseen: "The intake screen caught twigs. Workers checked it after rain. Reader: I do not know what a screen does." Ask: "Write one sentence that tells this reader what the screen does." Answer: it catches twigs so they stay out of the water. Wrong: "Screens are great" (W); "I like screens" (M).

**Exit 141.** Unseen: "Notes: A cracked lid lets dirt in. A new lid keeps dirt out. Opinion: Explain lid care first. Peer: I think lids do not matter." Ask: "Write one reply to your peer. Join the notes to the opinion with a link word." Answer: for example "I see it differently because a cracked lid lets dirt in, so lid care keeps water clean." Wrong: liking (M); a link word that misstates, "but" for "because" (N); a change of topic (AH).

## 6. Days

## Day 137


- **10 min. Long words.** Prompts on research, evidence, example; irregular words; the ending-rule line.
- **20 min. Skills block, Monday.** Grammar 5 minutes (a noun and its pronoun, "The note ... it"); partner rereading 15 minutes with a feedback card.
- **5 min. Opening.** Find a feature: given the question "how does a well stay clean?", find a heading on a print in two minutes; say the keyword aloud.
- **25 min. Reading.** Feature search 5 minutes; independent comparison of two texts 10 minutes (silent, one shared and one different point); the C137 reading responses 10 minutes (the card "Channel checks": RI.3.3, RI.3.5, RI.3.7, RI.3.8).
- **20 min. Research.** Notes in four categories with a source tag each; two 10-minute device turns for 15 children each, the others annotate the print pages and swap at the turn; each child writes at least four brief notes.
- **5 min. Partner talk.** Read one note aloud (2 minutes each, 1 to change over).
- **5 min. Exit.** The Wells card (exit 137).

Carried: the category sheet. Tool: 15 keyboard devices, light use, two turns of 10 minutes, practice with feedback. By hand: print notes. Evidence: exit 137, the research folder, C137 responses. ## 7. Talk and writing

Planned questions: "Which heading and keyword find this fastest?" (137); "What does the reader not yet know?" (140); "How does your evidence support the opinion?" (141, discussion). Written task: the O04 draft (opening, reason 1, reason 2). Look-fors: the opinion is stated; each reason uses a source detail; each reason has a link phrase; plurals are correct; the reader's need is met. Discussion look-fors: a prepared passage, a link to a peer, a request for detail, an explanation after listening.

## 8. Practice that returns

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: a word strip "research, evidence, example, conclusion" with pictures; the sentence frame "I think ... because ... for example ...". Access: a printed plan list on a card; extra time with the device turn; typing may replace pencil. Reading load: no new text. Extension: a third reason with a source, or a note that answers a reader's question.

## 10. Two rooms

A parent teaching one child: ask "What do you think, and why?" then write two reasons as a list; say each source aloud. One projector: show a heading search once; children work in print; the device turns are replaced by two ten-minute print turns.
