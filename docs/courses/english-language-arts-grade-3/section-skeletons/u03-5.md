- **RL.3.9** (days 98, 99): Compare and contrast the themes, settings, and plots of stories written by the same author about the same or similar characters (e.g., in books from a series).
- **W.3.1a** (days 98, 100): Introduce the topic or text they are writing about, state an opinion, and create an organizational structure that lists reasons.
- **W.3.1b** (days 98, 100): Provide reasons that support the opinion.
- **W.3.1c** (days 98, 100): Use linking words and phrases (e.g., because, therefore, since, for example) to connect opinion and reasons.
- **W.3.1d** (days 98, 100): Provide a concluding statement or section.
- **SL.3.1a** (days 98): Come to discussions prepared, having read or studied required material; explicitly draw on that preparation and other information known about the topic to explore ideas under discussion.
- **SL.3.1b** (days 98): Follow agreed-upon rules for discussions (e.g., gaining the floor in respectful ways, listening to others with care, speaking one at a time about the topics and texts under discussion).
- **SL.3.1c** (days 98): Ask questions to check understanding of information presented, stay on topic, and link their comments to the remarks of others.
- **SL.3.1d** (days 98): Explain their own ideas and understanding in light of the discussion.
- **SL.3.3** (days 98): Ask and answer questions about information from a speaker, offering appropriate elaboration and detail.
- **RF.3.3d** (days 98, 100): Read grade-appropriate irregularly spelled words.
- **L.3.2e** (days 98, 100): Use conventional spelling for high-frequency and other studied words and for adding suffixes to base words (e.g., sitting, smiled, cries, happiness).
- **L.3.2f** (days 98, 100): Use spelling patterns and generalizations (e.g., word families, position-based spellings, syllable patterns, ending rules, meaningful word parts) in writing words.
- **W.3.10** (days 98, 100): Write routinely over extended time frames (time for research, reflection, and revision) and shorter time frames (a single sitting or a day or two) for a range of discipline-specific tasks, purposes, and audiences.
- **RF.3.3c** (days 98, 100): Decode multisyllable words.
- **RL.3.1** (days 99): Ask and answer questions to demonstrate understanding of a text, referring explicitly to the text as the basis for the answers.
- **W.3.5** (days 100): With guidance and support from peers and adults, develop and strengthen writing as needed by planning, revising, and editing. (Editing for conventions should demonstrate command of Language standards 1–3 up to and including grade 3 on page 29.)
- **L.3.2a** (days 100): Capitalize appropriate words in titles.
