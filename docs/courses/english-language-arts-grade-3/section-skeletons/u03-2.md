- **RL.3.4** (days 90): Determine the meaning of words and phrases as they are used in a text, distinguishing literal from nonliteral language.
- **L.3.5a** (days 90): Distinguish the literal and nonliteral meanings of words and phrases in context (e.g., take steps).
