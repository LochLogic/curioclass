### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Rehearse access, evidence and prepare with "The words ___ explain ___." Offer large print and tactile or factual image descriptions. Scribe explanations, not spelling evidence. Read context aloud; record help on independent checks. Extend by defending a different detail from the same text. Keep the same goal.
