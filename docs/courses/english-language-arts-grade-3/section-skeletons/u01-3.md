## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **RL.3.1** (days 10, 11, 12, 13): Ask and answer questions to demonstrate understanding of a text, referring explicitly to the text as the basis for the answers.
- **RL.3.2** (days 10, 12, 13): Recount stories, including fables, folktales, and myths from diverse cultures; determine the central message, lesson, or moral and explain how it is conveyed through key details in the text.
- **RL.3.3** (days 10, 13): Describe characters in a story (e.g., their traits, motivations, or feelings) and explain how their actions contribute to the sequence of events.
- **RF.3.3d** (days 10, 11, 12): Read grade-appropriate irregularly spelled words.
- **L.3.2e** (days 10, 11, 12): Use conventional spelling for high-frequency and other studied words and for adding suffixes to base words (e.g., sitting, smiled, cries, happiness).
- **L.3.2f** (days 10, 11, 12): Use spelling patterns and generalizations (e.g., word families, position-based spellings, syllable patterns, ending rules, meaningful word parts) in writing words.
- **W.3.10** (days 10, 11, 12): Write routinely over extended time frames (time for research, reflection, and revision) and shorter time frames (a single sitting or a day or two) for a range of discipline-specific tasks, purposes, and audiences.
- **RL.3.4** (days 11, 12, 13): Determine the meaning of words and phrases as they are used in a text, distinguishing literal from nonliteral language.
- **L.3.5a** (days 11, 12, 13): Distinguish the literal and nonliteral meanings of words and phrases in context (e.g., take steps).
- **RL.3.7** (days 11, 13): Explain how specific aspects of a text’s illustrations contribute to what is conveyed by the words in a story (e.g., create mood, emphasize aspects of a character or setting).
- **L.3.4a** (days 11): Use sentence-level context as a clue to the meaning of a word or phrase.
- **L.3.4b** (days 11): Determine the meaning of the new word formed when a known affix is added to a known word (e.g., agreeable/disagreeable, comfortable/uncomfortable, care/careless, heat/preheat).
- **L.3.4c** (days 11): Use a known root word as a clue to the meaning of an unknown word with the same root (e.g., company, companion).
- **L.3.4d** (days 11): Use glossaries or beginning dictionaries, both print and digital, to determine or clarify the precise meaning of key words and phrases.
- **RF.3.3a** (days 11, 12): Identify and know the meaning of the most common prefixes and derivational suffixes.
- **W.3.4** (days 12): With guidance and support from adults, produce writing in which the development and organization are appropriate to task and purpose. (Grade-specific expectations for writing types are defined in standards 1–3 above.)
- **RF.3.4a** (days 12): Read grade-level text with purpose and understanding.
- **RF.3.4b** (days 12): Read grade-level prose and poetry orally with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings
- **RF.3.4c** (days 12): Use context to confirm or self-correct word recognition and understanding, rereading as necessary.
