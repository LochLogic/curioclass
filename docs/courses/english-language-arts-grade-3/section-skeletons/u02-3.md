> (shared across the unit) Non-assessment days use five text tasks: the main written response below, the hard-part reread, partner talk on that reread question, a word-in-sentence explanation from the current list, and U3's exit. Rereading and talk are separate responses: point to words first, then explain how a partner's answer changes your account. Budget reading and reread 15, word and sentence work 10, partner talk 10, writing 20, exit 5. The opening sits in reading or skills, not additional time. The 30-minute skills block is separate. Respond days use feedback 10, the written repair 20, reread 10, word and sentence work 10, talk 5 and exit 5. On 72 use feedback 10, repair 10, reread 10, word work 5, talk 5, conclusion drafting 15 and exit 5. On 77 use field 7's allocation, with rereading and word explanation inside discussion. On assessment days field 5 replaces these tasks; silent verification never becomes teacher correction. Later feedback and targeted component revisions replace related writing, sentence-work or talk practice within existing slots; students do not repeat whole sittings.

### U8. Practice that returns

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Daily skills begin with ten minutes of long-word reading and spelling: week 8 retrieves -ness, -ly and -able; weeks 9 to 10 apply -tion and -sion; 11 to 12 teach -ment and -ous; 13 to 14 mix suffixes and ending rules; 15 to 16 transfer them. Read bases, blend, then check meaning. Partner rereading twice weekly uses accuracy, understanding, natural pace and expression, with no rate target. Independent reading has a log and conference. Keep the whole frog book available for a sustained reread.
<!-- /skel -->

### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Preview habitat and transparent with diagrams. Use "The words ___ explain ___." Rehearse aloud before writing. Offer large print and factual diagram descriptions, keeping grade-level texts. Independent reading loads are a poem or 150 to 250 words; longer tales are first heard, then reread in chunks. Extend by defending another supported connection.
<!-- /skel -->
