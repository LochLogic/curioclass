- **10 min. Long words.** Prompts on announce, deliver, clarify; irregular words; the ending-rule line.
- **20 min. Skills block, log day.** Four conferences (16) and two log lines read aloud (4).
- **5 min. Opening.** Use the written move from 145 (L to R, AO) on one sentence each; the commonest group works with the teacher.
- **20 min. Hook and model.** Hook: the teacher reads two lines aloud, once flat and fast, once with pauses (3 minutes). The printed model "Cleaning River Water in Four Steps" (70 words, one bracket cue [Pause before heavy.]) is read aloud once and children mark main idea and details on a listening card (5 minutes); the teacher shows the worked example, a pause and not the cue words (4 minutes); a second read with marking of one pause and one slow phrase (5 minutes); share (3 minutes).
- **10 min. Reading.** Reread "The steps" in g3-u4-03 (about 190 words, silent 3 minutes, then partner reading 4 minutes); mark the four source facts the model used.
- **10 min. Writing.** On the printed model, children write the main idea and two details in two sentences (4 minutes); then the contrast pair, one fact joined by "because" and one by "therefore", and a close against no close: say which is clear (4 minutes); one complete-sentence clarification of a step name (2 minutes).
- **5 min. Partner talk.** Say the clarification aloud in a complete sentence (2 minutes each, 1 to change over).
- **5 min. Word in context.** "delivery" in its sentence.
- **5 min. Exit.** The bus yard and cue cards (exits 146 and 146b); the scrub question (146c) is asked aloud and written in the Exit box.

Carried: the printed model and the listening card. Tool: None. By hand: all. Evidence: listening response, delivery annotation, exit 146. Hand-backs in: 145 written moves. Out: listening card in folder. At home (35 minutes): read for 20 minutes; No score.

- **10 min. Long words.** Irregular words; two words with -ment (statement, movement); the ending-rule line.
- **20 min. Skills block, log day.** Four conferences (16) and two log lines read aloud (4).
- **5 min. Opening.** Read the printed model script from 146; find its beginning, linked facts and close.
- **20 min. Reading.** Reread the sources for the three key points (10 minutes); a peer reads the plan against the look-for "facts in order" (5 minutes); the teacher returns one comment on the plan (5 minutes).
- **20 min. Draft and type (I10).** Write the script; each child types for one 8-minute turn, in two turns of fifteen devices, leaving 4 minutes to switch and save (8 + 8 + 4); the typed turn holds one attributed paragraph and one digital question to a peer; the other fifteen draft on paper. The teacher watches the device room.
- **5 min. Partner talk.** The peer reads the paragraph aloud and says what the question asks (2 minutes each, 1 to change over).
- **5 min. Word in context.** "attribute" in its sentence, or "according to".
- **5 min. Exit.** The gate card (exit 148).

Carried: the script. Tool: 15 keyboard devices, light use, 8-minute turns, practice with feedback (mark: typed text, the saved file, the question). By hand: the other turn. Evidence: earlier script, device log, exit 148. Hand-backs in: plan comment. Out: script with a peer look-for. At home (35 minutes): read for 20 minutes; No score.

## Day 149

Sensitivity: water scarcity and disease plainly named; community solutions and real place names; no personal disclosures. Product I10: Announcement script; recording counted with script; informative, process; count once across 147-149; 153.

- **10 min. Long words.** Irregular words; a prompt on -ly and -ful words; the ending-rule line.
- **20 min. Skills block, Wednesday.** Grammar 5 minutes (written and spoken: "gonna" and "going to"; a cue is not said); partner rereading 15 minutes of the script with a feedback card.
- **15 min. Reading.** Opening 5 minutes: read the peer's digital question from 148. Listening response 10 minutes: the teacher reads aloud once "Water that stays" from g3-u4-02 (the sand dam paragraph, about 65 words) and once "Why the order matters" from g3-u4-03 (about 60 words); children write the main idea and one detail from each; the sheets are collected.
- **15 min. Cue plan and rehearse.** Compare the script with an audio cue plan on paper (pause, slow, emphasis; written cues are not spoken); revise after the peer's question (6 minutes); each child records one 4-minute turn on a device in two turns of fifteen (4 + 4 + 1 to switch): the speech, a clarification in a complete sentence, and one cue change; types a short reply to the peer. The others rehearse aloud in pairs. The teacher writes component feedback for 150.
- **20 min. Discussion.** Two minutes for the prepared passage and the question, then six recorded groups of five, 3 minutes each (6 x 3 = 18): each child asks or answers one question about the two accounts in a complete sentence (about 36 seconds). Six devices record; the other nine devices stay off.
- **5 min. Word in context.** "clarify" in its sentence.
- **5 min. Exit.** The cue card (exit 149).

Carried: the script and the cue plan. Tool: 15 devices, light use, 4-minute turns (mark: file saved, reply typed). By hand: the cue plan, the listening card. Evidence: 149 speech, clarification and cue change (recorded), digital exchange, listening response, exit 149. Hand-backs in: peer question. Out: script, cue plan and recordings for written feedback before 150. At home (35 minutes): read for 20 minutes; No score.

## 7. Talk and writing

Planned questions: "What is the main idea, and which detail proves it?" (146); "What does your listener already know?" (147); "Which words would you slow down so the fact is not lost?" (149). Written task: the I10 script (147 to 149). Look-fors: the main idea is stated first; each fact has a source; facts are in order and linked; the close returns to the main idea; sentences are complete; a written cue is shown by a pause, not said.

## 8. Practice that returns

146: opinion close (142), the order and cause links (u02-5), source facts in g3-u4-03. 147: the note card with source tag (137), the category sheet (128), the opinion frame (143). 148: the script model (146), plurals and abstract nouns (136, 139, 142). 149: the discussion with five boxes (130, 141) and partner rereading (u01-1); the 145 written moves.

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: a word strip "announce, listener, pause, clarify, source" with pictures; the frame "My main idea is ... One detail is ... because ...". Access: the script may be read from a printed card; a scribe may type; a partner may read the cue plan aloud; headphones for the recording turn. Check accommodations: not applicable (no check day). Reading load: 146 reads one printed model and one page; no new text. Extension: a second announcement for a different listener.

## 10. Two rooms

A parent teaching one child: the child says what a listener needs to know first, writes three key points, then says them aloud; mark a pause with a slash, never read aloud; a phone may record one minute if the family has one and it stays at home. One projector: show the model once; the device turns become two print turns; one recorder is passed from pair to pair.
