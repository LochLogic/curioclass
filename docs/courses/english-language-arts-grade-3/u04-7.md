## 4. Expected errors and first ideas


- **L** Wrong: "Water." Right: "Our class should explain taps first." Shown 146 (first five minutes). If many: 5 minutes, add the choice with "should" to three topic-only openings.
- **M** Wrong: "Wells are best because I like them." Right: "because a well keeps dirt out." Shown 146. If many: 5 minutes, swap liking for a source detail.
- **N** Wrong: "The clumps are heavy, therefore the water is stirred." Right: "Because the clumps are heavy, they sink." Shown 146 as the model contrast. If many: 6 minutes, name the relationship, then pick the connector.
- **O** Wrong: an announcement with no close. Right: a close that returns to the main idea. Shown 146. If many: 5 minutes, add a close to two model scripts.
- **P** Wrong: "I learned that I like sand dams." Right: "I learned a sand dam holds water in the sand." Shown 146. If many: 5 minutes, write one thing learned with its source.
- **Q** Wrong: copies a whole paragraph into a note. Right: six words and a source tag. Shown 146. If many: 5 minutes, shorten one fact.
- **R** Wrong: "mans." Right: "men." Shown 146. If many: 5 minutes, say singular and irregular plural together.
- **AO** Wrong: "boxs." Right: "boxes." Shown 146. If many: 5 minutes, compare box and boxes, then edit one more.
- **W** Wrong: asks for storage when the listener knows storage. Right: begins at the care claim. Shown 147. If many: 5 minutes, write what this listener knows and does not know above the plan.
- **AA** Wrong: "Turning off." Right: "Care means the crew turns off the hose." Shown 146 and 147. If many: 5 minutes, restate three fragments with a subject and verb, aloud then on paper.
- **AB** Wrong: reads "[Pause before care]" aloud. Right: a pause, with no cue words. Shown 146 and 149. If many: 5 minutes, the teacher reads a line with the cue spoken, then with a pause; children say which is right.
- **V** Wrong: "Maintenance is good." Right: "Maintain means to keep it working; checking the handle is one task." Shown 148. If many: 6 minutes, add a definition and one source detail to two vague sentences.
- **T** Wrong: a recopied cue plan. Right: a changed phrase that answers the peer. Shown 149. If many: 5 minutes, show one change from feedback.

## 6. Days

## Day 146


- **10 min. Long words.** Prompts on announce, deliver, clarify; irregular words; the ending-rule line.
- **20 min. Skills block, log day.** Four conferences (16) and two log lines read aloud (4).
- **5 min. Opening.** Use the written move from 145 (L to R, AO) on one sentence each; the commonest group works with the teacher.
- **20 min. Hook and model.** Hook: the teacher reads two lines aloud, once flat and fast, once with pauses (3 minutes). The printed model "Cleaning River Water in Four Steps" (70 words, one bracket cue [Pause before heavy.]) is read aloud once and children mark main idea and details on a listening card (5 minutes); the teacher shows the worked example, a pause and not the cue words (4 minutes); a second read with marking of one pause and one slow phrase (5 minutes); share (3 minutes).
- **10 min. Reading.** Reread "The steps" in g3-u4-03 (about 190 words, silent 3 minutes, then partner reading 4 minutes); mark the four source facts the model used.
- **10 min. Writing.** On the printed model, children write the main idea and two details in two sentences (4 minutes); then the contrast pair, one fact joined by "because" and one by "therefore", and a close against no close: say which is clear (4 minutes); one complete-sentence clarification of a step name (2 minutes).
- **5 min. Partner talk.** Say the clarification aloud in a complete sentence (2 minutes each, 1 to change over).
- **5 min. Word in context.** "delivery" in its sentence.
- **5 min. Exit.** The bus yard and cue cards (exits 146 and 146b); the scrub question (146c) is asked aloud and written in the Exit box.

Carried: the printed model and the listening card. Tool: None. By hand: all. Evidence: listening response, delivery annotation, exit 146. Hand-backs in: 145 written moves. Out: listening card in folder. At home (35 minutes): read for 20 minutes; No score.
