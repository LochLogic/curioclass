## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview habitat, evidence, sentence, publish and reflect with the book's drawings; frames "I changed ___ because the note said ___." and "You said ___; which words show it?"; rehearse aloud before writing or typing; a partner who shares a language.

- **Extension, same standard.** Add a fourth paragraph that compares what the book and the excerpt each give, linked to the conclusion.
