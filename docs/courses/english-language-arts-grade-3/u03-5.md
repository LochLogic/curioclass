## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.3.9** ("compare and contrast the themes, settings, and plots of stories written by the same author about the same or similar characters"): used on 98 (recounting both arcs) and assessed on 99 (owned item); repaired on 100.
- **RL.3.1** ("ask and answer questions ... referring explicitly to the text"): assessed on 99 with a comparison question (owned item).
- **W.3.1a, b, c, d** ("introduce the topic ... state an opinion"; "provide reasons that support the opinion"; "use linking words and phrases"; "provide a concluding statement"): practiced on 98 (a reasons list and a spoken reason) and 100 (O01). The taught lessons are 101 and after.
- **SL.3.1a, b, c, d and SL.3.3** (prepare; follow rules; ask to check understanding, stay on topic and link; explain ideas after the discussion; "ask and answer questions about information from a speaker"): practiced in the formal discussion on 98.
- **W.3.5** ("with guidance and support from peers and adults, develop and strengthen writing as needed by planning, revising"): practiced on 100 (one note, one change, original kept beside).
- **L.3.2a** ("capitalize appropriate words in titles"): practiced on 100 from a printed title model; taught on 102.
- **RF.3.3c, RF.3.3d, L.3.2e, L.3.2f, W.3.10** (decode multisyllable words; irregular words; suffix spelling; patterns; routine writing): week 20, with the affix words and retrieval.

Limits that bite: the discussion on 98 needs the teacher for 30 minutes at the table with six groups of five; 25 children write alone. Day 99 is silent with no reteaching. Day 100's Return and repair uses at most four groups. The teacher page must explain the five recorded boxes, the 22-minute and 8-minute item split on 99, and that the revised 97 recount is not a check item.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Day 99 items (30 minutes, silent, reserved passages P99a and P99b and the books).** Marking scale: 0, 1 or 2 on the unit keys, scored once after feedback on day 100; the repair is not rescored.
- **RL.3.9 (22 minutes).** Compare theme, setting and plot separately, giving a supported similarity and difference in each. Key: theme, both value questions (Ada names their help), but book 1 values testing accuracy while book 2 also values different needs; setting, both are school reading places (corner and courtyard), but one is indoors beside a cupboard and the other outdoors by a tree; plot, both revise Ada's drawing after Ben questions it, but walking exposes a wrong arrow in one while a partner's difficulty changes seating in the other. Score all six claims separately. Wrong: similarities without differences (F). Source to check: the day 99 key against both books before use.
- **RL.3.1 (8 minutes).** Ask and answer a comparison question with a located detail from each book. Key: How does listening help? It corrects the plan in book 1 and the seating in book 2. Wrong: Ben flies over the school to fix both plans (A).
- RL.3.2, the recount and message, is sampled through the retained 97 response and its revision in the independent preparation block (owned by section 3.4). The day 114 RL.3.9 row belongs to section 3.9.

**Exit questions (E98 to E100).** One ask each, on a new case. Printed "invented for practice", sentences of at most 15 words. Unscored.
- **E98.** A reader says, "I like stories where someone asks for help." Book: "In The Moss Path, Dev loses his trail map. He asks every hiker for one clue and finds the way." Ask: Write one sentence recommending The Moss Path to this reader. Use because and an event. Answer: I recommend The Moss Path because Dev asks every hiker for one clue and finds the way. Wrong: I recommend it because it is a good story (I); I recommend it because Dev flies over the trail (A). Printed "invented for practice". Data: s03-X98.
- **E99.** Book 1, chapter 12, from "At the end of the route"; book 2, chapter 5, from "By ten o'clock" (outside P99, P92 and C12). Ask: How are the endings alike and different? Use but. Answer: in both everyone finds a place, but in book 1 they walk a route to the corner and in book 2 each reader picks a courtyard spot. Wrong: everyone finds a place in both books (F); in book 2 the readers go home early (A). Data: s03-X99v2.
- **E100.** Review: "I recommend the Harbor Street books. First, Lu admits a mistake and starts over. Second, she waits for the last child to finish a puzzle." Ask: Write a closing line that tells who might like these books. Answer: Try them if you like stories about patient friends. Wrong: Try them if you like soccer (K); My lunch was good (K). Printed "invented for practice". Data: s03-X100.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas


Letters this section meets; if many made it, 5 to 10 minutes of repair.
- **F, similarities only.** Wrong: both are school reading places. Right: both are school reading places, but one is indoors and one outdoors. Shown 99 and 100. Repair: alike and different chart, one row each; 7 minutes.
- **A, invented evidence.** Wrong: Ben flies over the school. Right: Ben's question changes the plan in each book. Shown 98 to 100. Repair: finger on the page; 5 minutes.
- **I, opinion repeated as reason.** Wrong: I recommend it because it is good. Right: I recommend it because Ada asks the readers where to sit. Shown 98 and 100. Repair: ask what happened in the book that proves it; 7 minutes.
- **K, unrelated ending.** Wrong: Try them if you like soccer. Right: Try them if you like stories about patient friends. Shown 100. Repair: reread the reasons and name who they would suit; 5 minutes.
- **O, unrelated question.** Wrong: What is your favorite food? Right: Which change made you choose book 2? Shown 98. Repair: reread the linked-question frame; 5 minutes.
- **L, recopy as revision.** Wrong: the same sentence in neater writing. Right: one change made from the note. Shown 99 and 100. Repair: model one change in the margin; 5 minutes.
- **R and AM, title capitals.** Wrong: the courtyard club, or The Courtyard Club Of Books. Right: The Courtyard Club: A Place for Everyone, copied from the printed title model. Shown 100 (practice). Repair: compare with the model and lowercase small words; 5 minutes.
- **AI, wrong syllable division.** Wrong: o-pi-nion. Right: o-pin-ion. Shown 98. Repair: split aloud, check each chunk, blend; 5 minutes.

## 6. Days

## Day 98


- **10 min. Long words.** Three prompts on paper: read and spell isolated words (3 minutes), read and spell a sentence from today's chapter (3), decode one word in its paragraph and say what it means (4). Three-syllable words, split, check each chunk, then blend: o-pin-ion, hap-pi-ness, ex-cit-ing. Irregular: believe, busy. One line of ending-rule retrieval (drop e, double the consonant, change y). Week 20: check the meaning after decoding.
- **20 min. Independent reading and conferences.** Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each) and two children read a log line aloud at 2 minutes each (4 minutes); the heard-six column is filled. No new teaching.
- **5 min. Opening repair and retrieve.** Return E97 with its repair (2 minutes). Retrieve opinion with because from grade 2: pairs state an opinion about a food and a reason with because, then say how a reason differs from the opinion repeated (3 minutes).
- **20 min. Teaching and preparation.** Teacher teaches (8 minutes): an opinion says what I think; a reason says why, from a change in the books; a link word such as because, therefore or since joins them; a closing line tells who might like it. The class model is on book 1 (printed, teacher-made). Children write one prepared reason from a change in an arc and a linked question for a partner (6 minutes). Pairs rehearse the reason and the question, 2 minutes each child (4 minutes). Teacher restates the norms and roles (2 minutes).
- **30 min. Formal discussion window.** Formal text-based discussion (SL.3.1 practiced, not taught): six groups of five, 5 minutes each at the teacher's table (1 minute a child). The question: which book would you recommend first to a friend, and why? The other 25 write two reasons for their recommendation, each from a change in an arc, with a link word (15 minutes), then a slip on what the talk changed or kept (5 minutes, filled as their group returns), and the word in context: reason, in a sentence about a book (5 minutes). The teacher records five boxes per child: marked detail, turn, linked question, answer, what it clarified. A late group is not extended past the window.
- **5 min. Exit.** E98.

- **Shared shape.** The class model on book 1 and the reasons list share their shape with s03-X98 (W.3.1b): an opinion, because, and an event. The exit case (a trail map) is not in the books or the model.
Carried: each child's prepared reason and linked question, the reasons list and the slip; the teacher holds the discussion record and the 97 recounts. Tool: None. By hand: the prepared reason, the reasons list, the slip, the exit. Evidence: individual discussion record (five boxes), the reasons list, E98. Hand-backs in: E97. Out: the 97 recount goes to the teacher for a written note in the evening of day 98; E98 to the teacher. At home (30 to 40 minutes): read for 20 minutes from the log book, then tell someone at home which book you would give a friend first, and one reason. No score.

## Day 99


- **30 min. Skills, retrieval only.** Ten minutes of long-word retrieval on paper, no new pattern and no teaching: week 19 and 20 words, the affix words and the irregular words. Five minutes of ending-rule retrieval on slates. Fifteen minutes of independent reading from the log book; no conferences.
- **15 min. Source reading.** Silent. Children reread the two printed passages P99a and P99b (reserved, from the two books, each 150 to 250 words) with both books open to those pages, and their finished maps. The teacher answers nothing about content.
- **30 min. Items.** Silent, on paper. RL.3.9 (22 minutes): compare theme, setting and plot separately from P99a, P99b and the books, a supported similarity and a difference in each (six claims, scored separately). RL.3.1 (8 minutes): ask and answer a comparison question with a located detail from each book. Keys are in field 3.
- **10 min. Independent preparation.** The teacher returns the written note on each child's 97 recount and message. Children revise it with the one-note, one-change card (RL.3.2 practiced; not a check item and not inside the 22 minutes). The revision, with the first version beside it, goes to the teacher.
- **5 min. Exit.** E99.

- **Shared shape.** The items share their shape with s03-X99v2 (RL.3.9): a likeness and a difference for plots. The exit reads the two book endings, not the packet.
Carried: the maps, P99a and P99b and the recount stay in the folder; the packet goes to the teacher. Tool: None. By hand: the reading, the two items, the revision, the exit. Evidence: the six RL.3.9 claims, the RL.3.1 question, the revised recount, E99. Hand-backs in: the 97 recount with its note; E98. Out: the packet to the teacher for written feedback on the evening of day 99; E99 to the teacher. At home (30 to 40 minutes): read for 20 minutes from the log book; no writing needed. No score.

## Day 100


- **10 min. Long words.** Three prompts on paper: read and spell isolated words (3 minutes), read and spell a sentence from today's chapter (3), decode one word in its paragraph and say what it means (4). Three-syllable words, split, check each chunk, then blend: re-vis-ing, cap-i-tal, i-de-a. Irregular: pretty, whole. One line of ending-rule retrieval (drop e, double the consonant, change y).
- **20 min. Independent reading and conferences.** Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each) and two children read a log line aloud at 2 minutes each (4 minutes); the heard-six column is filled. No new teaching.
- **5 min. Returned feedback.** Children read the written note on their day 99 packet and mark the one change they will make.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Respond-day repair. Whole class, 2 minutes: one wrong example from the packet (only similarities, F) beside its correction (alike and different in the same row) and why the correction fits the words. At most four teacher groups by error letter, 1.5 minutes each (6 minutes); everyone else applies the same move in pairs, adding the missing difference to one row. Close in pairs, 2 minutes.
- **25 min. Reading and reread.** Teacher reads the printed complete book-review model aloud (about 150 words, 3 minutes; the review model is a model, not a core text, and is still to make). Pairs label opinion, reasons, link words and closing on it with four colors (8 minutes). Teacher models a revision on a printed weak review whose reason repeats its opinion (I) and whose last line is unrelated (K): one note, one change, the original kept beside it (8 minutes). Children copy the series title and the two book titles for their heading from the printed title model (practice only; the title rule is taught on day 102) (3 minutes). Share: three children read a label aloud (3 minutes).
- **10 min. Writing.** O01, the brief recommendation after the model (one sitting): a heading, an opinion, two reasons from changes in the books with a link word, and a closing line. Compare a reason that says why with one that repeats the opinion (I).
- **5 min. Talk.** Pairs, 2 minutes each child: read your recommendation; your partner names the strongest reason and says where it is from.
- **5 min. Exit.** E100.

- **Shared shape.** The printed review model and the weak review share their shape with s03-X100 (W.3.1d): a closing line that tells who might like the books. The exit review (the Harbor Street books) is not the model.
Carried: the model with its four colors, the packet with its note and O01 stay in the folder. Tool: None. By hand: the revision, the heading, O01, the exit. Evidence: the revised comparison row, O01 (one sitting, counted once), E100. Hand-backs in: the packet from day 99 and E99. Out: E100 and O01 to the teacher. At home (35 minutes): read for 20 minutes from the log book, then tell someone at home one reason you would give for a series you like, and a closing line. No score.

## 7. Talk and writing

Planned questions: day 98, which book would you recommend first, and why? Day 100, which reason is strongest, and where is it from? Written task: O01, a brief recommendation after the model. Look-fors: an opinion that names the series; two reasons from changes in the books, neither repeating the opinion; a link word; a closing that fits the reasons; in the discussion, a linked question and an answer that explains.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 98: opinion with because (grade 2); the discussion moves chart and heard-six column (unit 1). Day 99: alike and different charts and both maps (section 3.4). Day 100: one note, one change (unit 2 narrative feedback) and the printed title model. Every day: ending rules and irregular spellings.

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: preview recommend, reason, opinion with a gesture; offer the frames "I recommend ___ because ___" and "Try it if you like ___." Access: audio beside print; large print for P99a and P99b; oral rehearsal before the discussion. Check accommodations: read the items aloud on 99 (not the passages), extra time inside the 90 minutes, answers by mouth to the teacher. Reading load: P99a and P99b are 150 to 250 words each. Extension: write a recommendation of the book that would suit a different reader.

## 10. Two rooms

One child at home: the parent is the discussion partner, asks one linked question and writes one note on O01 before the child changes one thing. One projector: show the printed model and the discussion chart; the check items and exits stay on paper.
