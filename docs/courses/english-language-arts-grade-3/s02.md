## U8. Practice that returns

Daily skills begin with ten minutes of long-word reading and spelling: week 8 retrieves -ness, -ly and -able; weeks 9 to 10 apply -tion and -sion; 11 to 12 teach -ment and -ous; 13 to 14 mix suffixes and ending rules; 15 to 16 transfer them. Read bases, blend, then check meaning. Partner rereading twice weekly uses accuracy, understanding, natural pace and expression, with no rate target. Independent reading has a log and conference. Keep the whole frog book available for a sustained reread.

## U9. Supports and extension

Preview habitat and transparent with diagrams. Use "The words ___ explain ___." Rehearse aloud before writing. Offer large print and factual diagram descriptions, keeping grade-level texts. Independent reading loads are a poem or 150 to 250 words; longer tales are first heard, then reread in chunks. Extend by defending another supported connection.

## U10. Two rooms

At home the adult takes the partner role and gives the same written feedback. Record and type locally; paper alone leaves digital standards unfinished. On 77 apply one repair and assemble the book together. One screen displays shared passages; reserved questions stay on paper.
