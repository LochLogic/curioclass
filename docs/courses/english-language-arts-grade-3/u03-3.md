## 3. Checks, written first


**Day 92 items (30 minutes, silent, reserved packet P92 and sheet C12).** Marking scale: 0, 1 or 2 on the unit keys, scored once after feedback on day 93; the revision is not rescored. This section owns RL.3.3; RL.3.1 and RL.3.6 are keyed with section 3.2's codes.
- **RL.3.1 (8 minutes).** Write one question about why P92 ends successfully, then answer it with two chapter references. Key: Why do they reach the corner? Checking in chapter 11 corrects the arrow problem in chapter 10. Wrong: Magic brings them there (A).
- **RL.3.3 (8 minutes).** Write one sentence that tells how Ada's response changes and what it causes. Key: Ada asks everyone to check a new plan, so they follow it together and reach the reading corner. Wrong: Ada is good (C).
- **RL.3.6 (7 minutes).** Write the narrator's judgment, Ada's judgment and your own judgment of checking. Key: the narrator calls checking tiresome, Ada calls it useful, and my judgment is that checking helped, because the new plan led them to the reading corner. Wrong: Useful is the narrator's judgment (E).
- **RL.3.10 (7 minutes), on C12.** Read C12 alone, find one ending detail P92 lacks and say what it adds. Key: Ada keeps both drawings hanging beside the doorway, so readers can compare what changed. Wrong: a detail the chapter does not give (A). Source to check: the scope and sequence assessment table's concrete demand for RL.3.10.
- The day 99 and day 114 rows belong to sections 3.5 and 3.9.

**Exit questions (E91 to E93).** One ask each, on a new case (the unit plan's reserved exit panels are not used). Printed "invented for practice", sentences of at most 15 words. Unscored.
- **E91.** Book 1, chapter 11, from "But Ada did not mind" to "moved the yellow beanbag" (after the reread page). Ask: What did the friends' walks change in the plan? ## 4. Expected errors and first ideas


Letters this section meets; if many made it, 5 to 10 minutes of repair.
- **B, reversed order.** Wrong: she moved the path, so the ball rolled in. Right: the ball rolled in, so she moved the path. Shown 91 and 93. Repair: number the arc boxes; 5 minutes.
- **A, invented evidence.** Wrong: Magic brings them there. Right: checking in chapter 11 fixes the arrow in chapter 10. Shown 92. Repair: finger on the page for each claim; 5 minutes.
- **C, trait without action.** Wrong: Ada is good. Right: Ada asks everyone to check the new plan, so they follow it together. Shown 91 and 93. Repair: trait plus action plus effect on three cards; 7 minutes.
- **E, misattributed view.** Wrong: Useful is the narrator's word. Right: useful is Ada's; tiresome is the narrator's. Shown 93. Repair: three-column view table on P92's chapter; 8 minutes.
- **F, similarities only.** Wrong: both are beaches. Right: both are beaches, but one is near the waves and one near the dunes. Shown 93. Repair: alike and different chart, one pair each; 7 minutes.
- **L, recopy as revision.** Wrong: the same answer in neater writing. Right: one change made from the note. Shown 93. Repair: model one change in the margin; 5 minutes.
- **AI, wrong syllable division.** Wrong: sim-ilar, re-curr-ing. Right: sim-i-lar, re-cur-ring. Shown 91. Repair: split aloud, check each chunk, then blend; 5 minutes.

## 6. Days

## Day 91


- **10 min. Long words.** Repair first (4 minutes): regroup by the error shown in the day 90 word check and reteach the one missed pattern before anything new. Then read and spell isolated words (2 minutes), a sentence from chapter 11 (2), and decode one word in its paragraph (2). Week 19 begins: three-syllable words with familiar affixes, split and check each chunk, then check the meaning after decoding: re-cur-ring (re- and a doubled consonant before -ing), sim-i-lar, dis-a-gree. Irregular: again, learn. One line of ending-rule retrieval.
- **20 min. Independent reading and conferences.** Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line. Hear 12 children read the opening of chapter 12 (its first three sentences) at 45 seconds each (9 minutes), two 4-minute conferences (8 minutes, one record each) and two children read a log line aloud at 1.5 minutes each (3 minutes); the heard-six column is filled. No new teaching.
- **5 min. Opening repair and retrieve.** Repair from E90 (2 minutes). Retrieve the plan card from unit 2 (situation, events in order, ending): pairs add one box, the problem, and say what the card is missing (3 minutes). The arc is that card with a problem and a turn added; nothing else is new.
- **20 min. Reading and reread.** Teacher reads chapter 11 aloud (about 650 words, 6 minutes, and 2 minutes for the stops): Ada and Ben walk the route, Ada turns the arrow, everyone walks it (read aloud whole, so the class hears the arrow turn). Pairs reread the page where Ben suggests testing the route and they set out (8 minutes; the teacher keeps the reread off the page reserved passage P99a copies, source to check against the unit plan). Share: three pairs say what the test found (4 minutes).
- **20 min. Writing.** Teacher models placing one chapter on the arc (5 minutes): situation, problem, events, turn, ending, with chapters 1 to 4 already marked. Children place chapters 5 to 11 on their own arcs (10 minutes). Then a before-and-after link, two sentences (5 minutes): what chapter 11's action changed on the arc.
- **5 min. Word in context.** Word: route. Children say what it means in chapter 11 and draw a one-line route on the map with two stops.
- **5 min. Talk.** Pairs, 2 minutes each child: which chapter is the turn, and what makes it the turn?
- **5 min. Exit.** E91, alone, on paper.

- **Shared shape.** The before-and-after link (what chapter 11 changed on the arc) shares its shape with s03-X91v2 (RL.3.5): a test result and the change it causes. The exit reads the later part of chapter 11, after the reread page.
Carried: the arc and the chapter maps stay in the folder; the log stays with the child. Tool: None. By hand: the arc, the link sentences, the exit. Evidence: the arc with chapter 11 placed, the before-and-after link, E91. Hand-backs in: E90; the day 90 word-check pattern repair is taught in this day's skills block. Out: E91 to day 92's silent opening. At home (40 minutes): read for 20 minutes from the log book, then tell someone at home the arc of the book so far, in five short sentences. No score.

## Day 93


- **10 min. Long words.** Three prompts on paper: read and spell isolated words (3 minutes), read and spell a sentence from today's chapter (3), decode one word in its paragraph and say what it means (4). Three-syllable words, split, check each chunk, then blend: rec-om-mend, un-der-line, hap-pi-ly. Irregular: build, believe. One line of ending-rule retrieval (drop e, double the consonant, change y). - **20 min. Independent reading and conferences.** Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each) and two children read a log line aloud at 2 minutes each (4 minutes); the heard-six column is filled. No new teaching.
- **5 min. Returned feedback.** Children read the written note on their day 92 packet and mark the one change they will make.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Respond-day repair. Whole class, 2 minutes: one wrong example from the packet beside its correction (Ada is good, beside Ada asked everyone to check the new plan, so they followed it together) and why the correction fits the words. Error B and error C each get a fresh card (3 and 2 minutes). Then two groups of 2 minutes each by error letter on the packets (4 minutes); everyone else applies the same move in pairs. One pair says the move they used (1 minute). E91's repair (reversed order) is in the first group.

- **5 min. Exit.** E93.

- **Shared shape.** The two-column opening chart and the alike-and-different retrieval share their shape with s03-X93v2 (RL.3.9): a likeness and a difference in one sentence. The exit reads book passages the chart does not use.

## 7. Talk and writing

Planned questions: day 91, which chapter is the turn, and what makes it the turn? Day 93, in one way the openings are alike and in one way they differ? Written task: the arc with a before-and-after link, and a revised packet answer. Look-fors: the problem and the turn named; an action tied to what it changes; alike and different both given; a revision that changes something.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 91: the plan card (unit 2) and chapter links (section 3.1). Day 92: narrator, character and point of view (grades 1 and 2; day 84). Day 93: compare texts (grade 2) and comparison charts. Every day: ending rules and irregular spellings.

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: preview arc, turn and opening with a drawn line and a gesture; offer the frame "The turn is chapter ___ because ___." Access: audio beside print; large print for the arc. Check accommodations: read the packet items aloud on 92 (not the chapter), extra time inside the 90 minutes. Reading load: chapter 11 is the shortest; chapter 12 is read unaided with time to reread. Extension: place a favorite book on an arc and say its turn.

## 10. Two rooms

One child at home: the parent hears the arc told in five sentences and asks "What changed?" One projector: show the arc and the opening chart; the packet and exits stay on paper.
