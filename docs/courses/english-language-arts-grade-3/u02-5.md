## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **RI.3.1, RI.3.5, RI.3.7 (day 54):** "Ask and answer questions to demonstrate understanding of a text, referring explicitly to the text as the basis for the answers"; use the contents, headings, index and the life-cycle diagram to find the answer; read the picture and the words together. RI.3.1 also on day 55.

- **RI.3.2, RI.3.4, L.3.6 (day 55):** the main idea of the amphibian section with two supporting details; the meaning of amphibian, moist and frogspawn from the book's own sentences; time and place words.
- **W.3.2a, W.3.2b, W.3.2c, W.3.2d, L.3.1h, L.3.1i, L.3.1d, L.3.1e, L.3.1g (day 56):** a topic and related facts, a definition, linking words, a concluding statement, and sentence forms and verb forms applied in a tadpole explanation (Product I05, one sitting).
- **RF.3.4a, RF.3.4b, RF.3.4c (day 54), RF.3.3b, RF.3.3d, L.3.2e, L.3.2f (days 54 to 56):** partner rereading on day 54; week 11 teaches -ous (day 54) and applies -ment and -ous to longer bases (days 55, 56). Irregular words are mapped from the book. W.3.10: routine writing, days 54 to 56.
- **Limits that bite here.** The life cycle is taught from the book's diagram and words. Science unit 3 runs at the same time and is not assumed complete. - **Connections.** The book is read from its opening section (day 53) and carries to the research notes on day 57. Days 55 and 56 hand to 2.6: the day 56 explanation is collected, with feedback on day 57. The story map, moves chart and dated folder carry on.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to teach contents and index lookups with the page numbers kept in print; how to read a labelled diagram arrow by arrow with its words; how a main idea differs from a topic.

## 6. Days

## Day 54


- **10 min. Long words.** Three minutes repair E53's commonest letter. Week 11: teach -ous (full of, having) on famous, nervous and dangerous; read and spell on slates; map double from the book (7 minutes).
- **20 min. Rereading and grammar.** Five minutes grammar retrieval: comparisons and verb forms on slates. Fifteen minutes partner rereading of Under the Leaf with the feedback card, no speed; the teacher listens to three pairs for 3 minutes each, and the rest is feedback between partners.
- **15 min. Reading and reread.** The life-cycle bridge (8 minutes): in two minutes order egg, tadpole and frog on slates; the teacher reads From Egg to Frog aloud with the diagram, tracing each arrow and naming first, then, next (4 minutes); in two minutes find the words that say why a tadpole needs water. Hard-part reread, in pairs: where does a label explain an arrow? Pairs find the arrow, its label and the sentence (5 minutes); the class names them (2 minutes).
- **10 min. Word and sentence work.** Using the heading to find the life cycle (2 minutes). The teacher models a question, the contents or index entry, the page and the diagram, for gills (5 minutes); children try a question about metamorphosis the same way (3 minutes).
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs, 2 minutes a child: ask a question the book can answer; the partner finds the page in under a minute and shows the words (4 minutes). Three volunteers, 2 minutes each (6 minutes).
- **20 min. Writing.** Three questions about frogs from the contents and index, each answered in a sentence with a page number and the words: where gills are described, what comes after the tadpole, and why the diagram shows no numbers (20 minutes).
- **5 min. Exit.** E54.

Carried: the book copy and the answer sheet stay with the child; the teacher keeps the question card. Tool: None. By hand: slate order, lookup, three answers, exit. Evidence: E54. Hand-backs in: E53. Out: E54 to day 55's opening. At home (30 to 40 minutes): use the contents and index to find a page about gills and read it aloud to an adult. No score.

- **Data note.** The plan's E54 asked one question with a how-did-you-choose follow-up; the data splits it into X54 (the page) and X54b (the part of the book). Grade 2 text features are not yet in the ledger; treat them as planned only, not yet written.

## Day 55


- **10 min. Long words.** Three minutes repair E54's commonest letter. Week 11: read and spell -ment and -ous on longer bases, movement, enjoyment, famous and nervous; map the irregular word through (7 minutes).

- **15 min. Reading and reread.** Three minutes: name a stage and the one after it on slates. The teacher reads What Is an Amphibian? - **20 min. Writing.** Each child writes a short paragraph: the main idea of the amphibian section, two supporting details and one sentence saying how two sentences connect (20 minutes).
- **Shared shape.** The model, worked example and practice here share the shape of E55 (say what the word This points back to in the second sentence) and of its check item; the exit and the check item use new cases, so none of them is shown or practised on the same case.

Carried: the paragraph stays in the folder; the book copy stays with the child. Tool: None. By hand: slate words, meaning of moist, the paragraph, exit. Evidence: E55. Hand-backs in: E54. Out: E55 to day 56's opening. At home (30 to 40 minutes): reread pages 3 to 4 to an adult and say what the word This points to in one place. No score.

- **Day 54:** grade 2 order, reasons and text features; comparisons and verb forms. **Day 55:** stages; -ment and -ous. **Day 56:** time links; sentence forms; the day 52 tale note. The week 11 words return daily.
- **Fluency target.** Natural phrasing and expression on successive readings, no speed target.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview amphibian, moist, tadpole, froglet, gills and arrow with the diagram; frames "The word ___ points back to ___" and "First ___. Then ___."; say the answer before writing; a partner who shares a language.
- **Access.** Large print; a factual description of the diagram and each label, not of the answer; a scribe for the explanation, not for spelling evidence.
- **Check accommodations.** Exits may be read aloud except where decoding is the evidence; record help.
- **Reading load.** Alone: one page or 150 to 250 words. The amphibian section is read aloud first, then reread in chunks.
- **Extension, same standard.** Explain a second link in the same section and say how it differs from the first.

## 10. Two rooms

- **One projector.** Show the diagram and the contents; keep the exits and Panel A on paper.
