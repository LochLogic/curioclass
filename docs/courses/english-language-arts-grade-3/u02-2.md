## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview dry, hollow, log, warning and taunt with pictures; frames "First ___. Then ___." and "I said ___."; say the draft aloud before writing; a partner who shares a language.
- **Access.** Large print; a factual description of any picture; a scribe for ideas on drafts and for spelling not being checked; for the oral sample on day 45, a shorter list is not allowed because decoding is the evidence, but time may be extended.
- **Check accommodations.** Day 46 items may be read aloud except where decoding is the evidence; record every help. Extra time on days 45 and 46, recorded.
- **Reading load.** Alone: a poem or 150 to 250 words. Latona is about 360 words; a child who cannot read it alone has it read aloud and the support is recorded.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second spoken exchange with a feeling to the middle, or write the same event from the friend's view.
