# Section 1.8: End-of-unit checks and response: recordings, group talk, the revised contract, a transfer packet, and regrouping


## Header



## 1. Standards and practices


- **RF.3.4a to c and SL.3.5 (day 35, practiced 39):** retained recordings of a chosen story passage with a stated purpose, a reread and a display, scored from the saved file; day 39 partner rereading with the feedback card. No speed target.
- **SL.3.1a to d, SL.3.2, SL.3.3 (day 36, practiced 39):** "Come to discussions prepared"; "Follow agreed-upon rules"; "Ask questions to check understanding"; "Explain their own ideas ... in light of the discussion"; main ideas "presented in diverse media"; "Ask and answer questions about information from a speaker". Observed in six groups on day 36; day 39 is practice with a fresh strip.
- **W.3.2a to d, W.3.4, W.3.5, W.3.10 (day 37, practiced 39), L.3.2d to g (day 37):** the contract is revised after written feedback; conventions are scored from a fresh dictation and a dictionary lookup.
- **RL.3.1, RL.3.2, RL.3.3, RL.3.7, RL.3.4, RL.3.10, RI.3.1, RI.3.2, RI.3.7, RI.3.10, L.3.4a to d, L.3.5a (day 38):** a fresh tale and a fresh panel; taught in sections 1.1 to 1.6 and only sampled here. RF.3.3a, RF.3.3d (day 39): word retrieval, not rescored.
- **Limits that bite here.** Nothing is taught as new on days 35 to 38 and nothing is reteached; a miss routes to day 39. Items are answered unaided; read-aloud help is recorded and does not establish reading. A scribed answer cannot establish spelling or recording. Recordings stay on the room's device; none is shared outside the room.
- **Connections.** Every row run here is owned by an earlier section (1.1 to 1.7) and sampled from its key in s01.md. Day 39's repair routine is the one of days 25 and 29. The next unit (day 40) groups children by the day 39 regroup list.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to run the device for six three-minute recordings and score them after class; how to observe a group of five in seven minutes; how to give a feedback note that names a meaning change; how to mark 0, 1 or 2 by component; how to record support honestly; and how day 39 regroups children by the earliest unmet prerequisite.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned: none.** Every row run here is owned by an earlier section and keyed in s01.md: day 35 RF.3.4a, RF.3.4b, RF.3.4c and SL.3.5 (section 1.7); day 36 SL.3.1a, SL.3.1d and SL.3.2 (section 1.4) and SL.3.1b, SL.3.1c and SL.3.3 (section 1.5); day 37 W.3.2a to d, W.3.4, W.3.10, W.3.5, L.3.2e and L.3.2f (section 1.7) and L.3.2d and L.3.2g (section 1.5); day 38 the literary rows RL.3.1 to RL.3.3 and RL.3.7 (section 1.3), the informational rows RI.3.1, RI.3.2, RL.3.4, L.3.5a and L.3.4a to d (section 1.6), RI.3.7 (section 1.4) and RL.3.10 and RI.3.10 (section 1.1). This section fits them to the minutes below, scores each component 0, 1 or 2, and records help given.

**Exit questions (E35 to E39).** One ask each, unscored, on a new case; a miss is reteached on day 39. One scale for records: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
- **E35 (day 35).** The child's retained recording and display and the listener request, "Help me hear where one sentence ends and understand one pictured detail." Ask: "On paper, say how your recording meets this request." Answer: names a pause at a sentence end and the pictured detail the display shows. Wrong: says only "I read well" (W); says the display is "pretty" or unrelated to the passage (Y).
- **E36 (day 36; inside the nine-minute bookmark plan).** The child's contract and its written feedback. Ask: "Which feedback note will you use for your bookmark plan, and what will you change because of it?" Answer: names a real note and a change in meaning, such as moving a fact under its reason. Wrong: recopies unchanged (M); related facts left apart (H).
- **E37 (day 37; replaces five minutes of silent review).** Reader request, invented for practice: "Explain getting ready so I can try it tomorrow." Ask: "Revise one explanation in your contract so this reader could try it." Answer: a usable explanation with a step and its reason. Wrong: private reminders (K); unchanged copy (M).
- **E38 (day 38).** The first two tale items (RL.3.1, RL.3.2) are the exit; no extra time.
- **E39 (day 39; takes five discussion minutes).** Strip, invented for practice: "Eli read the page number as seven. His partner asked, 'Did you check the number?' Eli looked again. The page said eleven." Ask: "What should Eli do the next time he reads a number, and why does it help?" Answer: look again and check the number against the page, because it showed the mistake. Wrong: "read faster" (X); "guess" (P).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Children hold their retained recording, display, contract, feedback and process folder from day 35 to day 39. The shared device is the teacher's on day 35 and stays with her; recordings are local. The day 39 regroup list is the teacher's and goes to the next unit.
- **Key moment 1, day 35: an unaided recording in a three-minute window.** Ideal instrument: a recorder with playback. Spec: the recording device (light) fits; one shared device, local files. A room with nothing: read aloud to the teacher, who writes a note with the same four parts.
- **Key moment 2, day 36: a presentation with words, a picture and a labeled graph.** Ideal instrument: a printed graph beside the spoken script. Spec: fits; print.
- **A room with nothing.** Paper, pencils, strips, a dictionary and a printed bookmark strip.

## 6. Days

## Day 35


- **30 min. Skills, silent.** Ten minutes: retrieval on slates (affix words, no teaching). Twenty minutes: independent reading from the logs.
- **18 min. Final recordings.** The teacher is at the shared device in a quiet corner. Six children not yet recorded take 3 minutes each, unaided, in turn: state a purpose, read the chosen passage, reread one sentence more expressively, and show the picture and say how it clarifies one detail (30 seconds, 90 seconds, 30 seconds, 30 seconds). After a misread the teacher says only "Check that word". Everyone else spends 5 minutes on silent passage preparation of the day 36 discussion page and 13 minutes on independent reading.
- **30 min. Group draft exchanges.** Six groups of five, in turn, 5 minutes each, with the teacher observing; the others read the discussion page and prepare notes silently. Each group holds a first prepared exchange on the page "Lia and her brother and the river story", written feedback after class.
- **2 min. Independent work.** Label the display card.
- **5 min. Exit.** E35.
- **5 min. Collection.** Display cards and notes are collected.

Carried: children hold their retained recording and display, which are saved locally. Tool: the recording device (light), 18 minutes, teacher run. By hand: exit, notes, display card. Evidence: the four day 35 rows from the 30 retained recordings (scored after class, about 3 minutes each, 90 minutes in all); E35. Hand-backs in: the day 34 notes. Out: the recording scores and feedback on the exchanges to day 36's opening; the contract feedback to day 36. At home (30 to 40 minutes): read the discussion page aloud to an adult and mark one detail; then read together.

## Day 36


- **10 min. Opening.** Five minutes: one contract peer look-for (partners swap contracts and mark the strategy and the grouping). Five minutes: the teacher's written feedback is returned and read silently.
- **20 min. Skills, silent.** Independent reading from the logs, with silent retrieval cards.
- **9 min. Presentation and response.** The teacher presents the classroom library script twice with the picture and the labeled bar graph (about 3 minutes in all), then everyone prepares silently for 3 minutes and writes the main idea, a spoken detail, a picture detail and a number (3 minutes). SL.3.2.
- **9 min. Bookmark plan.** Each child plans the bookmark from the contract: strategy, one-line meaning, two details in short phrases and a closing line; E36 is answered inside these minutes.
- **42 min. Group windows.** Six groups of five, 7 minutes each, observed in turn: revised discussion of the Lia page, then questions to a speaker. In each group everyone speaks at least twice and asks one question. The other groups work independently on the bookmark plan and reading.

Carried: children hold the contract with feedback and their discussion draft. Tool: None. By hand: look-for, response, plan, exit. Evidence: the six day 36 rows; E36; observations recorded once for each child. Hand-backs in: E34 and the day 35 scores (opening). Out: the bookmark plan to day 37 and day 38; the revised contract plan to day 37. At home (30 to 40 minutes): tell an adult the change you will make to your contract; then read together.

## Day 37


- **30 min. Skills, silent.** Ten minutes: retrieval on slates. Twenty minutes: independent reading from the logs.
- **30 min. Contract revision.** Each child revises the contract after the written feedback. Five of these minutes are a silent draft audit.
- **5 min. Process annotation.** Each child marks the earlier plan, the peer suggestion, the teacher's note and the resulting revision (W.3.5).
- **15 min. Conventions.** A fresh dictation of two sentences, read three times (12 minutes): "The driver's bus was stopping. The girls' books were ready, and she was hoping to help." Then a dictionary lookup of the word and its correction (3 minutes), as keyed in s01.md.
- **5 min. Exit.** E37, in place of the silent review.
- **5 min. Collection.** Contracts and dictations are collected.

Carried: children hold their contract, feedback and process folder. Tool: None. By hand: revision, annotation, dictation, lookup, exit. Evidence: the thirteen day 37 rows (marked after class, about 4 minutes a child); E37. Hand-backs in: E36 and the contract feedback. Out: the scores to day 39; the revised contract to day 38 for the bookmark. At home (30 to 40 minutes): read your revised contract aloud to an adult; then read together.

## Day 38


- **30 min. Skills, silent.** Ten minutes: retrieval on slates. Twenty minutes: independent reading.
- **10 min. Tale reading.** Alone, silently: The Foolish, Timid Rabbit (595 words, about 10 minutes; read aloud for anyone who needs it, recorded as support).
- **8 min. First tale items (E38).** RL.3.1 (3 minutes) and RL.3.2 (5 minutes), answered on the assessment sheet.
- **9 min. Remaining literary items.** RL.3.3 (3), RL.3.7 (2), RL.3.4 (2), L.3.5a (2).
- **5 min. Panel reading.** Alone, silently: the library notice panel, with its diagram and glossary.
- **9 min. Informational items.** RI.3.1 (3), RI.3.2 (4), RI.3.7 (2).
- **8 min. Vocabulary items.** L.3.4a, b, c, d at 2 minutes each.
- **8 min. Bookmark publication.** Each child writes the bookmark from the plan and the revised contract (no more than 40 words), and signs and dates it.
- **3 min. Collection.** Sheets and bookmarks are collected.

Carried: children hold their bookmark plan. Tool: None. By hand: the items and the bookmark. Evidence: the fifteen day 38 rows (RL.3.10 and RI.3.10 add no time); E38. Hand-backs in: none (E37 goes to day 39). Out: the sheets and bookmarks to day 39. At home (30 to 40 minutes): read for 20 minutes and tell an adult what the bookmark says.

## Day 39


- **10 min. Return and share.** Six minutes: all end evidence is returned (recording note, group note, contract scores, item sheet), and each child marks one letter. Four minutes: partners show their published bookmark and say what changed (2 minutes each).
- **30 min. Skills.** Eight minutes: affix and irregular-word retrieval. Five minutes: grammar retrieval. Seven minutes: partner rereading of a 90-word part of g3-u1-05 twice each with the feedback card; the teacher listens to two pairs. Ten minutes: independent reading with two conferences of 5 minutes.
- **20 min. Apply repairs.** Four groups by letter: evidence (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, V, W, Z, AC, AD), writing (H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, AB), decoding (P, X) and discussion (Q, R, S, T, U, Y). The teacher leads decoding for 15 minutes; the other groups work in pairs from written repair cards; each child repairs one missed response. Five minutes: a fresh example to check the repair.
- **20 min. Discuss revisions.** Three minutes: each child marks one detail in g3-u1-05. Twelve minutes: six groups of five talk at once on how the carriers change who can read; the teacher spends 2 minutes with each group and records that group's heard-six child. Five minutes: E39.
- **10 min. Regroup.** The teacher assigns each child to a group for the next unit by the earliest unmet prerequisite from the component scores (4 minutes). Six children with an AA record take an individual unaided sample, 1 minute each (6 minutes); the rest read. Accurate components receive independent reading.

Carried: children hold their feedback and process folders; the regroup list is the teacher's. Tool: None. By hand: repair sentence, discussion notes, exit. Evidence: E39; the repaired response; the regroup list. Hand-backs in: all end evidence, E37 (Apply repairs) and the day 38 items (Return and share). Out: the regroup list and the folders to the next unit. At home (30 to 40 minutes): tell an adult one thing the unit taught you about getting past what stops us reading; then read together.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 36: "How do Lia's choices help her brother join in?" Day 39: "How do the carriers change who can read?" and "What changed your plan, and how do you know?"
- **Written task, days 36 to 38.** The bookmark plan and the published bookmark, from the revised contract; it publishes the contract concisely and is not another essay.
- **Look-fors, six.** The strategy is named; the meaning is explained; two accurate story details; a link or short phrase joining them; a closing line; the process folder shows a real change. Scored by component 0, 1 or 2 on the s01.md keys.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 35:** the recording routine. **Day 36:** discussion moves; bar graph labels. **Day 37:** the possessive, suffix spelling and the dictionary. **Day 38:** word strategies, charts and maps. **Day 39:** the repair routine of days 25 and 29; partner rereading; the word sequence. Only retrieval, never reteaching, on 35 to 38.
- **Fluency target.** Natural phrasing and expression on rereading; no speed target.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview presentation, graph, bar, panel and bookmark with a picture; frames "I changed ___ because ___." and "The words ___ show ___."; an oral first answer; items read aloud where reading is not the evidence.
- **Access.** Large print, spacing, familiar directions read aloud and dictation support; the graph and diagram described in words, never the answer. A scribed answer cannot establish spelling or audio creation.
- **Check accommodations.** Items may be read aloud except unaided reading and decoding rows; record every support. A child may give an item orally and the teacher writes it. A child who cannot be recorded reads aloud to the teacher.
- **Reading load.** Alone: the 595-word tale, the panel and the items. Read aloud: on request, with support recorded.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write a second bookmark line that gives a different story detail and justify it.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** The adult is the partner, listener and group. Record the child on a phone or by writing a note; use the printed items; do the 15-minute dictation aloud. **Last day of the unit (day 39):** celebrate with a small show of the bookmark and one thing the child changed; choose one repair for the next week.
- **One projector.** Show the script, picture and graph on day 36; keep the item sheets on paper.
