## 4. Readiness check, whole

Start-of-year check: none needed; retrieval only.

The daily ten-minute word sequence starts with weeks 1 and 2 retrieval of the six syllable types and vowel teams. Weeks 3 and 4 apply un-, re- and dis- to known bases. Weeks 5 and 6 teach pre-, mis-, -ful and -less. Weeks 7 and 8 teach -ness, -ly and -able with spelling changes, possessives and dictionary checks. Each cycle moves from explicit modeling to words, sentences and a short aligned passage. Decode before using context to confirm. Tuesday and Thursday partner rereading supplies accuracy, natural rate and expression feedback, not a speed contest. Map irregular words from the current text each week.

More than a third missing a retrieval means the next teaching day's warm-up and first five minutes repair it; otherwise use the existing conference group. For diagnosed foundational gaps, Monday, Wednesday and Friday conferences use the matching grade 2 cycle, aligned words, sentences and passage. Retest within days 15 and 30's existing word slots. Teacher records the pattern, independence and next move. Keep grade 3 text access with oral support during teaching, and gather unaided evidence separately. Day 3's oral sample finishes in conferences through day 10 without hearing a child twice for the same evidence.

## 5. End-of-unit assessment, whole

Question accounting: count every question, task and exit ticket, including isolated word exercises. At least 80 percent must require the named text. U3 holds the daily exits; do not count rubric rows for one product as separate assignments. Exit tickets ask one question with at most two parts, on an unworked passage of the day's text, covering every part of its goal.

Discussion preparation and observation: teach the routine on day 1, practice daily in pairs, and hold formal discussions on days 9, 17, 25 and 39, with assessed group talk on 36. Children mark a passage detail, rehearse a claim, listen, ask for detail, link a comment and explain what became clearer. The teacher models a pair exchange, records each child's evidence, gives one next step and fades sentence frames. During teaching, use "The words ___ show ___" and allow oral rehearsal with the original text; independent checks remove supplied evidence. On day 17 model a media response using words, an image and labeled quantities before day 25 practice.

Each numbered exit uses five minutes within existing work or review, not extra time. Withhold its question until the exit. Wrong answers route only to the described letter. A child's own product may be inspected with a newly revealed question. On check days these exits replace five minutes of silent review or independent preparation. Exits are unscored formative evidence.
