## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Preview habitat, stream, tree and conditions with pictures; frames "The words ___ explain ___" and "This section is mostly about ___ and ___"; say the gist aloud before writing; a partner who shares a language.
- **Access.** Large print; a factual description of any figure; a scribe for the tale, with spelling and punctuation left as the child writes them where those are the evidence; extra time on day 52, recorded.
- **Check accommodations.** The tale may be dictated except where conventions are the evidence; record any help. Models may be read aloud.
- **Reading load.** Alone: a poem or 150 to 250 words. The opening section is about 450 words, read aloud first, then reread in two chunks.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write the gist of the section in two ways, a short one and a longer one, and say which keeps more of the text.
