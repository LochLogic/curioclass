Letters this section meets; if many made it, 5 to 10 minutes of repair.
- **C, trait without action.** Wrong: Pia is clever. Right: Pia pulled over a stool, so she could water the seeds. Shown 87. Repair: trait plus action plus effect on three cards; 7 minutes.
- **A, invented evidence.** Wrong: she grew the seeds in the stool. Right: the stool let her reach. Shown 87 and 90. Repair: finger on the page for each claim; 5 minutes.
- **Y, subject-verb disagreement.** Wrong: The hikers rests. Right: The hikers rest. Shown 88. Repair: say the subject, say the verb, count one or many; 7 minutes.
- **Z, pronoun disagreement.** Wrong: The hikers rest. It drink water. Right: They drink water. Shown 88. Repair: draw an arrow from the pronoun back to its noun; 7 minutes.
- **E, misattributed view.** Wrong: Ivy says Jonah fusses. Right: the narrator does. Shown 89. Repair: three-column view table on one passage; 8 minutes.
- **O, unrelated question; P, no learning explained.** Wrong: What is your favourite colour? or no slip. Right: Which float found the leak? and I now see why Ivy disagrees. Shown 89. Repair: reread the linked-question frame; 5 minutes.
- **D, literal for nonliteral.** Wrong: heads or tails means a coin. Right: she cannot understand the drawing. Shown 90. Repair: context and figurative chart; 7 minutes.
- **AD, decoded word that conflicts with meaning.** Wrong: reads cupboard as cup-bored. Right: cup-board, two known words. Shown 90 with the word check. Repair: reread the sentence and ask if it makes sense; 5 minutes.
