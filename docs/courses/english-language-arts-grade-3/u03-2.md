## 4. Expected errors and first ideas


Letters this section meets; if many made it, 5 to 10 minutes of repair.
- **C, trait without action.** Wrong: Ada is brave (E87). Right: the last arrow still needs fixing, so Ada plans to check it with Ben. Shown 87. Repair: trait plus action plus effect on three cards; 7 minutes.
- **A, invented evidence.** Wrong: Ada fixed the arrow that day. Right: she plans to look at it on Monday. Shown 87 and 90. Repair: finger on the page for each claim; 5 minutes.
- **Y, subject-verb disagreement.** Wrong: The hikers rests. Right: The hikers rest. Shown 88. Repair: say the subject, say the verb, count one or many; 7 minutes.
- **Z, pronoun disagreement.** Wrong: The hikers rest. It drink water. Right: They drink water. Shown 88. Repair: draw an arrow from the pronoun back to its noun; 7 minutes.
- **E, misattributed view.** Wrong: Priya thinks Ben is wasting time. Right: Ada and the narrator do. Shown 89. Repair: three-column view table on one passage; 8 minutes.
- **O, unrelated question; P, no learning explained.** Wrong: What is your favorite color? or no slip. Right: Which arrow did Ben want to see? and I now see why Ben asked. Shown 89. Repair: reread the linked-question frame; 5 minutes.
- **D, literal for nonliteral.** Wrong: the mistakes hid in the room. Right: they were in front of Ada and still hard to see. Shown 90. Repair: context and figurative chart; 7 minutes.
- **AD, decoded word that conflicts with meaning.** Wrong: reads cupboard as cup-bored. Right: cup-board, two known words. Shown 90 with the word check. Repair: reread the sentence and ask if it makes sense; 5 minutes.
- **AI, wrong syllable division.** Wrong: an-ima-ls. Right: an-i-mals. Shown 88 and 90. Repair: split aloud, check each chunk, blend; 5 minutes.

## 6. Days

## Day 87


- **10 min. Long words.** Three prompts on paper: read and spell isolated words (3 minutes), read and spell a sentence from today's chapter (3), decode one word in its paragraph and say what it means (4). Three-syllable words, split, check each chunk, then blend: con-fi-dence, sur-pris-ing, and one more three-syllable word the teacher picks from the page. Irregular: though, answer. One line of ending-rule retrieval (drop e, double the consonant, change y).
- **20 min. Rereading and grammar.** Partner-rereading day. Five minutes of grammar retrieval on slates: name the function of the subject and of the verb in two sentences from chapter 6. Then 15 minutes of partner rereading with the feedback card; the teacher hears three pairs at 3 minutes each (6 children, 9 minutes) and floats for the rest.
- **5 min. Opening repair and retrieve.** Repair from E86 and the day 86 revision (2 minutes). Retrieve cause and effect between chapters from the maps (3 minutes): pairs say how chapter 6 led to chapter 7.
- **20 min. Reading and reread.** Teacher reads chapter 7 aloud (about 880 words, 8 minutes): the table blocks the shelf and Ada sees it first. Pairs reread the page where Ada moves the table and writes the note (8 minutes). Share: three pairs name the action that changes the route (4 minutes).
- **20 min. Writing.** Children take over the chapter map alone (5 minutes) and write the link to chapter 6. Then N03, the brief chapter-world episode, replaces the main writing (15 minutes: plan card 3, draft 10, reread 2). Constraints from chapter 7: Ada's note (Ask readers what else gets in the way), the table moved, the red bear book. Task: a new reader needs a book from a low shelf; show what Ada does and why. One sitting, labeled invented.
- **5 min. Word in context.** Word: confidence (also in the word prompt). Children say what it means in the paragraph and use it in a clause about Ada.
- **5 min. Talk.** Pairs, 2 minutes each child: what in chapter 7 changed because Ada saw the table first?
- **5 min. Exit.** E87, alone, on paper.

- **Shared shape.** The chapter 6 to chapter 7 link shares its shape with s03-X87v2 (RL.3.5): an earlier problem and the action that answers it. The exit reads the end of chapter 7, not the page the link works.

Evidence: the chapter link, N03 (a narrative with an episode tied to chapter 7 facts), E87. Hand-backs in: E86; the day 86 revision.
