# Tool asks: Biology

These headings collect what the biology unit plans ask of each tool, from each plan's field 8 (UNIT-PLAN.md rule 10). No tool is built here, and none exists yet: every one is spec only. Each plan also names printed runs for one projector or one child, so no day depends on a device. The detailed asks under each heading are still to write.

## Body feedback

Requested by [unit 1](s01.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only; verdict: partly fits. Days 4 to 8. Students test internal conditions that cannot be measured in class: vary the size of a disturbance and the strength of the response, graph the internal range apart from the response, and compare counteracting loops with amplifying ones. Paper route: printed stepped tables.

## DNA-to-protein code

Requested by [unit 2](s02.md), field 8. Light tool, spec only; verdict: partly fits, because extraction needs real observations. Day 15 compares evidence to show why (five minutes); day 16 shows why through sequence and shape (ten minutes); days 17 and 19 practice with feedback, day 19 on copying (ten minutes each). Paper route: printed model runs.

## Sealed-jar gas

Requested by [unit 3](s03.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only; verdict: partly fits. It shows why on days 26 to 28, 30, 32 to 34 and 36 to 38, and gives practice with feedback on days 29 and 35. An atom-tracking view supports tracer reasoning; the sealed systems exist only on screen. Paper route: printed runs.

## Energy pyramid and carbon reservoir

Requested by [unit 4](s04.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only; verdict: partly fits. It shows why on days 48, 49, 51 and 52 and gives practice with feedback on days 50 and 54. Students predict before they change a transfer or reservoir arrow. Paper route: printed runs.

## Population model

Requested by [unit 5](s05.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only; verdict: partly fits. It shows why on days 60 and 62, gives practice with feedback on days 61 and 71, and is used unaided on the day 73 check. Each student predicts and changes an input; one projector supports discussion but cannot show each student running the model.

## Cross and meiosis

Requested by [unit 6](s06.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only. Chromosome inheritance on day 80 and recombination on day 82 show why (ten minutes each); crosses on days 85 and 86 give practice with feedback (12 minutes each); measurement displays on days 87 and 88 give practice with feedback (eight minutes each). It needs labeled chromosome segments, independent draws, repeated samples, editable frequency graphs and a printed step path. Paper route: chromosome strips.

## Natural selection

Requested by [unit 7](s07.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only; verdict: partly fits until tested. Days 99 and 100 show why (ten minutes each); days 103 to 105 give practice with feedback (12 minutes each). Asks: inherited variation, resources, differential reproduction, repeated runs, environmental contrasts and printable records.

## River and dam trade-off

Requested by [unit 9](s09.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only, days 127 to 135; verdict: partly fits until it is checked against monitoring evidence. Students compare fish passage and flow options, predict before each test, inspect delayed impacts and keep their revisions. The spec must show its assumptions and keep invented trials apart from observations.

## Fishery and species protection

Requested by [unit 10](s10.md), field 8. Key tool, spec only; verdict: partly fits until formula editing and saved individual evidence are checked. Days 142 to 145 build resource updates; days 146 to 148 revise mortality rules; days 149 to 152 test combined packages; day 153 revises saved files; day 154 checks unaided; day 155 supports repairs.
