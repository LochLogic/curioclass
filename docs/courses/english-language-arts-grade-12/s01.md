## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How do Twain, Addams and Frost turn one moment into a claim about who they became?"
Read Twain's sunset beside his statement of acquisition and loss on day 1. Ask how the same water can sustain both judgments. Supporting questions are the daily prompts below: literal change, narrative construction, then comparison. On day 14 students defend a claim about retrospective self-making using Twain, Addams and Frost. Model one turn on day 2; release comparison to students on day 7 and independent reading on day 12.

## U8. Practice that returns

The three weekly sentence studies preserve source meaning while changing emphasis. Vocabulary returns before, during and after reading, then in revision. See the counted tasks in U3.

## U9. Supports and extension
