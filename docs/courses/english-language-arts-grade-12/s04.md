## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What does the American dream cost the people who chase it in these 1920s texts, and who pays?"
Open day 54 on Nick's first three paragraphs: what does his professed restraint reveal about his judgments? Introduce the compelling question before retrieval.

Model choice, evidence and effect on day 54, stereotyping on 57 and word choice on 59. Withdraw supplied charts by 66; students test alternative readings. Explain narrator, author and character separately.
