# Scope and sequence: Social and Emotional Learning, Grades 9 to 12

Course `social-and-emotional-learning-grades-9-to-12`, grades 9, 10, 11 and 12 (one band course over four years in the advisory period), draft, written 2026-11-26 from the frame; fix pass after the expert read the same day (docs/reviews/sequence-social-and-emotional-learning-grades-9-to-12-fixes-2026-11-26.md).

## How to read this

- **Days.** 256 sessions of 40 minutes, two a week over 32 weeks, 64 a year: six units (60 sessions) and four course-level check sessions a year. The units, their order, their days and their rows are the frame's; nothing here resizes a unit. The days live in data/plans/social-and-emotional-learning-grades-9-to-12/days.csv; this page holds the judgment.
- **School days.** The class meets on the second and fourth school day of each week: for course day k of its year, school day = 160 times (grade minus 9), plus 5 times (week minus 1), plus 2 on the week's first session or 4 on its second, where week is k divided by 2, rounded up. Week numbers below are weeks of that school year.
- **Codes.** Grades 9, 10 and 11 begin every code (the `~` codes in the codes column, from each row's progression standard; the three AASL ids ride with SM3, SA8 and RS4 as the frame's Standard bullet places them). Grade 12 owns every code for the band, so only grade 12 rows carry owned codes, and only grade 12 checks claim them.
- **Kinds of day.** Each sub-skill group runs Understanding (shown on an invented case, at most three new sub-skills, named as steps on the card), Fluency (the steps rehearsed on new cases; in a two-group unit the first Fluency rehearses every sub-skill of the group and the second replays them all against a changed case, so neither holds a sub-skill's only rehearsal), Application (the student designs the plan on a new case, names its trade-off and judges how it worked). Each unit closes with its Check (an unseen written plan page and a watched pair rehearsal, against look-fors) and its Respond session (a reteach in at most four step groups, then the rehearsal again). The third and sixth unit of each year hand their Check and Respond to the year's course-level check (the frame's Course-level checks table), so their own rows hold teaching days only.
- **Openings.** Every session opens with one 5-minute item: from unit 2's coping plan onward, the class's calm start (before it, an item on the last session's step card); on each group's Understanding session, one retrieval item with a short prompt for each row the group's sub-skills assume, at its earlier depth, in place of the calm start (never two openings), so every earlier form is warmed before the new depth is modeled and assessed days keep the calm start. The retrieves column names the rows.
- **Readiness.** Every row a unit assumes is the same sub-skill one grade before (grade 9 rows assume SEL 6 to 8's grade 8 rows), or a row taught earlier the same year, so every lean opens with retrieval and no readiness check is given anywhere in the band. A class that did not take SEL 6 to 8 gets the Given: items of unit 1 (Candidate 1).
- **Evidence.** A class sheet per unit marks each step "seen" or "not yet"; it records a step, never a rating of the student, and is thrown away at the end of its unit (a check's sheet at the end of its respond session). End-of-unit evidence begins in each unit's last Application (assess = end) and finishes in its Check; in grade 12 the Applications named under each unit are owner-year evidence points too. Feelings in a check are a character's; private pages (SA7, SA9) are checked only for the steps.
- **No grading, no study claim.** Curio Class claims no grading or credit in grades 9 to 12 (credit is the school's choice, Desk q-frames-scope-1), and no study has tried this course, so it is never called evidence-based.
- **Shared cards.** The I-statement frame, coping plan, talk turn card, goal steps plan page and refusal steps are quoted word for word from SEL STANDARD section 7 in their 9 to 12 form wherever a unit plan prints them.
- **Role play.** A partner never plays the unkind, pushing or abusive part: the teacher or a scene card plays it, and no real classmate is cast. Every unit carries health's disclosure steps (docs/courses/health/APPROACH.md, "If a student tells you something").
- **Settled cases.** One court case a year for SO6 (Mendez in unit 5, Brown in unit 9, Morgan in unit 18, Heart of Atlanta in unit 22), none repeated inside the band.

## Grade 9 (year 1): course days 1 to 64

### Unit 1. A new start

Course days 1 to 10, weeks 1 to 5. *How do I plan my way into high school, and how will I know the plan works?*

- Arc: group 1 (SM5, SM3, SA7), plan the work; group 2 (SO7, RS8, SA9), read the place and find help. Each group runs Understanding, Fluency, a second Fluency on a changed case, Application.
- Order: The plan page and the support map come first because every later unit plans on that page and names its helpers on that map; the six steps follow the plan so students judge a choice against a plan they already hold.
- Readiness and retrieval: no readiness check (every row the unit assumes was used the year before or earlier the same year); retrieval openings on g8-rel-ask-offer-help, g8-sa-i-can-try, g8-sa-what-interests-me, g8-sm-plan-and-tidy, g8-sm-stay-on-task, g8-soc-read-situation, each group's rows in one item on that group's Understanding opening, 5 minutes in place of the calm start.
- Assessment: end-of-unit evidence begins on day 8 (the last Application); the end-of-unit assessment is day 9 (RS8, SM3, SM5), response time day 10. No mid-unit assessment (no unit runs over 25 days).
- Candidate (a start-of-year readiness read of the grade 8 cards inside the first openings, for a class that did not have SEL 6 to 8): kept, no day: for a class without SEL 6 to 8, a Given: item on the grade 8 goal steps card takes day 1's opening and is marked on the class sheet; on "not yet" the first ten minutes teach its one-step form.

## Shorter path

**Once a week (32 sessions a year).** The frame's short path, unchanged: every unit and every group kept, fewer minutes a unit, never fewer units. A three-group unit runs in 5 sessions (each group's Understanding and Fluency share one session, the three Applications merge into one, then one shared Check and Respond); a two-group unit runs in 6 (each group's Understanding and first Fluency share one, its second Fluency keeps its own, one merged Application, one shared Check and Respond). Grades 9, 11 and 12 run 6, 5, 6, 5, 5, 5; grade 10 runs 6, 6, 5, 5, 5, 5; 32 in every year. The shared Check and Respond of the third and sixth unit is the course-level check and its respond (about 5 minutes opening, 15 for the page and rehearsals, 13 for the reteach and the rehearsal again, 7 to close and collect). On this path the mid-year check falls in session 17 (week 17), not weeks 15 and 16, and the year-end check in week 32 (For the lead 3).

**A shorter period (30 minutes).** The session keeps its parts in proportion: opening 3, teaching and rehearsal 20, exit item and class sheet 5, hand-out 2. An Understanding session still names at most three sub-skills; the Fluency cases drop from three to two; the Check keeps its full page and rehearsal and takes the next session's first 10 minutes for the rehearsals still unfinished, which that session's opening gives up. A widened course check keeps its unit page (about 9 minutes) and its two sampled items (about 3 minutes each, one prompt per part) in the session, and its unfinished rehearsals take the respond session's first 10 minutes the same way; the retrieval opening keeps one short prompt per row.

**A year about 10 school days short (four sessions).** Fold in the second Fluency of each two-group unit: the group's first Fluency rehearses every sub-skill of the group (fix pass 2026-11-26), and the second only replays them all against a changed case, with nothing assessed on it, so the fold removes no sub-skill's only rehearsal and no assessed or owned part is cut. Its changed case moves to the first 10 minutes of the group's Application, in place of part of that day's rehearsal time (the Application's own case is already a changed one). Each fold-in day saves one 40-minute session, about 30 minutes of teaching net of the 10 moved. Grade 9: days 3, 7 (unit 1), 24, 28 (unit 3). Grade 10: days 67, 71 (unit 7), 77, 81 (unit 8). Grade 11: days 131, 135 (unit 13), 152, 156 (unit 15). Grade 12: days 195, 199 (unit 19), 216, 220 (unit 21). Four a year (160 session minutes, about 120 of teaching), 16 over the band: 240 of the frame's 256 sessions. Where a year is shorter still, the next fold-in is a Respond session's second half, merged into the next unit's first opening; it is never a Check.

**Seniors who leave early.** Where grade 12 ends before week 32, unit 24 runs in its 5-session short form with the year-end check in its shared Check and Respond, in the school's last senior week; no sub-skill is dropped.

**A unit left out.** The frame's leave-out bridges hold: a later unit that needs a left-out unit's card teaches its one-step form in the first ten minutes of its first Understanding session, in place of the retrieval opening, and claims none of the left-out unit's sub-skills (the note on day 11 shows the form for unit 1 left out: the plan page and the support map; day 32's note adds the support map where units 1 and 2 are both left out). In grades 10 to 12, where the year before's unit was left out, the retrieval opening falls back to the row from the year before that.
