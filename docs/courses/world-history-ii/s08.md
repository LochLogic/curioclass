## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

Separate people, faiths, governments and armed groups. Correct false claims through evidence; students never declare a side.

## 8. Tools and supplies across the unit

**Tools:** None; all sections run on print, with no key tool or tool ask. Supply source cards, dated maps with patterned legends, scales, timelines, pencils and a record sheet. Provide every source, task and transcript in print; a projector is optional. Glossed passages total at most 600 words per teaching day, with legal and period language read aloud. Offer large print, tactile outlines, rehearsal and "This changed ___ because ___; however ___." Extension weighs conflicting evidence.

## 9. Family note

We will investigate how people sought independence and equal rights, using sources and maps to weigh achievements, costs and unfinished change. Students study difficult histories with care, without disclosing family experiences or personal beliefs.
