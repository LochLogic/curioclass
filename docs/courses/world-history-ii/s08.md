## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Which means of changing a society have worked best: nonviolence, negotiation, armed struggle or outside pressure?"
**Hook:** Day 108 pairs Nkrumah's independence speech with Lumumba's independence address and a dated colonial map. Why could declaring freedom leave unfinished work?

Teach decolonization, nationalism, partition, apartheid, sanctions, Zionism, antisemitism, Nakba and self-determination in context. Avoid reenactments, graphic images and family disclosure. Separate people, faiths, governments and armed groups. Correct false claims through evidence; students never declare a side.
