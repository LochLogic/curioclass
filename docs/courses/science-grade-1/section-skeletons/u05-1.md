## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1-PS4-2** (days 38, 39, 40, 41, 42): Observe and then explain, with evidence, that we see an object only when light shines on it or it makes its own light.
- skill s1-see-with-light (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Question board.** Group notices under seeing, sources and barriers. Day 38 asks whether the toy disappeared. Day 39 compares box, room and cave records and first meets sources that make light. Day 40 writes the source exception into the account; day 41 practises it on a fresh lamp and unlit toy; day 42 predicts interrupted illumination. This raises the barrier question: day 43 tests transmission, day 44 adds redirection, and day 45 organizes results and prepares a peer plan. Day 46 transfers to an unfamiliar cupboard display and new material samples; day 47 resolves remaining questions.
> - **Carried.** Keep earlier design, shape and resemblance charts. Day 38 starts a condition-to-seeing drawing using unit 4's causal comparisons and unit 3's evidence account. Day 40 revises it from room and cave observations and adds the source exception; day 41 strengthens it. Days 43 and 44 add transmission, shadow and redirection evidence. Day 45 revises kindergarten picture graphs into three transmission groups, with redirection recorded separately. Use the consensus account on day 46; revise and retain it on day 47.
