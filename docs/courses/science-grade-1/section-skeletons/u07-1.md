## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1-LS1-2** (days 56, 57, 58, 59, 60, 61): Use books and media to find patterns in how parents and their young behave that help the young stay alive.
- skill s1-parent-offspring (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Question board.** Group notices under young, adult and survival. Day 56 asks what the cheep might do; day 57 asks whether deer behave similarly. This raises whether pictures agree with words, tested on day 58. Day 59 asks what pattern fits both without erasing differences. Day 60 transfers to unfamiliar duck contact and alarm cases; day 61 answers remaining questions.
> - **Carried.** Keep unit 6's evidence habit; store its devices and design models for day 67. Day 56 begins a young-action, adult-action, survival picture model. Day 57 adds deer hiding and returning to nurse. Day 58 labels which source supports each link. Day 59 reaches a consensus: both generations' actions can help young survive, with different patterns across animals. Use this model on day 60 and retain day 61's revisions.
