## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **1-LS1-1** (days 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15): Solve a people problem with a design that copies how plants or animals use their outside parts to survive, grow and get what they need.
- skill s1-parts-mimic (grade 1)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Question board.** Group notices as holding, sensing and copying. What holds plants (days 11 and 12)? What else do outside parts do (days 13 and 14)? Which jobs could help people (day 15)? How can someone understand our idea (days 16 and 17)? Does it work (day 18)? What else can we solve (day 19)? Days 20 and 21 transfer to protecting a paper museum model.
> - **Carried.** Day 10 starts a parts drawing and retrieves patterns. Days 11 and 12 add roots, stems, leaves and light response; days 13 and 14 add sensing-to-action arrows from animal evidence. Day 15 starts shape-and-job links. Day 16 adds a planned sketch to the design cycle; day 17 revises it after partner interpretation. Day 18 test evidence revises supports and the sketch. Day 19 agrees a consensus chart joining needs, parts and tested shapes; retain it for transfer.
