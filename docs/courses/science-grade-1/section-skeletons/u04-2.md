| 33 | 1-PS4-1 | Plan and carry out tests showing that vibrating things produce sound and that sound sets things vibrating. | **Item 1, 12 minutes:** "A plastic ruler is flicked beside this paper drum and we hear a sound. What will you compare to find which part is sounding? Plan together, test and record." Key: select actual trials separating ruler and drum actions, then compare equal ruler flicks with its free end free and gently stilled. Observe both sources; a separate gentle drum tap supplies a comparison. Attribute sound only from the observations. Saying the stilled source keeps producing sound is A. **Item 2, 12 minutes:** "This thin paper ribbon hangs from a stand beside a playing speaker on the same table. What could explain that? Choose comparisons, test and use both investigations to revise your account." Key: separate supports to remove table contact (the day 32 question "could the table be moving it?"), then compare sound off and on with ribbon position fixed; record actual motion without touching. B denies possible sound-driven motion; C accepts touching as sound-only evidence. Failure to control table contact stays unclassified. | section 4.2 |
