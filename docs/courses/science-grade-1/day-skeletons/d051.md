> - **Safety and set-up.** Before each hands-on day, rehearse signals, secure flashlight batteries, inspect cups and tape down markers in one closed trial lane with a walking route around it. Adults pre-punch cups with a tool the safety read names (adult only), and cut string and cardboard. The farther mark lies within the string length less one arm span; the near mark is about half. Use cotton string without body loops, wire or elastic; keep it low, with gentle tension and no pulling contests. Never tap or shout into cups; hold receiving cups slightly away from ears. Other sounds stay at classroom level and away from ears. Aim lights at low cards, never eyes; no lasers. Stop for discomfort, loose anchors or damaged materials. Children use blunt scissors seated. No chemicals are used. Eye protection is decided per the safety read; none planned for intact blunt materials. Stop rather than continue with snapping apparatus. Only one group tests under direct supervision; others use paper.
