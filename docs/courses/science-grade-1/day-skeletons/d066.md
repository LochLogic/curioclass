## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Check a fresh seeing account and a peer material investigation in which each child carries out the plan and records and explains all four effects.
- **Note:** Year-end part 1 covers every part of 1-PS4-2 and 1-PS4-3: lit and self-luminous objects, direct or media observations, four material kinds and controlled-beam plan. Sentence frame or dictation available. Each child contributes to the plan, follows the procedure and records actual observations: full and partial transmission, blocking and shadow, and mirror redirection, then what these establish. Seeing prompt covers both illuminated and self-luminous objects with observational evidence. Only listed codes sampled. For 30 students: 3-minute launch, 4-minute plans, 12-minute parallel group trials with teacher circulation, 8-minute individual records and seeing account, 3-minute cleanup. Media route uses child-selected unlabelled comparisons under their plan with their own data collection, not completed charts.

- s1-light-materials (grade 1): taught or practised here
