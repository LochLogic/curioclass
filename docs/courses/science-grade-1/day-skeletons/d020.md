## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Prepare an individual account of outside parts and responses and sketch and build a nature-inspired solution for an unfamiliar human problem.
- **Note:** Window continues day 21. Each child's whole task covers both codes: plant and animal parts and sensing or response including plant photo sequence, a human solution copying a part, and a model explaining shape and function to someone else. First preparation day of the observation window: 30 students sketch and build alone or in pairs with each contribution identified. Each records plant and animal part jobs and sensing or response including plants. Day 21 observes the prepared whole task; no new build is required then.

- s1-sketch-shape (grade 1): taught or practised here

- **1-LS1-1:** Solve a people problem with a design that copies how plants or animals use their outside parts to survive, grow and get what they need.
