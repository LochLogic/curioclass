## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Check a new message-receiver challenge, match fresh animal and plant sources to claims and correct a seasonal claim in unit 1's earlier sky-watch context.
- **Note:** Only 1-PS4-4, 1-LS1-2 and 1-ESS1-2 sampled, each part reserved. The preserved built device and dated plan stand for making without another score; each child sends and receives in a new near-and-far challenge and explains the message and why it works. Others independently match new animal text and media evidence to young-signal/adult-response survival claims and compare sources; a brief new plant-and-young caption and animal sources establish that adult plants and animals produce young, without plant parental care. Reuse day 60's individual record without another oral observation or score on old work. Reuse day 64's individual record without another oral comparison or score on old work. These new task forms avoid the preceding two weeks' observation tasks.
