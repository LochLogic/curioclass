## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Check new sky-sequence selections and a nature-inspired material solution with outside-part evidence and an explanatory model.
- **Note:** Mid-year part 1 covers every part of 1-ESS1-1, 1-LS1-1 and K-2-ETS1-2 with talk, drawing and a small model, including plant response. Parallel tasks let the teacher record individuals over both meetings. Only listed codes sampled. Sky prompts: sun and moon paths and predictions and day/night stars. Design prompts: plant and animal part jobs, sensing-response including plants, a copied part meeting a human need and how model shape helps; the model communicates the design. Prepare products in parallel. Six groups of five use 3-minute linked-account turns; others independently finish the new sky or part-and-job record.

**Skills:**

- s1-sky-patterns (grade 1): taught or practised here
- s1-parts-mimic (grade 1): taught or practised here
- s1-sketch-shape (grade 1): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **1-ESS1-1:** Watch the sun, moon and stars and describe the sky patterns they show that can be predicted.
- **1-LS1-1:** Solve a people problem with a design that copies how plants or animals use their outside parts to survive, grow and get what they need.
- **K-2-ETS1-2:** Make a simple sketch, drawing or model showing how an object's shape helps it do the job a problem needs.
