# Section 4.2: Sound makes things vibrate

## Header




## 1. Standards and practices


- **1-PS4-1, this section's share.** "Plan and carry out tests showing ... that sound sets things vibrating": day 32 teaches the reverse direction with two cases (a light strip and rice sealed over a bowl); day 33 checks both directions on unfamiliar cases; day 34 reteaches and checks a fresh case.
- **Limits that bite here.** No wave drawings, wavelength, speed, pitch rules, ear anatomy or hearing tests. A motionless-looking material may move too little or too fast to see. Classroom volume only, never near ears, never raised to force motion; a null result is kept as a result. Children never touch the strip or the speaker.
- **Connections.** W.1.8 (ELA): recall from the investigation to answer a question, by drawing and one dictated sentence, days 32 and 34.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The speaker shakes, and the shaking passes to what is near it; children are not told this, they test it. A light strip may flutter from a draft, a breath or the table, not from sound: the strip hangs from a stand on a separate surface from the speaker, the speaker faces away from it, and the test is the same strip with the sound off and then on, no touching, no breath. A child who says "the table moved it" is asking the right question and is given separate supports to test it. Rice sits on taut film over a bowl, sealed under a raised clear cover; the teacher alone handles rice and loose film, and nothing is eaten. Review words (vibrate, still it, keep, change, watch, evidence arrow) are used as section 4.1 taught them.

## 2. Where it sits


- **Before:** section 4.1, ending day 31, which hands day 32 the hand-back for error A (field 4) and the parked question "can sound make something move too?" **After:** day 35 (a different unit). No readiness check. Day 33's records go to the record sheet that day 34 and day 36 reuse.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** items 1 and 2 (day 33, unit plan field 5), item 1 the ruler beside an unfamiliar paper drum, item 2 the paper ribbon beside a playing speaker. Their cases are held back from every other day. Section exit E34a and E34b (day 34) are the fresh sound-and-motion check. Their cases (a fringe, a new drum record) are also held back.

**Evidence form.** Spoken, drawn, pointed to or sorted. Every exit is asked aloud, one ask each, on picture cards after the teacher says "this is invented for practice"; the stimulus beside the card is a real, dated recording to be made (field 11); the card shows apparatus labels only. A rotating six is heard, 20 seconds each, with a "heard" column. One scale on every record, including day 33: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The unit plan's six components for each case are each marked on this scale; a child meets the code with at least 10 of the 12 components marked shown and at least 4 in each case (plan point 1). Missing evidence stays incomplete.

**Exit questions.**
- **E32 (day 32).** Recording: a foil square moves while a speaker plays. "Pip says the waving card moved the foil. Touch the picture that is a fair check of whether the sound moved it." Cards: speaker off then on, card held still, nothing touching; a hand pressing the foil while the speaker plays; the card waving, speaker off. Answer: the first. Wrong: "sound cannot move foil" (B); the pressing hand (C); the waving card with speaker off (unclassified).
- **E34a (day 34).** Recordings: two unlabelled records of a new hanging paper fringe, one with a hand near it, one with none. "Touch the record that is a fair check of whether sound moves the fringe." Answer: the one with no hand, fixed position, sound off then on. Wrong: the hand-near record (C); "sound cannot do that" (B).
- **E34b (day 34).** Same records plus a new drum record. "Touch the arrow on our chart that the fringe record helps show, and the one the drum record helps show." Answer: sound to moving for the fringe, shaking to sound for the drum. Wrong: a child who says the held drum still sounds (A); one who says the fringe arrow is unproven because sound cannot move things (B).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The class sound model, now two arrows: "it shakes, so it sounds" (4.1) and "it sounds, so it makes things shake" (added day 32, only from the observations). Children use it on day 33 and revise it on day 34. The model and each child's day 33 record are kept for day 36 (no second score on that work). Kindergarten's cause-and-effect model and unit 2's evidence arrow are the parents.
- **Key moment, day 32: a light strip moving.** Ideal instrument: a high-speed video. Spec: none in this unit; the eye sees a flutter or sees none. Reading step: moved, did not move, not clear. A not clear pair is repeated once and both kept. If classroom-volume sound does not move the strip, the named fallback is the adult-made dated motion sequence cards, said aloud to be a recording and marked indirect.
- **Key moment, day 32: rice on film.** Ideal: grains seen to hop. Same step and fallback; the grains stay sealed and the teacher alone handles them.
- **Key moment, day 33: the ruler and the ribbon.** Ruler held flat on a table edge, free end under 10 cm, fingertip flick, eye protection on. The ribbon is a light paper ribbon that days 28 to 34 never use elsewhere (not tissue, foil, card or fringe). If the ribbon shows no motion at classroom volume, use the actual held-back records marked indirect; never invent a result.
- **Equipment, counted.** One speaker, one unfamiliar drum, one ruler and one ribbon stand serve six groups of five, 3 minutes each (day 33, 18 minutes). Three groups run item 1 live and three run item 2 live; each takes the other item from held-back records. Day 32 is a class demonstration; each child's turn is the one request of "off" or "on".
- **Clean-up.** Day 32 (2 minutes): glasses wiped and bagged by the child, speaker off and film bagged by the teacher. Day 33 (3 minutes): glasses (2 minutes, wipes at the tables), teacher collects ruler and speaker. Day 34 uses paper only.
- **A room with nothing.** The records on paper carry every day, labeled indirect; a household speaker, tissue and a bowl with film are enough for day 32. With no speaker, use the sequence cards on days 32 to 34 and say so.

## 6. Days

## Day 32


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Hand-back in: day 31 exit, error A. Open the day 31 sheets at box 3 and say "I held it, I heard ___". The two-minute small group, if few, gathers at the demonstration table.
- **5 min. Plan.** The question board: "Can sound make something move too?" The class plans with Pip: keep the strip's place and touch nothing, change sound off or on, watch the strip. Pip asks what else could move it; the teacher presses the strip and waves a card beside it, and says a touch or moving air from a card is kept out so only the sound changes. A child asks "could the table be moving it?"; the strip hangs from a separate stand and the rice sits on its own stool.
- **8 min. Strip demonstration.** Teacher operates the speaker; a rotating child per group asks "off" or "on" twice. Everyone draws two boxes, sound off and sound on. Eye protection is not needed (no flying pieces).
- **6 min. Rice demonstration.** The same plan; rice on film under a clear cover. Two boxes drawn. If no motion is clear, use the sequence cards and say they are recordings.
- **3 min. Rug.** Add "sound makes things shake" to the class model only if both cases support it, with evidence arrows; a null result is kept as a result.
- **2 min. Clean-up.**
- **3 min. Exit.** E32.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice plans keep, change, watch for the strip and watches strip and rice; the exit gives a new foil with a waving card and Pip's claim, so the child picks the one picture that keeps air and touch out.

Held by: teacher (speaker, strip, rice, chart); children (two-box records). Tool: None. By hand: two-box record. Evidence: E32. Hand-backs in: A. Out: B, C to day 34.

## Day 33


- **3 min. Launch.** Groups of five take places; the teacher says this is a check, everyone wears eye protection, recordings are called recordings, and children may pass. Hand-backs in: none; no reteaching or score this day.
- **2 min. Picture plans.** Each child marks a plan on a two-panel sheet: the question and the comparison they will ask for.
- **18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each.** Per turn: 30 seconds to agree a plan, 45 seconds of shared comparisons with each child's one drum tap or one request, then five 15-second reasons, one from each child, heard by the teacher; 30 seconds to move. Groups 1, 3 and 5 run item 1 live and take item 2 from held-back records; groups 2, 4 and 6 do the reverse. Waiting children do the other item at their table from their group's sealed held-back record set, marking the second panel; it is scored after class from the panel.
- **4 min. Accounts.** Each child records their own paired observations and draws or dictates a revised two-arrow account.
- **3 min. Clean-up.**

Held by: teacher (speaker, ruler, record of heard); groups (the plan); children (sheet). Tool: None. By hand: two-panel sheet. Evidence: unit assessment items 1 and 2. Hand-backs out: gaps to day 34, by letter.

## Day 34


- **10 min. Read feedback and revise.** Two minutes: the teacher tells the three most common picture comments aloud. Then each child finds their own picture cue on the day 33 sheet and revises the account (8 minutes); only a rotating six dictate a sentence. New work is unscored.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs in: B and C from day 32, A, B and C gaps from day 33. Groups A, B and C each 3 minutes with the teacher, the two moves 30 seconds each; others work from picture directions on repair cards. One child follows the same sequence with an adult.
- **7 min. Fresh evidence and section exit.** Children say whether the fan record shows sound moved the fringe, then point to the arrow each record supports. X34a to X34c. Rotating six heard. If the records are not valid, the fresh check remains unfinished and is said so.
- **3 min. Collect.** Sheets collected; board revisited.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice revises the child's own day 33 arrows from cues; the exit lays new fringe, fan and cup records beside the chart and asks which arrow each record helps show, and whether a record with a fan running shows sound moved it.

Held by: teacher (groups, board); children (revised account). Tool: None. By hand: revised paper account. Evidence: X34a to X34c. Hand-backs in: A, B, C and gaps.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 32, "Could the table be moving it?"; day 33, "What did you compare, and what did you see?"; day 34, "Which record is fair, and what does it show?"
- Written task, day 34: the revised account, a drawing of both arrows with one dictated sentence (W.1.8 bridge).
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: each arrow is drawn the right way round; the evidence for each is a record the child can point to; no touching is counted as sound evidence.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 32 pulls day 31's sheets (error A) and the stopping test. Day 33 pulls the plan frame, the two-direction model and the picture plan. Day 34 pulls the day 33 sheet, the question board and section 4.1's chart.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: sound, shake, vibrate, ribbon, rice, speaker, on, off, fair. Frames: "When the sound was on, ___." "I held ___ the same, I changed ___." Home-language words and pointing are accepted.
- **Access.** Observe through sight or a neutral adult description of the motion that does not give its cause; do not score hearing or require humming. Large prints and pointing for any child.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all prompts aloud; mark pictures; accept dictation or an adapted marking.
- **Reading load.** None. The student page is boxes and labels (sound off, sound on, I saw), well under 100 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Plan a test for a second light material from the teacher's tray with the same plan frame.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 32: a small speaker, a paper strip hung from a chair, sound off then on, nothing touched. Day 33: as for day 32 with a new strip and a drum; draw both and say what you changed. Day 34, the last day of the unit: hear the picture comment, revise, choose the fair record, answer E34a and E34b. The adult writes the child's words and does not correct.
- **One projector.** Show the sound-off and sound-on records one at a time, large; each child still marks their own paper. Day 33's turns run in order at one table.
