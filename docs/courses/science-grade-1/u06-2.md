# Section 6.2: Can we depend on our device?

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **1-PS4-4, this section's share.** "Build a device of your own design that sends a message far away by light or by sound." This section carries the whole standard in a new problem: "of your own design" (an improved design that cites trial evidence, day 53), "build" (one added or changed part, day 53), "sends a message far away" (a send and a receipt at a near and a farther mark, observed on day 54 and repaired on day 55). Section 6.1 made the first device and its first trials.
- **Limits that bite here.** The farther mark is a safe classroom distance within the string length less one arm span. Light is aimed at low cards, never at eyes; sound stays at classroom level and away from ears; no emergency patterns. No wave diagrams, electronic workings or ranking of classmates' designs. No new construction on day 54.
- **Connections.** 1.MD.A.2, day 54: the marks are laid in whole strips (held-back counts, set by the teacher before class). Unit 5's "block", day 53: a cardboard wall stops light but not sound (called a wall, not a screen, so it is not mixed with unit 5's screen that light lands on).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** "Dependable" means the same device, tried the same way, gives the same receiver mark each time so far; two matching trials are a start, not a promise. An honest failed trial is evidence, not a poor mark. Noticing a signal is not understanding it. A child changes one designed part; a borrowed flashlight alone is not building. A partner's making part is named on the plan sheet. Day 54 is a check: no coaching, no reteaching.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** item 1 (day 54, unit plan field 5): the child's prepared device, a send and a receipt at near and farther marks, and a spoken why. It uses the day 53 plan and product and day 54 trials; no exit follows it.

**Evidence form.** Spoken, drawn, pointed to or sorted. Exits are asked aloud, one thing at a time, answered by touching a picture card, after the teacher says the case is invented for practice. A rotating six is heard, with a "heard" column. One scale on every record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 54 is recorded by the unit plan's components on the same scale: send at each distance, receipt recorded at each distance, the spoken why using the model, and the judgment of usefulness; the four day 53 points come from the revised sketch and the made product.

**Exit questions.**
- **E52a (day 52).** Pip tries a new device once and the friend marks the right card. Pip says, "It will work every time." "Is Pip sure yet? Say yes or no." Answer: no, try it again. Wrong: yes (F).
- **E52b (day 52).** Two pictured records for new devices: one with two marks that match the sender's card, one with a match and a miss. "Touch the record that gives a reason to depend on the device." Answer: the two matches. Wrong: the match and a miss (F).
- **E53a (day 53).** A trial record card is read aloud first: behind a cardboard wall, a lamp blink got no mark and a drum tap got the right mark. Then a new card: a friend behind a cardboard wall and a helper who must tell the friend the paint is dry. "The record shows what got through the wall. Draw a device that could get the message to the friend." Answer: a sound device (a drum, a tap, cups with a string). Wrong: a lamp (no letter; the wall blocks light, review of unit 5, said aloud).
- **E53b (day 53).** The same record card. "Touch the row of the record that tells you your device should work." All touch; three are heard say why. Answer: the drum tap row. Wrong: the lamp blink row (no letter).
- **E55a (day 55).** A pictured failed trial: a flap light aimed up at the ceiling, not at the low card; the friend marked a different card (a new case: the slack string is day 49's taught card). Three fixes: aim the light down at the card; shout louder; change the message. "Touch the step to fix." Answer: the aim. Wrong: shout (no letter; the problem is without shouting); change the message (no letter).
- **E55b (day 55).** "Which step of your own trial did you repair, and what did the friend mark after?" Each child points to the step on their trial record (signal, aim or string, distance, receiver) and to the friend's mark; a rotating six are asked aloud. Apply only the letter that matches the claim a child makes (A, C, D, F); other answers are recorded in plain words. The practice message is new (one long, one quick) and unused on days 51 to 54.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


**Carried.** The system model (sender, device, receiver) and the causal arrows, used for the spoken why on day 54 and repaired on day 55; the design cycle chart gains "improve" on day 53 (problem, sketch, build, test, improve); the day 51 results chart gains the day 52 repeat. The child's made product and trial records are preserved at the end of day 55 for day 67. The question board closes on day 55 with the class's dictated answer.

**Key moments and instruments.**
- The receiver's reading (days 52, 54, 55). Ideal: the receiver's own mark made before the sender's card is shown; no sensor. Fits. Marks sit at whole strips, the instrument's step; no half strip is read.
- Repeat comparison (day 52). Ideal: a table that lines up trial 1 and trial 2. The paper results chart fits.
- Tension (days 52 and 54). The chart records tight or not tight; string that is not straight is recorded, and the trial is repeated once with both kept.

**A room with nothing.** Cup and string, a box drum, and a shared flashlight with a child-made flap are the minimum. If a reading fails (not heard, battery gone, string slipped), the receiver marks "could not tell", one repeat is made and both are kept. If no lane is possible, the teacher gives invented trial records, says aloud they are invented, and the day practices reading records only; nothing is scored from invented records. If a device breaks on day 54, the child's turn records "device failed" and day 55's repair mends it.

## 6. Days

## Day 52


- **6 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs in: day 51 exit, errors C (3 min) and D (3 min). C: the near and farther results side by side; what was tested. D: two pictured pairs; point to the pair with one change.
- **2 min. Rug.** The board's new heading, dependable: "Does the same device give the same mark when we try it again?" The word names a comparison children already make on their chart; no new idea.
- **18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each.** The same lane, marks and message card as the child's day 51 chart (read from the chart). One group at a time under the teacher's eye; each child repeats the near and the farther send. Waiting groups sort invented trial pairs into "same both times" and "not the same".
- **3 min. Compare.** Each child writes the new marks beside the day 51 marks on the chart and circles "same" or "not the same" for near and for farther.
- **2 min. Clean-up.** Devices stored, string collected.
- **3 min. Exit.** E52a, E52b.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice repeats the class trial and circles same or not; the exit gives a new clapper tried once and two new records, so the child judges Pip's every-time claim and picks the record that gives a reason to depend on it.

Held by: teacher (lane, hand-backs, record of who was heard); groups (their turn); children (chart). Tool: None. By hand: the results chart, one more column (about 45 printed words). Evidence: E52a, E52b. Hand-backs in: C, D. Out: F to day 53.

## Day 53


- **3 min. Return and repair.** Hand-back in: day 52 exit, error F. A one-trial record beside a two-trial record (invented for practice); children say which tells more.
- **3 min. The new problem.** The display-helper case, printed "invented for practice": a helper at a display table far across the room must be told that a picture is ready. The class agrees one new pattern (three quick) and a farther mark. One question, answered by pointing to the recess bell and the display table: why do people send messages far away? No phone or radio workings.
- **22 min. Prepare.** Set-up of the day: each child makes a dated sketch citing one earlier trial for a feature they will add (5 min); adapts the device made on day 49 by adding or changing one designed part, a cover, a stand, a shutter or a drum surface, from pre-cut pieces and tape (13 min, while the teacher takes each child's dictated sentence, 20 seconds each); and revises the sketch to show what was built and why it should help (4 min). A new device only if the old one cannot be adapted. Partners mark their own making part. No live test today. The teacher gives a spoken check to a rotating six, 30 seconds each, during the building minutes; the design-cycle chart gains improve (30 seconds, in The new problem); every child gets the written picture-linked comment after class.
- **2 min. Clean-up.** Devices named and stored; pieces collected.
- **3 min. Exit.** E53a, E53b.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice adds one part to the child's own device for the display problem; the exit reads a new record behind a cardboard wall and asks for a device the record supports and the row that says so, a reason the build never asks.

Held by: teacher (hand-backs, checks, comments after class); groups (supplies); children (sketch, device). Tool: None. By hand: the sketch and plan sheet (about 50 printed words). Evidence: E53a, E53b, the revised sketch and the made product. Hand-backs in: F. Out: none.

## Day 54


- **3 min. Launch.** This is a check; it is called a check; children may pass; light is low and aimed at cards. Two marks, at three and six strips, laid before class; receivers stationed at the marks; message "picture is ready" is three quick, sent for the first time today.
- **18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each.** One lane, one group at a time. Five 30-second child turns plus 30 seconds to change over. Each child sends at the near mark and at the farther mark; the receiver at each mark marks a picture before the sender's card is shown; roles alternate across the five turns so every child receives. Cup devices note tight or not. No coaching. Waiting groups at their desks match familiar device-evidence cards to the model and draw a reason.
- **6 min. Records.** Each child finishes a paper trial record, draws how the device carries the message and rings what the two marks show (near and farther, near only, not yet); the rotating six give a spoken why, 30 seconds each; the teacher notes the model words used and marks the why and the judgment for every child from the record after class. The lane turns carry the two sends only.
- **3 min. Clean-up.** Devices and records kept for day 55.

Held by: teacher (lane, record sheet with a "heard" column); groups (their turn); children (record). Tool: None. By hand: the trial record (about 50 printed words). Evidence: unit assessment item 1. Hand-backs in: none, no reteaching. Out: gaps by letter to day 55.

## Day 55


- **8 min. Read feedback and revise.** The teacher tells the three most common picture comments aloud (2 min); each child corrects or adds one picture or label on the day 53 sketch using the written comment (6 min). A rotating six is heard. New work is unscored.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Hand-backs in: gaps from day 54 by letter. Group A with F, 4 min with the teacher; group C with D, 4 min; 2 min to move. Group B with E works from a written move: the sketch beside the made device, then six strips laid touching and recounted. One child follows the same sequence with an adult.
- **9 min. Exit: fresh trial.** Six minutes: children whose send or receipt failed on day 54 repair the named step and try once at the failed mark, about 20 seconds each, up to eighteen trials in the one lane; the new mark replaces the day 54 value for send and receipt, marked revised, and earns nothing else. The practice message is new. Others redraw the sender, device and receiver model with the arrows. Three minutes: E55a for all on paper; E55b heard from a rotating six. The closing dictation (how the device carries an agreed message to a receiver) is taken from three or four rotating children in the six minutes.
- **3 min. Collect and close.** Products and trial records are collected and kept for day 67. The class dictates one answer to the compelling question while sheets are collected.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice repairs a failed step in the child's own day 54 trial; the exit gives a new pictured failure, a light aimed at the ceiling, and asks which step to fix, then has repairers point to their step and the friend's mark.

Held by: teacher (groups, lane, scribe); children (records). Tool: None. By hand: the revised sketch and the fresh record (about 50 printed words). Evidence: E55a, E55b, the revised day 54 send and receipt points. Hand-backs in: gaps by letter. If more than eighteen need a trial, the rest are recorded unfinished and said so.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 52: "Did the friend mark the same both times?" Day 53: "What did you add, and which trial told you to?" Day 54: "How does your device carry the message to the friend?" Day 55: "Which step did you fix?"
- **Written task, day 53.** The revised sketch with a dictated sentence: "I added ___ because ___."
- **Look-fors.** A feature is tied to a trial record; the made product shows the added or changed part; the revision says how it should help the message reach its receiver.

## 8. Practice that returns

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: dependable, repeat, trial, record, improve, shutter, stand. Frames: "I tried it ___ times." "The friend marked ___ both times." "I added ___ because ___." Pointing and home-language words are accepted.
- **Access.** Either medium for every task; a child may be sender or receiver. A child who cannot hear a quiet drum uses light or the string. A child who cannot handle small pieces directs an adult to place a part; an adult steadies materials but does not choose the design or send for the child.
- **Check accommodations (day 54).** All prompts read aloud; accept pointing, enlarged pictures, dictation and adapted grips. An honest failed trial is credited as evidence.
- **Reading load.** None. Each student page stays under 100 words; all else is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** A child explains a failed trial from the record and proposes one change to a part.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 52: repeat the day 51 send and compare the two marks. Day 53: add one part to the device and tell the adult which trial told you to. Day 54: one send at each mark; the adult marks the picture. Day 55, the last day of the unit: fix one step, try once, and dictate to the adult how the device carries the message. The adult writes the child's words and does not correct.
- **One projector.** Show pictured cases large; every child marks their own page. Day 54's turns run in order at one lane.
