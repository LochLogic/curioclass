## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **4-ESS2-1** (days 57, 58, 59, 60): Observe or measure to gather evidence of how water, ice, wind or plants weather rock or how fast they cause erosion.
- **4-ESS1-1** (days 57): Find evidence in patterns of rock layers and the fossils in them to explain how a landscape has changed over time.
- skill s4-weathering-erosion (grade 4)
- skill s4-rock-layers (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 57: show the canyon photograph in field 10 without explanatory captions. Trace the river and matching stripes. Ask the compelling question; children draw tentative explanations and post questions.
> Model safe measurement on day 59 and evidence notes on day 69. Ask what would change a claim, then step back while children choose controls and compare accounts.
>
> Question board: "Where did the missing rock go?" drives days 58 to 62. "What changes the amount moved?" leads through days 63 to 65. "What was here before the river?" opens day 66 and drives days 67 to 69. Day 70 attaches evidence to answered questions and retains uncertainties. Transfer uses unfamiliar slopes, separate process records and an unfamiliar layered valley in field 5.
>
> Carried: retain cause-and-effect arrows and the pattern chart. Day 57 starts a landscape sequence with tentative scale and stability explanations. Day 62 revises arrows and starts the data graph from repeated measurements: breaking differs from carrying. Day 66 revises scale and stability explanations using small repeated changes over long times. Day 67 adds fossil order to the pattern chart. Day 68 revises layer matching after deformation. Day 70 reaches consensus on all five representations and their limits; children use them on transfer cases.
