## 1. Standards and practices

- skill s4-structures-function (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 94 reveals an original illustrated cat-at-night sequence: eyes shine during a flash; the cat crosses a dim yard and turns at a sound. Ask the compelling question before explanations. Day 104 reveals yesterday's white flower beside a newly colored one and starts the class comparison.
> Model an evidence-linked arrow on day 96, sufficiency of evidence on day 106, and a full evidence-based argument on day 110. Then ask which observation challenges an account; step back as children test and revise.
>
> Question board: day 94 groups seeing questions; days 95 to 98 ask what light must reach. That raises how information becomes action, days 99 to 103. Day 104 asks how a plant gets resources without walking; days 105 to 109 widen parts into systems. Days 110 to 114 ask what makes an argument convincing. Attach evidence to answers; retain unresolved questions. Transfer cases (field 5): a mirror-and-barrier seeing case, an odor-and-experience animal case, a plant record and a sea-otter record.
> Carried: keep unit 5's claim-evidence-reasoning frame; retain patterns as supporting evidence and its design record for the year handoff. Cause-effect and light-ray models begin day 94; revise after blocking and mirror evidence on days 95 to 97. System-model and structure-function begin day 99; revise after catches on day 100, learned signals on day 101 and owl and heat-sensing snake cases on day 102. Extend to plants on day 104; revise after stem, flower and animal evidence on days 107 to 109. Revise arguments on day 112 after critiques. Day 113 establishes consensus; day 114 challenges it, unscored. Days 115 to 116 use these models on new cases; never withdraw them.
