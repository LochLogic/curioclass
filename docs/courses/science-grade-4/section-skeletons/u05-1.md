## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **4-ESS2-2** (days 77, 78, 79, 80, 81, 82): Read maps and their data to describe patterns in Earth's features, such as where mountains, volcanoes and earthquakes are found.
- skill s4-earth-feature-maps (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 77: reveal separate earthquake and volcano overlays on a world map, without explanatory captions. Children trace clusters, draw a tentative pattern model and ask the compelling question. Field 10 fixes the records and their different time spans. The printed volcano key reads "erupted or possibly active within the past 10,000 years", so the dots are not read as 2025 eruptions.
> Model reading a key on day 78 and recording observations before claims on day 79. Step back during design choices; ask which evidence would change a recommendation.
>
> Question board: "What do the colours mean?" drives day 78; "What features follow the dots?" drives days 79 to 81; "Does another event fit?" drives day 82. "Can people stop the danger?" opens days 83 to 84; "Which protection works when conditions change?" drives days 85 to 90. Day 91 attaches evidence to answers and retains uncertainties. Transfer: unfamiliar paired maps and a new coastal town, field 5.
> Carried: retain unit 4's pattern chart and causal arrows. Day 77 starts the map model; day 81 revises patterns using unfamiliar land and seafloor evidence. Day 84 extends the design cycle and claim-evidence-reasoning frame to hazard protection. Days 85 and 88 revise protective arrows after shaking and changed-flow evidence; day 89 adds warning-to-action links. Day 91 reaches consensus on all three carried representations; day 92 uses them on new cases.
