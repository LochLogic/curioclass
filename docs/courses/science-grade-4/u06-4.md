# Section 6.4: Arguments about interacting structures


## Header


Arguments about interacting structures

## 1. Standards and practices


**4-LS1-1: "Construct an argument".** Bring animals to equal depth with plants: inside/outside parts support survival, growth, behavior and reproduction through interactions. Argument joins LS1.A and systems on diagrams and dated records. Organ lists alone are not arguments.

Day 109 teaches heart, lungs, stomach, brain and skin as visible-scale parts. Bird diagrams show ovaries, internal egg formation, shell/contents and development; not every egg develops. Also teach nonhuman mammal uterus (womb) and milk glands before unfamiliar assessment use, using a generic mammal diagram. No human organs or student-body comparisons. Day 110 models a full oral argument from familiar plant records, then practices components separately. Day 111 retrieves reasons/evidence, W.4.1. Explain that one observation can support survival and growth, but a confident label is not sufficient evidence. Review cause/effect and grade 3 claim/evidence/reason; no new cellular mechanism.
