## 1. Standards and practices

**Adult explanation.** Battery energy is stored; current transfers it to sound, with some warming. The battery does not create energy. A switch can join when a lid opens if a folded spring contact is released by the lid. Compare a released spring and a sliding contact proposal, both physically buildable. Adult prepares smooth contacts; no coil winding or cell chemistry. Review grade 3 u02-2 need, criterion, limit and token, and design-cycle chart: define, sketch, build, test, compare. ## 5. What is carried, and instruments

**Carried.** Review the grade 3 design-cycle chart and criterion wording, extending them with energy conversion. Each child keeps a single-state plan and initialed implementation/test notes. Day 27 adds mechanism predictions; day 28 adds the reason for change and retest; day 29 integrates. Teacher preserves originals through feedback/revision.

**Instrument.** Ideal: reproducible opening and detectable signal at fixed place. Five whole-count trials fit the criterion, not a measured energy/decibel claim. Six devices partly fit: one quiet test lane, six three-minute team slots (18 minutes). Five children each operate once for 30 seconds, with 30 seconds reset per team; one child records all events and everyone copies before/after results with initials. Rotate roles on retest. For deaf/hard-of-hearing access use a compatible visible indicator on the same circuit and a fixed sight point before setting criteria. No key or digital tool.

Pretested compatible cells/loads only; smooth adult-folded foil, adult-cut boxes, disconnect before changes, load always in loop. No outlets, shorts or hot wires; stop/disconnect if warm. No projectiles or liquids. No optional rubber-band activity, so no latex. Five minutes cleanup: disconnect, count, wipe hands/tables. No-kit fallback Alarm R: original two detections in five, three loose-lead failures; secured lead four in five, one loose-lead failure, same point/parts, invented. This supports paper reasoning, never replaces actual designing/testing. Hardware failure is recorded first; adult checks once within work time, then record fallback and missing performance.
