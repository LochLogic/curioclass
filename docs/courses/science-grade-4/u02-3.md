# Section 2.3: A reliable signal

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 4. Unit 2, Energy on the move. "How does energy get from place to place, and where does the energy we use come from?" Anchor: a hand-crank flashlight that lights when the handle turns, with no battery to change (days 16 to 38): see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 24 to 29.** Days by kind: understanding 2 (24, 27), application 4 (25, 26, 28, 29). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 3 of 5 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Defaults: [DEFAULTS.md](DEFAULTS.md). Marker blocks are inherited data, not printable copy. Operational directions below govern draft-run changes. Unit 1 grade 4 entries are planned only: reintroduce their ideas, never claim completed teaching. No earlier key or specialist read is assumed.

## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **4-PS3-4** (days 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29): Use science ideas to design, test and improve a device that turns one form of energy into another.
- **4-PS3-2** (days 29): Observe to gather evidence that sound, light, heat and electric current carry energy from one place to another.
- skill s4-energy-device (grade 4)
- skill s4-energy-transfer (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Group the question board by paths, control and origins. Days 16 to 19 ask, "Can energy reach something without the objects touching?" Observations raise "How does a wire carry energy?", answered on days 20 to 23. "Can we control a useful signal?" drives design on days 24 to 29. "Where did our useful energy come from?" drives days 30 to 34. Day 35 attaches evidence to answered questions and retains unresolved ones. Transfer uses new station arrangements, a pull-cord generator and a different resource-use case.
> Carried: retain unit 1 cause-and-effect and energy arrows on day 16. Revise on day 19 from sound, sunlight and hand-warming observations; day 23 adds the closed current path from circuit evidence. Begin the design cycle on day 24; day 27 failure records guide refinement, revised after day 28 retests. Day 34 adds resource-to-use and environmental-effect branches from combined sources. Day 35 forms consensus; individuals apply these models in the end task. Retain the explanation frame; force arrows remain distinct from energy paths.
<!-- /skel -->

**Share.** 4-PS3-4: "Apply scientific ideas to design, test, and refine" a converting device. Day 29 also uses 4-PS3-2 observation evidence. Designing solutions, PS3.B/D and energy and matter join in a closed-path signal that meets stated criteria/constraints.

**Adult explanation.** Battery energy is stored; current transfers it to sound, with some warming. The battery does not create energy. A switch can join when a lid opens if a folded spring contact is released by the lid. Compare a released spring and a sliding contact proposal, both physically buildable. Adult prepares smooth contacts; no coil winding or cell chemistry. Review grade 3 u02-2 need, criterion, limit and token, and design-cycle chart: define, sketch, build, test, compare. Connections: two-step whole-number budget day 24, not later grade 4 arithmetic. No optional car or solar heater in this draft: retain the qualifying signal.
