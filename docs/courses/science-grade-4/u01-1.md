## 1. Standards and practices


**Share.** 4-PS3-1: "Use evidence to construct an explanation" begins with observations, controls and repeated measurements. Day 4 supplies motion energy after children find the pattern. Practice: constructing explanations; core idea: PS3.A; concept: energy and matter, supported by cause and effect.

**Adult explanation and limits.** Compare one marble at two visibly different speeds immediately before contact. Release marks help repeat conditions; marks alone do not establish speed. Pretest and confirm the visible difference. Cup travel is an indicator in this controlled comparison, never an energy measurement. No different-mass ranking, speed arithmetic, stored-position energy or numerical energy. Day 3 retrieves whole-centimeter lengths and line plots from earlier mathematics.

**Operational print copy below replaces inherited wording, not the Goal map.** All exit cases and numbers are invented for practice. Print and say that label. Reserve them from practice. Each child responds on paper in five minutes. One record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Blank work gets a conference, no error letter. More than ten misses among thirty is many. Return each paper next lesson with a specific next step.

- **E1, day 1, 4-PS3-1.** Pictures: the same train hits the same paper tower at two speeds. Faster contact scatters more pieces. Print: "The pictures show two train speeds. Draw your possible explanation for the tower's different changes. Add one question that could test your idea." Answer: tentative connection between train motion and displaced pieces; a repeatable question, such as whether repeated faster rolls scatter more pieces. Accept plausible alternatives; no energy vocabulary required. Wrong: "My guess proves every future result" (I).
  Shape: tentative explanatory drawing plus a test question. Practice uses live noticing and an oral board, never a paired picture-to-explanation task or train case.
- **E2, day 2, 4-PS3-1.** Print: "A ring rolls into a lid. Plan a fair test of faster and slower ring rolls. Show what changes, what stays, and what you watch." Answer: vary ring speed; keep ring, lid, surface, contact arrangement and target start fixed; observe lid motion, repeating both speeds. Wrong: different lids for the speeds (G).
  Shape: design a complete comparison from a pictured apparatus. Practice diagnoses supplied setups, never a blank plan card or ring/lid.
- **E3, day 3, 4-PS3-1.** Drawing: washer target starts at ruler mark 6, ends at 15. Print: "The washer starts at 6 centimeters and stops at 15 centimeters. Another trial moves it 9 centimeters. Record both travel distances with units. Plot both trials on a labeled line plot." Answer: 15 minus 6 is 9; both distances 9 centimeters; two marks above 9 on a distance-in-centimeters axis. Wrong: first distance 15 (H); axis energy in centimeters (B).
  Shape: reconstruct nonzero-start travel and plot duplicate trials. Practice measures cup travel in a table; plot rehearsal places one given value, never this compound task or washer.
- **E4, day 4, 4-PS3-1.** Matching disk table/plot: slower 2, 3, 2 centimeters; faster 7, 8, 7. Print: "The same disk hits the same target. What repeated pattern connects its speed and motion energy? Use both displays." Answer: both show every faster trial moving the target farther; faster disk motion has more energy. Wrong: equal energy at both speeds (A); centimeters measure energy (B).
  Shape: synthesize two supplied displays into a pattern-to-energy statement. Practice builds a class display and annotates single observations, never a completed two-display inference card or disk.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


**Carried.** Review question board, "notices become questions" (science-grade-3 u01-1 day 1). Teacher holds the board; children retain observation notes and tentative ideas. Days 2/3 add controls and records; day 4 revises the explanation. Review claim, evidence and reason: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___" (science-grade-3 u06-4 day 113). Add energy only on day 4; nothing withdrawn.

**Equipment decision.** One demonstration station, two protected handlers and four observers in a rotating six. Six two-minute trials take twelve minutes; no thirty-child handling promise. Everyone keeps individual records. Days 1 to 3 use marble, guarded cardboard ramp, cup, tape and whole-centimeter ruler. Two fitted impact goggles, cleaning wipes and storage bags ready. Handlers remain protected through packing; other children stay behind the boundary. Five cleanup minutes count objects, clear the lane, wipe and bag two goggles. Stop for damaged guards or missing protection.

**Readings and fallback.** Ruler step one centimeter; report nearest whole centimeter, or less than one centimeter, never false zero. Failed read: unreadable; repeat once and retain both entries, a grade 3 review routine. If speed cannot be distinguished, containment fails or travel cannot be read, stop and use Ramp record R: slower 3, 4, 3; faster 8, 9, 8 centimeters. Print and say invented for practice; never merge invented and measured rows.

**Instrument verdict.** Ideal: real repeated rolls and visible travel. Ramp-and-cup simulation, spec only, does not exist, partly fits because replay supplies no observations. Day 4 Trial replay (light), show why, five minutes; paper strips fill that slot if unavailable. Asks: [TOOL-ASKS.md](TOOL-ASKS.md). No key tool. No-kit R retains explanation but cannot supply conducting evidence.

## 6. Days



Inherited day rows are reference data; timed blocks below give operative allocations.


## Day 1

- **3 min. Protection and setup.** Check guarded lane; two handlers fit goggles. Others behind boundary.
- **7 min. Anchor.** Two marble rolls into the same cup. Record differences without an energy answer. Use R only if live rolls fail.
- **5 min. Review position patterns.** Fresh invented strip: three equal steps and a blank. Children mark the next step; teacher traces intervals for misses.
- **20 min. Notice and question board.** Teacher holds board, children observation notes. Hear six at 45 seconds; collect other questions. Keep initial explanations tentative; no paired exit-shaped picture task.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Count objects, pack lane, wipe and bag two goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E1, individual paper. Tool: None. E1 to day 2 Return and repair.

**Shared shape.** Practice uses s01-P1: Invented for practice. A toy passes marks A, B and C at steady beats. The marks have equal gaps. Mark its next place and say what repeats; s01-K1: Watch the marble and cup. What changes do you notice? What possible cause should we test?. The independent exit uses s01-X1: The pictures show two train speeds. Draw your possible explanation for the tower's different changes. Add one question that could test your idea. Keep its case concealed until Exit.

## Day 2

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return E1; repair I. Every child separates observed from expected on paper.
- **8 min. Compare controls.** Revisit a board question. Diagnose supplied marble setups by crossing out extra changes; no blank plan task.
- **22 min. Try the comparison.** Next rotating six: two protected handlers, four observers. Six two-minute trials; setup and discussion use ten minutes. Everyone judges comparability and records independently.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Count, clear lane, wipe and bag two goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E2, individual paper. Tool: None. G to day 3 Return and repair.

**Shared shape.** Practice uses s01-P2: A child says, "The next cup roll will go farther." Label this as observed or expected; s01-K2: Two setups use the same marble and surface. Setup A changes speed and cup size. Setup B changes only speed. Cross out the extra change. The independent exit uses s01-X2: A ring rolls into a lid. Plan a fair test of faster and slower ring rolls. Show what changes, what stays, and what you watch. Keep its case concealed until Exit.

## Day 3

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return E2; repair G.
- **5 min. Review measurement.** Pencil from 2 to 7: count five intervals. Review ruler zero. Teacher plots one unrelated given value; child places one value, not a paired-distance task.
- **25 min. Measure repeated trials.** Fit protection. Six cup trials, two minutes each. Remaining thirteen minutes hold setup, demonstration, one unclear repeat and unit checking. Each child fills a speed/trial/distance table; no reconstruction-and-plot sheet.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Count and pack station; wipe and bag goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E3, individual paper. Tool: None. H/B to day 4 Return and repair.

**Shared shape.** Practice uses s01-P3: A slower roll uses one cup. A faster roll uses a larger cup. Cross out the change that hides the speed effect; s01-K3: Invented for practice. A pencil starts at ruler mark 2 and ends at 7 centimeters. Show its travel by counting intervals. The independent exit uses s01-X3: The washer starts at 6 centimeters and stops at 15 centimeters. Another trial moves it 9 centimeters. Record both travel distances with units. Plot both trials on a labeled line plot. Keep its case concealed until Exit.

- **15 min. Revise the class explanation.** Discuss controlled repeated effects; supply motion energy. Teacher holds revised class model; each child annotates an observed cup change. Hear six at 45 seconds, collect all notes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E4, individual paper. A/B to day 5 Return and repair.

## 7. Talk and writing



Day 1: "What did we see, and what do we still wonder?" Day 2: "Which change hides the cause?" Day 4: "What might these distances tell us about transfer?" Hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each inside a five-minute portion of work; collect everyone else's notes. Written task day 3: individual trial table. Look for each trial, units, condition and honest unreadable entries.

## 8. Practice that returns



Day 1 reviews grade 3 position table, turn/side/mark. Day 2 reviews fair test: "keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing"; predict; trial. Day 3 reviews centimeter, ruler zero, whole centimeter; repeat an unclear try once and keep both. Day 4 reviews pattern and claim, evidence and reason. No earlier unit of this grade. No timed fluency target.

## 9. Supports and extension



Words: faster, slower, motion energy. Frame: "I saw __. I still wonder __." Read data labels aloud. Provide large ruler drawings, tactile start/stop marks and a scribe within individual work. At most 100 independent reading words daily; read stems and describe pictures aloud. Extension: explain why changing the receiver weakens the inference.
