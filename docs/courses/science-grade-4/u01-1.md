## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** 4-PS3-1: "Use evidence to construct an explanation" begins with observations, controls and repeated measurements. Day 4 supplies motion energy after children find the pattern. Practice: constructing explanations; core idea: PS3.A; concept: energy and matter, supported by cause and effect.

**Adult explanation and limits.** Compare one marble at two visibly different speeds immediately before contact. Release marks help repeat conditions; marks alone do not establish speed. Pretest and confirm the visible difference. Cup travel is an indicator in this controlled comparison, never an energy measurement. No different-mass ranking, speed arithmetic, stored-position energy or numerical energy. Day 3 retrieves whole-centimeter lengths and line plots from earlier mathematics.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** none; section 1.4 administers items 1 and 2. This section prepares item 1.

All exit cases and numbers are invented for practice. Print and say that label. Reserve them from practice. Each child responds on paper in five minutes. One record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Blank work gets a conference, no error letter. More than ten misses among thirty is many. Return each paper next lesson with a specific next step.

- **E1, day 1, 4-PS3-1.** Pictures: the same train hits the same paper tower at two speeds. Faster contact scatters more pieces. Print: "The pictures show two train speeds. (a) Draw a possible explanation for the tower's different changes. (b) Add one question that could test your idea." Answer: tentative connection between train motion and displaced pieces; a repeatable question, such as whether repeated faster rolls scatter more pieces. Accept plausible alternatives; no energy vocabulary required. Wrong: "My guess proves every future result" (I).
  Shape: tentative explanatory drawing plus a test question. Practice uses live noticing and an oral board, never a paired picture-to-explanation task or train case.
- **E2, day 2, 4-PS3-1.** Print: "A ring rolls into a lid. Plan a fair test of faster and slower ring rolls. Use a plan table: change, keep, watch." Answer: vary ring speed; keep ring, lid, surface, contact arrangement and target start fixed; observe lid motion, repeating both speeds. Wrong: different lids for the speeds (G).
  Shape: design a complete comparison from a pictured apparatus. Practice diagnoses supplied setups, never a blank plan card or ring/lid.
- **E3, day 3, 4-PS3-1.** Drawing: washer target starts at ruler mark 6, ends at 15. Print: "The washer starts at 6 centimeters and stops at 15 centimeters. Another trial moves it 9 centimeters. (a) Record both travel distances with units. (b) Plot both trials on a labeled line plot." Answer: 15 minus 6 is 9; both distances 9 centimeters; two marks above 9 on a distance-in-centimeters axis. Wrong: first distance 15 (H); axis energy in centimeters (B).
  Shape: reconstruct nonzero-start travel and plot duplicate trials. Practice measures cup travel in a table; plot rehearsal places one given value, never this compound task or washer.
- **E4, day 4, 4-PS3-1.** Matching disk table/plot: slower 2, 3, 2 centimeters; faster 7, 8, 7. Print: "The same disk hits the same target. What repeated pattern connects its speed and motion energy? Use both displays." Answer: both show every faster trial moving the target farther; faster disk motion has more energy. Wrong: equal energy at both speeds (A); centimeters measure energy (B).
  Shape: synthesize two supplied displays into a pattern-to-energy statement. Practice builds a class display and annotates single observations, never a completed two-display inference card or disk.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

**Carried.** Review question board: "notices become questions" (science-grade-3 u01-1 day 1). Teacher holds the board; children retain observation notes and tentative ideas. Days 2/3 add controls and records; day 4 revises the explanation. Review claim, evidence and reason: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___" (science-grade-3 u06-4 day 113). Review cause and effect from science-grade-3 u01-1 day 1: a push or pull changes motion. Day 4 defines motion energy: "Energy an object has because it is moving." Nothing is withdrawn.

**Equipment decision.** One demonstration station, two handlers and four observers in a rotating six. Use the live route only with existing fitted impact eye protection for everyone present, including the adult; otherwise use R. Six two-minute trials take twelve minutes; no thirty-child handling promise. Everyone keeps individual records. Days 1 to 3 use marble, guarded cardboard ramp, cup, tape and whole-centimeter ruler. Keep everyone protected through packing. Supply six cleaning places, approved wipes and labeled storage bags. Cleanup takes five minutes: two to count and clear, then three for six captains to clean five student pairs each while the adult cleans their own. Follow the cleaning directions; store until ready for reuse. Stop for damaged guards or missing protection.

**Readings and fallback.** Ruler step one centimeter; report nearest whole centimeter, or less than one centimeter, never false zero. Failed read: unreadable; repeat once and retain both entries, a grade 3 review routine. If speed cannot be distinguished, containment fails or travel cannot be read, stop and use Ramp record R: slower 3, 4, 3; faster 8, 9, 8 centimeters. Print and say invented for practice; never merge invented and measured rows. Day 1 R uses motion/sound descriptions only, withholding distances until measurement teaching. On day 3 repeat the two launch conditions three times each, alternating slower and faster.

**Instrument verdict.** Ideal: real repeated rolls and visible travel. Ramp-and-cup simulation, spec only, does not exist, partly fits because replay supplies no observations. Day 4 Trial replay (light), show why, five minutes; paper strips fill that slot if unavailable. Asks: [TOOL-ASKS.md](TOOL-ASKS.md). No key tool. No-kit R retains explanation but cannot supply conducting evidence.

## 6. Days

## Day 1

- **3 min. Protection and setup.** Check guarded lane; everyone fits existing eye protection. Review raised-hand stop signal from science-grade-3 u01-1 day 1: freeze and look. Others stay behind the boundary.
- **7 min. Anchor.** Two marble rolls into the same cup. Record differences without an energy answer. Use R only if live rolls fail.
- **5 min. Review position patterns.** Fresh invented strip: three equal steps and a blank. Children mark the next step; teacher traces intervals for misses.
- **20 min. Notice and question board.** Teacher holds board; children label observed cup changes on notes. Hear six at 45 seconds; collect everyone's notes and questions. Build the initial class sketch from observation labels. Reserve individual explanatory drawings and test questions for E1.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Count and pack for two minutes; clean all eye protection for three, as field 5 specifies.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E1, individual paper. Tool: None. E1 to day 2 Return and repair.

**Shape:** Practice labels live observations and collects questions. E1 alone combines a tentative explanatory drawing with a test question. Avoid paired train pictures in practice or model cards.

## Day 2

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return E1; repair I. Every child separates observed from expected on paper.
- **8 min. Compare controls.** Revisit a board question. Diagnose supplied marble setups by crossing out extra changes; no blank plan task.
- **22 min. Try the comparison.** Next rotating six: two protected handlers, four observers. Six two-minute trials; setup and discussion use ten minutes. Everyone judges comparability and records independently.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Count and clear for two minutes; clean all eye protection for three.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E2, individual paper. Tool: None. G to day 3 Return and repair.

**Shape:** Practice diagnoses supplied setups. E2 alone designs a whole comparison from ring/lid equipment; no blank planning card in practice.

## Day 3

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return E2 and trial-record feedback; repair G. Each child marks the extra change on a supplied setup.
- **5 min. Review measurement.** Pencil from 2 to 7: count five intervals. Review ruler zero. Teacher plots one unrelated given value; child places one value, not a paired-distance task.
- **25 min. Measure repeated trials.** Fit protection. Six cup trials, two minutes each. Remaining thirteen minutes hold setup, demonstration, one unclear repeat and unit checking. Each child fills a speed/trial/distance table; no reconstruction-and-plot sheet.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Count and pack for two minutes; clean all eye protection for three.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E3, individual paper. Tool: None. H/B to day 4 Return and repair.

**Shape:** Practice records live distances in a table and places one given plot value. E3 alone reconstructs nonzero-start travel and plots duplicate trials; no washer card in practice.

## Day 4

- **10 min. Return and repair.** Return E3; H five minutes, B five.
- **5 min. Trial replay.** Trial replay (light), show why, five minutes if available; otherwise paper replay of class records or R, labeled by provenance.
- **10 min. Build the display.** Transfer class entries to a shared line plot, matching each mark to its row. No completed two-display inference card.
- **15 min. Revise the class explanation.** Discuss controlled repeated effects; supply motion energy. Teacher holds revised class model; each child annotates an observed cup change. Hear six at 45 seconds, collect all notes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E4, individual paper. A/B to day 5 Return and repair.

**Shape:** Practice builds a display and labels single observations. E4 alone infers a pattern and energy relationship from two completed displays; no disk inference card in practice.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 1: "What did we see, and what do we still wonder?" Day 2: "Which change hides the cause?" Day 4: "What might these distances tell us about transfer?" Hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each inside a five-minute portion of work; name them on the record. Collect everyone's notes, return one written next step in the next Return and repair slot, and retain the original. Written task day 3: individual trial table. Look for each trial, units, condition and honest unreadable entries.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: faster, slower, motion energy. Frame: "I saw __. I still wonder __." Read data labels aloud. Provide large ruler drawings, tactile start/stop marks and a scribe within individual work. At most 100 independent reading words daily; read stems and describe pictures aloud. Extension: explain why changing the receiver weakens the inference.
