## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **4-PS3-2** (days 36, 38): Observe to gather evidence that sound, light, heat and electric current carry energy from one place to another.
- **4-PS3-4** (days 36, 38): Use science ideas to design, test and improve a device that turns one form of energy into another.
- **4-ESS3-1** (days 37, 38): Gather and combine information to show that energy and fuels come from natural resources and that using them affects the environment.

- skill s4-energy-device (grade 4)
