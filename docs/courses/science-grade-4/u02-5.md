### s02-E3, day 37

Print: "Invented for practice. An unnamed river facility supplies lighting. After a passage is added, some fish travel upstream. Flooded habitat remains. Combine both sources to explain its energy use and environmental effects. Revise its model to show the improvement and what remains."

Shape: two-source hydroelectric model joining origin/use/renewal/effects and partial improvement.

Key: A: sunlight supports water cycling; rain/snow replenish river flow, turning turbine/generator for lighting. B: dams may block fish travel and reservoirs cover habitat. Marking: Ten points: origin 1; need 1; renewal timescale 1; technology 1; two causal effects 2; changed effect 1; remaining effect 1; accurate identified A contribution 1 and B contribution 1.
