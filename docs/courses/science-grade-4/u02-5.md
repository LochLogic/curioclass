### s02-E2, day 36

Print: "Invented for practice. A pull-cord turns a generator powering a marker lamp. The signal must light on every pull. Keep the listed parts and one box. Two failed pulls coincide with a joint opening. Explain conversion and a justified improvement within these limits. Include a retest prediction."

Shape: unfamiliar motion-to-current explanation plus constrained failure-based refinement. Reserve this response structure and case; use the different practice in field 6.

Key: Pulling supplies motion; generator converts motion to electrical energy; complete-path current carries energy to lamp light. Secure the joint because openings coincide with failed signals. Predict fewer failures under matching pulls, then retest all pulls, without guaranteeing success.

Wrong: "The generator creates energy without input" (A); "A change proves improvement without tests" (D).

Marking: Six points: motion input; generator conversion; complete current path; light output; matching failure evidence; feasible change with comparable retest prediction.

### s02-E3, day 37

Print: "Invented for practice. An unnamed river facility supplies lighting. After a passage is added, some fish travel upstream. Flooded habitat remains. Combine both sources to explain its energy use and environmental effects. Revise its model to show the improvement and what remains."

Shape: two-source hydroelectric model joining origin/use/renewal/effects and partial improvement. Reserve this response structure and case; use the different practice in field 6.

Key: A: sunlight supports water cycling; rain/snow replenish river flow, turning turbine/generator for lighting. B: dams may block fish travel and reservoirs cover habitat. Passage helps upstream travel; flooded land remains. Attribute mechanisms to A/B and facility outcomes to invented record. Renewal is not constant availability.

Wrong: "Renewable means no environmental effects" (E); "The passage removes every effect" (F).

Marking: Ten points: origin 1; need 1; renewal timescale 1; technology 1; two causal effects 2; changed effect 1; remaining effect 1; accurate identified A contribution 1 and B contribution 1.
