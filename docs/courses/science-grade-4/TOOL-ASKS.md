# Science Grade 4 tool asks

## Unit 2: Circuit paths

Requested by [Energy on the move](s02.md). Specification only; no built tool was inspected.

Days 21 and 23, 4-PS3-2: light tool, practice with feedback, five minutes. Students first observe a real matched battery circuit, then predict and explain a different arrangement. Show a single sealed cell, insulated leads, a bulb or buzzer, and a switch. Opening either lead stops the output. Closing a safe complete loop restores it. Show the current path as a loop and energy arrows from stored battery energy through the current to output; do not show energy returning unchanged to the cell. Do not animate invented electrons or measure energy numerically.

Verdict: partly fits the planned job until built and checked. This is optional practice and a weaker no-kit explanation aid. It cannot certify that a child wired, observed or tested a real circuit.

Provide keyboard controls, labels independent of color, a silent visible output, no flashing, a single-projector mode and printed before-and-after cards with matching evidence. Work offline without accounts or tracking. The teacher judges the explanation; the tool may check whether the loop is complete. Never reward a direct battery short. Include an explicit request to disconnect the real circuit before changing leads.

No-kit investigation paths: sound uses dated records of separated drums with sounding and silent trials; light uses dated paired sunlight and shade temperature records; heat uses dated hand-to-spoon observations; circuits use pretested output records. Each record identifies conditions, dates, controls and observational limits. These are supplied evidence, not observations a child made.

Classroom-inaccessible resource processes use a Curio Class resource-path simulation, specification only: day 33, five minutes within diagram comparison, light tool, show why. Animate qualitative particle capture at a generic plant while leaving extraction and land-use effects visible. Compare before and after without numerical emissions or an assertion of zero pollution. Give identical paper diagrams. Verdict: partly fits until the causal account is checked against the linked federal explanations in s02.md. Use no third-party artwork. No classroom combustion, mining or dam construction is attempted.

An air-gap drum effect and hand-to-spoon temperature difference must be detectable at safe settings in a teacher trial. If not, use clearly labeled supplied evidence and record the firsthand evidence still missing. Never raise sound to an unsafe level or use hot water to force an effect. A solar calculator is optional only if already available and pretested; it shows a light input and useful output but cannot establish surface warming.
