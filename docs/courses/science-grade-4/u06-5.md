# Section 6.5: Connect, check and repair the models


## Header

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 4. Unit 6, Senses and structures. "Can a cat see in complete darkness, and how does a plant that cannot move get water, stay safe and spread its young?" Anchor A: a cat crossing a yard at night: its eyes shine in a flashlight or a photo flash, it walks where we would stumble, and it bolts at a sound we did not hear (a photograph or clip, or a pet at home). ## 1. Standards and practices

| 115 | 4-PS4-2 | Develop a seeing model and test a cause by changing illumination. | Item 1, 17 minutes, 10 points. | 115 | 4-LS1-2 | Use a model to test information, processing and different responses. | Item 2, 17 minutes, 10 points. Reserved records, invented for practice: two animals smell the same odor.
