- **E84.** [s05-X84-ideas] "Invented for practice. A riverside workshop needs a dry floor. Clay, sand and cardboard are available. Draw three different possible dry-floor solutions." Key: Three feasible distinct causal sketches, such as wall, raised floor and diversion channel. Wrong: "Draw the same wall three times." (x). [s05-X84-limits] "Use the same record.   Shape: s05-X84-ideas: Distinct ways to keep the workshop floor dry. s05-X84-limits: One checkable criterion and one genuine resource limit.
- **E85.** [s05-X85-recommend] "Invented for practice. Matched models carry equal loads. A broad base stands under gentle and stronger motion. A narrow base stands only under gentle motion. Recommend a design using both motion records." Key: Broad base has better evidence: it stands in both motions while narrow base falls in stronger motion. Wrong: "Gentle success proves the narrow base works in every motion." (E); "Gentle success proves the narrow base works under every motion." (E). [s05-X85-challenge] "Use the same record. Describe a test that could challenge that recommendation." Key: Repeat matched trials or try another likely motion with the same load/start, allowing contrary outcomes. Wrong: "One gentle success proves every motion, so no challenge is needed." (E).
  Shape: s05-X85-recommend: Evidence from both matched conditions, bounded claim. s05-X85-challenge: Relevant controlled challenge with no guarantee.

Ideal instrument: matched buildings under repeatable motion. Nested tray on contained rubber balls partly fits: cardboard and small motions cannot represent real earthquakes/buildings. No digital or key tool. Six groups of five, four minutes each, one adult-controlled station. Equal-base building pairs differ only by braces; same nonbreakable load, too large to swallow. Mark two gentle stroke lengths using a one-centimeter ruler step; pretest below any flying-load threshold. Record whole-centimeter endpoints and standing/fallen counts, never magnitude. E85's broad/narrow-base comparison stays unseen.

Impact eye protection for everyone nearby, faces back; adult moves tray. Check latex allergies before rubber balls; substitute latex-free contained balls or records. If a motion jams or load escapes, stop; one safe reset, then announce Shake fallback S: invented repeated braced/unbraced outcomes at two motions. Shake fallback S has six matched pairs: braced models stand in all six gentler runs and five stronger runs; unbraced models stand in all six gentler runs and two stronger runs. The comparison supports reduced falling under the stronger tested motion, without proving universal protection. Keep invented rows separate from observed rows; no firsthand claim. Children remain dry observers. Cleanup uses individual approved goggle wipes/bags and adult containment, no sink queue.

## 9. Supports and extension

Read source cards aloud. Student print, including labels and tables, follows DEFAULTS.md: at most 350 words, sentences of 15 words or fewer, normal punctuation. Extend with a plausible condition reversing a comparison and evidence needed to test it.

## 10. Two rooms

One child researches four hazards with an adult, then observes both designs/motions and records both. Adult controls shaking/cleanup, both wear eye protection. Paper fallback preserves comparison. One projector shows source locators/live counts; every child has cards/log.
