- **E83.** [s05-X83-harm] "Invented for practice. The sketches show library braces and island monitoring with notices. They also show harbor alerts with inland routes and lane diversion. Explain the harm each design could reduce." Key: Braces reduce shaking damage; monitoring/notices support eruption-related protective action. Key: People or buildings protected under stated conditions, with a way to check the outcome. An uncheckable preference is an evidence gap.

- **E84.** [s05-X84-ideas] "Invented for practice. A riverside workshop needs a dry floor. Clay, sand and cardboard are available. Draw three different possible dry-floor solutions." Key: Three feasible distinct causal sketches, such as wall, raised floor and diversion channel. Repeated copies of one design are an alternatives gap. [s05-X84-limits] "Label one criterion and one constraint for comparing those solutions." Key: Floor wetness is a criterion; available clay, sand and cardboard are a constraint. Wrong: "My wall guarantees dryness in every flood." (D).
  Shape: s05-X84-ideas: Distinct ways to keep the workshop floor dry. Avoid an individual three-solution dry-floor sketch; pool oral shaking ideas.
  Shape: s05-X84-limits: One checkable criterion and one genuine resource limit. Avoid labeling criterion and constraint on a solution trio; use separate teaching strips.
- **E85.** [s05-X85-recommend] "Invented for practice. Matched models carry equal loads. A broad base stands under gentle and stronger motion. A narrow base stands only under gentle motion. Recommend a design using both motion records." Key: Broad base has better evidence: it stands in both motions while narrow base falls in stronger motion. Wrong: "Gentle success proves the narrow base works under every motion." (E). [s05-X85-challenge] "Describe a test that could challenge that recommendation." Key: Repeat matched trials or try another likely motion with the same load/start, allowing contrary outcomes. Wrong: "One gentle success proves every motion, so no challenge is needed." (E).
  Shape: s05-X85-recommend: Evidence from both matched conditions, bounded claim. Avoid selecting a winner from broad/narrow-base records; record same-base brace outcomes.
  Shape: s05-X85-challenge: Relevant controlled challenge with no guarantee. Avoid a next-test proposal following a recommendation; practice audits matched starts.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Teacher keeps question board; children keep notes/strips. Review "design-cycle chart: define, sketch, build, test, compare" (science-grade-3 u02-2 day 25). Day 84 joins design to less harm; day 85 qualifies arrows with both motion records, retaining originals.

Ideal instrument: matched buildings under repeatable motion. Nested tray on contained rubber balls partly fits: cardboard and small motions cannot represent real earthquakes/buildings. No digital or key tool. Six groups of five, four minutes each, one adult-controlled station. Same-base building pairs differ only by braces; same nonbreakable load, too large to swallow. Mark two gentle stroke lengths using a one-centimeter ruler step; pretest below any flying-load threshold. Record whole-centimeter endpoints and standing/fallen counts, never magnitude. E85's broad/narrow-base comparison stays unseen.

Impact eye protection for everyone nearby, faces back; adult moves tray. Check latex allergies before rubber balls; substitute latex-free contained balls or records. If a motion jams or load escapes, stop; one safe reset, then announce Shake fallback S: invented repeated braced/unbraced outcomes at two motions. Shake fallback S has six matched pairs: braced models stand in all six gentler runs and five stronger runs; unbraced models stand in all six gentler runs and two stronger runs. The comparison supports reduced falling under the stronger tested motion, without proving universal protection. Keep invented rows separate from observed rows; no firsthand claim. Children remain dry observers. Cleanup uses individual approved goggle wipes/bags and adult containment, no sink queue.

No-kit room: original source cards, strips and Shake fallback S; name missing practical evidence.
