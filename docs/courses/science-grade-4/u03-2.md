# Section 3.2: Testing a wave model

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 4. Unit 3, Waves and messages. "What do waves carry, and how can a pattern carry a message?" Anchor: a cork on a tub of water bobs up and down as waves pass, but goes nowhere; and the class cannot shout a message across the playing field (days 42 to 56): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 45 to 47.** Days by kind: fluency 1 (47), application 2 (45, 46). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 2 of 4 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Defaults: [DEFAULTS.md](DEFAULTS.md). Marker blocks preserve inherited data, not printable copy. Operational directions below replace conflicting inherited directions. No earlier key or specialist read is assumed.

Everyone returns individual paper evidence. Formative records use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Assessment points occupy a separate record. Many means more than ten of thirty. Hear a named rotating six, at most 45 seconds each within five minutes. Partners each have one 45-second turn. A scribe records individual reasoning without supplying it. Announce and print supplied cases as invented for practice. Keep actual observations, unclear readings and invented fallback rows separate.
