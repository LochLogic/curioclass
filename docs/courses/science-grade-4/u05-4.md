- **E89.** [s05-X89-time] "Invented for practice. Island homes occupy both volcano slopes; sensors cover only the north. Tsunami arrival: twelve minutes after the event.   Shape: s05-X89-time: Combine delay and travel, compare with arrival. s05-X89-gap: Monitoring coverage, not notice reach.
- **E90.** [s05-X90] "Invented for practice. A river settlement lies beside an offshore trench. Larger flow overtops its wall; raised models stay dry. Raising needs materials and the route needs testing; neither stops an event or guarantees protection. Other justified combinations count. Wrong: "Use only the wall; it guarantees protection despite overtopping." (D).
  Shape: s05-X90: two-sentence combination argument with tradeoff.
- **E91.** [s05-X91-map] "Invented for practice. An ocean-island map shows earthquakes along a ridge and one off-belt volcano. A protective model succeeds at low flow but fails at increased flow. Explain the mapped belt pattern, including its exception." Key: Earthquakes align with the ocean ridge; one volcano is off-belt. This is association, not a cause. Wrong: "The ocean floor is flat." (A). [s05-X91-design] "Use the same record. Explain what the higher-flow failure means for the protection claim." Key: Low-flow success supports only bounded protection; increased-flow failure rejects protection in every flow. Wrong: "The wall protects in every flow." (D); "One success proves all conditions." (E).
  Shape: s05-X91-map: Ridge/event association and isolated volcano exception. s05-X91-design: Use changed-condition counterevidence to limit a claim.
