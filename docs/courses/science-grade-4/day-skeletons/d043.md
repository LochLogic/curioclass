## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Compare repeating waves of different heights and spacings and observe cork motion.
- **Note:** Fifteen Older-row response minutes: five ruler vibration-produces-sound, five paper receiving-drum sound-causes-vibration, five signal retrieval. Teach missing directions as new. Twenty wave comparison, five E43 model explanation, five cleanup. Ruler alone does not demonstrate sound causing vibration.
