## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Combine two unfamiliar sources to explain a resource, its use technology and environmental consequences.
- **Note:** Every part of 4-ESS3-1: natural origin, human energy need, renewable or nonrenewable supply, technology that extracts or uses it, environmental effects and how a technology improvement changes one effect while others remain.
