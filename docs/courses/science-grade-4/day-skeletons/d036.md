## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Observe unfamiliar energy-transfer stations and trace an unfamiliar converting device beside previously collected design evidence.
- **Note:** Every listed code in full. New sound, light, heat and current cases: each student records where energy starts and goes. Explain motion producing current and its useful output from supplied evidence. Independently trace starting and ending energy forms and name the converting part in the unseen device. Use days 25 to 29 individual design, test and refinement records once for the actual performance, not a new build or interview. Six groups of five rotate four 5-minute observation stations and two 5-minute independent device/portfolio tasks: 3-minute launch, 30-minute rotations, 7-minute written synthesis, 5-minute cleanup. Adult manages the pretested live sunlight and circuit stations; use the dated sunlight contingency with its limitation and a live retry. No numerical energy. Response day 38. Use operational Day-block timing. Teach new terms explicitly. Fallback records are invented for practice; missing live or personal performance remains not observed.
