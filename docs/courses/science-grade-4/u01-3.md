## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** 4-PS3-3: "Ask questions and predict outcomes" about collision energy. Day 9 also uses 4-PS3-1's evidence explanation. Practices: asking questions and predicting; ideas: PS3.A/B/C; concept: energy and matter. Each day connects its records to where motion energy passes.

**Adult explanation.** Energy passing at contact changes both objects' motion. Sound and slight warming are additional destinations. Force arrows start on the object receiving a push; energy arrows connect starting energy to destinations. No force-size or numerical energy comparisons. Sound is a same-place qualitative report, not an energy meter. No temperature instrument or measured warming here.

**Review.** Grade 3 force arrow, balanced, unbalanced and cancel return day 7. Review force: "A push or a pull on one object" (science-grade-3 u01-1 day 1). Claim, evidence and reason and observation/inference are review. The new distinction is between force arrows and energy-destination arrows.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

**Carried.** Teacher holds question board/model; children separate predictions and observations. Day 7 defines contact: "Objects touch during a collision," and destination: "Where transferred energy goes." Energy arrows connect starting energy to destinations. Day 8 adds both motions; day 9 sound; day 10 warming from the account, labeled by source. Review "revise the record in a second color, keeping the original observations and initials" from science-grade-3 u01-2 day 8. Nothing withdrawn.

**Apparatus.** Days 7 to 9: one contained floor lane, cars, smooth block, existing room-wide eye protection and cleaning supplies as in section 1.1. Two handlers and four observers from the rotating six; all thirty record. Everyone present wears existing fitted impact eye protection, including the adult; use C if this is unavailable. Six two-minute trials take twelve minutes days 8/9. Day 7 has one three-minute trial plus two-minute setup. Visibly distinguish approach speeds; taped push lines alone cannot prove speed. Five-minute cleanup follows section 1.1: two minutes counting and clearing, three cleaning all eye protection at six supplied places. Everyone keeps protection on until the lane is packed. Stop for containment/protection failure.

**Fallback.** Keep failed rows. Invented Car record C: slower car slows, block moves a little, quiet contact, three trials; faster car slows, block moves farther, louder contact, three trials. Print and say invented for practice. Indistinguishable sound is unclear, never invented listening evidence; use C separately and retry later. Hearing access uses a named observer report. No numerical sound or temperature instrument is used; motion words and listening categories have no numeric step. An unreadable motion stays unclear; repeat once within the trial block or use C separately.

**Instruments.** Ideal: real motion and fixed-place listening; lane fits. No warming measurement or deliberate heating. Day 10 ideal: sourced account beside observations.
