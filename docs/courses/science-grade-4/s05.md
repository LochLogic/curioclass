## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Where do earthquakes and volcanoes happen, and how can people there stay safer?"

Field 10 fixes the records and their different time spans. The printed volcano key reads "erupted or possibly active within the past 10,000 years", so the dots are not read as 2025 eruptions.
Day 91: write what evidence supports belts rather than an even scatter, describing associations with mountains, trenches and continental edges without claiming a mechanism. Explain how tested designs and warnings reduce harm, using map tables and two-condition records.
Model reading a key on day 78 and recording observations before claims on day 79. Step back during design choices; ask which evidence would change a recommendation.
