<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 4. Unit 2, Energy on the move. "How does energy get from place to place, and where does the energy we use come from?" Anchor: a hand-crank flashlight that lights when the handle turns, with no battery to change (days 16 to 38): see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 30 to 35.** Days by kind: understanding 5 (30, 31, 32, 33, 35), application 1 (34). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 4 of 5 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.

## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **4-ESS3-1** (days 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35): Gather and combine information to show that energy and fuels come from natural resources and that using them affects the environment.
- **4-PS3-2** (days 35): Observe to gather evidence that sound, light, heat and electric current carry energy from one place to another.
- **4-PS3-4** (days 35): Use science ideas to design, test and improve a device that turns one form of energy into another.
- skill s4-energy-resources (grade 4)
- skill s4-energy-transfer (grade 4)
- skill s4-energy-device (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> On day 35, students explain the crank using transfer observations, circuit records and device tests, then connect useful energy to natural resources and environmental effects using source notes.
> Demonstrate safe handling and recording, then let children predict paths, choose comparisons and challenge explanations. Model combining sources on day 31; step back for the independent resource critique on day 34.
>
> Group the question board by paths, control and origins. Days 16 to 19 ask, "Can energy reach something without the objects touching?" Observations raise "How does a wire carry energy?", answered on days 20 to 23. "Can we control a useful signal?" drives design on days 24 to 29. "Where did our useful energy come from?" drives days 30 to 34. Day 35 attaches evidence to answered questions and retains unresolved ones. Transfer uses new station arrangements, a pull-cord generator and a different resource-use case.
> Carried: retain unit 1 cause-and-effect and energy arrows on day 16. Revise on day 19 from sound, sunlight and hand-warming observations; day 23 adds the closed current path from circuit evidence. Begin the design cycle on day 24; day 27 failure records guide refinement, revised after day 28 retests. Day 34 adds resource-to-use and environmental-effect branches from combined sources. Day 35 forms consensus; individuals apply these models in the end task. Retain the explanation frame; force arrows remain distinct from energy paths.
<!-- /skel -->

**Share.** 4-ESS3-1: "Obtain and combine information" about natural origins, uses and environmental effects. Source evaluation/communication, ESS3.A and cause/effect are inseparable: cite a resource, useful energy and causal effect. Day 35 also revisits "Make observations to provide evidence" (4-PS3-2) and "Apply scientific ideas to design, test, and refine" (4-PS3-4).

**Adult explanation.** Replenishment is different from impact and from constant local availability. Electricity/batteries are manufactured carriers/stores, not natural origins. Particle filters reduce particles, not every emission or extraction effect. Nuclear fuel is nonrenewable, with waste requiring management; no atomic model. Review grade 3 u05-2 changed condition/needs reasoning and u06-2 source wording. No family employment, energy bills, illness or disaster disclosures; evidence evaluates claims without praising/blaming jobs. Connections: supported source notes W.4.8 day 31; ten-share graph day 34, without percentages or numbers above 1,000.
