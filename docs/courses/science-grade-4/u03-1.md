## 1. Standards and practices


4-PS4-1, "Develop a model": initial observations day 42, comparison day 43, qualitative height and spacing day 44. Developing models joins PS4.A with patterns on the visible cork phenomenon. Information codes stay an unsolved problem.

Teacher explanation: review energy moving between places and energy arrows from grade 4 days 7 to 10 and 20. Apply those familiar meanings to waves, without assuming mastery. A cork may drift; distinguish local movement from a crest crossing the tub. Teach peak, trough, rest position, amplitude and wavelength on day 42 before exits. Amplitude is displacement from rest; wavelength is spacing between matching peaks. No numerical wave quantities, formal geometry, wave-kind names or light-wave model.

**E42.** Reserved independent paper. Print s03-X42: "Invented for practice. Three rope frames show a spot beside one fixed mark. Crests advance right. Draw how the spot and pattern move. Add one question your drawing cannot answer."

Key: Show local up/down spot motion at two moments and a rightward pattern arrow. Ask an investigable question, such as whether larger hand motion changes spot movement.

Wrong: The marked material travels to the far end with a crest. (A).
Shape: construct a two-moment rope model and question. Practice uses a single still-life tub sketch and oral notices; no rope sequence or temporal-model/question card.


**E43.** Reserved independent paper. Print s03-X43: "Invented for practice. Water frames A and B show floats beside fixed markers. B has taller, farther-apart peaks and greater local float movement. Draw both wave patterns. Explain the float's movement using the frames."

Key: B has greater amplitude and wavelength than A. Its float moves farther up/down locally. Pattern and energy pass; the float stays near its marker.

Wrong: The marked material travels to the far end with a crest. (A); Wider peak spacing always makes waves taller. (B).
Shape: paired pattern drawings with an evidence-linked motion account. Practice uses oral feature comparisons and an observation table, never paired explanatory drawings.


**E44.** Reserved independent paper. Print s03-X44: "Invented for practice. Rope observations show equal heights, different spacing, and a locally bobbing tag. A faulty model makes the wider pattern taller and carries its tag onward. Revise this model using the observations. Show wave spacing, height, and the tag's motion."

Key: Keep equal amplitude from rest and different peak spacing. Show local tag motion separately from onward pattern and energy.

Wrong: Wider peak spacing always makes waves taller. (B); The marked material travels to the far end with a crest. (A).
Shape: repair a faulty comparative model combining features and tagged motion. Practice labels correct single-feature strips; no comparative faulty-model repair.
