## 1. Standards and practices

4-PS4-1, "Develop a model": initial observations day 42, comparison day 43, qualitative height and spacing day 44. Developing models joins PS4.A with patterns on the visible cork phenomenon. Information codes stay an unsolved problem.

Teacher explanation: review transfer, energy passes from one object to another, from u01-2 day 5. Retrieve energy arrows from u01-3 days 7 to 10 and u02-2 day 20. Apply those familiar meanings to waves, without assuming mastery. A cork may drift; distinguish local movement from a crest crossing the tub. Day 42 teaches these words before exits. Wave: a repeating disturbance with a traveling pattern. Rest position: where the material lies before a disturbance. Peak: the highest part. Trough: the lowest part. Local motion: movement near an original place. Amplitude is displacement from rest; wavelength is spacing between matching peaks. No numerical wave quantities, formal geometry, wave-kind names or light-wave model.

**Connection.** 4-PS3-2, day 42: retrieve energy-transfer arrows from u02-2 while interpreting the cork observation.

## 3. Checks, written first

**End-of-unit assessment ownership:** preparation for item 1; section 3.2 collects its reserved record. No points here. All responses are individual paper.

**E42.** Reserved independent paper. Print s03-X42: "Invented for practice. Use these three rope frames. Draw a two-moment model with a question label about unexplained motion."

Teacher stimulus: same floor mark in all frames; the spot is at rest, above rest, then below. Peaks occupy successively rightward positions. No motion arrows or explanatory captions.

Key: Show local spot motion at two moments and a separate onward pattern arrow. A question label names a testable uncertainty, such as whether larger hand motion changes local spot movement. Accept tentative mechanisms; do not score initial accuracy.

Wrong: The marked material travels to the far end with a crest. (A).
Shape: construct one two-moment rope model with an unanswered question label. Practice uses a still tub sketch and a separate cork question, not this integrated rope model.

**E43.** Reserved independent paper. Print s03-X43: "Invented for practice. Use water records A and B. Draw both wave patterns with evidence labels about the float's movement."

Teacher stimulus: equal-size windows and rest lines; B has taller, wider-spaced peaks than A. Three uncaptioned positions per float show larger local movement in B beside fixed markers. Include no comparative labels.

Key: B has greater amplitude and wavelength than A. Its float moves farther up/down locally. Pattern and energy pass; the float stays near its marker.

Wrong: The marked material travels to the far end with a crest. (A); Wider peak spacing always makes waves taller. (B).
Shape: paired pattern drawings with an evidence-linked motion account. Practice uses oral feature comparisons and an observation table, never paired explanatory drawings.

**E44.** Reserved independent paper. Print s03-X44: "Invented for practice. Revise the faulty model using the rope observations about height, spacing, and tag motion."

Teacher stimulus: observations have equal rest-to-peak heights, different peak spacing and repeated tag positions near one mark. The separate faulty model enlarges the wider pattern and sends its tag onward. Do not caption the observations with conclusions.

Key: Keep equal amplitude from rest and different peak spacing. Show local tag motion separately from onward pattern and energy.

Wrong: Wider peak spacing always makes waves taller. (B); The marked material travels to the far end with a crest. (A).
Shape: repair a faulty comparative model combining features and tagged motion. Practice labels correct single-feature strips; no comparative faulty-model repair.

**Readiness 1 and 2 hand-back.** Cold keys: stopping ruler vibration stops its sound; a separately supported receiving surface moves during sound and rests during silence. Keep the latter a supplied record. Unrelated vibration misses get a component note, not an invented error letter.
Shape: causal explanation from sounding/stopped and sounding/silent records. Day 43 repairs returned cases without a new scored check; no advance practice.
