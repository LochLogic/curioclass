## 1. Standards and practices

**4-ESS1-1, this section's share.** "Identify evidence" in rock layers and fossils to explain how a landscape has changed. Day 67 builds an undisturbed layered model and reads fossil environments (land, then sea). Day 68 bends and offsets the model and traces a real exposure. Day 69 compares two accounts of one canyon with a cross-cutting channel. Day 70 joins all of it in one consensus explanation.

**4-ESS2-1, day 70.** "Make observations and/or measurements" returns as the class joins its tray measurements to the layer story and revises days 65 and 66 practice. No new measuring.

Limits that bite: relative order only, never a number of years; no named formations or rock-formation mechanisms to memorize; lower is older only in undisturbed layers; clay is an analogue, not rock; marine means sea-living and tells conditions, not an exact age. Separate finished models cannot show which change came first. The folded-rock photograph is a real exposure that is not the canyon and has no original-top marker. The canyon cards are adapted from a park service page. No child gives a personal home or disaster story.

Connections: grade 3 fossils (retrieved day 67); nature of science, past and present processes assumed consistent (day 69, from the frame); two-column source notes (day 30) as the ELA research unit's note routine overlaps; consensus (day 13).

Review in the ledger's words: "fossil, layer, older, younger, undisturbed" (grade 3), "inferred" (day 66), "lake" (grade 2), "evidence pointing" in 45-second paired turns (day 61). New: "marine: sea-living" (day 67); "bend, offset, deformation: changed shape or position" (day 68).

Teacher page must explain: a layer must exist before it is bent or cut; a channel crossing a displaced layer is later than the displacement; matching color alone proves nothing, but matching order plus fossils supports a tentative link; the age of Earth is great and stated plainly, but no record here gives a number. No child is asked for a personal belief about it, and family belief is not weighed as evidence.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** item 2 (day 72, 12 points), taught here. Day 69's history case is unscored practice with written feedback.

All reserved cases are printed "invented for practice". Each exit is independent paper, five minutes, read neutrally. One formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. If many (more than ten of thirty) miss an exit, the next day's Return carries one repair.

- **E67.** [s04-X67] "Invented for practice. An undisturbed exposure has marine shells below a layer without useful fossils. Rooted land plants are above that middle layer. Draw an evidence-labeled history model, including its uncertainty." Key: sea, then uncertain middle conditions, then land; shell layer oldest, rooted-plant layer youngest; the middle environment is unknown. Wrong: "All layers were land because upper roots prove every environment." (x: one layer's evidence stretched over all; no register letter).
- **E68.** [s04-X68] "Invented for practice. An intact side has J below K below L. Diagram U traces those layers through an overturned fold. Diagram V shifts them without overturning. Evaluate the lowest-is-oldest claim for both." Key: J is oldest in both; in U the overturned limb puts J above L; in V the order J, K, L holds; position alone fails after overturning. Wrong: "Lowest always means oldest, even after overturning." (F).
- **E69.** [s04-X69] "Invented for practice. A break shifts layers. An unshifted channel cuts across both displaced sides. Account A places cutting before the break; B places it afterward. Choose the better-supported history and give a timing limit." Key: B; the unshifted cut across already shifted layers; exact duration is not given. Wrong: "The channel must always have occupied its present position." (H).
- **E70.** [s04-X70] "Invented for practice. An abandoned channel lies above a stream carrying fresh sediment. Matching layers face across the canyon. Compare an unchanged-stream-position claim with continued cutting." Key: the abandoned upper channel and present lower carrying favor continued cutting; matching layers support missing material; exact duration and all causes stay uncertain. Wrong: "The river always occupied exactly its present position." (H).

Rule 4 holds: the shell-and-root exposure, J-K-L folds, break-and-channel accounts and abandoned channel appear on no teaching card. Teaching uses buttons with no claim (day 67), moderate bends (day 68) and a lake-below-roots record (day 69).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Carried: the pattern chart gains fossil order (day 67); layer matching is revised after deformation (day 68); the cause-and-effect arrows, data graph, scale explanation and stability-and-change model are joined with the pattern chart in a five-part consensus on day 70, each with its limit. Actual measurements stay separate from invented records. Original practice stays visible beside revisions.

- **Key moment 1, days 67 and 68: a layered stack, built, cut, bent and offset.** Ideal instrument: real rock layers or a cut core. Spec: modeling clay in three bands with large buttons, a blunt slicer and a tray partly fits; clay is an analogue and its bends cannot tell which change came first. No tool used. Gaps: TOOL-ASKS.md.
- **Key moment 2, day 68: a real folded exposure.** Ideal instrument: a field visit. Spec: the public-domain photograph, no caption shown, with the invented fold drawing as the labeled fallback; no visit.
- **Key moment 3, day 70: five joined representations.** Ideal instrument: a shared display the class edits. Spec: paper display fits; no tool.

Shared equipment fits. Six groups of five, one stack and one blunt slicer each; every child has a 30-second handling turn inside the group's five minutes (5 × 30 seconds = 150 seconds). Tokens are counted out and back. Clean-up: one sink cannot wash thirty children in two minutes, so washing runs in six rounds of five, about 50 seconds each, begun in the last ten minutes of work, and the five Cleanup minutes finish washing, count tokens and wipe tables. With no sink, children wipe hands with damp cloths and the adult washes the tools. Nothing is tasted. Day 69 is paper only.

A room with nothing: draw the layered stack and the bend on paper, said aloud to be invented; no firsthand credit for the clay turn.
