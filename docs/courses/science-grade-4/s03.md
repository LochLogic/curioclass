## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What do waves carry, and how can a pattern carry a message?"
Day 42: watch gentle repeating waves pass a cork beside a fixed marker. Draw what moves and what travels. Introduce the unsolved playing-field message problem without shouting; collect questions before explanations.
Day 54: explain the cork using observation records and revised wave drawings, then explain how tested signal patterns carry a message. Attach evidence to answered board questions.
Model safe observation and recording, not the explanation. Ask which evidence would change a drawing. Model reading a source on day 48; let teams choose codes and defend comparisons.
