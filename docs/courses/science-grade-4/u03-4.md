## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Carried: individual proposals, keys, logs, comparison notes, revised portfolios and question board. Review grade 3 u02-2 design-cycle chart; second-color revision keeps observations/initials, review grade 3 u01-2 day 8. Consensus revisits original cork and field-message problem without light-wave model.

Ideal instrument: complete sent/received logs and comparable elapsed-time readings. One measured trial station partly fits: six teams share a marked route/kit, so only named performers handle it. Each three-minute visit: two five-message runs, at most thirty seconds each, plus two minutes checking/recording/reset. Six visits take eighteen of twenty minutes; two contingency minutes allow one failed reading repeated, both retained. Roles sender, receiver, timer, checker, recorder; swap sender/receiver between systems. Everyone keeps results; role log names actual performance. Do not claim every child independently sent every message. Teach condition, the setting used for a test, before testing. Whole-second timer: measure from first signal to final agreed message boundary; round to whole seconds, never decimals. Correctness is a whole-message count. Obscured/borderline timing is unclear, not pass. No countdown or child speed ranking.

Pretest route/kit. No shouting, rapid flashing, lasers, mirrors or sun viewing. Adult services batteries; aim at cards. Gentle taps/conversation only, stop for discomfort. Keep positions and signal rules fixed. Use sender-only sequence cards; receivers commit before seeing the sent record. Match five-message length and message frequencies across systems and conditions; permute orders privately. Never announce an order. Waiting teams work silently during quiet trials. Two fixed adult-approved speakers supply gentle conversation in the second condition; keep their positions and volume unchanged between systems. Missed signals stay misses, never louder output. Timer failure: repeat once in contingency, then fallback T, invented for practice: quiet card five correct/24 seconds, tap five/26; conversation card five/25, tap three/28. Analyze separately, no firsthand-performance credit. Paper alone cannot establish sound performance. Tools: None, no key tool.
