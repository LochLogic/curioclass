## 5. What is carried, and instruments



Carried: individual proposals, keys, logs, comparison notes, revised portfolios and question board. Review grade 3 u02-2 design-cycle chart; second-color revision keeps observations/initials, review grade 3 u01-2 day 8. Consensus revisits original cork and field-message problem without light-wave model.

One measured trial station, six teams of five, shared marked route/kit. Each three-minute visit: two five-message runs, at most thirty seconds each, plus two minutes checking/recording/reset. Six visits take eighteen of twenty minutes; two contingency minutes allow one failed reading repeated, both retained. Roles sender, receiver, timer, checker, recorder; swap sender/receiver between systems. Everyone keeps results; role log names actual performance. Do not claim every child independently sent every message. Whole-second timer: round elapsed time to whole seconds, never decimals. Correctness is a whole-message count. Obscured/borderline timing is unclear, not pass. No countdown or child speed ranking.

Pretest route/kit. No shouting, rapid flashing, lasers, mirrors or sun viewing. Adult services batteries; aim at cards. Gentle taps/conversation only, stop for discomfort. Same positions, sequence/rules both days. Missed signals stay misses, never louder output. Timer failure: repeat once in contingency, then fallback T, invented for practice: quiet card five correct/24 seconds, tap five/26; conversation card five/25, tap three/28. Analyze separately, no firsthand-performance credit. Paper alone cannot establish sound performance. Tools: None, no key tool.
