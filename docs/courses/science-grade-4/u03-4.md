# Section 3.4: Comparing tested message systems

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 4. Unit 3, Waves and messages. "What do waves carry, and how can a pattern carry a message?" Anchor: a cork on a tub of water bobs up and down as waves pass, but goes nowhere; and the class cannot shout a message across the playing field (days 42 to 56): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 51 to 56.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (54), application 3 (51, 52, 53), check 1 (55), respond 1 (56). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 4 of 4 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.
