**4-PS4-2: "Develop a model".** Carry illumination, reflection and an incoming eye path together. Modeling joins PS4.B and cause and effect on observable box conditions. Day 94 ideas remain tentative; day 95 establishes source-object-eye links; day 96 adds mirrors. Explain opaque beside blocks light before testing it.

Review grade 1 u05-2 day 43 "lets light through, lets some through, blocks light" and day 44 "turns the light", in those words. Day 18 light-material teaching is written; retrieve those categories without assuming mastery.

Teacher distinguishes illuminating an object from viewing its image in a mirror. Eyes receive light; dull objects do not supply it. A dim yard still has light. No retina, cells or reflected colors. Draw direction arrows before naming geometric rays. Day 98 uses SL.4.5 to support a visual explanation.
