- **4-PS4-2:** Model how we see an object when light bounces off it and enters the eye. Limit: Leaves out which colors are reflected and seen, how eye cells work, and how the retina works.
- **4-LS1-2:** Use a model to show how animals take in information through their senses, process it in the brain, and react in different ways. - **4-LS1-1:** Argue that the inside and outside parts of plants and animals do jobs that help them survive, grow, behave and reproduce. Limit: Only parts large enough to see without a microscope.
