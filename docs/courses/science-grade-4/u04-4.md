## 1. Standards and practices

**4-ESS2-1, days 71 and 73.** "Make observations and/or measurements": on day 71 each child observes and measures a new one-variable slope comparison in whole centimeters, then explains it and four separate process records (ice, wind, an animal, rain with plants). Day 73 repairs by the recorded gap.

**4-ESS1-1, days 72 and 73.** "Identify evidence": on day 72 each child explains the relative history of a new layered valley from fossil order, matching layers, bending and displacement, and a later river cut, then rejects "all at once". Day 73 repairs by the recorded gap.

Limits that bite: only cases reserved from teaching; one process per record; relative order and no number of years; a tray gives no rate for a solid-rock canyon; the day 72 records do not fix a duration or the unique cause of the break. No reteaching on days 71 and 72, no practical retry on day 73, and no change to an original score after repair.

Connections: item 1 parts and item 2 sit on the unit plan; day 73 uses the unit's whole error register. Days 71 and 72 follow the standing rule that work scored on a day was made on an earlier day or is made unaided on that day without feedback; the day 65 to 70 practice is the unscored feedback loop.

Review in the ledger's words: "evidence" and "inferred" (day 66, day 73 Feedback), "ruler zero and end minus start" (day 3), "weathering" and "erosion" (days 58 and 66), "paired evidence pointing" (day 61). Nothing is new. Routines: independent evidence then delayed component feedback; an independent check against the earlier model before collection (day 72); four simultaneous paper repair groups with one assigned move (day 73).

Teacher page must explain the three records: assessment points (0, 1, 2) on assessment-record.md only; formative notes on record.md (shown, shown with help, not yet seen); original practical record kept for day 75. Blank means absent or not observed. Access help is noted apart from content evidence.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** item 1 (day 71, 16 points) and item 2 (day 72, 12 points), both on the unit plan with keys and sample answers. The components below show only how each stem maps to a two-point component.

All reserved cases are printed "invented for practice" and read aloud once, neutrally. Every child's paper is collected before any feedback.

- **Day 71, Part 1 (8 points).** [s04-E1-measure] observe the two new sand slopes in the group turn; record each front movement in whole centimeters and the visible carrying evidence. [s04-E1-cause] explain the proposed slope effect from all valid repeats, with controls and one limitation. Wrong: "I wrote expected readings without observing" (x); "One trial proves slope caused it even if water changed" (C). A ruler or unit slip is a measurement gap, not C.
- **Day 71, Part 2 (8 points).** [s04-E1-ice] matched stones with equal cracks widen equally whether frozen or not; does that isolate freezing? Key: no, the control changed too. Wrong: "Neither weathered because pieces did not travel" (A). [s04-E1-wind] marked grains move into a new pile with unchanged sizes; which process is established? Key: carrying, not breakage. Wrong: "Moving grains prove new rock breakage" (x). [s04-E1-animal] repeated pictures show an animal bringing soil onto a bare marked surface while the excluded patch is unchanged; is a plant required? Key: no. Wrong: "Only plants can change land" (D). [s04-E1-rain] matched plots, rain brings grooves and upright seedlings to one, the excluded plot has no new grooves and wilting seedlings that recover when watered; explain two effects. Key: rain reshaped soil and affects plant life. Wrong: "Rain changes grooves but cannot affect which plants live" (E).
- **Day 72 (12 points, six 2-point components).** [s04-E2-order] model the history of layers P, Q, R from bottom to top; [s04-E2-match] judge near and far matches including a color-only distractor; [s04-E2-deform] infer that all layers existed before bending and displacement; [s04-E2-cut] order a notch after displaced layers; [s04-E2-limit] reject "everything appeared together" with an evidence limit. Wrong: "All layers formed together in the same environment" (x); "Matching colors prove all exposures are the same" (G); "The break happened before any layer existed" (x); "Today's river always occupied this notch" (H); "Every feature appeared at once because it is visible now" (x).
- **Day 73 fresh case, unscored.** [s04-P73-fresh] "Invented for practice. An old level footpath cuts across tilted bands. Their tilted ends stop at its level base. Explain one event order and one limit." Key: the bands tilted before the path cut their ends; exact duration is unknown. Wrong: "The path proves an exact age." (x).

Groups never exceed four; each child gets only the move for their recorded gap, or its written paired repair. ## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Carried: the consensus model and its five representations are used unaided on day 72 only as a covered reference for the independent evidence check; every child's original work, readings and scores stay visible beside any revision. Day 71's practical record is kept unchanged for day 75.

- **Key moment 1, day 71: a new matched pair of slopes measured by each child.** Ideal instrument: a stream table and a ruler at the sediment front. Spec: two household trays with new liners, damp sand, equal cupfuls and a ruler fits; the ruler's step is 1 centimeter, so a smaller movement is recorded as no detected movement and expected numbers are never entered. Gaps: TOOL-ASKS.md.
- **Key moment 2, day 72: a new layered valley.** Ideal instrument: a real exposure. Spec: the invented valley drawing with printed side records partly fits; it is a drawing, not an exposure, said aloud. Paper only, no tool.

Shared equipment fits: six groups of five, four minutes each (45 seconds pour, five 30-second readings, 45 seconds reset), 24 minutes in all on one shared tray pair with fresh liners and sand each group; adults pour and reset, children read at the dry edge. Every group's distances are posted without comment. If a reading fails, one retry inside the slot, then unresolved; if the apparatus fails, the sealed fallback is used separately and never pooled, with no firsthand credit. Clean-up (5 minutes): the adult contains solids and washes; dry children clean their goggles at once with the supplied supplies; papers are filed. Solids settle in the bucket and go outdoors, never down a sink.

A room with nothing: the sealed fallback records carry the reasoning items; the practical part is marked "not run" and scores no points.

## 6. Days

## Day 71

- **3 min. Launch.** Independent work, no feedback. Name the single changed condition, slope; show equal water, sand, moisture, patch size and pouring position. Read directions neutrally.
- **24 min. Observation turns.** Six groups of five, four minutes each. Waiting children annotate the four invented process records on Side 2 and do not discuss answers.
- **13 min. Independent explanation.** Four minutes for the cause explanation from all resolved repeats, nine for the four records. Neutral rereading only; collect every paper before feedback.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** As above.
Tool: None. Evidence: items 1.1 to 1.2 on assessment-record.md; original practical record kept. Marking after class: 90 minutes for thirty children. Feedback returns day 73.

## Day 72

- **3 min. Launch.** Reveal the new valley; say it is invented; read directions neutrally. Labels, drawings and dictation allowed.
- **32 min. History explanation.** Ten, seven, five, five and five minutes on the five stems. No peer discussion, no prompts.
- **7 min. Evidence check.** The earlier class model is shown without this valley's answers; each child checks their own evidence links and may add annotations beside the original. No teacher evaluation.
- **3 min. Collection.** Collect every page and clear desks.
Tool: None. Evidence: item 2 on assessment-record.md. Marking after class: 60 minutes. Feedback returns day 73.

## Day 73

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read each child's written component notes from days 71 and 72. Day 70's papers with their written feedback are returned here too. Each child names their assigned gap; no score or reading changes.
- **10 min. Targeted repair.** Four groups work at once, named by color, never by error, with the move on each child's own card; two minutes a visit and two for transitions: breakage and carrying; controls and land and life; layer order; historical evidence. Measurement gaps use the ruler card. Each child does only their assigned row.
- **20 min. Revision.** Ten minutes with day 71's paper and ten with day 72's; each child revises one feedback link, originals visible; no practical retry.
- **5 min. Explain change.** Partners at once, 45 seconds each; the rest of the five minutes for a written evidence note. No serial interviews.
- **5 min. Fresh case.** E73 on a half slip, unscored; collect all papers.
Tool: None. Evidence: assigned repair rows, revision, E73. Goes to day 74 Launch with written feedback and no added repair minutes.

**Shared shape.** Practice uses s04-P73-revision: Use days 71 and 72 feedback to revise one named evidence link; s04-P73-change: Identify evidence that changed your explanation; s04-P73-fresh: An old level footpath cuts across tilted bands; s04-P73-rock: A fixed boulder develops a new split; s04-P73-land: Use your assigned day 64 record; s04-P73-layer: Use your original day 68 model; s04-P73-history: Use day 69's hill record; s04-P73-ruler: A patch moves from ruler mark 2 to mark 8 centimeters. The independent exit is E73, a fresh case on a half slip that no repair row uses. The exit case is invented and appears nowhere in practice or the preceding day's shared teaching pieces. Keep its case concealed until Exit.

## 7. Talk and writing

Planned questions: none on days 71 and 72 (independent); day 73, "What evidence changed your explanation?" Public talk hears nothing on days 71 and 72; on day 73 partners speak 45 seconds each, all at once, and anyone may pass.

Written task, day 73: the revision of one feedback link from each assessment. Judged with three look-fors: the original stays visible; the revised link names its evidence; a limit is stated where the record is silent.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 71 returns no new practice (the check samples earlier days); day 72 uses the earlier revised class model for the check only; day 73 returns ruler reading, evidence and inferred, weathering and erosion, day 64 process records, day 67 and 68 layer order and day 69 history record by assigned repair. No fluency target and no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: the words of the unit, with pictures; frame "The observation ___ supports ___ because ___." Access: read prompts aloud once, enlarged print or tactile diagrams, a scribe or an oral answer with each child's own measuring and evidence decisions, a dry reading position at the ruler. Check accommodations: dictated answers count; access help is recorded apart from content. Reading load: the stems are read aloud neutrally; no passage is read alone beyond the stems. Extension, same standard: evaluate a second history for the valley and say what evidence would separate the two.

## 10. Two rooms

Teaching one child: the adult pours and resets; the child reads both distances alone, then answers on paper; the adult records the child's own words only. Last day of the unit: go over the missed items together on day 73's rows. One projector: show only directions on days 71 and 72; the valley drawing is printed for each child, and the fresh-case drawing is shown on day 73.
