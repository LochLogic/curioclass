## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** 4-PS3-2: "Make observations to provide evidence" of transfer by sound, light and heat; current follows in section 2.2. Investigation uses PS3.A/B with energy and matter: join a source, recipient and observable change.

**Adult explanation.** Sound carries energy across an air gap; shared supports offer another path. Sunlight warms surfaces. Contact transfers energy from warmer to cooler objects. Temperature is not measured energy. No particles, numerical energy or causal vibration lesson; vibration belongs to unit 3. Review observation/inference from grade 3 u05-3 day 94. Review written unit 1 u01-2 day 5 transfer and u01-3 day 7 destinations/arrows. Connections: evidence reasoning day 16, nearest-mark thermometer reading day 18.

- **E16, day 16, 4-PS3-2.** Print: "Invented for practice. A sealed signal badge flashes when its tab slides. Report B claims flashing continues afterward, without observations. Draw an initial energy-path idea using recorded effects. Include a question that tests Report B."
  Shape: initial model plus unsupported-claim test question. Reserve this response structure and case; use the different practice in field 6.
  Key: Show observed tab motion and flashing light; uncertain inner steps allowed. Ask whether flashing continues after sliding stops. Do not treat continued flashing as observed. Conversion vocabulary is not required.
  Wrong: "The lamp makes energy from nothing." (A).

- **E17, day 17, 4-PS3-2.** Print: "Invented for practice. A speaker sounds beside a sound recorder. Record A: sound detected; a rod joins the supports. Record B: separate supports; sound detected only while the speaker sounds. Explain which record better supports sound transfer across the gap."
  Shape: rank rival records with a controlled-path reason. Reserve this response structure and case; use the different practice in field 6.
  Key: B: separate supports remove the joining-rod route. Sound detected only during speaker output links that output to the receiver. A leaves solid transfer unresolved. Sound detection alone is not a measurement of energy.
  Wrong: "The joining rod proves sound crossed only the air." (G).

- **E18, day 18, 4-PS3-2.** Print: "Invented for practice. Both patches start at 21 degrees Celsius. Pale patch in sunlight: 26 degrees; dark patch in shade: 22 degrees. A screen lets some light through; its patch has no temperature reading. Draw lit, blocked and partly transmitted paths with a warming-evidence caption."
  Shape: three-path drawing with warming-evidence caption. Reserve this response structure and case; use the different practice in field 6.
  Key: Light reaches the sunlit patch; opaque shade blocks the direct path; some light passes the screen. The sunlit patch warms five degrees versus one in shade from equal starts, supporting sunlight transfer. Screened temperature is unknown.
  Wrong: "The dark patch must be warmer because it looks dark." (H).

- **E19, day 19, 4-PS3-2.** Print: "Invented for practice. A warmed clip touches a cooler metal lid. The clip cools while the lid warms. An untouched matching lid stays unchanged. A model points from lid to clip: metal supplies warmth. Revise this arrow using the comparison."
  Shape: reverse a supplied lid-to-clip arrow using comparison evidence. Reserve this response structure and case; use the different practice in field 6.
  Key: Point from warmer clip to cooler lid. Clip cooling and recipient warming, unlike the untouched lid, support this direction. Metal does not supply warmth independently.
  Wrong: "The cooler metal lid creates warmth without receiving energy." (A).

Direction-only reversal: confer using warmer-source/cooler-recipient evidence. The listed A wrong response explicitly claims energy creation; do not label every wrong arrow A.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

**Carried.** Question board: paths, control, origins. Children keep observations; teacher holds tentative model, revised day 19. Review grade 3 u01-1 question board: notices become questions; rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Review u01-2 day 8: revise the record in a second color, keeping the original observations and initials. Review written unit 1 u01-3 day 7: energy arrows connect initial motion energy to destinations. Keep force arrows separate.

**Instruments.** Ideal: visible receiver movement and temperature changes above the instrument step. Separate drum supports partly fit: they reduce, not eliminate, alternatives. Shared non-glass thermometer, 1 degree Celsius per mark, fits detectable changes only; no decimals. Adult reads each surface at the same contact position. Pretest before class. Unresolved hand-to-spoon warming is recorded, never called zero transfer. No key tool.

**Safety/fallback.** Obtain rice allergy information before day 17; substitute light paper if needed. Adult taps gently. Use live grains only with existing fitted eye protection for everyone present throughout setup, use and cleanup. If unavailable, use D; record missing live evidence. Supply six cleaning places with wipes and storage bags. No tasting. Adults read cloth temperatures; no sun viewing or mirror beams toward eyes. Pre-expose matched patches with equal starts. Failed sunlight uses dated observations or fallback S: shaded 22 to 23, sunlit 22 to 27 degrees, invented. Failed drum uses D: still in silence, moving during taps, invented. Failed spoon uses H: spoon 20 to 23, untouched 20 to 20, invented. Keep supplied and measured rows separate. If the crank fails, keep the failed observation and use K: handle turns, light appears; no after-stop record. Print and announce K as invented for practice. No apparatus: records give record-based evidence only. Retry sunlight in day 19's ten-minute comparison, then days 20 to 23 if needed. Review grade 3 u01-1 day 2 return and repair, using five minutes for the letter here. In day 18's readiness group use a new exposure-label task, not its held readiness prediction card.

## 6. Days

## Day 16

**Goal:** I can model observed changes and ask an energy-path question. **Kind:** Understanding.

- **5 min. Connect.** Review the written unit 1 u01-1 marble/paper-cup drawing and u01-2 transfer: energy passes from one object to another. Define source: where transferred energy comes from; recipient: the object receiving it.
- **8 min. Hook.** Adult turns/stops sealed crank light. All observe; rotating six name changes, 45 seconds each here. Hold back the compelling question's answer.
- **10 min. Initial model.** Children sketch observed apparatus and arrange tentative path labels. Inner parts stay uncertain. This block contains no unsupported report or written test-question task.
- **12 min. Question board.** Partners ask questions orally, once each; teacher sorts paths/control/origins. Everyone copies an unresolved question. Keep the unsupported-claim test question exclusive to E16.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E16 alone.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Adult stores light; children collect labels/sheets and receive their unit 1 revised packets/categories, without another repair.

Held: teacher, board/model; children, sketches. Tool: None. By hand: sketch, labels, question, E16. E16 A to day 17.

**Shape:** Practice sketches live apparatus and asks oral questions. E16 alone joins recorded effects with a question testing an unsupported report; no practice card combines those.

## Day 17

**Goal:** I can gather observations showing sound carrying energy across a gap. **Kind:** Application.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** E16 A, or retrieve observed versus inferred.
- **5 min. Setup and safety.** Check rice allergies beforehand. Separate supports and use a catch tray. Fit existing protection to everyone; otherwise use D without grains. No tasting; gentle taps away from ears.
- **15 min. Sound investigation.** One tub pair, no opaque barrier. Six groups observe for two minutes each; three minutes cover reset/movement. With D, groups instead annotate the announced invented record. Adult taps and compares silence; helpers handle receiving tub only. Everyone logs still/moving/unclear; waiting groups predict on paper.
- **10 min. Evidence discussion.** Pairs compare log with prediction. Hear six inside five minutes; everyone writes evidence note. No paired-report ranking.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E17 alone.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** With protection on, brush grains into tray and count/store parts for two minutes. Clean protection at six supplied places for three minutes; wipe hands.

Held: teacher, class record; children, log. Tool: None. If unclear repeat once inside investigation, then fallback D. E17 G to day 18.

**Shape:** Practice logs one live drum comparison. E17 ranks rival sound-recorder reports and explains a competing solid route; no practice uses rival reports.

## Day 18

**Goal:** I can compare light paths and sunlight warming evidence. **Kind:** Understanding.

- **10 min. Response and repair.** First five minutes return E17 and repair G if needed. Next five begin day 15 sunlight readiness response. Finish readiness in the next block's first five minutes; others compare new exposure labels.
- **20 min. Light and warming comparisons.** If needed, first five minutes finish readiness group's full ten. Adult compares identical sun/shade patches, then dark/pale under matched exposure; pre-exposure began before class. Read shared 1-degree thermometer; log whole-degree starts/finishes. Six pair readers inspect one mark, 30 seconds each; adult confirms. Five minutes here retrieve lets light through, lets some through, blocks light; turns the light. Sort card, translucent sheet/acrylic mirror. Add opaque: blocks light; translucent: lets some through. Flashlight shows paths only, never substitutes for solar warming.
- **10 min. Evidence explanation and exit.** Five minutes orally link observation to transfer, without combined three-path drawing. Last five: E18 alone.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Adult shades/stores materials; wipe instrument/table, collect papers. No sink queue.

Held: teacher, exposure record/model; children, table. Tool: None. Cloud/unresolved rise: fallback S, announced invented; record missing live evidence, reserve day 19 retry. E18 H to day 19.

**Shape:** Practice sorts beam effects and logs temperature readings separately. E18 alone combines three drawn light paths with warming evidence; no combined practice diagram.

## Day 19

**Goal:** I can revise energy paths using warmer-source and cooler-recipient evidence. **Kind:** Understanding.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** E18 H, appearance beside actual readings.
- **5 min. Prepare.** Review nearest-mark reading; adult checks clean spoons/thermometer. Warm palms only. Name heat as energy transfer from a warmer object to a cooler one.
- **15 min. Contact investigation.** Adult palm-warms one spoon; matching untouched spoon remains. Compare before/after with 1-degree instrument. Six observers inspect for 30 seconds each; everyone logs shared readings. Hand-to-spoon only, never spoon-to-spoon.
- **10 min. Compare evidence.** Arrange correct labels and explain orally. Supervised sunlight retry replaces this block if needed; prepared patches permit readings. Keep spoon record for later model completion.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E19 alone.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Adult wipes/stores thermometer/spoons; all wipe hands, return papers.

Held: teacher, revised model; children, sheet. Tool: None. Unresolved spoon rise: fallback H, explicitly invented; record missing observation. E19 A to section 2.2 day 20 Return and repair.
