## 5. What is carried, and instruments



**Carried.** Question board: paths, control, origins. Children keep observations; teacher holds tentative model, revised day 19. Review grade 3 u01-1 question board/rotating six and u01-2 revision in a second color, preserving originals/initials. Unit 1 u01-3 energy arrows are planned-only review, explained anew. Keep force arrows separate.

**Instruments.** Ideal: visible receiver movement and temperature changes above the instrument step. Separate drum supports partly fit: they reduce, not eliminate, alternatives. Shared non-glass thermometer, 1 degree Celsius per mark, fits detectable changes only; no decimals. Adult reads each surface at the same contact position. Pretest before class. Unresolved hand-to-spoon warming is recorded, never called zero transfer. No key tool.

**Safety/fallback.** Obtain rice allergy information before day 17; substitute light paper if needed. Adult taps gently. Two active helpers wear the two available goggles; observers stay beyond catch tray. Supply wipes between users. No tasting. Adults read cloth temperatures; no sun viewing or mirror beams toward eyes. Pre-expose matched patches with equal starts. Failed sunlight uses dated observations or fallback S: shaded 22 to 23, sunlit 22 to 27 degrees, invented. Failed drum uses D: still in silence, moving during taps, invented. Failed spoon uses H: spoon 20 to 23, untouched 20 to 20, invented. Keep supplied and measured rows separate. No apparatus: records give record-based evidence only. Retry sunlight in day 19's ten-minute comparison, then days 20 to 23 if needed. Review grade 3 u01-1 day 2 return and repair, allowing five minutes here rather than its earlier two-minute contrasts.
