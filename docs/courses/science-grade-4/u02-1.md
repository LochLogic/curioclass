<!-- skel -->

**Share.** 4-PS3-2: "Make observations to provide evidence" of transfer by sound, light and heat; current follows in section 2.2. Investigation uses PS3.A/B with energy and matter: join a source, recipient and observable change.

**Adult explanation.** Sound carries energy across an air gap; shared supports offer another path. Sunlight warms surfaces. Contact transfers energy from warmer to cooler objects. Temperature is not measured energy. No particles, numerical energy or causal vibration lesson; vibration belongs to unit 3. Review observation/inference from grade 3 u05-3 day 94.   Shape: rank rival records with a controlled-path reason.
  Key: B: separate supports reduce a contact path through the board. Sound/silence comparison links receiver movement to sound. A alone leaves the support path unresolved.
  Wrong: "A proves sound crossed the air because both objects share a board." (G).
  Print the corresponding exit-17.md only that day. 

- **E18, day 18, 4-PS3-2.** Print: "Invented for practice. Both patches start at 21 degrees Celsius. Pale patch in sunlight: 26 degrees; dark patch in shade: 22 degrees. A screen lets some light through; its patch has no temperature reading. Draw lit, blocked and partly transmitted paths with a warming-evidence caption."
  Shape: three-path drawing with warming-evidence caption.
  Key: Light reaches the sunlit patch; opaque shade blocks the direct path; some light passes the screen. The sunlit patch warms five degrees versus one in shade from equal starts, supporting sunlight transfer. Screened temperature is unknown.
  Wrong: "The dark patch must be warmer because it looks dark." (H).
  Print the corresponding exit-18.md only that day. 

- **E19, day 19, 4-PS3-2.** Print: "Invented for practice. A warmed clip touches a cooler metal lid. The clip cools while the lid warms. An untouched matching lid stays unchanged. A model points from lid to clip: metal supplies warmth. Revise this arrow using the comparison."
  Shape: reverse a supplied clip-to-lid model using comparison evidence.
  Key: Point from warmer clip to cooler lid. Clip cooling and recipient warming, unlike the untouched lid, support this direction. Metal does not supply warmth independently.
  Wrong: "The cooler metal lid creates warmth without receiving energy." (A).
  Print the corresponding exit-19.md only that day. 

Direction-only reversal: confer using warmer-source/cooler-recipient evidence. The listed A wrong response explicitly claims energy creation; do not label every wrong arrow A.
