# Section 2.1: Following energy

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 4. Unit 2, Energy on the move. "How does energy get from place to place, and where does the energy we use come from?" Anchor: a hand-crank flashlight that lights when the handle turns, with no battery to change (days 16 to 38): see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 16 to 19.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (16), application 3 (17, 18, 19). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 1 of 5 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 45 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Defaults: [DEFAULTS.md](DEFAULTS.md). Marker blocks are inherited data, not printable copy. Operational directions below govern draft-run changes. Unit 1 grade 4 entries are planned only: reintroduce their ideas, never claim completed teaching. No earlier key or specialist read is assumed.

Formative record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, one cell per look-for. Many means more than ten of thirty. Everyone returns paper. Public talk hears a named rotating six, at most 45 seconds each inside five minutes. Partners each take one 45-second turn. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference, not a letter. Print and announce every invented record as invented for practice. Reserve exit and assessment cases and shapes across all five sections.

## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **4-PS3-2** (days 16, 17, 18, 19): Observe to gather evidence that sound, light, heat and electric current carry energy from one place to another.
- skill s4-energy-transfer (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 16: turn the sealed flashlight handle and watch the light appear. Stop and observe what happens. Students draw where energy might start and go, then pose the compelling question without receiving its answer.
>
> Group the question board by paths, control and origins. Days 16 to 19 ask, "Can energy reach something without the objects touching?" Observations raise "How does a wire carry energy?", answered on days 20 to 23. "Can we control a useful signal?" drives design on days 24 to 29. "Where did our useful energy come from?" drives days 30 to 34. Day 35 attaches evidence to answered questions and retains unresolved ones. Transfer uses new station arrangements, a pull-cord generator and a different resource-use case.
> Carried: retain unit 1 cause-and-effect and energy arrows on day 16. Revise on day 19 from sound, sunlight and hand-warming observations; day 23 adds the closed current path from circuit evidence. Begin the design cycle on day 24; day 27 failure records guide refinement, revised after day 28 retests. Day 34 adds resource-to-use and environmental-effect branches from combined sources. Day 35 forms consensus; individuals apply these models in the end task. Retain the explanation frame; force arrows remain distinct from energy paths.
<!-- /skel -->

**Share.** 4-PS3-2: "Make observations to provide evidence" of transfer by sound, light and heat; current follows in section 2.2. Investigation uses PS3.A/B with energy and matter: join a source, recipient and observable change.

**Adult explanation.** Sound carries energy across an air gap; shared supports offer another path. Sunlight warms surfaces. Contact transfers energy from warmer to cooler objects. Temperature is not measured energy. No particles, numerical energy or causal vibration lesson; vibration belongs to unit 3. Review observation/inference from grade 3 u05-3 day 94. Reintroduce planned-only unit 1 u01-3 energy destinations/arrows. Connections: evidence reasoning day 16, nearest-mark thermometer reading day 18.
