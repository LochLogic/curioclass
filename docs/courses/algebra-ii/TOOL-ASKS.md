# Algebra II: tool asks

What the Algebra II plans ask of a tool's spec, one heading for each tool (UNIT-PLAN.md, rule 10).

## s06: Live unit-circle graph (light)

- **Mark:** light.
- **Days and job:** days 122 to 124 (unit 6, [s06.md](s06.md), field 8): show why. A point moves on the unit circle
  and its coordinate traces the sine or cosine graph at the same time.
- **Verdict:** partly fits. The graphing calculator graphs the function but does not link it to the circle; paper
  tracing covers the link.
- **Asks (from s06.md):**
  1. A point dragged or animated on the unit circle, with its angle shown in radians.
  2. The matching graph point drawn live on the trigonometric graph beside it.

## s07: Simulation and normal areas (light)

- **Mark:** light.
- **Days and job:** statistical simulation on days 139 and 146 to 154 (show why) and day 156 (read supplied runs, no
  tool operated); normal calculator on days 142 and 143 (show why), day 144 (practice with feedback) and day 156
  (check unaided) (unit 7, [s07.md](s07.md), field 8).
- **Verdict:** partly fits through the paper path; matching digital specifications were not found.
- **Asks (from s07.md):**
  1. A simulation that keeps the chance model the class chose visible beside every run, so runs are never read apart
     from the model.
  2. Normal-curve areas shown shaded, with the boundary values in standard deviations.
