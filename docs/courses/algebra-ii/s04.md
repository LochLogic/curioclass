- **A-SSE.B.3:** Choose and produce an equivalent form of an expression to reveal and explain properties of the quantity represented by the expression.★ Parts, by verb: a (A-SSE.B.3a) Factor a quadratic expression to reveal the zeros of the function it defines.; b (A-SSE.B.3b) Complete the square in a quadratic expression to reveal the maximum or minimum value of the function it defines.; c (A-SSE.B.3c) Use the properties of exponents to transform expressions for exponential functions.
