# Course frame: Math Grade 6

Course `math-grade-6` (catalogue id `math-k8`), grade 6, draft frame (2026-11-13). The scope and sequence sets the days; fix pass 2026-11-15.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8 units, in Illustrative Mathematics' grade 6 unit order (IM 6.1 to 6.8): area and surface area,
  ratios, unit rates and percentages, dividing fractions, decimal arithmetic, expressions and equations, rational
  numbers, data. IM's optional ninth unit is left out (Source map).
- **Days.** 160 course days, one 50-minute lesson a day: 153 in units, 7 for course-level checks and respond days (checks 1 and 2 of 1 day and
  check 3 of 2 days, each followed by 1 respond day). Each unit's days are its IM lessons as kept in the source map plus Curio
  Class days: an end-of-unit assessment and its respond day in every unit; in unit 2 a mid-unit assessment and its
  respond day; in units 3, 4, 6 and 7 one mid-unit assessment whose respond work runs in the 5-minute openings of the next two
  lessons; and, where days allow, one mixed-practice day. Each unit's Curio Class days are named in the source map.
  Spaced, mixed practice runs in the daily openings. A row last used in grade g receives retrieval in g+1 and readiness in g+2 or later. Readiness comes before the unit in the previous unit's last ten minutes or an intervening respond day; unit 1 alone uses day 1 after the hook if it has an older assumed row. Retrieval does not reset last use.
- **The time a day.** One 50-minute lesson a day, five a week, 32 weeks. An IM lesson is built for a period of at
  least 45 minutes (ms-teacher-guide, about); the other 5 minutes open the day with spaced, mixed practice on earlier
  work (APPROACH-MATH, practice and fluency). The pick is in "For the lead".
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 6. All 47 grade 6 codes (lettered parts
  included) are owned by the 26 grade 6 progression rows, and every row sits in one unit below, so every code is
  taught; where a row owns a parent and other rows own its parts, the unit text says where the standard is finished
  (6.RP.A.3 in units 2 and 3). Cluster weights are Student Achievement Partners' (ccss-math-focus.csv): major 6.RP.A,
  6.NS.A, 6.NS.C, 6.EE.A to C; supporting 6.G.A; additional 6.NS.B, 6.SP.A, 6.SP.B. The bar is RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md.
  Math has no check set. The C3 column names the unit's practices; each "Check set adds" cell records its settled disposition (the Candidate prefix remains for the frame gate), as detailed in the scope and sequence,
  the Progressions check for the unit and one extension (several from IM's optional lessons, named in the source
  map's cut rows).
- **Major work.** The scope and sequence states the single focus measure and verified minute allocation: major-cluster instruction over instructional minutes, excluding assessment and response. It names formula/exponent work explicitly and counts each minute once. Unit order and day counts stay as set here.
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, 1st edition (2019 to 2021), grade 6, CC BY 4.0, from
  im.kendallhunt.com, in the source store (S:\CurioClass-sources\illustrative-math\grade-6, docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md).
  IM's lessons are adapted into Curio Class days in our voice, each with the credit line. Only IM's open pages are
  used: lessons, preparation pages, student task PDFs, blackline masters and practice problems without solutions.
  Cool-downs, answer keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken (LOGIN-GATED.csv; DESK-ANSWERS
  2026-11-13), so every exit ticket, check and end-of-unit assessment is Curio Class's own. The 2nd edition
  (accessim.org) is CC BY-NC and never used. Embedded applets and third-party images are not carried over.
- **Practices.** Each unit names the one or two Standards for Mathematical Practice it builds (in the C3 column,
  which for math carries the practices) and the unit plan names the task where students do them. All eight are
  placed: MP1 (unit 4), MP2 (units 2, 3, 4, 7), MP3 (units 6, 8), MP4 (units 1, 8), MP5 (unit 2), MP6 (units 3, 5,
  7), MP7 (units 1, 6), MP8 (unit 5).
- **Personal finance, lightly.** Unit 3's better-deal, unit price and percent tasks and unit 6's hourly-pay tables
  are the course's light personal finance touches (Cory, 2026-11-14: no course of its own, Economics teaches it);
  they use given prices and wages, never a family's own money, and add no day.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 6.NS.B.2 and 6.NS.B.3 are this grade's fluency standards (division is the grade 6
  algorithm). Unit 5 builds them; the daily opening practice keeps them up through units 6 to 8. The year-end check samples both directly on day 158 with an untimed independent station and no supplied work. No tool shows a
  countdown timer to a student.
- **Readiness or retrieval.** All 23 grade 5 prerequisites have planned teaching or assessment placements and receive retrieval. Older m4-factors-multiples is checked in day 119's last ten minutes and rebuilt in day 136's first GCF activity. Older m3-scaled-graphs is checked in day 141's last ten minutes and rebuilt in day 143's frequency demonstration. No readiness check sits on a unit first day.
- **Paper path and accounts.** Every task has a paper path (grid paper, nets to cut and fold, cubes, printed number
  lines and coordinate grids). Any Curio Class on-screen tool needs no student account and stores nothing off the
  device. Calculators are used only where a unit says so, after estimating.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh sensitivity agent's read (docs/pipeline/briefs/sensitivity-read.md);
  no unit has cultural, Native, hard-history, health or disability content that needs a named community study. The
  answer-key agent reads every check, assessment and key, solving each item cold beside the script check (BASELINE
  rule 27), before the course is called finished. Word problems use a range of names and settings; the unit plan
  lists the people and places its problems show.
