# Course frame: Math Grade 6

Course id math-grade-6, grade 6, draft frame (2026-11-13). The scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8 units, in Illustrative Mathematics' grade 6 unit order (IM 6.1 to 6.8): area and surface area,
  ratios, unit rates and percentages, dividing fractions, decimal arithmetic, expressions and equations, rational
  numbers, data. IM's optional ninth unit is left out (Source map).
- **Days.** 160 teaching days: 154 in units, 6 for course-level checks and respond days (three cumulative checks of
  1 day, each followed by 1 respond day). Each unit's days are its IM lessons as kept in the source map plus Curio
  Class days: an end-of-unit assessment and its respond day in every unit, and in the major-work units a mid-unit
  check, its respond day and mixed-practice days (the counts are in the source map).
- **The time a day.** One 50-minute lesson a day, five a week, 32 weeks. An IM lesson is built for a period of at
  least 45 minutes (ms-teacher-guide, about); the other 5 minutes open the day with spaced, mixed practice on earlier
  work (APPROACH-MATH, practice and fluency). The pick is in "For the lead".
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 6. All 47 grade 6 codes (lettered parts
  included) are owned by the 26 grade 6 progression rows, and every row sits in one unit below, so every code is
  taught; where a row owns a parent and other rows own its parts, the unit text says where the standard is finished
  (6.RP.A.3 in units 2 and 3). Cluster weights are Student Achievement Partners' (ccss-math-focus.csv): major 6.RP.A,
  6.NS.A, 6.NS.C, 6.EE.A to C; supporting 6.G.A; additional 6.NS.B, 6.SP.A, 6.SP.B. The bar is RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md.
  Math has no check set of other states: the "Check set adds" column records candidates only.
- **Major work.** Counted by days: 104 of 160 days (65 percent) on major clusters strictly (units 2, 3, 6, most of 4
  and 7, and about two thirds of the 6 course-level days); 123 days (77 percent) when supporting work that practises
  major work is counted too (unit 1, whose area formulas are evaluated expressions of 6.EE.A.2c and whose squares
  and cubes start 6.EE.A.1, and unit 4's two volume days, which practise fraction multiplication and division). By
  units: 5 of 8 strictly, 6 of 8 with unit 1. The counting rule is in "For the lead".
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, 1st edition (2019 to 2021), grade 6, CC BY 4.0, from
  im.kendallhunt.com, in the source store (S:\CurioClass-sources\illustrative-math\grade-6, docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md).
  IM's lessons are adapted into Curio Class days in our voice, each with the credit line. Only IM's open pages are
  used: lessons, preparation pages, student task PDFs, blackline masters and practice problems without solutions.
  Cool-downs, answer keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken (LOGIN-GATED.csv; DESK-ANSWERS
  2026-11-13), so every exit ticket, check and end-of-unit assessment is Curio Class's own. The 2nd edition
  (accessim.org) is CC BY-NC and never used. Embedded applets and third-party images are not carried over.
- **Practices.** Each unit names the one or two Standards for Mathematical Practice it builds (in the C3 column,
  which for math carries the practices) and the unit plan names the task where students do them.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 6.NS.B.2 and 6.NS.B.3 are this grade's fluency standards (division is the grade 6
  algorithm). Unit 5 builds them; the daily opening practice keeps them up through units 6 to 8. No tool shows a
  countdown timer to a student.
- **Readiness checks.** A short readiness check opens each unit on the earlier skill it rests on; the first unit that
  needs a skill from two or more grades back rebuilds it briefly: m4-factors-multiples in unit 7, m3-scaled-graphs in
  unit 8 (the rows' re-entry notes).
- **Paper path and accounts.** Every task has a paper path (grid paper, nets to cut and fold, cubes, printed number
  lines and coordinate grids). Any Curio Class on-screen tool needs no student account and stores nothing off the
  device. Calculators are used only where a unit says so, after estimating.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent sensitivity read. No unit needs a community reader. A
  qualified math content reviewer (PRODUCTION-PATH step 7) reads the checks and assessments.
