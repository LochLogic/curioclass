# Scope and sequence: Library and Research Skills, grade 2

Course `library-and-research-skills-grade-2` (catalog id `library-2`), grade 2, draft, 2026-11-26. Written from
docs/courses/library-and-research-skills-grade-2/frame.md; the days live in
data/plans/library-and-research-skills-grade-2/days.csv.

**Calendar.** 29 course days, one 25-minute session a week for 29 weeks, so course day n falls in week n.
`school_day` is filled as 5n (the last school day of week n), so week = (school_day - 1) // 5 + 1 = n for every day.
Library is a special in grade 2 and does not count in the classroom teacher's stagger (CALENDAR.md rule 1). Each
session keeps about 3 minutes at each end to gather and put books back, so about 19 working minutes; every assessment
below is costed against those 19 minutes for a class of 30. The course has no session in weeks 30 to 32; week 30 is
the school's (frame, For the lead item 3).

**Readiness and retrieval.** Every earlier-grade row the course assumes is a grade 1 library row, used the year before
(the frame's Seams table), so each opens as a five-minute retrieval task inside a day and the course has no readiness
check: g1-what-i-know (day 2), g1-story-or-fact-book (day 3, said by the children as the show-me's first case),
g1-find-in-a-book (day 8, and again on day 17 as a same-year retrieval), g1-device-stop-rule (day 8),
g1-author-and-title (day 9, said as the show-me's start), g1-group-helpers (day 22, opening with the shared talk turn
card), g1-class-book (day 23) and g1-star-and-wish (day 24, a one-minute frame before the say-back). This year's rows
come back the same way: the credit line on day 16 (said for the two turtle covers, leading into "who made it") and on
day 23, the fact sort on day 23. Every unit's first day (1, 7, 15, 21) holds only the hook, the compelling question
and the show-me move.

**The skill card.** Each unit's pictured card is held by the teacher on the show-me days, by pairs on the practice
day and in the pair tries that end days 3 and 9, and withdrawn on the unit's first inquiry day in units 1 to 3 (4, 10,
18). Unit 4 has no practice day, so its card is held on inquiry days 22 and 23 and withdrawn on day 24. No unit
assessment uses it.

**Call numbers.** Invented call numbers follow the common school form: nonfiction, a subject number over the first
three letters of the author's surname; fiction, a fiction mark over the author's letters. The number tells where a
topic sits (books about animals sit together under one number). No classification system is named on any student,
teacher or family page; pages say "call number".

**Evidence.** Daily evidence is the teacher's class checklist of the day's one named skill, thrown away at the end of
each unit, plus the child's sheet, drawing or spoken answer. End-of-unit assessments are sheet items read aloud,
scanned pointing items and, in unit 4, a station of three; sheets go home after marking. Each respond day forms its
groups from the checklist by the step missed. Response time is the unit's own respond day (6, 12, 20, 26), counted
once.

**Codes.** The two owned ids, AASL.IV.A.2 (unit 1) and AASL.VI.B.2 (unit 2), are written plain; the twelve "with
help" ids are written `~AASL...`, as the progression writes them. AASL.I.A.2 is grade 1's owned id and comes back here
as review on day 2 (the gate notes it as a Fluency day's first code: it is practice of a grade 1 move, not first
teaching).

**Sources not loaded.** No grade 2 day leans on an unloaded source. The library online-reasoning lessons, the
web-literacy book and the AASL 3 to 5 band file (later.csv L149) serve later grades; this course uses the PK to 2
band file, already read for the frame. The free K to 2 online-safety presentation's listing behind the stop rule was read 2026-11-25 for the
kindergarten frame and not re-read; it matters only on day 7's optional shared screen. No source-map.csv (no base
text; every day is Curio Class's own).

## Unit 1. Questions and where to look (days 1 to 6, weeks 1 to 6)

*Which of our questions could a source answer, and which kind of source should we try?*

Arc: each child's own "I wonder" and the teacher's think-aloud from a wonder to a question-word question (day 1);
pairs practice writing questions and see the teacher's sort of what a source could answer (day 2); the source-kind
cards shown and tried in pairs (day 3); the cycle run on a host question with the card withdrawn (day 4); the
end-of-unit assessment (day 5); shares, self-check and reteach (day 6). Questions come before kinds because the kind
is chosen for a question. Load: g2-question-to-research has days 1, 2 and 4; g2-kinds-of-sources, the owned id, has
day 3's show-me with 9 minutes of pair tries on five new questions, and day 4's inquiry.

Host: social-studies:ss2-map-parts (social studies grade 2 unit 1, weeks 1 to 5) or science:s2-water-where (science
grade 2 unit 1, weeks 1 to 4), both in step on day 4 (week 4); stand-alone "Where do frogs go in winter?". On day 4
pairs pick from 6 to 8 board questions the teacher stars as ones the source set answers; on a host day the set is the
host course's pages for that day, named by the unit plan. Leans: ela:e2-wh-questions (ELA days 1 to 77, weeks 1 to 16)
is in step; social-studies:ss1-supporting-questions is grade 1, so day 1
carries the big-to-small question move for a class new to it.

Assessment: end-of-unit assessment on day 5, about 15 minutes: three new questions, a kind and a "because" each
(AASL.IV.A.2, marked from the page), one question-word question about a new picture, a spider on its web
(~AASL.I.A.1), and the pointing
item. ~AASL.I.A.2 (what one already knows) is seen on the day 2 sheet as review, not on the assessment. Response time:
day 6. No readiness check (all retrieval).

Candidates settled: both kept on day 2, zero added days: gaps in what one knows (the know and wonder sheet's "what I
am not sure of" box) and knowing that questions can be answered by finding information (the teacher's sort, said
aloud). Sensitivity: neutral topics; a "person who knows" is school staff by job, never family (day 3); scribe for a
spoken "because". Day 4's sheets give the unnamed class know and wonder chart kept for day 29. Whose voices (C12): the
unit plan records the makers of the read-aloud, pages and pictures.

## Unit 2. Finding the book, giving the credit (days 7 to 12, weeks 7 to 12)

*How do we find the book we need, and how do we tell others where our facts came from?*

Arc: the catalog card and call number shown on a fossil book the class needs, the teacher saying what the label's
number tells about where the topic sits and finding the book by the number, then the letters (day 7; the printed
catalog holds 6 to 8 fossil and earth books, and the teacher finds one); lookups, one nonfiction book by its number,
and a mixed-up fiction shelf put in order by the author's letters (day 8); the credit line shown from a cover and a
page and tried in pairs on three covers (day 9); the cycle with the card withdrawn, each pair on a different title or
author: lookup, shelf, answering page, fact with credit line, book put back, and one other source-kind card pointed at
for the question (day 10); the end-of-unit assessment (day 11); shares and reteach (day 12). Finding comes before
crediting because the credit line copies the same title and author the catalog card shows. Load: g2-catalog-lookup
days 7, 8 and 10; g2-credit-line, the owned id, day 9's show-me with 9 minutes of pair tries and day 10. Skill card:
"look it up, copy the label, find the number, then the letters; title by author".

Host: ela:e2-text-features (ELA grade 2 unit 2, taught weeks 10 to 12), in step on day 10; stand-alone "What can a
fossil tell us?". Device: day 7 only, optional, the teacher at one shared screen with the invented catalog, the stop
rule and the school's device rules; the printed catalog cards do the same job in full. The stop rule is retrieved on
day 8 and pointed at on day 11.

Assessment: end-of-unit assessment on day 11, about 15 minutes: the named book circled from a catalog entry, then
one other kind of source circled to try if that book does not answer (~AASL.IV.B.1, more than one kind), four fiction
spines in order by the author's letters, a credit line for a new cover (AASL.VI.B.2, marked from the page) and the
pointing item "point at the one who respects the maker" (two pictured children, one copying a book's sentence as their
own, one writing the fact with a credit line; VI.B.2's rights half), the stop steps pointed in order (~AASL.VI.A.1).
Response time: day 12. Day 11 also writes the class count the mid-year sheet
carries. No readiness check (all retrieval).

Candidates settled: both kept, zero added days: the library area that holds a topic, said from the label's number
(day 7); knowing it is wrong to copy from an author or a peer, said with the credit line (day 9).
Sensitivity: honest use without shaming (days 9, 10, 12); borrowing by the kindergarten rule; no product on the
invented screen. Whose voices (C12): recorded by the unit plan.

## Unit 3. Checking facts (days 15 to 20, weeks 15 to 20)

*When two books tell us different facts, how do we sort what we found and decide what to trust?*

Arc: the sort into picture boxes shown on the two turtle books (day 15); the two facts that differ, the third source,
who made each source and why, and the cycle card's new "check" picture (day 16); practice of all three moves (day
17); the cycle on a host question with the card withdrawn (day 18); the end-of-unit assessment (day 19); the class box
mat, self-check and reteach (day 20). Sorting comes first because the fact that differs is found among sorted cards;
the maker question follows the differing fact, since it is part of deciding what to trust. Load: three "with help"
rows on one case set and one find step, each a short sorting or circling move: g2-fact-sort days 15, 17, 18;
g2-two-books-differ and g2-who-made-it days 16, 17, 18. Frame For the lead item 5 (keep the six days) holds at this
layer; the unit plan's day-level read confirms the load, and its fallback (unit 3's maker show-me folded into day 9)
is not used here.

Host: science:s2-plant-needs-test (science grade 2 unit 5, weeks 16 to 19), in step on day 18; or
science:s2-water-where, as review; stand-alone "How big does a pumpkin grow?" with two passages and a third (plant
facts, beside the science host), so the inquiry does not rerun the turtle show-me case or grade 1's sunflower
question (grade 1 days 1 to 4, group read 2026-11-26). The third source in each set
explains why the two differ (different kinds of turtle live different lengths; different kinds of pumpkin grow to
different sizes), so the lesson is "check, and see why", not "one book was wrong"; every figure is true of a named
kind, checked at the days' accuracy read. Retrieval: day 16 opens with a credit line said for the two turtle covers,
which leads into "who made it" (the teacher finds the differing pages herself); day 17 opens with the answering page
found by its heading, the first practice round. Leans: ela:e2-author-purpose (weeks 10
to 12) and social-studies:ss2-source-maker (weeks 2 to 8) come before. Two leans come later than the frame's table
says for one of them: social-studies:ss2-two-accounts is taught in weeks 22 to 23 (the frame said 18 to 23,
overlapping) and ela:e2-compare-texts in weeks 25 to 32, so day 16 carries the frame's "if later" bridge for both: the
unit shows the same-and-different compare itself, one neutral fact at a time, and borrows no social studies case.

Assessment: end-of-unit assessment on day 19, about 17 minutes: four fact cards glued into boxes and, for one card,
"It goes here because ___" written or said to a scribe (~AASL.IV.B.4, a plan one can explain), two
passages that disagree with the two facts circled and "check a third source" ticked (~AASL.II.B.2, and the checking
half of ~AASL.IV.B.3), and maker and purpose circled for a cover and a page (~AASL.IV.B.3). Response time: day 20. No
readiness check (all retrieval).

Candidates settled: both kept, zero added days: not everything online is true, said on the invented store page (day
16, practiced day 17); notes as words, phrases and drawings, never a copied sentence, the fact card rule (day 15).
Sensitivity: neutral differences only (animal and plant facts); the store page sells seed packets and names no
company; invented pages on paper, no open search. Whose voices (C12): recorded by the unit plan.

## Unit 4. Our research poster (days 21 to 26, weeks 21 to 26)

*What can we find out by reading and by asking a person, and how do we share it?*

Arc: a pretend poster, the template, and planning two questions for a person shown (day 21); the questions checked
in passing, then a whole-class interview of a visiting staff member, each group asking one question and one follow-up
(about 50 seconds a group), and what each member added said (day 22: retrieval 3, finishing questions 4, interview 8,
member-added round 3 minutes; if no staff member can visit, the teacher reads Curio Class's written first-person
answer card as "our school's gardener", APPROACH.md Sensitive ground item 8); a book fact found, facts sorted and the
poster drafted with credit lines, after a two-minute class book retrieval (day 23); the star and wish frame said once,
then the pair share, say-back, one fix and the spoken look-back (day 24); the
end-of-unit assessment (day 25); the poster wall, self-check and reteach (day 26). Asking comes before the poster so
the person's answers are among the facts sorted. Load: g2-group-research days 21, 22 and the station on 25;
g2-research-share days 21, 23, 24 and the sheet on 25.

Host: ela:e2-research (the pollination research of ELA grade 2 unit 3, weeks 18 to 24) or
science:s2-seed-pollinator-model (science grade 2 unit 6, weeks 23 to 25), both in step on day 23; stand-alone "How do
people at our school care for its plants?". Lean: social-studies:ss2-family-interviews (weeks 23 to 26) runs in the
same weeks; unit 4 asks only neutral school questions of a staff member, never that unit's family-history questions.

Assessment: end-of-unit assessment over days 24 and 25. Day 24's share is marked on the checklist as it happens
(about 30 seconds a child, about 15 minutes): the pair's poster, two found facts that answer the question with credit
lines (~AASL.I.B.3); each child's say-back (~AASL.I.C.1); the spoken look-back, one step that went best and one to do
better (~AASL.I.D.4); posters are marked again after class. Day 25, about 18 minutes: a sheet item on a pretend poster
not seen before, one thing it taught (~AASL.I.C.1 transfer, about 3 minutes), then a station of three per group (ten
groups, about 1.5 minutes a group, about 15 minutes) on a new topic card: each child asks the teacher, standing in as
the person, one question (two from the group's plan, the third child's a follow-up, "Can you tell us more about
___?"; ~AASL.II.D.2, ~AASL.III.D.1), about 15 seconds each, then each names what one member added (~AASL.II.A.1),
about 10 seconds each. The day 24 record is reused on day 25, not collected again. Response time: day 26. No
readiness check (all retrieval).

Candidates settled: both kept, zero added days: a first sense of audience, who the poster is for, said on day 21 and
before the drafting on day 23; asking another to explain an idea and offering one's own, as the group's follow-up
question on day 22. Day 22's group round opens with the shared talk turn card's two pictures in SEL's words, "eyes
look, wait my turn" (SEL STANDARD section 7): where the school runs SEL, a reminder; where it does not, taught there.
Sensitivity: interviews by APPROACH.md Sensitive ground item 8 (day 22's note lists the limits);
the person credited by job, never a full name; groups mixed by the teacher. Whose voices (C12): recorded by the unit
plan.

## Shorter path

**A shorter period (about 20 minutes).** The retrieval openings drop from five minutes to three and the put-away to
two minutes each end, so about 16 working minutes; practice and pair tries keep two tries instead of three. Teaching
days without an opening shed their share: inquiry days 4, 10, 18 and 23 move their share to the unit's respond day
(6, 12, 20, 26), which already opens with shares; day 22 runs as written (retrieval 3, questions 4, interview 8
minutes) with the member-added round cut to 1 minute. Assessment days drop no item: day 5 (15 minutes), day 11 (15)
and day 13 (14) fit as written; day 19's fact cards come pre-cut, about 15 minutes; day 25's sheet item is the seated
task while the stations run, about 15 minutes; day 27 reads each item once, about 16 minutes. Day 24's share keeps its
marking; the poster fix moves to the start of day 25's book-corner work, which the station time already holds.

**A year about 10 school days short (two weeks, two sessions).** Two fold-in days, both practice days, nothing
assessed and no owned part on them alone. Each receiving day frees its own minutes by moving its share and self-check
to the next day that already holds them, so it still fits 19 working minutes:

| Fold-in day | Folds into | Minutes saved |
|---|---|---|
| 8 (catalog and shelf practice) | Day 10: its partner share and self-check move to day 12, which already opens with those shares and the self-check; the freed 5 minutes hold one catalog lookup and a four-spine fiction shelf put in order. The grade 1 find-the-page retrieval is met by day 10's own answering-page step. The three stop pictures go into day 9's opening, with the grade 1 source line said inside the show-me, so day 9's pair tries run 6 minutes on this path | 25 (one session) |
| 17 (sort, differ and maker practice) | Day 18: its box-mat share and self-check move to day 20, which already holds them; the freed 5 minutes hold one fact card sorted and one invented page's maker and purpose. The find-the-page retrieval is met by day 18's own find step | 25 (one session) |

That is 2 of the frame's 29 sessions (27 kept), about 50 minutes of class time. AASL.IV.A.2 and AASL.VI.B.2 stay on
their show-me, inquiry, assessment and check days. A school that loses more drops no respond day and no check; the
checks keep weeks 13 and 27, and a lost week before a check moves teaching days, never the check.

**A unit left out** (STANDARD.md section 4). K to 5 case sets are neutral, so this is unlikely, but the rule holds: the
course page and the family note name the left-out unit's ids as not claimed that year (unit 1: AASL.IV.A.2; unit 2:
AASL.VI.B.2). Leave-out bridge: where a later unit uses a left-out unit's card, that unit teaches the card's one-step
form in its first ten minutes and claims none of the left-out unit's skills: a left-out unit 2 puts "title by author"
into day 15's first ten minutes, since units 3 and 4 credit every source; a left-out unit 1 puts "which kind of source?"
into day 7's first ten minutes; a left-out unit 3 puts "sort it into its box" into day 21's. The course-level check
then drops the left-out unit's items. With unit 1 left out, the mid-year check (day 13) has no unit 1 to sample, so it
samples unit 2 on new cases in forms not given on day 11: a catalog entry with a second-kind circle and the respect
pointing item, about 10 minutes.
