Course-level response addresses the course check alone. No assessment items, answer keys or scoring guides are written here. Unit plans must provide those guides and collect evidence for every part of every code listed on an assessment row. A submitted product, revision trail, reading log or observed discussion may supply that evidence where the row explicitly says so. Short reading prompts replace additional full essays on reading-assessment days.

## Reading, writing and time

Each period has about 50 minutes. On two days each week the CSV reserves 15 minutes for independent reading and conferences, leaving about 5 minutes for word/sentence work, 25 for core work and 5 for the written response. These blocks normally occupy unassessed days. Day 30's reading block moves to day 28 after narrative submission, and day 96's block moves to day 98, retaining two weekly blocks. Week 22 has only one such day: its second block follows day 107's submission of the already-written argument product, with no new timed composition shortened. On other teaching days the core can take about 40 minutes. Partner rereading takes four minutes inside the last unassessed day's core work each week. Timed reading-assessment days have no independent-reading block. Four nights each week retain the frame's approximately 20 minutes of independent reading, logged; novella reading assignments are divided between class and this existing home-reading time, with a class catch-up route rather than an increased home requirement. Logs distinguish the common novella from the student's choice book. Students without access to home reading receive the print text and supported reading in class; the unit plan must check the manuscript against this budget before use.

The novella has ten successive installments of about 3,000 to 3,500 words. Finish tenths 1 to 7 by days 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14; day 15's mid-unit assessment uses those seven completed tenths. Finish tenths 8 to 10 by days 16, 18 and 20, before narrative planning on day 21. A discussion on an installment's first day uses completed chapters and the new opening, not an unread ending. Divide first reading between adjacent class/core blocks and existing home time, or the supported class allocation below. These are manuscript-dependent boundaries; the unit plan sets chapter/pages and times actual reading before release. The whole novella stays. Supports give context before reading, chunk syntax alongside the original during reading, and return to original evidence afterward. Summaries never replace an anchor.

Formal text discussions occur on days 9, 18, 26, 36, 42, 52, 59, 68, 77, 81, 86, 92, 96, 104, 111, 119, 126, 133, 141, 147 and 155: no gap exceeds ten school days. The eight-minute discussions embedded in editing, publication or response work use prepared text and the same four-part look-for; they are not an extra period. Daily partner talk supports these discussions. Students prepare evidence, track goals and deadlines, elicit elaboration, bring talk back on topic and acknowledge or change views. Frames fade as students demonstrate the routine. The first two weeks include private one-minute oral checks for flagged and new students; weekly rereading continues, without a public rate or a diagnostic claim.

The nine frame commissions remain: three argument, three informative and three narrative pieces, with full-process pieces in Units 1, 2 and 4. The lead also requires one sustained literary analysis: days 13, 14, 17 and 20 plan, draft, extend and revise one 450 to 600-word claim about contrasting narrators and theme across the novella, citing several chapters. Day 20 submits it; day 27 scores the existing W.7.9a product alongside brief fresh reading prompts, with no second extended essay. This tenth named piece counts as argument: named-piece shares are 4/3/3, or 40/30/30 percent. Dominant composition-day allocations are argument 12 (13, 14, 17, 20, 96, 99 to 103, 107, 151), informative 11 (47 to 51, 53, 78 to 81, 149), narrative 9 (21 to 26, 28, 45, 129): 37.5/34.4/28.1 percent of 32 days, close to the 35/35/30 planning aim. These are day allocations, not all minutes of those days. The final task inventory must include short daily responses and assessment writing too. Three-page typing maintenance on days 25, 79 and 102 uses existing drafts/source notes in one sitting, not three extra compositions. Two-page final informative/argument products remain as framed.

## Unit 1. The salmon year

**30 days, course/school days 1 to 30, weeks 1 to 6.** The alternating-sibling novella builds the shared knowledge and evidence habits the later year needs. Setting and choices precede the harder account of theme development and author-built contrasting views. The paired salmon/flow reading on day 12 introduces informational evidence without waiting for science's ecosystem unit, days 97 to 128. The novella finishes on day 20 before students use its craft to plan, draft, revise and edit a narrative on days 21 to 28. More days go to sustained reading, several-evidence analysis and the full narrative process than to the two convention expectations.

Readiness: none needed; confirmed grade 6 retrieval is listed above. The mid-unit assessment is day 15, with response day 16: explicit and inferred evidence, theme development and objective summary, interacting story elements and contrasting views. End-of-unit evidence is spread across day 27's cold literary response, day 28's narrative/revision/editing product and day 29's observed discussion and existing reading-range record, then day 30's first-half fresh nonfiction response. For 30 students use six groups of five, six teacher-observed six-minute turns, and evidence annotation for waiting groups. Reuse recent conferences. Together they cover every owned expectation, including both W.7.9 parts, both reading-range codes, all narrative and discussion parts, purpose/audience revision, concise language, commas and spelling. Day 30 holds the single final response block after the short nonfiction sample, with next-unit retrieval in its last ten minutes. The 50-minute budget is 20 sample, 20 response, 10 retrieval; 40 minutes uses 15/15/10. Reading moves to day 28 after product submission.

**Candidate cells:** keep the novella text record, 0 additional days, a condition of text selection and reading support; keep the discussion look-for, within days 4, 9, 18, 26 and 29, 0 additional days; keep the integrated theme reading/writing task on day 27, 1 existing day.
