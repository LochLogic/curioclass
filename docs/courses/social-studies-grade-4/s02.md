## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

**Trade teacher background, as of November 2026:** the three countries trade under one agreement, which the United States calls USMCA, Canada CUSMA and Mexico T-MEC. At its joint review on 1 July 2026 the United States did not confirm a 16-year extension, so the agreement stays in force (to 2036) and is now reviewed every year.
