## 8. Tools and supplies across the unit

**Tools: None.** No spec or TOOL-ASKS.md is needed. Supply printed maps, atlas pages, dated source cards, note logs, pencils and record sheets. Print digital sources with their metadata; a shared screen is optional. At home the adult is the partner. No student timer is shown.

Read cards aloud, at most 80 words each, in grade 3 to 4 language. Teach credible, fact, opinion, specialize, import, export and interdependence. Frame: "Source __ shows __; this supports __ because __." Allow pointing, dictation, enlarged maps and oral rehearsal. Judge content separately from English grammar. Extension compares two supported explanations.

## 9. Family note

We will research Canada and Mexico and explain how trade connects people across borders. Ask your child which evidence supports a finding and whose experience another source might add.
