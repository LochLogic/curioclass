## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How do we know about people who lived here thousands of years ago?"
- **Hook, day 42:** compare a Mesa Verde wall photograph with the Pueblo Today card. What can each tell us about people? Who needs an accurate account?
- **Co-write on day 42:** How does archaeology work (43)? What do dates and routes suggest (44 to 48)? What do regions and objects show (49 to 51)? Why preserve ancestral places (52 to 54)?

- **Teaching:** model observation versus inference on 43, chronology on 44 and weighing evidence on 48. Step back for evidence selection on 55. Return to the question board after each supporting answer.
