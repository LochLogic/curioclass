## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->

- **D1.1.3-5** (days 17): Say why a big inquiry question matters to other people, such as classmates and adults.

- **D3.2.3-5** (days 21, 22): Use the difference between fact and opinion to judge how believable several sources are.
