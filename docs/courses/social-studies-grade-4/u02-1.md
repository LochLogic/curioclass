## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.4.T1.3** (days 17): Research Canada or Mexico using maps, atlases, digital sources, and articles, then present on topics such as its people, languages, government, cities, environment, and industries.
- **D1.1.3-5** (days 17): Say why a big inquiry question matters to other people, such as classmates and adults.

- **E17, Exit Food Case, invented:** "A made-up family buys food delivered across a border. Why might our question matter to them?" Key: a use of understanding the supply connection. Meets: "They need to know which delivery brings their food." Not yet: "The teacher likes maps." Wrong: class preference alone (P).
  Shape: explain another family's need for the inquiry. Practice sorts supporting questions about a classroom delivery diagram; no family-shopping or importance-explanation case.
- **E18:** "Which source kinds locate a lake and explain how nearby residents use it? Give each kind's job." Key: map/atlas for location; article/residents' account for use. Accept other kinds only with these distinct jobs explained. Wrong: decoration determines usefulness (J); only agreeing views count (A).
  Shape: generate two helpful kinds with reasons. Practice classifies labeled sources, never selects a pair for a two-part location/use question or lake.
- **E19, Exit Ferry Case, invented:** a ferry clerk's 2026 ridership table counts trips; a rider's 2026 blog describes crowded crossings. A 1990 visitor guide gives the former timetable. "Choose two sources for today's ferry use. Explain using makers, kinds, dates, and different views." Key: table and blog, count versus experience, both 2026; neither settles every rider's view. Guide is historical comparison only. Wrong: official table proves everyone's satisfaction (B); only the clerk's view matters (A); decorated cover decides (J).
  Shape: select complementary sources and justify metadata/views. Practice annotates metadata and identifies a missing voice without choosing and defending a complete set. The unit's census table is replaced: a census does not necessarily count riders.
- **E20, Exit Leaflet Case, invented:** "A school made this travel leaflet in 1995. It describes that year's buses. Explain its use for researching buses then and now." Key: what the school told travelers then; not proof of current routes. Date and school audience constrain use. Meets: "It records school travel advice in 1995. Today's routes need a current source." Not yet: "Printed means current." Wrong: publication guarantees usefulness now (B).
  Shape: historical usefulness/current limit from date and audience. Practice labels setting on an equipment notice; no then/now usefulness conclusion or bus leaflet.
- **E21, Exit Park Sign Case, invented:** "This park opened in 1950. It has the friendliest ducks. Explain how you would judge this sign's opening-year claim." Key: check year in a town opening record; friendliest is preference and neither proves nor cancels the date. Meets: "Check 1950 separately in the opening record." Not yet: "Nice ducks mean the year is true." Wrong: preference is factual proof (C); opinion cancels every claim (K); checkable means true (L).
  Shape: judge one claim within mixed fact/opinion and propose verification. Practice repairs links between already labeled claims and evidence, never judges a whole sign or this date/preference pair.
- **E22:** reserve a country map and separately authored geography card placing Lake Chapala in Mexico. "Check Lake Chapala's country using both sources. State one limit of their agreement." Key: both place it in Mexico; this does not establish water quality. Meets: "Both locate it in Mexico; neither tests the water." Not yet: "Agreement means safe water." Wrong: location proves safety (D); titles without compared details (N).
  Shape: corroborate one location and bound the agreement. Practice flags conflicts or copied origins, never a matching location-plus-limit response. Country supported by the opened [space-agency page](https://ciencia.nasa.gov/ciencias-terrestres/el-lago-de-chapala/); independent map/card rows remain to make.
