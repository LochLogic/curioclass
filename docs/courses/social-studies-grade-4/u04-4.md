Review century: a hundred years, from u03-1 day 42 and grade 3 u05-1 day 91. Century numbering is not checked here. New vocabulary: interval, the time between two year marks; simultaneous, happening during shared time. Apply these to proportional positions and duration comparison. Review u03-1 day 44's given timeline in thousands of years ago. Here children construct proportional century spacing.

Limits: historical St. Lawrence Iroquoian communities cannot be assigned wholesale to one modern nation. Opened Wendake homepage does not establish s04's kinship claim; omit pending direct own-community evidence. A:shiwi people govern today; Hawikuh is historical, not all present Zuni lands. An aim never establishes consent or justification.

## 6. Days

Every teaching day returns the previous exit and collected practice in Return and repair. Its five minutes and the first five work minutes serve one priority per child, including a fresh retry. Many means more than a third: teach that priority together; otherwise meet a small group. Other children follow their written cues. Never stack one repair per error or paper. The five-minute Question board hears six children, one 45-second ask each, totaling 4.5 minutes. Rotate five groups of six; record names. Everyone hands in paper. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously where named, 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total. Accommodation alone does not count as content help.
