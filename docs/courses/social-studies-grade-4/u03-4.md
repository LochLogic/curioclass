## 9. Supports and extension

Words: ancestor, descendant, preserve, context. Descendant means a person from a later generation; preserve means protect and care for. Frame: "The source names ___. This supports ___, but not ___." No child discloses ancestry. Teaching target 160 independent words; all print within DEFAULTS. Neutral image descriptions/tactile outlines and dictation preserve inference. Extension names missing evidence for a stronger historical statement.
