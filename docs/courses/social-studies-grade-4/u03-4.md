## 1. Standards and practices

MA.4.T2.4, "Give examples" and "explain": preserved Mesa Verde and Cahokia, their different contributions to history, and respectful preservation, days 52 to 54. D2.Geo.3 returns through printed-scale measurement on 52; D3.4 returns through bounded preservation reasoning. Teacher distinguishes material survival from living continuity, association from exclusive descent, and movement from disappearance. A past place is part of American history before European arrivals.

Review written grade 3 u04-4 day 84, "sovereignty (a nation's right to govern itself)"; u04-1 day 72 homeland, and u04-2 day 75 present lands. Pueblo communities are distinct, not a single nation. Osage testimony names an ancestral relationship, not the identity of every Cahokia builder. No invented Native speakers, sacred rooms, designs, burial images or identity role play.

**Q4 Shape:** name two preserved sites and status, explain each historical contribution from fresh views/captions, connect preservation to living descendants. Practice never writes that integrated two-site account, uses D views or supplies an answer paragraph. Cold key: Mesa Verde National Park retains Ancestral Pueblo building/farming evidence; Cahokia Mounds State Historic Site retains constructed earthworks and settlement evidence. Pueblo peoples and Osage Nation are living communities with ancestral relationships, not exclusive lists of descendants. Both histories precede European arrivals. Keeping context supports learning and respects continuing relationships. Meets: "These sites retain different evidence of earlier communities. Living descendants still care about these places." ## 9. Supports and extension

Words: ancestor, descendant, preserve, context. Descendant means a person from a later generation in a line of descent; preserve means protect and care for. Frame: "The source names ___. This supports ___, but not ___." No child discloses ancestry. Teaching target 160 independent words; all print within DEFAULTS. Neutral image descriptions/tactile outlines and dictation preserve inference. Extension names missing evidence for a stronger historical statement.
