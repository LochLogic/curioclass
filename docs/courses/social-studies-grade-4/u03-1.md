## 3. Checks, written first


**Owned:** Q3 methods; Q5's importance component is administered by u03-5 despite the copied ownership row. One component scale throughout: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Access support is not content help. All hypothetical cases are labeled invented for practice.

**Q3 Shape:** explain a research chain across footprint dating, methods and a separate settlement/culture strip. Avoid that combination in all teaching, including houses/crops and building/sunrise diagrams. Practice matches single methods to missing records, never a complete methods argument. Cold key: location/layer retain context; objects show skills; organic traces inform food/environment; independent dating and specialists test interpretations. Stored crops near floors may suggest settlement; repeated alignment may suggest seasonal observation, never sacred meaning or intention alone. Meets: "Several methods test the date. Storage suggests settlement, but the diagram cannot reveal beliefs." Not yet: "One expert guesses everything." Wrong: taking attractive objects is research (F); one expert settles it (O).
