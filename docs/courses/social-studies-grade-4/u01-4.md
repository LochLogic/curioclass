Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-4/1/s/1.4), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-4 u01-4.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 4 social studies, [Unit 1: North America on the map](s01.md). A plan, not a lesson: timing and materials are ours, untested.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Shares.** MA.4.T1.2: "Locate" through the grid extension, days 12 to 13; political parts return day 15. D3.4: "Use" evidence to "develop" a claim, and D4.1: "Construct" an argument from several sources, day 14. MA.4.T1.1 and D2.Geo.3 return in assessment.
- **Review, ledger words.** Grade 3 u01-2 day 4: equator (the line round the middle of the globe), prime meridian (an agreed line from pole to pole), hemisphere (a half of the globe). Its day 5: latitude (lines that run east and west) and longitude (lines that run from pole to pole), named as lines only; grid cell, letter then number. New: whole-degree measures and ordered coordinates of an intersection, not a cell.
- **Connections.** Grade 3 u01-6 day 19: claim, evidence and reasoning, how a detail supports a claim. Grade 3 u05-8 day 117: claim sheet, claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Return that routine and u01-2's source annotations.
- **Teacher explanation.** Lines run one way but measure angular position in the other direction. Latitude measures north/south from equator; longitude east/west from prime meridian. Write latitude first, retaining hemisphere labels. No decimals or memorized conversion factors.

## 6. Days

Each day block ends in individual paper evidence. Read printed stems once without solving them. Public sharing is optional. Pair turns happen simultaneously across 15 pairs; two 45-second turns take 90 seconds, not 45 minutes. A six-child hearing takes 4.5 minutes within a named five-minute slot. Collect the hand work from everyone else. Give repair groups one priority gap; all other children use written cues. A five-minute group includes a brief contrast and an individual fresh attempt, not four long oral turns. Source cards are paraphrases, credited to their row URLs; made-up people, places and pins are labeled and named aloud as made up.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: latitude, longitude, degree, intersection, claim, evidence, reasoning, limit. Frame: "The detail supports ___ because ___." Use tactile lines, large margins and scribe; read labels without locating answers. Extension: explain why a missing label cannot establish who lives there.
