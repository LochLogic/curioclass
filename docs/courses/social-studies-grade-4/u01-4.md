Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-4/1/s/1.4), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-4 u01-4.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Shares.** MA.4.T1.2: "Locate" through the grid extension, days 12 to 13; political parts return day 15. D3.4: "Use" evidence to "develop" a claim, and D4.1: "Construct" an argument from several sources, day 14. MA.4.T1.1 and D2.Geo.3 return in assessment.
- **Review, ledger words.** Grade 3 u01-2 day 4: equator (the line round the middle of the globe), prime meridian (an agreed line from pole to pole), hemisphere (a half of the globe). Its day 5: latitude (lines that run east and west) and longitude (lines that run from pole to pole), named as lines only; grid cell, letter then number. New: whole-degree measures and ordered coordinates of an intersection, not a cell.
- **Connections.** Grade 3 u01-6 day 19: claim, evidence and reasoning, how a detail supports a claim. Grade 3 u05-8 day 117: claim sheet, claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Return that routine and u01-2's source annotations.
- **Teacher explanation.** Lines run one way but measure angular position in the other direction. Latitude measures north/south from equator; longitude east/west from prime meridian. Write latitude first, retaining hemisphere labels. No decimals or memorized conversion factors.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** Q2 coordinates and Q4, with Q1/Q3 administered here, s01 field 5. All children submit paper. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component throughout, never a points total. Many means more than a third. Access accommodations are not content help.

- **E12.** "Mark X at 40 degrees north, 120 degrees west on Exit Grid." Key: that parallel/meridian intersection, not the adjacent cell. Wrong: swaps latitude and longitude (G); starts north/south from prime meridian (P).
  Shape: plot one given ordered coordinate. Practice reads existing points and sorts line labels, never plots a supplied pair or uses this coordinate.
- **E13.** "Give Y's coordinates on Exit Grid. Mark Z at 60 degrees north, 130 degrees west." Y is printed at 20 degrees north, 90 degrees west; key gives that ordered pair and correct Z intersection. Wrong: west coordinate first (G); latitude measured from prime meridian (P).
  Shape: read one point and plot a second given pair. Practice traces routes and diagnoses labels without producing a point's full pair or plotting a supplied pair. Neither Y nor Z appears before E13.
- **E14, Park Maps, invented.** Walking Map shows paths and a pond. Region Map places the park beside a town and omits paths. "Use both Park Maps to explain how their usefulness differs. Include a detail from each map and one limit." Teacher-judged: comparative claim, distinct relevant detail from each named map, reasoning and supported limit. Meets: "Walking Map helps find paths beside the pond. Region Map shows the town beside the park. Different views help different needs; Region Map cannot guide every turn." Does not: "Both maps have titles, so they show everything." Wrong: no visible detail (H); both details from one map (I); shows everything (N).
  Shape: independently construct a two-map usefulness argument with a limit. Practice annotates a supplied paragraph, then composes evidence sentences under a given claim. No complete model/practice paragraph argues usefulness and its limit; no park/town/pond pair before E14.

**Q2 coordinate Shape:** plot K, read L, combined with political locations/terms. Reserve 50 north/100 west and 30 north/110 west. Cold key: K at the first intersection; L reads 30 degrees north, 110 degrees west. Practices never pair plotting with reading or reproduce these coordinates.

**Q4 Shape:** independent argument about what C and D establish for a visitor, distinct evidence from both and a limit; then another reader's use. Practice is annotation or partial sentence construction, never that full response or visitor case. Cold sample: "C locates the Mackenzie River; D locates Manitoba. They locate different features, but cannot establish residents' languages." Final maps must show those details. Score claim/reasoning once under D3.4 and multiple-source support once under D4.1. E14's park-scale comparison uses a different question and case.

Q1/Q3 shapes and recomputed keys are in u01-1/u01-2. Day 16 fresh retries: Group 1 repairs a new symbol/direction record or scale construction; Group 2 repairs category/containment records from new Saskatchewan and Yucatan insets; Group 3 repairs swapped grid labels or a map-window statement; Group 4 adds missing evidence to an invented market-map claim. Real inset facts are source to check.

**Retry Shape:** single fresh component repair privately chosen per child, never copying an assessment answer. Withhold retry cards through day 15; use new layouts and details. Same record scale.

## 6. Days

## Day 12

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Dominant E11 gap, fresh containment or government annotation.
- **10 min. Globe retrieval and repair.** First five: name equator and prime meridian, then D2 on a letter-number grid. Key: both zero lines; column D, row 2. Next five: if needed, retrace a fresh cell and zero lines; whole class when many, otherwise one group while others annotate reference lines.
- **10 min. Numbered lines.** Label a known star at 10 degrees north, 70 degrees west by reading its lines outward to margins. Explain degree measures, latitude-first notation and intersection versus cell. Do not demonstrate plotting a given pair.
- **10 min. Supported work.** Children read existing stars at 30 north/80 west and 10 south/20 east, then sort line descriptions. Teacher reserves five minutes for E11's second group; retrieval repair already had its own minutes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E12, fresh grid, alone.

Teacher holds globe; children hold grids/zero-line cards. Tool: None. By hand: retrieval, readings, sorts, E12.

**Shared shape.** Practice: Circle both zero lines. Shade cell D2 on the letter grid. Read stars B and C: latitude first, then longitude. Exit ticket: Mark X at 40 degrees north, 120 degrees west on Exit Grid. The named exit record stays covered until independent work.

## Day 13

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Dominant E12 G or P; correct a new label rather than rehearse its point.
- **10 min. Line routes.** Pairs trace a premarked arrow along a parallel and across a meridian. Say which coordinate stays fixed. Use lines not matching exit or assessment values.
- **15 min. Supported work.** Repair swapped margin labels and coordinate-order statements on separate grids. Partners explain one correction each, at most three 90-second cycles. Teacher holds five minutes for one repair group. No read-a-point/plot-a-point game.
- **5 min. Check explanations.** Hear six, 45 seconds each; collect all corrected grids. Feedback after each short set, accuracy before ease.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** E13, reserved grid, alone.

Children hold grids; teacher tracing withdrawn. Tool: None. By hand: routes, repairs, E13. No timer or new content.

**Shared shape.** Practice: Trace arrows A and B. Name the measurement unchanged along each. Repair Route Card 1: its margin headings are swapped. Exit ticket: Give Y's coordinates on Exit Grid. Mark Z at 60 degrees north, 130 degrees west. The named exit record stays covered until independent work.

## Day 14

- **5 min. Return and repair.** E13's dominant gap on a fresh grid; collect all corrections.
- **8 min. Evidence retrieval.** Return E1 privately. Review the grade 3 claim sheet; annotate evidence and reasoning in a supplied paragraph about physical and political information, without a usefulness argument or limit.
- **15 min. Supported work.** Each child writes source-linked evidence sentences under a given claim about A/B. Pairs check whether each sentence actually uses its source, two 45-second turns. Teacher holds five minutes for one repair group. Preserve the full reference frame, not a complete model response.
- **5 min. Share evidence.** Hear six, 45 seconds each; collect all sentences. Update the board using supported details.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** E14, unseen Park Maps, independent paragraph.

Children hold A/B and frame; teacher holds board. Tool: None. By hand: annotations, sentences, E14. Partial practice replaces the unit's full paragraph to protect transfer.

**Shared shape.** Practice: Underline evidence in the supplied paragraph. Circle words linking evidence to its claim. Write one evidence sentence from A under the given claim. Exit ticket: Use both Park Maps to explain how their usefulness differs. Include a detail from each map and one limit. The named exit record stays covered until independent work.

## 8. Practice that returns

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: latitude, longitude, degree, intersection, claim, evidence, reasoning, limit. Frame: "The detail supports ___ because ___." Use tactile lines, large margins and scribe; read labels without locating answers. Read the practice paragraph aloud; at most 120 independent passage words on teaching days, with labels/tasks within 350. Split assessment directions into sentences of at most 15 words. Extension: explain why a missing label cannot establish who lives there.
