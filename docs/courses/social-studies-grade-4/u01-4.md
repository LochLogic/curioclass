- **Review, ledger words.** Grade 3 u01-2 day 4: equator (the line round the middle of the globe), prime meridian (an agreed line from pole to pole), hemisphere (a half of the globe). Its day 5: latitude (lines that run east and west) and longitude (lines that run from pole to pole), named as lines only; grid cell, letter then number. New: whole-degree measures and ordered coordinates of an intersection, not a cell.
- **Connections.** Grade 3 u01-6 day 19: claim, evidence and reasoning, how a detail supports a claim. Grade 3 u05-8 day 117: claim sheet, claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Return that routine and u01-2's source annotations.
- **Teacher explanation.** Lines run one way but measure angular position in the other direction. Latitude measures north/south from equator; longitude east/west from prime meridian. Write latitude first, retaining hemisphere labels. No decimals or memorized conversion factors.
