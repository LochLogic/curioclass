- **E151, held paper exit.** "Made up: A storm closed a fruit port; inland shops received less fruit. A factory accident closed a bridge; deliveries detoured through another county. Explain each event's distant effect." Key: Port closure reduced inland fruit deliveries. Bridge closure changed delivery routes and traffic in another county. No price change is stated. **Shape:** Explain two distant effects from fresh prose.
- **E152, held paper exit.** s07-X152: "Made-up flood: Town officials opened a shelter; a county offered buses. A national agency funded road repairs. Each controls its named service here. Identify the decision-maker for each service." Key: Town officials: shelter. County officials: buses. National agency: repair funding. These responsibilities are stipulated, not universal. **Shape:** fresh source-specific paper response. s07-X152b: "In this made-up flood case, explain one way residents could participate." Key: Residents could submit evidence about needs, attend a public meeting or join authorized adult relief. **Shape:** fresh source-specific paper response.
- **E153, held paper exit.** "Made-up school rule: Only the board approves rehearsal-room changes. Parents requested a room and supplied a survey. The board has not met. The principal later posted a room without board approval. Explain whether the request became policy under this rule." Key: It remains a proposal. Neither the request nor unauthorized posting supplies the board approval this rule requires. **Shape:** Judge status against a fresh adoption rule.
- **E154, held paper exit.** "Made-up island country: Cyclone flooding closed a coastal market. Council moved it uphill; stall funds remain short. Made-up United States: Flooding closed a market. Officials approved raised-ground reopening; only some stalls have funding. Compare a challenge and an opportunity." Key: Both lack stall funds. Higher sites offer less flood exposure and continued trading, without ending risk. Each needs its own decisions and conditions. **Shape:** Compare fresh countries' challenges and opportunities.
