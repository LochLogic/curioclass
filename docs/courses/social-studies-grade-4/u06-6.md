# Section 6.6: The national capital: buildings and public uses




## 1. Standards and practices


MA.4.T4b.4 carries "Describe" the national capital and "give examples" of civic/cultural resources. Review written grade 3 u02-2 day 28: branch (one part of a government with its own job: makes laws, carries them out, or decides cases). Buildings/national office matches extend that review; grade 5 owns constitutional checks.

Teacher separates office, building, branch and government level. D.C.'s congressional limit is a precise legal fact, not an invitation to debate. D.C. has local government and presidential voting; lack of senators/final House vote never means no government or no voting. Day 127 connects sourcing to civic use. Content note: monuments commemorate people without requiring agreement or praise.
