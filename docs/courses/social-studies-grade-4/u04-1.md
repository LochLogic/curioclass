Audience/purpose are review despite the frame. New work applies them to voyage evidence and distinguishes maker, later recorder and adapter.

**Limits and teacher explanation.** No conquest role play or imagined Native testimony. A primary source can be mistaken. A contemporary illustration need not be eyewitness evidence. Distinguish event, document and caption dates. Internal evidence supports a possible purpose. Teach archaeology through material evidence, separately from a historian's caption.

## 6. Days

Every teaching day returns the previous exit and collected practice in Return and repair. Its five minutes and the first five work minutes serve one priority per child, including a fresh retry. Many means more than a third: teach that priority together; otherwise meet a small group. Other children follow their written cues. Never stack one repair per error or paper. The five-minute Question board hears six children, one 45-second ask each, totaling 4.5 minutes. Rotate five groups of six; record names. Everyone hands in paper. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously where named, 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total. Accommodation alone does not count as content help.
