**Unit assessment items owned:** none last taught here. Prepare Q3 consequences and Q6 continuity; ownership stays u04-4/5. Use u04-1's one marking scale.

- **E68, Exchange Diagram.** Fresh arrows: Americas to Europe, maize; Europe to Americas, smallpox. Short sourced captions describe introduced crop use and illness. "Explain one effect of each movement." Key: maize added food crops to diets elsewhere; introduced smallpox caused severe Native losses. No balanced-benefit or single-cause claim. Sample: "A crop added food choices elsewhere; introduced smallpox killed many people in the Americas." Wrong: "Everyone gained equally." (E); "Disease explains every loss." (L). Shape: paired opposite-flow effects; practice classifies other entries' origins/directions and completes one effect link, never this pair. Exact captions/diagram source to check against opened university reference; reserve maize/smallpox effects from demonstrations.
- **E69, Tenochtitlan Route and Nahua Caption.** New inset labels coast, Tlaxcala, Tenochtitlan without connecting arrows. Fresh attributed factual caption names Spanish forces, Tlaxcalteca allies, Mexica defenders, no invented testimony. "Trace the inland route and explain who fought, using the caption." Key: coast through Tlaxcala toward Tenochtitlan; Spanish and allied Tlaxcalteca forces fought Mexica defenders, collective action rather than one man's conquest. Wrong: "Cortés fought alone." (Q); "All Native communities fought on one side." (Q). Shape: trace new configuration plus actor explanation; practice locates separate places and sorts actor labels, never route-plus-actors. Exact geography/caption source to check; fallback labeled not-to-scale schematic and attributed university-exhibit factual summary.
- **E70, Living Peoples Cards.** Reserve unused community details: Taíno artists maintain artistic traditions; Nahua authors produce books in Nahuatl. "Give one detail from each card showing present community life." Key: continuing art and Nahuatl authorship, each linked to named community/source. Sample: "The Taíno account describes artists today; Nahua educators' biographies describe books in Nahuatl." Wrong: "Both peoples vanished." (O). Shape: two communities' present activities from fresh cards; practice classifies past/present verbs and source makers separately, never a two-community activity list. No sacred object pictures/design copying. Source rows and exact wording to make; opened own-community pages support these facts.

**Check Shapes:** Q3 integrates two routes, people, judged motive and consequence; Q6 argues change/continuity from E/G. Practice here never performs either composite or uses reserved packet details.
