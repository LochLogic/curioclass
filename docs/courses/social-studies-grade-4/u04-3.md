## 1. Standards and practices

MA.4.T3.3: "Trace" Cortés's route and "evaluate" motives through effects, 68 to 70. Timeline construction waits for 73. Review alliance: an agreement between groups to help one another, grade 3 u05-4 day 103. Teach ally as a group helping another force, defender as resisting attack, and siege as surrounding a place to force surrender. Connections: D2.His.9 evidence limits on 68; D3.4 continuity statements on 70, practiced rather than newly owned.

Written review: conquest, taking control by force, from day 65. Also review the cause-effect chain: Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___., grade 3 u05-2 day 93; perspective: two sides of one event, grade 3 u05-3 day 98. Add intended-gain and documented-harm branches, not new models. Recall written grade 3 claim/evidence/reasoning; review u03-2 day 48's written source log and question board: qualified claim.

Limits: no single Native side. Name Mexica defenders and Tlaxcalteca allies; alliance did not mean all communities agreed. No graphic images, sacred designs, personal disclosures or conquest role play. Enslaved people were forced, never goods. Disease is one cause beside violence, forced labor and political choices. Taíno descendants and Nahua educators live and act today. Teacher separates defeat from disappearance.

## 6. Days

Every teaching day returns the previous exit and collected practice in Return and repair. Its five minutes and the first five work minutes serve one priority per child, including a fresh retry. Many means more than a third: teach that priority together; otherwise meet a small group. Other children follow their written cues. Never stack one repair per error or paper. The five-minute Question board hears six children, one 45-second ask each, totaling 4.5 minutes. Rotate five groups of six; record names. Everyone hands in paper. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously where named, 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total. Accommodation alone does not count as content help.
