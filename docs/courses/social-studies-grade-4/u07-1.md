## 1. Standards and practices

- **E137, paper exit.** "Made up: A planner wants a center people can reach after flooding. Why might our unit question matter to this planner?" Key: Community needs and land conditions affect usable access. The planner needs evidence about both. Wrong: "It matters only because the planner likes questions" (U). **Shape:** Explain usefulness for a supplied person.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid complete explanations of another supplied person's need; select missing source kinds instead.
- **E138, paper exit.** "In colonial New Amsterdam, enslaved Africans built houses and cleared shore areas for docks. Describe this contribution while distinguishing forced work from freedom." Key: They helped construct the town and prepare docks. Enslaved people were forced to work, without freedom to refuse. Wrong: "They freely chose all this work" (B). **Shape:** Interpret a new construction caption through contribution and coercion.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid construction captions and contribution/coercion explanations; mark source labels and scope instead.
- **E139, paper exit.** "Black residents established Eatonville, a self-governing Florida town. What does this add to an account showing African Americans only as enslaved people?" Key: It adds Black town-building and self-government, beyond the incomplete account of enslavement. Wrong: "The Southeast has only European culture" (A). **Shape:** Expand an incomplete account from a new town caption.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid account-expansion writing and Eatonville; match makers and dates instead.
- **E140, paper exit.** "An Arab and Chaldean American council advised Michigan's government. What does this add to a Midwest account about European immigrants alone?" Key: Arab and Chaldean Americans also contributed civic knowledge and participation in public decisions. Wrong: "Only European culture shaped the Midwest" (A). **Shape:** Expand a one-group account with new civic evidence.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid expanding one-group accounts and the Michigan council; mark stated motives instead.
- **E141, paper exit.** "Some African American soldiers built roads in the Southwest after the Civil War. Describe this contribution without making it every African American person's occupation." Key: Some soldiers constructed roads connecting army posts. This describes particular soldiers, not every African American person or occupation. Wrong: "Every African American person built roads" (I). **Shape:** Write a qualified occupational description from an unfamiliar caption.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid qualified occupational descriptions and roads; order source identities and dates instead.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Return the previous day's exit, retrieval and practice papers with one written priority cue for each child. Use the first five minutes for cue reading/comparison, the next five for a fresh attempt. More than a third means whole-class repair; otherwise the teacher meets a named group of at most six while others follow their cues.
