## 1. Standards and practices


D1.1 carries "Explain why" for another person's need; the four regional codes carry "Describe" every named group. The teacher distinguishes contribution from everyone's occupation, forced labor from choice, and living nations from historical illustrations. Contributions never earn rights or justify conquest. No sacred knowledge, role play or personal disclosures.

Review written grade 3 contribution (something a nation shares or makes that continues today), u04-4 day 83; extend to particular civic and institutional work. Review culture (what people make, do and share), u01-5 day 17; supporting question (a smaller question that helps answer the big one), u07-1 day 141. D2.His.1 connects dates to regional records. Examples concern specific people, places and times, never every member of a community.
