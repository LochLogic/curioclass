Explain how force changed this community contribution." Key: Flower businesses provided livelihoods. Government removal and confinement disrupted these businesses and denied choice, unlike a freely chosen job move. **Shape:** Connect an unfamiliar livelihood with forced removal.
- **E144, held paper exit.** "D: Duwamish people created a Longhouse and Cultural Center in Seattle. It shares their history and culture. W: Seattle's Wing Luke Museum uses a community-developed exhibit process. It presents Asian American, Native Hawaiian and Pacific Islander histories. Neither describes every resident's views or experience. W concerns several communities. **Shape:** Synthesize two new community contributions with a shared limit.

Explain continuity and change using land and community ties." Key: The pass still connects towns; transport and recorded goods differ. Terrain offers access; relatives help arrivals settle. Books carry ideas. The dates do not show when family networks began. **Shape:** Explain paired dates through land and social influences.
