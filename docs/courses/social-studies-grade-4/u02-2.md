## 9. Supports and extension

Review population, natural resource, historic landmark and industry in the ledger's words above. Parliament is the lawmaking institution in the Canadian source. Confidence means support to continue governing. A court interprets laws and resolves disputes. A sawmill cuts wood into lumber. Agriculture grows crops and raises animals. A service is work done for others. Gloss each before use. Frame: "This source describes ___, not everyone." Read shared introductions and adult-data summaries aloud. Independent source text at most 180 words daily; all print within DEFAULTS. Use enlarged maps, dictation or topic icons. Extension explains a second supported relationship.
