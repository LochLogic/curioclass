**Teacher notes.** The Whitman mission (Q3) is told differently by the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, the nations of this unit's hook. In 1847 a measles epidemic killed many Cayuse; some blamed the mission, and in November 1847 the Whitmans and others at the mission were killed. Five Cayuse men were hanged in 1850. Use the Whitmans only for what they said they wanted, beside the Confederated Tribes' account. Historians debate whether Olaudah Equiano (day 93) was born in Africa, since some records give South Carolina; his book is still a widely read account of the Middle Passage.
