Locate Mesa Verde and Cahokia using the map's key. Match a flaked point, clay vessel, constructed earth mound and cliffside masonry rooms to stone toolmaking, pottery, mound-building and cliff dwellings. Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No spec or TOOL-ASKS.md is needed. Supply printed packets, rulers, timeline strips, printed maps with a scale bar, pencils and record sheets. Every map, source and task has a printed version; a shared screen is optional. At home the adult is the discussion partner. No student timer is shown.

Frame: "Source __ shows __; this suggests __, but cannot establish __." Allow oral rehearsal and dictation. Extension compares two supported interpretations.
