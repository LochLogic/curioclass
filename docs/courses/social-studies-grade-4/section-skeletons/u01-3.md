## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Printed map sets, a ruler and movable paper bars fit the intended comparisons; no interactive spec or TOOL-ASKS.md is needed. Supply pencils, centimetre rulers, a globe or labelled ball, physical and political maps with insets, source cards and a record sheet. Print every map, source and task in black and white; one projector can enlarge them. At home, the adult takes the partner's part. No student timer is shown.
>
> Read map labels together and source cards aloud, at most 60 words each in grade 3 to 4 language. Teach scale, province, nation and evidence with examples. Frame: "Map __ places __ beside __, so __; it cannot tell __." Extension compares two defensible map choices for different readers.
