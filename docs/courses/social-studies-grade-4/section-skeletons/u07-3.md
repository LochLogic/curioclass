## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Supply printed cards, dated maps, record sheets, policy records, proposal paper and pencils. The teacher prints current town and state advice before day 155. At home the adult is the partner.
>
> Read sources aloud; individual cards stay under 70 words at grade 3 to 4 difficulty. Teach culture, migration, exclusion, incarceration, hazard, displacement and policy. Frame: "Source __ shows __; this explains __ because __." Accept home-language rehearsal, dictation and enlarged or tactile diagrams. Extension weighs a different supported explanation.
