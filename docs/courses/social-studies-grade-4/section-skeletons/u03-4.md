Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No spec or TOOL-ASKS.md is needed. Supply printed packets, rulers, timeline strips, printed maps with a scale bar, pencils and record sheets. Frame: "Source __ shows __; this suggests __, but cannot establish __." Allow oral rehearsal and dictation. Extension compares two supported interpretations.
