## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.4.T4a.1** (days 120): Locate the Northeast states on a political map of the United States.
- **MA.4.T4b.1** (days 120): Locate the Southeast states, the national capital, and Caribbean territories on a political map, and add the states' admission dates to the timeline.
- **MA.4.T4c.1** (days 120): Locate the Midwest states on a political map and add their admission dates to the timeline.
- **MA.4.T4d.1** (days 120): Locate the Southwest states on a political map and add their admission dates to the timeline.
- **MA.4.T4e.1** (days 120): Locate the Western states and Pacific territories on a political map and add the states' admission dates to the timeline.
- **MA.4.T4a.3** (days 120): Explain the benefits of statehood over being a British colony in the 1700s, and build a class timeline of when each Northeast state joined the United States.
- **MA.4.T4a.2** (days 120): Use atlases and maps to make a Northeast map showing cities, capitals, physical features, and a title, scale, compass, and key.
- **MA.4.T4b.5** (days 120): Use atlases or state websites to map a Southeast state with physical features, a title, scale, compass, and key.
- **MA.4.T4c.4** (days 120): Use atlases and sources to map a Midwest state with physical features, resources, and industries such as farming, plus a title, scale, compass, and key.
- **MA.4.T4d.5** (days 120): Use atlases, historical sources or state websites to map a Southwest state showing physical features such as waterways and mountains, climate, settlements and movements of Native peoples (including current reservation lands), and European exploration and pioneer settlement of the 1600s to 1800s, with title, scale, compass, and key.
- **MA.4.T4e.5** (days 120): Use atlases or state websites to map a Western state with physical features, landmarks, national parks, and historic sites, plus title, scale, compass, and key.
- **D2.Geo.1.3-5** (days 120): Make maps and other graphic pictures of both familiar and unfamiliar places.
- skill ss4-states-and-statehood (grade 4)
- skill ss4-state-maps (grade 4)

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Supply printed atlases, political and physical outlines, source cards, printed photographs, rulers, timeline strips, pencils and coloured pencils. At home the adult is the partner.
>
> Read source cards aloud, at most 70 words each. Teach admission, territory, reservation, resource and specialize. Frame: "Source __ shows __; this helps explain __ because __." Accept oral answers, dictation, enlarged or tactile maps and home-language rehearsal. Extension weighs another explanation.
