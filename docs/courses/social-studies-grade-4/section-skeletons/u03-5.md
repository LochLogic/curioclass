## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 55, 56, 58): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 56, 58): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.4.T2.1** (days 58): Evaluate competing theories about how people first reached North America, such as a land bridge or a sea route, and the evidence dating early populations to about 15,000 years ago.
- **MA.4.T2.2** (days 58): Use maps of Native culture regions and photographs to identify archaeological evidence of major civilizations, such as stone tools, pottery, mounds, and cliff dwellings.
- **MA.4.T2.3** (days 58): Explain how archaeologists research the past, through digs, artifacts, climate data, and teamwork, to build theories about migration, settlement, and culture in prehistory.
- **MA.4.T2.4** (days 58): Give examples of preserved Native archaeological sites, such as Mesa Verde and Cahokia Mounds, and explain why they matter to the full story of American life.
- skill ss4-first-peoples-evidence (grade 4)
- skill ss4-native-nations-sites (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Closing, day 56:** write two linked paragraphs answering the compelling question from the Archaeology Methods, Migration Evidence and Living Nations packets. Defend an emphasis on combined evidence or on its limits, naming two sources.
> - **Teaching:** model observation versus inference on 43, chronology on 44 and weighing evidence on 48. On 55 students select evidence without a teacher model. Return to the question board after each supporting answer.
>
> - **Carried:** return to map-scale on 49 and 52, with a ruler and bar; on 52 students measure from Mesa Verde to a named Pueblo community, no new exit. Revise claim-evidence-reasoning on 48 to include uncertainty; reduce its frame on 56. Introduce the given timeline on 44 and return on 48 and 57; students place dates but do not construct its scale. Keep unit 2's source log throughout; audience and independence deepen in unit 4.

> **Tools: None.** No spec or TOOL-ASKS.md is needed. Supply printed packets, rulers, timeline strips, printed maps with a scale bar, pencils and record sheets.
