Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Supply printed packets, historical and modern maps, rulers, timeline strips, coloured pencils and a record sheet of 0 or 1 by component. Projection is optional. At home the adult is the partner.
>
> Read primary cards aloud, at most 70 words each, with original grade 3 to 4 explanations. Teach territory, annexation, treaty, immigrant, enslavement and consent. Frame: "The source shows __; this affected __ because __." Accept home-language rehearsal, oral answers and dictation; distinguish language difficulty from historical confusion.
