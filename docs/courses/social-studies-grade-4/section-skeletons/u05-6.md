## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.4.T4.2** (days 98, 102): Give examples of how the United States added states and territories from 1791 to 1898: buying the Louisiana Territory from France, gaining Southwest land through war with Mexico, a treaty with Britain for the Oregon Territory, buying Alaska from Russia, annexing Hawaii, and adding territories such as Puerto Rico after a war with Spain.
- **D2.His.14.3-5** (days 98, 99, 102): Explain the likely causes and effects of events and developments.
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 100, 102): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 100, 102): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.4.T4.1** (days 102): Describe how canals, roads, and railways built in the 1800s helped the United States expand westward.
- **MA.4.T4.3** (days 102): Compare reasons men and women in the East moved west in the 1800s, and describe pioneer life on the frontier, including wagon trains and western settlements.
- **MA.4.T4.4** (days 102): Explain that many groups came to the United States by choice from other places while others, such as people from Africa, were brought against their will.
- **MA.4.T4.5** (days 102): Explain that by the mid 1800s Americans were deeply divided over slavery and its spread into the West, which led to the Civil War from 1861 to 1865.
- **MA.4.T4d.2** (days 102): Explain that Texas, Arizona, and New Mexico once belonged to Mexico, Texas declared independence in 1836, and Arizona and New Mexico came through the Mexican-American War of 1846 to 1848.
- **MA.4.T4e.2** (days 102): Explain that California, Colorado, and Utah once belonged to Mexico and were taken by the United States in the Mexican-American War of 1846 to 1848.
- skill ss4-westward-growth (grade 4)
- skill ss4-mexico-lands (grade 4)
- skill ss4-choice-and-force (grade 4)
