## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.4.T1.3** (days 17): Research Canada or Mexico using maps, atlases, digital sources, and articles, then present on topics such as its people, languages, government, cities, environment, and industries.
- **D1.1.3-5** (days 17): Say why a big inquiry question matters to other people, such as classmates and adults.
- **D1.5.3-5** (days 18, 19): Decide which kinds of sources would help answer the questions, allowing for people's differing opinions about the answers.
- **D3.1.3-5** (days 19, 20): Gather useful information from several sources, using their origin, structure and context to choose.
- **D3.2.3-5** (days 21, 22): Use the difference between fact and opinion to judge how believable several sources are.
- skill ss4-canada-mexico-research (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Hook, day 17:** compare the Country Atlas with the Goods Connections Map. Borders separate governments, yet goods cross them. Who needs to understand these connections?
> - **Co-write on day 17:** Which sources help us (18 to 22)? What can we learn about our neighbor (23 to 28)? How can explanations improve (29)? Why trade (30 to 31)? Who benefits and who bears costs (32 to 33)?
> - **Teaching:** model selection on day 19, context on 20, checking facts on 22, note-taking on 23 and a claim on 34. Students make research choices and select evidence without a teacher model. Revisit the question board after each supporting answer.
>
> - **Carried:** retain unit 1's map reference cards; return to map-key on days 18 and 31. Tie source-sourcing to a note log on 19; withdraw completed entries on 23. Return to corroboration on 22; independence comes in unit 4. Revise opportunity-cost on 30. Reduce the full claim-evidence-reasoning frame to organized notes on 35; keep optional frames.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No spec or TOOL-ASKS.md is needed. Supply printed maps, atlas pages, dated source cards, note logs, pencils and record sheets. Print digital sources with their metadata; a shared screen is optional. At home the adult is the partner. No student timer is shown.
>
> Read cards aloud, at most 80 words each, in grade 3 to 4 language. Teach credible, fact, opinion, specialize, import, export and interdependence. Frame: "Source __ shows __; this supports __ because __." Allow pointing, dictation, enlarged maps and oral rehearsal. Judge content separately from English grammar. Extension compares two supported explanations.
