## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 34, 35, 37): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 35, 37): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.4.T1.3** (days 37): Research Canada or Mexico using maps, atlases, digital sources, and articles, then present on topics such as its people, languages, government, cities, environment, and industries.
- **D1.5.3-5** (days 37): Decide which kinds of sources would help answer the questions, allowing for people's differing opinions about the answers.
- **D3.1.3-5** (days 37): Gather useful information from several sources, using their origin, structure and context to choose.
- **D3.2.3-5** (days 37): Use the difference between fact and opinion to judge how believable several sources are.
- **D4.5.3-5** (days 37): Critique explanations.
- **D2.Eco.14.3-5** (days 37): Explain how trade makes nations more dependent on one another.
- **D2.Eco.15.3-5** (days 37): Explain how growing economic interdependence affects different groups within the nations involved.
- skill ss4-canada-mexico-research (grade 4)
- skill ss4-trade-north-america (grade 4)

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** No spec or TOOL-ASKS.md is needed. Supply printed maps, atlas pages, dated source cards, note logs, pencils and record sheets. Print digital sources with their metadata; a shared screen is optional. At home the adult is the partner. No student timer is shown.
>
> Read cards aloud, at most 80 words each, in grade 3 to 4 language. Teach credible, fact, opinion, specialize, import, export and interdependence. Frame: "Source __ shows __; this supports __ because __." Allow pointing, dictation, enlarged maps and oral rehearsal. Judge content separately from English grammar. Extension compares two supported explanations.
