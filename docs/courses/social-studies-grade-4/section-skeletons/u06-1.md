## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D1.1.3-5** (days 107): Say why a big inquiry question matters to other people, such as classmates and adults.
- **MA.4.T4a.1** (days 108): Locate the Northeast states on a political map of the United States.
- **MA.4.T4b.1** (days 109): Locate the Southeast states, the national capital, and Caribbean territories on a political map, and add the states' admission dates to the timeline.
- **MA.4.T4c.1** (days 109): Locate the Midwest states on a political map and add their admission dates to the timeline.
- **MA.4.T4d.1** (days 110): Locate the Southwest states on a political map and add their admission dates to the timeline.
- **MA.4.T4e.1** (days 110): Locate the Western states and Pacific territories on a political map and add the states' admission dates to the timeline.
- skill ss4-states-and-statehood (grade 4)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Hook, day 107:** compare a historical mill photograph and river map with an original farm diagram showing soil and fields. What can land explain about work, and what still needs evidence?
> - **Co-write on 107:** Where are the states and territories (108 to 110)? What changes with statehood (111 to 113)? What must our maps show (114 to 118)? How do resources, choices and trade shape work (121 to 125)? What makes the national capital different (126 to 127)?
> - **Teaching:** model atlas use on 108, map construction on 114 and source-based causation on 122. On 118 and 128 students work without a teacher model; withdraw the paragraph frame on 129. Revisit the question board daily.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

<!-- skel -->
- days.csv names no tools, supplies or safety lines on these days.

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Supply printed atlases, political and physical outlines, source cards, printed photographs, rulers, timeline strips, pencils and coloured pencils. At home the adult is the partner.
>
> Read source cards aloud, at most 70 words each. Teach admission, territory, reservation, resource and specialize. Frame: "Source __ shows __; this helps explain __ because __." Accept oral answers, dictation, enlarged or tactile maps and home-language rehearsal. Extension weighs another explanation.
