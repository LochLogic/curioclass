- **MA.4.T4c.3** (days 159): Explain how natural disasters such as tornadoes and drought have affected the region and how government and citizens have responded.
- **MA.4.T4d.4** (days 159): Explain how natural disasters such as hurricanes and drought have affected the region and how government and citizens have responded.

- **D2.Geo.9.3-5** (days 159): Analyze how catastrophic environmental and technological events affect settlements and migration.

- **D2.Civ.13.3-5** (days 159): Explain how policies are developed to deal with public problems.
- **D4.6.3-5** (days 159): Use subject concepts to explain the challenges people have faced, and the openings they created, in dealing with local, regional and global problems at different times and places.
