## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 55, 56, 58): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 56, 58): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.4.T2.1** (days 58): Evaluate competing theories about how people first reached North America, such as a land bridge or a sea route, and the evidence dating early populations to about 15,000 years ago.
- **MA.4.T2.2** (days 58): Use maps of Native culture regions and photographs to identify archaeological evidence of major civilizations, such as stone tools, pottery, mounds, and cliff dwellings.
- **MA.4.T2.3** (days 58): Explain how archaeologists research the past, through digs, artifacts, climate data, and teamwork, to build theories about migration, settlement, and culture in prehistory.
- **MA.4.T2.4** (days 58): Give examples of preserved Native archaeological sites, such as Mesa Verde and Cahokia Mounds, and explain why they matter to the full story of American life.
