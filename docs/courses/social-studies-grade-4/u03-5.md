**Q5 Shape:** independent one-paragraph answer integrating the unseen packet's migration/material/community evidence, distinct support from two named makers, reasoning/limit, then a reader's need. Practice revises two supplied linked method paragraphs under a given claim, one faulty link at a time. It never generates an entire argument, a reader-need explanation or the unseen packet's integration. Do not model the complete check response. Cold key: a defensible explanation of how sources support knowledge of the past, two different source-linked details and reasons, a justified limit, and a named user's evidence-based need. Do not count a title as a detail or score claim twice. Meets: "A's dated traces show presence, while D's community statement identifies living descendants. Together they explain more than one source. Neither records every person's thoughts." Importance sample: "A descendant needs an accurate account so ancestors' lives are not invented." Not yet: "We know everything because A and D have titles." Wrong: unsupported certainty/preference (H); titles alone or one source twice (I); liking alone explains importance (N). Multiple supported emphases accepted.

**Print directions for Q1 to Q5:** split long unit prose into sentences of at most fifteen words. Preserve all parts and evidence. Q1 uses a supplied line/table, Q2 a four-row image table, Q3 labeled method cells, Q4 two site rows, Q5 a short paragraph plus reader line. No caption supplies an inference. Total packet, labels and directions together at most 350 student words, not 350 per item.

Shape: complete support under a supplied claim, not generate the unseen assessment's integrated argument; do not use these records in reteaching.
