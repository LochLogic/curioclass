- **E98, Strategic Caption.** New summary: "United States leaders sought greater power in the Caribbean. War ended with Spain ceding Puerto Rico in 1898." Ask: "Give one likely cause and one effect of taking Puerto Rico from this caption." Key: pursuit of regional power is a likely motive; United States territorial control replaced Spanish control. Meets: "Seeking regional power likely helped drive expansion; authority over Puerto Rico changed." Wrong: "Dates alone prove the cause." (H); "It immediately became a state." (A). A likely inference is not the sole cause.
  Shape: infer probable motive and political effect from caption. Practice restores map/date labels and recalls single facts only, never a causal pair or this selection.
- **E99, River Crossing Case, made up.** New 1820 schematic: bridge joins farms to a market. Record: "Deliveries rose after the bridge opened. Fields beside it were taken for a road." Ask: "Explain a likely cause of more deliveries and two effects of this change." Key: easier access supports more trips; trade increases while residents lose fields. Meets: "The bridge made trips easier; deliveries increased, but roadside residents lost farmland." Wrong: "Later deliveries caused the bridge's earlier opening." (O); "Dates alone explain everything." (H); "Everyone gained equally." (R). Other supported mechanisms accepted, not invented certainty.
  Shape: synthesize one likely cause/two unequal effects from map/record. Practice tests isolated links individually, never a full chain or bridge/farm/market case.
- **E100, Transfer Records, made up.** New canal route map crosses a farm strip and reaches a shipping dock. Route caption: "The canal gives shippers a cheaper route." Independent farm letter: "We lost the field beside the route. Our remaining field is smaller." Ask: "Write a claim about gain and loss using a detail from each record." Key: shippers gain cheaper access while the household loses land; route supports access, letter supports loss. Meets: "Shippers gained cheaper access; the farm lost land. The map and letter show different effects." Wrong: "Growth was good because I like boats." (K); "Two map details count as two sources." (Q); "Everyone gained equally." (R).
  Shape: integrated gain/loss claim from two independently made records. Practice writes separate one-source claims and annotates supplied rival claims, never this synthesis, canal/farm case or Q9's historical argument.
- **E101, Review Label and Record, made up.** Label: "Everyone chose to move and everyone gained." Record: "Some people were forcibly transported. Others stayed in place and lost land." Ask: "Repair the label using the record." Key: movement was not always chosen; people staying could lose land. Meets: "Some were forced to move; staying did not prevent land loss." Wrong: "All movement was a job choice." (I); "Every group gained equally." (R).
  Shape: correct a universal movement/benefit label from a record. Practice recalls/classifies individual facts, never repairs a label or uses this record.
