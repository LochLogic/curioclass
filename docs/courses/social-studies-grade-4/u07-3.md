- **E146, paper exit.** "Made-up Southeast: A storm damaged drinking-water pipes. Officials supplied clean water. Residents delivered it where requested. Explain how both responses addressed the damage." Key: Broken pipes disrupted safe water. Officials supplied an alternative; residents helped it reach people needing water.   Shape exclusion: Avoid complete damage/two-response explanations and water pipes; mark separate actors instead.
- **E147, paper exit.** "Made-up Midwest: A dry growing season reduced harvests. Officials offered farm assistance. Residents organized food collections. Wrong: "There was dry weather" (S). **Shape:** Explain slow hazard effects and two responses.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid full effect/two-response accounts and harvest assistance; classify duration evidence instead.
- **E148, paper exit.** "Made-up Southwest: Hurricane flooding closed a highway. Officials marked a detour; residents staffed supplies. Explain how both responses addressed the effect." Key: Flooding blocked travel. Officials redirected travel; residents supported supply access during disruption. Wrong: "Naming the hurricane explains the responses" (S). **Shape:** One fresh regional effect and two-response explanation; avoid composing an effect-plus-two-response account in practice. Supplemental paper check, E148b: "Made-up West: Low reservoir water closed a boat ramp and reduced available water. Officials shared water with a neighboring town; residents used the shared supply. Explain what these responses addressed and what remained unresolved." Key: Sharing and using supplies addressed limited water. Nothing shows the ramp reopened or drought ended. Wrong: "Sharing water creates rainfall" (T). **Shape:** Fresh supply response and unresolved-effect explanation; avoid supply-sharing/ramp records in practice.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid complete two-response accounts, highways and shared-reservoir records; mark isolated actions instead.
- **E149, paper exit.** "Made up: A storm hit a town. A bridge support had a construction defect. The bridge collapsed, closing the hospital road. Why does 'only weather' leave out a cause?" Key: The record identifies a construction defect as another possible cause. Weather alone omits that weakness. Road loss disrupted a connection; legal blame is not established. Wrong: "Only weather could contribute to collapse" (C). **Shape:** Critique a single-cause claim with fresh built-system evidence.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid critiquing single-cause claims and the bridge defect; classify separate cause evidence instead.
- **E150, paper exit.** "Made-up Spill Diagram. Before: occupied district; two towns have available halls. After: spill closes district; arrows lead to both halls, now temporary housing. Explain settlement changes, including receiving places." Key: People left unusable district housing. Both receiving towns gained temporary residents. Permanence and return remain unknown. Wrong: "People stayed in the closed district" (N); "Receiving towns were unaffected" (E). **Shape:** Infer origin and receiving changes from unfamiliar paired diagrams.
  Shape exclusion: Avoid paired before/after diagram explanations and the spill; annotate separate residence testimony instead.
