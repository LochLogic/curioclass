## 1. Standards and practices


This section carries "Say why" (D1.1) and "Locate" (all five regional codes). Admission dates belong to 6.2. The teacher distinguishes a state, a region, a capital and a territory. Use the anchor's five-region grouping, not a different atlas's grouping. D2.Geo.3.3-5 connects comparison of map extents on 110. Never infer political status from symbol size.

Review written grade 3 terms: "big question (a question too large to answer at once)", u01-1 day 1; "supporting question (a smaller question that helps answer the big one)", u07-1 day 141; "region (a group of states that sit together and share something; our classroom five-region key is one way to group them)", u01-4 day 11. Review its "political map shows borders; physical map shows land and water". These are reminders, not new terms.
