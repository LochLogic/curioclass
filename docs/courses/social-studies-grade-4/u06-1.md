**Unit assessment items owned:** F4's importance-to-another-person component. Location teaching also supports M1 and F1, jointly with 6.2; skeleton ownership is stale.

**Record:** shown, shown with help, not yet seen, one scale by component throughout these sections, including 120. Access support alone is not content help. Every exit is individual paper; neutral reading or a scribe is available. No public round is the exit.

- **E107, Exit Work Case, invented for practice.** A worker chooses between a river port and a farming town. "Write a question that would help the worker choose. Explain why its answer matters to the worker." Accept different researchable questions. Meets: "Which place needs boat repairs? The worker needs customers for repair work." Another: "Which place needs grain storage? The answer could help the worker find suitable work." Wrong: "Which picture do I like? It matters because I like blue." (X).
  Shape: produce a decision-relevant question and another person's reason. Practice sorts supplied questions under headings, never writes a question/reason pair or uses this worker.
- **E108, Connecticut Locator.** New outline and separate physical river strip, never shown earlier. "Locate Connecticut and Hartford on this outline. Use the river strip to explain Hartford's position." Key: Connecticut in southern New England; Hartford inland on the Connecticut River. Meets: correct placements, "Hartford is inland beside the Connecticut River." Wrong: coastal largest-city symbol as capital (K); label Rhode Island as Connecticut (L). Source to check: final political outline, capital symbol and river strip.
  Shape: produce a state/capital locator and infer position from another map. Practice identifies a supplied label's neighbor or reads one capital entry, never this two-map synthesis.
- **E109, Two Regions Locator.** Fresh numbered political outline, no completed labels. "Mark Delaware with Dover and Michigan with Lansing. Name each state's region." Key: Dover in central Delaware, Southeast; Lansing in southern Lower Michigan, Midwest. Accept numbered marks and region words. Wrong: put Delaware in the Northeast (L); Lansing in Michigan's Upper Peninsula (L). Source to check: final atlas/numbered positions. No reused grade 3 model map.
  Shape: recognize two complete state/capital/region combinations. Practice completes separate missing neighbors or checks individual atlas entries, never a paired region record.
- **E110, Oceans and Mainland Locator.** New outline with ocean-locator insets, not the teaching layout. "Locate Oklahoma with Oklahoma City, Colorado with Denver, Puerto Rico, and Guam. Explain why the islands need different ocean insets." Key: Oklahoma/Southwest and Colorado/West correctly placed; capitals located from supplied atlas; Puerto Rico in the Caribbean, Guam in the Pacific. Explanation: different ocean locations need separate enlargements; page neighbors are not geographic neighbors. Wrong: Guam beside the mainland because boxes touch (B); Puerto Rico is a state (M); Puerto Rico in the Pacific (L). Source to check: final locator/status evidence.
  Shape: one integrated location record plus an inset explanation. Practice reads individual inset coordinates/status labels, never this four-place record or same island-pair explanation.

**Check Shape:** F4 importance: identify another user and explain a need; no teaching card supplies the final response. M1/F1: multi-region state/capital and territory locator packet; avoid multi-region completed-response packets in practice.
