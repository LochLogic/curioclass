- **E30 (s02-X30), withheld paper exit:** Made-up case: one country specializes in glass jars. Another specializes in metal lids. Jar orders rise.   Shape: complementary specialization and increasing reliance. Practice uses the separate component sheet, never this case.


- **E31 (s02-X31), withheld paper exit:** Use the made-up trade map. Explain the oats flow from both countries' viewpoints, including how larger deliveries could increase reliance. Key: Canada exports oats; Mexico imports. Growing flows increase buyers' reliance on supplies and sellers' reliance on purchases. Look for: Correct arrow viewpoints and growth mechanism. Samples: Canada exports, Mexico imports; growing deliveries link buyers and sellers more. | Mexico exports oats to Canada.. Wrong: A goods list explains reliance. (E); This evidence proves an unrelated claim. (D).
  Shape: one flow from both viewpoints plus growth. Practice uses the separate component sheet, never this case.


- **E32 (s02-X32), withheld paper exit:** Made-up case: a country buys more imported flour. Bakers get steady supplies. Local millers lose orders. Explain the different effects on bakers and local millers. Key: Bakers gain reliable inputs; redirected purchases reduce local millers' orders. Look for: Both mechanisms within country. Samples: Bakers keep baking; millers sell less because purchases moved abroad. | Everyone wins.. Wrong: Everyone wins. (F).
  Shape: opposite effects within importing country. Practice uses the separate component sheet, never this case.


- **E33 (s02-X33), withheld paper exit:** Made-up case: cheaper imported parts lower repair costs. More customers choose repairs instead of replacement tools. Explain the effects on repair services and replacement-tool sellers. Key: Repairers gain customers as costs fall; replacement sellers lose purchases through substitution. Wrong: Everyone gains. (F); A goods list explains reliance. (E).
  Shape: infer connected effects through repair-replacement substitution. Practice uses the separate component sheet, never this case.
