**Assessment packets and construction evidence:** M1 samples one state and capital per region; the individual location records from 108 to 110 cover all fifty. Keep the anchor's five-region grouping: nine Northeast states; Delaware and Maryland in the fourteen-state Southeast; twelve Midwest states; Arizona, New Mexico, Oklahoma and Texas in the Southwest; eleven Western states. The Caribbean set is Puerto Rico and the United States Virgin Islands. The Pacific set includes American Samoa, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands, Midway Islands and Wake Island. Distinguish inhabited territories from uninhabited islands; do not imply that every Pacific island has a resident electorate.

M2 supplies Pennsylvania 1787, Florida 1845, Texas 1845, Iowa 1846 and Utah 1896 on cards. On a line from 1780 to 1900 with equal decade intervals, 1846 is printed. Students add the three missing date values 1787, 1845 and 1896, attach all five state labels and find the 109-year span. Assess all nine Northeast entries in the class timeline on 111: Connecticut 1788, Maine 1820, Massachusetts 1788, New Hampshire 1788, New Jersey 1787, New York 1788, Pennsylvania 1787, Rhode Island 1790 and Vermont 1791. Each child places and explains an entry; retain an individual copy of the complete line. The original thirteen states' conventional dates here record ratification of the Constitution, not the beginning of independence. Do not label Maine a separate state in 1788.

F2 supplies New Jersey 1787, Maryland 1788, Ohio 1803, Arizona 1912 and Oregon 1859 on a new line from 1780 to 1920. Students add all five labels and dates, then find the 125-year span. Dates are supplied because this tests locating events and finding intervals rather than memorizing fifty dates.

Each atlas page supplies a political outline, the named features at readable coordinates, climate or resource information where needed, north and a scale. Response outlines retain only boundaries and two scale calibration points. Students add the requested features, title, scale, compass and key. Keep atlas and response at the same printed size. No completed thematic map is supplied for M4 or F3d. The M5 maps are completed maps for reading only, and the F3 part prompts leave the asked features blank. **Exploration.** The anchor standard MA.4.T4d.5 names early European exploration as well as pioneer settlement; the local standards file drops exploration from its wording.
