# Section 1.3: Political places and living nations

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#social-studies/social-studies-grade-4/1/s/1.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py social-studies-grade-4 u01-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 4 social studies, [Unit 1: North America on the map](s01.md). A plan, not a lesson: timing and materials are ours, untested.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share.** MA.4.T1.2: "Locate" political places and "explain" their terms, days 9 to 11. Coordinates are u01-4's share.
- **Review, ledger words.** State (a part of our country), grade 3 u01-4 day 11; continent (a very large land area), grade 2 u04-1 day 48; country (people, land with borders, a government and laws), grade 2 u04-1 day 49; city (a large town), kindergarten u03-4 day 27. Review physical/political maps from u01-1.
- **New distinctions.** Province and territory are major Canadian divisions; county is a division within the state used in our example. Nation and country overlap but are not interchangeable. Do not make a universal hierarchy with every nation inside a state. Review a named Native nation's present government, adding the explicit distinction from an independent country.
- **Connections.** Day 10 extends u01-1's physical grouping to the Caribbean region. Day 11 reviews grade 3 reasoning and two-source evidence, returned in u01-2.
- **Teacher explanation.** Nation names a people with shared identity; the named Native nations here govern themselves. Not every people has an independent country. Nunavut's public territorial government is not an Inuit-only nation government. A reservation is not the whole ancestral homeland.

  Shape: find two named divisions and generate their categories. Practice reads boundaries and corrects supplied index/category records separately; never the paired locate-and-classify sheet.
- **E10.** "Locate Sonora, Barbados and the United States using the exit index. Give each place's political category." Key: Sonora, Mexican state; Barbados, Caribbean country; United States, country, with correct locations. Wrong: Sonora is a country (E); Barbados is a state (E).
  Shape: three-place index search followed by free category responses. Practice checks a supplied index route and boundary records, not an independent three-category search. No blank with only two possible choices.
- **E11, s01-X11.** "Use the Tucson inset to label each place's kind. Explain how city, county and state fit together." Key: Tucson city, Pima County county, Arizona state, United States country, North America continent. Tucson is in Pima County, within Arizona. Wrong: county contains state (E).

- **s01-P11-E, separate paper check.** "Must Card N's nation or Saskatchewan province be an independent country? Explain each." Key: no for both. ## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Political Map B, index and category reference cards stay available. Day 9 adds province/territory, day 10 revises region, day 11 separates nesting from nation/country. The diagram is an example, not a universal ladder.
- **Ideal instrument.** Indexed political map with readable insets and distinct boundaries. Partly fits until names and jurisdictions are checked. Tools: None; no spec. Printed cards replace a screen; board outlines retain index references.
- **Nation fallback.** Use the already opened Cherokee Nation government paraphrase in teaching if no local source or visitor is available. Never reuse E11's Citizen Potawatomi or Q2's Chickasaw government card. Printed source cards replace live sites; no invented nation.

## 6. Days

Each day block ends in individual paper evidence. Read printed stems once without solving them. Public sharing is optional. Pair turns happen simultaneously across 15 pairs; two 45-second turns take 90 seconds, not 45 minutes. A six-child hearing takes 4.5 minutes within a named five-minute slot. Collect the hand work from everyone else. Give repair groups one priority gap; all other children use written cues. A five-minute group includes a brief contrast and an individual fresh attempt, not four long oral turns. Source cards are paraphrases, credited to their row URLs; made-up people, places and pins are labeled and named aloud as made up.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: province, territory, county, city, state, country, nation, index. Frame: "The index leads to ___." Provide large insets and tactile boundaries. Read labels/cards aloud; dictate or place labels without category cues. At most 120 independent card words plus labels, within DEFAULTS overall. Extension: explain why the invented nesting example cannot define every nation's relationship to a country.
