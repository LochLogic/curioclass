**Unit assessment items owned:** Q6, administered with Q1 to Q5, and fresh component retries. Same scale throughout: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Separate cells, no points or second rubric labels. Access support is not content help.

- **E34, Pottery Case, invented:** Map P sends clay across a border to potters. Workshop Record R shows more pots sold back across that border. "State a tentative claim using one detail from each record." Key: reliance on cross-border material and buyers, supported by clay delivery and return sales. Meets: "Potters rely on trade: P supplies clay, and R shows buyers for more pots." Not yet: "P and R are nice." Wrong: unsupported preference (G); titles without distinct details (N).
  Shape: construct a tentative two-record reliance claim. Practice sorts details under a given claim, never generates a complete new two-record claim or uses pottery/clay.
- **E35, Clothing Case, invented:** Map S sends foreign cloth to a tailor. Buyer Record T shows lower clothing prices and fewer orders at a rival shop. "Write a claim explaining trade's uneven effects, using both records." Key: supply supports production; buyers pay less while rivals lose orders, with S/T details linked. Meets: "S supplies the tailor's cloth. T shows lower prices but fewer rival orders, so effects differ." Not yet: "Trade makes everyone rich. S and T." Wrong: unsupported preference (G); titles only (N); uniform gains (F).
  Shape: argument connecting a supply link to uneven group effects across unseen records. Practice writes a shared-reliance paragraph from established country notes; never a complete unequal-effects paragraph or clothing case. Blank frame headings carry no answers.
- **E36, Article Case, invented:** "This article describes Canadian books shipped to Mexico. The books crossed the border in April. They are the best books." "Identify one checkable claim and a source kind that could verify it." Key: shipment/time claim, matched to dated shipping/border record listing these books and destinations. A map alone cannot verify an April shipment. Wrong: best without a measure is checkable (C); unrelated general map proves shipment (D).
  Shape: extract a factual claim and generate a verifying kind. Practice recalls definitions or metadata on already labeled claims; never this combination or book shipment.

**Q6 Shape:** independent three-country argument, claim/reasoning and one relevant detail each from D/E. Cold key: shared reliance or uneven effects; D shows imported components/rising orders, E distinguishes buyers and competing assemblers. Score claim/reasoning once, source support separately. Practice annotates evidence or supplies partial links under a given claim, never a full three-country bicycle argument.

**Cold packet audit:** D/E are invented records, not actual national testimony. D states Mexican tubes, United States wheels, Canadian assembly, United States orders rising 4 to 7, Canadian workshop shifts/rival losses, Mexican supplier gains/rival losses. E states United States lower buyer prices/rival assembly losses. F's universal promotion lacks effect evidence. These support u02-1 Q2 and u02-4 Q5 keys without assumptions about real employment. Reserve all A/B/C/D/E/F and bicycles. Q1/Q3/Q4 geography keys and port entries remain provisional until final A/B maps/sources are checked. Their conditional cold keys/shapes are in u02-3.

**Fresh retries on 38, reserved bank:**
- Choosing sources: invented playground sound log counts noisy periods; a neighbor's account describes interrupted rest. Choose both to address measurement and experience, not agreement alone. Wrong: only agreeing views (A); format guarantees relevance (B).
- Checking facts: independently authored theater records both give Tuesday opening; their agreement establishes day, not seat comfort. Wrong: liking proves a date (C); opening day proves comfort (D).
- Trade: invented seed-envelope producer needs imported paper and foreign orders; delayed paper can stop production, purchasers and rival envelope sellers can differ. Wrong: goods list without reliance (E); everyone benefits (F).
- Claim/explanation: invented canal-maintenance notes record repairs; freight record reports resumed deliveries afterward. A supported claim connects upkeep and deliveries without proving everyone's benefit. Wrong: preference alone (G); liking as critique (H); titles only (N).
Give each child one component, not the whole bank. Collect source-linked repair/reason. Meets: new detail explained in the child's words. Not yet: copied old answer without new evidence.
