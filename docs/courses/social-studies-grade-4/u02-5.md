**E34 (s02-X34), withheld paper exit.** "Made-up Map P sends clay across a border to potters. Workshop Record R shows more pots sold back across that border. State a tentative reliance claim using one detail from each record."

Shape: Construct a tentative reliance claim from two new records; practice sorts details under supplied claims.

Key: Potters rely on cross-border material and buyers: P supplies clay, R shows return sales. Look for: Tentative claim; distinct relevant details connected. Wrong: True because I like it. (G); Two titles prove it. (N).

**E35 (s02-X35), withheld paper exit.** "Made-up Map S sends foreign cloth to a tailor. Buyer Record T shows lower clothing prices and fewer rival-shop orders. Write a claim explaining uneven trade effects using both records."

Shape: Construct an uneven-effects argument from two new records; practice revises supplied links.

Key: S supplies production; T shows lower buyer prices but rival order losses. Link distinct details to uneven effects. Look for: Unequal-effects argument; relevant S and T details. Wrong: True because I like it. (G); Two titles prove it. (N); Everyone gains. (F).

**E36 (s02-X36), withheld paper exit.** "Made-up article: Canadian books reached Mexico in April. They are the best books. Could a dated shipping record verify the April shipment? Explain using its needed details."

Shape: Judge a proposed verifier for an unseen shipment claim; practice matches labels and metadata.

Key: Yes, if it identifies books, Mexico as destination and April as the shipment month; it cannot verify best. Look for: Source suitability tied to the dated shipment. Shape: judge a proposed verifier for an unused shipment claim. Practice labels claim kinds and metadata separately, never judges this verifier. Wrong: A liking proves a fact. - **R38-B, checking facts (C/D/I/K/L/M).** Independent theater register and opening notice both date opening to May 6. An advertisement calls its seats best. "Explain what these records can verify about the theater." Key: independent agreement supports May 6 opening, not seat comfort/preference. Wrong: best is verified by the date (C); two copies would confirm independently (K). Shape: bound verification using held factual and preference records; practice traces origins separately. Assign only the missed credibility, scope, fact/preference or independence cell; retain other gaps open.
- **R38-C, trade (E/F/O).** Envelope makers import paper. Foreign seed orders rise; a competing local envelope shop loses orders. "Explain how the order change affects these two producer groups differently." Key: exporters gain purchases and need imported paper; competitor loses purchases. Wrong: everyone gains (F); everyone produces alone (O). Shape: explain unequal effects of one held demand change; practice labels isolated inputs. E asks only why the paper link matters; O asks only what a delayed paper delivery interrupts. Print only that one assigned ask.
- **R38-D, claim/explanation (G/H/N/P).** Canal repair log records reopening; independent freight record lists later deliveries. Given claim: reopening allowed freight again. "Complete the claim's support using both records." Key: reopening restored passage and later freight shows use; neither proves benefit to everyone. Wrong: titles alone (N); liking proves benefit (G). Shape: complete support under a supplied claim, not generate an argument; practice marks prewritten links. H/P receives only the missing reason or source-limit cell indicated by the cue.
