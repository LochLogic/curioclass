## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 75, 76, 78): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 76, 78): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.4.T3.1** (days 78): Explain how historians studying European voyages to the Americas use archaeology, maps, illustrations, and texts of the time, which are called primary sources.
- **MA.4.T3.2** (days 78): Explain who the Vikings were and describe evidence of their early encounters with Native Peoples on the North American Atlantic coast.
- **MA.4.T3.3** (days 78): Trace European explorations of North America and the Caribbean in the 1400s and 1500s, evaluate why nations sent them (empire, Asian trade routes, colonies, resources), and make a timeline of landings and conquests.
- **D2.His.1.3-5** (days 78): Make and use a time-ordered list of related events to compare developments that happened at the same time.
- **D2.His.9.3-5** (days 78): Summarize how different kinds of sources are used to explain past events.
- **D2.His.11.3-5** (days 78): Infer who a source was meant for and why it was made, using clues in the source itself.
