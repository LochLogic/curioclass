Short exits are paper from all thirty, using shown, shown with help, not yet seen. - **E155, Advice Case.** Card: "Town Emergency Office. 10 May 2020. Adults should use the marked flood detour." A class could print a notice or link to current advice. Ask: "Identify the advice's author and date. Explain what evidence would help the class choose its response." Key: Town Emergency Office, 10 May 2020; current route/advice verification and readers' access to print/links. Meets: "The office wrote it in 2020. Check current routes and whether readers can use links." Wrong: "Choose what I like without evidence" (H). **Shape:** source an unfamiliar advice card and justify needed choice evidence. Avoid detours, route notices, print-versus-link choices and combined author/date/action-choice answers in practice; separately annotate source status and relevance.
- **E156, Shelter Case.** "Limited staff delayed a shelter's opening. Trained adult volunteers offered help through the official coordinator. Explain one challenge and one opportunity shown here." Key: staff limits delay capacity; coordinated trained help could address staffing, not guarantee opening. Meets: "The shelter lacks staff. Coordinated adult help could make opening possible." Wrong: "Delay means improvement is impossible" (Q). **Shape:** infer a challenge/opportunity pair from a new capacity record. Practice avoids shelter staffing/volunteers and complete paired challenge/opportunity sentences; annotate separate proposal components.
- **E157, Notice Case.** "Only this school's principal may approve shelter-notice changes. A class requested a change. The principal has not decided. Explain who can decide and whether the request is already policy." Key: principal under stipulated rule; no, still a proposal. Meets: "The principal decides. No decision has adopted this proposal." Wrong: "Submitting it adopted the change" (F); "Only a national official can decide" (O). **Shape:** identify authority and status in one new case. Practice avoids shelter notices and combined authority/status verdicts; revision locates documents/missing stages. Explicit authority removes guessing.

- note: sending is the student's and teacher's choice; writing step 5: revise the proposal for its chosen reader; read written feedback 5, revise 20, share 13, new Notice Case exit 2 minutes; score the revised proposal after this chance

- **5 min. Read feedback.** "I can revise my proposal using evidence." Return draft/criterion notes privately. Q gets a fresh limit/opportunity component cue; others identify needed changes. Tools None.
- **20 min. Revise.** First five minutes complete Q repair with fresh annotation retry if needed; fifteen remain for individual revision. No teacher model. Revise a flagged component against policy-stage evidence; check reader, source, location, challenge, opportunity and feasibility. Keep draft, notes and revision together.
- **13 min. Share.** Fifteen concurrent pairs: one minute each to name a revision, two minutes total. Up to six rotating volunteers give 45-second comments, four and a half minutes; record names. Remaining six and a half hold written reflection, optional reader-copy preparation and collection. All submit revisions; no thirty-child public round. Sending is optional after student/teacher choice under school rules; classroom delivery is fallback, no reply promised.
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** E157 alone. Score revised proposal afterward on its separate 0 to 2 record. F/O and unresolved Q go to 158 Return and repair.
