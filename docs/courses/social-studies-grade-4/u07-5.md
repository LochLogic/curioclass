## 1. Standards and practices

D2.Civ.13.3-5: "Explain" policy development through local evidence choice, 155, and revision, 157. D4.6.3-5: "Use" concepts to "explain" challenges/opportunities through a feasible proposal, 155/156, revised before scoring. D3.4.3-5 connects supporting evidence to recommendations throughout.

Application only: source checking, policy stages and challenge/opportunity return from 7.4. Explain authority, feasibility and uncertainty; sending does not make a request policy. Children choose hazard and response from evidence, never a prescribed cause. No purchases, fundraising, rescue, site visits, private family data or required public contact. A proposal can remain in class. Any later delivery follows school rules; no external sending occurs in this planning job.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned:** P1 last-teaching evidence; P2 policy/challenge/opportunity preparation and revised proposal. Unseen P/J comparison stays on 159. The skeleton's last-teaching allocation governs the older Owner cells.

**Shape: P1.** Construct a complete four-stage account from a fresh policy record. Practice annotates individual local documents and missing stages; avoid complete shelter-access accounts and petition/hearing/vote narratives.
**Shape: P2 sitting.** Compare P and Christchurch J, then relate a proposal. Practice develops one proposal and refers separately to Florida/Japan; avoid full paired recovery comparisons and J.
**Shape: P2 revised product.** A chosen proposal retains written feedback and visible revision. These are successive states of the assessed product, not rehearsals of an unseen check. Never model a complete proposal to copy. Score revised reasoning, never sending, agreement or an official reply.

Daily paper checks use shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The revised product has its own 0-to-2 component record: 2 accurate, specific and connected; 1 accurate but missing a connection; 0 absent or contradicted. Keep one scale per record. Print and say that all constructed cases are made up.

- **E155, s07-X155.** "Made-up advice card: Town Emergency Office. 10 May 2020. Adults should use the marked flood detour. Identify this advice's author and date." Key: Town Emergency Office; 10 May 2020. Wrong: "The class's liking proves the advice" (H). - **C155, s07-X155b, supplemental independent paper check.** Keep the new advice record visible. "For this advice, a class could print notices or link current guidance. Explain what evidence would help the class choose." Key: check current route/advice accuracy and whether intended readers can use print or links. Wrong: "Choose because our favorite format is enough evidence" (H). **Shape:** justify needed choice evidence for a fresh communication decision. - **E156, s07-X156, held invented brief.** Reader: town event coordinator. Record: "Festival programs omit the reading tent. Visitors cannot find its schedule." Shape: compose an integrated reader/request/evidence opening for an unused public-event problem. Shape: independently repair an unused unsupported proposal line from one written criterion. Practice revises a self-chosen action/feasibility component; keep the evidence-need repair, park notice and supplied weak line held. Preserve the chosen draft/feedback/revision product separately.

## 6. Days

## Day 155

**Shape:** Practice selects needs and possible steps within a chosen school record. E155 attributes unfamiliar advice; C155 justifies evidence needed for a communication choice. Avoid complete maker/date answer pairs, print/link choice reasoning and the detour case in practice.
Before class: print cards-155.md and evidence-sheet.md; keep teacher-key.md separate. 

- **10 min. Return and repair.** "I can choose a response using evidence." E154/P154/F154 G/Q: comparison five and fresh relevance retry five. Tools None.
- **4 min. Retrieval.** All write what to find out before choosing help, s07 field 4: need, existing advice, alternatives and consequences. Collect each paper; do not assume last year's action happened.
- **22 min. Choice investigation.** Students select a local hazard from a prepared menu of opened official advice, or choose a supplied made-up school record. Mark supported need, source status and response components; weigh feasible choices. Retrieval misses use first ten minutes for a supplied school-record comparison, then twelve for choice. No teacher model. Read actual advice aloud; never carry out emergency directions as class activities. If unavailable, use field 11's invented school choices.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** C155 and E155 independently, two minutes each. Keep the advice visible. Return both plus RET155/P155/F155 in 156; H or a sourcing cue takes priority.

## Day 156

**Shape:** Practice drafts separate proposal components and checks relevance on other records. E156 integrates reader/request/evidence for the held festival-program brief. Reserve event coordinator and reading-tent schedule.
Before class: print cards-156.md and evidence-sheet.md; keep teacher-key.md separate. 

- **10 min. Return and repair.** "I can draft a proposal for a reader." All 155 papers: priority H or a sourcing cue, comparison five and fresh annotation retry five. Tools None.
- **5 min. Plan components.** Retrieve checklist/evidence; each child names reader, response, supporting detail and unresolved limit. No full exemplar.
- **21 min. Draft.** Independently write separate proposal components under the blank checklist headings; no complete challenge/opportunity answer exemplar. Describe existing policy development where documents support it; otherwise identify missing stages. Use location/government responsibility to explain need, challenge and possible improvement. Consult Florida/Japan separately for an applicable principle and difference, not copied actions. Teacher confers by component; student keeps the choice.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** E156 alone. Collect draft unscored. After class, write feedback on evidence, policy, challenge, opportunity and feasibility, about two minutes each, sixty for thirty, ours untested. Return all before 157; no promised oral conference per child.

## Day 157

**Shape:** Practice revises the chosen action/feasibility component. E157 repairs an unused evidence-need line from a written cue; reserve the faded notice, weak line and evidence-repair form.
Before class: print cards-157.md and evidence-sheet.md; keep teacher-key.md separate. 

- **5 min. Read feedback.** "I can revise my proposal using evidence." Return draft/criterion notes privately. Q gets a fresh limit/opportunity component cue; others identify needed changes. Tools None.
- **20 min. Revise.** First five minutes complete Q repair with fresh annotation retry if needed; fifteen remain for individual revision. No teacher model. Revise the flagged action or feasibility component against policy-stage evidence; check reader, source, location, challenge and opportunity without practicing a supplied weak-line evidence repair. Keep draft, notes and revision together.
- **13 min. Share.** Fifteen concurrent pairs: one minute each to name a revision, two minutes total. Up to six rotating volunteers give 45-second comments, four and a half minutes; record names. Remaining six and a half hold written reflection, optional reader-copy preparation and collection. All submit revisions; no thirty-child public round. Sending is optional after student/teacher choice under school rules; classroom delivery is fallback, no reply promised.
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** E157 alone. Score revised proposal afterward on its separate 0 to 2 record. H and unresolved Q go to 158 Return and repair; retain prior F/O only if still flagged.

## 7. Talk and writing

155: "Which evidence supports this need?" 156: "Which source supports your proposed step?" 157: "Which revision makes your reason clearer?" Written task: proposal revised after feedback. Look for reader/hazard, attributed advice, documented process or honest gaps, civic/geographic reasoning, feasible response and limit. s07 holds product look-fors; no reward for a preferred policy.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: proposal, evidence, policy, reader. Frame: "This evidence supports __ because __." The checklist asks whether a step fits authority, time and available materials, without testing a new vocabulary word on application days. Sources share 210 words; all directions, checklist labels and checks share 140. Each summary stays under 70 words. Dictation and enlarged boxes preserve decisions. Read checks without coaching. Extend with a consequence or missing decision record. No child reports home hazards or access needs.
