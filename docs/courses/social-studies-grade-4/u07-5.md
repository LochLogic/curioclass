## 1. Standards and practices


D2.Civ.13: "Explain" policy development through local evidence choice, 155, and revision, 157. D4.6: "Use" concepts to "explain" challenges/opportunities through a feasible proposal, 155/156, revised before scoring. D3.4 connects supporting evidence to recommendations throughout.

Application only: source checking, policy stages and challenge/opportunity return from 7.4. Explain authority, feasibility and uncertainty; sending does not make a request policy. Children choose hazard and response from evidence, never a prescribed cause. No purchases, fundraising, rescue, site visits, private family data or required public contact. A proposal can remain in class. Any later delivery follows school rules; no external sending occurs in this planning job.

## 7. Talk and writing

155: "Which evidence supports this need?" 156: "Which body could consider your request?" 157: "Which revision makes your reason clearer?" Written task: proposal revised after feedback. Look for reader/hazard, attributed advice, documented process or honest gaps, civic/geographic reasoning, feasible response and limit. s07 holds product look-fors; no reward for a preferred policy.

## 8. Practice that returns

155 retrieves written grade 3 nomination/choice work without claiming public impact. 156 returns sourcing/stages; 157 feedback-to-revision. Earlier grade 4 unit returns are planned only, independently supplied. No speed target or personal preparedness test.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: proposal, evidence, policy, reader, feasibility. "This evidence supports __; the responsible body is __." Sources at most 210 words plus 140 prompts/checklist/exit words daily. Read short summaries, each under 70 words. Dictation/enlarged boxes preserve decisions. Read exits without coaching. Extend with another consequence/missing decision record. No child reports home hazards or access needs.

## 10. Two rooms

One child: adult is reader/partner and gives written draft feedback before revision. A school-adult reader is optional. One projector enlarges a source while each child keeps paper. At home follow DEFAULTS.md; no public contact or purchase required.
