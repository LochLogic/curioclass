## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Is every length a number we can write exactly?"

**Hook, day 138:** For eight minutes, enclose the square with vertices (0, 1), (1, 0), (2, 1), (1, 2). Subtract four corner triangles from its bounding square. Its area is 2; what number measures its side? Ask the compelling question.
**Questions:** How do areas bound lengths (day 139)? Why does the theorem work (day 144)? Which infinite decimals are rational (day 153)?
**Closing claim:** Root notation names exact lengths; rational bounds describe approximations. F1 to F6 check the area proof, coordinate triangle and exact comparison on day 156.
**Teaching:** Model square notation and both equation signs on day 139. Teach expression estimates on day 140; day 141's worked comparison rehearses them. Model the area proof on day 144, the converse through equal-side triangle uniqueness on day 148, and two successive triangles on day 149. Introduce cube notation on day 151 before day 152 practice. Model repeated remainders and decimal conversion on day 153. Model bracketing, day 139; missing sides, day 144; days 141, 147 practice. Step back for independent comparisons on days 150, 154 and 155.


**Practices:** MP3, day 144, Equal leftovers: justify why rearranging congruent triangles proves the theorem for every right triangle. MP6, day 154, Zoom and compare: distinguish exact roots from decimal approximations and justify bounds on a sum.
**Rigor:** Eight understanding, four fluency and four application days follow the frame. No owned standard specifies fluency. Use untimed mixed practice on days 141, 142, 147 and 152; maintain 8.EE.C.7 in daily five-minute practice.
**Limits:** Positive rational radicands, small perfect squares and cubes. No required irrationality proof, general radical simplification, rational exponents, trigonometry or memorized distance formula. A failed fraction search is not proof.
**Carried:** Rebuild unit 7's withdrawn place-value chart on day 153 to align repeating blocks; withdraw it on day 154.
