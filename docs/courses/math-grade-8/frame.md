# Course frame: Math Grade 8

Course `math-grade-8` (catalogue id `math-k8`), grade 8, draft frame (2026-11-13). The scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8 units: rigid transformations and congruence, dilations and similarity, linear relationships,
  associations in data, linear equations and systems, functions and volume, exponents and scientific notation, and
  the Pythagorean theorem and irrational numbers. This is Illustrative Mathematics' grade 8 order with one move: IM
  unit 8.6 (data) comes fourth, right after linear relationships, for a same-year science need (reason in unit 4 and
  the source map). IM's optional ninth unit is left out.
- **Days.** 160 course days, one 50-minute lesson a day: 153 in units and 7 for course-level checks and respond days. Checks 1 and 2 each take one day; check 3 takes two, and each has one respond day. Each unit keeps its source lessons plus original launch, assessment, response or mixed-practice days as the source map names. Units 1 and 2 embed a short mid-unit assessment in a lesson opening and respond in the next two openings; unit 4 uses the next two openings for its mid-unit response. Units 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 reserve a mid-unit assessment day and a respond day. Units 3 and 5 each reserve a mixed-practice day; unit 6 keeps mixed practice in its openings after giving its separate practice day to the year-end course-level check. Every unit has an end-of-unit assessment and one respond day. Unit 1's launch holds older-row readiness after the hook; units 2 and 4 launch with retrieval. Spaced, mixed practice runs in daily openings.
- **The time a day.** One 50-minute lesson a day, five a week, 32 weeks: an IM lesson needs a period of at least 45
  minutes (ms-teacher-guide), and the other 5 open the day with spaced, mixed practice (APPROACH-MATH). The pick is
  in "For the lead".
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 8. All 36 grade 8 codes (lettered parts
  included) are owned by the 24 grade 8 progression rows, and every row sits in one unit below. Where a row's
  standard is begun in an earlier unit, the unit text says so (8.G.A.5 in units 1 and 2; slope in units 2 and 3;
  8.F.A.3 and 8.F.B.4 in units 3 and 6). Cluster weights are Student Achievement Partners' (ccss-math-focus.csv):
  major 8.EE.A to C, 8.F.A, 8.F.B, 8.G.A, 8.G.B; supporting 8.NS.A, 8.SP.A; additional 8.G.C. The bar is
  RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md. Math has no check set of other states: the "Check set adds" column records candidates only.
- **Major work.** The scope and sequence states the instructional-minute calculation once, with an allocation in each counted day note. Units 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 own only major clusters; unit 8 connects supporting irrational-number work to roots and lengths; unit 6 connects volume to functions; unit 4 applies unit 3 line reasoning to data.
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, 1st edition (2019 to 2021), grade 8, CC BY 4.0, from
  im.kendallhunt.com, in the source store (S:\CurioClass-sources\illustrative-math\grade-8, docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md).
  IM's lessons are adapted into Curio Class days in our voice, each with the credit line (with Open Up Resources).
  Only IM's open pages are used; cool-downs, answer keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken
  (LOGIN-GATED.csv; DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-13), so every exit ticket, check and assessment is Curio Class's own. The 2nd
  edition (accessim.org) is CC BY-NC and never used. Applets and third-party images are not carried over.
- **Practices.** Each unit names the one or two Standards for Mathematical Practice it builds (in the C3 column), and
  the unit plan names the task where students do them. All eight are placed: MP1 (unit 5), MP2 (units 3, 4, 6), MP3
  (units 1, 2, 8), MP4 (units 4, 6), MP5 (unit 1), MP6 (unit 8), MP7 (units 2, 5, 7), MP8 (units 3, 7).
- **Personal finance, lightly.** Unit 5's "when do two plans cost the same" systems use given, brand-free plans; that
  is the course's light personal finance touch (Cory, 2026-11-14: no course of its own, Economics teaches it), with
  no added day.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 8.EE.C.7 (solving linear equations) is the grade's expected fluency. Unit 5 builds it;
  the daily opening practice keeps it up through units 6 to 8. No tool shows a countdown timer to a student.
- **Readiness and retrieval.** A row last taught or assessed in grade g gets retrieval in grade g+1 and readiness in grade g+2 or later; retrieval does not reset last use. Unit 1 checks m4-symmetry and m6-four-quadrants in the final ten minutes of day 1 after its hook; rebuilding starts on day 2, with signed-coordinate teaching and assessment on days 6 and 7. Unit 6 exponent readiness is in the final ten minutes of day 92, the preceding respond day; day 105 rebuilds and assesses powers before volume. Unit 7 retrieves that same-year exponent work, and unit 8 retrieves same-year coordinates and exponents. Grade 7 rows receive retrieval, including triangle conditions in unit 1. The scope and sequence holds the verified earlier-row register.
- **Paper path and accounts.** Every task has a paper path (tracing paper, grid paper, ruler, protractor, printed
  coordinate grids, graph paper for scatter plots and lines of fit). Any Curio Class on-screen tool needs no student
  account. Where 8.EE.A.4 asks students to read scientific notation "generated by technology", a printed calculator
  display stands in. Calculators are used where a unit says so, after estimating.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh sensitivity agent's read (docs/pipeline/briefs/sensitivity-read.md);
  no unit has cultural, Native, hard-history, health or disability content that needs a named community study. The
  answer-key agent reads every check, assessment and key, solving each item cold beside the script check (BASELINE
  rule 27), before the course is called finished. Word problems use a range of names and settings; the unit plan
  lists the people and places its problems show.
