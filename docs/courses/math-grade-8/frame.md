# Course frame: Math Grade 8

Course id math-grade-8, grade 8, draft frame (2026-11-13). The scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8 units: rigid transformations and congruence, dilations and similarity, linear relationships,
  associations in data, linear equations and systems, functions and volume, exponents and scientific notation, and
  the Pythagorean theorem and irrational numbers. This is Illustrative Mathematics' grade 8 order with one move: IM
  unit 8.6 (data) comes fourth, right after linear relationships, for a same-year science need (reason in unit 4 and
  the source map). IM's optional ninth unit is left out.
- **Days.** 160 teaching days: 154 in units, 6 for course-level checks and respond days (three cumulative checks of
  1 day, each followed by 1 respond day). Each unit's days are its IM lessons as kept in the source map plus Curio
  Class days: an end-of-unit assessment and its respond day in every unit, and in the major-work units a mid-unit
  check, its respond day and mixed-practice days.
- **The time a day.** One 50-minute lesson a day, five a week, 32 weeks: an IM lesson needs a period of at least 45
  minutes (ms-teacher-guide), and the other 5 open the day with spaced, mixed practice (APPROACH-MATH). The pick is
  in "For the lead".
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 8. All 36 grade 8 codes (lettered parts
  included) are owned by the 24 grade 8 progression rows, and every row sits in one unit below. Where a row's
  standard is begun in an earlier unit, the unit text says so (8.G.A.5 in units 1 and 2; slope in units 2 and 3;
  8.F.A.3 and 8.F.B.4 in units 3 and 6). Cluster weights are Student Achievement Partners' (ccss-math-focus.csv):
  major 8.EE.A to C, 8.F.A, 8.F.B, 8.G.A, 8.G.B; supporting 8.NS.A, 8.SP.A; additional 8.G.C. The bar is
  RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md. Math has no check set of other states: the "Check set adds" column records candidates only.
- **Major work.** Counted by days: about 129 of 160 days (81 percent) on major clusters strictly. By units: 6 of 8
  wholly major, and unit 6's volume lessons (8.G.C.9) apply function work.
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, 1st edition (2019 to 2021), grade 8, CC BY 4.0, from
  im.kendallhunt.com, in the source store (S:\CurioClass-sources\illustrative-math\grade-8, docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md).
  IM's lessons are adapted into Curio Class days in our voice, each with the credit line (with Open Up Resources).
  Only IM's open pages are used; cool-downs, answer keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken
  (LOGIN-GATED.csv; DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-13), so every exit ticket, check and assessment is Curio Class's own. The 2nd
  edition (accessim.org) is CC BY-NC and never used. Applets and third-party images are not carried over.
- **Practices.** Each unit names the one or two Standards for Mathematical Practice it builds (in the C3 column), and
  the unit plan names the task where students do them.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 8.EE.C.7 (solving linear equations) is the grade's expected fluency. Unit 5 builds it;
  the daily opening practice keeps it up through units 6 to 8. No tool shows a countdown timer to a student.
- **Readiness checks.** A short readiness check opens each unit on the earlier skill it rests on: m4-symmetry in
  unit 1 (rebuilt briefly: lines of symmetry before reflections), m6-exponents in unit 7 (recalled in unit 8 for
  squares and cubes), m6-four-quadrants in unit 8 (coordinate distance).
- **Paper path and accounts.** Every task has a paper path (tracing paper, grid paper, ruler, protractor, printed
  coordinate grids, graph paper for scatter plots and lines of fit). Any Curio Class on-screen tool needs no student
  account. Where 8.EE.A.4 asks students to read scientific notation "generated by technology", a printed calculator
  display stands in. Calculators are used where a unit says so, after estimating.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent sensitivity read. No unit needs a community reader. A
  qualified math content reviewer (PRODUCTION-PATH step 7) reads the checks and assessments.
