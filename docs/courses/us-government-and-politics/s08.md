## 6. Error register

| A | The same issue means the same debate across time. | Ignores historical context. Day 64: compare purposes, means and affected groups. | Predicted, from memory. |

| C | A promised benefit is a measured result. | Confuses intentions and outcomes. Day 65: compare stated aims with observations. | Predicted, from memory. |
