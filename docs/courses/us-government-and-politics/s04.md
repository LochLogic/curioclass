## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Does the Constitution's design still work?"
**Hook:** Day 23 opens with Article I beside an original representation diagram. Can equal Senate representation and population-based House representation both provide fair voice? Return to the compelling question.
**Supporting questions:** Compose together: Who is represented (day 23)? Where can a bill stop (24)? What authorizes executive action (25 and 26)? What makes review independent (27)? Who decides disputed limits (28 and 29)? Which checks survive pressure (30 and 31)? How much government (32)? Which procedures serve civic purposes (33)?
**Closing claim:** Day 33 answers the compelling question using constitutional provisions, Youngstown, dated executive orders from two parties and four procedure cards. Day 34 tests transfer to an unseen action.
**Teaching:** Model sourcing and corroboration on days 23 to 27 and a legal claim on day 28; withdraw the model for the moot and hearing. Teach institutional vocabulary through examples. Students answer counterclaims without disclosing politics.
