## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What can we change here, and how?"
**Hook:** On day 74, compare a community's invitation to propose budget ideas with Boston's eligibility rules. What makes an idea actionable? Then open the team's local dossier.
**Supporting questions:** Compose together: Who can decide, and whose views differ (74)? What position answers the evidence (75)? What can contributors improve (76)? Which action fits our purpose (77)? What can we defend (78)? What changed or remains unknown (80)?
**Closing claim:** Write on day 78: what can change locally, through which body and action, with what limits? Cite the carried agenda, affected groups' statements and source record; revise on day 80 using the action record.
**Teaching:** Model authority, compromise, two participation routes, effectiveness criteria and two decision procedures (consensus; majority vote with recorded dissent) on day 74; teams record their rule before 77; model a sourced position and portfolio organization on day 75. Guide critique on day 76, then withdraw completed models. Teach local policy, stakeholder, civic virtue and informed action through examples. Explain that compromise cannot bargain away rights. Source author, date, audience and purpose before reading. Connect collaborative discussion to ela:SL.11-12.1 on day 78.
