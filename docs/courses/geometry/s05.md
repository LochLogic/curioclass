## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How much does a shape hold, and what happens when we scale it?"
**Hook, day 82:** Rotate a paper rectangle about each of two adjacent edges. Predict which container holds more and explain what measurements would decide.
**Supporting questions:** How does the cutting plane change a slice (day 83)? Why do area and volume scale differently (days 85 and 87)? How can equal-area layers justify volume (day 94)? Which design meets every constraint (day 100)?
**Closing claim:** Students connect slices, scaling, root graphs and volume arguments to a defensible container model. The layer comparisons, four-family graph record and revised container design supply evidence; day 101 checks remaining parts.
**Teaching:** Model axis choice on day 82, connect tables to root graphs on days 86 and 88, and teach choosing a length multiplier on day 88 before day 89 practice. Demonstrate dissection on day 93; step back for day 100 validation.
