## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What can we be sure of using only a compass and a straightedge?"

**Hook, day 1:** Compare a freehand circle with a fixed compass opening. What guarantees every marked point is equally far from its centre? Weave the retrieval prompts into this first five-minute exploration.

**Supporting questions:** What does an equal-radius intersection guarantee (day 3)? What specifies a motion completely (day 14)? Why is measurement insufficient for a proof (day 19)?

**Closing claim:** Construction marks justify distance constraints; precise motions justify angle relationships. The pattern record (day 22), argument repair (day 23) and day 25 proof supply the evidence.

**Teaching:** Model tool moves before independent use; require labels before reasons. State rigid-motion invariants and the unique-parallel postulate as assumptions before proofs. Contrast a measured example with a general argument on day 19, then step back during proof writing.
