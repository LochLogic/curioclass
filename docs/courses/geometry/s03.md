## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How can a photo, a map and a shadow tell us true lengths?"

**Hook, day 49:** Compare a map rectangle, labelled "invented for practice", with a faithful enlargement and a sideways stretch. Which could preserve true lengths through one scale? Take conjectures for five minutes, then draw a dilation from a marked centre.

**Supporting questions:** What changes under dilation (day 52)? What forces triangle similarity (day 57)? When can a shadow give a defensible height (day 64)?

**Closing claim:** A justified similarity establishes proportional lengths; appearance alone does not. The day 64 model comparison and day 66 proofs supply evidence.

**Teaching:** Model centre-to-image rays on day 49. Separate experimental evidence from deduction on day 52. Model an ordered correspondence on day 54; withdraw proof frames by day 59.
