## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How can algebra prove what a drawing only suggests?"
**Hook, day 106:** In five minutes, compare triangles (0,0), (3,0), (0,4) and (1,2), (4,2), (1,6), invented for practice. Can matching lengths establish congruence when drawings look different?
**Supporting questions:** What does a mapping preserve (day 108)? How does equal distance become an equation (days 109 and 113)? Why do slope rules work (days 115 and 116)? Why must three medians meet (day 121)?
**Closing claim:** Algebra establishes invariants, locates curves and justifies partitions. The median proof, parcel model and day 125 derivations show this.
**Teaching:** Model inputs on day 107 and distance equations on days 109, 112 and 113. Day 117 practises parallel and perpendicular equations, with no new theorem. Day 121 proves concurrence for (0,0), (6,0), (2,6), then with letters. Teach coordinate measurement on day 119.
