# Scope and sequence: Social and Emotional Learning, Grades K to 2

Course `social-and-emotional-learning-grades-k-to-2` (catalog id `sel-k-2`), kindergarten, grade 1 and grade 2 in one band course. Draft, written 2026-11-26 from the frame (docs/courses/social-and-emotional-learning-grades-k-to-2/frame.md). The days are in data/plans/social-and-emotional-learning-grades-k-to-2/days.csv.

## How the band is laid out

- **Sessions.** 192 sessions of 20 minutes, two a week over 32 weeks a year: 64 a year, 40 minutes a week (bar part 9 item 11). Course days 1 to 64 are kindergarten (units 1 to 6), 65 to 128 grade 1 (units 7 to 12), 129 to 192 grade 2 (units 13 to 18). Each year is 60 unit sessions and 4 for its mid-year and year-end course-level checks with their respond sessions.
- **Which standards fall where.** Grade 2 owns every CASEL code of the band (40 sub-skills) and the three library ids SEL carries (AASL.V.D.1 with SM3 in unit 14, AASL.V.D.2 with SA8 in unit 17, AASL.III.D.2 with RS4 in unit 18). Kindergarten and grade 1 rows begin each code (`~` in data/progressions/sel.csv) and own none, so their days carry the begun codes in the progression's form (`~CASEL...`) in the `codes` column; grade 2 days carry the owned codes, plus a `~` code where a grade 1 row is sampled (day 148). Every year teaches all 40 sub-skills at its own depth: kindergarten acts with puppets and pictures, grade 1 adds a partner and a second clue, grade 2 adds a cost, a second push or a second point of view.
- **school_day.** Counted across the band: kindergarten is school days 1 to 160, grade 1 161 to 320, grade 2 321 to 480 (each year's own school day is the number less 160 or 320). Course day n sits on school day ceiling(5n / 2), so the two sessions fall on the third and fifth day of each week and course day n of a year sits in that year's week n / 2, rounded up. The week of a day is (school_day - 1) // 5 + 1 within its year. Every unit and check lands in the weeks the frame and CALENDAR.md give.
- **Sections.** A unit's groups are its sections (APPROACH: a section is one group of related sub-skills), so `unit` reads `<unit>.<group>`; the Check and Respond sessions sit in the last group's section.
- **The session pattern.** Each group runs Understanding (name and model on an invented case, every child tries the pictured phrase once), Fluency (pairs or small groups rehearse until quick) and Application (a new scene card or a real class task). Then the unit's Check session and its Respond session. Every session opens with one 4-minute opening (from day 12 the calm-down routine together with one retrieval item, never two openings), about 13 minutes of teaching and rehearsal, and about 3 minutes to mark the class sheet and put things away.
- **End-of-unit assessment and response time.** In every unit the watched rehearsal from an unseen card is marked across the last Application session (`assess` = `observe`) and the Check session (`end`), pairs not yet seen going first in the Check; the pictured or scene page is taken by the whole class in the Check session, one prompt per sub-skill (one per code part in grade 2; where a watched performance already shows a whole code, it stands for that code and the goal says so: SM2 on day 146, SO5's thank-you card on day 135, RS5 on day 159, RS6 on day 167, SM4 on day 178, RS4 and AASL.III.D.2 on day 191). Response time is the unit's Respond session, always the day after the Check. No unit runs over 25 days, so none has a mid-unit assessment. No score comes from a session where a skill is first taught.
- **Readiness and retrieval (rule 2).** Kindergarten unit 1 opens with the start-of-year check, watched in day 1's circle game after the hook (day 1 carries `readiness`). Every other row a unit builds on was used the year before or earlier the same year, so every later unit opens with retrieval inside the opening, never a readiness check: each Understanding day's `retrieves` names the earlier-year row of each sub-skill it first models (grade 1 retrieves the kindergarten row, grade 2 the grade 1 row), and the openings of later days bring back the year's cards. The 4-minute opening holds the calm-down routine and one retrieval row (the one the day's first new step builds on); where `retrieves` names more rows, each other row is retrieved at the start of its own modeled step in the teaching block ("the kindergarten (grade 1) step, then one more"), and the note says which ("Opening: X; Y and Z are retrieved at the start of their own modeled steps"). A "Given:" item is used only where a school left out an earlier unit (Leave-out bridges, below).
- **Evidence.** A paper class sheet per unit marks a step "seen" or "not yet", never a rating of the child, and is thrown away at the end of its unit, except the calm-down column of units 2, 8 and 14, kept until the mid-year check's respond day (days 22, 82, 149) because that check reuses it under collect once, then thrown away with the check sheet; a check's sheet is kept only until its respond session. Feelings in any check are a character's. Private pages (SA7, SA9) stay with the child and are checked only that the steps were followed. Any child may rehearse with the teacher alone or with a card.
- **Every unit** names its carry-out cue (on the day it starts) and its family activity (on its Respond day), its disclosure route in every unit (health's steps: the school's reporting route and the counselor, on the unit's first Understanding day at least), and its agent reads. The teacher (or a teacher-held puppet) voices every pressure, dare, push or unkind line; no child plays the pushing, unkind or upset part. In pair rehearsal the teacher voices that line to the whole class and pairs answer it in turn; the partner is the coach, holding the step card and pointing to each step as it is done. Where two children simply want different things (one seat, one glue stick, the slide), the partner may play that side from a scripted, calm line, never upset, unkind or pushing. Watched rehearsals and Check days keep the teacher voicing the part. Supplies and safe use of space are in the day notes.
- **Shared cards, word for word (SEL STANDARD section 7, K to 2 form).** Talk turn card: "eyes look, wait my turn". Calm-down card: "stop, breathe" (the balloon breath) in kindergarten, then count to five in grade 1, then a stretch or asking for a break, chosen by the moment, in grade 2. I-statement: "I feel ___ when ___. I need ___.", with grade 2 adding "What do you need?" and a fair end. Refusal second push: say it again, move away, tell. Goal steps card: my goal and one step; then a helper and a check (grade 1); then a changed step (grade 2).
- **Leave-out bridges.** Where a later unit uses a card a left-out unit made, its first Understanding session teaches the card's one-step form in its first ten minutes and claims none of the left-out sub-skills: the feelings faces and calm-down steps (unit 2) on days 31, 39, 73 and 140; the talk turn card (unit 1) on days 39 and 65; stop-think-choose and the goal steps card (unit 6) on days 76, 83, 111 and 118; the problem size meter and I-statement frame (unit 9) on days 153, 161 and 182. Each is named in the day's note.

## Kindergarten (course days 1 to 64, school days 1 to 160)

### Unit 1. Hello, our class

Days 1 to 8, weeks 1 to 4. Talk and helpers come first because every later rehearsal needs a turn and a way to ask for help. Group 1 (days 1 to 3) names the talk turn card and "Can you help me?" in a circle game, rehearses them with a talking object and a puppet stuck on a zipper, and uses them on a new scene card. Group 2 (days 4 to 6) models the "all about me" page, "ask to play" and "I saw you ___" from an invented character, rehearses them, and uses them at real free play. Assessments: the start-of-year check on day 1 (observation in the game, no test); observe day 6 and end day 7 (a pictured page with a prompt for each of the five sub-skills, and "ask to play" from an unseen card); respond day 8. ELA's discussion rules run in the same weeks. Candidate settled: kept, the start-of-year check stays an observation inside day 1's game, 0 days; bar part 9 items 5 and 10 are read on the unit plan.

### Unit 2. Naming my feelings

Days 9 to 20, weeks 5 to 10. Feelings faces first (days 9 to 11), because the calm-down steps act on a named feeling; then "stop, breathe" and the puppet who stops before grabbing (days 12 to 14), in that order because the second uses the first; then group 3, getting ready to learn (days 15 to 18). From day 12 every session of the band opens with the calm-down routine. Assessments: observe day 18 (both calm-down steps on the card, small groups), end day 19 (a pictured page, one prompt for each of the eight sub-skills), respond day 20. The ELA story-elements lean starts in week 7, so days 9 and 10 carry the frame's bridge. No retrieval: nothing earlier in the course is used. Candidate settled: kept the first option, group 3's two Understanding sessions split SM6 and SM5 (day 15), then SM3 and SM7 (day 16), 0 days; dropped the alternative (the twelfth session as a second calm-down Fluency), because the routine already gets four minutes of rehearsal in every opening from day 12 and one Understanding session holding all four of group 3's phrases would break the frame's three-a-session limit.

### Unit 3. Being me

Days 23 to 30, weeks 12 to 15. After the mid-year check. Group 1 (days 23 to 25) models a strength, the private "I like" page and the true action with its cost; group 2 (days 26 to 28) models "I can try" with one step and one helper, and practice that makes a clapping pattern easier, never belief alone. The calm-down routine and "eyes on my work" return as retrieval. Assessments: observe day 28, end day 29 (a pictured page on invented cases, and the clapping pattern said as "practice made it easier"), respond day 30. Candidates settled: both kept at 0 days. The three clapping tries are timed inside the unit (one on day 26, two on day 27, about 5 minutes); the private page's "steps followed" look-for is a class sheet column, its content never recorded.

### Unit 4. Kind hearts

Days 31 to 38, weeks 16 to 19. Reading others follows reading oneself. Group 1 (days 31 to 33) reads a feeling's clue, what each character wants and a caring action; group 2 (days 34 to 36) is thanks, "ask, don't guess" and one class rule (social studies kindergarten unit 1 has taught why groups make rules). Retrieval: the feelings faces and calm-down steps. Assessments: observe day 36 (a caring action from an unseen card), end day 37 (a pictured page, one prompt for each of the six sub-skills), respond day 38. Candidate settled: kept, the drawn picture set is checked for range and for no group shown as the one who guesses wrong, on the unit plan and the sensitivity read, 0 days.

### Unit 5. Playing together

Days 39 to 50, weeks 20 to 25. Teamwork, leading and place pictures first (days 39 to 41, the follow-the-leader game on day 40); then when play goes wrong (days 42 to 45: the two-puppet ball scene and the refusal walk, with the frame's twelfth session as a second Fluency on day 44); then everyone can join (days 46 to 48: stand with, the "everyone can join" rule and Ampe on day 47). Retrieval: the talk turn card (unit 1), the calm-down steps (unit 2), the feelings faces and clues (units 2 and 4). Assessments: observe day 48 and end day 49 (the unseen two-puppet card, the teacher playing the upset puppet, and a pictured page, one prompt for each part of the eight sub-skills, RS3's one way different and one way the same included), respond day 50. Candidates settled: both kept at 0 days. Ampe is written from a Ghanaian education source, with the jumping on a marked spot (day 47); RS6's grown-ups use health kindergarten unit 4's trusted helpers' words, taught in the same weeks (day 42).

### Unit 6. Good choices

Days 53 to 64, weeks 27 to 32. After the year-end check. Stop-think-choose first (days 53 to 56, with the twelfth session as a second Fluency on day 55), because every other row of the unit uses its card; then fix it and check (days 57 to 59, the picture-vote count leaning on kindergarten math's sorting, taught before); then who it touches and the class goal (days 60 to 62). Assessments: observe day 62, end day 63 (a pictured page, one prompt for each of the eight sub-skills, and stop-think-choose on an unseen card), respond day 64. Grade 1's mid-year check samples the unit again. Candidate settled: kept, the class goal is set on day 60, tried on day 61 and checked on day 62, inside the unit's last weeks, 0 days.

### Unit 9. Small problems, big problems

### Unit 10. How do they feel?

Days 94 to 104, weeks 15 to 20. ### Unit 11. Families, friends and me

Days 105 to 115, weeks 21 to 26. ### Unit 12. I can try, I can help

I can try, the practice chart and the private page (days 118 to 120); the shared goal and thinking first (days 121 to 123); then helping well: the three-choice tally, both reasons and who it touches, ending in the help itself (days 124 to 126). ## Shorter path

Nothing assessed and no owned part is cut on any path; every unit and every group stays.

- **Once a week (32 sessions a year, the frame's short path).** Grades 1 and 2: each group's Understanding and Fluency share one session; a three-group unit runs 3 of these, 1 Application and 1 shared Check and Respond (5); a two-group unit runs 2 shared, 2 Application and 1 Check and Respond (5), except grade 1 unit 7 and grade 2 unit 13, which run as kindergarten's 8-session units do, 2 shared, 1 Application and 1 Check and Respond (4); units 12 and 18 take the freed session as a second Application (6); each course-level check shares one session with its respond (2), so each of grades 1 and 2 runs 4, 5, check, 5, 5, 5, check, 6 (32). Check weeks on this path: kindergarten session 11 (week 11) and session 26 (week 26); grades 1 and 2 session 10 (week 10) and session 26 (week 26). All are clear of health K to 2's check weeks (kindergarten 12 and 27, grades 1 and 2 11 and 27; CALENDAR rule 1 counts both) and of every core check week. Kindergarten: a 12-session unit runs 3 shared, 2 Application and 1 Check and Respond (6); an 8-session unit 2 shared, 1 Application and 1 Check and Respond (4); checks 2; units 1 to 6 run 4, 6, 4, 4, 6, 6. The page sits in the shared Check and Respond session and the rehearsal is marked in the Application session (`observe` moves there). The frame's twelfth sessions (days 16, 44, 55) fold into their group's shared session. Kindergarten unit 2's six short-path sessions, keeping the three-phrase cap: (1) group 1 shared Understanding and Fluency; (2) group 2 shared; (3) group 3 shared, with SM6 and SM5; (4) Application for groups 1 and 2, with SM3 and SM7 named and modeled in its first minutes on the center-time task (one-step pictured phrases); (5) Application for group 3, the observed calm-down steps; (6) Check and Respond. - **A year about 10 school days short (about 4 sessions a year).** Four named fold-in sessions a year, each a Fluency session with no `assess` whose rehearsal moves into the neighboring Application session and the next openings; each saves 20 minutes.
  - Kindergarten: day 13 (calm-down rehearsal, already in every opening from day 12), day 32 (the clue game, into day 33), day 44 (unit 5's second Fluency, into day 45), day 55 (unit 6's second Fluency, into day 56).
  - Grade 1: day 74 (calm-down and two clues, into the openings and day 75), day 84 (meter rehearsal, into day 85), day 98 (care and thanks, into day 99), day 122 (goal rehearsal, into day 123).
  - Grade 2: day 141 (calm plan, into the openings and day 142), day 151 (check-in and both sides, into day 152), day 170 (feedback and interest, into day 171), day 189 (weighing and the circle, into day 190).
