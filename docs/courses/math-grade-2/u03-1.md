Grade 2 Math. Unit 3, Measuring length, [unit plan](s03.md). **Days 39 to 43.** Days by kind: understanding 5. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md).

## 1. Standards and practices

- **2.MD.A.1.** “Measure” with touching, equal pieces, then a centimeter ruler and a meter stick. MP.6 on day 41: construct a ruler by marking the joints, then show the distance from zero. MP.5 on day 43: choose tools for a short card and a long tabletop strip.
- **2.MD.A.3.** “Estimate” in centimeters and meters before checking. A benchmark is a known length you can picture along the object.
- **2.MD.A.2.** “Measure” a fixed span in two unit sizes and “describe” why the larger unit takes fewer copies. Keep the endpoints fixed.
- **2.MD.A.4.** “Measure to determine how much longer” on days 40 and 41. Align the starts and label the extra length in centimeters. On day 39, connect the extra span to a unit 2 unknown equation, 2.OA.A.1.
- Use whole units. Explain how two marks bound each interval, why zero marks the start even when the ruler has a margin, and how a common unit makes two people's counts comparable.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- Equal loose pieces become centimeter units on day 40. On day 41 mark their joints on paper, tie the space count to the reading and remove the cubes. Children keep the ruler. Day 42 benchmark pictures carry a known span into an estimate before the ruler comes out.
- **Ideal for showing why on 41:** loose units and a blank strip that shows each joint. Fits on paper. **Ideal for 43 tool choice:** a checked ruler and a meter stick that can lie beside the objects. Fits with verified paper measures. The paper path has no key tool or digital spec.
- Use cut paper for tiles and cubes, verified actual-size paper rulers and a verified full-length paper meter. Substitute plain cardboard objects or strips with checked lengths. Adults cut the pieces and set long objects flat where aisles stay clear. Replace a bad print scale with a checked strip and date any deferred standard-unit evidence. Measure objects.

## Day 39

**Understanding.** I can cover a length with equal pieces. Partners carry pieces and paper records. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** List six names on the ordinary roster. Rehearse mixed mental facts.
- **5 min. Hook and setup.** Mark two pictures of one invented path covered by six short or three long pieces. Ask why both counts can fit.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** Everyone marks the row of touching pieces and orders aligned strips of 4, 7 and 9 units. Children bracket the excess on invented paper tracks of 34 and 21 units. The gap is 13 and the equation is 21 + □ = 34. Hear positions 1 to 6 during Measure. Put these pictures away after retrieval.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Return unit 2 feedback privately. Round 1 offers unit 2 A, D and G in parallel. Round 2 offers unit 2 B and E. Give each child one move per round, with a fresh retry and a dated carryover if needed.
- **30 min. Measure and compare.** Partners cover the same desk edge with equal-length pencils within each set, comparing with another set of a different length. Choose edges and sets that tile exactly. Then groups use the same checked one-centimeter pieces on matching strips and compare their counts. Trace the joints and label the chosen unit. Hear six here.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** X39.

## Day 40

**Understanding.** I can measure and compare in centimeters. Children carry cubes and their drawn edges. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Written within-100 strategy. If more than a third missed a retrieval, begin the ten-minute prerequisite repair here. Otherwise work with the small group while peers warm up.
- **5 min. Repair.** Finish the retrieval repair by retiling, aligning and bracketing. When there is no retrieval repair, use this time for a short common-start warm-up.
- **5 min. Setup.** Issue checked centimeter cubes or paper tabs and a checked longer paper measure.
- **10 min. Model.** Span a 5 cm peg with five touching cubes. Trace and label the spaces. Show how a verified 10 cm strip replaces ten touching cubes, with the same endpoints.
- **20 min. Measure.** First five for X39 A/D cards in parallel. Then partners measure three classroom strips, using cubes and a longer tool where it fits. Align starts, order lengths and bracket the excess in centimeters. Hear positions 7 to 12.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Round 1 offers unit 2 C and F. Round 2 offers unit 2 H and I, with one move per child per round. Date deferred turns and keep them in the unit 2 folder.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** X40.

## Day 41

**Understanding.** I can read the spaces on a centimeter ruler. Children build and keep the paper rulers. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Mixed mental facts.
- **5 min. Setup.** Check the unit pieces and the accuracy of the paper scales.

- **15 min. Model.** Make a ruler from touching units. Mark the start zero, then mark and label each joint through 18 cm. Lift the pieces, shade the intervals and show how the reading gives 18 spaces. Contrast 19 lines with 18 spaces.

## 9. Supports and extension

Say and show **unit**, one piece size used to cover the length. A **centimeter** is the length of one of the checked small cubes. Model its **cm** label on day 40. A **ruler** marks out equal spaces for measuring. A **meter** is the length of the checked long reference. Use **m** on day 43. Have children say “My unit is ___” while pointing. Point to the extra span for “how much longer.” Provide enlarged diagrams, tactile edges and signed or pointed reasons. Keep real measures at their verified size. Read every cue aloud and keep solo reading to short labels. Extend by changing a practice ruler's margin and comparing readings from the same zero.

## 10. Two rooms
