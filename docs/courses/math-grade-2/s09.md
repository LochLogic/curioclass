## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What do we know now about numbers, measuring and stories?"

**Hook, day 148:** In five minutes, show 7 + 8 beside an empty answer box. Which known sum could help? Children mark the box and explain a mental route. Connect their growing choices to the compelling question. This adapts source lesson 1.
**Questions:** How does measuring help us compare, day 150? How can different groupings name one number, day 153? What question fits these quantities, day 157?
**Closing claim:** Numbers, measurements and representations help us choose and check a solution. Use the measurement record, calculation samples and story S, revised on day 160.
**Teaching:** Reconnect ruler intervals to length equations on day 150 and exchanges to written units on day 152. Fluency days rehearse familiar strategies. Model interpreting quantities before sorting stories on day 156; step back for independent construction on day 159.


- **Progression skills reviewed, with ownership retained in earlier units (frame):** m2-fluency-within-20; m2-measure-with-tools; m2-length-difference; m2-length-problems; m2-line-plots; m2-fluency-within-100; m2-three-digit-place-value; m2-hundreds-unit; m2-add-within-1000; m2-subtract-within-1000; m2-two-step-problems; m2-explain-strategies; m2-number-line.

**Practices:** MP.1, day 159: plan story S, solve both steps and check against its question. MP.4, day 158: match each quantity in a story to its tape and equation; day 159 retains that connection in S.
**Fluency:** Maintain mental addition and subtraction within 20, including all one-digit sums from memory, and written strategies within 100. Mix five-minute practice without time pressure; days 148 and 149 allow eight minutes for targeted updates. Record accuracy and strategy separately, without blocks in fluency evidence.
**Limits:** Same-unit lengths and story calculations stay within 100; place-value calculations within 1000. No required standard algorithm or three-digit fluency claim. Two-step comparisons combine accessible subtypes. Extend by creating a second valid story for one tape.
**Carried:** Park unit 8's array. Reconnect tapes to equations on day 150, fade supplied tapes on day 156, restore them for matching on day 158 and withdraw supplied outlines on day 159; children draw their own. On day 151 connect the line-plot scale to ruler intervals and written calculations, then withdraw the supplied scale for its exit. Restore base-ten pieces on day 152, link exchanges to equations and withdraw pieces on day 154; retain drawings for three-digit reasoning. On day 157 connect jumps to equations, then withdraw completed jumps for the exit. Recorded access supports remain available.
