## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How is a number line like a ruler?"
**Hook, day 58:** Compare a ruler with an unlabelled line of equal intervals. What could the line tell us? Children mark matching features on paper. Allow five minutes.
**Supporting questions:** What do marks and spaces count, day 59? How do reference points locate numbers, day 60? Can subtraction travel right, day 66? How does a story match its diagrams, day 70?
**Closing claim, day 73:** "I can show numbers as lengths from zero and show sums and differences with jumps." Records R, N and D below establish each part.
**Teaching:** Model equal intervals on day 58 and arrow conventions on day 64. Compare counting back and finding a missing addend on day 66. Step back during day 70's matching task; ask what each number means.
