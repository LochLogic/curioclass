## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.OA.C.3** (days 132, 133, 134, 135, 136): Determine whether a group of objects (up to 20) has an odd or even number of members, e.g., by pairing objects or counting them by 2s; write an equation to express an even number as a sum of two equal addends.
- **2.OA.B.2** (days 135): Fluently add and subtract within 20 using mental strategies. By end of Grade 2, know from memory all sums of two one-digit numbers.
- skill m2-odd-even (grade 2)
- skill m1-compose-shapes (grade 1, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m2-fluency-within-20 (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Hook, day 132:** In five minutes, share eleven pictured counters between two drawn trays. Can both trays hold the same number without splitting counters? Connect the leftover to the compelling question. Adapt the first source lesson using objects rather than children or food.
> **Questions:** What remains after pairing, day 133? Why do doubles give even totals, day 135? How can rows and columns describe one total, day 139? How do equal squares fill a rectangle, day 142?
> **Teaching:** Model pairing and record the matching sum. Compare a complete pairing with one leftover. On day 135, rehearse why another pair preserves two equal groups. Step back for explanations on day 136. Model rows before columns; on day 141 demonstrate and practise a complete rectangle partition before independent work on day 142.
>
> **Practices:** MP.8, day 136: task P explains why adding another pair preserves evenness. MP.7, day 140: describe the same array by row sums and column sums, pointing to each addend's objects. Day 143 task A retains row-structure evidence.
>
> Balance follows the frame: eight understanding, three application and one check day. Major work receives 210 of 300 counted minutes, 70 percent: 45 minutes each on days 132 to 135 and 30 on day 141. Observation and check days are excluded. Four of five counted lessons predominantly practise major work, 80 percent; by principal new content, zero of one units is major. Preserve explicit arithmetic time; pairing, layout and geometry do not themselves earn major minutes.
