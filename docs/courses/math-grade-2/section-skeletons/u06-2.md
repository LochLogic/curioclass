## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.G.A.3** (days 97, 98, 99, 100): Partition circles and rectangles into two, three, or four equal shares, describe the shares using the words halves, thirds, half of, a third of, etc., and describe the whole as two halves, three thirds, four fourths. Recognize that equal shares of identical wholes need not have the same shape.
- **2.G.A.1** (days 100): Recognize and draw shapes having specified attributes, such as a given number of angles or a given number of equal faces. Identify triangles, quadrilaterals, pentagons, hexagons, and cubes.
- skill m2-halves-thirds-fourths (grade 2)
- skill m2-shape-attributes (grade 2)
