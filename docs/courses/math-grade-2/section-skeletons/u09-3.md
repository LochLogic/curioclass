## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.OA.A.1** (days 156, 157, 158, 159, 160): Use addition and subtraction within 100 to solve one- and two-step word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using drawings and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **2.NBT.B.9** (days 156): Explain why addition and subtraction strategies work, using place value and the properties of operations.
- **2.MD.B.6** (days 157): Represent whole numbers as lengths from 0 on a number line diagram with equally spaced points corresponding to the numbers 0, 1, 2, ..., and represent whole-number sums and differences within 100 on a number line diagram.
- **2.NBT.B.5** (days 160): Fluently add and subtract within 100 using strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- skill m2-two-step-problems (grade 2)
- skill m2-explain-strategies (grade 2)
- skill m2-number-line (grade 2)
- skill m2-fluency-within-100 (grade 2)
