## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.MD.D.10** (days 7, 8, 9, 10, 11): Draw a picture graph and a bar graph (with single-unit scale) to represent a data set with up to four categories. Solve simple put-together, take-apart, and compare problems using information presented in a bar graph.
- **2.OA.B.2** (days 11): Fluently add and subtract within 20 using mental strategies. By end of Grade 2, know from memory all sums of two one-digit numbers.
- skill m2-picture-bar-graphs (grade 2)
- skill m2-fluency-within-20 (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions:** How does a sum reveal a difference, day 3? How can two graphs show the same counts, day 10? Which quantity is missing, day 14? Why can "fewer" require addition, day 16?
>
> **Teaching:** Model matching quantities to equations on days 2 and 9. Model add-to and take-from stories with unknown changes on day 3; practise both on day 6. Model two unknown parts on day 11; practise on day 15. Replace repeated practice. On day 13, align bars before removing the grid. On days 14 to 16, name who has more before choosing operations and drawing independently.
>
> Balance follows the frame: ten understanding, four fluency and four application days, plus one assessment day. Major work receives 720 of 960 instructional minutes, exactly 75 percent; days 17 to 19 are excluded. Days 7 to 10 retain fifteen arithmetic minutes each. Twelve of sixteen instructional lessons primarily address major work, also 75 percent.
