## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.MD.B.6** (days 63, 64, 65, 66, 67): Represent whole numbers as lengths from 0 on a number line diagram with equally spaced points corresponding to the numbers 0, 1, 2, ..., and represent whole-number sums and differences within 100 on a number line diagram.
- **2.NBT.B.5** (days 63, 67): Fluently add and subtract within 100 using strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- **2.MD.B.5** (days 66): Use addition and subtraction within 100 to solve word problems involving lengths that are given in the same units, e.g., by using drawings (such as drawings of rulers) and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- skill m2-number-line (grade 2)
- skill m2-fluency-within-100 (grade 2)
- skill m2-length-problems (grade 2)

Reuse unit 3 tapes on days 66, 69, 70 and 72, then withdraw supplied tapes on day 72. Park categorical bars and plots. Day 68 compares open sketches with equally spaced diagrams for ten minutes; labels, not sketch spacing, record jump size.
