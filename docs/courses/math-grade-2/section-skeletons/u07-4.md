## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.NBT.B.7** (days 126, 127, 128, 129, 130, 131): Add and subtract within 1000, using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction; relate the strategy to a written method. Understand that in adding or subtracting three-digit numbers, one adds or subtracts hundreds and hundreds, tens and tens, ones and ones; and sometimes it is necessary to compose or decompose tens or hundreds.
- **2.NBT.B.9** (days 127, 128, 129, 130, 131): Explain why addition and subtraction strategies work, using place value and the properties of operations.
- **2.NBT.B.8** (days 131): Mentally add 10 or 100 to a given number 100–900, and mentally subtract 10 or 100 from a given number 100–900.
