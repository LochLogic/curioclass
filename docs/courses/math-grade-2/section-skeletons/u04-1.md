## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.MD.B.6** (days 58, 59, 60, 61, 62): Represent whole numbers as lengths from 0 on a number line diagram with equally spaced points corresponding to the numbers 0, 1, 2, ..., and represent whole-number sums and differences within 100 on a number line diagram.
- **2.NBT.B.5** (days 61): Fluently add and subtract within 100 using strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- skill m2-number-line (grade 2)
- skill m1-measure-length-units (grade 1, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m2-fluency-within-100 (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Hook, day 58:** Compare a ruler with an unlabelled line of equal intervals. What could the line tell us? Children mark matching features on paper. Allow five minutes.
> **Supporting questions:** What do marks and spaces count, day 59? How do reference points locate numbers, day 60? Can subtraction travel right, day 66? How does a story match its diagrams, day 70?
> **Teaching:** Model equal intervals on day 58 and arrow conventions on day 64. Compare counting back and finding a missing addend on day 66. Step back during day 70's matching task; ask what each number means.
>
> - **2.MD.B.6:** Represent whole numbers as lengths from 0 on a number line diagram with equally spaced points corresponding to the numbers 0, 1, 2, ..., and represent whole-number sums and differences within 100 on a number line diagram. Parts, by verb: represent whole numbers as lengths from 0 with equally spaced points (days 58 to 62); represent sums within 100 (days 64 to 65); represent differences within 100 (days 64 to 66). Practise all three on day 71; evidence N, R and D in field 5.
>
> **Carried:** Tie ruler lengths to point labels on day 58 and jumps to equations on day 65. Withdraw the physical ruler on day 60 and supplied jump diagrams on day 69; children draw their own. Reuse unit 3 tapes on days 66, 69, 70 and 72, then withdraw supplied tapes on day 72. Park categorical bars and plots. Day 68 compares open sketches with equally spaced diagrams for ten minutes; labels, not sketch spacing, record jump size.
>
> Balance follows the frame: eleven understanding, two fluency and two application days, plus one check. Days 58 to 72 allocate 805 of 900 minutes to major work, 89.4 percent; day 73 is excluded. Days 58 and 59 each reserve ten minutes for unit 3 response, excluded from the major-work numerator. All fifteen teaching lessons primarily address major work, making one major-work unit. No supporting cluster is assigned.
