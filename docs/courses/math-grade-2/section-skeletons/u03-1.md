## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.MD.A.1** (days 39, 40, 41, 42, 43): Measure the length of an object by selecting and using appropriate tools such as rulers, yardsticks, meter sticks, and measuring tapes.
- **2.MD.A.3** (days 39, 40, 41, 42, 43): Estimate lengths using units of inches, feet, centimeters, and meters.
- **2.MD.A.2** (days 39, 43): Measure the length of an object twice, using length units of different lengths for the two measurements; describe how the two measurements relate to the size of the unit chosen.
- **2.MD.A.4** (days 40, 41): Measure to determine how much longer one object is than another, expressing the length difference in terms of a standard length unit.
- skill m2-measure-with-tools (grade 2)
- skill m2-unit-size (grade 2)
- skill m1-measure-length-units (grade 1, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m1-order-compare-length (grade 1, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m2-length-difference (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Hook, day 39:** Invented for practice: two pictures show the same path tiled by six short pieces or three longer pieces. Is either count wrong? Children mark pictures before discussing why the unit matters. Allow five minutes.
>
> **Supporting questions:** What does a ruler count, day 41? Which tool fits, day 43? Why do larger units give fewer units, day 47? Why keep empty positions on a plot, day 53?
>
> **Teaching:** Model unit placement on day 40, intervals on day 41 and comparison unknowns on day 44. Step back during partner stories on day 50. On day 52, model nearest-mark readings for objects ending between marks before plotting. Practise on day 53 without fractions.
>
> **Practices:** MP.6, day 41: build a ruler and justify counting intervals from zero. MP.5, day 43: choose tools for a card and long tabletop strip, explaining each choice. T retains both.
>
> Balance: ten understanding, two fluency, four application and three check days. Days 39 to 54 allocate 760 of 960 minutes to major work, 79.2 percent; exclude days 55 to 57. Days 39 and 40 each reserve ten response minutes. Days 52 and 53 each give plots thirty minutes, separate from twenty estimating and measuring minutes; day 54 gives plots forty minutes and length stories ten. Five-minute facts practice counts once; setup and response do not count as major work.
