Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Hook, day 1:** Show two dot groups, 4 and 2. Ask how the picture helps find their total and difference. Children annotate paper copies before sharing. This five-minute original task opens the compelling question.
>
> **Supporting questions:** How does a sum reveal a difference, day 3? How can two graphs show the same counts, day 10? Which quantity is missing, day 14? Why can "fewer" require addition, day 16?
>
> **Teaching:** Model matching quantities to equations on days 2 and 9. Model add-to and take-from stories with unknown changes on day 3; practise both on day 6. Model two unknown parts on day 11; practise on day 15. Replace repeated practice. On day 13, align bars before removing the grid. On days 14 to 16, name who has more before choosing operations and drawing independently.
>
> - **Earlier-grade rows in the days.csv skills column (retrieval or readiness, not owned; field 4):** m1-fluency-within-10 (grade 1); m1-add-sub-within-20 (grade 1); m1-count-on (grade 1); m1-all-problem-types (grade 1); m1-compare-problems (grade 1); m1-add-take-problems (grade 1); m1-equal-sign (grade 1); m1-unknown-in-equation (grade 1); m1-data-three-categories (grade 1).
> **Practices:** MP.7, day 3: derive subtraction equations from a known sum and explain the shared parts and total. MP.4, day 13: build a tape diagram from two bars, label every length and interpret the unknown. Both return in day 19 evidence.
>
> **Fluency:** 2.OA.B.2 and 2.NBT.B.5 set yearlong targets. Use five-minute spaced, mixed practice daily, partner explanation and feedback without time pressure. Days 1, 4, 6 and 17 rehearse familiar methods. Record just knows, quick strategy or counts; objects support teaching, never fluency evidence.
>
> **Limits:** Here, 2.NBT.B.5 contributes addition through 50 on days 5 and 6; unit 2 completes within-100 addition and subtraction. The 2.OA.A.1 contribution is one-step situations within 20, including all unknown positions, start unknown and opposite-language comparisons. Two-step work and larger numbers follow in unit 2. No standard algorithm, scaled graphs or year-end memory verdict is required here.
>
> **Carried:** Tie ten-frames to equations on day 2, use two frames on day 3 and withdraw supplied frames on day 4. Tie bars to subtraction on day 12; withdraw the supplied graph on day 13 as children make tapes. Tie tapes to equations that day; fade supplied tapes by day 16. Student drawings remain available. Retain both graph forms for data tasks.
