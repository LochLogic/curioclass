## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.MD.C.7** (days 104): Tell and write time from analog and digital clocks to the nearest five minutes, using a.m. and p.m.
- **2.MD.C.8** (days 105, 106, 107): Solve word problems involving dollar bills, quarters, dimes, nickels, and pennies, using $ and ¢ symbols appropriately. Example: If you have 2 dimes and 3 pennies, how many cents do you have?
- **2.NBT.B.6** (days 106): Add up to four two-digit numbers using strategies based on place value and properties of operations.
- **2.OA.A.1** (days 107): Use addition and subtraction within 100 to solve one- and two-step word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using drawings and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **2.NBT.B.5** (days 107): Fluently add and subtract within 100 using strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.

- skill m2-add-four-two-digit (grade 2)
