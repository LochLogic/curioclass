## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.NBT.B.5** (days 29, 30, 31, 33): Fluently add and subtract within 100 using strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- **2.NBT.B.6** (days 29, 30): Add up to four two-digit numbers using strategies based on place value and properties of operations.
- **2.NBT.B.9** (days 30): Explain why addition and subtraction strategies work, using place value and the properties of operations.
- **2.OA.A.1** (days 31, 32, 33): Use addition and subtraction within 100 to solve one- and two-step word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using drawings and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.

- skill m2-explain-strategies (grade 2)
- skill m2-two-step-problems (grade 2)
- skill m2-add-sub-within-100 (grade 2)
- skill m2-one-step-problems (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions:** How does a missing part become subtraction, day 21? What stays equal when a ten is exchanged, day 24? Why do different groupings give the same sum, day 30? When are two steps necessary, day 34?
>
> **Teaching:** Model like-unit calculation on day 20 and exchanging on day 24. On day 26 compare 64 - 22 with 64 - 27. Day 27 models four-addend grouping; day 28 connects drawings to equations. Model every one-step subtype on days 22 and 31 to 33, including both opposite-language comparisons. Model two steps on day 34, then step back for partner stories on day 35.
>
> **Fluency:** 2.NBT.B.5 requires accurate, efficient within-100 calculation. Five-minute spaced, mixed practice maintains mental within-20 facts; after day 29 alternate with written within-100 methods. Days 23, 29, 30 and 36 rehearse taught methods without time pressure. Record just knows, quick strategy or counts; F supplies direct evidence without blocks.
>
> **Carried:** Retain unit 1 tapes and ten-frames for fact repairs; park its graphs. Tie base-ten pieces to equations on day 20, exchange them on day 24, replace them with drawings by day 28 and withdraw supplied pieces on day 29. Tie tapes to unknown equations on day 22; label their quantities on days 31 to 33 and withdraw supplied tapes on day 34. Children's own drawings remain available.
