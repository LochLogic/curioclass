## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.OA.A.1** (days 34, 35, 37, 38): Use addition and subtraction within 100 to solve one- and two-step word problems involving situations of adding to, taking from, putting together, taking apart, and comparing, with unknowns in all positions, e.g., by using drawings and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem.
- **2.NBT.B.9** (days 34, 36, 37, 38): Explain why addition and subtraction strategies work, using place value and the properties of operations.
- **2.NBT.B.6** (days 37, 38): Add up to four two-digit numbers using strategies based on place value and properties of operations.
- **2.NBT.B.5** (days 38): Fluently add and subtract within 100 using strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.

- skill m2-add-four-two-digit (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions:** How does a missing part become subtraction, day 21? What stays equal when a ten is exchanged, day 24? Why do different groupings give the same sum, day 30? When are two steps necessary, day 34?
>
> **Closing claim, day 38:** "I can choose and explain a strategy, calculate within 100 and represent a story's steps." E, M, T, S and F supply evidence.
>
> **Teaching:** Model like-unit calculation on day 20 and exchanging on day 24. On day 26 compare 64 - 22 with 64 - 27. Day 27 models four-addend grouping; day 28 connects drawings to equations. Model every one-step subtype on days 22 and 31 to 33, including both opposite-language comparisons. Model two steps on day 34, then step back for partner stories on day 35.
>
> **Practices:** MP.3, day 26: compare two subtraction methods and defend why an exchanged ten preserves value. MP.1, day 34: plan two story steps, solve and check that both are necessary. E and T retain these practices.
>
> **Fluency:** 2.NBT.B.5 requires accurate, efficient within-100 calculation. Five-minute spaced, mixed practice maintains mental within-20 facts; after day 29 alternate with written within-100 methods. Days 23, 29, 30 and 36 rehearse taught methods without time pressure. Record just knows, quick strategy or counts; F supplies direct evidence without blocks.
>
> Balance follows the frame: seven understanding, four fluency and seven application days, plus one check day. Major work receives 880 of 960 instructional minutes, 91.7 percent; days 36 to 38 are excluded. All sixteen instructional lessons primarily address major work, making this one major-work unit, with no supporting cluster.
