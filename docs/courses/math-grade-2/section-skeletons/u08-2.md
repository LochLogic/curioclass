## Day 137

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Understanding; assessment: observe. Meets new content: the goal is taught here, from a hook and a worked example.
- goal: Arrange counters in equal rows and use row counts to find the total within five by five.
- note: Base 2.8.7. Warm-up: 5 minutes of spaced mixed facts within 20; after day 29 alternate with written within-100 strategies. Untimed; no blocks in fluency evidence. Sensitivity: pair counters, never children; share objects, not food. Year-end facts window: a different quarter of the class gives one 2-minute untimed observation, at most 8 children per day, while peers work on arrays. Reconcile with the yearlong inventory of ALL one-digit sums and mental subtraction within 20; record just knows, quick strategy or counts. Day 145 reuses this evidence, no repeat card set or interview. Focus: assessment/observation or respond day excluded from instructional-minute totals. The code list names the observed facts only; array instruction continues with 2.OA.C.4, without claiming its full assessment before its equations have been taught.
- credit: adapted from IM G2 U8 L7, What is an Array? (keep)
