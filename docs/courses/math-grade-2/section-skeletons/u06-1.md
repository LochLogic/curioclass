Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Hook, day 93:** In five minutes, sort rotated paper triangles and nontriangles. Ask what stays true when a shape turns. Introduce the compelling question; save a paper circle for the clock connection. Adapt the first source lesson's shape sort.
> **Questions:** Which attributes decide a name, day 96? Must equal shares look alike, day 99? How do five-minute groups locate the hands, day 102? Why are four quarters one dollar, day 106?
>
> **Fluency:** Maintain 2.NBT.B.5 in five-minute warm-ups alternating within-20 mental facts and within-100 written strategies. Day 95 rehearses previously taught ruler use, with feedback. Neither is timed; blocks do not establish fluency.
> **Limits:** No fraction notation, angle measurement, decimal money or elapsed-time problems. Day 95 maintains measurement, not a new procedure. Physical coin sizes do not determine values. Day 109 optionally finds the fewest coins for an amount within 100 cents, at most ten existing minutes.
