## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.NBT.B.7** (days 111, 112, 113, 114, 115): Add and subtract within 1000, using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction; relate the strategy to a written method. Understand that in adding or subtracting three-digit numbers, one adds or subtracts hundreds and hundreds, tens and tens, ones and ones; and sometimes it is necessary to compose or decompose tens or hundreds.
- **2.NBT.B.8** (days 111): Mentally add 10 or 100 to a given number 100–900, and mentally subtract 10 or 100 from a given number 100–900.
- **2.NBT.B.9** (days 114): Explain why addition and subtraction strategies work, using place value and the properties of operations.
- skill m2-add-within-1000 (grade 2)
- skill m2-subtract-within-1000 (grade 2)
- skill m2-mental-ten-hundred (grade 2)
- skill m1-add-within-100 (grade 1, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)
- skill m2-explain-strategies (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Hook, day 111:** In five minutes, mark jumps from 397 to 403 on a paper number line. Could knowing the next hundred help? Connect the six-unit distance to the compelling question. Adapt the first source lesson's nearby-number comparison.
> **Questions:** Why combine like units, day 114? Where does a newly made ten go, day 118? What must change before subtraction, day 124? Can different written records describe equal exchanges, day 128?
>
> **Carried:** Keep unit 6's parked models. Tie number-line jumps to equations on day 111; withdraw supplied lines on day 114. Tie pieces to written like-unit calculations on day 112; drawings replace pieces on day 119. Restore pieces for decomposition on day 122. Withdraw supplied pieces on day 125, briefly restoring them across zero on day 126 and withdrawing on day 127. Children may draw throughout. On day 130 connect bar lengths to labelled quantities and equations, then cover the graph for calculation; retain the data table.
>
> Balance: fourteen understanding days, five fluency, one application and one check. Major work: 990 of 1080 counted minutes, 91.7 percent; days 111 to 128 each allocate 55 of 60. Observation days 129 and 130 and check day 131 are excluded. Eighteen of eighteen counted lessons and one of one units are predominantly major work. Day 130's supporting graph retrieval practises major addition and subtraction; graph setup contributes no major minutes.
