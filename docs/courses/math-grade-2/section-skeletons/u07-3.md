## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2.NBT.B.7** (days 121, 122, 123, 124, 125): Add and subtract within 1000, using concrete models or drawings and strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction; relate the strategy to a written method. Understand that in adding or subtracting three-digit numbers, one adds or subtracts hundreds and hundreds, tens and tens, ones and ones; and sometimes it is necessary to compose or decompose tens or hundreds.
- **2.NBT.B.9** (days 123, 124): Explain why addition and subtraction strategies work, using place value and the properties of operations.
- skill m2-add-within-1000 (grade 2)
- skill m2-subtract-within-1000 (grade 2)
- skill m2-explain-strategies (grade 2)
