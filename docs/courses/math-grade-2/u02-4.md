### Exit tickets

- **X34 (s02-X34), 2.OA.A.1, MP.1.** Kim has 83 sheets. Kim uses 7 for flags and 19 for signs. Give a single sheet stack with space for a plan and check. “How can you show you are right about how many sheets Kim has left?” Key: 83 − 7 = 76, then 76 − 19 = 57. Check: 57 + 19 + 7 = 83. Combining removals first also works. 76 shows error F when the work supports it. 63 shows error B when the work supports it.
  - Shape: Produce and verify a plan for two removals on one stock picture. Practice follows a removal and then an arrival.
- **X35 (s02-X35), 2.OA.A.1.** The first tape has 32 total and 7 out. The second adds 4 to what is left. Give the linked strips. Accept a labeled drawing or neutral scribing on the child’s paper. “What story could these two strips tell?” Key: One actor starts with 32, removes 7 to leave 25 and adds 4 to end with 29. 25 as the ending count shows error F when the work supports it. 39 shows error C when the work supports it.
  - Shape: Author a solved story from supplied linked tapes. Practice starts from described actions and has the partner draw.
- **X36 (s02-X36), 2.OA.A.1 practice evidence.** The short tune has 55 beats. It has 18 fewer beats than the long tune. Give two plain tune labels with space for a drawing. “What drawing shows how many beats the long tune has?” Key: Show 55 shared beats and 18 extra beats on the longer strip, for 73 beats. 37 shows error E when the work supports it. 63 shows error A when the work supports it.
  - Shape: Draw the unseen longer amount from a tune comparison. Practice checks supplied labels or explains a written method.
- **X37 (s02-X37), 2.OA.A.1.** A pattern starts with 56 dots. Lee stamps 7 more, then 21 more. Give a pattern outline with space for a plan. “What plan shows how many dots are in Lee’s finished pattern?” Key: 56 + 7 = 63 and 63 + 21 = 84. Grouping 7 + 21 = 28 first also works. 63 shows error F when the work supports it. 74 shows error A when the work supports it.
  - Shape: Produce a plan and value for two arrivals on one pattern. Practice and closing products use stock totals or an arrival and a departure.
