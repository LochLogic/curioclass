### Exit tickets

- **X34 (s02-X34), 2.OA.A.1, MP.1.** A printer has 24 sheets. Kim uses 3 for flags and 8 for signs. Give a plain sheet-stack picture and room for a checked plan. “What checked plan finds the sheets left?” Key: 24 − 3 = 21, 21 − 8 = 13, checked by 13 + 8 + 3 = 24. Also accept 3 + 8 = 11 and 24 − 11 = 13, checked by 13 + 11 = 24. Wrong: 21 after stopping at the first removal (F). 11 after combining the removed parts but stopping before subtracting from the stock (F).
  - Shape: Produce a plan for two removals on one stock picture, with a check. Practice follows a removal and then an arrival. Keep the two-removal stock and combined-removal decision for the exit.
- **X35 (s02-X35), 2.OA.A.1.** Supply two linked tapes. The first shows a whole of 32 split into 7 out and □ left. The second joins the remainder to 4 in, with an open end label. “What solved story fits these strips?” Children label the strips and tell the story with a few words or a scribe. Key example: “I hung 32 ribbons, untied seven, then tied on four. How many hang now? 29.” The linked steps are 32 − 7 = 25 and 25 + 4 = 29. Accept any one-actor story that fits. Wrong: asks only how many after seven are untied and ends at 25 (F). Ends at 39 after keeping three tens beside twelve ones at the exchange (C).
  - Shape: Author a solved story from given linked tapes. Practice authors from described actions and has the partner draw. Keep supplied linked tapes for this exit.
- **X36 (s02-X36), 2.OA.A.1 practice evidence.** A short tune has 55 beats, 18 fewer than a long tune. Provide two plain tune labels with room below, no picture lengths. “What drawing shows how many beats the long tune has?” Key: two aligned strips with 55 shared and 18 extra, making 73 beats. Wrong: 37 from subtracting because of “fewer” (E). 63 from losing the composed ten (A).
  - Shape: Draw the unseen longer amount from a tune comparison. Practice checks supplied labels or explains a written method. Keep the tune caption and blank comparison off the mixed sheet and E.
- **X37 (s02-X37), 2.OA.A.1.** A pattern starts with 56 dots. Lee stamps seven more, then four more. Give a plain outline with room for the plan. “What plan shows how many dots finish the pattern?” Key: 56 + 7 = 63 and 63 + 4 = 67. A valid grouping of the two arrivals also fits. Wrong: 63 from stopping after the first stamp (F). 57 from dropping the new ten in the first sum and then adding four (A).
  - Shape: Produce a plan and value for two arrivals on one pattern. Practice and closing products use stock counts or a tray that receives and gives away. Reserve the pattern scene and two-arrival plan.

## 9. Supports and extension

Read and say **first** for the event before the next action and **then** for what follows. Use **two-step** when both actions are needed for the answer. Children say the words while pointing to the action marks. “First ___, then ___.” Read or sign each cue and use large print, neutral tactile outlines, pointing or a scribe. Keep labels short. Record any help. Extend by changing a practice story's final question so one action is enough and explaining the change.
