Grade 2 Math. Unit 2, Adding and subtracting within 100, [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 20 to 23.** Days by kind: understanding 2, fluency 1, application 1. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md).

## 1. Standards and practices

- **2.NBT.B.5.** “Fluently add and subtract” within 100. Combine or remove like units without exchange and use the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- **2.NBT.B.9.** “Explain why” the strategies work on days 20 and 21. Each sum contains the same tens and ones regardless of which parts are added first. Subtracting a known part from a whole recovers the other part.
- **2.OA.A.1.** “Use addition and subtraction” to “solve” one-step stories on day 22. A square stands for the unknown start, change or result. Choose a written strategy from the quantities and their relationship. A standard algorithm is unnecessary.
- **Connections and practice.** 2.OA.B.2 returns in mental facts. On day 20, MP.3 compares reasons about the same sum. SL.2.1 gives children ways to agree or offer another method with a reason.

## 9. Supports and extension

Point to the complete group for **total** and to the square for the **unknown**, the amount still to find. An **equation** shows equal quantities on both sides. A **strategy** is your way to find the answer. Say it while pointing to the work. Rehearse “___ and ___ make ___” with marks for the parts. Read or sign cues, offer tactile marks or large blanks, and record scribing or other help. Keep only short labels on the page. Extend day 23 by comparing two ways to check a difference.
