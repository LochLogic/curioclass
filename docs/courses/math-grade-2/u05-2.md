Grade 2 Math. Unit 5, Numbers to 1,000, [unit plan](s05.md). **Days 79 to 83.** Days by kind: understanding 4, fluency 1. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md).

## 1. Standards and practices

- **2.NBT.A.1 and 2.NBT.A.3, days 79 and 80.** “Understand” digit values and “Read and write” numerals, names and expanded form through 1000. Ten hundreds make one thousand. At 1000 there is one nonzero expanded addend.
- **2.NBT.B.5, day 80.** “Fluently add and subtract within 100” using place value, properties or related operations. Children choose a familiar strategy.
- **2.MD.B.6, days 81 and 82.** “Represent whole numbers as lengths from 0” and “represent whole-number sums and differences within 100.” Keep arithmetic diagrams within 100 while extending separately labeled counting and position lines to three-digit values.
- **2.NBT.A.2, day 82.** “Count” within 1000 and “skip-count” by 5s, 10s and 100s from varied starts. Maintain the step size across a boundary.
- **2.NBT.A.4, days 81 and 83.** “Compare” three-digit quantities by position on a common scale. Section 5.3 connects this to digit values and all three signs.
- **MP.6, day 81.** Name the value of a space before measuring. **MP.7, day 82.** Explain why the same skip size works on both sides of a hundred.

## 9. Supports and extension

Say and use **number name** for a number in words and **one thousand** for ten hundreds. **Skip-count** means count with the same step each time. For **by fives** and **by tens**, add that many at each step. Review **by hundreds** from day 75 with a row of equal jumps. Use “From ___ to ___ is ___ spaces.” Offer tactile ticks, large print, a straight paper edge and a scribe for directed marks. Read or sign cues and name options. Children interpret the silent target numeral and read short labels. Extend by restoring a reference label within 1000.

## 10. Two rooms

At home an adult reads a label or clue and checks with the same paper model. The child then tries the exit alone. With one projector display one scale while every child measures a paper copy.
