# Section 5.3: Comparing and changing quantities

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-grade-2/5/s/5.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-grade-2 u05-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Grade 2 Math. Unit 5, Numbers to 1,000, [unit plan](s05.md). **Days 84 to 89.** Days by kind: understanding 4, application 1, check 1. A plan, not a lesson. Fields follow [UNIT-PLAN.md](../UNIT-PLAN.md).

## 1. Standards and practices

- **2.NBT.A.4, days 84 to 87 and 89.** “Compare” three-digit numbers by place, starting with hundreds and moving right only where places tie. Record with >, = and <.
- **2.NBT.B.8, days 84 to 86 and 89.** “Mentally add” and “mentally subtract” ten or one hundred from starts 100 to 900. An extra ten can form a hundred. Removing a ten can break a hundred. Adding or removing a hundred leaves tens and ones unchanged. Explain both crossings before asking for mental evidence.
- **2.NBT.A.2, days 88 and 89.** “Count” within 1000 and “skip-count” by 5s, 10s and 100s from varied starts. Apply the work from day 82 in collection counting.
- **2.NBT.A.1, 2.NBT.A.1a, 2.NBT.A.1b and 2.NBT.A.3, days 85 and 89.** “Understand” digit values and zero. One hundred “can be thought of as a bundle of ten tens.” Whole hundreds “refer to” one through nine hundreds with zero tens and ones remaining. “Read and write” names, numerals and expanded forms through 1000.
- **Maintenance, day 89.** 2.NBT.B.5 “Fluently add and subtract within 100” through retained F. 2.MD.B.6 “Represent” lengths from zero and sums and differences within 100 through L. Use equal unit spaces.
- **MP.7, days 84 and 89.** Explain a place-value exchange and check what stayed fixed. **MP.6, days 85 and 89.** Precisely name the unit of a digit and the place that decides a comparison.
