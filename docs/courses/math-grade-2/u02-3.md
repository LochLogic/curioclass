## 1. Standards and practices

- **2.NBT.B.5.** “Fluently add and subtract” within 100 using accurate, efficient written strategies. Exchange or add on when helpful. No standard algorithm is required.
- **2.NBT.B.6.** “Add up to four two-digit numbers” using place value and grouping. Four-addend totals may exceed 100.
- **2.NBT.B.9.** “Explain why” regrouping on day 30 preserves the same parts and total. MP.3: justify both groupings using each original part once.
- **2.OA.A.1.** “Use addition and subtraction” to “solve” one-step stories. Connect each part, whole or comparison length to an equation with a square. Teach the comparison with both language directions. MP.4, day 33: show the shared and extra spans with labeled quantities. The first two-step model comes on day 34.
- **Connections.** 2.OA.B.2 continues in the fact rotation. RI.2.1 on day 31 connects what a story says to what its numbers mean.

## 3. Checks, written first

**Unit assessment items owned for teaching coverage:** M on day 37 and F on day 38. Section 2.4 administers both using [unit field 5](s02.md).

- **M.** Shape: Track the four reserved categories in one market inventory and show a useful grouping. Practice uses pencil calculations and other quantities, away from the stock scene and full inventory form.
- **F.** Shape: Independently calculate the four fixed problems spanning operations and exchange conditions without models. Practice gives feedback, mixes in mental facts or exchanges individual partner cards. Withhold all four problems and the combined assessment form.

Read each cue aloud. Children mark individual paper pictures. Say and print “invented for practice” on story counts. Assign a letter only when the marks or explanation show its thinking. On days 29 and 30 use the individual written sums and differences for 2.NBT.B.5 alongside the four-number exits.

- **X29 (s02-X29), 2.NBT.B.6.** Show a printing plate picture with four dot rows, labeled 27, 46, 54 and 38. “How many dots belong on the whole-plate label? Put your groups beside your answer.” Key: 46 + 54 = 100 and 27 + 38 = 65, giving 165. Other valid groupings fit. Wrong: 127 from omitting 38 (G). 155 from counting 27 + 38 as 55 after losing a ten (A).
  - Shape: Produce one whole-plate label from four row labels and show the grouping. Practice alternates facts and written calculations. Keep picture-to-inventory labels off practice sheets.
- **X30 (s02-X30), 2.NBT.B.6 and 2.NBT.B.9.** Show 17, 28, 43 and 42 on four numeral slips drawn on paper. Give two empty pairing spaces. “How can you pair the numbers two ways to show why the total stays the same?” Key: (17 + 43) + (28 + 42) gives 60 + 70 = 130, and (17 + 28) + (43 + 42) gives 45 + 85 = 130. Other distinct valid pairings fit. Wrong: 88 from leaving out 42 (G). “Both 130 because I added” without showing the same parts in both pairings (H).
  - Shape: Generate two groupings from loose numerals. Practice checks and explains already supplied pairings. Keep unpaired four-slip prompts for the exit.
- **X31 (s02-X31), 2.OA.A.1 and 2.NBT.B.5.** A booklet has room for 41 pictures. It has 17 pictures pasted in. “Which card shows the empty spaces?” Each card has a tape and the work beside it. One shows a whole of 41 split into 17 and □, with 41 = 30 + 11 and 41 − 17 = 24. One joins 41 and 17 under an unknown whole and adds to get 58. One uses the correct tape but writes 41 = 40 + 11 and takes away 17 to get 34. Vary card order. Key: the split tape with 24 empty spaces. Wrong: traces a joined whole and adds for 58 (D). Keeps four tens alongside eleven ones and chooses 34 (C). Score the single card choice. Record a letter only if the child's own marks or words show the reason.
  - Shape: Choose one complete tape-and-calculation card for unused space. Practice builds tapes and computes from object counts. Reserve the booklet and complete choice cards.
- **X32 (s02-X32), 2.OA.A.1.** A rail holds 87 swatches, small pieces of cloth. Each piece is smooth or has ridges. “Which strip could show all the swatches?” Show three equal outlines labeled 87, with candidate part labels (42, 46), (42, 45), and (41, 45). Read each pair of labels aloud. Give the child space to mark how the two parts fit. Key: the strip with 42 + 45 = 87. Score the match. A choice alone leaves the child's reasoning open. Wrong: accepts 42 + 46, which makes 88 (I). Accepts 41 + 45, which makes 86 (I).
  - Shape: Verify candidate partitions of a fixed whole. Practice makes a split or solves one covered quantity. Keep choice sets with competing proposed splits off practice.
- **X33 (s02-X33), 2.OA.A.1 and 2.NBT.B.5.** Give a plain picture of a left and a right pattern, with no countable notches or drawn comparison lengths. Read the caption aloud: “The left has 92 notches. It has 38 more than the right.” “How many notches are on the right? Show it in a labeled number sentence.” Key: □ right + 38 extra = 92 left, so 92 − 38 = 54 right. A labeled equivalent equation that shows the unknown is valid. Wrong: 130 from adding because of “more” (E). 66 from reversing the ones (B). 64 from keeping nine tens after exchanging (C).
  - Shape: Build an equation from an opposite-language caption with no supplied comparison lengths. Practice calculates from given labeled equations or independently sketches a different strip relation. Reserve the pattern caption.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- Withdraw supplied base-ten pieces on day 29. Children carry the day 27 grouping method and their own drawings into written practice.
- Ideal on days 31 to 33: one proportional paper tape per whole, with aligned starts for comparison and enough space for amount labels. It fits because the same quantities can be traced into the equation. The digital specification does not exist. Re-establish the labeled tape on fresh paper, then let children make their own comparison tapes. Supplied teaching tapes are withdrawn on day 34. Draw a blank strip if a paper master is missing. No key tool.

## Day 30

**Fluency.** I can show why the same four numbers give the same total in different pairs. Children hold their own grouping records. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Written strategies within 100.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** X29 A and G, five minutes each.

## Day 31

**Application.** I can pick a drawing that shows why my story calculation needs a trade. The teacher starts a tape and children mark or draw their own. Paper tape (light), show why, five minutes of Model.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Mental facts.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** X30 G and H, five minutes each.
- **5 min. Story questions.** RI.2.1: use separate who, what and how-many cues on the read-aloud. Each child marks the quantity a cue identifies before the next.
- **15 min. Model.** Rin sorts an invented packet into 18 stamped sheets and 26 plain sheets, making 44. Show the two groups, then connect each tape segment with its part. Hide the total and then hide the plain part. Compare 18 + 26 = 44 with 44 − 18 = 26. For the subtraction, cross out one of the four tens and mark ten new ones before removing 18.
- **20 min. Practice.** Build tapes from sorted paper marks or drawn objects. For a comparison, give facts on separate labels and have children draw independent aligned tapes. Labels of 47 and 19 stamps give an extra 28 by 47 − 19; 19 + 28 checks 47. Hear positions 1 to 6.
- **5 min. Exit.** X31.

## Day 32

**Application.** I can use a tape to find the unknown part or amount. Children hold their tape paper. Paper tape (light), show why, five minutes of Model.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Written strategies.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** X31 D and C, five minutes each.
- **15 min. Model.** Generate a split of 73 such as 51 + 22. A second example, 50 + 23, fills the same whole. In a separate comparison, match rows of 51 and 22 to show a gap of 29 from either direction. Cover the larger label in the “29 more” relation: 22 + 29 = 51. Cover the smaller label in the “29 fewer” relation: 51 − 29 = 22. In Practice, read two different cases on separate slips. One wall has 59 stars and another has 24. Ask how many more stars the first wall has (35). On a fresh slip one board has 41 pins and another has 72. Ask how many fewer pins the first board has (31). Children draw their own strips. Save each response before giving feedback.
- **25 min. Practice.** Match familiar part-whole scenes to paper models, then solve a covered quantity and write its equation. Let children choose where to cut a practice strip of 73 with the first part between 49 and 52. Screen any further free-split whole against exit and assessment totals before offering it. For comparisons, children draw their own before checking it with a partner. Hear positions 7 to 12.
- **5 min. Exit.** X32.

## Day 33

**Application.** I can label a story's numbers in an equation and trade a ten to find the missing number. Children carry their own labels and drawings. Paper tape (light), show why, five minutes of Model.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Mental facts.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** X32 I.
- **20 min. Model.** Recall the name Day 14 Al and Bo tabs, then establish the relation using fresh loops. Al's label says 36 loops, 27 fewer than Bo's. In a separate version Bo's label says 63 loops, 27 more than Al's. Name the larger amount in each wording. Recover Bo's 63 with 36 + 27 and Al's 36 with 63 − 27. Match labels to the two quantities and the extra span before calculating.
- **25 min. Practice.** Children label shared and extra spans on sketches they make for other comparisons. Then work from supplied labeled equations to calculate with exchange. Hear positions 13 to 18.
- **5 min. Exit.** X33. Send E, B and C to day 34.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 30, “Which four parts stayed?” Day 33, “Whose strip extends?” On day 33, write a labeled equation from the class model. Look for a label on each quantity, the unknown in its correct place, and an answer that fits the relationship.

## 8. Practice that returns

- Day 29 brings back the day 28 exchange and day 27 grouping. Day 30 returns to two taught pairings with immediate feedback. Days 31 to 33 bring back the Unit 1 section 1.3 tape and owner routine, and day 22's square. Grade 1 section 6.4 and Unit 1 section 1.3 ledger targets are planned only. Demonstrate on fresh paper first.
- Alternate five-minute written within-100 work with mixed mental facts from day 30. Efficient, accurate calculation within 100 is the fluency target. Give untimed, spaced partner turns. Keep the four fixed fluency calculations private.
- Revoice kindergarten **more** and **fewer** through matching. After complete matching, the larger group has extras. The group with fewer has a match for each of its members, with some members of the other group left over. Extend that reasoning by marking the extra span.

## 9. Supports and extension

Rehearse “___ has ___ extra,” pointing to the whole and the excess. Read or sign each prompt. Use large labels, tactile blank strips or a neutral scribe, keeping the comparison construction with the learner. Record any support. Extend with another way to group and justify the day 30 practice addends during ten minutes of Grouping.

## 10. Two rooms

At home, cover one label on a plain paper tape and take turns finding its quantity. With a projector, use the screen for practice and hide it while each child marks a private exit picture.
