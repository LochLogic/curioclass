## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What held the Roman Empire together?"
- **Hook, day 134.** Put a Roman coin drawing beside the surviving aqueducts. Ask what connects payment, water and imperial power before retrieving the budget.
- **Supporting questions.** Write together: Who paid for government (134)? How did trade connect provinces (135 and 136)? Why could money lose purchasing power (137 and 138)? What kept public works working (139 and 140)? How did language travel (141 and 142)?
- **Closing claim, day 143, item 7.** Explain what held the empire together, combining the unseen Frontinus maintenance account with the trade map and coin evidence. Explain a cost and a source limit; accept different supported priorities.
- **Teaching moves.** Model sourcing on 134, a trade causal chain on 135 and comparing affected groups on 136. Guide the money model on 137, then withdraw completed explanations on 138. Model engineering inference on 139 and word analysis on 141; step back on 142. Teach tax, customs duty, currency, inflation, deflation, unemployment, aqueduct, arch, concrete, Latin and Romance languages in context.
