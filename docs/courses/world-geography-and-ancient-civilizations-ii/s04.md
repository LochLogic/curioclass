## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Who gained and who paid when China was united and the Silk Roads opened?"
- **Hook, day 38.** Meet the Qin bronze measure and its decree claiming peace through conquest. Ask whose experience the decree leaves out; post the compelling question.
- **Supporting questions.** Write together: How did Qin rulers impose unity (38 and 39)? What changed in writing and technology (40 and 41)? How did people connect regions (42 to 44)? What changed prices and opportunities (45 to 48)?
- **Closing claim, day 49.** Use the measure, Li Si's book-burning memorial, the Periplus and the route map. Compare gains and costs for rulers, traders and workers, distinguish Qin from Han, and acknowledge another reading and a source limit.
- **Teaching moves.** Model sourcing and causation on day 39, map and text comparison on 43, graphs on 45 and a sourced claim on 46. Withdraw frames on 49. Teach conscription, standardization, character, intermediary, scarcity, supply, demand, interest and exchange rate through examples.

On day 45, label price vertically and quantity horizontally, identify buyers and sellers, and distinguish a curve shift from movement along it. On days 47 and 48, state which conditions stay fixed and define wages, interest and exchange-rate units before predicting outcomes.
