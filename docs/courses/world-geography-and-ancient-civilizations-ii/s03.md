## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What held early China together: its land, its rulers or its teachers?"
- **Hook, day 26.** Show oracle bone 67.43.16 beside a river map. Ask how land and a ruler's questions might connect. Post the compelling question before map teaching.
- **Supporting questions.** Write these together: Where could people settle (days 26 to 29)? What did Shang rulers record (days 30 and 31)? What continued under the Zhou (day 32)? Who learned, and why (day 33)? How could teachers restore order (day 34)?
- **Closing claim, day 35.** Answer from the river map, oracle inscriptions 8 and 9, the school history and Analects 12:11. Defend one factor, acknowledge another and explain an evidence limit.
- **Teaching moves.** Model map comparison on day 26, causal chains on day 28, sourcing on day 30 and a claim on day 31. Pairs source independently on day 31; withdraw writing frames on day 35. Day 34 starts with rival states and weakened kings before teachers' remedies. Teach loess, dynasty, divination, mandate, virtue, filial piety and Dao through examples.

Caption: sayings attributed to Confucius, who lived about 551 to 479 BCE; compiled by followers after his death, for learners and those concerned with governing.
