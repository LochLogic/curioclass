New packet, "invented for practice": A, a keyed map headed Pine Coast survey, shows liquid ocean along the coast, liquid river in a valley and solid ice on high ground; B, a picture log headed Pine Coast observations, shows liquid lake and pond in low hollows and solid snow on a ridge; C, a picture log headed Other Cove observations, shows liquid sea and solid shore ice.
