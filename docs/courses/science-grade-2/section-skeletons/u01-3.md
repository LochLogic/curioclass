> | 2 | Per group: storage tray, damp clean play sand, three smooth large pebbles, small cup of tap water and cloth. Adult checks sand, edges and worktops the day before. Everyone nearby wears splash goggles throughout. Keep material below faces, never throw or taste, wipe spills immediately and wash hands. Adult removes sand into waste, never drains. Allow five clean-up minutes. |
> | 4 | Per group: site photographs, five map sheets and pencils. Adult checks paths, weather, insects and allergies the day before; use photographs if unsafe. > | 7 | Per group: two sealed plastic cups, one with ice and one with liquid water, tray, cloth and five source sheets. Adult checks seals the day before and prepares fresh ice before class. Handle briefly, never open or taste, wipe condensation. Use goggles during handling; adult empties cups. Allow four clean-up minutes. |
