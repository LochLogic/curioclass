## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2-PS1-4** (days 43, 44, 46, 47): Argue, using evidence, that heating or cooling causes some changes that can be undone and some that cannot.
- **2-PS1-3** (days 45, 46, 47): Observe and explain, with evidence, how something built from a few kinds of pieces can be taken apart and rebuilt as something new.
- skill s2-heat-cool-change (grade 2)
- skill s2-pieces-new-object (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> On day 47, write the class explanation: rearrangement reuses pieces; heating and cooling sometimes restore observed properties, but cooling did not uncook the egg. Cite days 40 to 44, including attempted returns.
>
> Group the day 39 question board: Where did the castle pieces go (40)? Can another set make different objects (41)? Does changing temperature back restore things (42)? Does that always work (43)? What evidence settles a disagreement (44)? Each answer raises the next question. Transfer uses unfamiliar card rocket and bus builds and new butter, cookie, juice and cucumber records.
> Carried: retain unit 3's property-to-purpose reasoning. Day 39 starts a parts-and-whole sketch and a change-arrow model with claim, observation and reason. Day 41 revises the parts model using two sets: shape and stiffness support different uses. Day 42 adds return arrows from water and chocolate evidence. Day 43 adds lasting branches from egg, leaf and paper records. Day 44's consensus joins the evidence-based account, parts-and-whole system and heating-or-cooling causes. Children use these models on days 45 and 46; day 47 repairs them.
