## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2-LS4-1** (days 70, 71, 72, 73): Observe plants and animals and compare how many kinds of living things are found in different habitats.
- skill s2-habitat-diversity (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 71's shared written explanation answers the anchor with both dated inventories: which kinds overlapped, which differed and what repeated counts support. Report inconclusive comparisons honestly.
>
> Question board: What lives here (66)? Does seeing more individuals mean more kinds (67)? Are other habitats alike (pond and forest, 68)? Can equal counts hide differences (desert and ocean shore, 69)? Would another survey agree (70)? What can we claim (71)? Each answer raises the next question. Day 72 transfers to unseen meadow, stream-edge and marsh scenes.
> Carried: day 66 begins a habitat picture model joining living things to observed places and recalled resources. Day 67 revises its graph to count kinds once. Days 68 and 69 revise patterns using shared kinds and equal-count contrasts. Day 70 adds a second dated inventory; day 71's consensus qualifies evidence-based claims with search limits. Systems, graph, patterns and claim-evidence reasoning remain available; day 72 uses this representation on new scenes.

Day 70 uses three for recall, four for preparation and movement, ten for surveying, eleven for comparison, three for modelling and four for cleanup. Each totals 35 minutes.
>
> The day before each survey, the teacher checks paths, weather, stinging insects, harmful plants, allergies and access. Stop for unsafe conditions; use indoor matched two-date photographs instead. Wear closed shoes; stay in sight on paths; touch, move and collect nothing. No splashing or flying material is planned, so goggles are unnecessary. Only adults inspect sites. Cleanup includes handwashing and checking clothing for insects.
>
