## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2-ESS2-1** (days 16, 17, 18, 19, 20): Compare several designed solutions that slow or stop wind or water from reshaping the land.
- **K-2-ETS1-3** (days 16, 17, 18, 19, 20): Use test results from two objects built for the same problem to compare what each does well and poorly.
- skill s2-erosion-solutions (grade 2)
- skill s2-compare-designs (grade 2)

> | Day | Prompt and answer |
> |---|---|
> | 20 | An unworked picture pair shows a curved screen and a staggered row of posts protecting the same garden strip. "Sketch both shapes and explain how to compare their protection fairly." Accept equal ground, fan position and trial conditions, measured retreat and shapes altering wind movement. |
>
> Carried: retain runoff arrows and source labels. Revise day 11's sketch on days 14 and 15 from slow and fast evidence. Sketch protection day 16, test day 17, improve day 18, revise from wind day 21. Strips become bars day 19; add wind day 22. Use models days 23 to 25; combine path evidence day 26.
> Day 16 introduces occupied-path width and two limits; day 19 graphs retreat, then models the space tradeoff. Day 22 independently compares a field's windbreak (retreat 2 then 3, width 7) with a wall (retreat 5 then 6, width 4), in centimeters. Limits: retreat at most 6, width at most 5. Only the wall meets both; it protects less. Case invented for practice.
