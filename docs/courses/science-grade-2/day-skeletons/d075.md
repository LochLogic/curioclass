## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Plan a fresh light and water investigation and carry out and record its starting measurements, then interpret supplied later observations.
- **Note:** Year-end part 2, week 28, one newly sampled code. Six groups of five at four minutes each plus six minutes set-up/transitions and five minutes record completion. Each child plans two single-factor comparisons against light-plus-water, conducts and dates an own baseline measurement/condition observation, then uses supplied later data to decide BOTH needs through cause/effect. Others independently complete the new framed plan/evidence sheet. No claim to grow plants in one lesson: identifiable unit 5 longitudinal conducting records are carried forward as existing evidence, never re-scored. Carry days 63/64 model and day 72 habitat records unchanged; day 72 is within ten days, so no new model/inventory task and neither code is listed. Fresh investigation form differs from recent inventory observations. No-kit: children plan, measure supplied scaled dated images and record their own observations; label supplied growth data as weaker evidence. Response day 76 only.
