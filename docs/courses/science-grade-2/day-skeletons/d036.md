## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Plan and carry out a safe test of unfamiliar materials and sort their observed properties, including cooler and warmer samples.
- **Note:** Unit 3 observation window: six groups of five at four minutes each, six minutes set-up/transitions, five minutes to revisit the hat. Every child proposes what to test and hold steady, conducts a gentle test, records color/texture/hardness/flexibility/light evidence and classifies solid/liquid water at two temperatures. Per-child plan/action checklist and framed paper record. Others independently sort familiar sample pictures. Response on day 38 only.

- s2-material-properties (grade 2): taught or practiced here
