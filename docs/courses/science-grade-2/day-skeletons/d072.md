## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Individually observe unfamiliar habitat image sets and record and compare plant and animal kinds.
- **Note:** Each child observes unfamiliar sets for several habitats, inventories plants AND animals, counts kinds rather than individuals and compares shared/different kinds, including an equal-count pair. Own paper observation/graph and evidence-based pattern; no scientific names. Unit response day 73 only. Keep this single record for year-end, no repeated inventory task.
