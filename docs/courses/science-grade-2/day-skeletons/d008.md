## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Use two unfamiliar sources to locate frozen and liquid water and independently choose a source for the question.
- **Note:** Unit 1 observation window begins. Each child selects a useful source and uses captions, a map key and a second source to record water in oceans, rivers, lakes, ponds, ice and snow, including solid and liquid states and location patterns. W.2.8 read-aloud and sentence frames support access. Independent paper record, not a teacher conference. Unit response on day 10 only.

- s2-water-where (grade 2): taught or practiced here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **2-ESS2-3:** Gather information to find where water is on Earth and show that it can be frozen solid or a liquid.
