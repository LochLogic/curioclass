# Section 1.3: Explain another landscape

## Header



## 1. Standards and practices


- **2-ESS2-2, this section's share (days 9, 10).** "Make a model showing the landforms and bodies of water in a place." Day 9: each child alone makes a model of an unfamiliar area and explains a land-water pattern from it. Day 10: the class model is revised into a consensus on the schoolyard runoff, with routes nobody saw marked uncertain.
- **2-ESS2-3, this section's share (day 10).** "Gather information to find where water is on Earth and show that it can be frozen solid or a liquid." Day 10 repairs source choice and states from the day 8 record, and a fresh unscored retry samples them again.
- **Limits that bite here.** No numerical scale; no water cycle; no amounts. No key or symbol is supplied on day 9. Day 9 keeps day 8's source record unchanged and samples only modeling. Day 10 is unscored. Class models are conventions to refer to, not answer maps.
- **Connections.** ELA W.2.8 (the day 10 retry uses supplied sources); social studies grade 2 world features (week 6).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to score on one scale with points: each of the unit plan's twelve components across days 8 and 9 is marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only "shown" counts a point. A dotted arrow means "we think the water goes this way but we did not see it". Written feedback names one component and one next step, not a guessed misconception. Hand-offs: a child who showed everything gets an extension, not a skipped day.

## 2. Where it sits


Before: section 1.2, ending day 8; the day 8 packet is not returned until day 10. After: the end of unit 1, then unit 2 day 11, which retrieves source practice and runoff. No readiness check opens unit 2. Day 9 is an assessment day with no reteach; results from days 8 and 9 go to day 10.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** the day 9 item (2-ESS2-2, six points, unit plan field 5). The day 8 item belongs to section 1.2.

**Evidence.** Day 9: every child makes a picture model with a key and a framed spoken-then-written explanation; scored components on one scale. No exit on day 9. Day 10: its exit is the fresh unscored retry (items s01-X10, s01-X10b, s01-X10c); only the repaired components are noted, with supported and independent evidence kept apart.
- **Retry (day 10).** The unit plan's fresh packet (field 5), "invented for practice", read aloud one step at a time: first mark the water places and states from the sources; then draw the keyed ridge, hollow and lower route; then say why with the frame "I know ___ because ___". Answer: pool and stream on low ground, snow on the ridge marked solid, the color caption not used. Wrong: preference chosen (A); no key (B); snow left out (C); land left out (D); the route climbs the ridge (E); only the first source used (F); the water body left off (G).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Day 9 withdraws all supports: no key, no source choice, no symbols. The class model is not used on day 9. On day 10 the class consensus model of the schoolyard runoff is finalised from the tray, the school map and the sources, with unobserved routes drawn as dotted arrows (revised, with the day 1 sketch pinned beside it). The teacher holds the consensus chart; each child holds their own retry.
- **Key moment 1, day 9: a model made alone.** Ideal instrument: a blank picture-map template on paper. Spec: fits; no tool. Gaps and asks: none.
- **Key moment 2, day 10: telling what was seen from what is guessed.** Ideal instrument: the class model chart with solid and dotted arrows. Spec: fits; a paper chart.
- **A room with nothing.** Day 9 and day 10 use paper only. Without cards, draw the retry packet large by hand and read it aloud.

## 6. Days

## Day 9


- **5 min. Directions.** The teacher reads the prompt aloud, neutrally, one step at a time with a pause (several asks, so each step is read and done before the next). The packet and a blank picture-map template are handed out; no features are placed.
- **25 min. The model.** Each child makes a keyed picture model of the unfamiliar bay, uses it to explain the lower water route in the frame, and shows how a map could represent a different area. No scale, no key supplied, no group conferences.
- **5 min. Check and collect.** Names checked, papers collected.
- Holds what is carried: each child their model. Tool: None. Evidence: the six day 9 components, one scale, "shown" counted as the point. Day 9 keeps day 8's source record unchanged.

## Day 10


- **5 min. Return and read feedback.** Each child gets their record with one written next step for one component and reads or hears it; six rotating children are heard saying it, 30 seconds each (3 minutes) at the end.
- **15 min. Repair by letter groups.** Four groups (A with F; B with D and G; C; E), each with the teacher for 3 minutes in turn, 12 minutes in all, while the others follow picture directions at their table; 3 minutes to move between groups. A child who showed everything works on the extension. If many made a letter, it takes the first 5 minutes (field 4).
- **10 min. Fresh retry.** The new packet, unscored, read one step at a time; each child marks paper and adds a framed reason. The teacher notes only the repaired components.
- **5 min. Anchor explanation.** The class finalises the consensus model of the schoolyard runoff and says the answer to the compelling question, marking unobserved routes with dotted arrows.
- **2 min. Close the board.** Questions answered, and one left open.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the consensus chart; each child their retry. A child may follow the same sequence one-to-one with an adult.
- Tool: None. By hand: drawing. Evidence: repaired components, noted in plain words.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice is repair by letter group and a class chart of routes; the exit is a fresh unscored retry on a new packet, marking water places, drawing a model, and saying why the stream is low.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 10, "Where does the water on our schoolyard go after rain" and "Which routes did we not see".
- Written task, day 10: the framed reason in the retry, "I know ___ because ___".
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: names a route or state; names the evidence (tray, photograph, map or page); marks the unseen as uncertain.

## 8. Practice that returns

- Day 9: nothing is practiced; it samples days 1 to 5. Day 10: the exit-wrong examples of days 1 to 7, as letters.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: uncertain, route, model, key. Frame: "I think ___, but we did not see it."
- **Access.** Read prompts aloud; large pictures, tactile outlines, pointing, oral recording or a scribe, without choosing evidence or placing features for the child.
- **Check accommodations.** As in section 1.2.
- **Reading load.** Labels only; prompts and captions read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Model a second invented area with its own shapes and show one pattern that differs.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 9: make a model on a blank page from two pictures, with no help choosing; read the prompt one step at a time. Day 10 (the last day of the unit): talk through the feedback, redo the weakest piece, then draw the schoolyard runoff and mark one route as not seen.
- **One projector.** Show the consensus chart on day 10; everything else is on paper.
