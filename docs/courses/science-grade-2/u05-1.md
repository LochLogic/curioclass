# Section 5.1: Plan and begin fair plant tests

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **2-LS2-1, this section's share (days 48 to 51).** "Investigate whether a plant needs light from the sun and water in order to grow." Here the children plan the investigation (day 48), set it up and start the dated record (days 49 and 50), and test what counts as fair and as healthy (day 51). The conclusion about the needs is drawn in section 5.2; here they gather and judge evidence.
- **Limits that bite here.** Each test changes only one thing at a time: the dark plant and the dry plant are each compared with the healthy plant, never with each other. Observable growth only: no photosynthesis, soil nutrients or germination. Warmth stays on the question board as an untested question; no cooler plant is added. Heights are read to the whole centimeter, so a change under 1 centimeter reads "same" and color, leaves and stem carry the rest.
- **Connections.** Math 2.MD.A.1 (ruler zero at the soil, whole centimeters), retrieved on day 49. Crosscutting cause and effect, days 48 and 51. Unit 4's spoken claim, "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___", day 51.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A fair comparison here: the healthy plant has light and water, and each other plant lacks exactly one. "Control" is the teacher's word; children say "the healthy plant". (2) The board on the lawn changed light, rain and perhaps warmth together, so it cannot say which one mattered. (3) A plant grown in the dark often grows tall, pale and weak, so tall is not healthy; the cause is not taught. (4) The dark plant is covered except for the short measuring turn, said aloud to the children. (5) Scoring is marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. (6) Real readings and fallbacks: field 5.

## 2. Where it sits

After: section 5.2, which opens on day 52 and takes the day 50 written feedback and the day 51 exit slips (errors A and B). Each child's entries from days 49 and 50 go into the one record kept to day 55.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** none. Both unit items (days 54 and 55) belong to section 5.2. This section's evidence is unscored: each child's day 49 and day 50 entries, and the exits below. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.

**Exits, days 48 to 51** (spoken, picture cards, all labeled "invented for practice" and said aloud; every child marks, six rotating give a reason, 20 seconds each). The cards use cases and numbers the teaching days do not.
- **E48.** Three matched mint pots, same cups, soil and starting size: P1 in sunlight, watered, new leaves; P2 in shade, watered, leaves falling; P3 in sunlight, no water, drooping. "Join the pots that make a fair test: one pair for light and one pair for water." Answer: P1 with P2 for light, P1 with P3 for water. Wrong: P2 with P3 (A).
- **E49** (two items, one touch or drawing each). A bean card dated February 6: three matching cups, equal soil, each seedling 5 centimeters with two green upright leaves. A has sunlight and two spoonfuls daily; B is under a dark cover with no water; C has sunlight and no water. A and B have full starting records; C's has the date, condition and height, and its leaf and stem boxes are blank. **E49a:** "Touch the change that makes both comparisons with A fair": give B the same water as A, darken C, or change nothing. Answer: give B the same water as A. Wrong: leave B dry or darken C or change nothing (A). **E49b:** "Draw what belongs in C's empty boxes." Answer: two green leaves, an upright stem. Wrong: "the height is enough" (E).
- **E50.** A fresh ruler photograph dated March 3: soil at zero, plant top at 8 centimeters, three green upright leaves, labeled sunlight with water. "Fill in this line of the dated table." Answer: the date, sunlight with water, 8 centimeters, three green upright leaves, drawn and labeled. Wrong: a height with nothing else (E). Other missing boxes get descriptive feedback, no letter.
- **E51.** Two pairs of young sunflowers. Pair M: matching cups, soil and water, one in sunlight and one under a cover. Pair N: different cups and soil, one in sunlight and one under a cover. "Pip says the taller plant is the healthier one. Touch the pair that checks Pip's idea fairly." Answer: pair M. Wrong: pair N (A). Six children then say what they would look at besides height; answer: leaf color, number of leaves, stem. Wrong: "the taller one, so it is healthier" (B).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The class needs-arrow model and comparison chart (new, day 48): a plant with a dotted arrow from the sun and one from the water, dotted until a record shows it; the dotted arrow is unit 1's route not seen, extended. Day 49 adds the three cups to the chart. Day 50 adds dated measurements. Day 51 revises "taller means healthier" and adds a condition row (color, leaves, stem). The teacher holds the chart; each child holds their own record. Unit 4's claim, evidence and cause reasoning is reviewed, not retaught.
- **Adult set-ups before the class (not class minutes).** The board is laid on grass two weeks before day 48 with photographs on the first and last days (frozen ground: the adult's earlier photographs, or a drawn pair labeled "invented for practice"). Before day 49: eighteen untreated radish seedlings grown past sprouting and matched in size, six spares from the same tray, equal damp soil in eighteen cups, the watering schedule trialled, covers checked for light leaks.
- **Key moment 1, days 49 and 50: a height read at the soil.** Ideal instrument: a centimeter ruler with zero at the soil. Spec: centimeter rulers fit. The step is 1 centimeter, so a gain under 1 centimeter reads "same" and is recorded as true; leaves, color and stem are recorded beside it.
- **Key moment 2, day 49: excluding light.** Ideal instrument: an opaque cover that lets air in. Spec: the stable ventilated opaque cover fits once the adult has looked under it for light the day before. It is lifted only for the measuring turn.
- **Key moment 3, days 49 and 50: equal water.** Ideal instrument: one level measuring spoon. Spec: the spoon fits; the actual amount is written on the cup card each time, and none for the dry plant.
- **When a reading fails.** A lost or mouldy plant is swapped for a spare marked "replaced", on the record. If the whole set fails, use dated measured photographs from earlier plants, said aloud to be kept records, not ours today.
- **A room with nothing.** Photographs of grass under a board and the dated measured photographs stand in; planning and reading remain, conducting does not (field 11). No tool is used; there is no key tool.
- **Clean-up (days 49 and 50).** Each child has fitted goggles in a labeled bag, wiped with a clean damp cloth (one cloth per table, 1 minute) before bagging. Tables are wiped, hands are wiped with a damp cloth, and hands are washed at the next scheduled washing, said aloud, because 30 children at two sinks do not fit.

## 6. Days

## Day 48


- **5 min. Hook.** The dated photographs: the board on the lawn on its first day, and lifted on the last, with pale grass beside green grass (1.5 min). The teacher asks the compelling question and offers no cause (0.5 min). Partners say what they see, all at once (1 min). Four children say what they notice and the teacher scribes any ideas unsorted, accepting none (2 min).
- **5 min. Retrieve.** One card of four earlier-course pictures, each ask read aloud and marked in turn, about 1 minute each: mark what a plant needs; point to roots and leaves; choose the light comparison that keeps everything else alike; mark the longer daylight bar. A miss gets a modeled second picture in the last minute, using the earlier chart or drawing.
- **12 min. Plan fair comparisons.** The class plans the light comparison with the teacher on the planning chart (3 min): keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing (review). Each table of five then fills its own chart with picture cards for the healthy plant, the no-light plant and the no-water plant (6 min); each child places and says one card, 30 seconds each, 2.5 minutes of the 6; the teacher spends 1 minute at each of the six tables. Two rotating tables show theirs, and Pip says "no light and no water is a fair test"; the class says what differs (3 min). Each child marks one choice on their own copy.
- **10 min. Needs-arrow model and question board.** The class draws a plant with a dotted arrow from the sun and one from the water (4 min). The hook ideas are sorted on the question board (3 min): light and water can be tested with plants; warmth and weight stay as questions; the board changed light, rain and perhaps warmth together, so it did not isolate a cause. Six rotating children say "I think ___ because ___", 30 seconds each (3 min).
- **3 min. Exit.** E48.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the chart and model. Tool: None. By hand: marking, placing cards, drawing. Evidence: E48, the retrieval marks and each child's copy of the chart, with the "heard" column. New: the fair plant comparison and the dotted needs arrow; everything else is review.

- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice plans fair comparisons on a chart and draws a needs-arrow model; the exit gives three new parsley pots to join into a light pair and a water pair, and a bucket-lawn picture to say what is seen.

## Day 49


- **3 min. Return and repair.** Error A from E48, 3 minutes: two cup cards, covered against dry and covered against healthy; touch what is different on each. If few made it, a small group at the teacher's table while the rest begin the retrieval card.
- **3 min. Retrieve.** Math: the paper plant strip, 9 centimeters. "Show where zero meets the soil, read this whole number, match it to a bar." A miss gets a modeled second strip.
- **4 min. Plan the cups.** The teacher shows the chart from day 48, names the three cups (healthy, dark, dry), holds up one live seedling and models the ruler at the soil once. Each table's roles are fixed: soil, seedling, water, measure, record. Compare each missing factor only with the healthy plant, never dark with dry.
- **13 min. Set up.** Goggles on from their bags (1 min). Soil is level in each damp cup and the seedling is set and firmed, with the tray under the cups (5 min). The watering child gives the healthy and dark cups the agreed level spoonfuls, none to the dry cup, and the table writes the amount (1 min). Measuring: five children, three plants, five rulers, in two rounds of 2 minutes (4 min): round one, three children read one plant each; round two, the other two read the same plants again. The adult puts the covers on and sets the trays in the window (2 min). The teacher spends about 2 minutes at each of six tables.
- **7 min. Starting records and model.** Each child fills their own starting record for the plant they read: date, condition, height, leaf box, stem box (4 min). Six rotating children say their entry, 20 seconds each, and the others check the boxes (2 min). The class adds the three cups to the chart (1 min).
- **3 min. Exit.** E49 (two items).
- **5 min. Clean up.** Goggles wiped and bagged, tables wiped, hands wiped, spills wiped at once (see field 5).
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the chart. Tool: None. By hand: planting, measuring, drawing. Evidence: E49 and each starting record, with the "heard" column; both unscored. Adult care between meetings adds no lesson.

- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice sets up its own seedlings and fills their starting records; the exit gives three new bean cups to choose the one change that makes both tests fair, then a record card for the third cup to fill in.

## Day 50


- **8 min. Return and repair, then the procedure.** Errors A and E, 3 minutes each, from E49. The A card is the covered cup against the dry cup, then against the healthy cup. For E the teacher draws a height-only line and the class fills the leaf and stem boxes (this is also the procedure again). Then every child says the ruler steps once with a partner (2 min). If many made one letter, that letter takes its 3 minutes whole class and the other goes to a small group.
- **16 min. Measure, sketch and record.** Goggles on (1 min). Measuring in two rounds of 3 minutes (6 min): three children read a plant each, then two children read the same plants again; the others sketch their assigned plant. Everyone then enters the date, condition, height to the whole centimeter and appearance in the dated table, with a sketch (5 min); the teacher hears a rotating six at the tables, 20 to 30 seconds each. The watering child gives the agreed spoonfuls to the healthy and dark cups, none to the dry cup, and the table writes the amount (2 min). The adult puts the covers back and the trays in the window (2 min).
- **8 min. Discuss the evidence and revise the model.** What changed since day 49 and what stayed the same, table by table, with true "same" readings said as such (4 min). The class adds dated measurements to the chart and keeps the arrows dotted unless a record shows a difference (4 min).
- **3 min. Exit.** E50.
- **5 min. Clean up.** As day 49.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the chart; each child their dated table. Tool: None. By hand: measuring, sketching, filling the table. Evidence: each child's day 50 entry (unscored practice, written feedback after class for revision on day 52) and E50. Failed readings: field 5.

- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice measures and sketches its own seedlings on rounds; the exit gives a ruler photograph of a new plant, a tomato, and asks the child to fill one dated table line from it.

## Day 51


- **3 min. Return and repair.** Error E from E50, 3 minutes: the height-only line and the filled one.
- **3 min. Retrieve the model.** Which arrows are dotted, which table lines we have, and what we still cannot tell.
- **17 min. Fairness and height evidence.** All cards are printed and invented for practice. First (8 min): three pair cards of corn seedlings, each marked fair or not and the difference touched. One pair is alike in cup, soil and water and differs in light; one differs in cup and soil as well as light; one differs in light and in water. The teacher spends 1 minute at each table. Then (9 min): a card series, Day 1, Day 6 and Day 12, of two matched, equally watered corn seedlings, both 2 centimeters on Day 1. On Day 12 the covered plant is 19 centimeters, pale, leaning, with two thin leaves; the lit one is 16 centimeters, green and firm, with four leaves. Tables mark color, leaves and stem with symbols (6 min); the class says which is taller and which is healthier, and why (3 min).
- **12 min. Discuss and revise.** Pip says "the plant that grew the most is the healthiest"; the class checks the line against the cards (4 min). The model gains a condition row, and each pair of children chooses what they will look at on day 52 that could support or challenge "plants need light and water" (5 min). Six rotating children say "I will look at ___ because ___", 30 seconds each (3 min).
- **3 min. Exit.** E51.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the chart. Tool: None. By hand: sorting, marking, saying. Evidence: E51 and the pairs' look-for choices, with the "heard" column. Fair comparison and "taller is not healthier" are taught on this day, with cards and no live plants.

- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice marks three pair cards fair or not and revises the model after Pip's claim; the exit gives two new sunflower pairs to pick the fair one, and a card to name what could challenge Pip.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 48, "What changed under the board?" Day 50, "What changed since day 49, and what stayed the same?" Day 51, "What besides height would you look at, and why?"
- **Written task, day 50.** Each child's dated table line with a sketch, drawn and labeled (a scribe if wanted).
- **Judged by the teacher with look-fors,** each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen: dated; condition named; height from the soil to the whole centimeter; leaves and stem recorded beside it. Written feedback, one note per child, takes about 30 minutes after class (ours, untested).

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Day 48:** the needs, the parts, a fair comparison and the daylight chart from earlier grades. **Day 49:** ruler zero and graph reading; the day 48 chart. **Day 50:** the day 49 procedure and starting record. **Day 51:** the day 50 table and the model. Spoken claims, "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___", return on days 48, 50 and 51.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: plant, light, water, dark, dry, pale, ruler, centimeter, same, different. Frames: "The ___ plant has ___ but not ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing is accepted.
- **Access.** Read every caption and prompt aloud. Offer enlarged high-contrast cards, condition symbols, tactile rulers, a scribe and pointing. A child directs an adult's handling steps and supplies the observations and decisions; record this.
- **Check accommodations.** Exits are read aloud, answered by touch, drawing or voice.
- **Reading load.** None alone: labels only, under 150 words a page, 18 point.
- **Extension, same standard.** Plan a fourth plant that would test only one more thing, and say what would change your mind.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 48: look at the board photographs, sort the ideas, fill the three-cup chart. Day 49: set up three cups with a ruler and a ventilated box as the cover; any home plant is optional and nothing needs buying. Day 50: measure and fill the dated line. Day 51: sort the pair cards and say what else to look at besides height. At home, any day skippable, a grown-up asks the exit aloud.
- **One projector.** Show the photographs and the chart on day 48; day 49 is hands-on at the tables; show the card series on day 51.
