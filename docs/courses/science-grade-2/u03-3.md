# Section 3.3: Choose from evidence and repair explanations

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **2-PS1-2, this section's share (days 37, 38).** "Use test results from different materials to decide which material's properties best fit a given job." Day 37 is the unit's end item on new test records; day 38 repairs what it showed and ends with the class explanation of the rain hat.
- **2-PS1-1, this section's share (day 38).** "Investigate materials to describe them and sort them by properties you can observe, such as color, texture, hardness and bendiness." Day 38 repairs the sorting gaps that day 36 showed (A, B, C, D), on fresh pictures. Day 36 is the unit's only collection for this code; it is carried, not rescored.
- **Limits that bite here.** Paper only on both days. Length only, whole centimeters. Matched conditions are given, never asked for. All new cases are "invented for practice". A paper retry cannot show a safe action, so a missing safe-action point on day 36 stays unconfirmed. Water entry is not breaking strength or total capacity.
- **Connections.** Engineering and society, day 37: how knowing a material helps people design an object from natural materials (the tree, wood, block picture).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Day 37 scoring: fourteen points, thirteen from property analysis on new cases, each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only "shown" counts a point; helped and independent work recorded apart. (2) Directions are read neutrally; no conferences; no hints. (3) What the teacher writes after class: one component and one next step for each child, 60 minutes in all. (4) How the day 38 letter groups are built from the two records. (5) That the boot and mitten question is only partly answered: the tests show water, not warmth.

## 2. Where it sits


Before: section 3.2, ending on day 36 (the record is carried). After: the end of unit 3; day 39 opens unit 4. Day 37 reteaches nothing; its packets and the day 36 records are read after class, and the response is day 38 only. The day 35 slips (error B) join the day 38 repair.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** the day 37 item (2-PS1-2, fourteen points, unit plan field 5); its numbers and answers sit there and this plan adds none. There are no exits on days 37 and 38; the item and the retry are the evidence. One scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

**Day 38 retries (unscored, fresh, "invented for practice").** Only the missed component is asked, one ask, read aloud, answered on paper by pointing or marking. A paper retry cannot confirm a safe action.
- **RA.** Two samples, equally rough, pressed with the same blunt stick and the same gentle press; one dents and one does not. "Touch the harder one." Answer: the one with no dent; the teacher then asks the rotating six what shows it. Wrong: both, "they are rough" (A).
- **RB.** Two same-colour strips, one bends and one stays stiff, in pictures. "Do matching colours tell us how they bend? Say yes or no." Answer: no. Wrong: yes (B).
- **RC.** An unlabelled sequence: water cooling in a sealed tray until it holds its shape. "Is it still water at the end? Say yes or no." Answer: yes; it changed from flowing to holding its shape. Wrong: no (C).
- **RD.** Two light-test plans on cards. "Touch the plan that keeps the light comparison fair." Answer: the plan with the lamp fixed. Wrong: the plan that moves the lamp (D).
- **RE.** New strips: smooth flexible P and rough stiff Q; matched water-entry tests show water climbing 1 centimeter along P and 7 along Q; separate matched hat tests leave the doll dry under P and wet under Q. Sorting ask: "Touch the strip that bends more." Answer: P. Wrong: Q, "it is rough" (B, nearest: sorts by one feature). Choice ask: "Touch the strip to make the replacement rain hat." Answer: P; Q draws water farther along its strip but leaks. Wrong: Q, "more water climbed" (H); P or Q, "it is my favourite" (E).
- **RF.** Equal strips in pictures; water climbed 0 centimeters on one and 4 on the other. "Touch the strip that shows water going into it." Answer: the 4. Do not claim its total capacity. Wrong: the 0 (F).
- **RG.** Equal-load bend lengths of 2 and 4 centimeters. "Touch the stiffer ledge." Answer: 2. Wrong: 4 (G).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The choice model and consensus chart from section 3.2 are used alone on day 37 with new data; nothing is added. On day 38 they join the pattern chart and the question board in the closing explanation: materials differ in water passage, so the chosen hat keeps the doll dry under tested conditions (days 30 and 31).
- **Key moment 1, day 37: a choice made alone from new records.** Ideal instrument: a printed packet of pictured records (bars, dent pictures, grip pictures) and a framed answer sheet. Spec: none needed; paper fits. It is "invented for practice", said aloud.
- **Key moment 2, day 38: a fresh retry.** Ideal instrument: pictured cards. Spec: none needed. The retry confirms the repair, not the unit.
- **A room with nothing.** The same paper path; no change.

## 6. Days

## Day 37


- **5 min. Directions.** Read aloud, neutrally, one step at a time; the packet and the framed answer sheet are handed out. Say aloud that all cases are invented for practice.
- **25 min. The item.** Each child alone organizes the records by tested property, chooses a material for each pictured job, shows the evidence, explains why changing the job can change the choice, and answers the tree, wood and block picture. Prompts are read aloud again as needed; no help with answers; no conferences. A scribe or an oral answer is allowed where recorded.
- **5 min. Collect.** Names checked, papers collected, supplies counted.
- Holds what is carried: each child their packet; the choice model is on the wall and not pointed to. Tool: None. By hand: marking, drawing. Evidence: the day 37 item (unit plan field 5). Response on day 38 only.

## Day 38


- **5 min. Return and read feedback.** Each child gets their records with one written next step for one component and reads or hears it. Six rotating children say theirs back.
- **15 min. Repair by letter groups.** Group A with B, group C, group D, group E with F, G and H, each with the teacher for 3 minutes in turn while the others work from picture directions at their table; 3 minutes to move. Hand-backs: the day 36 records, the day 37 packets and the day 35 slips (B). Paper repair moves: press two pictured samples and mark which dents; bend two same-colour pictures; line up a cooling-water sequence; tape the lamp on a picture; align bend lengths beside "stays level"; trace water outside beside water inside a strip.
- **10 min. Fresh retries.** Only the missed component, on paper, from field 3. Each corrected component is recorded apart. The retry never rescores the item.
- **5 min. Closing explanation.** The class answers the hat question with days 30 and 31: some materials let water pass and some do not, so the chosen hat keeps the doll dry under tested conditions. Then the compelling question: the tests show water; warmth was not tested and goes on the board as a question to keep.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the consensus chart; each child their retry. A child may follow the same steps one-to-one with an adult. Tool: None. By hand: repairs, retries. Evidence: the day 38 retries in field 3.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice is feedback reading, repair by letter group and a class explanation; the exit is a set of fresh retry cards, each a new case, touched or answered yes or no, only for the card on the child's feedback form.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 38, "What changed between the hat that failed and the hat we chose?" and "What did our tests show about boots and mittens, and what did they not show?"
- **Written task, day 37.** The item's explanation, framed: "I choose ___ for ___ because the test showed ___." (scribed if wanted). It is the item's evidence.
- **Judged by the teacher with look-fors:** names the job; names a tested property; links the result in the packet; does not use liking. Each is marked on the one scale.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 37 practises nothing; it samples days 27 to 35. Day 38 returns the letters from days 35 to 37. Fluency target: none; the standard sets none.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: tested, result, because, job, bends, dent, climb, leak. Frames: "I choose ___ because ___." "It is not good for ___ because ___." Pointing is accepted.
- **Access.** Read all captions aloud; enlarge pictures; offer tactile samples as a model beside the picture; allow pointing and a scribe. Keep the child's own decision and record physical help apart. Rehearse property words with gestures during teaching, never during the item.
- **Check accommodations.** Read aloud, large print, a scribe or an oral answer.
- **Reading load.** None alone; labels only, under 150 words a page; everything else read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** After the retry, write a test record for one more material and say which job it fits.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Day 37: read the packet aloud in two short sittings, the child points and explains; give no help. Day 38 is the last day of the unit: go over the feedback, redo the one weakest piece, then say why the chosen hat keeps the doll dry and what was not tested.
- **One projector.** Show the closing chart on day 38; the item is paper.
