# Section 4.2: Changes that remain, and arguments from evidence

## Header

<!-- skel -->
Science, Grade 2. Unit 4, Can we change it back? *Our block castle became a boat, and melted chocolate hardened again, but a cooked egg never runs again: which changes can be undone?* Anchor: three changes shown side by side (a block build taken apart and rebuilt, chocolate melted in a warm-water bath and cooled, an egg cooked by the teacher) (days 39 to 47): see [s04.md](s04.md). **Days 43 to 47.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (43), application 1 (44), check 2 (45, 46), respond 1 (47). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 2 of 2 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 35 to 40 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

- **2-PS1-4, this section's share (days 43, 44, 46, 47).** "Argue, using evidence, that heating or cooling causes some changes that can be undone and some that cannot." Day 43 gives the "cannot be undone" evidence (egg, leaf, paper); day 44 argues from every paired record; day 46 is the unit's argument item; day 47 repairs it.
- **2-PS1-3, this section's share (days 45, 46, 47).** "Observe and explain, with evidence, how something built from a few kinds of pieces can be taken apart and rebuilt as something new." No new teaching: days 45 and 46 are the unit's reassembly item and day 47 repairs it.
- **Limits that bite here.** Observable changes only: no particles, no chemistry words ("cooked" and "browned" are what we saw). A lasting change is shown by an attempted return, not by waiting. A state change back need not restore the outline. Egg and lettuce are sealed displays and photographs, heated paper is photographs only, no flame is in the room, and the check days use paper cards.
- **Connections.** Writing W.2.1, days 43 and 44: a spoken claim, evidence and reason before any framed writing. Crosscutting: cause and effect (day 44). Claims and causes return in unit 5 (days 51, 55), systems in unit 6 (days 63, 64).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Scoring: each look-for is marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only "shown" counts a point; the unit plan's points are those counts. (2) Check-day rules: directions read neutrally, no hints, no reteaching on days 45 and 46. (3) The observation windows: who is watched when, and what is written while watching. (4) The 45 minutes after class for marking both records, and how day 47's four letter groups are built. (5) What the egg and the leaf show: the temperature was changed back, or not, and the properties did not return. (6) Why a card with no cooling record means "not enough yet", not "no".

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Error D from E42, at most 5 minutes: two cards of a cup of water, before and after cooling, and a third card face down. The class says what it needs to see before it says the water went back; the teacher turns the card (warmed, liquid again). That third look is the attempted return.
- **8 min. The egg.** Each group has a sealed cooked-egg display and the photographs: the egg raw in a dish, cooked, then cooled (cooked by an adult at home or in a kitchen; no egg is cooked in class). Five children pass the display, 30 seconds each (2.5 min of the 8). "What did the heat do? We cooled it. Did that undo it?" The group marks: changed, tried cooling, stayed changed.
- **8 min. The leaf.** Each group has two sealed lettuce leaves: one frozen and then thawed by an adult, one never frozen. Photographs show it firm before freezing and limp after thawing. Nothing was opened; hands are washed at the next scheduled washing, said aloud, because 30 children at two sinks cannot wash in this time. 1 minute to wipe the tables.
- **1 min. Board.** "Does that always work?" gets its answer: no.

## 9. Supports and extension
