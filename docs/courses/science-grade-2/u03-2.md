## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


- **2-PS1-2, this section's share (days 32 to 35).** "Use test results from different materials to decide which material's properties best fit a given job." Day 32 gives the hat winner a second job (a spill pad) from the day 30 records; day 33 tests bend for a bridge deck; day 34 tests hardness and texture for a cover and a grip; day 35 revises the failed hat.
- **2-PS1-1, this section's share (days 33 to 36).** "Investigate materials to describe them and sort them by properties you can observe, such as color, texture, hardness and bendiness." Days 33 and 34 add bend, press and texture comparisons to the sorting; day 36 is the unit's observation of this part, with sealed ice and lukewarm water.
- **Limits that bite here.** Bend under a fixed load is not breaking strength. Length only, whole centimeters; 1 centimeter is "about the same". Texture alone does not tell grip on another surface, so grip comes from pictured results of the same block on the same slope. No skin-friction tests. An adult places every load. No glass or sharp metal in student hands; nothing is tasted.
- **Connections.** Math 2.MD.A.1, day 33 (whole centimeters). Writing W.2.7, day 34 (a framed observation page). Engineering and society, day 35: people choose materials by testing, and materials come from nature, some processed (wood from trees, then a block).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Direction of bend: less drop means stiffer, so a deck wants little bend and a strap may want much. (2) The same material can be best for one job and poor for another; the job comes first, then the property to test. (3) Hard means no dent under a gentle press; rough is a different property. (4) How day 36 is recorded: a live checklist for the proposal and the safe action, a paper record for the rest, one scale, one point only for "shown". (5) That a paper path cannot show a safe action.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** the day 36 item (2-PS1-1, six points, unit plan field 5). The day 37 item belongs to section 3.3. Day 36 is the item itself, so it has no exit. Exits E32 to E35 are one ask each, read aloud, answered by pointing or saying, with picture cards "invented for practice". **Exit questions.** If many miss one, the next day's "Return and repair" part opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E32.** Cards: strip T of a new material (water climbed 8 centimeters) and strip V of another (no climb; as a hat it kept its tab dry). "A mat under a dripping plant pot must soak up drips. Touch the strip to use." Answer: T. Wrong: V, "it was the best hat" (E).
- **E33.** Cards: two new shelf strips under the same block; strip R dropped 3 centimeters at the middle and strip S dropped 8. "Pip needs a shelf that stays level. Touch the strip to use." Answer: R. Wrong: S (G).
- **E34.** Cards: a woven doormat piece and a smooth tile, pressed with the same blunt stick and the same gentle press; the mat shows a dent and the tile none. "A cover must not dent when pressed. Touch the one to use." Answer: the tile. Wrong: the mat, "rough is hard" (A).
- **E35.** Cards: two green sheets tested as covers for a book box in the rain; the box stayed dry under one and got wet under the other. "Pip says: both are green, so both keep the box dry. Is Pip right? Say yes or no." Answer: no; the test shows which one. Wrong: yes (B).
- **Day 36.** The item is in the unit plan, field 5: six one-point look-fors on one scale. Wrong answers on it carry A, B, C and D as the unit plan lists.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The choice model from day 31 (job, the property we tested, what the test showed, our choice) takes a second row on day 32 (the pad), a bend row on day 33 and press and texture rows on day 34. The pattern chart keeps shared-property groups (day 28) and temperature-dependent water states (day 29) for day 36. On day 35 the consensus chart links purpose, tested property and evidence and is revised from the failed hat; the same chart is used on day 37 with new data. Each child's day 27 drawing comes back on day 35.
- **Key moment 1, day 33: bend read to the whole centimeter.** Ideal instrument: an equal strip across two low supports with a ruler held beside its middle; read the drop with and without the block. Spec: none needed; fits. Instrument step: 1 centimeter, so differences of 1 centimeter are "about the same". Six groups, two for each of card, plastic and fabric; the adult places 12 blocks in about 6 of the 14 minutes. If a strip slips or no strip drops a whole centimeter, use printed cards, said aloud to be invented (card 6, fabric 9, plastic 12).
- **Key moment 2, day 34: press and grip.** Ideal instrument: blunt stick and the same gentle press; pictured grip records of one block on one slope. Spec: none needed. If every sample dents or none does, the teacher presses a spare firm and a spare soft sample so the difference is seen. Grip is read from the pictures only.
- **Key moment 3, day 36: an individual test and its record.** Ideal instrument: a per-child checklist (proposed a test with something held steady; did it safely) and a framed paper record. Spec: none needed; paper fits. Hands: the teacher sees one group at a time at one station.
- **A room with nothing.** Test photographs and matching sheets for days 33 and 34; for day 36 the same, with the gap recorded: no child action can be credited from paper.

## 6. Days

## Day 32


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Error H from E31, at most 5 minutes: cover A took water in and its tab is wet, cover B took none in and its tab is dry (invented for practice). Children point to the cover that keeps the doll dry.
- **6 min. A second job.** The teacher pours a spill onto a tray at the front only. "Which tested material should be a pad to soak it up?" Pip says: "Our hat winner is best for everything." The class sets the two jobs side by side (error E).
- **10 min. Records to choices.** Pairs use printed copies of the class's day 30 and 31 records (or the invented cards from day 30). They mark the best material for the pad and the best for the hat, and the bar or tab that shows each.
- **6 min. Second-purpose argument.** This replaces repeated hat presentations. Three pairs give one reason each: "For the ___ I choose ___ because ___." The class says whether the evidence supports it.
- **5 min. Choice model.** Add the second row (job: a pad; property: water climb; result; choice) beside the hat row.
- **3 min. Say it.** Six rotating children, 30 seconds each.
- **3 min. Exit.** E32.
- **2 min. Board.** "Would a different job change our choice?" gets its first answer: yes, the pad and the hat.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the choice model. Tool: None. By hand: marking records. Evidence: E32. Nothing is taught as new.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice copies its own climb records and says choices for a hat and a pad; the exit gives two new strips and a drip mat job, and asks why the job changes which strip is used.

## Day 33


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Error E from E32, at most 5 minutes: the class pairs two jobs with two materials from the records and says why one material is not best for both.
- **3 min. Retrieval.** Strengths and weaknesses of two designs (grade 2 unit 2): "What did each do well, and poorly?" A miss: the teacher models one with a limit line.
- **4 min. Plan.** The job: a deck for a toy bridge that must stay level under a block. Fair test: same span between two low supports, same strip size, same block; change only the material; watch how far the middle drops. We compare how far it bends, not when it would break. Less drop means stiffer.
- **14 min. Test.** Six groups, two for each of card, plastic and fabric strips (new). The adult places the block over a catch tray; the measurer holds a ruler beside the middle and reads the drop to the whole centimeter; two trials a group. Goggles on, fingers and feet clear.
- **5 min. Compare.** Both groups' numbers for each material go on the class chart; a difference of 1 centimeter is "about the same". The class draws the three bars. Where two groups differ by more than 1 centimeter, the teacher asks where the block sat.
- **4 min. Clear blocks.** Blocks to the tray, strips returned, hands wiped.
- **3 min. Exit.** E33.
- **2 min. Board.** The class names a job that wants a strip to bend (the teacher offers a strap if none comes); the question stays open for day 35.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the class chart and the choice model. Tool: None. By hand: placing, reading a ruler, bars. Evidence: E33. Hear six rotating children during the compare.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice tests two bridge-deck materials and reads each bend; the exit gives the drops of two new shelf strips and asks which strip to use for a shelf that stays level.

## Day 34


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Error G from E33, at most 5 minutes: two pictured drops (invented for practice) lined up beside the words "stays level".
- **8 min. Guided press and texture.** The teacher guides, one step at a time. Each group has four samples (wood, rubber, fabric, plastic) on the sorting mat and one blunt stick. One child presses a sample gently below face height, another feels it; the group marks dent or no dent and rough or smooth. Impact eye protection stays on. Rough and hard are separate columns (error A).
- **8 min. A cover.** The job: a cover for a corner that must not dent when pressed. Pairs choose from the mat and say which result decides.
- **9 min. A handle grip.** Pictured grip records (invented for practice): one block on one slope on four materials; two hold, two slip. Pairs say what the holders share and choose a material for a handle grip from the results; no hands are tested.
- **3 min. Framed page.** "The ___ is ___, so it is good for ___." The teacher scribes (W.2.7).
- **3 min. Clear up.** Sticks and samples counted back; damp wipes for hands.
- **3 min. Exit.** E34.
- **1 min. Board.** The five properties used so far are named.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the choice model; each pair the mat. Tool: None. By hand: pressing, feeling, marking. Evidence: E34. Hear six rotating children during the grip part.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice presses and bends samples and circles a cover from its own results; the exit gives new test cards for a doormat and tile, and for two linings, and asks which to use for each job.

## Day 35


- **5 min. Return and repair.** Error A from E34, at most 5 minutes: a rough soft sample and a smooth hard sample on cards (invented for practice); the class presses the pictures' results.
- **5 min. Look back.** The class returns to the doll and the paper hat. Each child gets their day 27 drawing. "What did we learn that the first drawing did not know?"
- **10 min. Revise.** Each child alone revises the drawing with a material chosen from printed evidence cards and labels it with the job, the tested property and the result. The written task is in field 7.
- **7 min. The pattern chart.** The teacher holds the consensus chart: purpose, tested property, evidence. The class adds shared properties across different materials (for example two that bend, two that dent).
- **6 min. From nature.** A picture of a tree, a plank and a block. People test a material before they choose it, and many materials come from nature, some changed by people. The class names one natural material and one made from it.
- **3 min. Say it.** Six rotating children, 30 seconds each, give their revised choice.
- **3 min. Exit.** E35.
- **1 min. Board.** What explains the failed hat? answered: the paper took water through.
- Holds what is carried: the teacher the consensus chart; each child their drawing. Tool: None. By hand: revising, labeling. Evidence: E35. Nothing is taught as new except the picture of the tree.
- **Shared shape (note, not timed).** The practice revises the child's own day 27 drawing from five evidence cards; the exit gives Pip's choice of a sheet by color and a new rain test, and asks what Pip should change.

## Day 36


- **3 min. Directions.** Read aloud, neutrally, one step at a time. The order of the six groups is posted.
- **24 min. Observation windows.** Six windows of 4 minutes, one group at a time at one station: three unfamiliar samples (sanded pine, clear flexible plastic, new cotton, each 10 by 10 centimeters), a blunt stick, a flashlight, a sealed ice cup, a sealed lukewarm cup. Five children in 4 minutes: each gets about 45 seconds, a spoken proposal of one test and what stays steady (about 15 seconds), then one gentle action (about 30 seconds); the group's five proposals cover press, bend, light, the ice cup and the lukewarm cup, and peers share what they saw. The teacher marks, live and per child, the two checklist points: proposes a relevant test with a steady condition; conducts it safely. After its window a group goes to its table and finishes its framed paper record: the five properties, a sort by a chosen property, ice and lukewarm water as solid or liquid, one shared property. Waiting groups sort pictures of familiar samples, unscored.
- **3 min. Swaps and clean-up.** About 30 seconds between windows to swap samples and wipe condensation; one last minute for damp wipes at tables.
- **5 min. The hat again.** Each child alone revisits the day 35 hat drawing, without feedback, unscored. The last group finishes its record in these minutes.
- Holds what is carried: each child their paper record. Tool: None. By hand: one gentle test, a framed record. Evidence: the day 36 item (unit plan field 5); the checklist and the record are kept, nothing is taught. Response on day 38 only. The teacher's answer chart is made before class from the named samples.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: stiff, bends, drop, dent, grip, slip, cover, pad, shelf, strap. Frames: "It dropped ___ centimeters." "It is good for ___ because ___." "For the ___ I choose ___."
- **Access.** Tactile samples; a helper presses or bends while the child decides and says what to do; the checklist records the child's decision and action, with physical help noted apart; large pictures; a scribe.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all prompts aloud; point, say or draw; day 36 allows a scribe for the record.
- **Reading load.** None alone; labels only, under 150 words a page.
- **Extension, same standard.** Choose and test one more tray sample for a job the child names and predict its result first.
