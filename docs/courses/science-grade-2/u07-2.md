# Section 7.2: Repeating the look and explaining what we found

## Header

<!-- skel -->
Science, Grade 2. Unit 7, Habitats. *What kinds of plants and animals do we find in each place, and how are the places alike and different?* Anchor: the class's own first survey of two schoolyard spots (days 66 to 73): see [s07.md](s07.md). **Days 70 to 73.** Days by kind: application 2 (70, 71), check 1 (72), respond 1 (73). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 2 of 2 in the unit.
- Day length (frames.csv): 35 to 40 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->


## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **2-LS4-1** (days 70, 71, 72, 73): Observe plants and animals and compare how many kinds of living things are found in different habitats.
- skill s2-habitat-diversity (grade 2)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 71's shared written explanation answers the anchor with both dated inventories: which kinds overlapped, which differed and what repeated counts support. Report inconclusive comparisons honestly.
>
> Question board: What lives here (66)? Does seeing more individuals mean more kinds (67)? Are other habitats alike (pond and forest, 68)? Can equal counts hide differences (desert and ocean shore, 69)? Would another survey agree (70)? What can we claim (71)? Each answer raises the next question. Day 72 transfers to unseen meadow, stream-edge and marsh scenes.
> Carried: day 66 begins a habitat picture model joining living things to observed places and recalled resources. Day 67 revises its graph to count kinds once. Days 68 and 69 revise patterns using shared kinds and equal-count contrasts. Day 70 adds a second dated inventory; day 71's consensus qualifies evidence-based claims with search limits. Systems, graph, patterns and claim-evidence reasoning remain available; day 72 uses this representation on new scenes.
<!-- /skel -->

- **2-LS4-1, this section's share (days 70 to 73).** "Observe plants and animals and compare how many kinds of living things are found in different habitats." Day 70 repeats the observation at the same two spots; day 71 compares two dated inventories and answers the anchor; day 72 observes and compares on three unseen scenes, alone; day 73 repairs and compares afresh.
- **Limits that bite here.** Observed variety only, without names; describing labels are provisional. A week apart, same area, time and labels reduce but do not remove differences caused by who looked and when. An inconclusive comparison is reported as inconclusive. No habitat ranking and no "wet means more" rule. Day 72 scenes are invented for practice, said aloud, not survey results.
- **Connections.** Math 2.MD.D.10 (picture graph, one picture per kind), days 70 to 72. Nature of science, "look for patterns and regular order": repeat careful counts, days 70 and 71. Claim, evidence and reason, "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___" (unit 5), day 71.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) One look can be luck, so we look again the same way. (2) A second date may differ because the place changed, the weather or time of day changed, or the looking changed; keeping area, time and labels the same lets us say the looking did not; it never lets us say all kinds were found. (3) "We cannot tell" is a finished answer. (4) Day 72 is unscored; its revised, retained version is scored once after day 73 on ten components, each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen (the unit plan's ten points). Nothing taught reappears in an item.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words with pictures: kind, same, different, again, hiding, plant, animal, count. Frame: "Both days we saw ___. Only on ___ we saw ___." Pointing and dictated reasons are accepted.
- **Access.** Raised outlines, enlarged high-contrast scenes, pointing, a scribe, and an indoor photograph path for any child. The adult supplies access and never selects kinds or comparisons.
- **Check accommodations.** Prompts and feedback are read aloud without adding content; answers are drawn, pointed or dictated.
- **Reading load.** None alone: labels only, under 40 words a page, everything else read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Repeat a third look and say what it adds, or compare two sets from home photographs without a ranking.

- **Teaching one child.** Day 70: look again at the same two spots as on day 66, five minutes each, same labels, draw and compare with last week's sheet.
