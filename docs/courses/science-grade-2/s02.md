## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Why has the bare path down our slope become a ditch while the grass beside it has not, and how could we stop it?"
Day 11 opens on path photographs; children sketch causes. Model source comparisons day 12 and measuring day 16; then release choices.

Accept equal ground, fan position and trial conditions, measured retreat and shapes altering wind movement. |

Limits: no numerical event durations, plate motion, canyons or erosion formulas. Erosion varies in speed. Fabric models cover; fresh seedlings lack established roots.
Carried: retain runoff arrows and source labels. Revise day 11's sketch on days 14 and 15 from slow and fast evidence. Sketch protection day 16, test day 17, improve day 18, revise from wind day 21. Strips become bars day 19; add wind day 22. Use models days 23 to 25; combine path evidence day 26.
Day 16 introduces occupied-path width and two limits; day 19 graphs retreat, then models the space tradeoff. Day 22 independently compares a field's windbreak (retreat 2 then 3, width 7) with a wall (retreat 5 then 6, width 4), in centimeters. Limits: retreat at most 6, width at most 5. Only the wall meets both; it protects less. Case invented for practice.

## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Land that looks unchanged today cannot change. | Present appearance replaces change evidence. Repair by ordering two views showing a changed edge. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| B | Erosion must always be slow. | A process is assigned one fixed speed. Repair by comparing gradual retreat with a sudden flood-cut channel. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| C | A strong-looking barrier needs no test evidence. | Appearance replaces measured performance. Repair by matching each design to its result bars before choosing. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| D | A chosen design has no weakness. | Choice hides a tradeoff. Repair by marking one unmet preference in the chosen design's data. | Anticipated, from memory. |
| E | Different starting slopes make no difference to a comparison. | Starting conditions are ignored. Repair by resetting equal slopes before comparing barriers. | Anticipated, from memory. |
