# Scope and sequence: Music, grades 9 to 12

Course music-grades-9-to-12, grades 9 to 12 as four levels, 640 days of 45 minutes; draft, 2026-11-26; fix pass 2026-11-26 (docs/reviews/sequence-music-grades-9-to-12-fixes-2026-11-26.md).

## How this file reads

The course runs as four levels of 160 days each, one level a school year, five 45-minute sessions a week (32 weeks a level). Days run 1 to 640 across the band: Level 1 is days 1 to 160, Level 2 days 161 to 320, Level 3 days 321 to 480, Level 4 days 481 to 640. `school_day` in days.csv counts the day inside its level (1 to 160), since the levels are taken in separate years; the week is (school_day - 1) // 5 + 1, and course day 161 is Level 2's school day 1. Every unit and check sits in the frame's weeks by that formula. A level is open to any grade that has met the one before it, so levels, not grades, name the years.

Which NCAS standards fall in which level:

- **Level 1:** NCAS composition and theory high school proficient (.Ia, .Ib and .Ic ids), 21 ids, rows mu-g9-*.
- **Level 2:** NCAS composition and theory high school accomplished (.IIa and .IIb ids), 21 ids, rows mu-g10-*.
- **Level 3:** NCAS composition and theory high school advanced (.IIIa and .IIIb ids), 21 ids, rows mu-g11-*.
- **Level 4:** no new ids: the four mu-g12-* rows apply the advanced (.III) ids again on self-directed work, scored against Level 3's bar with a higher bar for independence.

Readiness and retrieval. Level 1 is open to students with no K to 8 music, so every Level 1 unit has a readiness check on the grade 8 rows it builds on, with its ten-minute reteach, which takes the warm-up of the unit's day 2, before its study, so the hook day stays clean (frame For the lead 2); the gate's count by grade number may print notes for it. In Levels 2 to 4 each unit's day 2 or day 3 opener retrieves the rows of the level before (the `retrieves` column), and day 1 of every unit stays clean for its hook. Each retrieval day's note gives the opener's minutes (five a row) and where a reteach goes: ten minutes a row missed, in the next day's warm-up and first minutes, whole class if more than a third missed, otherwise a small group (UNIT-PLAN rule 6). A student placed into Level 2 or 3 meets the same tasks.

The four processes (create, perform, respond, connect) are listed per unit below by course day, counted from what each day has students do. Each level holds all four, and each unit touches at least the processes its frame row names; a unit need not claim all four, and no day claims an id outside its unit's owned rows. Where a process is practiced without a claim (unit 17's performing), the day's note says "practiced, not claimed". Every unit with a showing has one critique day and a revision day before its end-of-unit assessment. Equipment, safe use of space, the device-free path, recording consent and sensitivity flags sit in each day's note; the course uses no tools, supplies or safety columns.

## Logic model

- **Inputs:** a 45-minute lesson five days a week, step cards, score cards, listening maps, the voice and found sounds.
- **Activities:** theory taught in steps, works studied by ear and from the score with their setting, composing with a critique and a revision, a showing each level.
- **Outputs:** a portfolio of scores, draft logs and takes, one piece a unit, and a concert each level, with two marks a unit per student on the main look-for.
- **Outcome claimed:** met marks on the level's 21 ids and growth on the look-fors. Nothing here has been tried with students, and Curio Class makes no claim that music study raises any other result (APPROACH "Sources").
