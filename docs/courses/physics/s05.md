## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How can moving a magnet make electricity, and how does that power a whole city?"
Day 66 opens with an original bicycle-dynamo observation sequence: stopped, turning slowly, turning faster, stopped again. Students notice brightness changes without an explanation, sketch mechanisms and open the question board. Field 10 names the sequence to make.
Day 73 connects wheel motion, changing field, current and lamp energy, then extends to turbines and power lines. Teach explanatory writing: ordered ideas, relevant evidence, varied transitions, precise disciplinary language and a supported conclusion. Day 74 independently explains which generator account fits the records, checking these five components; this is unscored practice.
Model safe measurement and uncertainty on day 68. Thereafter ask students to justify controls before trials, reconcile conflicting records and revise claims rather than supply the explanation.

**New case, invented for practice.** A closing door moves a magnet through a fixed coil connected to a sensor circuit. There is no battery in this harvester.
