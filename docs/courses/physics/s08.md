<!-- skel -->
- **HS-ESS1-1:** Build an evidence-based model of the sun's lifetime and of how fusion in its core releases energy that reaches Earth as radiation. Emphasis: how fusion energy travels out of the core to Earth; evidence might include how massive and long-lived other stars are, solar flares, the sunspot cycle of about 11 years and longer changes. Limit: No details of the atomic and subatomic steps of fusion in the sun.
- **HS-ESS1-3:** Explain to others how stars make elements over the course of their lives. Emphasis: which elements a star makes depends on its mass and its life stage. Limit: The many separate element-making pathways in stars of different masses are not assessed.
- **HS-ESS1-2:** Explain the Big Bang theory using evidence from light spectra, the motion of distant galaxies and the makeup of matter in the universe. Emphasis: redshift showing expansion, the cosmic microwave background as leftover radiation, and an ordinary matter mix of about three quarters hydrogen and one quarter helium, as the theory predicts.
- **RST.11-12.7:** Integrate and evaluate multiple sources of information presented in diverse formats and media (e.g., quantitative data, video, multimedia) in order to address a question or solve a problem. Parts, by verb: whole standard.
