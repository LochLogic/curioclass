# Scope and sequence: Economics, with personal finance

Course `economics`, grade 12 (taken in grade 11 or 12), spring half (school days 81 to 160), draft (2026-11-14). The days live in data/plans/economics/days.csv; this page gives the judgment behind them.

The course meets every school day of the spring half (50 minutes, no timers shown to students), so course day n is school day 80 + n and the `school_day` column is filled. Week = (school day - 1) // 5 + 1, so the course runs weeks 17 to 32. Every unit closes with a one-day end-of-unit assessment built on an unseen task, and the next day is its respond day (the response time, counted once). No unit is over 25 days, so none holds a mid-unit assessment. Days with hard-history, health or disability content, and the days where families' money could come up, carry a sensitivity flag in the note column; the sensitivity agent's read (docs/pipeline/briefs/sensitivity-read.md) comes with each unit plan. Literacy codes (RH, WHST) are also-met and sit only in notes. The partial base, Principles of Economics 2e, is credited by chapter in the notes; there is no full base text, so there is no source-map.csv.

Personal finance is taught inside units 4, 8 and 9 and adds no days. The six personal finance areas of the adoption bar sit here: earning income (unit 8 days 59 and 60, with taxes in unit 4 day 32 and the tax return on day 64), spending (unit 8 days 61 to 63), saving (unit 9 days 68 and 69), investing (unit 9 days 73, 74 and 76), managing credit (unit 9 days 70 to 72) and managing risk (unit 8 day 65, unit 9 day 75).

## Unit 1. Scarcity, choice and economic systems (days 1 to 8, school days 81 to 88, weeks 17 to 18)

Arc: scarcity and resources, then opportunity cost and marginal thinking, the production possibilities curve, incentives and interest, policy goals in conflict, and economic systems with property rights. Then the end-of-unit assessment and its respond day. Scarcity and opportunity cost come first because every later unit reasons from them. The curve follows so unemployment, growth and productivity have a picture before incentives and systems use them.

- Readiness: day 1, on grade 8's public budgets and opportunity cost (ss8-public-budget, idle two or more grades). The reteach is a station on day 2.
- End-of-unit assessment, day 7: an unseen production possibilities curve for a made-up country and a policy case, covering T1.1 to T1.9 and D2.Eco.1, 3 and 9. Respond day 8.
- Candidate: marginal thinking, kept inside T1.2 on day 2, 0 added days.

## Unit 2. Markets and prices (days 9 to 18, school days 89 to 98, weeks 18 to 20)

Arc: demand and supply curves, then equilibrium and prices as signals, the same reasoning in product, labor and financial markets, shifts and how they move equilibrium, elasticity, and price controls with an argument from marginal benefit and cost. The order follows what the graph needs: equilibrium, then shifts, then controls.

- Readiness: none. Day 9 opens with a retrieval task on the supply and demand graph met in grades 7 to 10.
- End-of-unit assessment, day 17: an unseen market case from a supplied data table (equilibrium, a shift, a control), covering T2.1 to T2.4, T2.6 to T2.10, D2.Eco.2 and 8. Respond day 18.
- Candidate: consumer and producer surplus, dropped as a taught day. It is an extension named in the unit plan, because the unit's ten days are full with the anchor's rows.

## Unit 3. Business, competition and work (days 19 to 27, school days 99 to 107, weeks 20 to 22)

Arc: profit and business forms, how firms raise money and what unions and nonprofits do, market structures, costs and marginal analysis, judging a real market's competition (with monopsony and the trusts retaught in brief), productivity, then earnings and productivity. Market structures need unit 2's graph. Productivity comes last because it sets up unit 8's earning.

- Readiness: none. Day 19 opens with a retrieval task on industrial markets (grade 10).
- End-of-unit assessment, day 26: an unseen market from public data (its structure, its competition and its wages), covering T2.5, T3.1 to T3.9, D2.Eco.4, 5 and 13. Respond day 27.
- Candidate: costs of production (fixed, variable, marginal), kept inside T3.6 on day 22, 0 added days.

## Unit 4. Government in the economy (days 28 to 34, school days 108 to 114, weeks 22 to 23)

Arc: public goods, market failures and externalities, then antitrust and regulation, costs and benefits of a policy, government budgets and kinds of taxes, and deficits, debt and tax rates since 1950 (PFL.T1.3). Market failure needs units 2 and 3. Budgets follow, because the cost of government action is paid from them.

- Readiness: day 28, on grade 8's public budgets (ss8-public-budget, dormant). The reteach is a station on day 29.
- End-of-unit assessment, day 33: an unseen policy case (a market failure and a proposed tax or rule) judged by costs and benefits, with a budget item, covering T4.1 to T4.7, PFL.T1.3, D2.Eco.6 and 7. Respond day 34.
- Candidate: externalities on the supply and demand graph, kept inside T4.1 on day 28, 0 added days.

## Course-level check 1 (days 35 to 36, school days 115 to 116, weeks 23 to 24)

Day 35 (school day 115, week 23, the frame's target) is the midpoint check on units 1 to 4: an unseen market case to graph, a shift to predict and a policy to judge. Day 36 is its respond day.

## Unit 5. Measuring the whole economy (days 37 to 44, school days 117 to 124, weeks 24 to 25)

Arc: the circular flow, GDP and growth, then unemployment and inflation (with the types of unemployment), causes of inflation and aggregate supply and demand on their own graph, current indicators, national income as the result of choices, and business cycles with an earlier banking crisis retrieved. This is the turn from one market to the whole economy. Both new models open on the first two days and are used after.

- Readiness: none. Day 37 opens with a retrieval task on the Great Depression (grade 10).
- End-of-unit assessment, day 43: an unseen set of current indicators (source and date given), covering T5.1 to T5.6, D2.Eco.10 and 11. Respond day 44.
- Candidate: types of unemployment (frictional, structural, cyclical), kept inside T5.2 on day 38, 0 added days.

## Unit 6. Money, banks and policy (days 45 to 51, school days 125 to 131, weeks 25 to 27)

Arc: what money does and the money supply, then banks, money creation and the Federal Reserve, fiscal and monetary policy defined, the Fed's tools, and which policy suits a recession or high inflation. It needs unit 5's measures, since policy is judged by its effect on them, and unit 4's budgets carry into fiscal policy.

- Readiness: day 45, short items on what money is, from grade 7's Roman money and debasement (ss7-roman-economy, idle four grades or more). No reteach lesson, as the day teaches the functions of money in full. The frame names the source row but not the check (For the lead 1).
- End-of-unit assessment, day 50: an unseen scenario with indicators, choose and defend a fiscal or monetary response, covering T6.1 to T6.4, T4.8 to T4.10 and D2.Eco.12. Respond day 51.
- Candidate: money creation through lending, kept inside T6.3 on day 46, 0 added days.

## Unit 7. Trade and the global economy (days 52 to 58, school days 132 to 138, weeks 27 to 28)

Arc: why people and countries trade, then comparative advantage on made-up and real data, tariffs and quotas and the free trade debate, the balance of trade and payments and exchange rates, and productivity and globalization's effects. Trade uses unit 1's opportunity cost, unit 2's markets and unit 5's measures.

- Readiness: none. Day 52 opens with a retrieval task on globalization (grade 10).
- End-of-unit assessment, day 57: unseen trade and income data for two countries, covering T7.1 to T7.8, D2.Eco.14, D2.Eco.15 and D2.Geo.11. Respond day 58.
- Candidate: none in the frame.

## Unit 8. Earning, spending and protecting (days 59 to 67, school days 139 to 147, weeks 28 to 30)

Arc: choosing a job, then the labor market and a first-year budget with a pay stub, advertising, ways to pay and consumer protection, the year's informed action (days 62 and 63), a first tax return, then insurance and identity protection. Earning comes before spending, and the informed action follows the consumer protection day it draws on. The look back on the action is on day 80.

- Readiness: day 59, on grade 8's budgets (ss8-public-budget, dormant). The reteach is a station that day.
- End-of-unit assessment, day 66: an unseen case on given figures (a described person's job offer, budget, ways to pay and insurance quotes), covering PFL.T1.1, T1.2, T1.4 to T1.6, T4.1 and T4.2. Respond day 67.
- Candidate: the pay stub and withholding, kept inside the budget day (day 60), 0 added days. The first tax return, kept, 1 day (day 64). Renting a first home, dropped as a taught day and named as an extension in the unit plan: the nine days are full.

## Unit 9. Saving, credit and investing (days 68 to 78, school days 148 to 158, weeks 30 to 32)

Arc: banks and interest, then real and nominal rates, saving policies and the comparison matrix (T6.5), credit as one running case, not repaying and a credit plan, paying for college, financial markets and diversification, a made-up portfolio tracked on paper across dated observations, the regulators, and the finished savings or investment plan. Saving comes first, then credit, borrowing, investing, and the regulators who watch all four. It needs unit 5's inflation, unit 6's banks and unit 2's markets.

- Readiness: day 68, on grade 8's interest on borrowing and investing (ss8-public-budget, dormant). The reteach is a station on day 69.
- End-of-unit assessment, day 77: an unseen made-up credit offer and savings goal on given figures, covering PFL.T2.1 to T2.6, PFL.T3.1 to T3.10, ECON.T2.11, T6.5 and T6.6, with the portfolio record scored on the same rubric. Respond day 78.
- Candidate: paying for college, kept, 1 day (day 72).

Check set additions with no home code: 2 of the 4 allowed days used (the tax return and paying for college).

## Course-level check 2 (days 79 to 80, school days 159 to 160, week 32)

Day 79 (school day 159, week 32, the finals week) is the final check on the whole course: an unseen data set on the economy, a policy to judge and a personal finance case. Day 80 is its respond day and the look back on the unit 8 informed action.
