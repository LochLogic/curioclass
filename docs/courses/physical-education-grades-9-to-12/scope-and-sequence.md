# Scope and sequence: Physical Education, grades 9 to 12

Course: physical-education-grades-9-to-12. Draft, 2026-11-26, written from docs/courses/physical-education-grades-9-to-12/frame.md. The day list is data/plans/physical-education-grades-9-to-12/days.csv (320 rows); this page says why the days fall as they do. Curio Class has not tried this course with students and claims no effect on fitness or health.

## How to read this page

- **Years and standards.** One band, four years, 80 sessions of 45 minutes each (2 to 3 a week), 74 in units and 6 on course-level checks. Grades 9 and 10 own all 43 SHAPE level 12 indicators (SHAPE.S1.12.x to SHAPE.S4.12.x): grade 9 owns most of S1 to S3, grade 10 owns the rest and all of S4. Grades 11 and 12 own none; their days carry the codes they use in new settings, so a student who takes only grades 9 and 10 meets every indicator.
- **school_day.** school_day = 2 x course day, counted across the band (frame.py splits the band into years of 160 school days). Within a year: school day = 2d - 160(y - 1) and week = (school day - 1) // 5 + 1. Every mid-year check sits in week 16, every year-end check in week 32, except grade 12, whose year-end check sits in week 28 so a senior who leaves early has finished it; grade 12 unit 24 runs weeks 29 to 32. This is the frame's calendar; 9 to 12 takes finals week only (CALENDAR.md), so week 16 clashes are not breaks under rule 4.
- **The day.** Every day runs the lesson shape: instant activity, warm-up, focus, cool-down, closure, at least half moderate to vigorous. Each unit's first day carries the hook, the compelling question and first teaching of the main cue with the baseline watch (unit 1 holds the hook and readiness on day 1 and the baseline on day 2). Retrieval sits in the second day's instant activity (the retrieves column). Two-day checks: groups 1 to 3 do the skill task, watched about five minutes a group, while groups 4 to 6 write the knowledge task; the next day swaps them. Conduct is marked once in the unit from the record kept in practice. The respond day ends each unit and carries the next unit's readiness where one is due.
- **Shared cards**, quoted in the notes word for word in the 9 to 12 form of SEL STANDARD section 7: the goal steps card ("the goal, dated steps, the trade-off accepted, a check date, a verdict") in units 6, 12, 16 and 20; the calm-down card as a coping plan ("stop, breathe, name it, choose, go on", a tool for each of three settings, a back-up tool and a review date) in units 6, 12 and 21.
- **Safety and space.** Every unit's notes name its space, gear and no-equipment or wet-weather path. No maximal lift; water units stay on dry land; striking-tool, mat, resistance and outdoor units are read by the safety agent before they go live. No weighing, body measures, calorie or diet talk; fitness records are private and never graded. Yoga is stretching and breathing only. Groups are teacher-made; no elimination, no human targets, no gender split. Calm steps are offered, never a punishment; the teacher page names the school counselor and carries health's disclosure steps.
- **Sensitivity.** Bhangra (unit 5, confirmed by Cory 2026-11-26) stays a candidate until the sensitivity agent confirms it at a Punjabi or Indian cultural institution's page. Sepak takraw (unit 9) is confirmed at a Southeast Asian body's own page. Unit 22's chosen game is read with its source before the event.
- **Sources not loaded.** The cultural sources of units 5, 9 and 22 are dated in the frame but not opened this run; the CDC drowning page (unit 11, life jackets and currents) was opened 2026-11-25 by the frame writer, and the National Weather Service's "Cold Water Hazards and Safety" page (cold-water shock and hypothermia) on 2026-11-26 by the expert read; reach and throw rescues come from the Palm Beach County Drowning Prevention Coalition's "Reach, Throw, Don't Go" page, opened 2026-11-26. No source-map.csv this run.

## Grade outcomes

As the frame sets them: grade 9 shows form in endurance, strength, two lifetime sports, backyard games and a designed dance phrase, and plans its own skill practice; grade 10 adds skill-related fitness, a mixed plan with rest, outdoor and water safety, games in culture, leadership and a plan to stay active after school; grade 11 improves one chosen activity, trains by effort, leads a session and runs a term program; grade 12 coaches from its own cue sheet, runs and reviews a full program, manages emotion in hard moments, leads a community event, plans an active adult week and reflects on what PE has meant.

## Grade 9 (course days 1 to 80, SHAPE grade 12 indicators, first year)

### Unit 1. Endurance form and effort (days 1 to 13, weeks 1 to 6)
Arc: form cue, effort scale, heart rate times six, zones and FITT, awareness of others, then a twelve-minute session. Why first: every later unit's warm-up uses the effort scale and FITT. Readiness day 1 (g7-effort-aerobic, g7-fitt-principles); retrieval day 2 (g8-fitness-dance-apply, g8-fitness-test-track, g8-leading). Math lean 6.RP.A.3 bridged in place. Check days 11 and 12. Candidate kept: the private twelve-minute log (day 10, repeated day 74).

### Unit 2. Strength form and feedback (days 14 to 24, weeks 6 to 10)
Arc: squat and plank, push-up and row, lunge with "one cue, one fix", a partner session by FITT. Why here: it reuses unit 1's FITT on a new type. Retrieval day 15. Check days 22 and 23; day 24 carries unit 3's readiness (g6-analyze-skill). Candidate kept: the partner cue card (day 20).

### Unit 3. Lifetime sports and force (days 25 to 37, weeks 10 to 15)
Arc: volleyball pass, set and serve, three-a-side, target golf swing, force and rotation, a before-and-after change. Retrieval day 26 (grade 8 game rows). Science lean MS-PS2-2 used in words. Check days 35 and 36. Candidate kept: before-and-after self-check (day 32).

### Unit 4. Backyard games and tactics (days 41 to 52, weeks 17 to 21)
Arc: disc, bean bag, badminton, then tactics in unit 3's sports and in backyard games. Retrieval day 42. Check days 50 and 51; day 52 carries unit 5's readiness (g7-dance-ideas). Candidate kept: the family backyard-game card (day 48).

### Unit 5. Dance design and forms (days 53 to 64, weeks 22 to 26)
Arc: a set sequence, canon and call and response, bhangra's basic step, then a designed phrase drafted (58), given feedback (59) and revised (60). Dance 9 to 12 Level 1 unit 2 keeps performance quality; PE keeps motor sequence and form. Check days 62 and 63. Candidate dropped: the class canon for another class (needs another class's time; some students prefer not to perform).

### Unit 6. Practice plans, principles and choices (days 65 to 77, weeks 26 to 31)
Arc: goal cycle and baseline, the four principles, the plan drafted (67) and revised (69), sampling for S4 groundwork, practice by the plan, the twelve-minute retest. Retrieval day 66 (g8-goals-obstacles, g8-reflect, g8-fitness-test-track, g7-fitt-principles; health 9 to 12 units 2 and 15 and SEL unit 1 in their own words). Check days 75 and 76. Candidate kept: retest of unit 1's log (day 74).

## Grade 10 (course days 81 to 160)

### Unit 7. Stretching and skill-related fitness (days 81 to 91, weeks 1 to 5)
Arc: dynamic and static stretching, agility, reaction, low plyometrics, a group-led warm-up. Readiness on day 81 (g7-skill-fitness-use), the year's first day after the grade 9 respond day (assess readiness, lead call 14). g8-fitness-dance-apply opens as retrieval on day 82 because grade 9 used it through the rows built on it (the gate's one-hop count agrees). Check days 89 and 90; day 91 carries unit 8's readiness (g7-rest, g8-tech-judge). Candidate kept: the group-designed warm-up (day 87).

### Unit 8. A mixed plan, rest and technology (days 92 to 106, weeks 5 to 11)
Arc: FITT by type, rest, plan drafted (94) and revised (95), a tool judged, quality of life, a first-term choice, a mid-way change. Retrieval day 93. Check days 104 and 105; day 106 carries unit 9's readiness (g8-culture-matters). Candidate kept: the plan's log reused unscored at year-end.

### Unit 9. Games in culture and leadership (days 107 to 117, weeks 11 to 15)
Arc: the 1896 Olympic revival, sepak takraw (modified), the leader's cue, movement and group problems, football in England and Brazil and the country-dance case; the case drafted (112) and revised (114). Social studies lean ss6-complex-societies carried by the cases. Check days 115 and 116. Candidate kept: a family game as the case, by the student's choice.

### Unit 10. Outdoor activity, safety and food (days 121 to 134, weeks 17 to 22)
Arc: map setting, the safety checklist, routes, outdoor tactics, food and water, a route plan, an orienteering course. Readiness day 120 (g8-outdoor-safety, g7-eat-move-well). Health 9 to 12 unit 6's source rating retrieved on day 127. Check days 132 and 133; day 134 carries unit 11's readiness (g8-water). Candidate kept: the family walking-route card (day 131).

### Unit 11. Water safety on dry land (days 135 to 141, weeks 22 to 25)
Arc: reach, life jacket fit, throw and open-water facts, scenes. One check day, 140. Candidate kept: the family water-safety card (day 139).

### Unit 12. Choosing activity after high school (days 142 to 157, weeks 25 to 31)
Arc: expressive and social blocks, fitting levels, emotion strategies compared, what affects staying active, the plan drafted (150) and revised (152), what PE has meant. All nine S4 indicators are owned here; S4.12.3 and S4.12.4 get a day of use in the blocks (days 145 and 147, a private log line on the health part and the interest), S4.12.9 a drafted reflection on day 154. Check days 155 and 156. Candidate kept: the plan as grade 11's program first page.

## Grade 11 (course days 161 to 240)

## Grade 12 (course days 241 to 320)

Day 280 carries the fallback readiness. Candidate kept: the event checklist (day 287).

### Unit 23. An active adult week (days 295 to 307, weeks 22 to 27)

### Unit 24. Reflection (days 311 to 320, weeks 29 to 32)
Arc: chosen sessions, the portfolio, the reflection drafted (314) and revised (316), one check day (319), respond (320). Day 310 carries the fallback readiness. Candidate kept: the letter to a grade 9 student, optional.
