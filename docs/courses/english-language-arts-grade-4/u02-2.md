## 9. Supports and extension

Words: scale, label, chronology, comparison, cause, solution, paraphrase. Frame: "This display adds ___ to the words." Enlarge axes; describe teaching labels. Scribe content without interpreting. Independent check reading stays silent; read listening text exactly twice. Private/signed live talk is available, support recorded.

Rereading passages are 150 to 250 words, about two to three reading minutes. P48 and displays use separate leaves. Each leaf stays within 350 printed words at 14 point; prompt sentences within 15 words. Existing core sentence-cap issues go to the text owner; do not rewrite them in a day. Counts never measure real survival. Content: non-graphic threats; no animal handling. Extend by identifying what a display cannot establish.
