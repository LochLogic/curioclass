## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Revise the presentation and assess reading connections among story, drama and their presentations.
- **Note:** Reads: g4-u1-lt. End component: 20-minute cold reading and fixed live reading/staging comparison; 25-minute presentation revision; 15-minute feedback. Every listed reading code has its own prompt. Base lesson map: G4M1U3L7. 15 minutes partner rereading of the named anchor passage for purpose and understanding, with feedback on accuracy, rate, expression and self-correction; the teacher listens to a rotating group. The teacher records only the group due in the current window, five children at two minutes each within the 15 minutes, cycling six groups of five over three weeks; any matching record from the preceding ten days is reused. Partner practice is not a second individual screen. Assessment evidence: use the scope and sequence evidence key for every listed sampled code and every part; partner practice is not a second individual assessment; the assigned teacher-listening record supplies RF.4.4 evidence where those codes are listed. Parts: 20 min. Cold reading collection; 25 min. Presentation collection; 15 min. Feedback; 10 min. Word work; 5 min. Grammar retrieval; 15 min. Partner rereading. Own drafts are unjudged. Print authorized text leaves only. No visitor or recording is required; paper figures supply live staging.
