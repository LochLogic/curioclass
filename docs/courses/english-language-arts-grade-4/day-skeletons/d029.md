## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Draft an original poem with deliberate images and line breaks.
- **Note:** Reads: g4-u1-01, g4-u1-05. Model first; daily long-word pattern check in the skills block. Five-minute pattern probe inside the 10-minute word block; next day correct or extend from evidence. Base lesson map: G4M1U3L2. 15 minutes partner rereading of the named anchor passage for purpose and understanding, with feedback on accuracy, rate, expression and self-correction; the teacher listens to a rotating group. The teacher records only the group due in the current window, five children at two minutes each within the 15 minutes, cycling six groups of five over three weeks; any matching record from the preceding ten days is reused. Partner practice is not a second individual screen.
