## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Prepare a discussion with several details about a character, setting and event.
- **Note:** Reads: g4-u1-01, g4-u1-04. Reread journal entries and Hughes poem; locate thoughts, words and actions and descriptions of setting and event. Base lesson map: G4M1U1L11. 15 minutes partner rereading of the named anchor passage for purpose and understanding, with feedback on accuracy, rate, expression and self-correction; the teacher listens to a rotating group. The teacher records only the group due in the current window, five children at two minutes each within the 15 minutes, cycling six groups of five over three weeks; any matching record from the preceding ten days is reused. Partner practice is not a second individual screen.
