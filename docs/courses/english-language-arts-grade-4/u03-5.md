**s03-X102.** Cards: "Bees enter Anansi's jar to settle his challenge. He closes it and sends it away." "Heracles knocks away Hydra heads. More grow back. Iolaus helps stop them returning." Ask: "Summarize each event. Compare their theme and trial pattern." Key: Anansi tricks bees during bargain tests; Heracles changes methods with help during assigned labors. Both overcome difficulty beyond direct force; deception differs from cooperation. Meets: "Anansi traps bees; Heracles needs help. Different approaches solve different trials." Wrong: "Both trap bees" (W); approval instead of summary (E). Shape: two summaries plus theme/pattern comparison; avoid complete comparative summaries and both reserved episodes. Teach one label/defect instead.

**s03-X103.** Cards: "Momotaro stops his companions' quarrel. Their journey continues." "Anansi ties the snake beside a measuring stick." "Heracles holds the sky while Atlas fetches apples. This is a Herculean burden." Ask: "Compare their solutions and event patterns. Explain Herculean here." Key: cooperation in a quest, deception in bargain tests, exchange in assigned labors; exceptional effort recalling Heracles. Meets: "Their solutions differ; holding the sky requires exceptional strength." Wrong: identical plots (W); carrying fruit defines Herculean (X). Shape: three-way solution/pattern comparison plus allusion; avoid completed charts, these cases and full allusion explanations.

**s03-X104.** Hear twice before print, invented modern arguments. A, Charge by the Load: "Set larger tolls for boats carrying heavier loads. Imagine one boat carrying ten sacks and another carrying only one. The larger load brings more goods through the canal. It should contribute more to upkeep." B, Count the Work: "Weight is not the only measure of canal work. Both boats must pass through a lock. The gate opens and closes for each, even if one carries little. A toll plan should account for that shared service too." Ask: "Explain each side's support. Give a text-linked follow-up for a peer who agrees." Key: unequal goods support unequal contributions; common gate work supports considering shared service. Ask how the small boat's gate passage affects the toll proposal. Neither side is uniquely stronger. Wrong: opinion alone (H); unrelated favorite-boat question (M). Shape: two heard supports plus a linked follow-up; avoid paired complete support explanations, toll/load/gate cards and whole-circuit rehearsal. Collect every paper before print.
