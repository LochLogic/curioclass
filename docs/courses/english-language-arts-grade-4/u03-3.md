**X92.** First read g4-u3-05, scene 1's closing sequence from "Ada picks up the basket" onward. Teacher's seated performance changes only Ben's last action: he keeps holding the stake. Perform after the script is released. Use separate lines for what changed and its effect. Ask: "Compare Ben's last action in the script with his last action in the performance."

Key: In the script Ben takes his hand off the stake and picks up the boat. In the performance he keeps holding the stake.
Wrong: "Holding the stake follows the stage direction." (AI).
Shape: Compare the final action across two forms.

**s03-X92-effect.** Ask: "How does the performed ending change Ben's response to Mother?"

Key: Holding the stake instead of turning to the boat makes him seem unwilling to follow her direction to leave it alone. Accept another effect grounded in that change.
Wrong: "He follows Mother's request in exactly the same way." (AI).
Shape: Explain one effect of the changed action.

**X93.** First read g4-u3-05, scene 2 from "I was thinking about a journey" to its final direction. Ask: "Describe Ben's response to Ada in this part of the scene."

Key: Ben realizes Ada was thinking about the start of the journey. He stops counting trips until he knows what the blocks mean and sets only one block beside the boat. Accept a supported description of that response.
Wrong: "He is nice." (F).
Shape: Describe the character's response from words and actions.

**s03-X93-progressive.** Ask: "Write one present progressive sentence about Ben's last action."

Key: He is setting one block beside the boat. Also accept another accurate description of the final direction using is plus a verb ending in -ing.
Wrong: "He set one block beside the boat." (G).
Shape: Use the present progressive for one stage action.

**X94.** First read g4-u3-05, scene 3 from "A gust lifts one corner" through the end. Ask: "Explain how the wind affects Ada's actions."

Key: The gust lifts a corner of the map, so Ada catches it.
Wrong: "It is a nice place." (F).
Shape: Explain a setting effect.

**s03-X94-phrase.** Ask: "Describe where Ada looks after Mother folds the map, using a complete prepositional phrase."

Key: Ada looks toward the tree. Accept a complete phrase that accurately identifies the tree as the direction of her gaze.
Wrong: "Ada looks toward." (U).
Shape: Use a complete prepositional phrase in a scene description.

**X95.** First read g4-u3-05, scene 4 from "Ada slides a blank sheet" onward. Teacher performs Ben placing one apple beside the sheet instead of two. Ask: "Explain one mismatch between the reading and script."

Key: The script gives two apples; performance gives one. Claiming these match is AI.
Shape: One script/performance mismatch using the reserved scene ending. Practice inspects a separate feature; no apple/sheet rehearsal.

**X96.** Hear g4-u3-02, M3's first sentence and final three sentences, first here. Identify the omitted middle explicitly; do not present the selections as continuous. Gloss interest, augmented, subscribed and transit duties without explaining the argument. Replay the same selections for both lines. Allow time for glossing, two readings and two short responses. Collect before giving any print copy. Ask: "What reason does the speaker give for confidence in paying for the canal?"

Key: The memorial expects new revenues to pay borrowing costs. Income from public salt works or higher state land values could cover interest, or tolls could pay down the debt.
Wrong: "They want the canal." (H). "Toll payments already paid the debt." (B).
Shape: Identify the heard reason.

**s03-X96-detail.** Ask: "Which specific detail does the speaker give about pledged land?"

Key: Individuals had pledged 106,632 acres for the work. Accept an accurate statement of the quantity and its pledged status.
Wrong: "Individuals had already paid the state more than a million dollars in cash." (C).
Shape: Locate a specific detail in the heard selection.

C95-ownership is a separate independent record in the last two minutes of Word work: "Correct the ownership word. There map remains beside the basket." Key: Their map remains beside the basket; possessive there V. Shape: one fresh ownership correction, never a complete paired performance/grammar response. Return it with X95 in the next named repair slot.
