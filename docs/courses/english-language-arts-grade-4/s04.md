**Installment 4 checkpoint.** Maya stood beside the attic table, eager to open the blue tin. Inside lay a folded letter and an envelope with a faded stamp. She copied the last line onto her chart: an initial, R., followed by a looping mark that might have been part of the signature. Below it she wrote, Ruth wanted everyone to vote.

"Does that letter say Ruth?" her brother asked.

Maya looked again. Only an initial appeared where she had expected a name. The letter spoke about a meeting but did not say who had been allowed inside. Her excitement had carried her farther than the words.

"Ruth?" she said. She added the question mark to her chart. "I want to find out, but I shouldn't put words in her mouth."

Checking every letter suddenly seemed a Herculean task. Maya remembered the hero facing one difficult labor after another. Still, she drew a second column beside her first. She headed it Questions and moved her claim into it. The tin held a beginning, not all the answers.
