## 9. Supports and extension

Words: conclude, addition, therefore, precise, contrast, revise, conjunction. Frame: "This source supports ___; it does not show ___." Bilingual teaching glossary, accessible print and a scribe for content; no supplied conclusion at exits. Read at most 300 authorized words per source block. Oral support does not replace measured independent reading. Extend by testing another interpretation of a source, never adding an unsupported historical fact.
