Wrong: assumes background (AN); no judgment (Z); promised money already received (B).

Shape: compose an introduction for a different reader's financing question. Avoid complete introduction models, this audience and the future-income overclaim. Own introduction remains unjudged.

**s03-X112.** Reference: g4-u3-01 chapter 5 and g4-u3-03 first reason. Draft: "The modern speech proves which dangers actual workers faced." Wrong: reconstruction treated as witness (AA); unsupported reason (A).

Shape: compose a source-corrected reason paragraph from history and reconstructed argument. Avoid this false labor-testimony claim and full two-source paragraph models. Field 11 names the source row; no extra labor facts are added.

**s03-X113.** Reference: g4-u3-03 incomplete-crossing paragraph. Draft: "One part works. Every question is answered." Ask: "Revise this reason with a source detail and a meaningful link. Open key: a short working portion does not settle full-route questions; use a complete place phrase, two complete joined clauses and an appropriate link. Meets: "Boats may pass through one section, but the full connection remains unfinished. For instance, the speech warns that one crossing cannot serve the whole route. I favor fuller reports." Wrong: through without an object (U); comma between subject and verb (T); no textual support (A).

Shape: revise a reason paragraph with evidence, link, phrase and compound comma. Avoid complete combined-feature revisions and this partial-route overclaim. Each sentence direction remains at most 15 words.

**s03-X114.** Reference: g4-u3-02 M3 and g4-u3-03 borrowing paragraph. Earlier reason: "Interest needs payment before every hoped-for income arrives."
