## 5. What is carried, and instruments


Review grade 3 u01-5's "heading: the main idea of a group of notes, saying what the group is for" and plan made of groups, without library cases. Review its dictionary and peer look-for routines, and grade 3 u04-6's "trace the writing process through the essay folder: plan, draft, feedback, revision, edit." Children carry source tags/folder; remove supplied aspect labels after day 22.

Review section 1.4's written rhetorical-situation: reader, purpose, form, and paragraph-frame: topic, grouped facts, logical links, precise words and related closing. Day 18 retrieves them; it does not claim first teaching. Ideal instruments: sources, flawed components, movable notes, folder. Print fits; no key tool/unbuilt spec. Hand-copy short cards if needed. Quotation models use invented speech or an actual printed sentence, never invented poet speech.

Student meanings before use: explicit means directly stated; inference means an unstated conclusion supported by details. A quotation keeps the source's exact words. Schwa is an unstressed vowel sound; check its spelling. Stress gives one syllable greater emphasis. A fragment is an incomplete thought punctuated as complete; a run-on fuses complete thoughts without a fitting boundary. Develop adds evidence and explanation; revise changes meaning or organization. Essay-structure has an introduction, two body paragraphs by aspect, and a conclusion. Review section 1.4 precise: specific enough to identify the intended subject or feature.
