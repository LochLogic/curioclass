**s01-X18, two paper items.**

**s01-X18-1.** Len wrote a verse each morning. Rejected pages filled his basket. He tried again after lunch.
Claim: Len wrote each morning.
Label this claim stated or inferred.

Key: Stated: the first sentence directly says he wrote each morning.

Wrong answers: Inferred. (A)

**s01-X18-2.** Len wrote a verse each morning. Rejected pages filled his basket. He tried again after lunch.
Claim: Len kept trying despite difficulty.
Label this claim stated or inferred.

Key: Inferred: rejected pages and trying again support persistence; the profile never directly names persistence.

Wrong answers: Stated. (A); His basket held prizes. (O)
Shape: independently classify two claims from a fresh profile. Avoid E/I sorting or label-matching practice; reject an unsupported claim instead.

**s01-X19.**

**s01-X19.** Iva erased the last verse. She tapped its beat, then changed two words. She kept the new version.
