Ideal instruments: print sources, weak-example cards, dated folder. Print fits; no key tool or unbuilt spec. Hand-copy short cards if necessary.

Current instruction defines purpose as what writing helps its reader do or understand. Form is the kind of writing used. Precise means specific enough to identify the subject or feature. A closing sentence returns to the explanation. A linking phrase shows how the next fact connects. These last two definitions are taught here independently of planned-only lower-grade entries. Biography means a text about a real person's life; quotation means exact borrowed words. Day 14 supplies these meanings before day 16 application.

## 9. Supports and extension

Preview spark, inspiration, detail, organize and conclusion in taught examples. Judgment-practice frame: "This sentence belongs here because ___." Offer enlarged movable statements and content scribing while retaining independent choice. Extend by challenging a different detail's relevance in the same source.

Spoken directions, large print, tactile line separators, signing and a scribe provide access without supplying an answer. Record help. Independent decoding and spelling remain the child's. Caption live presentations and supply their printed directions. Public speaking is optional; offer a private live exchange. Never infer oral skill from paper.

Use complete on-file core readings as separately identified reading copies. Keep the task sheet, including its reserved exit passage, within DEFAULTS.md. A typical first-read novel part is about 1,050 words, nine minutes at 120 words per minute. Shared reading with tracking fits the named reading part; independent practice uses at most 120 words at once. Do not shorten the whole text to fit the task sheet. Explain this distinction on the teacher page. Preserve source punctuation; our directions and new directions use sentences of 15 words or fewer. No stripped punctuation.
