## 1. Standards and practices

Cold solution: three early boats and none later on one invented day support morning service for those carriers. One day cannot establish seasonal needs or everyone's needs. Meets: "Morning service matches these arrivals; another day could differ." Wrong: everyone always needs mornings (R); opinion without support (H); uncited preference (A).

Shape: D108a explains a pattern's support; avoid completed evidence-to-proposal explanations. Shape: D108b identifies heard reason/counts before print; avoid complete listening response models. **s03-X105.** "I support the proposal because cheaper carriage could help farmers. Another reason concerns reaching markets. Both paragraphs discuss transport gains." Ask: "State the opinion. Explain how to group and support these reasons." Key: favor proposal; combine transport gains, add traceable evidence and keep another aspect separate. Meets: "Support building; combine these transport reasons and locate evidence." Wrong: duplicated gain groups (J); no facts needed (A). Shape: opinion plus grouping/support improvement; avoid completed grouping repairs and duplicated transport card.

**s03-X106.** "We should delay. My reason is uncertain building expense. For instance, the memorial gives an estimate, not a final bill." Ask: "Identify the reason and evidence. Explain the link for instance makes." Key: uncertain expense tells why; memorial estimate illustrates uncertainty; link introduces example. Wrong: swap reason/example labels (AM); link means addition only (AB). Shape: reason/evidence identification plus link function; avoid full three-label sets and estimate/bill case. Practice one faulty label.

**s03-X107.** "For a reader new to canals: They should do it. Boats. Fields." Ask: "Write an introduction and two reason groups. Use the memorial estimate and play's field question." Open key: named canal decision/judgment, distinct transport/field groups, traceable details. Meets: "The 1817 canal proposal promised cheaper carriage but left field questions. I favor further study. Transport gains and field burdens need separate groups." Wrong: unexplained they/it (AN); unsupported reasons (A). Shape: compose introduction plus supported groups; avoid full matched models and judged practice introductions. Own writing stays unjudged.

**s03-X109.** "Build it. Boats save money. The question about fields remains." Feedback: "State a qualified opinion and connect both reasons." Ask: "Revise with grouped reasons, a link and related ending. Include a compound sentence with its comma." Open key: qualified judgment, distinct related evidence groups, meaningful link/close, comma between coordinated complete clauses. Meets: "I favor more study, for cheaper carriage leaves field questions unanswered. The memorial predicts savings. The play raises field concerns. Therefore, deciding requires both questions." Wrong: neat copy only (S); comma before every and (T). Shape: whole miniature revision with feedback/syntax; avoid completed miniature revisions. Models isolate one relationship or clause; own drafts stay unjudged.
