**s03-X105.** Source: g4-u3-03 main argument. New plan for explaining its position to an absent reader: "Reasons: delay; do not start; wait. Evidence: blank." Ask: "State the writer's opinion. Replace the repeated labels with distinct, supported reason groups." Meets: "Delay full commitment. Expense estimates may miss obstacles; borrowing needs payment before future income." Wrong: wait and delay as separate reasons (J); opinions without details (A).

**s03-X106.** Source: g4-u3-02 M2 and g4-u3-03 first reason. New editor's note: "The estimate proves the work can have no unexpected expense." Ask: "Correct the note by distinguishing a reason from its supporting detail." Meets: "Similar works give the writers a reason for confidence. The estimate illustrates that confidence; this route may differ." Wrong: labels estimate as the reason itself (AM); treats estimate as final bill (B).

Shape: repair an editor's reason/detail confusion. Avoid a completed paired reason-and-evidence correction and this proof-of-no-obstacles assertion. This is explanatory writing transfer, not a new historical reading.

**s03-X107.** Source: g4-u3-03 main argument. Draft for an unfamiliar reader: "This shows it. Therefore, wait." Ask: "Write an introduction naming the decision and the reader's question. Add two source-labeled reason notes." Open key: introduce the 1817 canal commitment and whether to wait; use two distinct supported reasons with traceable locations. Meets: "Should the state commit before construction questions are answered? I favor waiting. Cost: first reason, obstacles. Payment: next reason, interest before income." Wrong: unexplained it (AN); notes without support (A). Other grounded positions accepted.

Shape: compose a reader-oriented opening and collect two located reason notes. Avoid a completed introduction-plus-note pair. Practice corrects one source label; own full compositions remain unjudged.

**s03-X109.** Source: g4-u3-03 incomplete-crossing paragraph. Task draft: "Every finished section settles every route question. The writer says build now." Feedback: "Correct the position and separate a partial connection from the full route." Ask: "Revise this miniature opinion with supported groups, a link and a related ending. Include one compound sentence." Open key: the writer favors delay; a shorter working section supplies information without resolving all full-route questions. Distinct reasons remain source-supported, joined meaningfully, with a related close and correct compound comma. Meets: "A short section may teach planners, but it cannot answer every route question. In addition, obstacles need reports. Therefore, delay full commitment." Wrong: neat recopy (S); comma before every and (T); unsupported position (A).

Shape: whole miniature revision from feedback. Avoid complete miniature revisions, this full-route overclaim and modeled introduction-to-close responses; inspect only isolated components.
