## 1. Standards and practices


W.4.1a/b/c/d: "introduce," "state," "provide," "link": opinion, grouped reasons, facts, links and conclusion on 105/106/109. RI.4.8, W.4.9b, SL.4.3: "explain," "draw evidence," "identify": written/heard support, assessed on 108. W.4.4/5: "produce," "develop and strengthen": reader knowledge and feedback on 107/109. L.4.1e/2c: "form and use," "use": phrases and compound-sentence commas on 109. RF.4.3a/4a/4b/4c, L.4.2d, W.4.10: "read accurately," "read," "self-correct," "spell," "write routinely": words, rereading and dated writing.

Explain: related reasons form groups, without grade 5 logical ordering. Evidence supports a reason, not another repeated judgment. Commas precede coordinating conjunctions joining complete clauses. Predictions remain predictions. Content includes land loss, dangerous labor and illness; present-day analysts, no role simulation.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Administer:** D108a RI.4.8 (12 minutes), b SL.4.3 (10), c W.4.9b (13), unit field 5. Also teach toward D116c and D119f, collected in 3.8.

Cold solution: three early boats and none later on one invented day support morning service for those carriers. One day cannot establish seasonal needs or everyone's needs. Meets: "Morning service matches these arrivals; another day could differ." Wrong: everyone always needs mornings (R); opinion without support (H); uncited preference (A).

Shape: D108a explains a pattern's support; avoid completed evidence-to-proposal explanations. Shape: D108b identifies heard reason/counts before print; avoid complete listening response models. Shape: D108c explains support and limit in writing; avoid full support-and-limit paragraphs. Keep lock-hours/log case withheld. Shape: D116c/D119f connect a specific example/estimate to a point; avoid those cases and completed responses.

**s03-X105.** "I support the proposal because cheaper carriage could help farmers. Another reason concerns reaching markets. Both paragraphs discuss transport gains." Ask: "State the opinion. Explain how to group and support these reasons." Key: favor proposal; combine transport gains, add traceable evidence and keep another aspect separate. Meets: "Support building; combine these transport reasons and locate evidence." Wrong: duplicated gain groups (J); no facts needed (A). Shape: opinion plus grouping/support improvement; avoid completed grouping repairs and duplicated transport card.

**s03-X106.** "We should delay. My reason is uncertain building expense. For instance, the memorial gives an estimate, not a final bill." Ask: "Identify the reason and evidence. Explain the link for instance makes." Key: uncertain expense tells why; memorial estimate illustrates uncertainty; link introduces example. Wrong: swap reason/example labels (AM); link means addition only (AB). Shape: reason/evidence identification plus link function; avoid full three-label sets and estimate/bill case. Practice one faulty label.

**s03-X107.** "For a reader new to canals: They should do it. Boats. Fields." Ask: "Write an introduction and two reason groups. Use the memorial estimate and play's field question." Open key: named canal decision/judgment, distinct transport/field groups, traceable details. Meets: "The 1817 canal proposal promised cheaper carriage but left field questions. I favor further study. Transport gains and field burdens need separate groups." Wrong: unexplained they/it (AN); unsupported reasons (A). Shape: compose introduction plus supported groups; avoid full matched models and judged practice introductions. Own writing stays unjudged.

**s03-X109.** "Build it. Boats save money. The question about fields remains." Feedback: "State a qualified opinion and connect both reasons." Ask: "Revise with grouped reasons, a link and related ending. Include a compound sentence with its comma." Open key: qualified judgment, distinct related evidence groups, meaningful link/close, comma between coordinated complete clauses. Meets: "I favor more study, for cheaper carriage leaves field questions unanswered. The memorial predicts savings. The play raises field concerns. Therefore, deciding requires both questions." Wrong: neat copy only (S); comma before every and (T). Shape: whole miniature revision with feedback/syntax; avoid completed miniature revisions. Models isolate one relationship or clause; own drafts stay unjudged.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


Children keep source notes/unjudged opinion folders; teacher retains written feedback and individual records. Ideal: blank reason groups beside separately identified texts; paper fits, no key tool/spec. Without print, copy authorized excerpts, never a whole file with cold packets.

Review grade 3 u03-5 day 98 "reason: tells why, a word in context" and u03-7 day 105 "evidence: the line or page that shows an idea is true (word in context)." Review u04-8 day 151 "group: put the facts about one thing together." Retrieve opinion functions without assuming planned wording.

Unit 1 paragraph-frame/essay-structure/sentence-combining and unit 2 citation are planned only. Independently explain topic, judgment, grouping, source labels and complete clauses through separate fresh defects. One marking scale on all records: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Separate criterion columns, no points or totals. This replaces the unit's numerical scale. Ordinary talk runs in concurrent pairs; hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each and collect a written note from everyone else.

Review grade 3 u01-1 day 2: "partner rereading with a feedback card: phrases and expression, no speed." In each 15-minute block, hear five due children for two minutes each; five minutes remain for directions/feedback. Other pairs return purpose, phrase and meaning notes. Reuse actual matching individual evidence within ten days. Successive poetry observations must exist separately; missing evidence stays missing. Never infer a teacher observation from a partner report.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: opinion, support, oppose, group, detail, instance, purpose, conjunction. Frame: "This detail supports ___ because ___." Accessible teaching text/bilingual glossary; no filled response frame at checks. Scribe content without interpretation. Hear listening twice before print. Ordinary source blocks read at most 300 authorized words; P108 takes under one minute per hearing. Extend by detecting one reason's limit, not producing the check paragraph.
