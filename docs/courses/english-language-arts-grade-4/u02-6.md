**s02-X69, The Wrong Classroom.** Text: "He stopped there. It was wrong." Notes: "Narrator: outside the story. Character: Dev, carrying a puppet. Setting: an empty art room before the show. Problem: Dev expected an audience." Ask: "Expand the opening so its situation, narrator and setting are clear." Key/sample: "Before the show, Dev carried his puppet into the empty art room. He had expected an audience." Require third-person view, participants, time/place and problem. Wrong: unchanged unexplained pronouns (Z); nice room with no specifics (AB).
Shape: expand a bare opening from notes. Avoid puppet/empty-room cases and full expanded opening models; own openings remain unjudged.

**s02-X71, The Loose Button.** Text: "Mina notices a loose button. She wants Omar to wait." Unpunctuated line: "Wait said Mina the button is loose". Ask: "Punctuate Mina's speech and explain its purpose here." Key/sample: '"Wait," said Mina. "The button is loose." Mina wants Omar to stop because the button needs attention.' Require quotation boundaries, separating comma and a purpose connected to waiting/button. Wrong: unmarked speech (AO); merely states that Mina speaks, with no purpose (AG).
Shape: punctuate a short fresh exchange and explain purpose. Avoid button/wait cases and complete edited exchanges; inspect a missing boundary, not a full punctuation production task.

**s02-X72, The Crooked Painting.** Scene: "A painting tilts above the door. Sol sees the cord slipping and reaches toward it." Weak dialogue: 'Sol said, "There is a thing."' Ask: "Revise Sol's dialogue to reveal a feeling and response to this event." Key/sample: 'Sol said, "Careful! That cord is slipping. Hold the frame with me."' Require concern/urgency and event-linked response; other fitting lines work. Wrong: lunch request (AG); unmarked speech (AO).
Shape: replace vague dialogue with a response/feeling. Avoid painting/cord cases and full revised dialogue exemplars; own dialogue remains unjudged.

**s02-X73, The Cold Cuff.** Scene: "A cold drop runs from a large woolen sleeve onto Ren's wrist." Weak sentence: "The woolen large sleeve felt nice." Ask: "Revise the sentence with a precise sensory detail and conventional adjective order." Key/sample: "A cold drop slipped from the large woolen sleeve onto Ren's wrist." Require size before material and concrete touch/action, without unsupported events. Other sensory details supported by the scene work. Wrong: woolen large (AF); nice retained without specifics (AB).
Shape: sensory sentence revision with adjective order. Avoid sleeve/drop cases and full edited-sentence models. Practice identifies one unsuitable adjective; it never rearranges pairs. Reserve large/rough/shell for D75h.
