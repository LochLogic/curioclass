# Course frame: English Language Arts, Grade 4

Course id ela-k12 (grade 4 row), grade 4, draft frame, written 2026-11-14; the scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 4, one for each module of the base text's grade 4 year, in the base text's order; unit 3 keeps the
  module's design (a history set, a short play, a piece urging a side, a two-sided discussion) on a new event.
- **Days.** 160 teaching days: 153 in units and 7 at course level (4 for two course-level checks, 3 respond days).
  One day is one literacy block of about 90 minutes, five days a week: the unit lesson (about 60 minutes) and the
  skills block (about 30 minutes: the long-word and spelling sequence every day, then partner rereading for
  fluency or independent reading conferences on alternate days). The base text plans one 60-minute module lesson a
  day (153 lessons in grade 4) plus a second 60-minute block; Curio Class keeps the module lesson whole, so 153
  lessons become 153 unit days (unit 1 adds 1 for the start-of-year screens, unit 3 swaps 3 lessons for 3 myth
  days, unit 4 merges 2 lessons into 1), and folds the second block's word study, fluency and independent reading
  into 30 minutes (For the lead 1).
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for ELA, grade 4 (data/standards/ccss-ela-standards-full.txt), all
  30 progression rows of grade 4 (data/progressions/ela.csv), every lettered part taught in the unit named below.
  The bar is docs/RUBRIC-ELA.md (grades 3 to 5, all three gateways, Gateway 1 in full: Cory's yes, 2026-11-12) and
  the approach is docs/APPROACH-ELA.md. The values rule (DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-12) governs every unit.
- **Base text.** The 2017 K to 5 language arts curriculum recorded in docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md (CC BY 4.0; copy
  in S:\CurioClass-sources\el-education-k5-2017), grade 4, modules 1 to 4. Its lesson design, sequence, protocols
  and assessments are adapted; its trade books are replaced by Curio Class texts (BASELINE rule 29: newer texts,
  mostly original Curio Class texts written to the grade 4 text spec, each read by a qualified ELA teacher before
  use); its own CC BY texts may be adapted; its third-party excerpts are replaced; public-domain texts may stay.
  Its name is never used on a Curio Class page; one credit line per adapted day. The 2025 edition and the 2024
  skills block are never used. The source map closes this frame.
- **Which row sits where.** A row sits in the unit that first teaches its grade 4 step. Before then a unit uses the
  grade 3 form of the skill, and the first unit that leans on a grade 3 skill opens with a readiness check on it.
  Later units practise every placed row again on new texts; the table names those returns as review.
- **Reading level.** Anchors sit in the grades 4 to 5 band (measure about 740 to 1010), most in its lower half
  with scaffolding at the high end (e4-reading-range); a few poems and the play sit below the band by measure but
  above it in meaning, the reason given in each text record. About half the anchors are literary and half
  informational. Every anchor gets a text record (measure, what makes it hard, the task, why here) in
  data/plans/english-language-arts-grade-4/texts.csv when its unit is planned.
- **Long texts.** Two read whole: an original verse novel (unit 1) and an original chapter book (unit 4).
- **Words, read and never guessed.** The long-word sequence (e4-long-words) runs every day from day 1 with a check
  every few weeks; spelling follows it.
- **Writing.** Every reading day ends in a written exit answer that needs the day's text (W.4.9, W.4.10). Each unit
  takes at least one piece through the whole process. The year keeps the grades 3 to 5 shares: about 30 percent
  opinion, 35 informative, 35 narrative. Research runs in unit 2 and again in unit 4.
- **Talk.** Partner talk daily; a formal text-based discussion about every two weeks, with roles from unit 1.
- **Paper path and devices.** Every anchor has a print copy and every task a paper route. Keyboarding, publishing,
  the poem recordings and the announcement happen in the school day on a shared classroom device rotation or a
  lab session, with no student account; home practice is optional. If no device route exists by year end, W.4.6
  is recorded unfinished for that class (e4-writing-process).
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent sensitivity read. Unit 3 (Haudenosaunee lands taken for
  the canal, Irish and other laborers' hard and dangerous work) and unit 4 (racism inside the suffrage movement,
  the exclusion of Black women) also need a qualified person's read, unit 3 a Haudenosaunee community reader, when
  one is found (FOUNDATION section 7).
