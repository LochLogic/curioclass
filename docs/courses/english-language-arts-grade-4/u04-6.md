Explain register through relationships/purpose without ranking language varieties. Participant explanation does not establish every motive. Reasons need concrete support; decoration is not evidence. Message choices never require advocacy.

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: preview source, evidence, audience and register; frame "This detail supports ___ because ___." Fade before independent evidence. Large print, visible presentation transcripts and a scribe for composition preserve the child's own wording. Do not read assessed decoding passages aloud; record accommodations by construct. Keep task sheets within 350 words, 14 point, every printed direction sentence at most 15 words. Core reading uses separate leaves. Preserve historical punctuation; read intact clauses during instruction. Extension qualifies a claim with another detail from the same source, on the same standard.

Teaching uses at most 300 source words plus 120 model-fragment words, under four reading minutes before tasks. G1 is about 75 words within 20 minutes. Historical quotations stay intact on source leaves. Content includes historical exclusion and omitted racist language in unselected source, with no slurs in selected clauses; voluntary civic messages. Quiet accessible speech/recording is available.
