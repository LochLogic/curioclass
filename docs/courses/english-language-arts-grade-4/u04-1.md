**A1. Shape:** action plus judgment inference. Avoid paired action/inference answers and initial-to-owner cases in practice. Key: moves a claim to Questions; values checking more than her first hope. R. proves Ruth is A.
**A2. Shape:** character through two details and setting. Avoid complete portraits and attic-discovery models. Key: excited yet willing to investigate; adds question mark/column when the initial proves too little. Family papers prompt the inquiry. She is nice is A.
**A3. Shape:** ordinary meaning, idiom and mythic allusion bundled in a new scene. Avoid that bundle and its target words in practice. Key: eager means keen; put words in her mouth means attribute unexpressed views; Herculean recalls demanding labors. The hero wrote the letter is F. **A4. Shape:** two located text/oral correspondences and one effect. Avoid completed two-match comparisons and checkpoint delivery in practice. Key: lifted Ruth? follows question mark; stress on only an initial limits certainty. Same topic alone is D.
**A5. Shape:** one antonym plus nuance between two near-synonyms. Avoid three-word relationship sets and eager/keen/impatient/reluctant in practice. Key: reluctant opposes eager; keen is similar; impatient adds difficulty waiting, not necessarily anger. The unit's irritation claim is not required. Sameness is P.
