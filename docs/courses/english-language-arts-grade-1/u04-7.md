# Section 4.7: A model paragraph, then the window portrait drafted, revised and published


## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 4, Caring for birds. *How can people help birds that need it?* (days 120 to 157): see [s04.md](s04.md). **Days 148 to 152.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (148), application 4 (149, 150, 151, 152). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 7 of 8 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every exit card is a new Curio Class microtext, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; texts are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures and labels. From 150 the foundations rotation carries a hearing of five children a day for the unit's reading record; the portrait is drafted, revised and published on paper and on one shared device, and a grammar item or two runs inside the writing each day.
