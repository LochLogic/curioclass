# Section 4.7: A model paragraph, then the window portrait drafted, revised and published


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every exit card is a new Curio Class microtext, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; texts are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures and labels. From 150 the foundations rotation carries a hearing of five children a day for the unit's reading record; the portrait is drafted, revised and published on paper and on one shared device, and a grammar item or two runs inside the writing each day.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** W.1.2 and RI.1.2 are taught on 148 on an original model and applied on 149 and 150. W.1.5 (revision after a written question) runs on 151 and W.1.6 (digital publication) on 152 to 154. L.1.1b to j are observed inside the drafting on 150 to 152; all were taught earlier. RF.1.3c to e are heard five children a day on 150 to 155; cycle 24 (148, 149) and cycle 25 (150 to 154) run beneath, with the cycle 24 sample on 149.
- **Limits that bite.** The 148 model is not the window-marks paragraph and is not the 150 key. The 150 draft omits where the marks go on purpose, since that is the detail the written question asks for on 151. The feeder article and bluebird account are not compared here. No child uses real glass, a feeder or a nest. The shared device is used for publication only, by ten children a day on 152 to 154, with no accounts and no private data.
- **Connections.** Reviewed: the four-part informative paragraph (unit 1), topic and detail (kindergarten), the research organizer (4.5), written peer question strips (units 3 and 4), the edit checklist (unit 1), the shared device turns (unit 2), nouns, verbs, pronouns, tense, adjectives, conjunctions, determiners, prepositions and the eight sentence forms (4.1 to 4.3).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) How to write the peer question and keep it a question (N). (2) The hearing sheet and how a child hears no help. (3) How to run ten 90-second turns on one device and log each. (4) Records use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; the unit plan's 0, 1 or 2 per part is read as 2 shown, 1 shown with help, 0 not yet seen.

## Day 148

- **15 min. Read.** Read the original model paragraph *Lights Out* (ours, in the day card, invented for practice; it is not the window-marks paragraph):
  "Lights Out. Bright lights at night can pull birds toward windows. People can turn off lights they do not need. Then birds are not pulled toward the glass. Please turn the lights out for birds."
