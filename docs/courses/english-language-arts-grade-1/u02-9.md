## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **W.1.3, this section's share (days 68 to 71).** "Write narratives in which they recount two or more appropriately sequenced events, include some details regarding what happened, use temporal words to signal event order, and provide some sense of closure." Taught 68 and 69 (the plan and the words); observed on 70 and 71 as one continuous independent sitting; scored once on 71.
- **W.1.7 and W.1.8, day 68.** "Participate in shared research and writing projects" and "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": a source-labeled sun fact beside the plan, with the invented part marked.
- **RL.1.4, RL.1.10, RI.1.10 (days 68, 69).** Feeling and sense words in a poem; the sun poem and the sun article are heard and reread. Child-read passages from 64 to 66 are scored on 68 (supported reading, 0 new minutes).

- **Limits that bite here.** The sun in the poem is imagined; the sky facts are in g1-u2-01 (Sun Across the Sky). Night is gentle. Models are withdrawn on 70 and 71: no wall chart, no shared poem, no feedback and no second narrative task. Reading and writing stay spoken, pointed, drawn and dictated, with a word label at most (a plan point, field 11). A child may use an approved scribe.
- **Connections.** Narrative-arc chart (to 69; withdrawn 70 and 71), writing-process chart, feeling and sense words, because and so, and the source label; all review from 2.1 to 2.8.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) The sun poem has four marks: two ordered events, details, time words, an ending. (2) The fact pointer and the invented-detail label are separate. (3) What counts as meeting and as a list of facts (F). (4) Day 70 and 71 are one sitting with one score; nothing is given back until 72. (5) How the cycle progress sample and the three local samples on 68 are given and recorded.

## 6. Days

## Day 68


- **5 min. Opening repair.** From the day 67 marks, only if many missed the same lapse (A or F).
- **10 min. Reread the sun article.** Reread g1-u2-01, Sun Across the Sky, pages 3 and 4, with the pictures. Partners point to the sun fact that must stay true.
- **10 min. Reread the sun poem.** Reread g1-u2-02, Sun's Day, chorally; the class points to a phrase that is voice, not information.
- **10 min. Talk.** Turn card: "Which two events will the imagined sun see? Which fact must stay true?" Partners point. Pip asks one clarifying question.
- **20 min. Plan.** The prompt is given orally: "Tell what the imagined sun sees. Use two events in order, details, time words and an ending. Mark a fact from our information and an invented detail." Each child fills the planning page: two event pictures, an ending, and a fact label and an invented label, with word labels only. The teacher hears the heard six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E68a and E68b.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: the long vowel in a pair (cub, cube); blend /f/ /l/ /i/ /ng/; first, vowel and last sound of tent; segment sink. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 11 lesson 60 (sample day): the cycle progress sample is taken in the rotation: six groups of five read a different assigned pair from kicked, filled, rested, looks, boxes, looking, faster and fastest (6 minutes); the whole class writes out, sand, rested and the (5 minutes); the teacher dictates dusk without showing it (3 minutes). Reserved words are never used in teaching or retries before today. The rest is familiar practice (46 foundations minutes in all).
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Print the letter pairs in dictation of the sun-plan labels from today's page; names with capitals.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (the 6-minute endings sample and the cohort reading records; familiar reading and spelling for the rest); the other children reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the charts; children hold the planning page. Tool: None. By hand: the planning page. Evidence: E68a, E68b; the day 68 foundations samples; the scored cohort reading.

## Day 69


- **5 min. Opening repair.** From E68, E, only if many missed it.
- **15 min. Rehearse the plan.** Each child tells a partner the plan once, pointing at the pictures, then once more with the time words; the teacher hears the heard six.
- **10 min. Time, feeling and sense words.** Reread Sun's Day, g1-u2-02, pages 1 to 3; partners point to a time word, a feeling word and a sense word and add them to the planning page as labels.
- **15 min. Talk.** Turn card: "Which two events? Which ending? Which fact stays true?" Linked exchanges in threes (maker, listener, asker).
- **10 min. Name the ending.** Each child says the ending line aloud to a partner and records it as a picture on the planning page.
- **5 min. Exit.** E69a, E69b and E69c.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: the long vowel in a pair (tub, tube); blend /s/ /t/ /a/ /ng/; first, vowel and last sound of bank; segment grunt. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Consolidation, no new pattern: small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread words from cycles 9 to 11 (-ll, s-blends, l- and r-blends, final clusters, ou, -ed /id/). Chain: spill, stamp, crab, lift, hunt, found, planted. The matched decodable is read for meaning and expression.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dictation: "The sun is out." (is, the, mapped). Names with capitals.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the charts; children hold the plan. Tool: None. By hand: the rehearsal and the labels. Evidence: E69a, E69b, E69c.

## Day 70


- **10 min. Source rereading.** Independent: each child looks at their own planning page and the sun article pictures. No chart and no model is displayed.
- **10 min. Planning.** Each child checks the page against the prompt: two events in order, details, time words, an ending, and the two labels. No help beyond the read-aloud prompt.
- **30 min. Drafting.** Each child draws and labels the events in order and tells the poem to an approved scribe, who writes it as spoken; one word label is the only writing the child does alone. The draft is kept.
- **10 min. Drawing.** Each child draws the scene to match the events.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: the long vowel in a pair (cut, cute); blend /s/ /l/ /e/ /d/; first, vowel and last sound of nest; segment brick. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Consolidation, no new pattern: small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread cycle 9 to 11 words with a matched decodable. No sample is taken.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dictation: "The tent is soft." Names with capitals.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds their own draft; nothing is displayed. Tool: None. By hand: the draft and the drawing. Evidence: the day 70 draft, kept for 71; not scored today, no feedback.

## Day 71


- **35 min. Finish the poem.** The same sitting continues: each child finishes the draft, with time words and an ending, and the drawing.
- **15 min. Reread against the five rows.** Each child (with a scribe if needed) rereads the poem and points to the five parts on the grid card: two events, order, details, time words, ending.
- **5 min. Mark the poem.** The child adds the fact pointer (a label for the sun fact) and the invented-detail label.
- **5 min. Submit.** The child hands in the poem and the drawing.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: the long vowel in a pair (rid, ride); blend /g/ /r/ /a/ /s/ /p/; first, vowel and last sound of loud; segment slip. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Consolidation, no new pattern: small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread cycle 9 to 11 words; read a short grade 1 topic passage with support.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dictation: "We found a loud bell." Names with capitals.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds their own poem; no models. Tool: None. By hand: the poem and its two labels. Evidence: day 71 row W.1.3, scored once (five rows, two labels).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: imagine, event, ending, fact, invented. Frames: "First ___. Then ___. At last ___." "A fact: ___. An invention: ___."
- **Access.** Enlarged cards, tactile picture choices, a scribe, home-language rehearsal; record the support.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a spoken answer or a scribe; stems and the prompt read twice; no time pressure; do not read a decoding word for the child.
- **Reading load.** The sun article and the poem are read aloud; children read word labels and the cycle passage.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a third event and a sense word without changing the topic.
