### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Preview tool names with pictures and gestures; allow home-language rehearsal before "It helps because..." Read rich texts aloud, then support children's reading of short parts. Enlarge print, offer tactile outlines and accept pointing or dictation for content. Preserve independent sound and spelling evidence. Extend by finding a second defensible detail in the same text.
