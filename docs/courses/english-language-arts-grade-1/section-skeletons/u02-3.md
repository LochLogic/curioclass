### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Preview sky words with pictures and home-language talk. Read rich texts aloud, then support child reading of short parts. Enlarge print and offer tactile outlines or dictation for content. Extend with a second defensible message or source connection.
<!-- /skel -->

### U10. Two rooms

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) One screen shows the page while every child marks paper. At home the parent reads, discusses and records the same criteria, then helps the child make a private typed page. Sharing on 75 stays with the class or family.
<!-- /skel -->

### U11. Limits and preparation

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Field 10 lists unfinished texts and required reads. Paper observation records replace home sky-watching. No direct sun observation is assigned.
