# Section 2.10: Revise, publish, share and respond

## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: peer, question, publish, scene, feeling. Frames: "My question is ___." "I add ___." "In the picture ___."
- **Access.** Enlarged print, tactile choices, a scribe, home-language rehearsal; the device with a partner who types; record the support.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a spoken answer or a scribe; stems read twice; no time pressure; no reteaching during an assessment.
- **Reading load.** All stems, cards and the book pages are read aloud; children read word labels and the cycle passages.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second detail to the revised poem and read it clearly with a drawing.
