# Section 3.2: Connecting a part to its job, and finding facts by a menu

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-1/3/s/3.2), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-1 u03-2.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits and end items read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); every other card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** Connect a body part to what it helps a bird do (RI.1.3, "Describe the connection between two individuals, events, ideas, or pieces of information in a text"), use a printed menu and its icons to find a fact (RI.1.5, the electronic menu and icons part, taught here on a printed screen), and check the topic, key details and the whole feature route on day 86 (RI.1.2, RI.1.5). Foundations run cycle 13 (compound words and closed two-syllable words; RF.1.2, RF.1.3d, e, g, RF.1.4) after the cycle 12 sample of 83.
- **Limits that bite.** Day 86 is a check: no teaching, no repair and no help; the day 85 exit miss waits for 87. The screen is a printed picture of a menu; a live screen, if there is one, is a teacher demonstration only, and no child operates it. Facts come only from the set and the cards.
- **Connections.** The connection arrow (a fact, an arrow and the joining word) and because and so (unit 2), the main-idea-details picture, heading, contents entry, label and glossary entry (3.1), the source note with its source named (unit 2).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A connection is two facts joined by a word, with the source sentence pointed to first (the repair for B). (2) A printed menu works like a contents page: choose, then follow the icon to the page. (3) Why the check is given cold: a new card, never a taught one. (4) How six independent paper traces are scored.

## 6. Days

## Day 84


- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E83, one component, a fresh card.
- **12 min. Words, then read Feet That Help.** Words with pictures: perch, webbed, claw, grasp. The teacher reads the whole article aloud (about 170 words), pausing at each heading for the picture.
- **13 min. Connecting two facts, taught.** The connection arrow on the sparrow paragraph: toes grip a branch, so it can perch. Contrast: wrong "a duck's beak helps it swim" against right (point to the sentence first). The words because and so are reviewed from unit 2.
- **10 min. Three questions.** Spoken: "What can the sparrow do with its toes?" "Which two facts are joined?" "How does gripping help it perch?" Pairs take two turns.
- **15 min. Draw the connection.** A three-box page: part picture, arrow, job picture, one joining word copied from the board. Eleven minutes drawing, four minutes hearing six children.
- **5 min. Exit.** E84.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: a long and a short vowel in a pair (rob, robe); blend /t/ /r/ /u/ /k/; first, vowel and last sound of plum; segment crisp. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 13 lesson 66, explicit teaching first: a compound word is two words joined; clap it, then chop between the words (back-pack, cat-fish, pig-pen, hot-dog). Contrast with rabbit, which does not split into two words. The cycle 12 reader is reread until the cycle's correspondences and irregular words are mapped.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dictation of two compound words and one sentence ("The cat is in the backpack." the, is, in mapped). Print the pairs.
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reader and spelling; the cycle 12 reader), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the arrow chart; each child holds a three-box page. Tool: None.. By hand: the three-box page. Evidence: E84.

## Day 85


- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E84, one component, a fresh part and job.
- **5 min. Words.** menu, icon, screen, with pictures. The printed screen is shown: four choices, each with a small bird icon.
- **15 min. A printed menu, taught.** The teacher models: choose the menu word, follow its icon to the page, find the fact. One optional shared screen shows the same route once, if the room has one. Contrast: the cover picture tells the book, not the page.
- **15 min. Pairs trace.** Pairs trace a route on a printed screen to the foot fact in Feet That Help, with a finger, then reread that exact sentence aloud.
- **10 min. Three questions.** Spoken: "Which choice leads to the foot fact?" "What does the icon do?" "Which sentence tells you?"
- **5 min. Talk.** Partners say the route aloud: "First I ___. Then I ___." Six pairs heard.
- **5 min. Exit.** E85.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: a long and a short vowel in a pair (cap, cape); blend /s/ /t/ /e/ /p/; first, vowel and last sound of blot; segment frost. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 13 lesson 67: connect the spellings to words and the matched reader, "Pam's Backpack" (adapted, written to the cycle's patterns only; its irregular words mapped first). Chain: backpack, catfish, sunset, cannot.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Decode, then spell, two compound words; dictation: "Pat has a backpack." Print the pairs.
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reader and spelling; the adapted reader Pam's Backpack), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the screen print; each pair holds a route. Tool: One optional shared screen, demonstration only, 3 minutes (light). By hand: the printed route. Evidence: E85.

## Day 86


- **5 min. Setup and read Duck at the Pond, page 2.** The teacher reads it aloud once. No teaching.
- **15 min. RI.1.2.** Each child marks the topic picture on paper, then retells the two details to the teacher or a scribe, about 30 seconds a child; the others draw any bird quietly. Nothing from the card is drawn.
- **20 min. RI.1.5, six paper traces.** Each child works alone: the contents entry, the heading, the label, the glossary line, the menu choice and the icon, then the two facts and the meaning of fringe. The teacher does not help.
- **20 min. Route talk.** Six groups of five, 3 minutes each, explain their routes; the others check their own traces quietly. Two minutes to move and collect.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: familiar words only (no new teaching): a long and a short vowel in a pair; a blend; first, vowel and last sound; segment. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Familiar cycle 12 and 13 patterns and matched rereading; no gap repair on an assessment day.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dictation of two taught words; print the pairs; no repair.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (familiar reading and spelling; the catch-up group is paused on an assessment day and resumes on 87), heard one child at a time, 60 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread and spell in pairs.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: children hold their traces; the teacher holds Duck at the Pond and the records. Tool: None.. By hand: the six paper traces and the topic marks. Evidence: day 86 items RI.1.2 and RI.1.5 (field 3).

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: menu, icon, screen, connect, because, so. Frames: "The ___ helps the bird ___." "I choose ___ because ___."
- **Access.** Enlarged menu print, tactile icons, a partner who points, content dictation.
- **Check accommodations (86).** Stems read twice; answers pointed or spoken; a scribe writes the labels; no time pressure; no help on the check.
- **Reading load.** Cards and articles are read aloud; children read word labels and the cycle sentences.
- **Extension, same standard.** Join a second pair of facts from Feet That Help with so.
