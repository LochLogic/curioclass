# Section 4.3: Nests, bluebird boxes and the two views of a city nest


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); cards for the other exits are new Curio Class microtexts, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; the stories are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures. The first information pages of the unit begin here; they are read aloud, and children point to the picture or the label.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** RI.1.9 ("Identify basic similarities in and differences between two texts on the same topic"): taught 131 on *Nests Need Space* and *Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes*, practiced 135 on the same pair. RI.1.8 ("Identify the reasons an author gives to support points in a text"): taught 132 and 133 on *A City Nest: Two Views*, practiced 134. RI.1.4, L.1.4a, L.1.4b and L.1.4c: sentence clues for cavity and nest (131, 132, 135); un- and re- and root families on 131 (L.1.4b, c). W.1.1 is only touched on 134 (an opinion and a reason said aloud and drawn); it is taught on 136. L.1.1g conjunctions taught 131 ("Use frequently occurring conjunctions"), determiners 132, prepositions and the eight sentence forms 133, all practiced 134. RL.1.9 is retrieved on 131 and 135 by the Both ___. But ___. frame, now on information. RL.1.10 and RI.1.10: three information pieces read whole. RF.1.2, RF.1.3, RF.1.3c to g, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, L.1.2e and RF.1.4: cycle 21 (oa, ai, ea; lessons 107 to 110) and the first day of cycle 22 (ay and long-o ow; lesson 111).
- **Limits that bite.** The nest page and the bluebird account are the pair for 131 and 135 only; the feeder and box pairing is kept for 154 and its procedural contrast is not shown. The word bill is reserved for the 135 exit. Claim-evidence-reasoning is new on 132 and is taught orally, with no written frame. Day 134's cycle sample takes the rotation. No child removes, touches or moves a nest; the debate is a class discussion on paper.
- **Connections.** Reviewed: the story-map and Both ___. But ___. (4.1, 4.2), the sentence clue (121), the main-idea-details picture (unit 1), source labels, the discussion-protocol with linked exchanges, groups of three talk (maker, listener, asker), Return and repair, the cycle progress sample.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Why the two sides are both given respect and no stance is required. (2) How a reason differs from a fact that merely sits beside the point (C). (3) How to say and model a because clause, and why but and because are not swapped (M). (4) That the city nest text was written for this unit; the real event is historical and no child is asked to judge a real person. (5) Content note: nothing graphic; no child approaches a wild nest.

## 6. Days

## Day 131


- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E130, one component (A), on a fresh card.
- **15 min. Read both pages.** Read *Nests Need Space* (five pages, about 330 words) and *Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes* (five pages, about 280 words), pointing to each labeled drawing. Words: nest, box, support, gather, provide, habitat, cavity. Ask: "What does cavity mean? Which words tell you?" (a hollow space, like a hole in a tree).
- **15 min. Talk.** Partners two turns, six heard: "What does each page describe?" "Which details are general facts and which describe an event?" (the nest page tells how nests are kept safe; the box account tells what volunteers did, in order) "What is alike and what is different?" Both ___. But ___.
- **7 min. Conjunctions.** Teach and, but, or, so and because (L.1.1g) on the page 3 sentence: Nests need space, because a nest is not safe if people come too close. Children say the cause link aloud, then finish four spoken sentences with a conjunction card.
- **6 min. Word parts.** Un- means not and re- means again; root and ending: protect, protected; place, replace (page 5 of the account); safe, unsafe. Children match the part cards to the root.
- **7 min. Pair of pages.** Each child draws one picture for what the pages share and one for what each adds, with one word label.
- **5 min. Exit.** E131.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** A long and a short vowel pair (rod, rode); blend /s/ /ai/ /l/; first, middle and last sound of seat; segment coat.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 21 lesson 107: connect the spellings to words and the matched reader; map one irregular word (named at build). Chain: eat, seat, beak, leaf, bean, read. Vowel-team syllables introduced: sailboat, raincoat, beanbag; say, clap and write the two parts.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Print two sentences from the day's pages as a copy, one letter form checked. Dictation: "A goat is on the road."
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (the adapted reader Sam's Throat Hurts, first use, conditional on the map), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the pair picture; the teacher holds both pages and the part cards. Tool: None. By hand: the picture. Evidence: E131.

## 7. Talk and writing

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: nest, box, reason, point, because, side. Frames: "The author says ___ because ___." "Both pages ___. But page A ___." Home-language talk and pictures preview each word.
- **Access.** Enlarged cards; tactile strips; content dictation; a picture route; partner pointing; a quiet exit from the discussion.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing or a spoken answer; stems read twice; a scribe for labels; a pair instead of the group of three.
- **Reading load.** Pages and the debate are read aloud; children read word labels and the cycle sentences.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second reason for one view and say whether it strengthens the point.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read a page aloud, ask for the point and the reason, and sort two strips. One parent can take each view in turn. The cycle work is a word chain and a dictated sentence; 35 minutes by the unit plan's split.
- **One projector.** Show the page and the strips one at a time; the discussion is in pairs at desks.
