## 1. Standards and practices


- **W.1.3, this section's share (days 63 to 67).** "Write narratives in which they recount two or more appropriately sequenced events, include some details regarding what happened, use temporal words to signal event order, and provide some sense of closure." Taught 63 to 65 on the poems; practiced 66 and 67 on a joint moon poem; the child's own sun poem begins on 68 (section 2.9).
- **RL.1.4 and RL.1.10, day 63 (also 64 and 65).** Feeling and sense words in a poem; the poems are heard, then reread chorally. Children do not decode the poems alone.
- **SL.1.4, SL.1.5, SL.1.6, day 64.** Describe what the imagined moon sees with details, add a drawing, say a complete sentence. Deepened from 59 to 62; scored again on 74 (owned here).
- **L.1.1j, day 65.** "Produce and expand complete simple and compound declarative, interrogative, imperative, and exclamatory sentences in response to prompts": expand a short sentence with one detail. Practiced on 66 with the eight-box sentence sheet; scored on 72 (owned here).
- **W.1.5 and W.1.6, days 65, 67.** Kind, specific, helpful feedback and revising by adding a detail (modeled on 65, practiced on 67); the shared device with a text editor and a drawing tool (taught on 67, observed on 73).
- **Foundations, days 63 to 67:** cycle 10 lesson 55 (cycle progress sample on 63) and cycle 11 lessons 56 to 59 (final -lt, -ft, -st, -nt, -nd, -ng, -nk; -ed said /id/; ou as in out, cloud, found): RF.1.2, RF.1.3a, b, f, g, RF.1.4a to c, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, e.
- **Limits that bite here.** The moon and the sun in the poems are imagined; the sky facts are in g1-u2-01. Night is gentle; class picture records replace home watching. The sun poem is not drafted here. The shared device is practiced in turns of five children for five minutes, six groups, and never alone with paper. Reading and writing stay spoken, pointed, sorted, drawn, with a word label at most.
- **Connections.** Narrative-arc chart begins 63 and stays through 71; the writing-process chart begins 65; sentence combining returns on 65; word parts and feeling words are review from 2.2; the story map from 2.2 returns on 63 with the poems.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Ordered events, details, time words and an ending are the four marks of a narrative poem. (2) A list of facts ("yellow, warm") is not a narrative. (3) What is imagined and what is a sky pattern stays marked. (4) Kind, specific, helpful feedback adds a requested detail and never changes the topic. (5) How final blends, -ed said /id/ and ou are mapped on 56 to 59.

## 6. Days

## Day 63


- **5 min. Opening repair.** From the day 62 marks, only if many missed the same lapse.
- **15 min. Read Sun's Day.** Words before, with pictures: wake, climb, slide, sink, hum. Read g1-u2-02, Sun's Day, whole, pausing after pages 2 and 3 for the pictures. The speaker is imagined; the sky facts under it are in g1-u2-01.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** "Which events happen? Which words tell the order?" Partners point to First, Next, Then, At last on the page.
- **10 min. Talk.** Turn card: "What does the sun see, hear or feel?" Partners point to a picture of a sense or feeling. Pip says one line.
- **15 min. Order the events.** Fifteen pairs, each with four event pictures and four time-word cards, set the order on the arc chart; the teacher circulates and marks shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The ending slot is filled with "Good night" and the feeling.
- **5 min. Exit.** E63.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: the long vowel in a pair (slim, slime); blend /s/ /l/ /i/ /d/; first, vowel and last sound of sled; segment slip. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 10 lesson 55 (explicit teaching first): review of l- and r-blends, three-consonant clusters, -es, -er and -est. Word chain: slim, slimmer, wish, wishes, tall, taller, tallest. The cycle progress sample is taken in the rotation (field 3).
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dictation: "The frog sits on a slim log." Names with capitals.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (the progress sample: read one reserved word, remove the card, write one dictated word; heard six by name); the other children reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the picture and word cards; the teacher holds the arc chart. Tool: None. By hand: sorting four pictures. Evidence: E63; the day 63 progress sample.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: first, next, then, at last, moon, sees, imagined. Frames: "First ___. Next ___." "The moon sees ___." "I can add ___."
- **Access.** Enlarged cards, tactile picture choices, a scribe, home-language rehearsal; a paper copy of the shared poem; record any support.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a spoken answer or a scribe; stems read twice; no time pressure; a typing partner for a child who cannot use the keyboard.
- **Reading load.** The poems are read aloud and reread chorally; children read word labels and the cycle passage.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a third event with a time word and an ending in one more sentence.
