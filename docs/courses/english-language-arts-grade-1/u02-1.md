# Section 2.1: The sky question and the tale of the sun and the water


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RI.1.3, "Describe the connection between two individuals, events, ideas, or pieces of information in a text."** Taught on days 34 and 35. Day 34 joins a picture record to a fact in *Sky Patterns*; day 35 joins two facts with "so". Children say, point to and draw it; the teacher writes the source note.
- **RL.1.5, "Explain major differences between books that tell stories and books that give information".** Day 35, built on the kindergarten story and information sort.
- **RL.1.2, "Retell stories, including key details, and demonstrate understanding of their central message or lesson."** Day 36 characters, setting and first events; day 37 the difficult events and a possible lesson; day 38 an ordered retelling with the lesson supported by one event.
- **RL.1.4, "Identify words and phrases in stories or poems that suggest feelings or appeal to the senses."** A first look on day 37 only (a feeling word, a sense word); taught in full on day 39.
- **W.1.7, "Participate in shared research and writing projects", and W.1.8, "gather information from provided sources to answer a question."** Day 34: the class asks, finds, notes and shares one note. First-teaching notes are not scored (unit field 5).
- **SL.1.1, SL.1.1a to c and SL.1.3, "Participate in collaborative conversations".** Practised on day 38; first scored on day 61.
- **Foundations, days 34 to 38 (cycle 5, lessons 26 to 30):** RF.1.1a, RF.1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, b, g, RF.1.4a to c, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, L.1.2e. Short o (review of kindergarten) with j, w and x added to the sound-spelling-map.
- **Limits that bite here.** Evidence is the words and pictures; the class picture record is teacher-made, and nobody looks at the sun. A child decodes only taught correspondences; the rich texts are read aloud. Talk is about the tale and the sky, never a child's home.
- **Connections.** L.1.1j, the four sentence kinds, spoken on day 35 (joined sentences grow from day 37 onward in later sections). L.1.1a: the unit's letter-form record starts day 34, eight forms a day inside 4 of the 10 handwriting minutes, scored on day 43 (section 2.3). Science 1-ESS1-1 comes first; if delayed, day 34's record is the bridge.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) x spells two sounds, /k/ and /s/, so fox has four phonemes. (2) The tale is credited aloud to the Efik and Ibibio people, collected by Elphinstone Dayrell in 1910, and told as a story people tell, never as wrong science. (3) Each exchange of the tale has two speakers; the day's questions ask one actor at a time ("Who asks? Who answers?"). (4) Words with two senses: "pattern" is the sky sense only (spelling work says "spelling"); "message" is taught to children as "lesson" (kindergarten's "class message" is a different thing); "compound" is the tale's big yard, never a sentence word (joined sentences are "joined sentences"). (5) Content notes: *Sky Patterns* has none. The tale has a flood that fills a house and forces the sun and moon into the sky: no death, injury, violence, religion or slur; fish and water animals only.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Kindergarten's research-cycle chart (ask, find, note, share) and connection arrow (a fact, an arrow, "so" or "because") are review: the teacher holds them on days 34 and 35. New here: text-structure (an information text built in an order, shown as a picture strip with "so"), held by the teacher on 34 and 35; story-map (unit 1 review), held by the teacher on 36 and 37 and by partners on 38; sentence-combining begins day 35 (joining two short sentences with "and", review of kindergarten); the feeling and sense word chart (a face for feelings, a hand, ear or eye for senses) opens on day 37. Unit 1's sound-spelling-map, main-idea-details, paragraph-frame and discussion-protocol stay on the wall. Filled maps fade to blank prompts at each observation.
- **Key moment 1, day 34: a picture record beside a fact.** Ideal instrument: wall strip of picture cards, a fact card and an arrow card. Spec: fits; paper. No gaps or asks.
- **Key moment 2, day 37: water rising.** Ideal instrument: a drawn house with a blue strip slid up to knee, head, roof. Spec: fits; paper.
- **A room with nothing.** Pictures drawn on scrap; the story-map is a folded sheet.

## 6. Days

## Day 34


- **15 min. Hook and read.** Read *Sky Patterns* whole (g1-u2-01, six pages, about 130 words), pausing after pages 3, 4 and 5. Words before: sky, day, observe, connect, notice, pattern, sunrise, sunset (with pictures). Match the page 3 and 4 sentences to the class picture record (teacher-made cards: sun coming up, sun going down), then say the compelling question.
- **8 min. Retrieval.** The Sana's Drawing card and the kindergarten tasks of unit field 4: name the title and author, retell, connect seeing to drawing, sort moon with sky words, describe, add a detail, give a note. Partners; hear the rotating six. A miss is modelled at the next small group.
- **7 min. Reread and question, with opening repair.** Reread pages 3 and 4. Partners point to the sentence that tells what the sun does. If many missed an item on unit 1's day 33, 5 minutes of opening repair come first.
- **5 min. Talk.** Turn card, one turn each: "What do you notice about when the sun comes up and goes down?" Pass allowed.
- **20 min. Make a connection.** Model on the class record: a picture, the arrow card, "so", a fact sentence from the page. Each child draws a sunrise or sunset record, adds a one-word label and a page sticker as the source note; the teacher writes the note.
- **5 min. Exit.** E34.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Spoken pairs: long or short vowel; blend and segment a four-sound word.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Explicit teaching: short o, and j, w and x added to the sound-spelling-map (x is /k/ /s/); the cycle 5 irregular words are mapped. Read the cycle 4 reader.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Eight letter forms into the unit record (a to h, 4 of the 10 minutes); dictation with taught words.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **3 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column and cycle record.
- **2 min. Retrieval.** Read and segment sun, then explain suns with two pictures (unit field 4).

Who holds what is carried: the teacher. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, one-word label, letter forms. Evidence: E34; the retrieval marks; the heard-six note.

## Day 35


- **15 min. Read the tale whole.** Words before: tale, invite, promise, warn, friend (five of the week 8 words, needed for the first reading; the rest on day 36). Read the retelling (g1-u2-03, about 300 words; to write) with four picture pauses, then *Sky Patterns* page 4 again. Credit aloud to the Efik and Ibibio people.
- **10 min. Compare, with retrieval first.** First 5 minutes: which day 34 source gives facts, which tale has imagined characters (kindergarten sort). Then two columns, tale and information, two features each: characters who talk and act, a lesson; real facts, "so".
- **10 min. Sentence kinds.** Pip says four lines, one at a time: statement, question, command, exclamation; the class shows the end mark gesture. Join two short sentences with "and" ("Sun came. Moon came.").
- **20 min. Connect two facts.** Page 4: the sun sets, so the sky grows dim. Arrow card between the two fact cards; pairs say it, then draw it. The heard six explain aloud (RI.1.3).
- **5 min. Exit.** E35.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Isolate the vowel in spoken pairs; blend and segment a five-sound word.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Decode then spell taught short o words; read the cycle 5 reader (adapted, to write) only after its words are mapped.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Eight letter forms (i to p) and day 34's forms revised; contrast b and d; dictation.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column and cycle record.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher on the tale, pairs on the sentence cards. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, sorting cards. Evidence: E35; the heard-six connection.

## Day 36


- **15 min. Reread the tale.** Reread for who and where (g1-u2-03). Words: house, remain, compound (the tale's big yard), and the rest of week 8. Pause after Sun's promise.
- **10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair.** Day 35 repair first if many missed E35. "Who is in the tale? Where do they live? What does Water say about the house?" Partners point to the picture.
- **10 min. Talk.** "Why does Water warn Sun?" Turn card; Pip says one line at a time.
- **20 min. Story-map.** Unit 1's story-map, with the teacher: characters (Sun, Water, Moon), place, three first events (Sun visits; Water says the house is too small; Sun promises a big compound). Each child draws one event.
- **5 min. Exit.** E36.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Segment and blend spoken words with x; count three sounds in box.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Map and spell short o words (box, jog); the cycle 5 irregular words.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Eight letter forms (q to x) and the revision; dictation.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column and cycle record.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher models; pairs hold the turn card. Tool: None. By hand: drawing events. Evidence: E36.

## Day 37


- **15 min. Reread the difficult events.** The repeated question: Water asks, Sun says yes, and the water rises to the knee, the head and the roof (g1-u2-03). A drawn house and a blue strip show it. No acting out of the tradition.
- **10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair.** "What does Sun say each time? What happens after? What does Sun not notice?" Partners point to the words. Day 36 repair first if many missed.
- **10 min. Talk.** "Which repeated question changes the tale?" Chain on cards: event, result, possible lesson.
- **20 min. Lesson, and feeling and sense words.** 12 minutes: connection arrow from "Sun said yes" to "the water rose" to a lesson, said as "listen to a warning before you promise"; children draw the event that shows it. 8 minutes: first look at a feeling word and a sense word (a face; an ear), from the tale's page about the water rising; full teaching is day 39.
- **5 min. Exit.** E37.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Isolate first, vowel and last sounds in spoken words with j and w.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Decode then spell j and w words.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Eight letter forms (y, z, A to F) and the revision; dictation.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column and cycle record.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher models; partners hold the cards. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, moving the blue strip. Evidence: E37.

## Day 38


- **15 min. Reread the tale whole.** The class says the repeated question with the teacher.
- **10 min. Order the story.** Partners place the story-map event cards in order and say first, next, last.
- **10 min. Talk with linked turns.** Groups of three (one speaks, one adds, one asks "Which part?"): "Which event matters most for the lesson?" Pip shows waiting, linking and asking, one line at a time (W to Z). Practice only; first scored on day 61.
- **20 min. Retell and lesson.** Partners retell with the map; each child draws the event that best supports the lesson, adds a word label and says "The lesson is ___ because ___". Hear the six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E38.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Segment all sounds in five-sound words.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 5 progress sample: read fox, remove the card, dictate job (6 minutes at the teacher table); same-block regrouping by result.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Eight letter forms (G to N) and the revision; dictation.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups at the teacher table, 6 minutes each, regrouped by the sample.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column and cycle record.

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the map and turn card. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, word label. Evidence: E38; the cycle 5 sample; the heard-six note.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 34, "Which words in the page tell what the sun does?"; day 37, "Which repeated question changes the tale?"; day 38, "Which event matters most for the lesson, and why?"
- Written task, day 38: a drawing of the supporting event with a word label and a spoken sentence the teacher writes.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) names an event in its place in the order; (2) states a lesson, not only a topic; (3) points to the picture or the words that show it.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 34: retrieval. Day 35: kindergarten story and information sort. Days 36 to 38: one repair of at most 5 minutes from field 4, in the reread part.
- **Retrieved.** Story-map (36 to 38), discussion-protocol (38), the turn card and Pip, sentence kinds (35 on), unit 1's cycle readers.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Pictures first; words sky, sunrise, sunset, tale, promise, warn. Frames: "The sun ___, so ___." "First ___. Then ___. Last ___."
- **Access.** Enlarged cards and strips; tactile outlines; a scribe for labels; the blue strip can be felt.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing or a spoken answer; a scribe; any picture may be held up.
- **Reading load.** Children decode only taught short o words and mapped words; the tale and *Sky Patterns* are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** A second lesson the tale could show, with its event.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the tale in two sittings, draw the three strips, say first, next, last.
- **One projector.** Show *Sky Patterns* and the tale's pictures page by page; the cards are paper.
