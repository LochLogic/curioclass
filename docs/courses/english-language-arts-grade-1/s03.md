## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How do a bird's body parts help it survive?"
**Hook, day 79.** Read *Beaks Find Food* whole. Match its feeding sentence to a beak drawing and open the compelling question.
**Inquiry.** On 81, which heading locates the beak fact? On 84, how do the foot article's linked facts explain movement? On 108, which story observation can the articles explain, and what does a poem imagine?
**Closing claim.** The research paragraph revised on 103 explains how one beak helps its bird obtain food, using two provided sources. The revised riddle on 117 transfers this explanation into accurate clues.
Model topic and details on 79, feature searches on 81 to 85, research on 90 and paragraph structure on 100. Step back for independent sources on 99 and 114.
