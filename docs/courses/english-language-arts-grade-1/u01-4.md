# Section 1.4: The later try, a check, and a repair


## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 1, Tools and work. *How do tools help people do their work?* (days 1 to 33): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 15 to 20.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (16), fluency 1 (15), application 2 (17, 18), check 1 (19), respond 1 (20). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 4 of 7 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 19 is the story check (Card B); day 20 returns and repairs it. Day 15 opens cycle 3 of the Skills Block on day 16.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: try, later, first, then, different, garage, dust, string. Frames: "First ___, then ___." "Lee tells the story." Preview pictures; rehearse in a home language first.
- **Access.** Large print cards; hearings may be done by pointing; the hearing is quiet and private.
- **Check accommodations.** Card B is read aloud; the child may point, draw or speak to a scribe.
- **Reading load.** None beyond the echoed lines and mapped words; the teacher reads all cards.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second pictured detail or a second event.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Reread the later try on the page, ask how the second try differs, then tell the story back with first, then, now. On the check, ask one question and let the child answer by pointing.
- **One projector.** Show the page and the picture; the child marks printed cards.
