# Section 1.4: The later try, a check, and a repair


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 19 is the story check (Card B); day 20 returns and repairs it. Day 15 opens cycle 3 of the Skills Block on day 16.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RF.1.4, 1.4a, 1.4b, RL.1.10, RI.1.10, day 15: "Read grade-level text orally with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings."** Children reread a supported passage (page 10 of g1-u1-01) and the hammer poem (g1-u1-03) by echo and choral reading; "bang" stays echoed. No rate target.
- **RL.1.3: "Describe characters, settings, and major events in a story, using key details."** Day 16 (a later episode changes the work), day 17 (compare first and later try), day 18 (prepare a whole description), day 19 (checked), day 20 (repaired).
- **RL.1.6, RL.1.7, RL.1.1, SL.1.2: "Identify who is telling", "Use illustrations and details", "Ask and answer".** Practiced on days 16 to 18 on the story; checked on day 19 on Card B (Jo and Tess).
- **Foundations, days 15 to 20 (cycle 2 lesson 15, cycle 3 lessons 16 to 20): RF.1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, RF.1.3b, RF.1.3g, RF.1.1a, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, L.1.2e.** Day 16 maps short i, ch and sh; mapped words look, like, and are new on day 16 (review of in, his, at); "with" is decodable. No -ng, -nk, clusters or plural -s: those wait for unit 2 and day 21.
- **Limits that bite here.** Day 19 cards are not modeled before the day. Children decode only taught patterns; oral long vowels never authorize a printed long-vowel spelling. Content notes: the story holds rain, a shed, a slipping screwdriver, a cat; no injury, death, violence, religion or slur. Talk is about the characters, never a child's home.
- **Connections.** RF.1.4 returns on days 23 to 25. SL.1.1 and 1.3 (day 10) are used again in the day 19 hearings.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** Why the check reads a new card; that a hearing marks only shown, shown with help or not yet seen; that repair on day 20 uses a fresh passage, never Card B; that the Card B retrieval event is the second thing a child may omit.

## 3. Checks, written first

**How day 19 runs (counted).** Setup and read-aloud: 10 minutes. Hearings: six groups of five children, five minutes a group, 30 minutes. While a group is heard, the other 25 children do the paper items: RL.1.3 draw and mark (10 minutes), RL.1.6 mark (5), RL.1.7 draw a word detail and a pictured detail with a word label (5). The hearing gives each child about one minute for the RL.1.1 answer and the SL.1.2 question and answer. Make-up: 15 minutes. Close: 5.

**Exit questions.** One spoken stem each, one ask; the teacher hears a rotating six. Reading exits reread an unworked part of Kofi and the Bicycle (DEFAULTS.md exit line). The E15 line prints "invented for practice".

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Story-map: partners hold it from day 14; the teacher holds it again on day 19 for the check items, children do not use it on the check. Discussion-protocol: partners hold the turn card; on the hearings it is not used. The teller card and speech card (from day 13) are held by children on days 16 to 18 and on day 20. The sound-spelling-map and paragraph-frame stay on the wall.
- **Key moment 1, day 19: the hearing.** Ideal instrument: a record sheet with the day's six names per group. Spec: fits; paper, and nobody is assessed aloud in front of the class.
- **Key moment 2, day 20: Return and repair.** Ideal instrument: the fresh card and three small groups. Spec: fits; paper.
- **A room with nothing.** The hearing needs only the record sheet and Card B printed; the repair needs the fresh card printed. Pictures can be drawn by the teacher.

## 6. Days

## Day 15


- **15 min. Reread aloud.** Reread page 10 of Kofi and the Bicycle (the supported passage) and the hammer poem (g1-u1-03): teacher, echo, then choral.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** Partners reread the poem's refrain and answer "Which words sound strong?" Hear the rotating six.
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs: "Which words make the poem sound like hammering?" Turn card.
- **20 min. Write one sentence.** Each child says one sentence about the story page ("Gran sits and Kofi works"), the teacher writes it, the child prints a word label and reads it back with the teacher.
- **5 min. Exit.** E15.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend, segment and count sounds in spoken words (flat, hand).
- **10 min. Map and read.** Cycle 2 closes: the, a, is, has with a short a and th chain: that, than, math, bath.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** N and n; dictation "that man has a hat".
- **30 min. Rotation.** Cycle progress sample plus the cycle 2 reader "Pats Mess" reread for meaning (two sentences with pictures).
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher models; partners hold the turn card. Tool: None. By hand: printing a word label. Evidence: E15.

## Day 16


- **15 min. Read pages 8 to 11.** Read the later episode: Gran sits and Kofi does the work, the first try slips, the second holds. Pause after page 9.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** "What does Kofi do differently?" Partners find the sentence (RL.1.1).
- **10 min. Talk.** "How does the work change?" Use "first" and "then". Hear a rotating six.
- **20 min. Draw the change.** Each child draws the first try and the later try in two boxes, with a word label on each.
- **5 min. Exit.** E16.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Cycle 3 opens: say words with /i/, /ch/ and /sh/ (spoken only).
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Map short i, ch and sh onto the sound-spelling-map; map look, like, and (new); review in, his, at; "with" decoded. Chain: sit, sat, sip, ship.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** O and o; dictation "a ship in a ship".
- **30 min. Rotation.** Reread "Pats Mess" for meaning.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, labeling. Evidence: E16.

## Day 17


- **15 min. Reread pages 4 to 11.** Reread the first and later attempts.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** "How is the second try different?" Pairs point to the first try and the later try.
- **10 min. Compare, spoken.** Two columns: first try; later try; children place picture cards and say "First ___, then ___." Hear a rotating six.
- **20 min. Draw and say.** Each child draws the two tries and says which detail changed.
- **5 min. Exit.** E17.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment words with /i/, /ch/, /sh/.
- **10 min. Map and read.** "Pats Map", the cycle 3 reader, first read after its patterns are mapped (an adaptation, still to make).
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** P and p; dictation "a chip in a dish".
- **30 min. Rotation.**
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the story-map and the turn card. Tool: None. By hand: drawing. Evidence: E17.

## Day 18


- **15 min. Reread pages 1 to 12 quickly.** Teacher rereads the story in order with the pictures.
- **10 min. Prepare.** Each pair chooses character, place and three events on the story-map and says them to a partner.
- **10 min. Talk.** "Who is in the story, where does it happen, and what happens?" Hear a rotating six.
- **20 min. Draw the description.** Each child draws character, place and three events and says it to the teacher or a partner.
- **5 min. Exit.** E18.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Spoken blending and segmenting.
- **10 min. Map and read.** "Pats Map" reread for meaning.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Q and q; dictation "a chin and a shin".
- **30 min. Rotation.**
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the story-map. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, labeling. Evidence: E18.

## Day 19


- **10 min. Setup and read-aloud.** Hand out Card B; read it aloud once to all.
- **30 min. Hearings.** Six groups of five, five minutes a group: the RL.1.1 answer, then the SL.1.2 question and answer, about one minute a child. Meanwhile the others do the paper items.
- **15 min. Make-up.** Absent children and any item left blank.
- **5 min. Close.** Collect papers; no teaching.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Spoken blending and segmenting.
- **10 min. Map and read.** Familiar words only; no reteaching.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** R and r; dictation "a chip".
- **30 min. Rotation.** Scheduled practice with "Pats Map"; cycle sample.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: nothing is used. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, marking. Evidence: items 1 to 5, the day 19 rows in the table above.

## Day 20


- **10 min. Read the fresh card.** Read the fresh Pia card (a hall; the door sign hangs crooked; Mr. Dane tapes it straight; Pia tells it) aloud with its picture; children listen only. E20 rereads Kofi and the Bicycle, page 2.
- **20 min. Return and repair.** Groups D, E and F take the repairs named in field 4 on the fresh card (D place, E the omitted event, F who tells); the other children draw their own description and label it.
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs say which part changed in their picture.
- **15 min. Draw a clearer description.** Each child redraws or adds to the day 19 drawing and says it to the teacher or a partner.
- **5 min. Exit.** E20.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Spoken blending and segmenting.
- **10 min. Map and read.** "Pats Map" reread for meaning.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** S and s; dictation "a ship at a dish".
- **30 min. Rotation.** Cycle sample and regrouping.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher in the three groups; others hold their own drawing. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, labeling. Evidence: E20; the day 19 results.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 16, "What happens between the first try and the second?"; day 17, "How is the second try different?"; day 18, "Where does it happen, and who is in it?"
- Written task, day 18: a drawing of the character, place and three events, with word labels and a spoken sentence the teacher writes.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) the character and place; (2) three events in order; (3) a word detail and a pictured detail.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Days 15 to 18: one repair of at most 5 minutes from the table in field 4. Day 20: the Return and repair part.
- **Retrieved.** The teller card (day 13), the story-map (day 10), the question move (day 2).
- **Fluency target.** Expression on echoed lines; no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: try, later, first, then, different, garage, dust, string. Frames: "First ___, then ___." "Lee tells the story." Preview pictures; rehearse in a home language first.
- **Access.** Large print cards; hearings may be done by pointing; the hearing is quiet and private.
- **Check accommodations.** Card B is read aloud; the child may point, draw or speak to a scribe.
- **Reading load.** None beyond the echoed lines and mapped words; the teacher reads all cards.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second pictured detail or a second event.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Reread the later try on the page, ask how the second try differs, then tell the story back with first, then, now. On the check, ask one question and let the child answer by pointing.
- **One projector.** Show the page and the picture; the child marks printed cards.
