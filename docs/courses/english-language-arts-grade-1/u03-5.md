## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits and end items read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); every other card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** Day 95 names the topic and key details of the beak article (RI.1.2, "Identify the main topic and retell key details of a text") and uses its features (RI.1.5). Days 96 and 99 locate facts with heading, diagram label and glossary, and with a second source (RI.1.5, W.1.8, "gather information from provided sources to answer a question"). Day 97 compares two provided pages and keeps source-labeled notes (W.1.7, "Participate in shared research and writing projects"; RI.1.2). Day 98 explains how a beak helps its bird (RI.1.3, "Describe the connection between two individuals, events, ideas, or pieces of information in a text"; W.1.2, "name a topic, supply some facts"). Foundations finish cycle 15 (a_e) and start cycle 16 (i_e, o_e) on 99.
- **Limits that bite.** The main product is assigned on 95: "Explain how one bird's beak helps it get food. Name the topic, give two source-supported facts and finish your explanation." Notes are gathered 95 to 99; this section scores nothing but the 118 row (field 3). Writing is a word label, a printed sentence, a drawing or scribing. Facts come only from the provided pages. A decoration is not a note. No outdoor watching; no touching nests, eggs or birds; predators and prey are non-graphic.
- **Connections.** The research-cycle chart, source labels, the two columns, the paragraph-frame and the closing from 3.4; the connection arrow (part, job) from 84; the main-idea-details picture from 79 to 82.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Topic and details are told apart by asking which words every detail is about. (2) A connection sentence names the part, the food and how the first gets the second. (3) A note without its source label is not yet a note. (4) The 98 sentence is the evidence for the 118 row, so it is kept with its feedback. (5) Silent e adds a letter but no sound (H).

## 6. Days

## Day 95


- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E94, one component, a fresh paragraph.
- **5 min. Assign the product.** The teacher reads the task: "Explain how one bird's beak helps it get food. Name the topic, give two source-supported facts and finish your explanation." Children say it back to a partner.
- **12 min. Words, then read Beaks Find Food.** Words with pictures: bird, food, beak, bill, shape, survive. The teacher reads the whole article aloud; fingers on the heading and the first sentence of each part.
- **13 min. Topic and details, taught.** The main-idea-details picture is reviewed. The teacher models: the topic is the one thing every detail is about; each detail is a fact about it. Pairs name the topic of the first part and one detail.
- **10 min. Talk.** Turn card: "Which details are about the beak?" Six pairs heard; pass allowed.
- **10 min. Record.** Each child draws the beak and prints one word label; the teacher hears six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E95.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: a long and a short vowel in a pair (tap, tape); blend /k/ /a/ /n/; first, vowel and last sound of cake; segment shave. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 15 lesson 77: a_e. Contrast a closed syllable and silent e (can, cane). Decode, then spell, taught words. A picture guess is shown and set aside: map, blend, confirm in the sentence (repair for I). The adapted cycle 15 reader is read only after its patterns and irregular words are mapped; reread for meaning and expression.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dictation: "Jake can make a cake." Print the pairs.
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). - **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: beak, shape, food, source, check, connect, explain, because. Frames: "The beak is ___." "It helps the bird ___." "Source ___ says ___."
- **Access.** Content dictation; enlarged article pages; partner pointing; a picture arrow in place of a written connector.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing or a spoken answer; stems read twice; a scribe for the connection sentence; no time pressure.
- **Reading load.** The articles and cards are read aloud; children read word labels, source labels and their own sentence.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second bird whose beak does a different job and note it with its label.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the beak article; name the topic and two details; make labeled note cards; say how the beak helps its bird and write or scribe one sentence. Verify one note in a second page by pointing to the line.
- **One projector.** Show the article and then two pages side by side with the notes folder; children copy from printed notes.
