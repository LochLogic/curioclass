# Section 3.5: Beak research: topic, features, two pages and a connecting sentence

## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 3, Birds' amazing bodies. *How do a bird's body parts help it survive?* (days 79 to 119): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 95 to 99.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (95), application 4 (96, 97, 98, 99). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 5 of 10 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits and end items read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); every other card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.
