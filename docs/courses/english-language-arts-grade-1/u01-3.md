# Section 1.3: Kofi and the Bicycle, first readings

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-1/1/s/1.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-1 u01-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RL.1.3: "Describe characters, settings, and major events in a story, using key details."** Days 10 to 13 teach it, day 14 applies it. Day 10 names the three parts on the story-map; day 11 characters; day 12 setting and events; day 14 a character's action. Children say, point to, draw or label; the teacher scribes.
- **RL.1.6: "Identify who is telling the story at various points in a text."** Day 13 (and 10 to 12 as the teller is noticed). Kofi tells the whole story; Gran speaks only in quotation marks and Kofi also speaks in them.
- **RL.1.7: "Use illustrations and details in a story to describe its characters, setting, or events."** Days 11 to 14: a word detail and a pictured detail for the same character or event.
- **RL.1.1, RI.1.1, SL.1.2: "Ask and answer".** Taught on days 11 and 12 on the story (the article work of days 2 and 4 now moves to a story).
- **SL.1.1 and SL.1.3, day 10 (taught).** "Participate in collaborative conversations" and "Ask and answer questions about what a speaker says in order to clear up any confusion". The discussion-protocol of kindergarten (turn card and starters) becomes explicit.
- **Foundations, days 10 to 14 (cycle 2, lessons 10 to 14): RF.1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, RF.1.3b, RF.1.3g, RF.1.1a, RF.1.4, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, L.1.2e.** Short a and th, mapped words, spoken blending and segmenting of four and five sounds, one letter pair a day. Clusters and endings are spoken only.
- **Limits that bite here.** Evidence is the story's words and pictures. The story is not decodable; children join only refrains and mapped words. Talk is about Kofi and Gran, never a child's own grandparent or family work. Content in the story: rain, a shed of tools, a screwdriver slipping off a bolt (no one is hurt), a cat; no death, violence, injury, religion or slur. Real screwdrivers and wrenches are shown by the teacher only, never handled.
- **Connections.** RF.1.4: the story is read on day 15. L.1.5: "tight, loose, still" words on day 11 (week 3 list).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** The difference between the teller (Kofi, "I") and a speaker (anyone in quotation marks); that the teller is also a character; that the picture holds details the words leave out and the reverse (the teacher note in g1-u1-01 lists them); that Kofi's spoken lines are on pages 4, 7 and 11.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Story-map: who, where and when, what happens (kindergarten, review), the teacher holds it on days 10 to 13 and partners hold it from day 14. It returns to the story through day 32. Discussion-protocol: explicit on day 10, partners hold it from day 11. The teller card ("I tell") and the speech card (quotation marks) begin on day 13. The sound-spelling-map and the paragraph-frame stay on the wall.
- **Key moment 1, day 10: the story-map.** Ideal instrument: a three-box strip of paper, a card for each box, event cards to order. Spec: fits; paper. No gaps or asks.
- **Key moment 2, day 13: the teller and speech cards.** Ideal instrument: two hand cards. Spec: fits; paper.
- **A room with nothing.** The story-map is a sheet folded in three. The story pictures can be drawn by the teacher on scrap paper.

## 6. Days

## Day 10


- **15 min. Read the story whole.** Read Kofi and the Bicycle (g1-u1-01, 433 words, twelve pages) with its pictures, pausing after pages 3, 6 and 10. Tool names are shown with pictures first.
- **10 min. Retrieval and repair.** Retrieval: kindergarten story-map. Ask "Who is in the opening, where are they, what happens first?" Each child marks one character card, one place card and orders two event pictures. If missed, the teacher models one entry and the child completes another. This is also the repair slot for day 9's gaps (AI), at most 5 minutes.
- **10 min. Discussion-protocol, made explicit.** Turn card, two turns each, pass allowed; starters "I agree because", "I would add", "Which part?". Partners talk about what Kofi wants. Hear the rotating six.
- **20 min. The story-map.** Teacher builds boxes one to three together (who, where and when, first event), children draw them.
- **5 min. Exit.** E10.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Reread the cycle 2 story orally: retell with sounds in mind; spoken rhyme.
- **10 min. Mapped words.** Practice the, is, has, a, this with their parts to remember.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** I and i; dictation "this is a hat".
- **30 min. Rotation.** Reread the cycle 2 reader for meaning (who is Pat; what happens).
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher. Tool: None. By hand: marking cards, drawing. Evidence: E10; the retrieval marks.

## Day 11


- **15 min. Read pages 1 to 7.** Reread the first attempt: Kofi tries with his hands, then a screwdriver, then Gran uses the wrench. Pause at pages 4, 5 and 7.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** Pairs ask one question about Gran or Kofi and point to the page that answers (RL.1.1).
- **10 min. Describe two characters.** Two-column cards: what the words say each does; what the picture adds (Kofi's green raincoat, Gran's gray curls and blue apron). The teacher holds the cards.
- **20 min. Character cards.** Each child draws Kofi or Gran, labels one word, says "The words say ___ and the picture shows ___".
- **5 min. Exit.** E11.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend a four-sound spoken word (flat) and a five-sound one (stamp); isolate first, middle vowel and last; segment all sounds, counting sounds, not letters.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Chain and spell words from the bank; the, is, a.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** J and j; dictation "Pat has a hat".
- **30 min. Rotation.**
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher models; pairs hold the turn card. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, labeling. Evidence: E11.

## Day 12


- **15 min. Read pages 1 to 7 for the place.** Focus on page 2 (the rain, the shed) and page 3 (what is wrong with the bicycle).
- **10 min. Reread and question.** "Where does the work happen? What problem is there?" Partners point to the sentence (RL.1.1).
- **10 min. Setting and events.** Talk: "Why do Kofi and Gran work inside?" ("Rain taps on the shed roof, so Gran and I will work inside.") Use "because".
- **20 min. Story-map, events.** Put the three first events in order (hands, screwdriver slips, wrench) on the map; each child draws one event.
- **5 min. Exit.** E12.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** As day 11; use sound counters.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Spell words from memory.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** K and k; dictation "a tan cat".
- **30 min. Rotation.**
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher. Tool: None. By hand: drawing. Evidence: E12.

## Day 13


- **15 min. Read pages 1 to 7 with two voices.** The teacher reads the telling in one voice and the quoted words in another; the class holds up the "I tell" card for the telling and the speech card for the quoted words. Inquiry: how does the story show its teller from a speaker?
- **10 min. Reread and question.** Page 1: who tells it, and which words show it ("I am Kofi")? Page 6: who speaks in the marks?
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs: "How can one passage hold telling and speaking?" (Kofi tells, Gran speaks; on page 4 Kofi also speaks, "Hands are not enough," I say.)
- **20 min. Mark the teller.** A three-panel strip of page 1, 4 and 7 sentences with pictures; place the "I tell" card on the teller and the speech card on the quoted words.
- **5 min. Exit.** E13.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Segment and blend.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Review the mapped words.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** L and l; dictation "I tell".
- **30 min. Rotation.**
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the cards; children place them. Tool: None. By hand: placing cards. Evidence: E13.

## Day 14


- **15 min. Read pages 8 to 12.** Reread the later attempt; pause at page 8 ("This time you do the work. I will watch.").
- **10 min. Partners find the evidence first.** Partners find the words and the picture that show why Gran sits on the stool (words: "This time you do the work"; picture: her folded hands) before the teacher responds. The teacher holds back.
- **10 min. Talk.** "Which words and which picture explain Gran's action?" Turn card; hear the rotating six.
- **20 min. Draw and describe.** Each child picks Kofi's or Gran's action, draws it, writes one word label and says it: "I know because the words say ___ and the picture shows ___."
- **5 min. Exit.** E14.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Spoken blends.
- **10 min. Map and read.** Connected practice with the cycle 2 reader words.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** M and m; dictation "a man at a map".
- **30 min. Rotation.**
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the story-map and the turn card. Tool: None. By hand: drawing. Evidence: E14.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 10, "What does Kofi want?"; day 13, "Who tells, and who speaks?"; day 14, "Which words and which picture explain Gran's action?"
- Written task, day 14: a drawing of one action with a word label and a spoken sentence the teacher writes.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) names the character; (2) gives a word detail from the page; (3) gives a pictured detail.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 10: story-map retrieval and day 9's repair slot. Days 11 to 14: one repair of at most 5 minutes from the table in field 4.
- **Retrieved.** The question move (day 2); two sources (day 3); the mapped words daily.
- **Fluency target.** Expression on the refrains; no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: story, character, place, first, next, tell, speak, Gran, Kofi. Frames: "Kofi tells the story." "Gran says ___." "The picture shows ___." Preview pictures; rehearse in a home language first.
- **Access.** Large print cards; the two hand cards can be coloured; pointing is an answer.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards are read aloud; the child may point or speak to a scribe.
- **Reading load.** None beyond refrains and mapped words; the teacher reads the story.
- **Extension, same standard.** Describe a second character or a second picture detail.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read a page, then ask who is telling and where it happens. Cover the picture, then uncover and ask what it adds. Take turns reading a quoted line aloud.
- **One projector.** Show a page at a time; mark on printed cards.
