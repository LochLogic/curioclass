## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 1, Tools and work. *How do tools help people do their work?* (days 1 to 33): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 10 to 14.** Days by kind: understanding 4 (10, 11, 12, 13), application 1 (14). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

## 3. Checks, written first

<!-- skel -->
<!-- /skel -->

**Unit assessment items owned:** none. The RL items on day 19 and day 32 belong to section 1.4's rows.

**Exit questions.** One spoken stem each, one ask. Every child marks or points; the teacher hears the day's rotating six, 20 seconds each. Fictional cards print "invented for practice". The cases are Ali and Grandpa, different from the story. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E10** (day 10). Card: "I am Ali. We sit on the porch. My wagon wheel squeaks. Grandpa brings an oil can." Pictures: a porch; a kitchen. Stem: "Point to the picture that shows where this story happens." Answer: the porch. Wrong: the kitchen, a setting not in the card (D).
- **E11** (day 11). Card: "I am Ali. I hold the lid. Grandpa pours the oil." Picture: Ali holding a lid; Grandpa in a blue hat pouring oil. Stem: "What does Grandpa do? Use the words." Answer: he pours the oil. Wrong: "He has a blue hat", a picture detail with no word detail (C); "Grandpa holds the lid", an action from another character (A).
- **E12** (day 12). Card: "We stop at the gate because my wagon tips over. Grandpa finds a rope and ties the load. We leave with the wagon straight." Pictures: three, scrambled. Stem: "Tell me, in order, what happens at the gate." Answer: the wagon tips; Grandpa ties the load with a rope; the wagon is straight. Wrong: "It tips and then it is straight", the rope missing (E).
- **E13** (day 13). Card, two panels. Panel 1: "I am Ali. Grandpa opens the box." Panel 2: "'Here is the oil,' Grandpa says. I take it." Stem: "Who is telling this story?" Answer: Ali. Wrong: Grandpa, because he speaks (F).
- **E14** (day 14). Card: "I cannot reach the pump. Grandpa lowers it. I fill the tire." Picture: Ali, Grandpa and a pump on a low stand. Stem: "Why does Grandpa lower the pump? Use the words." Answer: Ali cannot reach it. Wrong: "Grandpa is tall", a picture fact (C); "Grandpa wants to fill the tire" (A).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 10, "What does Kofi want?"; day 13, "Who tells, and who speaks?"; day 14, "Which words and which picture explain Gran's action?"
- Written task, day 14: a drawing of one action with a word label and a spoken sentence the teacher writes.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) names the character; (2) gives a word detail from the page; (3) gives a pictured detail.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Day 10: story-map retrieval and day 9's repair slot. Days 11 to 14: one repair of at most 5 minutes from the table in field 4.
- **Retrieved.** The question move (day 2); two sources (day 3); the mapped words daily.
- **Fluency target.** Expression on the refrains; no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: story, character, place, first, next, tell, speak, Gran, Kofi. Frames: "Kofi tells the story." "Gran says ___." "The picture shows ___." Preview pictures; rehearse in a home language first.
- **Access.** Large print cards; the two hand cards can be coloured; pointing is an answer.
- **Check accommodations.** Cards are read aloud; the child may point or speak to a scribe.
- **Reading load.** None beyond refrains and mapped words; the teacher reads the story.
- **Extension, same standard.** Describe a second character or a second picture detail.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read a page, then ask who is telling and where it happens. Cover the picture, then uncover and ask what it adds. Take turns reading a quoted line aloud.
- **One projector.** Show a page at a time; mark on printed cards.
