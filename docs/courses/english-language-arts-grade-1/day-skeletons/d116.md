## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Write an independent expert-bird riddle from the verified facts.
- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u3-01, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M3 U3 L9. Foundations: consolidate this unit's taught cycles with map-blend-read-spell-reread small groups, a matched decodable and supported short grade 1 topic reading; no new pattern. Text vocabulary before, during and after reading; sentence work from the text. Turn and talk with listening, several linked exchanges and clarification, then a brief text-based response. Sensitivity: non-graphic predators/prey; watch from window or picture if needed; supervised outdoors, no touching nests, eggs or birds. Handwriting: apply already taught forms in sentence dictation; new-letter teaching finished on 27. Foundations observation: ten different children today at 3 minutes each, 30 teacher minutes; remaining 30 minutes retain explicit review and matched reading. Count printed syllables by vowel sounds; divide and decode closed/open/silent-e two-syllable words; read -s/-es/-ing, all three -ed sounds, -er/-est and silent-e dropping, without doubling. Cohorts across 116 to 118 total 30; collect once. Response day 119 only.
