- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u1-04, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M1 U3 L8. Foundations: consolidate this unit's taught cycles with map-blend-read-spell-reread small groups, a matched decodable and supported short grade 1 topic reading; no new pattern. Text vocabulary before, during and after reading; sentence work from the text. Turn and talk with listening, several linked exchanges and clarification, then a brief text-based response. Foundations observation rotation 2 of 5: six different children, 5 minutes each, 30 teacher minutes inside the 60-minute foundations block. Hear sentence first word/capital/end mark; spoken long/short vowel contrast, blending, first/medial-vowel/last isolation and complete segmentation; taught a/i/u and th/ch/sh words and mapped irregular words; correct taught-word and plausible untaught-word spelling. Read brief supported grade 1 prose, poem and information, state a purpose and meaning, reread for accuracy/rate/expression and confirm or correct after decoding.
