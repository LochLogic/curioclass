## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Use a kitchen-tool text to ask a question and define a tool by its category and use.
- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u1-02, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M1 U1 L4. Foundations: 2017 Skills Block cycle 1, lesson 4; retrieve spoken rhyme, syllables, onset-rime, three-sound blending and VC/CVC reading. Decode then spell taught words; teacher-read cycle 1 poem and a kindergarten-pattern decodable whose spellings and irregular words are already mapped; reread for meaning and expression. Text vocabulary before, during and after reading; sentence work from the text. Turn and talk with listening, several linked exchanges and clarification, then a brief text-based response. Sensitivity: discuss workers in the texts, not family employment; real tools teacher-only and no blades handled; class names/photos stay in the room. Handwriting: rehearse taught upper/lowercase forms and apply them in dictation; days 2 to 27 cover one alphabet pair daily, then review. Word work: make a real-life connection to a safe tool used at school; no home possessions need be disclosed.
