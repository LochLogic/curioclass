## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

- **Goal:** Jointly draft a nest opinion and connect the reason to the opinion.
- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u4-04, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M4 U2 L7.
