## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Ask and answer key-detail questions about building-site tools.
- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u1-02, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M1 U1 L2. Foundations: 2017 Skills Block cycle 1, lesson 2; retrieve spoken rhyme, syllables, onset-rime, three-sound blending and VC/CVC reading. Decode then spell taught words; teacher-read cycle 1 poem and a kindergarten-pattern decodable whose spellings and irregular words are already mapped; reread for meaning and expression. Text vocabulary before, during and after reading; sentence work from the text. Turn and talk with listening, several linked exchanges and clarification, then a brief text-based response. RI.1.6 and SL.1.2 taught explicitly here, before social studies unit 2 starts on school day 18 and math unit 2 on school day 17. Sensitivity: discuss workers in the texts, not family employment; real tools teacher-only and no blades handled; class names/photos stay in the room. Handwriting: rehearse taught upper/lowercase forms and apply them in dictation; days 2 to 27 cover one alphabet pair daily, then review. Teach the sentence feature routine explicitly: locate first word, initial capital and end mark in a known decoded sentence, then reread to its end.
