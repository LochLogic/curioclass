- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u1-04, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M1 U3 L10. Foundations: consolidate this unit's taught cycles with map-blend-read-spell-reread small groups, a matched decodable and supported short grade 1 topic reading; no new pattern. Text vocabulary before, during and after reading; sentence work from the text. Turn and talk with listening, several linked exchanges and clarification, then a brief text-based response. Sensitivity: discuss workers in the texts, not family employment; real tools teacher-only and no blades handled; class names/photos stay in the room. Handwriting: apply already taught forms in sentence dictation; new-letter teaching finished on 27. Foundations observation rotation 4 of 5: six different children, 5 minutes each, 30 teacher minutes inside the 60-minute foundations block. Hear sentence first word/capital/end mark; spoken long/short vowel contrast, blending, first/medial-vowel/last isolation and complete segmentation; taught a/i/u and th/ch/sh words and mapped irregular words; correct taught-word and plausible untaught-word spelling. Read brief supported grade 1 prose, poem and information, state a purpose and meaning, reread for accuracy/rate/expression and confirm or correct after decoding. Use dated samples from 26 to 31 for all 52 letter forms, completing only missing forms. The other children read known texts and spell independently. Each child supplies this evidence once in the window; no whole-class repeat on 31. This is evidence to taught scope; short-vowel patterns continue in unit 2. Topic word-work observation: three different five-child groups, 6 minutes each (15 children today, the other 15 on the other word-work day). Each child sorts words, defines by category and attribute, gives a school-use connection, contrasts verb manner and adjective intensity, and explains with topic words and because. Adult guidance is allowed; record each part once. Drafting continues in the remaining topic minutes. Response day 33 only.
