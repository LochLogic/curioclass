## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Use the story and articles to identify a classroom job a designed tool could help.
- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u1-01, g1-u1-02, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M1 U3 L1. Foundations: 2017 Skills Block cycle 4, lesson 21; short u and qu; introduce plural -s on taught bases. Retrieval: ek-unknown-words, an ending as a clue to a known word's meaning, before plural -s. Explicit sound-to-spelling teaching before word chains and spelling; use previous-cycle decodable until current correspondences and irregular words are mapped. Text vocabulary before, during and after reading; sentence work from the text. Turn and talk with listening, several linked exchanges and clarification, then a brief text-based response. Retrieve the paragraph-frame first modeled on day 7, now for the designed tool: topic, facts, closing, drawing on main-idea-details from kindergarten. Teacher demonstrates any real tool; no child handles blades. Use a paper model or safe classroom craft materials. Sensitivity: discuss workers in the texts, not family employment; real tools teacher-only and no blades handled; class names/photos stay in the room. Handwriting: rehearse taught upper/lowercase forms and apply them in dictation; days 2 to 27 cover one alphabet pair daily, then review.
