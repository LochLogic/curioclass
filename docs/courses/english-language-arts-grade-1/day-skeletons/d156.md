## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Write one short opinion transfer about bird care with a reason and closure.
- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u4-01, g1-u4-02, g1-u4-03, g1-u4-06, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M4 U3 L16. Foundations: consolidate this unit's taught cycles with map-blend-read-spell-reread small groups, a matched decodable and supported short grade 1 topic reading; no new pattern. End-of-unit opinion transfer: one brief independent piece on a fresh bird-care choice using familiar sources. Name the topic/book, state an opinion, give one reason and a closing. The whole class writes together in 20 topic minutes; no individual writing conference. Grammar uses the 150 to 152 written record and comparison uses 153 and 154, without new performances today. Remaining topic time finishes the portrait for sharing. Response on 157 only. Sensitivity: injured bird in adult care, no graphic details, never pick up wild birds. Bird-care projects are class-based; no child needs a yard or feeder.
