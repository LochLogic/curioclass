## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Explore how classroom tools help people work and recover familiar reading routines.
- **Note:** Topic reads: g1-u1-02, whole at first reading unless this is an explicit reread or part of a set. Base topic: 2017 grade 1 M1 U1 L1. Foundations: 2017 Skills Block cycle 1, lesson 1; retrieve spoken rhyme, syllables, onset-rime, three-sound blending and VC/CVC reading.
