## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How do tools help people do their work?"
**Hook, day 1.** Read the complete building-site article, then hide its picture: which job can its words explain? Reveal the picture and open the compelling question.
**Inquiry.** Day 3: which farm fact comes only from the words? Day 13: how does the bicycle story distinguish its teller from a speaker? Day 25: which article detail explains a useful design change? Reread the relevant sentence before answering.
**Closing claim.** On days 29 to 30, explain how a designed tool helps a classroom job, using the tool articles and observations of the paper model; the sign model supplies structure.
Model locating evidence on 2, story mapping on 10 and paragraph parts on 7 and 22. On 14 and 23, partners find the evidence before the teacher responds.
