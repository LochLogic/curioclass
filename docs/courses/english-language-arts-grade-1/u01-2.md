# Section 1.2: Talking, writing and a check on the tool articles


## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 1, Tools and work. *How do tools help people do their work?* (days 1 to 33): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 5 to 9.** Days by kind: fluency 1 (5), application 2 (6, 7), check 1 (8), respond 1 (9). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 2 of 7 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 8 is the mid-unit check on the articles; day 9 repairs and retries it.
