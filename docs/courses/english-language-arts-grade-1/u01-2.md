# Section 1.2: Talking, writing and a check on the tool articles


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 8 is the mid-unit check on the articles; day 9 repairs and retries it.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RF.1.4, 1.4a to c, RL.1.10, RI.1.10: "Read grade-level text orally with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings."** Day 5, fluency, on the hammer poem (g1-u1-03): teacher read, echo, choral. Children read the poem only as joined refrains and echoed lines; "bang" is echoed, never decoded (ng is unit 2).
- **RI.1.1, RL.1.1, SL.1.2: "Ask and answer".** Practised days 6 to 9 on the articles (taught on 2 and 4, section 1.1).
- **SL.1.1, SL.1.3, SL.1.1a to c (day 6).** "Build on others' talk" and "ask for clarification" are practised here with kindergarten talk moves; the grade 1 teaching is day 10.
- **W.1.2: "name a topic, supply some facts about the topic, and provide some sense of closure."** Day 7 practises it as drawing, a word label and a dictated sentence set (topic, two facts, closing), with the sign model (g1-u1-04) as the paragraph-frame. The frame is first modeled here; day 21 teaches the main piece.
- **RI.1.6 and RI.1.7, days 8 and 9: "Distinguish" and "Use the illustrations and details ... to describe its key ideas".** Day 8 checks both on the sieve card; day 9 retries.
- **L.1.5d, day 5: "Distinguish shades of meaning among verbs differing in manner ... and adjectives differing in intensity".** Verbs tap, knock, bang and adjectives tiny, little, big, gigantic, acted and ordered. "Look, peek and glance" (word card V) waits for day 26 to 29.
- **Foundations, days 5 to 9 (cycle 1 end, cycle 2): RF.1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, RF.1.3b, RF.1.3g, RF.1.1a, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, L.1.2e.** Short a and th together (review of kindergarten, mapped on day 6), the, a, is, has as mapped words, CVC decoding and spelling, one letter pair a day. Plural -s waits for day 21.
- **Limits that bite here.** The check cards are not modeled before day 8. No blade is handled. Talk is about the texts, never a family. Evidence is spoken, pointed, sorted or drawn; decoding and spelling are scored only on taught patterns.
- **Connections.** SL.1.1 and SL.1.3, day 6; W.1.2, day 7; L.1.5d, day 5.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** How to read a poem with expression for a child who echoes it (phrase, beat, loud and soft); the difference between a puppet's one-line turn and a child's linked turns; why the sign model's four sentences are topic, fact, fact and closing; why the check is hearings in six groups of five and why the paper items run at the same time.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** day 8 RI.1.1 and SL.1.2 (rows above, on Card A, field 5 of the unit plan). Items for RI.1.6 and RI.1.7 on day 8 are section 1.1's rows, sat here on the same day.

**Day 8 record.** Card A is read aloud once to the class. Six groups of five are heard five minutes each (RI.1.1 and SL.1.2 together, thirty minutes); meanwhile every child does the two paper items at their seat, RI.1.6 (mark a word symbol and a picture symbol) and RI.1.7 (draw the sieve in use and add a word label). No new teaching. Marking: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Wrong answers use the letters in the unit's rows (A, B, C).

**Exit questions.** One spoken stem each, one ask. Every child marks or points; the teacher hears the day's rotating six, 20 seconds each. If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E5** (day 5). The teacher says: "Wipe, wipe, wipe, little sponge. Wipe the table clean." (invented for practice). Stem: "Say it back the way a sponge would wipe." Answer: three phrases, "Wipe, wipe, wipe", "little sponge", "wipe the table clean", with a steady repeated beat. Wrong: each word on its own, no phrases (R).
- **E6** (day 6). Pip the puppet reads one line from a card: "A mop has soft strings. It soaks up spills." Pip adds one line: "It helps with cleaning." Pictures: a mop on a wet floor; a broom. Stem: "Add one thing about the mop to what Pip said." Answer: any fact from the card, such as "Its soft strings soak up spills." Wrong: "My dog likes the mop" or "I like cleaning", an unrelated turn (AE).
- **E7** (day 7). Card: "A clip holds our drawings. Its jaws grip the paper. We can find our drawings together." Pictures: clipped drawings; a loose pile. Stem: "Tell me one fact about the clip that I could write after the topic." Answer: "Its jaws grip the paper" or "It keeps the drawings together." Wrong: "It is nice" or "I like it", no fact (AA).
- **E9** (day 9). s01-X9v2 rereads Tools in the Kitchen, paragraph 2 (the ladle) aloud; items.csv holds the stem, answer and wrongs.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Discussion-protocol: teacher holds it on 5, partners hold it from 6 (turn card, two turns each, Pip the puppet speaks one line at a time, a routine of kindergarten, review). Main-idea-details returns on day 7 and is joined by the paragraph-frame (topic, two facts, closing), which the teacher holds on day 7 and partners hold in section 1.3 onward; it is withdrawn to a checklist on day 30. The sound-spelling-map returns on every foundations part; th and short a are mapped on day 6.
- **Key moment 1, day 7: the paragraph frame.** Ideal instrument: the sign model on a strip for each sentence, so children can move strips into topic, fact, fact, closing. Spec: fits; paper strips. No gaps or asks.
- **Key moment 2, day 8: the six hearings.** Ideal instrument: a record sheet with the group, item and shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Spec: fits; one sheet, hand ruled.
- **A room with nothing.** Sentence strips can be torn paper. The hose and sieve pictures can be drawn. Pip is a sock.

## 6. Days

## Day 5


- **15 min. Read and join.** The teacher reads The Hammer Song (g1-u1-03, 56 words) twice, then children join the refrains "Tap, tap, tap" and "Bang, bang, bang" and echo each line. Echo and choral reading, a second and a third reading.
- **10 min. Connect poem and article.** Reread the hammer sentence in Builders at Work. Which poem words name the action (tap on a nail)? Which article sentence describes it (the flat end pushes a nail into wood)? Point to both.
- **10 min. Act and order words.** Act tap, knock, bang from soft to loud; order tiny, little, big, gigantic with hands (L.1.5d). Use "big" from the poem and "tiny" from its last line.
- **20 min. One sentence.** Each child draws a hammer job and says one sentence with the frame "A hammer ___"; the teacher writes it under the picture; the child writes one word label ("nail").
- **5 min. Exit.** E5.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Rhyming pairs from the poem (sings, things; booms, rooms).
- **10 min. Map and read.** Review mat, sat, map, tap, the; the sound-spelling-map.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Print D and d and take dictation "a map". Repair slot for any day 4 foundations gap, at most 10 minutes.
- **30 min. Cycle progress sample.** Six groups of five, five minutes at the teacher table: each child reads two CVC words, one mapped word and spells one word. Regroup for cycle 2 in the same block.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher. Tool: None. By hand: echoing, acting, drawing. Evidence: E5; the sample.

## Day 6


- **10 min. Reread the kitchen article.** Reread Tools in the Kitchen (g1-u1-02), the ladle paragraph.
- **10 min. Model a turn.** Pip says one line about the ladle ("A ladle lifts soup."). The teacher adds a fact ("The long handle keeps the cook's hand away from the hot pot.") and asks one clarifying question ("Which part is long?"). Kindergarten "Ask a classmate" moves, review. Pip speaks one line at a time.
- **15 min. Partner talk.** Fifteen pairs: one says a fact about a tool from any article, the other adds one fact, then asks "Which part?" Two linked exchanges each. The teacher listens to the rotating six (three pairs, about three minutes a pair) and records an addition and a clarification.
- **20 min. Draw the explained tool.** Each child draws the tool the partner told about and writes one word label; says "It helps because ...".
- **5 min. Exit.** E6.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Short a and long a, spoken: cap and cape, hat and hate. Count sounds in hat.
- **10 min. Map short a and th.** Short a with th together on the map (digraph: two letters for one sound, review of kindergarten). Decode and encode a word chain: at, cat, hat, that.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** E and e; dictation "that cat".
- **30 min. Rotation.** The kindergarten-pattern reader again, in new groups, until the cycle 2 reader is ready on day 7.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the turn card; the teacher holds Pip. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, labeling. Evidence: E6; the listening record (addition and clarification).

## Day 7


- **15 min. Read the sign model.** Read g1-u1-04, the classroom-sign paragraph (26 words, four sentences, a newly worded adaptation; credit in the day's credit line). Move strips: sentence 1 is the topic, 2 and 3 facts, 4 closing. Paragraph-frame begins; main-idea-details returns.
- **10 min. Teacher models.** With Builders at Work: topic "Builders use tools", two facts (hammer drives nails; level shows if a board is flat), closing "Tools help builders." The teacher says it and points at each strip.
- **10 min. Partner practice.** Pairs say a topic and two facts for the wheelbarrow. Hear the rotating six.
- **20 min. My tool, three parts.** Each child draws one tool and places three strips: topic, two facts; the teacher writes the dictated sentences; the child writes a word label. Closing is said aloud.
- **5 min. Exit.** E7.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment short a words (map, nap, pat).
- **10 min. Mapped words.** The, a, is, has: each with its part to remember (the s that says /z/), kindergarten mapped words, review. Chain: pat, pan, pal, van.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** F and f; dictation "a fat cat".
- **30 min. Rotation.** First reading of the adapted cycle 2 reader (Pats Mess, adapted; still to make) after short a, th and its mapped words are ready. Words only from the cycle 2 bank: at, am, an, can, cat, hat, map, nap, pan, pat, tap, that, this, the, a, is, has, and the name Pat.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the frame. Tool: None. By hand: moving strips, drawing, labeling. Evidence: E7; the strips are observed (AA, AB).

## Day 8


- **10 min. Setup and read-aloud.** Hand out the paper items and read Card A aloud once (fresh card, field 5 of the unit plan; never taught).
- **30 min. Hearings.** Six groups of five, five minutes each at the teacher table: each child asks a question about Card A, then answers one (RI.1.1) and answers another child's question (SL.1.2). Meanwhile each child does two paper items at their seat: RI.1.6 (10 minutes) and RI.1.7 (10 minutes); finished children draw quietly. No hint, no model.
- **15 min. Make-up.** Children not yet heard are heard now (three groups of five at five minutes); children who finish the paper items reread the poem in pairs.
- **5 min. Close.** Each child points to one thing they did well. No scores shown.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Spoken blends and segments, as day 7.
- **10 min. Map and read.** The sound-spelling-map and chain: pat, pan, tan, tap. No reteaching today.
- **10 min. Letters.** G and g form practice; no scoring of the new letter.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Reread the cycle 2 reader. Scheduled familiar practice.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Sort the paper items by error letter for day 9.

Who holds what is carried: children hold nothing; no model is shown. Tool: None. By hand: marking symbols, drawing, labeling. Evidence: the day 8 items (RI.1.1, SL.1.2, RI.1.6, RI.1.7).

## Day 9


- **15 min. Reread the article.** Reread Tools in the Kitchen; pairs find the sentence that answers "What keeps the oil off the table?" before the teacher responds.
- **10 min. Revise an answer.** Each child revises one answer from day 8 with the sentence pointed to, in a pair, using Read, Point, Talk, Draw.
- **20 min. Return and repair.** Four groups by letter, the four-minute move for each at the teacher table with a fresh card: A (point to the sentence first), B (cover the picture), C (add a word fact to a picture fact), AI (cover the picture, source of a pictured fact). The rest work in pairs from the written move cards. One group at a time: four groups, four minutes each, four minutes to change groups.
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs say an explanation using one word fact and one picture fact for any article tool.
- **5 min. Exit.** E9.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment, repair the day 7 gap (I, dropped consonant) if many.
- **10 min. Chain and spell.** Spell cat, hat, that from memory.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** H and h; dictation "that hat".
- **30 min. Rotation.** Retell the cycle 2 reader (who, what happens) and map the mapped words once more.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: partners hold the models. Tool: None. By hand: pointing, covering, drawing. Evidence: E9; the repair record.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 5, "Which poem words name the hammer action?"; day 6, "Which part?"; day 9, "Which source gives that detail?"
- Written task, day 7: a drawing of one tool, the topic and two facts as three strips, and one word label; the facts are dictated to the teacher.
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) the topic names the tool; (2) each fact comes from the text; (3) a closing sentence is said.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Days 6 to 8: one repair of at most 5 minutes from the table in field 4. Day 9 holds the long repair (named part above).
- **Retrieved.** Day 6: question move (day 2). Day 7: sort and define (days 3 and 4). Day 9: the two sources (day 3).
- **Fluency target.** Expression on the second and third reading of a poem line by echo; no timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: tap, bang, hammer, nail, soft, loud, ladle, clip, hose. Frames: "A ___ helps because ___." "I add ___." "Which part?" Preview pictures; rehearse in a home language first.
- **Access.** Strips in large print; echo reading by pointing at the picture for a child who does not speak.
- **Check accommodations.** Card A is read aloud; a child may point, draw or speak to a scribe. A child may be heard last.
- **Reading load.** Refrains and echoed lines; the cycle 2 reader (about 60 words across short pages); mapped words only.
- **Extension, same standard.** Ask a second question about Card A.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Say a poem line together, tap the beat, then say a fact about a tool. Take turns adding a fact. Day 8's card is read aloud; ask one question and answer one.
- **One projector.** Show the poem and the strips; mark on paper.
