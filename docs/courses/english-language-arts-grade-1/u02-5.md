# Section 2.5: The sun article and a first shared notebook

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-1/2/s/2.5), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-1 u02-5.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **RI.1.3, "Describe the connection between two individuals, events, ideas, or pieces of information in a text."** Days 50 to 54: a connection between two facts in the sun article, then "because" or "so" on day 54.
- **RI.1.10 and RL.1.10, "With prompting and support, read informational texts / prose and poetry appropriately complex for grade 1."** Whole readings on 50 and 51, rereads on 52 to 55.
- **W.1.7, "Participate in shared research and writing projects", and W.1.8, "With guidance and support from adults, recall information from experiences or gather information from provided sources to answer a question."** The shared sky notebook opens on 50; a two-source answer on 53; a supported answer with a drawing on 55.
- **Foundations, days 50 to 55 (cycle 8 lessons 42 to 45, cycle 9 lessons 46 and 47):** RF.1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, b, f, g, RF.1.4a to c, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, e. Cycle 8: wh, -ck, four- and five-phoneme clusters, -ed said /t/; cycle 9: doubled finals -ll, -ss, -ff, -zz, s-blends, -ed said /d/. A cycle 8 progress sample is taken on day 53.
- **Limits that bite here.** The class observation log is a class picture log; home sky watching is optional and never graded. No clock time and no numbers beyond counting. Children never look straight at the sun, and pictures stand in; this is said on 50 and 52. "Rise and set" are taught as what we see; the source says Earth turns (source: g1-u2-01, page 2).
- **Connections.** Capitals for names and months are begun on 50 (the unit plan says to continue them); end marks and a sentence's purpose on 54 (R); joined sentences with "and, but, so" on 53 and 54.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A note says what the source says, in a few words, with its source named. (2) An observation is what the class saw; a fact is what the article says; they sit in two columns. (3) "Because" gives a reason; "so" gives a result; the article's sentences use both; children do not add a cause the source lacks.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** Research-cycle chart (ask, find, note, share) is review from kindergarten (set on 34) and becomes explicit on 50. The class picture log and the shared notebook page hold sources and notes; partners hold them from 52. Text-structure (the order of an information text) returns on 51. Sentence-combining: "and, but, so" on 53 and 54. Unit 1's sound-spelling-map and discussion-protocol stay on the wall.
- **Key moment 1, day 50: the question and the first note.** Ideal instrument: a wall page with two columns, source and note, and a picture log strip. Spec: fits; paper.
- **Key moment 2, day 54: the sentence with because or so.** Ideal instrument: two fact cards and an arrow card, with the connecting word on a third card. Spec: fits; paper.
- **A room with nothing.** The notebook is folded paper; the log is drawn pictures.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 50, "Which fact makes you wonder?"; day 52, "Which drawing is what we saw?"; day 54, "Is it a cause or an order?"
- Written task: notebook notes (50, 52); a two-source answer said aloud and dictated (53); a shared answer (55).
- Judged by the teacher: (1) the source named; (2) two details joined; (3) nothing added that the source lacks; (4) a drawing the listener needs.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Retrieved.** Research-cycle chart, the notebook, two columns, sequence cards, sentence-combining words.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: rise, set, shadow, because, so. Frames: "The sun ___, so ___." "I saw ___."
- **Access.** Large cards; tactile arrows; a scribe.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing; a spoken answer; a scribe.
- **Reading load.** Children decode only taught patterns and mapped words; the article is read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second fact for a note.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read the article one page at a time; draw an observation; say a "because" sentence.
- **One projector.** Show the article's pictures page by page; cards are paper.
