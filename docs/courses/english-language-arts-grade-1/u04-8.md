# Section 4.8: Comparing stories and pages, sharing the portrait, and the unit's close


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); cards for the other exits are new Curio Class microtexts, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; texts are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures and labels. The last five days compare two stories (153) and two information pages (154), finish the readings record (155), run the opinion transfer (156) and end the unit with a share and one response allocation (157).

The 156 sitting gets no help, no hint and no reteaching. Records use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. 157 is the only repair slot (20 minutes). Nothing new is assessed on 155 or 157. The cumulative record is collected on 158 to 159 by the next section.
- **Connections.** Reviewed: Both ___. But ___. (4.1), organizer and plan (4.5, 4.7), four-part opinion (4.4), share in six groups of five (unit 3), shared device turns (4.7), the single response allocation with a fresh retry (4.2 day 130, 4.4 day 140).

(3) How the 20 response minutes are split by missed component. (4) Content notes: an injured bird in adult care, no graphic detail, never pick up a wild bird.

## 6. Days

## Day 153


- **5 min. Opening.** Opening repair (3 minutes): the commonest 152 exit miss, one fresh card. Say the day's three jobs: a comparison of two stories, the shared device for ten more children, and practice explaining the portrait. No model for the comparison is shown first.
- **15 min. Compare two stories (observation).** Item 1, unrehearsed. Read *Ben and the Bird* chapter 5 whole (about 300 words, 3 minutes) and *Nia Calls for Help* paragraph 4 aloud (about 80 words). Each child fills a Both ___. But ___. sheet (review, 4.1 day 124) from picture cards for each story's events: one likeness, one difference with an event from each; a word label on each. The teacher hears six after the sheets are in. Content note: both stories end with the bird in a trained helper's care, shown without any hurt, and say again that a child calls a grown-up and never picks up a wild bird.
- **15 min. Digital contribution (observation).** Item D2: the next ten children, 90 seconds each at the shared device, each adding a labeled drawing or a label beside a partner's drawing, logged by name and time. The 152 children are not repeated. The others finish their portrait drawings on paper.
- **20 min. Rehearse an explanation.** Each child explains one portrait fact to a partner with the drawing and names the page it came from ("The page says ___."); two turns each, six heard. No grammar or revision conference today.
- **5 min. Exit.** E153, asked aloud.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Count the syllables of said words; clap and say the vowel sound in each part (playground, teacher, seaweed, raincoat).
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Lesson 129: vowel-team syllable (play-ground, teach-er, sea-weed, rain-coat): read, divide, spell. Held out: sailboat, which the hearing card uses.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Printing set with written feedback. Dictate seaweed and raincoat, then "A teacher is on the playground."
- **30 min. Rotation.** Observation hearing first at the teacher table: the day's five children (hearing sheet, day 4 of six, names set in advance; 3 minutes each, 15 minutes) while the rest reread, chain and spell in pairs. Then three guided groups of 5 minutes (the catch-up six first, then two rotating groups of six, 50 seconds a child; Reading Together, the adapted cycle 25 reader). The heard-six column names them; a group not met today goes first tomorrow.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.
- **Carried and evidence.** Tools: the shared drawing device, 15 minutes, ten children. Evidence: item 1 (RL.1.9), D2 (W.1.6), items RF for the day's five (153), E153. Item D2 is scored on 154 from the log, not during production.
