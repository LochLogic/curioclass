## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits and end items read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); every other card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** Nothing here is taught as new in the topic block. Day 87 practices telling what the picture shows from what the words say (RI.1.6, "Distinguish between information provided by pictures ... and information provided by the words"; RI.1.7, "Use the illustrations and details in a text to describe its key ideas") in an annotated drawing that is then revised. Day 88 observes talk (SL.1.1, SL.1.1a to c, SL.1.3) on the window-watching story and links a story to the articles without treating the story as evidence. Day 89 responds to the mid-unit check (RI.1.2, RI.1.5) and begins cycle 14 (open syllables; RF.1.3c is first met here with RF.1.3d, e).
- **Limits that bite.** Day 88's 30 observed minutes take the place of the question and discussion slots; no repair happens that day, so the 87 exit miss goes to 89. Mina's story is an observation, not research evidence. A window or picture route stands in for any outdoor watching; no touching nests, eggs or birds.
- **Connections.** The story-map (who, where and when, what happens), the observation and article fact two-column sheet, source labels on notes, the discussion-protocol and the observed discussion of six groups of five, all reviewed from units 1 and 2.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A revision changes one detail because the words say so; it is not a redraw. (2) How the observed groups are timed (five children, five minutes, one minute a child) and recorded. (3) What each repair group does on 89 and which letters it takes (A, B, C topic and evidence; D features). (4) Content note: Mina worries a bird is sick and a hawk glides over; no bird is hurt, nothing is graphic.

## 6. Days

## Day 87


- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E85 (the 86 check came between), one component, a fresh menu.
- **8 min. Reread Feet That Help, page 2.** The teacher reads; children point to the picture facts and the words facts. Review of picture-only and words-only facts.
- **10 min. Three questions.** Spoken: "Which words say what the foot does?" "What does the picture show that the words do not?" "How do picture and words together describe the key idea?" Pairs take two turns.
- **22 min. Annotated drawing, then revise.** Each child draws one bird foot from the article (sparrow, duck or hawk), labels two parts by copying, and adds one source note with the label Feet That Help (twelve minutes). Partners compare the drawing with the words and each child changes one detail, pointing to the sentence (ten minutes).
- **10 min. Talk.** Partners say: "I changed ___ because the words say ___." Six pairs heard.
- **5 min. Exit.** E87.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: a long and a short vowel in a pair (pet, Pete); blend /s/ /k/ /i/ /n/; first, vowel and last sound of grin; segment drift. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 13 lesson 69: build, take apart and write compound and closed two-syllable words. Chain: pigpen, hotdog, catfish, bandstand; clap, chop, write each part.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Decode, then spell, two words; dictation: "A catfish is in the pond." Print the pairs.
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reader and spelling; the adapted reader Pam's Backpack), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds a drawing; the teacher holds the article pages. Tool: None.. By hand: the drawing and its revision. Evidence: E87.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: notice, observe, change, revise, window. Frames: "I changed ___ because the words say ___." "You said ___. What ___?"
- **Access.** A window or picture route for fears or allergies; enlarged drawing sheets; content dictation; partner pointing.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing or a spoken answer; stems read twice; a scribe; a small group instead of the observed group if a child prefers, with the same stems.
- **Reading load.** The story and the cards are read aloud; children read word labels and the cycle sentences.
- **Extension, same standard.** Revise a second detail and point to its sentence.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Draw one bird foot, check one detail against the words, and fix it. Read the story aloud and talk it over with the parent taking Mina's side. On 89 the parent reads the 86 gap from the note and retries it with a fresh card.
- **One projector.** Show the drawing sheets and the story pictures; the observed conversation is a small group with the teacher, one group at a time.
