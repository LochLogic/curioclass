## 6. Days

## Day 47


- **10 min. Read and set up.** Teacher reads Mira Waits for the Stars (cold passage T, invented for practice) twice; the three pictures are shown.
- **45 min. Check: retell the tale.** Six five-child groups, 5 minutes each, at the teacher table (item in field 3); the rest draw for 15 minutes and the rest reread quietly. No reteaching.
- **5 min. Collect and compare.** Collect the drawn strips; compare choices.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Oral sounds as usual.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Decode then spell taught words; cycle 7 lesson 39.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Letter forms in dictation.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Five six-child groups, 6 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level); the other children reread, chain and spell at stations. Today's heard six go first.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column and cycle record.

Who holds what is carried: nothing is held for the check; the pictures are given. Tool: None. By hand: three-picture ordering, drawing. Evidence: field 3 (RL.1.2).
