# Section 1.1: Listening to the tool articles

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-1/1/s/1.1), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-1 u01-1.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Grade 1 children read very little. The teacher speaks every stem with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking on every record is shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Key-detail questions, RL.1.1, RI.1.1, SL.1.2: "Ask and answer".** Days 2 and 4, on the three tool articles (g1-u1-02). A child asks one question the words can answer and answers one by pointing to the sentence. days.csv puts RL.1.1 on these informational days; the same move meets the story from day 11 (section 1.3).
- **RI.1.6: "Distinguish between information provided by pictures or other illustrations and information provided by the words".** Days 2 and 3. Day 2 matches a pictured detail to its sentence; day 3 sorts picture-only facts from words-only facts.
- **RI.1.7: "Use the illustrations and details in a text to describe its key ideas".** Days 2 and 3, as a spoken description that uses one pictured detail and one word detail. Words and picture used together are practiced from day 8.
- **Foundations, days 2 to 4 (cycle 1, review of kindergarten): RF.1.2, 1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, 1.3b, 1.3g, RF.1.1, 1.1a, RF.1.4, 1.4a to c, RL.1.10, RI.1.10, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, L.1.2e.** Oral work: "Distinguish long from short vowel sounds", "Orally produce single-syllable words by blending", "Isolate and pronounce" and "Segment" sounds. Print work: map letters to sounds, decode regular one-syllable words, read mapped words, print one letter pair a day, spell by sound. No new pattern is taught; digraphs, short vowels and mapped words come back as review of kindergarten.
- **L.1.5, 1.5a, 1.5b, 1.5c, L.1.6 (days 3 and 4).** Day 3: "Sort words into categories". Day 4: "Define words by category and by one or more key attributes" and "Identify real-life connections between words and their use". "Because" is spoken in the frame "It helps because ..." on both days. L.1.5d is on day 5 (section 1.2), not here: days.csv also lists it on days 3 and 4, but the manner and intensity words are taught once, on day 5 (Plan points).
- **Limits that bite here.** Evidence is the article's words or its picture, never "what I know". Every answer is spoken, pointed, sorted or drawn. Teacher shows all real tools; no blade is handled. Talk is about the workers in the texts, never a child's family or a parent's job, and no child names what they own at home. A child may pass. Oral long vowels never authorize a printed long-vowel spelling.
- **Connections.** SL.1.1: listening turns from day 1 (taught day 10). W.1.2: not yet, day 7. Science 1-LS1-1 is unit 3, not here.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** A key detail is a fact the text states that answers who, what, where or why. A fact may be only in the words, only in the picture, or in both: the day 3 sort sets the first two apart. Why oral sounds come first, and why a long vowel is heard but not yet read. That day 1's screen is a placement observation, not a test. That "category" means a group of things that go together.

## 2. Where it sits


Section 1.1 opens the year; nothing comes before it. It uses the day 1 retrieval screen (unit field 4: the kindergarten rows are retrieval, not a readiness check). Gaps found there are repaired in the named repair slot of days 2 to 5 (below). Its results go on the record sheet and set the six rotation groups, refreshed on day 5 (section 1.2).

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned:** day 8 RI.1.6 and RI.1.7, and day 32 RI.1.6 and RI.1.7 (rows above; items on the unit plan, field 5). The day 8 items sample days 2 and 3.

**Exit questions.** One spoken stem each, one ask. Every child marks or points on a picture card; the teacher hears reasons from the day's rotating six (20 seconds each, two minutes). The record names who was heard; a child not heard is "not yet seen", never "missed". Reading exits reread an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); other cards print "invented for practice". If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E1** (day 1). s01-X1v2 rereads Builders at Work, paragraph 3 (the level) aloud; items.csv holds the stem, answer and wrongs.
- **E2** (day 2). s01-X2v2 rereads Builders at Work, paragraph 4 (the wheelbarrow) aloud; items.csv holds the stem, answer and wrongs.
- **E3** (day 3). s01-X3v2 rereads Tools on the Farm, paragraph 3 (the watering can) aloud; items.csv holds the stem, answer and wrongs.
- **E4** (day 4). Card: "A whisk is a kitchen tool. Its wires mix the eggs." Pictures: a whisk; a spoon. Stem: "What is a whisk? Use the card." Answer: "A kitchen tool with wires for mixing eggs" (category and use, any order). Wrong: "A thing" or "a whisk" with no category or use (V).

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


- **Carried.** The compelling question and the discussion-protocol (turn card, two turns each, pass allowed: Read, Point, Talk, Draw, a routine of kindergarten, as the ledger words it) start on day 1; the teacher holds them on days 1 to 4. The sound-spelling-map (letter, keyword picture, sound) begins on day 2 with mapped letters only and grows as sounds are mapped. Main-idea-details begins on day 2 as a topic card with three detail cards (a model of kindergarten, review). Nothing is withdrawn in this section.
- **Key moment 1, day 3: the two-source sort.** Ideal instrument: the article's picture on a separate card, so a child can cover it and uncover it, beside the printed article. Spec: fits; paper cards, no tool. Gaps and asks: none (TOOL-ASKS.md has nothing for this section).
- **Key moment 2, day 2 to 4: the map.** Ideal instrument: a wall map and a card copy for each pair. Spec: fits; paper.
- **A room with nothing.** Fold a sheet in two for the picture cover. Hoops are two drawn circles on scrap paper. The poem and reader are copied on scrap paper by the teacher. A real brush, ladle and spoon from the room stand in for the exit pictures.

## 6. Days

## Day 1


- **12 min. Hook: read and hide.** Read Builders at Work whole (g1-u1-02, article 1, 141 words) with its picture hidden under a fold. Partners whisper the job the words explain (building walls). Reveal the picture and say what only it shows (hard hats, the crane).
- **8 min. The big question.** Post "How do tools help people do their work?" Children point to a pictured tool and say "A ___ helps because ___" (teacher frame; any answer accepted). Teacher charts three answers as pictures with a word each.
- **10 min. Reread, point, talk.** Reread the hammer paragraph. Point to the words that say what the claw does. This is Read, Point, Talk, Draw (kindergarten routine, review). The teacher holds it.
- **10 min. Partner exchange.** Turn card, two turns each, pass allowed (kindergarten routine). Fifteen pairs talk at once; the teacher listens to the rotating six, in three pairs, about three minutes a pair.
- **15 min. Draw.** Each child draws one tool the builder uses and dictates a word label or says it to a partner. No child draws a parent's tool.
- **5 min. Exit.** E1.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Retrieve rhyme, clapped syllables, first sound and rest, and blending m-a-t (K rows).
- **10 min. The cycle 1 poem, spoken.** The teacher says the poem (adapted from the Skills Block cycle 1 poem; still to make), claps beats, children join and find two rhyming pairs. No print yet.
- **10 min. Letters and sounds.** Retrieve K letters and sounds on cards, whole class, then point to the first word of a short line.
- **10 min. Blend and read.** Whole class reads mat, sat, map, tap on cards, sound by sound and then whole.
- **15 min. Station procedures.** Teach the four stations and the move signal; two practice rounds with the six groups of five, one group at a time at the teacher table for two minutes.
- **10 min. Placement screen.** Six groups of five, one and a half minutes a group and one to set the cards: "A cup holds pens. The cup sits on a mat." (invented for practice). Sample letters, sounds, mat and the. Unscored; the record sheet shows gaps. The teacher repairs on days 2 to 5.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds all models. Tool: None. By hand: speaking, pointing, drawing. Evidence: E1 and the placement record (observation only).

## Day 2


- **15 min. Read the article again, with the picture.** Reread Builders at Work whole, picture shown. Model one question the words can answer ("What does the claw do?") and answer it by pointing to the sentence. Start main-idea-details: a topic card, "Builders use tools", and three detail cards.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** Pairs reread the level paragraph by the teacher's voice, each asks one question the words answer and the partner points to the sentence (question and statement, kindergarten term, review). Hear the rotating six.
- **10 min. Picture beside sentence.** Show the picture with the hammer sentence. Which picture detail belongs beside this sentence (the kneeling builder)? Children point; the teacher names what only the picture shows (hats, crane).
- **20 min. Detail cards.** In pairs, draw one detail card each for hammer, level and wheelbarrow, with a word label from the article read aloud. Place the cards under the topic card.
- **5 min. Exit.** E2.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Count syllables in ham-mer and wheel-bar-row; split first sound and rest; segment three-sound words.
- **10 min. Message in print.** The cycle 1 poem in print: point to the first word, spaces, a capital, a line. The sound-spelling-map goes up with mapped s, a, m, t.
- **10 min. Retrieval repair, letters, dictation.** The repair slot (one repair, at most 10 minutes) for a day 1 sound or print gap; if none, print A and a and take dictation "at".
- **30 min. Rotation.** Reader "Sam sat. Sam sat at a mat." (Curio Class, 2026, used after s, m, a, t and the word "a" are mapped). The teacher table hears six groups of five, five minutes each; the others work at four stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Mark the record sheet; name the rotating six for tomorrow.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher. Tool: None. By hand: asking, pointing, drawing, printing. Evidence: E2.

## Day 3


- **15 min. Read the farm article.** Read Tools on the Farm whole (129 words), picture hidden, then shown. Inquiry: which farm fact comes only from the words (what the hoe does to weeds)? Reread that sentence before answering.
- **10 min. Reread and sort the two sources.** Two hoops. Children place picture-only cards (straw hat, red tractor, dog, green rows) and words-only cards (long handle lets the farmer stand tall, the rose breaks water into drops). Cover the picture to test. Teacher holds the model.
- **10 min. Talk.** "How do the picture and the words together explain the work?" Turn card; partners add one fact each.
- **20 min. Sort tool words.** Sort word-and-picture cards of builder and farm tools into two categories ("a group of things that go together"). Name a category and one attribute ("a tool with a long handle"). Say "It helps because ...".
- **5 min. Exit.** E3.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Hear and say cap and cape, pin and pine; long and short vowel, spoken only.
- **10 min. Sounds to letters.** Match sounds to print in the cycle 1 poem; review sh, th and ch (kindergarten) on the map.
- **10 min. Retrieval repair, letters, dictation.** Repair slot as day 2 (at most 10 minutes). Else print B and b (stick then bump) and take dictation "Sam sat."
- **30 min. Rotation.** Matched reading of the K reader and a second reading in new groups; same stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the sort and the map. Tool: None. By hand: sorting, printing. Evidence: E3; the sort is observed (B).

## Day 4


- **15 min. Read the kitchen article.** Read Tools in the Kitchen whole (158 words). Name the pattern "A ladle is a kitchen tool for soup." Which category does the ladle belong to? Teacher models a question and its answer.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** Partners ask one question about a kitchen tool ("What does the funnel do?") and answer by pointing to its sentence. Hear the rotating six.
- **10 min. Sort and talk.** Sort six kitchen cards (ladle, rolling pin, funnel, dough, soup, oil) into tools and not tools, saying one use for each tool. Then each pair names one safe tool in our room (a stapler, a glue stick). No home tools. Hear a rotating six.
- **20 min. Define a tool.** Each child draws a tool from the three articles and fills "A ___ is a ___ tool for ___", saying it to a partner and then the teacher; one word label written.
- **5 min. Exit.** E4.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Segment and count the sounds in lap, ship, mat (spoken); say the first and last sounds.
- **10 min. Shared writing.** The class composes one sentence about a tool; children print the first letter they hear in one word (L.1.2e); the teacher finishes the line.
- **10 min. Retrieval repair, letters, dictation.** Repair slot as day 2. Else print C and c and take dictation "a cat".
- **30 min. Rotation.**
- **5 min. Organize and record.**

Who holds what is carried: the teacher models; partners hold their own question. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, labeling. Evidence: E4.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 2, "What does the claw do?" and "What question can you ask?"; day 3, "Which fact comes only from the words?"; day 4, "Which category does the ladle belong to?"
- Written task, day 4: the filled frame "A ___ is a ___ tool for ___" with one word label by the child (the rest dictated and written by the teacher).
- Judged by the teacher with three look-fors: (1) names a category; (2) gives a use taken from the text; (3) a word label matches the drawing.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Each day from 2 reopens the day's repair, at most 5 minutes (error A, B, V as the table in field 4 says); the longer routing from day 1's placement takes the foundations repair slot.
- **Retrieved.** Day 2: kindergarten question and statement. Day 3: kindergarten sorting by one attribute (hoops). Day 4: the day 2 question move. Cycle 1 foundations return daily by design.
- **Fluency target.** Echo reading of the poem's lines; no rate target, no child-visible timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: builder, hammer, level, farmer, hoe, cook, ladle, tool. Frames: "A ___ helps because ___." "The words say ___; the picture shows ___." Preview tool names with pictures and gestures; a child may rehearse in a home language first.
- **Access.** Large print and tactile outlines of the pictures; the picture fold is a physical card. Pointing, a communication aid or a drawing is an answer.
- **Check accommodations.** Each exit is read aloud; the child may point, mark one card or answer to a scribe, who does not supply ideas.
- **Reading load.** No child reads alone beyond "Sam sat. Sam sat at a mat." and mapped words. The teacher reads every article.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second key detail, or a second picture-only fact, in the same article.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read one article together, picture covered. Ask one question the words answer; then uncover and ask what only the picture shows. Day 4: say "A ___ is a ___ tool for ___" about a spoon or a cup. Practice sounds by clapping syllables and saying the sounds of one word.
- **One projector.** Show the article page, then the picture beside it. Everyone marks printed cards; the screen never replaces the child's mark.
