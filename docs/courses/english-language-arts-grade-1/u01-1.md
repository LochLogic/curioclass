# Section 1.1: Listening to the tool articles


## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 1, Tools and work. *How do tools help people do their work?* (days 1 to 33): see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 1 to 4.** Days by kind: understanding 4 (1, 2, 3, 4). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 1 of 7 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Grade 1 children read very little. The teacher speaks every stem with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking on every record is shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- **Limits that bite here.** Evidence is the article's words or its picture, never "what I know". Every answer is spoken, pointed, sorted or drawn. Teacher shows all real tools; no blade is handled. Talk is about the workers in the texts, never a child's family or a parent's job, and no child names what they own at home. A child may pass. Oral long vowels never authorize a printed long-vowel spelling.

A fact may be only in the words, only in the picture, or in both: the day 3 sort sets the first two apart. Why oral sounds come first, and why a long vowel is heard but not yet read. That day 1's screen is a placement observation, not a test. That "category" means a group of things that go together.

**Unit assessment items owned:** day 8 RI.1.6 and RI.1.7, and day 32 RI.1.6 and RI.1.7 (rows above; items on the unit plan, field 5). The day 8 items sample days 2 and 3.

**Exit questions.** One spoken stem each, one ask. Every child marks or points on a picture card; the teacher hears reasons from the day's rotating six (20 seconds each, two minutes). The record names who was heard; a child not heard is "not yet seen", never "missed". Fictional cards print "invented for practice". If many miss one, the next day opens with that error's wrong example.
- **E1** (day 1). Card, read aloud: "A brush moves dust into a pan. The floor becomes clean." Pictures: a brush beside a dustpan; a pencil beside a book. Stem: "Which picture shows the work in these words?" Answer: brush and pan. Wrong: pencil and book, a job the words do not give (A).
- **E2** (day 2). Card: "A builder uses a ladder. Its rungs let her climb up to the roof." Pictures: a ladder against a roof; a ladder lying on grass. Stem: "Ask me one question that these words can answer." Answer: any question the card answers, such as "What do the rungs let her do?" or "Where does she climb?" The teacher answers by pointing to the sentence. Wrong: "What color is the ladder?" or "What does the hammer do?", a detail the card does not give (A).
- **E3** (day 3). Card: "A farmer uses a bucket to carry water." Picture: a yellow bucket beside a pair of boots. Stem: "Tell me one thing the picture shows that the words do not say." Answer: yellow, or the boots (any picture-only fact). Wrong: "It carries water", a words fact given to the picture (B); "There is a hose", not in the picture (A).
- **E4** (day 4). Card: "A whisk is a kitchen tool. Its wires mix the eggs." Pictures: a whisk; a spoon. Stem: "What is a whisk? Use the card." Answer: "A kitchen tool with wires for mixing eggs" (category and use, any order). Wrong: "A thing" or "a whisk" with no category or use (V).

- **12 min. Hook: read and hide.** Read Builders at Work whole (g1-u1-02, article 1, 141 words) with its picture hidden under a fold. Partners whisper the job the words explain (building walls). Reveal the picture and say what only it shows (hard hats, the crane).

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 2, "What does the claw do?" and "What question can you ask?"; day 3, "Which fact comes only from the words?"; day 4, "Which category does the ladle belong to?"

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** Each day from 2 reopens the day's repair, at most 5 minutes (error A, B, V as the table in field 4 says); the longer routing from day 1's placement takes the foundations repair slot.
- **Retrieved.** Day 2: kindergarten question and statement. Day 3: kindergarten sorting by one attribute (hoops). Day 4: the day 2 question move. Cycle 1 foundations return daily by design.
- **Fluency target.** Echo reading of the poem's lines; no rate target, no child-visible timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: builder, hammer, level, farmer, hoe, cook, ladle, tool. Frames: "A ___ helps because ___." "The words say ___; the picture shows ___." Preview tool names with pictures and gestures; a child may rehearse in a home language first.
- **Access.** Large print and tactile outlines of the pictures; the picture fold is a physical card. Pointing, a communication aid or a drawing is an answer.
- **Check accommodations.** Each exit is read aloud; the child may point, mark one card or answer to a scribe, who does not supply ideas.
- **Reading load.** No child reads alone beyond "Sam sat. Sam sat at a mat." and mapped words. The teacher reads every article.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second key detail, or a second picture-only fact, in the same article.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read one article together, picture covered. Ask one question the words answer; then uncover and ask what only the picture shows. Day 4: say "A ___ is a ___ tool for ___" about a spoon or a cup. Practise sounds by clapping syllables and saying the sounds of one word.
- **One projector.** Show the article page, then the picture beside it. Everyone marks printed cards; the screen never replaces the child's mark.
