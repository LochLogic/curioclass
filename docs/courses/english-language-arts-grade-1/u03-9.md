# Section 3.9: Gathering, organizing and verifying expert-bird facts, and the observation check


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md), except day 119, which says its own split. Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen (a unit plan "meets" is shown; "misses" is not yet seen). Reading exits and end items read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); every other card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label; each bird fact on a card is checked in sources.csv (g1-u3-f28 onward).

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** Days 112 to 114 gather, organize and verify the facts for the expert-bird riddle (W.1.7, "Participate in shared research and writing projects"; W.1.8, "gather information from provided sources to answer a question"; RI.1.5, "locate key facts or information in a text"). Day 115 is an observation check: a retelling of a cold passage, Owls (RI.1.2, "Identify the main topic and retell key details of a text") and a six-feature trace (RI.1.5). Foundations: cycle 18 on 112 and 113 (RF.1.3f, "Read words with inflectional endings"), then consolidation on 114 and 115 with no new pattern. Letter forms: the last two pairs on 112 and revision by 114 (L.1.1a).
- **Limits that bite here.** Facts come from the provided pages. Notes, labels and clues are printed or scribed words and drawings. Day 115 gives no help, no hint and no reteaching; its misses go to 119. The riddle is drafted on 116, not here.
- **Connections.** The expert-bird choice and route card (110, 111); the annotated drawing (87 to 89); the source label (86); verifying in a second source (99).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A clue is a verified fact with the bird's name held back. (2) The 114 catch-up slot repairs L, M, O and N for the foundations record scored on 118. (3) Day 115 reads the cold passage Owls, page 2, Quiet Feathers, and pages 1 to 3 with its own printed menu, unseen until the day (s03-A27v2, s03-A28v2, s03-A29v2; items.csv holds the stem, answer and wrongs). (4) The unit plan's exit cases for 112 to 114 reuse taught birds (heron, duck), so new cards are used (see the plan points).

## 6. Days

## Day 112



- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E111 (D or B), one component, a fresh page.
- **8 min. Reread.** The teacher rereads one page of the child's chosen bird (the pair the whole class shares), 120 words at most; children point to a fact worth a drawing.
- **12 min. Drawing from one source.** Each child draws the fact from page one, with a word label and the source label (annotated drawing, review of u03-3 day 87).
- **20 min. A second source.** Each child finds a fact on the second page and adds it to the drawing, with its source label beside it; the teacher hears six.
- **10 min. Talk.** Turn card: "What does the second source add to the drawing's explanation?" Six pairs heard.
- **5 min. Exit.** E112.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: a base and its ending (hate, hated; use, used); blend /m/ /a/ /k/ /t/; first, vowel and last sound of hated; segment baked. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 18 lesson 94: the three sounds of -ed (baked /t/, used /d/, hated adds /id/) on silent-e bases, the dropped e kept. Decode, then spell. The adapted cycle 18 reader is reread for expression.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Letter forms: print the last two pairs, Y y and Z z, dated and kept for 118. Dictation: "Dave hated the smoke." (hated mapped; the mapped).
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reader and spelling; the adapted cycle 18 reader), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the drawing with two labelled facts; the teacher holds the heard-six list. Tool: None. By hand: the drawing, the labels and the letter-form pairs. Evidence: E112.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the sorted notes and two clue drafts; the teacher holds the sample record. Tool: None. By hand: the sorted notes, two clues and the letter-form revisions. Evidence: E113; the cycle 18 sample (field 3).

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned questions.** Day 112: "What does the second source add to the drawing's explanation?" Day 113: "Which clue does not help the listener tell your bird?" Day 114: "Which route was fastest, and why?" Each is a turn-card exchange with linked turns; a child may pass.
- **Written task, days 112 to 114.** A source-labelled drawing (112), two ordered clue drafts (113) and verified clues with route marks (114).
- **Look-fors.** Each clue is a fact a page supports, with its source label and route; the bird's name is held back; the shape comes before the use.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: verify, route, clue, shape, use, topic card. Frames: "This clue is true because page ___ says ___." "I found it by ___."
- **Access.** Enlarged cards; a scribe for content; a pointer arrow; partner reading aloud.
- **Check accommodations (115).** The card read twice; a spoken or pointed answer; a scribe for content, never for facts; the trace may be drawn; no time pressure.
- **Reading load.** The pages are read aloud; children read their own labels and notes and the foundations words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Verify a third clue from a second page.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Gather two facts, sort them, verify each by a route; on 115 read Owls, page 2, Quiet Feathers, once, ask the two stems and trace six routes, with no help.
- **One projector.** Show a page and its contents; children number the route on paper. Printed screens stand in for the menu on 115.
