# Section 4.2: The long story's second half, the whole-story map and the first check


## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 4, Caring for birds. *How can people help birds that need it?* (days 120 to 157): see [s04.md](s04.md). **Days 125 to 130.** Days by kind: understanding 2 (126, 128), fluency 1 (127), application 1 (125), check 1 (129), respond 1 (130). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 2 of 8 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); cards for the other exits are new Curio Class microtexts, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; the stories are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures.
