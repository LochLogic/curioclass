## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); cards for the other exits are new Curio Class microtexts, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; the stories are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures.

- **Limits that bite.** Tense is taught on 126 although the unit plan spreads it over 125 to 127, because 125 is an application day and 127 a fluency day where nothing is taught as new. Day 127 drafts no writing. Day 129 is a sitting with no help; day 130 is the only repair slot, and it takes the 129 results, the 126 and 128 exit misses and the printing results together. The opening pair is checked on 129 only; the ending pair stays reserved for 153.
- **Connections.** The story-maps and the Both ___. But ___. chart of 4.1, the sentence clue, the heard-six column, Return and repair, the cycle progress sample (review of unit 2, day 58), the unit 3 poem Feather Coat reread on 127.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) How the 129 sitting is run and scored: components 0, 1 or 2, no help, the key accepts dad or parent. (4) What the 130 groups are, by missed component.

## 6. Days

## Day 125


- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E124, one component, a fresh invented card.
- **15 min. Reread two phone scenes.** Reread Ben chapter 2 page 4 (Dad phones) and Nia paragraph 3 (Coach Lee phones). Children point to who phones, what the adult says and what the child does.
- **15 min. Talk.** Partners, two turns each, six heard: "Who phones the helper in each story?" "What does each child do while the adult phones?" (Ben holds Dad's sleeve and listens; Nia stands very still and quiet). "What is the same and what is different?" Starter: Both ___. But ___.
- **8 min. Practice nouns, agreement and pronouns.** Six sentence cards from the two pages (Dad phones; Dad and Ben listen; She stands still): sort the nouns, say the matching verb for one and two, and name each pronoun's noun.
- **12 min. Compare on paper.** Each child draws two small event pictures (Ben listening, Nia standing still) and says Both ___. But ___. to a partner; the teacher scribes six. The comparison is of what each child does, not how the characters look (A).
- **5 min. Exit.** E125.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** A long and a short vowel pair (fin, fine); blend /h/ /er/; first, middle and last sound of herd; segment turn.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 20 lesson 101: map er (fern), ir (bird) and ur (fur): three spellings, one sound, on the sound-spelling-map. Chain: her, herd, hurt, turn, burn, burst. Clap, chop, write each.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Revise the four forms the written feedback on the 120 to 124 sets marked (the last revision day before the 129 scoring; nothing new is taught). Dictation: "Her fur is soft."
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (the use of a previous-cycle reader until er, ir and ur are mapped (the adapted cycle 20 reader Sam's Hammer from 126)), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the two event pictures; the teacher holds the two story pages and the chart. Tool: None. By hand: the comparison picture. Evidence: E125.

## Day 126


- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E125, one component (A: a likeness of looks, not of experience).
- **15 min. Read chapter 4.** Read pages 10 and 11 whole (about 270 words, two minutes with the pictures). Reread Ms. Torres' words: her job, what she checks, and why the call mattered. Words: child, adult, safe, protect, distance, rehabilitator (a bird helper who cares for hurt wild birds; source to check).
- **15 min. Talk.** "Who can safely help the robin?" (the trained helper). "What does job mean here? Which words tell you?" (RI.1.4: care for hurt wild birds). "How is Ms. Torres' part different from Ben's?" (she cares for the bird; he calls and stays back). Partners two turns, six heard.

- **5 min. Exit.** E126.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Blend /s/ /t/ /er/; segment first; isolate the first sound of skirt; a long and a short vowel pair (mat, mate).
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 20 lesson 102: connect the spellings to words and the matched reader; map one irregular word (named at build). Chain: stir, sir, first, girl, bird, shirt, skirt.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Copy one sentence from the day's reading, each form checked against its model. Decode, then spell, two words; dictation: "The bird is on the third fern."
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (the adapted reader Sam's Hammer, first use, conditional on the map), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the helper card; the teacher holds chapter 4, the tense strip and the ending cards. Tool: None. By hand: the helper card. Evidence: E126.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: trained, helper, wait, careful, ending. Frames: "First ___. Then ___. At the end ___." "Both ___. But ___." Pictures and home-language talk preview the words.

- **Extension, same standard.** Compare two helpers in the long story (Dad and Ms. Torres) with the frame.
