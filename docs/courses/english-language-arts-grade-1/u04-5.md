## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); cards for the other exits are new Curio Class microtexts, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; texts are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures and labels. The portrait is a drawing and a short informative paragraph about bird-safe windows, built from two pages and made on paper windows; this section launches it (141), teaches point and reason (142) and gathers the facts and a second method (143, 144).

## 6. Days

## Day 141


- **5 min. Opening.** Show the task card, a drawn plan like the kindergarten plans: a bird, a window, two facts and a closing. Say that this is the class's window bird portrait, due on 152, and that it is made from paper windows; nobody needs a window or a yard at home.
- **15 min. Read.** Read *Windows Birds Can See* (g1-u4-03), pages 1 to 3, whole and aloud (about 190 words, under 2 minutes with the pictures). Then reread page 1 with the picture and have the class point to the glass, the reflection and the bird on the labeled drawing; then page 3 with its marks picture.
- **15 min. Talk.** Three single-ask stems, one at a time: "What does the bird see in the glass?" "What can people put on a window?" "Why does that help the bird?" Partners take two turns each with the turn card; the teacher hears six (heard-six column).
- **20 min. Writing and language.** Three fact strips for the page (reflection fools the bird; many birds hit glass; marks show the glass) are ordered under the topic card "bird-safe windows". Teacher thinks aloud choosing two as the facts and saying a closing: "Make glass visible." Each child draws a plan (topic box, two fact boxes, closing box), writes one word label (glass or marks) and says one fact to a partner.
- **5 min. Exit.** E141, asked aloud.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Long and short vowel pairs (cub, cube; pin, pine). Blend /m/ /oo/ /n/ aloud. Isolate the middle sound of tree. Segment food.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 23, lesson 117: add oo (moon, zoo, food) and ee (see, tree, bee) to the sound-spelling-map; name the pattern, then chain moon, soon, spoon and bee, see, deep, keep. Held out of today's chains: feet and seed, which the 150 hearing card uses.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Dated printing set (ten or twelve forms) with the teacher's written feedback. Dictate moon, tree and the sentence "See the moon." (see is a new ee word, the mapped.)
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (A Little Seed, the adapted cycle 23 reader, only once its patterns and irregular words are mapped; until then the cycle 22 decodable), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.
- **Carried and evidence.** Tools: None (paper windows, strips, plan sheets). The task card, fact strips and each plan go in the portrait folder, kept to 152. Evidence: E141 only; no unit assessment item today. The exit case is a page about the sandbox: the topic and detail practice today uses the window facts, never a sandbox.

## Day 142


- **5 min. Opening.** Opening repair (3 minutes): the commonest 141 exit miss gets one fresh card (not the exit card).
- **15 min. Read.** Reread *Windows Birds Can See* pages 1 to 3 aloud. Teach the words point (what the writer wants) and reason (a fact that tells why), reviewing the point-and-reason strips from 4.3 (days 132 to 134). Point to page 3: "Making windows visible is a way to protect birds."
- **15 min. Talk.** "What does the writer want?" "Which fact tells why?" Partners two turns, six heard. Then the teacher holds up two practice cards, labeled invented for practice: "Some windows have curtains" and "A bird hits glass because it sees trees"; the class points to which is a reason for the point.
- **20 min. Writing and language.** Sort the strips under the point card: the two reasons from the page (reflection fools the bird; marks show the glass) and one practice strip set aside. Meaning questions: the teacher highlights reflection on a word card and models "What does reflection mean?"; each child chooses a highlighted word card (glass, visible, reflection), says a meaning question to a partner, and points to the page picture that answers it.
- **5 min. Exit.** E142, asked aloud.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Isolate the last sound in happy and baby. Clap two syllables in baby, funny and windy. Blend /h/ /a/ /p/ /ee/.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Lesson 118: final y says /ee/ in two-syllable words: happy, funny, windy, baby. Compare y as long i in my and fly (unit 2). Chain sun, sunny, wind, windy.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Printing set with written feedback. Dictate "The baby is happy." (baby and happy mapped by syllable.)
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (A Little Seed, the adapted cycle 23 reader), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.
- **Carried and evidence.** Tools: None. The sorted strips stay in the folder. Evidence: E142 (RI.1.8 taught here; the unit item is on 147).

## Day 143


- **5 min. Opening.** Opening repair (3 minutes): the commonest 142 exit miss, one fresh card, before the rest. Show the research organizer (question box, two note boxes, page tags) used in unit 3 (review). Question: "How can people keep windows safe for birds?"

- **15 min. Talk.** "Where do the marks go?" "How close are the marks?" One ask each, answered from the page; partners two turns, six heard. Each child says which page told them.
- **20 min. Writing and language.** Each child fills the organizer: note 1 from *Windows Birds Can See* (marks show the glass), note 2 from *Marks Outside* (marks go outside, close together), each as a small drawing plus a word label, with the page tag stuck beside it. The teacher checks every note has a tag.
- **5 min. Exit.** E143, asked aloud.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Count syllables in moon-light, bee-hive and tree-top; blend them from spoken parts; segment the first syllable of each.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Lesson 119: read and spell two-syllable words by syllables with oo, ee and final y: beehive, treetop, moonlight, bumpy. Cut each word, read the parts, join.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Printing set with written feedback. Dictate "A bee is in the treetop."
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (A Little Seed, the adapted cycle 23 reader), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

## Day 144


- **5 min. Opening.** Opening repair (3 minutes): the commonest 143 exit miss, one fresh card, before the rest. Retrieve the organizer (two notes, two tags). Say today's question: "How else do people help birds?"
- **15 min. Read.** Reread *Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes* (g1-u4-06) pages 1 and 5 and *Nests Need Space* (g1-u4-03) page 5. Read *Feeders Need Adult Care* page 1 to 3 aloud once as information only (about 330 words, 3 minutes): an adult does this job; children never clean a feeder. Do not compare it with the bluebird account today.
- **15 min. Talk.** "What do nest boxes give birds?" "What do window marks do for birds?" "Is each one a place or a safety?" Partners two turns, six heard.
- **20 min. Writing and language.** Each child writes note 3 on the organizer (nest boxes give a place to nest when holes are few; page tag) and completes a Both ___. But ___. chart (review, u04-1 day 124) for window marks and nest boxes: both protect birds; marks keep birds from hitting glass, boxes give a place to nest. Spoken, drawn, with word labels.
- **5 min. Exit.** E144, asked aloud.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Review: blend, segment and count two-syllable words with oo, ee and y.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Lesson 120, cycle progress sample: the six heard children read one reserved word (spoon), the card is removed, then write one dictated word (bunny), both held out of 141 to 143. Same-block regrouping follows from the 141 to 144 cycle record.

Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (A Little Seed, the adapted cycle 23 reader), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. ## 9. Supports and extension

- English learners: the words glass, reflection, marks, outside with picture cards; sentence frame "The marks help because ___." Others: point to the strip, say it to a partner first.
- Access: strips large; a child may point and label instead of drawing; a scribe for a word label.
- Reading load: no child reads the page; the teacher reads aloud, labels are single words.
- Extension: choose a third fact from the page and add a note; tell where it came from.

## 10. Two rooms

- A parent teaching one child: read the window page aloud, draw the window and bird together, say two facts and a closing, and tag each fact with the page. Use paper; no real glass.
- One projector: show the page pictures and the organizer; the class points on the screen.
