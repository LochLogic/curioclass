# Section 4.5: Window bird portrait, bird-safe windows and a second way to help


## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 4, Caring for birds. *How can people help birds that need it?* (days 120 to 157): see [s04.md](s04.md). **Days 141 to 144.** Days by kind: understanding 2 (141, 142), application 2 (143, 144). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 5 of 8 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); cards for the other exits are new Curio Class microtexts, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; texts are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures and labels. The portrait is a drawing and a short informative paragraph about bird-safe windows, built from two pages and made on paper windows; this section launches it (141), teaches point and reason (142) and gathers the facts and a second method (143, 144).
