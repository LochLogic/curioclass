## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md), except day 119, which says its own split. Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen (a unit plan "meets" is shown; "misses" is not yet seen). Reading exits and end items read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); every other card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label; each bird fact on a card is checked in sources.csv (g1-u3-f28 onward).

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** Day 106 shares the revised paragraph and explains how one fact connects to its source (W.1.2, "name a topic, supply some facts about the topic, and provide some sense of closure"; W.1.7, "Participate in shared research and writing projects"; W.1.8, "gather information from provided sources to answer a question"). Day 107 answers the 104 and 105 results: a fresh retry of the missed component only (RI.1.2, "retell key details of a text"; RI.1.5, "locate key facts or information in a text"). Both days carry cycle 17 foundations (RF.1.3c, "Know final -e ... conventions for representing long vowel sounds"; RF.1.3d to f) and the dated letter-form records for L.1.1a.
- **Limits that bite here.** Sharing is spoken, one fact and one pointer to its source; nothing is rescored. Day 107 gives each child one gap at most, in at most four groups; a retry never rescores 104 or 105.
- **Connections.** The revised paragraph and organizer (103); the 104 and 105 records; the cycle 17 map (104); the story words of Mina at the Window (g1-u3-02, 22 and 106).
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Sharing is for listening: a listener asks to see the source line; it is not a performance mark. (2) 107 is response time: repair first, then one fresh retry on the swallow card, then the exit. (3) The unit plan reserves a pelican card for 107; the pelican has been taught, so this plan uses a swallow card instead (see the plan points).

## 6. Days

## Day 106



- **5 min. Opening repair.** The commonest miss on E103 (B or D), one component, a fresh page.
- **8 min. Words, then a story page.** Words with pictures: tree, window, notice, remain, wonder, respond, perch, observer. The teacher rereads the first page of Mina at the Window aloud (under 120 words) and children point to a picture for each word; the words are practiced, not tested (RL.1.10).
- **7 min. Rehearse.** Each child picks one fact from the revised paragraph and its source label, and says it once to a partner.
- **25 min. Share in six groups of five.** Each child shares one fact and points to its source line (about 45 seconds each, 4 minutes a group). The teacher joins each of the six groups for about 4 minutes, which is 24 minutes in all, and writes one listening note per group; listeners say, "Where can I check that?"
- **10 min. Talk.** Turn card: "How does that fact support the paragraph's explanation?" Six pairs heard; pass allowed.
- **5 min. Exit.** E106.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: a long and a short vowel in a pair (can, cane); blend /k/ /l/ /o/ /z/; first, vowel and last sound of cute; segment those. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 17 lesson 88: u_e and e_e with two-syllable words that combine known syllable types (pancake, inside, sunrise). Clap the syllables, then map each part. Count vowel sounds, not letters (H). The cycle 17 reader is read only after its patterns are mapped, then reread for expression.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Letter forms: print four pairs, A a to D d, dated and kept for 118 (feedback written after class). Dictation: "Duke ate a pancake." (ate mapped).
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reader and spelling; the cycle 17 reader), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the revised paragraph and a share card; the teacher holds the group listening notes. Tool: None. By hand: the share card and the letter-form pairs. Evidence: E106; the listening notes (not scored).

## Day 107



- **20 min. Respond and repair.** Repair is by gap in at most four groups at the teacher table, 5 minutes each, from the 104 and 105 records: topic and evidence (A, B, C), features (D), writing and participation (E, F, G), foundations (H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O). The other groups read their own paragraph aloud to a partner. If more than a third missed one component, the teacher models it to everyone inside these 20 minutes.
- **35 min. Supported source rereading and a fresh retry.** The teacher reads the swallow card aloud once; each child retries only the missed component on that card, with the card's contents, heading and glossary routes in front. The teacher circulates and hears six; a child with no gap writes a topic sentence for the swallow card. Meeting answers must match this card.
- **5 min. Exit.** E107.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: a long and a short vowel in a pair (tub, tube); blend /f/ /l/ /u/ /t/; first, vowel and last sound of mule; segment plume. Spoken only.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 17 lesson 89: u_e and e_e, two-syllable words and the ending -s on silent-e bases (flutes, mules, tunes, rides). Decode, then spell. The cycle 17 reader is reread for meaning and expression.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Letter forms: print four pairs, E e to H h, dated and kept for 118. Dictation: "Pete has two mules." (has mapped).
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (matched reader and spelling; the cycle 17 reader), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record and the day's exit marks.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the swallow card and the missed component; the teacher holds the group list. Tool: None. By hand: the retry and the letter-form pairs. Evidence: E107; the retry is recorded as shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the 104 and 105 rows.

## 7. Talk and writing

- **Planned question.** Day 106: "How does that fact support the paragraph's explanation?" (turn card; linked turns; a child may pass). Day 107: a partner exchange about the swallow card, the K retry: restate the partner's point, then reply.
- **Written task.** None new; a child retries the one missed component on 107. The shared paragraph from 103 is the work.
- **Look-fors.** The child shares a fact that the paragraph holds, with its source line; the listener restates before replying.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **106.** Source labels (97, 99); the heading route (96); cycle 17 patterns (104, 105). **107.** The 104 and 105 components; the feature routes (96, 103, 105). **Foundations.** i_e, o_e, u_e and e_e together; endings -s on taught bases.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: share, source, check, wide, swallow, listener. Frames: "My fact is ___." "It comes from ___." "Where can I check that?"
- **Access.** Enlarged cards; a scribe for content; the teacher points while the child speaks.
- **Check accommodations.** Not a check day; the 107 retry may be spoken or pointed.
- **Reading load.** The swallow card and the story page are read aloud; children read their own label and the cycle 17 words.
- **Extension, same standard.** Share a second fact with its source line and say how it supports the same explanation.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** The child reads the paragraph aloud, says one fact and points to its source; on 107, read the swallow card and ask the two stems.
- **One projector.** Show the swallow card; children point on paper.
