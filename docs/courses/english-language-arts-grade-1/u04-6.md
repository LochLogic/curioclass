# Section 4.6: Why plants and animals need birds, and the author's reasons


## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 4, Caring for birds. *How can people help birds that need it?* (days 120 to 157): see [s04.md](s04.md). **Days 145 to 147.** Days by kind: understanding 2 (145, 146), check 1 (147). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 6 of 8 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every exit card is a new Curio Class microtext, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; texts are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures and labels. Two days teach reasons and word parts on two new pages; the third is the unit's first observation sitting on a different text, with no teaching in it.

- **5 min. Opening.** Opening repair (3 minutes) on the commonest 144 exit miss, one fresh card. Show the point-and-reason strips from 142 and say that today the strips come from a new page.
- **15 min. Read.** Read *Birds Help Plants* (g1-u4-03) whole and aloud (about 190 words, 2 minutes with pictures), then reread page 3 with its picture. Point: "Birds help plants grow in new places." Children point to fruit, seed and sprout on the labelled drawings.
- **15 min. Talk.** "What does the writer say birds do for plants?" "Which fact tells why?" "Does bright feathers tell why?" One ask each; partners two turns, six heard. The page itself says feathers are a fun fact and not why, so the class finds that line and points to it.
- **20 min. Writing and language.** Four strips (a bird eats fruit with seeds; seeds fall far from the plant; a sprout starts; bright feathers) are sorted under the point card into reasons and not a reason. - **5 min. Oral sounds.** Long i said and sorted: sit and night, pin and high, kit and sigh. Blend /n/ /igh/ /t/. Segment high.

- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Printing set with written feedback. Dictate "A bright night." (bright added by the igh pattern.)
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (the cycle 23 reader, A Little Seed, until the cycle 24 patterns are mapped), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.
- **Carried and evidence.** Tools: None. The sorted strips go in the portrait folder. Evidence: E145 (the exit case is a bigger tub; no practice uses a tub) (RI.1.8 reasons and an unrelated fact; the unit item is on 147).
