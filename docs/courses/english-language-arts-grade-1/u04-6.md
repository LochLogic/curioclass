# Section 4.6: Why plants and animals need birds, and the author's reasons


## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading exits read an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); cards for the other exits are new Curio Class microtexts, invented for practice, read once and kept out of teaching; texts are read aloud by the teacher and children follow the pictures and labels. Two days teach reasons and word parts on two new pages; the third is the unit's first observation sitting on a different text, with no teaching in it.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **Share of the unit's standards.** RI.1.8 is taught on 145 (plants) and 146 (animals) and observed on 147. RI.1.4 and L.1.4a to c are observed on 147; the word parts -ful, -less, root and ending were taught in 4.2 (day 126) and are reviewed on 146 with new words, and sentence clues were taught in 4.1 (day 121). Cycle 24 (ie and igh; lessons 121 to 123) runs beneath.
- **Limits that bite.** Useful, harmful, harmless, helps, helped, helping and bank are held out of teaching on 145 and 146 so the 147 items are new. The 147 sitting gets no help, no hint and no reteaching, and takes 40 topic minutes; the other 20 are familiar reading. Each 147 row is marked shown or not yet seen, with no shown with help, because no help is given. The unit plan's 0, 1 or 2 per part is read as 2 shown, 0 not yet seen; a 1 is recorded as not yet seen with the missing part named.
- **Connections.** Reviewed: point-and-reason strips (4.3, 4.5 day 142), sentence clue (4.1 day 121), -ful and -less and root and ending (4.2 day 126), turn card, heard-six column, printing sets.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) A reason tells why; a fun fact need not. (2) The sitting is silent for help. (3) How to take a dictated question word for word. (4) Content note: Birds Help Animals names hunters and food gently; nothing is shown in detail.

## 6. Days

## Day 145


- **5 min. Opening.** Opening repair (3 minutes) on the commonest 144 exit miss, one fresh card. Show the point-and-reason strips from 142 and say that today the strips come from a new page.
- **15 min. Read.** Read *Birds Help Plants* (g1-u4-03) whole and aloud (about 190 words, 2 minutes with pictures), then reread page 3 with its picture. Point: "Birds help plants grow in new places." Children point to fruit, seed and sprout on the labeled drawings.
- **15 min. Talk.** "What does the writer say birds do for plants?" "Which fact tells why?" "Does bright feathers tell why?" One ask each; partners two turns, six heard. The page itself says feathers are a fun fact and not why, so the class finds that line and points to it.
- **20 min. Writing and language.** Four strips (a bird eats fruit with seeds; seeds fall far from the plant; a sprout starts; bright feathers) are sorted under the point card into reasons and not a reason. Each child says "Birds help plants because ___" to a partner and writes one word label on a reason strip (seed, fruit or plant).
- **5 min. Exit.** E145, asked aloud.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Long i said and sorted: sit and night, pin and high, kit and sigh. Blend /n/ /igh/ /t/. Segment high.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Cycle 24, lesson 121: igh says long i (night, high, sigh, right). Compare the long-i spellings already taught, i_e (kite, bike) and y (my, fly); sort sixteen cards by spelling. Chain: high, sigh, night, right.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Printing set with written feedback. Dictate "A bright night." (bright added by the igh pattern.)
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (the cycle 23 reader, A Little Seed, until the cycle 24 patterns are mapped), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.
- **Carried and evidence.** Tools: None. The sorted strips go in the portrait folder. Evidence: E145 (the exit case is a bigger tub; no practice uses a tub) (RI.1.8 reasons and an unrelated fact; the unit item is on 147).

## Day 146


- **5 min. Opening.** Opening repair (3 minutes) on the commonest 145 exit miss, one fresh card.
- **15 min. Read.** Read *Birds Help Animals* (g1-u4-03) whole and aloud (about 220 words), then reread page 1 and page 3. Point: "Animals need birds because birds give them food." Say plainly that hunters such as hawks and owls eat other animals, and show only the labeled chain drawing: grasshopper, crow, hawk. Nothing is shown or described in detail.
- **15 min. Talk.** "What does the writer say animals need?" "Which fact tells why?" One ask each; partners two turns, six heard.
- **20 min. Writing and language.** Sort two reason strips under the point card (raccoons, opossums and snakes eat crow eggs; hawks and owls eat crows) and say "Animals need birds because ___." Then word work, reviewing 4.2 day 126: -ful (full of) and -less (without) and root and ending. Cards: joyful, thankful, colorful, cloudless, windless; play, plays, played, playing. Children match the picture to joyful and cloudless, then lay ending cards on the root play. The words useful, harmful and harmless are not used today.
- **5 min. Exit.** E146, asked aloud.
- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Blend /p/ /ie/ and /l/ /ie/. Segment pie. Say the long i in sky, night and pie and tell which sounds are the same.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Lesson 122: ie says long i in pie, lie, fried, cried. Compare igh, ie, i_e and y on the sort board. Chain: pie, lie, fried, cried.
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Printing set with written feedback. Dictate "Pie is on the high shelf."
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (Night Skies, the adapted cycle 24 reader, only once ie and igh and its irregular words are mapped), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.
- **Carried and evidence.** Tools: None. The affix and root cards stay in the folder as the 147 reference only if the child asks; nothing is taught on 147. Evidence: E146 (the exit words are sleeveless and yelps, yelped; the word work uses other words) (L.1.4b and L.1.4c are checked on 147 with new words).

## Day 147


- **40 min. Sitting.** Unit assessment on the adapted human-helpers text (g1-u4-05, *Birds as Human Helpers*), five rows, no help, no hint, no reteaching, one marking scale for the record. Pacing: row 1 at once, 12 minutes (about 3 minutes of whole-text read-aloud, then marking); row 2 six minutes; row 3 seven; row 4 seven; row 5 eight. Spoken stems with picture choices; answers marked, pointed, said or written as the item names. A child who cannot yet write the question in row 2 says it to the teacher during the next part, word for word, and it is written as said.

- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Mixed blend and segment of long-i words said aloud: bright, pie, sigh, fried.
- **10 min. Map and spell.** Lesson 123: sort ie, igh, i_e and y words by spelling on the board; read and spell a chain of eight words (night, high, pie, lie, fried, bike, fly, my).
- **10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation.** Printing set with written feedback. Dictate "The pie is on the high shelf." (not the 146 sentence; not scored).
- **30 min. Rotation.** The ten-minute catch-up group first (the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread). Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at the teacher table (Night Skies, the adapted cycle 24 reader, only once ie and igh and its irregular words are mapped), heard one child at a time, 50 seconds each; the heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.

## 9. Supports and extension

- English learners: picture cards for fruit, seed, droppings; frames "The writer says ___ because ___" and "___ means ___." Access: strips large, a scribe for item 2, a quiet corner for the sitting.
- Check accommodations: items read aloud, extra time inside the sitting, a partner's pointing recorded as the child's own only when the child points.
- Reading load: no child reads the page; the teacher reads, and sheets have single-word labels.
- Extension: add a third reason strip and say where on the page it is.
