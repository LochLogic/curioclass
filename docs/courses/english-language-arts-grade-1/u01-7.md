# Section 1.7: Analyze, draft, edit, check and share the explanation

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


**This section's share (verbs quoted).** **W.1.2**, "name a topic, supply some facts about the topic, and provide some sense of closure": day 28 analyzes the sign model and writes a topic sentence; day 29 drafts the topic and facts; day 30 adds the closing and revises after a partner's question; day 31 edits and scores once (topic, two accurate relevant facts, closure, each 0 or 1). **L.1.5, L.1.5a to d, L.1.6**: asked on day 30 (the second three groups of five; the first three were asked on day 29). **RF.1.1a to RL.1.10 and RI.1.10**: the observation rotations 2 to 5 (days 28 to 31) hear six new children a day with the day 27 items. **RL.1.1, RI.1.1, SL.1.2, RL.1.3, RL.1.6, RL.1.7, RI.1.6, RI.1.7**: the day 32 check. **SL.1.1, SL.1.1a to c, SL.1.3**: the day 33 discussion observation.

**Limits that bite.** Drafting is by children with the model, sound boxes and the four-part frame; invented spelling is expected (L.1.2e), and the sentences are four to six words. Exits stay spoken or pointed. Every new case on days 28 to 33 is invented (rack, ruler, tube, clip, watering can) and none repeats an exit, a taught case or an assessment card (A to F, V). Day 32 uses cards C and D and the unit's fresh texts only, never taught ones. Day 33's final 20 minutes are the unit's single response allocation.

**Connections.** Day 28 returns to the sign model (g1-u1-04) and day 7's frame; days 29 and 30 return to the tool articles; day 31 rereads the poem (g1-u1-03), the story (g1-u1-01) and an article; day 33 returns to the sign model and the hammer poem (the word "bang" is echoed, not decoded). **The teacher page must explain** the observation rotations, the scoring of the product only on day 31, and what a child may print.

## 2. Where it sits


Readiness used: the finished paper models and record sheets (days 24 to 27), the hearing records, the observation sheet of day 27. Results go to the observation sheet, the W.1.2 score sheet (day 31) and the day 33 response note.

## 3. Checks, written first


**Unit assessment items owned.** The day 28, 29, 30 and 31 rows in the table (each child's completed record from 27 to 31 is reused: 0 new minutes; the day's six different children use the day 27 table's items and seconds), the day 30 L.1.5 and L.1.6 rows, the day 31 W.1.2 row, the day 32 rows (RL.1.1, RI.1.1, SL.1.2, RL.1.3, RL.1.6, RL.1.7, RI.1.6, RI.1.7) and the day 33 rows (SL.1.1a to c, SL.1.3).

**How days 29 and 30 run (counted).** Word observation: three groups of five, 6 minutes each (1 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 1), 18 minutes a day; the other 25 children draft quietly. Day 29: word observation 18, text questions and talk 12, drafting 20, letter samples 5, exit 5. Day 30: word observation 18, text questions and talk 12, drafting 25, exit 5.

**How day 32 runs.** Setup and read-aloud of C and D: 8 minutes. Six groups of five, 5 minutes each, 30 minutes: 8 group minutes on D (RL.1.1), 7 on C (RI.1.1), 15 for each child's own question and answer after C and D (SL.1.2). The other children do the paper items: RL.1.3 5, RL.1.6 4, RL.1.7 4, RI.1.6 4, RI.1.7 5 (22 minutes). Make-up: 17. Close: 5.

**How day 33 runs.** Six groups of five, 5 minutes each, inside the first 40 topic minutes; finish all observation before the midpoint of the 120-minute day.

**Exit questions.** One spoken stem each, one ask; all children mark a picture; the rotating six give the reason.
- **E28** (day 28). Card: "Our paper rack is a tool for our drawings. Folded feet help it stand. Slots hold each drawing. The rack keeps our work together." Stem: "Say a sentence that names the tool and what it is for." Answer: names the rack and the drawings. Wrong: "It is good" (AB); "Folded feet help it stand", a fact and no topic (AB); "The slots are brown" (C).
- **E29** (day 29). Card: "A ruler measures our paper. Its marks show how long. Its straight edge guides our pencil." Stem: "Tell me two facts that explain how the ruler helps." Answer: the marks show how long; the straight edge guides the pencil. Wrong: "It is nice" (AA); "It is yellow" (C); "It cuts paper" (A).
- **E30** (day 30). Card: "Our paper tube carries rolled posters. Its closed ends keep them from sliding out." Partner: "How does that help when we walk to the rug?" Stem: "Say a closing sentence that answers the partner." Answer: a closing that says the tube lets us carry the posters without dropping them. Wrong: repeats one fact only (AC); "I like the tube" (AA); "The poster is red" (C).
- **E31** (day 31). Card: joined papers and loose papers; strips "The clip holds paper." and "the clip holds paper". Stem: "Point to the strip that is ready to share." Answer: the first. Wrong: the second; record the capital or ending mark mistake without an error letter.
- **E33** (day 33). Speaker: "A watering can has a long spout. Water goes from the spout to the roots." Partner: "I heard where the water goes." Pictures: a can watering roots; a can spraying leaves. Stem: "Which picture supports both speakers?" Answer: the roots. The six then add a fact or ask "What is a spout?" Wrong: an unrelated subject (AE).
- Day 32 uses the field 5 observation; it has no exit.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas


| Letter | Wrong, beside right | Day shown | If many made it (5 to 10 minutes, in the next opening repair) |
|---|---|---|---|
| AB | "It is good"; right: "My rack is a tool for drawings." | 28 | Contrast "It helps" with "My rack helps"; name the subject in a fresh opening. |
| A | "It cuts paper"; right: "Its marks show how long." | 29 | Reread the card; point to the sentence that says it. |
| C | "It is yellow"; right: "Its straight edge guides our pencil." | 29, 30 | Cover the picture, reread the words, uncover. |
| AA | "It is nice"; right: "Its marks show how long." | 29, 30 | Contrast "nice" with an observable fact; one fact per finger. |
| AC | Repeats one fact; right: says what the job becomes. | 30 | Lay the strips; point to the strip that closes the job; close a fresh piece. |
| AE | An unrelated turn; right: "I add that the water goes to the roots." | 33 | Contrast a new subject with adding to the sign claim; respond to a fresh comment. |

The rest of the register (G to T, AH on days 28 to 31; AD, AF, AG on day 33; U to Z for the L.1.5 and L.1.6 items) is recorded as it shows; the register's day 33 row is the repair, and nothing is taught new in a recording.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


**Carried.** The paragraph-frame fades to a checklist on day 30; main-idea-details, the sound-spelling-map, the story-map and the discussion-protocol return through day 33. Nothing here is a tool beyond printed cards, sound boxes, a booklet and the paper models.

**Key moments.** (1) The day 29 and 30 word observation: ideal is a printed set of word cards and a sound box, with the observation sheet; a room with nothing uses handwritten cards and a tally. (2) The day 31 W.1.2 score: ideal is the score sheet with the three 0 or 1 rows; a room with nothing uses a lined page and three ticks. (3) Day 32: Cards C and D printed on one page each, read aloud once. (4) Day 33's six groups: a chart of group order, one minute timers by feel.

## 6. Days

## Day 28


- **5 min. Opening repair.** Repair from E27 if many made A or C.
- **15 min. Read and analyze.** Read the sign model whole and show it on the four strips; call out the topic sentence and say why it names the tool. Then name a new tool and say its topic sentence with a partner.
- **10 min. Reread and question.** "Which sentence names the tool? Which gives a fact?" Point to the strip.
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs say a topic sentence; the listener asks "Which part?".
- **15 min. Write a topic sentence.** Frame "My ___ is a tool for ___." Say it, draw the tool, print the sentence with sound boxes.
- **5 min. Exit.** E28.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map and read.** Reread the cycle 4 reader.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Sentence dictation "Sam has a big tub."; letter samples N to T, dated.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Observation rotation 2 of 5: six different children, 5 minutes each; the other 24 read known texts and spell independently.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** File the observation sheet.

Who holds what is carried: pairs hold the four strips. Tool: None. By hand: printing a sentence. Evidence: E28 and that day's rows.

## Day 29


- **18 min. Word observation.** Three groups of five, 6 minutes each: sort tool and material words, define one by category and attribute, give a school use, act out look, peek and glance and compare tiny and little, then say a because sentence. The others draft quietly.
- **12 min. Text questions and talk.** Reread an article; partners find the fact, then say it with the listener asking "Which part?".
- **20 min. Draft.** Each child drafts the topic sentence and the first fact, then starts the second.
- **5 min. Letter samples.** U to Z, dated.
- **5 min. Exit.** E29.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map and read.** Reread the cycle 4 reader.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Sentence dictation "Tim has a tub."
- **30 min. Rotation.** Observation rotation 3 of 5: six different children, 5 minutes each.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** File the observation sheet.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds a booklet. Tool: None. By hand: drafting. Evidence: E29 and that day's rows.

## Day 30


- **18 min. Word observation.** The second three groups of five, 6 minutes each, as on day 29.
- **12 min. Text questions and talk.** A partner asks one specific question about the draft; the maker answers aloud.
- **25 min. Add the closing and revise.** Each child adds the closing sentence and revises one fact after the partner's question.
- **5 min. Exit.** E30.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map and read.** Reread the cycle 4 reader.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Letter samples completed and revised with written feedback; dictation "Pat has a hat."
- **30 min. Rotation.** Observation rotation 4 of 5: six different children, 5 minutes each.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** File the sheet.

Who holds what is carried: the paragraph-frame as a checklist. Tool: None. By hand: revising. Evidence: E30 and that day's rows.

## Day 31


- **15 min. Reread.** Reread g1-u1-04 (sign model), g1-u1-03 (hammer poem, "bang" echoed), g1-u1-01 (one page) and g1-u1-02 (one article line); children point to evidence.
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs find the facts their draft rests on.
- **10 min. Edit and submit.** Each child edits capital letters and ending marks and submits the explanation; the teacher scores W.1.2 once (topic, two accurate relevant facts, closure, each 0 or 1; facts attributed to the article and model; artistic quality is not graded).
- **20 min. Prepare to share.** Each child rehearses the explanation with the model.
- **5 min. Exit.** E31.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map and read.** Consolidate; no new pattern.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Letter samples score only; dictation "Sam has a big wig."
- **30 min. Rotation.** Observation rotation 5 of 5: six different children, 5 minutes each.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** File the sheet.

Who holds what is carried: the checklist. Tool: None. By hand: editing. Evidence: E31 and the W.1.2 row.

## Day 32


- **8 min. Setup and read-aloud.** Hand out the paper prompts; read C and D aloud once to all.
- **30 min. Hearings.** Six groups of five, 5 minutes each: ask and answer on D and on C. The others do the paper items: describe D's characters, place and events; mark D's teller and who says the words in the marks; give one word detail and one picture detail from D; put word and picture symbols on C's facts; describe the ramp's job.
- **17 min. Make-up.** Absent children and any item left blank.
- **5 min. Close.** Collect papers; no teaching.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map and read.** Familiar words only.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Familiar dictation.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Matched small-group reading and spelling.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Tidy.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds cards C and D. Tool: None. By hand: marking and drawing. Evidence: the day 32 rows.

## Day 33


- **5 min. Setup.** Name the six groups of five.
- **30 min. Observed discussions.** Six groups of five, 5 minutes each, on how the sign model shows that tools help. The others share models in pairs.
- **5 min. Regroup.**
- **15 min. Share.** A rotating six share their explanations with the class, each with one question from a listener.
- **5 min. Exit.** E33.
- **40 min. Independent reading and spelling.** Known texts and words (foundations, minutes 60 to 100).
- **20 min. Response.** Each child gets a note from days 27 to 33 evidence and retries one item on a fresh card of the same kind.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds the written note. Tool: None. By hand: sharing and retrying. Evidence: E33 and the day 33 rows.

## 7. Talk and writing

Questions: day 28, "Which sentence names the tool?"; day 30, "What does a partner not yet know?"; day 33, "Which part did you mean?" Written task: the four-part explanation (day 31 score). Look-fors: topic named; two facts from an article or the model; a closing about the job; talk that waits, builds and clarifies.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 28 reviews the sign model and the paragraph-frame of day 7; days 29 and 30 review the articles and shades of meaning; day 31 reviews plural -s, capital and end mark (kindergarten); day 32 reviews the story-map; day 33 reviews the discussion-protocol.

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: topic, fact, closing, spout; frame "My ___ helps us ___ because ___." Access: sound boxes; scribing for a child who cannot print; pointing answers. Check accommodations: extra seconds, a quiet corner. Reading load: only taught words. Extension: a second tool with its two facts.

## 10. Two rooms

A parent: ask your child to say their explanation with "because". Last day: the response note goes home with the model. One projector: show Cards C and D on day 32.
