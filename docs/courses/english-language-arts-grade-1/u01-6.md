# Section 1.6: A design change, a partner's question, and the first observation

## Header


## 1. Standards and practices


**This section's share (verbs quoted).** **W.1.2**, "supply some facts about the topic": on days 25 and 26 each child says, and the teacher writes for the heard six, one fact-based sentence "I changed my ___ because ___"; a printed word label is the most any child writes. **SL.1.1, SL.1.1a to c, SL.1.3**, "build on others' talk ... through multiple exchanges" and "ask questions ... to gather additional information": practiced on days 25 and 26 in pairs and trios (taught on day 21, section 1.5). **RF.1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, b, g, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, L.1.2e, RF.1.4a to c, RL.1.10, RI.1.10**: this section ends with the first of five observation rotations (day 27), where six children are heard and the verbs are those of the unit plan's day 27 table; the other days are practice and letter samples.

**Limits that bite.** Only revised reading records are scored on 27 to 31; the day 23 to 25 hearings are first tries with a written note, and the retry is the next day. Letter samples are dated, A to F on day 26 and G to M on day 27; Z is collected only from day 28. Day 27's observation words (thud, slip, stamp, cup, chip, shut, chin, cap, caps, tips, the, is) and Card F must not appear as taught cases, exit cards or dictation on any day. A new-letter pair is taught on days 25 to 27 (X, Y, Z) and no new sound pattern is taught: day 26 and 27 consolidate.

**Connections.** Day 25 returns to the articles and the funnel (day 21); day 26 to the discussion-protocol and the paragraph-frame; day 27 hands the finished model to section 1.7, which analyzes the sign model (day 28). **The teacher page must explain** the day 27 item order and seconds, a quiet teacher-only pace cue (a sand timer or a glance at a clock, never shown to children), and what to do with a child who is not yet seen.

Item seconds, in order: RF.1.1a 10, RF.1.2a 10, RF.1.2b 10, RF.1.2c 10, RF.1.2d 15, RF.1.3a 15, RF.1.3b 15, RF.1.3g 10, RF.1.3f 5, L.1.2d 20, L.1.2e 15, RF.1.4a 25, RF.1.4b 90, RF.1.4c 25. **Exit questions.** One spoken stem each, one ask; all children mark a picture; the rotating six give the reason. Reading exits reread an unworked part of the unit's own texts (DEFAULTS.md exit line); other cards print "invented for practice".
- **E25** (day 25). Card: "A deep box holds our blocks. A flat sheet lets them slide off." Pictures: a sheet with a raised edge; a longer sheet; a bluer sheet. Stem: "Which change would stop the blocks sliding off?" Answer: the raised edge. Wrong: "A new sheet is nice" (AA); "The box is brown" (C); "A lid would stop them", not on the card (A).
- **E26** (day 26). Card: "Your paper bin holds our books. How will I take one out?" Stem: "What would you change on the bin so a hand can take a book out?" Answer: a change that opens or lowers the front or top so a hand reaches in. Wrong: "Make it nicer" (AA); "Color it red" (C); "Make it bigger", with no fact behind it (A).
- **E27** (day 27). s01-X27v2 rereads Tools on the Farm, paragraph 2 (the hoe) aloud; items.csv holds the stem, answer and wrongs.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


**Carried.** The paragraph-frame, the sound-spelling-map and the discussion-protocol return; the design change is a model of the day: a change made on purpose because a fact says it will help (the funnel's narrow end is the worked example, never an exit case). Nothing here is a tool beyond printed cards, paper and tape.

**Key moments.** (1) The day 27 observation: the ideal instrument is a printed item card, a sound box and one picture-and-sentence card (F), with a quiet timer for the teacher; a room with nothing reads the same items aloud and records on a lined page. (2) Letter samples: a lined sample strip per child; a room with nothing uses plain paper with a drawn baseline.

## 6. Days

## Day 25


- **5 min. Opening repair.** Repair from E24 if many made A or AA.
- **10 min. Reread.** Reread the funnel paragraph and the funnel change: the teacher says "A change on purpose, because a fact says it will help."
- **10 min. Talk.** Pairs find one fact in an article and say how it could change their model; the listener asks "Which part?".
- **20 min. Draw the change.** Draw the change, print one word label, and say "I changed my ___ because ___" to a partner; the teacher writes the sentences of the heard six.
- **10 min. Linked share.** Two groups of fifteen; one child adds to another's change ("I add that ..."); the teacher hears linked exchanges.
- **5 min. Exit.** E25.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map.** Decode and spell with taught patterns; cycle progress sample.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** X and x; dictation "Six cats sit."
- **30 min. Rotation.** Hearing, cohort 3 (15 minutes): ten children, 90 seconds each, as on day 23. Retry, cohort 2 (15 minutes), using the written note.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** Collect drawings.

Who holds what is carried: each child holds a model and a change drawing. Tool: None. By hand: drawing, labeling. Evidence: E25.

## Day 26


- **5 min. Opening repair.** Repair from E25 if needed.
- **10 min. Reread.** Reread the sign model and each child's record sentence.
- **15 min. Partner questions.** Each partner chooses a question strip (How, Why or What) and points it at one part of the model; the maker answers aloud.
- **25 min. Revise.** Each maker revises the model or the drawing and says the revision with "because" to the partner. The teacher writes the sentences of the heard six.
- **5 min. Exit.** E26.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map.** Reread the cycle 4 reader; no new pattern.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Y and y; dictation "Yum, a bun."; letter samples A to F, dated.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Retry, cohort 3 (15 minutes); matched group reading (15 minutes).
- **5 min. Organize and record.** File the samples.

Who holds what is carried: partners hold question strips. Tool: None. By hand: revising a paper model. Evidence: E26.

## Day 27


- **5 min. Opening repair.** Repair from E26 if needed.
- **15 min. Finish the model.** Each child finishes the model and one record sentence.
- **20 min. Rehearse.** Trios take turns as maker, listener and asker; the maker says two facts with "because". The teacher hears a rotating six.
- **15 min. Closing.** Each child says a closing sentence about the job and draws the job done.
- **5 min. Exit.** E27.
- **5 min. Oral sound work.** Blend and segment.
- **10 min. Map.** Consolidate the cycle 4 patterns; no new pattern.
- **10 min. Letters and dictation.** Z and z; dictation "Zan has a zip."; letter samples G to M, dated.
- **30 min. Rotation.** Observation rotation 1 of 5: six different children, 5 minutes each; the other 24 read known texts and spell independently.
- **5 min. Organize and record.** File the observation sheet.

Who holds what is carried: the teacher holds the observation sheet. Tool: None (a quiet teacher-only pace cue is allowed). By hand: printing samples. Evidence: E27 and the day 27 rows.

## 7. Talk and writing

Questions: day 25, "Which fact says why the change helps?"; day 26, "How, why or what about this part?" Written task: the change drawing with one word label (day 25), and the revised model (day 26). Look-fors: a fact the article gave; a change that follows from it; "because" said; a partner's question answered on topic.

## 8. Practice that returns

Day 25 reviews the funnel and the articles; day 26 reviews the paragraph-frame and the plan sheet; day 27 reviews every taught cycle (the sound-spelling-map, plural -s, th, ch, sh, qu and the mapped words).

## 9. Supports and extension

English learners: change, fact, because; frame "I changed my ___ because ___." Access: pictures on strips; a partner may point; the observation items may be answered by pointing. Check accommodations: a quiet corner for the observation, extra seconds where the dialect differs. Reading load: only taught words. Extension: a second change with its fact.

## 10. Two rooms

A parent: ask your child to show one change on their model and say "because". One projector: show the question strips.
