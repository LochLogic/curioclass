# Section 2.7: Rehearse, check and respond: a sky talk

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#ela/english-language-arts-grade-1/2/s/2.7), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py english-language-arts-grade-1 u02-7.md`. <!-- facts link -->

## Header


Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.

## 1. Standards and practices


- **SL.1.4, SL.1.5, SL.1.6, this section's share (days 60 to 62).** Describe with relevant details and a feeling, add a drawing that clarifies, speak a complete sentence. Taught on 59; practiced on 60; checked on 61; revised on 62.
- **W.1.7 and W.1.8 (days 60 to 62).** "Participate in shared research and writing projects" and "gather information from provided sources to answer a question": the shared notebook is revised on 60 after the feedback written on 59, scored on 61 (the unit plan holds the row), and edited on 62.
- **SL.1.1 and SL.1.3 (days 60 to 62).** Follow discussion rules, build on others' talk, ask for clarification, ask and answer about a speaker's words. The two five-child groups heard on 59 revisit on 60; the revised record is scored on 61.
- **RI.1.3 (days 61, 62).** "Describe the connection between two individuals, events, ideas, or pieces of information in a text": taught on 56 and 58, checked on 61 on a fresh text.
- **L.1.1j, L.1.2a, b, c (day 62).** Complete sentences; a capital for a name or a date; an end mark; commas in a series and in a date: edited, not retaught.
- **Foundations, days 60 to 62:** cycle 10 lessons 52 to 54 (l- and r-blends, three-consonant clusters, -es, -er and -est on known bases): RF.1.2a to d, RF.1.3a, b, f, g, RF.1.4a to c, RF.1.1a, L.1.1a, L.1.2d, e.
- **Limits that bite here.** The check text (M, the Jo cloud pair) is a fresh invented source: nothing about clouds is taught earlier, and it checks comparison from one fixed view, not the daytime moon. Reading is by the teacher; children point, draw and say. Night fears: gentle descriptions, and class picture records replace any home observation. No new teaching on 61; no second conference on 61; reteaching is held for 62.
- **Connections.** The research-cycle chart and the connection arrow, turn card, groups of three (maker, listener, asker), the heard-six rotation: all review from 2.1 to 2.6, named on the wall.
- **What the teacher page must explain.** (1) Why a drawing helps a listener only when it shows what the talk says. (2) A good question to a speaker asks what the listener does not yet see, not what the speaker already said. (3) How a source-labeled fact differs from decoration. (4) How the teacher scores the oral rotation by component (details, an idea and a feeling, a complete sentence) and records "not observed" rather than guessing.

- **15 min. Revise the notebook contribution.** Each child reads the written feedback on the note from 59 (in pictures), then adds or fixes one source-labeled fact beside the group question. The border contrast (O) is shown first, once.
- **25 min. Rehearse the talk.** Ten groups of three (maker, listener, asker; one minute each role, three rounds, nine minutes of speaking each). The teacher hears the two five-child groups from 59 for 5 minutes each, with a written note of the linked exchange. Pip asks one clarifying question the way a partner does.

- **5 min. Oral sounds.** Four spoken prompts: the long vowel in a pair (stripe, strip); blend /f/ /r/ /o/ /g/; first, vowel and last sound of trip; segment grass. Spoken only.

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 60, "What does your drawing help us see?" (the partner's); day 61, "Tell us about Jo's pictures."; day 62, "Which source detail should our note include?"

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Retrieved.** 60: the heard-six routine, the shared notebook, and stars and the dark sky from 2.6. 61: no retrieval (assessment). 62: the sky patterns of 2.1 to 2.6, because and so from 2.5, capitals and end marks.
- **Foundations.** Each day maps the cycle's pattern, spells with it, rereads for meaning and expression, and returns to earlier cycles' words.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: dark, bright, draw, listener, question. Frames: "My drawing helps us see ___." "I agree with ___ because ___." "Which ___ do you mean?"
- **Access.** Enlarged print, tactile picture choices, a scribe, home-language rehearsal; record the support.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing, a spoken answer or a scribe; stems read twice; no time pressure; do not read a decoding word or supply a spelling.
- **Reading load.** The teacher reads M and the articles aloud; children read word labels and the cycle passage.
- **Extension, same standard.** Add a second source-labeled fact to the note and ask a partner a clarifying question about it.
