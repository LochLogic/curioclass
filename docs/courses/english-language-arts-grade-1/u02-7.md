# Section 2.7: Rehearse, check and respond: a sky talk

## Header

<!-- skel -->
English Language Arts, Grade 1. Unit 2, The sun, moon and stars. *What patterns can we see in the sky, and what stories do people tell about them?* (days 34 to 75): see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 60 to 62.** Days by kind: application 1 (60), check 1 (61), respond 1 (62). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

- Section 7 of 10 in the unit; the unit plan holds its section fields as U3 to U11 (a one-section unit until the 2026-11-16 cuts).
- Day length (frames.csv): 120 minutes.
<!-- /skel -->

Children read very little. Stems are spoken with picture cards; answers are spoken, pointed to, sorted or drawn, with at most a word label written. Each day is 120 minutes: a topic block of 60 and a foundations block of 60 (DEFAULTS.md). Marking is shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every card named below is a Curio Class original, invented for practice, and prints that label.
