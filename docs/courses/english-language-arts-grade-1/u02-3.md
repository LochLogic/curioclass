## Header

<!-- skel -->

## 7. Talk and writing

- Planned questions: day 43, "Which words tell how the speaker feels?"; day 44, "What does each one do?"; day 46, "What is the lesson, and which event shows it?"
- Written task: a drawn moment with one word label (day 45); the retelling is spoken.

## 8. Practice that returns

- **Warm-up.** One repair of at most 5 minutes from field 4, in the reread part each day.
- **Retrieved.** Feeling and sense chart (43, 45), story-map (44, 46), two columns (44), turn card, the cycle readers.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: poem, fact, lesson, last. Frames: "It is a poem because ___." "The lesson is ___."
- **Access.** Large cards; tactile cards for hand words; a scribe for labels.
- **Check accommodations.** Pointing; a spoken answer; a scribe.
- **Reading load.** Children decode only taught short e, short o and mapped words; the poems and tales are read aloud.
- **Extension, same standard.** Find a second describing word.

## 10. Two rooms

- **Teaching one child.** Read each poem and tale in two sittings; lay three strips; say the lesson.
- **One projector.** Show pictures page by page; cards and strips are paper.
