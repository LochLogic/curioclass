## 1. Standards and practices

K.MD.B.3, "Classify", "count", "sort": categories/count order, day 135. K.CC.A.3, "Write"/"Represent": counts/numerals, days 135/136/139. K.CC.B.5, "Count"/"count out": all arrangements and requests, days 136/139. K.CC.C.6, "Identify": more/fewer/equal, day 136. K.OA.A.2, "Solve": adding/taking one, day 137. K.CC.B.4c, "Understand": next word means one larger, days 137/138. K.CC.A.2, "Count forward": supplied starts, days 138/139.

Arranged sets/numerals stop at twenty; scattered sets/comparisons at ten; stories within ten. Oral counting alone reaches one hundred. No algorithm. MP.1 day 137 repairs a story model; MP.8 day 138 relates one change to neighboring count words. Explain tracking versus total and spacing versus amount. Geometry days 135/136 responds to unit 7.

- **K.MD.B.3** (days 135): Classify objects into given categories; count the numbers of objects in each category and sort the categories by count.
- **K.CC.A.3** (days 135, 136, 139): Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20 (with 0 representing a count of no objects).
- **K.CC.B.5** (days 135, 136, 139): Count to answer “how many?” questions about as many as 20 things arranged in a line, a rectangular array, or a circle, or as many as 10 things in a scattered configuration; given a number from 1–20, count out that many objects.
- **K.CC.C.6** (days 136): Identify whether the number of objects in one group is greater than, less than, or equal to the number of objects in another group, e.g., by using matching and counting strategies.
- **K.OA.A.2** (days 137): Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings to represent the problem.
- **K.CC.B.4c** (days 137, 138): Understand that each successive number name refers to a quantity that is one larger.
- **K.CC.A.2** (days 138, 139): Count forward beginning from a given number within the known sequence (instead of having to begin at 1).

- kind: Application. Applies taught work to a new task or case (an inquiry, a lab, a draft, a revision or edit); nothing is taught as new.
- goal: Sort, count and order category counts for classroom objects.
- note: Base K.8.1. Retrieval task: flat and solid features from unit 7 and category counts from unit 2. Unit 7 response in centers. Keep each category at most ten. Daily oral count by ones and tens to 100. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known.
