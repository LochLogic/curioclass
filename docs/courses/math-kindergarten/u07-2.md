## 1. Standards and practices

K.OA.A.1, "Represent", day 123: shape-piece parts/equations in either order. K.G.B.5, "Model", K.G.B.6, "Compose": flat construction review. K.OA.A.2, "Solve", day 124: joining/removal within ten. K.OA.A.3, "Decompose"/"record", K.OA.A.4, "find"/"record", day 125: multiple ten splits/missing partners. Retain units 3 to 5 whole-standard evidence. MP.7 day 123: parts/whole in both equation orders; MP.3 day 125: unchanged ten. No algorithm. Explain: every piece counts once regardless of area; equals states equal quantities, not a direction to calculate on the right. Calculation within ten.

## 3. Checks, written first

All evidence is judged=teacher. Read one stem, wait for its response, then read the next. Every child marks paper, including a sketch or trace of a build; children read no prompt independently. Hear a rotating six, one 45-second ask each, and record their names. On two-ask tickets hear three per ask; the other component uses paper evidence. Unheard language remains not yet seen. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Samples below distinguish independent success from a recorded error; record any prompt as help. Print and say "Invented for practice" for each invented story scene. One actor acts in each scene. Reserve all exit and check cases across teaching, games, repairs and home practice.

**Unit assessment items owned:** none new. Retain unit 4 F1/F2 and unit 5 F1/F2/F3 component judgments; unresolved evidence stays pending.

- **E123, K.OA.A.1.** New zigzag composition, three squares/four triangles. Read shuffled complete cards 7 = 3 + 4, 6 = 3 + 3, 8 = 4 + 4. All three equations are true, so arithmetic elimination cannot identify the matching composition. "Join this build to its equation." Answer 7 = 3 + 4. Shown: correct positive link. Not yet seen: 6 = 3 + 3 repeats the square group (N); 8 = 4 + 4 repeats the triangle group (N).   Shape: match complete composition to complete equation. Practice repairs one missing numeral/link in an incomplete equation, never a menu of complete equations.
- **E124, K.OA.A.2.** One actor/new sign scene: "Rin has five pieces. Rin adds two pieces. Show how many pieces Rin has now." Blank paper/pieces. Answer seven, e.g. five plus two marks enclosed together/7. Shown: seven visible pieces; not yet seen: only two arriving pieces counted as total (I).
  Shape: independently represent/solve a whole joining story on blank paper. Practice acts out teacher-started events and repairs supplied drawings, never a blank whole-story solution. The separate removal item below checks the opposite action without putting two asks into one stem.
- **E124r, s07-P124-exitbox-removal, K.OA.A.2.** New removal scene, one actor: "Rin makes a sign with eight pieces. Rin removes three pieces. Show how many pieces remain." Answer five, with three removed from the original eight or five remaining marks tied to the removal. Shown: five remain; not yet seen: eleven, adding the removed three (H).
  Shape: independently represent the remaining whole after a removal story. Practice completes the removed part of a supplied six-piece record, never constructs this blank eight-minus-three record.
- **E125a, K.OA.A.3.** Ten identical ungrouped pentagonal picture tabs, no partitions. "Split these ten pieces into two groups on your paper." Any two parts totaling ten, including clearly represented empty part, valid. Shown sample nine/one with labels 9/1; not yet seen sample eight/one claimed ten (J).
  Shape: independently partition a whole ten-piece collection. Practice completes supplied splits, never partitions ungrouped ten independently.
- **E125b.** Second copy: "Make a different split here." Any different unordered pair totaling ten, e.g. seven/three after nine/one. Shown: 7/3; not yet seen: 8/1 claimed ten (J). The second pair must differ as an unordered pair.
  Shape: generate another partition, not complete one empty part. Neither tab shape nor blank two-solution record is practiced.
- **E125c (s07-P125-exitbox), K.OA.A.4.** New ten-space strip, seven occupied in a nonconsecutive pattern. "Fill the empty part to make ten." Answer three new marks. Shown: three added/all ten occupied. Not yet seen: two added/nine called ten (J).

More than a third is many; otherwise same-slot small group. Ten-minute slots hold at most two actions including retries. E124 I uses day 125's other five minutes. Further needs remain for later centers, never shorten new teaching.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: equation, plus, minus, equals, part, whole, split, partner of ten. Frame: "___ and ___ make ___." These are reviews of written units 4 and 5. Read every equation aloud and point to its parts.

Read everything aloud; independent reading is zero. Offer large tactile outlines, high contrast, nonslip mats and broad pencils. Accept signing, home-language explanations, communication aids and directed adult movement. Scribe dictated labels, record help, never judge fine-motor skill. Patterns supplement colors; adults cut pieces. Every picture has a tactile model if needed. No public speech required. Extension: another solution or another feature rule within the same standard.
