## 1. Standards and practices

K.CC.A.3, "Write"/"Represent": extend quantity labels toward twenty; K.CC.B.5, "Count"/"count out": track arranged sets through twenty, scatters through ten, stop at requested quantities; K.CC.A.2, "Count forward": supplied starts day 106. Connect K.CC.B.4b day 107 to unchanged cardinality. MP.6 day 105: one visit per object; MP.7 day 107: unchanged members preserve the whole. No standard algorithm/numeral comparisons above ten. Adult explanation: order/spacing changes neither membership nor quantity; a circle needs a stopping boundary. Twenty is a count, not a teen decomposition. Review written u02-3 day 25 tracking and u02-4 day 31 quantity-label work.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: count, counted, uncounted, row, circle, same, numeral. Frame: "I counted ___ once; there are ___."

Read everything aloud; independent reading is zero. Use large, spaced, tactile, high-contrast pictures, pattern plus color, nonslip mats and broad pencils. Signing, home-language reasoning, pointing, dictation and directed adult movement are available. Preserve quantities/single asks; never supply answers. Record independent numeral formation separately from scribed quantity labels. No required fingers/public speech. Adult-cut paper squares replace counters; drawn frames replace printed frames; handwritten cards replace numeral cards; pencil marks replace crayons. Replace mouthed pieces with drawings. Extend with another tracking route/layout of the same quantity within the same limits.

## 10. Two rooms

One child: 30-minute table sitting, counting 3, demonstration/rehearsal 6, paper/partner work 8, repair 5, exit 8. Adult alternates handler/observer. Split sittings when evidence needs longer; reduce repetition, never goal/access. One shared classroom screen enlarges pictures only; all tasks have paper masters. No screen/printer: sketch masters. No equipment queue.
