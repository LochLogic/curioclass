- **E64.** Four striped and two plain large paper corks beside an empty mat. "Bring these groups together to show how many altogether." Everyone moves pieces and traces/dots the collection; six give totals. Answer/look-for: Both groups, six. Meets: six traces, "Six altogether." Does not meet: Only the two plain pieces brought (B). Shape: Independent loose-group combination on blank paper; practice follows supplied movement paths and repairs already-combined layouts, never independently combines on a blank mat or uses corks.

- **E65.** Three fox pictures and six owl pictures, separate groups. "Tell how many animals there are altogether." All dot the whole on a blank answer panel; hear six. Answer/look-for: Nine, both groups. Meets: nine dots, "Nine." Does not meet: Eight with one animal visibly skipped (A). Shape: Whole across separate animal groups without intermediate totals; practice corrects supplied classroom-object totals on already-combined mats, never separated animal groups/blank total panels.

- **E66.** One boot picture and seven scattered boot pictures, no route. "Find how many boots altogether without missing any." All mark a self-chosen route; hear six counts. Answer/look-for: Eight, each picture once. Meets: eight matched ticks and count eight. Does not meet: Seven with one boot untouched (A). Shape: Independently devise a route across scattered groups; practice follows adult-supplied routes and repairs a supplied missing tick, never invents a route or uses boots.

- **E67.** Two quiet chime sounds, pause, then six; repeat identically if needed. "Show these two sound groups put together." All act with paper disks or fingers and retain paper dots; hear six. Answer/look-for: Eight units, both bursts. Meets: two disks joined by six, eight dots. Does not meet: Only the six incoming sounds represented (B). Shape: Reconstruct heard bursts independently as action/paper trace; practice moves visible squares jointly or corrects supplied clap records, never reconstructs heard bursts. Quiet mouth sounds/visible beats or tactile taps replace a chime.
