## 6. Days

 Tools: None daily; no key tool. Children hold pieces/paper, teacher holds dated record. Practice uses joint actions, supplied partial work or corrections, never the independent check formats in field 3. Practice combinations only: 2 + 3 = 5, 1 + 3 = 4, 2 + 2 = 4, 3 + 3 = 6, 3 + 4 = 7, 4 + 4 = 8; 5 - 2 = 3, 4 - 1 = 3, 4 - 2 = 2, 6 - 3 = 3, 7 - 3 = 4, 8 - 4 = 4; 4 + 0 = 4, 4 + 1 = 5, 5 - 5 = 0, 9 + 1 = 10, 9 - 1 = 8, 10 - 5 = 5. Do not use reversed combinations reserved for evidence. Whole-class demonstrations hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds inside their block. Partner turns run simultaneously in 15 pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; handling fits connected work. Everyone returns a corrected paper record. Read prompts one at a time, never chorus check answers. Rin is sole story actor moving objects, never children. Fingers, quiet sounds, imagined actions and verbal accounts are alternatives, not simultaneous requirements. Tidy occupies the last two center minutes unless stated otherwise.

## 9. Supports and extension

New oral meanings: add/put together, bring groups into one whole; altogether/total, how many in the whole; adding to, more join a starting group. Review zero, none and one more in unit 2's sense; day 67 applies them to arrivals.

Use objects and gestures with the practice frame "These marks show ___." Read all print aloud; independent reading is zero. Offer enlarged/high-contrast or tactile pictures, nonslip mats, broad pencils and the child's usual communication method. Adult movement/scribing follows the child's directions; record mathematical help. A scribe must not supply the action. Keep child-directed paper marks for every exit, with the same look-fors; remove answer-bearing language frames. Fingers and loud sounds are optional. Extend by correcting another supplied model within ten, not introducing comparison stories, unknown-position equations or a required counting-on method.
