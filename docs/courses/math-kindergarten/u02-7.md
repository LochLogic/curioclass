Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 2, Numbers 1 to 10: see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 41 to 44.** Days by kind: understanding 1, fluency 0, application 2, check 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** K.MD.B.3: "Classify", "count" every category, "sort" categories by count, all taught day 41 before application. Each group at most ten; match shape exemplars without a geometry-name prerequisite. Day 41 teaches short/long category labels using individual exemplar strips, without measuring or the exit cards. Review B.4b invariance, B.5 count-out and A.3 labels within ten. C.6/C.7 retrieve more/fewer/same by quantities/numerals; day 44 collects missing evidence for these and B.4c only.

MP.3, day 42: justify larger category. MP.7, day 41: identify a full five in F1. Teacher pages distinguish category rule, group count and category order. No algorithm, operation/inequality symbols, preference survey or personal food report.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Carry mats, labels and counted sets; day 41 ties counts to numerals and orders categories. Day 42 changes the rule without changing membership. Day 43 withdraws partial completion support for its exit. Teacher keeps day 41 unscored practice separate from F4.

## 9. Supports and extension

Language: sort, put together by a rule; category, a group following that rule; rule, what belongs together; short/long, the lesser/greater length of the shown strips. Review fewest/most from u02-5. Frame: "This belongs here because ___." Teach exemplar meaning before tasks; no shape-name reading. Read all print aloud; no independent reading. Enlarge high-contrast/tactile pictures, use broad pencils, and accept pointing, signing or aided communication. Adult movement follows exact child directions; record help. A scribe records reasons, never selects/writes a numeral on a numeral-writing check. Separate motor formation from quantity understanding. Extend within ten by changing spacing/orientation and defending unchanged quantity, not a new operation.
