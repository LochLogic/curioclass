# Section 2.7: Sort, count and complete picture orders

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-kindergarten/2/s/2.7), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-kindergarten u02-7.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 2, Numbers 1 to 10: see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 41 to 44.** Days by kind: understanding 1, fluency 0, application 2, check 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** K.MD.B.3: "Classify", "count" every category, "sort" categories by count, all taught day 41 before application. Each group at most ten; match shape exemplars without a geometry-name prerequisite. Day 41 teaches short/long category labels using individual exemplar strips, without measuring or the exit cards. Review B.4b invariance, B.5 count-out and A.3 labels within ten. C.6/C.7 retrieve more/fewer/same by quantities/numerals; day 44 collects missing evidence for these and B.4c only.

MP.3, day 42: justify larger category. MP.7, day 41: identify a full five in F1. Teacher pages distinguish category rule, group count and category order. No algorithm, operation/inequality symbols, preference survey or personal food report.

## 3. Checks, written first

Teacher judged, path paper. Read every stem aloud; label pictures "Invented for practice" and say so. Each exit asks for one picture product on a new case. Read follow-ups separately to six named children only. Eight minutes: launch 1, simultaneous paper 2, six turns at 45 seconds 4.5, collect 0.5. Everyone returns paper; unheard reasoning/counting is recorded as not yet seen. Record names heard and support by component using only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Correct keys below are shown samples; observed wrong actions are not yet seen samples. Assign letters only from observed actions or heard reasons.

Reserve complete cases, art and response formats, not every integer: M3 contains all integers 0 to 10. Never rehearse a reserved format with other numbers. Use the reverse directions, partial work or critiques in each Shape line. A recognition choice needs quantity evidence, never elimination alone.

**Owned assessment: F4.** F1/F3 belong to u02-6, F2 to u02-2. Use u02-6 cold keys, separated prompts and Shape reservations. F1 fills the tenth space, labels 10, not the inherited ninth-space sample. Day 41 Connected work gives six minutes F1 paper. Day 41 centers hear children 11 to 20; day 42 hears 21 to 30, two minutes each for separate F2/F3 reasons. Thirty children then have one oral record per domain. Return F2/F3 feedback next day; F1 returns day 42 for two-minute revision. Preserve initial/revised work separately.

**F4, K.MD.B.3.** Day 42 independent ten-minute task: fresh mixed sticker-shape pictures, two circles, four squares, six triangles, category mats with examples. Separately read "Sort these pictures onto their mats." "Write each group's number." "Put the mats from fewest to most." Keys: 2 circles, 4 squares, 6 triangles; circle, square, triangle. Shown: all correctly grouped, counted, labeled and ordered. Wrong: triangle on square mat (F); one square omitted while counting (K); correct counts ordered 6/4/2 (G). Recognizable reversed numerals get formation feedback.

Shape: Independent three-category shape classification, counts and ascending mat order. No sticker shapes, 2/4/6 distribution or complete independent three-mat sort/order in practice; use adult-started color-square groups with some counts/order supplied. F4 uses fresh cards, overriding the unit's reused-card direction to protect its case. Written feedback after class day 42, fifteen teacher minutes; revision five minutes day 43, judgment from record day 44.

**E41, K.MD.B.3.** Mixed four mitten/seven sock pictures and two icon mats, each with the familiar number-label box. Read "Make a picture of the sorted groups." Key: four mittens on the mitten mat labeled 4, seven socks on the sock mat labeled 7; one integrated picture record, no appended comparison ask. Six follow-up: "What makes this picture belong here?" Shown: "It is a sock, like the mat picture." Wrong: mitten on sock mat (F); observes four mittens correctly but writes 7 (M).

Shape: Join new pictures to two given categories and write their counts. No mittens/socks, 4/7 case or independent two-mat sort-and-label sheet in practice; correct partial three-color groups with supplied labels/order.

**E42, K.MD.B.3.** Ten rectangles initially sorted striped/plain, five each. Two short striped, four short plain, three long striped, one long plain. New mats show short/long examples. The familiar record has movable category mats with count boxes and a fewest-to-most arrow. Read "Show the new sort on your paper." Key: six short labeled 6 and four long labeled 4, long mat before short; membership now uses length, not pattern. Six follow-up: "What rule did you use?" Shown: "Short here, long there." Wrong: keeps pattern groups despite length rule (F); correct counts ordered 6 then 4 under the ascending arrow (G).

Shape: Re-sort given cards by size, write both counts and order fewest to most. No striped/plain length cards, 6/4 redistribution or complete independent re-sort/count/order record in practice; correct supplied color/border classifications and existing labels separately.

**E43, K.CC.B.5.** Fresh blank paper pizza, order card mushroom icon/3 and pepper-piece icon/6 read aloud, ten spare paper pictures of each. Read "Fill this picture order." Key: exactly three mushrooms/six pepper pieces attached and outlined on paper. Six follow-up: "How did you know when to stop?" Shown: "The order said three mushrooms and six peppers." Wrong: keeps adding past six (D); omits a requested piece (K), only if observed. No appended writing/comparison ask.

Shape: Complete unfamiliar two-food order on a blank pizza. No mushrooms/pepper pieces, 3/6 order or blank full-order pizza in practice; repair partly filled five-ring/eight-strip orders and compare packed orders/supplied labels.

**Day 44 closure.** Reuse M1/M2/M3, F1/F2/F3 and revised F4, no extra exit. Judge earlier work only. Familiar centers 10, missing evidence up to 40, close records 10. No reteaching on day 44. Days 45/46 each allow one five-minute repair and five-minute fresh transfer. Teacher judgment: ten closing minutes plus thirty after class for thirty records. Keep initial, feedback, revision and judgment dates.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Carry mats, labels and counted sets; day 41 ties counts to numerals and orders categories. Day 42 changes the rule without changing membership. Day 43 withdraws partial completion support for its exit. Teacher keeps day 41 unscored practice separate from F4.

Ideal instruments: icon mats/movable large pictures and paper pizzas/orders. Hand-drawn mats, adult-cut paper squares and original food drawings fit; no digital spec/key tool. Duplicate border cues accompany color rules, so color vision is not required. A room with nothing draws/marks cards on scrap paper, no shared-device turns.

## 6. Days

Tools: None throughout. Children hold pieces and paper; teacher retains dated component records, originals, written feedback and revisions separately. Fifteen pairs work concurrently: two 45-second turns take 1.5 minutes per round, leaving handling/checking time. At most two public volunteers; passing is allowed. One puppet actor per scene, one spoken ask per turn. Everyone outside a conference returns a paper trace/corrected diagram. Use Finish tick from u02-1 to distinguish an intentional empty answer. Children hear every printed label; independent reading is zero. Printed practice scenes say "Invented for practice"; say this aloud too.

Return and repair reviews the u01-1 routine: return 1, model one correction 1, simultaneous fresh paper practice 2, collect 1 minute. After class, allow fifteen teacher minutes for thirty paper sets, thirty seconds each, to write one useful correction; assessment feedback below is additional. Keep originals and revisions. More than ten of thirty sharing a letter triggers the class version; otherwise a small group while others correct familiar paper. Queue additional letters across later slots. Never run a teacher-led repair during a teacher conference. Use the paper stand-ins in field 5; adult-cut large paper pieces and concurrent table-tray cleanup avoid cutting/sink queues.

## Day 41

**Shape:** Join new pictures to two given categories and write their counts. No mittens/socks, 4/7 case or independent two-mat sort-and-label sheet in practice; correct partial three-color groups with supplied labels/order.

- **5 min. Oral count and retrieval.** Retrieve numeral comparisons, never reserved pairs.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Day 40 F2/F3 feedback, one blocking comparison/numeral correction.

- **10 min. Model sorting and counts.** Name short/long using individual exemplar strips first. Model number boxes and the ascending arrow on an already partly completed record. Teach rule, all counts and ascending categories with adult-started red/blue/green squares, 3/5/8; duplicate border cues. Teach ties with two partial groups of six: either position is valid. No independent full-mat sort.

- **10 min. Connected work and F1.** Four minutes partial color-diagram correction; six independent F1 minutes with separate prompts. Keep sorting practice unscored.

- **20 min. Final reasons and centers.** Children 11 to 20 give one F2 and one F3 reason in two-minute turns. Others return familiar paper diagrams and feedback corrections.

- **2 min. Tidy.** Collect practice separately from final evidence.

- **8 min. Exit ticket.** E41, all paper/six reasons. F1/oral written feedback follows after class.

## Day 42

- **5 min. Oral count and retrieval.** Retrieve category rules, not rectangle re-sort.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** F1 revision two minutes, F2/F3 corrections three; keep originals.

- **8 min. Rehearsal.** Correct partly grouped color/border squares under a changed rule; discuss counts, no new content.

- **5 min. Connected repair.** One five-minute E41 or queued repair and fresh correction, class if many.

- **25 min. Final reasons and F4 centers.** First five minutes launch F4 prompts separately. Then hear children 21 to 30 for twenty minutes; no paper prompts during oral turns. Children have ten independent F4 minutes within block, then familiar centers.

- **8 min. Exit ticket.** E42, all paper/six rule explanations. F4 feedback after class.

## Day 43

**Shape:** Complete unfamiliar two-food order on a blank pizza. No mushrooms/pepper pieces, 3/6 order or blank full-order pizza in practice; repair partly filled five-ring/eight-strip orders and compare packed orders/supplied labels.

## 7. Talk and writing

## 9. Supports and extension

Language: sort, put together by a rule; category, a group following that rule; rule, what belongs together; short/long, the lesser/greater length of the shown strips. Review fewest/most from u02-5. Frame: "This belongs here because ___." Teach exemplar meaning before tasks; no shape-name reading. Read all print aloud; no independent reading. Enlarge high-contrast/tactile pictures, use broad pencils, and accept pointing, signing or aided communication. Adult movement follows exact child directions; record help. A scribe records reasons, never selects/writes a numeral on a numeral-writing check. Separate motor formation from quantity understanding. Extend within ten by changing spacing/orientation and defending unchanged quantity, not a new operation.
