# Section 2.7: Sort, count and complete picture orders


## 1. Standards and practices


**Share.** K.MD.B.3: "Classify", "count" every category, "sort" categories by count, all taught day 41 before application. Each group at most ten; match shape exemplars without a geometry-name prerequisite. Day 41 teaches short/long category labels using individual exemplar strips, without measuring or the exit cards. Review B.4b invariance, B.5 count-out and A.3 labels within ten. C.6/C.7 retrieve more/fewer/same by quantities/numerals; day 44 collects missing evidence for these and B.4c only.

MP.3, day 42: justify larger category. MP.7, day 41: identify a full five in F1. Teacher pages distinguish category rule, group count and category order. No algorithm, operation/inequality symbols, preference survey or personal food report.

**F4, K.MD.B.3.** Day 42 independent ten-minute task: fresh mixed sticker-shape pictures, two circles, four squares, six triangles, category mats with examples. Separately read "Sort these pictures onto their mats." "Write each group's number." "Put the mats from fewest to most." Keys: 2 circles, 4 squares, 6 triangles; circle, square, triangle. Shown: all correctly grouped, counted, labeled and ordered. Wrong: triangle on square mat (F); one square omitted while counting (K); correct counts ordered 6/4/2 (G). Recognizable reversed numerals get formation feedback.

Shape: Independent three-category shape classification, counts and ascending mat order. No sticker shapes, 2/4/6 distribution or complete independent three-mat sort/order in practice; use adult-started color-square groups with some counts/order supplied. F4 uses fresh cards, overriding the unit's reused-card direction to protect its case. Written feedback after class day 42, fifteen teacher minutes; revision five minutes day 43, judgment from record day 44.

**E41, K.MD.B.3.** Mixed four mitten/seven sock pictures, two icon mats. Read "Make the sorted picture with a ring around the pile with more." Key: mittens together, socks together, seven-sock pile ringed; one integrated sorted-comparison picture. Six follow-up: "How does your picture show more?" Shown: "Seven socks; four mittens; these have extras." Wrong: mitten on sock mat (F); acknowledges sock extras but calls socks fewer (I).

Shape: New two-pile object sort with larger-pile recognition. No mittens/socks, 4/7 case or independent two-mat larger-pile sheet in practice; correct partial three-color groups and given count order.

**E42, K.MD.B.3.** Ten rectangles initially sorted striped/plain, five each. Two short striped, four short plain, three long striped, one long plain. New mats show short/long examples. Read "Make the new length sort with a ring around the pile with more." Key: six short, four long; short ringed, membership determined by length. Six follow-up: "What rule did you use?" Shown: "Short here, long there." Wrong: keeps pattern groups despite length rule (F); revisits one short while counting (L), only when observed.

Shape: Reclassify a sorted two-property set under a new rule, indicate largest. No striped/plain length cards, 6/4 redistribution or full re-sort comparison sheet in practice; change rules in supplied partly completed color/border pictures, discuss corrected counts.

**E43, K.CC.B.5.** Fresh blank paper pizza, order card mushroom icon/3 and pepper-piece icon/6 read aloud, ten spare paper pictures of each. Read "Fill this picture order." Key: exactly three mushrooms/six pepper pieces attached and outlined on paper. Six follow-up: "How did you know when to stop?" Shown: "The order said three mushrooms and six peppers." Wrong: keeps adding past six (D); omits a requested piece (K), only if observed. No appended writing/comparison ask.

Shape: Complete unfamiliar two-food order on a blank pizza. No mushrooms/pepper pieces, 3/6 order or blank full-order pizza in practice; repair partly filled five-ring/eight-strip orders and compare packed orders/supplied labels.

## 6. Days

Tools: None throughout. Children hold pieces and paper; teacher retains dated component records, originals, written feedback and revisions separately. Fifteen pairs work concurrently: two 45-second turns take 1.5 minutes per round, leaving handling/checking time. At most two public volunteers; passing is allowed. One puppet actor per scene, one spoken ask per turn. Everyone outside a conference returns a paper trace/corrected diagram.

Return and repair returns prior paper with feedback and holds one five-minute action. More than ten of thirty sharing a letter triggers the class version; otherwise a small group while others correct familiar paper. Queue additional letters across later slots. Never run a teacher-led repair during a teacher conference. Use the no-kit stand-ins copied above; adult-cut large paper pieces and concurrent table-tray cleanup avoid cutting/sink queues.

## 9. Supports and extension

Language: sort, category, rule; review fewest/most from u02-5, planned only, demonstrate afresh; "This belongs here because ___." Teach exemplar meaning before tasks; no shape-name reading. Read all print aloud; no independent reading. Enlarge high-contrast/tactile pictures, use broad pencils, and accept pointing, signing or aided communication. Extend within ten by changing spacing/orientation and defending unchanged quantity, not a new operation.
