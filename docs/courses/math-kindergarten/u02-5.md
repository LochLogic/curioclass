Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 2, Numbers 1 to 10: see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 34 to 36.** Days by kind: understanding 2, fluency 1, application 0, check 0. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** K.CC.A.3: "Write" and "Represent" quantities with numerals, only 0 to 10. K.CC.B.4b: "Understand" totals and both invariances. K.CC.B.5: "Count" arranged/scattered sets and count out requests within ten; unit 6 completes twenty. Teach fewest/most and order on day 36 before the stair exit, and greater/more and less/fewer as equivalent comparison words before fluency. Day 36 begins K.CC.B.4c, "Understand" one larger, and K.CC.C.7, "Compare" written numerals, before fluency day 38. No algorithm or operation symbols.

Review u02-4 numeral/zero links through a fresh demonstration. Formation precedes unaided writing. MP.7, day 36: connect one extra square with the next number. MP.3: justify ordered towers by counting, not height alone. Explain recognizable reversals as formation feedback and distinguish quantity, number word and numeral.

## 9. Supports and extension

Language review: numeral/number label, the written mark naming how many; zero, the number for none. Day 36 teaches tower, equal squares stacked; next, the following count word; one more, exactly one additional object; order, fewest to most; fewest/most, smallest/largest count; greater/more and less/fewer connect the same comparisons to numerals. Frame: "This label tells ___ pieces." Explain tower as equal stacked squares, not measured height. Read all print aloud; no independent reading. Enlarge high-contrast/tactile pictures, use broad pencils, and accept pointing, signing or aided communication. Adult movement follows exact child directions; record help. A scribe records reasons, never selects/writes a numeral on a numeral-writing check. Separate motor formation from quantity understanding. Extend within ten by changing spacing/orientation and defending unchanged quantity, not a new operation.
