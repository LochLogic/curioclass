# Section 2.5: Write quantity labels and order number towers

## 1. Standards and practices


**Share.** K.CC.A.3: "Write" and "Represent" quantities with numerals, only 0 to 10. K.CC.B.4b: "Understand" totals and both invariances. K.CC.B.5: "Count" arranged/scattered sets and count out requests within ten; unit 6 completes twenty. Teach fewest/most and order on day 36 before the stair exit, and greater/more and less/fewer as equivalent comparison words before fluency. Day 36 begins K.CC.B.4c, "Understand" one larger, and K.CC.C.7, "Compare" written numerals, before fluency day 38. No algorithm or operation symbols.

Review u02-4 numeral/zero links through a fresh demonstration. Formation precedes unaided writing. MP.7, day 36: connect one extra square with the next number. MP.3: justify ordered towers by counting, not height alone. Explain recognizable reversals as formation feedback and distinguish quantity, number word and numeral.

## 6. Days

Tools: None throughout. Children hold pieces and paper; teacher retains dated component records, originals, written feedback and revisions separately. Fifteen pairs work concurrently: two 45-second turns take 1.5 minutes per round, leaving handling/checking time. At most two public volunteers; passing is allowed. One puppet actor per scene, one spoken ask per turn. Everyone outside a conference returns a paper trace/corrected diagram.

Return and repair returns prior paper with feedback and holds one five-minute action. More than ten of thirty sharing a letter triggers the class version; otherwise a small group while others correct familiar paper. Queue additional letters across later slots. Never run a teacher-led repair during a teacher conference. Use the no-kit stand-ins copied above; adult-cut large paper pieces and concurrent table-tray cleanup avoid cutting/sink queues.

## Day 34

**Shared shape.** Labels beside a counted and empty bowl. No bowls, feathers, paired full/empty labeling or group-to-card selection in practice; begin with supplied bag labels and verify contents, then guided formation.

## 9. Supports and extension

Language: numeral, zero, none, tower, next, one more; "This label tells ___ pieces." Explain tower as equal stacked squares, not measured height. Read all print aloud; no independent reading. Enlarge high-contrast/tactile pictures, use broad pencils, and accept pointing, signing or aided communication. Adult movement follows exact child directions; record help. A scribe records reasons, never selects/writes a numeral on a numeral-writing check. Separate motor formation from quantity understanding. Extend within ten by changing spacing/orientation and defending unchanged quantity, not a new operation.
