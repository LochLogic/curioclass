## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.OA.A.4** (days 97, 98, 99, 100): For any number from 1 to 9, find the number that makes 10 when added to the given number, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record the answer with a drawing or equation.
- **K.OA.A.3** (days 97, 98): Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
- skill mk-make-ten (grade K)
- skill mk-decompose-to-10 (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions.** What stays the same when parts change, day 89? Can two different answers both fit a story, day 95? How does a full frame help find a missing part, day 97?
>
> **Practices.** MP.8: day 89, move one cube between parts and explain why one grows while the other shrinks. MP.7: day 97, use five-and-some and empty spaces on a frame to explain a partner of ten. Hear both in the final record.
> **Rigor and fluency.** Seven understanding, four fluency and five application days give 43.75, 25 and 31.25 percent of teaching days. No owned standard sets a fluency threshold. Days 89 and 99 to 101 rehearse taught relationships. Continue daily untimed games within five; unit 8 owns fluent arithmetic.
> **Carried.** Keep unit 4's optional frames. Tie split collections to drawings on day 87 and total-left equations on day 91. On days 97 and 98 connect occupied and empty spaces to equations for ten. Withdraw supplied frames from practice on day 101; children may choose them afterward. F2's printed frames are the item's picture, not a support. Unit 6 receives the full-ten model.
