## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.OA.A.3** (days 91, 92, 93, 94, 95, 96): Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
- **K.OA.A.1** (days 91): Represent addition and subtraction with objects, fingers, mental images, drawings, sounds (e.g., claps), acting out situations, verbal explanations, expressions, or equations.
- **K.OA.A.2** (days 92, 93, 94, 95, 96): Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings to represent the problem.
- skill mk-decompose-to-10 (grade K)
- skill mk-add-sub-expressions (grade K)
- skill mk-story-problems (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions.** What stays the same when parts change, day 89? Can two different answers both fit a story, day 95? How does a full frame help find a missing part, day 97?
<!-- /skel -->

K.OA.A.3, "Decompose" and "record": drawings/total-left equations, then labeled story parts. K.OA.A.1, "Represent": day 91 ties quantities to both equation orders. K.OA.A.2, "Solve": total-unknown/both-addends-unknown stories, joining/removal review day 96. Limits: totals at most ten; no comparison story or unknown-position equation. MP.4, day 94: one mark per story object; MP.8, day 95: changed counts, fixed whole. Explain: unlabelled 1/5 and 5/1 repeat a pair, while one striped/five plain and five striped/one plain are different labeled story solutions. Equals names equal quantities, never an instruction to answer next.
