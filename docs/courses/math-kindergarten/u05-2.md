## 1. Standards and practices

K.OA.A.3, "Decompose" and "record": drawings/total-left equations, then labeled story parts. K.OA.A.1, "Represent": day 91 ties quantities to both equation orders. K.OA.A.2, "Solve": total-unknown/both-addends-unknown stories, joining/removal review day 96. Limits: totals at most ten; no comparison story or unknown-position equation. MP.4, day 94: one mark per story object; MP.8, day 95: changed counts, fixed whole. Explain: unlabeled 1/5 and 5/1 repeat a pair, while one striped/five plain and five striped/one plain are different labeled story solutions. Equals names equal quantities, never an instruction to answer next.

## 3. Checks, written first

Read each quoted stem aloud as one ask. Every child marks a paper picture; directing an adult to mark is available. Exits last eight minutes: one to launch, six for simultaneous independent paper work while six named children each have a 45-second listening turn, one to collect. Use five rotating groups of six, starting group one on day 87; record who was heard. Hear the same ask, without adding another question. Paper establishes its own component; an unheard explanation stays not yet seen. Teacher judgments use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record help separately. A bare wrong number does not establish an error letter.

Print and say "Invented for practice" for every story; Rin is its sole actor. Reserve each exit/check case and Shape across the unit, including repairs and cards. Practice completes or corrects supplied records; evidence requires independent production or a new correspondence. The full-range assessments necessarily revisit finite number pairs, but their cases, layouts and independent response formats are reserved. Timings and anticipated errors are ours, untested.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review u04-4 day 78 equation, equals and same quantity, and u04-2 day 70 retell in the ledger's sense: say the story events back in order. Define striped as lines across a picture and plain as without those lines; picture labels name categories, not reading tasks. Frame: "Rin has ___ altogether; ___ are ___ and ___ are ___." Teach picture labels before practice. Read all print aloud, including equation cards; independent reading is zero. Use large high-contrast pictures, pattern plus color, tactile outlines, nonslip mats and broad crayons. Children may sign, point, dictate or direct an adult to mark paper; record help without changing the goal. No required fingers, public speech or fine cutting. Adult cuts strips/pieces. Supervise large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawn marks. Checks retain quantities and one ask; never supply the missing part. Extend within ten by explaining another valid pair or repeat, never totals above ten.

## 10. Two rooms

At home, use a 30-minute table session: counting 3, model/rehearsal 5, paper work 9, repair 5, exit/conversation 8. Adult alternates handler/observer. Reduce repetition, not evidence or needed repair; split into sittings when needed. One shared classroom screen may enlarge pictures; all tasks have paper masters. No screen/printer: sketch original masters. No equipment queue.
