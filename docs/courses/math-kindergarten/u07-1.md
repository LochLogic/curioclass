# Section 7.1: Compose, count and compare flat builds

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-kindergarten/7/s/7.1), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-kindergarten u07-1.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Kindergarten Math, unit 7, [Solid shapes all around us](s07.md). A plan, not a lesson: these directions and timings are ours, untested. Use DEFAULTS.md for the standing day and page rules.

## 1. Standards and practices

K.G.B.5, "Model"/"building"/"drawing", and K.G.B.6, "Compose": apply u03-4/u03-5 construction. K.CC.A.3, "Write"/"Represent", and K.CC.B.5, "Count"/"count out": pieces/numerals day 120. K.CC.C.6, "Identify": group comparison day 121. K.OA.A.2, "Solve": counting stories day 122. Retain earlier whole-standard records. MP.3 day 121: justify more by matching; MP.7 day 120: boundary versus joins. No algorithm. Counts through twenty arranged/ten scattered; comparisons/calculation within ten. Explain piece count versus area covered; turning changes neither quantity nor name. A square is also a rectangle.

## 2. Where it sits

Receive u06-4 unresolved counting/teen components in ten-minute centers days 120/121. Retrieval follows day 120's hook, no readiness check. E122 goes to day 123 Return and repair; compositions feed equations.

## 3. Checks, written first

All evidence is judged=teacher. Read one stem, wait for its response, then read the next. Every child marks paper, including a sketch or trace of a build; children read no prompt independently. Hear a rotating six, one 45-second ask each, and record their names. On two-ask tickets hear three per ask; the other component uses paper evidence. Unheard language remains not yet seen. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Samples below distinguish independent success from a recorded error; record any prompt as help. Print and say "Invented for practice" for each invented story scene. One actor acts in each scene. Reserve all exit and check cases across teaching, games, repairs and home practice.

**Unit assessment items owned:** none new. Carry unit 3 F3/F4, unit 6 F1/F2, unit 2 F2 and unit 4/5 story records as s07 specifies. Day 120 supplies diagnostic evidence. No unit 7 end-of-unit item is administered here.

  Shape: solve own novel question. Practice corrects supplied story records, never invents and solves a whole story.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: compose, outline, gap, overlap, count, numeral, more, fewer, same, story. Frame: "This has more because ___." Review these words with their earlier shapes and gestures; do not assume that a written plan establishes an individual child's security.

Read everything aloud; independent reading is zero. Offer large tactile outlines, high contrast, nonslip mats and broad pencils. Accept signing, home-language explanations, communication aids and directed adult movement. Scribe dictated labels, record help, never judge fine-motor skill. Patterns supplement colors; adults cut pieces. Every picture has a tactile model if needed. No public speech required. Extension: another solution or another feature rule within the same standard.

## 10. Two rooms

One child: 30-minute sitting, count 3, demonstrate 5, repair 5, paper/building 9, exit 8. Adult/child alternate builder/checker. Split longer evidence into another sitting, preserving later revision. One optional shared screen enlarges pictures only; paper and objects serve every task without a queue. No printer: hand-draw masters.
