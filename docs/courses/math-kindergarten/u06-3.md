Kindergarten Math. Unit 6, Numbers 0 to 20: see [s06.md](s06.md). **Days 112 to 115.** Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 1. A plan, not a lesson; nothing here has been tried with children. Read with [DEFAULTS.md](DEFAULTS.md).

## 1. Standards and practices

K.NBT.A.1, "decompose"/"record": connect visible teen parts to drawings/expressions/equations; K.CC.A.3, "Write"/"Represent", K.CC.B.5, "Count": rehearse quantity labels day 115. Connect K.OA.A.1 representation language day 113. MP.7 day 113: map visible parts to symbols; MP.6 day 114: whole/parts agree. Decomposition eleven through nineteen. No algorithm, computation strategy above ten or unknown-position equations. Adult explanation: 14 = 10 + 4 records visible composition; equals relates equal quantities, reversing sides preserves relation. Teach notation before checking.

## 2. Where it sits

Day 112 receives E111 F. u06-2 frames become records; u06-4 receives day 115 products/feedback and E115 needs. No added readiness battery. The final window reserves 28 center minutes for conferences and independent paper work.

## 3. Checks, written first

Read each stem aloud as one ask. Every child marks the paper picture; usual directed adult marking remains available. Judge only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; retain initial performance and help. The eight-minute exit has one minute to launch, six for simultaneous paper work and six rotating 45-second listening turns, and one to collect. Record the names heard. With two items, hear three children on each; unheard words remain not yet seen, never inferred from a numeral or peer speech. A wrong total alone does not establish an error letter. Return annotated paper in the next day's Return and repair, reaching children not heard.

Print and say "Invented for practice" on every numerical picture. Rin is the sole actor if a story is used. Reserve each evidence case and Shape across models, cards, practice and repairs. Practice corrects supplied partial records or uses movable squares; evidence asks independent production or correspondence. Finite numerals must recur in full-range evidence, but pictures, layouts and response formats stay fresh. Timings and predicted errors are ours, untested.

**Unit assessment items owned:** none; section 6.1 retains F1 to F4. F1/F3 begin here; u06-4 closes all four. Its field 3 holds complete directions/keys. Day 115 gives F1 zero/eleven-to-twenty and F3 composition eight independent paper minutes each inside centers. Read each row/target separately beforehand. No coaching/peer keys; unfinished rows pending.

**E112, K.NBT.A.1.** Nineteen fresh leaves in a line, no choices. "Make a record that splits this number into ten ones and further ones." Whole-label box/part space. Key/look-for: ten/nine, whole 19. Shown: ten enclosed/nine separate, 19; not yet seen: 91 with reversal confirmed (E).

Shape: independently split unframed collection/label whole; practice corrects supplied twelve/fourteen frame splits/traces, never independently records complete unframed split.

**E113, K.NBT.A.1; MP.7.** Seventeen new square outlines on full frame/seven-place row, cards 10 + 7, 1 + 7, 10 + 8 read aloud. "Match this picture to the card that names its two parts." Key/look-for: 10 + 7 with links from 10 to all ten objects/7 to all seven further. Elimination alone is not shown. Shown: correct card/both links; not yet seen: 1 + 7 because frame called one object (F).

Shape: picture-to-expression recognition with positive correspondence; practice builds/corrects model beside one supplied equation, never selects expression cards or uses this picture.

**E114, K.NBT.A.1.** Blank panel, nineteen spoken, no equation frame. "Make a drawing-and-equation record for nineteen." Draw every one; the equation is recorded alongside the picture as part of this single mathematical record. The teacher reads the prompt; no second spoken question is added. Key: ten/nine, 19 = 10 + 9 or 10 + 9 = 19. Shown: nineteen individual marks grouped ten/nine, matching equation; not yet seen: one plus nine said to replace nineteen (F).

Shape: independently produce drawing/equation; practice completes one missing part on already-drawn twelve/fourteen record, never generates whole blank drawing-equation record.

**E115, K.CC.A.3.** Sixteen fresh shells on marked circle, no numeral cards. "Write how many shells there are." Key/look-for: independent 16 matching quantity. Dictation records quantity, not formation. Shown: 16; not yet seen: 61 with reversal confirmed (E).

Shape: unlabeled fixed collection to written numeral; practice matches cards/traces forms beside supplied labels, never independently writes quantity-to-numeral records. F1 remains separate evidence, not rehearsal.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: decompose, expression, equation, plus, equals, same quantity. Frame: "___ names these ___ ones."

Read everything aloud; independent reading is zero. Use large, spaced, tactile, high-contrast pictures, pattern plus color, nonslip mats and broad pencils. Signing, home-language reasoning, pointing, dictation and directed adult movement are available. Preserve quantities/single asks; never supply answers. Record independent numeral formation separately from scribed quantity labels. No required fingers/public speech. Adult-cut paper squares replace counters; drawn frames replace printed frames; handwritten cards replace numeral cards; pencil marks replace crayons. Replace mouthed pieces with drawings. Extend with another tracking route/layout of the same quantity within the same limits.

## 10. Two rooms

One child: 30-minute table sitting, counting 3, demonstration/rehearsal 6, paper/partner work 8, repair 5, exit 8. Adult alternates handler/observer. Split sittings when evidence needs longer; reduce repetition, never goal/access. One shared classroom screen enlarges pictures only; all tasks have paper masters. No screen/printer: sketch masters. No equipment queue. Final-window days: separate sittings as needed for sixteen paper minutes, one three-minute conference across window, later written-feedback revision/exit; never rush into ordinary table pattern.
