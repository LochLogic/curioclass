## 1. Standards and practices

K.CC.A.1, "Count": ones/tens to one hundred, days 146 to 148; day 146 connects decade changes to repeated ones words. K.OA.A.5, "Fluently add and subtract": familiar facts within five, days 147 to 151. No new arithmetic procedure. MP.8 day 146 explains transition with ten groups of ten individual ones. MP.1 comes from retained days 144/145, not recollected.

No hundred chart, written numerals beyond twenty, number line, bundled ten as one object, unknown-position equation or algorithm. Supplied frames withdraw day 149, choice remains. Explain accurate counting versus known fact/quick strategy/counting all. Counting all is not a misconception; four sampled facts cannot establish every fact's fluency. No child-facing timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

Decade is a teacher planning word; children use spoken number words. Ones means counting one object at a time; tens means moving a whole visible ten-group per spoken count. One hundred is ten of these ten-object groups. Day 146 connects these labels to the familiar count. Review part, whole, add, take away and zero. Point out hidden as covered and visible as what can still be seen before day 147 practice; no vocabulary test. Frame: "I know ___ because ___." Independent reading zero. Read all aloud, offer large/tactile dots, patterned halves, directed adult marks, home language/signs/usual aids. Record mathematical help separately. Extend another transition/representation of same permitted fact, not larger arithmetic.
