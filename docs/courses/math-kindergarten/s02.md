<!-- skel -->
- **K.CC.B.4:** Understand the relationship between numbers and quantities; connect counting to cardinality. Parts, by verb: b (K.CC.B.4b) Understand that the last number name said tells the number of objects counted. The number of objects is the same regardless of their arrangement or the order in which they were counted.; c (K.CC.B.4c) Understand that each successive number name refers to a quantity that is one larger.
- **~K.CC.B.5:** (begun here; the full expectation is owned and assessed later) Count to answer “how many?” questions about as many as 20 things arranged in a line, a rectangular array, or a circle, or as many as 10 things in a scattered configuration; given a number from 1–20, count out that many objects. Parts, by verb: whole standard.
- **~K.CC.A.3:** (begun here; the full expectation is owned and assessed later) Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20 (with 0 representing a count of no objects). Parts, by verb: whole standard.
- **K.CC.C.6:** Identify whether the number of objects in one group is greater than, less than, or equal to the number of objects in another group, e.g., by using matching and counting strategies. Parts, by verb: whole standard.
- **K.CC.C.7:** Compare two numbers between 1 and 10 presented as written numerals. Parts, by verb: whole standard.
- **K.MD.B.3:** Classify objects into given categories; count the numbers of objects in each category and sort the categories by count. Parts, by verb: whole standard.
