**Day 83:** close F1/F2 originals, feedback and revisions using owning sections' look-fors/samples. No repeated secure interviews. Score earlier-made work only after written feedback/revision. First-made/first-observed day 83 evidence stays pending until later revision/scoring. Keep day 64 diagnostic separate. One previously identified gap task per child, independent application of earlier feedback, no new score. Single asks read aloud with paper: A, "Mark each wheel as you count all" (four and five, nine; visibly skipped one gives eight, A); B, "Enclose all the wheels" (five blue/four yellow, nine; only yellow four, B); C, "Show how many wheels stay" (ten with four moved to bin, six; joins to fourteen, C); D, "Join this nine-dot picture to every equation that fits" (5 + 4 = 9 and 9 = 5 + 4, both; rejects second solely for order, D); E, "Mark the kites still on the hill" (ten with one moved by Rin, nine; marks removed one, E); F, "Show the tiles after none join" (eight stays eight; adds ninth, F); G, "Show the tiles after one joins" (eight becomes nine; unchanged eight, G); H, "Show both tile groups together" (five and five, ten; removes incoming five, zero, H). **A Shape:** independent tracking across two new wheel groups; practice uses supplied routes, never these groups.

**B Shape:** independently enclose the whole across colored wheel groups; practice corrects supplied incomplete loops, never these wheels.

**C Shape:** independently show the remaining wheel group after a move; practice repairs an already-moved square layout.

**D Shape:** independently connect one dot picture to both equation orders; practice jointly builds sides, never picture-to-many-equation matching.

**E Shape:** independently identify the kites still at the named location; practice corrects a supplied wrong question-group mark.

**F Shape:** independently show a fresh group after no arrival; practice corrects supplied four-plus-zero records.

**G Shape:** independently show a fresh group after one arrival; practice corrects supplied four-plus-one records.

**H Shape:** independently bring two tile groups into one whole; practice follows supplied movement cues, never this tile layout.

These are targeted applications of prior written feedback, never reteaching/repeated assessment. Reserve wheel/kite/tile/dot cases from every practice/model/card. Bare answers do not establish error letters.

Four separate 45-second asks each: F2 joining model, F2 removal model, F1 joining picture/equations, F1 removal picture/equations.
