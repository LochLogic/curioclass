## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.G.A.1** (days 50, 51): Describe objects in the environment using names of shapes, and describe the relative positions of these objects using terms such as above, below, beside, in front of, behind, and next to.
- **K.G.A.2** (days 50, 51): Correctly name shapes regardless of their orientations or overall size.
- **K.MD.A.1** (days 51, 52): Describe measurable attributes of objects, such as length or weight. Describe several measurable attributes of a single object.
- **K.MD.A.2** (days 51, 52): Directly compare two objects with a measurable attribute in common, to see which object has “more of”/“less of” the attribute, and describe the difference. For example, directly compare the heights of two children and describe one child as taller/shorter.
- **K.G.B.5** (days 52): Model shapes in the world by building shapes from components (e.g., sticks and clay balls) and drawing shapes.
- **K.G.B.6** (days 52): Compose simple shapes to form larger shapes. For example, “Can you join these two triangles with full sides touching to make a rectangle?”
- skill mk-shapes-positions (grade K)
- skill mk-measurable-attributes (grade K)
- skill mk-build-compose-shapes (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Teaching moves.** Model all six position words on day 45 with a fixed viewing direction. Contrast examples and nonexamples on day 50; days 51 and 52 line up ends as a plain routine, and day 54 teaches why ends must meet. On days 49, 52 and 57 the fifteen connected-work minutes open with partners telling where a piece or card goes, using two of the six position words. Step back during day 61 puzzles. Teacher pages explain that turning preserves shape and length, a square is a special rectangle, and capacity differs from height.

## 9. Supports and extension

No independent reading. Read all print aloud. Teach words with objects and gestures; remove language frames from independent checks. Offer large high-contrast or tactile boundaries, nonslip mats, broad pencils, signing and usual communication aids. A scribe records words without supplying them. Extend with another example or fit on the same standard, never units or new arithmetic. Words: triangle, circle, square, rectangle, closed, equal sides, longer, shorter, join. Frame: "These sides are ___." Teach equal length separately from planned u02-2 equal counts.
