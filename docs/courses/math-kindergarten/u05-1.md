## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.OA.A.3** (days 87, 88, 89, 90): Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
- skill mk-decompose-to-10 (grade K)
- skill mk-add-sub-expressions (grade K)

> **Practices.** MP.8: day 89, move one cube between parts and explain why one grows while the other shrinks. MP.7: day 97, use five-and-some and empty spaces on a frame to explain a partner of ten. Hear both in the final record.

Days 89 and 99 to 101 rehearse taught relationships. Continue daily untimed games within five; unit 8 owns fluent arithmetic.
> **Carried.** Keep unit 4's optional frames. Tie split collections to drawings on day 87 and total-left equations on day 91. On days 97 and 98 connect occupied and empty spaces to equations for ten. Withdraw supplied frames from practice on day 101; children may choose them afterward. F2's printed frames are the item's picture, not a support. Unit 6 receives the full-ten model.
<!-- /skel -->

K.OA.A.3, "Decompose" and "record": two exhaustive, disjoint parts, with more than one pair where possible. Totals zero to ten; zero has only 0 and 0, and one has only the unordered pair 0 and 1. Empty parts count. Reversing an unlabelled pair does not make a different pair. K.OA.A.1 connects objects/drawings day 87. MP.8, day 89: moving one makes one part grow and the other shrink. Teach this on day 87 so day 89 rehearses it. Teacher explanation: count pieces rather than area; a large shape is still one piece. No algorithm or speed threshold.
