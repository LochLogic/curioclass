## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Distinguish flat shapes from solid objects by handling and sorting.
- **Note:** Base K.7.7. Daily oral count by tens to 100, with ones practice. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Data: authored sixty-minute budget; exit: half. Extra paper items on 125/130 are independent student-page Exit boxes. Read all print aloud. Shared counting/game rows reused. Six 45-second exit samples, one ask each; unheard language not inferred. E122 questions reach all thirty by day 127. Final-window paper 130/131, roster 6/8/8/8 conferences on 130 to 133; feedback and later revision before 134 closeout.

- mk-analyze-shapes (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.G.A.3:** Identify shapes as two-dimensional (lying in a plane, “flat”) or three-dimensional (“solid”).
- **K.G.B.4:** Analyze and compare two- and three-dimensional shapes, in different sizes and orientations, using informal language to describe their similarities, differences, parts (e.g., number of sides and vertices/“corners”) and other attributes (e.g., having sides of equal length).
