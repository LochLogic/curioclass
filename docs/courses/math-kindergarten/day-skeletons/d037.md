## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Add one counter and name the next quantity without recounting.
- **Note:** Base K.2.18. Read every label aloud. Supported oral count toward ten, growing toward twenty; assessed collections stay within ten. Counting cohort 16 to 20: five separate 45-second asks plus notes, four minutes each, twenty total. Feedback after class; next-day repair/response revision. Mid-unit response: one five-minute repair and five-minute fresh transfer from completed evidence, not incomplete rosters.

**Skills:**

- mk-one-more (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.CC.B.4c:** Understand that each successive number name refers to a quantity that is one larger.
