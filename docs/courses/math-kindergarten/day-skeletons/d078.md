## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Connect addition and subtraction expressions to story actions.
- **Note:** Base K.4.14. Tie +, - and = to the model; use the total on the left as often as on the right for equations. Daily oral count and count-on within the known sequence. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known.
