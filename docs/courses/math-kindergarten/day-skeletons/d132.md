> **Digital tools: None.** Paper shapes, drawn outlines, pencils, frames and large paper counters cover all tasks. Inspect packaging for staples, sharp edges and residues; no glass or metal cans (general early-grade reference, field 10; the packaging and clay rules are ours, from memory). Check clay ingredients against known allergies or use paper joins. Supervise handling, stop mouthing or throwing, and keep builds low on tables. Paper drawings record builds; teacher models provide solid features when a drawing alone cannot.
