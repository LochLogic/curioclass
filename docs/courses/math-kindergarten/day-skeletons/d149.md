- **Note:** Base K.8.14. Daily oral count by ones and tens to 100. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Year-end direct fluency station days 149 to 151, after that day's practice: ten assigned children daily, four 45-second asks and 15-second transition each, 32.5 observation minutes plus 2.5 setup/tidy for ten assigned children.
