## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Write and match numerals 11 to 20 and connect them to collections.
- **Note:** Added writing day; retrieve zero to ten including zero as no objects. Complete the written range 0 to 20. Oral count-on starts from a spoken number, without using addition. Daily oral count by tens to 100, with ones practice. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Unit 6 final window: retain numeral and teen products, then hear assigned counting domains once, 20 minutes in centers. Data: authored section blocks control timing; exit: half. Read all print aloud. Everyone marks paper; six rotating 45-second oral samples, three per panel when two. Formation and unheard words recorded separately. Original masters separate from practice; familiar games untimed. Final window: 28 center minutes days 115 to 118; assigned roster eight/eight/eight/six, four 45-second asks each. Paper 16/16/8/0 minutes. Feedback after class; revise next day before judgment. Day 119 revision precedes ten-minute closeout.

**Skills:**

- mk-count-to-20 (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.CC.A.3:** Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20 (with 0 representing a count of no objects).
- **K.CC.B.5:** Count to answer “how many?” questions about as many as 20 things arranged in a line, a rectangular array, or a circle, or as many as 10 things in a scattered configuration; given a number from 1–20, count out that many objects.
