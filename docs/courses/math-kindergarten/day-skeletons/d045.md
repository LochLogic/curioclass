## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Describe classroom shapes and their positions with all six position words.
- **Note:** Base K.3.13. Move before social studies map unit 3, school days 47 to 71, weeks 10 to 15. Teach above, below, beside, in front of, behind and next to with room objects and simple cards. Retrieval task: count a group and use its numeral. Unit 2 response in centers. Daily oral count to 10 or 20. Same-year retrieval, not assessment evidence: mk-how-many.

**Skills:**

- mk-shapes-positions (grade K): taught or practised here
- mk-how-many (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.G.A.1:** Describe objects in the environment using names of shapes, and describe the relative positions of these objects using terms such as above, below, beside, in front of, behind, and next to.
- **K.G.A.2:** Correctly name shapes regardless of their orientations or overall size.

> **Digital tools: None.** Paper cards, large pieces and drawings carry every task; no calculator is needed. Supply blank paper, pencils, tactile outlines and a viewing-point arrow. Teacher prepares all cuts. Day 52 uses blunt strips and soft paper joins if clay is unsuitable. Check allergies and exclude small swallowable joins; keep materials out of mouths and wash hands afterward. Days 55 and 56 use light, sealed objects and unbreakable vessels. Pour small amounts of plain room-temperature water over a tray; stop for spills and dry the floor immediately. No tasting, food, glass or sand. A child may direct the adult's pour and mark the paper record; dry paper scoops are for handling rehearsal, not capacity evidence.
