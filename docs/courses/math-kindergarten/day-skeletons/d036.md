## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Order towers and numerals 1 to 10 and explain each next quantity.
- **Note:** Base K.2.17. Read every label aloud. Supported oral count toward ten, growing toward twenty; assessed collections stay within ten. Counting cohort 11 to 15: five separate 45-second asks plus notes, four minutes each, twenty total. Feedback after class; next-day repair/response revision. Introduce one more; this new-content exit is formative, not mid-unit assessment evidence. M3 revises in five Record minutes.  Exit samples ordering 4 to 6 within the taught 1 to 10 range; s02-O36 supplies separately heard reasons for six children only, not all-child reasoning evidence.

**Skills:**

- mk-how-many (grade K): taught or practised here
- mk-one-more (grade K): taught or practised here
- mk-compare-numerals (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.CC.B.4b:** Understand that the last number name said tells the number of objects counted. The number of objects is the same regardless of their arrangement or the order in which they were counted.
- **K.CC.B.5:** Count to answer “how many?” questions about as many as 20 things arranged in a line, a rectangular array, or a circle, or as many as 10 things in a scattered configuration; given a number from 1–20, count out that many objects.
- **K.CC.A.3:** Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20 (with 0 representing a count of no objects).
- **K.CC.B.4c:** Understand that each successive number name refers to a quantity that is one larger.
- **K.CC.C.7:** Compare two numbers between 1 and 10 presented as written numerals.
