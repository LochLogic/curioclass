## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Complete the addition and subtraction record and respond to model or symbol errors.
- **Note:** Window days 79 to 83: one record for both operations within ten; include joining, taking from, putting together and taking apart, spoken or acted representations and matched drawings, expressions and equations with totals on either side. Both-addends-unknown stories are the frame-required continuation in unit 5 and checked on day 103. Same-day small-group response; no separate unit 4 response time on day 86. MP.4 and MP.2: connect each model and symbol to a quantity in the story. Closing-day budget: ten minutes finish assigned evidence, ten minutes interpret the existing record, twenty minutes respond in two small groups while others use independent centers; remaining twenty minutes hold counting, synthesis and short class work.
