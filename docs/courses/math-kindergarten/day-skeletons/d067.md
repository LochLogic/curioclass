## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Show putting together and adding to with objects, fingers or sounds.
- **Note:** Base K.4.4. Add 10-frame counters beside concrete models for quantities 6 to 10. Include verbal explanations, imagined actions and claps; offer objects instead of fingers. Act out with objects, never children removed from a group. Daily oral count and count-on within the known sequence.
