## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Find pairs systematically and explain when a pair repeats.
- **Note:** Base K.5.4. Daily oral count and count-on within the known sequence. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Optional 10-minute center choice: record all pairs for ten in order, replacing another center; no added requirement. Data: authored 60-minute blocks control copied timings. Read all print aloud; exit: half. All thirty mark paper; six same-ask oral samples, unheard reasons pending. No new method on Fluency/Application days.

**Skills:**

- mk-decompose-to-10 (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.OA.A.3:** Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
