> - **Paper path:** add blank paper, pencils, teacher-cut large shapes and drawn place mats. Paper strips form a flat sculpture on day 17; folded paper models retain solid exploration on day 4. Use classroom supplies, not requested home collections. Follow the safety guide in field 10 for supervision, safer materials and handling, and hand washing; age-label and swallowable-piece checks are ours: check age labels, use non-toxic materials, reject sharp or damaged objects and replace swallowable pieces with large paper shapes. Keep pieces on trays, model keeping them out of mouths and wash hands after handling. Cut paper kites, boats and leaves large enough to move, and a tray.
> - **Access:** demonstrate words with gestures; rehearse "I moved each one once." Adults may scribe explanations. Partners alternate counting and watching. U3 supplies unseen paper exit tickets, read aloud; hear about six rotating children daily. Day 13 offers its optional five-minute tracking collection to twenty, without assessment.
