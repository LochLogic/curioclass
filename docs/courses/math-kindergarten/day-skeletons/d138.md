## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Explain one more and one less for a known collection.
- **Note:** Base K.8.4. Daily oral count by ones and tens to 100. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Read all directions aloud; every child marks paper. Open teacher-key.md privately in Before class. No reserved master in teaching or home practice.

**Skills:**

- mk-one-more (grade K): taught or practised here
- mk-count-on-from (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.CC.B.4c:** Understand that each successive number name refers to a quantity that is one larger.
- **K.CC.A.2:** Count forward beginning from a given number within the known sequence (instead of having to begin at 1).
