## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Count arranged collections to twenty and tell how many.
- **Note:** Base K.6.1. Retrieval task: identify ten on the full unit 5 frame. Unit 5 response in centers; begin daily oral tens to 100. Daily oral count by tens to 100, with ones practice. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Same-year retrieval, not assessment evidence: mk-how-many. Data: authored section blocks control timing; exit: half. Read all print aloud. Everyone marks paper; six rotating 45-second oral samples, three per panel when two. Formation and unheard words recorded separately. Original masters separate from practice; familiar games untimed.

**Skills:**

- mk-count-to-20 (grade K): taught or practised here
- mk-how-many (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.CC.A.3:** Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20 (with 0 representing a count of no objects).
- **K.CC.B.5:** Count to answer “how many?” questions about as many as 20 things arranged in a line, a rectangular array, or a circle, or as many as 10 things in a scattered configuration; given a number from 1–20, count out that many objects.
