> **Digital tools: None.** Paper cards, large pieces and drawings carry every task; no calculator is needed. Supply blank paper, pencils, tactile outlines and a viewing-point arrow. Teacher prepares all cuts. Day 52 uses blunt strips and soft paper joins if clay is unsuitable.
