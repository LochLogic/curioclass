## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Decide which written numeral is greater, less or equal.
- **Note:** Base K.2.21. Read every label aloud. Supported oral count toward ten, growing toward twenty; assessed collections stay within ten. Close last counting cohort after revision. F2 paper nine minutes, F3 paper four, then children 1 to 10 give two separate reasons in twenty minutes.

**Skills:**

- mk-compare-numerals (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.CC.C.7:** Compare two numbers between 1 and 10 presented as written numerals.
