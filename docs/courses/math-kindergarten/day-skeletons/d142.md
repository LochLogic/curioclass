## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Find classroom objects that satisfy number and shape clues.
- **Note:** Base K.8.8. School and classroom objects only; replace any source survey of classmates or homes. No body, family or home questions. Daily oral count by ones and tens to 100. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Read all directions aloud; every child marks paper. Open teacher-key.md privately in Before class. No reserved master in teaching or home practice. Separate new number-clue record in existing connected minutes; written feedback, day 143 revision, later judgment.

**Skills:**

- mk-count-to-20 (grade K): taught or practised here
- mk-analyze-shapes (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.CC.A.3:** Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20 (with 0 representing a count of no objects).
- **K.CC.B.5:** Count to answer “how many?” questions about as many as 20 things arranged in a line, a rectangular array, or a circle, or as many as 10 things in a scattered configuration; given a number from 1–20, count out that many objects.
- **K.G.A.3:** Identify shapes as two-dimensional (lying in a plane, “flat”) or three-dimensional (“solid”).
- **K.G.B.4:** Analyze and compare two- and three-dimensional shapes, in different sizes and orientations, using informal language to describe their similarities, differences, parts (e.g., number of sides and vertices/“corners”) and other attributes (e.g., having sides of equal length).
