## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Represent teen numbers with ten ones and some more on paired frames.
- **Note:** Base K.6.6. Daily oral count by tens to 100, with ones practice. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Data: authored section blocks control timing; exit: half. Read all print aloud. Everyone marks paper; six rotating 45-second oral samples, three per panel when two. Formation and unheard words recorded separately. Original masters separate from practice; familiar games untimed.

**Skills:**

- mk-teen-ten-ones (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.NBT.A.1:** Compose and decompose numbers from 11 to 19 into ten ones and some further ones, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each composition or decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 18 = 10 + 8); understand that these numbers are composed of ten ones and one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, or nine ones.
