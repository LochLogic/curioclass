## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Make ten from each starting number 1 to 9 and record the partner.
- **Note:** Base K.5.13. Daily oral count and count-on within the known sequence. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Unit 5 final window: retain drawings and equations and gather assigned child observations once, 20 minutes in centers. Data: authored 60-minute blocks control copied timings. Read all print aloud; exit: half. All thirty mark paper; six same-ask oral samples, unheard reasons pending. No new method on Fluency/Application days. F1 twenty panels over days 99/100; F2 once day 101. Three separate 45-second asks per conference: roster 1-5 day 101, 6-15 day 102, 16-30 day 103. Feedback, later revision, then judgment; day-103-first evidence stays pending.

**Skills:**

- mk-make-ten (grade K): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **K.OA.A.4:** For any number from 1 to 9, find the number that makes 10 when added to the given number, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record the answer with a drawing or equation.
