## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Break numbers to nine into two parts in more than one way.
- **Note:** Base K.5.1. Retrieval task: match a unit 4 equation to its objects. Continue spaced untimed within-five addition and subtraction in centers, 5 minutes daily. Daily oral count and count-on within the known sequence. Five-minute spaced addition and subtraction game within five, untimed; objects may be dropped as facts become known. Same-year retrieval, not assessment evidence: mk-add-sub-expressions. Data: authored 60-minute blocks control copied timings. Read all print aloud; exit: half. All thirty mark paper; six same-ask oral samples, unheard reasons pending. No new method on Fluency/Application days. Centers: day 86 feedback/revision five, diagnostic quantity repair five, sign repair five, game five; tidy within actions.

- mk-add-sub-expressions (grade K): taught or practised here
