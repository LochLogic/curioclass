# Section 5.4: Explain partners and close the record

Kindergarten Math. A section plan, not a lesson; nothing here has been tried with children. Read with [s05.md](s05.md) and [DEFAULTS.md](DEFAULTS.md).

## 1. Standards and practices

K.OA.A.4, "find" and "record": rehearse/explain partners of ten; K.OA.A.3, "Decompose" and "record": different labeled parts preserve ten; K.OA.A.2, "Solve": story continuation/retained unit 4 joining/removal evidence. Totals at most ten; make-ten starts one through nine. MP.7, day 101 conference: explain ten's structure; MP.8, days 101/102: one-up/one-down leaves the whole fixed. Explain: full-ten frame and tower name the same total, not a new counting unit. No new strategy on fluency/application/check days, algorithm or speed target.

## 2. Where it sits

E100 receives day 101 Return and repair. Close F1/F2/F3 day 103 using earlier products, feedback/revision. E102 feedback/unresolved gaps go to s05's ten-minute centers on each of days 104/105; no extra class time promised.

## 3. Checks, written first

Read each quoted stem aloud as one ask. Every child marks a paper picture; directing an adult to mark is available. Exits last eight minutes: one to launch, six for simultaneous independent paper work while six named children each have a 45-second listening turn, one to collect. Use five rotating groups of six, starting group one on day 87; record who was heard. Hear the same ask, without adding another question. Paper establishes its own component; an unheard explanation stays not yet seen. Teacher judgments use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record help separately. A bare wrong number does not establish an error letter.

Print and say "Invented for practice" for every story; Rin is its sole actor. Reserve each exit/check case and Shape across the unit, including repairs and cards. Practice completes or corrects supplied records; evidence requires independent production or a new correspondence. The full-range assessments necessarily revisit finite number pairs, but their cases, layouts and independent response formats are reserved. Timings and anticipated errors are ours, untested.

**Unit assessment items owned:** none newly owned; F1 remains u05-1, F2 u05-3, F3 u05-2. Administer F2 and close all three here.

**F2, K.OA.A.4; MP.7.** Day 101 ten independent paper minutes: nine fresh frame pictures, starting quantities 4,1,7,3,9,2,6,8,5. Read each separately: "Mark what this frame needs to make ten." Child marks empty cells, the drawing record. Keys in order: 6,9,3,7,1,8,4,2,5. Look-for: preserve all given marks; fill each empty cell once. Shown: seven given/three added, ten; not yet seen: seven added to seven because given part repeats (D), or three empty cells numbered 0,1,2 and reported two (E). Frame explanation is a separate conference ask. Shape: independently complete nine partly occupied frames; practice repairs completed square/tower records, never fills a nine-frame sheet. Every layout differs from E97/E98/E100. Finite partners recur; layouts/independent response format are reserved.

**Conferences, days 101/102/103.** Each child gets one turn with retained F1/F2/F3. Ask separately: "Tell what changes when one mark moves to the other part" (F1, MP.8); "Tell why your partner fills ten" (F2, MP.7); "Tell how your two solutions fit Rin's stamps" (F3). Allow 45 seconds per ask, 2.25 minutes a child plus transitions. Shown samples: "Three grows to four, five shrinks to four, still eight"; "Seven is five and two; the three empty places complete ten"; "Three and one or two and two both use four stamps." Not-yet-seen samples: unchanged 3/5 called different (B); repeats seven as partner (D); one stamp in both albums (F). Equation sample 8 = 3 + 5 matches eight; order-only rejection is C. Use owning-section look-fors. Record each ask separately with initial performance/help and written feedback. Shape: explain one's independently made retained products, never rehearse these explanations with evidence products during practice/model/cards. Never repeat a secure conference; missing explanations stay pending.

- **E101, K.OA.A.4; MP.8.** New tower picture shows ten unit pieces, seven visible, remainder screened, plus blank response box. "Show the partner that completes this tower of ten." Key/look-for: three marks; the paper response connects the three added marks to the visible seven to show a full ten. Six heard responses may explain that relationship; a numeral alone leaves the explanation not yet seen. Wrong sample: seven as its own partner (D). Shape: infer and justify a concealed tower part without a frame; practice checks supplied complete tower splits, never predicts concealed tower partners or uses this picture.
- **E102, K.OA.A.3; K.OA.A.4; K.OA.A.2.** Rin's ten fruit pictures show two peaches/eight plums; blank paper with picture labels. "Tell a fruit story for ten with a different pair from this one." All mark a complete labeled picture story with Rin as the actor; six tell their same response. Paper communicates the mathematical story, never exact unheard wording. All other children are reached through their complete paper scene and next-day written feedback. Key/look-for: one actor, two labeled fruit counts totaling ten, different from 2 peaches/8 plums. Shown sample: "Rin sets out three peaches and seven plums, ten fruit pictures." Wrong sample: repositions the same 2 peaches/8 plums as new (B). Other valid labeled pairs, including 8 peaches/2 plums, count. Shape: independently create a whole fruit story with changed pair; practice completes/corrects apple/banana story fragments, never invents a whole story or uses peaches/plums.

**Day 103:** no new exit/repeated battery. Interpret F1 four, F2 three, F3 three minutes from retained products. Judge earlier-made evidence only after feedback/revision; first-made/first-heard day 103 evidence stays pending for days 104/105. Shape: close existing component records, never recollect complete tasks. Mark only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; pending is a scheduling flag, never another grade.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

| Letter | Wrong beside right; shown day | Receiving slot/move |
|---|---|---|
| D | Day 101: four repeated gives eight versus four/six ten. | E101 to day 102 Return and repair five minutes: preserve given part, fill missing places on a supplied correction record. |
| E | Day 101 Return and repair: five empty cells numbered 0 to 4 versus 1 to 5. | E100 enters this five-minute slot; F2 misses get one day 102 five-minute slot. |
| B | Day 102: ten apples/bananas 4/6 rearranged unchanged versus changed 5/5. | E102 written feedback; days 104/105 centers, no teaching day 103. |
| A | Day 102 supplied six-square repair: 2/3 plus unused one versus 2/4. | Record for day 104 targeted group if still unmet. |
| C | Day 102 supplied 10 = 4 + 6 refused solely for order versus matched quantities. | Targeted response allocation, no third day 102 repair. |
| F | Day 102 ten squares counted 5/6 with one shared versus disjoint 5/5. | Trace each square to one label in its day 104 group. |

More than a third means many. Day 102 Return and repair holds two five-minute actions, E101 D plus first unresolved F2 need; smaller groups use the same minutes while others correct familiar records. More needs get feedback and stay pending for response, never compressed. Day 103 has no reteaching. Day 104 models an assigned component and revises its prior product; day 105 uses s05 field 6's fresh unscored transfer. First unmet component sets one group per child; no promise of six sequential teacher groups in ten minutes.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Carry Full-ten occupied-empty model from u05-3 into a supplied tower split; withdraw supplied practice frames day 101, retain optional access. F2 frames are item content. Children hold paper, teacher retains records. Ideal: movable ten-piece tower beside a completed frame record, fits because each piece has one place. Cubes become marked strips, counters large squares, cups folded covers, frames drawn lines. Digital tools: None; no key tool. Review u01-5 Close retained observation records before check day, written review.

## 6. Days

## Day 101

**Shape:** infer and justify a concealed tower part without a frame; practice checks supplied complete tower splits, never predicts concealed tower partners or uses this picture.

- **5 min. Counting.** Review u02-1 count/total with a fresh loose-square arrangement. All count/point together and return quantity marks; no individual oral round or new sequence.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** E100 E, one five-minute action and fresh partial-record retry.

- **7 min. Rehearsal.** I can explain ten in a tower. Check a supplied complete four/six tower split against a completed frame. No concealed-tower prediction or new content. Withdraw supplied practice frames, optional access retained. Demonstrate closing-record routine before check day. Replace the first four repeated-correction minutes with child-led concrete work: partners direct a complete 10-piece loose-key arrangement on two plain mats, one possible split 0/10, then verify the whole. Each partner has one 45-second directing/explaining turn; two minutes remain for handling/checking and 30 seconds to keep a small sketch. Accept other splits totaling 10, including an empty part. No dot-strip portfolio, blank fixed story picture, paired solution panel, train prediction or independent equation master is used. Teacher checks the sketch within the ordinary after-class budget; return it with that day's exit in the next named repair/revision slot.

- **7 min. Feedback revision.** Return F1/F3 written feedback. Revise earlier paper; teacher circulates for access, never a new lesson.

- **10 min. Partner sheet.** F2 once, independent paper, nine separately read rows; no explanations or simultaneous conferences.

- **18 min. Conferences and centers.** Assigned children 1 to 5: three separate 45-second asks each, 11.25 minutes plus 6.75 for transitions/access/tidy. Others check supplied complete tower splits and play five within-five game minutes with partner/model feedback. Everyone returns paper. Children hold paper/models; teacher keeps component records. Tools: None.

- **8 min. Exit ticket.** E101 all paper, six heard; D to day 102 Return and repair. After class reserve thirty teacher minutes for F2 feedback and five for written conference feedback, no same-period scoring.

## 7. Talk and writing

Questions: day 101, "What matches between this tower and frame?"; day 102, "Did these labeled counts change?" At most two 45-second contributions outside conferences. Written practice day 102: repair supplied four-apple/six-banana story fragment. Shown: corrects total eight to ten; not yet seen: assigns an apple twice (F). Judge correspondence/labels, never reading/artwork. No complete fruit-story generation in practice.

## 8. Practice that returns

Review written u01-1 day 1 Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, and day 2 Return and repair. One handles and one checks, then switch. A paper dot records an indicated object, not unheard speech. Use the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, review of u04-2 day 71: partners correct familiar joining/removal records, compare with the model, and return paper. 

Day 101 rehearses u05-3 days 97/98 partners/u05-1 fixed whole; day 102 rehearses u05-2 day 95 labeled solutions. Day 103 has familiar counting only. All three days include five simultaneous minutes of taught within-five games during conferences, with partner/model feedback, no individual teacher game turns. Owned standards set no fluency target; no timer. Review the written u02-3 frame model and u04-4 equality model before day 103.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review u02-5 tower, u04-4 same quantity, u05-1 whole and u05-3 partner of ten. Use their earlier picture/gesture definitions; frame: "___ and ___ still make ten." Picture fruit with patterned labels, never real food or meal questions. Read all print aloud, including equation cards; independent reading is zero. Use large high-contrast pictures, pattern plus color, tactile outlines, nonslip mats and broad crayons. Children may sign, point, dictate or direct an adult to mark paper; record help without changing the goal. No required fingers, public speech or fine cutting. Adult cuts strips/pieces. Supervise large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawn marks. Checks retain quantities and one ask; never supply the missing part. Extend within ten by explaining another valid pair or repeat, never totals above ten.

## 10. Two rooms

At home, use a 30-minute table session: counting 3, model/rehearsal 5, paper work 9, repair 5, exit/conversation 8. Adult alternates handler/observer. Reduce repetition, not evidence or needed repair; split into sittings when needed. One shared classroom screen may enlarge pictures; all tasks have paper masters. No screen/printer: sketch original masters. No equipment queue. Days 101/102: replace repetition with ten F2 minutes once, a 2.25-minute conference once, five revision minutes; split sittings if needed. Day 103 one child: familiar work 5, pending explanation 3, interpret 7, revise 10, synthesis/tidy 5. No new scored task; response remains days 104/105.
