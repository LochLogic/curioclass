**F2, K.OA.A.4; MP.7.** Day 101 ten independent paper minutes: nine fresh frame pictures, starting quantities 4,1,7,3,9,2,6,8,5. Read each separately: "Mark what this frame needs to make ten." Child marks empty cells, the drawing record. Keys in order: 6,9,3,7,1,8,4,2,5. Look-for: preserve all given marks; fill each empty cell once. Shown: seven given/three added, ten; not yet seen: seven added to seven because given part repeats (D), or three empty cells numbered 0,1,2 and reported two (E). Every layout differs from E97/E98/E100. Finite partners recur; layouts/independent response format are reserved.

**Conferences, days 101/102/103.** Each child gets one turn with retained F1/F2/F3. Ask separately: "Tell what changes when one mark moves to the other part" (F1, MP.8); "Tell why your partner fills ten" (F2, MP.7); "Tell how your two solutions fit Rin's stamps" (F3). Allow 45 seconds per ask, 2.25 minutes a child plus transitions. Shown samples: "Three grows to four, five shrinks to four, still eight"; "Five and two fill seven, three places were empty"; "Three and one or two and two both use four stamps." Not-yet-seen samples: unchanged 3/5 called different (B); repeats seven as partner (D); one stamp in both albums (F). Equation sample 8 = 3 + 5 matches eight; order-only rejection is C. Use owning-section look-fors. Record each ask separately with initial performance/help and written feedback. Shape: explain one's independently made retained products, never rehearse these explanations with evidence products during practice/model/cards. Never repeat a secure conference; missing explanations stay pending.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: tower, partner of ten, whole, same quantity; frame: "___ and ___ still make ten." Picture fruit with patterned labels, never real food or meal questions. Read all print aloud, including equation cards; independent reading is zero. Use large high-contrast pictures, pattern plus color, tactile outlines, nonslip mats and broad crayons. Children may sign, point, dictate or direct an adult to mark paper; record help without changing the goal. No required fingers, public speech or fine cutting. Adult cuts strips/pieces. Supervise large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawn marks. Checks retain quantities and one ask; never supply the missing part. Extend within ten by explaining another valid pair or repeat, never totals above ten.
