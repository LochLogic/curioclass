## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.OA.A.4** (days 101, 102, 103): For any number from 1 to 9, find the number that makes 10 when added to the given number, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record the answer with a drawing or equation.
- **K.OA.A.3** (days 102, 103): Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
- **K.OA.A.2** (days 102, 103): Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings to represent the problem.
- skill mk-make-ten (grade K)
- skill mk-decompose-to-10 (grade K)
- skill mk-story-problems (grade K)
