# Section 2.6: One more and comparisons of written numbers

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-kindergarten/2/s/2.6), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-kindergarten u02-6.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 2, Numbers 1 to 10: see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 37 to 40.** Days by kind: understanding 1, fluency 2, application 1, check 0. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** K.CC.B.4c: "Understand" that next names one larger, day 37. K.CC.C.7: "Compare" written numerals 1 to 10, including more, fewer, equal. K.CC.C.6: "Identify" comparisons by both matching and counting. Begin with quantities, connect numerals, withdraw supplied frames day 40; record chosen support. No inequality/operation symbols, difference calculation or algorithm.

MP.7, day 37: exactly one added piece makes the next quantity. MP.3, day 39: justify comparison with a representation. Teacher pages distinguish number value from print size and prediction from recounting. Equality and fewer are taught before fluency here.

## 2. Where it sits

u02-5 supplies one-more and numeral-order teaching; review it with support as needed. Retrieve u01-4 Counted-uncounted boundary. Last fifteen mid-unit conferences occur days 37 to 39; comparison collection starts day 40 and passes to u02-7 with a listening roster and feedback. No readiness check.

## 3. Checks, written first

Teacher judged, path paper. Read every stem aloud; label pictures "Invented for practice" and say so. Each exit asks for one picture product on a new case. Read follow-ups separately to six named children only. Eight minutes: launch 1, simultaneous paper 2, six turns at 45 seconds 4.5, collect 0.5. Everyone returns paper; unheard reasoning/counting is recorded as not yet seen. Record names heard and support by component using only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Correct keys below are shown samples; observed wrong actions are not yet seen samples. Assign letters only from observed actions or heard reasons.

Reserve complete cases, art and response formats, not every integer: M3 contains all integers 0 to 10. Never rehearse a reserved format with other numbers. Use the reverse directions, partial work or critiques in each Shape line. A recognition choice needs quantity evidence, never elimination alone.

**Owned final items: F1 and F3.** F2 belongs to u02-2. M1/M2 retain u02-5's separate stems, cold keys and Shape reservations. Five four-minute conferences daily on days 37 to 39 reach children 16 to 20, 21 to 25, 26 to 30. No full repeat interview. Keep M3 products; revise rolling feedback in named repair parts. Day 40 closes final mid-unit feedback before final collection.

**E37, K.CC.B.4c.** Five-square paper tower beside one loose square and blank numeral label. Read "Make the next tower with its number label." Launch identifies the one square to add, without naming the result. Key: one new square attached/outlined above five, label 6. Six follow-up: "What number comes next here?" Shown: "Six, one more than five." Wrong: adds one but says total stays five (H). A wrong label alone does not establish H.

Shape: Extend a five-square tower once and name the next quantity. No five-tower extension in practice; predict hidden additions to four/seven loose squares, then verify. Static ordered examples never become this extension worksheet.

**E38, K.CC.C.7.** Equal-print written 2 and 8 with open evidence space. Read "Show which number is greater." Key: 8 marked with two/eight drawn quantities or other quantity evidence. Six follow-up: "How do you know?" Shown: "Eight comes later; it has more." Wrong: represents both correctly but calls eight fewer (I). No print-size diagnosis from equal print.

Shape: Independent numeral comparison with child-produced reason. No 2/8 case or independent compare-and-prove sheet in practice; complete a supplied five/eight representation and critique its given reason.

**E39, K.CC.C.7; MP.3.** Written order labels 6 and 8 above blank panels. Read "Make a picture that shows which order has more." Key: six/eight marks linked to labels, eight indicated as more. Six follow-up: "What in your drawing proves it?" Shown: "Six match; two in eight have no partner." Wrong: acknowledges extras in eight but calls eight fewer (I); counts six but labels 3 (M).

Shape: Unaided quick-drawing comparison of numeral requests. No 6/8 order or paired blank comparison drawing in practice; compare physical three/five groups and annotate existing pictures.

**E40, K.CC.C.7.** Unseen written 10 and 2, the 2 printed larger, no supplied group pictures. Read "Ring the greater number." Key: 10 only. Six follow-up: "How did you decide?" Shown: "Ten comes after two; ink size does not change it." Wrong: large-print 2 chosen for its ink size (J). Ring alone establishes choice, not reason.

Shape: Bare-numeral recognition despite reversed print size. No 10/2 pair or bare pair-selection sheet in practice; build groups for equal-print cards and compare groups.

**F1, day 41, retained to 44.** Joined frames show five/four, one extra tab outside. Separate paper asks: "Circle a full five." "Mark where the extra tab goes." "Write the number now." Key: five circled, tenth space filled, 10 written. Nine spaces were already filled; the inherited ninth-space sample is wrong. MP.7 is visible in circled five plus five after adding. Shown: full five circled, tenth filled, 10. Wrong: tab added but total said to remain nine (H). Six paper minutes day 41 Connected work; feedback after class, two revision minutes day 42.

Shape: Nine-to-ten frame completion with identified five and new numeral. No nine-plus-one frames, full-five circling/completion sheet or exact layout in practice; use hidden four/seven-square predictions and supplied order gaps.

**F2, day 40 paper, later oral.** Separate asks on two/three tabs: match, write each counted total, indicate more, indicate fewer. On equal fours with unequal spacing: match, write each total, indicate same. Keys: two links, one extra in three, 2/3, three more/two fewer; four links, 4/4, same. Wrong: wide fours called more (B); unmatched-extra group called fewer (I). F2 oral ask: "Use counting to explain this comparison," pointing to the two/three case. Shown: "One, two; one, two, three. Three has more." Record both heard count sequences and the comparison, so counting is evidenced as well as paper matching. Keep equal-four matching/count labels on the same record, with no extra interview.

Shape: Two-case independent matching/counting paper plus one reason. No two/three tabs, equal-four comparison paper or whole interview sequence in practice; use partial five/eight diagrams.

**F3, day 40.** Written pairs 4/8, 9/6, 3/3, the 4 printed larger than 8. For each pair separately: "Ring the greater number, or both if they are the same." Keys: 8, 9, both threes. Conditional selection is one decision. Separate oral ask: "Explain one of your choices." Shown: "Nine comes later than six." Wrong: large-print 4 chosen for ink size (J).

Shape: Three bare pairs, including print-size trap and equality. No 4/8, 9/6 or 3/3 pairs, three-row bare grid or same-number threes in teaching; compare represented groups under equal-print labels.

**Final collection budget.** Day 40 Final evidence: F2 paper 9, F3 paper 4, ten two-minute oral turns 20, total 33. Each turn contains one F2 reason and one F3 reason, 45 seconds each plus 30 seconds recording. Children 1 to 10 here, 11 to 20 day 41, 21 to 30 day 42; others use independent centers. Written feedback after each day takes five teacher minutes per item. Revise next day's return/connected work. Retain F1/F3 to day 44, no re-administration. This expands u02-2's proposed oral slot to fit both reasons.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Day 37 H: four squares and one added called four beside five. Day 38 I: five/eight matched but eight called fewer beside more. Day 39 M: three counted squares labeled 6 beside 3. Day 40 J: large-print 3 called greater than small-print 5 beside five greater. Repair with prediction/uncovering; extras linked to more/fewer; counted total linked to numeral then cover/write; quantities built under equal-print cards.

For carried A/B/C/D/E/K/L errors, use u02-5 field 4's wrong/right examples on fresh practice pieces in the assigned response slot; never rehearse M1/M2. Days 37/38 Mid-unit response each hold one five-minute repair and five-minute fresh transfer from completed mid-unit work, not incomplete records. Day 39/40 Return and repair handles late feedback or H/I/M/J, one action per slot. Day 41 Return and repair receives unresolved day 40 work; day 42 Connected repair receives the next queued letter. Reserved towers/pairs never become error demonstrations.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Review u02-5 equal-unit towers/numeral order; show a support again as needed. Loose squares behind a folded-paper screen support predictions day 37. Frames remain available days 38/39, supplied frames withdraw day 40; record voluntary support. Ideal instrument: one piece changes a known group, tied to its next numeral. Paper squares/folder fit; no digital spec/key tool. Handwritten cards and drawings give the room-with-nothing path. Large paper squares replace counters/blocks; folded scrap paper replaces the screen; hand-drawn joined frames replace frame mats.

## 6. Days

Tools: None throughout. Children hold pieces and paper; teacher retains dated component records, originals, written feedback and revisions separately. Fifteen pairs work concurrently: two 45-second turns take 1.5 minutes per round, leaving handling/checking time. At most two public volunteers; passing is allowed. One puppet actor per scene, one spoken ask per turn. Everyone outside a conference returns a paper trace/corrected diagram. Use Finish tick from u02-1 to distinguish an intentional empty answer. Children hear every printed label; independent reading is zero. Printed practice scenes say "Invented for practice"; say this aloud too.

Return and repair reviews the u01-1 routine: return 1, model one correction 1, simultaneous fresh paper practice 2, collect 1 minute. After class, allow fifteen teacher minutes for thirty paper sets, thirty seconds each, to write one useful correction; assessment feedback below is additional. Keep originals and revisions. More than ten of thirty sharing a letter triggers the class version; otherwise a small group while others correct familiar paper. Queue additional letters across later slots. Never run a teacher-led repair during a teacher conference. Use the paper stand-ins in field 5; adult-cut large paper pieces and concurrent table-tray cleanup avoid cutting/sink queues.

## 7. Talk and writing

Day 37: "What did adding one change?" Day 38: "Why does counting order help?" Day 39: "Where are the extras?" Separate paired turns. Written practice day 39 annotates a supplied three/five-square diagram. Look for three matched places, two extras in five and a numeral link; six/eight blank drawing stays reserved.

## 8. Practice that returns

Warm-ups: day 37 review u02-5 ordered quantities; day 38 rehearse next-number language; day 39 review u02-4 zero/labels; day 40 mix known more/fewer/same and name one more orally. Count to ten, supported extension toward twenty. Review u01-1 Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record and Return and repair, plus u01-4 Counted-uncounted boundary. These have written ledger evidence. Fluency target: none; the standard sets none. Days 38/40 repeat taught comparisons with feedback, no new content or timer.

## 9. Supports and extension

Language review: one more, one added object; next, the following count word; greater/more, a larger count; less/fewer, a smaller count; equal/same, neither group has extras after matching. Frame: "___ is more because ___." Connect words to quantities, not independent reading. Read all print aloud; no independent reading. Enlarge high-contrast/tactile pictures, use broad pencils, and accept pointing, signing or aided communication. Adult movement follows exact child directions; record help. A scribe records reasons, never selects/writes a numeral on a numeral-writing check. Separate motor formation from quantity understanding. Extend within ten by changing spacing/orientation and defending unchanged quantity, not a new operation.
