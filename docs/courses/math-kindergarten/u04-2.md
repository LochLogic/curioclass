- **E69.** Silent toy-boat panels show five in play and Rin bringing two more. "Tell a joining story for these pictures." All make paper action marks independently, then enact for partners; teacher hears six. Answer/look-for: Five first, two join, seven now. Meets: "Rin had five boats, brought two, seven now." Does not meet: Counts just two arrivals as the whole (B). Shape: Independently narrate/enact a silent joining scene with a partner; practice supplies a missing action in adult-started square stories, never creates complete stories from silent panels or uses boats.

- **E70.** Read: "Rin puts six puppets on a stage. Rin adds two puppets. How many now?" Ask: "Tell this story back." All draw sequence reminders; hear six before any solving. Answer/look-for: Six first, two joining, whole-question, in order. Meets: "Six puppets, two more, how many now?" Eight is a later solution, not a second exit ask. Does not meet: Retells only two arrivals as the whole (B). Shape: Retell a complete unseen heard story before solving; practice predicts missing events with adult cues or finishes partial retellings, never retells an entire unseen story or uses puppets.

- **E71.** Read: "Rin has nine trucks outside and parks six inside. How many stay outside?" Ask: "Show this story as you tell it back." All move counters across paper regions and trace; hear six retell. Answer/look-for: Nine first, six inside, three outside. Meets: three traced outside, faithful retelling. Does not meet: Six reported outside (E); nine joined by six (C). Shape: Independent retelling coordinated with object/location action; practice follows adult narration event by event or repairs partial cues, never independently retells while moving or uses trucks.

## 6. Days

Live timing replaces copied budgets. Tools: None daily; no key tool. Children hold pieces/paper, teacher holds dated record. Practice uses joint actions, supplied partial work or corrections, never the independent check formats in field 3. Practice combinations only: 2 + 3 = 5, 1 + 3 = 4, 2 + 2 = 4, 3 + 3 = 6, 3 + 4 = 7, 4 + 4 = 8; 5 - 2 = 3, 4 - 1 = 3, 4 - 2 = 2, 6 - 3 = 3, 7 - 3 = 4, 8 - 4 = 4; 4 + 0 = 4, 4 + 1 = 5, 5 - 5 = 0, 9 + 1 = 10, 9 - 1 = 8, 10 - 5 = 5. Do not use reversed combinations reserved for evidence. Whole-class demonstrations hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds inside their block. Partner turns run simultaneously in 15 pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; handling fits connected work. Everyone returns a corrected paper record. Read prompts one at a time, never chorus check answers. Rin is sole story actor moving objects, never children. Fingers, quiet sounds, imagined actions and verbal accounts are alternatives, not simultaneous requirements. Tidy occupies the last two center minutes unless stated otherwise.
