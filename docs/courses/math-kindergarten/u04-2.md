## 9. Supports and extension

New oral meanings: take away/take from/subtract, move some out of the starting group; remain/left, still in the place asked about; taking apart, separate a whole into parts while keeping both; retell, tell the events again in order. A story problem asks something about a story. First names the starting event.

Use objects and gestures with the practice frame "These marks show ___." Read all print aloud; independent reading is zero. Offer enlarged/high-contrast or tactile pictures, nonslip mats, broad pencils and the child's usual communication method. Adult movement/scribing follows the child's directions; record mathematical help. A scribe must not supply the action. Keep child-directed paper marks for every exit, with the same look-fors; remove answer-bearing language frames. Fingers and loud sounds are optional. Extend by correcting another supplied model within ten, not introducing comparison stories, unknown-position equations or a required counting-on method.
