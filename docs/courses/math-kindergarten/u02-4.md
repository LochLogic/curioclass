Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-kindergarten/2/s/2.4), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-kindergarten u02-4.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 2, Numbers 1 to 10: see [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 30 to 33.** Days by kind: understanding 3, fluency 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

**Share.** K.CC.A.3: "Write" and "Represent" quantities with numerals, 0 to 10 here, including zero. Day 30 explicitly connects all eleven forms to quantities with guided formation before independent labels. Day 31 keeps the numeral when objects move. K.CC.B.5's "given a number ... count out that many objects" is within ten, day 32; day 33 rehearses drawing spoken/printed requests. K.CC.B.4/B.4b connect totals and invariance.

**Strategy and connection.** Track pieces, retain the requested stop, label the result. No algorithm/operation symbols. Review u02-3 five-and-some; teach none/zero on day 30, not new on fluency day 33. MP.2, day 32: numeral to quantity. MP.7, day 31: amount/label across arrangements. Teacher explanation separates words, quantities and shapes; recognizable reversals receive formation feedback, not an automatic M label.

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

One puppet demonstrates one practice mistake at a time, never a child's work. If more than ten of thirty share a letter, the next five-minute Return and repair models one correction, lets pairs retry simultaneously on a fresh practice case and collects everyone's corrected paper. Otherwise meet one group while others correct familiar work. Choose the earliest blocking action; queue other letters across later slots. Never stack five-minute repairs or use reserved art.

E, day 30: empty box labeled 1 versus 0. M, day 30: correctly counted five labeled 2 versus 5. B, day 31: moved eight called ten versus eight throughout. D, day 32: ten moved for five versus stop at five. O, day 33: one dot for zero versus intentional empty group. Fresh square practice cases only.

Repair E/O by clearing a group and connecting none/0; M by count-match-cover-write; B by checking unchanged membership; D by keeping the request visible and stopping there. Day 34 receives one five-minute repair, not five simultaneous repairs. No letter is diagnosed from a reversal alone.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

**Carried.** Review Counted-uncounted boundary from u01-4 and Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames from u02-3. Tie quantities to all numerals 0 to 10 on day 30; trace, then cover and form during guided work. Day 31 retains the label across arrangements, with then without frames. Day 32 keeps the requested stop visible, briefly covering it only after understanding. Day 33 rehearses drawing and none/0. Supplied-frame withdrawal for numeral comparison waits until day 40.

**Ideal instruments.** Equal-cell frames and numeral cards fit quantity-symbol ties; large paper pieces fit counting out. No-kit: draw cards, frames and pieces on scrap paper. No real buttons. Adult-cut large pieces stay out of mouths; replace damage. No digital instrument or spec is needed.

## 6. Days

All digital tools: None; no key tool. Children hold pieces and paper; the teacher holds dated records. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously: one 45-second turn each takes 1.5 minutes per round. Use no more than two public volunteers; anyone may pass. Label printed stories "invented for practice" and say this aloud. Object-only scenes need no actors.

Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from u01-1. Each child returns a marked practice record and exit slip. Before the exit, teach or review Finish tick from u02-1: a corner tick means the child has finished, including an intentionally empty group; it never gives the answer.

Return and repair gives back the previous day's exit, heard-response note and practice papers with written feedback. Its five minutes hold return 1, one modeled correction 1, simultaneous fresh practice 2, collection 1. Retain original and revision. Repair one earliest obstruction; queue other letters in later slots. Hear further children during their later scheduled turns, keeping earlier unheard components not yet seen. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each; write one useful correction. No reserved case or Shape becomes repair practice.

## 9. Supports and extension

**Language.** Numeral/number label: the written mark naming how many. Zero: the number for no objects. None: no objects here. Teach these on day 30 with empty and filled frames. Frame: "This numeral names ___ pieces." Review represent from u01-5 and total from u02-1.

**Access and exits.** Offer broad pencils, enlarged/tactile numeral cards and high-contrast pictures. An adult moves pieces exactly as pointed, signed or aided directions specify, recording help. A scribe records reasons without supplying the numeral on a writing item; keep physical formation support separate from numeral understanding. Face children and show visual turn cues. Read all print aloud; independent reading is zero. Extend by preserving quantity and label through new arrangements within ten.
