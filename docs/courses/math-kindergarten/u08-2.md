## 1. Standards and practices

K.CC.A.3, "Write"/"Represent", K.CC.B.5, "Count"/"count out": quantities/numerals, days 140 to 143. K.NBT.A.1, "Compose and decompose"/"record": teen parts both directions, day 141. K.G.A.3, "Identify", K.G.B.4, "Analyze and compare": flat/solid features across size/turn, day 142. K.OA.A.2, "Solve", K.OA.A.1, "Represent": joining/removal stories, days 143 to 145.

Arranged sets/numerals to twenty, scattered sets/comparisons/stories to ten. Ten means ten actual ones, never one substitute counter. Total may appear on either side of an equation; no unknown-position equations/algorithm. MP.1 days 144/145 retains first model and any genuine repair/explanation. Explain solid versus flat face, action versus answer size, access versus mathematical help.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Children carry book drafts days 140 to 142 and story records days 144 to 146; teacher keeps originals and feedback. Review written u06-2 Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible, u07-3 Whole solid beside its pictured object and traced flat face, and u04-4 Equal quantities linked to both equation orders. Ideal instrument, days 140 to 145: objects, original pictures and retained paper. Fits; tools None, no key or spec-only tool. Adult cuts; use low tabletop handling and no small loose pieces. A seated object tray and teacher drawings replace every classroom walk or unavailable object.

## 6. Days

Every day totals sixty minutes. Tools: None throughout. Children hold paper/models, teacher retains records/feedback. Icon directions are taught before independent centers; no extra adult/device assumed. Parallel partner turns, never thirty serial turns. Cleanup included in centers.
