## 1. Standards and practices

K.CC.A.3, "Write"/"Represent", K.CC.B.5, "Count"/"count out": quantities/numerals, days 140 to 143. K.NBT.A.1, "Compose and decompose"/"record": teen parts both directions, day 141. K.G.A.3, "Identify", K.G.B.4, "Analyze and compare": flat/solid features across size/turn, day 142. K.OA.A.2, "Solve", K.OA.A.1, "Represent": joining/removal stories, days 143 to 145.

Arranged sets/numerals to twenty, scattered sets/comparisons/stories to ten. Ten means ten actual ones, never one substitute counter. Total may appear on either side of an equation; no unknown-position equations/algorithm. MP.1 days 144/145 retains first model and any genuine repair/explanation. Explain solid versus flat face, action versus answer size, access versus mathematical help.

- **K.OA.A.2** (days 143, 144, 145): Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings to represent the problem.
- **K.OA.A.1** (days 144, 145): Represent addition and subtraction with objects, fingers, mental images, drawings, sounds (e.g., claps), acting out situations, verbal explanations, expressions, or equations.
