## 1. Standards and practices

Connection K.CC.B.5 day 132 visible-piece counts. MP.3 day 130 feature guessing; MP.7 day 131 flat supports. Informal language, no edge totals/nets/volume. Explain: cube's face square/whole solid; cylinder ends circular/cone one circular flat base/sphere no flat faces. Fixed view for relative positions. No algorithm.
