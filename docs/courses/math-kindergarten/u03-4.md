Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 3, Flat shapes and comparing: see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 57 to 60.** Days by kind: understanding 2, application 2, fluency 0, check 0. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

K.G.B.5, "Model" by drawing and constructing environmental boundaries; K.G.B.6, "Compose" larger shapes. K.G.A.1, "Describe" school shapes/positions; K.G.A.2, "Correctly name" across turns/sizes. F5/F6 collect previously taught K.MD.A.1/2, no new measurement teaching. MP.6, day 58: identify the particular flat boundary on an object, not call an entire box flat. MP.7, day 59: attend to full-side contact. Teacher explanation: drawings approximate a boundary; closure/corners matter, not neatness. Rotation preserves features. A nonsquare rhombus is not square merely because its sides match. Unit 7 owns solid naming and completes K.G.B.4. No algorithm, scale numerals or formal definitions.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review side, corner, closed, join and outline from u03-1/u03-2 with their objects. Draw means make a picture with lines. Outline is the outside boundary; compose is the teacher word for putting shapes together into a larger shape. Name gap as uncovered space between pieces. Retain u03-3 comparison language; every spoken description names the actual compared objects.

No independent reading. Read all print aloud. Teach words with objects and gestures; remove language frames from independent checks. Offer large high-contrast or tactile boundaries, nonslip mats, broad pencils, signing and usual communication aids. A scribe records words without supplying them. Adult movement follows the child's directions; record mathematical help separately. Direct comparisons cannot become picture guesses. Extend with another example or fit on the same standard, never units or new arithmetic. Frame: "This edge meets ___." Hallway fallback is an original drawing with the same reserved triangular boundary and relative position.
