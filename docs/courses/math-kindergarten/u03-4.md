## 1. Standards and practices


K.G.B.5, "Model" by drawing and constructing environmental boundaries; K.G.B.6, "Compose" larger shapes. K.G.A.1, "Describe" school shapes/positions; K.G.A.2, "Correctly name" across turns/sizes. F5/F6 collect previously taught K.MD.A.1/2, no new measurement teaching. MP.6, day 58: identify the particular flat boundary on an object, not call an entire box flat. MP.7, day 59: attend to full-side contact. Teacher explanation: drawings approximate a boundary; closure/corners matter, not neatness. Rotation preserves features. A nonsquare rhombus is not square merely because its sides match. Unit 7 owns solid naming and completes K.G.B.4. No algorithm, scale numerals or formal definitions.

## 6. Days

Tools: None throughout. Children hold pieces and paper; teacher keeps dated originals, feedback and revisions. Fifteen pairs work concurrently, two 45-second turns plus handling per round. At most two public volunteers, with passing allowed. Rin is the sole actor in invented demonstrations, one scene at a time. Return and repair holds one five-minute action: whole class if more than ten of thirty share a letter, otherwise small group while others correct familiar paper. Queue additional letters across later slots. Never shorten repairs or run them during conferences. Everybody returns a corrected diagram. Eight-minute exits allow 2.5 minutes directions/paper, six 45-second turns, one minute collection. Other children return paper; extra help is recorded. Adult-cut large pieces and table trays avoid cutting/sink queues.

## 9. Supports and extension

No independent reading. Read all print aloud. Teach words with objects and gestures; remove language frames from independent checks.
