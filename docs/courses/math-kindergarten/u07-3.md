# Section 7.3: Flat forms and solid objects

Read this plan with its facts from data (days, codes, minutes): [the Front Office](https://curioclass.org/front-office/#math/math-kindergarten/7/s/7.3), or `python -X utf8 scripts/plan_facts.py math-kindergarten u07-3.md`. <!-- facts link -->

Kindergarten Math, unit 7, [Solid shapes all around us](s07.md). A plan, not a lesson: these directions and timings are ours, untested. Use DEFAULTS.md for the standing day and page rules.

## 1. Standards and practices

K.G.A.3, "Identify": flat forms versus solid objects. K.G.B.4, "Analyze and compare": size/turn, sides, corners, equal sides/flat faces. K.G.A.2, "Correctly name": retrieve flat names, introduce sphere/cylinder/cone and reestablish cube day 127. K.G.A.1, "Describe": retrieve all six positions day 127/apply day 128. MP.7 day 127: cube versus square face; MP.3 day 128: feature sort. Connection K.G.B.5 day 129 construction. No algorithm. Explain: paper represents an ideal flat form or a solid, but paper itself is a thin object. Hollow models can represent solid shapes. Square is a rectangle; informal part words suffice. No required edge totals, volume or nets.

## 3. Checks, written first

All evidence is judged=teacher. Read one stem, wait for its response, then read the next. Every child marks paper, including a sketch or trace of a build; children read no prompt independently. Hear a rotating six, one 45-second ask each, and record their names. On two-ask tickets hear three per ask; the other component uses paper evidence. Unheard language remains not yet seen. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Samples below distinguish independent success from a recorded error; record any prompt as help. Print and say "Invented for practice" for each invented story scene. One actor acts in each scene. Reserve all exit and check cases across teaching, games, repairs and home practice.

**Unit assessment items owned:** content F1/F2/F4/shared F3, s07 field 5. Section 7.4 administers/closes all; content ownership does not create another sitting. Its F1 to F4 Shape notes bind this section's models/practice too.

- **E126, K.G.A.3.** New unfamiliar circle drawing beside real sphere/cube/cone with matching pictures. "Mark the flat shape." Answer circle drawing. Shown: circle marked. Not yet seen: sphere marked with "its picture is flat" (M); unexplained wrong mark gets descriptive note.
  Shape: identify sole flat form in new mixed display. Practice corrects single-object labels/handles objects one at a time, never selects from mixed display.
- **E127a, K.G.A.2.** New pointed paper cone model lying diagonally, matching real model. "Name this shape." Answer cone, recorded for three assigned speakers; remaining names pending, all mark E127b. Shown: cone; not yet seen: triangle from outline (F).
  Shape: name unfamiliar cone-shaped object. Practice corrects supplied labels on a known single model, never independently names a new environmental object.
- **E127b, K.G.B.4.** "Mark one part that helps you know." Answer circular flat base or pointed tip. Shown: circular base marked; not yet seen: traces only triangular silhouette/calls solid triangle (F).
  Shape: select diagnostic feature on unfamiliar cone. Practice traces supplied cube face/corrects an indicated part, never independently marks cone feature.
- **E128a, K.G.B.4/A.3.** Big cylinder/tiny circle outline, new poses/sizes. "Show whether these are the same kind of shape or different." Provide equally presented same/different icon spaces, taught on other pairs; the child marks one. Answer: different, cylinder solid and circle flat. Shown: different icon marked; not yet seen: whole cylinder flat because ends flat (O).
  Shape: directly compare big solid/small flat. Practice completes within-type feature sorts, never compares this cross-dimensional pair.
- **E128b.** "Mark what makes them different." Paper mark indicates cylinder's thickness beyond end. Shown: "This goes back; that is flat." Not yet seen: only color offered as difference (D). Rejecting a familiar flat name after a turn is C only when observed.
  Shape: independently annotate dimension difference on a size-mismatched pair. Practice repairs supplied feature labels, never annotates that pair.
- **E129, K.G.B.4/A.3; K.G.B.5 connection.** New upright cylinder-like storage-roll model; blank paper/clay, or rollable paper/soft joins. "Build a solid like this one." Answer roughly cylindrical body, curved side/two circular flat ends. Paper trace of end/side drawing records build. Shown: cylinder-like object; not yet seen: builds only a flat circle and says the whole tube is flat because its end is flat (O).
  Shape: independently construct whole cylinder-like object from a fresh model. Practice repairs partial cube/box frames, never constructs whole cylinder or follows a cylinder step card. Adult precuts a matched curved-body rectangle with broad tabs and two circular end pieces per child. Broad self-fastening paper slots or large soft adhesive tabs let children join without a serial adult queue; no small clips. Child directs any access help; judge shape, not craftsmanship.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Fits: whole remains visible beyond face; no digital/key tool. Drawn shapes/cards, clean plain boxes/tubes, paper balls/cones replace kits. Paper rods/soft joins replace day 129 sticks/clay. ## 9. Supports and extension

New words day 127: sphere, a ball-like solid; cylinder, a solid with two circular flat ends and a curved side; cone, a solid with a circular flat base and a pointed tip; face, a flat surface of a solid. Review flat from u03-1, solid and cube from u01-1. Show their meanings again on days 126 and 127. Frame: "Both have ___; this one has ___." Face means flat surface here, not a person's face. Gesture positions from fixed front view. Accept informal feature language with pointing.

Read everything aloud; independent reading is zero. Offer large tactile outlines, high contrast, nonslip mats and broad pencils. Accept signing, home-language explanations, communication aids and directed adult movement. Scribe dictated labels, record help, never judge fine-motor skill. Patterns supplement colors; adults cut pieces. Every picture has a tactile model if needed. No public speech required. Extension: another solution or another feature rule within the same standard.
