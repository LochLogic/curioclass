Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 1, Math in our world: see [s01.md](s01.md). **Days 12 to 15.** Days by kind: understanding 3, application 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **K.CC.B.4a, this section's share.** "say the number names in the standard order", pairing each word with exactly one object in lines and scattered collections.

- **Limits and strategies.** Teaching collections stay within ten. Day 14 last-word meaning is modeled, not scored as cardinality. Day 13 optional twenty-piece exploration is five minutes, never an exit or prerequisite. No algorithm.

- **Practices.** MP.5, day 13, choose a cover or boundary; MP.6, day 15, explain why each object receives one word.

- **Connections and adult explanation.** No other grade standard is claimed. Distinguish ordered words, object pairing and tracking from a total; a correct total can conceal an omission and a repeat.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments

- **Carried.** Use the Counted-uncounted boundary on day 12; withdraw teacher-directed moving on day 13, leaving chosen supports. Day 14 attaches the last word informally. Day 15 retains explanations and observation records.

- **Key moment and verdict.** Day 13 needs a reversible boundary or covers. Ideal: large pieces, paper line and covers. Paper fits because counted pieces remain distinct; no device, spec or key tool.

- **A room with nothing.** Large cut-paper squares replace counters; paper strips replace cubes; drawn/cut shapes replace pattern blocks; hand-drawn boxes replace mats and the 5-frame. Clean boxes, tubes or folded paper solids replace solid models. A folded-paper puppet replaces a toy. Exit objects are drawn or cut paper, never requested home supplies; a pencil replaces crayons for marks. Keep large pieces on trays, with no food, sharp parts or swallowable pieces.

## 9. Supports and extension

- **Language.** Line: objects laid one after another. Counted: already given a word; uncounted: not yet given a word. Boundary: the line between those parts. Scattered: spread about. Done pile: where finished pieces can rest; name it when gathering unused covers, without counting into a pile. Last word: the number word said at the end. Introduce these on days 12, 13 and 14 respectively. Review keep track from u01-1. Frame: "This side is counted already."

- **Access and exits.** Enlarge and space pictures; offer tactile outlines or paper pieces. An adult follows pointed, signed or aided directions without giving counting words, recording help. Face children and use visual turn cues. A child unable to dot directs adult paper marking. No independent reading: read every stem and label aloud. Repeat directions without demonstrating the reserved solution.

- **Extension.** Change route or arrangement within ten, explaining how each object is reached once; no operations or numeral writing.
