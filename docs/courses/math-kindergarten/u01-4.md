## 1. Standards and practices


- **This section's share.** "say the number names in the standard order", pairing each word with exactly one object in lines and scattered collections.

- **Limits and strategies.** Teaching collections stay within ten. Day 14 last-word meaning is modeled, not scored as cardinality. Day 13 optional twenty-piece exploration is five minutes, never an exit or prerequisite. No algorithm.
