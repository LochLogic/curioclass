Kindergarten Math, course `math-kindergarten`. Unit 3, Flat shapes and comparing: see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 61 to 63.** Days by kind: understanding 0, application 2, fluency 0, check 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children.

## 1. Standards and practices

K.G.B.5, "Model" through built/drawn boundaries, and K.G.B.6, "Compose" the same whole using different pieces. Day 63 closes K.G.A.1/2 and K.MD.A.1/2 records taught earlier; no new teaching. MP.7, day 61: changing internal joins can preserve the outer boundary. MP.6, day 62: distinguish names of component shapes from the larger picture. Teacher explanation: full-side contact, no gap/overlap, and identical outer boundary support a composition claim; equal area alone is not the check here. No fraction language, formal area, measurement units or algorithm. Withdraw supplied outlines for day 62 art, retaining tactile/motor access supports.

No answer captions or completed outlines cue an exit. The goal-map exit shapes stay fixed; change practice where necessary. Repeated classroom direction phrases are not repeated teaching or evidence cases.

**Unit assessment items owned:** F3/F4; F3 is collected day 59 in u03-4, with its Shape there. F1/F2 belong to u03-4; F5/F6 to u03-3. Day 63 closes existing component records, not six fresh interviews.

**F4 Shape:** independent two-triangle fill of reserved nonsquare rectangle, then a separately prompted shared-edge trace. Read "Fill this outline with these pieces"; after completion, "Trace where the pieces meet." Six minutes total on day 61. Two congruent right triangles from the diagonal cut of a nonsquare rectangle meet along their full longest sides, without gaps/overlap. Look-for: exact outer rectangle, full diagonal seam. Samples: filled rectangle and diagonal traced; visible gap (E), or overlap (K). Practice corrects supplied partial multi-piece designs, never independently fills an outline with two matching triangles or traces this seam. E61 is a square filled two ways, distinct format/case. F3/F4 feedback follows collection and precedes revisions/scoring.

- **E61, K.G.B.6.** Two equal fresh square outlines, one packet with four smaller equal squares, another with two matching right triangles cut from a square. "Show two different ways to fill this shape." Every child fits pieces, traces each fill on paper; six explain unchanged outside. Answer: four squares in a two-by-two arrangement; two triangles meet along full diagonals. Same square boundary, different internal joins. Other exact valid arrangements are accepted explicitly, but piece types must differ. Samples: complete square fills with different seams; gap (E); overlap (K). Shape: independent two-way fill of an unseen outline; practice replaces pieces in supplied partial hexagon designs and compares completed teacher fills, never independently fills any outline twice or uses square outlines. Keep the whole E61 packet reserved. Exit budget: launch 1, independent fitting/tracing 6, collect 1 minute. Hear the roster's six boundary descriptions during the final center block on a different fresh partial-hexagon case, six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; no E61 preview. Six paper minutes permit both fills without rushing. Data id: `s03-X61`.
- **E62, K.G.B.5/6.** A sealed original picture from invented partner Rin, never shown in teaching: rectangle joined full-side to a square, triangle joined along another matching edge, then the entire picture turned so the square rests on a corner. Shared edges stay collinear and equal in length. "Tell which shapes made your partner's picture." Every child marks component boundaries on its paper copy; hear six. Answer/look-for: rectangle, square despite turn, triangle; marks identify each actual component. Samples: "Rectangle, square, triangle" with each boundary marked; "That one cannot be square because it is turned" (B). Shape: analyze an unfamiliar partner's completed composition; practice creates one's own art, never identifies the components of a finished partner/teacher picture. Adult serves as partner in one-child room. Original reserved drawing removes dependence on classmates finishing on time. Data id: `s03-X62`.

## 9. Supports and extension

Review outline, join, gap and overlap with u03-4 pieces. Same whole means the same outside boundary here; different pieces means a different set or arrangement inside it. Turn alone can make a different arrangement, but E61 deliberately asks for different piece sets. Do not introduce fractions or area units.

No independent reading. Read all print aloud. Teach words with objects and gestures; remove language frames from independent checks. Offer large high-contrast or tactile boundaries, nonslip mats, broad pencils, signing and usual communication aids. A scribe records words without supplying them. Adult movement follows the child's directions; record mathematical help separately. Direct comparisons cannot become picture guesses. Extend with another example or fit on the same standard, never units or new arithmetic. Frame: "The outside is ___; the pieces are ___." E61 permits directed placement and raised boundaries, with help recorded; preserve different-piece requirement.
