# Section 3.5: Different pieces and the same whole

## 1. Standards and practices


K.G.B.5, "Model" through built/drawn boundaries, and K.G.B.6, "Compose" the same whole using different pieces. Day 63 closes K.G.A.1/2 and K.MD.A.1/2 records taught earlier; no new teaching. MP.7, day 61: changing internal joins can preserve the outer boundary. MP.6, day 62: distinguish names of component shapes from the larger picture. Teacher explanation: full-side contact, no gap/overlap, and identical outer boundary support a composition claim; equal area alone is not the check here. No fraction language, formal area, measurement units or algorithm. Withdraw supplied outlines for day 62 art, retaining tactile/motor access supports.

## 3. Checks, written first


Authored items control over copied U3 prompts. Read quoted stems aloud, one ask at a time. Every exit is teacher judged with paper from every child; hear six named children only. Mark each component shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Samples illustrate responses, not another scale. Never infer unheard speech. Reserve all check cases from teaching, repairs and centers throughout the unit. Original scenes are invented for practice, said aloud and printed on teacher masters. No answer captions or completed outlines cue an exit.

**Unit assessment items owned:** F3/F4; F3 is collected day 59 in u03-4, with its Shape there. F1/F2 belong to u03-4; F5/F6 to u03-3. Day 63 closes existing component records, not six fresh interviews.

**F4 Shape:** independent two-triangle fill of reserved nonsquare rectangle, then a separately prompted shared-edge trace. Read "Fill this outline with these pieces"; after completion, "Trace where the pieces meet." Six minutes total on day 61. Two congruent right triangles from the diagonal cut of a nonsquare rectangle meet along their full longest sides, without gaps/overlap. Look-for: exact outer rectangle, full diagonal seam. Samples: filled rectangle and diagonal traced; visible gap (E), or overlap (K). Practice corrects supplied partial multi-piece designs, never independently fills an outline with two matching triangles or traces this seam. E61 is a square filled two ways, distinct format/case. F3/F4 feedback follows collection and precedes revisions/scoring.

- **E61, K.G.B.6.** Two equal fresh square outlines, one packet with four smaller equal squares, another with two matching right triangles cut from a square. "Show two different ways to fill this shape." Every child fits pieces, traces each fill on paper; six explain unchanged outside. Answer: four squares in a two-by-two arrangement; two triangles meet along full diagonals. Same square boundary, different internal joins. Other exact valid arrangements are accepted explicitly, but piece types must differ. Samples: complete square fills with different seams; gap (E); overlap (K). Shape: independent two-way fill of an unseen outline; practice replaces pieces in supplied partial hexagon designs and compares completed teacher fills, never independently fills any outline twice or uses square outlines. Keep the whole E61 packet reserved. Data id: `s03-X61`.
- **E62, K.G.B.5/6.** A sealed original picture from invented partner Rin, never shown in teaching: rectangle joined full-side to a turned square, triangle joined along another matching edge. "Tell which shapes made your partner's picture." Every child marks component boundaries on its paper copy; hear six. Answer/look-for: rectangle, square despite turn, triangle; marks identify each actual component. Samples: "Rectangle, square, triangle" with each boundary marked; "That one cannot be square because it is turned" (B). Shape: analyze an unfamiliar partner's completed composition; practice creates one's own art, never identifies the components of a finished partner/teacher picture. Adult serves as partner in one-child room. Original reserved drawing removes dependence on classmates finishing on time. Data id: `s03-X62`.

**Day 63:** use F1 shape-language record, F2 label sheet, F3 construction trace/drawing, F4 composition, F5 attribute sheet, F6 direct-comparison record. Unit review allocations remain 2/1/2/1/1/3 minutes, total ten. Use component look-fors/samples in owning sections and s03 field 5, with the single marking scale only. First-made day 63 evidence remains pending until written feedback and revision; do not score it that period.

## 6. Days

Tools: None throughout. Children hold pieces and paper; teacher keeps dated originals, feedback and revisions. Fifteen pairs work concurrently, two 45-second turns plus handling per round. At most two public volunteers, with passing allowed. Rin is the sole actor in invented demonstrations, one scene at a time. Return and repair holds one five-minute action: whole class if more than ten of thirty share a letter, otherwise small group while others correct familiar paper. Queue additional letters across later slots. Never shorten repairs or run them during conferences. Everybody returns a corrected diagram. Eight-minute exits allow 2.5 minutes directions/paper, six 45-second turns, one minute collection. Other children return paper; extra help is recorded. Adult-cut large pieces and table trays avoid cutting/sink queues.

## 9. Supports and extension

No independent reading. Read all print aloud. Teach words with objects and gestures; remove language frames from independent checks. Offer large high-contrast or tactile boundaries, nonslip mats, broad pencils, signing and usual communication aids. A scribe records words without supplying them. Adult movement follows the child's directions; record mathematical help separately. Direct comparisons cannot become picture guesses. Extend with another example or fit on the same standard, never units or new arithmetic. Words: same whole, different pieces, outline, join, overlap. Frame: "The outside is ___; the pieces are ___." E61 permits directed placement and raised boundaries, with help recorded; preserve different-piece requirement.
