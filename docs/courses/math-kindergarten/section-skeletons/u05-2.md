## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.OA.A.3** (days 91, 92, 93, 94, 95, 96): Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
- **K.OA.A.1** (days 91): Represent addition and subtraction with objects, fingers, mental images, drawings, sounds (e.g., claps), acting out situations, verbal explanations, expressions, or equations.
- **K.OA.A.2** (days 92, 93, 94, 95, 96): Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings to represent the problem.
- skill mk-decompose-to-10 (grade K)
- skill mk-add-sub-expressions (grade K)
- skill mk-story-problems (grade K)

### U8. Practice that returns

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Daily counting retrieves cardinality. Within-five games mix joining and removal with immediate feedback; record just knows, quick strategy or counts all informally. Partner knowledge supports later fluency but is not itself a speed test.

### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Read everything aloud. Gesture part, whole and missing; offer "___ and ___ make ___." Provide tactile pieces, large pictures and directed adult movement. Extend by organizing more pairs for the same total and explaining why a pair repeats, within ten.
<!-- /skel -->
