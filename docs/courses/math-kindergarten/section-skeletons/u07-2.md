## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.OA.A.1** (days 123): Represent addition and subtraction with objects, fingers, mental images, drawings, sounds (e.g., claps), acting out situations, verbal explanations, expressions, or equations.
- **K.G.B.5** (days 123): Model shapes in the world by building shapes from components (e.g., sticks and clay balls) and drawing shapes.
- **K.G.B.6** (days 123): Compose simple shapes to form larger shapes. For example, “Can you join these two triangles with full sides touching to make a rectangle?”
- **K.OA.A.2** (days 124): Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings to represent the problem.
- **K.OA.A.3** (days 125): Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
- **K.OA.A.4** (days 125): For any number from 1 to 9, find the number that makes 10 when added to the given number, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record the answer with a drawing or equation.

### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Read everything aloud. Teach flat, solid, face, side, corner and position words with gestures: "Both have ___; this one has ___." Accept home-language reasoning before restating English names. Use tactile outlines, large stable pieces and adult movement at the child's direction. Extend by proposing and testing two different feature-based sorts of the same objects.
<!-- /skel -->

### U10. Two rooms

<!-- skel -->

A thirty-five-minute path uses counting five, model five, building ten, discussion and recording ten, exit five; include within-five practice in recording. Spread assigned evidence across the same window. One shared device is optional.
