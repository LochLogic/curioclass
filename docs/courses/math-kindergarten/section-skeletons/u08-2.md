## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.CC.A.3** (days 140, 141, 142, 143): Write numbers from 0 to 20. Represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20 (with 0 representing a count of no objects).
- **K.CC.B.5** (days 140, 141, 142, 143): Count to answer “how many?” questions about as many as 20 things arranged in a line, a rectangular array, or a circle, or as many as 10 things in a scattered configuration; given a number from 1–20, count out that many objects.
- **K.NBT.A.1** (days 141): Compose and decompose numbers from 11 to 19 into ten ones and some further ones, e.g., by using objects or drawings, and record each composition or decomposition by a drawing or equation (e.g., 18 = 10 + 8); understand that these numbers are composed of ten ones and one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, or nine ones.
- **K.G.A.3** (days 142): Identify shapes as two-dimensional (lying in a plane, “flat”) or three-dimensional (“solid”).
- **K.G.B.4** (days 142): Analyze and compare two- and three-dimensional shapes, in different sizes and orientations, using informal language to describe their similarities, differences, parts (e.g., number of sides and vertices/“corners”) and other attributes (e.g., having sides of equal length).
- **K.OA.A.2** (days 143, 144, 145): Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings to represent the problem.
- **K.OA.A.1** (days 144, 145): Represent addition and subtraction with objects, fingers, mental images, drawings, sounds (e.g., claps), acting out situations, verbal explanations, expressions, or equations.
- skill mk-count-to-20 (grade K)
- skill mk-teen-ten-ones (grade K)
- skill mk-analyze-shapes (grade K)
- skill mk-story-problems (grade K)
- skill mk-add-sub-expressions (grade K)
