## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->

- skill mk-story-problems (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions.** What changes when objects leave, day 68? How can a story become a model, day 71? Which marks show its action, day 75? What does each symbol mean, day 78?
> **Teaching moves.** Model actions before symbols. Ask what each object represents; compare clear and misleading drawings on day 74. Day 73 adds a heard put-together story. Step back during day 77 story creation. Explain that equals means the same quantity on both sides.
>
> **Practices.** MP.4: day 72, choose objects or a drawing for a heard story and explain the match. MP.2: day 78, identify what both numbers and the operation in an expression mean in its story.
