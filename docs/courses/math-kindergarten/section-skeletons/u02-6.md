## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.CC.B.4c** (days 37): Understand that each successive number name refers to a quantity that is one larger.
- **K.CC.C.7** (days 38, 39, 40): Compare two numbers between 1 and 10 presented as written numerals.
- **K.CC.C.6** (days 38, 39): Identify whether the number of objects in one group is greater than, less than, or equal to the number of objects in another group, e.g., by using matching and counting strategies.
- skill mk-one-more (grade K)
- skill mk-compare-numerals (grade K)
- skill mk-compare-groups (grade K)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Teaching moves.** Model moving without adding on day 20, matching on day 21 and counting another way on day 25. Use dot records and spoken totals before tying collections to numerals on day 30. Step back during day 39 comparisons. Model each sorting step on day 41. Teacher pages distinguish counting words, a quantity and its written numeral.
>
> **Carried.** Keep unit 1 tracking mats. Teach the 5-frame structure on day 26 and join two that day, as five and some more, before day 27 rehearsal. Name one more in the oral count on days 38 and 40. Tie frames to written numerals on day 30; vary arrangements on day 31. Withdraw supplied frames for numeral comparisons on day 40, retaining optional supports and recording their use. Frames return in unit 4.

### U9. Supports and extension

<!-- skel -->
> (shared across the unit) Read everything aloud; no independent reading is required. Pair gestures with more, fewer, same and category. Rehearse "This has more because ___." Use large high-contrast pictures or tactile pieces, a counter mat instead of fingers, and directed adult movement. Keep numeral writing observable. Extend by finding two arrangements that mislead sight but preserve the comparison, within ten.
<!-- /skel -->
