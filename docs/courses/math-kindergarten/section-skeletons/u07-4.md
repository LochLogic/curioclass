## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **K.G.A.3** (days 130, 131, 132, 133, 134): Identify shapes as two-dimensional (lying in a plane, “flat”) or three-dimensional (“solid”).
- **K.G.B.4** (days 130, 131, 132, 133, 134): Analyze and compare two- and three-dimensional shapes, in different sizes and orientations, using informal language to describe their similarities, differences, parts (e.g., number of sides and vertices/“corners”) and other attributes (e.g., having sides of equal length).
- **K.G.A.1** (days 130, 134): Describe objects in the environment using names of shapes, and describe the relative positions of these objects using terms such as above, below, beside, in front of, behind, and next to.
- **K.G.A.2** (days 134): Correctly name shapes regardless of their orientations or overall size.
- skill mk-analyze-shapes (grade K)
