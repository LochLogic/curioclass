## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

**Hook, day 135.** Compare Census household connectivity evidence with the public genome project's promises. Who gains from connection?
**Supporting questions.** Co-write: What changed, and for whom (135 and 136)? How did institutions respond (137 to 139)? Did security responses achieve their goals while protecting rights (140 to 142)?
