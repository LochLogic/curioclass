## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How well has the United States met the challenges of a connected world?"
**Hook, day 135.** Compare Census household connectivity evidence with the public genome project's promises. Who gains from connection?
**Supporting questions.** Co-write: What changed, and for whom (135 and 136)? How did institutions respond (137 to 139)? Did security responses achieve their goals while protecting rights (140 to 142)?
**Closing claim, day 143.** Judge the compelling question using Census, genome, statute, court and war records; answer counterevidence.
**Teaching.** Model sourcing and a qualified claim on 135, comparison on 136 and goals against outcomes on 138. Students weigh evidence on 142 and write independently on 143. On 142, practise D2.His.2.9-12 through changing security aims and D2.Geo.11.9-12 by weighing oil interdependence against security explanations using Gulf maps. Explain jurisdiction and unequal outcomes.
