## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Why did American politics and the Cold War both change so much between 1968 and 1991?"
**Hook, day 124.** Contrast Johnson's Great Society aims with Reagan's inaugural diagnosis. Why did both promise freedom? Give each equal space.
**Supporting questions.** Co-write: What did liberalism change (124 and 125)? Why did confidence weaken (126)? Who built conservatism (127 and 128)? What counts as success (129)? Who ended the Cold War (130 to 132)?
**Closing claim, day 132.** Explain the political turn and Cold War causes using Johnson, Reagan, labor accounts, Solidarity and diplomatic histories. Answer counterevidence.
**Teaching.** Model perspective on 124 (D2.His.4.9-12), economic causation on 126 and weighing causes on 129 (D2.His.14.9-12). Students lead comparison on 129 and write on 132. Use Schlafly as writer and organizer; distinguish recollection from contemporary evidence. Source each document before use.
