## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How did ordinary people use and challenge the law to change it?"
**Hook, day 86.** Compare Parks's earlier organizing with Brown's holding. Why did a ruling need organized action?

**Teaching.** Model sourcing on 86, corroboration on 90, causation on 91, data comparison on 94 and a counterclaim on 98. Research follows on 87 and 97; independent argument follows on 100.
