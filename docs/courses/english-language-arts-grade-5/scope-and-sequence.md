## Unit 1. Starting over: stories of human rights

Read the novel and declaration together before asking for an essay: first accurate quotation, main ideas and discussion, then responses and theme, then informative organization, then a narrative monologue and its recording. ## Assessment coverage and response conventions

A lettered code is carried by its own code. A parent code appears alongside children only when all its parts are listed on that day; it is not a substitute for missing parts. Every assessment's listed parts need a short task, a specified product feature or an observed individual performance. No one group score establishes an individual's speaking or reading proficiency.

The final days distribute reading, language, writing and speaking. The full essay and narrative are built earlier; final portfolio verification includes an independent explanation or brief revision against a new demand, not completion credit alone. W.5.5 evidence includes plans, feedback, revision, editing, rewriting and trying another approach. W.5.6 stays open after unit 1 until the unit 2 device evidence exists. L.5.3b is named initially in unit 2 and fully compared across forms in unit 4. Reading-range judgment is cumulative across stories, drama, poetry and information, with independent high-band evidence at year's end.

The daily skills and writing codes also appear on assessment rows. On these days, save an individual word-reading/spelling sample and the text-based exit response using that week's words. On Tuesday/Thursday assessment days, the partner rereading is an observed individual accuracy, purpose, expression and self-correction sample, not only partner participation. These samples stay within the thirty-minute skills block. Longer teacher-held oral-reading checks have their separate fall, winter and spring windows. The unit plan must align its task-to-part coverage ledger to these reservations; this scope writes no items or answer keys.

Each assessment has exactly one response allocation: for a multi-day mid-unit assessment it sits in the final day, except day 108's response on day 109; for each unit's complete closing assessment it is the final block of days 41, 80, 123 or 156. No extra respond day is hidden in the unit count. The course-level checks have their own three respond days.
