Daily partner talk and written text-based exit tickets continue across the year. Students prepare, carry roles, elaborate with evidence and conclude; frames fade. Writing models precede each process piece, followed by peer critique, teacher feedback, independent revision, editing and publication. ## Unit 2. The rainforest

**Calendar:** days 42 to 80, weeks 9 to 16. Sections 2.1 to 2.9 preserve three 13-lesson phases.

The question is how El Yunque's living things depend on one another and how people protect it. First understand the scientist's account, relationships and different text structures. Next learn to locate, paraphrase and list several sources before independent research. The two whole adventure stories then make narrator influence and comparison within one genre teachable. A modeled literary explanation leads to its own essay before the first-person canopy narrative uses the same facts and sensory language. Six new rows receive most new instruction in the first 17 days; the remainder teaches transfer through literary explanation, narrative revision and actual device use.

Science's ecosystem unit occupies school days 21 to 48, overlapping the opening week. Day 42 therefore includes a ten-minute food-web text bridge, even if science has already reached it. Social studies research does not begin until school day 147, so ELA explicitly teaches the source routine on days 45 to 46 and a ten-minute search model on day 52. No history lesson is assumed to have supplied it.

They cover informational relationships and structures, independent research and discussion, narrator influence and same-genre comparison, literary explanation and fluency, and the independent narrative draft. Feedback and retry are inside the last day of each assessment, not extra days.

The closing assessment is days 76 to 80: grammar functions and sentence changes, independent narrative revision, research transfer to withheld paragraphs with all four text structures represented, uninterrupted two-page typing, online exchange and publication, then independent narrator/genre/language-variety comparison and final folio verification. ## Unit 3. Athletes who led change
