## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How much of Earth's water can people use, and how should communities share it?"
**Hook, day 124.** Read the water-distribution table in 06 beside a hundredths grid. Why does abundant water not mean abundant usable freshwater?
**Inquiry.** Day 126: What does each account emphasize? Day 137: What do images tell beyond words? Day 139: How do successive stanzas develop reflection? Day 142: How do narrators shape the same challenge? Day 145: Which sources explain supply and protection?
**Closing claim.** Day 149's opinion recommends a protection action using 06 and 07; day 151 completes a fresh-choice opinion.
**Teaching.** Model paraphrasing, comparison, informative structure and visual analysis on days 124, 126, 131 and 137. Withdraw evidence choices by day 145.
