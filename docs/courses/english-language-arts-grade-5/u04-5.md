## 9. Supports and extension

Use aspect, relevant and consequently with “The source shows ___. This matters because ___.” Provide large print, accessible searchable text and a scribe or dictated written response as appropriate. Give directions aloud. Keep required source reading in school and let pupils find their own evidence during independent checks. Each reserved extract is at most 300 words on a separate reading copy. Extend by weighing whether one released source can answer a second question, citing its limits.
