## 5. What is carried, and instruments

Students carry dated source notes and the corrected opinion. Review grade 4 section 1.5 day 21, “heading: the main idea of a group of notes, saying what the group is for,” and day 18, “source tag: preserve the profile paragraph or poem line beside each evidence note.” Teacher models one tagged note on 111. Students select and organize their own notes by 113. The ideal instrument is a chart with question, paraphrase, title, line and print or digital columns. Paper fits source tracing, and a managed copy fits an observed digital reading turn. Tool: None. Use a notebook and printed pages if the device is unavailable.
