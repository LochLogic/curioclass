## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "What made these athletes leaders of change, beyond winning?"

**Hook, day 84.** Read the opening of *Jackie Robinson: A Life Beyond the Score*. Which action suggests leadership beyond winning?
**Inquiry.** Day 87: Which interactions changed opportunities? Day 96: What does each account emphasize? Day 98: Which evidence supports each point? Day 103: Which qualities best explain leadership across these sources?
**Closing claim.** Day 108 defends which qualities enabled Robinson to lead change, using all four sources; day 110 revises it.
**Teaching.** Model quotation, comparison and argument mapping on days 84, 91 and 97. Withdraw evidence choices by day 100.


**Text set.** Read 01 in ten ordered packets on days 84 to 87, 89 to 94; 06 on day 84; original replacements 03 on days 91, 92, 96, 98, 100 and 101, 04 on days 92, 96, 98, 100 and 101, and 02 on days 97 to 99 and 101; Abbott on day 111, Clemente and Rudolph on day 112. Editions follow in field 10.
**Complexity.** Preview segregation, track interacting causes and reread qualifying clauses. Fade the event line by day 94.
**Vocabulary.** Teach the scope and sequence lists on the named day, explain during reading, then reuse in an evidence sentence. Historical and writing terms are tier three; other academic words are tier two.

**02, An Argument Against Discrimination.** In 1949, Jackie Robinson spoke at a congressional hearing during a period of political suspicion about communism. His testimony challenged attempts to dismiss protests against racial discrimination as merely communist influence. The underlying injustice, he argued, mattered regardless of who objected to it. Our explanation draws two connected points from that argument. First, public understanding requires taking Black Americans' opposition to discrimination seriously. Their own experience gives them reasons to resist unfair treatment; political labels cannot explain those experiences away. Second, defending democracy requires opposing discrimination beyond baseball. Robinson identified unequal treatment in military service, transport and employment. These examples show that equal opportunity concerns several parts of daily life. The first point explains why protests deserve attention; the second connects democratic principles to specific injustices. This is our explanation, not Robinson's wording.
