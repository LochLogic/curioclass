English Language Arts, Grade 5. Unit 3, Athletes who led change, [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 118 to 121.** Days by kind: understanding 1, application 1, check 2. ELA days by main work: check 2, speak and listen 2. A plan, not a lesson.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **RI.5.6 “Analyze”** viewpoints on a shared event, **RI.5.8 “Explain”** which reason and evidence support each point, and **SL.5.3 “Summarize” and “explain”** a heard argument on 118. The printed and heard arguments stay separate.
- **SL.5.4 “Report” or “present” and “speak clearly”** with order, relevant facts and descriptive details. **SL.5.5 “Include”** visual or multimedia features that clarify an idea. **SL.5.6 “Adapt speech”** for familiar peers and a less-informed panel, using formal English where appropriate. **RI.5.9 “Integrate”** several sources using the corrected chart. Planning and new live performances supply practice. Verify individual speaking from the corrected rehearsal.
- **SL.5.1/a to d “Engage,” “Come,” “Follow,” “Pose and respond,” “Review”** with diverse partners. On 120, record preparation, role and rules, questions and elaboration, and a conclusion from ideas heard. **W.5.4 “Produce”** coherent visitor writing on 119. **W.5.6 “use technology,” “interact and collaborate,” “demonstrate”** through actual device evidence across days. **W.5.10 “Write routinely”** in brief and extended writing.
- **RF.5.3 “Know and apply”** and **RF.5.3a “Use combined knowledge”** of sounds, syllables and morphology in and out of context. **RF.5.4/a/b “Read”** with purpose, meaning, accuracy, suitable pace and expression across successive prose readings on 119. **RF.5.4c “Use context”** to confirm or self-correct after decoding. **L.5.6 “Acquire and use accurately”** presentation and relationship words.
- Explain how labels make a relationship visible and how the same evidence can serve a listener with different background knowledge. Preview racial exclusion, disability, polio, mockery of language and the reference to the fatal crash. Use the athletes' own terms where available. Provide a private pass and keep imitation and personal disclosure out.

## Day 119

Understanding. Speak and listen. “I can make a display that helps a visitor understand one idea.” Students hold the chart and source leaves. Tool: None.

- **10 min. Return and repair.** Return 118 written feedback and audience notes. Use five minutes for the leading common priority, five for independent response.
- **10 min. Words.** Blend and spell longer presentation words. Reuse audience and specifically.
- **5 min. Grammar.** Pick a cause clause that fits an unrelated source sentence.
- **15 min. Partner rereading.** Reread released 01:270 twice. With six managed stations, five pupils at each take two 45-second readings and a 45-second response, 11¼ minutes per station plus handovers. Collect purpose, meaning, accuracy, pace, expression and confirmation or self-correction. Review recorded parts afterward. With one listener hear six in 13½ minutes and use actual winter records for other completed parts. Date the remaining observations.
- **15 min. Display model.** Draw the shortstop's position from released 01:136. A label makes the area between two bases clear. Students use the location label practice sheet to consider the different destination. Ask “What makes the clearer label useful to a visitor?”.
- **15 min. Visitor caption.** Spend 12 minutes independently writing about 100 words for visitors unfamiliar with Robinson or baseball. Recommend a quality, group two reasons, use at least three texts with facts and an exact quotation, link with a clause and conclude. Use three minutes for parallel written peer feedback with teacher monitoring. Save the unscored first version for 120.
- **12 min. Display making.** Students prepare one useful feature and its source label, on paper or in managed files. Model one short spoken finding with an ordered fact and a descriptive detail. Speak it again in meaningful phrases at an understandable pace. Each child practices a finding with their own feature and writes how the feature develops the idea. Get a written cue on that explanation before 120. During managed file work, prioritize any missing individual digital source lookup and record the passage actually found.
- **8 min. Exit.** Exit 119. First release of 05:88 to 92.

**Shared shape.** Practice using a position label to locate a fielder or base. The exit draws a source-supported change with actions and people, then explains what a label makes clear.

- **20 min. Choice reading.** Log and confer where listening time permits. Keep 05:54, the broadcast sentences of 62 and 70 covered.
