## Day 20

Application. “I can use a reader's note to improve what a sentence says.”

- **10 min. Return and repair.** E19 comparison or comma boundary.
- **10 min. Word study.** Read and spell a new -ian word, then check spelling in a reference.
- **20 min. Independent reading.** Log purpose and detail. Confer privately.
- **20 min. Chapter.** Winter Squash, about 15 minutes. Cover the hours card, coat-drive request and proposed third evening. Read the earlier user notes in class.
- **22 min. Discussion and revision.** In six simultaneous groups of five, each brings a note about why Maren asks Ed about earlier users. Allow 45 seconds each to contribute, 45 to ask and 45 to reply, up to 11.25 minutes per group. Use reduced scaffolds and stagger six observed turns. Everyone records a peer's contribution. Choose which of two critique notes addresses focus, then exchange descriptive feedback and revise an own folder paragraph without judging it.
- **8 min. Exit.** E20. Collect the separate revision to a supplied sentence.

## Day 21
