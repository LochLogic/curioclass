- **48, RI.5.2.** Read the whole reserved 02 monitoring selection on printed page 17. Ask “Summarize two ideas about the parrots with one detail for each.” “Which source did you use?” Key: loss and return are supported by zero wild parrots in El Yunque by 2019 and 17 counted there in 2021 after reintroduction. Wrong: “Birds” (A). “There are exactly 17 in all Puerto Rico today” (AE).
  
  Shape: put two supported ideas from a dated report in a short summary and credit it. Keep page 17 and 03’s matching count off earlier pages and visuals. Teach with separate storm records.
- **49A, 12 min, RI.5.2.** On the new A1 to A4 leaves, give separate short prompts to state and support each main idea and then summarize. Key: fixed-place visits support comparison (A1). Damaged labels threaten identification and map checks protect site records (A4). Summary joins comparable measurements and traceable sites. Accept another supported pair such as light in canopy gaps and record protection. Wrong: “Scientists visited a forest” (A).
  
  Shape: synthesize two ideas across a cold set. No task gathers both A ideas before the check.
- **49B, 10 min, RI.5.3.** Ask for the relationship in A3 and each of its two details separately. Key: a falling branch leaves a gap. Fewer leaves intercept light, so more reaches plants below. Wrong: “The branch makes sunlight” (B).
  
  Shape: explain a mechanism with two details. Use released shade examples with other causes. Leave A3 and the branch-fall evidence sealed.
- **49C, 13 min, RI.5.5.** Ask separately how A1 and A4 are organized and what the difference lets each show. Ask separately for the A2 and A3 contrast. Wrong: “They all say then” (C).
  
  Shape: compare four purposes in two pairs. Use single outlines and avoid four-card rehearsal or the same pairings.
- **50, W.5.8 and SL.5.1c.** Read 03’s previously withheld El Verde work in the final two paragraphs of “When a count leaves questions.” Ask “Write one note in your own words.” “Write a question the passage could answer.” Give the note a labeled source-title box. Wrong: copying “Researchers follow changes in living things and their surroundings” unmarked (H). Leaving the title box empty (I).
  
  Shape: originate a sourced note and a question another reader could answer. Inspect copying and relevance in separate released examples. Do not prepare a model that does both on this passage.
- **51, RI.5.3 and SL.5.1d.** Read 01 packet 5’s final paragraph. ## 9. Supports and extension

Explain relevant as helping answer this question. Use “___ extends ___ by showing ___.” Use print heading tabs, accessible screens and a neutral scribe. At about 120 words per minute, leave at least 17 minutes for the longest new read. Read directions aloud. Extend by finding a released detail that narrows a claim.
