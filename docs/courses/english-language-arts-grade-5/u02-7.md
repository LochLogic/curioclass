English Language Arts, Grade 5. Unit 2, The rainforest (days 42 to 80), [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 68 to 72.** ELA days by kind: write 4, check 1. A plan, not a lesson. Nothing here is built or tried with students.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **W.5.3 and W.5.3a to e.** “Write narratives.” “Orient” the reader through teller, situation and connected events. “Use narrative techniques,” “Use a variety” of temporal words, phrases and clauses, “Use concrete words,” and “Provide a conclusion” that follows the event.
- **W.5.4, “Produce.”** Develop and organize a story for a new reader. **W.5.5, “develop and strengthen.”** Use feedback while keeping dated versions. **W.5.10, “Write routinely.”** Make brief planning notes and a sustained story for a named audience.
- **RL.5.6, “Describe.”** On 68, explain how a teller's position changes what readers can know. **L.5.1a, “Explain the function.”** On 70 explain what conjunctions, prepositions and interjections do generally and in a sentence. **L.5.6, “Acquire and use accurately.”** Use narrative and logical terms in context.
- **RF.5.3/a, “Know and apply” and “Use.”** Blend sounds and syllables with word parts. **RF.5.4/a to c, “Read” and “Use context.”** On 69 reread for meaning, accuracy, appropriate pace and expression. Confirm after decoding.
- **Teacher explanation.** A researcher or a forest animal may tell the imaginary event. A teller can describe an observation within their access. Label imagined action, dialogue and animal thoughts as fiction. Keep scientific claims within the federal selections and distinguish the composite account from those observations.
