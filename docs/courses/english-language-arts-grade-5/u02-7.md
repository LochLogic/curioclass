English Language Arts, Grade 5. Unit 2, The rainforest (days 42 to 80), [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 68 to 72.** ELA days by kind: write 4, check 1. A plan, not a lesson. Nothing here is built or tried with students.

## 1. Standards and practices

- **W.5.3 and W.5.3a to e.** “Write narratives.” “Orient” the reader through teller, situation and connected events. “Use narrative techniques,” “Use a variety” of temporal words, phrases and clauses, “Use concrete words,” and “Provide a conclusion” that follows the event.
- **W.5.4, “Produce.”** Develop and organize a story for a new reader. **W.5.5, “develop and strengthen.”** Use feedback while keeping dated versions. **W.5.10, “Write routinely.”** Make brief planning notes and a sustained story for a named audience.
- **RL.5.6, “Describe.”** On 68, explain how a teller's position changes what readers can know. **L.5.1a, “Explain the function.”** On 70 explain what conjunctions, prepositions and interjections do generally and in a sentence. **L.5.6, “Acquire and use accurately.”** Use narrative and logical terms in context.
- **RF.5.3/a, “Know and apply” and “Use.”** Blend sounds and syllables with word parts. **RF.5.4/a to c, “Read” and “Use context.”** On 69 reread for meaning, accuracy, appropriate pace and expression. Confirm after decoding.
- **Teacher explanation.** A researcher or a forest animal may tell the imaginary event. A teller can describe an observation within their access. Label imagined action, dialogue and animal thoughts as fiction. Keep scientific claims within the federal selections and distinguish the composite account from those observations.

  Shape: finish the full arc. Practice removing an irrelevant event from a released plan.

## 6. Days

## Day 68

Write (understanding). “I can plan a scene told by one character.” Students hold three arc boxes and released source lines. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Return.** Return 67F marked work and collect the independent correction for scoring.
- **10 min. Words.** Read and spell mixed four- and five-syllable science words. Check unstressed vowels from letters and parts.
- **20 min. Independent reading.** Log and confer. Reread short released portions of 01, 05 and 06. With one recorder, four pupils take their first four-minute reading from 66.
- **45 min. Model and plan.** Place three released events on the arc and check the link missing from a scrambled version. Compare narrated thought with what a teller actually observed. Assign the 500-word minimum across the drafting days and two typed pages on 78. Use 02 for three checked facts and 01 where it agrees. Keep packet 8's first paragraph unmarked for E68. Carry any needed arc repair to 69.
- **10 min. Exit.** E68. Collect every paper.

## Day 69

Write (application). “I can help a new reader enter my story.” Students hold their own plan. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Return.** Return E68 and retrieve the missing arc connection on a different sequence.
- **10 min. Words.** Mark unstressed syllables and read the word in a sentence.
- **5 min. Grammar.** Inspect a time clause on a released line.
- **15 min. Partner rereading.** Use released 05 speech, twice, for meaning, pace, accuracy and expression. Each partner records the reread cue.
- **45 min. Draft.** Spot missing audience information in an unrelated outline. Draft an unjudged opening. Choose a researcher or forest animal and label imagined speech and thoughts. Keep the muddy-shoe exchange off practice copies.
- **10 min. Exit.** E69.

## Day 70

Write (application). “I can show how my teller responds at an important moment.” Students hold their own arc and notes. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Return.** Correct E69 privately.
- **10 min. Words.** Apply sounds, syllables and morphemes to mixed science words before checking context.
- **20 min. Independent reading.** Log and confer. With one recorder, allow four first reading turns from 66.
- **45 min. Middle.** Retrieve unit 1 grammar functions on released examples. Show generally and in context what each class of word does. Match released dialogue to a response and listen for a slower beat. Draft the turning point privately as folder work. Set aside the snag scene.
- **10 min. Exit.** E70.

## Day 71

Write (application). “I can end my story in a way that follows what happened.” Students hold dated plan and draft. Tool: None.

- **5 min. Return.** Correct E70 pacing.
- **10 min. Words.** Compare base words with -ity, -ian and -ial forms. Verify spelling with references.
- **20 min. Independent reading.** Log and confer. With one recorder, allow four first reading turns from 66.
- **45 min. Draft.** Use the first five minutes for missing grammar functions. Choose an irrelevant beat to cut from a different arc. Finish an unjudged close and check the narrative against the beginning and its sources. Use sequence, clause, accelerate, pause, moment, revise, critique and although in released lines and talk.

Keep required reread leaves together within 350 words on the student page, including directions, about three minutes, inside the reading block.
