## 9. Supports and extension

Preview the meaning of opportunity and barrier and offer “___ could decide ___.” Before a first read give needed word or setting clues. During reading, mark the clause that sets a limit. Afterward, ask what that clause keeps the reader from claiming. Allow large print, oral directions and response access that preserves the skill. Keep A silent for independent evidence. Read the 89 exit aloud in the same voice for everyone. Packets run roughly 1,400 to 1,520 words, about 12 to 13 minutes per first pass. The 06 excerpts take under five minutes within Context. Extend by testing an alternate inference against another released line.
