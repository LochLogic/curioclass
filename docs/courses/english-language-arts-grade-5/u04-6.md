## 1. Standards and practices

- W.5.1 “Write” an opinion for an unfamiliar reader. For W.5.1a, “Introduce,” “state” and “create” a clear topic, position and grouping. In b “Provide” logically ordered reasons supported by facts. In c “Link” position and reasons using words, phrases and clauses. In d “Provide” a related close (150 to 151). W.5.4 “Produce” coherent writing developed and organized for task, purpose and audience.
- W.5.5 “develop and strengthen” a draft through planning, guidance, substantive revision and editing (151, 153). W.5.6 “use technology” to “produce and publish” and “interact and collaborate” in a closed class collection. For “type a minimum of two pages in a single sitting,” cite actual unit 2 keyboarding evidence once. L.5.2e “Spell” correctly with references (151).
- W.5.2 “Write” to explain. For W.5.2a, “Introduce,” “provide,” “group” and “include” a clear topic, focus, logical groups and helpful headings or illustration. In b “Develop” with facts, definitions and details. In c “Link” ideas within and across groups with words, phrases and clauses. In d “Use” precise terms. In e “Provide” a related close (152). W.5.7 “Conduct” inquiry into distinct aspects from several sources. W.5.8 “Recall” or “gather” relevant information, “summarize or paraphrase” and “provide a list” (152).
- RL.5.7 “Analyze” how a literary illustration contributes to meaning, tone and beauty (152). RL.5.6 “Describe” the influence of narrator access, and RL.5.9 “Compare and contrast” two same-genre approaches to a theme (153). RL.5.10 and RI.5.10 “read and comprehend” independently at verified high-band demand across stories, drama and poetry and across history, science and technical texts.
- Explain which claims a dated source supports and how an explanatory diagram serves a different purpose from literary art. Preview drought, threatened plants and interrupted supply, and distinct tribal water rights in the nation's account. Offer a released 06 science passage as a private alternative. Preserve independent evidence conditions and keep family access and bills private.
