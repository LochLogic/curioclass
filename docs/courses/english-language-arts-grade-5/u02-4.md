English Language Arts, Grade 5. Unit 2, The rainforest (days 42 to 80). See [s02.md](s02.md). **Days 55 to 58.** ELA days by kind: read literature 3, check 1. A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

## 1. Standards and practices

- RL.5.6 “Describe” how a teller’s vantage point shapes a description. RL.5.9 “Compare and contrast” approaches to one theme in two adventures. L.5.3b “Compare and contrast” conversational and reporting registers by audience and purpose.
- RL.5.1 “Quote accurately” for a stated action or a separate inference. RL.5.4 “Determine” contextual meaning. L.5.5a “Interpret” comparisons. L.5.5b “Recognize and explain” customary sayings. RL.5.10 “Read and comprehend” the grade-band stories, recording support.
- RF.5.3 “Know and apply” and “Use” sounds, syllables and meaningful parts. RF.5.4 “Read” with purpose, accuracy and expressive phrasing and “Use context” to confirm. W.5.10 “Write routinely.” L.5.6 “Acquire and use accurately” viewpoint and contrast words.
- Explain which thoughts a narrator lets the reader know, including in close third person. An idea is explicit when the text states it directly. An inference is supported by what the writer says or shows. Conversational language serves speakers and reporting language serves record readers.
