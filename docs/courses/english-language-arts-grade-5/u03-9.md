- **RI.5.9 “Integrate”** evidence from several sources in the corrected visitor caption on 122 and through the corrected rehearsal on 123. **SL.5.5 “Include”** a feature that develops a main idea. **SL.5.4 “Report” or “present” and “speak clearly”** through an ordered, audible report with relevant facts and description. **SL.5.6 “Adapt speech”** to different listeners and tasks, using formal English where appropriate. **W.5.10 “Write routinely”** for different purposes and audiences over short and sustained periods.
