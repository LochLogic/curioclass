## 1. Standards and practices

- **RI.5.6 “Analyze”** important similarities and differences in perspectives on a shared event. **RI.5.8 “Explain”** how a particular reason and its evidence support a point. **SL.5.3 “Summarize” and “explain”** a speaker's points and the support for each, on 97 to 99 and 101. Teach the link between a reason and evidence before the child chooses evidence independently.
- **L.5.2b “Use a comma”** after an introductory element and **L.5.2d “Use”** a title marking on 100. **L.5.6 “Acquire and use accurately”** words for argument and logical relationships. **W.5.10 “Write routinely”** in account comparisons and argument notes for the next extended piece.
- **RF.5.3/3a “Know and apply,” “Use combined knowledge”** of letters and sounds, syllables and morphology in and out of context. **RF.5.4/a/b “Read”** for meaning, accuracy, suitable pace and expression across successive prose readings. **RF.5.4c “Use context”** to confirm or self-correct after decoding.
- Explain how the same 1947 game can open a story about innings or hiring. Distinguish the 1949 hearing from the 2026 explanation of it. The accounts contain racial exclusion and unequal treatment in service, transport and hiring. The hearing includes suspicion directed at protests. Preview without slurs or role play. Keep claims within what a text supports.

## 9. Supports and extension

Use point, reason and evidence with “___ supports ___ because ___.” Before reading supply only the necessary hearing context. During reading chunk qualifying clauses. Afterward test which point each clause serves. Cap a pass through extracts at 250 words, about two to three minutes. Teacher voices the designated 02 and check selections. Use accessible print and directions. Keep independent reading independent and deliver the oral script equally. Extension tests whether one released detail can support two different defensible points.
