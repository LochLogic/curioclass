## 1. Standards and practices

- **W.5.1 “Write opinion pieces.”** **a “Introduce,” “state,” “create”** logical groups. **b “Provide”** ordered, supported reasons. **c “Link”** opinion and reasons with words, phrases and clauses. **d “Provide”** a conclusion tied to the position. **W.5.9b “Apply”** informational reading to support analysis, then use it in the opinion. **RI.5.9 “Integrate”** details from 01 to 04 around a shared idea. **W.5.4 “Produce”** coherent writing for a newcomer. **W.5.5 “develop and strengthen”** with an independent substantive revision and a separate edit. **W.5.10 “Write routinely”** across short tasks and a sustained essay. **L.5.2b “Use a comma”** after an introductory element on 109.
- **RF.5.3/3a “Know and apply,” “Use combined knowledge”** of sounds, syllables and parts in and out of context. **RF.5.4/a/b “Read”** for purpose, accuracy, suitable pace and expression on successive prose readings. **RF.5.4c “Use context”** to confirm or self-correct. **L.5.6 “Acquire and use accurately”** fact, detail and links including nevertheless.
- Explain how a quote advances a reason, where context belongs and why a related conclusion does fresh work. Distinguish an honor from a decision to hire and distinguish Jackie and Frank Robinson. Preview racism, threats, hiring exclusion and the biography's reference to Jackie's death. Keep slurs out, allow a private pass and keep questions on the text.

## 6. Days

## Day 107

- **10 min. Return and repair.** Return 106's written cue. Correct a missing piece of context or position independently.
- **10 min. Words.** Compare stress and spelling in conclusion and conclude, in isolation and in released material.
- **5 min. Grammar.** Choose the better of two ways to combine an unrelated supporting clause.
- **15 min. Partner rereading.** Revisit released 01:402 twice. Fifteen pairs swap 45-second turns and teacher hears six in 4½ minutes. Give purpose, meaning, accuracy, pace, expression and confirmation feedback.

## 9. Supports and extension

Offer reason, context and nevertheless with “This detail supports ___ because ___.” Preview relevant terms, chunk sources at qualifying clauses and compare what each contributes afterward. Keep each pass under 250 words, roughly two to three minutes. Offer accessible source text and oral directions. Record any response support and keep independent writing and spelling distinct. Extend through another warranted source choice for the same reason and audience.
