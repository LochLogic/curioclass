# Course frame: English Language Arts, Grade 5

Course id ela-k12 (grade 5 row), grade 5, draft frame, written 2026-11-14; the scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 4, following the base text's grade 5 modules 1 to 3 in order, with unit 4 on water (Cory's yes,
  DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-13) built on module 4's design (research, survival stories and poems, an opinion essay, a
  recorded announcement).
- **Days.** 160 teaching days: 153 in units and 7 at course level (4 for two course-level checks, 3 respond days).
  One day is one literacy block of about 90 minutes, five days a week: the unit lesson (about 60 minutes) and the
  skills block (about 30 minutes: the long-word and spelling sequence every day, then partner rereading for
  fluency or independent reading conferences on alternate days). The base text plans one 60-minute module lesson a
  day (151 lessons in grade 5) plus a second 60-minute block; Curio Class keeps the module lesson whole (units 1 to
  3 take their 118 lessons as 118 days) and gives unit 4 two more days than module 4's 33 for the community water
  research the new topic adds, and folds the second block's word study, fluency and independent reading into 30
  minutes (For the lead 1).
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for ELA, grade 5 (data/standards/ccss-ela-standards-full.txt), all
  30 progression rows of grade 5 (data/progressions/ela.csv), every lettered part taught in the unit named below.
  The bar is docs/RUBRIC-ELA.md (grades 3 to 5, all three gateways, Gateway 1 in full: Cory's yes, 2026-11-12) and
  the approach is docs/APPROACH-ELA.md. The values rule (DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-12) governs every unit.
- **Base text.** The 2017 K to 5 language arts curriculum recorded in docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md (CC BY 4.0; copy
  in S:\CurioClass-sources\el-education-k5-2017), grade 5, modules 1 to 4. Its lesson design, sequence, protocols
  and assessments are adapted; its trade books are replaced by Curio Class texts (BASELINE rule 29: newer texts,
  mostly original Curio Class texts written to the grade 5 text spec, each read by a qualified ELA teacher before
  use); its own CC BY texts may be adapted; its third-party excerpts are replaced; public-domain texts may stay.
  Its name is never used on a Curio Class page; one credit line per adapted day. The 2025 edition and the 2024
  skills block are never used. The source map closes this frame.
- **Which row sits where.** A row sits in the unit that first teaches its grade 5 step. Before then a unit uses the
  grade 4 form of the skill, and the first unit that leans on a grade 4 skill opens with a readiness check on it.
  Later units practise every placed row again on new texts; the table names those returns as review.
- **Reading level.** Anchors climb through the grades 4 to 5 band (measure about 740 to 1010) to its high end read
  alone by year end (e5-reading-range). About half the anchors are literary and half informational. Every anchor
  gets a text record (measure, what makes it hard, the task, why here) in
  data/plans/english-language-arts-grade-5/texts.csv when its unit is planned.
- **Long texts.** Two read whole: an original novel (unit 1) and an original biography (unit 3).
- **Words, read and never guessed.** The long-word sequence (e5-long-words) runs every day from day 1 with a check
  every few weeks; spelling follows it.
- **Writing.** Every reading day ends in a written exit answer quoting the day's text (W.5.9, W.5.10). Each unit
  takes at least one piece through the whole process. The year keeps the grades 3 to 5 shares: about 30 percent
  opinion, 35 informative, 35 narrative. Research runs in unit 2 and again in unit 4.
- **Numbers.** No math ahead of math: Earth's water is shown as hundredths (97 of 100 parts salt water, on a
  hundredths grid, taught in grade 4 math), never as a percent, which is grade 6.
- **Talk.** Partner talk daily; a formal text-based discussion about every two weeks.
- **Paper path and devices.** Every anchor has a print copy and every task a paper route. Keyboarding (two pages in
  a sitting), publishing, the presentation and the recorded announcement happen in the school day on a shared
  classroom device rotation or a lab session, with no student account; open searching is supervised; home practice
  is optional. If no device route exists by year end, W.5.6 is recorded unfinished for that class
  (e5-writing-process).
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent sensitivity read. Unit 1 (a father's death, a family's
  loss of its home, farm work and forced moves), unit 3 (racism and segregation in sport, the words of the time)
  and unit 4 (drought and flood, which some students have lived through) also need a qualified person's read when
  one is found (FOUNDATION section 7).
