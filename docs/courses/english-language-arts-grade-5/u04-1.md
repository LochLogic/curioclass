**s04-X124.** First release 06's “Table continued” rows through Rivers, with the first four rows still available. Ask: “Show fresh lakes and rivers on the freshwater grid. What does their size tell you?”
Key: fresh lakes are 0.26 per hundred freshwater and rivers 0.006 per hundred freshwater. Both are below one cell. Label pointers into the first cell as approximate and retain the table values beside them. Most freshwater is stored in ice or underground. Fresh surface stores take a very small share and storage alone does not establish drinkability. “Fresh lakes take 0.007 of the freshwater grid” (A).
Shape: interpret two small table entries on the freshwater whole. Model only whole-cell approximations on released upper rows. Withhold the continuation, its hints and both lower-row values from print, screen and audio.

**s04-X125.** First hear 08 oral paragraph 2, from “My written testimony,” and show its freshwater grid beside the all-water grid. Ask: “What two ideas belong in a summary of the talk and display? Support each idea.”
Key: in 2022 Touton connected heat, earlier snowmelt and dry soils to early, low runoff and described a basin-by-basin response. The global display shows separate wholes: about 97 of 100 of all water in oceans, seas and bays, and about 69 of 100 of freshwater frozen. Its storage picture gives context for why a river shortage matters. “69 cells out of all Earth's water are ice” (A); “The grids caused the low runoff” (AS).

**s04-X126.** First release 07's sentences about Entsminger's population and water use and Stern's two-year weather records. Ask: “How do the accounts see the same shortage differently? What finding do they support together?”
Key: both address shortage. The city account reports lower use despite growth. The federal account places the drought across western states. Municipal savings can coexist with regional drought. Name both sources. “Southern Nevada's population fell” (A); “Entsminger and Stern spoke” (V).
Shape: write a shared finding with a supported difference in emphasis. Practice labels single-source emphases and examines one weak label. Mask the complete comparison sentences and related captions, including the third and fourth accounts' definitions if they reveal a finding.

**s04-X127.** First release 07 from O'Toole's earlier reservoir through its role in his argument, and the Flores farmland choice through the December letter's conservation sentence. Ask: “How does each detail support its own point about water use?”

**s04-X128.** First release the 06 sentence ending with the separate agreement for Mexico in print and the 07 indoor-water sentence digitally. Ask: “Find Mexico in print and indoor water on screen. Compare the responses in a source-labeled note.”
