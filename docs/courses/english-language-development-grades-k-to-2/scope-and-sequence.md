# Scope and sequence: English Language Development, grades K to 2

> **Held on C11 (Cory 2026-11-26, Desk q-eld-scope-1).** A draft, written before the ELD C11 gate passed (docs/courses/eld/STANDARD.md section 6). It has had no expert read or fix pass; the read, fix, unit plans and days wait until C11 passes, and the read then runs on this draft.

Course `english-language-development-grades-k-to-2`, a band course of three grade years, 480 days (160 a year), written
2026-11-26 from the frame (docs/courses/english-language-development-grades-k-to-2/frame.md) under Cory's call "do the
rest of the scopes and sequences". The days live in data/plans/english-language-development-grades-k-to-2/days.csv; this
page carries the judgment.

## Years, standards and school days

| Year | Band days | Units | Standards owned | Standards carried (`~` in codes) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kindergarten | 1 to 160 | 1 to 6 | WIDA ELD-LA, SC, SS and MA kindergarten ids (both modes) | WIDA.ELD-SI.K-3 (owned in grade 3) |
| Grade 1 | 161 to 320 | 7 to 13 | WIDA ELD-LA, SC, SS and MA grade 1 ids (both modes) | WIDA.ELD-SI.K-3 |
| Grade 2 | 321 to 480 | 14 to 20 | none: cluster 2-3 ids are owned in grade 3 | WIDA.ELD-SI.K-3 and every 2-3 id |

ELD meets five days a week, so `school_day` = band day, and frame.py splits the band by 160. Year day = band day minus
160 times the year index (K 0, grade 1 1, grade 2 2); week = (year day - 1) // 5 + 1. Every unit and check below falls in
the weeks the frame and the CALENDAR.md table give.

How every unit runs (the CSV names each day): day 1 hook with the unit question; day 2 the retrieval task (grades 1 and
2, rule 2: the earlier year's rows, by grade number) or the SI routine; then text level, sentence level (the Language
Dive routine adapted from EL Education K-5 Language Arts, 2017, CC BY 4.0, credited on the day) and word level; the SI
row; the model built with the teacher; first application day (frames off for entry points 5 and 6 from the next day);
the change of audience and noticing what changed; the self-check page (from unit 2); make alone (frames off for entry
points 3 and 4; 1 and 2 keep the picture word bank and lose the cloze frame); oral rehearsal; two end-of-unit
assessment days (about 15 one-minute talk tasks each, for a class of 30); one respond day. Fluency is 30 percent of
teaching days (118 of 399), above the frame's quarter, since oral rehearsal carries K to 2. No unit needs a readiness
check (frame), so no day is marked `readiness`. Proficiency levels are the frame's three entry points (1 and 2, 3 and
4, 5 and 6), the same task at each; moves between entry points are decided on the respond day after a course-level
check.

## Kindergarten

### Unit 1. Toys and our classroom
Days 1 to 30, weeks 1 to 6. Host: ELA grade K unit 1, Toys and play. Arc: knee-to-knee partners (days 1 to 3), topic
and two facts on the text map (days 4 and 5), "It has", "It can" (days 6 to 8), SI Inform describing and directions
(days 9, 11 to 13), find-it game, all-about page with the teacher (day 16), first application day 19, hidden-object
audience change day 20. Mid-unit assessment day 15 (describe a new object), with ten minutes of regrouping on day 16.
End-of-unit assessment days 28 and 29, respond day 30. Candidates settled: California 1.26 (all four domains) on days
4, 19; PRIME Match.LE on day 28; bar item 12 oral self-check day 22; 0 days added.

### Unit 2. Weather stories
Days 31 to 61, weeks 7 to 13. Bridge: ELA reads its weather stories from day 49, so days 31 to 48 use class events and
ELA unit 1's toy story. Arc: two-step class telling (SI Narrate), story map (day 35), story stages named (day 36), past
tense and "and then" (days 38 to 41), audience change (days 42 and 43), feeling words, weather story taken apart from
day 49, own story with a character from day 52. Mid-unit assessment day 46. End days 59 and 60, respond 61. Candidates
settled: PRIME Depth.KLU day 36; bar item 12 self-check page first use day 56; 0 days added.

### Unit 3. Weather and living things
Days 62 to 85, weeks 13 to 17. Host: science K weather watch (retrieved), then living things from day 73. Day 63
retrieves unit 1's text map; SI Narrate review day 66 (three parts, one step harder than unit 2); "because" begun day
68. End days 83 and 84, respond 85 (see For the lead, item 1). Candidates settled: bar item 10 family audience day 80;
PRIME Breadth.LE on the end-of-unit assessment; 0 days added. Its record is carried unscored into the mid-year check.

### Kindergarten mid-year check
Days 86 and 87 (week 18), respond day 88. See "Course-level checks".

### Unit 4. Pushes, pulls and the sun
Days 89 to 113, weeks 18 to 23. Hosts: science K pushes and pulls (retrieved), sun from day 100. Arc: how-to and why
(SI Explain), recount of the push test (SI Narrate review day 92), cause chain (day 94), "When ___, ___ because ___"
(day 95), comparing two tries, sun and shade from day 100, first application day 103. End days 111 and 112, respond 113.
Candidates settled: California 3.3 language or learning need note day 89; PRIME Depth.ELD day 95; 0 days added.

### Unit 5. Helpers and work
Days 114 to 135, weeks 23 to 27. Host: social studies K doing our part, then goods and services from day 122 (no prices
or coins). Arc: helper descriptions on the text map, SI Argue choice with a reason (day 120), claim chart (day 124),
practice vote (day 125), class vote (day 128). End days 133 and 134, respond 135. Candidate settled: bar item 4 class
vote days 125 and 128; 0 days added. Its record is carried unscored into the year-end check.

## Shorter path

- **A year about 10 days short:** 10 fold-in Fluency days a year, 30 minutes each (300 minutes a year, 900 across the
  band, 30 of 480 days). Each folds into the next day's first ten minutes as a short oral round; nothing assessed and
  no owned part is cut. Kindergarten days 10, 22, 45, 53, 67, 72, 99, 109, 119, 152 (units 1 to 4 two each, 5 and 6
  one each). Grade 1 days 13, 18, 33, 37, 56, 72, 97, 107, 122, 155. Grade 2 days 6, 31, 41, 53, 64, 89, 95, 125, 129,
  152. The CSV marks none of them; the list here is the record.
- **A shorter period (20 minutes):** Fluency days run two rounds, not three; Understanding days keep the take-apart and
  drop the second rebuild; check days spread the 30 talk tasks over the two Check days and the first ten minutes of the
  Respond day.
- **Leave-out bridge rule:** where a fold-in day is dropped, the next day opens with its cards (the partner round or
  the sentence cards), so a later day that uses those cards still has them.
- **Newcomer strand:** an added 20-day period a year with the ELD teacher, outside the 160 days (frame For the lead,
  item 6, Cory 2026-11-26). Days 1 to 5 names, greetings, the room and asking for help; days 6 to 10 routines and
  directions (SI Inform); days 11 to 15 the year's Inform ids on the current host unit's pictures (kindergarten LA, MA,
  SC, SS; grade 1 the same at grade 1; grade 2 LA 2-3 Inform only); days 16 to 20 telling and the short catch-up card
  set for the current unit, then the newcomer joins it at entry points 1 and 2. The strand gets its own sensitivity
  read before use.

## Sources and sensitivity

No source a day leans on is unloaded: texts are the ELA host units' EL Education texts (2017 edition, CC BY 4.0, via
the ELA courses), the host units' Curio Class pages and records, two Aesop fables (Townsend 1867, public domain,
retold), public-domain agency photographs, and Curio Class's own end-of-unit content. Sensitivity rides in the notes:
own-story prompts always allow an invented character or chosen place (units 2, 8, 20); no task asks about family work,
a former school, where a family is from or how it came (units 5, 7, 11, 14, 16, 19); unit 12 never turns to "How are
you like your parents?"; unit 19's family note and alternative pair. Family notes go out in English and Spanish and,
with the newcomer strand, get the read before use.
