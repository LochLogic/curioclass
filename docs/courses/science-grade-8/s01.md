<!-- skel -->
- **MS-PS2-1:** Use the idea that paired forces are equal and opposite (Newton's third law) to solve a design problem about two objects colliding. Limit: Only collisions along one straight line, either vertical or horizontal.
- **MS-PS2-2:** Plan an investigation showing that how an object's motion changes depends on the total force on it and on its mass. Emphasis: balanced and unbalanced forces (first and second laws), qualitative comparisons of force, mass and motion change, frame of reference, and stating units. Limit: Only one-dimensional motion in a non-accelerating frame, changing one variable at a time; no trigonometry.
- **MS-PS3-1:** Make and read graphs of data to describe how kinetic energy depends on an object's mass and on its speed. Emphasis: describe how kinetic energy relates to mass apart from how it relates to speed.
- **MS-LS1-8:** Collect and combine information showing that sense receptors react to stimuli by signaling the brain, leading to quick behavior or stored memories. Limit: Leave out how the signals are carried.
- **MS-ETS1-2:** Judge competing design solutions step by step against the problem's criteria and constraints.
- **MS-ETS1-3:** Use test data to compare several designs and pick out the best features of each to combine into a better new design.
