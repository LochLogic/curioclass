## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How can a sound make something move?"

**Hook, day 27:** Show an original truck and window drawing and the quiet bowl demonstration. Distinguish reported window motion from observed rice motion; sketch their possible connection.
**Closing explanation, day 44:** Explain the window using vibration records, medium comparisons and receiver motion. Greater amplitude transfers more energy under matched conditions; source matter does not travel the whole path.
**Teaching:** Model axes on day 32 and source comparison on day 42; students choose comparisons and defend revisions. Day 41 compares a small and a larger driving amplitude in words only, so day 43 starts from an observed difference.

Days 33 to 36 distinguish amplitude and frequency. What crosses the gap? Days 37 to 40 compare media. Why does a receiver move? Days 41 to 44 connect matter, receptors and energy. Day 45 transfers to a vibrating inspection strip.
**Limits:** Quantify regular mechanical waves only; omit sound-speed equations, decibels, resonance and nerve-signal mechanisms. MS-PS4-2 contributes sound here; unit 4 completes light. No engineering expectation is claimed.
