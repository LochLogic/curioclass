- **MS-ESS1-1:** Build and use a model of Earth, sun and moon to explain the repeating patterns of moon phases, eclipses and seasons.
- **MS-PS4-2:** Build and use a model showing that materials reflect, absorb or let waves pass through them. Emphasis: both light and mechanical waves. Limit: Only qualitative uses involving light and mechanical waves.
- **MS-ESS1-3:** Use data to work out the sizes, distances and other scale properties of solar system objects. Emphasis: data from ground instruments, space telescopes and spacecraft, used to compare solar system objects (layer sizes, surface features, orbit size).
- **MS-PS2-4:** Make and present an evidence-based argument that gravity always pulls objects together and depends on the masses involved. Limit: Leave out the universal law of gravitation formula and Kepler's laws.
- **MS-ESS1-2:** Build and use a model of how gravity governs motion in galaxies and in the solar system. Emphasis: gravity holds the solar system and the Milky Way together and controls the orbits in them. Limit: Leave out Kepler's laws and the apparent backward (retrograde) motion of planets seen from Earth.
