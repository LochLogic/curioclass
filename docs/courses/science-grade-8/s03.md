- **MS-PS2-3:** Ask questions about data to find out what affects how strong electric and magnetic forces are. Limit: Questions needing number answers stay within proportional reasoning and algebraic thinking.
- **MS-PS2-5:** Run an investigation, and judge its design, to gather evidence that fields act between objects that push or pull without touching. Limit: Only electric and magnetic fields, with qualitative evidence that the fields exist.
- **MS-PS3-2:** Build a model showing that when objects that interact at a distance are rearranged, the system stores a different amount of potential energy. Emphasis: comparing amounts of potential energy, not computing them. Limit: Only two objects at a time, interacting through electric, magnetic or gravitational forces.
