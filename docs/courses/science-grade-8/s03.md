## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How can a magnet move another object without touching it?"

**Hook, day 47:** Observe the opened speaker and teacher-operated coil moving across a visible magnetic gap. Sketch its possible cause.
**Closing explanation, day 65:** Connect current direction, coil field, magnetic force and cone motion using compass, reversal and energy evidence. Revise on day 67; synthesize on day 72.
**Teaching:** Model measurement on day 49 and field arrows on day 54; students justify revisions.
