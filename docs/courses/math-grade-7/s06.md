## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How can an equation help us find what we do not know?"
**Hook, day 92:** A class delivery costs $7 plus $3 per packet, invented for practice. Why does doubling the packets not double the cost? Attempt for five minutes, then connect fixed and repeated amounts to the compelling question. Field 10 identifies the source structure.
**Questions:** Where does the added amount belong, day 97? What preserves equality, day 98? Why does a negative factor reverse order, day 107? What can a rewritten expression reveal, day 116?
**Closing claim:** Equations find unknown quantities, inequalities describe admissible values, and equivalent expressions expose relationships. The day 102 model, day 110 constraint and day 116 rewrite support this claim; day 117 checks it.
**Teaching:** Model the tape connection on day 94 and equivalent equation steps on days 98 and 99. Include signed rational examples on day 99 before fluency. Teach boundary testing on day 107 and rational distribution, factoring and collecting terms on days 111 and 112. Step back for days 102, 110 and 116.

- **7.EE.B.4:** Use variables to represent quantities in a real-world or mathematical problem, and construct simple equations and inequalities to solve problems by reasoning about the quantities. Parts, by verb: a (7.EE.B.4a) Solve word problems leading to equations of the form px + q = r and p(x + q) = r, where p, q, and r are specific rational numbers. ## U7. Talk and writing

Ask what an outside addition counts on day 97, why equal operations preserve a solution on day 98 and what changes when an inequality is divided by a negative on day 107. R1's explanation connects equivalent forms to the percent base and calculation order; field 5 gives look-fors and samples.

## U8. Practice that returns

Use field 4's retrieval. After day 102, mix both equation forms with signed arithmetic and decimal expansions. Require an independent attempt before feedback. Accuracy, flexible method choice and substitution show fluency without time pressure; no standard algorithm is prescribed. Days 113 to 115 consolidate expression transformations already taught on days 111 and 112.

## U9. Supports and extension

Teach coefficient, constant, equivalent, boundary and solution set with labeled examples. Offer "The boundary is ___, and ___ works because ___." Read contexts aloud; students rehearse explanations before writing. Keep independent reading to the short prompt. Offer large-print or tactile lines, explicit signs without color dependence and a scribe following directions. Extend by constructing two different contexts with the same inequality and explaining why their admissible values differ. Keep the same standard and goal.

## U10. Two rooms

With one child, the adult supplies a contrasting claim while the child draws, solves and explains independently. Keep the full independent assessment time. Day 118 ends with a repaired model and named unfinished parts for geometry. With one projector, display one prompt while every student works on paper; no device is required.
