# Course frame: Math Grade 7

Course `math-grade-7` (catalogue id `math-k8`), grade 7, draft frame (2026-11-13). The scope and sequence fixes every day (fix pass, 2026-11-15).

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8 units, in Illustrative Mathematics' grade 7 unit order (IM 7.1 to 7.8): scale drawings, proportional
  relationships, circles, percentages, rational number arithmetic, expressions, equations and inequalities, angles,
  triangles and prisms, probability and sampling. IM's optional ninth unit is left out (Source map).
- **Days.** 160 course days, one 50-minute lesson a day, five lessons a week for 32 weeks: 153 in units and 7 course-level check/respond days. Checks 1 and 2 each take one day, check 3 two days, each followed by one respond day. Every unit has an end-of-unit assessment window and one response allocation. Units 2, 4, 5 and 6 also keep their mid-unit assessment and respond days. The former readiness allocations in units 2 and 4 are retrieval-linked practice. Unit 5 keeps its decimal-expansion completion. Unit 8 merges two sample-space lessons to teach both population-comparison methods before its original two-day assessment window. The day-level source map names each allocation; mixed practice runs in existing openings.
- **The time a day.** One 50-minute lesson a day, five a week, 32 weeks: an IM lesson needs a period of at least 45
  minutes (ms-teacher-guide), and the other 5 open the day with spaced, mixed practice (APPROACH-MATH). The pick is
  in "For the lead".
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 7. All 43 grade 7 codes (lettered parts
  included) are owned by the 23 grade 7 progression rows, and every row sits in one unit below. Cluster weights are
  Student Achievement Partners' (ccss-math-focus.csv): major 7.RP.A, 7.NS.A, 7.EE.A, 7.EE.B; supporting 7.SP.A,
  7.SP.C; additional 7.G.A, 7.G.B, 7.SP.B. The bar is RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md. Math has no check set. The C3 column names
  the unit's practices; the "Check set adds" column records the settled Progressions planning constraint and extension decisions, with time inside the existing days.
- **Major work.** Count minutes on named major-cluster activities over instructional minutes, excluding assessment and respond days and assessment slots within teaching days. Count a minute once; earlier-grade retrieval does not qualify. Connected geometry/statistics work qualifies only where a grade 7 major-code activity and its time are explicit. The scope and sequence states the single calculation and the daily file supplies its allocations. Unit lengths and ownership stay fixed.
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, 1st edition (2019 to 2021), grade 7, CC BY 4.0, from
  im.kendallhunt.com, in the source store (S:\CurioClass-sources\illustrative-math\grade-7, docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md).
  IM's lessons are adapted into Curio Class days in our voice, each with the credit line (with Open Up Resources).
  Only IM's open pages are used; cool-downs, answer keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken
  (LOGIN-GATED.csv; DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-13), so every exit ticket, check and assessment is Curio Class's own. The 2nd
  edition (accessim.org) is CC BY-NC and never used. Applets and third-party images are not carried over.
- **Practices.** Each unit names the one or two Standards for Mathematical Practice it builds (in the C3 column), and
  the unit plan names the task where students do them. All eight are placed: MP1 (unit 4), MP2 (units 1, 2, 6), MP3
  (units 7, 8), MP4 (units 2, 4, 8), MP5 (units 3, 7), MP6 (unit 1), MP7 (units 5, 6), MP8 (units 3, 5).
- **Personal finance, lightly.** Unit 4 already teaches simple interest, tax, markups and markdowns, tips and fees
  (7.RP.A.3); its tasks use given prices, rates and wages, never a family's own money. These are the course's light
  personal finance touches (Cory, 2026-11-14: no course of its own, Economics teaches it), with no added day.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 7.NS.A.1 to 7.NS.A.3 and 7.EE.B.4a carry the grade's expected fluency (signed
  arithmetic, px + q = r and p(x + q) = r). Units 5 and 6 build it; the daily opening practice keeps it up through units 7 and 8. No
  tool shows a countdown timer to a student.
- **Readiness or retrieval.** A row last used in grade g receives retrieval in g+1 and readiness in g+2 or later; a passing retrieval does not reset last use. All grade 6 prerequisites get unmarked retrieval before first use. Unit 1 checks m4-angle-concepts and m4-protractor in day 1's last ten minutes after the hook, then rebuilds them on day 2. Unit 5 checks m5-add-sub-unlike and m5-fraction-add-sub-problems on day 68, then rebuilds on day 71 before fractional signed sums. Unit 7 checks m4-additive-angles, m5-shape-hierarchy and m5-volume-additive in day 120's last ten minutes; rebuilds occur on days 122, 126 and 132 respectively. Angle measure and protractor use, taught on days 2 and 3, get retrieval in unit 7. The scope and sequence lists every earlier row, last-use grade and first placement.
- **Paper path and accounts.** Every task has a paper path (grid and tracing paper, ruler, protractor, compass,
  number cubes, paper bags of slips for sampling). 7.G.A.2's "with technology" is a student performance: students
  construct and test shapes with a drawing or construction tool that needs no student account, in pairs or small
  groups taking turns at the controls on a shared device. The teacher's demonstration introduces the task; paper
  construction is further practice and evidence and does not replace the students' own digital construction. The
  unit plan names the tool and how every student gets a turn before it claims 7.G.A.2 in full. Calculators are used
  where a unit says so, after estimating.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh sensitivity agent's read (docs/pipeline/briefs/sensitivity-read.md);
  no unit has cultural, Native, hard-history, health or disability content that needs a named community study. The
  answer-key agent reads every check, assessment and key, solving each item cold beside the script check (BASELINE
  rule 27), before the course is called finished. Word problems use a range of names and settings; the unit plan
  lists the people and places its problems show.
