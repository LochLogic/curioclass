# Course frame: Math Grade 7

Course id math-grade-7, grade 7, draft frame (2026-11-13). The scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8 units, in Illustrative Mathematics' grade 7 unit order (IM 7.1 to 7.8): scale drawings, proportional
  relationships, circles, percentages, rational number arithmetic, expressions, equations and inequalities, angles,
  triangles and prisms, probability and sampling. IM's optional ninth unit is left out (Source map).
- **Days.** 160 teaching days: 154 in units, 6 for course-level checks and respond days (three cumulative checks of
  1 day, each followed by 1 respond day). Each unit's days are its IM lessons as kept in the source map plus Curio
  Class days: an end-of-unit assessment and its respond day in every unit, and in the major-work units a mid-unit
  check, its respond day and mixed-practice days.
- **The time a day.** One 50-minute lesson a day, five a week, 32 weeks: an IM lesson needs a period of at least 45
  minutes (ms-teacher-guide), and the other 5 open the day with spaced, mixed practice (APPROACH-MATH). The pick is
  in "For the lead".
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 7. All 43 grade 7 codes (lettered parts
  included) are owned by the 23 grade 7 progression rows, and every row sits in one unit below. Cluster weights are
  Student Achievement Partners' (ccss-math-focus.csv): major 7.RP.A, 7.NS.A, 7.EE.A, 7.EE.B; supporting 7.SP.A,
  7.SP.C; additional 7.G.A, 7.G.B, 7.SP.B. The bar is RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md. Math has no check set of other states: the
  "Check set adds" column records candidates only.
- **Major work.** Counted by days: about 98 of 160 days (61 percent) on major clusters strictly (units 2, 4, 5, 6, the
  ratio days of unit 1 and about two thirds of the course-level days); about 114 days (71 percent) when the
  supporting sampling and probability days, which use percent and proportional reasoning, are counted. By units: 4
  of 8 strictly, 5 of 8 with unit 1 (scale drawings are proportional reasoning applied, 7.RP.A.2 in use). The strict
  count is under the 65 percent line; the counting rule is in "For the lead".
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, 1st edition (2019 to 2021), grade 7, CC BY 4.0, from
  im.kendallhunt.com, in the source store (S:\CurioClass-sources\illustrative-math\grade-7, docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md).
  IM's lessons are adapted into Curio Class days in our voice, each with the credit line (with Open Up Resources).
  Only IM's open pages are used; cool-downs, answer keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken
  (LOGIN-GATED.csv; DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-13), so every exit ticket, check and assessment is Curio Class's own. The 2nd
  edition (accessim.org) is CC BY-NC and never used. Applets and third-party images are not carried over.
- **Practices.** Each unit names the one or two Standards for Mathematical Practice it builds (in the C3 column), and
  the unit plan names the task where students do them.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 7.NS.A.1 to 7.NS.A.3 and 7.EE.B.4a carry the grade's expected fluency (signed
  arithmetic, px + q = r). Units 5 and 6 build it; the daily opening practice keeps it up through units 7 and 8. No
  tool shows a countdown timer to a student.
- **Readiness checks.** A short readiness check opens each unit on the earlier skill it rests on. Skills from two or
  more grades back are rebuilt briefly in the first unit that needs them: m5-add-sub-unlike and
  m5-fraction-add-sub-problems in unit 5; m4-angle-concepts, m4-additive-angles, m4-protractor, m5-shape-hierarchy
  and m5-volume-additive in unit 7.
- **Paper path and accounts.** Every task has a paper path (grid and tracing paper, ruler, protractor, compass,
  number cubes, paper bags of slips for sampling). 7.G.A.2's "with technology" is met by a Curio Class drawing tool
  with no student account, or by the teacher on one shared screen, beside the paper construction. Calculators are
  used where a unit says so, after estimating.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent sensitivity read. No unit needs a community reader. A
  qualified math content reviewer (PRODUCTION-PATH step 7) reads the checks and assessments.

## For the lead

1. **Minutes per lesson.** Pick: 50 minutes, five a week, as in grades 6 and 8 (an IM lesson needs at least 45).
2. **Major work under 65 percent strictly.** IM's grade 7 gives the full additional geometry unit and the probability
   and sampling unit their own length, which puts the strict day count at about 61 percent; with the supporting
   sampling and probability days that use percent it is about 71 percent. Options: (a) keep IM's lengths and read the
   count with connected supporting work; (b) move 6 days from units 7 and 8 into units 4 to 6 as mixed practice,
   reaching about 65 percent strictly. Pick (b) if the expert reads the evidence guide's count strictly at baseline
   1, otherwise (a); the scope and sequence can make the move without changing units.
3. **IM 7.9 left out.** IM's ninth unit is optional (0 to 13 days). Extension candidates named in the source map.
4. **The 7.G.A.2 "with technology" path.** A Curio Class drawing tool with no account, or one teacher screen, plus
   paper. Pick: build none for now; the teacher screen and paper meet it, and a tool goes on the later list if the
   unit plan wants one.
