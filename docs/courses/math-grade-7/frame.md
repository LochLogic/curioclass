# Course frame: Math Grade 7

Course id math-grade-7, grade 7, draft frame (2026-11-13). The scope and sequence sets the days later.

## The course in brief

- **Units.** 8 units, in Illustrative Mathematics' grade 7 unit order (IM 7.1 to 7.8): scale drawings, proportional
  relationships, circles, percentages, rational number arithmetic, expressions, equations and inequalities, angles,
  triangles and prisms, probability and sampling. IM's optional ninth unit is left out (Source map).
- **Days.** 160 teaching days: 154 in units, 6 for course-level checks and respond days (three cumulative checks of
  1 day, each followed by 1 respond day). Each unit's days are its IM lessons as kept in the source map plus Curio
  Class days: an end-of-unit assessment and its respond day in every unit; in the major-work units 2, 4, 5 and 6 a
  mid-unit check and its respond day, and one mixed-practice day (units 2 and 4 also a readiness day, unit 5 a day
  that finishes decimal expansions). Each unit's Curio Class days are named in the source map. Spaced, mixed practice
  runs in the daily openings.
- **The time a day.** One 50-minute lesson a day, five a week, 32 weeks: an IM lesson needs a period of at least 45
  minutes (ms-teacher-guide), and the other 5 open the day with spaced, mixed practice (APPROACH-MATH). The pick is
  in "For the lead".
- **Standard.** The Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grade 7. All 43 grade 7 codes (lettered parts
  included) are owned by the 23 grade 7 progression rows, and every row sits in one unit below. Cluster weights are
  Student Achievement Partners' (ccss-math-focus.csv): major 7.RP.A, 7.NS.A, 7.EE.A, 7.EE.B; supporting 7.SP.A,
  7.SP.C; additional 7.G.A, 7.G.B, 7.SP.B. The bar is RUBRIC-MATH-K8.md. Math has no check set of other states: the
  "Check set adds" column records candidates only.
- **Major work.** The evidence guide counts supporting work whose connection to major work is explicit (indicator
  1e); each of units 1, 3, 7 and 8 names the major work it practises. By days: 98 of 160 (61 percent) by owning rows
  (units 2, 4, 5 and 6, 94 days, and about two thirds of the 6 course-level days); about 114 (71 percent) with
  connected supporting work. By units: 4 of 8 strictly, 6 of 8 with units 1 and 8. By lessons, from IM's tags: about
  88 of 125 kept lessons touch major work (70 percent). The connected figure is provisional: the year holds 8,000
  minutes (160 days of 50), so 65 percent is 5,200 qualifying minutes, and the scope and sequence lists the
  qualifying activities, their grade 7 standards and minutes, practice and assessments included, without counting a
  minute twice. Unit lengths stay as IM sets them.
- **Base text.** Illustrative Mathematics K-12 Math, 1st edition (2019 to 2021), grade 7, CC BY 4.0, from
  im.kendallhunt.com, in the source store (S:\CurioClass-sources\illustrative-math\grade-7, docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md).
  IM's lessons are adapted into Curio Class days in our voice, each with the credit line (with Open Up Resources).
  Only IM's open pages are used; cool-downs, answer keys and assessments are behind a login and are not taken
  (LOGIN-GATED.csv; DESK-ANSWERS 2026-11-13), so every exit ticket, check and assessment is Curio Class's own. The 2nd
  edition (accessim.org) is CC BY-NC and never used. Applets and third-party images are not carried over.
- **Practices.** Each unit names the one or two Standards for Mathematical Practice it builds (in the C3 column), and
  the unit plan names the task where students do them.
- **Fluency without a timer.** 7.NS.A.1 to 7.NS.A.3 and 7.EE.B.4a carry the grade's expected fluency (signed
  arithmetic, px + q = r). Units 5 and 6 build it; the daily opening practice keeps it up through units 7 and 8. No
  tool shows a countdown timer to a student.
- **Readiness checks.** A short readiness check opens each unit on the earlier skill it rests on. Skills from two or
  more grades back are rebuilt briefly in the first unit that needs them: m4-angle-concepts and m4-protractor in
  unit 1 (IM 7.1.4 measures corresponding angles with a protractor; the check sits in the opening of the day before);
  m5-add-sub-unlike and m5-fraction-add-sub-problems in unit 5; m4-additive-angles, m5-shape-hierarchy and
  m5-volume-additive in unit 7, where angle measure and the protractor are rechecked and refreshed.
- **Paper path and accounts.** Every task has a paper path (grid and tracing paper, ruler, protractor, compass,
  number cubes, paper bags of slips for sampling). 7.G.A.2's "with technology" is a student performance: students
  construct and test shapes with a drawing or construction tool that needs no student account, in pairs or small
  groups taking turns at the controls on a shared device. The teacher's demonstration introduces the task; paper
  construction is further practice and evidence and does not replace the students' own digital construction. The
  unit plan names the tool and how every student gets a turn before it claims 7.G.A.2 in full. Calculators are used
  where a unit says so, after estimating.
- **Sensitivity reads.** Every unit gets the fresh-agent sensitivity read. No unit needs a community reader. A
  qualified math content reviewer (PRODUCTION-PATH step 7) reads the checks and assessments.

## For the lead

1. **Minutes per lesson.** Pick: 50 minutes, five a week, as in grades 6 and 8 (an IM lesson needs at least 45).
2. **Major work under 65 percent strictly.** Settled (expert read 2026-11-14): option (a). IM's lengths stay, and the
   count follows the evidence guide (connected supporting work counts), with the connections named in units 1, 3, 7
   and 8 and the minutes allocation left to the scope and sequence ("The course in brief"). Moving 6 days out of units
   7 and 8 would cut real proportional reasoning to move a number.
3. **IM 7.9 left out.** IM's ninth unit is optional (0 to 13 days). Extension candidates named in the source map.
4. **The 7.G.A.2 "with technology" path.** Students construct with a tool themselves (see "The course in brief"); a
   teacher demonstration alone does not meet the standard. Pick: build no Curio Class tool now; the unit plan names a
   no-account drawing or construction tool and a turn-taking plan. If none fits, a Curio Class construction tool goes
   on data/plans/later.csv.

## Source map

Every lesson of IM grade 7 (145 lessons, units 7.1 to 7.9), placed or left out with a reason. Decision: keep, merge
(two IM lessons in one day), cut, ours (a Curio Class day). The day-level data/plans/math-grade-7/source-map.csv comes
with the scope and sequence. A merged day keeps one warm-up, the first lesson's main activity short, the second
