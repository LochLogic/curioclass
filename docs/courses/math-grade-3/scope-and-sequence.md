# Scope and sequence: Math Grade 3

Course `math-grade-3`, grade 3, draft, 2026-11-15.

The 160 course days are school days 1 to 160: 144 days in eight units and 16 course-level days. The frame's unit order, lengths and ownership stay fixed. The authoritative daily record is `data/plans/math-grade-3/days.csv`; `source-map.csv` places all 143 public first-edition base lessons, including 14 cuts, and names every added day. Source unit 4 is split between course units 4 and 5. The subtraction merge uses two days for three lessons. The eight added teaching days are 73 to 75, 82, 85, 94, 100 and 110. All assessment tasks and exit questions will be original; gated assessments, responses and cool-downs supply no material.

The section divisions below are scheduling groups of at most six days, not section plans. Day kinds describe the main work. A fluency day still includes reasoning; an application day still includes explicit teaching where needed. Read problems aloud on request, teach their mathematical words and accept an explanation spoken or drawn when writing is not the assessed skill. At home, the same problems and models work with one child. Drawn tools use the progression's model names; physical fraction strips, clocks, rulers and counters appear under supplies. No digital tool or unbuilt concept is required. Math's safety column stays empty; measurement precautions appear in notes.

## Unit 1. Introducing multiplication

**22 days, course days 1 to 22, weeks 1 to 5.** Sections: 1.1 days 1 to 4; 1.2 days 5 to 8; 1.3 days 9 to 14; 1.4 days 15 to 20; 1.5 days 21 to 22.

Eight graph days first make a scale meaningful. Twelve equal-group and array days then move from counting all to counting groups and writing products. Arrays explain why factor order can change without changing the total. This gives the compelling question, how to count without counting one by one, an answer in objects, pictures and symbols. Graphs receive less time than multiplication because they support the larger major-work arc. Problems with unknown totals and the first property and table observations prepare later owning units without displacing their instruction.

Retrieval is on days 1 and 2 as listed above; no readiness check. There is no required mid-unit assessment because the unit has fewer than 26 days. The end-of-unit assessment is day 21: draw and read both scaled graph types, use scales 2, 5 and 10 with up to six categories, solve one- and two-step more/fewer comparisons, interpret products as equal groups, describe a situation for a product, and connect arrays to products. It lists 3.MD.B.3 and 3.OA.A.1. Later-owned codes introduced in part receive formative evidence in teaching, then full assessment in units 4 and 5. Day 22 is the single response day.

**Candidate settled: kept.** The six-category limit and two-step graph comparisons are explicit on days 4 and 8 within their existing days. The scale-choice extension occupies part of day 6, with no added day. MP4 is the graph-to-situation modelling task on day 6 and the representations assessed on day 21; MP7 is the array argument on day 20. Survey contexts stay with classroom choices and objects, never personal, family-money or body information.

## Unit 2. Area and multiplication

**16 days, course days 23 to 38, weeks 5 to 8.** Sections: 2.1 days 23 to 26; 2.2 days 27 to 32; 2.3 days 33 to 38.

Four days establish coverage and the unit square; six connect the count to multiplication and meaningful named units; four connect products to facts and additive rectilinear area. Tiling precedes labelled ungridded rectangles, so multiplying side lengths has a reason. Square centimeters, inches, feet, meters and improvised units all appear. Rectilinear decomposition has three teaching days rather than being compressed into a final exercise.

Retrieve grade 2 rows and unit 1 groups on days 23 and 27; no readiness or mid-unit assessment. Day 36 retains an independent decomposition artifact for 3.MD.C.7d. Day 37 assesses 3.MD.C.5a and 5b, 6, 7a, 7b and 7d: define a unit square, tile without gaps or overlaps, count and label area units, show tiling agrees with a side-length product, represent a product by a rectangle, and add nonoverlapping rectangular areas. The day 36 artifact supplies the full decomposition performance once. Day 38 is the single response day. Distribution, 3.MD.C.7c, is completed in unit 4.

**Candidate settled: kept.** Rows as composite units and the corner-counting error occupy days 26 and 25 respectively. The three-rectangle room-plan extension is part of day 35. All fit in the 14 teaching days. MP6 is the unit-label explanation on days 28 to 32, carried into day 37; MP7 is the row-to-product connection on days 26 and 27. Paper squares and checked paper rulers provide the no-kit path.

## Unit 3. Adding and subtracting within 1,000

**21 days, course days 39 to 59, weeks 8 to 12.** Sections: 3.1 days 39 to 44; 3.2 days 45 to 49; 3.3 days 50 to 54; 3.4 days 55 to 59.

Six days connect place value and addition methods, five develop and compare subtraction methods, four establish rounding, and four begin two-step application. Three subtraction source lessons use days 46 and 47: retain lesson 8's warm-up and first activity, lesson 9's first activity, then lesson 10's two activities. The omitted matching and repeated-practice activities are named in the source map; days 48 and 49 still provide analysis and independent practice. Objects return to explain an error, while fluent work is written without them. The standard algorithm is not a grade 3 mastery requirement.

Retrieval falls on days 39, 40, 50 and 54, at the first relevant use of each grade 2 row; no readiness or mid-unit assessment. The end-of-unit assessment is day 58, with written addition and subtraction, including decomposing across zero, and rounding within 1000 to tens and hundreds. Three-digit-to-ten rounding is both taught on day 52 and checked on day 58. For an exact tie either adjacent multiple is accepted with correct distance reasoning; no particular tie convention is required. The standard codes assessed here are 3.NBT.A.1 and 3.NBT.A.2. Addition-table patterns and additive two-step records from days 40, 43 and 56 to 57 are formative portions of standards completed and assessed in units 4 and 5. Day 59 is the single response day.

**Candidate settled: kept.** The halfway qualification and scalable written methods are built into days 42 to 49 and 52, with no separate day. The interval-intersection rounding extension is inside day 53. MP3 is the written-method critique on days 43 and 48, with explanation retained in day 58; MP6 is place-value alignment and accurate rounding. The two-step work joins this unit's computation and estimates to the year's major problem-solving work.

## Unit 4. Division and the multiplication table

**16 days, course days 62 to 77, weeks 13 to 16.** Sections: 4.1 days 62 to 66; 4.2 days 67 to 71; 4.3 days 72 to 77.

Five days distinguish the two division meanings; three connect them to unknown factors and related facts; three explain operation-table patterns and rectangle strategies. The three added days then secure grouping factors, strategic fact choices and a private untimed facts record. This load is deliberately uneven: the five owned rows share representations and cannot each take an equal block. The facts record describes progress toward year-end fluency, not a claim that all products are already recalled.

Retrieve arrays on day 62, doubles on day 69, and strategy explanations and odd/even reasoning on day 70; retrieve same-year groups and area as their models return. No readiness or mid-unit assessment. Day 75 records known products and related quotients; the record is reused on day 76. The day 76 end-of-unit assessment covers 3.OA.A.2, 3.OA.B.6, 3.OA.B.5, 3.MD.C.7c, 3.OA.D.9 and 3.OA.C.7: both division stories, unknown-factor meaning, changing factor order, regrouping three factors, distributing a factor, a tiled concrete case of a x (b+c), and explained patterns in both addition and multiplication tables. Short prompts and the facts record avoid asking for several full performances in one day. Day 77 is the single response day.

**Candidate settled: kept.** Both division types occupy days 62 to 66; time and support for fluency occupy the three added days 73 to 75 and the daily practice rotation afterward. The split-7-times-8 extension is inside day 74. MP7 is choosing and explaining a fact decomposition; MP8 is the table-pattern argument on day 70, checked again on day 76. Scores and fact categories stay private, with no student countdown.

## Unit 5. Multiplying and dividing larger numbers

**14 days, course days 78 to 91, weeks 16 to 19.** Sections: 5.1 days 78 to 81; 5.2 days 82 to 86; 5.3 days 87 to 91.

Four days develop reasoning in tens and larger equal groups, the added measurement-quantity day and following equation day complete the one-step problem range, and six days connect two-step reasoning, operation order and exact division strategies. The measurement-quantity day supplies units and scale pictures; physical mass and liquid-volume measurement waits for unit 7. All non-tens products and division totals stay within 100; one-digit multiplication by multiples of ten may produce a larger answer. No remainders or multiplicative comparison are introduced.

Retrieve grade 2 tens on day 78 and additive two-step diagrams on day 84; retrieve same-year properties and rounding. No readiness or mid-unit assessment. Preserve the independent modelling record from day 89. Day 90 assesses 3.NBT.A.3, 3.OA.A.3, 3.OA.A.4 and 3.OA.D.8. The coverage grid reserves equal groups, arrays and measurement quantities with product, group-size and number-of-groups unknowns; unknown positions in multiplication and division equations; and two-step four-operation stories, a letter equation, conventional order without parentheses, and reasonableness by mental math and rounding. Use the day 89 record for the longer modelling performance and short prompts for the remaining parts. Day 91 is the single response day.

**Candidate settled: kept.** The unknown-position grid is explicit across days 79 and 82 to 83, with early product-unknown work retrieved from unit 1. Measurement quantities use added day 82; conventional order uses added day 85. These are two of the frame's existing 12 teaching days. The partner-written two-step extension is inside day 89. MP1 is making sense of the unknown on day 83; MP4 is the story, diagram and letter-equation connection on day 89. Both carry into the day 90 evidence.

## Unit 6. Fractions as numbers

**23 days, course days 92 to 114, weeks 19 to 23.** Sections: 6.1 days 92 to 97; 6.2 days 98 to 103; 6.3 days 104 to 109; 6.4 days 110 to 114.

Five days establish equal-area parts and fractions built from unit fractions. Six develop the length and number-line model, including a dedicated greater-than-one day and the start of equivalence. Six extend equivalence and whole-number fractions into comparison; four finish comparisons, confront errors and apply the models in a design. Thus length and number-line reasoning receive more time than shape partitioning. The added days are 94 for equal-area parts of different shapes, 100 for fractions greater than one, and 110 for the denominator and same-whole errors. Denominators are 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 throughout. No general equivalence algorithm or unlike-numerator-and-denominator comparison procedure is expected.

Retrieve grade 2 equal shares and measurement on day 92 and number lines on day 97. No readiness or mid-unit assessment. The independent day 112 design supplies partitioning evidence once. Day 113 assesses 3.G.A.2 and every listed fraction part: 3.NF.A.1, 2a, 2b, 3a, 3b, 3c and 3d. The evidence covers equal areas even with different shapes; a/b as a parts of size 1/b; the whole interval 0 to 1 and first endpoint 1/b; iteration from zero past one; equivalence as equal size and the same point; creating and explaining simple equivalents; whole numbers as fractions; both same-numerator and same-denominator comparisons with symbols and a same-whole justification. Day 114 is the single response day.

**Candidate settled: kept.** Length-first modelling is secured on days 96 to 100 and same-whole comparisons on days 107 to 111. The greater-than-one extension shares added day 100. No new days beyond the frame's 21 teaching days. MP3 is the comparison justification and error correction on days 109 to 111, then day 113. MP5 is choosing a strip or a number line to support it. A paper strip is a physical supply; its position is tied to a drawn number line. Food examples carry no diet or body talk.

Mass and volume return in those four application days, so their full load is not confined to three lessons. No unit conversions, compound units, fraction arithmetic on plot data or multiplicative comparison.

Retrieve grade 2 measuring on day 117, line plots on day 120 and five-minute time on day 125; no readiness or mid-unit assessment. Keep the independent measurement-and-plot artifact from day 121 and the object-measurement record from days 122 to 124. Include generating data and a fractional-scale plot, estimates and actual measurements in grams, kilograms and liters, reading scales, all four same-unit operations for mass and volume, telling and writing analog and digital minute time, measuring intervals, and adding and subtracting time intervals. Reuse the earlier individual measurement record rather than recollect it within ten days. Day 133 is the single response day.

## Load, practice and limits

The principal units for major work are 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7: six of eight. A planning estimate is about 108 of 160 day equivalents, roughly 68 percent, after splitting mixed work rather than counting every day of those units as major. Approximate contributions are 13, 16, 4, 16, 13, 21, 11 and 1 by unit, and 13 at course level. Scaled-graph and fractional-line-plot days support major work but are not automatically counted in full. This is a scheduling estimate; unit plans must verify actual task minutes, especially the boundary above the 65 percent line.

Days 15 onward reserve short, spaced, mixed fact practice on products already understood. Related quotients enter after division has meaning, from unit 4, and continue daily through day 160. From day 60, the same five-minute rotation also maintains written addition and subtraction within 1000. Alternate the balance of operations within that slot; do not add several separate warm-ups. Respond days use the recorded error and one earlier representation for a ten-minute group while others apply what they know. Assessments do not coach the items about to be taken. Fluency is never defined as work with blocks or a visible countdown. Base routines become short warm-ups rather than four additional course days.

Connections are explicit: scaled readings to groups, tiles to products, subtraction to unknown addends, area decomposition to distribution, rounding to two-step reasonableness, strips to fractional measurement, and area to perimeter. All eight mathematical practices have named tasks and assessment evidence in the unit sections. Supplies in the daily data are the planning inventory for unit plans and family notes; no supplies purchase is decided here.

## Shorter path

**Shorter period.** The baseline remains about 60 minutes. For 45 minutes, reserve 5 minutes for mixed retrieval, 8 for explicit teaching, 20 for the main connected task, 8 for synthesis and 4 for the exit ticket. Reduce parallel examples and the number of practice rounds, while retaining the first model-to-symbol connection, a contrasting error, and each owned part. Do not assign unfinished essentials to home. This reduces the baseline minutes by a quarter and is a draft adaptation, not a demonstrated equivalent.

Where a source lesson has two parallel practice activities, move at most one representative follow-up problem into the next day's existing warm-up, replacing another practice problem rather than adding time. The candidates are graph comparisons from day 7 into 8, array expressions from 18 into 19, area-fact practice from 33 into 34, addition-method practice from 41 into 42, subtraction-method practice from 48 into 49, rounding-pattern practice from 53 into 54, related-fact practice from 69 into 70, teen-product practice from 81 into 82, and denominator comparisons from 108 into 109. On other days reduce repeated trials within the day; do not move first instruction forward. Measurement stations may run in small groups while the rest use scale diagrams. Assessment windows keep their records and coverage, with the already named independent artifacts taking the longer performances. No extension or extra display-making displaces essential instruction or response. The unit plans must time the 45-minute path before it is offered as a runnable option.
