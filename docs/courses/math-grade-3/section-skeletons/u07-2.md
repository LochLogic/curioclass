## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **3.MD.A.2** (days 122, 123, 124): Measure and estimate liquid volumes and masses of objects using standard units of grams (g), kilograms (kg), and liters (l). Add, subtract, multiply, or divide to solve one-step word problems involving masses or volumes that are given in the same units, e.g., by using drawings (such as a beaker with a measurement scale) to represent the problem.
- **3.OA.C.7** (days 122, 123, 124): Fluently multiply and divide within 100, using strategies such as the relationship between multiplication and division (e.g., knowing that 8 × 5 = 40, one knows 40 ÷ 5 = 8) or properties of operations. By the end of Grade 3, know from memory all products of two one-digit numbers.
- **3.NBT.A.2** (days 122, 123, 124): Fluently add and subtract within 1000 using strategies and algorithms based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.

## 6. Days

## Day 122

<!-- skel -->
- kind: Understanding. Meets new content: the goal is taught here, from a hook and a worked example.
- goal: Estimate and measure the masses of objects in grams and kilograms.
- note: Base 3.6.6. Read scales as vertical number lines; no conversions. Sensitivity: never weigh students or report body weight. Water and balances: mop spills; dry hands near electrical devices. Warm-up: short spaced mixed multiplication and related division facts, feedback, no visible timer. Maintain written addition and subtraction within 1000 in the same five-minute rotation. Assessment window days 122 to 124: every child first estimates and then actually measures classroom-object mass in grams and kilograms and liquid volume in liters. Retain one named record per child for day 132 and the year-end handoff. Across three days use four groups of at most eight for 30 children, about ten station minutes per group each day; shared equipment stays at the station, others interpret scale diagrams. No repeated individual measurement conference. Focus budget: 55 of 60 instructional minutes on grade 3 major-cluster codes; remaining 5 minutes on supporting, additional or earlier-grade work, counted once.
- credit: adapted from IM G3 U6 L6, Estimate and Measure Weight (keep)
