## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **3.OA.D.8** (days 55, 56, 57): Solve two-step word problems using the four operations. Represent these problems using equations with a letter standing for the unknown quantity. Assess the reasonableness of answers using mental computation and estimation strategies including rounding.
- **3.NBT.A.2** (days 55, 56, 57, 58, 59): Fluently add and subtract within 1000 using strategies and algorithms based on place value, properties of operations, and/or the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- **3.OA.C.7** (days 55, 56, 57): Fluently multiply and divide within 100, using strategies such as the relationship between multiplication and division (e.g., knowing that 8 × 5 = 40, one knows 40 ÷ 5 = 8) or properties of operations. By the end of Grade 3, know from memory all products of two one-digit numbers.
- **3.NBT.A.1** (days 57, 58, 59): Use place value understanding to round whole numbers to the nearest 10 or 100.
- skill m3-two-step-problems (grade 3)
- skill m3-add-sub-1000 (grade 3)
- skill m3-mult-fluency (grade 3)
- skill m3-round (grade 3)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> **Supporting questions:** Why do addition methods agree (day 43)? What changes when a hundred is decomposed (day 48)? Which multiple is nearer (day 51)? How does an equation tell a story (day 56)?

>
> **Fluency:** 3.NBT.A.2 receives independent written practice on days 41, 44, 45, 48 and 49. Use varied numbers, method choice and feedback without timed recall.
