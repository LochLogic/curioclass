## 1. Standards and practices


MA.5.T1.4: "Locate" thirteen colonies and "describe" climate/crops on 10 and labor on 11; apply on 12. D3.4: "Use evidence" and D4.1: "Construct an argument" on 12 at grade 4 depth. Days 13/14 check and respond across the unit.

Teacher explains regions as a grouping model and colonial claims as contested, not evidence of Native consent. Massachusetts includes present-day Maine on the historical outline. Written review: legend (the map key, grade 4 u06-4 day 119); climate (what a place is usually like over many years, u06-3 day 117); scale-strip measurement (u01-2 day 5); region (a group of states that sit together and share something, u07-1 day 138), extended to colonial grouping; forced and chosen (people forced to work and not paid; enslaved named plainly, u07-2 day 142).

Review hired (chosen to do a job), grade 3 u02-2 day 29, adding the economic sense of wage employment here. Review forced labor: work people could not refuse (grade 4 u04-2 day 66). Teach indenture as bound service for a term and family work as work within a family. Never infer consent from a job or equate indenture and slavery. No auction, role play, forced-work simulation or home-money inquiry. Climate does not cause slavery. People and goods occupy separate diagram bands.
