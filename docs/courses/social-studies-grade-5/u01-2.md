A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.

Use [DEFAULTS.md](DEFAULTS.md). Skeleton quotations are planning data, never student masters. Split long copied prompts into punctuated sentences of at most 15 words before printing. Targets and pacing are ours, untested.

Each next-day Return and repair holds ten minutes: five for one priority explanation, five for a fresh retry or second linked need. Partners work simultaneously: fifteen pairs, one 45-second turn each, then paper from both. Public discussion hears six rotating children maximum, one 45-second ask each, within five minutes. MA.5.T1.1: "Explain" the Pequot War, King Philip's War and continuing nations on days 4 to 6. Review disease and land from section 1.1. Practice relevant details and single evidence connections; independent source weighing waits until unit 3.

Teacher explains distinct wars, specifically named nations and changing relationships. Underhill was an English participant publishing an account; Easton recorded leaders' complaints through his own report. Neither is a Native-authored transcript. Modern tribal histories have their own makers and purposes. Name massacre, deaths, displacement and enslavement plainly, without graphic details or unnecessary totals. Never present everyone on two timeless sides.

Written review: perspective: two sides of one event (grade 4 u04-3 day 68), applied to particular source makers, not a binary description of all peoples. Context: where, when and for whom a source was made (u02-1 day 20). Reasoning: how a detail supports a claim (u03-2 day 48).

## 9. Supports and extension

Picture glossary: massacre means killing many people. Review displacement: having to leave a place because it cannot be used safely (grade 4 u07-3 day 149). Extend its disaster use to forced removal during war. Name people forced into slavery plainly. Use non-graphic summaries, 170 to 250 total student words daily. Frame: "The maker is __. This detail supports __ because __." Offer neutral read-aloud, tactile chronology and dictation. Extend with source limits, never rankings or historical roles.
