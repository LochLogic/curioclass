## 1. Standards and practices

Teacher explains that a royal charter records an English claim, not universal consent. Aquinnah's communal practices do not describe every Native nation. Separate event and account dates. Modern tribal histories are sources about the past, not seventeenth-century eyewitness transcripts. No invented Native speaker, sacred account or reenactment.

Written review, ledger words: colony: a settlement far from the home country, which claims it (grade 4 u05-1 day 80); treaty: agreement between governments (u05-1 day 81); source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose (u04-1 day 62); timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks (u04-4 day 73). Review the word charter from kindergarten u01-2 day 5, a class agreement; explicitly teach its different historical sense here, an official grant. Introduce ownership as rights claimed over land and epidemic as disease spreading through a community.
