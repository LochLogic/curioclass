**E54.** s04-X54a: Ask: Using Cards E54, label B, C, and D with their economic conditions. Key: B: deflation, sustained general price fall. C: inflation, sustained general price rise. Key: Reform efforts already existed; leaders and states still had to choose. State suppression and possible relief show other actions, so chronology alone does not establish inevitability. Shape: Two historical causal links and contingent reform significance. Teaching sources and practice labels exclude this held arrangement. All thirty submit paper; exit: half.
