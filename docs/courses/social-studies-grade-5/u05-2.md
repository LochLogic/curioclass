# Section 5.2: Limits, branches and levels of government




## 1. Standards and practices


MA.5.T3.1 carries "Read" and "explain" six principles/responsibilities on 63. MA.5.T3.2 carries "Explain" duties, divided/shared power and checks on 64/65. MA.5.T3.3 carries "Describe" levels on 66. D2.Civ.1.3-5 carries "Distinguish" powers/responsibilities across levels, branches and times. Connect evidence links on 63 and change/continuity on 66.

Teacher: branches are jobs; levels concern jurisdictions, not human worth. School is an institution, not a fourth constitutional government level. Native nations govern through their own sovereign authority; Onondaga Nation governs today. Preamble purposes do not grant unlimited power. Teach general welfare, limited government, representative democracy and broader legal equality explicitly. Planned-only u04-1 rule of law receives independent instruction. Article II's presidential oath differs from Article VI's official oaths. Federal means national here; jurisdiction means authority over particular matters or places. General welfare concerns shared well-being; limited government means power has legal boundaries. Representative democracy uses chosen representatives. Explain habeas corpus as protection against unlawful detention. An oath or affirmation is a formal promise.

**E64, MA.5.T3.2, paper.** X64, invented: "Congress passes a river-safety law. National officials administer it. A federal court hears a challenge."

**E65, MA.5.T3.2, paper.** Withheld diagram: President nominates a federal judge; Senate withholds consent.

**E66, MA.5.T3.3; D2.Civ.1.3-5, paper.** X66, invented: "Congress regulates foreign trade. A state licenses drivers. A town repairs water pipes. Another town once appointed its water supervisor. Voters now elect that officer. The maintenance duty stays."

Shape: produce level/duty labels and change/continuity explanation. Practice sorts separate service cards and marks selection or duty separately, never this combined record or water case.

Cold Q1: principles may overlap; accept supported links, not only a compulsory one-to-one pairing. Q2: veto and override check different actors; a hearing alone proves no invalidity. Q3a: all levels respect rights while retaining different duties. Q3b: Senate selection changed, federal lawmaking continued.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: federal, branch, rule of law, general welfare, limited government, representative democracy, jurisdiction. Student print totals at most 350 words daily, including captions, directions and exits. Every printed sentence has at most 15 words. Use credited short renderings; long originals remain on the adult sheet and are read aloud. Independent source selections stay below 120 words each; teacher prose stays within 600 words. Provide large print, verbal descriptions, signing, a scribe or private dictation. Read checks neutrally. Frame: "This detail shows __ because __." Extend through a source's limits, within the same standard.
