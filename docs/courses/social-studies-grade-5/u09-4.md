## 9. Supports and extension

Words: coalition, accessibility, discrimination, strike, sit-in. Teach sit-in as remaining in a place
together to demand change, without acting it out. Practice frame: "This source names __."
Use identity-first or person-first language as appropriate to the source/person; no child is asked
to speak for a group. All student print, including credits, headings and prompts, stays within 350 words daily;
every sentence has fifteen words or fewer. Adult originals are reference; student renderings are
ours, credited. Read difficult text neutrally aloud. Use large print, described maps, dictation or
a scribe without supplying reasoning. Offer private pauses and equivalent non-graphic evidence.
No slurs, graphic harm, reenactment, simulation or personal disclosure. Extension adds a source
limit within the same goal. Independent captions normally stay below 100 words.
