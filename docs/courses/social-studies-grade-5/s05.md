## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

**Teacher explanation:** Congress gained taxing power; states retained powers. The 1791 Bill of Rights constrained federal government; later law extended many protections against states. Branches divide national work; levels divide responsibilities geographically. Native nations govern themselves as sovereign nations within the United States today. On 63 the President's oath (Article II) shows officeholders' responsibilities. Background for the teacher: District of Columbia v. Heller (2008) held the Second Amendment protects an individual right; checked 2026-11-14. Equality was an ideal unevenly protected, not a general equality guarantee in the original text.
