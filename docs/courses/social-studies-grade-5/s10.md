## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Teaching:** model question revision on 148, usefulness on 151, argument on 153 and critique on 154; guide an attempt before independent work. Use the 504 case for models, other topics for independent evidence. Teach interpretation, usefulness, corroboration, strategy and deliberation.

**Action boundaries:** students choose a real problem and a no-cost action within the classroom adult's authority, achievable on 157 and observable on 158. Gather no private information, require no political position, and plan no outside contact. Students may choose different approaches. Hearing another affected view need not involve interviewing anyone beyond the class. Record participation, not agreement with a cause. No new readiness check follows 160, the course's final day.

**Action comparison cards for 155, original hypothetical cases:** a school has shared materials people cannot find; neighbouring towns need usable flood warnings; communities across countries face ocean litter. For each, compare one person sharing information with groups coordinating a response, including who can act and what might result. These are practice cases, not the cause students must choose. Before deciding their own problem, groups collect an observation and hear another affected view within the classroom. Compare two or three historically encountered strategies, such as a reasoned request, shared information and collective organizing. Adapt methods to ordinary classroom action, never recreate historical exclusion.
