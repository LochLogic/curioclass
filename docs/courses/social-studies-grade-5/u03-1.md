**E27, paper.** New made-up archive club asks whether refusing an unfair rule was justified. "Write one supporting question about an idea people could interpret differently. Explain why it helps another reader." Key: e.g., "Whose consent makes authority rightful? This helps readers judge authority. Consent may mean direct agreement or elected representation." Accept other useful questions, audiences and interpretive ideas. Wrong: only my opinion matters (U); a date is the interpretive idea (V). Shape: generate a smaller question with another reader's use and an interpretive idea, two parts; practice sorts already supplied questions, never writes this combined response or uses an archive club.

**E28, paper.** Reserve a fresh Ohio-country map: overlapping British/French claim outlines over explicitly named Lenape and Shawnee homelands. Labels show claims, not ownership. Teacher-read source note: Delaware Nation describes land loss and Lenape alliances with France. Oneida Nation describes competing fur-trade expansion and different alliances. "Explain the conflict shown here. Explain why Native allies pursued their own aims." Key: competing imperial land/trade claims on Native homelands; security, land or trade interests explain choices, not automatic obedience. Do not infer every nation's allegiance from location. Wrong: France fought all Native peoples (H); every nation chose alike (T). Shape: integrated explanation from a new layered map; teaching uses separate text fragments and a physical-location base map, never overlapping claim interpretation or this map.

**E29, paper.** Reserve a new two-panel source summary: British revenue demands after expensive war; Proclamation restrictions amid land conflict. Use the Proclamation and the unit's war-cost reference, new selections. "Explain why Britain sought revenue after the war. Explain why it also restricted western settlement." Key: debts/defense costs encouraged revenue; settlement conflict and Native resistance encouraged boundary restrictions. Debt alone is insufficient for the second link. Wrong: war, taxes and independence were simultaneous (A); Native peoples had no independent role (H). Shape: two causal explanations from paired policy/land evidence; practice completes single labeled arrows, never paired explanatory prose or these panels.

Shape: Q9a asks another person's historical use; no historian/classmate-use answers modeled. Q9b supplies two interpretations of one idea; practice identifies interpretive vocabulary without rehearsing a paired answer. The archive-club exit is not assessment evidence.
