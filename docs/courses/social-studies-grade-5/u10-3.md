- **E153 / X153.** Reserved federal excerpts:
  "Source A, Roosevelt's order."
  "no discrimination in the employment of workers in defense industries or government"
  "Source B, Eisenhower's order."
  "take all appropriate steps to enforce any orders of the United States District Court"
  Captions: "Roosevelt issued his order on June 25, 1941, concerning defense employment.
  Eisenhower issued his order on September 24, 1957, concerning school attendance in Little Rock, Arkansas.
  Both presidents directed federal officials."
  "Revise this claim using both sources: Federal civil rights action concerned only schooling."
  Key/meets: "Federal action concerned jobs as well as schooling. Roosevelt directed employment nondiscrimination;
  Eisenhower directed enforcement of school-attendance court orders."
  Require credited details explaining the revision, not just dates.
  Wrong: "Employment evidence changes nothing; only schooling mattered" (S);
  "Roosevelt wrote an order. Eisenhower wrote one" (F).
  Shape: revise an overnarrow claim through two fresh legal directions. Practice labels claim,
  attribution and one evidence arrow separately; no paired-order revision or either entire work.
- **E154 / X154.** Reprint E151's excerpt and caption without its answer. This returns to a source,
  not a taught argument. "Made-up argument: Truman's committee could command the military because its job was advising."
  "Critique this evidence link and recommend one repair."
  Key/meets: "Advising does not establish command authority. Narrow the claim to an advisory role."
  A request for actual command-authority evidence counts without implying it exists.
  Wrong: "I agree, so the evidence supports it" (O).
  Shape: test the command/advice inference and suggest a repair. Practice marks an unsupported word
  or one source clue separately; no full critique, military claim or advice/command analogy.

## 6. Days

All parts total forty minutes. Fifteen pairs work concurrently, 45 seconds each way, 90 seconds total.
During a supported/independent block of at least six minutes, hear six single 45-second comments:
4.5 minutes plus transitions. Rotate recorded names; everyone else hands back individual paper.
No thirty-child public round. Five groups of six deliberate concurrently where specified.
After class, return one criterion cue per practice record, about two minutes each, sixty minutes
for thirty children, plus fifteen minutes sorting exits, ours, untested. More than ten misses means
whole-class repair; otherwise a small group while others revise. A priority contrast and fresh component
retry share the protected Return and repair block; secondary needs remain recorded. No repair uses
reserved check cases. Tool: blank; no digital or key tool. Every product is made by hand.
