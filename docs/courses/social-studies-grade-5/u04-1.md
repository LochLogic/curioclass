Teacher distinguishes inherent rights, protection in law, an argument and an accomplished legal change. The Declaration argues repeated abuses justify separation; grievances are Congress's accusations. Equality did not give everyone equal participation. Enslaved people were forced to work and denied freedom; women's exclusion and Native nations' exclusion differ from enslavement. Study religious language as the source maker's belief, without requiring belief. No slurs, reenactment, personal disclosure or sacred material.
