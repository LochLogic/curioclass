Rights: law and life
A. Historian, park and library records: Laws separated schools and restricted jobs and learning.
Taxes, tests and Ku Klux Klan threats blocked voting. Mobs murdered people.

Organizers demanded enforcement and equal participation, including for Black women.
E. 19th Amendment, 1920, paraphrased: Citizens' votes cannot be denied because of sex.
Historian context: Racist tests and intimidation continued.
F1. Movement archive: Greensboro students stayed at a segregated counter in 1960.
Others joined; sit-ins spread.
F2. Cone's participant account, 2013, paraphrased: Section 504 borrowed earlier civil rights protections.

Black Panthers supplied meals. Protest and a delegation helped secure the 1977 rules.
The goal was enforceable disability protection. The 1990 law was separate.

- **Q1:** four barriers: forced separation; limited jobs/schooling; voting taxes/tests; supremacist
  intimidation/killings. Three institution contributions: meeting space, education, information.
  Link to collective resistance. Meets: "Churches offered meeting space, clubs organized learning,
  and newspapers exposed injustice, helping people act together against imposed barriers."
  Not yet: "These institutions waited for others to act" (B); choice rather than coercion is L,
  universal equal access is A. Judge each barrier, institution and resistance link separately.
  Shape: seven-part content inventory and resistance inference from A/B. Practice never creates
  the same inventory or a full model response; do not use these passages before 141.
- **Q2:** citizenship did not establish women's suffrage; race protection omitted sex; advocates
  shared suffrage aims but differed over supporting the 15th. D continued organizing for equal
  participation and employment enforcement. Meets: "C sought votes but advocates split over
  supporting the 15th without sex protection. D continued rights work through employment demands."
  Not yet: "All advocates opposed Black men's voting" (C). A false 14th voting grant is K;

  Shape: earlier advocacy/split plus later link, not a timeline or single-demand annotation.
