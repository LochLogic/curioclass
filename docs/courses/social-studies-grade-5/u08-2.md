Teacher explains Grant's Union capture of Vicksburg and Lee's failed Confederate invasion at Gettysburg, where Meade commanded the Union army. Neither ended war. The Proclamation covered designated rebellious areas, excluding loyal slave states and specified occupied areas. Freedom required people's action and enforcement. Lincoln supported the 13th; Congress proposed and states ratified it. Explain its punishment-after-conviction exception. The 14th/15th followed Lincoln's death. No identical-rights claim or passive-freedom narrative.
