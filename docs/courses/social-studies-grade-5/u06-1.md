**E80, D1.1.3-5.** X80 is made up: a town proposes enlarging its market. A trader wants evidence about changed work.
Ask: "Write a supporting question about the trader's possible gains or costs." Then: "Explain why your question matters to the trader."
Key: an investigable question about deliveries, sales or work, with its practical stake. Many supported answers count. Meets: "Will deliveries take longer? Delays could reduce the trader's selling time." Not yet: "Which color is prettiest?" Wrong: an unrelated preference (H).
Shape: generate a new supporting question and justify another person's stake. Practice edits or sorts supplied questions; never models a complete question-plus-stake response or this market.

**E81, MA.5.T4.1.** X81, made up: a president asks several department leaders for recommendations. No later results appear.
Ask: "Explain Washington's advisory institution." Then: "Explain whether advice alone establishes successful leadership."
Key: the cabinet advises the president; department leaders administer distinct responsibilities. Advice may inform decisions, but outcome evidence is missing. Wrong: advice proves success (A).
Shape: explain the advisory institution and an evidentiary limit from a new decision record. Practice matches separate office/job labels, never produces this paired explanation.

**E82, MA.5.T4.1.** X82 is a new original historical card: Adams leaves office; rival Jefferson takes office in 1801. The change follows the constitutional electoral process.
Ask: "Explain why this peaceful change mattered despite party disagreement."
Key: competing parties could exchange governing power without war or a ruler refusing to leave. The constitutional process included resolving an electoral tie, not a direct popular presidential vote. Wrong: the presidents' policies were identical (I); Jefferson was the first president (V); parties first existed in 1801 (X).
Shape: infer the significance of a constitutional handover from a fresh account. Practice orders isolated party/date labels, never writes a handover-significance explanation.
