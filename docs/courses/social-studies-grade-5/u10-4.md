**Unit assessment items owned:** P10, P12, P13. Administer P1 to P13 and U on 159.

- **E155 / X155.** "Made up: A school garden needs weekend watering.
  One child offers one visit. A group offers a shared schedule."
  "Explain a difference in likely reach and one limit."
  Key/meets: "The group could cover more days, but missed turns could leave gaps."
  Other plausible coordination/permission limits count. Wrong: "The group guarantees water forever" (I).
  Shape: reach comparison plus coordination limit for two garden offers. No garden, watering,
  schedule or full reach/limit comparison in practice. Own-problem links stay on separate records.
- **E156 / X156.** "Made up: A group predicts clearer signs will help everyone.
  It votes before hearing a concern about small print."
  "Explain one prediction limit and one procedure to repair."
  Key/meets: "Test who can read the signs; hear and restate the omitted concern before deciding."
  Wrong: "Clearer guarantees everyone benefits" (I); "Most votes permit ignoring concerns" (G);
  "The concerned child is bad" (H). These are separate wrong responses, one letter each.
  Shape: diagnose an outcome-prediction limit and procedure fault in a new record.
  Practice uses separate actual choice components, never signs, small print or combined diagnosis.
- **E157 / X157.** "Made up: A group announces a poster. It records no making or use."
  "What evidence would distinguish its plan from completed action?"
  Key/meets: "A dated record of making or using the poster shows a completed step."
  Missing evidence does not prove no action occurred. Wrong: "Announcing proves completion" (J).
  Shape: specify missing implementation evidence in a new incomplete record. Practice performs the
  chosen action and logs steps, never diagnoses a missing poster record. Own observation stays separate.
- **E158 / X158.** "Made up: A group sees improvement once. It claims its action permanently solved the problem."
  "Explain what this observation supports and what remains uncertain."
  Key/meets: "It shows improvement in that observation; lasting change and sole cause remain unproved."
  Wrong: "Doing the action proves permanent success" (P).
  Shape: bound a permanence claim using one observation. Practice logs separate observations and
  prediction fragments, never a one-observation permanence diagnosis or full supports/unknowns answer.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


Children retain individual research, critique, strategy and action records. New causal strips separate
action, mechanism, expected result and uncertainty; written grade 3 Class Decision Record returns
with grade 5 reasons and conditions. Ideal instruments: loose mode/scale labels, private ballots,
dated observations; print fits, no tool/spec/key tool. Close filled models before 159;
retain blank access supports and evidence folders. Without equipment, use reused paper and existing materials.
Fallback: children choose feasible in-room trials after observation. If one cannot occur, choose another
screened classroom change and perform it on 157; never wait for outside permission or invent success.

## 6. Days

All parts total forty minutes. Fifteen pairs work concurrently, 45 seconds each way, 90 seconds total.
During a supported/independent block of at least six minutes, hear six single 45-second comments:
4.5 minutes plus transitions. Rotate recorded names; everyone else hands back individual paper.
No thirty-child public round. Five groups of six deliberate concurrently where specified.
After class, return one criterion cue per practice record, about two minutes each, sixty minutes
for thirty children, plus fifteen minutes sorting exits, ours, untested. More than ten misses means
whole-class repair; otherwise a small group while others revise. A priority contrast and fresh component
retry share the protected Return and repair block; secondary needs remain recorded. No repair uses
reserved check cases. Tool: blank; no digital or key tool. Every product is made by hand.

## 9. Supports and extension

individual (one person), collective (people together), prediction (a reasoned expectation), consensus.

Each printed sentence has fifteen words or fewer. Use fresh daily extracts rather than whole-folder rereading.

ours, untested. Read difficult originals aloud; original credited renderings are non-graphic.
Large print, source descriptions, private dictation or a scribe retain the evidence demand.
Read check directions neutrally without supplying reasoning. Students may pass publicly and submit private paper.
Extension adds a justified limit to the same claim, not a new standard.
