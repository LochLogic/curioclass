## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "When is breaking a law the right thing to do?"
- **Hook, day 27:** compare the Stamp Act Congress's claim that elected representatives must consent to taxes with Parliament's claim of authority in the Declaratory Act. Whose rule should colonists follow?
- **Co-write questions, day 27:** Why did war change British policy (28 to 30)? Who could consent to taxes (31)? How did resistance grow (32, 36 to 37)? How can accounts disagree (33 to 35)? Who shaped events (38 to 39)?
- **Closing claim, day 41:** answer the compelling question about colonial resistance using the Stamp Act Congress resolutions and Declaratory Act. Defend a condition for justified resistance through two attributed details and reasoning. Different conclusions are possible; revisit whose rights were denied using Wheatley's letter.
- **Teaching:** model causal links on 29, sourcing on 33 and comparison on 34. Guide evidence selection on 35; withdraw the worked argument on 41. Return to questions daily. Teach tax, representation, boycott, legislature, charter, audience, purpose and corroborate.
