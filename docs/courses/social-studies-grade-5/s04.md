## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "Did the Revolution keep the Declaration's promise?"
- **Hook, day 44:** set the Declaration's equality claim beside the 1777 freedom petition from Prince Hall and other Black petitioners. What promise were they asking lawmakers to keep?
- **Co-write questions, day 44:** What did independence promise (45 to 46)? Why did Britain lose (47 to 49)? Whose choices and leadership mattered (50 to 51)? Could the first government protect rights and meet needs (52 to 55)?
- **Closing claim, day 57:** answer the compelling question using the Declaration, Hall's petition and the Onondaga Nation's account of 1779. Attribute two details and explain their significance. Allow different judgments about how far the promise was kept, grounded in the record.
- **Teaching:** model the Declaration's argument on 45, perspective on 50 and a causal explanation on 53. Guide source comparison on 56; withdraw the worked argument on 57. Revisit questions daily. Teach natural rights, legal rights, consent, rule of law, Loyalist, neutral, confederation, inflation, deflation and unemployment.
