**Limits and teacher explanation:** grade 11 develops treaty disputes; unit 8 teaches the Civil War. Native governments predate written constitutions and persist today. The 1830 Act authorized removal treaties; coercive policy forced removal. Georgia's laws had no force in Cherokee territory under Worcester. Some Cherokee slaveholders forced enslaved Black people west on removal; their descendants are the Cherokee Freedmen. Day 105 traces abolition state by state.
