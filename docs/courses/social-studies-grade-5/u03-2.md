**E31, paper, operative data directions.** Original summaries: Penn charter, 1701: Eligible freemen choose an assembly yearly. It prepares bills for laws. Boston town vote, 1768: Choose people to represent the town at a convention. Virginia petition, 1764: Tax laws need representatives chosen by the people taxed. Freemen means a legally eligible group, not everyone. s03-X31: "Explain how these records connect earlier governing experience with conflict over parliamentary taxes." Key: Penn provided annual assemblies making bills; Boston selected delegates before independence. Virginia claimed locally chosen tax consent. Earlier powers informed resistance; participation was restricted. Shape: Integrate three new records into development-to-conflict explanation.
