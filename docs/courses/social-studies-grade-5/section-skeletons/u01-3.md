## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

<!-- skel -->
Error register rows (unit plan field 6) that name one of this section's days:

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | All colonies were founded for the same reason. | Flattens founding motives. On 7 sort two colonies by their documented purposes. | From memory; founding charters. |
| E | English influence followed simply from arriving first. | Confuses exploration with lasting institutions. On 9 trace language and government through continued settlement. | From memory; colonial charters. |
| I | Every colony protected religious dissent. | Confuses founding aims with equal religious rights. On 7 compare a conscience clause with a banishment record. | From memory; charter and Hutchinson records. |
| L | English influence proves English superiority. | Substitutes ranking for historical explanation. On 9 replace a ranking with evidence of continuing institutions. | From memory; colonial charters. |
