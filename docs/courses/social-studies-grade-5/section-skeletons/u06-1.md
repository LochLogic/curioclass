## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D1.1.3-5** (days 80): Say why a big inquiry question matters to other people, such as classmates and adults.
- **MA.5.T4.1** (days 81, 82): Identify the first three Presidents (Washington, Adams, Jefferson), summarize key developments of their terms such as political parties and the Louisiana Purchase, and evaluate their leadership.
- skill ss5-first-presidents (grade 5)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Hook, day 80:** place an 1803 territorial-claim map beside the Nez Perce Tribe's account of its homeland and government today. Ask what a growing border hides. Each student explains why the question matters to others (D1.1).
> - **Co-write questions:** What did presidents seek (81 to 83)? What did the Purchase change (84 to 86)? Why fight in 1812 (87 to 88)? How did industry change places and lives (89 to 93)?
> - **Teaching:** model leadership judgment on 81, route reading on 85 and economic reasoning on 90; guide source comparison on 92. Withdraw the worked claim on 94. Source every document before using it. Teach cabinet, political party, sovereignty, impressment, nationalism, productivity, human capital, external cost and opportunity cost.
