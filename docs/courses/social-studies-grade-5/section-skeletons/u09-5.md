## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D2.His.14.3-5** (days 140, 141): Explain the likely causes and effects of events and developments.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 140, 141): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.5.T5.7** (days 141): Describe African American life after the Civil War under Jim Crow, including limited opportunity, segregation, white supremacist violence such as the Ku Klux Klan, and the unifying role of Black churches, groups, and newspapers.
- **MA.5.T5.6a** (days 141): Describe women's rights advocacy around the 14th and 15th Amendments and its link to the later women's movement.
- **MA.5.T5.6b** (days 141): Explain that women won the right to vote with the 19th Amendment in 1920.
- **MA.5.T5.9** (days 141): Explain how the 20th century African American Civil Rights movement became a model for other civil rights movements, such as women's, disability, and LGBTQ rights.

- skill ss5-womens-suffrage (grade 5)
