## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.5.T5.4** (days 117): Identify the major military leaders and battles of the Civil War, such as Grant, Lee, Gettysburg, and Vicksburg.
- **MA.5.T5.5** (days 118): Describe Abraham Lincoln's role in building the Republican Party and his Civil War actions, including the Emancipation Proclamation and the 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments.
- **MA.5.T5.6** (days 118, 119): Explain how the Emancipation Proclamation and the 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments affected the rights of African Americans.
- **D2.Civ.12.3-5** (days 119): Explain how rules and laws change society and how people change them.

- skill ss5-reconstruction-amendments (grade 5)
