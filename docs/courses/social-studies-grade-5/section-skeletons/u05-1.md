## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D1.1.3-5** (days 60): Say why a big inquiry question matters to other people, such as classmates and adults.
- **MA.5.T3.4** (days 61, 62): Analyze the major Constitutional Convention debates, including power, state size, the Senate, and slavery, and explain how the 3/5 Compromise kept the states united but reinforced slavery.
- skill ss5-constitution (grade 5)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Hook, day 60:** put the Preamble's promise of liberty beside Benjamin Banneker's letter to Thomas Jefferson of 19 August 1791, which argues from the Declaration's equality claim. Ask who could use government to protect freedom.
> - **Co-write questions, day 60:** Why strengthen government (61)? Whose power grew through compromise (62)? How was power limited and shared (63 to 66)? Who lacked equal rights (67)? How could the text change (68)? Why add rights protections (69 to 72)?

Unit plan field 8 (tools and supplies across the unit):

> **Tools: None.** Supply sources, provision cards, branch diagrams, timeline strips, rulers, pencils and answer keys. Every source, map and task has a paper path. A projector enlarges print; the adult partners at home. Read originals aloud beside grade 4 to 5 explanations, at most 120 words independently per source.
