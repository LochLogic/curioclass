- **D2.Eco.13.3-5** (days 90): Describe how better tools and better skills let people produce more.

Name coercion, resistance and living Native governments; no reenactment or personal disclosure.

Introduce opportunity-cost through a voluntary training choice on 90, then distinguish it from external costs on 92. Never describe enslavement as the enslaved person's choice. Keep the benefits-and-costs chart, with people and time horizons, through 94.
