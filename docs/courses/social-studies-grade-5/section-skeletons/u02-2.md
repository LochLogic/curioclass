## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.5.T1.5** (days 18): Describe the origins of slavery, its legal status in all the colonies through the 1700s, and how common slave ownership was, including by many of the country's early leaders such as Washington, Jefferson, Madison, and Mason.
- **MA.5.T1.7** (days 19): Compare living and working conditions of enslaved and free Africans in the 1700s colonies, and explain how some enslaved people sought freedom.
- **MA.5.T1.7a** (days 19, 20): Describe how enslaved people were treated as property, denied education, abused, and punished for escape, and how these conditions led to rebellions.
- skill ss5-slavery-colonies (grade 5)

| C | Slavery existed only in southern colonies. | Confuses regional prevalence with legality. On 18 locate dated northern evidence. | From memory; Vassall history. |
| D | Enslaved people had no families. | Mistakes threatened separation for absent family bonds. On 19, connect a family relationship to evidence, then identify the legal threat to it. | Anticipated from memory; Smith and Vassall records inform correction. |
| E | Enslaved people accepted bondage. | Erases resistance. On 20 connect a documented action with a freedom-seeking purpose. | From memory; Stono and petitions. |
| K | Every colonial household owned enslaved people. | Generalizes from examples to prevalence. On 18 distinguish evidence of presence from a household count. | From memory; Vassall history. |
