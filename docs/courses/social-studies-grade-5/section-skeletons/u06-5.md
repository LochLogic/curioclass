## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D2.His.16.3-5** (days 94, 95): Use evidence to make a claim about the past.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 94, 95): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.5.T4.1** (days 95): Identify the first three Presidents (Washington, Adams, Jefferson), summarize key developments of their terms such as political parties and the Louisiana Purchase, and evaluate their leadership.
- **MA.5.T4.2** (days 95): Evaluate the importance of the Louisiana Purchase and trace the Lewis and Clark expedition with Sacagawea from 1803 to 1806.
- **MA.5.T4.3** (days 95): Describe the causes of the War of 1812 and how wartime events built American nationalism.
- **MA.5.T4.3a** (days 95): Explain British trade restrictions and impressment as causes of the war.
- **MA.5.T4.3b** (days 95): Describe major events of the war, including the USS Constitution, the burning of the Capitol and White House, and the Battle of New Orleans.
- **MA.5.T4.4** (days 95): Locate New England places important to textiles, machinery, whaling, shipping, and the China trade in the 1700s and 1800s, and give short- and long-term benefits and costs of these industries.
- **D2.Eco.6.3-5** (days 95): Explain how investing in skills and education affects productivity and later income.
- **D2.Eco.8.3-5** (days 95): Give examples of benefits and costs that fall on people outside a transaction.
- **D2.Eco.13.3-5** (days 95): Describe how better tools and better skills let people produce more.
- **D2.Geo.11.3-5** (days 95): Describe how patterns of economic activity in a place change over time through contact with nearby and faraway places.
