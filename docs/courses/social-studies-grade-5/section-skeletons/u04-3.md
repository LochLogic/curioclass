| 58 | MA.5.T2.8 | Explain why the Articles of Confederation were adopted in 1781, their weaknesses as a plan of government, and why they failed. | **Q5a (4 minutes):** Read R. In one sentence, explain why the states accepted this league; in a second, name a weakness and explain why it made the plan fail. Answer: states wanted union for common needs while keeping their own power after resisting a distant government; Congress adopted the plan in 1777 and all states ratified it by 1781. Congress could ask states for money but could not tax people directly, so it could not pay its debts or solve shared problems, and changes needed every state's agreement. Wrong: no government existed (F), Congress could levy national taxes (T), every ordinary decision required unanimity (O). | section 4.1 |
| 58 | MA.5.T2.9 | Analyze the causes of Shays' Rebellion and explain why it was a key event leading to the Constitutional Convention. | **Q5b (3 minutes):** R adds a Massachusetts case: debts and taxes had to be paid in scarce coins, property could be seized, petitions brought little relief, and armed groups closed courts in 1786. In one sentence, link two causes and explain why the events strengthened calls for the Convention. Answer: cash demands and debt cases threatened farms, so farmers protested and closed courts; the unrest convinced leaders already seeking reform that the national government could not handle crises. Wrong: only a disliked tax caused it (G); the Constitution already existed (Z). | section 4.1 |
| 58 | D2.Eco.11.3-5 | whole indicator (verb from the C3 file) | **Q6 (3 minutes):** A family case printed "invented for practice" has three short parts: prices rise while savings stay fixed; later prices fall and crops sell for less, but an eighteen-dollar debt stays the same; an adult looks for paid work and finds none. Label each part inflation, deflation or unemployment and briefly explain each term; explain one effect on the family. Answer: inflation means a general price rise; deflation means a general price fall; unemployment means seeking paid work but finding none. For example, inflation means fixed savings buy less. Wrong: one expensive item proves inflation (H), falling prices always help everyone (P), an unpaid child is necessarily unemployed (Q). | section 4.1 |

## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

<!-- skel -->
Error register rows (unit plan field 6) that name one of this section's days:

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| F | There was no government before the Constitution. | Ignores the Articles government. On 53 contrast an Ordinance achievement with a limited national power. | From memory; Articles and Ordinance. |
| G | Shays' Rebellion and the Convention followed from one isolated cause. | A sequence replaces interacting causes. On 55 connect debts, cash taxes, collection and insufficient relief; add existing reform efforts and state suppression. Check a fresh causal explanation. | From memory; agrarian unrest history. |
| H | One price increase proves inflation. | Confuses a single price with a general trend. On 54 compare several prices. | From memory; C3 economics. |
| O | Every Articles decision required all states. | Confuses ordinary votes with amendment. On 53 sort decision and amendment rules. | From memory; Articles XIII. |
| P | Deflation helps everyone. | Misses fixed debt burdens. On 54 lower crop income while keeping debt constant. | From memory; C3 economics. |
| Q | Everyone without paid work is unemployed. | Omits seeking paid work. On 54 compare a job seeker with someone not seeking work. | From memory; C3 economics. |
| T | Congress under the Articles could tax people directly. | Misstates national power. On 53 trace requests to state governments. | From memory; Articles VIII. |
