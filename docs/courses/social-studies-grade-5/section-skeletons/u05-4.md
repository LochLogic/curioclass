## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.5.T3.6** (days 71, 72, 74): Read the Bill of Rights, explain the freedoms it guarantees, and research and argue with evidence why one of the first ten Amendments was included in 1791.
- **D2.His.10.3-5** (days 71, 74): Compare what different historical sources say about the past.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 72, 74): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **D2.His.1.3-5** (days 72): Make and use a time-ordered list of related events to compare developments that happened at the same time.
- **MA.5.T3.1** (days 73): Read the Preamble and parts of the Constitution and explain how they reflect individual rights and responsibilities, equality, rule of law, general welfare, limited government, and representative democracy.
- **MA.5.T3.2** (days 73): Explain how the framers divided and shared power among three branches, what each branch does, and how checks and balances work.
- **MA.5.T3.3** (days 73): Describe the responsibilities of federal, state, and local government, such as protecting rights, law enforcement, and schools.
- **MA.5.T3.4** (days 73): Analyze the major Constitutional Convention debates, including power, state size, the Senate, and slavery, and explain how the 3/5 Compromise kept the states united but reinforced slavery.
- **D2.Civ.1.3-5** (days 73): Tell apart what different government officials, levels and branches may do and must do, across times and places.
- **D2.Civ.3.3-5** (days 73, 74): Examine where rules, laws and key parts of the U.S. Constitution came from and what they are for.
- **MA.5.T3.5** (days 74): Explain that the new Constitution did not extend voting and property rights to women.
- skill ss5-constitution (grade 5)
- skill ss5-revolution-causes (grade 5)

> **Carried:** keep unit 4's timeline, perspective, sourcing, corroboration and cause chain. Revise claim, evidence and reasoning on 70 to 72; remove the worked claim on 72. Build branches-of-government on 64; connect arrows to provisions on 65, then remove labels for independent cases on 70. Keep blank diagrams.
