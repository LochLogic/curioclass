Original paraphrase: "Chisholm compared women's rights with other groups' rights. She argued that laws could protect people and challenge prejudice, though change would take time." What approach did she share with Black civil rights work, and what goal went beyond voting? Key: seek legal protection against discrimination, including employment barriers discussed in the taught speech opening. Error E. | Q2, Q4; E. |
| 137 | X137, reserved original paraphrase of the disability-law overview and Cone's daily-meetings paragraph: "The 1977 rules implemented Section 504; the 1990 law broadened protections. Sit-in participants met daily to choose their next actions." Explain the legal difference and who made organizing decisions. Key: rules implementing an earlier law differ from a later law; disabled participants decided their actions. Error E for identical laws; H for helpers alone leading. Keep this paragraph outside teaching and F2. | Q4, Q5; E, H. |
