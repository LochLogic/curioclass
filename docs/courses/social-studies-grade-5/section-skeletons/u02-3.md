## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.5.T1.7b** (days 21): Explain that many enslaved Africans became skilled artisans and could be hired out to work.
- **MA.5.T1.7c** (days 22): Explain how some Africans were freed after service or by grant, and how some in the North sued for freedom, such as Elizabeth Freeman and Quock Walker, or petitioned legislatures to end slavery, as Prince Hall did.
- **MA.5.T1.7** (days 23): Compare living and working conditions of enslaved and free Africans in the 1700s colonies, and explain how some enslaved people sought freedom.
- **MA.5.T1.7d** (days 23): Explain that Northern states adopted gradual emancipation, with Massachusetts outlawing slavery in 1783, and that free African Americans could have families, property, and jobs.
