## 4. Expected errors and first ideas

Error register rows (unit plan field 6) that name one of this section's days:

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | A maker and date are enough to source a document. | Omits audience and purpose inferences. On 33 infer each from internal clues on a fresh notice. | From memory; C3. |
| D | Agreement between two sources proves every detail. | Overextends corroboration. On 34 separate a shared fact from a disputed detail. | From memory; trial accounts. |
| E | An argument needs no evidence-based reasoning. | Evidence is not connected to the question. On 35 and 40 contrast "resistance was right" with a conditional historical claim supported by an attributed detail and reasoning. Have students repair one link, then supply a new one. | From memory. |
| K | An engraving is a photograph of an event. | Ignores image construction. On 34 identify a selected visual feature and its persuasive effect. | From memory; Revere catalog. |
| M | A source with a purpose is useless. | Dismisses situated evidence. On 35 state a use and a limit for a purposeful account. | From memory; trial record. |
| R | A source's nationality determines its accuracy. | On 35 compare access and evidence rather than the maker's nationality. | From memory; trial accounts. |
