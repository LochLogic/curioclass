## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **D2.His.17.3-5** (days 123, 125): Sum up the main claim of a history book or article written by a later author.
- **D2.His.16.3-5** (days 124, 125): Use evidence to make a claim about the past.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 124, 125): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.5.T5.2** (days 125): Identify the major causes of the Civil War, such as slavery, western competition, and the rise of the Republican Party, and its key outcomes, such as the end of slavery and Reconstruction.
- **MA.5.T5.4** (days 125): Identify the major military leaders and battles of the Civil War, such as Grant, Lee, Gettysburg, and Vicksburg.
- **MA.5.T5.5** (days 125): Describe Abraham Lincoln's role in building the Republican Party and his Civil War actions, including the Emancipation Proclamation and the 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments.
- **MA.5.T5.6** (days 125): Explain how the Emancipation Proclamation and the 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments affected the rights of African Americans.
- **D2.His.14.3-5** (days 125): Explain the likely causes and effects of events and developments.
- **D2.Civ.12.3-5** (days 125): Explain how rules and laws change society and how people change them.
