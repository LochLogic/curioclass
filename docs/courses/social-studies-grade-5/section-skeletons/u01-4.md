## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.5.T1.4** (days 10, 11, 12, 13): Locate the 13 colonies, describe how regional climate affected crops in the Northern, mid-Atlantic, and Southern colonies, and describe varied sources of labor.
- **D3.4.3-5** (days 12, 13): Use evidence to build claims that answer a big inquiry question.
- **D4.1.3-5** (days 12, 13): Make arguments from claims and evidence drawn from several sources.
- **MA.5.T1.1** (days 13): Explain early relations between English settlers and Native Peoples, including disease, differing views of land ownership, and conflicts such as the Pequot War and King Philip's War.
- **MA.5.T1.2** (days 13): Compare why different colonies were founded and research one colony's founder, such as William Penn, Roger Williams, or John Winthrop.
- **MA.5.T1.3** (days 13): Analyze why English colonists most shaped the language and political ideas of the future United States, even though other European nations explored North America.
- skill ss5-colonial-regions (grade 5)
- skill ss5-colonial-encounters (grade 5)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> - **Co-write questions on day 1:** How did disease change relationships (2)? What did agreements mean (3)? Why did wars happen (4 to 5)? Who lives and governs here today (6)? > - **Closing claim, day 12:** answer the compelling question using the Narragansett Early History and Founding Providence cards. Both establish Native authority in a negotiated settlement; students may emphasize negotiation or the limits of a colonial claim. Name each source and connect its evidence to the answer.
>
> - **MA.5.T1.4:** Locate the 13 colonies, describe how regional climate affected crops in the Northern, mid-Atlantic, and Southern colonies, and describe varied sources of labor. Parts, by verb: whole standard.

Build the map key and regions on 10, revise for labor on 11, apply on 12. Perspective connects land views on 2, war accounts on 4 to 5 and present voices on 6. Keep these models; withdraw the worked claim on 12.
