## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **MA.5.T2.1a** (days 30): Identify the main British policies: the Proclamation of 1763, the Sugar, Stamp, Townsend, and Tea Acts, and the Intolerable Acts.
- **MA.5.T2.1b** (days 31): Explain the slogan "no taxation without representation."
- **MA.5.T2.3** (days 31): Explain how colonial governments, such as legislatures, town meetings, and charters of rights, developed and helped lead to the Revolution.
- **MA.5.T2.1c** (days 32): Describe the roles of the Stamp Act Congress, the Sons of Liberty, the Boston Tea Party, the Suffolk Resolves, and the early battles at Lexington, Concord, and Bunker Hill, ending with the British leaving Boston.
