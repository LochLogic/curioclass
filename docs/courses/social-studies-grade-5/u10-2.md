- **E150 / X150.** "Made up: A researcher has only a ruling about school access.
  They ask how families experienced access."
  "Name another useful source kind and a differing view."
  Key/meets: "Family accounts show experiences; documented officials' opposition helps explain barriers."
  Accept relevant alternatives with a different documented perspective. Wrong: "Any matching title" (C).
  Shape: supply an additional source kind and view for a fresh mismatch. Practice labels kinds,

- **E151 / X151.** Executive Order 9981, section 3: "The Committee shall confer and advise the Secretary of Defense".
  Caption: "President Harry Truman issued this order on July 26, 1948.
  It came from the White House. It assigned federal officials duties concerning armed-services equality."
  "Use the caption to judge usefulness for assigned duties and committee members' private opinions."
  Key/meets: "Truman's July 26, 1948, White House instructions addressed federal officials.
  Their duty-setting purpose establishes an advisory assignment, not committee members' private beliefs."
  Use maker, date, origin, audience and purpose in the judgment; copied labels alone are incomplete.
  Wrong: "The archive issued the order" (M); "Official duties reveal everyone's private beliefs" (D).
  Shape: integrated five-context judgment for two contrasting uses. Practice tags attributes singly
  and marks one relevance clue; no complete use/limit paragraph, presidential order or private-belief inference.
  Reserve the whole order except its planned return for E154.
- **E152 / X152.** "Made up: Pages A, B and C copy one organizer's speech about a school campaign.
  Page D separately describes one family's difficulty attending meetings."
  "Identify the distinct works and what relevant evidence each could supply."
  Key/meets: "A/B/C are one campaign speech. D separately describes a participation barrier."
  Do not invent a speech detail: only its topic is given. Wrong: "Four pages mean four works" (L).
  Shape: group copied pages by original work and distinguish possible contributions.
  Practice credits isolated notes; no duplicate-page grouping or speech/family pair.

## 5. What is carried, and instruments


Children carry source folders, question slips and dated revisions. A fresh context strip expands
written grade 4 sourcing with origin. Label attributes separately on 151; close completed labels
before independent revised-log assembly on 152. Evidence assembly follows feedback on 153.
Ideal instruments: credit strips and loose detail labels; print fits, no tool/spec/key tool.
Without a screen read credited summaries; copy short labels, never full originals. Withhold exit works and U.

## 6. Days

All parts total forty minutes. Fifteen pairs work concurrently, 45 seconds each way, 90 seconds total.
During a supported/independent block of at least six minutes, hear six single 45-second comments:
4.5 minutes plus transitions. Rotate recorded names; everyone else hands back individual paper.
No thirty-child public round. Five groups of six deliberate concurrently where specified.
After class, return one criterion cue per practice record, about two minutes each, sixty minutes
for thirty children, plus fifteen minutes sorting exits, ours, untested. More than ten misses means
whole-class repair; otherwise a small group while others revise. A priority contrast and fresh component
retry share the protected Return and repair block; secondary needs remain recorded. No repair uses
reserved check cases. Tool: blank; no digital or key tool. Every product is made by hand.

## 9. Supports and extension

Words: maker, origin, audience, purpose, usefulness. Review written grade 4 u04-1 day 60:
primary source and secondary source, for one question (primary is also called firsthand).
A modern guide is not an eyewitness to a campaign. Frame: "The creator made this for __." All daily student print, including credits, records and exits, stays within 350 words.
Each printed sentence has fifteen words or fewer. Use fresh daily extracts rather than whole-folder rereading.
Plan about 200 source words and 150 task words, with ten reading/rereading minutes inside work blocks,
ours, untested. Read difficult originals aloud; original credited renderings are non-graphic.
Large print, source descriptions, private dictation or a scribe retain the evidence demand.
Read check directions neutrally without supplying reasoning. Students may pass publicly and submit private paper.
Extension adds a justified limit to the same claim, not a new standard.


## 10. Two rooms

One child uses the same printed sets; an adult reads difficult selections and supplies a documented
contrasting view, then leaves judgment to the child. One projector enlarges teaching sources;
individual paper keeps credits. No live connection is needed during class.
