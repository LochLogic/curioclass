# Section 3.4: Putting it together and showing what you know

## Header

<!-- skel -->
Science Grade 3. Unit 3, Growing up and taking after. "How do living things grow and change, and why do they look like their parents but not exactly?" Anchor: the class's own plants, grown from one packet of seeds sown on planting day (science day 18): they come up looking like the picture of the parent plant but not quite like each other; and photographs of one kind of hydrangea bush with blue flowers in one garden and pink in another (the soil decides the color) (days 37 to 58): see [s03.md](s03.md). **Days 53 to 58.** Days by kind: understanding 1 (53), application 1 (54), check 3 (55, 56, 57), respond 1 (58). A plan, not a lesson: nothing here is built or tried with children. Fields follow UNIT-PLAN.md.
