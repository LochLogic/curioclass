## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Use readiness evidence to revisit pushes, pulls and predictions while establishing the weather record.
- **Note:** One-lesson support from day 1 results, with an evidence extension for ready students; weather log starts this week. Weather log: 5 minutes within this day, temperature, rain or snow, wind direction and sky; below zero in words only. Support both push/pull reasoning and predicting from an observed pattern. Explicitly teach weather observation, dated picture/table entries and how repeated entries reveal a pattern, rather than assume the kindergarten habit.

**Skills:**

- s3-balanced-forces (grade 3): taught or practised here
- s3-motion-patterns (grade 3): taught or practised here
- sk-weather-watch (grade K): earlier-grade row, review (retrieval) here, not taught as new

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**

- **3-PS2-1:** Investigate how balanced and unbalanced forces change, or do not change, the way an object moves, and use the results as evidence.
- **3-PS2-2:** Observe or measure an object's motion to show that a repeating pattern lets you predict how it will move next.
