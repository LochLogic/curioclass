## The day as the scope and sequence sets it

<!-- skel -->
- **Goal:** Plan and carry out a fair test showing how balanced and unbalanced forces affect a new object.
- **Note:** All 3-PS2-1 parts: each child records a plan with one variable, fixed conditions and repeated trials, contributes an identifiable trial and records motion evidence and force arrows explaining speed or direction changes. Six groups of five use five-minute test turns, five minutes launch/transitions, six minutes independent explanation and four minutes cleanup = 45. Others independently finish plans and records. Collect once, response on day 16 only. Every part of each listed code is asked through the named prompts or product, after teaching.

- s3-balanced-forces (grade 3): taught or practised here

**Codes, in our words where the standards file has them:**
