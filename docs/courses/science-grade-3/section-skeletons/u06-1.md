## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **3-ESS2-1** (days 98, 99, 100, 101, 102): Show weather data in tables and graphs to describe the weather usually expected in a given season.
- skill s3-weather-data (grade 3)
- skill sk-weather-watch (grade K, earlier grade: review, never taught as new)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 98: display contrasting dated pages from the class log, with seasonal headings hidden. Children predict the next page. Reveal dates: why can days differ yet seasons have patterns?
> Model graph scales on day 100 and source comparison on day 104; then ask children to justify revisions before class agreement.
>
> Group the question board: what repeats, answered days 99 to 102; whether one year describes everywhere, days 103 to 107; how expected hazards cause less harm, days 108 to 112. Each answer raises the next question. Days 113 and 114 connect them.
> Carried: day 98 starts a seasonal expectation model. Day 100 revises its displays from counts; day 102 adds exceptions. Day 107 adds regional climate range and variation from combined sources. Day 109 revises the design cycle with flood criteria; day 110 tests and improves one roof. Day 111 revises cause-and-effect arrows from lightning evidence. Day 113 joins these in consensus, used in transfer tasks.
