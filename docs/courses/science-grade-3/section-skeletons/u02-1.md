## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **3-PS2-3** (days 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22): Ask questions to find what causes and changes electric or magnetic pushes and pulls between two objects that are not touching.
- skill s3-magnet-static (grade 3)
- skill m2-measure-with-tools (not a progression row)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Day 17: show a tethered clip held above its anchor by a magnet across a gap, then rubbed balloon and paper attraction. Substitute a comb for latex. Children draw explanations first.
> Model questioning on day 18; children then choose changes. Use notebooks to choose support.
>
> Group the question board into distance, materials, turning and uses. Days 17 to 22 ask what changes the clip's pull, using controlled comparisons to identify causes. This raises whether paper attraction works similarly: days 23 and 24 ask static questions and observe switched magnetism. Days 25 to 28 ask how this evidence meets a cupboard need through problem definition, one trial and improvement. Days 29 and 30 ask whether that definition permits judging solutions. Transfer uses unfamiliar interaction records on day 31 and approaching toy cars on day 32.
> Carried: retain arrows and cause-and-effect comparisons. Day 17 adds gaps; day 19 revises lengths from distance evidence; day 20 revises direction from turning magnets; day 22 consolidates both. Day 23 adds static evidence. Design records begin on day 25, add criteria and limits on day 26, and change after day 27 trials and day 28 user needs. Day 29 consensus joins these models for independent use on days 31 and 32.
