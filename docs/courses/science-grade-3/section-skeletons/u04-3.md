## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **3-LS4-3** (days 69, 70, 71, 72, 73): Argue from evidence that in a given habitat some living things do well, some do poorly, and some cannot live at all.
- skill s3-habitat-survival (grade 3)

Unit plan field 2, the lines naming this section's days:

> Group the question board into groups, differences and places. Days 59 to 63 ask how grouping helps; that raises why individuals still differ in success. Days 64 to 68 explain variation, raising whether an advantage works everywhere. Days 69 to 73 argue from needs and conditions; day 74 joins the answers. Transfer uses unfamiliar plant and bird records, animal groups and a pond.
> Carried: initial day 59 system drawings gain group cause-and-effect arrows on day 62 from animal records. Revise unit 3's claim, evidence and reasoning frame on day 63 after challenges. Start structure-and-function comparisons on day 64; revise on days 66 and 68 from camouflage counts, thorn evidence and bird records. Day 69 revises habitat systems from needs and conditions; day 73 adds local evidence. Day 74 consensus retains these models for assessment annotation.
