## 1. Standards and practices

<!-- skel -->
- **3-LS1-1** (days 53, 54, 56, 58): Make models showing that living things have many different life cycles, yet all are born, grow, reproduce and die.
- **3-LS3-1** (days 53, 54, 57, 58): Use data as evidence that plants and animals get traits from their parents and that members of a similar group differ in those traits.
- **3-LS3-2** (days 53, 54, 55, 58): Use evidence to back the explanation that surroundings can shape an organism's traits, such as a plant stunted by too little water.
- skill s3-life-cycles (grade 3)
- skill s3-inherited-traits (grade 3)

| 55 | 3-LS3-2 | Use evidence to back the explanation that surroundings can shape an organism's traits, such as a plant stunted by too little water. | Item 1, 32 minutes, independent paper explanation. New records, invented for practice: same-kind marigolds receiving adequate water grow 12 and 13 centimetres; matched plants receiving too little grow 6 and 7, with light, age, soil and spacing fixed. Two same-kind ducklings previously ignored a bell; after repeated bell-before-food experiences both approach it. A second plant record shows short and tall marigold families: under matched adequate water, parent and offspring heights share the family's shorter or taller pattern; limited water reduces heights in both families. Ask children to explain the water effect, learned response and combined influences, selecting observations and giving causal reasons. Expected: surroundings can affect growth and learning; the same height trait reflects inherited family patterns and water conditions. Matching wrong claims: every difference is inherited (C), or a learned response was inherited (D). Five minutes directions, two care, three checking and three cleanup complete 45. Fresh work is diagnostic; score the revised earlier item below, outside sitting time. | section 3.4 |
| 56 | 3-LS1-1 | Make models showing that living things have many different life cycles, yet all are born, grow, reproduce and die. | Item 2, 34 minutes. Use these new records, invented for practice. Zinnia Z: day 0 seed containing a new plant, day 5 seedling, day 50 flowering adult, day 70 dried flower head, day 95 adult dead. Lady beetle L: day 0 egg, day 4 larva, day 18 pupa, day 25 adult, day 35 adult lays new eggs, day 90 adult dead. Intervals are the differences between recorded days, not universal life spans. Draw models showing birth, growth, reproduction and death for each; explain the shared pattern and differences in stages and timing, and predict what follows when a new zinnia seed begins growing in the recorded conditions. Then reveal a second card: on day 70 Z's opened head contains new seeds; one begins growing on day 75 and is a seedling on day 80. Revise the model to connect Z with new individuals and explain the evidence. Expected: new individuals develop from seeds or eggs; adults can produce offspring, while individuals eventually die. Offspring continue the pattern. Flowering plants have no pupal stage; intervals differ, such as 5 days from zinnia seed to seedling and 4 from beetle egg to larva. Predict a seedling after 5 days of growth; add seeds in the head and an arrow to offspring, not to the dead adult. Wrong: the same individual starts again after death (A). Six minutes directions, three checking and two packing complete 45. No live care task is assigned today. Score only the eligible earlier model. | section 3.4 |
| 57 | 3-LS3-1 | Use data as evidence that plants and animals get traits from their parents and that members of a similar group differ in those traits. | Item 3, 34 minutes. New records, invented for practice: bean parents and all six offspring have broad leaves; offspring lengths are 4, 4, 5, 5, 6 and 6 centimetres. Guinea-pig parents have fur; their four young have fur with differing patch patterns. Organize both records and explain an inherited likeness and within-kind variation in each, identifying the evidence. Explain why these data do not predict a particular future offspring. Expected: shared leaf form and fur support inherited likeness; leaf lengths and patch patterns show variation. Wrong: relatives must match every feature (F). Five minutes directions, four independent checking and two packing complete 45. Score only the eligible earlier trait record. | section 3.4 |
