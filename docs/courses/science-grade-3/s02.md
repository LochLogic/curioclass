- **3-PS2-3:** Ask questions to find what causes and changes electric or magnetic pushes and pulls between two objects that are not touching. Emphasis: how distance makes the force stronger or weaker, and how turning a magnet changes which way the force acts. Limit: Only forces from objects students can handle themselves; electric forces are limited to static electricity.
- **3-PS2-4:** Name a simple design problem that ideas about magnets could help solve, such as a door latch. (The "such as" list gives examples, not required parts.)
- **3-5-ETS1-1:** Name a simple design problem that meets a need or want, with set measures of success and limits on materials, time or cost.
