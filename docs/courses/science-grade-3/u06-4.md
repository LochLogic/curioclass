Its 30-year atlas says warm summers, while station records show summer rainy days ranging from 2 to 9. A proposed wind shelter costs six tokens, within a six-token limit. Its goal is to retain at least five of six markers. Matched trials retain 6, 6, 6 markers with the shelter and 2, 1, 2 without it. "(a) Make a table of the weather data, graph the rainy days and explain the likely seasonal pattern using all three measures and both climate sources. (b) Draw arrows for the shelter. ## 9. Supports and extension

- **English learners.** Words: pattern, range, source, hazard, harm, merit, limit, trial. Frames: "In ___ it is usually ___. The atlas says ___; the station says ___. ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Home-language answers count.
- **Access.** Enlarged and tactile graph grids; a scribe or recorded spoken reasoning, without a second individual interview; large print.
- **Check accommodations.** Read all directions and cards aloud without interpreting data; the same evidence demands. Do not supply the scale, the link between sources or the limit.
- **Reading load.** Cards of 60 words or fewer, no sentence over 15 words, read aloud by the teacher; no independent long reading.
- **Extension, same standard.** Write the one record that would change your climate account, or the one trial that would test your design's limit.
