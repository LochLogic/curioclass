- **3-LS1-1:** Make models showing that living things have many different life cycles, yet all are born, grow, reproduce and die. Emphasis: the changes over a life form a pattern. Limit: Plant life cycles are limited to flowering plants; human reproduction is not covered in detail.
- **3-LS3-1:** Use data as evidence that plants and animals get traits from their parents and that members of a similar group differ in those traits. Emphasis: likenesses and differences between parents and young, or among brothers and sisters, using living things other than people. Limit: No genetic mechanisms and no predicting traits; examples are not human.
- **3-LS3-2:** Use evidence to back the explanation that surroundings can shape an organism's traits, such as a plant stunted by too little water. (The "such as" list gives examples, not required parts.)
