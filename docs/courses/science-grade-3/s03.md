## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How do living things grow and change, and why do they look like their parents but not exactly?"
Day 37: compare the labeled class seedlings with a same-kind adult picture, then two bigleaf hydrangea pictures showing different colors. Ask how related plants can look alike yet differ. Hide causal captions; collect initial explanations.
Day 52 closes the anchor: offspring share inherited features yet vary; surroundings can also shape features. Cite parent-young records, the actual crowding results and the sourced hydrangea case. Pictures alone cannot establish the cause.
Model reading arrows on day 38, comparing records on day 44 and linking evidence to a claim on day 50. Then children propose revisions; ask which observation warrants each change.

## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A cycle means the same individual lives again after death. | Confuses a repeating pattern with an individual's return. Repair by giving parent and offspring different identity labels on the arrows. | Anticipated, from memory. |
