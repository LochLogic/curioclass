## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

Day 45's claim explains how hospitality changes across books I, VI, XIV and XXIII. Day 49 compares Tennyson with Homer and Ovid with the painting.
Model theme refinement on day 23 and sentences on days 24 to 28. Partners trace evidence on day 29; individuals choose details by day 45.
