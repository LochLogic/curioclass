## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | One fictional household represents a whole population. | The inference exceeds its source. Repair: Limit the claim to the represented setting. | From memory. |
| B | A character's hope proves that the event occurs. | Belief replaces narrative evidence. Repair: Separate imagined events from narrated actions. | From memory. |
| C | Any audible pause permits a semicolon. | Clause structure is ignored. Repair: Verify an independent clause on each side. | From memory. |
| D | Similar-looking words are interchangeable in editing. | Meaning is lost. Repair: Check the intended word in a dictionary. | From memory. |
| E | A dependent fragment is a complete sentence. | A main clause is missing. Repair: Attach an independent clause. | From memory. |
| F | Independent clauses need no stronger boundary than a comma. | Sentence boundaries are insecure. Repair: Separate the clauses with a period. | From memory. |
| G | Pronoun person can change for the same referent. | Person consistency is insecure. Repair: Keep the same person. | From memory. |
| H | A plural antecedent permits an unexplained singular pronoun. | Number agreement is insecure. Repair: Match pronoun number to its antecedent. | From memory. |
| I | Two possible antecedents leave a pronoun clear. | Reference is ambiguous. Repair: Replace the pronoun with its intended noun. | From memory. |
| J | Forms in a word family are interchangeable. | Grammatical function is ignored. Repair: Match the form to its sentence role. | From memory. |
| K | A guessed word meaning needs no checking. | Vocabulary verification is missing. Repair: Consult an entry and test the meaning in context. | From memory. |
| L | Unsupported opinion counts as discussion preparation. | Text preparation is missing. Repair: Mark and bring supporting passages. | From memory. |
| M | Discussion rules need no agreement. | Procedures are imposed. Repair: Agree the procedures together. | From memory. |
| N | A monologue counts as collaborative discussion. | Others cannot contribute. Repair: Ask an inclusive clarifying question and hear its answer. | From memory. |
| O | Contrary evidence needs no response. | A view stays insulated from evidence. Repair: Qualify the claim using the differing passage. | From memory. |
| P | A spelling that looks or sounds right needs no dictionary check. | Spelling verification is missing. Repair: Check the word in an entry and reread the corrected word in the sentence. | From memory. |

These are anticipated errors, not observed frequencies.
