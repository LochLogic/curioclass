| A | A rhetorical inference exceeds its cited evidence. | Repair: limit it to what the reread words support. | From memory; anticipated. |
| B | Naming a device explains its effect. | Repair: link specific wording to an audience value and purpose. | From memory; anticipated. |
| C | A persuasive choice guarantees agreement. | Repair: qualify likely effect and separate intention from observed reception. | From memory; anticipated. |
| D | Every union speech has the same occasion. | Repair: place each date beside its audience and public task. | From memory; anticipated. |
| E | A shared topic proves identical treatment. | Repair: pair precise passages with different civic obligations. | From memory; anticipated. |
| F | Age alone establishes significance. | Repair: connect public occasion and crafted expression to civic purpose. | From memory; anticipated. |
