## 6. Error register

| | The error or first idea | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | An inference contradicts the cited action. | Evidence does not license the inference. Repair: reread the action and replace the contradicted claim. | From memory; anticipated, not observed. |
| B | A topic label or reaction substitutes for central-idea analysis. | Details do not yet explain development. Repair: sequence three details, explain their effect on one idea and summarize objectively. | From memory; anticipated. |
| C | A changed word form keeps the original grammatical job. | Suffix change is not linked to syntax. Repair: place each form in a sentence and name its job. | From memory; anticipated. |
| D | Separate source summaries count as synthesis. | Sources are not related to a finding. Repair: group two sources under one finding and explain their relationship. | From memory; anticipated. |
| E | Changing a few source words removes attribution needs. | Paraphrase is confused with substitution. Repair: restate the meaning independently and restore attribution. | From memory; anticipated. |
| F | An author name alone makes a citation traceable. | Source location is missing. Repair: match a locator to a complete source entry. | From memory; anticipated. |
| G | Any attractive digital image advances reasoning. | Decoration substitutes for purpose. Repair: replace decoration with an explained, labeled relationship. | From memory; anticipated. |
| H | Louder delivery adapts a finding to another audience. | Expertise does not shape explanation. Repair: rewrite the opening around what the new listener needs defined. | From memory; anticipated. |
| I | Copied comprehension proves independent reading. | Individual reading evidence is absent. Repair: obtain a fresh passage response with assistance recorded. | From memory; anticipated. |
| J | One life establishes a universal rule about a people. | The claim exceeds its evidence. Repair: limit the claim to narrator and setting. | From memory; anticipated. |
| K | One final paragraph demonstrates writing across time and purposes. | The record lacks range. Repair: recover dated short and revised pieces and identify their purposes. | From memory; anticipated. |
| L | A filmed life supplies every narrated thought. | Medium is confused with complete access to experience. Repair: contrast an observed shot with a narrated thought and mark the limit. | From memory; anticipated. |
| M | Credible publication guarantees accurate interpretation. | Authority replaces checking claims. Repair: check a claim against source data, separately from provenance. | From memory; anticipated. |
| N | A research question's scope changes without evidence. | Refocusing is arbitrary. Repair: name the source detail that requires a narrower or broader question. | From memory; anticipated. |
| O | A qualifying detail leaves the original interpretation unchanged. | Counterevidence is ignored. Repair: identify the qualifying detail and rewrite the claim to account for it. | From memory; anticipated. |
