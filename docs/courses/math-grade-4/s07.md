## 2. Standards, limits, and what is carried

- **Compelling question (frame):** "How can place value and area help us multiply big numbers?"

**Hook, day 96:** Invented for practice: Start with one row of four dots; add one identical row each step. Draw step three. Ask how rows could help count a much larger collection, then pose the compelling question.
**Supporting questions:** What continues beyond the drawn terms (day 99)? Why multiply every place-value part (day 103)? Where is each product in the rectangle (day 106)?
**Closing claim:** I can generate and explain patterns and use place value to explain large products. Growing Rows (day 96), Hidden Features (day 99) and Matching Regions (day 106) prepare day 109.
**Teaching:** Supply every rule. Model decomposition on days 101 to 104; compare records on day 107; step back for day 108 application.


**Practices:** Hidden Features develops MP7 on day 99 by explaining multiples of nine through tens minus groups. Growing Rows develops MP8 on day 96: extend terms, identify an unstated feature and justify its continuation.
**Fluency:** Maintain 4.NBT.B.4 three warm-ups weekly; grade 3 facts on two. Fractions and decimals share two warm-ups. Day 100 diagnoses facts; days 105 and 106 practise already-taught methods with feedback, without speed scores.
**Balance:** Eight understanding, three fluency and two application days give 480, 180 and 120 minutes. This mixed unit has eight main major-work lessons among thirteen. Major time is 460/780 minutes, 59.0 percent: five daily on days 96 to 99 and 55 daily on days 101 to 108. Exclude assessment and response; day 100 earns none. Day 104 uses supporting factor language to split rectangles and practise 4.NBT.B.5; count only those major tasks. This unit's share is below 65 percent.
**Limits:** One supplied pattern rule; no guessing, coordinate graphs or remainder methods. Products stop at four digits by one digit or two digits by two digits. Multiplication-algorithm fluency belongs to grade 5. Factors and algorithms maintain earlier units; unit 8 completes 4.OA.A.3.
**Carried:** Keep unit 6's tape and number line for contexts. Tie arrays to equations on day 101; withdraw counted dots on day 102. Tie base-ten drawings to equations on day 102; withdraw supplied drawings on day 103. Tie partitioned rectangles on day 101 to partial products; label each region through day 106, then withdraw supplied partitions on day 107. Students still draw explanations; keep one area picture into grade 5.

Not meeting: "Square, square, triangle, square, triangle, square" (C).
