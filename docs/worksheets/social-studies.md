# Worksheets: Social Studies

Covers Kindergarten through Grade 5 Social Studies by grade, the three middle school courses (World Geography, World History, US History to 1877) and the five high school courses (World History, US History since 1877, Government, Economics, Psychology). Each list follows the C3 Framework and runs in teaching order through the year, with review sheets at natural checkpoints.
