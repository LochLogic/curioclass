# Worksheets: English Language Arts

Covers Kindergarten through Grade 12 English Language Arts, one list per grade, following CCSS-ELA: foundational skills lead in K-2, then reading literature and informational text, writing, speaking and listening, and language. Each list runs in teaching order through the year; passages are original unless a public-domain text is named.
