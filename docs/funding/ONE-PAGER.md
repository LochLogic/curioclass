# Curio Class: free hands-on tools for every K-12 classroom

**curioclass.org** · Cory, maker · cory@curioclass.org

## The problem

Good interactive classroom material sits behind paywalls, logins and district contracts. Teachers already spend
their own money and evenings to fill the gap, and every new login is one more account holding student data.

## What Curio Class is

A free library of interactive tools for every grade and subject, K-12, aligned to national standards. No paywall, no
accounts, no ads, no tracking. Every tool is one self-contained file that works offline: on a smartboard, on a
locked-down Chromebook, or from a USB stick with no internet. Content is CC BY 4.0 and code is MIT, so anyone can use,
adapt and share it.

Each tool comes with a printable lesson plan a substitute could run, a Sub Day packet, an exit ticket, an anchor chart,
vocabulary cards in English and Spanish, and a family game for home.

## Where it stands (live since October 2026)

- **76 tools live,** from a kindergarten computer builder to a high school fact-check lab. Open any of them now.
- **797 tools planned** across every grade and subject. The 721 still to build are on the site already, as cards on
  "the bench" at curioclass.org/bench, so you can see exactly what your support finishes.
- **Supply Closet live:** 4,400+ vocabulary cards in English and Spanish, 104 family games, anchor charts and posters.
- **Reading Room in progress:** about 700 public domain stories, curated and processed.
- Every tool goes through a written build process: a spec, a build, three independent quality reviews (teaching,
  accessibility, play), a final check, then a human review before it goes live.
