---------- Tell us about the project's reach and impact ----------

Curio Class (curioclass.org) is a free, openly licensed K-12 curriculum: 118 courses across every grade and the core subjects, each with a full-year scope and sequence, day-by-day lessons, printables and interactive tools. No paywall, no accounts, no ads, no tracking. It works offline, the printables stand on their own, and the 92 interactive tools live today are each one self-contained HTML file that runs on a smartboard, a locked-down school Chromebook or a USB stick. Code is MIT and content is CC BY 4.0, so any teacher, district or translator can adapt, rebrand and print it.

Kindergarten to grade 3 science is finished and signed off, and about 1,380 days of lessons are written across science, social studies and English language arts for those grades. The rest of the plan is public in the repo, so anyone can see exactly what's done and what's left.

The gap it fills: good curriculum sits behind subscriptions, logins and district contracts, and teachers often pay for material themselves. Curio Class is free for good, and because it never collects student data, schools can use it without privacy paperwork.

For the open source ecosystem, the repo is also a working, documented recipe for building careful educational content with Claude Code: written standards, a pipeline where Claude drafts, separate Claude readers check teaching, accessibility and sensitivity, and a person signs off every page, plus a shared MIT kit of reusable engines for interactive tools. Anyone building free educational material can copy it.

It's new and not marketed yet, so it doesn't have downloads or dependents. Its reach will be counted in classrooms, not packages.

---------- How will you use the subscription for your project? ----------

All of it goes into writing the rest of the curriculum. Claude Code is the engine of the whole project: Claude drafts units and days against the written scope, separate Claude readers check each one cold, and I fix and sign off. The usage limit is the bottleneck: runs stop mid-grade when limits hit, and quality depends on giving each check its own fresh read instead of cutting corners.

Six months of Ultra lets those runs go uninterrupted. In that time I'll carry the finished K-3 work up the grades in science, social studies and English language arts, and everything produced, curriculum, tools and pipeline improvements, is published openly under CC BY and MIT as it lands.

Every token turns into something free: a lesson any teacher, student or parent can open, print and keep, with no account and no paywall.
