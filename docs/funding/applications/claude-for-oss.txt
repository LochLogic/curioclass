---------- Tell us about the project's reach and impact ----------

Curio Class (curioclass.org) is building something that has never existed: a complete K-12 curriculum, every grade and every core subject, free forever to every child in the world, with no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no student data. It's fully philanthropic. Nothing is sold, ever. Code is MIT and content is CC BY 4.0, so any teacher, district, ministry or translator can adapt, rebrand and print it.

The ambition is millions of children, starting with the classrooms that can least afford a curriculum. Every tool is one self-contained HTML file that runs offline on a smartboard, a locked-down Chromebook or a USB stick, and every printable works in black and white, so it reaches schools with no budget and no bandwidth.

By the numbers, five weeks in (counted 2026-11-27):
- 118 courses, kindergarten to grade 12, every one with a full-year scope and sequence
- 2,115 lesson days written; kindergarten to grade 3 science finished and signed off
- 92 interactive tools live
- 1,978 commits, 38,000+ files in a public repo
- More than 4 billion AI tokens of drafting and checking, with a person signing off every page

It's also a working, open recipe for building careful educational content with Claude Code: written standards, Claude drafting, separate Claude readers checking teaching, accessibility and sensitivity, and human sign-off. Anyone building free learning material can copy it.

---------- How will you use the subscription for your project? ----------

Every token goes into finishing the curriculum. Claude Code is the engine of the whole project: Claude drafts units and days against the written scope, separate Claude readers check each one cold, and I fix and sign off. The usage limit is the bottleneck. Runs stop mid-grade when limits hit, and the quality bar depends on giving every check its own fresh read.

Six months of Ultra turns that into uninterrupted runs. In that time I'll carry the finished kindergarten to grade 3 work up through the grades in science, social studies, English language arts and math, and publish everything as it lands: curriculum, tools and pipeline improvements, all under CC BY and MIT.

Every token Anthropic gives us becomes a lesson any child, teacher or parent anywhere can open, print and keep, free, forever.
