# Contact sheet: calls, emails and lunches

Gathered 2026-10-28 from each organization's own website. Only published office contacts and named staff are listed;
where no direct line is published, use the main line and ask for the person by name. Denver metro assumed.

The rule for this list: **act this week.** Funders with closed grant windows still take calls and coffee now, and a
relationship started in November is the one that gets funded when the window opens.

## This week, in order

### 1. InnEdCO conference proposal (deadline Sunday 2026-11-01)

Colorado's statewide innovative education conference, run by a 501(c)(3). The 2027 conference is June 11 to
13 in Breckenridge. Teachers, teacher librarians, district leaders and superintendents attend: exactly the people who
should know about Curio Class, and the people funders listen to.

- **Submit a session proposal:** https://bit.ly/InnEdCOCFP27 (first-time presenters welcome). Closes 2026-11-01.
- Email: info@innedco.org · President: Becca Skiver · Mail: 6547 N Academy Blvd #444, Colorado Springs, CO 80918
- Session idea: "797 free tools, zero logins: building a no-tracking K-12 library" (live demo of 3 tools, the Sub
  Day packet, Word Wall prints).
- Later: booth sponsorships run $2,000 to $3,000, a good line for a funder to pay for.

### 2. Colorado Nonprofit Development Center (fiscal sponsor) - call first

Unlocks almost every grant below. About 10 percent of incoming funds.

- Phone: **(720) 855-0501** · Email: info@cndc.org · Mail: P.O. Box 18770, Denver, CO 80218
- Inquiry form: https://cndc.org/partner-with-cndc/sponsorship/ (they aim to reply within 5 business days)
- Steps: inquiry form, invitation to apply, partnership discussion, CNDC board approval.
- Eligibility: benefits Colorado communities, charitable intent, committed leadership, US-only operations. They do not
  take medical or legal practices or one-on-one mentoring of children (Curio Class is neither).
- Ask on the call: typical time from inquiry to approval, the fee and what it covers, and whether a solo-led project
  needs an advisory committee.

### 3. Gates Family Foundation (Denver) - ask for an introductory conversation

Not the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation. Their page says "We welcome introductory conversations" and asks you to
contact the program officer for your area before applying. Generally funds 501(c)(3)s serving Colorado, so mention the
CNDC application.

- Phone: **(303) 722-1881** (ask for the education team) · Email: info@gatesfamilyfoundation.org
- Mail: 1390 Lawrence Street, Suite 400, Denver, CO 80204
- Education staff: **Russell Ramsey**, Vice President, Education; **Ana Soler** (listed as Director, Equity and
  Engagement, and as Senior Program Officer, Education).
- Ask: a 20-minute call or coffee to show the site and ask whether it fits their education priorities.

- Next cycle expected to open around 2027-01-15 (letter of inquiry first). Ask about the pre-application workshop.
- Ask: a meeting to walk through fit before the cycle opens.

### 7. Rotary District 5450 (Denver area) - lunch talks

Rotary clubs meet weekly over lunch or breakfast and always need speakers; club members are local business owners and
often fund local education.

- Club directory with meeting times and contacts: https://www.rotary5450.org/clubdirectory/
- District contact page: https://www.rotary5450.org/sitepage/contact-district-5450
- District grants: https://www.rotary5450.org/sitepage/grants
- Speaker database clubs search: https://rotaryd5450speakers.netlify.app/ (a club adds a speaker after a good talk at
  https://form.jotform.com/261675739043060, so the first talk gets you listed for the rest)
- Ask: contact two or three nearby clubs' program chairs from the directory for a 20-minute talk slot.

### 8. Daniels Fund - take the eligibility quiz

K-12 education is a program area. Invitation only, rolling, and some fiscally sponsored projects are eligible (the
sponsor's mission must align and an MOU is needed). Individuals are not eligible, so this follows CNDC.

- Eligibility quiz: https://grants.danielsfund.org/s/eligibility-quiz
- Phone: **877-791-4726** · Mail: 101 Monroe Street, Denver, CO 80206
- A decline means a one-year wait, so take the quiz only once the fiscal sponsor is in place.

### 9. Denver Public Schools Foundation - teacher pilot

an educator group that would use the tools.

- Contact page: https://dpsfoundation.org/ (Partner and Volunteer page) · My Spark line for reference: (720) 423-1070
