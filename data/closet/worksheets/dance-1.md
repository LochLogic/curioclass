# 1. Traveling Moves Picture Dictionary
minutes: 25
materials: pencil, crayons, open floor space
ican: I can name and do 8 ways to travel across the floor.
directions: (Read aloud.) Trace each move word. In each box, draw a stick figure doing that move. Then match each move to the clue about how feet do it.
mistakes: Skip, gallop and slide are easy to mix up. Skip is step-hop with feet taking turns; gallop goes forward with the same foot always in front; slide goes sideways with the same foot leading. Hop and jump get mixed up too: a hop is one foot, a jump is two feet.
notes: Do each move together before children draw it, across the room in small groups so no one runs into anyone. Run only in a large clear space, or switch to "jog in place" in a small room. Leaps can be tiny: over a pretend puddle, not over real objects. Stick figures can show a move with motion lines, a bent knee or one foot up; a child who draws feet off the ground for jump has the idea.

## Stretch: How do the feet move?
match: (Read aloud.) Match each move to its clue.
  - hop = one foot up, land on the same foot
  - jump = two feet up, land on two feet
  - leap = push off one foot, fly, land on the other foot
  - skip = step, hop, step, hop
  - slide = go sideways, step together

# 21. Story Time Improvise
minutes: 25
materials: pencil, crayons, open floor space
ican: I can make up moves that show the beginning, middle and end of a story.
directions: (Read aloud.) Listen to the story. Dance it with me as I read. Then answer the story questions and circle the moves you made.
mistakes: Children often do the same big move for the whole story. Read it a second time slowly and pause at each part: "Show me curled up small. Now show me pushing up." Some children circle every word: ask "Which one did your body really do?"
notes: Read the story once while children listen, then again while they improvise in their own space. There are no wrong moves in Part B; the circles are each child's own. Children who cannot get down to the floor can curl up small while sitting or standing.

## Warm up: The story
passage: The Little Seed
  A little seed sleeps under the ground. It is curled up small and still.
  Then the rain comes, drip, drop. The sun comes out, warm and bright. The seed pushes up, up, up. It grows a stem and stretches its leaves wide.
  The wind blows, and the tall flower sways from side to side. At last the flower opens wide to the sun.

## Story questions
choice: (Read aloud.) At the beginning, the seed is
  * curled up small
  - tall and wide
  - blowing in the wind
choice: (Read aloud.) In the middle, the seed
  - falls asleep
  * pushes up and grows
  - rolls away
choice: (Read aloud.) At the end, the flower
  - curls up small
  - melts
  * opens wide

## Main: Circle the moves you made
text: (Read aloud.) Circle one move you made in each row.
table: Part of the story | Move | Move | Move | Move
  - Beginning | curl up | shrink | hide | be still
  - Middle | push up | grow | stretch | reach
  - End | sway | spin | open wide | float
