# 1. Wash, Wash, Wash! Handwashing Steps
minutes: 20
materials: pencil, scissors, glue stick, crayons
ican: I can wash my hands in the right order.
directions: (Read aloud.) Say each picture's name. Write a number under each picture to show the order: 1 is first and 5 is last. Then cut out the pictures and glue them in order on the sink strip.
pairs: habit-hero
mistakes: Children often put soap before getting hands wet, or skip rinsing and go straight to drying. Act out the steps at a pretend sink: wet, soap, scrub, rinse, dry. The faucet picture (wet) and the hands under water (rinse) look alike: point out the soap bubbles are gone in the rinse step because it comes after scrubbing.
notes: Name every picture together before anyone writes: water on, soap, scrub with bubbles, rinse, dry with a towel. The steps follow the CDC: wet, lather, scrub for at least 20 seconds (about two times through the "Happy Birthday" song), rinse, dry. If cutting is too much for your group, the numbers alone show the order; skip the strip.

## Main: Number the steps
picword:
  - soap | soap {2}
  - towel | dry {5}
  - faucet | wet {1}
  - rinse | rinse {4}
  - suds | scrub {3}

## The sink strip
draw: Glue step 1 here at the left, then 2, 3, 4, and step 5 at the right. | 1.4

## Stretch: When do we wash?
choice: (Read aloud.) When is a time to wash your hands?
  - While we look at the clouds
  * Before we eat a snack
  - While we sing a song at circle time

# 21. One Swing, Two Friends
minutes: 20
materials: pencil, crayons
ican: I can take turns with a friend.
directions: (Read aloud.) Look at each picture. Two friends want the same thing. Draw how they can take turns.
mistakes: Some children draw one friend playing and the other crying or walking away. Ask, "What does the other friend do while they wait? When is it their turn?" A good drawing shows both friends, one using it and one waiting, or a number or a timer for "next."
notes: Before they draw, explain taking turns: one friend goes first, the other waits and counts to 20, then they switch. Act out one problem first with two children and a real book: one reads while the other counts to 20, then they switch. Words to practice: "Can I have a turn when you're done?" and "You go first, then me." Early finishers can tell a partner what their friends are saying.

## Main: Draw the turns
picword:
  - swing | 1. swing
  - slide | 2. slide
  - book | 3. book
draw: 1. Mia and Leo both want the swing. Draw them taking turns. | 1.05
draw: 2. Sam and Ava both want the slide. Draw them taking turns. | 1.05
draw: 3. Kai and Rosa both want the book. Draw them taking turns. | 1.05

## Stretch: Kind words
choice: (Read aloud.) Which words help friends take turns?
  - "Mine! Go away."
  * "You go first, then me."
  - "I had it first, so you never get it."
