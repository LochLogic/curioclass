# 1. Blend Builders: st, fl, gr
minutes: 25
materials: pencil, scissors, glue stick
ican: I can hear and write the blends st, fl, gr and cl.
directions: Say the name of each picture. Write the blend that starts the word. Then cut out the word cards and glue each one under its blend.
mistakes: Children who write one letter (s for st) are hearing the first sound only: have them stretch the word slowly, "sssttt-ar," and listen for both sounds. Watch for fl and fr mixed up in flower.
notes: Say each picture name together before students start so nobody gets stuck on a picture (flashlight, stamp and grill are the least familiar). The cards on the second part can be sorted before gluing so you can check them.

## Warm up: Listen for the blend
example: star starts with **st**: s + t. Say it slowly: st-ar.

## Main: Write the blend
picword:
  - star | {st}ar
  - flag | {fl}ag
  - grapes | {gr}apes
  - clock | {cl}ock
  - stairs | {st}airs
  - flower | {fl}ower
  - graph | {gr}aph
  - cloud | {cl}oud
  - stamp | {st}amp
  - flashlight | {fl}ashlight
  - grill | {gr}ill
  - clam | {cl}am

## Sort: Blend chart
sort: Glue each word under its blend.
  - st: star, stairs, stamp
  - fl: flag, flower, flashlight
  - gr: grapes, graph, grill
  - cl: clock, cloud, clam
