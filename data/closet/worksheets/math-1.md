# 1. Pond Stories: Add To and Take From
minutes: 30
materials: pencil, crayons
ican: I can write an equation for an add to or take from story and solve it.
directions: (Read aloud.) Listen to each pond story. Draw a picture or jump on the number line. Then write the equation and the answer.
mistakes: Children often add every story because both numbers are "in it". Ask: "Did more frogs come, or did some go away?" In items 7 and 9 the missing number is the change, not the total; children who write 8 + 11 = 19 for item 7 need to act it out with counters.
notes: Read each story twice. Act out item 3 with counters or paper frogs before the sheet. Equations may be written other ways that are still true (for item 7, 11 - 8 = 3 is fine). A number line jump: start at the first number, jump forward to add, back to take away.

## Warm up
example: 6 frogs sit on a log. 2 more frogs hop on. Start at 6 and jump 2: 6 + 2 = 8. There are 8 frogs.
fill:
  - (Read aloud.) 4 frogs. 1 more hops in. {4 + 1 = 5}
  - (Read aloud.) 7 frogs. 2 hop away. {7 - 2 = 5}

## Stretch: Your own story
write: (Read aloud.) Make up your own pond story for 10 - 4. Draw it or tell it to a friend, then write the answer.
  lines: 2
  key: Answers vary. A take from story with 10 at the start and 4 leaving, such as "10 ducks swim. 4 ducks fly away. 6 ducks are left." The answer is 6.

# 24. Add Tens to Any Number
minutes: 25
materials: pencil
ican: I can add tens to a two-digit number.
directions: (Read aloud.) Add the tens. Jump by tens on the number line, or draw tens and ones. Write the sum.
mistakes: The most common error is adding to the ones: 23 + 30 written as 26. Ask: "Is 30 three ones or three tens?" Children who count by ones on the number line will lose count; show them one big jump for each ten.
notes: Only the tens digit changes in every problem, which is the pattern to notice in the stretch. A ten can be drawn as a long stick and a one as a dot. Problem 10 starts with the tens (40 + 38); children can start from 38 and jump 4 tens instead.

## Warm up: 10 more
example: 36 + 20. Start at 36. Jump 10 to 46. Jump 10 more to 56. So 36 + 20 = 56.
fill:
  - 10 more than 24 is {34}
  - 10 more than 57 is {67}

## Stretch: Find the mistake
write: (Read aloud.) Mia says 34 + 20 = 36. What did Mia do? What is the right answer?
  lines: 2
  key: Mia added 2 ones instead of 2 tens. 34 + 20 = 54: the tens go from 3 to 5 and the ones stay 4.
