# 1. Story Mountain: The Enormous Turnip
minutes: 30
materials: pencil
ican: I can tell the beginning, middle and end of a story and find the most exciting part.
directions: (Read aloud.) Listen to The Enormous Turnip. Fill in the story mountain: the beginning at the bottom, the middle at the top, the end on the way down. Then circle the part with the most excitement for an audience.
mistakes: Students often put "they pulled" in every part. The beginning sets up the problem (the turnip is too big), the top of the mountain is the biggest moment (everyone pulls, even the mouse), and the end is how it turns out (pop, everyone falls). Some think the beginning is the most exciting because it comes first.
notes: The Enormous Turnip is a Russian folktale, retold here in Curio Class's own words (about 110 words). Read it aloud twice, then let small groups act it out in a line, each actor holding the waist of the one in front. Ask the audience when they leaned forward: that is the top of the mountain.

## Listen
passage: The Enormous Turnip
  Grandpa planted a turnip seed. "Grow, little turnip! Grow big and sweet!" The turnip grew and grew until it was enormous.
  Grandpa pulled and pulled, but he could not pull it up. So he called Grandma. They pulled and pulled. Still the turnip stayed in the ground.
  Grandma called the girl. The girl called the dog. The dog called the cat. They all pulled and pulled. Still it stayed!
  At last the cat called a tiny mouse. The mouse pulled the cat, the cat pulled the dog, the dog pulled the girl, the girl pulled Grandma, and Grandma pulled Grandpa. Pop! Out came the enormous turnip, and everyone fell down in a heap.

## Main: Story mountain
write: Bottom of the mountain (the beginning): What happens first?
  lines: 2
  key: Grandpa plants a turnip seed, and it grows enormous. He cannot pull it up.
write: Top of the mountain (the middle): What is the biggest moment?
  lines: 2
  key: Grandpa calls for help again and again: Grandma, the girl, the dog, the cat, and at last the tiny mouse. They all pull together.
write: Down the mountain (the end): How does it turn out?
  lines: 2
  key: Pop! The turnip comes out, and everyone falls down in a heap.

## Circle the most exciting part
choice: (Read aloud.) Which part has the most excitement for an audience?
  - The beginning: Grandpa plants a seed.
  * The top: everyone, even the mouse, pulls together. Will it come out?
  - The end: the turnip is out.

## Stretch
write: How could actors make the top of the mountain exciting for the audience?
  lines: 2
  key: Answers vary: pull harder and harder, make straining faces and grunting sounds, slow down before the pop, count "one, two, three, pull!" together, fall down with a big tumble.

# 21. What Do the Puppets Like? Bar Graph
minutes: 25
materials: pencil, crayons
ican: I can make a bar graph and answer questions about it.
directions: (Read aloud.) Read the chart about 4 puppets. Make a bar graph of how many times each puppet played its favorite game. Then answer the questions.
mistakes: Students may color one box too many by starting at 1 instead of at the bottom line, or color the space between numbers. Show them that each bar starts at 0 and stops on the line for its number. Watch for "How many more" answered with the bigger number (8) instead of the difference (5).
notes: The graph grid numbers the puppets 1 to 4 along the bottom; students draw each bar as a box centered on its number (the key shows the bars). Before graphing, have students point to each puppet's number in the chart. Puppet names and data are made up.

## Read the chart
table: Puppet | Likes to eat | Likes to play | Times it played this week
  - 1 Fox | berries | tag | 6
  - 2 Frog | crackers | hopscotch | 8
  - 3 Owl | carrots | hide and seek | 3
  - 4 Bear | apples | catch | 5

## Warm up
fill: Owl likes to eat {carrots}.
fill: Bear likes to play {catch}.

## Main: Make a bar graph
graph: Puppet (1 Fox, 2 Frog, 3 Owl, 4 Bear) | 0 5 1 | Times played | 0 10 1
  key: 0.7,0 0.7,6 1.3,6 1.3,0 1.7,0 1.7,8 2.3,8 2.3,0 2.7,0 2.7,3 3.3,3 3.3,0 3.7,0 3.7,5 4.3,5 4.3,0

## Questions
choice: Which puppet played its game the most times?
  - Fox
  * Frog
  - Owl
  - Bear
choice: Which puppet played its game the fewest times?
  - Fox
  - Frog
  * Owl
  - Bear
fill: How many more times did Frog play than Owl? {5} more times

## Stretch
write: Plan a puppet picnic scene. Which snacks should go on the prop table so every puppet gets the food it likes? Use the chart.
  lines: 2
  key: Berries for Fox, crackers for Frog, carrots for Owl and apples for Bear.
