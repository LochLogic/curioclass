# 1. Push or Pull? Picture Sort
minutes: 20
materials: pencil, scissors, glue stick, a ball and a small toy wagon or box with a string to demonstrate
ican: I can tell a push from a pull.
directions: (Read aloud.) A push moves something away from you. A pull moves it toward you. Listen to each picture's words. Cut out the cards and glue each one under Push or Pull.
mistakes: Children often sort by the object instead of the action (both drawer cards go together). Act out each card with them: a push moves something away from you, a pull moves it toward you. A wagon can be pushed from behind; if a child says push and shows how, accept it.
notes: Read every card aloud and point to its picture before cutting, since the cards carry words, not pictures. Hold up each finished card and have the class act it out with their hands before gluing. A quick demo first: push a ball away across the floor, then pull a toy back by its string.

## Main: Look and listen
picword:
  - ball | kick a ball
  - light-switch | press a switch
  - drawer | shut a drawer
  - cart | roll a cart
  - drawer | open a drawer
  - sock | put on a sock
  - flower | pick a flower
  - wagon | tow a wagon

## Sort: Push or pull?
sort:
  - Push: kick a ball, press a switch, shut a drawer, roll a cart
  - Pull: open a drawer, put on a sock, pick a flower, tow a wagon

# 22. Feed the Animals Match
minutes: 20
materials: pencil, crayons
ican: I can tell what animals eat.
directions: (Read aloud.) Write the letter of each animal's food. Circle the plant eaters.
mistakes: Children may match by where an animal lives (frog with fish, since both live near water). Ask "What does it put in its mouth?" Some think the bee eats the whole flower: it drinks nectar and eats pollen from the flower.
notes: Name every picture together first: grass, flower, leaf, fly, fish, worm; then cow, bee, caterpillar, frog, cat, bird. Plants: grass, flower, leaf. Animals: fly, fish, worm. Children who cannot write the letter yet can draw a line from the animal to its food instead. Talk about it after: a cow, a bee and a caterpillar eat only plants; a frog, a cat and this bird eat animals.
