# 1. Agree and Disagree Frames
minutes: 25
materials: pencil
ican: I can agree or disagree with a classmate's idea and give a reason.
directions: Read each opinion card. Decide if you agree or see it differently. Write your answer with a sentence frame and give a reason.
mistakes: Students often write "I agree" or "I disagree" with no reason. Point to the word "because" and ask, "Why?" Some students think disagreeing is rude; model a friendly voice and remind them they are talking about the idea, not the person.
notes: There is no right side on any card; a strong answer uses a frame and gives a reason that fits. Read the cards aloud first. For speaking practice, have pairs read a card aloud and answer each other out loud before they write. Students can cut the cards apart and use them again in a small-group talk.

## Warm up: How we answer
example: **Card:** Ruby says, "Cats are the best pets because they are quiet."
  **Agree:** I agree with Ruby because cats do not bark, so they are good for apartments.
  **Disagree:** I see it differently because dogs can learn tricks and play outside with you.
text: **Word bank:** agree, disagree, because, opinion, reason, I see it differently, I think, also, but, so
choice: Which answer disagrees in a kind way and gives a reason?
  - "That is a bad idea."
  - "No. You are wrong."
  * "I see it differently because winter has snow days, and I love sledding."
  - "I agree."

## Main: Opinion cards
cards: Read each card.
  - Lena says: "Recess should be longer, because kids need time to run and play."
  - Omar says: "Every class should have a class pet, because it teaches kids to be responsible."
  - Priya says: "Summer is the best season, because the days are long and warm."
  - Diego says: "Reading a book is better than watching the movie of it."
  - Mai says: "Students should help choose the school lunch menu."
  - Sam says: "Board games are more fun than playing outside."
write: Card 2 (Lena): Do you agree that recess should be longer?
  lines: 2
  starter: I agree with Lena because ... / I see it differently because ...
  key: Answers vary. Agree: I agree with Lena because playing outside helps us focus better in class. Disagree: I see it differently because we need that time to learn.
write: Card 3 (Omar): Do you agree that every class should have a pet?
  lines: 2
  starter: I agree with Omar because ... / I see it differently because ...
  key: Answers vary. Agree: I agree with Omar because feeding a pet every day teaches us to remember jobs. Disagree: I see it differently because some kids are allergic to animals.
write: Card 4 (Priya): Do you agree that summer is the best season?
  lines: 2
  starter: I agree with Priya because ... / I see it differently because ...
  key: Answers vary. Agree: I agree with Priya because we can swim and play outside late. Disagree: I see it differently because fall has cool weather and colorful leaves.
write: Card 5 (Diego): Do you agree that a book is better than the movie?
  lines: 2
  starter: I agree with Diego because ... / I see it differently because ...
  key: Answers vary. Agree: I agree with Diego because a book tells more about what the characters think. Disagree: I see it differently because a movie lets you see and hear the story.
write: Card 6 (Mai): Do you agree that students should help choose the lunch menu?
  lines: 2
  starter: I agree with Mai because ... / I see it differently because ...
  key: Answers vary. Agree: I agree with Mai because students know which foods they will eat. Disagree: I see it differently because the cooks need to make sure the meals are healthy.
write: Card 7 (Sam): Do you agree that board games are more fun than playing outside?
  lines: 2
  starter: I agree with Sam because ... / I see it differently because ...
  key: Answers vary. Agree: I agree with Sam because board games make you think and plan. Disagree: I see it differently because outside you can run and play with more friends.

## Stretch: Talk back
write: Pick one card you disagreed with. What could that classmate say back to you? Write their answer.
  lines: 3
  starter: That is a good point, but ...
  key: Answers vary. Example for Diego: "That is a good point, but some movies show things you can't picture in your head, like a real dragon flying." The answer responds to the reason, not the person.

# 7. Fishbowl Talk Moves Tally
minutes: 30
materials: pencil, a fishbowl discussion (4 to 5 students in an inner circle, the rest watching)
ican: I can notice talk moves in a discussion and tell why a move helped the group.
directions: First, read the practice discussion and make a tally mark for each talk move you find. Then watch the fishbowl. Make a tally mark each time you hear a talk move. Write down two strong examples you heard.
mistakes: Students often tally every sentence, even ones that only share an idea. A talk move answers or builds on someone else. Watch for "I agree" with no reason: it does not count as "agree with a reason" until the speaker says "because".
notes: Seat 4 or 5 volunteers in an inner circle with a simple question (for example, "Should our class have a reading corner or a building corner?"). Everyone else sits outside with this sheet. Run the fishbowl for 6 to 8 minutes, then switch circles if you have time. In the practice discussion, line 4 (Maya explains) and line 7 (Ben says "Yes") are not talk moves, so they get no tally.

## Warm up: Practice tally
text: **Word bank:** talk move, agree, disagree, because, ask, paraphrase, add on, idea, reason, fishbowl
text: **Practice discussion: Should our class get a fish tank?**
text: 1. **Maya:** I think we should get a fish tank because fish are calm to watch.
text: 2. **Leo:** I agree with Maya because watching fish could help us relax.
text: 3. **Sofia:** What do you mean by calm?
text: 4. **Maya:** I mean the fish swim slowly, and the water sounds quiet.
text: 5. **Ben:** I see it differently because someone has to clean the tank every week.
text: 6. **Leo:** So you are saying a fish tank is a lot of work?
text: 7. **Ben:** Yes.
text: 8. **Sofia:** I want to add on to Ben's idea. We could make a job chart for cleaning.
text: 9. **Maya:** I agree with Sofia because then everyone shares the work.
table: Talk move | Frame | Tally in the practice
  - Agree with a reason | I agree with ___ because ___. | {2 (lines 2 and 9)}
  - Disagree politely | I see it differently because ___. | {1 (line 5)}
  - Ask a question | What do you mean by ___? | {1 (line 3)}
  - Say it again (paraphrase) | So you are saying ___? | {1 (line 6)}
  - Add on | I want to add on to ___'s idea. | {1 (line 8)}

## Main: Watch the fishbowl
chart: Tally marks | Who used it?
  labels: Agree with a reason; Disagree politely; Ask a question; Say it again; Add on
  height: 0.55
  key: Answers vary with the discussion. Check that tallies go with real moves (a reason, a question, a repeat, an add-on), not every sentence.
write: Write one strong talk move you heard. Who said it? Why was it strong?
  lines: 3
  starter: ___ said, "___." It was a strong ___ because ___.
  key: Example: Jada said, "I see it differently because a building corner lets more kids play at once." It was a strong disagree because she was kind and gave a reason.
write: Write a second strong talk move you heard.
  lines: 3
  starter: ___ said, "___." It was a strong ___ because ___.
  key: Example: Omar said, "So you are saying the reading corner would be quiet?" It was a strong paraphrase because he checked that he understood before he answered.

## Stretch: Help the group
write: Which talk move did the group use the least? Write a sentence someone could say next time to use it.
  lines: 3
  starter: The group used ___ the least. Next time someone could say, "___."
  key: Example: The group used ask a question the least. Next time someone could say, "What do you mean by a building corner?"

# 20. Explain Your Thinking
minutes: 30
materials: pencil
ican: I can explain how I solved a multiplication problem and why I chose my strategy.
directions: Solve each problem. Show your work in the box. Then explain your thinking with the frame: "I used ___ because ___. My answer is ___."
mistakes: Students often name a strategy but give a "because" that does not explain ("because it is easy"). Push for a reason about the problem: "because there are 4 equal groups." Watch for answers with no unit: "24" should be "24 apples."
notes: Any correct strategy is fine: equal groups, an array, skip counting, repeated addition or a fact I know. Have partners read their frames aloud to each other before they write. The models show one good strategy for each problem.

## Warm up: Strategy words
example: **Problem:** Each box has 5 crayons. How many crayons are in 3 boxes?
  **Work:** 5, 10, 15
  **Explain:** I used skip counting because there are 3 equal groups of 5. I counted 5, 10, 15. My answer is 15 crayons.
text: **Word bank:** equal groups, array, rows, skip counting, repeated addition, a fact I know, multiply, because, my answer is
fill:
  - Rows and columns of dots make an {array}.
  - 4 + 4 + 4 = 12 is {repeated addition}.
  - Counting 2, 4, 6, 8 is {skip counting}.

## Main: Solve and explain
text: **Problem 1:** Ms. Lee puts 6 apples in each bag. She fills 4 bags. How many apples does she have?
draw: Show your work. | 1.1
write: Explain your thinking.
  lines: 2
  starter: I used ___ because ___. My answer is ___.
  key: I used equal groups because there are 4 bags with 6 apples in each. 4 x 6 = 24. My answer is 24 apples.
text: **Problem 2:** The gym has 3 rows of chairs. Each row has 7 chairs. How many chairs are there?
draw: Show your work. | 1.1
write: Explain your thinking.
  lines: 2
  starter: I used ___ because ___. My answer is ___.
  key: I used an array because the chairs are in 3 rows of 7. 3 x 7 = 21. My answer is 21 chairs.
text: **Problem 3:** A pack has 8 markers. Tom buys 5 packs. How many markers does he have?
draw: Show your work. | 1.1
write: Explain your thinking.
  lines: 2
  starter: I used ___ because ___. My answer is ___.
  key: I used skip counting because there are 5 packs of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40. My answer is 40 markers. (A fact I know, 5 x 8 = 40, is also fine.)

## Stretch: Another way
write: Solve Problem 2 a different way. Explain your new way.
  lines: 3
  starter: I also used ___ because ___. I still got ___.
  key: I also used repeated addition because each row has 7 chairs: 7 + 7 + 7 = 21. I still got 21 chairs.

# 21. Same Answer, Different Way
minutes: 30
materials: pencil, scratch paper
ican: I can explain if I agree with a classmate's solution and show another way to solve it.
directions: Read each classmate's solution. Check it. Then answer with a frame: "I agree because ___" and "I solved it a different way: ___."
mistakes: Students may write "I agree because it is right." Push for a reason that checks the math ("because 4 x 6 is also 24"). In the stretch, students often agree with Raj's answer because his steps look neat; have them check the last skip-count step.
notes: Every solution in Main is correct, so students practice agreeing with a reason and showing a second strategy. Raj's solution in the Stretch has a mistake on purpose: his last skip-count jumps by 6, not 8. Good second strategies: arrays, repeated addition, skip counting, a number line, related facts, place value.

## Warm up: Read the model
example: **Jo's solution:** 5 x 3 = 15. "I skip counted by 5 three times: 5, 10, 15."
  **Answer:** I agree because 5 + 5 + 5 is also 15. I solved it a different way: I drew 3 rows of 5 dots and counted 15.
text: **Word bank:** agree, because, strategy, equal groups, rows, skip count, count up, related fact, add, subtract, multiply, divide, place value
fill:
  - Jo used the strategy of {skip counting} by 5.
  - 3 rows of 5 dots is an {array}.

## Main: Check and respond
text: **Sam's solution:** 6 x 4 = 24. "I drew 6 groups with 4 dots in each group. Then I counted all the dots: 24."
write: Respond to Sam.
  lines: 3
  starter: I agree because ... I solved it a different way: ...
  key: I agree because 6 groups of 4 is 24. I solved it a different way: I skip counted by 4 six times: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24. (Or 4 x 6 = 24, or 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24.)
text: **Ana's solution:** 53 - 28 = 25. "I counted up from 28. 28 to 30 is 2. 30 to 53 is 23. 2 + 23 = 25."
write: Respond to Ana.
  lines: 3
  starter: I agree because ... I solved it a different way: ...
  key: I agree because 28 + 25 = 53. I solved it a different way: 53 - 30 = 23, then I added 2 back because I took away 2 too many: 23 + 2 = 25.
text: **Leo's solution:** 36 ÷ 4 = 9. "I thought of a related fact: 4 x 9 = 36, so 36 ÷ 4 = 9."
write: Respond to Leo.
  lines: 3
  starter: I agree because ... I solved it a different way: ...
  key: I agree because 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 = 36. I solved it a different way: I shared 36 counters into 4 equal groups and got 9 in each group.
text: **Kim's solution:** 125 + 248 = 373. "Hundreds: 100 + 200 = 300. Tens: 20 + 40 = 60. Ones: 5 + 8 = 13. Then 300 + 60 + 13 = 373."
write: Respond to Kim.
  lines: 3
  starter: I agree because ... I solved it a different way: ...
  key: I agree because 373 - 248 = 125. I solved it a different way: I added 125 + 250 = 375, then took away 2 because 248 is 2 less than 250: 373.
choice: Which strategy did Leo use?
  - He drew a picture.
  - He counted up.
  * He used a related multiplication fact.
  - He used place value.

## Stretch: Check carefully
text: **Raj's solution:** 7 x 8 = 54. "I skip counted by 8 seven times: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 54."
write: Do you agree with Raj? Explain, then show your way.
  lines: 3
  starter: I do not agree because ... I solved it a different way: ...
  key: I do not agree because 48 + 8 is 56, not 54; Raj's last jump was 6. I solved it a different way: 7 x 8 = 7 x 4 + 7 x 4 = 28 + 28 = 56.

# 33. Yesterday's Verbs
minutes: 25
materials: pencil
ican: I can change sentences about a town from present tense to past tense, including irregular verbs.
directions: Read each sentence about the town of Maple Creek today. Write the verb in the past tense to tell what happened yesterday.
mistakes: Students often add -ed to irregular verbs ("goed", "builded", "selled"). Have them say the sentence aloud and listen. Watch the spelling of regular verbs too: carry becomes carried (y to i) and shop becomes shopped (double the p).
notes: Maple Creek is a made-up town. Items 1, 3, 4, 5, 8 and 10 use irregular verbs; 2, 6, 7 and 9 are regular. For speaking practice, read each "Today" sentence aloud and have the class answer with the "Yesterday" sentence.

## Warm up
example: **Today** the baker **opens** the shop. **Yesterday** the baker **opened** the shop. (regular: add -ed)
  **Today** the kids **go** to the park. **Yesterday** the kids **went** to the park. (irregular: a new word)
table: Verb now | Verb yesterday | Regular or irregular?
  - jump | {jumped} | {regular}
  - see | {saw} | {irregular}
  - stop | {stopped} | {regular}

## Main: Maple Creek yesterday
fill:
  - Today the town clock **rings** at noon. Yesterday the town clock {rang} at noon.
  - Today the baker **bakes** fresh bread. Yesterday the baker {baked} fresh bread.
  - Today the children **go** to school on the bus. Yesterday the children {went} to school on the bus.
  - Today workers **build** a new bridge. Yesterday workers {built} a new bridge.
  - Today Mrs. Diaz **sells** flowers at the market. Yesterday Mrs. Diaz {sold} flowers at the market.
  - Today the mail carrier **carries** letters to every house. Yesterday the mail carrier {carried} letters to every house.
  - Today families **shop** at the market. Yesterday families {shopped} at the market.
  - Today people **eat** lunch in the park. Yesterday people {ate} lunch in the park.
  - Today the firefighters **wash** their truck. Yesterday the firefighters {washed} their truck.
  - Today the mayor **gives** a speech. Yesterday the mayor {gave} a speech.

## Main: Sort the verbs
chart: Regular (add -ed) | Irregular (a new word)
  rows: 1
  height: 1.1
  key: Regular: baked, carried, shopped, washed. Irregular: rang, went, built, sold, ate, gave.

## Stretch: The town fair
write: Last summer, Maple Creek had a town fair. Write 2 sentences about it. Use one regular and one irregular past-tense verb.
  lines: 3
  starter: At the town fair, ...
  key: Example: At the town fair, a band played music in the park. Kids ate corn and rode a small train.
