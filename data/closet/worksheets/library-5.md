# 1. AND, OR, NOT Practice
minutes: 30
materials: pencil
ican: I can use AND, OR and NOT to make a search wider or narrower.
directions: Study the three Venn diagrams in the warm up. For each question, write a search using AND, OR or NOT. Then shade the part of its Venn diagram that your search finds. The first word goes in the left circle, the second word in the right circle.
mistakes: Students often use AND when they mean OR ("frogs AND toads" finds only pages about both). Remind them: AND narrows (fewer results, both words), OR widens (more results, either word), NOT removes. With NOT, the word after NOT is the one taken away, so students shade only the part of the left circle outside the overlap.
notes: Library databases and catalogs understand AND, OR and NOT (often typed in capitals). Many web search engines treat a space as AND and use a minus sign instead of NOT (bats -baseball); quotation marks keep a phrase together ("mountain lions"). Accept quotation marks around two-word terms. Shading key: AND shades only the middle; OR shades both whole circles; NOT shades the left circle without the middle.

## Warm up: What each word finds
picword:
  - venn-and | AND
  - venn-or | OR
  - venn-not | NOT
example: cats AND dogs: only pages about both (the middle).
  cats OR dogs: pages about either one or both (both whole circles).
  cats NOT dogs: pages about cats that do not mention dogs (the left circle without the middle).

## Main: Write it, then shade it
write: You want pages about pandas that also talk about bamboo.
  lines: 1
  key: pandas AND bamboo. Shade only the middle.
write: You want every page about frogs and every page about toads.
  lines: 1
  key: frogs OR toads. Shade both whole circles.
write: You want pages about bats, the animals, without pages about baseball.
  lines: 1
  key: bats NOT baseball. Shade the left circle (bats) without the middle.
write: You want pages about volcanoes in Hawaii.
  lines: 1

  lines: 1

  lines: 1

## Stretch: Most and fewest
choice: Which search finds the most pages?
  - cats AND dogs
  * cats OR dogs
  - cats NOT dogs
write: Explain why. Use the Venn diagrams.
  lines: 3
  key: OR shades both whole circles, so it includes every page about cats, every page about dogs and the pages about both; AND keeps only the middle and NOT keeps only part of one circle.

# 21. Report Planner With Citations
minutes: 45
materials: pencil, 3 sources on one topic (books or printed web pages), index cards or scrap paper (optional)
ican: I can plan a report from three sources and list them in a works cited list.
directions: Practice two works cited entries first. Then plan your own 3-paragraph report: write your question, record your 3 sources, take keyword notes, plan each paragraph, and write your works cited list.
mistakes: Students often write the URL alone as the citation, or put the first name first. In MLA style the author's last name comes first, then a comma, then the first name. A source with no author starts with its title in quotation marks. Works cited entries go in ABC order by their first word, not in the order students found them.
notes: Citations follow MLA style (9th edition). Book: Last, First. Title of Book. Publisher, Year. Web page: Last, First. "Title of Page." Name of Website, day Month year, URL. With no author, start with the title. MLA recommends leaving out https:// in URLs, abbreviates every month except May, June and July (Jan., Feb., Mar., Apr., Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov., Dec.), and alphabetizes by the first word, skipping A, An and The. Titles of whole works (books, websites) are in italics when typed; students underline them by hand, since this sheet cannot print italics. A typed list also gets a hanging indent (second and later lines indented). The books and websites in the practice are made up; the example.org addresses are reserved for examples and do not lead anywhere.

## Warm up: Build two entries
example: Book: Last name, First name. Title of Book. Publisher, Year.
  Web page: Last name, First name. "Title of Page." Name of Website, day Month year, address.
  No author? Start with the title. Underline the titles of books and websites when you write by hand (they are in italics when typed).
  Model: Kim, Sora. "How Sea Turtles Find Home." Ocean Kids Lab, 14 Apr. 2025, oceankidslab.example.org/turtles.
write: Book facts: author Jamal Rivers; title Volcanoes Up Close; publisher Stone Bridge Books; year 2022. Write the entry.
  lines: 2
  key: Rivers, Jamal. Volcanoes Up Close. Stone Bridge Books, 2022. (Volcanoes Up Close underlined or in italics.)
write: Web page facts: no author; page title How Lava Cools; website Earth Kids Lab; posted 5 May 2025; address earthkidslab.example.org/lava. Write the entry.
  lines: 2
  key: "How Lava Cools." Earth Kids Lab, 5 May 2025, earthkidslab.example.org/lava. (Earth Kids Lab underlined or in italics.)
choice: In a works cited list with these two entries, which comes first?
  * "How Lava Cools."
  - Rivers, Jamal.
