# 1. Title Page Treasure Hunt
minutes: 25
materials: pencil, a real picture book with a title page
ican: I can find the title, author, illustrator and publisher on a title page.
directions: (Read aloud.) A title page is inside the book, near the front. Read each title page with me. Find the title, the author, the illustrator and the publisher. Copy each one onto its line.
mistakes: Children often copy the author's name on the illustrator line. Point out the clue words: "by" or "written by" comes before the author, and "pictures by" or "illustrated by" comes before the illustrator. The publisher is usually small, at the bottom of the page.
notes: The three books are made up for this sheet, so children cannot look them up. Show a real title page first and find the four parts together. Long titles are fine to copy slowly; early writers can copy just the first word of the title and the last names. The stretch title page has an author who also drew the pictures.

## Warm up: The clue words
example: Title: the big words at the top.
  Author: the name after **by** or **written by**.
  Illustrator: the name after **pictures by** or **illustrated by**.
  Publisher: the company name at the bottom.

# 21. Answer From a Diagram
minutes: 20
materials: pencil, crayons
ican: I can use the labels on a diagram to answer a question.
directions: (Read aloud.) This diagram shows the parts of a tree. Each arrow points to a part, and its label names it. I will read each question. Point to the part on the diagram, then write its name on the line.
mistakes: Children may write "leaf" on the branch line because the branch is near the leaves: have them follow the arrow with a finger to its label. Some think roots are on top of the ground; point to the ground line in each picture.
notes: The four pictures show the same tree, each with one arrow and its label, so together they work like one labeled diagram. Science check: roots hold the tree in the ground and take in water; the trunk holds the tree up and carries water up to the leaves; leaves use sunlight to make food for the tree; branches grow out from the trunk and hold the leaves. Spelling help is on the diagram, so expect correct copying.

## Warm up: Read the diagram
text: (Read aloud.) Touch each arrow. Read its label with me: leaves, branch, trunk, roots.
picword:
  - tree-leaves | leaves
  - tree-branch | branch
  - tree-trunk | trunk
  - tree-roots | roots

## Main: Find the part
fill:
  - Which part grows under the ground? The {roots}
  - Which part makes food from sunlight? The {leaves}
  - Which part holds the tree up tall? The {trunk}
  - Which part grows out from the trunk and holds the leaves? A {branch}
  - Which part takes in water from the soil? The {roots}
