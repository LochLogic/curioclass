# 11. Odd and Even Products Color by Code
minutes: 30
materials: pencil, a yellow and a light blue crayon
ican: I can multiply and tell if a product is odd or even.
directions: Find each product. Then color each square of the picture: odd products yellow, even products light blue. Last, write the rule you notice.
mistakes: Students who color by the factors instead of the product (7 x 4 colored yellow because 7 is odd) need to look at the answer, not the problem. A wrong product shows up as a stray square in the picture, which makes a good self-check.
notes: The picture is a star. Squares are lettered; each letter's product decides the color, so a student can check their facts by whether the star looks right.

## Warm up: Odd or even?
fill:
  - 6 is {even}
  - 9 is {odd}
  - 14 is {even}
  - 21 is {odd}

## Main: Solve and color
colorcode: Find each product, then color the squares with that letter.
  code: y = yellow: odd | b = light blue: even
  problems: A 3 x 5, B 4 x 6, C 7 x 9, D 6 x 7, E 5 x 5, F 2 x 9, G 9 x 3, H 5 x 8, I 7 x 7, J 6 x 6, K 1 x 9, L 4 x 9, M 5 x 7, N 8 x 7, O 3 x 3, P 2 x 5, Q 9 x 9, R 6 x 5, S 7 x 3, T 8 x 3
  bbbbyybbbb
  bbbbyybbbb
  yyyyyyyyyy
  byyyyyyyyb
  bbyyyyyybb
  bbyyyyyybb
  byyybbyyyb
  byybbbbyyb
  bbbbbbbbbb

## Stretch: Write the rule
write: Look at the yellow problems. When is a product odd? When is it even?
  lines: 3
  starter: A product is odd when ...
  key: A product is odd only when both factors are odd (3 x 5). If either factor is even, the product is even (4 x 9, 6 x 7).
