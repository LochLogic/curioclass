# 1. Rational or Irrational?
minutes: 30
materials: pencil
ican: I can tell rational numbers from irrational numbers and write a repeating decimal as a fraction.
directions: Write R if the number is rational or I if it is irrational, and give a reason. Then change each repeating decimal into a fraction in simplest form, using the method in the example.
mistakes: Students often call every square root irrational: √16 = 4 and √(9/25) = 3/5 are rational because they are roots of perfect squares. Others call 1.414 irrational because it looks like √2; it is a terminating decimal, so it is rational. 0.1010010001... has a pattern but does not repeat a fixed block, so it is irrational.
notes: Reasons: 3/7 is a fraction of integers (R); √16 = 4 (R); √20 is between 4 and 5 and 20 is not a perfect square (I); π never terminates or repeats (I); 0.2727... repeats (R); -5 = -5/1 (R); 0.1010010001... never repeats a block (I); 2.5 = 5/2 (R); √(9/25) = 3/5 (R); √50 = 5√2 (I); -√49 = -7 (R); 1.414 = 1414/1000 terminates (R). For 0.1666..., only the 6 repeats: multiply by 10 and by 100 and subtract (100x - 10x = 15, so 90x = 15).

## Warm up
fill:
  - A rational number can be written as a {fraction} of two integers.
  - As a decimal, a rational number either terminates or {repeats}.
  - √36 = {6}, so √36 is rational.

## Main: Repeating decimals to fractions
example: x = 0.818181... Two digits repeat, so multiply by 100: 100x = 81.818181... Subtract: 100x - x = 81, so 99x = 81 and x = 81/99 = 9/11.

## Stretch: A famous puzzle
write: Use the same method on x = 0.999... What do you find? Do you believe it? Explain.
  lines: 4
  key: 10x = 9.999..., so 10x - x = 9, 9x = 9 and x = 1. So 0.999... equals 1 exactly; they are two names for the same number (there is no number between them).

# 21. Taxi Fare Models
minutes: 35
materials: pencil
ican: I can write a linear function y = mx + b for a situation and explain what the slope and the y-intercept mean.
directions: For each situation, find the rate of change m and the starting value b, write the equation y = mx + b, and explain what m and b mean in the situation with units.
mistakes: Students often swap m and b in situations told in words, especially when the starting value is mentioned second. In the candle and the tank, the slope is negative because the amount goes down; watch for answers with a positive slope. For the bike rental table, the starting value is not the first row (1 hour costs $14): work back one hour to get b = 8.
notes: Bike rental: slope = (20 - 14) / (2 - 1) = 6, and 14 - 6 = 8. Tank: slope = (290 - 350) / (5 - 2) = -20, then 350 = -20(2) + b gives b = 390; the tank is empty at x = 19.5 minutes. Stretch: 3.50 + 2.25x = 6 + 1.75x gives x = 5 miles.

## Warm up: Spot m and b
fill:
  - y = 4x + 7: m = {4}, b = {7}
  - y = 15 - 2x: m = {-2}, b = {15}

## Main: Model it
example: A taxi charges $3.50 to start the ride plus $2.25 for each mile. m = 2.25, b = 3.50, y = 2.25x + 3.50. The slope means the fare goes up $2.25 per mile; the intercept means the ride costs $3.50 before you go anywhere. An 8-mile ride costs 2.25(8) + 3.50 = $21.50.
fill: A rental scooter costs $1.00 to unlock plus $0.30 for each minute of riding. m = {0.30}, b = {1.00}, y = {0.30x + 1.00}
write: What do m and b mean for the scooter? How much does a 20-minute ride cost?
  lines: 2
  key: m = 0.30: the cost rises 30 cents per minute. b = 1.00: the $1 unlock fee before riding. 20 minutes: 0.30(20) + 1 = $7.00.
fill: A gym charges a $40 sign-up fee plus $25 per month. m = {25}, b = {40}, y = {25x + 40}
write: What do m and b mean for the gym?
  lines: 2
  key: m = 25: the total cost rises $25 for each month. b = 40: the $40 sign-up fee paid at month 0.
fill: A candle is 12 inches tall and burns down 0.5 inch each hour. m = {-0.5}, b = {12}, y = {-0.5x + 12}
write: What do m and b mean for the candle? When does it burn out?
  lines: 2
  key: m = -0.5: the height drops half an inch per hour. b = 12: the height in inches before it is lit. It burns out when y = 0, after 24 hours.
fill: Bike rental: 1 hour costs $14, 2 hours $20, 3 hours $26, 4 hours $32. m = {6}, b = {8}, y = {6x + 8}
write: What do m and b mean for the bike rental?
  lines: 2
  key: m = 6: each extra hour costs $6. b = 8: a fixed $8 fee charged no matter how long you ride.
fill: A water tank is draining at a steady rate. After 2 minutes it holds 350 gallons; after 5 minutes it holds 290 gallons. m = {-20}, b = {390}, y = {-20x + 390}
write: What do m and b mean for the tank? When will it be empty?
  lines: 2
  key: m = -20: the tank loses 20 gallons per minute. b = 390: it held 390 gallons when draining began. Empty when -20x + 390 = 0, at 19.5 minutes.

## Stretch: Two taxi companies
write: Taxi A charges $3.50 plus $2.25 per mile. Taxi B charges $6.00 plus $1.75 per mile. Write both equations. For what trip length do they cost the same? Which is cheaper for a 12-mile trip?
  lines: 4
  key: A: y = 2.25x + 3.50. B: y = 1.75x + 6. Equal when 0.5x = 2.5, at 5 miles ($14.75 each). For 12 miles, A costs $30.50 and B costs $27.00, so B is cheaper; B is cheaper for any trip longer than 5 miles.
