title: Time Traveler Press Conference
grade: 12
order: 19
kind: Pretend
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 2+
materials: paper and pens, a table and chair at the front, a "microphone" (a hairbrush or a spoon), a few props, at least one partner to be the press (sibling, friend, or the family after dinner)
nice: a costume that looks slightly "off," a cardboard name tag, index cards for reporters

## The big idea
You have just arrived from the year 2126. You are holding a press conference, and the reporters (your family) can ask
anything: What happened to cars? Do people still go to school? What are the biggest regrets of the 2020s? You must
answer in character, with a future so detailed it feels real.

## Goals
- A future world built with at least ten specific, believable details.
- A live press conference where you answer surprise questions in character.

## Objectives
- I can build a consistent imagined future from trends I know today.
- I can improvise answers that stay true to my world.
- I can perform a character with a clear voice and attitude.

## Get ready
Set up the "podium" with a table and microphone. Give the reporters index cards to write questions on.

## Steps
1. Build your future (20 minutes). Answer these in notes: How do people get around? What do they eat? What is school
   like? What happened to the climate? What is the most popular job? What do people laugh at?
2. Create your character: a name, a job, and a reason you traveled back.
3. Prepare an opening statement of 60 seconds: who you are and why you came.
4. Plan for curveballs: two things you will refuse to answer ("I cannot tell you about the election of 2048").
5. Hold the press conference (20 minutes). Reporters ask, you answer. Stay in character, even when it gets silly.
6. End with a warning or a gift for the present.

## Make it yours
- Arrive from the distant past instead, confused by everything in the room.
- Arrive from an alternate present where one historical event went the other way.
- Have two time travelers from different futures who disagree with each other.

## Stuck?
- You cannot answer a question: say "That is classified under the Temporal Accords," then add one clue.
- The world feels thin: pick one detail and go deep: what is breakfast like in 2126?

## Show it off
Hold the press conference after dinner, and let the "press" write a headline about it.

## Clean up
Return props and keep your future notes for a sequel.

## For the grown-up
This builds improvisation, worldbuilding, public speaking and critical thinking about trends. **Ask at dinner:** "What
is the best thing about your future?"
