nice: fabric markers or fabric paint, a second shirt for a second bag

## The big idea
An old shirt is one of the easiest things in the world to turn into something useful. With just scissors and knots, no sewing, you'll make a strong tote bag. Then make it your own design.

## Goals
- A finished no-sew tote bag that can carry at least five books.
- A design on the bag that's yours.

## Objectives
- I can measure and mark cuts before making them.
- I can tie strong double knots to join fabric.
- I can test a design and make it stronger.

## Get ready
Get permission for the shirt. Lay it flat on a clean table or floor, smoothed out. Have your scissors ready.

## Steps
1. **Cut off the sleeves.** Cut just inside the seams, leaving the shoulder parts as the bag's handles.
2. **Cut the neck.** Trace a bowl or plate around the collar to draw a big U, about 10 to 15 cm deep. Cut through both layers. This is the bag's opening.
3. **Mark the fringe.** Turn the shirt inside out. Draw a line across the bottom, about 8 cm up from the hem.
4. **Cut the fringe (10 minutes).** Cut strips from the bottom up to the line, about 2 cm wide, through both layers. Keep the strips even.
5. **Tie it shut.** Tie each front strip to the back strip right behind it with a double knot. Pull tight.
6. **Close the gaps.** There will be small holes between knots. Tie each strip pair to the pair next to it to close them up.
7. **Turn it right side out.** Your fringe is hidden inside. Test it: load five books and carry them around the house.
8. **Make it yours.** Use fabric markers or paint to add a design: a logo, a pattern, a slogan, or a drawing. Plan it on paper first.

## Make it yours
- Braid the fringe instead of knotting for a more decorated bottom.
- Make a mini bag from a kid's shirt for snacks, or a big beach bag from an adult's extra-large shirt.
- Design a brand: name your bag line and make a paper tag for each one.

## Stuck?
- The bottom has holes: tie the strip pairs to their neighbors, and double knot everything.
- The handles are stretching too much: cut them a little wider next time, or make the shirt a second layer by doubling up.
- The cuts are wobbly: draw the lines with a ruler first and cut slowly.

## Show it off
Use it for its first real job: pack it for a trip to the library or the store.

## Clean up
Save the sleeves and scraps for headbands, cleaning rags, or stuffing, and sweep up the threads.

## For the grown-up
This builds measuring, fine-motor skills and some sustainable thinking (reuse instead of throw away). Sharp scissors are fine at this age; just check the shirt is okay to cut. **Ask at dinner:** "How did you make the bottom strong enough to hold books?"
