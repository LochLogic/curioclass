title: Family Lemonade Stand
grade: 3
order: 20
kind: Kitchen
time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: 3 lemons or bottled lemon juice, sugar, water, a pitcher, a big spoon, cups, a measuring cup, paper, markers, tape, a small table
nice: ice, mint leaves, berries, a juicer

## The big idea
Open for business! Make your own lemonade, build a stand, and sell to your family with pretend money. Will your recipe be the best in town?

## Goals
- You make a pitcher of lemonade from your own recipe.
- You build a stand with a sign and a price list.

## Objectives
- I can measure ingredients with cups and spoons.
- I can taste and change a recipe to make it better.
- I can count change in pretend money.

## Get ready
Wash your hands. A grown-up cuts the lemons in half. Clear a counter. Set out the pitcher, cups and measuring cup.

## Steps
1. Squeeze the lemons into a cup. Catch the seeds with a spoon. You need about half a cup of juice.
2. Mix the base: 4 cups of cold water, half a cup of lemon juice, and 4 big spoons of sugar.
3. Stir until the sugar is gone. Taste it. Too sour? Add 1 spoon of sugar. Too sweet? Add water.
4. Write down your final recipe. Give it a name, like "Sunny Squeeze."
5. Make a second flavor. Try mashing berries in, or adding mint.
6. Build the stand (15 minutes). Use a small table. Make a big sign with the name and prices.
7. Make pretend money. Cut 10 paper coins worth 1 and 5 paper bills worth 5.
8. Open the stand! Pour, serve and give change.

## Make it yours
- Make a "taste test" with 2 cups. Which do customers like better?
- Invent a mascot, like a lemon with sunglasses.
- Make it a fancy cafe with napkins and a "special of the day."

## Stuck?
- The juice is too sour: add sugar 1 spoon at a time and stir well.
- No lemons: use orange juice and water, and call it a "Sun Stand."
- Spills: pour over a tray and fill cups only halfway.

## Show it off
Open the stand when your family gets home. Give the first cup free!

## Clean up
Wash the pitcher and spoons. Wipe sticky spots. Put sugar away.

## For the grown-up
This builds measuring, adjusting a recipe and counting money. Please cut the lemons. The stand is for family and invited friends at home. If your child wants to sell to the public, go with them and stay with them. Check allergies before adding berries or other fruit. **Ask at dinner:** "What did you change to make your recipe better?"
