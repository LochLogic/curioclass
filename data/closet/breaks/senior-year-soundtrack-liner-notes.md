title: Senior Year Soundtrack Liner Notes
grade: 12
order: 7
kind: Music
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 1
materials: paper, pens, a ruler, scissors, glue, a piece of cardboard about 12 cm by 12 cm
nice: a phone or radio to listen to songs, old photos, colored pens

## The big idea
Albums once came with **liner notes**: a little booklet with the songs, the story behind them and the people who made
them. You are going to make the liner notes for the soundtrack of your senior year. Pick twelve songs, write why each
belongs, and design the case and booklet like a real album release.

## Goals
- A 12-track list in a deliberate order, with a short note for each song.
- A handmade album case and fold-out booklet.

## Objectives
- I can choose and order songs to create an emotional arc.
- I can write short, vivid notes that connect music to memory.
- I can design an album cover and layout.

## Get ready
Brainstorm 20 songs that meant something this year: songs from car rides, parties, sad days, studying. Do not judge
yet.

## Steps
1. Cut it to 12 (10 minutes). Keep the songs tied to a moment, not just your favorites.
2. Order them. Albums have arcs: open strong, dip into a quiet middle, build to a big finish. Example arc: September
   nerves, winter grind, spring freedom.
3. Write a liner note for each (25 minutes): 40 to 80 words. Start with a moment, not an opinion. Example: "Track 4.
   The bus broke down outside town and someone played this on a phone speaker. Twenty people sang along."
4. Name the album, like "Last Year Before Everything."
5. Design the cover on the cardboard square. Use one strong image: a drawing, a collage, a photo, or bold type.
6. Make the booklet: fold paper accordion-style to fit the case, and write the notes inside.
7. Add credits: thank the people who were part of the year.

## Make it yours
- Make a "director's cut" with three hidden tracks and secret notes.
- Make it for a friend's senior year instead, and give it to them.
- Make it for a fictional character, a band or a movie that does not exist.

## Stuck?
- Too many songs: pick one per month of the school year.
- Notes feel generic: write what you saw, heard and smelled in that moment, not what the song is "about."

## Show it off
Play a few tracks at dinner while you read the liner notes aloud.

## Clean up
Recycle scraps and keep the case somewhere safe.

## For the grown-up
This builds writing, design and curation, and helps your teen make sense of a big year. **Ask at dinner:** "Which
song is the most 'this year'?"
