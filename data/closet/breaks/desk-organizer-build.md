where: Indoors
mess: Medium

materials: sturdy cardboard boxes, a ruler, a pencil, scissors or a craft knife, a cutting mat or thick cardboard underneath, glue or tape, cardboard tubes, paper or fabric scraps for covering
nice: a glue gun, paint, wrapping paper, a metal ruler

## The big idea
Designers do not start with a box; they start with a problem. Look at your desk: what do you lose, what tips over,
what piles up? You will measure your stuff, design a modular organizer around it, and build it from cardboard so well
it looks bought.

## Goals
- A finished desk organizer with at least four compartments designed for your actual stuff.
- A measured sketch with dimensions.

## Objectives
- I can design to fit real objects using measurements.
- I can cut and join cardboard cleanly and strongly.
- I can improve a design after testing it.

## Get ready
Clear your desk. Make a list of everything that needs a home: pens, chargers, notebooks, headphones, keys, snacks.

## Steps
1. Measure (10 minutes). Measure the tallest pen, the widest notebook, your headphones. Write each measurement.
2. Sketch the design with dimensions. Plan compartments for each item, with a little extra room (5 mm).
3. Cut the pieces (30 minutes). Score fold lines with the back of the knife or scissors so folds are crisp. Cut
   straight lines with a metal ruler.
4. Assemble with glue or tape. Reinforce corners from the inside with small cardboard triangles.
5. Add a feature: a phone stand at 60 degrees, a hidden drawer, a cable slot, or a notch for headphones.
6. Cover it with paper or fabric, folding neatly around edges.
7. Test it with your stuff. Fix anything that wobbles or does not fit.

## Make it yours
- Make it modular: three separate pieces that can be rearranged.
- Make it look like a building, a ship or a city skyline.
- Add a secret compartment for something small and important.

## Stuck?
- Corners are flimsy: double the cardboard or glue a strip along each inside corner.
- Cuts are ragged: replace the knife blade or use more shallow passes instead of one deep one.

## Show it off
Give a "before and after" desk tour to the family.

## Clean up
Sweep up cardboard scraps, put the knife away with the blade covered, and recycle leftovers.

## For the grown-up
This builds design, measurement and fabrication skills. Craft knives should be used with a cutting mat, cutting away
from the body, with permission. **Ask at dinner:** "What feature did you design that you're proudest of?"
