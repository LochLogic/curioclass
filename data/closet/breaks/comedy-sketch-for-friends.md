title: Comedy Sketch for Friends
grade: 12
order: 8
kind: Pretend
time: 75-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 2+
materials: paper and pens, simple props from around the house, a space to perform, at least one partner (a friend over, a sibling, or a grown-up after work)
nice: hats, wigs, glasses and costumes, a timer

## The big idea
Sketch comedy runs on one rule: find the **game** of the scene, the one funny pattern, and heighten it until it
explodes. A waiter who keeps bringing the wrong food is a premise. A waiter who brings things that get more and more
impossible (a live goose, a canoe) is a game. Write and perform a three-minute sketch with friends.

## Goals
- A written sketch of about three minutes with a clear game.
- A live performance with at least one partner.

## Objectives
- I can find and heighten the game of a comedy scene.
- I can write a sketch with a setup, escalation and a button ending.
- I can perform with timing and commitment.

## Get ready
Gather props and clear a "stage." Agree on how many people are performing.

## Steps
1. Brainstorm premises (10 minutes). Normal place plus one weird thing: a job interview where the interviewer is a
   toddler, a support group for retired villains, a tour guide who has never been outside.
2. Find the game: what is the funny pattern? Write it in one sentence.
3. Write the setup (first 30 seconds): who, where, what is normal.
4. Heighten three times. Each beat bigger than the last.
5. Write a **button**: a final line or action that ends with a laugh. Example: the toddler interviewer offers the
   job, then bursts into tears because it is naptime.
6. Rehearse twice for lines, then twice with full commitment. Play it straight; characters do not know they are funny.

## Make it yours
- Make it a parody commercial for a ridiculous product.
- Do it as a silent sketch with only actions.
- Write a series of three short sketches that connect at the end.

## Stuck?
- It is not funny: you probably have a premise, not a game. Find the pattern and repeat it bigger.
- The ending fizzles: end on the biggest heightening or a surprise twist.

## Show it off
Perform it for family or friends as "a special presentation," with a host introducing the sketch.

## Clean up
Return costumes and props, and keep the script.

## For the grown-up
This builds writing, timing, collaboration and stage confidence. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the game of your sketch?"
