title: Family Talent Show Producer
grade: 3
order: 28
kind: Pretend
time: 60-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: paper, markers, tape, a blanket or sheet for a curtain, chairs, a flashlight for a spotlight
nice: a hairbrush for a pretend microphone, dress-up clothes, a timer

## The big idea
Lights! Curtain! You are the producer. You run the whole show: the acts, the stage, the tickets and the host. Plan a talent show for tonight, starring you, the family and maybe some stuffed animals.

## Goals
- You plan and run a show with at least 4 acts.
- You make a stage, tickets and a program.

## Objectives
- I can plan an event step by step.
- I can make a list and put things in an order.
- I can speak clearly as a host.

## Get ready
Pick a room. Hang a blanket over a doorway or between two chairs for a curtain. Line up chairs for the audience.

## Steps
1. Plan the acts (15 minutes). Write a list. You could do a dance, a joke, a magic trick, or a song. Stuffed animals can "sing" too.
2. Pick your own act. Practice it 3 times.
3. Make the program. Fold paper in half. Write the show name, and list the acts in order.
4. Make tickets. Cut paper strips. Write "ADMIT ONE" on each.
5. Write a host script. "Welcome! Our first act is the amazing Mr. Bear!" Write one fun line for each act.
6. Plan the lights. Choose who flicks the flashlight for each act.
7. Do a full practice run. Time it. 15 to 20 minutes is perfect.
8. After dinner: hand out tickets and start the show!

## Make it yours
- Add judges who hold up paper scores from 1 to 10.
- Make a "commercial break" in the middle with a funny fake ad.
- Make it a theme show, like "The Great Animal Star Show."

## Stuck?
- No one else is home yet: plan acts for the family to do later, and practice yours.
- You get nervous: practice in a mirror, then in front of one stuffed animal.
- The show runs long: cut one act or make each one 2 minutes.

## Show it off
Run the show tonight. Everyone gets a turn, even the shy ones with one quick wave.

## Clean up
Fold the curtain. Put the chairs back. Keep the program as a souvenir.

## For the grown-up
This builds planning, writing, speaking and leadership. Please play along and do an act, even a small one. Keep your child's planned acts gentle for anyone shy. **Ask at dinner:** "What is the hardest part of being a producer?"
