title: Nature Collage
grade: k
order: 20
kind: Outside
time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Medium
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: a paper bag or basket, a sheet of cardboard (from a box) or a paper plate, school glue or tape, crayons, a pencil
nice: clear contact paper, a magnifying glass, a hole punch and string for hanging

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Outside is full of treasures: leaves, petals, seeds, sticks and feathers. Let's collect some and use them to make a picture. Your picture can be a face, an animal, or a whole tiny world!"

## Goals
- Your child collects at least 10 different natural things.
- Your child makes a collage picture from them.

## Objectives
- Your child can sort what they find by color, size or kind.
- Your child can describe textures: bumpy, smooth, soft, rough.
- Your child can arrange pieces to make a picture they planned.

## Get ready
Pick a spot: a yard, a park, a sidewalk with trees, or a balcony with plants. Talk about the rule: pick things from the ground, not from living plants (or ask first). Bring the bag.

## Steps
1. **The collecting walk** (20 minutes). Your child gathers leaves, small sticks, petals, grass, seeds, pinecones, feathers you've checked are clean. Ask: "Which one is the softest? The bumpiest?"
2. At home, spread everything out. **Sort it**: by color, by size, or by kind. Your child picks how.
3. **Plan the picture.** Ask: "What do you want to make?" Ideas: a face with leaf hair, a lion with a petal mane, a house in a forest, a fish with leaf scales.
4. **Lay it out first** (15 minutes). Your child arranges pieces on the cardboard without glue. Move things around until it looks right.
5. **Glue it down** (15 minutes). Lift one piece at a time, add a dot of glue, and press. Use tape for thick sticks.
6. Your child adds drawn details with crayon: eyes, a sun, a ground line.
7. Give the collage a title. Write it at the bottom.

## Make it yours
- Make a nature crown: tape the leaves and flowers to a strip of paper and wear it.
- Make it a "color hunt" collage where everything is one color, like yellow or green.
- Make a creature that does not exist and give it a name, like a "Stick-a-saurus."

## Stuck?
- Things won't stick: press glued pieces under a heavy book for 10 minutes, or use tape loops on the back.
- No outdoor space: collect from houseplant trimmings, kitchen scraps (onion skin, dry beans, pasta) and a walk to a nearby tree.

## Show it off
Hang it on the fridge or a door. Your child gives a "museum tour" telling where each piece came from.

## Clean up
Shake leftover bits outside. Your child washes hands and wipes the glue off the table.

## For the grown-up
This builds observation, sorting (an early math skill), texture words and planning a picture. Check what your child picks up: no mushrooms, berries, droppings, or anything that might be from a plant you don't know. Wash hands after. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the most interesting thing you found outside?"
