title: Paper Clip Fishing Game
grade: 1
order: 14
kind: Game
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: paper or thin cardboard, crayons or markers, scissors (child can use safety scissors), metal paper clips, a magnet (a fridge magnet works), string, a wooden spoon or a stick, tape, a blue towel or a laundry basket for the pond
nice: a stronger magnet, a timer

## The big idea
Read aloud: "The fishing pond is full of strange fish today: rainbow fish, spotty fish, even a shark wearing sunglasses! Draw your fish, make your fishing pole, and see how many you can catch."

## Goals
- Your child draws and cuts out at least 10 fish and builds a magnet fishing pole.
- Your child plays a fishing game with points and invents a new rule.

## Objectives
- Your child can tell that magnets pull on some metal things.
- Your child can add up points (each fish has a number).
- Your child can follow and invent game rules.

## Get ready
Tape the magnet to one end of a string about 2 feet long. Tie or tape the other end to the spoon handle. Lay the blue towel on the floor as the pond.

## Steps
1. **Magnet test** (5 minutes). Your child touches the magnet to things around the house: a spoon, a key, a crayon, a paper clip, the fridge. Ask: "What sticks? What doesn't?"
2. **Draw the fish** (20 minutes). Your child draws 10 fish, each about the size of a hand. They can be any color and any shape: a striped fish, a fish with legs, a pizza fish.
3. **Cut them out.** Your child cuts with safety scissors (you help with tricky fins).
4. **Points.** Your child writes a number 1 to 5 on the back of each fish. The weird ones are worth more!
5. Slide a paper clip onto the nose of each fish. Spread them face up in the pond.
6. **Go fishing** (15 minutes). Take turns. Each person gets 5 casts. Lower the magnet, catch a fish, and add up the points on the back.
7. **New rules.** Your child invents one: "If you catch the shark, you lose 2 points," or "You must fish with your eyes closed."

## Make it yours
- Write letters on the fish and catch them to spell words like CAT and SUN.
- Make a deep-sea pond with an octopus, a whale and a treasure chest worth 10 points.
- Make a story: the fish are escaping from a pirate, and you rescue them to a new pond.

## Stuck?
- The magnet won't pick up the fish: use a bigger paper clip, or two, or a stronger magnet.
- Your child is frustrated: make the string shorter so it's easier to control.

## Show it off
Hold a fishing tournament with the family after dinner. Your child is the judge.

## Clean up
Store fish in a zip bag with the pole. Count the paper clips to make sure none are on the floor.

## For the grown-up
This builds magnet science (what is magnetic), addition practice, cutting and game rules. Small magnets and paper clips are dangerous if swallowed, so keep them away from babies and toddlers and count them at cleanup. **Ask at dinner:** "What things in our house stick to a magnet?"
