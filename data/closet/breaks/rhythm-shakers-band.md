title: Rhythm Shakers Band
grade: k
order: 25
kind: Music
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: 3 or 4 clean empty containers with lids (plastic bottles, yogurt cups, paper towel tubes), dry rice, dry beans, dry pasta, strong tape, paper, crayons, a funnel or rolled paper
nice: colored tape, ribbon, stickers

## The big idea
Read aloud: "A band is coming to town, but they forgot their instruments! Let's make shakers that sound different: one like rain, one like a rattlesnake, one like a train. Then you can be the band leader!"

## Goals
- Your child makes 3 shakers that make 3 different sounds.
- Your child leads a band and plays along with songs.

## Objectives
- Your child can compare sounds: loud and soft, high and low, fast and slow.
- Your child can keep a steady beat with a shaker.
- Your child can predict how a filling will sound.

## Get ready
Wash and dry the containers. Put rice, beans and pasta in three bowls. Set a tray down to catch spills. You will do the filling and taping, since dry beans and rice are small parts.

## Steps
1. **Sound guessing.** Your child shakes each bowl gently with a spoon or touches each filling. Ask: "Which one will be loud? Which will be quiet?"
2. **Fill the shakers** (15 minutes). Your child chooses a filling for each container and how much: a little or a lot. You pour with a funnel and tape the lid on tight, all the way around.
3. Shake and compare. Ask: "Does rice sound like rain? Do beans sound like a storm?" Your child names each shaker ("Rain Maker," "Thunder Shake").
4. **Decorate** (15 minutes). Your child wraps the shakers in paper and draws on them. Tape the paper down well.
5. **Find the beat.** Sing a song you both know. Your child shakes on every beat. Then try shaking fast, slow, loud, soft.
6. **The band** (15 minutes). Line up stuffed animals as band members. Your child gives each one a shaker part. Your child is the leader: when they point, you shake.
7. Make up a "shaker song": your child picks the order, like rain, rain, thunder, rain.

## Make it yours
- Make a mystery shaker: you secretly fill one with something new (salt, a few dry noodles, a crumpled foil ball), tape it shut, and the family guesses what is inside.
- Play a "copy me" game: your child shakes a pattern and you copy it, then switch.
- Tell a story with shakers: rain starts, a train comes, a snake slithers by.

## Stuck?
- The lid pops off: use more tape, wrapping it around the whole lid twice.
- The sound is too quiet: add more filling, or try a harder container.

## Show it off
Give a band concert for the family. Hand them shakers so they can join in.

## Clean up
Sweep up spilled rice and beans right away. Your child puts the shakers in a "band box."

## For the grown-up
This builds steady beat, listening for differences in sound (an early science and music skill) and fine motor control. Dry rice and beans are a choking hazard for young children, so do all the filling yourself, tape lids very securely, and check the shakers before each play. Keep them away from babies and toddlers. **Ask at dinner:** "Which shaker sounds the most like rain?"
