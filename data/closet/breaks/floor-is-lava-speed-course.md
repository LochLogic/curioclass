time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: couch cushions, pillows, folded towels, a few sturdy cardboard pieces or bath mats, paper and a pencil, a clock or kitchen timer
nice: sidewalk chalk and hula hoops for an outdoor course, a stopwatch, a sibling or friend to race

## The big idea
The floor is lava. It has always been lava. But this time, you're not just jumping on the couch: you're designing a timed course with rules, levels and a record board, the way people plan real obstacle races.

## Goals
- A course with at least 10 "safe islands" and three difficulty levels.
- A record board with your best times on each level.

## Objectives
- I can design a course that is challenging and safe.
- I can improve my time with practice and better strategy.
- I can write fair rules that everyone can follow.

## Get ready
Get permission for the room and the cushions. Move anything breakable and pad sharp table corners with a folded towel. Check the floor: cushions slide on wood and tile, so build on a rug or carpet, or tape the cushions down.

## Steps
1. **Map it.** Draw the room from above and mark where each island goes. Islands should be about one big step apart, not a jump-for-your-life gap.
2. **Write the rules.** Example: touch the floor, add 5 seconds. Fall in twice, restart. Must touch the "checkpoint" pillow with your hand.
3. **Build level 1 (15 minutes).** Lay out at least 10 islands. Walk it slowly first to check it's safe.
4. **Time it.** Run it three times and write down each time. Notice where you lose seconds.
5. **Level 2.** Add a challenge: carry a cup of water (half full) without spilling, or crawl under a "laser" made of string tied between chair legs at knee height.
6. **Level 3.** Add a rule twist: some islands "sink" after you step on them once (flip them over), or you must go backward through one section.
7. **Record board.** Make a poster for each level with times. Try to beat your own record by 10 seconds.

## Make it yours
- Story mode: you're escaping a volcano island, and each checkpoint is a part of the story.
- Make it a puzzle: hide clue cards on islands that tell you where the next checkpoint is.
- Take it outside: chalk circles on the sidewalk, sticks as balance beams, a frisbee as the finish.

## Stuck?
- Cushions slip: put them on a rug or skip the slipperiest ones.
- It's too easy: shrink the islands with smaller towels, or add a time limit.
- It's too hard and you keep falling: move islands closer. The goal is fast and fun, not crashing.

## Show it off
Hold an "official time trial" when your family is home and let them try to beat your record.

## Clean up
Put every cushion back where it came from (the right way up), fold the towels, and untie any string.

## For the grown-up
This is exercise plus design: planning a course, testing it for safety, and tracking times to see improvement. Please do a quick safety look before they start: no standing on furniture that can tip, and no cushions on slick floors. **Ask at dinner:** "What was your best time, and how did you shave seconds off?"
