title: Fingerprint Detective
grade: 2
order: 20
kind: Puzzle
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: With you
players: 2+
materials: a soft pencil, clear tape, white paper, a magnifying glass if you have one, a few glasses or jars, 2 or 3 people for fingerprints (you, a sibling, or a grown-up after work)
nice: cocoa powder and a soft paintbrush for dusting, an ink pad, a detective hat

## The big idea
Read aloud: "A cookie has gone missing from the kitchen, and there's a fingerprint on the jar! You are the detective. Every person has fingerprints that are different from anyone else's. Can you solve the case?"

## Goals
- A fingerprint card for each person at home.
- The mystery solved by matching a print.

## Objectives
- Your child can make a clear fingerprint with pencil and tape.
- Your child can compare patterns and name them: loop, whorl and arch.
- Your child can explain how they solved the mystery.

## Get ready
Secretly press your finger on a clean glass so it leaves a print. Set it near a "missing cookie" plate. Clear a table and lay out paper, tape and the pencil.

## Steps
1. **Make a pencil pad.** Your child scribbles hard on a scrap of paper until there's a dark, shiny patch.
2. **Take a print.** Rub a fingertip on the patch, then press a piece of clear tape on the fingertip. Peel it off and stick it on white paper. There's a print!
3. **Make fingerprint cards** (15 minutes). Take one print from each person. Your child labels each with a name.
4. **Learn the patterns.** Look closely with a magnifying glass. Is it a **loop** (lines come in and go back), a **whorl** (circles like a snail), or an **arch** (lines go up like a hill)? Your child writes the pattern under each card.
5. **Dust for prints.** Gently brush cocoa powder or rub pencil dust on the glass, then lift the print with tape and stick it on paper.
6. **Solve the case.** Your child compares the mystery print to each card. Ask: "Which one matches? How can you tell?"
7. **Write a detective report.** "Case: The Missing Cookie. Who did it: ___. How I know: ___."

## Make it yours
- Make fingerprint art: turn prints into animals, bugs or faces with a pen.
- Set up a new mystery for someone at home to solve.
- Take toe prints and see if they have patterns too.

## Stuck?
- Prints are blurry: press the tape firmly and peel it slowly.
- Patterns are hard to tell apart: draw a big example of each one on a card to compare.

## Show it off
Announce the solved case at dinner and show the detective report and the matching prints.

## Clean up
Wash hands with soap, throw away used tape, and wipe the table.

## For the grown-up
This builds careful observation, comparing and contrasting, and writing a conclusion. Detectives and scientists both use evidence, and this is a fun first taste of it. Keep powder away from eyes and noses. **Ask at dinner:** "How did you know whose fingerprint was on the glass?"
