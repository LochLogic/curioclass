title: Mirror Paint Butterflies
grade: 1
order: 23
kind: Art
time: 45-75 min
where: Indoors
mess: High
grownup: With you
players: 1
materials: paper (printer paper or thicker), washable paint in 3 or 4 colors (or watercolors made thick), a spoon or paintbrush, newspaper, a pencil, scissors (child can use safety scissors), paper towels, an old shirt
nice: a pipe cleaner for antennae, glitter, string to hang them

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Butterflies have a secret: one side of their wings is a mirror of the other side! Today you'll make magic mirror butterflies. You paint just one side, fold it, and when you open it... ta-da!"

## Goals
- Your child makes at least 3 mirror butterflies with matching wings.
- Your child hangs them as a butterfly garden.

## Objectives
- Your child can explain symmetry: both sides match like a mirror.
- Your child can predict what the other side will look like.
- Your child can choose and mix colors on purpose.

## Get ready
Put on the old shirt. Cover the table with newspaper. Fold each sheet of paper in half and open it again so it has a crease. Put a spoonful of each paint color on a plate.

## Steps
1. **Mirror check.** Look in a mirror together. Ask: "Is your face the same on both sides?" Show a real butterfly picture or draw one. The wings match!
2. **Paint one side** (10 minutes). Your child drops blobs of paint on just one side of the crease, near the fold. Use 2 or 3 colors.
3. **Guess.** Ask: "What will happen when we fold it?"
4. **Fold and press.** Fold the paper closed. Your child smooths it from the fold out with their hand, pressing hard.
5. **Open it!** Slowly unfold. Both sides match! Ask: "What does it look like?" Some look like butterflies, others like monsters or flowers.
6. **Make more** (20 minutes). Try different ideas: only dots, only lines, lots of paint, a little paint. Which looks most like a butterfly? Let them dry.
7. **Cut out the butterflies.** Draw a butterfly shape around each painting with pencil. Your child cuts it out with safety scissors. Add pipe cleaner or paper antennae.

## Make it yours
- Make mirror monsters instead, then add eyes and teeth.
- Fold the paper in quarters for a four-wing "super butterfly."
- Make a butterfly for each person in the family in their favorite colors.

## Stuck?
- Paint is too dry to print: use more paint, and fold right away.
- Everything turns brown: use only 2 colors that mix nicely (like blue and yellow) on each one.

## Show it off
Hang the butterflies in a window or on string across a doorway for a "butterfly garden" the family walks through.

## Clean up
Fold up the newspaper and throw it away. Your child washes the brush and wipes the table.

## For the grown-up
This builds symmetry (a first-grade math idea), color mixing and prediction. It's messy and very satisfying. Use washable paint and old clothes. **Ask at dinner:** "What is symmetry? Can you find something at this table that's symmetrical?"
