title: Secret Life of a Lost Sock
grade: 4
order: 3
kind: Story
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 1
materials: paper, a pencil, markers, a stapler or tape, one sock without a partner
nice: a sock puppet you make while you write, a timer

## The big idea
Socks go into the wash in pairs. They come out alone. Where do the lost ones go? Write the story from the sock's point of view. That means the sock tells it as "I." Maybe it ran away to join a circus!

## Goals
- You write a story told by a lost sock, at least 1 page long.
- You make it into a small book with pictures.

## Objectives
- I can write from a character's point of view.
- I can use details to make a made-up world feel real.
- I can build a story with a problem and an ending.

## Get ready
Find a lonely sock. Set it on the table where you can see it. Fold 2 sheets of paper in half for a small book.

## Steps
1. Name your sock. Give it a personality. Is it brave, grumpy, or dreamy?
2. Brainstorm (10 minutes). Where did it go? Under the dryer? A secret Sock City? Out the window on a bird?
3. Pick one idea. Write 3 things that happen there.
4. Write the beginning. Start with "I." Example: "I was spinning in the dryer when a crack opened up."
5. Write the middle. Add a problem. Maybe the sock wants to get home but there is a guard.
6. Write the ending. Does it go home? Does it stay? Does it find its missing partner?
7. Add sock words. Use words like stretchy, stinky, fuzzy and toe-hole.
8. Draw pictures for each page. Make a cover with a title.

## Make it yours
- Write the same story from the washing machine's point of view.
- Make the sock world a whole country with its own king.
- Write a "missing sock" poster as the last page.

## Stuck?
- No idea where it went: list 5 wild places, then pick the funniest.
- The story is short: add what the sock sees, hears and smells.
- You keep writing "he" or "she": go back and change it to "I."

## Show it off
Read your story to the family using the sock as a puppet.

## Clean up
Put the sock back in the drawer. Maybe its partner will show up!

## For the grown-up
This builds point of view, a key reading and writing skill in grade 4, plus describing words and story structure. **Ask at dinner:** "Where did your sock go, and would you want to visit?"
