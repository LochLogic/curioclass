title: Tape Road City
grade: k
order: 15
kind: Build
time: 60-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: painter's tape or masking tape, toy cars, small boxes and cereal boxes, paper, crayons, scissors (grown-up only), blocks, small toy people or animals
nice: a sheet of cardboard for a parking lot, paper cups for traffic cones

## The big idea
Read aloud: "The toy cars need a city! There are no roads, no houses, no school and no gas station yet. You are the city builder. Where should the roads go?"

## Goals
- Your child builds a tape road city across the floor with at least 5 places.
- Your child drives cars around and tells where each one is going.

## Objectives
- Your child can plan where roads go and connect places.
- Your child can use direction words: left, right, straight, around, under.
- Your child can make simple signs and say what they mean.

## Get ready
Pick a floor area at least 4 feet by 4 feet. Test the tape on a corner first to make sure it comes up cleanly. Gather the boxes and blocks.

## Steps
1. Ask: "What does a city need?" Make a list together: houses, a school, a store, a park, a fire station, a pet shop.
2. **Lay the roads** (15 minutes). Your child picks where the first road goes. You tear the tape, they press it down. Make long straight roads, a curvy road and a round-and-round circle.
3. **Build the places** (25 minutes). Your child turns boxes into buildings. They draw doors and windows, and you write the names. Set them along the roads.
4. **Signs.** Draw a red STOP sign, a green GO sign and an arrow. Fold them and stand them up at corners.
5. **Drive!** Your child drives cars to each place. Give them jobs: "The fire truck has to get to the bakery! Which way?"
6. Add a problem: a road is closed (put a block on it). Your child finds a new way.
7. Your child chooses one more thing to add to the city: a bridge from a book, a tunnel from a box, a river from blue paper.

## Make it yours
- Make it a city for animals, where the stuffed animals live and the cars are buses.
- Build an airport with a long tape runway and paper airplanes.
- Make a race track with a start line and finish line and hold a big race.

## Stuck?
- The tape won't stick to carpet: use a large flattened box or wrapping paper on the floor and draw roads with marker.
- Your child gets bored with building: start driving right away, and add a new building every time a car "needs" to go somewhere.

## Show it off
Give the family a city tour. Your child drives the tour bus and points out each place.

## Clean up
Peel up the tape and roll it into a ball. Your child parks all the cars in the "parking lot" (a box).

## For the grown-up
This builds spatial thinking (maps and directions), planning, problem solving and early writing as your child dictates signs. Painter's tape is gentlest on floors; test it first, and don't leave it down for more than a day or two. **Ask at dinner:** "If you could live in one building in your city, which one would it be?"
