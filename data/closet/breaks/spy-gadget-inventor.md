title: Spy Gadget Inventor
grade: 3
order: 13
kind: Build
time: 60-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: cardboard tubes, small boxes, tape, scissors, string, paper clips, rubber bands, a small mirror or foil, markers, paper
nice: a flashlight, a magnet, sticky notes, a lunch box for a gadget case

## The big idea
Agent, we need you. Headquarters is out of gadgets! Your mission: invent and build 3 spy tools from things around the house. Each one needs a name, a job and a test.

## Goals
- You invent and build at least 3 working spy gadgets.
- You make a secret gadget case with a manual.

## Objectives
- I can design a tool to solve a problem.
- I can build from a sketch and fix what goes wrong.
- I can write simple steps to explain how a tool works.

## Get ready
Gather boxes, tubes and tape on a table. Get a sheet of paper for each gadget. Pick your agent name.

## Steps
1. Think of spy problems. How do I see around a corner? How do I hear through a door? How do I grab something far away?
2. Sketch 3 gadget ideas (10 minutes). Draw each one. Label the parts.
3. Build gadget one. Example: a tube with a slanted foil or mirror piece at each end to peek around corners.
4. Build gadget two. Example: a grabber from 2 rulers, tape and a rubber band hinge.
5. Build gadget three. Your own idea! A tripwire alarm with string and a stack of cups? A fake book hiding spot?
6. Test each gadget 3 times. Does it work? Fix one thing and test again.
7. Write a gadget card for each: name, what it does, and 3 steps to use it.
8. Pack all the gadgets in a box. Label it "AGENT EYES ONLY."

## Make it yours
- Give your gadgets silly code names, like the "Snack Snatcher 3000."
- Make a mission for a family member to use your gadgets.
- Disguise a gadget so it looks like something boring, like a cereal box.

## Stuck?
- Can't think of a gadget: think of a problem first, then a tool to fix it.
- The tube peeker shows nothing: tilt the mirror pieces so they face each other at a slant.
- Tape won't hold: wrap it all the way around, not just a small strip.

## Show it off
Hold a "gadget briefing." Show each tool to your family and let them test one.

## Clean up
Put scraps in recycling. Keep the gadget case on a shelf.

## For the grown-up
This builds engineering design: plan, build, test, improve. Stay nearby for scissors. If you have a small mirror, tape its edges or use foil instead of glass. Tripwires should be at ankle height or lower and only in a clear room, never on stairs. **Ask at dinner:** "Which gadget worked best, and what did you fix?"
