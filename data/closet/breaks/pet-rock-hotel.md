title: Pet Rock Hotel
grade: 1
order: 19
kind: Pretend
time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Medium
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: 3 to 5 smooth rocks about the size of an egg, markers or paint, a shoebox or several small boxes, fabric scraps, cotton balls, paper, crayons, tape, scissors (child can use safety scissors)
nice: googly eyes and glue, yarn for hair, a toy bell for the front desk

## The big idea
Read aloud: "The pet rocks are going on vacation, and they need a place to stay! You're opening the fanciest hotel in town just for rocks. Each guest needs a room, a bed and their favorite thing to do."

## Goals
- Your child makes at least 3 pet rocks with names and personalities.
- Your child builds a hotel and runs it, checking guests in and taking care of them.

## Objectives
- Your child can describe a character's personality (grumpy, sleepy, silly).
- Your child can design spaces to meet someone's needs.
- Your child can solve pretend problems with ideas of their own.

## Get ready
Find rocks outside or in a garden. Wash and dry them. Set the shoebox on its side to make hotel floors (or line up small boxes as rooms).

## Steps
1. **Meet the guests** (15 minutes). Your child draws faces on the rocks: eyes, a mouth, maybe glasses or a mustache. Add yarn hair if you have it.
2. **Guest cards.** For each rock, your child decides a name and one thing about it: "Rocky loves to dance." "Pebbles is afraid of the dark." You write it on a small card.
3. **Build rooms** (20 minutes). Each guest gets a room in the box. Your child makes a bed from a fabric scrap and cotton ball pillow. Ask: "What does this guest need?" (Pebbles needs a night light: draw one.)
4. **The front desk.** Make a sign with the hotel's name and a bell (or a cup to tap).
5. **Check in.** You play the guests arriving. Your child greets each one, gives them a key (paper square) and shows them to their room.
6. **Hotel problems** (15 minutes). You bring problems: "Rocky's bed is too hard!" "Two rocks want the same room!" Your child fixes each one.
7. **Hotel fun.** Your child plans one hotel activity: a pool (a bowl of water), a dance party, a rock talent show.

## Make it yours
- Add a hotel restaurant with a menu for rocks ("gravel soup").
- Make it a spooky hotel or a hotel on the moon.
- A surprise guest arrives: a giant rock who can't fit through the door. How does your child solve it?

## Stuck?
- Markers won't show on dark rocks: use white paper eyes taped on, or pick lighter rocks.
- Your child runs out of ideas: switch roles, and you be the hotel manager for a while.

## Show it off
Give the family a hotel tour and introduce each guest.

## Clean up
Your child tucks the rocks into bed. Put the hotel on a shelf. Wash markers off hands.

## For the grown-up
This builds storytelling, empathy (thinking about each guest's needs), planning and problem solving. Rocks the size of an egg are safe; keep small ones and googly eyes away from younger siblings. **Ask at dinner:** "Which guest at your hotel was the hardest to please?"
