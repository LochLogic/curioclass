grade: 4

kind: Art
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low

players: 1+
materials: yarn or thick string in 3 or more colors, scissors, tape, a ruler
nice: embroidery thread, beads, a safety pin

## The big idea
Friendship bracelets show someone you care. Each knot, braid and color has meaning. Learn two patterns, then design your own bracelet for a friend or family member.

## Goals
- You make at least 2 bracelets with different patterns.
- You give one bracelet to someone special.

## Objectives
- I can follow a pattern and keep it going.
- I can tie a forward knot.
- I can design my own color pattern.

## Get ready
Cut 3 strings, each about 2 feet long. Tie them together in a knot, leaving 3 inches at the top. Tape the knot to a table.

## Steps
1. Make a braid first. Cross the left string over the middle. Cross the right string over the new middle. Repeat.
2. Braid until it fits around your wrist. Tie a knot at the end.
3. Now learn a forward knot. Use 4 strings. Hold the left string. Cross it over the next string to make a "4" shape.
4. Pull it under and through the loop. Pull up tight. Do this twice.
5. Move to the next string and repeat. Go all the way across.
6. Start again with the new left string. Watch the stripes grow!
7. Design your own (20 minutes). Pick colors that mean something. Example: blue for calm, yellow for happy.
8. Tie it on someone's wrist with a double knot.

## Make it yours
- Add beads in the middle.
- Use colors from someone's favorite team or pet.
- Make a matching pair: one for you, one for a friend.

## Stuck?
- Strings tangle: comb them straight with your fingers before every row.
- The bracelet twists: tape it down tighter.
- Knots slip: pull each knot all the way up to the top.

## Show it off
Give a bracelet at dinner. Tell the person what each color means.

## Clean up
Wind leftover strings into small balls. Put scissors away.

## For the grown-up
This builds patterns, counting, persistence and fine motor skills. It's also a kind gift to a friend or relative. **Ask at dinner:** "Who would you make a bracelet for next, and what colors would you pick?"
