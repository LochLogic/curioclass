title: Pizza Box Solar Oven
grade: 4
order: 10
kind: Science
time: 90-120 min
where: Outdoors
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: a pizza box or a shoebox with a lid, foil, plastic wrap, black paper, tape, a ruler or stick to prop the lid, a plate, a food to heat (crackers with cheese, or a tortilla with cheese)
nice: an oven thermometer, chocolate pieces, a stopwatch

## The big idea
The sun is a giant heater, 93 million miles away. Can you trap its heat to cook a snack? Build a solar oven, an oven powered by sunlight, and melt cheese with no fire and no plug.

## Goals
- You build a solar oven from a box.
- You melt cheese or chocolate using only sunlight.

## Objectives
- I can explain how foil reflects light, or bounces it.
- I can explain why black paper gets hot.
- I can measure changes over time.

## Get ready
Pick a sunny day, best between 11 in the morning and 2 in the afternoon. Clear a table. A grown-up cuts the lid flap.

## Steps
1. A grown-up cuts a flap in the lid. Leave about 1 inch of lid on 3 sides. Fold the flap up on the 4th side.
2. Cover the inside of the flap with foil, shiny side out. Smooth it flat.
3. Line the inside bottom of the box with black paper.
4. Tape plastic wrap over the hole in the lid. Seal the edges tight. This traps the warm air.
5. Put your snack on a plate inside the box.
6. Set the oven in the sun. Prop the flap with a stick so it shines light into the box.
7. Check every 10 minutes (40 minutes). Draw what you see each time.
8. Turn the box as the sun moves, so the flap catches the most light.

## Make it yours
- Build two ovens: one with foil, one without. Which gets hotter?
- Try melting different things: cheese, chocolate, a crayon in foil.
- Give your oven a name and make a sign for "Sun Kitchen."

## Stuck?
- Nothing melts: wait longer, or check that the flap points at the sun.
- Heat escapes: tape any gaps in the plastic wrap.
- No sun: try again another day. Clouds block the heat.

## Show it off
Serve your sun-cooked snack to the family. Tell them how far the heat traveled.

## Clean up
Throw away food scraps. Fold the box to save it, or recycle it.

## For the grown-up
This builds understanding of light, heat, reflection and absorption. Please cut the box flap yourself. Solar ovens get warm, not dangerously hot, but use an oven mitt to remove the plate. Check allergies for any food. **Ask at dinner:** "Why did the black paper help?"
