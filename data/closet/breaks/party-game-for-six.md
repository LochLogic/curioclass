## The big idea
The best party games are about the people, not the pieces: bluffing, guessing, laughing and accusing. You'll design an original party game for six or more players, with simple rules, a twist, and moments where the whole room shouts at once.

## Goals
- A party game for 6 or more players, with cards and a rule sheet.
- A playtest, even a small one, and one improvement.

## Objectives
- I can design a game around social mechanics like bluffing, guessing or hidden roles.
- I can make rules that scale from 4 to 10 players.
- I can write rules short enough to explain in two minutes.

## Get ready
Get cards, pens and a hat. Think of a party game moment you love: a perfect bluff, a hilarious guess.

## Steps
1. **Pick a core** (10 minutes):
   - **Hidden roles**: one or two players secretly have a different goal.
   - **Word guessing**: describe, draw or act out with a twist.
   - **Bluffing**: players claim things that may or may not be true.
2. **Add a twist.** Example: in a word game, the guesser can only ask yes or no questions, but one player is a secret "liar" who answers wrong.
3. **Make the cards** (20 minutes). Write 30 to 50 prompt cards, or role cards for every player count.
4. **Write the rules**: setup, a round, scoring, how the game ends. Keep it to one page.
5. **Scale it.** What changes with 4 players? 10? Adjust role counts or time limits.
6. **Playtest.** With a group, or by "simulating": write six player names and play through a round, imagining what each would do. Look for dull moments.
7. Revise one rule and give the game a name that makes people want to play.

## Make it yours
- Make it about your family or friends, with prompts only your group would get.
- Add a physical element, like everyone touching their nose when a secret word is said.
- Design a version that works outside, in a park or a yard.

## Stuck?
- The game is quiet: add a moment where everyone acts at once, like a vote or a grab.
- One person dominates: give everyone a turn to be the center of the round.

## Show it off
Bring it out at the next family gathering, sleepover or night with friends.

## Clean up
Rubber-band the cards and keep them in an envelope with the rules.

## For the grown-up
Party game design builds creativity, rule-writing and social awareness. It's a ready-made screen-free activity for the next time friends or family visit. **Ask at dinner:** "Can we play a round of your game right now?"
