title: Cup Constellation Projector
grade: 3
order: 27
kind: Science
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: 3 or 4 paper cups, a flashlight, a pushpin or a sharp pencil, a pencil, paper, tape
nice: a dark room, glow-in-the-dark stickers, a star chart from a library book

## The big idea
Long ago, people saw pictures in the stars. A hunter! A bear! A swan! Make a cup that shines stars on your ceiling. Then invent your own star pictures and tell their stories.

## Goals
- You make 3 cups that shine star patterns on the ceiling.
- You invent one new star picture and tell its story.

## Objectives
- I can name a star picture, called a constellation.
- I can copy a pattern from paper onto a cup.
- I can tell how light makes the dots on the ceiling.

## Get ready
Draw 4 circles on paper by tracing the bottom of a cup. These are your star maps.

## Steps
1. Draw the Big Dipper in one circle. It has 7 stars shaped like a pot with a long handle.
2. Draw Cassiopeia in another. It has 5 stars shaped like a W.
3. Draw your own star picture in the third. A cat? A guitar? A taco?
4. Tape each circle to the bottom of a cup. Draw stars backward if you want them right on the ceiling.
5. Poke a hole at each star (15 minutes). Push the pin through the paper and cup. Do it on top of an eraser or a folded towel.
6. Peel off the paper. Darken the room.
7. Put the flashlight inside the cup. Point it at the ceiling. Stars!
8. Write the story of your own star picture. How did it get into the sky?

## Make it yours
- Make bigger holes for bright stars and tiny ones for dim stars.
- Make a whole "night sky show" with 5 cups and a story for each.
- Invent a star picture for each family member.

## Stuck?
- Stars look blurry: move the cup closer to the ceiling.
- You can't see them: make the room darker, or wait until night.
- Holes rip: use a smaller pin and go slowly.

## Show it off
Give a star show at bedtime. Turn off the lights and tell each story.

## Clean up
Put the pushpin back in its box. Stack the cups. Recycle scraps.

## For the grown-up
This builds space science (constellations), pattern copying, and light. The pushpin is sharp: stay nearby, and have your child poke onto an eraser, never toward a hand. **Ask at dinner:** "Can you tell me the story of the constellation you made?"
