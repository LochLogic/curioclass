title: Rubber Band Box Guitar
grade: 1
order: 11
kind: Music
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: a tissue box or a shoebox, 4 to 6 rubber bands of different thicknesses, a paper towel tube, 2 pencils, tape, scissors (grown-up only), markers or crayons
nice: a ruler, stickers, a strap made of yarn or a ribbon

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Every rock star needs a guitar! You're going to build one out of a box and some rubber bands. Then you'll find out how to make high notes and low notes, and write your first song."

## Goals
- Your child builds a box guitar that plays different notes.
- Your child makes up a short song and performs it.

## Objectives
- Your child can make a sound by plucking and tell high sounds from low sounds.
- Your child can explain that thin, tight bands sound higher and thick, loose bands sound lower.
- Your child can play a pattern of notes and repeat it.

## Get ready
If using a shoebox, cut a hole about the size of a fist in the lid (grown-up). A tissue box already has one. Gather rubber bands and sort them thin to thick.

## Steps
1. **Decorate** (10 minutes). Your child decorates the box: lightning bolts, stars, their band name.
2. **String it up.** Your child stretches the rubber bands the long way around the box, over the hole. Space them about a finger's width apart.
3. **The bridge.** Slide a pencil under the bands on each side of the hole. Now the bands are lifted and they ring!
4. **Listen** (10 minutes). Pluck each band. Ask: "Which one is the highest? The lowest?" Your child lines them up from low to high.
5. **Change the sound.** Push one pencil closer to the hole. Does it sound different? Pull a band tighter. What happens?
6. **The neck.** Tape the paper towel tube to one end of the box as the neck. Draw frets (lines) on it.
7. **Write a song** (15 minutes). Your child makes a pattern: low, low, high, low. Add words: "My dog is fast, my dog is fast!" Practice it 3 times.

## Make it yours
- Make a second instrument: a "box harp" with the bands stretched over an open box with no lid.
- Make up a song for a stuffed animal, a pet or a family member.
- Start a band: you clap or tap a pot while your child plays guitar.

## Stuck?
- The bands go "thud" instead of ringing: lift them higher with the pencils, or stretch them tighter.
- The box bends: use a sturdier box, or tape a piece of cardboard inside the lid.

## Show it off
Perform your child's song for the family. They can pick a rock star name and introduce themselves.

## Clean up
Keep the guitar on a shelf. Collect any broken rubber bands and throw them away.

## For the grown-up
This builds early music ideas (high and low pitch) and sound science: tighter or thinner means a faster vibration and a higher sound. Watch for snapping rubber bands near eyes; pluck, don't pull back hard. Keep rubber bands away from babies and toddlers. **Ask at dinner:** "How do you make a high note on your guitar?"
