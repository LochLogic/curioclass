grade: 4
order: 28
kind: Pretend
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: On your own
players: 1+
materials: stuffed animals, boxes, blankets, towels, paper, markers, tape, a bell or a spoon and pot
nice: a small notebook for a guest book, a toy cash register

## The big idea
The Fluffy Paws Hotel just opened, and it's booked solid! Every guest has special needs. One snores. One wants a pool. One is scared of the dark. You're the manager. Make every guest happy.

## Goals
- You build a hotel with at least 4 rooms.
- You check in guests and handle at least 3 problems.

## Objectives
- I can plan and set up spaces for different needs.
- I can write signs, menus and a guest book.
- I can solve problems using my imagination.

## Get ready
Gather stuffed animals and boxes. Pick a corner of a room for the hotel.

## Steps
1. Name the hotel. Make a big sign.
2. Build 4 rooms from boxes and blankets. Give each a theme: "Jungle Room," "Ocean Suite."
3. Make a front desk. Put a bell, a guest book and a room key board.
4. Check in each guest. Write their name, room and special needs.
5. Make a room service menu with 5 items and prices.
6. Handle problems (20 minutes). The bear snores. The cat wants a view. The dog lost its key. Solve each one.
7. Write a "guest review" from each animal.

## Make it yours
- Add a pool, a spa or a game room.
- Make an emergency: a storm cuts the power! What do you do?
- Hire staff: a stuffed animal chef and a doorman.

## Stuck?
- Not enough boxes: use pillows and shoe boxes.
- You run out of problems: let each guest ask for something strange.
- Too much to do: pick 3 guests and give them all your attention.

## Show it off
Give the family a hotel tour. Let them read the guest reviews.

## Clean up
"Check out" all guests. Put boxes and blankets back.

## For the grown-up
This builds planning, writing for a purpose and creative problem solving. **Ask at dinner:** "Which guest was the hardest to please?"
