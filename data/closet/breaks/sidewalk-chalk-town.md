title: Sidewalk Chalk Town
grade: 1
order: 12
kind: Outside
time: 60-90 min
where: Outdoors
mess: Medium
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: sidewalk chalk, a driveway, sidewalk, patio or playground space, toy cars or a ride-on toy, a bucket of water and a sponge
nice: a tricycle or scooter, a bike helmet, a jump rope

## The big idea
Read aloud: "The town needs a builder! It has no roads, no houses, no school and no fire station. With your chalk, you can build the whole town right on the ground. Then you can drive through it!"

## Goals
- Your child draws a chalk town with roads and at least 6 places.
- Your child drives, walks or rides through the town on a mission.

## Objectives
- Your child can plan where roads go and connect places.
- Your child can draw and label simple signs.
- Your child can follow directions with left, right and straight.

## Get ready
Pick a safe spot away from traffic: a driveway (with the car moved), a back patio, a playground or an empty part of a sidewalk with a grown-up watching. Set out the chalk.

## Steps
1. **Plan the town.** Ask: "What does a town need?" Make a list out loud: a house, a school, a store, a library, a park, a pizza shop, a fire station.
2. **Draw the roads** (15 minutes). Your child draws two lines side by side for a road. Make it wide enough for feet or a toy car. Add a curvy road and a circle.
3. **Build the places** (20 minutes). Your child draws each building beside a road: a square with a triangle roof, windows and doors. Write names on them.
4. **Signs and lines.** Add a stop sign at a corner, a dotted line down the middle of a road, a crosswalk, a parking lot.
5. **Your child adds a surprise.** A volcano park? A dragon zoo? A slide that goes to the moon?
6. **Missions** (15 minutes). Give missions like "Deliver a pizza to the school, then go to the park." Your child walks, drives a toy car or rides through town following the roads. Then they give you a mission.
7. **Traffic rules.** Your child is the crossing guard and calls "Stop!" and "Go!"

## Make it yours
- Draw a giant board game instead, with squares to hop on.
- Make it an ocean town for fish, or a space city with rocket ports.
- Use the water bucket for a "rain storm" that washes away a road, and rebuild it.

## Stuck?
- No safe outdoor space: tape paper together or open up brown grocery bags on the floor and draw the town with crayons.
- Chalk is breaking: use the broken pieces for coloring in; whole sticks for lines.

## Show it off
Give the family a tour of the town. Let them walk the roads while your child gives directions.

## Clean up
Put chalk back in its box. Leave the town for others to enjoy, or wash it with the sponge and bucket.

## For the grown-up
This builds map skills, direction words, planning and drawing, and it gets kids moving outdoors. Stay close the whole time, keep away from streets and driveways in use, and use a helmet on anything with wheels. Check whether your building or landlord has rules about chalk on shared paths. **Ask at dinner:** "If you lived in your chalk town, where would your house be?"
