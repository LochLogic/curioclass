title: Straw Pan Flute
grade: 4
order: 14
kind: Music
time: 45-75 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: Nearby
players: 1
materials: 8 plastic or paper straws, scissors, tape, a ruler, a piece of cardboard about 2 by 5 inches, a marker
nice: a second set of straws for a friend, a recorder or keyboard to compare notes

## The big idea
A pan flute is one of the oldest instruments on Earth. It's a row of tubes, each one a different length. Blow across the top and it sings! Build one from straws and play your own tune.

## Goals
- You build a flute with 8 straws of different lengths.
- You play a simple tune of your own.

## Objectives
- I can explain why shorter tubes make higher notes.
- I can cut straws to measured lengths.
- I can play a pattern of notes.

## Get ready
Clear a table. Line up 8 straws. Get the ruler and marker ready.

## Steps
1. Measure and mark the straws. Make them 7, 6.5, 6, 5.5, 5, 4.5, 4 and 3.5 inches long.
2. Cut each straw at the mark.
3. Line them up from longest to shortest, tops even.
4. Lay them on a strip of tape, sticky side up. Press them down.
5. Tape the cardboard strip across the straws to keep them straight.
6. Cover the bottom end of each straw with a small piece of tape. This makes a better sound.
7. Play (15 minutes). Hold the flute to your bottom lip. Blow gently across the top, like blowing over a bottle.
8. Make a tune. Play short ones, then long ones. Make a pattern and repeat it.

## Make it yours
- Make a "baby flute" with 4 tiny straws and a "giant flute" with long ones.
- Write the notes as numbers. 1 is the longest straw. Play "1, 3, 5, 3, 1."
- Write a short song for an animal, like a bird or a snake.

## Stuck?
- No sound: blow softer and across, not into, the straw.
- All straws sound the same: check the bottom ends are taped shut.
- Straws fall out: add more tape on both sides.

## Show it off
Play a mini concert at bedtime. Play your tune 3 times, a little faster each time.

## Clean up
Put scraps in the trash. Keep the flute in a safe place.

## For the grown-up
This builds understanding of sound and pitch (shorter tube, higher note), measuring, and music. Stay nearby for scissors. **Ask at dinner:** "Which straw made the highest note, and why?"
