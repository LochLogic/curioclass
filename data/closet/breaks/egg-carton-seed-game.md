title: Egg Carton Seed Game
grade: 4
order: 30
kind: Game
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 1+
materials: an egg carton (12 cups), 48 small things like dry beans or buttons or pebbles, 2 small bowls, markers
nice: a partner (a sibling, a friend or a grown-up after work), paint to decorate

## The big idea
This game is thousands of years old. People played it in the dirt with seeds and stones. It's all about counting and planning ahead. Build the board, learn the game, then invent your own twist.

## Goals
- You build a game board and play at least 3 games.
- You invent a new rule and test it.

## Objectives
- I can count and plan ahead to win.
- I can follow and explain game rules.
- I can change a rule and see how it changes the game.

## Get ready
Open the egg carton. Set a bowl at each end. These are the "stores." Put 4 beans in each cup.

## Steps
1. Sit with the carton between you and your partner. Each player owns the 6 cups on their side. The bowl on your right is yours.
2. On your turn, pick up all the beans from one of your cups.
3. Drop one bean in each cup going right, around the board, like a circle. Drop one in your store when you pass it. Skip the other player's store.
4. If your last bean lands in your store, take another turn!
5. Does your last bean land in an empty cup on your side? Take it and all the beans across from it. Put them in your store.
6. The game ends when one side is empty. The other player puts their leftover beans in their store.
7. Count your store. Most beans wins!
8. Invent a twist (15 minutes). Example: "If your last bean lands on 3 beans, you get 2 more turns." Test it.

## Make it yours
- Decorate the carton like a treasure chest.
- Play solo against yourself, switching sides.
- Use 5 beans in each cup to see if the game changes.

## Stuck?
- Rules confuse you: play one turn at a time, slowly, saying each step.
- No partner: be both players. Try to make the best move for each side.
- Beans roll: use flat things like buttons or pasta.

## Show it off
Teach the game to the family. Hold a tournament with a paper medal.

## Clean up
Pour the beans into a bag. Close the carton.

## For the grown-up
This is a version of an ancient "sowing" or "count and capture" game played around the world, especially in Africa. It builds counting, strategy and planning ahead. Keep beans away from small children. **Ask at dinner:** "What's your best strategy to win?"
