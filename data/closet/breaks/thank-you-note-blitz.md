kind: Kindness
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 1
materials: paper or card stock, markers, colored pencils, envelopes or paper to fold into envelopes, a pencil
nice: stickers, a list of addresses from a grown-up, stamps

## The big idea
How many people help you every week? A teacher, a bus driver, a librarian, a coach, a neighbor. Most of them never hear "thank you." In one hour, write and decorate thank-you notes for as many helpers as you can.

## Goals
- You write at least 6 thank-you notes.
- You deliver or mail them to real people.

## Objectives
- I can name what someone did that helped me.
- I can write a short, kind note with details.
- I can sign and address notes.

## Get ready
Make a list of 8 to 10 people who help you. Think about school, home, sports and your street.

## Steps
1. Pick your top 6 helpers.
2. Fold paper in half for cards. Make 6 cards.
3. Write each note (5 minutes each). Use this plan: Dear ___, Thank you for ___. It helped me because ___. From, ___.
4. Add one detail. Example: "Thank you for helping me find dragon books. I read all 4!"
5. Draw a small picture on the front of each card.
6. Fold or make envelopes. Write each person's name on the front.
7. Ask a grown-up to help you mail or deliver them.

## Make it yours
- Make a thank-you note for someone you don't know, like a delivery driver.
- Write a thank-you song, 4 lines long. Sing it to a family member who helps you.
- Make a "thank-you jar" for your home for everyone to fill.

## Stuck?
- You can't think of helpers: who makes your food, drives you, teaches you?
- Notes feel boring: add a joke or a memory.
- Your hand gets tired: take a 5-minute dance break.

## Show it off
Before mailing, line up the notes and read one out loud to a grown-up.

## Clean up
Put the markers back. Recycle scraps.

## For the grown-up
This builds kind writing, details and gratitude. Help your child find addresses and mail or deliver the notes. **Ask at dinner:** "Who do you think will be most surprised by your note?"
