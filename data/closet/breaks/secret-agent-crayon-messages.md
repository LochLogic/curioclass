title: Secret Agent Crayon Messages
grade: 1
order: 17
kind: Game
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: white paper, a white crayon, watercolor paints or markers mixed with water, a paintbrush, a cup of water, paper towels, an envelope
nice: sunglasses and a hat for the agent costume, a code name badge

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Agent, we need your help. Secret messages must be sent across the house, but they must be invisible! Only someone with the special paint can make them appear. Your mission starts now."

## Goals
- Your child writes invisible messages and makes them appear with paint.
- Your child sends a secret mission to a family member.

## Objectives
- Your child can write words or draw pictures that carry a message.
- Your child can explain why the paint doesn't stick to the crayon (wax and water don't mix).
- Your child can follow a clue to complete a mission.

## Get ready
Set out white paper, white crayons and watercolors. Put paper towels down. No watercolors? Scribble a washable marker on a plate, add a little water, and use that as paint.

## Steps
1. **Agent names.** Your child picks a code name ("Agent Thunderbean"). Make a badge.
2. **Test the secret** (5 minutes). Draw a smiley face on white paper with the white crayon, pressing hard. Can you see it? Now paint over it with watery paint. It appears!
3. **Why?** Ask: "Why doesn't the paint cover the crayon?" Crayon is wax, and water slides off wax.
4. **Write a secret message** (15 minutes). Your child writes or draws a message in white crayon: "MEET AT THE COUCH," a map, a treasure picture, or a word they know. Press hard!
5. **Swap and reveal.** You write one for your child too. Paint over each other's messages to read them.
6. **The mission** (20 minutes). Your child writes a mission for you: "Find the sock under the bed." You paint to reveal it and go do it. Then you write a mission for your child.
7. **The message chain.** Hide 3 invisible messages around the house, each one telling where the next is. Bring the paint along to decode.

## Make it yours
- Send a secret birthday wish or love note to someone in the family.
- Use different colors of paint to make the secret message look like fireworks.
- Make a secret agent code book with pictures that mean words (a star means "good job").

## Stuck?
- The message doesn't show: press harder with the crayon, and use watery paint, not thick paint.
- Writing is hard: draw pictures instead. Spies use symbols too.

## Show it off
Leave a secret message for a family member to discover when they get home, with a paintbrush and a note: "Paint me."

## Clean up
Rinse brushes and dump the paint water. Your child puts away the crayons and sets messages out to dry.

## For the grown-up
This builds writing for a purpose (kids write more when it's secret), following clues and simple science (wax resists water). No candles or flames are needed; a white crayon is all it takes. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the secret message, and where did you hide it?"
