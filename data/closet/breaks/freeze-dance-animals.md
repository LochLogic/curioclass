title: Freeze Dance Animals
grade: k
order: 14
kind: Game
time: 45-75 min
where: Either
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: paper, crayons, scissors (grown-up only), a bowl or hat, space to move, music (you can hum, sing or clap)
nice: a music player, scarves to wave, a timer

## The big idea
Read aloud: "When the music plays, you turn into an animal and move like one. When the music stops, FREEZE! Can you hold still like a statue of a flamingo? Let's play!"

## Goals
- Your child plays freeze dance as at least 8 different animals.
- Your child makes a deck of animal cards and invents a new rule.

## Objectives
- Your child can stop and start their body on a signal.
- Your child can move in different ways: hop, slither, gallop, waddle.
- Your child can follow a two-part rule (move like the card, freeze when it stops).

## Get ready
Clear a space with nothing to trip on. Cut a sheet of paper into 8 cards. On each one, write an animal name and leave room for a picture.

## Steps
1. **Make the cards** (15 minutes). Your child chooses 8 animals. Ideas: frog, snake, elephant, bird, penguin, kangaroo, bear, crab. They draw one on each card. You write the name.
2. Put the cards in a bowl.
3. **Practice the moves.** For each card, ask: "How does it move?" Frog hops. Snake slithers on the floor. Penguin waddles. Crab walks sideways.
4. **Play** (20 minutes). Pull a card. Start the music (or sing). Your child moves like that animal. Stop the music suddenly. Freeze! Count to 5 out loud. If they hold still, they win a point.
5. Make it trickier: freeze in a funny shape, like a flamingo on one leg or a bear standing tall.
6. **Your child's rule.** Let them invent one: "When the music stops, you have to make the animal's sound," or "If you pull the elephant, everyone must move in slow motion."
7. Switch: your child controls the music, and you dance.

## Make it yours
- Add mixed-up animals: pull two cards and move like both at once (a frog-snake!).
- Play in a "jungle" with pillows as rocks and a blanket as a river you can't step in.
- Make it a story: the animals are going to a party and have to freeze when the zookeeper walks by.

## Stuck?
- Your child keeps wiggling during freeze: make the freeze time 3 seconds instead of 5, then grow it.
- No music: clap, sing a song you both know, or bang a pot gently.

## Show it off
Teach the family the game after dinner. Your child pulls the cards and runs the music.

## Clean up
Your child puts the animal cards in an envelope and labels it "Freeze Dance."

## For the grown-up
Freeze games build self-control (stopping on a signal is a big kindergarten skill), body awareness, balance and listening. Clear sharp corners from the dance space and play in socks on carpet or bare feet, not socks on hard floors. **Ask at dinner:** "Which animal was the hardest to freeze as?"
