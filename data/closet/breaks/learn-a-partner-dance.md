## The big idea
Partner dances like swing, salsa and the waltz are a conversation without words: one person leads, the other
follows, and both listen through their hands. You will learn the basic step of one dance, a turn, and a simple
lead-and-follow pattern, then teach it to someone and dance a full song together.

## Goals
- A basic step, a turn and one fancy move learned with a partner.
- A full song danced without stopping.

## Objectives
- I can perform a basic step in time with music.
- I can lead or follow using clear, gentle signals through the hands and frame.
- I can teach a sequence to someone else.

## Get ready
Clear the space. Choose a dance and a song with a clear beat: swing (fast, bouncy), salsa (quick-quick-slow), or
waltz (1-2-3).

## Steps
1. Learn the basic step alone (10 minutes). **Waltz:** step forward left, step side right, close left to right; then
   back right, side left, close right to left. Count "1, 2, 3."
2. Find your **frame**: face your partner, leader's right hand on follower's shoulder blade, follower's left hand on
   leader's shoulder, other hands held at shoulder height. Keep arms firm but not stiff.
3. Practice the basic step together until you can do eight in a row without looking down.
4. Learn the lead: the leader signals with slight pressure through the frame, not by pulling.
5. Add a turn (15 minutes). Leader raises the joined hands; follower turns under them in four counts.
6. Add one flourish: a dip, a spin, a sway.
7. Dance a whole song together. Switch leader and follower for the second song.

## Make it yours
- Learn a dance from your family's culture or from a country you want to visit.
- Make up a new partner dance with its own basic step and name.
- Dance in costume to a song from another decade.

## Stuck?
- You keep stepping on toes: slow down and check your basic step; leader steps first.
- The lead is unclear: use more gentle pressure, earlier, and stay connected through the frame.

## Show it off
Dance a song for the family after dinner, then teach them the basic step.

## Clean up
Put furniture back and turn off the music.

## For the grown-up
This builds coordination, rhythm, communication and confidence, and partner dancing is a skill for life. Clear the
space of anything breakable. **Ask at dinner:** "Was it harder to lead or to follow?"
