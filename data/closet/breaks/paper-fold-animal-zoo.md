title: Paper Fold Animal Zoo
grade: 2
order: 23
kind: Art
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: square paper (cut printer paper into squares, about 6 by 6 inches), markers or crayons, tape, a cardboard box or lid for the zoo, scissors (for the grown-up to cut squares)
nice: colored paper, googly eyes, glue, a book of paper folding from the library

## The big idea
Read aloud: "The zoo is empty! All the animals need to be made by hand, using only paper and folds. You are the zookeeper and the animal maker. How many animals can you fold for your zoo?"

## Goals
- At least 5 folded paper animals.
- A zoo with labels for each animal home.

## Objectives
- Your child can follow folding steps in order, matching corners and edges.
- Your child can make sharp creases with a fingernail.
- Your child can invent a new animal by changing a fold.

## Get ready
Cut 15 squares of paper. Clear a flat table. Practice one fold together: corner to corner, then crease hard with a fingernail.

## Steps
1. **Fold a dog face** (10 minutes). Fold the square in half corner to corner to make a triangle, point down. Fold the two side corners down to make floppy ears. Fold the bottom point up a little for the chin. Draw eyes and a nose.
2. **Fold a cat face.** Same start, but fold the side corners UP for pointy ears. Fold the top point down. Draw whiskers.
3. **Fold a fish.** Fold a triangle. Fold the two side corners toward the bottom point, overlapping a little. Turn it sideways: there's a fish with a tail! Draw scales.
4. **Fold a bird.** Fold a triangle, then fold it in half again. Fold the wing tips up a finger's width on each side. It stands!
5. **Invent a new animal** (15 minutes). Your child mixes folds: cat ears on a fish, three-eared dog, a dragon from a bird with extra folds. Give it a name.
6. **Build the zoo.** Use a box or lid. Divide it into areas with paper strips: the pond, the jungle, the barn.
7. **Label the homes.** Your child writes a sign for each animal: "DOG. Likes bones." "FISHY DRAGON. Breathes bubbles."

## Make it yours
- Make a baby for each animal using a smaller square.
- Fold a whole ocean of fish in different colors for an aquarium.
- Write a "zoo map" so visitors can find each animal.

## Stuck?
- Folds don't line up: press the corners together first, then crease from the middle out.
- The paper tears: start with fresh paper and fold gently before you crease.

## Show it off
Give a zoo tour after dinner, with your child as the zookeeper telling one fun fact about each animal.

## Clean up
Keep the zoo on a shelf and save extra squares in an envelope.

## For the grown-up
Paper folding builds hand control, following steps in order, and early geometry (triangles, halves, symmetry). Inventing animals adds imagination. **Ask at dinner:** "Which animal was the hardest to fold, and which one did you invent?"
