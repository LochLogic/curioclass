title: Nature Bracelet Walk
grade: 1
order: 8
kind: Outside
time: 60-90 min
where: Outdoors
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: wide masking tape or packing tape, paper, crayons, a small bag
nice: a magnifying glass, a hat for each walker, a water bottle

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Let's go on a walk and wear what we find! Your bracelet will be sticky side out, so every leaf, petal and feather you find can hop right on. By the end, you'll be wearing the whole walk on your wrist."

## Goals
- Your child makes a nature bracelet with at least 8 different treasures.
- Your child tells the story of the walk using the bracelet.

## Objectives
- Your child can find and name different leaves, seeds and petals.
- Your child can compare treasures by color, size and texture.
- Your child can retell events in order.

## Get ready
Wrap a strip of masking tape around your child's wrist, sticky side out. Make it loose enough to slide off. Make one for yourself. Plan a short walk: a yard, a block, a park, or a path near your home.

## Steps
1. Talk about walk rules: stay with your grown-up, pick only from the ground, and leave living flowers on plants unless they're yours to pick.
2. **The walk** (30 minutes). Your child looks for tiny treasures: a small leaf, a petal, a blade of grass, a seed, a feather, a bit of bark. Press each one onto the tape.
3. **Mix it up.** Ask: "Can you find something yellow? Something fuzzy? Something smaller than your fingernail?"
4. Let your child choose the path at every corner: left, right or straight.
5. **Back home, look closely** (10 minutes). Slide off the bracelet. Use the magnifying glass. Which treasure has lines? Which is the softest?
6. **Tell the walk story.** Your child goes around the bracelet in order: "First I found this leaf by the gate. Then a feather by the big tree..."
7. **Draw a map** (10 minutes). Your child draws the path you walked and tapes a picture of each treasure where it was found.

## Make it yours
- Make a color bracelet: find something in every color of the rainbow, in order.
- Make a crown instead, with tape around a paper band.
- Make a gift bracelet for someone at home with treasures they would love.

## Stuck?
- Tape stops sticking: add a new strip on top or wrap a fresh layer.
- No safe walking area: do it on a balcony with potted plants, in a courtyard, or with houseplant trimmings and kitchen herbs.

## Show it off
Wear the bracelet when the family gets home and tell them the walk story.

## Clean up
Lay the bracelet flat on paper to keep, or put it in a zip bag. Wash hands after the walk.

## For the grown-up
This builds observation, describing words, sequencing and early map-making. Keep your child away from mushrooms, berries, broken glass and plants you don't know; poison ivy and similar plants are common along paths. Wash hands after. **Ask at dinner:** "What's the story of your bracelet?"
