grade: 5
order: 17
kind: Outside
time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: a big sheet of paper (or 4 sheets taped together), a pencil, an eraser, colored pencils or markers, a clipboard or a hard book, a second color pen
nice: a compass, a printed or paper map of your area to compare at the end

## The big idea
How well do you really know your street? Draw a map of your neighborhood using only your memory. Then take a walk to check it. Where were you right? What did you forget completely? Mapmakers call this ground truthing.

## Goals
- You draw a map of your block or area from memory.
- You walk it with a grown-up, check it, and fix it in a new color.

## Objectives
- I can draw a map from above, called a bird's-eye view.
- I can make a legend, which is a key that explains map symbols.
- I can compare my memory to the real place and correct mistakes.

## Get ready
Clear the table. Put your home in the center of the paper and draw it as a small square. Draw an arrow showing which way is north if you know it.

## Steps
1. Draw the streets first (10 minutes). Long lines for roads. Write any names you remember.
2. Add landmarks: a store, a park, a tree you like, a mailbox, a dog that always barks.
3. Make symbols and a legend. Example: a small triangle for trees, a star for places you like.
4. Mark three things you are not sure about with a question mark.
5. Go for a walk with your grown-up (20 to 30 minutes). Bring the map and the second color pen.
6. Fix your map in the new color as you go. Add what you missed. Cross out what was wrong.
7. Back home, count your corrections. Add a title and a compass rose (the star with N, S, E, W).

## Make it yours
- Make a "secret map" with your own names for places, like Snack Corner or Squirrel Highway.
- Draw a map of the future: what would you add to your neighborhood?
- Make a treasure map version and hide a note somewhere in your home for someone to find.

## Stuck?
- You can't remember much: start with the path you take most often, like the way to school.
- The map gets crowded: draw buildings as simple squares and use numbers with a list.
- You can't walk today: map your home or building from memory, then check room by room.

## Show it off
Hang your map up. Give a tour: point to the places you forgot and the ones you got just right.

## Clean up
Put away pens and pencils. Roll the map up or hang it on a wall.

## For the grown-up
This builds map skills (bird's-eye view, legends, directions) and noticing the world closely. Please walk with your child; the walk is a nice chance to chat. Keep to routes you already know. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the biggest thing you forgot on your map?"
