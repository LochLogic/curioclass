title: Cardboard Pinball Machine
grade: 4
order: 18
kind: Build
time: 90-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: a shallow box lid or a cut-down box, cardboard scraps, a marble or small ball, rubber bands, tape, glue, scissors, cardboard tubes, a pencil, a paper cup
nice: clothespins, bottle caps, foil, markers

## The big idea
Pinball machines flash, bounce and ding. Build your own from a box! Add bumpers, ramps, traps and flippers. Then play to beat your high score.

## Goals
- You build a working pinball game with at least 5 features.
- You make a scoring system and set a high score.

## Objectives
- I can design a game board with slopes and obstacles.
- I can build simple flippers and launchers.
- I can test and improve how the ball moves.

## Get ready
Prop one end of the box lid on a book so it slopes down toward you. Gather your cardboard and tape.

## Steps
1. Make the launcher. Stretch a rubber band across a corner channel. Pull it back with the marble and let go.
2. Make flippers (20 minutes). Cut 2 cardboard strips. Push a pencil-point hole near one end. Pin each to the box bottom with a short pencil or paper fastener so they swing.
3. Add bumpers. Wrap rubber bands around tube pieces. Glue them standing up.
4. Add a ramp. Fold a strip of cardboard into a slope.
5. Add a trap or a hole. Cut a hole and tape a paper cup under it.
6. Make a scoring chart. Bumper: 10 points. Ramp: 50 points. Hole: 100 points.
7. Test the board. Does the ball get stuck anywhere? Move things to fix it.
8. Play 5 rounds. Write your high score.

## Make it yours
- Pick a theme: jungle, space, haunted house, or deep sea.
- Add a bonus target that lights up with a flashlight.
- Make a two-player version with flippers on both ends.

## Stuck?
- The ball gets stuck: make the slope steeper with a second book.
- Flippers won't swing: make the hole bigger.
- The ball flies out: add tape walls along the sides.

## Show it off
Host a "pinball tournament" for the family. Write the high score on a sign.

## Clean up
Put the marble in a cup. Recycle the scraps. Wipe away glue drips.

## For the grown-up
This builds design, engineering and math (scoring and adding). Stay nearby for scissors and poking holes. Keep marbles away from small children. **Ask at dinner:** "What part of the pinball machine was hardest to make?"
