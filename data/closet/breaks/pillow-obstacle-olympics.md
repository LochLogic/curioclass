title: Pillow Obstacle Olympics
grade: k
order: 26
kind: Move
time: 45-75 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: pillows and couch cushions, a blanket, a chair or table to crawl under, a hallway or open floor, paper, crayons, tape, a soft ball or rolled-up socks
nice: a timer or a phone stopwatch, paper plates as stepping stones, a jump rope laid flat

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Welcome to the Pillow Olympics! Athletes must hop, crawl, balance and jump to win a gold medal. You will design the course, test it, and go for the gold!"

## Goals
- Your child designs and runs an obstacle course with at least 5 stations.
- Your child wins a homemade gold medal.

## Objectives
- Your child can move in many ways: crawl, hop, jump, balance, roll.
- Your child can follow a course in order.
- Your child can try again to beat their own score.

## Get ready
Clear a long space with no sharp corners and nothing breakable nearby. Bare feet are best (socks slip on hard floors). Gather pillows and cushions.

## Steps
1. Ask your child: "What should athletes have to do?" Make a list: crawl through a tunnel, hop on pillows, walk a balance beam, jump over something, throw a ball into a basket.
2. **Build the course** (15 minutes). Your child decides where each station goes. Cushions in a row become "stepping stones." A blanket over two chairs is a tunnel. A line of tape is a balance beam. A laundry basket is the throwing goal.
3. Make a number sign (1 to 5) for each station so athletes know the order.
4. **Test run.** You go first, slowly, and your child coaches you. Is anything too hard or unsafe? Fix it together.
5. **Olympics time** (20 minutes). Your child runs the course. Count out loud or time it. Run it again and try to be faster, or try to do it with no falls.
6. **Silly rounds.** Your child picks a new rule each time: hop the whole way, walk backward, carry a stuffed animal, move like a bear.
7. **Medal ceremony.** Your child draws a gold medal on a paper circle, tapes on a yarn loop, and wears it on the "podium" (a cushion) while you hum a song.

## Make it yours
- Make it a jungle course: the floor is a river and pillows are rocks.
- Add a mission: rescue a stuffed animal at the end of the course and carry it back.
- Make a ninja course where you must be totally silent.

## Stuck?
- Pillows slide on the floor: put them on a rug, or switch to paper plates taped down.
- Your child gets wild: add a "freeze station" where they must stop and count to 5.

## Show it off
Hold the Olympics for the family after dinner. Your child is the coach, and everyone gets a medal.

## Clean up
Your child puts pillows back on the couch and carries the blanket to its place. Make it a timed race.

## For the grown-up
This builds gross motor skills (balance, coordination, strength), planning and perseverance, and burns energy on indoor days. Clear corners and hard edges, keep jumping low, and skip anything that involves climbing on furniture. **Ask at dinner:** "Which station was the hardest, and how did you get better at it?"
