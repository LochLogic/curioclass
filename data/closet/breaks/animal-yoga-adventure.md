title: Animal Yoga Adventure
grade: 2
order: 18
kind: Move
time: 45-75 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: a soft floor, a rug, mat, or towel, paper, crayons or markers, tape
nice: calm music, a bell, stuffed animals to "join in"

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Today you will go on a jungle adventure without leaving the room. Every animal you meet teaches you a new way to move your body. Can you stretch like a cat, balance like a flamingo, and roar like a lion?"

## Goals
- A set of at least 6 animal poses your child learned or invented.
- A pose-card "story path" taped on the floor that your child can lead.

## Objectives
- Your child can hold a balance pose for 5 slow breaths.
- Your child can follow and give clear movement directions.
- Your child can invent a new pose and describe it with a name.

## Get ready
Clear a space about two arm-lengths wide. Lay out a mat or towel. Take off socks for better grip.

## Steps
1. **Warm up.** Stand tall, reach up high, then fold over and dangle like a monkey. Breathe in slowly through your nose, out through your mouth.
2. **Learn 4 poses** (15 minutes). Try them together:
   **Cat:** hands and knees, round your back and say "meow."
   **Cow:** hands and knees, drop your belly and look up, say "moo."
   **Flamingo:** stand on one foot, other foot on your ankle, arms out like wings.
   **Lion:** kneel, hands on knees, open your mouth wide and roar!
3. **Invent 2 more** (10 minutes). Your child makes up two new animal poses, like a sleepy sloth or a jumping frog. Give each a name and a sound.
4. **Make pose cards.** Your child draws each animal on a half sheet and writes its name. Tape them on the floor in a path.
5. **Tell the adventure.** Make up a jungle story as your child walks the path: "You wake up as a cat. You stretch, then hear a lion roar..." At each card, do the pose.
6. **Switch leaders.** Your child tells the story and you follow.
7. **Rest.** Lie on your back like a sleepy bear for one minute with eyes closed.

## Make it yours
- Switch to an ocean adventure with a starfish, a crab and an octopus.
- Make a "freeze" game: play music, and when it stops, the leader calls an animal.
- Do a partner pose, like two trees holding hands.

## Stuck?
- Balance is hard: hold the wall or a chair with one hand.
- Your child gets silly and wild: use the bell to mean "freeze and breathe."

## Show it off
Teach a short animal yoga class to the family before bed, with your child as the teacher.

## Clean up
Roll up the mat and keep the pose cards in an envelope for another day.

## For the grown-up
This builds balance, strength, body awareness and calm breathing. Leading the class builds confidence and clear speaking. Keep the space clear of sharp corners. **Ask at dinner:** "Which animal pose was the hardest to hold, and which was the most fun?"
