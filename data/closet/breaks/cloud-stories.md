title: Cloud Stories
grade: 1
order: 29
kind: Story
time: 45-75 min
where: Either
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: a blanket or towel to lie on, blue paper or white paper with blue crayon, white crayons or chalk, cotton balls, glue, a pencil
nice: a window with a view of the sky, a clipboard

## The big idea
Read aloud: "Look up! The sky is a giant picture book. One cloud looks like a dragon, another like an ice cream cone. They change shape while you watch! Let's find pictures in the clouds and turn them into stories."

## Goals
- Your child finds at least 5 cloud shapes and tells what they look like.
- Your child makes a cloud picture book and tells a story with it.

## Objectives
- Your child can describe shapes using words like puffy, long, round, wispy.
- Your child can imagine a story from a shape they see.
- Your child can tell a story with a beginning, middle and end.

## Get ready
Pick a day with some clouds. Lay a blanket outside on grass or on a balcony, or set up by a big window. Set out paper and cotton balls inside for later.

## Steps
1. **Cloud watching** (15 minutes). Lie on your backs and look up. Ask: "What do you see?" Point out shapes and take turns. "That one looks like a sheep with a hat!"
2. **Watch it change.** Pick one cloud and watch it for a minute. Did it get bigger? Did it turn into something else?
3. **Remember your favorites.** Your child picks 3 favorite cloud shapes to make into pictures.
4. **Make cloud pictures** (20 minutes). Inside, your child pulls cotton balls apart and glues them on blue paper in the shape of each cloud. Add details with crayon: eyes, a tail, a hat.
5. **Tell the story.** Line up the 3 pictures. Ask: "How are these clouds connected? What happens to them?" Example: "A cloud dragon flew to visit a cloud rabbit, but a cloud storm came..."
6. **Write it.** Your child tells the story and you write one sentence under each picture.
7. Staple or tape the pages together into a "Cloud Book" with a cover.

## Make it yours
- Make a cloud that rains on someone in the story, and what happens next.
- Make one cloud picture into a puppet on a straw for a cloud puppet show.
- Draw a cloud shape for someone else and see if they can guess what it is.

## Stuck?
- It's a clear blue sky: lie down anyway and look at tree shapes, or do it on another day. You can also make cloud shapes from cotton balls and guess each other's.
- Your child sees "just clouds": start with a fun one ("I see a giant shoe!") to get them going.

## Show it off
Read the Cloud Book to the family. Then go look at the sky together and find new shapes.

## Clean up
Shake out the blanket. Your child picks up cotton bits and puts the glue away.

## For the grown-up
This builds imagination, describing words, storytelling and early weather science (clouds change shape as wind moves them). Never look straight at the sun. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the most amazing thing you saw in the clouds?"
