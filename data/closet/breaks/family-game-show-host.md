title: Family Game Show Host
grade: 5
order: 20
kind: Pretend
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: Nearby
players: 2+
materials: paper, index cards or paper squares, a pen, markers, two pot lids and spoons for buzzers, a timer or clock, a hairbrush or spoon for a microphone, a "prize" (a homemade trophy or a drawing)
nice: a sparkly shirt or a jacket for the host, a whiteboard or big paper for the scoreboard

## The big idea
Lights! Music! You are the host of the hottest show in your home! Contestants will race to buzz in, guess answers, and win the grand prize. Your job is to write the questions, build the set, and keep the energy high.

## Goals
- You create a game show with three rounds and a final round.
- You host it for at least two players, like family after work or a friend.

## Objectives
- I can write clear questions with one right answer.
- I can keep score fairly and follow my own rules.
- I can speak loudly and clearly in front of an audience.

## Get ready
Pick a name for your show, like "Know Your House!" or "Brain Blast." Set two chairs side by side for the contestants. Put a pot lid and spoon on each chair.

## Steps
1. Plan three rounds (15 minutes). Round 1: Family Facts ("What color is Grandma's front door?"). Round 2: Guess the Sound. Round 3: Speed Round.
2. Write 5 questions for each round on cards. Put the answers on the back.
3. Plan round 2. Hide behind the couch and make a sound: crinkling foil, pouring water, a zipper. Contestants guess.
4. Write a final round question. Make it worth double points.
5. Build the set. Draw a big scoreboard. Make a sign with the show name.
6. Practice your host lines. "Welcome back to ___!" "Ooh, so close!" "Let's go to the final round!"
7. Host the show (30 minutes). Read each question. Whoever bangs the lid first answers. Keep score on the board.

## Make it yours
- Add a "steal" rule: a wrong answer lets the other player try.
- Make one round about your home's strange facts, like how many spoons are in the drawer.
- Do a commercial break. Act out an ad for a made-up product, like "Self-Folding Socks."

## Stuck?
- Players tie: use a tiebreaker question with a number answer. Closest wins.
- Questions are too hard: give three answer choices.
- Only one contestant: let them play against the clock. Beat 10 points in 3 minutes to win.

## Show it off
That is the show! Hand out the prize, take a bow, and say your sign-off line.

## Clean up
Put the lids and spoons back. Keep your question cards for the next episode.

## For the grown-up
This builds speaking skills, writing clear questions, fair scoring, and confidence in front of people. It makes a great after-dinner game; your child does the hosting, you just play. **Ask at dinner (or after the show):** "What was the hardest part about being the host?"
