title: Stranded on Couch Island
grade: 4
order: 24
kind: Pretend
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: On your own
players: 1+
materials: couch cushions, blankets, pillows, a laundry basket, paper, markers, tape, a flashlight, stuffed animals, food from the kitchen (ask first)
nice: a bandana, a spoon for a shovel, a cardboard tube for a telescope

## The big idea
Your ship crashed! You're stranded on a desert island in the middle of the living room. The floor is the ocean. Build a shelter, find food, make a signal, and survive until rescue.

## Goals
- You build a shelter and a survival camp on the couch island.
- You write a survival journal with at least 3 entries.

## Objectives
- I can plan what I need to survive.
- I can solve problems with what I have.
- I can write a journal from a character's point of view.

## Get ready
Ask permission to use the couch and cushions. Clear the floor around it. The floor is now the ocean!

## Steps
1. Write your castaway name and how you crashed. "I am Captain Ruby. A storm smashed my ship."
2. Build a shelter (15 minutes). Use cushions, blankets and pillows. It must be big enough to sit inside.
3. Make a supply list. What do you need? Water, food, light, a signal.
4. "Find" supplies. Use only what's in the room or one trip to the kitchen.
5. Make an SOS signal. Write HELP in giant letters on paper and tape it up.
6. Make a map of your island. Draw a coconut grove, a lagoon and a secret cave.
7. Write 3 journal entries. Day 1, Day 2, Day 3. What happened each day?
8. Plan your rescue. A ship? A plane? A giant whale?

## Make it yours
- Add a shark in the "ocean" that you must sneak past.
- Make friends with a stuffed animal native, and invent their language.
- Build a raft from a basket and paddle to "the mainland."

## Stuck?
- The shelter keeps falling: lean cushions against each other in a triangle.
- You're bored: invent a storm that wrecks part of camp. Rebuild!
- No room for a big shelter: use a corner of the room and a chair.

## Show it off
Give the family a "rescue." They row over and you tell your survival story.

## Clean up
Put cushions back. Fold blankets. Put food away.

## For the grown-up
This builds problem solving, planning, journal writing and pretend play. Please let your child use the couch for an hour, and check what food they may take. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the hardest part about surviving on the island?"
