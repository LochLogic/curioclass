title: Pace and Compass Treasure Hunt
grade: 4
order: 13
kind: Outside
time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Low
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: paper, a pencil, a small "treasure" (a toy or a note), a container, sticky notes or chalk to mark spots
nice: a real compass, a ruler, a grown-up's phone compass

## The big idea
Pirates hid treasure with secret directions. "Ten paces north, then five paces east." Create your own treasure map using paces and directions. Then see if someone can find it.

## Goals
- You hide a treasure and write directions to find it.
- Someone follows your directions to the right spot.

## Objectives
- I can measure distance in paces, or steps.
- I can use north, south, east and west.
- I can write directions in the right order.

## Get ready
Find north. The sun rises in the east and sets in the west. In the morning, face the sun. North is on your left. Mark "N" with chalk or a sticky note.

## Steps
1. Measure your pace. Walk 10 normal steps. Measure the distance with a ruler, or just count.
2. Pick a starting spot, like a tree, a door or a chair.
3. Hide the treasure. Put it in a container. Hide it in a safe spot.
4. Walk back from the treasure to the start. Count paces and directions as you go.
5. Write the directions in order. Example: "Start at the back door. Walk 8 paces north. Turn east. Walk 5 paces."
6. Draw a map of the area. Add an X where the treasure is. Fold the map.
7. Test it. Follow your own directions from the start. Do you land on the X?
8. Give the directions (not the map) to someone. Can they find it?

## Make it yours
- Add a riddle at every stop that tells the next step.
- Make an indoor version with chairs and rooms.
- Make a fake map with a wrong X to trick pirates.

## Stuck?
- Paces don't match: grown-ups take bigger steps. Write "kid paces" on the map.
- North is confusing: draw a big arrow on paper and lay it on the ground.
- The treasure gets lost: write down where you hid it right away.

## Show it off
Challenge a grown-up after dinner. Time how long they take to find it.

## Clean up
Pick up all markers. Bring the treasure in.

## For the grown-up
This builds measurement, directions, map skills and writing steps in order. Stay nearby outdoors; go along if the hunt goes past your yard. **Ask at dinner:** "How did you figure out which way was north?"
