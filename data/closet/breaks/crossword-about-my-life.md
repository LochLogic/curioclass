title: Crossword About My Life
grade: 8
order: 12
kind: Puzzle
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 1
materials: graph paper (or rule your own 1 cm grid), a pencil, an eraser, a pen, a ruler, scrap paper
nice: a black marker for blank squares, a second copy for the answer key

## The big idea
Crossword writers are some of the sneakiest people in the world, hiding answers in clever clues. Make a crossword where every answer is about your life: your pet's name, your favorite food, the street you live on. Then see how well your family really knows you.

## Goals
- A finished crossword with at least 15 answers about your life.
- A clean puzzle copy and an answer key.

## Objectives
- I can fit words together in a grid so they cross correctly.
- I can write clues that are fair but clever.
- I can check my puzzle for errors before someone solves it.

## Get ready
On scrap paper, list 25 words or names from your life: family names, pets, foods, places, hobbies, favorite things, inside jokes. Mix long and short words.

## Steps
1. **Pick a grid.** Draw a 13 by 13 grid of 1 cm squares. Smaller is fine for a first puzzle: 11 by 11.
2. **Place the longest word.** Put your longest answer across the middle row.
3. **Cross it (20 minutes).** Find words that share a letter with your first word and place them going down through that letter. Then cross those with more across words. Every word must connect to the grid.
4. **Fill in blanks.** Color in any square that's not part of a word in black.
5. **Number the squares.** Starting at the top left and going left to right, row by row, number every square that starts an across or down word.
6. **Write clues (20 minutes).** Mix easy and clever ones. Easy: "My dog's name (4)." Clever: "What I'd eat for every meal if I could, and it's round (5)" for PIZZA. Inside jokes count: "What Dad called the minivan."
7. **Make the clean copy.** Copy the grid with numbers and black squares only, no answers. Write Across and Down clue lists under it. Keep the filled one as the key.

## Make it yours
- Make it about someone else: a crossword gift for a grandparent, friend or sibling.
- Make a themed puzzle where the three longest answers are connected, like three favorite books.
- Make a cryptic clue or two: a clue with a hidden word or wordplay.

## Stuck?
- Words won't cross: swap in a shorter word, or pick a different crossing letter.
- Too many black squares: try to add short words (2 or 3 letters) in the gaps.
- Clues are too hard: test them on yourself the next day. If you can't get it, nobody can.

## Show it off
Give a copy to each person at home and see who solves it first.

## Clean up
Keep the answer key safe from cheaters.

## For the grown-up
This builds vocabulary, spelling, spatial reasoning and writing clever clues, which is a real writing skill. **Ask at dinner:** "What's the hardest clue in your crossword?"
