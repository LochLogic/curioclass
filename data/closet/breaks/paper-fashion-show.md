title: Paper Fashion Show
grade: 2
order: 19
kind: Art
time: 90-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: newspaper or wrapping paper, paper bags, tape, scissors, markers or crayons, string or yarn, a stuffed animal or doll to be a model, a full-length or hand mirror
nice: tissue paper, aluminum foil, ribbon, buttons for decoration, music for the runway

## The big idea
Read aloud: "The Paper Fashion Show is tonight, and the designers need you! Every outfit must be made from paper. Will your style be fancy, silly, or from the future? Get ready to design, build and walk the runway."

## Goals
- At least two paper outfits, one for your child and one for a stuffed model.
- A runway show with a name for each outfit.

## Objectives
- Your child can design an outfit on paper first, then build it.
- Your child can measure and cut paper to fit.
- Your child can describe their design using color and shape words.

## Get ready
Clear a large floor space. Lay out newspaper, paper bags and tape. Choose a hallway or path to be the runway.

## Steps
1. **Sketch the look** (10 minutes). Your child draws 2 outfits on paper: one for themselves, one for a stuffed animal. Label each part: "crown," "skirt," "cape."
2. **Measure.** Hold paper up to your child's body (or the toy's) and mark where to cut. You cut anything near clothes or skin.
3. **Build** (40 minutes). Easy pieces: a paper skirt made by taping a strip around the waist and adding hanging strips, a vest from a paper bag (cut up the front and arm holes), a cape from a wide sheet, a hat from a cone of rolled paper.
4. **Add details.** Fringe on the edges, foil buttons, paper flowers, drawn patterns. Your child picks the style.
5. **Name each outfit.** Your child writes a card for each one: "The Moon Explorer. Made of foil and paper." Short descriptions.
6. **Dress the model.** Put the stuffed animal's outfit on.
7. **Practice the walk** (10 minutes). Walk to the end of the runway, pause, turn, walk back.

## Make it yours
- Pick a theme for the whole show: underwater, outer space, or royal palace.
- Make an outfit that has a secret, like a cape that turns inside out to a new color.
- Design one outfit for a grown-up at home and surprise them.

## Stuck?
- Tape won't hold paper tight: use more tape, or fold the edges over before taping.
- Paper rips: use two layers taped together.

## Show it off
Hold the fashion show when the family gets home. Your child walks the runway while you read their outfit cards like an announcer.

## Clean up
Fold outfits flat to keep, recycle scraps, and return scissors and tape.

## For the grown-up
This builds design thinking, measuring, cutting and labeling. Walking the runway builds confidence. Keep scissors away from clothes being worn and do that cutting yourself. **Ask at dinner:** "If you could make one outfit out of anything at all, what would you use?"
