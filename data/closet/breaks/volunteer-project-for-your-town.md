title: Volunteer Project for Your Town
grade: 12
order: 10
kind: Kindness
time: 90-120 min
where: Outdoors
mess: Medium
grownup: On your own
players: 1+
materials: gloves (garden or kitchen), trash bags, a bucket, paper and pen, water
nice: a grabber tool, a friend or family member, a sign you make yourself

## The big idea
Real change in a town starts small: one person cleans one corner, and others notice. You are going to find a spot
near home that needs care, a littered path, a messy bus stop, a neglected planter, and fix it in one afternoon. Then
you will make it easy for others to keep it that way.

## Goals
- One spot near home cleaned up and noticeably better.
- A small sign or plan that helps keep it that way.

## Objectives
- I can identify a real need in my community.
- I can plan and carry out a small project from start to finish.
- I can measure the impact of what I did.

## Get ready
Tell a grown-up exactly where you are going and when you will be back. Pick one spot close to home. Gather gloves,
bags and water.

## Steps
1. Scout the spot (10 minutes). Take a "before" sketch or photo. Note what the problems are.
2. Plan the work: what can you fix in an hour? Picking up trash, sweeping, pulling weeds, clearing a drain.
3. Do the work (45 minutes). Wear gloves. Skip anything sharp, like broken glass or needles, and tell an adult instead.
4. Sort what you collected: trash, recycling and things to report.
5. Measure the impact: count the bags, guess the weight, compare before and after.
6. Make a sign or note for the spot: "This corner was cleaned on [date]. Please help keep it clean."
7. Write a plan to come back: once a week, once a month.

## Make it yours
- Organize a cleanup with friends and give everyone a role.
- Make it a game: points for the strangest item found.
- Adopt the spot: plant flowers or paint a new trash can label (with permission).

## Stuck?
- You cannot find a spot: start with your own street or building entrance.
- It is too big: pick the smallest piece you can finish today.

## Show it off
Show the family before and after, and invite them to come see the spot.

## Clean up
Bag and dispose of trash and recycling, wash your gloves, and wash your hands well.

## For the grown-up
This builds civic responsibility, planning and follow-through. Make sure they know to never touch needles, broken
glass or anything hazardous, and to stay where they said they would be. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the strangest
thing you found?"
