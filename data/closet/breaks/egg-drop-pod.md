title: Egg Drop Pod
grade: 3
order: 21
kind: Science
time: 60-120 min
where: Either
mess: High
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: 2 or 3 raw eggs (ask first), small boxes, paper, cotton balls or socks, straws, rubber bands, tape, plastic bags, a trash bag or tray to drop onto
nice: a paper cup, string for a parachute, a step stool for a grown-up

## The big idea
An astronaut egg is coming back from space. It has to land without cracking! Design a pod to keep it safe. Then drop it and see if your egg survives.

## Goals
- You build a pod that protects an egg from a fall.
- You test, change and test your design again.

## Objectives
- I can design a solution to a problem.
- I can test my design and say what worked.
- I can improve a design after a fail.

## Get ready
Ask how many eggs you may use. Lay a trash bag or tray on the floor or work outside on grass. Gather your soft stuff.

## Steps
1. Plan (10 minutes). Draw 2 pod ideas. Will it be soft? Will it have a parachute? Will it be a box?
2. Pick one. Build it (20 minutes). Pack soft stuff all around the egg. Leave no gaps.
3. Close it so the egg can't fall out.
4. Test drop 1. Stand up and hold the pod at shoulder height. Let go over the tray.
5. Open it. Is the egg OK?
6. Test drop 2. Hold your arm straight up over your head. Drop it again.
7. Big drop. A grown-up stands on a step stool and drops it.
8. Did it crack? Write what went wrong. Change one thing and try again.

## Make it yours
- Build a pod with no tape, just paper and folds.
- Add a parachute from a plastic bag and 4 strings.
- Build a pod for two eggs: an astronaut and a co-pilot.

## Stuck?
- The egg cracked right away: add more soft stuff on the bottom.
- The pod tips over: add weight to the bottom, like a sock ball.
- Out of eggs: test with a small zip bag of water, closed tight.

## Show it off
Hold a "landing show" for your family. Count down from 10 before the big drop.

## Clean up
Clean any broken egg with a paper towel and soap. Wash your hands with soap after touching raw egg.

## For the grown-up
This builds engineering design and the idea of force and cushioning. Do the high drops yourself; children stay on the floor, not on chairs or stairs. Raw egg can carry germs, so hands get washed after. **Ask at dinner:** "What did you change after your first drop?"
