title: Ramp Race Lab
grade: 2
order: 22
kind: Science
time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Low
grownup: With you
players: 1+
materials: a long piece of flat cardboard or a board (about 2 to 3 feet), books or blocks to stack, 3 or 4 toy cars or round things (a ball, a can, a spool), a ruler or tape measure, paper and a pencil, masking tape
nice: a towel, sandpaper or foil to change the ramp surface, a timer

## The big idea
Read aloud: "The great Ramp Race is on! Which car will roll the farthest? Does a steeper ramp make it faster? Today you are the race scientist. You will test, measure and find the champion."

## Goals
- A ramp your child built and changed to test different heights.
- A chart that shows how far each car rolled.

## Objectives
- Your child can make a fair test by changing only one thing at a time.
- Your child can measure distance with a ruler or by counting steps.
- Your child can say which ramp height made cars go farthest and guess why.

## Get ready
Find a long, clear floor, a hallway, or a driveway or sidewalk outside. Lean the cardboard on one stack of books. Tape a start line at the top of the ramp. On a paper, draw a simple chart with car names down the side.

## Steps
1. **Pick racers.** Your child chooses 3 or 4 things to roll and names each one: "Red Rocket," "Soup Can."
2. **First race.** Ramp on 2 books. Let each car go from the start line. Don't push! Mark where each stops with a small piece of tape.
3. **Measure** (10 minutes). Use a ruler, or count heel-to-toe steps from the ramp to each car. Your child writes each number on the chart.
4. **Make it steeper.** Add 2 more books. Ask: "Will they go farther or not as far?" Test and measure.
5. **Test steeper again.** Add 2 more books. Test again. Look at the chart: what's the pattern?
6. **Change the surface** (15 minutes). Lay a towel on the floor or foil on the ramp. Ask your child to guess before each test. Write the results.
7. **Crown a champion.** Your child picks the winning car and the best ramp, and writes it at the bottom: "Champion: Red Rocket. Best ramp: 6 books."

## Make it yours
- Build a jump at the end of the ramp with a short book and see which car flies farthest.
- Add a curve to the ramp using a cereal box wall so cars must turn.
- Race two ramps side by side, one steep and one low.

## Stuck?
- Cars fall off the side: fold up the edges of the cardboard about an inch to make walls.
- Results jump around: run each race 3 times and use the middle number.

## Show it off
Hold a championship race for the family. Your child explains their chart and predicts the winner.

## Clean up
Pull up the tape, return the books, and flatten the ramp to save.

## For the grown-up
This is a real fair test with measuring and recording. Kids see how height and surface change speed, early ideas about gravity and friction. If outside, stay off any street and away from cars. **Ask at dinner:** "What happened when you made the ramp steeper?"
