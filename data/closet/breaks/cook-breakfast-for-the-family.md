grade: 6
order: 6
kind: Kitchen
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: eggs, butter or oil, salt and pepper, bread, fruit (bananas, apples, oranges, whatever you have), a frying pan, a spatula, a bowl, a fork, a toaster, a cutting board, a butter knife, plates, a paper and pencil
nice: cheese, milk, cinnamon, a kitchen timer, a cloth napkin for a fancy fold

## The big idea
Cooking for other people is a power move. Tonight (or tomorrow morning, or a weekend "breakfast for dinner") you run the kitchen. Plan the menu, time it so everything is hot at once, and serve a real meal to your family like a pro.

## Goals
- You cook and serve a complete breakfast: eggs, toast, and a fruit plate.
- Everything lands on the table warm at the same time.

## Objectives
- I can follow kitchen safety rules for heat and knives.
- I can cook scrambled eggs on the stove until they're done but still soft.
- I can plan a cooking timeline by working backward from serving time.

## Get ready
Get a grown-up's okay to use the stove and check for allergies. Wash your hands. Read through every step before you turn anything on. Set the table first so you're not scrambling later.

## Steps
1. Write your menu and a timeline. Work backward from "Serve at 6:30": eggs take 5 minutes, toast takes 3, fruit plate takes 10 and can wait. So start the fruit first.
2. Make the fruit plate. Slice bananas with a butter knife. Peel oranges and separate segments. Arrange it in a pattern, like a sun or a rainbow.
3. Crack the eggs (2 per person) into a bowl. Fish out any shell with a bigger piece of shell. Add a pinch of salt and a splash of milk if you have it. Whisk with a fork until it's all one yellow.
4. Start the toast so it finishes about when the eggs do.
5. Heat the pan on medium-low with a little butter. When the butter foams, pour in the eggs.
6. Wait 20 seconds, then gently push the eggs from the edges to the center with the spatula. Keep going slowly. Take the pan off the heat while they still look a little wet; they finish cooking on the plate.
7. Plate everything, add your own touch (cheese on the eggs, cinnamon on the toast), and call everyone in.

## Make it yours
- Make it a themed breakfast: "Diner Morning" with a handwritten menu and you as the server taking orders.
- Invent a signature dish name for your eggs, like "Cloud Eggs Supreme," and serve it with a story about the "secret recipe."
- Try a second egg style once you've nailed scrambled: a fried egg, or an omelet with cheese folded in.

## Stuck?
- The eggs are rubbery: the heat was too high or they cooked too long. Next time, lower heat and pull them sooner.
- Something finished early: put it on a plate in the turned-off microwave to keep warm (don't turn it on).
- Shell in the bowl: wet your fingers or use a bigger piece of shell to scoop it out.

## Show it off
Serve it like a restaurant: "Good evening, tonight's special is..." Ask everyone to rate the meal (gently).

## Clean up
Cleanup is half of cooking. Turn off the stove and check it twice. Wash the pan, wipe the counter, and load or wash the dishes.

## For the grown-up
This builds real kitchen independence, planning, and timing, plus a chance for your child to do something kind for the family. Please give the okay for the stove, check in during the cooking, and check for food allergies. Teach the "pan handle turned inward" rule if they don't know it. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the hardest part of getting everything done at the same time?"
