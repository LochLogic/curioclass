title: Optical Illusion Art
grade: 5
order: 23
kind: Art
time: 60-90 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 1
materials: white paper, a pencil, an eraser, a ruler, black marker, colored markers or pencils
nice: a compass or round lid for circles, a light pad or a sunny window for tracing

## The big idea
Some drawings trick your brain. Flat lines look like they bulge out. A triangle seems to twist in a way that's impossible. Today you become an illusion artist and make art that fools your family's eyes.

## Goals
- You make at least two optical illusion drawings.
- You fool at least one person with your art.

## Objectives
- I can use lines and shading to make a flat drawing look 3D.
- I can follow careful steps to draw an impossible shape.
- I can explain how an illusion tricks the brain.

## Get ready
Clear a space. Sharpen your pencil. Test that your markers work. Put your ruler and eraser in reach.

## Steps
1. **Illusion 1: the 3D hand.** Trace your hand lightly in pencil in the middle of the page.
2. With a ruler, draw straight lines across the page, 1 cm apart. Stop each line at the hand's edge.
3. Inside the hand, connect the lines with curved bumps that arch up. Now it looks like the hand pops off the page.
4. Go over the lines in colors. Try a rainbow, one color per line. Erase the pencil hand outline.
5. **Illusion 2: the impossible triangle (20 minutes).** Draw a triangle. Then draw a second triangle a bit smaller inside it. Connect them with short lines at the corners, so each corner bends the "wrong" way. It takes a few tries.
6. Shade one side of each bar dark, one medium, and one light. That makes it look solid.
7. **Illusion 3: your choice.** Draw a checkerboard and bend the lines. Or draw circles inside circles, alternating black and white, and watch them buzz.

## Make it yours
- Draw an "impossible staircase" that goes up forever.
- Combine two illusions in one picture, like a 3D hand holding an impossible triangle.
- Make a whole "Museum of Illusions" with a title card for each piece.

## Stuck?
- The hand doesn't look 3D: make the bumps taller in the middle of the hand.
- The impossible triangle won't work: draw it very lightly first and erase parts that don't fit.
- Lines get crooked: hold the ruler down firmly with your whole hand.

## Show it off
Set up a gallery. Ask your family: "Does this look flat or does it stick out?" Then tell them the secret.

## Clean up
Put the markers back with the caps on. Recycle scraps.

## For the grown-up
This mixes art with how vision works: the brain fills in depth and shape from clues like curves and shading. It also builds careful measuring and patience. **Ask at dinner:** "How did you trick our brains with just a pencil?"
