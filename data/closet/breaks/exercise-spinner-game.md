title: Exercise Spinner Game
grade: 3
order: 18
kind: Move
time: 60-90 min
where: Either
mess: Low
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: a paper plate or a cardboard circle, a paper clip, a pencil, markers, a ruler
nice: a timer, a pushpin, a second plate for a second spinner

## The big idea
Spin the arrow. It lands on "Frog Jumps!" Ten jumps, go! Make a spinner game that turns exercise into a wild surprise. Who can last the longest?

## Goals
- You make a spinner that really spins.
- You play at least 3 rounds of your game.

## Objectives
- I can divide a circle into equal parts.
- I can invent moves and give them silly names.
- I can count reps and keep a score.

## Get ready
Clear a space as big as a car parking spot. Move breakable things away.

## Steps
1. Draw a line across the plate through the middle. Draw another line to make 4 parts. Draw 2 more to make 8.
2. Write a move in each part. Give it a silly name. Examples: "Frog Jumps," "Crab Walk," "Starfish Jacks," "Flamingo Stand," "Rocket Squats."
3. Write a number next to each move, like 10 or 20 seconds.
4. Make the spinner. Put a paper clip at the center. Hold it there with a pencil tip. Flick the clip!
5. Make a second spinner for "how": "Slow motion," "As a robot," "Super fast," "Backward."
6. Play (30 minutes). Spin both. Do the move. Mark a tally for each round you finish.
7. Make a "Wild Card" rule. If it lands on a line, you pick any move.

## Make it yours
- Make it a story: you are training to be an animal ninja.
- Play with family. Everyone does the move, and the spinner says who picks next.
- Add "rest" spaces with a funny job, like "tell a joke."

## Stuck?
- The clip won't spin: push the pencil straight down and flick harder.
- The moves are too hard: make the number smaller.
- You get bored: add music and spin every time the song changes.

## Show it off
Challenge a grown-up after work. Can they do 5 spins in a row?

## Clean up
Put the spinner on a shelf. Put the room back how it was.

## For the grown-up
This builds fitness, counting and dividing a circle (a first look at fractions). Make sure the space is clear and the floor is not slippery; socks on hard floors can slide. **Ask at dinner:** "What was the hardest move you invented?"
