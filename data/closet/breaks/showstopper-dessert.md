grade: 12
order: 18
kind: Kitchen
time: 90-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: High
grownup: On your own
players: 1+
materials: baking ingredients you have (flour, sugar, eggs, butter, milk, baking powder, cocoa or vanilla), mixing bowls, measuring cups and spoons, a whisk, a baking pan, an oven, a cooling rack or plate
nice: fruit, whipped cream, chocolate, a piping bag or a zip bag with a corner snipped, edible flowers

## The big idea
Pastry chefs build desserts that make a whole room go quiet when they land on the table: height, layers, a sauce that
pours just right. You will build your own showstopper from basic pantry baking: a cake, a crisp, or a stack of crepes,
then plate and finish it like a pastry chef with layers, contrast and one dramatic flourish.

## Goals
- A finished dessert with at least three components (a base, a filling or sauce, and a topping).
- A plated presentation that gets an audible reaction.

## Objectives
- I can follow and adapt a baking recipe with accurate measuring.
- I can combine textures and flavors for contrast.
- I can finish and plate a dessert with care.

## Get ready
Ask permission for the oven. Check allergies. Read the whole plan first. Preheat the oven if baking.

## Steps
1. Choose a base (10 minutes): a simple sponge cake, crepes, a crumble topping or shortbread.
2. Choose two other parts: a **cream** (whipped cream, yogurt, custard), a **fruit or sauce** (cooked berries,
   caramel, chocolate sauce), a **crunch** (toasted oats, crushed cookies).
3. Measure carefully. In baking, flour is spooned into the cup and leveled, not scooped.
4. Make the base (40 minutes). While it bakes or cools, make the sauce and cream.
5. Cool completely before assembling. Warm cake melts cream.
6. Assemble with height: stack, layer or pile. Use the contrast of smooth and crunchy, sweet and sharp.
7. Finish with one dramatic touch: a pour of sauce at the table, a dusting of cocoa, a single piece of fruit on top.

## Make it yours
- Make a dessert inspired by a place or a memory: a beach (crumble sand, blue cream).
- Make a dessert that looks like something else: a potted plant, a burger, a planet.
- Make individual desserts in glasses, like a layered parfait.

## Stuck?
- The cake sinks: the oven door was opened too early or there was too much baking powder. Turn it into a trifle.
- The cream will not whip: chill the bowl and cream for 15 minutes and whisk hard.

## Show it off
Bring it to the table dramatically, lights dimmed, with a name and a two-line description.

## Clean up
Wash everything, wipe the counters, and turn off the oven.

## For the grown-up
This builds baking chemistry, measurement and presentation. Oven use is with your permission. Check allergies (eggs,
dairy, wheat, nuts) before serving. **Ask at dinner:** "What makes this dessert a showstopper?"
