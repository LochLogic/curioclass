kind: Outside
time: 60-120 min
where: Outdoors
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: a cardboard box or plastic bottle cut open, cardboard tubes, sticks, dry leaves, pine cones, bark, straw or dry grass, string, tape
nice: a magnifying glass, a notebook, an old flower pot, a clay pot

## The big idea
Bugs need homes too! Ladybugs want a cozy spot. Beetles want damp bark. Build a hotel with rooms for different bugs. Then check in on your guests.

## Goals
- You build a bug hotel with at least 4 kinds of rooms.
- You set it outside and check for guests.

## Objectives
- I can tell what different bugs need to live.
- I can build a home with many small spaces.
- I can watch bugs without hurting them.

## Get ready
With a grown-up, go outside. Collect sticks, leaves, bark and pine cones. Lay them out in piles.

## Steps
1. Pick your hotel frame. A box on its side works. So does a bottle with the top cut off.
2. Make rooms. Stack cardboard tubes in one part. Stuff bark in another. Push leaves in a third.
3. Pack each room tight. Bugs like small spaces with no breeze.
4. Make a damp room with bark and moss for beetles and wood lice.
5. Make a dry room with straw for ladybugs.
6. Make a hotel sign. Name it, like "The Buggy Inn."
7. Place it outside (15 minutes). A quiet spot by a wall, bush or fence. Put a rock on top so it won't blow away.
8. Check it in a day or two. Draw who you see.

## Make it yours
- Make a "price list" for bug guests: one aphid a night!
- Build a fancy penthouse on top for butterflies.
- No yard? Make a small one in a can on a balcony with sticks and leaves.

## Stuck?
- Rooms fall out: pack them tighter or tie string around the box.
- No guests yet: wait a few days. Bugs are shy!
- Cardboard gets soggy: use a plastic bottle or a clay pot instead.

## Show it off
Take your family on a tour. Show each room and who it's for.

## Clean up
Pick up leftover sticks and scraps. Wash your hands.

## For the grown-up
This builds life science (habitats and needs) and gentle respect for small animals. Stay nearby outdoors. Remind your child to watch bugs, not grab them, and to wash hands after. If you cut a bottle, do the cutting yourself. **Ask at dinner:** "Which room do you think will get the first guest?"
