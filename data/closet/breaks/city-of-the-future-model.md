title: City of the Future Model
grade: 5
order: 29
kind: Build
time: 90-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Medium
grownup: Nearby
players: 1+
materials: a big piece of cardboard for the base (a box side is great), recycling (boxes, tubes, bottle caps, cups), paper, tape, glue, scissors, markers, a ruler
nice: foil, string, a flashlight for night mode, toy cars or figures

## The big idea
It is 100 years from now. Cities float, fly, or grow on trees. People need clean energy, food, and ways to get around. You are the city planner. Design and build a model city that solves a real problem in a brand new way.

## Goals
- You build a model city with at least six buildings or places.
- Your city solves one big problem, like traffic, pollution, or floods.

## Objectives
- I can plan a city with homes, work, transport, and green space.
- I can build three-dimensional models from flat materials.
- I can explain how my design solves a problem.

## Get ready
Clear a big space on the floor or table. Sort your recycling into piles: tall things, flat things, round things. Lay the base flat.

## Steps
1. Pick your problem. Too many cars? Hot summers? Rising water? Write it at the top of a planning page.
2. Pick your big idea for solving it. Example: the city floats on rafts, so it rises with the water.
3. Draw a map on the base in pencil (10 minutes). Mark where homes, parks, shops, and energy go. Leave room for paths.
4. Build homes and buildings from boxes and tubes. Make at least one that is shaped strangely for a reason.
5. Build transport (20 minutes). Zip lines from string? Moving sidewalks from paper strips? A tube train?
6. Add energy and green space. Foil solar panels on roofs. Paper trees on every corner. A rooftop garden.
7. Label each part with a small sign. Write a one-line name for your city.

## Make it yours
- Add a hidden underground level, drawn on paper below the base.
- Make a city for animals and people to share.
- Design one invention that only exists in your city, like a trash-eating robot.

## Stuck?
- Buildings tip over: tape a coin or bottle cap inside the bottom to make them heavier.
- The base feels empty: add roads, rivers, and parks first. Then fill in buildings.
- You can't pick a problem: choose the one that bugs you most where you live.

## Show it off
Give a city tour like a mayor. Explain the problem, your solution, and the strangest building.

## Clean up
Put leftover recycling back in the bin. Keep the model if you have room, or take a photo for the family before recycling it.

## For the grown-up
This builds engineering, social studies (how cities work), and problem solving. Glue and tape will be everywhere for a while; a big flattened box under the work area helps. **Ask at dinner:** "What problem does your future city solve better than our real one?"
