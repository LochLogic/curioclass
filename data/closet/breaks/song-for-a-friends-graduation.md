title: Song for a Friend's Graduation
grade: 12
order: 23
kind: Music
time: 75-120 min
where: Indoors
mess: Low
grownup: On your own
players: 1
materials: paper and a pen, your voice, something to keep a beat (a table, a pot, a shaker of rice)
nice: a guitar, keyboard or other instrument, a recording device, a friend to sing harmony

## The big idea
Graduation songs are usually generic. Yours will not be. Write a song for one specific friend that captures the
moments only the two of you know: the inside jokes, the bad days, the time you got lost. It is the most personal
gift you can give, and it will be listened to for years.

## Goals
- A finished song with at least two verses and a chorus.
- A performance or recording to give your friend.

## Objectives
- I can write lyrics using specific memories and images.
- I can build a chorus that captures the main feeling.
- I can create a simple melody and rhythm.

## Get ready
Brainstorm 15 memories with your friend, from funny to serious. Write them fast without judging.

## Steps
1. Pick the feeling (5 minutes): proud, grateful, bittersweet, excited, a little scared.
2. Write the chorus first (15 minutes). Keep it short: four lines that say the main feeling in plain words.
3. Write verse one about the past: three specific memories, with names, places and details.
4. Write verse two about the future: where they are going, what you hope for them.
5. Find a melody by speaking the chorus slowly, then exaggerating the ups and downs. Repeat until it sticks.
6. Add a rhythm on the table or a pot. Pick a simple pattern: boom-tap, boom-boom-tap.
7. Rehearse three times, then perform or record it.

## Make it yours
- Write it as a duet so you and another friend can sing it together.
- Add a spoken bridge in the middle with a short message.
- Write a "remix" version that is fast and funny instead of sweet.

## Stuck?
- The lyrics are too general: replace one feeling word with a detail. Not "fun times," but "the summer we lived on gas station nachos."
- You cannot find a melody: borrow the rhythm of a song you know and invent new notes.

## Show it off
Perform it for the friend in person, or give them a handwritten lyric sheet with a recording.

## Clean up
Keep your lyrics and memory list in a safe place.

## For the grown-up
This builds songwriting, personal expression and friendship. **Ask at dinner:** "What memory made it into the song?"
