title: Make a contraction | Haz una contracción
idea: A contraction squishes two words into one. The apostrophe takes the place of missing letters. | Una contracción junta dos palabras en una. El apóstrofo ocupa el lugar de las letras que faltan.
example: do + not = don't. The o in not falls out. | do + not = don't. Se cae la o de not.

- Write: Write the [[two words]]: is not. | Escribe: Escribe las [[dos palabras]]: is not.
- Squish: Push them together and take out a [[letter]]. | Junta: Pégalas y quita una [[letra]].
- Mark: Put an [[apostrophe]] where the letter fell out: isn't. | Marca: Pon un [[apóstrofo]] donde se cayó la letra: isn't.
- Check: Read it. Does it mean the [[same]] thing? | Revisa: Léela. ¿Quiere decir lo [[mismo]]?
