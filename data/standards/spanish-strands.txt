# source: ours. A Spanish sounds and spelling strand for Levels 1 to 4, named in docs/courses/world-languages/STANDARD.md section 6 and APPROACH.md ("Accent marks, sounds and spelling are taught directly").
# basis: the sound and spelling topics the open college Spanish books cover (Lecciones de Español and Semillas, docs/reviews/world-languages-open-material-2026-11-20.md), listed from that scout and from memory; the books were not reopened for this file. The 2024 proficiency guidelines (read 2026-11-22) count pronunciation in listening and speaking and the writing system in reading and writing, which is why the strand exists; no wording comes from them.
# written: 2026-11-22. Untested: no Spanish teacher has read it, and no unit uses it yet.
# variety: Latin American default (APPROACH.md, Heritage speakers); regional sounds are taught as varieties, never errors.
# level: one sounds or spelling skill, SPA.Sounds.<n>. Each id is owned once, by the level that first teaches it (scripts/progression.py, subject spanish); later levels may carry it as ~.
# words: after each id, a tab and one line in Curio Class's own words, a verb phrase addressed to the learner.
SPA.Sounds.1	Say and hear the five Spanish vowel sounds, each one short and steady.
SPA.Sounds.2	Read a new Spanish word aloud from its spelling, letter by letter, sound by sound.
SPA.Sounds.3	Say and spell the letters h, j, ñ and v and the pair ll, which sound unlike English.
SPA.Sounds.4	Tell the single tapped r from the trilled rr, and say each one clearly.
SPA.Sounds.5	Choose c, qu, z, g, gu or j to spell a sound before each vowel.
SPA.Sounds.6	Split a word into syllables and say its stressed syllable with the right stress.
SPA.Sounds.7	Use the written accent to mark stress and tell si from sí, el from él.
SPA.Sounds.8	Write the opening ¿ and ¡ marks and the letters ñ and ü correctly.
SPA.Sounds.9	Raise or lower your voice to mark a question or a statement when speaking.
SPA.Sounds.10	Hear and copy words run together in a natural spoken phrase, as speakers link them.
SPA.Sounds.11	Recognize regional sounds, such as s, ll and z, as varieties of Spanish, not errors.
