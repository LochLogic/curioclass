# level: one indicator, coded as the guidelines print it: <domain><goal>.<stage><n>, e.g. CD1.K1. Domains CD (Career Development) and ALL (Academic and Lifelong Learning); stages K (knowledge acquisition), A (application), R (reflection). The guidelines say the stages are not tied to an age. Ids are kept as printed, with no prefix.
# note: 135 CD and ALL indicators in the guidelines (CD1 15, CD2 27, CD3 18, CD4 21, CD5 9, ALL1 24, ALL2 21). 130 are listed here: each one is owned by a career education course (Career Exploration, grade 8, or Career Pathways, grade 10). Five are left out as written only for adults already at work: CD3.R6, CD4.R7, CD5.R1, CD5.R2, CD5.R3 (reasons in docs/courses/career-education/STANDARD.md section 3).
# note: the guidelines' third domain, Social Emotional Learning (goals SEL1 to SEL4, 93 indicators), is not listed: those goals are leans on the SEL courses, not owned here (Cory, 2026-11-21; STANDARD.md section 2).
