# English language development progression: edges outside data/progressions/eld.csv

All assumes and hands_on run inside eld.csv (K to 12 in one file). The leans on ELA, math, science and social
studies rows are written in each row's cross_subject column and are not repeated here.

## Needs from earlier grades

- Newcomers in grades 3 to 12 who need phonics: ELD does not teach it (STANDARD.md section 5). They use the ELA
  K to 2 foundational rows (ela:ek-letter-sounds, ela:ek-cvc-decoding, ela:e1-short-vowels, ela:e1-long-vowels,
  ela:e2-vowel-teams), with a note on the newcomer strand, which sits beside the grade frames and is not in this file.

## Hands on to later grades

- None. Grade 12 rows hand on nothing; no ELA row names ELD in its cross_subject column (STANDARD.md section 5
  is the record).
