# ELA grades 9 to 10: edges across bands

Cross-band assumes and hands_on cells in ela-910.csv are left empty; the stitcher fills them from these lists.
Within the band, every grade 9 row hands on to its grade 10 row and both ends are written.

## Models grade 9 uses that an earlier grade must hand on

claim-evidence-reasoning, main-idea-details, narrative-arc, text-structure, figurative-language, context-clues,
word-parts, argument-map, paragraph-frame, essay-structure, writing-process, research-cycle, citation,
discussion-protocol, sentence-combining. Grade 9 introduces rhetorical-situation (e9-ri-purpose-rhetoric) unless
grade 8 already hands it on; if it does, add it to that row's model_in.

## Needs from earlier grades

- e9-rl-evidence: grade 8 citing the evidence that most strongly supports an analysis of literature (RL.8.1).
- e9-rl-theme: grade 8 theme traced through characters, setting and plot, with an objective summary (RL.8.2).
- e9-rl-complex-characters: grade 8 how dialogue or incidents propel action and reveal character (RL.8.3).
- e9-rl-word-choice-tone: grade 8 connotation, analogy and allusion and their effect on tone (RL.8.4); figures of speech and connotation among near-synonyms (L.8.5).
- e9-rl-structure-time: grade 8 comparing the structures of two texts and their effect on meaning (RL.8.5).
- e9-rl-myth-retold: grade 8 how a modern story draws on myth and renders it new (RL.8.9); judging a filmed or staged version against the script (RL.8.7).
- e9-rl-world-stories: grade 8 points of view of characters and reader, dramatic irony (RL.8.6).
- e9-rl-range-scaffolded: grade 8 reading literature at the high end of the 6-8 band independently (RL.8.10), including a play.
- e9-ri-evidence-central-idea: grade 8 strongest evidence, central idea and objective summary in nonfiction (RI.8.1, RI.8.2, RI.8.10).
- e9-ri-unfolding-ideas: grade 8 role of sentences in one paragraph and connections among ideas (RI.8.3, RI.8.5); technical meanings (RI.8.4).
- e9-ri-purpose-rhetoric: grade 8 author's point of view and response to conflicting viewpoints (RI.8.6).
- e9-ri-argument-evaluate: grade 8 delineating an argument and judging soundness and relevance (RI.8.8, SL.8.3).
- e9-ri-mediums: grade 8 advantages and limits of mediums (RI.8.7); purpose and motive behind media (SL.8.2).
- e9-w-analysis-routine: grade 8 drawing evidence from texts in writing (W.8.9) and routine writing (W.8.10).
- e9-w-informative: grade 8 informative writing with organization, transitions and formal style (W.8.2).
- e9-w-argument: grade 8 argument with acknowledged opposing claims and logical reasons (W.8.1).
- e9-w-narrative: grade 8 narrative with point of view, pacing and reflection (W.8.3).
- e9-w-process: grade 8 writing process with guidance and support (W.8.4, W.8.5, W.8.6).
- e9-w-short-research: grade 8 short research projects, credibility of sources, quoting and paraphrasing with a citation format (W.8.7, W.8.8).
- e9-sl-discussion: grade 8 following discussion rules, connecting speakers' ideas, qualifying views (SL.8.1).
- e9-sl-presentation: grade 8 presenting claims with evidence, multimedia and formal English (SL.8.4, SL.8.5, SL.8.6).
- e9-l-phrases-clauses: grade 8 verbals and active and passive voice (L.8.1).
- e9-l-punctuation: grade 8 comma, ellipsis and dash, spelling (L.8.2).
- e9-l-word-strategies: grade 8 context, Greek and Latin roots, reference works, verifying a meaning (L.8.4, L.8.6).

## Hands on to later grades

- e10-rl-evidence: thorough evidence across a whole long text, for RL.11-12.1 (where the text leaves matters uncertain).
- e10-rl-theme: two themes traced across a novel or play, for RL.11-12.2 (how themes interact and build on one another).
- e10-rl-complex-characters: character development across a whole work, for RL.11-12.3 (the author's choices in developing elements of a story).
- e10-rl-word-choice-tone: cumulative impact of word choice; figures of speech in their role, for RL.11-12.4 and L.11-12.5 (words with multiple meanings, beauty of language).
- e10-rl-structure-time: several structural choices across a long text, for RL.11-12.5 (how choices about structure contribute to meaning and aesthetic impact).
- e10-rl-world-literature: cultural point of view across world literature, for RL.11-12.6 (what is directly stated and what is meant: satire, irony).
- e10-rl-source-transformation: source and adaptation analysis and two-medium comparison, for RL.11-12.7 and RL.11-12.9 (foundational American literature).
- e10-rl-range-independent: independent reading at the high end of the 9-10 band, for RL.11-12.10.
- e10-ri-evidence-central-idea: central ideas and objective summary of long nonfiction, for RI.11-12.1, RI.11-12.2 (two or more central ideas interacting), RI.11-12.10.
- e10-ri-unfolding-ideas: comparing how authors order ideas, for RI.11-12.3 and RI.11-12.5 (evaluating the effectiveness of a structure).
- e10-ri-word-choice: register and cumulative impact, for RI.11-12.4 (how a key term is refined over a text).
- e10-ri-rhetoric: rhetoric for an audience, for RI.11-12.6 (style and content contributing to power and persuasiveness).
- e10-ri-seminal-documents: a set of seminal U.S. documents on one theme, for RI.11-12.8 and RI.11-12.9 (constitutional principles, eighteenth to twentieth century foundational documents).
- e10-ri-argument-evaluate: full evaluation of arguments and speakers, for RI.11-12.8 and SL.11-12.3 (premises, links among ideas).
- e10-ri-mediums: integrating and judging source sets across media, for RI.11-12.7 and SL.11-12.2 (informed decisions, discrepancies among data).
- e10-w-argument: claims and counterclaims weighed fairly for an audience, for W.11-12.1 (significance of claims, the full range of counterclaims).
- e10-w-informative: explanatory writing with graphics and implications, for W.11-12.2 (each new element building on the last).
- e10-w-narrative: multiple points of view and plot lines, for W.11-12.3 (tone and outcome, a particular effect).
- e10-w-process: independent revision and publishing, for W.11-12.4 to W.11-12.6 (updating in response to ongoing feedback).
- e10-w-research: a sustained research project with synthesis and citation, for W.11-12.7 and W.11-12.8 (strengths and limits of each source, avoiding overreliance on one).
- e10-w-text-analysis: the analytic essay, timed and through the process, for W.11-12.9 and W.11-12.10.
- e10-sl-seminar: student-led discussion, for SL.11-12.1 (promoting divergent and creative perspectives, resolving contradictions).
- e10-sl-presentation: research presentation with media, for SL.11-12.4 to SL.11-12.6 (distinct perspective, alternative views addressed).
- e10-l-syntax-style: parallel structure and phrase and clause choices for style, style manual, for L.11-12.1 and L.11-12.3 (usage as a matter of convention, syntax for study of complex texts).
- e10-l-punctuation: semicolon and colon in use, for L.11-12.2 (hyphenation conventions).
- e10-l-word-strategies: word families, etymology and a personal word log, for L.11-12.4 and L.11-12.6.
