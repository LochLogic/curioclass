# ELA K to 2: edges to other bands

## Needs from earlier grades

None. K to 2 is the first band. Kindergarten assumes oral language and no reading or letter knowledge.

## Hands on to later grades

Grade 3 should build on these (the standards' wording change from grade 2 to grade 3, and the base text's grade 2
Skills Block end point, read 2026-11-13).

- e2-vowel-teams: long and short vowels and all common vowel teams decoded (sound-spelling-map). Grade 3 RF.3.3 (multisyllable words, where these teams sit inside longer words).
- e2-two-syllable-words: two-syllable decoding by syllable type, including consonant-le (syllable-types). Grade 3 RF.3.3c (decode multisyllable words).
- e2-prefixes-suffixes: common prefixes and suffixes decoded on known base words (word-parts). Grade 3 RF.3.3a and RF.3.3b (most common prefixes and derivational suffixes, Latin suffixes) and L.3.4b.
- e2-tricky-spellings: inconsistent patterns and grade 2 irregular words (sound-spelling-map). Grade 3 RF.3.3d (grade 3 irregularly spelled words). The grade 3 phonics plan must start here: the base text's grade 2 ends with -ous, -cle and schwa, not with three-syllable words.
- e2-spelling: generalizing taught patterns and checking with a beginning dictionary. Grade 3 L.3.2e, L.3.2f and L.3.2g.
- e2-fluency: grades 2 to 3 band reading, accuracy and expression on rereading, self-correction after decoding. Grade 3 RF.3.4, RL.3.10 and RI.3.10 (the top of the same band).
- e2-wh-questions: who, what, where, when, why, how answered from the text. Grade 3 RL.3.1 and RI.3.1 (referring explicitly to the text as the basis for answers).
- e2-fables-folktales: fables and folktales from diverse cultures and their moral (story-map). Grade 3 RL.3.2 (explain how the message is conveyed through key details).
- e2-character-response: characters' responses to events. Grade 3 RL.3.3 (traits, motivations, feelings, and how actions drive events).
- e2-rhythm-and-meaning: sound devices that carry meaning (figurative-language). Grade 3 RL.3.4 and L.3.5a (literal and nonliteral language).
- e2-story-structure: beginning introduces, ending concludes (narrative-arc, story-map). Grade 3 RL.3.5 (chapters, scenes, stanzas, each part building on earlier ones).
- e2-points-of-view: characters' points of view, voiced in reading aloud. Grade 3 RL.3.6 (the reader's point of view against the narrator's or a character's).
- e2-images-and-text: what illustrations and diagrams add. Grade 3 RL.3.7 and RI.3.7 (maps, photographs and how illustrations create mood).
- e2-compare-texts: two versions of a story, most important points of two texts. Grade 3 RL.3.9 (stories by the same author about the same characters) and RI.3.9.
- e2-main-topic-paragraphs: main topic and paragraph focus (main-idea-details). Grade 3 RI.3.2 (main idea and how details support it).
- e2-connections-series: a series of events, ideas or steps (text-structure). Grade 3 RI.3.3 (time, sequence and cause/effect language) and RI.3.8 (logical connections between sentences and paragraphs).
- e2-word-strategies: context, prefixes, roots, compounds, glossaries and dictionaries (context-clues, word-parts). Grade 3 RI.3.4 and L.3.4.
- e2-text-features: captions, bold print, subheadings, index; locating facts efficiently. Grade 3 RI.3.5 (text features and search tools, including key words, sidebars, hyperlinks).
- e2-author-purpose: main purpose of a text. Grade 3 RI.3.6 (the reader's point of view against the author's).
- e2-reasons-support: how reasons support an author's points (claim-evidence-reasoning). Grade 3 RI.3.8.
- e2-opinion: opinion with reasons, linking words and a concluding statement (claim-evidence-reasoning, paragraph-frame). Grade 3 W.3.1 (organizational structure that lists reasons, linking words such as therefore, since, for example).
- e2-informative: topic, facts and definitions, concluding statement (main-idea-details, paragraph-frame). Grade 3 W.3.2 (grouped information, illustrations, linking words).
- e2-narrative: one well-elaborated event with actions, thoughts, feelings (narrative-arc). Grade 3 W.3.3 (situation and narrator, dialogue, descriptions of actions to show response).
- e2-revise-edit: revising and editing as named steps with peers and adults; digital publishing (writing-process). Grade 3 W.3.4, W.3.5 and W.3.6 (planning added; keyboarding skills).
- e2-research: shared research and gathering from provided sources (research-cycle). Grade 3 W.3.7 and W.3.8 (short research projects; take brief notes and sort evidence into categories).
- e2-discussion: gaining the floor, linking comments, asking for clarification (discussion-protocol). Grade 3 SL.3.1 (come prepared, explicitly drawing on the reading) and SL.3.3.
- e2-tell-and-record: coherent spoken story, audio recording, formal and informal English. Grade 3 SL.3.4, SL.3.5, SL.3.6 and L.3.3b (spoken and written standard English).
- e2-grammar: irregular plurals and verbs, reflexive pronouns, adjectives and adverbs. Grade 3 L.3.1 (function of parts of speech, abstract nouns, simple verb tenses, comparatives).
- e2-sentences: expanding and rearranging simple and compound sentences; capitals, commas, apostrophes (sentence-combining). Grade 3 L.3.1h and L.3.1i (compound and complex sentences) and L.3.2.
- e2-word-relationships: real-life connections and shades of meaning. Grade 3 L.3.5 and L.3.6.

Models handed on to grade 3: sound-spelling-map, syllable-types, word-parts, context-clues, story-map, narrative-arc,
figurative-language, main-idea-details, text-structure, claim-evidence-reasoning, paragraph-frame, writing-process,
research-cycle, discussion-protocol, sentence-combining. No new model names were added.
