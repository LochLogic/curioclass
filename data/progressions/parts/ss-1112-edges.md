# Social studies progression, grades 11 to 12: edges to earlier bands

Band 11 to 12 (us1-, us2-, gov-, econ-). All handoffs between grade 11 and grade 12 rows are written in
ss-1112.csv. The cross-band edges below are left out of the CSV for the integrator to join.

## Needs from earlier grades

Skills:
- us1-research-inquiry: the grade 9 to 10 inquiry skill of writing supporting questions and sourcing a teacher-given set (maker, date, audience, purpose).
- us1-argument-writing: the grade 9 to 10 sourced argument (claim, evidence from two or more sources, a counterclaim named).
- us1-revolution-causes: grade 5 causes of the Revolution and the colonial regions; grade 9 world history on the British Empire and the Seven Years' War if taught there.
- us1-declaration: grade 5 Declaration of Independence (its main ideas) and grade 8 civics on natural rights.
- us1-articles-constitution: grade 8 civics on the Constitution's structure (branches, federalism) and grade 5 Articles and Convention.
- us1-early-republic: grade 8 civics on the Supreme Court and the first parties, if taught there.
- us1-jackson-removal: grade 5 (or grade 4) Native nations of the Southeast and Indian removal in outline.
- us1-westward-expansion: grade 4 westward growth (ss4-westward-growth in the calibration set) and map scale.
- us1-slavery-economy: grade 5 slavery in the colonies and the Atlantic trade (ss5-slavery-colonies in the calibration set).
- us1-slavery-resistance: grade 5 sourcing and corroboration of two accounts (ss5-sourcing-corroboration in the calibration set).
- us1-sectional-crisis: grade 5 road to the Civil War, in outline.
- us1-civil-war, us1-reconstruction: grade 5 Civil War and Reconstruction amendments, in outline.
- us1-industrialization, us1-immigration: grade 10 world history on the Industrial Revolution and global migration.
- us1-empire, us1-wwi: grade 10 world history on imperialism and World War I (causes, alliances).
- us1-news-reading, us1-press-history: grade 8 news/media literacy (8.T7: news versus opinion, checking a source).
- us2-wwii-war, us2-isolation-fascism, us2-holocaust-response: grade 10 world history on World War II, fascism and the Holocaust.
- us2-cold-war-origins, us2-cold-war-end: grade 10 world history on the Cold War and decolonization.
- us2-econ-toolkit, econ-scarcity-choice: middle grades economics on scarcity, opportunity cost and simple markets.
- econ-markets-prices: any earlier grade that names supply and demand in words (the graph is introduced here).
- econ-earning-income, econ-saving-interest, econ-credit: middle grades budget skill (income, spending, saving).
- gov-foundations, gov-federalism, gov-courts, gov-executive: grade 8 civics on the three branches, federalism and state and local government (taught there as the student's own state).
- gov-rights-cases, gov-free-press: grade 8 civics on the Bill of Rights and the First Amendment.
- gov-local-action: grade 8 civics informed action (choosing an issue, weighing views).

Models (used in model_in here, introduced or carried by earlier bands; the gate fails on this part alone until the
full file is stitched):
- grade 11 rows use: timeline, map-scale, cause-effect-chain, source-sourcing, corroboration, claim-evidence,
  perspective, continuity-change, branches-of-government. Grade 11 rows carry each on to grade 12.
- grade 12 rows also use: opportunity-cost, budget, region (not carried by any grade 11 row; needed from grades 9 to 10
  or earlier).
- introduced in this band: supply-demand (the formal graph, us2-econ-toolkit and econ-markets-prices, both grade 12
  since either course may come first), production-possibilities (new name, econ-scarcity-choice), circular-flow
  (econ-macro-measures; if an earlier band introduces it, change that row to carry it).
