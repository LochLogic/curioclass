# Science K to 2: edges to other bands

## Needs from earlier grades

None. K to 2 is the first band.

## Hands on to later grades

Grades 3 to 5 should build on these (NGSS articulation lines from the DCI arrangements, read 2026-11-13).

- sk-push-pull: pushes and pulls change motion; a bigger push, a bigger change; contact forces only (cause-effect). Grade 3 balanced and unbalanced forces (3-PS2-1, 3-PS2-2) and grade 4 energy of motion (4-PS3-1).
- sk-ramp-design: judging whether a design works as intended from a test. Grade 3 and 4 design work (3-PS2-4, 4-PS3-4).
- sk-sun-warms: sunlight warms surfaces, in relative terms. Grade 3 weather and climate (3-ESS2-1) and grade 4 energy transfer (4-PS3-2), where energy-transfer is introduced.
- sk-living-needs: what plants and animals need; animals eat plants or other animals (patterns). Grade 3 groups and survival (3-LS2-1, 3-LS4-3) and grade 5 food webs and plants' matter from air and water (5-LS1-1, 5-LS2-1).
- sk-weather-watch: a whole-year weather record read for patterns (data-graph). Grade 3 weather data by season (3-ESS2-1, 3-ESS2-2).
- sk-forecast-severe: forecasts help people prepare for severe weather. Grade 3 weather hazards (3-ESS3-1).
- sk-needs-places: living things live where their needs are met; organisms and surroundings form a system (system-model). Grade 3 habitats and survival (3-LS4-3).
- sk-reduce-impact: communicating solutions that lessen human impact. Grade 4 natural resources (4-ESS3-1) and grade 5 protecting Earth's resources (5-ESS3-1).
- s1-sound-vibrates: vibrating materials make sound and sound makes materials vibrate. Grade 4 waves (4-PS4-1), where wave-model is introduced.
- s1-see-with-light and s1-light-materials: objects are seen only when lit; materials pass, block or reflect light. Grade 4 light reflected into the eye (4-PS4-2).
- s1-signal-device: a device that sends a message with light or sound (design-cycle). Grade 4 patterns to send information (4-PS4-3).
- s1-parts-mimic: external parts of plants and animals help them survive (structure-function, system-model). Grade 4 internal and external structures (4-LS1-1) and senses (4-LS1-2).
- s1-parent-offspring: parent and offspring behaviour that helps the young survive (patterns). Grade 3 group behaviour (3-LS2-1).
- s1-like-parents: young are like but not exactly like their parents. Grade 3 inherited traits and variation (3-LS3-1).
- s1-sky-patterns and s1-daylight-seasons: predictable sun, moon and star patterns; less daylight in winter. Grade 5 shadows, day length and star patterns (5-ESS1-2), where earth-sun-moon is introduced.
- s1-sketch-shape: a labelled sketch or model showing how shape helps function. Grades 3 to 5 design (3-5-ETS1-2).
- s2-material-properties and s2-best-material: observable properties of materials and choosing a material by test data (patterns, data-graph). Grade 5 measuring and identifying materials by properties (5-PS1-3).
- s2-pieces-new-object: the same pieces can make a new object. Grade 5 particles too small to see (5-PS1-1) and conservation (5-PS1-2), where particle-model is introduced.
- s2-heat-cool-change: reversible and irreversible changes by heating and cooling. Grade 5 mixing and new substances (5-PS1-4).
- s2-plant-needs-test: a fair test, one variable at a time, of plant needs (cause-effect, data-graph). Grade 5 plants get matter mainly from air and water (5-LS1-1).
- s2-seed-pollinator-model: plants depend on animals to move seeds and pollen (system-model). Grade 3 groups and survival and grade 5 matter moving among organisms (5-LS2-1).
- s2-habitat-diversity: counting the kinds of living things in different habitats. Grade 3 habitats and survival (3-LS4-3, 3-LS4-4).
- s2-land-water-model: a model of land and water shapes in an area. Grade 4 maps of Earth's features (4-ESS2-2).
- s2-water-where: where water is on Earth, solid or liquid. Grade 5 amounts of salt and fresh water (5-ESS2-2).
- s2-fast-slow-events: Earth events can be quick or slow (stability-change). Grade 4 rock layers and erosion (4-ESS1-1, 4-ESS2-1) and natural hazards (4-ESS3-2).
- s2-erosion-solutions: comparing designs that slow wind and water erosion. Grade 4 reducing the impacts of natural Earth processes (4-ESS3-2).
- s2-compare-designs: comparing two tested designs by their strengths and weaknesses (design-cycle). Grades 3 to 5 criteria, constraints and fair tests of solutions (3-5-ETS1-1, 3-5-ETS1-3).

## Models handed on

patterns, cause-effect, data-graph, claim-evidence-reasoning, system-model, design-cycle and structure-function are
introduced in K or grade 1 and used in every grade after. stability-change is a new name added by this band (grade
2, for the stability and change crosscutting concept, which no shared name covers); grades 3 to 5 should pick it up
in grade 4 (4-ESS1-1, 4-ESS2-1) or drop it at the stitch if the lead prefers not to add a name.
