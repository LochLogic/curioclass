# Social studies grades 6 to 8: edges to other bands

Cross-band assumes and hands_on cells in ss-68.csv are left empty; the integrator writes them from these lists.

## Models the band needs from grades 3 to 5

Grade 6 rows use these models in model_in, so the 3 to 5 band (or K to 2) must hand them on: timeline, map-scale,
map-key, compass-directions, region, source-sourcing, corroboration, claim-evidence, cause-effect-chain,
perspective, opportunity-cost. Grade 7 (ss7-roman-republic) needs branches-of-government from grade 5.
Grade 6 introduces latitude-longitude (ss6-mena-maps) and continuity-change (ss6-paleolithic-neolithic);
grade 7 introduces supply-demand (ss7-silk-road-trade) and has budget in model_out (ss7-roman-economy). If an
earlier band already introduces any of these, add it to the row's model_in so the row carries it.

## Needs from earlier grades

- ss6-social-science-lenses: grade 3 to 5 skill of asking questions about the past and naming where evidence comes from.
- ss6-changing-interpretations: grade 5 sourcing and claim-with-evidence (ss5-sourcing-corroboration).
- ss6-timeline-deep-time: grade 4 or 5 scaled timeline in centuries (ss4-exploration timeline).
- ss6-paleolithic-neolithic: grade 4 first-peoples evidence (artifacts, archaeology: ss4-first-peoples-evidence).
- ss6-mena-maps: grade 4 map scale, key and directions (ss4-map-scale).
- ss6-mena-settlement: grade 4 or 5 regions and how climate shapes work and settlement (ss5-colonial-regions).
- ss6-surplus-specialization: grades 3 to 5 economics: scarcity, choice and opportunity cost, producers and consumers.
- ss6-hammurabi-code: grade 4 sourcing a document (ss4-primary-source) and grade 5 claim with evidence.
- ss6-nubia-kush: grade 5 corroborating two accounts (ss5-sourcing-corroboration).
- ss6-phoenician-trade: grades 3 to 5 economics: trade, money as a medium of exchange.
- ss6-research-contribution: grade 5 claim with evidence from two sources (ss5-sourcing-corroboration).
- ss6-caribbean-peoples: grades 3 and 4 Native nations taught in present and past tense from their own materials (ss4-native-nations-sites).
- ss6-west-africa-trade, ss6-axum-swahili: grade 4 or 5 cause and effect chains (ss5-revolution-causes).
- ss6-egypt-society: grade 5 perspective (Loyalist and Patriot views; ss5-independence-war).
- ss7-roman-republic: grade 5 three branches and checks and balances (ss5-constitution).
- ss8-declaration: grade 5 causes of the Revolution and the Declaration (ss5-revolution-causes; ss5-independence-war).
- ss8-articles-convention: grade 5 Articles of Confederation, Shays' Rebellion and the Convention's compromises (ss5-independence-war; ss5-constitution).
- ss8-constitution-text, ss8-three-branches: grade 5 Preamble, branches, Bill of Rights (ss5-constitution).
- ss8-state-local-government, ss8-federal-state-powers: grade 3 levels of government (ss3-levels-of-government) and grade 5 federal, state and local roles (ss5-constitution).
- ss8-amendments-civil-war: grade 5 slavery, the road to the Civil War (5.T4) and grade 5 civil rights topic (5.T5).
- ss8-civil-rights-laws: grade 5 fight for civil rights (5.T5 rows).
- ss8-public-budget: grades 3 to 5 economics: taxes pay for public services, budgeting.

## Hands on to later grades

- ss7-rome-fall: the Western empire's breakup by about 476 CE and the Eastern empire continuing; World History I (grade 9, WHI.T1) starts at about 500.
- ss7-buddhism-east-asia: Buddhism in China, Korea and Japan to about 1300; WHI East Asia topics build on it.
- ss6-islam-origins: Muhammad, the Qur'an, Mecca and Medina to 632; WHI's spread of Islam starts there.
- ss6-christianity-origins, ss6-ancient-israel: the faiths' origins; WHI's medieval Europe and Byzantium build on them.
- ss6-west-africa-trade, ss6-axum-swahili: Ghana, trans-Saharan and Indian Ocean trade to about 1000; WHI's Mali, Songhay and the Swahili coast pick up.
- ss6-maya-andes-research, ss6-caribbean-peoples: Mesoamerican, Andean and Caribbean societies; WHI's encounters and conquest topics, and US History I.
- ss7-silk-road-trade, ss7-roman-economy: supply-demand model and inflation; the Economics course (ECON.T2 markets, ECON.T4 inflation).
- ss8-public-budget: budgets, taxes, interest rates, economic data; Economics (ECON, PFL) and Government.
- ss8-three-branches, ss8-courts-review, ss8-state-local-government: branches-of-government model; US Government and Politics (GOV.T2 to T4) and US History I.
- ss8-court-research, ss8-civil-rights-laws, ss8-amendments-civil-war: case reading and amendments; US History I and II and Government.
- ss8-news-literacy, ss8-editorial-analysis: source checking and argument critique; the high school NML.T1 to T5 placements.
- ss8-civic-action: the yearly informed-action project; each high school course's once-a-year action.
- ss7-research-greek, ss8-court-research: research reports with credibility checks and arguments with limits; high school research writing (C3 9-12 D3 and D4).
- ss6-mena-maps, ss7-time-zones: latitude-longitude and time zones; high school world history and geography map work.
