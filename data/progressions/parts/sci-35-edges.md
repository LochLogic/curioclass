## Models expected from K to 2

Grade 3 rows carry (model_in and model_out) these models, which the K to 2 part is expected to hand on: patterns,
cause-effect, structure-function, design-cycle, data-graph, claim-evidence-reasoning, system-model. If the K to 2 part
does not hand one on, the first grade 3 row that uses it switches to introducing it (model_out only).

Introduced in grades 3 to 5: force-diagram (s3-balanced-forces), energy-transfer (s4-energy-transfer), wave-model
(s4-wave-model), light-path (s4-light-eye, a new name: the arrows light follows from source to object to eye; no
shared name fits, and it is not a wave model at this grade), particle-model and scale-proportion (s5-particles),
food-web and matter-cycle (s5-matter-movement), earth-sun-moon (s5-shadows-sky). If the K to 2 part already
introduces force-diagram (K-PS2-1 pushes and pulls) or earth-sun-moon (1-ESS1-1, patterns of sun, moon and stars),
the stitcher turns the grade 3 or grade 5 row into a carry; for earth-sun-moon, grades 3 and 4 have no sky standard,
so the model rests there.

## Needs from earlier grades (K to 2)

- s3-balanced-forces: pushes and pulls of different strength and direction change motion (K-PS2-1, K-PS2-2).
- s3-motion-patterns: noticing and recording patterns in observations (1-ESS1-1, patterns of sun and moon).
- s3-magnet-static: pushes and pulls as causes (K-PS2-1).
- s3-design-problem: a problem stated as something people want to change, and a simple sketch or model of a solution (K-2-ETS1-1, K-2-ETS1-2).
- s3-life-cycles: young plants and animals are like but not exactly like their parents (1-LS3-1); what plants and animals need (K-LS1-1).
- s3-animal-groups: parents and offspring behave in ways that help offspring survive (1-LS1-2).
- s3-inherited-traits: offspring are like but not exactly like their parents (1-LS3-1).
- s3-environment-traits: plants need water and light to grow, from a planned investigation (2-LS2-1).
- s3-fossils: Earth events can be slow, over long times (2-ESS1-1).
- s3-variation-advantage: structures of plants and animals help them survive (1-LS1-1).
- s3-habitat-survival: the variety of life in different habitats (2-LS4-1); animals and plants live where their needs are met (K-ESS3-1).
- s3-environment-change-solution: plants and animals change their environment, and people reduce their impact (K-ESS2-2, K-ESS3-3).
- s3-weather-data: recording and describing local weather patterns over time (K-ESS2-1).
- s3-climates: shapes and kinds of land and water in an area (2-ESS2-2); weather patterns (K-ESS2-1).
- s3-weather-hazard: weather forecasting helps people prepare for severe weather (K-ESS3-2); comparing solutions that slow wind or water changing land (2-ESS2-1).
- s4-wave-model: sound comes from vibrating materials and can make materials vibrate (1-PS4-1).
- s4-light-eye: objects can be seen only when lit; light passes through some materials and is blocked or reflected by others (1-PS4-2, 1-PS4-3).
- s4-information-codes: devices that use light or sound to communicate over a distance (1-PS4-4).
- s4-structures-function: plant and animal external parts help them survive (1-LS1-1).
- s5-particles, s5-identify-materials, s5-new-substances, s5-conserve-weight: describing and sorting materials by observable properties, testing which material suits a purpose, an object made of small pieces can be taken apart and built into something new, heating and cooling changes that can and cannot be reversed (2-PS1-1, 2-PS1-2, 2-PS1-3, 2-PS1-4).
- s5-shadows-sky: patterns of the sun, moon and stars in the sky, and daylight through the year (1-ESS1-1, 1-ESS1-2).
- s5-water-distribution: where water is found on Earth and that it can be solid or liquid (2-ESS2-3).
- s5-design-test: comparing two objects designed for the same problem by testing them (K-2-ETS1-3).

## Hands on to later grades (6 to 8)

Grade 6 to 8 unit numbers are the base text's order (6.1 to 8.6), which is the middle school grade split. The unit
for each code is from the unit titles in S:\CurioClass-sources\openscied-ms, not from a standards map; the stitcher
checks them against the 6 to 8 part.

- s3-balanced-forces, s5-gravity-down: forces with direction and strength, gravity toward Earth's center, force arrows (MS-PS2-1, MS-PS2-2 in 8.1; MS-PS2-4 in 8.3).
- s3-magnet-static: magnetic and static electric forces at a distance, stronger when closer (MS-PS2-3, MS-PS2-5 in 8.3).
- s3-inherited-traits, s3-environment-traits: inherited traits versus traits shaped by the environment (MS-LS1-5, MS-LS3-1, MS-LS3-2 in 8.5).
- s3-animal-groups: animal behaviors that raise survival (MS-LS1-4).
- s3-variation-advantage, s3-fossils: variation and survival, the fossil record (MS-LS4-1, MS-LS4-4 in 8.6).
- s4-speed-energy, s4-collisions, s4-energy-transfer: energy of motion grows with speed, energy moves in collisions (MS-PS3-1, MS-PS3-5 in 8.1; MS-PS3-3, MS-PS3-4 in 6.2).
- s4-wave-model: amplitude and wavelength (MS-PS4-1 in 8.2).
- s4-light-eye: the light-path model, seeing by reflected light (MS-PS4-2 in 6.1).
- s4-information-codes: patterns that carry information, digital signals (MS-PS4-3).
- s4-structures-function, s4-senses-brain: body systems and information through the senses (MS-LS1-3, MS-LS1-8 in 6.6).
- s4-rock-layers: relative age from rock layers and fossils (MS-ESS1-4, MS-ESS2-2 in 6.4).
- s4-earth-feature-maps: belts of mountains, volcanoes and earthquakes, left as a question (MS-ESS2-3 in 6.4; MS-ESS3-2 in 6.5).
- s4-energy-resources: natural resources and their uneven distribution (MS-ESS3-1 in 7.6).
- s4-hazard-solutions: natural hazard forecasting and mitigation (MS-ESS3-2 in 6.5).
- s5-particles, s5-conserve-weight, s5-new-substances, s5-identify-materials: the particle model, conservation of matter, new substances with new properties (MS-PS1-1, MS-PS1-2, MS-PS1-5 in 7.1; MS-PS1-4 in 6.2).
- s5-food-energy-sun, s5-plant-matter, s5-matter-movement: energy flow and matter cycling in organisms and ecosystems, food webs (MS-LS1-6, MS-LS1-7 in 7.3 and 7.4; MS-LS2-3 in 7.4 and 7.5).
- s5-shadows-sky, s5-star-brightness: the Earth-sun-moon model and scale of space (MS-ESS1-1, MS-ESS1-3 in 8.4).
- s5-sphere-interactions, s5-water-distribution: Earth's systems and the water on Earth (MS-ESS2-4, MS-ESS2-6 in 6.3).
- s5-protect-resources: human impact and its reduction (MS-ESS3-3 in 7.6).
- s5-design-test: the full design cycle with fair tests (MS-ETS1-1 to MS-ETS1-4).
