# Science grades 9 to 11 (high school): edges across bands

Cross-band assumes and hands_on cells in sci-hs.csv are left empty; the stitcher fills them from these lists.
Grade 8 (OpenSciEd units 8.1 to 8.6) is the next grade down for most needs, but the middle school order puts some
core ideas in grade 6 or 7 only, so several needs below reach back past grade 8. Each one says which grade and unit
it expects. There is no core science course in grade 12, so nothing is handed on past grade 11.

## Models grade 9 to 11 rows use that an earlier grade must hand on

particle-model, energy-transfer, force-diagram, wave-model, system-model, food-web, matter-cycle, earth-sun-moon,
water-cycle, rock-cycle, plate-model, data-graph, claim-evidence-reasoning, design-cycle, cause-effect,
scale-proportion, structure-function, patterns.

This band introduces three new model names: computational-model (bio-population-carrying-capacity, grade 9),
periodic-table (chem-atomic-structure-periodic-patterns, grade 10) and field-model (phys-gravity-coulomb, grade 11).
If the grade 6 to 8 writer already hands on field-model (forces at a distance, unit 8.3) or a computational model,
add it to those rows' model_in. Gaps the gate will note, each explained in the row that reactivates the model:
force-diagram unused in grades 9 and 10 (phys-newton-second-law); wave-model and earth-sun-moon unused in grade 9
(chem-earth-energy-climate); water-cycle and rock-cycle unused in grade 9 (chem-water-earth-materials).

## Hands on to later grades

None. Grade 11 is the last core science grade. Every HS performance expectation is owned in grades 9 to 11, and the
electives (Earth and Space Science, Environmental Science) have no rows.
