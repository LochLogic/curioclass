## Needs from earlier grades (K to 2)

Grade 3 rows carry (model_in and model_out) the models the K to 2 part already hands on: timeline, map-key,
compass-directions, map-scale, region, perspective, continuity-change, cause-effect-chain, source-sourcing,
claim-evidence, opportunity-cost, budget. Grade 3 introduces latitude-longitude; grade 4 introduces
branches-of-government. Corroboration (grade 2) is carried again only in grade 5.
Likely K to 2 ids in brackets, from parts/ss-k2.csv as read 2026-11-13; the integrator confirms.

## Hands on to later grades (6 and up)

- ss5-sourcing-corroboration: sourcing every document and corroborating two accounts; written claim from two sources (models source-sourcing, corroboration, claim-evidence). The checked example names ss6-interpretations as the grade 6 row that takes it.
- ss5-civil-rights-research: student-written supporting questions, judging a source's usefulness, a claim with evidence from several sources, critiquing an argument; no counterclaim yet (grades 6 to 8 add it) (claim-evidence, corroboration, source-sourcing).
- ss5-revolution-causes and ss5-civil-war: the cause-effect chain with several causes weighed; timeline to scale (cause-effect-chain, timeline).
- ss5-colonial-regions and ss5-republic-growth: measuring with map scale, finding places by latitude and longitude, regions defined by climate and land (map-scale, latitude-longitude, region). For grade 6 world geography.
- ss5-native-sovereignty-removal and ss5-independence-war: explaining past perspectives from their context (perspective).
- ss5-reconstruction-amendments, ss5-jim-crow, ss5-civil-rights-movements: continuity and change in rights over time (continuity-change).
- ss5-constitution: the three branches and checks and balances, federal, state and local duties, the Bill of Rights (branches-of-government). For grade 8 civics and high school government; US History I (grade 11) restarts at 1763 on ss5-revolution-causes and ss5-constitution.
- ss3-deliberation and ss5-informed-action: structured civic discussion and a student-chosen, evidence-based action. For grade 8 civics.
- ss5-new-england-industry and ss4-trade-north-america: costs and benefits including costs to others, productivity, trade and interdependence (opportunity-cost). For high school economics and grades 6 and 7 trade between civilizations.
- ss3-money-saving: money, banks, interest and a simple savings plan (budget). No later grade 3 to 5 row carries budget; for high school personal finance (PFL) and any grade 6 to 8 row that uses a budget.
