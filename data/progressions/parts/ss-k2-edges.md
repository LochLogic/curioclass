# Social studies K to 2: edges to other bands

## Needs from earlier grades

None. K to 2 is the first band.

## Hands on to later grades

Grade 3 (Curio Class's own year, docs/courses/social-studies/grade-3-outline.md) should build on these.

- ss2-map-parts: reads a map's title, key, compass rose and a whole-unit scale (models map-key, compass-directions, map-scale). Grade 3 unit 1 (map grid, scale, legend).
- ss2-make-map: makes a map with all four parts and a simple scale. Grade 3 unit 1.
- ss2-projections: knows a flat map distorts the round Earth (Mercator against Peters). Grade 3 unit 1 (globe and map) and grade 4 map work.
- ss2-continents-features: places the seven continents, five oceans and major features on map and globe. Grade 3 unit 1 (continents and oceans, equator; latitude-longitude is not yet introduced).
- ss2-climate-regions: weather against climate, and the region model (an area sharing a trait). Grade 3 unit 1 (US regions) and unit 4 (how the land shaped where Native nations lived).
- ss2-people-environment: how people use, change and adapt to their environment (region). Grade 3 unit 1 (people changing the land).
- ss2-countries: physical against political geography; what makes a country and its government. Grade 3 unit 2 (levels of government) and grade 4.
- ss1-elected-leaders: elected leaders, what government does, terms of office; Native nations as governments. Grade 3 unit 2 (local, state, national leaders).
- ss1-group-roles: roles in a group and how a community gets shared work done. Grade 3 unit 2.
- ss1-voting: voting and elections as fair decisions; equality and fairness. Grade 3 unit 2 (class vote, how people take part).
- ss1-citizens: who is a citizen; rights and responsibilities of all community members. Grade 3 unit 2 (rights with responsibilities).
- ss1-community-problems: names a community problem and ways to help (informed action, student choice). Grade 3 unit 7 (class contribution to the community).
- ss1-leaders: qualities of leaders from history, with questions about how a person made a change. Grade 3 biography thread.
- ss1-national-symbols: national symbols, motto, songs and their meaning. Grade 3 unit 6 (founding ideas, symbols and holidays).
- ssk-civic-holidays: what civic holidays remember, Thanksgiving taught from the record and from Wampanoag people today. Grade 3 unit 5 (harvest-feast stories from the record) and unit 6.
- ss2-why-people-move: chosen and forced migration; possible reasons weighed (model cause-effect-chain). Grade 3 unit 5 (why Europeans crossed; slavery legal in every colony) and unit 7 (newcomers).
- ss2-culture-migration: elements of a culture and how migration changes a place (cause-effect-chain, perspective). Grade 3 unit 7.
- ss2-nation-of-immigrants: "nation of immigrants" with its honest limits (Native nations first, enslaved Africans forced). Grade 3 units 4, 5 and 7.
- ss2-family-interviews: writes interview questions and treats an interview as a source; family timeline (timeline). Grade 3 unit 7 (interviews with family and neighbors).
- ss1-calendar-time and ss2-family-interviews: timeline read in days, months and years. Grade 3 timeline thread (years, decades, centuries).
- ss2-source-maker: finds a source's maker, date and place; fact against opinion (source-sourcing). Grade 3 primary and secondary source routine.
- ss2-two-accounts: compares two accounts of one event (corroboration, first form). Grade 3 inquiry arc and grade 4 to 5 sourcing and corroboration.
- ss2-claim-sources: written claim with reasons from a named source (claim-evidence). Grade 3 inquiry arc (claim with evidence).
- ss2-country-report: short research from one or two sources, presented with a map. Grade 3 unit 3 (comparison with a community in another country) and unit 7 biography.
- ss1-supporting-questions: compelling and supporting questions; naming the discipline. Grade 3 inquiry arc.
- ss1-choices: limited money forces choices; benefit and cost (opportunity-cost). Grade 3 unit 3 (scarcity and choices).
- ss1-saving: reasons to save, banks, the spend-save-share budget model. Grade 3 unit 3 (a simple savings plan).
- ss1-goods-services and ss1-income-jobs: goods and services, prices, income and wages. Grade 3 unit 3 (producers and consumers, money).
- ss2-resources-industries and ss2-trade-world: natural resources and industries; trade and goods from far away (opportunity-cost). Grade 3 unit 3 (what is made here and what comes from far away).
- ss2-people-resource: human capital and capital goods. Grade 3 unit 3.

Models leaving the band: timeline, map-key, compass-directions, map-scale, region, claim-evidence, source-sourcing, corroboration, cause-effect-chain, perspective, continuity-change, opportunity-cost, budget (budget last carried at grade 1). latitude-longitude, branches-of-government, supply-demand and circular-flow are not introduced in K to 2.
