# Math grades 3 to 5: edges to other bands

Rows in parts/math-35.csv (76 rows: grade 3 24, grade 4 27, grade 5 25). Cross-band cells are left empty there; the
stitcher writes them from this list. Written blind: the K to 2 and 6 to 8 parts were not read, so the skills below are
described by skill and standard code, not by row id; the stitcher matches them.

## Models expected from K to 2

Grade 3 rows carry (model_in) these models, which the K to 2 part is expected to hand on: number-line (2.MD.B.6),
base-ten-blocks (2.NBT.A), array (2.OA.C.4), tape-diagram (grade 1 and 2 word problems), bar-graph and dot-plot
(2.MD.D.9 line plots, 2.MD.D.10 picture and bar graphs). If the K to 2 part does not hand one on, the first grade 3
row that uses it switches to introducing it (model_out only, model_in emptied).

Introduced in grades 3 to 5: place-value-chart (m3-add-sub-1000; if K to 2 already introduces it for 2.NBT.A.1, the
stitcher turns the grade 3 row into a carry), area-model (m3-area-multiply), fraction-strip (m3-unit-fractions),
shape-hierarchy (m3-quadrilaterals), coordinate-plane (m5-coordinate-system), function-table (m5-two-patterns).

New model names (not on the shared list): bar-graph (scaled picture and bar graphs; no shared name fits a categorical
graph, and data-graph is science's name) and shape-hierarchy (a diagram of nested shape categories, carried from 3.G.A.1
to 5.G.B.4). dot-plot is used for the K to 5 line plot because the CCSS glossary treats the two as one display.

## Needs from earlier grades (K to 2)

- m3-scaled-graphs: picture and bar graphs with a scale of one, up to four categories (2.MD.D.10).
- m3-equal-groups: arrays up to 5 by 5 and a total as a sum of equal addends (2.OA.C.4); skip counting by 5s, 10s, 100s (2.NBT.A.2).
- m3-mult-div-problems: one- and two-step add and subtract word problems with unknowns in all positions (2.OA.A.1).
- m3-division-meaning: equal shares of shapes (2.G.A.3) and arrays (2.OA.C.4).
- m3-properties: properties of addition as strategies (1.OA.B.3).
- m3-mult-fluency: add and subtract within 20 fluently (2.OA.B.2), even and odd (2.OA.C.3).
- m3-two-step-problems: two-step add and subtract word problems within 100 (2.OA.A.1).
- m3-arithmetic-patterns: even and odd numbers and doubles (2.OA.C.3).
- m3-round: three-digit place value and comparing (2.NBT.A.1, 2.NBT.A.4); numbers on a number line (2.MD.B.6).
- m3-add-sub-1000: add and subtract within 1,000 with models and strategies (2.NBT.B.7), within 100 fluently (2.NBT.B.5).
- m3-multiples-of-ten: hundreds, tens and ones as units (2.NBT.A.1).
- m3-area-concepts: partition a rectangle into rows and columns of same-size squares and count them (2.G.A.2).
- m3-area-multiply: same as m3-area-concepts (2.G.A.2) and arrays (2.OA.C.4).
- m3-partition-shapes: halves, thirds and fourths of circles and rectangles (2.G.A.3).
- m3-unit-fractions: equal shares named halves, thirds, fourths (1.G.A.3, 2.G.A.3); length units iterated (2.MD.A.1).
- m3-fractions-number-line: whole numbers as lengths from 0 on a number line (2.MD.B.6).
- m3-time: tell and write time to the nearest five minutes, a.m. and p.m. (2.MD.C.7).
- m3-mass-volume: measure and estimate lengths in standard units (2.MD.A.1, 2.MD.A.3).
- m3-line-plots: measure lengths to the whole unit and show them on a line plot (2.MD.A.1, 2.MD.D.9).
- m3-quadrilaterals: recognize and draw shapes with given attributes: triangles, quadrilaterals, pentagons, hexagons (2.G.A.1).
- m3-perimeter: add lengths in the same units, length word problems (2.MD.B.5).
- m3-compare-fractions, m3-equivalent-fractions: compare numbers with >, = and < (2.NBT.A.4).

## Hands on to later grades (6 to 8)

- m3-scaled-graphs: reading a scaled graph, to grade 6 statistics (6.SP.B.4, displays of data). A skip of grades 4 and 5, which have no categorical-graph standard.
- m4-angle-concepts, m4-protractor, m4-additive-angles: angle measure and additive angles, to grade 7 angle facts (7.G.B.5). A skip of grades 5 and 6, which have no angle standard.
- m4-symmetry: line symmetry, to grade 8 reflections (8.G.A.1). A skip of grades 5 to 7.
- m5-expressions: numerical expressions with grouping symbols, to grade 6 expressions (6.EE.A.1, 6.EE.A.2).
- m5-two-patterns: two related patterns, ordered pairs and the function table, to grade 6 ratio tables and graphs (6.RP.A.3a) and dependent and independent variables (6.EE.C.9).
- m5-graph-points, m5-coordinate-system: first-quadrant graphing, to all four quadrants (6.NS.C.6, 6.NS.C.8).
- m5-fraction-as-division, m5-unit-fraction-division: fraction as quotient and division with unit fractions, to dividing fractions by fractions (6.NS.A.1).
- m5-fraction-mult-problems, m5-scaling: fraction products and scaling, to ratio and rate reasoning (6.RP.A.3).
- m5-divide-two-digit: division with two-digit divisors by strategies, to the standard division algorithm (6.NS.B.2).
- m5-decimal-operations, m5-multiply-algorithm: decimal operations by strategies and the multiplication algorithm, to fluent decimal algorithms (6.NS.B.3).
- m5-powers-of-ten: whole-number exponents for powers of 10, to exponents in expressions (6.EE.A.1).
- m5-measurement-conversion: unit conversion within a system, to converting units with ratio reasoning (6.RP.A.3d).
- m5-volume-formulas, m5-fraction-area: volume of prisms with whole-number edges and area with fractional sides, to volume with fractional edges (6.G.A.2) and area of triangles and polygons (6.G.A.1).
- m5-line-plots: line plots of fraction data, to dot plots and measures of center (6.SP.B.4, 6.SP.B.5).
- m5-shape-hierarchy: classification by properties, to grade 7 drawing figures with given conditions (7.G.A.2).
- m5-add-sub-unlike: adding fractions with unlike denominators, to adding rational numbers (7.NS.A.1). A skip of grade 6, whose fraction work is division.
- m5-read-compare-thousandths, m5-round-decimals, m5-place-value-decimals: decimals to thousandths, to rational numbers on the number line (6.NS.C.6, 6.NS.C.7).
