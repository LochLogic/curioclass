# Math grades 6 to 8: edges to other bands

Cross-band assumes and hands_on cells in math-68.csv are left empty; the stitcher writes them from these lists.
Grade split and order: Illustrative Mathematics 6-8 (1st edition) unit order, 6.1 to 6.9, 7.1 to 7.9, 8.1 to 8.9. Every
grade 6 to 8 code is owned by one row of its own grade (73 rows: grade 6 26, grade 7 23, grade 8 24); no exclusions.
Progressions pages are cited as "PDF page (printed page)" from the 2023 compiled edition.

## Needs from earlier grades

Grade 5 is the expected source for each line; where the real prerequisite sits in grade 3 or 4, the line says so.

- m6-polygon-area: rectangle area with whole and fractional side lengths (5.NF.B.4b); area of a rectangle by tiling and the area formula (4.MD.A.3, grade 4, a skip because grade 5 does not reteach it).
- m6-surface-area-nets: two-dimensional shapes classified by properties (5.G.B.3, 5.G.B.4); area of rectangles (5.NF.B.4b).
- m6-ratio-language: multiplicative comparison and "times as many" (5.NF.B.5a scaling; 4.OA.A.1 in grade 4).
- m6-equivalent-ratios: first-quadrant coordinate plane and plotting pairs from two patterns (5.G.A.1, 5.G.A.2, 5.OA.B.3); multiplication fluency (5.NBT.B.5).
- m6-ratio-parts-problems: tape diagrams for multiplicative comparison and fraction problems (5.NF.B.6).
- m6-unit-rate: fraction as division (5.NF.B.3); multi-digit division with strategies (5.NBT.B.6).
- m6-unit-conversion: converting among sizes of a measurement unit within one system (5.MD.A.1).
- m6-percent: decimal fractions in hundredths (5.NBT.A.3); fraction of a quantity (5.NF.B.4a).
- m6-divide-fractions: dividing unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions (5.NF.B.7a, 5.NF.B.7b, 5.NF.B.7c); fraction multiplication (5.NF.B.4).
- m6-prism-volume-fractional: volume of right rectangular prisms with whole-number edges, V = lwh and V = bh (5.MD.C.3, 5.MD.C.4, 5.MD.C.5); fraction multiplication (5.NF.B.4b).
- m6-long-division: whole-number division with up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors by place value strategies (5.NBT.B.6).
- m6-decimal-operations: the four operations with decimals to hundredths by strategies (5.NBT.B.7); multi-digit multiplication with the standard algorithm (5.NBT.B.5); powers of ten and place value (5.NBT.A.1, 5.NBT.A.2).
- m6-gcf-lcm: factors and multiples, prime and composite (4.OA.B.4, grade 4: grade 5 has no factor standard).
- m6-exponents: whole-number exponents for powers of ten (5.NBT.A.2).
- m6-expressions-variables: writing and interpreting numerical expressions with grouping symbols (5.OA.A.1, 5.OA.A.2).
- m6-equivalent-expressions: properties of operations used as strategies (5.NBT.B.5, 5.NBT.B.6; 3.OA.B.5 for the distributive property).
- m6-one-step-equations: a letter for the unknown in an equation (4.OA.A.3, grade 4); fraction and decimal operations (5.NF.B.4, 5.NBT.B.7).
- m6-dependent-variables: two numerical patterns, ordered pairs and graphing them (5.OA.B.3, 5.G.A.2).
- m6-negative-numbers: fractions and decimals on the number line (3.NF.A.2, 4.NF.C.6).
- m6-order-absolute-value: comparing fractions and decimals (4.NF.A.2, 5.NBT.A.3b).
- m6-four-quadrants: first-quadrant coordinate plane (5.G.A.1, 5.G.A.2).
- m6-statistical-questions and m6-data-displays: line plots of measurement data in fractions of a unit (5.MD.B.2).
- m6-center-variability: fair-share redistribution on a line plot (5.MD.B.2); decimal division (5.NBT.B.7).
- m7-chance-probability, m7-probability-models, m7-compound-events: fractions as numbers between 0 and 1 and equivalent fractions (4.NF.A.1, 5.NF.A.1); no in-band row precedes them.
- m7-triangle-conditions: measuring and drawing angles with a protractor (4.MD.C.6) and classifying triangles by sides and angles (4.G.A.2), both grade 4, a skip because grades 5 and 6 add no angle standard.
- m7-angle-relationships: angle measure is additive (4.MD.C.7, grade 4, same reason).

## For the lead

1. Accelerated path. data/courses.csv lists Algebra I as grades 8 to 9. If some students take Algebra I in grade 8, they need grade 8 standards somewhere (the base text has an accelerated grade 7 course that compresses grades 7 and 8). These rows assume the standard three-year path. My pick: keep grades 6 to 8 as written; decide an accelerated path later at the course frame step.
2. New model name "net" (m6-surface-area-nets, used again in m7-cross-sections and m7-area-volume-composite): no shared name covers a flat pattern that folds into a solid. My pick: add it.
3. function-table is introduced in grade 6 (6.EE.C.9) here. If the grades 3 to 5 band introduces it for pattern tables (5.OA.B.3), the stitcher should make the grade 6 row a user rather than an introducer.
4. Major work by owning rows: grade 6 18 of 26, grade 7 13 of 23, grade 8 19 of 24. Rows are not days; grade 7's additional rows (geometry and sampling) are small units in the base text, but the grade 7 course frame should check the 65 percent line by days.
