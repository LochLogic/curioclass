## Hands on to later grades

None in core math. There is no core math course in grade 12; Precalculus, Statistics and Calculus are electives with no rows. The (+) standards in math-hs-excluded.csv are what those electives teach. What grade 11 hands on to them, for whoever plans the electives later: alg2-unit-circle-radians and alg2-trig-functions-graphs (trigonometric functions, toward the excluded F-TF (+) codes), alg2-complex-numbers (toward the N-CN (+) codes), alg2-logarithms (toward logarithms as inverse functions, F-BF.B.5), alg2-sample-estimates-margin and alg2-experiments-significance (toward formal inference and S-MD).

Skips inside the band: Algebra II rows assume Algebra I rows directly in 18 handoffs, because Geometry carries no algebra or functions unit (base-text course order Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II). Each skipping row says so in its notes.

## For the lead

1. F-IF.C.7d (+, rational functions with asymptotes) is owned by alg2-rational-functions: the base text teaches asymptotes and end behaviour in Algebra II unit 2 lessons 16 to 19 but tags only F-IF.C.7. My pick: keep it owned (it is taught); the alternative is excluding it and treating those lessons as F-IF.C.7 only.
2. S-CP.B.8 (+, general Multiplication Rule) is owned by geo-conditional-independence: Geometry unit 8 lesson 8 uses it, though the Progressions overview lists it among the plus standards. My pick: keep it owned.
3. F-BF.B.4a (find an inverse by solving f(x) = c) is owned in Algebra I with linear functions only; the standard's own examples are nonlinear (2x cubed, a rational function). My pick: one applying day in Algebra II with cube roots (alg2-rational-exponents), not a new row.
4. F-TF.A.3 and F-TF.A.4 (+) are touched (unit-circle symmetry, special angles) in Algebra II unit 6 but excluded, since no lesson names them. My pick: keep excluded.
5. Many Algebra I rows carry only ~codes for widely applicable prerequisite clusters (A-SSE, A-CED, A-REI.A, F-IF.C) because the base text completes those codes in Algebra II. Grade 9 still teaches them heavily; anyone counting owned major work per grade will see Algebra I look lighter than it is. My pick: leave ownership as written and count ~codes when judging balance.
