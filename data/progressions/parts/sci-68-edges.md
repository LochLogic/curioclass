# Science grades 6 to 8: edges to other bands

Cross-band assumes and hands_on cells in sci-68.csv are left empty; the stitcher writes them from these lists.
Grade split: OpenSciEd Middle School unit order (6.1 to 6.6, 7.1 to 7.6, 8.1 to 8.6). Each PE is owned by the
grade of the last unit whose "builds toward" list names it, with one exception: MS-PS4-2 is owned by grade 6
(s6-light-paths), because every PE of unit 6.1 is completed in grade 8 and the gate forbids a row with no standard.

## Models the band needs from grades 3 to 5

These rows use models in model_in that only an earlier band can hand on. If grades 3 to 5 do not introduce one, the
first grade 6 row that uses it should move it to model_out without model_in (introduce it here).

- particle-model (5-PS1-1): s6-particle-temperature, s6-insulator-design, s6-state-change, s6-water-cycle-energy.
- energy-transfer (4-PS3-2, 4-PS3-4): s6-particle-temperature and most grade 6 earth rows.
- wave-model (4-PS4-1): s6-digital-signals.
- system-model (5-ESS2-1, 4-LS1-1): s6-insulator-design, s6-water-cycle-energy, s6-climate-circulation, s6-rock-cycle, s6-cells-body-systems.
- structure-function (4-LS1-1): s6-cells-body-systems.
- earth-sun-moon (5-ESS1-2): s6-climate-circulation, then s8-earth-sun-moon and s8-gravity-solar-system.
- food-web and matter-cycle (5-LS2-1): food-web is first used in grade 7 (s7-ecosystem-matter-energy, s7-resources-populations, s7-interaction-patterns, s7-ecosystem-disruption); grade 6 has no ecosystem unit, so the model is idle in grade 6.
- force-diagram (3-PS2-1, 3-PS2-2): first used in grade 8 (s8-collision-forces and the 8.3 and 8.4 force rows); grades 6 and 7 have no forces unit in the base text, so the model is idle there.
- data-graph, design-cycle (3-5-ETS1), scale-proportion, cause-effect, patterns, claim-evidence-reasoning.

Grade 6 introduces: light-ray (new shared name, s6-light-paths: ray diagrams of light paths), water-cycle
(s6-water-cycle-energy; a carry if K to 5 already hands it on), rock-cycle (s6-rock-cycle), plate-model
(s6-plate-evidence). Grade 7 introduces matter-cycle as a carry-forward from s7-cell-respiration-growth if grades
3 to 5 do not hand it on.

## Needs from earlier grades

- s6-light-paths: grade 4 model of light reflecting from objects into the eye (4-PS4-2).
- s6-particle-temperature: grade 5 particles too small to see (5-PS1-1); grade 4 energy moves by heat (4-PS3-2).
- s6-insulator-design: grades 3 to 5 design process, criteria and constraints (3-5-ETS1-1 to 3).
- s6-state-change: grade 2 heating and cooling change materials (2-PS1-4) and grade 5 conservation of matter (5-PS1-2).
- s6-water-cycle-energy: grade 5 Earth spheres interacting (5-ESS2-1) and where water is found (5-ESS2-2).
- s6-air-masses-weather: grade 3 weather data and patterns (3-ESS2-1).
- s6-climate-circulation: grade 3 climates of regions (3-ESS2-2); grade 5 sun and shadow patterns (5-ESS1-2).
- s6-rock-cycle: grade 4 weathering and erosion (4-ESS2-1).
- s6-surface-change-scales: grade 4 rock layers and landscape change (4-ESS1-1).
- s6-plate-evidence: grade 4 maps of Earth features (4-ESS2-2).
- s6-geologic-time: grade 4 rock layers and fossils as evidence of change (4-ESS1-1); grade 3 fossils (3-LS4-1).
- s6-hazard-forecast: grade 4 reducing the effects of Earth processes (4-ESS3-2); grade 3 weather-hazard solutions (3-ESS3-1).
- s6-digital-signals: grade 4 patterns to transfer information (4-PS4-3); grade 4 waves (4-PS4-1).
- s6-cells-body-systems: grade 4 internal and external structures and their functions (4-LS1-1); grade 4 senses (4-LS1-2).
- s7-ecosystem-matter-energy, s7-resources-populations: grade 5 food web and matter cycling model (5-LS2-1), grade 5 energy from the sun in food (5-PS3-1).
- s8-collision-forces, s8-forces-motion-mass: grade 3 balanced and unbalanced forces (3-PS2-1, 3-PS2-2); grade 4 collisions and energy (4-PS3-3).
- s8-electromagnetic-forces, s8-fields: grade 3 electric and magnetic interactions (3-PS2-3, 3-PS2-4).
- s8-gravity-mass, s8-gravity-solar-system: grade 5 gravity pulls down (5-PS2-1).
- s8-earth-sun-moon: grade 5 patterns of shadows, day length and seasonal sky (5-ESS1-2); grade 1 moon and sun patterns (1-ESS1-1).
- s8-solar-system-scale: grade 5 distance and brightness of stars (5-ESS1-1).
- s8-inheritance, s8-genes-proteins-traits: grade 3 traits inherited from parents and variation (3-LS3-1, 3-LS3-2).
- s8-natural-selection, s8-anatomical-relationships: grade 3 variation and survival (3-LS4-2, 3-LS4-3).
