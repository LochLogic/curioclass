# Math K to 2: edges to other bands

Written blind (2026-11-13) for the stitcher. Ids in the later band are not known here, so each line names the
skill the next grade needs; the stitcher matches it to the grade 3 row ids and writes both ends.

## Needs from earlier grades

None. Kindergarten starts the subject. The Progressions note that children who arrive without small numbers or
counting to ten need extra Kindergarten time (Progressions, PDF 26 (printed 20)); that is a readiness check for
mk-count-sequence, mk-one-to-one and mk-how-many, not a skill another row owns.

## Hands on to later grades

- m2-fluency-within-20: all single-digit sums from memory and the related subtractions, used in grade 3
  multiplication and division strategies and in 3.NBT.A.2 (Progressions, PDF 34 (printed 28)).
- m2-two-step-problems: one- and two-step addition and subtraction problems with unknowns in all positions, for
  grade 3 two-step problems with all four operations (3.OA.D.8).
- m2-one-step-problems: tape diagrams for Compare and part-whole situations, carried into grade 3 problems and
  fraction work (model tape-diagram).
- m2-odd-even: doubles, pairing and counting by 2s, for grade 3 patterns in the addition and multiplication tables
  (3.OA.D.9).
- m2-arrays-equal-addends: arrays up to 5 by 5 and sums of equal addends, for grade 3 meaning of multiplication
  (3.OA.A.1, 3.OA.A.3) (model array).
- m2-partition-rows-columns: rectangles tiled in rows and columns of same-size squares, for grade 3 area
  (3.MD.C.5 to 3.MD.C.7) (model array).
- m2-three-digit-place-value and m2-hundreds-unit: hundreds, tens and ones to 1000 and expanded form, for grade 3
  rounding (3.NBT.A.1) and adding and subtracting within 1000 (model base-ten-blocks).
- m2-compare-three-digit and m2-count-skip-1000: order and position of numbers to 1000 on a number line, for
  grade 3 rounding (3.NBT.A.1).
- m2-add-within-1000, m2-subtract-within-1000, m2-explain-strategies: place-value strategies and written methods
  within 1000, for grade 3 fluency within 1000 (3.NBT.A.2); the shorter written method is needed by grade 4
  (Progressions, PDF 65 (printed 59)).
- m2-mental-ten-hundred and m2-count-skip-1000: skip-counting by 5s, 10s and 100s, for grade 3 multiplying by
  multiples of 10 (3.NBT.A.3) and count-by strategies for multiplication.
- m2-number-line: whole numbers as lengths from 0 on a number line, for grade 3 fractions on the number line
  (3.NF.A.2) and rounding (model number-line).
- m2-halves-thirds-fourths: equal shares of circles and rectangles described as halves, thirds and fourths, for
  grade 3 unit fractions (3.NF.A.1).
- m2-measure-with-tools, m2-unit-size, m2-length-problems: rulers and length units, for grade 3 measuring to
  halves and fourths of an inch (3.MD.B.4) and perimeter (3.MD.D.8).
- m2-line-plots: line plots with whole-number scales, for grade 3 line plots in halves and fourths (3.MD.B.4)
  (model dot-plot).
- m2-picture-bar-graphs: picture and bar graphs with a scale of one, for grade 3 scaled graphs (3.MD.B.3) (model
  bar-graph, a new name).
- m2-time-five-minutes: time to five minutes, for grade 3 time to the minute and elapsed time (3.MD.A.1).
- m2-money: coin values and the $ and cent symbols, used in grade 3 problems and in grade 4 decimal money
  (4.MD.A.2).
- m2-shape-attributes: shapes named by sides and angles, for grade 3 shape categories and quadrilaterals
  (3.G.A.1).
- ten-frame (last used in m2-fluency-within-20): not handed on; grade 3 does not need it.
