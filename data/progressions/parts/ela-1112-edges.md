# ELA progression, grades 11 to 12: edges to earlier bands

Band 11 to 12 (e11-, e12-). All handoffs between grade 11 and grade 12 rows are written in ela-1112.csv
(each grade 11 row hands on to its grade 12 owner; e11-argument also to e12-routine-writing, e11-research also to
e12-presentation). The cross-band edges below are left out of the CSV for the stitcher to join to the grade 10 ids.
Every 11-12 code is owned by one grade 12 row; grade 11 rows name them as ~CODE. No code is excluded.

## Needs from earlier grades

Skills (each needs the grade 10 row that owns the matching 9-10 code):
- e11-evidence-uncertain: grade 10 strong and thorough citation of explicit and inferred meaning (RL.9-10.1, RI.9-10.1).
- e11-lit-themes: grade 10 one theme traced in detail with an objective summary (RL.9-10.2).
- e11-lit-author-choices: grade 10 complex characters and how they advance plot and theme (RL.9-10.3).
- e11-lit-word-tone: grade 10 cumulative impact of word choice on tone (RL.9-10.4) and the band's Shakespeare play.
- e11-lit-structure: grade 10 structure, order and time for mystery, tension, surprise (RL.9-10.5).
- e11-lit-irony: grade 10 point of view and cultural experience in world literature (RL.9-10.6); grade 8 dramatic irony (RL.8.6).
- e11-lit-interpretations: grade 10 one subject in two artistic mediums (RL.9-10.7).
- e11-lit-american-periods: grade 10 how an author transforms source material (RL.9-10.9).
- e11-reading-band: grade 10 reading at the high end of the 9-10 band independently (RL.9-10.10, RI.9-10.10), and one whole long text read the year before.
- e11-info-central-ideas: grade 10 a central idea traced and summarized (RI.9-10.2).
- e11-info-ideas-events: grade 10 how an author unfolds a series of ideas or events (RI.9-10.3).
- e11-info-key-terms: grade 10 figurative, connotative and technical meanings (RI.9-10.4).
- e11-info-structure: grade 10 how claims are developed by sentences, paragraphs and sections (RI.9-10.5).
- e11-info-rhetoric: grade 10 how rhetoric advances a purpose (RI.9-10.6) and evaluating a speaker, including fallacies (SL.9-10.3).
- e11-info-multiple-sources: grade 10 accounts in different mediums (RI.9-10.7) and integrating sources with credibility judged (SL.9-10.2).
- e11-info-founding-reasoning: grade 10 delineating and evaluating an argument, validity, sufficiency, fallacies (RI.9-10.8).
- e11-info-founding-documents: grade 10 seminal U.S. documents read for related themes (RI.9-10.9).
- e11-argument: grade 10 argument with claim, counterclaim, reasons and evidence (W.9-10.1) and drawing evidence from texts (W.9-10.9).
- e11-informative: grade 10 informative and explanatory writing (W.9-10.2).
- e11-narrative: grade 10 narrative writing (W.9-10.3).
- e11-process: grade 10 writing process and publishing with technology (W.9-10.4 to 6).
- e11-research: grade 10 short and sustained research, advanced searches, usefulness of sources, standard citation (W.9-10.7, W.9-10.8).
- e11-routine-writing: grade 10 routine writing over long and short time frames (W.9-10.10).
- e11-seminar: grade 10 collaborative discussion with roles and evidence (SL.9-10.1).
- e11-presentation: grade 10 presenting clearly and logically with digital media and formal English (SL.9-10.4 to 6).
- e11-usage-conventions: grade 10 parallel structure, phrases and clauses, semicolons and colons (L.9-10.1, L.9-10.2) and the progressive skills.
- e11-syntax-effect: grade 10 writing to a style manual and knowledge of language (L.9-10.3).
- e11-word-strategies: grade 10 word-meaning strategies (L.9-10.4).
- e11-figurative-nuance: grade 10 euphemism and oxymoron, nuances (L.9-10.5).
- e11-academic-words: grade 10 general academic and domain words (L.9-10.6).

Models (grade 11 rows carry them, so the gate needs a grade 10 or earlier row to hand each on; none is
introduced in this band): claim-evidence-reasoning, main-idea-details, narrative-arc, text-structure,
figurative-language, context-clues, word-parts, rhetorical-situation, argument-map, essay-structure,
writing-process, research-cycle, citation, discussion-protocol, sentence-combining. If grade 10 does not hand one
on, the stitcher either adds it to the grade 10 row's model_out or makes the grade 11 row introduce it.

Texts (to check at reconcile): grade 11 assumes the 9-10 band read one Shakespeare play other than Hamlet, and did
not anchor on The Scarlet Letter, Douglass's Narrative, Trifles or The Great Gatsby.

## Hands on to later grades

Grade 12 is the last grade; no ELA row comes after it. What grade 12 gives beyond school, and to other subjects:
- e12-research: the self-generated sustained research cycle, the same one social studies uses in us2-research-paper.
- e12-info-seminal-reasoning: reading majority opinions and dissents, which the government course leans on (gov-courts, gov-rights-cases).
- e12-info-multiple-sources: integrating data, charts and reports for a decision (the lean of gov-policy-debates; math S-IC.B.6).
- e12-reading-band: independent reading at the high end of the 11-CCR band, the standards' college and career line.
