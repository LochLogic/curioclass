# SEL progression edges, grades K to 12

Edges from data/progressions/sel.csv (all 520 rows, K to 12) to rows outside this file, which here means other subjects only. Every edge between SEL grades is written in the CSV (assumes and hands_on, both ends), including the handoffs across band lines: each grade 2 row hands on to the grade 3 row of its sub-skill, each grade 5 row to the grade 6 row, and each grade 8 row to the grade 9 row, which assumes it.

## Needs from earlier grades

None. Kindergarten rows assume nothing for their sub-skill (docs/courses/sel/STANDARD.md section 3, Ownership); grades 1 to 12 assume the same sub-skill one year before, inside this file.

## Hands on to later grades

None. Grade 12 is the last year and its 40 owner rows hand on to no later row; their hands_on cells are empty.

The seven models (feelings faces, calm-down steps, talk turn card, stop-think-choose, goal steps, problem size meter, I-statement frame) run K to 12 with no grade gap. In grade 9 each takes its 9 to 12 form, a plan the student designs and then judges: the feelings scale adds what set a feeling off and what it pushes toward; the calm-down steps become a coping plan with a tool per setting, a back-up and a review date; the talk turn card adds a check-back (the listener says back the main point); stop-think-choose adds a sixth step, look back and judge; the goal steps card becomes a dated plan with its trade-off, a check date and a verdict; the problem size meter becomes a support map (each size of problem beside the people and offices that fit it, in school and out); the I-statement frame adds a request and a check-back. No model was added.

## For the kindergarten frame

Units 2, 5 and 6 each teach eight sub-skills in three groups. On the short path a group's Understanding and Fluency share one 20-minute session, so about three new sub-skills would be named and rehearsed in 20 minutes by five-year-olds. The progression rows stay as they are; the K frame uses the option docs/courses/sel/STANDARD.md section 2 gives to move a session between units ("How the sessions add up"), gives those three units a session each from units 1, 3 and 4 (five or six sub-skills each) so the short path stays 32, and says so (expert read 2026-11-21, finding 10).
