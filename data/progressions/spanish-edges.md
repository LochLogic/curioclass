# Spanish progression: edges outside data/progressions/spanish.csv

All assumes and hands_on run inside spanish.csv (Levels L1 to L4 in one file). The early-years companions of
Levels 1 and 2 use the same rows, so they add no edges.

## Needs from earlier grades

- None. A Spanish level assumes no row of another subject: a learner of any age starts at Level 1, and every
  lean below is a pointer for the teacher that the unit reteaches, never a prerequisite (world-languages
  STANDARD.md section 6).

## Leans on other subjects' rows (cross_subject, pointers only)

- l1-interpersonal: ela:ek-discussion (grade K), turn-taking in a partner exchange.
- l1-conn-making: social-studies:ss3-home-region (grade 3), reading a political map.
- l1-comp-language: ela:e2-sentences (grade 2), what a sentence is, to compare word order.
- l2-cult-practices: social-studies:ss2-culture-migration (grade 2), the parts of a culture only; no task asks how
  a family moved.
- l2-cult-products: social-studies:ss4-regional-cultures (grade 4), Spanish place names and buildings in US regions.
- l2-comp-language: ela:e3-word-strategies (grade 3), word parts to work out a new word.
- l3-interpretive: ela:e5-figurative (grade 5), simile and metaphor in a song or poem.
- l3-comp-language: ela:e6-word-strategies (grade 6, so from Level 3), shared Latin roots.
- l4-presentational: ela:e9-sl-presentation (grade 9, so from Level 4), a presentation listeners can follow.
- l4-conn-making: social-studies:wh1-geography-shapes-contact (grade 9, so from Level 4), geography and where
  languages meet.

## Hands on to later grades

- None. Level 4 rows hand on nothing, and no other subject's row names a Spanish row.
