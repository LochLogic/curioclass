# Working in the Curio Class repository

Read this first, whatever model you are. Curio Class is a free K-12 curriculum and tools site. Several workers
(Codex, Claude on more than one account, people) take turns in this one repository. Never two at once.

## Taking a turn

1. Every job runs inside a turn. `python -X utf8 scripts/turn.py start <your name> --job <id>` (or `--role <role>`)
   pulls, checks that nobody holds the turn, takes it, and prints your job brief. If it refuses, stop and say why.
2. Do the job the brief describes, and only that job.
3. `python -X utf8 scripts/turn.py end <your name>` runs the gates. If the job added a failure, the turn stays with
   you: fix it and run `end` again. If you cannot fix it, `end <your name> --fail "why"` hands the turn back and
   marks the job failed for a person.
4. You never commit, push, pull, rebase, stash, reset or switch branches yourself: `start` and `end` do all git.

If a runner started you, it has already run `start` and will run `end`; just do the job in the brief you were given.

The lead session (the Claude session Cory works with directly) and people work outside the queue, commit and push
their own work, and do so only while nobody holds the turn (`turn.py status` says "Turn: free").
`python -X utf8 scripts/turn.py status` shows the queue. The pipeline guide is docs/pipeline/README.md.

## Rules that bind every job

- Never use em dashes (use a comma, a colon, a period or "to"). Write "Curio Class" in full, never "Curio" alone.
- No company or product names in anything a student, teacher or family reads. No claim that anything was reviewed,
  approved or endorsed by anyone, and no claim that it meets a rubric.
- Use the locked words in docs/GLOSSARY.md (unit, section, scope and sequence, end-of-unit assessment, exit ticket,
  topic). Ids, folders and CSV columns keep older names on purpose (`stretch` means unit, `unit` means section).
- Base texts and references (S:\CurioClass-sources\, record in docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md): a base text (CC BY,
  e.g. Illustrative Mathematics) may be adapted into our days, molded to our voice and format, and its source goes
  in the day's credit. Anything under `reference-only-do-not-copy\` is for checking ideas, sequence, models and
  pacing only: never copy its problems, wording or images into a Curio Class page.
- Course facts live in data/plans/<course>/ as CSV. Plan tables between `<!-- gen:... -->` markers are generated by
  `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py tables <course>`; never edit them by hand. Log defects with `plan.py defect`.
- The gates are `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py check` and `python -X utf8 scripts/lesson.py check`; build pages
  with `python -X utf8 scripts/lesson.py build --pdf` (build/ is committed).
- Never touch: data/closet/words, closet/words, data/media-manifest.txt, docs/Curio-Class-Plan.pdf,
  "docs/US Curriculum Design Blueprint.pdf", docs/Return/, docs/agents/audit/wordwall-movement-cards.jpg,
  anything under .claude/, or the folder S:\CurioClass-spec2.
- Questions of scope, money, people or audience belong to Cory: put them in your last message, never decide them.
  Item-level choices (a number, a wording, a key) you decide and say so.
- Work directly: read what you need, write, run the gates, stop. Your token use is measured.
