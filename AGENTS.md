# Working in the Curio Class repository

Read this first, whatever model you are. Curio Class is a free K-12 curriculum and tools site. Several workers
(Codex, Claude on more than one account, people) work in this one repository.

## The gate

One command checks a course: `python -X utf8 scripts/gate.py --course <course>`. It runs the plan, lesson, unit
plan, lint and ledger checks for that course plus the shared checks, prints every failing line, and compares them
with the baseline. Store the baseline before you change anything (`--course <course> --baseline`); afterwards the
gate exits 1 only when you added a failure. `--changed` checks the courses your uncommitted files touch, and
`--all --site` is the whole repository (the lead runs it before and after each larger step). Never run plan.py
check or lesson.py check unfiltered on your own: the gate does it.

## Who commits, and side by side work

Two ways to work:

1. **A queued job** runs inside a turn. `python -X utf8 scripts/turn.py start <your name> --job <id>` (or
   `--role <role>`) pulls, checks that nobody holds the turn, takes it, and prints your job brief. Do only that
   job. `python -X utf8 scripts/turn.py end <your name>` runs the gate for the job's course. If the job added a
   failure, the turn stays with you: fix it and run `end` again. If you cannot fix it,
   `end <your name> --fail "why"` hands the turn back and marks the job failed for a person. You never commit,
   push, pull, rebase, stash, reset or switch branches yourself: `start` and `end` do all git. If a runner started
   you, it has already run `start` and will run `end`; just do the job in the brief you were given. The queue
   (data/jobs.csv) stays in use until the pilot it belongs to retires.
2. **The lead session** (the Claude session Cory works with directly) and people work outside the queue. The lead
   commits and pushes its own work and its agents' work, staging only its own paths, and only while nobody holds
   the turn (`turn.py status` says "Turn: free").

Agents may work side by side when each works in a different course. Two workers never write the same course, or
the same file, at once. Ledgers are per course and written through locked writes (scripts/rows.py), so separate
courses do not collide. Shared files (data/plans/later.csv, docs/, scripts/) are the lead's to change.
`python -X utf8 scripts/turn.py status` shows the queue. The pipeline guide is docs/pipeline/README.md.

## Rules that bind every job

- Never use em dashes (use a comma, a colon, a period or "to"). Write "Curio Class" in full, never "Curio" alone.
- Use US spelling (behavior, color, practice as verb and noun, analyze). Fix UK spellings in a page when it is next
  edited; no separate pass (Cory, 2026-11-21, Desk q-fix-health-2).
- A student 18 or over (some grade 12 students) gets the family note themselves and makes their own leave-out or
  consent choice, in every subject; the school's own rules still apply (Cory, 2026-11-22, Desk q-prog-sel-1).
- No company or product names in anything a student, teacher or family reads. No claim that anything was reviewed,
  approved or endorsed by anyone, and no claim that it meets a rubric.
- Use the locked words in docs/GLOSSARY.md (unit, section, scope and sequence, end-of-unit assessment, exit ticket,
  topic). Ids, folders and CSV columns keep older names on purpose (`stretch` means unit, `unit` means section).
- Base texts and references (S:\CurioClass-sources\, record in docs/sources/BASE-TEXTS.md): a base text (CC BY,
  e.g. Illustrative Mathematics) may be adapted into our days, molded to our voice and format, and its source goes
  in the day's credit. Anything under `reference-only-do-not-copy\` is for checking ideas, sequence, models and
  pacing only: never copy its problems, wording or images into a Curio Class page.
- A source page that refuses automated reads (a 403, a bot wall) is never fetched another way: try one other solid
  source, and if none opens, keep the fact with the url and status "browser check" and list it in your report. The
  lead opens those pages in Claude in Chrome (standing rule, 2026-11-22).
- Course facts live in data/plans/<course>/ as CSV. Plan tables between `<!-- gen:... -->` markers are generated by
  `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py tables <course>`; never edit them by hand. Log defects with `plan.py defect`.
- The gates are `python -X utf8 scripts/plan.py check` and `python -X utf8 scripts/lesson.py check`; build pages
  with `python -X utf8 scripts/lesson.py build --pdf` (build/ is local output, git-ignored since 2026-11-24).
- Never touch: data/closet/words, closet/words, data/media-manifest.txt, docs/Return/ (Cory's private folder,
  git-ignored), or anything under .claude/.
- Questions of scope, money, people or audience belong to Cory: put them in your last message, never decide them.
  Item-level choices (a number, a wording, a key) you decide and say so.
- A decision changes the doc that holds the rule, in place, and adds one line to
  docs/agents/decisions/DESK-ANSWERS.md, the decision log (who decided what, when and why). Never add a new dated
  paragraph under an old rule. Living docs state each rule once, as it stands now; dated reports under docs/reviews/
  are records and are never rewritten.
- Work directly: read what you need, write, run the gates, stop. Your token use is measured.
