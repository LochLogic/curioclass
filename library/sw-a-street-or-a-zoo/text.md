![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

Sonu, Monu and Rina set out to play.  
They saw a kitten.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

She was looking at a big rat.  
"Oh look!" shouted Sonu.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Rina saw a tiny ant going towards the big rat.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

Suddenly, a very big shadow fell upon them all.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

A very big eagle came down  
to the wall.  
A tiny ant, a small kitten,  
a big rat and a very big eagle  
in the same long street!

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

What would the three clever children do now?

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

They clapped their little hands!  
The eagle spread its big wings and flew away.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

Rina made the ant climb onto a leaf. She left the leaf on the wall.  
The ant saw a grain of sugar. She picked it up and ran home.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

The big rat picked up a half-eaten pakoda and went back into the drain.  
The small kitten said 'miaaww' and started licking her paw.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Monu got her some milk in a cup.  
The three kids played with her.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

The very big eagle sitting on the tree blinked and flew far away.
