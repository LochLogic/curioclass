![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

Nina is going on her first train ride!

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

But she is not happy at all. Her family is moving from Kolkata to New Delhi.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

“Do we really have to move?” Nina asks. “All my friends and Nani-Nana are going to miss me. Where will I find the perfect puchkas? Why can’t you find another job in Kolkata, Baba?”

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“Don’t be sad, Nina,” says Baba. “You will make new friends, and find a new puchkawalla.”  
But Nina doesn't want to listen. Ma and Baba try to cheer her up.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

“Would you like some ice cream in a cup?”  
“Ice cream in a cone?”  
“Ice cream on a stick?”  
“Cucumbers?”  
“Toffees?”  
“No! I said no!” Nina shakes her head.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Ma and Baba do not know what else to do. Then Baba smiles.  
“I see a...” begins Baba.  
“Oooh! My favourite game,” says Ma.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

“I see something that is brown,” says Baba.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

“My shoes? The hills? The suitcase?” asks Ma.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

“Suitcase is correct!” Baba says, clapping.  
Nina puts down her book and starts listening.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

“My turn,” says Ma. “I see something that is yellow.”  
“Mustard field!” says Baba.  
“My hairband!” says Nina.  
“Correct,” Ma claps for Nina.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

Nina laughs and joins in. “I see something blue.”  
“The sky!” says Ma.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

“I see something brown,” says Ma.  
“That hill!” says Baba.  
“I see something white,” says Baba.  
“A cow!” says Nina.

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

“I see something green,” says Nina  
“Grass!” reply Ma and Baba.  
“I see something black,” says Ma.  
“A crow!” says Nina.

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

“I see a tall animal with brown spots,” says Baba.  
“Huh! You saw a leopard?” asks Ma.  
“No, a giraffe!” says Baba.  
Nina looks out of the window and yells, “Giraffes do not live in India! You cheated, Baba.” Everyone starts laughing.

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

Baba and Ma spread a blanket on their seat. They fluff up a pillow for Nina.  
“Can we play this game again when we reach Delhi?” Nina smiles.  
“I am sure it will be even more fun to play this game in a new place!”
