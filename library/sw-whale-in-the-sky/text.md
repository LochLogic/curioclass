Whale Watching!  
Here are some fun facts about whales.  
Whales are of many shapes and sizes, and live in all the oceans of the planet.  
They even breathe out fountains in different shapes and sizes! Scientists who study them can tell different whales apart by observing their breathing.

---

![Picture for page 17](art/17.jpg)

Blue whales don’t have teeth. Instead, they eat very small animals by swallowing a large amount of water and then filtering the animals in their baleen plates.  
They are mammals, which means they have babies, just like we do. Their babies are called calves. The calves swim with their mothers for years to learn all the wisdom needed to live happily in the oceans.

Crossing the Line  
During summer, a mother whale and her calf move towards the polar regions, where there is more food. In winter, when the polar seas freezes, they move towards the tropics. Whales which live in the Southern hemisphere never cross over to the North. Those that live in the North never cross over to the South. Since the equator is not a real line in the oceans, only the whales know why they do not cross it! Even with all our technology, we still do not know how they do it.
