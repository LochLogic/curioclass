The Secret of the Magic Block  
The magic block in the story is called a bar magnet. Magnets come in many shapes. They can be round, rectangular, square or U-shaped.  
A magnet cannot stick to all objects. It can only stick to objects that have special elements in them. These special elements that allow an object to stick to a magnet are iron, nickel and cobalt.
