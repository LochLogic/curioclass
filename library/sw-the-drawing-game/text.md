![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

Jeenu is spending her summer holidays at her grandma’s house. She has no friends here. She is bored.  
“Play with me, Ajji!” she says.  
Ajji says, “Go get a piece of chalk. Let’s play a game.”

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

Jeenu is excited. What game will Ajji play? Ajji draws a shape.  
“Can you guess what this is?” she asks.  
“It is a circle.” Jeenu makes a face. She does not like this silly game.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

“Yes, but it is also something more,” says Ajji.  
Jeenu thinks, then she smiles.  
“It is a smiley face!” she says.  
Ajji claps. “You understood the game!”

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“Look at what I have drawn!” says Jeenu.  
Activity: What can you draw with three circles?

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Ajji draws another shape.  
“A triangle!” Jeenu shouts.  
Then she laughs.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

“Look what I have made!”

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

“Well done, Jeenu!” says Ajji. “Look who has come to play with us!”  
Activity: What can you draw with three circles and four triangles?

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

Ajji draws a shape with four sides.  
“Is it a rectangle?” Jeenu asks.  
“Look carefully. All the sides are the same length.”  
“It is a square! Hmmm...see Ajji, I have drawn a window.”

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

“Do you want a tree near your window?” asks Ajji.  
“Yes Ajji, thank you!” says Jeenu.  
She looks at all the chalk drawings. Can she paint them now?  
Activity: What can you draw with a triangle and a rectangle?

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Soon, Jeenu holds up her painting.  
“Ah, so many circles  
and triangles and squares!” says Ajji.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

“Yes,  
but it is also something more, Ajji! It is a bird flying to its  
home in a tree. And guess who is coming to lunch? Her best friend,  
Mr. Giraffe!”

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

“That’s  
so clever, Jeenu!” Ajji exclaims.  
Jeenu  
wants to draw some more. What do she and Ajji draw next?  
Activity: What  
would you draw using circles, triangles and squares?
