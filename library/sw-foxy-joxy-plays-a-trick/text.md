Foxy Joxy was a sly fox. He sold big, fresh watermelons.  
Watermelons for sale!

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

“Your price is too high,” said Rabbit Jabbit.  
“You are asking too much,” said Gia Giraffe.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Foxy Joxy had a clever idea.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

He slit open a big watermelon,

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

scooped out the fruit,

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

and gobbled it up.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

Then he filled it with water!  
He took a needle and thread and sewed the watermelon back together.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

He sold his water melons for a cheap price.  
Many animals bought from Foxy Joxy.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

“This watermelon has only water in it,” complained Funky Monkey.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

“Well, you got what you paid for,” said Foxy Joxy.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

“We need to teach Foxy Joxy a lesson,” said Ellie Elephant.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

The animals gathered around and came up with a plan.  
“I have an idea,” said Oliver Owl.

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

They took an empty  
watermelon,

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

stuffed Foxy Joxy into it...

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

and played a game of football with it.

---

![Picture for page 16](art/16.jpg)

Foxy Joxy was dizzy!  
He learnt his lesson and never cheated again.
