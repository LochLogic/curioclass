![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

Once upon a time, a traveller fell from the sky.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

It landed on a field of tomatoes.  
Kamala and her Thatha ran out. All their tomatoes were crushed, leaving only a shoe.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Kamala picked up the shoe. It put out a claw and scratched her. HISSSSS.  
“Shoo cat!” Kamala yelled.  
The shoecat yawned.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

Kamala picked up the shoecat and took it to the goat shed. She gave it a bowl of milk. The shoecat sniffed it and spat in disgust. Thoo! Then it ate up all the goats’ grass.  
After that, the shoecat went wherever Kamala went.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

At school, it scratched Shamim Miss. “Shoo! Cat!” she yelled at it. The shoecat spat at her.  
It scratched all Kamala’s classmates. Where the shoecat scratched, the wounds glowed green.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

The shoecat scratched the newspaper man. It scratched seventeen goats, thirteen hens, three dogs, and one Thatha. It scratched everyone except Kamala, because it had already scratched her.  
“Ow! Shoo cat!” they all yelled in Kannada and English, Tamil and Urdu and Telugu, Goatish and Doggo and Chickenam. The cat replied, “Thooo!”

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

The school put up a sign: NO CATS

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

The shop put up a sign, too: CATS NOT ALLOWED

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

Even the playground got a sign: CATS STAY OUT

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Kamala and the shoecat went home, sadly.  
They found Thatha putting up his own sign: CAT GO BACK

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

That night, Kamala and the shoecat climbed up to the roof to watch the skies. The moon glowed green and warm.  
The shoecat turned its head up and yowled, “Owoooo!”

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

In the goat shed, seventeen goats turned their heads up and howled: Owoooo!  
In his bedroom, Thatha sat up and put on his glasses. Sleepily, he said, “Owoo?”

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

Across the village, Kamala’s teacher, her classmates, the newspaper man, thirteen hens, and three dogs lifted their faces to the green moon and howled: “Owoooo!”  
On the roof, Kamala bellowed:  
“ OWOOO! ”

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

With a flash of lightning, it began to rain. The no-cat signs melted away.

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

Then the thunder started, BA-DUM DUM-DUM! Every piece of glass in the village broke. PUTT! went the light bulbs. Pop-POP went the pickle jars. Mirrors CRACKED and crumbled.  
Windows slammed against walls and smashed glass flew everywhere, even on the roof.

---

![Picture for page 16](art/16.jpg)

Kamala hid under her bed.

---

![Picture for page 17](art/17.jpg)

The next morning, Kamala crawled out from under her bed. Thatha was sitting on the steps.  
He was eating a furry green tomato.  
“Wonderful! Thank your thoocat,” Thatha said. “What a good shoecat!”

---

![Picture for page 18](art/18.jpg)

In the field were lots of tall tomato plants, some medium-sized goats, and one short shoecat.

---

![Picture for page 19](art/19.jpg)

“Thoo!” said the shoecat, in a friendly way.  
“Thoo!” Kamala agreed as she bit into a green tomato.
