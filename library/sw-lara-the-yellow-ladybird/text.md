![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

Lara the ladybird was a special bug.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

Unlike all her  
friends, she had bright yellow wings.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Everyone loved  
her yellow wings.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

Each morning,  
Bibi Butterfly said hello.  
And Manto Mantis  
always waved.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Even Sesa, the  
sulky spider,  
was happy to see her.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

At school, she  
played with lots of friends.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

But Lara wanted  
to be like the  
other ladybirds.  
“I wish I had  
red wings like you,  
Mama,” she  
cried.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

So one day, to  
cheer her up, Lara’s mother painted her wings bright red.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

The next morning, nobody greeted Lara on her way to school.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

And when she got  
there, none of her  
friends said hello.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

Lara sat all  
alone.  
No one noticed  
her new red wings.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

Until Miss Miya  
spotted her and said: “You’ve painted  
your lovely yellow wings!”

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

Lara’s classmates  
were shocked.  
“Your wings are special!”  
“So unique!”  
“So rare!”

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

“Lara,” Miss Miya  
said, “your yellow wings are what make you you.  
Like Sipho’s spot… and Sally’s legs.”

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

Back home, Lara  
took a long bath and scrubbed until her golden wings gleamed.

---

![Picture for page 16](art/16.jpg)

“I’ll never paint  
my wings again!”  
she thought.  
Except, maybe once or twice …

---

![Picture for page 17](art/17.jpg)

To try a bit of  
purple …  
or something nice.  
But not for ever  
and just for fun.
