Roja is sad. Amma is going to work.  
“I will be back before bedtime, kannamma,” says Amma.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

“But at what time?” asks Roja.  
“At exactly 9 o’clock tonight,” replies Amma.  
Tick tock! Tick tock!  
It is 6 o’clock in the morning.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

“I want Amma to give me milk. Not you!” says Roja to Appa.  
Tick tock! Tick tock! It is 8 o’clock in the morning.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“Let’s buy potatoes, Thaatha!” says Roja.  
“But we had potatoes just yesterday. How about brinjal today?” Thaatha asks.  
Tick tock! Tick tock! It is 10 o’clock in the morning.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

“Paati, let’s eat. Thaatha is making pappadam!” Roja yells.  
Tick tock! Tick tock! It is 1 o’clock in the afternoon.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Honk! Honk! Roja’s Akka is back from school.  
“Paati says I will go to school with you after this summer. Won’t that be fun, Akka?” says Roja, excitedly.  
Tick tock! Tick tock! It is 3 o’clock in the afternoon.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

“It’s time to go home, Roja,” Appa calls out.  
“Just one last time!” shouts Roja.  
Tick tock! Tick tock! It is 5 o’clock in the evening.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

“Selvi, eat your dosai quickly! Only then will you become big and strong,” says Roja.  
Tick tock! Tick tock! It is 7 o’clock at night.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

“AMMMAAAA!” Roja shouts.  
“What did you do all day, Roja?” Amma asks.  
Tick tock! Tick tock!  
It is almost 9 o’clock at night.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

“I had a lot of fun today, Amma!  
Appa fed me breakfast.  
I bought brinjal with Thaatha.  
He made pappadam for lunch.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

I picked up Akka from the school van.  
I played in the park.  
I fed Selvi dinner.  
Appa told me a story and now you are back!” Roja says, with a wide smile.
