![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

Ghum-Ghum was excited. She  
was going for her first swim.  
"BBRRRAAAP! GHRRUMB!" said  
her family, who were excited too.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

Ghum-Ghum’s  
snout touched the water and… WHOOOSH!  
The  
river rushed all around her. Ghum-Ghum  
paddled her feet and swished her tail.  
"Look, Papa! I’m swimming! Look…!"

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

"… Papa?"  
Ghum-Ghum  
looked in front and looked behind. She looked to the right, and then to the  
left. "Papa?"  
She  
was alone. Her family had swum off without her.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

An  
otter floated past.  
"Have  
you seen my family, Mr. Otter?" asked Ghum-Ghum.  
"No,  
but I’ve seen a lot of stars," said the otter. "Would you like to see some?"  
"Maybe  
next time. Thanks!" said Ghum-Ghum.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Just  
then – "BRROFFTT!  
RRRAAPP!"  
It  
was her family! Ghum-Ghum giggled happily, and swam towards the sound.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Suddenly,  
a dolphin leapt up in front of her.  
"Do  
you know where my family is, Miss Dolphin?" asked Ghum-Ghum.  
"No,  
but I do know where the yummiest fish are," said the dolphin. "Would you like  
to eat one?"  
"Maybe  
next time. Thanks!" said Ghum-Ghum.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

Just  
then – "DRRUPHH! GHRAAAP!"  
It  
was her family! Ghum-Ghum giggled happily, and swam towards the sound.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

A  
snail lay on a rock by the river bank.  
"Can you help me find my  
family, Sister Snail?" asked Ghum-Ghum.  
"I can help you find a nice  
flat rock to sit on, if you want," said the snail.  
"Maybe next time. Thanks!"  
said Ghum-Ghum.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

Just then – "PHBTHRR!  
FFRAFFFT!"  
It was her family! Ghum-Ghum  
giggled happily, and swam towards the sound.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Ghum-Ghum  
saw a big old gharial. Many little gharials swam around him.  
"Have  
you seen my family, Grandpa Gharial?"  
Ghum-Ghum asked.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

"Is your name Ghum-Ghum?" asked the old gharial.  
"Yes, I’m Ghum-Ghum," she said.  
"Then I HAVE seen your family! They’ve been looking everywhere for you, little one. Listen! There, down the river! Can you hear them?"

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

Ghum-Ghum listened. All the  
other little gharials listened too.  
Just then, from up the river  
– "GHRAPP! GHUM! BRRAFFTT!  
GHUM! GHUUUM! GHOOOOOOM!"  
It was her family, and they  
weren’t very far off! Ghum-Ghum giggled, and swam towards the sound.

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

Soon, Ghum-Ghum reached a  
sand bank. She crawled onto it, tired  
from all her swimming.  
"Ghum-Ghum?" said a  
voice.  
"Papa?"

---

![Picture for page 16](art/16.jpg)

A large, dark shape came towards  
her.  
"Papa!"  
Papa bent down and lovingly nudged Ghum-Ghum.  
"Welcome home,  
Ghum-Ghum!" said Papa. "Where did you go off to?"  
"I went on a glorious  
adventure!" said Ghum-Ghum, giggling. "And tomorrow, I want to go again!"

![Picture for page 18](art/18.jpg)

Ghum-Ghum lives by the great river Ganga with her big, noisy family of gharials. She goes swimming every day, to catch insects and fish for her lunch. Ghum-Ghum meets all kinds of creatures on her daily swims: dolphins, otters, snails, water birds, fishermen in boats, buffaloes, snakes, and many others.  
How many of Ghum-Ghum's river friends can you spot in this page and the next?
