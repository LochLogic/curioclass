Anu loves to look at things,  
big things and small.  
Houses on hills,  
ants on a wall.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

Things in the kitchen,  
things on the shelf.  
Things in the garden,  
things on herself.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Anu sees patterns  
and shapes everywhere,  
nothing at all can escape her stare!

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

Spots on a ladybird,  
circles in trees,  
shapes made by people,  
shapes made by bees.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Lines on a leaf, just  
like lines on her hand.  
The marks of her feet  
in wet seaside sand.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Waves in the water,  
waves in her hair.  
Mushrooms on tree  
trunks, birds in the air.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

Words in a book that  
can take her to places,  
full of new colours,  
new stories, new faces.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

Things of the colour that  
she loves best - RED!  
The three wiggly hairs on  
her brother Boo's head.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

Sprinkles on the cake  
Daddy baked today,  
like the stars that twinkle  
at her from far away.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Rabbits in clouds  
floating up in the sky.  
Elephants in shadows -  
come, give it a try!

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

Anu is happy with the  
things that she sees.  
Do you also see what  
little Anu sees?
