![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

One day, MOUSE found a very big bun.  
“I will take this bun into my house,”  
he said.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

He pushed the bun from the back. It  
did not move.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

He pushed it from the front. The bun did not move.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

He pushed it from the right, the bun did not move.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

He ran around the bun and pushed it from the left, but it still did not move.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Hmm... I will tie a string around the  
bun and pull it into my house.  
Mouse ran to the house and got a string, but it was too short.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

He went back and got a longer string.  
He tied the bun with the long string, then pulled and pulled but it did not move.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

Mouse sat down and ate a little bit of the bun.  
It was very good. He ate a little more, then a little more, and a little more till the bun became smaller and smaller.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

He pushed the bun, it rolled right  
into his house. Mouse was very happy.  
The bun was safely in his house.  
But he had eaten so much bun that his  
stomach had become so... so... big.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Mouse pushed and pushed himself but  
he could not get into his house.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

Mouse sat outside his door holding his stomach  
and groaned, “I should not have eaten so much bun.  
I should have shared it with someone.”
