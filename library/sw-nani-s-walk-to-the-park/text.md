Once a week, Nani\* goes to the park.  
Today, Venki wants to go with her.  
“I walk slowly,” says Nani. “And I take my time getting there.”  
“I don’t mind,” says Venki.  
So Nani picks up her shopping bag and starts to cross the road.  
“But Nani,” says Venki, “the park is that way!”  
\*Nani means grandmother.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“We’ll go this way,” says Nani,  
“through the Lane of Treasures.”

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

The Lane of Treasures? What a wonderful name  
for the market. The shops here sell everything!  
When Nani finishes shopping, Venki points  
to a gate and asks, “Shall  
we take a shortcut, Nani?  
We’ll be at the park in no time.”

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

“No,” says Nani.  
“We’ll go through the Lane of Beauty.”

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

In the Lane of Beauty, the people stitch amazing goddhis.\*  
A lady gives Nani a goddhi in which Venki sees a piece  
of her favourite saree and a bit of his Ma’s kurta.  
“Shall we go to the park now?” asks Venki.  
\*A goddhi (pronounced go-the-dee) is a traditional hand-stitched quilt  
made of recycled material, usually soft, old cotton sarees.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

“Yes,” says Nani.  
“We’ll take the Lane of Happiness.”

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

“Come, come,” Nani calls softly and Venki laughs  
as dogs and cats tumble out from everywhere!

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

Nani pours the milk she bought at the market and they watch as the animals lick their bowls clean.  
Venki doesn’t want to leave the Lane of Happiness but Nani reminds him that they are on their way to the park.  
Next, Nani and Venki walk through a lane behind a little school.

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

The children are playing langdi, lagori and sakhli.\*  
“Is this the Lane of Noise?” asks Venki.  
“No, it’s the Lane of Mischief. It reminds me of you and your friends!” laughs Nani.  
“Have you named all the lanes in the city?” asks Venki.  
“Not all,” smiles Nani, “only the ones that are important to me!”  
\*These are some traditional games played in India.

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 16](art/16.jpg)

“The park is not far now, but you will have to be a little patient.  
I want to stop at the Lane of Friendship.”

---

![Picture for page 17](art/17.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 18](art/18.jpg)

In the Lane of Friendship, Nani visits her dear friend.  
They were girls together and they have grown old together.  
They whisper and they giggle.

---

![Picture for page 19](art/19.jpg)

Then, Nani gives her friend a hug and says,  
“It is time to leave. We will now walk  
through the Lane of Dreams.”

---

![Picture for page 20](art/20.jpg)

The Lane of Dreams has more books than Venki’s school library!  
The bookseller sells old books for very little money  
and buys the ones people have finished reading.

---

![Picture for page 21](art/21.jpg)

And they walk down the Lane of Magic.

---

![Picture for page 22](art/22.jpg)

Nani chooses a book and says,  
“Let’s go, we are almost at the park now.”  
And they walk down the Lane of Magic.

---

![Picture for page 23](art/23.jpg)

Venki looks up at the big trees  
that offer shade, and feels a cool breeze.  
Nani shows him Gulmohar, Amaltas and Jarul trees.

---

![Picture for page 24](art/24.jpg)

The flowers on the road look like a magic carpet.

---

![Picture for page 25](art/25.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 26](art/26.jpg)

At last they are at the park.  
It has been a long time since they left home.  
Venki looks around and thinks of all the people  
and places he has seen that morning.

---

![Picture for page 27](art/27.jpg)

---

![Picture for page 28](art/28.jpg)

“I like your lanes, Nani,” he says.  
“May I come with you next week too?”
