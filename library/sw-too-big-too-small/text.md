“I can’t lift you up, Shanu!” says Ammi.  
“You are too big!”

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

“You can’t walk  
to school alone, Shanu!” says Abbu.  
“You are too small!”

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

“You can’t sleep in the baby’s cot, Shanu!” says Dadu.  
“You are too big!”

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“You can’t carry the baby to the park, Shanu!” says Dadi.  
“You are too small!”

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Shanu is puzzled.  
Too big! Too small!  
How can she be  
too big and  
too small all at once?

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Too big  
to wear her old  
pink frock.  
Too small to make dosas  
at the stove.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

Too big  
to climb up on Dadu’s back?  
Too small  
to carry the baby  
on hers?

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

“What am I  
the right size for?”  
Shanu wonders.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

Ammi smiles and says, “Why, you are just big enough to go to big school.”

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

“And you are just small enough for me to carry you  
on my shoulders,” says Abbu.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

“You are just big enough to take me for my morning walks,” says Dadu.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

“And you are just small enough for me to tell stories to,” says Dadi.

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

“And you will always, always be the perfect size...  
for this!" all say,  
and give  
her a warm, wonderful hug.
