![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

“I have a wiggly  
tooth , Mama!”  
This is Bunu’s  
first loose tooth.  
She is excited, but also a little scared.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

"Push it with your tongue,” Mama says.  
Bunu pushed  
and pushed  
and pushed.  
“It is not ready,” Mama says. “Wait for some time and it will fall out on its own.”

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Bunu goes to Papa. “Papa,” Bunu says.  
“I have a wiggly tooth!”  
Papa shakes the tooth gently. "It is not  
ready,” he says. "Wait for some time and it will fall out on its  
own."

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

But Bunu does not want to wait. She runs to her room with a long piece of string.  
She ties one end of the string around her tooth and the other around the door knob.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Bunu sits on the floor  
and waits  
and waits  
and waits.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Finally Bunu gets tired of waiting and waiting and waiting.  
She unties the string from the door and sits on her bed with a loud sigh.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

Chunnu, her sister,  
walks in after some time. “That was a terrible  
idea,” she says, pointing at the door. “Why don’t I pull the string, and your  
tooth will come out like that?”

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

Chunnu snapped her  
fingers.  
Bunu closes her eyes  
and opens her mouth.  
"One, two,  
three!" Chunnu pulls .  
"OWWW!” Bunu screams.  
"It is not  
ready,” Chunnu says.  
"Wait for some time and  
it will fall out on its  
own."

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

“This tooth will  
never come out!” Bunu says.  
"When we were  
small, we would throw our teeth on the  
roof and ask Musa to give us his in  
return,” says Mama.  
“Because we wanted teeth as strong as a mouse’s.”  
Bunu runs out and  
cries, “Musa, Musa!  
Here is my big strong tooth for you.  
If you like it, please  
come and take it away!”

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

That night, Bunu  
dreams of a big mouse.  
Musa is holding  
something gleaming between his teeth.  
He stops and stares  
at her, then scurries away into a hole.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

As soon as Bunu wakes up, she pushes the tooth with her tongue.  
But there is nothing there!  
Did she swallow it? Did someone pull it out when she was sleeping?  
Where is it?  
There it is! Right next to the pillow. Bunu picks the tooth up and dashes outside.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

“Musa, Musa, please  
take my tooth  
and give me a new one!” she says.  
She throws her  
tooth on the roof  
and runs back inside to tell Mama.
