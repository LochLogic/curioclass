![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

All of us went to the Dasara fair.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

Papa bought Chintu fancy glasses.  
Mother bought me a bright blue cap.  
The baby got sugar candy.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

On the way home, a very strong wind came.  
It blew my cap away.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

My cap got stuck on a branch of the old Pipal tree.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

I cried a lot.  
And I did not eat my dinner.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

Later that night, the moon came up.  
It looked at my cap on the old Pipal tree.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

It tried on my cap.  
The moon smiled happily.  
I had to smile too.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

After school the next day, my mother gave me a shiny new red cap. “The moon sent it,” she said.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

That night, both the moon and I wore our caps and smiled.  
We were happy.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Do you think the sun needs a hat?

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

Guess which cap I am wearing today!
