Hashim loves mangoes.  
This summer, the mangoes are about to spoil.  
Hashim wants to save them.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Baba tells Hashim that if he removes water from  
the mangoes, they will not spoil.  
“Germs cannot live without water,” he says.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“How do we take water out of the mangoes?” asks Hashim.  
“Add salt to them!” says Baba.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Then Baba and Hashim keep the mangoes out in the sun to dry.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

They put the mangoes in a bottle with some oil.  
Oil doesn’t let germs from the air reach the mangoes.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

“Baba, we have made pickle!” says Hashim.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

How can we preserve our favourite  
fruits and vegetables?

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

Potato slice + boil + dry = potato chips  
(to be fried and eaten)  
Any fruit + sugar + lemon + cook = jam

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Tomato + salt + sun + oil = sun-dried tomatoes  
Lemon + salt = lemon preserves
