![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

On her birthday,  
Tra's mother gave her a special gift.  
“Wow! Red beans and a gardening book," Tra said. "Now I can grow my own red bean plant. ”

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

First, plant the seed,  
Tra read.  
So she did.  
“You’re doing great, Tra!” the book told her.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Now, water it.  
La La La! Tra sang as she did just that.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“My red bean plant will soon grow up,” Tra told her friends.  
“You have to water it a lot to keep it healthy," they said. " Just like kids have to drink a lot of milk to grow big and strong!”

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

A few days later,  
the red bean sprouted.  
“Look, Mom!” Tra called.  
"It's growing!"

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

The next morning, Tra watered her plant again.  
" La La La! Drink, drink, drink!" she sang. "If you drink more water, you'll grow up faster!”  
"Oh my, Tra, not like that!" said the book.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

Tra watered again at noon. La La La!  
"Oh my, Tra!" the book said, louder now. " My pages say not to water a plant at noon."

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

But Tra kept right on watering.  
La La La, La La La!  
Oh my, Tra! You've forgotten to read me!

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

The next day,  
Tra gave her mother instructions as she left for school. "Give my plant more water at noon, please!"  
“Have you read the book?" her mother asked.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

"In my pages, it says to water the plant only once a day," the book said sadly.  
Oh my, Tra!

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

Three days later, Tra was sad, too.  
"My poor plant!" she cried.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

At school, she talked to her friends.  
“I have been taking such good care of my plant. Why is it so sad?"  
“What does the book say?” they asked.

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

“Oh my," Tra said. "I stopped reading it."

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

At home, Tra showed the plant to her mother.  
“I'm sorry, Mom,” Tra said. "I'm going to read the book now."  
"That's a great idea," her mother replied.

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

Do not water the plant too much, or its roots will become soggy.  
Tra read.  
The leaves will then droop and turn brown.

---

![Picture for page 16](art/16.jpg)

“I’m so sorry, Red Bean,” Tra whispered.

---

![Picture for page 17](art/17.jpg)

“The plant won’t be sad, because he  
knows you love him," her mother said.  
"You can show him your love  
by learning how to take good care  
of him."

---

![Picture for page 18](art/18.jpg)

"You can also say sorry to the book, because you ignored her. She needs your attention, too!"

---

![Picture for page 19](art/19.jpg)

A few days later, Red Bean was healthy again.  
Now Tra and her gardening book were best friends!
