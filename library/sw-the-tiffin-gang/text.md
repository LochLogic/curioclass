![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

TRRRRRRRIIIIIING.  
Tiffin time.  
Meenu finds idli and podi in her tiffin box.  
When will Amma pack something new?  
Meenu hates lunch break.

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

Yummy smells waft towards her  
from other tables. Meenu’s tummy  
growls and her mouth waters.  
She pushes her lunch away.  
Three rows behind, Kamlesh is  
sulking. Her tiffin looks yummy.  
Doesn’t she like it?

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

Kamlesh looks up and sees Meenu  
with her tiffin box. “Amma has  
sent me idlis again,” says Meenu.  
Kamlesh shrugs at her own box.  
“Jowar bhakri and bhaji. Again.”

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

Meenu’s tummy rumbles.  
GUR  
GUR GUR  
GUR  
“Can I taste it?” asks Meenu.  
Kamlesh pushes her tiffin towards  
Meenu and grabs Meenu’s box.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

Kamlesh gobbles up Meenu’s idlis.  
Meenu wolfs down Kamlesh’s  
jowar bhakri.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

The next day, Meenu counts the minutes to tiffin time. Amma has packed extra idlis for Kamlesh.  
Trrrrrring!  
Kamlesh slides over to make space for Meenu. Today, Kamlesh’s box is filled with mutke.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

Sachi comes over with her tiffin box.  
“I have pakhala bhata with potatoes. I saw  
both of you sharing your tiffin yesterday,”  
she says. “Can I join in?”

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

“Yes, yes, Sachi!” says Meenu.  
She stands up and bangs her  
tiffin box lid on the desk.  
“Attention, everyone! Kamlesh, Sachi  
and I are having a tiffin party.  
Today we will eat idlis, mutke and  
pakhala bhata. Who wants to join us?”

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

There is a scramble.  
The whole class crowds  
around Kamlesh’s desk  
for the tiffin party.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

Some tiffin boxes have a little food in them, some have lots.  
Some have spicy food, some sweet.  
There is tangy pickle and some bitter karela.  
But there is enough to fill all the hungry tummies in the room.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

Meenu loves tiffin time now.  
Why wouldn’t she when every day is a party?

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

Santula, tarwat bhaji,  
aloo poha, parantha and achaar…  
Meenu has found her tiffin box gang.

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

Idli: This is a savoury  
steamed rice cake.  
Podi: This is made from ground spices.  
Mixed with a little coconut or sesame oil,  
it is eaten with idlis.  
Jowar bhakri: This is a roti made of jowar or sorghum flour and water.

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

Mutke: This is made with  
coriander leaves and  
jowar flour.  
Pakhala bhata: This is slightly  
watery rice that can be eaten  
with vegetables.  
Bhaji: This is made from tarwat, a leafy wild vegetable.
