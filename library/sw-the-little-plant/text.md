﻿ Parts of a plant  
​Look at a plant closely. Its roots are below the soil. Its leaves, flowers and fruits grow on the stem, above the soil. Here are the main parts of a plant —  
Seeds  
Flower  
Fruit  
Leaf  
Stem  
Roots
