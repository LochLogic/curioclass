![Picture for page 1](art/01.jpg)

Pongal the pig loves sleeping. Sometimes  
she sleeps all day!  
No wonder her tail gets bored easily.  
One day, when Pongal wakes up from her  
afternoon nap, her tail is nowhere to be found.  
It has gone on an adventure without her!

---

![Picture for page 2](art/02.jpg)

The pig feels silly without a tail. So, she decides to go look for it.

---

![Picture for page 3](art/03.jpg)

SCRRRUNCH! “This forest  
is much too untidy!”  
Pongal says.  
EEEK! She trips over a log.  
DHADAAAM!  
She falls into  
a bush.

---

![Picture for page 4](art/04.jpg)

“Has anybody seen my tail?” Pongal asks.  
“Why don’t you try a mushroom tail instead?” Langur replies.  
“HUMPH!” Pongal says. “But I want my own tail back!”  
She goes to look for it somewhere else.

---

![Picture for page 5](art/05.jpg)

OOOOF! “This city is much too noisy!” Pongal says.  
BEEP-BEEP! She dodges a zigzagging auto ricksha.  
DRRING-DRRING! She tries to crawl under a bicycle.

---

![Picture for page 6](art/06.jpg)

“Has anybody seen my tail?” Pongal asks.  
“Why don’t you try a balloon tail instead?” Crow replies.  
“GAAAH!” Pongal says. “But I want my own tail back!” She goes to look for it somewhere else.

---

![Picture for page 7](art/07.jpg)

GRRRUMPH! “This cave  
is much too dark!” Pongal says.  
CLICK-CLACK! She carefully  
steps forward on the stone floor.  
KHADAAAK! She tumbles into  
a pile of rocks .  
“Has anybody seen my tail?” Pongal asks.

---

![Picture for page 8](art/08.jpg)

“Why don’t you try a stalactite tail instead?” Bat replies.  
“UFFFF!” Pongal says. “But I want my own tail back!” She goes to look for it somewhere else.

---

![Picture for page 9](art/09.jpg)

SPLISH-SPLOSH! “It’s much too wet under  
the ocean!” Pongal says.  
DHUM-DHUM! Some colourful fish want to dance with Pongal.

---

![Picture for page 10](art/10.jpg)

WHOOSH! She hides behind some coral.  
“Has anybody seen my tail?” Pongal asks.  
“Why don’t you try a seaweed tail instead?” Turtle replies.  
“UGGGGH!” Pongal says.  
“But I want my own tail  
back!” She goes to look  
for it somewhere else.

---

![Picture for page 11](art/11.jpg)

PLONK! “This desert is much too hot!” Pongal says.  
FOOOSSH! She doesn’t realise she is  
sitting at the edge of a dune.  
WHUMP! She rolls all the way down  
to the bottom.  
“Has anybody  
seen my tail?”  
Pongal asks.

---

![Picture for page 12](art/12.jpg)

“Why don’t you try a cactus tail instead?” Camel replies.  
“AAAOOON!” Pongal squeals.  
“But I want my own tail back!” She goes to look for it somewhere else.

---

![Picture for page 13](art/13.jpg)

BRRRRRR! “The top of a mountain is much too cold!” Pongal says.  
AYIEEEE! The rest of the world is  
a long way below.  
THUMMMP-THUMMMP! She  
steps back before she feels  
too dizzy.

---

![Picture for page 14](art/14.jpg)

“Has anybody seen my tail?” Pongal asks.  
“Why don’t you try a sock tail instead?” Yak replies.  
Pongal stares at her newest tail.

---

![Picture for page 15](art/15.jpg)

“This is very pretty,” Pongal says. She remembers all the other tails she tried on.  
HARRRUMPH! “I’m certainly not going to  
wear a cactus ever again!”  
But having so many different tails is actually quite fun. Everybody else has the same one forever, she realises.  
Pongal comes to a decision. The tail can travel the world and have its adventures. The pig will stay back home and have her own.
