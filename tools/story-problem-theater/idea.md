# Story Problem Theater (idea card)

- Module: math-1-addition-and-subtraction-stories, grade 1. Codes: 1.OA.A
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good, with the branch made meaningful: the number sentence the child picks is what the actors perform next, so a wrong pick plays out visibly wrong.
- One idea: Short puppet-theater stories stop at the question; you act the story out with counters, pick a number sentence, and the puppets replay the story exactly as your sentence says, so you can see whether it fits.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a small stage with a red curtain and a counter tray. "The show stops at the question. Tap the curtain."
  - Core loop: a 20-second scene plays (Ana has 8 shells, finds some more, now has 13). The curtain freezes on the question. The child drags counters to act it out, then picks a sentence (8 + ? = 13, or 13 + 8 = ?). The puppets replay the scene following the chosen sentence; if it is wrong, Ana ends up with 21 shells and the audience giggles.
  - Story types rotate: add to, take from, put together, take apart and compare ("how many more?"), with the unknown at the end, the change or the start. Some shows have three groups (3 + 5 + 7 shells).
  - The aha or the end state: "How many more?" stories can be solved by adding or by subtracting, and the replay proves both sentences fit.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the class acts the scene out with real objects before the replay.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 1.OA.A: solving add-to, take-from, put-together, take-apart and compare stories within 20 with the unknown in every position, using counters and equations with a symbol for the unknown (1.OA.1), and three-addend shows with sums to 20 (1.OA.2).
- Not like: bus-stop (kindergarten, within 10, only on and off), lost-and-found (grade 2, two steps within 100), make-ten-machine (fact strategies, no stories).
- Art: people (illustrated): a small cast of puppet characters from different families, because the stories are about people.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Puppet stories stop at the question; act them out with counters, pick a number sentence, and the puppets replay the story the way your sentence says. All story types within 20, unknowns anywhere.
- Flags: none
