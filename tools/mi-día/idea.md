# Mi Día (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-k-12-school-and-daily-routine, grade k-12. Codes: ACTFL.Novice Mid
- Format / family: timeline / spanish. Fit: good: a day is a timeline, and clock times pin each event.
- One idea: Build your school day in Spanish on a clock-face timeline (me levanto a las siete, tengo matemáticas a las nueve), then lay it beside the day of a pen pal at a school in another Spanish-speaking country and talk about what is the same and what is different.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a long day strip from morning to night with a clock on top. A pen pal's message: "¿Cómo es tu día?"
  - Core loop: drag routine cards (me despierto, me cepillo los dientes, desayuno, voy a la escuela) and class cards (ciencias, arte, educación física) onto the strip; each card asks for its time, set on the clock (Es la una. Son las dos y media.). Every card is spoken. Reflexive verbs show the little "me" lighting up, with a "who does it to whom" picture.
  - The aha or the end state: the pen pal's day appears under the student's, and the student fills a short comparison with frames ("Yo almuerzo a las doce. Ella almuerza a las ___."). The finished day reads back as a short spoken paragraph.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a made-up character's day by default; "my real day" option kept on this device only (the screen says so).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Novice Mid: lists and memorized phrases for classes, telling time and daily activities, including common reflexive routine verbs.
- Not like: calendario-vivo (days and dates, not clock time); mi-casa (places and people); time-machine-talk (English past tense).
- Art: illustrated day cards and pen pal (people, illustrated).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Build your school day in Spanish on a clock timeline, routine verbs and classes each set to a time, then compare it with a pen pal's day at a school in another Spanish-speaking country.
- Flags: needs a data source (recorded Spanish voices; the pen pal's school schedule checked against a real country's norms at spec); K-12 span check (gf-04).
