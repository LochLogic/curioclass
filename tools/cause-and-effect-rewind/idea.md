# Cause and Effect Rewind (idea card)

- Module: eld-3-5-cause-and-effect-language, grade 3-5. Codes: WIDA.ELD-4, WIDA.Explain
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good: each rewind is a small logic puzzle about which step caused which.
- One idea: A science scene plays out (an ice pop melts in the sun, a puddle disappears, a hillside washes away in a storm, a bulb lights when the circuit closes), then rewinds one step at a time, and you link each pair of steps with because, so or as a result, finding that "so" and "because" point in opposite directions.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a short animated scene with a "Rewind" lever. "Rewind it one step. Tell me why each step happened."
  - Core loop: each pull of the lever steps back one link. The student joins the two steps with a connector and builds the sentence: "The puddle got smaller because the sun heated the water." or "The sun heated the water, so the puddle got smaller." Putting "so" where "because" belongs makes the scene play the wrong way (the puddle grows back), and the student sees why. Later puzzles shuffle the steps and include a step that happened but did not cause anything.
  - The aha or the end state: the full chain from first cause to final effect, written as a short paragraph with "as a result" and "this caused", and read back aloud.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): Help Lots / Some / Little; the class predicts the next rewind step.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - WIDA.ELD-4: the language of science: heating, melting, evaporating, erosion and circuits in grade 3-5 contexts.
  - WIDA.Explain: explaining how and why things happen with because, so, as a result and this caused, in both orders.
- Not like: math-explainer (sequence of math steps); time-machine-talk (past tense in history); opinion-builder (reasons for an opinion).
- Art: generated scenes for each science chain, so the physical change is easy to see.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: A science scene (a melting ice pop, a shrinking puddle, a washed-out hillside) rewinds one step at a time; link each step with because, so or as a result, and see the scene run backward if the connector points the wrong way.
- Flags: science facts in each chain checked at spec; home-language glossary may matter (open question, not decided).
