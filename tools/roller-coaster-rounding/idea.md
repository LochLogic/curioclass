# Roller Coaster Rounding (idea card)

- Module: math-3-rounding-and-multi-digit-operations, grade 3. Codes: 3.NBT.A
- Format / family: simulator / number-line. Fit: good, if the park runs three jobs on one ride, not three separate games.
- One idea: A coaster car stops on a number-line track and rolls down to the nearest ten or hundred; then you run the ride, estimating riders by rounding, adding exactly, and filling trains of cars by multiples of ten.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a coaster track drawn as a number line, a station in a valley at every ten and a hilltop at every 5. "Drop the car at 47. Which way will it roll?"
  - Core loop, three jobs:
    - Rolls: drop the car anywhere; it rolls down into the nearest station (a ten or a hundred), which sits in a valley. At exactly 45 the car teeters on the peak and the park rule rolls it up; the rule is stated plainly.
    - Line counts: queue sizes (278, 346) are rounded for a fast estimate, then added or subtracted exactly with a place-value strategy; the two are compared.
    - Trains: 6 cars of 40 seats is 6 x 4 tens = 24 tens = 240 seats.
  - The aha or the end state: the estimate tells you whether your exact answer is sensible; the ride opens when the numbers check.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): set your own track to round to hundreds.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 3.NBT.A: rounding to the nearest 10 or 100 (3.NBT.1), adding and subtracting within 1000 with place-value strategies (3.NBT.2), and multiplying one-digit numbers by multiples of 10 (3.NBT.3).
- Not like: jump-line (grade 2 open number line), stadium-count (grade 4 rounding to any place), rollercoaster-polynomials (grade 11; also near in name).
- Art: generated scenes (an amusement park); the track is drawn in code as an exact number line.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: A coaster car rolls down to the nearest ten or hundred station (see why 45 rolls up); then run the ride: estimate riders by rounding, add exactly within 1000, and fill trains of 40-seat cars.
- Flags: overlap risk (title near rollercoaster-polynomials; different grades, fine to keep)
