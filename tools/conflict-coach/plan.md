arc: explore-first
minutes: 45
focus: CASEL.Relationship Skills
goal: I can say a hard thing with an I-statement, say back the other person's side, and suggest a plan that works for both of us.
materials: The tool on a projector. Optional: one laptop per pair. Printed Partner Cards for The Markers, one set per pair. Scrap paper for the exit check.
grouping: Whole class for the first scene, then partners.
---
## Before class

- Open **Scenes** on the scene picker. Sound is on. The move cards show letters A to D for class votes.
- Switch to **Partner Cards**, pick **The Markers** and press **Print cards**, one copy per pair.
- In the teacher notes, read Good to know: if a scene stirs something up for a student, the school counselor is the next step. Every scene is made up; students never share their own conflicts.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "Two people want to say the same thing: 'my stuff got wrecked.' One gets an apology. One starts a fight. How can the same message go two ways?" (Tone. Blaming. The words they picked.)
2. "Today we'll say a line, watch the other kid react, then rewind and say it another way."

## Explore (18 min)

**The Slides**, teacher drives, class votes (11 min):

1. Pick **The Slides** and read Jordan's setup aloud. Point at the meter: it starts at **Tense**.
2. Turn 1, **Say it**. For this first round, pick the blame card on purpose: "You never do your part. You're so lazy." "What happened to the meter? To Jordan's arms?" (It dropped to Heated. Jordan crossed their arms and leaned away.)
3. Read Jordan's reply. Stop here: "What did Jordan know that we didn't?" (Their grandma is staying with them and they have no quiet place to work.) This is the surprise.
4. Press **Rewind**. Partners whisper, then vote A to D. Pick the I-statement. "Look at the faded **Last time** bubble beside the new line. Same problem. What changed?" (No blame: a feeling, what happened, what we need. The meter went to Listening.)
5. Press **Next** to **Hear them**. If the class picks "Just do them at lunch then," ask: "That sounds helpful. Why didn't the meter move?" (We fixed it before showing we heard them.) Rewind and try the reflective listen.
6. Press **Next** to **Fix it**. Try "Never mind, I'll do all of it" first. "The argument ended. Why is the meter stuck?" (Our need disappeared.) Rewind and pick a plan, then read the ending card.

**Partners** (7 min):

7. Pairs on laptops pick **The Lab Controls** or **The Weekend Plan**. "Try one weak line each turn, rewind, then go for a **Clean run**." Rewinds don't break it.

## Make sense (7 min)

1. On the board: Say it = I-statement, Hear them = reflective listen, Fix it = both-sides plan or ask what works.
2. Open **Line Builder** with The Slides. Use the feeling tile "like you don't care" and three good tiles, then **Check my line**. "Why isn't that a feeling?" (It's a thought about them. A feeling is one word, like stressed or worried.)
3. Swap in "stressed" and press **Say it to them**. "What changed in Jordan's reply?" (It went from guarded to open.)
4. "In every scene, the other kid had a reason we didn't know. When did we find it out?" (Only after we stopped blaming and listened.)

## Apply (10 min)

1. Hand out the printed **The Markers** cards. Partner 1 takes the Speaker card, Partner 2 the Listener card.
2. Speaker reads the I-statement frame aloud and fills it in. Listener says back "So you feel ___ because ___. Did I get that right?", then "From my side, ___." Agree on one plan.
3. **Swap roles** with the same cards and do it again.
4. Project **Show both sides** for The Markers. "What did each of you not know at the start?" Call on two pairs.

## Exit check (5 min)

Read a new made-up moment: "Your locker partner keeps piling wet gym clothes on top of your books." On scrap paper, students write one I-statement with all four parts: I feel ___ when ___ because ___. Could we ___? (I feel annoyed when my books get wet, because I need them for class. Could we each use one shelf?) Students who write "I feel like you..." or skip the ask need another round in **Line Builder**.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** keep the frame "I feel ___ when ___ because ___. Could we ___?" on the board all period.
- **Stretch:** "In **Line Builder**, use all four trap tiles, check the line, then fix one trap at a time. Which fix changed the reply most?"
- **Stretch:** "Write a blame line for the exit check moment, then rewrite it as a reflective listen from the other side."

## Short on time

- Hook (3 min), then **The Slides** only: blame card, **Rewind**, I-statement, and the Jordan surprise (10 min).
- End with the exit check I-statement (4 min).
