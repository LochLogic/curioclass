## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Scenes** and pick **The Slides** (Jordan). Read the setup card out loud.
2. Turn 1, Say it: pick **A, B, C or D** (or press 1 to 4). Watch the meter at the top, Jordan's face and posture, and the reply. The coach line in the panel names the move.
3. Press **Rewind** (or R) and pick a different card for the same turn. The faded "Last time" bubble and the dashed marker on the meter show what happened before, so the class can compare the two.
4. Press **Next** (or N) through Hear them and Fix it to reach the ending card: Still stuck, Okay for now or Worked it out. On a projector, let the class vote on each card before you pick.

## The three modes

- **Scenes:** five ordinary, fictional school moments: The Slides (Jordan), The Markers (Priya), The Tryout (Mateo), The Lab Controls (Aiyana), The Weekend Plan (Sam). Each has three turns (Say it, Hear them, Fix it) with four full-sentence choices. The meter has five stops (Heated, Tense, Calm, Listening, Working it out), starts at Tense and moves by the move's amount: I-statement +2, half I-statement +1, brush-off 0, blame minus 1; reflective listen +2, fix it too fast 0, yeah-but minus 1, dismiss minus 1; both-sides plan +2, ask what works +2, give in 0, demand minus 1. It stops at both ends. Ending: Heated or Tense = Still stuck, Calm = Okay for now, Listening or Working it out = Worked it out. Rewind replays the current turn as many times as you like. A **Clean run** (the strong move kept on every turn, rewinds allowed) puts a small check on that scene's card; **Clear checks** removes them.
- **Line Builder:** build one I-statement from tiles: "I feel [1] when [2] because [3]. Could we [4]?" Each row has three good tiles and one trap (a thought instead of a feeling, a judgment instead of the facts, a reason about them instead of you, a demand instead of an ask). **Check** marks each part and explains any trap. **Say it to them** plays the other kid's reaction: no traps, one trap, or two or more.
- **Partner Cards:** for two students reading aloud. Partner 1 gets the Speaker card (their side, the sentence frame, two feeling words), Partner 2 the Listener card (the other kid's reason that only they know, and the say-back frame). **Swap roles**, then **Show both sides** for the after-talk. **Print cards** prints both cards for the scene, black on white.

## Talk about it

- Pick the blame card, then rewind and pick the I-statement. What changed in the reply? Why do people defend themselves when a line starts with what they did wrong?
- In Hear them, why does the reflective listen move the meter more than fixing it fast, when fixing it sounds helpful?
- In every scene the other kid had a reason you didn't know at the start. What was it, and when did you find out?
- Give in ends the argument. Why does the meter still not move?
- In Line Builder, what makes "like you don't care" a thought and not a feeling?

## Good to know

- Keys: 1 to 4 or A to D pick a card, R rewinds after the reply, N goes on. Every control also works with Tab and Enter.
- There is no text box anywhere. Nothing asks about a student's own life, family or classmates, and no feelings are scored. The only thing saved is which scenes had a Clean run, on this device only (plus the Motion setting shared by Curio Class tools). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- The meter measures how the talk is going in the story, not the student.
- If a scene stirs something up for a student, or a student is upset, the teacher's school counselor is the right next step.
- Motion off makes the meter, the faces and the card flip change instantly. Read speaks the current hint and the last coach line; Sound off keeps the tool silent.
