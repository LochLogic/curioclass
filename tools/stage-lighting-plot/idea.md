# Stage Lighting Plot (idea card)

- Module: arts-9-12-lighting-and-technical-design, grade 9-12. Codes: TH:Cr1, TH:Cr3, TH:Pr5
- Format / family: sandbox / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Light the last scene of a public-domain play (A Doll's House, 1879: a lamp-lit room at night and a door that closes) on a stage where light behaves like light: research what a Norwegian home then was lit by, hang and aim instruments, choose color and intensity, write the cues, and revise until the light turns when the story turns.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the empty stage in work light, the scene's text with its emotional turns marked, and a research board (lamps, candles and window light of the period, public domain paintings of interiors from the time).
  - Core loop: hang instruments on the plot (front, side, back, top, a practical lamp), aim and focus them, pick gels and levels. The stage renders angle, color and falloff for real: low front light flattens faces, high side light sculpts them. Write cues with fade times on the script.
  - The aha or the end state: run the scene with actors and cues. The director's notes ask for the moment the room stops feeling safe; the student revises until the light supports it, and the before and after runs play back to back.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a second scene from another public-domain play in a different period.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - TH:Cr1: researching the period and place to build the visual idea of the scene.
  - TH:Cr3: refining the design and cues until they support the story and its turns.
  - TH:Pr5: using researched technical elements (motivated light, angle, color) to raise the scene's impact.
- Not like: stage-blocker (grade 6-8 actors and sightlines); director-s-chair (grade 9-12 directing across traditions); frame-it (photo lighting).
- Art: drawn in code for the lit stage (the light itself is the lesson and must be computed); people (illustrated) for actors; public domain period paintings (Met or AIC ids to check).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Light the last scene of A Doll's House: research the period's lamps and windows, hang and aim instruments, pick color, level and cues, then run the scene and revise until the light turns when the story turns.
- Flags: needs a data source (period lighting facts; a public domain translation of the play)
