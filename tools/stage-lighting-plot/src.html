How the light works

A simple model, not a photometric prediction. Throw is the distance to the focus ring, 1.6 m above the floor. Pool radius is throw × tan(half the beam angle). Oblique pools stretch into ellipses.

Center energy = level × output ÷ pool area × gel transmission. Level runs from 0 to 1. Outputs are 10 model units, or 14 for PAR. Edges soften by type. Shown brightness is 1 − exp(−energy), with ambient 0.03.

Gels, transmissions and warmth scores are approximations. Brightness is a floor average; warmth is energy weighted. The face line compares head-point brightness. Graphs show each beat's completed look, rather than live fade samples.

The floor and back wall are mapped to the painting. Furniture uses that surface light, without cast shadows. Gobos are modeled as a pattern inside a beam. The focus ring projects onto the floor in this simplified view.
