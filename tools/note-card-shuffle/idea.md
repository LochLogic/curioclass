# Note Card Shuffle (idea card)

- Module: ela-3-notes-and-categories, grade 3. Codes: W.3.7, W.3.8
- Format / family: sort / research. Fit: good.
- One idea: The student picks a question about an animal or an invention, reads three short kid pages, writes facts on note cards in their own words and sorts them into the project's categories, and an empty or crowded category shows what to look up next, so they learn that research is a loop of reading, noting and sorting.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a desk with a blank card box and four question cards: "How do octopuses hide?", "How does a bicycle work?", "What do bats eat?", "Who invented the ice cream cone?"
  - Core loop: read a kid page, highlight a fact, then write it on a card in a few words; if the card copies five or more words in a row, the card wrinkles and asks for the student's own words. A "something I know already" card lets the student add what they have seen themselves. Then sort the cards into category pockets (body, home, food, enemies); each card shows the page it came from.
  - The aha or the end state: the category bar shows one pocket empty and one stuffed, and a "look it up" button sends the student back to the pages for just what is missing; the finished box turns into a short report outline.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print the cards for a real poster project.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - W.3.7: a short research project on one question, from three sources to an outline.
  - W.3.8: recalling what they know, gathering facts from print and digital pages, brief notes in their own words, sorted into categories.
- Not like: research-rover (grade 4, sources list and two-text combining); big-question-chart (k-2 class chart); fact-fusion (grade 5 report from three sources).
- Art: generated scenes for the kid pages (photo-like animals and machines), drawn in code for the cards and pockets.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (with research-rover; the research family should keep grade 3 to notes and categories and grade 4 to sources and outlines)
