# Who's My Baby? (idea card)

- Module: science-1-parents-and-offspring, grade 1. Codes: 1-LS1-2, 1-LS3-1
- Format / family: cards / none. Fit: good
- One idea: A matching card game where each baby plant or animal goes to its parent by spotting what is alike and what is not exactly alike (a spotted kitten among three cats, a seedling among three trees), and parent cards show how parents keep their young safe.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a table of face-down cards and a friendly guide. "Find each baby's parent. Look closely."
  - Core loop: flip a baby card; three possible parents show; pick one and say which clue matched (stripes, ear shape, leaf shape). Tricky rounds have parents of the same kind (three dogs, three sunflowers), so the student must look at details; the game points out what is like the parent and what is a little different.
  - Care cards: when a pair is matched, a short read-aloud card and a short clip show what that parent does (a penguin keeps the egg on its feet, a kangaroo carries its joey, a crocodile carries hatchlings in her mouth, a deer hides her fawn). The student sorts care into feeding, keeping warm, protecting and teaching.
  - The aha or the end state: a family album where babies sit next to parents with "same" and "different" labels, and a care chart that shows the patterns.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): two players take turns against each other.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 1-LS1-2: care cards and clips are the texts and media; sorting them shows patterns in how parents help offspring survive.
  - 1-LS3-1: the tricky rounds ask for evidence that young plants and animals are like, but not exactly like, their parents.
- Not like: puppy-traits (grade 3 inheritance and environment); life-cycle-wheel (stages over a life).
- Art: generated scenes (real-looking animals and plants, true to each species), because the clues are in the details.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Match each baby plant or animal to its parent by spotting what is alike and what is not exactly alike; care cards show how parents feed, warm, protect and teach their young.
- Flags: none
