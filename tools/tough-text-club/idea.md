# Tough Text Club (idea card)

- Module: ela-9-12-reading-range-and-text-complexity, grade 9-12. Codes: RL.9-10.10, RL.11-12.10, RI.9-10.10, RI.11-12.10
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Take on a ladder of complex texts (a founding document, a scientific article, a nineteenth-century novel chapter, a poem), plan how to read each, drop the supports one at a time, and track the range you have read.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a ladder of four complex texts. "Plan how you will read the first one."
  - Core loop: take on a founding document, a scientific article, a nineteenth-century novel chapter and a poem; plan how to read each, drop the supports one at a time, and track the range read.
  - The aha or the end state: the student reads complex texts independently and proficiently.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RL.9-10.10, RL.11-12.10, RI.9-10.10, RI.11-12.10: reading complex literature and informational texts independently.
