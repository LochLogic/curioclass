# Echo Reader (idea card)

- Module: ela-1-reading-fluency, grade 1. Codes: RF.1.4
- Format / family: lab / read-aloud. Fit: good: the child runs small reading experiments (fast, slow, flat, with feeling) and compares.
- One idea: The child hears a model reading of a short passage, echoes it into the microphone, and plays both side by side as two waveforms, so they can see and hear pauses at periods, a voice that rises at a question mark, and a smoother second try.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a short passage about a lost mitten and a big Listen button. "Listen first. Then you read it back like an echo."
  - Core loop: model reads one sentence with a highlight; the child echoes it; the two waveforms line up, showing pauses and pace. Experiments: read it like a robot, then like the character; read too fast, then just right. A "Does that make sense?" check: one line has a word that was easy to misread (house for horse), and the picture shows the funny result until the child rereads and fixes it.
  - The aha or the end state: the child reads the whole passage alone, then plays the first try and the last try back to back. No scores, just the two recordings.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher passage sets. On screen: recordings never leave this device; no speech recognition.
- Standards, one line per code: RF.1.4: reading with purpose and understanding (a picture question after each passage), oral reading with accuracy, rate and expression on successive readings (echo and rereads), using context to self-correct (the house or horse check).
- Not like: Poem Drum (poems with a beat). Script Read-Through (grades 3-5 sibling, group parts). Blend Bakery (decoding single words).
- Art: generated scenes (one illustration per passage, plus the funny misread pictures), because the misread picture is what drives self-correction.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Hear a model read a short passage, echo it into the microphone, and see both readings as waveforms side by side; reread to smooth it out and fix any line that does not make sense. No scores.
- Flags: none (microphone: privacy line required)
