## A 5-minute start

1. Clear a space. Everyone finds their own **bubble space**: arms out, turn around, touch nobody.
2. Open **Levels**, keep **Middle** on and tap **Stretch**. The dancer counts in (4 clicks), then stretches for 8 counts and freezes. The class copies.
3. Tap **Low**, then **Curl**. Then tap **High** in the middle of a move: the dancer changes level on the next count, and so does the class.
4. Switch to **Pathways**, pick **Zigzag**, **Walk** and **High**, then tap **Go**. Follow the dancer across the room (or march the shape in place). Look at the ribbon it left on the floor.

## The four modes

- **Levels:** six moves that stay in one place (stretch, twist, bend, swing, shake, curl) at high, middle and low. Each move is a count-in and 8 counts that end in a freeze. **Up and down** runs one move for 40 counts through high, middle, low, middle, high. **Surprise** picks a move and a level.
- **Pathways:** the dancer travels a straight, curved or zigzag pathway by walking, skipping, galloping or sliding, at a level you choose. The ribbon on the floor keeps the pathway: its shape is the pathway, and its color and pattern are the level (blue tiptoe prints for high, a solid green band for middle, a wide striped amber band for low). The icon at the end of each ribbon says the same thing. Up to three ribbons stay; **Clean floor** wipes them.
- **Mystery path:** the floor hides the ribbon. Watch the trip, draw the pathway in the air with one finger, then tap the answer. A right pathway paints the ribbon in; then name the level. A wrong answer plays the trip again. Eight rounds end in a gallery of all eight ribbons and a bow.
- **Draw a path:** draw a pathway on the floor, starting at the star. The tool splits it into straight, curved and zigzag parts, numbers them and says them in order. Tap a part's number badge to give that part its own level (high, middle, low, then round again), or tap a Level button to set every part at once. Pick a way to move, then tap **Dance it**: the dancer changes level part by part, and so does the class. **Surprise path** draws one for you, with mixed levels.

## Talk about it

- Was the dancer high, middle or low? How could you tell?
- Which pathway did the dancer make? Show me with your finger in the air.
- What else moves in a zigzag pathway? A curved one?
- Can you make a curved pathway at a low level? A straight one at a high level?
- Which moves stayed in your bubble space? Which ones traveled?
- In your drawn dance, where did the level change? Can you plan one that goes high, then low, then middle?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 4 switch modes. Every button has a picture and every line is spoken, so non-readers can follow along.
- In a small room, travel the pathway in place: march the shape on the spot or trace it with a hand.
- Low level is on the floor (sitting, kneeling or lying). A child who can't get down low, kneel or go on tiptoe shows the level with their arms or upper body instead; any way of getting closer to the floor or to the sky counts.
- **Motion** off keeps the same counts and music; the dancer just steps from count to count without the in-between.
- Standards (NCAS dance): Cr1.1.Ka and Kb, Cr1.1.2b (Draw a path: one dance that changes level part by part), Pr4.1.Ka, 1a and 2a (levels; straight, curved and zigzag pathways), Re7.1.Kb (Mystery path), Cr3.1.Kb and 2b (the ribbon map as symbols for pathway and level), Pr5.1.Kb; SHAPE America S2.
- The studio, the dancer and the music are made in the browser. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere.
