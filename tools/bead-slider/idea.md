# Bead Slider (idea card)

- Module: math-1-adding-tens-and-ones, grade 1. Codes: 1.NBT.C
- Format / family: simulator / number-path. Fit: good.
- One idea: In a hundred-chart apartment building every floor holds ten apartments, so the elevator adds or takes away tens and the hallway adds ones; delivering packages turns two-digit sums into rides.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a cutaway building, ten doors per floor numbered like a hundred chart, a delivery cat with a parcel. "Deliver this from 34 to 34 + 25."
  - Core loop: the child presses Up 10 (twice) and walks 5 doors; the route writes itself: 34 + 10 + 10 + 5 = 59. Ones that pass the end of a hall wrap to the next floor (37 + 5 reaches 42), and the tool shows those ten ones making one more ten.
  - Quick rides: "10 more than 63?" The child answers before riding; the elevator checks. "70 - 30": three floors down, no hallway.
  - The aha or the end state: adding tens never changes the door you face, only the floor. The child sees why 47 + 30 ends in 7.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): hide door numbers so the child must think in tens and ones.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 1.NBT.C: adding a two-digit number and a one-digit number or a multiple of ten with the building as the model, including making a new ten in the hallway (1.NBT.4), ten more and ten less in your head (1.NBT.5), and subtracting multiples of 10 from 10 to 90 (1.NBT.6).
- Not like: number-path-rescue (reading and writing numerals to 120), crayon-boxes (tens and ones as bundles, comparison), jump-line (grade 2 open number line to 1000).
- Art: generated scenes (a lively cutaway building with neighbors in windows), because the residents make the deliveries feel real.
- Proposed title: Bead Slider (applied in the 2026-10-27 audit)
- Proposed concept line (applied in the 2026-10-27 audit, docs/agents/audit/clashes-k8.md K04; it replaces the One idea above where they differ): Slide beads on a 100-bead string by tens and ones to solve 34 + 20 or 47 + 6, make a new ten when the ones run over, slide back for 10 less or 60 - 30, and write each slide as an equation.
- Flags: none
