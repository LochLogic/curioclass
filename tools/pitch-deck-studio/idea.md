# Pitch Deck Studio (idea card)

- Module: ela-8-presentations-and-media, grade 8. Codes: SL.8.2, SL.8.4, SL.8.5
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good: build a short slide pitch, review media you plan to use, and rehearse it.
- One idea: The student builds a three-minute slide pitch to the student council (bring back the school garden, start a repair cafe), and before any outside media goes on a slide, a media-motive lens makes them ask who made it and why (a seed company ad, a city report, a news story), then they rehearse and record, so they learn to present a claim with sound evidence and media that strengthen it, and to read the motives behind the media they borrow.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a blank five-slide deck, a pitch card ("Council has three minutes and $500"), and a media library of charts, photos, ads and video stills.
  - Core loop: the student writes the claim slide, then adds reasons and evidence. Every library item they drag in opens the lens: who made it, what they want (sell, inform, persuade a vote), and what that means for using it. An ad's "grow your own food, save $600 a year" claim gets flagged as commercial; the student either drops it or uses it while saying where it came from. A checklist flags slides with no evidence, a chart that doesn't match its claim, and slides with more words than the audience can read. Rehearse: record each slide's talk, with a per-slide timer and volume meter (no speech recognition), and the on-screen line "Your recordings stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded."
  - The aha or the end state: a council member card reacts to the deck: the evidence slide wins a vote, the ad slide loses one if its motive wasn't named. The student does a final run-through and marks their own delivery for eye contact, volume and pronunciation.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): live pitches with a class council vote.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - SL.8.2: the media-motive lens on each item, naming commercial, social or political motives.
  - SL.8.4: presenting the claim with focused points, relevant evidence and sound reasoning, and rehearsing eye contact, volume and pronunciation.
  - SL.8.5: choosing and integrating charts, images and stills that clarify the claim and strengthen evidence.
- Not like: docent-tour-talk (6-8, an informative tour adapted to audiences); ad-agency (grade 12, running and auditing ad campaigns); explainer-studio (grade 7, a written magazine page).
- Art: generated scenes and drawn-in-code charts for the media library; people (illustrated) for the council.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Build a three-minute slide pitch for the student council; every chart, ad or photo you borrow goes through a media-motive lens first, then rehearse and record each slide while a council member reacts.
- Flags: records audio (on-screen line that nothing leaves the device; no speech recognition)
