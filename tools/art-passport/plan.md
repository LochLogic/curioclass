arc: explore-first
minutes: 35
focus: VA:Re7; VA:Cn11
goal: I can say what I see and feel in a work of art, and tell where in the world it was made and what it was for.
materials: The tool on the big screen. Paper and crayons for the Apply drawing.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet.
---
## Before class

- Open **Travel**. Fourteen works of art are pinned on the world map where they were made.
- If a new class is using the tool, press **Start a new passport** in **Passport**.
- Read the cultural notes for the amo (house post) and the ci wara headdress. They are respected objects; their movement prompts are about the shape and the dance.

## Hook (4 min)

1. "Today we're going on a trip around the world without leaving the carpet. We need a passport!"
2. Show the empty **Passport**. "Every place we visit, we get a stamp."

## Explore (13 min)

1. On **Travel**, tap the giant wave in Japan. The visit stamps the passport.
2. Ask the **See, Think, Wonder** questions, tapping each to move on. Then press **Find 1** and let a child point to the detail.
3. "How does it make you feel?" Tap the faces to tally the class. Finish with the **Try it** movement.
4. Press **Surprise trip** and visit one or two more works the same way. A good pair: the blue hippo from Egypt and the Diné (Navajo) wearing blanket.

## Make sense (6 min)

1. Open **Compare** with two of the works you visited. "What is the same? What is different?" Press **A clue** if the class gets stuck.
2. "Some of these were made to be used: worn, danced with, or to hold food. Which ones? What were they for?"
3. "People in these places still make art today."

## Apply (8 min)

Children choose a favorite work from today and draw it, adding one thing they noticed up close. Under it, they "write" (or tell you) where it was made.

## Exit check (4 min)

Show one work from today. Each child tells a partner one thing they see and one feeling it gives them, using "I see... It makes me feel..." Listen in for children who name a detail.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** ask only the "See" question at first, and accept pointing.
- **Support:** visit fewer places and spend more time on each one.
- **Stretch:** "Why do you think the artist made it this way?"
- **Stretch:** find the place on the map and say if it is near or far from us.

## Short on time

- Visit one work in **Travel** with **See, Think, Wonder** and **Find 1** (6 min).
- Open **Compare** with a second work (4 min).
