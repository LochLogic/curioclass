# Scarf Juggling Ladder (idea card)

- Module: health-pe-3-5-juggling-and-practice-habits, grade 3-5. Codes: SHAPE.S1, SHAPE.S4
- Format / family: arcade / none. Fit: change to lab, because the real activity is practicing with scarves, logging tries and drawing a conclusion from your own practice data, not fast on-screen rounds.
- One idea: Climb a ladder of scarf-juggling rungs (one scarf toss and catch, one scarf hand to hand, two scarves, three-scarf cascade), check each rung against slowed-down cue animations, log your catches each practice, and watch your own chart show that steady practice beats quitting after a drop.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a ladder with six rungs and a "get your scarves" card (three light scarves, or squares of thin fabric or tissue paper).
  - Core loop: each rung shows a slow animation with three cues (toss across your body, catch with a claw from above, "toss, toss, catch" rhythm); the student practices for one minute, then taps how many catches in a row they got.
  - Self-check: three cue checkboxes per rung ("Did the scarf go above your head?"); the student marks which cues they hit and sees a tip for the one they missed.
  - Practice log: a chart of best streaks by day. A line on screen says the log stays on this device and never leaves it.
  - The aha or the end state: the chart dips on hard days and rises over the week; drops are part of learning, and the next rung opens when a streak target is met.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - SHAPE.S1: learn the toss and catch pattern of scarf juggling in steps, from one scarf to three.
  - SHAPE.S4: self-check against cues, log practice and keep going after drops.
- Not like: target-toss (K-2 throwing experiment); partner-skill-coach (grade 6-12 peer feedback); animal-moves (K-2 movement copying).
- Art: people (illustrated) juggler shown from the front in slow motion, with color-coded scarves.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs equipment (three light scarves or fabric squares); stores a practice log on the device (rule 7 line)
