## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Learn**. Tap **May I go to the bathroom?** Each word lights up as it's read.
2. On the 3-2-1 count, everyone says it together while the words light up again.
3. Try two or three more, then play **What do I say?** with just the pictures.

## The three modes

- **Learn:** sixteen phrases in four groups: needs (bathroom, water, not feeling well, tissue), help (I need help, say that again, I don't understand, how do you say this in English?), things (pencil, where do I put this, I'm finished, I lost my mitten) and friends (can I play too, thank you, excuse me, good morning). The tip says when we use each one.
- **What do I say?:** eight pictures. The class says the phrase first, then picks it from three.
- **Situations:** a short story ("Snap! Your pencil broke.") and three phrases to choose from. Act it out with a partner before tapping.

## Language support

- Newcomers can point to a picture card to ask for what they need; post the cards by the door or on desks.
- Practice the polite words (*may, please, thank you, excuse me*) as a set.
- Invite families to share the same phrases in their home languages, and say them together too.

## Talk about it

- Which phrase do you use most?
- What's another way to ask for help?
- What do you say when someone says "thank you" to you?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes.
- The words light up at a steady pace. Turn Motion off to stop the pulsing "Say it together!" without changing the timing.
