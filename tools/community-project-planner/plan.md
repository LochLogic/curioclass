arc: explore-first
minutes: 45
focus: CASEL.Relationship Skills
goal: I can lead a team project by matching people to work they're good at, checking in, and giving people rest, and explain why that keeps the team going.
materials: The tool on a projector. One laptop per pair. Scrap paper for the exit check.
grouping: Whole class for weeks 1 and 2, then partners, then groups of three or four.
---
## Before class

- Open **Run a Project** on the projector. The three project cards show; Riverside Cleanup says "Start here".
- In the teacher notes, read Good to know: the volunteers and numbers are a made-up model, and the school counselor is the next step if a student is upset.
- On shared laptops, **Save on this device** keeps a plan in that browser for the next class to see. Plan to print, or to press **Clear**, then **Clear everything**, at the end.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "A club has six volunteers and six weeks. Leader A says: everyone works as hard as they can, every week. Leader B gives people rest weeks. Hands up: who finishes more?" (Most pick A.)
2. "Today you lead the team. Half of you will test Leader A's way. We'll see who was right."

## Explore (18 min)

**Riverside Cleanup**, teacher drives, class votes (8 min):

1. Press **Lead this project** on Riverside Cleanup. Point at one volunteer card: filled icon is the strong skill, outline the okay skill, then hours, the energy word **Fired up** and "Cares: ?".
2. Week 1: click a volunteer, then a task. Put Mei on **Map the trash hot spots**, Luis and Grace on **Sign up cleanup volunteers**, Dana and Ben on **Sort the recycling**. Press **Buy signboards $90** and put Tariq on **Keep It Clean trail signs**.
3. Stop on the recycling card: "It says 2 extra hours wasted. What is it telling us?" (Too many people on a small task.)
4. Class votes on a lead move. Pick **Check in**, then **Run week 1**. "Grace and Ben got +13, everyone else +3. Why?" (They care about Check-ins. Now every card shows what each person cares about.) This is the surprise: people want different things.
5. Press **Start week 2**. Read the event aloud ("Rain raised the river and moved the trash.") and vote between **Re-map the hot spots** and **Wait a week to look**. "Either way, what happens to Cleanup day?" (It waits.)

**Partners** (10 min):

6. Pairs open Riverside Cleanup and take turns: one places people, one picks the lead move. Pair A's way: everyone on one task and **Push harder** every week. Pair B's way: strong skills, rest anyone Tired, **Check in** first, then take turns with **Thank the team** and **Ask for ideas**. Play to the report.

## Make sense (8 min)

1. Two A pairs and two B pairs read their report's first line aloud. (A pairs usually get "Partly done" or "Not this time"; B pairs usually get "Finished, and the team wants to do another one.")
2. "**Push harder** gets more done this week. When did the cost show up?" (Around week 4 or 5. Energy hit 0 and people were **Stepping back** for two weeks.)
3. "Did anyone thank the team two weeks in a row? What did the summary say?" ("Same move as last week: it lands a little softer.") "Why might that be true for real people too?"
4. On the board: match skills, check in, rest, recognize. "Which did you skip?"

## Apply (10 min)

1. Groups of three or four switch to **Plan Your Own** and plan a real project (a food drive, a hallway mural, a park cleanup). Stuck groups press **Start from an example** and change it.
2. Fill in **Project name**, **Who it helps** and **What done looks like**, then tasks with a skill, a role and weeks W1 to W6. Use roles like Build lead, never classmates' names.
3. Tick at least two boxes under **How I'll keep the team going** and read the notes in the panel. "The plan says Week 2 has 4 tasks at once. What would you move?"
4. Press **Print plan**, or **Save on this device**.

## Exit check (4 min)

On scrap paper: "Week 3. Ben starts the week Tired. Luis cares about Having a say. Who do you rest, which lead move do you pick, and why?" (Rest Ben so he comes back strong. Ask for ideas, because Luis gets a bigger boost and the team's idea cuts work.) Students who push everyone or skip rest should revisit their report's **Week by week** list.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** give the rule "strong skill first, check in week 1, rest anyone Tired."
- **Stretch:** "Finish **Winter Coat Drive** with the top outcome. Money is tight: when is **Fundraise** worth the hours?"
- **Stretch:** "In **Library Lot Garden**, some tasks wait for others. Plan the order in **Plan Your Own** before you play it."

## Short on time

- Hook (3 min), then weeks 1 and 2 of Riverside Cleanup as a class, with the **Check in** surprise (10 min).
- Skip Plan Your Own. End with the exit check (4 min).
