arc: explore-first
minutes: 45
focus: CTE.Career Clusters
goal: I can name career clusters, give an occupation in each, and show how several clusters work together behind one thing.
materials: The tool on the projector, and one laptop or tablet per pair (low volume, or Sound off). Paper and pencils. Printed exit tickets (optional). Write on the board: career cluster, occupation, industry, career pathway, certification, apprenticeship.
grouping: Whole class for the hook and the model, then pairs on devices, then whole class.
---
## Before class

- On every device, open the tool and press **Start over**, then **Yes, start over**, so all 14 lanterns are dark. Lanterns save on that device only.
- On the projector, stay in **Festival**. The narrator speaks a short welcome on the first tap, so set the room volume.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Point at the lights in this room. "How many different jobs did it take to make these lights turn on?" List guesses on the board. (An electrician, a line worker, someone at the power plant, a truck driver.)
2. "Each of those is an **occupation**, a specific job. Jobs that belong together form a **career cluster**. There are 14. Today you visit a fair where all 14 are at work."

## Explore (17 min)

1. Model on the projector (3 min). In **Festival**, tap the glowing marker on **The Ferris wheel** (drag the town or tap ▶ if it is not in view). The card shows four jobs. Tap **Welder** and read the sheet aloud: what they do, the median pay ($53,750 a year), the entry-level education (a high school diploma), the job outlook and **How people get there**. Press **Close**, then **Close** on the card. The **Manufacturing** lantern at the top lights up.
2. Pairs explore **Festival** (9 min). Goal: light at least eight lanterns. Partners take turns tapping and reading aloud. On paper they write each lit cluster and one occupation they met in it.
3. Stuck pairs tap a dark cluster under **Still dark** (or **Where?** on a small screen), and the town pans to a place that has one.
4. In **Trails** (5 min), pairs tap **Kettle corn** and press **Next** to the end. The last stop says "This bag of kettle corn took 4 clusters". Fast pairs try **Stage lights** or **Your ticket**.
5. Walk around and ask: "Which job did you not expect at a fair?"

## Make sense (10 min)

1. "Which clusters did kettle corn take?" (Agriculture, Supply Chain and Transportation, Hospitality, Financial Services.) "Could the stand open if one of those people stayed home?" (No. It took all of them.)
2. On the projector, choose **Clusters** and tap Healthcare and Human Services. Read the line at the top. "Each column is a way in: the education most people start with." Point out Home Health Aide (no degree needed, $35,800) and Dental Hygienist (associate's, $98,100). Every cluster uses one pay scale.
3. "Does the longest bar mean the most important job?" (No. Pay is one fact. Every job at the fair was needed for the night to happen.)
4. Tap **All clusters**, then Construction. Tap Electrician and read **How people get there**: "A four-to-five-year paid apprenticeship and a license." "What is an **apprenticeship**?" (Learning a trade from an expert while you get paid.) "How is a **certification** different?" (An official credential that shows a skill, like a welding certificate.) A **career pathway** is the series of classes and jobs that lead into a field.
5. "One job's outlook says Decline. Does that mean it is disappearing?" (No. Slightly fewer jobs are expected by 2035. People still do it.)

## Apply (8 min)

1. Each pair picks one thing in this room (a pencil, a desk, a lunch tray, the clock).
2. They draw a trail of four or five people behind it, like the tool's trails: each person's occupation, its career cluster, and one sentence ("A logger cuts the wood.").
3. They count the clusters, then trade with the next pair and check each other's clusters in **Clusters**.

## Exit check (5 min)

On paper or the printed ticket: name one career cluster and two occupations in it, then pick one thing at the fair or in the room and name two clusters that worked on it, with a job from each. Two jobs but no cluster means: back to the lanterns.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** press **List**, then **Not opened yet**, to see every place as a plain list.
- **Support:** give a frame: "A ___ is an occupation in the ___ cluster. They help with ___ at the fair."
- **Stretch:** light all 14 lanterns. Hint: every Manufacturing job is at the Ferris wheel.
- **Stretch:** in **Clusters**, compare Hospitality (ways in at 2 of 5 levels in this tool) with Education. An empty column says "None in this tool (the real cluster may have some)". What real jobs might fill it?

## Short on time

- Model the Ferris wheel, then pairs light five lanterns in **Festival** (10 min).
- One trail together in **Trails** (4 min), then the exit check (4 min).
