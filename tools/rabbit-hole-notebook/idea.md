# Rabbit Hole Notebook (idea card)

- Module: library-6-8-rabbit-hole-notebook, grade 6-8. Codes: AASL.Explore, D1.4.6-8
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good, because the student browses a web of short linked sources and the notebook maps the path.
- One idea: Start from a big question you choose from a shelf (Why do cities have so many pigeons? Who decides what goes on a stamp?), follow short linked sources that each answer a little and raise new, smaller questions, and watch the notebook draw how your big question and the small ones change each other until you decide where to stop.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a notebook open to a blank page and a shelf of six big question cards. "Pick the question you most want answered."
  - Core loop: each source is a short page (a museum note, a city report, an interview, a chart) written for the tool; at the bottom, two or three new questions it raised. Pick one to follow; the notebook adds a branch.
  - Rewrite: every few sources the notebook asks, "Does your big question still fit?" and the student can reword it (Why so many pigeons? becomes Why did people bring pigeons to cities?); old wording stays crossed out.
  - Stop rule: the student decides when they have enough to answer, and writes a three-line answer with the supporting questions that got them there.
  - A line on screen says the notebook stays on this device and never leaves it.
  - The aha or the end state: the map shows that small questions shaped the big one and the big one picked the small ones.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - AASL.Explore: follow curiosity across varied sources and reflect on where it led.
  - D1.4.6-8: show how supporting questions and the compelling question reshape each other on the notebook map.
- Not like: enduring-questions (grade 9-12 enduring issues and expert positions); wonder-wall (K-2 question sorting); windows-and-mirrors-shelf (grade 3-5 reading lives).
- Art: generated sketchbook style with hand-lettered branches; source pages with public domain images where checked.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (sourced facts behind each short page); stores the notebook on the device (rule 7 line)
