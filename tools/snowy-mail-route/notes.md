## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Snow Town**. The Star house glows: that is where the letter goes. Tap another house to change it.
2. Ask the class: "Which way should Pip go first?" Tap the arrow they call out. Each arrow becomes a numbered card.
3. Before you press **Go**, have everyone trace the path in the air with a finger. Press **Guess** and tap the square
   where the class thinks Pip will stop. Then press **Go**.
4. Pip walks one card at a time and leaves a pair of numbered footprints for each card. Tap a footprint: its card
   lights up and is picked, so the next arrow you tap replaces it. That is how you find and fix a wrong card.

## The two modes

- **Snow Town:** free play on one map with six houses (Star, Heart, Moon, Fish, Tree, Sun) and three snowbanks. Tap a
  house to address the letter, lay up to twelve arrow cards, and press Go. Paths from the Post Office: Fish is up 3;
  Star is up 3, right 1; Sun is right 2; Tree is right 5; Moon is right 2, up 1, right 1, up 1; Heart is up 3, right 5.
- **Mail Route:** eight letters in order. Levels 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8 are build levels: the cards start empty. Levels 4,
  6 and 7 are fix-it levels: another mail carrier made the cards, and the class finds the bug.
  - The round button between Start over and Next shows the eight levels. Tap it, then tap a letter to open any level;
    the gold dot is the level you are on. Nothing is saved between visits.
  - Level 4, Wrong Way: card 5 points down into a snowbank. Change it to right.
  - Level 6, One Too Many: there is one right too many, so Pip bumps the edge of town at card 7. Take one away.
  - Level 7, Mixed Up: cards 2 and 3 are swapped, so Pip bumps a snowbank at card 2. Swap them back.

## The controls

- **Arrows:** tap to add a card. If a card is picked, the arrow changes that card instead.
- **Cards:** tap a card to pick it, and tap it again to put it down. The buttons under the cards move it earlier or
  later or take it away.
- **Footprints:** tap one to pick the card that made it. After a bump, a small "?" marks the card that made Pip bump,
  but only once the class has tapped a footprint or card, or the hint has asked twice: the screen does not give the
  answer away first.
- **Go** walks every card (it turns into **Stop** while Pip walks). **Step** walks one card, then waits; press Step
  again, or Go to walk the rest. **Slow / Quick** sets Pip's speed.
- **Guess** puts a flag on a square. Tap Guess again to take the flag away.
- **Clear** (Snow Town) empties the strip. **Start over** (Mail Route) puts the level's cards back; **Next** opens
  the next letter once this one is delivered.
- Keys: Tab moves between controls (footprints included, once Pip has walked), Enter taps, arrow keys on the arrow tray add cards, arrow keys on a picked card
  move between cards, Delete takes a card away, Escape puts it down, **G** is Go and **S** is Step.

## Talk about it

- Where do you think Pip will stop? Why? (Trace it with your finger first.)
- We changed one card. What changed for Pip? (Change one arrow and press Go again.)
- Pip bumped a snowbank. Which footprint is the last one? Which card comes right after it?
- Is there more than one path to the Heart house? Which one is shorter?
- What is a bug? (A mistake in the program.) What did we do to fix it? (Debugging.)

## Good to know

- Pip only does what the cards say. When the class says "Pip went the wrong way", ask "which card told Pip to go that
  way?" The footprint numbers answer it.
- Any change to the cards sends Pip back to the Post Office and wipes the footprints, because they no longer match.
- Motion off (the Motion button) stops Pip from sliding between squares; the timing stays the same.
- Words used: algorithm, program, bug, debugging (from the Code.org CS Fundamentals glossary). Pip says "bug" when
  level 4 opens and "debugging" when a fix-it level is delivered.
- Standards: CSTA E1-ALG-PS-01, E1-PRO-PD-04, E1-PRO-TR-07, E1-ALG-IM-03 (2017: 1A-AP-08, 1A-AP-10, 1A-AP-14).
- Everything is drawn in code and works offline.
