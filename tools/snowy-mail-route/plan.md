arc: teach-then-practice
minutes: 40
focus: E1-PRO-PD-04;E1-PRO-TR-07
goal: I can put arrow cards in order to take Pip to a house, and find and fix the card that is wrong.
materials: The tool on the big screen. An envelope. Tape and six paper arrows (three right, two up, one left) for Apply. Optional: Word Wall cards for algorithm, program, arrow, path, debug and turn.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet, with turn-and-talk partners.
---
## Before class

- Open the tool: **Snow Town**, Star house glowing. Sound on and loud. Don't tap yet.
- Tape a 3 by 3 grid of big squares on the floor. Put the envelope on the top right square.
- Post the Word Wall cards. The Teacher notes explain every button.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Hold up the envelope. "How does a mail carrier find the right house?" (The address, a map.)
2. "I am a robot. I only do exactly what you say." Ask the class to send you to the door. If they say "go to the door", wait: "Which way? How many steps?" Take exactly the steps called.
3. "Pip the penguin carries the mail and walks exactly what the arrows say."

## Teach (8 min)

1. Tap the Star house. Pip says, "Welcome to Snow Town!" Let Pip finish.
2. "The letter in the top corner has a star. Where does it go?" A child points to it.
3. "Which way first?" (Up.) Tap the up arrow: Pip says "Up!" and card 1 appears. The class calls the rest: Up, Up, Right.
4. Word Wall: "Our list of steps is an algorithm. Cards Pip follows are a program. The way Pip goes is a path."
5. "Trace the path in the air." Tap **Guess**, tap the square the class votes for, then **Go**. Pip leaves numbered footprints: "Delivered!" and "Your guess was right!"
6. Tap the Tree house (bottom right), then **Clear**. Tap the right arrow four times, then **Go**: "Pip stopped in the snow. Is there a card missing?" Count the footprints. (One more Right.) Add it, **Go**: delivered.

## Practice (15 min)

Let Pip finish talking before you tap. Partners plan, then the class calls the arrows.

1. Tap **Mail Route**. Level 1, First Delivery: the Star house. "How many squares?" (Three: Right, Right, Right.) If they say two or four, try it.
2. **Next**. Level 2, Down the Lane: the Heart house. (Right, Right, Right, Down, Down.)
3. **Next**. Level 3, The Snowbank. If the class goes straight right, Pip bumps at card 2: "Flump!" The hint says to tap the last footprint. Tap footprint 1. "Which card sent Pip into the snowbank?" (Card 2.) Tap **Start over** and build around: Down, Right, Right, Right, Right, Up.
4. **Next**. Level 4, Wrong Way. Pip says one card is wrong: "A mistake in the cards is called a bug." Tap **Guess**, flag a square, then **Go**. Pip bumps a snowbank at card 5.
5. "Which footprint is last?" (4.) Tap it. "The card after it is the bug." (Card 5, Down.) "Which way should it point?" (Right.) Tap card 5, then the right arrow.
6. **Go**: "Delivered! You fixed the bug. Finding and fixing a bug is called debugging." Point to the debug card.

## Apply (7 min)

1. A volunteer Pip stands on the bottom left taped square. Up means toward the board.
2. Lay paper arrows on the board ledge: Right, Right, Up, Left. Pip steps one square per arrow as the class says each one. Pip misses the envelope.
3. "Which card is the bug?" (Card 4, Left.) "What should it be?" (Up.) Swap it; a new Pip walks it to the envelope.
4. New Pip: Right, Right, Right, Up. Pip steps off the tape on card 3. "Which card is extra?" (One Right.) Take it away, add the up arrow, walk again.

## Exit check (5 min)

1. Tap **Snow Town**, the Sun house (two squares right of the Post Office), **Clear**, the right arrow, the up arrow.
2. "Will Pip get to the Sun house? Thumbs up or down." (Down: card 2 goes up into a snowbank.) **Go**: "Flump!"
3. "Hold up fingers: which card is the bug?" (2.) "Point the way it should go." (Right.) Tap card 2, the right arrow, **Go**.
4. Turn and talk: "What do we call finding and fixing a bug?" (Debugging.) Listen in.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** tap **Step** instead of **Go**: Pip walks one card and waits. Ask "Where next?"
- **Support:** before Go, touch each card while the class says its arrow.
- **Stretch:** level 7, Mixed Up (the level button between **Start over** and **Next**, then 7). Pip bumps at card 2. Tap card 2 and the right button under the cards: cards 2 and 3 swap, and Pip delivers.
- **Stretch:** in **Snow Town**, find two paths to the Heart house. (Up 3, Right 5, or Right 5, Up 3.)

## Short on time

- Teach steps 1 to 5 (5 min).
- Practice steps 4 to 6, the bug in level 4 (7 min).
- Exit check steps 2 and 3 (3 min).
