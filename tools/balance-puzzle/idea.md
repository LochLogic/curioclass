# Balance Puzzle (idea card)

- Module: math-6-one-step-equations-and-inequalities, grade 6. Codes: 6.EE.B
- Format / family: puzzle / balance. Fit: good.
- One idea: Weigh sealed sacks at a farmers market scale: remove the same weight from both pans or split both pans into equal groups to find what one sack holds, and when the scale tips instead of leveling, find every weight that could be inside.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a market stall with a two-pan scale, one sealed sack x and a 3 kg weight on the left, 10 kg on the right. Farmer Ruth: "What's in the sack? Prove it before you open it."
  - Equation levels: the student removes equal weights from both sides (x + 3 = 10 becomes x = 7) or splits both sides into equal groups (3 identical sacks = 12 kg, so one sack is 4 kg). Weights include 1/2 kg and 0.25 kg pieces. Opening the sack checks the answer by substitution.
  - Write-it levels: a stall story ("Ruth bought some apples and a 2.5 kg pumpkin; the total was 6 kg") becomes an equation the student builds on the scale, then solves.
  - Tipping levels: the pan tips. The sack plus 2 kg is heavier than 5 kg. The student tests values, finds the sack is more than 3 kg, writes x > 3, and shades every possible weight on a number line (and why there are infinitely many).
  - The aha or the end state: solving is finding the values that make the statement true, and an inequality has a whole range of them.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): build a puzzle for a partner, who must solve it in the fewest moves.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 6.EE.B: solving as finding values that make an equation or inequality true, checked by substitution (6.EE.5); variables standing for unknown amounts in stall stories (6.EE.6); solving x + p = q and px = q with whole numbers, fractions and decimals (6.EE.7); writing x > c or x < c from a tipped scale and graphing its infinitely many solutions (6.EE.8).
- Not like: balance-the-beam (same engine, grade 1 meaning of equals), equation-golf (grade 9 multi-step solving), phone-plan-showdown (grade 7 two-step inequalities in context).
- Art: generated scenes (the market stall, produce); people (illustrated) for Farmer Ruth; scale and number line drawn in code so tilt matches the weights.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Find what's in sealed sacks at a farmers market scale by removing equal weights or splitting into equal groups; when the scale tips instead of leveling, write the inequality and shade every weight that could be inside.
- Flags: none
