# Gallery Critique

## One idea
In a panel debate about a real painting, every claim has to point at the picture: pin each statement to the spot that backs it, challenge the ones that can't be pinned, and only what is pinned goes on the record you take away.

## Not like
- art-through-time (6-8, same family and group): many works, linked by history. Here one work at a time, argued in depth.
- art-style-match (3-5, same family and group): names a style from visible tells. Here style is at most one claim among many; the work is interpreted and judged.
- director-s-chair (9-12, story): choices that change a performance's meaning. Here nothing is changed; the painting is fixed and the argument moves.
- dance-styles-explorer (9-12): writes dance critiques with a vocabulary guide. Here critique is spoken by three roles and every sentence must be pinned to evidence; no free vocabulary list.
- virtual-gallery (9-12, same group): presenting a set of works. Here responding to one.
- fair-use-court and other 9-12 roleplays: no rules or verdicts here; the "record" is a critique, not a ruling.

## Who and how
- Grade 9-12, visual arts, module "Critique and Interpretation (Visual Arts)".
- Driver: students (laptops, alone or in threes taking one role each in turn); a teacher can run one work on a projector and let the class vote on pins. 20 to 40 minutes.
- Standards: NCAS VA:Re8 (interpret intent and meaning), VA:Re9 (apply criteria to evaluate artistic work); also VA:Re7 (perceive and analyze: the Describe and Analyze rounds).

## Screens and modes
Header: title, `Read`, `Motion`, `Sound`. Kit theme `theme-912`. Two screens: `Choose` and `Panel`, plus the `Record`.

**Choose.** Focus: the three framed works, side by side on the painted gallery wall, the biggest thing on screen. Under them, three seat cards: `Critic`, `Curator`, `Artist`, each with its painted emblem (Art) and its goal in one line (Content "Roles"). Tap a work and a seat (both stay highlighted), then `Take your seat` (always enabled; pressed with a work or seat missing it says "Pick a painting and a seat first." and outlines the missing row). A small `How it works` disclosure lists the four rounds.

**Panel.** Focus: the painting, large in the art-museum frame (Family engine) on the left, about 60% of the stage width at 1366x768, zoomable; while pinning, the painting is the only thing that takes taps. Evidence spots show as faint numbered rings only while pinning (Content). Right panel, top to bottom:
1. A round strip: `Describe`, `Analyze`, `Interpret`, `Judge`, with the current round marked. Rounds move only forward, by `Next round` (enabled once every claim in this round is pinned or challenged and the student has pinned one of their own). In Judge the button reads `Finish` under the same rule and opens the Record.
2. The floor: the claims spoken this round, as speech cards with the speaker's emblem badge and name. Each card has two buttons: `Pin` and `Challenge`.
   - `Pin`: the spots appear on the painting and the card shows `Done` and `Cancel`; tap the spot (or spots, up to two) that backs it, then `Done`. If the claim is supported by one of the chosen spots, the card is marked "On the record" with a pin icon and the spot numbers. If not: "That spot doesn't show it. Tap another spot, or tap Cancel and challenge it." Unlimited tries. `Cancel` (or Escape) leaves pinning with nothing changed.
   - `Challenge`: three reasons as buttons: `No evidence in the picture`, `Wrong round` (said too early), `A fact that needs a source`. The right reason strikes the claim through with the reason in words, and the speaker's answer (Content) shows on the card. A wrong reason: "Look again at what kind of claim this is."
3. Your claim: a sentence builder for this round (Content "Frames"), with menus, then `Pin it` (the student pins their own claim the same way; it must match a spot that supports it, from Content "Support tables").
4. In Judge only: the lens selector (Content "Lenses"), above the floor.
- `Record` (top right) opens the record at any time.

**Record.** Focus: the record text, one centered column. The transcript of everything on the record, grouped by round, each line with its speaker and spot numbers, struck claims listed under "Challenged" with reasons. Then the role's product (Content "Roles") in an editable text area with live checks beside it. Buttons: `Print`, `Back to the panel`, `New panel` (back to Choose). Nothing is saved; the page warns on New panel if the text was edited.

**Frames** (the student's own claim; menus in brackets):
- Describe: "I see [a thing from this work's spot list]."
- Analyze: "The [line, color, shape, space, light, pattern] at [spot] [leads my eye to, balances, contrasts with, repeats] [spot]."
- Interpret: "[Spot] suggests [one of four meanings for this work]." Meanings, rain: "people alone in a crowd", "a new, modern city", "a quiet, cool moment", "wealth and comfort". gulf: "danger from every side", "calm in the face of danger", "uncertain rescue", "hard work at sea". bath: "care as daily work", "closeness", "a private moment", "everyday life as worth painting".

- A panelist's Judge claim pins only while the lens selector is on its own lens (Design is Well designed, Real is Looks real, Feeling is Strong feeling, Job is Does a job) and a chosen spot is in its list; on another lens it shows "Not under this lens. Switch the lens, then pin it."

**Lenses** (Judge round): `Looks real` (does it convince as a real scene?), `Well designed` (do the parts work together?), `Strong feeling` (does it move the viewer?), `Does a job` (does it teach, record or honor something?). One line under the selector: "A judgment depends on the lens. Say which one you're using."

**Roles:**
- Critic: "Reach a judgment and back it." Product: an editable review, seeded with one pinned claim per round (the student's own first claim in that round, else the first pinned panel claim), then the lens; checked live: 150 words or fewer. (The old rule, "the pinned claims in order, 150 words or fewer", could not hold: the pinned panel claims alone run 95 to 101 words per work and up to twelve own claims add about 130 more.)
- Curator: "Write the wall label." Product: an editable label, seeded from pinned Describe and Interpret claims, checked live: 100 words or fewer, no sentence longer than 25 words. The seed runs 41 to 55 words from panel claims and can reach about 120 with six own claims; trimming it is the Curator's job.
- Artist: "Explain how it was made." Product: editable notes listing the pinned Analyze claims with their spots; no word limit.

## Interactions in detail
- Claim order in a round: the two panelists alternate; a claim written for the student's seat is said by "A visitor". All three claims of a round appear after a short stagger (400 ms each; instant with Motion off). A tap anywhere on the floor during the stagger shows all three at once, and every button works from the first frame.
- A claim card can be pinned or challenged, not both. Undo: `Unpin` and `Withdraw challenge` on the card until `Next round`.
- Wrong-round claims (said too early) can't be pinned in this round; Pin on one says "This belongs to a later round. Tap Challenge and pick Wrong round."
- Flawed claims are never supported by any spot.
- The student's own claim is required once per round; more are allowed (up to three).
- Product text areas count words (split on spaces) and, for the label, sentences (split on ., ! and ?), with the counts beside them in words; they never block printing, only warn.
- Keyboard: claims are buttons in reading order; Pin then arrow keys move between spots, Enter chooses; Escape cancels pinning.
- Sizes: painting and panel side by side down to 760 px wide, painting the wider; in the 680x425 preview the painting fills the left 55% and the floor shows one claim at a time with `Previous` and `Next`. At 375 the painting sits on top at 40% height.

## Voice
Bank voice: `calm-professor` (the panel chair; different from art-passport's and dance-styles-explorer's knowing-guide and from every spec in this batch). Style: even and clear. Uses `Curio.voice`, scope = screen. Panelists' claims and replies are on screen, not voiced (one voice can't be three people); Read speaks the current card.
- welcomeChoose (how; Choose entered; stale when Take your seat is pressed): "Tap a painting, then tap a seat, then tap Take your seat."
- welcomePanel (how; Describe starts; stale once a claim is pinned or challenged): "We start by describing. Tap Pin on a claim, then tap the spot in the painting that shows it."
- challengeHow (how; the first Pin on a flawed claim, or the first missPin; stale once a challenge is made): "If a claim can't be pinned, tap Challenge and pick the reason."
- ownHow (how; the floor is first cleared in Describe; stale once Pin it is pressed): "Now build your own claim with the menus, then tap Pin it."
- nextHow (how; the first time Next round turns on; stale when it is pressed): "The floor is clear. Tap Next round."
- roundAnalyze (how; Analyze starts; stale on its first pin or challenge): "Now analyze how the parts work together. Pin each claim to its spot, or challenge it."
- roundInterpret (how; Interpret starts; same staleness): "Now interpret what it might mean. Pin each claim to the spot that shows why, or challenge it."
- roundJudge (how; Judge starts; stale once a lens is picked): "Now judge. Pick a lens first, then pin each claim or challenge it."
- pinned (event; every good pin; stale on the next tap): "On the record."
- missPin (event; every pin that misses; stale on the next tap): "That spot doesn't show it. Tap another spot, or tap Cancel and challenge it."
- laterRound (event; Pin on a wrong-round claim; stale on the next tap): "This belongs to a later round. Tap Challenge and pick Wrong round."
- wrongLens (event; a Judge pin under another lens; stale on the next tap): "Not under this lens. Switch the lens, then pin it."
- struck (event; every right challenge; stale on the next tap): "Struck from the record."
- missChallenge (event; a wrong reason; stale on the next tap): "Look again at what kind of claim this is."
- recordReady (event; Finish opens the Record; stale when the Record closes): "The record is ready. Edit your piece, then tap Print or New panel."

## Done when
1. Every supported claim pins on its listed spots and no other; every flawed claim pins on none and is struck only by its right reason.
2. Wrong-round claims refuse Pin with the laterRound line.
3. In Judge, a panelist claim pinned with the selector on another lens shows "Not under this lens"; a student Judge claim is checked against the Support table for the chosen lens.
4. Each role's product seeds from the record and is editable; the counts are right (test a 101-word label, a 26-word sentence and a 151-word review).
5. `Next round` stays off until the floor is cleared and one own claim is pinned; in Judge, `Finish` opens the Record.
6. Focus: the painting is the largest thing in Panel at 1366x768 and in the 680x425 preview; the three works are in Choose.
7. A tap during the 400 ms claim stagger shows all three cards at once and the tapped button acts in the same frame.
8. How lines once each in five pins in a row; pinned, missPin and struck every time, dropped when stale; no line names a control that is off or hidden at that moment.
9. Painting, floor and Pin are visible without scrolling at every STANDARDS.md size; Print gives a clean black-on-white record.
10. All 8 images load; no generated image shows a person; nothing is stored.
