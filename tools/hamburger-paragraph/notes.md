## A 5-minute start

1. Pick **Dogs**. Read the sentence cards together (the **Read** button reads the one you pick).
2. For each card, ask the class: "Is this the top bun, a filling, the bottom bun, or not about dogs at all?" Click the card, then its place.
3. Press **Serve it!** The bear tells you if the burger holds together. Fix it and serve again.

## The big idea

A paragraph is built like a burger. The **top bun** is the topic sentence: it tells what the paragraph is about. The **fillings** are details that tell more. The **bottom bun** is a closing sentence that wraps it up. Without the buns, the burger falls apart. A sentence that isn't about the topic doesn't belong, like a boot in a burger.

## Talk about it

- How did you know which sentence was the topic sentence?
- Why did the boot sentence not belong?
- In **Plant a seed**, does the order of the fillings matter? Why?

## Good to know

- **Write your own** lets the class type a burger together. Try it after one or two built ones.
- Click a sentence on the burger to take it off again.
- Sound starts on (the Sound button turns it off). There's no score.
