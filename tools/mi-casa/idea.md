# Mi Casa (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-k-12-family-and-home, grade k-12. Codes: ACTFL.Novice Mid
- Format / family: explorer / spanish. Fit: good, with a task added: exploring the house is the input, and the family's questions turn it into use.
- One idea: Explore a busy house on the morning of abuela's birthday party; tap people and rooms to hear their names and short descriptions, then help the family by answering their questions (¿Dónde está el gato? ¿Quién es alta?), and notice that the describing word changes its ending with the person.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a cutaway house, every room busy. A cousin at the door: "¡Hola! Soy Diego. ¿Me ayudas?"
  - Core loop: explore mode: tap anyone or anything to hear it (la cocina, el tío, la abuela es simpática). Help mode: family members ask short questions, and the student answers by tapping the right person or room and choosing the short phrase (Está en la sala. Es mi prima.). Descriptions come in pairs (el tío es alto, la tía es alta) and the ending lights up when it changes.
  - The aha or the end state: the student describes three family members on their own from word tiles, matching gender and number (los primos son altos), and the party starts with everyone in the right room.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): "Describe your own home" (made-up or real, saved only on this device; the screen says so).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Novice Mid: lists and memorized phrases for family members, rooms and descriptions, used to answer simple questions.
- Not like: asl-family-tree (ASL sentences about people and places); mi-día (routine across a day); calendario-vivo (dates and weather).
- Art: illustrated family and cutaway house (people, illustrated), detailed enough to reward exploring.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Explore a house on the morning of abuela's birthday; tap people and rooms to hear them in Spanish, answer the family's where and who questions, and watch describing words change their endings with the person.
- Flags: needs a data source (recorded Spanish voices); light sensitivity (family depiction, fresh review); K-12 span check (gf-04).
