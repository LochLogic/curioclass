Ripple

Stretch strings across dark water, pluck them, and watch every note ripple out.

  1. 1Sweep to Play. Move your mouse across any string to pluck it. Faster sweeps are brighter and louder. Every vibration sends matching ripples across the dark water.
  2. 2Stretch & Shape. Drag empty space to create a string (hold Alt to snap angles). Click a string's center badge to pick from 30 instruments, 12 scales, and chord voicings.
  3. 3Express & Loop. Right-click & slide to sustain/bow, drag a string sideways to pitch-bend, play notes with the A to ' keys, add a beat, loop layers, and save a video, a sound file or every part on its own.

Every sound is made right here on this device. No camera, no microphone, and it works offline.

Controls & Shortcuts

Mouse

  • Pluck / Strum: Sweep cursor across a string (Hover-Strum default; press M for Click-Strum). Hold Ctrl to pass over strings silently.
  • Create string: Left-drag empty space. Hold Alt to snap to 0°/45°/90°.
  • Sustain / Bow / Roll: Hold Right-Click on a string and slide along it.
  • Pitch bend & snap: Left-drag a string body sideways (±2 semitones); release to snap-pluck.
  • Edit / Move: Click the center badge for the radial menu; drag the badge to move the whole string; drag endpoints to resize (shrink to delete).
  • Acoustic XY Pad: Shift + drag (or Middle-drag) empty space to darken/brighten the mix and add stereo echo.
  • Quick Octave / Gain: Scroll wheel over a string for octave; Shift + Scroll for string volume.

Keyboard

  • A S D F G H J K L ; ': Play notes on the focused string
  • 1 to 9 / Tab: Focus string · Delete: Remove focused string · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: Undo/Redo
  • Space: Play/Stop beat · L: Record loop layer · Z: Undo last layer
  • [ / ]: Change key · - / =: Tempo · M: Hover/Click Strum · R: Record · F: Film · H: Hide controls
