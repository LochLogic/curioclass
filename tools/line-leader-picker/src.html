Manual

The Turn Machine

Choose two pictures and run 20 pretend school days. People choose the rule; the machine follows its two steps. Tap a friend for a count readout. Read speaks the current readout. Motion and Sound are on the wooden power panel.

Four rules

A keeps the first friend at the front. B moves the leader to the back. C draws from all 20 names each day and puts the name back. D leaves drawn names out until the jar is empty. The line starts in ABC order.

Bee's switch makes her absent on day 2 only. Under B her turn passes. In either jar rule, draw from the friends present that day. Under D Bee's name stays in the jar until she leads.

Reading the evidence

The blue dashed stars are Before; gold stars count turns in this month. A ring marks a friend with no turns. The three evidence cards are optional: use them in any order, or make a Fair / Not fair call without them. Yes and No record what you noticed, then show the rule or chart fact. The fact mark describes the data; it does not grade your answer.

Same chance refers to the rule with everyone present: A no; B, C and D yes. With a sick day, attendance can change an outcome. A blue mark on a rule card points to its steps. The last evidence card marks the tallest count and the empty columns. Our chart keeps the latest call for each rule and attendance setting for this page load. No personal information is saved.

Things people have found

A name can have the same chance each day and still get no turn in a particular month. Moving the front friend to the back changes the whole pattern. A day away can matter under one rule and leave another outcome unchanged.
