# Inflation Wallet (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-10-between-the-wars, grade 10. Codes: D2.His.14.9-12, D2.Civ.8.9-12
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Live as one Berlin family from 1919 to 1933 with a wallet, a pantry and a ballot: buy bread through the 1923 hyperinflation and the Depression, and watch how both crises, among other causes, drained trust in Weimar democracy until it fell.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the family's kitchen in 1922, a loaf of bread, a pay packet and a savings book: "Spend now or save?"
  - Core loop: each turn is a month (1922 to 1924) or a year (later); prices follow real series; the student chooses when to shop and whether to hold marks, buy goods or trade for foreign money. Savings melt in 1923; a new currency steadies things; then the Depression brings mass unemployment.
  - Ballot layer: at each real election, family members weigh the parties' promises; a chart shows the real national vote shares, including the Nazi party's climb after 1929 to the largest party in 1932.
  - The aha or the end state: a cause board where the student weighs hyperinflation, the Depression, the Versailles burden, street violence, the constitution's emergency powers and elite deals, and finds that the Nazi surge came after 1929, not in 1923. The close shows how Weimar's democratic design was dismantled in 1933.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): compare Italy and the Soviet Union in the same years.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.14.9-12: the cause board with real timing, separating 1923 from 1929 to 1933.
  - D2.Civ.8.9-12: a constitution panel evaluates Weimar's democratic principles (rights, elections, Article 48 emergency powers) and how each was used against it.
- Not like: stock-market-1929 (US margin trading, spec); behind-the-podcast (sources about the US Depression).
- Art: generated scenes (the kitchen changing over time) and people (illustrated) for the family, plus public domain banknote images, because the family carries the human side.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Live as a Berlin family from 1919 to 1933: buy bread through hyperinflation and the Depression, vote in each election, then weigh which causes really ended Weimar democracy.
- Flags: needs a data source (German price and election series, checked); sensitive (Nazi rise and antisemitism, shown plainly, no Nazi imagery used decoratively)
