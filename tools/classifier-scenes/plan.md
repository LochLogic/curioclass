arc: model-then-make
minutes: 40
focus: ACTFL.Intermediate Mid
goal: I can use ground and figure, classifier handshapes and the signer's perspective to describe where things are and how they move.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Real objects for the Share part (a cup, a book, a toy car).
grouping: Whole class, then pairs.
---
## Before class

- Open picture 1 (a cup on a table).
- The tool practices the spatial grammar of classifiers. It does not show how full signs are made, and the hands are drawings. Check signs with your ASL teacher and with Deaf signers.
- Classifier choices vary between signers and regions. Treat the tool's answers as one common way.
- If you have one, cue up a short video of a Deaf signer describing a scene.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Sign or show a scene with a cup on a table, without English. "Where is the cup? How did you know?"
2. "ASL draws the picture in the air. Today you learn the rules for drawing it."

## Model (10 min)

1. In picture 1, ask: "Which one stays still?" Tap **The table**. That's the ground.
2. Choose the handshape for the table (**B**, a flat hand), then for the cup (**C**).
3. Tap in the signing space to place the table, then the cup on top. Watch it play back.
4. Press **Viewer's view**. "What changed? How does the viewer know where things really are?"
5. Show the B, C, 1, 3 and bent V handshapes on your own hands and have the class copy.

## Make (14 min)

1. Pairs work through pictures 2 to 8 with **Next**. For each, they decide the ground and figure, choose handshapes and place them.
2. After each playback, one partner signs the scene and the other checks it against the tool.
3. Move on to the moving pictures: things that pass, go up, down, onto and off. Left-handed students turn on **Left-handed**.
4. Ask about picture 3: "The blue car is on the right. Why does the signer put it on their right too?"

## Share (8 min)

1. Pairs set up a real scene with classroom objects and sign it to another pair, who arrange their own objects to match.
2. If you have the video, watch the Deaf signer describe a scene. "What did they do the same? What was different?"

## Exit check (4 min)

Show a new scene: a person walking past a car. Each student signs it to you or a partner. Check: the car (3 handshape) goes first and stays, and the person (1 handshape) moves past.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** stay with still scenes and the B and C handshapes first.
- **Support:** use the steps along the top of the panel (What stays still, Handshapes, Place, Play) as a checklist.
- **Stretch:** describe a scene with two figures that both move.
- **Stretch:** write a gloss for a scene, then compare it with the tool's gloss.

## Short on time

- Picture 1 modeled together, with **Viewer's view** (6 min).
- Pairs do pictures 2 to 4 (8 min), then the exit scene (3 min).
