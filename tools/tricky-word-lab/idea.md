# Tricky Word Lab (idea card)

- Module: ela-6-8-spelling-through-word-families, grade 6-8. Codes: L.6.2, L.7.2, L.8.2
- Format / family: lab / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Solve commonly misspelled words through roots and word families (definite from finite, separate from par), test a spelling rule against a word list, and keep a personal tricky-words list that comes back for review.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a lab bench with a misspelled word under glass: "seperate". "Find the root that settles it."
  - Core loop: trace tricky words to their roots and families (separate from par, definite from finite), test a spelling rule against a word list and find its exceptions, and add each beaten word to a personal tricky-words list that comes back for review.
  - The aha or the end state: the student spells a word they always missed and says the family that proves it.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher adds a class list.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.6.2, L.7.2, L.8.2 (spelling parts): spell correctly using roots, word families and patterns. Punctuation parts stay with pause-and-break.
- Not like: context-roots-dictionary (roots for meaning, not spelling); pause-and-break (punctuation).
- Art: drawn in code, because the words are the content.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: privacy line on screen: the tricky-words list stays on the device (rule 7); overlap risk (context-roots-dictionary)
- Origin: new tool from the 2026-10-27 overlap and gap audit (docs/agents/audit/gaps.md); codes from pause-and-break (L.6.2b, L.7.2b, L.8.2c).
