## A 5-minute start

1. Open **March** and press **▶ March!** The buddies march and a heart lights on every beat. Everyone pats their knees on each heart.
2. Try **🐢 Slow** and **🐇 Quick**. The beat gets slower or faster, but it stays steady. The tune can play on **🪵 Marimba**, **🎁 Music box** or **🎷 Soft band**.
3. Turn on **🧊 Freeze game**. At a surprise moment the music stops and the owl says freeze. Everyone freezes until the music starts again.
4. Switch to **Rhythm** and press **▶ Play** to clap the buddies' pattern together.

## The rhythm cards

| Card | Say | Clap | Music note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🐻 Bear | ta | one clap on the beat | quarter note |
| 🐰 Bunny | ti-ti | two quick claps on one beat | two eighth notes |
| 🐌 Snail | ta-a | one clap, then hold it for two beats | half note |
| 🦉 Owl | shh | no sound: open your hands for a quiet beat | quarter rest |

Tap a card along the bottom, then tap one of the four boxes. A snail takes two boxes. **▶ Play** counts in "1, 2, 3, 4" and plays the pattern four times. **❤️ Hear the beat** plays a soft heartbeat under the rhythm, so the class hears both. **♩ Show notes** adds the music notes under each card, ready for reading music later. **🎲 Surprise me** makes a new pattern.

## Echo

The four cards turn face down and the buddies play a pattern. The class listens, gets a short pause to get ready, hears "Ready... Go!" on the beat, then claps it back while the hearts keep the beat. **👀 Show me** turns the cards over and plays it again. Pick which cards can appear with the picture buttons: bears and bunnies, then add owl rests, then snail long sounds.

## The big idea

The **beat** is the steady pulse that keeps going underneath the music, like a heartbeat. The **rhythm** is the pattern of long and short sounds and silences on top of it. A bear lands right on the beat, a bunny fits two sounds into one beat, a snail stretches one sound over two beats, and the owl rests for a beat while the beat keeps going.

## Talk about it

- Pat the beat while the tune plays. Did the beat change when the tune had quick notes?
- Which card has more sounds than beats? Which has fewer?
- Is the owl's beat gone, or is it still there? How can you tell?
- Make a pattern for your name: "Mi-a" is ti-ti, "Sam" is ta.

## Good to know

- The space bar starts and stops the music.
- Speeds are 76, 100 and 126 beats a minute.
- Turning Motion off keeps the music and the hearts but stops the marching, for classes that find movement on screen distracting.

## Voice

- A narrator (the lively-host voice from the bank) says a welcome for each game, short tips, each card's sound and the echo steps, once the class taps or presses a key. Nothing talks before the first tap.
- The narrator never talks over the beat. It waits until the march or rhythm stops; "Everybody freeze!" plays in the quiet freeze.
- **🔊 Read** stops the beat and says the instructions on screen. **Sound** off silences the narrator at once.
