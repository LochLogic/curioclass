arc: teach-then-practice
minutes: 30
focus: MU:Pr4; MU:Cr1
goal: I can keep a steady beat, tell the beat from the rhythm, and clap a four-beat pattern with long sounds, short sounds and rests.
materials: The tool on the big screen with sound on. Space to sit in a circle.
grouping: Whole class in a circle.
---
## Before class

- Open **March**. The space bar starts and stops the music.
- Turn Motion off if the marching on screen distracts your class; the music and hearts keep going.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Everyone puts a hand on their heart. "Feel that? It keeps going and going. Music has a heartbeat too."
2. Press **March!** and pat knees on every heart.

## Teach (9 min)

1. Try **Slow** and **Quick**. "Did the beat change? Did it stay steady?" (It got slower or faster, but it stayed steady.)
2. Switch to **Rhythm**. Introduce the cards one at a time: **Bear** says ta (one clap), **Bunny** says ti-ti (two quick claps), **Snail** says ta-a (clap and hold), **Owl** says shh (open hands, no sound).
3. Explain the big idea: the beat is the steady heartbeat underneath, and the rhythm is the pattern of sounds on top.

## Practice (9 min)

1. Build a pattern together: tap a card, then a box. Press **Play** and clap it four times.
2. Turn on **Hear the beat** so the class hears the heartbeat under the rhythm.
3. Press **Surprise me** for two or three new patterns to clap.
4. Turn on the **Freeze game** in **March** for a quick wiggle break: freeze when the music stops.

## Apply (5 min)

1. Switch to **Echo**. The cards turn face down and the buddies play a pattern. The class listens, then claps it back on "Ready... Go!"
2. Press **Show me** to check. Start with bears and bunnies, then add the owl.

## Exit check (3 min)

Clap your name: "Mi-a" is ti-ti, "Sam" is ta. Children clap their own names one at a time around the circle while you pat a steady beat. Then ask: "Is the owl's beat gone, or still there?" (Still there.)

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** stay with bear and bunny cards only.
- **Support:** pat the beat on knees while you say the card words, before clapping.
- **Stretch:** turn on **Show notes** to see the music notes under each card.
- **Stretch:** a child builds a pattern for the class to clap, then the class echoes it.

## Short on time

- **March** with Slow and Quick (4 min).
- Build and **Play** one rhythm pattern (5 min).
