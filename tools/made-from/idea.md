# Made From (idea card)

- Module: science-7-synthetic-materials, grade 7. Codes: MS-PS1-3
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Trace an everyday synthetic material (a nylon jacket, a plastic bottle, a medicine) back to the natural resource it came from and forward to its effects on society.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a nylon jacket. "Where did this come from?"
  - Core loop: trace a synthetic material back to its natural resource (petroleum, plants) through the chemical process, and forward to its effects on society (uses, waste, jobs).
  - The aha or the end state: every synthetic thing starts as a natural resource and changes how people live.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-PS1-3: synthetic materials come from natural resources and impact society.
- Not like: molecule-builder (atoms and molecules).
- Art: generated scenes (factories and products), because the chain must read clearly.

- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (checked process steps per product)
