arc: model-then-make
minutes: 30
focus: WIDA.ELD-5; WIDA.Inform
goal: I can name places and helpers in a community and say where I go and who works there in full sentences.
materials: The tool on the big screen. Sentence frame cards: "I go to the ___." "A ___ works at the ___." Paper and crayons.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet, then partners.
---
## Before class

- Open **Explore** with **Show place names** on.
- Post the sentence frames where children can see them.
- Think of two or three real places near school the children know.

## Hook (4 min)

1. "Where did you go last weekend?" Take two or three answers.
2. "A town has lots of places, and each place has helpers. Let's take a walk around this town."

## Model (8 min)

1. In **Explore**, tap the **library**. Listen: "This is the library. Librarians work here." Say it together.
2. Tap the fire station, the bakery and the vet. Each time, the class repeats the sentence.
3. Model a frame with your own life: "I need bread. I go to the bakery."

## Make (10 min)

1. Switch to **Where do I go?** Read the need aloud. Children answer with the frame, "I go to the ___," then a child taps the place.
2. A wrong tap names that place and what happens there. Talk about it, then try again.
3. Switch to **Who helps?** Children use the frame "A ___ works at the ___."
4. Partners take turns: one says a need, the other answers with the frame.

## Share (5 min)

1. Turn off **Show place names**. Point to a place. The class names it from the picture.
2. Ask: "What is this place called in your home language?" Let children share.
3. "Who helps you when you're sick? When you're lost?" Remind them: in an emergency, call 911 or tell a grown-up.

## Exit check (3 min)

Each child draws one helper and tells you a sentence: "A ___ works at the ___. They ___." Listen for the place word and the helper word.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** beginners point and say one word in the blank. Keep the frames on the screen.
- **Support:** use only six places at first.
- **Stretch:** add a "because": "I go to the vet because my dog is sick."
- **Stretch:** "What helper would you like to be? Why?"

## Short on time

- **Explore** four places, saying each sentence together (5 min).
- One round of **Where do I go?** (5 min).
