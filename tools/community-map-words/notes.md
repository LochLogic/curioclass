## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Explore**. Tap the **library**. Hear "This is the library. Librarians work here. They help you find books."
2. Say it together, then tap two or three more places.
3. Switch to **Where do I go?** Read "I need a book about dinosaurs." Children answer "I go to the library," then tap it.

## The three modes

- **Explore:** twelve places (school, library, fire station, hospital, police station, post office, grocery store, bakery, park, vet, bus stop, farm), each with its helper and what they do.
- **Where do I go?:** a need for each place. Answer with the frame "I go to the ___." A wrong tap names that place and what happens there.
- **Who helps?:** meet a helper and find where they work: "A ___ works at the ___."

## Language support

- Frames: *This is the ___. I go to the ___. A ___ works at the ___. They ___.*
- Turn off **Show place names** once children know the words, so they read the pictures instead.
- Ask children to name the places in their own neighborhood, and in their home language too.

## Talk about it

- Which place have you visited? What did you do there?
- Who helps you when you're sick? When you're lost?
- What helper would you like to be?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes.
- The fire station, police station and hospital remind children: in an emergency, call 911 or tell a grown-up.
