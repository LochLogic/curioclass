## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Build**. Tap the **Computer box** tile. It flies to the desk and the narrator names it: "The computer box! The brain is inside. It does the thinking."
2. Tap the **Screen** and **Keyboard** tiles. Now tap the keyboard on the desk: a key lights up, teal dots run along the cable to the box, and an H appears on the screen. Tap it again for E, L, L, O.
3. Ask: "What if we take the box away?" Tap the **Computer box** tile again to put it back on the shelf, then tap the keyboard on the desk. Nothing happens, and the dots stop at a loose plug. Every part needs the brain.

## The three modes

- **Build:** free play. Tap a tile to put a part on the desk, tap the tile again (or drag the part off the desk) to put it back. Tap a part on the desk to see its power: the box hums and blinks, the screen lights up, the keyboard types HELLO, the mouse paints a rainbow, the speakers play a tune, the microphone hears the class say hello, the camera takes a picture of Bubbles the fish. Some parts need others: the camera's picture needs a screen to show it, and the microphone needs speakers to answer. With all seven parts on the desk, **All in one** unlocks: the desk turns into a laptop, and **Tablet** shows a tablet held in two hands. Tap the round spots to find each part inside, even the hidden brain. **Clear desk** puts every part back.
- **Jobs:** pick a job card (watch a cartoon, write a word, paint a picture, say hello, take a picture of Bubbles, video chat with the panda at the zoo). The class says which parts the job needs, you tap them onto the desk, then press **Try it!** If a part is missing, the computer shows what it can't do and that part's empty spot pulses blue. Extra parts are fine: they get a rest ("Zzz"). A finished job gets a check mark; **Next job** picks the next one.
- **Missing:** the desk is fully built. Press **Start**: everyone closes their eyes, Nibbles the mouse sneaks by and unplugs one part, then the computer tries a job. The class works out which part is unplugged from what went wrong, and you tap it. A wrong part just shows that it works. Seven rounds, the plug marker shows how many are fixed; after round 7, **Start** begins again.

## Words to use

- **Computer box** (the case): holds the brain, the part that does the thinking.
- **Screen**, **keyboard**, **mouse**, **speakers**, **microphone**, **camera**: the parts and their powers.
- **Hardware:** the parts of a computer you can touch.
- The teal dots flow in to the box (keyboard, mouse, microphone, camera send things in). The orange stars flow out of the box (screen and speakers show and play things). You can point at the arrows without naming them.

## Talk about it

- Which parts send things in to the computer? Which parts let the computer show or tell us things?
- A laptop has no mouse. How do you move the arrow? What does a tablet use instead of a mouse and a keyboard?
- In Missing, the cartoon played with no sound. Which part could be unplugged? How do you know it is not the screen?
- What job could we do with only the box, the keyboard and the screen?

## Good to know

- On a smartboard, children can come up and tap the tiles and parts themselves. With a keyboard, Tab reaches every button, then the parts on the desk from left to right; Enter or Space taps. In Build, typing letters on the real keyboard shows them on the screen (when the box, keyboard and screen are on the desk).
- Nothing here uses the real microphone or camera. The tool only acts them out.
- **Read** speaks the line at the top again. **Sound** off stops the narrator and the sounds; the shows keep the same timing.
- **Motion** off keeps the same timing and sounds, but parts appear in their slots without flying, cables show a still arrow instead of moving dots, and the blanket in Missing fades instead of dropping.
- Standards: CSTA K-12 Computer Science Standards (2026) E1-SYS-HW-11. The matching 2017 standards are 1A-CS-01, 1A-CS-02 and 1A-CS-03. Keeping accounts safe (E1-SYS-SE-12) is left to another tool.
- The parts, desk, laptop, tablet and screens are drawn in code, and the sounds are made in the browser, so it works offline.
