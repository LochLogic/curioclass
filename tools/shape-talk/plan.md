arc: model-then-make
minutes: 30
focus: WIDA.ELD-3; WIDA.Explain
goal: I can describe a shape in full sentences using the words side, corner, flat, solid and face.
materials: The tool on the big screen. A bag of real shapes or classroom objects (a block, a ball, a can, a cone). Sentence frame cards: "It has ___ sides." "It has ___ corners." "It is flat / solid."
grouping: Whole class on the carpet, then partners.
---
## Before class

- Open **Meet the shapes**. Keys 1 to 3 switch modes.
- Put the real objects in a bag for the Hook.
- Accept "point" or "vertex" for corner. The tool says "corner" because that is the K-1 word.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Reach into the bag without looking and describe one object: "It is solid. It can roll. It looks like a can." Children guess.
2. "Today you get to be the describer. First, let's learn the words."

## Model (8 min)

1. Tap the **triangle** and read the sentences together: "It is flat. It has 3 sides. It has 3 corners."
2. Press **Count with me**. Count the sides and corners out loud as they light up.
3. Tap the **cube**. "Is it flat or solid?" Read the faces sentence together. Children touch a real block's faces.
4. Compare the **square** and the **rectangle**: "How are they the same? How are they different?"

## Make (10 min)

1. Switch to **Guess my shape**. Read the first clue aloud and press **Cross out**. "Which shapes are left?"
2. Read clues until children can guess. If a guess is wrong, read the reason the tool gives together.
3. Switch to **Partner talk**. One child is the describer. Everyone else closes their eyes while the describer holds **peek**.
4. The describer points at the frames on screen and says the sentences. The class guesses. Do three or four describers.

## Share (5 min)

1. Partners take turns at their spots: one picks a real object from the bag and describes it with the frame cards, and the other guesses.
2. Ask a few partners to share their best sentence with the class.

## Exit check (3 min)

Hold up one real object. Each child tells you one full sentence about it: "It has ___." or "It is ___." Listen for the math words. Children who answer with one word try again with a frame.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** beginners can answer with one word in the blank. Let them point at the frame while they speak.
- **Support:** stay with the flat shapes before the solids.
- **Stretch:** fluent speakers add "It looks like..." with something from the room.
- **Stretch:** "Which solids can roll? Which can stack? Which can do both?"

## Short on time

- **Meet the shapes** with the triangle and **Count with me** (5 min).
- One round of **Guess my shape** (5 min).
