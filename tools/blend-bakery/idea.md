# Blend Bakery (idea card)

- Module: ela-1-phonics-blends-and-digraphs, grade 1. Codes: RF.1.2, RF.1.3
- Format / family: arcade / none. Fit: good, as a calm order-up arcade (customers wait, no lives lost).
- One idea: Customers order words, and the child bakes them by mixing sound ingredients in order (sh + i + p), sliding them through the oven to hear the blend, then reading the word on the box, so blending sounds into words and pulling words apart into sounds become one kitchen routine.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a bakery counter, a customer with a picture order (a ship). "Can you bake ship? Mix the sounds."
  - Core loop: drop sound jars into the mixer in order; the oven blends them aloud (sh...i...p, ship). Orders grow by station: digraph jars (sh, ch, th, wh, ck); the silent-e topping that turns cap into cape (said as "e makes the vowel say its name"); vowel-team jars (ee, ai, oa); two-layer cakes for two-syllable words (rab + bit); -s, -ed and -ing sprinkles; tricky-word boxes (said, was) that must be read whole. Reverse orders: a customer hands a finished cake and the child splits it back into sound jars.
  - The aha or the end state: a nonsense mix (shib) comes out as a cookie the child can still read, and the baker explains that reading new words is the same blending; the child then reads a short decodable menu of everything baked.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher picks stations.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RF.1.2: long versus short vowel sounds (cap or cape), blending sounds into words, isolating first, middle and last sounds, splitting words into all their sounds (reverse orders).
  - RF.1.3: digraphs, decoding one-syllable words, final-e, vowel teams, counting syllables and two-syllable words, -s, -ed and -ing endings, irregular words.
- Not like: Rhyme Time Rumble (kindergarten, sounds without letters). Vowel Team Tag (grade 2 vowel teams and affixes at speed). Letter Lanterns (single letter sounds).
- Art: generated scenes (a cozy bakery, picture-word customers' orders), because the picture order tells a beginning reader which word to bake.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Customers order words; mix sound jars in order (sh + i + p), slide them through the oven to hear the blend, and read the box. Stations add silent-e, vowel teams, two-layer words, endings and tricky words.
- Flags: too big (two broad codes; plan by stations)
