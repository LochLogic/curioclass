# Island Maker (idea card)

- Module: science-2-maps-of-land-and-water, grade 2. Codes: 2-ESS2-2, 2-ESS2-3
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Sculpt an island with mountains, hills, valleys, plains, rivers, lakes, bays and a snowy peak with a glacier, then flip to a map view with a key and see that the same island can be shown as a picture and as a map; water shows up as liquid (ocean, river, lake) and solid (snow, ice).
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: open ocean and a sculpting hand with tools (raise, lower, smooth, add water, add snow). "Make an island. Then we will map it."
  - Core loop: shape land; water flows downhill to make rivers and fills low spots as lakes; a high peak gets snow and a glacier. The tool names each landform and body of water as it forms, and the student can tap to label their own.
  - Map step: switch to the top-down map; the student builds the key (colors and symbols) and matches map spots to the 3D view. Challenges: "Make a bay a boat can shelter in", "Give the island a lake fed by a river."
  - Water hunt: a guide counts where the island's water is (ocean, river, lake, snow, glacier) and whether it is solid or liquid, then shows the same on a real world map (most water is in the ocean, most fresh water is frozen).
  - The aha or the end state: the student's island as a postcard and a finished map with a key.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 2-ESS2-2: sculpting the island and drawing its map with a key is building a model of the kinds and shapes of land and water.
  - 2-ESS2-3: the water hunt identifies where water is found and whether it is solid or liquid, then checks the real Earth.
- Not like: fast-or-slow (how land changes over time); erosion-table (grade 4 carving with water and wind).
- Art: drawn in code for the sculptable terrain and map (it must respond to every stroke), with a painted look and a generated postcard frame.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Sculpt an island with mountains, rivers, lakes, bays and a glacier, then turn it into a map with a key and find where its water is and whether it is liquid or frozen.
- Flags: none
