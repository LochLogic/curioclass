# Fact Rally (idea card)

- Module: math-2-fluency-within-20, grade 2. Codes: 2.OA.B
- Format / family: arcade / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: Volley addition and subtraction facts with a tennis bot; answering with a named strategy (doubles, near doubles, make ten, think addition) powers up your shot, and by the end the sums are just known.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a tennis court, a friendly bot across the net. "Hit the ball back by answering the fact. Tap Serve."
  - Core loop: each ball carries a fact within 20 (7 + 8, 15 - 9). The child taps the answer before the ball lands. A strategy chip glows when it fits (near double for 7 + 8); tapping it first shows the quick picture and earns a power shot.
  - Rallies adapt: facts a child misses come back sooner; facts they know come back less often. A chart of all 100 one-digit sums fills in as they become automatic.
  - The aha or the end state: the fact chart turns green; most facts got there through a strategy, not memorizing in isolation.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): two-player hot-seat match on one device.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 2.OA.B: fluent addition and subtraction within 20 using mental strategies, toward knowing all sums of two one-digit numbers from memory (2.OA.2).
- Not like: make-ten-machine (grade 1, builds the strategies slowly with counters), multiplication-dungeon (grade 3 times tables), jump-line (two- and three-digit strategies).
- Art: generated scenes (a sunny court, an expressive tennis bot), because a fluency game lives on its feel.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (make-ten-machine teaches the same strategy names; multiplication-dungeon also repeats missed facts, so give this one a different repeat mechanic and look)
