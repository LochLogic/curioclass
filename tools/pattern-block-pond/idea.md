# Pattern Block Pond (idea card)

- Module: math-k-build-a-shape, grade k. Codes: K.G.B
- Format / family: sandbox / pattern-blocks. Fit: good.
- One idea: Fill lily pad outlines with pattern blocks, build shapes from cattail sticks and pebbles, and count sides and corners to see which shapes are alike.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a pond with frogs, a lily pad outline and a tray of pattern blocks. "Drag blocks to fill the lily pad."
  - Core loop: blocks snap at edges. When two green triangles make a blue rhombus, the frog cheers and shows both. Three triangles make a trapezoid; six make a hexagon.
  - Stick-and-pebble bank: the child builds a triangle, square or rectangle from cattail sticks with pebbles at the corners; the frog counts sides and corners aloud.
  - Compare stones: two blocks side by side (a big tilted square and a small square). "Same shape or different?" The frog names sides, corners and size. Tip a block on its edge to see it is a thin solid with faces.
  - The aha or the end state: one hexagon outline filled three ways (one hexagon, two trapezoids, six triangles).
  - Class mode or extras (optional): free pond for the child's own pictures, saved as an image that never leaves the device.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - K.G.B: comparing shapes of different sizes and turns by sides and corners (K.G.4), building shapes from sticks and pebbles (K.G.5), and composing small shapes into larger ones to fill outlines (K.G.6).
- Not like: shape-spy (finds and names shapes), tile-the-floor (grade 3 area), garden-grid (grade 2 rows and columns).
- Art: generated scenes (pond, frogs, cattails); blocks drawn in code in the standard pattern-block colors so they match classroom sets.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Fill lily pad outlines with pattern blocks (two triangles make a rhombus), build shapes from cattail sticks and pebble corners, and count sides and corners to compare shapes.
- Flags: none
