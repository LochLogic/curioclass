# Friend Moves Practice (idea card)

- Module: sel-k-2-making-and-keeping-friends, grade k-2. Codes: CASEL.Relationship Skills
- Format / family: roleplay / none. Fit: good, because the student plays one kid in short scenes and practices the words.
- One idea: Step into four short playtime scenes as a new kid (joining a block tower, sharing the last red crayon, taking turns on the swing, bumping someone's sandcastle) and try a friend move with real words; the other kid's face, words and the play that follows change with the move you chose.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a playroom with four play spots glowing. "Pick a spot to play."
  - Core loop: each scene stops at one moment; three moves appear as picture cards with words to say ("Can I build too?", grabbing a block, standing and watching). Choose one, hear your kid say it, then see the friend's face and what happens next. Rewind and try another move.
  - Real answers: sometimes a friend says "Not now, I'm almost done"; the student practices a next move (wait, ask later, find another game) instead of giving up.
  - Fix-it scene: after the sandcastle bump, a sorry plus a fix ("I'm sorry. Can I help build it back?") gets a different face than a sorry alone.
  - The aha or the end state: a "friend moves" pocket fills with the moves that worked, each with its words, ready to use at recess.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - CASEL.Relationship Skills: practice asking to join, sharing, taking turns and repairing a small problem with words, and see how each move builds or strains the friendship.
- Not like: conflict-coach (grade 6-8 I-statements); feelings-faces (naming feelings on the board); recess-upstander (grade 3-5 bullying).
- Art: people (illustrated) kids with clear, readable faces, because the face change is the feedback.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: none
