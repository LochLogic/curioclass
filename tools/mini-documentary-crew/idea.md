# Mini Documentary Crew (idea card)

- Module: arts-9-12-documentary-production, grade 9-12. Codes: MA:Pr4, MA:Pr5, MA:Pr6, MA:Cn10
- Format / family: tycoon / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Lead a four-person crew making a five-minute documentary about the last shoe repair shop on a made-up Main Street; schedule interviews and shoots around real limits (the owner only talks before opening, rain, a crew member's exam week), cut it from interviews, footage, archive photos and music, test the cut on viewers, and plan where it premieres.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the shop, the people in its story (the owner, her daughter, a longtime customer, the landlord), a two-week calendar and the crew with roles.
  - Core loop: plan and assign the shoot days; each day yields footage bins with transcripts written for Curio Class. Ask for consent and decide how each person is shown. Edit on a timeline; a test screening draws a viewer attention line keyed to the cut (where it drags, where it confuses), and the student revises.
  - The aha or the end state: the premiere plan: a library screening, a local festival or an online release, each with its own rules (length, captions, music rights, audience). The student writes a director's note on what the story means to the town and to them.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): students split the crew roles.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MA:Pr4: integrating interviews, footage, archive and music into one unified film.
  - MA:Pr5: leadership and production roles, schedule and creative problem solving.
  - MA:Pr6: designing the premiere and distribution for intended impact.
  - MA:Cn10: research and the student's own response feeding the film.
- Not like: morning-show-crew (grade 6-8 one story across formats in a week); storyboard-studio (grade 9-12 shot planning); viral-video-breakdown (analysis).
- Art: generated scenes for the shop and footage bins; people (illustrated) for the subjects and crew; public domain archive photos (to check); music original to Curio Class.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Lead a crew making a five-minute documentary about the last shoe repair shop on Main Street: schedule shoots around real limits, cut interviews, footage and archive photos, test the cut on viewers, and plan the premiere.
- Flags: overlap risk (morning-show-crew); too big (tycoon plus editor; the spec should keep the edit to a few bins)
