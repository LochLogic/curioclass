# Sphere Connections (idea card)

- Module: science-5-earth-s-spheres, grade 5. Codes: 5-ESS2-1, 5-ESS2-2
- Format / family: cards / none. Fit: good (solo against a computer player)
- One idea: Play chains of event cards that cross rock, water, air and life (a volcano erupts, ash shades the sky, rain falls, a forest regrows), where fresh water is the scarce currency, so the student sees the spheres push on each other and feels how little of Earth's water is fresh and reachable.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: four sphere decks (geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, biosphere), a water bank, and a short guided first hand against the computer player.
  - Core loop: play a card that the last card could cause (each card names what it needs and what it sets off); a chain scores more for each sphere it crosses. Life and town cards cost fresh water drawn from a bank that mirrors Earth: of 100 water tokens almost all are salty ocean, about 2 are locked in ice, and groundwater, lakes and rivers share what is left. Melting-glacier and well cards unlock a little more.
  - The aha or the end state: after the game the student sorts the bank into a bar graph and a percent table, then replays the longest chain as a labeled cause-and-effect diagram with one link explained in a sentence.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): pass-and-play for two on one device.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5-ESS2-1: every chain is a model of sphere interactions, and the end diagram has the student explain one interaction with an example.
  - 5-ESS2-2: the bank's real proportions make fresh-water scarcity the main rule of play, and the student graphs amounts and percentages for each reservoir.
- Not like: raindrop-s-journey (one molecule's path, grade 6); bond-battle (cards about bonding, grade 10); erosion-table (one process at a time).
- Art: generated scenes on every card in a painted landscape style, with sphere-colored frames and the water bank drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Card game where each event sets off the next across rock, water, air and life (volcano to ash to rain to forest); fresh water is the scarce currency, held in a bank with Earth's real proportions.
- Flags: needs a data source (USGS water distribution figures, checked, with rounding chosen so the 100 tokens add up honestly)
