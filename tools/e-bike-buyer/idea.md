# E-Bike Buyer (idea card)

- Module: cte-6-8-e-bike-buyer, grade 6-8. Codes: NSPFE.Spending, NSPFE.Managing Risk
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good, because the purchase and the year after it play out as choices with consequences.
- One idea: Save up for an e-bike, choose one from an ad, a review site and a friend's advice, add up the true cost (charger, lock, helmet, lights, repairs), then pick a warranty, an insurance add-on or your own repair fund and live through a year of flats, a battery that dies and a theft attempt to see which choice paid off.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a savings jar at $900 and three bikes on a phone screen: a flashy ad bike, a review-site favorite and the one your friend owns.
  - Choose: compare the ad's claims ("50-mile range!") against the review's tested range and the friend's story; check the battery's safety certification label; add the extras to a true-cost receipt.
  - Protect: choose a 2-year warranty ($90), theft insurance ($8 a month), a repair fund ($10 a month into a jar) or nothing.
  - Live the year: monthly event cards: two flats, a battery that fails at month 10, a lock cut at month 7 (a good lock slows the thief and the bike is still there; a cheap one doesn't). Each event draws from the protection you chose or your savings.
  - The aha or the end state: the sticker price is not the cost, and each kind of protection fits some risks and not others.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - NSPFE.Spending: compare information sources and add up the full cost of ownership before buying.
  - NSPFE.Managing Risk: choose among a warranty, insurance, a repair fund or nothing and see how each handles real losses.
- Not like: the-credit-card-diaries (grade 9-12 credit); first-apartment-budget (grade 9-12 monthly budget); workplace-scenarios (grade 6-8 work choices).
- Art: generated scenes (bike shop, streets, a bedroom savings jar), generic bikes with no brand marks.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (realistic e-bike, repair, warranty and insurance prices and theft rates, sourced and dated, rule 9)
