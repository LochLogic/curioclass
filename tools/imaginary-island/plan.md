arc: explore-first
minutes: 50
focus: N-CN.A; N-CN.C
goal: I can add, multiply and conjugate complex numbers, see multiplication by i as a turn on the complex plane, and locate the complex solutions of a quadratic.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Graph paper.
grouping: Pairs on devices, with whole-class pauses.
---
## Before class

- Open **Explore** with the lantern at the Orchard (z = 3 + 2i).
- Keyboard shortcuts with the map focused: I is × i, R is Reflect, S is Square it, U is Undo.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "What is the square root of −1?" Let students argue that it doesn't exist.
2. "Mathematicians named it i and built a whole island with it. Today you explore the island."

## Explore (17 min)

1. On the projector, read the panel: "Traveler z = 3 + 2i, distance 3.61, heading 33.7 degrees."
2. Press **× i** once and read the worked line: (3 + 2i) × i = 3i + 2i² = −2 + 3i. "Where did the lantern go? Why did i² turn into −2?"
3. Pairs turn on **Distance rings** and press **× i** three more times. The lantern visits the Fox Den, the Salt Flats and the Waterfall, then lands back on the Orchard. "What stayed the same? What changed?"
4. Pairs try **Reflect**, **Square it**, **+ w** and **× w** from different landmarks and record what each does to distance and heading.

## Make sense (10 min)

1. Show the Powers of i dial: i¹ = i, i² = −1, i³ = −i, i⁴ = 1. "Multiplying by i is a quarter turn."
2. "Adding w moves every point the same way. Multiplying by w does not. What does × w do to a far point compared with a near one?" (It turns and stretches.)
3. "Multiply a landmark by its own conjugate. Why is the answer always real?"

## Apply (13 min)

1. Pairs do four clues in **Clue Trail**: predict on paper, drop a guess pin, then press **Walk it**. A guess within 0.25 counts as landed.
2. Pairs switch to **Springs**. With b = 0, they raise c from −4 and watch the springs slide together on the Real Road, meet at 0, then split north and south.
3. "Why do the two complex solutions always come as a mirror pair across the Real Road?"

## Exit check (5 min)

On a card: "Compute (2 − i)(3 + 4i) and plot it. Then solve x² + 9 = 0 and say where its solutions sit on the map." Cards that leave i² as i² show who needs the worked lines in **Explore** again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** turn on **Show the arithmetic** in **Clue Trail** before walking.
- **Support:** stay with × i and **Reflect** until the turn and mirror ideas are solid.
- **Stretch:** "Which four landmarks share a distance of 4.12? Why?"
- **Stretch:** in **Springs**, connect the discriminant b² − 4c to when the springs leave the Real Road.

## Short on time

- **× i** four times with **Distance rings** (8 min).
- **Springs** with b = 0 (6 min), then the exit card (5 min).
