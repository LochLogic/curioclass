# Dancers on Tour

## One idea
The same six-dancer piece breaks in three different ways on tour: scout a gym, a park and a small theater, then adapt spacing, facing, tempo and moves on a live floor plan until the dancers fit, stay safe distances apart, can be seen from the seats and are warmed up for that surface, and watch the run change with every adjustment.

## Not like
- choreography-board (6-8, studio): builds and revises a dance with devices on a timeline. Here the dance is fixed and the space changes; the student adapts it and keeps people safe.
- stage-blocker (6-8, stage family): blocks actors on one proscenium and checks seats. Here three very different spaces, dancers in motion, surfaces and warm-ups.
- dance-styles-explorer (9-12): reads styles as traces and rhythms. No adapting there.
- levels-and-pathways and the dance family: teach movement concepts with the class moving. This tool is a planning simulator; the class rehearses the result in the room.
- dance-hall-decades (this band): social dance history. This is concert dance on tour.

## Who and how
- Grade 6-8, dance, module "Performing in Different Spaces (Dance)". Topic line: adapting a dance to new spaces.
- Driver: students (pairs on one laptop work well); the class can then rehearse the adapted version in the room. 20 to 35 minutes.
- Standards: NCAS DA:Pr4 (use space, time and energy with intent), DA:Pr5 (embody technique; safe practice, warm-up), DA:Pr6 (perform for an audience in a venue; adapt to the space).
- Header: title, topic line, venue tabs **Gym**, **Park**, **Theater** (the tour, in order; all open from the start), mode buttons **Scout**, **Adapt** (default after Scout), **Warm up**, then kit `motion`, `sound`, `read`.

## Screens and modes

### Scout
- Focus: the painted venue view filling the stage (Art), with up to five tappable hotspots (code rings) for what to notice (Content): the floor, the audience, an obstacle, the weather or light, the exits. Each tap opens a card with the fact and a small painted surface swatch for the floor.
- Panel: the venue's spec sheet (size, surface, audience, temperature) and **Go to Adapt**.

### Adapt (the simulator)
- Focus: the top-down floor plan (code), the biggest thing on the stage: the venue's performing area to scale, the safe area (0.75 m in from every edge, dashed), obstacles, the audience area with sample seats as small chairs, and the six dancers as numbered circles at the current phrase's formation. A small audience view (Dance floor with the venue painting as backdrop, the six code dancers posed) sits at the top right of the stage, about a quarter of it.
- Phrase strip under the plan: four phrases (Content) with the current one highlighted; tap one to show its formation.
- Controls (panel): **Spread** slider per phrase (0.5 to 1.5, step 0.05), **Front** (which side faces the audience: four arrows; the venue's audience side is marked), **Tempo** (85% to 110% of 112 beats a minute, step 5%), **Swaps** per phrase (Content: for example "Jumps to relevés", "Floor work to middle level", "Diagonal to split diagonal"), **Run** (becomes **Stop**), **Reset venue**.
- Live readouts (four lights over the plan, green with a check or amber with an exclamation, each with a number): **Fit** (dancers inside the safe area: "6 of 6"), **Spacing** (closest two dancers in m: "1.9 m"), **Seen** (share of sample seats that see all six dancers in this phrase: "8 of 9 seats"), **Safety** (the risk meter, Content), and a **Notes** checklist: the choreographer's three notes for this venue, each ticked when its rule passes.
- Run: the piece plays (counts spoken as clicks with a count number, music in code), the dancers glide between formations on the plan and in the audience view; the lights update per phrase; a phrase that fails turns its strip cell amber.

### Warm up
- Focus: the warm-up builder: a 15-minute bar the student fills with blocks, and the venue's conditions above it (surface, temperature, what the piece asks of the body after the swaps).
- Blocks (tap to add, tap again to remove, drag or arrows to reorder): Pulse raiser (4 min), Joint circles (2 min), Dynamic stretches (4 min), Feet and calves (2 min), Rehearse key moves at half size (3 min), Long static holds (3 min).
- Readout: the warm-up's checks (Content), which feed the Safety light in Adapt.

## Content

### The piece: "Six Lanterns" (made up for Curio Class), 4 phrases of 8 counts at 112 beats a minute (17.1 s of counts, about 22 s with transitions)
Formations in meters at spread 1.0, x across (0 at center, + to the audience's right), z from the front edge (toward the back):
1. **Wedge** (arms and turns, standing): (0, 1.5), (-1.5, 2.7), (1.5, 2.7), (-3, 3.9), (3, 3.9), (0, 4.5).
2. **Window** (jumps, standing): (-4.5, 2), (-1.5, 2), (1.5, 2), (4.5, 2), (-3, 4.5), (3, 4.5).
3. **Circle** (floor work, low level): six points on a circle of radius 3 centered at (0, 4), at 0, 60, ... 300 degrees.
4. **Diagonal canon** (traveling, standing): (-5, 1.5), (-3, 2.5), (-1, 3.5), (1, 4.5), (3, 5.5), (5, 6.5). Swap "split diagonal": (-3.5, 1.5), (-2, 2.5), (-0.5, 3.5), (0.5, 1.5), (2, 2.5), (3.5, 3.5).

### Venues
| Venue | Area | Surface | Audience | Conditions | Obstacles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gym | 14 x 9 m (half the court, in front of the bleachers) | wood laid over concrete, little give, dusty | bleachers along the front, 9 sample seats, eye heights 1.0, 1.8, 2.6 m (three rows) | 20 degrees C, indoor | basketball hoop pole at the back left corner (outside the area) |
| Park | 10 x 7 m of lawn | grass, uneven, wet with dew at 9:30 a.m. | blankets in a half circle in front, 9 sample seats, eye height 0.8 m | 12 degrees C, sun low in the audience's eyes from the east (the hotspot card) | a sprinkler head at x 3, z 2.5 |
| Theater | 8 x 6 m stage | sprung floor with a vinyl dance surface | raked seats, 9 sample seats, eye heights 1.2, 1.9, 2.6 m | 21 degrees C | wings 1 m wide each side (outside the area) |

### Choreographer's notes (three per venue; each has a rule)
- Gym: (1) "The floor is hard. Swap the jumps for relevés, or warm up feet and calves first." Rule: phrase 2 uses "Jumps to relevés", or the warm-up includes Feet and calves and Rehearse key moves. (2) "Fill the floor. It's a big room." Rule: Window spread at least 1.3. (3) "The top rows look down on you. Keep the floor work; they'll love it." Rule: phrase 3 stays low level.
- Park: (1) "Wet grass. Slow it down." Rule: Tempo 95% or less. (2) "Blankets sit low. They'll lose the floor work." Rule: phrase 3 uses "Floor work to middle level". (3) "Mind the sprinkler head." Rule: no dancer within 0.75 m of it in any phrase.
- Theater: (1) "It's tight. Make the diagonal fit." Rule: phrase 4 uses "Diagonal to split diagonal" and passes Fit and Spacing. (2) "This floor is built for jumping. Keep the jumps." Rule: phrase 2 keeps its jumps. (3) "Use the depth. The rake lets everyone see." Rule: every phrase Seen 9 of 9.

### Readout rules (the tool's own guides, said on screen as rules of thumb)
- Fit: every dancer at least 0.75 m inside the area edge. Spacing: closest pair at least 1.5 m (arms-wide room for this piece).
- Seen: a standing dancer is hidden from a seat when another dancer is closer to that seat and within 0.45 m of the straight line from the seat (on the plan) and the seat's eye height is under 2.0 m. A low-level dancer is hidden when any other dancer is closer and within 0.8 m of the line and the seat's eye is under 1.5 m. A seat "sees all six" when none is hidden.
- Safety meter, points added: jumps on the gym floor 2 (on grass 1, on the sprung floor 0); turns on wet grass at more than 95% tempo 2; temperature under 15 degrees C without a warm-up of at least 12 minutes 2; each missing warm-up part 1 (Pulse raiser, Dynamic stretches, and Feet and calves when the piece still jumps); Long static holds placed right before Rehearse key moves or before Run 1. 0 to 1 low (green), 2 to 3 medium (amber), 4 or more high (amber, "Stop and fix this first").
- Warm-up checks: starts with Pulse raiser; at least 10 minutes (12 under 15 degrees C); includes Dynamic stretches; Feet and calves if the piece jumps; Long static holds are suggested for after the show ("Save these for the cool-down").

## Interactions in detail
- Every slider, arrow and swap redraws the plan, audience view and lights at once, while stopped or during Run (Run keeps its clock; a change takes effect in the current count).
- Spread limits come from the numbers (Sources): the Window fits the park only from 0.51 to 0.94, and the Diagonal cannot fit the theater at any spread with 1.5 m spacing (it needs at least 0.67 and fits only up to 0.65), which is why the split diagonal swap exists. Formations are centered in the area with the front row nearest the audience side; spread scales x about 0 and z about the formation's middle depth.
- Dragging a dancer: not allowed (the piece is set); only spread, front, tempo and swaps change it. A tap on a dancer shows its number, phrase position and closest neighbor.
- Run with amber lights still runs; collisions draw a red ring between the pair (with a word "too close").
- Venue tabs keep each venue's settings; Reset venue restores the piece's defaults there.
- Saved on this device: settings and warm-ups per venue (localStorage, try/catch).
- Motion off: dancers jump between formations; counts and lights keep time.
- Sizes: at 1366x768 the plan, the four lights, Spread and Run are visible without scrolling. Preview 680x425: plan and lights readable. At 375 wide: plan first, lights as a row, controls under.

## Voice
Bank voice: `wise-mentor` (the choreographer giving notes on tour; no neighbor of this tool or other spec in this band uses it). Style from the bank. `Curio.voice` with `scope: () => state.venue + state.mode`. The count during Run is clicks and numbers on screen, never spoken; no line plays during Run.
- welcomeScout (how; first Scout of each venue; stale when a hotspot is tapped): "New space. Tap the rings to scout it before we change anything."
- welcomeAdapt (how; entering Adapt; stale when any control moves): "Here's the floor from above. Move Spread, Front, Tempo and Swaps until the lights go green, then press Run."
- tooClose (event; Spacing turns amber after a change; stale on the next change): "Too close. Someone will get an elbow."
- noteMet (event; a note's rule passes for the first time per venue): "Good. That note's done."
- venueReady (event; all lights green and all three notes met; stale on the next change): "That works here. On to the next stop."
- welcomeWarm (how; entering Warm up; stale when a block is added): "Build a warm-up for this floor. Tap blocks to add them."
- staticHow (how; first time Long static holds is placed before Rehearse key moves): "Save long holds for after. Keep it moving before you dance."

## Sources
