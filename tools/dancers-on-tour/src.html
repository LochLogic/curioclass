    How the four lights work

    Rules of thumb: dancer centers stay 0.75 m inside every edge. Closest dancers need 1.5 m.

    A nearer dancer blocks a standing dancer within 0.45 m of a seat's sightline, for eyes under 2 m. For low dancers, use 0.8 m and eyes under 1.5 m. Front rotates the formation; seats stay fixed.

    Safety adds points for the floor, cold, tempo and missing warm-up parts. 0-1 low; 2-3 medium; 4+ high. It rates the whole piece. Open Warm up for the breakdown. Seat icons are compressed; rays use actual meter positions.

    Some formations retain blocked seats under this model. Compare the tradeoffs; all-green is not guaranteed.
