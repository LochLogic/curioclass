# First, Next, Last (idea card)

- Module: ela-1-writing-sentences-and-stories, grade 1. Codes: W.1.1, W.1.2, W.1.3
- Format / family: builder / sentence-train. Fit: good.
- One idea: The child builds a short piece of writing from picture cards and sentence frames in one of three kinds (a how-to, a story, an opinion), and a helper robot follows it exactly, so a missing step, a missing reason or a missing ending shows up right away.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: three job cards: "Teach Robo to make a sandwich", "Tell Robo what happened", "Tell Robo what you like best and why".
  - Core loop: order picture cards, pick a sequence word for each (first, next, then, last), and finish each sentence. How-to: Robo acts out the steps in order and makes a mess if one is missing. Story: Robo plays the events and asks "And then?" until there is an ending. Opinion: Robo asks "Why?" until there is a reason, then "So?" until there is a closing.
  - The aha or the end state: the finished piece reads back as one short paragraph with the child's words, and Robo does it right.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print the piece; writing kept on this device only.
- Standards, one line per code: W.1.1: opinion with a topic, a reason and a closing; W.1.2: how-to naming a topic with facts in order and a closing; W.1.3: story with two or more events in order, sequence words and an ending.
- Not like: Sentence Train (kindergarten sibling, single sentences). My Story Strip (kindergarten drawing). Hamburger Paragraph (grade 2 paragraph parts).
- Art: generated scenes (picture cards) and an illustrated robot, because literal-minded Robo is the joke that teaches.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Write a how-to, a story or an opinion from picture cards and sequence words, and Robo follows it exactly: a missing step makes a mess, a story with no ending keeps asking "And then?", and an opinion needs a reason.
- Flags: none
