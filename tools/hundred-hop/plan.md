arc: teach-then-practice
minutes: 30
focus: K.CC.A
goal: I can count to 100 by ones and by tens, and count on from any number.
materials: The tool on the big screen. Room to hop in place.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet.
---
## Before class

- Open the tool. The frog is waiting on the **Start** rock in **Count along**.
- Decide whether to leave **Say the numbers** on. Turn it off later, so the class does all the counting.

## Hook (4 min)

1. "Can anyone count to 100? Let's see how far we get together." Count out loud as a class until it gets shaky.
2. "Today a frog helps us count all the way to 100, with a shortcut too."

## Teach (8 min)

1. Press **Hop!** and count "one!" together. Then press **Keep hopping** and count along out loud.
2. Stop at 19. "What comes next?" (20.) Stop again at 29 and at 39. "What do you notice?" (After every 9 comes a new ten.)
3. Switch to **10s**. The frog goes back to the start rock and jumps a whole row at a time: 10, 20, 30. "Look, the tens all have a flower."
4. Count by tens to 100 together, holding up all ten fingers for each jump.

## Practice (10 min)

1. Tap a lily pad, like 46, and count on from there together. Try two more starting pads.
2. Switch to **Where will it land?** The numbers hide. Ask: "What number is the frog on?" Then press **Go!** and count along with the hops.
3. "Where did it land?" The class calls it out, then press **Reveal** to check.
4. Press **New puzzle** for two more. Turn on **Show the tens** if the class needs help.

## Apply (5 min)

1. Hop in place and count together: ten hops by ones, then count by tens with a big jump for each ten.
2. Start in the middle: "Start at 52 and hop 3 times. Where are we?" (55.)

## Exit check (3 min)

Call on children one at a time: "Start at 27 and count on 3." "What comes after 39?" "Count by tens from 50." Note who still needs to start from 1.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** keep **Say the numbers** on and count along with the voice.
- **Support:** count to 20 or 30 first, then build up over a few days.
- **Stretch:** turn off **Say the numbers** and let the class do all the counting.
- **Stretch:** "The frog is on 52 and hops 3 times. Where will it land? How do you know?"

## Short on time

- Count by ones to 30 with **Hop!**, then count by tens to 100 (6 min).
- One **Where will it land?** puzzle (4 min).
