# Comma Chameleon (idea card)

- Module: ela-5-conjunctions-tense-and-commas, grade 5. Codes: L.5.1, L.5.2
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good for L.5.2; a comma puzzle can't teach L.5.1 (perfect tenses, tense shifts, correlative conjunctions) well.
- One idea: A chameleon changes the picture to match whatever a sentence says, and the student places commas until the picture shows what was meant ("Let's eat, Grandma" against "Let's eat Grandma"), so they learn that a comma is a meaning tool, not decoration.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a target picture (a family at a table inviting Grandma to dinner) and a sentence with no commas; the chameleon's picture shows Grandma on a plate. "Make the chameleon's picture match the target."
  - Core loop: drag commas into gaps; the chameleon redraws the scene after each move. Levels: items in a series (bought eggs, milk, cheese and crackers or cheese crackers), after an introductory word or phrase, with yes and no, tag questions (It's cold, isn't it?), and direct address. A titles level marks book titles with italics and song titles with quotation marks. Later levels hide one wrong comma among right ones.
  - The aha or the end state: a "trickster" round where the student places commas to make the chameleon draw something silly on purpose, then fixes it.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): make your own two-meaning sentence for a friend.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.5.2: series commas, introductory elements, yes and no, tag questions, direct address, and title marks, each with a picture that proves the meaning.
  - MOVE L.5.1: new tool: a grade 5 tense time-line where events slide on a track and the student picks the verb (had finished, will have finished) and either/or, neither/nor pairs, and fixes tense shifts in a story.
  - MOVE L.5.2 (spelling, part e): to draft-car-wash's grade 5 Polish station.
- Not like: grammar-garage (grade 4 grammar repairs); sentence-remixer (grade 6 combining); story-blanks (grade 3 parts of speech).
- Art: illustrated chameleon and scene pieces that recombine, because the picture is the feedback.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs new tool (grade 5 tense time-line, queued by rule 10)
