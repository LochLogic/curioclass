# Calendario Vivo (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-k-12-numbers-colors-and-calendar, grade k-12. Codes: ACTFL.Novice Low
- Format / family: sandbox / spanish. Fit: good, with gentle goals added: free play with the calendar is the heart, and small requests from a pen pal give a solo student a reason to use each word.
- One idea: Keep a living wall calendar in Spanish with a pen pal in Buenos Aires: you set the date, the weather and color-coded plans, and everything you tap is spoken, so numbers, colors, days, months and weather become one daily habit, and July turns out to be winter for your pen pal.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a big Spanish wall calendar (weeks start on Monday, day names in lowercase) and a weather window. A note from the pen pal: "¿Qué día es hoy?"
  - Core loop: tap any day, number, month or weather icon to hear it. Free play: drag weather stickers, color in birthdays and holidays with named colors (marca el cumpleaños en rojo), count down days. Light requests from the pen pal steer play ("Mi cumpleaños es el doce de agosto. ¿Qué día de la semana es?"), and the student answers by tapping and saying it.
  - The aha or the end state: the two calendars side by side: same date, opposite seasons (hace calor here, hace frío there), and the student builds the full date sentence "Hoy es lunes, el 14 de octubre" and the weather report for both cities.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a projector morning calendar routine in Spanish for the class.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Novice Low: memorized words and phrases for numbers to 31, colors, days, months, the date and weather, used in short answers to the pen pal.
- Not like: asl-number-bingo (ASL numbers and time in Sign Strip panels); mi-día (daily routine and clock time); hola-hallway (greetings by time of day).
- Art: generated scenes for the two city windows and weather; illustrated pen pal (people, illustrated).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Keep a Spanish wall calendar with a pen pal in Buenos Aires; tap anything to hear it, color in plans, set the date and weather, and answer the pen pal's questions, finding that your summer is their winter.
- Flags: needs a data source (recorded Spanish voices; Buenos Aires climate facts checked at spec); K-12 span check (gf-04).
