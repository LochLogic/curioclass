Infinite Hike is a calm walk, not a contest. There is nothing to win and no score: a trail winds on forever through summer forest, autumn forest, flower meadows and mountain country, with birdsong, wind and the odd bear, squirrel or rabbit in the distance. It works well as a quiet minute between tasks, or left on auto-walk as a calm screen for the room.

**Things to notice together.** How the plants change as the trail climbs: thick forest, then open meadow, then rock, snow and a treeline. How the light and sounds change from morning to night (press T). The corner reading shows distance walked and height above sea level in meters.

**How to play.** W and S or the arrow keys walk; Space turns on auto-walk; drag to look around. On a touch screen, hold the arrow buttons. The menu can start the walk in a forest, an autumn forest, a meadow or the mountains, and sets the detail level (Low suits older computers).

**Good to know.** It needs a browser with WebGPU, which recent browsers on most computers and newer phones have; older devices show a short message instead. The only thing it saves on the device is the detail setting.
