## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Lines**. A line draws itself across the savanna. Ask: "What kind of line is this?"
2. Press **Name it!** The line takes its color and the card gives its name and a way to move it with your body.
3. Press **Draw along** and trace the line on the board (or let a child do it).
4. Press **Next stop**. Lines from earlier stops stay in the picture but fade back, and the new line glows yellow so everyone knows which one to look at. After ten stops the whole picture is made of lines.

## The ten lines

| Stop | Line | In the picture | Move it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Horizontal | where the land meets the sky | arm flat, side to side |
| 2 | Vertical | tall grass | stand up tall |
| 3 | Diagonal | rain blowing in the wind | lean like a slide |
| 4 | Zigzag | mountains | finger zigzag in the air |
| 5 | Curved | a rainbow | rainbow over your head |
| 6 | Wavy | a river | wiggle your arm like water |
| 7 | Spiral | a snail shell | spin your finger from small to big |
| 8 | Loopy | a bird doing loops | fly a loop-the-loop |
| 9 | Dotted | footprints on the path | tap your knees |
| 10 | Thick and thin | a tree trunk and its branches | wide, then skinny |

## Shapes, Colors and Draw

- **Shapes**: the safari camp is made of cut-paper shapes. Pick a shape at the bottom and the class finds every one (4 circles, 3 squares, 3 triangles, 4 rectangles, 3 ovals, 1 diamond, 2 stars and 1 heart). Tapping the wrong shape names it, so mistakes still teach.
- **Colors**: tap one primary paint to turn the chameleon red, yellow or blue, then a second to mix a secondary color: red + yellow = orange, yellow + blue = green, red + blue = purple. **Hide and seek** asks the class to help the chameleon hide on something its color.
- **Draw**: brushes for straight, curvy, zigzag, wavy, dotted and spiral lines, shape stamps (drag out from the middle to size them), thin to thick, nine colors, undo and clear.

## Talk about it

- Where do you see a zigzag line in our classroom? A wavy line?
- Which lines feel calm? Which feel busy or excited?
- What shapes make up your house? Your face?
- What two colors would you mix to make the chameleon green?

## Good to know

- Lines, shapes and colors are three of the elements of art. Paint Mixer (grades 3-5) goes further with color.
- Turning Motion off shows each line at once instead of drawing it; the teacher sets the pace either way.
