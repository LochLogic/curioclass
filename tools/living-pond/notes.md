## What it teaches

An open ecosystem instrument shows matter moving among algae, animals, decomposers and water and air. Students can investigate how sunlight enters a food web, heat leaves, and a change affects other populations over seasons.

## Key skills

- Change a habitat factor and compare its history with an unchanged copy.
- Trace matter through living and nonliving pools.
- Read population traces and oxygen in units.
- Distinguish a simplified model from a measured pond.

## Support at home

- Watch a nearby patch of water from a safe place and notice which organisms share it.
- Look at fallen leaves and discuss where their material might go.
- Notice how shade and seasons change plant growth outside.

## Using the instrument

It opens running. The tray stocks the tank immediately; Net changes stocking buttons to removals. All habitats are live controls. Life, Matter and Energy are lenses on the same pond, with no assigned sequence. The Manual explains the web and the readouts. Save keeps action histories on this device.

## Good to know

Matter units are illustrative, not grams. This is one made-up pond. Rates are model parameters, and inherited minnow shade is sped up. There is no measured population dataset. It uses browser speech because no ranger recordings are bundled. Keyboard shortcuts are in the Manual. Local storage may be blocked; play still works.

Size: The final bundle size is recorded in codex-report.md. One packed atlas holds the 36 painted objects; the working water, organisms' positions, chart, matter and energy are drawn in code.

## Cold review clarifications

The energy readout shows net production after respiration. A negative number means stored chemical energy is being used; no negative transfer percentage is shown. Fish food and stocking also bring stored chemical energy into the tank.

Food arrows connect to visible organisms; missing kinds are labelled absent. In Life, focus the tank, move with the arrow keys and press Enter to inspect the nearest creature. The food table describes the simplified model, rather than every real species diet.

Named species references and current reuse terms are in the Manual and tool.draft.json. No source text, illustrations or measured population dataset are copied.

## Recorder and model findings

Floating algae and algae on stems have separate traces. The optional Shade key shows average inherited shade on its own 0 to 7 scale. The Energy lens retains a last-year column when the current year's totals reset. In Matter, Enter follows the dot nearest the keyboard pointer, and the trip reports its waiting time in weeks.

Some feeding is not a promise that fish survive: the unchanged ten-winter Starter run with Some feeding has winterkill conditions in six winters and about 0.86 minnows at the end. The Manual names this model result. Algae use sunlight to build from carbon dioxide and water; nutrients from muck are a small share combined into the model's Water & air pool. OpenStax Biology 2e 8.1 and NOAA Ocean Exploration independently support the photosynthesis explanation; their terms and URLs are in the Manual and metadata.
