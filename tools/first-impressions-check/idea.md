# First Impressions Check (idea card)

- Module: sel-9-12-bias-and-first-impressions, grade 9-12. Codes: CASEL.Self-Awareness, CASEL.Social Awareness
- Format / family: mystery / none. Fit: good, because each rider is a small case: make a claim from scant clues, gather more, revise.
- One idea: Ride a city bus for one route and make a quick guess about six riders from what you can see (a teen in a hoodie with headphones, an older man in a suit, a woman in scrubs asleep against the window); at each stop, gather one more clue about each rider, see which of your guesses held, which broke, and which clue your first guess leaned on.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the inside of a bus at dawn, six riders, a notebook. "First impressions: what do you guess about each rider?"
  - Guess: for each rider, pick from neutral guesses (where they're headed, what they do, if they would help you if you dropped your bag) and how sure you are.
  - Clues: at each stop one new clue arrives per rider (a phone call overheard, a book title, a name tag, how they treat the driver). Guesses that break get a mark showing which first clue (clothes, age, posture) the student leaned on.
  - Take their view: at the end, each rider narrates a short part of their morning; the student then rereads their notebook.
  - Reflection: a private pattern card shows which cues the student leaned on most. A line on screen says guesses stay on this device and never leave it.
  - The aha or the end state: everyone forms first impressions; checking them against evidence, and against the person's own story, is the skill.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - CASEL.Self-Awareness: notice your own quick judgments and which cues drove them.
  - CASEL.Social Awareness: take each rider's perspective through their own story and revise the picture.
- Not like: conflict-coach (grade 6-8 conflict talk); mind-map (mental health); weekend-substance-choices (decisions).
- Art: people (illustrated) riders drawn with care, avoiding caricature; each rider's story is not a twist built to shame the guesser.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: sensitive (stereotypes by race, class, age and disability: a fresh review agent checks every rider and clue, rule 5; guesses stored on the device only, rule 7)
