# Moon Base Drama (idea card)

- Module: arts-k-2-drama-in-role, grade k-2. Codes: TH:Cr3, TH:Re9, TH:Cn11
- Format / family: roleplay / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Commander Ada (an illustrated character in role, voiced by the tool) calls the child onto a moon base crew; pick a prop that shows your job, answer her in role with your voice and body, use true moon facts to save the greenhouse before the long moon night, then change what your character said and watch the story change.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the base airlock, a prop shelf (wrench, seed tray, camera, map) and "Choose the prop that shows your job".
  - Core loop: Ada speaks in role and asks questions; the child answers with choice cards read aloud, or acts it out (big slow hops for low gravity, a helmet check). The problem: the greenhouse power is low and night is coming. Fact cards (no air, about one sixth of Earth's gravity, about two weeks of sunlight then about two weeks of dark) are the clues.
  - The aha or the end state: the crew moves the plants under the lamps and saves power for the night; then the rewind button lets the child change one line their character said and replay, and the scene goes a different way.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a teacher reads Ada's lines from a script card and the class becomes the crew.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - TH:Cr3: rewinding and changing a line or action to adapt the drama.
  - TH:Re9: choosing a prop and costume piece that shows a job, and saying why it works.
  - TH:Cn11: using science facts about the moon inside the drama (knowledge from another subject).
- Not like: act-it-out (acting a folktale as a class); gallery-critique (grade 9-12 roleplay debate).
- Art: generated scenes for the base and moon surface; people (illustrated) for Ada and the crew.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Join Commander Ada's moon base crew: pick a prop that shows your job, answer her in role, use true moon facts to save the greenhouse before the long night, then rewind and change one line to see the story change.
- Flags: needs a data source (moon facts checked against NASA pages)
