# Story Stage (idea card)

- Module: ela-k-story-time-characters-and-setting, grade k. Codes: RL.K.1, RL.K.2, RL.K.3
- Format / family: sandbox / story-stage. Fit: good: free staging, with the narrator's questions as gentle prompts in place of a teacher.
- One idea: After hearing a short folktale, the child puts it on a puppet stage, choosing the backdrop (where), the characters (who) and each big event in turn (what happened), and the narrator asks one question per scene that the child answers by pointing on the stage.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the curtain opens and the narrator tells The Little Red Hen with pictures. Then: "Now you put on the show. Where does it happen? Pick a backdrop."
  - Core loop: the child drags characters on, then moves them through the big events; between scenes the narrator asks one key-detail question (Who would not help? What did she plant?) and the child taps the answer on stage.
  - The aha or the end state: the child plays the whole show back as a puppet retell; if a big event is missing, the puppet audience looks puzzled at that spot until it is added.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): three tales (The Little Red Hen, The Gingerbread Man, The Three Billy Goats Gruff); a free-play stage with any characters.
- Standards, one line per code: RL.K.1: the narrator's key-detail questions, answered by pointing; RL.K.2: retelling the tale by staging it in order; RL.K.3: choosing characters, setting and major events as separate steps.
- Not like: Bedtime Retell (ELD sibling, retells in words with sentence frames; this one stages it). Story Map Treasure (grade 1, problem, solution and lesson). Two Sides Theater (grade 2 point of view).
- Art: generated scenes (backdrops) and people (illustrated) puppets, because a stage needs warmth to invite play.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Hear a short folktale, then put it on a puppet stage: pick where it happens, bring on the characters, move them through each big event, and answer the narrator's question about each scene by pointing.
- Flags: overlap risk (bedtime-retell, mild: different subject and skill)
