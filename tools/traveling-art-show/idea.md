# Traveling Art Show (idea card)

- Module: arts-3-5-caring-for-and-exhibiting-art, grade 3-5. Codes: VA:Pr4, VA:Pr5, VA:Pr6, VA:Re9
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Six student artworks go to the town library for a month; choose the ones that fit the theme with a checklist, place each one away from the library's real hazards (a sunny window, a heater vent, the story-time rug, the busy door), then fast-forward a month and see what faded, smudged, curled or cracked.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the library floor plan with its hazards hidden, a crate of eight works (chalk pastel, watercolor, clay bowl, glued collage, paper sculpture, printed photo, yarn weaving, marker poster) and the show's theme.
  - Core loop: pick six with the theme checklist, choose how to protect each (glass frame, pedestal with a cover, sleeve, a spot out of the sun), place it, and write a short label (title, artist, material, a sentence about the idea). Hazards reveal as you look around.
  - The aha or the end state: the month fast-forwards; sun fades the watercolor in the window, heat curls the collage, a toddler smudges the chalk. Fix and replay. A last screen sets the library beside a museum: who guards it, how the light and air are kept steady, and what each place is for.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a teacher can set the theme and the venue.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - VA:Pr4: choosing works for the show against the checklist, and seeing how each material's needs limit where it can go.
  - VA:Pr5: protecting and placing each work for this venue, and writing its label.
  - VA:Pr6: the library-and-museum compare (purposes of museums, galleries and other venues).
  - VA:Re9: applying one set of criteria (the theme checklist) to several works.
- Not like: hallway-art-museum (K-2: what a museum is and who works there); virtual-gallery (grade 9-12: sequencing a portfolio); school-wall-mural (grade 6-8 public art).
- Art: generated scenes for the library; the eight works made as illustrated student art; drawn effects for fading and curling.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Get six student artworks ready for a month at the town library: choose them with a theme checklist, protect and place each away from sun, heat and busy hands, write labels, then fast-forward a month to see what survived.
- Flags: needs a data source (what light and heat really do to each material, checked against conservation guidance)
