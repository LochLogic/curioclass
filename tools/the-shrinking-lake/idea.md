# The Shrinking Lake (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-6-the-shrinking-lake, grade 6. Codes: D2.Geo.9.6-8, D2.Geo.12.6-8
- Format / family: timeline / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Slide through sixty years of Lake Chad on real satellite images as drought and irrigation shrink it and the population around it grows; herders, farmers and fishers move, land use changes, and the four bordering countries (Chad, Cameroon, Niger and Nigeria) choose at each point whether to compete for the water or share it.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the lake in the 1960s, a decade slider and four border markers.
  - Core loop: move the slider; the satellite image, a population counter and a land-use map change. At decision points, a herder, a farmer and a fisher explain how their lives moved, and the student picks a response (new dams and canals, shared water rules, nothing) and sees a modeled effect on conflict and cooperation.
  - The aha or the end state: a cause board separating natural causes (drought) from human ones (irrigation, dams, growing demand), and an honest note that scientists weigh these causes differently and that the lake has partly recovered in places.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): four students each play one country.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Geo.9.6-8: the decision points link human-caused change to competition or cooperation among the four countries.
  - D2.Geo.12.6-8: population and land-use layers show how moving people change land use around the lake.
- Not like: five-themes-field-trip (one city, building a map); region-compare (comparing regions in data); hunter-to-farmer (ancient farming).
- Art: public domain (NASA and USGS Landsat images of Lake Chad) plus people (illustrated) for the herder, farmer and fisher.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (lake area by decade and population, with the debate over causes); sensitive (armed conflict and displacement in the region named plainly, with care; fresh review)
