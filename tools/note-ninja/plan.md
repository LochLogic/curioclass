arc: teach-then-practice
minutes: 40
focus: MU:Pr4
goal: I can name notes on the treble staff, find middle C, and fill a measure with notes and rests that add up to the time signature.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Optional: rhythm sticks or desks to tap on. A large staff drawn on the board.
grouping: Whole class, then pairs on devices.
---
## Before class

- Open **Explore** with the treble clef showing.
- Draw a large five-line staff on the board.
- Decide on a class saying for the lines, or use Every Good Bird Does Fly.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Play a short, familiar tune on the projector with **Play**, or hum one. "How could a musician who never heard this play it exactly right?"
2. "Music has its own alphabet, written on five lines. Today you learn to read it."

## Teach (10 min)

1. In **Explore**, tap the bottom line: it's **E**. Tap the spaces going up: **F A C E**.
2. Press **Lines** for the saying, then **Spaces**. Students point to the lines on their hands (five fingers are five lines).
3. Tap a piano key and watch where the note lands. **C4** is middle C, on its own little ledger line.
4. Explain the clef: the treble clef curls around the G line.

## Practice (14 min)

1. Switch to **Note Ninja** and pick **Treble lines**. The class calls out each note before it reaches the red line, then a student taps the letter.
2. Pairs play on their own devices: **Treble lines**, then **Treble spaces**. They try to beat their best score.
3. Switch to **Count the beats** and pick **4/4**. Model filling one measure. The green boxes show which beats are filled, and the counts appear under each note.
4. Pairs fill two measures, then press **Play** to hear them counted in on a drum.

## Apply (8 min)

1. Try a **Puzzle** in **Count the beats**: the measures have a gap. "Which note or rest fits?"
2. Switch to **3/4**. "What note fills a whole measure by itself?" (A dotted half note.)
3. The class claps and counts the pairs' measures out loud. Rests are a silent open hand.

## Exit check (4 min)

Draw three notes on the board staff: one on a line, one in a space, and middle C. Students write the letter names on a card. Then: "In 4/4, a measure has a half note and a quarter note. What is missing?" (One beat: a quarter note or a quarter rest.)

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** turn on **Letter names** so every line and space is labeled while students practice.
- **Support:** stay with **Treble lines** until it is solid.
- **Stretch:** **Note words** asks for a word spelled with note letters (BAG, CAGE, CABBAGE).
- **Stretch:** switch to **Bass**, or play **Note Ninja** on the **Grand staff**.

## Short on time

- Teach the lines and spaces in **Explore** (6 min).
- One round of **Note Ninja** on **Treble lines** (6 min), then the board check (3 min).
