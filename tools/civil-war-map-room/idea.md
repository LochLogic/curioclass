# Civil War Map Room (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-8-the-civil-war, grade 8. Codes: D2.His.14.6-8, D2.His.15.6-8
- Format / family: map / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Step through an animated map of the war from 1861 to 1865, stopping at turning points (Antietam and the Emancipation Proclamation, Gettysburg and Vicksburg, the fall of Atlanta), read a short real letter from a soldier or civilian on each side at each stop, then rank the causes of Union victory (industry and railroads, the blockade, emancipation and Black soldiers, leadership, will to fight) and defend the top pick.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a war room table with the 1861 map and a stack of dated folders.
  - Core loop: open a folder; the map animates the campaign; a letter, a photograph and two facts follow. The student pins each turning point to the causes it shows (Vicksburg: control of the Mississippi; emancipation: about 180,000 Black soldiers in the Union army).
  - The aha or the end state: a ranking board of causes, with evidence pinned from the folders, and the student's written case for the most important one; a closing card on the war's human cost and the end of slavery.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): compare rankings with a historian's view.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.14.6-8: the turning points pinned to their causes and effects.
  - D2.His.15.6-8: ranking the causes of Union victory and defending the ranking.
- Not like: expansion-map (territory over time); voices-of-freedom (sources on slavery before the war); reconstruction-amendments.
- Art: public domain (Library of Congress maps, photographs and letters, to check at spec).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Step through an animated map of the Civil War's turning points, reading real letters from both sides at each stop, then rank the causes of Union victory and defend your top pick with evidence.
- Flags: sensitive (war deaths and slavery as the cause of the war, told plainly; no glorifying either side; fresh review)
