# Sea Search Grid (idea card)

- Module: math-5-the-coordinate-plane, grade 5. Codes: 5.G.A
- Format / family: cards / coordinate. Fit: change to map, because the play is a search on a gridded sea chart, not cards; and the old "hide boats and call shots" line echoes a well-known board game.
- One idea: Join a harbor rescue crew: read reported positions as (east, north) from the harbor, call search points to find a drifting kayak, and use each "you are 3 east and 2 south of it" reply to learn what each coordinate means.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a sea chart with a harbor at the origin, x as kilometers east and y as kilometers north, and Captain Lena on the radio. "A kayaker's last call put her at (4, 7). Mark it."
  - Learn the grid: the student places marks from radio calls, names the axes and origin, and hears why (4, 7) and (7, 4) are different places.
  - Search rounds: the kayak drifts to a hidden point; the student calls a point, and the reply says how far east or west and north or south the kayak is. Fewer calls earn a better crew rating.
  - Log rounds: a drift log (hour 0 at (2, 1), hour 1 at (3, 3), hour 2 at (4, 5)) is plotted; the student answers in context: how far north did she drift each hour? Where will she be at hour 4 if the wind holds?
  - The aha or the end state: each number in a pair is a distance in one direction from the origin, and plotting a log turns a story into a picture you can read.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): one student plots a route for the boat, and a partner follows it by coordinates only.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5.G.A: the coordinate system (axes, origin, first and second numbers as distances along each axis) learned from radio calls (5.G.1); graphing points in the first quadrant to solve real problems and interpreting coordinates in context, from the drift log (5.G.2).
- Not like: two-rule-race (same grid engine, grade 5 patterns), deep-sea-dive (grade 6 four quadrants), fire-lookout-triangles (grade 12 bearings).
- Art: generated scenes (harbor, sea chart texture, rescue boat); people (illustrated) for Captain Lena and the kayaker; grid drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Join a harbor rescue crew: mark radio positions as (east, north) from the harbor, call search points to find a drifting kayak from "3 east, 2 south" replies, and read a drift log on the grid.
- Flags: none
