# Hunter to Farmer (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-6-early-humans-and-the-neolithic-revolution, grade 6. Codes: D2.His.1.6-8, D2.Geo.6.6-8
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Lead one band in the Fertile Crescent about 11,000 years ago through twenty years of seasons, choosing each season to forage, tend wild wheat or plant fields; farming feeds more people but ties them to one place, with more work, stored grain to guard and new kinds of homes, and the end map shows farming beginning separately in other parts of the world.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a hillside camp with wild wheat, gazelle herds and a river, and a season dial.
  - Core loop: each season, split the band's work among gathering, hunting, tending and planting; watch food, population, work hours, health and the camp itself (tents to mud-brick houses, granaries, shrines) change. Droughts and good years test the choice.
  - The aha or the end state: farming wins on numbers but costs work and health (a real finding from early farming skeletons); the final map links the student's village to separate beginnings of farming in China, the Americas and New Guinea.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): two bands side by side, one that never farms.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.1.6-8: the end map connects one village's shift to the wider, worldwide change.
  - D2.Geo.6.6-8: the place's wild wheat, animals and river shape what the band becomes (homes, beliefs, how they bury their dead).
- Not like: river-kingdom (later states and laws); settle-the-valley (grade 4, settlers changing a valley); smoke-and-honey (economics, not origins).
- Art: generated scenes for the changing camp across seasons and people (illustrated) for the band, because the change in daily life must be seen.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Lead a band in the Fertile Crescent through twenty years of seasons: forage, tend wild wheat or plant fields, and watch food, people, work, health and homes change, then see farming begin separately around the world.
- Flags: needs a data source (dates and health findings checked with current archaeology)
