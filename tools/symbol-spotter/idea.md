# Symbol Spotter (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-k-symbols-and-holidays, grade k. Codes: D2.Civ.8.K-2, D2.His.3.K-2
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Find the flag, the bald eagle, the Liberty Bell and the Statue of Liberty hidden in a parade-day town, hear the idea each one stands for (freedom, fairness, welcome), then open holidays that honor people who changed the country and ask your own questions about them.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a town on parade day with symbols tucked into the scene. "Find something red, white and blue."
  - Core loop: tap a symbol to open a one-minute read-aloud story that ends on its idea: the Liberty Bell and freedom, the flag's stars counting every state equally, the Statue of Liberty welcoming newcomers.
  - Holiday doors: Independence Day, Presidents' Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Veterans Day. A question wheel (Who? Why? What changed?) lets the student build a question ("Why did Dr. King march?") and the story answers it.
  - The aha or the end state: a sticker book of symbols with the idea each holds, plus the student's best question for each holiday person.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher adds state or local symbols.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Civ.8.K-2: each symbol story names one democratic principle (equality, fairness, freedom, respect for fair rules) and shows it in kid terms.
  - D2.His.3.K-2: the student generates questions about the people behind holidays with the question wheel, and hears answers.
- Not like: festival-parade (world cultural festivals, not national civic symbols); changemaker-cards (grade 2 community changemakers on cards).
- Art: people (illustrated) and generated scenes, because portraits of real people must be respectful and consistent.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Find national symbols hidden in a parade-day town and hear the idea each stands for (freedom, fairness, welcome), then open holidays that honor people who changed the country and ask your own questions about them.
- Flags: sensitive (holiday choice and the people honored; fresh review agent per rule 5)
