# ASL Family Tree (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-k-12-family-home-and-school, grade k-12. Codes: ACTFL.Novice High
- Format / family: builder / asl. Fit: good, if the build reaches home and school too: the module is family, home and school, and Novice High means simple sentences, not just labels.
- One idea: Help a Deaf teen named in the tool build a "my people and places" board for a school project: you place family members on a tree, rooms of the home and places at school, and for each one you read and then sign a short sentence about it, so the board becomes a signed introduction to a life.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the teen (video) signs: "I need help with my project. My family, my home, my school." An empty board with three sections.
  - Core loop: each tile is a family member, a room or a school place. The student watches the teen sign a sentence about it (SISTER MY, TALL, LIKE SOCCER), picks the matching picture details, and places the tile. Then the student builds the same kind of sentence for a second family (a made-up classmate) from sign tiles, and the video plays the full sentence back by a Deaf signer to check it. Families include many shapes: grandparents raising kids, two moms, step-siblings, a foster brother.
  - The aha or the end state: the finished board plays as one signed introduction, and the student sees that ASL describes people by placing them in space: the teen points to where each person was set up instead of repeating the sign.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): "Make your own" board with any family shape, saved only on this device (the screen says so); pairs present their boards.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Novice High: understanding and building simple signed sentences about family members, home and school, with descriptions (tall, funny, likes).
- Not like: mi-casa (Spanish house explorer, spoken); asl-number-bingo (receptive drill); classifier-scenes (classifiers for objects in space, not describing people).
- Art: real signing video by fluent Deaf signers (required); illustrated families, rooms and school places (people, illustrated).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Help a Deaf teen build a signed project board about family, home and school; read each signed sentence, place the tile, then build sentences for a second family and check them against video.
- Flags: needs a data source (signing video); sensitive (family structures and Deaf characters: fresh review, rule 5); privacy line on "make your own" (rule 7); K-12 span check (gf-04).
