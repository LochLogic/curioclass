## Before class

Open **Teacher setup** in the panel, choose your state and type your town's name. On the state map, drag the star to where your town is. These are saved on this device only.

## A 5-minute start

1. Start in **My room**. Ask: what do you see? Where is the bed? This is a map: we are looking down from above.
2. Press **Zoom out**. The room shrinks into a glowing box on top of the house. Ask a child to point to where the room is.
3. Keep zooming out: street, town, state, country, continent, Earth. Then zoom back in, all the way home.

## The big idea

Every place is inside a bigger place. My room is in my home, my home is on my street, and so on, out to planet Earth. Maps show places from above, and they show land and water.

## Good to know

- **Show where** makes the glowing box blink and says which smaller place it shows.
- **Land or water?** works on the state, country and continent maps: tap anywhere and hear "Land!" or "Water!"
- The ladder in the panel jumps to any level; the zoom passes through every level on the way, so children see the nesting.
- The room, home, street and town are storybook pictures, not your real neighborhood. Ask: how is our street the same? How is it different?
- The narration is recorded with a natural voice. It says "my town" and "my state"; the names you set appear on screen.
- Teacher-led on a projector or smartboard.
