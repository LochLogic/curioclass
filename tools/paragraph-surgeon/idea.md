# Paragraph Surgeon (idea card)

- Module: ela-8-analyzing-structure-and-paragraphs, grade 8. Codes: RI.8.5
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good: each paragraph is a patient, and each operation tests a sentence's role.
- One idea: The student operates on one strong informational paragraph at a time, removing or moving a sentence and watching a vital-signs monitor show what the key concept loses (its definition, its limits, its example, its "but"), then labels each sentence's role and restores the strongest order, so they learn how particular sentences develop and refine a key concept.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an operating table with a paragraph from a science or history explainer (what makes a species "endangered", what counts as a "drought") and a monitor whose lines track the concept's parts: defined, narrowed, illustrated, qualified.
  - Core loop: the student lifts out a sentence; the monitor drops on the line that sentence held up, and a reader card shows the misunderstanding that follows ("So any rare animal is endangered?"). They tag each sentence's role (defines, narrows, gives an example, qualifies, connects to a bigger idea) and try a different order; some orders keep every line steady but read worse, and the student explains why. Later patients are paragraphs with one sentence that does nothing, to be removed safely.
  - The aha or the end state: a final paragraph that arrives scrambled; the student rebuilds it and writes a one-line "surgical note" for each sentence's job.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a "case conference" where the class debates one sentence's role.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RI.8.5: analyzing the structure of a specific paragraph and the role of each sentence in developing and refining a key concept.
- Not like: explainer-sections (6-8, how sections build a whole text); essay-train (grade 5, transitions between paragraphs); argument-map (grade 12, which claims hold up an essay).
- Art: drawn in code for the operating room and monitor, with an illustrated surgical team (people, illustrated).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Operate on an informational paragraph: remove or move a sentence and a monitor shows what the key concept loses (its definition, limits, example or exception), then label each sentence's role and restore the strongest order.
- Flags: none
