# Terrarium Pal (idea card)

- Module: science-k-what-living-things-need, grade k. Codes: K-LS1-1
- Format / family: creature / terrarium. Fit: good
- One idea: Care for a terrarium with a bean plant, a land snail and a pill bug over a sped-up week, giving or holding back water, light, food and air, and find the pattern: every living thing needs water and air, animals need to eat, and plants need light instead of food.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a glass terrarium on a windowsill with a plant, a snail and a pill bug; a care tray holds a water drop, a lamp, a lettuce leaf and a lid with air holes. "Pick something to give, then tap to the next day."
  - Core loop: each day the student chooses what to give each living thing; the plant perks up or droops, the snail comes out or tucks into its shell. A picture log keeps each day with a face for each living thing.
  - Try-it cards: "What if the plant gets lettuce but no light?" The plant cannot use the lettuce and droops, so light is what it needs.
  - The aha or the end state: a sorting board ("needs water", "needs air", "needs food", "needs light") where the student places the plant, the snail, the pill bug and a child, using the log as evidence.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the class votes on each day's care on the projector.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - K-LS1-1: the care log is the observation record, and the sorting board describes the pattern of what plants, animals and people need (plants do not take in food).
- Not like: plant-experiment-shelf (grade 2 fair test of light and water, same engine); beaver-builders (animals changing a place).
- Art: generated scenes (a real-looking terrarium and its states), because the droop and the hiding snail must read clearly. Nothing dies on screen: a neglected snail stays in its shell and the plant wilts, and both recover with care.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Care for a terrarium with a plant, a snail and a pill bug over a sped-up week, giving or holding back water, light, food and air, and find what every living thing needs and what only plants or animals need.
- Flags: none
