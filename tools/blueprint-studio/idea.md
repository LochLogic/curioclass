# Blueprint Studio (idea card)

- Module: math-7-scale-drawings-and-constructions, grade 7. Codes: 7.G.A
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good for scale drawings and triangle constructions; see MOVE for cross sections.
- One idea: Design your dream bedroom on a scale floor plan, check it in a 3D preview, redraw it at a new scale, and build the triangular pieces (a corner desk, a loft brace) from given sides and angles, finding out when the parts make one triangle, many or none.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: graph paper with a room outline at 1 inch = 2 feet, a furniture tray, and an architect named Ms. Ruiz. "Will a queen bed fit? Measure on the plan first."
  - Plan rounds: the student places furniture, reads real lengths from the scale and checks fit in the 3D preview. Area surprises: doubling the scale makes the drawn rug four times the paper area.
  - Rescale rounds: redraw the plan at 1 inch = 3 feet for a smaller printout; the student computes new lengths and areas.
  - Triangle workshop: build a corner desk top from three sides (3 ft, 4 ft, 8 ft will not close: no triangle); from two sides and the angle between (exactly one); from three angles (many sizes, all the same shape). The student uses ruler and protractor tools and records which conditions give one, many or no triangles.
  - The aha or the end state: a scale multiplies every length, but area by the square, and some measurements fix a triangle while others do not.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print the plan, or design a classroom layout together.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 7.G.A (7.G.1, 7.G.2): computing actual lengths and areas from a scale drawing and redrawing at a different scale (7.G.1); drawing shapes from given conditions with ruler and protractor tools, and finding when three measures make one triangle, more than one, or none (7.G.2).
  - MOVE 7.G.A (7.G.3, plane sections of right rectangular prisms and pyramids): new tool: a grade 7 clay-cutting bench where the student slices prisms and pyramids with a wire at different angles and predicts each cut face before it opens.
- Not like: slice-and-stack (grade 10 cross sections and solids of rotation), shrink-ray (grade 5 scaling by fractions), net-folder (grade 6 nets).
- Art: generated scenes (studio desk, 3D room preview with furniture); people (illustrated) for Ms. Ruiz; plans drawn in code to exact scale.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Design a bedroom on a scale floor plan, check the fit in 3D, redraw it at a new scale (area grows by the square), and build corner-desk triangles from given sides and angles to see when they make one triangle, many or none.
- Flags: too thin for the whole 7.G.A cluster (needs the MOVE above)
