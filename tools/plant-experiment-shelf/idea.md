# Plant Experiment Shelf (idea card)

- Module: science-2-plants-need-water-and-light, grade 2. Codes: 2-LS2-1, 2-LS2-2
- Format / family: lab / terrarium. Fit: good
- One idea: Plan a fair test on four bean plants (light and water, no light, no water, neither), time-lapse two weeks and measure them, then at a second bench build a model animal (a fuzzy bee, a sticky sock dog) and test how well it carries pollen or seeds.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a shelf with four pots, a lamp, a closet and a watering can; a notebook page. "Which thing will you change? Keep everything else the same."
  - Core loop: set each pot's light and water, check that only one thing differs between two pots (the notebook flags an unfair test), run two sped-up weeks, measure height with a ruler and color the leaves on a chart.
  - Bench two: flowers with pollen and a field of burrs and seeds; build a model from parts (pipe cleaner, cotton ball, felt, a smooth plastic bead), run it flower to flower or through the field, and count pollen grains moved or seeds carried. Compare fuzzy and smooth models.
  - The aha or the end state: plants need light and water to grow (the plant in the dark grows pale and tall, then flops; the dry one wilts), and fuzzy or hooked bodies move pollen and seeds, the way bees and furry animals do.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 2-LS2-1: planning and running the four-pot fair test and recording growth.
  - 2-LS2-2: building and testing a model that mimics an animal dispersing seeds or pollinating flowers.
- Not like: terrarium-pal (kindergarten care log, same engine); three-pigs-lab (testing materials, not living things).
- Art: generated scenes for the shelf and the time-lapse plant stages, because the dark-grown plant must look real; the chart is drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Run a fair test on four bean plants with and without light and water and measure two weeks of growth, then build a fuzzy model animal and test how well it carries pollen and seeds.
- Flags: none
