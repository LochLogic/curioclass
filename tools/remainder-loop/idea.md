# Remainder Loop (idea card)

- Module: math-7-terminating-and-repeating-decimals, grade 7. Codes: 7.NS.A
- Format / family: lab / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Divide 1 by 7 the long way and watch the remainders cycle, then try other fractions and find out why every fraction's decimal must end or repeat.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a long division of 1 by 7, one step at a time. "Watch the remainders."
  - Core loop: divide fractions the long way and track each remainder on a loop; a repeat means the decimal repeats, a zero means it ends; try many fractions and explain why it must be one or the other.
  - The aha or the end state: every fraction's decimal ends or repeats, because there are only so many remainders.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 7.NS.A (7.NS.2d): converting a rational number to a decimal with long division; the decimal terminates or repeats.
- Not like: square-root-hunt (repeating decimals, light overlap); hot-air-and-sandbags (signed numbers).
- Art: drawn in code, because the remainders are the picture.
