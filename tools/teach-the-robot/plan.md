arc: explore-first
minutes: 40
focus: E2-ALG-ML-02
goal: I can tell how Kip learns from examples, and why more different pets with the right labels help it guess better.
materials: The tool on the big screen. A whiteboard and marker. Optional: the Word Wall cards for data, label, train, model and machine learning.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet, with turn-and-talk partners.
---
## Before class

- Open the tool in **Teach**, sound on and loud. Don't tap anything yet, so Kip's welcome is ready.
- Post the Word Wall cards. Pick partners.
- The example cards go cat, dog, cat, dog. If the class can't agree, that is the answer. The Teacher notes explain every button.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "How did you learn what a dog is? Did someone tell you a rule, or did you see lots of dogs?" (We saw lots of dogs.)
2. Tap **Read**. Kip says: "This is Kip. Kip is a robot that learns from examples."
3. "Kip has never seen a cat or a dog. Today we are Kip's teachers."

## Explore (15 min)

1. Tap **Next pet**. Marmalade walks in. Kip asks, "Is it a cat or a dog?" The class says it, then tap **Cat**. The card flies onto Kip's memory wall. **Kip remembers:** shows 1.
2. **Next pet** again: Bear. Tap **Dog**.
3. Tap **Mystery pet**. When Kip says "What will Kip guess? Say it out loud!", everyone calls out. Kip draws a dotted line to the pet it remembers that looks most alike. Maple's line goes to Marmalade: cat, and a check.
4. **Mystery pet** twice more: Nutmeg (right), then Luna, a fluffy black cat. Kip guesses dog; the line goes to Bear. Stop: "Why did Kip think Luna was a dog?" (Luna is dark and fluffy, like Bear. Kip only knows two pets.) "How can we help?" (Show Kip more pets.)
5. Tap **Pet Parade**, then **Start parade**. Ten new pets walk by. Kip's guesses are not spoken, so the class calls "check!" or "oops!" for each. Count together: 6 of 10. A bar grows on **Kip's Chart**, with 2 under it for the pets Kip remembers.
6. Tap **Teach** and teach nine more with **Next pet** and **Cat** or **Dog**: Inky, Pebble, Snowball, Rocket, Tiger, Biscuit, Coco, Pudding, Smoky.
7. **Pet Parade**, **Start parade** again. "Say the name of any pet Kip misses." (9 of 10. Kip misses Pippin.)

## Make sense (8 min)

1. "Why did Kip think Pippin was a cat?" (Pippin is a dog with pointy ears, and the pointy-eared pets Kip knows are cats.) "What pet should we show Kip?" (A dog with pointy ears.)
2. **Teach**, **Next pet**: Ziggy, a pointy-eared dog. Tap **Dog**. Run the parade: 10 of 10. Kip says, "The more different pets Kip saw, the better Kip guessed!"
3. Read **Kip's Chart**. "Which bar is tallest? How many pets did Kip remember then?" (The last, 12.) "Did we ever tell Kip a rule?" (No. Only examples.)
4. Point to the cards: the pets are the data; "cat" or "dog" is the label; showing examples is how we train Kip; what Kip knows now is its model. A computer learning from examples is machine learning.

## Apply (7 min)

1. Draw two made-up creatures and label them: a "Zib" (round, spots) and a "Zab" (tall, stripes).
2. Draw a new round, spotted one. "Be Kip. Which does it look most like?" Children point, then say the label. (Zib.)
3. Draw a tricky one: round, with stripes. Partners talk: "What example would help?" Draw and label one more, then guess again.
4. Change the Zib label to Zab. "Now what would Kip guess for a round, spotted one?" (Zab, which is wrong.) "Does a wrong label help?" (No. Kip learns it wrong.)

## Exit check (5 min)

1. "Kip thinks a fluffy dog is a cat. What should we show it?" 1 finger for five more cats, 2 for one more fluffy dog. (2: an example like the new pet.)
2. "If we tell Kip a cat is a dog, will its guesses get better or worse?" Thumbs up or down. (Down: worse.)
3. Partners tell each other how Kip learns. Listen to four or five pairs for "from examples with labels".

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** before each **Mystery pet**, point to two wall cards: "Does it look more like this one or this one?"
- **Support:** a frame on the board: "Kip guessed ___ because it looks like ___."
- **Stretch:** teach a wrong label. Tap Marmalade's card, tap **Forget**, then **Next pet** brings her back; tap **Dog**. The parade drops to 8 of 10, and Kip asks, "Did Kip learn something wrong?"
- **Stretch:** "A fair model works for every kind of pet. When Kip knew two pets, was it fair to fluffy black cats?" (No. It called Luna a dog.)

## Short on time

- **Read**, teach Marmalade and Bear, one parade: 6 of 10 (7 min).
- Teach up to Ziggy, the twelfth pet, and parade again: 10 of 10 (8 min).
- The fingers question from the Exit check (3 min).
