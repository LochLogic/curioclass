# My Life Timeline (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-1-timelines-and-me, grade 1. Codes: D2.His.1.K-2, D2.His.9.K-2
- Format / family: timeline / timeline-maker. Fit: good
- One idea: Build a timeline of a life from moments and the proof that each happened (a photo, a hospital bracelet, a drawing, a story Grandpa tells), so the student learns to put events in order and sees that history is built from different kinds of sources.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: Juno, an illustrated six-year-old, holds a shoebox of keepsakes. "Help me put my life in order. Pull something out of the box."
  - Core loop: each keepsake is a source (a baby photo, a tiny shoe, a first-day-of-school picture, a birthday card, a voice memory from Grandpa); the student names what kind of source it is and drags it to its spot on the timeline. Ages under the line help ordering.
  - The aha or the end state: the finished timeline shows the same event can be proven by different kinds of sources, and some moments only have a story. Then "Make your own": the student builds their own timeline from stickers and drawings; nothing is uploaded and the screen says it never leaves the device.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print the student's timeline; a second sample life (Juno's grandma as a kid).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.1.K-2: order multiple events on Juno's timeline and on the student's own.
  - D2.His.9.K-2: sort each keepsake by kind of source (photo, object, paper, spoken memory) and say how it shows the past.
- Not like: then-and-now-flip (objects across eras); state-story-timeline (sibling engine, state history at grade 4).
- Art: people (illustrated) for Juno and family, generated scenes for keepsakes, because a warm real-feeling family matters here.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Help Juno put her life in order from a shoebox of keepsakes (a baby photo, a tiny shoe, a birthday card, Grandpa's story), naming each kind of source, then build your own life timeline from stickers and drawings.
- Flags: sensitive (family situations vary: adoption, foster care, loss; the own-timeline step must not require baby photos or birth facts; teacher note per rule 8); privacy statement needed (rule 7)
