# Claim Repair Shop (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-10-claim-repair-shop, grade 10. Codes: D3.3.9-12, D4.4.9-12, D4.5.9-12
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Broken explanations of why the Berlin Wall fell in 1989 come into a repair shop, each with a fault (sources that contradict each other, steps out of order, a claim too vague, a single cause); the student diagnoses the fault against a bench of real sources and rebuilds a stronger claim.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a workbench with a cracked explanation: "The Berlin Wall fell because Reagan said 'tear down this wall.'" A bench of sources sits beside it.
  - Core loop: each level is one explanation; the student tags the fault (credibility, contradiction, sequence, vague claim, missing detail), checks it against the sources (Gorbachev's reforms, Hungary opening its border, the Leipzig marches, the botched November 9 press conference), and swaps in better evidence or reorders steps. Faults get subtler as levels rise.
  - The aha or the end state: a final open repair: the student writes their own explanation, then the shop runs the same checks on it and shows its strengths and weaknesses.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): students break an explanation for a partner to repair.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D3.3.9-12: every repair pulls evidence from several sources and detects where they conflict to revise the claim.
  - D4.4.9-12: the credibility checks critique how each explanation uses claims and evidence.
  - D4.5.9-12: the sequence and detail faults critique reasoning, order and supporting details.
- Not like: alliance-web (WWI causes, grade 9 simulator); cold-war-crisis-room (decisions in 1962); behind-the-podcast (tracing a secondary work's sources).
- Art: generated scenes (a repair shop bench, Berlin 1989 backdrops) and public domain photos where checked, because the shop setting makes the puzzle concrete.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Broken explanations of why the Berlin Wall fell arrive in a repair shop: contradictory evidence, steps out of order, claims too vague; diagnose each fault against real sources and rebuild a stronger claim.
- Flags: overlap risk (current concept uses WWI causes, same as alliance-web; topic changed to 1989 here)
