# Word Zoo (idea card)

- Module: ela-1-grammar-basics, grade 1. Codes: L.1.1, L.1.2
- Format / family: sort / none. Fit: good for parts of speech (sorting into habitats), with a second "zoo show" round where sorted words build sentences; not for the conventions code (see MOVE line).
- One idea: Word animals wander in and the child sorts them into habitats (naming words in the meadow, action words in the river, describing words in the trees, joining words on the bridges), then puts on a zoo show where the animals must line up into sentences that agree, so the child sees that each kind of word has a job in a sentence.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a zoo map with empty habitats and a keeper (Mia). "New word animals just arrived. Where does each one live?"
  - Core loop: sort word animals into habitats; inside a habitat, sort again (common and proper names, one and many, I, me, they; past, present and future verbs that walk, are walking, will walk). Show round: line animals into a sentence (The seal claps. The seals clap.); a mismatch makes the show stop with a puzzled seal. Joining words (and, but, or, because) link two short shows into one; where words (on, under, behind) move animals in the picture; the and a (determiners) pick one or any.
  - The aha or the end state: a grand show where the child grows a short sentence into a long one using one animal from every habitat, and the narrator reads it.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher picks habitats in play.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.1.1: sorting nouns (common, proper, possessive), verbs by time, adjectives, conjunctions, determiners, prepositions and pronouns; subject-verb agreement in the show round; building and growing simple and compound sentences.
  - MOVE L.1.2: new tool idea: a grade 1 postcard writer where the child capitalizes names and dates, adds end marks, puts commas in dates and lists, and spells words by common patterns (it cannot be taught well by sorting).
- Not like: Sentence Train (kindergarten sentence basics). Story Blanks (grade 3 parts of speech). Context Clue Hunt (word meanings).
- Art: generated scenes (a zoo map and word animals), because each habitat must be instantly recognizable.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Sort word animals into zoo habitats (naming words in the meadow, action words in the river, describing words in the trees), then put on a show where they line up into sentences that must agree.
- Flags: new tool needed for L.1.2
