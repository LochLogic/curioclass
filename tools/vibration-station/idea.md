# Vibration Station (idea card)

- Module: science-1-sound-and-vibration, grade 1. Codes: 1-PS4-1, 1-PS4-4
- Format / family: lab / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Pluck a rubber band, tap a drum with rice on top and hum into a tube to see that every sound comes from something shaking, see a loud speaker make the rice jump, then build a cup-and-string phone or a drum signal to get a message to a friend across a playground.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a workbench with a rubber band box, a drum sprinkled with rice, a tuning fork by a bowl of water and a cardboard tube. "Make a sound. Then look closely."
  - Core loop: make each sound; a slow-motion view shows the band blur, the rice bounce, the water splash from the fork, and a wiggle line shows the sound. Stop the shaking (a finger on the band) and the sound stops. A speaker turned up makes rice on a second drum jump, so sound can make things shake too.
  - Hum step (optional, with the microphone): hum into the tube and watch a wiggle line move with the voice. On-screen line: "Your sound stays on this device. Nothing is recorded or sent." No speech recognition is used.
  - Design step: a friend is far across the playground. Choose parts (cups, string, a can, a drum, a flashlight) to build a device that sends a message; test it, see if the message arrives, and fix it (pull the string tight).
  - The aha or the end state: a picture record: "shaking makes sound, sound makes things shake", plus the device that worked.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 1-PS4-1: the bench tests show vibrating materials make sound and sound makes materials vibrate (rice on the drum, water by the fork).
  - 1-PS4-4: the playground step, designing, testing and fixing a device that uses sound or light to talk over a distance.
- Not like: secret-code-signals (grade 4, codes and wave patterns, not building a device); shadow-puppet-studio (light, not sound).
- Art: generated scenes for the bench and playground; the slow-motion vibration and the wiggle line are drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Pluck rubber bands, tap a drum with rice on it and hum into a tube to see that shaking makes sound and sound makes things shake, then build a cup phone or drum signal to reach a friend far away.
- Flags: none (microphone is optional; the privacy line shows whenever it is on)
