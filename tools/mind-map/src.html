Get help now

If you or a friend are struggling, you don't have to handle it alone. Talk to an adult you trust: a parent, school counselor, nurse, coach or teacher. These free, confidential services are open to teens:

988 Suicide & Crisis LifelineCall or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org. Any time, day or night, for any kind of emotional crisis.
EmergencyIf someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
Crisis Text LineText HOME to 741741. Any time.
The Trevor ProjectFor LGBTQ+ young people: call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678. Any time. thetrevorproject.org
Teen LineTalk to a trained teen: text TEEN to 839863 or call 800-852-8336, evenings (Pacific time). teenline.org
SAMHSA National HelplineTreatment and support for mental health and substance use: 1-800-662-4357. Any time.

Outside the United States, findahelpline.com lists free helplines by country. This tool is for learning and can't diagnose anyone.
