arc: explore-first
minutes: 50
focus: NHES.S1; NHES.S8
goal: I can describe the mental health continuum and common conditions, correct myths that feed stigma, and practice helping a friend connect to support.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. The names of your school counselor and nurse on the board. The crisis lines: 988 (call or text), text HOME to 741741, and 911 in an emergency.
grouping: Whole class, then pairs on devices.
---
## Before class

- Preview the whole tool. It covers depression, eating disorders, trauma and suicide.
- Decide whether to use the second practice conversation ("Sam sends a goodbye"), which talks about suicide.
- Tell your counselor about the lesson, and tell students the day before what's coming and who they can talk to.
- Check the crisis lines again if you print them.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Point out the **Get help now** button and the names on the board. "Anyone can step out today if they need to."
2. "Your body has a health continuum, from fit to sick. Does your mind? Today we find out."

## Explore (15 min)

1. On the projector, open **The map** and start with **Everyday stress** in the middle.
2. Tap the zones on the continuum: thriving, coping, struggling and unwell. "Can someone with a diagnosis be thriving? Can someone without one be struggling?" (Yes to both.)
3. Pairs pick two conditions and read the cards: what it is, what it can look like, what it is not, and what helps.
4. Play **Myth or fact** as a class vote, reading the evidence after each one.

## Make sense (8 min)

1. "Which myth surprised you? Where do you think it comes from?"
2. Read the last **Myth or fact** screen together: how myths feed stigma.
3. "What makes it hard for teens to ask for help? What would make it easier?"

## Apply (17 min)

1. Pairs open **Help a friend** and work through "Jordan has been quiet." Each choice gets feedback; only the most helpful reply moves on. Name the steps: notice, ask, listen, connect.
2. If you are using it, do "Sam sends a goodbye" together as a class. "Why was asking directly the best reply?" Highlight refusing to keep the secret and calling 988 together.
3. Pairs open **Stress bucket**. Add stressors, then open taps. Notice what clogs the taps. Try the box-breathing guide together.

## Exit check (5 min)

On a private card: "Name two taps you could open this week. Name one adult at school you could connect a friend to." Collect the cards, and follow up privately with anything that worries you.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** focus on **Everyday stress** and one condition, and the four helping steps.
- **Support:** let students read silently first, then talk.
- **Stretch:** compare two conditions' "what it is not" sections. Why does that section matter?
- **Stretch:** design a poster for the school that corrects one myth, with its source.

## Short on time

- The continuum in **The map** (7 min).
- **Help a friend** with Jordan (8 min), then the exit card (4 min).
