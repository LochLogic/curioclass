# Energy Pyramid (idea card)

- Module: science-7-matter-and-energy-in-ecosystems, grade 7. Codes: MS-LS2-3
- Format / family: builder / ecosystem. Fit: good
- One idea: Build an energy pyramid for a grassland by feeding each level from the one below; only about a tenth of the energy moves up while the rest leaves as heat, yet an atom view shows matter looping back through decomposers, so energy flows through and matter cycles.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a grassland with the sun, grass, grasshoppers, mice, snakes and hawks, and an energy counter on the grass level.
  - Core loop: stack a level and decide how many animals it can feed; wavy heat arrows leave every level (moving, keeping warm, waste) and the counter drops. The share passed up varies between levels (roughly 5 to 20 percent, about a tenth on average). Try a fifth level and there is not enough energy to support it.
  - Matter view: switch to atoms. Droppings and dead bodies go to decomposers, into soil and air, and back into grass; the atoms count never drops, while energy must keep arriving from the sun.
  - The aha or the end state: the pyramid explains why hawks are few, and the two views side by side show the student that energy flows one way while matter goes around.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-LS2-3: the energy view and the atom view together model energy flow and matter cycling among the living and nonliving parts of an ecosystem.
- Not like: food-web-wobble (grade 5, no numbers); predator-prey-island (populations over time); leaf-factory (one organism's matter and energy).
- Art: generated scenes for the grassland and its animals; pyramid, arrows and counters drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Build a grassland energy pyramid: about a tenth of each level's energy feeds the next and the rest leaves as heat, while an atom view shows matter looping back through decomposers.
- Flags: none
