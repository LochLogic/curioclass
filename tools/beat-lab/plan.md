arc: model-then-make
minutes: 50
focus: MU:Cr2
goal: I can build a beat, add a bassline in a key, and arrange my loops into a short track with a clear build.
materials: The tool on the projector and on every student device. Headphones for every student (splitters let pairs share).
grouping: Whole class for the model, then students alone or in pairs on devices.
---
## Before class

- Test headphones and volume on one device. The tool saves itself on each device, so pairs should keep the same machine.
- Load the **House** demo on the projector and play it once to check the sound.
- Write the brief on the board: an 8-bar track, at least two patterns, one moment where the energy drops and comes back.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Play the **House** demo with **Pump** at zero, then turn **Pump** up. "What changed in the feel?" (The synths duck on every kick, so it breathes and bounces.)
2. "Every track you know is built from short loops that come and go. Today you build one."

## Model (12 min)

1. Load a fresh track. Press play: the starter is a kick on every beat. In the **Channel rack**, add hats with **Fill** (every eighth), then put the **Snare** on steps 5 and 13. "Why does the snare on 2 and 4 make people nod?" (It's the backbeat.)
2. Right-click a hat step to make a ghost note, then turn up **Swing**. Ask what it does to the groove.
3. Open the **Piano roll**, pick **Bass**, and click in a bassline that lands with the kick. Point out that scale lock keeps every row in the key.
4. Open **Sounds** and press **Use kit** on Lo-fi, then on Trap. "Same pattern, different kit. Did the genre change?"
5. Go to **Song**: copy the beat into pattern A and put the bass in B. Lay them out so the bass drops out for two bars and comes back. Play it in **SONG** mode.

## Make (22 min)

1. Students build their own 8-bar track to the brief. The **Producer missions** in the left panel are a guide for anyone stuck.
2. Check in at about 10 minutes: "Play me your groove. Where is your drop?"
3. At 18 minutes, everyone moves to the **Mixer**: balance the levels so no part buries another, and try the **Filter** sweep into the drop.
4. Finished tracks: **Export WAV** (in SONG mode) or **Save file** for the project.

## Share (7 min)

1. Three or four volunteers play their track on the projector.
2. Listeners name one choice that worked and where the energy changed: "the hats came in at bar 5", "the filter sweep before the drop".

## Exit check (4 min)

On a sticky note or in the class form: "Name one thing you changed to make a loop feel different, and why you changed it." Notes that only say "it sounded better" show who needs the vocabulary (backbeat, swing, layer, drop) again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** start from a **Demo tracks** loop and change one layer at a time instead of building from empty.
- **Support:** keep the **Snare** on 5 and 13 and the kit fixed, and focus only on the arrangement.
- **Stretch:** add a **Chords** part with **Chord stamp** and write a lead line with **Record** armed, playing the letter keys.
- **Stretch:** record a sample (a beatboxed snare or a word) and play it as a **Sampler** in the piano roll.

## Short on time

- Hook with the **Pump** demo (3 min), then model only the beat and the **Snare** backbeat (7 min).
- Students make one 4-bar loop with a kit swap (8 min), and two share (2 min).
