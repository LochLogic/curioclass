# Pause and Break (idea card)

- Module: ela-6-8-commas-dashes-and-ellipses, grades 6-8. Codes: L.6.2, L.7.2, L.8.2
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good for punctuation: the reading voice makes every mark audible, and the target reading is the goal state.
- One idea: A reading voice pauses exactly where the punctuation tells it to, and the student adds or moves commas, parentheses, dashes and ellipses until the sentence sounds the way the writer meant (an aside set off, two adjectives balanced, a thought that trails off, a quotation with words left out), so they learn that these marks are instructions to the reader's voice and mind.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a sentence on a strip of tape with a play button and a "how it should sound" card (a short recording of the intended reading, pauses shown as gaps on a waveform).
  - Core loop: the student plays the sentence as punctuated, compares the gaps with the target, and drags marks in or out. Grade 6 levels set off extra information with commas, parentheses or dashes and show how each makes the aside softer or louder. Grade 7 levels sort adjective pairs: a comma only where the adjectives could swap places ("a cold, dark night" but "a bright red kite"). Grade 8 levels use a dash for a sudden break, an ellipsis for a pause, and an ellipsis inside a quotation where words were cut, with a check that the cut didn't change the speaker's meaning.
  - The aha or the end state: a level where the same words with different marks say two different things aloud, and a final paragraph the student punctuates from scratch, then hears.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a "read it as written" partner game where one reads, one guesses the marks.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.6.2: commas, parentheses and dashes around nonrestrictive and parenthetical elements. The spelling part (L.6.2b) is not taught here.
  - L.7.2: commas between coordinate adjectives. The spelling part (L.7.2b) is not taught here.
  - L.8.2: dashes and ellipses for pauses and breaks, and ellipses for omissions in quotations. The spelling part (L.8.2c) is not taught here.
  - MOVE (spelling parts of L.6.2, L.7.2 and L.8.2): new tool: a grades 6-8 spelling lab where commonly misspelled words are solved through roots and word families (definite from finite, separate from par), with a personal tricky-words list. No 6-8 tool teaches spelling now; adding a spelling round to a voice-based tool would be thin, since misspellings make no sound.
- Not like: punctuation-power-ups (grade 10, semicolons and colons in a race); comma-chameleon (grade 5, commas that change a picture); parallel-parking (grade 9).
- Art: drawn in code for the tape strip and waveform; generated scenes behind each level's sentence (a dark night, a red kite) to keep it warm.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs voice production (target readings with exact pauses; synthesized voices must honor the marks, checked at spec)
