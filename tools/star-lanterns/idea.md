# Star Lanterns (idea card)

- Module: science-5-star-brightness, grade 5. Codes: 5-ESS1-1
- Format / family: lab / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Carry identical lanterns farther and farther across a dark field and measure how dim they look, then find that the sun looks brightest only because it is the closest star.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a dark field and two identical lanterns. "Carry one farther away. What happens?"
  - Core loop: move identical lanterns to different distances and measure how bright they look; then compare the sun with other stars by distance.
  - The aha or the end state: the sun looks brightest only because it is the closest star.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5-ESS1-1: argument that the apparent brightness of the sun and stars is due to their relative distances from Earth.
- Not like: shadow-clock (shadows and sky patterns).
- Art: generated scenes (a night field), because darkness and glow carry the idea.

- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: none
