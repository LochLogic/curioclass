arc: explore-first
minutes: 30
focus: NHES.S4; NHES.S8
goal: I can name a feeling from clues on a face and in a story, and choose kind words to help a friend.
materials: The tool on the big screen. A small mirror or two, if you have them.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet, then partners.
---
## Before class

- Open **Face clues**.
- Faces give clues, not answers. Be ready to ask, "What else could help you know?"

## Hook (4 min)

1. Make a surprised face without saying anything. "How do I feel? How did you know?"
2. "Our faces send clues. Today we become face detectives."

## Explore (10 min)

1. In **Face clues**, ask: "How does this friend feel? How can you tell?" Take ideas.
2. Press **Show the clues**. Labels point at the eyebrows, the eyes and the mouth.
3. Tap the feeling the class agrees on, and everyone makes the same face. A wrong tap explains what that feeling's face would look like.
4. Do four or five faces. Partners look at each other's faces (or in a mirror) when they copy.

## Make sense (6 min)

1. Open **Make a face**. Move the sliders for the eyebrows and the mouth. "What changed the feeling the most?"
2. "What else could help you know how a friend feels?" (What happened, how their body looks, asking them.)

## Apply (7 min)

1. Switch to **What happened?** Read a short story. "How might the friend feel?" More than one answer can fit.
2. Read the three choices for what to say or do. The class votes, then taps.
3. Talk about an answer like "Don't be sad." It isn't mean, but why doesn't it help a friend feel heard?

## Exit check (3 min)

Partners practice: one makes a sad face, the other says one kind thing. Then they switch. Listen for questions like "Are you okay? Do you want to play?"

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** start with four feelings: happy, sad, mad and scared.
- **Support:** use the clue labels every time.
- **Stretch:** "Have you ever felt two feelings at once?"
- **Stretch:** in **Make a face**, slide from one feeling to another and name the faces in between.

## Short on time

- Three faces in **Face clues** with **Show the clues** (5 min).
- One story in **What happened?** (4 min).
