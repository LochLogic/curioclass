# Skate Park Angles (idea card)

- Module: math-4-angles-and-line-plots, grade 4. Codes: 4.MD.B, 4.MD.C
- Format / family: builder / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: Build a skate park where every turn is a fraction of a full circle (a 180 is half of 360 degrees), measure ramp angles with an on-screen protractor, and log jump heights in eighths of a foot on a line plot.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an empty lot seen from above and from the side, a skater named Dev, and a toolbox: ramps, rails, a turn dial and a protractor.
  - Turns: the student sets turns on the dial; a 90 is a quarter turn, a 180 a half, a 360 a full turn, and one degree is 1/360 of a turn. Combo turns add (90 + 45 = 135); a missing-turn puzzle asks how much more Dev must spin to face the exit (180 - 125 = ?).
  - Ramps: drag the protractor onto a ramp to read its angle, or sketch a ramp at a given angle. Dev can only land ramps in a safe range; too steep and he bails (helmet on, laughing).
  - Jump log: each test run records a jump height to the nearest 1/8 ft. Dots stack on a line plot, and questions follow: how far apart are the highest and lowest jumps? How much do all the 3/4 ft jumps add up to?
  - The aha or the end state: angles are pieces of a turn, and they add like any other measure.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): share a park design as a code; a friend measures every angle in it.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 4.MD.B (4.MD.4): a line plot of jump heights in halves, quarters and eighths of a foot, with fraction addition and subtraction to answer questions from it.
  - 4.MD.C (4.MD.5, 6, 7): angles as turns made of 1/360 pieces of a circle; measuring and sketching ramp angles with a protractor; adding angles and finding missing turns, written as equations.
- Not like: mirror-studio (grade 4 naming lines and angle types, not measuring), ramp-and-roof (grade 10 trig ramps), shape-courtroom (grade 5 shape hierarchy).
- Art: generated scenes (the park and ramps); people (illustrated) for Dev and other skaters, varied in age and look; protractor and dial drawn in code for exact degrees.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Build a skate park where every turn is a fraction of 360 degrees: add combo turns, find missing ones, measure ramps with a protractor, and log jump heights in eighths of a foot on a line plot.
