# Shape Spy (idea card)

- Module: math-k-shapes-all-around, grade k. Codes: K.G.A
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: Spy circles, squares, triangles, rectangles, hexagons, cubes, cones, cylinders and spheres hidden in real places, find them tilted and tiny, and say where they are with position words.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a park scene and a spyglass. "Find a circle. Tap it."
  - Core loop: a voice asks for one shape; the child taps it and the outline glows with its name. Then a position question: "What is above the slide?" The child taps the round sun. Each scene (park, kitchen, street) hides flat and solid shapes.
  - Trick finds: a square turned on its corner, a tiny triangle on a sign. "Still a square. Turning it does not change it."
  - Flat or solid: the child files each find on a flat page (a drawn circle) or a solid shelf (a ball); solids can be spun.
  - The aha or the end state: a spy notebook fills with every shape found, flat and solid on separate pages.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): "I spy" mode where the child gives a position clue and a friend finds it.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - K.G.A: naming shapes in real scenes and telling their position (above, below, beside, in front of, behind, next to), naming shapes at any size or turn, and sorting them into flat (two-dimensional) and solid (three-dimensional).
- Not like: pattern-block-pond (builds and compares shapes), toy-box-sort (sorts toys, shape is one attribute), shape-family-tree (grade 3 quadrilateral categories).
- Art: generated scenes (park, kitchen, street with clear, findable shapes), because the shapes must sit in believable places.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Spy circles, squares, triangles, cubes and cones hiding in a park, kitchen and street; find them tilted and tiny, say where they are (above the slide), and sort flat from solid.
- Flags: none
