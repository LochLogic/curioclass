# Mirror Studio (idea card)

- Module: math-4-lines-angles-and-symmetry, grade 4. Codes: 4.G.A
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good, once the studio covers drawing and classifying, not only symmetry.
- One idea: Design quilt blocks with a drafting kit of points, segments, rays and lines; the mirror line finishes half a block, and every finished block gets a pattern label that names its parallel and perpendicular lines, its angle types and its lines of symmetry.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a quilting table in a community center, Auntie Mae's half-finished block, and a drafting kit with point, segment, ray and line tools. "Draw two parallel lines and one perpendicular to both."
  - Drafting: drawing tools snap and name what you make (segment AB, ray CD); parallel and perpendicular guides glow when lines meet the rule; corners are tagged right, acute or obtuse as you draw.
  - Mirror mode: draw half a block, place a mirror line, and the other half appears. Then hunt: how many lines of symmetry does this block have? Drag candidate lines and fold to test. Some blocks have none, some have four.
  - Sorting the scrap bin: cut pieces sorted by their features (has a right angle, has parallel sides, is a right triangle) on a pattern label, not by name alone.
  - Flag hunt: a set of real national and state flags; find each one's lines of symmetry, and spot the flags that look symmetric but are not.
  - The aha or the end state: lines and angles are how you describe and sort shapes, and a mirror line is one more line you can test.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print a class quilt from every student's block (assembled on the device, nothing uploaded).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 4.G.A: drawing and naming points, lines, segments, rays, right, acute and obtuse angles, and parallel and perpendicular lines (4.G.1); classifying pieces by parallel or perpendicular sides and angle types, and spotting right triangles (4.G.2); lines of symmetry, found and drawn (4.G.3).
- Not like: skate-park-angles (grade 4 measuring angles in degrees), shape-family-tree (grade 3 quadrilateral families), dance-floor-moves (grade 8 reflections as transformations).
- Art: people (illustrated) for Auntie Mae and the quilting circle; generated scene for the room; blocks and flags drawn in code so lines stay exact.
- Proposed title: Quilt Block Studio
