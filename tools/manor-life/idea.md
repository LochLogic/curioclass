# Manor Life (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-7-medieval-europe, grade 7. Codes: D2.His.1.6-8, D2.His.4.6-8
- Format / family: creature / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Look after one household on an English manor in the 1190s (a peasant family, the lord's household, or a monk at the nearby abbey) through the seasons; harvests, labor days, tithes, a crusade tax and the king's ransom shape their wellbeing, and the same events look very different from each household.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the manor from above (fields in strips, mill, church, manor house, abbey) and three household portraits to choose from.
  - Core loop: each season, make the household's choices (which strips to plant, whether to pay or bargain, whom to ask for help); its meters (food, health, standing, faith, coin) respond. Big events arrive for everyone: a wet harvest, a crusade tax, the tax to ransom King Richard.
  - The aha or the end state: replay one event as another household and compare: what each person owned, owed and believed shaped how they saw it; the end card links the manor to the wider world of crusades, kings and the Church.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): three students run the three households side by side.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.1.6-8: big events tie the manor to the Crusades, the Church and the crown.
  - D2.His.4.6-8: replaying the same event as different households shows the factors behind each perspective.
- Not like: panel-of-experts (the Black Death through four fields; this tool avoids the plague on purpose); hunter-to-farmer (the start of farming); mill-town (factory work, a story).
- Art: public domain manuscript scenes of farm life (to find and check at spec) plus generated scenes and illustrated households.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Look after one household on a 1190s English manor (a peasant family, the lord's or a monk's); seasons, labor days, tithes and a crusade tax shape each life, and the same events look different from each place.
- Flags: needs a data source (1190s taxes and manor duties, checked)
