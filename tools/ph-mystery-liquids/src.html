Model & safety notes

Virtual samples at 25 °C. The bulb uses total ion concentration: dark below 0.0005 mol/L, dim through 0.02, bright above.

Dilution means making 10 mL up to 100 mL with distilled water, then taking 10 mL of that as the new sample.

Buffer pH is held constant in this simplified model. Spill volume change is ignored and carbon dioxide escapes.

After adding soda, pH uses the acid left unreacted. Acetate buffering is omitted, so this is not a real vinegar titration curve.

Never mix bleach with acids or ammonia. Students report real spills to a teacher; this simulation is not a cleanup instruction.
