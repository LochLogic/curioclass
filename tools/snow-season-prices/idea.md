# Snow Season Prices (idea card)

- Module: cte-6-8-snow-season-prices, grade 6-8. Codes: D2.Eco.3.6-8, D2.Eco.4.6-8, D2.Eco.6.6-8
- Format / family: tycoon / market. Fit: good, because running a shop through a season makes prices and wages move.
- One idea: Run a ski-rental shop in a mountain town through one winter, setting prices and wages week by week while a big snow, a new rival shop and a hotel worker shortage shift what skiers will pay, what rivals charge and what workers ask, then explain each change on a season chart.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a ski town map (lift, hotels, two shops), your rental shop, a $3,000 bank loan offer for new skis and a week-1 price board.
  - Core loop: each week set a daily rental price and a wage for your two staff; skiers (buyers) arrive based on snow and price; rivals set theirs. Week 4 brings a huge snowfall (more skiers, higher prices hold); week 7 a new rental shop opens (you must match or lose customers); week 9 hotels raise pay and your staff ask for more.
  - Take the loan or not: buy more skis on credit at a set interest rate and see the payment each week.
  - Season chart: price, skiers, wages and profit by week, with each event marked; the student writes one line for each turn.
  - The aha or the end state: prices and wages move when buyers, sellers and competitors change, not because one shop wants them to.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Eco.3.6-8: play seller in the rental market, employer in the labor market and borrower in the credit market, and see the buyer's side each time.
  - D2.Eco.4.6-8: see a new rival push prices down and a hotel hiring push wages up.
  - D2.Eco.6.6-8: explain each price and quantity change from a supply or demand shift (snow, a new shop, fewer workers).
- Not like: market-day (grade 1 tokens and choices); the-buyout (grade 12 monopoly); concert-ticket-market (grade 12 supply and demand curves).
- Art: generated scenes (snowy town, shops, skiers) in a bright winter palette.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Run a ski-rental shop through one winter, setting prices and staff wages week by week, as a big snow, a new rival shop and a hotel worker shortage move what buyers pay and workers ask; explain each change.
- Flags: none
