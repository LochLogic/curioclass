# Verb Conjugation Race (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-6-12-present-tense-verbs, grade 6-12. Codes: ACTFL.Intermediate Low
- Format / family: race / spanish. Fit: good: speed with accuracy fits conjugation, as long as each answer means something.
- One idea: Race a rally car where your co-driver calls out sentences with the verb missing (Nosotros ___ (vivir) en la costa), and each correct form keeps you on the road; as you race, the ending chart and the "boot" of stem-changing verbs build themselves from your own answers.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a rally car at the start line, a ghost of your best run, and the co-driver's map. Stage 1: regular -ar verbs.
  - Core loop: the co-driver reads a short sentence aloud; the student types or taps the form (accent buttons on screen). Right answers keep speed; a wrong one slows the car and shows the ending that was needed. A missing accent counts as "almost" and shows why it matters (está vs esta). Stages add -er and -ir verbs, stem changers (e to ie, o to ue, e to i), yo-irregulars (tengo, hago, salgo) and fully irregular ser, ir, estar.
  - The aha or the end state: after each stage the student's answers light up a chart, and the stem changers form the "boot" shape (nosotros outside it), so the pattern is found, not memorized; the final stage mixes all verbs at full speed against the ghost.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a vosotros toggle for Spain-focused courses; a projector team race.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Intermediate Low: creating with language in simple present-tense sentences, using regular, stem-changing and irregular verbs accurately.
- Not like: detective-del-pasado (past tenses); consejero (subjunctive); el-restaurante (gustar in a dialogue).
- Art: drawn in code for the rally track and car (speed and clarity); illustrated co-driver (people, illustrated).
- Proposed title: keep

- Flags: needs a data source (recorded Spanish voice for the co-driver); none else.
