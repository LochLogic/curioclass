# Camp Cook Share-Out (idea card)

- Module: math-5-fractions-as-division, grade 5. Codes: 5.NF.B
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Share pans and scoops at camp: 3 pans for 4 tables gives each 3/4 of a pan, half a pan splits among 3 tables, and the cook counts quarter-cup scoops in 2 cups, so fractions are division and dividing by a unit fraction makes sense.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a camp kitchen with 3 pans of cornbread and 4 hungry tables. "How much does each table get?"
  - Core loop: share pans among tables to see that 3 pans for 4 tables is 3/4 of a pan each; split half a pan among 3 tables; count quarter-cup scoops in 2 cups; write each share as a division and a fraction.
  - The aha or the end state: a fraction is a division, and dividing by a unit fraction counts how many fit.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher sets pan and table counts.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5.NF.B (5.NF.3): a fraction as division of the numerator by the denominator.
  - 5.NF.B (5.NF.7): dividing unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions. 5.NF.4 to 5.NF.6 stay with shrink-ray.
- Not like: how-many-fit (grade 6 fraction division); fraction-kitchen (grade 5 unlike fractions).
- Art: generated scenes (camp kitchen), because the shares must be easy to see.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (how-many-fit; fraction-kitchen also cooks with fractions in grade 5, rule hit to judge)
- Origin: new tool from the 2026-10-27 overlap and gap audit (docs/agents/audit/gaps.md); codes from shrink-ray (5.NF.3, 5.NF.7).
