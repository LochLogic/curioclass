# Pool Repair Vote (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-3-pool-repair-vote, grade 3. Codes: D2.Civ.13.3-5, D2.Civ.10.3-5, D2.Eco.12.3-5
- Format / family: roleplay / none. Fit: good
- One idea: The town pool has a cracked floor and won't open this summer; follow the fix from problem to staff report to public hearing to council vote, hear a grandparent, a swim team parent, a shop owner and a teen lifeguard explain what they believe and why, and choose how to pay: taxes, entry fees or both.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a fenced-off pool with a "Closed for repairs" sign. "The town has a problem. Tap the sign to see what's wrong."
  - Core loop: a path board shows the policy steps (problem, staff report with the repair cost, options, hearing, vote). At the hearing, each speaker gives a view and the student identifies what underlies it (an experience, a value, a need: the grandparent swims for her knees; the shop owner fears higher taxes).
  - Paying for it: a slider splits the cost between property taxes, entry fees, or both, and shows who pays more under each (families who swim often vs everyone in town).
  - The aha or the end state: the council votes on the student's plan; a summer-later screen shows who uses the pool and who feels the cost.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): class plays the four speakers.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Civ.13.3-5: the path board follows a policy from public problem to adopted plan.
  - D2.Civ.10.3-5: each speaker's view is traced to their beliefs and experiences.
  - D2.Eco.12.3-5: taxes and fees show how government pays for a service.
- Not like: town-council (grade 2 one land decision); ripple-street (individual choices and incentives).
- Art: people (illustrated), because four real-seeming neighbors carry the perspectives.
