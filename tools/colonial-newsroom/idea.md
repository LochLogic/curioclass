# Colonial Newsroom (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-8-road-to-revolution, grade 8. Codes: D2.His.14.6-8, D2.His.4.6-8, D2.His.10.6-8, D2.His.6.6-8
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Edit two Boston papers, one Patriot and one Loyalist, for two editions (the Stamp Act in 1765 and the shooting on King Street in 1770): from the same pile of evidence, write each paper's headline and first lines, then lay them beside real pages and accounts and find what each side, and the surviving record, leaves out.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a print shop with two type cases labeled with each paper's name and an evidence tray.
  - Core loop: read evidence (the Stamp Act's text, merchants' letters, Revere's engraving, Captain Preston's account, trial testimony); pick and order facts for each paper and write a headline; a "who's paying for this paper?" card explains each editor's interests.
  - The aha or the end state: the student's two front pages beside real ones; a cause-and-effect strip from 1765 to 1770; and a "missing voices" check (Crispus Attucks left no account; enslaved Bostonians and women appear only through others' words).
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print both front pages side by side.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.14.6-8: the strip linking the Stamp Act, protests, troops and the shooting as causes and effects.
  - D2.His.4.6-8: the editors' interest cards explain the factors behind each paper's view.
  - D2.His.10.6-8: the missing-voices check on the surviving record.
  - D2.His.6.6-8: comparing how each side's view shaped what its pages recorded.
- Not like: headline-writer (ELA main idea); revolution-road (grade 5 timeline from three viewpoints); museum-label-writer (neutral explanation).
- Art: public domain (Revere's 1770 engraving and colonial newspaper pages, Library of Congress, to check at spec).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Edit a Patriot paper and a Loyalist paper in 1765 and 1770: headline the Stamp Act and the King Street shooting from the same evidence, then compare real pages and find what each side, and the record, leaves out.
- Flags: none
