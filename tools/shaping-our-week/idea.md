# Shaping Our Week (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-8-shaping-our-week, grade 8. Codes: D2.Civ.6.6-8, D2.Eco.9.6-8
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Follow one family through a week and tap each group that shapes it (the city council's bus routes, the company where a parent works, that parent's union, the food bank, the youth soccer league); remove any one and replay the week to see what changes for each person.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a week calendar and a cutaway of the family's neighborhood with groups glowing.
  - Core loop: step through the days; tap a glowing group to learn what kind it is (government, company, union, nonprofit, club), who runs it and what it does; then switch one off and replay the week (no late bus, no overtime pay rule, no Saturday pantry, no league).
  - The aha or the end state: a web the student completes showing which group touches which family member and how, and a sentence on how companies, unions and nonprofits each work in a market economy.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): map the groups in your own week.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Civ.6.6-8: tapping and switching off groups shows how political, civil and economic organizations shape lives.
  - D2.Eco.9.6-8: the company, the union and the food bank show their roles in a market economy.
- Not like: levers-of-power (acting on a problem); community-helpers-village (grade 1, helpers); motion-on-the-floor.
- Art: generated scenes of the week and people (illustrated) for the family.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: none (unions and companies shown evenly)
