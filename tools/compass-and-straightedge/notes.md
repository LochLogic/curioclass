## What it teaches
Students construct with circles and unmarked straight lines and inspect the relationships their constructions produce.
This is an open instrument, with no prescribed path. It is the second card for G-CO.D.12 and D.13.
G-CO.C proofs are taught in proof-tower; measurement here explores a construction without proving it.

## Key skills
- Locate and reuse intersections.
- Copy lengths with a collapsing compass.
- Bisect segments and angles, and draw perpendiculars and parallels.
- Inspect polygon lengths and angles without treating measurement as proof.
- Read, undo and revise a construction record.

## Support at home
Look for six-fold shapes in familiar objects and compare a radius with a hexagon side.
Fold scrap paper to find a segment midpoint and discuss what the fold preserves.
Explain a drawing aloud using only points, circles and straight lines.

## A five-minute start
Drag A to B and B to A. Choose Edge and join a crossing to A and B. Measure the triangle, or keep drawing.
The Book is an independent reference, never a sequence to finish.

## The instruments
Compass, straightedge, free point, erase, ink, measure. Keys are in the Manual.
Modern compass retains its opening; Euclid's rule closes it after each circle.
The Paper cabinet has starting sheets, four local saves, and print. Saves never leave the device.

## Talk about
What do the circles guarantee before any length is measured? Why does a radius walk round its circle six times?
Which claims need proof beyond four decimal places?

## Good to know
Double precision and tolerance are disclosed in the Manual. Near tangencies and extreme magnification can lose accuracy.
Book compound sub-constructions name earlier propositions. Ghost points cannot anchor the working instruments.
The Book figure shows each requested ghost stroke while its drawer is open, with the newest stroke darker.
Read describes the current instrument or empty sheet, then the last move and selected measurement.
Damaged save slots are checked before loading, so an unreadable slot leaves the live drawing intact.
Browser speech is the fallback for the unrecorded knowing-guide bank voice.
Blank paper uses u for the original reference unit, so later free points named A and B do not silently redefine it.
