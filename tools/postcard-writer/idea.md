# Postcard Writer (idea card)

- Module: ela-1-capitals-punctuation-and-spelling, grade 1. Codes: L.1.2
- Format / family: builder / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Write postcards from a class trip: capitalize names and dates, add end marks, put commas in dates and lists, and spell words by common patterns; the mail carrier delivers a card only when it is right, and the friend's reply shows what was read.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a postcard rack after a class trip to the aquarium. "Write to Grandma. The mail carrier only takes cards that are ready."
  - Core loop: build a short postcard from word tiles: capitalize the name and the date, add an end mark, put commas in the date and in a list (fish, crabs, and seals), and spell new words by common patterns; the carrier checks each card and the friend reads it back aloud, exactly as written.
  - The aha or the end state: the friend's reply card arrives, and the child hears their own card read correctly.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher picks the trip and the spelling patterns.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.1.2: capitals for dates and names, end marks, commas in dates and series, spelling with common patterns and phonetic spelling of new words.
- Not like: word-zoo (grade 1 grammar); dialogue-director (grade 3 punctuation in dialogue).
- Art: generated scenes (postcards, a friendly carrier), because the cards must look like real mail.
