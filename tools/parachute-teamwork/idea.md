# Parachute Teamwork (idea card)

- Module: health-pe-k-2-cooperative-games, grade k-2. Codes: SHAPE.S3, SHAPE.S4
- Format / family: showdown / none. Fit: good, with a solo default: one child plays with an animated circle of kids and moves along at home; the real parachute is class mode.
- One idea: Hold one edge of an animated rainbow parachute with eight cartoon kids and play the classic games (waves, the mushroom, popcorn, trading places); the game only works when everyone follows the call and takes turns, and a heart check before and after shows that moving made your heart beat faster.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a gym floor with a rainbow parachute and one empty spot in the circle. "Stand up. You hold this edge."
  - Core loop: the leader calls a game; the child copies the arm move (small waves, big waves, lift high for the mushroom) while the animated kids move with the call. When one cartoon kid pulls early, the mushroom collapses; the child picks the fix (wait for "three", say it together).
  - Turn-taking: in trading places, kids under the parachute go one color at a time; the child cheers for others ("Go, Sam!") and the cartoon kids cheer back on the child's turn.
  - Heart check: hand on chest before and after, then tap slow, medium or fast.
  - The aha or the end state: the parachute only flies when the group moves together, and moving feels good in the body.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): with a real parachute, the board calls the games, counts down each lift and shows whose turn it is.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - SHAPE.S3: follow the leader's calls, take turns and cheer for others so the group game works.
  - SHAPE.S4: notice the faster heartbeat and good feeling after moving, and say why moving is good for you.
- Not like: animal-moves (copy animal movements); target-toss (throwing experiment); friend-moves-practice (friend words at play).
- Art: generated scenes (bright gym, rainbow parachute, varied kids including one in a wheelchair holding the edge).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Hold an edge of a rainbow parachute with a circle of kids and play waves, the mushroom and trading places; the parachute flies only when everyone follows the call and takes turns, and a heart check shows how moving feels.
- Flags: none (class mode needs a parachute; solo needs none)
