# Equal Groups Zoo (idea card)

- Module: math-3-meaning-of-multiplication-and-division, grade 3. Codes: 3.OA.A, 3.OA.B
- Format / family: sandbox / arrays. Fit: good.
- One idea: Feed zoo animals in equal groups, and the same feeding shows up at once as a picture, an array, a skip-count and an equation; division is the same job backward (share 24 fish among 6 penguins, or how many penguins get 4 each).
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a zoo map with a keeper, Ms. Ama, and a food cart. "5 otters each get 3 fish. Drag the fish."
  - Core loop: the child drags food into equal groups; four linked panels update together (groups, array, skip-count 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 5 x 3 = 15). Division jobs run both ways: sharing (24 fish, 6 penguins, how many each?) and grouping (24 fish, 4 per penguin, how many penguins?), each written as 24 / 6 = ? and 6 x ? = 24.
  - Array tricks: rotate an array (5 x 3 becomes 3 x 5); split a 7 x 8 array into 5 x 8 and 2 x 8 with a slider; group three factors in different orders.
  - The aha or the end state: dividing is finding a missing factor; the child sees 6 x ? = 24 and 24 / 6 on the same picture.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): free zoo where the child writes their own feeding story.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 3.OA.A: products as equal groups (3.OA.1), quotients as sharing and as grouping (3.OA.2), feeding word problems with groups and arrays (3.OA.3), and unknowns in any position (3.OA.4).
  - 3.OA.B: rotating arrays (commutative), grouping three factors (associative), splitting arrays (distributive) (3.OA.5), and division as an unknown factor (3.OA.6).
- Not like: multiplication-dungeon (fluency), garden-grid (grade 2 rows and columns as geometry), prime-garden (grade 4 factor pairs).
- Art: generated scenes (a lively zoo, animals with personality); arrays drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Feed zoo animals in equal groups; the same feeding shows as a picture, an array, a skip-count and an equation. Share food to divide, flip and split arrays, and see division as a missing factor.
- Flags: none
