# Life Cycle Wheel (idea card)

- Module: science-3-life-cycles, grade 3. Codes: 3-LS1-1
- Format / family: sort / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Build life cycle wheels for a frog, a butterfly, a bean plant, a chicken and a human by sorting stage cards into order, then lay the wheels side by side to see that every cycle is different but all share birth, growth, reproduction and death.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an empty wheel and a shuffled pile of real photos for the frog (eggs, tadpole, tadpole with legs, froglet, adult). "Put the stages in order around the wheel."
  - Core loop: drag cards into the wheel and spin it to check; wrong spots wobble with a hint ("Legs come before the tail is gone"). Each organism adds a twist: the butterfly's pupa, the bean's seed and flower, the chicken's chick hatching with no tadpole stage, a person growing with no big change in body shape.
  - Compare step: the wheels stack on a board, and the student tags each stage as birth, growth, reproduction or death; the matching tags line up across all five.
  - The aha or the end state: the student builds a wheel for a new organism (a salmon, a sunflower or a sea turtle) from cards, showing the model works for any life.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 3-LS1-1: building and comparing life cycle models that are different in their stages but share birth, growth, reproduction and death.
- Not like: who-s-my-baby (grade 1 parents and young); puppy-traits (inherited traits).
- Art: generated scenes or real photo-style art for each stage, because the stages (a pupa, a tadpole with legs) are the content. Death is shown plainly and gently (a leaf falling, an old frog), with no gore.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Sort stage cards into life cycle wheels for a frog, a butterfly, a bean, a chicken and a human, then line them up to see every cycle differs but all share birth, growth, reproduction and death.
- Flags: none
