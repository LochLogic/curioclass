# Intersection Angles (idea card)

- Module: math-7-angle-relationships, grade 7. Codes: 7.G.B
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Find missing angles at a busy intersection where crossing streets, rail crossings and scissor gates make vertical, adjacent, supplementary and complementary angles, writing and solving an equation for each.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a busy intersection from above with one angle labeled 62 degrees. "The crossing guard needs the angle across the street."
  - Core loop: find missing angles where streets, a rail crossing and scissor gates cross: vertical, adjacent, supplementary and complementary angles, writing and solving an equation for each.
  - The aha or the end state: a whole intersection labeled from one given angle.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 7.G.B (7.G.5): facts about supplementary, complementary, vertical and adjacent angles in multi-step problems with equations.
