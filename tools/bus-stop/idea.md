# Bus Stop (idea card)

- Module: math-k-putting-together-and-taking-apart, grade k. Codes: K.OA.A
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good, if the branches are real: the child picks the route (park, library, farm), and each route brings different riders and problems.
- One idea: Drive the school bus: at each stop riders get on or off, you show it with counters, and you pick the number sentence that tells the story.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the bus at school with driver Ms. Rosa. "You drive today. Pick a road." Three road signs with pictures.
  - Core loop: at a stop, riders hop on or off (within 10). Ms. Rosa asks "How many now?" The child drags counters or taps a finger strip, then picks a number sentence (3 + 2 = 5 or 5 - 2 = 3). The bus leaves only when the sentence matches the stop.
  - Seat stops: 7 riders sit in the front and back rows; the child tries every way (7 and 0, 6 and 1, ...) and the tool keeps the list. Ten stops: the bus holds 10; "6 riders: how many empty seats?"
  - Express stops: quick plus and minus within 5, said out loud.
  - The aha or the end state: the seat list shows every pair that makes 7, and the child sees that 3 and 4 is the same as 4 and 3.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the class tells each stop's story out loud before the riders move.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - K.OA.A: add and take away within 10 with counters and fingers (A.1), solve riders-on and riders-off story problems (A.2), seat riders every way (decompose within 10, A.3), count empty seats to make 10 (A.4), and express stops for fluency within 5 (A.5).
- Not like: story-problem-theater (grade 1, within 20, all problem types), sheep-count (counting, no operations), make-ten-machine (grade 1 strategies).
- Art: people (illustrated): a varied busload of kids and the driver, since the riders are the counters.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Drive the school bus: riders get on and off at each stop, and you show it with counters and a number sentence. Seat 7 riders every possible way, and count the empty seats to 10.
- Flags: none
