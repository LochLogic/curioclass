Bus Stop · Manual

Using the bus

Choose a road. The story is in the dashboard. Tap the cup for new riders; tap a seat for riders getting off. Done opens number sentences to explore. A sentence acts out or counts its story. Drive on lets you move along whenever you like.

Split moves counters between two places. Keep it writes a grouping into the list. More and Fewer change the group. Express has small rider stories: say a number, tap it, or use Show me. Fill up changes the starting seats from one to nine.

Read repeats the current story and action. One quiet hint can speak on first use; after that narration waits for Read. Motion removes movement while keeping the same timing. Sound controls tones. Keyboard: Tab, Enter or Space; Express also uses 0 to 5.

Reading the counters

Yellow discs were aboard. Blue discs with a white ring and plus mark just got on. Dashed rings mark seats left behind. The chart has two rows of five seats; its driver mark is at the right, like the bus's front. Split equations and dot pictures show the same group in two places.

Things people have found

Moving a counter between places leaves the whole group unchanged. Swapping both groups gives a turn-around pair. The empty seats and occupied seats together make ten. A number sentence can be true and describe a different bus story.
