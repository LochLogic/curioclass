# ASL Number Bingo (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-k-12-numbers-colors-and-time, grade k-12. Codes: ACTFL.Novice Mid
- Format / family: cards / asl. Fit: good: bingo is a natural receptive drill, and computer players make it work for one student.
- One idea: An illustrated Deaf caller signs numbers, colors, days and times in Sign Strip panels while you play bingo against three illustrated players, so you must read each sign as it plays at Natural speed to mark your card, and the rounds teach the patterns that make ASL numbers different from English counting.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a community center bingo night. The caller (the illustrated signer in the Sign Strip player) waves hello. Your card and three rival cards sit on the table. "Read the sign, mark the square."
  - Core loop: themed rounds. Numbers 1 to 10, then 11 to 30, then to 100; colors; days of the week; clock times and "how many hours / days". The caller signs (tweened playback), the student marks; a miss replays the sign as stepped start, movement and end panels with the handshape inset lit and the palm turn or wrist point marked by its arrow. Rival players mark their cards too, so speed matters a little but accuracy matters most (a wrong daub costs a turn). After each round, a short "your turn" step: the student copies three of the signs from the panels, with an optional camera mirror beside them (camera view, never recorded; the screen says so).
  - The aha or the end state: the pattern cards earned along the way, each a pair of panels: numbers that turn the palm, times that point at the wrist, days that share a movement. The final round mixes everything at Natural speed, and the student wins by reading, not guessing.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): printable cards for a real class game with the projector as caller; Slow / Natural speed; Left-handed mirrors the caller.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Novice Mid: recognizing and producing lists of numbers, colors, days and times in memorized signs and short phrases (AGE 7, 3 O'CLOCK, MONDAY).
- Not like: fingerspell-flash (letters, not numbers); calendario-vivo (Spanish calendar and weather, spoken); asl-hello-booth (conversation openers).
- Art: graphics only, per docs/agents/ASL-GRAPHICS.md: the caller is the shared illustrated signer (rendered body and face plates, people review) with vector Sign Strip hands, arrows and insets; illustrated bingo hall and rival players (people, illustrated). No video, no photos of people.
- Proposed title: ASL Bingo Night (the module covers colors, days and time, not only numbers)
- Proposed concept line: Play bingo against three rivals while an illustrated Deaf caller signs numbers, colors, days and times; read each sign as it plays to mark your card, then copy the signs and collect the patterns behind them.
- Flags: sign entries for every number, color, day and time checked by the fresh blind review (ASL-GRAPHICS.md), number-sign rules on the pattern cards too; camera mirror shows the on-device line (rule 7); the on-screen practice note (one common form; practice with a fluent Deaf signer or your ASL teacher when you can); K-12 span check (gf-04).
