# ASL Number Bingo (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-k-12-numbers-colors-and-time, grade k-12. Codes: ACTFL.Novice Mid
- Format / family: cards / asl. Fit: good: bingo is a natural receptive drill, and computer players make it work for one student.
- One idea: A Deaf caller signs numbers, colors, days and times on video while you play bingo against three illustrated players, so you must read each sign at real speed to mark your card, and the rounds teach the patterns that make ASL numbers different from English counting.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a community center bingo night. The caller (video) waves hello. Your card and three rival cards sit on the table. "Read the sign, mark the square."
  - Core loop: themed rounds. Numbers 1 to 10, then 11 to 30, then to 100; colors; days of the week; clock times and "how many hours / days". The caller signs, the student marks; a miss shows the clip again slowed with the handshape highlighted. Rival players mark their cards too, so speed matters a little but accuracy matters most (a wrong daub costs a turn). After each round, a short "your turn" step: the student copies three of the signs in the mirror (camera view, never recorded) against the model.
  - The aha or the end state: the pattern cards earned along the way: numbers that turn the palm, times that point at the wrist, days that share a movement. The final round mixes everything at natural speed, and the student wins by reading, not guessing.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): printable cards for a real class game with the projector as caller; a slow / natural speed switch.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Novice Mid: recognizing and producing lists of numbers, colors, days and times in memorized signs and short phrases (AGE 7, 3 O'CLOCK, MONDAY).
- Not like: fingerspell-flash (letters, not numbers); calendario-vivo (Spanish calendar and weather, spoken); asl-hello-booth (conversation openers).
- Art: real signing video by a fluent Deaf caller (required); illustrated bingo hall and rival players (people, illustrated).
- Proposed title: ASL Bingo Night (the module covers colors, days and time, not only numbers)
- Proposed concept line: Play bingo against three rivals while a Deaf caller signs numbers, colors, days and times on video; read each sign at real speed to mark your card, then copy the signs and collect the patterns behind them.
- Flags: needs a data source (signing video by fluent Deaf signers; number-sign rules on the pattern cards checked by the fresh review); camera mirror shows the on-device line (rule 7); K-12 span check (gf-04).
