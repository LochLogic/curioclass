# Fraction Kitchen (idea card)

- Module: math-5-adding-unlike-fractions, grade 5. Codes: 5.NF.A
- Format / family: puzzle / fraction-food. Fit: good.
- One idea: Hit exact amounts in a test kitchen by pouring from cups of different sizes (1/2, 1/3, 1/4 cup) into a bowl marked in shared pieces, so adding 1/2 and 1/3 means renaming both as sixths, and every pour is estimated first.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a bakery test kitchen, a recipe card that asks for 5/6 cup of yogurt, and a shelf with only a 1/2 cup and a 1/3 cup. Chef Amara: "Guess first. Will it overflow a 1-cup bowl?"
  - Core loop: the student estimates with benchmarks (more or less than 1/2? than 1?), then pours. The bowl's marks are blank until they choose a shared piece size; picking sixths makes 1/2 = 3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6 visible, and the sum lands at 5/6.
  - Harder levels: three cups at once (1/2 + 1/3 + 1/4 in twelfths); mixed numbers (1 1/2 cups of flour and 2/3 cup more); subtraction (a 2 1/4-cup bowl, take out 5/6 cup).
  - Fix-the-card rounds: a helper's note says 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7. The student shows with a benchmark that it cannot be right (1/2 plus anything is more than 1/2, and 3/7 is less), then pours to find 9/10.
  - The aha or the end state: unlike pieces add once they are cut into the same size, and a quick estimate catches wrong answers.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a challenge list where the class finds the fewest pours for a target.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5.NF.A: adding and subtracting unlike fractions and mixed numbers by renaming with a common denominator (5.NF.1); word problems with benchmark estimates to judge whether an answer is reasonable, such as rejecting 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7 (5.NF.2).
- Not like: recipe-scaler (grade 4, single-size scoops and like denominators), fraction-wall-duel (grade 4 comparing), how-many-fit (grade 6 dividing fractions).
- Art: generated scenes (the test kitchen, ingredients); people (illustrated) for Chef Amara; cups, bowl marks and pours drawn in code for exact levels.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Hit exact amounts by pouring from different-size cups into a bowl you mark in shared pieces, so 1/2 + 1/3 becomes sixths; estimate every pour first and fix a helper's 2/5 + 1/2 = 3/7.
- Flags: overlap risk (recipe-scaler, same family: keep this one to unlike denominators)
