# Rhyme Time Rumble (idea card)

- Module: ela-k-rhymes-and-sounds, grade k. Codes: RF.K.2
- Format / family: showdown / none. Fit: good, with a solo default: the child answers with big Clap and Stomp buttons (or the space bar), and the whole-class clap-and-stomp is the projector extra.
- One idea: A rumble of listen-only sound rounds moves from rhyme to syllables to onset and rime to single sounds, so the child hears that spoken words come apart into smaller and smaller pieces and go back together.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: two silly pictures (a cat, a hat) and the prompt "Clap if they rhyme, stomp if they don't."
  - Core loop: rounds unlock in order: rhyme or not; pick the picture that rhymes (producing a rhyme); clap the beats in a word while a drum counts along (syllables); glue c + at into a word (onset and rime); where is the /m/ in mop (first, middle or last sound); swap one sound (cat becomes hat, hat becomes hot).
  - The aha or the end state: a sound-swap chain (cat, hat, hot, hop) turns one picture into another, and the child sees it all happened by changing one sound at a time.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): class clap-and-stomp vote on the projector; teacher picks which round to play.
- Standards, one line per code: RF.K.2: rhyme recognition and production; counting, blending and segmenting syllables; blending onset and rime; isolating first, middle and last sounds in CVC words; adding or swapping one sound to make a new word. All rounds are oral, with no letters on screen.
- Not like: Letter Lanterns (letter to sound, with print). Blend Bakery (grade 1 decoding with letters). Poem Drum (rhythm and rhyme inside whole poems).
- Art: generated scenes (simple, funny picture cards, about 120), because each picture word must read instantly to a five-year-old.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Listen-only sound rounds with silly pictures: clap if two words rhyme, clap the beats, glue c + at, find the /m/ in mop, then swap one sound to turn a cat into a hat.
- Flags: needs a data source (a checked picture-word bank of CVC words with clean recorded audio)
