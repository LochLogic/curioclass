## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Zoo**. The four instrument families each live in their own home with a keeper: giraffe (strings), owl (woodwinds), elephant (brass) and gorilla (percussion).
2. Tap a home, then tap an instrument. It plays a short tune and the keeper says one thing about it.
3. Press **High to low**. The family plays from smallest to biggest. Ask: did the notes get higher or lower?
4. Switch to **Who's playing?** and press **Listen**. The class guesses with the picture hidden.

## The families

| Family | How it makes sound | In the zoo |
|---|---|---|
| Strings | strings shake when bowed or plucked | violin, cello, double bass, harp |
| Woodwinds | air blown across a hole or through a buzzing reed | flute, oboe, clarinet, saxophone, bassoon |
| Brass | lips buzz into a metal mouthpiece | trumpet, trombone, French horn, tuba |
| Percussion | hit, shaken or scraped | triangle, gong, cymbals, timpani, snare drum, bass drum, xylophone, tambourine, piano |

The saxophone is metal but a woodwind (it uses a reed), and the piano sits with percussion because hammers hit its strings. Both make good "surprise" conversations.

## Who's playing?

Every instrument plays the same tune (Twinkle, Twinkle), so students listen to the sound of the instrument, not the song. Three levels:

- **Which family?** four keepers to choose from.
- **Which instrument?** four instruments from different families.
- **Same family**: the hardest. Is it the trumpet or the tuba? Listen for high and low.

A wrong answer can be tried again; the score counts first tries.

## Zoo band

Pick a **Melody**, a **Bass** and a **Beat** instrument and press **Play the band**. The three parts play Twinkle, Twinkle together. Mute a part while it plays to hear what it adds. Try a flute melody with a tuba bass, then a violin with a cello.

## Talk about it

- Which family uses air? Which uses lips? Which do you hit?
- Why do you think the biggest instruments play the lowest notes?
- Which instrument would you choose to sound like a bird? An elephant? Rain?

## Good to know

- The recordings are real instruments from the University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples, free for any use. Single notes are re-pitched in the browser to play the tunes. The harp, timpani, snare drum, bass drum and triangle are synthesized.
- Escape stops any sound. Space plays and stops the band.
- Turning Motion off stops the bouncing and floating notes; the music is unchanged.

## Voice

- A narrator (the playful-friend voice from the bank) says a welcome for each mode, each family's intro, each instrument's fact, the guessing question and the right-answer lines, once the class taps or presses a key. Nothing talks before the first tap.
- The narrator waits for the music: it never talks over an instrument, the band or Play all, and a tune starting cuts the line short.
- **🔊 Read** stops the music and says the instructions on screen. **Sound** off silences the narrator at once.
