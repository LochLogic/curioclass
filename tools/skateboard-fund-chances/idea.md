# Skateboard Fund Chances (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-4-bike-fund-chances, grade 4. Codes: NSPFE.Investing, NSPFE.Managing Risk
- Format / family: cards / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Save toward a skateboard for a year with a chance card each month: keep money in a jar or buy shares in the class juice company that may grow or shrink; once the board is yours, choose to lock it, insure it or take the risk as rain, a cracked wheel and a theft come up.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a jar, a juice company stock certificate, and a skateboard in a shop window at $60. "Each month you get $8. Where does it go?"
  - Core loop: each month the student splits money between the jar (safe, no growth) and juice company shares (can grow or shrink). A chance card reveals the month (a hot summer sells lots of juice; a lemon shortage hurts). A small chart tracks both.
  - Protect it: once the board is bought, risk cards come (rain, a cracked wheel, a theft at the park). Before each, the student can pay for a lock, a small "insurance" fee, or nothing, and sees the result. Avoid, reduce, share or keep the risk are named plainly.
  - The aha or the end state: the year-end card compares the jar-only path, the all-shares path and the student's mix, and the protected vs unprotected board, so the student sees that higher possible reward comes with risk and that protection has a cost too.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): replay with a different deck.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - NSPFE.Investing: jar vs juice shares shows saving vs investing, and that investments can rise or fall (grade 4 outcomes matched at spec).
  - NSPFE.Managing Risk: lock, insure or risk choices show ways to protect what you have and what each costs (grade 4 outcomes matched at spec).
- Not like: piggy-bank-planner (grade 2 saving); interest-seesaw (grade 5 interest); covered-insurance-claims (grade 9-12 insurance).
- Art: generated scenes (skate park, juice stand), because the chance moments need quick, fun visuals.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: module title says "Bike Fund Chances" while the tool is a skateboard fund; align the module name (rename to "Skateboard Fund Chances") or the tool, so the catalog matches
