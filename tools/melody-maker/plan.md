arc: explore-first
minutes: 45
focus: MU:Cr1; MU:Pr4
goal: I can read and play melodies with solfege, describe steps, skips and leaps, and compose a short tune that ends on the home note.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices with headphones. Classroom bells, if you have them.
grouping: Whole class, then pairs on devices.
---
## Before class

- Open **Compose**. Frère Jacques is already on the grid.
- The bell colors match the common classroom set (do red, re orange, mi yellow...). This is movable do.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Sing sol-mi ("Hel-lo!") and have the class echo it.
2. Press **Play** on the tune in **Compose**. "You know this one. But how would you write it down?"

## Explore (15 min)

1. Watch each syllable pop up and the notes light up on the staff. Read the **Melody shape** card: how many steps, skips and leaps?
2. Pairs tap cells to change notes. Every row is in the scale, so every note sounds good. An empty column is a rest.
3. Pairs change the **Key** and listen. "What stayed the same?" (The shape and the syllables.)
4. Pairs open **Scale ladder**. "Where are the rungs close together?" (Mi-fa and ti-do: the half steps.)

## Make sense (8 min)

1. On the piano in **Scale ladder**, find the pattern: whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half.
2. "What would happen if every step were the same size?"
3. Switch to **Finish the tune**. Try each ending. "Which sounds more finished: ending on do or on ti?"

## Apply (12 min)

1. Pairs press **Clear** (or start over) and write their own 16-beat tune that asks a question halfway (ending on sol or re) and answers at the end (on do).
2. Pairs who want an idea press **Surprise me** and change it.
3. Two or three pairs play their tunes for the class. The class names one step, one skip and one leap.

## Exit check (5 min)

Switch to **Echo it** on the projector. The robin sings a short pattern; the class sings it back in solfege, then a student plays it on the bells. Then ask: "Why does an answer phrase end on do?"

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** use the pentatonic scale (no fa or ti) so it's hard to sound off.
- **Support:** start **Echo it** at sol-mi.
- **Stretch:** change your tune to minor, where la is home. "How does the mood change?"
- **Stretch:** start the ladder on G, D or F and explain why the key needs a sharp or a flat.

## Short on time

- **Play** the tune and change a few notes in **Compose** (8 min).
- One round of **Finish the tune** (5 min), then **Echo it** (4 min).
