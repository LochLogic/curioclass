# Fridge Goal Chart (idea card)

- Module: health-pe-k-2-health-goals-at-home, grade k-2. Codes: NHES.S2, NHES.S3, NHES.S6
- Format / family: builder / none. Fit: good, because the student builds a goal chart, then tests it on a pretend week before using it for real.
- One idea: Pick one health goal (more water, an earlier bedtime, a fruit at snack, an hour of play outside), build a fridge chart with what helps and who can help, test it on a pretend week where a TV ad and a busy night get in the way, then use it for a real week and add a star each day.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a kitchen fridge with magnets and a blank chart. "Pick one goal for this week."
  - Build: choose the goal picture; drag helper magnets onto the chart (a water bottle by the door, a bedtime song) and pick who can help (a grown-up at home, the school nurse, a big sister).
  - Test week: seven pretend days play out; on Tuesday a cartoon ad says a sugary drink makes you strong, on Thursday soccer runs late. A chart with a matching helper keeps its star; one without misses that day, and the student adds a fix.
  - Real week: the student taps a star each day. A line on screen says the stars stay on this device and never leave it.
  - The aha or the end state: goals hold when you plan for what gets in the way and you know who can help.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - NHES.S2: spot what pulls on the goal (an ad, a busy night, a friend's choice) and say if it helps or gets in the way.
  - NHES.S3: name trusted adults and helpers (family, the school nurse, a doctor) who can help with a health goal.
  - NHES.S6: choose one goal, plan helpers, track it for a week and look back at the stars.
- Not like: habit-hero (caring for a hero's daily habits); lunch-tray-builder (food groups on a tray); game-day-fuel (grade 9-12 meal planning).
- Art: generated scenes (a warm kitchen, magnets, a family), varied homes and family shapes.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: stores a week of stars on the device (rule 7 line)
