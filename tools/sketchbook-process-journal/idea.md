# Sketchbook Process Journal (idea card)

- Module: arts-9-12-artistic-investigation-and-tradition, grade 9-12. Codes: VA:Cr1, VA:Cn10, VA:Cn11
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Start an artwork from a present-day issue you choose and an older tradition that once answered a similar question (Dutch still lifes about having too much, botanical plates about naming what grows, woodblock prints of city crowds, 1930s public posters), and keep a sketchbook journal whose idea map grows a branch every time research, culture or history changes your plan, so your own voice shows where you broke from the tradition.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an open sketchbook, an issue picker (or write your own) and a shelf of traditions, each with three public domain works and who made them for whom.
  - Core loop: each page is a step: a question, a thumbnail (drawn in the book), a research card from the tradition, a decision. A "what changed and why" line is required on each page. Look-closer tools (zoom, compare, notes from the tradition's history) feed the research cards.
  - The aha or the end state: the idea map: the first thumbnail at the root, branches labeled by what changed them, and the final plan at the tip. The student marks where they took the tradition and where they left it, and writes a short artist's statement from the map.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a teacher can see an exported journal as a PDF.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - VA:Cr1: shaping an artistic investigation of present-day life and generating plans from it.
  - VA:Cn10: documenting the idea's growth from first thumbnail to full plan.
  - VA:Cn11: how knowledge of the tradition's culture and history changed the student's response.
- Not like: art-through-time (grade 6-8 timeline of movements); virtual-gallery (grade 9-12 showing finished work); gallery-critique (grade 9-12 critique).
- Art: public domain (Met and AIC open access works for each tradition, object ids checked at spec); the student's own thumbnails.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Start from a present-day issue and an older art tradition, log each step from thumbnail to plan in a sketchbook, and watch an idea map show where research and history changed your idea and where your own voice broke from the tradition.
- Flags: sensitive (each tradition's culture and history: a fresh review agent checks them, rule 5); privacy (the journal stays on the device, stated on screen)
