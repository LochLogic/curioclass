# Sign Shop (idea card)

- Module: math-6-area-of-polygons, grade 6. Codes: 6.G.A
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Price the paint for odd-shaped signs by cutting and sliding pieces: a parallelogram becomes a rectangle, a trapezoid becomes two triangles and a rectangle, and composite signs split into shapes you know.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a sign shop order: a trapezoid sign for a bakery. "Paint costs by the square foot. How much paint?"
  - Core loop: cut and slide pieces to find areas: a parallelogram becomes a rectangle, a trapezoid becomes two triangles and a rectangle, a composite sign splits into known shapes; price the paint.
  - The aha or the end state: the student derives each area formula from a cut and slide they made.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): design your own sign.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 6.G.A (6.G.1): area of triangles, special quadrilaterals and polygons by composing and decomposing. 6.G.2 to 6.G.4 stay with net-folder.
- Not like: net-folder (nets and volume); tile-the-floor (grade 3 area).
- Art: drawn in code (exact shapes), because the cuts must be precise.

- Proposed concept line: keep
