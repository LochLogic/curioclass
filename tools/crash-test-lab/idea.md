# Crash Test Lab (idea card)

- Module: science-8-forces-and-motion, grade 8. Codes: MS-PS2-1, MS-PS2-2
- Format / family: lab / forces. Fit: good
- One idea: Push and crash carts of different masses on a track with force meters on both bumpers, plan fair tests of force and mass, and use the matched force readings to design a bumper that keeps an egg passenger safe.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a long track, two carts, a stack of bricks, fan units and an egg in a tiny seat.
  - Push bench: the student plans a test (what to change, what to keep the same, what to measure), then adds fans (more push), bricks (more mass) or a felt strip (friction) and measures the change in speed. A results table builds up, and the student states how the change in motion depends on the total force and the mass.
  - Crash bench: two carts collide head-on or one catches the other. Both force meters always show the same peak in opposite directions, even a heavy cart against a light one; the light cart's motion changes more. Then the design task: pick bumper materials (spring, foam, clay, a crumple box) within a weight and length limit and test until the egg survives the standard crash.
  - The aha or the end state: forces come in equal and opposite pairs; what differs is how much each cart's motion changes, and a bumper works by spreading the push over more time.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-PS2-1: the bumper design applies Newton's third law to a two-object collision.
  - MS-PS2-2: the push bench has the student plan the investigation that links change in motion to the sum of forces and the mass.
- Not like: collision-lab (grade 11, momentum and impulse in numbers); tug-of-war-arena (grade 3 balanced forces); curling-stones (grade 11 F = ma with friction).
- Art: track, meters and graphs drawn in code; generated scenes for the test hall and the egg passenger.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Collide carts of different masses with force meters on both bumpers, plan push tests that change force or mass, then design a bumper that keeps an egg passenger safe.
- Flags: overlap risk (collision-lab; keep this one to force pairs and fair tests, no momentum math)
