# Compass Rose Quest (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-1-maps-and-globes, grade 1. Codes: D2.Geo.1.K-2, D2.Geo.3.K-2
- Format / family: puzzle / me-map. Fit: good, if the last levels have the student make a map, not only follow one.
- One idea: Follow north, south, east and west across a park map, reading its key to tell the pond from the playground, then draw your own treasure map of your bedroom with a key and see whether a pirate parrot can find the treasure from it.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a park map with a compass rose and a key; Pip the parrot says "The treasure is two steps north of the pond. Tap the pond."
  - Core loop: levels of clues ("go east to the place people built for play") move a token across the map; each clue uses a key symbol, so the student must tell natural features (pond, hill, woods) from built ones (school, store, bridge). Later levels zoom out to a globe: "sail west from North America to reach the big ocean."
  - The aha or the end state: map-making level. The student lays out a familiar room or schoolyard with stickers, builds a key, hides a treasure, and writes two direction clues; Pip follows them and either finds it or gets lost, showing exactly which clue or symbol was unclear.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print the student's map; teacher sets a classroom map.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Geo.1.K-2: the student constructs a map of a familiar place with a key and directions, tested by Pip.
  - D2.Geo.3.K-2: key symbols and the globe level identify environmental (pond, ocean, hill) and cultural (school, bridge) characteristics.
- Not like: me-on-the-map (zooming levels from room to planet); map-maker (grade 3 scale and grid of an imaginary town).
- Art: generated scenes (park and globe in one storybook style) plus a drawn-in-code sticker set for the student map, because stickers must snap and recolor.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Follow compass directions and a map key across a park to find treasure, telling natural features from built ones, then make your own room map with a key and clues and watch a parrot try to follow it.
- Flags: none
