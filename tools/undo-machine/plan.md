arc: explore-first
minutes: 50
focus: F-BF.A; F-BF.B
goal: I can compose functions, write and verify an inverse by undoing steps in reverse order, read inverses as a reflection across y = x, and restrict a domain to make a function one-to-one.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Graph paper.
grouping: Pairs on devices, with whole-class pauses.
---
## Before class

- Open **Workshop** with an empty floor.
- The 8 test tokens are −6, −2.5, −1, 0, 0.5, 2, 3 and 9.
- Keyboard: select a card and press [ or ] to move it.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "You put on socks, then shoes. How do you undo that?" (Shoes off first.)
2. "Functions work the same way. Today you build machines and the machines that undo them."

## Explore (17 min)

1. On the projector, tap **Multiply by a**, then **Add a**, and set them to 3 and 4. The strip reads **f(x) = 3x + 4**.
2. Build the undo line in the "obvious" order: **Divide by 3**, then **Subtract 4**. Press **Run**. "2 came back as −0.6667. Why?"
3. Pairs swap the two undo cards and run again. All 8 tokens come home, and the strip shows **g(x) = f⁻¹(x)**.
4. Pairs build three of their own top lines, then the undo lines, and check with **Run** and **Send my number**.
5. Pairs build **Add 3**, **Square** and look at the verdict: **Can't be undone as built**. "−2 and −4 both come out as 1."

## Make sense (10 min)

1. "Why must the undo line run in reverse order?"
2. Open **Graph**: f solid, g dashed, y = x dotted. Press **Mirror**. "Why is the inverse the mirror image across y = x?"
3. For Add 3, Square, turn on **Gate** with x ≥ −3 and build the undo line. "Why does the gate fix it? What does the vertical line on the graph show?"

## Apply (13 min)

1. Pairs open **Puzzles** and work through levels 1 to 8. **Hint** gives up to three hints.
2. On level 9, ask: "Rounding forgets more than a sign. Why can no gate fix it?"
3. Pairs who finish try level 11: "Which 2-machine chains undo themselves?"

## Exit check (5 min)

On a card: "f(x) = (x − 5)/2. Write f⁻¹(x) and check it with x = 9." Then: "Why does g(x) = x² need a restricted domain to have an inverse? Name one domain that works." Cards that subtract before multiplying in the inverse show who needs the swap again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** stay with two-machine top lines and say each step out loud before undoing it.
- **Support:** use **Send my number** to trace one value through both lines.
- **Stretch:** "What is the smallest gate that works for Add 3, Square?"
- **Stretch:** build a 4-machine top line with a gate and verify its inverse on the graph.

## Short on time

- The 3x + 4 swap in **Workshop** (8 min).
- **Mirror** on the **Graph** (5 min), then the exit card (5 min).
