# Simile Slam (idea card)

- Module: ela-5-figurative-language, grade 5. Codes: RL.5.4, L.5.5
- Format / family: arcade / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: In fast rounds the student finishes similes and metaphors, reads them in short poems and stories to say what they mean, and swaps tired comparisons for fresh ones, so they learn that a comparison works by what it makes you see.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a stage with a mic stand and a timer. "Round 1: Finish it. As quiet as..."
  - Core loop: short timed rounds. Finish: pick or type an ending; a worn-out one (a mouse) scores less than a fresh one that fits (a snowfall at midnight). Mean It: a line in a short poem or story passage ("The classroom was a zoo") and the student picks what it says about the thing. Simile or Metaphor: sort lines fast. Say It Plain: match an idiom, adage or proverb to its meaning. Double Take: homographs (wind, wind; bow, bow) and pairs of synonyms and antonyms in sentences.
  - The aha or the end state: a "Fresh Wall" of the student's own best comparisons, kept on the device, and a last round where the student reads a short public-domain poem and names what each comparison makes them see.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a shared class wall on the projector.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RL.5.4: the Mean It round and the closing poem: what figurative lines mean in a text.
  - L.5.5: similes and metaphors in context, idioms, adages and proverbs, and homographs, synonyms and antonyms, one round each.
- Not like: idiom-illustrator (grade 3, literal pictures); word-thermometer (grade 6, shades of meaning); tone-dial (grade 10).
- Art: drawn in code for the stage and timer, illustrated pictures for the closing poem.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Fast rounds of figurative language: finish similes with fresh endings, say what a metaphor means in a poem, match proverbs to plain meanings and catch homographs; your best comparisons go on a Fresh Wall kept on your device.
- Flags: none (the Fresh Wall stores text: the privacy line is required)
