arc: model-then-make
minutes: 45
focus: MU:Cr2; MU:Cr3
goal: I can build a song in AB, ABA or rondo form, and explain how repetition and contrast give it its shape.
materials: The tool on the big screen with speakers loud enough for the back row, and one laptop per pair (headphones or a low volume, so pairs can hear their own song). Paper and pencils. Write on the board: form, AB form, ABA form, repetition, contrast, compose.
grouping: Whole class for the model, then pairs on laptops.
---
## Before class

- Open the tool on the big screen and test the speakers with **Hear** under the A block on the shelf. The tool plays music the whole period, so set the room volume first.
- On the big screen, choose **Listen** and leave the **Song** menu on **Hot Cross Buns**.
- On each laptop, press **Clear song**, then **Yes**, so every pair starts with an empty strip. Songs save on that laptop only.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "Think of a song you love. Is there a part that keeps coming back?" (The chorus.) "Why do songwriters bring it back?" (It's catchy, easy to remember, you can sing along.)
2. Sing Twinkle Twinkle together. "Did the beginning come back at the end?" (Yes.) "Musicians give each part a letter. The first part is A. A new part gets a new letter. The way the parts are put in order is the song's form."

## Model (13 min)

1. On the big screen, in **Listen**, press **Listen**. Part 1 plays and becomes an orange A block.
2. Part 2 plays. The class calls "same", "almost" or "new" before you tap. Tap **Same as A**. Part 3: tap **New** (it becomes B). Part 4: **Same as A**. Press **Hear it again** any time the class disagrees. A wrong answer plays the two parts one after the other, so just try again.
3. "Hot Cross Buns is A A B A. Which part gave us contrast?" (B, the quick part.) "Which part is repetition?" (A, three times.)
4. Switch to **Build**. Tap the orange **A** on the shelf, then space 1 in the strip. Put **B** in space 2 and **A** in space 3. Press **Play song**. The Form card says "Ternary form: away and back home." "That's ABA form. Why does it feel finished?" (It comes back home to A.)
5. Tap **Edit** under the shelf's A and tap two new notes on the grid. Both A blocks ripple and the Form card says "2 copies changed". Press **Done**, then **Play song**. "Why did both A blocks change?" (Same letter, same music.)
6. Tap the second A in the strip, then **Make it A′**. "A prime is a twin: the same start with a new ending." Tap **Edit** on the striped A′ block on the shelf and change beats 7 and 8 only. Press **Done**.

## Make (15 min)

1. Pairs compose their own song in **Build**. Step 1: an ABA form. Use **Edit** to make A and B sound really different (the Form card warns if two sections have the same notes).
2. Step 2: press **＋ New section** to add C, then build a rondo, A B A C A. The Form card should say "Rondo".
3. Step 3, refine: play it at **Slow**, **Medium** and **Quick**, change the **Instrument** in the editor, and check the end of the Form card. "Ends on do: sounds finished" is what most songs want.
4. Each pair writes its form in letters on paper (A B A C A) and one sentence about which part gives contrast.
5. Walk around and ask: "Which letter is your home part? What did you change to make B new?"

## Share (7 min)

1. Two or three pairs play their song on the big screen with the Form card covered by a sheet of paper. The class closes their eyes, listens, and calls the form.
2. Uncover the Form card to check. "How did you know A came back?" (It sounded the same, the same notes and instrument.)

## Exit check (5 min)

On paper or the printed ticket: a song goes verse, chorus, verse. Students write its form in letters (A B A) and explain what happens to every A block when you change one note in A, and why (they all change, because the same letter means the same music). Students who write A B C need step 2 of the Model again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** choose **Sunny Day** from **Starter songs** for a ready ABA song, then change one section.
- **Support:** in **Listen**, try **Twinkle Twinkle** (A B A) before Mary Had a Little Lamb.
- **Stretch:** build AABA, "a shape used in many popular songs", or A B A C A B A.
- **Stretch:** in **Listen**, label **Ode to Joy**, which has two twins (A A′ B A′).

## Short on time

- Model with Hot Cross Buns in **Listen**, then build ABA in **Build** together (10 min).
- Pairs build one rondo (7 min), then the exit check (3 min).
