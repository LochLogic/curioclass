# Levers of Power (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-8-levers-of-power, grade 8. Codes: D4.6.6-8, D4.7.6-8, D2.Civ.2.6-8
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: A dangerous crossing near school is also a state road problem and a worldwide traffic safety issue; the student zooms between local, state and global views, tries levers through the roles citizens play (petitioner, voter, testifier, reporter, organizer, juror), and judges what each lever can actually change at each level.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the crossing at school pickup, with a zoom control (street, city, state, world).
  - Core loop: at each level, see the problem's shape and causes (road design, speed limits, state funding, worldwide road deaths) and who decides; pull a lever by choosing a role and an action; a modeled outcome shows what moved, how long it took and how many people it needed.
  - The aha or the end state: a capacity board where the student rates what they alone and what a group could do at each level, and picks a realistic plan.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the class pools levers as a group.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D4.6.6-8: the zoom levels show one problem's characteristics and causes locally, at the state level and worldwide.
  - D4.7.6-8: the capacity board and plan, weighing levers, strategies and likely outcomes.
  - D2.Civ.2.6-8: each lever is tied to a real citizen role.
- Not like: tools-for-change (grade 5, strategy cards with examples); motion-on-the-floor (running one meeting); shaping-our-week (who shapes daily life).
- Art: generated scenes for each level and people (illustrated) for the roles.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (global road death figures and a real state process, checked); overlap risk (with tools-for-change, different grade focus)
