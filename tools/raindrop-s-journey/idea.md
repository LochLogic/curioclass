# Raindrop's Journey (idea card)

- Module: science-6-water-cycle-and-weather, grade 6. Codes: MS-ESS2-4, MS-ESS2-5
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good for MS-ESS2-4 only
- One idea: Be one water molecule and choose where to go next, but each choice opens only when the sun's energy or gravity allows it, so the water cycle becomes a set of physical rules the student can feel.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a molecule in a puddle at noon, with a sun meter (energy in) and a gravity arrow beside it.
  - Core loop: branching moves, each labeled with what drives it: evaporate (needs the sun's energy), rise and cool, condense on dust, fall as rain or snow (gravity), soak into the ground, run downhill to a river, freeze into a glacier, get pulled up by a tree and breathed out of a leaf. A log keeps the time spent in each place, from days in the air to thousands of years deep underground or in ice. No dead ends: every path loops back.
  - The aha or the end state: a journey map of every loop the student took, colored by driver: the sun lifts water up, gravity brings it down.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): compare journey maps; whose molecule spent the longest underground?
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-ESS2-4: every branch names whether sunlight or gravity drives it, and the end map is the student's model of water cycling through Earth's systems.
  - MOVE MS-ESS2-5: new tool: "Front Line" (grade 6), collect readings from a row of weather stations as a cold air mass meets a warm one, then use the data to forecast the next day's weather.
- Not like: sphere-connections (grade 5 cards across spheres); ocean-conveyor (circulation and climate); erosion-table (grade 4 surface change).
- Art: generated scenes for each place (lake, cloud, glacier, aquifer, leaf); the molecule is a small character drawn in code.
