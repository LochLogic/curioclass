# Story Graphs (idea card)

- Module: math-8-modeling-with-functions, grade 8. Codes: 8.F.B
- Format / family: sort / graphing. Fit: good for matching and sketching, with a model-building round added so linear models are built, not only sorted.
- One idea: Match short life stories ("I biked to school, stopped for a friend, then raced the bell") to the graphs that tell them, sketch graphs from stories, and for steady stories find the rate and the starting value to write the model.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a comic strip of a morning and four distance-time graphs. A student narrator, Lucia: "Which graph is my morning? One of them is a trap."
  - Sort rounds: stories and graphs are dragged into pairs; the student tags each part of a graph (increasing, decreasing, flat, steady or speeding up) and says which words in the story match it. Traps include graphs that go back in time or a "faster" story with a shallower line.
  - Sketch rounds: the student draws a graph for a new story (filling a bathtub, pulling the plug, a phone battery through the day), and the tool replays the story along the sketch to check it.
  - Model rounds: steady stories become linear models. A pool already has 200 gallons and fills at 50 gallons a minute; a taxi ride costs $7 at 2 miles and $13 at 5 miles. The student finds the rate of change and the initial value from a description, two points, a table or a graph, writes y = mx + b, and says what each number means in the story.
  - The aha or the end state: a graph is a story told in shape, and a straight story is two numbers: where it starts and how fast it changes.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): write your own story for a partner to graph.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 8.F.B: constructing linear models from a description, two points, a table or a graph, and interpreting rate of change and initial value in context (8.F.4); describing graphs qualitatively (increasing, decreasing, linear, nonlinear) and sketching graphs from stories (8.F.5).
- Not like: graph-story-lab (grade 9 real graphs with domain and range), ski-slope-designer (same graphing family, grade 8 slope), function-machine-factory (grade 8 defining functions).
- Art: people (illustrated) for Lucia and the story comics, varied; graphs drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Match short life stories to the graphs that tell them, sketch graphs from new stories, and turn steady stories into linear models by finding where they start and how fast they change.
- Flags: none
