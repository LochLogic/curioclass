# Clay Cutting Bench (idea card)

- Module: math-7-cross-sections-of-solids, grade 7. Codes: 7.G.A
- Format / family: lab / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Slice clay prisms and pyramids with a wire at different angles and predict each cut face before it opens.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a clay rectangular prism and a cutting wire. "If you cut here, at this angle, what shape is the face?"
  - Core loop: predict the cut face, then slice prisms and pyramids parallel, at an angle and through corners; the face opens and is compared with the prediction.
  - The aha or the end state: the student can predict any plane section of a right rectangular prism or pyramid.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 7.G.A (7.G.3): plane sections of right rectangular prisms and right rectangular pyramids.
- Not like: slice-and-stack (grade 10 cross sections); blueprint-studio (scale drawings).
- Art: drawn in code (3D clay with lighting), because the cut must be exact.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (slice-and-stack; keep this one to predicting plane sections)
- Origin: new tool from the 2026-10-27 overlap and gap audit (docs/agents/audit/gaps.md); codes from blueprint-studio (7.G.3).
