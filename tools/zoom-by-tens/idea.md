# Powers of Ten Zoom (idea card)

- Module: math-5-powers-of-ten-and-decimals, grade 5. Codes: 5.NBT.A
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good, after a change of scale: zoom on a meter stick, not the cosmos.
- One idea: Zoom into a meter stick through tenths (decimeters), hundredths (centimeters) and thousandths (millimeters) to measure small real things, and watch each zoom step multiply or divide by 10 as the decimal point slides.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a nature table with a magnifier over a meter stick and a tray of small things: a pencil, a feather, an acorn, a ladybug, a grain of rice, a sand grain.
  - Core loop: place an item on the stick and zoom in; each zoom shows ten new marks between the old ones, and the length gains a digit (0.2 m, 0.18 m, 0.175 m). A place-value chart beside the stick shows each digit worth 10 times the one to its right and 1/10 of the one to its left.
  - Slide rounds: 10 rice grains in a row, 100, 1,000; the length is multiplied by 10, 10^2, 10^3, and the decimal point slides one place per zero. Then divide back down.
  - Compare and round rounds: line up the ladybug (0.008 m) and the rice grain (0.007 m); which is longer, 0.25 or 0.205? Zoom out to round a measurement to the nearest hundredth or tenth.
  - Name it rounds: write 0.175 in expanded form (1 x 1/10 + 7 x 1/100 + 5 x 1/1000) and in words.
  - The aha or the end state: every place is ten of the place to its right, so multiplying by 10 just moves every digit one place.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): measure a real object at home with a ruler and enter it (stays on the device).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5.NBT.A: digit values ten times and 1/10 as large (5.NBT.1); zero patterns and decimal-point shifts when multiplying or dividing by powers of 10, written as exponents (5.NBT.2); reading, writing and comparing decimals to thousandths in numerals, words and expanded form (5.NBT.3); rounding decimals to any place by zooming out (5.NBT.4).
- Not like: size-of-everything (grade 8 scientific notation across the universe), money-and-meters (grade 4 tenths and hundredths), stadium-count (grade 4 whole-number place value).
- Art: generated illustrations of the objects and the nature table; stick, marks and place-value chart drawn in code.
- Proposed title: Zoom by Tens (the old title and line echo a well-known short film and sit on top of size-of-everything)
- Proposed concept line: Zoom into a meter stick through tenths, hundredths and thousandths to measure a ladybug, a rice grain and a feather; each zoom is a power of ten, and the decimal point slides as you go.
- Flags: needs a data source (typical lengths of the sample objects, sourced); overlap risk (size-of-everything, if the cosmic zoom stays)
