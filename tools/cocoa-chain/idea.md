# Cocoa Chain (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-5-cocoa-chain, grade 5. Codes: D2.Eco.14.3-5, D2.Eco.15.3-5, D2.Geo.12.3-5, D4.2.3-5
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Follow a season's cocoa from a farm family in Ghana through the port, a grinding factory abroad and a candy maker here to a chocolate bar in a store; when drought cuts the harvest, the student traces with real prices how a farmer, a dockworker, a factory worker and a shopper are each affected, and builds the explanation step by step.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a chocolate bar on a store shelf with a "where did this come from?" thread running off toward a map.
  - Core loop: pull the thread to visit each stop (farm, buying station, port, factory, candy maker, store); each stop shows who works there and what they earn from the bar. Turn on drought: the harvest drops and prices move along the chain. The student orders sentence tiles, each with a number, to explain who is affected and why.
  - The aha or the end state: the countries depend on each other; the same drought means a smaller crop for the farm family, higher costs for the factory and a smaller or pricier bar here.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a slider showing how much of the bar's price reaches the farm.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Eco.14.3-5: the chain map showing how trade ties Ghana, a factory country and ours together.
  - D2.Eco.15.3-5: each stop's worker card shows how the same change helps or hurts different groups in each country.
  - D2.Geo.12.3-5: the drought in one place changes prices and jobs far away.
  - D4.2.3-5: the ordered explanation built from tiles with reasons, numbers and details.
- Not like: where-s-my-phone-from (grade 10 supply-chain data desk); colonial-trade-routes (historical trade); barter-bay (money and trade inside one town).
- Art: generated scenes for each stop and people (illustrated) for the workers, because the people along the chain are the point.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: sensitive (Ghana shown with care and accuracy; child labor in cocoa named plainly and with care; fresh review agent); needs a data source (farm-gate price, share of a bar's price by stop, a real recent drought or price spike, dated)
