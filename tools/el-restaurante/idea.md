# El Restaurante (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-k-12-food-and-ordering, grade k-12. Codes: ACTFL.Novice High
- Format / family: roleplay / spanish. Fit: good: ordering is a real conversation with turns.
- One idea: Eat out at a small family restaurant in Mexico City and order for your table in Spanish: you read the menu, say what you and your friends like and do not like (me gusta, me gustan), ask politely (quisiera, ¿me trae...?), and work around a friend who cannot eat something.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a table for three, a handwritten menu with pictures, and the server: "¡Buenas tardes! ¿Qué van a tomar?"
  - Core loop: each turn the server or a friend speaks; the student answers by building a sentence from tiles. Friends state likes and dislikes, and one is vegetarian, so ordering means reading the menu closely. "Me gusta la sopa" versus "me gustan los tacos" lights up the noun that decides the ending. Polite and blunt versions get different reactions from the server (a smile versus a pause), never a buzzer.
  - The aha or the end state: the whole meal goes right (the right dishes for everyone, the check asked for politely with "La cuenta, por favor"), and the student sees their order as a short spoken dialogue they built.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a second restaurant in another country with its own menu, so food words are tied to real places; a printable menu for pair role play.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Novice High: simple sentences to state likes and dislikes, ask for food and drink politely, and answer a server's questions.
- Not like: hola-hallway (greetings only); consejero (opinions with the subjunctive, grade 9-12); mi-casa (family and home).
- Art: illustrated restaurant, server and friends (people, illustrated); dishes drawn true to the real cuisine.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Order for your table at a family restaurant in Mexico City; read the menu, say what each friend likes with me gusta and me gustan, work around a vegetarian friend, and ask politely from first drink to the check.
- Flags: needs a data source (recorded Spanish voices; real dishes and menu checked at spec); light sensitivity (food culture, fresh review); K-12 span check (gf-04).
