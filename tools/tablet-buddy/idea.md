# Tablet Buddy (idea card)

- Module: computer-science-k-2-using-tech-well, grade k-2. Codes: EK-SYS-IM-13, E1-SYS-IM-13, E2-SYS-IM-13
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good, because each choice has a visible result for the character and friends.
- One idea: Guide Pip, a young panda in a class of animal friends, through a school day with the class's one shared tablet; every choice (clean hands, a two-hand carry, headphones, logging out, a break after a long video) shows what it does to Pip and to the next friend who uses it.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: Pip waves from a cozy classroom; the shared tablet sits in its charging basket. "Help Pip use the tablet today."
  - Core loop: six moments in the day, each with two or three picture choices. Sticky snack hands smudge the screen for Otter; a one-hand carry drops it; no headphones makes the reading corner noisy; leaving his game open lets Fox change Pip's score; three videos in a row and Pip's eyes feel tired and he misses the playground.
  - Good uses get equal time: a video call with Grandma Panda, a reading app that reads a hard word aloud, a photo of the bean plant for science.
  - The aha or the end state: a day map shows Pip's energy and each friend's face at every moment, so the student sees the tablet helps when it is shared with care and can hurt when it is not.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a printable "our tablet rules" card built from the student's best choices.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - EK-SYS-IM-13: pick the careful way to hold, clean and share the device, and see why.
  - E1-SYS-IM-13: compare a helpful use and a too-long use of screen time in the same day.
  - E2-SYS-IM-13: explain to a friend character why logging out and taking turns matter, by choosing the reason that matches what happened.
- Not like: internet-journey (grade 3, a message crossing the internet); secret-keeper (K, what is OK to share); habit-hero (health habits through a day).
- Art: generated scenes with illustrated animal characters, warm and soft for K-2.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Guide Pip the panda through a school day with the class's one shared tablet; each choice (clean hands, headphones, logging out, a break after a long video) shows how it helps or hurts Pip and the friends who share it.
- Flags: none
