# Morning Show Crew

## One idea
One message, four media, twelve crew hours: build the spring-musical audition story as a video segment, a poster, a site post and an announcement that must all carry the same four facts, then spend the week's hours on who to reach and how often, and on Friday at 3:15 see, group by group, who actually got the message and who never heard it.

## Not like
- storyboard-studio (9-12, studio): plans film shots and cuts. Here the shots are given and the job is the same message across media and reaching people.
- brand-builder (9-12): one mark tested across surfaces. Here a message, with facts that must match, and an audience model over a week.
- design-fixer (6-8): fixes one poster's layout. Here the poster is one of four versions and its layout is not the point.
- viral-video-breakdown (this band): how a video spreads and changes without the maker. Here the student plans the spread on purpose, inside one school.
- school-wall-mural (this band): one public artwork judged by criteria. This is a message across media and time.

## Who and how
- Grade 6-8, media arts, module "Media Production and Distribution (Media Arts)". Topic line: one story across media and audiences.
- Driver: students (pairs: producer and editor on one laptop). 25 to 40 minutes.
- Standards: NCAS MA:Pr4 (integrate content and forms across media), MA:Pr6 (present and distribute for audiences and contexts), MA:Cn10 (relate media to purpose and audience).
- Header: title, topic line, modes **Make** (default), **The week**, **Friday**, then kit `motion`, `sound`, `read`.

## Screens and modes

### Layout (all modes)
- Top strip: the four facts the story must carry (What, When, Where, How to sign up), each a chip, and four small icons for the media, each showing which facts its current version carries (a check per fact) and a "matches" or "doesn't match" badge.

### Make (build the four versions)
- Focus: the selected medium's canvas, center stage: Video (a 16:9 monitor), Poster (a 3:4 sheet on the painted hallway corkboard), Site post (a web card), Announcement (a script card with a time bar). Tabs **Video**, **Poster**, **Site**, **Announcement** above it.
- Video: a 4-slot shot strip under the monitor. Tap a slot, then a shot from the shot bin (8 painted shots, Content) to fill it. **Headline** (three choices), **Lower third** text: tap fact chips to add them (each chip has a right and a wrong variant, Content). **Play segment** plays the 4 shots, 3 s each, with the headline title card and lower third (12 s plus the 2 s title).
- Poster: **Headline** (three choices), **Picture** (any shot), fact lines (tap chips to add, at most 4), **Big** toggles one line large.
- Site: **Headline**, fact lines, **Picture**.
- Announcement: tap sentence chips (Content) to build the script; the time bar shows its length at 2.5 words a second and turns amber over 30 s. **Hear it** reads it with the narrator voice.
- Responds: the top strip updates at once; a version with a wrong variant gets "doesn't match" and the wrong chip is outlined.

### The week (tycoon)
- Focus: the week board, four day columns (Monday to Thursday) by the four media rows plus Spanish and Captions sub-rows, each cell a job the student can buy with crew hours, and the hours meter (3 a day: three crew members, one hour each before school).
- Jobs and costs (Content). Tap a cell to buy or cancel a job for that day. A job bigger than a day's hours (the video edit) spreads over days: buying it starts it and its bar fills day by day; it airs from the day it finishes.
- **Run day** plays the day: a short animation of the school map (code) with dots by audience group lighting as they meet a version, then the day's numbers: how many in each group have met the message once, and how many twice or more. **Undo day** steps back one day.
- Audience panel: five groups (Content) with their sizes and a bar: heard of it (met once or more) and got the message (met at least twice, with a complete, matching version).

### Friday
- Focus: the result board at 3:15 on Friday: each group's got-it share as a big bar, with the count, and under it the audition line: how many students came (8% of the students who got the message, the tool's own guess, said on screen).
- **What reached whom**: one row per medium: the meetings it produced per group.
- **Try the week again** returns to The week with the same versions; **Back to Make** keeps the week plan.

## Content

### The story (made up): Spring musical auditions
- What: auditions for the spring musical, no experience needed. Right chip: "Spring musical auditions, no experience needed." Wrong chip: "Spring musical tryouts for chorus members" (it leaves people out).
- When: right "Friday at 3:15"; wrong "Friday at 3:30" (last year's time).
- Where: right "Music room"; wrong "Room 121" (the old music room).
- How: right "Sign up on the sheet outside room 112 by Thursday"; wrong "Sign up at the office".
- Headlines (any one is fine; all three fit): "Your Stage Is Waiting", "Spring Musical Auditions Friday", "Ever Wanted to Be in a Show?"
- Announcement sentence chips (words): "Good morning, Wildcats!" (3); "Spring musical auditions are Friday at 3:15 in the music room." (11); "No experience needed." (3); "Sign up on the sheet outside room 112 by Thursday." (10); "Auditions start at 3:30 on Friday." (6, wrong time); "Ask the office to sign up." (6, wrong); "You don't have to sing alone; you can audition with a friend." (12); "See you there!" (3). Seconds = words / 2.5. Buying "Announcement in Spanish" adds a Spanish reading of the same right facts (a fixed, recorded script checked by a Spanish speaker before release), shown under the English card.
- Shots (painted, Art): ghost light on an empty stage; sign-up sheet on a door (blank lines; text in code); music room with a piano; costume rack; stack of scripts; hallway with lockers; wall clock (hands in code at 3:15); a ticket table with a cash box. A shot carries a fact visually: clock = When, music room = Where, sign-up sheet = How; any shot can carry What only through text.

### Jobs (crew hours) and reach (expected meetings per member per day while the job runs; the tool's own model, shown in the teacher notes)
| Job | Hours | On time | Late bus | Learning English | Families | Teachers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Announcement: write 0.5 once, read 0.25 each day | 0.5 + 0.25/day | 0.45 | 0 | 0.10 | 0 | 0.35 |
| Announcement in Spanish (read) | 0.25/day | 0 | 0 | 0.35 | 0 | 0 |
| Video: edit 4 (spreads over days), air 0.5 each day | 4 + 0.5/day | 0.50 | 0 | 0.20 | 0 | 0.30 |
| Video captions (adds) | 1 once | 0.10 | 0 | 0.15 | 0 | 0.05 |
| Poster: design 2 once, each printed copy 0.25 (per copy, from the day printed) | 2 + 0.25/copy | 0.08 | 0.12 | 0.08 | 0.01 | 0.05 |
| Poster in Spanish (one copy) | 1 once | 0 | 0.02 | 0.10 | 0.02 | 0 |
| Site post | 1 once | 0.02 | 0.06 | 0.01 | 0.15 | 0.08 |
| Site post in Spanish | 1 once | 0 | 0 | 0.03 | 0.12 | 0 |
- Meetings add up over the days a job runs (Monday to Thursday, from the day it is done). With m expected meetings, heard = 1 minus e^(-m); got the message = 1 minus e^(-m)(1 + m), counting only versions that carry all four right facts. A version with a wrong or missing fact still counts for "heard", never for "got".
- The week's limit: 3 hours a day, 12 for the week; unspent hours are lost.

## Interactions in detail
- Make: every chip, shot and headline tap updates the canvas and the top strip at once. Play segment and Hear it keep every control live: changing a shot slot while it plays swaps it at once if that slot hasn't played, and on the next loop otherwise (the strip outlines it); Stop is immediate.
- The week: buying a job more than the day allows is blocked with "Only 0.5 hours left today." A job for a medium whose version doesn't match is allowed, with a warning badge, because that is a real mistake teams make.
- Run day animation is about 4 s (Motion off: the numbers appear at once). Run day on Thursday leads to Friday.
- Friday is reachable any time; it uses the days run so far.
- Saved on this device: versions and the week plan (localStorage, try/catch). **Reset** asks to confirm.
- Sizes: at 1366x768 the top strip, the canvas or week board, and its main buttons are visible without scrolling. Preview 680x425: canvas and top strip readable. At 375 wide: top strip as two rows, canvas, then controls.

## Voice
Bank voice: `lively-host` (the morning show's student host; no neighbor or other spec in this band uses it). Style from the bank. `Curio.voice` with `scope: () => state.mode`. Nothing narrates while the segment plays or the announcement is heard; Hear it reads the student's script in the same voice through the browser fallback if no clip exists (its text varies).
- welcomeMake (how; entering Make; stale when any chip or slot is tapped): "Good morning, crew! One story, four ways. Tap the fact chips to build each version."
- mismatch (event; a version gets a wrong chip; stale when fixed): "Hold on. That fact doesn't match the others."
- allMatch (event; all four versions match; stale on the next change): "All four say the same thing. Now plan your week."
- welcomeWeek (how; entering The week; stale when a job is bought): "Three hours a day. Tap a job to spend them, then press Run day."
- dayDone (event; after each Run day): "That's the day. Check who heard it." (numbers on screen)
- fridayLine (event; entering Friday after Thursday ran): "It's three fifteen on Friday. Here's who got the message."
- noOne (event; a group at 0% on Friday): "Someone never heard about it. Who was it, and why?"

## Sources
- National Core Arts Standards, Media Arts MA:Pr4, Pr6, Cn10: https://www.nationalartsstandards.org/
- Repetition and reach (an audience usually needs to meet a message more than once): the "effective frequency" idea in media planning, Britannica, "Advertising: media planning", https://www.britannica.com/topic/advertising ; the two-meeting rule here is the tool's own simple model.
- Speaking rate about 150 words a minute for timing the announcement: National Center for Voice and Speech, https://ncvs.org/
- School, story, groups, jobs, hours, reach numbers and the 8% audition rate: written for Curio Class (a model, said on screen).
- Checks run (Python, 2026-10-29), Monday to Thursday, all versions matching:
  - Announcement plus Spanish read every day (2.5 h): got it on time 54%, late bus 0%, learning English 54%, families 0%, teachers 41%; 276 of 985 people got it, 435 heard it.
  - Balanced (Mon announcement and poster design; Tue 4 copies and site post; video edit Tue to Thu, airs Thu; Spanish site post Thu; announcement daily): on time 84%, late bus 48%, learning English 80%, families 15%, teachers 72%; 524 got it, 751 heard.
  - Posters first (Mon design and 4 copies; Tue Spanish poster, site, Spanish site; Wed announcement and Spanish from Wed, 2 more copies; 8 h): on time 72%, late bus 74%, learning English 79%, families 29%, teachers 58%; 539 got it, 802 heard. The same plan with the poster showing "3:30": got it falls to 211 while heard stays 802, which is the lesson about matching facts.
  - No plan inside 12 hours reaches every group: families stay under 40% in every plan tried.
  - Announcement lengths: the four right chips plus greeting and sign-off (3 + 11 + 3 + 10 + 3 = 30 words) = 12 s.
  - Contrast ratios recomputed with the WCAG 2.1 formula.

## Done when
1. Focus: in Make the selected canvas is the biggest, central thing; in The week the week board is; in Friday the group bars are; at 1366x768 and 680x425.
2. A wrong chip in any version flips its badge to "doesn't match" and outlines the chip; fixing it flips it back.
3. The three week plans in Sources reproduce their got-it and heard numbers within 1 person per group (recompute in Python).
4. Day hours cap at 3; the video edit spreads over days and airs from the day it finishes.
5. Tap while it plays: during Play segment a slot change and Stop act at once; during Run day, Undo day and mode buttons act at once.
6. Friday shows each group, the audition count (8% of students who got it), and What reached whom.
7. How lines once in five repeats; mismatch, dayDone and noOne speak every time; nothing narrates over the segment.
8. Saved versions and plan return after reload; Reset clears them.
