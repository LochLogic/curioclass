# Morning Show Crew (idea card)

- Module: arts-6-8-media-production-and-distribution, grade 6-8. Codes: MA:Pr4, MA:Pr6, MA:Cn10
- Format / family: tycoon / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Run the school's morning show crew for one week with one story (the cafeteria starts composting, and nobody knows which bin is which); tell it as a video segment, a hallway poster, a post on the school site and a 20-second announcement, keep the message the same in every form, and spend limited crew hours to reach the audiences that matter.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the crew room, a week calendar, crew members with skills and hours, and the story file (facts, quotes, photos).
  - Core loop: build each version from the same facts with a central-idea check (does this version still say "food scraps in the green bin"?). Schedule release across the week. Audiences behave realistically: homerooms watch the video if it is short, sixth graders read posters at eye level by the cafeteria, families read the site on Sunday, the custodian hears the announcement.
  - The aha or the end state: Friday's results: how many people in each group got the message, and how many green bins were used right, traced to which version reached them. The student explains which mix worked and why, and plans next week's story from a topic poll of what students care about.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the class splits the four formats and compares reach.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MA:Pr4: integrating video, image, text and sound around one central idea across formats.
  - MA:Pr6: choosing presentation formats and planning distribution for each audience.
  - MA:Cn10: drawing on research (the story file, the topic poll) and students' interests to shape the work.
- Not like: mini-documentary-crew (grade 9-12: one long film, a full crew); field-trip-slideshow (K-2); brand-builder (grade 9-12 brand surfaces).
- Art: generated scenes for the school and crew room; people (illustrated) for the crew and audiences.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Run the school morning show for a week with one story: tell it as a video segment, a poster, a site post and an announcement that all say the same thing, then spend crew hours to reach each audience and see who got the message.
- Flags: overlap risk (mini-documentary-crew; both crew tycoons, kept apart by scope and grade)
