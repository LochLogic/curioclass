arc: model-then-make
minutes: 45
focus: E5-PRO-PD-04; E5-PRO-TR-08
goal: I can remix a program, give credit to the person who made it, and use what changed to find a bug.
materials: The tool on the projector, plus one laptop per pair. Sticky notes for the exit check.
grouping: Whole class for the model, then partners on laptops (one drives, one navigates, switch halfway).
---
## Before class

- Open the tool on the projector. It opens in **Arcade** with the shelf of games. Sound on.
- Write the exit check on the board and cover it.
- The teacher notes explain every button and saving.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "You build a LEGO castle from a friend's design, then add a dragon and a moat. Whose castle is it?" (Both. The idea started with your friend.)
2. "You post a photo of it. What would be fair to write under it?" (Something like "based on Maya's castle.")
3. "Coders do this all the time. It's called a remix. Today you'll remix a game and find out why the credit never comes off."

## Model (13 min)

1. Pick **Fetch!** by Maya and press **Play**. Click the stage (or use the arrow pad) and chase the ball. Press **Stop**.
2. Press **Slow-mo**, then **Play**. "Watch the code, not the puppy. What runs when the puppy touches the ball?" (Change score by 1, play sound bark, jump ball to a random spot.)
3. Press **Remix this**. Point to the locked line: "Remix of Fetch! by Maya." "Why can't we delete this?" (Maya made the game. Her credit stays.)
4. Class vote on one change, or use this one: click the **1** in "change score by 1" under "when puppy touches ball" and pick **3**. Point out the **changed** tag and the new row in **What changed**.
5. Play it, then press **Original** and play that. "Same start, same luck. Is our remix different the way we wanted?" Press **My remix** to switch back.
6. Back in **Arcade**, pick **Upside Fetch** by Kai: "Help! My puppy can't go up." Ask: "This program has about 25 lines. Where would you start looking?" (In the changes Kai made. That's where it stopped working.)
7. Press **Remix this**. Under **Changes Kai made**, tap the row. Tap **down**, pick **up**, and test the up arrow. Name the strategy: "Compare with a working original. The bug hides in what changed."

## Make (18 min)

1. Pairs open **Pumpkin Rush** by Jay ("Faster pumpkins! But my score is stuck."), press **Remix this**, and fix it.
2. Before they touch anything: "Jay made two changes. Which one did he mean, and which is the bug?" (Pumpkins jumping every 1 second was on purpose. Change score by 0 is the bug.) The fix keeps the faster pumpkins.
3. Then pairs make their own remix: pick **Fetch!**, **Pumpkin Pop** or **Snow Bounce**, press **Remix this**, and say a goal out loud ("harder" or "sillier").
4. Make two to four changes, pressing **Play** after each. Stuck? Open **Challenges** and press **Try it** on one.
5. Type a **Title** and a first name or nickname in **Remixer**.
6. Walk around and ask: "What did you change, and how do you know it worked?" "Who gets credit for your game?"

## Share (5 min)

1. Pairs press **Save**, then swap laptops with the next pair.
2. Visitors play the remix and guess what changed, then check the **What changed** list.
3. Ask two or three pairs: "Read the credit lines. Whose work is in this game?" (The remixers and the original maker. A borrowed sprite adds a name.)

## Exit check (4 min)

Uncover the board. Each child answers on a sticky note:

> Lee remixed Snow Bounce by Ana: "My snowball never falls now!" What changed shows:
> - In when game starts: set snowball speed to 4 → 0
> - backdrop: snowy hill → park
>
> 1. Which row is the bug? 2. Write the credit line Lee's remix needs.

(1. The speed: at 0 the snowball can't move. 2. "Remix of Snow Bounce by Ana.") Anyone who picked the backdrop needs the compare strategy again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** start with the challenge "Make the ball worth 3 points in Fetch!": Try it loads the game, and only one number changes.
- **Support:** use **Slow-mo**, or **Pause** then **Step**, to watch one line at a time.
- **Stretch:** "Make Snow Bounce harder with exactly one change." "Whose one change made the biggest difference? Why?"
- **Stretch:** **Borrow** the skunk from Pumpkin Pop. "Why did a second credit line appear?" (You used Leo's work too.)

## Short on time

- Hook question (2 min), then model steps 1 to 5 with one change to **Fetch!** (6 min).
- Fix **Upside Fetch** together with **What changed** (5 min).
- Pairs fix **Pumpkin Rush** and say which change was the bug (5 min).
