# Read-Along Shelf (idea card)

- Module: ela-k-2-reading-range-and-text-complexity, grade k-2. Codes: RL.K.10, RL.1.10, RL.2.10, RI.K.10, RI.1.10, RI.2.10
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: A shelf of stories, poems and fact books: kindergarten joins read-alouds with a purpose question for each, grades 1 and 2 read along with help that fades page by page, and a range map shows the kinds of texts read.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a shelf of stories, poems and fact books. "Pick a book. Here is what to listen for."
  - Core loop: kindergarten joins read-alouds with a purpose question for each; grades 1 and 2 read along with help that fades page by page; a range map shows the kinds of texts read.
  - The aha or the end state: the range map is full across stories, poems and fact books.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RL.K.10, RL.1.10, RL.2.10: read and listen to literature at grade level with fading support.
  - RI.K.10, RI.1.10, RI.2.10: the same for informational texts.
- Not like: library-adventure (live, library layout).
- Art: public domain texts and illustrations (data/library/stories-pd.csv).
