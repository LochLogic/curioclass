Earthquake Table Manual

1. Design Anything on the 5-Bay Blueprint Grid

In Design & Build mode, a 5-bay by 8-story drafting grid opens over the steel platen and the camera automatically frames your structure big and clear. Hit Blank Platen to build from scratch, or explore starter blueprints like the Pyramid, Arch Viaduct, and Skybridge.

Click or drag-and-drop parts right onto the grid. Right-click any cell to unbolt. As you add shapes, watch the live Nat. Period and green Resonance Curve update immediately.

  • Triangles (◢ ▲ ◣): Inherently rigid truss shapes that are lighter than full box bays (0.85 kg vs. 1.2 kg) and deliver 2.2× lateral stiffness. Click an existing triangle to rotate its slope—perfect for pyramids, A-frames, and flying buttresses.
  • Steel Arches: Curved ribs that channel gravity loads outward into compression thrust—great for open colonnades, viaducts, and arched skybridges spanning between twin towers.
  • X-Brace & Chevron V-Brace: Click any box bay with the X / V tool to cycle between crossed diagonals and modern inverted-V seismic bracing.
  • Shear Walls, Heavy Columns & Brass Ballast: Mix stiff steel core walls with flexible outer frames, taper column gauges from thick at the bottom to light at the top, or drop heavy brass weights onto specific bays.

2. Resonance & Mode Shapes

Every structure has natural sway rhythms called modes. Its slowest rhythm is Mode 1 (period T₁ in seconds, frequency f₁ in Hz). When shaking matches f₁, sway grows dramatically. Click M1 or M2 on the scope to watch your structure's exact Mode 1 (single whip) and Mode 2 (S-bend) shapes.

3. Four Classic Protection Systems

Rigid Frame Columns bend in an S-shape to absorb motion. Open and ductile, but flexible—tall narrow frames feel strong gravity P-Δ push once they lean.
Trusses, Braces & Walls Triangles, X/V braces, and shear walls stiffen the frame and cut drift sharply, raising natural frequency—but overloaded braces and walls can snap abruptly.
Tuned Mass Damper A brass weight on roof rails tuned to f₁. At resonance it swings opposite the roof, splitting one tall resonant peak into two small humps.
Base Isolation Laminated rubber-lead bearings let the steel platen slide underneath while the structure glides almost straight. Weakest near very slow 0.5 Hz motion.
Side-by-Side Comparison & Real Quake Files: Toggle 2 Rigs to bolt two different designs onto one shake table (e.g., an Arch Viaduct vs. a Box Frame) and watch them ride the exact same quake. In Shake Test mode, click .AT2/CSV to load any local PEER NGA .at2 or CSV ground-motion file offline.
