## A 5-minute start

1. Have everyone find their own space: arms out, no touching.
2. Open **Move along** with the **frog**. Read the three moves together, press **Go!** and hop to the beat. The timer ends with a whistle and FREEZE.
3. Try **Surprise animal** a few times, then play the **Freeze game**.

## The three modes

- **Move along:** twelve animals, one skill each, with two or three form cues and a beat that matches the move. Choose 10, 20 or 30 seconds.
  - Frog: hop. Kangaroo: jump. Horse: gallop. Crab: slide. Penguin: waddle (walk). Bear: bear walk. Flamingo: balance. Elephant: stomp (march). Eagle: soar (stretch). Snake: wiggle (twist). Cheetah: run in place. Cat: tiptoe.
- **Freeze game:** eight animals in a row. The music stops at a random moment and everyone freezes like a statue.
- **Mix it up:** the same animal fast or slow, big or small, high or low. **Random mix** picks a challenge for you. Fast and slow change the beat.

## Teaching notes

- The cues are the ones K-2 PE teachers use: land softly with bent knees, same foot leads in a gallop, look where you're going in a slide.
- In a small classroom, use the in-place skills: frog hop, kangaroo jump, flamingo, elephant, eagle, snake, cheetah, cat. Save gallop, slide and bear walk for the gym or outside.
- Watch for children who need a modification: flamingo can hold a chair; bear walk can be on knees.

## Talk about it

- Which animal moved at a low level? A high level?
- Which move was hardest to do slowly? Why?
- Can you make up an animal move for the class to copy?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes.
- **Sound** turns off music and voice together. With Motion off, the animal stays still but the timer and freeze still work.
