arc: model-then-make
minutes: 30
focus: SHAPE.S1
goal: I can hop, jump, gallop and balance with good form, and move fast or slow, big or small, high or low.
materials: The tool on the big screen with sound on. Open floor space (the gym or outside is best for gallop, slide and bear walk). Optional: a chair for flamingo balance.
grouping: Whole class, each child in their own space.
---
## Before class

- Check the space. In a small classroom, use the in-place animals: frog, kangaroo, flamingo, elephant, eagle, snake, cheetah and cat.
- Pick 10, 20 or 30 seconds for each move. Shorter is better at first.
- Plan modifications: flamingo can hold a chair, bear walk can be on knees.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Everyone finds their own space: arms out, no touching.
2. "Animals move in all kinds of ways. Can you move like a frog? Like a flamingo? Today you learn their moves."

## Model (8 min)

1. Open **Move along** and pick the **frog**. Read the three cues together and show them yourself: bend your knees, push off, land softly.
2. Press **Go!** and everyone hops to the beat. The whistle means FREEZE.
3. Do the same with the **flamingo** (balance) and the **horse** (gallop, same foot leads), modeling the cues first.

## Make (12 min)

1. Press **Surprise animal** three or four times. Children read the cues with you, then move.
2. Switch to **Mix it up**. Do the same animal fast and slow, then big and small, then high and low. Press **Random mix** for a challenge.
3. Switch to the **Freeze game**. Eight animals in a row, and the music stops at a surprise moment. Everyone freezes like a statue.

## Share (4 min)

1. Two or three children make up a new animal move. The class copies it.
2. Ask: "Which animal moved at a low level? A high level? Which move was hardest to do slowly?"

## Exit check (2 min)

Call out a cue: "Land softly!" Children show a frog hop. "Same foot leads!" Children show a gallop. "Still as a statue!" Children balance like a flamingo. Watch for bent knees and soft landings.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** hold a chair or a wall for flamingo, and do bear walk on knees.
- **Support:** use only 10-second moves and the in-place animals.
- **Stretch:** hold the flamingo balance on the other foot, or with eyes closed for three seconds.
- **Stretch:** in **Mix it up**, combine two challenges: fast and low, slow and big.

## Short on time

- Three animals in **Move along** (6 min).
- One round of the **Freeze game** (4 min).
