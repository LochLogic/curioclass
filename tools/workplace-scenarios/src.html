Your job card

Junior counselor, Lakeside Rec Center summer camp (made up). A volunteer role for ages 12 to 15. You help a senior counselor run a group of eight 7-year-olds, the Bluebirds.

  • Counselors only use the glue guns.
  • Count campers at every move.
  • Radio Kai before leaving the group.
  • Tell Ms. Ortega about any problem with space or safety.

Under United States law, most paid jobs outside farms are closed to people under 14, and 14- and 15-year-olds can work only certain jobs for limited hours. So many people start with volunteer roles like this one.
