## What it teaches

Students can use function notation, compare families of curves, measure average rates of change and distinguish a fitted model from measurements. Graph Studio is an open instrument, with no assigned sequence.

## Key skills

- Connect a formula to the shape it draws.
- Vary a parameter and describe what changes.
- Trace values and measure change between two points.
- Compare linear, quadratic, exponential and power fits.
- Read linear and logarithmic axes with their units.

## Support at home

- Notice a curved shape outdoors and describe how its slope changes along it.
- Compare two everyday quantities, such as time and distance, and discuss what a straight-line model assumes.
- Talk about how doubling one quantity might change another, without needing a screen or buying anything.

## Starting the instrument

It opens directly on y = x^2. Drag its number tab vertically, scroll over it or focus it and use arrow keys. Type a letter in the formula to get a ruler automatically. No setup or reading is required.

## Instrument drawers

Data accepts two columns and includes NOAA annual carbon dioxide, NASA planets and Census population. Table samples any pen or the fitted curve, with start and step remembered per pen. Notebook has six setups and four save slots, kept only on this device. Manual contains grammar, measurements, limitations and source permissions. Axes sets the visible axes and the polar or parametric interval. Long tapes and small drawers scroll internally; the equation stays pinned while its rulers scroll.

Measurements occupies the tape, leaving the paper visible while changing a fit. Two columns opens for pasting or inspecting values. Drawers allow the power panel and Read to remain usable; Escape closes a drawer and Close returns focus to its handle. An open table follows changing rulers. It sits beside the tape on wide screens and above the tape on phones, leaving the active equation and rulers usable.

## Talk about what appears

Which changes leave the shape recognizable? What does a slope measure here? How does a log axis change a pattern? What can a good fit still fail to predict?

## Good to know

This is numerical, not symbolic. Undefined values stay undefined. Extremely fast oscillations can alias; dense or repeated numerical features can be missed. Exponential and power R² are measured in log space. No data leave the browser. There is no plan.md in this build; this remains a second card beside the lesson tool.

The Census series mixes historical enumeration coverage and changing boundaries. Early counts excluded many Indigenous people. The Manual names the count versions and sources; do not read the series as a fixed-boundary census of everyone living here. Source verification on 2026-11-05 corrected the spec's 1940, 1970 and 1980 values. NASA distances are rounded J2000 mean semimajor axes; approximate periods are sidereal, with Saturn rounded to 29.45. NOAA means include observations made at Maunakea during the 2022-2023 interruption. Current OpenStax pages use CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; they are references only, with no textbook content bundled.

## Palette contrast

Ratios against #f4f1e4: black 15.214:1, red 5.258:1, blue 7.094:1, green 5.720:1, violet 7.637:1, brown 6.564:1, teal 6.105:1 and orange 5.285:1. All exceed 4.5:1. Green grid lines are paper markings; dark axis labels carry the coordinates. Colour is supported by pen numbers, labels and trace selection.

## Scope

Built and cold-reviewed locally. No commits or publication. Explicit inequalities and general implicit equations remain outside this instrument, following the spec's lean. Read-only URL hashes can preload a single expression.

## Refine fixes

Literal fraction exponents reduce to lowest terms and use real roots on negative bases when the denominator is odd. Decimal exponents keep the previous domain. OpenStax College Algebra 2e 1.3 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and MathWorld Cube Root (copyright Wolfram Research) are references only; no source text or figures are bundled. Checked 6 November 2026.

Newly typed, loaded and setup curves ink with pen travel when Motion is on. A quiet synthesized scratch follows drawing after browser audio activation; Sound off stops it. Number scrubs and ruler edits draw instantly. Coarse ruler sweeps dwell on the configured step; fine steps retain smooth motion. Rose and Lissajous setups open on equal physical scales. Numerical key points settle after input and refresh at most four times a second during ruler play; intersections compare the active pen with the other pens.
