arc: model-then-make
minutes: 45
focus: VA:Cr2
goal: I can use a horizon line and one vanishing point to draw forms that recede in space, and shade them to show value.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Paper, pencils and rulers for the Make part.
grouping: Whole class for the model, then individual drawing.
---
## Before class

- Open **Find it** and pick **Railroad**.
- The paintings in **Find it** were made with an image generator, so their edges may be a little off. That's worth talking about if lines don't meet perfectly.
- Decide whether students draw on screen, on paper, or both.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Show the **Railroad** picture. "The rails are parallel in real life. Do they look parallel here?"
2. "Artists use one trick to make a flat page look deep. Today you learn it."

## Model (12 min)

1. Drag along one rail, then the other. The lines meet at one point: the vanishing point.
2. Press **Show the horizon**. "The vanishing point sits on the horizon line, the artist's eye level."
3. Switch to **Draw** and choose **Box**. Drag one box below the horizon, one above and one to the side. "Which box shows its top? Which shows its bottom?"
4. Drag the red vanishing point and watch every box turn. Move it low ("a worm's-eye view") and high ("a bird's-eye view").
5. Add a **Road** and a **Row** of posts. "What happens to the spaces between the posts as they go back?"

## Make (18 min)

1. Students draw a street scene with at least three boxes (buildings), a road and a row of posts, on screen or on paper with a ruler.
2. On paper: draw the horizon line first, then the vanishing point, then the front faces, then lines back to the point.
3. On screen: students turn off **Guides** to see the finished drawing, then turn on **Shading** for lighter tops and fronts and darker sides.
4. Paper drawers add value with their pencils the same way: light tops, darker sides.
5. Students who want a model can load **Example street** and study it first.

## Share (6 min)

1. Partners swap drawings and find the vanishing point in each other's work by tracing two edges back.
2. Two or three students show their drawing. "Where is the eye level? How can you tell?"
3. On-screen drawings can be saved with **Save picture**.

## Exit check (4 min)

On a card, draw a horizon line, a vanishing point and one box above the horizon. Shade one side darker. Boxes that show their top above the horizon show who needs the model again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** start with one **Box** and one **Road** before a full street.
- **Support:** keep **Guides** on so the construction lines stay visible.
- **Stretch:** draw a hallway with **Room** and add doors on the side walls.
- **Stretch:** "Real scenes have objects at many depths. How would you draw a box behind another box?"

## Short on time

- **Find it** with the **Railroad** (5 min).
- **Box** above and below the horizon (5 min), then the exit card (4 min).
