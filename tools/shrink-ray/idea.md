# Shrink Ray (idea card)

- Module: math-5-multiplying-and-dividing-fractions, grade 5. Codes: 5.NF.B
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good for multiplying fractions as scaling; see MOVE for fractions as division and dividing unit fractions.
- One idea: An inventor's ray multiplies whatever it hits by the number on its dial; predict before firing whether a picture will shrink or grow, then measure the new sides and tile the new area to see what multiplying by a fraction really does.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: Dr. Ines's workshop, a ray on a stand, a photo 1 unit square on the wall, and a dial set to 3/4. "Before you fire: bigger, smaller or the same?"
  - Predict rounds: the dial shows a factor (5/6, 1, 1 1/2, 7/7, 9/10) and a number (7); the student calls the result bigger, smaller or equal without multiplying, then fires. A tally tracks the rule they are forming.
  - Area rounds: the ray scales width by 2/3 and height by 3/4; the student tiles the new rectangle with 1/3 by 1/4 tiles and finds 6 tiles of 1/12, so 2/3 x 3/4 = 6/12. Then larger cases with mixed numbers.
  - Job rounds: real requests from the neighborhood, such as a mural plan scaled by 1 1/2, or a garden bed 3/4 of the old size; each one is a fraction or mixed-number product in context.
  - The aha or the end state: multiplying by less than 1 shrinks, by more than 1 grows, and (a/b) x q is a parts of q split into b pieces.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a "beat the ray" round where the class predicts sizes on the projector.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5.NF.B (5.NF.4, 5.NF.5, 5.NF.6): fraction times whole or fraction as a parts of a partition, and areas of rectangles with fractional sides by tiling (5.NF.4); multiplication as scaling, judging the product against the factor without multiplying (5.NF.5); real-world products of fractions and mixed numbers (5.NF.6).
  - MOVE 5.NF.B (5.NF.3 fraction as division, 5.NF.7 dividing unit fractions and whole numbers): new tool: a grade 5 camp-cook share-out where 3 pans shared by 4 tables gives 3/4 pan each, 1/2 pan splits among 3 tables, and the cook counts 1/4-cup scoops in 2 cups. It could share the fraction-wall bars with how-many-fit.
- Not like: how-many-fit (grade 6 dividing fractions), recipe-scaler (grade 4 whole number times fraction), blueprint-studio (grade 7 scale drawings).
- Art: generated scenes (the workshop, the ray, neighborhood jobs); people (illustrated) for Dr. Ines; scaled shapes and tiles drawn in code for exact sizes.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Predict whether the ray's fraction dial will shrink or grow a picture before you fire, then tile the new rectangle to see why 2/3 x 3/4 is 6/12, and take scaling jobs from the neighborhood.
- Flags: too thin for the whole 5.NF.B cluster (needs the MOVE above)
