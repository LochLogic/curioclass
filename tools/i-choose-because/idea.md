# I Choose Because (idea card)

- Module: eld-k-2-choosing-and-giving-reasons, grade k-2. Codes: WIDA.ELD-5, WIDA.Argue
- Format / family: showdown / eld-talk. Fit: good when solo-first: a puppet friend makes the other choice, and the class vote is the class mode.
- One idea: Your town has to pick between two things (a new playground or a bigger library, a bus stop here or there, a park clean-up day or a book swap); you choose one and say why with "I choose ___ because ___", then answer a friend's opposite choice with "I agree" or "I disagree because".
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a town meeting with two big picture choices and a friendly mayor. "Our town can do one. Which one? Why?"
  - Core loop: the student taps a choice, then builds a reason from picture-word tiles (because kids can play, because we can read more books). A puppet friend chooses the other one and says why. The student answers with a frame (I agree because..., I disagree because...). A "because" with no reason (because I want it) gets a gentle nudge from the friend: "But why is it good for the town?"
  - The aha or the end state: the town tally shows everyone's reasons, and the mayor decides based on the best reason, not the loudest; the student hears their sentence read back in full.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): projector vote, with the class saying the frame together and pairs agreeing or disagreeing.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - WIDA.ELD-5: the language of social studies: community places, helpers and choices a town makes.
  - WIDA.Argue: stating a choice, giving a reason with because, and agreeing or disagreeing politely with a reason.
- Not like: classroom-phrases-game (requests, not reasons); talk-moves-trainer (grade 3-5 group discussion moves); because-bridge (ELA, reasons from a text).
- Art: illustrated town, mayor and puppet friend (people, illustrated).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Your town can only do one thing (a playground or a library, a clean-up or a book swap); choose and say why with I choose ___ because ___, then agree or disagree with a friend's choice and give a reason.
- Flags: home-language support matters for K-2 newcomers (open glossary question, not decided); overlap risk (because-bridge, keep the town and community content); K-2 span check (gf-04).
