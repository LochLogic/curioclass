# Aquarium Builder (idea card)

- Module: math-7-surface-area-and-volume-of-prisms, grade 7. Codes: 7.G.B
- Format / family: builder / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Plan composite prism tanks and planter boxes: buy the glass (surface area) and fill the water (volume) for shapes built from cubes and right prisms.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an aquarium shop order for an L-shaped tank. "How much glass, and how much water?"
  - Core loop: plan composite prism tanks and planter boxes built from cubes and right prisms; buy the glass (surface area) and fill the water (volume).
  - The aha or the end state: the student finds both measures for a shape they designed.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 7.G.B (7.G.6): area, volume and surface area of objects made of triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons, cubes and right prisms.
- Not like: net-folder (grade 6 nets); big-wheel-small-wheel (circles).
- Art: generated scenes (glass tanks), because water level and glass must read clearly.
