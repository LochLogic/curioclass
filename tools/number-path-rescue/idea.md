# Number Path Rescue (idea card)

- Module: math-1-counting-to-120, grade 1. Codes: 1.NBT.A
- Format / family: race / number-path. Fit: good, with a calm option: the rising river is gentle and can be turned off, since a timer can freeze a six-year-old.
- One idea: Stepping stones on a 120 chart have washed away; type the missing numbers to rebuild the path before the river rises, and the chart's patterns (down one row is ten more) make you faster.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a riverbank laid out as a 1 to 120 chart of stepping stones, a duck family waiting to cross. "Some stones are missing. Tap one and type its number."
  - Core loop: the child taps a blank stone and types its numeral on a keypad. Later paths start in the middle (count on from 87) and run past 100 (101, not 1001: the tool shows "one hundred one" and its digits side by side).
  - Fish baskets: a basket of up to 120 fish in rows; the child counts and writes how many to buy a stone.
  - The aha or the end state: the child notices a missing stone under 46 must be 56 and fills a whole column fast; a tally shows how the patterns cut their time.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): race a ghost of your best run; calm mode with no river.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 1.NBT.A: counting to 120 from any starting number, reading and writing numerals (typing the missing stones, including 101 to 120), and writing the numeral for a counted set (the fish baskets).
- Not like: hundred-hop (kindergarten, counting aloud to 100 on a path), elevator-hundred (adding tens and ones in a hundred-chart building), place-value-vault (grade 2, to 1000).
- Art: generated scenes (river, stones, ducks); chart numbers drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Stones on a 1 to 120 river path have washed away; type the missing numbers before the river rises. Start anywhere, cross 100 without writing 1001, and use the chart's patterns to go faster.
- Flags: overlap risk (hundred-hop shares the number-path engine; vary the look and keep this one about reading and writing numerals)
