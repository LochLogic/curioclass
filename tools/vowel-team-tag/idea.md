# Vowel Team Tag (idea card)

- Module: ela-2-long-vowels-and-two-syllable-words, grade 2. Codes: RF.2.3
- Format / family: race / none. Fit: good, with a gentle timer that can be turned off (accuracy first).
- One idea: Vowel team pairs (ai, ee, oa) run through a playground maze and the child steers them to tag the words they belong in, reading each tagged word aloud, so the child learns which letters team up to make which long vowel sound and then uses that to read longer words.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the ai team at the gate. "Find every word where ai says its name. Tag it!"
  - Core loop: steer a vowel team through the maze; words sit on platforms with a gap (r _ _ n); tagging fills the gap and the word is read aloud for the child to echo. Levels: short or long (hop or hope); vowel teams (ai, ay, ee, ea, oa, ow); two-syllable words (rain + bow); prefix and suffix gates (un-, re-, -ful, -less) that split the word before the team reads it; odd-pattern words (head, bread: ea that does not say its name); tricky words on a rule-breaker wall.
  - The aha or the end state: a sort board shows every word the team tagged grouped by spelling, and the child sees that ee, ea and e_e can all make one sound.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): timer on for a race; teacher word lists.
- Standards, one line per code: RF.2.3: long and short vowels in one-syllable words; vowel team spellings; two-syllable words with long vowels; common prefixes and suffixes; spellings that do not follow the usual sound; irregular grade-level words.
- Not like: Blend Bakery (grade 1 blending). Syllable Surgeon (grade 3 multisyllable and Latin suffixes). Word Lab (grade 2 affixes for meaning, not decoding).
- Art: generated scenes (a bright playground maze and vowel team characters), because the teams are the characters a child remembers.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Steer vowel teams (ai, ee, oa) through a playground maze to tag the words they belong in, read each one aloud, and work up to two-syllable words, prefixes and suffixes, and rule-breaker words.
- Flags: none
