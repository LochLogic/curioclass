# Essay Rope Team (idea card)

- Module: ela-5-organized-essays, grade 5. Codes: W.5.1, W.5.2
- Format / family: builder / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: Each paragraph is a climber on a rope team going up a mountain, and each transition is the rope between them; the student orders the climbers so each one leads to the next and ties strong ropes (consequently, in contrast, especially), and a weak rope lets a climber slip back, so they learn that an essay holds together by its order and its links.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a mountain, a base camp, and five paragraph climbers milling around. "Get your essay to the summit. Who goes first?"
  - Core loop: Opinion climb: line up the intro with the opinion, reasons in a logical order each with facts, and a conclusion; tie each rope with a linking word, phrase or clause that fits (for example, specifically, as a result). Explain climb: an informative essay where the student groups facts under headings, adds a definition or a quote, picks precise subject words, and ropes the sections together (in contrast, especially). Then the climb runs: a wrong order or a rope that doesn't fit the link makes a climber slip and says why.
  - The aha or the end state: at the summit, the essay reads aloud top to bottom; the student then writes their own short essay in the same frames and sends it up.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print the finished essay.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - W.5.1: the opinion climb: logical order of reasons with facts, linking words, phrases and clauses, and a conclusion.
  - W.5.2: the explain climb: a focus, grouped sections with headings, facts, definitions and quotes, precise words, linking across sections, and a conclusion.
- Not like: sentence-train (grade k, a train of words; the old train metaphor repeated it); hamburger-paragraph (grade 2, one paragraph); blueprint-builder (grade 4, reading structures).
- Art: illustrated climbers and mountain scenes, because the slip and the summit carry the feedback.
- Proposed title: Essay Rope Team (Essay Train repeats sentence-train's coupled-cars idea)
- Proposed concept line: Paragraphs are climbers on a rope team and transitions are the ropes; order them so each leads to the next and tie links that fit, or a climber slips, in both an opinion essay and an explaining essay.
- Flags: none
