# Expression Match (idea card)

- Module: math-7-equivalent-expressions, grade 7. Codes: 7.EE.A
- Format / family: cards / algebra-tiles. Fit: good, once matching is earned by rewriting, not by memory alone.
- One idea: Play a card game against a rival where a match only counts if you make it: use move cards (distribute, factor out, combine like terms) to rewrite your expression into one on the table, and story cards show what each form tells you (a + 0.05a is the price plus tax, 1.05a is 105% of it).
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a game table, a rival named Jonah, five face-up expression cards (4x - 6, 1.05a, 2(l + w)...) and the student's hand (2(2x - 3), a + 0.05a, 1/2(8x - 12)...).
  - Core loop: the student picks a hand card and plays move cards on it; each move animates on algebra tiles, including negative tiles. When the result matches a table card, the pair is won. Illegal moves are shown as wrong with a counterexample value.
  - Rational coefficient levels: 1/2(8x - 12), -3(x - 4), 0.75y - 0.25y; factoring out negatives and fractions.
  - Story cards: some table cards carry a context. a + 0.05a and 1.05a ("the price grew by 5%"); 2l + 2w and 2(l + w) (the fence around a pen); the student picks which form answers a given question faster.
  - Jonah plays too, and sometimes cheats: the student challenges a bad match by plugging in a value.
  - The aha or the end state: equivalent forms are the same quantity, and each form tells a different part of the story.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): two students on one device.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 7.EE.A: adding, subtracting, factoring and expanding linear expressions with rational coefficients using properties (7.EE.1); rewriting expressions in context to show how quantities relate, such as a + 0.05a = 1.05a (7.EE.2).
- Not like: expression-garden (same tile engine, grade 6 whole numbers and exponents), algebra-tile-studio (grade 9 quadratics), equation-golf (grade 9 solving equations).
- Art: people (illustrated) for Jonah; generated scene for the game table; cards and tiles drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Win pairs against a rival by rewriting your expression into one on the table with move cards (distribute, factor, combine), then read what each form tells you, like a + 0.05a and 1.05a for a 5% raise.
- Flags: none
