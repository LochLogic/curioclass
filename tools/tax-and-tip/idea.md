# Tax and Tip (idea card)

- Module: math-7-proportional-relationships, grade 7. Codes: 7.RP.A
- Format / family: tycoon / ratio. Fit: good.
- One idea: Run the register at a family diner for a week of shifts, where tax and tips are proportional to the bill (a straight line through the origin) and a flat service fee is not, and every receipt is a percent problem with a constant you can find.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a diner counter at breakfast, a register, and the owner, Mr. Haddad. "Tax here is 8%. Ring up table 3."
  - Register rounds: the student rings up bills with tax, discounts (25% off for seniors), and tips (15%, 20%). Each receipt adds a point to a graph of bill vs total. Tax and tip points line up through the origin; the student names the constant (total = 1.08 x bill) and what the point (1, 1.08) means.
  - Proportional or not: a catering order with a flat $5 delivery fee makes a line that misses the origin; the student tests tables and graphs and says which relationships are proportional.
  - Unit-rate rounds: the delivery cyclist rides 1/2 mile every 1/4 hour; the cook uses 3/4 cup of batter per 1/2 batch. The student finds unit rates from fractions.
  - Back-office rounds: menu prices go up 6% (percent increase); a server's mental tip vs the exact tip (percent error); the owner's loan for a new grill at simple interest; a supplier's markup.
  - The aha or the end state: a proportional relationship has one constant that runs through every receipt, and a fixed fee breaks it.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a rush-hour round on the projector.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 7.RP.A: unit rates from ratios of fractions (7.RP.1); proportional relationships tested in tables and graphs, the constant of proportionality, equations like t = 1.08b, and the meaning of (1, r) (7.RP.2); multistep percent problems with tax, tips, discounts, markups, simple interest, percent change and percent error (7.RP.3).
- Not like: smoothie-lab (same ratio family, grade 6 equivalent ratios), grocery-run (grade 5 decimals and budget), lemonade-stand (grade 6 dependent variables).
- Art: generated scenes (the diner, plates, the register); people (illustrated) for Mr. Haddad, staff and varied customers; receipts and graph drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Run a diner register for a week: tax and tips line up through the origin while a flat fee does not; find the constant in every receipt, then work discounts, price rises, percent error and a simple-interest loan.
- Flags: none (teacher note: tipping customs vary by country)
