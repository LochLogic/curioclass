# Bedtime Retell (idea card)

- Module: eld-k-2-retelling-stories, grade k-2. Codes: WIDA.ELD-2, WIDA.Narrate
- Format / family: sandbox / story-stage. Fit: good: retelling with picture cards and a puppet is open play with a gentle goal.
- One idea: A sleepy puppet friend hears a short picture story, then asks you to tell it again; you put the picture cards in order and retell beginning, middle and end with frames (First... Then... At the end...), and the puppet only drifts off to sleep when your retell has all three parts.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a cozy bedroom at night, a yawning puppet in bed. "Read me the story? Then tell it back to me."
  - Core loop: the story is read aloud with pictures (Help set to Lots, Some or Little, mapped to WIDA levels). Then the puppet asks: "What happened first?" The student drags a picture card into the first slot and picks or says the frame ("First, the cat lost her hat."). The puppet asks "Then what?" for the middle and "How did it end?" for the end. A card out of order makes the puppet sit up, puzzled: "Wait, she found it before she lost it?"
  - The aha or the end state: with beginning, middle and end in order, the puppet hears the whole retell read back in the student's chosen words and falls asleep; the student can record their own retell and play it to the puppet (kept on this device only, the screen says so).
  - Class mode or extras (optional): new stories on later nights; at Little help, the frames fade to just "First" and a blank.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - WIDA.ELD-2: the language of language arts: story words, characters and events in a read-aloud.
  - WIDA.Narrate: retelling beginning, middle and end in order with sequence words and past-tense frames.
- Not like: story-stage (K, teacher-led folktale staging with backdrops); classroom-phrases-game (classroom requests); i-choose-because (reasons, not sequence).
- Art: illustrated puppet, bedroom and story pictures (people, illustrated), warm night palette.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: home-language support matters here (newcomer K-2 learners; depends on Cory's open glossary question, not decided); mic is optional with the on-device line (rule 7), no speech recognition; K-2 span check (gf-04).
