# Letter Lanterns

## One idea
A dark village of 26 sleeping lantern creatures wakes one letter at a time: each lantern lights when the child finds it by its sound, says it, and traces it, and the lit lanterns then hang over shop signs the child can sound out, so the glowing village is a picture of every letter and word the child knows.

## Not like
- rhyme-time-rumble: sounds with no letters (rhyme, beats, first sounds). Here every sound is tied to a printed letter.
- big-book-pointer: letter names and print rules (left to right, word gaps). Here letters are learned by their sounds and read in words.
- sentence-train: spells cargo by sound (encoding) and owns L.K.1. Here the child decodes print into sound; tracing letters is the only overlap.
- blend-bakery (grade 1): digraphs, silent-e, vowel teams. Here single letters, CVC words and the open/closed long-short contrast only; silent-e is left to it.
- sight-words and nonsense-words (grade 2 cards): plain drill lists. Here 10 sight words live on village banners inside a story world.
- calm-corner: traces a star for breathing. Here tracing is checked stroke by stroke for start point, direction and coverage.

## Who and how
Kindergarten, ELA (Foundational Skills). Driver: teacher on a projector or smartboard, the class answering out loud; works the same for one child alone at home (every prompt and choice spoken). 10 to 20 minutes a sitting; progress saves on the device, so a class wakes a few lanterns a day. Standards: RF.K.3 (a, b, c, d). L.K.1a is taught here in the tracing step but stays on sentence-train's module (audit gaps.md row 14). No personal prompts.

## Screens and modes
Header: kit Motion, Sound, Read; mode buttons **Wake** and **Village**. Panel on the right (hint, then buttons).

**On load.** Wake mode, the village square at night: three sleeping lanterns on a cart in the center (m, s, a), an empty string across the top, and a big gold **Wake the village** button over the cart. Nothing speaks (kit rule). Hint: "Tap Wake the village to start." First tap on it: the button goes, the welcome and the first Find line play as one sequence ("Welcome to the lantern village..." then "Which lantern says mmm? Tap it."); the hint changes to the Find line at once. A first tap on a lantern instead starts Find m directly (that tap counts as an answer). A saved device skips the button and resumes at its step, hint first. First tap on Village: the street, its welcome. On Read: the hint.

**Wake (main path).** Focus: the lantern(s) the step is about, center stage, biggest thing on screen. At 1366x768: header 56 px; stage about 1030x712 at left; panel 320 px at right. Cart lanterns 200x260 px each (letters 110 px on the glass); the lit string across the top shows each lit lantern at 56 px. Panel: hint (24 px), **Hear it again** (replays the current question), **Next** (shown only when a step is done), **Teacher** (opens settings). Everything above the fold; no scroll. At 680x425: panel becomes a 150 px column of icon+word buttons with a 2-line hint (15 px); cart lanterns 120x156 (targets stay over 60 px); the string shows a count chip ("9 lit") instead of tiny lanterns.

Steps for each new lantern, in ORDER (Content):
1. **Find.** Three sleeping lanterns: the target plus two unlit letters from the next six in ORDER (never two of b, d, p, q together on a first meeting). Right: eyes open, glow, chime, the intro line ("Mmm. M says mmm, like moth."). Wrong: that lantern stirs and says its own sound, the question repeats; after a second miss the right lantern pulses.
2. **Say.** The awake lantern alone, center. The class says its sound, then taps it to hear it.
3. **Vowels only: two glows.** Small round flame (with the short anchor, the creature itself) and tall flame (with the long anchor picture) sit beside the lantern, 90 px each. Tap each to hear it, then "Which flame says ā? Tap it." Shapes and pictures differ, not only color.
4. **Trace.** The scene dims; a 420x420 slate (top line, dashed midline, baseline, descender) holds the capital, then the small letter. One stroke shows at a time: green start dot, numbered arrow, faint path. Buttons: **Show me** (animates this stroke), **Clear** (wipes this stroke). A passed stroke turns gold; the next appears. After both letters: the lantern brightens, flies to the string, and the panel shows **Write it yourself** and **Next**.
5. **Events.** Up to three ready events play after the lantern (EVENTS in Content), the street panning to each: Sign, Twins, Long/short or Banner. Every fourth lantern adds a Lantern check. Then the next lantern's Find.

- **Sign.** The shop's blank sign with its lit lanterns hanging over the letters. Tap a lantern: its sound. Below the sign, three painted pictures (110 px). Pick the right one: the shop opens (lit window, goods inside), the narrator reads the word. Wrong: the picture names itself ("That's a mop."), and the lantern that differs flickers if it is a twin. The whole word is never spoken before the right pick.
- **Twins.** Two open shops side by side. Tap the lantern that differs: it flickers on both signs, both sounds and both words play. Then "Which sign says cot?" Tap a sign.
- **Long/short.** Two word boards (no, not) with the vowel lantern over each; its flame shows tall on one, small on the other. The narrator explains, then asks for one.
- **Banner.** A new banner unrolls with a whole word; the narrator models it and asks the class to say it. No check here; checks come in Lantern checks.
- **Lantern check.** Three Finds on the lit lanterns with the lowest glow (ties: lit earliest), choices from lit letters; one See and say; one Banner find if two or more banners are up. First-try Finds feed glow (Interactions).

**Village (explore).** Focus: the street, one quarter of a long panorama at a time (four streets of six shops, banners between), filling the stage. Lit lanterns hang along the top with their glow level. Tap a lantern: sound and intro. Tap an open shop: its word, goods glow. Tap a banner: its word. Tap a closed shop: the asleep line. Panel: hint, **Read the street**, **Stop** (shown while reading), **Lantern check**, arrows **Back street** / **Next street** (also at the stage edges, 64 px), **Teacher**. At 680x425 the street shows two shops at a time with the arrows. Read the street: each open sign and banner on this street in turn gets a gold outline and "Read it!", 3 s for the class, then the narrator reads it; about 6 to 8 items, about 30 to 40 s for a full street (it is the class reading finale, so the length is on purpose). A tap on any shop, banner, arrow or Stop acts at once; reading continues from what was tapped.

**Outcome picture (watch it change).** Each lantern shows one of four states, each different at a glance: asleep (sleepy pose, dark, no halo), awake (awake pose, small halo, 1 star under it), glowing (bigger halo, 2 stars), bright (full halo, 3 stars, faint sparkle unless Motion is off). Shops: closed (dark window, door shut, blank sign) or open (warm window, goods visible, word on sign). The end state: every lantern lit, every shop open, the narrator's end line, then Village.

**Teacher (overlay).** 26 letter toggles (60 px), 10 sight-word toggles, **Wake all** (lights every letter at glow 1, opens every shop, for a demo), **Start over** (asks once, then clears), privacy line: "Lanterns are saved on this device only. Nothing leaves it." Off letters are skipped in Wake and checks; shops and banners that need an off letter stay hidden.

## Interactions in detail
- **Trace check** (per stroke, all in slate heights h, the top line to baseline distance): pass when the first point is within .16h of the green dot, the last point within .20h of the stroke end, at least 80% of path samples (every .01h) lie within .12h of the ink, at least 75% of ink points lie within .14h of the path, and direction holds (taking every 6th ink point of the middle 80%, at least 60% of moves go forward along the path). Dots: a tap or mark within .12h. Write it yourself: only the lines and stroke 1's start dot show; tolerances .16h for coverage and start, .18h for ink on path, .20h for the end; the direction rule is unchanged. Ink is a 14 px round line; pointer and touch both work; one finger only.
- **Misses** (per stroke): miss by start: "Start at the green dot." Miss by end or coverage: "Follow the arrow to the end." Each cause's line plays once per stroke, then hint only. After the third miss on one stroke, Show me plays on its own, the stroke fills gold and moves on ("Let's do it together." once per letter). No letter is ever failed; the lantern still lights.
- **Write it yourself** is optional and never blocks; a pass adds a small gold star on that lantern's trace badge; a miss shows the model over the child's ink and offers **Try again** or **Next**.
- **Glow**: lit = level 1 (awake). In Lantern checks, two first-try Finds in a row for a letter raise its level by 1 (max 3, bright); a miss resets that streak, never the level. The first-meeting Find never counts. Village **Lantern check** runs the same round anytime.
- **Taps while anything plays**: every tap acts at once; a new step drops lines that have not started (V.dropQueued), Read the street moves its outline to what was tapped. Taps on the cart during the welcome count as answers.
- **Hear it again** replays the current question (Find sound, picture question, twin question); disabled during Trace (the hint names Show me there).
- **Extremes**: all letters toggled off: Wake shows "Turn on a letter in Teacher." with the Teacher button; one letter on: Find uses lit letters as distractors, and with none, two from the full ORDER. All 26 lit: Wake shows the end card with **Go to Village** and **Lantern check**. Wake all then Start over returns to the load screen. Saved data that fails to read starts fresh, silently.
- **Save** (localStorage `curio-letter-lanterns`, try/catch): lit letters, glow and streaks, opened shops, banners, event queue, step, toggles.
