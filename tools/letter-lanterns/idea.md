# Letter Lanterns (idea card)

- Module: ela-k-letter-sounds-and-sight-words, grade k. Codes: RF.K.3
- Format / family: creature / letter-pals. Fit: good: each lantern creature wakes when the child gives it its sound, and the village at night shows what the child knows.
- One idea: A dark village wakes one lantern creature at a time: each letter glows when the child matches it to its sound, vowels learn two glows (short and long), and lit lanterns string together into the first words the child can read on the village signs.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a dark street, one sleepy lantern (m). "Mmm. Which lantern says mmm? Tap it."
  - Core loop: hear a sound, tap the lantern; a lit lantern moves into the village. Vowel lanterns get a short glow and a long glow. Lit lanterns hang on strings to spell CVC words on shop signs. Sight-word signs (the, a, I, see, my) must be read whole to open a shop. Twin signs (cat and cot) flicker the one lantern that differs and play both sounds.
  - The aha or the end state: the whole alphabet village lit at night, every shop sign readable, and the child reads the sign street aloud.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher sets which letters and sight words are in play; tracing a letter relights its lantern (letter printing, see the sentence-train card).
- Standards, one line per code: RF.K.3: consonant sounds (lantern matching), short and long vowel sounds (two glows), high-frequency words by sight (shop signs), similarly spelled words told apart by the letter that differs (twin signs).
- Not like: Rhyme Time Rumble (sounds without letters). Big Book Pointer (letter names and print mechanics). Blend Bakery (grade 1 digraphs and decoding).
- Art: generated scenes (a warm night village with 26 distinct lantern creatures), because the creatures carry the whole tool.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Wake each letter lantern by matching it to its sound; vowels learn a short and a long glow, and lit lanterns spell shop signs, sight words and look-alike word pairs across a glowing alphabet village.
- Flags: none
