Curio Class Curiosities · Letter Lanterns

Manual

Choose a lantern and listen. Trace its big or small letter, compare the two vowel glows, or open the Wordbook. Everything is available in any order. No microphone is used.

The slate

Follow the green dot and arrow. Lift between strokes. Show me draws a stroke. Next stroke and Back to lantern are always available. Write it yourself hides the guides; if a mark leaves the trail, the whole letter appears over your ink. Nothing is graded.

The village

Lanterns glow when you tap them, simply to show where you have explored. They do not measure reading ability. Shops open when you choose to reveal their windows. Tap the small lanterns above signs to hear their sounds. Banners speak as whole words.

Sound models

Isolated sounds use recordings. Without a recording, you hear an anchor word instead of a letter name. This village uses US English examples. A voice cannot check what you say. The small flame shows the short vowel; the tall flame shows the long vowel. The long sound is the letter name. C and g use their hard sounds here. X uses the last sounds in ox; q is shown with the sound in quail.

Things people have found

Pan and pin share their outside letters. Cat and cot change in the middle. The p and g in pig hang below the line. No and not have different vowel sounds. The banner I has bars; the small l is one tall stroke.

Keyboard

Tab moves between controls; Enter activates them. Scroll the letter tray to choose any letter. On the slate, Space starts ink, arrow keys move it, and Space lifts it. Shift makes smaller moves. Escape closes a drawer. Motion removes movement without changing sound timing.

Privacy

Your village is saved on this device only. Nothing leaves it. Settings can hide letters and banners, light the whole village, or clear the saved village.
