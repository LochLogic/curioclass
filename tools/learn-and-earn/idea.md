# Learn and Earn (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-4-learn-and-earn, grade 4. Codes: D2.Eco.6.3-5, NSPFE.Earning Income
- Format / family: tycoon / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Run a kid's bike repair business for a year, spending some weeks learning new skills or saving for better tools instead of fixing bikes, and compare the bikes fixed and money earned with a friend who never trains.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a garage with a toolbox and a line of broken bikes; Sam, the friend, has a garage across the street. "Each week, choose: fix bikes, take a class, or save for a tool."
  - Core loop: a 52-week calendar. Fixing earns now; a class (wheel truing, brake repair) earns nothing that week but unlocks faster or higher-paying jobs; a tool speeds every job after. Sam always fixes and never trains.
  - Earning income lens: job cards show how pay depends on skill, how wages differ from tips and selling, and that people choose careers by what they like, what they can learn and what pays.
  - The aha or the end state: the year's chart shows Sam ahead early and the student ahead later, so learning costs time now and raises income later.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): compare class strategies.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Eco.6.3-5: the chart links investment in skills to productivity and future income.
  - NSPFE.Earning Income: job cards and the year's choices cover how people earn income, why pay varies with skills and education, and choosing a career (grade 4 outcomes to be matched at spec).
- Not like: paper-plane-assembly-line (team specialization); skateboard-fund-chances (saving and risk).
- Art: people (illustrated) and generated scenes, because the two kids' garages should feel lived in.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (paper-plane-assembly-line also covers productivity)
