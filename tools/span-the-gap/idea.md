# Bridge Builder (idea card)

- Module: science-8-engineering-design, grade 8. Codes: MS-ETS1-1, MS-ETS1-2, MS-ETS1-3, MS-ETS1-4
- Format / family: builder / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Write the problem first (a farm road must cross a creek: what must it carry, what must it not harm), then design truss bridges on a budget, test them with trucks to find the weakest member, and compare designs against the criteria to combine the best parts.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a creek with a washed-out crossing and three people with needs (a farmer with a loaded grain truck, a fish biologist who needs the stream bed left alone, a county engineer with a budget).
  - Core loop: the student turns those needs into criteria and constraints (load, span, cost, no piers in the stream). Then build: connect wood or steel members into a truss; costs add up. Test with a light truck, then a heavy one; members glow from green to red with strain and the first to fail snaps. Save each design, change it, and test again.
  - Compare step: a decision table scores up to three saved designs against the criteria; the student picks the strongest features of each (a deeper truss here, steel in the bottom chord there) and builds one final bridge.
  - The aha or the end state: the best bridge is the one that meets the criteria for the people who use it, found by testing and improving, not the strongest or cheapest alone.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-ETS1-1: the student writes the criteria and constraints from the people's needs.
  - MS-ETS1-2: the decision table evaluates competing designs against the criteria.
  - MS-ETS1-3: test data from several designs identify the best features to combine.
  - MS-ETS1-4: the simulator is the model that generates data for repeated testing and improvement.
- Not like: design-challenge-studio (grade 12 open global challenges); zipline-energy (grade 11 energy design); three-pigs-lab (grade 2 material tests).
- Art: bridge and strain colors drawn in code; generated scenes for the creek valley and illustrated people for the three users.
- Proposed title: Span the Gap (the current title is the name of a known commercial game)
- Proposed concept line: Write the criteria for a farm-road creek crossing with the people who use it, design truss bridges on a budget, test them with trucks to find the weakest member, then compare designs and combine the best parts.
- Flags: none
