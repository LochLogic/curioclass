arc: teach-then-practice
minutes: 35
focus: ACTFL.Novice Low; WRS.Communication
goal: I can form the ASL manual alphabet, fingerspell my name with a smooth rhythm, and read short fingerspelled words.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. A list of students' names.
grouping: Whole class, then pairs.
---
## Before class

- Open **Alphabet**.
- The drawings show a right-handed signer from the front. **Left-handed** flips every hand.
- The drawings are not a signing model. Plan to show video of Deaf signers fingerspelling too.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Fingerspell your own name slowly. "What do you think I just did?"
2. "Signers use fingerspelling for names, places and words without a sign. Today you learn to spell your name in ASL."

## Teach (10 min)

1. In **Alphabet**, tap **A**, read how to make it, and have everyone copy. Tap through the letters in small groups.
2. Tap **M** and compare it with the look-alike letters outlined in orange: A, E, M, N, S and T are all fists.
3. Show **J** and **Z**, which move. The drawings show the path.
4. Teach good form: the hand stays in one place near the shoulder, the rhythm is even, and double letters slide or bounce once.

## Practice (11 min)

1. Switch to **Spell it**, type a student's name, and press **Fingerspell it**. Everyone spells along at **Slow**, then **Steady**.
2. Use the **Letter** buttons to step through one shape at a time for tricky names.
3. Pairs type their own names, practice, then fingerspell to each other at Steady speed. "Can your partner read it?"
4. Turn on **Hide the letters** and play a few classmates' names as a reading check.

## Apply (6 min)

1. Switch to **Read it** and pick **Short words**. The class watches, then calls out what they saw.
2. Each word read the first time speeds the next one up. Watch the speed climb.
3. Talk: "Did you read each letter, or the shape of the whole word?"

## Exit check (4 min)

Each student fingerspells their name to you or a partner at Steady speed. Watch for a still hand, an even rhythm and clear fist letters.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** practice the fist letters in **Read it** with **One letter**.
- **Support:** spell only the first three letters of a name at Slow speed.
- **Stretch:** try **Names** and **Places** in **Read it** and beat your best speed.
- **Stretch:** "When would a signer fingerspell instead of using a sign?"

## Short on time

- Teach the alphabet in **Alphabet** (6 min).
- Names in **Spell it** (6 min), then fingerspell your name (3 min).
