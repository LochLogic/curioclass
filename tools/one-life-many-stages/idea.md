# One Life, Many Stages (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-12-lifespan-development, grade 12. Codes: APA.Development and Learning
- Format / family: timeline / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Follow one person from infancy to old age through real developmental research (attachment, Piaget's stages, adolescence, aging), predicting each stage before the research shows it.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a baby photo album. "Predict: what will Mai do at 8 months when her parent leaves the room?"
  - Core loop: follow one person from infancy to old age through real developmental research (attachment, Piaget's stages, adolescence, aging), predicting each stage before the research shows it.
  - The aha or the end state: development is physical, cognitive and social, at every age.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - APA.Development and Learning (lifespan half): physical, cognitive and social development from infancy to old age. The learning half stays with train-your-dog.
