# Printing Press (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-7-renaissance-and-reformation, grade 7. Codes: D2.His.2.6-8, D2.His.3.6-8
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Run a 1500s print shop in a German town: set type, ink and pull pages, and ship pamphlets to other cities while a scribe next door copies the same text by hand; a map shows the idea racing across Europe, and the student then asks questions about Gutenberg, printers, readers and Luther to decide why the press mattered and what it did not change.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a print shop and a scribe's desk side by side, each with a copy counter.
  - Core loop: choose what to print (a Bible page, a pamphlet in German, a woodcut broadsheet), set type, run the press, and pick cities to ship to; copies spread on the map while the scribe's single copy crawls. Costs, paper supply and readers in each city matter.
  - The aha or the end state: a change and continuity board (cheaper books, faster spread, common languages in print; most people still could not read, and pictures and preachers still carried ideas aloud), and a question builder about the people involved that leads to a written judgment of significance.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): two rival print shops.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.2.6-8: the change and continuity board after the run.
  - D2.His.3.6-8: the question builder about Gutenberg, printers, readers and Luther, leading to the significance judgment.
- Not like: house-of-wisdom (hand copying and translation, earlier); colonial-newsroom (writing slanted headlines); mongol-messenger (moving messages, not copies).
- Art: public domain woodcuts and printed pages (Met open access and Library of Congress, to check at spec) plus a generated print shop scene.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Run a 1500s print shop against a scribe copying by hand: set type, print pamphlets and ship them across Europe, then ask who made the change matter and sort what printing changed and what stayed the same.
- Flags: sensitive (Reformation religious conflict shown fairly to all sides; fresh review); needs a data source (copy counts and speeds, checked)
