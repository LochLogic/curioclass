# Why the Pony Express Ended (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-5-why-the-pony-express-ended, grade 5. Codes: D2.His.16.3-5, D3.3.3-5, D3.4.3-5, D4.1.3-5
- Format / family: mystery / none. Fit: good
- One idea: A station keeper's desk holds evidence cards about why the Pony Express shut down after about 18 months (1860 to 1861); the student builds a claim, pins at least two sources to each reason, and finds that the famous answer (the telegraph) is only half the story once the cost ledger shows the company losing money from the start.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a relay station at dusk, a locked case file ("Why did the riders stop?") and a desk of face-down cards.
  - Core loop: open cards (a rider's letter, a telegraph company notice, a cost ledger, a newspaper item, a route map); highlight the line that matters; drag it under a claim stem ("The Pony Express ended because..."). Each reason needs two cards from different kinds of sources before it locks.
  - The aha or the end state: a skeptical newspaper editor challenges the claim ("The telegraph alone explains it!"); the student answers by pointing to a card. Strong claims name more than one cause.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print the finished claim as a one-page argument.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.16.3-5: the claim the student finishes about why the service ended.
  - D3.3.3-5: the two-source rule for each reason, drawn from different cards.
  - D3.4.3-5: the claim is rewritten as evidence is added; weak reasons drop out.
  - D4.1.3-5: the editor's challenge, answered with the claim plus cited cards.
- Not like: evidence-corkboard (grade 8, adds what evidence cannot show); resource-detective (grade 2 mystery, traces objects); mongol-messenger (relay riders as a race, not an argument). Vary the look: station ledger and lantern light, not a detective office.
- Art: public domain (Pony Express posters and 1860 to 1861 newspaper pages, Library of Congress; items to find and check at spec) plus an illustrated rider and station keeper.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (real 1860 to 1861 documents and the company's finances; any card written for the game is labeled "made for this game")
