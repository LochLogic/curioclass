## A 5-minute start

1. Open **How do I feel?** and tap **Before**. Children show which face matches their body right now; tap the face most of the class picks.
2. Go back to **All tools** and choose **Balloon breathing**. Hands on bellies; breathe in as the balloon grows and out as it shrinks. Five breaths.
3. Open **How do I feel?** again, tap **After**, and compare. Did the tool help?

## The tools

- **Balloon breathing:** five slow belly breaths. The balloon grows while you breathe in and shrinks while you breathe out, with a count on screen. **Slow** is 3 seconds in and 4 out; **Slower** is 4 in and 5 out.
- **Star breathing:** a dot traces a star. Breathe in on the way up to each point and out on the way down. Children trace in the air with one finger.
- **Slow counting:** count up to 10 in a whisper while a rainbow fills, then back down to 1, more slowly.
- **Stretch with Shelly:** five slow stretches shown by a plain figure: reach for the sky, lean like a tree, turtle tuck, shoulder rolls, squeeze and let go.
- **Five senses:** 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, and 1 big slow breath. Tap **Found one!** as children share.
- **How do I feel?:** four faces (very upset, wiggly, okay, calm) before and after a tool. Nothing is saved.

## Teaching tips

- Practice when the class is calm. A strategy learned on a good day is ready on a hard one.
- Name it every time: "We're using balloon breathing to help our bodies calm down."
- Let children choose their favorite and add it to your classroom calm corner.
- **Calm music** adds a soft background pad. **Motion** off keeps the same timing with no movement, for children who find movement distracting.

## Talk about it

- Which tool helped your body the most? How could you tell?
- When might you use star breathing at home?
- What does your body feel like when you're upset? When you're calm?

## Good to know

- If a child stays very upset, a calm tool is not a substitute for a grown-up's help. Follow up quietly.
- Nothing is typed, saved or sent anywhere.
