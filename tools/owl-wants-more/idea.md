# Owl Wants More (idea card)

- Module: ela-k-2-adding-details-and-fixing-sentences, grade k-2. Codes: W.K.5, W.1.5, RF.1.1, W.2.5
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good: the owl is the peer who asks, so a child alone still gets questions and suggestions.
- One idea: A short story looks thin, and a curious owl keeps asking for more (What color? Where? How did it feel?); every detail the child adds grows into the picture, and a last polish fixes capitals and end marks before the owl reads the better story aloud, so the child sees revising as answering a reader's questions.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a plain picture and a thin story ("The dog ran."). The owl blinks: "Which dog? Where did it run?"
  - Core loop: the owl asks one question at a time; the child picks or types a detail (a big brown dog, to the pond), and the picture adds it. At grade 2 the child writes their own short story first and the owl asks about it. Then the polish step: the owl reads with no capitals or end marks and runs out of breath; the child fixes the first word and each end.
  - The aha or the end state: the owl reads the thin story and the grown story one after the other, and the picture shows the difference.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): print both versions; story kept on this device only, with a note saying so.
  - Grade levels: K (pick details from pictures), 1 (type detail words; fix capitals and end marks), 2 (revise and edit your own short story).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - W.K.5 and W.1.5: answering the owl's questions by adding details.
  - RF.1.1: the polish step (first word capital, end mark, what a sentence looks like).
  - W.2.5: revising and editing the child's own story with the owl's questions.
- Not like: My Story Strip (kindergarten drawing and telling). Sentence Train (building one sentence). Editors in the Margin (grades 9-12 revision).
- Art: generated scenes (a picture that grows with each detail) and an illustrated owl, because the growing picture is the reward.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: A short story looks thin; a curious owl keeps asking (What color? Where? How did it feel?), and each detail you add grows into the picture. A polish fixes capitals and end marks before the owl reads it aloud.
- Flags: none
