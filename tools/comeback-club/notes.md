## A 5-minute start

1. Open the **Gym** and tap **Bix**. Bix hops: that is the Rookie move. Ask: what do you think helps Bix get better at things?
2. Tap the **Pattern Ladder** door and play the first rung together. Then try a harder rung. When a choice says **Not yet**, the strategy cards slide up. Pick one as a class (for example **Break it into parts**) and solve it.
3. Look at Bix's headband: a gold comeback stripe. Point out the Know-how bar. A first-try solve adds 1, a comeback adds 3, and a comeback after a strategy card adds 4. Ask: why would coming back be worth more?

## The three modes

- **Gym:** five rooms of real puzzles, each a ladder of rungs that get harder. **Pattern Ladder** (what comes next) and **Memory Mirror** (watch the pads, then copy them) are open from the start. **Light Mat** (flip squares until all nine are lit), **Number Lift** (two buttons, reach the target floor) and **Summit Circuit** (one hard puzzle from every room) open one at a time, each time a goal is finished. **I'm stuck** opens the six strategy cards at any time: Try a new way, Slow down and check, Use a hint, Take three breaths, Break it into parts, Ask a friend. Each card really changes the puzzle (a slower replay, labels on the jumps, a glowing next step, a halfway floor).
- **Goals:** pick one goal card from the board, then make a plan: the strategy you will try first when you get stuck. Your goal and its progress show in the panel. Finishing it opens the next room and asks what helped most.
- **Locker:** Bix in everything it has earned (sweatband, sneakers, gym bag, star medal), a sticker for every strategy used (a gold star for each one that helped finish a goal), Bix's color, and six **Coach's corner** cards to flip.

Know-how never goes down, and a mistake never costs anything. Points come only the first time a rung is solved, so each rung pays only once and replaying it is free practice.

## Talk about it

- What did you do right after a not-yet? Did it work?
- Which strategy card helped you most today? When else could you use it?
- Was your goal specific enough to know when you had reached it? How could you make it clearer?
- Think of something that is easier for you now than it was last year. What practice helped?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes. Escape closes the strategy cards. In the puzzles, arrow keys move between targets and Enter or Space taps.
- **Save Bix** (in the Locker) keeps progress on this computer for a later day. Nothing saves on its own, so a shared classroom laptop starts fresh for the next student unless someone pressed Save. **Start over** clears the saved Bix after a confirm. Students never type anything.
- On a projector, the class can vote on each answer and on which strategy card to try.
- The tool says "practice helps" and never that the brain grows like a muscle. Studies of growth-mindset messages find small average effects, so the tool sticks to what is well supported: practice, new strategies and asking for help.
- **Take three breaths** is one quick pause, not a calm-down activity. If a student is upset, the school counselor is the right next step.
- Bix, Dot and the gym, rooms and locker are illustrations (made with image tools, then edited for Curio Class); the puzzles are drawn in code. Every Light Mat and Number Lift puzzle was checked to be solvable in its listed fewest moves.
