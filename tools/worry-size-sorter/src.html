Worry Size Sorter · Manual

At the desk

Drag a written worry into a basket, or pick it up and choose a basket with the keyboard. Small and Medium are flexible ways to think about help. Big situations always bring up adult help. Next worry keeps the desk open. There is no last card to reach.

Body clues opens five places worry can show up. The Try buttons open the matching tool. Tools has a breathing square, helpful replies, muscle relaxation and a worry box.

The readouts

The 1 to 5 meter is your own rating, never a diagnosis or a test. A lower rating describes what you reported; it cannot tell us why it changed. No number is a target. Stop is always available during practice.

Breathing uses three rounds of four equal phases: 48 seconds at count 4, or 36 seconds at count 3. One more round is optional. Stay comfortable; never strain. Muscle practice uses 5 seconds tight and 10 seconds loose.

The worry box is a reminder to talk, not a way to hide a Big worry. Choose about 10 minutes with a grown-up, earlier than bedtime. Do not put off getting help if someone is unsafe.

Privacy and limits

Only your helper-tool choices and Motion setting are saved on this device. No personal worry is typed or saved. Nothing is sent anywhere. Sound and Read use the device's voice when a recording is unavailable.

Things people have found

A tool can feel useful even when a rating stays the same. Different replies can feel useful on different days. Asking an adult is always an available choice.
