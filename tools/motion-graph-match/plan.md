arc: explore-first
minutes: 50
focus: HS-PS2-1
goal: I can read and make position, velocity and acceleration graphs, and explain that each graph is the slope of the one above it and the area under the one below.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Graph paper. Optional: a motion sensor or a long hallway for walking graphs.
grouping: Pairs on devices, with whole-class pauses.
---
## Before class

- Open **Explore** with **Hand** and the cart at 1 m.
- The cart is 2.0 kg on a 10 m track. Top speed 3 m/s.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Sketch a position graph that rises, stays flat, then falls. "Walk this graph." One student walks it in front of the room.
2. "A graph is not a picture of the path. Today you learn to read three graphs at once."

## Explore (15 min)

1. On the projector, drag the cart from 1 m to about 7 m and let go. "Why does the velocity graph make a bump? Why does acceleration go up, then down?"
2. Pairs pick **Throttle**, press **Clear** and push the slider to 1.0 m/s. "What does position do?" (Climbs 1 m every second.)
3. Pairs pick **Pedal**, press **Clear** and hold 0.2 m/s² for 5 s. They record velocity (1.0 m/s) and position (about 2.5 m). The force arrow reads 0.40 N.
4. Pairs let a run reach 12 s and turn on **Show links**.

## Make sense (10 min)

1. With **Show links** on, move across the graphs. The slope on each graph matches the value on the graph below it.
2. The shaded area under velocity matches the change in position. "Position is the running total of velocity."
3. Connect to F = m a: "The force arrow is 0.40 N with a 2.0 kg cart. What is the acceleration?"

## Apply (15 min)

1. Pairs switch to **Race** and pick a target card. Press **Start** and keep the line inside the shaded band.
2. Pairs try the same target with a different control. "Which control makes position targets easiest? Hardest? Why?"
3. Discuss target 6, Turnaround: "The cart stops for an instant at 3 s. Is the acceleration zero there?" (No: it is −0.5 m/s² the whole time.)
4. "Why can't a real cart follow target 1 exactly?" Press **Show all three** to see.

## Exit check (5 min)

On a card, sketch the velocity and acceleration graphs for this position graph: still for 2 s, then rising steadily for 4 s, then still. Then: "Where on a position graph is the velocity largest?" Velocity graphs with a ramp instead of a flat step show who needs **Show links** again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** start with **Throttle** and velocity targets only.
- **Support:** use the keyboard steps to set exact slider values.
- **Stretch:** try the "hard way" targets: **Pedal** on a position target.
- **Stretch:** beat your best run's ghost by 10 percent.

## Short on time

- **Throttle** and **Pedal** runs in **Explore** (8 min).
- **Show links** (5 min), then the exit sketch (5 min).
