Controller manual

Use the street

Press either curb button. Follow the lit rule, signal lamps and firing tape. Tap a rule to edit its chips. All its conditions must fit; the first match from the top fires. Pause and Step expose one pass round the loop.

Setups supplies five repeatable traffic scripts, in any order. Run script watches your rules on that traffic. Read record advances the same model to its 120 s observation cap. There is no grade or finish to earn.

Alt plus Up or Down moves a focused rule; Delete removes it. Enter edits it, Left and Right switch slots, and Escape returns. Six rules is the controller's capacity. Defaults restores the live program.

Readouts

The street clock uses seconds. The tape labels the firing rule and G, Y, R or W for its signal action. The record reports actual waits, light durations and the ambulance's motion, with unsafe moments you can replay. A red interval that never returns to green remains visible as a held red, even when walkers cross.

Fair-time references (this tool's own): greens at least 30 s, reds under 45 s, walker waits under 60 s. Cars stopped with nobody crossing is the longest empty red interval; 10 s is this tool's comparison reference. These are observations and model choices, not engineering requirements.

Things people have found

A rule lower down can lose its turn even when its conditions fit. A WALK request can hold up a return to green. A long yellow can stop an ambulance before the light turns red. One chip can change the whole street.

Honesty notes

This is a simplified signal programming model, not road design guidance. WALK is 7 s and flashing clearance is 10 s: the example uses 35 feet divided by 3.5 feet per second. Walkers take 10 s to cross. Vehicles move at a fixed model speed; a driver more than 2 s from the stop line when yellow starts stops. Real signals need many additional safety systems.

The detector is a model input, not a real emergency preemption system. Only the Motion setting persists on this device. Sound and rules last for this page. Nothing is sent.
