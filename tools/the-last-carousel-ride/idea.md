# The Last Carousel Ride (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-2-the-last-carousel-ride, grade 2. Codes: D2.His.6.K-2, D2.His.14.K-2, D2.His.16.K-2
- Format / family: mystery / none. Fit: good
- One idea: The town carousel stopped running in 1975; a woman who rode it as a girl and the owner's grandson remember why in different ways, an old newspaper adds a clue, and the student compares the tellings, lists possible reasons and picks the most likely one.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a still carousel under a tarp in the town park, a "Closed" sign. "Why did it stop? Tap the bench to meet someone who remembers."
  - Core loop: hear Ms. Ruth (she remembers a broken horse and a sad last day) and Ben, the grandson (his grandpa said too few riders and costly repairs). Put their tellings side by side: what they agree on, what is different. A 1975 newspaper clipping and a ticket price list add clues.
  - Reason board: the student drags possible reasons onto a board (broken parts, fewer riders, a new mall, the owner got old), each with clue pins showing which source supports it.
  - The aha or the end state: the student picks the most likely reason (or two working together) by counting clues; the ending reveals the full story and why two honest people remembered it differently.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): class votes on the reason.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.6.K-2: compare two accounts of the same closing side by side.
  - D2.His.14.K-2: generate possible reasons on the reason board.
  - D2.His.16.K-2: choose which reasons are more likely, using clue counts.
- Not like: capsule-from-1965 (reading objects for maker and date); why-the-pony-express-ended (grade 5 evidence claim).
- Art: people (illustrated) and generated scenes, because the two rememberers must feel real.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: none (fictional town and carousel; say so on the page)
