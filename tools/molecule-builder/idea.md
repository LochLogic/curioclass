# Molecule Builder (idea card)

- Module: science-7-atoms-and-molecules, grade 7. Codes: MS-PS1-1, MS-PS1-3
- Format / family: sandbox / molecules. Fit: good for MS-PS1-1 only
- One idea: Snap atoms together into small molecules, then repeat a pattern to grow extended structures (a salt crystal, diamond, copper), so the student sees that every substance is atoms in an arrangement and why some combinations hold together.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an atom tray (hydrogen, carbon, nitrogen, oxygen, sodium, chlorine, copper), each atom drawn with its number of bonding spots.
  - Core loop: snap atoms together; a structure with every spot filled settles and glows, one with open spots wobbles. A recipe drawer of optional finds (water, carbon dioxide, methane, ammonia, oxygen gas) checks off what the student made, and never blocks free building. Extended mode: place a few atoms in a pattern and tap "repeat" to tile it out until a grain of salt or a diamond fills the screen.
  - The aha or the end state: a compare card shows the same substance as a formula, a ball-and-stick model and a space-filling model, and sorts the student's builds into molecules (a set number of atoms) and extended structures (a pattern that goes on).
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a "strangest stable molecule" gallery saved only on this device.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-PS1-1: the student models the atomic makeup of simple molecules and extended structures in more than one representation.
  - MOVE MS-PS1-3: new tool: "Made From" (grade 7), trace an everyday synthetic material (a nylon jacket, a plastic bottle, a medicine) back to the natural resource it came from and forward to its effects on society.
- Not like: reaction-kitchen (atoms rearranging in reactions); bond-battle (grade 10 ionic and covalent bonding); elements-inspector (built, single elements).
- Art: atoms and bonds drawn in code so they stay exact, with generated backdrops of the everyday things each structure makes up.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Snap atoms into molecules like water, methane and ammonia, then repeat a pattern to grow salt, diamond and copper; fill every bonding spot and the structure holds.
- Flags: none
