# Word Lab (idea card)

- Module: ela-2-word-building, grade 2. Codes: L.2.4, L.2.5
- Format / family: lab / word-lab. Fit: good: mixing word parts in beakers is a real experiment with a predict-then-test step.
- One idea: The child mixes word parts in beakers (un + happy, sun + flower, add + -ition), predicts what the new word means, tests it in a sentence, and checks it in the lab's glossary, so they learn that a word's parts tell you about its meaning.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a lab bench with beakers labeled with word parts and a scientist (Dr. Oyelaran). "Pour two parts together. What do you think you made?"
  - Core loop: pour a prefix and a known word (re + tell), predict the meaning from three pictures, then the word glows and a test sentence appears; the child checks it against the sentence's context and in a pocket glossary or kid dictionary. Compound beakers (bird + house, book + mark). Root beakers (add, addition, additional). A shades shelf: line up related verbs or adjectives by strength (toss, throw, hurl; thin, slender, skinny, scrawny) on a measuring tube and hear each in a sentence. Real-life cases: tag foods that are spicy or juicy.
  - The aha or the end state: the lab notebook shows every word the child made, grouped by the part that did the work, and one made-up word (unjumpy) the child defines from its parts.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher part sets.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.2.4: context clues in the test sentence, prefix meanings, root words, compound words, and glossary and dictionary checks.
  - L.2.5: real-life connections (food tags) and shades of meaning among verbs and adjectives (the measuring tube).
- Not like: Root Detective Cards (grade 4 sibling, Greek and Latin roots). Vowel Team Tag (affixes for decoding). Context Clue Hunt (grade 1 context mysteries).
- Art: generated scenes (a bright science lab) and people (illustrated) for the scientist, because a friendly lab invites experiments.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Mix prefixes, compound parts and root words in beakers, predict what the new word means, test it in a sentence and check the glossary; a measuring tube lines up words like toss, throw and hurl by strength.
- Flags: none
