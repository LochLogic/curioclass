# Unit Ladder (idea card)

- Module: math-4-measurement-conversions, grade 4. Codes: 4.MD.A
- Format / family: race / none. Fit: good for conversions and measurement word problems, raced against your own ghost; see MOVE for the area and perimeter formulas.
- One idea: Each rung of the ladder is one bigger unit written in smaller ones (1 ft = 12 in, 2 ft = 24 in), so climbing builds the conversion table, and then a track-meet day uses that table to solve distance, time and liquid problems on a number line.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a tall ladder beside a school track, a ghost climber from your last best run, and a choice of unit sets (ft and in, yd and ft, m and cm, km and m, kg and g, lb and oz, L and mL, hr, min and sec).
  - Core loop: each rung asks for the next row of the table, and the row joins a two-column table on the side. Later rungs skip ahead (7 ft = ? in), so the pattern, not counting, carries the climb.
  - Track meet: the table comes along to events. Relay legs are 400 m and the race is 2 km; one runner finishes in 3 min 45 s and the next in 228 s, who was faster; a cooler holds 4 L and each cup holds 250 mL. Each problem is drawn on a number line (the track itself) before the answer.
  - The aha or the end state: bigger units are bundles of smaller ones, and putting everything in the same unit is how the problem gets solved.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): race the class ghost (best class time) instead of your own.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 4.MD.A (4.MD.1, 4.MD.2): relative sizes of units within one system and two-column conversion tables, built rung by rung; four-operation word problems on distance, time intervals, liquid volume, mass and money, with fractions and decimals, drawn on a number-line track.
  - MOVE 4.MD.A (4.MD.3, area and perimeter formulas for rectangles): new tool: a grade 4 community-garden planner where each plot has a set area or fence length and the student finds the missing side (a 48 sq ft plot that is 6 ft wide).
- Not like: kitchen-lab (grade 3 measuring and elapsed time), fence-builder (grade 3 area vs perimeter idea), seed-sprout-data (grade 5 conversions with line plots).
- Art: generated scenes (the school track, the ladder, the meet); people (illustrated) for the runners; tables and number lines drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Climb a conversion ladder where each rung adds a row to the table (1 ft = 12 in, 2 ft = 24 in) against your ghost, then use the table at a track meet to solve distance, time and liquid problems on a number line.
- Flags: too thin for the whole 4.MD.A cluster (needs the MOVE above)
