# Recipe Scaler (idea card)

- Module: math-4-adding-and-multiplying-fractions, grade 4. Codes: 4.NF.B
- Format / family: tycoon / fraction-food. Fit: change to builder, because there is no shop or economy over time; the student builds a scaled recipe card and a test bake checks it.
- One idea: Make Grandpa Lou's cornbread for the class party with only one-size scoops (1/4 cup, 1/3 cup, 1/8 cup), so every amount becomes a count of unit fractions, and scaling to 6 batches is just 6 times that count.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a kitchen counter, a handwritten recipe card and a drawer of single scoops. "We lost the measuring cups except the 1/4 cup. How many scoops is 3/4 cup of milk?"
  - Core loop, building fractions: the student fills a cup scoop by scoop and writes 3/4 = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 3 x 1/4, then splits amounts other ways (3/8 = 1/8 + 2/8). Mixed amounts (1 3/4 cups) are filled as wholes and fourths, then rewritten as 7/4 and back.
  - Adding and subtracting (like denominators): two bags of flour (1 3/4 and 2 2/4 cups) poured together; 2 1/4 cups of sugar minus the 1 3/4 already used. Word problems come from the kitchen.
  - Scaling: 6 batches of 2/3 cup is 6 x 2/3 = 12/3 = 4 cups, and the cups fill on screen. Then: which batch count first needs more than 5 cups? Between which two whole numbers does 5 x 3/8 land?
  - The aha or the end state: n x a/b is just n x a scoops of 1/b, so scaling a recipe is counting scoops.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): type in a family recipe and scale it (stays on the device, said on screen).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 4.NF.B: fractions as sums of unit fractions and other decompositions (4.NF.3a, 3b); adding and subtracting mixed numbers with like denominators and kitchen word problems (4.NF.3c, 3d); a/b as a x 1/b and whole number times fraction, with estimates between wholes (4.NF.4a to 4c).
- Not like: fraction-kitchen (grade 5, mixed cup sizes and unlike denominators), kitchen-lab (grade 3 mass, volume and elapsed time), grocery-run (grade 5 budget shopping).
- Art: generated scenes (a warm kitchen, the recipe card, ingredients); people (illustrated) for Grandpa Lou; cups and fill levels drawn in code so amounts stay exact.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Scale a family cornbread recipe for the class party using only one-size scoops: every amount becomes a count of unit fractions, like amounts add and subtract, and 6 batches is 6 times the scoops.
- Flags: overlap risk (fraction-kitchen, same family: keep this one to single-size scoops and like denominators)
