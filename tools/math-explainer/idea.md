# Math Explainer (idea card)

- Module: eld-3-5-explaining-my-solution, grade 3-5. Codes: WIDA.ELD-3, WIDA.Explain
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good: the student produces an explanation (typed or recorded) with real tools.
- One idea: Solve a word problem with blocks on a work mat, then explain your steps to a classmate who was absent: each sentence you build (First I..., Next I..., So the answer is...) replays its step on the mat, so a missing step or a step out of order shows up in the replay.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a word problem read aloud ("Maya has 3 bags with 8 apples each...") and a mat with base-ten blocks and a number line. A note: "Kofi missed class. Can you show him how?"
  - Core loop: the student solves on the mat; every move is logged as a step. Then they explain: one sentence per step, built from sequence words (first, next, then, finally), math verbs (I added, I grouped, I counted on) and the numbers. Each sentence replays its step for Kofi. A skipped step makes the replay jump, and the student sees the gap; a vague sentence ("I did it") replays nothing.
  - The aha or the end state: the full explanation plays as a short movie of the strategy with the student's words as captions, and Kofi solves a new problem by following it. The student may record the explanation in their own voice (kept on this device only, the screen says so).
  - Class mode or extras (optional): Help Lots / Some / Little; two students' strategies for the same problem side by side.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - WIDA.ELD-3: the language of mathematics: operation words, quantities and units in word problems.
  - WIDA.Explain: explaining steps and strategy in order with sequence words and cause words (so, because).
- Not like: then-what-happened (a listener redraws a story; here the replay is a math strategy, and the check is completeness of steps); word-problem-decoder (grade 6-8, reading problem language); cause-and-effect-rewind (science chains).
- Art: illustrated classmate (people, illustrated); manipulatives drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Solve a word problem with blocks, then explain it to an absent classmate one sentence per step with first, next and so; each sentence replays its step, so a skipped or out-of-order step shows in the replay.
- Flags: mic optional with the on-device line (rule 7), no speech recognition; overlap risk (then-what-happened's "your words drive the picture" mechanic; vary the look); home-language glossary may matter (open question).
