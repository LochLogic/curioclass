arc: model-then-make
minutes: 45
focus: TH:Cr1;TH:Pr5
goal: I can block a scene with stage directions so every seat sees the right actor, and fix it from the director's notes.
materials: The tool on the projector, and one laptop per pair (Sound low or off). Three chairs at the front of the room. Printed exit tickets (optional). Write on the board: blocking, upstage, downstage, set design, cue, stage manager.
grouping: Whole class for the hook and the model, then pairs (a director and a stage manager), then whole class.
---
## Before class

- Open the tool on every laptop. It starts in **Block** on **The Note**, beat 1, seat B3. Blocking lasts until the page closes.
- Try the model once yourself (5 minutes).

## Hook (5 min)

1. Face the class and raise your right hand. "Which side of the room is my hand on for you?" (Our left.)
2. "Theatre settles it: stage right and stage left are always the actor's right and left, facing the audience. **Downstage** is toward the audience, **upstage** is away."
3. "Planning where actors stand and move on each line is **blocking**. Today you direct."

## Model (13 min)

1. On the projector, in **Block**, read beat 1 aloud. The house (seat dots) is at the bottom of the plan, so the **SR wing** is on the screen's left. "Why is stage right on our left?" (The actors face us, so their right is our left.)
2. Tap **Next beat** twice (beat 3). The note "Ava is not upstaged" has a ✗. Tap **House**: from seat B3 we see Ava's back, and the eye icon sits over Ben. "Ava talks. Who do you watch?" (Ben.) "That is upstaged: your partner is behind you, so you turn your back on the house."
3. Tap **Block**, tap Ava on the plan, and tap **Turn right** twice. The note shows ✓. "Ava cheated out: she turned partly to the house. Another fix?" (Bring Ben down level with her.)
4. **Next beat** (beat 4): drag Ben into DSR. An arrow shows his cross. "Say Ben's move in stage words." (He crossed downstage right.)
5. **Next beat** (beat 5): drag Gran from the **SL wing** into DSL. Turn on **Focus**. "Why does the eye go to Gran?" (She speaks, in full light, facing out, just moved.) Beat 6 is already ✓: each beat starts as a copy of the last.
6. **Next beat** (beat 7). The class directs: tap Ben, then **Off stage** (he exits SR). Drag Ava into DSC (she stands up) and tap **Turn right** twice so she faces out.
7. Tap **Run** at the top, then **▶ Run** (about 40 seconds). Point out "Cue 4: GO" in the corner: the voice is the **stage manager**, who calls every **cue**. It should end "All 11 notes met."

## Make (15 min)

1. Pairs open **Scene**, pick **Bus Stop**, tap **Leave**. The stage manager reads each line and its notes aloud; the director drags and turns. Swap at beat 4.
2. Goal: tap **Run**, then **▶ Run**, and get every note met. After a run, tap a ✗ note to jump to its beat.
3. Ask: "Which seat would see a problem first?" (A corner seat, like A1 or A5.) "Turn on **Sightlines**. What does a red dashed line mean?" (That seat cannot see the face.)
4. Key, if a pair is stuck: beat 2, Leo from the SR wing into DSR. Beat 4, Sam from the SL wing into DSL. Beat 5, Leo onto the empty end of the bench (he sits). Beat 6, Sam just upstage of the bench, between them.
5. Finished pairs try **The Trophy**. Coach starts in the dark: in **Lights**, tap USC.

## Share (7 min)

1. Set the three chairs as the bench. Three volunteers play Bus Stop beats 5 and 6; a pair directs in stage words only ("Sam, cross upstage of the bench").
2. The class is the house. Far-side students answer: "Can you see all three faces?" "Is anyone upstaged?"
3. "Your bench and the lamp post are the **set design**. How did the bench change your blocking?" (Two could sit; the third had to stand where everyone could see.)

## Exit check (5 min)

On the ticket or paper: an actor is told "cross downstage left". Which way does she walk, and which side is that for the audience? Then: Ava sits talking to Ben, who stands right behind her, upstage. Name the problem and give two fixes.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** open **Stage words** for the areas, upstaged, cheat out and the five body positions.
- **Support:** in **House**, tap a seat button (A1 to C5, **Balcony**) to check one beat from that seat.
- **Stretch:** in The Trophy, beat 3, Riley needs the eye while Coach speaks. Bring Riley downstage facing out, then turn on **Spot** and drag its circle onto Riley.
- **Stretch:** in **Free stage**, write a four-beat scene, block it, then **Save on this device**.

## Short on time

- Model beats 1 to 3 (7 min).
- Pairs block and run Bus Stop (10 min), then the exit check (3 min).
