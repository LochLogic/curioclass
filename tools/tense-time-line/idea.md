# Tense Time-Line (idea card)

- Module: ela-5-verb-tense-and-conjunctions, grade 5. Codes: L.5.1
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Events slide on a story track; pick the verb that fits each spot (had finished, will have finished, was running), fix tense shifts that scramble the order, see what conjunctions, prepositions and interjections do, and join ideas with either/or and neither/nor.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a story track with three event cards. "Yesterday Ada ___ the race before the rain came. Which verb fits this spot?"
  - Core loop: drag verbs onto the track (had finished, will have finished, was running) and watch the events line up or scramble; fix tense shifts in a short story; tap conjunctions, prepositions and interjections to see what each does to a sentence; join ideas with either/or and neither/nor.
  - The aha or the end state: a scrambled story replays in the right order once every verb fits.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): teacher chooses which tenses are in play.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.5.1: function of conjunctions, prepositions and interjections; perfect tenses; tense to show time and sequence; fixing tense shifts; correlative conjunctions.
