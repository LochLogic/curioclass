# Museum Courtyard Performance (idea card)

- Module: arts-9-12-site-specific-performance, grade 9-12. Codes: DA:Pr4, DA:Pr5, DA:Pr6
- Format / family: tycoon / none. Fit: good
- One idea: As the rehearsal director, produce a dance concert in a museum courtyard over six weeks: the space has columns, a fountain that drowns quiet music, a stone floor, a balcony audience and a sunset at 7:40, so every choice (where the dancers move, when to rehearse on stone, what light and sound to rent, how the audience walks in) must answer the site.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the courtyard plan with sightlines from the floor and the balcony, a six-week calendar, a budget and a crew of five with skills.
  - Core loop: adapt the choreography to the site (move a duet away from the column, use the balcony as a second front). Schedule rehearsals with a body-load meter (stone floor hours, rest days, warm-ups). Spend the budget on lights, sound and a marley floor. Assign crew tasks and handle what goes wrong (a dancer's sore ankle, rain in week four).
  - The aha or the end state: opening night plays from three seats; audience flow, sound and light, and dancer health show the results of each choice. The student writes a leadership log: which calls they made and how they kept the company on track.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a different site (a parking garage roof, a library atrium).
- Standards, one line per code:
  - DA:Pr4: adapting spatial design and audience relationship to the site.
  - DA:Pr5: a rehearsal plan built on safe practice and healthy load.
  - DA:Pr6: leading the production: production elements, crew and performance practice.
- Not like: dancers-on-tour (grade 6-8: the performer adapting across three venues); stage-lighting-plot (theater lighting design); morning-show-crew (media).
- Art: generated scenes for the courtyard; people (illustrated) for dancers and crew, their own figures.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Produce a dance concert in a museum courtyard over six weeks: adapt the piece to columns, a fountain, a stone floor and a balcony audience, plan safe rehearsals, spend a budget on light and sound, and lead the crew to opening night.
- Flags: overlap risk (dancers-on-tour); needs a data source (dance load and surface facts, shared check with dancers-on-tour)
