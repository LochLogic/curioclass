## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Build**. The default track is P(x) = −0.2(x + 4)(x − 4): one hill, 32 m tall at x = 0. Ask: where are the zeros, and where are the stations?
2. Drag the right footing from 4 to 2. Watch both equations change and the hill lean. Then press **2** on the zero at x = 2: the track now touches the ground there and turns back.
3. Press **Flip track**. Hills become tunnels, and the end-behavior sentence changes. Press **Ride** and listen for the tone at each ground contact.

## The three modes

- **Build** (open, no goal): drag the diamond footings along the ground. Each footing is a zero; its number (1, 2 or 3) and the small glyph under it show how the track meets the ground (crosses, touches and turns back, crosses flat). The panel shows factored and standard form, **Show the multiplication** (the factors multiplied one at a time), the leading coefficient a (a log slider, a number box and **Flip track**), and readouts for degree, end behavior, highest point, deepest tunnel and ride length. One grid square is 10 m each way, so the shape is true to scale. Degree stops at 6 and multiplicity at 3.
- **Survey** (the remainder theorem): the office sends a plan in standard form only and the track hides in fog. Move the stake and press **Survey here** (or Enter): synthetic division by (x − c) gives the remainder, and the post planted at c is that tall. A remainder of 0 means (x − c) is a factor, so a footing locks there. **Check for a double footing** divides the remaining part by (x − c) again. When the remaining part is a constant (or, on Plan 9, a quadratic that is never zero), the fog lifts and a table shows factored and standard form agree at x = −2, 0 and 2.
- **Commissions** (optional): six orders (C1 to C6) list what the park wants, and four blueprints (B1 to B4) show a ghost track to match. The checklist ticks live. Approved cards keep a check on this device.

## Talk about it

- Why does a zero of multiplicity 2 never let the track cross the ground? What does the sign of each factor do on either side?
- Two tracks have the same zeros but different a. What stays the same, and what changes?
- In Survey, why does the remainder equal the height of the track at c? Try c = 0 on any plan: what do you notice about the constant term?
- Plan 9 has only one footing. Where did the other two zeros go?
- Can C5 (Big Three) be built with the tunnel zeros far apart? Why do they have to sit close together?

## Good to know

- Footings: Tab to one, Left and Right move it (by halves, or tenths with **Snap to half** off), keys 1, 2 and 3 set multiplicity, Delete removes it. Dropping one footing on another merges them if the multiplicity stays 3 or less.
- a can never be 0; the slider and box stop at 0.001 in size. Typing a negative number in the box sets the sign too.
- Heights beyond the plane still show their true value in the readouts, marked "off the blueprint".
- With Motion off, Ride places the car at End and lists every ground contact in order.
- Survey heights are in plan units, not meters.
