# Town Footprint (idea card)

- Module: science-8-human-impact-on-earth, grade 8. Codes: MS-ESS3-3, MS-ESS3-4
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Watch a town's water, land and energy use grow with its population and spending, then design a way to monitor and shrink one impact.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a small town and a population dial. "The town is growing. Watch its water, land and energy."
  - Core loop: grow the population and spending and watch impacts rise; then design a way to monitor and shrink one impact and test it.
  - The aha or the end state: impacts grow with people and consumption, and a design can cut one.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-ESS3-3: designing a method for monitoring and minimizing a human impact.
  - MS-ESS3-4: growth in population and consumption increases impacts on Earth's systems.
- Not like: up-the-watershed and lake-commons (9-12); hazard-forecaster (hazards).
- Art: generated scenes (one town over time).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (up-the-watershed, lake-commons)
- Origin: new tool from the 2026-10-27 overlap and gap audit (docs/agents/audit/gaps.md); codes from hazard-forecaster (MS-ESS3-3, MS-ESS3-4).
