# Mongol Messenger (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-7-medieval-asia, grade 7. Codes: D2.His.2.6-8, D2.Geo.7.6-8
- Format / family: race / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Carry an urgent message across the Mongol Empire by the yam relay, swapping horses at stations and showing your paiza (a metal pass) to get fresh mounts, and race a ghost of the same trip before the Mongols; then the same roads are shown carrying merchants, missionaries and ideas, and the student sorts what the empire changed and what stayed local.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a route from Karakorum to a distant governor, a paiza and a timer.
  - Core loop: ride stage by stage; at each station choose quickly and correctly (show the paiza, pick a rested horse, read the route sign); mistakes cost time. Beat the ghost of the older route, which has no stations and unsafe roads.
  - The aha or the end state: a map overlay shows the same stations and roads used by traders and travelers (paper money, gunpowder knowledge, foods); a sort board separates change (speed, connection) from continuity (local rulers, languages and religions kept).
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a ghost of the student's own best time.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.2.6-8: the closing sort of change and continuity under Mongol rule.
  - D2.Geo.7.6-8: the relay and safe roads show how communication technology changed connections and the spread of ideas.
- Not like: why-the-pony-express-ended (grade 5, a mystery about another relay); silk-road-traders (an earlier era, card trading); historians-disagree (same empire, but comparing historians' accounts).
- Art: generated scenes of steppe and stations, people (illustrated) for riders; a public domain paiza (Met open access, to check at spec).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Race an urgent message across the Mongol Empire by the yam relay, swapping horses and showing your paiza; beat the slower old way, then see what the same roads carried and sort what the empire changed and what stayed local.
- Flags: overlap risk (topic shared with historians-disagree, grade 7; different mechanic and skill); needs a data source (station spacing and riding speeds, checked)
