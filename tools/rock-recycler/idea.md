# Rock Recycler (idea card)

- Module: science-6-rock-cycle-and-earth-s-changing-surface, grade 6. Codes: MS-ESS2-1, MS-ESS2-2
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Follow one mineral grain through melting, cooling, weathering, burial and uplift, powered by the sun and Earth's inner heat, with each step on its real clock, from a landslide in seconds to uplift over millions of years.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: one mineral grain in a granite mountain. "Where will it go next?"
  - Core loop: follow the grain through weathering, burial, melting, cooling and uplift, choosing each path; the sun drives the surface steps and Earth's inner heat the deep ones; each step runs on its real clock, from a landslide in seconds to uplift over millions of years.
  - The aha or the end state: the grain is back where it started, and the student names what powered each step.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-ESS2-1: model of the cycling of Earth's materials and the energy that drives it.
  - MS-ESS2-2: geoscience processes change the surface at varying time and spatial scales.
- Not like: plate-puzzle (plates); erosion-table (grade 4 erosion).
- Art: generated scenes (cross sections), because the depths must read at a glance.

- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: none
