# Mood in the Pictures (idea card)

- Module: ela-3-5-pictures-panels-and-performances, grade 3-5. Codes: RL.3.7, RL.4.7, RL.5.7
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: One public-domain story scene comes three ways (plain text, illustrated comic panels, and a read-aloud with music), and the student marks what each picture and sound adds or changes, so they see that an illustrator or performer is also telling the story.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: three doors labeled Words, Panels, Performance, over a scene from a public-domain tale (for example the wolf at the door in an Aesop fable, or the moment the troll speaks in "The Three Billy Goats Gruff").
  - Core loop: read the plain text first and set a mood dial. Then open the panels: tap anything the pictures add (a shadow, a worried face, a clue in the background) and tag it mood, character, setting or clue. Then the performance: tap where the music or voice changes the feeling. Each tag is checked against the words: follows the text, adds to it, or changes it.
  - The aha or the end state: a "mood strip" shows the mood dial for each version side by side, and the student swaps one panel for another artist's version of the same moment and sees the mood swing while the words stay the same.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a gallery of three different illustrators' takes on one line.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RL.3.7: tagging what the illustrations add to mood, character and setting.
  - RL.4.7: matching the read-aloud and the panels to the exact descriptions and directions in the text.
  - RL.5.7: the mood strip and the panel swap: how visuals and sound shape meaning, tone and beauty.
- Not like: windblown-pages (k-2, putting pictures back on pages); page-to-screen (grade 10 storyboard); dialogue-director (making a comic).
- Art: illustrated panels by at least two visual styles plus a produced read-aloud with music; public domain illustration (for example Arthur Rackham or Walter Crane plates, Met or AIC ids to be checked at spec) as one of the styles.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (public-domain illustration ids to check; produced audio)
