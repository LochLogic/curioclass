# House of Wisdom (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-7-islamic-civilizations, grade 7. Codes: D2.His.1.6-8, D2.Geo.7.6-8
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Follow one book's journey: Ptolemy's Greek astronomy is translated into Arabic in ninth-century Baghdad under the Abbasid caliphs, copied on paper made with skills that came from China, improved by scholars, and centuries later translated into Latin in Toledo; each stop is a room to explore, and the student sees how knowledge survived by moving between languages and places.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an old Latin book on a desk in Toledo with a thread leading back across a map.
  - Core loop: step back through rooms (Alexandria's scrolls, a Baghdad paper mill, the translators' workroom, an observatory, a hospital, al-Khwarizmi's algebra lesson, Toledo); in each, inspect objects and pick up a "carried forward" card (an idea, a tool, a word like algebra or algorithm).
  - The aha or the end state: the student lays the cards on a route map and writes how paper, roads and trade let ideas travel, and how Baghdad connected older Greek, Persian and Indian learning to later Europe.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the same journey for a medical book.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.1.6-8: the rooms connect Baghdad's scholars to the wider story of learning before and after them.
  - D2.Geo.7.6-8: paper, roads and trade on the route map show how communication technology spread ideas.
- Not like: printing-press (a later leap in copying); silk-road-traders (goods and beliefs by trade); panel-of-experts (how different fields question one event).
- Art: public domain manuscript pages and instruments (Met open access, ids to check at spec) plus generated scenes of the rooms.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Follow one book from Greek scrolls to Arabic translation in Abbasid Baghdad, onto paper from China and into Latin in Toledo; collect the ideas carried at each stop and map how knowledge survived by moving.
- Flags: sensitive (Islam and its history shown accurately and respectfully; fresh review); the card notes honestly that historians debate what the House of Wisdom itself was
