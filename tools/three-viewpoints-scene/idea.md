# Three Viewpoints Scene (idea card)

- Module: arts-6-8-devising-a-scene, grade 6-8. Codes: TH:Cr2, TH:Pr4, TH:Pr6, TH:Cn10
- Format / family: roleplay / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Devise a two-minute scene about a school issue (the late bus that makes some kids miss breakfast, a lunch table rule, a phone policy) from three characters' viewpoints, with an illustrated devising partner who keeps offering ideas you must build on, then tighten the pacing on a beat board and perform it.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the issue card, three perspective cards (a student it hurts, a student it helps, the adult who made the rule) and a beat board.
  - Core loop: draft the scene as beats; each character wants something. The illustrated actors read the draft back. Sam, the devising partner, offers an idea card at set points ("What if the adult has a reason no one knows?"); the student says "yes, and" by folding it in, or explains why not.
  - The aha or the end state: the pacing pass: the beat board shows each beat's length, and the student cuts and moves beats until the turn lands; the before and after play back to back. Then perform: the student plays one role live (for family or the class) with the other two voiced by the tool, and a last card asks whose view the student's own community would share.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): three students take the three roles and a fourth directs.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - TH:Cr2: building the scene with a partner's ideas and refining choices.
  - TH:Pr4: finding the essential beats and testing pacing to tell the story.
  - TH:Pr6: performing the devised scene for an audience.
  - TH:Cn10: how each character's actions reflect a community's perspectives.
- Not like: neighborhood-issue-play (grade 3-5: a play from neighbors, with a toy theater); stage-blocker (grade 6-8 blocking); monologue-rehearsal (grade 9-12 solo text).
- Art: people (illustrated) for the characters and Sam; generated scene for the school.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Devise a short scene about a school issue from three viewpoints: draft its beats, build on a devising partner's ideas, tighten the pacing until the turn lands, then perform one role live with the others voiced.
- Flags: records audio if the student records their role (on-screen line that it never leaves the device); overlap risk (neighborhood-issue-play)
