arc: explore-first
minutes: 45
focus: F-IF.A.3; A-SSE.B.4
goal: I can write a sequence explicitly and recursively, read a balance as a partial sum, and use the arithmetic and geometric series formulas.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Notebooks for the derivations.
grouping: Whole class for the hook, then pairs on devices.
---
## Before class

- Open **Race** on **Penny vs ten**. Plan A puts in $10 every week; Plan B starts with one cent and doubles it every day.
- Every deposit is rounded to the cent, so the ledger is exact. The shortcut can differ by a few cents for multiply plans.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Read the two plans aloud. "A 30-day race. Who wins?" Take a vote before anyone touches anything.
2. Most pick Plan A. "Let's find out, day by day."

## Explore (15 min)

1. Walk the days on the projector: hover the chart, or Tab to it and use the arrow keys. A leads until day 11, when the penny passes it.
2. Pairs open the **Ledger** and find the day 30 deposit by itself ($5,368,709.12). "How does that compare with everything Plan A puts in?"
3. Pairs switch between **What you have** and **What goes in**. "Which one is the sequence and which is the series?"
4. Pairs try **Log scale** and **Who leads** on two other races, and write each plan's rule both ways, explicit and recursive, checking against the card.

## Make sense (10 min)

1. On the board, name the terms: a_n is one deposit, S_n is the balance after n deposits. "Point to each one on the chart."
2. For Plan A, press **Show the shortcut** and step through it. "Why does every column add to the same thing?"
3. For Plan B, step through the multiply shortcut. "Why does almost everything cancel?" Step 5 lands on the same $10,737,418.23 as the **Ledger**.
4. Students copy both formulas, each with the race's numbers filled in.

## Apply (10 min)

1. Pairs open **Plan Desk** and take the first two customers. Each has a goal, a deadline and a cap on any one deposit.
2. Before pressing **Run the plan**, pairs check their plan with the right series formula on paper. One run per customer.
3. Ask: "Ivy's goal is $2,000. How can you prove no plan works without trying them all?"

## Exit check (5 min)

On a card: "A plan starts at $3 and adds $2 each week. Write the rule both ways and find the balance after 10 weeks." Then: "A plan starts at $1 and triples each day. Write the sum for 8 days as a formula." Cards that use a_n where S_n belongs show who needs the chart again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** stay with add plans until the explicit and recursive rules are solid.
- **Support:** keep the **Ledger** open next to the chart while pairs write rules.
- **Stretch:** "The halving plan adds less and less forever. Why can it never pass $128?"
- **Stretch:** finish the whole **Plan Desk** season and aim for a perfect $225.

## Short on time

- **Penny vs ten** race with the vote (8 min).
- **Show the shortcut** for B as a class (7 min), then the exit card (5 min).
