# Kitchen Lab (idea card)

- Module: math-3-time-volume-and-mass, grade 3. Codes: 3.MD.A
- Format / family: lab / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: Cook a family dinner for 6 p.m.: weigh ingredients in grams and kilograms, measure liquids in liters, and plan the start times with an elapsed-time number line so everything is ready together.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a home kitchen, a clock at 4:15, a recipe card from Grandpa Leo. "Dinner is at 6:00. Soup takes 45 minutes. When should it start?"
  - Core loop: the child drags jumps on a time line (from 5:15, +45 minutes is 6:00), and sets the start on a clock to the minute. Then ingredients: pour water into a measuring jug to 2 liters, weigh potatoes on a kitchen scale in grams and kilograms. Estimate first ("Is a pumpkin about 3 grams or 3 kilograms?"), then weigh.
  - Problems: one-step, whole numbers only, all four operations: 4 bags of 250 grams of rice, 9 liters of soup shared among 3 pots, 2 kilograms of flour minus 1 kilogram used.
  - The aha or the end state: all three dishes come out at 6:00 when the plan is right; a late plan shows the soup still bubbling.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): plan your own family meal.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 3.MD.A: telling and writing time to the minute and solving elapsed-time problems on a number line (3.MD.1), and estimating and measuring liquid volume and mass in liters, grams and kilograms with one-step problems in all four operations (3.MD.2).
- Not like: clock-shop (grade 1 hour and half hour), smoothie-lab (grade 6 ratios), estimation-station (length).
- Art: generated scenes (a warm family kitchen), people (illustrated) for Grandpa Leo.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Cook dinner for 6 p.m.: plan start times on an elapsed-time number line to the minute, weigh ingredients in grams and kilograms, and measure liquids in liters, estimating before you measure.
- Flags: none
