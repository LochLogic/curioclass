# Place Mood Makeover (idea card)

- Module: arts-3-5-mood-and-place-in-art, grade 3-5. Codes: VA:Cr1, VA:Cr3, VA:Re8, VA:Cn10
- Format / family: sandbox / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Take an ordinary place (a bus stop, the lunchroom, a front porch) and give it a mood by changing the time of day, the light, the colors, the weather and the small details, then tag your clues; the Mood Reader reads only your clues back, and you revise until every clue points the same way.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: three versions of the same bus stop made by three illustrated artists (lonely, cozy, spooky). The student guesses each mood and taps the clues that gave it away.
  - Core loop: choose a place and a mood. Change the sky and light (dawn, noon, dusk, night; warm or cool), the palette, the weather, and add or remove details (a lit window, a lost glove, puddles, a cat). Tag each choice with the mood it adds.
  - The aha or the end state: the Mood Reader lists what the clues say ("cool blue light: lonely; a lit window: cozy") and shows when two clues pull against each other. The student revises, then writes one sentence: what this place means to them and why they chose this mood.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a partner guesses the mood before seeing the tags.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - VA:Cr1: brainstorming several moods for one place and choosing among them.
  - VA:Cr3: revising until the clues agree, and explaining the change.
  - VA:Re8: reading the three artists' versions by their clues (mood, details, light, color).
  - VA:Cn10: the place starts from the student's own life, and the sentence says why it matters.
- Not like: near-and-far (depth cues); paint-mixer (color mixing); frame-it (grade 6-8 photo composition).
- Art: generated scenes for each place, built as layers so light, color, weather and details change for real.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Give an ordinary place (a bus stop, the lunchroom, a porch) a mood by changing its light, colors, weather and details, tag your clues, and revise until the Mood Reader finds every clue pointing the same way.
- Flags: none
