arc: explore-first
minutes: 45
focus: NHES.S5; NHES.S7
goal: I can spot a hazard at home or online, decide if I need a grown-up first, compare actions, and do the safe thing.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices.
grouping: Whole class in the Kitchen, then pairs on devices.
---
## Before class

- Open the house on the projector. Do not open a room yet.
- Read the **Help rule** in the **Manual**: medicine, fire, heat or climbing means get a grown-up first. If you can make it safe without touching those, act now, then tell.
- The topics are poisoning, fire, drowning and online contact from strangers. Nothing asks a child about their own home. Do not invite personal stories in the group. If a child tells you a stranger online asked for personal details, or describes a fire, a poisoning or an unsafe home, follow school procedure, including the counselor and a report when required.
- Dad and Grandma June stand for any trusted grown-up, not a family shape.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "A house can look fine and still hide a problem. Some things that look risky are safe. Today you are safety sweepers."
2. "Who would you tell if you found something unsafe at home?" Take two or three answers: any trusted grown-up counts.

## Explore (15 min)

1. On the projector, open the **Kitchen**. Students point; you tap. Tap the pot first: its handle points to the back, so it looks fine. Then tap the cleaners.
2. Show **Compare actions**. Tap **What could happen** on all three actions before anyone picks. "Which action keeps everyone safe?" Then **Try the fix**: drag the bucket away from the bottles and tap Tell Dad.
3. Pairs take devices and sweep the other rooms in any order: **Living room**, **Hallway**, **Bathroom**, **Game room**, **Backyard**. Each room has two hazards and one look-alike that is fine.
4. Before each fix, pairs say out loud: "Do we need a grown-up first?" Then they check the **Help rule**.
5. Walk with one question: "Who is safer now: you, Theo, or everyone?" The note after each fix names who it protects.

## Make sense (10 min)

1. "What looked risky but was fine? What detail changed your mind?" (The pot handle turned back; the battery candle; the medicine up high and closed.)
2. "Why did you move the cleaners' bucket yourself, but call Dad for the towel by the burner?" (A cool, empty bucket is safe to move; heat, flames and medicine are for a grown-up.)
3. "Which fixes helped Theo most? Which helped the whole house?" (Theo: the pills, the gummies, the tub, the pool gate. Everyone: the fire ones.)
4. In the **Game room**: "Why is a prize message that asks for your password a trick?" (Real prizes never need your password; close it, type nothing, tell.)

## Apply (10 min)

1. Open **Habits to keep** in the **Manual**. Pairs pick two habits they found and say each in their own words.
2. Pairs choose one hazard and replay it with **Reset this scene**. One partner reads the three actions aloud; the other says which is safest and why, then taps **What could happen** to check.
3. Share out: one hazard that needed a grown-up, and one safe thing a child could do before telling.

## Exit ticket (5 min)

On a card: "A lighter is on the backyard table. What do you do, and who do you tell?" (Leave it alone; tell a grown-up now. A lighter is a grown-up tool.) Then: "A game chat asks for your school. What do you do?" (Do not reply; block, report and tell.) Cards that say "put the lighter away" show who needs the **Help rule** again.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** sweep the **Living room** together on the projector before pairs start.
- **Support:** keep the frame on the board: "This needs / does not need a grown-up because ___."
- **Support:** **Read** speaks the hint and the help line aloud.
- **Stretch:** in the **Hallway**, use the heater ruler. "Why does 3 feet matter, and why is it the cool blanket you move?"
- **Stretch:** "Why is a self-latching pool gate safer than a rule to close the gate?"

## Short on time

- Sweep the **Kitchen** together, with **Compare actions** and **Try the fix** (8 min).
- Pairs sweep the **Game room** (5 min), then the exit card (5 min).
