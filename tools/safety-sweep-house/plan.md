# Safety Sweep House: one period

Goal: I can spot a hazard, think about help, compare choices, and check who is safer.

Allow 20 to 30 minutes. Students can work alone or in pairs on one device. Project one room to introduce the four steps.
Spend five minutes exploring the Kitchen together, then let students explore the remaining rooms in any order.
Ask students to peek at possible outcomes and explain their choices. The final Safety card gathers all twelve habits.
Finish by discussing one choice that needed a grown-up and one safe action a child could take before telling.

The sensitive topics are poisoning, fire, drowning, and online contact from strangers. Do not ask for personal disclosures.
If a child discloses a stranger asking for personal information, a fire, poisoning, or an unsafe home, follow school procedure,
including counselor referral and reporting when required. The tool collects nothing.
