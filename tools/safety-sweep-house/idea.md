# Safety Sweep House (idea card)

- Module: health-pe-3-5-safety-at-home-and-online, grade 3-5. Codes: NHES.S5, NHES.S7
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good, because the student searches a house room by room and inspects each hazard.
- One idea: Sweep a house room by room for hidden hazards (medicine on a low table, two cleaners about to be mixed, a smoke alarm with its battery out, a stranger in a game chat asking for your address), choose the safe action for each, and see the "what could happen" card before fixing it.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a cutaway house with six rooms dimmed. "Tap a room to start your safety sweep."
  - Core loop: each room has two or three hazards to find (a pill bottle near a toddler's toy, bleach and ammonia cleaners side by side, a candle by curtains, a pool gate left open, a game chat message: "what school do you go to? I'll send you free coins"). Tap one and choose from three actions; the safe action fixes it, the others show the "what could happen" card, then let you choose again.
  - Decision steps: each choice uses the same four steps (stop, think about what could happen, choose, check with an adult).
  - Online room: the game chat lets the student block, report and tell a grown-up; giving the address is never the right path.
  - The aha or the end state: the house lights up room by room, and a safety card lists the fixes, including the Poison Help number (1-800-222-1222 in the US) and calling 911 for fire.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - NHES.S5: use decision steps to choose the safe action for each hazard.
  - NHES.S7: practice the safe behaviors (store medicine up high, never mix cleaners, test smoke alarms, block and tell online).
- Not like: safety-street (K-2 street, fire drill and water safety); body-systems-tour (body systems); secret-keeper (K, what is OK to share).
- Art: generated scenes (a warm, lived-in house with readable hazards).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: none (check the Poison Help number and cleaner-mixing facts at spec time)
