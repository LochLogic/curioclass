# Stretch Shelf (idea card)

- Module: ela-3-5-reading-range-and-text-complexity, grade 3-5. Codes: RL.3.10, RL.4.10, RL.5.10, RI.3.10, RI.4.10, RI.5.10
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Choose stories, poems, plays and fact texts at your grade, read a stretch text with supports (word taps, chunk recaps, audio) you switch off one at a time, check understanding at each chunk, and fill a range map across genres and subjects.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a shelf by genre and subject. "Choose a stretch text. Supports are on; switch them off when ready."
  - Core loop: read stories, poems, plays and fact texts at grade level, then a stretch text with supports (word taps, chunk recaps, audio) switched off one at a time, with a check at each chunk; fill a range map.
  - The aha or the end state: the student reads a top-of-band text with the supports off.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RL.3.10, RL.4.10, RL.5.10: literature at the top of the band, independently.
  - RI.3.10, RI.4.10, RI.5.10: informational texts at the top of the band.
- Not like: research-rover (grade 4 sources); read-along-shelf (K-2, same skill one band down).
- Art: public domain texts and illustrations.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: span check 3-5 (gf-04); range map on the device (rule 7)
- Origin: new tool from the 2026-10-27 overlap and gap audit (docs/agents/audit/gaps.md); codes from uncovered RL.3.10 to RL.5.10, RI.3.10 to RI.5.10.
