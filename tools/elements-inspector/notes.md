## A 5-minute start

1. Project the table and ask: "Which element do you want to look inside?" Let the class pick.
2. Spin the atom. Count protons, electrons and shells together.
3. Open its emission spectrum. Every element's light is a fingerprint, which is how we know what stars are made of.

## Wander, then talk

- Pick two elements in the same column. What do their atoms have in common?
- Which element surprised you, and why?
- Why might scientists arrange the table in rows and columns at all?

There's nothing to score here. Let students click around first, then bring the class together.
