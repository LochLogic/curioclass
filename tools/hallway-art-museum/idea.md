# Hallway Art Museum (idea card)

- Module: arts-k-2-showing-our-art, grade k-2. Codes: VA:Pr4, VA:Pr5, VA:Pr6, VA:Re9
- Format / family: builder / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Turn a plain school hallway into a real little museum, choosing kid artworks for a theme, giving each the right home (frame, stand, glass case) and hanging it where a kid can see, then playing the museum's people (curator, guard, guide, visitor) on opening day to find out what makes a museum different from any other hallway.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: an empty hallway, a cart piled with kid art (a painting, a clay turtle, a chalk drawing, a paper crown, a yarn weaving) and a sign: "Our show is about ___".
  - Core loop: pick a theme card (animals, my family, outside), pick four works that fit and tap a reason chip (colors, it is funny, it shows the theme). Prepare each: the clay turtle needs a stand, the chalk needs glass, the painting a frame. Hang it; a kid-height visitor walks by and says whether she can see it.
  - The aha or the end state: opening day. The student switches hats: the guard keeps hands off the chalk, the guide tells a visitor why a work was chosen, the visitor picks a favorite with art words. A last screen compares the hallway museum with a library, a store and a home: what only a museum does.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): add the child's own drawing to the cart (saved on the device only); a class vote on the theme.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - VA:Pr4: choosing works for the theme and saying why (K portfolio choice, grade 2 sorting by theme).
  - VA:Pr5: matching each material to how it is prepared and where it goes (frame, stand, case, height).
  - VA:Pr6: the museum hats on opening day and the museum-or-not compare (what a museum is, who works there, how art shown in school helps the community).
  - VA:Re9: the visitor's favorite, with a reason in art words.
- Not like: traveling-art-show (grade 3-5: protecting art in a non-museum venue over time); virtual-gallery (grade 9-12: sequencing a portfolio); art-passport (looking at real artworks).
- Art: generated scenes for the hallway; kid artworks made as warm illustrated "child art" pieces; people (illustrated) for the museum hats.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Turn a school hallway into a museum: choose kid artworks for a theme and say why, give each the right frame, stand or case and hang it at kid height, then play curator, guard, guide and visitor on opening day.
- Flags: overlap risk (traveling-art-show has the same four codes at 3-5; keep this one about what a museum is and who works there)
