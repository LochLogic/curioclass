# Front Line (idea card)

- Module: science-6-air-masses-and-weather, grade 6. Codes: MS-ESS2-5
- Format / family: data / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Collect readings from a row of weather stations as a cold air mass meets a warm one, then use the data to forecast tomorrow's weather.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a row of five weather stations as a cold air mass arrives. "Read the stations every hour."
  - Core loop: collect temperature, pressure, wind and humidity readings as a cold mass meets a warm one; chart them and forecast tomorrow.
  - The aha or the end state: the forecast matches what the next day brings, explained by the moving air masses.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-ESS2-5: data on how the motions and interactions of air masses change weather.
