# Clock Shop (idea card)

- Module: math-1-telling-time-to-the-half-hour, grade 1. Codes: 1.MD.B
- Format / family: sandbox / clock. Fit: good, with gentle customer orders for a child who wants a goal.
- One idea: Turn the hands of a big geared clock and the digital clock and the sky outside move with them, so you see that at half past three the short hand sits halfway between 3 and 4.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a clockmaker's shop, a big clock and a window. "Drag the long hand around. Watch the short hand."
  - Core loop: dragging the minute hand once around moves the hour hand to the next number; the digital clock and the window (sunrise, lunch, sunset, moon) follow. Snap points at :00 and :30.
  - Customer orders: a customer asks "Set it to 7:30" or shows a digital time; the child sets the analog clock. Another customer shows an analog clock and the child types the digital time.
  - The aha or the end state: at 3:30 the short hand is not on the 3; a magnifier shows it halfway to 4, and "half past three" makes sense.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a daily-routine strip where the child sets times for breakfast, school and bed.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 1.MD.B: telling and writing time in hours and half hours on analog and digital clocks (1.MD.3), both directions.
- Not like: kitchen-lab (grade 3 elapsed time to the minute), corner-store (grade 2 time and money, closing by 3:45).
- Art: generated scenes (a warm clockmaker's shop with many clocks); the working clock drawn in code so hands are exact.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Turn the hands of a big geared clock and the digital clock and the sky outside change with it; set clocks for customers to the hour and half hour, and see why the short hand sits between numbers.
- Flags: none
