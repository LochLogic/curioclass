# Warming Evidence Desk (idea card)

- Module: science-8-evidence-of-rising-temperatures, grade 8. Codes: MS-ESS3-5
- Format / family: data / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Ask questions of real records (temperature, carbon dioxide, the sun's output, volcanoes) to sort out which factors explain the rise in global temperature over the past century.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a desk of real records: temperature, carbon dioxide, the sun's output, volcanoes. "What explains the rise?"
  - Core loop: ask questions of each record, line them up against global temperature, and sort out which factors fit the rise over the past century.
  - The aha or the end state: the student argues from the records which factors explain the rise.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-ESS3-5: questions to clarify evidence of the factors that have caused the rise in global temperature over the past century.
- Not like: climate-dashboard (grade 9).
- Art: real data (charts).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: needs a data source (NOAA, NASA records); overlap risk (climate-dashboard)
- Origin: new tool from the 2026-10-27 overlap and gap audit (docs/agents/audit/gaps.md); codes from hazard-forecaster (MS-ESS3-5).
