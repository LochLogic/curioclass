# Shadow Puppet Studio (idea card)

- Module: science-1-light-and-shadows, grade 1. Codes: 1-PS4-2, 1-PS4-3
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Put on a shadow puppet show: first find out that in a dark room you can see a puppet only when light shines on it, then test cutouts made of cardboard, wax paper, clear plastic and foil in the flashlight beam, and use what each one does (block, glow through, pass through, bounce) to light your own show.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a dark room with a puppet theater; the room is black. "Can you see anything? Turn on the flashlight."
  - Dark room step: move the flashlight around; only the things it lights up can be seen. A closed box hides a toy until a light goes in, so seeing needs light.
  - Core loop: pick a material for a puppet, hold it in the beam; a sorting tray fills: blocks light (dark shadow), lets some light through (soft glow), lets light through (almost no shadow), bounces light (a spot on the wall). Move the light closer and the shadow grows.
  - The aha or the end state: the student makes a short show with a cardboard character, a wax-paper moon, a clear-plastic window and a foil mirror that bounces light onto the hero, and plays it back.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): share the show on the projector.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 1-PS4-2: the dark room and the closed box give evidence that objects can be seen only when lit.
  - 1-PS4-3: the material tests in the beam sort transparent, translucent, opaque and reflective materials by what they do to light.
- Not like: vibration-station (sound); sky-diary (sun and moon patterns); shade-builder (sunlight warming).
- Art: drawn in code for the beam and shadows (they must respond exactly to the light), with generated scenes for the theater and illustrated puppet characters.
