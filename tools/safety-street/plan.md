minutes: 30
focus: NHES.S1; NHES.S5
goal: I can choose safe actions for crossing streets, riding, cars, fire drills, hot things, water and medicine.
materials: The tool on the big screen. A strip of tape on the floor as a pretend curb. Your school's fire drill meeting place.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet.
---
## Before class

- Open **Walk the street** at the first stop.
- Each stop ends with what to do, not with what goes wrong. Keep the tone calm.
- Some children have had scary experiences with cars, fires or water. Watch for worry and follow up quietly.

## Hook (3 min)

1. "**Biscuit** the dog is going on a walk today, and Biscuit needs your help to stay safe."
2. "When you see a choice, hold up one finger or two to vote."

## Explore (13 min)

1. Read the first stop: Biscuit wants to cross to the park. Children vote, then tap the choice.
2. A safe choice moves Biscuit on. An unsafe one explains what could happen, and the class tries again.
3. Visit all seven stops: the crosswalk, a bike helmet, buckling up, a school fire drill, a hot stove, the pool and a bottle of medicine.

## Make sense (5 min)

1. "Which stop was hardest? Why?"
2. Fire safety: "Get out, stay out, never hide, never go back for toys. Where is our meeting place?"
3. Medicine: "Only from a trusted grown-up. If you find pills, tell a grown-up and don't touch them."

## Apply (6 min)

1. Switch to **Crossing practice**. Biscuit waits at the curb. The class says "Look left, right and left again," and waits for the walking person.
2. Children practice at the tape curb: stop, look left, right, left, and hold a grown-up's hand. Remind them that young children cross with a grown-up.

## Exit check (3 min)

Switch to **Safe or not?** and read four cards. Children answer with thumbs up or thumbs down. Listen to their reasons for "Chase a ball into the street."

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** stay with three stops: the crosswalk, the helmet and the fire drill.
- **Support:** act out each safe choice with the whole body.
- **Stretch:** "What would you tell a younger child about the stove?"
- **Stretch:** "Do you know your grown-up's phone number?" Practice saying it at home.

## Short on time

- Four stops in **Walk the street** (6 min).
- **Crossing practice** (4 min).
