## What it teaches

This Curiosity is a microscope bench for open observation. Its material is real, licensed photography at physical scale; its optics and mechanical stage are code. It lets students observe plant and animal cells, cell walls, nuclei and dividing chromosomes, and practise micrometry without a lesson sequence or assessment.

## Key skills

- Focus and follow material with an inverted mechanical stage.
- Prepare a wet or dry mount, compare actual unstained and iodine-stained onion fields, handle cover glass and air.
- Compare four objectives, light, aperture and immersion oil.
- Calibrate an eyepiece reticle separately for each objective and measure real photographic material.
- Turn focus through nine photographs of the same pond colony, taken at different depths.
- Draw, label and print observations in a private local sketchbook.

## Support at home

Sketch a natural object from observation. Compare two rulers against the same object. A teacher or adult can use the Manual to discuss what a photographed field does and does not show.

## An unhurried start

Pond water is already on the stage, blurry at 100× labelled total magnification. One coarse focus step sharpens the photographic organisms. Move the slide, try the turret, prepare another mount, or open the sketchbook. There is no required order.

## The instrument

Eleven glass-slide choices: pond assemblage, onion skin, hair, sand, leaf, crane-fly wing, prepared root tip, blood, cheek swab, fixed pond colony and exact stage micrometer. Source images retain physical scale when enlarged. The true modelled objective powers are 3.9, 9.8, 41 and 98, slightly different from their labels; field diameters are 4.62, 1.84, 0.44 and 0.18 mm. The reticle is independent of specimen focus.

## Good to know

The pond uses real photographic cutouts moving on computed paths. The fixed colony uses nine measured focal planes, 25 µm apart. Blur has a field-scaled logarithmic response. Per-slide, medium, cover-glass and objective offsets, a modelled hair depth gradient, bubbles and oil use declared optical approximations. Prepared samples keep their original stains. The prepared cheek field is methylene blue. Its biological-size scale is approximate; use the blood fields for measurement. Salt, sugar, feather and methylene-blue preparation were omitted under the specification's curation fallback.

Only finite photographed fields are supplied. The root-tip base is a real field with an explicitly inferred scale and four separate control insets. Its base scale is approximate. The pond now uses six different cutouts, each once, including three different photographed ciliates. The assembly and computed motion are disclosed in the Manual. High zoom cannot reveal absent source detail. Hair uses an inferred standard counting-grid pitch. The sand is reflected light and the focal colony is source darkfield/pseudocolour, as stated in the Manual.

The sketchbook stays on this device with a guarded localStorage adapter and visible failure messages. No accounts, analytics or automatic network requests. `codex-report-1.md` preserves the incomplete first build; the fresh report covers this second build.

Size: 1,613,120 bytes for the single offline HTML file (about 1.54 MiB), within the 2 MB goal. Real WebP photographs and code, no generated bitmap anatomy or video.

## Calibration and field notes

Calibrate opens beside the live scales; the stage remains usable while the counts are entered. Each objective keeps its own factor. The field guide clears when the optical state changes and does not identify a specimen through a closed iris. The sand view retains its reflected-light source.

## Sensitivity

The prepared blood image is from a published malaria sample. It supports observation and measurement, with its source context retained; it does not assess the viewer or represent normal blood. Hair and cheek cells are presented without assumptions about identity.

## Refine repair limits

Broad contiguous root, blood and onion fields and resolved wing hairs remain open. Colour iodine still lacks a defensible scale; the actual monochrome source stays captioned. The new CC BY methylene-blue cheek field resolves nuclei but uses an explicitly approximate biological-size scale. No stain tint, inpainting, invented anatomy or tissue tiling was used.

Defocus now reconstructs a reduced-resolution layer instead of running a software Gaussian filter on every pan. This remains a declared rendering approximation, not a measured optical point-spread function.
