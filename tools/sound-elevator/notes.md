## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Ride**. Tap floor **8**. The elevator sings every floor on the way up. Ask: did the sound get higher or lower?
2. Tap floor **1**. Children move a hand down as the elevator goes down.
3. Turn the dial to **soft** and tap a floor, then to **loud**. The louder the note, the brighter the elevator glows.

## The four modes

- **Ride:** eight floors, the eight notes of a C major scale with solfège (do re mi fa sol la ti do). Arrow keys move up and down one floor. **Ride the whole scale** goes from bottom to top or top to bottom.
- **High or low?:** two notes with the doors closed. Children answer with their hands, then tap **Higher** or **Lower** to watch the ride. The first few pairs are far apart; later pairs get closer.
- **Loud or soft?:** one note, either loud or soft. Children make a big shape or a tiny shape, then see the glow.
- **Echo:** a three-note tune. Sing it back on "loo," then **Show the shape** to see the line go up, down or stay the same.

## Teaching notes

- High and low are about pitch, not volume. Children often say "loud" when they mean "high." The two separate controls (floors and the dial) help untangle them.
- Have students show pitch with their bodies: crouch for floor 1, stand tall with arms up for floor 8.
- Echo's line shape is an early step toward reading melodic contour on a staff.

## Talk about it

- Can a sound be high and soft? Low and loud? Try it on the elevator.
- What animals make high sounds? Low sounds?
- When do you use a soft voice? A loud voice?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 4 switch modes.
- Sound must be on. Notes are made in the browser, so the tool works offline.
