arc: explore-first
minutes: 30
focus: MU:Re7
goal: I can hear and show high and low sounds, loud and soft sounds, and whether a tune moves up or down.
materials: The tool on the big screen with sound on. Space for children to crouch and stretch.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet.
---
## Before class

- Open **Ride**. Sound must be on.
- High and low are about pitch, not volume. Children often say "loud" when they mean "high." The floors and the dial keep the two apart.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Sing a very high "hello," then a very low one. "What's different?"
2. "Sounds can go up and down, like an elevator. Let's ride!"

## Explore (12 min)

1. Tap floor **8**. The elevator sings every floor on the way up. "Did the sound get higher or lower?"
2. Tap floor **1**. Children move a hand down as the elevator goes down.
3. Press **Ride the whole scale** and have children crouch for floor 1 and stand tall with arms up for floor 8.
4. Turn the dial to **soft** and tap a floor, then to **loud**. "The louder the note, the brighter the elevator glows."

## Make sense (5 min)

1. "Can a sound be high and soft? Low and loud?" Try it on the elevator.
2. Switch to **High or low?** The doors are closed. Children answer with their hands, then tap **Higher** or **Lower** to watch the ride.

## Apply (6 min)

1. Switch to **Loud or soft?** Children make a big shape for loud and a tiny shape for soft, then see the glow.
2. Switch to **Echo**. Sing the three-note tune back on "loo," then press **Show the shape** to see if it went up, down or stayed the same.

## Exit check (3 min)

Play two notes in **High or low?** Each child shows with their body: tall for higher, crouched for lower. Then ask: "Was that sound high or loud?" Children answer with the word that fits.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** use only the far-apart pairs at the start of **High or low?**
- **Support:** keep one hand moving with every note on the ride.
- **Stretch:** "What animals make high sounds? Low sounds?"
- **Stretch:** a child sings a three-note tune and the class shows its shape with their hands.

## Short on time

- Floors **8** and **1** in **Ride** (4 min).
- Three rounds of **High or low?** (4 min).
