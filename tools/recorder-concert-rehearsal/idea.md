# Recorder Concert Rehearsal (idea card)

- Module: arts-3-5-ensemble-rehearsal-and-concert, grade 3-5. Codes: MU:Pr5, MU:Pr6, MU:Re9
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: You lead rehearsal for a four-player recorder group before the spring concert; listen to each take, mark the checklist (together, in tune, steady tempo, dynamics), find who and where, and give one note; the next take fixes exactly what you named, so a vague or wrong note gets you nowhere.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: four illustrated kids with recorders in a music room, a score of a public-domain tune (Hot Cross Buns, then Au Clair de la Lune, then a two-part round) and a rehearsal clipboard.
  - Core loop: play a take. The simulator builds each take from real problems (one player sharp, the group rushing in bar 3, a late entrance, everyone the same loudness). Mark the checklist, tap the player and the bar, and pick a note card ("Maya, cover the bottom hole all the way", "Watch me for the start"). The next take changes only what the note addressed.
  - The aha or the end state: concert day. The student joins as the fifth player (on a real recorder, singing or clapping the rhythm), learns concert manners (tune, wait for quiet, bow, stay still between pieces) and hears the group's first and final takes back to back.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the class marks the checklist together; an optional microphone check of the student's own part, computed on the device.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MU:Pr5: using the checklist and feedback to refine the ensemble's accuracy and expression, take by take.
  - MU:Pr6: performing the student's own part at the concert with expression, and the concert manners for this setting.
  - MU:Re9: evaluating each take against the established criteria.
- Not like: band-practice (grade 6-8: your own timing against a band); orchestra-zoo (instrument families); melody-maker (composing).
- Art: people (illustrated) for the players and audience; generated scene for the music room and stage; recorder audio recorded or sampled for Curio Class.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Lead a recorder group's rehearsal: mark each take on a checklist, find who and which bar went wrong, give one note and hear the next take fix just that, then join the spring concert yourself.
- Flags: records audio if the optional microphone check is used (on-screen line that it never leaves the device)
