# Case Conference (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-12-psychological-disorders-and-treatment, grade 12. Codes: APA.Mental and Physical Health
- Format / family: roleplay / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Sit on a clinic team with fictional case summaries: look at each through the biological, psychological and social lenses, see how disorders are classified, compare treatments by their research evidence, and trace how stress affects health; support resources stay on every screen.
