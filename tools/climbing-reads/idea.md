# Climbing Reads (idea card)

- Module: ela-6-8-reading-range-and-text-complexity, grade 6-8. Codes: RL.6.10, RL.7.10, RL.8.10, RI.6.10, RI.7.10, RI.8.10
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Climb from grade-level to top-of-band texts in fiction, poetry, drama, history, science and technical writing, using supports you remove one by one, with a check after each section and a range map of what you read.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a climbing wall of texts by genre. "Start at grade level; the top holds are the hardest."
  - Core loop: climb from grade-level to top-of-band texts in fiction, poetry, drama, history, science and technical writing, removing supports one by one, with a check after each section and a range map.
  - The aha or the end state: the student reads at the top of the band without supports.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RL.6.10, RL.7.10, RL.8.10 and RI.6.10, RI.7.10, RI.8.10: reading range and complexity, independently at the top of the band.
- Not like: stretch-shelf (3-5); tough-text-club (9-12).
- Art: public domain texts.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: range map on the device (rule 7)
