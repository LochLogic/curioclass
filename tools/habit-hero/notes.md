## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Hero's day**. Read the first moment: Pip just ate breakfast. What next?
2. Children hold up one finger or two. Tap each card to count the votes, then press **Decide**.
3. Watch Pip's meters move. Read Pip's line together, then press **Next**. Seven moments take you to bedtime.

## The three modes

- **Hero's day:** seven moments from 7:00 in the morning to 8:00 at night: brushing, handwashing after a sneeze, recess, a drink, free time, a bath and bedtime. Three meters (energy, clean, smile) go up or down. After a less healthy choice you can **Try the other choice**. The next morning shows how Pip feels.
- **When do we...?:** six habits (wash hands, brush teeth, sleep, drink water, move, cover a cough). Tap every right answer; one is always silly.
- **Timers:** handwashing in five steps with 20 seconds of scrubbing, and toothbrushing for 2 minutes, 30 seconds in each part of the mouth, with music.

## Facts behind it

- Handwashing: wet, soap, scrub at least 20 seconds, rinse, dry (CDC).
- Brushing: twice a day for two minutes (American Dental Association).
- Sleep: about 10 to 13 hours for ages 3 to 5 and 9 to 12 hours for ages 6 to 12 (American Academy of Sleep Medicine). Pip says "about ten to twelve hours."
- Moving: about an hour a day for children 6 and older (CDC).

## Talk about it

- Which habit do you already do every day? Which one could you add?
- Why does Pip feel grumpy after staying up late?
- When should you wash your hands at school?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes.
- Habits depend on home routines that children don't always control. Keep it about what helps our bodies, never about whose family does what.
