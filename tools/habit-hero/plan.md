arc: explore-first
minutes: 30
focus: NHES.S1; NHES.S7
goal: I can choose a healthy habit and tell how it helps my body.
materials: The tool on the big screen. Room to stand and move. Optional: a sink for real handwashing.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet.
---
## Before class

- Open **Hero's day**. The first moment is Pip after breakfast.
- Habits depend on home routines that children don't always control. Keep the talk about what helps bodies, never about whose family does what.
- Decide whether the class will wash hands at a sink during Apply.

## Hook (4 min)

1. "Who has a superpower that keeps them strong and happy every day?" Pause. "You all do. It's called a healthy habit."
2. "Today we help Pip the fox be a hero all day long, from breakfast to bedtime."

## Explore (12 min)

1. Read the first moment. Children hold up one finger or two to vote. Tap each card to count the votes, then press **Decide**.
2. Watch Pip's meters move: energy, clean and smile. Read Pip's line together, then press **Next**.
3. When the class picks a less healthy choice, press **Try the other choice**. "What changed on the meters?"
4. Keep going through the seven moments to bedtime. Stop at the bedtime choice: "Why does Pip feel grumpy after staying up late?"
5. Look at how Pip feels the next morning.

## Make sense (6 min)

1. Switch to **When do we...?** Tap every right answer for wash hands and drink water. One answer is always silly. Let children find it.
2. Ask: "Which habit helped Pip's energy meter? Which helped clean?" Make two lists on the board.

## Apply (5 min)

1. Open **Timers** and do handwashing together: five steps with 20 seconds of scrubbing, acted out with pretend soap. If you have a sink, do it for real.
2. Stand up and move with the music for 30 seconds of toothbrushing: "top teeth, bottom teeth, front, back."

## Exit check (3 min)

Each child turns to a partner and finishes the sentence: "My healthy habit is ___. It helps my body ___." Listen to four or five pairs. Children who name a habit but not how it helps need the meters again tomorrow.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** act out each habit before voting (brushing, washing, sleeping), so the cards make sense.
- **Support:** use the pictures on the cards and let children point instead of speaking.
- **Stretch:** "Which habit do you already do every day? Which one could you add?"
- **Stretch:** draw a picture of Pip doing their favorite healthy habit, with one sentence.

## Short on time

- **Hero's day** to bedtime, with voting (10 min).
- The handwashing timer to finish (3 min).
