# Invasive Species Alert (idea card)

- Module: science-7-ecosystem-change-and-biodiversity, grade 7. Codes: MS-LS2-4, MS-LS2-5
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Arrive at a small inland lake the summer zebra mussels are found, and over ten years choose responses scored on cost, biodiversity and services like clean water and fishing, watching the lake's populations and the town that depends on it change with each choice.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a lake town's dock, a boat with mussels on its hull, and a lake biologist who hands the student the new job. The setting is fictional, built from documented cases in North American lakes.
  - Core loop: each year brings two or three options (boat-wash stations, an awareness campaign, scraping intake pipes, chemical treatment, doing nothing). Before choosing, the student scores the options on a criteria card (cost, harm to native species, effect on clean water and fishing, chance of success). Then the year plays out: mussels filter the water clear, light reaches deeper, weeds spread, native mussels decline, the water plant's intake clogs. Year-by-year graphs show each population.
  - The aha or the end state: there is no perfect fix and prevention works best; the student writes an argument for one change, using the graphs as evidence, and compares their ten-year lake to the "do nothing" lake.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): compare ten-year lakes across students.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-LS2-4: each physical or biological change shows up in the population graphs, and the student argues its effect from that evidence.
  - MS-LS2-5: the criteria card has the student evaluate competing solutions for keeping biodiversity and ecosystem services.
- Not like: food-web-wobble (grade 5, removal); reserve-designer (grade 9 land budget); lake-commons (grade 9 shared fishing over generations).
- Art: generated scenes of the lake through the years; people (illustrated) for the biologist, an angler and the water plant worker.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Zebra mussels turn up in a small lake; over ten years pick responses scored on cost, biodiversity and clean water, and watch native species, fishing and the town's water intake respond.
- Flags: needs a data source (documented zebra mussel effects from USGS or Sea Grant, checked)
