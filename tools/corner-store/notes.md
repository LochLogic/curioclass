## A 5-minute start

1. The first customer walks up and orders. Find the snack on the menu board and read its price together.
2. The customer pays. Press **Count it together** (or tap each coin, biggest first) and count along out loud: "25, 35, 40."
3. If they paid more than the price, make change by **counting up**: start at the price and add coins from the drawer until you reach what they paid. Then press **Give change**.

## The big idea

Making change is counting up. If a pretzel costs 35¢ and you pay with two quarters, start at 35¢ and count up to 50¢: a dime makes 45¢, a nickel makes 50¢. Your change is 15¢. Counting the biggest coins first makes it faster.

## Talk about it

- Is there more than one way to give the same change? Which way uses the fewest coins?
- Why is a dime worth more than a nickel, even though it's smaller?
- The clock moved 5 minutes for every customer. How many customers fit between 3:00 and 3:45?

## Good to know

- The first customers pay exactly, so there's only counting. Change gets harder as the day goes on, ending with dollar bills.
- Coins are drawn at their real sizes next to each other. **Values** shows or hides the labels under each coin, for when the class is ready to name them on sight.
- Tap a coin in **Your change** to take it back. Too much or too little change, and the customer says so.
- At 3:45 the bell rings and the day's money is added up in dollars and cents. **Open again** starts a new day with new customers.
- Sound starts on. There's no score.
