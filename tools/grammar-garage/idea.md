# Grammar Garage (idea card)

- Module: ela-4-grammar-in-use, grade 4. Codes: L.4.1
- Format / family: puzzle / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: Broken sentences roll into a repair shop with a clear symptom (it means the wrong thing, it won't start, it's in pieces), and the student diagnoses and fixes each with the right part, so they learn that grammar parts like who and which, was running, might and must, or a well-placed phrase change what a sentence means.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a garage bay with a sentence on a lift: "The dog chased the cat who was barking." A mechanic: "Customer says the cat isn't the one barking. Find the problem."
  - Core loop: each bay is one part: relative pronouns and adverbs (who, whose, which, where, why), progressive tenses (was running, will be running), modals (can, may, must, might) where the fix changes meaning (You may go against You must go), adjective order (a small old red wagon), prepositional phrases placed to say the right thing, fragments and run-ons that won't start, and confusing word pairs (to, too, two; there, their). Each fix is tested by a picture that acts the sentence out.
  - The aha or the end state: a final "mystery car" sentence with three problems, and the student explains each fix in a repair note.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a timed shift for review.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.4.1: one bay per part of the standard (relative pronouns and adverbs, progressive tenses, modals, adjective order, prepositional phrases, complete sentences, confused words), each fixed and acted out.
- Not like: story-blanks (grade 3 arcade); comma-chameleon (grade 5 commas); sentence-remixer (grade 6).
- Art: illustrated garage and mechanic, plus illustrated act-out pictures for each sentence, because the picture shows what the sentence means.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Broken sentences roll into a repair shop with a symptom (wrong meaning, won't start, in pieces); fix relative pronouns, progressive tenses, modals, adjective order, phrases and run-ons, and a picture acts out each repair.
- Flags: none
