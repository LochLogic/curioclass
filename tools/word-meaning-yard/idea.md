# Word Meaning Yard (idea card)

- Module: ela-k-word-meanings-and-word-play, grade k. Codes: L.K.4, L.K.5
- Format / family: sort / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Sort farmyard pictures by meaning (a duck and a boy who ducks), add -ed, -s, re- and un- to change a word, pair opposites, find words at home, and act out walk, march and strut to feel shades of meaning.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a sunny farmyard and a picture card of a duck. "Is this duck the bird, or a boy who ducks? Drag it to the right pen."
  - Core loop: sort picture pairs by meaning (duck and duck, bat and bat); snap -s, -ed, re- and un- onto a word and watch the picture change (pack, unpack, repack); pair opposites at the gate; then act out walk, march and strut with the farm animals to feel shades of meaning.
  - The aha or the end state: the yard fills with sorted pens, and the child explains one word that means two things.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a "find it at home" card asks for something cozy or squishy to name.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - L.K.4: multiple-meaning words sorted by picture; -ed, -s, re-, un- change a known word.
  - L.K.5: sorting into categories, opposites at the gate, real-life word connections, acting out shades of verbs.
- Not like: vocabulary-jar (new words from read-alouds); sentence-train (building sentences).
- Art: generated scenes (a farmyard with clear picture cards), because K pre-readers work from pictures and audio.
