# Leaf Factory (idea card)

- Module: science-7-photosynthesis-and-respiration, grade 7. Codes: MS-LS1-6, MS-LS1-7
- Format / family: tycoon / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Run a leaf as a factory that takes in light, water and carbon dioxide and turns out sugar and oxygen, then decide how to spend the sugar (burn it for energy, build new parts, store it), and follow one carbon atom into a caterpillar that eats the leaf.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a leaf cut open as a factory floor, with chloroplast assembly lines, stomata doors, a sugar warehouse and a sunrise.
  - Core loop: by day, open the stomata to let carbon dioxide in (and lose water), and sunlight runs the lines; by night the lines stop but the factory still burns sugar to stay running. Spend sugar on roots (more water), new leaves (more light), starch for winter or a flower. Seasons and a dry spell test the plan; the ledger always shows atoms in and out.
  - Atom tracker: tap one carbon atom in the air and follow it into sugar, then into the cellulose of a new leaf. A caterpillar eats that leaf, and the same atom is rearranged into caterpillar body or breathed back out as carbon dioxide.
  - The aha or the end state: plants make their own food and still respire; food is rearranged into new molecules for growth or broken down to release energy, in the plant and in whatever eats it.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-LS1-6: the factory ledger and atom tracker give the evidence for a short explanation of photosynthesis's role in moving matter and energy into and out of organisms.
  - MS-LS1-7: sugar spending and the caterpillar model food rearranged into new molecules that support growth or release energy.
- Not like: energy-accountant (grade 9 carbon ledger); food-web-wobble (grade 5 web); lake-commons (grade 9 tycoon of a commons).
- Art: generated scenes for the factory floor and the seasons; the atom tracker and ledger drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Run a leaf as a factory: take in light, water and carbon dioxide, make sugar, then spend it to grow, store or burn at night; follow one carbon atom into a caterpillar that eats the leaf.
- Flags: overlap risk (energy-accountant, grade 9; keep this one about one leaf's choices)
