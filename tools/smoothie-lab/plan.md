arc: explore-first
minutes: 45
focus: 6.RP.A
goal: I can use ratio language, tables of equivalent ratios, graphs and unit rates to scale a recipe.
materials: The tool on the projector and on student devices. Graph paper for ratio tables.
grouping: Whole class for the hook, then pairs on devices.
---
## Before class

- Open **Explore** with an empty jug.
- The jug holds 24 scoops. Adding fruit after blending starts a fresh jug.
- Smoothie color is mixed from the fruit amounts, so equivalent recipes really are the same color.

## Hook (5 min)

1. Make 4 scoops of **strawberry** and 6 of **banana**. Press **Blend and taste**. That's batch 1.
2. "I want a smaller batch that tastes exactly the same. How many scoops of each?" Take guesses.

## Explore (13 min)

1. Make 2 strawberry and 3 banana, and blend. The owner says it tastes exactly like batch 1, and the log marks it "= #1."
2. Pairs try other recipes and predict which will taste the same as batch 1. They blend to check.
3. Pairs look at the graph. "Where do the equal-tasting recipes land?" (On one straight line through 0.)
4. Ask: "The layers in the jug are each one scoop. What fraction of the smoothie is strawberry?"

## Make sense (10 min)

1. "Why do 4 : 6 and 2 : 3 taste the same?" (Both parts were divided by 2.)
2. Build a ratio table on the board: 2 : 3, 4 : 6, 6 : 9, 8 : 12. "What do you multiply by to get each row?"
3. Find the unit rate: "How many scoops of banana for 1 scoop of strawberry?" (1.5.)
4. "Why do all the equal-tasting recipes sit on a straight line that starts at 0?"

## Apply (12 min)

1. Pairs switch to **Orders**. Each order asks for a recipe plus one condition: scale up, fill to a total, simplest form, a percent, a unit rate.
2. Every try lands on the graph: red tastes wrong, gold tastes right but misses the condition, green fills the order.
3. "What did you multiply by? How do you know?"

## Exit check (5 min)

On a card: "A smoothie uses 3 scoops of mango for every 5 of yogurt. Make a ratio table with three rows. How much mango for 20 scoops of yogurt? What is the unit rate of yogurt per scoop of mango?"

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** stay with doubling and halving before other scale factors.
- **Support:** use the hint after two wrong tries.
- **Stretch:** fill every order in **Orders** on the first try.
- **Stretch:** "Two batches are the same color. What do you know about their ratios?"

## Short on time

- Batch 1 and batch 2 in **Explore** (8 min).
- Two orders in **Orders** (8 min), then the exit card (4 min).
