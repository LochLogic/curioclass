# Border Trade Post (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-6-border-trade-post, grade 6. Codes: D2.Eco.5.6-8, D2.Eco.15.6-8, D2.Eco.2.6-8
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: At a border post between two neighboring countries, the student runs three rounds of trade (barter, then money, then a tariff on imported bikes), tallying time lost and trades made, then compares a tariff with other answers to the same problem and sees who gains or pays: shoppers, bike makers, their workers and the other country.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a border gate with two market stalls and a clock.
  - Core loop: round 1, barter: hunt for someone who has what you want and wants what you have; the clock shows the time wasted. Round 2, money: the same trades happen fast. Round 3, set a tariff; shoppers pay more, home bike makers sell more, and the other country may answer with its own tariff.
  - The aha or the end state: a scoreboard of who gained and who paid under each choice (tariff, no tariff, help for laid-off bike workers), and a short written judgment of which is fairest and why.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): two students run the two countries.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Eco.5.6-8: rounds 1 and 2, timing trades with and without money.
  - D2.Eco.15.6-8: round 3's tariff and its costs and benefits to people, businesses and society.
  - D2.Eco.2.6-8: comparing the tariff with other answers, group by group.
- Not like: barter-bay (grade 4, barter to money, inflation inside one town; here barter is a short opener); smoke-and-honey (hidden costs inside one town); cocoa-chain (a supply chain).
- Art: generated scenes and people (illustrated) for traders and workers.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (with barter-bay's barter round; keep round 1 brief and timed)
