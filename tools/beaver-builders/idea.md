# Beaver Builders (idea card)

- Module: science-k-living-things-change-their-environment, grade k. Codes: K-ESS2-2, K-ESS3-1, K-ESS3-3
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Help a beaver family chew trees and stack sticks and mud across a stream, watch it become a pond that frogs, ducks, fish and a moose move into, then see that people change places too and choose ways a family nearby can take less and share the valley.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a fast stream in a valley with aspen trees and few animals. "The beavers need deep water to be safe. Help them build."
  - Core loop: tap trees for the beavers to chew, drag sticks and mud onto the dam; over sped-up seasons the water rises and spreads into a pond with a lodge. New animals arrive, each with a card showing what it needs.
  - Matching step: drag each plant and animal to the part of the valley that meets its needs (fish in deep water, a moose eating willows at the edge, a squirrel in the trees).
  - People step: a family moves in and builds a house and a road. The student picks choices with them (a pipe under the road so water flows, leave trees by the water, reuse and recycle, walk or bike) and sees the pond and its animals stay healthy or struggle.
  - The aha or the end state: a before-and-after picture of the valley, and the student says how the beavers changed it to meet their needs and how the family helps.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - K-ESS2-2: the dam turning a stream into a pond is the evidence that animals change their environment to meet their needs; the family clearing land for a house is the people case.
  - K-ESS3-1: the matching step is a model of which plants and animals live where their needs are met.
  - K-ESS3-3: the people step, choosing and telling ways to reduce impact on land, water, air and living things.
- Not like: terrarium-pal (needs of living things in a jar); shade-builder (people designing against sunlight).
- Art: generated scenes for the valley as it changes, with illustrated animals, because the before-and-after has to look real.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Help a beaver family dam a stream and watch it become a pond that new animals move into, then help a family nearby choose ways to share the valley and take less from it.
- Flags: none
