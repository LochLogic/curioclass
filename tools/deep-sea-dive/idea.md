# Deep Sea Dive (idea card)

- Module: math-6-negative-numbers, grade 6. Codes: 6.NS.C
- Format / family: map / number-line. Fit: good.
- One idea: Map a coastline in cross-section, where up is above sea level, down is below, east of the pier is positive and west is negative, so divers, gulls and crabs become points in all four quadrants, and the water's surface becomes a mirror line.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a vertical number line at the end of a pier, sea level at 0, a gull at +8 m and a diver, Noa, at -12 m. "Who is farther from the surface?"
  - Number-line rounds: place creatures and gear at positive and negative heights; write opposites (+5 and -5 meet at 0); order them; read statements like -3 > -7 as "-3 m is higher than -7 m". Absolute value is distance from the surface, and the student sorts claims: "the diver is deeper" (order) vs "the diver is farther from the surface" (absolute value).
  - Opposites in other places: the dive shop's account at -30 dollars is a debt of 30; water at -2 degrees C; each card is a sign plus a meaning.
  - Chart rounds: the map widens into a coordinate plane; the student plots a kelp bed at (-4, -6) and a lighthouse at (3, 10), names quadrants by signs, and reflects points across the surface or across the pier line.
  - Distance rounds: a gull at (2, 6) right above a fish at (2, -5) is 11 m away; a buoy and a boat on the same depth line; the student finds distances from coordinates and checks on the map.
  - The aha or the end state: a negative number is a direction as well as a size, and absolute value keeps only the size.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a dive log challenge for the class.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 6.NS.C: signed numbers for heights, depths, debts and temperatures (6.NS.5); opposites, points on the number line and in four quadrants, and reflections across the axes (6.NS.6); ordering, inequalities in context and absolute value as distance (6.NS.7); four-quadrant problems and distances between points sharing a coordinate (6.NS.8).
- Not like: hot-air-and-sandbags (grade 7 operations with signed numbers), sea-search-grid (grade 5 first quadrant), square-root-hunt (same number-line engine, grade 8).
- Art: generated scenes (a cross-section of a rocky coast with sky, sea and seabed); people (illustrated) for Noa; grid and number line drawn in code.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Map a coast in cross-section with sea level at 0: place divers and gulls on a vertical number line, compare order and distance from the surface, then plot sea life in all four quadrants and reflect points across the water.
- Flags: none
