## A 5-minute start

1. Everyone stands up. Open **Dance Floor** and tap three moves in the tray, for example **Wave**, **Clap** and **Jump**. Each tap adds a picture card to the strip, and Bolt shows the move once.
2. Press **Go!** Bolt dances every card in order, left to right, one step on each beat. The class dances along with Bolt.
3. Ask: "What if we swap two cards?" Tap a card, use the arrow buttons to move it, and press **Go!** again. Same moves, different order, different dance.

## The two modes

- **Dance Floor:** free play and a quick brain break. Tap a move to add a card (up to 8). Tap a card to pick it up: then tap a move to change it, use the arrows to move it earlier or later, or the trash can to take it away. **Clear** empties the strip. The turtle and rabbit button switches between Slow and Quick.
- **Fix-It Show:** 8 short rounds. Dot knows the real dance. Bolt's cards start with one bug in them. Press **Watch Dot** to see the real dance (as often as you like), then **Go!**: both robots dance together, and Bolt trips on the first step that does not match. That card wobbles with a red "?" and Dot's thought bubble shows the right move. The class fixes the cards and presses **Go!** again. When Bolt does the whole dance, the round is fixed and **Next round** lights up.
  - Rounds 1 to 4: one wrong card. Pick it up and tap the right move.
  - Rounds 5 and 6: two cards in the wrong order. Use the arrows.
  - Round 7: a card is missing. Add it (it goes at the end), then walk it into place with the left arrow.
  - Round 8: one card too many. Use the trash can.
  - After round 8, both robots dance the last dance together and the team totals show. **Play again** starts over.

## Teams

**Teams** is on at the start: odd rounds are Team Star's turn, even rounds Team Moon's, and the team whose turn it is gets a star when its round is fixed. Trips never cost anything, because tripping is how we find bugs. Stars can't be lost, and **Start over** keeps a star already earned. Turn **Teams** off for whole-class play: the badges hide and the narrator praises everyone. Turning it back on brings the stars back.

## Words to use

- **Algorithm:** a list of steps to finish a task. The cards are Bolt's algorithm.
- **Bug:** a part of a program that does not work correctly. Here, the wrong card.
- **Debugging:** finding and fixing the bug.
- "Bolt does exactly what the cards say." Bolt never guesses. If the cards are wrong, the dance is wrong.

## Talk about it

- Bolt tripped on step 3. Is the bug in card 3, or in card 1? How do you know?
- In round 5 every move is right, but the dance is still wrong. Why?
- What did Bolt do when there were no more cards? What did Bolt do when Dot was already finished?
- Can you make a dance with the same moves in a different order? Is it the same dance?

## Good to know

- On a smartboard, children can come up and tap the cards themselves. With a keyboard: Tab to a card, Enter to pick it up, left and right arrows to move it, Delete to take it away, Escape to put it down.
- **Read** speaks the line at the top of the panel again. **Sound** off stops the narrator and the dance sounds.
- **Motion** off keeps the same beat and sounds, but the robots switch poses without moving between them, there is no confetti, and Bolt sits down at once when it trips.
- Standards: CSTA K-12 Computer Science Standards EK-ALG-PS-01, EK-PRO-PD-04, EK-PRO-TR-07 (2017: 1A-AP-08, 1A-AP-10, 1A-AP-14).
- Everything, including the robots, is drawn in code, and the sounds are made in the browser, so it works offline.
