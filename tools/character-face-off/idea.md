# Character Face-Off (idea card)

- Module: ela-5-comparing-characters-and-themes, grade 5. Codes: RL.5.3, RL.5.9, RL.5.2
- Format / family: cards / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: The student builds trading cards for two characters from two adventure stories, each stat backed by a detail from the text, then plays them head to head on how each meets a challenge, so they learn to compare characters, settings and themes on evidence and see how two stories in one genre handle the same big idea.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: two public-domain adventure stories in short adapted form (for example a lost-at-sea chapter and a lost-in-the-woods chapter) and two blank trading cards.
  - Core loop: fill each card's slots (wants, fears, how they solve problems, setting, turning point), and each slot needs a pinned detail from the story. Then face-off rounds: a challenge card (Lost and alone, A friend in trouble) asks how each character responded, and the student plays the matching details and says which choice was braver, smarter or kinder, and why. A summary slot on each card holds a three-sentence summary.
  - The aha or the end state: the theme round: both characters faced being alone; the student writes what each story says about it, and the cards show where the two themes match and where they part.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): two players each build one card and play.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RL.5.3: comparing the two characters, settings and key events, slot by slot, on specific details.
  - RL.5.9: the theme round compares how two adventure stories approach the same theme.
  - RL.5.2: each story's theme from how its character meets challenges, and the summary slot.
- Not like: character-web (grade 9, one character's pulls); theme-tracker (grade 4, one story's theme); character-choice-story (grade 3 choices).
- Art: illustrated trading cards and public domain illustrations from the original books where available (ids to be checked at spec).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Build trading cards for two characters from two adventure stories, every stat backed by a detail, then play them head to head on how each met a challenge, and compare what each story says about the same theme.
- Flags: needs a data source (two public-domain adventure stories with comparable themes, adapted for grade 5)
