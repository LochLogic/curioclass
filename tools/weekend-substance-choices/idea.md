# Weekend Substance Choices (idea card)

- Module: health-pe-9-12-alcohol-drugs-and-tobacco, grade 9-12. Codes: NHES.S1, NHES.S4, NHES.S5, CASEL.Responsible Decision-Making
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good, because a weekend of choices with consequences is the play.
- One idea: Live one weekend as a high school junior (a Friday party, a Saturday car ride, a Sunday sleepover) where alcohol, vapes and cannabis edibles show up; choose what to say and do, and see each choice's effects on the body, under the law and in your friendships, then recognize when a friend is in danger and get them help.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a phone lock screen with Friday's group chat. "Your weekend starts now."
  - Core loop: each scene stops at a decision (a cup handed to you, a vape passed around, a friend who drank offering a ride, an edible that "isn't working yet"); choose words and an action, then three panels update: body (what the substance does, with real timing, such as edibles taking 30 minutes to 2 hours to kick in), law (under 21 is illegal for alcohol, cannabis and tobacco everywhere in the US; driving impaired), and friendships.
  - Help a friend: at the Sunday sleepover, a friend can't be woken and is breathing slowly; the student checks the danger signs, turns them on their side, stays with them and calls 911, and hears how many states protect people who call for help.
  - The aha or the end state: a weekend map shows each decision, its effects, and that saying no and getting help were real choices at every point.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - NHES.S1: learn what alcohol, nicotine and cannabis do to a teen body and brain, with real timings and risks.
  - NHES.S4: practice refusal words and asking an adult or 911 for help.
  - NHES.S5: use a decision process at each choice point and weigh the outcomes.
  - CASEL.Responsible Decision-Making: think through consequences for yourself, friends and others on the road.
- Not like: pressure-point (grade 6-8 peer pressure and refusal); captain-s-choices (grade 9-12 team culture); mind-map (mental health).
- Art: people (illustrated) teens in grounded, non-glamorous scenes; no product brands.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: sensitive (alcohol, cannabis, vaping, alcohol poisoning; teacher notes name these, rule 8); needs a data source (medical facts and Good Samaritan law coverage, sourced and dated, since laws differ by state)
