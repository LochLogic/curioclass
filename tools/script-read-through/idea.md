# Script Read-Through (idea card)

- Module: ela-3-5-readers-theater-and-fluency, grade 3-5. Codes: RF.3.4, RF.4.3, RF.4.4, RF.5.3, RF.5.4
- Format / family: showdown / read-aloud. Fit: change to studio, because the default must be one student alone: they read one part while a recorded cast reads the others; small groups become the class mode.
- One idea: The student takes a part in a short script, rehearses with a scrolling prompter that marks pauses and stress, cracks long words by tapping them into syllables and parts, and records a take that plays with the cast, so they hear their own reading get smoother and more expressive from first take to last.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a stage with a script on a stand: "The Lighthouse Keeper's Cat," four parts. "Pick your part. The cast will read the rest."
  - Core loop: the prompter scrolls at a chosen pace with pause marks and bold stress words; tapping a long word (unbelievable, extraordinary) splits it into prefix, syllables and suffix, and plays it slowly. The student records their lines; the cast's recorded lines play in between. The on-screen line says recordings never leave the device; there is no speech recognition, so the student checks against the model reading.
  - The aha or the end state: take 1 and take 3 play back to back with a waveform that shows pauses and steadier pace, and a self-check card (Did I pause at the dashes? Did my voice rise at questions? Did I fix a slip using what the sentence means?).
  - Class mode or extras (optional): groups split the parts on one screen and record a full performance; a cast who speaks in different registers (a radio announcer, a great-grandmother's 1920s letter, a text-message kid) lets grade 5 compare varieties of English.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - RF.3.4, RF.4.4, RF.5.4: reading a part with accuracy, pace and expression, rereading and self-correcting from context, checked across takes.
  - RF.4.3, RF.5.3: tapping long words into syllables, prefixes, suffixes and roots, then reading them in place.
  - Takes in from draft-car-wash: L.5.3 (comparing varieties of English) through the register cast, in class mode and solo.
- Not like: echo-reader (grade 1, echo a model passage); planetarium-guide (planning and giving a talk); syllable-surgeon (grade 3 decoding clinic).
- Art: people (illustrated) cast and stage sets; recorded cast voices, because the solo reader needs scene partners.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Take a part in a short script and rehearse with a scrolling prompter while a recorded cast reads the other parts; tap long words to split them into syllables and parts, and compare your first and last takes.
- Flags: sensitive (register cast lines get a fresh review per rule 5 so no variety of English is mocked); needs a data source (recorded cast voices)
