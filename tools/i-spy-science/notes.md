## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Explore** on the **Pond**. Tap the frog. It zooms in and the tool says: "Frog. It is green. It is small. It has four legs."
2. Say it together. Then tap something else and let a volunteer describe it first.
3. Switch to **I spy**. Read the first clue and let children point before you tap.

## The three modes

- **Explore:** four places (pond, garden, forest floor, beach) with six things each. Tapping zooms in, says the word and three describing sentences, and the tip says whether it's living.
- **I spy:** four clues, one at a time: color, size, parts, what it can do. Children point; a wrong tap names the thing and nudges back to the clues. Then let a child give the clues.
- **Describe it:** tap a thing, then choose its color, size, parts and texture from word cards (one right word in each row). **Say it together** reads the whole sentence.

## Language support

- Sentence frames: *I see a ___. It is ___. It has ___. It feels ___. It can ___.*
- Beginners can point and say one word; more fluent speakers add a second sentence or compare two things ("The frog is smaller than the duck").
- Texture words (smooth, bumpy, fuzzy, slimy) are easier with real objects. Bring in a pinecone or a shell if you can.

## Talk about it

- Which things are living? Which are not? How do you know?
- What's the same about the bee and the ladybug?
- I spy something in our classroom that is...

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes.
- Change places with the buttons in the side panel.
