# Choreography for a Cause

## One idea
Turn research into movement: every card in an issue's fact file (a number, a voice, a picture) becomes a motif through a stated rule (a falling number sinks a level, a split becomes two dancers pulling apart, a winding river becomes a curved pathway), you arrange the motifs in a dance form, and a panel of model viewers watches it and says what they think it's about, so you revise until they can name your issue.

## Not like
- choreography-board (6-8, studio, dance family): revises 8-count phrases with choreographic devices and compares versions in sync. Here the motifs come from research, devices are a minor tool, and the test is whether viewers read the meaning.
- dance-styles-explorer (9-12, explorer): traces of existing styles for critique. Here you make an original dance about an issue.
- poem-to-movement (3-5, this batch): turns a poem's words into moves. Here the source is data and research, the dance has a form, and an audience reads it back.
- museum-courtyard-performance (this band): produces a dance in a site with budget and crew. Here nothing is produced; you create and revise the piece itself.
- sketchbook-process-journal (this band): an issue and a tradition become a visual plan, tracked on an idea map. Here an issue becomes movement, tracked by what viewers read.

## Who and how
- Grade 9-12, dance, module "Choreographing an Issue (Dance)".
- Topic line: choreographing a dance about an issue
- Driver: students on laptops, alone or in pairs. 30 to 45 minutes; versions are saved on this device. Students can stand up and try each motif in the room (a line on the Motifs screen invites it).
- Standards: NCAS DA:Cr1 (explore movement from stimuli), DA:Cr2 (plan and structure a dance), DA:Cr3 (revise from feedback), DA:Cn10 (synthesize research and personal experience in dance).

## Screens and modes
Header: title, topic line, `Read`, `Motion`, `Sound`. Own light palette (Look). Modes (`.seg`): `Fact file` (default), `Motifs`, `Structure`, `Viewers`. Motifs opens once an issue is chosen; Viewers once a structure has at least 3 filled slots.

**Fact file.** Focus: the open folder of five cards, the biggest thing on screen. Three issue folders (Light pollution, Food waste, The Colorado River) as tabs; each opens to five cards: two Data cards (the number and a small code chart), one Voice card (a short quote), two Picture cards (painted). Every card shows its source line. `Choose this issue` (changing later asks first; motifs from the old issue are kept but grayed).

**Motifs.** Focus: the stage with the dancers performing the selected motif, at least 55% of the stage area at 1366 x 768. Left: the issue's cards as a column; tap one, then `Make a motif`. The card's rule (Content) proposes an 8-count motif and plays it once (dancers on the engine's `stage` floor). Panel controls for the motif: `Pose A` / `Pose B` (pickers of four poses each, from the card), `Dynamic` (the card's default plus smooth, sharp, suspend, collapse, shake), `Level` (start and end: high, middle, low), `Pathway` (none, line, curve, circle, zigzag), `Dancers` (1, 2, 3), `Name` (30 characters) and `What it shows` (80 characters, the student's own meaning). `Play motif` (loops until Stop), `Stop`, `Delete motif`. Up to 6 motifs; each keeps its source card's badge (D, V or P with the card's short name). A line under the stage: "Stand up and try it. Does it feel like the card?"

**Structure.** Focus: the form strip (slots in order) with the stage above it. `Form` picker: `ABA`, `Rondo ABACA`, `Theme and variations`, `Narrative`. The strip shows the form's slots with their letters; drag a motif onto a slot (or select a slot and tap a motif). Per slot: `Repeat` (1 or 2 times: 8 or 16 counts), `Dancers` (1, 2, 3), `Together` toggle (unison or canon, 2 counts apart), `Device` (as made, bigger, smaller, slower, reversed). In Theme and variations all four slots take the A motif and must differ by device; in Narrative 2 to 6 slots in any order. `Play dance` plays the whole structure with music (up to 96 counts at 100 beats a minute, under 60 s: it is the dance), with the current slot lit on the strip; `Stop`; tap any slot to play from it. `Save version` (v1, v2, ... up to 8).

**Viewers.** Focus: the four viewer cards around the stage, with the stage replaying the dance in the middle. `Show viewers` plays the dance once more while each viewer's card fills in after the slot their reading depends on (the friend's after the last slot, and so on). Each card: who they are, "I think it's about ..." (a phrase), "I remember ..." (the motif's name), and one craft note when its rule fires. Over the cards: "3 of 4 viewers named your issue." A note says: "These viewers are a model of how audiences read dance, not real people." `Add a real viewer`: a classmate types what they think it's about and what they remember (shown as a fifth card, stored with the version). Panel: the version list (version, form, viewers who named the issue) and `Back to Structure` to revise.

## Content
**Fact file** (card id, kind, text shown, source short name, the rule's proposal, viewer words: first word is what a viewer says, "about ..." phrase):
Light pollution (issue words: light, night, stars, dark, sky):
- L-D1 Data "More than 80% of the world's people live under light-polluted skies; in the US, more than 99%." (Falchi 2016). Rule "Most of us": three dancers, two finish in the same bright open shape, one stays apart. Poses arms-wide, reach-up, releve, stand; dynamic smooth; level middle to high; pathway none. Words: everyone, "about something that happens to almost everyone".
- L-D2 Data "The Milky Way is hidden from more than a third of humanity, including nearly 80% of North Americans." (Falchi 2016). Rule "Hidden": reach up, then fold the arms over the head and curl. Poses reach-up, curl, crouch, contraction; suspend; high to low; none. Words: hidden, "about something being hidden".
- L-V Voice (made up for this tool, an astronomy club member): "When I was little I could see the Milky Way from our porch. Now I drive an hour to find it." Rule "Searching": walk, stop, look up, accents on counts 1, 4 and 7. Poses step, point, reach-up, twist; suspend; middle; line. Words: searching, "about searching for something far away".
- L-P1 Picture (a town's glow dome at night): Rule "Glow dome": arms sweep a wide arc overhead while circling. Poses arms-wide, reach-up, bend-side, releve; smooth; middle to high; circle. Words: light, "about too much light".
- L-P2 Picture (a dark desert sky full of stars): Rule "Starfield": three dancers hold still star shapes, one breaks the stillness each two counts. Poses star-jump, reach-up, arabesque, releve; sharp; high; none. Words: stars, "about the stars".
Food waste (issue words: food, waste, trash, eating, hungry):
- F-D1 Data "In 2010, 31% of the food at US stores and homes went uneaten." (USDA ERS). Rule "One in three": three dancers move together; one drops out to the floor on count 5. Poses step, plie, sit, lie; collapse; middle to low; none. Words: loss, "about losing something".
- F-D2 Data "That was 133 billion pounds of food, worth $161.6 billion." (USDA ERS). Rule "Too much": the motif grows each 2 counts (bigger, wider, heavier). Poses plie, lunge, arms-wide, stamp; sharp; middle to low; none. Words: heaviness, "about something heavy piling up".
- F-V Voice (made up, a cafeteria worker): "Every day I scrape full trays into the bin. Whole apples. Whole sandwiches." Rule "Scrape": sharp sweeping arm on counts 1, 3, 5, 7, the body bending forward. Poses bend-fwd, twist, clap, stand; sharp; middle; line. Words: throwing away, "about throwing things away".
- F-P1 Picture (a bin overflowing with food scraps): Rule "Tumble": bodies tip and spill to the floor one after another. Poses bend-fwd, crouch, sit, lie; collapse; middle to low; zigzag. Words: food, "about food in the trash".
- F-P2 Picture (a fridge shelf of forgotten leftovers): Rule "Forgotten": frozen shapes that slowly sag. Poses kneel, curl, sit, contraction; suspend; low; none. Words: forgotten, "about forgetting".
The Colorado River (issue words: water, river, dry, drought, flow):
- R-D1 Data "About 40 million people rely on Colorado River water." (US Bureau of Reclamation). Rule "Many, one source": three dancers travel in to meet at the center. Poses step, point, arms-wide, plie; smooth; middle; line. Words: sharing, "about sharing something".
- R-D2 Data "A 1922 agreement split 15 million acre-feet a year: 7.5 million upstream, 7.5 million downstream." (Colorado River Compact). Rule "Split": two dancers mirror each other and pull apart. Poses lunge, arms-wide, twist, stand; sharp; middle; line (apart). Words: dividing, "about splitting something up".
- R-V Voice (made up, a farmer near Yuma): "My grandfather never once worried about the water. I check the reports every week." Rule "Waiting": a steady walk that stops dead on count 8, then a look back. Poses step, stand, twist, point; swing then stop; middle; line. Words: worry, "about worrying and waiting".
- R-P1 Picture (a low reservoir with a white ring on the canyon walls): Rule "Sinking": a level drop in four steps, high to low. Poses releve, stand, crouch, kneel; collapse; high to low; none. Words: water, "about water going down".
- R-P2 Picture (a river winding through desert): Rule "Flow": a curved, continuous traveling path in canon. Poses step, arms-wide, bend-side, releve; smooth; middle; curve. Words: river, "about a river".

**Viewer panel** (the model; each viewer reads one motif):
- The friend reads the motif seen most: presence = sum over slots of 8 x repeats x dancers.
- The dance teacher reads the A motif (the first slot), and gives the craft note "Your B looked too much like your A; I couldn't feel the change" when A and B (the second slot's motif) differ in fewer than 2 of: dynamic, level start and end, pathway, dancers.
- The stranger reads the last slot, and gives "It just stopped. It didn't feel like an ending" unless the last slot repeats an earlier motif or uses 3 dancers together.
- The kid reads the biggest motif: size = level span (high 2, middle 1, low 0) + travel (none 0, line 0.5, curve, circle or zigzag 1); ties go to presence.
- A viewer names the issue when their motif's first word is one of the issue's words. Devices don't change a motif's words.

**Music.** One original groove for Curio Class, "Lantern", 100 beats a minute in 4/4, made with the engine's `Dance.music`: kick on 1 and 3, shaker on the off-beats, a soft pad holding A minor, F major, C major, G major (one chord per 8 counts), a pluck playing A4 C5 E5 C5 on beats 1 to 4 of each bar at low volume. Count-in 4 clicks.

## Interactions in detail
- One run at a time (dance family rule): any Play stops the clock, play, music and voice before starting; Stop halts in the same frame; tapping a slot while playing jumps there at once.
- Motion off: poses jump once per count (engine), timing the same.
- A motif with Dancers 1 placed in a slot with Dancers 3 plays as three in unison or canon (the slot wins).
- Empty slots are skipped in playback and show "Empty" on the strip; Viewers stays disabled until 3 slots are filled (its button hint says so in words).
- Theme and variations: two slots with the same device show "Variations should differ" and the second gets the next unused device automatically if the student taps `Fix`.
- Extremes: one motif in every slot gives all four viewers the same reading (the friend, teacher, stranger and kid agree) and the teacher's contrast note fires. A Narrative of 6 slots each repeated twice is 96 counts, 57.6 s.
- Version list: each saved version stores form, slots, motifs, the model results and any real viewer cards. Opening an old version loads it into Structure (with a confirm if the current one is unsaved).
- Saving: localStorage `curio-choreo-cause`. `Clear all` (confirm).
- Sizes: the stage keeps the dancers at least 120 px tall at 1366 x 768 and 70 px at 680 x 425; at 375 wide the form strip scrolls sideways with Play pinned.

## Voice
Bank voice: `lively-host` (Sadachbia), "Say with lively energy, like a friendly game show host", played here as the dance company's upbeat rehearsal captain. The bank lists it for showdowns and races; every 9-12 voice is taken by a neighbor (see Open questions). `Curio.voice`, scope = mode.
- welcomeFile (how; Fact file entered; stale once a folder tab is tapped): "Open a folder and read the five cards. Then tap Choose this issue."
- motifHow (how; Motifs entered with no motifs; stale once Make a motif is tapped): "Tap a card, then tap Make a motif. Watch, then stand up and try it."
- tuneHow (how; the first motif made; stale once any motif control changes): "Change the poses, the dynamic or the pathway until it feels like the card."
- structHow (how; Structure entered with no filled slots; stale once a slot is filled): "Pick a form, then drag a motif onto each slot."
- playHow (how; 3 slots filled for the first time; stale once Play dance is tapped; needs `#playDance`): "Tap Play dance to watch the whole piece."
- viewersHow (how; Viewers entered; stale once Show viewers is tapped): "Tap Show viewers. Four people will watch and say what they think it's about."
- allNamed (event; each viewer run where all four name the issue; stale when the mode changes): "All four viewers named your issue."
- noneNamed (event; each viewer run where none do; stale when the mode changes): "Nobody named the issue yet. Try putting a picture motif where they'll see it most."

## Family engine
Uses the dance engine (`kit/dance.js`, docs/families/dance.md), though the catalog lists no family for this tool: `Dance.floor(svg, 'stage', {backdrop})`, three `Dance.dancer` figures with badges, `Dance.poses` and `Dance.level`, `Dance.dyn`, `Dance.phrase` (two keyframe poses per motif, 8 counts, level from start to end, travel along a `Dance.paths` line, curve, circle or zigzag), `Dance.clock(100)`, `Dance.play` with `offsets` for canon, `Dance.music` (the Lantern track), `Dance.counts` in dots mode. Devices map to the phrase: bigger (pose scaled toward the extreme with `Dance.mix` at 1.25 and travel x1.5), smaller (0.6), slower (counts doubled within the same 8, half the poses), reversed (keyframes and path reversed). The tool brings its own cards, rules, viewer model and form strip.

## Done when

2. Each of the 15 cards makes its stated motif, and it plays on the engine's dancers with Motion on and off.
3. The two versions in Sources give 0 of 4 and 4 of 4; the teacher's and stranger's notes fire exactly on their rules.
4. While the dance plays, Stop and a slot tap act in the same frame; no two clocks or music tracks ever run at once.
5. Theme and variations flags two equal devices and Fix resolves them.
6. Versions survive a reload with their results and real viewer cards.
7. How lines once each over five motifs in a row; allNamed and noneNamed every time.
