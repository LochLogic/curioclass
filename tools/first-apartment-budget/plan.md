arc: explore-first
minutes: 50
focus: NSPFE.Spending
goal: I can build a monthly budget from a real paycheck, weigh needs and wants, and use savings and credit wisely when surprises hit.
materials: The tool on student devices and the projector. A class code written on the board (any word, like PERIOD3).
grouping: Individuals on devices, then pairs to compare.
---
## Before class

- Pick a class code so everyone gets the same surprises. Same code and same plan means the same events.
- Decide on a challenge for everyone, or let students choose. **Paycheck to paycheck** and **Big city, small budget** make strong first runs.
- This is a simulation for learning, not financial advice. Rents come from HUD, pay from BLS, and taxes are simplified.

## Hook (5 min)

1. "Your first job pays $18 an hour. How much rent can you afford?" Take guesses.
2. "Most people guess wrong, because the paycheck is smaller than the pay. Let's find out by living a year."

## Explore (20 min)

1. Students open **Plan**, pick a challenge, then a city, a job, a home, furniture, a way to get to work and benefits. The paycheck updates with every choice.
2. Students read the gauges: housing against the 30% guideline, and needs, wants and saving against the 50/30/20 rule. They adjust until the verdict box says the plan holds together.
3. Students type the class code and press **Move in**.
4. In **Live the year**, students press **Live July** and decide how to pay for each surprise: checking, savings or the credit card. They can change groceries, eating out and auto-save between months. **Fast** speeds up the days.

## Make sense (10 min)

1. Pause the class around month six. "What share of your gross pay went to housing? Who added a roommate? What changed?"
2. "Which surprise hurt most? Would an emergency fund or insurance have changed it?"
3. Look at the paycheck as a class: gross pay, taxes, Social Security and Medicare, 401(k), health premium, take-home.

## Apply (10 min)

1. Students finish the year and open **Report**. It grades the year out of 100.
2. Pairs with the same challenge compare reports. "Which single decision made the biggest difference?"
3. Discuss: "A frugal year with no fun scores poorly on Life. A fun year with a maxed card scores poorly on Debt. What does balance look like?"

## Exit check (5 min)

On a card: "Your take-home pay is $2,400 a month. Using 50/30/20, how much goes to needs, wants and saving? Name one surprise from your year and how you'd plan for it next time."

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** start with **Free play** and a short commute, and focus on the 30% housing gauge.
- **Support:** pause after each month and talk through one decision with a partner.
- **Stretch:** try **The car question** and compare a bus commute, an old car and a financed car.
- **Stretch:** "Is a big tax refund good news?" Find the tax-time card's answer and explain it.

## Short on time

- Build a plan in **Plan** to a passing verdict (12 min).
- Live three months in **Live the year** (10 min), then the exit card (5 min).
