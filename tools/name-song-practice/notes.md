## What it teaches
Students make a four-bar song about a name and a favorite thing, choose one improvement, and compare two sung tries. They then perform the song with an expressive feeling for a toy audience.

## Key skills
- Clap the parts of a name into a rhythm.
- Follow high sol and lower mi on a two-line staff.
- Listen to a first try and apply one useful practice fix.
- Compare two tries without needing to choose a winner.
- Change how a song feels through expression and accompaniment.

## Support at home
- Clap the parts of family names together. Try one name with high notes, then with high and low notes.
- Sing a short hello song about something your child likes. Choose one small change and sing it again.
- Perform a song for a favorite toy, first happily and then sleepily. Ask what changed.

## A 5-minute start
1. Open Make. Say a child's name aloud. Tap Clap once for each syllable, up to four.
2. Choose a name shape and a favorite picture. Tap Hear it, then sing along.
3. Open Practice. Tap Sing take 1 and join the four-beat count-in.
4. Listen or use the optional thumbs counts. Tap Pick a fix, choose one fix, and sing take 2.
5. Compare while thinking about the chosen fix. The Take 2 card shows that cue. Choose Take 1, Take 2 or Both good, and go to Perform. Pick a feeling and sing for the toys.

## Modes
**Make:** The song has four bars of four beats. The name and favorite each fill two beats. Dots under multiple name notes stand for the spoken name parts; no child's name is entered. Note spacing gives each word room; the sound and ball carry the exact rhythm. Hovering a note shows its duration.

**Practice:** The steps are Take 1, Listen, Pick a fix, Take 2 and Compare. The microphone starts off. Without a microphone, students hear the guide, sing aloud, and reflect with thumbs. Thumbs counts are optional observations, not scores. With a microphone, Play take 1 and Play take 2 replay the class recordings.

**Perform:** Sing live with Happy, Sleepy, Proud or Silly accompaniment. A feeling change takes effect at the next beat, including the remaining time of a held syllable. The chosen take's practice cue carries forward; the tool does not play the class recording as the performance. Both good carries take 2's cue. The animals' comments are scripted encouragement, not an assessment of pitch, clarity or beat accuracy.

## Talk together
- Which one change would help our second try?
- What did we hear differently in take 2?
- How could we make the same song sound sleepy or proud?

## Good to know
- K-2, teacher led. A child can also sing along alone. No reading or typing of names is needed.
- The only melody notes are G4 (sol, 392.00 Hz) and E4 (mi, 329.63 Hz). Higher sol asks singers to aim for the high note; it does not transpose the guide.
- The bar 4 phrase has two quick notes on beat 1, one note on beat 2, then two beats of rest.
- Make and Practice run at 96 beats per minute. Happy is 108, Sleepy 76, Proud 96 and Silly 120.
- Microphone access is requested only when the teacher taps Microphone. Denied or unavailable access leaves the complete thumbs-based activity available.
- Recordings stay in this page's memory, never leave the device, and are erased on Start over, a confirmed song edit or leaving/reloading the page. There are at most two takes, capped at 15 seconds each. No song or recording is saved between visits. Leaving Practice turns the microphone off. The kit may remember only the shared Motion setting.
- The loudness picture uses a short RMS sample and an illustrative classroom scale. It is not a sound-pressure meter or a judgment of singing. Microphone distance and room noise affect it.
- Sound off silences guide, accompaniment and recording playback. Read still speaks. Read stops a playing song first.
- Motion off removes bounces while preserving the music's timing. Keyboard: C claps in Make; Space plays/stops; 1 to 5 choose a fix in Practice. All controls support Tab and Enter.
- Fixed narration uses the browser voice fallback. The big-sister (Leda) recording manifest is supplied, but no narration clips are recorded.

## Standards and sources
MU:Cr3 is practiced by making and refining the song. MU:Pr5 is practiced through two takes and one fix. MU:Pr6 is practiced through a live expressive performance. MU:Cn10 is practiced through the child's name rhythm and favorite thing.
