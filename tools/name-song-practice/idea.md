# Name Song Practice (idea card)

- Module: arts-k-2-making-up-songs, grade k-2. Codes: MU:Cr3, MU:Pr5, MU:Pr6, MU:Cn10
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Clap your name, pick a favorite thing, and the tool sets them into a two-line sol-mi song; sing it, hear yourself back, pick one fix, sing again, and see the two takes side by side before you perform it with a chosen feeling.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a stage made from a cardboard box, an audience of stuffed animals, and "Clap your name" (no typing needed: the child claps or taps the syllables; a grown-up may type the name, and it stays on the device).
  - Core loop: tap a favorite-thing picture (a dog, rain, tacos, the moon). The song is built on sol, mi and la with one note per syllable, and a bouncing ball shows the tune. Record a take with the microphone; play it back with a simple pitch line under the target line. Pick one fix card (sing the end higher, slow down, clap on your name, sing louder) and record again.
  - The aha or the end state: take 1 and take 2 play side by side, and the child says which is better and why. Then pick a performing feeling (happy, sleepy, proud) and sing it for the stuffed-animal audience, who react to loud, soft and expression.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a friend chooses the fix card instead; perform for the class.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MU:Cr3: choosing a fix from feedback, revising the song, then presenting the final version.
  - MU:Pr5: hearing take 1 against the target and refining in take 2.
  - MU:Pr6: performing with a chosen expression for an audience.
  - MU:Cn10: the song is built from the child's own name and favorite thing.
- Not like: melody-maker (grade 3-5 composing on a scale grid); character-voice-booth (acting voices, not singing); beat-buddies (steady beat).
- Art: people (illustrated) for the box stage and the stuffed-animal audience.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Clap your name and pick a favorite thing to make a short sol-mi song, sing it, hear yourself back, pick one fix and sing it again, then perform the better take with feeling for a stuffed-animal audience.
- Flags: records audio (on-screen line that recordings never leave the device; pitch line computed on the device; no browser speech recognition)
