# Name Song Practice

## One idea
Practice is a loop you can see: the class claps a name into a short sol-mi song, sings take 1, picks one fix (louder, steadier, a higher sol, clearer words), sings take 2 with that fix glowing on the song, hears both takes side by side, and then performs the better one with a feeling for a row of stuffed animals who react.

## Not like
- sound-elevator (k-2, sandbox): plays high and low notes freely. Here there are only two notes, sol and mi, and they make a song the class sings and improves.
- beat-buddies (k-2, showdown): moves to a steady beat. Here the beat carries words: a name clapped into rhythm.
- parade-and-bedtime-music (k-2, sort, this batch): listens and sorts tunes. Here the class makes and sings its own.
- melody-maker (3-5, studio): composes on a scale grid. Here the song is a fixed four-bar frame; the choices are the name's rhythm and shape, the favorite thing, one fix and one feeling.
- act-it-out (k-2, showdown): performs a story with bodies and voices. Here the performance is a song, and the point is the second try being better than the first.

## Who and how
- Grade K-2, music, module "Making Up Songs".
- Driver: teacher on a projector or smartboard. One child's name at a time, the whole class sings it; repeat for other children. 10 to 20 minutes.
- Standards: NCAS MU:Cr3 (refine and present a made-up song), MU:Pr5 (rehearse, apply feedback to improve), MU:Pr6 (perform with expression for an audience), MU:Cn10 (a song about yourself and what you like).
- No name is typed or stored: the class claps the name and the teacher taps the claps.

## Screens and modes
Header: title, `Read`, `Motion`, `Sound` (kit). Mode buttons (`.seg`, icon plus word): `Make` (pencil), `Practice` (loop arrows), `Perform` (star). Every control is 60 px or more with an icon and a word.

**Make.** Focus: the song strip, four big bars across the stage on a two-line staff (top line sol, bottom line mi), each note a rounded block on its line with its word under it. The name bar and the thing bar start as dashed empty slots.
- `Clap` (big hands button): each tap adds one name syllable (1 to 4) and plays a soft clap; the name slot fills with that many blocks. `Clear claps` empties it.
- Name shape buttons (each with a three-dot contour icon): `High high` (all sol), `High low` (sol, mi, sol, mi), `Low high` (mi, sol, mi, sol).
- Favorite thing: a row of 8 painted picture cards; tap one and its syllables fill the thing bar.
- `Hear it` (play icon) plays the whole song once with a bouncing ball over the blocks.

**Practice.** Focus: the same song strip, larger, with a take panel to the right. Steps along the top, each a pill that lights in turn: `Take 1`, `Listen`, `Pick a fix`, `Take 2`, `Compare`.
- `Sing take 1` (gold): a 4-beat count-in (clicks, big numbers 1 to 4), then the song plays its guide melody softly while the ball bounces and the class sings. If the microphone is on (below), the take is recorded in memory and a live loudness bar beside the strip shows how loud the class is singing.
- `Listen`: `Play take 1` plays the recording (mic on). Mic off: the class shows thumbs up, sideways or down, and the teacher taps `Thumbs` counts (optional).
- `Pick a fix`: five big chips: `Louder` (big speaker), `Softer` (small speaker), `Steady beat` (metronome), `Higher sol` (up arrow), `Clear words` (mouth). One at a time.
- `Sing take 2`: the same as take 1, with the fix shown on the strip (below) and a badge of the fix's icon over the strip.
- `Compare`: `Play take 1`, `Play take 2` (mic on), then three chips, `Take 1`, `Take 2`, `Both good`, for which was better.
- Panel: `Microphone` toggle (off at first) with a line under it: "Recordings stay in this page and are erased when you leave." `Start over` clears both takes.

**Perform.** Focus: the stuffed-animal audience on a cozy toy stage (painted), four plush toys on cushions facing the class, with the song strip small along the bottom. Feeling buttons: `Happy` (sun), `Sleepy` (moon), `Proud` (star), `Silly` (curly line). `Perform` (gold): count-in, then the song with the feeling's accompaniment; the class sings. The audience listens (listen poses) and at the end cheers (cheer poses), and one animal's speech bubble says what it noticed (Content).

## Content
**Song frame** (4/4, sol = G4 392.00 Hz, mi = E4 329.63 Hz; ta = 1 beat, ti-ti = two half beats):
- Bar 1, "Hel-lo, hel-lo": sol mi sol mi, ta ta ta ta.
- Bar 2, name plus "that's me": name syllables in beats 1 to 2, then sol (beat 3, "that's"), mi (beat 4, "me").
- Bar 3, "I like" plus the thing: sol sol (beats 1 to 2), thing syllables in beats 3 to 4.
- Bar 4, "yes I do!": sol sol (ti-ti, beat 1), mi (ta, beat 2), rest (beats 3 to 4).
- Syllables fill two beats: 1 syllable = one long note (2 beats); 2 = ta ta; 3 = ti-ti ta; 4 = ti-ti ti-ti.
- Name shape: High high = all sol; High low = sol, mi, sol, mi; Low high = mi, sol, mi, sol (cut to the syllable count). The thing's notes are always sol, mi, sol, mi (cut to its count).

**Favorite things** (painted card; word; syllables): pizza, piz-za, 2; puppies, pup-pies, 2; rainbows, rain-bows, 2; trains, trains, 1; dinosaurs, di-no-saurs, 3; strawberries, straw-ber-ries, 3; soccer, soc-cer, 2; butterflies, but-ter-flies, 3.

**Tempo:** Make and Practice at 96 bpm (song 10.0 s, with count-in 12.5 s).

**Fixes on take 2:**
- Louder: the loudness bar shows a "loud" line at 70% and turns to a check when the class passes it; the badge is a big speaker.
- Softer: the bar shows a "soft" line at 30% and a check while the class stays under it.
- Steady beat: the beat clicks play louder and a beat dot pulses on every count.
- Higher sol: the sol blocks rise a little and glow; the guide plays sol slightly louder.
- Clear words: the words under the blocks grow 1.5 times and each lights as it is sung.
(Mic off: the loudness bar is hidden; Louder and Softer show only the badge.)

**Feelings** (accompaniment, all made in code):
- Happy: 108 bpm, bright pluck chords (C and G), claps on 2 and 4.
- Sleepy: 76 bpm, soft pad, no drum, a gentle music-box sol and mi.
- Proud: 96 bpm, brass chords (sawtooth through a low-pass), kick on 1 and 3, snare on 4.
- Silly: 120 bpm, bouncy bass, a slide whistle "boing" on beat 4 of each bar.
**Audience notes** (one shown after each performance, cycling): octopus "I heard your name loud and clear!"; dinosaur "Your voice sounded <feeling>!"; lamb "I liked the high sol!"; whale "You kept the beat. Sing it again!"

## Interactions in detail
- Make: `Hear it` and `Sing` stay disabled until the name has 1 or more claps and a thing is chosen; the hint and the line say which is missing. A fifth `Clap` does nothing but a soft "full" tick and the line "Four claps is the most."
- Changing the name, shape or thing during Practice clears both takes (with `Yes, change it` and `Keep my song` buttons first).
- Playback: one sound at a time. A tap on any control while the song plays acts at once: `Stop` (the gold button turns into Stop while playing) stops it, a new step starts, a mode switch stops all sound. The ball and blocks follow the audio clock, not a timer.
- Microphone: asked for only when the toggle is first turned on. Denied or missing: the toggle turns off, the line says "No microphone. You can still practice: use thumbs." The tool works fully without it. Recordings are kept as in-memory audio (at most two takes, each up to 15 s) and erased on `Start over`, a song change, or leaving the page. Nothing is saved or sent.
- Loudness bar: the mic level (RMS over 50 ms) scaled so 30% is quiet speech and 70% is strong singing in a classroom; it is a picture of "more" and "less", not a measurement, and has no number.
- Perform: the feeling can change while the song plays; the accompaniment changes from the next beat (within one beat at 76 bpm, 0.8 s) and the feeling button lights at once. The audience cheers at the end every time (event).
- `Start over` (Practice) returns to Take 1. Nothing is stored between visits.
- Motion off: the ball jumps from block to block on each note, the audience swaps poses without a bounce, the count-in numbers appear without a pop.
- Keyboard: C claps, Space plays or stops, 1 to 5 pick a fix in Practice.

## Voice
Bank voice `big-sister` (Leda), style "Say in a friendly, youthful way, like a helpful older student". A singing buddy one step older than the class. Not used by any K-2 arts neighbor (line-safari bright-teacher, art-passport knowing-guide, beat-buddies lively-host, orchestra-zoo playful-friend, sound-elevator hero-coach, act-it-out gentle-storyteller, levels-and-pathways warm-teacher) or by another K-2 spec in this batch. `Curio.voice` with `scope: () => state.mode`; no line plays during the song or count-in (`valid: () => !state.playing`).

- how, welcome (Make; stale after the first clap): "Let's make a name song! Clap the name with me, and tap Clap for every clap."
- how, after the first clap (stale when a thing is chosen): "Now tap a favorite thing."
- event, thing chosen: "I like <thing>!" (8 lines; stale on the next tap)
- how, song ready the first time (stale when Hear it is tapped): "Tap Hear it to hear your song."
- event, Hear it ends: "That's your song! Tap Practice when you are ready to sing."
- how, welcome (Practice; stale when Sing take 1 is tapped): "First we sing it once. Tap Sing take 1. Watch the ball and sing."
- event, take 1 ends: "Nice singing! Now let's listen." (mic on) / "Nice singing! Show me with your thumb: how did it go?" (mic off)
- how, first Listen done (stale when a fix is tapped): "Pick one thing to make it even better. Tap a fix."
- event, fix chosen: "Louder! Big voices." / "Softer. Gentle voices." / "Steady beat. Listen for the clicks." / "Higher sol. Reach up for the high note." / "Clear words. Make every word sharp."
- how, after a fix (stale when Sing take 2 is tapped): "Tap Sing take 2, and try your fix."
- event, take 2 ends: "Let's hear both takes. Tap Play take 1, then Play take 2." (mic on) / "Was take 2 better? Tap Take 1, Take 2 or Both good." (mic off)
- event, compare chip: "Take 2! Practice made it better." / "Take 1! Sometimes the first try is great." / "Both good! You're ready to perform."
- how, welcome (Perform; stale when a feeling is tapped): "Our audience is waiting! Tap a feeling, then tap Perform."
- event, the end of every performance: the audience note in Content.
- event: "No microphone. You can still practice: use thumbs." / "Four claps is the most."
- Read speaks the hint, which always names the next button.

## Sources
- National Core Arts Standards, Music, grades K to 2 (MU:Cr3, MU:Pr5, MU:Pr6, MU:Cn10): https://www.nationalartsstandards.org
- Sol-mi (the falling minor third) as the first pattern in Kodaly practice, and ta and ti-ti rhythm names: Organization of American Kodaly Educators, "The Kodaly Concept" (https://www.oake.org/about-us/the-kodaly-concept/); Choksy, L. (1999), The Kodaly Method I, Prentice Hall.
- G4 and E4 are inside young children's comfortable singing range (about D4 to A4): Rutkowski, J. (1990), "The measurement and evaluation of children's singing voice development", The Quarterly 1(1 to 2).
- Pitch math: equal temperament, A4 = 440 Hz. The song frame and all accompaniments are original.
- Checks run (Python, 2026-10-29): G4 = 392.00 Hz, E4 = 329.63 Hz. Every syllable rhythm fills exactly 2 beats (1: 2; 2: 1 + 1; 3: 0.5 + 0.5 + 1; 4: four 0.5s), so every bar sums to 4 beats for every name and thing. Song length 16 beats: 12.6 s at 76, 10.0 s at 96, 8.9 s at 108, 8.0 s at 120; with a 4-beat count-in, 15.8 s at the slowest, inside the 15 s recording cap only for the Practice tempo (96 bpm: 12.5 s), and Perform is not recorded. Name shapes cut correctly for 1 to 4 syllables (High low with 3: sol, mi, sol; Low high with 4: mi, sol, mi, sol). Contrast ratios as listed.

## Done when

2. Every combination of 1 to 4 claps, three shapes and eight things plays four full bars of 4 beats, with the ball on the right block.
3. Practice walks Take 1, Listen, Pick a fix, Take 2, Compare in order, with and without the microphone (denied, missing and allowed all tested).
4. Each fix changes take 2 the way Content says; Louder and Softer lines show only with the mic on.
5. Recordings are erased on Start over, a song change and reload; nothing is in localStorage and no request leaves the page.
6. Tap while it plays: Stop, a mode switch or a feeling change during a song acts at once (the feeling button lights at once, the accompaniment within one beat).
7. The audience cheers and shows a note after every performance (event every time).
8. How-to lines are spoken once in a run of five actions; no line plays during a song or count-in.
9. Every control is 60 px or more with an icon and a word; Clap, Sing and Perform are visible without scrolling at every size in STANDARDS.md.
10. Motion off: the ball jumps note to note, no bounces; timing is unchanged.
