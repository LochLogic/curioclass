# Movement Strategy Room (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-11-civil-rights-movement, grade 11. Codes: D2.His.3.9-12, D2.Civ.14.9-12, D2.Civ.5.9-12
- Format / family: story / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Sit in the planning room of three real campaigns (the Montgomery bus boycott, the Delano grape strike and boycott, the 1977 Section 504 sit-in for disability rights), make the organizers' choices before you see theirs, and judge what worked and why.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a church basement in Montgomery, December 1955, a map of bus routes and a question from an illustrated organizer: "Rosa Parks was arrested today. What do we do tomorrow?"
  - Core loop: at each branch, choose a tactic (boycott, carpool, lawsuit, march, sit-in, press, negotiation) with real constraints (money, jobs at risk, violence, the law); then a "what they did" card shows the organizers' real choice with their words, and the student's choice plays forward plausibly but is labelled as a what-if.
  - The three campaigns are told in sequence so tactics carry across groups (the 504 activists learned from earlier sit-ins).
  - The aha or the end state: an effectiveness board where the student rates each campaign's results (laws, court rulings, public opinion, costs to people) and a "then and now" note on how the significance of figures such as Jo Ann Robinson, Dolores Huerta and Judy Heumann has grown as histories were rewritten.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): small groups plan rival strategies and compare.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.3.9-12: the then-and-now note asks how each organizer's significance changed over time and context.
  - D2.Civ.14.9-12: the tactic choices compare means of changing society and protecting rights.
  - D2.Civ.5.9-12: the effectiveness board evaluates how well citizens and institutions (courts, Congress, agencies) addressed each problem.
- Not like: voices-of-freedom (grade 8 primary sources); who-decides (present-day local action); independence-timeline (anticolonial paths).
- Art: people (illustrated) organizers and participants, plus public domain photographs where checked, because real faces belong to real campaigns.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Plan three real campaigns (the Montgomery bus boycott, the Delano grape boycott, the 504 disability sit-in): choose tactics before seeing the organizers' choices, then judge what worked and whose roles history now recognizes.
- Flags: sensitive (racist violence and segregation; fresh review agent per rule 5)
