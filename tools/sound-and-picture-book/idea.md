# Sound and Picture Book (idea card)

- Module: arts-k-2-picture-and-sound-stories, grade k-2. Codes: MA:Cr1, MA:Cr2, MA:Cr3, MA:Pr4
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Plan a three-page story with picture cards, make a picture and a sound for each page, put them in order and play the book; then the Swap button trades two sounds or two pages and the child hears the story turn into a different one.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: three empty book pages labeled beginning, middle and end, and a basket of idea cards (a lost mitten, a storm, a new puppy, a surprise).
  - Core loop: pick idea cards and lay them on the pages (the plan). For each page, draw a picture or snap one with the camera, then record a sound or choose one from a sound shelf (rain, footsteps, a door, a dog). Play the book and fix what is not right (move a page, record again).
  - The aha or the end state: Swap mode. The child trades the happy bark on page 3 for the thunder from page 2 and hears the ending change. The child picks the version they meant and saves it as their book.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): books play for the class on the projector; a print view of the three pages.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MA:Cr1: choosing and sharing ideas with the idea cards.
  - MA:Cr2: making a plan on the three pages before making anything.
  - MA:Cr3: playing back, testing with Swap, and changing a page or sound to fix the story.
  - MA:Pr4: combining pictures and sounds into one media story.
- Not like: weekend-drawing-share (one drawing about a real event); cartoon-soundtrack-mixer (grade 3-5 scoring a silent cartoon); field-trip-slideshow (crew jobs and presenting).
- Art: people (illustrated) for the idea cards and book frame; the child's own drawings and photos fill the pages; sound shelf recorded for Curio Class.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Plan a three-page story with picture cards, draw or snap a picture and record a sound for each page, play your book, then swap two sounds or pages and hear the story change.
- Flags: records audio and camera (on-screen line that pictures and sounds never leave the device; no browser speech recognition)
