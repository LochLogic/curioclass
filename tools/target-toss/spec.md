# Target Toss

## One idea
The class runs a real throwing experiment: throw soft balls at a paper target on the wall beside the projected one, one way, then another way (step with the opposite foot, or keep feet still), the board stacks every hit in ten-frames, and the class sees for itself which way hits more.

## Not like
- animal-moves (K-2 PE, showdown): the class copies a moving animal to a beat; nothing is thrown or counted. Target Toss is a lab with a question, two rounds, a count and a conclusion.
- habit-hero (K-2 health, creature): choices move a hero's meters. Target Toss has no character to care for; the class's own throws are the data.
- lunch-tray-builder (K-2 health, builder): builds a plate on screen. Target Toss is played with the body, and the screen only records and coaches.
- class-poll (grade 1 math, data): a vote becomes a picture graph. Target Toss also ends in a picture graph, but its numbers come from an experiment with a fair test (same throwers, same line, one change).

## Who and how
- Grade K-2, Physical Education, module Throwing and Catching (manipulative skill basics). This tool teaches throwing only; catching belongs to a later K-2 tool (on the board).
- Driver: teacher, on a projector. The class throws at a paper target with the same three rings, taped on the wall two big steps from the screen (never at the screen); the teacher (or a helper student) taps the screen target where each ball hit.
- Minutes: 15-30 (one gym or classroom block). Soft yarn balls or beanbags, a tape line on the floor.
- Standards: SHAPE.S1 (develops a variety of motor skills: underhand and overhand throwing).
- Topic line: "underhand and overhand throwing".
- Modes (tool.json): ["Form", "Test it", "Free throw"].

## Screens and modes
Header: title "Target Toss" with small line "Underhand and overhand throwing", three mode buttons with icons **🙆 Form**, **🧪 Test it**, **🎯 Free throw** (keys 1 to 3), then **Motion**, **Sound**, **Read**. Stage on the left, panel on the right (below the stage under 760 px wide). Every control is 60 px tall or more at every size (K-2 targets, preview sizes included) and has an icon. The panel always ends with **📋 How to set up** and the safety tip (see Content).

Two settings sit at the top of the panel in every mode, as two-button toggles:
- **Throw:** **🫳 Underhand** / **💪 Overhand** (default Underhand).
- **Hand:** **✋ Right hand** / **🤚 Left hand** (default Right). This only mirrors Bo and swaps which foot steps. A tap says both tags ("Throw with your left hand. Step with your right foot.").

### Form (opens first)
- Stage: Bo, a bear cub drawn in code, stands on the left facing a target on the right. Under Bo, a strip of five cue cards (icon plus one or two words). The current cue card is raised and outlined; Bo holds that pose. The throwing arm and the stepping foot get a gold glow and a text tag ("Step: LEFT foot" with a footprint icon).
- Panel: **▶ Show me** (Bo does all five cues in a row, 1.6 s each, the narrator says each cue, then Bo throws a ball that flies into the bullseye), **⏭ Next cue** (one cue at a time; after the fifth it says "Now you try! Press Throw with Bo to throw together."), **🙋 Throw with Bo** (no ball: the class shadows Bo. "Ready, aim..." starts at once, then the five cues on a slow beat, at least 1 s after each cue line ends and never under 2.2 s a cue, so the class has time to do each move; it ends with "Great throwing!", then the safety line on a first run).
- Cue 3's spoken line names the foot from the Hand setting ("Step with your left foot, the opposite foot." for a right-handed thrower), matching the tag.
- Tap a cue card: Bo jumps to that pose and the cue is spoken.

### Test it (the experiment)
Steps run in order; a step strip at the top of the stage shows **1 Ask, 2 Guess, 3 Way A, 4 Way B, 5 Look** with the current step outlined.
1. **Ask:** four big question cards on the stage (Content). Tap one.
2. **Guess:** the card shows Way A and Way B side by side with their icons. The class votes with fingers (one finger for Way A, two for Way B), so the sign never hints at an answer. Buttons: **☝️ Way A**, **✌️ Way B**, **🤷 Skip the guess**, and a quiet **🃏 Other question** (back to Ask); Skip the guess and Other question share a row so both stay in view in short boxes. The coached way is Way A on Step or stay? and Close or far?, Way B on Where do you look? and Big or small?, so position never gives the answer away.
3. **Way A:** the class picks its throwers; each throws once, in order. The Way name is drawn in the stage's step strip area, under the ball dots, so every miss above the target shows. Panel: a counter "Throw 3 of 10" with a ten-frame (one ball per throw: filled for a hit, an empty circle with a small x for a miss), **⬇ Too short** (the ball did not reach the wall), **↶ Undo**, then apart from them a quiet **■ Stop test**. After the last throw: "Way A is done!" and **▶ Start Way B** takes Too short's place; Undo stays where it was and still takes back Way A's last throw.
4. **Way B:** the same throwers in the same order, with Way B's banner; the start line says what stays the same and the one thing that changes. For the "Small target" way, the outer two rings fade to grey with the words "Only the gold counts" and a tap on a grey ring counts as a miss.
5. **Look:** a picture graph: two columns of ten-frames (hits first, then misses), Way A on the left and Way B on the right, each with its icon, its hit count as a big numeral and a bar. In the stacked layout the stage grows on Look so the ten-frames read at 375 wide; the winner box hugs its column. The narrator asks "Which way got more hits?" Panel: **↶ Undo last throw** (back to Way B), **👀 Show the answer** (the column with more hits gets a gold outline and a crown of stars; a tie outlines both), then **🔁 Test again** (same question, fresh rounds), **🃏 New question**.
- Throws per round: **5**, **10** (default) or **20** (two ten-frames), chosen on the Guess step.

### Free throw
- Stage: the target on a wall; nothing else. Each tap leaves a ball dot where it was tapped (on the rings for a hit, on the wall around them for a miss). A small tally in the corner: hits and throws.
- The class throws at the paper target and the teacher taps the screen where each ball hit.
- Panel: **⬇ Too short**, **↶ Undo**, a "Coach says" card that updates after every fifth throw (Content; before then it says "Throw five balls. Then look here."), and apart from them a quiet **🧽 Clear the wall**.

### How to set up (a card over the page, every mode)
A picture (screen, "2 big steps", the paper target with three rings, the tape line) and six steps: tape a paper target on the wall two big steps from the screen; draw the screen's three rings on it; put a tape line on the floor; pick throwers who throw soft balls at the paper target one at a time; tap the screen where each ball hit; pick up the balls only when the teacher says, after everyone has thrown. The page behind is inert while it is open (Show me cannot run under it); the hint and Read follow the card. All six steps stay in view at every size.

## Content

### The target
Three rings drawn in code, centered on the stage, diameter about 70 percent of the stage height: outer ring blue, middle ring red, center gold (the bullseye) with a star. Each ring also has a different edge pattern (blue dashed edge, red solid edge, gold double edge) so the rings never depend on color alone. A hit anywhere inside the outer ring is a hit; a gold hit adds a small star to the ball dot. The wall around the target is a soft plain color with faint block lines.

### Form cues (underhand), five cards
| # | Icon | Card | Spoken | Bo's pose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 👀 | Face it | Face the target. | stands square to the target, ball in throwing paw at waist |
| 2 | 🔙 | Arm back | Swing your arm back. | throwing arm straight back, like a pendulum |
| 3 | 👣 | Step | Step with the opposite foot. | foot opposite the throwing arm steps forward |
| 4 | ⬇ | Let go low | Let go between your knee and your waist. | arm swings forward, ball leaves the paw at knee to waist height |
| 5 | 👉 | Follow through | Follow through to the target. | arm finishes pointing at the target |

### Form cues (overhand), five cards
| # | Icon | Card | Spoken | Bo's pose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ↔ | Side to it | Turn your side to the target. | side faces the target, other hand points at it |
| 2 | 🔙 | Arm way back | Bring your arm way back. | throwing paw back and up, elbow at shoulder height |
| 3 | 👣 | Step | Step with the opposite foot. | opposite foot steps toward the target |
| 4 | 🔄 | Turn | Turn your hips and shoulders. | body rotates to face the target as the arm comes through |
| 5 | ↘ | Follow through | Follow through across your body. | throwing paw ends near the opposite hip |

### Question cards (Test it)
| Card | Icon | Way A | Way B | Coached way | After "Show the answer" (tip card, voiced) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Step or stay? | 👣 | Step with the opposite foot | Keep your feet still | A | Coaches teach the step because it helps your whole body push the ball and aim it. |
| Where do you look? | 👀 | Eyes on your feet | Eyes on the target | B | Good throwers look at the target the whole time. |
| Close or far? | 📏 | Throw from the close line | Throw from the far line | A | Closer is easier to hit. Step back to the far line when you are ready for a challenge. |
| Big or small? | 🎯 | Small target: only the gold counts | Big target: all rings count | B | Bigger targets are easier to hit. Practice small targets to get even better. |
The "Close or far?" setup line ("Make two tape lines: three giant steps from the wall and six giant steps from the wall.") shows and is spoken on its Guess step. The tip never says which way "should" win before the class has looked at its own count, and the result line is always the class's numbers. When the class's winner is not the coached way (and it is not a tie), "Our test came out different this time. Try more throws, then test again." comes before the tip.

### Coach says (Free throw), checked after every fifth throw, on the last five throws
"Above" means a miss tapped higher than the outer ring's top edge; "low" means a miss below its bottom edge or a Too short; "side" means a miss left or right of the ring (and not above or below). Rules in this order, first match wins:
1. 3 or more hits: "Great aiming! Take one giant step back for a new challenge."
2. 3 or more above: underhand "Too high. Let go lower, between your knee and your waist." overhand "Too high. Follow through down across your body."
3. 3 or more low: "Too short. Take a bigger step and swing all the way through."
4. 3 or more side: underhand "Face the target, and point your hand at it when you let go." overhand "Turn your side to the target, then point your hand at it after you throw."
5. Otherwise: "Keep going. Step, swing and follow through."

### Safety tip (panel, every mode)
"Soft balls or beanbags only. Throw at the paper target on the wall, never at the screen, a TV, a touch panel or the laptop. Wait behind the line for your turn."

## Interactions in detail
- Taps on the stage are hits or misses only in Test it rounds and Free throw; in Form the target is decoration.
- Hit sound: a soft thump and a two-note chime (a three-note chime for gold). Miss: a soft whoosh. Too short: a low bonk. No voice on single throws (too chatty for a class line).
- A tap lands where the finger or mouse is. On an interactive board a ball strike may register as a tap; the tool treats it the same, and the teacher can Undo a stray tap.
- Undo removes the last ball dot and count. Until Way B's first throw it takes back Way A's last throw (Way A reopens). Nothing rebuilds under the pointer: Start Way B takes Too short's place, never Undo's.
- Ten-frames fill left to right, top row first, 5 per row, in throw order during a round (hits first, then misses, on Look); a hit is a filled ball in the accent color with a white star for gold, a miss is an empty circle with a small x. Counts never depend on color.
- Look step: the bars grow from zero over 600 ms (instant with Motion off). Show the answer: "Way A got more hits!", "Way B got more hits!" or "It's a tie! Try more throws." If a guess was made, the next line says "That matches our guess!" or "That's different from our guess. That's why we test!"
- Stop test returns to the Ask step and keeps nothing. With throws to lose (any Test it step holding throws, Look included, before or after the answer), Stop test, Clear the wall, a mode button or keys 1 to 3 first ask Yes / No in a card built in the page ("Stop the test? All the throws will be gone. Press Yes or No."); focus starts on **No, keep them** and Escape means No; after Yes on a mode switch, focus goes to the new mode's first control. Changing Throw or Hand during a round is allowed and does not reset the round, and does not rewrite a Coach line already given.
- Mode switch: stops speech, keeps Free throw dots until Clear, resets Test it to Ask.
- Focus always moves to the next control (question card to Way A, Way A to the stage, end of Way A to Start Way B, Stop test and New question to the first question card, Test again to the Guess step's Way A).
- Form: Show me and Throw with Bo can be stopped by pressing the same button (it reads **■ Stop** while running). Motion off: Bo jumps between poses without the tween; timing and narration stay.
- Layout: draw the stage at real pixel size per box. In short landscape boxes (under about 640 px tall) keep stage and panel side by side and tighten text and gaps; controls stay 60 px. The target stays at least 300 px across at 1366x768.

## Done when
1. Form: Show me runs all five cues with Bo's pose, card highlight and spoken cue in step; switching Hand to Left mirrors Bo, the tag reads "Step: RIGHT foot" and cue 3 says "Step with your right foot".
2. Test it: a full run (question, guess, 10 throws A, 10 throws B, Look, Show the answer) works with held clicks, and the counts on the picture graph equal the taps (verify agent recomputes).
3. Small target card: in Way B a tap on the red or blue ring counts as a miss; a gold tap counts as a hit.
4. Undo after Way A's last throw takes it back; Undo in an empty Way B reopens Way A; Look's Undo returns to Way B; Too short adds a miss.
5. Free throw: five scripted misses above the target produce the "Too high" line for the current Throw setting; three hits in five produce "Great aiming!".
6. No throw tap speaks; round ends, results and coach lines speak every time; each how-to line is spoken once per load (count in a run of five actions).
7. Every control is 60 px or more with an icon at every size (680x425 to 1920x1080, 760x900, 375 in the host); stage and panel stay side by side at 1000x625; nothing scrolls the main controls off screen at 375 wide in the iframe host.
8. Rings differ by edge pattern as well as color; contrast values in Look hold.
9. Motion off: no tweens, same timing and narration.

## Open questions
- Throwing at the screen: the tool assumes a projector on a wall or a pull-down screen and says never to throw at a TV or touch panel. Is that the right line for classrooms with interactive flat panels, or should the setup card suggest a hoop taped to the wall beside the screen instead?
