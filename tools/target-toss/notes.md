## A 5-minute start

1. Tape a paper target on the wall two big steps **away from** the screen, never on it, and draw the screen's three rings on it (gold in the middle, then red, then blue; a poster-size sheet works well). Put a tape line on the floor and hand out soft yarn balls or beanbags. Balls are picked up only when you say, after everyone has thrown. **📋 How to set up** shows all of this with a picture.
2. Open **Form** and tap **▶ Show me**. Bo the bear does the five cues of an underhand throw, and the narrator says each one. Then tap **🙋 Throw with Bo**: the class stands up and throws an air ball along with Bo.
3. Switch to **Test it** and tap **Step or stay?**. The class votes with fingers (one for Way A, two for Way B). Pick your throwers: each throws once, in order, at the paper target. Tap the screen target where each ball hit (or **⬇ Too short**). Way B uses the same throwers in the same order.
4. After Way A and Way B, the board shows both counts side by side. Ask "Which way got more hits?", then tap **👀 Show the answer**.

## The three modes

- **Form:** Bo shows the five cues for **Underhand** or **Overhand**, with the throwing arm and the stepping foot glowing gold. **Show me** runs all five and ends with a throw into the bullseye. **Next cue** goes one at a time. **Throw with Bo** says "Ready, aim..." and then the cues on a slow beat, with no ball, so the class can copy. Tap a cue card to jump to that pose. **Left hand** mirrors Bo and swaps the stepping foot.
- **Test it:** a small experiment in five steps: Ask, Guess, Way A, Way B, Look. Four questions: step or stay, where to look, close or far, big or small target. Each round is 5, 10 or 20 throws, picked on the Guess step: pick the size that fits your throwers, since the same children throw in both rounds in the same order, one throw each. That keeps the test fair: only one thing changes. Each throw fills a ten-frame (filled ball for a hit, an empty circle with an x for a miss). Look shows both counts as ten-frames, a big number and a bar. **Show the answer** crowns the way with more hits and says whether it matched the class's guess.
- **Free throw:** open practice at the paper target. Every tap on the screen leaves a ball mark on the target or on the wall around it. After every fifth throw, **Coach says** looks at the last five and gives one tip (too high, too short, off to the side, or great aiming).

## What to look for

- **Kindergarten (S1.E13.K):** the underhand throw with a step on the foot opposite the throwing hand. Watch the feet more than where the ball lands.
- **Grade 1 (S1.E13.1):** two of the five underhand cues.
- **Grade 2 (S1.E13.2):** all five underhand cues, a mature pattern, and a start on the overhand throw (S1.E14: Overhand in Form, two of its five cues).

## Talk about it

- Which foot steps when you throw with your right hand? With your left hand?
- Where should your hand be when you let go of an underhand throw?
- What was the one thing we changed between Way A and Way B? What stayed the same?
- Did the test match our guess? If we tested again, would we get the same numbers?
- Why do we count hits instead of just saying which way felt better?

## Good to know

- **Safety:** soft balls or beanbags only. Students throw at the paper target on the wall, never at the screen, a TV, a touch panel or the laptop. Wait behind the line for your turn. The screen shows the target and keeps score; someone taps where each ball hit.
- For **Close or far?**, make two tape lines: three giant steps and six giant steps from the wall.
- **Undo** takes back the last tap, including Way A's last throw until Way B's first. On Look, **Undo last throw** goes back to Way B. **Stop test** and **Clear the wall** sit apart from Undo and ask Yes or No before any throws are lost; so do the mode buttons and keys 1 to 3 in the middle of a test.
- This tool teaches throwing. The module is Throwing and Catching; catching is a later K-2 tool's job.
- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes. On the stage, the arrow keys aim and Enter or Space throws. Every button has a picture and every line is spoken, so non-readers can follow along.
- **Motion** off keeps the same timing and narration; Bo just jumps from pose to pose.
- Standards: SHAPE America Standard 1 (motor skills), grade-level outcomes S1.E13 (underhand throw) and S1.E14 (overhand throw); the fair test in Test it follows NGSS Practice 3 (planning and carrying out investigations).
- Everything is drawn and played in the browser. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere.
