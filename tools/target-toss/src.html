🧢 Coach saysThrow five balls. Then look here.

🛡 Soft balls or beanbags only. Throw at the paper target on the wall, never at the screen, a TV, a touch panel or the laptop. Wait behind the line for your turn.

📋 How to set up

  1. Tape a paper target on the wall, two big steps away from the screen.
  2. Draw three rings on it like the screen: gold in the middle, then red, then blue. (A poster-size sheet works well.)
  3. Put a tape line on the floor.
  4. Pick your throwers. They throw soft balls at the paper target, one at a time.
  5. Tap the screen where each ball hit.
  6. Pick up the balls only when the teacher says, after everyone has thrown.
