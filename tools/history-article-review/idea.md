# History Article Review (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-5-history-article-review, grade 5. Codes: D2.His.17.3-5, D4.4.3-5, D4.5.3-5
- Format / family: explorer / none. Fit: change to studio, because the student writes a one-sentence summary and a review rather than browsing a collection
- One idea: The student is the reviewer for a kids' history magazine: three short articles about the Revolution each hide one weakness (too little evidence, steps out of order, a missing voice); the student writes each one's main claim in a sentence, marks the weakness, and gives stars with reasons before the editor's own review appears.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a reviewer's desk with three article proofs, a red pencil and a review card (Main claim / Argument / Explanation / Stars).
  - Core loop: read an article; write its main claim in one sentence (a hint compares it to the headline); check the argument (enough evidence for the claim?) and the explanation (do the steps follow in order?), and ask whose voices are missing (Loyalists, Native nations, enslaved and free Black people); give stars with a reason.
  - The aha or the end state: the editor's review appears after the student's own; a well-written article can still rest on thin evidence, and a plain one can be the most trustworthy.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a fourth article with no planted weakness, to test whether students invent problems.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.His.17.3-5: the one-sentence main-claim summary for each article.
  - D4.4.3-5: the argument check (is the evidence enough, and does it fit the claim?).
  - D4.5.3-5: the explanation check (steps in order, causes linked to effects).
- Not like: revolution-road (timeline of the same events from three viewpoints); source-scout (judges raw sources, not a finished article); headline-writer (ELA main idea of a news story).
- Art: generated scenes (magazine spreads, the reviewer's desk) and an illustrated editor, because the magazine must look real enough to review.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: sensitive (the missing-voices article covers Native nations and enslaved people in the Revolution; fresh review agent)
