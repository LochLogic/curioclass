# Fossil and Bone Bench (idea card)

- Module: science-8-evidence-of-common-ancestry, grade 8. Codes: MS-LS4-1, MS-LS4-2, MS-LS4-3
- Format / family: lab / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Order fossils through rock layers to find patterns of change, compare limb bones (a whale flipper, a bat wing, a human arm), and compare early embryos across species.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a bench with rock layers, three limb bones and embryo drawings. "Start with the layers."
  - Core loop: order fossils through rock layers to find patterns of change, compare a whale flipper, a bat wing and a human arm, and compare early embryos across species.
  - The aha or the end state: three kinds of evidence point to common ancestry.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): none.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MS-LS4-1: patterns in the fossil record.
  - MS-LS4-2: anatomical similarities and differences.
  - MS-LS4-3: embryological development.
- Not like: fossil-clues (grade 3 fossils and environments); bug-hunt (natural selection).
- Art: public domain or generated (bones and embryos, checked).
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: keep
- Flags: overlap risk (fossil-clues; different depth)
- Origin: new tool from the 2026-10-27 overlap and gap audit (docs/agents/audit/gaps.md); codes from bug-hunt (MS-LS4-1, MS-LS4-2, MS-LS4-3).
