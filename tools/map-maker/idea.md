# Map Maker (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-3-map-skills-and-regions, grade 3. Codes: D2.Geo.1.3-5, D2.Geo.2.3-5
- Format / family: studio / me-map. Fit: good, if a built-in traveler replaces the partner trade for solo use.
- One idea: Make a map of a new town on an island you have only seen from a satellite photo, placing the port, farms and homes where the land suits them, with a key, a scale bar and a grid, then hand it to a traveler who follows it and shows where your map would get them lost.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a satellite-style photo of an unfamiliar island (coast, river, hills, forest) beside a blank map sheet. "Trace the coastline onto your map."
  - Core loop: trace landforms, then place town features; each has a reason check (a port needs deep water by the coast, farms do best on flat land by the river). Add key symbols, a scale bar and grid letters.
  - Traveler test: Nia the traveler gets three errands ("go 2 km from the port to the market in square C4") and follows the student's map; a missing key symbol, wrong scale or mislabeled grid sends her the wrong way, with the step shown.
  - The aha or the end state: the student fixes the map until Nia finishes every errand, then sees a second map of their own school area to label as a familiar place.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): trade maps with a partner, as the original concept had.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - D2.Geo.1.3-5: construct maps of an unfamiliar island and a familiar place with key, scale and grid.
  - D2.Geo.2.3-5: use the satellite image to explain why features sit where they do (port on the coast, farms by the river).
- Not like: compass-rose-quest (grade 1 directions and a room map); me-on-the-map (zooming levels).
- Art: generated scenes for the satellite-style island; drawn in code for the student map so it can be edited.
