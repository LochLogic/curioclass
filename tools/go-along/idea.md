# Conformity Experiments (idea card)

- Module: social-studies-12-social-psychology-and-mental-health, grade 12. Codes: APA.Sociocultural, APA.Individual Variations, APA.Mental and Physical Health
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good for social psychology; the other two codes need their own tools (see below)
- One idea: Sit in a recreated Asch line study with illustrated classmates who all give the same wrong answer, feel the pull, then step through the bystander studies and Milgram's obedience work, learn what they really found (and what the famous Kitty Genovese story got wrong), and judge each study's ethics.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a classroom table, five illustrated students, a card of lines: "Which line matches?" Only after the run does the tool reveal the set-up.
  - Core loop: experience a scaled-down version where it is ethical (the Asch task), then change the conditions (one ally, a private answer, a bigger group) and see how conformity shifts in the published results. Bystander and obedience studies are presented as documented history with their data, not re-enacted.
  - Ethics check: for each study, the student rates harm, consent and deception against today's research rules and sees how the field changed after these studies.
  - The aha or the end state: a "when I'd speak up" note the student keeps on the device (stated on screen), and the human side: the real people who did step in.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): an anonymous class conformity run.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - APA.Sociocultural: conformity, group size, obedience, bystander effect and research ethics, through the student's own run and the documented studies.
  - MOVE APA.Individual Variations: personality, intelligence and motivation don't fit a conformity tool; new tool: a personality and motivation lab comparing trait theories with real measures and their limits.
  - MOVE APA.Mental and Physical Health: to mind-map (health 9-12 mental health explorer) if its spec teaches disorders, treatment and stress and health in full; otherwise a new psychology tool on disorders and treatment, with support resources.
- Not like: illusion-lab (perception); jury-room (deliberation); brain-explorer (biology).
- Art: people (illustrated) participants and generated lab scenes, no reuse of the original study photos unless public domain and checked, because the student must feel seated in the room.
- Proposed title: Go Along? (the new tool is a social psychology lab; mental health moves out)
- Proposed concept line: Sit in a recreated line-judging study as classmates all answer wrong, then examine the bystander and obedience studies, what they really found and how ethical they were.
- Flags: sensitive (deception in research, distress in Milgram's study; support resources kept if any mental health content stays)
