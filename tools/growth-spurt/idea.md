# Growth Spurt (idea card)

- Module: math-4-multiplicative-comparison, grade 4. Codes: 4.OA.A
- Format / family: simulator / none. Fit: good.
- One idea: In a school greenhouse, two plants grow side by side and two sliders ask different questions, "taller by" and "times as tall," so you see when the gap stays the same and when it multiplies; then you pack seedlings into trays and decide what the remainder means.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a sunny greenhouse with a bean and a sunflower and a gardener, Mr. Haddad. "The sunflower is 3 times as tall as the bean. Drag the bean taller and watch."
  - Core loop: the child grows the smaller plant; in "times as" mode the bigger one keeps its multiple (bean 8 cm, sunflower 24 cm, written 24 = 3 x 8), in "taller by" mode it keeps its gap (bean 8 cm, sunflower 24 cm, then 10 and 26). A strip diagram under the plants shows which kind of comparison is running.
  - Word problems: "A pumpkin vine is 4 times as long as the pea vine, which is 9 inches." The child picks multiply or add, then solves.
  - Tray jobs: 50 seedlings, 6 per tray. How many trays to hold them all (9)? How many full trays to sell (8)? How many left for the class (2)? The child picks which answer fits the question.
  - The aha or the end state: "3 times as many" and "3 more" look alike in words but behave differently as plants grow; and a remainder means different things in different questions.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): multistep greenhouse orders with a letter for the unknown and an estimate check.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 4.OA.A: reading a multiplication equation as a comparison (4.OA.1), solving comparison problems by multiplying or dividing and telling them apart from additive comparison (4.OA.2), and multistep problems where remainders must be interpreted, with letters for unknowns and estimation checks (4.OA.3).
- Not like: equal-groups-zoo (grade 3 meaning of multiplication), shrink-ray (grade 5 multiplying by fractions as scaling), smoothie-lab (grade 6 ratios).
- Art: generated scenes (greenhouse, plants that grow smoothly), people (illustrated) for Mr. Haddad.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Two greenhouse plants grow side by side; switch between "taller by" and "times as tall" and watch the gap stay fixed or multiply. Then pack seedlings into trays and decide what the remainder means.
- Flags: none (made-up greenhouse numbers; no real animal facts needed)
