arc: explore-first
minutes: 30
focus: AASL.Explore; AASL.Curate
goal: I can find picture books, fiction and nonfiction in the library and use spine labels and ABC order to find a book.
materials: The tool on the big screen. Three real books from your library: a picture book, a fiction book and a nonfiction book.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet.
---
## Before class

- Open **Explore**.
- Libraries label spines differently (E or EASY, J or FIC). Check your own shelves and pick real books whose labels you can compare with the tool's.

## Hook (4 min)

1. Hold up the three real books. "These all live in different places in our library. How could we know where each one goes?"
2. Point to a spine label. "This little sticker is a secret address."

## Explore (12 min)

1. Press **Take the tour**. Hoot visits picture books, fiction, nonfiction, the checkout desk and the reading rug.
2. Tap nonfiction and look at its neighborhoods: 500 science, 590 animals, 900 places and history.
3. Switch to **Spine detective**. Read the label together: "E, ELL." "Where does this book live?" A child drags it to the section.
4. Keep going for the round. A book sent to the rug gets a smile: a good place to read it, but where does it live?

## Make sense (5 min)

1. "What's the difference between fiction and nonfiction?" (Made-up stories, and true information.)
2. Compare the tool's labels with your three real books. "Our library uses ___ for picture books."
3. "Why does it matter that books go back in the right place?"

## Apply (6 min)

Switch to **ABC shelf**. Children tap the picture book that comes first in ABC order, then the next, until the shelf is full. Sing or say the alphabet when the class gets stuck.

## Exit check (3 min)

Hold up each real book. Children point to where in the room (or the library) it lives and say the section name: picture books, fiction or nonfiction.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** use only E and a number in **Spine detective** at first.
- **Support:** keep an alphabet strip on the board for **ABC shelf**.
- **Stretch:** "Why do fairy tales live in nonfiction, at 398?"
- **Stretch:** find a real book from the 590 animal neighborhood during library time.

## Short on time

- **Take the tour** in **Explore** (4 min).
- One round of **Spine detective** (5 min).
