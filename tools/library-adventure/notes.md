## A 5-minute start

1. Open **Explore** and press **Take the tour**. Hoot visits picture books, fiction, nonfiction, the checkout desk and the reading rug.
2. Switch to **Spine detective**. Read the label on the book together: "E, ELL."
3. Ask: where does this book live? Drag it to the section, or tap the section.

## The three modes

- **Explore:** tap any part of the library. Nonfiction also shows its "neighborhoods": 398 fairy tales, 500 science, 590 animals, 600 how things work, 700 art and sports, 800 poems and jokes, 900 places and history.
- **Spine detective:** eight books per round. E means a picture book, FIC means fiction, a number means nonfiction. Sending a book to the desk or the rug gets a smile: a good place to read it, but where does it live?
- **ABC shelf:** five picture books with different first letters. Tap the one that comes first in ABC order, then the next, until the shelf is full.

## Make it match your library

Libraries label spines differently. Some use E or EASY, some use J or JF for fiction, some shelve picture books by the first letter only. Before using Spine detective, show students a real book from your shelves and compare.

## Talk about it

- What's the difference between fiction and nonfiction?
- Why do fairy tales live in nonfiction (398)?
- Why does it matter that books go back in the right place?

## Good to know

- Keys 1 to 3 switch modes.
- All titles and authors are made up, so no real book is right or wrong.
