Cargo Hold is a sliding-block puzzle in a ship's hold seen from above. Crates, barrels, canvas bales and long spars are packed around one brass-bound sea chest, and the goal is to slide them until the chest sits in the square of daylight under the hatch; then a rope and hook haul it up and out. There are 38 holds on five decks, from a 4 by 4 locker solved in 3 slides to a crowded hold that takes 41. Each slide is one move, and stars go to players who use the fewest: three stars for matching the hold's "par", the smallest number of slides it can be done in.

**Things to notice together.** The best players plan backward: start from the goal ("the chest has to end up there, so that crate must move, and before that the barrel") and only then touch a piece. Ask a child to say the next three slides out loud before making them. After a solve, the par is a fair challenge, not a guess: every hold was checked by a computer search that tried every arrangement, so "can you do it in fewer moves?" always has an answer. Comparing two solutions of the same hold, and finding where the longer one wasted a slide, is the heart of the game. The iron masts never move, which makes a good question: how does one fixed post change every route around it?

**How to play.** Drag a crate, barrel or the chest along a straight line; it slides until something stops it, and you can turn a corner mid-drag. Or tap a piece, then tap where it should go in the same row or column. On a keyboard, Tab picks a piece, the arrow keys slide it one square (Shift and an arrow slides it all the way), Z undoes, R resets, H shows a hint and M turns sound off. Holds opens the list of all 38, and every hold is open from the start.

**Good to know.** It works with a mouse, a trackpad or a finger, on a phone or a laptop. Undo and Reset are always there, and a slide that is undone does not count; sliding the same piece back and forth along one line counts as one move. The lantern hint shows one good next slide; it does not take stars away, so you may want to agree on when to use it. Stars and best scores are saved on this device only, nothing is sent anywhere, and reduced motion settings make pieces move in one step and skip the hoist.
