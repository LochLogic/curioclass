arc: explore-first
minutes: 30
focus: NHES.S1
goal: I can name the five food groups, sort foods into them, and build a lunch with all five.
materials: The tool on the big screen. Paper plates or a drawn tray for each child, and crayons.
grouping: Whole class on the carpet, then at tables.
---
## Before class

- Open **Build a lunch**.
- There are no "good" or "bad" foods in this tool. Cookies and chips are "sometimes foods" that don't fill a group.
- Be mindful of children's food situations. Focus on trying new foods and what helps our bodies, not on what anyone's family eats.

## Hook (4 min)

1. "What did you eat for lunch yesterday?" Take a few answers.
2. "Our bodies need different kinds of food to grow and play. There are five groups. Let's build a lunch and find them all."

## Explore (10 min)

1. In **Build a lunch**, a child picks a food from the shelf. It lands in its group's spot on the tray.
2. Keep going with different children. When someone asks "What's still empty?", press **Is my tray ready?** to make the empty spots glow.
3. Fill all five spots and watch the tray celebrate. Add a cookie: it goes on the napkin.

## Make sense (6 min)

1. Name the groups together: fruits, vegetables, grains, protein and dairy. Point to each spot on the tray.
2. Switch to **Sort it**. The class decides the group for each food, then a child drags it. A wrong spot gives a hint.
3. "Corn and peas: fruit or vegetable?" (Vegetable.) "Which group helps build strong bones?" (Dairy.)

## Apply (7 min)

1. Switch to **Fix the tray**. Look at pizza day. "Which group is missing?" Add it.
2. At tables, children draw their own lunch on a paper plate with one food from each group.

## Exit check (3 min)

Children hold up their plates. Point to a spot and ask the child to name the group. Then ask the class: "Name a food from the grains group." Listen for answers from each group.

## Support and stretch

- **Support:** use the picture spots on the tray as clues for each group.
- **Support:** sort only fruits and vegetables first.
- **Stretch:** "Can you name a vegetable that's a root? A leaf? A seed?"
- **Stretch:** "What would you add to the picnic tray?"

## Short on time

- Fill one tray in **Build a lunch** (6 min).
- One lunch in **Fix the tray** (4 min).
