# Detective del Pasado (idea card)

- Module: world-languages-6-12-past-tenses, grade 6-12. Codes: ACTFL.Intermediate Mid
- Format / family: mystery / spanish. Fit: good: the case turns on what was going on versus what happened, which is the preterite and imperfect split.
- One idea: Solve the case of the missing cake at a town festival by reading witness statements where the tense is the clue: "Cuando llegué, Marta salía" (she was leaving as I arrived) and "Cuando llegué, Marta salió" (she left after I arrived) put different people at the scene, then tell the story of what happened in your own report.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: the festival hall after the party, an empty cake stand, and four witnesses. The inspector: "Escucha bien. El tiempo del verbo importa."
  - Core loop: witnesses give statements aloud and in text. The student places each clause on a timeline: imperfect clauses become the background band (estaba lloviendo, eran las ocho, la música sonaba) and preterite clauses become points (entró, rompió, se fue). Contradictions appear when two statements cannot both fit. The student then asks follow-up questions and writes key lines of the report, choosing tenses that decide who is a suspect.
  - The aha or the end state: the student names the culprit and tells the whole story in a short paragraph, background in the imperfect and events in the preterite, and sees that changing one verb's tense changes who could have done it.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): a second case; a "rewrite the statement" challenge where one tense change clears a suspect.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - ACTFL.Intermediate Mid: narrating and describing in the past in strings of sentences, choosing preterite or imperfect by meaning.
- Not like: verb-conjugation-race (present forms); then-what-happened (ELD English narration); consejero (subjunctive).
- Art: illustrated festival hall and witnesses (people, illustrated); drawn in code for the timeline.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Solve the case of the missing festival cake from witness statements where the tense is the clue; place imperfect background and preterite events on a timeline, find the contradiction, and tell what happened in your report.
- Flags: needs a data source (recorded Spanish voices); none else.
