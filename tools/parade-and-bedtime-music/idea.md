# Parade and Bedtime Music (idea card)

- Module: arts-k-2-music-for-every-moment, grade k-2. Codes: MU:Re8, MU:Re9, MU:Cn11
- Format / family: sort / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Sort short original tunes into the moments of a day (bedtime, a parade, a birthday, a rainy afternoon) by their clues, then find the twist: the parade tune slowed down and played softly becomes a lullaby, so the moment lives in how the music is played, not only in the tune.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a picture-book day with four moment scenes and a row of music boxes to tap and hear.
  - Core loop: hear a tune, drag it to a moment, then tap the clue that told you (soft or loud, fast or slow, smooth or bouncy, high or low). If a tune lands in the wrong place, the scene reacts (the baby wakes up, the parade drags).
  - The aha or the end state: the twist round: one tune and two knobs, speed and loudness. Turn them until it fits bedtime, then until it fits the parade. Then the child picks a tune for a real purpose (a song for grandma's birthday, music to clean up to) and says why with a clue chip.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): the class votes on a favorite and the reasons are tallied.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - MU:Re8: naming the expressive clue (dynamics, tempo) that shows what the music is for, and changing it with the knobs.
  - MU:Re9: choosing music for a stated purpose and giving a personal reason.
  - MU:Cn11: moments from daily life in different homes and places (a market morning, a train ride, a family celebration), each with music made for it.
- Not like: sound-elevator (high and low, loud and soft as sound only); beat-buddies (steady beat); orchestra-zoo (instrument families).
- Art: generated scenes for each moment, warm picture-book style; all music original to Curio Class.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Sort short original tunes into the moments they fit (bedtime, a parade, a birthday, a rainy day) and name the clue; then turn one tune's speed and loudness until a parade march becomes a lullaby.
- Flags: none
