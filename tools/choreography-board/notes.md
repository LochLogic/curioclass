## What it teaches
Students create eight-count movement phrases and revise a dance with repetition, retrograde, mirror, level, tempo, dynamics, size and canon. They compare versions in motion and explain how a revision changes their idea.

## Key skills
- Arrange movement ideas into a longer dance.
- Change one choreographic device and watch its effect.
- Recognize high, middle and low levels and six energy qualities.
- Compare revisions using movement and simple counts.
- Explain a choice and respond to a partner's feedback.

## Support at home
- Make four comfortable shapes together. Repeat them in reverse order and talk about how the ending changes.
- Try the same small hand gesture smoothly and sharply. Ask which one communicates your idea.
- Take turns starting a simple seated arm phrase one count after each other to try canon.

## A five-minute start
Press Play to watch First Light. Select Wave on the timeline and try Retrograde. Open Compare and press Play both. Ask what changed that the numbers did not capture. Return to Build, try a different device and add a reason in Compare.

## Modes
Build has Devices, Cast, Phrases and History sections. Compact windows use phrase and move pickers to keep short choice sets together. Build places phrases on a timeline and edits one block at a time. Tap a phrase chip, then a + gap, or press Enter to append it. Select a block to choose dancers, formation or devices. In compact views, an open device uses Device and Device option pickers; choose Back to all devices to return to the buttons. Tap the same active device or option to remove it. Devices stack in this order: Level, Size, Dynamics, Mirror, Retrograde, Tempo, Repeat, Canon.

Make a phrase opens eight slots. Select a slot, then a move, level and energy quality. Hold copies the previous shape. Try it previews all eight counts. Save it with a short descriptive name, then place it on the timeline. Edit a copy opens a starter without changing the original.

Compare has Before, Measures and Reasons sections. It plays any saved version against the current one from count 1 on one clock. A shorter dance holds its final shape. Ghost overlays Before as faint outlines. Go back to Before makes a new version. Add an optional reason or note to the current version.

Show presents the entire dance after a two-second title, with a color wash on each block and a bow at the end. Stop and Back work immediately. Duration depends on dance length and track. First Light lasts about 19 seconds on Drift; 96 counts on Drift lasts about 76 seconds, plus the title and bow.

## Talk questions
- What does your first shape suggest, and does the last shape complete that idea?
- Which revision changes the movement without changing its ingredients?
- How does a canon change what you notice about the same phrase?
- Ask your partner for one concrete observation. Revise one choice and explain why you kept or changed it.

## Good to know
The stage is an audience view. Stage left is the dancer's left, which appears to your right. Formation changes take two counts. There is no top-down placement.

Levels and dynamics count every dancer on every count, including holds before a canon entrance and after a dancer finishes. Formation changes count differences between adjacent blocks. Unison and canon are timeline counts, not dancer-counts. The bars describe choices; they are not quality scores. Rounded percentages may total 99 or 101.

Half time doubles block duration, double time halves it, Repeat doubles it, and canon adds the last dancer's delay. There are at most eight blocks, 96 counts and 40 versions. The oldest version drops at the limit. Editing after Undo appends a new version while keeping the intervening versions.

Work saves only in this browser on this device. No names are requested and nothing is sent anywhere. New dance resets the dance and its history while retaining custom phrases. Clear saved removes all saved work; the starter appears as a fresh unsaved workspace. The next edit starts saving again.

Space plays or stops. Keys 1, 2 and 3 open Build, Compare and Show. Delete removes the selected block. Control+Z and Control+Y move through history. Tab reaches panel controls before timeline blocks. Motion off keeps count timing and music while displaying count keyframes. Sound off silences music and voice; Read still reads the current hint and stops playback first.

Four painted dancers use articulated upright poses plus seven complete painted low and bending poses each. Floor and deep-bend paintings change on the count so the body stays intact. The cast includes varied skin tones and builds and a dancer with a prosthesis. The theater and dancers are generated art. Narration uses browser speech for this build; the fixed hero-coach recording list is prepared but no audio was recorded.
