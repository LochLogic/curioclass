# Food Web Wobble (idea card)

- Module: science-5-food-webs-and-matter-cycles, grade 5. Codes: 5-LS1-1, 5-LS2-1, 5-PS3-1
- Format / family: simulator / ecosystem. Fit: good
- One idea: Build a pond-edge food web that includes the sun, air, water, soil and decomposers, run it, then pull out one species or the decomposers and watch matter stop moving and the web wobble, so the student sees that food is built from air and water and every animal's energy began as sunlight.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a pond edge with clover, a grasshopper, a frog and a heron already placed, a tray of more cards (fungi, worms, bacteria, cattail, minnow), and a sun, an air cloud and a water drop along the top.
  - Core loop: draw "is eaten by" and "breaks down into" arrows, then press Run and let seasons pass. Matter dots travel from air and water into plants, up to animals, down through decomposers into soil and back out to air. Yellow energy marks ride along and fade as animals move, grow and keep warm. Remove a species or the decomposers: dead leaves pile up, soil runs thin, plants slow, and the animals that ate them drop.
  - Plant check: a sunflower grows in a pot on a scale; at the end the soil weighs almost what it did at the start, and the plant's gain matches what came in as carbon dioxide and water.
  - The aha or the end state: three "where did it come from?" questions about one frog: its body (matter from plants, which built it from air and water), its hop (energy from the sun, through food), and where its matter goes when it dies.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 5-LS1-1: the pot-on-a-scale run is the evidence; the student picks and defends the claim that the plant's material came mostly from air and water, not soil.
  - 5-LS2-1: the matter dots model movement among plants, animals, decomposers and the environment, and removing the decomposers breaks the loop.
  - 5-PS3-1: the energy marks trace each animal's food energy (repair, growth, motion, warmth) back to sunlight.
- Not like: energy-pyramid (grade 7, counts energy lost per level); predator-prey-island (two populations over time); carrying-capacity (grade 9 logistic growth).
- Art: generated scenes for the pond and organism cards in a painted style, with dots and arrows drawn in code, because the living things should look real and the flows must stay crisp.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Build a pond food web with decomposers, air and water, then run it: matter dots loop through, sunlight energy flows one way, and pulling out one species or the decomposers makes the web wobble.
- Flags: none
