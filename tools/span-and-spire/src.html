Span and Spire / Field manual

At the test yard

Choose Bridge or Tower at the console. Any load, wind or shake is yours to try, in any order.

Build and measure

Drag from a joint to make a beam. Close triangles. Pick a piece, then tap a beam to change its material. Tap a member to read its length, price and force limits. Shift drag moves a free joint.

Press Go or drag a truck across. Worst spots shows the whole crossing. In Tower, drag the top sideways to pluck it, then compare its own sway rate with Speed. Shake runs for 15 seconds. Wind records a steady 5 seconds.

The dials

Load reads vehicle mass or top mass. Worst member reads the current force as a percent of its limit; a loose frame has no valid reading. Sway reads the actual top displacement, before visual magnification. Gauge needles stop at the end of their scale; the number keeps reading beyond it.

Budget starts off. Switch it on, set a limit and choose Done. Budget stays on; an over-limit design still builds and tests. The county brief always uses $9,000 independently of your dial. Costs are classroom estimates, not construction quotes.

Honesty notes

A two-dimensional, pin-jointed axial model. The bridge draws one of two sides, each carrying half the truck and 2.0 kN/m of road. Members carry their full stated self-weight. Timber has one simplified 20 MPa strength. Euler buckling assumes pinned ends. Cables pull only; crossing beams connect only at a joint.

Shakes are synthetic sine waves with 5% damping, not historic earthquakes. Sag and wind displacement are shown ×20 and shake sway ×5; True size removes that magnification. Motion off keeps the same test duration.

Keep a record

Save to Yard keeps designs here. Test all compares them; Open edits a copy. Borrow overlays a saved design so you can adopt its members and materials. Old farm bridge is always available to test.

Saved designs and logbook records stay on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Keyboard

Tab to controls or the grid. Arrows move the cursor; Enter starts and ends a member; Delete erases. Keys 1 to 5 pick a material. Space runs or stops. R rebuilds after a collapse. Shift with an arrow plucks a tower.

Model sources: Hibbeler, Structural Analysis; USDA Wood Handbook FPL-GTR-282; ASTM A36; FHWA Bridge Formula Weights; Chopra, Dynamics of Structures; OpenStax hydrostatics.
