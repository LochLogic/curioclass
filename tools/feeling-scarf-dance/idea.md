# Feeling Scarf Dance (idea card)

- Module: arts-k-2-dancing-a-feeling, grade k-2. Codes: DA:Cr2, DA:Pr6, DA:Cn10
- Format / family: studio / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Pick a feeling, then build a tiny dance in three parts (a starting shape, a moving middle, an ending shape) and dance it yourself with a real scarf or any soft cloth, as an on-screen dancer and her scarf show how floaty, sharp or wiggly movement makes the feeling visible.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a feeling card deck (calm, excited, grumpy, brave, sleepy) and a set-up card: "Find a scarf, a dish towel or a T-shirt. Make a stage on the floor and an audience spot."
  - Core loop: choose a beginning shape (tall, curled, wide), a middle movement (float, swirl, snap, shake) and an ending shape. The on-screen dancer shows the three-part dance, her scarf trailing the movement; original music matches. Then the child dances it with their own cloth while the tool counts in, plays the music and calls "beginning, middle, end, hold".
  - The aha or the end state: the same three shapes danced with a floaty middle and then a snapping middle, side by side; the scarf trails look completely different, and so does the feeling. The child chooses which feeling each one shows.
  - Class mode or extras (optional): half the class dances while the other half watches from the audience spot, then swaps.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - DA:Cr2: choosing and ordering a beginning, middle and end that express the feeling.
  - DA:Pr6: dancing in a marked stage space for an audience (family, stuffed animals or the class), using a hand-held prop.
  - DA:Cn10: "When did you feel this way?" picture cards link the dance to the child's own experience.
- Not like: levels-and-pathways (space words, reading floor ribbons); beat-buddies (steady beat); act-it-out (acting a story).
- Art: people (illustrated) for the dancer, with her own figure (not shared with PE tools, per the levels-and-pathways call); scarf drawn with flowing cloth motion.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Pick a feeling, build a three-part dance (a starting shape, a moving middle, an ending shape), watch a dancer's scarf show it, then dance it yourself with any soft cloth for an audience at home or in class.
- Flags: none
