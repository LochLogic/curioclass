## What it teaches

Children can organize and perform a dance with a beginning, middle and end that expresses a feeling. They can describe the movement they noticed as audience members.

## Key skills

- Choose shapes and movement to show a feeling.
- Link a held beginning, a moving middle and a held ending.
- Notice how smooth, sharp, turning and shaking movements change a scarf's flow.
- Perform for someone and give specific, kind observations.

## Support at home

- Use a tissue, dish towel or empty hand to make a dance about something that happened today.
- Take turns performing three parts. Ask, "What did the movement make you think or feel?"
- Try the same feeling two different ways. There is more than one good dance for a feeling.

## A five-minute start

1. Open Build and tap a feeling. Invite a child to connect it to a moment in their life, without requiring a personal disclosure.
2. Choose a start shape, a middle move and an end shape. Tap their part cards to change choices in any order.
3. Feeling idea offers one suggestion. It is a starting point, not a correct answer.
4. Tap Watch it, then Play. Watch what the scarf does in each part.
5. Tap Your turn. Find a little space and a start shape, then tap Go. The four-count introduction comes before the dance.

## The modes

**Build:** Five feelings, five starting and ending shapes, and six middle moves. Tap Start, Middle or End to see its choices. Nothing is saved after the page closes.

**Watch:** A four-count start, eight-count middle and four-count end. Tap a part card to open its choice menu, including during a run. Tap the feeling button to change feeling. A feeling or Slow change updates the music tempo at the next count. Again starts over. Stop holds the current image.

The choice menu has its own Read button. It stops an active dance before reading the current choice instruction, so narration never speaks over the music. Tapping the current mode again keeps its instruction and dance in place.

**Your turn:** The dancer disappears, leaving the scarf and a very faint hand-location mark. Go includes four count-in clicks. Mirror flips the view. Freeze holds the final image for three quiet seconds. The spoken freeze reminder follows the quiet hold. An empty hand works as well as a soft cloth. A solo child can perform for an imagined audience or a toy.

**Share:** Audience members name what they saw, then the teacher taps the matching chips. They are observations, not a score. Swap clears the observations for the other half of the class. Go again returns to Your turn.

## Talk together

- What happened in your life that this feeling could remind you of?
- Where could you see the beginning and the ending?
- How did the scarf help you see the middle move?
- Could a calm dance include a quick movement? Show your idea.
- What did you notice in another group's version of the same feeling?

## Good to know

- Teacher led, grades K-2, about 15 to 25 minutes. Kindergarten can decide together; grades 1 and 2 can propose and compare their own three-part dances.
- NCAS DA:Cr2 is practiced through organizing three parts; DA:Pr6 through Your turn performance for an audience; DA:Cn10 through a feeling linked to lived experience. The teacher's invitation to make that connection is part of the lesson.
- Music is original synthesis: Happy 112 beats per minute, Sad 66, Calm 72 in triple meter, Mad 100, Surprised 96. Slow uses 70 percent of the normal tempo. These are expressive choices, not rules about how people must feel.
- Leave room between dancers. Cloths stay in each dancer's own space; children can use seated or small movements.
- Motion off keeps the same timing but shows one pose per count with a straight hanging scarf. The picture shifts upward when needed to keep the straight cloth visible.
- Read stops an active run before reading the current instruction. Sound controls narration and music. The shared kit remembers only Motion preference; dance choices and audience observations stay only in page memory.
- Keyboard: Space on the scene plays or stops in Watch or Your turn. Keys 1 to 5 choose feelings. S, M and E jump to the corresponding Build choices. Tab and Enter reach every control; Escape closes a choice menu.
- The current dancer is the temporary Dance kit figure. The owner requested painted dancers for the next art pass. No people were generated in this build.
- Narration currently uses browser speech. The requested bank voice and every authored narration line are listed in voice/lines.json; no recordings were made.
