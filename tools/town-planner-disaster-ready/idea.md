# Town Planner: Disaster Ready (idea card)

- Module: science-4-natural-resources-and-hazards, grade 4. Codes: 4-ESS3-1, 4-ESS3-2
- Format / family: tycoon / none. Fit: good
- One idea: Run a growing river town on a coast near a fault line and a volcano for twenty years: choose where its energy comes from (coal, gas, wind, sun, river dam), see what each one takes from the land and puts in the air and water, and spend a budget on ways to protect people from floods, earthquakes, a tsunami and ash.
- How it plays (solo student by default):
  - First screen: a town map with a river, a beach, hills and a mountain on the skyline; a mayor's desk with a budget. "Your town needs power, and it needs to be ready."
  - Energy loop: each year, pick power sources; a resource card shows where each comes from (a mine, a well, wind, sunlight, a river) and what it does (smoke, spills, a flooded valley behind a dam, land for panels). A town meter tracks air, water, cost and steady power.
  - Hazard loop: events arrive with a real-world basis (spring floods, an earthquake, a tsunami warning, an ashfall). Before each, the student compares two or three solutions (levees or moving homes uphill, bracing buildings, warning sirens and evacuation routes, ash masks and covered water) by cost and lives protected.
  - The aha or the end state: a twenty-year report: the town's power mix and its trade-offs, and which protections worked; a replay shows the same events with no protections.
- Standards, one line per code:
  - 4-ESS3-1: resource cards and the town meter show that energy and fuels come from natural resources and their use affects the environment.
  - 4-ESS3-2: comparing several solutions before each hazard to reduce its effects on people.
- Not like: grid-operator (high school electricity markets); climate-postcards (grade 3 weather hazards); community-solutions (grade 5 town meeting on local environmental problems).
- Art: generated scenes for the town in each season and event, drawn-in-code map overlays; people (illustrated) for the townspeople, shown safe or in need of help, never harmed on screen.
- Proposed title: keep
- Proposed concept line: Run a coastal river town for twenty years, choose its energy sources and see what each takes from the land and air, and compare ways to protect people from floods, earthquakes, a tsunami and volcanic ash.
- Flags: sensitive (natural disasters; keep losses as town numbers and damage, not injuries shown, and point to real preparedness information)
