# Play-picture cards (day 22)

Six picture cards of play for each child, and a rule card for the wall. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Sort by speed only.

## The cards

- **Fast play:** a hoop rolling down a hill; two children racing across a yard; a top spinning.
- **Slow play:** a child stacking blocks one at a time; a child pouring sand slowly from a cup; a child drawing a long line with a wide brush.

## The rule card

**Picture:** a hare on one half and a snail on the other half, with the words left off. **Say:** one group is fast play and one is slow play.

## How the cards are used

Each child sorts six cards into fast play and slow play, then says the rule: "fast" or "slow". Then show fast and slow as opposites with two fingers. Ask where in the room we move slowly; children point. If a child sorts by color or by who is in the picture, keep the rule card above the groups and sort again (T). Never ask for a stronger word for slow; fast is the opposite of slow.
