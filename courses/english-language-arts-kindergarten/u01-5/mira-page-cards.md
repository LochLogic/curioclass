# Mira page cards (days 20, 21, 22 and 26)

Four small picture cards, one for each of pages 3, 5, 6 and 7 of Mira Makes a Road, with a copy for each pair. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. The pictures match the book's own pictures; the teacher reads the sentence for each page aloud. These are practice cards, not exit cards.

## Page 3

**Picture:** a long road of blocks winding across a rug past a small chair and a table leg, with Mira lying at the end, humming. **Read:** "The road grew. It went past the reading chair."

## Page 5

**Picture:** Mira holding a block in the air; a gap shows in the middle of her road. **Read:** "Mira slid a block into the gap. It fit just right."

## Page 6

**Picture:** a road of blocks that bends around a corner, a blue scarf lying across it and a small block bridge over the scarf. **Read:** "Mira tried two short blocks side by side. The road bent around the corner."

## Page 7

**Picture:** Mira sitting with folded hands and a calm smile, looking at the long road. **Read:** "Mira sat back and smiled at her road."

## How the cards are used

On day 21 each pair has the cards for pages 5, 6 and 7. Read one sentence. One child points to the picture that goes with it; the partner says which words told them. Swap, then read the next sentence. Ask the same question every time: "Which words go with your picture?" The cards also work on days 20, 22 and 26 for pointing from words to a picture. A child who points to a picture of a different page has not yet matched the moment; lay the two pictures beside the sentence and read it again, one line at a time.
