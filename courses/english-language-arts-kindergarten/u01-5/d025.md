# Day 25: A poem, a story and an information book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 24's exit and choose the one error most children made. Hang the three kind cards and the parts card where the rug can see them. Lay out the pencil label and the brush label for each child. Set the credit page for the class book on the table. Today's six are the twenty-fifth six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Say: "These two cards are made up for practice." Reread the day 24 cards, the ramp. If many said "I know a ramp" (error E, a reply that gives no help), say the puppet's line beside a real question: the puppet says "I know a ramp," and you say "What is a ramp?" Ask: "Which one gets help?" Children say the question, echo it once, and ask a partner "What is a ___?" about a word of their own. If many circled two children both rolling (error B, a picture of a different moment), set it beside the picture where one child waits and reread the second card. If only a few erred, take them as a small group during Turn and talk.

#### 8 min. Shared reading

Whole class. Show the large poem Many Ways, and say: "This is a poem." Point under each word, left to right, and read it aloud once (2 minutes). Reread, and the class says the lines with you while fingers act: walking, marching, tiptoeing on the rug (4 minutes). Review: walk, march, tiptoe: three ways of stepping, acted in place (firm and even, or light and quiet) (day 13). Say: "A poem has short lines that go together." Ask the class to point to the card that says poem (2 minutes).

#### 6 min. A story, again

Whole class. Review: Mira Makes a Road, which you heard on days 20 to 22. Read pages 1 and 2 aloud, holding up each picture (3 minutes). Say: "This is a storybook. Someone does things, and the story goes in order." Review: a poem, a story and an information book are three kinds of text (day 9). Ask the class to point to the story card, then to the poem card (3 minutes).

#### 7 min. An information book

Whole class. Review: Push, Pull, Roll, which you heard on days 9, 15, 16, 18, 21 and 23. Hold up the book, and say: "This is an information book. It tells facts." Review: the parts of a book: front cover, back cover and title page (day 2). Show the front cover and the title page (2 minutes). Close the book and turn it over; the class points to the back cover and says how they know: "It is on the outside, on the other side from the front." A child who marks the last inside page as the back cover (error AC) turns the closed book over and finds the outside.

#### 6 min. Makers

Whole class. Review: author (makes the words) and illustrator (makes the pictures) (day 2). Read the two credit lines on the title page of each of the three texts you used: the words are by Curio Class and the pictures are by the Curio Class art team. The class says "words" and "pictures" with their hands: a hand writing, a hand painting. A child who says the writing team made the pictures (error I, the wrong maker) sees the pencil label beside the words and the brush label beside the pictures.

#### 12 min. Class book page

Whole class, then alone. Say: "Our class book, Our Classroom Toys, needs a credit page." Hold up the credit page. It has two places, one for the word-maker label and one for the picture-maker label (2 minutes). You print the title, Our Classroom Toys, with a finger under each word as you write; no child drafts writing (2 minutes). Then each child places the pencil label and the brush label on a small page at their place, saying "Pencil, words; brush, pictures" (6 minutes). Walk and listen to the day's six, a minute each. A child may point and not say.

#### 10 min. Turn and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass (day 1). Model with the puppet, one line at a time (2 minutes): Puppet: "How are a poem, a story and an information book different?" You: "A poem has short lines. A story has someone who does things." Puppet: "Tell me more." You: "An information book tells facts." Review: "Tell me more" keeps a talk going (day 22). Pairs then talk, 4 minutes each way, with the turn card passed. The partner says "Tell me more." Walk to the day's six, three pairs, a minute each, and listen for words from the page. A child may pass; the puppet says "I will ask you later." Last, two minutes: three children tell the class one thing they heard, "I heard ___."

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Hand out the day 25 cards, three kinds, a back cover and two makers ([[s01-X25a]], [[s01-X25b]], [[s01-X25c]]). Read the three cards aloud, then each child circles the card that comes from an information book; show the small booklet and each child circles the back cover; read the two credit labels and each child circles the one that says who made the pictures (3 minutes). Then each of the day's six hears one spoken question, 30 seconds each: two children hear "What kind of book is your card from?", two hear "How do you know it is the back cover?", and two hear "What did the writing team make?"

Signs: the short lines that go together circled as an information book (error J, a poem called an information book); the last inside page circled as the back cover (error AC); the Words label circled for the pictures (error I, the wrong maker).

#### 5 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say two words and the class says if they end the same: star and jar (yes); star and stick (no). A child who says star and stick rhyme because they start alike (error Q) hears the endings stretched: star, jar; stick, kick. Then syllables (2 minutes). Review: clap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first (day 18): doll (one), scooter (two), tricycle (three). Say the word, then clap.

#### 5 min. Onset and rime

Whole class, then pairs. Review: onset and rime: a spoken word split into its first sound and the rest, then blended with a hand sweep (day 24). Do "c - art" and "r - ing" again, saying the parts and then sweeping them into the whole word (2 minutes). Then two new words: "wh - eel", then "b - all" (2 minutes). Say the parts, sweep them together, and the class says the whole word. Hold "eel" and "all" together. Pairs try one word each way (1 minute): one child says the parts, the partner sweeps them together. Hear six. A child who leaves "wh - eel" in two parts (error AF) sees the sweep again. A child who splits "eel" into two sounds (error AG) hears "eel" held whole, and only the first sound set apart.

#### 10 min. Letters n and c

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Chant: "n, n, /n/, nest; c, c, /k/, cow" (2 minutes). Review: go both directions on the sound-spelling map: sound to letter, letter to keyword and sound (day 19). Say /n/ and each child holds up a card from their set; say /k/ and /h/ the same; point to a letter on the map and the class says its sound and keyword (4 minutes). Then the large poem Many Ways (4 minutes): children come up one at a time to touch an n in the title, in "and" in "Some play fast and some play slow", or a c in "march", and the class says the name. Naming only: the c in "march" has an h beside it, so it is a different team that comes later; touch it and name it, and say no more.

#### 10 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print n and c on handwriting paper, saying the sound as you form each: n goes down, then up and over the hump; c starts at the top right, goes back and around, and ends there (6 minutes). A child who prints only the capital when small is asked (error P) sees the lowercase path written once. Then mapped words (4 minutes). Review: mapped words the and a, read whole by sight (day 21). Write "the a the a" on chart paper and point; the class reads each. Then show the large poem My Choice and point under "the" in "Some like the cart that rolls down the hall" and under "a" in "We each have a favorite"; you read the other words.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets about 40 seconds (4 minutes, one minute for the move): the name and sound of n and c, then the and a in a list. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger n and c in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /n/ and then /k/. Each child chooses the letter card first, then prints the letter on the card. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we ask a classmate about a toy."

### Other rooms

One child: hold a poem, a story and an information book; point to the front cover, back cover and title page of each, and say who made the words and who made the pictures. Place the two labels on the credit page. Do the exit cards aloud. One projector: show one text at a time and have children point on the screen; mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No labels: draw a pencil and a brush. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the class book page, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. Three kinds.** Show a poem, a story and a fact book; your child says each kind.
  **Say.** "How do you know this is a poem?" Expect: short lines that go together.
- **5 min. Back cover.** Close a book; your child finds the back cover.
- **5 min. Who made it?** Point to the credit; your child says words and pictures.
- **5 min. Split a word.** Say "c - art"; your child sweeps it into cart.
- **5 min. n and c.** Say /n/ and /k/; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X25a]] Read the three cards aloud. Your child circles the information book, finds the back cover and says who made the pictures. [[s01-X25b]] [[s01-X25c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the story reread and the second pair turn; keep the kinds, the makers, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure names the maker of a book they choose from the shelf.

### Words and supports

Words: poem, story, information book, author, illustrator. Frames: "This is a ___." "I heard ___." "Tell me more." A home language, pointing, or a drawn pencil and brush counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 26 begins the partner interview, and its Reopen repairs the most common of errors I, J and AC: the Words and Pictures labels beside the credit lines (error I), the poem beside an information page (error J), or the closed booklet and its outside back (error AC). Day 26 asks a classmate a question about a classroom toy.
