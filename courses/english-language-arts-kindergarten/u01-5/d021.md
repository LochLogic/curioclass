# Day 21: The gap and the corner

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 20's exit and the cycle 2 check notes. Choose the one error most children made on each. Put the day 20 strip and its three pictures where you can reach them. Lay out the blocks for pairs. Hang the letter n and letter c cards, face down, until the foundations block. Today's six are the twenty-first six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Hold up the day 20 strip and reread page 8 of Mira Makes a Road once. If many circled the zoo picture on day 20's exit (error B, a picture of a different moment), put the zoo picture and the lion-on-the-road picture beside the reread sentence "You can walk on it," said Mira, and every child points to the action the sentence names. If many said Kiko was sad with no word from the page (error A, an idea that is not in the text), reread the first two lines and point to his mouth fell open and wow; children point to the words that answer. If only a few erred, take them as a small group during Draw and talk while the rest draw.

#### 3 min. Words first

Whole class. Use real blocks: a gap (two blocks with a space between them; children put a finger in it), a corner (a block turned to bend the line), fit (a block that goes in the space just right). Say each word, the class says it and does the gesture. These are the words on pages 5 and 6.

#### 12 min. The hard moment

Whole class. Say: "Today we go back to the hard part of the story." Read page 5 and page 6 only, holding up each picture (about 2 minutes). Review: Read, Point, Talk, Draw: hear a text, point back, talk, draw one thing and tell a partner (day 2).

**Ask.** What was in the middle of Mira's road? Expect: a gap. Show the picture and put a finger in the gap; the class points to it.

**Ask.** What did Mira do when the long block was too long? Expect: she tried two short blocks side by side. Point under the words as you reread "Mira tried two short blocks side by side." Model pointing from the words to the picture: "The words say the road bent. Here in the picture is the bend." Then the class points to the bend in the picture (3 minutes each Ask).

Pairs then try it with the blocks: make a short road, leave a gap, fill it; make a corner and bend it (4 minutes). A child who builds the road but does not say what fit may point and say "It fit."

#### 12 min. Match picture and sentence

Pairs on the rug, each pair with the page cards for pages 5, 6 and 7 and a turn card. Review: picture and moment: a picture shows one moment (day 4), and "This shows ..." (day 4). Read one sentence at a time:

- "Mira slid a block into the gap. It fit just right." (page 5)
- "Mira tried two short blocks side by side. The road bent around the corner." (page 6)
- "Mira sat back and smiled at her road." (page 7)

In the first round (6 minutes) one child points to the card for each sentence and the partner says "This shows ..."; the card holder passes the turn card to start. In the second round (6 minutes) swap, and read the three sentences in a different order. Walk to the day's six, three pairs, two minutes a pair, and listen for the words that tell. A child who points to the card of a different page (error B) hears the sentence again, one line at a time, with the two cards side by side.

#### 8 min. Information return

Whole class. Review: Push, Pull, Roll, which you heard on days 9, 15, 16 and 18. Review: a picture shows one moment (day 4); here the picture shows a toy in the words. Reread page 4: "A cart has wheels, so it rolls when you push it. A box with no wheels slides and rubs along the floor." Show the picture of the two toys.

**Ask.** Which toy in the picture is the box that slides? Expect: the flat box with no wheels. All point.

**Ask.** Which toy in the picture is the cart that has wheels? Expect: the toy on four round wheels. All point. Then three children say how they know ("The box has no wheels.").

#### 15 min. Draw and talk

Alone, then pairs. Each child draws how Mira fixed the corner on the student page (8 minutes). If a small group needs the day 20 repair, take them now. Then pairs tell each other what they drew and which words match, in two exchanges (4 minutes): "This shows ___." "I heard ___." Then three children share a drawing and the words with the class (3 minutes). A child may point and not tell. Look for: a drawing of the short blocks or the bend; the words "short blocks" or "corner" said or pointed to.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Read page 3 of Mira Makes a Road aloud again; today's work used pages 5 to 7. Hand out the day 21 strip ([[s01-X21v2]]); each child circles the picture for the last sentence (3 minutes). Hear the day's six, 30 seconds each: "Which words go with your picture?" Reread the page if a child asks.

Signs: the road with no one there circled (error B, a picture of a different moment); "Mira is crying" (error A, an idea that is not in the text).

#### 10 min. Response and fresh retry

Whole class, then small groups. Review: repair then fresh retry: wrong example beside a right one, then a new try (day 6). The cycle 2 check on day 20 is the evidence; use the most common slip (4 minutes). The puppet shows one:

- Error N: the puppet says "aitch" for /h/ when the sound is asked. Say the name, then the sound with the letter and keyword hat.
- Error O: the puppet prints h when it hears /p/. Hear the sound, choose the letter card first, then print.
- Error P: the puppet prints only P when lowercase is asked. Model the lowercase path; each child prints it once.
- Error AA: the puppet calls h a p. Hold the two cards side by side and name each.

Then a fresh retry of that one component only (6 minutes), not scored: children who missed it form a group on the rug and work with the cards while you move between groups. Look for: the right name, the right sound, starts at the top, closes the hump or bump.

#### 5 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say the lines of the poem My Choice, "Some like the blocks and some like the ball, / Some like the cart that rolls down the hall." The class jumps on the rhyming words. Then pairs: one says a word that ends like wall, the partner says another (2 minutes). Review: clap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first (day 18): classroom (two), toys (one), dinosaur (three). Say the word first, then clap.

#### 10 min. Letters n and c

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Turn the two new cards face up. The n card: a nest with two eggs; say "n, name en; sound /n/, keyword nest" (a hum through the nose). The c card: a brown cow; say "c, name see; sound /k/, keyword cow. Today c means only this sound." Add both to the map. Say the keyword, the sound, and point to the letter; the class repeats (3 minutes). Then hold up each letter card, capital and small, and the class says the name (2 minutes). Then go both directions (5 minutes). Review: go both directions on the sound-spelling map: sound to letter, letter to keyword and sound (day 19). Say /n/ and each child holds up a card from their set (a, t, h, p, n, c); say /k/, /h/, /p/ the same. Point to a letter on the map and the class says its sound and keyword. A child who says "en" when the sound is asked (error N) hears the name, then the hum: nnn. The name and the sound are different.

#### 5 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print N and n on handwriting paper, saying the sound as you form each: N goes straight down, then a slant down, then straight up; n goes down, then up and over the hump (3 minutes). Then mapped words (2 minutes). Write "the" and "a" on chart paper. Say: "These are words we remember. We know them by sight; we do not sound them out." Point to each; the class says it. Mark the part to remember: for "the", the whole word; for "a", the letter a says its word sound here, not the sound in apple. Say: "A toy. The toy." and point under each word.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets about 40 seconds (4 minutes, one minute for the move): the name and sound of n and c and the words the and a. This is practice only; n and c are never scored today. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger n and c in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /n/. Each child chooses the n card first, then prints n on the card. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we find n and c in a poem."

### Other rooms

One child: reread pages 5 and 6 from the booklet with blocks, match the three cards, draw the corner, and do the exit strip aloud. Hold up the n and c cards, saying name, sound and keyword. One projector: show one picture at a time and have children point on the screen, and mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No blocks: use books or boxes. No page cards: use the story's pictures. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. The hard part.** Read pages 5 and 6 of Mira Makes a Road; your child points to the gap.
  **Say.** "What did Mira do when the long block was too long?" Expect: she used two short blocks.
- **5 min. Build it.** Make a gap and fill it with two blocks or boxes.
- **5 min. Match.** Read one sentence from page 7; your child points to the picture.
- **5 min. n and c.** Say /n/ and /k/; your child points to each letter.
- **5 min. Print n.** Your child prints n and says /n/.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X21v2]] Read page 3 of Mira Makes a Road aloud. Your child circles the picture for the last sentence, then says the words.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the information return and the pair blocks; keep the hard moment, the matching, the exit and the response. Longer: a child who is secure writes the first sound of "corner" on the drawing and says "This shows ___."

### Words and supports

Words: gap, corner, fit. Frames: "This shows ___." "I heard ___." A home language, pointing, or building with blocks counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the road with no one there (error B), day 22's Reopen sets the two page 3 pictures beside the last sentence. Day 22 stays with Mira and asks why she is content, using words heard on pages 4, 7 and 8.
