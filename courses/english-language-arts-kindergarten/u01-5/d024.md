# Day 24: Reread and ask for help

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 23's exit and choose the one error most children made. Hang the poem Our Play where the rug can see it. Lay out a picture for each pair from page 3 of One Hoop, Two Friends and page 4 of Mira Makes a Road. Today's six are the twenty-fourth six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Reread page 8 of The Magic Bow, from the day 23 strip. If many said the people get the bow back (error A, an idea that is not in the text), reread "They kept on trying, and they asked each other for help," and put a finger under the words; every child points under them, and you say: "The bow is still gone. The people keep trying." If many circled the people with their heads down (error B, a picture of a different moment), set that picture beside the child stacking blocks and reread the sentence that goes with the blocks. If only a few erred, take them as a small group during Draw and say.

#### 15 min. Partners reread

Whole class, then pairs. Review: Read, Point, Talk, Draw: hear a text, point back, talk, draw one thing and tell a partner (day 2). Show the two pictures and read the two lines aloud once, finger under each word: Teo asks "Can I have a turn?" (One Hoop, Two Friends, page 3) and Mira says "Thank you for asking." (Mira Makes a Road, page 4). The puppet speaks one line at a time and you answer the other, so the class hears a question and then a reply (3 minutes). Review: question and statement: a question asks for help; a statement tells (day 1).

**Ask.** Which line is a question? Expect: "Can I have a turn?" Children point to the picture of Teo.

Pairs choose one of the two pictures and reread the lines by pointing (8 minutes): one child points under the words, the partner points to the picture, and they swap, with the turn card passed to start. A child may pass and point to a picture. Walk to the day's six, three pairs, a minute each, and listen for the words heard on the page. Last, two pairs show the class where the words are (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Ask for help

Whole class, then pairs. Review: ask for help: "Please read that again" and "What is ___?" (day 22). Read two lines with a part a listener might want again, the puppet listening (3 minutes): "'In a minute,' said Noor." and "There was a gap in it, right in the middle." The puppet says "Please read that again" and you reread; then the puppet says "What is a gap?" and you answer with the blocks, a space between two of them. Review: gap: a space between two blocks (day 21).

Pairs then try, three turns each (5 minutes): you read one of the two lines, one child asks "Please read that again" or "What is ___?", the partner rereads or shows, and they swap. A child may pass; the puppet says "I will ask you later." Hear the day's six, 30 seconds each, a rotating two at a time (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. How do they say what they want?

Whole class. Say: "Each character says what they want. We point to the words." Hold up the picture from each story. Point to Teo's words and ask what Teo wants; the class says "a turn." Point to Mira's words and ask what Mira wants; the class says "to make her road." Then say each as a sentence (6 minutes): "Teo says, 'Can I have a turn?' He wants a turn." "Mira says, 'I like making it.' She wants to keep her road." Children echo each sentence, then three children say their own, with a finger under the words. Last, pairs say one thing a character wanted and where they heard it (4 minutes).

#### 15 min. Draw and say

Alone, then pairs. Each child draws a toy a character used on the student page (8 minutes). Then each child tells a partner which page shows it: "This shows ___." (4 minutes). Then three children share with the class (3 minutes). A child may point and not tell. Look for: a drawing of the hoop or the blocks; the page said or shown.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Say: "These two cards are made up for practice." Hand out the day 24 cards, the ramp ([[s01-X24]]). Read the two cards aloud once, then each child circles the one picture that fits both cards (3 minutes). Hear the day's six, 30 seconds each, on the spoken question. Reread the cards if a child asks.

Signs: two children both rolling circled (error B, a picture of a different moment); "I know a ramp" (error E, a reply that gives no help).

#### 5 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say two words and the class says if they end the same: car and star (yes); car and cow (no). A child who says car and cow rhyme because they start alike (error Q) hears the endings stretched: car, star; cow, now. Then syllables (2 minutes). Review: clap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first (day 18): scooter (two), balloon (two), tricycle (three). Say the word, then clap.

#### 5 min. Onset and rime

Whole class, then pairs. Say: "We can split a word into its first sound and the rest." Model, with a hand sweep: say "b - all", then sweep the hand and say "ball" (the word blended). Do "c - art" and "r - ing" the same way: say the parts, then the whole word (2 minutes). Then the segmenting turn: say "ball" and split it as "b - all"; the class echoes. Hold "all" together; do not break it into two sounds.

Pairs try one word each way (2 minutes): one child says the parts, the partner sweeps them together; swap. Hear six, 10 seconds each. A child who leaves "b - all" in two parts when the whole word is asked (error AF) sees the sweep again, slowly. A child who splits "all" into "a" and "ll" (error AG) hears "all" held whole, and only the first sound set apart.

#### 10 min. Letters n and c

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Chant: "n, n, /n/, nest; c, c, /k/, cow" (2 minutes). Review: go both directions on the sound-spelling map: sound to letter, letter to keyword and sound (day 19). Say /n/ and each child holds up a card from their set; say /k/ the same, and then /h/ and /p/; point to a letter on the map and the class says its sound and keyword (4 minutes). Then the large poem Our Play (4 minutes): children come up one at a time to touch an n or a c, lowercase or capital, in "Come and play", "and", "turn" or "can", and the class says the name. Naming only: no one reads the words.

#### 10 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print N, n, C and c on handwriting paper, saying the sound as you form each: N goes straight down, then a slant down, then straight up; n goes down, then up and over the hump; C and c start at the top right, go back and around, and end there (6 minutes). A child who prints only the capital when small is asked (error P) sees the lowercase path written once. Then mapped words (4 minutes). Review: mapped words the and a, read whole by sight (day 21). Write "the a" on chart paper; the class reads each. Then point under "the" in "Share the toy and share the day" and under "a" in "There's a toy for us today" in the large poem; you read the other words.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets about 40 seconds (4 minutes, one minute for the move): the name and sound of n and c, then the and a in a list. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger n and c in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /n/ and then /k/. Each child chooses the letter card first, then prints the letter on the card. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we read a poem, a story and an information book."

### Other rooms

One child: reread pages 3 and 4 from the booklets, point to the question, practise "Please read that again" and "What is ___?" with the puppet, then draw. Do the two exit cards aloud. Say onset and rime with a hand sweep. One projector: show one picture at a time and have children point on the screen; mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No puppet: use a soft toy. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. Reread.** Read "Can I have a turn?" from One Hoop, Two Friends.
  **Say.** "Which part is the question?" Expect: "Can I have a turn?"
- **5 min. Ask for help.** Read a line; your child says "Please read that again."
- **5 min. What is it?** Show a real toy; your child asks "What is this?"
- **5 min. Split a word.** Say "b - all"; your child sweeps it into ball.
- **5 min. n and c.** Say /n/ and /k/; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X24]] Read the two cards aloud. Your child circles one picture, then says a question.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the draw and the second pair turn of Ask for help; keep the reread, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure asks a partner "What is ___?" about a word in a poem, and the partner points to the line.

### Words and supports

Words: question, statement, gap. Frames: "Please read that again." "What is ___?" "This shows ___." A home language, a gesture or pointing counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many said "I know a ramp" (error E), day 25's Reopen models one question that gets the missing help, and children echo it. Day 25 revisits a poem, a story and an information book and asks who made the words and who made the pictures.
