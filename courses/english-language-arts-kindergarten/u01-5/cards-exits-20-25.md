# Exit cards, days 20 to 25

Each card is marked by every child and goes with a spoken question heard from the day's six. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Day 20 card: the parade

**Read:** "Everyone plays catch outside. Rue sits by the shelf with toy dinosaurs. Rue lines them up. 'The parade is ready!' Rue says, and smiles."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: children throwing a ball outdoors; a girl with toy dinosaurs in a line; a child with crayons at a table.

**Mark:** circle what Rue chooses to play. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Which words help you know how Rue feels?"

## Day 21 card: the truck

**Read:** "The toy truck slides off the shelf. Gus picks it up. He parks it by the window."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a truck sliding off a shelf; a boy setting the truck down by a window; a truck sitting on the shelf.

**Mark:** circle the picture for the last sentence. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Which words go with your picture?"

## Day 22 card: the peg board

**Read:** "The class sings a song together. Kai sits by the window with a peg board. 'I like pressing the pegs in,' says Kai."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: children singing in a group; a boy by a window with a peg board; a child standing at the door.

**Mark:** circle where Kai chooses to play. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Which words tell you why Kai picked it?"

## Day 23 card: the box

**Read:** "Ana sees an empty box. 'This box is no good,' she says. A kitten curls up inside. 'It can be a bed!' says Ana."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a girl looking at an empty box; a kitten asleep in the box; a girl walking away.

**Mark:** circle the picture where the story turns. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What changed in what Ana thought?"

## Day 24 cards: the ramp

**Read two cards:** "Mo sends a ball down a ramp." "Lee waits by the ramp for a turn."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: two children each rolling a ball; a boy rolling a ball down a ramp with a second child waiting beside it; a ball alone on the floor.

**Mark:** circle the one picture that fits both cards. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What could you ask if one word on these cards was new to you?"

## Day 25 cards: three kinds, a back cover and two makers

**Read three cards, each with a small picture:** "A toy train gets lost, then it comes back." (a train on a track, then a train beside a station) "Round goes the train, round once again." (a small train on a loop) "A toy train has wheels." (a close picture of train wheels)

**Mark:** circle the card that comes from an information book. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What kind of book is your card from?"

**Then show a small booklet, The Lost Train,** with its front cover, its title page and its last inside page, and its back cover turned over beside them. **Picture strip:** the last inside page; the outside back; the title page.

**Mark:** circle the back cover. **Spoken:** "How do you know it is the back cover?"

**Then read two credit labels from the same booklet:** "Words: Curio Class writing team." "Pictures: Curio Class art team." Each label has a small pencil or brush picture.

**Mark:** circle the label that says who made the pictures. **Spoken:** "What did the writing team make?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 20: the girl with the toy dinosaurs. Spoken: "ready" or "smiles". Not yet: the catch picture (B); "Rue is sad because she is alone" with no word from the card (A).
- Day 21: the boy setting the truck down by the window. Spoken: "parks it by the window". Not yet: the truck sliding (B); "Gus is crying" (A).
- Day 22: the boy with the peg board. Spoken: "I like pressing the pegs in". Not yet: the singing group (B); "Kai does not like the class" (A).
- Day 23: the kitten asleep in the box. Spoken: first the box was no good, now it is a bed. Not yet: the empty box (B); "Ana throws the bed away" (A).
- Day 24: the boy rolling a ball down the ramp with a second child waiting. Spoken: a question about a word, such as "What is a ramp?" or "What does sends mean?" Not yet: two children both rolling (B); "I know a ramp" (E).
- Day 25, card: the third card; an information book. Not yet: the second card, the short lines that go together (J).
- Day 25, back cover: the outside back; it is on the outside, on the other side from the front. Not yet: the last inside page (AC).
- Day 25, labels: the Pictures label; the writing team made the words. Not yet: the Words label for the pictures (I).

Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may point, act or use a home language; say the English back.
