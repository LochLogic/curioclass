# Exit cards, days 20 to 25

Each card or strip is marked by every child and goes with a spoken question heard from the day's six. Only the day 24 cards are made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice" and print "invented for practice" at their foot. The strips for days 20 to 23 and 25 go with pages of the class books, read aloud again. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Day 20 strip: Mira Makes a Road, page 8

**Read:** page 8 of Mira Makes a Road, aloud again.

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: Mira and Kiko with the toy lion on her road; Mira playing zoo with the group; Mira pushing the lion away.

**Mark:** circle what Mira does when Kiko comes. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Which words help you know how Kiko feels about the road?"

## Day 21 strip: Mira Makes a Road, page 3

**Read:** page 3 of Mira Makes a Road, aloud again.

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: Mira lying by her road, tongue poking out, thinking; the road going past the chair with no one there; Mira standing at the block shelf.

**Mark:** circle the picture for the last sentence. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Which words go with your picture?"

## Day 22 strip: Mira Makes a Road, page 9

**Read:** page 9 of Mira Makes a Road, aloud again.

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: Mira laying a new block at the end of her road; Mira with the zoo group; Mira at the door.

**Mark:** circle where Mira chooses to stay. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Which words tell you Mira still likes her road?"

## Day 23 strip: The Magic Bow, page 8

**Read:** page 8 of The Magic Bow, aloud again.

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a child stacking blocks while a friend points to help; the people with their heads down; the empty stool.

**Mark:** circle the picture that shows the people after the story turns. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What do the people do now that the bow is gone?"

## Day 24 cards: the ramp

**Read two cards:** "Mo sends a ball down a ramp." "Lee waits by the ramp for a turn."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: two children each rolling a ball; a boy rolling a ball down a ramp with a second child waiting beside it; a ball alone on the floor.

**Mark:** circle the one picture that fits both cards. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What could you ask if one word on these cards was new to you?"

## Day 25 strip: three books, a back cover and two makers

**Read, holding up each book:** page 5 of The Magic Bow; the first two lines of the poem Toy Clues; page 3 of Attributes of Toys. **Picture strip:** the three covers in a row, each in a ring.

**Mark:** circle the cover of the information book. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What kind of book is your circled one?"

**Then show Attributes of Toys** with its front cover, its title page and page 9, and its back cover turned over beside them. **Picture strip:** page 9; the outside back; the title page.

**Mark:** circle the back cover. **Spoken:** "How do you know it is the back cover?"

**Then read the two credit lines on its title page,** each beside a small pencil or brush picture.

**Mark:** circle the line that says who made the pictures. **Spoken:** "What did the writing team make?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 20: Mira and Kiko with the lion on the road. Spoken: "his mouth fell open" or "wow". Not yet: the zoo picture (B); "Kiko is sad" with no word from the page (A).
- Day 21: Mira with her tongue poking out. Spoken: "her tongue poked out" or "thinking hard". Not yet: the road with no one there (B); "Mira is crying" (A).
- Day 22: Mira laying a new block. Spoken: "she hummed her tune" or "began a new bit at the end". Not yet: the zoo group (B); "Mira does not like her friends" (A).
- Day 23: the child stacking blocks with a friend. Spoken: they keep trying, or they ask each other for help. Not yet: the people with heads down (B); "they get the bow back" (A).
- Day 24: the boy rolling a ball down the ramp with a second child waiting. Spoken: a question about a word, such as "What is a ramp?" or "What does sends mean?" Not yet: two children both rolling (B); "I know a ramp" (E).
- Day 25, books: Attributes of Toys; an information book. Not yet: the poem Toy Clues, the short lines that go together (J).
- Day 25, back cover: the outside back; it is on the outside, on the other side from the front. Not yet: page 9 (AC).
- Day 25, credit lines: the art team line; the writing team made the words. Not yet: the writing team line for the pictures (I).

Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may point, act or use a home language; say the English back.
