# The letter n and letter c cards (days 21 to 25)

Two large cards and one small copy for each pair. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading.

## The n card

**Picture:** a bird's nest with two eggs in it, large enough to fill most of the card. **Letters:** N and n, printed large, side by side. **Say:** the keyword is nest, the sound is /n/ as at the start of nest, a hum through the nose. **Mouth shape:** a face in side view with the tongue just behind the top teeth and a hum coming out.

## The c card

**Picture:** a brown cow standing in a field, large enough to fill most of the card. **Letters:** C and c, printed large, side by side. **Say:** the keyword is cow, the sound is /k/ as at the start of cow, a quick click at the back of the mouth. Today c means only this sound. **Mouth shape:** a face in side view with the back of the tongue lifted and a small puff.

## How the cards are used

Say the keyword, say the sound, point to the letter. Add both cards to the sound-spelling map on day 21. The pictures are reminders of the sounds; never ask a child to guess a word from them. The name of a letter and its sound are different: say "en" for the name and "nnn" for the sound. Practice only; the cycle check is the response on days 21 and 27.
