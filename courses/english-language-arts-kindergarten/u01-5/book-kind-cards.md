# Book-kind cards (days 20, 25 and 28)

Three large cards for the pocket chart and one small set for each pair. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading.

## The story card

**Picture:** an open picture book showing a girl kneeling beside a long road of blocks. **Say:** a story has people or animals who do things; something happens and then something changes. The class story is Mira Makes a Road.

## The poem card

**Picture:** an open page with five short lines of squiggles stacked one above the other and a small drum at the side. **Say:** a poem has short lines that go together; you can hear the words go along in beats. The class poem is My Choice.

## The information book card

**Picture:** an open book showing a ball and a cart, each with an arrow beside it. **Say:** an information book tells facts about one topic. The class book is Push, Pull, Roll.

## The parts card

**Picture:** one book shown three ways, side by side, with an arrow to each: the front cover, the title page and the outside back. **Say:** front cover, title page, back cover.

## How the cards are used

Hold up the book, say its kind and place it beside the matching card. Then ask: "Who made the words? Who made the pictures?" The author made the words and the illustrator made the pictures. The cards are for practice and never carry an exit or end item's own words. A child may point, act or use a home language; say the English back.
