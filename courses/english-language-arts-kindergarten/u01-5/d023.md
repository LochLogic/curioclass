# Day 23: The Magic Bow

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 22's exit and choose the one error most children made. Hang the poem My Choice where the rug can see it. Put the four page pictures in a basket for each pair. Read The Magic Bow once yourself, aloud. Today's six are the twenty-third six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Reread page 9 of Mira Makes a Road, from the day 22 strip. If many said Mira does not like her friends (error A, an idea that is not in the text), reread the page and put a finger under the words "She hummed her tune"; every child points under the same words, and you say: "Mira likes her road." If many circled the zoo group (error B, a picture of a different moment), set the zoo picture beside the sentence "Mira stayed by her road" and point to the one the sentence names. If only a few erred, take them as a small group during Draw and say.

#### 4 min. Words first

Whole class. Use real things. Borrow: hold out a crayon and say "May I borrow it?"; a child lends it, you use it and give it back. Try: a child lifts a block and tries again, with a smile each time. Believe: a hand on the chest, "I believe I can." Say each word, the class says it and does the gesture. These are the words in the story.

#### 8 min. First hearing

Whole class on the rug. Say: "We made this story for our class. The woman and her friends are pretend." Read The Magic Bow once, whole and without a pause (about 3 minutes), holding up each picture. Do not ask questions yet. Then the class shows with open hands what the woman does at the end. Content note: the bow stays in words and pictures only; no one acts it or aims it. "Someone took it" is said once and plainly; the picture shows an empty stool. Say: "The people were sad. The woman helped them." Say the credit aloud: "The words are by Curio Class. The pictures are by the Curio Class art team."

#### 15 min. Turn and talk and join

Whole class, then pairs. Reread in two pieces: pages 1 to 4, then pages 5 to 8 (about 4 minutes). After page 7, read the poem's first two lines slowly, finger under each word: "Choose the thing you want to learn. / Believe in you, and take your turn." The class joins twice (2 minutes), so we remember what the woman said. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass (day 1). Model with the puppet, one line at a time (2 minutes):

- Puppet: "What did people first believe about the bow?" You: "They believed it made them learn." Puppet: "Tell me more." You: "Anyone who held it learned something."

Review: "Tell me more" keeps a talk going (day 22). Pairs then talk, 4 minutes each way, with the turn card passed. Ask 1: "What did people first believe about the bow?" Ask 2: "What did the woman explain?" Expect: they believed the bow made them learn; the woman said they learned because they tried. The partner says "Tell me more." Walk to the day's six, three pairs, a minute each, and listen for words from the page. A child may pass; the puppet says "I will ask you later." Last, one minute: two children tell the class one thing they heard, "I heard ___."

#### 8 min. Join the information reading

Whole class. Review: Push, Pull, Roll, which you heard on days 9, 15, 16, 18 and 21. Show page 5 with its two small pictures and read it aloud once, finger under the words (2 minutes). Review: a picture shows one moment (day 4); here two pictures show a push and a pull. Reread, and the class points under push and then under pull each time you say them (3 minutes). Review: question and statement: a question asks for help; a statement tells (day 1).

**Ask.** Which word tells about a toy going away from you? Expect: push; children point to the child pushing the cart. Then ask, "Which word tells about a toy coming toward you?" Expect: pull. All point. Three children say how they know ("The cart goes away from her.").

#### 10 min. Point back to the turn

Pairs on the rug, each pair with four page pictures from The Magic Bow (pages 3, 4, 5 and 6) and a turn card. Review: picture and moment: a picture shows one moment (day 4), and "This shows ..." (day 4). Say: "Find the picture where the story changes. Point to the words that go with it." Read one line from each page (4 minutes). Pairs lay the cards in order, then point to the picture where the people were happy, and then to the one where they were sad. The card holder passes the turn card to start. Swap (4 minutes). Walk to the day's six, a minute a pair. Then, as a class (2 minutes), ask which picture shows the change; children hold up the page 4 picture, the empty stool. A child who points to the page 3 picture as the turn (error B, a picture of a different moment) hears the line again with the two pictures side by side.

#### 5 min. Draw and say

Alone, then pairs. Each child draws something in our room they could get better at by trying (3 minutes), then says it to a partner (2 minutes): "I can try ___." Say: "Trying helps. Asking for help is allowed." A child may point and not tell. Look for: a drawn thing in the room; the word try said or pointed to.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Read page 8 of The Magic Bow aloud again; today's pair work pointed back to pages 3 to 6. Hand out the day 23 strip ([[s01-X23v2]]); each child circles the picture that shows the people after the story turns (3 minutes). Hear the day's six, 30 seconds each: "What do the people do now that the bow is gone?" Reread the page if a child asks.

Signs: the people with their heads down circled (error B, a picture of a different moment); "they get the bow back" (error A, an idea that is not in the text).

#### 10 min. Response and fresh retry

Whole class, then small groups. Review: repair then fresh retry: wrong example beside a right one, then a new try (day 6). The evidence is the day 21 and day 22 foundations work; use the most common slip (4 minutes). The puppet shows one:

- Error N: the puppet says "en" for /n/ when the sound is asked. Say the name, then the sound with the letter and the keyword nest.
- Error O: the puppet prints n when it hears /k/. Hear the sound, choose the letter card first, then print.
- Error P: the puppet prints only N when lowercase is asked. Model the lowercase path; each child prints it once.

Then a fresh retry of that one component only (6 minutes), not scored: children who missed it form a group on the rug and work with the cards while you move between groups. Look for: the right name, the right sound, starts at the top, closes the hump.

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say two words and the class says if they end the same: bow and go (yes); bow and bed (no). A child who says bow and bed rhyme because they start alike (error Q) hears the endings stretched: bow, go; bed, red. Each child tells a neighbor a word that ends like go (low, dough). Then syllables (4 minutes). Review: clap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first (day 18): borrow (two), magic (two), believe (two), amazing (three). Say the word, then clap. A child who counts every sound (error R) hears the whole word and its beats.

#### 10 min. Reread the shared poem

Whole class. Review: the poem My Choice, which you read on days 16, 20 and 22. Show the large poem. Point under each word, left to right, line by line, and read it aloud (3 minutes). Say: "Between each word there is a space." Put a finger in a space and move to the next. Then the class reads with you, and children come up one at a time to point under a word and a space (5 minutes). Last, the class finds the line "Some like the cart that rolls down the hall" and counts the words with fingers (2 minutes). You read each word; no child decodes.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets about 40 seconds (4 minutes, one minute for the move): the name and sound of n and c, then the and a in a list. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger n and c in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /n/ and then /k/. Each child chooses the letter card first, then prints one capital and one small letter on the card from memory. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we read two play stories again."

### Other rooms

One child: read The Magic Bow from the booklet, ask the two questions and point back together, find the turn in four pictures, then draw. Do the exit strip aloud. One projector: show one page at a time and have children point on the screen; mark on printed cards. A room with no sand tray prints with a finger on a table.

### At home

**Swap.** No page pictures: use the story's pictures. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. The story.** Read The Magic Bow aloud, pictures shown.
  **Say.** "What did people first believe about the bow?" Expect: it made them learn.
- **5 min. The woman explains.** Read page 6; your child says what the woman explained.
- **5 min. Push and pull.** Read page 5 of Push, Pull, Roll; your child points under push and pull.
- **5 min. Try.** Your child draws one thing to get better at by trying.
- **5 min. n and c.** Say /n/ and /k/; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X23v2]] Read page 8 of The Magic Bow aloud. Your child circles one picture, then says what the people do now.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the information reading and the second pair question; keep the story, the turn, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure says what they would tell a friend who gave up, with a word from the story.

### Words and supports

Words: borrow, try, believe. Frames: "I heard ___." "This shows ___." "Tell me more." "I can try ___." A home language, pointing, or acting out trying counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many said the people get the bow back (error A), day 24's Reopen rereads page 8 of The Magic Bow and points to what the people do. Day 24 stays with the two play stories and asks how to get help with a word.
