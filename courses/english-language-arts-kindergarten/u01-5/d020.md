# Day 20: A girl who plays her own way

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the page cards in a basket for each pair. Hang the three kind cards and the parts card where the rug can see them. Put a classroom toy beside you for each of road, block and choose. Read Mira Makes a Road once yourself, aloud. Today's six are the twentieth six names on the record sheet; the cycle 2 check happens at the teacher table in the rotation.

### The day

#### 4 min. Words first

Whole class. Show each word with a real toy and a gesture: road (run a finger along the floor, "a path for cars to go"), block (stack two), choose (hold two toys, pick one). The class says each word and does the gesture. Review: choose, which you used on day 15 with favorite and prefer. The feeling words come on day 22.

#### 9 min. First hearing

Whole class on the rug. Say: "We made this story for our class. Mira and her friends are pretend." Say the credit aloud: "The words are by Curio Class. The pictures are by the Curio Class art team." Read Mira Makes a Road once, whole and without stopping (about 4 minutes), holding up each picture. Do not ask questions yet. Then ask the class to show the road with their arms: one long, bending line. Content note: children play and one child chooses to play alone; no one is unkind.

#### 10 min. Return and ask

Whole class, then pairs. Review: question and statement: a question asks for help; a statement tells (day 1). Review: Read, Point, Talk, Draw: hear a text, point back, talk, draw one thing and tell a partner (day 2). Model three questions, each with its answer, pointing to the page as you answer (4 minutes):

- "What does Mira make?" Point to page 2: "I am making a road."
- "Who asks her to play zoo?" Point to page 4: Kiko calls, "Come and play zoo!"
- "How does she feel at the end?" Point to page 7: she smiles and says "That is just right." Those words show how she feels.

The class echoes each question (2 minutes). Then each child asks a partner one of the three, and the partner answers and points; a child may pass (review: turn and talk, day 1; 2 minutes, the turn card passed). Last, one question on Push, Pull, Roll (2 minutes). Show page 3 and ask:

**Ask.** What is a pull? Expect: a pull brings a toy toward you; children point to the duck coming toward the girl. This begins the information questions.

#### 8 min. Pictures and words

Whole class, then pairs. Review: picture and moment: a picture shows one moment (day 4), and "This shows ..." (day 4). Model with two pages (3 minutes). Hold up page 5 and read: "Mira held a block in the air and watched the zoo for a little while." Say: "This shows the sentence: Mira holds a block and looks at the zoo." Do page 7 the same: "Mira sat back and smiled at her road."

Then pairs (4 minutes). Each pair has the page cards for pages 3 and 6. You read one sentence: "The road went past the reading chair." One child points to the card; the partner says "This shows ...". Swap, with "The road bent around the corner." Last, one minute with the class: hold up two cards and ask which one fits; children point. A child who points to a picture of a different moment (error B) hears the sentence again, one line at a time, with the two pictures side by side.

#### 10 min. Book kinds, parts and makers

Whole class. Everything here is review. Review: a poem, a story and an information book are three kinds of text (day 9). Place the three kind cards on the chart (3 minutes). Hold up Mira Makes a Road and say "story"; hold up the poem My Choice and say "poem"; hold up Push, Pull, Roll and say "information book". Ask the class to point to the card for each.

**Ask.** What is the same about the story and the information book, and what is different? Expect: both are books you hear; the story has someone who does things, the information book tells facts about toys.

Review: the parts of a book: front cover, back cover and title page (day 2). Show Mira's front cover, then its title page, then turn it over to the back cover; the class points to each (3 minutes). Review: author (makes the words) and illustrator (makes the pictures) (day 2). Read the two credit lines on the title page of Mira and then of Push, Pull, Roll: the words are by Curio Class and the pictures are by the Curio Class art team. The class says "words" and "pictures" with their hands: one writing, one painting (4 minutes).

#### 14 min. Draw and talk

Alone, then pairs. Each child draws one thing Mira did, on the student page (8 minutes). Then each child tells a partner what they drew and shows the page card that is nearest, or says the words that go with the picture: "This shows Mira ___" (3 minutes each way, 6 minutes in all). Walk and listen to three pairs, two minutes each. A child may point and not tell. Look for: says or points to a thing Mira did; names the page card or the words.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Read page 8 of Mira Makes a Road aloud again; today's questions used other pages. Hand out the day 20 strip ([[s01-X20v2]]). Each child circles what Mira does when Kiko comes (3 minutes). Then hear the day's six, 30 seconds each: "Ask me a question about what the words on this page show about Mira." Reread the page if a child asks.

Signs: the zoo picture circled (error B, a picture of a different moment); "Mira is nice," a statement and not a question (error E).

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Play Rhyme Time with pictures: say a word, show the picture, and the class says the rhyming word: ball, hall; dough, go; star, car. Then the class jumps on the rhyming word in the first two lines of the poem My Choice, as you say them ("ball", "hall"). Each child tells a neighbor one more word that ends like ball. A child who offers a word that only starts the same (ball, bed; error Q) hears the endings stretched: ball, hall.

Then syllables (4 minutes). Review: clap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first (day 18). Clap blocks (one), cart (one), scooter (two), balloon (two). Say the whole word, then clap. Do not count sounds (error R).

#### 10 min. Letters h and p

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Review: letter h: name aitch, sound /h/, keyword hat; letter p: name pee, sound /p/, keyword parrot (day 14). Chant: "h, h, /h/, hat; p, p, /p/, parrot" (2 minutes). Hold up the letter cards H, h, P, p one at a time and the class says each name. Review: go both directions on the sound-spelling map (day 19). Say /p/ and each child holds up a card from their set of a, t, h and p; say /h/ the same, then /a/ and /t/ (4 minutes). Then point to a letter on the map and the class says its sound and keyword (4 minutes). Hold the h and p cards side by side when a child mixes them up (error AA).

#### 10 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print H, h, P and p on handwriting paper with a thick pencil, saying the sound as you form each: start at the top, a straight line down; for the hump or the bump, close it (6 minutes). Then mapped words (4 minutes). Review: mapped word I, read whole (day 3), and mapped word me, read whole (day 14). Point under "I ask, you tell" and "Eyes on you and ears on me" in the large poem Ask and Listen. The class reads I and me by sight; you read the other words. Say: "We do not sound these out. We know them by sight."

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes today. Five groups of six. The cycle 2 check is the teacher-table turn: each child is heard about 40 seconds (6 children, 4 minutes, one minute for the move): name H, h, P and p, then say the sound of h and of p. Mark names and sounds each as shown, shown with help or not yet seen, notes only and no reteaching today. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger h and p in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /h/ and then /p/. Each child picks the letter card first, then prints the letter on the card for dictated sounds. Collect the cards. Say: "I will look at your cards. Tomorrow we try again."

### Other rooms

One child: read the story from the booklet, ask the three questions, point back together, then draw. Do the exit strip aloud and have the child circle and say. Show the three kinds with real books and the h and p cards. One projector: show one page at a time and have children point on the screen and mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No page cards: use the story's pictures. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The partner turns, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. The story.** Read Mira Makes a Road aloud, pictures shown.
  **Say.** "What does Mira make? Which words tell you?" Expect: a road; "I am making a road."
- **5 min. Show and match.** Read "The road bent around the corner" and your child points to the picture.
- **5 min. Three kinds.** Show a story, a poem and a fact book; your child says each kind.
- **5 min. Who made it?** Point to the credit; your child says words and pictures.
- **5 min. h and p.** Say each sound; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X20v2]] Read page 8 of Mira Makes a Road aloud. Your child circles one picture, then asks a question about what the words show about Mira.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the book kinds and the rhyme game; keep the story, the three questions, the exit and the cycle check. Longer: a child who is secure asks the partner a question the story answers on a page you name.

### Words and supports

Words: road, block, choose, author, illustrator. Frames: "This shows ___." "I heard ___." "Mira makes a ___." A home language, pointing, or acting out the road counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many marked the zoo picture (error B), day 21's Reopen puts both pictures beside the reread page 8. If many said Kiko was sad with no word from the page (error A), it rereads page 8 and points to his mouth fell open and wow. Day 21 returns to a hard moment in Mira Makes a Road and matches a picture to what Mira does. The cycle 2 check notes decide the first ten minutes of day 21's foundations block.
