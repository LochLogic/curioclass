# Day 22: Why is Mira content?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 21's exit. Lay a six-card set on each child's place. Hang the rule card, a hare and a snail, on the wall. Mark a clear space for movement and move anything with sharp edges aside. Today's six are the twenty-second six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Say: "This card was made up for practice." Reread the day 21 card, the truck. If many circled the truck sliding off the shelf on day 21's exit (error B, a picture of a different moment), set the picture of the truck sliding beside the picture of Gus parking it, reread "He parks it by the window", and every child points to the picture that goes with the last sentence. If many said Gus is crying (error A, an idea that is not in the text), reread the card and point to the words that tell what Gus does.

#### 8 min. Words with actions

Whole class. Teach content with a calm smile and still hands: "Content means happy and calm, with nothing more to want." Teach invite with an open hand held out: "To invite is to ask someone to come and join." Say each word, the class says it and does the gesture. Then march and tiptoe. Review: walk, march, tiptoe: three ways of stepping, acted in place (firm and even, or light and quiet) (day 13). The class shows each with two fingers on the rug, seated; then show a plain walk beside them, so the three are side by side. A child who shows march and tiptoe the same way (error W) sees firm, even steps next to light, quiet ones.

#### 12 min. Why is Mira happy?

Whole class on the rug. Reread page 4, page 7 and page 8 of Mira Makes a Road, holding up each picture (about 3 minutes). Review: a feeling shown by words: a word in the text shows how a character feels (day 20).

**Ask.** What does Mira say she likes? Expect: "I like making it." Point under the words on page 4 and say them with the class.

**Ask.** Which words show she is content? Expect: she smiled at her road; she said "That is just right"; she folded her hands. Model pointing back: put a finger under the words on page 7, then touch the picture of the calm smile.

**Ask.** What does Mira say when Kiko likes her road? Expect: "You can walk on it. I invite you." Point to the open hand in the picture.

A child who calls the noisy zoo content (error V, a feeling word given to something that does not fit) is shown the calm picture on page 7 beside the busy zoo picture on page 1 and asked which one is calm.

#### 13 min. Turn and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass (day 1). Show two exchanges with the puppet, one line at a time (3 minutes):

- Puppet: "Why is Mira content?" You: "She smiles. She says 'That is just right.'" Puppet: "Tell me more." You: "She likes making it."
- Read one line from page 7. Puppet: "Please read that again." You reread it. Puppet: "What is content?" You: "Happy and calm."

Say: "'Tell me more' keeps a talk going. 'Please read that again' and 'What is content?' ask for help. They are questions."

Pairs then talk, 4 minutes each way, with the turn card passed: one asks "Why is Mira content?", the partner answers and points to the words, and the asker says "Tell me more." Walk to the day's six, three pairs, a minute each, and listen for words from the page. A child may pass; the puppet says "I will ask you later." Last, two minutes: three children tell the class one thing they heard, "I heard ___."

#### 10 min. Word play

Whole class, then alone. Review: sort real objects by one attribute, with a rule card above each group (day 11). Each child sorts six play-picture cards into fast play and slow play, under the rule card (4 minutes). A child who sorts by colour or by who is in the picture (error T) sees the rule card kept above the groups and sorts again. Then show fast and slow as opposites with two fingers, one moving quickly and one moving slowly (3 minutes). Review: opposites: two words that mean far apart (day 12). Then ask: "Where in our room do we move slowly?" Children point: by the shelf, in the line, at the sink (3 minutes). A child who gives "gentle" as the opposite of fast (error U) sees fast and slow acted side by side with the fingers. Never ask for a stronger word for slow.

#### 7 min. Act it out

Three groups of ten in the clear space, two minutes a group (6 minutes, one for the move). Say: "Mira's lion walked, then marched, then tiptoed." Each group walks, marches, then tiptoes at your word, with a path kept clear; the other children finger-walk on the rug. A child who prefers to stay seated finger-walks; it is the same evidence. Pause the group at any crowding. The marchers show firm, even steps; the tiptoers show light, quiet steps.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the day 22 card, the peg board ([[s01-X22]]). Read it aloud once, then each child circles where Kai chooses to play (3 minutes). Hear the day's six, 30 seconds each, on the spoken question. Reread the card if a child asks.

Signs: the singing group circled (error B, a picture of a different moment); "Kai does not like the class" (error A, an idea that is not in the text).

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say two words and the class says if they end the same: roll and bowl (yes); roll and bed (no, they only share a feeling of play). A child who says ball and bed rhyme because they start alike (error Q) hears the endings stretched: ball, hall; bed, red. Each child tells a neighbor a word that ends like car (star, jar). Then syllables (4 minutes). Review: clap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first (day 18): scooter (two), tricycle (three), drum (one), trumpet (two). Say the word, then clap. A child who counts every sound (error R) hears the whole word and its beats.

#### 10 min. Letters n and c

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Chant: "n, n, /n/, nest; c, c, /k/, cow" (2 minutes). Then go both directions (4 minutes). Review: go both directions on the sound-spelling map: sound to letter, letter to keyword and sound (day 19). Say /k/ and each child holds up a card from their set; point to a letter on the map and the class says its sound and keyword. Then the large poem My Choice (4 minutes): children come up one at a time to touch an n or a c, lowercase or capital, and the class says the name. Naming only: the sound /k/ is for the c in "cart". The c in "choose" has an h beside it, so it is a different team that comes later; touch it and name it, and say no more.

#### 10 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print C and c on handwriting paper, saying /k/: start at the top right, go back and around, and end there (6 minutes). A child who prints only the capital when small is asked (error P) sees the lowercase path written once. Then mapped words (4 minutes). Review: mapped words the and a, read whole by sight (day 21). Write "the a the a" on chart paper and point; the class reads each. Then point under "the" in "Some like the cart" and under "a" in "We each have a favorite" in the large poem; you read the other words.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets about 40 seconds (4 minutes, one minute for the move): the name and sound of c and n, then the and a in a list. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger n and c in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /k/. Each child chooses the c card first, then prints c on the card. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we hear a new story."

### Other rooms

One child: reread pages 4, 7 and 8 from the booklet, ask the three questions and point back, then act march and tiptoe with fingers. Do the six cards and the exit card aloud. One projector: show one page at a time, and have children point and mark on printed cards. Safe paths matter in a small room: finger-walk seated.

### At home

**Swap.** No play pictures: use two real toys, one that moves fast and one slowly. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the marching group, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. Why content?** Reread page 7 of Mira Makes a Road.
  **Say.** "Which words show Mira is content?" Expect: she smiled; "That is just right."
- **5 min. Tell me more.** Ask "Tell me more." Your child adds one thing.
- **5 min. Fast and slow.** Show a fast toy and a slow toy; your child says fast and slow with fingers.
- **5 min. March and tiptoe.** Your child shows each with fingers.
- **5 min. n and c.** Say /n/ and /k/; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X22]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles one picture, then says the words.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the act-it-out and the second question of Word play; keep the three questions, the turn and talk, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure names a place in the room where we move slowly, and why.

### Words and supports

Words: content, invite, march, tiptoe, fast, slow. Frames: "I heard ___." "Tell me more." "Please read that again." "What is ___?" A home language, a gesture or a finger-walk counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many said Kai does not like the class (error A), day 23's Reopen rereads the card and points to the words I like. Day 23 hears a different story, The Magic Bow, whole, and asks what changed in what people thought.
