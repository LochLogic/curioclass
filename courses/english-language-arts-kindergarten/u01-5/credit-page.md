# Class book credit page (day 25, again on days 28 and 32 to 34)

The class makes a book, Our Classroom Toys, across the unit. This is its credit page. One large copy, plus a small label set for each child.

## The page

- **Top:** the title Our Classroom Toys, printed large by the teacher.
- **Middle:** two label boxes, one with a pencil picture and one with a brush picture. Under each box, a line for a name.
- **Foot:** the words "made by our class", printed by the teacher.

## The labels

Two sets of small cards, each a pencil or a brush picture with a place to print one name. Children place the pencil card in the first box and the brush card in the second. The pencil means the person who made the words. The brush means the person who made the pictures.

## How the page is used

On day 25 the class places the labels together and the teacher prints the title. On day 28 the class adds portrait labels and, on days 32 to 34, more pages. No child drafts writing for this page. For a book the class made, the child may be both the words-maker and the pictures-maker; say both aloud.
