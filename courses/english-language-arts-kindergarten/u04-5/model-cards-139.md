# Model cards, day 139

The teacher shows a wrong answer beside a right one, on new cases. A puppet says the wrong one. Nothing here is an exit or end card.

- Place and liking: puppet says "A bench." Teacher says "A bench. I like it by the fence."
- A reason and a liking: card, "Put the sign by the door. Then visitors can find us." Puppet says "I like the sign." Teacher says "Then visitors can find us."
- Two texts on one topic: Card 1, "A bean grows up a pole." Card 2, "A bean grows in a pot." Puppet says "Both are the same." Teacher says "Card 1 says where it climbs; card 2 says where it grows."

Children say which answer says the place and the liking, which gives the writer's reason, and one difference between the cards.

These cards are for teaching and may be shown.
