# Section 4.5 opener: a planting place and the reason a writer gives

## What the section does

Days 137 to 139 ask each child for a planting-place opinion with a topic box, a preference box and a drawing. The class rereads the reasons text with a purpose card, and on day 138 each child is heard once on the opinion and once on a reason the writer states. Day 139 answers what day 138 showed, then compares the reasons text with the planting text. Foundations finish the doubled final letters ll, zz, ss and ff, and on day 139 return to letter names, print direction, rhyme and syllables.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and a five-minute exit (days 137 and 139) or the observation windows (day 138). The foundations block holds sounds, letters, the reader and the rotation. A child outside the six heard is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read the cards; the teacher reads every card aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **An opinion names a place and a liking.** "A pot" names a place. "I would put our tree by the window. I like it there" adds the liking.
- **A reason is a sentence the writer states.** In the reasons text it follows the word because or then. A child's own liking is said as their own and never as the writer's.
- **Two texts on one topic need not say the same thing.** One tells why; the other tells what to do.

## The errors, in plain words

- **F, G:** a place with no liking, or a liking with no place.
- **C, B, Y:** a liking given as the writer's reason, a point given as a reason, or a sentence of the other text.
- **E:** two texts on one topic taken to say the same thing.
- **AF:** watching the reread and not taking part.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion; there is no yard, only the class pot or the school grounds.

## Supplies

The reasons text and the planting text in print, three purpose cards, the plan sheet, the exit cards, the unseen card for day 138, five sentence cards, the box and arrows mat, letter tiles and cards, counters, whiteboards, the observation sheet and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Read from print. Draw the cards on scrap paper with plain pictures, and say each exit card is made up for practice. Point and speak in place of the mat and arrows.
