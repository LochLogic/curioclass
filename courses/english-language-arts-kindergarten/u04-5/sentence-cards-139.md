# Sentence cards, day 139

Five picture-and-sentence cards from the two information texts, read aloud by the teacher, for a pair to sort into a reason pile and a step pile.

- Reason pile: "Trees give us shaded places to rest." (Trees Help Our Community, page three)
- Reason pile: "Trees give birds shelter." (Trees Help Our Community, page five)
- Step pile: "Next, an adult digs the hole." (Planting Together, page three)
- Step pile: "After that, everyone fills the hole with soil, a little at a time." (Planting Together, page five)
- Step pile: "Next, children carry water in watering cans." (Planting Together, page six)

Each card has one plain picture and the sentence in large print for the teacher. Pairs then hang each reason on an arrow of the box and arrows mat, tied to its page, and say one sentence back.

These cards are for teaching and may be shown. They do not repeat any exit or end card.
