# Plan sheet, days 137 and 138

One sheet for each child, in three parts: a topic box at the top left, a preference box at the top right and a large space below for one drawing. The teacher writes the child's day-137 plan in the margin when the child dictates it.

- Topic box: the place the child chooses, drawn small, with a label if the child makes one.
- Preference box: what the child likes about it, drawn or dictated ("I like it by the window").
- A reason is welcome, not required. It is marked "the writer says" when it comes from the reasons text and "my reason" when it is the child's own.

After class the teacher writes one line of feedback in the margin. On day 138 each child hears the note, changes the plan and keeps the first version beside the revision.

A room with nothing: fold a sheet of paper into three parts and draw the boxes by hand.
