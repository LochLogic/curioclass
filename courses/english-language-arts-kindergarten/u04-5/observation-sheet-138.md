# Observation sheet, day 138 (O1)

Kept in the teacher folder. Roster numbers 1 to 30 down the left, six tables of five, and two parts recorded apart for each child:

- W.K.1: the child's revised planting opinion read as made. Look for a named place, a preference and a drawing of the same place. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help, after one prompt, "Which place?") or 0 (not yet seen).
- RI.K.8: the unseen card. Look for the stated reason. Mark 2, 1 or 0.

A third column names the prompt used. A child may pass; leave the cells blank and try another day. Nothing composed today after the observation window is scored.

A room with nothing: rule the columns by hand on a sheet.
