# Day 137: Plan a place for our tree

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record for day 136: list who made error V or error AE on the exit. Pick the day's six, none heard on day 136. Write the two Words cards and the model card from the parts below. Keep E137 face down. Copy the bench and bird purpose cards once each. Keep the reserved pages 3 and 9 of Reading Under the Tree closed.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Whole class. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child. Hand-back from day 136's exit, one minute each.

- Error V, 1 minute. Card: "Mia waters the young tree by the door." The puppet says, "Mia waters it at night." Say: "The card does not say night. We leave it out." Children say one thing the card does say: Mia waters the young tree.
- Error AE, 1 minute. Card: "Rain falls on the pot. The soil in the pot is wet." The puppet says, "The wet soil makes the rain fall." Say: "No. First the rain falls. Then the soil is wet. The arrow goes from the action to its result." Children say it together: "First the rain falls. Then the soil is wet."
- Words, 3 minutes. Review: shade, the cool dark place under a tree (day 129), and helper (day 132). New today: place, where something goes, and reach, to stretch out and touch. Show the picture and make the gesture for each. Fifteen pairs say each word in a sentence, about 20 seconds a word; you model the first two. Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Reread with a purpose card

Whole class, then five groups of six, then whole class. Review: Trees Help Our Community (days 129 and 130) and the purpose card (day 134). 4 min: reread pages 3 to 5 aloud, pointing to each picture. 6 min: each group holds one purpose card (a bench, a bird or a resting child; two cards are used twice), finds the sentence that answers it, points to it on the page and says it back, about 1 minute a group. Expect: the bench is page 4, "This is why a bench under a tree is a good place to rest"; the bird is page 5, "A bird rests on a branch"; the resting child is page 3, "A cool, shady place helps them rest." Say the word shelter once on purpose. Review: ask what a word means (day 123). A child asks "What does shelter mean?" and you answer from page 5: a place that keeps birds safe from wind and rain. A group that finds no sentence hears the page once more and tries again (error AF); the others watch and listen. 5 min: whole class, pages 5 and 6 of Reading Under the Tree, read aloud once. Purpose card: "Where does Ben go?" Children point to the picture, then say "behind the tree."

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then six children. 6 min, three turns of 2 minutes, all pairs at once, partners take turns. **Ask.** "Which reason in the page helps you choose a place?" Expect: shade, "Trees give us shaded places to rest" (page 3). **Ask.** "Where could our class pot go: by the window or by the door?" **Ask.** "What do you like about the place you picked?" 3 min: you model one share: "I would put our tree by the door. I like it where we pass every day." Say the frame once, "I would put our tree ___. I like it ___." 3 min: six children share, 30 seconds each. Hold the last 3 minutes for any child who needs a second try or a pass. The class pot or a place in the room is the only choice; there is no yard.

#### 20 min. Plan and draft the opinion

Whole class, then individuals. Review: opinion and prefer (day 133); the box with the topic and a preference (unit 1). 5 min: on chart paper show the plan sheet: a topic box, a preference box, a drawing space. Model with a new place, the library shelf: draw a shelf in the topic box, then say "I would put our tree by the library shelf. I like it near the books." Draw a small book in the preference box. The puppet says, "A shelf." Say: "That names a place. It does not say what you like." That is error F. The puppet says, "I like it." Say: "I like what? Name the place first." That is error G. Say it right together. 12 min: each child draws a chosen place in the topic box and a liking in the preference box on side 1. You take dictation from about eight children, 90 seconds each, and write it in the margin; the rest label or tell a partner. A reason is welcome, not required: label it "the writer says" when it comes from the page and "my reason" when it is the child's own (day 133). 3 min: three children show. Collect every sheet; you write one line of feedback on each after class, about 30 minutes, to hand back tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E137 twice and show its two pictures, then ask [[s04-X137]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others add a second detail to their drawing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: a place with no liking (error F); a liking with no place (error G).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 22, lesson 113: double final ll, zz, ss, ff)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. No new pattern. 3 min: Review: doubled final letters, two letters for one sound (day 135). Build bell, buzz, mess and puff with the tiles and say each. 6 min: sort the word cards hill, doll, jazz, fuzz, miss, moss, huff and cuff by final sound, ll, zz, ss or ff, and blend each word; pairs do it, then read the sorted piles. 4 min: with counters, pairs say the first, middle and last sound of huff, miss and jazz. 5 min: Review: changing one letter on tiles and naming the sound that changed (day 135). Change bell to bill, miss to moss and huff to puff, naming it each time, for example "the middle sound, e to i." 2 min: Review: but, not and no (day 135). Read each in a sentence before alone: "It is a mess, but it is not bad."

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: Moss on a Log (days 135 and 136). Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six; tasks 7 and 8 are left out today. Teacher table, about 50 seconds a child: find the event that answers the purpose card, then choose which of two event descriptions matches the hard sentence (tasks 1 and 2). Station 2, task 3: match a sentence to a picture and say why. Station 3, task 4: copy the sentence that answers a new question, with spaces and letter shapes. Station 4, tasks 5 and 6: Tree Care Words, verse 4, heard on day 135; find the line that gives the order and dictate one sentence. Station 5: partner reread. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. On chart paper write together "Ben has a rip in his cuff." Say it, count the words, leave a space between words, and children point left to right as it is read. Two children each add a word with a doubled final from the sorted cards to the class chart.

#### 5 min. Check

Whiteboards. Say tell, then fuss; each child writes both words. The six of the foundations rotation, not the six of the exit, are marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread pages 3 to 5, take the purpose card, draw the plan sheet together and take E137 alone. One projector: show one picture at a time; draw the three parts of the plan sheet on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No plan sheet: fold a paper in two parts and draw a box in each. No tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the marks.

- **10 min. Read.** Read pages 3 to 5 of Trees Help Our Community aloud.
  **Say.** "Which sentence says why a bench under a tree is a good place?" Expect: it is a shady place to rest.
- **10 min. Plan.** Fold a paper in two. Your child draws a place for a pot, then what they like about it.
  **Say.** "Where would you put our tree, and what do you like about it?" Expect: a place and a liking.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say puff. Your child pushes a button for each sound and changes the first sound to make cuff.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X137]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the plan and draft, the exit and the sound change; cut the Moss on a Log rotation to the teacher table. Longer: a secure child adds a reason from page 3 or 5 and says "the writer says" before it.

### Words and supports

Words: place, reach, shade, helper, plan. Frame: "I would put our tree ___. I like it ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can name a place and a liking. Day 138 hands back each plan with its one-line note; its Revise part takes errors F and G with a new place, then each child is heard once on the plan and once on a reason an author states. Children who watched the reread and pointed to nothing (error AF) carry a new purpose card in free reading.
