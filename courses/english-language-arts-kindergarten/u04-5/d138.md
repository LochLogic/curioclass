# Day 138: Check, a planting opinion and a writer's reason

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the observation sheet. Keep the unseen card covered. Choose the next six of the foundations rotation. Put out familiar picture books.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: place and reach (day 137), shade (day 129), helper (day 132). Show a picture and gesture for each, and for water, to pour water on a plant. No test today.

#### 10 min. Reread Trees Help Our Community

Whole class, pictures shown. Review: Trees Help Our Community (day 137). Read it whole, about 280 words, 3 minutes. Then pairs point to a picture and say what it shows, 20 seconds each. No new questions.

#### 10 min. Revise the day-137 plan

Hand-back from day 137's exit, errors F and G, 2 minutes each, with new places. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Error F: the puppet says, "The sink." Say: "That names a place. What do you like?" Children say, "I would put our tree by the sink. I like it near the water." Error G: the puppet says, "I like it." Say: "Point to the place first, then say it." Then hand each child their sheet and read the note aloud as you pass, about 10 seconds a child. Each child changes the plan: names the place if it is missing, adds a liking. The day-137 draft stays beside the revision.

#### 30 min. Observation windows, O1

Six groups of five, 5 minutes a group, one child at a time at your table while the others read and draw tree helpers with a partner. No reteaching happens in a window. About 50 seconds a child. First ask [[s04-O138a]] with the child's revised plan and drawing. Then read the unseen card once, say "The class wants to decide where to put its pot," and ask [[s04-O138b]]. One prompt is allowed, 15 seconds: for the plan, "Which place?"; for the card, read it once more. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the sheet, each part apart. Signs: a place with no liking (error F); "I like it" with no place (error G); a liking given as the writer's reason (error C). Nothing composed today is scored. Day 142 reuses these marks.

#### 5 min. Partner share

Each child tells a partner the place chosen and what they like. Spoken, not scored. Collect the plan sheets, the revisions and the observation sheet at the end of the day.

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 22, lesson 114: double final ll, zz, ss, ff)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. No new pattern. 4 min: Review: doubled final letters (day 135); say bell, buzz, moss and puff. 6 min: read the cards fell, fill, hiss, jazz, doll and dull; change fell to fill, doll to dull and fuzz to fizz, naming the changed sound. 5 min: with counters, say the first, middle and last sound of hiss, fizz and cuff. 5 min: spell huff and miss on whiteboards. 2 min: Review: but, not and no (day 135).

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: Moss on a Log (day 137). Five stations of 5 minutes, six children each: you at the table, tasks 1 to 6 at three stations and partner reread, and tasks 7 and 8 at their normal station: say three sounds without a text, read one mapped word and spell one taught word. You see six children.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Echo Tree Care Words, verse 4. Write "First, we visit the tree with our class." with a space between words. Children point to each word and listen for the doubled final in class.

#### 5 min. Check

Say pill, then toss; each child writes both on a whiteboard. Mark the next six of the rotation on the three-point scale.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hand back the plan, then ask both items alone.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the marks.

- **10 min. The assessment, the plan.** Show your child their plan. Ask for the place and what they like.
  **Say.** "What is your opinion: where should we plant our tree?" Expect: a place and a liking.
- **10 min. The assessment, the reason.** Read once: "Place the pot where helpers can reach it. Then they can water it."
  **Say.** "Which reason from the writer helps us choose?" Expect: helpers can reach it and water it.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say fizz. Your child says the first, middle and last sound with a button each.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws a helper of a tree.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check; cut the partner share. Longer: a secure child finds a reason in Trees Help Our Community.

### Words and supports

Words: place, reach, helper, reason, water. Frame: "I would put our tree ___. I like it ___." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the card twice if asked, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who named a place with a liking and who stated a writer's reason. Day 139 repairs errors F, G and C in its Topic response part, then compares Trees Help Our Community with Planting Together.
