# Purpose cards, day 137

Three plain picture cards for the reread of Trees Help Our Community, pages three to five, one card for each group of six, five groups in all, so the bench and the bird cards are copied once and used twice: a bench under a tree; a bird on a branch; a child resting in the shade. No printed word.

The teacher rereads the pages. Each group holds its card, finds the sentence that answers it, points to it on the page and says it back:

- Bench: "This is why a bench under a tree is a good place to rest." (page four)
- Bird: "A bird rests on a branch." (page five)
- Resting child: "A cool, shady place helps them rest." (page three)

The teacher says one unfamiliar word on purpose (shelter) so a child can ask what it means. A child may point instead of saying. A group that finds no sentence hears the page read again, once, and tries once more.

These cards are for teaching and may be shown. They do not repeat any exit or end card.
