# Day 139: Respond to the check, reasons and steps in two texts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the observation sheet from day 138: list who made error F or G on the plan, two tables of at most five, and who made error C or B on page 2 of Planting Together. The opinion and the writer's reason are two records; do not combine them. Pick the day's six, none heard on day 138. Mark page 7 of Planting Together for sheet E139. Write the repair card and the two place cards from the part below.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: reason, a sentence the writer states to support the point (day 130); step, one thing done in order (day 131); shade (day 129); helper (day 132); place (day 137). Show a card for each. Children point to the card you name, then say it. A check by showing, not new teaching.

#### 15 min. Topic response, E139

Whole class, then two tables, then the day's six. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child. Day 138's check used new cases only; today's retry uses new cases only too.

- Model, 5 minutes. Show a wrong answer beside a right one. The puppet says, "A bench." Say: "A bench. I like it by the fence." The puppet says, "I like the sign." Read the card "Put the sign by the door. Then visitors can find us." and say: "Then visitors can find us." Children say which answer gives the writer's reason. Show two cards, "A bean grows up a pole." and "A bean grows in a pot." The puppet says, "Both are the same." Say: "Card one says where it climbs; card two says where it grows." Each child says one difference to a partner.
- Repair, 5 minutes, two parts of 2.5 minutes. Hand-back from day 138's assessment, its two items. Part one, a table of up to five who made error F or G, three turns each of 10 seconds, new place card, the coat hooks: the puppet says, "The coat hooks." Children point to the place first, then say "I would put our tree by the coat hooks. I like it ___." Part two, a table of up to five who made error C or B, while the others partner-reread Planting Together by its pictures. Read "Hang the apron on the hook. Then it stays clean." The puppet says, "I like aprons." Say: "Find the sentence that says why, after the word then." Each says, "Then it stays clean." If more than five need a part, hear five and write the others on the record sheet for day 140's Reread.
- Retry, 5 minutes. Individuals, the day's six. Read page 7 of Planting Together aloud again (sheet E139), then ask [[s04-X139v2]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others draw a tree on their strip. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: a liking given as the reason (error C); a sentence of the other text (error Y).

#### 15 min. Sort reasons and steps

Pairs with the box and arrows mat. Review: the box with arrows, the point and its stated reasons (day 130). Read Trees Help Our Community pages 3 and 5 and Planting Together pages 3, 5 and 6 aloud, about 225 words, 2 minutes. Say: "A reason says why. A step says what to do, in order." Each pair sorts five sentence cards, read aloud, into a reason pile and a step pile. Then each pair sets the two reasons on the arrows below the point in the box, "Trees help our community," pointing to the page, and says one sentence back: "Trees help our community because ___." Hear six pairs, 30 seconds each.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Review: Both ___. But ___. (day 131). 3 min: say it once with the two texts, "Both are about trees, but one tells why and one tells how." Paired turns of 2 minutes, all pairs at once. **Ask.** "What do the words of Trees Help Our Community tell us that Planting Together does not?" Expect: why trees help, shade and shelter. **Ask.** "What do the pictures of Planting Together show?" Expect: people planting a young tree, step by step. Six children share, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes; the rest of the time is for a second look at a picture. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Draw and dictate

Individuals. Each child draws one reason on the left of a strip and one step on the right, and dictates one sentence. You take about eight children, 75 seconds each. A child who points to a picture counts. Scored from the work you handed in.

#### 20 min. Retrieval, sounds and print

Pairs, 10 minutes each. Review: print and letters (unit 1 and the cycles so far); no new pattern. First, with letter cards: follow a finger left to right, top to bottom, page by page along the chart and find the spaces; name each letter in both cases; you listen to the day's six. Second: print three of the letters; write a letter for the first sound of net, jet, jam and dig; hear bell, sell and hill and say which two rhyme, then give another word; clap basket and jam; say /j/ /am/ and /d/ /ig/ and blend each.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: Moss on a Log (day 138). Five stations of 5 minutes, six children each. Only taught text: no new pattern. You at the table: tasks 1 and 2. Stations 2 to 4: tasks 3 to 6 with the cycle 22 verse. Station 5: partner reread. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Write together "We look at each leaf as we pass." with a space between words. Each child points to the spaces; two children print a doubled-final word from the sentence.

#### 5 min. Check

Say less and loss, then gull and gill, then kill and kiss. Children say which sound changed. Mark the rotating six on the three-point scale.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the model, the retry alone and the sort with the mat. One projector: show a card at a time; speak in place of the arrows.

### At home

**Swap.** No mat: draw a box and two arrows. No tiles: use buttons.

**Skip.** The tables, the stations and the marks.

- **10 min. Reasons.** Read page 3 of Trees Help Our Community aloud.
  **Say.** "What reason does the writer give?" Expect: trees give shaded places to rest.
- **10 min. Steps.** Read page 3 of Planting Together aloud. Your child says one thing the adult does.
  **Say.** "How are the two texts different?" Expect: one tells why, one tells how.
- **10 min. Draw.** Fold paper in two. Your child draws a reason and a step.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X139v2]] Read page 7 of Planting Together aloud, then ask what reason the writer gives for keeping the mulch back.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retry, the sort and the sound check; cut the second repair part. Longer: a secure child finds a reason and a step on a page of Planting Together.

### Words and supports

Words: reason, step, both, different. Frames: "Trees help our community because ___." "Both ___. But ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can point to the writer's reason. Day 140's Reread takes errors C and Y with a new text, two minutes each. Day 140 then rereads the planting account and models an appreciation card.
