# Check card, day 138 (O1, the reason a writer gives)

Keep this card unseen until day 138. Never read, show or teach it before the check. Read it aloud once. Each group of five has two and a half minutes of this card and the opinion question together.

**Card:** "Place the pot where helpers can reach it. Then they can water it."
Say: "The class wants to decide where to put its pot." Ask: "Which reason from the writer helps us choose?" Right: helpers can reach the pot to water it.

A liking ("I like blue") given as the reason is error C. Give one prompt a child and never the answer; a prompted answer is marked shown with help. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card.
