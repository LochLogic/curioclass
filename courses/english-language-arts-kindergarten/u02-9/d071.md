# Day 71: Draft three events in order

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the more common of day 70's errors G and H from its exit marks. Draw the repair card: "In the morning, Nori and Dad stand at a bus stop in fog." Hang the arc chart with the class story (Pia, the market) and leave its three middle boxes empty. Put card F2 and the object cards out of sight. Open the day 70 letter check record to see who needs a retry. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card twice. Repair the more common of day 70's errors, one only. Error G: Pip puts the bus stop in the who box. **Ask.** "Does the bus stop act in the story? Who does?" Expect: Nori and Dad. Error H: Pip puts the lake in the where box. **Ask.** "Does the card say lake? Which place does it say?" Expect: no; the bus stop. Children point to the person in the who box and the place in the where box. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Review: first, next, last, the words for the order of events (day 51); today then can stand for the last one. Review: a naming word names a person or thing and an action word tells what it does (day 36). Review: a plural names more than one, with the ending s or es: drop, drops, dish, dishes (days 39 and 40); say each pair and hold up one finger, then two.

#### 10 min. Hear 02a, the middle events

Whole class. Read pages 3 and 4 of The Picnic Plan again with their pictures. **Ask.** "Which event follows the opening?" Expect: a gray cloud slides over the sun, the wind blows and the first drop falls. Show the picture on page 4. **Ask.** "Look at the rain on the path. Which word names more than one drop?" Expect: drops. Say: "The ending s says more than one." Children turn to a partner and tell what happened first, next and then in the picture story, with three fingers.

#### 5 min. Model

Whole class. Look at the arc chart: the first box holds Pia at the market. Draw the three middle boxes one at a time, saying the event aloud and pointing to first, next, then. First the wind pulls at the awning. Next Pia pulls her scarf up. Then Pia buys two peaches and three rolls. Under the last box write the plural words large and tap the ending of each: peaches, rolls. Point to Pia, then to the action word in each sentence: pulls, buys. Say: "The writer draws one event in each box and keeps them in order."

#### 30 min. Draft the events

Individuals, with a call-up. Each child draws three events in order in the three small boxes of the paper arc, numbering the boxes 1, 2, 3, then says each event aloud to a partner with first, next and then. Partners say back any plural word they hear: "You drew two ___." A child who needs it dictates the three events to a helper and points as they are read back.

First read card F2 once to everyone (30 seconds, inside the 30): say "This card is made up for practice," then read it from the stem of s02-G1a, which sits with day 70's items. Then call the other fifteen children one at a time to the table, about 2 minutes each (15 children, 30 minutes of teacher time): the same battery as day 70, not a second look at the first fifteen. Each child takes the five items of day 70, named by id and not repeated here: s02-G1a (a noun and an action from the card), s02-G1b and s02-G1c (two plurals, with the object cards), s02-G1d (the six printed questions, then one the child asks) and s02-G1e (two placements to follow and two to describe). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: unclear description (error F), no s (error X), no es (error Y), a time for where (error Z), reversed location (error AA). Record only what you hear; finish an unfinished piece in spare minutes, never infer it. Anyone from day 70 with a piece still open finishes it now, in the same words.

#### 5 min. Exit E71

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its three pictures (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X71a]]; children number the three pictures on the exit sheet (30 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X71b]] to the day's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the picture of the last event numbered before the rain (error AH); the single word said again for two (error X).

#### 10 min. Foundations: return and repair

Small groups, by the day 70 letter check record. Children marked shown with help come to your table for the one missed letter, sound or sound-writing, with a fresh retry (about 30 seconds each, at most ten children). This is the response to the letter check, counted only here. A sound said for a name (error U): hold the card, say its name, then its sound, and sort two cards into name and sound. A wrong consonant sound (error V): say the taught sound beside the letter, then the child says it. A wrong letter written (error W): say the sound, pick its card, then write it. The rest print one line each of b, o and w. No new pattern is taught.

#### 10 min. Foundations: j, e and z

Whole class (cycle 11, lesson 56). New letters. Hold up each keyword card: jar, elephant, zebra. Children say the word, its first sound and the letter name: j, /j/; e, /e/; z, /z/. The e is the fifth vowel: lay its card on colored paper beside a, i, o and u, and say that every word needs one. Skywrite each letter as the keyword is said, then print one line of Jj, Ee and Zz, saying the sound as it is written. Say a sound; children point to its letter card.

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 11, lesson 56). Rhyme: say bed and red; children give another (head). Clap rain (one), jacket (two) and elephant (three).

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words see and does

Whole class. New words, read whole (cycle 11). Write see; point under s, e, e and say /s/ /ee/; the part to remember is the two e's together saying /ee/. Write does; the part to remember is oe saying /u/, and s saying /z/ at the end. Then write "You see a log." and "Does Pip see you?" and point under each word; children read them. Review: a and you, read whole (day 66).

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /j/ /e/ /t/ and children say jet. Segment zip into three. Isolate the first sound of van, the middle sound of bed and the last sound of jam. Add /b/ in front of us to make bus. Change the last sound of bed to /g/ to make beg. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 20 min. Foundations: rotation

Table and stations. Six children at your table, 2 minutes each (12 minutes), with counters: s02-P1a (first, middle and last sound), s02-P1b1 (blend), s02-P1b2 (segment), s02-P1b3 (add a sound first), s02-P1b4 (change the first sound), all items of day 70. These are the second six; yesterday's six are not asked again. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: wrong first, middle or last sound (errors N, O, P); cannot blend (error Q); a sound left out (error R); a sound added in the wrong place (error S); the wrong sound changed (error T). Keep the record for days 77 and 78. The others move among stations: letter cards, handwriting paper, a printing line of j, e and z. For the last 8 minutes visit the stations and write who was heard on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear pages 3 and 4, draw three events in order, say them with first, next and then, and say each plural aloud; do the oral battery and the sound work as a short talk with counters and cards. One projector: show the arc chart; the drawing stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall chart: draw three boxes on paper. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The stations and the letter retry.

- **10 min. Three events.** Tell a short story with first, next and then; your child shows each with a finger.
  **Say.** "What happens first, next and then?" Expect: three events in order.
- **10 min. Draw them.** Your child draws three events in order on side 1 and tells each to you.
- **5 min. More than one.** Hold up one dish, then two.
  **Say.** "Here is one dish. Two what?" Expect: dishes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X71a]] [[s02-X71b]] Read twice: "First Lia picks up a pail. Next it rains. Then Lia carries the pail indoors." Show three pictures; your child puts them in order. Then say: "A drum, a bird. Say it for more than one: two ___."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the three events, the oral battery and the exit; cut the retry groups to one. Longer: a secure child draws a fourth event and says the plural words aloud.

### Words and supports

Words: first, next, then, plural. Frames: "First ___. Next ___. Then ___." "Two ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 72 finishes the events, adds an ending and a reaction, and prepares a question for a partner. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of today's errors AH (events out of order) and X (a plural said without its s), and the first fifteen children take their word and sentence turns.
