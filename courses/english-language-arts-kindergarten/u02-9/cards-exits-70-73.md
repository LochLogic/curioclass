# Exit cards, days 70 to 73

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 70

**Draft card:** "At dawn, Rafa and Mum wait at a barn door in rain." **Item a:** three drawings, Rafa and Mum at a barn door in rain; a barn door and rain with no people; Rafa and Mum at the beach in rain. "Which drawing begins a story from these words?" **Item b**, dictated beside the chosen drawing: "Say the words that go with it."

## Day 71

**Story card:** "First Rafa packs a lunch. Next rain begins. Then Rafa takes the lunch inside." **Item a:** three picture cards, mixed: a lunch packed; rain begins; the lunch inside. "Put these pictures in the order of the words." **Item b:** Say: "A map, a pen." Ask: "Say it for more than one: two ___." The child says maps, then pens.

## Day 72

**Story card:** "Rafa waits inside. Rain ends. Rafa smiles and says, 'Now we can go together.'" **Item a:** four cards, rain ends; Rafa smiles; Rafa waits with rain still falling; the door alone. "Which two pictures finish the story?" **Item b**, six children: "Ask the writer one question about the ending to learn more."

## Day 73

**Draft card:** "Snow fell. I stayed." A partner asks: "Where did you stay?" **Item a:** two pictures, a child staying in the hall with snow outside; the first snow picture again. "Which picture adds an answer to the draft?" **Item b:** "Say the words you will add."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 70: a, Rafa and Mum at the barn door; not yet: the door and rain with no people (G); the beach (H). b, the people and the place ("Rafa and Mum wait at the barn door."); not yet: only the door (G); the beach (H).
- Day 71: a, lunch packed, rain begins, lunch inside; not yet: the lunch inside before the rain (AH). b, maps; pens; not yet: map or pen for two (X).
- Day 72: a, rain ends and Rafa smiles; not yet: the waiting picture and the door alone, no reaction (AG). b, a question about a missing detail ("Where do they go?"); not yet: "What is for lunch?" (AW).
- Day 73: a, the child staying in the hall; not yet: the first snow picture again (AI). b, words naming a place ("I stayed in the hall."); not yet: no words added, or "Snow fell again" (AI).
