# Day 72: Finish the story, show a feeling and plan a question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the more common of day 71's errors AH and X from its exit marks. Draw the repair card: "First Teo fills a pail with water. Next he waters the seeds. Then he puts the pail away." Make three loose pictures for it. Hang the arc chart with the class story. Put cards F3 and F8 out of sight. Read the student page aloud. After class, within 30 minutes, write one comment on each draft you heard today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card twice. Repair the more common of day 71's errors, one only. Error AH: Pip says putting the pail away comes first. **Ask.** "Did putting the pail away come first?" Expect: no; filling it came first. Children lay the three pictures in order while rereading with first, next and then. Error X: Pip says, "One pail, two pail." **Ask.** "Is that right for two?" Expect: two pails. Children say one pail, two pails and one dish, two dishes, holding up fingers. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Review: a question asks for help or for more; a useful question asks about something the writer left out (day 69). Review: the three-card routine for a word: the word, a picture, a sentence, then ask what it means (day 37). A word can mean two things: wave. Show a hand moving up and over for a wave of the sea, then a hand moving side to side for a wave hello. Say each meaning in a sentence. Ask what wave means in a new sentence you say, and have children show it.

#### 10 min. Hear 02a, the ending

Whole class. Read pages 5 and 6 of The Picnic Plan again with their pictures. **Ask.** "How does the ending show how Tomas feels?" Expect: he smiled; his shoulders lifted; he said, "This is the best picnic." **Ask.** "Which question helps clear up an event?" Expect: a question about something the page leaves out, such as where Tomas found his toy. Children turn to a partner and ask one question about page 6; the partner answers or says "I do not know."

#### 5 min. Model

Whole class. Look at the arc chart. Draw the ending event before the last box: the wind stops and the clouds clear over the market. Point to the reaction box from day 69: Pia smiles and says, "I am warm now." Say: "A feeling can show in what a person does too." Add an action beside it: Pia hums a song and hops. Then ask a partner question about the draft: "What song does Pia hum?" Say why it helps: the draft does not say. The class answers and you add notes beside Pia.

#### 30 min. Finish your draft and conferences

Individuals, with a call-up. Each child finishes the ending event and the reaction in the last boxes of the paper arc, then rehearses one question for a partner with the frame "I want to know ___." and says it aloud to a neighbor. A child who needs it dictates the ending and the reaction to a helper. Weather stays gentle with a safe ending.

First read page 3 of The Picnic Plan once to everyone, words only (about 20 seconds), for [[s02-M1av2]]; then say "This card is made up for practice" and read card F3 twice, for [[s02-M1b]] (inside the 30). Then call the first fifteen children one at a time, about 2 minutes each (15 children, 30 minutes of teacher time). Each child: [[s02-M1av2]] (a question about the word dropped and its meaning on that page, about 40 seconds), [[s02-M1b]] (a new sentence with the second meaning, about 40 seconds), then read card F8 from [[s02-C1]] and ask for a longer sentence (about 40 seconds). Mark each piece apart: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a question not about the word (error AB); only the first meaning (error AC); a fragment (error AE); the same sentence again (error AF). Record only what you hear. The other fifteen have theirs tomorrow. After class, write one comment on each of the fifteen drafts: one part that works and one question the writer could answer.

#### 5 min. Exit E72

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its four cards (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X72a]]; children mark two of four cards on the exit sheet (30 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X72b]] to the day's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the waiting picture and the door alone, with no reaction (error AG); a question that learns nothing missing (error AW).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 11, lesson 57). Rhyme: say snow and glow; children give another (go). Clap cloud (one), window (two) and umbrella (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: j, e and z

Whole class (cycle 11, lesson 57). Review: j, e and z, names, sounds and keywords jar, elephant and zebra (day 71). Skywrite each letter while saying the steps: j goes down and hooks, then a dot; e goes across, then curves around; z goes across, slants down, then across. Children print two lines each of Jj, Ee and Zz on handwriting paper and circle their best letter. Watch each grip and correct it quietly.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words see and does

Whole class. Review: see and does, read whole (day 71). Point under "Pip does see you." and "You see a log."; children read them. Name the part to remember in each word: the two e's saying /ee/ in see; oe saying /u/ and s saying /z/ in does.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /h/ /u/ /t/ and children say hut. Segment log into three. Isolate the first sound of fin, the middle sound of zip and the last sound of cab. Add /p/ in front of in to make pin. Change the first sound of men to /t/ to make ten. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. First reading of verse 9, Look Out and See, with the pointer. Read it aloud twice, tapping the spaces. Count the words in the first sentence, "Jed looks out the window.", one finger a word. Find see and does in line 1. Find a j, an e and a z in the verse. Clap window (two) and jacket (two). Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner says it back.

#### 20 min. Foundations: rotation

Table and stations. Six children at your table, 2 minutes each (12 minutes), with counters: s02-P1a (first, middle and last sound), s02-P1b1 (blend), s02-P1b2 (segment), s02-P1b3 (add a sound first), s02-P1b4 (change the first sound), all items of day 70. These are the third six; no one heard on days 70 or 71 is asked again. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: wrong first, middle or last sound (errors N, O, P); cannot blend (error Q); a sound left out (error R); a sound added in the wrong place (error S); the wrong sound changed (error T). Keep the record for days 77 and 78. The others move among stations: letter cards, handwriting paper, a verse line pointed to with a partner. For the last 8 minutes visit the stations and write who was heard on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear pages 5 and 6, finish the ending and the reaction, ask and answer one question, and do the word and sound work as a short talk. One projector: show the arc chart and the verse; the drawing stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall chart: draw three boxes on paper. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The stations and the shared verse.

- **10 min. The ending.** Tell the ending of a story you know.
  **Say.** "What happens last, and how does the person feel?" Expect: the last event, and a feeling shown by a smile or an action.
- **10 min. Finish and ask.** Your child draws an ending and a feeling on side 1. You ask one question about something left out.
  **Say.** "What would you ask the writer to learn one more thing?" Expect: a question about a missing detail.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints j, e and z on side 2.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X72a]] [[s02-X72b]] Read twice: "Tuck shivers by the window. Clouds clear. Tuck cheers and says, 'Hooray, I can play.'" Show four cards: clouds clear; Tuck cheers; Tuck shivers with clouds; the window alone. Your child points to the two that finish the story, then asks the writer one question to learn more.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the ending, the reaction, the question and the exit; cut the verse and one station. Longer: a secure child adds a second feeling shown by an action.

### Words and supports

Words: ending, reaction, question, wave. Frames: "My character feels ___." "I want to know ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk.

### Tomorrow

Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of today's errors AG (the reaction left out) and AW (a question that learns nothing missing). The first fifteen get their written comments back.
