# Day 73: Answer a partner's question and add a detail

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the more common of day 72's errors AG and AW from its exit marks. Draw the repair card: "The kite lifts. Mika holds the string." Hang the writing-process poster and the arc chart. Put cards F3, F4 and F8 out of sight. Return the first fifteen drafts with your written comments. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the prompts and repair

Whole class. Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Hold the Rain and Snow and Picnic Plan pictures beside you. Read card F3 twice from the stem of s02-M1a, then card F4 twice from [[s02-M2a]], words only; children listen for a word to ask about. Then repair the more common of day 72's errors, one only, with the repair card read twice. Error AG: Pip says the story is finished. **Ask.** "How does Mika feel? Pip left that out." Expect: a feeling shown by a smile or a cheer. Error AW: Pip asks, "What is Mika's favorite food?" **Ask.** "Which question learns something the card left out: 'What color is the kite?' or Pip's?" Expect: the first. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 10 min. M1 for the other fifteen

Individuals. Call the other fifteen children one at a time, about 40 seconds each (15 children, 10 minutes): card F3 is already read; each child answers s02-M1a (a question about the word and its meaning here) and s02-M1b (a new sentence with the second meaning), the items of day 72. Mark each piece apart: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a question not about the word (error AB); only the first meaning (error AC). The first fifteen reread their drafts aloud to a partner.

#### 15 min. Peer question and added detail

Pairs, then individuals. Point to the poster: plan, ask, add (Review: day 69). Show it once with Pip. Pip reads "The wind stops." You ask, "What did Pia see?" Pip says, "A rainbow over the stalls." Draw it beside the sentence. Then each writer reads or points through their draft; the partner asks one question that learns something left out; the writer adds one detail beside the earlier draft by drawing and dictation, and you print the dictated words for the child to label with a taught letter ([[s02-V1]]). Frames: "I want to know ___." "I will add ___." Walk among the pairs and note six pairs on the record sheet. Unscored until day 75, when your written feedback and day 74's revision are read together.

#### 25 min. Conferences, the other fifteen

Individuals, with a call-up. Call the other fifteen one at a time, about 100 seconds each (15 children, 25 minutes of teacher time). Review: a word part changes a word's meaning (day 53). Each child: [[s02-M2a]] asks a question about one word on card F4 (about 30 seconds), [[s02-M2b]] explains two of the eight word-part words, drawn by rotation so all eight are heard across the fifteen (about 50 seconds), then s02-C1 with card F8, the item of day 72 (about 20 seconds). Mark each piece apart: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a question not about a word (error AB); the word part ignored (error AD); a fragment (error AE); the same sentence again (error AF). Record only what you hear. The first fifteen revise their drawings, using your comment to add one answer to their draft.

#### 5 min. Exit E73

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the draft and the partner's question twice with the two pictures (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X73a]]; children mark one of two pictures on the exit sheet (30 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X73b]] to the day's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the first picture marked again, or no words that answer the question (error AI).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 11, lesson 58). Rhyme: say tree and bee; children give another (knee). Clap rainbow (two), zebra (two) and elephant (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: j, e and z

Whole class (cycle 11, lesson 58). Review: j, e and z, names, sounds and keywords (day 71). Say a sound; children point to its letter card, then a partner says the letter name. Skywrite each letter, print one line of Jj, Ee and Zz, and count the letters in Jed and in zip with counters.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words see and does

Whole class. Review: see and does, read whole (day 71). Point under "Pip does see you." and "You see Jed."; children read them. Name the part to remember in each word.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /l/ /e/ /g/ and children say leg. Segment dig into three. Isolate the first sound of gum, the middle sound of cab and the last sound of bus. Add /t/ in front of an to make tan. Change the last sound of cap to /t/ to make cat. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. Review: reread verse 9, Look Out and See, with the pointer, tapping the spaces. Review: mystery word clues (day 47). Say the clues one at a time: it is in line 1; it has three letters; it has two e's; you do it with your eyes. Children point to the word (see) and say it. Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner says it back.

#### 20 min. Foundations: rotation

Table and stations. Six children at your table, 2 minutes each (12 minutes), with counters: s02-P1a (first, middle and last sound), s02-P1b1 (blend), s02-P1b2 (segment), s02-P1b3 (add a sound first), s02-P1b4 (change the first sound), all items of day 70. These are the fourth six; no one heard on days 70 to 72 is asked again. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: wrong first, middle or last sound (errors N, O, P); cannot blend (error Q); a sound left out (error R); a sound added in the wrong place (error S); the wrong sound changed (error T). Keep the record for days 77 and 78. The others move among stations: letter cards, handwriting paper, a verse line pointed to with a partner. For the last 8 minutes visit the stations and write who was heard on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the draft, ask one question, let the child add a detail, and do the word and sound work as a short talk with counters and cards. One projector: show the poster; the drawing stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No poster: draw plan, ask, add as three small pictures. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The stations and the verse.

- **15 min. Ask and add.** Your child points through their drawing and says what is in it. You ask one question about something left out. Your child adds the answer to the drawing.
  **Say.** "What would you ask the writer to learn one more detail?" Expect: a question about something left out.
- **5 min. A word with two meanings.** Say: "I wave to Nana." Then: "A wave rolls in the sea."
  **Say.** "What does wave mean here?" Expect: moving a hand; then water in the sea.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints j, e and z on side 2.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X73a]] [[s02-X73b]] Read twice: "Wind blew. I hid." A partner asks, "Where did you hide?" Show two pictures: a child hiding under a table with wind outside; the first wind picture again. Your child points to the picture that answers, then says the words to add.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the question, the added detail and the exit; cut the word-meaning turn. Longer: a secure child adds a second detail that answers a second question.

### Words and supports

Words: question, detail, add. Frames: "I want to know ___." "I will add ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 74 opens with your written feedback coming back, and the first 5 minutes repair error AI, a draft left unchanged after a good question. It also describes a familiar weather scene aloud with a helpful drawing, and the children who did not get a comment today get theirs.
