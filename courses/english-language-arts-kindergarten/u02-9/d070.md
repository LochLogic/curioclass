# Day 70: Draft who is in the story, and where

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the more common of day 69's errors AG and AW from its exit marks. Draw the repair card: "Mina feeds the birds by the fountain. The birds hop close." Hang the arc chart beside the class story (Pia, the market). Put card F2 and the object cards out of sight. On the record sheet, unit 2, head columns for the oral battery, the sound work, the letter check and who was heard. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card twice. Repair the more common of day 69's errors, one only. Error AG: Pip stops after "the birds hop close" and says the story is done. **Ask.** "How does Mina feel? Pip left that out." Expect: a reaction, such as she laughs or claps; children show the feeling with their faces. Error AW: Pip asks, "What is the bus driver's name?" **Ask.** "Which question learns something the card left out: 'What do the birds eat?' or Pip's?" Expect: the first one. Children say one helpful question to a neighbor. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Draft: a first try at a piece of writing; it can change later. Show it by writing lightly in the air, then waving the page away to try again. Review: a character is a person who acts in a story, never an object (day 49). Review: the setting is where and when a story happens (day 67). Frames: "My story is about ___." "It happens at ___."

#### 10 min. Hear 02a, the opening

Whole class. Read pages 1 and 2 of The Picnic Plan again with their pictures. **Ask.** "Which words show who is in the story?" Expect: Tomas and Grandma Rosa. **Ask.** "Which place word should an opening keep?" Expect: the park by the pond. Accept the kitchen too, then say: an opening keeps the place where the first events happen. Children turn to a partner and use the frames to say who is in their own story and where it happens; you listen to three pairs.

#### 5 min. Model

Whole class. Point to the first box of the arc chart. Say: "I am the writer. I draw first, then I find my words." Draw Pia in a long scarf walking between the market stalls, wind lifting the awning. Say two lines and write them large beside the drawing: "Pia walks through the market." "A cold wind blows." Say why: the first line tells who and where, the second tells the weather. Touch under each word with the pointer, tapping the spaces, and point to the capital P and the end mark. Say: "This is a draft. I can change it later."

#### 30 min. Draft your own character and setting

Individuals, with a call-up. Each child draws, in the first box of their paper arc, their own character with the place around them, using the plan card from days 66 and 67 (about 10 minutes); then labels the name with taught letters and says the opening to a partner with the frames. Weather stays gentle: rain, wind, snow or fog, with a safe ending; a child who does not want a stormy picture draws a sunny day or passes.

First read card F2 once to everyone (30 seconds, inside the 30): say "This card is made up for practice," then read it from [[s02-G1a]]. Then call the first fifteen children one at a time to the table, about 2 minutes each (15 children, 30 minutes of teacher time). Each child: [[s02-G1a]] (noun and action), [[s02-G1b]] and [[s02-G1c]] (two plurals, with the object cards), [[s02-G1d]] (the six printed questions, about 6 seconds a turn, then one the child asks), [[s02-G1e]] (two placements to follow and two to describe, drawn so all eight position words are heard across the fifteen). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: unclear description (error F), no s (error X), no es (error Y), a time for where (error Z), reversed location (error AA). Record only what you hear; finish an unfinished piece in spare minutes, never infer it. The other fifteen do this tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit E70

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its three drawings (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X70a]]; children mark one of three drawings on the exit sheet (30 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X70b]] to the day's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the door and rain with no people, a place named as the character (error G); the beach for the place (error H).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 10, lesson 55). Rhyme: say log and dog; children give another (frog). Clap wind (one), puddle (two) and butterfly (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Review: the counters work from days 43 to 53 and 65 to 69. Blend /t/ /o/ /p/ and children say top. Segment web into three sounds. Isolate the first sound of bed, the middle sound of dog and the last sound of web. Add /s/ in front of it to make sit. Change the first sound of cot to /p/ to make pot. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words you and are

Whole class. Review: you and are, read whole (day 66). Point under "You can sit on a log." and "Are you on a van?"; children read them. Name the part to remember in each word: ou saying /oo/ in you; ar saying /ar/ with a quiet e in are.

#### 10 min. Foundations: b, o and w pattern check

Whole class, then six aloud, then all write. This is the cycle 10 check; teach nothing new. Say a letter name, such as "double-u," and every child holds up that card; you see every card (3 minutes, six prompts). Then a rotating six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes): show b, o and w in both cases; each child names every letter ([[s02-C70a]]) and gives its sound ([[s02-C70b]]); mark the cycle 10 record. Last, every child writes the letter for each sound you say, /b/, short o, /w/, on a slip ([[s02-C70c]]) (3 minutes). Signs: a sound said for a name (error U), a wrong consonant sound (error V), a wrong letter written (error W). Naming aloud for all thirty is finished on days 77 and 78.

#### 30 min. Foundations: rotation

Table and stations. Six children at your table, 2 minutes each (12 minutes), with counters: [[s02-P1a]] (first, middle and last sound), [[s02-P1b1]] (blend), [[s02-P1b2]] (segment), [[s02-P1b3]] (add a sound first), [[s02-P1b4]] (change the first sound). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: wrong first, middle or last sound (errors N, O, P); cannot blend (error Q); a sound left out (error R); a sound added in the wrong place (error S); the wrong sound changed (error T). Keep the record for days 77 and 78. The others move every 10 minutes among three stations: shared print of a poem, letter cards, handwriting paper. Then two more small groups of up to five, about 9 minutes each (18 minutes): the verse prompts missed on days 67 and 68, with a fresh line of verse 8. Right-to-left tracking: model one line with the pointer. A word not matched to its letters: point under you and are in line 4. Sounds counted as beats: tap the beats of puddle.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear pages 1 and 2, draw the character and place, say the opening, and do the oral battery and the sound work as a short talk with counters and cards. One projector: show the arc chart and the poem; the drawing stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall chart: draw three boxes on paper. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The stations and the slip.

- **10 min. Who and where.** Tell or read a short story aloud.
  **Say.** "Who is in the story, and where is it?" Expect: a person and a place.
- **10 min. Draw the opening.** Your child draws a made-up character and the place on side 1, then says the words that go with it. Write them beside the drawing.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints b, o and w on side 2.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X70a]] [[s02-X70b]] Read twice: "At night, Kai and Nan sit by a window in snow." Show three drawings: Kai and Nan at a window in snow; the window and snow with no people; Kai and Nan on a hill in snow. Your child points to the drawing that begins the story, then says the words that go with it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the draft, the oral battery, the exit and the sound work for the day's six; cut one station and the second verse group. Longer: a secure child adds a second character or a second detail about the place.

### Words and supports

Words: draft, character, setting. Frames: "My story is about ___." "It happens at ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 71 continues the draft with three events in order and plural nouns said aloud. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of today's errors G (a place named as the character) and H (the wrong place), and the other fifteen children take the oral battery. Its foundations block opens with a fresh retry for anyone whose letter check today was marked shown with help.
