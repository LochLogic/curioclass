# Exit cards, days 65 to 69

Days 66 to 68 use pages of the unit's own read-alouds, read aloud again; they are not made up, so the teacher only makes their pictures. For the made-up cards (days 65 and 69), say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every made-up card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards or pictures is shown in teaching.

## Day 65

**Story card:** "At the school gate, hot sun shines on Mina and her aunt. They step under a tree. Mina feels glad it is cool." Three boxes: who, where, what happens. Six plan cards: Mina and her aunt; a bicycle; the school gate; the park; they step under the tree; the sun alone. **Ask:** "Put the plan cards in their boxes." Six children: "Which words told you?"

## Day 66

**Story page:** section 5, page 3, of Yusuf and the Parcel (texts/gk-u2-03.md), read aloud again, words only, its picture covered. **Item a:** four drawings, a boy at a kitchen table wearing blue mittens with a red stripe; a boy in red mittens with no stripe; a pair of mittens alone; a bus. "Which drawing is Yusuf at the end?" **Item b**, six children: "Tell me one more detail to add to the drawing."

## Day 67

**Story page:** page 1 of Priya at the Window (texts/gk-u2-02.md, selection b), read aloud again, words only. **Item a:** three pictures, a kitchen window on a rainy afternoon; the same window on a sunny morning; a bus shelter. "Which picture shows where and when?" **Item b**, six children: "Say a longer sentence about the place."

## Day 68

**Story page:** section 4, page 2, of Yusuf and the Parcel (texts/gk-u2-03.md), read aloud again, words only. **Item a:** five pictures: Yusuf hears a rumble and presses his nose to the window; the plow pushes the snow to the side; the big flakes turn small; the orange light alone; the lamp post alone. "Which three pictures show things that happen in the story?" **Item b:** the same three pictures. "Put them in the order the words tell."

## Day 69

**Story card:** "Fog lifts. Ona sees the hill again, grins and waves." **Item a:** three pictures, Ona grinning and waving; Ona looking at the ground; the hill alone. "Which picture shows how Ona reacts?" **Item b**, six children: "Ask the writer one question to learn more."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 65: Mina and her aunt in who; the school gate in where; they step under the tree in what happens. Not yet: the bicycle as who (G); the park as where (H); the sun alone as the event (I).
- Day 66: a, Yusuf in the blue mittens with a red stripe; not yet: the red mittens, the mittens alone or the bus (D). b, a detail the page gives (Mama and Baba, the brown paper and string, the note, the snow outside) or one the child says is imagined; not yet: a detail added as if the page says it, such as a red coat (A).
- Day 67: a, the kitchen window on a rainy afternoon; not yet: the sunny morning or the bus shelter (H). b, a complete sentence with where or when ("Priya kneels by the kitchen window on a rainy afternoon."); not yet: the short sentence again (AF); "kitchen window" (AE).
- Day 68: a, Yusuf at the window, the plow pushes the snow, the flakes turn small; not yet: the orange light alone or the lamp post alone as an event (I). b, in that order; not yet: the plow before the rumble (AH).
- Day 69: a, Ona grinning and waving; not yet: the hill alone (AG). b, a question about a missing detail ("Who does Ona wave to?"); not yet: "What is for lunch?" (AW).
