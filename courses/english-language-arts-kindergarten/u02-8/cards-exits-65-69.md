# Exit cards, days 65 to 69

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 65

**Story card:** "At the school gate, hot sun shines on Mina and her aunt. They step under a tree. Mina feels glad it is cool." Three boxes: who, where, what happens. Six plan cards: Mina and her aunt; a bicycle; the school gate; the park; they step under the tree; the sun alone. **Ask:** "Put the plan cards in their boxes." Six children: "Which words told you?"

## Day 66

**Character card:** "Zia wears a green scarf and carries a paper boat. Wind blows." **Item a:** four drawings, a girl in a green scarf holding a paper boat; a girl in a red scarf with no boat; a paper boat alone; a bus. "Which drawing is Zia?" **Item b**, six children: "Tell me one more detail to add to the drawing."

## Day 67

**Story card:** "At dawn, the girl sits on the steps of the boat house. Mist hangs over the water." **Item a:** three pictures, the boat house at dawn; the boat house at noon; a bus shelter. "Which picture shows where and when?" **Item b**, six children: "Say a longer sentence about the place."

## Day 68

**Story card:** "A girl flies a paper plane at the pier. The wind stops. The plane floats down and she picks it up." **Item a:** five pictures: flies the plane; the wind stops; the plane floats down and she picks it up; the plane alone; the pier alone. "Which three pictures show things that happen in the story?" **Item b:** the same three pictures. "Put them in the order the words tell."

## Day 69

**Story card:** "Fog lifts. Ona sees the hill again, grins and waves." **Item a:** three pictures, Ona grinning and waving; Ona looking at the ground; the hill alone. "Which picture shows how Ona reacts?" **Item b**, six children: "Ask the writer one question to learn more."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 65: Mina and her aunt in who; the school gate in where; they step under the tree in what happens. Not yet: the bicycle as who (G); the park as where (H); the sun alone as the event (I).
- Day 66: a, the girl with the green scarf and the paper boat; not yet: the bus, the boat alone or the red scarf (D). b, a detail the card gives or one the child says is imagined; not yet: a detail added as if the card says it (A).
- Day 67: a, the boat house at dawn; not yet: noon or the bus shelter (H). b, a complete sentence with where or when ("The girl sits on the boat house steps at dawn."); not yet: the short sentence again (AF); "boat house" (AE).
- Day 68: a, flies the plane, the wind stops, the plane floats down and she picks it up; not yet: the plane alone or the pier alone as an event (I). b, in that order; not yet: the wind stops first (AH).
- Day 69: a, Ona grinning and waving; not yet: the hill alone (AG). b, a question about a missing detail ("Who does Ona wave to?"); not yet: "What is for lunch?" (AW).
