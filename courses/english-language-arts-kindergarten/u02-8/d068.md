# Day 68: Events in order: first, next, last in the story boxes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the more common of day 67's errors H, AE and AF from its exit marks. Draw the repair card: "By the pond at noon, Tavi feeds the ducks." Make three event cards for the class plan. Mark the two T1 pages; set out the verse and three station tables. The T1 and H1 record sheet (u02-8/t1-h1-record) is not made yet: until it exists, mark on the unit record sheet. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card twice. Repair the more common of day 67's errors, one only. Error H: Pip says it happens in a kitchen. **Ask.** "Which words tell where and when?" Expect: by the pond, at noon. Error AE: Pip says only "pond". **Ask.** "Who is in it, and what happens?" Expect: Tavi feeds the ducks. Error AF: Pip says "Tavi feeds the ducks" again. **Ask.** "What can we add?" Expect: by the pond, or at noon. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Sequence: the order things happen. Review: first, next and last (day 51), with the three gestures. Review: event, what happens in a story (day 50). Today the events go in the middle box, in order.

#### 10 min. Hear 02a again

Whole class. Lay the three event cards from day 65 on the chart: they walk to the park; rain starts and they go home; they have the picnic on the living room floor. Read pages 2 to 5 aloud again with their pictures. **Ask.** "What follows the first event?" Expect: the sky changes and rain starts. Swap two cards. **Ask.** "What changes if two event cards swap?" Expect: the story does not make sense; the rain is why they go home. Children put the cards back and say first, next, last.

#### 10 min. Model

Whole class. Look at the class plan from day 65. Choose three events for its weather change and set them in the middle box in order, saying first, next, last. For snow: first the snow starts to fall; next the wind pushes the flakes sideways; last Pia pulls her cart home. For rain: first the drops start; next puddles fill the path; last Pia splashes home. Read the matching sentence from Rain and Snow aloud ("Wind can push snowflakes sideways," page 4, or "Rain falls as drops of water," page 2). Children find that page in the large print and point to its picture. Say why the weather fact is there: it is true, so the reader learns something.

#### 15 min. T1 and event drawing

The other fifteen children take T1, as on day 67: three groups of five at your table, four minutes each, then three minutes with all fifteen. In each group read section 3, page 2, of Yusuf and the Parcel twice, words only (40 seconds); each child takes about 40 seconds: ask a question about the page and answer it, then ask you to help find one detail. For the last three minutes read page 1 of Clouds twice and talk with the fifteen together: name what the page is about and tell its details, with turn-taking heard as the children talk; a detail question and a request to read again are marked for whoever offers them. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record only what you hear; the rest stays open for days 77 and 78. The first fifteen draw three events in the boxes on side 1 of the student page, one picture a box. Written feedback on T1 goes back within 30 minutes of class.

#### 10 min. Own plan

Pairs. Each child tells a partner their three events in order with first, next and last, and points to one weather fact, one minute each way. You listen to six children, about 40 seconds each. Frames: "First ___." "Next ___." "Last ___." A child who cannot speak points to the pictures in order.

#### 5 min. Exit E68

Read section 4, page 2, of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud again, words only (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X68av2]]; children mark the three event pictures on the exit sheet (45 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X68bv2]]; children write 1, 2 and 3 beside the three they marked (45 seconds). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a thing or a place marked as an event (error I); the middle event first (error AH).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 10, lesson 53). Rhyme: say log, dog and frog; children give another (jog). Clap rain (one), sweater (two) and umbrella (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: H1 formation

All children print both cases of v, s, g, i, d, f, l, k, y, q, u and x on handwriting paper, one pair at a time as you say its name: [[s02-H1h]]. About 50 seconds a letter; no tracing. This replaces the sound-writing practice today. Collect the pages: written feedback goes back after class, and children revise it for 10 minutes on day 69.

#### 5 min. Foundations: b, o and w

Whole class. Review: b, o and w (day 66). Say each name; children give its sound and point to its card.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words you and are

Whole class. Review: you and are, read whole (day 66). Point under "You sat on a mat." and "Are you my cat?"; children read them.

#### 5 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /f/ /i/ /g/ and children say fig. Segment hen into three. Isolate the last sound of fog. Change the first sound of hen to /p/ to make pen. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 30 min. Foundations: H1 rotation, the other fifteen children

Two minutes each at your table with verse 8, Dressed for Weather, the same seven prompts as day 67, about 15 seconds each. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record only what you hear. The others work at three stations: partner print tracking, letter cards, handwriting. Written feedback on H1 goes back within 30 minutes of class.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Order the three events with the cards, match the weather fact, then answer the exit. One projector: show the pictures one at a time; the cards stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three small pictures. No story: use any short story with three things that happen.

**Skip.** T1, H1 and the stations.

- **10 min. In order.** Read a short story with a weather change.
  **Say.** "What happens first, next and last?" Expect: three events, in order.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws three events in order in the boxes on side 1 of the student page and tells them with first, next, last.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints b, o and w on side 2.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X68av2]] [[s02-X68bv2]] Read section 4, page 2, of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud. Show five pictures: Yusuf at the window; the plow; the small flakes; the orange light alone; the lamp post alone. Your child picks the three that happen, then puts them in order.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the boxes and the exit; cut the pair talk. Longer: a secure child adds a fourth event between two others.

### Words and supports

Words: sequence, first, next, last. Frames: "First ___." "Next ___." "Last ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 69 adds a reaction to the arc and models a partner question. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of today's errors I (a thing or place named as an event) and AH (events out of order). The formation page comes back with written feedback to revise.
