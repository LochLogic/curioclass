# Narrative-arc chart

A large wall chart and a small paper copy for each child. Three boxes in a row, left to right.

## The boxes

1. **Character and setting.** A box for who (a person who acts) and where and when.
2. **Events.** Three small boxes inside, numbered one, two, three, with the words first, next, last in large letters for the teacher to point to.
3. **Reaction.** A box for what the character does or says about what happened.

## How to use it

Day 65 the teacher draws the boxes on The Picnic Plan (Tomas and Grandma Rosa at the park path; rain starts and they go home; the indoor picnic and "This is the best picnic"). Day 65 also holds the joint plan for a made-up story, three choices a box by show of cards. Days 66 to 69 each child draws their own character, setting, events and reaction in the small copy; children draw, they do not write words.

## Key (teacher)

The first box holds a person and a place, never an object alone (G, H). The events are real events, never a minor object (I), and in order (AH). The reaction is a feeling shown by what the character does or says (AG).
