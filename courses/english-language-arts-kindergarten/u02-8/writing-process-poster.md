# Writing-process poster: plan, ask, add

A poster for the wall, begun on day 69, with three large hand-drawn pictures in a row and one word each.

## The three steps

1. **Plan.** A hand holds a pencil above a set of three boxes.
2. **Ask.** Two children at a table; one has a hand raised. The partner asks one question to learn a missing detail.
3. **Add.** An arrow from the question to the drawing, and a new detail added beside it.

## How to use it

The teacher points to each step and says it. On day 69 the class adds a reaction to the class plan, a partner asks one question, and the writer adds a detail by dictation. The poster is used again on day 73.

## Key (teacher)

A good question asks about something the writer left out (for a draft that says "Pip climbed.": "What did Pip climb?"). A question that does not develop the writing ("What is for dinner?") is not useful (AW). A writer who answers the question adds new words beside the first draft (AI if nothing is added).
