# Day 66: A character in a weather story: draw one with clear details

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the class arc from day 65 on the wall. Draw the repair card: "On the beach path, Sami and his dad open a red umbrella." with a picture for each of who, where and what happens. Make the exit sheet. Read day 65's exit marks and pick the more common of errors G, H and I. Keep the exit card to yourself. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card twice. Repair the more common of day 65's errors, one only. Error G: Pip puts the umbrella in the who box. **Ask.** "Can an umbrella act in the story?" Expect: no; the person who acts goes in who. Error H: Pip says it happens in a kitchen. **Ask.** "Which words tell where?" Expect: on the beach path. Error I: Pip says the umbrella alone is what happens. **Ask.** "What do Sami and his dad do?" Expect: they open the umbrella. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Person and place: Review: the question words who and where (day 43); who asks for a person, where for a place. Character: Review: a person who acts in a story (day 49). Detail: Review: one fact about a topic (u01-3, day 9). Today a detail makes a character exact: point to your hair, then to a hat.

#### 10 min. Hear 02a again

Whole class. Show page 1 of The Picnic Plan and read it again. **Ask.** "Which details tell you who Tomas and Grandma Rosa are?" Expect: Tomas has curly dark hair; Grandma Rosa has a straw hat. **Ask.** "Which words make the picture exact?" Expect: a colorful blanket and a wicker basket, not just a blanket and a basket. Children point to each detail on the picture. A child may point and not speak.

#### 10 min. Model

Whole class. Look at the class arc. Draw the person the class voted for on day 65 on chart paper and say each choice aloud. Add two details: one that shows the weather (a sun hat for hot sun, boots for rain, mittens for snow) and one that shows who the person is (hair, a basket, a cap). Say why each helps: "The boots tell the reader it is raining. The red cap tells the reader which person this is." Children name a third detail they could add; you draw it.

#### 20 min. Draw and describe

Pairs. Say: "Imagine a weather character of your own." For 12 minutes each child draws a person with one clear detail and a weather sign on side 1 of the student page. For 8 minutes pairs describe: one minute each way, then the listener says one detail that is clear and the artist adds it. You listen to six pairs. A child who cannot draw points to cards or dictates the detail. Frames: "My character is ___." "My character has ___."

#### 5 min. One source detail

Pairs. Each child points to one detail in the Picnic Plan pictures that helped them know a character, and tells their partner (about 40 seconds each). Three children share with the class.

#### 5 min. Exit E66

Read section 5, page 3, of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud again, words only, its picture covered (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X66av2]]; children mark one of four drawings on the exit sheet (30 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X66bv2]] to the day's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a drawing that has neither mitten detail (error D); a detail added as if the page said it, such as a red coat (error A).

#### 10 min. Foundations: return and repair

Groups, by the cycle 9 record from day 65. Children marked shown with help come to your table for the one missed letter, sound or sound-writing, with a fresh retry (about 30 seconds each, at most ten children). This is the response to the cycle 9 check, counted only here. The rest print one line each of q, u and x. No new pattern is taught.

#### 10 min. Foundations: b, o and w

Whole class (cycle 10, lesson 51). New letters. Hold up each card; children give its name, sound and keyword: b, bat, /b/; o, octopus, /o/; w, web, /w/. Skywrite each, then print one line of Bb, Oo and Ww, saying the sound as it is written. Say a sound; children point to its letter card.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /m/ /o/ /p/ and children say mop. Segment web into three. Isolate the first sound of bag, the middle sound of dog and the last sound of web. Change the first sound of dog to /l/ to make log. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words you and are

Whole class. New words, read whole (cycle 10). Write you; point under y, o, u and say the sounds /y/ /oo/; the part to remember is ou. Write are; the part to remember is the a and the r together saying /ar/, with a quiet e. Then write "You sat on a mat." and "Are you my cat?" and point under each word; children read them. Review: a, my and on, read whole.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. First reading of verse 8, Dressed for Weather, with the pointer. Read it aloud twice, tapping the spaces. Find you and are in line 4. Clap sweater (two). Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner says it back. Then reread the journal strip.

#### 10 min. Foundations: rotation

A rotating six, 1 minute each (6 minutes): name b, o and w, give each sound, write one letter; mark the cycle 10 record. The others print b, o and w on handwriting paper.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Look at the pictures of The Picnic Plan, draw one made-up character and describe it. One projector: show the pictures one at a time; the drawing stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: draw four small pictures. No cards: say the sounds aloud.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the rotation.

- **10 min. Look.** Look at a picture of a person in a story book.
  **Say.** "What tells you who this person is?" Expect: the hair, a hat, something they carry.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child imagines a weather character and draws a person with one clear detail on side 1 of the student page.
  **Say.** "Tell me about your character." Expect: a sentence naming a detail.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints b, o and w on side 2.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X66av2]] [[s02-X66bv2]] Read section 5, page 3, of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud. Show four drawings: Yusuf in blue mittens with a red stripe, a boy in red mittens, mittens alone, a bus. Your child points to Yusuf, then tells one more detail to add.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the drawing and the exit; cut the source detail. Longer: a secure child draws a second character for the same place.

### Words and supports

Words: person, place, character, detail, exact. Frames: "My character is ___." "My character has ___." "The ___ tells me ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 67 plans where and when the event happens and says an expanded sentence about the place. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of today's errors D (a drawing that misses the page details) and A (a detail the page does not say). Today's marks go on the record sheet.
