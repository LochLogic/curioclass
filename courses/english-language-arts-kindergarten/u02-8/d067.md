# Day 67: Where and when: plan the setting and grow a sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the more common of day 66's errors D and A from its exit marks. Draw the repair card: "Pia wears a blue cap and carries a basket. Snow falls." Split the class into a first fifteen and a second fifteen. Set out the T1 cards F6 and F7, the verse and three station tables. The T1 and H1 record sheet (u02-8/t1-h1-record) is not made yet: until it exists, mark on the unit record sheet. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card twice. Repair the more common of day 66's errors, one only. Error D: Pip draws the basket alone. **Ask.** "Is that Pia?" Expect: no; Pia has the blue cap and the basket. Error A: Pip adds yellow boots as if the card said so. **Ask.** "Which words say boots?" Expect: none; Pip imagined them. A child may say a detail and call it imagined. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Setting: where and when a story happens; sweep one hand across the room, then point to a clock. Review: detail (u01-3, day 9). Review: a sentence grows when you add where or when (day 40). Where is a place; when is a time of day.

#### 10 min. Hear 02a, pages 1 and 2

Whole class. Read pages 1 and 2 of The Picnic Plan again, with their pictures. **Ask.** "Which words tell where?" Expect: in the kitchen; the park by the pond; under the big tree. **Ask.** "Which words tell when?" Expect: Saturday morning. **Ask.** "How does adding where or when make a sentence clearer?" Expect: you know the place or the time, not only who. Compare "They walked." with "They walked to the park by the pond."

#### 10 min. Model

Whole class. Read Warmer and Cooler page 2 aloud: "At noon, the sun is high and bright." Then page 3: "When the sun goes down, the air feels cooler." Invent a setting for the class plan from day 65 using one of those details. Say the plain sentence, then grow it twice: "Pia waits." "Pia waits in the market." "Pia waits in the market at noon, when the sun is high." Point to where and when on the chart. Children say the grown sentence back, and one child adds a detail.

#### 15 min. T1 and setting drawing

The first fifteen children take T1: three groups of five at your table, four minutes each, then three minutes with all fifteen. In each group read F7 twice (40 seconds); each child takes about 40 seconds: [[s02-T1a]], then [[s02-T1g]]. For the last three minutes read F6 twice and talk with the fifteen together: [[s02-T1c]], with [[s02-T1d]] and [[s02-T1e]] heard as the children take turns; [[s02-T1b]] and [[s02-T1f]] are marked for whoever offers them. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: an answer the card does not give (error A); a topic left out (error B); information left out (error C); interrupting (error AR); stopping after one turn (error AS); no request for help or for a reread (error AT). Record only what you hear; the rest stays open for days 77 and 78. The other fifteen draw the setting for their plan on side 1 of the student page and say a grown sentence to a partner, one minute each way. Written feedback on T1 goes back within 30 minutes of class.

#### 10 min. Dictate

Each child, 20 seconds. Say: "Tell me one sentence about your place." You print it on the sentence strip with a capital at the start and a mark at the end; the child points to the capital and to the mark. While others wait they label their picture with a letter they know. Look for: it names where or when; it is a whole sentence; the child points to the capital and the mark. A sentence that meets: "Pia waits in the market at noon." One that does not: "market" (error AE). The strip stays in the child's folder.

#### 5 min. Exit E67

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its picture (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X67a]]; children mark one of three pictures on the exit sheet (30 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X67b]] to the day's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the noon picture or the shelter (error H); the short sentence said again (error AF); only a place word (error AE).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 10, lesson 52). Rhyme: say bed, red and head; children give another (said). Clap rain (one), window (two) and umbrella (three). Then clap the words in "Pia sat on a mat." (five).

#### 10 min. Foundations: b, o and w

Whole class. Review: b, bat, /b/; o, octopus, /o/; w, web, /w/ (day 66). Say each sound; children point to its card and print it. Print Bb, Oo and Ww, one line each, with no model. Tap the spaces in "Pia sat on a mat." and count the words.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words you and are

Whole class. Review: you and are, read whole (day 66). Point under "You sat on a mat." and "Are you my cat?"; children read them. Name the part to remember in each word.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /b/ /e/ /d/ and children say bed. Segment wig into three. Isolate the first sound of log, the middle sound of wet and the last sound of pig. Change the last sound of dog to /t/ to make dot. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 30 min. Foundations: H1 rotation, first fifteen children

Two minutes each at your table with verse 8, Dressed for Weather: [[s02-H1a]], [[s02-H1b]], [[s02-H1c]], [[s02-H1d]], [[s02-H1e]], [[s02-H1f]] and [[s02-H1g]], about 15 seconds a prompt; skip any a child has shown already. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: right-to-left tracking (error AO); a space counted in a word (error AP); a heard word not matched to its letters (error AQ); a shared first sound called a rhyme (error K); sounds counted as beats (error L); every sound split (error M); a word said from the picture (error AU). The others work at three stations: partner print tracking, letter cards, handwriting. Written feedback on H1 goes back within 30 minutes of class. The second fifteen are heard on day 68.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read pages 1 and 2, grow a sentence with where and when, and answer the exit; T1 and H1 become a talk and a poem with you. One projector: show the pictures one at a time; the cards and strips stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: write on any paper. No cards: draw the pictures.

**Skip.** T1, H1 and the stations.

- **10 min. Where and when.** Look at a picture from a story.
  **Say.** "Where is this, and when?" Expect: a place and a time of day.
- **10 min. A longer sentence.** Say a short sentence, then add where and when with your child.
  **Say.** "Say it again with where and when." Expect: a whole sentence with a place or a time.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints b, o and w.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X67a]] [[s02-X67b]] Read twice: "At dusk, Nell waits on the porch. Cool air moves over the grass." Show three pictures: the porch at dusk, the porch at noon, a swing. Your child points to where and when, then says a longer sentence about the place.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the dictation and the exit; cut the drawing. Longer: a secure child grows the sentence with both where and when.

### Words and supports

Words: setting, detail, where, when. Frames: "It happens at ___." "It happens in the ___." "It is ___ time." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 orders the imagined events with the narrative-arc boxes. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of today's errors H, AE and AF. T1 and H1 are heard from the other fifteen children; day 68's formation page is revised on day 69.
