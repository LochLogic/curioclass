# Section 3.9 opener: explain, share and respond

## What the section does

Days 115 to 118 close the tree inquiry. Day 115 rehearses what the collage teaches, day 116 shares it at tables, day 117 makes a class caption and closes the records, and day 118 gives one response to what the records showed, with a fresh card read only that day. Foundations finish cycle 18 and review the unit's letters and words.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading or rehearsal, the talk and a five-minute exit, after which six children, a rotating group named on the record sheet, are heard saying why. Day 116 runs the share: six tables of five, one child speaking at a time, while the teacher hears the speaking child at each table once. A child outside the heard group is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read alone; the teacher reads every card, direction and text aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A good explanation names the tree and joins the facts with "so".**
- **A card says who depends on a tree.** We use its line.
- **A tree's need and its dependent go together.** One arrow in, one arrow out.
- **A fresh card is read once.** It tells us what we can do on our own.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, C:** facts listed with no link, or no tree named. Say the tree first, then join with so.
- **E, D:** a fact that does not say who depends on the tree, or something the card never says. Point to the line.
- **N, I:** a voice too soft to hear, or no specific detail. Rehearse toward a listener one arm away; add one card fact.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No picture or question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion.

## Supplies

The finished collages, the exit cards, the S1 record sheet, the caption sheet, chart paper, crayons, a talking token, texts 05a and 03 in large print, the cycle 18 reader and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Rule a roster by hand for the S1 sheet, write the caption on chart paper and tear a strip for the talking token.
