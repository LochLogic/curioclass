# S1 record sheet

Kept in the teacher folder for day 116. Roster numbers 1 to 30 down the left, six tables of five.

Three asks by number, marked 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen), in the minute each child speaks to the table:

- 1: speaks so the table can hear
- 2: points to the part of the drawing that matches what is said
- 3: gives a complete idea the listener can repeat

A fourth column names a tree need and the living thing that depends on it. The collage page itself is scored for all thirty after class. A child may pass; leave the cells blank and try another day.

A room with nothing: rule the columns by hand on a sheet.
