# Day 116: Share the collage

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E116. Make six tables of five and set a table card on each. Name six children not yet heard for the share, one a table, and the speaking order for each table (see the share). Pick six more for the exit and six for the sound warm-up. Roster children 26 to 30 take the F1 battery at your table. Set the S1 sheet. Read aloud: "Draw what you learned" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Preview share and display by gesture: two hands open toward a friend, then a hand holding a picture up on a wall (new: share, display). Repair at most one, four minutes: if many chose the facts with no link on E115 (error A), lay two loose pictures, a tree and a bird at its nest (06). The puppet says, "A tree. A bird." Say, "These two do not say how they go together." Draw an arrow from the tree to the bird and say, "The tree holds the nest up, so the bird has a home." Children add an arrow to a loose pair, a deer and an oak with an acorn, and say the whole sentence with you.

#### 10 min. Read 05a

Whole class. Review: The Oak (days 92 and 106). Reread pages 3 and 5 with the pictures, about three minutes, as a listening task. Then **ask**, "Which reading did the acorn fact come from?" Children point to the large print of 05a. A child may pass. Read the line "A squirrel eats acorns." and say, "That line is from 05a." Children say, "The oak, so the squirrel has food."

#### 10 min. Prepare to share

Pairs, then tables. Review: the three-part explanation (day 115). Children say it once to a partner, one turn each of a minute, pointing to the name strip, the arrow in and the arrow out. Then children carry their collage pages to their tables and sit. Tell the order: the first speaker at each table, then the second, and so on round the five.

#### 25 min. Share the collage

Six tables of five, four minutes a child. Review: the talking token (day 85). Tables 1 to 3 begin at once. Tables 4 to 6 begin two minutes later, rehearsing to a partner first. In each four minutes the speaker takes the token and speaks for a minute, holding the collage up. Then two listeners each say one thing they learned. Then the token passes on. A child may pass and point while a partner reads the caption. You visit one table at a time and hear the speaker at that table. In the first three four-minute turns, stand at one of tables 1 to 3 for the first two minutes and at one of tables 4 to 6 for the next two, so that you hear six children, one at each table. Ask the speaker three times, each once: [[s03-S1a]] then [[s03-S1b]] then [[s03-S1c]]. Mark each shown, shown with help, not yet seen, on the S1 sheet, with the names of the six. Table time is five children times four minutes, 20 minutes, and 22 for tables 4 to 6.

#### 10 min. Exit ticket and close

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E116, read the card twice. A child shows a picture of the persimmon and the opossum. Read the three strips aloud. Ask [[s03-X116a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers. Mark the day's six, then ask them [[s03-X116b]], 30 seconds each. Signs: the strip about the tree alone (error E); the strip the card never says (error D). Then close, three minutes: **ask**, "What did a friend tell you that taught you something?" Children tell a neighbor, then three children say it to the class. A child may pass.

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85). Say mop, map and mug; children push a counter for each sound. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, say the first, middle and last sound of log, kit, hug, pet or tap. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 10 min. All short vowels review

Whole class, then pairs. Review: all short vowels (day 112) and chains on tiles (day 88). Chain lad, led, lid and then fan, fin, fun, naming the changed sound each time. Then blend the new line "Meg will hug Pam." and say what happens. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map are and will

Whole class. Review: are (day 66), will (day 112), be and by (day 107). Reread each on a card, then in two new strips, "Ned will sit by Zip." and "Beth and Rob are with Tim." Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery

Roster children 26 to 30 each take three minutes at your table, 15 minutes in all. Take each through the held back F1 cards in order: the pair, naming the changed letter and its sound [[s03-F116a]]; spelling by sounds [[s03-F116b]]; the fresh decodable, saying what it tells and pointing to its words [[s03-F116c]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never teach on these cards. The other twenty-five rotate through three stations of about six minutes: A Pet at Home in pairs, picture covered, a page each, then say what happened; tiles; printing verse words and letter cards. Review: A Pet at Home (days 112 to 115). Signs: points to the wrong letter (error L); names the letter, not the sound (error AH); leaves out a sound (error M); reads the words fast and cannot say what the page tells (error K); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Whole class. Dictate dog, hum and sit; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verse 7 (days 112 to 115). Read it with the actions and echo the rhyming words tight and light. Collect counters and tiles; the collage pages stay for you.

### After class

Score the collage pages of all thirty on four rows, each 2 shown, 1 shown with help, 0 not yet seen: the tree is named (error C); the fact is supported (error D); the detail is from the right reading (error AI); the picture adds information (error J) [[s03-W116]]. A page that meets all four has a labelled oak and an acorn fact from 05a. Look at the kept R1 and C1 records, with no new minutes [[s03-R116a]] [[s03-R116b]] [[s03-R116cv2]]. Reopen only a missing part, never a full second conference.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the table. Read 05a once, listen to the child say the three-part explanation about the collage, and take the exit card alone. Read the reader once and chain lad, led and lid. One projector: hold the collage and the exit card on paper; project one card at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse.

- **10 min. Share.** Your child shows their collage and says the tree, a fact and how they go together, as to a table.
  **Say.** "Which fact came from the reading?" Expect: your child points to a fact and says the reading it came from.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain lad, led, lid on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads are and will on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice: "A cherry tree has red fruit. Robins eat the fruit." Say these: "One: Robins eat the fruit. Two: Cherries are red. Three: Robins like to sing." Ask [[s03-X116a]], then [[s03-X116b]]: "Show me the line."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the share, the exit and the spelling; cut the sentence strips and the verse. Longer: a secure child tells a friend how two sources agree.

### Words and supports

Words: share, display, reading, fact, explain. Frame, only if a child asks: "My tree is ___. It needs ___. ___ uses it, so ___." A child who does not speak aloud may point to the collage while a partner reads the caption. Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can pick the line that says who depends on a tree. If many chose the strip about the tree alone (error E), day 117 opens with five minutes: lay a new card and a gap picture and place the line that names who uses the tree. Day 117 closes the records and writes a class caption for the display.
