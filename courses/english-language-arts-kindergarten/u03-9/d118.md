# Day 118: One response and a closing page

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E118. Pick six children for the retry and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Read the exit and record sheets from day 117 and name the one error most children made. Rule the closing chart into thirty spaces. Read aloud: "Write the letters I say" and "Print the letters of your name."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: what a tree needs (day 117). Preview care by gesture: two hands cupped around a small plant (new: care). Say, "Tomorrow we ask how people can care for trees." Nothing more is asked of children today.

#### 15 min. Targeted reteach and fresh retry

Whole class, then six. 8 min: reteach the one error most children made on the collage or on E117, using the wrong example beside the right one. If it is error A, one half only, draw two oaks on the board. The puppet's oak has roots with an arrow in from water and nothing else. Say, "That is half." Your oak has the same arrow in and a deer with an arrow out from the acorns. Say, "The oak needs water. A deer eats its acorns." Children say it with you, pointing to each arrow. 7 min: the fresh retry. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E118, read the card once and show the three pictures. Ask [[s03-X118a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X118b]], 30 seconds each. Signs: the mangrove with no fish (error A); the fish far out in open water (error D).

#### 15 min. Read 01b

Whole class, then pairs. Review: What a Tree Needs (days 83, 85 and 88). Read the six pages with the pictures, about five minutes, as a listening task. Then pairs. **Ask.** "What does a tree need?" One turn each, 45 seconds a turn, all pairs at once; any child may pass. Expect: water, air, light or soil. **Ask.** "Who uses a tree?" Same turns. Expect: a squirrel, a bird, a deer or a person. Take answers from three pairs after each ask. The text ends by asking who needs the tree; children answer from their collages.

#### 20 min. Close the unit

Whole class, then individuals. No new instruction. 3 min: show the closing chart, thirty spaces. Say, "Each of you adds one need and one living thing that uses it." 5 min: each child tells a partner one thing a tree needs and one living thing that uses it, one turn each. 10 min: each child draws the need and the living thing on a strip with an arrow between them, tells you the pair, and glues the strip in a space; write each pair under the strip as the child says it, about 20 seconds each, thirty children. A child may pass and draw only. 2 min: read the chart together, pointing. Keep the pairs for day 119.

#### 5 min. Bridge

Whole class. Tell the class: tomorrow we ask how people can care for trees. **Ask.** "What do you wonder about caring for trees?" Take three answers, write each on chart paper and say that day 119 starts there. A child may pass.

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85). Say vet, gum and jab; children push a counter for each sound and say the middle sound, about two minutes. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, say the first, middle and last sound of fog, lip, vet, gum or jab. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Whole class, then groups. Repair at most one, five minutes: if many chose a drawing with only one half on E117 (error A), give each child a card with a maple, a drop of water and a songbird in its nest. Say, "Draw one arrow in from the water and one arrow out to the bird." Then children say how they go together: "The maple needs water, and the songbird nests in it." Then five minutes of sounds. 2 min: each child rereads one stumbled sentence from the reader with a partner. 3 min: groups by letter with you or a helper, the two largest groups first: guesses from the picture (error R), reads fast with no meaning (error K), points to the wrong letter (error L), says the letter name (error AH), leaves out a sound (error M), a letter form that cannot be recognized (error AK). Each group ends with one new word on tiles or paper.

#### 10 min. Mixed short vowels

Whole class, then pairs. Review: all short vowels (day 112) and chains on tiles (day 88). Chain sat, set, sit and then tap, tip, top, naming the changed sound each time. Then blend the new line "Tim will hug Ned." and say what happens. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map are and will

Whole class. Review: are (day 66), will (day 112), be and by (day 107). Read each on a card, then in two new strips, "Dad will be with Ken." and "Zip and Ned are by the van." Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 15 min. Reread taught text

Pairs. Review: A Pet at Home (days 112 to 117). Partners read the reader, a page each, picture covered, then say what happened and point to the words that tell them. No new pattern. Close the five records for the second half of the roster as on day 117 (about a minute a child, from the evidence already kept; a part that is missing is asked for alone, at most three children), then listen to three pairs, two minutes each. Nothing else is scored. Signs: guesses from the picture (error R); reads the words fast and cannot say what happened (error K).

#### 8 min. Dictate sounds

Whole class. Review: spelling by sounds (days 112 to 114). Say a sound, children write a letter that could make it on side 1. Say /b/, /s/ and /n/, then the short vowel sounds in a, e, i, o, u order, eight in all, about a minute each. Any plausible letter is shown. Sign: leaves out a letter (error M).

#### 7 min. Print letters

Whole class, then individuals. Show the models for T t, M m, S s and B b. Each child prints the letters of their own name and those four, upper and lowercase, on side 2 with the model beside. Walk round and note the forms, about 12 seconds each. Sign: an unrecognizable letter form (error AK).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 01b once, ask the two questions, make the closing strip together and take the retry card alone. Tell the child that tomorrow we ask how people can care for trees. One projector: hold the retry card on paper; project one picture at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The reader and the sounds.

- **10 min. Talk.** Look at a tree outside or in a picture.
  **Say.** "What does a tree need?" Expect: water, light, air or soil.
  **Say.** "Who uses a tree?" Expect: a bird, a squirrel or a person.
- **5 min. Letters.** Say the sounds /b/, /s/ and /n/. Your child writes a letter for each.
- **5 min. Tell.** Tell your child that tomorrow the class will ask how people can care for trees.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice: "A pine tree has long needles. Squirrels eat the seeds in its cones." Describe these: "One: a pine with an arrow from its cones to a squirrel. Two: a pine and no squirrel. Three: a squirrel far from any tree." Ask [[s03-X118a]], then [[s03-X118b]]: "Which words tell us?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retry, the closing page and the printing; cut the sentence strips. Longer: a secure child adds a second living thing to the strip.

### Words and supports

Words: care, need, uses, closing page. Frame, only if a child asks: "A tree needs ___. ___ uses it." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A scribe may write the label, never choose the letters on a spelling day. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can show who depends on a tree. Day 119 opens the question of how people can care for trees. It starts by recalling what trees need from the closing page, and by reading the three wonderings you wrote today.
