# Display caption sheet

Used on day 117. A large sheet of chart paper for the class caption: a heading line for the display, then one sentence in each of several boxes, written by the teacher as children dictate.

A caption sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a period or a question mark. The teacher reads each sentence back, pointing, one word at a time. Children add a drawn picture beside their sentence if they like.

A room with nothing: tape together four sheets of scrap paper and rule the boxes.
