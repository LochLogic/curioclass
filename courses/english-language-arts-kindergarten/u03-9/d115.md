# Day 115: Say what the collage teaches

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark the first part of page 6 of The Sugar Maple for the exit. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Roster children 21 to 25 take the F1 battery at your table: set the F1 sheet and keep its cards out of sight. Put each child's finished collage page on their desk. Read aloud: "Draw what you will say" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: explain (days 39 and 57) and source sticker (day 92). Preview share by gesture: two hands open toward a friend (new: share). Repair at most one, four minutes: if many chose a note the card never says, or one that says the opposite, on day 114 (error D), lay the oak card line "Birds rest on its branches." (05a) in large print. The puppet reads two notes with the 05a sticker: "Birds rest on its branches." and "Oaks have the best leaves." Read the line twice, point to it for the first note and say, "That is on the card." For the second say, "No line says it," and cross it out. Children say, "Show me the line," and point.

#### 15 min. Read 03 and 05b

Whole class. Review: A Tree Is a Living Thing (days 89 and 113) and The Sugar Maple (days 103, 111, 112 and 114). Read the chant 03 whole, each line twice, about four minutes. Then reread pages 3 and 4 of 05b in two-line chunks, pointing under the lines. After each line say its source: "That line is from 05b." Children point to the line that tells what a maple needs and to the line that tells who uses it. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "What does our collage teach about the tree?" One turn each, 45 seconds a turn, all pairs at once; any child may pass. Expect: the tree's name and a fact from a reading. **Ask.** "How do the need and the living thing go together?" Same turns. Expect: an arrow, or the word so. Take answers from three pairs after each ask.

#### 20 min. Practice and edit

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the research talk (my question, my line from the card, so; days 107, 109 and 110), the opening that names the tree (day 104), the arrow with so (day 103) and edit (day 74). 8 min: model on the class page. Point to each part as you say it. "My tree is a sugar maple. It needs water. A squirrel uses it, so the squirrel has seeds to eat." Say it a second time and children say it with you, pointing: the name strip, the arrow in, the arrow to the squirrel. Say, "Now you say it about your own page." 12 min: 4 min in pairs, one turn each of a minute, partner listens and points to the part said. Then 8 min each child edits the page: check the tree's name, add one label by sounds, check that one source sticker matches its line, and ask, "Does my picture show what I said?" Add or move one thing if it does not. Walk round and ask six children, 30 seconds each: "Show me the line." Nothing is scored. Signs: no tree named (error C); two facts with no arrow (error A); a fact with a sticker from the wrong reading (error AI); a picture that adds nothing (error J).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read the first part of page 6 of The Sugar Maple aloud again, the sap and the syrup, its picture covered. Read the three explanations aloud, each with its picture. Ask [[s03-X115av2]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers on your count. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X115bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class puts its pages away. Signs: the facts listed with no link (error A); the sentence with no tree named (error C).

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85). Say dig, dog and dug; children push a counter for each sound and say the middle sound, about two minutes. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, say the first, middle and last sound of jam, fig, hop, hum or web. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 10 min. All short vowels review

Whole class, then pairs. Review: all short vowels (day 112) and chains on tiles (day 88). Chain ham, hem, him, hum and then jog, jig, jug, naming the changed sound each time. Then pairs read a set 18 sentence card, say who did what and check one word against the map. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map are and will

Whole class. Review: are (day 66), will (day 112), be and by (day 107). Read each on a card, then in two new strips, "Pam will sit with Meg." and "Tim and Dan are by the van." Point to the double l in will. Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery

Roster children 21 to 25 each take three minutes at your table, 15 minutes in all. Take each through the held back F1 cards in order: the pair, naming the changed letter and its sound [[s03-F115a]]; spelling by sounds [[s03-F115b]]; the fresh decodable, saying what it tells and pointing to its words [[s03-F115c]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never teach on these cards. The other twenty-five rotate through three stations of about six minutes: A Pet at Home in pairs, picture covered, a page each, then say what happened; tiles; printing verse words and letter cards. Review: A Pet at Home (days 112 to 114). Signs: points to the wrong letter (error L); names the letter, not the sound (error AH); leaves out a sound (error M); reads the words fast and cannot say what the page tells (error K); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Whole class. Dictate jam, hop and wig; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verse 7 (days 112 to 114). Read it with the actions, tracking line by line, and echo the rhyming words tight and light. Collect counters, tiles and glue sticks, and file the collage pages.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 03 once, say the three-part explanation about one page and take the exit card alone. Read the reader once and chain ham, hem and him. One projector: hold the class page and the reader on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No glue: draw the sticker. No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse.

- **10 min. Say it.** Your child holds their collage or a drawing of a tree and a living thing that uses it.
  **Say.** "What does your tree teach?" Expect: the tree's name, a need, and who uses it.
  **Say.** "How do they go together?" Expect: an arrow, or so.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain ham, hem, him on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads are and will on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read the first part of page 6 of The Sugar Maple aloud. Say three ways to explain it: one names the tree and joins the facts with so, one lists them, one leaves out the tree. Ask [[s03-X115av2]], then [[s03-X115bv2]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the practice, the exit and the spelling; cut the sentence cards and the verse. Longer: a secure child explains a second thing that uses the tree.

### Words and supports

Words: explain, share, tree, need, arrow, so. Frame, only if a child asks: "My tree is ___. It needs ___. ___ uses it, so ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can say how a tree and its helper go together. If many chose the facts with no link (error A), day 116 opens with five minutes: put an arrow with so between a tree and what uses it, and say the whole sentence. Day 116 is the share: each child shows the collage to a table of five.
