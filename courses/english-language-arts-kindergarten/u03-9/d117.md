# Day 117: A caption for our display, and the records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E117. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Lay out the unit 3 record sheet and the records it closes: the research log, the source-note record, the connection record, the sound-to-letter samples and the spelling record. Write nothing on the caption sheet yet. Read aloud: "Write the letters I say" and "Print T and t."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: share and display (day 116) and source sticker (day 92). Repair at most one, four minutes: if many chose the strip about the tree alone on E116 (error E), lay a new gap picture: an oak with an arrow in from water and an empty box beside it. The puppet reads two strips from 05a: "An oak is a big tree." and "A jay eats acorns from the oak." Say, "Which line says who uses the oak?" Children point. Place the jay line in the box and say, "A jay depends on the oak."

#### 15 min. Reread 03

Whole class. Review: A Tree Is a Living Thing (days 89, 113 and 114). Reread the chant whole as a listening task, each line twice, about four minutes. No new text. Then reread pages 2 and 4 in two-line chunks. Children point to the lines that tell what a tree needs: roots take in water, leaves use sunlight, air and water. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "What does a tree need?" One turn each, 45 seconds a turn, all pairs at once; any child may pass. Expect: water, light, air or soil. **Ask.** "Who uses a tree?" Same turns. Expect: a squirrel, a bird, a deer or a person. Take answers from three pairs after each ask.

#### 20 min. Class caption

Whole class, then individuals. Review: a caption (day 74), a capital letter and a period (day 56). 5 min: say, "A caption tells what a display teaches." The class says one sentence for the display: "Trees are alive." 5 min: you write it on the caption sheet word by word, saying what you do. "A capital T first. A space. A period at the end." Children point to the capital and to the period. 10 min: each child makes the sound /t/ aloud and writes a capital T on a strip, then points to the capital on the caption sheet and to the mark at the end. Walk round and note what each child did, 20 seconds each, thirty children. Records only; nothing is scored. Sign: a letter form that cannot be recognized (error AK).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E117, read the card twice. Read the three drawings aloud, each with its picture. Ask [[s03-X117a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X117b]], 30 seconds each, while the class puts its pages away. Signs: the drawing with only the water (error A); the drawing with only the person (error A). Errors wait for day 118.

#### 7 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85). Say rug, web and tap; children push a counter for each sound and say the middle sound, about three minutes. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, say the first, middle and last sound of jug, wet, bit, mop or lap. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 15 min. Reread taught text

Pairs. Review: A Pet at Home (days 112 to 116). Partners read the reader, a page each, picture covered, then say what happened and point to the words that tell them. No new pattern. While they read, close the records for the first half of the roster only (about a minute a child; the second half close on day 118) from the evidence already kept: the research log [[s03-R117a]], the source-note record [[s03-R117b]], the connection record [[s03-R117c]], the sound-to-letter samples [[s03-L117a]] and the spelling record [[s03-L117b]]. Mark each indexed. If one part is missing, ask that child for that part alone, at most four children, about a minute each, as you walk. Never run a full second conference. A child not yet heard stays unscored. Signs: guesses from the picture (error R); reads the words fast and cannot say what happened (error K).

#### 10 min. All short vowels review

Whole class, then pairs. Review: all short vowels (day 112) and chains on tiles (day 88). Chain pat, pet, pit and then rag, rig, rug, naming the changed sound each time. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map are and will

Whole class. Review: are (day 66), will (day 112), be and by (day 107). Reread each on a card, then in two sentence cards, "Ken is with Beth." and "Dad will fix the van with Tim." Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 13 min. Spell and print

Whole class. 6 min: dictate rug, tap and web; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 1. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M). 7 min: print one line of T and t beside your model on side 2, then point to the best one. Sign: an unrecognizable letter form (error AK).

#### 10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verse 7 (days 112 to 116). Read it with the actions and point to the rhyming words tight and light. Collect counters and tiles, and put the caption sheet where all can see it.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the class. Reread 03 once, say the caption aloud and write the capital T with the child, and take the exit card alone. One projector: hold the caption sheet on one screen; the child still writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse.

- **10 min. Talk.** Look at a tree outside or in a picture.
  **Say.** "What does a tree need?" Expect: water, light, air or soil.
  **Say.** "Who uses a tree?" Expect: a bird, a squirrel or a person.
- **5 min. Letters.** Write "Trees are alive." Your child points to the capital and the period, then writes a capital T.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain pat, pet, pit on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice: "A spruce takes in sunlight through its needles. Chickadees eat the seeds from its cones." Say these: "One: needles with the sun coming in, and a chickadee at a cone, joined with arrows. Two: needles with the sun and no chickadee. Three: a chickadee at a cone and no sun." Ask [[s03-X117a]], then [[s03-X117b]]: "What does the tree need, and who uses the tree?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the caption, the exit and the spelling; cut the sentence cards and the verse. Longer: a secure child dictates a second caption for a friend's picture.

### Words and supports

Words: caption, display, need, uses. Frame, only if a child asks: "A tree needs ___. ___ uses it." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A scribe may write the label, never choose the letters on a spelling day. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can join what a tree needs to who uses it. If many chose a drawing with only one half (error A), day 118's Return and repair takes five minutes: join the need and the one who uses the tree with two arrows and say how they go together. Day 118 gives one response with a new tree card and a fresh reading and spelling task, and tells the class that day 119 asks how people can look after trees.
