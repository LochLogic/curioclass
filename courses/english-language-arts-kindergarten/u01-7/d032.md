# Day 32: Words that go together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 31: note who was not heard on each T item. The observation groups are six groups of five in record-sheet order; today's six are the thirty-second six names. Set a clear space beside the teacher table for the acting. Put the pillow, the block and a shiny thing within reach. Have the C4 and C5 strips and the word-relationship cards at the table.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Take the more common of error R and error B from day 31's top strip. Say: "These repair cards are made up for practice." For error R, Pip claps every sound of ribbon and gets five; say the whole word, tap its two beats with the class, and say beats are not letters or sounds. For error B, lay two pictures beside the sentence "The kite goes up" (a kite high in the air; a kite on the ground) and have the class point to the one that matches the action. If only a few erred, take them as a small group during the observation groups.

#### 3 min. Words first

Whole class. Review: describing words and opposites, two words far apart (days 10 to 12). Hold up the pillow and the block: soft and hard; the class shows each with its hands (1 minute). Hold up a bead and then point to the door: tiny and gigantic, shown with fingers close and arms wide (1 minute). Pairs point to something rough in the room and say "The ___ is rough" to a partner (1 minute).

#### 14 min. Shared reading

Whole class in six groups on the rug. A child joins purposefully when the joining has a job: to find or say a named word; quiet looking alone is not evidence. Review: pointing under each word, left to right, spaces shown (day 23). Point under the large poem Read Together and read it, then the class reads it with you, twice, pointing (4 minutes). Name the word in, a mapped word read whole (day 28). The class finds it in "Up and down and in and out" and says one classroom thing that is in something: a crayon in the tub, a block in the basket (2 minutes). Review: Mira Makes a Road (days 20 to 25); content, happy with nothing more to want (day 22). Read page 7 aloud with its picture (35 words) and ask "What words tell how Mira felt?" Expect: content, or a child points to her smile (3 minutes). Then the class rereads the poem in pairs with small copies, a finger under each word, while you move from group to group, 30 seconds each ([[s01-V6]]; 6 times 30 seconds, 3 minutes; 2 minutes to move between groups). Each group joins the line "Up and down and in and out"; then two children, a different two each group, point to or tell a moment from page 7: Mira sits back and smiles at her road. Mark who joined and who found a moment.

#### 8 min. Hear 05

Whole class, then groups. Review: Attributes of Toys (days 10 to 12). Read it aloud whole with the pictures (about 2 minutes). Show pages 3 and 7 and say: "Point to a toy that a describing word tells about, and say it." Pairs point and say, "The bead is tiny" or "The rabbit is soft" (1 minute). Then hear each group, 30 seconds ([[s01-V7]]): each child points to the toy a describing word tells about and says its sentence; the partner says it back. Signs: a word that is not on the page (error A). A child may point and not say; mark shown with help.

#### 18 min. Observation groups

Six groups of five at the teacher table, 3 minutes each (6 times 3 is 18), in record-sheet order. Say each card's name aloud before the child answers. By the child's place in the group:

- All five sort the six length pictures into two piles, one child placing each card and the group saying the rule, "These are all ___" (45 seconds; [[s01-V1]]). Review: sort by one rule, with the rule card above each pile (day 11). A pile that mixes color with length is error T.
- Child 1 pairs wet with dry; child 2 pairs the drum with the sleeping child, loud with quiet; child 3 hears the stop card and says its opposite (45 seconds; [[s01-V2]]). Wet with soaked is error U; saying stop again is error AE.
- Children 4 and 5 each choose the shiny card and point to a shiny thing in the room (30 seconds; [[s01-V3]]). A dull block called shiny is error V.
- All five act hop, creep and stomp in the clear space beside the table, or with seated fingers (1 minute; [[s01-V4]]). Light springs, slow low steps, heavy flat steps; all three the same way is error W.

While they work, note any learned word a child uses on its own, such as tiny or rough, and whether it fits its object ([[s01-V5]]). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen; an item a child was not heard on stays blank for day 33. The other twenty-five work alone: each draws one classroom toy on a page for the class book Our Classroom Toys, labels it with a letter they know and tells a partner one describing word for it.

#### 2 min. Move and clear the space

Whole class. Chairs in, cards to the pair baskets, walk to the rug with walking feet. Check the paths between the tables are clear.

#### 5 min. Turn and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: turn and talk (day 1). Say two questions and a model for each. **Ask.** "Which learned words describe different play?" Expect: soft and hard tell how a toy feels; march and tiptoe tell how we move (day 13). **Ask.** "Which kinds of words stay the same?" Expect: color, size, shape and how it feels, the four kinds in Attributes of Toys. Each child speaks for 2 minutes, the partner says one thing back (4 minutes). Then three children tell the class one thing they heard, "I heard ___" (1 minute).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice: "The soup is hot. The ice is cold. We gallop like horses, then we glide like boats." Each child circles the strip that shows hot against cold and gallop against glide ([[s01-X32]]; 2 minutes). Then each of the day's six hears one spoken question, 30 seconds each: "Say a word that is the opposite of day." Signs: the strip with two steaming bowls (error V); the strip with both movements the same (error W). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 30 min. Cycle 4 check

Teacher table, at most 1 minute a child (30 times 1 is 30). First read the record sheet: keep any dated matching mark from days 29 to 31 and collect only the missing parts ([[s01-C4]]). In order: the child names M m R r on the card; prints the four forms; writes m and r for /m/ and /r/; says the sounds of the printed letters; shows the word in and then reads it in "The cart is in the box", which you read aloud. Signs: m called n (error AA); a name given for a sound (error N); a different letter written (error O); only the capital (error P); in guessed from the picture (error S). A part not reached stays blank. The other children rotate through two stations, 15 minutes each: letter cards with the sound-spelling map in pairs, and printing M m R r on handwriting paper.

#### 15 min. Onset and rime check

Teacher table, spoken only, half a minute a child (30 times half a minute is 15; [[s01-C5]]). Say: "These words are made up for practice." Say s, then un, with a pause, and the child says the whole word. Then say fish and the child says its first sound and the rest. Not yet: s and un left apart (error AF); f, i and sh (error AG). The other children print letters or draw a toy quietly at their tables.

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme (day 16): bed and head (yes); toy and boy (yes); toy and tub (no; the words start alike, error Q) (4 minutes). Review: tap the beats (day 18): hammer (two), apple (two), umbrella (three), cake (one) (3 minutes). Then the class says a word that ends like "star": car, jar, far (3 minutes).

#### 5 min. Letters m and r

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Review: letters m and r, name, sound and keyword (day 28). Go both ways: say /m/, children hold up the card; show the card, children say the sound; do the same for /r/ (3 minutes). Say a sound; each child chooses the letter card first, then prints it in the air with a finger (2 minutes). Naming only; not scored.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; sort the length pictures, ask for the opposite of two words, act the three verbs seated with fingers, and hear the poem with a finger under each word. One projector: show one page or picture at a time; children mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the letters on paper. No soft and hard things: use a sock and a spoon.

**Skip.** The groups, the check at the table and the stations.

- **5 min. Opposites.** Hold a soft thing and a hard thing; your child says each word.
  **Say.** "Which is soft? Which is hard?" Expect: the right word for each. Then ask for the opposite of hard: soft.
- **5 min. Steps.** Your child marches, then tiptoes; you say which is which.
- **5 min. Letters.** Say /m/ and /r/; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Fact.** Your child tells one true fact about a toy and where they could see it in a picture.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say b, then ell; your child says the whole word, bell.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X32]] Say "The soup is hot. The ice is cold. We gallop like horses, then we glide like boats." Your child circles the strip with hot against cold and different movements, and says the opposite of day.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut Words first and the shared reading of Mira page 7; keep the groups, the exit and the checks. Longer: a child who is secure finds one more pair of opposites in the room and says where it is.

### Words and supports

Words: opposite, describing word, sort, join. Frames: "The opposite of ___ is ___." "The ___ is ___." "These are all ___." A pointed card, a gesture or a home-language word is an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 opens with a five-minute repair of the more common of error W and error V on today's strips, then closes the opinion record, makes up the spoken items from days 31 and 32 that children were not heard on, and gives ten minutes to the cycle 4 check by missed component. Today's V items, exit and checks are marked after class.
