# Detail cards (day 31)

A set of picture cards for each pair and two topic cards for the wall. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words.

## The topic cards

- **Topic 1:** toys that roll. A cart and a ball together.
- **Topic 2:** toys that turn. A top and a pinwheel together.

## The detail cards

Four cards, each a picture of one detail from the two class information books: a cart's wheels with short rolling lines; a ball rolling away from a hand; a top spinning on its point; a cart's wheels turning in a circle.

## How the cards are used

Set the two topic cards on the wall. A pair holds the four detail cards and places each under its topic, then says what the detail tells. Ask: "How does a picture tell you more than the words?" Each detail belongs under one topic; if a card is placed under both, ask which words in the book give the detail. A child may point or use a home language; say the English back. The cards are practice cards, not an exit or end item.
