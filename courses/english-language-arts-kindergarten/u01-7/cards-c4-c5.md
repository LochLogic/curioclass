# The cycle 4 check strips (day 32; teacher only)

For the teacher, one child at a time. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not see the answers.

## C4: m and r

- A card of printed letters: M m R r in a row. The child names each.
- Two clean lines of handwriting paper. The child prints M, m, R and r.
- The teacher says two sounds, /m/ and /r/. The child writes the letters.
- The child says the sounds of the printed m and r.
- One word card with "in" printed large, then one strip with the sentence "The cart is in the box." The adult reads the sentence aloud, and the child shows and reads in.

**Key.** Both names and both sounds; four recognizable forms; the letters m and r for the heard sounds; in read alone and in the sentence. Not yet: a missing name, a printed form unrecognizable, a wrong letter written (O); a letter given another letter's name (AA).

## C5: spoken onset and rime

No print. The teacher says the parts aloud and the child answers.

- Blend: "s ... un". Key: sun. Not yet: leaves s and un apart (AF).
- Split: "fish". Key: f and ish. Not yet: f, i and sh (AG).

These words are fresh and never taught or practised. Keep them out of the other days.

## Day 34 retry (unscored)

After repairing with bat and mop, say "l ... og" for the child to blend and ask the child to split "cup" into c and up. A child retries only the part that was the child's own target.

Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A component not reached stays blank.
