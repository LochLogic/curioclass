# Word-relationship cards (days 32 and 33)

Picture cards and word cards for the day 32 observation groups, and one copy for the make-up hearings on day 33. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Length sort (a fresh set)

Six picture cards of toys: a scarf, a ribbon and a train of blocks (long); a crayon stub, a button and a bead (short). The child sorts by length only and says the rule.

## Opposites

Two pairs of picture cards: a wet sponge and a dry sponge; a drum being struck and a sleeping child. A verb card: a hand held up in front of a rolling cart, with the word "stop" printed large.

## A new word

A card with the word "shiny" printed large and a small star with sparkle lines. The child links the word to a real shiny object in the room.

## Verb cards for acting

Three cards, each with a picture and a printed word: hop, creep, stomp. The child acts each one in a clear path, or with fingers seated.

## Key (teacher)

- Length sort: long toys together, short toys together, rule named as long and short.
- Opposites: wet with dry, loud with quiet; for the verb card stop, the opposite is go.
- A new word: a real classroom object that is shiny, such as the bell.
- Acting: hop is light springs; creep is slow, low steps; stomp is heavy, flat steps.

Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Seated finger movements count. Observation groups run about three minutes each on day 32.
