# Day 34: Try it once more

## Teacher page

### Before class

Take out the exit cards, a portrait sheet for each child and the class book credit page. Mark each child's target: the interview from day 29 (error D), the portrait from day 30 (error M) or the opinion from day 18 (error L). List children with a miss on day 32's sound check. Day 33's make-up marks stay as they are. Put a turn card at the interview table. Today's six are the thirty-fourth six names.

### The day

#### 15 min. Response and fresh retry

Three small groups by recorded target, 4 minutes each at the teacher table in turn (12 minutes); the others draw alone from the start. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice to everyone: "Our puppet has a soft hat. Jo likes this classroom puppet." (1 minute). Each child draws the puppet and a label with a letter they know, and tells the teacher the label ([[s01-X34]]; 2 minutes at their place). Then at the table, repair first with a contrasting example from the group's own error (1 minute), then the child finishes the card. The interview group (error D): Pip answers "It rolls" and stops; you add "What about you?" and the class asks a follow-up; pairs try one with the turn card. The portrait group (error M): Pip shows a top labelled top and nothing more; add "It spins" from the picture. The opinion group (error L): Pip says "This is a top" and then "My favorite classroom toy is the top"; the class says which tells a preference. Hear the day's six wherever they sit, 30 seconds each: "Say one fact about the puppet." Signs: the label "I like it" with no toy named (error X); the toy named and no fact (error M). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen; nothing is scored.

#### 10 min. Hear 03 again

Whole class, then pairs. Review: Push, Pull, Roll (days 9 to 31). Read it whole with the pictures (about 2 minutes). **Ask.** "Point to one fact and say its page." Partners take turns; each speaks for 2 minutes (4 minutes). Expect: a push moves a toy away (page 2); a pull brings it toward you (page 3); a ball and a wheel roll (page 4). Then ask three pairs to tell the class their fact and its page (4 minutes). A fact that is not in the book is error A: reread the page and point.

#### 12 min. Shared reading

Whole class. Review: the poem Read Together (days 32 and 33) and the class big book. Point under each word of the large poem, left to right, top to bottom, with the spaces shown, and read it; the class joins in twice (6 minutes). Show page 2 of the class big book: "We have a ball. A ball rolls." Slide a finger under each word; the class reads it back and a child points to a space between two words (3 minutes). Name the mapped word in (day 28) and find it in "Up and down and in and out" (3 minutes).

#### 12 min. Class book share

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the class book Our Classroom Toys (day 25). Each child opens to their own page and shows it to a partner, saying "This is my page. It shows ___." (4 minutes each way, 8 minutes). Then three children show their page to the class, about 1 minute each, with a finger under the label (3 minutes). Thank them; the class says "I saw ___" (1 minute). A child may pass or point.

#### 6 min. Look back

Pairs, then whole class. Review: Ask a classmate, ask for help, ask what a word means, then ask a follow-up (day 26). **Ask.** "What can you ask a classmate about a toy now?" Each child says a question aloud to a partner, who answers; the partner then asks a follow-up and the first child answers (two exchanges, 2 minutes each way, 4 minutes). Then three pairs say their questions to the class (2 minutes). Expect: "What can it do?", "Can you help me pick a toy?", "What do you mean by ___?"

#### 5 min. Portfolio

Tables go in turn (30 children at about 10 seconds each is 5 minutes). Each child puts today's drawing and label into the portfolio folder and says one thing they could add. You note on the record sheet each child's next step in one line, such as "adds a fact" or "asks a follow-up". Nothing is scored; the folder goes home with the unit.

#### 15 min. Sound repair and fresh retry

Two spoken groups of up to eight at the teacher table, 7 minutes each (14 minutes, 1 to settle), the children whose day 32 onset and rime check showed a miss. Repair first, 3 minutes: say b, then at, and sweep your hand to bat; the class says the whole word (error AF repaired). Say mop and split it into m and op, holding op together; a split into m, o and p (error AG) is held together as op. Then each child retries once, 20 seconds each (8 children is under 3 minutes): say l, then og, and the child says the whole word; say cup and the child gives its first sound and the rest, c and up (s01-C5). The other children each retry only the print or sound part that was their own target (s01-C4) on an unused strip with the letter cards, a partner hearing it. Unscored.

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme (day 16): pen and hen (yes); pig and big (yes); pig and pot (no, the words start alike, error Q) (4 minutes). Review: tap the beats (day 18): lemon (two), banana (three), moon (one) (3 minutes). Review: onset and rime, swept together (day 24): k, ite sweeps to kite; n, ose sweeps to nose (3 minutes, each child sweeps once with a partner).

#### 5 min. Reread

Whole class. Review: the poem Read Together (days 32 to 34). Point under each word of the large poem and read it with the class, a finger under each word.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, five turns of 5 minutes (5 times 5 is 25). Teacher table: each child's target, a letter dictated and printed, or a spoken word swept; about 50 seconds a child. Four stations: letter cards with the map, printing M m R r, oral picture games, pointing in the class big book. You record on the sheet.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. One quick task on the child's own target: print one letter from a heard sound, name a letter card, or say a swept word. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we ask a new question, about the weather."

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; repair the child's own error with a contrasting example, then read the fresh card and ask for a drawing, its label and one fact. One projector: show one page at a time; children mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No puppet: use any toy or a drawing. No cards: draw the letters on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the class book share and the rotation.

- **5 min. Ask.** Your child asks you a question about a toy and a follow-up.
  **Say.** "What can you ask me about a toy now?" Expect: a question such as "What can it do?" and then a second question.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say k, then ite; your child says the whole word, kite.
- **5 min. Letters.** Say /m/ and /r/; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Rhyme.** Say pen and hen; your child says if they end the same.
- **5 min. Beats.** Say lemon and banana; your child claps each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X34]] Say "Our puppet has a soft hat. Jo likes this classroom puppet." Your child draws the puppet, says its label and says one fact.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut Look back and the class book share to the partner turn; keep the response, the sound repair and the portfolio. Longer: a child who is secure draws a second toy and tells two facts about it.

### Words and supports

Words: question, fact, label, portfolio. Frames: "What can it do?" "My favorite classroom toy is the ___." "This is my page. It shows ___." A pointed card, a gesture or a home-language word is an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 opens the weather question: we look at today's weather, find one detail again from the toy story, and hear a page about weather. Today's retry cards go in the portfolio, unscored.
