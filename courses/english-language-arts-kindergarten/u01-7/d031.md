# Day 31: Tell what a page is about

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the topic chart with the two topic cards on the wall and put a set of the four detail cards at each pair. The observation groups are six groups of five in record-sheet order; the last seven names are today's battery; today's six are the thirty-first six names. Put Folio B's three inside pictures at the teacher table. Keep Folio B hidden until its part, and do not explain its words beforehand.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Say: "These portraits are made up for practice." Take the day 30 portraits (s01-P1) one more time: Pip shows two drawings of a cart with rolling wheels. The first is labelled cart and says nothing more (error M): ask "What can you see the cart do?" and add one fact, "Its wheels roll." The second is labelled "I like it" (error X): say the toy first, then the fact. Children point to the better portrait of each pair and say it with you. If only a few erred, take them in the gap before the groups.

#### 8 min. Return to 03 and 04

Pairs, then whole class. Review: Push, Pull, Roll (days 9 to 30) and A Toy Can Turn (days 14 to 30). Read page 4 of each aloud with its picture (about 1 minute). Review: a topic is what the whole text is about, and a detail is one fact under it (day 9).

**Ask.** "Which details belong together?" Each pair places the four detail cards under the two topic cards on the wall (3 minutes), then says the sentence one card shows. Expect: the cart's wheels with rolling lines and the ball rolling away under toys that roll; the top spinning and the wheels turning in a circle under toys that turn. If a card sits under both, ask which words in the book give it (page 4 of Push, Pull, Roll; pages 2 and 4 of A Toy Can Turn).

**Ask.** "How does the picture add to the heard words?" Hold up page 4 of A Toy Can Turn. Expect: curved arrows show the wheel turning and a straight arrow shows the cart going forward. Partners point to an arrow and say what it adds (2 minutes). Review: a picture can explain a movement (day 14).

#### 4 min. Hear a new information booklet

Whole class on the rug. Say: "This booklet is made up for practice." Show the front cover and say front cover (day 2). Open to the title page and point to the credit lines, "Words: Curio Class writing team. Pictures: Curio Class art team." (1 minute). Read Folio B whole, once, turning each picture to the class (2 minutes). Show the back cover (30 seconds). Then say: "Keep it in your head. You will tell about it soon." Take no questions: asking is part of the groups. Children sit with hands still.

#### 24 min. Observation groups

Six groups of five at the teacher table, 4 minutes each (6 times 4 is 24), in record-sheet order. Lay Folio B's three inside pictures and its back cover on the table; read the booklet once more, 15 seconds. First 40 seconds, all five at once: each points to the picture for "A ball falls to the floor and bounces up", then to the picture that shows what the whole booklet is about. Pointing is an answer. Then ten hearings of 20 seconds (5 children times 2), by the child's place in the group:

- Child 1 hears [[s01-T1]] (name the topic and two details) and [[s01-T2]] (ask one question about the booklet; child 2 answers).
- Child 2 hears [[s01-T2]] (answers child 1) and [[s01-T3]] (say how the pictured action matches the sentence).
- Child 3 hears [[s01-T3]] and [[s01-T4]] (ask a question about the spring toy, then ask for one phrase to be read again).
- Child 4 hears [[s01-T4]] and [[s01-T1]].
- Child 5 hears [[s01-T1]] and [[s01-T3]].

Review: ask for help with "Please read that again" (day 22). If no child asks for a reading again in [[s01-T4]], offer "Is there a part you want read again?" and mark shown with help. Mark each hearing shown, shown with help or not yet seen; a hearing a child did not get stays blank for day 33. Signs: the bean bag named as the whole topic (error K); a fact the booklet never said (error A); the picture where the ball only goes down (error AD). The other twenty-five work alone at their tables: each draws two details from Push, Pull, Roll or A Toy Can Turn in the box on the student page, then tells a partner which page each came from.

#### 8 min. Parts of words

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the beats of a word are its parts, tapped whole word first, not its sounds (day 18). Clap and say: kite (one), ribbon (two), tricycle (three) (3 minutes). A child who claps every letter or sound (error R) hears the word said whole again and taps with you. Then pairs take four picture cards, balloon, teddy, trumpet and bicycle: one partner says the name, the other claps and says how many, then swap (4 minutes, 2 minutes each). Walk the room and listen. Say each word aloud before the child claps it.

#### 6 min. Turn and talk

Pairs. Review: turn and talk, ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass (day 1). Show page 6 of Push, Pull, Roll (a toy car rolls, stops, turns, with a hand at each step). **Ask.** "How did a picture tell you more than the words?" Expect: the hand at each step shows who pushes; the three steps show it going, stopping and turning. Frame: "The picture shows ___." Each child speaks for 3 minutes, the partner says "Tell me more" once (6 minutes). A child may point and not say.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice: "This page is about rattles. Our rattle shakes. The handle fits my hand." Each child circles the picture that explains the topic with a detail ([[s01-X31]]; 2 minutes). Then each of the day's six hears one spoken part, 30 seconds each: "Clap the parts of rattle." Signs: the drum or the rattle lying on the shelf (error B); one clap for each letter or sound (error R). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme, words that end the same (day 16). Say bear and chair (yes); bee and tree (yes); bee and bus (no). A child who says bee and bus rhyme because they start alike (error Q) hears the endings stretched: bee, tree (4 minutes). Then say star; the class says a word that ends the same: car, jar, far (1 minute). Review: tap the beats (day 18): crayon (two), pencil (two), butterfly (three), tree (one) (3 minutes). Review: onset and rime, the first sound and the rest, swept together (day 24): b, ell sweeps to bell; d, ig sweeps to dig (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters m and r

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Review: letter m, name em, sound /m/, keyword moon; letter r, name ar, sound /r/, keyword rake (day 28). Chant "m, m, /m/, moon; r, r, /r/, rake" (2 minutes). Say /m/ and each child holds up a card, then show a letter and the class says the sound (3 minutes). Write rope, mug and arm on chart paper; children come up one at a time to touch an m or an r and name it (5 minutes). Naming only: you read the words. Review: the word in is read whole by sight, not sounded out (day 28).

#### 28 min. Common battery, final seven

Teacher table, 4 minutes a child (7 times 4 is 28), with the prompts and keys of day 28's items: book kinds and makers (s01-B1, s01-B2, s01-B13, s01-B14); track, sequence and spaces (s01-B4, s01-B5, s01-B6); letter names (s01-B3); formation (s01-B7); sound writing (s01-B8); consonant sounds (s01-B9); rhyme (s01-B10); syllables (s01-B11); mapped words (s01-B12). A component not reached stays blank. The other twenty-three rotate through four stations, 7 minutes each: letter cards, printing, oral picture games, pointing in shared print.

#### 7 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print M m R r on handwriting paper, saying the sound as you form each (4 minutes). A child who prints only the capital when small is asked (error P) sees the lowercase path written once. Review: mapped word in (day 28). Show the large poem Toy Clues, point under "in" in "A toy in the yard", and the class reads it as a whole word (3 minutes).

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /r/ and then /m/. Each child chooses the letter card first, then prints the letter on the card. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we find words that go together."

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; read Folio B once, ask the child for the topic and two details, to ask you one question and to point to the matching picture; then clap two toy names. One projector: show one page at a time; mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the letters on paper. No book: put one toy on the table.

**Skip.** The groups, the battery and the stations.

- **5 min. Topic and details.** Put one toy on the table. Your child tells what it is and two things about it.
  **Say.** "What is it? Tell me two things about it." Expect: its name and two true facts, such as it rolls.
- **5 min. Beats.** Say kite and ribbon; your child claps each (one, two).
- **5 min. Rhyme.** Say bear and chair; your child says if they end the same.
- **5 min. Letters.** Say /m/ and /r/; your child prints each.
- **5 min. Moves.** Hop, creep and stomp like a toy; say which toy moves each way.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X31]] Say "This page is about rattles. Our rattle shakes. The handle fits my hand." Your child circles the picture that explains the topic and claps the parts of rattle.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second Ask in Return to 03 and 04 and the onset and rime in Oral work; keep Folio B, the groups, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure tells a third detail and says its page.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, detail, beat, front cover, title page. Frames: "This page is about ___." "The picture shows ___." "Please read ___ again." Pointing, a home-language word or an acted detail counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 opens with a five-minute repair of the more common of error R and error B on today's rattle card, then uses describing words and opposites in shared reading. Today's T items and the exit are marked after class; spoken T items a child was not heard on stay blank for day 33.
