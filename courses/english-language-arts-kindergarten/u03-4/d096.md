# Day 96: Plan the first animal piece

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the bird-nest note ahead (a bird in a nest on a branch, sticker 06, page 3). Write labels for deer, woodpecker and chipmunk. Keep C2a, C2b and C2c unseen. Cover E96. Choose six children for the day's talk and six for onset and rime. Read aloud: "I can plan my animal piece" and "Write the letters I say." The cards are made up for practice; say so each time.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Review: the day 94 sentence, "A bird needs the oak because it eats the acorns and the branches hold its nest." Say it with the class. Say source, detail, information and check with a gesture each: a finger on a sticker, a small pinch, an open book, a pointing finger on the line. Show a squirrel, a nut, an oak and an acorn. If many marked the table alone or the oak alone (error B) or named three things with no link (error A) on day 95's exit, spend three minutes on the repair: hold up a person drawing from day 95 and trace the person, what the tree gives and the arrow with a finger, saying "so" at the arrow.

#### 10 min. Reread two sources

Whole class. Review: The Oak (day 92) and Who Depends on a Tree? (day 90). Reread page 3 of The Oak and the squirrel page of Who Depends on a Tree?. **Ask.** "Which facts answer our question: how does an animal depend on a tree?" Expect: a squirrel eats acorns; it finds nuts and seeds in the tree. Children point to each sentence as you reread it. Write the two facts on chart paper with a source sticker each.

#### 5 min. The wrong note

Whole class, then pairs. The puppet puts the bird-nest note beside the squirrel facts. **Ask.** "What changes if we use the wrong animal's note?" Pairs say why the nest note does not belong. Expect: it is about a bird, not the squirrel. No child is the example of a mistake; the puppet made it. Signs: a fact that answers a different question (error E).

#### 10 min. Model a plan

Whole class. On the chart, plan one piece for a jay from The Oak, page 3, and say each step aloud: the animal (draw a jay, add the label jay), one fact ("A jay eats acorns from the oak."), the source sticker (The Oak, page 3). Write at the top, "My piece names the animal, one fact and the card." Leave the chart up for days 97 to 99. This models the steps only; no source card is used and no answer for the children's cards is shown.

#### 5 min. Partners select evidence

Pairs. Each pair points to one sentence in The Oak, page 3, or in Who Depends on a Tree? that gives a fact about an animal other than the jay (the squirrel, the bird or the deer). Walk past each pair and ask them to say the animal and put a finger on the sentence. Signs: pointing to a picture instead of a sentence.

#### 15 min. First animal piece (R1)

Individuals. Say aloud: "These cards are made up for practice." Read C2a, C2b and C2c once each with their pictures, about a minute in all. Each child takes the picture copy of one card, draws the animal on a drawing card, takes the label you wrote for its name, says or writes one fact, and puts a source sticker with the card's letter on it. Draw for five minutes. Take dictation at about 25 seconds a child: in seven minutes you reach about sixteen children; take the rest during the next part. Children who write need no help with spelling. No answer is modelled: cover the jay plan on the chart and leave only its top line showing. Signs: an animal drawn with no name (error C); a fact the card does not give (error D); a fact from another card (error AI).

#### 5 min. Show your card

Partners. Review: the arrow and so (day 95). Partners tell each other the animal, the fact and the card. Finish the dictation for any child not reached. Collect the pieces. After class, write one line of feedback on a slip for each piece and staple it on: name the animal, add one fact from a card, or show the card. Read each slip aloud tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E96 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X96a]]: children point to one picture. Mark the day's six only, then ask them [[s03-X96b]], 30 seconds each, while the class puts its pieces away. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a plan with only one source (error B); a fact that is not about the tree (error E); the facts swapped between sources (error AI).

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Whole class, then groups. Show the three slips from the cycle 14 check that most children made: not joining the parts, guessing from the picture, remapping is or it. Model each for three minutes, whole class, on tiles. Groups of the children who made each slip then work four minutes on it; visit the two largest groups for two minutes each while the others work in pairs with tiles. Retry for three minutes with a new word or picture, not the card of the check. Signs: guessing the word from the picture (error R); cannot join /f/ /an/ (error AF); says the changed letter is the vowel when the end changed (error L); a short i word read with no meaning (error K); is or it not read without remapping (error S).

#### 4 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: first, middle and last sounds with counters (day 94). Say bus and gum; children push a counter for each sound and say the first, the middle and the last. Sign: dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 8 min. New: short u against a and i

Whole class. Say sun slowly and push a counter for each sound: /s/ /u/ /n/. Say: "The letter u says /u/ as in sun." Write s-u-n under the counters and blend it. Add u to the map with a sun picture. Chain hat, hit, hut on tiles and ask which sound changed each time. Expect: the middle one. Signs: writing i for the vowel in sun (error U); naming the first sound when the middle changed (error L).

#### 4 min. Words was and his

Whole class. Review: is and it (day 91). Map was: /w/ /u/ /z/, and mark the a as the part to remember. Map his: /h/ /i/ /z/, with the s saying /z/. Read each on its card, then in "It was fun." Sign: cannot read was or his without remapping (error S).

#### 8 min. Gus in a Tub, reader 3

Pairs. Read the title only. Partners take turns reading one sentence a page with the picture covered and say what happened. Walk past six pairs. Signs: guessing the word from the picture (error R).

#### 4 min. Tree Words, verse 4

Whole class. First hearing of verse 4, adult-read. Children join the actions: flick a hand for the tail, run two fingers up an arm, hop. Find the first line in the large print and point to was.

#### 22 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each on onset and rime: blend /r/ /ug/ and split rug into its first sound and the rest. Hear five other children two minutes each on tracking print, using the verse in large print: track the first line, turn the page, point to was, put a finger between two words. Both are notes on the record sheet, not items. The rest reread Gus in a Tub and chain one more word on tiles in pairs. Signs: cannot join /r/ and /ug/ (error AF); pointing across a space to read two words as one (error AB); reading the second line before the first (error AJ).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the cards one at a time, let the child choose, and do not model an answer. Spell sun with buttons. One projector: show the sources, the chart and the cards on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No stickers: write the card's letter in a circle.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the verse.

- **10 min. Plan a piece.** Read twice: "An oak grows acorns. A squirrel eats acorns." Your child draws the squirrel; you label it.
  **Say.** "What is one fact, and which card did it come from?" Expect: it eats acorns, from the card you read.
- **10 min. Short u.** Say sun; your child pushes a button for each sound and writes a letter for each.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads was and his on cards.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads "A cub can tug a rug."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X96a]] and [[s03-X96b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "Source A: A robin builds its nest in a tree. Source B: A robin sits on its eggs in the nest." Draw a robin on a nest in a tree labelled A and B, a robin on the ground labelled A, and a tree with no robin labelled B.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sources, the model, the first piece, the exit and short u; cut the verse and the print tracking. Longer: a secure child takes a second card and adds a second fact with its card.

### Words and supports

Words: source, detail, information, check. Frame: "My animal is a ___. It ___. I found it on card ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick a plan that uses both sources and say what to take from each. If many marked a plan with one source (error B), gave a fact that is not about the tree (error E) or swapped the sources (error AI), day 97 opens with five minutes: match each fact to its source. Day 97 returns each piece with its line of feedback; children 1 to 10 explain their pieces in groups of five. Foundations go on with short u.
