# Day 95: A person needs, the tree gives

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the chart question "Who needs trees?" and the three-box chart. Cover E95 and the set 14 check card. Pick six children not yet heard for the talk on text 04 and six for onset and rime. Read aloud: "I can show what a person gets from a tree" and "Write the words I say." Nothing on the student page is read by the children.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Review: depend, to need something (day 90). Say person, wood, shade and depend with a gesture each: a hand on the chest, a stack of planks, a hand over the eyes, two hands leaning together. Point to the chart question "Who needs trees?" and to the note picture on the research-cycle wall. If many said two notes with no link on day 94's exit (error A), spend four minutes on the repair: the puppet says "A squirrel. A nut." Ask the class to join them with because or so. Expect: "The squirrel eats the nut, so it needs the tree." Say it again with the arrow card between the two.

#### 10 min. Read 04 whole

Whole class. Read We Depend on Trees aloud once, without music, pausing at each picture. The class joins the repeated line, "We depend on trees." At each page children point to the person and say what the person needs. No outside tune. Signs: guessing a need the page does not give.

#### 5 min. What do people obtain?

Whole class. **Ask.** "What do people get from trees?" Children point to the picture on page 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 that answers. Expect: wood, shade, apples, paper, syrup. Write each word beside its picture on chart paper.

#### 10 min. Model the link (RI.K.3)

Whole class. Use page 3 only. Draw the child, then the shade, then an arrow from the child's need to the tree. Say: "The child needs shade, so the tree gives shade." Point to the arrow as you say so. Then show how the repeated line explains it: "We depend on trees" means we need what trees give. Read the sentence again and ask children to say it with you, hand on the arrow. Leave the model on the wall. **Ask.** "How does the repeated line link people and trees?" Expect: people need things, and trees give them. Signs: three things named and none joined (error A).

#### 15 min. Connect and note (W.K.7, W.K.8)

Pairs. Each pair picks a different page from 4, 5 or 6 (apples, paper or syrup) than the one next to them, draws the person, what the tree gives and an arrow on a picture note card, puts a source sticker on it (text 04 and the page), and says the connection with the frame. Draw eight minutes. While they work, hear six children not yet heard on text 04, two minutes each: "What is this text mostly about?" and "Who needs a tree on your page?" Write the child in the heard column; this is a note, not an item. Signs: a picture of the tree alone (error B); a list with no link (error A).

#### 10 min. Say the chant

Whole class, then individuals. Speak the whole chant together, "We depend on trees," a page at a time. Then each child draws one person of their own choice and what the tree gives that person on a drawing card, with an arrow. Say aloud when you collect the cards: "This keeps the arrow going from the tree to the person." Keep the cards for the wall.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E95 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X95a]]: children point to one picture. Mark the day's six only, then ask them [[s03-X95b]], 30 seconds each, while the others finish their cards. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the boat alone or the cedar alone (error B); three things named with no link (error A); a fact the card does not give, such as eating seeds (error D).

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: first and last sounds with counters (day 85). Say /h/ /it/, /p/ /it/ and /s/ /it/; children say the word, then push a counter for each sound. Sign: dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 8 min. Short i, again

Whole class. Review: short i (day 91). Build sit, hit and lip from tiles, a sound for each tile, then the word. Chain pin, pit, pig: change the last sound each time, and the class names the changed sound. Sign: naming the vowel as the changed letter when only the last sound changed (error L).

#### 10 min. A Pig in a Pit, reader 2

Small groups. Review: A Pig in a Pit (days 91 to 94). Six groups of five reread with the picture covered and say what happened. Walk past each group. Signs: guessing the word from the picture (error R).

#### 7 min. Spell and change

Whole class. Review: counters for spelling (day 85). Dictate dip and fit; children push a counter for each sound and write a letter for each, on side 2. Then dictate bit and ask them to change one letter to make big and say the changed sound. This is the written half of the cycle 14 check: do not teach. Mark nothing now; look at the papers after class. Sign: writing a for the vowel (error U).

#### 30 min. Rotation and cycle 14 check

Table and floor stations. Hear six children two minutes each on onset and rime: 90 seconds on [[s03-P4c]] (blend /f/ /an/, then split fan) and 30 seconds reading the covered set 14 check card, "The kid is in that big bin.", cold. Hear the other twenty-four at 35 seconds each, 14 minutes in all: they read the check card cold, then the cards for is and it without sounding out. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; write notes only. The rest reread the reader and chain words in pairs at the floor stations. No reteaching today; repair waits for day 96. Signs: cannot join /f/ and /an/ (error AF); cannot read is or it without remapping (error S).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read pages 3 and 4 of the chant, draw the arrow together, and spell dip and fit with buttons. One projector: show 04 and the chart on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The chant, the pair drawings, the reader and the check.

- **10 min. The chant.** Read aloud: "A child needs shade on a hot day. The tree gives shade." Your child draws it.
  **Say.** "What does the child need, and what does the tree give?" Expect: shade, and the tree gives it.
- **10 min. Another one.** Say: "A family needs fruit to eat." Your child draws it.
  **Say.** "Say it with so." Expect: the family needs fruit, so the tree gives apples.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say dip and fit; your child pushes a button for each sound and writes a letter for each.
- **5 min. Read.** Your child reads is and it on cards.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X95a]] and [[s03-X95b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "A baker needs a wooden spoon. A spoon maker uses wood from a maple tree to make it." Draw the maker with an arrow to the spoon and baker, a spoon alone, a maple alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the pair drawing, the exit and the rotation check; cut the chant drawing. Longer: a secure child draws a second person on another page and says it with so.

### Words and supports

Words: person, wood, shade, depend. Frame: "The ___ needs ___, so the tree gives ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the drawing that joins a person to the tree and say how. If many marked the boat alone or the cedar alone (error B) or named three things with no link (error A), day 96 opens with five minutes: trace the person, what the tree gives and the arrow. Day 96 uses two provided texts to plan an animal-needs piece with evidence, and starts short u. The cycle 14 notes set its repair groups.
