# Feedback slips

A small slip to be stapled to the animal piece on day 96 (after the piece is made), and read to the child. One slip, one next step.

Name ______________________

My next step is (circle one):

- Name the animal.
- Add one fact from a card.
- Show the card.

The teacher writes the step and reads it aloud. The child adds it on the next piece day. The slip is kept with the piece and the revised piece so the two can be compared.

A room with nothing: cut a strip of paper and write the three steps by hand.
