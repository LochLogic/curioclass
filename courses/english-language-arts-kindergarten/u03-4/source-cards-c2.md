# Source cards C2a, C2b and C2c

Three unused cards offered on day 96 for the first animal piece. They are not modelled and their answers are not shown. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each card.

## Card C2a

**Read:** "A pear tree grows fruit. A deer eats fruit that falls. The deer gets food from the pear tree."

**Picture:** a pear tree with fruit on the ground and a deer eating one.

## Card C2b

**Read:** "A woodpecker digs a hole in a tree trunk. The hole is the woodpecker's nest. The woodpecker has a home in the tree."

**Picture:** a woodpecker at a hole in a tree trunk.

## Card C2c

**Read:** "A hazel tree grows nuts. A chipmunk climbs it for nuts. The chipmunk saves the nuts for winter."

**Picture:** a hazel tree with nuts and a chipmunk carrying one.
