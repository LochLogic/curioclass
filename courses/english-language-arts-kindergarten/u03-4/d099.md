# Day 99: Check: explain the animal piece (children 21 to 30)

## Teacher page

### Before class

Children 21 to 30 are today's groups. Copy day 98's items onto the record sheet first, including any mark for a missing link (error A). Put C2a, C2b and C2c on the group table. Put the pieces, each with its slip, in roster order. Read aloud: "I can show my animal and its card" and "Write the letters I say." Nothing is taught today. The cards are made up for practice; say so when you read them.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Say source, detail, information and check with a gesture each. Read the chart of what a good piece names: the animal, one fact, the card. No repair today; it is a check day.

#### 10 min. Revise (all thirty)

Whole class. Return each piece with its slip. Pass along the rows and read each slip's step quietly to its child, about eight seconds each. Each child changes one named thing: adds the animal's name, adds one fact from a card, or shows the card. No reteaching. Signs: a piece handed back unchanged after the slip (error AA).

#### 20 min. R1 groups, children 21 to 30

Two groups of five at your table, ten minutes each: two on contribution, three on source information, three on the earlier writing, two to move. The other twenty write one more fact for the same piece at their tables; this is unscored.

- **Contribution, two minutes.** Ask each child in turn [[s03-R99a]], about 24 seconds each.
- **Source information, three minutes.** With the cards on the table, ask each child [[s03-R99b]], about 36 seconds each.
- **The earlier writing, three minutes.** Look at the revised piece with each child for [[s03-R99c]], about 36 seconds each.

Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Read a card again, twice in all, if a child asks. Signs: nothing added to the inquiry (error F); a fact that does not answer the question, such as "I like deer" (error E); the animal left unnamed (error C); a fact the card does not give (error D); a piece unchanged after the slip (error AA). No first-taught work and no same-day product is scored. Name no child as an example.

#### 15 min. Reread

Whole class. No new teaching. Reread the squirrel, bird and deer pages of Who Depends on a Tree?, about three minutes. Then read C2a, C2b and C2c again, once each. After each card, children who chose it hold up their piece and point to the line on the picture copy that holds their fact.

#### 5 min. Notebook

Individuals. Each child adds one drawn detail to a copy of the three boxes (the animal, what it needs, where I heard it) and says its source to a partner. Collect the notebook pages and the student pages when the part ends.

#### 5 min. Close

Whole class, then partners. Lay all thirty pieces on the floor. Children walk past in a line, stop at a piece and tell the child beside them one thing they learned about an animal and a tree; then three volunteers share with the class. No exit on a check day.

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: onset and rime (unit 1, day 24) and short u (day 96). Blend /b/ /un/, /r/ /ug/ and /t/ /ub/; children say each word, then split it into the first sound and the rest. Sign: cannot join the parts (error AF).

#### 10 min. Short u against a and i

Whole class. Review: short u (day 96) and short i (day 91). Dictate hat, hit and hut. For each, push a counter for each sound and write the vowel letter on side 2. Partners check each other's letters against the counters. Signs: writing i or a for the vowel in hut (error U); naming the changed letter as h when the middle changed (error L).

#### 12 min. Gus in a Tub, reader 3

Pairs. Review: Gus in a Tub (days 96 to 98). Partners reread with the picture covered and say what happened and what Gus did. Hear six children, a minute each, and record who read each page without guessing from the picture. Signs: guessing the word from the picture (error R).

#### 8 min. Change one letter

Pairs. Review: change one letter and name the changed sound (day 96). Each pair picks one of three chain cards, cap to cup to cut, bat to bit to but, or tab to tub to tug, builds it on tiles and names the changed sound each time. Sign: saying the changed letter is the first one when the middle changed (error L).

#### 6 min. Words was and his

Whole class. Review: was and his (day 96). Read each on its card. Then read aloud "His mug was big." and ask children to hold up the his card when they hear his.

#### 4 min. Tree Words, verse 4

Whole class. Review: verse 4 (day 96). Adult-read only; no new teaching. Clap the two-syllable word: squirrel.

#### 15 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each reading short u words from cards: cup, bun, sun and tug. Write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest read set 15 cards 1 to 5 in pairs and say who did what. Signs: i for u when reading cup (error U); guessing from the picture (error R).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the card twice, ask the three prompts, and reread the reader. Spell hut with buttons. One projector: show the card and the reader on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the stations.

- **15 min. The assessment: the piece.** Read twice: "A boy swings on a rope tied to a branch. The branch holds the rope." Your child draws the boy and the branch.
  **Say.** "What is one fact, and which card has it?" Expect: the branch holds the rope, from the card you read.
- **10 min. Vowels.** Say hat, hit and hut; your child writes the vowel letter for each.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads was and his on cards.
- **10 min. Change.** Chain cap, cup, cut on squares; your child names the changed sound.
- **5 min. Blend.** Say /b/ /un/; your child says the word.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision, the groups and the spelling; cut the notebook and the verse. Longer: a secure child finds a second fact on the same card and shows it.

### Words and supports

Words: source, detail, information, check. Frame: "My animal is a ___. It ___. I found it on card ___." The cards are read aloud twice. A child may point, draw or answer privately, and a scribe records without adding ideas. Use an agreed access method rather than a default "not yet seen".

### Tomorrow

All thirty children have now been heard on the animal piece. Marks for no added fact, an answer that is not about the question, an unnamed animal, an unsupported fact and a piece unchanged after the slip go to the day 101 repair groups. Day 100 shares the animal piece and compares its statements with the texts that support it; copy day 99's items onto the record sheet first.
