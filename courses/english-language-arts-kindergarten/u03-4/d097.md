# Day 97: Check: explain the animal piece (children 1 to 10)

## Teacher page

### Before class

Children 1 to 10 are today's groups. Put C2a, C2b and C2c on the group table and bring the unit 3 record sheet. Put the pieces, each with its slip, in roster order. Read aloud: "I can show my animal and its card" and "Write the vowel letter." Nothing is taught today. The cards are made up for practice; say so when you read them.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Say source, detail, information and check with a gesture each. Read the chart of what a good piece names: the animal, one fact, the card. If many marked a plan with one source (error B), gave a fact that is not about the tree (error E) or swapped the sources (error AI) on day 96's exit, spend four minutes on the repair: the puppet says the squirrel eats acorns, from Who Depends on a Tree? page 3. Ask the class to match each fact to its source: the squirrel and acorns are on The Oak, page 3, and the nest held by branches is on Who Depends on a Tree?, page 3.

#### 10 min. Revise (all thirty)

Whole class. Return each piece with its slip. Pass along the rows and read each slip's step quietly to its child, about eight seconds each. Each child changes one named thing: adds the animal's name, adds one fact from a card, or shows the card. No reteaching. Signs: a piece handed back unchanged after the slip (error AA).

#### 20 min. R1 groups, children 1 to 10

Two groups of five at your table, ten minutes each: two on contribution, three on source information, three on the earlier writing, two to move. The other twenty write one more fact for the same piece at their tables with the picture copy of their card; this is unscored.

- **Contribution, two minutes.** Ask each child in turn [[s03-R97a]], about 24 seconds each.
- **Source information, three minutes.** With the cards on the table, ask each child [[s03-R97b]], about 36 seconds each.
- **The earlier writing, three minutes.** Look at the revised piece with each child for [[s03-R97c]], about 36 seconds each.

Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Read a card again, twice in all, if a child asks. Signs: nothing added to the inquiry (error F); a fact that does not answer the question, such as "I like deer" (error E); the animal left unnamed (error C); a fact the card does not give (error D); a piece unchanged after the slip (error AA). Name no child as an example.

#### 15 min. Reread

Whole class. No new teaching. Reread The Oak, page 3, and the three pages of Who Depends on a Tree? about a squirrel, a bird and a deer, about five minutes. Then read C2a, C2b and C2c again, once each. After each card, children who chose it hold up their piece and point to the line on the picture copy that holds their fact.

#### 5 min. Notebook

Individuals. Each child adds one drawn detail to a copy of the three boxes (the animal, what it needs, where I heard it) and says its source to a partner. Collect the notebook pages and the student pages when the part ends.

#### 5 min. Close

Partners. Children 1 to 10 tell a partner their piece in one sentence: the animal, the fact, the card. No exit on a check day.

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: first and last sounds with counters (day 85). Say nut, rub and hum; children push a counter for each sound and say the first and the last. Sign: dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 10 min. Short u, again

Whole class. Review: short u (day 96). Build hat, hit and hut from tiles with the class, a sound for each tile. For each word call on three children in turn to say the middle sound, about 15 seconds each. Signs: writing i for the vowel in hut (error U); naming the first sound when the middle changed (error L).

#### 12 min. Gus in a Tub, reader 3

Pairs. Review: Gus in a Tub (day 96). Partners reread with the picture covered and say what happened. Hear six children, a minute each, and record who read each page without guessing from the picture. Signs: guessing the word from the picture (error R).

#### 8 min. Change one letter

Pairs. Review: change one letter and name the changed sound (day 96). Chain hat to hit to hut on tiles and say which sound changed each time. Expect: the middle one. Sign: saying the changed letter is h (error L).

#### 6 min. Words was and his

Whole class. Review: was and his (day 96). Read each on its card. Find was on page 8 of the reader. His is not in the reader: find it on set 15 card 3, "His mug was big." Sign: cannot read was or his without remapping (error S).

#### 4 min. Tree Words, verse 4

Whole class. Review: verse 4 (day 96). Adult-read only; no new teaching. Find the first line in the large print.

#### 15 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each reading short u words from cards: bus, rug, nut and hum. Write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest read set 15 cards 1 to 5 in pairs and say who did what. Signs: i for u when reading hut (error U); guessing from the picture (error R).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the card twice, ask the three prompts, and reread the reader. Read hat, hit and hut on paper squares. One projector: show the sources and the cards on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the stations.

- **15 min. The assessment: the piece.** Read twice: "A bird sits on a branch. The branch holds the bird up." Your child draws the bird and says one fact.
  **Say.** "What is your animal, your fact and your card?" Expect: a bird; the branch holds it up; the card you read.
- **10 min. Short u.** Read hat, hit and hut on squares; your child says the middle sound of each.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads was and his on cards.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads "His mug was big." and says what happened.
- **5 min. Counters.** Say nut; push a button for each sound and say the first and last.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision, the groups and the short u reading; cut the notebook and the verse. Longer: a secure child finds a second fact on the same card and shows it.

### Words and supports

Words: source, detail, information, check. Frame: "My animal is a ___. It ___. I found it on card ___." The cards are read aloud twice. A child may point, draw or answer privately, and a scribe records without adding ideas. Use an agreed access method rather than a default "not yet seen".

### Tomorrow

Day 98 hears children 11 to 20 on the same three items and adds the sapling card C1, reused inside the animal explanation. Copy day 97's items from your group notes first. Marks of no added fact (error F), an answer that is not about the question (error E), an unnamed animal (error C), an unsupported fact (error D) and a piece unchanged after the slip (error AA) go to the day 101 repair groups with the other check days' marks.
