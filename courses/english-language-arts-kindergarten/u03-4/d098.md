# Day 98: Check: explain the animal piece (children 11 to 20)

## Teacher page

### Before class

Children 11 to 20 are today's groups. Copy day 97's items onto the record sheet first. Put C2a, C2b and C2c on the group table with page 3 of A Tree Is a Living Thing (card C1) marked beside them. Put the pieces, each with its slip, in roster order. Read aloud: "I can show my animal and its card" and "Write the letters I say." Nothing is taught today. The cards are made up for practice; say so when you read them.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Say source, detail, information and check with a gesture each. Read the chart of what a good piece names: the animal, one fact, the card. No repair today; it is a check day.

#### 10 min. Revise (all thirty)

Whole class. Return each piece with its slip. Pass along the rows and read each slip's step quietly to its child, about eight seconds each. Each child changes one named thing: adds the animal's name, adds one fact from a card, or shows the card. No reteaching. Signs: a piece handed back unchanged after the slip (error AA).

#### 20 min. R1 groups, children 11 to 20

Two groups of five at your table, ten minutes each: two on contribution, three on source information, three on the earlier writing, two to move. The other twenty write one more fact for the same piece at their tables; this is unscored.

- **Contribution, two minutes.** Ask each child in turn [[s03-R98a]], about 24 seconds each.
- **Source information, three minutes.** With the cards on the table, ask each child [[s03-R98b]], about 36 seconds each. As the child explains the animal and the fact, listen for the link between what the tree gives and what the animal needs, for [[s03-R98dv2]]; this takes no extra minutes. Record only a missing link. If a child names the animal and the tree with no link (error A), read page 3 of A Tree Is a Living Thing (card C1) once, and ask again how the tree helps the animal; mark that child shown with help.
- **The earlier writing, three minutes.** Look at the revised piece with each child for [[s03-R98c]], about 36 seconds each.

Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Read a card again, twice in all, if a child asks. Signs: nothing added to the inquiry (error F); a fact that does not answer the question, such as "I like deer" (error E); the animal left unnamed (error C); a fact the card does not give (error D); a piece unchanged after the slip (error AA). Name no child as an example.

#### 15 min. Reread

Whole class. No new teaching. Reread The Oak, page 3 (the acorns) and page 5 (the branches), about three minutes. Then read C2a, C2b and C2c again, once each. After each card, children who chose it hold up their piece and point to the line on the picture copy that holds their fact.

#### 5 min. Notebook

Individuals. Each child adds one drawn detail to a copy of the three boxes (the animal, what it needs, where I heard it) and says its source to a partner. Collect the notebook pages and the student pages when the part ends.

#### 5 min. Close

Partners. Children 11 to 20 tell a partner their piece in one sentence: the animal, the fact, the card. No exit on a check day.

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: onset and rime (unit 1, day 24). Blend /h/ /ut/, then /k/ /ut/; children say each word, then split it into the first sound and the rest. Sign: cannot join the parts (error AF).

#### 10 min. Short u spelling

Whole class, then partners. Review: counters for spelling (day 85) and short u (day 96). Dictate sun, hut and cup. For each, push a counter for each sound and write a letter for each. Partners check each other's letters against the counters. Write on side 2. Signs: writing i for the vowel (error U); dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 12 min. Gus in a Tub, reader 3

Pairs. Review: Gus in a Tub (days 96 and 97). Partners reread with the picture covered and say what happened. Hear six children, a minute each, and record who read each page without guessing from the picture. Signs: guessing the word from the picture (error R).

#### 8 min. Change one letter

Pairs. Review: change one letter and name the changed sound (day 96). Chain cap, cup and cut on tiles and say which sound changed each time. Expect: the middle, then the last. Sign: saying the changed letter is c (error L).

#### 6 min. Words was and his

Whole class. Review: was and his (day 96). Read each on its card.

#### 4 min. Tree Words, verse 4

Whole class. Review: verse 4 (day 96). Adult-read only; no new teaching. Find the two words that rhyme: run and fun.

#### 15 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each spelling bun and rug with counters and a letter for each sound. Write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest read set 15 cards 1 to 5 in pairs and say who did what. Signs: dropping the middle sound (error V); i for u (error U).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the card twice, ask the three prompts, and reread the reader. Spell sun and hut with buttons. One projector: show the card and the reader on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the stations.

- **15 min. The assessment: the link.** Read twice: "A cat naps in the shade of a tree. The leaves make the shade." Your child draws the cat and the tree.
  **Say.** "How does the tree help the cat?" Expect: it gives shade, so the cat can rest.
  **Say.** "What is one fact, and which card has it?" Expect: the leaves make shade, from the card you read.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say sun, hut and cup; your child pushes a button for each sound and writes a letter for each.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads was and his on cards.
- **10 min. Change.** Chain cap, cup, cut on squares; your child names the changed sound.
- **5 min. Blend.** Say /h/ /ut/; your child says the word.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision, the groups and the spelling; cut the notebook and the verse. Longer: a secure child finds a second fact on the same card and shows it.

### Words and supports

Words: source, detail, information, check. Frame: "My animal is a ___. It ___. I found it on card ___." The cards are read aloud twice. A child may point, draw or answer privately, and a scribe records without adding ideas. Use an agreed access method rather than a default "not yet seen".

### Tomorrow

Day 99 hears children 21 to 30 on the same items. Copy day 98's items from your group notes first. Marks of no link (error A), no added fact (error F), an answer that is not about the question (error E), an unnamed animal (error C), an unsupported fact (error D) and a piece unchanged after the slip (error AA) go to the day 101 repair groups with the other check days' marks.
