# Picture sort cards: rhymes, onset and rime, and sh, th, ch

Draw each picture on its own card, thick line, one object a card, with no printed word. Use them for spoken work only; children name the picture and listen. Twenty-four cards in all.

## Rhyme and onset-rime (twelve cards)

cat, hat, mat, bat; sun, run, bun; pen, hen, ten; log, dog.

Use: put two cards down and ask "Do these end the same?" Then keep the ending and change the start: "cat, hat, mat: keep the end, change the start."

## The digraph sorts (twelve cards)

- sh: ship, shell, shop, shoe.
- th: thumb, thorn, three, thistle.
- ch: chair, cheese, chin, chick.

Use: say the word and its first sound; place the card under the sound card (/sh/, /th/ or /ch/). Children say the sound; the teacher shows the two letters that spell it on the sound-spelling map. Printed words are never read from these cards.

A room with nothing: draw each picture on a scrap of paper and use three boxes as the sorting mats.
