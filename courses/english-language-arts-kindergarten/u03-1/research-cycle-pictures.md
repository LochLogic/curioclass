# Research-cycle wall pictures

Four large pictures, one on each of four sheets, hung left to right on the classroom wall on day 80 and kept up through day 118. They are the same four pictures every day; children point to the one the class is on.

1. **Ask.** A child with a hand raised and a question mark above the head. Say: "We ask a question."
2. **Find.** A child looking closely at a page with a pointing finger on a sentence. Say: "We find the answer in a text."
3. **Note.** A child drawing a small picture with a label line under it. Say: "We make a note."
4. **Share.** Two children facing each other, one speaking, one listening, a talking token between them. Say: "We share what we found."

Draw in thick black line on white; one printed word under each picture (ask, find, note, share), in large letters for the teacher to read aloud. On day 80 the class is on "ask"; the chart grows a check mark under each step as the unit goes on. No picture shows a real child.

A room with nothing: draw four boxes on a long strip of paper.
