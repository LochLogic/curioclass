# Day 83: Make a labelled note

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 82's exit marks. Set out the tree photographs. Decide the optional visit (below): check family allergy plans for pollen and nuts first, and if the route or the weather is not safe, use the photograph route. Set out the labelled note pages and cover the exit card. Read aloud to children: the student page says "I can draw and label what a tree needs" and "Write sh, th or ch."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Do the one or two repairs most children needed, five minutes at most; the puppet says each wrong example. From day 82's exit:

- **If many gave a reason the reading does not give (error D).** The puppet says "A plant grows because it is pretty." Point to the sentence in the reading that says why; set aside the part it does not say.
- **If many said the roots eat soil (error Q).** Show page 3: water goes up, light and air come in, and the leaf makes the food. Say it: soil is not the tree's food.
- **If many left two facts unjoined (error A).** Put an arrow card between two facts and say the joining word.

#### 15 min. Reread 01b pages 2 to 4

Whole class. Reread the three pages with their pictures (about 270 words, about two minutes, with pauses). Children listen for water first, then for air and light; raise a hand each time one is said. Say "minerals" yourself and show its picture; no child defines it. Say aloud: "Roots take in water. Leaves use light and air." Children say it back.

#### 15 min. Talk and picture look

Pairs, then whole class. Talking token, one turn each, pass allowed. **Ask.** "What water detail is stated?" Expect: the rain sinks into the soil and the roots take in the water. Children point to the picture detail that matches. **Ask.** "What does its explanation say about air and light?" Expect: the leaves take in air and catch light, and they use them to make food. Children point to the matching picture. Pairs talk one minute for each question; hear six children, 30 seconds each, after each. Optional, 10 minutes of this part: a visit to a tree within a two-minute walk on the accessible route, on a dry day, after family allergy plans are checked. One adult stays at the tree and one with the line. Touch only; nothing tasted, picked or taken. Three groups of ten stand at the trunk for two minutes each. If anything is unsafe or not reachable, use your tree photographs in the same minutes and look for the trunk, the branches and the leaves.

#### 20 min. Model and make a labelled note

Whole class, then individuals. Model on the chart (4 minutes): draw a tree with roots and water arrows; write the label tree; dictate "Roots take in water." Point back to the sentence on page 2 it came from. Then each child makes a note on the labelled note page (16 minutes): a drawing, a label and one fact from What a Tree Needs. Children choose a fact from the pages, a different one from yours where they can: the leaves catch light; the leaves take in air; the tree grows taller; soil is not the tree's food. You write each child's label and dictated fact; take the day's six first, then others as time allows, about 30 seconds each. A child may point to the sentence it came from. Spelling by sound is never marked down. Collect the notes; written feedback follows after class, and the note is revised on day 85. Look-fors: the topic is named; the fact is accurate and from the reading; the drawing shows the fact; the child can point to the sentence it came from. Sign: a drawing with no word for its topic (error C).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E83 and read it twice. Ask [[s03-X83a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X83b]], 30 seconds each, while the class draws. Signs: a drawing that does not show the stated detail (error B); a drawing with no word for its topic (error C); a fact the reading does not give (error D).

#### 8 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Clap the parts of picture words: tree (one), monkey (two), elephant (three). Review: clapping syllables, the beats of a word (unit 1, day 18). Spoken only; no counters.

#### 12 min. Sort the digraphs

Pairs. Three bins marked sh, th and ch. Each pair has six picture cards; they say each word, say its first sound and place it in a bin. Use the sound-spelling map for the two letters. Walk past the pairs. Printed words are never read from the cards.

#### 8 min. Letters H to N

Pairs. Review: letter names, both cases (unit 2). Pairs name shuffled upper and lowercase cards for H to N, then match each capital to its small letter. Hear six pairs, about a minute each; write who was heard on the record sheet. Signs: a capital named but not its small letter (error AC).

#### 7 min. Words to and do

Whole class. Show the cards to and do; children read each whole, without sounding it out. Name the part to remember: o says /oo/. Review: the, a and in are read whole.

#### 12 min. Tree Words, verse 1

Whole class. Reread with the actions. Find the two words that rhyme by ear, tall and small; children say them and a third word that ends the same.

#### 13 min. Rotation

Table and stations. Hear six different children, two minutes each: "Which rhymes with log: dog or bun?" then "Say one more." Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Sign: picks a rhyme but cannot make another (error AD). The others write sh, th or ch for the starting sound of three picture words, shop, three and cheese, and place each picture. One minute to write who was heard.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread pages 2 to 4, make the note together on the labelled note page and write the label and the fact. One projector: show pages 2 to 4 on one screen; the child still draws on paper. No visit: use the photographs.

### At home

**Swap.** No book: describe one tree you know. No note page: rule a box and two lines on paper.

**Skip.** The visit, the sort and the rotation.

- **10 min. A note.** Your child draws a tree and its roots and tells you its name; you write the label.
  **Say.** "What fact do you want to write beside it?" Expect: roots take in water, or leaves use light.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say shop, three and cheese; your child says the first sound and writes sh, th or ch.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child names H to N, big and small.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X83a]] and [[s03-X83b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "A goat climbs rocks. So it reaches the high grass." Draw a goat high on a rock with the word goat, a goat on flat ground with the word goat, and a goat high on a rock with no word. Ask which drawing and label would teach this page.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the note and the exit; cut the sort to six cards. Longer: a secure child adds a second fact beside the first.

### Words and supports

Words: need, root, leaf, air. Frames: "My tree has ___." "Roots take in ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick a drawing and label that teach a page. If many left a topic unnamed (error C) or marked a drawing that does not show the detail (error B), day 84 opens with five minutes: name the picture first, aloud, then dictate a label and you write it. Day 84 reads a story about one tree across many years.
