# Day 82: A tree needs water, so its roots take it in

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 81's exit marks. Write feedback on each A to G strip (a star for a readable letter, a dot to form again). Draw the lunch picture card. Set out each pair's first, next and then cards (rain on soil; roots take in water; water goes up). Mark page 5 of What a Tree Needs for the exit. Read aloud to children: the student page says "I can say because for two facts" and "Print the letter for the sound."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Do one repair, five minutes at most; the puppet says each wrong example. From day 81's exit, the more common of two errors:

- **If many said two facts side by side on day 81's exit (error A).** The puppet says: "A seed gets rain. A seed sprouts." Put an arrow card between the two facts and say the joining word: "The seed gets rain, so it sprouts." Children say it with you.
- **If many marked an arrow to something the words do not say (error B).** The puppet draws an arrow from a seed to a hat. Reread the exact sentence and trace its object in the picture.

#### 15 min. Read What a Tree Needs (01b), whole

Whole class. Read pages 1 to 6 with the pictures (429 words, about four minutes of reading, with pauses for each picture). Preview need (a hand held out for something), root (fingers wiggling down), water and because as they come. Say "minerals" yourself and show its picture; children do not define it.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, then whole class. Talking token, one turn each, pass allowed. **Ask.** "What do roots take in?" Expect: water, from the soil. Pairs talk one minute; hear six children, 30 seconds each. Say the sentence from page 2 together: "The tree needs water, so its roots take water from the soil." Say it turned around: "Its roots take water from the soil because the tree needs water." Review: because gives a reason (unit 2, day 43). **Ask.** "How do its because words join a need with a process?" Expect: they say what the tree needs and what the roots do about it. Pairs talk one minute; hear six more. Name soil: soil is not the tree's food (error Q). If someone says the roots eat the soil, the puppet says it and you show page 3: water goes up, light and air come in, and the leaf makes the food.

#### 10 min. Bridge: first, next, then

Pairs. Review: first, next and last, retelling in order (unit 2, day 51); today then joins them. Each pair places three picture cards in order and says "First ... next ... then ...": rain falls on soil; roots take in water; water goes up the trunk. Two pairs from different tables swap sets to check. Walk past six pairs and listen. No timeline is built.

#### 10 min. Model the because link and draw

Whole class, then individuals. Model: "The tree needs water, so its roots take water from the soil." Draw the arrow between the two facts. Then each child chooses one of two prepared pairs, from page 3: "The leaves catch light." and "The tree makes food."; from page 4: "The tree uses its food." and "The tree grows." The child draws the arrow and says the link with because or so. Take dictation from the day's six first, 30 seconds each; the others say it to a partner and draw. Nothing is marked.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read page 5 of What a Tree Needs aloud again, its picture covered; the lesson worked pages 2 to 4. Show strip E82 and ask [[s03-X82av2]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X82bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class draws. Signs: an arrow to something the words do not give as the reason (error B); a reason the reading does not give (error D); moving given as the reason (error P); two facts left unjoined (error A).

#### 5 min. Wrap

Whole class. Children place the day's drawings on the research chart under the wall picture "find". Say: "We find answers in a text."

#### 8 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Blend by picture: /ch/ /in/, /ch/ /ick/ and the earlier /sh/ /ip/. Children say the whole word each time.

#### 12 min. New sound: ch

Whole class. Show ch on the sound-spelling map beside sh and th. Two letters, one sound; never /k/ then /h/. Read verse 3 of Two Letters, One Sound twice; children say /ch/. Show chin, chick and lunch: chin and chick start with /ch/, lunch ends with it. Children say each word, trace ch in the air and print it.

#### 8 min. Letters

Individuals. First 3 minutes: hand back the A to G strips; each child forms again any letter with a dot. Sign: a letter that cannot be read (error AK). Then 5 minutes, sound to letter: say /b/, /k/, /d/, /f/, /g/ and /h/ one at a time; each child writes a letter for each on a slip, then partners say each sound from a consonant card. Keep the slips. Signs: the letter name written for the sound (error T); b written for /d/ (error O).

#### 7 min. Words to and do

Whole class. Build each word with letter tiles, say it and tap the sounds. Name the part to remember: o says /oo/. Review: the, a and in are read whole.

#### 12 min. Tree Words, verse 1

Whole class. Reread with the actions. Point to the first line and track left to right. Find to and do.

#### 13 min. Rotation

Table and stations. Hear six different children, two minutes each: "Which rhymes with pen: hen or log?" then "Say one more." Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Sign: picks a rhyme but cannot make another (error AD). The others sort picture words by /ch/ in partners. One minute to write who was heard.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read What a Tree Needs aloud, say the because sentence together, then draw the arrow and write the sounds the child hears. One projector: show the six pictures on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw three on paper. No letter tiles: write letters on scraps.

**Skip.** The pairs, the wrap and the rotation.

- **10 min. Because.** Say: "A plant needs light, so it leans toward the window."
  **Say.** "Say it with because." Expect: it leans toward the window because a plant needs light.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say /b/, /d/ and /f/; your child writes a letter for each.
- **5 min. Order.** Draw three cards: rain falls, roots take in water, water goes up.
  **Say.** "What is first, next and then?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X82av2]] [[s03-X82bv2]] Read page 5 of What a Tree Needs aloud. Your child finishes: "A tree is alive because ..."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the Read, the model and the exit; cut the Wrap to one drawing. Longer: a secure child says a second because sentence about the tree.

### Words and supports

Words: need, root, water, because. Frame: "The tree needs ___ because ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can give the reason a reading states. If many gave a reason the reading does not (error D, or moving, error P), or said the roots eat soil (error Q), or left facts unjoined (error A), day 83 opens with five minutes on the repair: point to the sentence that says it, set aside the part the reading does not give, and show page 3 for soil. Day 83 rereads What a Tree Needs and makes a labelled note.
