# Exit cards, days 80 to 84

Sheets E80, E81, E82 and E84 go with a page of the unit's own texts, read aloud again with its picture covered; they are not made up, so say nothing about practice for them. Each sheet is a strip of three pictures. Card E83 is made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice", read it twice with its picture, and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot only.

The sheets and the card use plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these strips is shown in teaching.

## Day 80 sheet E80: page 2 of A Tree and a Toy

**Read again:** page 2 of A Tree and a Toy (texts/gk-u3-01-a.md), the playground tree.

**Mark (item a):** Which picture shows what this page is all about? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: the playground tree; its roots under the ground; one green leaf.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What would you like to find out about the tree on this page?"

## Day 81 sheet E81: page 6 of What's Alive and What's Not?

**Read again:** page 6 of What's Alive and What's Not? (texts/gk-u3-02.md), the grass and the flowers.

**Mark (item a):** Which picture shows how the two facts on this page go together? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: rain soaking into grass, with an arrow to taller grass; grass with an arrow to a bench; flowers with an arrow to a truck.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "How do the two facts on this page go together?"

## Day 82 sheet E82: page 5 of What a Tree Needs

**Read again:** page 5 of What a Tree Needs (texts/gk-u3-01-b.md), why a tree is alive.

**Mark (item a):** Which picture goes with the word because on this page? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a tree with arrows in for water, air and light, and a seed beside it; a toy truck rolling; a big rock.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Finish my sentence. A tree is alive because ..."

## Day 83 card E83

**Read:** "A whale breathes air. So it swims up to the top of the water."

**Mark (item a):** Which drawing and label would teach this page? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a whale at the top of the water, with the word whale; a whale deep under water, with the word whale; a whale at the top, with no word.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What fact will you tell me to write beside it?"

## Day 84 sheet E84: page 10 of The Tree Beside the Path

**Read again:** page 10 of The Tree Beside the Path (texts/gk-u3-07.md), Sam and the tall tree.

**Mark (item a):** Which two pictures show first and later? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: the small tree at Noor's shoulder, then the tall tree with Sam; the tall tree, then the small tree; two pictures of the small tree.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words tell us that time went by?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 80: item a, The playground tree picture. Not yet: marks the roots or the leaf as the topic (X, a detail named as the topic). Item b, Any question about the playground tree itself, for example: What do its roots do? Not yet: asks something that has nothing to do with trees, for example: Can I go to recess? (E, a wonder unrelated to the class question).
- Day 81: item a, The rain soaking into the grass, with an arrow to taller grass. Not yet: marks the bench or the truck picture (B, an arrow to something the words do not say). Item b, Plants take in water and air, so they grow. Not yet: says two facts side by side, for example: Grass. Flowers. (A, a list with no link).
- Day 82: item a, The tree with arrows in for water, air and light, and a seed beside it. Not yet: marks the rolling truck or the rock (B, an arrow to something the words do not give as the reason). Item b, it needs water, air and light, it grows and it makes seeds (one need or one thing it does is enough). Not yet: says because it is big (D, a reason the reading does not give); says because it moves (P, moving taken as proof of life); says It needs water. It grows. with no joining (A, two facts left unjoined).
- Day 83: item a, The whale at the top of the water, with the word whale. Not yet: marks the deep-water whale (B, a drawing that does not show the stated detail); marks the whale with no word (C, a topic left unnamed). Item b, It comes up to breathe air. Not yet: says It eats fish (D, a fact the reading does not give).
- Day 84: item a, The first pair: the small tree first, the tall tree later. Not yet: marks the pair in the wrong order (Z, first and later reversed); marks the two pictures of the small tree (B, a pair that shows no change). Item b, It was small once. When I was five. Not yet: names words that do not say time passed, for example: How big this tree is (D, not the time words the page gives).
