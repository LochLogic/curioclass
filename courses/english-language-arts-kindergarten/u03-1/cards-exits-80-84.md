# Exit cards, days 80 to 84

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 80 card E80

**Read:** "A pine tree has needles. Its needles are green. Its cones hold seeds."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture shows what this page is all about? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a pine tree; a pine cone; a green needle close up.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What would you like to find out about a pine tree?"

## Day 81 card E81

**Read:** "A fish breathes with gills. Its gills take the oxygen out of the water."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture shows how these two facts go together? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: water flowing into the gills, with an arrow; the fish pointing to a plant, with an arrow; fish food falling toward the mouth, with an arrow.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Tell me how the two facts go together."

## Day 82 card E82

**Read:** "A cactus has thick stems. Its stems hold water. A cactus can live where it hardly ever rains."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture goes with the word because in these facts? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a thick stem full of water, with an arrow to a cactus in dry sand; a cactus with an arrow to a rain cloud; a cactus with an arrow to a thorn.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Say it: A cactus can live where it hardly ever rains because ..."

## Day 83 card E83

**Read:** "A whale breathes air. So it swims up to the top of the water."

**Mark (item a):** Which drawing and label would teach this page? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a whale at the top of the water, with the word whale; a whale deep under water, with the word whale; a whale at the top, with no word.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What fact will you tell me to write beside it?"

## Day 84 card E84

**Read:** "When Omar was five, he could not reach the top shelf. Two years later, he reached the shelf all by himself. Omar was taller."

**Mark (item a):** Which two pictures show first and later? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a small Omar looking up at the shelf, then a taller Omar reaching it; the taller Omar, then the small Omar; two pictures of the small Omar.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words tell us that time went by?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 80: item a, The pine tree picture. Not yet: marks the pine cone or the green needle as the topic (X, a detail named as the topic). Item b, Any question about the pine tree itself, for example: What do its needles do? Not yet: asks something that has nothing to do with trees, for example: Can I go to recess? (E, a wonder unrelated to the class question).
- Day 81: item a, The picture of water flowing into the gills. Not yet: marks the plant picture (B, an arrow to something the words do not say). Item b, The gills take the oxygen out of the water, so the fish gets what it needs to breathe. Not yet: says two facts side by side, for example: A fish breathes. Gills. (A, a list with no link).
- Day 82: item a, The picture of the thick stem full of water with an arrow to the cactus. Not yet: marks the rain cloud or the thorn picture (B, an arrow to something the words do not give as the reason). Item b, its thick stems hold water. Not yet: says because it is green (D, a reason the reading does not give); says It has thick stems. It hardly ever rains. with no joining (A, two facts left unjoined).
- Day 83: item a, The whale at the top of the water, with the word whale. Not yet: marks the deep-water whale (B, a drawing that does not show the stated detail); marks the whale with no word (C, a topic left unnamed). Item b, It comes up to breathe air. Not yet: says It eats fish (D, a fact the reading does not give).
- Day 84: item a, The first pair: small Omar first, taller Omar later. Not yet: marks the pair in the wrong order (Z, first and later reversed); marks the two pictures of the small Omar (B, a pair that shows no change). Item b, Two years later. (With help, when Omar was five.) Not yet: names a word that does not say time passed, for example: shelf (D, not the time words the reading gives).
