# Day 84: A tree changes over many years

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 83's exit marks and the notes handed in. Draw the five cards for the digraph check: sheep, dish, path, chair and peach (none was taught or sorted before). Set the story-map frame on the chart. Mark page 10 of The Tree Beside the Path for the exit. Read aloud to children: the student page says "I can show a tree that grows over years" and "Write sh, th or ch for each sound."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Do one repair, five minutes at most; the puppet makes each mistake. From day 83's exit, the more common of two errors:

- **If many left a topic unnamed on day 83's exit (error C).** The puppet draws a leaf and writes nothing. Children say what it is; name it first, aloud, then dictate a label and you write it.
- **If many marked a drawing that does not show the detail (error B).** Reread the exact sentence from What a Tree Needs and trace its object in the picture.

#### 15 min. Read The Tree Beside the Path (07), whole

Whole class. Say the purpose first: "Listen for what changes when seasons pass and what changes when years pass." Say aloud: "Noor, Sam and the tree are made up." Read pages 1 to 11 with the pictures (419 words, about four minutes of reading, with pauses). Then reread pages 2 to 5, the year's change. Children give a signal, a hand up, each time a season changes.

#### 15 min. Talk with detail questions

Pairs, then whole class. Talking token, one turn each, pass allowed. Model the questions first: "Who?" "What did Noor see?" and a clarifying one, "Do you mean the leaves or the trunk?" Children may ask it from today. **Ask.** "What changes across years?" Expect: the tree grows taller and its trunk grows wider; Noor grows too. Pairs talk one minute; hear six children, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "Which time words show that years passed, not only a season?" Expect: next spring, when Noor was seven, now Noor was nine, each year. Pairs talk one minute; hear six more. Then read What a Tree Needs page 4, the growth line, and ask one key-detail question: "What does the page say a tree does after many years?" Expect: it grows much taller, and its trunk grows wider.

#### 20 min. Story-map and strip

Whole class, then individuals. Review: the story-map, who, where and what happens (unit 2, day 49). Fill the chart: who, Noor and the tree; where, the path by the park; what happened, the seasons came and went and the tree grew. Then each child draws a two-box strip in the notebook: a small tree, then a taller tree. Take one time word from the day's six first, "years", 30 seconds each, and write it under the strip; the others say it to a partner. Nothing is marked.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read page 10 of The Tree Beside the Path aloud again, its picture covered; the lesson worked the seasons pages. Show strip E84 and ask [[s03-X84av2]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X84bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class draws. Signs: the later picture placed first (error Z); a pair that shows no change (error B); a word that does not say time passed (error D).

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: rhyme and syllables. Say bun and sun, and ask for another; clap monkey (two) and elephant (three).

#### 10 min. Digraph review

Whole class. Hold up sh, th and ch on the sound-spelling map; children say each sound. Hold up the picture cards from days 80 to 83 one at a time; children say the word and whether it starts or ends with sh, th or ch.

#### 8 min. Words to and do check

All thirty children, about 15 seconds each. Each child reads the cards to and do whole, without sounding out, while partners page through picture words. Mark each on the record sheet: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Sign: a child who has to sound out do again (error S); map it d, o (/oo/), then say the word.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Table and stations. Hear six different children, two minutes each, with [[s03-P4a]]: "Which word rhymes with pig: wig or fox?" then "Say another word that rhymes with pig." Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Sign: picks a rhyme but cannot make another (error AD). The others practice forming letters on handwriting paper. Write who was heard on the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Tree Words, verse 1

Whole class. Reread with the actions. Find the first line in print and point under each word, left to right.

#### 15 min. Cycle check

Individuals, all at once. Say each of five words twice: sheep, dish, path, chair, peach. Each child writes sh, th or ch for the beginning or the end sound and places the picture card. No word is printed for children to read. Walk past the tables and note, on the record sheet, who wrote a plausible digraph for each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Plausible means the two letters that make the sound heard.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the story, ask who and what, draw a small tree and a taller one and write the sounds the child hears for the five words. One projector: show the pictures of the story on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No story: look at two pictures of a plant at two sizes. No picture cards: draw five on paper.

**Skip.** The story-map, the rotation and the check on the cards.

- **10 min. Over time.** Draw a small tree, then a taller tree beside it.
  **Say.** "What changes across years?" Expect: the tree grows taller and its trunk wider.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say dish and chair; your child writes sh or ch for the sound they hear.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads to and do and finds them in a verse.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X84av2]] [[s03-X84bv2]] Read page 10 of The Tree Beside the Path aloud. Your child puts the small tree and the tall tree in order and names the words that tell time went by.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the story, the strip and the exit; cut the check to three words. Longer: a secure child tells which words in the story say a season and which say years.

### Words and supports

Words: change, season, year, later. Frame: "Years passed. The tree ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can place two pictures in order and name the words that say time passed. If many put the later picture first (error Z), marked a pair that shows no change (error B) or named a word that does not say time passed (error D), day 85 opens with five minutes: place the pair beside its two sentences, first and later. Day 85 starts reading short words with a sound you know and prepares a science talk from two provided texts; its foundations block begins with 10 minutes of response and a fresh retry. Day 85 also returns the day 83 notes for revision.
