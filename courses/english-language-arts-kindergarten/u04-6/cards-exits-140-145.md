# Exit cards, days 140 to 145

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 140 card E140

**Read:** "A child hangs a bird feeder on the cherry tree. The page says finches come to eat."
**Picture:** a child hanging a feeder on a tree.
**Say:** "Now think of your own card." **Ask:** "Tell me what your card is about and what you like about it." Right: a named subject and a preference, for example "My card is about the oak by the gate. I like to find acorns." Wrong: "I like it" (G); a subject with no liking (F).

## Day 141 card E141

**Read:** "I like the tree by the school door. A child sits on its low branch with a book."
**Pictures:** a child sitting on a low branch with a book; a rainbow; the tree alone.
**Ask:** "Mark the picture that adds useful information to the card." Right: the child on the branch. Wrong: the rainbow (K); the tree alone (J).

## Day 144 card E144

**Read:** "Draft: I like this tree. Peer: What do you hear there? Source: Wind chimes ring in its branches."
**Pictures:** wind chimes hanging in a tree; the unchanged draft; a lone cloud.
**Ask:** "Mark the picture that answers the partner." Then each of the day's six says the detail they would add to the draft. Right: the wind chimes; a detail such as wind chimes ring in its branches. Wrong: the unchanged draft (M); the lone cloud (K).

## Day 145 card E145

**Read:** "Opinion: I like watching ants on this tree. Source: A line of ants climbs it."
**Box:** one empty sketch box.
**Ask:** "On your card, make a quick pencil sketch that adds information from the source line." Right: a line of ants climbing the tree. Wrong: a cloud or a sun (K); a blank box (J).
