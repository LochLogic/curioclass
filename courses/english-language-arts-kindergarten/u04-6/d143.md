# Day 143: Check, talk in turns and questions about two cards

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the record sheet. Mark the two check pages. Day 142's assessment, the cards with your notes, goes back to children at the start of Words return.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: look, listen, wait, take a turn (unit 1). Show ask, answer, take turns and wait.

#### 10 min. Reread 02a

Review: Trees Help Our Community (day 140). Read it whole, about 280 words, pictures shown.

#### 30 min. Observation windows, D1 and T1

Six groups of five, 5 minutes a group, at your table. The others reread. No reteaching.

- Pages, about 90 seconds. Read page 1 of Reading Under the Tree and page 1 of Wangari and the Tree Planters twice each, the map shown.
- Questions, about 100 seconds. Each child asks one question about the story page, [[s04-T143av2]], and answers the partner's, [[s04-T143bv2]]; the same for the information page, [[s04-T143cv2]] and [[s04-T143dv2]].
- Clarification, about 30 seconds. Say: "Show where it is on the map." A child who asks what is meant, [[s04-T143fv2]], hears "Kenya" and says back where it is, [[s04-T143ev2]].
- Talk, about 1 minute. [[s04-D143av2]] and [[s04-D143bv2]]: listen for waiting, answering the partner's detail, a follow-up and a reply.

One prompt: read the card again. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: talking over the partner (error H); one answer only (error I); no question (error X); "her mother" (error Y); the unclear word unasked (error W).

#### 15 min. Order the planting steps

Pairs. Review: first, next, then, after that, last (day 131). Read Planting Together, about 290 words. Each pair orders six pictures and tells one step. Not scored.

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Cycle 23, lesson 118. Review: long a and i (day 141), 4 min. Say date, game, nine and pile; point to a or i, 8 min. Counters for jam, wet, bus, 4 min. Change hot to hit, 4 min.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes. A Cat Can Sit and verse 23: tasks 1 to 6; tasks 7 and 8 in place, six at your table.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Write "Tim has the nut. Then Tim can sit." Children point to each word and count eight; two print a word.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate dog, then pen; children write both. Mark the next six. Collect the record sheet and cards at the end of the day.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; ask and answer with both cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No step pictures: draw six on scrap paper. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The groups and the marks.

- **10 min. The assessment, the page.** Read page 1 of Reading Under the Tree twice. Your child asks you one question about it.
  **Say.** "Ask me one question about the page." Expect: a question such as "When does Ben go to the bench?"
- **10 min. Answer.** Ask your child the question you were asked.
  **Say.** "When does Ben take his book to the bench?" Expect: every day at lunch recess.
- **10 min. Steps.** Read page 3 of Planting Together aloud; your child says the step in a sentence.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws one planting step.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check. Longer: a secure child asks a second question.

### Words and supports

Words: ask, answer, take turns, wait. Frames: "I ask ___. You answer ___." "What do you mean?" Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 144's Try and practice part takes errors H and I, 1 minute each; its Check part takes errors X, Y and W with a new card. Any child not reached today comes first there.
