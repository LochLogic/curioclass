# Day 142: Check, the card opinion in words and drawing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Stack the day-141 cards, each with your note. From the record sheet copy each child's day-138 marks for O1 onto today's line; no child is asked anything for them. Choose the next six of the rotation for the check.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: appreciate, card, subject (day 140) and detail (day 141). Show a picture and gesture for each. No test today.

#### 10 min. Revise the day-141 card

Whole class. Hand-back from day 141's exit, errors K and J, 2 minutes each, with new cards. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Error K: the puppet adds a balloon to a card about Ben's bench. Say: "The page names a wooden bench. Swap the balloon for the bench." Error J: the puppet draws only a young tree. Say: "Point to a detail the page names, then draw it." Children point to the green watering can by the door on a page of A Tree Beside Our Door and say it. Then hand each child their card and read the note aloud as you pass, about 10 seconds a child, 5 minutes. Each child changes the card: names the subject, adds a liking or adds a detail. The first version stays beside the revision.

#### 15 min. Reread 02a

Whole class, pairs. Review: Trees Help Our Community (day 140). Show the pictures first, and each pair points to a picture and says one detail it shows, 20 seconds each. Then read it whole, about 280 words, 3 minutes, and pairs say one detail from the words, 20 seconds each.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, all at once. Unscored.

- **Ask.** "What is your card about?" Pairs, 2 minutes, 1 minute each. Expect: a named tree or tree activity. Hear three children, 30 seconds each.
- **Ask.** "What do you like about it?" Pairs, 2 minutes. Expect: a liking. Hear three children.
- **Ask.** "Which detail on your card came from a page?" Pairs, 2 minutes. Expect: a detail a page names. Hear three children. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Finish the card words and drawing

Individuals, the check. Children finish the subject, the liking and the drawing so the opinion is clear. Read each card as made, about 30 seconds each, from the stack at your table: [[s04-W142]], O1 retained and the day-141 card. No interview; if a child's card needs a prompt, say once, "Point to your subject." Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: a subject with no liking (error F); "it" with no subject named (error G). If the stack is not done, read the rest after class. Collect the cards and the record sheet at the end of the day.

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards and counters, cycle 23, lesson 117. No new pattern.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long a and i by ear (day 141); say name and five; children hold the card a or i.
- Pairs of words, 8 minutes. Say hat and hate, kit and kite, pin and pine, mad and made: children show a flat hand for the short vowel and a sweep for the long one, then point to the card a or i. Spoken words only.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say sun, pet, lip; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Change cap to cup, bag to big and ten to tin; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, six children each. A Cat Can Sit and Tree Care Words, verse 23: tasks 1 to 6 at the stations; the wall cards what, when and then are there to read. You at the table see six children and take tasks 7 and 8 in their normal place: say three sounds without a text, read one mapped word and spell one taught word. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Build on chart paper "When the nut fell, Tim ran to it." from the reader, eight words. Children point to each word and count them; read what, when and then wherever they show. Two children write a word.

#### 5 min. Check

Say lake, then hike. Every child points to the card a or the card i; look along the row. Mark the rotating six on the three-point scale.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the card as made, then do the long sounds alone. One projector: show the pictures. No counters: use buttons.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons. No cards: write a and i on paper.

**Skip.** The stations and the marks.

- **10 min. The assessment, the card.** Show your child the card as made and read your note aloud. Do not question more than once.
  **Say.** "What is your card about, and what do you like about it?" Expect: a named tree and a liking.
- **10 min. Card.** Your child adds one thing the note asked for.
- **10 min. Reread.** Read page 3 of Trees Help Our Community aloud.
  **Say.** "Which detail could go on a card?" Expect: shade or a bench.
- **10 min. Long sounds.** Say lake, hike. Your child holds up a or i on paper.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revise part and the check; cut the third Ask. Longer: a secure child adds a second detail from a page.

### Words and supports

Words: appreciate, card, subject, detail. Frame: "My card is about ___. I like ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the card as made; one prompt only. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who named a subject with a liking. Day 143 is the observation day for talk in turns and questions; it does not reuse day 142's items. Errors F and G come back in day 144's Try and practise part, 2 minutes each.
