# Partner-question strip, days 144 to 149

A long strip of paper for each child, in three parts, left to right: the draft (the card's words or a small copy of the drawing), the partner's question (written by the teacher or drawn as a question mark and a picture) and the answer, with a space for the added detail.

The model for day 144: Draft, "I like the school tree." Peer, "Where do you sit?" Source, "Ben sits on a wide root." The answer is written beside the draft, then the detail is added.

The strip is kept with the card. On day 148 the draft, the revision and the strip are read together; on day 149 the strip is used again with a new partner.

A room with nothing: fold a long sheet of paper in three.
