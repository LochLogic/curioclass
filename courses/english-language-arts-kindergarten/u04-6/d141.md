# Day 141: Return to the tree-care stories and draw a detail on the card

## Teacher page

### Before class

From day 140's record, list who made error G or F on E140 (two tables of at most five), and from the F4 papers who made error AR, AS, P or AP. Write the sample card sentence on chart paper; put out the appreciation card sheets. Keep the exit cards E140 to E145 together and E141 covered. Choose the six for the exit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: appreciate, card, subject (day 140), shade (day 129), detail (day 129). Show a picture and gesture for each; a detail is one fact a picture or a page shows, so point to one small thing. Children point to the card you name, then say it.

#### 15 min. Return to the stories

Whole class, pictures shown, then pairs. Review: A Tree Beside Our Door and Reading Under the Tree (days 120 and 122). Read A Tree Beside Our Door pages 1, 4 and 5, then Reading Under the Tree pages 1, 4, 5 and 6, about 450 words, 4 minutes. Never read page 3 or page 9 of either story. Each pair points to a picture and says one detail it shows, 30 seconds each, about 2 minutes. Then show the puppet's drawing of the cracked soil with stars added. The puppet says, "I added stars." Say: "The page does not name stars. The page names the dry, cracked soil." Swap the stars for cracked lines and say the right detail aloud. Children point to the picture and name one more detail that the page names, then say it to a partner, 2 minutes each.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs, all pairs at once. Review: look, listen, wait, take a turn (unit 1).

- **Ask.** "What does Lina enjoy?" Pairs, 2 minutes. Expect: saying hello to her tree and looking after it. Hear two children, 30 seconds each.
- **Ask.** "What does Ben enjoy?" Pairs, 2 minutes. Expect: reading on the bench under the tree and on the root. Hear two children.
- **Ask.** "Which drawn detail could make an opinion clearer?" Pairs, 2 minutes. Expect: one a page names, such as the dry soil or the wide root. Six children share, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes.
- Speak so a partner can hear, 3 minutes. Each child says one detail aloud and the partner says it back; if a partner cannot, say it again nearer. A child may pass.

#### 20 min. Compose the initial card

Whole class, then individuals. The card has a subject box, a preference box and a drawing with one detail.

- Sample, 7 minutes. The class composes one card together while you draw: "I like helping young trees. Lina checks the dry soil." Hand-back from day 140's exit, errors G and F, 2 minutes each, with new cards. Error G: the puppet says, "I like it." Say: "Name what the card is about first." Children say, "I like helping young trees." Error F: the puppet says, "The young tree." Say: "That names it. What do you like?" Children say, "I like helping the young tree."
- Compose, 13 minutes. Each child draws the subject and one detail from a page on the card sheet and dictates or writes the subject and the preference. Take dictation from about eight children, 60 seconds each, as you pass. Write each dictation under the drawing. After class write one line on each card, about 30 minutes: name the subject, name the liking or name the detail to add.

#### 5 min. Exit E141

Individuals. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E141 twice, show its three pictures and ask [[s04-X141]], 30 seconds each for the day's six, about 3 minutes. The others tell a partner their subject. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: the rainbow (error K); the tree alone (error J).

#### 10 min. Foundations response to F4

Whole class, then a table. Hand-back from day 140's F4 papers, no scores. Model, 3 minutes: the commonest of errors AR, AS and P, the puppet's wrong paper beside a right one. Table, 3 minutes, up to five children by their sign, one sound or form each. Include error AP: sound the regular part of but, not and no, mark the part to remember and read them in a new order. Fresh retry, 4 minutes, whiteboards: print C c, G g and L l; write a letter for /k/ and for /j/; spell rug, pin, hot.

#### 10 min. Long a and i by ear

Whole class, then pairs, spoken words only. Say cake and time slowly. Show the card a: "This letter is a. It says /a/ in apple. It can also say its name, /ā/, as in cake." Show the card i the same way with time. Say cake, name, bike and five; children hold the card a or the card i for the vowel that says its name. Then pairs say a word and show the card, 4 minutes. No word is read; if a child reaches for printed letters in a word, say "Hear it, then point to the letter card" (error Q).

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, six children each. A Cat Can Sit (the title is read by you) and Tree Care Words, verse 23, are both new today. You at the table: read each page, the children echo it and point to the words they can sound out; what, when and then are told today, because they are mapped in the next part. Tasks 1 to 6 at stations. The response and the long-vowel hearing take the place of tasks 7 and 8. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Map what, when, then

Pairs with tiles, then the chart. Show wh as a pair that says /w/. Build what: wh, a, t; the a says /o/ here, the part to remember. Build when: wh, e, n. Build then: th, e, n. Say each, then read the three words. Read together the chart sentence "When Pam sat, then the cat sat." and the cycle 23 sentence "What is in the bag?"

#### 5 min. Check

Say tape, then fine. Every child holds the card a or the card i; look along the row. Mark the rotating six on the three-point scale.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the sample, the exit and the long sounds alone. One projector: show the pictures. With no cards, write a and i on two papers.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: use buttons. No card sheet: fold paper in three.

**Skip.** The tables, the stations and the marks.

- **10 min. Stories.** Read page 4 of A Tree Beside Our Door aloud.
  **Say.** "Which drawn detail could make an opinion clearer?" Expect: the dry, cracked soil.
- **10 min. Card.** Your child draws a tree and one detail the page named and finishes "I like ___."
  **Say.** "What is your card about?" Expect: a named tree and a liking.
- **10 min. Long sounds.** Say cake, name, bike, five. Your child holds up a or i on paper.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X141]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sample, the exit and the long sounds; cut the second Ask. Longer: a secure child adds a second detail from a page.

### Words and supports

Words: appreciate, card, subject, detail, shade. Frame: "I like ___. ___ (a detail)." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the picture that adds useful information. Day 142's Revise part takes errors K and J, 2 minutes each, then each child hears the one-line note and changes the card. Day 142 also reads each card as made.
