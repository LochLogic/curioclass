# Day 144: Answer a partner's question and add a detail to the card

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the day-142 record, list who made error F or G on their card (up to six in all), and from day 143 who made error H or I. Put each child's day-141 card out with a strip. Keep the exit cards E140 to E145 together and E144 covered. Choose the six for the exit. Hand back day 143's assessment: the marks tell you who was not reached; list them.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: appreciate, card, subject, detail (days 140 and 141); ask and answer (day 143). New today: question, add and record. Show a picture and gesture for each: a raised finger for a question, a hand placing a piece for add, a mouth and a small box for record. Children point to the card you name, then say it.

#### 15 min. Reread 01a

Whole class, pictures shown. Review: A Tree Beside Our Door (day 120) and Reading Under the Tree (day 122). Read A Tree Beside Our Door pages 6 and 7, the rain, then Reading Under the Tree page 6, about 130 words of text, about 200 with the picture notes, 2 minutes. Never read page 3 or page 9 of either story. Pairs point to a picture and say one detail it shows, 30 seconds each. Then ask the whole class **Ask.** "What did the rain do to Lina's plan?" Expect: it watered the tree, so Mr. Dube did not need the can. Hear three children, 30 seconds each. Say each answer back so a child can hear it.

#### 10 min. Model the partner's question

Whole class, you and one child at the front. Review: the draft, revise, check against a source routine (unit 3). The strip lies in three parts on the board.

- Draft. Say: "My draft is 'I like the school tree.'" Write it in the first part.
- Question. The child asks, "Where do you sit?" Write it in the second part. Say: "A question can show what a reader wants to know."
- Source. Read from Reading Under the Tree page 6: "Ben sat down on a wide root." Say: "The page names a wide root. I check the page before I change my card."
- Answer and add. Say the answer beside the draft first: "I sit on a wide root." Then add a small drawing of the root to the card. Read the card again with the new detail.

Say the routine in four words, with a hand for each: draft, question, page, add.

#### 15 min. Try and practise

Whole class, then pairs. Unscored.

- Hand-back from day 142's cards, errors F and G, 2 minutes each, with new cards. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Error F: the puppet says, "The wet soil." Say: "That names it. What do you like?" Children say, "I like the wet soil." Error G: the puppet says, "I like it." Say: "Name what the card is about first." Children say, "I like the rain on the leaves."
- Hand-back from day 143, errors H and I, 1 minute each. Error H: pass a small object from child to child; the puppet talks over a partner. Say: "Wait. The one with the card talks." Error I: a child says one answer and stops. Say: "Ask 'What else?', then listen for the reply."
- Together, 3 minutes. Draft: "I like reading by the tree." Question: "Who sits with you?" Read Reading Under the Tree page 7: "Tomas sat on one root. Mei sat on another." Answer beside the draft: "Tomas and Mei sit with me." Add two small figures.
- Partners, 6 minutes. Each child asks a question about the partner's day-141 card, 1 minute each. The partner answers beside the card on the strip, then adds one detail a page names. Pass among the pairs; point to the page if a child cannot find the detail.

#### 10 min. Show the recorder

Whole class, in turn. Unscored. The recordings stay in class.

- Show, 3 minutes. Say aloud into the recorder, "Ben sat on a wide root." Play it back. Say: "The recorder keeps what I say, so I can listen again."
- Choose, 5 minutes. Say two lines: "I like the school tree." and "Ben sat on a wide root." Three children choose which line goes first and say why in a word or a point. If a child only watches, say: "You choose. Which first?" and wait (error N).
- Listen back, 2 minutes. Play both lines in the chosen order.

#### 5 min. Exit E144

Individuals. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E144 twice, show its three pictures and ask [[s04-X144]], 30 seconds each for the day's six, about 3 minutes. The others tell a partner what they added to their card. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: the unchanged draft (error M); the lone cloud (error K).

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards and counters, cycle 23, lesson 119. No new pattern.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long a and i by ear (day 141); say cake and bike; children hold the card a or i.
- New spoken words, 8 minutes. Say pane, tile, lane and mine; children hold the card a or i. Spoken words only.
- Change one letter, 8 minutes. Change bat to bit, pen to pan and cut to cat; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, six children each. A Cat Can Sit and Tree Care Words, verse 23, again: tasks 1 to 6 at the stations; the wall cards what, when and then are there to read. You at the table take the time of tasks 7 and 8 to read pages again with the six. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Build on chart paper "What did Ned get?" from the cycle 23 sentences, four words. Children point to each word and count them; read what wherever it shows. Two children write a word.

#### 5 min. Check

Whole class, then a table. Say wave, then shine. Every child holds the card a or the card i; look along the row. Mark the rotating six. Hand-back from day 143, errors X, Y and W, 1 minute each, with a new card read twice: "Omar's class has a seed pot by the window. Omar waters it. A green shoot is up. Omar shows his friend Dina." Ask for one question (error X), then answer "Who does Omar show?" from the card (error Y), then ask what a shoot is (error W). Take first any child day 143 did not reach, about 45 seconds each.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the model with the strip, the card and the recorder alone. One projector: show the pictures. No cards: write a and i on two papers.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: fold paper in three. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The recorder, the tables and the marks.

- **10 min. Read.** Read page 7 of Reading Under the Tree aloud.
  **Say.** "Which two children sit on roots?" Expect: Tomas and Mei.
- **10 min. Question.** Your child asks you one question about their card, you answer, and your child adds one detail.
  **Say.** "Ask me a question about your card." Expect: any question, such as "What do you like?"
- **10 min. Long sounds.** Say pane, tile, lane, mine. Your child holds up a or i on paper.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X144]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the exit and the long sounds; cut the recorder choice. Longer: a secure child asks a second question about the partner's card and adds a second detail from a page.

### Words and supports

Words: question, answer, add, record. Frame: "My partner asks ___. I add ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the picture that answers a partner. Day 145's Draft part takes error M, 2 minutes, and error K, 1 minute, then each child drafts the final card.
