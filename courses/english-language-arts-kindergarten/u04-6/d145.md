# Day 145: Draft the final card with a pencil sketch that adds information

## Teacher page

### Before class

From day 144's record, list who made error M or K on E144, and write the two repair cards. Keep the exit cards E140 to E145 together and E145 covered. Put the F5 card where only you see it. Choose the six for the exit. Turn the wall cards what, when and then to the wall.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: card, detail, add (days 141 and 144). New today: sketch, a light pencil drawing; pencil; explain, to say how so a listener understands; audible, loud enough to hear. Show a picture and gesture for each: a light hand drawing, a pencil held up, two hands opening, a hand cupped at the ear. Children point to the card you name, then say it.

#### 15 min. Reread both stories

Whole class, pictures shown. Review: A Tree Beside Our Door and Reading Under the Tree (days 120 and 122). Read A Tree Beside Our Door page 8, then Reading Under the Tree page 8, about 120 words with the picture notes, 1 minute. Never read page 3 or page 9 of either story. Show each picture and pairs point to one detail it shows, 30 seconds each. Then ask **Ask.** "Which enjoyment does the page name?" Expect: Lina enjoys the dripping leaves after the rain; Ben enjoys a good place to read. Hear four children, 30 seconds each.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs, all pairs at once. Review: look, listen, wait, take a turn (unit 1), and a question and an answer (day 144). Say the first words of the answer so a child can borrow them: "A sketch can show ___."

- **Ask.** "How can a pencil sketch add detail?" Pairs, 2 minutes, 1 minute each. Expect: it can show something the page names, such as the wide root. Hear two children, 30 seconds each.
- Explain, 6 minutes. Each child explains the sketch idea for their card to the partner, 1 minute each, so the listener can say it back. Stand the partners face to face, near each other. If a partner cannot hear, say it again, nearer (error L). Six children share the partner's idea aloud, 30 seconds each. A child may pass.

#### 20 min. Draft the final card

Whole class, then individuals. Each card has words and a light pencil sketch that adds one detail from reading.

- Hand-back from day 144's exit, errors M and K, with new cards, 3 minutes. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Error M: the card reads, "Draft: I like the pond tree. Peer: What lives there? Source: A heron stands in the shallow water." The puppet leaves the draft unchanged. Say: "Write the answer beside the draft first, then change the card." Children say, "A heron stands there." That is 2 minutes. Error K: the puppet draws a rainbow over the pond. Say: "Name the source detail, then draw only it." Children say "the heron" and point to the page. That is 1 minute.
- Draft, 17 minutes. Each child states the opinion, writes or dictates the card words dark and spaced for paint on day 146, and adds a light pencil sketch of one detail from reading. Take dictation from about eight children, 60 seconds each, as you pass, and write the words under the sketch. No written feedback today.

#### 5 min. Exit E145

Individuals. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E145 twice, show its three sketches and ask [[s04-X145]], 30 seconds each for the day's six, about 3 minutes. The others tell a partner the detail on their sketch. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: the cloud (error K); the blank sheet (error J).

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards and counters, cycle 23, lesson 120, the last day of the cycle. No new pattern before the F5 check.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long a and i by ear (day 141); say cake and bike; children hold the card a or i.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say bake, pipe, cane, dime, safe and bite; children hold the card a or i. Spoken words only.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say mop, ten, bib; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Change pot to pet, fin to fan and tap to tip; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading and F5 words

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. At your table each group gets 3 minutes for the F5 word list, [[s04-F145b]]: show what, when and then on cards, one child at a time, about 30 seconds each, no feedback. Then 2 minutes of A Cat Can Sit. Stations 2 to 5: A Cat Can Sit and Tree Care Words, verse 23, tasks 1 to 6. F5 replaces tasks 7 and 8. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. F5 matching

Whole class, a card a and a card i for each child. Read the twelve words of the F5 card aloud, one at a time, in mixed order; children hold up a or i for the long vowel heard. Scan the room and mark the misses on the record sheet. Do not repeat a word. Then [[s04-F145a]]: say the long sounds in mixed order, one at a time; each child points to the letter. Mark meets or not yet: a child who holds i for long a (error AO). Mark the support used.

#### 5 min. Check

Whole class, then a table. The F5 wrap-up: look at any child who held the wrong card; say two of their missed words again and mark whether they now hold the right card. Do not teach. Collect the record sheet and the final cards at the end of the day.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Draft the card and sketch it alone; do the matching with the F5 words on paper. One projector: show the pictures. No cards: write a and i on two papers.

### At home

**Swap.** No card paper: fold a sheet in half. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The tables, the exit and the F5 marks.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read page 8 of A Tree Beside Our Door aloud.
  **Say.** "Which enjoyment does the page name?" Expect: Lina enjoys the dripping leaves.
- **10 min. Sketch.** Your child sketches one detail from the page in light pencil.
  **Say.** "How does your sketch add detail?" Expect: it shows something the page names.
- **10 min. Long sounds.** Say bake, pipe, cane, dime. Your child holds up a or i on paper.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X145]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the draft, the exit and the F5 matching; cut the second part of Questions and talk. Longer: a secure child adds a second detail from a page.

### Words and supports

Words: sketch, pencil, explain, audible. Frame: "My sketch shows ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the sketch that adds information. Day 146's Reread part takes errors K and J, 2 minutes each, and its Foundations response part takes errors AO and AP, 2 minutes each, with new words and a new sentence. Day 146 then adds watercolor to the sketch.
