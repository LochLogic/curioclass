# Check sheet, day 143 (D1 and T1)

The check uses two pages heard once and never worked: page 1 of Reading Under the Tree (texts/gk-u4-01-b.md) and page 1 of Wangari and the Tree Planters (texts/gk-u4-03.md), with its map. Do not work them before the check. Read each aloud twice. The five children of a group sit in two pairs and a helper; the pages are read in about 90 seconds.

**Story page, Reading Under the Tree page 1.** Each child asks a partner one question about it and answers the partner's question (a detail question, for example "When does Ben take his book to the bench?": every day at lunch recess).

**Information page, Wangari and the Tree Planters page 1.** Each child asks a partner one question about it and answers the partner's (for example "Where is Kenya?": on the east side of Africa, beside the Indian Ocean).

Then say, without naming the thing: "Show where it is on the map." A child who asks what is meant hears "Kenya" and says back where it is. Asking and answering are marked apart for each page. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
