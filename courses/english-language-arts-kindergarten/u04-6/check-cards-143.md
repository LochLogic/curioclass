# Check cards, day 143 (D1 and T1)

Keep these two cards unseen until day 143. Read each aloud twice. The five children of a group sit in two pairs and a helper; the cards are read in about 30 seconds.

**Story card:** "Nia sits by the pond. She hears a splash and sees a frog. She calls her brother to look."
Each child asks a partner one question about it and answers the partner's question (a detail question, for example "Who does Nia call?").

**Information card:** "A spider's web hangs between two tall stems. A stem is the thin part that holds up a flower."
Each child asks a partner one question about it and answers the partner's (for example "Where is the web?").

Then say, without naming the thing: "Show where it hangs." A child who asks what is meant hears "the web" and says back where it hangs. Asking and answering are marked apart for each card. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each card.
