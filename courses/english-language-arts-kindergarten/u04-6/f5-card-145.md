# F5 card, day 145: twelve spoken words

Said aloud by the teacher, one at a time, mixed. Children hold up the card a or the card i for the long vowel heard. None of these words was used on days 141 to 144.

gate, slide, snake, drive, plate, smile, shape, ride, brave, hive, chase, wise

Six words carry long a and six carry long i. The teacher scans the room and records misses (a child who holds i for long a is error AO); no word is shown in print.

A room with nothing: write a and i on two pieces of paper.
