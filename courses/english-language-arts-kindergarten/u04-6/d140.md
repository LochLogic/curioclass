# Day 140: Reread the planting account and plan an appreciation card

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 139's record: list up to five who made error C and up to five who made error Y on E139. Choose the six for the exit and a different six for the model part, none heard on day 139. Write the two repair cards. Fold the Moss on a Log page that says "No, Gus, not off!" under until a group has done its word list. Keep the exit cards E140 to E145 together and E140 covered.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Whole class. Review: shade (day 129). New today: appreciate, to like something and be glad it is there, with a hand on the heart. Also card, a paper with a drawing and words, and subject, what the card is about. Show a picture card and a gesture for each (a hand on the heart; hold up a card; point at the thing in the picture; fan the face for shade). Children point to the card you name, then say the word.

#### 15 min. Reread the planting account

Whole class, then two tables. Say each time you read an invented card: "This card is made up for practice."

- Read, 5 minutes. Review: Wangari and the Tree Planters (day 134). Read it whole, about 410 words, pictures shown. Point to Kenya on the map first; say that many women of Kenya organized and planted together. Page 9 says in one plain sentence that Wangari Maathai died in 2011; read it as written, answer one question about it, and go on.
- The pages again, 4 minutes. Read pages 5 and 6, about 170 words. Hold the picture of page 6 as the purpose card and say, "Listen for what people did together." Pairs point to a picture and say it, 20 seconds each.
- Repair and reasons, 6 minutes. Read Trees Help Our Community pages 3 and 5, about 160 words, 1.5 minutes. Pairs then find a sentence that says a reason and point to it. Meanwhile, hand-back from day 139's exit, 2 minutes a table, up to five children. Error C table: read the new card "Put the bird house in the tree because birds need shelter." The puppet says, "I like birds." Say: "Find the words after because. Say them." Children say, "Birds need shelter." Error Y table: read "Text A: Pour water slowly at the roots. Text B: A tree by the gate gives shade because its canopy blocks the sun." The puppet says, "Pour water slowly." Say: "Find Text B's sentence, then say its reason." Children say, "Its canopy blocks the sun." Close with one pair answer to **Ask.** "What reason can a card use?" Expect: one a page gives, such as trees give shaded places to rest. If more than five need a table, hear five and write the rest on the record sheet.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs, all pairs at once. Review: look, listen, wait, take a turn (unit 1). Say the first words of each answer so a child can borrow them: "The women ___ together." "I could appreciate ___."

- **Ask.** "What did the women do together?" Pairs, 2 minutes, 1 minute each. Expect: they grew seedlings and planted them. Hear two children, 30 seconds each.
- Show the four picture cards of school tree things; no child tells about home.
- **Ask.** "Which tree or tree activity could you appreciate?" Pairs, 2 minutes. Expect: a named tree or activity at school or in the class pot. Six children share, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes. A child may pass.

#### 20 min. Model the card

Whole class, then partners and individuals.

- Model, 8 minutes. Draw a maple by the gym door in the planning box and say: "My card is about the maple by the gym door. I like to watch its leaves fall." The puppet says, "I like it." Say: "It? Name what the card is about first, then say what you like." Say the model again. Write on the board "My card is about ___. I like ___." State the prompt: "Show a tree or tree activity you appreciate. Tell your preference. Add a detail from reading." Say that the detail from reading comes tomorrow.
- Partners and drawing, 12 minutes. Pairs each say a subject and a liking, 30 seconds each, 1 minute. Then each child draws the subject small in the planning box on the student page, 7 minutes. During the drawing hear the six, about 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Repair error G on the spot: point to the picture, name it, then say the liking.

#### 5 min. Exit E140

Individuals. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E140 twice, show its picture, say "Now think of your own card" and ask [[s04-X140]], 30 seconds each for the day's six, about 3 minutes. The others draw the tree again. One prompt of 10 seconds is allowed, "What is your card about?", and then it is shown with help. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: a liking with no subject (error G); a subject with no liking (error F).

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with tiles, cycle 22, lesson 115, the last day of double final ll, zz, ss, ff. No new pattern.

- Recall, 5 minutes. Review: doubled final letters (day 135). Say bell, buzz, moss and puff; children say each word and hold up two fingers for the two letters at the end.
- Read and change, 8 minutes. Read the cards hiss, toss, dull, doll and cuff, then change toss to moss, dull to doll and cuff to huff, naming the sound that changed. Read together the cycle 22 sentences "Tess has a doll." and "Ben has a rip in his cuff."
- Spell, 5 minutes. Dictate kiss, then jazz, 2.5 minutes each: counters for the sounds, then whiteboards.
- Mapped words, 2 minutes. Review: they and where (day 124), we and all (day 130) on tiles; mark the part to remember.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading and F4 words

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. At your table each group gets 3 minutes for the F4 word list, [[s04-F140d]]: show but, not and no, one child at a time, about 30 seconds each, no feedback. Then 2 minutes of Moss on a Log, tasks 1 and 2. Stations 2 to 5: Moss on a Log, tasks 3 to 6, with the "No, Gus, not off!" page folded under. F4 replaces tasks 7 and 8. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. F4 written, part 1

Whole class on letter paper. First, [[s04-F140a]]: say each of B b D d F f H h M m N n P p R r S s T t V v W w by name and children print it, 26 forms at about 10 seconds a form, 5 minutes. Then [[s04-F140b]]: say twelve sounds in this order, 2 minutes: /m/ /a/ /s/ /e/ /t/ /i/ /b/ /o/ /p/ /u/ /n/ /d/; children write a letter for each. The other 3 minutes are for paper, names and moves. Collect nothing yet.

#### 5 min. F4 written, part 2

Dictate mud, fit, then cob, about 30 seconds each, [[s04-F140c]], and collect the papers. Mark meets or not yet on the record sheet: unreadable forms (error AR); b for /m/ (error AS); md for mud (error P); another word read for but, not or no (error AP). A sound-based cob such as kob meets.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the account, model the card, then ask the exit alone and do the letters on one sheet. One projector: show the map and the pictures. No tiles: use paper squares.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw a tree. No tiles: use buttons.

**Skip.** The tables, the stations and the F4 marks.

- **10 min. The reason.** Read page 3 of Trees Help Our Community aloud.
  **Say.** "What reason can a card use?" Expect: trees give shaded places to rest.
- **10 min. The planting account.** Read page 6 of Wangari and the Tree Planters aloud.
  **Say.** "What did the women do together?" Expect: they planted the seedlings.
- **10 min. Plan.** Your child names a tree at school or a plant in a pot and draws it small.
  **Say.** "What do you like about it?" Expect: a liking, not just "it".
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X140]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the exit and F4; cut the second Questions and talk ask. Longer: a secure child says a second liking for the subject.

### Words and supports

Words: appreciate, card, subject, shade. Frame: "My card is about ___. I like ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who named a subject with a liking. Day 141's Compose part takes errors G and F, 2 minutes each, and its Foundations response part takes errors AR, AS, P and AP from the F4 papers. Day 141 then returns to the tree-care stories and adds a drawing to the card.
