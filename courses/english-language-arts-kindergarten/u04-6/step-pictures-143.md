# Six planting step pictures, day 143

Six plain pictures from the planting text, one step each, no printed word: choose a good place; an adult digs a wide hole; the young tree is set in the hole; the hole is filled a little at a time; children water slowly; mulch is spread in a ring.

The teacher reads Planting Together aloud. Pairs put the six pictures in order, saying first, next, then, after that and last, and tell a partner one step in a sentence. Spoken, no scoring. A pair that is stuck is shown the page, once.

These cards are for teaching and may be shown. They do not repeat any exit or end card.
