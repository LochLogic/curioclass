# Appreciation card sheet, days 141 to 145

One sheet of card paper for each child, in three parts: a subject box (the tree or the tree activity, named or labelled), a preference box ("I like ...") and a large space for one drawing of the subject with one detail from reading.

- Day 141: the child draws and says or writes the subject and the preference; the teacher takes dictation from about eight and writes a one-line note after class.
- Day 142: the child hears the note and revises; the first version stays beside the revision.
- Day 145: the child makes the final words, dark and spaced, and a light pencil sketch that adds one detail from reading.

A room with nothing: fold a sheet in three and draw the boxes by hand.
