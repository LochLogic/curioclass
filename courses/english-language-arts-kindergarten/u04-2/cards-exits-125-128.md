# Exit cards, days 125 and 128

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 125 card E125

**Read:** "Opal rakes the leaves around her tree. Perry rakes the leaves around his tree."
**Ask the pair of partners:** "Ask your partner a question about Opal or Perry, listen to the answer, then ask one more question." Right: a question about the card, an answer heard, and a follow-up that uses the answer. Wrong: one question and one answer, then stops (I); asks before the partner has finished (H).

## Day 128 card E128 (fresh retry)

**Read:** "Dee waits for a sprinkler to stop, then enjoys a tree's shade. Rai waits for a cart to roll past, then enjoys a tree's shade."
**Ask:** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about Dee and Rai." Right: both wait and then enjoy the shade, but Dee waits for a sprinkler and Rai for a cart. Wrong: hats or clothes (A).
