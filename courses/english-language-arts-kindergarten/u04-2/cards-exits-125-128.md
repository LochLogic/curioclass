# Exit cards, days 125 and 128

Card E125 is made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice", read it twice with its picture, and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. Sheet E128 goes with a page of each story, read aloud again; it is not made up, so say nothing about practice for it. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read aloud. None of these is shown in teaching.

## Day 125 card E125

**Read:** "Opal rakes the leaves around her tree. Perry rakes the leaves around his tree."
**Ask the pair of partners:** "Ask your partner a question about Opal or Perry, listen to the answer, then ask one more question." Right: a question about the card, an answer heard, and a follow-up that uses the answer. Wrong: one question and one answer, then stops (I); asks before the partner has finished (H).

## Day 128 sheet E128 (fresh retry): page 5 of each story

**Read again:** page 5 of A Tree Beside Our Door, then page 5 of Reading Under the Tree.
**Ask:** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about Lina and Ben." Right: both find a way when there is a problem at their tree, but Lina asks Mr. Dube for help and Ben walks around the trunk himself. Wrong: how they look, such as braids or glasses (A).
