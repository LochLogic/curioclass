# Day 128: Repair the comparison and the talk, and retrieve print and sounds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for days 125 to 127: list the children who made error A, H or I, and who made each of S, T, AG, AH, AI, AJ and AK on the day 123 check and was not seen since. Pick six for the exit, first those with error A, then children not yet heard. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 15 min. Return and repair, with a fresh retry

10 min, hand-back from day 126's and day 127's items and day 125's exit: errors A, H and I. A group of up to six who made one error gets a small-group repair; if more than ten made it, the whole class does that repair and the others reread. Error A: the clothing card beside the event cards, then a new Both ... but ... sentence together: "Both speak softly to the tree, but Lina touches a leaf and Ben opens his book." Error H: the puppet talks over a partner; the group passes the turn card and waits for it, then answers the speaker. Error I: with the follow-up question card, a three-turn talk on the pictures of page 1 of each story. Then 5 min: the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E128 twice, then ask [[s04-X128]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others draw a favorite tree. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: hats or clothes (error A).

#### 10 min. Reread new passages

Whole class on the rug. Read page 8 of A Tree Beside Our Door and page 8 of Reading Under the Tree, twice each; page 8 of A Tree Beside Our Door was also reread on day 124. Pages 3 and 9 stay closed. **Ask.** "Which event does the comparison describe?" Expect: each changes a plan. **Ask.** "Where does each story show it?" Children point to the page and say Lina or Ben.

#### 15 min. Compare the two passages

Pairs. Review: the chart and Both ... but ... (day 123). Each pair says two sentences from the chart and the pages, one about an event and one about a feeling. Expect: both change a plan, but rain changes Lina's and Ben moves himself; both feel good, but Lina feels warm and Ben feels calm. Each says theirs, one turn each, about a minute a pair; hear the day's six and name them in the record column. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Draw and dictate

Everyone draws the two passages on side 1, one picture in each column. The rotating six dictate one Both ... but ... sentence to you, and you write it; the rest copy-trace the frame. Look for something both do or feel, one difference, and a drawing that matches both parts. Scored from the work you handed in.

#### 5 min. Two invented cards

Whole class. Say aloud: "These sentences are made up for practice." Read "A stem holds a flower." and "A stem holds a leaf." twice with their pictures. **Ask.** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence." Expect: both are about a stem, but one holds a flower and one holds a leaf. Partners say it; hear three pairs.

#### 5 min. Partner share and tidy

Partners show their drawing and say the sentence. Put the cards away.

#### 15 min. Foundations: retrieval of print and sounds

Review: print, letters, sounds, rhyme and syllables (unit 1 and the cycles so far); no new pattern. Five stations of six children, 3 minutes a station, one rotating routine. 1: follow a finger left to right, top to bottom, then tap one word for each space on "Sam has a pan." (error T). 2: match B and b, H and h, A and a to one name each (error S). 3: say the sound of letters m, s and t, and write one letter for each sound you say. 4: hear ham, jam, pot and pick the rhyming pair; tap the parts of puppet. 5: blend /p/ and /an/, then say the first part and the last part of pan. You sit with the day's six and hear each, about 30 seconds a station; the other groups run from the picture cards.

#### 20 min. Foundations: response and fresh retry

Hand-back: the cycle-20 practice of days 124 to 127 and any day 123 component not yet seen. For an error more than a third made, the whole class repeats the repair; the others retry alone in small groups. Fresh cases only: T, tap one word for each group between spaces on "Pam ran to Sam." (3 min); AG, follow a finger along two new lines, "Sam has a can." then "It is at the van." (3 min); AH, point to the printed word ram with the picture covered (3 min); AI, name B and b and say they are one letter (3 min); AJ, hear ran, pan, pig, pick the pair that rhymes, then give another word for pan (4 min); AK, tap each part of puppet and pan (4 min). Mark each retry on the record sheet.

#### 15 min. Foundations: reread taught readers

Pairs. Reread A Hen Can Nap and Sam and Pam, taught text only, with a new question each time: "Why does Peg run?" and "Who has the can?" Hear the rotating six, one sentence each, about 2 minutes each and the rest of the time circulating; name them in the record column.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print and writing

Whole class. Reread Tree Care Words, verse 2, by echo. Write one source sentence together, "The bark needs air, or so they say.", and mark each space with a finger.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Use the one child as the six in every part, take the exit card alone and do each fresh retry once. One projector: show a picture alone; draw the chart on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters or tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The stories, the stations and the readers.

- **10 min. Both and but.** Show two things from home. 
  **Say.** "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Expect: one thing the same, then one thing each does, not a color.
- **10 min. Print.** Point to one word on a page. Your child says one word for each space.
  **Say.** "Show me the first word. Where do I go next?" Expect: left to right, then down.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say pan. Your child says the first, middle and last sound with a button each.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X128]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exit and the fresh retries; cut the second invented card and the third station. Longer: a secure child says a Both ... but ... sentence about a third page.

### Words and supports

Words: both, same, different. Frame: "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can say what two characters share and one difference. Day 129 begins the next section with a text about why trees matter; for any child who again compared clothing (error A) a small group meets the event cards at the start of its Vocabulary part. Nothing else carries but the records.
