# Two invented cards for retrieval, days 125 and 128

Say aloud: "These sentences are made up for practice." Read each pair twice with a plain picture of each sentence. Children say one Both ... but ... sentence. These are for teaching and may be shown.

- Day 125: "A branch holds leaves." and "A branch holds a nest." Right: both are about a branch, but one holds leaves and one holds a nest.
- Day 128: "A stem holds a flower." and "A stem holds a leaf." Right: both are about a stem, but one holds a flower and one holds a leaf.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card.
