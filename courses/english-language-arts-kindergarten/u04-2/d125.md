# Day 125: Build the comparison and keep a talk going

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 124: name errors A, X and Y from the exit and Z, AA and AB from the retry. Pick six for the exit (three pairs) and six other children for the reading, none heard yet. Children 1 to 15 are the three watched groups of five; list them on the record sheet. Make a second set of the six chart pictures for each pair. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Two small groups, two minutes each, while the rest reread the chart quietly; if more than a third made one error, the whole class does that repair. Error A: the clothing card, two coats, beside the event cards, and say a coat tells nothing about the tree. Then say one new sentence together: "Both look after the tree, but Lina asks about the dry soil and Ben brushes twigs off his bench." Errors X and Y: show the picture on page 6 of Reading Under the Tree, point to Ben on the wide root, ask "Who sits on a wide root?", find the sentence and answer "Ben." If a child also left out the person, the place or the change (errors Z, AA, AB), put the matching card beside the event for one minute.

#### 15 min. Reread both scenes

Whole class on the rug. Read the rain moment, pages 6 and 7 of A Tree Beside Our Door, twice, then the noise moment, pages 4 to 6 of Reading Under the Tree, twice, about 5 minutes in all. Pages 3 and 9 stay closed. **Ask.** "Which details belong in the shared column?" Expect: both enjoy a tree; both have a plan that changes. **Ask.** "Which details tell the experiences apart?" Expect: rain and waiting for Lina, noise and moving for Ben. Take two answers each, then tell them the chart comes next.

#### 15 min. Make the comparison chart

Pairs at the tables, each with the six pictures. Review: the two-column chart and Both ... but ... (day 123). Say the order: shared pictures first, then the pictures that tell the stories apart. Pairs lay them out, a shared row between two columns, and say one Both ... but ... sentence to each other. Walk round and hear three pairs. The puppet sets two hats beside the event cards and says "Both have hats, but one is red." Say "Someone might say that. A hat tells nothing about the tree" (error A). If many pairs put a picture in the wrong place, move one card with the class and say why.

#### 15 min. Rehearse discussion exchanges

Whole class first, 3 minutes. Review: wait for the turn card, and a follow-up (unit 1, days 26 to 29; day 123). Show a new three-turn talk: ask, hear the whole answer, then ask a follow-up that uses it. The puppet talks over you (error H); then asks one question, hears one answer and stops (error I). Show the right way with the follow-up question card: "Where does it say so?" Also model "What does trunk mean?" and answer it with the picture. Then three watched groups of five, children 1 to 15, 4 minutes each, about 45 seconds a child: they sit with the token and turn card at page 7 of Reading Under the Tree and ask each other. Mark each on the record: waits (error H), a follow-up (error I), shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Their talk record is carried to day 127. All the others rehearse in pairs with the turn card and the follow-up card, the same talk, unwatched, until the watched groups are done. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Two invented cards

Whole class, then partners. Say aloud: "These sentences are made up for practice." Read "A branch holds leaves." and "A branch holds a nest." twice with their pictures. **Ask.** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence." Expect: both are about a branch, but one holds leaves and one holds a nest. Partners say it to each other; hear three pairs.

#### 5 min. Exit

Three pairs, the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E125 twice to the six together, then ask [[s04-X125]], one pair at a time, about a minute each. The others finish their drawing of a tree place on side 1. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: one question and one answer, then stops (error I); asks before the partner has finished (error H).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 20, lesson 102: -an and -am)

Whole class, counters and tiles. Review: the an and am families (day 124). 5 min: read tan, ran, Pam and Sam with a counter for each sound; the last two sounds stay together in each family. 5 min: with tiles, ham to him, and say the sound that changed, /a/ to /i/. 5 min: review there, they and where, each read in a sentence before alone: "There is the van." "They ran to Sam." "Where is Pam?" Keep of, have and from on the wall. 5 min: read with a finger under each word: "Pam has a tan can." "Sam ran to the pan of jam." Signs: guesses from the picture (error O); drops a sound (error P); takes a sound away (error R).

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Groups of six. Review: Sam and Pam (day 124); the adult reads the title. Assignments 1 to 6 only, with no isolated tasks today. The day's other six read with you for 10 minutes, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question ("Why do Sam and Pam run to the pot?" Expect: the pot is not wet), one pointing task ("Point to there"). The other four groups partner-read and use the assignment cards; you go table to table. Name the six in the record column. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Reread Tree Care Words, verse 2, by echo; point to the line that gives the order. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, "Then keep the mulch away from the trunk.", a space between words, and point to from. Each child writes him by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. More reading

Whole class and partners. Reread Sam and Pam for meaning. **Ask.** "What did Sam and Pam do to fix the dry pot?" Expect: they got the can and gave it water. Partners tell each other.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Make the chart with the six pictures, do one three-turn talk, take the exit card and read the reader. Use the one child as the six in every part. One projector: show a picture alone; draw the chart on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters or tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The stories, the reader and the watched groups.

- **10 min. Both and but.** Pick two things at home, like a spoon and a cup. Say what is the same and what is different.
  **Say.** "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Expect: one thing the same, then one thing each does, not a color.
- **10 min. Talk.** Ask your child a question about a picture book page, listen to the whole answer, then ask one more.
  **Say.** "Where does it say so?" Expect: your child finds a page or a word that tells.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say ham. Your child pushes a button for each sound, changes the middle sound to make him and writes it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X125]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exit, the three-turn talk and the sound change; cut the second invented card and the fourth sentence. Longer: a secure child makes a second Both ... but ... sentence about the two stories.

### Words and supports

Words: both, same, different, follow-up. Frames: "Both ___. But ___ and ___." and "What does ___ mean?" Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can ask, listen and ask again. If many stopped after one turn (error I) or talked over a partner (error H), say so once in tomorrow's plan; the repair waits for day 128. Day 126 is a check day: groups of five compare page 3 of each story, and children 1 to 15 are heard first.
