# Day 127: Check, a talk about two stories

## Teacher page

### Before class

List children 16 to 30 in three groups of five on the record sheet; children 1 to 15 keep their day 125 talk record and day 126 marks. Open both books to the third page. Pick six for reading and six for the sound check, none heard.

### The day

#### 5 min. Familiar words and the day's plan

Read there, they, where, of, have and from from the wall; no teaching. Say the plan. If day 126's assessment showed many comparing looks (error A), say so once; the repair waits for day 128.

#### 10 min. Familiar reread, whole class

Read the noise moment, pages 4 to 6 of Reading Under the Tree, once. No comparison task.

#### 30 min. Observation, C1 and the talk rows: groups four to six

Three groups of five, children 16 to 30, ten minutes each. Reread the two third pages once (90 seconds), then ask each child in turn [[s04-C1c]] and [[s04-C1d]], with day 126's one prompt. Then the five talk for three minutes with the token; say "Take turns, listen, and answer the person who spoke" ([[s04-D127a]]) and "Ask each other questions about the two pages" ([[s04-D127b]]), and nothing more. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Errors H and I. Children 1 to 15 partner-read Sam and Pam and draw a tree place at school; no second performance.

#### 15 min. Draw and dictate, everyone

Everyone finishes the drawing with "I like ___." The next rotating six dictate to you. Collect the pages at the end of the day.

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 20, lesson 104: -an and -am)

Review: the an and am families (day 124). With tiles chain ram, rim, saying the changed sound. Read there, they and where in a sentence: "They have a pan of ham."

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: Sam and Pam. Assignments 1 to 6 use the reader and the verse; 7 and 8 are the isolated tasks. Hear the day's six for 10 minutes, about 100 seconds each, and name them in the record column. The others partner-read with the cards.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Echo Tree Care Words, verse 2; point to the line that gives the order. Write "The bark needs air, or so they say." together; each child writes rim on side 2.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Say gap; six children say its middle sound. Unscored.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; ask the two stems, then trade a question each.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters or tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the group talk.

- **10 min. The assessment, part one.** Read page 3 of each story aloud once. One prompt only: "Who does Lina invite? Who does Ben invite?"
  **Say.** "What is the same, and what is different?" Expect: an invitation to the tree, then smelling blossoms and feeling bark.
- **10 min. Talk.** Ask your child a question about the pages and listen to the whole answer; then ask what your child would like to ask you.
  **Say.** "Ask me something about the two pages." Expect: a question, then a follow-up question about your answer.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws a tree place and says "I like ___."
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say ram. Your child pushes a button for each sound, changes the middle sound to make rim and writes it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the two stems and the sound check. Longer: a second question.

### Words and supports

Words: same, different, answer. Frame: "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Pointing, a drawing and a home language all count. Read each stem twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The check tells you who named a shared event and a difference, and who waited and asked a follow-up. Day 128 takes errors A, H and I from day 127's assessment and tries a fresh card.
