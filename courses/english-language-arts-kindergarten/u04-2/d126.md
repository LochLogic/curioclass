# Day 126: Check, how two characters' days are alike and different

## Teacher page

### Before class

List children 1 to 15 in three groups of five on the record sheet. Open both books to the third page. Pick six for reading and six for the sound check, none heard.

### The day

#### 5 min. Familiar words and the day's plan

Read there, they, where, of, have and from from the wall; no teaching. Say the plan. If many stopped after one turn (error I) or talked over a partner (error H) on day 125's exit, say so once; the repair waits for day 128.

#### 10 min. Familiar reread, whole class

Read the rain moment, pages 6 and 7 of A Tree Beside Our Door, once. No comparison task.

#### 30 min. Observation, C1: groups one to three

Three groups of five, ten minutes each: reread the two third pages once (90 seconds), then ask each child in turn [[s04-C1a]] and [[s04-C1b]], about 30 seconds a stem. One prompt only: "Who does Lina invite? Who does Ben invite?" Mark shown, shown with help (after the prompt) or not yet seen; a child may pass. Error A: looks, bags or places only. The other twenty-five partner-read Sam and Pam and begin a drawing of a tree place at school; a child with a question asks three neighbors first.

#### 15 min. Draw and dictate, everyone

Everyone finishes the drawing with "I like ___." The rotating six dictate to you. Collect the pages at the end of the day.

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 20, lesson 103: -an and -am)

Review: the an and am families (day 124). With tiles chain can, cap, tap, saying each changed sound. Read there, they and where in a sentence: "They ran to the van."

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: Sam and Pam. Assignments 1 to 6 use the reader and the verse; 7 and 8 are the isolated tasks (sounds without a text; one mapped word read, one taught word spelled). Hear the day's six for 10 minutes, about 100 seconds each, and name them in the record column. The others partner-read with the cards.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Echo Tree Care Words, verse 2; point to the line that gives the order. Write "Then keep the mulch away from the trunk." together; each child writes cap on side 2.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Say dam; six children say its first, middle and last sound. Unscored.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; ask the two stems once.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters or tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups and the reader.

- **10 min. The assessment, part one.** Read page 3 of each story aloud once. If your child stops, give one prompt only: "Who does Lina invite? Who does Ben invite?"
  **Say.** "What is the same, and what is different?" Expect: an invitation to the tree, then smelling blossoms and feeling bark, not looks.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws a tree place and says "I like ___."
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say can. Your child pushes a button for each sound, changes the last sound to make cap and writes it.
- **10 min. Read.** Read a short page together and talk about it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the two stems and the sound check. Longer: a second thing that is the same.

### Words and supports

Words: same, different, invite. Frame: "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Pointing, a drawing and a home language all count. Read each stem twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The check tells you which children can name a shared event and a difference. Day 127 repeats it for children 16 to 30 and adds a talk. Day 128 takes errors A, H and I from day 126's assessment.
