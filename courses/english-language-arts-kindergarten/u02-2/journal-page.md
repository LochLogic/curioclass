# Class picture journal page

One page for each weather picture the class makes from day 41 on. Print one page on a sheet; keep the pages in order on the wall, and use them on day 46 as the talk prompts.

**Top of the page:** a box for the picture, large enough to fill most of the sheet. **Under it:** two lines, each with a space for the teacher to write what the child says (a naming word and an action word, and a place or time). **Corner:** the date, written by the teacher.

Children draw and say; they do not write words. The teacher writes what the child says under the picture, and a child may add letters they know.
