# Day 39: Rain and snow, and more than one

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the three request cards. Cut the paper drops and flakes, and lay out the dishes and glasses. Draw the exit cards P39a and P39b from the cards-exits-39-42 sheet and keep them out of sight; today uses both. Make the tree picture pair. Today's six are the next two unheard from each foundations group.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair day 38's exit for the more common sign on the record sheet. For error I: show the tree pair (say it is made up for practice), leaves swirling, then the same tree with leaves still. Pip says "A tree." Ask "What is different?" and the class says the leaves moved, then stopped. For error F: the class tells both pictures. For error AT: Pip hears a mumble and says nothing; the class holds up card 3, "Can you say that again?" Then one fresh sentence: "At first ___. Later ___." about a picture card, said by two children.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Say each word, the class says it with a gesture, and you point to its picture. Wet: shake your fingers. Dry: rub your hands. Protect: cross your arms over your head like a roof. Explain: open one hand and tell how. Record: draw in the air what you see. Frame: "An umbrella protects people from rain." Nobody is asked what they wear or own.

#### 10 min. Reading 01c

Whole class, then partners. Read Rain and Snow whole, pages 1 to 4, showing each picture and not stopping (about 2 minutes). Page 4 shows wind pushing snow; a child who dislikes the picture may look at the floor. Then **Ask.** "What falls from the clouds?" Expect: rain and snow; drops and flakes. Partners tell each other first (3 minutes). Then point to each of the four pictures and let one child say one thing about it (3 minutes). Rain is wet drops. Snow is flakes of ice.

#### 12 min. Reread and ask about a word

Whole class, then pairs. Reread pages 2 and 3 (3 minutes). Review: you asked "What does ___ mean?" with card 2 on day 37. First, a model (4 minutes). Pip holds up card 2 and asks "What does puddle mean?" Read the line "Many drops make a puddle" and point to the picture of rain in the street. Say: "A puddle is water that gathers on the ground." Second, the children (5 minutes). Each pair holds card 2 and asks one another "What does snowflake mean?" The partner finds the answer in the line "Snowflakes are made of ice" and the picture of the boy with the mitten. Two pairs share. Say again: rain is wet drops; snow is flakes of ice.

#### 13 min. Model: more than one

Whole class. First, the ending /s/ (4 minutes). Review: a naming word names a thing (day 36). Hold up one paper drop: "one drop." Drop a pile: "many drops." Tap once for the /s/ at the end. Children echo. Repeat with one flake and many flakes, and with the picture of one puddle and the picture of three puddles. You may say the ending as /z/ in puddles; that is right, and children need not change it. Say once: a word that means more than one has a grown-up name, plural.

Second, the beat /es/ (5 minutes). Pip says "two drop" (error X, the /s/ left off) beside "two drops", with a tap for the /s/. The class says it right. Show one dish: "dish" has one beat; show two dishes: "dishes" has two beats, a finger for each. Pip says "two dish" (error Y); the class fixes it with two fingers. Repeat with one glass and two glasses.

Third, the children first (4 minutes). Hold up one hat, then two: children say "two ___" before you do. Do the same with a boot and a mitten, a dish and a glass. A child who adds no ending hears you say the word right, once, with no comment.

#### 10 min. Partners

Pairs. Round one (5 minutes): each pair has five two-sided cards. Child A turns a card and says "one boot, two boots" while the pointer touches the front and then the back; child B takes the next card; swap on each card. Round two (4 minutes): each child picks two cards and says "I see two ___ in the rain." Share two (1 minute). You listen at six of the 15 pairs, 40 seconds each. Signs: the ending left off (error X); "dishs" or no beat in dishes and glasses (error Y). Say the word right, once.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Six children at the heard table, 40 seconds each (6 times 40 is 4 minutes). Say: "This card is made up for practice." Show P39a and ask item a, then P39b and ask item b ([[s02-X39b]]). A child may point to two objects and say the word. Signs: "two frog" (error X); "two peach" or "peachs" (error Y). Mark the two items apart: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw many drops in the box on their page.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Review: tracking left to right (day 4). Read the verse Rain whole, pages 1 and 2, twice with the pointer, and the class joins in. Find the words with s: Sam, hops, puddles, gets, splash, boots. Say /s/ each time. The verse has no v, so the search ends. Review he and we from days 37 and 38: find each on page 2 and touch it.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Rhyme Time (8 minutes). Read the end of each line on page 1 and page 2: by and sky, wet and yet. Say "wet, yet" and ask for a new word that rhymes with a new first sound: net, pet, set. Ask the same for by: my, fly. Any real or silly rhyme counts.

Cycle 5 check on paper (5 minutes), all children: say only the sound, never the keyword. "Write the letter for /v/." Then "Write the letter for /s/." Collect the papers; record them on the foundations record. Closing share (2 minutes): hold up one letter you like.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: the cycle 5 check, one child at a time: name v, name s, say the sound of each (10 children at 40 seconds is under 7 minutes; 30 children take 20 minutes across the three turns). With the minutes left, review segmenting van and sat with a counter for each sound heard. Errors: "vee" given for the sound (error U); s written for /v/ (error W). Writing place: finish the sound-writing check on paper, then practice v and s in lines. Sound and verse place: rhyme cards, match pairs that rhyme (bat and hat, sun and bun, rain and train) and say one more; find rhyming words in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read Rain and Snow, count real objects (one spoon, two spoons; one dish, two dishes) and do the exit pictures. One projector: show each page and picture one at a time; hold the real objects and cards up.

### At home

**Swap.** No paper drops: draw drops. No picture cards: use real dishes and spoons. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The pairs, the rotations and the group sort.

- **5 min. Listen.** Read the page Rain and Snow aloud from the lesson text.
  **Say.** "What falls from the clouds?" Expect: rain and snow.
- **5 min. One and many.** Show one spoon, then two. Your child says "two spoons."
- **5 min. Dishes.** Show one dish, then two. Clap "dish" (one beat), then "dishes" (two beats).
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say /v/ and then /s/; your child writes each letter.
- **5 min. Rhyme.** Say "wet." Your child says a word that rhymes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X39a]] [[s02-X39b]] Draw one frog, then two. Say: "There is one frog. Now there are two. Say it: two what?" Then one peach and two peaches the same way.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second round of pairs and the rhyme game; keep the reading, the model, the exit and the check. Longer: a child who is secure sorts the cards into words that end with /s/ and words that need the beat /es/.

### Words and supports

Words: wet, dry, protect, explain, record, puddle, snowflake, plural. Frames: "One ___, two ___." "I see two ___ in the rain." "What does ___ mean?" A pointed picture, two fingers, a gesture or a home-language word names the object; say the English word back once. Judge the ending apart from loudness and clarity.

Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of error X (two drop) and error Y (two dish) with objects and picture cards, then say one fresh plural. The cycle 5 check marks go to its foundations response: error U (vee for the sound) and error W (s written for /v/).
