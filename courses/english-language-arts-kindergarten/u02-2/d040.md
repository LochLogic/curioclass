# Day 40: A sentence that grows

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set up the pocket chart with the word cards in a pile beside it. Draw the exit card P40 and keep it out of sight. Sort the day 39 check papers into the two groups for the response: the children who said "vee" for the sound (error U) and the children who wrote s for /v/ (error W). Today's six are the next two unheard from each foundations group.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair day 39's exit for the more common of error X and error Y. For error X: Pip says "two drop"; hold up the paper drops, one and then many, and the class says "drops" with a tap for the /s/. For error Y: Pip says "two dish"; show one dish and two dishes, a finger for each beat. Then three children each say one fresh plural from a picture card: one sweater and two sweaters, one sock and two socks.

#### 10 min. Reread 01b

Whole class, then partners. Warmer and Cooler was read on days 37 and 38. Reread pages 2 and 3 (3 minutes) and ask the children to listen for when the air feels warmer and when it feels cooler. **Ask.** "When does the air feel warmer?" Expect: at noon. **Ask.** "When does the air feel cooler?" Expect: when the sun goes down; at night. Partners say each answer as a whole sentence to each other (3 minutes), for example "The air feels warmer at noon." Then three children say a sentence for the class (4 minutes).

#### 15 min. Model: a sentence grows

Whole class. Review: a sentence says who or what and what it does, a naming word and an action word (day 36).

First, add a place or a time (6 minutes). Put the cards "the air" and "feels cool" in the pocket chart and read them: "The air feels cool." Pip says the same sentence again (error AF, nothing added). Ask: "When?" and add the card "at night": "The air feels cool at night." Then Pip says "At night." alone (error AE, the who and what are missing). The class says who and what first, then when. Do the same with "The sun shines" and the question "Where?": "The sun shines over the roofs."

Second, join two with and (6 minutes). This is new today. Put "The sun goes down." and "The air gets cooler." on two strips. Say each short sentence, then put the card "and" between them: "The sun goes down and the air gets cooler." Children say it with you, then say it alone. Try a second pair: "Snow falls." "Wind blows." joined with and.

Third, children first (3 minutes). Say "The wind blows." and children say where or when before you do: in the park; at noon.

#### 5 min. Shared writing

Whole class. Write on chart paper, as the children say it, "The sun goes down and the air gets cooler." Say each word as you write it. Children watch where the writing starts, which way it goes and where it stops. Then touch under each word while the class reads it back to you. No term for the capital or the end mark is taught yet.

#### 20 min. Pairs

Pairs. Each pair has three picture cards (all invented for practice; say so). Each child makes three sentences for a card: a short one, the same one made longer with where or when, and two short ones joined with and. Child A takes the card, child B listens and then takes the next card; they share the third card (7, 7 and 4 minutes), then two pairs share with the class (2 minutes). Sentences to expect:

- Dog on a rug: "The dog sleeps." "The dog sleeps on the rug." "The rain taps and the dog sleeps."
- Boy at a window: "The boy watches." "The boy watches at the window." "The snow falls and the boy watches."
- Girl in a puddle: "The girl hops." "The girl hops in the puddle." "The rain stops and the girl hops."

Any true sentence the picture shows counts. You listen at six of the 15 pairs, 40 seconds each. Signs: the sentence said again with nothing added (error AF); a place or time alone, with no who or what (error AE). For either, the partner asks "Where?" or "When?" and the child answers.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Six children at the heard table, 40 seconds each (6 times 40 is 4 minutes). Say: "This picture is made up for practice." Show P40 and ask the child. A child may point to the place first and then say the sentence. Signs: "The girl waits." again (error AF); the place alone (error AE). Mark each look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw the sentence they made in the box on their page.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Reread the verses Hot Sun and Rain with the pointer, tracking each line left to right and then down. Find he in each verse: "He puts it on his head" and "He gets wet." Touch it and read it whole.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Cycle 5 response (10 minutes), in two groups by the day 39 check. Review: the puppet shows one slip and the child makes a fresh try on that one part only (day 27); nothing is scored. With the group that said "vee" for the sound (error U): sort name cards from sound cards for v and s, saying "/v/, the sound" and "vee, the name". With the group that wrote s for /v/ (error W): say the sound, pick the letter card, then write it. Spend 5 minutes with each group. The rest of the class chants the ten letters with a partner, pointing: a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s, v. Then for the last 5 minutes the whole class chants the ten letters with their keywords and rereads only the taught verses and the words he and we. No new letter or pattern today.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute. Table: small groups by the cycle 5 check, sort name cards from sound cards for v and s; blend and segment the spoken words sat, van and vat with counters. Writing place: write v and s; write the letter for a dictated sound; trace he and we. Sound and verse place: beat and rhyme cards, clap the beats of sunny, weather, puddle and umbrella; match two rhyming pictures (bat and hat, sun and bun, rain and train). The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread the two pages, grow two sentences with the cards, then do the exit picture. One projector: show the cards one at a time on the screen and say each addition aloud; children say their sentences to a partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No pocket chart: lay the word cards on a table or a folded sheet. No picture cards: draw a dog on a rug. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The pairs, the rotations and the group response.

- **5 min. Listen.** Read pages 2 and 3 of Warmer and Cooler from the lesson text.
  **Say.** "When does the air feel cooler?" Expect: when the sun goes down; at night.
- **5 min. Grow it.** Say "The air feels cool." Ask "When?" Your child adds "at night."
- **5 min. Join it.** Say "The sun goes down." "The air gets cooler." Your child joins them with "and."
- **5 min. Your turn.** Draw a dog on a rug. Your child says a short sentence, then a longer one.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say /v/ and /s/; your child writes each letter.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X40]] Draw a girl under a porch roof in the rain. Say "The girl waits." Ask: "Make it longer: tell where she waits."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second picture card in the pairs; keep the model, the exit and the response. Longer: a child who is secure adds both where and when to one sentence.

### Words and supports

Words: sentence, longer, and, where, when. Frames: "The ___ ___ at ___." "___ and ___." A pointed picture, a gesture or a home-language word is an answer; say the English back once. A child may point to the place card and the teacher adds the card to the chart.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 rereads Rain and Snow and starts the class picture journal. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of error AF (the same sentence again) and error AE (a place alone, like "At night.") with word cards: say who and what first, then add where, then when.
