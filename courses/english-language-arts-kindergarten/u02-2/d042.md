# Day 42: A talk with exchanges

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the three sign cards and the three talk-move cards. Give each pair a picture from page 2 or page 3 of Rain and Snow. Draw the exit card C42 and its picture and keep them out of sight. Note the six children for the exit and put them in three pairs. Divide the class into five groups of six for the group talk. Today's six are the next two unheard from each foundations group.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair day 41's exit for the more common of error B and error A. Read an invented page aloud (say it is made up for practice): "This page tells about mittens. Mittens keep hands warm. Mittens come in pairs." Pip puts the card "warm hands" on top as the topic (error B, a detail given as the topic). The class puts "mittens" on top and the two details under it. For error A: Pip says "Mittens are red," which the page never says; reread the exact words and point to what the page says. Then one fresh detail question about Rain and Snow: **Ask.** "What fills the dishes on the porch?" Expect: drops of rain, page 2. Then point to the class picture journal page on the wall and let one child say what it is about.

#### 10 min. Reread 01c, page 4

Whole class, then pairs. Reread page 4 (1 minute): the observer Ben and his partner. A child who dislikes the windy snow picture may look at the floor. **Ask.** "How does Ben know snow is coming?" Expect: the clouds are gray, the air is cold, ice is in the clouds. Show the three sign cards in a row. Pairs find the three signs Ben noticed and point to each sign card (4 minutes), then say them in a sentence: "Ben noticed gray clouds." Share (3 minutes). Review: an observer looks and listens closely and notices (day 35).

#### 10 min. Model: a talk with exchanges

Whole class. Review: turn and talk, where you ask, answer, trade places and say one thing back (day 1), and "Tell me more" after an answer (day 22). Today a talk goes on for several exchanges.

First, show the three talk-move cards (3 minutes): say, ask, answer and add. Hold up each card and make its sign.

Second, you and Pip hold a talk (5 minutes). You say a detail from page 2: "Many drops make a puddle." Pip asks: "Where does a puddle gather?" You answer and add one more: "On the ground. The sun can dry it." Pip answers and asks one more: "What does the sun do next?" You answer: "It dries the ground." Count each turn on your fingers; stop after four.

Third, show what a talk looks like (2 minutes): one speaks, one listens, and a token passes. Pip grabs the token and talks over you (error AR, two voices at once); the class says "wait for the token." Then Pip asks one question, hears one answer and stops (error AS, the talk stops too soon); the class says "and one more."

#### 15 min. Pairs

Pairs, with the token and a picture from page 2 or page 3. Round one (6 minutes): the child holding the token says one detail about the picture, the partner asks one question, the first child answers and adds one more, and the partner asks one more question. Pass the token. Round two (6 minutes): the other child begins. Share two pairs with the class (3 minutes). You listen at six of the 15 pairs, one minute each, and write the number of exchanges next to each pair's names. Signs: both talk at once (error AR); the pair stops after one question and answer (error AS). For either, put the token between them and say "one more."

#### 15 min. Group talk

Five groups of six, one group at a time in a circle with you for 3 minutes (5 times 3 is 15). The other four groups draw a detail from Rain and Snow at their tables. Each circle talks about page 2 or page 3 with the token: say, ask, answer and add. Six children at 30 seconds each is 3 minutes. Any child may pass the token. Write the exchanges you hear on the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Three pairs at the heard table, the day's six (80 seconds a pair, after you read the card to the six). Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card C42 twice, showing its picture. Ask each pair ([[s02-X42]]). Partners trade turns, at least two exchanges each. Signs: both talk at once (error AR); the pair stops after one question and answer (error AS). Mark each look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw the detail they liked best from Rain and Snow.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Letter search in the verse Snow. Point to the letters g and i, in both cases, with a finger and say each sound: /g/ in grass and go, /i/ in is and mitten.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Meet g and i again (10 minutes). Review: you met g and i on day 41. Say the verbal path while the whole arm writes in the air. For g: curve back and around to the left like a c, then straight down and hook to the left. For i: a short line down, then a dot. Capital G curves like a c with a short line in; capital I is a tall line with a line across the top and the bottom. Say the keyword and the sound with each, then write each on handwriting paper, three times, saying the sound.

Map a new word, to (5 minutes). Say the line "We go to dig" and point to to. The t says /t/, as in top, and the o is the part to remember here. Read to whole by sight. Children find to in the verse, and find on, which you mapped on day 41.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute. Table: adding a sound, spoken with counters: lay the counters for am, then place a new counter before them and say the new word: add /h/ to am to make ham; add /s/ to am to make Sam; add /h/ to it to make hit; add /s/ to it to make sit. Error S: the new sound goes at the end (am-s); place the new counter first. Writing place: write g and i in lines; write the letter for a dictated sound. Sound and verse place: say the new word after a counter is added; find the letters g and i on the verse. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the talk with the token between you for four turns, then the exit card, taking both parts. One projector: show the page 4 picture and the sign cards on the screen; the circle talk is a floor circle for six, not a screen task.

### At home

**Swap.** No sign cards: draw gray clouds, a cold breath and ice bits. No token: pass a spoon. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The group circle and the rotations.

- **5 min. Listen.** Read page 4 of Rain and Snow aloud from the lesson text.
  **Say.** "How does Ben know snow is coming?" Expect: gray clouds, cold air, ice in the clouds.
- **5 min. A talk.** Hold the spoon. Say "Drops make a puddle." Pass the spoon; your child asks a question.
  **Say.** "Where does a puddle gather?" Expect: on the ground. Answer and ask one more.
- **10 min. Take turns.** Talk about a picture of rain or snow, four turns each, passing the spoon.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say /g/ and /i/; your child writes g and i.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X42]] Read: "Dew shines on the grass. The sun climbs. The dew is gone." Your child tells one thing, then answers your question and asks one more, two turns each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second round of pairs; keep the model, the group talk, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure keeps a talk going for four exchanges.

### Words and supports

Words: observer, notice, exchange, question, answer. Frames: "I noticed ___." "What ___?" "I think ___. What do you think?" A child who finds group talk hard may talk in the pair rounds only; pointing, a token on a picture and a communication aid count.
