# Day 92: Make a note that answers our question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one line of feedback on each day 89 H to N paper, such as "Make the small k again." Cut page 3 of The Oak into sentence strips. Make labels and stickers. Cover E92. Choose six children for the T2 record and six for onset and rime. Read aloud: "I can make a note" and "Write the words I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Say oak, acorn, squirrel and jay, each with a picture. Habitat and resource are heard in the reading; nobody is asked to say them yet. Point to the research-cycle wall pictures: today is find and note. Day 91's exit asked children to choose the drawing the reading supports; some chose a picture the words did not say (error B). Reread one sentence aloud, "The bird builds a nest high in the branches", and trace with a finger what the bird does in two pictures, one that matches and one that does not.

#### 10 min. Read 05a whole

Whole class. Read The Oak, part a, aloud once, showing each page's picture. Children listen for food. **Ask.** "What food does the oak give?" Expect: acorns. Nuts stay pictures only; nothing is passed round.

#### 5 min. Which sentence?

Pairs. Our class question today is "What does a squirrel get from an oak?" Lay the sentence strips from page 3 on the floor. **Ask.** "Which sentence would answer our animal question?" Pairs point to one strip. Expect: "A squirrel eats acorns." Walk past six pairs. Signs: pointing to a sentence that is true but answers another question, such as the roots taking water (error E).

#### 10 min. Model a note (W.K.7, W.K.8)

Whole class. Review: the labelled note (day 83). On the chart draw a squirrel holding an acorn on a picture note card. Add the label squirrel, copied from the text. Say: "We hold two texts. The Oak is text A. Who Depends on a Tree? is text B." Glue a source sticker below the picture: A, page 3. This is new: a source sticker names the text a fact came from. Say the fact in a child's words: "A squirrel eats acorns from the oak." Leave the fact line empty; tomorrow children say their facts for writing. Let the puppet slip: "The squirrel is from text B." Ask the class how to check. Expect: look at where we heard it, page 3 of The Oak. Signs: a right fact with the wrong text named (error AI).

#### 5 min. Return to 07 (RL.K.1)

Pairs. Review: asking and answering about key details (unit 1, day 3). Reread page 4 of The Tree Beside the Path, the leaves falling, and say again that the story is made up. Each pair asks one question about a key detail and answers it, then points to the sentence.

#### 10 min. Make a note

Individuals. Each child chooses another fact from The Oak, a jay eating an acorn, roots taking water or a bird on a branch, and draws it on a picture note card. Take a label from the pile and a source sticker marked A with the page. Children say the fact to a partner. Walk past six cards and ask each child where the fact was heard. Signs: no label (error C); a fact the page does not give (error D).

#### 10 min. Ask about what we heard (RI.K.1, SL.K.2)

Pairs, with six heard in turn. Partners ask one question about The Oak and answer it, and one asks "What does ___ mean?" once. While they work, call the six not yet heard to you, about 100 seconds each. Ask each to ask a question about a dependent, answer it, name the topic, and retell two details. Then say "A squirrel holds an acorn. It nibbles." and ask what "it" means. Expect: the squirrel. Mark on the T2 columns (shown, shown with help, not yet seen). Signs: silence when a word is not clear (error Y).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E92 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X92a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X92b]], 30 seconds each, while the class adds colour to its notes. Signs: a true fact that does not answer the question (error E); a picture neither text supports (error D); the right fact credited to the wrong text (error AI).

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: onset and rime (unit 1, day 24). Blend /s/ /it/, /h/ /it/ and /p/ /it/, a counter for each part. Children say each word.

#### 10 min. Short i, again

Whole class, then pairs. Read sit, hit and pit from tiles, all together. Then chain sat, sit, sip on tiles, one letter changing each time, and say which sound changed. Pairs chain pit to pat. Signs: naming s as the changed letter in sat and sit (error L).

#### 12 min. A Pig in a Pit, reader 2

Small groups of three. Review: A Pig in a Pit (day 91). Groups reread, one sentence a page, and say what happened on their page. Cover the picture while they read.

#### 6 min. Words is and it

Whole class. Review: is and it (day 91). Read each on its card, then find them in the reader.

#### 7 min. Revise H to N

Individuals. Review: revise after one line of written feedback (unit 3, day 85). Hand back each paper and read its line aloud. Each child reforms the letters named. Signs: an unrecognisable N or K (error AK); B bee but not b (error AC).

#### 5 min. Tree Words, verse 3

Whole class. Reread verse 3 with the actions. Find two rhyming words, twig and big, and point to each in the large print.

#### 15 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each on onset and rime (P4): say "/h/ /it/. What word?" and "Split sit into its first sound and the rest." Write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest read these cards in pairs: "Tim hit it." "Is it a pig?" "Kim sat in the pit." Signs: cannot join /s/ and /it/ into sit (error AF).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read The Oak a page at a time, make one note with a drawn sticker, and chain sat, sit, sip with buttons. One projector: show the page pictures and the chart on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No sticker: draw a small circle and write A in it. No tiles: write letters on paper squares.

**Skip.** The record, the groups and the rotation.

- **10 min. A note.** Say twice: "An oak grows acorns. A squirrel eats acorns." Your child draws a squirrel with an acorn and says the word squirrel as you write it.
  **Say.** "Which line does your note come from?" Expect: A squirrel eats acorns.
- **10 min. A source.** Your child draws a circle under the note and puts A in it: where the fact was heard.
- **10 min. Blend.** Say /s/ /it/, then sit. Do hit and pit. Your child pushes a button for each part.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X92a]] and [[s03-X92b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "Our question is what a robin gets from a tree. Text A: A robin builds a nest on a branch. Text B: A robin has a bright chest." Draw a robin on a nest labelled A, a robin labelled B, and a robin hopping with no label.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the model note, the exit and short i; cut Tree Words. Longer: a secure child makes a second note and sticks a different source on it.

### Words and supports

Words: oak, acorn, squirrel, jay, note, source sticker. Frame: "My note is from text ___, page ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can choose the note that answers the question and name the text it came from. If many credited the right fact to the wrong text (error AI), day 93 opens with five minutes: match the note to its sentence and fix the sticker. Day 93 models writing one fact from a note under its topic.
