# Exit cards, days 89 to 94

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 89 card E89

**Read:** "This page is about a palm tree. Its big leaves, called fronds, make shade. A family rests in the shade on a hot day."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture group shows the topic with one detail? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a palm tree with an arrow to a family resting in its shade; a family on a blanket with a small tree behind; a sun with no tree.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Who needs something the palm gives, and what is it?"

## Day 90 card E90

**Read:** "A bee visits an apple tree. It drinks sweet nectar from the blossoms. Nectar is the bee's food."

**Mark (item a):** Which drawing shows the animal and what it gets from the tree? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a bee at a blossom on a branch; a bee flying alone; an apple tree with no animal.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What does the bee get, and which words say so?"

## Day 91 card E91

**Read:** "A small owl sleeps in a hollow tree during the day. The hollow tree is the owl's resting place."

**Mark (item a):** Which drawing would add an accurate detail to the notebook? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: an owl inside a hollow in a tree trunk; an owl on a bare branch at night; an owl flying at night.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words in the reading support your drawing?"

## Day 92 card E92

**Read:** "Our question is what a rabbit gets from a tree. Text A: A rabbit can eat tree leaves. Text B: A rabbit has soft fur and long ears."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture note answers our question? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a rabbit eating a leaf, labelled A; a rabbit with long ears, labelled B; a rabbit hopping, with no label.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which text did your note come from?"

## Day 93 card E93

**Read:** "This text is about a birch tree. Its bark peels in thin, papery pieces."

**Mark (item a):** Which labelled drawing would teach this to a friend? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a birch tree with peeling bark, labelled birch; the same drawing with no label; a stack of paper, labelled paper.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What fact will you dictate?"

## Day 94 card E94

**Read:** "First note: A caterpillar chews a leaf. Second note: A caterpillar needs food to grow."

**Mark (item a):** Which one picture shows both notes together? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a caterpillar chewing a leaf on a twig; a leaf alone; a caterpillar on bare ground.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What sentence joins the two notes?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 89: item a, The palm tree with an arrow to the family in its shade. Not yet: marks the family on the blanket as the topic (X, a minor detail chosen as the topic); marks the sun with no tree (B, a picture that does not show the page). Item b, The family needs shade. Not yet: says The palm gives a nest (D, a fact the page does not give).
- Day 90: item a, The bee at a blossom on a branch. Not yet: marks the bee flying alone or the tree with no animal (B, a picture that leaves out the animal or what it gets). Item b, Nectar, its food (the words: drinks sweet nectar, nectar is the bee's food). Not yet: says A bee. A tree. with no link (A, two things named, none linked); says The bee gets a nest (D, a need the reading does not give).
- Day 91: item a, The owl inside a hollow in a tree trunk. Not yet: marks the owl on the bare branch or the flying owl (B, a drawing the reading does not support). Item b, sleeps in a hollow tree (or: the hollow tree is the owl's resting place). Not yet: says It hunts mice (D, a fact the reading does not give).
- Day 92: item a, The rabbit eating a leaf, labelled A. Not yet: marks the long-ears rabbit (E, true, but it does not answer the question); marks the hopping rabbit (D, supported by neither text). Item b, Text A. Not yet: names Text B for the leaf note (AI, a right fact given the wrong source).
- Day 93: item a, The birch tree with peeling bark, labelled birch. Not yet: marks the drawing with no label (C, a topic left unnamed); marks the stack of paper (B, a drawing of the wrong thing). Item b, Birch bark peels in thin pieces like paper. Not yet: says Birch bark is warm (D, a fact the text does not give).
- Day 94: item a, The caterpillar chewing a leaf on a twig. Not yet: marks the leaf alone or the caterpillar on bare ground (B, a picture that shows only one note). Item b, A caterpillar eats a leaf to get the food it needs to grow. Not yet: says A caterpillar chews. It needs food. with no link (A, two notes left unjoined); says It eats bark (D, a fact the notes do not give).
