# Day 93: Write one fact under its topic

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out each child's day 92 note and hang the three-box chart. Write the labels. Hang chart paper with a line near the top for the topic and a line below for a fact. Cover E93. Choose six children for the T2 record and six for onset and rime. Read aloud: the student page says "I can write about my topic" and "Write the words I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Review: note and source sticker (day 92). Say topic, fact, note and source with a gesture each: a hand over the head, a pointing finger, a writing hand, a finger on a sticker. Show the day 92 notes. Day 92's exit asked children to name the text a note came from; some gave the right fact the wrong text (error AI). Hold up one note, read its fact, and point to the strip on page 3 of The Oak it came from; the puppet says "text B" and children fix the sticker.

#### 10 min. Reread 05a

Whole class. Reread pages 1 and 3 of The Oak aloud. **Ask.** "What must the opening name?" Expect: what the page is about, an oak. **Ask.** "Which sentence supports its food detail?" Expect: "A squirrel eats acorns." Signs: naming a detail as the topic (error X).

#### 15 min. Model informative writing (W.K.2)

Whole class. Review: a drawing with a label and one fact (unit 1, day 16). On the chart paper write while children watch. First the topic: draw an oak and write oak above it. Then one fact below: say "An oak grows acorns," and write it, saying each word. Then point to the source sticker, A, page 2. Say the three moves aloud and have children say them with you: "Name it. Tell one fact. Show where I heard it." Let the puppet show a drawing of an oak with no label and say "This is my page." Ask what is missing. Expect: the name, oak. Signs: a topic left unnamed (error C).

#### 10 min. Write together

Whole class. Turn a different day 92 note, the jay with an acorn, into a sentence. Children say it: "A jay eats an acorn." You write it while they say it, and children point to each word in the air. Read it back and point to the note's sticker.

#### 15 min. Write your own page

Individuals, with six heard in turn. Each child draws the topic on a drawing card, takes a label you wrote before class, and says one fact from the day 92 note to a partner, who says it back. Draw eight minutes. Then call the six not yet heard to you one at a time, about 90 seconds each: they ask a question about page 3 and answer it, name the topic, retell two details of The Oak, and dictate their fact, which you write. Mark on the T2 columns (shown, shown with help, not yet seen). Finish the other facts after class. Pages are kept, not scored today. Signs: a fact the text does not give (error D); no label (error C).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E93 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X93a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X93b]], 30 seconds each, while the class adds a label to its page. Signs: a drawing with no label (error C); a drawing of the wrong thing (error B); a fact the text does not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Blend /s/ /it/ and /f/ /it/, then split fit and sit back into two parts. Children say each part and the word.

#### 11 min. Short i spelling

Whole class, then individuals. Review: short i (day 91). Dictate sit, hit and lip. For each, push a counter for each sound, write a letter for each, and say which sound changed from the day 91 words: sit to hit, the first; sit to lip, the first and the last. Eleven minutes, three words. Write on side 2. Signs: writing a for the vowel (error U); dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 12 min. A Pig in a Pit, reader 2

Pairs. Review: A Pig in a Pit (days 91 and 92). Partners reread, one pointing under each word while the other reads, swapping at page 5. They say what happened. Signs: points across a space (error AB); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 6 min. Words is and it

Whole class. Review: is and it (day 91). Read each on its card. Read a sentence aloud, "A pig is in a pit," and children hold up the is card at the word.

#### 8 min. Letters O to T

Whole class, then partners. Show shuffled letter cards O to T. Each child names both cases of one card, about 15 seconds a child, seven and a half minutes for thirty. While they wait, partners page through picture words. Record on the P2 column. Signs: names B bee but not b, for any pair (error AC).

#### 5 min. Tree Words, verse 3

Whole class. Reread verse 3 with the actions. Clap the two-syllable word, maple, and say it in two parts.

#### 13 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each on onset and rime (P4): "Say /h/ /it/. What word?" and "Split fit into its first sound and the rest." Write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest read these cards in pairs: "A pig is big." "Tim can sit." "Kim did a dip." Signs: cannot join /f/ and /it/ (error AF).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread The Oak a page at a time, write one topic and one fact together on paper, and spell sit, hit and lip with buttons. One projector: show the chart and the page on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart paper: write on plain paper. No buttons: use pebbles.

**Skip.** The record, the letters and the rotation.

- **10 min. Name it.** Say twice: "An oak grows acorns." Your child draws an oak and says the word oak as you write it above.
  **Say.** "What is your page about?" Expect: an oak.
- **10 min. One fact.** Your child tells you one fact, and you write it below. They point to where they heard it.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say sit, hit and lip. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes a letter for each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X93a]] and [[s03-X93b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "This text is about a willow tree. Its long branches hang down to the ground." Draw a willow labeled willow, the same drawing with no label, and a curtain labeled curtain.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the exit and short i spelling; cut Tree Words. Longer: a secure child writes the topic word and a second fact on the page.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, fact, note, source. Frame: "My page is about ___. One fact is ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the labeled drawing and say one fact the text gives. If many chose the drawing with no label (error C), day 94 opens with five minutes: name the tree in a label first. Day 94 puts two notes together into one sentence about an animal and a tree.
