# Three-box chart

A wall chart made on day 90 and kept up, with three large boxes joined by arrows, left to right.

1. **The living thing** (an animal). A picture box.
2. **What it needs.** A picture box.
3. **The tree part that gives it.** A picture box.

Under the boxes, a line for the joining word (because, so). The class fills the boxes with the animal, the need and the tree part from the day's text, a box a day. Children have a small paper copy.

A room with nothing: fold a sheet of paper into three and draw arrows between the parts.
