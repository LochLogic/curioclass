# Day 89: What is the whole page about?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the needs chart and the class question chart; keep the card "Who needs trees?" turned over. Set out the topic card (a tree) and four detail picture cards: roots take in water; the trunk carries the water up; leaves make food; the tree grows. Mark E89's page. Choose six children not yet heard for the day's record. Read aloud: the student page says "I can say what a page is about" and "Spell the words I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Review: need and because (unit 3, days 80 to 88). Say each with a gesture, then connect and repeat: hands joined for connect, a rolling hand for repeat. Point to the sun and the ground on the needs chart. Day 88's exit asked children to join two facts; some left them apart (error A). Use the repair card for error A: two fact pictures with an empty arrow. Say two chart facts, "Roots take in water" and "Leaves make food", then say the joining word on the arrow, "so". Children say it with you, once. Trunk and mineral wait for the reading.

#### 10 min. Read 03 whole

Whole class. Show the cover of A Tree Is a Living Thing and say it is a chant. Read the six pages as printed, no tune: you say each line, the class joins the second time. Say "tiny bits in the soil" once for minerals and touch your arm for the trunk. Ask no question while reading. After the last page children whisper to a partner one thing the tree does.

#### 10 min. Model main topic and details (RI.K.2)

Whole class, then pairs. Review: topic and detail (unit 1, day 9) and main topic (unit 2, day 49). Say: "What do all the lines tell about?" **Ask.** Expect: a tree. Place the topic card (a tree) on the chart. Place two detail cards under it: roots take in water (a need the chant names) and the tree grows (a thing it does). Let the puppet say a slip: "The page is about roots!" Say: "Roots are one detail. All the lines tell about the tree." Pairs retell the two details to each other, two minutes, while you walk past six pairs. Signs: one detail named as the whole topic (error X).

#### 10 min. Repeated lines (RI.K.3)

Whole class, then pairs. Reread page 3 and page 4 slowly. Model how a repeated line explains a need: say the line, "The trunk carries the water up"; say what the tree gets, "The leaves get water"; say "because": "The leaves get water because the trunk carries it up." Review: the connection arrow (unit 3, day 81). Place the arrow card between the two ideas. Then read page 2 of What a Tree Needs and say its line in plain words: "The roots drink water from the soil." Pairs say page 4's line the same way: line, what the tree gets, because. **Ask.** "What does the tree get?" Expect: food. Walk past six pairs. Signs: two facts named with no joining word (error A).

#### 5 min. Return to the story (T1)

Whole class, six children not yet heard in front. Review: the story-map (unit 2, day 49). Reread The Tree Beside the Path page 5, the winter buds (about 50 words). Say: "Noor and the tree are made up." Say the T1 prompt: "Ask a question about how the tree changes." Each of the six asks a question and answers it from the page, about 45 seconds each, four and a half minutes in all. The class points to the sentence while each speaks. Record on the T1 columns. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Who needs trees?

Whole class. Turn over the card and write the new class question on the chart: "Who needs trees?" Pairs whisper one living thing that might need a tree, one minute. Hear six pairs, about 35 seconds each, and draw each idea quickly beside the question. There is no right answer yet; every idea goes up. Any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Draw and retell

Individuals, then pairs. Each child draws the topic and two details from the chant on a drawing card, six minutes. Pairs retell the topic and two details, two minutes, all at once. Take dictation from the day's six, about a minute each, while the others draw; label for the rest as you walk, and finish their words after class. Signs: a detail drawn alone with no topic (error X).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read page 4 of We Depend on Trees aloud twice, its picture covered; the class reads it on day 95. Show strip E89 and ask [[s03-X89av2]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest for the picture group. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X89bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class finishes its drawing cards. Signs: the family taken as the whole topic (error X); a picture with no tree (error B); a fact the page does not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Clap pencil, cat and banana. Children clap each word in parts and say how many.

#### 8 min. Chain a word

Whole class, then pairs. Review: change one letter on tiles and say which sound changed (unit 3, day 88). Model on tiles: mat, then sat (the first sound changed), then sap (the last), then tap (the first). Say each time which sound changed. Pairs then build tap, change the last letter to make tan, and change the first to make pan. Signs: naming a as the changed letter (error L).

#### 10 min. Ants at a Mat, reader 1

Ten groups of three, then pairs. Review: Ants at a Mat (days 85 to 88). Each group reads one page aloud, in order, page 1 to page 10, and the group says what happened on its page. Then pairs reread the whole reader, one reading while the other points under each word. Walk past six pairs. Signs: guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 6 min. Spell with counters

Whole class, then individuals. Review: a counter for each sound (unit 2, day 42). Model once: say mat, push a counter for each sound, write a letter for each. Each child spells mat and sap on side 2. Signs: dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 4 min. Words at and has

Whole class. Review: at and has (day 85). Read each on its card, then find them in the reader.

#### 7 min. Letters H to N

Individuals. Review: letter names, both cases (unit 2). Say each letter name, H to N, about 55 seconds a letter. All thirty print the capital and the small letter from dictation (P2). Keep the papers for written feedback. Signs: a small letter drawn in the capital's shape (error AC); an unrecognisable N or K (error AK).

#### 5 min. Sounds j, k, l, m, n, p

Whole class, then pairs. Say each sound one at a time; each child writes a letter (P3), three minutes. Partners then say the sounds from the consonant cards, two minutes. Signs: the letter name for a sound, "em" for m (error T); b for m (error O).

#### 15 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children not yet heard, two minutes each, twelve in all: ask [[s03-P4b]] and write who was heard on the record sheet. Signs: counting every letter as a part (error AE). The rest sit in pairs and reread these short a cards: "Nat has a mat." "A cat sat." "Dan has a map." "Zac can nap." Each pair then chains one more word on tiles.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the chant a page at a time, then place the topic card and two details. Spell mat and sap with buttons. One projector: show the chant's pictures and the chart on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No buttons: use pebbles.

**Skip.** The chart, the groups and the rotation.

- **10 min. A topic.** Say twice each: "A tree is a living thing. Roots take in water from the ground. Leaves make food for the tree." Your child draws a tree and two things about it.
  **Say.** "What is this about?" Expect: a tree.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say mat. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes a letter for each. Then sap.
- **10 min. Letters.** Say h, j and k as sounds; your child writes each letter.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X89av2]] [[s03-X89bv2]] Read page 4 of We Depend on Trees aloud. Your child says what the page is about and who needs what the tree gives.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the topic model, the exit and the spelling; cut the letters to three. Longer: a secure child names a second thing the tree does and says it with because.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, detail, living. Frame: "The page is about ___. One detail is ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the topic with one detail and say who needs what the tree gives. If many chose the family as the topic (error X), day 90 opens with five minutes: place the details under the topic card. Day 90 reads Who Depends on a Tree?, fills three boxes for an animal and what it needs, and ends with an exit ticket on a new card.
