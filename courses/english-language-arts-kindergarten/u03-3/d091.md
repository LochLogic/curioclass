# Day 91: Add what we heard to the notebook

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the day 90 check records into four groups by recorded error. Hang the three-box chart and the pictures of a branch and a shelter. Cover E91. Choose six children for the T2 record, six for syllables and five for the print card. Write the cards: "Tim sat in a pit." "A pig can dig." "Kim has a lid." Read aloud: "I can add a detail" and "Write the words I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Review: depend (day 90). Say gather, record and support with a gesture each: arms pulling in, a finger writing, a hand under the other. Show the picture of a branch and of a shelter. Day 90's exit asked children to name the animal, what it gets and how they go together; some named the animal and the tree with no link (error A). On the chart place an arrow card between two boxes, a squirrel and nuts, and say "because": "The squirrel needs nuts because it eats them." Children say it once.

#### 10 min. Reread 06 (RI.K.3)

Whole class. Reread the bird page, page 3, aloud. **Ask.** "Which words tell where the bird's nest is?" Expect: high in the branches. **Ask.** "Which words tell how the branches and the nest go together?" Expect: the branches hold the nest up. Pairs point to the nest picture, then to the picture of the branch. Signs: pointing to a sentence that is not about the nest (error B).

#### 10 min. Rereading record (T2)

Pairs, with six heard in turn. Pairs reread the 06 pictures and point to the nest page, ten minutes, while you call the six not yet heard to you one at a time, about 90 seconds each. Ask each: [[s03-T2a]], then [[s03-T2b]]; then read the nest line on page 3 with the child and ask [[s03-T2c]]. Then say [[s03-T2d]]. Mark each (shown, shown with help, not yet seen) on the T2 columns. Signs: silence when a word is not clear (error Y).

#### 10 min. Ask and answer

Pairs. Review: a question and clarify (unit 2, day 43). Model once with a child: you ask "Who depends on the tree?", the child answers, and you ask one more question. Then ask for clarification: "What does depend mean?" Partners take turns: one asks "Who depends on the tree?" and one more question, the other answers, and one partner asks "What does ___ mean?" with the talking token. Fade the starters after two pairs. Walk past six pairs.

#### 20 min. Notebook

Individuals, then pairs. Each child adds one detail to a notebook page: draw it, take a label you wrote before class, tell a partner the fact, and point to the place in 06 where it was heard. Draw eight minutes; tell and point three minutes. Take dictation from ten children over the rest, about a minute each, and finish the others after class. Detail ideas from 06: the squirrel's nuts, the bird's nest held by branches, the deer's leaves. Signs: a drawing the reading does not support (error B); a fact the reading does not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E91 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X91a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X91b]], 30 seconds each, while the class adds a label to its notebook drawing. Signs: a drawing the reading does not support (error B); a fact the reading does not give (error D).

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Four groups. Use the day 90 check records (cycle 13). Group one: reads from the picture, or cannot read at, has or it (errors K, R and S): cover the picture, blend the letters, then check. Group two: names the changed letter wrongly or says a letter name for a sound (errors L, T and U): line up the pair and touch the different letter. Group three: drops the middle sound, writes b for another letter or counts every sound as a syllable (errors V, O and AE): a counter for each sound, then clap the parts. Group four: points across a space (error AB): frame one word at a time. Three minutes of modeling, four of repair, three of retry with a word or picture not used before. You lead the group with most unresolved errors; partners use the card.

#### 4 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Clap ladder, window and dog. Children say each in parts and how many.

#### 8 min. New: short i

Whole class, then individuals. Review: the letter i, sound /i/, keyword igloo (unit 2, day 41). Point to i on the sound-spelling map. Say: "In sit, i says /i/." Push a counter for each sound of sit, write s, i, t, and blend it. Then chain sat to sit on tiles and say which sound changed: the middle one. Each child writes sit on side 2. Add sit under i on the map. Signs: writing a for the vowel in sit (error U); saying /s/ /t/ with no middle (error V).

#### 4 min. Words is and it

Whole class. Map each word. For is: /i/ and /z/, with s saying /z/ as the part to remember. For it: sound it out, /i/ /t/. Review: at and has (day 85). Read all four on cards. Signs: cannot read is without sounding it out (error S).

#### 8 min. A Pig in a Pit, reader 2

Pairs. You read the title. Pairs read one sentence a page, then say what happened. Cover the picture while they read. Walk past six pairs. Signs: guesses the word from the picture (error R).

#### 4 min. Tree Words, verse 3

Whole class. Read verse 3 aloud once. Children join the actions: wave hands, flutter fingers down. Find the first line in the large print and point to it.

#### 22 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each, syllables: ask "How many parts in pumpkin? Say it in parts, then together," and the same for tiger. Then hear five children two minutes each on the print card (P1): track the two pages, turn the page, point to leaves, mark a space between words. The rest reread reader 2 in pairs, read the short i cards, and chain one more word. Signs: reads the second line first (error AJ); points across a space (error AB). Write who was heard on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread the bird page, draw one detail, and chain sat to sit with buttons. One projector: show the page, the chart and the map on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No buttons: use pebbles.

**Skip.** The record, the repair groups and the print card.

- **10 min. The nest.** Say twice: "A bird builds a nest high in the branches. The branches hold the nest up." Your child draws it.
  **Say.** "Which words tell what holds the nest?" Expect: the branches.
- **10 min. Notebook.** Your child draws one more thing from the lines and points to the words that tell it.
- **10 min. A new sound.** Say sit. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes s, i, t. Change sat to sit.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X91a]] and [[s03-X91b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "A robin builds a nest on a branch of a maple tree. The branch holds the nest up." Draw a robin on a nest on a branch, a robin flying over bare ground, and a maple tree with no bird.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the notebook, the exit and short i; cut Tree Words. Longer: a secure child adds a second detail and points to both places.

### Words and supports

Words: depend, gather, record, support. Frame: "The ___ shows ___ because ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can choose the drawing the reading supports and point to the words that say so. If many picked a drawing the reading does not support (error B), day 92 opens with five minutes: reread a sentence and trace what the animal does in each picture. Day 92 reads The Oak and makes a picture note with a source sticker.
