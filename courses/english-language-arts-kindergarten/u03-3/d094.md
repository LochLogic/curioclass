# Day 94: Put two notes together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make three notes on picture note cards with source sticker: a jay with an acorn (A, page 3), a nest held by branches (B, page 3) and a squirrel with an acorn (A, page 3). Mark page 3 of The Sugar Maple for the exit. Choose six children for the T2 record and six for onset and rime. Read aloud: "I can put two notes together" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Review: note and source (day 92). Say note, put together and source with a gesture each: a writing hand, two hands joined, a finger on a sticker. Show two day 92 notes side by side. Day 93's exit asked children to choose the labeled drawing; some chose the drawing with no name on it (error C). Hold up a drawing of an oak with no label and ask what to write first. Expect: oak. Write it above the drawing.

#### 10 min. Reread two sources

Whole class, with six heard in turn. Reread page 3 of The Oak and page 3 of Who Depends on a Tree?, the nest page. **Ask.** "What does the second note add to the first?" Expect: where the bird's nest is held, in the branches. While pairs point to each sentence, call the six not yet heard to you, about a minute each, for the T2 record on 06: ask "What holds the bird's nest?" and "What is this text mostly about?" Mark on the T2 columns (shown, shown with help, not yet seen).

#### 10 min. Two notes on the chart

Whole class. Place the three notes you made on the chart. Say the animal each shows: a jay, a bird in a nest, a squirrel. **Ask.** "Which note belongs with the animal we name?" Ask the class to name the animal, a bird. Expect: the jay note and the nest note. Set the squirrel note aside and say why: "It is a squirrel, not a bird." Say aloud the stickers: the jay note came from text A, the nest note from text B. Signs: setting a note aside with no reason (error D); a note joined to the wrong animal (error B).

#### 15 min. Compose together (W.K.2)

Whole class. Review: a connection with because (unit 3, day 82). Draw an arrow between the two bird notes. The class says one sentence that names the animal and the tree and joins both notes: "A bird needs the oak because it eats the acorns and the branches hold its nest." You write it while children say it; children point to each word in the air, and name each source, A for the acorns and B for the nest. Read it back. This is a model for the class, not a score, and later work does not repeat it. Signs: two notes said one after the other with no joining word (error A).

#### 15 min. Add a detail

Individuals. Each child draws one combined picture, an animal and a tree it needs, on a drawing card, takes labels you wrote before class for the animal and the tree, and says one more true detail from either source to a partner. Draw eight minutes. Take dictation from the day's six, about a minute each, while the others draw; finish the others after class. Signs: a fact the sources do not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read page 3 of The Sugar Maple aloud twice, its picture covered; the class first reads it on day 103. Say its two notes, show strip E94 and ask [[s03-X94av2]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X94bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class finishes its pictures. Signs: a picture that shows only one note (error B); two notes said with no link (error A); a fact the notes do not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: onset and rime (unit 1, day 24). Say /p/ /in/, /l/ /id/ and /w/ /in/ with a sweep of the hand; children say each word. Then split win into /w/ and /in/.

#### 8 min. Short i spelling

Whole class, then partners. Review: short i (day 91). Dictate pin, lid and win. For each, push a counter for each sound and write a letter for each. Partners check each other's letters against the counters. Write on side 2. Signs: writing a for the vowel (error U); dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 12 min. A Pig in a Pit, reader 2

Pairs. Review: A Pig in a Pit (days 91 to 93). Partners reread and say what happened. No new pattern. Walk past six pairs. Signs: guesses the word from the picture (error R).

#### 5 min. Words is and it

Whole class. Review: is and it (day 91). Read each on its card.

#### 7 min. Print O to T

Individuals. Review: letter names, both cases (unit 2). Say each letter name, O to T. All thirty print both cases from dictation, [[s03-P2b]], about 70 seconds a letter. While they print, hear up to six children whose day 93 turn was not heard or not clear, 30 seconds each, name the shuffled cards O to T: [[s03-P2a]]. Keep the printing for written feedback. Signs: B bee but not b for any pair (error AC); an unrecognisable form (error AK).

#### 5 min. Sounds q, r, s, t, v, w

Whole class, then partners. Say each sound once: q as /kw/, r, s, t, v, w, then short a, e, i, o and u; each child writes a letter for each, [[s03-P3b]], three minutes. Then partners say the sounds from the consonant cards while you hear four children, 30 seconds each, give the letter for a sound and the sound for a letter card: [[s03-P3a]]. Signs: the letter name for a sound (error T); b for another letter (error O).

#### 5 min. Tree Words, verse 3

Whole class. Reread verse 3 with the actions. Find the first line in the large print and track its words with a finger.

#### 13 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children two minutes each on onset and rime (P4): "Say /p/ /in/. What word?" and "Split lid into its first sound and the rest." Write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest read these cards in pairs: "Tim is in a pit." "Kim hit it." "It is a big pig." Signs: cannot join /l/ and /id/ (error AF).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Lay two notes side by side, say one joined sentence, and spell pin, lid and win with buttons. One projector: show the notes and the chart on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No stickers: write A or B in a circle. No buttons: use pebbles.

**Skip.** The record, the letters and the rotation.

- **10 min. Two notes.** Say twice: "A jay eats an acorn from the oak." and "Branches hold a bird's nest." Your child draws each in a small box.
  **Say.** "How do the two go together?" Expect: a bird needs the oak because it eats acorns and branches hold its nest.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say pin, lid and win. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes a letter for each.
- **10 min. Letters.** Say q, r and s as sounds; your child writes each letter.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X94av2]] [[s03-X94bv2]] Read page 3 of The Sugar Maple aloud. Your child says one sentence that joins the seed and the squirrel.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the two notes, the joined sentence, the exit and short i spelling; cut Tree Words. Longer: a secure child joins a third note with because or so.

### Words and supports

Words: note, put together, source. Frame: "A ___ needs the ___ because ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the picture that holds both notes and say a sentence that joins them. If many said the two notes with no link (error A), day 95 opens with five minutes: join two notes with because or so. Day 95 reads We Depend on Trees and connects a person's need with a tree resource.
