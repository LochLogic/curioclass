# Day 90: Which animal needs the tree?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fold chart paper into three boxes with an arrow between each, and tape it up: the animal, what it needs, where I heard it (a small book picture). Under the boxes leave a line for the joining word. Mark E90's page. Choose six children not yet heard for the story return and six for the syllable check. Read aloud: the student page says "I can fill the three boxes" and "Write the words I say. Change one letter."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. Say animal and need with a gesture (review, unit 3). Say depend as new: "To depend on something means to need it"; hold your hands out as if holding on. Point to the chart question "Who needs trees?". Gather and record are said in the reading. Day 89's exit asked children to pick the topic with one detail; some took a detail as the whole topic (error X). Use the topic card and detail picture cards from day 89: the puppet puts roots on the topic card and says "This page is about roots." Children fix it: the topic is the tree, roots go under it.

#### 10 min. Read 06 whole

Whole class. Read Who Depends on a Tree? aloud once, showing each page's picture. Then reread page 2 and say the clues without the name: small, furry, noisy, hops along branches. **Ask.** "Which clue names the squirrel?" Children point to the picture on the page that matches. Expect: small and furry, or hops along the branches. Do the same for page 3: "Which picture shows who builds a nest?" Expect: the bird. Signs: pointing to the tree with no animal in it (error B).

#### 5 min. Reread for the need

Whole class. Reread the squirrel page, page 2. **Ask.** "What does the squirrel need from the tree?" Expect: nuts and seeds to eat. Say it again as a child's words: "The squirrel needs food."

#### 15 min. Model the three boxes (RI.K.3)

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the connection arrow and because (unit 3, days 81 and 82) and "Where did you hear that?" (day 85). On the chart fill the three boxes for the squirrel: a squirrel picture; nuts and seeds; a small book picture marked "06, page 2". Place an arrow card between the boxes. Say the connection: "The squirrel needs nuts and seeds from the tree because it eats them." Children say it with you. Let the puppet slip: "A squirrel. A tree." Ask the class what is missing. Expect: the joining word. Pairs then say the connection for the squirrel to each other, one minute, using "The ___ needs ___ from the tree because ___." Walk past six pairs. The chart's third box is read today as where I heard it; the tree part goes into the spoken sentence. Signs: two things named with no joining word (error A).

#### 5 min. Return to the story (T1)

Whole class, six more children in front. Reread The Tree Beside the Path page 5, the winter buds, and say again that Noor and the tree are made up. Say the T1 prompt: "Ask a question about how the tree changes." Each of the six asks a question different from yesterday's and answers it from the page, about 45 seconds each. The class points to the sentence. Record on the T1 columns. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Draw and retell

Individuals, then pairs. Each child chooses the bird or the deer, the animals the squirrel page did not use, and fills three boxes on side 1: the animal, what it needs (the branches that hold the nest, or leaves and acorns), and where it was heard. Eight minutes. Then each child retells two details of 06 to a partner, three minutes, all pairs at once. Take dictation for the day's six, about a minute each, while the others draw; label for the rest as you walk, and finish their words after class. Signs: a drawn need the page does not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read page 3 of The Oak aloud twice, its picture covered; the class first reads The Oak on day 92. Show strip E90 and ask [[s03-X90av2]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X90bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class adds a colour to its boxes. Signs: a picture that leaves out the animal or what it gets (error B); the animal and the tree named with no link (error A); a need the reading does not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Clap robin, nut and elephant. Children say each word in parts and how many.

#### 8 min. Short a, again

Whole class, then six. Read mat, sat and sap from tiles, all together. Then six children each point to one word and say its middle sound, about 40 seconds each. Signs: guesses from a picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Ants at a Mat, reader 1

Small groups of three. Review: Ants at a Mat (days 85 to 89). Groups reread, one sentence a page, and each says what happened on its page. Pairs then reread the whole reader once, pointing under each word.

#### 7 min. Spell and change

Individuals. This is the written half of the cycle 13 check and teaches nothing new. Say tap and jam; each child writes each word on side 2, a counter for each sound if they wish. Then write map, say "change one letter to make mat," and write the new word. Collect the page. Signs: dropping the middle sound (error V).

#### 30 min. Rotation and cycle 13 check

Table and floor. At the teacher table hear every child, about 35 seconds a child: read the sentence "A man has a cap." from a card, then read at and has from cards without sounding out (P5). Thirty children at 35 seconds is 17 and a half minutes. Hear the six chosen for syllables, two minutes each, 12 minutes: ask them "How many parts in ladder? Say it in parts, then together," then "tiger". Together that is 29 and a half minutes. Children not at the table reread the reader and chain words in pairs at the floor stations. No reteaching today; repair waits until day 91. Signs: reads at or has by sounding it out wrongly (error S).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 06 a page at a time, fill the three boxes for one animal, and spell tap and jam with buttons. One projector: show the page pictures and the chart on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No chart: fold a page into three.

**Skip.** The chart, the story return and the check.

- **10 min. Three boxes.** Say: "A squirrel is small and furry. It finds nuts and seeds in the tree and eats them." Your child folds a page into three, draws the squirrel, the nuts and seeds, and a book.
  **Say.** "What does the squirrel need from the tree?" Expect: nuts and seeds.
- **10 min. A link.** Your child says: "The squirrel needs nuts and seeds from the tree because it eats them."
- **10 min. Spell.** Say tap, then jam. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X90av2]] [[s03-X90bv2]] Read page 3 of The Oak aloud. Your child says what the squirrel gets and which words say so.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the three boxes, the exit and the spelling; cut the second picture clue. Longer: a secure child fills the boxes for the second animal and says where each fact was heard.

### Words and supports

Words: animal, need, depend. Frame: "The ___ needs ___ from the tree because ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the picture of an animal with what it gets and say the words that show it. If many named the animal and the tree with no link (error A), day 91 opens with five minutes: fill three boxes and put an arrow between them. If many picked a picture that left out the animal (error B) or gave a need the reading does not say (error D), day 91 points to the sentence for each. Day 91 rereads 06 and its bird page and adds evidence to the notebook.
