# Day 101: Repair, then gather one more note

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the record sheet into four topic groups by recorded error, from days 97 to 100, and into four foundations groups from the cycle 15 check. Pull the repair cards by letter. Write a feedback line on each piece and put it on its owner's desk. Cover E101. Set the P1 print card aside, unseen until the rotation. Read aloud: "I can add a note from a source" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Whole class. Review: match and adds (day 100). Read the chart "Who needs trees?" aloud, about 30 seconds. Day 100's exit showed some children marking both sentences checked or both crossed (error B), so repair it now. The puppet holds up a source, "A robin sits on a post," and says "I checked this one: A robin sings on a gate, and this one: A robin sits on a post. I checked both." Children show where the puppet went wrong: thumb up for a check, crossed arms for a cross. Retry with a new source, "A squirrel hops on a branch," and the statements "A squirrel hops on a branch" and "A squirrel naps on a branch"; the whole class shows a thumb or crossed arms for each. These cases are made up. No mark today.

#### 15 min. Return and repair

Four groups. Review: repair groups with a picture repair card (days 88 and 96). Group one: two facts with no link, a picture that does not show the detail, movement taken as proof of life, or roots said to take prepared food (errors A, B, P and Q). Group two: the animal unnamed, an unsupported fact, no specific detail, a drawing unrelated to the detail, or a piece unchanged after the slip (errors C, D, I, J and AA); the unnamed animal comes back from day 97 and the unsupported fact from day 98. Group three: a fact that does not answer the question, no added fact, or the wrong source named (errors E, F and AI); the answer to another question comes back from day 99. Group four: calling out, stopping after one comment, speaking too softly to hear, a tree losing leaves taken as dying, a minor detail taken as the topic, silence when a detail is missing, or events in the wrong order (errors G, H, N, W, X, Y and Z). Five minutes of modelling with the repair card, five of repair, five of retry with a sentence or picture not used before; a group under three children joins the next. Each child repairs one recorded error, about 40 seconds. You lead the group with most unresolved errors; partners use the card for each other's letter. Keep each original and its retry together. Wrong examples are said by the puppet. Use cards other than C2a, C2b and C2c.

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class. Review: The Oak (day 92). Write the shared question "What does an oak give?" on chart paper. Reread The Oak, page 3, up to "So acorns are food for animals," and the first three sentences of page 6, about two minutes. **Ask.** "Which note answers the shared question?" Children point to the sentence on the large print. **Ask.** "What does the source actually say?" Expect: the squirrel and the jay eat acorns; people make chairs and tables from the wood. Pairs say one answer to each other with the token, any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Gather a note

Individuals. Each child draws one new note that answers "What does an oak give?" on a paper square, from page 3 or page 6, and puts a source sticker with the page on it. Take dictation from the day's six, about 45 seconds each; label for the others as you walk and finish their words after class. Look for: the note answers the question, it is from the page, and the sticker names the page. Nothing is scored today. Signs: a fact that does not answer the question (error E); a note the page does not say (error D).

#### 15 min. Tell and show

Clusters of three. Review: continue a talk with the token (day 85). Each child shares one note in two minutes: says the note, points to the sentence that holds it and says "The source says ___." The other two listen with the token and may ask one question. You listen to one cluster; the other clusters run themselves. Walk past a second cluster. Pass is always allowed. Signs: no sentence shown (error D); calling out while a partner speaks (error G).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E101 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X101a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X101b]], 30 seconds each, while the class adds its notes to the chart. Signs: marking the antlers or the lake picture (error E); "A moose builds a nest in this tree" (error D).

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Four groups, cycle 15 errors from the check. Review: repair with a picture and a fresh retry (day 96). Group one: reading a word but not its meaning, guessing from the picture, not recognising was or his (errors K, R and S). Group two: naming the changed letter as the first, the letter name for a sound, writing a for u (errors L, T and U). Group three: a heard sound left out of a spelling, an unrelated letter written, a dropped middle sound, a rhyme not made (errors M, O, V and AD). Group four: reading across a space, the lines in the wrong order, a letter case not known, an unrecognisable letter (errors AB, AC, AJ and AK). Three minutes of modelling, four of repair, three of retry with an unused word or letter, such as bus or nut. Hold to the minutes; each child repairs one error, about 30 seconds, with a partner. You lead the group with most errors.

#### 4 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: rhyme (day 80), syllables (day 83) and onset and rime (unit 1, day 24). Say: "Which rhymes with rug: hug or hen?" then ask for another word that rhymes. Clap acorn and squirrel. Say /h/ /ug/ with a sweep of the hand; children say the word. Signs: hears the rhyme but cannot make a third word (error AD).

#### 8 min. Reread, tracking print

Pairs. Review: tracking print (days 85 and 88). Reread Gus in a Tub only. One partner reads while the other points under each word, left to right and page by page; swap at page 6. A finger marks the space between two words. Walk past every pair. Signs: reads the second line first (error AJ); points across a space (error AB); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 8 min. Letters and sounds

Pairs, then individuals. Review: letter names, both cases (unit 2). Pairs name letter cards H to N and O to T mixed, three minutes. Each child prints one letter from each set on the handwriting slip, one minute. Say the sounds of j, k, l, m, n and p; each child writes the letter for each sound you say, four minutes. Signs: a letter name for a sound, "em" for m (error T); b for m (error O); B named but not b (error AC); an unrecognisable N or K (error AK).

#### 4 min. Words was and his

Whole class. Review: was and his (day 96). Read each on its card; children say the part to remember, then read the word.

#### 4 min. Tree Words, verse 4

Whole class. Review: verse 4, The Squirrel (day 96). Reread with the actions. Children find the first line in the large print, "A squirrel was up in a big oak tree," and point to was.

#### 22 min. Rotation

Table and floor. Hear six children not yet heard on isolation, about 100 seconds each, for [[s03-P4d]]: say "Tell me the first sound, the middle sound and the last sound of map." Then hear five children not yet heard on print, two minutes each, with the P1 two-page card, for [[s03-P1a]], [[s03-P1b]] and [[s03-P1c]]; read the card aloud first. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest take a pair card (hum and him, tug and tag, run and ran), build it on tiles, write the changed letter on side 2 and reread Gus in a Tub. Signs: dropping the middle sound, /m/ /p/ (error V); reading across a space (error AB); reading line 2 before line 1 (error AJ).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Use the repair card for the recorded error, reread page 3 and gather one note. Build hum and him on tiles; show the print card and ask the P1 questions once. One projector: show the repair card and the print card on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No class set: use any short book.

**Skip.** The groups, the cluster and the stations.

- **10 min. Letters.** Say j, k and l as sounds; your child writes each letter.
- **10 min. Print.** Write "A pup can run." and "It is fun." on two lines; your child points under each word, left to right, top to bottom.
  **Say.** "Show me the space between two words." Expect: a finger between A and pup.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads was and his on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X101a]] and [[s03-X101b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "Question: What does a girl get from this tree? Source: A girl picks a pear from this tree." Draw a girl picking a pear with a source sticker, a girl with a long braid, and a girl by a river.
  **Say.** "What fact will you add to our notes?" Expect: a girl picks a pear from this tree.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the new note, the exit and the print work; cut Tree Words and the sounds. Longer: a secure child gathers a second note from page 6 and shows its sentence.

### Words and supports

Words: source, note, question, answer, match, adds. Frames: "The source says ___." and "My note answers ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the note that answers a question and say the fact. If many marked a note true but not about the question (error E), or said a fact the source does not give (error D), day 102 opens with five minutes: say the question, then find the one sentence that answers it. Day 102 opens a second shared inquiry about a tree and retrieves the research-cycle steps.
