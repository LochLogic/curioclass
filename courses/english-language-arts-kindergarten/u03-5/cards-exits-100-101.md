# Exit cards, days 100 and 101

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 100 card E100

**Read:** "Source: A chickadee makes its nest in a hole in a tree. Draft: A chickadee makes its nest in a hole in a tree and in a bush."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture shows whether the draft matches the source? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: the source and the draft side by side with a check beside the hole in a tree and a cross beside the bush; both sentences checked; both crossed.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What will you say when you share the comparison?"

## Day 101 card E101

**Read:** "Question: What does a moose get from this tree? Source: A moose strips this tree's leaves in summer."

**Mark (item a):** Which fresh picture note answers the question? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a moose eating leaves from a branch, with a source sticker; a moose with large antlers; a moose standing in a lake.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What fact will you add to our shared notes?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 100: item a, The side-by-side card with a check beside the hole in a tree and a cross beside the bush. Not yet: marks both sentences checked or both crossed (B, a comparison that does not follow the sentences). Item b, It does not match: the draft adds a bush, and the source says only a hole in a tree. Not yet: says It matches (D, the added statement is not in the source).
- Day 101: item a, The moose eating leaves from a branch, with a source sticker. Not yet: marks the antlers moose or the lake moose (E, true, but it does not answer the question). Item b, A moose eats this tree's leaves. Not yet: says A moose builds a nest in this tree (D, not in the source).
