# Comparison card and T3 prompt card

## Comparison card (day 100)

Two columns on a card.

| Source | Draft |
|---|---|
| (the source sentence) | (the draft sentence) |

Beside each draft statement, a small box to draw a check or a cross. A line at the foot reads aloud: "What does the source say?" Pairs draw the check or the cross beside each part and say why.

## T3 prompt card (day 100)

Said by one partner to the other: "Which source supports the animal piece?" The other partner points to the sentence and names the source. Then the partners reverse. A copy of these words is kept in the teacher's folder.

A room with nothing: fold a sheet of paper into two columns and draw boxes.
