# Exit cards, days 47 and 48

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching. The day 48 card is an unscored retry.

## Day 47 card C47: the door

**Read:** "Wind pushes the door. Mai holds the door still."

**Picture:** a girl braced against a door in strong wind, hair blown. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "A friend asks, who held the door? Tell your friend one thing from the card." The partner then asks one more thing.

## Day 48 card C48: the harbour (not scored)

**Read:** "Fog sat on the harbour. Boats waited at the dock."

**Picture:** a harbour in fog, boats tied at a dock, gray hills faint behind. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Tell me one detail that shows what the weather was like."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 47: "Mai held the door", or another detail from the card, said to the partner; the child answers the partner's second question or says "I do not know yet". Not yet: "A door" or "wind" with no answer to who (F); no reply to the second question (AS).
- Day 48: "Fog sat on the harbour" or "boats waited". Not yet: "boats" alone (F); a drawing of something other than the detail told (D). Not scored.
