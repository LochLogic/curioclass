# Day 47: Share the weather piece

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 46's assessment pieces with their three rings; note whether error B (no topic) or error C (a liking, not a fact) is more common. Check the three request cards are on the wall. Set out the mitten, the box, the topic card and two detail cards. Hang the sound-spelling map with the cards d, f, l and dog, fish, leaf. Note the day's six: the next two unheard from each foundations group.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Two repairs, 5 minutes each, taken from day 45's exit and day 46's drafts.

First (5 minutes), error AA from day 45's exit, under and on swapped. Pip puts the mitten on the box and says "The mitten is under the box." The class says "That is on," and a child moves the mitten under the box. Then pairs: one child puts a counter on, under or beside a cup, and the partner says the place in a sentence ("The counter is under the cup."); swap after one minute.

Second (5 minutes), the more common of error B and error C. For error B: Pip holds a draft with a drawing and no topic word; the class finds the empty topic ring, and you lay the topic card on top and the detail cards under it. For error C: Pip says "I like clouds"; ask "What does the page say about clouds?" and the class adds "Clouds are made of tiny drops of water." Each child then fixes the day 46 piece they will share: the topic on top, a fact under it. Stand near the day's six.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class, with gestures. Review: share (day 2), question (a question asks, day 1), clarify (ask again so you understand, day 43) and detail (one fact under a topic, day 9). Point to the request card again as you say clarify. Say each word, do its gesture and have the class repeat it. Say: "Today a partner asks a question to clarify, and you answer with a detail from your page or your picture."

#### 10 min. Reread 01a

Whole class. Read Weather Changes, pages 1 to 4, showing each picture (about 2 minutes). Review: you heard this page on days 35 and 36. Then, a page at a time, ask the children to find one detail and point to it on the picture. **Ask.** "What do children wear on a sunny day?" Expect: sun hats. Each child picks one detail on the pages or the pictures that they will share, and holds a finger on it. Pairs tell the detail to each other once (3 minutes).

#### 5 min. Model

Whole class. Pip shares a piece: a drawing of a sunny playground and the words "Children wear sun hats because the sun is bright." Hold up the question card for why and ask "Why do children wear sun hats?" Pip points to page 2 and answers "Because the sun is bright." Pip then asks you one back with the where card: "Where did you see a detail?" You point to page 3 and say "In the rain picture." That is two turns, one each. Say what happened: a partner asks one question to clarify, the sharer answers from the page or picture, and then the partner is asked one back. Nobody is told they are wrong.

#### 15 min. Share the weather piece

Pairs. Each pair has one paper token and the question cards. Child A holds the token and tells the topic and one fact of the day 46 piece, pointing to the drawing (1 minute). Child B asks one question with a card, and A answers from the page or the picture (1 minute). Swap the token and do the same (2 minutes). Then each asks one more question and answers (2 minutes). Review: you wait for the token and ask one more (day 43). A child may pass. You hear six pairs, about 2 minutes each, and mark in the heard column who answered a second question. The other pairs finish by drawing on their piece.

#### 10 min. Add a detail

Alone. Each child adds to the piece one drawn or dictated detail that answers the partner's question. You walk; 30 children at about 20 seconds each is 10 minutes. Write any dictated words beside the drawing. Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six at the heard table, in three pairs. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card C47 twice with its picture (20 seconds). Say the stem for [[s02-X47]]: the first child tells one thing from the card (about 15 seconds); the partner asks one more thing (10 seconds); the first child answers (15 seconds). Swap. Each pair takes about 80 seconds. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others add a detail to their drawing. Signs: only "wind" or "a door" with no answer to who (error F); no reply to the second question (error AS).

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Read the verse Wind twice with the pointer, tracking each line. Clap the beats of ribbon (rib-bon) and of along and song for the rhyme. Show the cards d, f and l and point to each in the verse: d in red and and; f in flag; l in along and flag. Children listen and look; they are not asked to read the verse.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Meet three new letters (8 minutes). For d: the name is dee, the sound is /d/, the keyword is dog; the tongue taps behind the top teeth. For f: the name is eff, the sound is /f/, the keyword is fish; top teeth on the bottom lip and blow. For l: the name is ell, the sound is /l/, the keyword is leaf; the tongue touches behind the top teeth and the sound keeps going. Show both cases of each; say the path as a child traces it in the air: for d, round like a c, then up tall and straight down; for f, a curve over the top, down, then a line across; for l, one straight line down. Add the cards to the sound-spelling map. No word that ends in /l/ is used for sound work.

Map a new word, and (7 minutes). Review: a mystery word has clues (days 37 and 38, he and we). Clues: it is a word; it is in the verse Wind; it has three letters, a, n and d. Point to the three letters and say the sound of each: /a/, /n/, /d/. All three say their usual sounds, so the word has no tricky part; the new letter is the d at the end. Read and whole by sight. Children find and twice in the verse (flag and hums; red and a kite) and touch it each time.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: about one minute for each child. Review: you pushed a counter for each sound of a three-sound word (days 41 to 44). Say dip, say its three sounds one at a time with a counter for each, then name the first, the middle and the last sound; say lap the same way. Error T: the child gives the last sound when asked for the first; point to the first counter and ask again. Writing place: write d, f and l, big then small, on handwriting paper; trace and on a card. Error AM: an f that cannot be read; trace its path, then write it fresh. Sound and verse place: pairs match picture cards to the first sound (dog, fish, leaf and cards for sounds already taught); find and in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Share the piece, ask and answer two questions, add one detail, then do card C47 aloud with a second question from you. One projector: show Weather Changes pages 1 to 4 one at a time; the cards and the token stay real.

### At home

**Swap.** No question cards: ask "Who? What? Where?" aloud. No token: pass a spoon.

**Skip.** The group rotations.

- **10 min. A page.** Look at a weather picture or draw one. Your child tells you one detail and draws one more.
  **Say.** "Which one?" Expect: a detail from the picture.
- **10 min. Two turns.** Your child tells a weather fact; you ask one question; they answer; they ask you one back.
- **5 min. Letters.** Say /d/, /f/, /l/ (dog, fish, leaf); your child finds them in a book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X47]] Read twice: "Wind pushes the door. Mai holds the door still." Ask the question, then one more.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep one exchange of the share and the exit; cut the added detail to a drawing. Longer: a child who is secure in and meets of in the verse and hears that the f says /v/ there.

### Words and supports

Words: share, question, clarify, detail. Frames: "My topic is ___." "The page says ___." "Can you say more about ___?" Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk in a quiet corner and may keep the token as long as they need.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 is a response day, and nothing is scored. Groups made from the day 46 record repair error F (an unclear idea), error D (a drawing of something else), error E (a voice too quiet) and error AX (a feeling with no idea clear), and error AS (the talk stops after one turn) from today's exit. Then the whole class hears Weather Changes and tries a fresh card.
