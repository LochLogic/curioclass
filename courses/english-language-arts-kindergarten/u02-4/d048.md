# Day 48: A response, and a fresh try

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the response group list from the day 46 record and day 47's heard column: group A (error F, D, E, AX, and any B or C still showing) and group B (error AS, no second turn). Write it on the record sheet; no child is named as a miss. Set out tokens, one journal page from the wall, Weather Changes, the verses Snow and Wind, and the day's six: the next two unheard from each foundations group.

### The day

#### 15 min. Response

Groups. Nothing is scored. Group A comes to the table first, group B second, and everyone else works in pairs with a token, taking turns to tell one detail from the pictures of Weather Changes, page 2 or page 3, or of the journal page, and ask one question about it (two turns each).

Group A (10 minutes, in two steps of 5). First, error F, an unclear idea. Pip says "A thing and the other thing"; ask "Which thing? What happened?" Then say the clear way: "The children wore sun hats on the playground." Each child looks at a picture of page 2 and says a sentence with a named place or thing and what happened (about 30 seconds each for ten children). Second, three short turns by what the record shows. For error D, a drawing of something else: the child tells one detail aloud first, then redraws only that detail and points to it. For error E, a voice too quiet: the child says the sentence to a partner across the table, who raises a hand when the voice reaches. For error AX, a feeling with no idea clear: Pip says "I felt happy"; ask "What did you see that made you feel happy?" and the child says the idea, then you say it back. For any error B or C still showing: the topic card on top, a fact from the page under it.

Group B (5 minutes). Error AS, the talk stops after one turn. Pip answers one question and stops; the class says "and one more," and Pip asks one back. Then group B takes a token: a child tells a detail, the partner asks, the child answers, the token passes and the partner asks one more (about 30 seconds each for ten children). Review: you wait for the token and ask one more (day 43).

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Review: detail (one fact under a topic, day 9) and question (a question asks, day 1). Review: clear means easy to see through (day 44). Say that a voice or an idea can be clear too: easy to hear and easy to understand. Pip says a sentence very quietly and then clearly; the class picks the clear one.

#### 15 min. Reading 01a

Whole class. Read Weather Changes whole, pages 1 to 8, showing each picture (about 3 minutes). Review: you heard this page on days 35 and 36 and again on 47. At each picture page, children point to one detail. Do not stop the reading for questions. **Ask.** "What did you hear about the weather?" Expect: weather changes; it changes what people wear and do; places have different weather. Pairs tell each other first (4 minutes), then six children say one thing each (about 40 seconds apiece).

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with a token. Each child tells one detail from Weather Changes, the partner asks one question with a question card, and the child answers from the page or the picture. Swap, then each asks one more (two exchanges in all). A child may pass. You hear five pairs, about 2 minutes each, and mark in the heard column who answered the second question.

#### 10 min. Draw and dictate

Alone. Each child draws a weather topic and says or dictates one new fact from Weather Changes. You walk; 30 children at about 20 seconds each is 10 minutes. Write each fact beside the drawing, saying each word as you write. Look for three things: a topic is named; one fact comes from the text; the fact is about the topic. This is practice and is not scored. The pieces stay with the child.

#### 5 min. Fresh card

The day's six at the heard table, an unscored retry. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card C48 twice with its picture (20 seconds). Ask [[s02-X48]] of each child in turn, about 40 seconds each. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: "boats" alone (error F); a drawing of something other than the detail told (error D). The others finish their drawing.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Reread the verses Snow and Wind with the pointer, tracking each line. Find on in Snow and and in Wind, and touch each. Clap the beats of mitten and ribbon.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Cycle 7 response, 10 minutes, in groups by the day 47 heard column. The group that gave the last sound when asked for the first (error T) says dip and lap with a counter for each sound and replaces only the counter named; the group whose f could not be read (error AM) traces the path with a finger and then writes it fresh; the others chant the letters with a partner and a pointer. All chant the fifteen letters taught so far for the last 5 minutes: a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s, v, g, i, d, f and l. No new pattern is taught.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: about one minute for each child. Review: you put a sound before a word to make a new one with counters (day 42). Say in, add /f/ to make fin, then add /p/ in its place to make pin; each child says the new word. Writing place: write d, f and l in lines; trace and on a card. Sound and verse place: beats and rhyme cards (mitten, ribbon; along and song); blend /d/ /i/ /g/ to make dig, and segment fin into three sounds. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Share one detail, answer a question, ask one back, then try the fresh card. One projector: show Weather Changes one page at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No question cards: ask "Who? What? Where?" aloud. No token: pass a spoon.

**Skip.** The group work.

- **10 min. Listen.** Read pages 1 to 4 of a weather page aloud. Ask your child to point to one detail.
  **Say.** "What is one detail you heard?" Expect: a thing the page tells.
- **10 min. Two turns.** Your child tells you a detail, you ask one question, they answer, then they ask you one.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a topic and tells you one new fact; write it beside the drawing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X48]] Read twice: "Fog sat on the harbour. Boats waited at the dock." Ask for one detail that shows the weather.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the response groups, the talk and the fresh card; cut the drawing to a topic and one spoken fact. Longer: a child who is secure asks a second question to a partner, said and heard.

### Words and supports

Words: detail, clear, question. Frames: "I saw ___." "Can you say more about ___?" "The ___ was ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk in a quiet corner.

### Tomorrow

Day 49 opens section 2.5: the class compares weather in three places, then hears a weather story whole and meets the story-map. Today's heard column and the fresh card go on the record sheet and inform the groups for day 49; no one is graded on them.
