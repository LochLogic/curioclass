# Section 2.4 opener: sharing the weather piece and a response day

## What the section does

Day 47 is a day to share. After a ten-minute return and repair, each child shares the weather piece with a partner, and the partner asks about a detail the text can answer; the child answers or says "I do not know yet". Day 48 is a response day: you meet response groups chosen from the day 46 record, and everyone else rereads and talks. Nothing on day 48 is scored.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 47 begins with a ten-minute return and repair, then reading, sharing, and a five-minute exit heard from the day's six. Day 48 begins with a fifteen-minute response in small groups and ends with an unscored fresh card. A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A question helps the writer.** A partner who asks "Which one?" or "Where?" helps the writer add a detail the page can answer.
- **A detail shows the weather.** "Snow lay on the steps." tells what the weather was like; "steps" alone does not.
- **Response is repair, not a second score.** The day 46 record shows what to practice; the response group practices it and the fresh card shows whether it held.

## The errors, in plain words

- **AA:** under and on swapped. Move a picture and say the place.
- **B, C:** no topic, or a liking alone. Put the topic on top and add a fact.
- **F, D, E, AX:** an unclear idea, an unrelated drawing, a voice too quiet, a feeling with no idea. Practiced in the day 48 response groups.
- **AS:** the talk stops after one turn. Practice a second turn with the token.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet, never by a child. Never name a child as an example of a miss.

## Supplies

The children's own pieces, the three marks, the class picture journal, the passing token, paper, pencils and crayons, a response group list made from the day 46 record, the exit cards and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Use the children's own drafts and a torn paper token. Make the group list on the back of the record sheet.
