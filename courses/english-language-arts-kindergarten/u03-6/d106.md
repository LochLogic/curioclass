# Day 106: Group notes from the oak page

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E106. Make six groups of five and five rotation groups of six, and write both on the record sheet. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard on manipulation. Set the five station cards. Read aloud: "Draw a note with your group" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Preview select and evidence by gesture: select is picking one, evidence is showing the line that says it (new). Repair at most one, three minutes: if many gave a fact the wrong card on day 105 (error AI), the puppet shows the loose note "A jay eats acorns from the oak" with the sticker 06 and says "This came from 06." Children find the line on The Oak, page 3, in the large print, and say the right sticker, 05a; the teacher relabels the card. No mark today.

#### 15 min. Read 05a

Whole class. Review: The Oak (days 92 and 101). Reread page 3 up to "So acorns are food for animals," and the first three sentences of page 6, about two minutes, showing the pictures only. Children point to the squirrel and the jay, then to the acorn. Ask "Which animal is on this page?" and take three one-sentence answers; others may add with the token. Pass is always allowed. Say aloud: this is the oak page we know, and today we use it as evidence for group notes.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Six groups of five, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "What is this selection about?" Each group talks, one turn each, one minute a turn, any child may pass. **Ask.** "How does the acorn connect to an animal?" Same turns in the group. The teacher visits each group about two minutes. Take one answer from each of three groups with the so word: "The squirrel needs food, so it eats the acorns."

#### 20 min. Draw and dictate

Groups, then individuals. 5 min: model naming the source on a note: the oak in the middle, an acorn and a jay, an arrow from the acorn to the jay and a 05a sticker; point to the line on page 3. 15 min: in the six groups each group draws one evidence note on one sheet (an acorn and an animal), names its source sticker, and one child per group says the link; each child then adds one note to their own notebook with a sticker. The teacher visits the six groups, about two minutes each, and takes dictation from the day's six. Nothing is scored. Signs: an arrow missing (error A); an animal not on the page (error B); a fact the page never says (error D).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E106 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X106a]]: children point to one of three picture notes. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X106b]], 30 seconds each, while the class finishes its notes. The errors on this exit wait for day 108. Signs: the bat eating a fruit (error D); the bat flying far from the tree (error B).

#### 10 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: add or swap a sound (day 105). The class answers together: "Add /h/ before it. What word?" then "Change /h/ to /s/." Then six children not yet heard, not the six of day 105, one at a time, each add or swap one sound in a new word (say top, change /t/ to /p/: pop), one minute each. Write their names in the record column. Signs: changes the wrong sound (error AG).

#### 5 min. Revise sounds (P3)

Individuals. Review: sounds to letters (day 105). Return the day 105 sound page with its written feedback; each child revises one letter that was flagged and says its sound. No new score today.

#### 20 min. Reading and spelling rotation

Five groups of six at five stations, four minutes each. Teacher table: each child reads or and for on cards, 20 seconds a child, two minutes (the P5 cells), then reads one short o word, hog, and writes one dictated word, mom, on a slip, two minutes; this is the cycle 16 check, notes only. Other stations: reader partners reread A Log in a Pond and tell what happened; tile chain with short o against a and i; verse print, pointing under each word of verse 5; letter forms, printing u to z with the model. Write who was heard. Signs: cannot read or or for without help (error S); a sound left out (error M); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Whole class. 5 min: dictate rob, dog and hit; children push a counter a sound and write the letters. Signs: a sound left out (error M); changes the wrong sound (error AG). 5 min: letters from the handwriting slip revised again where the feedback asked, then one line of print under the model. Signs: an unrecognisable letter (error AK).

#### 15 min. Tree Words verse

Whole class. Review: verse 5, Apple Tree (day 102). Choral reading with the actions twice, five minutes. Pairs find the rhyming words hop and top in print and point, three minutes. Children point under each word of the first line of page 2, left to right, three minutes. Clap apple, one minute. A last reread, then collect cards and counters, three minutes.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread page 3 of the oak page, draw one note with an arrow and a sticker, read or and for, chain dig, dog and dug on tiles and read the reader once. One projector: show the chart and the large print on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No stickers: write the book's name on the note.

**Skip.** The verse and the revising.

- **10 min. A note.** Read twice: "A fig tree has soft round fruit. Birds eat the fruit." Your child draws the tree, a bird at the fruit and an arrow from the fruit to the bird.
  **Say.** "How do the fruit and the birds go together?" Expect: the birds eat the fruit.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child reads or and for on cards and writes mom after you say it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X106a]] and [[s03-X106b]] about three drawings you make, read twice: "A cat naps under a plum tree. Plums fall in summer." Draw a plum on a branch, the cat under the tree, and the cat far away.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the group note, the exit and the rotation check; cut the verse and the revising. Longer: a secure child adds a second note to the group sheet and names its sticker.

### Words and supports

Words: select, evidence, source, note, fact. Frame, only if a child asks: "The card says ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can pick the note a card supports and show the line. Its errors are repaired in day 108's Return part, five minutes, not tomorrow. Day 107 observes the first research talks: a question, source information and a connection, from the notes children made on days 97 to 99 and here. The next foundations block begins with ten minutes of response and a fresh retry.
