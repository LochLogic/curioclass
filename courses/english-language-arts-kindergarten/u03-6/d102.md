# Day 102: A second question about one tree, and short o

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the four research-cycle pictures (ask, find, note, share) and the sound-spelling map. Put the octopus picture beside a blank o space on the map. Write "What does our tree need, and who needs it?" on chart paper, covered. Cover E102. From the record sheet pick six children not yet heard on isolation, and five not yet heard on print. Read aloud: "Draw one thing you wonder about trees" and "Write the letter I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. If many of day 101's marks showed error E or D (a note that does not answer the question, or a fact the source never says), spend the first two minutes on it: the puppet says "The question is What does an oak give? My note says An oak has a thick trunk," and the class finds the one sentence on The Oak, page 3, that does answer it. Otherwise begin at once. Four words, one gesture each, about 30 seconds each. Research is four hand moves: ask, find, note, share (new). Focus is pointing at one thing (new). Review: describe (kindergarten, day 36), hands show the shape of something; observe (day 35), a hand over the eyes, looking. Then review the needs from What a Tree Needs (day 82): hold up one finger for each thing a tree needs (four: water, air, light, soil). Seed, water, grow and tall are known.

#### 15 min. Read 03 and reread

Whole class. Review: A Tree Is a Living Thing (day 89). Read the chant in large print, each line said twice, about three minutes; the class joins on the second saying. Reread page 2 slowly: roots take in water and minerals; the leaves make the food (roots do not feed the tree). Children join the repeated line "A tree is a living thing!" with an arm stretched up. Ask three children, one sentence each, to say one thing a tree does in the chant; others may add with the token. Pass is always allowed. About eight minutes of listening and saying, the rest of the time rereading page 4.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Review: ask a question (kindergarten, day 1). **Ask.** "What does the chant explain?" Take answers from three volunteers. **Ask.** "What new question could we ask about one tree?" Pairs talk with the token, one turn each, 30 seconds a turn, any child may pass. Model two starters aloud: "What does the tree need?" and "Who needs the tree?" Starters fade from day 96, so say them once and do not repeat them. Take two questions from pairs. Uncover the chart and read the working question the class will use: "What does our tree need, and who needs it?" Then model fit on the chant: point to page 2 and say "This page tells what a tree needs." Point to page 6's picture and say "The chant does not say who needs it. We will find another source." Children point to the page that answers the first half.

#### 20 min. Draw and dictate

Whole class, then individuals. 8 min: retrieve the research cycle. Review: ask, find, note, share (day 80). Point at the four pictures; children say which step the class took when it asked about an animal and show it with the hand move. Read the collage prompt aloud: "Name your tree. Show what it needs and one living thing that depends on it. Use details from two readings." Say the collage is made later, not today. 12 min: notebook page one, "I wonder" (new). Each child draws one thing they wonder about trees, tells a partner in one sentence, and the teacher takes dictation from the day's six (about 45 seconds each); the others label by sounds or the teacher finishes their words after class. Nothing is scored. No child reports on a tree at home.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E102 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X102a]]: children point to one of three question cards. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X102b]], 30 seconds each, while the class finishes its notebook page. Signs: marks the sleeping giraffe or the acacia in the dark (error E); "ask a friend" or a guess (error D).

#### 7 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral, no print. Review: counters for the middle sound (day 101). Say hop, job and rock; the class pushes a counter for each sound and says the middle sound together, about two and a half minutes. Then six children, one at a time, say the first, middle and last sound of a new word (dog), 45 seconds each, four and a half minutes. Write their names in the record column. Signs: /m/ /p/ with the vowel dropped (error V).

#### 12 min. Short o lesson (new)

Whole class, then pairs, 15 sets of counters. Say /o/ and trace o on the map under the octopus picture; the picture only recalls the sound, the sound never gives a word. Children say /o/ and trace it in the air. Sort six picture cards by middle sound: mop and dog go under o, ham and cap under a, pig under i, jug under u; each child says the middle sound of one card. Blend o with known letters on tiles: o-n, then t-o-p; children say each sound and the word. Chain hot, hat, hit on tiles (RF.K.3d). After each change say: "Which sound changed?" Pairs tell each other the changed sound. Review: change one letter and say which sound changed (day 85). Signs: says /o/ for the changed letter in hit and hot (error AH).

#### 6 min. Map or and for

Whole class. Mark or on a sentence strip: the o-r sound to remember, not short o; for is f plus or. Read each in a strip, "Sit or hop." and "It is for Dot." Review: to, do, at, has, is, it, was and his; point as you say them. Children say the word each time you point. Signs: cannot read or or for without help (error S).

#### 20 min. Partner and small-group reading

Pairs, with the teacher table. The teacher reads the title A Log in a Pond and page 1 aloud. Review: partner reading and pointing (day 101). The 15 pairs read pages 1 to 4, one page each, swap each page, covering the picture, then say what happened. Hear five children not yet heard on print, two minutes each, ten minutes in all, with the P1 print card; read the card aloud first. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Walk past the pairs between. Write who was heard. Signs: points across a space (error AB); starts page 2 first (error AJ); guesses the word from the picture (error R); calls words with no meaning (error K).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Whole class. 5 min: dictate log, mop and dog. Children push a counter for each sound and write letters they think fit; a spelling by sound is accepted. Signs: a sound left out (error M). 5 min: handwriting. Review: letter forms O to T (day 94, second date). Children print o and p on the slip, revising forms the written feedback flagged. Signs: an unrecognisable letter (error AK).

#### 5 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Whole class. Verse 5, Apple Tree (new), read by the adult with the actions; no new decoding pattern. Children find the two words that rhyme, hop and top, on the large print, and clap apple. Track each line left to right with a finger. Collect cards and counters, 2 minutes.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the chant, ask the two questions, draw one wonder together, sort the picture cards and build hot, hat and hit on tiles, read pages 1 to 4 and show the print card once. One projector: show the chart and the large print on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use coins or buttons.

**Skip.** The sorting and the verse.

- **10 min. A question.** Look at a tree or a picture of one. Your child draws one thing they wonder about it.
  **Say.** "What do you wonder about this tree?" Expect: any question about the tree.
- **10 min. The sound.** Say hop, log and top. Push a button for each sound. Your child says the middle sound, /o/.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say dog. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes the letters.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads or and for on two cards.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X102a]] and [[s03-X102b]] about a card you read twice: "A plum tree has purple fruit. Bees visit the flowers." Draw three questions: a bee at a flower, a bee asleep, a plum tree at night.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the question, the cycle, the exit and the short o lesson; cut the verse and one chain. Longer: a secure child says a second question and points to the page that could answer it.

### Words and supports

Words: research, focus, describe, observe, question, source. Frame, only if a child asks: "I wonder ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can tell which question a card can answer. If many marked a question the card cannot answer (error E), day 103 opens with five minutes: read the card again, point to what it can answer, and retry with a new card. Day 103 reads a sugar maple profile and notes what the tree needs and who uses it.
