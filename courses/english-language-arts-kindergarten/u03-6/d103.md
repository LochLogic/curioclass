# Day 103: What a maple needs and who uses its seeds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark page 4 of The Sugar Maple for the exit. Draw the chart: a tree in the middle with room for a card on each side. Pick six children for the exit, six not yet heard on isolation, and five not yet heard on print. Set out the twelve naming cards. Read aloud: "Draw a note with an arrow" and "Print the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: research, focus, describe and observe (day 102), one gesture each, 30 seconds. Repair at most one, three minutes: if many marked a question the card cannot answer on day 102 (error E), the puppet reads a new card twice, "A pear tree has white flowers in spring. Bees visit the flowers," and says "My question is Where does a bee sleep?" Children say whether the card can answer it (thumb up or crossed arms), then say the question the card can answer. Retry with an unused card, "A pine tree has green needles all year." No mark today.

#### 15 min. Read 05b

Whole class. Say aloud: "This maple may not grow near us. It is our provided source." Read The Sugar Maple pages 1 to 3 in large print, about four minutes, pausing at each picture. Page 4 waits for the exit; later pages are not read today. Reread the seed lines on page 3, "A maple has seeds. Squirrels eat its seeds.", with children pointing to the squirrel in the picture. Review: depend (day 90): ask "What does a squirrel depend on a maple for?" Take three one-sentence answers; others may add with the token. Pass is always allowed.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: ask a question about a text (day 102) and the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "What does the maple need?" Pairs talk with the token, one turn each, 30 seconds a turn. **Ask.** "Who uses its seeds?" Same turns. Then model joining two facts on the chart, about five minutes. Put the need card water on the left of the tree with an arrow pointing in. Put the seeds card and the squirrel card on the right, with an arrow from the seeds to the squirrel. Say aloud: "The squirrel needs food, so it eats the maple's seeds." Review: so and because (day 82). Children say the so sentence together and trace the arrow with a finger.

#### 20 min. Draw and dictate

Individuals. 5 min: model one note on a paper square: the tree in the middle, a need card in, a dependent card out, an arrow, and a source sticker "05b". 15 min: each child draws their own note for the maple (any need, any user named on pages 1 to 3), puts a 05b sticker on it, and says the so sentence to a partner. The teacher takes the day's six, 90 seconds each, nine minutes in all, asking each to name the need, the one who uses it and the link, while the rest draw. Take dictation of the so sentence from those six; label for the others as you walk. Nothing is scored. Signs: facts drawn with no arrow (error A); a user the page never names (error B).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read page 4 of The Sugar Maple aloud, its picture covered; the lesson read pages 1 to 3 only. Show strip E103 and ask [[s03-X103av2]]: children point to one of three drawings. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X103bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class adds one detail to its notebook note. Signs: no arrow (error A); the wrong animal (error B).

#### 7 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: middle sounds with counters (day 102). Say jog, rob and pond; the class pushes a counter for each sound and says the middle sound, about two and a half minutes. Then six children not yet heard, one at a time, say the first, middle and last sound of a new word, 45 seconds each. Write their names in the record column. Signs: /m/ /p/ with the vowel dropped (error V).

#### 8 min. Short o review

Pairs, tiles and counters. Review: short o (day 102). Chain top, tip, tap on tiles and then hot, hat, hit; only short a, i and o words today. After each change the pair says which sound changed (RF.K.3d). Walk past each pair. Signs: says /o/ for the changed letter in hit and hot (error AH).

#### 5 min. Map or and for

Whole class. Review: or and for (day 102). Read both in new strips, "Tom can hop or nod." and "A dog is for Dot." Point at the part to remember in each word and say it. Signs: cannot read or or for without help (error S).

#### 20 min. Partner and small-group reading

Pairs, with the teacher table. Review: A Log in a Pond, pages 1 to 4 (day 102). Partners read the whole reader, one page each, and say a fact from it, not only the words. Hear five children not yet heard, not the five of day 102, on print, two minutes each, with the P1 print card; read the card aloud first. Mark each and write who was heard. Signs: points across a space (error AB); starts page 2 first (error AJ); guesses from the picture (error R); calls words with no meaning (error K).

#### 15 min. Spell, print and letter naming

Whole class, then individuals. 4 min: dictate jog, rob and hop; children push a counter a sound and write letters that fit. Signs: a sound left out (error M). Then 11 min: each child prints letters U to Z in both cases on the handwriting slip at the table. Meanwhile the teacher hears letter naming one child at a time with the twelve shuffled cards, 30 seconds each, which is 22 children in these 11 minutes; the other eight are heard on day 104 in its printing part. Write who was heard. Signs: names the capital but not the small letter (error AC); an unrecognisable letter (error AK).

#### 5 min. Tree Words verse

Whole class. Review: verse 5, Apple Tree (day 102). Reread with the actions. Children find the opening line in the large print and the two rhyming words, hop and top, and point to them.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read pages 1 to 3 of the maple profile, ask the two questions, draw one note with an arrow together, chain top, tip and tap on tiles and read the reader. One projector: show the chart and the large print on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No stickers: write the book's name on the note.

**Skip.** The verse and the naming.

- **10 min. A note.** Read twice: "A pear tree needs water. It grows pears. A deer eats the pears." Your child draws the tree, the water and the deer, with an arrow from the pears to the deer.
  **Say.** "How do the pears and the deer go together?" Expect: the deer eats the pears.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say jog, rob and hop. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes the word.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads or and for on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read page 4 of The Sugar Maple aloud. Ask [[s03-X103av2]] and [[s03-X103bv2]] about three drawings you make: one with an arrow, one with no arrow, one with a different animal.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the note, the exit and the chain; cut the verse and the second read of the reader. Longer: a secure child draws a second note for another user of the maple.

### Words and supports

Words: need, depend, arrow, so, source, maple. Frame, only if a child asks: "The tree gives ___ to ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can join a need and a user with an arrow. If many drew the two facts with no arrow (error A), day 104 opens with five minutes: put an arrow between two loose facts and say so. Day 104 names a tree on a label strip and gives one fact with its source sticker.
