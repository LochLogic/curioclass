# Day 104: One tree named, one fact from the source

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E104. Write "sugar maple" on a label strip. Pick six children for the exit, six not yet heard on isolation, six pairs to listen to, and the eight children not yet named on set U to Z. Read aloud: "Name the tree. Add one fact." and "Print the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: focus and describe (day 102), one gesture each, 30 seconds. Repair at most one, three minutes: if many drew a need and a user with no arrow on day 103 (error A), the puppet holds two loose picture cards, a cherry tree with fruit and a bird eating fruit, and says "I put them side by side. That is all." Children say what is missing; add an arrow from the fruit to the bird and say "The bird eats the fruit, so the tree gives it food." Children add an arrow to a second loose pair, a seed and a squirrel, in the air. No mark today.

#### 15 min. Read 05b and 03

Whole class. Review: The Sugar Maple (day 103) and A Tree Is a Living Thing (day 102). No new text. Reread page 2 of the maple and pages 2 and 4 of the chant, about five minutes, listening for what the tree needs. Children hold up a finger each time they hear a need. Then reread page 3 of the maple, the seed lines, and have children point to the squirrel's seed in the picture.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "Which tree should our page name?" Pairs talk, one turn each, 30 seconds a turn. The class settles on the sugar maple; children may choose an oak for their own collage later. **Ask.** "Which fact would you put on the page?" Pairs talk again with the token, any child may pass. Take three facts and say the page and the line each one is on. Hold up the label strip "sugar maple" and read it. A fact comes from the source, so say where it is.

#### 20 min. Draw and dictate

Whole class, then individuals. 6 min: model a shared opening on chart paper in two steps. First put the label strip "sugar maple" at the top and read it; say "Our page is about the sugar maple." Then draw one fact, a seed with a paper wing, and say "Its seeds have paper wings." Put a 05b sticker beside the fact. 14 min: each child picks a different note from their notebook, draws it again as the opening of a page, writes the tree name by sounds or with a teacher's label strip, and dictates one fact. Take dictation from the day's six, 45 seconds each. Nothing is scored. Signs: a flower or leaf drawn with no tree name (error C); a fact the source never says (error D).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E104 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X104a]]: children point to one of three picture groups. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X104b]], 30 seconds each, while the class finishes its openings. Signs: no label strip (error C); a picture the card never says (error D).

#### 7 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: middle sounds with counters (day 103). Say job, hop and rod; the class pushes a counter for each sound and says the middle sound, about two and a half minutes. Then six children not yet heard, one at a time, say the first, middle and last sound of a new word, 45 seconds each. Write their names in the record column. Signs: /m/ /p/ with the vowel dropped (error V).

#### 8 min. Chain and spell

Pairs, tiles and counters. Review: short a, i and u words and short o (days 85 to 103). Chain hop, hot, hat on tiles; after each change say which sound changed (RF.K.3d). Then spell top and job by sounds: one counter a sound, then the letters. Signs: says /o/ for the changed letter (error AH); a sound left out (error M).

#### 5 min. Map or and for

Whole class. Review: or and for (day 103), and to, do, at, has, is and it. Read both in text strips, "Hop on a log or sit." and "A bun is for Dot.", then point to the earlier words as you say them. Signs: cannot read or or for without help (error S).

#### 15 min. Partner reading

Pairs. Review: A Log in a Pond (day 103). Partners reread the reader and say what happened. Each pair finds one line and points to its words, one point for each spoken word. The teacher listens to six pairs, two minutes each, twelve minutes in all, and writes the pairs in the record column. Signs: points across a space (error AB); guesses from the picture (error R); calls words with no meaning (error K).

#### 10 min. Print letters

Individuals. Review: letter forms U to Z (day 103, second date). Dictate letters U to Z in both cases; children print them on the handwriting slip, six minutes. Meanwhile hear the eight children not yet named on day 103 name the twelve cards, 30 seconds each, four minutes in all. Collect the slips; the written feedback is done after class, a minute a page. Signs: an unrecognisable letter (error AK); names one case only (error AC).

#### 15 min. Tree Words verse and print retrieval

Whole class, then pairs. Review: verse 5, Apple Tree (day 102). Choral read with the actions, three minutes. Review: tracking print (days 85 and 88). On the large print children find the first word of a line, put a finger between two words, and show where to turn the page; one try each in pairs with the strip, six minutes. Clap-and-say apple, two minutes. Close with the class saying the rhyming words hop and top. Signs: points across a space (error AB); starts the second page first (error AJ).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread page 2 of the maple, name the tree on a label strip and draw one fact with its sticker, chain hop, hot and hat on tiles and show the print once. One projector: show the chart and the large print on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No stickers: write the title on the page.

**Skip.** The verse and the naming.

- **10 min. A page.** Your child draws a tree you pick, writes its name on a strip with you and says one fact about it.
  **Say.** "What is this tree called, and what is one fact?" Expect: the name, then one thing true about it.
- **10 min. Print.** Say the letters u, v, w, x, y and z, and your child prints each.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say top and job. Your child pushes a button a sound and writes the word.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X104a]] and [[s03-X104b]] about a card you read twice: "This page is about a cherry. Its fruit is small and red." Draw the cherry tree with a strip, a red fruit with no strip, and the strip with a bare tree.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the opening, the exit and the chain; cut the verse retrieval. Longer: a secure child draws a second opening for the oak with its sticker.

### Words and supports

Words: focus, fact, label, source, detail. Frame, only if a child asks: "My tree is ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can start a page with the tree's name and one fact. If many drew the flower with no name (error C), day 105 opens with five minutes: add the name to a loose picture and say the name before the fact. Day 105 adds one more fact to the shared opening and checks which card it came from.
