# Exit cards, days 102 to 106

Sheet E103 goes with a page of the unit's own texts, read aloud again with its picture covered; it is not made up, so say nothing about practice for it. The sheet is a strip of three pictures. The other cards are made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice", read each twice with its picture, and print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each of them.

The sheet and the cards use plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 102 card E102

**Read:** "An acacia tree has green leaves. Giraffes eat the leaves."

**Mark (item a):** Which question can this card answer? Three question cards, each with a picture: a giraffe reaching up to acacia leaves; a giraffe lying down asleep; an acacia tree in the dark with a moon.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "How would you find the answer?"

## Day 103 sheet E103: page 4 of The Sugar Maple

**Read again:** page 4 of The Sugar Maple (texts/gk-u3-05-b.md), the songbird nest; the lesson reads pages 1 to 3 only.

**Mark (item a):** Which drawing joins what this tree gives and who uses it? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a maple branch holding a songbird's nest, with an arrow from the branch to the bird; the same branch and bird with no arrow; the branch with a squirrel at the nest, with an arrow.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "How do the branches and the songbird go together?"

## Day 104 card E104

**Read:** "This page is about a magnolia. Its flowers are big and white."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture group would start our page about this tree? Three picture groups: a magnolia tree with a label strip the teacher reads, "magnolia", and a big white flower; a big white flower with no label strip; the labelled magnolia with bare winter branches.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Say the opening for the page."

## Day 105 card E105

**Read:** Three source cards. Source A: "A willow grows by the water." Source B: "A willow's roots help stop the bank from washing away." Source C: "A frog sits on a lily pad."

**Mark (item a):** Our note says the roots help stop the bank from washing away. Which card did that fact come from? Three cards, each with a picture and a letter sticker: a willow by a pond; willow roots in a bank; a frog on a lily pad.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Show me the line."

## Day 106 card E106

**Read:** "A baobab tree has big white flowers that open at night. Bats visit the flowers."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture note belongs in our group notes? Three picture notes: a bat at a big white flower with a baobab sticker; a bat eating a fruit; a bat flying far from the tree.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words tell us?"
