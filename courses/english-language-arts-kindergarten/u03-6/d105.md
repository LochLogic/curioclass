# Day 105: One more fact, and the source that says it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E105. Have the day 104 opening chart on the wall. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard on manipulation. Set the consonant cards in order for the saying check. Return the day 104 handwriting slips with their feedback. Read aloud: "Add a fact. Say which card." and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: source, detail, information and check (earlier words), one gesture each, about 30 seconds each. Repair at most one, three minutes: if many drew a flower with no tree name on day 104 (error C), the puppet holds a loose picture of a pink flower and says "Its flowers are big and pink." Children say what the page needs first; put the label strip "pear" on the picture and say the name before the fact. Children say the name and the fact together. No mark today.

#### 15 min. Read 05b and 06

Whole class. Review: The Sugar Maple (day 104). Read Who Depends on a Tree? (text 06) whole in large print, about four minutes; it is short information and has no new word. Review: depend (day 90). Then reread the maple's seed lines on page 3, "A maple has seeds. Squirrels eat its seeds." After each page of 06 ask "Which text did we hear that in?" Three children answer, one sentence each; others may add with the token. Pass is always allowed. Say the two names aloud: 05b is the maple, 06 is the tree page.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "What further fact helps our opening?" Pairs talk, one turn each, 30 seconds a turn. Take three facts and write each as a short line on the chart. **Ask.** "Which source supports that exact statement?" Take it one fact at a time; point to the page that says it. Say aloud: a source supports a statement only if its own words say that statement.

#### 20 min. Draw and dictate

Whole class, then pairs. 8 min: jointly compose one more line for the shared opening. The class picks one fact, "Squirrels eat its seeds"; the teacher writes it under the opening and sets it beside the two pages. Page 2 of 06 says a squirrel eats nuts and seeds from a tree but never names the maple; page 3 of 05b says the maple's seeds. Read both lines. Say "05b says it, so the sticker is 05b." Children trace the sticker on the chart. 12 min: each child chooses a different fact from their notebook, draws it, puts a sticker on it and says which card it came from. Then pairs show each other the note and the line on the large print that says it; the teacher takes the day's six, 45 seconds each. Nothing is scored. Signs: a fact with the wrong sticker (error AI); a fact the page never says (error D).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E105 and read the three cards twice with their pictures. Ask [[s03-X105a]]: children point to one of three cards. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X105b]], 30 seconds each, while the class finishes its notes. Signs: the willow by the water or the frog (error AI).

#### 7 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: add or swap one sound (day 85 and earlier). Say "Add /s/ before it. What word?" (sit), then "Change /s/ to /h/. What word?" (hit); the class answers together. Use new words, never sat or mat. About two and a half minutes. Then six children not yet heard, one at a time, each add or swap one sound in a new word (say hop, change /h/ to /m/: mop), 45 seconds each. Write their names in the record column.

#### 10 min. Sound dictation (P3), writing

Whole class. Review: sounds to letters (days 94 and 101). Say the sounds in the four P3 groups one at a time, with the five short vowels, /k/ for c and /g/ for g; children write the letter that fits, about five minutes. Collect the page for written feedback. Then five minutes to revise the handwriting slip from day 104 after its feedback. Signs: b for /m/ (error O); "em" for m (error T); e for short a (error U).

#### 15 min. Sound saying (P3), one at a time

Individuals. Each child views the consonant cards and says the sound for each letter, 30 seconds a child, on sound saying, one child at a time at the teacher table; with 30 children that is the whole 15 minutes. The rest read A Log in a Pond with a partner, then tell a fact from it. Write who was heard. Signs: a letter name for a sound (error T); b for m (error O).

#### 8 min. Chain and spell

Pairs, tiles and counters. Review: short o against a and i (day 104). Chain dig, dog, dug, naming the changed sound each time. Then spell two new short o words by sounds, dot and nod: one counter a sound, then the letters. Signs: says /o/ for the changed letter in hit and hot (error AH); changes the wrong sound (error AG); a sound left out (error M).

#### 5 min. Map or and for

Whole class. Review: or and for (day 104). Reread both in new strips, "Dot can hop or nod." and "It is for Tom.", with the part to remember pointed. Signs: cannot read or or for without help (error S).

#### 15 min. Tree Words verse

Whole class. Review: verse 5, Apple Tree (day 102). Reread with the actions twice, four minutes. Echo the two rhyming words, hop and top. Children find or and for in the large print and read them. Pairs: one partner says a line and the other points to it, left to right, four minutes. Clap apple. Signs: points across a space (error AB).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 06 and the seed lines, add one fact with its sticker and say which card it came from, chain dig, dog and dug on tiles, and say the sounds on the consonant cards once. One projector: show the chart and the large print on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No stickers: write the book's name beside the note.

**Skip.** The verse and the sound saying.

- **10 min. A fact.** Read twice, two sentences from any book you have, one at a time. Your child draws one fact and writes the book's name beside it.
  **Say.** "Which book did that fact come from?" Expect: the book you read it from.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say dot and nod. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes the word.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads or and for on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X105a]] and [[s03-X105b]] about three cards you read twice: "Card A: A birch has white bark. Card B: A birch's leaves turn yellow in fall. Card C: A fox naps in the grass." Ask which card says the leaves turn yellow, and to show the line.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the joint line, the exit and the sound dictation; cut the verse. Longer: a secure child adds a second fact with its sticker.

### Words and supports

Words: source, detail, information, check, fact, card. Frame, only if a child asks: "It came from ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can name the card a fact came from. If many marked the wrong card (error AI), day 106 opens with five minutes: match a fact to its exact line and relabel the card. Day 106 reads the oak page again and takes small-group picture notes.
