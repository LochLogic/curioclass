# Exit cards, days 111 to 114

Sheet E111 goes with a page of the unit's own texts, read aloud again with its picture covered; it is not made up, so say nothing about practice for it. The sheet is a strip of three pictures. Cards E112 to E114 are made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice", read each twice with its picture, and print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of those three only.

The sheet and the cards use plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 111 sheet E111: page 5 of The Oak

**Read again:** page 5 of The Oak (texts/gk-u3-05-a.md), the oak as a habitat. Show a collage page that names the oak and shows its need, water.

**Mark (item a):** Which picture goes next, to show who depends on the tree? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a songbird on its nest in the oak's branches; the oak alone in summer; a bird at a birdhouse far from any tree.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words on the page tell us?"

## Day 112 card E112

**Read:** "A beech tree grows tall. Wild turkeys eat its nuts."

**Mark (item a):** Which strip is a fact from the card for the collage? Three strips read aloud, each with a picture: (a) "Wild turkeys eat the nuts." with a turkey under a beech; (b) "Beeches are the best trees." with a beech; (c) "Wild turkeys gobble loudly." with a turkey.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Tell me a fact from the card that you would draft for our collage."

## Day 113 card E113

**Read:** "An aspen has smooth pale bark. Its leaves flutter in the wind. Beavers eat aspen bark." A partner's question card, read aloud: "Where does the beaver get its food?"

**Mark (item a):** How should the drawing change to answer the question? Three drawing changes: a beaver at the trunk with an arrow from the bark to the beaver; a bigger aspen; a beaver asleep in a lodge with no link to the tree.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words tell us?"

## Day 114 card E114

**Read:** The source card, with sticker "ash card": "An ash tree has seeds with wings. Evening grosbeaks eat the seeds."

**Mark (item a):** Which note does the card support? Three notes read aloud, each with a sticker "ash card": (1) "Ash seeds have no wings." (2) "Rabbits eat ash leaves." (3) "Evening grosbeaks eat ash seeds."

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Show me the line."
