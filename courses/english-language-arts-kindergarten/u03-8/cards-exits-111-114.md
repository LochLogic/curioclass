# Exit cards, days 111 to 114

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 111 card E111

**Read:** "A sweetgum tree has leaves shaped like stars. Goldfinches eat its seeds."

**Mark (item a):** Which picture goes next, to show who depends on the tree? The class collage page names the tree and shows the need (water). Three pictures to go next: a goldfinch eating seeds on a sweetgum branch; the sweetgum in autumn colors; a goldfinch at a feeder far from any tree.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words on the card tell us?"

## Day 112 card E112

**Read:** "A beech tree grows tall. Wild turkeys eat its nuts."

**Mark (item a):** Which strip is a fact from the card for the collage? Three strips read aloud, each with a picture: (a) "Wild turkeys eat the nuts." with a turkey under a beech; (b) "Beeches are the best trees." with a beech; (c) "Wild turkeys gobble loudly." with a turkey.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Show me the line."

## Day 113 card E113

**Read:** "An aspen has smooth pale bark. Its leaves flutter in the wind. Beavers eat aspen bark." A partner's question card, read aloud: "Where does the beaver get its food?"

**Mark (item a):** How should the drawing change to answer the question? Three drawing changes: a beaver at the trunk with an arrow from the bark to the beaver; a bigger aspen; a beaver asleep in a lodge with no link to the tree.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which words tell us?"

## Day 114 card E114

**Read:** The source card, with sticker "ash card": "An ash tree has seeds with wings. Evening grosbeaks eat the seeds."

**Mark (item a):** Which note does the card support? Three notes read aloud, each with a sticker "ash card": (1) "Ash seeds have no wings." (2) "Rabbits eat ash leaves." (3) "Evening grosbeaks eat ash seeds."

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Show me the line."
