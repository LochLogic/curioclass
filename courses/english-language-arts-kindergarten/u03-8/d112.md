# Day 112: Draft a fact for the collage

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E112. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Roster children 11 to 15 take the F1 battery at your table: set the F1 sheet and keep its cards out of sight. Lay out the twelve picture cards and the map. After class write one step on each child's feedback strip (see Tomorrow). Read aloud: "Draw my fact" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: draft (day 70), detail (day 9) and source sticker (day 92), shown by gesture: a hand writes lightly, a finger adds one more, a hand presses a label. Repair at most one, four minutes: if many chose the tree again on day 111 (error J), use a new card, "A dogwood tree has white flowers in spring. Robins eat its red berries." Show only the dogwood. Read the line twice, then draw the feature it names, a robin at the red berries, and point to it while saying the line. Children say who depends on the dogwood.

#### 15 min. Read 05b and 06

Whole class. Review: Who Depends on a Tree? (days 90 and 105) and The Sugar Maple (days 103 and 111). Read 06 whole with its pictures, about five minutes. Then reread page 3 of 05b, the seeds and the squirrel, and page 4, the songbirds, in two-line chunks, and page 2 of 06 for the squirrel. After each line say its source: "That line is from 06." Children point to the living thing in each picture. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "Which fact will you draw for your tree?" Pairs, one minute a turn, any child may pass. Expect: a squirrel eats the maple's seeds; songbirds nest in its branches; a deer eats leaves and acorns. **Ask.** "Which reading is it from?" Same turns. Expect: 05b or 06. Take answers from three pairs after each ask.

#### 20 min. Draft a factual detail

Whole class, then individuals. Review: draft (day 70) and the written feedback strip (day 96). 4 min: model on the class page. Reread the line on page 4 of 05b, "Songbirds build nests in its branches." Draw a songbird in a nest on a branch, glue a 05b sticker in the corner and say the sentence: "A songbird nests in the maple." Write it under the picture. 16 min: each child chooses one fact from 05b or 06 for their tree, draws it by the tree, glues the right sticker in the corner and dictates one sentence while you write it. Walk round and ask six children, 30 seconds each: "Which reading is your fact from?" After class write one step on each strip, for the first look-for that fails: (1) the tree is named, else "Name the tree"; (2) the fact is on the card, else "Add one fact from a card"; (3) the sticker names the right reading, else "Show the card line"; (4) the picture adds information, else "Draw the arrow". Nothing is scored. Signs: no tree named (error C); a fact the reading never says (error D); the wrong sticker (error AI); a picture that adds nothing (error J).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E112, read it twice with its strips and pictures. Ask [[s03-X112a]]: children point to one of three strips. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X112b]], 30 seconds each, while the class puts its pages away. Signs: the opinion strip or the noise strip, or a drafted line the card does not give (error D).

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85). Say hat, leg and wig; children push a counter for each sound and say the middle sound, about two minutes. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, say the first, middle and last sound of dog, jug, cap, hen, lid or rug. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 12 min. All short vowels (cycle 18)

Whole class, then pairs. New: all five short vowels, a, e, i, o and u, compared by their middle sounds, not by the names of the letters. 5 min: sort the twelve picture cards into five groups by middle sound, with the map beside the cards: hen, jet, leg and web; cap and ham; lid and wig; log and dog; rug and tub. 5 min: review chains on tiles (day 88): hat, hit, hot and jet, jig, jug, naming the changed sound each time. 2 min: show A Pet at Home. Read the title aloud yourself, then read page 1, "Ned has a pet.", with the class, pointing under each word. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 6 min. Map are and will

Whole class. Review: are (day 66), be and by (day 107). New: will; the w and i are regular, and the double l is one sound, the part to remember. Read with by sounding it out w, i, th (th is one sound, review of cycle 12). Read each word on a card, then the sentence strips "Rob will get a big jet.", "Ken is with Beth." and "You are a fun pal." Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery

Roster children 11 to 15 each take three minutes at your table, 15 minutes in all. Take each through the held back F1 cards in order: the pair, naming the changed letter and its sound [[s03-F112a]]; spelling by sounds [[s03-F112b]]; the fresh decodable, saying what it tells and pointing to its words [[s03-F112c]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never teach on these cards. The other twenty-five rotate through three stations of about seven minutes: A Pet at Home in pairs, picture covered, a page each, then say what happened; tiles; printing verse words and letter cards. Signs: points to the wrong letter (error L); names the letter, not the sound (error AH); leaves out a sound (error M); reads the words fast and cannot say what is in the pot (error K); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Whole class. Dictate wig, log and jug; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 7 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verses 1 to 6. New: verse 7, Grow. Hear it twice with its actions: curl up, rise, reach, chirp. Find the rhyming words tight and light, clap sapling, and point out will and are in the lines. Collect counters, tiles and glue sticks, and file the collage pages.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 06 once, ask the two questions, and draft one fact on the page together with its sticker. Read the reader's first page, chain hat, hit and hot, and take the F1 cards alone. One projector: hold the class page and the reader on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No glue: draw the sticker. No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The picture sort, the reader and the verse.

- **10 min. A fact and where it came from.** Read twice: "A maple has seeds. Squirrels eat its seeds." Your child draws a squirrel at the seeds on a tree and you write the sentence under it.
  **Say.** "Which fact did you draw? Which reading is it from?" Expect: a squirrel eats its seeds; the one you just read.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain hat, hit, hot on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads will on a card.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X112a]] and [[s03-X112b]] about three strips you read aloud, after reading twice: "A fig tree has soft leaves. Bats eat its figs." Strips: "Bats eat the figs." "Figs are the best snack." "Bats sleep upside down."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the draft, the exit and the spelling; cut the sort and the verse. Longer: a secure child adds a second fact and its sticker.

### Words and supports

Words: draft, detail, source sticker, reading. Frame, only if a child asks: "My fact is ___. It is from ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can pick a fact the card gives. If many chose an opinion or a line the card never says (error D), day 113 opens with five minutes: point to the source line and set aside the unsupported part. Day 113 hands back each feedback strip, adds a second detail and hears roster children 16 to 20 for the F1 battery.
