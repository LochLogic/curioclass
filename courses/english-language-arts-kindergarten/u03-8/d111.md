# Day 111: Begin the collage page

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape the class collage page to the board, empty. Put each child's talk sticker in the notebook, from the record sheets (R2 and F1). Cover E111. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Roster children 6 to 10 take the F1 battery at your table: set the F1 sheet and keep its cards out of sight. Read aloud: "Write my tree's name" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Review: collage (day 107), need (days 80 and 82) and dependent (day 103), shown by gesture: hands frame a page, hands pull in, a hand points out. New: the collage page. Hold up the blank page and point to its spaces: the tree in the middle, a need coming in, a place for a living thing, a corner for a source sticker. Then hand back the research talks, three minutes: day 110's assessment (the talks of children 21 to 30 and the F1 battery of children 1 to 5), and the talks of day 107 (children 1 to 10) and day 109 (children 11 to 20), which had not gone back. Each notebook carries a talk sticker: a star is shown, a star with a dot is shown with help, a circle is not yet seen. Partners show each other a circle and say what they will add on the collage page today; a child with no circle says which part was hardest. No repair is routed here.

#### 15 min. Read 05b

Whole class. Review: The Sugar Maple (days 103 and 108). Read all six pages with the pictures, about five minutes, and say: listen for what the maple needs and who uses it. Then reread pages 2 and 3 in two-line chunks, pointing under the lines; the children echo water, light, air and soil with a gesture each. After each line say its source: "That line is from 05b." Last, ask children to point to the water in the page 2 picture and to the squirrel on page 3. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "Which tree is our collage about?" Pairs, one minute a turn, any child may pass. Take answers from three pairs. Expect: the sugar maple; a child whose plan sheet shows the oak says the oak. **Ask.** "What does it need, and who uses it?" Same turns. Expect: it needs water, light and soil; squirrels eat its seeds and songbirds nest in its branches. Write the six exit children in the record column as they speak.

#### 20 min. Collage composition begun

Whole class, then individuals. 3 min: on the class page, glue a trunk and a round crown in the middle. Write the label strip maple, read it aloud and glue it at the top. Say: you may write the sounds you hear or tell me the name and I will write it. Glue a sun above the crown and draw an arrow from the sun to the leaves. Say: "The maple needs light." Leave the living-thing space and the corner empty; the next days fill them. 17 min: each child puts a tree in the middle of the collage page, names it with a label strip by sounds or dictation, and adds one need from the reading with an arrow going in. A child whose plan sheet shows the oak keeps the oak. Spelling earns no mark. Walk round and ask six children, 30 seconds each: "What is your tree? What does it need?" Look for: (1) the tree is named; (2) the need is one the reading gives; (3) an arrow goes in. Nothing is scored. Signs: no tree named (error C); a sun with no arrow into the leaves, or leaves for show (error J).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E111, read it twice with its pictures, and point out that the class page shows the maple and its need, water. Ask [[s03-X111a]]: children point to one of three pictures. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X111b]], 30 seconds each, while the class puts its pages away. Signs: the autumn tree picture (error J); the feeder picture (error D).

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85) and adding a sound to make a new word (day 42). Say vet, hen and set; children push a counter for each sound and say the middle sound, about two minutes. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, answer [[s03-P4e]]. Write their names in the record column. Signs: drops the vowel (error V); changes more than one sound (error AG).

#### 10 min. Short e review

Whole class, then pairs. Review: short e (day 107) and chains on tiles (day 88). Chain ten, tan, tin and then set, sat, sit, naming the changed sound each time: "ten to tan: the sound /e/ changed to /a/." Then read set 17 card 3, "Deb got a wet web.", and ask what Deb got. Expect: a wet web. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map be and by

Whole class. Review: be and by (day 107), then the other mapped words, read in the strips. Read two new strips, "Meg can be by the hen." and "Ted sat by the van." Point at the e in be and the y in by, the parts to remember. Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery

Roster children 6 to 10 each take three minutes at your table, 15 minutes in all. Take each through the held back F1 cards in order: the pair, naming the changed letter and its sound [[s03-F111a]]; spelling by sounds [[s03-F111b]]; the fresh decodable, saying what it tells and pointing to its words [[s03-F111c]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never teach on these cards. The other twenty-five rotate through three stations of about seven minutes: A Hen for Meg in pairs, picture covered, a page each, then say what happened; tiles; printing verse words and letter cards. Signs: points to the wrong letter (error L); names the letter, not the sound (error AH); leaves out a sound (error M); reads the words fast and cannot say what is in the pot (error K); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Whole class. Dictate jet, men and vet; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verse 6 (day 107). Choral read it twice, pointing under each word line by line, and find the rhyming words rest and best. Then collect counters, tiles and glue sticks, and file the collage pages.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 05b once, ask the two questions, and make the collage page together with a tree and one need. Read the reader once, chain ten, tan and tin, and take the F1 cards alone. One projector: hold the class page and the reader on one screen; the child still glues and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No glue: draw the shapes. No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The sentence cards, the reader and the verse.

- **10 min. A tree and a need.** Read twice: "A maple needs water. Its roots take water from the soil." Your child draws a tree in the middle of a page, a drop of water and an arrow to the tree, and says the tree's name.
  **Say.** "What is your tree? What does it need?" Expect: a maple, and water.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain ten, tan, tin on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads be and by on two cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X111a]] and [[s03-X111b]] about three drawings you make, read twice: "A pine tree has long needles. Chickadees eat its seeds." Draw a chickadee eating seeds on a pine branch, a pine tree in snow, and a chickadee at a feeder far from any tree. Say: "Our page shows the pine and the water it needs."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the page, the exit and the spelling; cut the sentence card and the verse. Longer: a secure child adds a second need with its arrow.

### Words and supports

Words: collage page, need, dependent, source sticker. Frame, only if a child asks: "My tree is ___. It needs ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can pick the picture that shows who depends on a tree. If many chose the tree again (error J), day 112 opens with five minutes: lay the two pictures beside the card, read the line, then draw the feature the line names and point to it while speaking. Day 112 hears roster children 11 to 15 for the F1 battery and drafts a fact on the collage.
