# Day 114: Finish the page and check each fact

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E114. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Put each child's collage page and feedback strip on their desk. Lay out the four partner question cards. There is no F1 battery today. Read aloud: "Draw one last thing" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Preview note by gesture: a hand writes a short line (new: note). Review: source sticker (day 92) and feedback strip (day 112). Repair at most one, four minutes: if many chose the beaver asleep with no link on day 113 (error A), draw two facts from 05b, a squirrel and a maple seed, and put an arrow between them. Say, "The maple gives the squirrel seeds." Children say the link.

#### 15 min. Read 05b

Whole class. Review: The Sugar Maple (days 103, 111 and 112). Read all six pages with the pictures, about five minutes. Then reread pages 3 and 4 in two-line chunks, pointing under the lines. After each line say its source: "That line is from 05b." Children point to the line that tells what the squirrel does and what the songbirds do. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "Which line says so?" A partner holds up the card that asks it. Pairs, one minute a turn, any child may pass. Expect: a child points to a line in the large print for a fact on the page. **Ask.** "What will you add to finish your page?" Same turns. Expect: a fact, an arrow, a name or a sticker. Take answers from three pairs after each ask.

#### 20 min. Finish and check each detail

Whole class, then individuals. Review: the source sticker (day 92), the feedback strip (day 112) and revising after a question (day 113). 4 min: model on the class page. Read the line "Songbirds build nests in its branches." and look at the songbird on the class page. Check it: point to the line, say "That is on the card," and press a tick beside the sticker. Then find a note with no line: "Maples are the best." Say, "No line says it," and cross it out. 16 min: each child reads the step on their feedback strip, makes that change and then checks each fact by pointing to its line in the large print. A child adds the arrow, the name or the sticker the strip asks for, and dictates one last sentence while you write it. Walk round and ask six children, 30 seconds each: "Show me the line." Nothing is scored. Signs: a fact with no card line (error D); the wrong sticker (error AI); a picture that adds nothing (error J); two facts with no arrow (error A).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E114, read the source card and its sticker, the ash card, twice. Read the three notes aloud, each with the same sticker. Ask [[s03-X114a]]: children point to one of three notes. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X114b]], 30 seconds each, while the class puts its pages away. Signs: the note that says the opposite (error D); the note the card never says (error D); the winged seeds sentence for the grosbeaks note (error AI).

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Whole class, then individuals. Repair at most one, five minutes: if many chose the bigger aspen on day 113 (error J), use a new card, "A willow has long thin leaves. Deer eat its leaves." Read the line twice, then draw the feature the line names, a deer at the leaves, and point to it while saying the line. Then each child puts a last touch on the page, five minutes: add the feature a line names, or move one thing so the drawing shows it. Put the pages in a pile for tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85). Say pan, pen and pin; children push a counter for each sound and say the middle sound, about two minutes. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, say the first, middle and last sound of bug, dig, fox, hen or cap. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 10 min. All short vowels, application

Whole class, then pairs. Review: all short vowels (day 112) and chains on tiles (day 88). Chain pan, pen, pin, pun and big, bag, bug, naming the changed sound each time. Then pairs read a set 18 sentence card, say who did what and check one word against the map. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map are and will

Whole class. Review: are (day 66), will (day 112), be and by (day 107). Read each on a card, then in two new strips, "Dad will be by the van." and "Pam and Sam are with Meg." Point to the double l in will. Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Partner reading

Pairs. Review: A Pet at Home (day 112). Partners read the reader, a page each, picture covered, then say what happened and point to the words that tell them. Walk round and listen to six pairs, three minutes each. Nothing is scored. Signs: guesses from the picture (error R); reads the words fast and cannot say what happened (error K); names the letter, not the sound (error AH).

#### 5 min. Spell and print

Whole class. Dictate fan, pig and bug; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 5 min. Tree Words verse

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verse 7 (days 112 and 113). Reread it with the actions and echo the rhyming words tight and light. Collect counters, tiles and glue sticks, and file the collage pages.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 05b once, check one fact against its line and take the exit card alone. Read the reader once and chain pan, pen and pin. One projector: hold the class page and the reader on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No glue: draw the sticker. No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse.

- **10 min. Show me the line.** Read twice: "A maple has seeds. Squirrels eat its seeds." Your child draws a squirrel at the seeds.
  **Say.** "Show me the line." Expect: your child points to the squirrel sentence.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain pan, pen, pin on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads are and will on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X114a]] and [[s03-X114b]] after reading twice: "A birch tree has thin bark. Chickadees eat its seeds." Notes: "Birch bark is thick." "Mice eat birch leaves." "Chickadees eat birch seeds." Then say: "Show me the line."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the check, the exit and the spelling; cut the partner reading and the verse. Longer: a secure child checks a second fact on a friend's page.

### Words and supports

Words: note, source sticker, card line, check. Frame, only if a child asks: "My fact is ___. The line says ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can check a note against its card line. If many chose a note the card never says or one that says the opposite (error D), day 115 opens with five minutes: check each note against its sticker card line, point to it and set aside the unsupported one. Day 115 rehearses an explanation of what the collage teaches about the tree.
