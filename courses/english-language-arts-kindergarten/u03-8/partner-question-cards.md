# Partner question cards

Used on day 113. Each card holds one question the partner may ask, with a small picture, read aloud by the teacher and then by the child asking:

- Where does it get its food?
- Where does it live?
- What does the tree give it?
- Which card says so?

The asking child holds the talking token and listens to the answer. The answering child adds the thing asked for to the drawing, with an arrow, and points to the card line. Every question can be answered from a card the class has heard.

A room with nothing: write one question on each of four scrap cards and draw a small picture.
