# Collage page and precut shapes

One page for each child, used on days 111 to 114.

The page is a sheet in portrait with four spaces: a top strip for the tree's name (the child writes by sounds or dictates), a large space for the tree, a space beside it for one need, and a space for one living thing that depends on the tree, with a place for an arrow between them and a corner box for a source sticker.

Precut shapes: a trunk, a round crown, a few leaves, a drop of water, a sun, a small bird, a small animal and three arrows. A pre-glued tree is offered if fine motor work is hard. The child draws or glues the shape the card names, never a decoration.

A room with nothing: fold a sheet of paper in four, tear shapes from scrap paper and draw the arrows.
