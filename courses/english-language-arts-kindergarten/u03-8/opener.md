# Section 3.8 opener: making the collage

## What the section does

Days 111 to 114 build the collage: a page with the tree named, what it needs, one living thing that depends on it, a source sticker and an arrow. Day 111 starts the page, day 112 drafts a fact, day 113 adds a second detail and changes the drawing after a partner asks a question, and day 114 checks every detail against its card. Foundations finish the short vowels, with five children heard each day at the teacher table.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the collage work and a five-minute exit, after which six children, a rotating group named on the record sheet, are heard saying why. The foundations block holds sounds, words and a rotation. A child outside the heard group is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read alone; the teacher reads every card, direction and text aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Draw what the words say.** A decoration is not a detail.
- **A partner's question can change a drawing.** We add the thing that answers it.
- **Every detail has a card.** We point to the line before we keep it.

## The errors, in plain words

- **J:** a drawing that does not carry the detail. Draw the named feature and point to it.
- **D, AI:** something the card never says, or a fact given to the wrong card. Point to the line and set the extra aside.
- **A, AA:** no arrow, or a draft left as it was after a question. Add the arrow or the thing asked for.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No picture or question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion.

## Supplies

The collage page, precut paper shapes, crayons and glue sticks, source stickers, the partner question cards, the feedback strip, the exit cards, texts 05b, 06 and 03 in large print, the cycle 17 and 18 readers and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Fold a sheet of paper in half for the page, tear shapes by hand and draw the source stickers on scrap. Use buttons for counters.
