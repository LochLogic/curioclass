# Day 113: A second fact and a partner's question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover E113. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Roster children 16 to 20 take the F1 battery at your table: set the F1 sheet and keep its cards out of sight. Put each child's feedback strip, with the step you wrote after day 112, on their collage page. Lay out the four partner question cards. Read aloud: "Draw what I changed" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Preview question and revise by gesture: a hand asks, a hand changes the page (new: partner question, revise). Review: draft (day 70). Hand back each feedback strip and read its step aloud, half a minute. Repair at most one, four minutes: if many chose a line the card never says on day 112 (error D), use a new card, "An elm tree has rough bark. Sparrows nest in its branches." Read it twice. Read the strips "Sparrows nest in its branches." and "Elms are the best.", point to the card line that says each, and set aside the strip with no line.

#### 15 min. Read 03 and 05b

Whole class. Review: A Tree Is a Living Thing (days 89 and 108) and The Sugar Maple (days 103 and 112). Read the chant 03 whole, each line said twice and the class joining the second time, about four minutes, pictures only. Then reread pages 3 and 4 of 05b, the squirrel and the songbirds, about three minutes. After each line say its source: "That line is from 05b." Children point to the line in the large print for the water, the leaves in the light and the seeds. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "What second fact can we add?" Pairs, one minute a turn, any child may pass. Expect: a squirrel eats the seeds; songbirds nest in the branches; leaves make food from light, air and water. **Ask.** "What question will your partner ask about your drawing?" Read the four partner question cards aloud: Where does it get its food? Where does it live? What does the tree give it? Which card says so? Same turns. Expect: one of the four cards. Take answers from three pairs after each ask.

#### 20 min. Second detail and partner question

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the written feedback strip (day 112) and revising after one step (day 97). 4 min: model on the class page. Add a second fact from page 3 of 05b: draw a squirrel beside the maple, glue a 05b sticker and say, "A squirrel eats the seeds." The puppet asks the card question "What does the tree give it?" Answer from the line, then revise: draw winged seeds falling from the maple and an arrow from the seeds to the squirrel, and say, "The maple gives the squirrel seeds." 16 min: 5 min, each child adds a second fact from 05b or 03 with its sticker and dictates the sentence. Then pairs with the token, 4 minutes a child: the partner holds up one question card and asks it, the child answers by adding or moving one thing on the drawing, with an arrow if the answer joins two things, and points to the line. Walk round and ask six children, 30 seconds each: "What did you change?" After class write the next step on each strip, for the first look-for that fails: the tree is named, the fact is on the card, the sticker is right, the picture adds information. Nothing is scored. Signs: a question answered in words only, with the drawing unchanged (error J); a bird and a tree with no arrow (error A); a fact the reading never says (error D); the wrong sticker (error AI).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E113, read it and the partner's question twice, and show its three drawing changes. Ask [[s03-X113a]]: children point to one of three drawings. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X113b]], 30 seconds each, while the class puts its pages away. Signs: the bigger aspen (error J); the beaver asleep with no link (error A); the leaves sentence (error J).

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: a counter for each sound (day 85). Say tub, men and cap; children push a counter for each sound and say the middle sound, about two minutes. Then six children not yet heard, 30 seconds each, say the first, middle and last sound of rug, dog, jet, lid or wig. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 12 min. All short vowels (cycle 18)

Whole class, then pairs. Review: all short vowels, compared by their middle sounds (day 112). With tiles and counters chain tap, tip, top and dig, dog, dug, naming the changed sound each time. Then read set 18 card 4, "Dad will fix the van with Tim.", and ask who will fix the van. Expect: Dad. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 6 min. Map are and will

Whole class. Review: are (day 66), will (day 112), be and by (day 107). Read each on a card, then in two new strips, "Ted will mop the mat with Chad." and "Beth and Meg are by the van." Point to the double l in will. Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery

Roster children 16 to 20 each take three minutes at your table, 15 minutes in all. Take each through the held back F1 cards in order: the pair, naming the changed letter and its sound [[s03-F113a]]; spelling by sounds [[s03-F113b]]; the fresh decodable, saying what it tells and pointing to its words [[s03-F113c]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never teach on these cards. The other twenty-five rotate through three stations of about seven minutes: A Pet at Home in pairs, picture covered, a page each, then say what happened; tiles; printing verse words and letter cards. Signs: points to the wrong letter (error L); names the letter, not the sound (error AH); leaves out a sound (error M); reads the words fast and cannot say what is in the pot (error K); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Whole class. Dictate ham, leg and rug; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 7 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verse 7 (day 112). Reread it with the actions and echo the rhyming words tight and light. Collect counters, tiles and glue sticks, and file the collage pages.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read 03 once, add a second fact with its sticker, hold up one question card and revise the drawing in answer. Read the reader once, chain tap, tip and top, and take the F1 cards alone. One projector: hold the class page and the reader on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No glue: draw the sticker. No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse.

- **10 min. A second fact and a question.** Read twice: "A maple gives shelter. Songbirds build nests in its branches." Your child draws a tree and a songbird beside it.
  **Say.** "Where does the songbird live? Show me on your drawing." Expect: in a nest in the branches; your child adds a nest.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain tap, tip, top on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads are and will on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X113a]] and [[s03-X113b]] about three drawings you describe, after reading twice: "A hemlock tree has tiny cones. Squirrels eat its seeds." Question: "What does the tree give the squirrel?" Drawings: seeds from the cones with an arrow to the squirrel; a taller hemlock; a squirrel asleep with no seeds.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the second fact, the question, the exit and the spelling; cut the chant and the verse. Longer: a secure child answers a second question card on the same drawing.

### Words and supports

Words: partner question, revise, fact, arrow. Frame, only if a child asks: "My partner asks ___. I add ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can change a drawing to answer a question. If many chose the bigger aspen (error J), day 114's Return and repair takes five minutes: draw the feature the line names and point to it while speaking. If many chose the beaver asleep with no link (error A), day 114 opens with five minutes: put a because arrow between two facts and say so. Day 114 has no F1 battery; it checks each fact against its source.
