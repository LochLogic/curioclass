# Exit cards and the retry card, days 6 to 8

Each exit has a card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's six. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Day 6 card: the cups

**Read:** "Two friends stack cups. One holds the pile steady while the other adds a cup."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: one child steadying a stack of cups while another adds a cup; a child alone drinking from a cup; two children running.

**Mark:** circle the picture that belongs in a class message about how these friends play. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Tell me one thing the message could say about these friends."

## Day 7 card: the toy shop

**Read:** "Three children open a toy shop. One sorts the toys on a shelf."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a child sorting toys on a shelf; one toy alone on a floor; a playground.

**Mark:** circle the picture that goes in a drawing plan about this shop. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What will you tell a reader about the shop?"

## Day 8 retry card (not scored)

**Read:** "Tal offers the drum. Bo asks for a turn. Tal passes it."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: Tal passing the drum to Bo; Tal holding the drum away; Bo walking off.

**Mark:** circle the picture that matches the last sentence. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "How could a partner ask for help?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 6: the steadying and adding picture. Spoken: "One holds the cups" or "They take turns." Not yet: the child drinking alone (A); a name alone, "cups" or "I like cups" (M).
- Day 7: the sorting picture. Spoken: names the shop and one action, "One child sorts the toys." Not yet: the lone toy (A); "I like it" (X).
- Day 8: Tal passing. Spoken: "Can you help me?" Not yet: Tal holding it away (B); "I cannot do it" (E). The retry is not scored.

Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may point, act or use a home language; say the English back.
