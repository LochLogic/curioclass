# Day 8: Respond: a fresh card, the story again, and partner talk

## Teacher page

### Before class

Choose the two most common slips of days 1 to 7 from the record sheet, and the one most missed foundations slip (t, a, N, O, P or AA). Have the puppet, the retry card and a fresh strip ready: three empty boxes on handwriting paper. Put a turn card in each pair's basket. Today's six are the eighth six names on the record sheet; the retry is not scored.

### The day

#### 15 min. Targeted reteach and retry

Whole class, then at the desk. The retry follows day 7's exit: if many circled the toy alone on the floor (error A), that is one of the two slips.

**Reteach, 7 minutes.** The puppet shows each wrong example one line at a time, you show the right one, the class tries once (about 3 minutes each, one minute to move on).

- Error A: the puppet circles a child drinking alone for a card about friends stacking cups. Reread the card sentence, point to the matching picture, and each pair says one fact the picture shows.
- Error B: the puppet picks the picture of Teo talking for "He scuffed his shoe in the grass." Lay both pictures beside the reread sentence and match the action.
- Error E: the puppet says "This lid is stuck." Model "Can you help me open it?" and the children turn two statements into questions.
- Error M: the puppet writes only "cups". Add one fact the picture shows: "They take turns."
- Error X: the puppet says "I like it." Ask "Which toy?" and "What do you do with it?" and attach a toy label.

**Retry, 8 minutes.** Say: "This card is made up for practice. It is a retry, and nobody gets a mark on it." Hand out the day 8 card (the drum). Read aloud once: "Tal offers the drum. Bo asks for a turn. Tal passes it." Every child circles the picture that matches the last sentence: Tal passing the drum to Bo, Tal holding it away, or Bo walking off ([[s01-X8]], part a: 2 minutes). Hear today's six, 30 seconds each: "How could a partner ask for help?" Expect "Can you help me?" or a question like it. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet.

Signs: Tal holding the drum away (error B, a picture of a different moment); "I cannot do it" (error E, a statement where a question is needed).

#### 12 min. Reread pages 5 to 9

Whole class, then pairs. Review: One Hoop, Two Friends (Noor and Teo); you read it on days 2 to 5 and on day 6. Read pages 5 to 9 aloud (about 3 minutes), holding up each picture. Review: routine: Read, Point, Talk, Draw: hear a text, point back, talk, draw one thing and tell a partner.

**Ask.** Which words tell how Teo felt at the end? Expect: "His whole face lit up", "Teo grinned." Pairs talk for a minute; children point back to the words as you reread page 8 and page 9 (the words are read to them). Hear three pairs.

**Ask.** What does the last picture show? Expect: the two friends walking side by side to the school door with the basket and the hoop. Then reread page 9 and ask a child to point to the words that go with the picture: "Noor put the hoop back in the basket." Review: skill: point back to the words and the picture that tell. Say "This shows ___." as a frame (Review: skill: match a picture to the moment just heard and say why the other does not fit).

#### 13 min. Partner talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass, with the turn card.

**Ask.** What was the best way Noor and Teo shared? Expect: they took turns, they counted to ten, one pushed and then the other. Each pair has two exchanges (about 4 minutes). Then each pair asks one question to get a missing detail, such as "Who went first?" or "What did they count?" (about 3 minutes); review: a question asks and a statement tells ("Can you tell me more?" is a question). Hear three pairs, about 80 seconds each. Mark who was heard.

#### 10 min. Read, Point, Talk, Draw

Alone, then pairs. Reread page 8 once. Each child draws one moment of passing on the student page (6 minutes), then tells a partner which words fit the drawing (3 minutes); the partner says "I heard ___." A child may point to the words in the booklet instead of saying them.

#### 10 min. Share

Whole class. Six children (today's six) say one sentence about their drawing, 40 seconds each, and the class says "I heard ___." (6 minutes). Then say the poem Ask and Listen together once, with the class joining "We listen well." and you pointing under each line (Review: poem: a text made to say together, read once, then with the class joining the repeated line). Look at the class message on the wall and reread it together, finger under each word (2 minutes).

#### 3 min. Opening

Whole class. Chant for t and a: "t, t, /t/, top." "a, a, /a/, apple." (Review: letter t: name "tee", sound /t/, keyword top.) Hold up the two cards to say each name and sound.

#### 10 min. Foundations response

Whole class. Pick the one slip more children made in day 7's lines and sounds (error N, O, P or AA, or a printed t or a that is hard to read). The puppet shows it and you show the right way (4 minutes).

- Error N: the puppet says "ay" for /a/. Say the name, then the sound with the letter and apple.
- Error O: the puppet writes t when it hears /a/. Hear the sound, pick the letter card, then write.
- Error P: the puppet prints only A when lowercase is asked. Model the lowercase path (curve round, close it, straight down); each child writes it once in the air.
- Error AA: the puppet calls the letter a "t". Hold the t and a cards side by side and name each.

Then the fresh retry on a new strip (6 minutes), not scored: say /a/, each child holds up the a card from the pair (t and a) and prints lowercase a in box 1; say /t/, holds up the t card and prints lowercase t in box 2; say /a/ and print capital A in box 3. Walk the room and look for: starts at the top, the curve closes, the cross goes left to right, says the sound and not the name.

#### 5 min. Mapped words

Whole class. Review: mapped words I and my (read as whole words). Show a strip "I have my toy." Point under each word, the class says it, then children point to I and to my. The other words are read to them; do not ask anyone to sound out have or toy.

#### 2 min. Closing

Chant for t and a.

#### 40 min. Rotations

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five 8-minute turns. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets a minute (6 minutes, 2 for the move): say the name and the sound of t and of a, pick each card from the pair, print one of them on a slate or paper, say whether "mat" rhymes with "cat" and whether "top" does, and clap the beats of a toy name (puzzle, teddy, dinosaur: 2, 2, 3). Table notes go on the record sheet for today's six only. Stations as day 7: finger a or t in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: read pages 5 to 9, ask the two questions, and ask the child to ask you one question about a missing detail. Do the drum card together and print t and a. One projector: show the pictures and the card on one screen; children circle on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No large story: tell pages 5 to 9 aloud. No sand tray: write with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The reteach, the share, the rotations and the stations.

- **5 min. Reread.** Read or tell the end of the story.
  **Say.** "Which words tell how Teo felt at the end?" Expect: his whole face lit up.
- **5 min. Talk.** Talk about the best way the friends shared. Your child asks you one question to find a missing detail.
  **Say.** "What was the best way they shared?" Expect: they took turns.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one moment of passing and tells you which words fit.
- **5 min. Print t and a.** Say each sound, your child picks and prints the letter.
- **5 min. The poem.** Say Ask and Listen together, finger under each line.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles a picture and says how a partner could ask for help. It is a retry, so no mark.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the share and one of the two questions; keep the reteach, the retry and the foundations response. Longer: a child who is secure asks a second question to find a missing detail.

### Words and supports

Words: retry, felt, passing, question. Frames: "This shows ___." "Can you help me?" "I heard ___." A home language, signs and pointing count as answers; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The retry and the notes from days 6 to 8 go on the record sheet and set the order of the groups for day 9. If many circled Tal holding the drum away (error B) or said "I cannot do it" (error E) in day 8's retry, day 9's first minutes repeat that one. Day 9 reads the first text about how toys move and models a main-topic box with details.
