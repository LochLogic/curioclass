# Day 6: A class message about playing together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang chart paper at the rug and put a green dot at the left edge of every line. Keep the pointer, the puppet and the two sentence strips (I take turns. / My turn.) beside it. Lay the page 4 and page 6 pictures out. Put a turn card in each pair's basket. Today's six are the sixth six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Repair

Whole class. Look at the record sheet for day 4's exit and day 5's items (the marks you judged after class from day 5's assessment) and pick the one slip more children made. The puppet shows the wrong way, you show the right way, the class tries once.

- Error B, a picture of a different moment: the puppet points to the page 6 picture for "He scuffed his shoe in the grass." Lay the page 4 and page 6 pictures beside the reread sentence and match the action.
- Error E, a statement where a question is needed: the puppet says "This lid is stuck." The class turns it into "Can you help me open it?"
- Error A, an idea that is not in the story: the puppet says "A dog ran by." Reread "Noor pushed the hoop again." and every child points to the matching picture.

#### 6 min. Reread the story

Review: One Hoop, Two Friends (Noor and Teo), heard whole on day 2. Say: "Listen for what Noor and Teo do to play together." Read all nine pages, holding up each picture. No questions yet.

#### 4 min. Talk

Pairs. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass. The child holding the turn card speaks.

**Ask.** What do friends do to play together? Expect: they take turns, they count, one pushes and one waits, one passes the hoop. Pairs take two exchanges each (about 2 minutes). Hear three pairs, 30 seconds each. If a child says "I like hoops", say "That is a good thing to like. What did Noor and Teo do?"

#### 10 min. Model the class message

Whole class at the chart. Say: "A message tells a reader something. I want to tell a reader how Noor and Teo played." Think aloud: "They took turns. That is my idea. You tell me the idea, and I put the letters down."

Write "We take turns." Say each word as you write it. Put the pointer under it, start at the green dot, and slide left to right. The class says each word as you point (they echo the words; they are not asked to sound them out). Count the words with the class: We, take, turns, three, with a space between each (hold two fingers in the gaps).

Write a second line: "I wait for my turn." Point and say each word. Ask a few children to come up and point to the two words they already know: I and my (Review: mapped words I and my, read as whole words). Everyone points to them in the air.

**Ask.** Where does the line start? Expect: at the green dot, on the left. If a child slides right to left (error H), mark the start edge and track one line together.

#### 15 min. Compose the message together

Pairs, then whole class. Each pair chooses one fact from the story (1 minute) and whispers it to the pair beside them (2 minutes). Facts to expect: Noor pushes the hoop. Teo asks for a turn. Teo waits. They count to ten. They take turns. Teo says "Tomorrow you go first." (the facts are the story's, not a child's toy at home).

Take six ideas, one a minute (6 minutes), calling first the pairs that hold the day's six. Say each idea back, then write it. Join or choose so the chart ends with three short sentences, such as: "Noor pushes the hoop. Teo waits. They take turns." Use the class's own words. Reread all three, pointer under each word, the class joining (3 minutes). The message stays on the wall for day 8. A child who gives only "hoop" or "I like it" gets the prompt "What do they do?" (error M).

Why you write while they dictate: the child supplies the idea, you supply the letters. Say this to the class once.

#### 15 min. Draw and dictate

Alone, then pairs, then the table. Each child draws one picture for the message on the student page: a friend passing, or a friend waiting (6 minutes). Then each child tells a partner one sentence about the picture and the partner says it back (routine: dictate a sentence to a partner who says it back, then to the teacher), 2 minutes, both at once.

For the last 7 minutes take dictation from today's six, about a minute each, at the table. The child says the sentence, you write it under the picture saying each word, finger under it, and the child says it back with you. The other 24 children keep drawing or color; they are heard on later days. The sentence strips for the next part come from these sentences when a child has said "I take turns." or "My turn." If not, write the two strips yourself.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the day 6 card (the cups). Read aloud: "Two friends stack cups. One holds the pile steady while the other adds a cup." Children circle the picture that belongs in a class message about how these friends play: the child steadying a stack while another adds a cup, a child alone drinking, or two children running ([[s01-X6]], part a: 2 minutes). Hear today's six, 30 seconds each: "Tell me one thing the message could say about these friends." Expect "One holds the cups" or "They take turns." Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

Signs: the child drinking alone (error A, a picture that leaves out how the friends play); "cups" or "I like cups" (error M, a name or a feeling with no fact).

#### 3 min. Opening

Whole class. Review: letter t: name "tee", sound /t/, keyword top. Chant "t, t, /t/, top." Review: name game: the word we heard, the word we see; one word, left to right. Hold up the name cards one at a time; the class says the name and points left to right along the card.

#### 9 min. Foundations response

Whole class. Look at day 5's print and sound notes and pick the one slip more children made. The puppet shows it, you show the right way (3 minutes).

- Error H, print followed from right to left: the puppet slides right to left under "We take turns." Show the green dot and slide left to right.
- Error N, the name for the sound: the puppet says "tee" for /t/. Say the name, then the sound /t/ with the keyword top.
- Error O, a different letter for /t/: the puppet writes l. Hear /t/, pick the t card from t and l, then write.
- Error P, only T when lowercase is asked: show the lowercase path (down, then a cross at the middle line), and each child writes it once in the air.

Then a fresh retry (5 minutes) on a new line of handwriting paper: say "/t/", children hold up the t card from the pair (t and l), then print lowercase t once, saying /t/. Walk the room and look for: starts at the top, cross goes left to right, says /t/ and not "tee". One minute to put pencils down.

#### 5 min. Mapped words

Whole class. Review: mapped words I and my (read as whole words). Show the strip "I take turns." Point under each word, the class says it, then children point to I. Show the strip "My turn." and do the same for my. They read these two words by sight; do not ask them to sound out turn or take. The other words are read to them.

#### 3 min. Closing

**Ask.** Where is the start of the line? Children point to the green dot on the chart, then slide a finger left to right in the air.

#### 40 min. Rotations

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five 8-minute turns. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets a minute (6 minutes, 2 for the move): say the name and the sound of t, pick the t card from the pair, say whether "hop" rhymes with "top" and whether "cup" does, and clap the beats of a toy name (doll, wagon, dinosaur: 1, 2, 3). Table notes go on the record sheet for today's six only. Stations (six children each, no teacher): finger t in the sand tray, say /t/ each time; draw and label (draw a toy from our room, a partner says what it is); name-card match (match name cards to the chart letters by pointing); big-book browse (turn pages, finger under the words).

### Other rooms

One child: reread the story, ask the same talk question, write two sentences together, the child draws one picture and says it to you. One projector: show the chart, strips and exit card on one screen; children circle on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No story booklet: tell the story from memory. No chart paper: use any big sheet.

**Skip.** The repair, the rotations and the stations.

- **5 min. Hear the story.** Read or tell it.
  **Say.** "What do friends do to play together?" Expect: they take turns.
- **5 min. A message.** Write your child's idea in big letters, finger under each word.
  **Say.** "Tell me one fact about Noor and Teo." Expect: Teo waits.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a friend passing or waiting and tells you one sentence. Write it under the picture.
- **5 min. I and my.** Write "I take turns." and "My turn." Your child points to I and to my.
- **5 min. Print t.** Say /t/ and print t once, saying /t/.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles a picture and says one thing the message could say.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: take three ideas, write two sentences, and keep the model, the dictation and the exit. Longer: a child who is secure adds a second fact to the dictated sentence.

### Words and supports

Words: message, take turns, pass, wait. Frames: "We take turns." "One ___, one ___." A home language, signs and pointing count as answers; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the child drinking alone (error A) or gave a name or a feeling with no fact (error M) in day 6's exit, day 7 opens with a four-minute repair of the more common one. Day 7 has each child draw a plan for playing in our room and say what the drawing tells a reader.
