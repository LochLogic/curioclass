# The letter a card (day 7)

One large card and one small copy for each pair. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading.

## The large card

**Picture:** a whole apple with one leaf and a short stem, large enough to fill most of the card. **Letters:** A and a, printed large, side by side. **Say:** the keyword is apple, the sound is /a/ as at the start of apple, and the name is the letter a.

## The mouth shape

**Picture:** a face in side view with the mouth open and the jaw dropped for /a/. **Say:** "Open wide and say /a/."

## How the card is used

Say the keyword, say the sound, point to the letter. Then add the card to the sound-spelling map. Never ask a child to find a word by looking at the apple.
