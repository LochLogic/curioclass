# The letter h and letter p cards (day 14)

Two large cards and one small copy for each pair. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading.

## The h card

**Picture:** a hat on a peg, large enough to fill most of the card. **Letters:** H and h, printed large, side by side. **Say:** the keyword is hat, the sound is /h/ as at the start of hat, a puff of breath. **Mouth shape:** a face in side view with the mouth a little open, breath coming out.

## The p card

**Picture:** a green parrot on a branch, large enough to fill most of the card. **Letters:** P and p, printed large, side by side. **Say:** the keyword is parrot, the sound is /p/ as at the start of parrot, a pop of breath. **Mouth shape:** a face in side view with the lips closed, then popping.

## How the cards are used

Say the keyword, say the sound, point to the letter. Add both cards to the sound-spelling map. The two letters look alike to a young reader: hold the cards side by side and say each name. The pictures are reminders of the sounds; never ask a child to guess a word from them.
