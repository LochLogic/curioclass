# Day 11: Sort toys by how they feel, and say the rule

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the student page lines aloud; children do not read them. Put six small toys on each table, three soft and three hard, and keep two rule cards at hand per group. Set the red bear, the red block, the blue ball and the blue brick on the rug. Make a strip of three boxes for each child. Today's six are the eleventh six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. If many circled the swapped drums or said "black" for the big drum (error V) in day 10's exit, hold the real bead and ball beside the words of the text and match each (3 minutes). Write each word large on a word card as you teach four words with real things: texture (rub a hand on cloth), soft (the bear), hard (the block), smooth (a bead).

#### 10 min. Reread Attributes of Toys, pages 5 to 9

Whole class. Review: Attributes of Toys: you heard pages 1 to 4 on day 10. Read pages 5 to 9 aloud once (about 1 minute), holding up each picture. Say the words feel for each toy on page 7 as you point.

**Ask.** What does texture mean here? Expect: how something feels. Reread the start of page 7: "Texture is how something feels."

**Ask.** Which words let us sort by texture alone? Expect: smooth, rough, soft, hard, squishy, bumpy; not red or round. Children point under the words as you reread page 7.

#### 8 min. Model a sort

Whole class on the rug. Show the four toys: a red bear, a red block, a blue ball, a blue brick. Put the rule card for soft above an empty space. Say: "We sort by touch. The rule is soft." Feel each toy and place the soft ones, the bear and the ball, under the card. Say: "These are all soft." Put the hard ones, the block and the brick, in a second group. Then show the other rule card, red, and say: "Color is a different rule." The bear and the block are both red, but one is soft and one is hard. Say: "If the rule is soft, color does not decide where a toy goes" (error T, sorting by color when softness was asked). Put the rule card above each group before every sort. The class says "These are all soft."

#### 10 min. Bridge: sort and match

Table groups of five, six tables. This bridge goes to math: later in the year children count and compare groups, and today they only match. Each group sorts its six small toys by touch into a soft group and a hard group, with the two rule cards above (4 minutes). Then they match one soft toy to one hard toy, one for one, saying "one for each", and see whether any toy is left over without counting (4 minutes). Walk the room for 2 minutes: ask each group "What is your rule?" Expect "soft" or "hard". A group that sorted by color hears the rule card read again.

#### 14 min. Explain the groups

Whole class, then alone. Each table says its rule in a sentence, "These are all ___.", about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Then each child draws the two groups on the student page and copies the rule word from the card, or you write it for a child who asks (10 minutes). While they draw, the day's six come to the table in turn, 90 seconds each, to say which toys they put in each group and why, with "These are all ___.". Judge each with three look-fors: (1) the rule is a touch word; (2) the toys in the group fit it; (3) the drawing and the sentence agree. Shown: "These are all soft." with a bear and a ball. Shown with help: the rule said after a prompt. Not yet seen: a color named as the rule (error T).

#### 9 min. Talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass.

**Ask.** Which toy does not fit, and why? Each child takes one hard toy and sets it beside the soft group; the pair says why it does not fit. Expect: "It is hard. The rule is soft." Pairs talk for 3 minutes. Hear three pairs, a minute each, pointing to the rule card and the toy (3 minutes). The last 3 minutes put the toys away: one soft, one hard, one for each.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the day 11 card (three things to sort). Read aloud once: "The page says the pillow and the sock are soft. The shell is hard. The shell goes in another group." Children circle the picture of the groups that follows the page ([[s01-X11]], part a: 2 minutes). Hear today's six, 30 seconds each: "What is the rule for the group with the pillow and the sock?" Expect "soft". Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

Signs: the pillow and shell together with the sock apart, or "They are both red" (error T, a sort by something other than the page's rule).

#### 3 min. Opening

Whole class. Chant "a, a, /a/, apple." and "t, t, /t/, top." (Review: letter a: name "ay", sound /a/, keyword apple. Review: letter t: name "tee", sound /t/, keyword top.) Point to I and my on the class message and the class says each (Review: mapped words I and my, read as whole words).

#### 10 min. Writing for a sound

Whole class at the desk. Each child has the strip of three boxes and the a and t card pair. Say a sound: "/a/." Each child holds up the letter card for that sound, then prints that lowercase letter in the first box. Do six sounds over the strip, two strips (/t/, /a/, /a/, then /t/, /t/, /a/), 1 minute for each. Look for: the child chooses the letter first, then writes. A child who writes t for /a/ (error O) hears the sound again, chooses the card, then writes. A child who prints only a capital when lowercase is asked (error P) writes the lowercase path once more.

#### 4 min. Map

Whole class. Review: sounds both ways: say a sound and find the letter, see the letter and say the sound (day 9). Point to the a card on the map and say its keyword and sound; then say /t/ and a child points to the card. The class says the keyword each time.

#### 3 min. Closing

Whole class. Chant "a, t, /a/, /t/."

#### 40 min. Rotations

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five 8-minute turns. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets a minute (6 minutes, 2 for the move): hear a sound and hold up the letter card (sound to letter), see a card and say its sound (letter to sound), and print the letter on a slate or paper. Table notes for the day's six only go on the record sheet. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger a and t in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: read pages 5 to 9, ask the two questions, sort five objects from around the room by touch (a spoon, a cloth, a block) with a rule card, and do the exit card. Say a sound and the child chooses and prints the letter. One projector: show the pictures, the rule cards and the map on one screen; children sort on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No large book: tell pages 5 to 9 aloud. No small toys: a sock, a cloth, a block and a spoon. No sand tray: write with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The reopen, the table groups, the rotations and the stations.

- **5 min. The words.** Feel a soft thing and a hard thing. Name them: soft, hard, smooth.
  **Say.** "How does it feel?" Expect: soft, or hard.
- **5 min. Sort.** Put a rule card, soft, above one group. Sort four things by touch.
  **Say.** "What is the rule?" Expect: These are all soft.
- **5 min. One for each.** Match one soft thing to one hard thing, saying "one for each".
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the two groups.
- **5 min. A sound.** Say /a/ or /t/. Your child chooses the letter and prints it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X11]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles the groups, then says the rule for the group with the pillow and the sock.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the match and the second set of sounds; keep the model, the explain part and the exit. Longer: a child who is secure sorts the same toys by a second rule, such as hard or smooth, and says it.

### Words and supports

Words: texture, soft, hard, smooth, rule, group. Frames: "These are all ___." "The rule is ___." "It does not fit because ___." A home language, signs and pointing count as answers; say the English back once. A child who cannot touch a toy points to a picture.

### Tomorrow

If many sorted by color or named a color as the rule (error T) in day 11's exit, day 12's first 4 minutes keep the rule card above each group and sort again. Day 12 hears opposite action words, the class poem Toy Clues, and the words smooth, rough, soft and hard on real objects.
