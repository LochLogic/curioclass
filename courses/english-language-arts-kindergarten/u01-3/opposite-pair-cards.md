# Opposite pair cards (day 12)

Four picture pairs for pairs of children to match and say. Each pair is two cards of the same object, side by side, so a child sees only the one idea that changes. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. The teacher says the words aloud: opposite means far apart.

## Pair 1: up and down

**Picture:** a seesaw with the left end high and the right end low, then the same seesaw reversed.

## Pair 2: open and close

**Picture:** a book lying open on a table, then the same book closed.

## Pair 3: full and empty

**Picture:** a basket piled with balls, then the same basket with nothing in it.

## Pair 4: in the air and on the ground

**Picture:** a kite high in the air, then the same kite lying on the grass.

## How the cards are used

Hold one card up and ask the class to act the idea. Hold the second and ask what is different. Children say the pair together: "up, down". Mix the eight cards and let pairs find each match. Never ask a child to give a stronger word for an opposite; an opposite is the far side of one idea.
