# Day 12: Opposite words, the poem Toy Clues, and words for real objects

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the student page lines aloud; children do not read them. Print Toy Clues large with a green dot at each line start. Put the tray (sandpaper, smooth paper, soft cloth) at the front, covered. Write four word cards large (smooth, rough, soft, hard). Today is the cycle 1 check in the rotations: have the record sheet ready for notes on the day's six, the twelfth six names.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen and words

Whole class. If many sorted by color or named a color as the rule (error T) in day 11's exit, keep the rule card above each group and sort the four toys again (3 minutes). Then teach two words by acting: same (two hands doing the same thing) and opposite (two hands going far apart).

#### 10 min. Model opposites

Whole class. Say: "Opposites are two words that mean far apart. They are two ends of one idea." Act open and close with a book, then up and down with your arm. Show the first pair card, the seesaw with one end high and then low; the class acts it and says "up, down". Show the open book and closed book card: "open, close". Show the lamp: "on, off". Then act soft with a cloth and hard with a block. The class acts each and says each pair. Say: "Tiny and gigantic are far apart too" (Review: tiny and gigantic, which you acted on day 10). This is a short model: opposites have had no earlier teaching day, so the class learns the idea here and uses it in the next parts. If a child gives a stronger word, such as "huge" for "big" (error U), act big beside huge to show they are close, then act tiny beside gigantic to show far apart. If a child shows "on" twice for on and off (error AE), act open, then close, and ask which one reverses it.

#### 8 min. Shared reading, p03

Whole class. Review: poem: a text made to say together, read once, then with the class joining the repeated line (the poems Our Play and Ask and Listen). Read Toy Clues in large print with the pointer under each line, left to right, top to bottom, from the green dot. The picture pairs show soft and hard, smooth and rough, tiny and gigantic. Read it twice more with the class joining, 2 minutes each, and once with the class reading the pairs alone: "soft, hard; smooth, rough; tiny, gigantic." Children do not read the words; they say the poem as you point.

#### 10 min. Word and object

Pairs. Each pair holds two word cards that are opposites (smooth and rough, or soft and hard); you read each card aloud as you hand it out. Say: "Find one thing in the room for each word and bring it back. The frame is 'The ___ is rough.'" Pairs look for 5 minutes, paths clear, stopping for crowding. Pairs then tell each other their two sentences with the turn card passed (2 minutes). Hear three pairs, a minute each (3 minutes), and ask the class "Is it?" and each child touches the object to check. A pair that picks an object that does not fit hears the word again as you feel the object with them.

#### 12 min. Reread Attributes of Toys

Whole class. Review: Attributes of Toys: you heard it on days 10 and 11. Reread pages 3, 4 and 7 aloud, holding up each picture (about 2 minutes).

**Ask.** Which words in the book are opposites? Expect: tiny and gigantic; soft and hard; smooth and rough. Children point to the pictures; reread page 7 so they hear the pairs. Pairs talk for 2 minutes, then hear three pairs.

**Ask.** Which real toy fits a word you heard? Expect: a toy from the room, with "The ___ is ___." Pairs say it to each other (2 minutes). Hear three pairs, a minute each, the child pointing to the toy.

#### 11 min. Draw

Alone. Each child folds a paper in half, and draws one opposite pair, one picture on each side (open and closed book, a full and an empty basket, or the tiny and gigantic toys). While they draw (8 minutes) the day's six come to the table one at a time, a minute each, and you write the label a child asks for, or a child writes the first letter heard if it is a or t. Then each child tells a partner the two words (3 minutes).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the day 12 card (the light). Read aloud once: "The light is on. Now the light is off." Children circle the pair that shows opposites ([[s01-X12]], part a: 2 minutes). Hear today's six, 30 seconds each, with the tray of sandpaper, smooth paper and a soft cloth: "Show me the rough one." Expect the child touches or points to the sandpaper. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

Signs: the pair on and on circled (error AE, the same action where the opposite is asked); the smooth paper chosen for rough (error V, a describing word for an object it does not fit).

#### 3 min. Opening

Whole class. Chant for a and t: "a, a, /a/, apple." "t, t, /t/, top." (Review: letter a: name "ay", sound /a/, keyword apple. Review: letter t: name "tee", sound /t/, keyword top.)

#### 10 min. Whole group

Whole class at the desk. First, dictate sounds: /a/, /t/, /t/, /a/, /a/, /t/. Each child chooses the letter card first, then prints the letter on handwriting paper (4 minutes). Then a poem letter search in Toy Clues: children come up one at a time to find an a or a t, lowercase or capital, in the shared print, and the class says name and sound (5 minutes). The search is by shape, not by the picture. Last minute: Review: sounds both ways: say the sound and find the letter, see the letter and say the sound.

#### 4 min. Mapped words

Whole class. Review: mapped words I and my, read as whole words. Write "I see my top." on chart paper and point under each word, left to right. The class reads I and my as whole words; the other words are read to them and not sounded out. Children point to I and to my, one at a time.

#### 3 min. Closing

Whole class. Chant for a and t.

#### 40 min. Rotations (cycle 1 check)

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five 8-minute turns. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets one minute (6 minutes, 2 for the move). Say the name and sound of a and of t, print each in lowercase and capital, and write the letter for a sound you say. Take notes only: shown, shown with help or not yet seen for each of the four, and nothing is reteached today; the notes choose day 13's foundations response. A child may pass. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger a and t in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: act the pairs with the pair cards, read Toy Clues pointing under each line, find a rough and a smooth thing, and do the exit card. Check each letter a and t at the end. One projector: show the poem and the pair cards on one screen; children point to the matches on the screen.

### At home

**Swap.** No large poem: copy it big by hand. No pair cards: act open and close, up and down. No tray: sandpaper and a smooth sheet.

**Skip.** The word and object search, the check at the table, the rotations and the stations.

- **5 min. Act.** Act open and close, up and down, soft and hard. Say: "Opposites are far apart."
- **5 min. The poem.** Read Toy Clues together, finger under each line.
  **Say.** "Which two words go together in the first lines?" Expect: soft and hard.
- **5 min. Word and thing.** Find one rough thing and one smooth thing; say "The ___ is rough."
- **5 min. Draw.** Fold a paper; draw one pair of opposites.
- **5 min. Letters.** Say /a/ or /t/; your child picks the letter and prints it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X12]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles the opposites, then shows you the rough one.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second shared reading and the draw; keep the model, one word and object search, and the exit. Longer: a child who is secure draws a second pair and says both words.

### Words and supports

Words: opposite, same, smooth, rough, soft, hard, open, close, up, down. Frames: "The ___ is ___." "___ and ___ are opposites." A home language, signs and pointing count as answers; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many circled on and on (error AE), or pointed to the smooth paper for rough (error V), in day 12's exit, day 13's first 4 minutes act open beside close and choose the reversing action, or compare the object with the word. The cycle 1 notes for the day's six choose day 13's foundations response. Day 13 reads Toy Riddles and acts out walk, march and tiptoe.
