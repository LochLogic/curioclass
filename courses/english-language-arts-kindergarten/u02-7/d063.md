# Day 63: Check: who, where and what happened in the whole story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Turn the wall map to the wall. Print section 5 large. Set out the roster sheet, three station tables and a timer. Make nothing else.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Plowed: a road a plow has cleared; push with both arms. Untied: Review: un- means the opposite (day 53); untied is the string coming off. Preview only; nothing is marked.

#### 15 min. Hear section 5, Home (309 words)

Whole class. Pictures covered. Read the whole section straight through (about 3 minutes), then uncover and look. Talk: "How did Yusuf feel at the end? What did Baba say about waiting?" This talk is not scored.

#### 30 min. S1

Each child has one minute with you; the other 29 are at stations: draw a favorite part, a partner picture sort, the journal. Ask three things, with no map and no pictures: [[s02-S1a]] who carries the parcel home (section 5); [[s02-S1b]] where the story happens while the snow gets thick; [[s02-S1c]] one big thing that happens. Give no hint beyond repeating the ask. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, and the help used. Signs: the parcel as the character; a place the story does not give.

#### 10 min. Celebration and journal

Whole class. Hang the finished pictures; nothing is scored. Write today's weather line and read it. Children draw it. Collect the student pages and journals at the end of the day.

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Oral. Rhyme: day, say, play; children add one. Clap parcel (two), home (one), umbrella (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: q, u and x

Chant "q, quail, /kw/." "u, umbrella, /u/." "x, fox, /ks/." Say a sound; children write its letter.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

With counters: blend /s/ /u/ /n/; segment bud; change the first sound of nut to /c/. Never end in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words a and my

Review: a and my, read whole. Point under "A cat sat on my mat." Pairs read it back.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Reread verse 7, Rainbow, and the journal line with the pointer. Tap the spaces.

#### 15 min. Foundations: rotation

The next six unheard, 1 minute each (6 minutes): name q, u and x, give each sound, write one letter. Others: letter cards and handwriting.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Ask the three questions and mark. One projector: show section 5 pictures after reading.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw.

**Skip.** The stations.

- **15 min. Before the assessment.** Read section 5 aloud. **Say.** "Who carries the parcel home? Where are they while the snow gets thick?" Expect: Yusuf; the street and the bakery.
- **10 min. One big thing.** Your child tells one big event.
- **10 min. Print.** Your child prints q, u and x.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a favorite part.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep S1. Longer: secure children add what changes after the plow comes.

### Words and supports

Words: plowed, untied. Frames: "Yusuf carries ___." "It happens in the ___." Pointing and spoken answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 62's exit error A and S1b error H go to day 64, which reteaches the most missed of G, H, I, J and AV, with A for feelings if many, and records the Picnic Plan retell. S1b goes back first.
