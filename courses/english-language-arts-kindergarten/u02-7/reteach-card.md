# Reteach card for day 64

A new picture story for the targeted reteaching on day 64. It is never 03, never The Picnic Plan, and never an exit card.

## The story

"On the sand path, Ola and Mum carry a bucket. A wave wets the path. They climb the dune."

## The pictures

Four picture cards on index cards: Ola and Mum; the bucket; the sand path; a wave wets the path and they climb the dune. A who box, a where box and a what happens box for the group.

## How to use it

The teacher reads the card twice. One group at a time puts the cards in the boxes and says what happens in order with first, then, last. A second pass uses two more cards, one fresh, for the error the class missed most: G, H or I at first, then J or AV.

## Key (teacher)

Who: Ola and Mum, not the bucket (G). Where: the sand path, not a place the card does not give (H). What happens: a wave wets the path and they climb the dune, not the bucket alone (I). In order: first they carry, next the wave, last they climb (J). All three events told (AV).
