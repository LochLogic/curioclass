# Day 64: Respond: fix what the check showed, then retell a weather story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 63's assessment, with S1b first, and read the exit marks from days 59 to 62 and the S1 marks; count the groups that need a repair. Lay out the reteach card with its four pictures and three boxes. Draw your own fresh card with a row of faces. Turn the wall map back to the room. Set three station tables. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Targeted reteaching

Whole class, then groups, one group at a time if more than one error is many; the others work with the cards. Read the reteach card twice: "On the sand path, Ola and Mum carry a bucket. A wave wets the path. They climb the dune." Say: "This card is made up for practice." Then three repairs of about 5 minutes. Error A, from day 62's exit: Pip says Ola is pleased when the card says "Ola yawns, rubs her eyes and leans on Mum." Reread the exact sentence and match its detail to the face: sleepy. Error H, from the S1 setting ask: Pip says the story happens at the shop. Reread the place sentence, "On the sand path," and choose its picture. Then the most missed of G, H, I, J and AV from the exits and S1: put the pictures in the who, where and what happens boxes and tell them with first, next, last (G is the bucket as who; I is the bucket alone as what happens; J is the dune before the wave; AV leaves out the wave). Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 30 min. R1

Each child retells The Picnic Plan to you, 1 minute each; the other 29 are at stations: draw a weather picture, a partner picture sort, handwriting of q, u and x. No retell cards from selection a are out. Say: "Retell The Picnic Plan with important details in order." [[s02-R1]] Listen for the plan, the change and the ending, in order. Accept the pond or the park by the pond. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, and the help used. Hear first the children who did not need a repair; hear those who did last and mark them in a separate cell. Signs: a feeling alone, such as "Tomas was sad" (error AV); the events in the wrong order (error J). Finish marking after class if needed.

#### 15 min. Familiar reading

Whole class. Read The Picnic Plan again, whole (314 words, about 3 minutes), with the pictures. Talk for 12 minutes: **Ask.** "What did Grandma Rosa do when the rain came?" Expect: they went home and had the picnic on the floor. Partners tell one detail, then three children share. Never name a child as the example of a mistake.

#### 10 min. Foundations: response and fresh retry

Groups, by the cycle 9 record from days 59 to 63. Children marked shown with help on a letter, sound or keyword come to your table for the one missed, with a fresh retry (about 30 seconds each, at most ten children). The rest print one line each of q, u and x. No new pattern is taught.

#### 10 min. Foundations: letters q, u, x

Whole class. Hold up each letter card; children give its name, sound and keyword: q, quail, /kw/; u, umbrella, /u/; x, fox, /ks/. Say a sound; children point to its letter.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral. Rhyme: say snow, go and show; children give another. Clap quiet (two), parcel (two) and umbrella (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /b/ /u/ /s/ and children say bus. Segment gum into three. Change the first sound of bud to /m/. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class. Reread only taught text, with the pointer: verse 7, Rainbow, the journal strip and "My cat sat on a mat." (the word cards a and my). Tap the spaces. Pairs take turns pointing while the partner reads it back.

#### 10 min. Foundations: rotation

The next six unheard, 1 minute each (6 minutes): name q, u and x, give each sound, write one letter; mark the cycle 9 record. The others print q, u and x on handwriting paper.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the reteach card and retell The Picnic Plan aloud. One projector: show the pictures of The Picnic Plan one at a time; the boxes stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw four small pictures.

**Skip.** The stations.

- **10 min. Retell.** Read a short story your child knows.
  **Say.** "What happened first, next, last?" Expect: the start, the change and the ending, in order.
- **10 min. Feelings.** Act out sleepy, pleased and worried for your child to name and say what told them.
- **10 min. Print.** Your child prints q, u and x.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child retells a familiar weather story, with the start, the change and the ending.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reteaching, R1 and the retry; cut the shared print. Longer: a secure child retells with because and a feeling word.

### Words and supports

Words: sequence, event, first, next, last. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "The rain came, so ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 65 studies a weather-story model and plans a character, setting and event together. Its first 5 minutes repair the most missed part of today's R1 retell, one only. Today's R1 marks, including the repair cell, go on the record sheet as the unit's retell record.
