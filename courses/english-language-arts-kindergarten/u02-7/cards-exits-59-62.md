# Exit cards, days 59 to 62

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching. The wall map stays up on days 59 to 62 and is not part of these cards.

## Day 59

**Story card:** "In the yard at dusk, Lia and Uncle hang a blue rug. A hen pulls it down. Lia picks it up." Three boxes: who, where, what happened. Six picture cards: Lia and Uncle; the rug; the yard at dusk; the school; the hen pulls it down and Lia picks it up; the rug alone. **Ask:** "Put each card in its box." Six children are asked: "Which words told you?"

## Day 60

**Story card:** "In the shed, Max stacks cups on a crate. A dog jumps onto the crate and knocks the cups down." **Item a:** three pictures, the inside of a shed, a park and a barn. "Which picture shows where Max is now?" **Item b:** three pictures, the dog knocks the cups down, the stacked cups alone and the dog alone. "Which picture shows the big change?"

## Day 61

**Story card:** "First Bea finds a torn page. Next she tapes the tear. Last she returns the book to its shelf." Three pictures, mixed: the torn page; the tape on the tear; the book on its shelf. **Ask:** "Put the pictures in order and tell the story with first, next, last."

## Day 62

**Story card:** "At the desk, Cam finishes a puzzle and grins. 'I found the missing piece!' Cam says." **Item a:** four faces, pleased, sad, sleepy, cross. "Which face shows how Cam feels?" **Item b**, six children: "Tell me what Cam did or said that shows it."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 59: Lia and Uncle in who; the yard at dusk in where; the hen pulls it down and Lia picks it up in what happened. Not yet: the rug as who (G); the school as where (H); the rug alone as the event (I).
- Day 60: a, the shed; not yet: the park or the barn (H). b, the dog knocks the cups down; not yet: the stacked cups alone or the dog alone (I).
- Day 61: torn page, tape, book back, told with first, next, last; not yet: tape before the tear (J); only the taping (AV).
- Day 62: a, the pleased face; not yet: sad, sleepy or cross (A), repaired on day 64, not day 63. b, "Cam grins." or "I found the missing piece!"; not yet: only the feeling word with nothing from the card (F).
