# Exit cards, days 59 to 62

Every exit on days 59 to 62 uses a page of the unit's own read-alouds, read aloud again; nothing here is made up, so the teacher only makes the pictures. Day 59 uses The Picnic Plan (texts/gk-u2-02.md, selection a); days 60 to 62 use Priya at the Window (texts/gk-u2-02.md, selection b). Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read; you read the page aloud. None of these pictures is shown in teaching. The wall map stays up on days 59 to 62 and is not part of these cards.

## Day 59

**Story page:** page 2 of The Picnic Plan, read aloud again with its picture. Three boxes: who, where, what happened. Six picture cards: Tomas and Grandma Rosa; the wagon; the path to the park by the pond; the school; they walk to the park and the sky begins to change; the jug alone. **Ask:** "Put each card in its box." Six children are asked: "Which words told you?"

## Day 60

**Story page:** page 4 of Priya at the Window, read aloud again with its picture. **Item a:** three pictures, Priya at the kitchen window, a park by a pond and a bakery. "Which picture shows where Priya is now?" **Item b:** three pictures, the sun shining on the wet leaves after the rain stops, Dev alone and the notebook alone. "Which picture shows the big change?"

## Day 61

**Story page:** page 2 of Priya at the Window, read aloud again with its picture. Three pictures, mixed: Priya looks at the drops on the glass; Priya listens to the tapping on the roof; Priya writes in her notebook and draws the drops. **Ask:** "Put the pictures in order and tell the story with first, next, last."

## Day 62

**Story page:** page 5 of Priya at the Window, read aloud again with its picture. **Item a:** four faces, pleased, sad, sleepy, cross. "Which face shows how Priya feels at the end?" **Item b**, six children: "Tell me what Priya did or said that shows it."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 59: Tomas and Grandma Rosa in who; the path to the park in where; the walk as the sky changes in what happened. Not yet: the wagon as who (G); the school as where (H); the jug alone as the event (I).
- Day 60: a, the kitchen window; not yet: the park or the bakery (H). b, the sun on the wet leaves; not yet: Dev alone or the notebook alone (I).
- Day 61: looks, listens, writes and draws, told with first, next, last; not yet: the writing before the looking (J); only the writing (AV).
- Day 62: a, the pleased face; not yet: sad, sleepy or cross (A), repaired on day 64, not day 63. b, "Priya grins." or "Can we go out now?"; not yet: only the feeling word with nothing from the page (F).
