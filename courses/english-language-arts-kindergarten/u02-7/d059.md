# Day 59: A long story begins: who, where and what happens

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang chart paper with three boxes headed who, where and what happens, and draw the cards for the wall map: Yusuf and Baba; the street by the post office; the parcel leaving the post office; the first flakes; the wind at the corner. Print section 1 large and cover its two pictures. Make the day's six: the next six unheard. Keep the exit card to yourself. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. If many children missed day 58's exit, repair the most common of errors D, E and AD; the other two go to small groups in the last foundations part. Error D: Pip says "Frost is ice on a cold window" and draws a hat. **Ask.** "Does the drawing show the fact?" Expect: no. Draw ice on a window. Error E: Pip whispers the same fact. **Ask.** "Can your partner hear?" Expect: no; say it toward the partner. Error AD: Pip says "Mina refolds the blanket" and points to the picture of the first fold. Review: re- means again (day 53). **Ask.** "Which picture shows refolds?" Expect: the second fold. Each child then redoes one spoken detail with a partner. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class, with a real wrapped box and a picture of a wrapped parcel. A parcel is a package that is wrapped and sent; hold up the box. A path is a way to walk; trace one on the floor. Review: an event is what happens in a story, not an object (day 50). Sequence is the order things happen; lay three cards and say first, next, last (review: day 51). **Ask.** Pip asks the word question: "What does parcel mean?" Expect: a wrapped package. Pip then asks "Can we have snow today?" (error AB); the class says that does not ask about the word, and Pip asks again. Wrapped: Review: -ed tells it already happened, as in packed (day 53). Children wrap a box in the air and say "I wrapped it."

#### 15 min. First hearing of section 1, The Parcel (212 words)

Whole class. Keep the pictures covered, so no one guesses the story from them. Read the whole section straight through, page 1 then page 2 (about 2 minutes), unabridged. Uncover page 1 and read it again with its picture (about 3 minutes); pause once after "something soft was inside." **Ask.** "Who is holding the parcel?" Expect: Yusuf. Uncover page 2 and read it again; pause once after Yusuf catches a snowflake. **Ask.** "What is the sky doing?" Expect: it is gray and flakes are falling. For the last 7 minutes, children point to one thing in each picture and tell a partner. A child may pass, point or only look. Ask nothing about storms at home or what anyone wears.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Answer the first question yourself to show how, then fade the starters. **Ask.** "Who carries the parcel? What weather do you hear? Where are they?" Expect: Yusuf, with Baba beside him; cold air, a gray sky, flakes, a push of wind; on the street between the post office and home. Pairs turn and talk, one minute for each question; then six children answer, about 40 seconds each. Starters: "Yusuf ___." "I hear ___." "They are ___." Error G is "the parcel" for who: say "The parcel is carried. Who carries it?"

#### 15 min. Model and map

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the story-map has three boxes, who, where and what happens; a character is a person who acts (day 49). Place each card on the wall map and say why. Who: Yusuf and Baba. Where: the street by the post office, late afternoon, cold; where is the place the story is in now, not Aunt Salma's far away house (error H). What happens: three linked events, placed with first, next, last: they leave the post office with the parcel, the first flakes fall, the wind swirls the flakes at the corner. An event is a change that moves the story on. Pip puts the parcel card in what happens; **Ask.** "Is that a change?" Expect: no, an object is not an event (error I). Pairs then draw their own map on side 1 of the student page: who, where, one event (9 minutes). Talk with six pairs, about 40 seconds each: "Who? Where? What changes?"

#### 5 min. Class weather journal

Whole class. Say today's weather from the closed window or a printed scene. Write one line on the journal strip, such as "The sun is up. The air is quiet." Change it to match the window, keeping an up or a quiet word if you can. Children draw the weather in their own journal.

#### 5 min. Exit E59

Read page 2 of The Picnic Plan aloud again with its picture (40 seconds); ask [[s02-X59v2]]. Children circle one picture in each row of the exit sheet (1 minute 30 seconds). Then ask the day's six, about 25 seconds each: "Which words told you?" Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the wagon as who (error G); the school as where (error H); the jug alone as what happened (error I).

#### 10 min. Foundations: return and repair

Groups, by the cycle 8 record from day 58. Response and a fresh retry, counted only here. The group that gave a sound for a name (error U) sorts name cards from sound cards for k and y with you. The group with the wrong sound for y (error V) says yak and yellow, listens to the first sound and says /y/. The group that wrote the wrong letter (error W) hears /k/ or /y/, writes the letter on a card and checks it on the chart. The rest chant "k, kangaroo, /k/" and "y, yak, /y/" with a partner. Then each child in the three groups names k and y, gives each sound and writes the letter for one (about 30 seconds each; no group larger than ten, 5 minutes).

#### 10 min. Foundations: q, u and x

Whole class (cycle 9, lesson 46). Show the quail picture; say quail and the first sound /kw/ with round lips; children say it. Name the letter q and skywrite it while saying quail. Do the same with u and umbrella (/u/), and x and fox (/ks/ at the end). Vowels are letters like a, i and u; every word has at least one. Tell a very short story of your own: an umbrella opens, goes up in the wind and is brought safely under a roof; children act it. Chant each: "q, quail, /kw/." "u, umbrella, /u/." "x, fox, /ks/." Print each letter, little and big, on handwriting paper: say each sound as it is written.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, oral only. Rhyme first: say sun, bun and run; children give another (fun). Clap the beats of umbrella (three), quiet (two) and sun (one). Review: you did the counter work on days 42 to 51. With a counter for each sound, isolate the first, middle and last sounds of mug; blend /s/ /u/ /n/ and children say sun; segment bus into three; add /b/ to the front of us to make bus; change the last sound of mud to /g/ to make mug. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words a and my

Whole class. Review: you learned a (day 21) and my (day 5); read them whole. Map each under counters. In a the letter says /uh/, the part to remember. In my the m says /m/ and the y says /ī/, the part to remember; the y in yak says /y/. Write a line and point under each word: "My cat sat on a mat." Children read it; cat, sat and mat are blended from taught letters; my, on and a are read whole.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. Reread the journal strip with the pointer, left to right, no speed target. Tap the spaces, count the words, and find the capital at the start and the period at the end (review: capital and period, day 56). Find u or q where they appear. Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner reads it back.

#### 10 min. Foundations: rotation

Teacher table: the day's six, 1 minute each (6 minutes). Each names q, u and x, gives the sound of each and writes the letter for one sound; mark the cycle 9 record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Then call at most six children with the other two day 58 errors, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). The others work in pairs with letter cards, sorting name cards from sound cards for q, u and x.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear the section, draw the three boxes, and do the exit with the six pictures. One projector: show the two pictures one at a time after the first reading; the map stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall map: draw three boxes on a sheet. No cards: draw small pictures. No wrapped box: use any box.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the foundations rotation.

- **10 min. The story.** Read section 1 of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud once, then again with its pictures.
  **Say.** "Who carries the parcel? What is the sky doing?" Expect: Yusuf carries it; the sky is gray and flakes fall.
- **10 min. The map.** Your child draws three boxes, who, where and what happens, and a picture in each.
  **Say.** "What happens first, next, last?" Expect: they leave the post office, the first flakes fall, the wind swirls them.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints q, u and x.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X59v2]] Read page 2 of The Picnic Plan aloud. Your child sorts six drawn cards into who, where and what happened.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the map and the exit; cut the journal and the pair print reading. Longer: a secure child adds a second event to the map and says why it is a change.

### Words and supports

Words: parcel, path, event, sequence, wrapped. Frames: "Yusuf is ___." "They are ___." "First ___, next ___, last ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 60 reads section 2 and adds the place now and the change to the map. Its first 5 minutes repair the most common of errors G, H and I from today's exit, one only; the rest go to the rotation. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet.
