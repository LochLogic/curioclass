# Day 62: The quiet: how Yusuf feels, and the words that show it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the feeling chart with its five faces and lay out the action cards. Add the wind stops and the plow comes to the wall map. Print section 4 large and cover its pictures. Mark Rain and Snow page 4. Make the day's six: the next six unheard. Keep the exit card to yourself. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. If many children missed day 61's exit, repair the more common of errors J and AV; the other goes to the rotation. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Lay three mixed pictures for "First Kit packs the sled. Next Kit pulls it up the hill. Last Kit rides it down." Error J: Pip puts the ride first. **Ask.** "Did the ride come first?" Expect: no; first is the packing. Error AV: Pip tells only the ride. **Ask.** "What did Pip leave out?" Expect: the packing and the pulling. Children say the three events with first, next, last. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Worried: a tight feeling that something may go wrong; hold your hands tight. Relieved: a breathing-out feeling when the worry goes; breathe out slowly and let your shoulders drop. The story shows it without the word. Plow: a big truck that pushes snow out of the road; push with both arms. Fell: Review: snow falls, and it fell (days 41 to 43). A second sense is new: "his face fell" means he looked sad, and the words show it. Say each sense with its motion.

#### 15 min. Hear section 4, The Quiet (264 words)

Whole class. Look at the wall map and say the story so far (1 minute). Keep the pictures covered and read the whole section straight through (about 2 minutes), unabridged. Then uncover page 1 and read it again with its picture; reread the hard turn on purpose: "The wind has stopped. That is good. But the snow is deep." Give about 12 minutes to the two pictures in all: each child tells a partner one thing, three tell the class. A child may pass, point or only look.

#### 10 min. Questions

Whole class, then pairs. Reread Rain and Snow page 4 aloud: "Wind can push snowflakes sideways. Then the air looks full of white. It is hard to see far." Review: the word question (u02-1): a child asks about a word. **Ask.** "What does sideways mean?" Expect: to the side, not down. Error AB is a question about something else, such as "Can we go outside?". **Ask.** "Which fact about wind and snow explains what Baba does?" Expect: the wind hid the street, so they waited; the snow is still deep and the plow has not come. **Ask.** "Which words suggest a feeling?" Expect: his face fell; a hot, wriggling feeling; he leaned against Baba's arm. Pairs ask each other one (4 minutes); six children answer, about 40 seconds each.

#### 10 min. Infer with evidence

Whole class, then pairs. Review: a feeling shown by words (u01-5, day 20). A feeling can be shown by what a person does or says, and the word may not be in the story. Place each action card under a face on the chart and say what told you: "his face fell" under sad; "a hot, wriggling feeling" under worried; "leaned against Baba's arm, his chest less tight" beside relieved. Word questions: "wriggling" means moving this way right now (-ing, new: it tells what is happening now); "fell" the second sense. Pairs then take two action cards each, place them and tell the words that told them (6 minutes). Hear six pairs, 40 seconds each. Error A is a face the words do not support, such as pleased for his face fell. Pip puts one card under cross; **Ask.** "Which words show cross?" Expect: none are in the story.

#### 5 min. Describe and draw

Whole class, then pairs. Look at the plow picture on page 2. **Ask.** "What is one thing you see?" Expect: a bright orange light, a wide blade, snow pushed to the side. Each child says one detail loudly enough for a partner (review: describe and be clear, days 36 and 52), then adds that detail to the plow on side 1 of the student page.

#### 5 min. Class weather journal

Whole class. Review: the class weather journal (day 41). Say today's weather from the window or a printed scene. Write one line and read it. Children draw it in their journal.

#### 5 min. Exit E62

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its picture (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X62a]]; children mark one of four faces on the exit sheet (30 seconds). Then ask [[s02-X62b]] to the day's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the sad face for a card that gives no sad words (error A); "He is happy" with nothing from the card (error F).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 9, lesson 48). Rhyme: say plow, cow and now; children give another (how). Clap plow (one), shelter (two) and umbrella (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: q, u and x

Whole class. Chant "q, quail, /kw/." "u, umbrella, /u/." "x, fox, /ks/." Say a sound; children write its letter on handwriting paper, little and big, and say the sound as it is written. Print one line each of q, u and x with no model.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /n/ /u/ /t/ and children say nut. Segment tub into three. Isolate the first sound of cub and the last sound of sun. Change the first sound of bud to /m/ to make mud. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words a and my

Whole class. Review: a (day 21) and my (day 5), read whole. Write "A cat sat on my mat." and point under each word; children read it. Map the part to remember in a and in my. Then reread "My cat sat on a mat." Partners read each line to each other.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. Reread verse 7, Rainbow, with the pointer, then the journal strip. Slide the movable windows over one word at a time. Tap the spaces; count the words in line 1. Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner reads it back. Find q and u.

#### 15 min. Foundations: rotation

The day's six, 1 minute each (6 minutes): name q, u and x, give each sound, write the letter for one sound; mark the cycle 9 record. Then up to six children from the cycle 9 record, 90 seconds each (9 minutes). The others work in pairs with letter cards and handwriting paper.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear section 4, place two action cards under faces and say what told you. One projector: show the two pictures one at a time after the reading; the chart stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: draw four faces on a sheet. No cards: act out the feeling.

**Skip.** The pair questions and the rotation.

- **10 min. The story.** Read section 4 of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud, then again with its pictures.
  **Say.** "How does Yusuf feel, and which words or actions tell you?" Expect: worried; his face fell, a hot wriggling feeling; then calmer when he leans on Baba.
- **5 min. The plow.** Your child tells one detail of the plow and draws it.
- **5 min. Feelings.** Act out worried, relieved and sad for your child to name.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints q, u and x.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X62a]] [[s02-X62b]] Read twice: "At the desk, Cam finishes a puzzle and grins. 'I found the missing piece!' Cam says." Show four faces: pleased, sad, sleepy, cross. Your child points to the face, then tells what Cam did or said that shows it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the feeling chart and the exit; cut the drawing. Longer: a secure child finds a second feeling in section 4 and the words that show it.

### Words and supports

Words: worried, relieved, plow, fell, sideways. Frames: "Yusuf feels ___." "The words ___ show it." "I see ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 63 reads section 5 and is the story check: no reteaching, no new words. Errors on today's exit, A first, wait for day 64's reteaching. Today's marks go on the record sheet.
