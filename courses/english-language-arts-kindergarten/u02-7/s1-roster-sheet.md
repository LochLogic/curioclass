# S1 roster and prompt sheet

One page for the teacher on day 63. Down the left, the numbers 1 to 30 and a space for each child's name. Across, three columns, one for each of the three asks, and a fourth for the support used.

## The three asks

- One ask is about the section heard today (section 5, Home), chosen by the child's roster number: 1, 4, 7, and so on, character; 2, 5, 8, setting; 3, 6, 9, event. The other two asks are about the whole story.
- Character: "Who is the story about?" Setting: "Where were they while the snow was too thick to walk?" Event: "Tell me one big thing that happens."

## Marking

Each cell takes one of three marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A prompted answer ("Who carried it?") is shown with help. The map is turned to the wall; the teacher gives only the prompts above.

## Look-fors (teacher)

The child names the person or the place, or says what changed. Samples that meet: "A boy, Yusuf." "They waited inside the bakery." "The plow came and then they walked home." Written feedback follows after class.
