# Feeling chart

A chart for day 62: five feeling faces in a row (pleased, sad, sleepy, cross, worried), each a round plain face drawn by hand, and a space under each for word and action cards the class adds.

## The cards that match

Picture cards of actions, each from the story or from a made-up child, to be placed under a face:

- A child claps and jumps with open arms: pleased.
- A child sits with eyes down and a slow step: sad.
- A child rubs an eye and yawns: sleepy.
- A child folds arms and frowns: cross.
- A child looks back over a shoulder and holds a hand tight: worried.
- A child leans on a grown-up's arm and breathes out: relieved, which sits beside pleased.

## How to use it

The teacher reads a line about a person; children place the action card under the face it matches and say the word that told them. The words "his face fell" are placed beside the sad face with a note that this is a different sense of fell from the snow falling.

## Key (teacher)

A feeling is shown by what a person does and says. The word may not appear in the story at all. A face the words do not support is an unsupported answer (A).
