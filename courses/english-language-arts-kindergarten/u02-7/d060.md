# Day 60: The snow thickens: where they are now, and what changed

## Teacher page

### Before class

Add cards to the wall map: the bakery; the snow thickens; Baba will not walk in it; they go into the bakery; Baba calls Mama. Print section 2 large and cover its pictures. Set out a hat, a basket and a table. Make the day's six: the next six unheard. Keep the exit card to yourself. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. If many children missed day 59's exit, repair the most common of errors G, H and I, one only; the others go to the rotation. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hold a new hand-drawn card: "In the lane, Nina and Dad pull a wagon. The wheel sticks and Dad pushes it free." Error G: Pip puts the wagon in who. **Ask.** "Does the wagon act? Who acts?" Expect: Nina and Dad. Error H: Pip puts the market in where. **Ask.** "Which place does the card give?" Expect: the lane. Error I: Pip picks the wagon alone for what happens. Read the sentence and **Ask.** "Which picture matches it?" Expect: the stuck wheel and Dad pushing. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Shelter is a place that keeps you safe from weather; make a roof with your arms. To pause is to stop for a short time and go on; hold up a hand, count three, go on. Place words: Review: in and on (days 44 to 47). Put the hat in the basket, then on the table. By is new: near, at the side; put the hat by the basket. Children place the hat and say the word. Put out one hat, then two. Review: -s means more than one (day 39).

#### 15 min. Hear section 2, The Snow Thickens (279 words)

Whole class. Look at the wall map first. **Ask.** "Who is on the map? Where were they?" Expect: Yusuf and Baba; the street (1 minute). Keep the pictures covered and read the whole section straight through (about 2 minutes), unabridged. Then uncover page 1 and give 6 minutes to its picture: each child tells a partner one thing, three tell the class. Do the same for page 2. Yusuf's tight stomach is in the story: say "Yusuf is worried, and Baba stays close." A child may pass, point or only look.

#### 10 min. Questions

Whole class, then pairs, with the six question picture cards (review: who, what, where, when, day 43). **Ask.** "Where are they now?" Expect with the place words: in the bakery; by the window; the parcel on the little table. Then ask who (Mrs. Chen is with them), what (Baba says they will not walk in the snow) and when (when it is safe). Each pair holds one card and asks the other; swap (4 minutes). Six children then answer for the class, about 40 seconds each. Error Z is a time for a place, such as "at lunch"; error AA is the wrong place word, such as "on the window" for "by the window". Say the right place word and point.

#### 15 min. Map

Whole class, then pairs. Keep yesterday's cards up and add the bakery to where, with "now" over it. Place the first two event cards, the snow thickens and Baba will not walk in it, and say why each is a change that moves the story on. The snow alone is not the event; what they do about it is (error I). Aunt Salma's house is not where they are (error H). Each pair then gets the other two cards, they go into the bakery and Baba calls Mama, and sets them in order on its strip, saying "first" and "next." Pairs tell the order to the next pair (8 minutes). Check three pairs.

#### 5 min. Sentence expansion

Whole class, then pairs. Review: a sentence grows when you add where or when (day 55). Write "Yusuf sat." and read it. **Ask.** "Where?" Expect: by the window, in the bakery. Read "Yusuf sat by the window in the bakery." Each pair grows "Baba stood." with in, by or on and says the whole sentence to another pair.

#### 5 min. Exit E60

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its picture (30 seconds). Ask [[s02-X60a]]; children mark one of three pictures (30 seconds). Ask [[s02-X60b]]; they mark again (30 seconds). Then ask the day's six, about 30 seconds each: "Which words told you?" Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the park or the barn for where (error H); the stacked cups alone or the dog alone for the change (error I).

#### 10 min. Foundations: response and fresh retry

Groups. Cycle 9 evidence so far: the day 59 record. Children marked shown with help on a letter, sound or keyword come to your table for the one missed, with a fresh retry (about 30 seconds each, at most six children). The rest chant the three letters, then print one line each of q, u and x. No new pattern is taught.

#### 10 min. Foundations: q, u and x review

Whole class. Hold up each letter card; children give its name, sound and keyword: q, quail, /kw/; u, umbrella, /u/; x, fox, /ks/. Say a sound; children point to its letter. Print each letter in the air and on handwriting paper, saying the sound as it is written.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral. Rhyme: say snow, go and row; children give another (low). Clap bakery (three), window (two) and snow (one). Review: you split a word into its first sound and the rest, then blended it (days 24 to 27). Say s-un, m-ud and h-ut; children blend each.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Isolate the middle sound of hut and nut. Blend /g/ /u/ /m/; children say gum. Segment pup into three. Add /s/ to the front of it to make sit. Change the first sound of tub to /k/ to make cub. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class. Reread only text already taught, with the pointer: yesterday's journal strip, the verse Thunder and "My cat sat on a mat." Tap the spaces. Children point under each word as the class reads. Find q, u and x. No new text.

#### 10 min. Foundations: rotation

The day's six, the next six unheard, 1 minute each (6 minutes): name q, u and x, give each sound, write the letter for one sound; mark the cycle 9 record. Call back, for 4 minutes, the children from the response whose retry needs another look (at most six, 40 seconds each). The others work in pairs: handwriting paper for q, u and x, and sorting name cards from sound cards.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear section 2, place the hat in, on and by, and grow one sentence. One projector: show the two pictures one at a time after the reading; the map stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall map: use the sheet from yesterday. No hat or basket: use a toy and a bowl.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the rotation.

- **10 min. The story.** Read section 2 of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud.
  **Say.** "Where are Yusuf and Baba now, and what changed?" Expect: in the bakery, because the snow got too thick to walk in.
- **5 min. Place words.** Put a toy in a bowl, on a table and by a bowl; your child says each place word.
- **5 min. A longer sentence.** Say "Yusuf sat." Ask where, and say it again with the place.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints q, u and x.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X60a]] [[s02-X60b]] Read twice: "In the shed, Max stacks cups on a crate. A dog jumps onto the crate and knocks the cups down." Show three pictures for where Max is, then three for the big change.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the map and the exit; cut the sentence expansion. Longer: a secure child adds the window and the parcel on the table as places, with in, by and on.

### Words and supports

Words: shelter, pause, in, by, on. Frames: "They are in the ___." "Yusuf sat by the ___." "The snow thickens, so ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 reads section 3 and joins the events with first, next, last. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of errors H and I from today's exit, or errors Z and AA from the talk; one repair only, the rest to the rotation. Today's marks go on the record sheet.
