# Day 61: Waiting: the events join up in order

## Teacher page

### Before class

Add cards to the wall map: the paper gets damp; the plastic bag; the wind grows quiet. Print section 3 large and cover its pictures. Draw one repair card. Write verse 7 large and cut two blank cards for windows. Make the day's six: the next six unheard. Keep the exit card to yourself. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. If many children missed day 60's exit, repair the more common of errors H and I; Z and AA go to the rotation. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hold a new hand-drawn card: "On the pier, Jo sits by a boat. A gull lands on the mast and the boat tips." Error H: Pip says Jo's school. **Ask.** "Which place does the card give?" Expect: the pier. Error I: Pip says the boat alone is the big change. **Ask.** "Is a boat a change?" Expect: no; the gull lands and the boat tips. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Review: sequence is the order things happen (day 59); lay three event cards and say first, next, last. Review: an event is what happens, not an object (day 50). Review: because tells why (day 43); hold up a hand and say "It is dark because the lamp is off", then ask children to finish "We wait because ___." Pip then says "Event is a box." The class says an event is something that happens.

#### 15 min. Hear section 3, Waiting (228 words)

Whole class. Look at the wall map and name the events so far (1 minute). Keep the pictures covered and read the whole section straight through (about 2 minutes), unabridged. Uncover page 1 and read it again with its picture; each child tells a partner one thing in the picture. Do the same for page 2. Give about 12 minutes to the two pictures in all; three children tell the class each time. Ask nothing about snow days at home. A child may pass, point or only look.

#### 10 min. Questions

Whole class, then pairs. **Ask.** "Which earlier event explains this pause?" Expect: the snow is too thick, so they wait in the bakery. **Ask.** "What new event should the retell have?" Expect: the paper gets damp and Yusuf puts the parcel in the bag. Say the because sentence together: "Yusuf uses the bag because the paper is damp." Pairs ask each other one question with a picture card (4 minutes); six children answer, about 40 seconds each. Error J is two events swapped; error AV is an event left out, such as the bag.

#### 15 min. Rehearse the linked events

Whole class, then pairs. Cards from sections 1 to 3 are on the wall. Model the first three: leave the post office, the first flakes fall, the snow thickens. Say them with first, next, last (3 minutes). Pairs then get the later cards, they go into the bakery, the paper gets damp, the bag, and retell all six in order, one partner each, 1 minute each (all at once). The teacher hears six pairs, 2 minutes each (12 minutes); the other pairs retell again with the cards turned over. Never name a child as the example of a mistake.

#### 5 min. Class weather journal

Whole class. Review: the class weather journal (day 41). Say today's weather from the window or a printed scene. Write one line and read it. Children draw it in their own journal.

#### 5 min. Exit E61

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its picture (40 seconds). Ask [[s02-X61]]. Children number the three mixed pictures 1, 2, 3 on the exit sheet (1 minute). Then the day's six tell it with first, next, last, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: two pictures swapped (error J); one told and the rest left out (error AV).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 9, lesson 47). Rhyme: say snow, go and show; children give another (low). Clap quiet (two), umbrella (three) and snow (one).

#### 10 min. Foundations: q, u and x

Whole class. Chant "q, quail, /kw/." "u, umbrella, /u/." "x, fox, /ks/." Say a sound; children write its letter on handwriting paper, little and big, and say the sound as it is written (formation and sound-to-letter writing).

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend /b/ /u/ /s/ and children say bus. Segment gum into three. Add /p/ to the front of up to make pup. Change the last sound of bun to /g/ to make bug. Isolate the middle sound of hut. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words a and my

Whole class. Review: you learned a (day 21) and my (day 5); read them whole. Write "My dad sat on a mat." and point under each word; children read it. Then write "I pat my cat and he sat." Under counters, map the part to remember in a and in my. Children read each line to a partner.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class. Read verse 7, Rainbow, with the pointer: "The rain is done. The sun comes through. Quick, look up! There is red and blue." The words are heard, not decoded. Slide the two blank windows around one word at a time so the spaces show; children count the words in line 1. Find the capital and the period (review: capital and period, day 56). Find q and u in the first word of line 2.

#### 15 min. Foundations: rotation

The day's six, 1 minute each (6 minutes): name q, u and x, give each sound, write the letter for one sound; mark the cycle 9 record. Then up to six children from the cycle 9 record, 90 seconds each (9 minutes). The others work in pairs with letter cards and handwriting paper.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear section 3, put the cards in order and say first, next, last. One projector: show the two pictures one at a time after the reading; the map stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw five small pictures. No wall map: lay them on a table.

**Skip.** The pair retell and the rotation.

- **10 min. The story.** Read section 3 of Yusuf and the Parcel aloud, then again with its pictures.
  **Say.** "Why did Yusuf put the parcel in the bag?" Expect: the paper got damp, so he kept it dry.
- **10 min. In order.** Your child lays out five drawn events from sections 1 to 3 and tells them with first, next, last.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints q, u and x.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X61]] Read twice: "First Bea finds a torn page. Next she tapes the tear. Last she returns the book to its shelf." Your child puts three mixed pictures in order and tells it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the rehearsal and the exit; cut the journal. Longer: a secure child retells all six events with because.

### Words and supports

Words: sequence, event, because. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "Yusuf used the bag because ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 62 reads section 4 and talks about how Yusuf feels. Its first 5 minutes repair the more common of errors J and AV from today's exit, one only; the other goes to the rotation. Today's marks go on the record sheet.
