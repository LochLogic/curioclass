# Exit cards, days 146, 147 and 149

Cards E146 and E149 are made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice", read each twice with its pictures, and print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each. Sheet E147 goes with two pages of the unit's own texts, read aloud again; it is not made up, so say nothing about practice for it. Children do not read the cards; you read aloud. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 146 card E146

**Read:** "Opinion: I like to watch the seeds spin down. Source: Winged seeds twirl from a maple tree."
**Pictures:** three finished cards: one with readable words and a painted twirling seed; one with paint over the words; one with painted flowers and readable words.
**Ask:** "Mark the card that keeps the words readable and shows what the source says." Right: the first card. Wrong: the flowers (K). Paint over the words has no register letter; the repair is to blot with a dry towel and write the words again in dark pencil.

## Day 147 sheet E147: page 2 of Trees Help Our Community and page 7 of Planting Together

**Read again:** page 2 of Trees Help Our Community (texts/gk-u4-02-a.md), then page 7 of Planting Together (texts/gk-u4-02-b.md).
**Ask:** "Say one thing that is different on the two pages about the bark." Right: Trees Help Our Community says the bark covers the trunk like a coat; Planting Together says the mulch is kept back so the bark stays dry. Wrong: "They say the same thing" (E); bark, the shared topic, offered as the difference (E).

## Day 149 card E149

**Read:** "Draft: I enjoy the tree. Peer: What do you do there? Source: Children gather fallen pinecones in a basket."
**Pictures:** children gathering pinecones in a basket; the unchanged draft; a lone cloud.
**Ask:** "Mark the picture that answers the peer." Right: the pinecone picture. Wrong: the unchanged draft (M); the lone cloud (K).
