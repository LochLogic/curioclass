# Cycle check card F6, day 150

Kept in the teacher folder; show it only at the small-group table on day 150. Keep it unseen until then. Each child gets about 50 seconds. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card.

**Card, side 1 (F150b).** The three printed words: you, said, were. Ask: "Read each word." Mark each as read as printed.

**Card, side 1, lower half (F150c).** The two printed words: pig, pit. Ask: "Say the sound that changed from pig to pit." Look for the last letter and both sounds, /g/ then /t/.

**Card, side 2 (F150d).** The passage: "A bug is in a cup. A pup can tip it. The bug is on a mat." Say: "Read to find where the bug ends up." After the child reads, ask: "Point to the sentence that tells where the bug ends up." Look for the last sentence and "on a mat".

**Marks.** Shown, shown with help, not yet seen, one mark for each of reading, understanding and the sound or letter named. Signs: said read for a different word (error AP); letters named with no sounds (error AQ); a guess from the picture of the cup (error O); an answer that says the bug stays in the cup (error Y). One prompt only, never the answer.
