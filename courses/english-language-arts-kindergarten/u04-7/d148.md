# Day 148: Use details from the texts to strengthen the card

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the observation sheet, days 148 to 151. Lay each child's painted card and strip together by table. Keep the sheet and your notes ready; this day has no exit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: detail, question, feedback and revise (days 144 to 147); explain (day 145). Show each with a gesture: a finger on one small part of a picture, a raised hand, a nod, an eraser. Children point to the one you name, then say it.

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the two tree-care stories (days 134 to 141) and Trees Help Our Community (days 129 to 147).

- Reread, 6 minutes. Read pages 6 and 7 of the first tree-care story aloud, then page 5 of Trees Help Our Community, about 120 words, pictures shown.
- Repair from day 147's exit, error E, 2 minutes. Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Put page 3 of Trees Help Our Community beside page 3 of Planting Together. The puppet says they tell the same thing. Say: "Point to what each page tells." Children point to the shade reason on one and the digging step on the other and say one difference.
- **Ask.** "What is one detail on page 5?" Pairs, 2 minutes, 1 minute each. Say the first words: "On page 5 I see ___." Expect: a nest, a bird, a branch.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: Both ___. But ___. (days 125 and 139).

- **Ask.** "What detail did your partner's card add?" Pairs, 4 minutes, 2 minutes each, cards on the table. Say the first words: "The card adds ___." Expect: the thing the question asked about.
- Share, 3 minutes. Hear six children, 30 seconds each; a child may pass.
- **Ask.** "Which sentence in the text does your detail come from?" Show pages 3 to 5; pairs point, 3 minutes. Expect: a sentence about shade, a bench, a bird or a nest.

#### 20 min. R1 windows

Six groups of five, 150 seconds a group, at your table. The others add one more detail to the strip or tell a partner "My sketch adds ___". No reteaching. Keep the first teacher feedback and the partner's question beside the card, laid flat.

- Ask [[s04-R148]]: "Where in the text did your new detail come from?" Each child gets about 30 seconds in all. One prompt: point to the card again. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the observation sheet.
- Signs: "I did not change it" (error M, the detail asked about left as it was); a detail named with no sentence behind it. A child may pass; leave the cells blank and try day 149.

#### 5 min. Shared caption

Review: the shared text editor (day 75). Six children, one word each, 40 seconds each, add the line "Next, an adult digs the hole." from page 3 of Planting Together to the caption from day 147: Next, an, adult, digs, the, hole. The class says the line aloud after each word. Say what each key did. Unscored.

#### 10 min. Explanation rehearsal

Pairs, then the whole class. Review: explain and audible (day 145).

- Rehearse, 6 minutes. Each child says to a partner, 3 minutes each way, "My card shows ___ because the text says ___." The partner says it back.
- Whole class, 4 minutes. Hear four children, 30 seconds each, speak to the far wall. A child who cannot be heard says it again, nearer (error L).

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards o and u and counters, cycle 24, lesson 123.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long o and u by ear (days 146 and 147); say bone and tube; children hold card o or u.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say cod, then code; tub, tube; rob, robe; cub, cube. Children say which word has the long sound and hold card o or u. Then pairs: one child says a pair, the partner points, 1 minute each way. Spoken words only.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say bed, hum and fig; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Change bad to bed, hit to hat and mud to mad; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Pup in a Hut and Tree Care Words, verse 24 again. Review: both were first used on day 146. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station; the time of tasks 7 and 8 goes to reading. At your table each child reads one page, then says what happened. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Review: you, said, were (days 146 and 147). Write "You were at the shop," said Ben. on the chart. Count the words, six, and the spaces, five. Children point to you and were and say each; two children each point to the part to remember.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate joke, then cute; children say the long sound and write the vowel on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Say what your card adds and which sentence it comes from, then hold o and u on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No texts in print: describe the page you have. No cards: write o and u on two papers.

**Skip.** The windows, the typing and the marks.

- **10 min. Detail.** Look at one page of Trees Help Our Community with your child.
  **Say.** "What is one detail on this page?" Expect: a nest, a bird or a bench.
- **10 min. Card.** Your child shows their card and tells what the new detail adds.
  **Say.** "What does your card add?" Expect: the detail from the question, named.
- **10 min. Long o and u.** Say cod, code, tub, tube. Your child holds up o or u when they hear a long sound.
- **10 min. Words.** Write you and were. Your child says each and marks the part to remember.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check; cut the shared caption. Longer: a secure child says a second detail and its sentence.

### Words and supports

Words: detail, question, feedback, revise. Frame: "My card adds ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Mark and move on; do not teach inside a window.

### Tomorrow

The windows tell you who left a questioned detail unchanged. Day 149's Add a useful detail part takes error M, 2 minutes, then each child exchanges feedback with a partner and adds one more detail.
