# Day 150: Finish the card and compare the familiar story experiences

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the observation sheet, days 148 to 151. Clip page 3 and page 9 of each tree-care story shut for the other children. Keep the picture cards, the purpose card and card F6 covered. Day 149's exit marks go back to children in Finish and tell. This day has no exit; the C3 windows and the cycle check are the observation.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: same and different (days 12 and 49). Show two shoes that match and a shoe beside a cup. Children point to "same" or "different" as you name each pair, then say it. Use no tree picture, so nothing here gives the check away.

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class. Review: Trees Help Our Community (days 129 to 148). Read it whole, about 280 words, pictures shown. No questions.

#### 30 min. Observation windows, C3 and T2

Six groups of five, 5 minutes a group, at your table. The others retell pages 4 to 8 of a tree-care story with a partner, pictures open, pages 3 and 9 shut. No reteaching. About 300 seconds a group.

- Page nine of each story, about 70 seconds. Read page nine of the first story, then page nine of the second, once each, with no reread. "These stories are made up for practice."
- Same, about 40 seconds. Ask each child in turn [[s04-C3a]], about 8 seconds a child.
- Different, about 40 seconds. Ask each child in turn [[s04-C3b]], about 8 seconds a child.
- Who, where, what, about 60 seconds. Reread page nine of the first story, then ask each child [[s04-T150a]], [[s04-T150b]] and [[s04-T150c]], one short ask after another, about 12 seconds a child.
- Picture, about 25 seconds. Show the two picture cards and ask [[s04-T150d]]; each child points.
- Purpose, about 40 seconds. Give the purpose card to the group and reread page nine; ask [[s04-T150e]].

One prompt: read the page again. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the observation sheet. Signs: a likeness of looks, "both wear a shirt" (error A); no character, setting or event named (errors Z, AA and AB); a picture matched to an event the page does not state (error U); the purpose card ignored (error AF). The timing is tight: if a group runs over, say the picture ask to the group and score it once.

#### 15 min. Finish and tell

Individuals, then pairs. Day 149's exit marks go back to children.

- Hand-backs, 3 minutes. Error M, 2 minutes: the puppet's card still leaves the questioned detail unchanged ("made up for practice"); children name the detail and say where it goes. Error K, 1 minute: the puppet adds a rocket beside the bench; children name the source word and keep only it.
- Finish, 8 minutes. Each child finishes the card: the sketch, the painted detail, the words kept readable. A child who is done adds one more detail from the text.
- Tell, 4 minutes. Pairs, 1 minute each way: "My card shows ___." The listener says it back. If a listener cannot hear, say it again, nearer (error L).

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards o and u and counters, cycle 24, lesson 125.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long o and u by ear (days 146 to 149); say stove and fuse; children hold card o or u.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say cone, mute, hope and dune; after each, children say the long sound and hold the card. Then pairs: one child says a word, the partner holds the card, 1 minute each way. Spoken words only. In the last 2 minutes say the long sounds o and u alone, in mixed order, and children point to the matching card. This is [[s04-F150a]] for the whole class; scan the room and mark it on the record sheet.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say sun, mop and bat; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Change dig to dug, bit to bat and pot to pat; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Pup in a Hut and Tree Care Words, verse 24 again. Review: both were first used on day 146. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station; the time of tasks 7 and 8 goes to the cycle check. At your table, in about 50 seconds a child, show card F6 and ask [[s04-F150b]], [[s04-F150c]] and [[s04-F150d]]. Mark reading, understanding and the sound or letter apart. One prompt only. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Review: you, said, were (days 146 to 149). Write "You said they were at the shop," on the chart. Count the words, seven, and the spaces, six. Children point to you, said and were and say each; two children each name the part to remember.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate lid, then cut; children say the sounds and write each on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read page nine of each story aloud and say one thing the same and one thing different. Finish the card and hold o and u on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No texts in print: describe the page you have. No cards: write o and u on two papers.

**Skip.** The windows, the cycle check and the marks.

- **10 min. Same and different.** Put two spoons on the table, one big and one small.
  **Say.** "What is the same, and what is different?" Expect: both are spoons; one is big and one is small.
- **10 min. Finish.** Your child finishes their card and tells what it shows.
  **Say.** "What does your card show?" Expect: a detail from the reading, named.
- **10 min. Long o and u.** Say cone, mute, hope, dune. Your child holds up o or u.
- **10 min. Words.** Write you, said and were. Your child says each and marks the part to remember.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check; cut the extra detail. Longer: a secure child tells one more same and one more different.

### Words and supports

Words: same, different. Frame: "Both ___. But ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt in a window and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

The window marks tell you who mixed up the long sounds or a mapped word. Day 151's Foundations response part takes errors AO 2 minutes, AP 1 minute, AQ 2 minutes, O 1 minute and Y 1 minute, with fresh words and a fresh passage.
