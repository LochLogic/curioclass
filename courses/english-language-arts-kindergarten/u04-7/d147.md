# Day 147: Reread the planting text and compare it with the reasons text

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put your one-line note on each painted card and lay the cards out by table. Open the shared text editor. Keep E146 to E149 together, E147 on top and covered. Write the repair card for error K: "I like the birds in the branches." The puppet paints a big sun over the branches. Make the day-146 exit's marks ready for the hand-back.

### The day

#### 3 min. Words return

Review: step (day 131), reason (day 130), same and different (days 12 and 49). Show a picture and gesture for each: a footprint, a hand that says "because", two matching cards, two unlike cards. Children point to the one you name, then say it.

#### 2 min. Typing reminder

Review: the shared text editor (days 69 and 75). Show its keys for about 30 seconds, then point to the space bar and the letters and say what each does.

#### 15 min. Reread and compare

Whole class, then pairs. Review: Planting Together (days 131 and 139) and Trees Help Our Community (days 129 to 140).

- Reread, 5 minutes. Read Planting Together aloud, about 290 words, pictures shown.
- Echo, 5 minutes. Read one sentence from Trees Help Our Community page 3, "Trees give us shaded places to rest," and one from Planting Together page 6, "Next, children carry water in watering cans." In each pair one partner echoes the first, the other echoes the second, then they swap, 1 minute each way.
- **Ask.** "Which planting step does page 6 tell?" Hear three children, 30 seconds each. Expect: watering. Then pairs point to one thing the page 6 picture shows and say it, 2 minutes.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: Both ___. But ___. (days 125 and 139).

- **Ask.** "What step does Planting Together tell?" Pairs, 2 minutes, 1 minute each; each child names a different step. Expect: choose a place, dig, set the tree, fill, water or mulch.
- **Ask.** "What does Trees Help Our Community tell that Planting Together does not?" Pairs, 2 minutes. Expect: why trees help, shade and shelter for birds.
- Share, 3 minutes. Hear six children, 30 seconds each; a child may pass.
- Build one sentence on the chart with the class, 3 minutes: "Both ___. But ___." Expect: both are about trees, but one tells why and one tells how.
- **Ask.** "Which sentence in Trees Help Our Community gives a reason?" Show pages 3 and 5; pairs point, 2 minutes. Expect: trees give shaded places to rest; trees give birds shelter.

#### 15 min. Revise the day-146 card

Pairs. The painted card stays as it was; the answer and the new detail go on the strip beside it.

- Repair from day 146's exit, error K, 2 minutes. Say: "This card is made up for practice." The puppet paints a big sun over the branches. Say: "Name the source word and paint only it." Children say "nest", point to page 5 and say what to paint.
- Hear the note, 4 minutes. Go table to table and read each child's note aloud once, about 8 seconds a child. Children set the card and a strip side by side.
- Partner question, 3 minutes. Review: a question and an answer (days 143 and 144). Each child asks the partner one question about the painted card, beginning "What ___?" or "Where ___?", 1 minute each way.
- Answer and add, 6 minutes. Each child answers on the strip beside the draft by drawing, writing or dictating, and adds the detail the question asked about. Take dictation from about six children, 40 seconds each. The strip and the painted card stay together for day 148.

#### 5 min. Shared caption

Review: the shared text editor (day 75). Six children, one word each, 40 seconds each, build "First, choose a good place." from page 2 of Planting Together: First, choose, a, good, place, and the period. The class says the sentence aloud after each word and picks the next. Say what each key did. Unscored. A room with no device uses the paper strip.

#### 5 min. Exit E147

Individuals. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E147 twice and ask [[s04-X147]], 30 seconds each for the day's six, about 3 minutes. The others tell a partner one thing different between the two texts they heard today. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: "They say the same thing" (error E).

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards o and u and counters, cycle 24, lesson 122.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long o and u by ear (day 146); say home and cube; children hold card o or u.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say bone, nose, use and tube; after each children say the long sound and hold the card. Then in pairs one child says a word and the partner holds the card, 1 minute each way. Spoken words only.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say rug, net and dip; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Change fan to fun, sit to sat and rob to rib; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Pup in a Hut and Tree Care Words, verse 24 again. Review: both were first used on day 146. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station; the time of tasks 7 and 8 goes to reading. At your table each child reads one page, then says what happened. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Review: you, said, were (day 146). Write "Where were you, Tess?" said Ben. on the chart, as page 4 of the reader prints it. Count the words, six, and the spaces, five. Children point to you, said and were and say each, and name the part to remember.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate pan, then wet; children say the sounds and write each on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; ask one question about the painted card and answer it beside the draft. Read both texts and say one thing different.

### At home

**Swap.** No texts in print: read from the page you have and describe the other. No cards: write o and u on two papers.

**Skip.** The typing, the share and the marks.

- **10 min. Compare.** Read one page of Planting Together aloud, then page 3 of Trees Help Our Community.
  **Say.** "How are the two pages different?" Expect: one tells a step, such as watering; one tells a reason, such as shade.
- **10 min. Revise.** Ask your child one question about their painted card; they answer by drawing the detail beside it.
  **Say.** "What do you see in your picture?" Expect: a detail named from the reading.
- **10 min. Long o and u.** Say bone, nose, use, tube. Your child holds up o or u on paper.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X147]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision, the exit and the long o and u; cut the reasons ask in Questions and talk. Longer: a secure child names two steps and the reason that goes with one.

### Words and supports

Words: step, reason, same, different. Frame: "Both ___. But ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say one difference between two texts. Day 148's Reread part takes error E, 2 minutes: two cards side by side, one difference each. Day 148 then hears each child on the revision in the R1 windows, with the painted card, the strip and the question beside it.
