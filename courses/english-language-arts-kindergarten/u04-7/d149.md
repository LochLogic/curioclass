# Day 149: Exchange feedback, answer a partner's question and add one detail

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the repair card for error M: the puppet's card reads "I like the pond", a partner asks "What lives there?" and the puppet leaves the card as it was. Keep E146 to E149 together, E149 on top and covered. Nothing is scored in the windows today; this day is practice only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: feedback, question, answer and detail (days 144 to 148). Show each with a gesture: a thumb up with a pointing finger, a raised hand, a nod, a finger on one small part. Children point to the one you name, then say it.

#### 15 min. Reread

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the two tree-care stories (days 134 to 141).

- Reread, 7 minutes. Read pages 4 and 5 of the first tree-care story and page 5 of the second aloud, about 110 words, pictures shown.
- **Ask.** "What detail is in the picture on page 5?" Pairs, 3 minutes, 1 minute each, then four children share, 30 seconds each. Expect: a detail named, such as a watering can, a leaf or a branch.
- Point to the words, 5 minutes. Read one sentence from each page; pairs point to the picture the sentence matches and say one thing the picture shows that the sentence does not.

#### 15 min. Exchange feedback

Pairs, cards on the table. Review: feedback (days 17 to 141) and a question (day 1).

- Model, 3 minutes. Say: "This card is made up for practice." The puppet shows a card that says "I like the bench." You say: "I like your bench. What is in the tree above it?" The puppet answers aloud, "A bird." Children say what you did: told one good thing, asked one question.
- Exchange, 8 minutes. Each child tells the partner "I like ___" and then asks the partner one question about the card, beginning "What ___?" or "Where ___?". 4 minutes each way. The partner answers aloud before anything is drawn.
- Share, 4 minutes. Hear six children, 40 seconds each, say one thing a partner told them. A child may pass.

#### 20 min. Add a useful detail and rehearse words

Individuals, then pairs. Review: revise (day 147) and the audio recorder (day 144).

- Repair from day 148's windows, error M, 2 minutes. Say: "This card is made up for practice." The puppet leaves its card as it was. Say: "Point to a detail in the text and add it." Children name one detail from page 4, "a bench", and say where the puppet puts it.
- Add, 10 minutes. Each child answers the partner's question by adding one detail to the strip: a drawing, a word they write, or dictation. Take dictation from about six children, 40 seconds each. The painted card stays as the original.
- Audio words, 8 minutes. Show the recorder for 30 seconds: press once, speak, press again. Each child chooses their words, "My card adds ___ because ___", and says them aloud twice to a partner, 3 minutes each way. A room with no device uses the partner only.

#### 5 min. Exit E149

Individuals. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E149 twice, show its three pictures and ask [[s04-X149]], 30 seconds each for the day's six, about 3 minutes. The others tell a partner the detail they added. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: the unchanged draft marked (error M).

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards o and u and counters, cycle 24, lesson 124.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long o and u by ear (days 146 to 148); say joke and cute; children hold card o or u.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say stove, fuse, hole and tune; after each, children say the long sound and hold the card. Then pairs: one child says a word, the partner holds the card, 1 minute each way. Spoken words only.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say van, bud and fin; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Change dim to dam, mud to mad and wig to wag; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Pup in a Hut and Tree Care Words, verse 24 again. Review: both were first used on day 146. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station; the time of tasks 7 and 8 goes to reading. At your table each child reads one page, then says what happened. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Review: you, said, were (days 146 to 148). Write "Were you at the pond?" said Pam. on the chart. Count the words, six, and the spaces, five. Children point to were and you and say each; two children each name the part to remember.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate jug, then gum; children say the sounds and write each on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Ask one question about your own card, answer it aloud and add the detail.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: say it aloud. No cards: write o and u on two papers.

**Skip.** The windows, the recorder and the marks.

- **10 min. Feedback.** Your child shows their card. Say one thing you like, then ask one question.
  **Say.** "I like ___. What ___?" Expect: an answer that names a detail.
- **10 min. Add.** Your child adds that detail beside the card.
- **10 min. Long o and u.** Say stove, fuse, hole, tune. Your child holds up o or u.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X149]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exchange, the exit and the long o and u; cut the audio words. Longer: a secure child adds a second detail from the story pages.

### Words and supports

Words: feedback, question, answer, detail. Frame: "I like ___. What ___?" Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the picture that answers a partner's question. Day 150's Finish and tell part takes error M, 2 minutes, and error K, 1 minute, then each child finishes the card and says what it shows.
