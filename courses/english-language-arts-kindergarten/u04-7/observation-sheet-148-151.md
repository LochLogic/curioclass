# Observation sheet, days 148 to 151

Kept in the teacher folder. Roster numbers 1 to 30 down the left, six tables of five. Each part is recorded apart and marked 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen):

- Day 148, R1 (W.K.5): the detail the peer asked about is added, and the child names the sentence it came from.
- Day 150, C3 (RL.K.9): what is the same in what happens on the two ninth pages; what is different. T2: who, where, what happens, the picture, and joining the reread with the purpose card. F6: the cycle check, with purpose, reading and understanding recorded apart.
- Day 151, C4 (RI.K.9): how cards A and B are the same, and how they are different in the words or the pictures.

A column names the one prompt used. A child may pass; leave the cells blank and try another day.

A room with nothing: rule the columns by hand on a sheet.
