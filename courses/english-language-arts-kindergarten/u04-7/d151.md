# Day 151: Finish the illustration and compare the two information texts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark page 9 of Wangari and the Tree Planters and page 4 of Planting Together for the check sheet, day 151. Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the observation sheet, days 148 to 151. From day 150's F6 marks write five repair cards, one for each of errors AO, AP, AQ, O and Y. This day has no exit; the C4 windows are the observation.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: same and different (days 12, 49 and 150), and detail (day 9). Show two cups that match and a cup beside a hat. Children point to "same" or "different" as you name each pair, then say it.

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class. Review: Planting Together (days 131 to 147). Read it whole but page 4, which waits for the windows, about 250 words, pictures shown. No questions.

#### 30 min. Observation windows, C4

Six groups of five, 5 minutes a group, at your table. The others tell a partner what their painted card shows. No reteaching. About 180 seconds a group.

- Pages, about 60 seconds. Read page 9 of Wangari and the Tree Planters and page 4 of Planting Together once each, showing each picture.
- Same, about 60 seconds. Ask each child in turn [[s04-C4av2]], about 12 seconds a child.
- Different, about 60 seconds. Ask each child in turn [[s04-C4bv2]], about 12 seconds a child.

One prompt: read the page again. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the observation sheet. Sign: "They say the same thing" (error E). A child may pass; leave the cells blank. Time left over goes to the next group.

#### 15 min. Finish the illustration

Individuals. Each child finishes the card illustration: a thin wash or a pressed color on one part, the words kept readable. A child who is done tells a partner "My picture adds ___". Hear six children, 30 seconds each, speak to the far wall (error L).

#### 10 min. Foundations response to F6

Whole class, then tables. Review: long o and u (days 146 to 150) and you, said, were (days 146 to 150).

- Model, 2 minutes. Say: "This is made up for practice." The puppet hears pole and holds card a; you hold card o. Children say the long sound, then hold the card.
- Table repair, 1 minute. Children who erred sit by the error they made and say the word again.
- Fresh retry, 7 minutes. Error AO, 2 minutes: say vote, rude, pole and flute; children hold card o or u. Error AP, 1 minute: show "Ben said you were in the hut." and point to each mapped word as the child says it. Error AQ, 2 minutes: show hop and hot; children name the letter that changed and the sound before and after. Errors O and Y, 1 minute each: show "A ram is in a pen. A hen can nip it. The ram is on a log." and ask where the ram ends up; the child reads the last sentence, points to it and says on a log.

#### 10 min. Long e by ear

Whole class with card e. Review: you learned long a, i, o and u by ear in the same way (days 141 to 150). No word is read.

- Hear it, 3 minutes. Say tree slowly. The vowel says its name: e. Say feet slowly. Children say the long sound with you.
- Hold the card, 5 minutes. Say tree, feet, seed and leaf, one at a time. After each, children say the long sound, then hold card e.
- Heard only, 2 minutes. Say car, fork and bird. Children say each word back. No card is held; the vowel sound in these words is heard only today.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Hen Has an Egg (the title is adult-read) and Tree Care Words, verse 25, A Visit to Her Tree, both first used today. Give egg as a part to remember: e, then the doubled g. At your table read each page and have the group echo it; each child says what happened to Peg in one sentence, 15 seconds each; then say verse 25 together. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Map she, her, like

Whole class, then pairs with letter tiles. Review: a mapped word has a part to remember (you, said, were, day 146).

- she: sh says /sh/, the regular part; e is the part to remember.
- her: h is regular; er is the part to remember.
- like: l and k are regular; i and e are the part to remember.

Pairs build each word with tiles, 3 minutes, and put a counter above the part to remember. Read "She has her cap. Pam can like it." on the chart by echo. Six children each point to one of the three words and say it, 20 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate bee, then green; children say the long sound and write the vowel on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear both cards and say one thing the same and one thing different. Finish the card and hold e on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No texts in print: describe the page you have. No cards: write e on a paper.

**Skip.** The windows, the repair cards and the marks.

- **10 min. Compare.** Show two socks, both striped but one red and one blue.
  **Say.** "What is the same, and what is different?" Expect: both are striped; one is red and one is blue.
- **10 min. Finish.** Your child finishes their picture and tells what it adds.
  **Say.** "What does your picture add?" Expect: a detail named.
- **10 min. Long e.** Say tree, feet, seed, leaf. Your child holds up e.
- **10 min. Words.** Write she, her and like. Your child says each and marks the part to remember.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check; cut the heard-only words. Longer: a secure child tells one more same and one more different.

### Words and supports

Words: same, different, detail. Frame: "Both ___. But ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt in a window and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

The window marks tell you who still says two texts are the same. Day 152 begins the next section, and the long e work continues in its Foundations part.
