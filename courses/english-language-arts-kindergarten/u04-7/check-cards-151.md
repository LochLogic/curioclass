# Check sheet, day 151 (C4)

The check uses two pages not worked on until day 151: page 9 of Wangari and the Tree Planters (texts/gk-u4-03.md) and page 4 of Planting Together (texts/gk-u4-02-b.md), each with its picture. Keep page 4 out of the day's reread. Read each page aloud once, showing its picture. Each group of six has one minute to hear the pages and then three asks of about 12 seconds a child.

Ask: "How are the two pages the same?" (both tell about people putting a young tree in the ground; both pictures show people planting a young tree). Ask: "How are the two pages different, in the words or in the pictures?" (page 9 tells that Wangari Maathai won a prize and people in Kenya still plant trees; page 4 tells the trunk is not buried; one picture shows children at a school in Kenya, the other two adults lowering the tree).

"They say the same thing" is error E. Give one prompt a child and never the answer. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
