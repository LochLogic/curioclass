# Check cards, day 151 (C4)

Keep these cards unseen until day 151. Read card A aloud once and then again; read card B once. Each group of six has one minute to hear the cards and then three asks of about 12 seconds a child.

**Card A:** "One tree has round leaves. Another tree has long thin leaves." Picture A: a round-leaf tree beside a long-leaf tree.
**Card B:** "One tree has round leaves. Its leaves turn gold in autumn." Picture B: the same round-leaf tree in summer and in autumn.

Ask: "How are card A and card B the same?" (both tell about a tree with round leaves). Ask: "How are card A and card B different, in the words or in the pictures?" (A adds a tree with long thin leaves; B adds that the leaves turn gold in autumn; the pictures differ in the second tree and in the season).

"They say the same thing" is error E. Give one prompt a child and never the answer. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each card.
