# Picture cards and purpose card, day 150 (T2)

Keep these cards unseen until day 150. Two plain pictures for the check: Lina handing a fallen leaf to a neighbor; Lina watering the tree from a can. And a purpose card with a pointing hand and no other picture.

After the first question the teacher rereads page nine of the first story aloud. Each child answers who, where and what happens. Then the child points to the picture that shows this part of the story and says why. For the purpose card, the teacher says "find what Lina shares", gives each child the card and rereads the page with the group. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each card.
