# Day 146: Add watercolor to the sketch and keep the words readable

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out 15 palettes, 15 cups of water, brushes, cloths and a dry towel. Have the wash sample card ready. Keep E146 to E149 together, E146 on top and covered. From day 145's record write the two repair cards below. Find the commoner of errors AO and AP in the F5 marks for the model.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: card, detail, sketch and pencil (days 141, 144 and 145). New today: readable, words a reader can still read; wash, a thin layer of paint; brush; palette, the tray that holds the paint. Show a picture and gesture for each: a finger tracing a line of words, a light brush stroke, a brush held up, a flat tray. Children point to the one you name, then say it.

#### 10 min. Reread 02a and talk

Whole class, then pairs.

- Reread, 2 minutes. Review: Trees Help Our Community (days 129 to 140). Read pages 3 to 5 aloud, about 150 words, pictures shown.
- Repair from day 145's exit, 4 minutes. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Error K, 2 minutes: the card reads, "I like the shade by the bench." The puppet sketches a rocket beside the bench. Say: "Name the source detail, then sketch only it." Children point to the shaded bench on page 4 and say "a shaded bench". Error J, 2 minutes: the puppet's sketch sheet is blank. Say: "Point to one detail in the source, then add it." Children point to the nest on page 5, say "a nest", and the puppet adds it.
- **Ask.** "Which benefit goes with your card detail?" Pairs, all pairs at once, 2 minutes, 1 minute each. Say the first words so a child can borrow them: "My detail goes with ___." Expect: shade, or shelter for birds. A child whose detail came from a story names the story.
- Share, 2 minutes. Hear four children, 30 seconds each; a child may pass. Two repairs leave room for four, not six.

#### 5 min. Model the wash

Whole class at the front. Say the safety words first: the paint is labeled "Conforms to ASTM D-4236" with no cautionary label; use small amounts; no food near the paint; wipe spills; wash hands at the end. Show the wash sample card: dry words, a thin wash on the sketch, one color for each part. Then paint a second thin wash in front of the class in four moves: dip the brush, wipe it on the rim, paint one color on one part, keep the brush off the words. Say each move once; children say it back. **Ask.** "Can a reader still read the words?" Expect: yes, the words are dry.

#### 25 min. Paint the card

Pairs share a palette; each child paints alone.

- Paint, 20 minutes. Each child paints their sketch with a thin wash, one color for each part; the words stay dry. Move from table to table and stay close to the water and the paint. A child who loads the brush blots it on the dry towel. If a child paints over the words, stop that child: blot with the dry towel, then write the words again in dark pencil beside the picture, 5 minutes. If many do, stop the class and do the same. The register has no letter for this.
- Tell, 5 minutes. Cards lie flat to dry. Pairs, 1 minute each way: "My paint shows ___." The listener says it back. If a listener cannot hear, say it again, nearer (error L). Listen to six pairs, 30 seconds each.

After class write one line on each card, about 30 minutes: one thing the card shows and one thing a reader could ask. Cards dry flat overnight.

#### 10 min. Clean-up and handwashing

Wipe the tables, put brushes in cups, lay cards flat on the rack. Two children at a time go to two sinks, about 20 seconds each, 15 children a sink, about 5 minutes. The rest wipe and carry, then swap.

#### 5 min. Exit E146

Individuals. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E146 twice, show its three finished cards and ask [[s04-X146]], 30 seconds each for the day's six, about 3 minutes. The others tell a partner what their paint shows. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: the flowers (error K); the card with paint over the words (no letter: blot and write again).

#### 10 min. Foundations response to F5

Whole class, then tables. Review: long a and i (days 141 to 145) and what, when, then (day 141).

- Model, 3 minutes. Take the commoner of error AO or AP. Say: "This is made up for practice." Error AO: the puppet hears cage and holds card i; you hold card a. Children say the long sound, then hold the card. Error AP: the puppet reads "when" as "went"; you read "when", say each sound, and mark the part to remember.
- Table repair, 3 minutes. Children who missed the F5 sit by the error they made. AO tables: say tide and cage; they say the long sound, then hold the card. AP tables: point to the wall cards what, when and then and say each.
- Fresh retry, 4 minutes. Fresh words for error AO, 2 minutes: say cave, mail, hide and light; children hold card a or i. A new sentence for error AP, 2 minutes: show "When Ben ran, then what did Tim get?" and the children who erred point to when, then and what and say each, 15 seconds each.

#### 10 min. Long o and u by ear

Whole class with cards o and u. Review: you learned long a and i by ear in the same way (days 141 to 145). No word is read.

- Hear it, 3 minutes. Say home slowly. The vowel says its name: o. Say cube slowly. The vowel says its name: u. Children say each long sound with you.
- Hold the card, 5 minutes. Say rope, mule, home and cube, one at a time. After each, children say the long sound, then hold card o or u.
- Name the cards, 2 minutes. Show each card; children say its long sound.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Pup in a Hut (the title is adult-read) and Tree Care Words, verse 24, Taking Turns, both first used today. At your table read each page and have the group echo it; each child says what happened to Tess in one sentence, 15 seconds each; then say verse 24 together. Stations 2 to 5: the reader and verse 24, tasks 1 to 6. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Map you, said, were

Whole class, then pairs with letter tiles. Review: a mapped word has a part to remember (they and where, day 140; what, when and then, day 141). New today: you, said, were.

- you: y says /y/, the regular part; ou is the part to remember.
- said: s and d are regular; ai is the part to remember.
- were: w and r are regular; e is the part to remember.

Pairs build each word with tiles, 3 minutes, and put a counter above the part to remember. Read "You said they were in a hut." on the chart by echo. Six children each point to one of the three words and say it, 20 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Check

Say note, then huge (new words). After each, children say the long sound, then hold card o or u. Scan the room and mark the six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Paint your own card, say "My paint shows ___", and hold o and u on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No paint: use colored pencil, pressed lightly. No cards: write o and u on two papers.

**Skip.** The sinks, the tables and the marks.

- **10 min. Words.** Trace a line of words with a finger; say readable. Your child says wash and brush.
- **10 min. Paint.** Your child puts one thin color on each part of their sketch and leaves the words dry.
  **Say.** "What does your paint show?" Expect: a detail from the reading, such as a bench or a bird.
- **10 min. Long o and u.** Say home, rope, cube, mule. Your child holds up o or u.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X146]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the paint, the exit and the long o and u; cut the share in Reread 02a and talk. Longer: a secure child paints a second detail from page 5.

### Words and supports

Words: readable, wash, brush, palette. Frame: "My paint shows ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the card that keeps the words readable and shows the source. Day 147's Revise part takes error K, 2 minutes: name the source word and paint only it. Day 147 then has each child answer a partner's question beside the draft, with these painted cards kept as the original.
