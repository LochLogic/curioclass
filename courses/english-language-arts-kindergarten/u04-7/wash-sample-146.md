# Wash sample card, day 146

One sample card for the teacher's model, made before class on heavy paper: the words "I like the tree by the library door. A kite is stuck in its branches." written dark and spaced, a pencil sketch of the tree and the kite, and a thin watercolor wash painted around the words, one color for each part. The words are left dry.

The teacher paints a second thin wash in front of the class around the words and shows the dry words staying readable. Safety, said aloud: watercolor labeled "Conforms to ASTM D-4236" with no cautionary label, small amounts, closely supervised, no food near the paint, wipe spills, wash hands at the end.

This card is for teaching and may be shown. It does not repeat any exit or end card.
