# Section 4.8 opener: share, record and answer once more

## What the section does

Days 152 to 157 close the unit. On day 152 each group hears an unseen card that makes a point and gives two reasons, and the class rereads the true account of Wangari Maathai. Days 153 and 154 hear each child describe the finished card, show what the drawing adds and speak so the group can hear (S1). On day 155 the class records its card versions in turns on the teacher-held recorder, each child choosing a wording and a place in the order. Day 156 reads each child's written feedback and scores the plan sheet and the recording as made (P1). Day 157 answers what the closing checks showed, with a fresh retry, and returns to letters, rhyme and syllables. Foundations finish long e and r-controlled sounds heard only (cycle 25) and the mapped words she, her and like.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and a five-minute exit (days 155 and 157) or the observation windows (days 152 to 154 and 156). The foundations block holds sounds, letters, the reader, the response to the cycle check and the rotation. A child outside the six heard is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read the cards; the teacher reads every card aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A point and its reasons are different sentences.** What the writer wants is the point; why is the reason.
- **Clearing something changes what can happen.** Without branches on the path, carts have room to roll.
- **A description adds a detail, a drawing adds information, and the voice carries both to the listener.**
- **The class chooses wording and order together.** Each child decides their own part.
- **Two texts on one topic compare by what they share and what each adds.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **B, C, AC, AD, AE, Y, AF:** a reason taken for the point, a liking taken for a reason, the wrong topic, no details retold, a result reversed, the blocked path chosen, or watching the reread.
- **J, K, L:** a name with no detail, an unrelated drawing, or speech too soft to repeat.
- **N:** watching the recorder and choosing nothing.
- **A, E:** characters compared by their clothes, or two texts taken to say the same thing.
- **AO, AP:** a vowel letter that does not match its sound, or a mapped word mis-read.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No picture or question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion; there is no yard, only the class pot or the school grounds. The account names no violence, and "millions" is said in words only.

## Supplies

The true account, the reasons text and the planting text in print, the finished cards, the unseen card and picture strips for day 152, the observation sheet, the audio plan sheet, the teacher-held recorder (light, day 155), the exit cards, the model cards for day 157, cards e, letter tiles, counters, whiteboards and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Read from print. A room with no recorder cannot finish the audio part of W.K.6 by paper or narration alone: the audio part stays open for the course window and the paper plan sheet is kept. Make the cards from scrap paper and say each invented card is made up for practice.
