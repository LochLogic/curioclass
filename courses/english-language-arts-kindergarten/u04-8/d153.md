# Day 153: Share the finished card with a clear voice and a helpful drawing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the first 15 children on the class list in three groups of five on the observation sheet, days 152 to 156; the other 15 are for day 154. Note any child whose day 152 C5 cells are blank. Lay out the finished cards. This day has no exit; the S1 windows are the observation.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: describe (days 10 and 74), detail (day 9). Add means to put one more detail with it; show means to point to it in the drawing; loud means a voice the whole group can hear. Say each with a gesture; children say it back.

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class. Review: A Tree Beside Our Door, Reading Under the Tree and Trees Help Our Community (days 119 to 150). Read page 8 of each story and page 7 of the community text, about 130 words, pictures shown, then talk about the pictures: partners say one thing a picture shows that the words do not. The last 3 minutes: a child whose C5 cells were blank on day 152 comes to your table (up to three children). Read the day 152 card twice and ask the point and then the two reasons, the same two asks as day 152. Mark the sheet. Any further child waits for day 157.

#### 15 min. Observation windows, S1

The first 15 children, three groups of five, 5 minutes a group, at your table, each with their own finished card. The other 15 rehearse their share in pairs, then read in the reading corner. No reteaching. There is no slack: about 55 seconds a child and 25 for the change of group.

- Tell, about 25 seconds a child. Say the card's subject, for example "Your card shows a bench under a tree," and ask [[s04-S153a]]. After one pause, say the second prompt.
- Show, about 15 seconds. Point to the drawing and ask [[s04-S153b]].
- Loud, about 15 seconds. The child faces the group and answers [[s04-S153c]]; the group says back the idea.

One prompt a child. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the observation sheet, and name the support used. Signs: a name with no detail after the prompt (error J); a drawing detail the card does not name, such as stars (error K); a voice the group cannot repeat (error L). A child may pass; leave the cells blank.

#### 15 min. Card gallery

Pairs. Review: a gallery visit (day 30). Stand the cards on tables, 3 minutes. Each pair visits two cards other than their own, 1 minute at each, and says to the maker, "Your drawing shows ___." Then six children say one thing a drawing showed, 20 seconds each. Spoken, unscored. If many name a detail the card does not show, hold up one card and name one picture detail beside one the words give.

#### 15 min. Draw and dictate

Individuals. Each child draws one thing they will say about their card for the class audio version, 5 minutes. Then you take about eight children, 75 seconds each, and write what they say under the drawing. A child who points counts. These stay in the folder for day 155's plan sheet.

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards e and a, cycle 25, lesson 128. Tasks 7 and 8 are left out today.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long e by ear (days 151 and 152). Say bee and green; children say the long sound and hold card e.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say me, he, queen and jeep; after each, children say the long sound and hold card e. Then pairs, 1 minute each way: one says a word, the partner holds the card. Spoken words only.
- Same in the middle, 8 minutes. Heard only; no letter is shown. Say car, star, fork and corn. Children say which two words sound the same in the middle, car and star, then which two are fork and corn. Say each triple again: star, car, corn; fork, corn, star. Children point to the odd word.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Hen Has an Egg and Tree Care Words, verse 25. Review: both from day 151. At your table the group reads page 5 and page 6 of the reader by echo; each child says what Peg has at the end, 15 seconds each; then say verse 25 together. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station, with the time of tasks 7 and 8 given to reading. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Review: like, a mapped word (day 151). Write "I like it," said Peg. on the chart. Count the words, five, and the spaces, four. Each of six children points to I, like or it and says it, 15 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate dog, then bell; children say the sounds and write each on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear the child tell about the card, then the drawing, then say it clearly across the room. Hold e on paper and write dog and bell.

### At home

**Swap.** No finished card: use any drawing. No cards: write e on a paper.

**Skip.** The windows, the gallery and the marks.

- **10 min. Tell.** Your child holds a drawing.
  **Say.** "Tell me about your drawing." Expect: the subject named. Then say, "Tell me one more thing." Expect: a detail.
- **10 min. Show.** Your child points to something in the drawing.
  **Say.** "What does your drawing show that your words do not say?" Expect: a detail named from the picture.
- **10 min. Long e.** Say me, he, queen, jeep. Your child holds up e.
- **10 min. Words.** Write like. Your child says it and points to the part to remember. Dictate dog and bell; your child writes the sounds.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check; cut the gallery to one card. Longer: a secure child tells two details and shows both in the drawing.

### Words and supports

Words: describe, add, show, loud. Frames: "This tree gives ___." and "My drawing shows ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count; a child who is quiet may repeat the idea with a partner. Give one prompt in a window and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

The window marks tell you who named a detail, showed it and was heard. Day 154 takes the other fifteen children in the same windows with the same three asks, and no child performs them twice; draw and dictate takes about eight children who were not heard today. The long e work continues in its Foundations part.
