# Day 152: Find the author's point and reasons on a new card

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the check card, its strips and the purpose card covered. Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the observation sheet, days 152 to 156. From day 151's window marks, note who still said two texts were the same; day 157 takes them. This day has no exit; the C5 and T3 windows are the observation.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: point and reason (days 129 and 130), and path (day 59). Show a picture and a gesture for each of point, reason, path, branch and cart. Say that a cart is a small box on wheels that carries things. Children point to the picture you name, then say it. No sentence of the check card is read.

#### 30 min. Observation windows, C5 and T3

Six groups of five, 5 minutes a group, at your table. The others tell a partner what their finished card shows and add to it. No reteaching. There is no slack: about 275 seconds of asks and 25 for the change of group.

- Card, about 60 seconds. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice, showing the three strips.
- Point and reasons, about 65 seconds. Ask each child in turn [[s04-C5a]], about 5 seconds a child; then each in turn [[s04-C5b]], about 8 seconds. A child may point to a strip.
- Topic, about 110 seconds. Ask each child in turn [[s04-T152a]], about 4 seconds; then [[s04-T152b]] and [[s04-T152c]], about 7 seconds each; then show the two path pictures and ask [[s04-T152d]], about 4 seconds.
- Purpose, about 40 seconds. Give each child the purpose card, reread the card once with the group and ask [[s04-T152e]] of the group.

One prompt: read the card again. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the observation sheet. Signs: a supporting detail given as the point (error B); a liking given as a reason (error C); a detail named as the topic (error AC); no detail retold (error AD); the result turned round (error AE); the blocked path pointed to (error Y); a child who watches the reread (error AF). A child may pass; leave the cells blank. A child not finished is taken in day 153's Reread.

#### 15 min. Reread 03

Whole class. Review: Wangari and the Tree Planters (days 134 to 140). Read it whole, about 410 words, pictures shown. Page 9 says in one plain sentence that Wangari Maathai died in 2011; read it as written. If a child asks how many trees, say "millions of trees" in words, never as a number. Then reread page 8 and point left to right along its five pictures as you say first, next, then, after that, as a result. Partners say what they see in the pictures of pages 5 and 6, 30 seconds each. Show Kenya on the map on page 1 and the women as the ones who plant.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Say the frame: "People ___ together." **Ask.** "What did people do together in 03?" One paired turn of 2 minutes, all pairs at once. Expect: grew seedlings and planted them together. Six children share, 30 seconds each; the rest of the time is a second look at the pictures of pages 5 and 6. A child may pass. No score.

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards e and a and counters, cycle 25, lesson 127.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long e by ear (day 151). Say tree and feet; children say the long sound and hold card e. Then say car and fork; children say each word back and hold no card: the vowel in these words is heard only.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say team, bean, seed and key; after each, children say the long sound and hold card e. Then pairs: one child says a word, the partner holds the card, 1 minute each way. Spoken words only; no word is read.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say pen, hum and lid; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Review: change one letter and say which sound changed (day 85). Change hat to hot and pin to pan; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Hen Has an Egg and Tree Care Words, verse 25, A Visit to Her Tree. Review: both were first used on day 151; egg is a part to remember. At your table read each page and have the group echo it; each child says what Peg did on one page, 15 seconds each; then say verse 25 together. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station; tasks 7 and 8 in place. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Review: she and her, mapped words (day 151). Write "She has her egg." on the chart. Count the words, four, and the spaces, three. Six children each point to she or her and say it, 15 seconds each; the group says egg together as a part to remember.

#### 5 min. Check

Say leaf, then sleep. After each, children say the long sound and point to e on their card. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the card twice and ask the point and the two reasons by pointing. Hold e on paper and read 03 aloud with the pictures.

### At home

**Swap.** No texts in print: describe the page you have. No cards: write e on a paper.

**Skip.** The windows and the marks.

- **10 min. Point and reasons.** Read this card twice: "Put the shoes on the mat. Then the floor stays clean. Then people can walk by."
  **Say.** "What is the writer's point?" Expect: put the shoes on the mat.
  **Say.** "What reasons does the writer give?" Expect: the floor stays clean; people can walk by.
- **10 min. Together.** Tell your child about people who planted trees together.
  **Say.** "What did the people do together?" Expect: grew seedlings and planted them.
- **10 min. Long e.** Say tree, feet, team, key. Your child holds up e.
- **10 min. Words.** Write she and her. Your child says each and points to the part to remember.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check; cut the second look at the pictures. Longer: a secure child says one more thing the people did together.

### Words and supports

Words: point, reason, path, branch, cart. Frames: "The point is ___. A reason is ___." and "People ___ together." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt in a window and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

The window marks tell you who can find a point and its reasons. Day 153 takes any child not finished in its Reread, and the finished cards come out for the first fifteen children's windows. The long e work continues in its Foundations part.
