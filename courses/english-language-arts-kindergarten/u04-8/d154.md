# Day 154: Finish sharing the cards with the same explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the remaining 15 children on the class list in three groups of five on the observation sheet, days 152 to 156; the first 15 were heard on day 153 and do not repeat. Lay out the finished cards. This day has no exit; the S1 windows are the observation. The comparison and author-reason records of days 150 to 152 are reused, not asked again.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: describe, add, show and loud (day 153). Say each with its gesture; children say it back and show it: a describing hand, an adding hand, a pointing finger and a loud voice, then a quiet voice beside a loud one.

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class. Review: Trees Help Our Community and Planting Together (days 129 to 151). Read pages 3 and 5 of the community text and page 8 of the planting text, about 160 words, pictures shown. Then talk about the pictures: partners say one thing a picture shows that the words do not, 30 seconds each, and six children share.

#### 15 min. Observation windows, S1

The remaining 15 children, three groups of five, 5 minutes a group, at your table, each with their own finished card. The first 15 rehearse their share in pairs, then read in the reading corner. No reteaching. There is no slack: about 55 seconds a child and 25 for the change of group.

- Tell, about 25 seconds a child. Say the card's subject, for example "Your card shows a boy under a tree," and ask [[s04-S154a]]. After one pause, say the second prompt.
- Show, about 15 seconds. Point to the drawing and ask [[s04-S154b]].
- Loud, about 15 seconds. The child faces the group and answers [[s04-S154c]]; the group says back the idea.

One prompt a child. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the observation sheet, and name the support used, as on day 153. Signs: a name with no detail after the prompt (error J); a drawing detail the card does not name, such as stars (error K); a voice the group cannot repeat (error L). A child may pass; leave the cells blank.

#### 15 min. Card gallery

Pairs. Review: a gallery visit (days 30 and 153). Stand the cards on tables, 3 minutes. Each pair visits two cards other than their own and other than yesterday's, 1 minute at each, and says to the maker, "Your drawing shows ___." Then six children say one thing a drawing showed, 20 seconds each. Spoken, unscored. If many name a detail the card does not show, hold up one card and name one picture detail beside one the words give.

#### 15 min. Draw and dictate

Individuals. Each child draws one thing they will say about their card for the class audio version, 5 minutes. Then you take about eight children, 75 seconds each, not the eight of day 153, and write what they say under the drawing. A child who points counts. These stay in the folder for day 155's plan sheet.

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards e and a and counters, cycle 25, lesson 129.

- Recall, 4 minutes. Review: long e by ear (days 151 to 153). Say keep and meal; children say the long sound and hold card e.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say leaf, beet, sea and tree; after each, children say the long sound and hold card e. Then pairs, 1 minute each way: one says a word, the partner holds the card. Spoken words only.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Review: counters for the sounds (days 96 to 152). Say bug, fan and wet; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 4 minutes. Review: change one letter and say which sound changed (day 85). Change tap to tip and cut to cot; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Hen Has an Egg and Tree Care Words, verse 25. Review: both from day 151. At your table the group reads pages 3 and 4 of the reader by echo; each child says what Peg said to Tim, 15 seconds each; then say verse 25 together. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station; tasks 7 and 8 in place. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Review: she, her and like, mapped words (day 151). Write "Then Tim ran up to her." on the chart. Count the words, six, and the spaces, five. Each of six children points to then or her and says it, 15 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Check

Say cheap, then mean. After each, children say the long sound and point to e on their card. Mark six on the record sheet, not the six of day 153. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Hear the child tell about the card, then the drawing, then say it clearly across the room. Hold e on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No finished card: use any drawing. No cards: write e on a paper.

**Skip.** The windows, the gallery and the marks.

- **10 min. Tell.** Your child holds a drawing.
  **Say.** "Tell me about your drawing." Expect: the subject named. Then say, "Tell me one more thing." Expect: a detail.
- **10 min. Show.** Your child points to something in the drawing.
  **Say.** "What does your drawing show that your words do not say?" Expect: a detail named from the picture.
- **10 min. Long e.** Say leaf, beet, sea, tree. Your child holds up e.
- **10 min. Words.** Write her and like. Your child says each and points to the part to remember.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the check; cut the gallery to one card. Longer: a secure child tells two details and shows both in the drawing.

### Words and supports

Words: describe, add, show, loud. Frames: "This tree gives ___." and "My drawing adds ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count; a child who is quiet may repeat the idea with a partner. Give one prompt in a window and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

The window marks tell you who named a detail, showed it and was heard. Day 155 makes the class audio version: bring the drawings kept in the folder from days 153 and 154 for the plan sheets, test the recorder before class and find a quiet corner. The long e work continues in its Foundations part, and day 155 holds its cycle check.
