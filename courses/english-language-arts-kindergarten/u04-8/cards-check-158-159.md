# Check cards, days 158 and 159

Teacher only. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice. Nothing is graded." Read each card twice; children do not read it. Print "invented for practice" at its foot. Not shown in teaching.

## Form for day 158

- **Passage card:** Ben has a wet mop. Ben and Tom mop the mud. Tom has a big jug. The child reads it aloud; then ask who has the mop.
- **List of ten mapped words:** a, the, to, do, she, her, and, is, has, I
- **Line of print:** She is to the hut and I do the mud. Her pup has a mop.
- **Information card:** A bat sleeps in a cave. It hangs upside down to rest. A bat can fly at night. Ask: Where does a bat sleep?
- **Spellings:** fog, zig, hub
- **Story card (read twice):** Lin the duck fell in the mud. A goat pulled her out. Lin said thank you. Ask: Tell me one thing that happened.

## Form for day 159

- **Passage card:** Dax has a big tub. Jax has a red mop. Dax and Jax mop. Jax sat on a mat. The child reads it aloud; then ask who has the tub.
- **List of ten mapped words:** a, the, to, do, she, her, and, is, has, I
- **Line of print:** She has a big cup and I do the jog. Her pig is to the hut.
- **Information card:** A snail has a hard shell. It pulls its body into the shell to stay safe. A snail can slide on a trail of slime. Ask: What does a snail pull into its shell?
- **Spellings:** pod, rib, nab
- **Story card (read twice):** Sal the cat sat on a wall. A bee flew by. Sal ran home. Ask: Tell me one thing that happened.
