# Model cards, day 157

The teacher shows a wrong answer beside a right one, on new cases. A puppet says the wrong one. Nothing here is an exit or end card.

- Two texts and what both do: Card 1, "A frog sits on a log." Card 2, "A duck sits on a log." Puppet says "One is green." Teacher says "Both sit on a log."
- A point and a reason: card, "Close the book gently. Pages can tear if they are bent." Puppet says "I like books." Teacher says "Pages can tear if they are bent."
- A reason taken for the point: the same card. Puppet says "The pages." Teacher says "Close the book gently."

Children say which answer says what both do, which gives the writer's reason, and which sentence tells what to do.

These cards are for teaching and may be shown.
