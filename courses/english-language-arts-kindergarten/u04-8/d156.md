# Day 156: Change or keep the audio choice, and answer the long e check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the observation sheet, days 152 to 156. Put each child's written note, plan sheet and ticks W and O together. From day 155's class list pick the commonest of errors AO and AP for the model, and write the repair cards for errors N, AO and AP, each marked "invented for practice". Keep the day 155 recording closed; it is scored as made, not replayed. This day has no exit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: change (day 38), keep (day 22), because (day 43) and same (day 12). Hand-back from day 155's exit, 1 minute. Error N: the puppet says "You choose" when asked which line goes first, "Birds sing in the tree" or "People rest under the tree." Three children choose a line and say why, "I choose this one first because ___"; the rest vote with fingers, one or two. Then say each word with a gesture and children say it back.

#### 10 min. Familiar reread

Whole class. Review: A Tree Beside Our Door and Planting Together (days 119 to 154). Read page 5 of the first and page 4 of the second, about 170 words, pictures shown. Not page 3 or page 9 of the first. No questions; partners say one thing they remember, 20 seconds each.

#### 30 min. Observation windows, P1

Six groups of five, 5 minutes a group, at your table. The others read in the corner and draw. No reteaching. There is no slack: about 55 seconds a child and 25 for the change of group.

- Note, about 10 seconds a child. Read the child's written note and show its picture.
- Change, about 15 seconds. Say: "You may change a word or the order on your plan sheet, or keep them." The child changes the sheet or keeps it; a reason is welcome, not needed.
- Ask, about 30 seconds. Ask [[s04-P156]] of each child in turn.

Score the plan sheet as the child leaves it, the ticks W and O and the day 155 recording as made; do not replay it for a score. A recording made today is practice and is not scored. Mark 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen), naming the support used. Sign: a child who only watched the adults work the recorder (error N). A child may pass; leave the cells blank. Index the day 75 typed story record and the day 155 audio contribution for each child on the sheet; nothing is asked again.

#### 15 min. Gallery reread

Pairs. Each child tells their card to a partner in their own words, then names one detail from A Tree Beside Our Door, Reading Under the Tree or Trees Help Our Community that the card used, 2 minutes each way. Six pairs share, 40 seconds each. Spoken, unscored.

#### 10 min. Foundations response to F7

Whole class, then tables. Review: long e by ear (days 151 to 155); she, her and like (day 151). Say: "This is made up for practice."

- Model, 3 minutes. Show the commoner of the two slips from day 155's list, wrong beside right. If it is error AO: the puppet holds card i for the long e in "seam" and you hold card e. If it is error AP: the puppet reads the card her as "he" and you read her, sounding the regular h and marking the part to remember. Children say which is right.
- Table repair, 3 minutes. Children who erred sit by their error. Error AO table, 1 minute, hand-back: say each long sound and hold card e or card i, then say the word again. Error AP table, 2 minutes, hand-back: sound the regular part and mark the part to remember on the cards.
- Fresh retry, 4 minutes. Error AO, 2 minutes: say seal, soap, weed, hat, beam and bike; children hold card e for the long e and nothing for any other vowel. Error AP, 2 minutes: show "She has her pen. Meg can like it." and each child points to she, her and like and says each as it is printed.

#### 10 min. Retell by ear

Pairs. Reread verse 25 of Tree Care Words twice, the two pages shown. Review: first, then and last (day 51). Each pair retells in their own words what the girl does first, then, and last, pointing to the pictures, 1 minute each way. Spoken; no new pattern.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Hen Has an Egg and the earlier readers, familiar text only. At your table the group reads a page by echo and each child says one thing that happened, 15 seconds each. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Write "Peg has an egg." on the chart. Count the words, four, and the spaces, three. Each of six children points to a word and says it, 15 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate pan, then miss; children say the sounds and write each on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the child's note, let them change or keep the choice, and hold e and i on paper. With no recorder, keep the paper plan sheet; the audio part stays open.

### At home

**Swap.** No plan sheet: use a drawing. No cards: write e and i on two papers.

**Skip.** The windows and the marks.

- **10 min. Change or keep.** Look at your child's drawing from yesterday.
  **Say.** "You may change something or keep it. What do you choose?" Expect: a change or a keep, with or without a reason.
- **10 min. Long e.** Say seal, soap, weed, hat, beam, bike. Your child holds up e for the long e.
- **10 min. Words.** Write she, her and like. Your child reads each. Read "She has her mop. Dan can like it." together.
- **10 min. Retell.** Your child tells what they did today, first, then and last.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the windows and the foundations response; cut the gallery reread to one pair. Longer: a secure child says one more thing they would change and why.

### Words and supports

Words: change, keep, because, same. Frame: "I keep ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt in a window and never the answer; the recording is judged as made.

### Tomorrow

The window marks complete the record of the audio version. The foundations record cites the day 158 or 159 reading record, with no one interviewed again. Day 157 is the last day of the unit. Its Topic response takes errors A and E at one table and errors B and C at the other, from the cards of days 150 to 152, with fresh cases, and it returns to print, letters, rhyme, syllables and sounds.
