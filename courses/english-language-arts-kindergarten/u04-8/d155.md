# Day 155: Make the class audio version, and check long e

## Teacher page

### Before class

Test the recorder and find a quiet corner. Lay out the drawings from day 153 and day 154. Keep the exit card E155 and the F7 card covered. Pick the day's six for the exit, none who need the recorder first. This day has no scored exit; the recording and the plan sheets are kept for day 156.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: publish, said of the class piece (day 75); source (day 92); record, order, first and last (days 39 and 51). Show a gesture for each: a recorder held out, cards in a row, a hand at the front of the line, a hand at the back. Say that to publish is to share what the class made. Children say each word back.

#### 10 min. Reread 02a

Whole class. Review: Trees Help Our Community (days 129 to 148). Read it whole, about 280 words, pictures shown. **Ask.** "Which page gives a source for what you will say?" Each child points to a page or picture in their own copy of the story, or to a partner's; hear six pointings.

#### 10 min. Plan sheets

Individuals, on side 1 of the student page, the audio plan sheet. Each child draws the one card detail they will say, from their drawing of day 153 or 154, and ticks one of three boxes, first, middle or last, for their place in the order. You write the child's words under the drawing from dictation for about eight children; the rest say them to a partner. The children choose both. If more than ten choose one place, those children choose again between the other two. Tick W (the child chose the wording) and O (the child chose the place) on the class list as each child decides.

#### 25 min. Record the class audio version

The teacher holds the recorder in the quiet corner. Thirty turns of 45 seconds are 22.5 minutes and thirty changes of about 5 seconds are 2 minutes 30 seconds: 25 minutes, with no slack. Call the children in the order of their places, first to last, with the next child standing ready. Each child says their words from the plan sheet. Give no prompt and no replay; a child may pass and record on day 156 as practice. The others work quietly at their tables on their cards or read in the reading corner. The recording stays on the recorder; no one uploads it. Mark on the class list who spoke in their own place.

#### 5 min. Exit E155

Individuals, the day's six, 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes; the others draw. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read E155 twice and show cards A and B, then ask [[s04-X155]]. Unscored. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: a child who waits for you to choose (error N).

#### 5 min. Listen back

Whole class. Play the first minute of the recording. Each child says one thing they heard to a partner, 20 seconds each way; six children share.

#### 20 min. Sounds and spelling

Pairs with cards e and i and counters, cycle 25, lesson 130, the last of the cycle. No new pattern before F7.

- Recall, 5 minutes. Review: long e by ear (days 151 to 154). Say seed and sea; children say the long sound and hold card e.
- Spoken words, 8 minutes. Say week, seat, bee and deep; after each, children say the long sound and hold card e. Then pairs, 1 minute each way: one says a word, the partner holds the card.
- Counters, 4 minutes. Say map, red and bus; each child moves a counter for each of the three sounds and says them.
- Change one letter, 3 minutes. Review: change one letter and say which sound changed (day 85). Change bed to bad and mop to map; children say which sound changed.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading and F7 words

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Hen Has an Egg and Tree Care Words, verse 25. At your table, 3 minutes: show the printed cards she, her and like and ask [[s04-F155b]] of each child in turn, about 30 seconds a child, no feedback. Then 2 minutes on the reader, tasks 1 and 2. Stations 2 to 5: tasks 3 to 6, one task at each. F7 replaces tasks 7 and 8. No timer is shown. Signs: another word read for a mapped word (error AP).

#### 10 min. F7 matching

Whole class with card e. Say the twelve words of the F7 card one at a time, with a pause: children hold up card e for the long e they hear, and hold nothing for any other vowel. Scan the room and write on the class list who holds e wrongly (error AO). Then say the long sound e alone and ask [[s04-F155a]]: each child points to the card e or the card i. Mark the whole class on the record sheet. No feedback today.

#### 5 min. Check

Whole class. Each child tells a partner what they chose today, "I chose my words and my place," 1 minute each way. Look at the class list and write who held the card wrongly or misread a word, for day 156.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Record the child's lines on a phone or any recorder, then play them back. Hold e and i on paper. With no recorder, keep the plan sheet and say the lines aloud; the audio part stays open.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: say the lines aloud to a listener. No cards: write e and i on two papers.

**Skip.** The class recording, the marks and the station reading.

- **10 min. Choose.** Your child looks at a drawing of a tree.
  **Say.** "What will you tell people about your tree?" Expect: a detail from the drawing. Then ask, "Do you want to go first or last?" Expect: a choice.
- **10 min. Say it.** Record your child's lines on a phone, or listen. Say nothing while they speak.
- **10 min. Long e.** Say week, seat, bee, deep. Your child holds up e. Point to e or i for the long sound e.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card twice, then ask [[s04-X155]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the recording and the F7 matching; cut Listen back to 20 seconds. Longer: a secure child says a second detail from the community text.

### Words and supports

Words: record, order, first, last, source, publish. Frames: "I will say ___." and "I want to go ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count; a quiet seat near the recorder helps a child who speaks softly. The recording is judged as made; no child is scored on a replay.

### Tomorrow

The class list shows who chose their words and their place and who held the card wrongly. Day 156 begins with a minute for error N, three children choosing which of two lines goes first and the rest voting, and its foundations response takes errors AO and AP, 2 minutes each, with new words and a new sentence. The record of this cycle goes to day 158 or 159 without another interview. After class write a note for each child, a picture and a few words about one thing done well, about 30 notes; day 156 reads each to its child.
