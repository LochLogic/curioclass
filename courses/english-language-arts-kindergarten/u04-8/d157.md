# Day 157: Respond to the closing checks with a fresh card and a fresh comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 156's windows finished the audio record. Read the C3, C4 and C5 results. List who made error A or E and who made error B or C; pick the day's six, children from those lists first, then by rotation. Write the repair cards from the part below. Keep the E157a pictures face down; mark the exit pages. The retry is unscored practice for the unit record; it does not change the window marks.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words return

Review: author (day 2), point and reason (days 129 and 130), both and different (days 125 and 131). Show a picture or gesture for each. Children point to the one you name, then say it.

#### 15 min. Topic response, E157

Whole class, then two tables, then the day's six. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

- Model, 5 minutes. Show a wrong answer beside a right one, then the model cards. Card 1 "A frog sits on a log." and card 2 "A duck sits on a log." The puppet says, "One is green." Say: "Both sit on a log." Read "Close the book gently. Pages can tear if they are bent." The puppet says, "I like books." Say: "Pages can tear if they are bent." The puppet says, "The pages." Say: "Close the book gently." Children say which answer says what both do, which gives the writer's reason and which tells what to do.
- Repair, 5 minutes, two parts of 2.5 minutes, from the results of days 150 to 152. You take one table for errors A and E while the others sort picture cards, then the other table for errors B and C. Error A, 1 minute, hand-back from day 150's C3: read "Ruth stacks blocks in a red shirt. Omar stacks cups in a blue shirt." The puppet says, "One is red and one is blue." Say: "Both stack." Error E, 1 minute, hand-back from day 151's C4: show "Pia carries a pail to the gate." beside "Pia carries a pail to the barn." The puppet says, "They say the same thing." Each child says one difference: to the gate, to the barn. Error B, 1 minute, hand-back from day 152's C5 point: read "Hang the coat on the hook. Then it will not fall." The puppet says, "It will not fall." Ask which sentence tells what to do; each says, "Hang the coat on the hook." Error C, 1 minute, hand-back from day 152's C5 reasons: the same card; the puppet says, "I like coats." Say: "Find the sentence that says why." Each says, "Then it will not fall." The 15 minutes hold one repair; a table with more than five hears five, and the rest wait for the retry.
- Retry, 5 minutes. Individuals, the day's six, in two parts of 2.5 minutes. First read page 10 of each story aloud again and show the three pictures of E157a, then ask [[s04-X157av2]] of each child, about 20 seconds each. Then read page 8 of Planting Together aloud again and ask [[s04-X157bv2]] of each child, about 20 seconds each. The others sort picture cards. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: the picture of two children in coats (error A); a liking given as the author's reason (error C).

#### 15 min. Reread 02a

Whole class. Review: Trees Help Our Community (days 129 to 155). Read it whole, about 280 words, pictures shown. **Ask.** "What does the author claim?" Expect: trees help our community; children point to page 1 or page 7. **Ask.** "Which stated reasons support it?" Expect: shaded places to rest and shelter for birds; children point to pages 3 and 5. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Say the frames: "The point is ___. A reason is ___." Paired turns of 2 minutes, all pairs at once. **Ask.** "What is the author's point?" Expect: trees help our community. Six children share, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "Say one reason." Expect: they give shade, or they give birds shelter. Six more share, 30 seconds each. The last 4 minutes: a second look at pages 3 and 5; children point to the sentence that holds each reason. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Draw and dictate

Individuals. Each child draws the author's point and one reason on the strip and dictates one sentence. You take about eight children, 75 seconds each. A child who points counts. Scored from the work you handed in.

#### 20 min. Retrieval, sounds and print

Pairs, 10 minutes each. Review: print and letters (units 1 to 3 and the cycles so far); no new pattern.

- First, 10 minutes. Follow a finger left to right, top to bottom and page by page along the chart; find the spaces between words. Then pairs with the letter cards V v, Z z, W w, R r, H h, N n, P p and B b: match each capital to its small letter and name both. You listen to six children, 20 seconds each.
- Second, 10 minutes. Each child prints three of the letters. Say web, rob, hot and tub; children write a letter for the first sound of each. Say cap, lap and rug; children say which two rhyme, then give another word that rhymes with cap. Clap pencil, then ant, and say how many claps. Say h-ot and t-ub; children blend each. You listen to six, new to you today.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Five stations of 5 minutes, five groups of six. A Hen Has an Egg and the earlier readers, familiar text only. Review: all were read in cycles 19 to 25. At your table the group reads a page by echo and each child says one thing that happened, 15 seconds each. Tasks 1 to 6 at every station. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Review: she, her and like (days 151 to 156). Write "She has her egg. I like it." on the chart. Count the words, seven, and the spaces, six. Each of six children points to she, her or like and says it, 15 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Check

Dictate rib, then buzz; children say the sounds and write each on the whiteboard. Mark six on the record sheet. Do not reteach.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the model, the repair for the error you saw, the retry alone and the letters on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the cards on scrap paper. No letter cards: write the letters on paper.

**Skip.** The tables, the stations and the marks.

- **10 min. Point and reason.** Read this card twice: "Wipe your feet on the mat. Then the floor stays clean."
  **Say.** "What does the writer want?" Expect: wipe your feet on the mat.
  **Say.** "Say a reason the writer gives." Expect: the floor stays clean.
- **10 min. Both.** Read "A cat naps on the rug." and "A dog naps on the rug."
  **Say.** "What do both do?" Expect: nap on the rug.
- **10 min. Letters.** Write R r and H h. Your child names each. Say web, rob, hot, tub; your child writes the first letter.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X157av2]] Read page 10 of each story aloud and ask what Lina and Ben both do. Then [[s04-X157bv2]] read page 8 of Planting Together and ask the reason for watering again and again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retry, the reread and the check; cut the second paired turn. Longer: a secure child says a second reason and the page it is on.

### Words and supports

Words: author, point, reason, both, different. Frames: "The point is ___. A reason is ___." "Both ___. But ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit card twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say what two texts share and the writer's reason; the marks go on the unit 4 record sheet. This is the last day of the unit. Day 158 begins the year-end reading and information battery for the first fifteen children and carries these records.
