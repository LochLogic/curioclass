# F7 card, day 155: twelve spoken words

Said aloud by the teacher, one at a time, mixed. Children hold up the card e for the long e heard, or hold nothing for another vowel. None of these words was used on days 151 to 154.

peach, cake, sheep, rope, creek, pig, speed, pond, teeth, cup, wheel, kite
