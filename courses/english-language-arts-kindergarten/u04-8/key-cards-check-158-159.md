# Key for the check cards, days 158 and 159 (teacher only)

Never print this page for children. The cards are in cards-check-158-159.md.

## Day 158
- Passage card (Ben has a wet mop): who has the mop? Key: Ben.
- Information card (a bat): where does a bat sleep? Key: In a cave.

## Day 159
- Passage card (Dax has a big tub): who has the tub? Key: Dax.
- Information card (a snail): what does a snail pull into its shell? Key: Its body.
