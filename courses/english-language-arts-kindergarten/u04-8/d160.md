# Day 160: Respond to the year-end check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet from day 158's assessment and day 159's assessment. Make four small groups by what it shows: a vowel linked to the wrong sound (error K), a spelling with a sound left out (error L), a word guessed and not sounded out (error M), a detail named as the topic (error N). A child with no need joins the reread group. Keep the fresh retry cards covered.

### The day

#### 10 min. Retrieval and sounds

Whole class. Say a short and a long vowel, then three words to blend. Hold up a card for each vowel as you say it. Nothing is marked.

#### 40 min. Response groups

Four groups, 10 minutes each at your table; the others reread familiar readers in partners. Name the group's slip and repair it with a model, one card at a time. Review: the repair routines of day 158 and day 159, each of 1 minute. Vowels: say a short and a long word; the child holds the card for each (error K). Spelling: a counter for each sound, then a letter for each (error L). Sounding out: cover the picture and blend left to right (error M). Topic: two cards, a whole picture and a part; the child points to the topic (error N), and hear the card again to draw the animal it is about (error O). A child whose changed sound is missed points to the letter that is different (error P).

#### 20 min. Fresh retry

Each group, in turn, five minutes: new cards, one ask each, no marks. Ask [[c2-X160a]], then [[c2-X160b]], then [[c2-X160c]], then [[c2-X160d]], then [[c2-X160e]]. Say nothing is marked; write on the sheet what changed since day 158 or day 159: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. One prompt.

#### 20 min. Reread informational card

Whole class. Reread a familiar informational card twice; partners say the topic and one detail, 30 seconds each. Hear six pairs.

#### 15 min. Letters and words

Pairs with cards. Match each vowel to its short and long sound and spell one word by sound. Hear six pairs.

#### 10 min. Draw and tell

Each child draws an animal and tells a partner one thing about it. No score.

#### 5 min. Records

Write the grade 1 handoff: letters and sounds, sounds in words, decoding, mapped words, understanding of texts, writing and digital participation, with what is still needed and what the fresh retry changed. The family letter goes with it. Nothing here is a second end-of-year assessment.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the group; take the four repairs in turn, each 3 minutes. No cards: draw them by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons or beans.

**Skip.** The group reread and the sheet.

- **20 min. Day 158's and day 159's assessment.** Ask one question from each part your child met: read three small words, say the short and long sound of a vowel, write one word by sound, say what a short card is about. Do not help; write what you saw.
- **5 min. Say it.**
  **Say.** "What do you remember of this story or card?" Expect: one thing it said.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws an animal and says one thing about it.
- **10 min. Reading.** Your child reads a short line they know.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the response groups and the fresh retry; cut the reread. Longer: a secure child writes two words by sound.

### Words and supports

Words: vowel, spell, topic, detail, sound out. Frame: "The card is about ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

There is no day after this one in kindergarten. The handoff goes to the grade 1 teacher with day 158's assessment, day 159's assessment and what the retry changed, beside the grade 1 baseline.
