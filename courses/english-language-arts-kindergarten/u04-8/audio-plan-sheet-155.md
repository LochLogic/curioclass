# Audio plan sheet, days 155 and 156

One sheet for each child, in two parts: the detail of the finished card the child will say (a small picture, with the words the teacher writes from dictation) and a row of three boxes, first, middle and last, for the place the child chooses in the order.

The teacher ticks W (the wording was chosen by the child) and O (the order was chosen by the child) on the class list as each child decides. The sheet is kept with the recording. On day 156 the child may change a wording or the order after hearing the written feedback; an unchanged choice with a reason is allowed.

A room with nothing: draw the boxes by hand on a sheet and keep it.
