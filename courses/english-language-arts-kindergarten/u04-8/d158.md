# Day 158: Year-end reading and information, first half

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the check cards covered. List the first fifteen children in hearing order; the other half does familiar practice. Carry, without asking again, the day 150 and day 155 records, the day 77 or 78 consonant record, the day 142 opinion and the day 75 and 155 records; a child absent then is asked today in spare minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Set-up and records

Say the cards are made up for practice and nothing is graded. Seat the first fifteen in waiting seats; the rest go to their tables. Say each child has a turn of five minutes and may pass any ask.

#### 15 min. Whole-group information card

Whole class, the first fifteen answering. Read the information card twice. Ask [[c2-Y10]] and [[c2-Y11]]; children say it to a partner and show it by pointing to the picture; hear six, 20 seconds each. Then [[c2-Y12]], then [[c2-Y13]] with partners asking, then [[c2-Y14]]. Give each child paper for [[c2-Y16]]: draw the animal and say or dictate a topic and one thing the card says, about 5 minutes. Mark each child on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. One prompt: read it again.

#### 75 min. Individual conferences

Fifteen children, five minutes each. About 290 seconds a child: the passage card, [[c2-Y01]], 60 seconds, then [[c2-Y02]], 15 seconds; vowel cards a and i, [[c2-Y03]], 20 seconds; mapped words, [[c2-Y04]] in a list and [[c2-Y05]] in a line, 80 seconds; one-letter change, [[c2-Y06]], 25 seconds; three spellings, [[c2-Y07]], [[c2-Y08]] and [[c2-Y09]], 60 seconds; a short story card read twice, [[c2-Y15]], 30 seconds. The change of child takes the rest. Stop a section after five misses in a row. Give no model and no answer. Signs: a short vowel given its long sound (error K); a spelling with a sound left out (error L); a word guessed from its first letter or the picture (error M); a detail named as the topic (error N); the answer drawn or said about something else (error O); the changed sound missed (error P); the text replaced by what the child knows (error J).

#### 15 min. Familiar practice for the others

The rest of the class, in partners: reread a familiar reader, sort word cards or draw. An adult or an older child keeps the room. No new teaching.

#### 10 min. Records

Write on the sheet which children need a short second hearing and which parts they passed. Day 157's exit gave who can say what two texts share; its marks stay on the unit 4 record sheet. Collect the student pages at the end of this part. Day 160 reads the sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the battery in about 12 minutes; ask the group asks one at a time. No printer: write the cards by hand, large.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: say the words aloud and have your child write them.

**Skip.** The group card and the record sheet.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Show a short line of three-letter words and ask your child to read it, then say three words for your child to write by sound, then ask what a short read-aloud card is about. Do not help; write what you saw.
- **10 min. The two sounds.**
  **Say.** "What is the short sound of a? Its long sound?" Expect: the short sound, then the name. Say a letter's short sound, then its long sound, for a and i.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws an animal and says one thing about it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the passage, the spellings and the topic; do the rest in spare minutes. Longer: a child at ease reads the passage twice and says what the animal does.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, detail, vowel, mapped, spell. Frame: "The card is about ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 159 hears the other fifteen on the same battery with different cards. Day 158's assessment goes to day 160 with day 159's.
