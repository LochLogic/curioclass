# Day 122: A second story, and its map

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the day's six for the map telling and the exit, and six other children for the reading, all not yet heard. Read the record sheet and name whether error U showed on day 121's exit. Hang the large story map. Keep strip E122, its picture cards and its map face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

Whole class. 3 min, the repair for day 121's exit: if many chose a picture of an event the words do not tell (error U), read the sentence from page 4 of A Tree Beside Our Door, "The soil around the trunk looked pale and cracked." The puppet holds up a picture of Lina saying hello to her tree and says it shows the sentence. Find the sentence first: it tells about soil, not a hello. Then show the cracked soil. If few missed, use one pair. 2 min: say and echo with pictures bench (a seat for two or three), trunk (the thick middle of a tree) and bark (the tree's rough skin). Review: trunk, from asking about it in unit 3.

#### 20 min. Read-aloud, the whole second story

Whole class on the rug. Read Reading Under the Tree in one reading, about 520 words. Pause at the pictures of pages 4 to 7. Read pages 3 and 9 straight through, with no pause, question or comparison. Content: a loud work truck and machine near the school fence; nobody is hurt; Ben covers his ears, then moves. Tell the class first that the story is about a school tree.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then partners. Three asks, one each, one turn each, 30 seconds a turn, all pairs at once, then three pairs answer. **Ask.** "What interrupts Ben's reading?" Expect: the loud truck and machine. **Ask.** "What does Ben do?" Expect: he walks around the trunk to a quieter place. **Ask.** "How does he feel after?" Expect: calm, happy or glad. Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Story map

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the three-box map (unit 2; day 120). 3 min: on the large map, draw each box as you say it. Who: Ben. Where: the big school tree beside the path. What happens: the noise comes and Ben moves to the quiet side. 12 min: each pair has a blank map. One child draws who and where, the other draws what happens, then each says their boxes to the other. Hear the day's six say a map, about a minute each, and name them in the record column. Look for who, where and what changes. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Partner share and tidy

Partners. Each says, "My map shows ___." One turn each, then tidy.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 7 aloud again, show the three picture cards of E122 and the three-box map, then ask [[s04-X122v2]], 30 seconds each, one child at a time, about three minutes. The others draw their favorite part of Ben's story on the back of side 1. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: the who box empty (error Z); the where box empty (error AA); the roots card in the what-happens box (error AB).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 19, lesson 99: short vowels and digraphs)

Whole class, counters and tiles. Review: cot to cut (day 121), one counter exchanged, the changed sound said before and after. Practice: say jug, push three counters, exchange the middle counter and say jag. Name the sounds, /u/ and /a/, and spell jag with tiles. 6 min: read three new sentences together, a finger under each word and a pause at each space: "Chad got a log from Josh." "Beth will have a chat with Mom." "Tom will rush up the path." Retrieve of, have and from, each read in a sentence before alone. Signs: guesses from the picture (error O); drops a sound (error P); takes a sound away (error R).

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Groups of six, then pairs. Reread A Hen Can Nap with a new question: "What can Peg do in the shed?" Only assignments 1 to 6 today; the saved time goes to more reading, with no standalone isolated tasks. The day's six read with you for 10 minutes, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task, "Point to the two letters that make one sound in shed." The other four groups partner-read, picture covered, and do assignments 1 to 4 on cards: find what Peg does, choose the matching sentence from two, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers the new question. Assignments 5 and 6 use the verse in the next part. Name the six in the record column. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Reread Tree Care Words, verse 1, by echo, a finger under each line. Assignment 5: **Ask.** "Which line gives the order: first, then, last?" Children point. Assignment 6: model one detail that adds to the reader, "You wait for the water to sink", then each child tells a partner another. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, "Last, I wait for the last of it to sink.", a space between words, and each child writes jag by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. More reading

Pairs. The saved check minutes go to a second reading of A Hen Can Nap for meaning, picture covered, one page each, then each says what happened. Assignments 1 to 6 only.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the story once, ask the three questions, draw the map together and take the exit card alone. Use the one child as the six in every part. One projector: show a picture alone; the map is drawn on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons. No tiles: write letters on paper squares.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse; read the story only for the exit.

- **10 min. Map.** Your child chooses a story or film they know and draws one box of a map: who, where or what happens.
  **Say.** "Who is in it? Where is it? What changes?" Expect: a person, a place and one change.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say jug. Your child pushes a button for each sound, changes the middle sound to make jag and writes it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X122v2]] Read page 7 of Reading Under the Tree aloud, then ask who, where and what happens.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the map, the exit and the sound change; cut the partner share and the more-reading minutes. Longer: a secure child draws Ben's whole map alone.

### Words and supports

Words: bench, trunk, bark. Frame: "My map shows ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can place who, where and what happens. If many left the person (error Z), the place (error AA) or the change (error AB) out, day 123 opens with five minutes: an actor card beside the event card, the place words found, two event pictures in order. Day 123 puts the two stories side by side and begins Both ... but ....
