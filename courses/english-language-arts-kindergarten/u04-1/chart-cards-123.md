# Two-column chart cards, day 123

Plain picture cards for the chart of the two stories, one picture each, no printed word. Two columns, one for A Tree Beside Our Door and one for Reading Under the Tree, with a shared row between them.

- Shared row: a tree with a child beside it (both enjoy a tree); a plan with an arrow turned aside (both have a plan that changes).
- First story column: Lina watering with a can; rain falling on the wet soil.
- Second story column: Ben brushing twigs off his bench; a loud truck near a fence.
- One wrong-example card, shown last and set aside: two children each in a coat, one blue and one red. Say: "Someone might say Both have a coat, but this tells us nothing about the tree."

The frame is spoken: "Both ___. But ___ and ___." The teacher says it twice, then the class together.
