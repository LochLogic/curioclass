# Picture and caption pair, day 121

Say aloud: "This picture and caption are made up for practice."

Draw one picture: a fox curled up asleep under a big tree, with a cool dark patch of shade under the branches and bright sun all around it. Under it, the teacher reads one caption: "A fox naps in the cool shade under a big tree."

Ask: "What does the picture show about the words?" Children point to the part of the picture that shows the shade and the sleeping fox. Then change one thing, for example the fox awake in the sun, and ask whether the picture still shows the caption.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card.
