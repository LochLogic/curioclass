# Day 121: Match the picture to the words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the day's six for the dictation and the exit, and six other children for the reading and the sound check, all not yet heard. Read the record sheet and name which of errors Z, AA and AB day 120's exit showed. Lay out the two pictures, the caption pair and the repair cards. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

Whole class. 4 min, the repair for day 120's exit, on Lina's story: if many left out the person (error Z), put the actor card (Lina) beside the event card (rain) and say who the rain changes; if the place (error AA), find the place words, "beside the tree", and set the place card; if the change (error AB), put two event pictures in order, Lina at her window hearing rain, then Lina at the wet soil. If few missed, use one pair. 1 min: say and echo with pictures soak (new: water goes all the way in) and puddle (Review: you asked about puddle in unit 2).

#### 15 min. Reread, the rain moment

Whole class on the rug. Read pages 6 and 7 of A Tree Beside Our Door, about 170 words, twice. First for the story. Then slowly for one line, "The rain already gave the tree its drink." **Ask.** "What makes the watering unnecessary?" Expect: the rain already watered the tree, and the soil is wet. Page 3 stays closed.

#### 15 min. Match the picture to the words

Whole class. 10 min: start from the sentence, never from the picture. Say, "A picture shows one moment of the words." Reread the sentence from page 7. The puppet holds up the sunny watering scene and says it shows that sentence. **Ask.** "Do these words tell about watering?" Children give thumbs. Expect: no, the words say the rain already gave the drink, so the picture shows an event the words do not tell (error U). Then show the wet soil and the empty can and read the sentence once more: **Ask.** "Do these words tell about this picture?" Expect: yes, the soil is wet and the can is empty. 5 min, a new case, invented for practice, said aloud: show the picture and caption pair of the napping fox. **Ask.** "What does the picture show about the words?" Children point to the shade and the sleeping fox. Then change one thing, the fox awake in the sun, and ask whether the picture still shows the words.

#### 20 min. Draw and dictate

Individuals. Each child draws on side 1 the moment the sentence tells, "The rain already gave the tree its drink." Hear the day's six say "My picture shows ___.", about 2 minutes each, and write one line for each. Name them in the record column. The others label the picture with letters they know. Look for the event in the words, not a sunny watering scene. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E121 twice, show its three pictures, then ask [[s04-X121]], 30 seconds each, one child at a time, about three minutes. The others finish their drawing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: points to a picture of an event the words do not tell (error U).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 19, lesson 98: short vowels and digraphs)

Whole class, counters and tiles. Review: tin to ten (day 120), one counter exchanged and the changed sound said before and after. Practise: say cot, push three counters, exchange the middle counter and say cut. Name the sounds, /o/ and /u/, and spell cut with tiles. 6 min: read three new sentences together, a finger under each word and a pause at each space: "Josh will have a bath." "Chet has a job from Mom." "Mom will not rush Josh." Retrieve of, have and from, each read in a sentence before alone. Signs: guesses from the picture (error O); drops a sound (error P); takes a sound away when asked to change it (error R).

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Groups of six, then pairs. Reread A Hen Can Nap with a new question: "What does Peg do in the shed?" The day's six read with you for 10 minutes, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one isolated task. Assignment 7, without text: say "us", add /b/, say bus. Assignment 8: read of, have or from from a card, then spell cut with tiles. The other four groups partner-read, picture covered, and do assignments 1 to 4 on cards: find what Peg does, choose the matching sentence from two, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers the new question. Assignments 5 and 6 use the verse in the next part. Name the six in the record column. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Reread Tree Care Words, verse 1, by echo, a finger under each line. Assignment 5: **Ask.** "Which line gives the last action?" Children point. Assignment 6: model one detail the reader lacks, "Roots take in the water down in the dark", then each child tells a partner another. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, "Down in the dark, the roots have a drink.", a space between words, and each child writes cut by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Whole class, counters in hand; six children heard. Say zip; each of the six says the middle sound and pushes a counter for each sound, 30 seconds each. Unscored; the record column notes shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread pages 6 and 7, work the sentence and the two pictures, draw together and take the exit card alone. Use the one child as the six in every part. One projector: show a picture alone, never with its words; the cards are drawn on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons. No tiles: write letters on paper squares.

**Skip.** The story, the reader and the verse.

- **10 min. Words and picture.** Say, "A bird sits on a branch." Your child draws it.
  **Say.** "Does your picture show my words?" Expect: yes, a bird and a branch.
  **Say.** "What would change if the bird flew away?" Expect: the bird is gone.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say cot. Your child pushes a button for each sound, changes the middle sound to make cut and writes it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, show the three pictures, then ask [[s04-X121]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sentence work, the exit and the sound change; cut the caption pair and the third sentence. Longer: a secure child draws a second sentence from page 6.

### Words and supports

Words: soak, puddle. Frame: "My picture shows ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can find the words before they choose the picture. If many chose a picture of an event the words do not tell (error U), day 122 opens with five minutes: find the sentence first, then choose the picture. Day 122 reads a second story whole, Reading Under the Tree.
