# Day 119: Why do people care for trees?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the day's six for the opinion dictation and the exit, and six other children for the reading and the sound check; all twelve not yet heard. Cover the words on page 4's picture. Mark page 1 for sheet E119 and put the Nell card face down. Set the closing chart and the three wonderings from day 118, and the unit 1 opinion card, on the rug. Cut the three cards: of, have, from.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and hook

Whole class. Read aloud the three wonderings you wrote on day 118 about caring for trees, and say today we start to find out. Then show the picture of dry, cracked soil by a young tree. **Ask.** "What do you notice?" Take three answers. Expect: dry ground, cracks, a small tree. Say and echo each word with a picture: notice (Review: you learned notice in unit 2; it means look and listen closely), care (Review: you saw care on the last day of unit 3; two cupped hands, look after) and soil (new: the ground a tree's roots grow in).

#### 15 min. Read-aloud, the opening

Whole class on the rug. Read A Tree Beside Our Door, pages 1 to 5, about 280 words, showing each picture when the page ends. Pause only at the pictures of pages 4 and 5. Read page 3 straight through with no pause and no question; nobody discusses it. Then ask the unit's question for spoken answers only: **Ask.** "Why do people care for trees?" Take three answers, write none, and say we will keep asking it. Any child may pass. The tree is outside Lina's apartment building; our own trees are at school or in a class pot.

#### 5 min. Retrieval (unmarked)

Whole class. Review: what a tree needs (the closing chart, day 118) and an opinion that names a topic and a preference (unit 1, opinion card). **Ask.** "Point to a need on the closing chart." Three children point and say it. **Ask.** "Hold up the opinion card: say what it is about and what you like." Three children say it. Nothing is recorded. If a child says only the topic, say the whole sentence back with "I like" and move on.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then partners. Review: ask and answer about a detail (unit 3). Reread page 4 aloud. **Ask.** "What does Lina notice?" Children point to the picture, then say it to a partner, one turn each, 30 seconds a turn, all pairs at once. Expect: the soil is dry and cracked. Reread the last paragraph of page 5. **Ask.** "Which words explain her plan to look after the tree?" Same turns. Expect: they will fill the can and water the tree together, tomorrow morning. Take answers from three pairs after each ask. Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Draw and dictate an opinion

Whole class, then individuals. 3 min: model "I like ___ in this story." Draw the pink blossoms of page 2 on chart paper and say, "I like the pink blossoms in A Tree Beside Our Door." Show the story named and the "I like". Then 12 min: each child chooses a favorite part from page 1, 2, 4 or 5, draws it on side 1 and tells a partner. Keep page 3 closed. Take dictation from the day's six, about 2 minutes each, during the drawing, and name them in the record column. The others write the letters they know under the drawing. Look for three things: the story or tree is named, a preference is stated, the drawing shows what the sentence says. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 1 aloud again (sheet E119) and ask [[s04-X119av2]]. Then say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read the Nell card twice and ask [[s04-X119b]]. 20 seconds an ask, one child at a time, about four minutes. The others draw their favorite tree on the back of side 1. Mark each of the six shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: says water (error V); says only the class plant (error F); says "She likes it" (error G).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 19, lesson 96: short vowels and digraphs)

Whole class, counters and tiles. Review: a counter for each sound (unit 2), all short vowels (cycle 18, days 112 to 114) and sh, th and ch (unit 3, days 80 to 82). 8 min, modeled today and practiced tomorrow: say dig, push a counter for each sound, /d/ /i/ /g/, and say the middle sound. Cover the picture and read the tiles d i g from their sounds. Write dig by sounds on the board. Then change dig to dog by exchanging the middle tile, and say the sound before and after: /i/, then /o/. Only the middle sound changes. 6 min: read three sentences together with a finger under each word and a pause at each space: "Tom got a lid from Dad." "Jen has a tub of gum." "Chip will dig with Josh." Chip and Josh each begin and end one sound with two letters. 6 min: map of, have and from. Mark the regular letters and the part to remember: of, the o is regular and the f says /v/; have, the e at the end says nothing; from, the o says /u/. Read each in a sentence before alone. Signs: guesses a printed word from the picture (error O); writes dg for dig (error P).

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Groups of six, then pairs. First use: A Hen Can Nap; the adult reads the title. The day's six read with you for 10 minutes, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question ("Where did Peg go?"), one isolated task. Assignment 7, without text: say "in", add /f/, say fin. Assignment 8: read of, have or from from a card, then spell dig with tiles. The other four groups partner-read, picture covered, then do assignments 1 to 4 on cards: find where Peg goes, choose the matching sentence from two, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers "Where does Peg nap?" Assignments 5 and 6 use the verse and wait for the next part. Name the six in the record column. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. First heard today: Tree Care Words, verse 1, A Drink for the Tree. Read it twice. The adult reads the rich language. Assignment 5: **Ask.** "Which line gives the first action?" Children point to First. Assignment 6: model one verse detail, "You touch the soil to see if it is dry", then each child tells a partner a different detail. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, "First, I touch the soil.", a space between words, and each child writes dig by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Whole class, counters in hand; six children heard. Say jet; each of the six says the middle sound and pushes a counter for each sound, 30 seconds each. Unscored; the record column notes shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read pages 1 to 5, ask the two questions, take the dictated opinion and the exit card alone, and use the one child as the six in every part. One projector: show a picture alone, never the words; the chart is drawn on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons. No tiles: write letters on paper squares.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse; read the story only for the exit.

- **10 min. Talk.** Look at a plant, a tree or a picture of one together.
  **Say.** "What do you notice?" Expect: the leaves, the soil or its color.
  **Say.** "Say one thing you like about it. I like ___." Expect: a topic and a liking.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say dig. Your child pushes a button for each sound and writes a letter for each.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X119av2]] Read page 1 of A Tree Beside Our Door aloud and ask why Lina stops by her tree. Then read the Nell card twice and ask [[s04-X119b]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exit, the dictation and the sound change; cut the second reading of the verse and the sentences. Longer: a secure child draws a second favorite part and says what it shows.

### Words and supports

Words: notice, care, soil. Frame: "I like ___ in this story." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can give a reason from a page and can say what an opinion is about and what is liked. If many said only the class plant (error F) or "She likes it" (error G), day 120 opens with five minutes: re-model one card, name the topic, then say "I like". Day 120 reads the whole story and starts the story map.
