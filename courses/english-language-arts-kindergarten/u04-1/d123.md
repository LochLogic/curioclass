# Day 123: Both ... but ..., and the cycle 19 check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the day's six for the exit. The check hears every child once, in the rotation, so list all thirty in the record column. Read the record sheet and name which of errors Z, AA and AB day 122's exit showed. Hang the chart with its two columns and shared row. Print the three check pages large and keep them covered.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

Whole class. 4 min, the repair for day 122's exit, on Ben's story: if many left out the person (error Z), put the Ben card beside the noise picture; if the place (error AA), find the place words, "the big school tree", and set the tree card; if the change (error AB), put the noise picture and the move card in order. If few missed, use one pair. 1 min: the words both and but, said with two hands, both hands up, then one hand tipped for but. Review: same and different (unit 1) and compare (unit 2).

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class on the rug. Read the rain moment, pages 6 and 7 of A Tree Beside Our Door, then the noise moment, pages 4 to 6 of Reading Under the Tree. Two short readings, about 3 minutes each. Pages 3 and 9 of both stay closed.

#### 15 min. Model two columns and Both ... but ...

Whole class. Show the chart: one column for each story and a shared row. Review: compare (unit 2). Put each card where it belongs and say it. Shared row: a child beside a tree (both enjoy a tree) and a plan turned aside (both have a plan that changes). First column: Lina watering, then rain on the wet soil. Second column: Ben brushing twigs off his bench, then a loud truck near a fence. Say the frame twice yourself: "Both enjoy a tree. But rain changes Lina's plan and noise changes Ben's." Then the class says it with you. The puppet then holds up two children in coats, one blue and one red, and says, "Both have a coat, but one is blue." Say, "Someone might say that. It tells us nothing about the tree." What each character does and feels is an experience; a coat is only how someone looks (error A). **Ask.** "What does each enjoy?" Then **Ask.** "Which event changes each plan?" Take two answers each.

#### 10 min. Turn-taking and asking what a word means

Whole class. Review: wait and take a turn (unit 1) and ask "What does ___ mean?" (unit 2). The puppet talks over a partner. Pass the turn card and say, "We wait for the card." The puppet answers one line only when it holds the card (error H). Then ask about a word from page 2 of A Tree Beside Our Door: **Ask.** "What does blossoms mean?" Answer by showing the picture of the pink flowers on the branch and saying, "Blossoms are the flowers on a tree." Children say the question with you once (error W).

#### 15 min. Partners try another pair

Partners. A new pair for the chart: Lina says good morning to her tree before school, and Ben says thank you to his tree when the bell rings. Each pair says a Both ... but ... sentence, then draws two columns on side 1, one picture in each. Expect: both speak to their tree, but Lina says good morning before school and Ben says thank you at the bell. The day's six say theirs to you, about a minute and a half each, and you name them in the record column. Look for something both do and one difference in what each does, not clothes. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Others pass the turn card.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 10 of each story aloud again (sheet E123), then ask [[s04-X123v2]], 30 seconds each, one child at a time, about three minutes. The others finish drawing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: compares how they look, such as braids or glasses (error A).

#### 15 min. Shared lesson (cycle 19, lesson 100: short vowels and digraphs)

Whole class, tiles. The last lesson of the cycle. 5 min: chain chop, chip, chap on tiles, saying each changed sound. 4 min: read one joint reading with a finger under each word: "Josh will have a chip from Beth." Retrieve of, have and from, each read in a sentence before alone. 3 min: dictate chop, chip and chap, a letter for each sound on side 2. 3 min: reread Tree Care Words, verse 1, by echo; point to the line that gives the order.

#### 45 min. Cycle check F1 (prompts in s04 field 5), in the rotations

One child at a time with you, about 90 seconds each; no help is given. A child not finished in 90 seconds is marked shown with help on that component and finishes in tomorrow's response. The other children partner-read A Hen Can Nap with a new question, "Why does Peg run?", and use assignment cards 1 to 4. Ask in order: [[s04-F123a]], track the new page left to right and onto the next page; [[s04-F123b]], point to the printed word hen; [[s04-F123c]], mark the spaces; [[s04-F123d]], name the letters A to M, both cases, in mixed order; [[s04-F123e]], which two words rhyme; [[s04-F123f]], say another word that rhymes with rug; [[s04-F123g]], tap each part of basket; [[s04-F123h]], read of, have and from. Mark each component alone, shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: reverses the tracking (error AG); points to the picture instead of the print (error AH); reads two words as one (error T); gives no letter name (error AI); picks a pair that does not rhyme (error AJ); taps single sounds for parts (error AK).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the chart with two cards, take the exit card alone and run the check as a single sitting, about 90 seconds. One projector: show one picture at a time; draw the chart on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters or tiles: write letters on paper squares.

**Skip.** The reader and the check; read the stories only for the exit.

- **10 min. Both and but.** Pick two things at home, like a spoon and a cup, or two people.
  **Say.** "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Expect: one thing the same, then one thing each does.
  **Say.** "Is a color a good thing to say?" Expect: no, say what each does.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say chop. Your child changes the middle sound to make chip, then chap, and says each changed sound.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X123v2]] Read page 10 of each story aloud, then ask for a Both ... but ... sentence about Lina and Ben.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chart, the exit and the check; cut the turn-taking practice to the card and the question. Longer: a secure child makes a second Both ... but ... sentence about the two stories.

### Words and supports

Words: both, but, blossoms, what does ___ mean. Frame: "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can say what two characters share and one difference. If many compared how they look (error A), day 124 opens with five minutes: the clothing card beside the event cards, then one new Both ... but ... sentence together. Day 124 also gives ten minutes of response to the check, whole class for any of errors T, AG, AH, AI, AJ and AK that more than a third made, and a fresh retry alone for the rest.
