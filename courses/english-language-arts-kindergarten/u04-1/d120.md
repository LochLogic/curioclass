# Day 120: The whole story, and a map of it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the day's six for the map telling and the exit, and six other children for the reading and the sound check, all not yet heard. Read the record sheet and name which of errors F and G day 119's exit showed more. Hang the large story map. Mark page 2 for sheet E120.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

Whole class. 3 min, the repair for day 119's exit: if many said only a topic (error F) or never named it (error G), the puppet holds up the watering can card and says only "The watering can." Ask the class what is missing. Say it whole together: "I like the watering can in this story." Then name the topic first, then the liking, once more. If few missed, do it with one pair and go on. 2 min: say and echo with pictures: rain (Review: you heard about rain in unit 2), root (Review: roots in unit 3) and sapling (new: a young tree).

#### 20 min. Read-aloud, the whole story

Whole class on the rug. Read A Tree Beside Our Door in one reading, about 550 words. Pause at the pictures of pages 4 to 7 and let children look. Read pages 3 and 9 straight through, with no pause, question or comparison. Content: a bee hums at the blossoms and nobody is stung; Lina feels worried, then glad; the tree is outside her apartment building.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then partners. **Ask.** "Who acts beside the apartment?" One turn each, 30 seconds a turn, all pairs at once, then six hands. Expect: Lina and Mr. Dube. **Ask.** "How does rain change her plan?" Same turns. Expect: the rain gives the tree its drink, so they do not need the can today. Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Story map

Whole class, then individuals. Review: the three-box story map from unit 2 (who, where, what happens). 5 min: on the large map, model the start of the story. Who: Lina. Where: beside the tree by the apartment door. What happens: Lina says hello to her tree. 10 min: each child draws the changed event on their own map, with Lina in the who box, the tree and door in the where box, and the rain and the tree's drink in the what-happens box. Each labels one box with letters they know. Hear the day's six say their map, about a minute each, and name them in the record column. Look for who, where and what changes. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Partner share and tidy

Partners. Each says to the other, "My map shows ___." One turn each, then tidy the crayons.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 2 aloud again (sheet E120), then ask [[s04-X120v2]], 30 seconds each, one child at a time, about three minutes. The others draw their favorite part of the story on the back of side 1. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: no one named (error Z); no place (error AA); nothing happens (error AB).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 19, lesson 97: short vowels and digraphs)

Whole class, counters and tiles. Review: dig to dog (day 119), one counter for each sound. Practise: say tin, push three counters, then exchange the middle counter and say ten. Say the sound before and after, /i/ and /e/. Then spell ten with tiles, one letter for each sound. Repeat with a partner. 6 min: read three new sentences together, a finger under each word and a pause at each space: "Beth got a dish from Mom." "Zed has a dish of fish." "Josh will not chat with Beth." Retrieve of, have and from: read each in a sentence before alone. Signs: guesses from a picture (error O); drops a sound in a spelled word (error P); takes a sound away when asked to change it, in for tin (error R).

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Groups of six, then pairs. Reread A Hen Can Nap with a new question: "Why did Peg go to the shed?" The day's six read with you for 10 minutes, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one isolated task. Assignment 7, without text: say "it", add /k/, say kit. Assignment 8: read of, have or from from a card, then spell ten with tiles. The other four groups partner-read, picture covered, and do assignments 1 to 4 on cards: find where Peg goes, choose the matching sentence from two, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers "Why does Peg go to the shed?" Assignments 5 and 6 use the verse in the next part. Name the six in the record column. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Reread Tree Care Words, verse 1, by echo, a finger under each line. Assignment 5: **Ask.** "Which line gives the order: first, then, last?" Children point to the line. Assignment 6: model one verse detail that Peg's story lacks, "You touch the soil to see if it is dry", then each child tells a partner a different one. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, "Then I pour from my can.", a space between words, and each child writes ten by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Whole class, counters in hand; six children heard. Say jog; each of the six says the first, middle and last sound and pushes a counter for each, 30 seconds each. Unscored; the record column notes shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the story once, ask the two questions, draw the map together and take the exit card alone. Use the one child as the six in every part. One projector: show a picture alone; the map is drawn on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons. No tiles: write letters on paper squares.

**Skip.** The reader and the verse; read the story only for the exit.

- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you a short story they know, from a book or their day.
  **Say.** "Who is in it? Where is it? What happens?" Expect: a person, a place and one thing that changes.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say tin. Your child pushes a button for each sound, then changes the middle sound to make ten and writes it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X120v2]] Read page 2 of A Tree Beside Our Door aloud, then ask who, where and what happens.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the map, the exit and the sound change; cut the partner share and the third sentence. Longer: a secure child adds Mr. Dube to a second map.

### Words and supports

Words: rain, root, sapling. Frame: "My map shows ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can name who, where and what changes. If many left out the person (error Z), the place (error AA) or the change (error AB), day 121 opens with five minutes: an actor card beside the event card, the place words found, two event pictures put in order. Day 121 rereads the rain moment and matches a picture to its words.
