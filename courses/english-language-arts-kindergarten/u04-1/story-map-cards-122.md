# Story map picture cards, for days 123 and 124

Make three plain picture cards on heavy paper, one picture each, no printed word: Ben, a boy with a notebook; the school tree beside a bench; Ben carrying his notebook to the quiet side of the school. They belong to the story Reading Under the Tree and are for teaching; they may be shown.

Do not use them on day 122. The day 122 exit asks a child to place three picture cards in the boxes of a story map, so the day 122 teaching draws the boxes instead (the teacher draws each box aloud; pairs draw their own). On day 123 the teacher uses the cards for the repair of errors Z, AA and AB: the Ben card beside the event card, the school tree card for the place words, the move card after the noise picture. On day 124 they come back for the actor, place and event retrieval, with a new actor, place and event for the retry. The three-box map is the one from unit 2 and day 120.
