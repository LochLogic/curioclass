# Section 4.1 opener: two stories of enjoying and caring for a tree

## What the section does

Days 119 to 124 give the class two stories, one about a girl who looks after a young tree beside her apartment building and one about a boy who reads under a school tree. Each story is heard whole, mapped, and then compared with the Both ... but ... frame. The foundations block finishes cycle 19 (short vowels) with a first check on day 123 and starts cycle 20 on day 124.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds sounds, letters, the day's words, the reader and the rotation. The teacher hears a rotating six children for each exit; a child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read the stories; the teacher reads every page, card and direction aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A story map names who, where and what changes.** The change is the event.
- **A picture shows one moment of the words.** The child finds the sentence first, then the picture.
- **Two stories can be alike and different.** Compare what each character does and feels, never clothes or hair.
- **Talk takes turns.** A turn card marks whose turn it is; a question can ask what a word means.

## The errors, in plain words

- **F, G:** an opinion with no preference, or no named subject. Re-model one card with "I like".
- **Z, AA, AB:** a story map with no character, no place or no change. Put the actor card beside the event card.
- **U:** a picture for an event the words do not tell. Find the sentence first.
- **A, H, W:** clothes compared, a child talking over a partner, or no question about a word.
- **X, Y:** no question about a detail, or an answer the sentence contradicts.
- **T, AG to AK:** foundations slips with words, print order, letters, rhyme and syllables.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No picture or question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion; the tree is at a school or in a class pot, never a yard.

## Supplies

The two story manuscripts read aloud, picture cards, chart paper, a turn card and a puppet, buttons for sound counters, letter tiles, the cycle-19 and cycle-20 readers, Tree Care Words verses, repair cards by error letter and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Read the stories from print. Draw each card on scrap paper and say it is made up for practice. Use buttons for counters and a torn strip for the turn card.
