# Day 124: Ask, find and compare again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the day's six for the comparing and the exit, and six other children for the reading and the sound check, all not yet heard. Read the record sheet for day 123: name error A from the exit, and each of T, AG, AH, AI, AJ and AK that more than a third made on the check. Make the five retry cards. Mark page 2 of A Tree Beside Our Door and page 8 of each story for sheet E124.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. Hand-back from day 123's exit, error A: put the clothing card, two coats, beside the event cards on the chart and say that a coat tells nothing about the tree. Then say one new sentence together about Lina and Ben: "Both visit their tree, but Lina stops before school and Ben sits at lunch recess." If more than a third made error A, the whole class does it; if fewer, the others reread the chart quietly while a small group does it with you.

#### 10 min. Reread, the quiet place

Whole class on the rug. Read pages 5 to 7 of Reading Under the Tree, then page 8 of A Tree Beside Our Door. About 300 words, once. **Ask.** "Where does Ben find quiet?" Expect: on the far side of the tree, behind the trunk, on the roots. Pages 3 and 9 stay closed.

#### 15 min. Ask and answer about key details

Partners. Review: a key detail (unit 1) and a who question (unit 3). Show page 7 of Reading Under the Tree. Model: point to the child sitting on a root, ask, "Who sits on a root?", then find the sentence and answer, "Tomas sat on one root." Partners take turns: one points to a detail in the picture and asks a who question, the other finds the sentence and answers. One turn each, 45 seconds a turn, all pairs at once, then three pairs share. Show two wrong examples, said by the puppet. After you read a line, the puppet asks nothing (error X): "Who comes around the trunk?" is a question. The puppet answers "Tomas sat alone." (error Y); find the sentence, "Tomas sat on one root. Mei sat on another." Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Characters, settings, events

Whole class, then pairs. Review: who, where and what changes (days 120 and 122). With the Ben cards, put an actor card beside the event card (who acts), find the place words, and put two event pictures in order. Then a retry with a new actor, place and event, from page 5 of A Tree Beside Our Door: Mr. Dube, by the elevator and then beside the tree, he looks at the dry soil and says it needs a drink. Pairs say it to each other, one turn each, about a minute. If a child leaves out the person, the place or the change (errors Z, AA, AB), put the matching card beside the event and say it again.

#### 10 min. Compare again

Partners. Review: Both ... but ... (day 123). Partners say one new sentence about Ben's move and Lina's wait, using the chart: "Both have a plan that changes, but Ben moves to a quiet place and Lina waits for the water." Each says theirs; hear the day's six, about a minute each, and name them in the record column. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 2 of A Tree Beside Our Door aloud again, say its first sentence once more and ask [[s04-X124av2]]; read page 8 of each story again and ask [[s04-X124bv2]], 20 seconds an ask, one child at a time, about four minutes. The others draw a favorite tree on the back of side 1. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: asks no question (error X); asks about another sentence (error Y); compares how they look (error A).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 20, lesson 101: -an and -am)

Whole class, counters and tiles. New pattern: the an family and the am family. 6 min: read man, pan and van, then ham, jam and ram, with a counter for each sound; the last two sounds stay together in each family. 4 min: change ham to jam by exchanging the first counter, and say the sound before and after, /h/ and /j/. 6 min: map there, they and where. There: th says /th/, the rest is the part to remember. They: th says /th/, the ey is the part to remember. Where: learn the whole word; the first sound is /w/ and the rest is the part to remember. Keep of, have and from on the wall. 4 min: read three sentences together, a finger under each word: "The man has a van." "Where is the ham?" "They have a pan of jam." Signs: guesses from the picture (error O); drops a sound (error P); takes a sound away (error R).

#### 15 min. Small-group reading

Groups of six, then pairs. First use: Sam and Pam; the adult reads the title. Assignments 1 to 6 only; the isolated assignments 7 and 8 are replaced by the response below. The day's six read with you for 8 minutes, about 80 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question ("What does Pam need?"), one pointing task ("Point to where"). The other four groups partner-read, picture covered, and do assignments 1 to 4 on cards: find the event, choose the matching sentence from two, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers "Where is the can?" Assignments 5 and 6 use the verse in the later part. Name the six in the record column. No timer is shown.

#### 10 min. Response and fresh retry

Whole class, then individuals. Hand-back from day 123's check. For each error more than a third made, the whole class repeats the repair; the others retry alone with a fresh case, which replaces that probe. Fresh cases, none from the check: error T, tap one word for each group between spaces on "They have a pan." (2 min); error AG, follow a finger along two new lines, "The man has a van." then "It is on the path." (2 min); error AH, point to the printed word jam with the picture covered (2 min); error AI, name G and g and say they are one letter (2 min); error AJ, hear ham, jam, pot and pick the pair that rhymes, then give another word for ham, ram (1 min); error AK, tap each part of robin, rob and in (1 min). Mark each retry on the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. First heard today: Tree Care Words, verse 2, A Blanket of Mulch. Read it twice. The adult reads the rich language. Write one source sentence together on chart paper, "First, spread the mulch there on the ground.", a space between words, and point to there and ground. Each child writes jam by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Whole class, counters in hand; six children heard. Say bud; each of the six says the first, middle and last sound and pushes a counter for each, 30 seconds each. Unscored; the record column notes shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread the pages, ask and answer one who question, take the exit card alone and do each retry once. Use the one child as the six in every part. One projector: show a picture alone; the chart and cards are drawn on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters or tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The reader and the check response; read the stories only for the exit.

- **10 min. Ask.** Read a sentence from any book together. Your child points to a detail in the picture and asks a who question.
  **Say.** "Who is it about? Find the words that tell." Expect: a name or a person, found in the sentence.
  **Say.** "Say one way two things are the same and one way they differ." Expect: both, then but, and not colors.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say ham. Your child pushes a button for each sound, changes the first sound to make jam and writes it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X124av2]] Read page 2 of A Tree Beside Our Door aloud and ask for a who question about its first sentence. Then [[s04-X124bv2]] read page 8 of each story and ask how Lina and Ben feel.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exit, the questions and the sound change; cut the second retry cards and the third sentence. Longer: a secure child asks a who question about a second picture and finds its sentence.

### Words and supports

Words: who, detail, quiet. Frame: "Who ___?" Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can ask a who question about a sentence and say what two characters share and one difference. If many compared how they look (error A), asked no question (error X) or asked about another sentence (error Y), day 125 opens with five minutes: the clothing card beside the event cards and one new Both ... but ... sentence, then a point to a detail turned into a who question. Day 125 begins the next section and prepares a two-column comparison.
