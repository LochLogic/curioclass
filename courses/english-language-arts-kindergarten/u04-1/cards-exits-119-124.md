# Exit cards, days 119 to 124

Sheets E119 to E124 go with a page of the unit's two stories, read aloud again; they are not made up, so say nothing about practice for them. Only the second E119 card (Nell) is made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice", read it twice, and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot only. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the sheets; you read the pages aloud. None of these sheets is shown in teaching.

## Day 119 sheet E119: page 1 of A Tree Beside Our Door

**Read again (item a):** page 1 of A Tree Beside Our Door (texts/gk-u4-01-a.md), Lina before school.
**Ask:** "Why does Lina stop by her tree every morning?" Right: to say hello to her tree. Wrong: to water it (V).

**Card (item b), made up:** "Nell says, I like watering our class plant."
**Ask:** "What is Nell's opinion? Say it so we hear what it is about and what she likes." Right: Nell likes watering the class plant. Wrong: "The class plant" (F); "She likes it" (G).

## Day 120 sheet E120: page 2 of A Tree Beside Our Door

**Read again:** page 2, Mr. Dube and the blossoms.
**Ask:** "Tell the story map for this page: who, where and what happens." Right: Mr. Dube; by the young tree at the front door; he planted it last fall and now it has blossoms. Wrong: no one named (Z); no place (AA); nothing happens (AB).

## Day 121 sheet E121: page 8 of A Tree Beside Our Door

**Read again:** page 8, its picture covered.
**Pictures, three in a row:** Lina smiling up at the dripping leaves; Lina pouring the green can on dry soil; Lina at her window at night with rain on the glass.
**Ask:** "Which picture shows the last sentence?" Right: Lina smiling at the dripping leaves. Wrong: the other two (U).

## Day 122 sheet E122: page 7 of Reading Under the Tree

**Read again:** page 7 of Reading Under the Tree (texts/gk-u4-01-b.md), the friends come.
**Cards and map:** a three-box story map; three picture cards: Tomas peeking around the trunk; the roots behind the tree; three children reading on the roots.
**Ask:** "Where does each picture card go: who, where or what happens?" Wrong: empty who (Z), where (AA) or what happens (AB).

## Day 123 sheet E123: page 10 of each story

**Read again:** page 10 of A Tree Beside Our Door, then page 10 of Reading Under the Tree.
**Ask:** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about Lina and Ben on these pages." Right: both end at their tree, glad, but Lina stands alone in the sun and Ben sits with two friends. Wrong: braids, glasses or backpacks (A).

## Day 124 sheet E124: page 2 and page 8

**Read again (item a):** page 2 of A Tree Beside Our Door, then say its first sentence once more.
**Ask:** "Ask me one who question about that sentence." Right: Who looked after the building? Wrong: no question (X); a question about another sentence, such as the bee (Y).
**Read again (item b):** page 8 of each story.
**Ask:** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about how Lina and Ben feel." Right: both feel good, but Lina feels warm all over and Ben feels calm. Wrong: boots or glasses (A).
