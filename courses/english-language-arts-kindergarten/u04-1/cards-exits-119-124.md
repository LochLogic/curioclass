# Exit cards, days 119 to 124

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 119 card E119

**Read (item a):** "A small tree grows in a shady corner. Its leaves are pale. Ivo moves its pot to the sunny step."
**Ask:** "Why did Ivo move the tree?" Right: the tree needed sun. Wrong: water (V).

**Read (item b):** "Nell says, I like watering our class plant."
**Ask:** "What is Nell's opinion? Say it so we hear what it is about and what she likes." Right: Nell likes watering the class plant. Wrong: "The class plant" (F); "She likes it" (G).

## Day 120 card E120

**Read:** "Dov eats lunch on a mat under an oak by the school fence. The bell rings. Dov stands up and lines up with his class."
**Ask:** "Tell the story map for this card." Right: Dov; on a mat under an oak by the school fence; the bell rings and he lines up. Wrong: no one named (Z); no place (AA); no change (AB).

## Day 121 card E121

**Read:** "Rosa ties a flag to a low branch. The flag flaps out to one side."
**Pictures, three in a row:** the flag flapping out to one side on a low branch; Rosa tying a flag with the flag hanging straight down; a low branch with no flag.
**Ask:** "Point to the picture that shows the second sentence." Right: the flag flapping out. Wrong: the other two (U).

## Day 122 card E122

**Read:** "Hana draws the elm outside the library window. A cold drizzle starts. Hana carries her paper to the dry porch."
**Cards and map:** a three-box story map; three picture cards: Hana; the elm outside the library window; the drizzle and Hana at the porch.
**Ask:** "Put each picture card in the box where it belongs: who, where, what happens." Wrong: empty who (Z), where (AA) or change (AB).

## Day 123 card E123

**Read:** "Fay sweeps leaves off the path by her tree, then sits beneath it. Hugo sets a tipped pot upright by his tree, then sits beneath it."
**Ask:** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about Fay and Hugo." Right: both sit beneath their trees, but Fay sweeps leaves and Hugo sets a pot upright. Wrong: coats or hats (A).

## Day 124 card E124

**Read:** "Una asks an adult to hold a ladder so she can hang a bird house. Vic asks an adult to hold the hose so he can water his tree." Say the first sentence again and point to the word ladder.
**Ask (item a):** "Ask me one who question about this sentence." Right: Who holds the ladder? Wrong: no question (X); the hose question about the first sentence (Y).
**Ask (item b):** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about Una and Vic." Right: both ask an adult to help, but Una hangs a bird house and Vic waters his tree. Wrong: hats (A).
