# Day 109: Check: second research talks (children 11 to 20)

## Teacher page

### Before class

Groups 3 and 4 are children 11 to 20. Copy days 97 to 99 marks onto the record sheet (R2). Cover the pear and bees card.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Review: source and line (day 107). Repair one: if many chose a fact about the tree itself on day 108 (error E), lay a new card and gap picture, read each line twice, place the line that names who uses the tree.

#### 15 min. Read 05b and 06

Review: The Sugar Maple (day 107). Read 06 (5), reread the lines of 05b that name a living thing; name each source: "That line is from 06."

#### 15 min. Prepare the talk

Pairs, a new partner, one turn each (token, day 85): say your question; the partner finds your line; say so and who uses it.

#### 20 min. Observed research talks

Two groups of five, ten minutes each. Ask for a part the record lacks: the question and source choice [[s03-R109a]], the line [[s03-R109b]], the so link [[s03-R109c]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others finish the plan sheet. Signs: no arrow (error A); a fact about the tree alone (error E); no part recorded (error F).

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs choose a friend line for the collage. No exit on a check day.

#### 7 min. Sound warm-up

Review (day 85): say fed, beg, den; say the middle sound. Six children alone, a new word each. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 10 min. Short e, application

Review: short e (day 107). Chain hen, men, ten, tin, then set, sat on tiles, naming each changed sound. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map be and by

Review: be and by (day 107); point to to, do, at, has, is, it, was, his, or and for as you say them. Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Partner and small-group reading

Review: A Hen for Meg (day 107). Ten: partners reread and say one line in their own words. Ten: hear five not yet heard, two minutes each, read a sentence and say what it means. Signs: guesses from the picture (error R); no meaning (error K).

#### 8 min. Spell and print

Dictate jet, leg, net; write on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Review: Tree Words, verse 6, Shade (day 107). Choral read twice with actions. Collect counters.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The groups and the verse.

- **15 min. The assessment: a talk.** Read twice: "A maple has sweet sap. Bees visit its flowers." Your child says a question, the line that answers it, and so.
  **Say.** "What is your question? Which line answers it? Now say so." Expect: Who visits the flowers? Bees visit its flowers. The flowers have food, so bees come.
- **10 min. The sound e.** Say fed, beg and den; your child pushes a counter for each sound.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say jet, leg and net; your child writes the letters.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads be and by.
- **5 min. Chain.** On paper squares, hen, men, ten.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the verse. Longer: a secure child finds a second line on the page.

### Words and supports

Words: source, line, share, fact. Frame, if asked: "My question is ___. The page says ___. So ___." A child may point, draw, pass or have a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 110 hands back day 109's assessment with a repair, hears groups 5 and 6, children 21 to 30, and checks every child has a note, an arrow and a source label. It starts the F1 battery.
