# Day 110: Check: last research talks (children 21 to 30)

## Teacher page

### Before class

Groups 5 and 6 are children 21 to 30. Keep the F1 cards out of sight. Copy marks onto the record sheet (R2).

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and words

Review: gap, collect, share (day 108). Return day 109's assessment; repair one (3). For error A (no arrow), lay a bee note and a flower note; children add the arrow with so. For error E (a tree fact), place the line that names who uses it.

#### 15 min. Read 05b again

Review: The Sugar Maple (day 108). Listen for lines naming a living thing (8); name each source.

#### 15 min. Prepare the talk

Pairs, one turn each (token, day 85): rehearse so, joining a need to its user.

#### 20 min. Observed research talks

Two groups of five, ten minutes each. Check the roster so every part exists once. Ask for a missing part: the question and source choice [[s03-R110a]], the line [[s03-R110b]], the so link [[s03-R110cv2]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: no arrow (error A); a fact about the tree alone (error E); no part recorded (error F).

#### 5 min. Close

Verify each child has a note, an arrow and a source label. No exit.

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Review (day 85): say peg, yes, vet; say the middle sound. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 10 min. Short e review

Review: short e (day 107). Chain peg, pig, pug and wet, web, wed, naming each changed sound. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 5 min. Map be and by

Review: be and by (day 107), then every mapped word. Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Rotation with the F1 battery

Roster children 1 to 5, three minutes each at your table. Take each through the held back F1 cards: the pair, naming the changed letter and its sound [[s03-F110a]]; spelling by sounds [[s03-F110b]]; the fresh decodable, saying what it tells and pointing to its words [[s03-F110c]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never teach on these cards. The others rotate through stations. Signs: wrong letter (error L); letter name for sound (error AH); a sound left out (error M); fast reading with no meaning (error K).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Dictate peg, wet, hen. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 10 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Review: Tree Words, verse 6 (day 107). Choral read. Collect counters and cards.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The verse and the chain.

- **15 min. The assessment: a talk.** Read twice: "A maple has leaves. A bird sits in its shade." Your child says a question, the line that answers it, and so.
  **Say.** "What is your question? Which line answers it? Now say so." Expect: Who sits in the shade? A bird sits in its shade. The leaves make shade, so the bird rests.
- **10 min. The sound e.** Say peg, yes and vet; your child pushes a counter for each sound.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say peg, wet and hen; your child writes the letters.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads be and by.
- **5 min. Chain.** On paper squares, peg, pig, pug; your child names each changed sound.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the verse. Longer: a secure child finds a second line on the page.

### Words and supports

Words: gap, collect, share, line. Frame, if asked: "My question is ___. The page says ___. So ___." A child may point, draw, pass or have a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 111 repeats the F1 battery for the next five on the roster and starts drafting the collage from the notes.
