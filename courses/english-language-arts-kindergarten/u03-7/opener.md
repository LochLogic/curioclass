# Section 3.7 opener: first research talks

## What the section does

Days 107 to 110 are the research talks. Each child says a question, points to a labeled note and a line, and joins a need to a living thing that depends on the tree with "so". The teacher watches and fills only a part missing from the earlier records; nothing is collected twice. Day 108 is a teaching day: children fill one gap on a collage plan. Foundations bring in short e and the words be and by.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. On days 107, 109 and 110 the topic block is an observation block: two groups of five at the teacher table, about two minutes a child, while the others draw a collage plan sheet. Day 108 has a five-minute exit with six children heard. A child outside the heard group is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read alone; the teacher reads every card, direction and text aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A talk has three turns:** my question, my line, so.
- **A gap is something the collage does not show yet.** We find the line that fills it.
- **A friend's line can teach us.** We name it and keep one for the collage.

## The errors, in plain words

- **E:** a fact that does not fill the gap. Lay a new gap picture and place the line that names who uses the tree.
- **A, F, G, H:** no link, no contribution, no turn taken, or a talk that stops early. A token and one follow-up question help.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No picture or question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion.

## Supplies

The collage plan sheet, the research talk record sheet, the exit card, texts 05a, 05b, 03 and 06 in large print, a talking token, crayons, notebooks, the cycle 17 reader and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Fold a sheet of paper into two columns for the plan sheet and rule a roster by hand. Use a torn strip for the talking token and buttons for counters.
