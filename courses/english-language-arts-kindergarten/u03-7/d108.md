# Day 108: Fill a gap on the collage

## Teacher page

### Before class

Draw the class collage page on chart paper: the sugar maple in the middle, an arrow of water into its roots, and a squirrel with a seed and an arrow out. Leave out the songbirds. Cover E108. Pick six children for the exit and six not yet heard for the sound warm-up. Set the letter groups from day 107's reader notes. Read aloud: "Draw a note that fills the gap" and "Write the letters I say."

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Whole class. Preview collect and gap by gesture: collect is hands gathering, gap is hands apart (new). Review: need (days 80 and 82) and dependent (day 103). Repair at most one, three minutes: if many showed no arrow on day 107's assessment (error A), lay two loose notes, a squirrel and a seed, and add the arrow with so: "The squirrel needs food, so it eats the seed." Children add the arrow.

#### 15 min. Read 03 and reread 05b

Whole class. Review: A Tree Is a Living Thing (day 89) and The Sugar Maple (day 103). Read the chant 03 whole, each line said twice and the class joining the second time, about three minutes, pictures only. Then reread pages 3 and 4 of 05b, the squirrel and the songbirds, about two minutes. Say: listen for a living thing our class page does not show yet. Pass is always allowed.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then whole class. Review: the talking token (day 85). **Ask.** "What does our collage not show yet?" Pairs talk, one turn each, 40 seconds a turn, any child may pass. **Ask.** "Which line in the reading fills the gap?" Same turns. Take one answer from each of three pairs; a child may point to the page. Expect: the songbirds, and the line "Songbirds build nests in its branches" on page 4 of 05b.

#### 20 min. Draw and dictate

Whole class, then individuals. 5 min: model on the class page. Say "The page has no songbirds." Put the gap card beside the maple, point to the line on page 4 of 05b, draw a songbird in a nest on a branch, add an arrow and a 05b sticker, and say the link: "The songbird needs shelter, so it nests in the maple." 15 min: each child checks the plan sheet from day 107, finds one gap (a need or a living thing the readings give that the sheet does not show), draws one note to fill it with an arrow and a source sticker (05a, 05b, 03 or 06), and dictates the so sentence. Take six children, about 90 seconds each, to name the need, the dependent and the link. Look for: (1) the need or the dependent is named; (2) the line is from the reading; (3) the sticker names the right text; (4) an arrow joins them. Nothing is scored. Signs: no arrow (error A); a fact about the tree alone, with no living thing or need (error E); a fact the page never says (error D); the wrong sticker (error AI).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E108, read it twice with its pictures and say its gap sentence. Ask [[s03-X108a]]: children point to one of three strips. Mark the day's six (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X108b]], 30 seconds each, while the class finishes its notes. Signs: the red berries strip or the sharp leaf strip (error E).

#### 5 min. Sound warm-up

Whole class, oral. Review: the middle sound with counters (day 103). Say jet, wet and men; the class pushes a counter for each sound and says the middle sound, about two minutes. Then six children not yet heard, one at a time, say the first, middle and last sound of ten, hen, leg, net, pet or web, 30 seconds each. Write their names in the record column. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Partners, then groups. 5 min: with the children whose day 106 exit showed the bat eating a fruit or flying far from the tree (errors D and B), reread the exact sentence of E106 and trace its object and action in each picture; meanwhile each other child rereads with a partner one sentence they stumbled on from day 107. 3 min: groups by letter with you or a helper. Reread for the stated purpose and point to one event (error K); cover the picture, blend, then check (error R); stack the pair and touch the different letter (error L); say the sound, not the name (error AH); a counter a sound, then write (errors M and V); replace only the named sound counter (error AG). 2 min: a fresh retry with a new pair, peg and pig.

#### 10 min. Short e review

Whole class, then pairs. Review: short e (day 107). With tiles and counters chain ten, tan, tin and set, sat, sit, naming the changed sound each time. Then isolate the middle sound alone in hen and leg, and swap: change /h/ in hen to /m/; change /n/ in net to /p/. Signs: changes two letters (error AG); says the letter name (error AH).

#### 5 min. Map be and by

Whole class. Review: be and by (day 107). Read both in two new strips, "Jen can be by Ted." and "Deb is by the web." Point at the e in be and the y in by, the parts to remember. Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Partner and small-group reading

Pairs, then your table. Review: A Hen for Meg (day 107). 10 min: partners reread pages 1 to 8, picture covered, a page each, and say what happened. 10 min: at your table hear five children not yet heard, two minutes each, read one page and say what it means; observation only, the day 110 battery is later. Write who was heard. Signs: guesses from the picture (error R); no meaning (error K).

#### 5 min. Spell and print

Whole class. Dictate men, web and wet; children push a counter a sound and write the letters on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 5 min. Tree Words verse

Whole class. Review: Tree Words, verse 6 (day 107). Reread it with the actions, pointing to the first word of each line.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Look at the class page together, find the gap, reread the line, and draw a note with an arrow and a sticker. One projector: show the page and the reader on one screen; the child still draws and writes on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No stickers: write the book's name on the note. No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair and the verse.

- **10 min. Find the gap.** Draw a maple with a squirrel eating a seed. Read twice: "Songbirds build nests in its branches." Your child draws a songbird in the tree and an arrow.
  **Say.** "What does our picture not show yet? Which line fills the gap?" Expect: the songbirds, and the line about nests in its branches.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Chain ten, tan, tin on paper squares; your child names each changed sound.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads be and by on cards.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X108a]] and [[s03-X108b]] about three drawings you make, read twice: "A cherry tree has pink flowers in spring. Robins eat the cherries in summer. Cherry bark is smooth and shiny." Draw pink flowers on a branch, a robin at cherries and smooth bark. Say: "Our picture shows the cherry tree but not who needs it."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the gap work, the exit and the Return; cut the verse and the second reading ten minutes. Longer: a secure child fills a second gap and names its sticker.

### Words and supports

Words: collect, gap, source, line. Frame, only if a child asks: "The page does not show ___. The reading says ___. So ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you which children can place the line that names who uses the tree. If many chose a fact about the tree itself (error E), day 109 opens with five minutes: a new card and a gap picture, read each line and place the one that names who uses the tree. Day 109 hears groups 3 and 4 for the second research talks, children 11 to 20.
