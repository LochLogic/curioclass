# Research talk record sheet (R2)

Kept in the teacher folder. Roster numbers 1 to 30 down the left. Five columns for each child, marked 2 (alone), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen):

- my question
- my line, pointed to on the card
- so, joining a need and a living thing
- took a turn with the token
- a friend's line named

A sixth column holds the date and the group. Fill a cell only where the days 97 to 99 records have no part; never repeat a full talk. A child may pass; leave the cell blank and try another day. The sheet never names a child as an example of a mistake.

A room with nothing: rule the columns by hand on a sheet.
