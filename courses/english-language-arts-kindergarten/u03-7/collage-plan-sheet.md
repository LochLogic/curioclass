# Collage plan sheet

A sheet in two columns, drawn by each child on days 107 to 110 while others are heard.

| The tree | A need, and a living thing that depends on it |
|---|---|
| (draw and label the tree) | (draw one need, one living thing, and an arrow between them) |

A line at the foot is read aloud: "What does our collage not show yet?" On day 108 each child draws one note that fills a gap, with an arrow and a source label, and dictates the so sentence. Drawing and dictation are accepted.

A room with nothing: fold a sheet of paper into two columns.
