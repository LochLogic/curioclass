# Exit card, day 108

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 108 card E108

**Read:** "A holly tree has red berries in winter. Waxwings eat the berries. Holly leaves are green and sharp."

**Mark (item a):** Which line fills the gap? The teacher says: "Our collage shows the holly tree but does not show who depends on it." Three strips, each with a picture: (a) "A holly tree has red berries" with a holly branch; (b) "Waxwings eat the berries" with a bird at berries; (c) "Holly leaves are green and sharp" with a leaf.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "Which line on the card did that strip come from?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 108: item a, Strip (b), "Waxwings eat the berries", with the bird at berries. Not yet: Strip (a), the red berries on the holly branch (E, a fact that does not fill the gap); Strip (c), the sharp leaf (E, a fact that does not fill the gap). Item b, "Waxwings eat the berries." Not yet: points to the sentence about green, sharp leaves (E, a fact that does not fill the gap).
