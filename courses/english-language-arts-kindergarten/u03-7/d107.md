# Day 107: Check: first research talks (children 1 to 10)

## Teacher page

### Before class

Groups 1 and 2 are children 1 to 10. Copy days 97 to 99 marks onto the record sheet (R2). List day 106's flagged children.

### The day

#### 5 min. Words and warm-up

Review: research and share (day 102). Three fingers: my question, my line, so.

#### 15 min. Read 05a and 05b

Review: The Oak (day 101), The Sugar Maple (day 103). Read both (5), reread page 3 of each; name each line's source: "That line is from 05a."

#### 15 min. Prepare the talk

Pairs, one turn each (token, day 85): draw and say your question; the partner finds the line; say so, a need and who uses it.

#### 20 min. Observed research talks

Two groups of five, ten minutes each; the card is the page on the child's sticker. Ask for a part the record lacks: the question and source choice [[s03-R107a]], the line [[s03-R107b]], the so link [[s03-R107cv2]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw the plan sheet; a child may pass. Signs: no arrow (error A); a fact that answers nothing (error E); no part recorded (error F).

#### 5 min. Close

No exit on a check day.

#### 7 min. Sound warm-up

Review (day 85): say hen, net, pet and leg; say the middle sound. Sign: drops the vowel (error V).

#### 12 min. Short e lesson (new)

New: short e, as in ten and net. Trace e on the map. Sort twelve picture cards by sound. Chain ten, tan, tin (day 102), naming each changed sound. Signs: says the letter name (error AH); changes two letters (error AG); points to the wrong letter (error L).

#### 6 min. Map be and by

New: be and by; e and y are the parts to remember. Read each on a card and in set 17 cards 4 and 5; point to or and for (day 102). Sign: needs help (error S).

#### 20 min. Partner and small-group reading

Ten minutes: day 106's response and a fresh retry; flagged children reread or and for (reader 4, pages 7 and 9) and write hot; the rest reread in pairs. Ten: read page 1 of A Hen for Meg; pairs read pages 1 to 4, picture covered, and say what happened. Signs: guesses from the picture (error R); no meaning (error K).

#### 10 min. Spell and print

Dictate ten, net, leg; write on side 2. Sign: leaves out a sound (error M).

#### 5 min. Tree Words verse and clear-up

Review: Tree Words, verse 5 (day 102). Verse 6, Shade, is new: hear it twice with actions. Collect counters.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The groups, the picture sort and the verse.

- **15 min. The assessment: a talk.** Read twice: "An oak grows acorns. Squirrels eat acorns." Your child says a question, the line that answers it, and so.
  **Say.** "What is your question? Which line answers it? Now say so." Expect: What eats acorns? Squirrels eat acorns. Acorns are food, so squirrels eat them.
- **10 min. The sound e.** Say ten, net and leg; your child pushes a counter for each sound and says the middle sound.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say the same words; your child writes the letters.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads be and by on cards.
- **5 min. Chain.** On paper squares, ten, tan, tin; your child names each changed sound.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the verse. Longer: a secure child finds a second line on the page.

### Words and supports

Words: research, share, question, line, card. Frame, if asked: "My question is ___. The card says ___. So ___." A child may point, draw, pass or have a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 108 hands back day 107's assessment: it repairs error A, then fills a gap on the collage; errors K, R and S go to its Return and repair.
