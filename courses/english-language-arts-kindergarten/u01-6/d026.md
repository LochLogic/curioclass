# Day 26: Ask a classmate

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 25's exit and choose the one of errors I, J and AC that most children made. Make Pip from a sock. Set the toy bus where the rug can see it, and hang the three question starters on a chart. Put one real shelf toy at each pair's place. Today's six are the twenty-sixth six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Take the one error most children made on day 25. For error I (the wrong maker), hold the pencil label beside the words credit and the brush label beside the pictures credit, and children say "words" and "pictures" with their hands. For error J (a poem called an information book), hold the poem beside an information page and say "short lines that go together" for the poem and "tells facts" for the information book. For error AC (the last inside page marked as the back cover), close the booklet, turn it over and find the outside back. If only a few erred, take them as a small group during Practice.

#### 5 min. Words first

Whole class. New word: interview, when you ask someone questions to learn something about them. Pip says "I will interview you" and asks the class "Which toy do you prefer, the ball or the cart?" Children point to one. Review: prefer, to pick the toy you like best, with favorite and choose (day 15). Review: a question asks for help; a statement tells (day 1). Pip says "I like the cart" and then "Which do you like?" The class says which one asked. Say the word interview twice with the class.

#### 10 min. Return and read

Whole class on the rug. Review: Mira Makes a Road (days 20 to 22). Read page 6 aloud, holding up its picture (1 minute). Show three of the Mira page cards (pages 3, 6 and 7). Ask: "Which picture goes with these words?" and each child points (2 minutes). Then Review: Push, Pull, Roll, which you heard on days 9, 15, 16, 18 and 25. Read it whole, pointing to each picture (about 2 minutes plus pictures). Ask two questions, each answered by pointing back to a page.

**Ask.** "Which detail does this picture show?" Hold up page 2. Expect: a boy pushing a cart away from him.

**Ask.** "Which movement fact could a partner check?" Expect: a wheel rolls (page 4), or a push moves a toy away (page 2). A child may point to the page and not say it.

#### 10 min. Model the interview

Whole class. New routine, Ask a classmate: ask for help, ask what a word means, then ask a follow-up. Say: "This is a made-up interview." You ask, and Pip answers, one line at a time, with the toy bus on the rug (3 minutes):

- You: "Pip, can you help me pick a toy to draw?" Pip: "Draw the bus. The bus is my pick."
- You: "What can it do?" Pip: "It rolls on its wheels. Zoom!"
- You: "What do you mean by zoom?" Pip: "Zoom means it goes fast."
- You: "What makes the wheels roll?" Pip: "A push." (a follow-up grows out of the answer)

Draw the bus on chart paper and write the label bus beside it, saying the sounds (2 minutes). Then Pip slips: it says "I like the bus too" where a question belongs (error E, a statement offered when a question is needed). Ask: "Question or statement?" Children say statement, then say the question: "What can it do?" Echo each of your three questions with the class once (2 minutes). Ask: "Which question asked what a word means?" Expect: "What do you mean by zoom?" Children say it again with you.

#### 12 min. Practice

Fifteen pairs at tables, each with one real shelf toy. Say: "These interviews are about toys on our shelf." A child who talks about a toy at home (error A, an idea outside the task) hears: "Ask about the toy on the shelf." One child asks the three questions and the partner answers; then they swap (5 minutes each way, 2 minutes to change). The turn card goes to the child whose turn it is; a child may pass. Walk and listen to the day's six, three pairs, 2 minutes a pair. Hear a real question, not "I like it too" (error E). Each pair's first question uses the starter "Can you help me pick a toy?"

#### 8 min. Joint portrait

Whole class. New: a toy portrait is a drawing of a classmate's pick with a label and one fact. Look at the bus again. Children say the picture to draw and the label bus (2 minutes). Turn to page 4 of Push, Pull, Roll: "A wheel rolls." Children point to the sentence on the page (2 minutes). Write on chart paper "Pip picks the bus. Its wheels roll." with a finger under each word, left to right, and the class points along (4 minutes, including reading the as a whole word, as you learned on day 21).

#### 5 min. Draw

Alone. Each child draws a partner's pick on a half sheet, not their own. Labels continue tomorrow. Walk and look for the right toy.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the day 26 card and read Pip's line twice: "My pick is the xylophone. I like how it chimes." Each child circles Pip's pick ([[s01-X26]]; 2 minutes). Then each of the day's six hears one spoken question, 30 seconds each: "What could you ask before you label it?" A child may point and not say.

Signs: the toy telephone circled (error B, a picture of a different moment); "I like the xylophone too" (error E); "I have a xylophone at home" (error A). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say two words and the class says if they end the same: bell and well (yes); block and clock (yes); bell and ball (no). A child who says bell and ball rhyme because they start alike (error Q) hears the endings stretched: bell, well; ball, hall (5 minutes). Review: tap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first, not its sounds (day 18): top (one), robot (two), dominoes (three). Say the word, then tap. A child who counts the sounds in robot (error R) hears it again by beats.

#### 10 min. Letters n and c

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Review: letter n: name en, sound /n/, keyword nest; letter c: name see, sound /k/ (hard c only), keyword cow (day 21). Chant "n, n, /n/, nest; c, c, /k/, cow" (2 minutes). Review: go both directions on the sound-spelling map: sound to letter, letter to keyword and sound (day 19). Say /n/ and each child holds up a card from their set, then /k/ (4 minutes). A child who gives the name when the sound is asked (error N) hears the name, then the sound. Then the class big book, Our Classroom Toys: children come up one at a time and touch the c in "cart" on page 3 and the n in "spins" on page 4, and the class says the name (4 minutes).

#### 10 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print N, n, C and c on handwriting paper, saying the sound as you form each: N goes down, slants down, then goes up; n goes down, then up and over the hump; C and c start at the top right, go back and around, and end there (6 minutes). A child who prints only the capital when small is asked (error P) sees the lowercase path written once. Then mapped words (4 minutes). Review: mapped words the and a, read whole by sight (day 21). Write "the a the a" on chart paper and the class reads each. Then point under "the" in "Pip picks the bus" and the class reads it.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets about 40 seconds (4 minutes, one minute for the move): this is the cycle 3 check, notes only. Say the name and sound of n and c, then print one of them. Look for: names en and see, sounds /n/ and /k/ (error N), starts at the top. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger n and c in the sand tray; draw and label a toy; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Say /n/ and then /k/. Each child chooses the letter card first, then prints the letter on the card. This is practice, not scored. Collect the cards. Say: "Tomorrow we ask a new partner about a toy."

### Other rooms

One child: you are Pip and the child asks the three questions; the child draws what you pick, and you ask for a fact before the label. One projector: show one page of Push, Pull, Roll at a time and children point on the screen; mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No puppet: use a sock or a spoon. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the joint portrait, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. Ask.** Your child asks you three questions about a toy: "Can you help me pick a toy?" "What can it do?" "What do you mean by that word?"
  **Say.** "Which toy do you pick?" Expect: a toy from the room.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws your pick and says one label.
- **5 min. Interview.** Say "interview": asking someone questions to learn something.
- **5 min. Letters.** Say /n/ and /k/; your child prints n and c.
- **5 min. Mapped words.** Point to "the" on a page; your child reads it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X26]] Say "My pick is the xylophone. I like how it chimes." Your child circles the pick and asks one question.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second round of Practice and the Draw; keep the model, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure asks a second follow-up question and points to the page that holds a fact.

### Words and supports

Words: interview, question, prefer, portrait. Frames: "Can you help me pick a toy?" "What can it do?" "What do you mean by ___?" A home language, pointing or a drawn toy counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 opens with a five-minute repair of the most common of errors A, B and E: a question beside a statement (error E), the right picture beside the wrong (error B), or the shelf toy beside the home toy (error A). In the first ten minutes of foundations on day 27, children missing a name or sound of n or c from today's check get a fresh retry (error N). Day 27 asks a new partner a question and begins the portrait.
