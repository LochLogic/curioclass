# The letter m and letter r cards (days 28 to 32)

Two large cards and one small copy for each pair. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading.

## The m card

**Picture:** a bright full moon over a small hill, large enough to fill most of the card. **Letters:** M and m, printed large, side by side. **Say:** the keyword is moon, the sound is /m/ as at the start of moon, a hum with the lips together. **Mouth shape:** a face in side view with the lips closed and a hum coming out.

## The r card

**Picture:** a garden rake leaning against a fence, large enough to fill most of the card. **Letters:** R and r, printed large, side by side. **Say:** the keyword is rake, the sound is /r/ as at the start of rake, a growl with the tongue pulled back. **Mouth shape:** a face in side view with the lips a little round and the tongue lifted.

## How the cards are used

Say the keyword, say the sound, point to the letter. Add both cards to the sound-spelling map on day 28. The two letters begin with a curve and a line; compare M with N and R with P by holding the cards side by side. The pictures are reminders of the sounds; never ask a child to guess a word from them. Practice only until the cycle 4 check on day 32.
