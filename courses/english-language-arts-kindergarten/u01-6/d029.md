# Day 29: Check, interviews and early reading, second group

## Teacher page

### Before class

The second fifteen names are the children not yet recorded for the interview on day 28's sheet; the next eight on the battery are the next names after day 28's eight. Return each day 27 portrait with its one line of written feedback, face down at each place. The teacher table holds the battery cards, Folio C and the record sheet. A child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Whole class. Review: the parts of a book (day 2) and author and illustrator (day 2). Hold up A Toy Can Turn and the class points to its front cover, its title page and its back cover. Read its credit lines: the words are by Curio Class and the pictures are by the Curio Class art team. The class says "words" and "pictures" with their hands: a hand writing, a hand painting.

#### 10 min. Hear the second information book again

Whole class on the rug. Review: A Toy Can Turn (days 14, 16, 19 and 27). Read it whole, holding up each picture (about 1.5 minutes plus pictures). Then hold up a page and ask the class to point to the matching sentence; a partner places one fact under the topic on the topic chart.

**Ask.** "Which fact matches this picture?" Hold up the page 3 picture, the top spinning, leaning and lying down. Expect: a top that spins fast stands up straight; when it slows, it leans and falls. Children point to the page and say it; a child may point and not say.

#### 18 min. Interviews, remaining fifteen

Three groups of five, 6 minutes each, with the children not yet recorded on day 28; do not repeat a recorded child. Do not prompt. Each child asks once and answers once in the first 3 minutes ([[s01-I4]]), then a second round adds a follow-up ([[s01-I6]]). Observe listening and turns throughout ([[s01-I5]]). Signs: a statement for a question (error E), talking over (error C), stopping after one answer (error D). Day 28's items for the first fifteen stay on the record sheet. The other twenty-five practise asking and answering in pairs at tables, with a turn card and a shelf toy; no teacher is needed.

#### 20 min. Revise and edit labels

Whole class, then pairs. Say: "Now we make our portrait better." Each child turns over the day 27 portrait, and you read its one line of feedback aloud. Each child adds one fact from Push, Pull, Roll or A Toy Can Turn and you scribe it, then fixes one label using only the letters a, t, h, p, n and c (the first sound is enough). A child whose portrait says "I like it" with no named toy (error X) names the toy first; one who names the toy and stops (error M) is asked what the page says. A label that names a detail instead of the toy (error K) is changed to the toy. Hear the day's six, three pairs, 2 minutes a pair, while the others work alone. The portraits stay in the class folder for scoring.

#### 7 min. Read, Point, Talk, Draw

Pairs. Review: Read, Point, Talk, Draw: hear a text, point back, talk, draw one thing and tell a partner (day 2). Each child reads aloud the new fact (you may help), points to the page that holds it, and tells a partner what they added.

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say two words and the class says if they end the same: bike and hike (yes); bike and ball (no). A child who says bike and ball rhyme because they share a sound at the start (error Q) hears the endings stretched: bike, hike. Review: tap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first, not its sounds (day 18): bubbles (two), dinosaur (three), kite (one). A child who counts the separate sounds in bubbles (error R) hears it again by beats.

#### 10 min. Letters m and r

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Review: letter m: name em, sound /m/, keyword moon; letter r: name ar, sound /r/, keyword rake (day 28). Chant "m, m, /m/, moon; r, r, /r/, rake" (2 minutes). Say /m/ and each child holds up a card from their set, then /r/ (4 minutes). A child who gives the name when the sound is asked (error N) hears the name, then the sound. Then the large poem A Partner Speaks: children come up to touch an m in "My" or "me" or an r in "more" or "heard", and the class says the name (4 minutes). Naming only.

#### 32 min. Common battery, next eight

Teacher table, 4 minutes a child, with the prompts and keys of day 28's items: book kinds and makers, track, sequence and spaces, letter names, formation, sound writing, consonant sounds, rhyme, syllables and mapped words. A component not reached stays blank. The other twenty-two rotate through four stations, 8 minutes each: letter cards, printing, oral picture games, pointing in shared print.

#### 8 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print M m R r on handwriting paper, saying the sound as you form each: M goes down, up, down, up; m goes down, up over a hump and over a second hump; R goes down, then up and around, then slants out; r goes down, then up and over (4 minutes). A child who prints only the capital when small is asked (error P) sees the lowercase path written once. Review: mapped words the and a (day 21) and in (day 28). Show the large poem Toy Clues and point under "in" and under "the" in "A toy in the yard"; the class reads each as a whole word (4 minutes). Collect the portraits and the pages.

### Other rooms

One child: hear the interview with you as the partner and the battery in order. Return the portrait and the child adds one fact. One projector: show one page of A Toy Can Turn at a time; mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the letters on paper. No book: say the fact aloud.

**Skip.** The groups, the stations and the portrait edit.

- **10 min. The assessment, part one.** Your child interviews you about a toy: asks for help, asks what a word means, asks a follow-up.
  **Say.** "Which toy do you pick?" Expect: a toy from the room.
- **10 min. The assessment, part two.** Your child names M m R r and N n C c and says the sounds of m and r.
- **10 min. Portrait.** Your child draws your pick, says the toy, and tells you one fact for you to write.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say bike and hike; your child says if they end the same.
- **5 min. Words.** Point to "in" and "the" in a line you write; your child reads each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second Read, Point, Talk, Draw round; keep the interviews, the battery and the revision. Longer: a child who is secure adds a second fact and points to its page.

### Words and supports

Words: interview, question, label, fact. Frames: "Can you help me pick a toy?" "What do you mean by ___?" "I added ___." A home language, pointing or a drawn toy counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 opens with a five-minute repair of the most common of errors C, D and E from the interviews on days 28 and 29: a pause with a counted breath (error C), a follow-up after one answer (error D), or a question beside a statement (error E). Children make a new edition of a portrait with two facts, and day 29's items and the revised portrait are scored after class.
