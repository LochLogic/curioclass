# Battery cards (days 28 to 31)

The cards for the common battery, heard one child at a time at the teacher table. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Each card has one copy for the teacher and a second set for the stations. Children do not read passages; they name letters, point under words, say sounds and hear words.

## Letter-names card

Large type, scattered across the card in no order: A a T t H h P p N n C c.

## Sound card

Large lowercase letters in a row: t h p n c. The child says each primary sound, the hard sound for c.

## Pat and tap strips

Two long strips, each with one word printed large: "pat" and "tap". The teacher says the spoken word for each strip and asks whether switching the letters keeps the same word. The letters are the same; the order records a different spoken word.

## Word-boundary strip

One long strip with the line "I pat my hat" printed large with clear spaces. The child boxes each separate word with a finger or a thick crayon.

## Rhyme picture cards

Two picture cards: a mat; a pal (two friends sitting side by side). The teacher reads the line from the class poem aloud and asks which one rhymes with the spoken word.

## Syllable pictures

One picture of a puppet, one of a wagon. They help the child hold the word in mind; the counting, saying and blending are spoken.

## Mapped-word cards

Five cards, one word printed large on each: I, my, me, the, a. Shuffled for the list reading and shown again in the line of print.

## Key (teacher)

- Letter names: each of A a T t H h P p N n C c named.
- Sounds: t, h, p, n, and the hard c.
- Pat and tap: no; the order records a different spoken word.
- Boxes: four, one around each separate word, split at the spaces.
- Rhyme: mat for pat, then hat, bat or another real rhyme. Not yet: pal (Q, a shared first sound).
- Syllables: puppet has two parts, pup and pet; wag and on blend to wagon.
- Mapped words: the five read in both settings.
