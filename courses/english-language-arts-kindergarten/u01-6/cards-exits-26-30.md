# Exit cards, days 26, 27 and 30

Each card is marked by every child and goes with a spoken question heard from the day's six. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Day 26 card: Pip's pick

**Say, as Pip:** "My pick is the xylophone. I like how it chimes."

**Picture:** Pip the puppet beside an empty label box with the words Pip's pick under it, read aloud.

**Record:** draw Pip's pick in the label box. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What could you ask before you label it?"

## Day 27 card: the page about beads

**Read:** "The red bead is round. The blue bead is round too. The green bead is round." **Say, as Pip:** "I pick the blue one."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a blue bead with the label "beads"; a red bead with the label "beads"; a blue bead with the label "blue". The labels are printed by the teacher in large letters; children do not read them.

**Mark:** circle the labeled picture that tells the page's topic and Pip's pick. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What fact will you tell for the label?"

## Day 30 card (not scored): the fox

**Read:** "The toy fox sits in its box. Its tail is soft."

**Picture:** three drawings in a row, each in a ring: a fox with a soft tail and the label "fox"; a fox with the label "I like it"; an empty box with the label "box".

**Mark:** circle the labeled drawing that teaches a fact about the toy. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Say a word that rhymes with tail."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 26: the xylophone. Spoken: "What do you mean by chimes?" or "Can you show me how it chimes?" Not yet: the toy telephone (B); "I like the xylophone too" (E); "I have a xylophone at home" (A).
- Day 27: the blue bead labeled beads. Spoken: "It is round." Not yet: the red bead (B); the label "blue" as the topic (K); "beads" and no fact (M); "I like it" (X).
- Day 30: the fox with a soft tail, labeled fox. Spoken: a word that ends like tail, such as mail, sail or pail. Not yet: the "I like it" label (X); "tub", which shares only a first sound (Q). Not scored.

Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may point, act or use a home language; say the English back.
