# Day 27: Rehearse the interview and draft a portrait

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet for day 26's exit and choose the one of errors A, B and E that most children made. Hang the topic chart with its topic picture, and put the picture cards for a top and a cart wheel beside it. Set a portrait sheet at each place. Put a different shelf toy at each pair's place. Today's six are the twenty-seventh six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Say: "These questions are made up for practice." Take the most common of errors A, B and E. For error E (a statement offered when a question is needed), Pip says "I like the horse too" and then the question "What do you mean by rocks?" Children say which one asks, then say the question back with you once. For error B (a picture of a different moment), lay the picture for Pip's pick beside the other two and say Pip's words again. For error A (a toy from home), Pip says "We have a horse at home" and you say "Ask about the toy on the shelf." If only a few erred, take them as a small group during Rehearse.

#### 3 min. Words first

Whole class. Review: turn, spin and circle: words for toys that move in circles (day 14). Hold the top and spin it (spin); turn the cart to face the other way (turn, to spin or to change direction, not to take turns). Review: a label is a word written beside a part of a drawing (day 7). Point to the word bus on the chart from yesterday. The class says each word and does its action.

#### 12 min. Hear 04 and name the topic

Whole class on the rug. Review: A Toy Can Turn, which you heard on days 14, 16 and 19. Read it whole (159 words), holding up each picture (about 1.5 minutes plus pictures). Review: topic and detail: a topic is what the whole text is about; a detail is one fact under it (day 9). Show the topic chart. Children place the top picture and the cart wheel picture under it as you read the sentence each shows: "A top spins." "A wheel spins."

**Ask.** "What is its topic?" Expect: toys that turn. A child who names only the top (error K, one detail named as the whole topic) sees the cart wheel put beside it under the same topic.

**Ask.** "Which detail could a partner confirm about a toy?" Expect: a top spins, or a cart's wheels spin. Children point to the page that shows it.

#### 13 min. Rehearse the exchanges

Pairs, with new partners (each child moves one place to the right), each with a shelf toy. One child asks, the partner answers, then they swap (5 minutes each way, 3 minutes to change). Use the starters on the chart: "Can you help me pick a toy?" "What can it do?" "What do you mean by ___?" Then a follow-up that grows from the answer. Review: Ask a classmate: ask for help, ask what a word means, then ask a follow-up (day 26). Walk and listen to the day's six, three pairs, 2 minutes a pair. A child who answers with a question of their own is welcome to keep going; a child may pass.

#### 17 min. Draft the portrait

Alone, at the table. Say: "Today you draw the toy your new partner picked." Each child draws the toy in the frame of the portrait sheet (8 minutes). Then, for 9 minutes, each child writes a label in letters they know (a, t, h, p, n, c; the first sound is enough) and says one fact from Push, Pull, Roll, from A Toy Can Turn or from the interview. You write the fact for the day's six only (about 1.5 minutes a pair); every other child says the fact to a partner, and you write it for them during the after-class feedback. Model once with a sentence frame on the chart: "Our classmate prefers the ___. Its ___ ___." A child who writes "I like it" (error X, no named toy) is asked to name the toy first, then give a fact. A child who names the toy and stops (error M, no fact) is asked what the page says. Hear the day's six, three pairs, 1.5 minutes a pair; the others label. The sheets stay in the class folder; you attach one line of written feedback to each after class (30 teacher minutes).

#### 5 min. Read, Point, Talk, Draw

Pairs. Review: Read, Point, Talk, Draw: hear a text, point back, talk, draw one thing and tell a partner (day 2). Each child shows their portrait to a partner, says the toy and the fact, and points to the page of the book that holds the fact.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six children. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice: "The red bead is round. The blue bead is round too. The green bead is round." Then Pip says "I pick the blue one." Show the three labeled pictures, reading each label aloud. Each child circles the labeled picture that tells the page's topic and Pip's pick ([[s01-X27]]; 2 minutes). Then each of the day's six hears one spoken question, 30 seconds each: "What fact will you tell for the label?"

Signs: the red bead circled (error B); the label blue circled as the topic (error K); a label with no fact (error M); "I like it" (error X). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Response and fresh retry

Whole class, then small groups. Review: response and fresh retry: the puppet shows one slip from the cycle check, then a retry of that component only, not scored (day 21). Use the most common slip from yesterday's cycle 3 check (4 minutes). The puppet shows one: error N, it says "en" when the sound of n is asked; say the name, then the sound with the letter card and keyword nest. Error O, it prints c when it hears /n/; hear the sound, choose the card, then print. Error P, it prints only N when small is asked; model the small path. Then a fresh retry of that one component only (6 minutes), not scored: children who missed it work in groups on the rug with the cards while you move between them.

#### 5 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme: words that end the same, ball and hall (day 16). Say two words and the class says if they end the same: sled and bed (yes); sled and slide (no). A child who says sled and slide rhyme because they start alike (error Q) hears the endings stretched: sled, bed. Review: tap the beats of a toy name, the whole word first, not its sounds (day 18): drum (one), teddy (two), tambourine (three).

#### 5 min. Onset and rime

Whole class, then six. Review: onset and rime: a spoken word split into its first sound and the rest, then blended with a hand sweep (day 24). Do "d - oll", "k - ite" and "t - ent": say the parts, sweep them into the whole word, and the class says it (3 minutes). Hear six children, one word each, 20 seconds each: you say the parts and the child sweeps them into the whole word (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class. Review: poem Our Play, which you read on days 1 and 24. Show it in large print and point under each word as you read it, then the class reads it with you (4 minutes). Children come up one at a time to touch a c in "Come" and an n in "and" or "spin", and the class says the letter name and sound (4 minutes). Then point under "the" in "Share the toy" and under "a" in "a toy for us today", and the class reads each as a whole word (2 minutes). Review: mapped words the and a, read whole by sight (day 21).

#### 25 min. Rotation

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five turns (day 1); each turn is 5 minutes. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets about 40 seconds (4 minutes, one minute for the move): hear the sound /n/ or /k/ and the child chooses the card, then prints the letter. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger n and c in the sand tray; draw and label a toy; name-card match; big-book browse.

#### 5 min. Check and feedback

Whole class. Each child prints one capital letter and one small letter from memory on the page, from the letters n, c, a, t, h and p. This is practice, not scored. Collect the pages. Say: "Tomorrow you interview a classmate and I listen."

### Other rooms

One child: you are Pip and the child asks the three questions; the child draws what you pick and you scribe a fact. One projector: show one page of A Toy Can Turn at a time and children point on the screen; mark on printed cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: say the topic aloud. No sand tray: print with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the retry, the rotation and the stations.

- **5 min. Topic.** Read a page about a toy that turns. Your child says what it is about.
  **Say.** "What is this page about?" Expect: the toy, not one small part.
- **5 min. Ask.** Your child asks you two questions about a toy and one follow-up.
- **10 min. Portrait.** Your child draws your pick, says one label and tells you one fact for you to write.
- **5 min. Rhyme.** Say sled and bed; your child says if they end the same.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X27]] Say "The red bead is round. The blue bead is round too. The green bead is round. I pick the blue one." Your child circles the picture and says one fact.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second Rehearse turn and Read, Point, Talk, Draw; keep the topic, the draft, the exit and the letters. Longer: a child who is secure adds a second fact and points to the page that holds it.

### Words and supports

Words: turn, spin, label, topic, fact. Frames: "Our classmate prefers the ___." "Its ___ ___." "What do you mean by ___?" A home language, pointing or a drawn toy counts as an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 is the first check day. It opens with a five-minute repair of the most common of errors B, K, M and X: the labels beside their facts, and two different details under one topic picture (error K). Then the first fifteen children interview a partner while the others draw a second toy, and eight children are heard on the early reading and sound tasks. There is no exit ticket.
