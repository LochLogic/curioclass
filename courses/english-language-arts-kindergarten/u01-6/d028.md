# Day 28: Check, interviews and early reading, first group

## Teacher page

### Before class

The first fifteen names on the record sheet make three groups of five; the first eight are the battery. The teacher table holds the battery cards, Folio C and the record sheet. A child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Take the most common of errors B, K, M and X from day 27: lay each label beside its fact (error K); add a fact to a bare label (error M); name the toy first (error X); set the picture beside Pip's words (error B).

#### 8 min. Hear again

Whole class. Review: Push, Pull, Roll (day 26). Read it whole. Partners point to one picture and say its fact.

#### 18 min. Interviews, first fifteen

Three groups of five, 6 minutes each. Do not prompt. Each child asks once and answers once ([[s01-I1]]), then a second round adds a follow-up ([[s01-I3]]). Observe listening and turns ([[s01-I2]]). Signs: a statement for a question (error E), talking over (error C), stopping after one answer (error D). The other twenty-five draw a second toy and label it.

#### 12 min. Shared reading

Whole class. Point under each word of the poem A Partner Speaks in large print, left to right, showing the spaces. Read it, then the class reads it with you.

#### 10 min. Class book page

Whole class. Children place their portrait labels in the class book Our Classroom Toys; you print its title.

#### 7 min. Turn and talk

Pairs, 3 minutes each way: "What did you ask? What did your partner say?"

#### 10 min. Oral work

Whole class. Review: rhyme (day 16): drum and plum (yes); drum and dress (no, error Q). Review: tap beats (day 18): ball, robot, dominoes.

#### 10 min. Letters m and r

Whole class. Add the m and r cards to the map: "m, m, /m/, moon; r, r, /r/, rake." Hold M beside N and R beside P. Not scored.

#### 32 min. Common battery, first eight

Teacher table, 4 minutes a child, in order: book kinds and makers ([[s01-B1]], [[s01-B2]], [[s01-B13]], [[s01-B14]]); track, sequence and spaces ([[s01-B4]], [[s01-B5]], [[s01-B6]]); letter names ([[s01-B3]]); formation ([[s01-B7]]); sound writing ([[s01-B8]]); consonant sounds ([[s01-B9]]); rhyme ([[s01-B10]]); syllables ([[s01-B11]]); mapped words ([[s01-B12]]). A component not reached stays blank. The other twenty-two rotate through four stations, 8 minutes each: letter cards, printing, oral picture games, shared print.

#### 8 min. Formation and mapped words

Whole class. Print M m R r, saying the sound. Map the word in, read whole. Collect the pages.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; hear the battery in order.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the letters on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the stations and the class book.

- **10 min. The assessment, part one.** Your child interviews you: asks for help, asks what a word means, asks a follow-up.
  **Say.** "Which toy do you pick?" Expect: a toy from the room.
- **10 min. The assessment, part two.** Your child names A a T t H h P p N n C c and says the sounds of t, h, p, n and c.
- **10 min. Printing.** Your child prints A and a, and writes the letters for /a/ and /t/.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say bell and well; your child says if they end the same.
- **5 min. Words.** Say the word in; your child says it back.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: letter names and sounds only. Longer: a second toy interview.

### Words and supports

Words: interview, question, label. Frames: "Can you help me pick a toy?" "What can it do?"

### Tomorrow

Day 29 hears the remaining fifteen in interviews and the next eight on the battery, and returns the portraits for a fact and a label.
