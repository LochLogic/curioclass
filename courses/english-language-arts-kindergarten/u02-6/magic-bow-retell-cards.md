# The Magic Bow: retell cards

Four picture cards for a pair, hand-drawn on index cards from the pictures of The Magic Bow (gk-u1-07). The bow rests beside a person or on a stool; it is never held up and never aimed.

## The cards, in order

1. The little old woman sits in her doorway; the bow rests on her knees.
2. People walk up the path to borrow the bow; she is happy to share.
3. The empty stool: the bow is gone, and the people stand with their heads down.
4. The woman speaks to the group: it was never the bow; they learned because they tried. People go home and keep trying.

## How to use them

Day 57. The teacher rereads the story whole, then pairs shuffle the four cards, put them in order, and each partner retells one card to the other: first, then, last. The map of this story goes beside the map of The Picnic Plan: the same three boxes, a different story.

## Key (teacher)

A good retell has the beginning (a woman with a bow that helps people learn), the change (someone took it and the people were sad), and the ending (she says it was never the bow; they keep trying), in that order. Telling only the sad part leaves out events (AV); swapping the cards is out of order (J).
