# Sort cards: weather, information, story

Nine picture cards for pairs on day 58, hand-drawn, plus three heading cards: **seen weather**, **information**, **story event**.

## The nine cards

- Seen weather: the sky through the window; a printed scene of a gray sky; rain on the glass.
- Information: a sentence card "Rain falls from clouds." with a cloud picture; a sentence card "Snow is ice." with a snowflake picture; a card from the reading about drops.
- Story event: Tomas holds a hand out to the first drop; Priya writes in her notebook; the woman speaks to the people.

## How to use them

The teacher reads each card aloud. A pair puts each card under the heading it belongs to and says why in one sentence. The class rereads two pages of the weather reading and the sorted cards are checked against them.

## Key (teacher)

Seen weather is something the child can see now. Information is a true fact from a book or a page. A story event is something a character does in a made-up story. No card in this set is an exit card.
