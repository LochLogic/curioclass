# Exit cards, days 54 to 58

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 54

**Story card:** "At the bus shelter, Kofi and Ma wait. A shower starts. Kofi opens his umbrella." Three boxes on the child's paper map: who, where, what happened. Six picture cards: Kofi and Ma; the umbrella; the bus shelter; the market; the shower starts and the umbrella opens; the umbrella alone. **Ask:** "Put each card in its box on your map." Six children are asked: "Which words told you?"

## Day 55

**Story card:** "Nell draws a calm lake. Later wind comes and little waves cover the lake. Nell draws the waves." Three pictures: a calm lake; a lake covered in little waves; a boat on a lake. **Item a:** "Which picture did Nell draw first?" **Item b**, six children: "Tell me what happened in a sentence."

## Day 56

Strips printed by the teacher and read aloud; no child reads them. **Item a:** "the puddle is big." and "The puddle is big." Ask: "Circle the strip that starts the right way." **Item b:** "Soon i run." and "Soon I run." Ask: "Circle the strip with the I written the right way." **Item c:** a period, a question mark and an exclamation mark on three cards with no sentence. Ask: "Circle the question mark." The six also say the name of each mark. **Item d:** three strips read aloud, each beside a picture: "A seal naps." (a seal asleep); "A seal naps on the rock." (a seal asleep on a rock); "A seal." (a seal). Ask: "Circle the sentence that says where the seal naps."

## Day 57

**Story card**, read twice: "A boy fills a jar with seeds. A bird tips it over. The boy sweeps up the seeds and sets a heavier jar outside." Five pictures, mixed: the jar filled; the bird tips it; the boy sweeps; a heavier jar; a bird alone. **Ask:** "Put the pictures in order and tell the story." The six tell it aloud.

## Day 58

**Information card:** "Hail is ice that falls from clouds. Hail falls when it gets too heavy." A cloud with ice falling. **Item a:** "Tell one fact from the card and show your drawing." **Word card:** "Pia rehangs the wet coat on the peg." Three pictures: a coat hung on the peg the first time; a coat that fell and is hung on the peg a second time (two small panels); a coat taken down. **Item b:** "Mark the picture that shows what rehangs means."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 54: who, Kofi and Ma; where, the bus shelter; what happened, the shower starts and the umbrella opens. Not yet: the umbrella as who (G); the market as where (H); the umbrella alone as the event (I).
- Day 55: a, the calm lake; not yet: the waves picture or the boat picture (A). b, a sentence like "Wind makes little waves on the lake."; not yet: "wind lake" (AE); the same short sentence again when asked for more (AF).
- Day 56: a, the capital P; not yet: the lowercase strip (AJ). b, the capital I; not yet: the lowercase i (AK). c, the question mark, and the names period, question mark, exclamation mark; not yet: a wrong circle or name (AL). d, the seal asleep on a rock, "A seal naps on the rock."; not yet: the short sentence with no where (AF); the fragment "A seal." (AE).
- Day 57: the jar filled, the bird tips it, the boy sweeps, the heavier jar, told with first, then, last; not yet: sweeping before the bird (J); only that the bird tipped the jar (AV).
- Day 58: a, hail and one fact (ice falls from clouds, or it falls when too heavy) with a drawing that shows it, and a voice that reaches the partner; not yet: a drawing of something else (D); a voice too quiet to hear (E), scored apart. b, the coat hung on the peg a second time; not yet: the first hanging or the coat taken down (AD).
