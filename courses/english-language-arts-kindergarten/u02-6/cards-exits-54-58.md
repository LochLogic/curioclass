# Exit cards, days 54 to 58

Days 54 and 57 use pages of The Picnic Plan (texts/gk-u2-02.md, selection a), read aloud again; they are not made up, so the teacher only makes their pictures. For the made-up cards (days 55, 56, 58), say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every made-up card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards or pictures is shown in teaching.

## Day 54

**Story page:** page 6 of The Picnic Plan, read aloud again. Three boxes on the child's paper map: who, where, what happens. Six picture cards: Tomas and Grandma Rosa; the toy duck; the blanket on the living room floor; the park by the pond; Tomas sets the duck on the blanket; the lemonade jug alone. **Ask:** "Put each card in its box on your map." Six children are asked: "Which words told you?"

## Day 55

**Story card:** "Nell draws a calm lake. Later wind comes and little waves cover the lake. Nell draws the waves." Three pictures: a calm lake; a lake covered in little waves; a boat on a lake. **Item a:** "Which picture did Nell draw first?" **Item b**, six children: "Tell me what happened in a sentence."

## Day 56

Strips printed by the teacher and read aloud; no child reads them. **Item a:** "the puddle is big." and "The puddle is big." Ask: "Circle the strip that starts the right way." **Item b:** "Soon i run." and "Soon I run." Ask: "Circle the strip with the I written the right way." **Item c:** a period, a question mark and an exclamation mark on three cards with no sentence. Ask: "Circle the question mark." The six also say the name of each mark. **Item d**, spoken, to the day's six: a picture of a seal asleep on a rock. Say: "A seal naps." Then: "Make the sentence tell more. Say it again and add where the seal naps."

## Day 57

**Story pages:** pages 3 and 4 of The Picnic Plan, read aloud again. Five pictures, mixed: a gray cloud slides over the sun; a drop lands on Tomas's nose; they hurry home with the wagon; Tomas is wet and sad; the picnic on the blanket (the extra). **Ask:** "Put the four pictures in order and tell what happens." The six tell it aloud.

## Day 58

**Information card:** "Hail is ice that falls from clouds. Hail falls when it gets too heavy." A cloud with ice falling. **Item a:** "Tell one fact from the card and show your drawing." **Word card:** "Pia rehangs the wet coat on the peg." Three pictures: a coat hung on the peg the first time; a coat that fell and is hung on the peg a second time (two small panels); a coat taken down. **Item b:** "Mark the picture that shows what rehangs means."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 54: who, Tomas and Grandma Rosa; where, the blanket on the living room floor; what happens, Tomas sets the duck on the blanket. Not yet: the duck as who (G); the park as where (H); the jug alone as the event (I).
- Day 55: a, the calm lake; not yet: the waves picture or the boat picture (A). b, a sentence like "Wind makes little waves on the lake."; not yet: "wind lake" (AE); the same short sentence again when asked for more (AF).
- Day 56: a, the capital P; not yet: the lowercase strip (AJ). b, the capital I; not yet: the lowercase i (AK). c, the question mark, and the names period, question mark, exclamation mark; not yet: a wrong circle or name (AL). d, the seal asleep on a rock, "A seal naps on the rock."; not yet: the short sentence with no where (AF); the fragment "A seal." (AE).
- Day 57: the cloud over the sun, the drop, the hurry home, Tomas wet and sad, told with first, then, last; not yet: the hurry home before the drop (J); only that it rained (AV).
- Day 58: a, hail and one fact (ice falls from clouds, or it falls when too heavy) with a drawing that shows it, and a voice that reaches the partner; not yet: a drawing of something else (D); a voice too quiet to hear (E), scored apart. b, the coat hung on the peg a second time; not yet: the first hanging or the coat taken down (AD).
