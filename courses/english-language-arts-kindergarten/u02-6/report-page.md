# Weather report page

One page for each child on day 58. A large box at the top for a drawing. Under it, three short lines, each with a small heading the teacher writes by hand: topic, one true fact, and what people do or wear in the text.

The child draws one weather fact from the reading or from the window, says the topic and the fact, and the teacher writes what the child says on the lines. A child may add any letters already taught. Children never write about what they wear or do at home; the third line is about the people in the text.

## Look-fors (teacher)

The topic is named. The fact is true to the text or the window. The drawing shows that fact. The voice is loud enough for the partner. A liking alone ("I like boots") has no fact (C).
