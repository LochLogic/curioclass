# Day 55: A picture weather journal, and a longer sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the Priya cards in the three story-map boxes. Put the journal chart where all can see it. Set the closed window or the printed weather scene. Make the day's six: the next six unheard in the heard column. Keep the exit cards to yourself. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. If many children missed day 54's exit, repair the more common of errors G, H and I. For error I (a small thing named instead of what happens), say an action sentence and children point to its event picture among the three Priya cards: "The drops get smaller." Then one fresh card: show the Picnic Plan picture of Tomas holding out his hand to the first drop and ask which box it goes in and why. For error G, lay two new person cards from The Picnic Plan beside the basket and ask who acts. For error H, reread the place sentence aloud and choose its scene. If few missed, point at the Priya map and let pairs say it back.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Review: a sentence is the words we say and you write (day 6), and a sentence grows when we add where or when (day 40). Complete: a sentence that tells who or what, and what happens. Say "Rain falls." and show that it is complete. Expand: stretch your hands apart, and stretch the sentence by adding where or when. Children say expand and complete with the gesture.

#### 15 min. Second hearing of Priya at the Window

Whole class, then pairs. Read selection b once through with the pictures (about 3 minutes). Then show the pictures of pages 2, 3 and 5 for talk (12 minutes). Pairs talk, then take answers from the group; hear six.
**Ask.** "What weather change did Priya see?" Expect: the rain got lighter, then stopped, and the sun came out.
**Ask.** "Which words did she write down?" Expect: "Rain falls." Later "Rain falls on the green leaves." and "The rain stops. The sun shines." Point to each on the page as it is named. Point out that "Rain falls." on page 2 grew into "Rain falls on the green leaves." on page 3; that is Priya's own move, and it is the one the class makes next.

#### 20 min. Own picture journal begins (W.K.2)

Whole class, then alone. Model one entry (5 minutes) from the sky through the closed window; if it shows nothing useful, use the printed scene and say it is a picture. Draw the sky big, say one true fact, and say a line to write: "The sky is bright." Write it left to right while they watch. The goal: color carefully so the picture shows the fact. Children draw their own entry and say a line (this is practice and is not scored). Take dictation at the table from the day's six, about 1 minute each (6 minutes), while the others draw. Then walk among the others and hear each line in one breath, about 10 seconds each (4 minutes); write each beside the drawing after class. The last 5 minutes, children color carefully and tell their line to a partner. A child with nothing to say points at the window or picture and you name it. Nobody tells what the weather is like at home.

#### 10 min. Shared sentence expansion

Whole class, then pairs. Review: say who and what, then add where or when (day 40); the question cards where and when. Priya's move: show "Rain falls." and "Rain falls on the green leaves." Pip says "Gray sky." **Ask.** "Who or what? What happens?" Expect: the sky, is gray. Say it back as a whole sentence: "The sky is gray." Write it. Now expand it: **Ask.** "Where? Or when?" Expect: over the school; today. Write the new sentence, "The sky is gray over the school." Count words by tapping each (six). Pairs then expand one of their own journal lines with where or when, one minute each way (15 pairs at once, two minutes). Hear six pairs. A child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit E55

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice with its picture. All children mark by circling a picture on the exit sheet: ask [[s02-X55a]]. Then, to the day's six, one at a time, about 30 seconds each: ask [[s02-X55b]]. If the sentence is only the short one, ask once, "Tell me where or when." Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the waves picture or the boat circled (error A); "wind lake" (error AE); the same short sentence again when asked for more (error AF).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 8, lesson 42: k and y). Rhyme: kit, sit and hit; children say another word that rhymes with kit. Syllables: clap the beats of kitten (two), yellow (two), kangaroo (three) and yesterday (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: k and y

Whole class. Reread the verse Fog with the pointer. Letter search: curl your hands round your eyes like glasses and find k in blanket and y in gray and away; circle each. Chant: "k, kangaroo, /k/; y, yak, /y/." Then print k and y, little and big, in lines on handwriting paper. Say the path as they go: k, a tall line down, then an arm in and a leg out; y, a slant down, then a long slant that dips below the line. Say each sound as it is written. Sound to letter: say /y/, children write y; say /k/, they write k.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with three counters each. Isolate the first, middle and last sound of tub, fig and ten. Blend: you say /f/ /i/ /g/; children say fig. Blend /b/ /u/ /s/. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words up and do

Whole class. Review: do (day 54) and up (day 53). Map each word: say its sounds, touch each letter, and put a star under the part to remember (the o in do). Write the strip "We look up." and "We do." Children read up, we and do in the lines with the pointer, left to right. Find do again by covering the other words.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class. Reread today's expanded sentence with the pointer, left to right: "The sky is gray over the school." Tap each space between words. Count the words (six). Say the first word, then the last. Find k in sky and y in gray.

#### 15 min. Foundations: rotation

Teacher table and stations. The teacher hears the day's six at the table, 2 minutes each (12 minutes), and writes the cycle 8 record: names and sounds of k and y, and writing each from its sound. The others work in pairs at stations: handwriting k and y, and letter cards. The last 3 minutes are for moving.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Draw one entry, say a line and expand it. One projector: show the Priya pages and the chart; the drawing stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No window: use a picture of the sky. No question cards: ask where and when aloud.

**Skip.** The expansion with a partner and the foundations rotation.

- **10 min. Journal.** Look at the sky from a window or a picture. Your child draws it big, tells one true fact, and you write the line.
  **Say.** "What is the sky doing? Tell me one true fact." Expect: a fact that fits the picture, such as the sky is bright.
- **10 min. Longer sentence.** Say their line back, then ask where or when.
  **Say.** "Where? Or when? Say it again with the new words." Expect: a longer sentence, such as the sky is bright over the roof.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints k and y.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X55a]] [[s02-X55b]] Read twice: "Nell draws a calm lake. Later wind comes and little waves cover the lake. Nell draws the waves." Ask which picture Nell drew first, then ask for what happened in a sentence.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the second hearing, the journal and the exit; cut the pair expansion. Longer: a secure child adds both where and when to one line.

### Words and supports

Words: expand, complete, sentence. Frames: "The sky is ___." "It happens ___ (where or when)." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 opens with 5 minutes for the more common of errors AE and AF on today's exit: add where or when to the sentence and say it back. The picture mistake, error A, is mended there with one sentence: first, later. Then the class hears pages 3 to 5 of the story again and learns the capital letter and the end marks. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet.
