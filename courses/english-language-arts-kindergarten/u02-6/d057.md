# Day 57: Practice: retell The Magic Bow with the cards

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the four retell cards in an envelope for each pair. Hang a clean story-map chart next to the Picnic Plan map. Make the day's six: the next six unheard, taken as three pairs so they retell to each other. Keep the exit cards to yourself. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. If many children missed day 56's exit, repair the more common of errors AJ, AK and AL. For error AJ (a lowercase first word), write a new strip with a small first letter, circle the first word and change its first letter. For error AK (a small i), find the word I in a model strip, then fix the strip. For error AL (a wrong name for a mark), say a telling sentence and a question aloud; children make the gesture of the period for the first and the question mark for the second, then say each name.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class, with gestures. Retell: Review: to tell a story again in order, with first, next and last (day 51); you may say then for next. Believe: Review: to feel sure, a hand on the chest (day 23). Explain: Review: to tell how or why, open hands (day 39). Children say each word with its gesture.

#### 10 min. The Magic Bow, reread whole

Whole class. Review: you heard The Magic Bow on day 23. Say it is a made-up story, and read it whole from the held text with its eight pictures (about 4 minutes). Read it aloud only; no child reads. Then show the pictures of pages 3 to 6 once more and have the class say who is in each (3 minutes). The bow stays in the pictures and words; it is never held up or aimed. End by saying the poem on page 7 together with a clap for each line (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Questions

Pairs, then the whole group. Ask two questions, one at a time; no score.
**Ask.** "What do the people first believe?" Expect: the bow does the work; with it gone, nobody will learn again.
**Ask.** "What does the woman explain instead?" Expect: it was never the bow; they learned because they tried. If an answer does not match the story (error A), reread pages 5 and 6 and ask again. If the answers are out of order, lay the four retell cards in order and say first, then, last (error J). Pairs talk about 2 minutes a question; hear six.

#### 10 min. Retell in pairs

Pairs. Pip shows the retell wrongly first (2 minutes): Pip tells only that the bow was gone (error AV) and puts the empty stool card first (error J). The class puts the cards in order and says what Pip left out. Then each pair shuffles the four cards, lays them in order, and each partner retells the whole story with the cards, first, then, then, last (about 2 minutes a child, 4 in all). Sit with the day's six, taken as three pairs, about 2 minutes a pair (6 minutes); the others stay with their cards.

#### 10 min. Compare two maps

Whole class. Fill the clean map for The Magic Bow with the class: who (the little old woman and the people), where (her doorway and the path to her house), what happens (the bow helps people learn; someone takes it and the people are sad; she says it was never the bow). Review: the Picnic Plan map from day 49. Point to the two maps side by side.
**Ask.** "How are the two maps the same, and how are they different?" Expect: the same three boxes; a different story in each. Pairs say it to each other, one minute each way; hear six.

#### 5 min. Journal

Alone. Children add a small detail to the drawing in their journal and tell a longer sentence to a partner. Write the day's six sentences with a capital and a mark, about 30 seconds each; the others print the first letter if they know it. The rest of the lines are written after class. A child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit E57

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice so it is familiar. All children mark the exit sheet by putting dots, one to four, on the pictures in order and leaving the extra picture empty: ask [[s02-X57]]. The day's six also tell the story aloud, 30 seconds each, while the others check their dots. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the sweeping picture before the bird (error J); only that the bird tipped it (error AV).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 8, lesson 44: k and y). Rhyme: say yes, guess and mess; children say another word that rhymes. Syllables: clap the beats of key (one), kitten (two), yellow (two) and kangaroo (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: k and y

Whole class. Say the names, sounds and keywords: k, kangaroo, /k/; y, yak, /y/. Print k and y, little and big, in lines. Say each sound as it is written. Then sound to letter in a new order, with no model.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters. Blend: you say /b/ /e/ /d/; children say bed. Blend /k/ /u/ /p/. Segment: you say bug; children say /b/ /u/ /g/ and push a counter for each. Segment ten. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words up and do

Whole class. As day 55: map each word, touch each letter, star the part to remember. Review: up, we and do. Read the strip "We look up." and "We do." with the pointer, then find each mapped word in the first lines of the poem on page 7.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class. Read the first two lines of the poem with the pointer, left to right: "Choose the thing you want to learn. Believe in you, and take your turn." Rhyme recognition: which words end alike? Children point to learn and turn. Rhyme production: children give another word that rhymes with turn (burn, fern). Find the capital letter at the start of each line and the period at the end of each. Find y in you.

#### 15 min. Foundations: rotation

Teacher table and stations. The teacher hears the next six, 2 minutes each (12 minutes): names, sounds and writing for k and y. The others work in pairs: handwriting and letter cards. The last 3 minutes are for moving.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread the story, lay the four cards in order and retell it to a toy. One projector: show the eight pictures and the two maps; the ordering stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No retell cards: draw four small pictures on scrap paper. No chart: draw two maps on one page.

**Skip.** The pair retell, the second map and the rotation.

- **10 min. Story.** Read The Magic Bow aloud with the pictures.
  **Say.** "What do the people first believe? What does the woman explain instead?" Expect: the bow does the work; she says they learned because they tried.
- **10 min. Retell.** Your child lays the four cards in order and retells the story, saying first, then, then, last.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints k and y.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X57]] Read twice: "A boy fills a jar with seeds. A bird tips it over. The boy sweeps up the seeds and sets a heavier jar outside." Ask your child to put four pictures in order and tell the story.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the retell with cards and the exit; leave the map comparison for tomorrow's warm-up. Longer: a secure child retells with a detail from each picture.

### Words and supports

Words: retell, believe, explain, first, then, last. Frames: "First ___. Then ___. Last ___." "The people believed ___. The woman explained ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 58 opens with 5 minutes for the more common of errors J and AV on today's exit: order the four cards while rereading first, then and last. Then the class turns weather facts into a drawn and spoken report and works on word parts. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet.
