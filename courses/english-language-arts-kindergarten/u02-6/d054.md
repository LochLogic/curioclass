# Day 54: A second weather story, mapped in three boxes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the story-map chart with the Picnic Plan cards beside it. Set out the box and scarf. Make the response groups from day 52's cycle 7 check: group A (error U, the letter named when the sound was asked) and group B (the rest). Make the day's six: the next six unheard in the heard column. Print the exit sheet from the exit card; keep the exit cards to yourself. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Whole class. Repair the one error most children made on yesterday's exit: error AD (wrap heard for rewrap), or else error B (no place or weather named) or error C (only a liking). For AD (3 minutes), wrap the box in the scarf and say "wrap". Take the scarf off: "unwrap". Wrap it a second time: "rewrap". **Ask.** "Which time did I rewrap?" Expect: the second time, the wrapping again. For B or C, Pip says "I like snow"; the children give a fact from the five-place chart instead. Then 2 minutes. Review: you filled the story-map for The Picnic Plan on day 49. Point to the three boxes. With the old cards, children say who (Tomas and Grandma Rosa), where and when (the park, the living room) and what changed the plan (the rain starts). This is a reminder, not a check.

#### 5 min. Words before reading

Whole class, with gestures and pictures. Window: frame the classroom window with your hands. Room: spread your arms wide. Observer: Review: you met observe on day 35 and weather observers in Weather Changes. To observe is to look and listen closely. Add -er to the end: observer. An observer is a person who observes. Then sing and singer: a singer is a person who sings. Children say each word and "a person who ___". Today's story has a weather observer in it.

#### 15 min. First hearing of Priya at the Window

Whole class. Say: this story is made up. Read selection b (313 words) once, straight through, with the picture of each page held up (about 3 minutes). Do not stop. Then reread pages 1 to 5, one pause a page (12 minutes with the talk). Take one or two answers a page.
**Ask.** After page 1: "What is Priya's job today?" Expect: weather observer.
**Ask.** After page 2: "What does Priya see and hear?" Expect: fat drops on the glass, a gray sky, tapping.
**Ask.** After page 3: "What changes?" Expect: the drops get smaller and fewer; the tapping goes soft.
**Ask.** After page 4: "What happens next?" Expect: the rain stops and the sun comes out.
**Ask.** After page 5: "What does Priya write?" Expect: the rain stops, the sun shines. Page 5 names three marks; read it as written and say they come back on day 56. The story ends safely; nothing in it is scary.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Pairs, then the whole group. Review: turn and talk (ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass). Ask three questions one at a time. Model the first answer yourself with its full frame; give half a frame for the second; give none for the third.
**Ask.** "Who watches the rain?" Expect: Priya (and Dev is with her). Frame: "Priya is the ___."
**Ask.** "Where is Priya?" Expect: in the kitchen, at the window. Frame: "Priya is at the ___."
**Ask.** "What changes?" Expect: the rain gets lighter, then stops, and the sun comes out.
Pairs talk about 1 minute a question; take answers from the whole group. Then the word question. Review: ask about a word, "What does ___ mean?" (day 37). **Ask.** "What does observer mean?" Expect: a person who looks and listens closely. Hear six children across the questions.

#### 15 min. Model and map

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the story-map of three boxes from day 49. Model (6 minutes): hang the Priya cards one at a time in the chart. Who: Priya and Dev. Where and when: the kitchen window and a rainy afternoon. What happens, in order: rain falls, the rain gets lighter, the rain stops and the sun comes out. Each time say why: "Priya acts, so she goes in the who box." Pip then tries three cards wrongly. Pip puts the notebook in the who box: **Ask.** "Does the notebook act?" Expect: no, a person acts (error G). Pip puts the school in the where box: **Ask.** "Is the school in the story?" Expect: no, the kitchen window is (error H). Pip puts only the crayon in the happens box: **Ask.** "Is that what happens?" Expect: no, the rain changes (error I). Pairs (9 minutes): each pair lays the seven Priya cards on a paper map, then each child draws one picture in each box of side 1. Confer with the day's six, one minute each; the others work as pairs.

#### 5 min. Class weather journal

Whole class. Review: the class picture journal (day 41). Show the printed gray-sky scene and say it is a picture. Draw the sky as the children say what to add (three or four ideas). Write one line while they watch, left to right: "The sky is gray." Read it with the pointer. The line stays on the chart for the shared print part.

#### 5 min. Exit E54

Read page 6 of The Picnic Plan aloud again with its picture; the lesson mapped Priya, so this page is new work. Then ask [[s02-X54v2]]. Every child marks the exit sheet by drawing a line from each of the six pictures to its box. The day's six also place real cards on a map at the heard table, 30 seconds each, and are asked "Which words told you?" Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: the toy duck in the who box (error G); the park in the where box (error H); the jug alone in the what happens box (error I). A fair answer names a word of the page, such as blanket or duck.

#### 10 min. Foundations: return and repair

Small groups, counted only here. Response and fresh retry to day 52's cycle 7 check, with new cards. Group A (error U) with you for 5 minutes: sort the name cards dee, eff and ell from the sound cards /d/, /f/ and /l/; then retry, one card each, "What sound does this letter make?" (about 25 seconds a child; take up to 12, the rest wait for tomorrow's rotation). Group B practices name-and-sound cards in pairs. Switch for the next 5 minutes: group B retries with you and group A practices.

#### 10 min. Foundations: letters and sounds

Whole class. Review: k and y from day 53. Show the letter cards. Names: kay and why. Sounds: /k/, as in kangaroo, and /y/, as in yak. Chant: "k, kangaroo, /k/; y, yak, /y/." Sky-write each letter with a big arm: for k, a tall line down, then an arm in and a leg out; for y, a slant down, then a long slant down that dips below the line. Then sound to letter: say /k/ and children write k on handwriting paper; say /y/ and they write y. Write both the little and the big form of each. Walk among them and check the grip.

#### 10 min. Foundations: rhyme, syllables, onset and rime

Whole class, oral. Rhyme: say gray, day, play and ask which does not rhyme with look; children say a word that rhymes with day. Pairs: wet and net; look and book. Syllables: clap the beats of Dev (one), Priya (two), kitchen (two) and afternoon (three). Onset and rime: say the first sound and the rest, then blend with a hand sweep: r-ain, d-ay, s-un.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with three counters each. Isolate: say cup; children push a counter for the first sound, the middle, then the last, and say each (/k/, short u, /p/). Do the same with bed. Blend: you say /s/ /u/ /n/; children say sun. Segment: you say hen; children say the three sounds with counters. Add: say up, then add /k/ at the front: cup. Substitute: say bed, change /b/ to /r/: red. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: mapped words

Whole class. Review: he and we (day 37), on and to (days 41 and 42), and (day 47), of (day 50) and up (day 53). Hold up each card: children say the word, tap its sounds on their fingers and touch each letter. Put a star under the part to remember: the e in he and we, the o in to, the f in of; on, and and up need no star. New word, do (5 minutes): hold up the card and say it, /d/ /oo/. The d is a letter we know and says /d/. Here the o says /oo/, as in to; put a star under it. **Ask.** "Which word we know is like do?" Expect: to. Children read do from the card and find it in a line you write, "We do."

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class. Reread the strip "The sky is gray." from the journal with the pointer, left to right. Tap each space between words (three spaces). Point to the first letter and say its name: T. Show the letter cards T and t: it is one letter in two forms. Then find k in sky and y in sky and gray, and touch each.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the story, fill the three boxes with the cards, and draw. One projector: show each page and the cards on one screen; marking stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw seven small pictures on scrap paper. No window: use any picture of rain.

**Skip.** The group retry, the class journal and the pair work.

- **10 min. Story.** Read Priya at the Window aloud with the pictures, once through. Ask who, where and what changed.
  **Say.** "Who watches the rain? Where is she? What changes?" Expect: Priya; at the kitchen window; the rain gets lighter and stops.
- **10 min. Three boxes.** Your child draws one picture in each box on side 1: who, where, what happens.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints k and y on side 2.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X54v2]] Read page 6 of The Picnic Plan. Ask your child to tell you who, where and what happens.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the first hearing, the model and map, and the exit; give the questions to the pairs for one minute only. Longer: a secure child adds a second event to the map and says it in a sentence.

### Words and supports

Words: window, room, observer, singer. Frames: "Priya is the ___." "Priya is at the ___." "The rain ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group. No child tells what the weather is like at home.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 opens with 5 minutes for the more common of the errors G, H and I on today's exit: match a sentence about an action to its event picture, then one fresh card. Then the class hears Priya again and begins its own picture weather journal. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet.
