# Word-part cards

Eight cards for pairs on day 58, large letters, with a small picture on each. Children do not read them alone; the teacher says each word and the part to look for.

## The cards

- refilled: a cup filled again (re-).
- retell: a story told again (re-).
- reopen: a door opened again (re-).
- precut: a loaf cut before the meal (pre-).
- drops: three raindrops (-s, more than one).
- clouds: two clouds (-s, more than one).
- observer: a child at a window (-er, a person who).
- singer: a child singing (-er, a person who).

## How to use them

Pairs sort the cards under three part cards, **re- again**, **pre- before**, **-s more than one**, and set the two -er cards apart. The partner says the whole word, the part, and what the part tells. The other fifteen children are heard in one-minute word conferences.

## Key (teacher)

re- means again, pre- means before, -s means more than one, -er means a person who. A child who names only the base word for the changed word, or picks a picture that shows only the base word, ignored the part (AD). The exit card for this skill is not in this set.
