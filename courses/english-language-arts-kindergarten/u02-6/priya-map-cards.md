# Priya at the Window: map cards

Picture cards for the three-box story-map on day 54, hand-drawn on index cards, one picture each, plain black line on white. Children do not read them; the teacher says the label while pointing. A card may be held, moved and put back.

## Who (two cards)

- Priya: a girl with a long braid, kneeling, a notebook and a crayon in her hands.
- Dev: an older brother with a book.

## Where and when (two cards)

- The kitchen window: a large window with a chair beside it.
- A rainy afternoon: a gray sky and drops on the glass.

## What happens (three cards, in order)

- Rain falls: drops slide down the glass.
- The rain gets lighter: the drops are smaller and farther apart.
- The rain stops and the sun comes out: a patch of blue, sunshine on wet leaves.

## How to use them

Day 54, the teacher puts the cards in the three boxes while saying the page, then children, in pairs, fill their own paper map from a second set. On day 55 the cards are on the wall; on day 57 the map is set beside the map of The Magic Bow.

## Key (teacher)

Who holds people who act, Priya and Dev, never the window or the notebook (G). Where holds the place, the kitchen window and the rainy afternoon (H if another place). What happens holds the three events in order, never one object alone (I).
