# Day 58: Practice: a weather report, and what word parts tell

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the five-place chart. Lay out the nine sort cards under their headings, face down. Put the eight word-part cards in an envelope for each pair. Split the roster in two; the first half is heard in the word conferences. Make the day's six: the next six unheard. Make six groups of five for the cycle 8 check. Keep the exit cards to yourself. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 30 min. Weather inquiry and report (W.K.2, SL.K.4 to 6, RI.K.1)

Whole class, then pairs, then alone. Sort (5 minutes). Read page 3 of Weather Changes and page 2 of Rain and Snow aloud (about 2 minutes). Then you read the nine cards one at a time and children say which heading fits: seen weather (something we can see now), information (a true fact from a page) or a story event (what someone does in a made-up story). Cards: seen weather, the sky through the window, a printed gray sky, rain on the glass; information, "Rain is drops of water.", "Snow falls as flakes." and the card from the reading about drops; story event, Tomas holds a hand out to the first drop, Priya writes in her notebook, the woman speaks to the people. Say each sorted card back: "This is information, because a page says it."
Model and draw (10 minutes). Review: the five-place chart from day 49 (place, weather, one detail). Model one report on the report page with the Mawsynram column: topic, "Rain"; one true fact, "Rain falls and falls"; what people do in the text, "carry rain gear". Draw the fact big. Then each child chooses one weather fact from the readings and what people do or wear in the text, never at home, and draws the fact in the box (8 minutes). The teacher writes the lines the children say.
Report (6 minutes). Pairs report, one minute each way (2 minutes, all at once). Then the day's six share with the class, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). **Ask.** After each: "What is your weather fact?" Expect: the topic and one true fact that the drawing shows.
Reflect (9 minutes). Pip reports "I like boots" and then whispers a fact. **Ask.** "Is that a fact? Can your partner hear?" Expect: a liking is not a fact; say the fact, loud enough for the partner. Show what a clear voice sounds like and a drawing that shows the fact. Children redraw or add to their report (6 minutes). Never name a child as the example of a mistake.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Whole class. If many children missed day 57's exit, repair the more common of errors J and AV. Lay the four Magic Bow retell cards out of order and reread the story's pages in order, saying first, then and last as each card is placed. For error AV, count the events Pip left out and add a card for each.

#### 20 min. Word work (L.K.4, RL.K.4, RI.K.4)

Whole class, then conferences and pairs. Whole class (5 minutes), with real things or pictures. Re-: Review: re- means again (day 53). Refilled is in The Picnic Plan (page 5); refills is in Rain and Snow (page 2). Pre-: the story says Grandma Rosa had precut the apples (page 1). Pre- means before, so precut means cut before; show the picture of a loaf cut before the meal. -s: Review: more than one (day 39), as in drops. -er: Review: a person who, as in observer (day 54). Each word is said, then its part, then what the part tells.
Conferences and cards (15 minutes). The first roster half (15 children) are heard, 1 minute each. Each draws one word-part card, says the word, points to it and asks, "What does ___ mean?" Then the child says the part and what it tells. Listen for error AB, a question about something else, such as "Can I go outside?"; model asking about the word. The other fifteen children work in pairs: they sort the eight cards under re- again, pre- before and -s more than one, and set the two -er cards apart. The partner says the whole word, the part and what it tells. Marking is done after the day (15 minutes); the second roster half is heard in a later conference round.

#### 5 min. Exit E58

Say: "This card is made up for practice." First card, read twice (40 seconds); then children draw the fact on the exit sheet (1 minute) and tell a partner, 30 seconds each way; listen to the day's six: ask [[s02-X58a]]. Second card, read twice (30 seconds); children mark a picture on the exit sheet: ask [[s02-X58b]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; score the voice and the drawing apart. Signs: a drawing of something else (error D); a voice too quiet to hear (error E); the first hanging or the coat taken down marked (error AD).

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 8, lesson 45: k and y). Rhyme: say kite, night and bite; children give another word that rhymes. Syllables: clap the beats of kitchen (two), kangaroo (three) and yellow (two).

#### 10 min. Foundations: k and y

Whole class. Chant the names, sounds and keywords: k, kangaroo, /k/; y, yak, /y/. Print k and y, little and big, in lines with no model. Say each sound as it is written.

#### 5 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class. Isolate the first and last sound of fog and net. Blend /f/ /o/ /g/; children say fog.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words up and do

Whole class. Read up, we and do in a line you write, "We look up. We do." with the pointer, left to right.

#### 15 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. Reread a journal strip, such as "The sky is gray over the school.", with the pointer and no speed target. Tap the spaces. Find the capital at the start and the period at the end. Pairs then take turns pointing under each word of a partner's journal line while the partner reads it back. Find k and y where they appear.

#### 20 min. Foundations: rotation and cycle 8 check

Six groups of five, about 3 minutes a group and 20 seconds to change (6 groups, 20 minutes). Teacher table: say /k/ and /y/ aloud and the group writes each letter on a card (30 seconds); then each child in turn answers four prompts: the name of k, the name of y, the sound of k, the sound of y (about 30 seconds a child). Record: the sound given for the name (error U), the wrong sound for y (error V), the wrong letter written for a sound (error W). The others work in pairs at stations: letter cards, handwriting, rereading the journal strip. Response and a fresh retry are the first 10 minutes of day 59.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Sort the cards, draw one fact and report it aloud, and sort the word-part cards. One projector: show the two pages and the sort cards; the drawing stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No sort cards: use any three pictures of weather and one of a story. No five-place chart: use a picture of rain.

**Skip.** The conferences, the cycle 8 check and the pair report.

- **10 min. Report.** Read a weather page or look at a weather picture. Your child draws one true fact big; you write the topic, the fact and what people do or wear in the picture.
  **Say.** "What is your weather fact? What do the people in the picture do or wear?" Expect: the topic and one fact the drawing shows, and what the people do or wear.
- **10 min. Word parts.** Say refilled, drops and observer. Ask what the part tells in each.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints k and y.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X58a]] [[s02-X58b]] Read twice: "Hail is ice that falls from clouds. Hail falls when it gets too heavy." Your child draws one fact and tells it loudly. Then read twice: "Pia rehangs the wet coat on the peg." Which of three pictures shows rehangs?

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the report, the conferences and the exit; leave the sort. Longer: a secure child reports two facts and shows which is seen weather and which is information.

### Words and supports

Words: report, fact, refilled, precut, drops, observer. Frames: "My topic is ___." "My fact is ___." "In the text, people ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group. No child tells what is worn or done at home.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 opens with 10 minutes for the cycle 8 check: response and a fresh retry for the sound given for a name (error U), the wrong sound for y (error V) and the wrong letter written (error W). Its 5-minute warm-up repairs the most missed of errors D, E and AD from today's exit (redraw one spoken detail); the other two go to small groups. Today's exit marks and the conference notes go on the record sheet.
