# Day 5: Check, questions about a short story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Six groups of five. The table needs the three Folio A pictures and the record sheet. Each pair keeps its turn card in the basket; do not prompt it. No reteaching. A child may pass.

### The day

#### 6 min. Set-up

Say: "This story is made up." Read The Puppet (Folio A) once, showing its three pictures. Children not at the table draw two children sharing a toy, not from home.

#### 48 min. Six groups of five, 8 minutes each

Groups come in the order of day 1's six. Pencils down. Each child asks the next child, who answers.

- A1, 2 minutes, about 24 seconds a child ([[s01-A1]]): ask one question about what Emi requests; answer the one put to you.
- A2, 1 minute, about 12 seconds a child ([[s01-A2]]): point to the picture that matches "Sol lifts the puppet high for Emi" and say the moment.
- A5, 3 minutes, about 36 seconds a child ([[s01-A5]]): ask where Emi asks, what happens next and what lifts means.
- A6, 2 minutes, about 24 seconds a child ([[s01-A6]]): ask about the key action, then ask politely for one phrase to be read again. Reread only that phrase.

Observe throughout [[s01-A3]] (waits, listens, answers the point) and [[s01-A4]] (question, answer, related question, answer). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: a home toy (error A), the singing picture (error B), talking over (error C), one answer then leaving (error D), a statement for a question (error E).

#### 6 min. Closing

Pairs say one thing about asking for help; hear three volunteers. Collect the drawings. No exit ticket today.

#### 3 min. ABC song

Sing it once, a finger moving left to right along the strip.

#### 5 min. Keyword t

Say top, /t/ together and read the map entry aloud. "Tee" for the sound: say the name, then the sound (error N).

#### 8 min. Printing t and T

Model lowercase t (down, then a cross at the middle line) and T (down, then across the top). Children print each once, saying /t/. Look for: starts at the top; the cross goes left to right; says /t/, not "tee". Writing l: hear the sound, pick the t card (error O). Only T: show the lowercase path (error P).

#### 2 min. The word my

Read "my" on your "my toy" labels together, as a whole word.

#### 2 min. Closing

Chant "t, t, /t/, top."

#### 40 min. Rotations

Familiar practice. At the table, the day's six print t and T and say /t/ (1 minute each, 2 for the move). Stations as day 3.

### Other rooms

One child: read Folio A and ask the same questions.

### At home

**Swap.** No Folio A page: tell the story aloud and draw three quick pictures. No alphabet strip: sing it.

**Skip.** The six groups and the stations.

- **10 min. The assessment, part one.** Read Folio A once with its pictures. Your child asks you what Emi asks for, then points to the picture where Sol lifts the puppet.
  **Say.** "What does Emi ask for?" Expect: to see the puppet.
- **10 min. The assessment, part two.** Your child asks three questions: where does Emi ask, what happens next, what does lifts mean? Then asks for one phrase to be read again.
- **10 min. Listening.** Do you wait, listen and ask a related question?
- **5 min. ABC.** Sing it once, pointing left to right.
- **10 min. Print t and T.** Your child prints each once, saying /t/.
- **5 min. My.** Read "my toy" together.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: A1 and A2 only. Longer: a second question.

### Words and supports

Words: question, help, lifts, my. Frames: "Where ___?" "What ___?"

### Tomorrow

Day 6 opens with a five-minute repair of the most common error, including error B from day 4's exit and day 5's items, then children dictate a class message.
