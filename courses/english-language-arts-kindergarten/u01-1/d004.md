# Day 4: A picture for the moment, and the sound t

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover the page 4 picture with paper, and set the page 6 picture beside it. Put the three word picture cards (scuffed, wobble, tight) and four picture cards in each pair's basket. Keep the talk-rules poster hung, and a turn card for each pair. Have the letter t card ready; the map entry is made at 8 minutes of the foundations block. Today's six are the fourth six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen and words

If many circled the pulling picture or said "Teo is happy" in day 3's exit (error A or error E), spend these minutes on the one more children made: reread page 7 of the story and point to the matching picture together, or model one question that gets the missing help. Otherwise teach three words: picture (a hand frames a picture), moment (a snap of the fingers: one moment) and the frame "What does ___ mean?"

#### 12 min. One page, two pictures

Read page 4 aloud with its picture covered. Show two pictures: the page 4 picture (Teo scuffing his shoe) and the page 6 picture (the friends face to face).

**Ask.** Which picture fits these words? Expect: the page 4 picture, Teo alone with his arms down.

**Ask.** Which words tell? Expect: "He scuffed his shoe in the grass" or "his arms hung down". Point back to the words and the picture. Say the frame "This shows ___": "This shows Teo waiting." Then say why the other picture does not fit: it shows two children talking, and the words do not say that (error B). If a child chooses the other picture, hold both beside the reread sentence and match the action.

#### 10 min. Ask what a word means

Say: "I do not know the word scuffed. I ask, What does scuffed mean?" A child acts it (rubs a shoe on the floor). Each pair picks one of three picture cards (page 4, 8 or 5); you say its word again: scuffed, wobble or tight. One partner asks "What does ___ mean?", the other answers by acting or saying, then they swap. Hear three pairs. If nobody knows, say the meaning once: scuffed is dragging a shoe on the ground, wobble is rocking from side to side, tight is squeezed small.

#### 14 min. Match a different moment

Each pair holds four picture cards: pages 5, 7, 8 and 9. Read aloud one sentence at a time: "Teo's whole face lit up" (page 8). Pairs point to the matching picture, say "This shows ___", and say why. Then read two more, each with its page's picture to find: "Then she looked at Teo. Teo was looking at the ground." (page 5) and "When the bell rang, Noor put the hoop back in the basket." (page 9). One pair at a time shows its answer to the class. Pointing, acting or a home language counts.

#### 11 min. Draw the moment

Reread page 9. Each child draws one moment from it on the student page, then tells a partner which words it shows. A child may point to the words.

#### 4 min. Reflect

**Ask.** How does a picture help the words? Expect: it shows what the words tell.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Read page 2 of the story aloud again with its picture covered; today's work used other pages. Children circle the picture that goes with the last sentence on the day 4 strip: Noor running after the rolling hoop, the two friends running to the basket, or Teo asking Noor for a turn ([[s01-X4v2]], part a: 2 minutes). Hear today's six, 30 seconds each: "What could you ask if one word on this page was new to you?" Expect a question such as "What does edge mean?" or "What does called mean?". Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

Signs: the friends running to the basket (a picture of a different moment, error B); "I do not know that word" (a statement, error E).

#### 5 min. Showing the sound t

Say the sound /t/ with a tongue tap, short and crisp, with no "uh" after it. Everyone taps and says it: tap, tap, /t/. No letter story is read; children only say the sound and do the tap.

#### 8 min. The letter t and its keyword

Show the letter t card with the keyword picture, a top. Say the name "tee", then the sound /t/ (error N: if a child says "tee" for the sound, say the name, then the sound with the keyword). Hang the t card on the sound-spelling map. A keyword picture reminds us of the sound; it never helps us guess a word. Say the keyword and the sound together: top, /t/.

#### 4 min. Lines for t

In the air, on a partner's back and on paper: a straight line down, then a straight line across.

#### 3 min. Closing

Chant together: "t, t, /t/, top."

#### 40 min. Rotations

Five groups of six, five turns of 8 minutes. At the table, each child says the name and the sound of t and finds t on the map (1 minute a child, 2 minutes for the move). Table notes go on the record sheet for today's six only. Stations: write t with a finger in the sand tray, draw and label, name-card match, big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: read page 4, show the two pictures, ask the two questions; show the letter t and say its sound. One projector: show pages 4 and 6 together.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: use the printed pictures. No sand tray: write t with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The word cards, the map, the stations.

- **5 min. One page, two pictures.** Read page 4, then show the page 4 and page 6 pictures.
  **Say.** "Which picture fits these words?" Expect: Teo alone, shoe scuffing.
- **5 min. What does it mean?** Say "I do not know scuffed. What does scuffed mean?" Your child acts it.
  **Say.** "What does wobble mean?" Expect: rocking from side to side.
- **5 min. Match the moment.** Read "Teo's whole face lit up". Your child points to the matching picture.
- **5 min. Draw the moment.** Your child draws one moment from page 9.
- **5 min. The sound t.** Tap and say /t/. Show a top: top, /t/.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X4v2]] Read page 2 of the story aloud. Your child circles the picture and asks about a new word.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: use two pictures of the matching part and cut the draw; keep the word question, the sound and the exit. Longer: a child who is sure explains why the other picture does not fit.

### Words and supports

Words: picture, moment, mean, top, sound. Frames: "What does ___ mean?" "This shows ___." A home language, signs and pointing count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the friends running to the basket (error B) in day 4's exit, day 6 opens with a five-minute repair; day 5 does no repair because it is a check day. Day 5 is the check: you observe small groups talk about a short story you read once, and children print t and T.
