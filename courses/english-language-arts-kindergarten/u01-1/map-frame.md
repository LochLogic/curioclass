# The sound-spelling map (class wall chart)

A large chart for the wall, hung empty on day 3 and filled one card at a time. Each card holds the letter in both cases, a keyword picture, and a short space for the sound. Say the keyword, say the sound, point to the letter. A keyword reminds children of a sound; they never use it to guess a word.

Draw the frame as a grid of four tall cards, left to right, with thick black lines on white paper and no printed words except the letters.

## Card 1: t (added on day 4)

**Picture:** a spinning top, seen from the side. **Letters:** T and t. **Sound:** /t/, the sound at the start of top.

## Card 2: a (added on day 7)

**Picture:** a red apple with a leaf. **Letters:** A and a. **Sound:** short /a/, the sound at the start of apple.

## Card 3: h (added on day 14)

**Picture:** a hat on a peg. **Letters:** H and h. **Sound:** /h/, the sound at the start of hat.

## Card 4: p (added on day 14)

**Picture:** a green parrot on a branch. **Letters:** P and p. **Sound:** /p/, the sound at the start of parrot.

Leave the space for later cards blank. Do not add a word to a card beyond the keyword name said aloud.
