# Day 2: One Hoop, Two Friends, and who made a book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make or print one booklet for each pair, with the title page behind the front cover (title, then the line "Words by the Curio Class writing team. Pictures by the Curio Class art team."). Put Push, Pull, Roll and the exit strips aside. Hang the talk-rules poster, and keep the author and illustrator labels beside the pencil and brush pictures. Today's six are the second six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and words

If many circled one dot or said "I say my idea too" in day 1's exit (error E or error C), spend these minutes on the one more children made. For error E, the puppet says "This lid is stuck" beside "Can you help me open it?" and children turn two statements into questions. For error C, the puppet talks over you beside waiting with the turn pause; pairs rehearse waiting. Otherwise skip the repair. Gesture and say three words: share (two hands pass one thing between them), ask (a hand raised with a question face), answer (a hand out, palm up).

#### 3 min. The closed book

Hold up the closed storybook One Hoop, Two Friends. Show the front cover (the picture and title), turn it over to the back cover, and run a finger down the spine. Say: "This is a storybook. Yesterday we heard a poem."

#### 12 min. First hearing, whole

Say: "Noor and Teo are made up, and so is this story." Read all nine pages aloud once without stopping, holding up each picture. Do not stop to question; the story is for listening. Content note: a child waits and feels left out; there is no injury, death, violence or home life.

#### 5 min. Talk

**Ask.** What does the title name? Expect: a hoop, two friends.

**Ask.** Which toy did they have? Expect: a big red hoop. Pointing to the cover picture counts.

Partners then tell each other one thing they remember; a child may point instead.

#### 10 min. Title page and covers

Pairs share a booklet. Model with your copy: hold up the front cover (title and picture), turn to the title page (the title again and the makers' names), then the back cover (the plain outside back). Pairs find each, hold it up and name it. Walk around; say "Show me the back cover" to a pair and watch. If a pair holds up the last inside page, close the book together and find its outside (error AC).

#### 8 min. Who made it

Show a pencil picture and a brush picture with two label cards. Read the credits aloud: "Words by the Curio Class writing team. Pictures by the Curio Class art team." The author makes the words (write in the air); the illustrator makes the pictures (paint in the air). Children make each gesture.

**Ask.** Who made the pictures? Expect: the illustrator, or pointing to the brush. If a child says the illustrator writes the words, match the pencil to the author and the brush to the illustrator, then try again (error I).

#### 12 min. Draw one thing from the story

Each child draws one thing from the story on the student page, then tells a partner what the picture shows, pointing to the part. A child may point alone. Say the English back for a home-language answer.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Hold up Push, Pull, Roll, a book we have not heard yet. Show its front cover, page 2 and its back cover, then read the two credit lines on its title page aloud. Each child circles the back cover on the strip of three pictures ([[s01-X2v2]], part a: 2 minutes). Hear today's six, 30 seconds each: "Who made the pictures in this book?" Expect: the art team, the illustrator. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

Signs: page 2 circled (error AC); "the writing team" (error I).

#### 6 min. The word we heard, again

Name cards. Slide a finger under the whole name, left to right: "A name is one word made of letters." The puppet points to each letter in turn and says "That is five words" (error G, a puppet error only). Show the whole name under one finger: one word.

#### 11 min. Letters and words

Pairs sort a few name-card letter tiles and whole name cards into two piles: "one word" and "letters" (one word, many letters). Then open the class big book to page 1. With the picture covered (error S), slide a finger under each word as you read: We, have, blocks; then the second line, Blocks, stack, up. Put a green dot at the start edge so the finger starts there and goes left to right (error H); then top line, then the line below it. Children copy the finger on their own hand.

#### 3 min. Closing

Say: "Show me one word and one letter." Children point to a name card and a single letter tile.

#### 40 min. Rotations

Five groups of six, five turns of 8 minutes. At the table, each child hears one pair of words and says same or different at the end: mine and fine (same), ball and cart (different), day and play (same), turn and mine (different), today and say (same), top and cart (different). Then each child claps the beats of one toy picture: top, cart, ball (1 beat), wagon, puzzle (2). That is one minute a child, two for the move. Table notes go on the record sheet for today's six only. Stations as day 1: draw and label, name-card match, big-book browse, sand tray.

### Other rooms

One child: read the story pages aloud, ask the two questions, show the covers on any book. One projector: show the nine pictures one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No story booklet: read the printed story aloud and use any book for its covers. No pencil and brush pictures: draw them quickly.

**Skip.** The big book, the stations, the name cards.

- **5 min. Look at a book.** Show the front cover, the back cover and the title page of any book.
- **10 min. The story.** Read One Hoop, Two Friends aloud once, holding up the pictures.
  **Say.** "Which toy did they have?" Expect: a big red hoop.
- **5 min. Who made it.** Author makes the words; illustrator makes the pictures. Your child shows each with a hand.
  **Say.** "Who made the pictures?" Expect: the illustrator.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one thing from the story and says what it shows.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2v2]] Show the back of any book and ask who made its pictures.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the sorting of letter tiles; keep the hearing, the covers, the makers and the exit. Longer: a child who is sure finds the title page and says why it comes first.

### Words and supports

Words: storybook, front cover, back cover, title page, author, illustrator. Frames: "The front cover is ___." "The illustrator makes ___." A home language, signs and pointing count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many circled page 2 (error AC) or said the writing team made the pictures (error I) in day 2's exit, day 3 opens with a four-minute repair: close the book and find its back, or match the pencil and brush to the makers. Then we reread pages 3 to 6 of the story and ask what each friend says and does.
