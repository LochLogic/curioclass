# Day 3: Questions about what the friends say and do

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the four picture cards (pages 3 to 6) in each pair's basket with a turn card. Hang the talk-rules poster where children can see it. Keep the name cards and the author and illustrator labels at hand for the repair. Make the empty sound-spelling map frame ready but do not hang a card on it. Today's six are the third six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen and words

If many circled page 2 or said the writing team made the pictures in day 2's exit (error AC or error I), use these minutes on the one more children made: close the book and find its outside back, or match the pencil and brush to the author and illustrator. Otherwise teach two words with gestures: turn (a hand passes across) and wait (hands in lap).

#### 14 min. Return to pages 3 to 6

Show page 3 and read it again. Then point back: find the words that tell and the picture that shows.

**Ask.** What does Teo ask? Expect: "Can I have a turn?" Point to Teo's raised hand in the picture and to the line under it.

Read page 4.

**Ask.** What does Teo do while he waits? Expect: he watches, his arms hang down, he scuffs his shoe.

Read page 5.

**Ask.** What does Noor look at? Expect: Teo, and the hoop.

Read page 6.

**Ask.** What does Noor ask Teo? Expect: "Did you want a turn?"

For each answer, hold the page beside the child's pointing hand. Pointing, acting or a home language counts. The questions ask only about the story, never about home (error A: if a child tells about a hoop at home, thank them and say "In our story ...").

#### 8 min. Model asking and answering

Ask a question about page 5 yourself and answer it by pointing: "Who stopped smiling?" Then say "I heard ..." and name what the page says: "I heard Noor's tummy felt tight." A child asks one question about the page (starters: Who? What?), and the class answers by pointing. Say once: "A question asks. An answer tells." A statement given as an answer to a question is welcome; ask for the question next.

#### 12 min. Partner questions

Pairs with the four picture cards and a turn card: the child holding the card asks one question, the partner answers by pointing and saying, then the turn card swaps. Model first with the puppet, which asks, you answer, then the puppet asks a related question, and you answer again: that is a two-turn exchange. Pairs try two exchanges each. Hear three pairs, one minute each. A child may pass. Never name a child as a mistake; the puppet voices any wrong example.

#### 10 min. Question and answer cards

Say four questions, one at a time, and every pair points to the picture card with the answer, then says it in a sentence frame ("Teo asks for a turn"). The four questions: Who asks for a turn? (Teo, page 3.) Who says "In a minute"? (Noor, page 3.) What does Teo do while he waits? (scuffs his shoe, page 4.) What does Noor ask Teo? ("Did you want a turn?" page 6.) Walk and look at the cards held up; each question takes about 2 minutes with the class answering together.

#### 7 min. Talk rules check

Point to the poster. Partners tell each other, then the class: Which rule did Teo follow? Expect: wait, or take a turn. Which rule did Noor forget? Expect: look, or listen. Teo and Noor are story people; no child is the example.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Read page 7 of the story aloud again; the class has not worked on it yet. Children circle how the friends will share the hoop on the day 3 strip: the friends counting to ten, the friends pulling the hoop between them, or Noor walking off with it ([[s01-X3v2]], part a: 2 minutes). Hear today's six, 30 seconds each: "Ask me one question about what Teo says on this page." Expect a question such as "Is it Teo's turn next?" or "What does Teo say?". Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

Signs: the pulling picture (an idea that is not on the page, error A); a statement such as "Teo is happy" (error E).

#### 4 min. The word I

Show the poem Our Play in large print. Find the word I in "then I take mine." Say: "This is a word, and it is a letter, both at once." Read it whole; children do not sound it out. Hold up the card with the word I and say it together.

#### 7 min. Skywriting

Large arm motions for straight lines (down, across) and curved lines (a hill, a bowl) in the air and on a partner's back, then fingers on the table. No letter names today. Say: "Straight down. Straight across. Round we go."

#### 6 min. Handwriting paper

Show the three lines: top, middle, bottom. Children trace the two dashed lines on the practice-lines page from left to right, then draw two straight lines down and two curved lines on handwriting paper, starting at the top and moving down. Watch the thumb and finger grip on the thick pencil.

#### 3 min. Closing and the map

Hang the empty sound-spelling map. Say how it will work: say the keyword, say the sound, point to the letter. A keyword picture reminds us of a sound; it never helps us guess a word. Nothing is on it yet.

#### 40 min. Rotations

Five groups of six, five turns of 8 minutes. At the table, each child finds the word I on the poem, says it, and says "I take a turn" with a finger under the poem line (1 minute a child, 2 minutes for the move). Add one beat clap for each of: top, wagon, puzzle, cart, ball, blocks. Table notes go on the record sheet for today's six only. Stations: line making in the sand tray, draw and label, name-card match, big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: read pages 3 to 6, ask the four questions, answer by pointing. One projector: show page 3 to 6 pictures one at a time while children point on their own cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: use the printed story pictures. No turn card: pass a spoon.

**Skip.** The sand tray, the map and the class stations.

- **5 min. Return to the pages.** Read pages 3 to 6 of the story aloud. Point to the picture and the words that tell.
  **Say.** "What does Teo ask?" Expect: "Can I have a turn?"
- **5 min. Ask and answer.** You ask one question; your child answers by pointing. Then your child asks you one.
  **Say.** "What does Noor ask Teo?" Expect: "Did you want a turn?"
- **5 min. Two turns.** Pass a spoon: the one holding it speaks. Ask, answer, ask a related question, answer.
- **5 min. Lines.** Your child draws two straight lines down and two curved lines, starting at the top.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws what Teo does while he waits.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3v2]] Read page 7 of the story aloud. Your child circles the counting picture and asks a question about what Teo says.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep pages 3 and 6 for the return; keep the exit. Longer: a child who is sure asks a second question about page 4.

### Words and supports

Words: question, answer, wait, turn. Frames: "I heard ___." "Who ___?" "What ___?" A home language, signs and pointing count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the pulling picture (error A) or said "Teo is happy" (error E) in day 3's exit, day 4 opens with a four-minute repair: reread page 7 and match the picture, or model one question that gets the missing help. Then we match a picture to the moment just heard and ask what a word means.
