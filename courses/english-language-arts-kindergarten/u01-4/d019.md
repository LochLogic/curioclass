# Day 19: Respond, a fresh choice and a fact

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet: day 17's items and day 18's items (the revision, listening and turn-taking, talking on, and the scored piece) and the notes for h and p from days 14 to 17. Choose the one error most children made. Put the puppet and the opinion chart where the whole class sees them. Today's six are the nineteenth six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reteach and fresh retry

Whole class, then alone. Seven minutes of reteach, from the most common error of days 15 to 18, which is nearly always error L, a toy named with no preference. The puppet says "This is a wagon." Then it says "My favorite classroom toy is the wagon." Review: statement: a statement tells (day 1), and the opinion routine from day 15: a statement tells what a toy is; an opinion says what you prefer.

**Ask.** Which sentence tells what the toy is, and which tells what the puppet prefers? Expect: "This is a wagon" tells; "My favorite classroom toy is the wagon" says what it prefers. Each child turns to a partner and adds a preference to a named toy: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___" (a child may pass and point to a picture). If error M or error X from day 16's exit was more common, use that instead: the puppet draws a wagon with only the word "wagon" (error M), or only a happy face (error X); the class adds one fact the picture shows, such as "The wagon rolls."

Then eight minutes of retry. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the day 19 card (the shelf). Read aloud once, a new menu: "The shelf has a doll, a puzzle and bells." Children circle the toy for their opinion box ([[s01-X19]], part a). Hear the day's six, a minute each: "Say your sentence." Expect "My favorite classroom toy is the ___" naming the circled toy. The retry is not scored. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the sheet, but add nothing to the unit scores.

#### 8 min. Reread A Toy Can Turn, pages 2 to 5

Whole class on the rug. Review: A Toy Can Turn, which you heard on days 14 and 16. Read pages 2 to 5 aloud, pointing under the words and holding up each picture: a top spins, a spinning top leans, a wheel spins, a toy car turns.

**Ask.** What turns, and which words tell how? Expect: the top turns, and the words "spins round and round" tell how; the wheel turns; the car turns right and left when you steer it. Children point to the picture and then under the sentence you reread.

#### 12 min. Partner share

Pairs on the rug, each pair with a turn card. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass (day 1). Each child picks the toy from their day 16 drawing and tells a partner one fact about it and their preference: "It rolls. My favorite classroom toy is the wagon." The partner says it back. They swap: two exchanges each way. Hear three pairs, a minute each; the class says "I heard ___." A child who gives only the toy's name hears the puppet say the preference sentence beside it.

#### 15 min. Add a fact

Alone. Each child adds one fact you can see about the toy to their drawing, and says it (informative practice, W.K.2). Review: a fact is a true thing you can see (day 16). The child dictates the fact to a partner, who says it back. Then you take dictation from the day's six, a minute each, writing the fact under the drawing, finger under each word, left to right. Others tell you the fact as you walk. A child who prefers to point may point to a part of the drawing. A reason is welcome and never needed.

#### 10 min. Share and keep

Whole class. Six children share the drawing, the fact and the preference sentence; the class says "I heard ___." A child may pass. Drawings go to the portfolio. Day 16's piece, revised on day 17, stays in the folder with its marks.

#### 3 min. Opening

Whole class. Letter chant for h, p, a and t: "h, h, /h/, hat; p, p, /p/, parrot; a, a, /a/, apple; t, t, /t/, top." (Review: letters h, p, a and t with their keywords.)

#### 10 min. Foundations response

Whole class. Review: repair then fresh retry: wrong example beside a right one, then a new try (day 6). The puppet shows the one slip most children made in cycle 2, and you show the right way (4 minutes). This replaces the usual prompt.

- Error N: the puppet says "aitch" for /h/. Say the name, then the sound with the letter and the keyword hat.
- Error O: the puppet prints p when it hears /h/. Hear the sound, choose the letter card first, then print.
- Error P: the puppet prints only H when lowercase is asked. Model the lowercase path; each child prints it once.
- Error AA: the puppet calls the letter h a "p". Hold the two cards side by side and name each.
- The word I or the beats: point to I in Our Play and say it whole; or say "robot" and tap its two beats, not its four sounds.

Then a fresh retry on a new strip (6 minutes), not scored: say /p/, each child holds up the p card and prints lowercase p in box 1; say /h/, holds up the h card and prints lowercase h in box 2; say /p/ and prints capital P in box 3. Look for: starts at the top, the line is straight, the bump or hump closes, says the sound.

#### 4 min. Map

Whole class at the sound-spelling map. Review: the sound-spelling map: keyword, sound, letter (day 3). For the letters of the response, go both directions. Say the sound and a child points to the letter. Then point to a letter and the class says the sound and the keyword: "h, /h/, hat." Do h, p, a and t.

#### 3 min. Closing

Whole class. Chant for h and p.

#### 40 min. Rotations

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five 8-minute turns. Five groups of six. At the table each child gets a minute (6 minutes, 2 for the move): the retry for the one slip they made, then the name and sound of h and p and the word I. Table notes for the day's six go on the record sheet. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger h and p in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: use the puppet or a soft toy for the two sentences, then do the retry card and add one fact to the drawing. Show the h and p cards on the map. One projector: show the page and the chart on one screen; children point and say the sentence.

### At home

**Swap.** No puppet: use a soft toy. No chart: use paper. No sand tray: write with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The pairs, the share, the rotations and the stations.

- **5 min. Two sentences.** Hold a toy. Say "This is a ___." Then say "My favorite is the ___."
  **Say.** "Which one says what I like?" Expect: "My favorite is the ___."
- **5 min. Read.** Read page 2 of the turning book; your child points to what turns.
- **5 min. A fact.** Your child adds one fact to their drawing.
  **Say.** "What can you see the toy do?" Expect: "It rolls" or "It spins."
- **5 min. h and p.** Say each sound; your child prints each once.
- **5 min. The map.** Say /h/; your child points to the letter.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X19]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles one toy, then says the sentence.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the share and the map; keep the reteach, the retry and the added fact. Longer: a child who is secure adds a drawn arrow for a reason and says "because".

### Words and supports

Words: favorite, prefer, fact, turn. Frames: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___." "It ___." "This is a ___." A home language, signs and pointing count as answers; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The retry is not scored. Day 20 begins section 1.5: it reads a story about a child who plays her own way and asks what the words show about her.
