# The class opinion chart and sentence strips (day 15)

A large chart for the wall and a set of strips for the children. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading.

## The chart

A wide sheet divided into three boxes in a row. Each box holds the picture of one classroom toy: a ball, a jump rope and a set of blocks. Above the three boxes, the heading in large letters: Our favorite toys. Under each box, a blank strip where the teacher writes "Our favorite classroom toy is the ___" after the class chooses.

## The sentence strips

Each strip is a long thin band of paper with the sentence frame printed in large type: "My favorite classroom toy is the" and a short blank line. Make several for each child to hold. The teacher writes the toy name on request, finger under each word, left to right. A child may add a drawn arrow to show one reason.

## How the chart and strips are used

On day 15 the class shows hands for one toy and the teacher writes the class sentence. Pairs then use the strips and say "My favorite classroom toy is the ___" and "What is your favorite?" The chart returns on day 18 for the shared reading. A child who prefers not to choose may point to a picture or pass.
