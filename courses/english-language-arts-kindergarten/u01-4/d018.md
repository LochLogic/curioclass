# Day 18: Check, my drawn opinion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Six at the table, one minute each. Each child has a folder with the day 16 piece, revised on day 17; you score it after class. The talk-rules poster stays up; do not prompt. Today's six are the eighteenth six names on the record sheet. No repair and no reteaching. A child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

Whole class. Say: "Today I look at your work. I do not teach." Day 17's items (the revision, listening and turn-taking, and talking on) are on the record sheet; nothing is repaired today. Look together at the day 15 class opinion chart: "Our favorite classroom toy is the ___."

#### 10 min. Reread Push, Pull, Roll, pages 4 to 7

Whole class on the rug. Review: Push, Pull, Roll, which you heard on days 9, 15 and 16. Read pages 4 to 7 aloud, pointing under the words and holding up each picture.

**Ask.** Which toys do you push? Which do you pull? Expect: children point to the cart or the box they push, and the duck or the wagon they pull. Point back to the words and the picture. No scoring.

#### 20 min. Draw and confer

Alone. Each child draws how one classroom toy moves for the portfolio (12 minutes), with a real toy beside them if they want one. Call the day's six to the table, one minute each. Each shows the revised day 16 piece and says their preference sentence; you check that it matches the drawing and write nothing new. Hearing a child say it is evidence for [[s01-O4]], which you score after class. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet. A child who prefers not to answer is not yet seen and is asked again later in the week.

#### 10 min. Partner share

Pairs on the rug. Review: turn and talk: ask, answer, swap, say one thing back; a child may pass (day 1). Each child tells their partner the toy they drew and the way it moves, in two exchanges (6 minutes). Pairs then swap drawings with another pair and say what they see (4 minutes).

#### 8 min. Shared reading

Whole class at the chart. Point under each word of "Our favorite classroom toy is the ___" from left to right and the class says it with you. Then one child points under each word while the class says it again. Review: left to right: a finger starts at the green dot and moves under each word.

#### 8 min. Portfolio and tidy

Collect the folders. Say: "I will look at your work after class." Tell the class that a toy name alone is a good start, and that a sentence that says what you like is an opinion. After class, score the day 16 piece, revised on day 17, in about 30 minutes ([[s01-O4]]). The prompt is: name a classroom toy and tell your preference. Mark the topic and the preference separately, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The topic is shown when a classroom toy is named in the drawing, a label or the dictated sentence. The preference is shown when "favorite" or "prefer" is said or dictated. A drawing, dictation and letters all count; a reason and spelling are not scored. Shown: a drawing of a top with "My favorite toy is the top." Not yet seen: "This is a top" (error L, a toy named with no preference). Keep the marks for day 19 and for day 33.

#### 3 min. Opening

Whole class. Sing the ABC song along the alphabet strip, the pointer under each letter left to right. Then the letter chant for h, p, a and t. (Review: letters h, p, a and t with their keywords.)

#### 10 min. The word I

Whole class. Review: mapped word I, read whole in Our Play (day 3). Say: "I am thinking of a word. It is a word we remember. It is a capital letter. It is me." Give the three clues one at a time and wait after each. Then point under "I" in "then I take mine" on the large poem Our Play. The class says it. Say: "We do not sound it out. We know it by sight." Children find a capital I on the alphabet strip and touch it. Then write "I like my toy." and point under each word: the class says it, and children point to I and to my. The other words are read to them.

A child who says a word from the picture instead of the letters (error S, a word named from its picture) sees the picture covered and the finger put under the letter.

#### 4 min. Feel the beats

Whole class. Review: clap the beats of a toy name, as at the table on day 13. Say "puzzle" and tap its beats on your hand: one, two. Then "robot": one, two. Children tap with you. Say the whole word first, then tap. Do not count sounds: "puzzle" has two beats, not five sounds (error R, counting sounds as parts). Ask for the beats of "ball" (one) and "teddy" (two).

#### 3 min. Closing

Whole class. Chant for h and p.

#### 40 min. Rotations

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five 8-minute turns. Five groups of six. Familiar practice only. At the table each child gets a minute (6 minutes, 2 for the move): say the name and the sound of h, p, a and t, and point to the word I on the chart. Table notes for the day's six go on the record sheet. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger h and p in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: do the reread, draw one toy that moves, and say the opinion sentence. Show the word I and clap two toy names. One projector: show the pages and the chart on one screen; children point.

### At home

**Swap.** No class toys: use a toy from a shelf. No chart: write the sentence on paper. No sand tray: write with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The six at the table, the partner share, the rotations and the stations.

- **10 min. The assessment.** Your child draws one toy that moves and says what they like best. Write what they say under the drawing.
  **Say.** "Which toy do you like best?" Expect: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___."
- **5 min. Look.** Read the page aloud; your child points to the toy.
- **10 min. The word I.** Give three clues; your child points to I on a card.
- **5 min. Beats.** Say "teddy" and tap its beats. Your child taps with you.
- **10 min. h and p.** Say the name and the sound; your child prints each once.
- **10 min. Share.** Your child tells you about the drawing, and you ask one question.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the drawing and conference, and the portfolio. Longer: a child who is secure adds a drawn reason arrow to the drawing and says it.

### Words and supports

Words: favorite, prefer, opinion, beat. Frames: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___." "It ___." A home language, signs and pointing count as answers; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 acts on day 18's items: it opens with a 15-minute reteach of the most common error of days 15 to 18, nearly always error L, and a fresh retry; rereads A Toy Can Turn; and has children add a fact to their drawings. The marks from the record sheet say who needs the reteach.
