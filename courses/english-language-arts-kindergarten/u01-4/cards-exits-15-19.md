# Exit cards and the retry card, days 15, 16 and 19

Each card is marked by every child and goes with a spoken question heard from the day's six. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Day 15 card: the shelf

**Read:** "The class shelf has a drum and a puzzle. The drum is loud. The puzzle has pieces."

**Picture:** two pictures side by side, each in a ring: a drum; a puzzle.

**Mark:** circle the toy you choose for your opinion box. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Say your sentence about that toy."

## Day 16 card: the rocking horse

**Read:** "The class rocking horse rocks back and forth. It stops by the door."

**Picture:** none; one large empty box for the child's drawing.

**Draw:** in the box, the horse, so the drawing teaches a reader how it moves. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "What will you tell a reader about the horse?"

## Day 19 retry card (not scored)

**Read:** "The shelf has a doll, a puzzle and bells."

**Picture:** three pictures in a row, each in a ring: a doll; a puzzle; a set of bells.

**Mark:** circle the toy for your opinion box. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Say your sentence."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 15: either picture; the choice is the child's. Spoken: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___" naming the circled toy. Not yet: "The drum is loud" or "The puzzle has pieces" (L, a toy named with no preference).
- Day 16: the horse with motion lines. Spoken: "It rocks" or "It rocks back and forth." Not yet: the happy face with no motion (X); "It is a horse" (M).
- Day 19: any picture. Spoken: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___." Not yet: "This is a puzzle" (L). The retry is not scored.

Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may point, act or use a home language; say the English back.
