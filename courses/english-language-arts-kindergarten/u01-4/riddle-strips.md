# Riddle strips (day 17)

Three short riddles for the teacher to read aloud, each on a long strip with a small picture of its answer on the back. Children do not read them. Each riddle gives three clues, one after the other. After each clue, ask "What toy could it be?" Then ask "Which clue helped you?"

## Riddle 1: the ball

**Read:** "I am round. I roll when you push me. You can catch me with two hands. What am I?" **Answer picture on the back:** a ball.

## Riddle 2: the jump rope

**Read:** "I am long. You hold me by the handles. I go over your head and under your feet. What am I?" **Answer picture on the back:** a jump rope.

## Riddle 3: the blocks

**Read:** "We are many. We have flat sides. You stack us up, and we can fall down. What are we?" **Answer picture on the back:** a set of blocks.

## How the strips are used

Read one riddle on day 17. Children show the picture they think fits and say why. Keep the other two for later reuse. A child may pass or point. No child is told a guess is wrong; say the next clue and ask again.
