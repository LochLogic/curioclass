# Day 17: Check, choosing a toy and talking about it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Six groups of five. The table needs the record sheet. After day 16, read each opinion page and stick on one picture sticker with one line you will say aloud: a toy with a question mark ("Which toy?"), a heart ("Say what you like best") or a star ("Add one thing you see"). Do not score today. The talk-rules poster stays up and is not prompted. A child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Set-up

Say: "Today I listen. I do not teach." Name the six groups of five and say what the others do: draw one toy and one clue about it for a riddle strip, with no words. No repair and no reteaching on a check day. Note on the record sheet the signs you saw in day 16's exit, error X (a drawing that does not teach about the toy) and error M (a toy named with no fact), for day 19; do not repair them today.

#### 8 min. Revise the day 16 piece (O1)

Alone. Hand each child the day 16 opinion page with its sticker (about 8 seconds each, as you go) and read the sticker's line aloud. Each child adds one thing, by drawing or by telling you what to write: the toy, a preference or one thing they see ([[s01-O1]]). Look for: a toy is named; a preference is said; a change is made from the sticker. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Keep the pages for day 18; do not score a new piece today. Signs: a toy name alone (error L, no preference).

#### 30 min. Six groups of five, 5 minutes each (O2, O3)

Groups come in the order of the six names on the record sheet; the other 25 children draw. Review: talk rules: look, listen, wait, take a turn (day 1). Each group sits at the table with the record sheet. Pencils down.

- O2, 2 minutes, about 24 seconds a child ([[s01-O2]]): each child says a classroom toy choice, "My favorite classroom toy is the ___", while the others look, listen and wait. Meets: listens and takes a turn. Note on the sheet whether the child named a toy and said a preference: this heard sentence is evidence for day 18's O4. Signs: talks over a partner (error C).
- O3, 3 minutes ([[s01-O3]]): the group asks, answers and keeps talking about toy choices: a question, an answer and a related question, two back-and-forth turns. Review: partner questions with the turn card: ask, answer, a related question, answer (day 3). Signs: one answer and then silence (error D).

Do not prompt during O2 or O3. If a child stops, wait; at the end give one cue, "What could you ask?", and mark shown with help. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. A child who passes is not yet seen and is asked again later in the week.

#### 10 min. Riddles

Whole class. Review: riddle and clue: a riddle gives clues; each clue narrows the toy (day 13). Read the riddle on page 8 of Toy Riddles aloud, with the answer page covered: "What is squishy and soft and comes in a tub? You can roll it, and press it, and give it a rub." Hold up four picture cards: a ball, blocks, play dough, a puppet. Children show the card they think fits. Then pairs tell which clue helped. Uncover page 9: play dough. Then read riddle 1 from the riddle strip sheet, one clue at a time, asking after each clue what toy it could be: "I am round. I roll when you push me. You can catch me with two hands." Show its picture on the back: a ball. Pairs say which clue helped. No child is told a guess is wrong: say the next clue and ask again.

#### 8 min. Share and tidy

Pairs show each other the toy and clue they drew while waiting, and say the clue aloud. Collect the opinion pages and the riddle strips; tidy the picture cards.

#### 3 min. Opening

Whole class. Letter chant for h, p, a and t: "h, h, /h/, hat; p, p, /p/, parrot; a, a, /a/, apple; t, t, /t/, top." (Review: letters h, p, a and t with their keywords.)

#### 10 min. Foundations response

Whole class. Review: repair then fresh retry: wrong example beside a right one, then a new try (day 6). Read the cycle 2 notes for h and p from days 14 to 16 and choose the one slip most children made (4 minutes). The puppet makes the wrong example; you show the right way.

- Error N: the puppet says "aitch" for /h/. Say the name, then the sound with the letter and the keyword hat.
- Error O: the puppet prints p when it hears /h/. Hear the sound, choose the letter card first, then print.
- Error P: the puppet prints only H when lowercase is asked. Model the lowercase path; each child prints it once.
- Error AA: the puppet calls the letter h a "p". Hold the two cards side by side and name each.

Then a fresh retry on a new strip (6 minutes), not scored: say /h/, each child holds up the h card and prints lowercase h in box 1; say /p/, holds up the p card and prints lowercase p in box 2; say /h/ and prints capital H in box 3. Look for: starts at the top, the line is straight, the hump or bump closes, says the sound.

#### 4 min. Mapped words

Whole class. Review: mapped words I, my and me, read as whole words (days 3, 5 and 14). Point under I in "I choose the top" on the large poem My Choice, and the class says it. Point to my in "My favorite toy is" and the class says it. Then point to me on the day 15 chart, "Come and play with me.", and the class says it. The other words are read to them; nobody sounds out choose or toy.

#### 3 min. Closing

Whole class. Chant for h and p.

#### 40 min. Rotations

Review: foundations rotations: table for the day's six, four stations, five 8-minute turns. Five groups of six. Familiar practice only; no reteaching beyond the response above. At the table each child gets a minute (6 minutes, 2 for the move): the retry of the one slip they made, then say the name and sound of h and p. Table notes go on the record sheet. Stations (six each, no teacher): finger h and p in the sand tray; draw and label; name-card match; big-book browse.

### Other rooms

One child: do O1 with the sticker and then O2 and O3 with you as the partner, saying your own toy choice and asking a related question. Read the riddle and do the retry. One projector: show the riddle pages and the cards on one screen; children point.

### At home

**Swap.** No class toys: use two toys from a shelf. No riddle book: say the riddle from memory. No sand tray: write with a finger on a table.

**Skip.** The six groups, the other children's drawing, the rotations and the stations.

- **10 min. The assessment, part one.** Show your child their day 16 drawing; say one thing you see. Your child adds one thing to it by drawing or telling you.
- **10 min. The assessment, part two.** Your child says their favorite toy; you listen and wait.
  **Say.** "What is your favorite toy?" Expect: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___."
- **10 min. Keep talking.** Ask one question about the choice; your child answers and asks you one back. Do not prompt.
- **5 min. Riddle.** Say the play dough riddle; your child points to a toy.
- **10 min. h and p.** Say /h/ with a breath and /p/ with a puff. Your child prints each once.
- **5 min. I, my, me.** Point to each word on a card; your child says it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep O1, O2 and O3 and cut the riddle. Longer: a second riddle from the same book, one clue at a time.

### Words and supports

Words: revise, listen, riddle, clue. Frames: "My favorite classroom toy is the ___." "What is your favorite?" A home language, signs and pointing count as answers; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 is also a check day, so nothing is repaired. It rereads pages of Push, Pull, Roll, children draw how a toy moves while you confer with the day's six about day 17's items, and you score the revised piece after class.
