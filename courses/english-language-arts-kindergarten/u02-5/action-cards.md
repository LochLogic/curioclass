# Action cards: un- and re-

Real things to act out and say, for day 53. Each pair gets three things: a box, a cup and a bow. Hand-drawn picture cards of the same acts go on the wall.

- **pack and unpack a box:** put things into the box; take them out. un- means the opposite.
- **fill and refill a cup:** pour water in; pour again. re- means again.
- **tie and retie a bow:** make a bow; undo it and make it again. un- and re- together.

Also act "packed": the box is finished packing, it already happened (-ed).

## How to use them

The teacher models one: "I pack the box. Now I unpack it. Now I repack it." Children act each word and say it. Ask one question each time: What does the changed word tell about what the person does? Words with pre-, -ful, -less and -ing are only heard on this day, not taught.

## Key (teacher)

Un- gives the opposite; re- gives again; -ed gives the past. A child who names only the base word ("tie") for retie has ignored the word part (AD).
