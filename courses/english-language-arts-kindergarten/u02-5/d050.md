# Day 50: Two more places, and where and when in the story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the five-place chart and the story-map from day 49. Sort the pictures for Utqiagvik and San Francisco. Set out the where cards (kitchen, park, home) and the when cards (morning, afternoon). Make the day's six: the next two unheard from each foundations group, using day 49's heard column. The exit cards are yours alone and are never shown in teaching. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and words

Whole class. Repair for error I, a small thing named instead of the event (3 minutes). Show three pictures from The Picnic Plan: a raindrop landing on Tomas's nose; the wagon; the lemonade jug. Read "A drop landed on Tomas's nose." Pip points to the jug. Ask: "Which picture shows what happened?" The children fix it: the drop. Say: an event is what happens, and an object is only a thing. Then the words, with pictures (2 minutes): fog, ocean, ferry, midday.

#### 10 min. Reread two places

Whole class, then pairs. Review: you heard Warmer and Cooler on days 37, 38 and 40, and Clouds on day 43. Read the Utqiagvik page (page 4 of Warmer and Cooler) and the San Francisco fog page (page 5 of Clouds) aloud with their pictures (about 3 minutes). Pairs then point to the weather in each picture and say one detail to each other (5 minutes); two children tell the class.

#### 15 min. Add two places

Whole class, then pairs. Add Utqiagvik and San Francisco to the chart (3 minutes): place-name picture, weather picture, one sentence each, and write it. Review: a noun for more than one adds /s/ (day 39). Model a sentence with a plural noun and a place word: "Fog drifts over the boats." Say it again and clap the two parts, boat and boats. Each pair takes one place (see SL.K.4, SL.K.5 and SL.K.6), draws the weather in the box on the student page, and says one sentence to a partner with a place word, toward the partner (8 minutes). Review: the request card "Can you say that again?" (day 35); a partner who cannot hear holds it up. Hear six pairs, about 40 seconds each. Listen for error E (a whisper) and error AE (a fragment such as "thick coats"); recast it as a sentence.

#### 5 min. Reread 02a

Whole class. Review: you heard The Picnic Plan yesterday. Reread pages 1 to 3 aloud. Say first: listen for where, and listen for when.

#### 12 min. Where and when

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the question picture cards where and when (day 43). Show the where cards (kitchen, park, home) and the when cards (morning, afternoon). Model (4 minutes). Read, "Grandma Rosa was already in the kitchen." **Ask.** "Where is Grandma Rosa?" Expect: the kitchen; lay the kitchen card. Read, "On Saturday morning, the sky was cloudless and blue." **Ask.** "When is it?" Expect: morning; lay the morning card. Pairs then each get two sentences, one at a time, and hold up the card that answers (5 minutes): "They walked to the park by the pond." (where: park) and, said by Pip and made up for practice, "In the afternoon, Pip pulls the wagon." (when: afternoon). Listen for error H, a place or time the sentence does not give. **Ask.** "Where does the plan change?" Expect: in the park, on page 3.

#### 8 min. Pairs locate

Pairs. Six pairs, one at a time, come to the story-map and lay a where card and a when card under the second box (about 1 minute each); the others hold up their cards from the mat and say the same to a partner. Each pair then points to the first box and the third and says what goes there: Tomas and Grandma Rosa; the rain starts. Say it in one sentence: "In the park, in the morning, the rain starts." Hear the six pairs.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six, one at a time, about 40 seconds each; the others finish the drawing from the student page. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Ask [[s02-X50a]], then [[s02-X50b]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a place from the other card (error H); "in the garden" for the fog (error A).

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Chant the letter sounds, finishing d, f and l. Reread the verse Wind with the pointer, tracking each line. Find and and touch it.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Articulatory gestures. Show the mouth picture for each sound and children copy it. For /d/ the tongue taps behind the top teeth and the voice is on. For /f/ the top teeth rest on the lip and air blows out with no voice. For /l/ the tongue tip lifts and the voice stays on. Then a mystery word, of: clues: it is in the verse Wind, in the second page, after "a ribbon". Map the letters: o is the first, f is the second. The part to remember is f: here f says /v/. Find of and touch it. Do not ask children to decode other words with the same pattern.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: about one minute for each child. Review: you change the first sound with counters (days 43 and 48). Say fan, then change /f/ to /k/ for can, then to /p/ for pan; then dip to lip. Each child says one new word. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/. Writing place: write d, f and l in lines; trace of on a card; write and. Sound and verse place: pairs change the first sound with counters (cap to lap; dig to fig); find and and of in the verse. The day's six are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Add two places to the chart, reread page 3 of the story, lay the cards, and say the sentence. One projector: show the chart and the map; the cards are paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write kitchen, park and morning on slips of paper. No story-map: fold a sheet in three.

**Skip.** The foundations rotations and the pairs.

- **10 min. Listen.** Read a story or a page about a place. Ask your child to tell where the story happens and when.
  **Say.** "Where are they? When is it?" Expect: a place and a time of day.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the weather in a place and says one sentence with a place word.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints d, f and l.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X50a]] [[s02-X50b]] Read twice: "Fog covers the bridge. Sunshine reaches the garden." Ask where the fog is. Then read twice: "In the morning, Ana reaches the school door. Wind shuts the door. Ana waits beside it." Ask where Ana waits.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the where and when cards and the pairs locate; cut the second place on the chart to a picture and one word. Longer: a secure child lays a when card for a second page of the story.

### Words and supports

Words: fog, ocean, ferry, midday, where, when. Frames: "Fog drifts over the ___." "It happens in the ___." "The plan changes in the ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 uses the chart to answer how weather changes what people wear or do, then models the retell. It opens with 5 minutes for error H, the most likely slip today: a place the sentence does not give. Reread the place sentence and pick the scene. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet and inform the pairs.
