# Day 49: Three places, and a story with a map

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the five-place chart with five columns; only the first three are used today. Hang the story-map with its three boxes empty. Sort the place pictures into three piles. Read the student page aloud to the class when it is handed out. Make the day's six from the day 48 heard column: the next two unheard from each foundations group. The exit cards are yours alone and are never shown in teaching.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and words

Whole class. Show the letter card g and then i; children say the sound of each and, for one of them, the name (the cycle 6 letters). Then the words, each with a picture: village, desert, mountaintop and compare. Compare means to look at two things and say how they are the same and how they are different. Review: same means alike (day 12).

#### 10 min. Reread 01a

Whole class. Review: you heard Weather Changes on days 35, 36, 47 and 48. Read pages 5 to 8 aloud, showing each picture (about 2 minutes): Mawsynram, the Atacama Desert and Mount Washington. After each page children point to the weather in the picture, then say one word for it: rain, dry, wind. Do not ask anyone to read a word.

#### 15 min. Begin the five-place chart

Whole class, then pairs. Say: this chart will hold five places. We start with three. Model one column for Mawsynram (4 minutes): put the place-name picture at the top, the rain picture under it, then say one sentence, "Rain falls and falls in Mawsynram," and write it. Point to page 6 as the source: "I know this because page 6 says it." Each pair then takes the pictures of Atacama or Mount Washington, picks one detail, says a sentence and points to where the page shows it (5 minutes); you write the sentence under that column. Hear six pairs, about 40 seconds each. **Ask.** "What is this chart mostly about?" Expect: weather in different places. Say it back: the main topic is weather in different places.

#### 3 min. Unknown word

Whole class. Review: the three-card routine from days 37 and 43 (a word card, a picture, a sentence). The word is cloudless. Show the word card, show the picture of the Atacama sky with no clouds, and say the sentence: "The sky is cloudless today." Children say the word and the sentence with you. Cloudless means with no clouds in the sky. Do not split the word into parts.

#### 8 min. Read 02a whole

Whole class. Say: this story is made up. It is called The Picnic Plan. Read the six pages aloud with their pictures (about 3 minutes of reading, the rest looking). Stop once, at the end of page 2, after "the sky began to change." **Ask.** "What happens when the sky changes?" Expect: it rains; the picnic changes. Then read on without stopping. Nothing in the story is scary and it ends safely.

#### 12 min. Model the story-map

Whole class, then pairs. Point to the three boxes and say each name: who; where and when; what happens. Review: the question picture cards who, where, when and what (day 43); hold up the matching card beside each box. Box 1, who (4 minutes). **Ask.** "Who acts in the story?" Expect: Tomas and Grandma Rosa. A character is a person who acts. Name one actor at a time: Tomas pulls the wagon; Grandma Rosa packs the basket. Pip puts the wagon in the who box. Ask: "Does the wagon act?" The children fix it: no, a person acts. Box 2, where and when (3 minutes): morning, the kitchen, then the park, then the living room. Box 3, what happens: the change. **Ask.** "What changes the plan?" Expect: the rain starts. Write rain starts in the middle small box; the first and last boxes wait for day 51. Pairs then point to each box in turn and say what goes in it (5 minutes). Listen for error G (an object named as the character) and error I (a small thing instead of the rain starting).

#### 2 min. Move

Whole class. Children walk quietly to the exit places, a seat or a mat, while the six come to the heard table.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six, one at a time, about 40 seconds each; the others draw the weather of their favorite chart place in the sketch box on the student page. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Ask [[s02-X49a]], then [[s02-X49b]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: pointing to the pond or the field (error A); "the basket" or "the window" (error I).

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Chant the sounds of the letters, finishing with d, f and l and their keywords: dog, fish, leaf. Reread the verse Wind with the pointer. Find d in and and in red; find f in flag and flap; find l in along and flap.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Letter search and formation. Review: you met d, f and l on day 47. Children find the three letters in the verse and touch each one. Then write each in the air with a big arm, on a card with a finger, and in lines with a pencil. Say the path as they go: d, make a round curve, then go up tall and down; f, hook over the top, go down, and cross; l, a tall straight line down. Say the sound for each as it is written.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: about one minute for each child. Say d-i-g slowly with a counter for each sound; the group blends it to dig. Each child then blends one of fan or lip, said slowly (about 50 seconds each). Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/. Writing place: write d, f and l in lines; trace and on a card, then write it from the card. Sound and verse place: beats and rhyme cards (sandwich, grapes, lemonade; dog and log, bed and red); find and in the verse. The day's six are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read one page of the three places, add it to the chart, read The Picnic Plan, and point to the boxes together. One projector: show the chart and the story-map; everything else is paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No place pictures: draw a sun, a rain cloud and a wind line. No story-map: fold a sheet in three.

**Skip.** The foundations rotations and the chart.

- **10 min. Listen.** Read a short weather page or any story with a problem. Ask your child to point to the weather in one picture.
  **Say.** "Where is it raining, or dry, or windy?" Expect: a place named from the page.
- **10 min. Three boxes.** Fold a sheet in three: who, where, what happens. Your child says one thing for each box about a story you know.
  **Say.** "Who acts? What changes the plan?" Expect: a person, and the event.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints d, f and l.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X49a]] [[s02-X49b]] Read twice: "Rain falls by the pond. Snow falls on the hill. Wind blows across the field." Ask for the picture where snow falls. Then read twice: "At breakfast, Sol sits by the window. Rain starts. Sol moves the basket inside." Ask what changes Sol's morning.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chart, the story reading and the story-map; cut the pairs to one place and move the beats cards to tomorrow. Longer: a secure child tells a second event in the story.

### Words and supports

Words: village, desert, mountaintop, compare, cloudless, character. Frames: "Rain falls in ___." "The ___ acts." "The plan changes when ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 adds two more places to the chart and asks where and when in the story. It opens with 5 minutes for error I, the most likely slip today: a small thing named instead of the rain starting. Match an action sentence to its picture. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet and inform the pairs.
