# Section 2.5 opener: weather in five places and a story retold

## What the section does

Days 49 to 53 compare the weather in five places, one place at a time, and read the first weather story, The Picnic Plan. The class builds a five-place chart, learns the three boxes of a story-map (who, where and when, what happens), and retells the story with three cards: first, next and last. Day 52 is a fluency day: practice only, no record. Day 53 adds writing about the weather in the places studied and the word parts un- and re-.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block ends with an exit of two spoken items, one about information and one about the story, heard from the day's six. Day 52 has the same items as practice. Every day begins with a five-minute warm-up that repairs one thing.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Information tells where.** The chart holds a place, its weather and what people do there. A question about where is answered from the chart or the page.
- **A story has a who, a where and a what happens.** The character is a person who acts (never the basket or the parcel); the setting is a place; an event changes the plan.
- **A retell goes first, next, last.** Three cards in order, with one key detail each.
- **Word parts change meaning.** un- means the opposite; re- means again; -ed means it already happened.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, H:** a detail the card does not say, or a place from the other card. Reread the exact words and pick the scene.
- **G, I:** an object named as a character, or a minor thing named instead of the event. Match the person who acts to the character box and an action sentence to its picture.
- **J:** events out of order. Order three cards while rereading first, next and last.
- **E, AE, B, C, AD:** a whisper, a fragment, no topic, a liking alone, or the base word named for the changed one.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet, never by a child. Weather places are shown by pictures; no one is asked about weather at home.

## Supplies

Chart paper, the place pictures for the five-place chart, the story-map in three boxes, retell cards, the six question picture cards, un- and re- action cards, real things (a box, a cup, a bow), the pages and story in large print, the exit cards and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Draw the chart on a large sheet of scrap paper. Fold three index cards a pair for first, next and last. Use a real box, cup and string.
