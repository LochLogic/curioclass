# The retell cards

Three cards for each pair, hand-drawn on index cards: **first**, **next** and **last**. Each card has the word in large letters for the teacher to point to, a small number of dots (one, two, three) and an arrow to the next card. Children do not read them.

## How to use them

The pair lays the three cards in a row. One partner holds up a card and tells what happened at that part of the story with one key detail ("First, they packed the basket."). The partner then holds up the next card. Wrong examples are said by a puppet: the puppet tells the last thing first; then orders the cards and tells them in order.

## Key (teacher)

A good retell has the beginning (first), the change (next) and the ending (last) in order, with one detail each. A retell with only a reaction ("they were sad") leaves out the events (AV, scored in a later section); in this section, out-of-order is J.
