# Day 53: A weather piece, and the word parts un- and re-

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the five-place chart. Set out one box, cup of beans and ribbon a pair. Make the response group list from day 52's cycle 7 check: group A (error U, the letter named when the sound was asked) and group B (the rest). Make the day's six: the next two unheard from each foundations group, using day 52's heard column. Keep the exit cards to yourself. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair for error AE, a fragment instead of a sentence (3 minutes). Pip says "Cold wind." Ask "Who or what? What happened?" and say it back as a whole sentence: "The wind is cold." Each child tells the person beside them a short sentence about the chart. A whisper (error E) from yesterday's note joins this repair: say it toward the partner. Then reread the five-place chart (2 minutes), pointing to each column.

#### 5 min. Model the piece

Whole class. Model one journal piece on the Atacama Desert column. Draw sand and an empty sky. Say the weather, "It is dry," and dictate one fact from the chart: "Rain does not fall for years." Write it. Ask yourself aloud, "Where? What?" and point: the place is the desert, the fact is the rain. Review: the three marks (topic, fact, drawing) from day 45. Show the three rings on the piece.

#### 20 min. Write the piece

Alone. Each child draws one place from the chart in the box on the student page, says or dictates one fact about it, and writes any taught letters or the mapped words he, we, on, to and and. This is practice and is not scored. Take dictation at the table from the day's six (about 90 seconds each, 9 minutes). Walk among the others and hear each child's fact in one breath (about 10 seconds each); write it beside the drawing after class. Look for three things: a place is named; one true fact from the chart; the fact belongs to the place. Return the pieces with the three marks. The pieces stay with the child.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Real things, a box, a cup and a ribbon. Pack a box and unpack it. **Ask.** "What does un- tell about the action?" Expect: the opposite; take it out. Fill a cup and refill it. **Ask.** "What does re- tell?" Expect: again. Tie a bow and retie it. Then say, "I packed the box." **Ask.** "What does -ed tell?" Expect: it already happened. Un- means the opposite; re- means again; -ed means it already happened.

#### 8 min. Heard in 02a

Whole class. Review: you heard The Picnic Plan on days 49 to 52. Reread page 5 aloud. Hold up two word cards: pack and unpacked. Children point to the one the story said, then to the base word. Do the same for fill and refilled. Spread has no part added; say it as a whole word. Then say colorful, precut and cloudless once each, with the picture. These three are heard only; do not teach their parts.

#### 7 min. Act and say

Pairs. Each pair acts pack and unpack, then fill and refill, with the box and the cup. Then each child says what the changed word tells, two turns each. Hear six pairs, about 40 seconds each. Listen for error AD, naming the base word for the changed one, such as "fill" for refill.

#### 5 min. Sort

Pairs. Each pair sorts six action cards under three signs: un-, re- and -ed. The cards are untie, unpack, refill, retie, packed and filled. Read each card aloud as the pair places it. Un- cards show the opposite; re- cards show again; -ed cards show something that happened.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six, one at a time, about 40 seconds each; the others finish the drawing on the student page. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Ask [[s02-X53a]], then [[s02-X53b]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: no place or weather named (error B); "I like snow" (error C); pointing to the first wrapping or the unwrapped gift (error AD).

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Cycle 8 opening. Reread the verse Fog and the earlier verses with the pointer; the verse Thunder comes on day 56. Find k in blanket and y in gray and away, and touch each. Clap the beats of blanket and of away.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. The cycle 7 response, 10 minutes, in groups by the day 52 check. Group A (error U) sorts name cards from sound cards for d, f and l: dee, eff and ell on one side, /d/, /f/ and /l/ on the other. Group B chants the fifteen letters taught so far (a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s, v, g, i, d, f and l) with a partner and a pointer. Then 5 minutes, whole class, cycle 8, lesson 41: mystery word up. Clues: it is the opposite of down; it has two letters. Map the letters, u and p, saying the sound of each. Hold up the word card up.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: about one minute for each child. Say at, add /s/ to make sat; say at again, add /p/ for pat. Each child says one new word. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/. Writing place: write k and y in lines; trace up on a card. Sound and verse place: beats and rhyme cards; find k and y in the verse Fog. The day's six are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do one piece, act pack and unpack with a real box, and sort the cards. One projector: show the chart; the cards are paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No action cards: draw six small pictures. No ribbon: use a shoelace.

**Skip.** The sort and the foundations rotations.

- **10 min. Piece.** Your child draws one place from a weather picture and tells you one fact; write it beside the drawing.
  **Say.** "What place is this? What is one true fact?" Expect: a place and a fact that belong together.
- **10 min. Word parts.** Pack and unpack a box; fill and refill a cup.
  **Say.** "What does un- tell? What does re- tell?" Expect: the opposite; again.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints k and y.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X53a]] [[s02-X53b]] Read twice: "Snow covers the grass beside the pond. The path by the school is bare." Ask what they will draw to show the weather. Then read twice: "Mara wraps a gift. Then Mara unwraps it. Then Mara rewraps it." Ask your child to point to the picture that shows rewraps.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the piece, the real-things words and the exit; cut the sort. Longer: a secure child adds a second place and a second fact.

### Words and supports

Words: pack, unpack, fill, refill, tie, retie, packed. Frames: "It is ___ in ___." "Un- means ___." "Re- means ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 opens with 5 minutes for error AD, the most likely slip today: the base word named for the changed one. Contrast the base, un- and re- forms with a real thing. It also takes errors B and C, a piece with no topic or only a liking. Then the class hears a second short weather story and maps it. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet.
