# The story-map

A large chart for the wall with three boxes, hung empty on day 49 and filled in with the class for The Picnic Plan. The teacher holds the pen on day 49; partners hold their own small maps on day 52.

- **Box 1: Who.** A picture of a person (a head and shoulders). The character is a person who acts. A basket or a wagon is not a character (G).
- **Box 2: Where and when.** A picture of a place and a sun or moon. The setting: where the story happens and at what time of day.
- **Box 3: What happens.** Three small boxes in a row, first, next and last, each with space for a picture or a word the teacher writes.

Draw the frame with thick black lines on white and no printed words except the box names, which the teacher reads aloud. Children point to a box and say or draw what goes in it.

## How to use it

Day 49: read The Picnic Plan whole, then point to each box and ask one question: Who is in the story? Where are they? What changes the plan? A child names a person, a place and the event that changes the plan. Day 52: partners use their own maps to retell.

## Key (teacher)

Character: a person who acts. Setting: the place and the time. Event: a change, such as the rain starting, not a minor object.
