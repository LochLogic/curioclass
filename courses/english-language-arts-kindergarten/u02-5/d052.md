# Day 52: Practice: describe a place, and retell with the map

## Teacher page

### Before class

This is a practice day: nothing new is taught and nothing is scored. Hang the five-place chart and the story-map. Fold one small map a pair. Make the day's six: the next two unheard from each foundations group, using day 51's heard column. Keep the exit cards to yourself. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Whole class. Repair for error J, cards in the wrong order (3 minutes). Lay three new picture cards in a row: a seed in a pot, a sprout, a flower. Pip puts the flower first. Read the cards aloud with the class, first, next, last, and fix the order with the class chanting the words as you point. Then one retrieval (2 minutes): the class chants first, next, last with the three retell cards and tells The Picnic Plan in a sentence for each.

#### 5 min. Model

Whole class. Review: clear means easy to hear and easy to understand (day 48). Pip describes one place from the chart with its drawing, but says it in a very small voice: "Mount Washington is windy." **Ask.** "Could you hear Pip? What should Pip do?" Expect: say it louder, toward the partner. Pip says it again so the back of the room hears. This is the one fix. The class then says what made it clear: a place, the weather, and a voice you can hear.

#### 12 min. Rehearse descriptions

Pairs. No new content. Each pair picks one place on the chart and takes turns to describe it with its drawing, toward the partner, two turns each (see SL.K.4, SL.K.5 and SL.K.6). Hear six pairs, about 1 minute each. Listen for error E, a whisper, and for error AE, a fragment such as "wet ground". Do not correct in front of the class; recast it quietly as a sentence and make a private note for tomorrow.

#### 8 min. Revise aloud

Pairs. Each pair makes one description clearer by adding one detail or one place word (such as in, on, by or over), and says it again toward the partner. **Ask.** "What did you add?" Expect: a detail or a place word.

#### 8 min. Reread 02a

Whole class. Review: you heard The Picnic Plan on days 49 to 51. Reread the whole story aloud with the pictures. Children hold up one finger at the first thing that happens, two at the next, three at the last. Do not stop the reading.

#### 12 min. Retell with the map

Pairs. Partners hold their small story-map and the three cards. Each partner retells The Picnic Plan with first, next and last, two turns each, using the map to point: who, where and when, what happens. Hear six pairs, about 1 minute each. Listen for the order (error J) and a detail on each card.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Practice only. The day's six, one at a time, about 40 seconds each; the others draw their favorite place on the student page. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the weather card twice with its picture; ask [[s02-X52a]]. Then read page 1 of The Picnic Plan aloud again; ask [[s02-X52bv2]]. Nothing goes on the record sheet. Make a private note of any whisper (error E) or fragment (error AE), or the first event given for the last (error J).

#### 5 min. Share

Whole class. Two pairs who agree share a retell. The class says what made it clear. Nothing is scored and no one is held up as a miss.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Chant the sounds of d, f and l and of the earlier letters. Reread the verse Wind with the pointer.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Rhyme Time: find the rhymes in the verse (along and song; green and scene) and say new rhymes with the pictures. Then the cycle 7 check on paper, all children, 5 minutes: say the sound /d/, then /f/, then /l/, one at a time, and children write the letter for each on handwriting paper. Nothing new is taught.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: the cycle 7 check, one child at a time. Each child names d, f and l and says the sound of each (30 children, about 40 seconds each, 20 minutes in all across the three turns, so about 7 minutes a turn). Mark in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Note a child who names the letter when asked for the sound (error U). For the rest of each turn, the group says the sounds of the verse words with counters. Writing place: finish the sound-writing check; write d, f and l in lines. Sound and verse place: rhyme cards; find d, f or l in the verse. The day's six are marked in the heard column.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Describe a place, revise it, retell the story with the map, then take the check. One projector: show the chart; the cards and maps are paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No story-map: fold a sheet in three. No retell cards: draw three small pictures.

**Skip.** The rotations and the share.

- **10 min. Describe.** Your child picks a place from a weather picture and describes it toward you in a whole sentence.
  **Say.** "Tell me one thing about the weather there." Expect: a sentence, spoken so you can hear it.
- **10 min. Retell.** Your child retells a short story with three drawings: first, next, last.
  **Say.** "What happens last?" Expect: the final event.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints d, f and l.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X52a]] [[s02-X52bv2]] Read twice: "Wind bends the reeds by the shed. The ground below is wet." Ask for one thing about the weather, in a sentence. Then read page 1 of The Picnic Plan and ask what happens last on the page.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep one description and one retell with the map; cut the share. Longer: a secure child describes a second place or retells with a second detail on each card.

### Words and supports

Words: describe, retell, first, next, last. Frames: "In ___, the weather is ___." "First ___, next ___, last ___." Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 opens with 5 minutes for error AE, the most likely slip today: a fragment such as "wet ground". Say who or what, and what happened. A whisper (error E) goes to the same warm-up. Then children write a weather piece and meet un- and re-. Today's cycle 7 check forms the groups for the day 53 response (error U: naming the letter when asked for the sound).
