# Exit cards, days 49 to 53

Days 49 to 51, and item b of day 52, use pages of the unit's own texts, read aloud again; they are not made up, so the teacher only makes their pictures. For the made-up cards (day 52 item a, day 53), say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every made-up card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards or pictures is shown in teaching. Each day has an information item (a) and a story or word item (b). Day 52 is practice only.

## Day 49

**Information page:** page 2 of Weather Changes (texts/gk-u2-01.md, selection a). Three place pictures in a row, each in a ring: the shade by the bench; the swings; the sunny path. **Item a:** "Point to the picture where children sit when they feel hot."

**Story page:** page 5 of The Picnic Plan (texts/gk-u2-02.md, selection a). **Item b:** "What happens on this page that changes the picnic again?"

## Day 50

**Information page:** page 2 of Clouds (texts/gk-u2-01.md, selection d). **Item a:** "Where is some water that we cannot see?"

**Story page:** page 4 of The Picnic Plan. **Item b:** "Tell me where Tomas and Grandma Rosa are going on this page."

## Day 51

**Information page:** page 3 of Clouds. **Item a:** "Ask me a question that this page answers."

**Story page:** page 5 of The Picnic Plan. Three pictures, shuffled: Tomas unpacks the basket; Tomas spreads the blanket on the floor; Tomas puts out the plates and cups. **Item b:** "Put the pictures in the order they happen."

## Day 52 (practice only)

**Information card:** "Wind bends the reeds by the shed. The ground below is wet." **Item a:** "Tell me one thing about the weather on this card, in a sentence."

**Story page:** page 1 of The Picnic Plan, read aloud again. **Item b:** "Tell me what happens last on this page."

## Day 53

**Information card:** "Snow covers the grass beside the pond. The path by the school is bare." **Item a:** "What will you draw to show the weather on this card? Say it in a sentence."

**Word card:** "Mara wraps a gift. Then Mara unwraps it. Then Mara rewraps it." Three pictures: Mara wraps a gift the first time; the gift unwrapped, the paper beside it; the gift wrapped a second time (two small panels: the opened paper, then the gift wrapped again). **Item b:** "Point to the picture that shows rewraps."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 49: a, the shade; not yet: the swings or the sunny path (A). b, they have the picnic on the living room floor; not yet: "the blanket" or "the grapes" (I).
- Day 50: a, in the air; not yet: "in the pond" or "in the ocean" (A). b, home; not yet: the park or the kitchen (H).
- Day 51: a, any question page 3 answers ("What colors can clouds be?" "Are all clouds the same?"); not yet: "How high is the sky?" (A). b, unpacks, spreads the blanket, puts out the plates; not yet: any other order (J).
- Day 52: a, "The wind bends the reeds." or "The ground is wet."; not yet: a whisper (E); "wet ground" alone (AE). b, Tomas packs the blanket; not yet: Tomas jumps out of bed, the first event (J).
- Day 53: a, a place and its weather ("Snow covers the grass."); not yet: no place or weather (B); "I like snow" (C). b, the gift wrapped a second time; not yet: the first wrapping or the unwrapped gift (AD).
