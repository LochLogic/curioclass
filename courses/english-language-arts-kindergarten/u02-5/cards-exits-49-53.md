# Exit cards, days 49 to 53

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching. Each day has an information card (item a) and a story or word card (item b). Day 52 is practice only.

## Day 49

**Information card:** "Rain falls by the pond. Snow falls on the hill. Wind blows across the field." Three place pictures in a row, each in a ring: the pond with rain; the hill with snow; the field with wind. **Item a:** "Point to the picture where snow falls."

**Story card:** "At breakfast, Sol sits by the window. Rain starts. Sol moves the basket inside." **Item b:** "Tell me what happens that changes Sol's morning."

## Day 50

**Information card:** "Fog covers the bridge. Sunshine reaches the garden." **Item a:** "Tell me where the fog is."

**Story card:** "In the morning, Ana reaches the school door. Wind shuts the door. Ana waits beside it." **Item b:** "Tell me where Ana waits."

## Day 51

**Information card:** "Rain falls by the school. People open umbrellas to stay dry." **Item a:** "Ask me a question that this card answers."

**Story card:** "Bo packs a snack. Rain begins. Bo eats beside the classroom window." Three pictures, shuffled: Bo packs a snack; rain begins; Bo eats at the window. **Item b:** "Put the pictures in the order they happen."

## Day 52 (practice only)

**Information card:** "Wind bends the reeds by the shed. The ground below is wet." **Item a:** "Tell me one thing about the weather on this card, in a sentence."

**Story card:** "Jin holds a toy truck. Rain fills a puddle. Jin watches from the dry step." **Item b:** "Tell me what happens last."

## Day 53

**Information card:** "Snow covers the grass beside the pond. The path by the school is bare." **Item a:** "What will you draw to show the weather on this card? Say it in a sentence."

**Word card:** "Mara wraps a gift. Then Mara unwraps it. Then Mara rewraps it." Three pictures: Mara wraps a gift the first time; the gift unwrapped, the paper beside it; the gift wrapped a second time (two small panels: the opened paper, then the gift wrapped again). **Item b:** "Point to the picture that shows rewraps."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 49: a, the hill; not yet: the pond or the field (A). b, rain starts; not yet: "the basket" or "the window" (I).
- Day 50: a, on the bridge; not yet: "in the garden" (A). b, beside the school door; not yet: "in the garden" or "on the bridge", from the other card (H).
- Day 51: a, any question the card answers ("What do people open?" "Why do they open umbrellas?"); not yet: "What color are the umbrellas?" (A). b, packs, rain begins, eats at the window; not yet: any other order (J).
- Day 52: a, "The wind bends the reeds." or "The ground is wet."; not yet: a whisper (E); "wet ground" alone (AE). b, Jin watches from the step; not yet: the first event (J).
- Day 53: a, a place and its weather ("Snow covers the grass."); not yet: no place or weather (B); "I like snow" (C). b, the gift wrapped a second time; not yet: the first wrapping or the unwrapped gift (AD).
