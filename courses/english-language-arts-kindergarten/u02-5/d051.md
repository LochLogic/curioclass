# Day 51: What the weather changes, and a retell

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the five-place chart (all five places now) and the story-map. Set out the six question picture cards and the three request cards. Lay out one set of three retell cards and three picture cards a pair. Make the day's six: the next two unheard from each foundations group, using day 50's heard column. Keep the exit cards to yourself. Read the student page aloud when it is handed out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and words

Whole class. Repair for error H, a place the sentence does not give (3 minutes). Show three pictures from The Picnic Plan: the kitchen, the park and the living room. Read, "They walked to the park by the pond." Pip points to the kitchen. Read the sentence again and ask: "Which scene is it?" The children fix it: the park. Then the words with gestures (2 minutes): retell means to tell the story again in order, with first, next and last. First is a fist at the shoulder, next is a step to the side, last is an arm held flat.

#### 10 min. Reread the chart

Whole class, then pairs. Reread the five-place chart, one column at a time, pointing to each picture. Pairs then find how the weather changes what people do or wear in one place (4 minutes). Use the chart pictures only. Do not ask children what they wear or do at home. Two pairs say one change to the class: "Where it is windy, people lean into the wind."

#### 15 min. Discussion with questions

Pairs, then circles. Review: the six question picture cards (day 43) and the token talk: wait for the token, ask one more (days 42 and 43). Model once (3 minutes). **Ask.** "A question the chart answers is: Why do people wear thick coats on Mount Washington?" Expect: it is windy there. Then Pip asks, "What is the weather observer's name?" Ask the children whether the chart answers it. They say no, so the question needs a different page. A good question is one the page or picture can answer. Pairs then draw a question card, ask a partner a question about the chart, and the partner answers with a detail; trade roles and ask once more (two exchanges each, 6 minutes). Hear six pairs. Then three circles of ten sit with one token each; it goes around once and each child says or asks one thing about the chart (about 20 seconds each, 5 minutes in all, with you moving between the circles). A child who did not hear an answer holds up the request card "Can you say that again?" (review of day 35). A child may pass.

#### 3 min. Words

Whole class. Before, after and last tell the order of things. Do it with the body: stand up, clap, stamp, then turn. "I clap before I stamp. I stamp after I clap. I turn last." Children do each with you and say the sentence.

#### 8 min. Reread 02a

Whole class. Review: you heard The Picnic Plan on days 49 and 50. Reread pages 4 to 6 aloud with the pictures. Stop at the ending detail. **Ask.** "What shows that Tomas feels better at the end?" Expect: he smiles and his shoulders lift. Review: his shoulders dropped on page 3.

#### 10 min. Model the retell

Whole class. Lay the three retell cards in a row: first, next, last. Lay the three picture cards under them: packing the basket, rain on the path, the picnic on the living room floor. Model (5 minutes). "First, Tomas and Grandma Rosa packed a basket. Next, rain began, so they hurried home. Last, they ate on the blanket indoors, and Tomas smiled." Each part has one key detail. Then Pip tells the last card first. **Ask.** "Is that the order?" Expect: no. The children fix it by pointing to the cards in turn and reading the words first, next, last aloud with you (error J). Pip tries again.

#### 4 min. Pairs retell

Pairs. Each pair lays its three cards and retells The Picnic Plan, one card each turn, two turns each. Hear six pairs, about 40 seconds each. Listen for the order and for one detail on each card.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six, one at a time, about 40 seconds each; the others finish the drawing on the student page. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Ask [[s02-X51a]], then [[s02-X51b]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a question the card does not answer (error A); the pictures in another order (error J).

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Chant the letter sounds. Launch the verse Fog: read it aloud twice with the pointer and picture (the first reading is a listen). Find of in the second page and touch it.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Feel the beats. Review: you clap the beats of a word (days 38 and 44). Clap picnic (two) and blanket (two), saying each part with the clap. Blend two parts into a word: pic, nic; blan, ket. Segment: the group says blanket, then claps and says each part. Do two more two-beat words from the verse or the story, as many as time allows.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: about one minute for each child. Substitute the last sound: fad to fan; dig to dip. Each child says the new word with a counter moved for the sound that changed. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/. Writing place: write d, f and l in lines; write and and of from cards. Sound and verse place: clap the beats of three picture words (mitten, ribbon, grapes); match two words that rhyme (bat and hat; net and pet). The day's six are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Ask and answer one question about the chart, reread the ending of the story, lay the three cards and retell it. One projector: show the chart; the cards are paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No retell cards: draw three small pictures on a sheet folded in three. No token: pass a spoon.

**Skip.** The circles and the foundations rotations.

- **10 min. Two questions.** Show a picture of weather. Your child asks you one question the picture can answer, and you ask them one.
  **Say.** "What can you ask about this picture?" Expect: a question the picture answers.
- **10 min. Retell.** Your child retells a short story you know with three drawings: first, next, last.
  **Say.** "What happened first? Next? Last?" Expect: three events in order.
- **5 min. Print.** Your child prints d, f and l.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X51a]] [[s02-X51b]] Read twice: "Rain falls by the school. People open umbrellas to stay dry." Ask your child to ask a question that the card answers. Then read twice: "Bo packs a snack. Rain begins. Bo eats beside the classroom window." Have your child put three pictures in order.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the discussion in pairs, the model retell and the pairs retell; cut the circles. Longer: a secure child retells with a second detail on each card.

### Words and supports

Words: retell, first, next, last, before, after. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "Why do people ___?" Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. A pair may talk away from the group.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 is a practice day: nothing new is taught. It opens with 5 minutes for error J, the most likely slip today: pictures in the wrong order. Lay three cards and reread first, next and last. Then it rehearses weather descriptions and the retell. Today's heard column and the exit marks go on the record sheet and inform the pairs.
