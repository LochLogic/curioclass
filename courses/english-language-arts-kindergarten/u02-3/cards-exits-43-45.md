# Exit cards, days 43 to 45

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 43 card C43: the farmer

**Read:** "In the evening, a farmer looks at the sky over her barn. Dark clouds gather. She brings the cows in."

**Picture:** a farmer at a barn door at dusk, dark clouds above, cows in a line. **Spoken, to the day's six, item a:** "When does the farmer look at the sky?" **Item b:** "Ask me a question that starts with who."

## Day 44 card C44 and picture P44: clear and under

**Read:** "The window is clear. I can see a bird." **Picture:** a window with a bird outside, nothing blocking it. **Item a pictures:** a glass of water you can see through; a blue sky with no clouds. **Spoken, item a:** "Which picture shows what clear means here?"

**P44:** a boot under a bed. **Item b:** "Tell me where the boot is."

## Day 45 picture P45: where things are

**Picture:** a cat on a stool, a pail under the stool, a bird beside the stool. **Mark:** place a counter on the pail. **Spoken, to the day's six:** "Tell me where the pail is."

## Key (teacher)

- Day 43: item a, in the evening; not yet: "the farmer" or "at the barn" (Z). Item b, any question about a person ("Who looks at the sky?" "Who brings the cows in?"); not yet: "Who is in the evening?" (Z).
- Day 44: item a, easy to see through; not yet: "no clouds" alone (AC). Item b, under the bed; not yet: "on the bed" (AA).
- Day 45: under the stool; not yet: "on the stool" or "beside the stool" (AA).
