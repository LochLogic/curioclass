# The three marks

The teacher writes three marks by hand on a child's draft: a mark for the topic, a mark for the fact, and a mark for the drawing. Use a small ring for each, drawn in a different color, with a tick in the ring when the part is there.

- **Topic ring:** ticked when the draft names a weather topic (a label, a word said or a word dictated).
- **Fact ring:** ticked when the draft holds one true fact about the topic that a weather page says.
- **Drawing ring:** ticked when the drawing shows something the topic or fact tells.

Marks go on the draft as written feedback the child can look at; they are never a grade, and a child's draft is never shown to the class as an example of a miss. Return the draft on day 45; the child revises it for five minutes on day 46 before it is read.
