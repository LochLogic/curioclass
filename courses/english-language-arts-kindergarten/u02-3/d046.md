# Day 46: Weather check

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 45, mark each draft with the three rings (never a grade). Put the journal pages (day 41's entry and any made since) on the wall, one for each pair; if fewer, two pairs share a page and talk apart. Put the record sheet on a clipboard. Set a table with the day's six noted. Nothing is taught today and no earlier error is repaired.

### The day

#### 5 min. Marks and plan

Whole class. Hand back each draft with its rings. Show the three marks once: a ring with a tick means the part is there; an empty ring means add it. Tell the plan of the day: revise, make a new piece, draw, then talk. Error AA from day 45's exit waits for day 47; nothing is repaired today.

#### 10 min. Revise (I1)

Alone, then with a partner. Each child revises the draft by its rings and says the fact to a partner. You move through the room and give no help beyond the rings. Ask the day's six, and any child whose fact you cannot place, to point to the page the fact came from. A child whose fact ring is empty may say a fact to you as you pass; write it in the corner (about 20 seconds). Dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count. After class, score each revised draft by the three look-fors on [[s02-I1]]: a topic is named; one true fact from the pages is given; the fact belongs to the topic. Do not mark spelling or letter shape beyond g and i.

#### 15 min. Independent piece (F1)

Alone. Each child draws a new weather topic and tells or dictates one fact from the pages. You move through the room and write each child's fact (30 children at about 30 seconds each is 15 minutes). A child may print letters of taught sounds. Keep F1 with I1; it is not scored again.

#### 10 min. Prepare a drawing

Pairs. Each pair opens to one journal page from the wall (a different page for each pair where you can). Each child draws on the student page the one detail they will tell about it. You walk and say only "what will you tell?"

#### 20 min. Describe a weather page (O1)

Fifteen pairs at one table with you, one pair at a time, about 75 seconds each (15 times 75 is 18 and three-quarter minutes). The child holds the drawing and tells the partner what is on the page. Prompt once: "What else did you see?" Record three things apart on the record sheet: [[s02-O1a]] (tells what is on the page in an order and adds a detail), [[s02-O1b]] (the drawing shows the detail told and the child points to it) and [[s02-O1c]] (heard from where the partner sits; an idea or feeling that can be said back). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: an unclear idea (error F); a drawing of something else (error D); a voice too quiet (error E, recorded apart); a feeling with no idea clear (error AX). The other pairs finish F1 or colour their drawing quietly. A child may talk in a quiet corner. At the end of the talk, collect the revised drafts, the new pieces and the drawings.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Reread the verse Snow and the verses Hot Sun and Rain with the pointer; find on and he.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Cycle 6 response, 10 minutes, in groups by the day 45 check. The group that named a letter when asked for its sound (error U) sorts name cards from sound cards for g and i with you; the others chant the twelve letters with a partner and a pointer. All chant for the last 5 minutes. No new pattern is taught.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Table: small groups by the cycle 6 check; sort name cards from sound cards for g and i; change one sound with counters (cat to hat); about one minute for each child. Writing place: write g and i; trace on and to. Sound and verse place: clap beats and match rhyme cards; blend and segment the spoken words sip and cap. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the listener. The child revises a draft by its rings, then draws one detail from a journal page and tells it to you, with one prompt. One projector: not needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No journal page: use a drawing of the weather the child made. No rings: tick a circle drawn in the corner.

**Skip.** The group talk and the rotations.

- **10 min. The assessment, part one.** Look at the child's draft. Ask for the topic, then one fact; the child adds what is missing.
  **Say.** "What does the page say about your topic?" Expect: one true fact.
- **10 min. The assessment, part two.** Your child draws one weather detail and tells it to you, pointing to the drawing, then adds one more.
- **10 min. Your own piece.** Your child makes a new page: a topic and one fact.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say /g/ and /i/; your child prints g and i.
- **5 min. Rhyme.** Say cat and hat; your child says if they end the same.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the independent piece to a drawing and one spoken fact; keep the revision and the talk. Longer: a child who is secure tells a second detail in the talk.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, fact, detail, journal. Frames: "My topic is ___." "The page says ___." "I saw ___." A drawing, dictation, pointing and a communication aid all count for I1. The talk may be heard in a quiet corner and the prompt may be repeated once. Volume (error E) and clarity (errors F and AX) are recorded apart.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 shares the weather piece. Its first 5 minutes repair error AA (under and on swapped) from day 45's exit and the more common of error B (no topic named) and error C (a liking, not a fact) from today's revised drafts: a topic card on top, details under, one fresh piece. Errors F, D, E and AX from the talk are heard in a response group on day 48.
