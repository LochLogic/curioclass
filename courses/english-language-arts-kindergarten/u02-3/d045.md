# Day 45: Describe a scene, and a first draft

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make five scene cards, one for three pairs each. A: umbrella beside the door, puddle under the step, leaf in the puddle. B: sled on the hill, mitten in the snow, snowman beside the tree. C: kite in the tree, flag on the pole, hat beside the fence. D: hat on the swing, cup in the shade, ball under the slide. E: kite on the hill, cart beside the fence, nest in the tree. Draw P45; hide it.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair day 44's exit for error AC (only the sky meaning of clear). Say two sentences, "The sky is clear" and "The water is clear," and sweep hands wide for the first and look through a frame of hands for the second. Hold the cup of water and the cup of milk; children say which one you can see through. Pip says "The milk is clear" (error AC); the class looks at the spoon and says which. Then one fresh sentence, said by three children: "The ___ is clear." Move a picture card under, then on: Pip puts the dog on the tree for under (error AA) and the class moves it.

#### 12 min. Describe a scene

Whole class, then pairs. Reread page 4 of Rainbows; children listen for where each thing is (2 minutes). Hand each pair a scene card and say that the scene is made up for practice. Each child says two sentences, "The ___ is ___ the ___." (6 minutes). Then they join their two sentences with and: "The kite is in the tree and the flag is on the pole." Review: you joined two short sentences with and on day 40. Listen at six pairs, one minute each. **Ask.** "Where is the hat?" for card C. Expect: beside the fence. Sign: under and on swapped (error AA); move the picture and say the place.

#### 8 min. Pick a topic and a fact

Whole class, then pairs. Show the chart of six weather topics, each with a picture: rain, snow, clouds, rainbows, warmer and cooler, wind. Each child picks one, says one true fact from the pages to a partner (4 minutes), and the partner says it back. Review: a topic is what the whole page is about, and a detail goes under it (days 9 and 41). Children point to the chart picture for their topic. You walk and find the page with each child in the next part, with the page open to the picture.

#### 5 min. The teacher's marks

Whole class. Hold the model draft about fog, which is not a topic on the chart. Show the three marks: a topic ring, a fact ring and a drawing ring. A ring with a tick means the part is there; an empty ring means add it. Children try each mark on the model draft by pointing. **Ask.** "What does an empty fact ring tell you to add?" Expect: one fact from the page. The marks are never a grade, and no child's draft is shown to the class.

#### 25 min. Draft an information piece

Alone. Each child draws the topic big in the box (6 minutes), then says one fact from the page to you as you come to them. About 30 seconds each, 30 children, 15 minutes: write the fact on the line as the child says it, saying each word as you write. A child who waits adds a label or any letters of taught sounds. The last 4 minutes: children add to the drawing. The draft is practice; mark it with the three rings after class. A fact is a thing the page says; "I like clouds" is a liking, and you ask "What does the page say about clouds?" Hear six children say the fact and point to the page it came from.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six at the heard table, about 50 seconds each. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Show picture P45 and read its caption twice. Ask ([[s02-X45]]). A child may put a counter on the pail first. Sign: on for under, or beside for under (error AA). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw their topic again on the back of the draft.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Chant the twelve letters with keywords: a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s, v, g, i. Reread the verse Snow with the pointer.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Rhyme Time (6 minutes): find the rhymes in the verse Snow, street and feet, right and white, and say a new rhyme for each (tree and bee; cat and hat). Cycle 6 check on paper (5 minutes, all children): say the sound /g/ and the child prints the letter; say the sound /i/ and the child prints it. Then review the mapped words on and to by sight (4 minutes).

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. Table: the cycle 6 check, one child at a time: name g, name i, say the sound of each; about 40 seconds each, ten children in about 7 minutes of the 12. Error U: the child names the letter when asked for its sound. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Writing place: finish the sound-writing check; write g and i in lines. Sound and verse place: rhyme picture cards; find the words with g or i in the verse (grass, go, dig; is, air, I). The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column.

### Other rooms

One child: use one scene card and say two place sentences with the child; draft one page and say the fact. One projector: show page 4 of Rainbows; the cards and the cups stay real.

### At home

**Swap.** No scene card: arrange three toys around a cup and describe where each is. No ring marks: tick a circle drawn in the corner.

**Skip.** The group rotations and the check.

- **5 min. Where.** Move a toy under, on, beside and in a cup.
  **Say.** "Where is the toy?" Expect: under the cup.
- **10 min. A scene.** Your child says two place sentences about three toys, then joins them with and.
- **10 min. A page.** Your child draws one weather topic and tells you one fact from a weather page.
  **Say.** "What does the page say?" Expect: a fact, not "I like it."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X45]] Show a picture of a cat on a stool, a pail under the stool and a bird beside the stool. Ask where the pail is.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second sentence on the scene card; keep the draft, the exit and the rotations. Longer: a child who is secure adds a second fact or asks a why question about the draft.

### Words and supports

Words: on, under, beside, in, topic, fact. Frames: "The ___ is ___ the ___." "My topic is ___." "The page says ___." A home-language label may go on the draft. A child who cannot hold a crayon chooses between picture cards; dictation and a communication aid count.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 is a check: nothing is taught and nothing is repaired. Children get the draft back with its rings, revise it, make a new piece and describe a journal page. Error AA from today's exit is repaired on day 47.
