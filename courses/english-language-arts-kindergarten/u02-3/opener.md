# Section 2.3 opener: clouds, question words, rainbows and the first check

## What the section does

Days 43 to 45 read the pages about clouds and rainbows and teach three things: the six question words (who, what, where, when, why, how) and which kind of answer each asks for; the two meanings of a familiar word (clear); and the place words (on, under, beside, in) for telling where a thing is. On day 45 each child makes a first draft of a page that names a weather topic and gives one fact. Day 46 is a check day: nothing is taught.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Days 43 to 45 begin with a five-minute warm-up that repairs one thing from the day before and end the topic block with a five-minute exit heard from the day's six. Day 46 has no exit: you hear fifteen pairs describe a page of the class picture journal, and you read each child's revised draft and the new independent piece.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Each question word asks for one kind of answer.** Who asks for a person, where for a place, when for a time. The six picture cards carry the idea; children point to the card before they answer.
- **A word can mean two things.** Clear can mean easy to see through (a cup of water, a window) or no clouds (the sky). We hold the real thing and gesture each sense.
- **Place words are about position.** Under, on, beside: we move a picture and say where it is. Under is not on.
- **A weather page names its topic and gives a fact.** "Clouds are made of tiny drops of water." A liking ("I like clouds") is not a fact.
- **A description can be said, drawn and shown.** A drawing helps only if it shows the detail told.

## The errors, in plain words

- **Z:** a time offered for where, or a person for when. Match who to a person first, then where.
- **AC, AA:** only the familiar meaning of clear, or under and on swapped. Contrast with real things and move a picture.
- **B, C, D, E, F, AX:** no topic, a liking alone, an unrelated drawing, a voice too quiet, an unclear idea, or a feeling with no idea. Day 46 records these apart.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet, never by a child. No weather is asked about at home.

## Supplies

A puppet, a clear cup of water and a cup of milk, a window, the pages on clouds and rainbows in large print, the six question picture cards, picture cards for place words, counters, the class picture journal pages, paper and crayons, the three marks, the exit cards and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Use a glass jar of water for the clear cup. Draw the six question cards by hand with a small picture on each. Use a book and a shelf for under, on and beside.
