# Day 44: Two meanings of clear, and where things are

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pour water in one cup and milk in the other and drop a spoon in each. Cut the picture cards from page 4 of Rainbows. Give each pair a cup, a counter and the six cards. Draw the exit cards and keep them out of sight. Note the day's six (the next two unheard from each foundations group).

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class, then pairs. Repair day 43's exit for error Z (a person or a place for a when question). Hold up the six cards. Say three answers and children hold up the card each answers: "my aunt" (who), "on the hill" (where), "in summer" (when). Pip is asked "When does fog come in?" and says "Over the water." The class says "that is where; when needs a time" and Pip says "in summer." Then each pair asks and answers one fresh question about a picture page from Clouds (2 minutes). **Ask.** "What kind of answer does when ask for?" Expect: a time.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Say each word and make its sign: rainbow (an arc with the arm), light (open both hands), bend (bend an elbow), sunshine (fingers fan out), raindrop (a finger drops down). Children say each word back with its sign. Review: you met sunlight on day 37; sunshine is the same idea.

#### 10 min. Reading Rainbows

Whole class, then pairs. Say: "Listen for what a rainbow needs." Read Rainbows whole, pages 1 to 4, showing each picture (about 2 minutes). **Ask.** "What does a rainbow need?" Expect: sunshine and raindrops. Pairs tell each other (3 minutes), then three children share (2 minutes). Do not stop to explain the word clear yet.

#### 10 min. Reread the explanation

Whole class, then pairs. Reread pages 2 and 3. Pairs find the two things a rainbow needs, and where the sun and the rain must be, and point to the matching picture (4 minutes). Share three pairs (3 minutes). **Ask.** "Where must the sun be?" Expect: behind you. **Ask.** "What do the raindrops do to the light?" Expect: they bend it. The text says behind and in front; they are heard here and not taught.

#### 12 min. Two meanings of clear

Whole class, then pairs. First (2 minutes). Review: you learned clear on day 36 as "no clouds." Reread the page 4 sentence "The sky is clear." Sweep both hands wide across the sky. Second (4 minutes): hold the cup of water and the cup of milk side by side. Say: "Look at the spoon." The spoon shows through the water and not through the milk. Say: "Clear can also mean easy to see through." Reread the page 4 sentence "A drop of rain is clear." Look through two hands held like a frame. Say: "One word, two meanings." Third (4 minutes): pairs each say one sentence for each meaning ("The sky is clear." "The water is clear."), then swap; listen at six pairs, one minute each. Fourth (2 minutes): Pip holds the milk and says "This is clear because there are no clouds" (error AC, only the sky meaning). The class looks at the spoon and says "no, you cannot see through it." The day checks only these two meanings.

#### 13 min. Where things are

Whole class, then pairs. First (4 minutes): use the cup and a counter and say the place word as you move it: on the cup, under the cup, beside the cup, in the cup. Children say each with you. Second (4 minutes): on the page 4 scene, move the picture cards and say where each is: the boy on the fence, the dog under the tree, the boat under the bridge, the girl beside the boy. Children say "The dog is under the tree." Pip puts the dog on the tree (error AA, under and on swapped); the class moves it under. Third (5 minutes): pairs with a cup and a counter. One child says "Put the counter under the cup" (or on, beside, in), the partner moves it, then swap; listen at six pairs. The text also says in front of; it is heard here and not taught.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six at the heard table, about 50 seconds each. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card C44 twice, showing its picture. Show the two pictures, the see-through glass and the sky with no clouds, and ask item a ([[s02-X44a]]). Then show picture P44 and ask item b ([[s02-X44b]]). Signs: the sky picture, the other meaning of clear (error AC); on for under (error AA). Mark each item: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw something they can see through.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Chant the twelve letters with keywords: a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s, v, g, i. Review: g and i from days 41 to 43.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Review: feel the beats (day 38). Clap and count the beats: rain-bow has two; sun has one; sun-shine has two (6 minutes). Blend and segment two-beat words: say rain, bow and children say rainbow; say sun, shine (3 minutes). Review: you mapped on, to and he. Show the cards and read each by sight; children find to beside on in the verse Snow (6 minutes).

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute. Table: change the last sound with counters. Lay the counters for cat, say "change /t/ to /p/" and the child says cap. Ten prompts, one for each child: cat to cap; cap to cat; tip to tin; tin to tip; pin to pit; pit to pin; hip to hit; hit to him; him to hip; sip to sit. Error T: the child changes the first sound; replace only the counter named. Writing place: trace to on a card (t is known; o is taught in a later cycle); write g and i from memory. Sound and verse place: clap the beats of three picture words (rainbow, puddle, umbrella); match two words that rhyme from cat and hat, pin and tin, tree and bee. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Use a clear cup and a milk cup, then move a toy on, under, beside and in a cup. One projector: show the page 4 scene on the screen; the cups and the counters stay real.

### At home

**Swap.** No milk: use a cup you cannot see through, such as a mug. No picture cards: move a toy on, under and beside a cup.

**Skip.** The pairs and the rotations.

- **5 min. Listen.** Read Rainbows aloud from the lesson text.
  **Say.** "What does a rainbow need?" Expect: sunshine and raindrops.
- **10 min. Clear.** Show a clear glass of water and a mug. Say: "The sky is clear. The water is clear."
  **Say.** "Can you see through the mug?" Expect: no.
- **5 min. Where.** Move a toy on, under, beside and in the cup; your child says where.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say /g/ and /i/; your child prints g and i.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X44a]] [[s02-X44b]] Read: "The window is clear. I can see a bird." Show a see-through glass and a clear blue sky; ask which shows what clear means here. Then show a boot under a bed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the reread of pages 2 and 3 to one question; keep the two meanings, the place words and the exit. Longer: a child who is secure makes a sentence that uses clear in each meaning.

### Words and supports

Words: rainbow, light, bend, sunshine, raindrop, clear, on, under, beside, in. Frames: "The ___ is clear." "The ___ is ___ the ___." A child who finds sentences hard may point to the cup and show the meaning with a gesture; each counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 45 uses the place words to describe a weather scene and starts the first draft of an information piece. Its first 5 minutes repair error AC (only the sky meaning of clear) from today's exit: two sentences, one with the sky and one with the cup, with a sign for each meaning. Error AA (under and on swapped) is moved on a picture in the same warm-up.
