# Day 43: Six question words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put a set of the six question cards in a basket for each pair. Give each pair a picture page from Clouds. Draw the exit card C43 and its picture and keep the exit cards out of sight. Note the day's six (the next two unheard from each foundations group) and set the class in 15 pairs. Hang the verse Snow where all can see it.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class, then pairs. Repair day 42's exit for the more common of error AR (two voices at once) and error AS (the talk stops after one question and answer). For AR: Pip grabs the token and talks over you; the class says "wait for the token," Pip waits, and the token passes. For AS: Pip asks one question, hears one answer and stops; the class says "and one more," and Pip asks again. Then each pair holds one fresh talk of two turns (2 minutes): one child says a detail from page 2 of Rain and Snow ("Drops tap the roof."), the partner asks one question and the first child answers. **Ask.** "What do we do when two people want to talk?" Expect: wait for the token.

#### 6 min. Words

Whole class. Say each word and make its sign (90 seconds a word). Cloud: hands puff up high; sky: point up. Review: you know cloud and sky from the verse Rain (day 36). Because: two fingers, "this, so that": "We wear boots because the street is wet." Clarify: ask again so you understand. Pip says "Look at the big one." The class holds up the request card "Which one?" and Pip says which. Children say each word back once with its sign.

#### 10 min. Reading Clouds

Whole class, then pairs. Say: "Listen for what a cloud is made of." Read Clouds whole, pages 1 to 5, showing each picture (about 2.5 minutes). For fog, use the three-card routine (word card, picture on page 5, sentence card "Fog is a cloud that sits low near the ground"). Review: you asked "What does ___ mean?" with card 2 on days 37 and 39. **Ask.** "What is a cloud made of?" Expect: tiny drops of water, tiny bits of ice, or both. Pairs tell each other (3 minutes), then three children share (2 minutes). A child who looks at the floor during the dark rain picture on page 4 is not asked to look.

#### 14 min. Model: the six question words

Whole class, in three steps. First (4 minutes): show the six cards one at a time, say the word and hold the card. Review: you met the question starters Who? and What? on day 3, and added where or when to a sentence on day 40. New today are why and how, and one idea: each word asks for its own kind of answer. Who asks for a person, what for a thing, where for a place, when for a time, why for a reason, how for a way.

Second (5 minutes): read the first line of page 1, then the last paragraph of page 5. Point to the card for each question and children name the kind of answer.

- What do you see? Clouds. (a thing)
- Who watches the clouds? Weather observers. (a person)
- Where do they look? Up at the sky. (a place)
- When do they look? Every day. (a time)
- Why do they look? To learn what the weather may do. (a reason)
- How do they look? With their eyes and their notebooks. (a way)

Third (5 minutes): you and Pip ask four questions about page 4, showing its picture. **Ask.** "Who has umbrellas?" Pip answers "On the hill" (error Z, a place for a who question). The class says "that is where; who needs a person." Expect: two children. Then **Ask.** "Where does the rain fall?" Expect: over the school and the hill. **Ask.** "Why do people look at the clouds?" Expect: because a cloud can tell us weather may be coming. **Ask.** "What falls when the drops grow heavy?" Expect: rain or snow. Say that Pip makes mistakes on purpose; no child is the example.

#### 12 min. Sort the answers

Pairs, each with the six cards. Read an answer twice; pairs lay down the card whose question it answers and, at "show," hold it up (about 90 seconds each, 5 answers). After each show, say the answer's question aloud.

- Two children hold umbrellas. (who)
- Fog often comes in from the ocean in summer. (when)
- The rain falls over the school and the hill. (where)
- The drops fall because they grow heavy. (why)
- A cloud is made of tiny drops of water. (what)

Scan the 15 cards each time. If many show the wrong card on one answer, ask "is it a person, a place or a time?" and read it again. The last 4 minutes: pairs choose a card, find the answer to its question in the pictures of Clouds, and say it.

#### 8 min. Partners ask

Pairs, with their picture page. One child takes the who, where or when card, asks a question about the picture and the partner answers; swap. For example, page 1: "Who lies on the hill?" (children); page 1: "Where are the children?" (on a hill); page 5: "When does fog come in from the ocean?" (in summer). Listen at six pairs, one minute each. Sign: error Z, an answer of the wrong kind. For Z, point to the card and ask "what kind of answer?" Then the pair tries again.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

The day's six at the heard table, about 50 seconds each. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card C43 twice, showing its picture. Ask item a ([[s02-X43a]]), then item b ([[s02-X43b]]). A child may point to a card. Sign: error Z, a person or a place for a when question, or a who joined to a time. Mark each item: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw a cloud from page 1 of Clouds.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Review: the letters g and i (days 41 and 42). Say /g/ and /i/ with the mouth pictures on the letter cards: the back of the tongue lifts for /g/; a small smile for /i/. Point to g and i, in both cases, in the verse Snow.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Review: you mapped on on day 41 and to on day 42. Mystery word game (5 minutes): cover on in "Snow falls ___ the grass." Clues: it is in line 1; it ends with /n/; look at the picture. Uncover it and read it by sight (the n says /n/; the o is the part to remember). Children find on and to in the verse and touch each with a finger (5 minutes). Chant the twelve letters with keywords: a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s, v, g, i (2 minutes). Say the verbal path for g and i while the whole arm writes in the air (3 minutes).

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute. Table: change the first sound with counters. Lay the counters for cat, then say "change /k/ to /h/" and the child says hat. Ten prompts, one for each child: cat to hat; pin to tin; hip to sip; tap to nap; cap to tap; hat to pat; sip to tip; nap to cap; pat to cat; tip to hip. Error T: the child changes the last sound; place the counters again and replace only the first. Writing place: write g and i in lines; trace on on a card (o is taught in a later cycle). Sound and verse place: pairs change the first sound with counters; find on in the verse. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; use the six cards, ask one question of each kind about Clouds and answer, then ask the exit card. One projector: show the pages of Clouds on the screen; the cards and the counters stay real.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a face, a hand with a toy, a pin, a sun and moon, an arrow and a turning hand. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The pairs, the sorting and the rotations.

- **5 min. Listen.** Read Clouds aloud from the lesson text.
  **Say.** "What is a cloud made of?" Expect: tiny drops of water or bits of ice.
- **10 min. Six cards.** Show each card and say what it asks for. Ask about the last paragraph of page 5.
  **Say.** "Where do weather observers look?" Expect: up at the sky.
- **5 min. Your turn.** Your child asks you a who, where or when question about a picture; you answer.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say /g/ and /i/; your child prints g and i.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X43a]] [[s02-X43b]] Read: "In the evening, a farmer looks at the sky over her barn. Dark clouds gather. She brings the cows in." Ask the two questions on the card.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second half of the sort; keep the model, the partners, the exit and the rotations. Longer: a child who is secure asks a why or a how question about a picture and answers it.

### Words and supports

Words: cloud, sky, because, clarify, who, what, where, when, why, how. Frames: "Who ___?" "Where ___?" "It is a person, a place or a time." A child who finds talk hard may point to the card, answer by pointing to the picture or use a communication aid; each counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 reads the passage Rainbows and teaches two meanings of the word clear. Its first 5 minutes repair error Z (a person or a place for a when question) from today's exit: match who to a person, then where, with the six picture cards.
