# Day 76: Share the finished story and respond

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the records of days 70 to 75 and pick the one error most missed, counting the capital and mark errors together (AJ, AK, AL), then each other letter alone. Draw its repair card from the list below and have its retry card ready. Put the finished stories out. Read the student page aloud. Today nothing new is recorded.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reteach and fresh retry

Whole class, then pairs. Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is marked today." Do one repair for 10 minutes, then its retry for 5 minutes. A retry informs support only; keep it in the folder. Pip says the wrong examples, never a child. Branches, with the retry card to read twice:

- **Error AI** (a draft left unchanged after a question; days 73 to 75). Repair: write "Fog came. We stopped." Ask, as the partner, "Who stopped?" Draw the person beside it, say the words "Dad and I stopped", and print them. Pairs do the same with their own drafts, drawing first. Retry: read [[s02-X76]] from the retry card: "Wind blew. I ran." The partner asks, "Where did you run?" Ask: "Say the words you will add." Expect: words that name a place.
- **Error AJ, AK** (a lowercase first word or i; day 75's s02-E1a). Repair: write "rain fell. i hid." on chart paper. Circle the first word and change its first letter; find the i and change it. Retry: "snow is soft. i like it." Children fix it with a finger and a crayon.
- **Error AL** (a mark misnamed; day 75's s02-E1b). Repair: show the three mark cards with their gestures. Say "We hop." (pat), "Can we hop?" (hand up) and "We hop!" (arms up); children match each to its mark card and name it. Retry: show three new marks on a strip each; children name them.
- **Error AE, AF** (a fragment or the same sentence again). Repair: "Sam jumps." becomes "Sam jumps over the puddle." Retry: "Bo hops." Children say a longer one.
- **Error AG, AH** (no reaction, or events in reverse). Repair: lay three pictures, the puddle dries, Dara puts on boots and Dara jumps in; tell them with first, next and last; add a feeling card. Retry: three new cards in a mixed order.
- **Error F, D, E, AX** (day 74's talk). Repair: recast "There" as a person and an action; redraw one spoken detail; say it toward a partner across the room; say back the feeling you heard. Retry: describe a new picture of a child under an umbrella.
- **Error AB, AC, AD** (words). Repair: a word with two meanings, wave. Retry: "bark" in "The dog barks" and "Tap the bark of the tree."
- **Error G, H, I** (day 63's s02-S1a to s02-S1c). Repair: match the person who acts to the character box and reread the place sentence. Retry: a new card, "Mina and Dad wait in the library while rain falls." Ask: "Where do they wait?"
- **Error AN** (watching, not choosing; day 75's s02-D1). Repair: offer two wordings and take the pick. Retry: a new pair of wordings.

If the sound errors (N to W) were most missed, give them the foundations response and use these 15 minutes for the next most missed.

#### 25 min. Share the finished stories

Pairs, then the whole class. Children share in pairs, at once: one child shows the drawing and tells the story in about 40 seconds; the partner asks one question; the child answers. Swap, and then find a new partner for a second round (about 4 minutes a round, 8 minutes in all). Ask the pairs: "What would you ask the writer to learn one more detail?" Then the day's six share with the whole class, about 60 seconds each, and the others listen for a question (6 minutes). A child may point to a card, or you read the dictation. Hang the class book. Talk and record only what helps you plan; collect nothing.

#### 20 min. Reread the last fifth of 03, Yusuf and the Parcel (309 words)

Whole class, then pairs. Review: Yusuf and the Parcel, section 5, Home (day 63). Read it again with the pictures (about 3 minutes). **Ask.** "How did the weather change what Yusuf did?" Expect: the snow was thick, so they waited at the bakery; when the snow stopped and the plow came, they walked home. Partners point to a picture and say, "The snow ___ so Yusuf ___." Then **Ask.** "What would you ask the writer to learn one more detail?" Expect: a question about something the page leaves out, such as what the note said. Children draw one thing the weather made Yusuf do and tell a partner.

#### 10 min. Foundations: response

Small groups, by the day 75 records of the pattern check and the fifth six. Come to your table in groups of three or four for the one missed, about 6 minutes in all, then a fresh retry of about 4 minutes. Error U: sort name cards from sound cards for j, e and z. Error V: say the taught sound after you, then with a partner. Error W: say the sound, choose its card, then write it. Errors N, O, P: stretch the word and name each sound with a counter. Error Q: hold the last sound, then blend. Error R: one counter for each sound. Error S: place the new sound at the front only. Error T: replace only the named counter. Retry words: jam, net, hen; add /b/ in front of ig to make big; change the first sound of hen to /t/ to make ten. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/. Write nothing on the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral. Rhyme: say night and light; children give another (kite, bright). Clap snowflake (two), jacket (two) and umbrella (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: j, e and z

Whole class, practice only (days 71 to 75). Chant "j, jar, /j/." "e, elephant, /e/." "z, zebra, /z/." Build Jed and zip with letter cards, saying each sound as the card goes down, then print them on handwriting paper. Find a j, an e and a z in verse 9. No new pattern is taught.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words see and does

Whole class. Review only: see and does, read whole (day 71). Point under "Does Pip see you?" and "Jed does see it."; children read them. Name the part to remember in each word.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. Reread only what has been taught: verses 8 and 9 with the pointer, tapping the spaces. Find see and does in verse 9. Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner says it back.

#### 20 min. Foundations: rotation

Table and stations. You hear verse prompts from verse 8 with two groups of five, 10 minutes each, to help and not to record (the prompts of day 67). The others move among stations: letter cards, handwriting paper, and rereading the poem Read Together with a partner pointing. At the end write on the unit record sheet, beside each child, whether day 77 or day 78 is theirs; the mid-year records continue from there.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the listener. Do the reteach card for the one error most missed in the child's own work, hear the child tell the finished story and ask one question, reread section 5 aloud, and do the sound work as a short talk. One projector: show the pictures of section 5; the drawings stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw them on paper. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The pairs, the stations and the verses.

- **10 min. Tell the story.** Your child shows a picture of their story and tells it.
  **Say.** "What would you ask the writer to learn one more detail?" Expect: a question about something left out; your child may answer it.
- **10 min. Weather and a person.** Read a short weather story your child knows.
  **Say.** "How did the weather change what the person did?" Expect: the person waited, hurried or changed plans because of the weather.
- **10 min. Print.** Your child prints Jj, Ee and Zz and says a word that starts like each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X76]] Read twice: "Wind blew. I ran." A partner asks, "Where did you run?" Your child says the words to add.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reteach and retry and the share in pairs; cut the second pair round. Longer: a secure child tells a second detail and asks their partner for one.

### Words and supports

Words: share, question, detail. Frames: "I want to know ___." "I will add ___." "The snow ___ so Yusuf ___." Pointing, dictation and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk or share.

### Tomorrow

Day 77 starts the mid-year battery for the first fifteen children and day 78 finishes it for the other fifteen. Both days reuse today's records for the parts already heard: day 75's items and day 74's items (the end marks, the pattern check, the fifth six of the sound record and the talk), the verse prompts and the earlier grammar and word records. Day 79 is the course response; today's retry marks nothing and informs only.
