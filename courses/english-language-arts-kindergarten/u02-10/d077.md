# Day 77: Day 77: first-half mid-year battery

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the check cards covered. List the first fifteen children in hearing order; the other half does familiar practice. Carry, without asking again, the day 70 and day 75 letter and sound-writing records (b, o, w, j, e, z) and the day 74 and day 75 items; a child absent then is asked today in spare minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Set-up and records

Say the card is made up for practice and nothing is graded. Seat the first fifteen in a ring of waiting seats; the rest go to their tables. Say each child has a turn of five minutes with you and may pass any ask.

#### 15 min. Whole-group text pair

Whole class, the first fifteen with the rest listening. Read the story card twice: ask [[c1-M20]]; children show the answer on side 1 of the page by pointing to a picture or drawing it. Then read the information card twice and ask [[c1-M21]]. Then say the word on the card and have partners ask each other a question about it ([[c1-M22]]); listen to five pairs, 20 seconds each, and write each child's question on the record sheet. Mark each child on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. One prompt: read it again.

#### 75 min. Individual conferences

Fifteen children, five minutes each, at your table. About 300 seconds a child: print, [[c1-M01]], [[c1-M02]] and [[c1-M03]], 30 seconds; letters, [[c1-M04]] and [[c1-M05]], 30 seconds, and stop after five letters in a row not named; sounds, [[c1-M06]] and [[c1-M07]], 30 seconds; writing, [[c1-M09]], 20 seconds; rhyme, [[c1-M10]] and [[c1-M11]], 20 seconds; word parts, [[c1-M12]] and [[c1-M13]], 20 seconds; first sounds and the rest, [[c1-M14]] and [[c1-M15]], 20 seconds; sounds in a word, [[c1-M16]], [[c1-M17]] and [[c1-M18]], 40 seconds; the retell story and its three cards, [[c1-M19]], 50 seconds. That is 260 seconds; 40 seconds change the child. After class look at the child's many-letter product for [[c1-M08]]. Mark the same scale on each ask. Give no model and no answer; one prompt of the ask is allowed where the item says. A child who misses five in a row on a section skips to the next. Signs of the common slips are in the wrong answers: a name for a sound (error A); a look-alike letter (error B); the wrong start on the print (error C); a word and a letter mixed (error D); rhyme by the first sound (error E); letters counted for beats (error F); a dropped sound (error G); the middle given as the first (error H); a retell with no order (error I); the text replaced by what the child knows (error J); a short vowel given its long sound (error K); spelling by letter names (error L).

#### 15 min. Familiar practice for the others

The rest of the class, in partners: reread a familiar poem, sort letter cards or draw. A teaching assistant or an older child keeps the room. No new teaching. A child not yet heard waits or draws.

#### 10 min. Records

Write on the sheet who needs a short second hearing and which items a child passed. Day 76's retry marks nothing and informs only. The verse prompts of day 67 are carried only for a child absent or unable then. Collect the student pages at the end of this part. Day 79 reads the sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the whole battery in about 13 minutes; skip the partner questions and ask each in turn. No letter sheets: write the letters by hand, large.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: say the sounds and words aloud. No print: write the letters yourself.

**Skip.** The group text and the record sheet.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Ask your child for the names of five letters of your choosing and their sounds, then one rhyme pair, one clap of a long word and one three-sound word to push counters for. Do not help; write what you saw.
- **10 min. Story.**
  **Say.** "Tell me the story with these three pictures." Read a short made-up three-event story twice first. Expect: the events in order. Draw three pictures on paper first.
- **10 min. Words.** Say two words that rhyme and one that does not; your child says which.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep letters, sounds, rhyme and sounds in a word; do the rest on the next free minutes. Longer: a child at ease names the letters of their name and writes one word by sound.

### Words and supports

Words: letter, sound, rhyme, part, word. Frame: "This letter says ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 78 hears the other fifteen children on the same battery with different cards; the first half does not sit it. Day 77's assessment goes to day 79 with day 78's. Day 79 groups children by need.
