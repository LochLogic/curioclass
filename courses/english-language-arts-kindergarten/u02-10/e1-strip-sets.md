# E1 strip sets

Thirty sentence strips, unused until day 75, for the end-mark check: each strip is short, large print, and ends with a period, a question mark or an exclamation mark. They are made up in three sets of ten, mixed within each set, and no child sees a strip before the prompt.

## Set one

- The cat naps.
- Where is the bus?
- Look at the rainbow!
- Rain taps on the roof.
- Can you see the moon?
- What a loud clap!
- We clap our hands.
- Who is at the gate?
- The bell rang!
- A bee sits on the leaf.

## Set two

- Is it cold today?
- The bus stops here.
- I can jump!
- What is on the shelf?
- Mum hums a song.
- Hooray for the sun!
- Do you hear the bell?
- Frost is on the glass.
- Stop the bus!
- When does the rain stop?

## Set three

- The sun is warm.
- Why is the sky gray?
- It is so bright!
- We walk to the gate.
- Will you hold my hand?
- We won the game!
- A bird sings.
- How tall is the tree?
- Splash!
- Watch out for the step!

## How to use them

Day 75, three prompts a child on unused strips: the teacher shows a strip, reads it aloud, and asks the child to say what the mark at the end is called. The teacher records and gives no hint.

## Key (teacher)

A period tells, a question mark asks, an exclamation mark shows a strong feeling. Misnaming a question mark as a period is AL.
