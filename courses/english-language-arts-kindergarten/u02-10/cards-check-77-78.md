# Check cards, days 77 and 78

Teacher only. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice. Nothing is graded." Read each card twice; children do not read it. Print "invented for practice" at its foot. Not shown in teaching.

## Form for day 77

- **Print card:** "Wes zips a zipper." and "Ivy has a quilt."
- **Capital letters:** M R T K Q S N P L H V Y
- **Small letters:** m r t k q s n p l h v y
- **Story card:** Wilma the mouse found a quilt in a tent. She gave it to Zack. Zack said thank you. Ask: Who did Wilma give the quilt to? Key: Zack.
- **Retell story (three picture cards, teacher drawn):** Tia the goat lost her bell in the grass. A bird found it and rang it. Tia heard it and ran to get it.
- **Information card:** A carrot grows under the ground. Its green top grows in the air. You can eat a carrot. Ask: Where does a carrot grow? Key: Under the ground; the green top is in the air.
- **Word parts:** rab, bit; br, oom

## Form for day 78

- **Print card:** "Oz mixes a melon." and "Uma has a vine."
- **Capital letters:** D F G I Q U X A C L H Y
- **Small letters:** d f g i q u x a c l h y
- **Story card:** Zoe the otter found a hornet by a tulip. She said, Go away. The hornet flew to a vine. Ask: Where did the hornet fly to? Key: A vine.
- **Retell story (three picture cards, teacher drawn):** Kai the pup dropped a ball in the creek. A duck pushed it to the bank. Kai ran and grabbed it.
- **Information card:** A walrus has two long tusks. It uses them to climb onto the ice. A walrus can rest on the ice. Ask: What does a walrus use its tusks for? Key: To climb onto the ice.
- **Word parts:** pil, low; gl, ove

## Key (teacher)

The ask, the right answer and the wrong answers for each card are in the items for days 77 and 78. Each half meets different cards of the same demand; a card heard on day 77 is not used again on day 78.
