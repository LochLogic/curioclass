# Day 74: Check: tell about my weather picture

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the O1 group list: six groups of five, in the order they come to the table. Put each child's edited narrative, revised day 46 drawing and your written comment in a pile by name. Open Priya at the Window and Rain and Snow. Set a quiet corner. Read the student page aloud. Nothing is taught today and there is no exit ticket.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback returned

Whole class. Hand back the other fifteen drafts with the comment you wrote after day 73; the first fifteen hold theirs already. Each comment ends in one question. Error AI (day 73's s02-X73b): a draft left unchanged after a good question. Each child answers their own question beside the draft by drawing, then says the words while you print them. Walk and help; teach nothing new.

#### 10 min. Hear 02b, Priya at the Window, whole (313 words)

Whole class. Review: Priya at the Window, first read on day 54. Read it straight through with the pictures (about 3 minutes). **Ask.** "Who was there, and what did you see?" Expect: Priya and Dev; rain drops on the glass, a gray sky, the sun and sparkling leaves. **Ask.** "How does Priya feel at the end?" Expect: glad, proud or pleased; she grins. Partners say one thing they would add to Priya's notebook. Talk is not scored.

#### 5 min. Weather fact from 01c

Pairs. Review: Rain and Snow, read on days 39 to 42. Read pages 2 and 3 aloud (about 1 minute). Each child points to one fact they would add to their own story and tells it to the partner (15 pairs, 4 minutes).

#### 30 min. O1, six groups of five

Groups of five come to your table, 5 minutes a group, about 60 seconds a child (30 children, 30 minutes). Say: "Tell us about your weather piece from day 46." Listen about 40 seconds. Give one prompt only: "Where was it?" or "What did it look like?" Then the child shows the revised drawing and points to the detail it shows (about 20 seconds). Mark three cells apart on the group list: [[s02-O2a]] (who was there and what happened, with a prompted detail), [[s02-O2b]] (the drawing shows the detail and the child points to it) and [[s02-O2c]] (heard by the group; a thought or feeling that can be said back). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: an idea you cannot identify (error F); a drawing of something else (error D); too quiet to hear (error E, recorded apart); a feeling with no clear idea (error AX). A child who prefers not to talk points to the drawing and you read what is written; a child may use the quiet corner. The other children edit their captions with their finger and a crayon (next part) and revise their drawing from your comment. Family alternative: a person you know, no private details.

#### 10 min. Edit and revise

Individuals. Review: the three looks at a caption (days 56 and 67): put a finger under the first word and circle its first letter; find I; look at the end mark. Children finish their captions and drawings. Walk and give the three looks only; never fix a letter for a child. Collect the work for day 75.

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 11, lesson 59). Rhyme: say rain and train; children give another (chain, plane). Clap boot (one), puddle (two) and umbrella (three).

#### 10 min. Foundations: j, e and z

Whole class (cycle 11, lesson 59). Review: j, e and z, names, sounds and keywords jar, elephant and zebra (day 71). Chant "j, jar, /j/." "e, elephant, /e/." "z, zebra, /z/." Skywrite each letter, then print one line of Jj, Ee and Zz on handwriting paper and circle the best letter.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words see and does

Whole class. Review: see and does, read whole (day 71). Point under "Jed does see a pig." and "You see Pip."; children read them. Name the part to remember in each word.

#### 10 min. Foundations: oral three-sound work

Whole class, with counters, practice only; no one is marked. Blend /j/ /e/ /t/ and children say jet. Segment jam into three. Isolate the first sound of jug, the middle sound of web and the last sound of cab. Add /p/ in front of an to make pan. Change the first sound of ten to /h/ to make hen. Never use a word that ends in /l/, /r/ or /x/.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. Review: reread verse 9, Look Out and See, with the pointer, tapping the spaces. Find see and does in line 1 and a j, an e and a z in the verse. Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner says it back.

#### 20 min. Foundations: rotation

Table and stations. Two groups of five at your table, 10 minutes each (10 children, 1 minute each): the verse prompts of day 67 on verse 8, Dressed for Weather, that these children did not complete on days 67 to 69 (s02-H1a to s02-H1g). Mark only the parts you hear: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others move among stations: matching both cases of j, e and z, handwriting paper, and rereading the poem Read Together with a partner pointing. Write who was heard on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the listener. Hear the child tell about one drawing, with one prompt, then check one caption for a capital first word, a capital I and an end mark. One projector: show the group list; the drawings stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No letter cards: write j, e and z on paper. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The group turn, the stations and the verses.

- **15 min. The assessment: tell about the picture.** Your child tells you about a weather picture.
  **Say.** "Tell me about your weather piece." Expect: who was there and what happened. Then ask once: "Where was it?" Expect: a place; your child points to it in the drawing.
- **5 min. Answer the comment.** Your child answers the question on the comment beside their draft, by drawing, and says the words while you print them.
- **10 min. Check a caption.** Your child puts a finger under the first word, circles its first letter, finds I, and adds an end mark.
- **10 min. Print.** Your child prints Jj, Ee and Zz and says a word that starts like each.
- **5 min. Listen.** Say rain and train. Your child says another word that rhymes.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the talk with the drawing and the caption check; cut the fact pairs and the shared print. Longer: a secure child adds a second detail to the drawing and says it.

### Words and supports

Words: describe, detail, clear. Frames: "My weather piece is about ___." "This shows ___." Pointing, dictation and a communication aid all count. Read each prompt twice; a child may sit nearer you for volume. Volume (error E) and a clear idea (errors F and AX) are recorded apart.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 publishes the weather story and closes its records: the class piece with each child's choice, the end marks on unused strips (errors AJ, AK and AL), and the sound record for the fifth six. It scores the kept work for events and a reaction (errors AG and AH) and the added detail (error AI). No error is repaired on a check day; errors F, D, E and AX from today's talk wait for the response group on day 76.
