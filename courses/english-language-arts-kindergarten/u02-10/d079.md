# Day 79: Respond to the mid-year check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet from day 77's assessment and day 78's assessment. Make four small groups by what the sheet shows: a name and a sound mixed (error A or B), a dropped sound (error G), a spelling by letter names (error L), a retell or an answer without the text (errors I and J). A child with no repair need joins the reread group. Hang the three picture cards. Keep the fresh retry cards covered.

### The day

#### 10 min. Retrieval and sounds

Whole class. Say a letter and its sound, then three words to clap and blend. Name three rhymes aloud and have children hold up a hand for the ending. Nothing is marked; it warms the room.

#### 40 min. Response groups

Four groups, 10 minutes each at your table; the others reread toy texts in partners. Name the group's slip and repair it with a model, one card at a time. Review: the repair routines of day 77 and day 78, each of 1 minute. Name and sound: touch the card the teacher asks for (error A) and trace two look-alike letters (error B). Dropped sounds: push a counter for each sound (error G). Spelling: a counter for each sound, then a letter for each (error L). Retell and answers: lay three cards in order (error I) and hear the text again, then point to the answer (error J). Say each group's start for the unit 3 foundations routine: a child who names few letters joins the daily letter and sound group in unit 3.

#### 20 min. Fresh retry

Each group, in turn, five minutes: new cards, one ask each, no marks. Ask [[c1-X79a]], then [[c1-X79b]], then [[c1-X79c]], then [[c1-X79d]]. Say that nothing is marked; write on the sheet what changed since day 77 or day 78: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. One prompt.

#### 20 min. Reread toy texts

Whole class. Reread a familiar toy text twice; partners say what happened first and last, 30 seconds each. Hear six pairs.

#### 15 min. Letters and words

Pairs with cards. Match capital to small letter and say its sound; then clap and blend one word. Circulate and hear six pairs.

#### 10 min. Draw and tell

Each child draws one thing from the toy text and tells a partner one detail about it. No score.

#### 5 min. Records

Write on the sheet which children grow and which still need the daily unit 3 routine. Day 80 starts from it.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the group; take the four repairs in turn, each 3 minutes. No cards: draw them by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons or beans.

**Skip.** The group reread and the sheet.

- **20 min. Day 77's and day 78's assessment.** Ask one question from each part of the home form your child met: name and sound of three letters, one rhyme, the sounds of a short word with counters. Do not help; write what you saw.
- **5 min. Say it.**
  **Say.** "What do you remember of this story or card?" Expect: one thing it said.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one thing from a toy story and says one detail.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say two three-sound words; your child pushes a counter for each sound.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the response groups and the fresh retry; cut the reread. Longer: a secure child writes two letters for sounds you say.

### Words and supports

Words: letter, sound, part, rhyme, detail. Frame: "This letter says ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. Give one prompt and never the answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 80 opens unit 3 and reads the sheet: a child who could not blend three sounds sits at the sound table, and a child who names few letters joins the daily letter and sound group.
