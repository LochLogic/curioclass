# Fresh retry card, day 76

Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice. Nothing is marked today." Read the card twice. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card. Children do not read it; you read it aloud. It is not shown in teaching.

## The card

**Draft card:** "Wind blew. I ran." A partner asks: "Where did you run?" **Ask:** "Say the words you will add."

## Key (teacher)

Words that name a place ("I ran to the barn." "I ran home.") meet. The draft said again or nothing added (AI) is not yet. No record is kept for this retry; it informs the next day of teaching only.
