# O1 group list

One page for the teacher on day 74. Six boxes, one for each group of five, with the five names written in the order the groups come to the table. Beside each name, four small columns for the look-fors: describes the place or event; adds a detail when asked once; the drawing helps; the voice is audible.

Each child gets about 60 seconds at the table: 40 for describing, 20 for the drawing. Marks are shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Volume (E) and a clear thought or feeling (AX) go in their own cells.

## Look-fors (teacher)

The child tells what is on the page in an order and adds one detail after one prompt ("What else did you see?"). The child points to the drawing while telling the detail. A child who prefers not to talk points to the drawing and the teacher reads what is written.
