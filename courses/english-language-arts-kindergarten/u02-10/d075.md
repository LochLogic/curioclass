# Day 75: Check: publish the weather story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write each child's two wordings on a card: the sentence from their draft, and the same sentence with the detail added on day 73. Open the editor on one shared screen with large type and a paper copy beside it, or lay out chart paper. Hang the writing-process poster. Mix each E1 set. Pick the day's six. Read the student page aloud. No exit ticket today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hear the ending of 02a again

Whole class. Review: The Picnic Plan, pages 5 and 6 (day 72). Read them again with the pictures (about 1 minute). **Ask.** "How did Tomas's picnic change?" Expect: from the pond to the living room floor; he smiled and said it was the best picnic. Talk only; nothing is marked.

#### 23 min. Publish the class piece (D1)

Individuals at the shared screen, 45 seconds each (30 children, 22.5 minutes). The class book has one page for each child. Read the child's two wordings aloud twice. Say: "Which way should our book say it?" Or the child may choose where the page goes in the book, at the start, the middle or the end. Type the choice in large letters; a child may press a key for a letter already learned. Mark [[s02-D1]]: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Sign: watching without choosing (error AN); offer the two wordings again, then record the pick. If the editor is not ready, print the choice on chart paper; paper keeps the composition but does not complete typing, so mark that part not yet seen. The others finish their pictures for the class book.

#### 15 min. End marks (E1)

Individuals, 30 seconds each (30 children, 15 minutes). The class takes one of the three E1 sets by thirds. Show a child three unused strips, one at a time, and read each aloud in a plain voice. Ask [[s02-E1b]] each time: "What is this mark called?" Give no hint. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Sign: a question mark named a period (error AL). The others draw quietly.

#### 17 min. Display preparation

Individuals. Point to the writing-process poster: plan, ask, add (Review: day 69); the class book is the finished add. Children place their drawings and the typed pages in order for the share on day 76. Collect the student pages and slips at the end of the day. Meanwhile, with no new child minutes, read the kept work and mark the record sheet: [[s02-N1]] (events in order and a reaction; error AG, a reaction left out; error AH, events in reverse), s02-V1 (a detail added that answers the partner; error AI, the draft unchanged) and [[s02-E1a]] (a capital on the first word and on I; error AJ, a lowercase first word; error AK, a lowercase i). Copy day 74's assessment marks (s02-O2a, s02-O2b and s02-O2c) from the group list, and the retained marks for s02-S1a, s02-S1b and s02-S1c (errors G, H and I), s02-C1 (errors AE and AF), s02-G1a to s02-G1e (errors F, X, Y, Z and AA) and the verse prompts s02-H1a to s02-H1g (errors K, L, M and AM) from the record sheet. Never score first-teaching work. Mark only parts you actually hold; the rest are completed on days 77 and 78.

#### 5 min. Foundations: rhyme and syllables

Whole class, oral (cycle 11, lesson 60). Rhyme: say cloud and loud; children give another (proud, crowd). Clap mitten (two), butterfly (three) and boot (one).

#### 10 min. Foundations: j, e and z pattern check

Whole class, all three cards at once. Review: j, e and z were taught on day 71. Today is a check; nothing is taught. First, 3 minutes: show each of the six cards (both cases of j, e and z) one at a time; every child holds up the matching card from their own set; the day's six each say the three names aloud in turn, about 30 seconds each ([[s02-C75a]]). Next, 3 minutes: six groups of five, 30 seconds a group (about 6 seconds a child); show each card and each child says its sound ([[s02-C75b]]). Last, 4 minutes: say /j/, then short e, then /z/; every child writes the letter for each on a slip ([[s02-C75c]]). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a sound given for a name (error U); a wrong consonant sound (error V); a wrong letter written (error W). Names for the other twenty-four are heard on days 77 and 78.

#### 5 min. Foundations: mapped words see and does

Whole class. Review: see and does, read whole (day 71). Point under "Pip does see Jed." and "You see a jet."; children read them. Name the part to remember in each word.

#### 10 min. Foundations: shared print

Whole class, then pairs. Review: reread verse 9, Look Out and See, with the pointer, tapping the spaces (day 72). Say clues for a word: it is in line 1; it has three letters; it has two e's; you do it with your eyes. Children point to the word and say it. Pairs take turns pointing under each word while the partner says it back.

#### 30 min. Foundations: rotation

Table and stations. Six children at your table, 2 minutes each (12 minutes), with counters: s02-P1a (first, middle and last sound), s02-P1b1 (blend), s02-P1b2 (segment), s02-P1b3 (add a sound first), s02-P1b4 (change the first sound), all items of day 70. These are the fifth six; no one heard on days 70 to 73 is asked again. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: wrong first, middle or last sound (errors N, O, P); cannot blend (error Q); a sound left out (error R); a sound added in the wrong place (error S); the wrong sound changed (error T). Keep the record for days 77 and 78. The others move among stations: letter cards, handwriting paper, and the poem Read Together reread with a partner pointing. For the last 18 minutes, visit the stations, watch grips and write who was heard on the record sheet. Do not retry or reteach anything today.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the listener. The child picks between two wordings and you type or print the choice, then you show three strips for the end marks and ask for the three letters by sound. One projector: show the class book on one screen; the drawings stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: print the choice large on paper. No letter cards: write j, e and z on paper. No strips: write three short sentences yourself, each ending in a different mark.

**Skip.** The shared screen, the strips from school, the stations and the verse.

- **15 min. The assessment: pick the words.** Your child picks how a sentence from their story should sound.
  **Say.** "Which way should the story say it?" Expect: either wording; any pick is right. Print the pick large.
- **10 min. End marks.** Write "Rain falls." "Is it cold?" and "Look out!" on three strips.
  **Say.** "What is this mark called?" Expect: a period; a question mark; an exclamation mark.
- **10 min. Letters.** Say /j/, then short e, then /z/. Your child writes the letter for each, then says each name.
- **5 min. The ending.** Your child tells you how their story ends and how the character felt.
- **5 min. Rhyme.** Say cloud and loud. Your child says another word that rhymes.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the class piece, the end marks and the letter check; cut the shared print. Longer: a secure child chooses the page order for a friend and says why.

### Words and supports

Words: publish, mark, question. Frames: "I pick ___." "The mark at the end is a ___." Pointing, dictation and a communication aid all count. Read each prompt twice. A child who cannot say a name aloud may point to it on a card of the three marks; record that help.

### Tomorrow

Day 76 is a response day. Its first 15 minutes reteach the one error most missed across the records of days 70 to 75 (error AJ, AK or AL for capitals and marks; error AI; error AF; the sound errors N to W), with a fresh retry on a new card that is not marked. The children also share their finished stories, each with one question from a partner, and reread the last part of Yusuf and the Parcel.
