# The Puppet

Sol has a puppet. Emi asks, 'May I see it?' Sol says, 'After this song.' Sol finishes the song. Sol lifts the puppet high for Emi.

Pictures (three, one plain line each):
1. Sol sits singing, with the puppet in his lap.
2. Emi stands by Sol and asks, with one hand held out.
3. Sol lifts the puppet high for Emi, who smiles. This is the target picture for item A2.

Credits on the back page: "Words: Curio Class writing team. Pictures: Curio Class art team."

Measure: 28 words, 5 short sentences. Easy listening; the turn-taking idea (waiting for a song to end, then sharing) is shown, not told.
