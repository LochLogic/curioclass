# Weekly class poems and the class big book

Seven short poems, one a week, then the big book. Each is a whole text, written by Curio Class in 2026. Verse has no numerical measure; repetition and rhyme support shared print. A few words in the poems are among the class's mapped words (I, the, a); everything else is read to the children and pointed to, never guessed from a picture or an initial letter.

## p01: Our Play
Used: Day 1 (first weekly poem, heard beside two classroom toys); an invitation to join in.

Come and play, come and play,
There's a toy for us today.
Roll it, spin it, push it, pull,
Come and play, there's room for all.

Come and play, come and play,
Share the toy and share the day.
You take a turn, then I take mine,
Come and play, and we'll be fine.
Picture: A shared page of two four-line verses. Two children, one in a wheelchair, hold out a toy cart and a toy top toward the reader.

## p02: Ask and Listen
Used: Day 7 (shared reading; turn and talk).

I ask, you tell.
You ask, I tell.
We listen well.
We listen well.

Eyes on you and ears on me,
Then it's your turn, wait and see.
I ask, you tell.
We listen well.
Picture: Two children sit face to face on a rug, one with a hand cupped to her ear, the other with her mouth open to speak.

## p03: Toy Clues
Used: Day 12 (attribute words and opposites; reread with Attributes of Toys).

Soft and hard, soft and hard,
A bear, a block, a toy in the yard.
Smooth and rough, smooth and rough,
A top, a rope, that's quite enough.
Tiny, gigantic, tiny, gigantic,
A little cart, a truck so big.
Soft and hard, smooth and rough,
Tiny, gigantic, that's the stuff!
Picture: A row of classroom toys sorted in pairs: a soft bear and a hard block, a smooth top and a rough rope, a tiny cart and a gigantic truck.

## p04: My Choice
Used: Day 16 (names a toy and says a preference).

Some like the blocks and some like the ball,
Some like the cart that rolls down the hall.
I choose the top, you choose the dough,
We each have a favorite, and off we go.

Say it out loud, say it out loud:
My favorite toy is . . . (say it, proud!)
Picture: Four children each hold their own choice of classroom toy: blocks, a ball, a cart and a top. The last child, a boy with a hearing aid, points to his chest.

## p05: Many Ways
Used: Day 25 (revisited to name a poem; walk, march, tiptoe).

We walk, walk, walk.
We march, march, march.
We tiptoe, tiptoe, tiptoe. Shh!

Some play fast and some play slow,
Some play high and some play low.
We walk, walk, walk.
We march, march, march.
We tiptoe, tiptoe, tiptoe. Shh!
Picture: Three children in a line on a classroom floor. The first walks, the second marches with high knees, the third tiptoes with a finger to her lips.

## p06: A Partner Speaks
Used: Day 28 (named as a poem in the book-kinds check); interview.

My partner says, "I like the top."
I listen well, I do not stop.
My partner says, "It spins around."
I listen well, I hear each sound.

Now I ask, and you will say,
Tell me more about your play.
Then I say what I have heard,
Every one of your good words.
Picture: Two children sit side by side at a table. One holds a toy top and talks. The other listens, leaning in.

## p07: Read Together
Used: Day 32 (purposeful shared reading with word relationships); returns on days 33 and 34.

Read together, you and me,
Fast and slow and one-two-three.
Soft voice, loud voice, high and low,
Read together, off we go!

Up and down and in and out,
Read together, say it out.
Fast and slow and one-two-three,
Read together, you and me.
Picture: A whole class sits in a half circle on a rug, looking at a large poem chart. The teacher points to the line with a pointer.
