# We Depend on Trees

[page 1]
People depend on trees. We depend on trees.
Picture: Many people of different ages stand under one big tree: a builder, a child, a teacher, a baker.

[page 2]
A builder needs wood for a house. The tree gives wood. We depend on trees.
Picture: A builder carrying planks of wood toward a half-built house.

[page 3]
A child needs shade on a hot day. The tree gives shade. We depend on trees.
Picture: A child resting on a blanket in the cool shade of a leafy tree.

[page 4]
A family needs fruit to eat. The tree gives apples. We depend on trees.
Picture: A family picking red apples from low branches, with a basket on the ground.

[page 5]
A student needs paper to draw on. Paper is made from wood. The tree gives wood. We depend on trees.
Picture: A student drawing a picture on a sheet of paper at a desk, with a small picture of a tree beside the paper.

[page 6]
A family needs syrup for pancakes. The maple tree gives sap. The sap is boiled to make syrup. We depend on trees.
Picture: A maple tree with a bucket hanging on its trunk, and a plate of pancakes with syrup beside it.

[page 7]
We depend on trees. All of us depend on trees. Say it with me: We depend on trees!
Picture: The same people from the first page, smiling together under the tree.
