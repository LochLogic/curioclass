[page 1]
The sun is up.

Picture: A bright farmyard morning. A brown hen, Peg, stands in the open yard with the sun high above her.

[page 2]
The sun is on Peg.

Picture: Close on Peg. Sunlight falls on her feathers and her beak is a little open.

[page 3]
Peg ran from the sun to the shed.

Picture: Peg runs across the yard toward a small shed. A young tree stands beside the shed and throws a patch of shade.

[page 4]
The sun is not in the shed.

Picture: Peg steps into the cool shadow inside the shed door. The yard behind her is bright.

[page 5]
Peg can nap in the shed.

Picture: Peg lies on a pile of straw in the shed with her eyes closed.

[page 6]
Peg has a bit of a nap.

Picture: Peg asleep in the straw. A small ray of light crosses the floor beside her.
