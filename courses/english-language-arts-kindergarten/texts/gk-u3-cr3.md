# Gus in a Tub

- text_id: gk-u3-cr3
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable reader, 10 pages, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the adult reads the title
- word count: 39 (the sentences children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: cycle 15 reader for foundations; children read one sentence a page with a partner, then say what happened (RF.K.4, RF.K.3d); the picture is covered while they read
- read on: days 96 (first), 97, 98, 99, 100, 101
- decodable through: cycle 15 (first day 96)
- patterns: short a, i and u, with sh, th and ch (cycle 12), one consonant letter each side of the vowel, no blend, no plural s, no ending beyond the words below
- high-frequency (mapped) words by its first day: the same, plus was, his. Those used in this reader are listed under each page below by the checker line.
- kept out on purpose: pen, pin, bed, sun and the F1 passage words (Ben, hut, pot, mud, red, cup, hid); also the exit and card nouns net, bug, Jed, Sam, bag; no exit card reuses this reader's title or nouns
- note on the plan: Plan title is Mud in a Tub. Retitled Gus in a Tub because mud is the answer word of the F1 passage (days 110 to 116) and is reserved.

New words and the pattern each one practices:

- pup, tub, Gus, run, ran, fun, hug: short u, one consonant each side
- sat, can, Jan: short a, review
- in, it: mapped, review

High-frequency words in the body: a, at, has, in, is, it, see, the, was.

Teacher notes: a story, invented for practice; no real-world facts beyond what a child already knows. Every word of the body was checked against the letters, patterns and mapped words taught by day 96.
