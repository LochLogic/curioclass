[page 1]
A community is a group of people who live, work, or learn near one another. Your school is part of a community. So is your street.
Trees help our community.

Picture: A sunny street seen from above with a school, houses, a store, and a park. Trees line the street. Children and adults of many ages walk along it.

[page 2]
Look at a tree. Its trunk holds it up. Its bark covers the trunk like a coat. Its branches reach out, and its leaves make a leafy top that is called the canopy.

Picture: One tree drawn large. Labels point to the trunk, the bark, a branch, a leaf, and the canopy.

[page 3]
Why do trees help our community? Here is the first reason. Trees give us shaded places to rest.
The canopy blocks some of the sun. Under it, the air feels cooler than it does on a bright sidewalk. That matters because a hot day can make people tired. A cool, shady place helps them rest.

Picture: A tree with a wide canopy. The sun is bright all around it, and a cool, dark patch of shade lies under it.

[page 4]
This is why a bench under a tree is a good place to rest. People sit on the shaded bench. Children read under the leaves. Neighbors stop and talk in the cool shade.

Picture: A shaded bench under a tree. An older man sits on it with a newspaper, a girl reads a book on the grass, and two neighbors chat nearby.

[page 5]
Here is the second reason. Trees give birds shelter.
Birds build nests among the branches. A bird rests on a branch. Baby birds grow up in the nest, high above the ground. When wind blows or rain falls, thick leaves give a bird a place to hide. That is why a bird needs a tree.

Picture: A tree in the rain. A small bird sits on a branch under thick leaves. A nest with two eggs rests in a fork of the branches nearby.

[page 6]
Birds that find shelter in our trees stay near our homes and schools. We can watch them hop from branch to branch. We can hear them sing in the morning. A tree with birds makes our community a lively place.

Picture: Children at a school window and on the grass look up at a bird in a tree. One child points.

[page 7]
Trees help our community. They give shaded places to rest, and they give birds shelter. People plant trees and water them because trees help everyone who lives nearby. That is why people care for their trees.

Picture: The street from page 1 again. This time, a shaded bench, a bird on a branch, and a child watering a young tree are shown, each with a small circle around it.
