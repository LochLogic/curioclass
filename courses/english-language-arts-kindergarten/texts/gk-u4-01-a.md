[page 1]
Lina lived on the third floor of a tall brick building. Beside the front door grew a young tree. It was only a little taller than Lina.
Every morning, before school, Lina stopped to say hello to her tree. She touched one green leaf. She looked up at the thin branches. "Good morning, tree," she whispered.

Picture: A tall brick apartment building with a bright front door. A girl with dark braids and a backpack stands beside a slender young tree, one hand on a leaf.

[page 2]
Mr. Dube looked after the building. He had planted the tree last fall, and Lina had watched him set it in the ground. Now it was spring. The tree wore soft pink blossoms, and a bee hummed from flower to flower.

Picture: A man in work gloves, Mr. Dube, leans on a shovel beside the young tree, which is covered in pink blossoms. A bee hovers near one flower.

[page 3]
One morning, Mrs. Hassan from the second floor came out with her shopping bag. Lina waved her over. "Come and smell the blossoms!" she called.
Mrs. Hassan set down her bag. She bent close to a branch and shut her eyes. "Oh," she said. "They smell sweet, like honey." She smiled. "Thank you for showing me, Lina."
The two of them stood together for a long minute, breathing in the spring air.

Picture: An older woman in a green headscarf leans toward a blossoming branch with her eyes closed. Lina stands beside her, smiling. A shopping bag rests on the step.

[page 4]
Days went by, and the sun grew hot. One afternoon Lina came home from school and knelt beside her tree. The soil around the trunk looked pale and cracked. She pressed her finger into it. It was dry as dust.
Lina's smile went away. She did not feel like saying hello.

Picture: Lina kneels at the base of the tree, her finger in pale, cracked soil. Her shoulders are low. The sun is bright overhead.

[page 5]
She ran inside and found Mr. Dube by the elevator. "The soil around my tree is dry," she said. "Does it need water?"
Mr. Dube went outside to look. He nodded. "A young tree has small roots," he said. "On hot days it needs a drink." He pointed to the big green watering can by the door. "Tomorrow morning, we will fill the can and water the tree together. We will pour slowly, so the water sinks down to the roots."
"Tomorrow morning," said Lina. "First thing."

Picture: Mr. Dube crouches beside the tree and touches the dry soil. Lina stands next to him. A big green watering can sits by the front door.

[page 6]
That night, Lina could not stop thinking about her tree. She looked out her window at the dark street. Clouds were gathering over the rooftops. Then something tapped on the glass.
Tap. Tap. Tap-tap-tap.
Rain!

Picture: Lina at her third-floor window in her pajamas. Outside, dark clouds and the first drops of rain on the glass.

[page 7]
In the morning, Lina hurried downstairs in her boots. Rain had fallen all night. Puddles shone on the path, and the soil around the trunk was dark and wet. The rain had soaked all the way down to the roots.
Mr. Dube set down the watering can. "The rain already gave the tree its drink," he said. "We do not need the can today."

Picture: Puddles on the path. The soil around the young tree is dark. Raindrops drip from the leaves. Mr. Dube holds the empty can, and Lina wears yellow boots.

[page 8]
Lina laughed. "I made a plan," she said, "and the rain changed it."
"A good change," said Mr. Dube.
Lina looked at the dripping leaves and felt warm all over, even in the gray morning.

Picture: Lina and Mr. Dube smile at each other beside the wet tree. Lina has her face tilted up toward the leaves.

[page 9]
That afternoon the wind blew, and one green leaf drifted down onto the path. Lina picked it up. It was cool and damp.
Mrs. Hassan came out of the door just then. Lina held out the leaf. "This fell from our tree," she said. "It is for you."
Mrs. Hassan tucked the leaf carefully into her pocket. "Someday this tree will be tall," she said, "and it will make shade for all of us on the steps."

Picture: Lina holds a single green leaf out to Mrs. Hassan beside the young tree. The ground is still damp, and the sky is breaking into blue.

[page 10]
Lina stood beside her tree once more. "Good afternoon, tree," she said.
The tree stood in the sunshine with its roots in the wet earth, and Lina stood a little taller, too.

Picture: Lina stands very straight beside the young tree in sunshine. Her shadow and the tree's shadow lie side by side on the path.
