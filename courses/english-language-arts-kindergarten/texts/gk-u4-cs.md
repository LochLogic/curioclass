# Cycle decodable sentences, cycles 19 to 25

- text_id: gk-u4-cs
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary (sentence sets for joint reading)
- form: seven sets of short decodable sentences, one set a cycle, read together after the explicit pattern teaching and then in small groups and with a partner
- word count: 362 in all (set by set below)
- use: the 'read and reread the cycle's decodable sentences' step of each foundations lesson (shared lesson, then partner reread); the day lists the sentence it uses; they are practice, never the cycle-check passages
- read on: cycle 19 days 119 to 123, cycle 20 days 124 to 129, cycle 21 days 130 to 134, cycle 22 days 135 to 140, cycle 23 days 141 to 145, cycle 24 days 146 to 150, cycle 25 days 151 to 155
- decodable through: each set only through its own cycle (cycles 23 to 25 add no new decoding pattern: long vowels and r-controlled sounds are heard, never printed)
- kept out on purpose: the F1 to F7 prompts and passages, the reader and verse words that the plans reserve, and the sound-change teaching words of the cycle

## Cycle 19 (from day 119)

1. Beth can run in the sun.
2. Josh has a big dish.
3. I can see a fish in the dish.
4. The shop is not shut.
5. Ned had a chip.
6. Seth is in the bath.
7. Gus will rush to the shop.
8. Chip is a pet cat.
9. I have a bag from the shop.
10. Beth has a bit of a bun.

Patterns and high-frequency words: short a: bag, can, cat, had; short a + digraph th: bath; short e: ned, pet; short e + digraph th: beth, seth; short i: big, bit; short i + digraph ch: chip; short i + digraph sh: dish, fish; short o: not; short o + digraph sh: josh, shop; short u: bun, gus, run, sun; short u + digraph sh: rush, shut; high-frequency (mapped): a, from, has, have, i, in, is, of, see, the, to, will.
