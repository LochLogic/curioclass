# Bouncing Toys

This book is about bouncing toys. A ball falls to the floor and bounces up. A spring toy bounces up and down. A bean bag lands and stays on the floor.

Booklet parts: front cover, title page (with the team credits: "Words: Curio Class writing team. Pictures: Curio Class art team."), three inside pages, back cover.

Pictures (plain line each):
- Front cover: a ball in the air above a floor line.
- Page 1: a ball falling to the floor, then the same ball rising.
- Page 2: a spring toy stretched up, then pressed down.
- Page 3: a bean bag lying flat on the floor.
- Back cover: a ball, a spring toy and a bean bag in a row.

Measure: 31 words, 4 sentences, one topic and three details. Easy listening; "bounces" and "spring toy" are the new words.

Withheld: texts 03 and 04 teach other movements with other toys; this wording and idea stay out of teaching until day 31.

Facts: a ball squashes where it hits and springs back, which pushes it up; a soft, filled bag takes in the hit and stays put.
