[page 1]
Ben's school had a big tree beside the path to the playground. Its trunk was wide and rough. Its branches spread out like a green roof. Under the branches stood a wooden bench.
Every day at lunch recess, Ben took his book to the bench under the tree. It was his favorite place in the whole school.

Picture: A school playground path. A very wide tree with spreading branches shades a wooden bench. A boy with glasses and a library book sits there, reading.

[page 2]
Ben took care of the tree and the bench in small ways. Before he sat down, he brushed the fallen twigs off the bench so that anyone could sit there. He never pulled the leaves. He never picked at the bark.
"Hello, tree," he said softly. Then he opened his book.

Picture: Ben sweeps twigs off the bench with his hand. A few leaves lie in the grass. The tree's trunk is rough and ridged.

[page 3]
One sunny day, Ben's classmate Tomas walked by. "Why do you always sit here?" he asked.
"Come and feel the bark," said Ben. He laid his hand flat on the trunk. Tomas did the same.
The bark was rough, with deep ridges like long cracks. "It feels bumpy," said Tomas.
"And cool," said Ben.
They grinned at each other. Then Tomas sat down on the bench beside Ben, and soon both boys were reading.

Picture: Ben and a classmate, Tomas, who has curly black hair, press their palms against the ridged trunk. Both are smiling.

[page 4]
The next day, a truck rumbled up to the school fence. Workers began to fix a broken part of the fence, and a loud machine started up. Buzzzz-RATTLE! Buzzzz-RATTLE!
Ben tried to keep reading. But the words got lost in the noise. He read the same line three times. He pressed his hands over his ears. His shoulders crept up toward them.

Picture: A work truck and two workers with a loud machine at the fence. Ben on the bench covers his ears, his book open on his lap, his forehead scrunched.

[page 5]
Ben did not want to leave his tree. But he could not read here today. He looked up at the branches. He looked down at the path. Then he looked around the trunk.
On the far side of the tree, thick roots curved up out of the ground. "Maybe it is quieter over there," Ben thought.
He picked up his book and walked around the trunk.

Picture: Ben, holding his book against his chest, walks around the wide trunk. On the far side, big roots curve up from the ground like the arms of a chair.

[page 6]
Behind the tree, the machine sounded far away. The leaves made cool shade. Ben sat down on a wide root and leaned his back against the trunk.
He opened his book. Now he could hear the words in his head again.

Picture: Ben sits on a wide root with his back against the trunk, dappled shade on his book. In the distance, the workers and the machine look small.

[page 7]
Soon Tomas peeked around the trunk. Mei came, too. "Where did you go?" asked Tomas.
"It is quieter back here," Ben whispered. "Come and see."
Tomas sat on one root. Mei sat on another. Nobody said much. The branches swayed, and the leaves rustled like soft rain.

Picture: Ben, Tomas, and Mei, who has a short black bob and a red sweater, sit on three roots behind the tree. Each holds a book. The branches sway above them.

[page 8]
Ben had changed his plan, and the tree still gave him a good place to read. He smiled. He felt calm all the way down to his shoes.
When the bell rang, he patted the trunk. "Thank you, tree," he said.

Picture: Ben pats the trunk as the other two children gather their books. A school bell hangs by the door in the background.

[page 9]
The next morning, the fence was fixed and the machine was gone. Before school, Ben drew the trunk in his notebook. He drew every ridge of the bark.
At recess he found Tomas beside the tree. "This is for you," Ben said, tearing out the page. "It is the bark we felt."
Tomas grinned and tucked the drawing into his book.

Picture: Ben holds out a pencil drawing of a ridged trunk to Tomas beside the tree. A fixed, straight fence stands in the background.

[page 10]
That day, the three of them sat together under the branches, and the whole tree was quiet and cool.

Picture: Ben, Tomas, and Mei sit side by side on the bench in the shade, reading, the playground path beyond them.
