# A Hen Has an Egg

- text_id: gk-u4-cr7
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable cycle reader, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the title is adult-read
- word count: 31 (the body words children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: the cycle 25 reader of the foundations block, read in small groups and with a partner; assignments 1 to 6 (find the event, choose the matching event description, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers a question) use this text; it is the only print the children read alone
- read on: first used day 151 (cycle 25), reread on the days of the cycle and the week after
- decodable through: cycle 25
- patterns and high-frequency words used (every body word is in one of these lists):

  - short e: peg
  - short i: sit, tim
  - short o + doubled final ff: off
  - high-frequency (mapped): has, her, i, it, like, no, on, said, she, then, to, up
  - one word is outside the taught patterns: egg (short e followed by the doubled g; the plan field 11 names it). The teacher gives it as a part to remember on the first day, like a mapped word.
- kept out on purpose: the words of the cycle checks and exit cards (F1 to F7 prompts and passages), and the reserved retrieval words; the sound-change teaching words are not reused as story words
- title note: A Hen Has an Egg (adult-read; the first use of its spelling-pattern words is in the body)
