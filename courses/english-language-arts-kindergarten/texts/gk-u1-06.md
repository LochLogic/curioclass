# Toy Riddles

[page 1]
Look around our classroom floor. There are toys, and toys, and more! Here are some clues. Can you guess which toy is near?
Picture: A wide view of a classroom floor and shelves with many toys: a ball, building bricks, blocks, play dough, a teddy bear and puppets.

[page 2]
What is fuzzy, soft and brown? It has two ears that stick up round. It waits for a hug when you sit down.
Picture: The top of a toy's head is hidden behind a card: only soft brown fuzz and two round ears show. (Answer card kept covered.)

[page 3]
I am a teddy bear! Give me a hug.
Picture: A brown teddy bear on a classroom rug, with a child hugging it.

[page 4]
What is round and bouncy and fits in your hand? You can toss it and roll it, and catch it. That is grand!
Picture: A child's open hand holding something round, with the rest hidden behind a card.

[page 5]
I am a ball! I roll across the rug.
Picture: A red ball rolling across a classroom rug toward two children sitting on the floor.

[page 6]
What is bumpy and snaps, with so many small parts? You can click them together to build towers and carts.
Picture: Plastic pieces with bumps on top, partly hidden behind a card.

[page 7]
We are building bricks! Snap us together and make anything you choose.
Picture: Two children snapping building bricks into a long wall at a classroom table.

[page 8]
What is squishy and soft and comes in a tub? You can roll it, and press it, and give it a rub.
Picture: A round tub with the lid half off and something soft and colorful peeking out.

[page 9]
I am play dough! You can make me flat, or round, or tall.
Picture: A child's hands rolling play dough into a snake on a classroom table, with a flat cake and a ball beside it.

[page 10]
What is hard and wooden and stacks up high? Build a tall tower and reach for the sky.
Picture: A stack of plain wooden shapes seen from the side, the top hidden behind a card.

[page 11]
We are blocks! Build us up, and watch us fall down.
Picture: Two children building a tower of wooden blocks on the floor, the top block just placed.

[page 12]
What has a face and a body, and moves when you play? Put your hand inside me, and hear what I say.
Picture: A hand reaching toward a soft cloth shape, only its arm and a bit of its face showing.

[page 13]
I am a puppet! I can walk, march and tiptoe. Let me tell a story with you.
Picture: A cloth hand puppet with button eyes on a child's hand, standing on a table, the other children watching.

[page 14]
Now you can make a riddle. Think of three clues and an action. Then ask a friend, "What toy am I?"
Picture: A child whispering clues to a friend, with a toy hidden behind the child's back.
