# Two Letters, One Sound

- text_id: gk-u3-dp
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary (poem)
- form: one short shared poem of three verses, teacher-read twice with big print; children join the sound refrain
- word count: 75 (the words read aloud)
- use: the digraph poem of days 80 to 84 (one verse a day, sh first on day 80, th on day 81, ch on day 82, all three on days 83 and 84); children say the refrain, find the two letters in print and sort picture words by sound; not an independent reader
- read on: days 80 to 84
- patterns: sh, th, ch, each two letters for one sound, never /s/ then /h/
- kept out on purpose: pen, pin, bed, sun, and the F1 passage words

---

[verse 1: sh]
Two letters, s and h,
Stand close and say /sh/.
A ship and a shell and a fish in the sea,
All start or end with /sh/.

Picture: a toy ship, a shell and a fish in a row; the letters s and h side by side, joined by a small arch.

[verse 2: th]
Two letters, t and h,
Stand close and say /th/.
A thumb and a thorn and a thin, thin thread,
All start with /th/.

Picture: a raised thumb, a rose thorn and a thin thread; the letters t and h side by side, joined by a small arch.

[verse 3: ch]
Two letters, c and h,
Stand close and say /ch/.
A chick and a chin and a chunk of cheese,
All start with /ch/.

Picture: a chick, a child's chin and a chunk of cheese; the letters c and h side by side, joined by a small arch.

---

Hooks: three picture sets for sorting by /sh/, /th/ and /ch/ (ship, shell, fish; thumb, thorn, thread; chick, chin, cheese). The poem never asks children to decode the picture words; the letters under the pictures are for finding the two-letter pair. Rhyme pairs by verse: none required (the refrain repeats the digraph).

Teacher notes: say the sounds, not letter names, in the refrain. Chunk and cheese are food words: show them as pictures only. No source needed; the poem makes no real-world claim beyond what a child can see in the pictures.
