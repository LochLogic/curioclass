# A Hen for Meg

- text_id: gk-u3-cr5
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable reader, 8 pages, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the adult reads the title
- word count: 39 (the sentences children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: cycle 17 reader for foundations; children read one sentence a page with a partner, then say what happened (RF.K.4, RF.K.3d); the picture is covered while they read
- read on: days 107 (first), 108, 109, 110, 111
- decodable through: cycle 17 (first day 107)
- patterns: short a, i, u, o and e, with sh, th and ch (cycle 12), one consonant letter each side of the vowel, no blend, no plural s, no ending beyond the words below
- high-frequency (mapped) words by its first day: the same, plus be, by. Those used in this reader are listed under each page below by the checker line.
- kept out on purpose: pen, pin, bed, sun and the F1 passage words (Ben, hut, pot, mud, red, cup, hid); also the exit and card nouns net, bug, Jed, Sam, bag; no exit card reuses this reader's title or nouns
- note on the plan: Plan title is A Hen in a Pen. Retitled A Hen for Meg because pen is reserved for the F1 word pair (pen and pin, days 110 to 116).
