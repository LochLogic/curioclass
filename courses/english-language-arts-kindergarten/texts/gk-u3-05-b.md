[page 1]
This page is about a sugar maple.
A sugar maple is a tall tree. It has a straight trunk and many branches. Its leaves have pointed parts, like fingers on a hand.

Picture: A tall sugar maple on a quiet street. A boy in a red jacket holds up a maple leaf next to his hand to compare.

[page 2]
What does the maple need?
A maple needs water. Its roots take water from the soil. A maple needs light and air. Its leaves catch light from the sun and take in air. The leaves make food for the tree.
A maple needs soil to stand in. Its roots hold the tree in place.

Picture: A cutaway of a maple. Roots in soil below, leaves in sunlight above, with arrows labeled water, air and light.

[page 3]
A maple has seeds.
Its seeds have a paper wing. When a seed falls, the wing spins. The seed twirls down to the ground like a tiny helicopter.
Squirrels eat its seeds. A squirrel picks up a seed and nibbles. So the squirrel depends on the maple for food.

Picture: Two maple seeds with paper wings spin through the air above a lawn. A squirrel sits on the grass, holding one seed in its paws.

[page 4]
A maple gives shelter.
Songbirds build nests in its branches. The branches hold the nest up high. The leaves are a roof over it. That is shelter.

Picture: A small brown songbird sits in a nest in a fork of a maple. Leaves cover the nest like a roof.

[page 5]
A maple changes in the fall.
Its leaves turn yellow, orange or red. Then the leaves fall off. In spring, new leaves grow.

Picture: A maple in three small drawings: green in summer, red and orange in fall, bare in winter.

[page 6]
Some people use maple, too.
A sugar maple has sap inside its trunk. Sap is a watery liquid. In late winter, people collect sap in buckets. They boil it until it is thick and sweet. Then it is maple syrup.

A maple needs water, light and soil. Squirrels need its seeds. Birds need its shelter. People get syrup. One tree is a home and a resource for many.

Picture: A bucket hangs from a maple trunk in the snow. A girl and her aunt pour sap into a big pot over a fire, steam rising.
