# A Log in a Pond

- text_id: gk-u3-cr4
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable reader, 9 pages, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the adult reads the title
- word count: 38 (the sentences children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: cycle 16 reader for foundations; children read one sentence a page with a partner, then say what happened (RF.K.4, RF.K.3d); the picture is covered while they read
- read on: days 102 (first), 103, 104, 105, 106
- decodable through: cycle 16 (first day 102)
- patterns: short a, i, u and o, with sh, th and ch (cycle 12), one consonant letter each side of the vowel, no blend, no plural s, no ending beyond the words below
- high-frequency (mapped) words by its first day: the same, plus or, for. Those used in this reader are listed under each page below by the checker line.
- kept out on purpose: pen, pin, bed, sun and the F1 passage words (Ben, hut, pot, mud, red, cup, hid); also the exit and card nouns net, bug, Jed, Sam, bag; no exit card reuses this reader's title or nouns
- note on the plan: A Log in a Pond (plan title kept; adult reads the title, so pond is never decoded)

New words and the pattern each one practises:

- log, dog, hop, top, got, Tom, Dot, bob, nod: short o, one consonant each side
- on: short o, a vowel-consonant word
- or, for: mapped words taught this cycle (day 102)

High-frequency words in the body: a, for, has, is, it, or, the.

Teacher notes: a story, invented for practice; no real-world facts beyond what a child already knows. Every word of the body was checked against the letters, patterns and mapped words taught by day 102.
