# A Cat Can Sit

- text_id: gk-u4-cr5
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable cycle reader, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the title is adult-read
- word count: 35 (the body words children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: the cycle 23 reader of the foundations block, read in small groups and with a partner; assignments 1 to 6 (find the event, choose the matching event description, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers a question) use this text; it is the only print the children read alone
- read on: first used day 141 (cycle 23), reread on the days of the cycle and the week after
- decodable through: cycle 23
- patterns and high-frequency words used (every body word is in one of these lists):

  - short i: sit, tim
  - short u: nut, rug
  - high-frequency (mapped): a, has, is, it, on, the, then, to, what, when
- kept out on purpose: the words of the cycle checks and exit cards (F1 to F7 prompts and passages), and the reserved retrieval words; the sound-change teaching words are not reused as story words
- title note: A Cat Can Sit (adult-read; the first use of its spelling-pattern words is in the body)
