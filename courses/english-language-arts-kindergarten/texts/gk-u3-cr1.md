# Ants at a Mat

- text_id: gk-u3-cr1
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable reader, 10 pages, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the adult reads the title
- word count: 35 (the sentences children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: cycle 13 reader for foundations; children read one sentence a page with a partner, then say what happened (RF.K.4, RF.K.3d); the picture is covered while they read
- read on: days 85 (first), 86, 87, 88, 89, 90
- decodable through: cycle 13 (first day 85)
- patterns: short a, with sh, th and ch (cycle 12), one consonant letter each side of the vowel, no blend, no plural s, no ending beyond the words below
- high-frequency (mapped) words by its first day: I, my, me, the, a, in, you, are, see, does, to, do, at, has. Those used in this reader are listed under each page below by the checker line.
- kept out on purpose: pen, pin, bed, sun and the F1 passage words (Ben, hut, pot, mud, red, cup, hid); also the exit and card nouns net, bug, Jed, Sam, bag; no exit card reuses this reader's title or nouns
- note on the plan: Ants at a Mat (plan title kept; adult reads the title, so the plural ants is never decoded)

New words and the pattern each one practises:

- Nat, mat, sat, had, nap, ham, jam, can: short a, one consonant each side
- Zac: short a; c says /k/ (letter c, cycle 3), a name
- dash: short a with sh at the end (digraph, days 80 to 84)
- that: th at the start (digraph)
- chat: ch at the start (digraph)

High-frequency words in the body: a, has, the, to.

Teacher notes: a story, invented for practice; no real-world facts beyond what a child already knows. Every word of the body was checked against the letters, patterns and mapped words taught by day 85.
