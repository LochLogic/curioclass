# A Hen in a Tub

- text_id: gk-u4-cr3
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable cycle reader, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the title is adult-read
- word count: 40 (the body words children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: the cycle 21 reader of the foundations block, read in small groups and with a partner; assignments 1 to 6 (find the event, choose the matching event description, match a sentence to a picture, copy the sentence that answers a question) use this text; it is the only print the children read alone
- read on: first used day 130 (cycle 21), reread on the days of the cycle and the week after
- decodable through: cycle 21
- patterns and high-frequency words used (every body word is in one of these lists):

  - short e: peg, red, wet
  - short i + digraph th: with

  - short u: rub, tub, us
  - high-frequency (mapped): a, all, has, have, in, is, of, one, the, to, we
- kept out on purpose: the words of the cycle checks and exit cards (F1 to F7 prompts and passages), and the reserved retrieval words; the sound-change teaching words are not reused as story words
- title note: A Hen in a Tub (adult-read; the first use of its spelling-pattern words is in the body)

Picture: Peg, the brown hen, stands in the tub with water up to her chest.

Picture: Peg shakes herself. Water flies off her wings.

[page 5]

Picture: Four children run toward the tub with their arms out.

[page 6]

Picture: One child holds up a rag and smiles.

[page 7]

Picture: Two children rub Peg gently with the rag while she stands on the grass.

[page 8]
