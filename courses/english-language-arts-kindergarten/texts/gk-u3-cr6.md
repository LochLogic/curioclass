# A Pet at Home

- text_id: gk-u3-cr6
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable reader, 10 pages, one sentence a page, a plain one-line picture description a page; the adult reads the title
- word count: 44 (the sentences children read, not the title or picture notes)
- use: cycle 18 reader for foundations; children read one sentence a page with a partner, then say what happened (RF.K.4, RF.K.3d); the picture is covered while they read
- read on: days 112 (first), 113, 114, 115, 116, 117, 118
- decodable through: cycle 18 (first day 112)
- patterns: all five short vowels, with sh, th and ch (cycle 12), one consonant letter each side of the vowel, no blend, no plural s, no ending beyond the words below
- high-frequency (mapped) words by its first day: the same, plus are (already mapped), with, will. Those used in this reader are listed under each page below by the checker line.
- kept out on purpose: pen, pin, bed, sun and the F1 passage words (Ben, hut, pot, mud, red, cup, hid); also the exit and card nouns net, bug, Jed, Sam, bag; no exit card reuses this reader's title or nouns
- note on the plan: A Pet at Home (adult reads the title, so home, with its silent e, is never decoded)

New words and the pattern each one practices:

- Ned, Zip, pet, hop, hug, van, big, sad, fun, nod: all five short vowels, one consonant each side
- with, will, are: mapped words (are from unit 2; with and will taught day 112)
- is, it, in, a, the: mapped, review

High-frequency words in the body: a, are, has, in, is, it, the, will, with, you.

Teacher notes: a story, invented for practice; no real-world facts beyond what a child already knows. Every word of the body was checked against the letters, patterns and mapped words taught by day 112.
