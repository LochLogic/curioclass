# Play Lines

Page 1:
I pat my hat.
The cat is at a mat.

Page 2:
A pal sits
with me.

(Three lines over two pages with a line break inside the second page, for the track item.)

Pictures (plain line each):
- Page 1: a child pats a hat on her head; a cat sits on a mat.
- Page 2: two children sit side by side on a rug.

Measure: 15 words. Verse, no numerical measure; repetition of -at supports shared print, not guessing.

Fresh word-order strips for the letter-sequence item: "pat" and "tap" on two strips (to make).
