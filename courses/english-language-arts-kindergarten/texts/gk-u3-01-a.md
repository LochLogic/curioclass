# A Tree and a Toy (Living Things, part a)

[page 1]
Here is a toy truck. It is red. It has four round wheels.
Push the truck, and it rolls across the floor. It rolls fast. It rolls slow. It stops when it bumps the wall.

Picture: A child with brown skin kneels on a classroom floor and pushes a small red toy truck toward a wall. Other toys sit on a shelf behind.

[page 2]
Here is a tree. It stands at the edge of the playground.
The tree has a rough trunk, long branches, and many green leaves. Under the ground, it has roots. We cannot see the roots, but they are there.
The tree does not roll. It does not run. It stays in one place.

Picture: A tall tree at the edge of a school playground. A cutaway shows its roots spreading under the grass. Leaves are green.

[page 3]
Let us compare the truck and the tree.
The truck is the same today as it was last year. It has the same size. It has the same wheels. It does not get bigger. It only moves when somebody pushes it.

Picture: Two pictures of the same toy truck side by side, one labeled "last year" and one labeled "today." They look alike.

[page 4]
The tree is not the same as it was last year. Leila looked at an old photo of the playground. In the photo, the tree was thin and small. Now the tree is taller than the slide. Its trunk is wider. It has more leaves.
The tree grew.

Picture: A girl with dark curly hair, Leila, holds up an old photo of the playground, small tree in it. Behind her stands the same tree, much bigger.

[page 5]
Leila noticed something else. She touched a leaf with one finger. She touched the rough trunk. Then she looked up at the branches.
"The truck can move, but I push it," said Leila. "The tree does not move, but it grows. How can I tell if a tree is alive?"

Picture: Leila reaches up and gently touches a leaf. A friend stands beside her, looking up.

[page 6]
Leila had a question.
Does moving tell us if something is living? A pushed truck moves. A tree stays still.
What does a tree have that a truck does not?
What does a tree need?
Let us find out.

Picture: Leila and two classmates sit on the grass under the tree with a toy truck between them, thinking. Question marks float above the page.
