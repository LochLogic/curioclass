# Who Depends on a Tree?

[page 1]
Many animals depend on trees. To depend on something means to need it. Here are three animals that depend on a tree.
Picture: A wide picture of a big tree with a squirrel on a branch, a bird near a nest, and a deer standing under it.

[page 2]
A squirrel depends on a tree. The squirrel is small and furry, and it is noisy. It hops along the branches. It finds nuts and seeds in the tree, and it eats them.
Picture: A small furry squirrel on a branch holding a nut between its paws, with seeds on the branch.

[page 3]
A bird depends on a tree. The bird is quick and light. It builds a nest high in the branches. The branches hold the nest up. The nest is a home for the eggs and the baby birds.
Picture: A small bird on a branch beside a cup-shaped nest held where two branches meet, with three eggs showing inside.

[page 4]
A deer depends on a tree. The deer is tall and graceful, and it is shy. It eats leaves and acorns that come from trees.
Picture: A deer standing under the lower branches of an oak tree, reaching up to a leaf, with acorns on the ground.

[page 5]
Squirrels, birds and deer all depend on trees. What other living thing needs a tree?
Picture: The tree with the squirrel, the bird's nest and the deer, and a question mark above the branches.
