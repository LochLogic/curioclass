# Day 132: Check, a point and reasons, and two texts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write children 1 to 30 in six groups of five on the record sheet. Keep pages 4 and 7 of Wangari and the Tree Planters closed; they have never been read or shown. Pick six to scribe and six for the sound check.

### The day

#### 5 min. Familiar words and the day's plan

Read the wall words. Any child may pass. If day 131's exit showed many error E, say so once; its repair waits for day 133.

#### 10 min. Familiar reread, whole class

Read page 4 of Trees Help Our Community and page 6 of Planting Together once each. No teaching.

#### 30 min. Observation, C2: six groups of five

Five minutes a group. Read page 4 of Wangari and the Tree Planters twice (30 seconds). Each child in turn answers [[s04-C2av2]], then [[s04-C2bv2]], about 10 seconds a stem. Read page 7 once. Each child answers [[s04-C2cv2]] and [[s04-C2dv2]], about 20 seconds. Give one prompt a child, never an answer. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: a detail as the point (error B); a liking as a reason (error C); both say the same (error E). The other twenty-five partner-read A Hen in a Tub and draw a way a tree helps with the frame "I like ___."

#### 15 min. Draw and dictate, everyone

Everyone draws a helper of a tree on side 1. You scribe the day's six, about 2 minutes each. Collect the pages at the end of the day.

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 21, lesson 108: short e and u)

Whole class with tiles. Review: short e and short u (day 130). Build cut; change the middle letter to make cat; name the changed sound; spell cut. Ending sounds ank and ink are heard only. Read we, all and one in "We all have one bun." before alone; read "We run to the hut." twice.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: A Hen in a Tub. Assignments 1 to 6 use the reader and the verse; 7 and 8 are sounds without a text, and one mapped word read with one taught word spelled. Hear the day's six, about 100 seconds each. The others partner-read.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Echo Tree Care Words, verse 3. Write "First, we tie one wide, soft strap in place." together; each child writes cut on side 2.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Say gum; six children say its first, middle and last sound. Unscored.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; read both pages and ask the four stems.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the marks.

- **10 min. The assessment, page 4.** Read page 4 of Wangari and the Tree Planters twice.
  **Say.** "What is the writer's point? Say one reason." Expect: trees can help, then one reason the page gives, not a liking.
- **10 min. The assessment, page 7.** Read page 7 once.
  **Say.** "How are the two pages the same? How are they different?" Expect: both say trees keep soil in place and hold rainwater; page 4 adds food and firewood, page 7 more people joining.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws a helper of a tree and says "A ___ helps a tree."
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say cut. Your child changes the middle sound to make cat.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the two pages and the sound check. Longer: a secure child finds a second reason in Trees Help Our Community.

### Words and supports

Words: point, reason, both, different. Frame: "A ___ helps a tree." Pointing, a drawing and a home language all count.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who named a point, a reason and a difference. Day 133's Return and repair takes errors B, C and E from this check, in ten minutes; nothing else carries.
