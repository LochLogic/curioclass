# Day 130: A box with arrows for the writer's reasons

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record for day 129: list children who said a detail as the point (error B), and children who made error S, AN or AI on the check or were not finished. Pick six for the exit and the pair talk, none heard on day 129. Draw the box and two arrows on chart paper. Keep E130 face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

2 min: Review: point (day 129), the one sentence the writer wants us to remember. New word: a reason is a sentence the writer says that tells why. Hold the point card in one hand and a card for a reason in the other. 3 min, hand-back from day 129's exit, error B: if more than ten made it, the whole class; otherwise a small group. Use the text, not the exit card. The puppet says, "The point is shade." Read pages 3 and 7. Say: "Shade is one thing the writer says. The point is the whole sentence." Then hold up two cards, "Children read under leaves" and "Trees help our community," and children point to the sentence that is the point.

#### 10 min. Reread, the reasons and the point

Whole class on the rug. Read pages 3 to 7 once. **Ask.** "Which sentence states the point?" Expect: "Trees help our community," the first sentence of page 7; children put a finger on it. **Ask.** "Which two reasons explain it?" Expect: trees give us shaded places to rest (page 3) and trees give birds shelter (page 5); each follows "Here is the first reason" or "Here is the second reason." Partners say them, 20 seconds each, all pairs at once; hear three pairs.

#### 20 min. Model the box with arrows

Whole class. Review: because (unit 3), a reason that joins what the tree does with a need. Show the chart paper: a box at the top and two arrows down. 4 min: write the whole sentence in the box, "Trees help our community," and tap page 1 and page 7. 5 min: arrow one. Point to the writer's sentence on page 3, "Trees give us shaded places to rest." Set the page 3 picture card on the arrow and say it with because: "Trees help our community because trees give us shaded places to rest." 5 min: arrow two the same way from page 5, "Trees give birds shelter." **Ask.** "Which sentence on page 5 is the reason?" Expect: trees give birds shelter. The puppet says, "One reason is I like trees." Say: "The puppet likes trees. The writer did not say it. A reason is a sentence the writer says, so we point to it first." That is error C. 6 min: six children come up in turn, 30 seconds each, tap the box and one arrow and say its sentence; others say it with them.

#### 20 min. Partners try a new card

Pairs. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read twice: "The rug is soft to sit on. The shelf holds our books. Our reading corner is a good place." Each pair gets the mat and three sentence cards. 8 min: find the whole claim and put it in the box, put the other two on the arrows, pointing to each sentence on the card before setting it, then say it with because. Expect: the point is the last sentence, "Our reading corner is a good place," with the soft rug and the shelf on the arrows. Hear the day's six in their pairs, about 2 minutes a pair, and name them in the record column. 6 min: each child draws the box and two arrows on side 1 and says each reason. 3 min: two pairs show theirs. Look for: the point is a whole claim; both reasons are ones the card states; no liking is placed as a reason. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E130 twice, with its four reason cards, then ask [[s04-X130]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others finish their drawing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: puts I like mats or The mat is blue on an arrow (error C).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 21, lesson 106: short e and u)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. 5 min: Review: short e (day 107; keyword picture an egg) and the sound-change chain on tiles (unit 3). New: short u, the sound in nut and tub. Build net and say /e/; change the middle letter to make nut and say /u/; change the first letter to make hut. Each time name the sound that changed. 3 min: ending sounds, heard only. Say bank and sink; children tap the ending sounds. No printed ank or ink word is read or written. 5 min: map we, all and one. In we the w is regular and the e says its own name; in all the l's are regular and the a is the part to remember; in one the n is regular and the rest is the part to remember. Read each in a sentence before alone: "We get one red nut." "All of us can run." Keep there, they, where, of, have and from on the wall and read each once. 4 min: with a finger under each word, read "We get the red bus." twice, then pairs read "One red cat is on the bed." 3 min: dictate bed and bud; each child writes a letter for each sound on side 2.

#### 15 min. Small-group reading

Review: Sam and Pam (day 124). First use of A Hen in a Tub (title adult-read; the body uses taught sounds and mapped words only). 3 min: read it once together with a finger under each word. 8 min: hear the day's six, about 80 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question ("Who is wet?"), one pointing task ("Point to wet"). Name them in the record column. 4 min: circulate while the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards 1 to 6, with no timer shown.

#### 10 min. Response and fresh retry

Review: response and fresh retry (unit 1). Hand-back from day 129's check. 3 min, error S: if more than a third made it, the whole class; others retry alone. Lay out R and r and match them to one name, then a fresh pair, W and w. 3 min, error AN: the puppet says em for m; sort name responses from sound responses, then a fresh consonant, z. 2 min, error AI: name one letter while matching its two cases in a new order, Y and y. 2 min: with you, up to six children who were not through the check finish what was not reached, about 20 seconds each; the others retry from cards in groups. The retry replaces the matching mark on the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Read Tree Care Words, verse 3, aloud; it is first heard today, and you read its rich language. Echo it twice, point to the line that gives the order, and find the rhyme in place and space. Write together "First, we tie one wide, soft strap in place." and mark each space with a finger; find we and one.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Say web; six children say its first, middle and last sound. Unscored; note shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Build the box with two cards, take E130 alone and do each retry once. One projector: show the box as a drawing; the cards are held, not projected.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles or counters: use buttons and paper squares. No mat: draw a box with two arrows.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the retries.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 3, 5 and 7 of Trees Help Our Community.
  **Say.** "Which sentence states the point?" Expect: trees help our community.
- **10 min. Box and arrows.** Draw a box with two arrows. Your child says what goes in the box and a reason for each arrow, pointing to the writer's sentence first.
  **Say.** "What reasons does the writer give?" Expect: shaded places to rest, and shelter for birds.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say net. Your child changes the middle sound to make nut and says the new sound.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X130]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exit and the retries; cut the drawing in the partner part to one arrow. Longer: a secure child finds a second sentence on page 3 that supports the same reason.

### Words and supports

Words: point, reason, because. Frame: "The writer says ____________________. One reason is ____________________." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can put the two stated reasons on the arrows and leave a liking off. Day 131 reads the planting text whole and compares it with the reasons text. If many put a liking or the blue card on an arrow (error C), its Vocabulary and return has children point to the writer's sentence first, then draw the arrow.
