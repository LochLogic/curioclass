# Day 131: How planting is different from why trees matter

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record for day 130: list children who put a liking on an arrow (error C). Pick six for the exit and the step talk, none heard on day 130. Draw two columns and a shared row on chart paper. Make a set of four page-picture cards and a tree card for each pair. Mark page 1 of Planting Together and page 5 of Wangari and the Tree Planters for sheet E131.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

2 min, whole class: say nursery (a place where young trees are grown), mulch (a layer of wood chips) and pot (the big pot on page 1), each with its picture from Planting Together. Review: pot (Sam and Pam). 3 min, hand-back from day 130's exit, error C: if more than ten made it, the whole class; otherwise a small group. Use the reasons text and the box and arrows mat, not E130. The puppet says, "One reason is birds are my favorite." Read page 5. Children point to the writer's sentence, "Trees give birds shelter," first, then set its arrow.

#### 15 min. Read-aloud, the planting text

Whole class on the rug, pictures shown. Read Planting Together, all eight pages (286 words), and pause at each picture to ask, "What do you see?" (about 8 minutes). Then look again at pages 2 to 7, one picture each, and say the time word: first, next, then, after that, last (about 4 minutes). Last, 3 minutes: children hold up a finger for each step as you say it: a good place, the hole, the tree, the soil, the water, the mulch. No digging is done today. The adult digs and children stand back. The text names a class pot or the school grounds, and no question asks about a child's home or yard.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. **Ask.** "What happens first in planting?" Expect: an adult checks for a good place with sunlight and room. Show the pictures of page 3 of this text and page 4 of Trees Help Our Community. **Ask.** "How are the words and pictures different from the reasons text?" Expect: this text tells what people do, in order, and shows people planting; the other tells why trees help and shows a bench and a bird. Partners say it, 20 seconds each, all pairs at once; hear three pairs.

#### 15 min. Compare two texts

Whole class, then pairs. Review: Both ___. But ___. and the two-column chart (day 123), and same and different (unit 1). 7 min: show the chart. Shared row: a tree, both are about trees. Column one, Trees Help Our Community: why, shade and birds. Column two, Planting Together: how, the steps in order. Say it twice yourself, then the class with you: "Both are about trees, but one tells why and one tells how." The puppet says, "Both say the same." Say: "They are about the same topic. They tell different things." That is error E. 8 min, pairs: with the four page-picture cards and the tree card, choose one likeness and one difference, then say "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Expect: both show trees, but one shows a bird and one shows people planting. Hear three pairs. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. A step and its result

Whole class, then pairs. Review: so and because (unit 3). 3 min: show the card so. Say: "The hole is wide, so the roots have room." (page 3) and "The soil is patted, so the tree stays steady." (page 5). **Ask.** "What does the picture on page 3 show about the step?" Expect: the adult digs and the children stand back and watch. 7 min, pairs: each child takes the page 5, 6 or 7 picture and says one step with so, one minute each, for example "The adult spreads mulch, so the soil stays damp." Hear the day's six in turn, about a minute each, and name them in the record column. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 1 of Planting Together and page 5 of Wangari and the Tree Planters aloud again (sheet E131), then ask [[s04-X131v2]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others draw the two texts on side 1. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: says both say the same (error E).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 21, lesson 107: short e and u)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. 2 min: Review: short e and short u (day 130); say net and nut and children show a thumb for the middle sound. 5 min: build pen; change the last letter to make peg; name the sound that changed, "the last sound." 3 min: ending sounds, heard only: say bank and sink; children tap the ending sounds; no printed word. 5 min: Review: we, all and one (day 130) and there, they, where, of, have and from; read each in a sentence before alone: "We all have one bun." "We will get wet." 5 min: read "All of us can run." twice with a finger under each word, then pairs read "All of us can hug Ben."

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: A Hen in a Tub (day 130). 3 min: reread it together with a new question, "How do the children help Peg?" Expect: they rub Peg with a rag. 10 min: hear the day's six, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task. Name them in the record column. 12 min: circulate while the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards 1 to 6, with no timer shown; there are no stand-alone isolated tasks today.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Echo Tree Care Words, verse 3, and point to the line that gives the order. On chart paper write together "Then we watch the top sway and bend." with a space between words. Each child writes pen by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. More reading

Pairs reread A Hen in a Tub a second time for meaning, assignment cards 1 to 6 only. Name the six if any were not heard.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Compare the two texts with the picture cards, take E131 alone and trade a "so" sentence each. One projector: show one picture at a time; draw the chart on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles or counters: use buttons and paper squares. No picture cards: draw four small pictures on paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the step talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Read Planting Together aloud, showing each picture. Do not dig.
  **Say.** "What happens first in planting?" Expect: someone checks for a good place with sun and room.
- **10 min. Two texts.** Show one picture from Trees Help Our Community and one from this text.
  **Say.** "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Expect: both are about trees, but one tells why and one tells how.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say pen. Your child pushes a button for each sound and changes the last sound to make peg.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X131v2]] Read page 1 of Planting Together and page 5 of Wangari and the Tree Planters aloud, then ask for a Both ... but ... sentence about the two pages.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exit and the compare part; cut the second look at the pictures and the step talk to one page. Longer: a secure child says a second so sentence about page 6.

### Words and supports

Words: nursery, mulch, pot, first, next, then, last. Frame: "Both ___. But ___ and ___." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say what two texts share and one difference. Day 132 is a check day: groups of five hear two new pages of Wangari and the Tree Planters and say the point, a reason, and how the pages are alike and different; nothing new is taught. If many said both say the same (error E), say so once; its repair waits for day 133's Return and repair.
