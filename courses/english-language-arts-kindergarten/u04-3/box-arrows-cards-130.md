# Box and arrows mat and cards, day 130

## The mat

A sheet of heavy paper with one large box at the top and two arrows pointing down from it to two small boxes. The teacher also draws it on chart paper for the model.

## The model

The whole help claim from Trees Help Our Community goes in the box; one arrow leads to "shaded places to rest" (page three) and one to "birds shelter" (page five). Say "because" along each arrow.

## The practice card, day 130

Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read it twice: "The rug is soft to sit on. The shelf holds our books. Our reading corner is a good place."

Each pair gets three sentence cards, one for each sentence (the teacher reads each aloud; a small picture on each), and the mat. The pair decides which sentence is the whole claim and puts it in the box, then puts the other two on the arrows, pointing to the sentence the card says before each is set. Right: the point in the box, "Our reading corner is a good place" (it comes last here), and the two other sentences on the arrows. The pair says it with "because": "Our reading corner is a good place because the rug is soft to sit on."

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card.
