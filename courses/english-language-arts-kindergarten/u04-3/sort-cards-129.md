# Detail and point sort cards, day 129

Make five plain picture cards, one picture each, no printed word, from the pages of Trees Help Our Community: a shaded bench; a nest in the fork of a branch; children reading under leaves; a bird sitting in the rain under thick leaves; and the point card, a street with trees, a bench and a bird together in one picture with a ring around the whole.

Children sort the four detail pictures from the point card, then retell two details in order to a partner. The teacher says the point as one sentence, "Trees help our community," from page seven.

These cards are for teaching and may be shown.
