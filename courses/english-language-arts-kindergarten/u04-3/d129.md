# Day 129: The point of a text about trees, and the cycle 20 check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick six for the exit and the retell, none heard on day 128. The check hears every child once, so list all thirty in the record column. Lay one set of the five sort cards for each group of five. Print the three check sheets large (letters N to Z in both cases mixed, the consonants, there they where) and keep them covered. Mark page 6 for sheet E129.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

2 min, whole class: say community, shade and shelter, each with its picture from Trees Help Our Community (the street, the shade under a tree, a bird under thick leaves); children say each word and point. A community is people who live, work or learn near one another. Review: shelter (unit 2), a place that keeps you safe from weather. 3 min, hand-back from day 128, error A: only children who again chose a coat or a hat meet you (up to six; if more than ten, the whole class). Lay the clothing card beside two new invented cards, a girl pouring water on a tree and a boy sweeping leaves from the path. Say aloud: "These cards are made up for practice." Say together: "Both look after a tree place, but one waters and one sweeps." The others draw one thing they would do in the shade.

#### 15 min. Read-aloud, the reasons text

Whole class on the rug, pictures shown. Read Trees Help Our Community, all seven pages (282 words), and pause at each picture to ask, "What do you see?" (about 7 minutes). Then reread pages 1, 3, 5 and 7 slowly (about 5 minutes) and tap the labels on page 2: trunk, bark, branch, canopy. Last, 3 minutes: say "Where is the bench? Where is the nest?" and children point in the air to the page. The text holds birds, nests and baby birds; no animal is harmed. If a child tells about a home or a bird, listen and return to the page. No question asks about a child's home.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Review: the main topic is what the whole text is about, and a detail is one fact under it (units 1 and 2). Say it yourself and write nothing: "The main topic is trees in our community." **Ask.** "What is the main topic?" Expect: trees, or trees in our community. Now the new word: the point is the one sentence the writer wants us to remember about the topic. Read the last line of page 1 and the first line of page 7, "Trees help our community." Tap both: the writer says it first and says it last. Put the sentence on a card. **Ask.** "What point does the writer make about trees?" Expect: trees help our community. Partners say it to each other, 20 seconds each, all pairs at once; hear three pairs, one sentence each. A child may pass.

#### 20 min. Topic, details and the point

Review: topic and detail (unit 1). 4 min, whole class with large cards: hold up the point card, the street with a ring around everything, and say, "This is the whole idea: trees help our community." Hold up the bench picture, "This is one detail, a shady place to rest (page 4)," and set it under the point. 8 min, six groups of five with a set each: one child at a time takes the talking token, picks a card, says which page it is from and "detail" or "point," and sets it. Expect: the street is the point; the bench, the nest, children reading under leaves and the bird in the rain are details. 8 min, pairs: each child retells two details in order, "First ___, then ___," one minute each. Hear the day's six during this, 30 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 6 aloud again (sheet E129), then ask [[s04-X129v2]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others draw one detail on side 1. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Sign: says a detail as the point (error B).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 20, lesson 105: an and am)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. The last lesson of the cycle. 5 min: review the an and am families (days 124 to 127). Build ran; change one letter to make rat, then ram; each time say which sound changed, "the last sound." 5 min: map there, they and where; the th or wh is regular, the rest is the part to remember. Read each from the wall in a sentence before alone: "There is Sam, in the van." "They ran to Dan." "Where is the pan?" Review of, have and from the same way. 5 min: with a finger under each word, read "Pam ran to the van." twice, then pairs read "Pam can pat the cat." 5 min: dictate man and ham; each child writes a letter for each sound on side 2.

#### 30 min. Cycle check F2, in the rotations

One child at a time with you, about 60 seconds each, no help given. Ask in order: [[s04-F129a]] on a mixed sheet of N to Z in both cases, [[s04-F129b]] on the consonants and [[s04-F129c]] on there, they and where. Mark each component alone and record each consonant and each letter case. A child not through in 60 seconds is marked shown with help on what was not reached and finishes in day 130's response. The other children partner-read Sam and Pam with a new question, "Why do Sam and Pam run to the pot?", and use assignment cards 1 to 4. Signs: treats N and n as different letters (error S); gives no letter name (error AI); gives a letter name for a sound (error AN); reads another word for a mapped word (error AP).

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Echo Tree Care Words, verse 2, and point to the line that gives the order. Write together "First, spread the mulch there on the ground." and mark each space with a finger; find there.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Sort the five cards, retell two details, take E129 alone and run the check as one sitting. One projector: show one picture at a time; draw the sort cards on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles or counters: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The sort groups, the check and the reader.

- **10 min. Read.** Read Trees Help Our Community aloud, showing each picture.
  **Say.** "What is the topic?" Expect: trees, or trees in our community.
- **10 min. The point.** Show the street picture, then one detail picture.
  **Say.** "What is the writer's point?" Expect: trees help our community, said as a whole sentence, not just a bench.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say ran. Your child pushes a button for each sound, then changes the last sound to make rat.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X129v2]] Read page 6 of Trees Help Our Community aloud, then ask for the writer's point on that page.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the exit and the check; cut the second reading to pages 3 and 5 and the retell to one detail. Longer: a secure child sorts a fifth detail, a nest with two eggs (page 5).

### Words and supports

Words: community, shade, shelter, point, detail. Frame: "The point is ___. A detail is ___." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say the point as a whole sentence and not a detail. Day 130 rereads the whole reasons text and builds a box with arrows for its two stated reasons. If many said a detail (error B), its Vocabulary and return puts the whole sentence in the box and the detail on an arrow. Day 130 also answers the check: errors S, AN and AI, and finishing for anyone not through.
