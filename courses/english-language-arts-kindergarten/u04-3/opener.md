# Section 4.3 opener: an author's point and reasons, and two texts on one topic

## What the section does

Days 129 to 132 turn to two short informational pages. Trees Help Our Community states a point and two reasons; Planting Together tells the steps of planting a young tree in order. Day 129 finds the topic, details and the point. Day 130 builds a box with arrows for the point and its reasons. Day 131 compares the two pages. Day 132 is a check day: groups of five hear two new cards and name the point, the reasons, and how the cards are alike and different. Foundations finish cycle 20 on day 129 (check) and start cycle 21 on day 130.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and a five-minute exit, or on day 132 the group observation. The foundations block holds sounds, letters, the reader and the rotation. A child outside the six heard is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read the pages; the teacher reads every page and card aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **The point is the whole claim; a reason is a sentence that backs it.** The box holds the point and each arrow leads to a stated reason.
- **A liking is not the writer's reason.** "I like green" is the child's own; the writer's reasons are sentences in the text.
- **Two texts on one topic can say different things.** One tells why, one tells how.
- **A step and its result can be joined with "so".**

## The errors, in plain words

- **B:** a supporting detail given as the point. Put the whole claim in the box and the detail on an arrow.
- **C:** a liking given as the writer's reason. Point to the writer's sentence first.
- **E:** the same topic taken to mean the same information. Place one unique detail beside each text.
- **S, AN:** foundations slips from the day 129 check.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No picture or question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion. No digging is done on day 131.

## Supplies

The two texts in print, four detail pictures and a point card, the box and arrows mat with its cards, the check cards held unseen until day 132, the cycle-20 and cycle-21 readers, letter tiles and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Read from print. Draw the box on chart paper and make the cards from scrap paper. Say each invented card is made up for practice.
