# Exit cards, days 129 to 131

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 129 card E129

**Read:** "Put a stool by the tree. It helps children reach the low branch. It keeps them steady."
**Ask:** "What is the writer's point?" Right: put a stool by the tree. Wrong: a detail given as the point (B).

## Day 130 card E130

**Read:** "Wipe your boots on the mat. It keeps the floor clean. It keeps the mud outside."
**Cards:** a box that already holds the point, two arrows, and four reason cards: It keeps the floor clean; It keeps the mud outside; I like mats; The mat is blue.
**Ask:** "Put the two reasons the writer gives on the arrows." Right: the floor-clean and mud-outside cards. Wrong: I like mats or The mat is blue (C).

## Day 131 card E131

**Read:** "Text A: Worms make tunnels in the soil. Text B: Worms eat dead leaves."
**Ask:** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about Text A and Text B." Right: both are about worms, but A says tunnels and B says dead leaves. Wrong: both say the same (E).
