# Exit sheets, days 129 to 131

Sheets E129 to E131 name pages; they go with a page of the unit's own texts, read aloud again; they are not made up, so say nothing about practice for them. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read; you read the pages aloud. None of these is shown in teaching.

## Day 129 sheet E129: page 6 of Trees Help Our Community

**Read again:** page 6 of Trees Help Our Community (texts/gk-u4-02-a.md), the birds.
**Ask:** "What is the writer's point on this page?" Right: a tree with birds makes our community a lively place. Wrong: we can watch them hop, or we can hear them sing, given as the point (B).

## Day 130 sheet E130: page 6 again, for its reasons

**Read again:** page 6 of Trees Help Our Community.
**Cards:** a box that already holds the point, two arrows, and four reason cards: watch the birds hop; hear the birds sing; I like birds; The bird is brown.
**Ask:** "Which two reasons does the writer give?" Right: the hop and sing cards. Wrong: I like birds or The bird is brown (C).

## Day 131 sheet E131: page 1 of Planting Together and page 5 of Wangari and the Tree Planters

**Read again:** page 1 of Planting Together (texts/gk-u4-02-b.md), then page 5 of Wangari and the Tree Planters (texts/gk-u4-03.md).
**Ask:** "Say a Both ... but ... sentence about the two pages." Right: both tell about a nursery where young trees are grown, but the school tree comes from a nursery in a big pot and the women in Kenya grow seedlings from seeds. Wrong: "They say the same thing", or the big pot given as shared (E).
