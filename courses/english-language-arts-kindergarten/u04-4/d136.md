# Day 136: Actions and their results in the account

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record for day 135: list who pointed to a liking or to the other author's reason, and who named a detail as the topic or retold none. Pick the day's six, none heard on day 135. Write the Return and repair cards from the part below. Mark page 2 for sheet E136. Set the two large cards and the arrow aside for the model.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand-back from day 135's exit and pairs. Whole class for any error more than a third made, otherwise a small group. If many made error C or Y on day 135's exit, say so once. Fresh cards only, never the same text again. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child.

- Errors C and Y, 5 minutes. Card: "Author P: Keep the path clear because walkers can pass. Author Q: Keep the path clear because it is easy to sweep." Error C, 3 minutes: the puppet says, "Author P says I like paths." Children point to Author P's sentence first, then say the reason: walkers can pass. Error Y, 2 minutes: ask "Which reason does Author Q give?" Children find Author Q's sentence, then choose: it is easy to sweep.
- Errors AC and AD, 5 minutes. Review: the topic and its details (day 129). New short text: "Ants live in soil. Ants dig small tunnels. Ants carry crumbs home." Show four subject cards, read aloud: a crumb, ants, a tunnel, a bird. Pairs point to the subject all the details share: ants. The puppet says, "The topic is a crumb." Say: "A crumb is one detail." Then each pair tells two things the text says, such as "Ants dig small tunnels." Hear three pairs.

#### 10 min. Reread, actions and outcomes

Whole class, pictures shown. Review: Wangari and the Tree Planters (days 134 and 135). Read pages 5 to 7 aloud once. Say plainly what the pages tell: the women set up nurseries, planted seeds and watered them, and the seeds grew into seedlings; they planted the seedlings together; more trees grew. The words "as a result" on page 7 join what the women did to what happened. Say: "A result is what happens because of an action." Review: so and result (day 131). Point to the page 8 strip beside the pages.

#### 15 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. **Ask.** "What do people do together?" Expect: the women set up nurseries, grow seedlings and plant them together. Partners say it, 30 seconds each, all pairs at once; hear three pairs. **Ask.** "How does the account connect growing and planting?" Expect: the women grow seedlings, then they plant them. Partners say it, 30 seconds each; hear three pairs. Show the strip and point to each picture as the answer is said: nursery, seedlings in pots, planting. Say the account only tells what happened and compares no place or people. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Draw one action and its result

Whole class, then individuals. Review: an action and its result joined with so (day 131). 2 min: show two large cards joined by an arrow, the cause card first. Card one: the women grow seedlings in a nursery. Card two: the women plant the seedlings together. Say: "First the women grow seedlings. Then they plant them." 2 min: the puppet says, "The planting makes the nursery." Say: "No. The account says the seedlings are grown, then planted. The arrow goes from the action to its result." That is error AE. Say it right together. 6 min: each child draws two small pictures with an arrow between them on side 1, the first action on the left. Hear the day's six while the others draw, 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes: "First ___. Then ___." Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Stated or not

Pairs with the three statement cards. Read each card aloud twice: "The women grew seedlings in nurseries, then planted them together." "The women planted trees, and then the seedlings were grown." "The women planted the seedlings at night." Say: "A stated fact is one a page holds. Some things the account does not say." Each pair points to the page that holds the stated card, pages five and six, and says what the others change. Expect: the second turns the order around; the third adds night, which the account never says. Hear three pairs. The puppet says, "The women planted at night, so it is true." Say: "The account does not say it, so we leave it out." That is error V. Then set the cards beside the page 8 strip, cause first.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Read page 2 aloud again (sheet E136), then ask [[s04-X136v2]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others add a second small picture to their drawing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: being a scientist made her study (error AE); an event the page does not tell (error V).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 22, lesson 112: double final ll, zz, ss, ff)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. 3 min: Review: doubled final letters, bell, fuzz, moss and puff, two letters for one sound (day 135). 5 min: build moss with the tiles; change the first letter to make boss; name the sound that changed, "the first sound, m to b." Pairs build huff and change the first letter to make puff, naming the changed sound. 4 min: Review: but, not and no (day 135), and the earlier mapped words; read each in a sentence before alone: "It is a mess, but it is not bad." 4 min: read "Seth got the lid off." twice with a finger under each word, then pairs read "Tess has a doll." once. 4 min: read "Beth will miss the bus, but Tim will not." twice together.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: Moss on a Log (day 135). 3 min: reread it together with a new question, "Who pats the moss?" Expect: Meg and Gus. 10 min: hear the day's six, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task. Name them in the record column. 12 min: circulate while the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards 1 to 6, with no timer shown; there are no stand-alone isolated tasks today.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Echo Tree Care Words, verse 4, and point to the line that gives the order. On chart paper write together "Then we feel the soil, but not too much." with a space between words. Each child writes boss by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. More reading

Pairs reread Moss on a Log a second time for meaning, assignment cards 1 to 6 only. Name the six if any were not heard.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread pages 5 to 7, draw the two cards and arrow together, take E136 alone and trade one action and its result each. One projector: show one picture at a time; draw the arrow cards on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No statement cards: write three sentences on paper. No tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the Return and repair cards.

- **10 min. Read.** Read pages 5 to 7 of the account aloud.
  **Say.** "How does the account connect growing and planting?" Expect: the women grow seedlings, then they plant them.
- **10 min. Draw.** Draw two boxes with an arrow. Your child draws what the women did first, then what they did next.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say moss. Your child pushes a button for each sound and changes the first sound to make boss.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X136v2]] Read page 2 of Wangari and the Tree Planters aloud, then ask how her studying and her becoming a scientist go together.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the draw, the exit and the sound change; cut the statement cards to the stated card and one other. Longer: a secure child says a second action and its result from page 7, cause first.

### Words and supports

Words: result, connect, together, grow, seedling. Frames: "First ___. Then ___." "The account does not say ___." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say how two actions are connected, cause first. Day 137's Words part takes errors V (a claim the account does not make) and AE (an action and its result reversed), a minute each, with a new pair of actions; the section 4.5 plan must hold that slot. Day 137 then plans a planting-place opinion with the topic and preference box.
