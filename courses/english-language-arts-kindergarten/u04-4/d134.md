# Day 134: The true account of Wangari Maathai

## Teacher page

### Before class

The sensitivity read of this account and the study of the movement's own pages that the frame names must be finished before you teach this day; if either is not, hold the day. Hang the map and the work scene. Pick the day's six, none heard on day 133. Make a purpose card for each child. Keep E134 face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Vocabulary and return

Whole class with the map and pictures. 1 min: Kenya, a country in Africa, found on the map (new); Review: nursery, a place where young trees are grown (day 131); seedling, a very young tree (new), with its picture. Then the hand-back from day 133's exit. If many made error F or C, say so once and use the whole class; otherwise a small group. 2 min, error F: the puppet says, "By the fence." Say: "That names a place. It does not say what you prefer." Together: "I would plant it by the door. I prefer it there." 2 min, error C: read page 3 of Trees Help Our Community, "Trees give us shaded places to rest." Say: "The writer says that." Then model your own: "I say I like the cool air." Children say which is which: "the writer says" or "I say." Review: opinion and the writer says (day 133).

#### 20 min. Read-aloud, the true account

Whole class on the rug, pictures shown. Show the map first and point to Kenya. Say where it is, not how far: "Kenya is a country on the east side of Africa, beside the Indian Ocean. Its capital city is Nairobi." Do not call it poor or far away. Then read Wangari and the Tree Planters, all ten pages (408 words), and pause at each picture to ask, "What do you see?" (about 12 minutes). Say plainly what the account says. Organized means got people together to plan and work. A real woman is named. Page 6 says the women were paid a small amount. Page 9 says she won a prize and died in 2011: say that once, briefly, then read on to what people in Kenya still do. The account names no violence, and it says many women organized and planted, so never say she did it alone. Last, 3 minutes: read page 8 and set its five pictures on a strip in order: women talking beside a dry stream, a meeting circle, a nursery, seedlings in pots, women planting. Say first, next, then, after that, last. Leave the strip on the wall.

#### 10 min. Questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. **Ask.** "Who organizes with whom?" Expect: Wangari Maathai organizes with women across Kenya (page 4). **Ask.** "Which place does the account name?" Expect: Kenya. **Ask.** "Which actions does the account name?" Expect: the women set up nurseries, grow seedlings and plant them together (pages 5 and 6). After each ask, partners say it, 20 seconds each, all pairs at once; hear three pairs. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Group reading with a purpose

Whole class, a purpose card in every hand. A purpose card tells you what to listen for or find before you hear the page. 5 min: the card shows an ear and says "listen for who plants." Read page 6 aloud three times; children join in "side by side" each time. **Ask.** "Who plants?" Expect: the women, together. 5 min: the card shows an eye and says "find who comes to the quiet place." Review: Reading Under the Tree (days 122 to 128); read page 7 aloud once. **Ask.** "Who comes to the quiet place?" Expect: Tomas and Mei.

#### 5 min. Ask what a word means

Whole class, then pairs. Review: ask what a word means (day 123). Say: "I do not know this word, so I ask. What does seedling mean?" Show the picture on page 5. Children say it with you, then answer from the picture and the page: a very young tree. Pairs take turns, one asking "What does nursery mean?" and the other answering from the picture. Hear two pairs.

#### 5 min. Draw and dictate

Individuals. Each child draws the people and the place on side 1. The day's six say "___ plant trees in ___." one at a time, 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes; you write it under the drawing. Expect: women plant trees in Kenya. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The rest add to their drawing.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E134 twice, then ask [[s04-X134]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others finish drawing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: says "I do not know" and asks nothing (error W); tells what a word means and asks nothing (error X).

#### 15 min. Shared lesson (cycle 21, lesson 110: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only)

Whole class with tiles in pairs; the last lesson of the cycle. 2 min: Review: short e and short u (day 130); build bud, change the middle letter to make bed, then the last letter to make beg, naming each changed sound. 3 min: ending sounds, heard only: say pink and rank; children tap the ending sounds; no printed word. 3 min: Review: we, all and one (day 130), and there, they, where, of, have and from; read each in a sentence before alone: "We get the red bus." and "We will get wet." 4 min: read "We get the red bus." twice with a finger under each word, then "We will get wet." once. 3 min: echo Tree Care Words, verse 3, and point to the line that gives the order.

#### 45 min. Cycle check F3 (prompts in s04 field 5), in the rotations

At the teacher table, one child at a time, 90 seconds each: 30 children is 45 minutes. Ask each child, in order, [[s04-F134a]] (blend and split an onset and rime), [[s04-F134b]] (the three sounds of a word), [[s04-F134c]] (add a sound, then swap a sound), [[s04-F134d]] (a short vowel sound to its letter, on the printed row) and [[s04-F134e]] (read three words). Give no help and no feedback during the probe. Mark each component alone: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. A child not finished in 90 seconds is marked shown with help on the part not reached and finishes in day 135's response. The other children partner-read A Hen in a Tub, reread with a new question each time ("Where is Peg?" Expect: in the tub), and use assignment cards 1 to 6, with no timer shown. Signs: a letter name in place of the onset (error AL); the last sound given as the middle (error AM); a sound dropped instead of swapped (error R); a short vowel matched to the wrong letter (error AO); another word read for a mapped word (error AP).

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Read the account with the map at the table, ask the three questions, take E134 alone and ask the child one question back. One projector: show the map and one picture at a time; draw the strip on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: draw Africa on paper and mark the east side. No tiles: skip the sounds.

**Skip.** The purpose cards, the reader and the cycle check.

- **10 min. Map and read.** Point to Kenya on the east side of Africa. Read the account aloud, showing each picture.
  **Say.** "Who organizes with whom?" Expect: Wangari Maathai with women across Kenya.
- **10 min. Talk.** **Say.** "Which actions does the account name?" Expect: the women set up nurseries, grew seedlings and planted them together.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the people and the place and says "___ plant trees in ___."
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X134]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read-aloud, the draw and the exit; cut the purpose cards to the first one and the word question to one pair. Longer: a secure child finds the page that says what the women did together and points to it.

### Words and supports

Words: Kenya, nursery, seedling, organize, question. Frames: "What does ___ mean?" "___ plant trees in ___." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. The map in large print for a child who needs it. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can ask a question about a detail. Day 135's Return and repair takes errors W and X in five minutes with a new word card and a new who question. The cycle check marks go to day 135's Response and fresh retry: errors AL, AM, R, AO and AP, each with a fresh case. Day 135 returns to pages 3 to 6 for the stated reasons.
