# Day 135: Return to the account for stated reasons

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record for day 134: list who asked a question about a detail and who did not, and who needs a retry on each part of the cycle check. Pick the day's six, none heard on day 133. Write the five sentence cards and four subject cards from the parts below. Keep E135 face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back from day 134's exit, errors W and X; one small group each, 2 minutes apiece, fresh cards only. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Error W: show a word card, a picture of a canopy. The puppet says, "I do not know." Say: "When I do not know a word, I ask." Children ask "What does canopy mean?" and answer from the picture: the leafy top of a tree (Trees Help Our Community, page 2). Error X: read "Neighbors stop and talk in the cool shade." Say: "Turn it into a who question." Together: "Who stops and talks in the cool shade?" Expect: neighbors. Review: ask what a word means (day 123); question and statement (unit 1).

#### 10 min. Reread, the organizing and planting passage

Whole class, pictures shown. Review: Wangari and the Tree Planters (day 134). Read pages 3 to 6 aloud once, then reread the actor names and the "she" and "they" sentences with their pictures: "she" is Wangari Maathai (page 4) and "they" is the women (page 5). Then read page 7 of Trees Help Our Community. **Ask.** "What concerns are heard?" Expect: streams drying up, less food, a longer walk for firewood (page 3). **Ask.** "Which reason does each source state?" Expect: one stated reason from each source, pointed to on its page, such as "Trees hold the soil in place" (account, page 4) and "They give birds shelter" (reasons text, page 7). Partners say each answer, 20 seconds each, all pairs at once; hear three pairs.

#### 15 min. Main topic and key details

Pairs, then the day's six. Review: the main topic and its details (day 129). 2 min: reread pages 5 and 6. 4 min: show four subject cards, read aloud: a dry stream, a prize, planting trees, a school. Pairs point to the subject all the details share. Expect: planting trees. The puppet says, "The topic is a prize." Say: "A prize is one detail. The topic is what every detail is about." That is error AC. 7 min: each pair tells two things the text says, such as "The women set up nurseries" and "They planted the seedlings together." Hear the day's six, about 60 seconds each, and name them in the record column. A child who tells no detail (error AD) points to a picture and says what it shows. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Stated reasons

Pairs with the box and arrows mat. Review: the box with arrows and the writer says (days 130 and 133). 3 min: put "Trees can help with these concerns" (page 4) in the box. Show five cards, read aloud: "Trees hold the soil in place." "They catch rainwater." "They give food and firewood." "I like to hug a tree." "They give birds shelter." The puppet sets "I like to hug a tree" on an arrow. Say: "That is something I like. The writer does not say it." That is error C. 8 min: each pair points to the sentence on page 4, then sets two stated reasons on the arrows and says "The writer says ___." The bird card is stated, but not on page 4: set it aside. 4 min: three pairs show their box. Expect: any two of the three page 4 sentences.

#### 10 min. Pictures and words

Pairs, five picture cards of page 8 in a shuffled row. Review: a picture and the words that tell it (day 121). Read page 8 one piece at a time: "First, women shared their concerns." "Next, Wangari Maathai organized with them," "and they set up nurseries." "Then the seeds grew into seedlings." "After that, the women planted the seedlings together." After each piece, pairs point to the picture that tells it. Then each pair says what one picture shows. Expect: for example, women kneel in rows of small trees in pots. Check the order against the strip on the wall.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E135 twice and show the three reason cards, then ask [[s04-X135]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others set one arrow in their box. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: points to the liking (error C); points to the other author's reason (error Y).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 22, lesson 111: double final ll, zz, ss, ff)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. 3 min: Review: the short vowels and changing one sound (days 130 to 133). 6 min: show bell, fuzz, moss and puff on cards and say each. The two letters at the end spell one sound. Review: will, whose double l is one sound (day 112). Point to each word and say its sounds, then tap them: fuzz is /f/ /u/ /z/, three sounds and four letters. 4 min: build fuzz with the tiles; change the first letter to make buzz; name the sound that changed, "the first sound, f to b." Pairs build moss and change the first letter to make boss. 4 min: map but, not and no on the wall. In but and not every letter says its sound. In no, n is regular and the o says its own name, the part to remember. Read each in a sentence before alone: "Tess is not at the shop, but Tim is." 3 min: read "Gus will huff." twice with a finger under each word, then pairs read it once.

#### 15 min. Small-group reading

Moss on a Log, first use; the title is read to the children. 2 min: read page 1 together and talk about the picture. 8 min: hear the day's six, about 80 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task. Name them in the record column. 5 min: circulate while the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards 1 to 6, with no timer shown. Cards 7 and 8 are replaced by the response below.

#### 10 min. Response and fresh retry

Hand-back from day 134's assessment, the cycle check F3. Whole class repeats the repair for any error more than a third made; the others retry one at a time with a fresh case, about 20 seconds a child; a retry replaces the matching mark. Error AL, 2 minutes: say /h/ stretched into /en/, then hen; children stretch the first sound into the rest, never a letter name. Error AM, 2 minutes: say bed; three counters; point to the middle one, /e/. Error R, 2 minutes: change /b/ in bug to /r/ by exchanging a counter; then /j/ in jet to /p/. Error AO, 2 minutes: the letters o, u, e, a, i in a shuffled row; say each short sound and children point to its letter. Error AP, 1 minute: read one, we, all in a new order. If more retries are needed than fit, hear the children who made the error first and mark the rest not yet seen; do not squeeze.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Read Tree Care Words, verse 4, to the children; it is first heard today and you read its rich language. Echo the first two lines with a finger under each word. On chart paper write together "First, we visit the tree with our class." with a space between words, marking each space with a finger.

#### 5 min. Foundations check

Say toss; six children say its first, middle and last sound, about 50 seconds each. Unscored.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread pages 3 to 6, set the two reasons in the box, take E135 alone and trade one reason each. One projector: show one picture at a time; draw the box on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No mat: draw a box and two arrows on paper. No tiles: use buttons and paper squares.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the cycle repairs.

- **10 min. Read.** Read pages 3 to 6 of the account aloud.
  **Say.** "What concerns are heard?" Expect: streams drying up, less food, a longer walk for firewood.
- **10 min. Reasons.** Draw a box and two arrows. Read page 4. Your child points to two sentences that say how trees help.
  **Say.** "What does the writer say trees do?" Expect: hold the soil, catch rainwater, give food and firewood.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say fuzz. Your child pushes a button for each sound and changes the first sound to make buzz.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X135]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the stated reasons, the exit and the sound change; cut the picture match to three pictures. Longer: a secure child finds a stated reason in each of the two texts and says which text it is from.

### Words and supports

Words: concern, reason, topic, nursery, canopy. Frames: "The writer says ___." "Tell two things the text says." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can point to the reason a named author gives. Day 136's Return and repair takes errors C and Y in five minutes with new authors, and errors AC and AD from today's pairs in five more with a new short text and two new details. Day 136 then rereads pages 5 to 7 for what people do and what happens next.
