# Day 133: An opinion about where to plant

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the day's six, none of them heard on day 132's check. On chart paper draw the opinion box from unit 1: a picture box with a line under it. Write the six cards for Return and repair from the part below. Keep E133 face down.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand-back from day 132's assessment (the check C2) and day 131's exit. Whole class for any error more than a third made, otherwise a small group on the rug while the rest quietly reread Trees Help Our Community by its pictures. Fresh cards only; the check pages are not used in the repair. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice. The puppet says each wrong example, never a child. Review: point, reason and the box with arrows (day 130).

- Error B, 2 minutes. Card: "Use the blue bin. It holds the scrap paper. It keeps the floor clear." The puppet says, "The point is it keeps the floor clear." Say: "That is one reason. The point is the whole claim." Children point to the sentence that is the whole claim and you put it in the box; the other two go on arrows.
- Error C, 2 minutes. Card: "Wash your hands before lunch. Soap lifts the dirt. Water rinses it away." The puppet says, "One reason is I like the soap smell." Children point to the writer's sentence first, "Soap lifts the dirt," then set its arrow. A liking is the puppet's own, not the writer's.
- Error E, 4 minutes, two pairs. Pair one (day 131's group): "A hook keeps your coat off the floor." and "To hang a coat, put the loop over the hook." Pair two (day 132's group): "A seed packet shows a picture of the plant." and "A seed packet says how deep to plant the seed." The puppet says, "Both say the same." Say: "Both are about one thing, but each says something the other does not." Each group says it with "Both ___. But ___."

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class, pictures shown. Review: Trees Help Our Community (day 129). Read pages 3 and 4 aloud once. **Ask.** "What place could receive shade?" Expect: under the tree, such as the bench on page 4. Partners say it, 20 seconds each, all pairs at once. **Ask.** "Which stated reason might help our choice?" Expect: "Trees give us shaded places to rest" (page 3). Hear three pairs. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Model an opinion about where to plant

Whole class. Show the opinion box from unit 1 (a topic and "I prefer"). Review: prefer and opinion (unit 1); pot (day 131). Two choices only: the class pot or the school grounds. Say: "Our class could plant a tree in the class pot or on the school grounds." Draw each choice in a small box. Model: "I would plant our tree on the school grounds. I prefer it there." Add a reason the text states, labeled: "The writer says trees give birds shelter" (Trees Help Our Community, page 5; point to the sentence). Add a reason of your own, labeled: "My own reason: I like to look at it from our door." Children echo the frame with you: "I would plant it ___. I prefer it there." The reason is optional. The puppet says, "A pot." Say: "That names a place. It does not say what you prefer." That is error F. Say it right together: "I would plant it in the class pot. I prefer it there."

#### 25 min. Draw and dictate the opinion

Individuals. Each child draws the place chosen, the class pot or the school grounds, in the box on side 1. Scribe the day's six, about 3 minutes each (18 minutes): the child says "I would plant it ___. I prefer it there." and you write it under the drawing. The rest write the letters they know and copy the frame. Any child may pass or point to a picture instead. If a child gives a reason, ask "Is that the writer's, or yours?" and write "the writer says" or "I say" with it. Look for: the place is named (class pot or school grounds); a preference is stated; any reason is marked as the writer's or the child's own. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Collect the pages at the end of the day.

#### 5 min. Exit

Individuals, the day's six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read E133 twice, then ask [[s04-X133]], 30 seconds each, about 3 minutes. The others add a detail to their drawing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Signs: a place with no preference (error F); a liking said as the writer's reason (error C).

#### 20 min. Awareness and sound-spelling (cycle 21, lesson 109: short e and u; -ank and -ink heard only)

Whole class with tiles in pairs. 3 min: Review: short e and short u (day 130); say beg and bug and children show a thumb for the middle sound, e or u. 6 min: build jet with the tiles j, e, t, saying /j/ /e/ /t/; change the middle letter to make jut; name the sound that changed, "the middle sound, e to u." Pairs build bed and change one letter to make bud, then peg to pug, each time naming the changed sound. 3 min: ending sounds, heard only: say tank and wink; children tap the ending sounds; no printed word. 4 min: Review: we, all and one (day 130), and there, they, where, of, have and from; read each in a sentence before alone: "We get one red nut." and "One red cat is on the bed." 4 min: read "One pet is in the bed." twice with a finger under each word, then pairs read it once.

#### 25 min. Small-group reading

Review: A Hen in a Tub (day 130). 3 min: reread it together with a new question, "What is red?" Expect: the tub. 10 min: hear the day's six, about 100 seconds each: one sentence read aloud, one meaning question, one pointing task. Name them in the record column. 12 min: circulate while the other four groups of six partner-read and use assignment cards 1 to 6, with no timer shown; there are no stand-alone isolated tasks today.

#### 10 min. Shared print and writing

Whole class. Echo Tree Care Words, verse 3, and point to the line that gives the order. On chart paper write together "It holds the tree, but gives all its branches space." with a space between words. Each child writes jut by its sounds on side 2.

#### 5 min. More reading

Pairs reread A Hen in a Tub a second time for meaning, assignment cards 1 to 6 only. Name the six if any were not heard.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Do the model with the two choices, then the exit alone, and trade one plan each. One projector: show one picture at a time; draw the opinion box on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles or counters: use buttons and paper squares. No opinion box: draw a box on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the Return and repair cards.

- **10 min. Read.** Read pages 3 and 4 of Trees Help Our Community aloud.
  **Say.** "What place could receive shade?" Expect: under the tree, such as the bench on page 4.
- **10 min. Choose.** Draw two boxes: the class pot and the school grounds. Say "I would plant it ___. I prefer it there." Your child says it and draws the place.
  **Say.** "Where would you plant it, and what do you prefer?" Expect: a named place and "I prefer it there."
- **10 min. Sounds.** Say jet. Your child pushes a button for each sound and changes the middle sound to make jut.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read twice, then ask [[s04-X133]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the exit and the sound change; cut the second reread question and the second pair of Return and repair cards. Longer: a secure child says where else the writer's reason about shade would suit, with "the writer says".

### Words and supports

Words: opinion, prefer, choice, the writer says. Frame: "I would plant it ___. I prefer it there." Pointing, drawing, a home language and a communication aid all count. Read the exit card twice, with no added hint. Wrong examples are said by the puppet, never by a child.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who named a place with a preference. Day 134's Vocabulary and return takes errors F (a place with no preference) and C (a liking said as the writer's reason), one repair each, in one five-minute round with the map words. Day 134 then reads the true account of Wangari Maathai whole; nothing else carries.
