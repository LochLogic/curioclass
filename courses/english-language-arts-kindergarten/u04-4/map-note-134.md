# Kenya map and work scene note, day 134

Draw two original pictures, plain and respectful, in thick black line on white paper, large enough to see from the back of the room.

- **The map.** A simple outline of Africa with Kenya coloured in on the east side beside the Indian Ocean (label it), and a dot for Nairobi. No people shown. Print only the three labels: Africa, Kenya, Indian Ocean, and Nairobi by the dot.
- **The work scene.** Women in everyday work clothes and sun hats, kneeling in rows of small potted seedlings at an outdoor nursery, planting and watering. Show the women as active adults in ordinary clothes; show no costume, no stereotype and no one in distress.

Show the map first, before the reading, and point to Kenya. These are for teaching and may be shown.
