# Statement cards, day 136

Three statement cards for pairs, read aloud by the teacher, about the true account of Wangari Maathai. Children point to the page that holds the stated one and say what the others change.

- Stated: "The women grew seedlings in nurseries, then planted them together." (pages five and six)
- Reversed (error AE): "The women planted trees, and then the seedlings were grown."
- Not in the text (error V): "The women planted the seedlings at night."

Print one statement on each card. Children do not read them; the teacher reads each aloud twice. Show the cause-first strip from page eight beside them.

The cards are for teaching and may be shown. They do not repeat any exit or end card.
