# The three request cards

Three cards for the wall and for puppets and pairs, drawn by hand on card stock, large enough to see from across the room. Each card has a small picture, a few words and a hand sign. You say the words; children say them back with the sign. Nobody reads them.

- **Card 1: "Tell me about a detail."** Picture: a finger pointing at one small thing in a big picture. Hand sign: point with one finger, then open the hand.
- **Card 2: "What does ___ mean?"** Picture: a word card with a blank, and a question mark above it. Hand sign: touch the picture of the word, then lift both hands, palms up.
- **Card 3: "Can you say that again?"** Picture: two speech curls, one after the other. Hand sign: make a small circle with one finger beside the ear.

## How to use them

Day 35: the teacher shows card 3 once with the puppet. Day 37: card 2 for a word the page uses. Day 38: card 3 again when a speaker mumbles a word. Later days: a pair may pick a card and use it. Wrong examples are said by a puppet, never a child: the puppet is told "Pick up the red one" and says nothing; then it uses the card and says "Can you say that again?"

## Key (teacher)

A good use: the child says the words of a card, or points to it, at the moment something is not clear. A child may use a home language or only the hand sign.
