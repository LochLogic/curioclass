# Day 35: Weather Changes, and what a page is mostly about

## Teacher page

### Before class

Post the three request cards and write the question "How does the weather change what people wear and do?" on chart paper. Lay out the topic card and the two detail cards face down. Draw the exit cards C35 P36 C37 S38 and keep them out of sight; today uses C35 and its picture row. Today's six are the next two unheard from each foundations group on the record sheet. Pip hides behind the token.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook

Whole class. Day 34 said we would look at today's weather: look out the window in silence and say one thing you notice (1 minute). Show the first picture of Weather Changes, a yard in sun and the same yard in rain. Read page 1 aloud. Post the question and say it twice; the class says it back. Children point to what is different between the two halves. No one is asked about their own clothes or what they have at home.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Pairs. Review: The Magic Bow (day 23, a story from unit 1). Say: "Tell your partner one thing the story said." Each child speaks for about 2 minutes, then swaps. You listen at five pairs, 20 seconds each, and mark nothing aloud. Expect a detail the story gave: the bow, the woman, a wish, what she said. Mark on the record sheet: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A detail that is not in the story is error A, repaired in the model below. Say: "Today's next step is to ask and answer about a detail."

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Say each word, the class says it and does the gesture, and you point to its picture on the wall. Weather: sweep one arm toward the window. Observe: both hands make binoculars. Notice: point with one finger. Detail: pinch two fingers, one small piece (review: detail, in kindergarten, day 9). Sun and wind are named by gesture only. Frame: "I noticed ___."

#### 14 min. Reading 01a

Whole class. Read Weather Changes whole, pages 1 to 8, showing each picture and not stopping (about 4 minutes). Then reread pages 1 to 4, one question a page, and the children point to the picture detail while you say the page's matching words (about 10 minutes).

**Ask.** Page 1: "Which side of the picture is rainy?" Expect: a point to the right side. Page 2: "What do the children wear on a sunny day?" Expect: sun hats. Page 3: "Where do some children play on a rainy day?" Expect: inside, in the doorway. Page 4: "What does a weather observer do?" Expect: looks at the sky, listens, feels the air, writes.

#### 8 min. Model: ask and answer, and the main topic

Whole class, then Pip. First, the question (4 minutes). Ask about page 3: "What do the children wear on the rainy day?" Point to the boots and umbrellas and read the words: "Children wear boots and open umbrellas." Then Pip answers and says something that is not on the page: "Scarves." Say: "It says boots and umbrellas. I can point to it." (error A beside the right way). Then you ask Pip a long question very fast. Pip says nothing (error AT). Show card 3, "Can you say that again?" with its hand sign, and ask again slowly. Pip answers. The class makes the sign.

Second, the topic (4 minutes). Review: topic and detail (day 9). Turn the cards over: the topic card "weather" goes on top, the two detail cards under it. Say: "The topic is what the whole page is mostly about. A detail is one thing it says." Pip says "This page is about boots" (error B). Say: "Boots is one detail. The whole page is mostly about weather." Pip tries again and the class helps.

#### 8 min. Partners

Pairs, with the token. Review: the talk rules, look, listen, wait, take a turn (day 1). Name page 2 for the first turn and page 3 for the second. One child holds card 1, "Tell me about a detail." The other points to one detail in the picture and says "I noticed ___." Then swap. Each turn is about 3 minutes. Add: after the answer, the first child says "Tell me more" (review: unit 1, day 22). You listen at six pairs, 30 seconds each, among 15 pairs. Signs: both talking at once (error AR); an answer and then silence (error AS). If many, practise the token with puppets for 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Science bridge

Whole class. If the science weather picture record is on the wall, read it together: point to each picture and say a weather word. If not, look at page 1 again and name two weather words. Weather is words and pictures here, never numbers.

#### 6 min. Draw a detail

Alone, then partners. Each child draws one detail from page 2 or page 3 in the box on the student page (4 minutes), then tells a partner "I noticed ___" (2 minutes). A child may dictate a label to you.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Six children at the heard table, 40 seconds each (6 times 40 is 4 minutes). Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card C35 twice with its picture. Ask each child item a ([[s02-X35a]]), then item b ([[s02-X35b]]). A child may point to a picture or answer in a home language; say the English back once. Signs: a feather or a boat named as the topic (error B); the sun or a flag for what wind moves (error A). Mark each look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The other children finish their drawing and add a label with a letter they know.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Review: pointing under each word, left to right. Read page 1 of the verse Hot Sun twice, pointing, and the class joins in. Find the first word and name its first letter in both cases, T and t (review: letter t, day 4). Find the word the and read it whole (review: the, day 21).

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Meet the letters s and v. Show the sun card: "What sound do you hear first in sun? /s/." The letter is s, called ess; skywrite it with the whole arm while saying sun. Children give two more words that start with /s/ (sock, sit); you circle the s on the chart. Show the van card: say /v/ with the top teeth on the lower lip, a buzz like a motor. The letter is v, called vee; skywrite it. Words: van, vest. Error V: a child says /w/; show the teeth on the lip. Then a new chant: say each letter, its keyword and its sound, for a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s and v (apple, top, hat, parrot, nest, cow, moon, rake, sun, van), pointing to the cards. Add s and v to the sound-spelling map. Keywords remind; they never help guess a word.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute (10 children, 10 minutes). Table: say the first sound of sun, sit, van, vet; review the cards a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r. Writing place: skywrite v and s, then trace them on paper along the arrow path, saying the sound. Sound and verse place: sort the picture cards by first sound (sun, van, sock, vest, hat, pan: s, v or neither); find s in the verse with the pointer. The verse has no v, so the search ends: v is on the van and vest cards instead. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; read the weather page, ask one question a page and let the child point; do the exit card at the end. One projector: show one picture page at a time; children draw on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No weather page: use the sentences you say. No cards: draw a sun and a van.

**Skip.** The partners, the groups and the verse.

- **5 min. Look.** Look out a window together.
  **Say.** "What do you notice?" Expect: any detail of the sky, trees or ground.
- **5 min. A page.** Say: "On a rainy day children wear boots. They open umbrellas." Your child draws it.
  **Say.** "What do the children wear?" Expect: boots, umbrellas.
- **5 min. Topic.** Say: "The page is mostly about the weather."
  **Say.** "Is boots the topic, or one detail?" Expect: one detail.
- **5 min. Say again.** Say a long question fast; your child asks "Can you say that again?"
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say sun and van; your child says the first sound of each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X35a]] [[s02-X35b]] Say "Wind can push things. Wind spins a pinwheel. Wind lifts a feather. Wind pushes a sailboat." Ask the two questions.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the science bridge and the second partner turn; keep the model, the exit and the new letters. Longer: a child who is secure finds a second detail on the same page and asks a partner about it.

### Words and supports

Words: weather, observe, notice, detail, topic. Frames: "I noticed ___." "Tell me about a detail." "Can you say that again?" A pointed picture, a gesture or a home-language word is an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 rereads Weather Changes for naming words and action words. Its first 5 minutes repair what this exit shows: the topic picture on top with detail pictures under it for error B, or the page's own words for error A.
