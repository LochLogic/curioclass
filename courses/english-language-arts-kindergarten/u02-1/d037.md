# Day 37: Warmer and cooler, and asking about a word

## Teacher page

### Before class

Post the three request cards. Hang the picture cards for the four words on the wall, and keep the snug card in your pocket. Put the story card ST37 face down. Draw the exit cards C35 P36 C37 S38 and keep them out of sight; today uses C37. Today's six are the next two unheard from each foundations group.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair day 36's exit for the more common of error AE, error F and error E. For error AE: hold up the card for drops and the card for drip, and say "Drops drip." Pip says "Drip." and the class adds who or what. For error F: Pip says "It is nice" about a picture; the class says what the picture names and what it does. For error E: say a sentence in a whisper and then in a clear voice; the class chooses the clear one. Then show a picture card, the bird on a branch (say it is made up for practice), and three children each say one sentence about it.

#### 6 min. Words

Whole class. Say each word, the class says it with its gesture, and you point to its picture. Temperature: how warm or cool something feels, with no numbers; hold a hand near a cup. Warmer: warm one hand with the other. Cooler: lay the same hand on the cool table. Sunlight: open both hands like rays. Frame: "The air is ___."

#### 9 min. Reading 01b

Whole class. Read Warmer and Cooler whole, pages 1 to 4, showing each picture and not stopping (about 2 minutes). Then ask the two questions, each with a partner turn first.

**Ask.** "What is the air like at noon?" Expect: warmer; people wear light shirts. **Ask.** "What is the air like when the sun goes down?" Expect: cooler; people pull on sweaters.

#### 15 min. Reread and ask about a word

Whole class. Reread pages 2 and 3 (3 minutes). Review: you asked "What does ___ mean?" on day 4. First, a word the page explains (4 minutes). Pip asks "What does cooler mean?" Point to the words "Cooler means less warm" and read them. Second, a word the page does not explain (5 minutes). Say "snug" from page 3. Pip says "Mm-hm" and does nothing (error AT). Show card 2, "What does ___ mean?" with its hand sign: "What does snug mean?" Then show three ways to find out: look at the picture (the girl with a sweater, going inside), ask a person, ask me. Hold up the snug card. Say: "Snug means warm and cozy." Pip then asks "Is the girl happy?" about the word (error AB, a story question). Say: "That asks about the girl. A word question points at the word first." Third, the three cards (3 minutes). Pip holds up each card and the class says its words with the sign: "Tell me about a detail." "What does ___ mean?" "Can you say that again?"

#### 4 min. A short story card (RL.K.1)

Whole class, then pairs. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read story card ST37 twice with its picture: "Ravi's hat blows away. He runs and catches it." Each pair asks one detail question, who or what, and points to the part of the picture that answers (2 minutes). Two pairs share (1 minute). Expect: "What blows away?" with a point to the hat; "Who runs?" with a point to Ravi.

#### 16 min. Partners

Pairs, with the token. Review: the talk rules, look, listen, wait, take a turn (day 1). First round (6 minutes): one child asks about a detail on page 2 or page 3, using card 1; the other points to the picture and answers; swap. Second round (4 minutes): each child asks the partner "What does ___ mean?" about warmer, cooler or sunlight, using the pair's picture card; the partner finds the answer in the picture or the words. Third round (4 minutes): one child says the sentence "Sunlight warms the air" very quietly; the other uses card 3, "Can you say that again?" with its sign; the first says it clearly; swap. Gather and share (2 minutes). You listen at six of the 15 pairs, 40 seconds each. Signs: a question about the girl instead of a word (error AB); an answer and then silence (error AS); no request for the quiet sentence to be said again (error AT).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Six children at the heard table, 40 seconds each (6 times 40 is 4 minutes). Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card C37 twice with its picture. Ask each child ([[s02-X37]]). A child with no words may point to the cloak in the card's picture. Signs: a question about the story, not a word (error AB). Mark each look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others draw a weather word they like and label it with a letter they know.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Show the mouth card. Say /s/: tongue behind the teeth, air pushing out. Say /v/: top teeth on the lower lip, a buzz. The class copies and feels the buzz at the throat. Read page 1 of the verse Hot Sun twice, pointing under each word. Point to each s and say /s/. The page has no v, so the search ends.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. A mystery word. Read page 2 of Hot Sun twice, pointing: "Sam's sunhat is big and red. He puts it on his head." Give clues: the word names a boy; it starts with /h/, like hat; it is the first word of the second line. Children find it. Show the word card he and map it: h says /h/ (the h card from day 14), and e is the part to remember here. Read he whole by sight. Children find he on the verse and underline it with a finger. Say the sentence with the class. Keep to one new word.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practiced ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute (10 minutes). Table: say /s/ /a/ /t/ and sweep a finger under three dots to blend sat; blend van and vat with counters, then say the last sound of each. Error Q: a child cannot join the sounds; sweep the finger while saying them. Writing place: trace he on a card (only h is written from memory); write v and s from memory. Sound and verse place: find he on the verse; blend and say two spoken words with a partner and counters. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; ask about cooler and snug, do the story card, then the exit card. One projector: show each picture page and card one at a time; children point on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw a sweater and a sun. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The pairs, the groups and the token.

- **5 min. Words.** Warm one hand with the other: warmer. Lay it on a cool table: cooler.
  **Say.** "What does cooler mean?" Expect: less warm.
- **5 min. A new word.** Say: "At night you go inside where it is snug."
  **Say.** "What can you ask about a word you do not know?" Expect: "What does snug mean?" Then say: warm and cozy.
- **5 min. Story.** Say: "Ravi's hat blows away. He runs and catches it."
  **Say.** "What blows away?" Expect: his hat.
- **5 min. Say again.** Say a sentence in a whisper; your child asks "Can you say that again?"
- **5 min. Word he.** Write he; say "h says /h/; remember the e." Your child reads it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X37]] Say "Mina pulls on a cloak. The cloak keeps the wind off." Ask: "Ask me a question about the word cloak."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the story card and the second partner round; keep the word questions, the exit and the mystery word. Longer: a child who is secure asks a question about a word on page 1, such as temperature.

### Words and supports

Words: temperature, warmer, cooler, sunlight, snug. Frames: "What does ___ mean?" "Can you say that again?" "The air is ___." A touch on the picture card, a gesture or a home-language word is an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 uses the same pages to describe weather at two times. Its first 5 minutes repair what this exit shows: point to the word first, then ask about it, for error AB.
