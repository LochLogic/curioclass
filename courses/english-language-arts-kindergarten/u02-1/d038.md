# Day 38: The weather at two times

## Teacher page

### Before class

At the start of the school day make a quick sketch of the window: sky, trees, ground. Post the three request cards. Draw the exit cards C35 P36 C37 S38 and keep them out of sight; today uses S38. Draw the Pia strip: Pia on a porch step under a bright sky with a cup; then dark clouds and Pia at the door with the cup. Today's six are the next two unheard from each group.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair day 37's exit for error AB. Pip points to the picture card for scarf and asks "Is the girl happy?" (error AB, a question about the girl, not the word). Say: "A word question points at the word first." Pip tries again: "What does scarf mean?" Then three children each ask a fresh "What does ___ mean?" about the scarf card and a partner answers from the picture.

#### 8 min. Reread 01b

Whole class, then pairs. Reread pages 2 and 3 of Warmer and Cooler, noon and when the sun goes down (3 minutes). Children listen for what changes between the two times and point to the picture detail.

**Ask.** "What changes between noon and when the sun goes down?" Expect: the air goes from warmer to cooler; light shirts become sweaters and scarves. Partners tell each other first (3 minutes).

#### 8 min. Two times

Whole class. Look at the window now and say what the sky and trees look like. Show your quick sketch from the start of the day: "At the start of the day it looked like this." If nothing has changed, say "This scene is made up for practice" and show scene 1 with the vane turning, then the same roof with the vane still: the wind stopped. Children name one weather word for each time (windy; not windy). Nobody goes outside and nobody is asked what they wear.

#### 10 min. Draw two times

Alone. On the student page each child draws the first time in box 1 and the later time in box 2. A child may ask you to write one word below a box; you write it large (about 8 children at 15 seconds each is 2 minutes). Collect nothing; the page stays with the child as practice.

#### 12 min. Describe with a drawing

Pairs, with the token. First child (5 minutes): holds the drawing and says what the weather was at the first time and at the later time, in a clear voice. The partner asks one question with card 1, "Tell me about a detail," and then uses card 3, "Can you say that again?" once on any part that was quiet or unclear. Then swap. Gather two pairs to share (2 minutes). You listen at six of the 15 pairs, 40 seconds each. Signs: only one picture described (error F); a thing named and no change told (error I).

#### 12 min. Questions about both kinds of text

Whole class, then pairs. First, information (3 minutes). **Ask.** "What do people wear when the sun goes down?" Expect: sweaters, scarves; children point to page 3. Second, a story (9 minutes). Say: "This strip is made up for practice." Show the Pia strip and tell it: "Pia sits on the porch. Her cup is beside her. The clouds come. Pia carries the thing inside." Pairs ask one detail question each way and point to the picture (3 minutes). Then say "Pia carried the thing inside" again and the class uses card 3 to ask which thing; reply "the cup" (2 minutes). Then **Ask.** "Where does Pia sit at the start?" Expect: on the porch step (2 minutes). Say again what you learned: you can ask about a detail, and you can ask for a repair when something is not clear (2 minutes).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Six children at the heard table, 40 seconds each (6 times 40 is 4 minutes). Say: "This strip is made up for practice." Show strip S38. Ask each child item a ([[s02-X38a]]), then item b ([[s02-X38b]]). A child may point and say one word; say the English back once. Signs: the thing named and the change missed (error I); one picture only (error F); nothing said, or "OK" to what was not clear (error AT). Mark each look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others add a word to box 2 with a letter they know.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. The chant of the ten taught letters: for each card say the letter, its keyword and its sound, pointing: a, t, h, p, n, c, m, r, s, v (apple, top, hat, parrot, nest, cow, moon, rake, sun, van). Go once slowly and once faster.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Feel the beats. Review: you tapped the beats of toy names in kindergarten (day 18). Read page 1 of the verse Hot Sun twice and tap the beat of each line (3 minutes). Clap the beats of the picture cards: sunny (2), weather (2), observer (3), puddle (2); show one finger for each beat (5 minutes). Blend two-beat words: say "sun" then "hat" and the class says sunhat (3 minutes). Segment: say puddle and the class claps and says each part, pud and dle (3 minutes). Partners try one each (1 minute). Error L: a child counts sounds when asked for beats; tap the table for beats first, sounds come later.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute (10 minutes). Table: segment van, sat and sit, one counter for each sound heard; map the second mapped word we beside he: w says /w/, e is the part to remember here, and the word is read whole. Error R: a child pushes two counters for van; say the sounds slowly and push one for each. Writing place: trace we on a card (w is taught later); write v and s from memory. Sound and verse place: clap the beats of three picture cards, then place counters for the sounds of one. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; look at the window and draw two times, then tell the Pia strip and ask one detail question, then do the exit strip. One projector: show each page and strip one at a time; children draw on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw a van, a sun and a puddle. No counters: use buttons.

**Skip.** The pairs, the strip and the groups.

- **5 min. Two times.** Look out a window together.
  **Say.** "What did the sky look like earlier? What changed?" Expect: a difference between then and now.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the sky earlier and now in two boxes.
- **5 min. Say it clearly.** Your child tells you the two pictures. If it was quiet, say "Can you say that again?"
- **5 min. Beats.** Clap the word weather (2 beats), then observer (3 beats).
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say van; your child pushes one button for each sound (3).
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X38a]] [[s02-X38b]] Draw a goat on a hill under a gray sky, then the same goat in bright sun. Ask what changed. Then say "The goat looked at the big one" and ask: "What could you say to me?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the information question and the second round of pairs; keep the two-box drawing, the repair and the exit. Longer: a child who is secure draws a third time and tells two changes.

### Words and supports

Words: change, windy, scarf, beats. Frames: "At first ___. Later ___." "What thing?" "Can you say that again?" A pointed picture, a gesture or a home-language word is an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 reads the rain and snow pages. Its first 5 minutes repair what this exit shows: match the two pictures and say the one thing that is different, for error I; both pictures told for error F; and asking "Which one?" for error AT.
