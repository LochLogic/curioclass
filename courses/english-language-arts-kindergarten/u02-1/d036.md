# Day 36: Naming words and action words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the naming cards on the left of a chart and the action cards on the right, face down. Set the window so every child can see it, or choose scene 1 or scene 2 from the scene cards. Draw the exit cards C35 P36 C37 S38 and keep them out of sight; today uses P36. Check the record sheet for day 35's marks. Today's six are the next two unheard from each foundations group.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Whole class. Repair day 35's exit for the more common of error B and error A. For error B: Pip says "This page is about hats" for page 2; set the topic card on top and the three detail cards under it, and say "The page is mostly about a sunny day. Hats is one detail." For error A: Pip says something page 2 does not say; point to the page's own words. Then one fresh detail question about page 3: "What gathers on the ground on a rainy day?" Expect: puddles, with a point to the picture.

#### 5 min. Words

Whole class. Say each word, the class says it with a gesture, and you point to its picture. Rain: fingers drip down. Describe: tell about a picture with an open hand (review: you described a toy in kindergarten, day 10). Clear: when the sky has no clouds, arms spread wide. Naming word: a word that names a person, place or thing; tap a card with one finger. Action word: a word that tells what it does; shake both fists.

#### 15 min. Reading 01a again

Whole class. Say: "Listen for one naming word and one action word." Reread pages 1 to 4 of Weather Changes (about 4 minutes), showing each picture. After pages 2, 3 and 4 point to the picture and ask the pair of questions.

**Ask.** Page 2: "Children run. Which word names who? Which word tells what they do?" Expect: children; run. Page 3: "Children open umbrellas. Which word names who? Which word tells what they do?" Expect: children; open. Page 4: "A weather observer looks. Which word names who? Which word tells what the observer does?" Expect: observer; looks. Give each page about 3 minutes, with a partner turn before the whole class answers.

#### 12 min. Model: naming words and action words

Whole class, then pairs. First, sort (4 minutes). Turn each card over, say its word and ask: "Does this word name something, or tell what it does?" Children tap for a naming word and shake fists for an action word. Naming: rain, children, sky, drops, puddles. Action: tap, run, splash, drip. Second, build (4 minutes). Put rain and tap side by side: "Rain taps." Pip says only "Taps." (error AE, an action with nothing named); the class helps add the naming word. Pip says "It is nice" (error F, no naming and doing); the class says what is named and what it does. Third, pairs (4 minutes). Each pair takes one naming card and one action card, says the sentence to each other and swaps cards. If the pair cannot make sense of the two, they swap one card.

#### 18 min. Describe today's weather

Whole class, then pairs. Look through the window for 1 minute. If the sky shows nothing new, say "This scene is made up for practice" and show scene 1 or scene 2. Children draw in the box on the student page (7 minutes). Then pairs (10 minutes): one child says a sentence with a naming word and an action word, then adds a detail ("Rain taps. It taps the roof."), holding the drawing; the partner says it back; swap (4 minutes each way). Three children share with the class (2 minutes). You listen at six of the 15 pairs, 30 seconds each. No one is asked what they wear or have at home, and nobody goes outside.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Six children at the heard table, 40 seconds each (6 times 40 is 4 minutes). Say: "This picture is made up for practice." Show picture P36. Ask each child ([[s02-X36]]). A child may point and say one word, or use a home language; say the English back once. Signs: a naming word alone (error AE); a feeling or an opinion with no naming and doing (error F); a voice too quiet to hear, noted apart in its own cell (error E). Mark each look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The others finish drawing and label it with a letter they know.

#### 5 min. Foundations opening

Whole class. Review: tracking left to right, and the letter t (day 4). Read page 2 of the verse Rain twice, pointing under each word, and the class joins in. Find the capital S in the first word of the first line and a small s at the start of splash; say /s/ each time. Look for v: the page has none, so the search ends.

#### 15 min. Foundations work time

Whole class. Skywrite s and v with the whole arm. For s: start at the top right, curve back to the left, curve down and around to the right, and stop at the bottom. For v: slant down to the point at the bottom, then slant up (3 minutes). Keywords: sun for s, van for v. Capital and small letters share a shape. Trace each on the desk with a finger (3 minutes). Then on handwriting paper, a thick pencil, three s and three v, saying the sound with each (7 minutes). Walk the room and say the path aloud for a child who needs it. Share one letter you like (2 minutes). Error V: a child says /w/ for v; show the teeth on the lip.

#### 2 min. Foundations closing

Whole class. Say: "Today I practised ___." Three children finish the sentence; the rest say it with a thumb.

#### 38 min. Foundations rotations

Three groups of ten, three 12-minute turns and 2 minutes to move (3 times 12 plus 2 is 38). Teacher table, writing place, sound and verse place. At the table each child has about one minute (10 minutes). Table: the last sound of bus, yes and glass; the first and last sounds of sat and van. Compare with the first-sound turns on day 35. Writing place: write v and s three times each, then write the letter for a sound you say (/s/ then /v/). Error W: a child writes s for /v/; say the sound, pick the letter card, then write it. Sound and verse place: pairs find the picture cards that start with the same sound; point to s or v in the verse and name it. The day's six (two from each group) are heard at the table and marked in the heard column: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner; do the sort, build one sentence, look out the window and say a sentence, then do the exit picture. One projector: show the page pictures and the sort cards one at a time; children draw on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw rain and a tap on scrap paper. No window: use a picture.

**Skip.** The sort, the pairs and the groups.

- **5 min. Look.** Look out a window together.
  **Say.** "Tell me about the weather in one sentence." Expect: a thing and what it does, such as "Clouds move."
- **5 min. Two words.** Say "Rain" and "taps."
  **Say.** "Which word names it? Which tells what it does?" Expect: rain; taps.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws today's weather and says a sentence about it.
- **5 min. Letters.** Skywrite s and v and say their sounds.
- **5 min. Last sound.** Say bus and yes; your child says the last sound, /s/.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X36]] Draw a snowman in a yard with snow on it. Ask: "Tell me about this picture in one sentence."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the sort to four cards and the pairs build; keep the reread, the window and the exit. Longer: a child who is secure adds a second action word to a sentence ("Rain taps and drips").

### Words and supports

Words: rain, describe, clear, naming word, action word. Frames: "The ___ is ___." "___ ___s." A pointed card, a gesture or a home-language word is an answer; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 rereads the warmer-and-cooler page and asks about a word. Its first 5 minutes repair what this exit shows: a naming word with an action added for error AE, or the picture's own naming and doing for error F, and a louder voice for error E.
