# Exit cards, days 85 and 88

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Read each card twice with its picture. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 85 card E85

**Read:** "Text A: A frog's skin dries out in hot, dry air. Text B: On a hot day, a frog moves into the shade."

**Mark (item a):** Which card joins what Text A and Text B say? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: the sun with an arrow to a frog in the shade; a frog on a sunny stone with an arrow to the sun; two frogs side by side.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What do the two readings tell us together?"

## Day 88 card E88

**Read:** "A camel's hump holds fat. A camel can use that fat for energy when food is scarce."

**Mark (item a):** Which arrow picture shows how these two facts go together? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: the hump with an arrow to a camel walking with energy; a camel with an arrow to a pond; a rain cloud with an arrow to a camel.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What does the connection explain?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 85: item a, The sun with an arrow to the frog in the shade. Not yet: marks two frogs side by side (A, two facts side by side with no link); marks the frog on the sunny stone (B, an arrow the readings do not give). Item b, A frog's skin dries out in hot air, so on a hot day it moves into the shade. Not yet: says A frog moves, so it is alive (P, moving taken as proof of life); says I have a pet frog (E, unrelated to the question).
- Day 88: item a, The hump with an arrow to the walking camel. Not yet: marks the pond or the rain cloud picture (B, an arrow to something the words do not say). Item b, why a camel has energy when food is scarce. Not yet: says A camel has fat. It is scarce. with no link (A, two facts left unjoined).
