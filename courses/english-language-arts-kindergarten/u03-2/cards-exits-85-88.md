# Exit cards, days 85 and 88

Sheet E88 goes with a page of the unit's own texts, read aloud again with its picture covered; it is not made up, so say nothing about practice for it. The sheet is a strip of three pictures. Card E85 is made up: say aloud "This card is made up for practice", read it twice with its picture, and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot only.

The sheet and the card use plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any label the key names. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. None of these cards is shown in teaching.

## Day 85 card E85

**Read:** "Text A: A frog's skin dries out in hot, dry air. Text B: On a hot day, a frog moves into the shade."

**Mark (item a):** Which card joins what Text A and Text B say? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: the sun with an arrow to a frog in the shade; a frog on a sunny stone with an arrow to the sun; two frogs side by side.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What do the two readings tell us together?"

## Day 88 sheet E88: page 2 of The Oak

**Read again:** page 2 of The Oak (texts/gk-u3-05-a.md), the acorns; the class first reads The Oak on day 92.

**Mark (item a):** Which arrow picture shows how the facts on this page go together? Three pictures in a row, each in a ring: an acorn in soil with an arrow to a small oak; an acorn with an arrow to a truck; a cloud with an arrow to an acorn.

**Spoken, to the day's six (item b):** "What does the connection on this page explain?"

## Key (teacher)

- Day 85: item a, The sun with an arrow to the frog in the shade. Not yet: marks two frogs side by side (A, two facts side by side with no link); marks the frog on the sunny stone (B, an arrow the readings do not give). Item b, A frog's skin dries out in hot air, so on a hot day it moves into the shade. Not yet: says A frog moves, so it is alive (P, moving taken as proof of life); says I have a pet frog (E, unrelated to the question).
- Day 88: item a, The acorn in soil with an arrow to the small oak. Not yet: marks the truck or the cloud picture (B, an arrow to something the words do not say). Item b, How a new oak grows: the acorn is the oak's seed, so it can grow into a new oak. Not yet: says An acorn. An oak. with no link (A, two facts left unjoined).
