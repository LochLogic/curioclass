# Day 86: Check: explain a connection

## Teacher page

### Before class

Number the record sheet one to thirty: 1 to 15 are today's children. Make three groups of five. Cover the sapling card. Set two floor stations: the 01b cards in order, and a drawing spot.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Reread the needs chart. No repair.

#### 3 min. Sapling card (all thirty)

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice; show its three pictures. Each child marks the ring under the picture the words explain. Collect the slips.

#### 30 min. Connection groups (children 1 to 15)

Three groups of five, ten minutes each: reread 01b page 1 (2), connections (4), pictures (3), move (1). The others use the stations.

- **Connections.** Ask each child [[s03-C1a]], 25 seconds, with the token. Pairs answer each other ([[s03-D86a]]); ask [[s03-D86b]] for a second turn.
- **Picture explanations.** Return each slip; ask [[s03-C1b]], 35 seconds each.

Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: stake and wind not linked (error A); a picture that does not hold the sapling (error B); calling out (error G); one comment only (error H).

#### 10 min. Read again

Reread What's Alive and What's Not? and What a Tree Needs page 2; children raise a hand at each need.

#### 7 min. Notebook

Draw a fact; tell a partner its source.

#### 5 min. Return and rotate

Hear five, one minute each: show a 01b picture, read two sentences; the child points to the match. No exit ticket.

#### 6 min. Say it

Review (day 85): say jam, nap and ham; children push a counter for each sound, then blend.

#### 10 min. Short a, again

Find a on the sound-spelling map. Read mat, sat and tap from tiles, a sound for each tile. A child says the middle sound.

#### 12 min. Ants at a Mat, reader 1

Partners reread with the picture covered and say what happened. Hear six, one minute each; record who reads without guessing.

#### 8 min. Change one letter

Pairs. Change mat to map to nap with tiles; say which sound changed. Write the new word. Sign: naming a as the changed letter (error L).

#### 6 min. Words at and has

Read each on its card, then find it in the reader.

#### 18 min. Rotation

Hear six children, two minutes each, on syllables from pictures. The rest read sentence card 1 (Dan has a fan) in pairs and say who did what.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. One projector: show card and reader on one screen.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No book: use one picture and say what it shows.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the stations.

- **15 min. The assessment: a covered card.** Read twice: "Wind blows a kite. A string holds the kite." Show a string tied to a flying kite, a string on the ground beside a kite, and a bird eating a worm.
  **Say.** "Which picture shows what the words say? Tell me why." Expect: the string tied to the kite; it holds the kite.
- **10 min. Short a.** Spell sat on squares; change one letter to make tap.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads at and has.
- **10 min. A fact.** Your child draws a fact and says where it came from.
- **5 min. Counters.** Say jam; push a button for each sound.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the card, groups and notebook; cut the rotation. Longer: a secure child says a second link.

### Words and supports

Words: fact, link, picture. Frame: "The ___ helps because ___." A child may mark, point or answer privately; a scribe records without adding ideas. Use an agreed access method, not "not yet seen".

### Tomorrow

Day 87 hears the other fifteen children, reviews the slips and rereads Ants at a Mat. If many erred on error A, day 88 repairs it with an arrow card.
