# Section 3.2 opener: connect two facts and check them

## What the section does

Days 85 to 88 move from joining two facts to a first science talk. Day 85 revises the labeled note and prepares a talk from two texts. Days 86 and 87 are check days: the class hears one new covered card, groups of five give connection talks, and the teacher records. Day 88 returns what the checks showed and repairs it. Foundations start short a: blending, changing one letter, and the words at and has.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and a five-minute exit or, on check days, the observation groups. The foundations block holds sounds, letters, the day's words and the rotation. A child outside the six heard for the exit is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Children do not read alone; the teacher reads every card, direction and text aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A connection joins two facts.** The arrow shows which fact leads to which.
- **A picture can show what the words explain.** A child can say why a picture matches its sentence.
- **Turns are taken and continued.** A talking token marks whose turn it is; a second turn follows the partner.
- **Short a is read from letters taught, never guessed from a picture.** Cover the picture, blend the letters, then check.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, B:** two facts side by side, or a picture that does not show the stated support. Repair on day 88 with an arrow card and a reread sentence.
- **E, F:** an answer unrelated to the question, or no added fact on the note. Restate the question; offer two note roles.
- **G, H:** calling out, or stopping after one comment. The token passes for one turn; model a follow-up.
- **L, R, U, V, AB, AJ:** foundations slips with letters, counters and print order. Repair in the rotation.

Wrong examples are said by a puppet or as "someone might say", never by a child. No picture or question asks about a child's home, family, money, body or religion.

## Supplies

The covered card, the 02 and 01b pictures in large print, the reader Ants at a Mat, short a sentence cards, letter tiles, counters, a talking token, repair cards by error letter and the unit record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Draw each card on scrap paper and say it is made up for practice. Cut scrap paper into cards. Use buttons for counters, a torn strip for the talking token and a leaf or a drawn tree for the tree. Write the sound-spelling map as a strip by hand.
