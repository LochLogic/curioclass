# Day 87: Check: talk together and add to the note

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy day 86's items onto the record sheet. Cover the C1 pictures. Lay the 01b cards in order; pile the revised notes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Reread the needs chart.

#### 7 min. Paper review and picture explanations

Return the slips; reread chant page 3. Hear the other fifteen say why their picture fits, 28 seconds each; mark the C1 column. The rest add a detail; collect the slips.

#### 30 min. Connection groups (children 16 to 30)

Three groups of five, ten minutes each: reading (2), connections (3), discussion (4), move (1).

- **Connections.** Read chant page 3 twice; ask each child [[s03-C1cv2]], 35 seconds each.
- **Discussion.** Pairs talk with the token ([[s03-D87av2]]: waits, listens, answers); ask [[s03-D87bv2]] for a second exchange.

Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: unlinked trunk and water (error A); a picture that does not show the water going up (error B); calling out (error G); one comment only (error H).

#### 8 min. Read again

Reread What's Alive and What's Not?; raise a hand at each need.

#### 5 min. Notebook

Each child points to the 01b card of a revised note's fact; record [[s03-R0b]], 10 seconds each. Signs: a toy preference, no fact (error E); a fact the reading does not give (error Q). Then draw a fact; tell a partner its source.

#### 5 min. Return and rotate

Hear five, one minute each, on a picture-sentence match. No exit ticket. After class mark each revised day 83 note for [[s03-R0a]]. Sign: no added fact (error F).

#### 6 min. Say it

Review (day 85): say sap, tab and ham; push a counter per sound; say the first and last.

#### 10 min. Short a, again

Build pan, man and pat from tiles; say the middle sound.

#### 12 min. Ants at a Mat, reader 1

Partners reread, picture covered, and say what happened. Hear six not yet heard, one minute each.

#### 8 min. Change one letter

Change sat to sap to tap with tiles; say which sound changed; write the word. Sign: naming a as the changed letter (error L).

#### 6 min. Words at and has

Read each card. Say "She has a hat"; hold up has.

#### 18 min. Rotation

Hear six, two minutes each. The rest read sentence card 2 (That chap can chat) in pairs.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. One projector: show card and reader on one screen.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No book: use one picture and say what it shows.

**Skip.** The groups, the reader and the stations.

- **15 min. The assessment: a talk.** Read page 3 of the tree chant twice.
  **Say.** "How does the trunk help the tree?" Expect: it carries the water up to the leaves.
  **Say.** "What else can you say about the trunk?" Expect: another true remark, such as it is strong.
- **10 min. Short a.** Spell man on squares; change one letter to make pan.
- **5 min. Words.** Say "She has a hat." Your child holds up has when they hear it.
- **10 min. A fact.** Your child draws a fact and says where it came from.
- **5 min. Counters.** Say sap; push a button for each sound and name the first and the last.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the card, groups and notebook; cut the rotation. Longer: a secure child asks the partner a question about the support.

### Words and supports

Words: fact, link, source. Frame: "The ___ helps because ___." A child may mark, point or answer privately; a scribe records without adding ideas. Use an agreed access method, not "not yet seen".

### Tomorrow

Day 88 sets repair groups from days 85 to 87 (errors A, B, F, G, H), hands back day 87's assessment with the revised notes and the slips, rereads Ants at a Mat, and ends with an exit ticket on a new card.
