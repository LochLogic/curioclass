# Day 85: Join two facts from two readings

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one line of feedback on each day 83 note: the topic named, the fact true, the drawing showing it. Hang the needs chart (water, air, light, soil) and the story-map frame. Cover E85. Set out counters, tiles, the map and the record sheet. Read aloud: the student page says "I can join two facts with an arrow" and "Write the letter for each sound."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Whole class. Day 84's exit: if many put the later picture first (error Z), the puppet lays the tall-tree card of Noor's story before the small-tree card. Children fix it. Read two sentences, "The tree was small. Years passed. The tree was tall." Lay each card beside its sentence and say first, then later. If many named a word that does not say time passed (error D), reread pages 6 and 7 and have the class pick out the year words, next spring and each year; set aside words such as green. Two repairs at most.

#### 10 min. Revise the day 83 note

Individuals. Return each note and say its feedback line aloud as you hand it back. Each child draws one more fact from the needs chart beside the first, fixes anything the line named and says the fact to a partner. Take dictation from the day's six, about 45 seconds each; label for the others as you walk and finish their words after class. Look for: a fact that is about what a tree needs, from the heard text, and a pointing finger on the page it came from. Nothing is scored today; the revised note is scored on day 87.

#### 15 min. Recall from two sources

Whole class, then pairs. Reread What a Tree Needs page 1, then What's Alive and What's Not? with its pictures (about 270 words, four minutes with pauses). Model on the chart: "A tree needs water" (the tree article) and "The grass and flowers take in water too" (the picture book). Say where each was heard. Review: partner questions (unit 1). Children ask "Where did you hear that?" and answer it. Pairs choose one fact from each text and say the source of each, four minutes, then hear three pairs, about a minute each. **Ask.** "What does each reading supply?" Expect: the tree article says what a tree needs; the picture book says what makes a thing alive.

#### 5 min. Return to the story (T1)

Whole class, the day's six in front. Review: the story-map (unit 2, day 49). Reread The Tree Beside the Path page 5, the winter buds, and page 8, the wide trunk (about 100 words). Say: "Noor and the tree are made up." Ask [[s03-T1a]] of each of the six, about 40 seconds each. The class points to the page that shows the change while each speaks, which is [[s03-T1b]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Signs: a question that does not ask about the tree's change; no answer from the story.

#### 10 min. Prepare the talk

Pairs. Review: the talking token, one turn each, pass allowed (unit 3, day 80). The puppet calls out over a partner; children say what to do instead: wait for the token. Model once: "A tree needs water because its leaves use it to make food." Each partner says their own, then asks one more question ("Why do you think so?") and listens. Two rounds, three minutes each. Hear four pairs. Signs: calling out (error G); stopping after one comment (error H).

#### 10 min. Draw the talk card

Individuals. Review: the connection arrow and the joining words so and because (unit 3, days 81 and 82). Each child draws two facts their pair chose with an arrow between them. Walk and write the joining word on each arrow, about 20 seconds a child, from the child's own words. Talk cards go in the notebook.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Uncover E85 and read it twice with its pictures. Ask [[s03-X85a]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X85b]], 30 seconds each, while the class draws. Signs: two facts side by side with no link (error A); an arrow the readings do not give (error B); moving taken as proof of life (error P); an answer to a different question (error E).

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Four groups of about seven. Review: cycle 12 (days 80 to 84). The puppet shows each slip in turn, 45 seconds each, three minutes: cannot read do again (error S); no second rhyme (error AD); a capital named but not its small letter, or a letter formed wrongly (errors AC, AK); a letter name for a sound, or b for d (errors T, O). Groups repair four minutes with picture, letter and word cards; you sit with the group with most children. Each child retries three minutes with a card not used before.

#### 5 min. Say it

Whole class, oral. Review: first, middle and last sound with counters (unit 2). Say jam: push a counter for /j/, /a/, /m/ and name each. Do the same for nap and dig. No letters yet.

#### 10 min. New: short a

Whole class, then individuals. Review: you learned the letter a in unit 1; today it is the middle of three-sound words. Show a on the map: a is a vowel and says /a/ as in mat. Model on the chart: say mat, push a counter for each sound, write m, a, t under the counters, blend /m/ /a/ /t/, mat. Do sat together. Each child pushes counters for jam, writes the three letters on the writing side and blends it. Signs: dropping the middle, /m/ /t/ (error V); writing e for a (error U).

#### 5 min. Words at and has

Whole class. Show at: sound it, /a/ /t/. Show has: map /h/ /a/ /z/ and mark the part to remember, s says /z/ (error S if a child cannot remap it). Review: read the, a and in, then to and do, whole.

#### 8 min. Ants at a Mat, reader 1

Pairs. Read the title aloud: "Ants at a Mat." Partners take turns reading one sentence a page with the picture covered, then say what happened and uncover the picture. Walk past each pair. Signs: guessing a word from the picture (error R); reading two words as one (error AB).

#### 22 min. Rotation

Table and stations. Hear six children two minutes each, syllables from pictures (pumpkin, kitten, window): "Say it in parts, then put it together." Hear five children two minutes each on the print card: you read it aloud; each child tracks it left to right, top to bottom, turns the page, points to leaves and puts a finger between two words. Signs: reads the second line first (error AJ); points across a space (error AB). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The rest reread Ants at a Mat with a partner and change mat to map on tiles, saying which sound changed (the last, t to p). Sign: naming a as the changed letter (error L). Write who was heard on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Reread page 1 and the picture book, say two facts, draw an arrow, push counters for jam and read the reader with the picture covered. One projector: show the pictures of the two texts on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use buttons. No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No book: say two facts you know about a plant.

**Skip.** The token talk, the reader and the stations.

- **10 min. Two facts.** Your child draws two facts about a plant and an arrow between them.
  **Say.** "How do the two facts go together?" Expect: a tree needs water, so its roots take it in.
- **10 min. Short a.** Say pan. Your child pushes a button for each sound, writes p, a, n and blends it.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child reads at and has, then finds them in a book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X85a]] and [[s03-X85b]] about a card you draw. Read twice: "One reading: A puppy gets hot. Another reading: A puppy drinks water to cool down." Draw a puppy with an arrow to a water bowl, a puppy beside a cat, and a bowl with an arrow to a cloud. Ask which picture joins the two readings.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the two sources, the talk card, the exit and short a; cut the repair groups to two. Longer: a secure child joins a second pair of facts and says the joining word.

### Words and supports

Words: fact, source, link, vowel. Frames: "A tree needs ___ because ___." "I heard it in ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the picture that joins two readings and say the link. If many put two facts side by side (error A), day 88 repairs it with an arrow card; mark the others on the record sheet for that day. Day 86 is a check day: no repair, and the class marks a new covered card once while groups of five explain connections to you. Day 86 reads Ants at a Mat again and changes one letter on tiles.
