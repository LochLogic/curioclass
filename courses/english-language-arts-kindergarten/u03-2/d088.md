# Day 88: Repair a link and share it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the record sheet into four groups by recorded error, from the exit records of day 85, the card records of day 86 and the note records of day 87. Pull the repair cards for each group. Write a feedback line on each marked slip and each revised note. Mark page 2 of The Oak for the exit. Read aloud: the student page says "I can join two facts and say how" and "Write the letter that changed."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Whole class. Reread the needs chart. Hand back the marked slips and the revised notes, with day 87's assessment marks in your hand. Say each feedback line aloud as you hand a page back. Marks are never read out.

#### 15 min. Return and repair

Four groups. Group one: joined facts, a picture that does not show the support, or moving taken as proof of life (errors A, B and P, and Q as needed). Group two: a label missing or a reason the reading does not give (errors C and D). Group three: an answer to another question or no added fact (errors E and F). Group four: taking turns and a second turn (errors G and H), and facts out of order (error Z). Five minutes of modelling with a repair card, five of repair, five of retry with a sentence or picture not used before. You lead the group with most unresolved errors; partners use the card for each other's letter. Keep each original and its retry together. Wrong examples are said by the puppet.

#### 5 min. Reread

Whole class. Read What a Tree Needs page 6, the joining page, aloud. Children listen for the two facts it joins and hold up two fingers.

#### 5 min. Return to the story (T1)

Whole class, six children not yet heard in front. Review: the story-map (unit 2, day 49). Reread The Tree Beside the Path page 5, the winter buds (about 50 words). Say: "Noor and the tree are made up." Ask each of the six to ask a question about the winter tree and answer it from the page, about 45 seconds each. The class points to the sentence while each speaks. Record on the T1 columns. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Share a link

Five clusters of three pairs. Review: "Where did you hear that?" (day 85). Model for two minutes with your own repaired link. Each pair shows its talk card and says "My two facts go together because ___", pointing to the sentence for each fact, three minutes a pair. You listen to one cluster; the others use the talking token. At the end, two pairs from that cluster say their link to the class. Signs: two facts and no joining word (error A).

#### 10 min. Add to the chart

Individuals. Each child draws one new fact from What a Tree Needs on a paper square and places it on the needs chart. Take dictation from the day's six, about a minute each; label for the others as you walk, and finish their words after class.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Whole class, then six. Read page 2 of The Oak aloud twice, its picture covered; the class first reads The Oak on day 92. Show strip E88 and ask [[s03-X88av2]]: children hold up one, two or three fingers against their chest. Mark the day's six only (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), then ask them [[s03-X88bv2]], 30 seconds each, while the class draws. Signs: an arrow to something the words do not say (error B); two facts left unjoined (error A).

#### 6 min. Say it

Whole class, oral, then six. Clap tiger, butterfly and dog. Hear six children one at a time in the class circle, about 40 seconds each: "How many parts in window? Say it in parts, then together."

#### 12 min. Reread, tracking print

Pairs. Reread Ants at a Mat only. One partner reads while the other points under each word, left to right, page by page; swap at page 6. A finger marks the space between two words. Walk past every pair. Signs: reads the second line first (error AJ); points across a space (error AB); guesses from the picture (error R).

#### 10 min. Letters and sounds

Pairs, then individuals. Review: letter names, both cases (unit 2). Pairs name letter cards A to G and H to N mixed, three minutes. Each child prints three of them, three minutes. Say the sounds of b, c, d, f, g and h; each child writes the letter for each sound you say, four minutes. Signs: a letter name for a sound (error T); b for d (error O).

#### 6 min. Words at and has

Whole class. Review: at and has (day 85). Read each on its card, then find them in the reader.

#### 14 min. Change one letter

Pairs. No new pattern. On tiles make had, change it to ham, then to jam, saying which sound changed each time (the last, then the first). Walk past four pairs. Sign: naming a as the changed letter (error L).

#### 12 min. Rotation

Table and stations. Hear six children two minutes each, syllables from pictures; write who was heard on the record sheet. The rest take a changed-letter pair card (sat and sap, man and pan, pat and pad) and write the letter that changed on the writing side.

### Other rooms

One child: you are the partner. Use the repair card for the recorded error, reread page 6 and the reader, and change had to ham to jam on tiles. One projector: show the repair card and the reader on one screen; the child still draws on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on paper squares. No reader: write three short sentences with at and has.

**Skip.** The cluster, the chart and the stations.

- **10 min. A link.** Your child draws two facts about a tree and an arrow between them.
  **Say.** "Which sentence joins your two facts?" Expect: a tree needs water, so its roots take it in.
- **10 min. Letters.** Say b, c, d, f, g and h as sounds; your child writes each letter.
- **10 min. One letter.** Spell had on squares; change one letter to make ham.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X88av2]] [[s03-X88bv2]] Read page 2 of The Oak aloud. Your child says what the link on the page explains.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the share and the exit; cut the letters to three. Longer: a secure child finds a second link between two pages and says the joining word.

### Words and supports

Words: link, fact, join. Frame: "My two facts go together because ___." Pointing, dictation, a home language and a communication aid all count. A child may pass in any talk. Read each exit prompt twice, with no added hint.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can pick the arrow picture that shows a link and say what it explains. If many left two facts unjoined (error A) or marked an arrow to something the words do not say (error B), day 89 opens with five minutes: say the joining word between two facts. Day 89 reads A Tree Is a Living Thing whole and asks who needs trees.
