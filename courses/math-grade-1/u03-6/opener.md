# Choose a subtraction strategy and close the record

## The mathematics

Choose a strategy that fits the numbers. A short count up makes a small difference easy to see. A related double or a ten can make a larger difference easier. A missing start can be rebuilt by returning the removed pieces to those that remain.

## In class

Withdraw shared towers on day 64. Keep the observed fact responses separate from paper revisions. On day 68 assemble prior evidence with the two fresh slips. Collect those slips before numerical checking. Send dated feedback and a fresh priority retry to days 69 and 70.

## First repairs and supplies

For I, join the two known parts to recover the start. For F, match the leaving group. For B, open a folded ten strip. For H, mark the changed amount. Paper mats, ten strips, scraps and pencils cover every task.

Read the prompts aloud and offer large dark pictures or a neutral scribe. Hear one rotating group of six in Synthesis or Close. Six 45-second turns leave 30 seconds to switch. Children may pass and respond privately later. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Adult-cut large pieces stay on trays and are collected after practice. Offer individual drawings whenever the printer or a shared device is unavailable. Open each reserved check only in its named part.
