# Counting and calculating in tens

Review the full frame to tower to drawn ten-stick. Start with ten individual marks. Join them, draw all ten cells and say what the joined unit counts. Say both the number of tens and the total. For a decade, say that the zero means no leftover ones. For subtraction, keep the starting rods visible and show exactly what leaves. An empty remainder is zero. Compare thirteen and thirty beside their pictures.

## What to watch for

A counts a ten-stick as one. G confuses the spoken teen and decade. E moves a single when a ten must move. F leaves the starting amount after all tens leave.
