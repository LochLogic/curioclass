# Teacher key for the section exits

Teacher only. Confirm the observed reason before assigning any error letter. Record what the paper shows separately from what you heard.

### s04-X69

Seven groups of ten; tag 70.

Shape: Organize a fixed scatter into a countable record.

- A: 7. Counts each group as one one.
- A: 61. Counts six groups by tens and the last group as one one.

### s04-X70

90, ninety; nine tens and no loose ones.

Shape: Generate the missing numeral from an unlabeled tower picture.

- A: 9. Counts the towers as nine ones.
- G: Nineteen. Reads nine tens as the similar teen word.

### s04-X71a

70; one whole ten joins. 60 + 10 = 70.

Shape: Predict an arrival from a covered start, then make a confirming record.

- E: 61. Adds a single one instead of a ten.

### s04-X71b

50; one whole ten leaves. 60 - 10 = 50.

Shape: Predict a departure from the original covered start, then confirm.

- E: 59. Removes one single instead of a ten.

### s04-X72a

Draw five remaining tens worth 50. 90 - 40 = 50.

Shape: Supply the missing tray remainder after partial removal.

- A: 5 ones. Treats each remaining ten as one one.

### s04-X72b

An intentionally empty tray, marked 0. 30 - 30 = 0.

Shape: Supply the missing empty tray after all groups leave.

- F: 30. Keeps all three tens when they all leave.

### s04-X73

20, two tens. 60 - 40 = 20.

Shape: Reconstruct an unseen remainder from a strip label and a spoken loss.

- A: 2. Counts two remaining tens as two ones.
- E: 56. Removes four singles.
