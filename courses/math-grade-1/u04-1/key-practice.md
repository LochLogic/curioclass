# Teacher key for the unit practice pieces

Teacher only. Name this piece in Before class when using these cards and sheets.

## Counting collections, retrieval and Unit 3 response

Forty is four tens. Sixty is six tens. Twelve is one ten and two further ones. Rin’s groups are 3 + 3 + 8 = 14. The stamps total 2 + 4 + 9 = 15.

## Whole tens and calculation rounds

The matching values are 10, 30, 40 and 50. Thirteen has one ten and three ones. Thirty has three tens and zero ones. 20 + 10 = 30 and 30 − 10 = 20. Folded cards: 40 + 10 = 50 and 50 − 10 = 40. Marked rods: 50 − 20 = 30, 20 − 20 = 0, 70 − 50 = 20. Facts: 6 + 5 = 11, 8 + 7 = 15, 5 + 5 = 10, 9 − 4 = 5.

## Units, words and expressions

25 = 20 + 5, 23 = 20 + 3, 22 = 20 + 2 and 44 = 40 + 4. 25 has two tens and five ones. Removing two loose ones gives 23.

## Ten moves and fluency

22 to 32 to 42. 96 − 10 = 86, 15 − 10 = 5, 95 − 10 = 85, 14 − 10 = 4, 22 + 10 = 32, 98 − 10 = 88, 12 − 10 = 2. 11 − 6 = 5 and 16 − 8 = 8.

## Comparison and order practice

9 > 6 and 8 > 5. 25 < 32 and 32 < 42 by tens. 22 = 22 by both places. 42 < 54 by tens. 20 < 23 by ones. 25 < 32 < 44 by tens in both links. 22 < 25 by ones.

## Exchange and estimation practice

2 tens and 2 ones equal 1 ten and 12 ones. 4 tens and 14 ones equal 5 tens and 4 ones. 3 tens and 5 ones equal 2 tens and 15 ones. 54 > 44. 22 is closer to 20 than to 40. Group two tens and two singles to verify.
