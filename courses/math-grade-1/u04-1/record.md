# Unit 4 record

Record the date and help actually used. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Log each paper response and heard explanation separately. In a five-minute slot, allow six individual 45-second turns plus 30 seconds to cue and collect. A later turn supplies new evidence. On day 80, allow nine minutes for the six children’s two directions. Use error letters only when the marks or words show the error.
