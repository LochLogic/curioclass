# Section 1.2: Sorting and showing

A sorting rule puts each object in exactly one group. A picture label lets a reader know which group the marks represent. One object gets one mark. How many more is the unmatched count after one-to-one pairing. Single tally marks only until day 11, with at most three groups and no graph axes.

Sort, group and rule are review from written kindergarten sorting; category mats carry that experience forward. Re-establish count routines whose earlier ledger entry is planned only. The ten-frame leaves the shared display day 7. Tie real piles to picture-labeled marks before children fill the ready columns. Each day has 60 minutes, including five-minute Warm-up and repair and five-minute Synthesis plus five-minute paper exit. Day 7 also has a five-minute sorting retrieval; its misses get ten minutes within day 8 Fill the display.

Error D puts one response in two categories: move it to exactly one mat. Error E leaves one out: tick a slip when its mark is made. Error G omits labels: draw a category picture. Error F gives the larger group instead of the extras: match and count what remains. Error A or C on day 10 uses the earlier counting repair. More than 10 of 30 means one priority whole-class repair; fewer misses get one five-minute practice group. Do not add several warm-up repairs.

Before class print category-mats.md, display-sheet.md, retrieval-mat.md for day 7, cards-shapes.md for day 8, color-slips.md for day 9 and cards-comparison.md, practice-puzzles.md for day 10. Each day has its own closed exit picture page; key-exits.md is teacher only. Use the unit record in u01-1. Day 8's judged display returns within day 9 Record the slips.

Use large spoons, cloth squares and paper strips, or large cut-paper stand-ins. A folded sheet replaces every display and mat; the teacher can copy the exact pictures. Red/blue/yellow slips also have stripe/dot/wave cues. Anonymous choices are optional; an invented slip is available. Two groups of 15, with missing members replaced by pretend responses said aloud to be invented. No child reports on home or family. Adult-cut pieces stay on trays and out of mouths under supervision.


## Hearing and return

Use the rotating-six schedule in u01-1/record.md: one 45-second ask each within five-minute Synthesis, all pairs otherwise work concurrently. Names not heard today have their next slot five days later; hearing is new evidence. Each child marks a paper exit now. Before class name the current teacher-only answer key, and reveal no held item or whole assessment format in practice. Return every judged paper on the day in handbacks.csv, using only the named part's minutes. The ten-minute group for kindergarten retrieval is distinct from the five-minute exit repair. At home the adult is the partner and may hear the child privately.
