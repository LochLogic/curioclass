# Exit answer key

Teacher only. Before class, print and keep apart from child tickets. Read all prompts aloud; paper marks are evidence for every child. Shown, shown with help, not yet seen; a described unmatched answer gets no guessed letter.

## s01-X7a

Draw loops to sort these by one rule.

Answer: By shape: triangles 3, circle 1, squares 2; or striped 3, plain 3.

Every pictured shape belongs in exactly one loop under a consistent rule; accept another coherent partition into at most three categories.

Shown: Every shape in one consistent loop | Not yet seen: One shape in two loops, error D

- One shape in two loops: error D. Overlapping membership; counts 3, 2, 2 for shape groups.
## s01-X7b

Write each loop's count.

Answer: 3, 1, 2 by shape; 3, 3 by pattern.

Counts match the child's valid grouping in X7a; scribed numerals accepted.

Shown: 3, 1, 2 with shape groups | Not yet seen: One left out; 3, 1, 1, error E

- 3, 1, 1 with one square uncounted: error E. Omits a response.
## s01-X8

Make a display another child could read.

Answer: Three picture-labeled groups with 4 triangles, 3 circles, 1 square.

One mark for each shape; three category picture labels; no prepared columns on this exit.

Shown: Labeled groups with 4, 3, 1 marks | Not yet seen: Correct marks without labels, error G

- Correct marks without labels: error G. Reader cannot identify groups.
- 4, 3, 0 with the square left out: error E. Omits the square.
- 9 marks, one shape recorded in two categories: error D. Only classify D when the paper shows membership in two categories; duplicate within one category gets a description.
## s01-X9a

Make one mark for each answer I read.

Answer: 3 marks.

Teacher reads drum, bell, drum, with a pause for one mark each. No printed response list is visible before this task.

Shown: Three distinct marks | Not yet seen: Only two marks, error E

- Only 2 marks: error E. One heard response left out.
## s01-X9b

Make a labeled display for drum and bell.

Answer: Drum 2, bell 1; picture labels accepted.

Use the remembered response list and the three marks; exactly one category per response.

Shown: Drum picture with two marks, bell with one | Not yet seen: No labels, error G

- 2 drums and 2 bells, same response in both: error D. One response placed in both categories.
- No labels: error G. Categories not identifiable.
## s01-X10a

Mark how you count boats and planes altogether.

Answer: 9; hold 6 and count 7, 8, 9.

Three boat marks and six plane marks. Hold either group; record one count per new response.

Shown: 6 held; 7, 8, 9 | Not yet seen: 6, 7, 8, error A

- 8 with six as first step: error A. Counts the start.
- All marks numbered 1 to 9: error C. Recounts the kept group.
## s01-X10b

Mark how many more planes than boats.

Answer: 3.

Match three boats to three planes; three plane marks remain.

Shown: Three unmatched planes marked | Not yet seen: 6, the plane count, error F

- 6, without matching evidence: error F. Uses larger category count.
