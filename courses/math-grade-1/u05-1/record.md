# Unit 5 record

Keep one private roster. Date evidence and write the support used. On each dated paper mark **shown**, **shown with help**, or **not yet seen**. Keep a space blank if evidence was missed, and enter the next offer. Keep a revised attempt separate from the original. Put one priority move on the feedback slip.

All children mark each exit. For the optional explanation hear one rotating group each day. Hear roster 19 to 24 on days 96, 101 and 106, and 25 to 30 on days 97, 102 and 107. Hear 1 to 6 on days 98, 103 and 108, 7 to 12 on days 99, 104 and 109, and 13 to 18 on days 100, 105 and 110. Allow 30 seconds for one link and 10 seconds to note and change turns. Offer a missed turn privately at the next available slot. Date that later evidence separately.

For the Addition record hear 1 to 15 privately on day 108 and 16 to 30 on day 109. Allow 45 seconds for the explanation and 15 seconds to record it and change turns. Use the uncorrected P5 paper. Record one link per child. On day 110 assemble prior papers and mark Fresh transfer separately. Return the final record on day 111, with a second repair and private offer on day 112 as needed.
