# A missing part, then a ten

## The mathematics

In a joining story the start belongs to the story even while it is hidden. Separate the arrivals from the finish to check the start. On day 47 show ten actual ones, snap or fold them into one ten, and trace the ten to the same quantity in the drawing and numeral.

## In class

Use Rin alone in the invented seal scene. In Cover, lift and rebuild, have pairs preserve the parts and confirm by lifting once. A full frame, tower and ten-stick show the same amount. A cover, one or two drawn ten-frames, scrap counters, and a folded ten-mark strip suffice.

## First repairs and supplies

For B, open the strip and touch each mark before refolding. For A, separate new steps from the held amount. For G, cross off each part once. Put a mark where a changed quantity differs from a known fact for H.

Read the prompts aloud and offer large dark pictures or a neutral scribe. Hear one rotating group of six in Synthesis or Close. Six 45-second turns leave 30 seconds to switch. Children may pass and respond privately later. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Adult-cut large pieces stay on trays and are collected after practice. Offer individual drawings whenever the printer or a shared device is unavailable. Open each reserved check only in its named part.
