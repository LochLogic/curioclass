# Story record key

Teacher copy. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Date later help and retain the first paper.

## s06-AS1

Rin hangs the paper lanterns from this strip. How many lanterns are on the board now?

**Answer.** 13 + 3 = 16. Link both starting rails and the separate arrival.

- 3 on the final board Error I. Uses the arrival as the ending.

## s06-AS2

Rin added paper umbrellas to the board. How many did she add?

**Answer.** 5 + ? = 18; 13 arrive. Link both pictured boards; mark the missing arrival.

- 18 added Error I. Uses the ending as the change.

## s06-AS3

Rin added the leaves on this slip. How many leaves were on the board first?

**Answer.** ? + 4 = 17; 13 first. Mark the four arrivals within seventeen. Descriptive.

- 17 at the start Error I. Uses the ending as the start.

## s06-AS4

Rin takes seven counters from a tray of eighteen. How many stay?

**Answer.** 18 − 7 = 11. Show eighteen on the tray and distinguish the removed seven from the remaining group.

- 7 stay Error I. Uses the departure as the ending.

## s06-AS5

Rin took counters from these pockets. How many did she take?

**Answer.** 15 − ? = 2; 13 leave. Link all three pockets before and the two still present.

- 2 taken Error I. Uses the ending as the departure.

## s06-AS6

Rin took these circles off the board. How many were there first?

**Answer.** ? − 2 = 16; 18 first. Join the two removed and sixteen remaining to reconstruct the start. Descriptive.

- 16 first Error I. Uses the end as the start.

## s06-AS7

How many cards are on Rin's tray?

**Answer.** 7 + 13 = 20. Mark triangles and both circle packets as parts of one whole.

- 13 altogether Error J. Treats one part as the whole.

## s06-AS8

Rin's other cards are triangles. How many triangles are there?

**Answer.** 4 + ? = 20; 16 triangles. The four circles belong within the twenty, not beside them.

- 20 triangles and 4 circles Error J. Treats the whole as a separate part.

## s06-AS9a

How could Rin split these cards between two trays?

**Answer.** Any ordered pair of whole numbers totaling 19; for example, 9 + 10. Accept zero on either tray. Use all cards once.

- 19 on both trays Error J. Uses the whole as each part.

## s06-AS9b

What is another split Rin could make?

**Answer.** A different ordered pair totaling 19; for example, 2 + 17 after 9 + 10. Accept switching unequal counts between named trays or a zero count. Compare with the first paper.

- 19 on one tray and 1 on the other Error J. Treats the whole as one part and adds a unit.

## s06-AS10

How many more cubes are on red than blue?

**Answer.** 11 + ? = 20; 9 more on red. Derive both totals; mark only the unmatched extra.

- 31 because of more Error H. Uses the keyword to choose addition.

## s06-AS11

How many fewer cubes are on green than yellow?

**Answer.** 9 + ? = 16; 7 fewer on green. Match bars; mark the extra on the larger tray.

- 16 as the difference Error J. Uses the larger whole as the extra.

## s06-AS12

Orange has this many more cubes than purple. How many are on orange?

**Answer.** 9 + 4 = 13 on orange. Combine two pairs as four and extend the purple bar.

- 4 on orange Error J. Uses the extra as the entire larger whole.

## s06-AS13

Brown has six fewer cubes than gray. How many are on gray?

**Answer.** ? − 6 = 13; 19 on gray. Mark six beyond the brown bar. Descriptive.

- 7 from 13 − 6 because of fewer Error H. Chooses subtraction from a keyword.

## s06-AS14

White has this many fewer cubes than pink. How many are on white?

**Answer.** 11 − 4 = 7 on white. Join the difference pieces as four; mark that span at the end of the longer bar.

- 4 on white Error J. Uses the difference as the smaller whole.

## s06-AS15

Black has eight more cubes than tan. How many are on tan?

**Answer.** ? + 8 = 19; 11 on tan. Match bars and mark the given extra of eight on the larger bar. Descriptive.

- 27 from more Error H. Chooses addition from a keyword.
