# Show the missing quantity in a story

Read one scene aloud at a time. A joining or removing story has a before amount, a change and an after amount. For part-whole stories, draw a bracket around the two parts. Use the box or question mark to show the missing quantity. A situation equation records what happened. A related solution equation can help find the amount.

Match cubes or paper squares from a common start before tracing labeled bars. Name the drawing a tape diagram. Use the same unit in each bar. Choose a relation by locating the unknown, and let children show more and fewer with the same extra span. Keep start-unknown and hard language forms descriptive. Use blank mats, precut paper and pencils. If prepared practice cards are unavailable, sketch their givens and read them aloud.

For error I, mark before, change and after. For error J, bracket the whole around its parts. For error H, match the bars before choosing an operation. Give written feedback on the retained stories and keep revisions with the dated originals.
