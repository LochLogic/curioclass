# Exit ticket key

Teacher copy. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Keep papers private.

## s06-X121a

Rin removes fifteen units. What equation tells the length left?

**Answer.** 16 − 15 = 1. Mark the five triples, one loose unit and the removed part.

- 15 as the length left. Error I. Puts the removed amount in the ending position.
- 16 + 15 = 31. Error J. Adds a part to the whole it already belongs to.

## s06-X121b

Rin joins two more units. What equation tells the new length?

**Answer.** 17 + 2 = 19. Mark both old pieces as the start and the arrival as the change.

- 2 as the whole path. Error J. Treats the added part as the whole.
- 17 as the ending. Error I. Uses the start as the ending.

## s06-X122

The lid is one unit longer than the strip. How would two bars show the lid's length?

**Answer.** 15 units; 14 + 1 = 15. Two bars share a start; label the lid as the full unknown bar and mark the one-unit extension.

- 1 as the entire lid. Error J. Treats the extension as the whole.
- Lid bar is 14, with one extra unit drawn inside it. Error J. Places the extra inside the already matched part.

## s06-X123a

Rin took away the tiles on this slip. What equation shows what happened?

**Answer.** ? − 5 = 12, or 17 − 5 = 12 once the start is found. Match 2 and 3 to the five removed and the three groups of four to twelve left. Descriptive.

- 12 − 5 = ?. Error I. Moves the end to the start.
- ? − 12 = 5. Error I. Swaps what left and what stayed.

## s06-X123b

What addition equation finds Rin's starting number?

**Answer.** 12 + 5 = 17 or 12 + 2 + 3 = 17. Reversed addends also work. Trace both departure groups into the starting whole. Descriptive.

- 17 + 5 = 22. Error I. Uses the original amount as the group left.

## s06-X124a

Rin wants to know how many stamps she added. Where does the missing number go in her equation?

**Answer.** Change position on the time mat; 0 + ? = 11. The mark links to the arrival. The final eleven and the eleven added have different roles.

- Marks the empty start. Error I. Uses the first moment as the change.
- Marks the final book. Error I. Uses the ending as the change.

## s06-X124b

Rin knows how many stamps there are and how many are plain. Which part of the picture shows what she still needs to find?

**Answer.** The printed-stamp part on the whole-and-parts mat. Mark only the printed part, distinct from the known plain part and the whole sealed sheet.

- Marks the whole sheet. Error J. Treats the whole as the missing part.
- Draws a second whole sheet as the missing part. Error J. Uses a whole in place of a part.

## s06-X125

How would you tell a partner this same difference using ‘more’ and ‘fewer’?

**Answer.** Long row: 5 more. Short row: 5 fewer. Mark both directions from the same excess span. A bare 5 gives no direction. Descriptive when language needs support.

- 25, because more means add. Error H. Chooses from a keyword.
- 10 as the difference. Error J. Uses the whole smaller row as the extra.
