# Practice key

Teacher copy. Read each prompt aloud and show its matching model after children try.

## s06-P121-retrieve

Seven squares are on a tray. Three more arrive. How many now?

**Answer.** 10. 7 + 3 = 10. Invented for practice. Children mark the join.

## s06-E121-model

Rin adds 2 units to a strip of 4. How long is it now?

**Answer.** 6 units. 4 + 2 = 6. Invented for practice. Use a paper strip and two matching pieces, then draw three moments.

## s06-E122-model

How many cubes stick out when we match 6 with 4?

**Answer.** 2 cubes. 4 + 2 = 6. Align the starts. Match one to one. Trace the two bars and label the extra end.

## s06-E123-model

Rin takes 3 pieces away. Five are left. How many were there first?

**Answer.** 8. ? − 3 = 5; 5 + 3 = 8. Invented for practice. Restore three, then name the starting amount.

## s06-E125-larger

One strip is 8 units long, 2 fewer than another. How long is the other?

**Answer.** 10 units. Invented for practice. Match the strips, then extend the smaller by two.

## s06-E125-smaller

A 12-unit strip is 2 units longer than another. How long is the other?

**Answer.** 10 units. Invented for practice. Cover the extra two along the long bar.
