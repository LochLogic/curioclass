# Section 8.2 opener: find the missing amount

Review Grade 1's Twelve-cell story inventory, Labeled tape diagram and Cover one quantity in a labeled model. Act out each kind on fresh paper before asking children to draw it. Review change as the amount that joins or leaves. Part names a group within the whole. The unknown is the amount to find. A label names the group. Difference is the length left over after matching. Review Kindergarten's more, fewer, numeral and Echo supplied start then continue.

The situation equation keeps the unknown in the story's place. A solution equation may put it at the end. The three moving-story panels show before, move and end. A part-whole drawing holds the whole fixed. In a comparison, equal units and aligned beginnings let the gap show either how many more or how many fewer. Keep start-unknown and opposite-language feedback descriptive.

For error A, keep the starting count fixed. For error C, point to before, change and after separately. For error D, align the bars and mark only the unmatched part. For error E, open a ten-stick into ten visible ones and rebuild it. Assign a letter only when first marks or speech show the reasoning.

Use the double ten-frame, story cards, open-and-cover mat, labeled comparison bar mats, counting and numeral matching cards, half slips and Exit boxes. Read the separate prompts one at a time. Keep the keys with the teacher except for the covered partner key on day 157. Send the day 157 papers home after partner feedback; tally anonymous stick-count slips for the next day and discard them.

Large paper squares replace cubes, joined strips replace ten-sticks, and folded scrap replaces a sleeve. Hand-draw matching bars and counting marks. An adult cuts before class. Keep pieces on a tray and away from mouths. No device is needed.
