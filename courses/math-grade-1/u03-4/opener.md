# Make a helpful pair and keep every part

## The mathematics

An intact group can move without losing any of its pieces. Pair addends to make a ten first, then include the third. When only part of an addend moves, account for the piece or pieces that stayed. Taking away depends on the start, so a reversal can run out of pieces.

## In class

Connect each paper or cube train to its matching term. Show the correct work before the puppet contrast. During Written feedback and one revision in a second color, keep both dates. Day 57 checks dated evidence and one new make-ten slip; finish with familiar practice.

## First repairs and supplies

For D, touch all three groups. For E, build both starts. For C, trace moved and remaining pieces. For H, mark the difference from the known double. Folded ten strips, drawn frames and large scrap paper shapes substitute for cubes and counters.

Read the prompts aloud and offer large dark pictures or a neutral scribe. Hear one rotating group of six in Synthesis or Close. Six 45-second turns leave 30 seconds to switch. Children may pass and respond privately later. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Adult-cut large pieces stay on trays and are collected after practice. Offer individual drawings whenever the printer or a shared device is unavailable. Open each reserved check only in its named part.
