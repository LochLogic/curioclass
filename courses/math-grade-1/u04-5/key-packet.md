# Teacher key for the Place-value record and Fresh traces

Teacher only. Use the common packet on days 89, 90 and 91. Read private mental starts out of earshot, one prompt at a time. Confirm the first mental answer and its method before showing the mat. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Keep the original mental entry when the child revises paper work.

### s04-A89digit

How would you draw what each digit in sixty-four is worth?

Draw six tens worth 60 and four ones worth 4, with both contributions marked.

Shape: Generate and annotate both contributions independently from a bare numeral.

- B: Four tens and six ones. Reverses the digits.

### s04-A89decade

How would you draw eighty so I can see what the zero means?

Draw 80 as eight tens with no loose ones. Zero means zero leftover ones.

Shape: Independently draw the decade and make the empty place explicit.

- A: Eight ones. Counts the eight sticks as eight singles.

### s04-A89unit

What trade can you draw for the loose squares?

Circle 10 uncovered squares and draw and label one equivalent ten-stick; both show 10. Flap stays shut.

Shape: Replace only the visible loose pieces inside a partly covered pack and label the equal values.

- A: One stick worth one one. Counts the replacement stick as one single.

### s04-A89teen

How would you finish this drawing of seventeen?

Show 17 by restoring two cells and drawing seven loose ones. Another complete one-ten and seven-ones drawing also works.

Shape: Complete an unfamiliar torn ten-stick record for a named teen.

- B: Seven tens and one one. Swaps tens and ones.

### s04-A89teen-equation

What goes in the empty box in seventeen equals ten plus box?

7, the loose ones beyond the full ten.

Shape: Complete the addend from a separately read teen equation.

- B: 1. Uses the count of tens for the loose ones.

### s04-A89tens

92 and 57. What comparison does your drawing show?

92 > 57; the left has more tens even though the right has more ones.

Shape: Create the place-value traces and give the one comparison they support.

- C: 92 < 57 based on loose ones. Compares ones first.
- D: 92 < 57 after saying the left is greater. Writes the opposite sign despite correctly comparing.

### s04-A89equal

45 and 45. What comparison does your drawing show?

45 = 45; both places match.

Shape: Create the place-value traces and give the one comparison they support.

- A: One side is 9. Counts each rod on just one side as one one.

### s04-A89difference

How would your drawing explain ninety take away thirty?

Nine tens with three crossed out leave six tens. 90 - 30 = 60. Accept a linked related-addition drawing.

Shape: Generate a first-principles removal drawing and equation from a bare subtraction.

- A: 6 ones. Counts each surviving stick as one one.

### s04-A89zero

How would your drawing explain fifty take away fifty?

Five tens with all five crossed out leave zero tens. 50 - 50 = 0.

Shape: Generate a full-removal record from an empty drawing panel.

- F: 50 remains. Leaves the starting tens after all are removed.
