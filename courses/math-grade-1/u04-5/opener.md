# Trading and recording place value

Review the ten as a bundle of ten ones and the estimate as a first guess. Open one marked ten or gather ten singles into the same-sized strip. Trace where the ten marks go. To compare flexible forms, check each amount, then compare tens and ones. Offer movable pieces in practice and fold them out of sight during the two private mental prompts.

## What to watch for

A loses the unit size, B reverses the places and H drops ten loose ones. Unfold each cell, align with the labeled places, or circle the ten singles that become the new strip. Use C, D, E, F and G only when the work or words show the matching cause.

## Prepare

Seat one packet group a day on days 89 to 91. Cover all prior feedback and hear the two mental turns privately. Score revised paper on day 92, keep the original mental observation, and return the fresh work day 95. Use large pieces on trays and keep them out of mouths. An adult counts pieces back in during the last partner turn. Draw ten-cell strips and singles if cubes are unavailable, draw trays if needed, and handwrite cards. Read prompts aloud. Partners swap roles. Use one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return papers privately and use a fresh practice case for any retry. Keep keys in Before class, for the teacher.
