# Teacher key for the section exits

Teacher only. Confirm the observed reason before assigning any error letter. Record what the paper shows separately from what you heard.

### s04-X87

Three tens and eleven singles; total 41.

Shape: Generate an exchanged picture from a numeral alone.

- A: Three tens and two singles. The opened ten becomes one single.
- B: Fourteen singles. Starts with one ten and four singles instead of four tens and one single.

### s04-X88

Choose seven tens and two loose ones, worth 72. Account for the ten traded singles and their new ten-stick.

Shape: Recognize one equal amount in a picture bank and mark the pictured connection.

- H: Six tens and two loose ones. Ten loose ones disappear.
- B: Two tens and seven loose ones. Exchanges correctly and reverses tens and ones.

### s04-X89

56 > 55. After the trade both have five tens; six ones exceed five.

Shape: Compare mixed pictured forms after trading ten loose ones on one side.

- H: 46 < 55. Loses ten loose ones.
- D: 56 < 55. Reverses the sign after correctly identifying the greater amount.

### s04-X90

Shape: Generate a picture that justifies a given true comparison.

- C: 79 is greater because nine is more than four. Compares ones first.
- A: 12 < 16. Counts each rod as one one in both amounts.
