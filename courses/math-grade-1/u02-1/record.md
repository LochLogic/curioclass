# Unit 2 record sheet

Use one roster row per child. Write the actual date and support with each item. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Leave uncollected cells blank with a next date. An unsupported error receives a description. A scribe follows the child's directions. Read all questions aloud and keep independent paper evidence for every child, including those who pass a public turn.

## Hear six each day

Roster group 1 is heard on days 17, 22, 27, 32 and 37. Group 2 is heard on days 18, 23, 28, 33 and 38. Group 3 is heard on days 19, 24, 29 and 34. Group 4 is heard on days 20, 25, 30 and 35. Group 5 is heard on days 21, 26, 31 and 36. At 45 seconds per child, six turns take four and a half minutes. In the first rotation, hear each child's day 17 story retell in the assigned Synthesis slot. Record all other paper evidence as it is made. Keep a missed or passed turn for a private offer.
