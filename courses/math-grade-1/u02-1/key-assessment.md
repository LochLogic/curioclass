## S25a

How many tiles are on the mat?

**Answer.** 11 tiles; 6 + 5 = 11 or equivalent.

Use the separate booklet page with six round and five square paper tiles. Mark both groups as one whole.

Shape: Independently supply a whole on one two-category mat.

- **H.** 10 tiles. Includes six as the first new count.
