# Section 2.1 opener

## Actions and changes

Start with the two library scenes and the question about showing what happens. The retrieval follows the hook, then the separate workroom story is read twice. Take an action apart before naming the start, change and result. Tie movements to plus and minus on day 17, then compare the amounts on either side of an equation on day 18.

## The mathematics

A joining action adds pieces to the counted start. A removal action takes pieces out of that group. The last amount is the result. In a change-unknown story, keep both pictured states available. Draw the change separately before marking the box. Give every child the paper; keep the ten-frame optional when an independent sketch holds the quantities.

## Errors to listen for

A: read the equal sign both ways. C: place the change label on the moved group. H: start the next count with the first new piece. I: cross out only the pieces that leave.

Use the correct example first, then the folded-paper puppet's invented error. Return feedback privately. Record one rotating group of six at 45 seconds each in Synthesis and collect paper marks from everyone. A child may pass in public talk. Read every stem aloud and offer large dark outlines or a scribe who makes only the marks the child directs. Mark evidence as shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

## Supplies

Library picture cards, workroom sequence, action retrieval sheet, paired paper ten-frames, two-side equation mat, change flap, action mat, and day-specific slips. Replace counters with large patterned scraps, a puppet with a sketched face, and the shared display with individual paper.

Use adult-cut paper pieces larger than a small-parts tube. Keep them on trays and collect them after use. Every task can be drawn on paper. Keep the teacher keys and assessment pieces closed until their named parts.
