# Daily exit key

Teacher copy. Read each exact stem from its learner piece. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Record a letter only when the picture or the child's words show that reasoning.

## E17

What expression shows Ari's move?

**Answer.** 12 + 7 or 7 + 12. The acted total is 19.

Ari has twelve paper kites and adds seven. Link the two addends to the before group and the arriving group. Marks may replace loose pieces.

Shape: Generate an expression from an independently acted scene.

- **H.** 12 + 7 with 18 pictured. Counts twelve as the first new step.

## E18

How does this equation tell the story?

**Answer.** 14 first, 3 join, 17 altogether; plus joins and equals shows the same quantity, 17.

Uma puts three more paper birds beside fourteen on a branch picture. Match the numerals and signs in 17 = 14 + 3 to the pictured groups and relationship. Indicated arrows or circles can show the links.

Shape: Interpret a supplied total-first equation as a complete picture story.

- **A.** Crosses out 17 = 14 + 3. Rejects a true equation solely because the total comes first.

## E19

What goes in the box?

**Answer.** 8 stars in the arrivals panel.

Jo has eleven paper stars and puts up some more. Now there are nineteen. Use 11 + □ = 19 to identify the missing arriving group.

Shape: Reconstruct unshown arrivals from pictured before and after states.

- **C.** 19 arriving stars. Copies the ending quantity.

- **H.** 9 arriving stars. Includes eleven as a new step.

## E20

What goes on the patch?

**Answer.** 6 flags came down; 18 - 6 = 12.

Sam starts with eighteen paper flags. Sam takes some down and twelve stay up. The marked patch shows six departures.

Shape: Infer the removal change between static before and after views.

- **C.** 12 flags on the patch. Marks the number left as the change.

## E21

What joining story fits this equation?

**Answer.** One actor starts with 16, adds 2 and ends with 18.

Accept any drawn or acted single-actor joining story for 16 + 2 = 18, for example Kit adds two bells to sixteen on a tray.

Shape: Invent the whole scene from a supplied equation.

- **I.** 16 bells lose 2 and end with 18. Uses addition for pieces that leave.
