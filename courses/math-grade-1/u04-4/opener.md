# Comparing quantities

Review comparing single-digit numerals and matching pictured quantities. Pair the whole tens first. Compare ones only when the tens tie. Words and the sign read left to right. Read greater than, less than and equal to alongside the quantities. The wide side faces the greater amount. When ordering, identify what decides each neighboring link.

## What to watch for

C looks at ones while tens differ. D reverses the sign after a correct quantity comparison. Match the tens before showing ones, then read a supplied sign and place a sign for a spoken statement.

## Prepare

Use the comparison picture cards and prepared order row. Remove shared models for day 85. Individual drawings stay available. Use large pieces on trays and keep them out of mouths. An adult counts pieces back in during the last partner turn. Draw ten-cell strips and singles if cubes are unavailable, draw trays if needed, and handwrite cards. Read prompts aloud. Partners swap roles. Use one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return papers privately and use a fresh practice case for any retry. Keep keys in Before class, for the teacher.
