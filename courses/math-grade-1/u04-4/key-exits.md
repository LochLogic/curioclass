# Teacher key for the section exits

Teacher only. Confirm the observed reason before assigning any error letter. Record what the paper shows separately from what you heard.

### s04-X82

52 circled or marked, with five tens exceeding four tens.

Shape: Choose from bare numerals and make the deciding marks.

- C: 47 because seven ones beat two. Lets ones decide with unequal tens.
- B: 47 with seven tens shown. Treats the ones digit as the number of tens.

### s04-X83a

63 is greater than 58; mark the left side.

Shape: Read a supplied true assertion, marking the side the sign calls greater.

- D: 63 is less than 58. Reverses the sign despite correctly comparing the amounts.

### s04-X83b

38 is less than 34; mark the right side as the greater side the printed sign claims. The assertion is false.

Shape: Read a supplied false assertion literally, without correcting it.

- D: The point means 38 is greater. Correctly compares the amounts but reverses what the printed sign claims.

### s04-X84

46 < 81. Four tens are fewer than eight tens.

Shape: Write the sign in the first box of a fixed picture strip and mark why.

- D: 46 > 81. Writes the opposite sign after correctly saying the first amount is less.

### s04-X84b

81 = 81. Each group has eight tens and one one.

Shape: Write the sign in the middle box and show the equal amounts from differently arranged pictures.

- A: < in the middle box. Counts the eight sticks on the left as eight ones, treating that side as nine.

### s04-X84c

81 > 69. Eight tens are more than six tens.

Shape: Write the sign in the last box of the strip and identify the deciding place.

- C: 81 < 69. Compares the single one with the nine loose ones despite unequal tens.

### s04-X85

27, 62, 65, in that order.

Shape: Order a complete scrambled set independently in one open response space.

- C: 62, 65, 27. Makes 27 greatest because its ones digit is greatest.

### s04-X85b

27 < 62. Two tens are fewer than six tens.

Shape: Record the first fixed link after the independent ordering and mark the deciding tens.

- D: 27 > 62. Writes the opposite sign after saying twenty-seven is less.

### s04-X85c

62 < 65. Six tens match, and two ones are fewer than five ones.

Shape: Record the second fixed link after the independent ordering and mark the deciding ones.

- D: 62 > 65. Writes the opposite sign after saying sixty-two is less.

### s04-X86

33 < 37. Both have three tens; three ones are fewer than seven.

Shape: Generate both labels and their comparison from two fixed, ungrouped pictures.

- D: 33 > 37. Writes the opposite sign despite saying the left has fewer.
- B: 33 < 73. Reverses 37 after correctly grouping it.
