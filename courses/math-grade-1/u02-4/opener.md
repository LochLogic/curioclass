# Section 2.4 opener

## Stories and equations

Build both sides before judging equality. Withdraw the shared ten-frame on day 33 and keep each child's own sketch or frame available. Move paper role labels to identify starting, changing and ending groups before attaching a number or box.

## The mathematics

A true equation has equal values on both sides. Reversing its sides preserves that statement. A situation equation names what happened; a solution equation makes the unknown easier to find. Both can refer to the same moved group. Restore pieces to rebuild an unknown subtraction start. Day 36's story start is supported and unscored.

## Errors to listen for

A: read both orientations. B: build and check both sides. C: point to the moved group before locating the box. F: put the removed pieces back into the remainder.

Use the correct example first, then the folded-paper puppet's invented error. Return feedback privately. Record one rotating group of six at 45 seconds each in Synthesis and collect paper marks from everyone. A child may pass in public talk. Read every stem aloud and offer large dark outlines or a scribe who makes only the marks the child directs. Mark evidence as shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

## Supplies

Two paper workspaces, role cards, a folded-paper flap, Equality strip, Unknown strip, day-specific half slips with separate day 33 Exit box, and original match-a-picture cards. Make all pieces from paper, and use a drawing if a folded flap is unavailable.

Use adult-cut paper pieces larger than a small-parts tube. Keep them on trays and collect them after use. Every task can be drawn on paper. Keep the teacher keys and assessment pieces closed until their named parts.
