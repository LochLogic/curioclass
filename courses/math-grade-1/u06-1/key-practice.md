# Practice key

Teacher copy. Read each prompt aloud and show its matching model after children try.

## s06-P114-retrieve

Is 8 = 10 − 2 true?

**Answer.** True. Both sides are 8.

## s06-P114-1

**Answer.** 27

## s06-P114-2

**Answer.** 82

## s06-P114-3

**Answer.** 37

## s06-P114-4

**Answer.** 34

## s06-P114-balance

How can you fix 5 + 4 = 16 − 6?

**Answer.** Change 6 to 7, for example: both sides are 9. Accept other single changes giving equal totals within 20.
