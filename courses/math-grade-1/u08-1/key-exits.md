# Section 8.1 exit key

Teacher copy. Read the cues aloud. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen where the response is judged. Keep unheard speech open. Attribute an error only when the child's marks or words show it.

## Day 151

**s08-X151.** Show the addition fact this picture makes. 3 + 3 + 1 = 7, or 3 + 4 = 7 with the flap and tab joined. Look for marks linking all groups to one total. Accept a matching drawing, numbers or neutral dictation. Keep strategy evidence separate from mental fluency. Error A: 6. Starts one set of new counts at the held amount. Error A: 5. Starts both sets of new counts at the held amount.

## Day 152

**s08-X152.** Show the addition fact that puts all eight back together. 1 + 3 + 4 = 8, or 4 + 4 = 8 with the cup and bag joined. Look for four still on the mat and marks showing the two returned groups. Accept a picture, equation or neutral dictation that uses all locations. Error A: 4 + 4 = 7. Starts the four new counts with four.

## Day 153

**s08-X153a.** Which fact counts the dots still outside the full ten? 4 + 6 = 10. Look for a mark on the right fact. The moved dot belongs to the filled ten and the four and six outside combine without counting it again. Error B: 5 + 6 = 11. Counts the moved dot in its former group.

**s08-X153b.** Which strip finds how many tiles stayed out? The strip from 10 through 17. It shows 8 new counts. Look for the strip whose first added count comes after all nine removed tiles. Keep the first slip covered. Error A: Nine through seventeen. Includes the starting amount as a new count.
