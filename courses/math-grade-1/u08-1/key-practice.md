# Section 8.1 practice key

Keep this with the teacher. The two-group deck totals in reading order are 5, 8, 4, 9, 5 and 10. The related-fact pairs are 9 − 5 with 5 + 4, 8 − 6 with 6 + 2, 7 − 4 with 4 + 3, and 9 − 7 with 7 + 2. Have partners mark before you uncover a matching pair.

In the mixed deck, the first line groups a ten, the second steps back through ten, and the third breaks an addend to make ten. Values are 12, 8 and 14. Children may describe a true alternative with fresh marks.
