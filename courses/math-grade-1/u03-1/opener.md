# Which facts can I use?

## The mathematics

Build two equal groups and trace each group to its addend. Changing the order of the same two groups preserves the total. A held group has already been counted. When you count on, one new piece earns one new number. A related addition can show why removing one part leaves the other.

## In class

Use the unfinished table before retrieval. Model a double and its one-more neighbor before the sort. During Partner rounds with a counters check and retry, have the child try first, then point to a check on paper. Use the unit 2 centers on days 40 and 41.

## First repairs and supplies

If the held start gets a new count, mark one step per new counter for A. For H, point to the actual dot that moved out or the extra dot that joined. Frame, cover, fact cards and large scraps. A folded scrap can be the cover or puppet.

Read the prompts aloud and offer large dark pictures or a neutral scribe. Hear one rotating group of six in Synthesis or Close. Six 45-second turns leave 30 seconds to switch. Children may pass and respond privately later. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Adult-cut large pieces stay on trays and are collected after practice. Offer individual drawings whenever the printer or a shared device is unavailable. Open each reserved check only in its named part.
