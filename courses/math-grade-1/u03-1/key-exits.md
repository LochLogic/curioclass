## E41

Why does counting on from the big packet work both ways?

The same larger packet stays counted in both orders. Match both packets and mark one step for each of the two new counters.

Shape: Explain a swap even though the larger count is hidden. Practice swaps groups with known counts. Keep both packet pictures out of practice.

- **A.** Taps the big packet for the first step and marks only one striped counter.

## E42

Which card shows just as many buttons as this picture?

The middle card. Match the closed bag to itself and the three loose buttons individually.

Shape: Match two expressions for a collection whose bag count stays hidden. Practice builds known amounts from pieces.

## E43

Both pockets hold 10 dots. What total stays after these moves?

9. An internal move keeps the total 10. Only the dot that falls out reduces it.

Shape: Predict a nearby sum from two different moves without needing the starting split. Practice builds and changes one known pair on a frame. Reserve the pocket picture and both arrows.

- **H.** Keeps 10 after noting that a dot left the pockets.

## E44

How does the adding picture show how many clips stay?

4 clips. The sleeve and 4 new clips filled the tray. Removing the same sleeve leaves exactly those 4 new clips.

Shape: Use the addition to undo a removal without knowing how many are inside the sleeve. Practice starts with a known total and rebuilds a visible whole. The tray holds at most ten clips.

- **A.** Marks 5, tapping the held sleeve and then the four new clips as steps.
