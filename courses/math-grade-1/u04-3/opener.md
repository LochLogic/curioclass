# Changing one ten while keeping the ones

Review the ten as one unit made from ten ones. Join or remove one marked strip at a time. When extra tens arrive, keep leftover ones in view and point to the matching chart move. In mental practice, hide objects and drawings before the first answer. Let the child confirm afterward with their own drawing. A restored starting amount adds back the group that left.

## What to watch for

E moves a single instead of a full ten. B reverses the tens and ones when labeling a correct build. Repair with marked strips and known quantities before another unaided try.

## Prepare

Use one chart column and folded direction cards. Keep starting numbers for ten more below ninety. On day 80 allow the six children both private 45-second turns before pictures are uncovered. Use large pieces on trays and keep them out of mouths. An adult counts pieces back in during the last partner turn. Draw ten-cell strips and singles if cubes are unavailable, draw trays if needed, and handwrite cards. Read prompts aloud. Partners swap roles. Use one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return papers privately and use a fresh practice case for any retry. Keep keys in Before class, for the teacher.
