# Practice key

Teacher copy.

## P104-mat

Check the finished mat for 27 plus 35.

62. Six tens and two ones.

Keep five original tens and trade ten of the twelve loose ones.

## P104-decade

Check the finished mat for 25 plus 35.

60. Six tens with no loose ones.

Five old tens and ten loose ones make six tens.

## P105-moves

Try all three move cards for 75 plus 18.

93 in all three orders.

Tens first: 70 + 10 = 80, then join 13 ones. Ones first: 5 + 8 = 13, then join the eight old tens. Add on: 75 + 10 = 85; 85 + 8 = 93.

## P106-orders

Try both orders for 38 plus 24.

62 in both orders.

The eight and four loose ones make twelve. The three and two old tens make five tens. Either order gives 50 + 12 = 62.

## P107-endpoint

Check the last move for 64 plus 36.

100. Ten tens and zero loose ones.

64 + 30 = 94. 94 + 6 = 100. The four and six singles form the tenth strip.

## P107-caption

Match a step to the pieces for 65 plus 12.

77. One route is 65 + 10 = 75; 75 + 2 = 77.

Join the ten to the whole starting amount, then add two loose ones. Accept equivalent matched steps.
