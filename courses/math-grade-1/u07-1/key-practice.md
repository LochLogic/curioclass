# Section 7.1 retrieval key

Teacher only. On 129, the cube goes beside the cylinder. The triangle is flat and the cube takes up space. On 130, matching the two upright legs keeps the right corners side by side on one straight base. To make a rectangle after the first attempt, rotate one piece by a half-turn about the midpoint of the longest side. The original legs are unequal, so the rectangle is not a square. On 131, the broad triangle target can be filled by joining its supplied two halves along their upright legs.
