# Sort shapes and keep a solid build together

Review flat, solid, face, curved, side, corner and rule using objects. A face is flat. A cube is a rectangular prism with equal square faces. A right circular cone has a flat circular base and a curved surface. A right circular cylinder has two flat circular ends and a curved surface. Turn an object in the child’s hand, then look at its sketch. The sketch records the solid from one view.

Review Partner turns. One child handles each object while a partner watches, then they exchange jobs. Guess a hidden rule from the objects that belong together. On 130 join two objects and move the joined whole in one piece before extending it. The paper sketch records its outer outline and its joins. Review the Labeled tape diagram and Place-value columns with grouped pictures on paper before number practice.

Use the original sorting mat, retrieval cards, flat cards and solid sketches. Substitute clean boxes, rolled tubes and adult-folded paper cones for solid kits. An adult cuts large paper pieces and checks objects for sharp edges or loose parts. Use plain paper tabs on stable surfaces. Keep small or damaged materials away from children.

For error A, keep the solid fixed while changing its turn or its apparent color and have the child feel or trace a feature that remains. For error D, move a joined practice pair whole before adding one piece. Return all marks privately. Day 129 and 130 Centers are reserved for the unit 6 response. Send the bilingual family note on 129. No readiness sitting is scheduled; use the two kindergarten retrieval tasks in practice.
