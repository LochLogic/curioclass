# Section 7.1 exit key

Keep this key with the teacher. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Leave an unheard response open. Record language separately when a picture already shows the mathematics.

**s07-X129a.** Circle the solids whose outsides are flat everywhere. The cube and the noncube rectangular prism only. Both have flat faces throughout. The cone and cylinder each have a curved surface. Error A: Circles the red cube and red cone because both are red.

**s07-X129b.** Show what makes each circled solid belong. Marks flat faces on both chosen solids. Accept tracing or pointing at flat faces on the matching paper views. No spoken word is required. Error A: Marks only the largest solid because of its size.

**s07-X130a.** Draw a cylinder joined to this build. A cylinder touches the outlined cube-prism build. Keep the cube and rectangular prism in their original contact. Error D: Separates the cube and prism to insert the cylinder.

**s07-X130b.** Trace the first build inside your new one. Traces around the original joined cube and prism together. The trace includes both initial solids and leaves the added cylinder outside. Error D: Traces two separate solids after pulling the first build apart.

**s07-X130c.** What shapes are in your build? Cube, rectangular prism, cylinder. Match the three paper picture labels to the parts or accept the corresponding words. Judge names separately from the join.

**s07-X131a.** Circle shapes that belong together because of their sides. Both triangles for three straight sides, or both triangles and the square for only straight sides. Credit another consistent side rule only if the group includes all and only its members. Error A: Circles only the upright triangle because the other is turned.

**s07-X131b.** What is your side rule? Three straight sides, only straight sides, or another matching side rule. Accept a picture mark of the shared feature or neutral transcription. A word omission alone does not undo a correct mark. Error A: Gives blue as what makes the triangles triangles.
