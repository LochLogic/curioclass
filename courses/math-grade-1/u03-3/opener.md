# Change the ones and keep the ten

## The mathematics

In these teen additions and removals, a complete ten keeps its value. Track singles coming or going separately and show the corresponding change in the expression. In take away pictures, the change is the amount leaving. The result is the group that stays.

## In class

During Counting observation, half the class a day, place the private cards out of the other children’s view. Allow about thirty seconds for six brief responses, fifteen for a strategy note and fifteen to record and switch. The original oral record and the independent blank paper panels have separate dates.

## First repairs and supplies

Open the ten-strip for B. Trace new counts for A, the leaving group for F, each removal once for G and the changed part for H. Use adult-cut patterned squares, folded ten strips and blank practice mats. Draw the models if the kit is unavailable.

Read the prompts aloud and offer large dark pictures or a neutral scribe. Hear one rotating group of six in Synthesis or Close. Six 45-second turns leave 30 seconds to switch. Children may pass and respond privately later. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Adult-cut large pieces stay on trays and are collected after practice. Offer individual drawings whenever the printer or a shared device is unavailable. Open each reserved check only in its named part.
