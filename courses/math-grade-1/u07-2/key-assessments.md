# Section 7.2 assessment key

Keep this key with the teacher. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Leave an unheard response open. Record language separately when a picture already shows the mathematics.

**s07-AR1-sort.** Which one is a triangle? The first, closed scalene triangle only. Look for three straight sides that join. Read position without naming the chosen figure. Error A: Rejects the closed triangle because it is tilted. Error B: Marks the figure with the gap. Error C: Marks the closed figure with the arc.

**s07-AR1-sort-feature.** Mark the parts that make it a triangle. Marks all three straight sides and their joined endpoints. Use the same reserved card after the first choice is recorded. Error C: Marks a curved edge as an acceptable straight side.

**s07-AR1-nondef.** Cross out the things that can change in a triangle. Crosses out color, turn and size. Read the icon labels in order. The closed straight-sided boundary still defines membership. Error A: Leaves turn as required because a triangle must stand up.

**s07-AR1-build.** Build a triangle with these strips. Uses all three unequal strips in a closed three-sided build. The longest strip is shorter than the other two together. Save the child’s outline as evidence. Error B: Leaves a gap at one joint and calls it a triangle.

**s07-AR1-draw.** Draw around your triangle. Makes a closed drawing with three straight sides. Trace the edge of the child’s own build. Accept intended edges despite planned motor supports. Error B: Traces an open path that omits a join and calls it a triangle.

**s07-AR1-feature.** Mark what makes your drawing a triangle. Marks three straight sides that meet to close the outline. Judge marked attributes on that child’s own drawing. Error C: Counts a curved mark as a straight side.

**s07-AR1-color.** Give your triangle a new color. Changes color with one stroke and retains the triangle. The stroke can be anywhere inside the original tracing. Error A: Says changing its color stops it being a triangle.

**s07-AR1-size.** Make a smaller copy here. A smaller closed three-straight-sided outline on the loose card. No exact scale is required. Keep this paper with the original outline. Error A: Says a smaller copy cannot be a triangle.

**s07-AR1-turn.** Turn your copy a different way. Rotates the loose triangle drawing without changing it. Keep original and rotated copy together. Mark its new direction before filing. Error A: Rejects the turned copy as a triangle.

**s07-AR1-rect.** Mark all the rectangles. Marks the tilted square and the long thin rectangle only. Both have four straight sides and four square corners. Error J: Marks the two-square-corner figure as a rectangle. Error A: Leaves out the square solely because it is tilted.

**s07-AR2-solid.** Make one build with all four solids. All four specified solids form one joined build. Record cube, noncube right rectangular prism, right circular cone and right circular cylinder separately. Flat pictures alone do not evidence physical building.

**s07-AR2-solid-parts.** Draw the pieces in your build. The sketch identifies the four constituent solids. Accept rough drawings or single-word labels that identify each part and show the contacts.

**s07-AR2-solid-outline.** Trace around the whole build in your drawing. One outer outline of the solid composite. The trace goes around the child’s entire connected build.

**s07-AR2-solid-use.** Show your first build inside a bigger one. Adds a touching solid and visibly preserves the first build. The paper shows the same four solids and original contacts inside the later construction. Hear one reason during the assigned turn. Error D: Takes apart the original joined solids to make the larger build.

**s07-AR2-flat.** Make one design with all six pieces. Six specified flat pieces form one connected design. Mark each piece used: rectangle, square, trapezoid, triangle, half-circle and quarter-circle. Keep record of connected placement.

**s07-AR2-flat-outline.** Trace around your whole design. One outline around the six-piece flat design. Trace the exterior of the child’s own connected design.

**s07-AR2-flat-use.** Use your whole design in a bigger one. Adds a touching piece while all six parts stay together. The initial outline and contacts remain identifiable in the later design. Error D: Rearranges the original six into unrelated pieces before adding.
