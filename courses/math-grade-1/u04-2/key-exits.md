# Teacher key for the section exits

Teacher only. Confirm the observed reason before assigning any error letter. Record what the paper shows separately from what you heard.

### s04-X74

Four groups of ten and three loose squares; 43 squares in all.

Shape: Organize fixed marks to distinguish tens from loose ones.

- B: 3 tens, 4 ones. Reverses the quantities by place.
- A: 7 ones. Counts four groups as four single ones.

### s04-X75

The 2 stands for 20 and the 6 stands for 6.

Shape: Annotate both digit contributions in linked callouts.

- B: 2 ones and 6 tens. Reverses the places.
- A: Two rods worth 2 ones. Treats each rod as a single.

### s04-X76

82; eight tens and two ones.

Shape: Recover a missing total from a grouped display presented in two tower clusters.

- B: 28. Reverses the places.
- A: 10. Counts the towers and singles all as ones.

### s04-X77

50 + 9 or 9 + 50; the addends account for the whole 59.

Shape: Generate an expanded expression from a supplied drawing.

- A: 5 + 9. Counts each rod as one one.
- B: 90 + 5. Swaps the counts of tens and ones.

### s04-X78b
