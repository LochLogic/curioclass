# Reading and writing tens and ones

Review a full frame to tower to drawn ten-stick. Move loose squares into complete groups before recording what remains. Read the tens place as groups of ten and the ones place as individual ones. Move a digit card above the two places and rebuild to show why the digit changes value. Point from each piece of an addition expression to the objects it counts.

## What to watch for

A treats each ten as one. B reverses the digit positions. Unfold a strip for A. For B match each unit to its own column before reading the numeral.

## Prepare

Space the two panels of the final exit and hear three children on each. Return the two panels together. Use large pieces on trays and keep them out of mouths. An adult counts pieces back in during the last partner turn. Draw ten-cell strips and singles if cubes are unavailable, draw trays if needed, and handwrite cards. Read prompts aloud. Partners swap roles. Use one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return papers privately and use a fresh practice case for any retry. Keep keys in Before class, for the teacher.
