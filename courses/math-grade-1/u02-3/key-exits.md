# Daily exit key

Teacher copy. Read each exact stem from its learner piece. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Record a letter only when the picture or the child's words show that reasoning.

## E27

Which triangles have no partner?

**Answer.** 12 triangles marked after one valid pair.

One square and thirteen triangles are unpaired. Any one triangle may be paired with the square.

Shape: Find and mark every unmatched object on an unpaired picture.

- **E.** All 13 triangles ringed. Counts the larger group instead of the extra.

## E28

What drawing shows how many more brushes?

**Answer.** 9 more brushes; 2 matched pairs and 9 extra brushes.

Give only blank drawing space and picture labels for eleven brushes and two covers. Accept labeled rows or groups if marks distinguish two partners from the nine extras.

Shape: Generate a matching drawing from an oral comparison story.

- **E.** 11 extra brushes. Rings the whole larger group.

- **H.** 10 extra brushes. Counts two as a new step.

## E29

How many fewer blue choices than red choices?

**Answer.** 15 fewer blue choices.

The invented display has sixteen red, one blue and three green choices, twenty total. The mark for one blue choice pairs with one red; fifteen red choices remain unmatched. Green is a different category.

Shape: Independently select two of three categories and show their gap.

- **E.** All 16 red marks ringed. Reports the larger group as the gap.

## E30a

What addition shows the extra leaves?

**Answer.** 10 + 8 = 18 or equivalent, with 8 extra leaves marked.

Print eighteen leaves matched against ten flowers with the extras unmarked. Accept reversed addends or equality sides.

Shape: Generate the addition relation from a given matched picture.

- **E.** 10 + 18 = 28. Uses the larger count as the missing addend.

- **H.** 9 extra leaves. Includes ten as a new step.

## E30b

What subtraction shows the leaf difference?

**Answer.** 18 - 10 = 8 or 8 = 18 - 10.

Use the same untouched matched picture. Record the difference where the equation shows it.

Shape: Generate the reverse operation from the same matched picture.

- **E.** 18 as the difference. Uses the full larger group as the difference.

## E31a

Which label fits Nia's cups?

**Answer.** Joining; mark the ten cups that arrive.

Nia adds ten paper cups to ten already there. Read the two labels. Record unsupported label choices descriptively.

Shape: Pick a story relationship from reusable labels and mark the clue.

## E31b

Which label fits the spoons and forks?

**Answer.** Comparing. Mark the 10 extra spoons.

Twenty spoons and ten forks sit side by side. Read both labels. Mark the clue for comparison.

Shape: Recognize a static comparison in the same label bank.

- **E.** Rings all 20 spoons. Confuses the larger group with the extra.

## E32

What fact tells the triangle difference?

**Answer.** 8 extras; 12 + 8 = 20 or 20 - 12 = 8, or equivalent.

The static game card has twenty triangles and twelve circles. Accept either operation linked to the marked extras.

Shape: Generate a related fact from the extra in a static game picture.

- **E.** 20 extras. Marks all triangles.

- **H.** 9 extras. Includes twelve as a new step.
