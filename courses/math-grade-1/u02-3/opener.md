# Section 2.3 opener

## Matching comparisons

Begin with a paper retrieval whose groups are spaced differently. Introduce aligned matching rows and give each object at most one partner. Label both groups and point out only the extra. Move between more and fewer language one direction at a time.

## The mathematics

In a comparison neither group moves. The difference counts members of the larger group with no partner. The difference is the addend needed to build the larger amount from the smaller amount, and the result of subtracting the smaller from the larger. For a three-category display, find the two categories the question names first.

## Errors to listen for

E: show one valid link per pair and ring just the marks with no partner. H: one move per new count, after the known smaller amount.

Use the correct example first, then the folded-paper puppet's invented error. Return feedback privately. Record one rotating group of six at 45 seconds each in Synthesis and collect paper marks from everyone. A child may pass in public talk. Read every stem aloud and offer large dark outlines or a scribe who makes only the marks the child directs. Mark evidence as shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

## Supplies

Spacing sheet, matching rows mat, comparison cards, art-choice display, comparison and flag booklet pages, familiar fact cards. Replace cube towers with drawn equal-size marks. Use the invented art display if the shared display is unavailable. Keep feedback and revisions with the day 30 and day 31 booklets.

Use adult-cut paper pieces larger than a small-parts tube. Keep them on trays and collect them after use. Every task can be drawn on paper. Keep the teacher keys and assessment pieces closed until their named parts.
