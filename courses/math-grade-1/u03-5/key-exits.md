Teacher copy. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Record a letter only when the marks or words show its reasoning.

## E58

Which way helps you find how many buttons Rin has?

13. For ten, move 4 dotted buttons beside 6, leaving 1 plus 2. For a double, move 1 striped button beside 5, making two sixes and leaving 1.

Shape: Choose between a ten and a double in an unworked three-group story. Practice compares two supplied records. Keep the three button groups and method-choice sheet out of practice.

- **C.** Moves 4 but still adds all 5 dotted and both striped buttons to 10, giving 17.

## E59

How could you add the groups to find Rin's total after the slide?

16. After the transfer, 7 + 7 + 2 makes 16. Before the transfer, 8 + 2 made 10 with 6 left. Accept another valid order accounting for all three unchanged total groups.

Shape: choose an order to predict a total across one pictured transfer. Practice groups trains and reads static number rows, with no slide card.

- **D.** Uses the pre-slide 8 and 2, then stops at 10 and omits the 6.

- **D.** Pairs the two sevens and gives 14 without the two.

## E60

How can one pair help you find Rin's whole score?

Pair 4 and 6 and keep 2: (4 + 6) + 2 = 12.

Shape: reconstruct one score from a pictured turn record. Practice uses familiar partner cards and no three-turn score slips.

- **D.** Stops at 10 after omitting 2.

- **D.** Uses only 2 and 6, omitting 4.

## E61

Rin will take away 19 dots in all. Ten are on the mat now, and 10 still need to go. How many are already gone?

9. Nine gone and ten still to go make 19. Write 9 in the Already gone box.

Shape: Recover the covered removal from the whole removal and the part still to go. Practice begins with a visible start and physically separates what goes. Reserve the folded record.

- **F.** Writes 10 in Already gone by copying the number of dots still on the mat.

## E61-left

Rin takes away the 10 on the mat. How many are left at the end?

0. Cross out each of the ten on the mat once and write 0 in the Left at the end box.

Shape: Show the second removal on the same strip, so the two parts make the whole 19. Practice begins with a visible start and physically separates what goes. Reserve the folded record.

- **G.** Tries to take all 19 again from the ten on the mat and runs out.

## E62

How can this ten show what stays when 7 come off 14?

7 stay. Take 4 and then 3, or take all 7 from the ten and retain the other four.

Shape: independently choose where to remove from a given ten-and-ones picture. Practice compares worked approaches on paper and traces a changed quantity.

- **G.** Takes off 4 and then a fresh 7, leaving 3.

## E63

17 take away 16. How could both pictures show what is left?

1. Cross off 16, then connect the remaining dot to the one new count, 17, from the held 16.

Shape: Build both records and match the leftover to the new count. Practice compares two completed methods on separate surfaces. Reserve this pair of panels.

- **A.** Makes 2 new steps by counting 16 itself.
