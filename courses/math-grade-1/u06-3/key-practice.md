# Practice key

Teacher copy. Read each prompt aloud and show its matching model after children try.

## s06-P118-retrieve

How many squares are in this collection?

**Answer.** 18. Move the eighteen loose squares one at a time and mark the count.

## s06-P119-more

What is ten more than 94?

**Answer.** 104. Join one ten-strip to the known collection.

## s06-P120-add

What is 61 + 27?

**Answer.** 88.

## s06-P120-more

What is ten more than 93?

**Answer.** 103.
