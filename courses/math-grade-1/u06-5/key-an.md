# Number record key

Teacher copy. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Date later help and retain the first paper.

## s06-AN-count1

Can you count from 88 through 102?

**Answer.** 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 93, 94, 95, 96, 97, 98, 99, 100, 101, 102. Record the actual spoken sequence and any help. Describe a skipped or repeated numeral without an error letter.

## s06-AN-count2

Can you count from 117 through 120?

**Answer.** 117, 118, 119, 120. Record the spoken sequence and any help.

## s06-AN-read3

**Answer.** One hundred twenty. Show only the single numeral card. Record the spoken numeral.

## s06-AN-more

What is ten more than 96?

**Answer.** 106. Record the spoken result. No ten-strip is in view.

- 97 because ten adds one single. Error K. Adds a ten as one one.

## s06-AN-write1

What numeral gives the whole count?

**Answer.** 112. Trace groups or mark a count path through all eight rows of fourteen.

- 113 after counting a square twice. Error G. Counts one square twice.

## s06-AN-write2

How many squares are here altogether?

**Answer.** 107. Eight tens and three rows of nine account for the whole.

- 1007 after finding one hundred seven. Error F. Appends ones to 100.

## s06-AN-write3

How many squares are tied up here?

**Answer.** 120. Count each of twelve full bundles once.

- 130 after counting a bundle twice. Error G. Counts one bundle twice.
