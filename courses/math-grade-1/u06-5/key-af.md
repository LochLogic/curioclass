## s06-AF-short

What length belongs on the short strip's tag?

**Answer.** 11 units. Mark eleven equal touching lengths across the whole strip.

- 10 pieces with open spaces. Error C. Accepts gaps.

## s06-AF-long

What length belongs on the long strip's tag?

**Answer.** 16 units. Mark sixteen equal touching lengths across the whole strip.

- 17 pieces with overlap. Error D. Accepts doubled coverage.

## s06-AF-extend

How many more units does the short strip need to reach the end of the long strip?

**Answer.** 5 more units; 11 + 5 = 16 or 16 − 11 = 5. Align both starts and link the shorter strip and marked extension to the whole longer strip.

- 16 more units. Error J. Uses the longer whole as the added part.
