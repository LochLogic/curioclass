# Practice key

Teacher copy. Read each prompt aloud and show its matching model after children try.

## s06-P127-leaves

Rin adds two paper leaves to six. How many are there now?

**Answer.** 8 leaves. Invented for practice. Show the before and after groups on a blank mat.

## s06-P127-trails

A paper fox trail is nine units long. A paper hare trail is two units shorter. How long is the hare trail?

**Answer.** 7 units. Invented for practice. Mark the known length and the two-unit difference.

## s06-P128-cover

A tray holds 12 cards. Five are circles. How many are triangles?

**Answer.** 7 triangles. Invented for practice. Use familiar practice after the transfer and assessment revisions.
