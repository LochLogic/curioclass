## s06-X126a

What numeral belongs on the tag?

**Answer.** 113. Mark the eleven tens and three singles once each.

- 114 after recounting a single. Error G. Counts a single twice.
- 123 after recounting a ten. Error G. Counts a ten twice.

## s06-X126b

How would you mark this strip so someone can tell its length?

**Answer.** 4 equal touching intervals span the strip. Mark the joints and cover the whole strip.

- 3 units with spaces. Error C. Counts a gapped row.
- 5 overlapping units. Error D. Counts overlapping units.

## s06-X127a

What length belongs on the puppy's path tag?

**Answer.** 19 units. One ten and nine touching singles is one valid arrangement.

- 18 units with gaps. Error C. Accepts gaps.
- 20 units with an overlap. Error D. Accepts overlap.

## s06-X127b

The puppy path is nineteen units long. The plant path is shorter by the length of this row. How would your bars show the plant path?

**Answer.** 12 units; 19 − 7 = 12. Combine 2, 2 and 3 as the difference. Align bars; the unknown spans the whole shorter bar.

- 7 is the plant path. Error J. Uses the difference as the smaller whole.
- Plant bar is 19 with a separate 7. Error J. Fails to remove the extra from the larger whole.
