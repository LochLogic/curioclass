# Keep and finish the records

Use familiar paper examples for feedback. Close worked bars before independent checks. Keep the original marks, private feedback and dated revisions. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen for every component. Keep oral count and reading evidence separate from writing and collection matching.

Day 126 finishes the length comparisons, written inventory and first fifteen number turns. Day 127 finishes the number turns and gathers the measurement arrangements and paper judgments. On day 128 use the retained work and the three separate transfer prompts. Collect both measured strips before naming their given lengths for the last prompt. Day 129 returns the final papers, and days 129 and 130 use the named repair centers.

Large adult-cut equal paper lengths replace cubes, and paper squares replace counters. Check the units against the printed spans. Keep rows on tables and the walkways clear. When a paper path is missing, draw only the givens, with matched unit sizes.
