# Unit 1 record sheet

Use one row per child. Write the date and support used beside each component: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No points. Paper marks cover everyone; a correct numeral alone never proves a count-on strategy. Blank means not collected, not a judgment. Give an unmatched response a description instead of an error letter.

The rotating six: days 1/6/11 hear names 1 to 6; days 2/7/12 hear 7 to 12; days 3/8/13 hear 13 to 18; days 4/9/14 hear 19 to 24; days 5/10/15 hear 25 to 30. One ask per child at 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes in Synthesis. Each public turn may be passed; make a private offer later. Unheard language stays not yet seen until new evidence is heard, never inferred from a partner.

C1 is different: half A, names 1 to 15, day 14; half B, names 16 to 30, day 15. At most 30 seconds for each of its two asks, one minute per child. Keep dated verbatim count, paper marks, written feedback and second-color revision. Half A revises day 15, half B day 16. No second interview. D1 retains the exact group raw list, original display, written note and day 16 revision; score afterward. Score the fresh D2 product after day 16, with 30 teacher minutes for reading and records. Return one packet in the day 17/18 centers.

Keep an extra component sheet for each child with the item ids below. Each item has its own cell for date, paper evidence, heard words if any, support, marking, feedback, revision date and next action.
