# Practice answer key

Teacher only; keep apart from cards and child pages. All cases invented for practice.

## Day 1

6 + 1 = 7; 3 + 3 = 6; 4 + 2 = 6; 2 + 3 = 5; 5 + 1 = 6; 7 + 1 = 8; 

## Day 2

5 + 1 = 6; 3 + 3 = 6; 7 + 3 = 10; 1 + 6 = 7; 4 + 2 = 6; 8 + 2 = 10; 

## Day 3

3 + 1 = 4; 6 + 1 = 7; 9 + 1 = 10; 2 + 1 = 3; 3 + 2 = 5; 4 + 2 = 6; 6 + 1 = 7; 1 + 2 = 3; 

## Day 4

10 - 1 = 9; 5 - 1 = 4; 7 - 1 = 6; 5 - 2 = 3; 4 - 2 = 2; 3 - 1 = 2; 9 - 1 = 8; 8 - 2 = 6; 

## Day 5

6 + 1 = 7; 9 + 1 = 10; 7 - 1 = 6; 4 + 3 = 7; 8 + 2 = 10; 6 - 1 = 5; 5 - 2 = 3; 10 - 1 = 9; 

## Day 6

5 + 1 = 6; 3 + 2 = 5; 8 + 2 = 10; 4 + 2 = 6; 9 + 1 = 10; 7 + 3 = 10; 10 - 2 = 8; 6 - 1 = 5; 5 - 2 = 3; 9 - 1 = 8; 4 - 2 = 2; 10 - 3 = 7;
