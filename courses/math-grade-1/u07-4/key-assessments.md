# Section 7.4 assessment key

Keep this key with the teacher. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Leave an unheard response open. Record language separately when a picture already shows the mathematics.

**s07-AS1-c2.** What label belongs on one piece of this circle? Half of the circle. Accept the matching label on either share of the diagonal-diameter circle. Read every option aloud.

**s07-AS1-c4.** What label belongs on one piece of the second circle? One fourth of or one quarter of the circle. Accept either label on any sector. Save the first label before giving the later synonym prompt.

**s07-AS1-csize.** Circle the smaller share on the circle pair. The fourth of the circle. Compare the child’s marks in the matching wholes. Error I: Circles the half because two shares make smaller pieces.

**s07-AS1-r2.** Mark half of this rectangle. Marks one of the two triangular halves. Either region on either side of the single diagonal has equal area. Error E: Marks a subregion that is smaller than a half and calls it equal to the rest.

**s07-AS1-r4.** Mark a quarter of this rectangle. Marks any one of the four triangles. A center line and one diagonal in each half make four matching triangles; any one is a quarter. Stacking checks it. Error E: Marks a small corner of one triangle and calls all the shares equal.

**s07-AS1-rsize.** Circle the smaller share on the rectangle pair. The marked quarter of the rectangle. Both rectangles match. Compare only the marked pieces. Error I: Circles the half because a quarter has the larger share count.

**s07-AS1-halves.** The whole circle is two ___. Halves. Read the sentence with the blank and accept the placed word or a word label.

**s07-AS1-fourths.** The other whole circle is four ___. Fourths or quarters. Either plural word names the four equal shares of the partitioned circle.

**s07-AS1-quarter.** What is another name for that share? One quarter of after one fourth of, or the reverse. Score only an alternate name after saving the first label. Accept the single word or the alternate phrase card.

**s07-AS1-why.** How can you show why your circled piece is smaller? Divides the half along its symmetry line into two equal fourths of the same circle. Accept the valid dividing mark or exact neutral transcription. The wholes stay the same size. Error I: Says that the fourth is larger because four is more than two.

**s07-AR4-ah-read.** What time does this first clock show? Nine o’clock. Hear the individual read. Accept nine when it clearly denotes the hour. Error G: Calls it twelve o’clock from the long hand.

**s07-AR4-ah.** Write the first clock’s time in digits. 9:00. On the independent sheet, read short hand at 9, long hand at 12. Error G: Writes 12:00 from the long hand.

**s07-AR4-a30-read.** What time does the second clock show? Half past ten, or ten thirty. Hear the half-hour from the reserved face. Accept the learned spoken name on day 143. Error G: Calls it six o’clock from the long hand.

**s07-AR4-a30.** Write the second clock’s time in digits. 10:30. The short hand has passed 10; the long hand at 6 means half past. Day 143 papers are descriptive practice only. Error H: Writes 10:06.

**s07-AR4-dh-read.** What time do these first numbers show? Seven o’clock. Hear the reading of 7:00, separate from drawing.

**s07-AR4-dh.** Draw the hands for the first digit card. Long hand at 12 and short hand at 7. Judge both hands on the independent blank face. Error G: Draws the short hand at 12 and long hand at 7.

**s07-AR4-d30-read.** What time do these last numbers show? Half past one, or one thirty. Hear the reading of 1:30. Day 143 speech is descriptive practice only. Error H: Says six minutes past one because half past points to 6.

**s07-AR4-d30.** Draw the hands for the last digit card. Long hand at 6, short hand halfway from 1 to 2. Day 143 paper is descriptive. On later collection days accept intended halfway placement. Error F: Draws the short hand directly on 1. Error G: Draws the short hand at 6 and the long one at the midpoint.

**s07-AS2-write.** Write this clock’s time in digits. 8:30. Long at 6 shows half past; the short hand has moved halfway from 8 to 9. Error H: Writes 8:06.

**s07-AS2-draw.** Draw the hands for the time on this card. Long hand at 6, short hand halfway from 11 to 12. Judge both lengths and placements on the blank face. Error F: Draws the short hand exactly at 11. Error G: Draws the short hand at 6 and the long one at the midpoint.
