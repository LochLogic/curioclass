# Read both clock hands and close the unit record

Review half with a paper strip and a half-turn of a circle. Watch the long minute hand make one turn while the short hour hand moves only to the next number. At a whole hour, the long hand points to 12 and :00 shows zero minutes past the hour. At a half-hour, the long hand points to 6 and the short hand is halfway to the next number. Move both hands together. Teach the spoken time on 142 and the :30 notation on 143.

For error G distinguish the long minute hand and short hour hand by length before trying a fresh case. For error F move the short hand steadily while the long hand makes the half-turn. For error H connect the half-turn to 30 minutes without copying the numeral at the end of the long hand. Use a plain teaching clock, adult-secured paper hands or pencil snapshots. Cover the pivot and remove loose parts. Say aloud that the event examples were invented for practice.

Use the motion cards, practice faces and event sheet before withdrawing worked examples for the R4 records. Give each assigned child four brief oral reads, 15 seconds each, and allow 30 seconds to record and change places. Give six separate quiet paper minutes to draw or write. Keep each group’s cards private. Day 143’s new half-hour notation work is descriptive. On 146 collect S1 and S2 in 16 minutes, join the retained evidence and return exit feedback. Leave missing components open. Use the paper portfolio on 147 and 148 and reserve the response cases for 150.
