# Section 7.4 practice key

Teacher only. On 141, the long hand is at 12 on all whole-hour faces and the short hand points to the named hour. On 142, the short hand at half past four is midway from 4 to 5 and the long hand points to 6. At five o’clock, the short hand has reached 5 and the long hand is back at 12. On 143, half past four is 4:30. The face for 3:30 has the long hand at 6 and the short hand midway from 3 to 4. On 144, correct Music to short at 10 and long at 12. Library has short midway from 3 to 4 and long at 6. On 145, add the long hand at 12 to the practice face. On 146, the tilted rectangle has four square corners and the slanted four-sided shape does not. The printed rows have 5 and 3 marks, 8 altogether.
