# Section 7.4 exit key

Keep this key with the teacher. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Leave an unheard response open. Record language separately when a picture already shows the mathematics.

**s07-X141a.** Mark the hour hand. Marks the short hand, pointing to 4. Use hand length or an accurate tactile mark. Error G: Marks the long hand at 12 as the hour hand.

**s07-X141b.** Write the time on this face in digits. 4:00. Read the hour from the short hand and use :00 since the long hand is at 12. Error G: Writes 12:00 using the long hand as the hour hand.

**s07-X141c.** What time does the clock show? Four o’clock. Record only individually heard words. Accept four, or four o’clock, if the clock reading is clear. Error G: Says twelve o’clock from the long hand.

**s07-X142a.** Draw the hands for half past five. Long hand at 6 and short hand halfway between 5 and 6. Judge both placements and the relative lengths. Accept intended midpoint. Error F: Draws the short hand right on 5. Error G: Draws the short hand at 6 and long hand at the midpoint.

**s07-X142b.** Show why the short hand belongs there. Marks half the gap from 5 to 6 or shows that half the hour has passed. Accept a midpoint mark with movement forward from 5 or a neutral transcription accompanying the child’s clock. Error F: Leaves the hour hand fixed on 5 through the half hour.

**s07-X143a.** Match each hand to its job. Short hand to hour, long hand to minute. Match the word labels to the hands on the paper clock. Error G: Links the long hand to hour.

**s07-X143b.** Write the time. 6:30. The long hand shows the half hour. The short hand has passed 6 and has not reached 7. Error H: Writes 6:06 from the numeral below the long hand.

**s07-X144a.** Draw the clock for art. Long hand at 6 and short hand halfway from 2 to 3. The complete drawing translates the stated event time. Error F: Puts the short hand exactly at 2. Error G: Puts the short hand at 6 and long hand halfway from 2 to 3.

**s07-X144b.** Write the assembly time in digits. 3:00. The short hand at 3 names the hour. The long hand at 12 means :00. Error G: Writes 12:00 using the minute hand as the hour hand.

**s07-X145a.** Draw a copy of this joined piece. A second intact offset pair of equal squares. Both copy squares share part of a side with the offset preserved. Error D: Draws the copied squares apart.

**s07-X145b.** How many squares are there altogether? 4 squares. Count each square once in the two separate motifs. Record counting slips descriptively.

**s07-X145c.** Write the time on this clock in digits. 12:30. The short hand has left 12 and has not reached 1. The long hand at 6 gives :30. Error H: Writes 12:06, using the dial numeral as elapsed minutes.
