# Section 7.3 practice key

Teacher only. Match and stack the folded paper. The equal parts cover the whole exactly once. The centered S-cut circle has two equal areas related by a half-turn. Matching loose fourths cover one detached half when their boundaries line up. In the missing-share practice the rectangular gap is one fourth and the circular gap is one half.
