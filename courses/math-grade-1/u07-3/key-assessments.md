# Section 7.3 assessment key

Keep this key with the teacher. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Leave an unheard response open. Record language separately when a picture already shows the mathematics.

**s07-AR3-c2.** Draw two equal shares in this circle. A diameter or another division into two equal areas. Check the whole is covered once and the intended areas are equal. Verify after the attempt with the matching overlay where applicable. Error E: Draws a chord near the rim and calls both areas equal.

**s07-AR3-c4.** Draw four equal shares in this circle. Two perpendicular diameters or another division into four equal areas. Check the whole is covered once and the intended areas are equal. Verify after the attempt with the matching overlay where applicable. Error E: Draws radial cuts with a clearly narrower sector and calls all areas equal.

**s07-AR3-r2.** Draw two equal shares in this rectangle. A midline, a diagonal or another division into two equal areas. Check the whole is covered once and the intended areas are equal. Verify after the attempt with the matching overlay where applicable. Error E: Draws a line near one short edge and calls the unequal parts equal.

**s07-AR3-r4.** Draw four equal shares in this rectangle. Four equal strips, a centered cross or another division into four equal areas. Check the whole is covered once and the intended areas are equal. Verify after the attempt with the matching overlay where applicable. Error E: Draws four strips of visibly unequal widths and calls them equal.
