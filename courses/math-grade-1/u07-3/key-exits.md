# Section 7.3 exit key

Keep this key with the teacher. Use shown, shown with help, and not yet seen. Leave an unheard response open. Record language separately when a picture already shows the mathematics.

**s07-X137a.** Mark the pictures that show equal shares. Marks the stair-step circle and the rectangle with four equal strips only. The stair-step halves match after a half-turn. Test equality of area and use the entire whole. Error E: Marks the off-center cap and remainder as equal.

**s07-X137b.** Where should this line go to make the shares equal? Draws a straight line across the circle through its center. Any diameter works. Allow intended equality despite motor imprecision. Error E: Redraws the off-center chord and calls its shares equal.

**s07-X137c.** Where should the dashed line go? Midway between the two solid dividers. Keep both solid dividers. The repaired rectangle has four equal-width strips. Error E: Leaves the uneven dividers and calls the four strips equal.

**s07-X138a.** Label one share of this rectangle. Half of the rectangle. Accept a half label on either horizontal band. Read labels aloud. Error E: Marks a narrow sliver and treats it and the rest as equal halves.

**s07-X138b.** Label one share of this circle. A fourth of or a quarter of the circle. Mark one sector and accept either name. Error E: Labels three sectors as half and says they equal the remaining sector.

**s07-X138c.** How many like this make the rectangle? Two halves. Mark or write 2 beside the indicated half. A child can mark the chosen numeral.

**s07-X138d.** How many like this make the circle? Four fourths or four quarters. Mark or write 4 beside the indicated quarter.

**s07-X139a-circle.** Which starred circle piece is smaller? The starred fourth of the circle. Compare only the starred shares of the matching disks. Error I: Marks the half as smaller because two is less than four.

**s07-X139a-rectangle.** Which starred rectangle piece is smaller? The starred fourth of the rectangle. Compare only the starred horizontal bands of the matching rectangles. Error I: Marks the half as smaller because fewer equal shares make smaller pieces.

**s07-X139b.** Show why the piece you picked is smaller. Shows that the starred half splits into two equal fourths of the same-sized circle. Accept a line through the star-marked half to match fourths or exact neutral transcription on the paper. File as descriptive first-teaching evidence. Error I: Says a fourth is larger because four is more than two.

**s07-X140a.** Label one piece of this rectangle. A quarter of or a fourth of the rectangle. One of the four equal cells has the chosen label.

**s07-X140b.** Which marked piece is smaller? The marked quarter in the cross-divided rectangle. Use the indicated quarter and half from matching rectangles. Error I: Marks the half because fewer equal pieces must be smaller.
