# Measure with equal units

Start with the side length of one cube. Repeat that length along the whole edge. When equal units touch end to end, their count names the length in those units. Place the first unit at the object’s end and mark each joint before writing the count. For the exploration on day 117, two smaller units cover the same span as one double-length unit; the object stays the same.

Use adult-cut equal paper tabs in place of cubes or clips. Keep rows on tables and keep pieces out of mouths. Check the piece against the actual printout because scaling can shift. When children need a larger grip, cut larger tabs and make a matching strip. A pencil trace gives an independent paper path.

For error C, bring the equal pieces together so no length is left open. For error D, slide overlapping pieces until the ends meet. For error E, choose one length unit before counting. For error G, trace each piece once. Correct a practice arrangement, then give a different span for the retry.
