# Practice key

Teacher copy. Read each prompt aloud and show its matching model after children try.

## s06-E115-model

How long is the bookmark in these units?

**Answer.** 10 units. Begin with two joined units. Extend with eight more touching units. Mark every boundary.

## s06-P117-compare

How many small pieces cover one large piece?

**Answer.** 2 small pieces.
