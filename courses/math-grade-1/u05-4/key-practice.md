# Practice key

Teacher copy.

## P106-orders

Try both orders for 38 plus 24.

62 in both orders.

The eight and four loose ones make twelve. The three and two old tens make five tens. Either order gives 50 + 12 = 62.

## P107-caption

Match a step to the pieces for 65 plus 12.

77. One route is 65 + 10 = 75; 75 + 2 = 77.

Join the ten to the whole starting amount, then add two loose ones. Accept equivalent matched steps.

## P109-cart

What is the cart's new label with 26 books arriving?

91 books.

The twenty-six-book delivery adds fourteen compared with twelve. 77 + 14 = 91. A fresh check is 65 + 20 = 85, then 85 + 6 = 91.

## P110-close

Where is the new ten in 44 plus 8?

Ten of the twelve loose ones become one new strip. 52.

Familiar, unscored closure. Children mark a group on their own sketch. The rotating six explain one link privately.
