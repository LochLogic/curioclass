## s05-X108

How did you put these two groups together?

**Answer.** 72. 49 + 1 = 50, then 50 + 22 = 72. Or 40 + 20 = 60, 9 + 3 = 12, 60 + 12 = 72.

Accept a picture or a marked equality path showing a new ten, a first move and the finish. In adding on, 23 splits into 1 and 22.

Shown: Moves one of the 23 to 49, then accounts for two tens and two remaining loose ones. Not yet seen: Shows 62 after dropping the new ten (A).

- 62 (A). Drops the new ten.
- 82 (B). Keeps the exchanged singles and their strip.

## s05-X109

A child counts 38 books on one shelf and 29 on another. How many books are on the two shelves together?

**Answer.** 67 books. 30 + 20 = 50, 8 + 9 = 17, 50 + 17 = 67.

Accept a single linked record with 67 books and evidence of a new ten from seventeen loose ones. Read the story and question aloud.

Shown: Marks ten of the seventeen loose ones into a new strip and records 67 books. Not yet seen: Marks 57, leaving out the ten represented by the circled group (A).

- 57 (A). Loses the exchanged ten.
- 77 (B). Leaves the ten loose ones in the total as well as their strip.
