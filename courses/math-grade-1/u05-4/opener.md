# Explaining and checking addition

The Addition record comes before practice and the first private rotation. Cover practice models, read one stem at a time and retain dated originals. In the rotation hear each child once on P5, with the original in front of the child. The child can say, sign or point to the link. Record the sum, model, written method and reason separately. Keep a missed turn open. Use familiar cart labels to practice during the rotation. All book counts are invented for practice.

Keep the fresh packing label out of sight until day 110. Save any revision separately from the original and date the help. For A and B, rebuild the exchange without losing or keeping a second copy of the ten. For C and D, count each strip as ten and keep the singles visible. For E, use one true equality at a time. Return marked papers privately on day 111. Use separate practice during the two later centers.

Supply paper, pencils, adult-precut marked strips and large paper squares if there are no connecting cubes. A folded sheet can stand in for a mat. Handwrite cards or draw the groups when there is no printer. Keep loose pieces on trays and out of mouths; an adult counts them back in. Read all stems aloud. Use enlarged or raised cells and accept child-directed marks. Offer a private turn to a child who passes. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.
