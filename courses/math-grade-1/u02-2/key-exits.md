# Daily exit key

Teacher copy. Read each exact stem from its learner piece. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Record a letter only when the picture or the child's words show that reasoning.

## E22

What equation shows the whole tank?

**Answer.** 20 fish; 14 + 6 = 20 or 6 + 14 = 20, in either equality orientation.

The invented tank has fourteen striped fish and six spotted fish. Marks show the two groups inside one whole.

Shape: Generate an equation for a whole from two pictured categories.

- **H.** 19 fish. Includes fourteen as the first added count.

## E23

What two parts could make this whole?

**Answer.** Any one split totaling 10, for example 10 striped and 0 spotted.

The invented tank has ten fish, each striped or spotted. Mark two disjoint counts totaling ten. An empty part is valid.

Shape: Invent one labeled split without a supplied starting split.

- **G.** 5 striped and 6 spotted. The two parts make eleven instead of ten.

## E24

How many triangles belong here?

**Answer.** 9 triangles; 4 + 9 = 13.

Thirteen shapes are circles or triangles. Four are circles. Mark the unpictured category with nine triangles or a matching numeral.

Shape: Infer a missing category in a static whole.

- **D.** 17 triangles. Adds the whole and known part.

- **H.** 10 triangles. Counts four as a first new step.

## E25

Which drawing shows this story?

**Answer.** The unchanged whole with 5 circles and 9 squares.

Fourteen paper shapes include five circles and the rest are squares. Identify the matching mat by marking its known circle group. Use three equally styled layouts and vary position across copies.

Shape: Recognize a fixed whole from three pictures, one of which shows removal.

- **D.** Chooses the mat with 5 circles and 19 squares. Adds fourteen and five to find the missing category.

## E26

What addition shows the covered part?

**Answer.** 5 + 15 = 20 or equivalent, with 15 identified.

The card shows five of twenty counters with a cover for the others and 20 - 5 = □. Mark the covered group or record the related addition in the picture.

Shape: Generate an addition relation from a subtraction relation on a static picture.

- **D.** 25 hidden. Adds whole and known part.

- **H.** 16 hidden. Includes five as a new step.
