# Daily exit key

Teacher copy. Read each exact stem from its learner piece. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Record a letter only when the picture or the child's words show that reasoning.

## E37

What small story makes this fact true?

**Answer.** One actor starts with 13, adds 1 and ends with 14.

Use 13 + 1 = □. Example: Tia adds one ticket to thirteen on a tray. Read the equation aloud.

Shape: Invent a story to complete the given addition fact.

- **H.** 13 pictured at the end. Includes the start as the added step.

## E38

What difference fits all three boxes?

**Answer.** 10 unmatched triangles; 10 fits each linked box.

Display fourteen triangle, four circle and two square choices. Link the extra to 4 + □ = 14, 14 - 4 = □ and 14 = 4 + □. Accept one marked group and one numeral carried through the three links.

Shape: Transfer a single difference across three layouts after selecting two relevant categories.

- **E.** 14 marked as the difference. Rings the larger category.

- **A.** Rejects 14 = 4 + □ with 10 placed. Rejects equality because the total comes first.
