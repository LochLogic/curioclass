# Section 2.5 opener

## Review and retained evidence

Day 37 returns to taught fact practice. Day 38 uses the original three-category display to connect one difference to more than one equation. Continue the paper path on both days. Day 39 gathers the work with the revisions for the end-of-unit assessment.

## The mathematics

One unmatched quantity serves as both the missing addend and the difference. The related equations each compare equal values. Confer once with each child using the dated comparison page. Pre-open the pages. Hear half A day 37 and half B day 38, one minute a child. Keep the written note and the child's paper revision.

## Errors to listen for

E: distinguish the extra from the larger group. D: rebuild the whole from its parts. A: read the equal sign in both orientations. Use the unit register for other errors and teacher feedback.

Use the correct example first, then the folded-paper puppet's invented error. Return feedback privately. Record one rotating group of six at 45 seconds each in Synthesis and collect paper marks from everyone. A child may pass in public talk. Read every stem aloud and offer large dark outlines or a scribe who makes only the marks the child directs. Mark evidence as shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

## Supplies

Familiar fact cards, paper data cards, individual frames and sketches, conference note sheet, dated Story booklet, equation strips and day-specific slips. Hand-draw the exact display if necessary. An absent child uses a separate make-up sitting with the original paper.

Use adult-cut paper pieces larger than a small-parts tube. Keep them on trays and collect them after use. Every task can be drawn on paper. Keep the teacher keys and assessment pieces closed until their named parts.
