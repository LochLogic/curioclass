# Section 1.4: Our survey and check

Days 14 and 15 rehearse already taught work and gather retained evidence. Day 16 brings one fresh data product and written revisions, with no new individual interview or reteaching. Day lengths are 60 minutes; the paper tally model is withdrawn from the shared display on day 16.

C1 has two separate asks on one pre-marked paper card: count on for addition and count steps for subtraction. Half A, 15 children, is heard day 14; half B day 15. Thirty seconds per ask means one minute per child, 15 minutes per half. Keep dated verbatim counts and marks. Written feedback after class, half A revises day 15 and half B day 16, five minutes each; no second oral count. Score the revised paper with the retained count.

D1 uses three of six original imaginary-animal pictures. Groups of 15 gather one anonymous slip each, or an optional pretend response. Missing children are represented by pretend slips said aloud to be invented. Save the exact mixed raw list, never a preset count distribution. Each child makes a separate display. Feedback after day 15, five-minute revision day 16, then score. Day 15 questions are practice; D2 supplies the scored question evidence.

D2 on day 16 is fresh. Read every question-composition ask separately from its answer ask. Picture questions are accepted, with at least one original wording or drawing beyond a copied frame. Twenty-five minutes covers the whole product. One scale throughout: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, by component. Do not use points or infer a method from a correct number alone.

Errors A to H use the unit register and earlier moves: hold start; count steps; keep a counted group; put each response in one category; omit none; match extras; label groups; count responses rather than groups. Unmatched errors get descriptions. Return day 15 exits with the day 16 packet in unit 2 centers: day 17 for A/B/C, day 18 for D/E/F/G/H, ten minutes each, five teacher-group and five transfer minutes. These slots are already reserved in unit 2. An unfinished day 15 third exit component goes to day 17 too.

Before class print counting-check.md and teacher-only key-assessment.md; keep closed until the assigned observation. Day 15: survey-slips.md and survey-record.md; preserve raw list, original, feedback and revision. Day 16: cloud-check.md, question frames if needed, and known practice-puzzles.md from u01-2 for unscored finishing. Print the day's half exit; day 15 also uses exit-box-15.md. No day 16 exit. Keys stay separate from child pages. Count children who pass as not yet heard, not wrong; arrange private access without a repeated interview.

Paper slips, folded sheets and large adult-cut squares replace every purchased material. Supervise handling, no food or sharp edges. A printer or screen is optional: copy the exact figures and read prompts aloud. No choice asks about home, money, bodies or religion. No visitor or outside picture is needed.


## Hearing and return

Use the rotating-six schedule in u01-1/record.md: one 45-second ask each within five-minute Synthesis, all pairs otherwise work concurrently. Names not heard today have their next slot five days later; hearing is new evidence. Each child marks a paper exit now. Before class name the current teacher-only answer key, and reveal no held item or whole assessment format in practice. Return every judged paper on the day in handbacks.csv, using only the named part's minutes. The ten-minute group for kindergarten retrieval is distinct from the five-minute exit repair. At home the adult is the partner and may hear the child privately.
