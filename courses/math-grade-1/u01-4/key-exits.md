# Exit answer key

Teacher only. Before class, print and keep apart from child tickets. Read all prompts aloud; paper marks are evidence for every child. Shown, shown with help, not yet seen; a described unmatched answer gets no guessed letter.

## s01-X14a

Mark how you count all three groups altogether.

Answer: 8; keep 5, mark 6, 7, 8.

Flags 5, hooks 2, zigzags 1. Preserve a starting group. The picture labels are separated from response marks.

Shown: 5 held; 6, 7, 8 marked | Not yet seen: 5, 6, 7, error A

- 7 with five counted as first step: error A. Counts start.
- All marks numbered 1 to 8: error C. Recounts the starting group.
- 3: error H. Counts groups.

## s01-X14b

Mark how many fewer hooks than flags.

Answer: 3.

Three unmatched flags or three steps from 2 to 5.

Shown: 3 with matching or steps | Not yet seen: 5 with no steps, error F

- 5 with no step marks: error F. Larger count used as difference.
- 5 with steps ending at 5: error B. Endpoint instead of three steps.

## s01-X15a

Make a labeled display.

Answer: Winged 8, finned 3, furry 1; picture labels.

Count every mixed slip once under its category picture. The shaggy creature is furry; the other creatures show wings or fins. Read these labels aloud without giving counts.

Shown: Three labeled groups with 8, 3, 1 marks | Not yet seen: Correct marks with no labels, error G

- A slip placed in two groups: error D. Overlapping membership.
- One slip left out: error E. One response omitted.
- No labels: error G. Groups cannot be identified.

## s01-X15b

Mark how many altogether.

Answer: 12.

Count all marks once; count-on method may be visible but this ask judges the total.

Shown: 12 | Not yet seen: 11 with start counted, error A

- 11 with eight counted as a step: error A. Counts start.
- 3: error H. Counts groups.

## s01-X15c

Mark how many more winged than finned.

Answer: 5.

Student-page Exit box, revealed only at exit; count five unmatched winged marks or five steps from 3 to 8.

Shown: 5 | Not yet seen: 8, larger count, error F

- 8 without step marks: error F. Larger count used as difference.
- 8 with step marks ending at 8: error B. Endpoint instead of five steps.
