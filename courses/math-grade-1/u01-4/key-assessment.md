# End-of-unit answer key

Teacher only. Before class print and keep apart from the child check. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, by component; no points. C1 uses retained dated verbatim counts and revised pictures. D1 is scored only after day 16 revision. Read numerical answers during Check answers with pencils down, after every D2 paper is closed.

## s01-C1a

Mark how you count 11 + 3.

Answer: 14; hold 11 then count 12, 13, 14; holding 3 and counting 4 through 14 is also valid.

Three new words after 11, with original paper, dated verbatim count, feedback and revision kept together. Hear half A day 14 and half B day 15, two 30-second asks per child; no repeat interview. Revise A day 15 and B day 16; final scoring day 16. Accept either held addend with the matching number of new steps. A numeral alone does not show the method.

Shown: 11 held; 12, 13, 14 | Not yet seen: 11, 12, 13, error A

- 13 with eleven counted as first added step: error A. Counts start.
- Counts the whole pictured start from one: error C. Does not keep a counted group.

## s01-C1b

Mark how you count 16 - 12.

Answer: 4; four steps up from 12 to 16, or twelve steps back from 16 to 4.

Count up: 13, 14, 15, 16 are four steps after 12. Count back: twelve steps after 16 end at 4. Accept either shown method. Keep original and revision together. Hear half A day 14 and half B day 15, two 30-second asks per child; no repeat interview. Revise A day 15 and B day 16; final scoring day 16.

Shown: four up-step marks with answer 4, or twelve back steps ending at 4 | Not yet seen: 16 reported after four up steps, error B

- 5 steps including twelve: error A. Only for counting up from 12: counting the held start makes five rather than four steps. Do not apply this label to a valid backward count.
- 16, with four steps shown: error B. Reports endpoint.

## s01-D1

Make a labeled display of our slips.

Answer: One mark per raw response, three picture-labeled groups; total 15. Counts depend on retained raw list.

Use exactly the group's raw list; every slip goes in exactly one category. Feedback after class; five-minute written revision day 16; score afterward. Compare each component with the raw list, never a preset distribution.

Shown: for a scoring-only list 7, 6, 2, labeled groups with 7, 6, 2 marks. | Not yet seen: 6, 6, 2 after one response omitted, error E.

- A response in two categories: error D. One slip belongs to two groups.
- One response omitted: error E. Marks do not cover the raw list.
- Correct marks without labels: error G. Groups not identifiable.

Judge against the child's immediately preceding comparison question. Kites 6, puffs 2, spirals 3. Match or count the unmatched marks. A wrong larger count gets F only when the chosen groups and evidence support that interpretation.

Shown: 4, 3 or 1 with the corresponding chosen comparison | Not yet seen: larger group count used as the difference, error F

- 3 as the larger count for spirals and puffs: error F. Three spirals compared with two puffs have one unmatched response, not three.
