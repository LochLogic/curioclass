# Day 157: Revising with evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return every draft with private feedback-sheet cues before class. Prepare cards-157 directions, evidence-sheet columns and proposal-sheet checklist. Read opener, teacher-key and current answers. Keep the new exit sealed. Set aside end-record for revised-product components.

### The day
#### 5 min. Read feedback
Return day 156's s07-X156, s07-P156 and s07-F156. Children read private feedback and choose a needed change. Review proposal and feasibility. If many showed error Q, limits stop improvement, compare [[s07-K157-R]] for five minutes; written cues reach everyone. Otherwise confer with up to six while others mark their priority.

#### 20 min. Revise
Everyone attempts fresh [[s07-F157]] in the first five minutes; collect for day 158. Fifteen remain for independent [[s07-P157]]. Review policy-headings and sourcing: who made it, when and why. Check documented stages or honest gaps, reader, location, challenge, opportunity and feasible step. Use cards-157 directions, evidence-sheet notes, proposal-sheet and feedback-sheet fields. Locator and question-board retain the chosen school location and public need. No completed model is supplied. Keep draft, feedback and revision together.

#### 13 min. Share
Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute each to name a revision and its evidence. Up to six rotating volunteers may speak for forty-five seconds each, four and a half minutes total. Passing is allowed. The remaining six and a half minutes hold private reflection, reader-copy preparation and collection. All submit revisions. Sending is optional under student/teacher choice and school rules; classroom delivery is the fallback. No reply is promised. After class, allow thirty products times two minutes, sixty minutes. Score [[s07-P2-product]] from the collected revision. Use end-record's separate 0-to-2 component scale: 2 accurate and connected, 1 accurate but missing a connection, 0 absent or contradicted. Keep unobserved blank. Retain evidence for day 159; never score sending or agreement.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X157]] on a half-slip; say it is made up. Give ninety seconds independently, then collect. Read neutrally; accept short phrases or exact dictation. After class, thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen minutes. Use record's shown, shown with help, not yet seen scale. Return every paper on day 158.

### Other rooms
Copy blank revision fields. Retain the source and original draft beside the new writing.

### At home
**Swap.** Use plain paper and the saved draft.
**Skip.** Public sharing and external delivery.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 156 papers and your written cues. Compare [[s07-K157-R]] if needed.
- **20 min. Revise.** Try [[s07-F157]], then complete [[s07-P157]] independently.
  **Say.** "Which detail supports your change?" Expect a cited detail explaining the revision. Let your child keep the choice.
- **13 min. Share.** Be the reader. Keep draft, notes and revision together. A classroom copy is enough.
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X157]] before its answer. Keep papers for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim public comments, not individual revision or the exit. Longer: name a consequence still needing evidence.

### Words and supports
Review challenge, opportunity, feasibility and proposal. Allow home-language rehearsal, enlarged lines or exact dictation. Private feedback names a component rather than a child as an error.

### Tomorrow
Day 158 builds a claim banner with separate evidence notes. Return day 157 papers. If many show error F, request equals adoption, or error O, any body decides, use its protected ten-minute repair. Carry unresolved error Q cues too.

Ours. Original summaries and made-up cases; factual source records are in sources.csv.
