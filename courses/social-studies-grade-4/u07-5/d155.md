# Day 155: Choosing with evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare integrated cards-155 summaries and evidence-sheet/proposal-sheet columns. Read opener and current generated answer key; keep teacher-key separate. Return day 154 papers with private cues. Keep exits hidden. Today uses the made-up fallback; actual town advice remains unknown.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 154's s07-X154, s07-P154 and s07-F154. Review Return and repair. If many showed error G, copy everywhere, or error Q, limits stop progress, compare [[s07-K155-R]] for five minutes before revealing its explanation. Everyone attempts fresh [[s07-F155]] for five minutes. Otherwise confer with six while others follow written cues. Collect retries for day 156.

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Review grade 3's nomination card: a problem and who it touches, never my family. Alone, answer [[s07-RET155]]. Read papers during investigation; seek need, advice, alternatives and consequences. Review taking part: staying informed is one way to participate.

#### 22 min. Choice investigation
Say both school records are made up, not emergency guidance. Children choose either. Use [[s07-P155]] with proposal-sheet and evidence-sheet columns. Review sourcing: who made it, when and why; mark unknown dates honestly. Policy-headings name stages, not proof those stages happened. No complete proposal model. If retrieval misses are widespread, use ten minutes to contrast the chosen record's supported detail with an unsupported preference; twelve remain for choice. Otherwise use all twenty-two. Review question-board: add one public need. Locator keeps the chosen location at school. Collect each child's annotations; never ask for home information.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X155]] and [[s07-X155b]] together on a half-slip. Allow three minutes independently, then collect. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class: thirty papers times forty-five seconds, about twenty-three minutes. Record each exit on record's shown, shown with help, not yet seen scale. Return everything on day 156.

### Other rooms
Copy short records and headings onto paper. Point to evidence without a screen.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy records and blank columns onto paper.
**Skip.** The public question board.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 154 papers. Compare [[s07-K155-R]], then try [[s07-F155]].
- **4 min. Recall.** Your child answers [[s07-RET155]].
  **Say.** "What should we compare first?" Expect need, advice, choices and consequences.
- **22 min. Choose.** Read both made-up school records. Your child chooses and completes [[s07-P155]]. Point to supporting words; leave actual town advice unknown.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X155]] and [[s07-X155b]] before showing their keys. Keep all papers for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated sharing, not either choice or the exit. Longer: identify another unknown before recommending action.

### Words and supports
Review proposal, a plan the class could try, now a public request. Feasibility asks whether authority, time and available means fit a step. Allow enlarged text, rehearsal or exact dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 156 drafts for a chosen reader. Return day 155 papers. If many show error H, liking as evidence, protect five minutes comparing relevance and five on a fresh annotation. Missing source labels, error R, get private sourcing cues.

Ours. Original summaries and made-up cases; factual source records are in sources.csv.
