# Day 153: Document clues and missing stages

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-153 as Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, policy-headings, locator and question-board. Read opener. Keep teacher-key and exit-masters separate; current generated keys correct the older practice key. Return day 152 papers with private cues. Use repair-cards as the repair-record format. Keep the current exit covered.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 152's papers: `s07-X152`, `s07-X152b`, `s07-RET152`, `s07-P152` and `s07-F152`. Review Return and repair. If many show error O, one body controls everything, compare [[s07-K153-R]] for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others use private cues. All attempt fresh [[s07-F153]] for five minutes before feedback. Keep this to role-label evidence, not a combined authority/participation answer.

#### 5 min. Retrieval and orientation
Review day 152's policy-stage headings: problem, proposer, authorized adoption, later revision. Children recall meanings privately, then compare the printed headings. Review policy, an adopted public course of action, and sourcing: who made it, when and why. Review question-board's responses-decided question. Introduce no new model.

#### 17 min. Document investigation
Alone for seven minutes, children attempt [[s07-P153]], labeling separate evidence-sheet rows with policy-headings. Use FL1 and FL2 separately, without a completed four-stage narrative. In concurrent pairs, each partner takes one minute to point to a clue. Use eight minutes to revise missing-evidence marks. The study commission is an investigating body; the cards do not identify the proposer of exact wording. Taking effect is implementation evidence, not the missing authorization document. Public review does not prove a particular revision passed. Do not fill gaps from guesses or the teacher-only source's additional history.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Four rotating volunteers may locate one evidence detail, 45 seconds each, three minutes total. Record names, including passes. Everyone submits stage annotations in the remaining minute; papers provide cues for all thirty. Update question-board after class. No child is the example of a mistake.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X153]]. Give three minutes alone, then collect. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class, budget 30 children times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for status, authority and private cues. Use record's one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all papers on day 154.

### Other rooms
Copy individual source tags and blank headings on paper. Point without a projector. No live page, photograph or visitor is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Hand-copy source tags and headings without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** Public observations and the class board.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 152 papers. Compare [[s07-K153-R]], then try [[s07-F153]] before feedback.
- **5 min. Recall.** Ask your child to recall the four stage headings; show them afterward.
- **17 min. Investigate.** Your child tries [[s07-P153]]. Be the evidence partner after independent marks.
  **Say.** "Which stage lacks its own decision document?" Expect: authorized adoption; taking effect is implementation evidence.
- **4 min. Record.** Locate one clue; keep the annotations.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X153]] independently before showing its key. Keep all work for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated speaking, preserving every document and missing-evidence mark. Longer: refine the distinction between review and an enacted revision.

### Words and supports
Review problem, proposer, authorized adoption and revision from day 152. Use enlarged text, home-language rehearsal and exact dictation. Blank headings support organization without supplying answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 154 examines recovery challenges and openings abroad. Return day 153 papers. If many show error F, request equals adoption, use ten minutes: five comparing document features, five fresh feature labels. Do not repeat a status-verdict task.

### Credit
Ours. Original summaries from Sources FL1 and FL2. Role notices are made up.
