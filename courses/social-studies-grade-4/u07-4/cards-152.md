# Day 152 evidence cards

## AID

National disaster aid has eligibility rules. Where federal help is unavailable, state agencies or other groups may assist.

Source: United States government.

## FL1

After Hurricane Andrew exposed building-code weaknesses in 1992, Florida created a study commission in 1996. A statewide code took effect in 2002.

Source: Florida Senate.

## FL2

Florida's commission uses public comments and technical recommendations when considering code changes.

Source: Florida Building Commission.
