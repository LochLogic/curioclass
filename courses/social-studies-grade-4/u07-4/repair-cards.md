# Fresh evidence features

All records below are made up. Use only today's assigned line.

| Feature | Comparison | Fresh attempt |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | One theater group rehearsed; the record names that group. | A repair crew painted one kiosk. |
| Choice | A worker accepted a job; another was forced into labor. | A notice orders someone to leave; no choice is offered. |
| Date | A 1990 record and a 2020 record have different dates. | A 2005 caption names one event. |
| Connection | A broken cable interrupts a remote workshop's power. | A stopped train cancels a distant museum visit. |
| Authority | A signed decision names a responsible committee. | A proposed rule lacks its decision record. |
| Evidence | A list names inspected rooms; other rooms remain unknown. | A note dates one repair; other repairs remain unknown. |

These lines never replace the independent exit or final packet.
