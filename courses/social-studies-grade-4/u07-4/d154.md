# Day 154: Recovery challenges and openings

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-154 as the corrected Side 2 summaries, evidence-sheet columns, policy-headings, locator and question-board. Read opener. Keep teacher-key and exit-masters separate; use current generated keys instead. Return day 153 papers with private cues. Use repair-cards as the repair-record format. Keep the current exit hidden.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 153's exit, annotations and retry. Review Return and repair. If many show error F, request equals adoption, compare [[s07-K154-R]] for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others use private cues. All attempt fresh [[s07-F154]] for five minutes before feedback. Label document features only; do not rehearse a status verdict.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Read JP as an original summary of Japan's 2013 housing report. Locate Japan in the source attribution. **Ask.** [[s07-K154-1]] before showing its key. Model one evidence annotation: detailed planning shows work still needed. Review day 152's challenge, a difficulty, and opportunity, an opening for improvement. Explain that planning housing better protected from disasters offers improvement, without proving every home safe. Relocation was underway, not complete for everyone.

#### 14 min. Evidence investigation
Alone for six minutes, children attempt [[s07-P154]] in separate evidence-sheet rows: JP first, then Florida's FL2. Review sourcing: who made it, when and why. For each, distinguish a stated action, an inferred opening and an unknown outcome. Retain policy-headings for public-decision clues. Concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate a phrase. Use six minutes for revisions and private cues. Do not model a finished paired comparison; children investigate one response at a time. Keep countries, dates and local conditions distinct.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Fifteen concurrent pairs explain one annotation, one minute per partner. Collect every paper in the remaining two minutes. Review question-board; record one current evidence label. Passing public talk is allowed. Keep Florida/Japan annotations in folders for proposal work.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X154]] on a half-slip. Say the market records are made up. Give three minutes alone, then collect. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class, budget 30 children times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for challenge, opportunity and private cues. Use record's sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all papers on day 155.

### Other rooms
Copy records and tasks on paper; point without projecting. No photograph, visitor or live page is required.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy the records without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** Public sharing and the class board.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 153 papers. Compare [[s07-K154-R]], then try [[s07-F154]].
- **8 min. Learn.** Read JP. Your child tries [[s07-K154-1]] before its key.
  **Say.** "Which detail shows work still needed?" Expect: planning, temporary housing or relocation underway.
- **14 min. Investigate.** Your child tries [[s07-P154]], one source at a time. Be the evidence partner afterward.
- **4 min. Record.** Keep Florida/Japan annotations and one private cue.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X154]] independently before its key. Keep all papers for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated sharing; preserve both records, repair and exit. Longer: identify what evidence could test a hoped-for improvement.

### Words and supports
Review challenge, opportunity, policy and sourcing. A chance is an opening, not a promise. Allow enlarged text, home-language rehearsal or exact dictation. No personal emergency accounts.

### Tomorrow
Day 155 chooses a hazard and examines advice before proposing action. Return day 154 papers. If many show error G, copy everywhere, or error Q, limits mean no progress, protect ten minutes: five comparing evidence, five on fresh relevance labels. Written cues reach all thirty.

### Credit
