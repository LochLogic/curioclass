# Day 151: Connections beyond the damaged place

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-151 as Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, locator and question-board. Read opener. Keep teacher-key, exit-masters and generated keys separate; use generated keys for corrected items. Cover the Exit box until its turn. Return day 150 papers with private cues. Use repair-cards only as the blank repair-record format.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 150's written tasks: s07-P150, s07-F150. Use the existing private cue and repair time.

Return day 150's exit, annotations and retry. Review Return and repair. If many show error N, missing relocation, or error E, origin only, compare [[s07-K151-R]] for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six; others follow private cues. All attempt fresh [[s07-F151]] for five minutes before feedback. Review day 149's displacement: having to leave a place because it cannot be used safely.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Say the Orchard, Wind and Fire records are made up. Review trade: goods move from where made to where sold. Review price and cost: what a buyer gives and what making it uses. **Ask.** [[s07-K151-1]] before revealing its key. Model underlining the lost order, not a finished distant-effects paragraph. The higher quote is explicit; never infer every disaster raises prices.

#### 14 min. Evidence investigation
Alone for six minutes, attempt [[s07-P151]] in separate evidence-sheet rows. Review sourcing: who made it, when and why. Review cause-effect: because something happened, something else followed. Supply no connecting arrows. Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate evidence. Use six minutes for revisions and private cues. Available rooms do not prove every resident moved. No paired settlement diagrams or complete effect answers are practiced.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Review question-board. Fifteen concurrent pairs point to one connecting detail, one minute each partner. Collect all papers in the remaining two minutes; update the board from them. Passing public talk is allowed.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X151]] on the half-slip and [[s07-P151-E]] in the Exit box. Allow three minutes alone, then collect both. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class, budget 30 children times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for both papers and private cues. Use record's one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 152.

### Other rooms
Copy records and tasks on paper without a printer. Point to labels without projecting. Keep exit cases covered until their turn.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy the page without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** Public sharing and the class board.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 150 papers. Compare [[s07-K151-R]], then try [[s07-F151]] before feedback.
- **8 min. Connect.** Read Orchard. Try [[s07-K151-1]] before showing its answer.
  **Say.** "Which detail links the bakery and store?" Expect: its lost ingredient order.
- **14 min. Investigate.** Read Wind and Fire separately. Your child tries [[s07-P151]]. Point to evidence after independent marks.
- **4 min. Record.** Keep annotations and one private cue.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s07-X151]] and [[s07-P151-E]]. Your child answers independently; keep both for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated sharing; preserve repairs and exits. Longer: identify an unstated effect without inventing evidence.

### Words and supports
Review trade, price and cost, displacement and sourcing. Enlarge text, allow home-language rehearsal or exact dictation. Use printed labels; no personal disaster accounts.

### Tomorrow
Day 152 examines public responsibilities and participation. Return day 151 papers. If many show error E, missing distant connections, protect ten minutes: five comparing connection labels, five on fresh evidence. Written cues reach everyone.

### Credit
Ours. All event records are made up.
