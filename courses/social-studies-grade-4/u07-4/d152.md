# Day 152: Who can decide, and who can take part

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-152 on Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, policy-headings, locator and question-board. Read opener. Keep teacher-key, exit-masters and current generated keys separate. Return day 151 papers with private cues. Use repair-cards as the repair-record format. Keep the half-slip and student Exit box covered until their turn.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 151's papers: `s07-X151`, `s07-P151`, `s07-F151` and `s07-P151-E`. Review Return and repair. If many show error E, missing distant connections, compare [[s07-K152-R]] for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others follow private cues. Everyone tries fresh [[s07-F152]] for five minutes before feedback. Review sourcing: who made it, when and why.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
All thirty independently try [[s07-RET152]]. Review grade 3's level of government: school, town, state, country. Review taking part: vote, go to a meeting, serve on a board, volunteer, stay informed. Collect every response. Size alone does not establish authority. Tribal governments have their own authority, Source GOV152.

#### 9 min. Teaching first
Read AID, FL1 and FL2 aloud as dated summaries for learners. Review policy: a rule for all the schools. Extend to an adopted public course of action. Review proposal: a plan the class could try; extend to a public request. Teach challenge, a difficulty, and opportunity, an opening for improvement. Introduce policy-headings: problem, proposer, authorized adoption, later revision. Adoption requires a decision by the body with authority; revision changes an adopted action. **Ask.** [[s07-K152-1]] before its key. Model one request-word annotation. Then ask [[s07-K152-2]] before its key; model decision and revision words. Read the separate role notices; these stipulated assignments are not universal rules.

#### 12 min. Evidence investigation
Children annotate [[s07-P152]] alone, one source per evidence-sheet row. Use policy-headings to label individual clues, not a complete four-stage narrative. For retrieval misses, spend the first five minutes at a side table locating service and participation words; others annotate. All use seven minutes for remaining sources and revisions. A request is evidence of participation, not proof of adoption. Keep exact proposer evidence unknown where absent. Collect every paper; update question-board privately.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X152]] on the half-slip and [[s07-X152b]] in the student Exit box. Keep the flood record visible while children answer both. Allow three minutes independently, then collect. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class, budget 30 children times 50 seconds, 25 minutes, for components and cues. Use record's sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 153.

### Other rooms
Copy the summaries and headings on paper. Read without projecting. No visitor or live emergency is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Hand-copy records without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** Public collection and the class board.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 151 papers. Compare [[s07-K152-R]], then try [[s07-F152]].
- **5 min. Recall.** Your child tries [[s07-RET152]]. Give a private cue afterward.
- **9 min. Learn.** Read Side 2. Explain the four stage headings. Try [[s07-K152-1]] and [[s07-K152-2]] before their keys.
  **Say.** "Which words show a request?" Expect: asking for a warning sign.
- **12 min. Investigate.** Your child tries [[s07-P152]], one source per row. Point to a clue after independent marks.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover the half-slip [[s07-X152]] and Exit box [[s07-X152b]]. Your child answers both independently, using the same flood record. Keep all papers for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated commentary, preserving retrieval, stages, repair and exits. Longer: label one absent authority detail.

### Words and supports
Challenge: difficulty. Opportunity: opening for improvement, not promised success. Read neutrally; allow enlarged text, home-language rehearsal and exact dictation. Never seek private losses or family accounts.

### Tomorrow
Day 153 investigates policy documents using today's headings. Return day 152 papers. If many show error O, one body controls everything, protect ten minutes: five comparing authority features, five labeling fresh roles. Return participation and retrieval cues privately.
