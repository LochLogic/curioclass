# Day 139: Southeast community evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read opener. Prepare current cards-139, evidence-sheet, question-board, locator, repair-cards and record. Keep teacher-key and today's generated key private. Copy only today's half slip from exit-masters. Attach one private cue to each day 138 paper.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 138's exit, repair and annotations. If many show error B, compare chosen and forced on repair-cards for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others follow private cues. Review forced and chosen from grade 3 day 146. Everyone attempts fresh [[s07-F139]] for five minutes, then sees [[steps:s07-F139]]. For error I point to the named actors; for error J distinguish past events from today's source makers. Keep retry for day 140.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review region from grade 3 day 11; put Southeast on locator. Review culture: what people make, do and share, grade 3 day 17. People sharing a culture differ. Read EBC-T; the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians supports members' education today. Review sourcing from grade 3 day 95. **Ask.** [[s07-P139]] Then reveal [[steps:s07-P139]] for EBC-T only. Publication date is unknown; “today” describes the account's present work.

#### 14 min. Regional evidence
Read all four cards. The Gullah Geechee account concerns African American cultural preservation. Enslaved people were forced to work; Black history also includes community institutions. Read no sacred content. Cuban mutual aid means people helping one another. Review grade 3 day 100's St. Augustine date, 1565; contributions never justify taking land.

Fifteen simultaneous pairs use [[s07-P139]] on each fragment, one minute per partner, then independent annotations. Match makers, dates and evidence words using evidence-sheet's columns. Mark unknown dates honestly. These are word-and-label records, not completed group-contribution sentences or additions to an incomplete account. Keep the held town caption concealed.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Connect today's source labels to question-board's community question. Collect every annotation; return any outstanding day 138 cue. Hear at most four 45-second source-label observations, three minutes total. Record speakers; others submit marks and may pass public talk. Keep locator in Regional Voices.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X139]]. Children answer alone on half slips; explain “self-governing” as making decisions through its own town government. Give no example answer. After class allow 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for exit and practice. Record components using shown, shown with help, not yet seen only. Return all papers on day 140.

### Other rooms
Copy current fragments and tasks on plain paper. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. No live page or visitor is required.

### At home
**Swap.** Without a printer, copy current cards and tasks onto plain paper.
**Skip.** Class meetings and speaker sampling.
- **10 min. Feedback.** Return yesterday's papers. Your child attempts [[s07-F139]]. Read its key privately afterward.
- **8 min. First source.** Read EBC-T and begin [[s07-P139]].
  **Say.** “Who provides the education account?” Expect the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians.
- **14 min. Other sources.** Read the remaining cards-139. Finish the same source annotations together, then independently.
- **4 min. Board.** Add today's labels beside the community question. Check dates and limits.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X139]] alone. Explain self-governing as having its own town government. Save the response for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim sampled talk, keeping every fragment and the exit. Longer: check the evidence for an existing annotation.

### Words and supports
Review culture, contribution, sourcing and forced and chosen in their learned meanings. Read aloud, enlarge print or accept exact dictation. Nobody reports private family history.

### Tomorrow
Day 140 studies Midwest communities. Return day 139's exit, retry and annotations. If many show error I, one job describing everyone, use its ten-minute Return and repair for source limits. Others receive private evidence cues.

### Credit
Ours. Sources s07-EBC-T, s07-GU-T, s07-SP-T, s07-CU-T and s07-EA-X support today's facts.
