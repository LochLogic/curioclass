# Day 138 evidence cards

## MW-T

Mashpee Wampanoag leaders reserved land by deed in 1665. Plymouth Colony confirmed that deed in 1685.

Source: Mashpee Wampanoag Tribe.

## MW-T

English settlers established Plymouth on Wampanoag land in 1620.

Source: Mashpee Wampanoag Tribe.

## BOS

Black Bostonians used the African Meeting House for abolition meetings and civic education.

Source: National Park Service.

## IR-T

In 1822, Irish laborers came to build Lowell canals, factories and boardinghouses.

Source: National Park Service.

## SB-T

A Somali Bantu community organization helps newcomers navigate public services in Maine.

Source: Somali Bantu Community Association.
