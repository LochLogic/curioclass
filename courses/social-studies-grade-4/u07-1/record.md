Record item/component, source evidence, date and the next written move.
No totals or averages. Regional first-encounter work is practice, never an end score.
All thirty submit paper. A child may pass public talk.
At most four 45-second observations fit a four-minute record part.
Everyone else receives paper feedback in the named receiving block.
