# Day 141 evidence cards

## MES

The Mescalero Apache Tribe includes Mescalero, Lipan and Chiricahua people today. Its reservation lies within ancestral homelands.

Source: Mescalero Apache Tribe.

## BS-X

Mescalero Apache archaeologists worked with students to study public history at Pine Springs.

Source: National Park Service.

## NIX

Lawrence Aaron Nixon established a medical practice in El Paso after arriving in 1907.

Source: Texas State Historical Association.

## CAM

Source: National Park Service.

## CAM

The Mexican government established the village of Dona Ana in 1843.

Source: National Park Service.

## VN-T
