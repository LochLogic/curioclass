# Day 137: Questions about communities and land

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener. Prepare cards-137 and town-pictures; conceal exit-masters except today's half slip. Use today's generated item key; teacher-key supplies annotation guidance. Prepare record, evidence-sheet, locator and question-board. Send family note; students aged 18 or older receive it and choose themselves, subject to school rules. Keep repair-cards available for private cues.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook
Day 136 promised regional peoples, land and supporting questions. Keep its unresolved components in record; provide fresh directions. Read MT and JO. Makah people live today; rebuilding Joplin schools added shelters. These are separate accounts, not a single community. Offer passing in public talk; never request personal disaster stories.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
The public-hall drawings are made up. Review reading dated community evidence; use these fresh drawings without assuming recall. Everyone answers [[s07-RET137]] alone. Collect the response for day 138. A ramp appears only in the recent drawing; the drawings cannot describe every resident.

#### 10 min. Questions and evidence
Post the unit question from question-board. Review supporting question: a smaller question that helps answer the big one, from grade 3 day 141. Review its question-board routine. Pairs attempt [[s07-P137-questions]] before seeing the five board questions. Model narrowing the topic label “communities” to a question about sources. Co-write the five questions using board topics and answer days; retain children's useful wording.

#### 10 min. Regional Voices orientation
Review sourcing: who made it, when and why, from grade 3 day 95. Our summaries are for learners; source pages address public readers. Undated means no publication date supplied, not no history. **Ask.** [[s07-P137]] Then show [[steps:s07-P137]]. Fifteen pairs take one minute per partner simultaneously. Use evidence-sheet as the annotation model. Review day 107's Paterson reminder: river channels powered factory machinery. Supply “river” and “work” labels independently. No new Paterson question is judged. Keep locator in Regional Voices.

#### 6 min. Evidence selection
Each child answers [[s07-P137-evidence]]. Hear six rotating observations at 45 seconds each, totaling 4.5 minutes. Record names; everyone submits a written source choice. Compare the choices using the key afterward. No model explains another person's need.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s07-X137]] only now. Children answer alone on half slips, with neutral reading or exact dictation. Allow all four minutes for responding. After class, allow 30 children times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for tickets and annotations. Use record's sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all collected work on day 138 with one private cue.

### Other rooms
Copy current summaries and tasks onto plain paper; draw the two hall diagrams. Keep the exit hidden. No visitor, image or live page is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy current cards and hall drawings onto scrap paper.
**Skip.** Class speaker sampling and the large wall board.
- **5 min. Two accounts.** Read cards-137 together.
- **5 min. Change.** Your child completes [[s07-RET137]].
  **Say.** “What changed in the drawings?” Expect a ramp, without a claim about everyone.
- **10 min. Questions.** Your child tries [[s07-P137-questions]]. Read the board's five smaller questions afterward.
- **10 min. Evidence.** Complete [[s07-P137]], then [[s07-P137-evidence]]. Read the item key privately after each attempt. Save the paper for tomorrow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X137]] alone. Your child answers without hints. Keep it for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Trim sampled observations; preserve question writing and the exit. Longer: refine the same supporting question.

### Words and supports
Review supporting question and sourcing in the meanings above. Read aloud; accept home-language rehearsal and exact dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 138 studies Northeast communities. Return day 137's retrieval, questions, source choices and exit. If many show error U, preference instead of the planner's need, use its ten-minute Return and repair. Include dated-evidence gaps there; retain other day 136 needs privately.

### Credit
Ours. Sources s07-MT, s07-JO and s06-paterson support the factual summaries.
