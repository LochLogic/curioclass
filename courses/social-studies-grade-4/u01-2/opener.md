# Section 1.2 opener

## The ideas

A ruler measures paper. A scale bar links paper length to represented ground distance. The half-bar bridge is taught here before the later math unit. Conversion pairs are supplied approximations, not values to memorize. Extent, retained detail and distance scale differ. Cultural feature and environmental feature keep their earlier meanings.
