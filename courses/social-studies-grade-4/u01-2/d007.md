# Day 7: Windows on one place

## Teacher page

### Before class
Cut paper windows matching the town and state boxes. Frames show map extents, not state borders. Keep Side 2 and exit slips hidden. Prepare the teacher-only generated answer key. Return day 6's exit and explanation. Keep the four-part map card visible.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 6's work. If many showed error C, use [[s01-P7-R]]; others follow written cues. Repair only the priority gap. Review yesterday's separate kilometer and mile labels.

#### 10 min. Windows on one place
First three minutes: review grade 3's claim, evidence and reasoning, how a detail supports a claim. Read the supplied claim. **Ask.** [[s01-K7-1]] Then reveal [[steps:s01-K7-1]].

Next seven: review Boulder as an earlier place-name example; these maps are new. Say the hall, pond and roads are made up. Read each map's maker and date. Teach extent as the area a map covers. Lay the town window on the state view and the state window on the continent view. Wider extent can lose local detail. Scale links distance; extent names coverage. Source 5S supports that distinction; Source 7C and Source 7N support wider locations.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Pairs attempt [[s01-P7-1]] and [[s01-P7-2]]. Each child directs a window alignment, then annotates independently. Two 90-second explanations total three minutes. Review grade 1's cultural feature, something people make, learn or do; environmental feature, land, water, weather. The invented hall and pond illustrate these categories. Alone, repair [[s01-F7-1]] into a spatial sentence. Show the teacher answer key after attempts. Hold five minutes for one repair group; review the legend and north arrow. Other children follow written instructions. Retrieval and window-alignment work stay in the folder.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the question board. Collect window annotations and everyone's [[s01-P7-3]]. Hear six optional 45-second explanations, 4.5 minutes total. A missing label does not prove an absent feature. Anyone may pass publicly.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal Side 2 and [[s01-X7]]. Denver returns as a place-name reminder; this river-library case is new. Say its library and roads are made up. Children write independently on half slips. Collect. Mark after class: 30 children times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Record each component as shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Return day 8 with annotations and one cue.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the three windows onto individual paper; keep the exit set separate. Make scrap-paper windows. Read small labels aloud without supplying relationships.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: draw the three views and cut scrap-paper windows.
**Skip.** Public sharing and the class board.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 6's cue; complete [[s01-P7-R]].
- **10 min. View.** Read the claim and try [[s01-K7-1]]. Move the windows. **Say.** "Where does the smaller window fit?" Expect: the town box on the state view.
- **12 min. Compare.** Your child completes [[s01-P7-2]] and [[s01-F7-1]]. Check afterward.
- **3 min. Annotate.** Complete [[s01-P7-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Side 2 and [[s01-X7]]. Your child answers alone. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fewer public explanations; keep both window comparisons. Longer: use the legend to verify each crossed-out label.

### Words and supports
Extent: area covered. Review cultural feature, environmental feature, claim, evidence, reasoning, legend and north arrow. Allow dictated spatial sentences and raised paper windows.

### Tomorrow
Day 8 returns day 7's exit and annotations. If many show error D, align fresh windows; error O needs a relationship rather than a list. For error H or error N, connect one printed detail to a map statement. Repair one priority gap before critiquing supplied map choices.

### Credit
Ours. Source 7C and Source 7N support wider positions; Source 7R supports Denver's river.
