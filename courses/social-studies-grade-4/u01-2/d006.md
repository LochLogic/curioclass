# Day 6: Keep the units

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 5's exit and construction explanation with one cue. Supply the day 5 scale page, Side 1 only. Remove tracing guides; retain the ruler, bar and approximate table. Keep new exit slips face down. Prepare the teacher-only generated answer key.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 5's work. Review yesterday's whole bars, half bar and ruler zero. If many showed error C, attempt [[s01-P6-R]] with correct paper and ground unit labels. Repair one dominant gap; other children follow their cues independently.

#### 10 min. Table retrieval
Review day 5's kilometer, mile and supplied approximate table. Each child completes [[s01-P6-1]] by drawing matching lines. Pairs compare after trying; each has one 60-second explanation, two minutes total. Reveal the matching entries using the teacher answer key. Keep half-bar and whole-bar rows separate; no new conversion method or factor.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Alone, try [[s01-F6-1]], [[s01-F6-2]] and [[s01-P6-2]]. Partners point to the table row that supports each correction. Each has one 90-second explanation, three minutes total. After attempts, show the corrected entries from the answer key. Keep five minutes for one error C side group: relabel the two-bar row, double both entries, then retry the four-bar row. Other table work stays in the folder.

#### 5 min. Explain a correction
Collect everyone's [[s01-P6-3]]. Hear six volunteers for at most 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total; anyone may pass. Review why doubling applies to both distance units. These explanations are feedback, not an additional class-wide spoken check.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s01-X6]] for independent paper work. Keep yesterday's practice table separate from the new table in the stem. Collect slips. Mark after class: 30 children times 20 seconds, 10 minutes. Record each component as shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Return day 7 with the correction explanation and one written cue.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the table and tasks onto individual paper. No ruler: use yesterday's measured centimeter strip. The supplied table remains visible; never ask children to remember an exact conversion.

### At home
**Swap.** No printed page: copy its table and tasks. No ruler: use yesterday's measured strip.
**Skip.** Public explanations and the side group.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 5's cue; complete [[s01-P6-R]].
- **8 min. Match.** Use yesterday's table for [[s01-P6-1]]. **Say.** "Which row is half a whole bar?" Expect: 56 kilometers, about 35 miles.
- **12 min. Correct.** Your child tries both repairs and [[s01-P6-2]]. Show the answer key afterward.
- **5 min. Explain.** Complete [[s01-P6-3]] together; point to the supporting row.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X6]]. Your child works alone. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: hear fewer public explanations; keep both repairs and the missing row. Longer: verify each corrected row by repeated addition of whole-bar entries.

### Words and supports
Review: whole bar, half bar, centimeter, kilometer and mile from day 5. Read column labels aloud. Pointing and dictated labels preserve the distance reasoning. Keep approximate mile answers marked "about."

### Tomorrow
Day 7 returns day 6's explanation and exit. If many show error C, separate the unit labels in a fresh table repair. Then compare one place across town, state and continent maps.
