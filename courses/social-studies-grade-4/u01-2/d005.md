# Day 5: From paper to ground

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print prepared strips at actual size; measure their 12-, 14- and 16-centimeter lengths. Check the practice bar at 4 centimeters, exit bar at 2 and exit segment at 5. Cut out the prepared strips; students answer on their day page. Keep key-05.md teacher-only and the exit source hidden. Return day 4 work.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 4's annotations, s01-P4-E and s01-X4 with one cue. Review country and continent from day 4. If many showed error E, use [[s01-P5-R]] and distinguish the broader continent label. Others act on their cues. Handle one priority gap.

#### 10 min. Build a scale
Review grade 3's paper strip laid end to end, counted in whole bars. Review the four-part map card. A centimeter measures paper length. Teach kilometer and mile as ground-distance units; use the supplied approximate table, never a memorized factor. Source 5S supports the scale concept; our distances are invented.

**Ask.** [[s01-K5-1]] Let children try, then reveal [[steps:s01-K5-1]]. Put ruler zero, not its edge, at the start. The folded half must have equal lengths.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Alone, children measure the prepared strips for [[s01-P5-1]] and [[s01-P5-2]], checking their labels. Partners each explain one check for 45 seconds, 90 seconds total. Then repair [[s01-F5-1]] and [[s01-F5-2]]. After attempts, show their worked steps from the teacher answer key. Keep five minutes for one error C group; rebuild a bar and correct a fresh label. Strip checks stay in the folder.

#### 5 min. Compare constructions
Collect everyone's [[s01-P5-3]]. Hear names 25 to 30 from day 4's rotation, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Public passing is allowed. Review the question board's distance question; connect ruler length, bar count and ground units.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal Side 2 only now. Children independently answer [[s01-X5]] on the half slip using their own printed map. Collect slips. After class, mark 30 responses at 20 seconds each: 10 minutes. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen for each component. Return day 6 with the strip-check explanation and one cue.

### Other rooms
No printer: hand-draw individual bars and segments at the verified lengths. No ruler: provide a teacher-measured centimeter strip, including zero and half-bar marks. Never measure a screen.

### At home
**Swap.** No printed master: draw the bar and exit segment at the lengths in Before class. No ruler: use a measured centimeter strip.
**Skip.** Public sharing and the question board.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 4's cue and complete [[s01-P5-R]].
- **10 min. Build.** Try [[s01-K5-1]] together. Put ruler zero at the start.
- **12 min. Check strips.** Your child completes [[s01-P5-1]], then both label repairs. **Say.** "How does your strip stand for its ground distance?" Expect: its bars each represent 112 kilometers.
- **3 min. Explain.** Complete [[s01-P5-3]]; keep the strip checks.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Side 2. Your child answers [[s01-X5]] alone. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public explanations; keep both prepared-strip checks and repairs. Longer: check each supplied label by laying equal bars along it again.

### Words and supports
Review: whole bars, country and continent. Half bar: two equal pieces make one bar. Centimeter measures length; kilometer and mile name ground-distance units here. Read table labels aloud. A child may direct a scribe's ruler placement and labels.

### Tomorrow
Day 6 returns day 5's exit and strip-check explanation. If many show error C, repair a fresh unit label before retrieving the same approximate table. Withdraw tracing guides; keep rulers and the table.

### Credit
Ours. Source 5S informs the map-scale explanation.
