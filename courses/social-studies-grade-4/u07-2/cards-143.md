# Day 143 evidence cards

## JA-T

After California's 1920 land restrictions, many Japanese immigrants entered urban gardening.

Source: Japanese American National Museum.

## IN-T

In 1942, the government forced Japanese Americans into confinement. Citizens were among those denied freedom.

Source: National Archives.

## JA-T

In 1988, federal law provided an apology for wartime incarceration.

Source: Japanese American National Museum.

## FI-T

Seattle Filipino community organizations trace their beginnings to 1935. Today they provide youth and senior services.

Source: Filipino Cultural Heritage Society of Washington.
