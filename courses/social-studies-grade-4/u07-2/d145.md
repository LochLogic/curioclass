# Day 145: Movement, land and community connections

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare Side 2 from cards-145, evidence-sheet columns, continuity-change headings, locator, question-board, repair-cards and record. Keep exit-masters and teacher-key hidden. Use the generated item key: the exit does not establish an earlier vehicle. Return day 144 local records and exits with one evidence-location cue per child. Keep Regional Voices ready.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 144's local record `s07-P144`, retry `s07-F144` and exit `s07-X144`. If many show error A, one culture, or error I, every resident represented, compare repair-cards' Evidence line for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others follow cues. Everyone tries fresh [[s07-F145]] for five minutes before [[steps:s07-F145]]. This archive is made up. Each child acts on the returned local-record cue.

#### 5 min. Retrieval and orientation
Review day 143's continuity-change headings: then, now, what stayed, with sources. Review people, goods and ideas, and its club-sketch connection. Physical features return earlier land work. Review sourcing: who made it, when and why. Children hold Regional Voices; teacher holds question-board.

Say: "Today's regional records are made up. They model movement, not actual regional history." Point to dates and region labels only. Keep completed change explanations out of practice. No new model is shown.

#### 17 min. Five-region investigation
**Ask.** [[s07-P145]] Supply the claim: "Land alone explains every movement." Children work alone for six minutes, matching all six single-date fragments to printed dates and people, goods or ideas. They annotate each fragment separately in evidence-sheet columns, not a paired map or complete change explanation.

Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate a supporting or challenging detail. Each may pass public talk. During five further minutes alone, children underline social evidence and revise one type/date mark. In the final four minutes, audit all five region labels on locator and mark each terrain detail. Land can provide access; a route record alone never proves everybody's reasons. These constructed dates do not establish historical regional patterns.

#### 4 min. Evidence audit
Everyone checks one maker/date label and marks a missing explanation on the same annotations. Hear at most four 45-second evidence-location observations, three minutes total; record speakers. Collect every sheet and retry. Retain continuity-change headings and locator in Regional Voices for day 158's withdrawal of completed supports.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X145]] on half slips. Give three minutes independently, with neutral reading or exact dictation. Collect all responses. After class, allow 30 times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for exit components and practice cues. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 146.

### Other rooms
Copy the current route fragments and tasks on plain paper. Keep each date separate during practice and the exit hidden until its turn. No screen or local map is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy current fragments and tasks without a printer. Point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** Class board updates and sampled observations.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 144 work with its cue. Your child tries [[s07-F145]], then sees its key.
- **5 min. Recall.** Review day 143's headings and movement types. Explain that today's routes are made up.
- **17 min. Investigate.** Complete [[s07-P145]] with six separate fragments. Be the location partner after independent marks.
  **Say.** "Which social detail challenges land alone?" Expect: neighbors exchange repair advice.
- **4 min. Audit.** Check one maker/date and collect annotations.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X145]] independently before reading its key. Keep all papers for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim sampled talk, preserving six fragments, private revision and the exit. Longer: improve a terrain annotation.

### Words and supports
Review continuity, change, goods, ideas and source limits. Read neutrally, enlarge labels or scribe exactly. Never request a child's relatives' movements.

### Tomorrow
Day 146 studies Southeast hurricanes and floods. Return day 145 papers. If many show error D, land alone, use its ten-minute Return and repair for a fresh evidence-link comparison and retry; carry local-source gaps with private cues.
