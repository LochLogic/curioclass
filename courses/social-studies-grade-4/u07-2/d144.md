# Day 144: Public sources and community life

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-144's printed West fallback through Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, locator, question-board, repair-cards and record. Keep teacher-key and exit-masters separate. Use today's generated item key privately. Return day 143 work with a cue. Use the supplied public summaries; no visitor, live page or local photograph is needed.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 143 work: `s07-P143`, `s07-F143`, `s07-K143-1`, `s07-K143-2` and `s07-X143`. If many show error B, freely chosen confinement, error K, protection, or error V, identical histories, use repair-cards' Choice comparison for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others follow cues. Review force and particular-group labels. Everyone attempts fresh [[s07-F144]] for five minutes before [[steps:s07-F144]]. Say the notice is made up; do not act it out.

#### 5 min. Local packet orientation
Use the plan's studied-West fallback, not unprepared local substitutions. Review sourcing: who made it, when and why, from grade 3 day 95. Review day 137's Makah account and day 143's Filipino service account. These public institutional summaries teach about named communities. They do not date residents' arrival. Today's services never prove recent arrival.

Point to the student task and evidence-sheet columns. Supply independent directions: label the studied region West; put each community beside its source label. These locator directions return day 107's routine. No new model or teacher answer is shown.

#### 17 min. Independent local investigation
**Ask.** [[s07-P144]] Children work alone on both fragments for seven minutes, marking evidence words, event date or unknown, and a missing-source cue. Use their own wording or marks, not complete contribution descriptions.

Fifteen simultaneous pairs take one minute per partner to locate one mark on the page. Partners may pass public sharing. Each child then spends five minutes improving annotations independently. In the final three minutes, label both communities on locator and mark arrival date unknown. Review region and culture from days 138 to 139: a classroom region can hold overlapping communities. These cards do not set community boundaries. Never combine the two cards into a contribution-and-limit paragraph.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Add source labels beside question-board's community question. Hear at most four 45-second evidence-location observations, three minutes total; record speakers. Collect everyone's local annotations and retry. Add one evidence-location cue after class; return it in day 145's Return and repair. Keep locator in Regional Voices.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X144]] on half slips. Give three minutes alone, with neutral reading or exact dictation. Collect every response. After class, allow 30 times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for the exit and local-record cues. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Hand every paper back on day 145.

### Other rooms
Use the same printed West fallback. Copy summaries and tasks on plain paper; point to labels without a projector. Keep exits concealed until their turn.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy Side 2 and tasks without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** Class board updates and sampled observations.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 143 papers. Your child attempts [[s07-F144]], then sees its key.
- **5 min. Orient.** Read the public-source directions. Use the West fallback without family research.
- **17 min. Investigate.** Your child completes [[s07-P144]] on both fragments alone. Be the source-location partner afterward.
  **Say.** "Which date does this card state?" Expect: neither gives an arrival date. Mark unknown.
- **4 min. Record.** Collect the annotations for a private cue tomorrow.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X144]] independently before opening its key. Keep papers for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim sampled talk, preserving both source annotations and the exit. Longer: improve a missing-evidence mark.

### Words and supports
Review contribution, culture, region and forced/chosen meanings. Read summaries neutrally, enlarge print or scribe exactly. No child reports private family or immigration information.

### Tomorrow
Day 145 investigates movement using made-up regional route fragments. Return day 144's local record and exit. If many show error A, one culture, or error I, every resident represented, compare limited-source evidence before a fresh retry in Return and repair.
