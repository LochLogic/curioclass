# Day 142 evidence cards

## MT

Makah people sustain contemporary community life in their homeland. Some Makah artists sell their work today.

Source: Makah Tribe.

## PICO

Pio Pico, an Afro-Latino Californian, served as governor under Mexican rule.

Source: National Park Service.

## PR-T

Spanish colonists established San Francisco's military garrison in 1776.

Source: National Park Service.

## PM-T

After Mexican independence, California officials changed their allegiance from Spain to Mexico. Their institutions also changed.

Source: National Park Service.

## CH-T

Chinese immigrants sought gold and later helped build railroads, farms and towns.

Source: California State Parks.

## EX-T

The 1882 law restricted Chinese labor immigration and barred naturalization. Some groups had exceptions.

Source: National Archives.
