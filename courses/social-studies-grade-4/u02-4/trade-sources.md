# Selected national trade records

## Day 31: United States and Canada, 2024
Maker: United States Census Bureau. Format: annual goods table.
Units: millions of nominal United States dollars; not seasonally adjusted.

| United States exports to Canada | United States imports from Canada |
|---|---|
| 350,605.6 | 411,771.6 |

Original short extract of factual data: [goods table](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/balance/c1220.html).
The table describes goods values, not every worker's experience.

## Day 33: two accounts concerning Mexico, 2025
Canada's foreign affairs department describes growing two-way goods trade with Mexico.
It names wheat sent from Canada and fresh vegetables sent from Mexico.
Its format is a country-relations article.

Original summary: [Canada-Mexico relations](https://www.international.gc.ca/country-pays/mexico-mexique/relations.aspx?lang=eng).

The United States Census Bureau supplies a goods table.
Units: millions of nominal United States dollars; not seasonally adjusted.

| United States exports to Mexico | United States imports from Mexico |
|---|---|
| 337,281.7 | 534,314.9 |

Factual data extract: [United States-Mexico goods table](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/balance/c2010.html).
Both accounts concern 2025. Compare emphasis and format, not different national totals.
No Canadian-dollar value appears here. These records cannot prove a fictional worker's job effect.
