# Day 32: Follow one changed purchase

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md, teacher-only key-exits.md for exits and the generated answer key for practice, including the yarn record. Incorporate practice-32.md into the student page. Separate printing-chain.md's six one-group cards; reveal them individually. Seal exit-32.md half slips. Bring day 31 papers, question-board.md and record.md. All printing situations are invented; their country labels do not describe actual national employment.

### The day
#### 5 min. Warm-up, Return and repair
Return day 31's s02-X31, s02-P31, s02-P31-R and s02-P31-B. Review origin-to-destination arrows. If many showed error D, reversed direction, use [[s02-P32-R]]; for error E, naming only goods, use [[s02-P32-R2]] to restore one viewpoint. Otherwise support one group. Choose one priority per child, continuing during Group work; others read feedback. Review revising below the first attempt. For the yarn repair, reveal [[steps:s02-P32-R2]] after children answer.

#### 8 min. One purchase changes
Review day 30's printing input chain and interdependence, needing each other. Review grade 3 buyer and seller. Introduce rival: another seller competing for purchases. An added purchase gives its seller an order; a moved purchase can remove an order. A gain for one group does not establish a gain for everyone. Show only the Canadian printing-shop card.
**Ask.** [[s02-K32-K]]
Reveal [[steps:s02-K32-K]]. This case's country label cannot prove what every resident experiences. Keep the example to this one group's change.

#### 17 min. Group work
First five minutes extend one repair; others start [[s02-P32]]. Then reveal each card separately: Canadian printing shop, Mexican paper supplier, United States notebook buyer, Canadian rival printer, Mexican rival paper seller, United States rival seller. Each child records one changed purchase per card on separate lines. Judge each component, not a team answer. Pairs point to the purchase words; each child has one 45-second turn, simultaneously. Do not form a paired gain/loss answer or a three-country table. If many give error F, all change alike, return to the named group and its stated purchase. Collect every paper.

#### 5 min. Read the mechanism
Partners each explain one of their existing annotations, 45 seconds each, in parallel. Public passing is allowed; the written annotation remains evidence. Review source limits: these invented cards support their own groups only. Teacher adds one bounded group finding to question-board.md; no additional questions enter a teacher queue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the unseen half slip, exactly [[s02-X32]]. Each child answers independently on paper. Offer neutral read-alouds, enlarged text or dictation. Collect all. After class budget 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits, plus 30 times 90 seconds, 45 minutes, for component practice. Use record.md's shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component, with access recorded separately. Return every paper day 33.

### Other rooms
Use the printed group cards one at a time. A screen adds nothing essential. Copy their short statements if printing is unavailable; keep the exit hidden.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy each group card on a separate scrap. Hide the exit.
**Skip.** Class collection and public sharing.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 31 papers. Choose [[s02-P32-R]] or [[s02-P32-R2]] for one repair.
- **8 min. One group.** Review buyer, seller and interdependence. Explain rival as another seller competing for purchases. Use [[s02-K32-K]], then reveal its steps.
- **12 min. Cards.** Reveal cards separately. Your child records [[s02-P32]] on separate lines. Point to the changed purchase rather than a country-wide label.
- **5 min. Explanation.** **Say.** "Whose purchases changed?" Expect the named buyer's purchases from the selected card. Your child explains that existing annotation.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X32]] alone. Retain all work for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce talk; keep all countries' separate components, repairs and exit. Longer: sharpen the purchase detail in one annotation.

### Words and supports
Review buyer, seller, interdependence and source limits. Rival: another seller competing for purchases. Allow pointing and neutral read-alouds. Judge the purchase mechanism separately from grammar.

### Tomorrow
Day 33 applies group effects to a made-up factory and farm. Return s02-X32, s02-P32, s02-P32-R and s02-P32-R2. Error F, claiming all groups gain, gets one fresh group repair during Return and repair, extended during Case work.
