# Invented printing chain cards

All records are made up for practice, not actual national statistics.

## Input card
Paper supplier → printing shop → notebook customers.
Paper is delivered across a border.

## One-group cards, reveal one at a time
Canadian printing shop: customers place another notebook order.
Mexican paper supplier: the printing shop requests more paper.
United States notebook buyer: a new supplier offers a lower price.
Canadian rival printer: one customer switches away.
Mexican rival paper seller: an order goes elsewhere.
United States rival seller: buyers choose a different supplier.

Each card describes only its stated group and changed purchase.
Read the assigned card.
