# Day 33: Keep the evidence with its claim

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md, teacher-only key-exits.md and the generated answer key. Use the generated answer key for current rows. Incorporate practice-33.md into the student page. Prepare only day 33 from trade-sources.md and the factory-farm.md diagram. Seal exit-33.md half slips. Bring day 32 papers with one priority each, question-board.md and record.md. Explain that the diagram's factory and farm are made up, not actual national job records.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 32's s02-X32, s02-P32, s02-P32-R and s02-P32-R2. Review different group effects: a purchase changes a named group's orders. If many showed error F, all gain together, use [[s02-P33-R]] with all; otherwise one group. Others read their feedback and revise below their first answer. Continue one priority during Case work.

#### 8 min. Real accounts
Review source-sourcing from grade 3: maker, date, audience, purpose. Read the two day 33 extracts aloud. Canada's foreign affairs department describes two-way trade with Mexico and selected goods in a country-relations article. The United States Census Bureau gives national goods values with Mexico in a table. Both concern 2025. Its exports are 337,281.7; imports 534,314.9, in millions of nominal United States dollars, not seasonally adjusted. Review day 31's value unit. General readers seeking country relations or trade values are possible audiences; label this as inference. Children complete [[s02-P33-S]]. Compare emphasis and format, not Canadian and United States currency totals.

#### 17 min. Case work
First five extend the group repair; others read the made-up diagram. Then children mark [[s02-P33-F]] individually and correct [[s02-P33]]. The factory expansion and farm buyer switch are separate supplied links. Require the case detail supporting the correction; the national total cannot establish this farm's lost buyer. Review day 32's changed-purchase reasoning. Each partner gets one 45-second turn on one existing annotation, simultaneously across pairs. Collect individual papers, including children who pass publicly. If many confuse scope, point to each record's named subject before revision.

#### 5 min. Scope notes
First three minutes: write [[s02-P33-B]] individually. Last two minutes: pairs talk simultaneously. One partner explains the written note for 45 seconds; the other then speaks for 45 seconds. This uses 90 seconds across all pairs, leaving 30 seconds to collect notes. Teacher adds one evidence limit to question-board.md. Keep real national evidence separate from the invented groups.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the unseen half slip, exactly [[s02-X33]]. Children respond independently on paper; offer neutral read-alouds, enlarged text or dictation without causal hints. Collect all. After class allow 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits, plus 30 times 90 seconds, 45 minutes, for practice. Use record.md's shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; record access separately. Return every paper day 34.

### Other rooms
Prepared paper extracts replace live pages. Copy the diagram's labels and blank response fields if needed; keep the exit separate.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy the two short extracts and draw the case links. Hide the exit.
**Skip.** Class collection and public sharing.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 32 papers. Use [[s02-P33-R]] for one group repair.
- **8 min. Accounts.** Read the two national records aloud. Your child completes [[s02-P33-S]]. Review who made each, its year and its purpose.
- **12 min. Case work.** Explain that the factory and farm are made up. Your child marks [[s02-P33-F]] and corrects [[s02-P33]] using a diagram detail.
- **5 min. Scope.** Your child writes [[s02-P33-B]]. **Say.** "Can a national total prove this farm lost its buyer?" Expect no: the total does not name this farm's buyer.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X33]] alone. Keep all work for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: shorten partner talk; preserve both case links, source comparison and exit. Longer: sharpen one existing evidence-limit note.

### Words and supports
Review source-sourcing, buyer, seller, rival and changed purchases. Review national goods values from day 31. Allow pointing and dictation; judge causation separately from handwriting or English fluency.

### Tomorrow
Day 34 gathers evidence for the unit question. Return s02-X33, s02-P33, s02-P33-R, s02-P33-S, s02-P33-F and s02-P33-B. Error F, everyone gains, or error E, a list without purchase mechanisms, gets one fresh component repair in Return and repair plus five Evidence work minutes.
