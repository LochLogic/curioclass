# Section 2.4 teacher answer key

Teacher only. Keep out of student packets.
One scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Access support is separate.

## s02-X30

More jar orders require more lids from the other producer. Lid sellers increasingly rely on those jar makers' purchases.

Look for: Explains increasing deliveries and purchases in both directions.

Samples, shown then needing work: Jar makers need more lids; lid sellers need the growing purchases. | Glass jars and metal lids.

## s02-X31

Canada exports oats; Mexico imports the same shipment.

Look for: Correctly interprets the same arrow as export at origin and import at destination.

Samples, shown then needing work: Canada exports oats and Mexico imports them. | Mexico exports oats to Canada.

## s02-X32

Bakers gain reliable inputs; redirected purchases reduce local millers' orders.

Look for: Both mechanisms within country.

Samples, shown then needing work: Bakers keep baking; millers sell less because purchases moved abroad. | Everyone wins.

## s02-X33

Look for: Both effects explained through changed purchases.

Samples, shown then needing work: Repairers gain work; replacement sellers lose orders as customers choose cheaper repairs. | All sellers gain.

## s02-P32
