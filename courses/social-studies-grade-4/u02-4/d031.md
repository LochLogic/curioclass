### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 30's s02-X30, s02-P30, s02-P30a, s02-P30b, s02-P30-R, s02-P30-R2 and s02-P30-L. If many showed error E, listing without dependence, or error O, self-sufficiency, use [[s02-P31-R]] with all; otherwise one group. Review input and relies on from day 30. Mark the needed supply; continue one priority during Map work. Others read feedback and revise below their first attempt.

#### 8 min. Map perspective
Review legend, the map key, from day 18; supply it again: arrow means goods moving. Tail is origin; arrowhead is destination. The diagram shows selected exchanges, not distances. Review grade 2 import, comes into a country, and export, goes out. Point to Canada's label.
**Ask.** [[s02-K31-K]]
Reveal [[steps:s02-K31-K]]. On the other arrow:
**Ask.** [[s02-K31-Kb]]
Reveal [[steps:s02-K31-Kb]]. Keep these separate one-view readings; do not add growth to them.

#### 17 min. Map work
First five extend the input repair; others begin [[s02-P31]]. Each child marks origins on their own map. Then read aloud the national table's labels: United States Census Bureau, 2024, goods values, millions of nominal United States dollars, not seasonally adjusted. Exports to Canada: 350,605.6; imports from Canada: 411,771.6. Explain nominal as dollars recorded then, without adjusting for changing prices. These values are not item counts. Children mark [[s02-P31-B]]. Review day 30 interdependence: needing each other. Note that national values cannot identify a particular producer's reliance. Collect maps and annotations.

#### 5 min. Evidence boundary
Partners point to their table annotation, one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Partners may pass publicly and submit paper. Teacher observes without a thirty-child queue. Review source scope: goods totals cannot establish every worker's experience. Add the table's year and scope to question-board.md.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the separate unseen map and half slip, exactly [[s02-X31]], for independent paper responses. Read neutrally; allow enlargement or dictation without causal hints. Collect all. After class budget 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits, plus 30 times 45 seconds for practice. Use record.md's shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; record access separately. Return all papers day 32.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 30 papers. Use [[s02-P31-R]] for one needed-supply repair.
- **8 min. Direction.** Point to the map key. Review import as coming in, export as going out. Use [[s02-K31-K]] and [[s02-K31-Kb]], revealing each explanation afterward.
- **12 min. Map work.** Your child marks [[s02-P31]] and [[s02-P31-B]]. Read the large values aloud; explain that they measure goods values.

- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the new map with [[s02-X31]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce partner talk; preserve both directions, scope and exit. Longer: reread the value-unit label precisely.

### Words and supports
Review legend, origin, destination, import, export and interdependence. Thicken arrowheads; supply neutral read-alouds and dictation. Judge direction and reasoning separately from English fluency.
