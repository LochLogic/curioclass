# Day 27: Draft a country report

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md, teacher-only key-exits.md and the generated answer key. Prepare the named print pieces. Bring all day 26 papers, marked topic/source gaps and individual source folders. The student page incorporates the blank report-frame.md and practice-27.md; print no duplicates. Keep exit-27.md hidden. Put the question-board.md where children can revisit the research question.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 26's s02-X26, s02-P26, s02-P26-R, s02-P26-1 and s02-P26-2. If many showed error I, an overbroad group claim, or error D, unsupported evidence, use [[s02-P27-R]]. Explain that this tally is made up. Otherwise meet one group while others trace their own notes. Review source-linked evidence from day 25: match a statement to its source detail. Extend one priority during Draft work.

#### 5 min. Report order
Review day 22's order, detail and accuracy separately: sequence, useful information, agreement with evidence. Children arrange their blank topic headings before drafting. Define finding: an answer supported by research evidence. Review the individual six-topic notes from day 23. No complete report is modeled. Review checkable and opinion: a liking is not a reason. A checkable claim still needs checking; independent records can disagree.

#### 20 min. Draft work
First five minutes extend the day 26 priority. Each child then completes [[s02-P27]] independently using existing notes. Keep six topics distinct even where headings share space. Retain dated counted-group information, government offices/functions, city locator, resource, landmark and a resource/work explanation. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, purpose, useful detail and use. Locate map, atlas, article and printed digital entries. Flag missing evidence rather than invent it. Drafts receive feedback only.

#### 5 min. Source pass
Pairs complete [[s02-P27-S]] on their own pages. Each has one 45-second turn pointing to the sentence, source and detail; pairs run simultaneously. Others listen without supplying answers. Collect every report, log and student page. Write one criterion cue per child after class, about two minutes each, 60 minutes total. Retain feedback for day 29; return today's component papers on day 28.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-27.md, exactly [[s02-X27]]. Everyone writes alone. Read neutrally or take dictation without interpreting. Collect every half slip. Mark afterward: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes. Record components as shown, shown with help, not yet seen; distinguish access from content help.

### Other rooms
Copy blank headings and retained sources onto paper. Copy the hidden exit separately. No live source or screen is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No print: copy blank headings and retain the earlier source pages.
**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 26 feedback; complete [[s02-P27-R]].
- **5 min. Order.** Arrange the six headings with your child.
- **15 min. Draft.** Your child completes [[s02-P27]] from existing notes.
- **5 min. Source pass.** Complete [[s02-P27-S]]. **Say.** "Which detail supports this sentence?" Expect a matching detail in the named source. Write one next move for day 29.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X27]]. Your child answers alone; discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit optional sharing; preserve six topics, source pass and exit. Longer: trace another existing sentence to its source.

### Words and supports
Review source log, order, detail and accuracy. Allow enlarged print, oral rehearsal or dictation. Judge reasoning separately from spelling. Everyone may pass in public talk.

### Tomorrow
Day 28 presents findings. Return s02-X27, s02-P27-R and s02-P27-S with draft feedback. Error C, preference as proof, error D, unsupported evidence, or error L, checkable treated as true, gets one priority during Return and repair and Listener work. Written report/log feedback returns before day 29 revision.

### Credit
Ours. The tally and exit leaflet are made up. Research uses each child's retained source records.
