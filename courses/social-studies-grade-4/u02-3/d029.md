# Day 29: Critique and revise

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md; use the generated answer key for s02-X29. A depot inventory does not establish how many trams run. Prepare named print pieces and speaking-roster.md. Bring day 27 report/log feedback and every day 28 paper with oral cues. Student page incorporates practice-29.md, feedback-slips.md and today's listener fields. Cover the Exit box, incorporating exit-29.md and tram-route.svg. Retain source-log.md, topic-notes.md, government-table.md and report-frame.md; display question-board.md.

### The day
#### 5 min. Read feedback and repair
Return day 27's report draft and source log, plus day 28's s02-X28, s02-P28, s02-P28q, s02-P28-R and s02-P28-B. If many showed error D, unsupported evidence, complete [[s02-P29-R]] with all; otherwise one group. The chart is made up. This is one five-minute accuracy priority only. Others read feedback. Review grade 3's one-line criterion cue: revise below the first answer, retaining both versions; only revision is scored.

#### 7 min. Critique
Review critique: use order, supporting detail and accuracy criteria, not liking. Order helps a reader follow; details supply needed information; accuracy matches evidence. First three minutes: pairs use [[s02-P29]] and [[s02-P29b]] on separate slips. Each child gets one 45-second turn addressing those two criteria, simultaneously across pairs; remaining time is for writing. No combined accuracy model. Last four minutes: uncover the Exit box, incorporating exit-29.md with its map, exactly [[s02-X29]], for independent paper responses. This is an end item; no separate exit ticket.

#### 5 min. Revise report
First three minutes: each child revises one flagged report component below the original. Further gaps wait for day 38. Keep sources open. Last two minutes: pairs ask one listener question each, with one 45-second reply per child. Review listener question: "Which page says that?" This is rehearsal for the retained written [[s02-P29q]], finished silently during finding slots. Keep both versions open for findings; collect afterward.

#### 23 min. Repeat findings
Hear [[s02-E8]] at desks in roster order. Thirty times 45 seconds is 22.5 minutes, leaving 30 seconds for transitions. No extra listener question enters the teacher queue. Others silently finish their own listener record; collect it afterward for [[s02-E8b]]. Judge revised folders through [[s02-E7]], and peer slips through [[s02-E11]] and [[s02-E12]], once each. Review source-linked evidence and map selection from day 25. Public passing uses a private/signed/aided response in the same slot. Add one supported revision to question-board.md after class.

After class, use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component in record.md. Record access separately; no totals. Allow 30 times two minutes for revised folders/peer slips, 60 minutes, plus 30 times 45 seconds for exits, 22.5 minutes. Roster cues support oral judgments.

### Other rooms
Use retained paper sources and incorporated blank peer/listener fields. Copy the route map into the covered Exit box. No screen or recording is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy the blank peer/listener fields and hidden route map.
**Skip.** Class roster and waiting for other speakers.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Read earlier cues; complete [[s02-P29-R]].
- **7 min. Critique.** Use a retained draft as the partner report for [[s02-P29]] and [[s02-P29b]]. Your child then answers [[s02-X29]] alone.
- **8 min. Revise.** Your child revises one flagged component below its first attempt.
- **15 min. Repeat findings.** Hear one revised source-linked finding. **Say.** "Which page says that?" Expect a matching source detail. Your child records [[s02-P29q]]. Retain the report and slips for [[s02-E7]], [[s02-E8b]], [[s02-E11]] and [[s02-E12]].

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit optional sharing; preserve revision and every finding. Longer: strengthen another component using existing evidence.

### Words and supports
Review critique, order, detail, accuracy, finding and listener question. Read feedback neutrally. Allow enlargement, source pointing and dictation; judge reasoning separately from grammar.

### Tomorrow
Day 30 begins trade and specialization. Return s02-X29, s02-P29-R, s02-P29, s02-P29b and s02-P29q. Error H, liking instead of criteria, or error D, unsupported inference, gets one fresh priority during Return and repair plus five Trade work minutes. Return day 29's performance items on day 38; unresolved components wait there.
