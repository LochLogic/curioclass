# Day 52: Ancestral places and living governments

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read teacher-only keys. Keep s03 exit 52 covered. Retrieve s03 food sequence for reminders and s03 site practice for source labels. Check s03 map scale's printed bar is 1.25 inches. Return day 51 papers with one priority cue. Lay out strips and rulers. Select six unheard names on s03 record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return all day 51 papers, including every written task. If many day 51 tickets showed error P, use [[s03-P52-R]] together. Otherwise meet the priority group; others revise returned food sequences. Review day 51's distinction: written crop evidence and visible walls answer different questions. Finish in Site work's first five minutes.

#### 8 min. Mesa Verde model
Show the teaching Balcony House view as a reminder, separately from community records. Mesa Verde National Park preserves Ancestral Pueblo places. Source 42B describes farming communities there before European arrivals. Introduce ancestor and descendant as earlier and later generations in a line of descent, without asking about children's families. Preserve means protect and care for.

Review sovereignty from grade 3 day 84: a nation's right to govern itself. Source 52G says each New Mexico Pueblo is a sovereign nation. Name Acoma Pueblo separately; the Hopi Tribe is also a sovereign nation, not another name for all Pueblo peoples. Review homeland and present lands from grade 3 day 78. Moving does not mean disappearing.

#### 17 min. Site work
First five minutes finish priority repairs. Children use [[s03-P52a]] and [[s03-P52b]] with separate source labels, not a paired site-and-continuity answer. Review day 43's archaeological context: recorded position and relationships. Source 52H explains preservation for future learning. Care protects both records and places important to descendants.

Review whole bars from grade 3 day 7. Give measuring directions explicitly with [[s03-P52c]]: mark the supplied line on a strip, compare against the printed bar, then estimate the remaining fraction. Allow a ruler comparison. Each child records bar count and kilometers. The reference is about 275 kilometers, roughly 2.75 bars; accept 260 to 290. This generalized locator is not a road route or a claim of descent.

Review the source log. Collect all individual labels and measurements for descriptive feedback.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the board routine. Partners each give one 45-second source observation, 1.5 minutes in parallel. During the block hear six staggered single 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes; public pass allowed. If repairs continue, collect writing instead. Post one accurately attributed statement.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal s03 exit 52's fresh view and community card: [[s03-X52a]] [[s03-X52b]]. Each child answers independently on paper. After class allow 30 times 35 seconds, 17.5 minutes, plus ten minutes for practice feedback. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component, access support separately. Return all papers on day 53.

### Other rooms

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit sharing; keep source labels, measurement and exit. Longer: explain why a locator cannot establish a person's ancestry.

### Words and supports
Ancestor and descendant name generations, never a child's assigned identity. Preserve means protect and care for. Review sovereignty and archaeological context. Read labels neutrally; offer pointing, enlarged images or child-directed dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 53 studies Cahokia and attributed Osage history. Error G treats movement as disappearance. Return all day 52 papers in Return and repair and Source work's first five minutes. More than ten misses: shared priority repair; otherwise a group. Use a fresh current-community label.
