# Section 2.1 opener

## Before class
Print teaching sheets only for the current day. Print the separate exit half slip last.
Keep assessment pieces and teacher-only keys out of practice packets.
Read key-exits.md before class; it contains look-fors and two sample responses per item.
The unit uses no readiness sheet: retrieval tasks occur on days 17 and 30.
Send family.md from section 2.1 on day 17.
Print the unit component record here; do not use a second marking scale.

## What holds
Forty minutes. At most 600 teacher words and 350 student words per day.
Cards have short sentences; provide neutral read-alouds, enlarged maps and dictation.
Explain original classroom scenes and families are made up.
Use named Indigenous peoples in the present. Never ask for household or identity disclosures.
A child may pass publicly and respond privately. Students 18 or older choose for themselves.
No visitor, screen or live page is required: use the prepared printed sources and original diagrams.
If projection fails, display paper. Land diagrams replace photographs with unchecked rights.
Use only that day's selected source cards, not this whole section at once.

## Continuity
Return source-sourcing (maker, date, audience, purpose), corroboration, checkable and opinion where written evidence exists.
Provide explicit instruction for planned-only prerequisites; never claim they were learned.
Review grade 3 specialize, opportunity cost, industry, population and natural resource with the same meanings.
The question board carries the big question and supporting questions with answer days.
Use blank note headings; remove filled examples before independent research.

## Feedback and turns
Use the one scale in record.md, with content and access support distinguished.
More than a third missing: teach one priority to all. Otherwise one small group, written moves for others.
Each normal repair uses five opening minutes and five named work minutes; day 29 has five only.
Pairs each get one 45-second turn. Six public comments need 4.5 minutes; log heard names.
Everyone submits written evidence, returned on its named day, including children not heard publicly.
Days 28 and 29 each hear thirty findings: thirty times 45 seconds is 22.5 minutes.
The 23-minute block leaves 30 seconds for transitions; teacher visits seated children.
Retained paired question/reply slips capture every listener response in two minutes.

## Reserve
Withhold each exit diagram/case until its own exit. Withhold the complete end packet through day 36.
No teaching sheet uses an exit/end case. Keep day 38 component cards sealed until fresh attempts.
Never model a complete individual six-topic report or the complete end argument.
Use feedback to repair one component on new classroom evidence, not copy a returned key.
