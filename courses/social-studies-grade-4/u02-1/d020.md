# Day 20: Connect context with usefulness

## Teacher page

### Before class
Use the generated key for practice; use key-exits.md for exits. Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md. Prepare record.md, the question-board.md and returned day 19 papers. The student page incorporates practice-20.md and source-log.md fields; print no duplicate log. Keep exit-20.md hidden. These classroom records are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return s02-X19 and day 19's written task: s02-P19, s02-P19-R, s02-K19-1, s02-P19-1, s02-P19-2. If many show errors A, B or J, choose one priority: missing voices, metadata or contents. Use [[s02-P20-R]]. A promotion misconception from practice enters the same route. Otherwise meet one group while others write. Review source-sourcing rather than treating an official maker as proof of relevance.

#### 8 min. Context model
Introduce context: where, when and for whom a source was made. Review day 19's equipment notice and source-sourcing labels. Use [[s02-K20-1]] before showing steps.
[[steps:s02-K20-1]]
Explain the reasoning: an instruction addresses its intended readers and task. It may omit other people's experiences. A date tells when information was produced; it does not automatically update. A question about current conditions needs suitable current evidence. Do not work the exit's historical/current travel comparison.

#### 17 min. Supported work
Protect the first five minutes for the same day 19 priority; others complete [[s02-P20]]. Then everyone completes [[s02-P20-1]] and [[s02-P20-2]]. Attach a relevant source detail to the log's question. Hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, during the remaining twelve minutes. Record heard names; everyone submits written annotations. Children may pass publicly. For relevance misses, point back to the actual audience and detail, not the title alone.

#### 5 min. Read the log
Review the source log. Partners each take 45 seconds reading one maker/setting entry. They identify omissions without supplying each other's answers. Update "Which sources help us?" on the question board with the role of context. Collect all pages and annotations for day 21.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-20.md, exactly [[s02-X20]]. Children respond independently on the half slip. Neutral read-aloud or dictation is available. Collect all. After class: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; 30 times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for practice. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; record access separately.

### Other rooms
Hand-copy the two short records and log fields if needed. Paper records replace screens; no local visit is required.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy the records and use folded paper for the log.
**Skip.** Public turns and class groups.
- **5 min. Return.** Read yesterday's cue; complete [[s02-P20-R]].
- **8 min. Context.** Try [[s02-K20-1]], then read its steps. Complete [[s02-P20]].
- **12 min. Work.** Complete [[s02-P20-1]] and [[s02-P20-2]].
- **5 min. Read.** Read the log together. **Say.** "What does this detail help answer?" Expect: placement instructions or one helper's access experience, depending on the record.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X20]]. Your child answers alone. Discuss the key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public log reading, keeping repairs and source reasoning. Longer: name a reader whose question each record leaves unanswered.

### Words and supports
Review source-sourcing and source log. Context connects place, date and audience to a question. Enlarge records, point or dictate; content matters separately from grammar.

### Tomorrow
Day 21 distinguishes checkable claims and opinions. Return s02-X20 and practice. Error B, printed means current, gets fresh date/audience annotation in Return and repair and the first five Evidence work minutes.
