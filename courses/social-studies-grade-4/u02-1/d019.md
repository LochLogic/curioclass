# Day 19: Read origins and missing views

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md. Prepare record.md and the retained question-board.md. The student page incorporates practice-19.md and selected blank headings from source-log.md; do not print duplicate sheets. Return day 18's work. Fold exit-19.md out of sight until its own turn.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return s02-X18 and all day 18 practice. If many show error J or omit people's experiences, use [[s02-P19-R]] with everyone. Otherwise meet one group while others write. Cover the decorative title; read the actual contents. A record can fit one question while leaving another unanswered.

#### 8 min. Metadata model
Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Add kind and format; metadata means information about the source. Use the equipment notice. Children answer [[s02-K19-1]] before steps appear.
[[steps:s02-K19-1]]
Introduce the source log's question and useful-detail fields. Model one entry only, not a complete source-set defense. Review that a maker's name does not establish every claim's usefulness.

#### 17 min. Metadata work
First five minutes extend the same day 18 repair; others annotate the notice. Children complete [[s02-P19]] and [[s02-P19-1]]. They label sources and spot missing views in [[s02-P19-2]]. Teach that a seller's promise needs affected people's evidence. No group chooses and defends an entire pair yet. Let children revise entries when the source lacks requested information; "not supplied" is honest.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the question board. Partners each take 45 seconds naming one source's contents and one unanswered question, 90 seconds total. Record the source-selection tests under "Which sources help us?": fit the question; check origins; seek different experiences. Collect every page and log for day 20.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-19.md, exactly [[s02-X19]]. Every child selects and explains independently on the half slip. Read neutrally or take dictation. Collect all. After class, mark exits at 30 times 60 seconds, 30 minutes; practice at 30 times 60 seconds, another 30 minutes. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; note access separately.

### Other rooms
Copy the compact source set and log fields without a printer. Printed page descriptions replace screens. No outside visitor is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy the short notices and a blank log.
**Skip.** Public turns and class grouping.
- **5 min. Return.** Read yesterday's cue and complete [[s02-P19-R]].
- **8 min. Model.** Read the notice. Try [[s02-K19-1]], then read its steps.
- **12 min. Sources.** Complete [[s02-P19]] and [[s02-P19-1]].
- **5 min. Views.** Complete [[s02-P19-2]]. **Say.** "Whose experience is missing?" Expect: visitors in Set One; users or repair staff in Set Two.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X19]]. Your child answers alone; discuss the key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: cut public log reading, keeping the repair and both source sets. Longer: identify information a missing voice could contribute.

### Words and supports
Review source-sourcing and source kinds. Metadata labels a source; format is how it presents information. Read dates neutrally. A child may pass publicly and dictate privately.

### Tomorrow
Day 20 explains context. Return s02-X19 and practice. Errors A/B/J get one fresh missing-view or metadata repair in Return and repair and Supported work. A promotion misconception from practice uses that same priority route. Keep each child's other written cues private.
