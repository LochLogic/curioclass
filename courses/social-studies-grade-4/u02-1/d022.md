# Day 22: Check origins and limits

## Teacher page

### Before class
Use the generated key for practice; use key-exits.md for exits. Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md. Prepare record.md and returned day 21 papers. The student page incorporates practice-22.md and blank source-log.md fields. Retain the question-board.md. Keep exit-22.md, chapala-map.svg and its geography card together, hidden until the exit. No teaching packet contains them.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return s02-X21 and day 21's written task: s02-P21, s02-P21-R, s02-K21-1, s02-P21-1, s02-P21-2. If many show errors C, K or L, choose one priority using [[s02-P22-R]]. Otherwise meet one group while others write. Review checkable and opinion; a count needs a relevant record. Judge claims separately. Protect the model; continue this same repair in Origin work.

#### 8 min. Origins and notes model
Three minutes: review corroboration, two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing. Review independent account: a separate teller, not a copy. Read notes A/B/C and use [[s02-K22-1]] before steps.
[[steps:s02-K22-1]]
Three minutes: demonstrate blank note-frame mechanics. Put a question before source labels, then a detail beside its maker. Use [[s02-P22-2]] without completing a country report. Two minutes: teach order, detail and accuracy separately through [[s02-P22-3]], [[s02-P22-4]] and [[s02-P22-5]]. Never model a complete critique or the exit's country comparison.

#### 17 min. Origin work
First five minutes extend day 21's priority; others annotate origins. Turn back to Side 1. Everyone completes [[s02-P22]] and [[s02-P22-1]]. A conflict calls for another relevant check, not majority voting. Two matching copies supply only one origin. Agreement supports the checked detail, not unrelated conclusions. Children revise their own note placement and criterion fragments. Collect all annotations for day 23.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the question board. Partners each take 45 seconds explaining what separate origins add, 90 seconds total. Public passing is allowed. Record the class's source-selection answer. Keep blank headings for country research; remove completed teaching entries before day 23. No complete location-plus-limit response is rehearsed.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-22.md, exactly [[s02-X22]], with its map and separately authored geography card. Every child compares independently on the half slip; neutral reading or dictation is available. Collect all. Mark afterward: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; 30 times 75 seconds, 37.5 minutes, for practice. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; note access separately.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: cut public explanations; retain origins, note mechanics, criterion fragments and exit. Longer: describe a further independent check for the conflicting count.

### Words and supports
Review corroboration, independent account and source log. Accuracy means a detail matches evidence. Order helps readers find information. Read neutrally, enlarge or take dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 23 researches people and languages using blank note headings. Return s02-X22 and practice. Errors D/N, unrelated conclusions or titles without details, enter Return and repair and the first five Research work minutes. Withdraw filled logs; retain country questions.
