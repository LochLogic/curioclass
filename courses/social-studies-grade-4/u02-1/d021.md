# Day 21: Check claims against relevant records

## Teacher page

### Before class
Use the generated key for practice; use key-exits.md for exits. Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md; prepare record.md and returned day 20 work. The student page incorporates practice-21.md and selected source-log.md fields. Retain the question-board.md. Keep exit-21.md hidden. All teaching claims and records describe a made-up classroom.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return s02-X20 and day 20's written task: s02-P20, s02-P20-R, s02-K20-1, s02-P20-1, s02-P20-2. If many show error B, printed means current, annotate [[s02-P21-R]] together. Otherwise meet one group while others write. Review context: a date and audience narrow a source's use. Carry the same priority into Evidence work, protecting the model.

#### 8 min. Claims and evidence
Review checkable and opinion: a liking is not a reason. A checkable claim has a way to test it; it can be false. Call a claim a fact only when suitable evidence establishes it. Use [[s02-K21-1]] before revealing its steps.
[[steps:s02-K21-1]]
Read the already labeled claims on Side 2. An inventory tests a count; a receipt tests delivery; a personal liking has no agreed best-color test. Model the conflicting chair count without combining a preference and fact into a sign judgment.

#### 17 min. Evidence work
First five minutes extend the day 20 date/audience repair; others match records independently. Complete [[s02-P21]] and [[s02-P21-1]]. Isolate the errors in [[s02-P21-2]]: a preference proves no count; one preference cancels no other claim; checkable does not mean true. Keep these three component links separate. Use the source log's useful-detail field for verification notes. If many confuse claim and evidence, place their two exact lines beside each other.

#### 5 min. Compare reasons
Review the question board. Partners each take 45 seconds explaining one evidence link, 90 seconds total. They may point to the printed record or pass publicly. Update "Which sources help us?" with claim-by-claim checking. Collect every page and note for day 22; no extra oral queue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-21.md, exactly [[s02-X21]]. Children judge the new sign independently on the half slip. Read neutrally or take dictation without hints. Collect all. Mark afterward: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; 30 times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for practice. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; record access separately.

### Other rooms
Copy the short labeled claims and records without a printer. Keep evidence beside its claim on paper; no screen is required.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy claims and records on separate paper strips.
**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.
- **5 min. Return.** Read yesterday's cue; complete [[s02-P21-R]].
- **8 min. Evidence.** Try [[s02-K21-1]], then read its steps.
- **12 min. Match.** Complete [[s02-P21]] and [[s02-P21-1]]. **Say.** "Does checkable mean true?" Expect: no; the inventory contradicts five chairs.
- **5 min. Reasons.** Work [[s02-P21-2]] as three separate links.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X21]]. Your child answers alone. Discuss the key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce partner sharing, keeping all three error links. Longer: identify a record that could test another classroom count.

### Words and supports
Review checkable, opinion, context and source log. Accept evidence pointing, labeled arrows or dictation. Keep grammar separate from the source judgment.

### Tomorrow
Day 22 checks origins and agreement. Return s02-X21 and practice. Errors C/K/L, preference as proof, opinion cancels all, or checkable means true, get one fresh evidence-link move in Return and repair and Origin work.
