# Day 111: Dates on one class timeline

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read opener. Print cards-111, practice-111, record and source-index. Separate teacher-key and generated item keys. Hold exit-111-stimulus, exit-111-line and exit-111. Cut the 48 admission-cards; prepare the two exit records separately. Sort the 48 among fifteen pairs: twelve sets of three, three of four. Prepare five class-line copies at one common enlargement, with year ticks and separate lanes. Keep complete reference dates teacher-side; prepare day 110 returns.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 110's exit tickets and all practice. Review Return and repair: one priority, then fresh paper. Assign only [[s06-R111-B]], [[s06-R111-L]] or [[s06-R111-M]], matching error B, error L or error M. If more than a third share one need, repair together. Others begin their date checks. Extra repair uses the first five Class timeline minutes.

#### 7 min. Recall directions
Review grade 3's timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks.
**Ask.** [[s06-K111-spacing]]
Then use its explanation. Review admission, joining as a state, from day 81. Review grade 3's constitution: a basic plan that sets up a government, says what it may and may not do, and may list rights. Read original-state labels as agreement to the Constitution; later-state records show admission. Neither label dates independence. Changed control alone does not prove admission. Do not model a two-record span.

#### 20 min. Class timeline
Pairs verify assigned records with [[s06-P111-1]] on practice-111. Each child records one assigned check; partners alternate pointing. Keep equal scales across strips; shared years use separate lanes. Review region: a group of states that sit together and share something. Use our five classroom groupings. Five placers assemble strips, one minute each. Everyone verifies the nine Northeast entries using the checklist, without reconstructing an individual timeline. Children then solve [[s06-P111-2]] as bare-year subtraction, apart from state cards. Collect every child's paper for feedback. No public queue of thirty turns.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket
Cover class strips. Reveal exit-111-stimulus and exit-111-line for the first time. Children answer [[s06-X111a]] and [[s06-X111b]] on the supplied line and half slip. Read neutrally; a scribe follows the child's directions. Collect all paper for after-class marking and return on day 112.

#### 2 min. Close
Add the two withheld cards after collecting exits, completing fifty records. Keep the class line through day 130, covered during independent checks. Reopen the question about statehood's institutions.

### Other rooms
No wall space: keep five strips in a folder. No printer: copy assigned records and equally spaced lines; hold exit records separately. Enlarge every regional scale together.

### At home
**Swap.** No class set: copy assigned records and one equal-year line.
**Skip.** Five placers and checking every class card.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 110 paper. Use one assigned repair if needed.
- **7 min. Recall directions.** Use [[s06-K111-spacing]].
  **Say.** "What does each equal gap show?" Expect: ten years.
- **17 min. Class timeline.** Check three assigned records with [[s06-P111-1]]. Use practice-111. Solve [[s06-P111-2]] separately. Read the nine Northeast entries together.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Reveal exit-111-stimulus and its line. Your child answers [[s06-X111a]] and [[s06-X111b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit partner explanations, keeping all placements and exit. Longer: check two same-year cards' lanes.

### Words and supports
Review timeline, region, admission and constitution. Read dates aloud; enlarge ticks. Record content help separately from access support.

### Tomorrow
After class, mark thirty packets at 40 seconds each, 20 minutes outside the lesson. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component on record. Day 112 returns day 111's practice and exit. Error C uses a fresh single-date spacing retry; arithmetic slips use subtraction. Error N checks event labels. Choose one priority, with remaining repair in Institution work. Then examine institutions and voting limits.
