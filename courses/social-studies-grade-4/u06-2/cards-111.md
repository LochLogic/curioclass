# Day 111 teaching cards

Use admission-cards.md and class-line.svg. Hold Wyoming and Hawaii until after the exit.

Equal years need equal spaces. Same-year cards share a year in separate lanes.
Original thirteen dates here mean constitutional ratification. They do not date independence.
Later-state dates mean admission. A change of control does not automatically create statehood.

Assign three or four cards per pair. Five placers assemble regional strips.
