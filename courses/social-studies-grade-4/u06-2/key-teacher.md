## s06-X112a

Explain what state institutions would give the territory's residents.

Key: A state constitution organizes state government; voting congressional members help make national laws.

## s06-R113-O

Look for: No. A territory can have local government without a final House vote.

Samples: No. A territory can have local government without a final House vote. | No final House vote means no government.

## s06-R113-P

Samples: No. Committee service does not give the delegate a final House vote. | Committee service includes the final House vote.
