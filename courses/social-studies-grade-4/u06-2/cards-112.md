# Day 112 teaching cards

## Institution cards

Constitution: a basic plan that sets up government and limits its powers.
Congress: the national legislature. States have senators and voting House members.
Territory: has local government but lacks state representation in Congress.
Delegate: may serve on committees but lacks the final House vote.

## Historical limit

Statehood did not give everyone equal voting rights. Most women lacked the vote at the founding.

Match isolated cards to functions. Do not build the held petition response in practice.
