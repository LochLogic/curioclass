# Day 65: Aims and ways of sailing

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 64 papers with one cue each. Keep teacher-key.md and the generated answer key separate. Cover exit-65.md and the student-page Exit box. Use practice-65.md as a teacher stimulus master, not another child response sheet. Prepare its four cards separately. All hypothetical rulers and repair drawings are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 64's exit tickets and written tasks. If day 64's exit showed error B, connect the whetstone with sharpening using [[s04-P65-5]]. Error C: recall earlier Indigenous use; error U: presence alone cannot locate a meeting. Observed error A: distinguish a modern drawing from eyewitness evidence. Choose one priority if more than a third missed it; otherwise meet a group.

Review grade 3's colony: a settlement far from the home country, which claims it. Use [[s04-P65-7]] with Side 1's hypothetical clue.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Whole class: distinguish aims from ways of traveling. Explain one aim at a time: reaching Asian trade, obtaining resources, establishing colonies, extending an empire. Source JOURNAL supports westward aims and claimed authority; Source CARTIER supports seeking riches. Do not expose its held commission passage.

Review natural resource: something from nature that people use. Review trade: goods move from where they are made to where sold. Empire means one power rules several lands or peoples. Conquest means taking control by force. Review grade 3: enslavement forces people into bondage and denies freedom; people are never goods.

Model Card 2. **Ask.** [[s04-P65-2]] Expect trade: markets are the clue. A voyage can serve several aims; this card isolates one.

Review compass direction from grade 1. Explain the maritime compass's direction role, Source COMPASS. Review latitude from day 12. A mariner's astrolabe estimates north-south position using sky angles, Source ASTROLABE. Discuss only; no Sun viewing or instrument handling. Source SHIP describes triangular lateen sails helping a caravel sail closer to the wind.

#### 17 min. Reason work
First five finish the repair. Use Side 1's fresh modern-maker clue and [[s04-P65-6]]; expect no: the drawing interprets evidence. Its maker lacked eyewitness access. Feedback bounds what the drawing can establish.

Children classify Card 1 with [[s04-P65-1]], choose direction equipment with [[s04-P65-3]], and identify the design feature with [[s04-P65-4]]. Each response stands alone. Fifteen parallel pairs explain one choice, 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total. Give teacher feedback. Review retained source-log prompts and voyage map. Last three minutes: cover practice, reveal [[s04-P65-8]] in the Exit box, and collect independent corrections without feedback.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's board routine. Group A's six explain their Card 1 choice, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Public pass allowed. Record names; retain completed Exit boxes. Cover practice before exit.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the half slip. Read [[s04-X65-1]] and [[s04-X65-2]] exactly. Children work independently. After class: thirty times ninety seconds, 45 minutes for all papers, including Exit boxes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Return all papers on day 66.

### Other rooms

  **Say.** Which aim fits? Expect trade, because the ruler wants a market route.
- **12 min. Reason work.** Use [[s04-P65-6]]. Explain navigation without handling instruments. Read Cards 1, 3 and 4; use [[s04-P65-1]], [[s04-P65-3]] and [[s04-P65-4]]. In the last three minutes, reveal [[s04-P65-8]] without hints.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated pair explanations. Longer: rehear one card and explain its clue again. Protect repair and exit.

### Words and supports
Review colony, natural resource, compass direction, latitude and enslavement. New: empire, conquest, mariner's astrolabe and lateen sail. Read neutrally; accept dictation.
