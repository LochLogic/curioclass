# Day 64: What remains can show

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 63 papers with one cue. Keep teacher-key.md and the generated answer key separate. Cover the exit master and its image. Use practice-64.md as a teacher stimulus master; children use today's page. Prepare three route labels from its first card. No photograph or live source is needed.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 63's exit tickets and practice pages. Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose, from day 62. If day 63's exit showed error H, read the made-up message on Side 1; use [[s04-P64-4]]. Expect map readers, not today's class. Error M: distinguish the requested action from a source's title. Prioritize one gap if more than a third missed it; otherwise meet a small group.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Whole class: Norse seafarers traveled from northern Europe through Iceland and Greenland toward Atlantic North America around 1000. Source SAGA supplies the route; Source CAMP records the camp at L'Anse aux Meadows.

Review archaeology from day 61. Read Card 2 aloud. **Ask.** [[s04-P64-2]] Wait, then model: a recorded whetstone is an object used for sharpening. It supports tool use. It cannot tell us everyone's thoughts. Children mark their classification.

Separately explain: sagas are stories told before being written down. Source ENCOUNTER describes regional encounters; these records do not identify a modern nation as participants. Never guess an identity or reenact an encounter.

#### 17 min. Evidence work
First five finish the opening repair with the fresh Side 1 message and [[s04-P64-5]]. Expect bringing boards. Give immediate feedback.

Pairs order detached route labels for [[s04-P64-1]], then add only that segment to the accumulating voyage map. No camp or encounter account joins this response. Separately read Card 3 and use [[s04-P64-3]]. Expect earlier Indigenous use, rejecting empty land, error C. Children answer individually. Review the source-log routine from day 19; retain its blank prompts without adding another task.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's question board routine. Group E's six explain their Card 3 response, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Public pass allowed. Record names and one bounded answer under the source question. Collect every page; cover practice before exit.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-64.md and its image now. Read [[s04-X64-1]] and [[s04-X64-2]] exactly. Children answer independently on the half slip; neutral reading or dictation is available. After class: thirty children times sixty seconds, 30 minutes for all papers. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; unobserved stays blank. Return all papers on day 65.

### Other rooms
No printer: hand-copy today's page and the covered exit image with every label. Read the saga note neutrally. A text-only replacement loses the plan evidence.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated route rehearsal. Longer: reverse the loose labels before ordering again. Protect repair and exit.

### Words and supports
Norse: seafarers from northern Europe. Saga: a story recorded after oral telling. Review material evidence, source-sourcing and the source-log prompts. Read cards aloud; allow dictated answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 65 examines voyage aims and navigation. Return all papers. Error B needs bounded evidence; error C needs earlier presence; error U needs the difference between presence and meeting. Observed error A needs maker access. Share one priority across Return and repair and Reason work's first five minutes.
