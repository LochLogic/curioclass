# Day 66: Arrival and human consequences

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 65 papers with one cue each. Keep teacher-key.md and the generated answer key separate. Cover exit-66.md, its inset and the student-page Exit box. Prepare three loose labels from practice-66.md's first card. Use the cards as teacher stimuli, not extra response sheets. No graphic images, reenactment or personal-disclosure questions.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 65's exit tickets and written tasks. Review aims from day 65. If day 65's exit showed error D, use the made-up power clue and [[s04-P66-4]]. Expect empire. For observed error B, distinguish an instrument's contribution from all voyage knowledge. More than a third: repair together; otherwise meet a group. Protect one priority and its fresh retry.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review day 60's Source TAINO, the United Confederation of Taíno People's public history. Taíno people are Indigenous Peoples of the Caribbean; they live today. They were already there when Columbus arrived. Review the Caribbean region from day 10.

Source JOURNAL records departure from Spain through the Canaries. The arrival was in the Bahamas, Source LOC. Model ordering the detached Spain, Canaries, Caribbean labels; these are not a map. Explain the Bahamas are within the Caribbean region. Point to the Caribbean on the accumulating map without exposing the exit inset.

Separately read Card 2. Review forced labor: work people could not refuse, from grade 3. Review enslavement from day 65. Columbus's men enslaved Taíno people. Source HARMS describes violence, forced labor and disease contributing to severe population losses. Some Taíno people fled forced labor. People did not disappear. Do not read the source's stereotypes or disputed totals. Never invite children to act as captives or captors.

#### 17 min. Route work
First five finish the repair with the fresh wood clue and [[s04-P66-5]]. Expect resources. Give immediate feedback.

Children independently order labels for [[s04-P66-1]]. Read Card 2 again, separately; use [[s04-P66-2]]. Expect forced labor or violence, not disease alone, error L. Read the made-up Card 3 notice; **Ask.** [[s04-P66-3]] Expect assertion. A visitor's announcement cannot establish residents' agreement, error N. No child represents a people. Review source-log prompts. Last three minutes: cover practice, reveal [[s04-P66-6]] in the Exit box, and collect independent corrections without feedback.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's board routine. Group B's six explain their Card 3 classification, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Public pass allowed. Record names and add the bounded conclusion to the board. Retain completed Exit boxes. Cover all practice.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the complete map and caption now. Read [[s04-X66-1]] and [[s04-X66-2]] exactly. Children trace and write on the half slip, independently. Neutral reading or dictation is available. After class: thirty times ninety seconds, 45 minutes for all papers, including Exit boxes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Return all papers on day 67.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the student page and covered inset with every label. Loose scraps replace route tiles. Read the caption neutrally, without suggesting a route or consequence.

**Swap.** Use scraps for tiles. Copy pages if needed; keep both exit pieces covered until assigned.

  **Say.** Does the announcement record agreement? Expect no, only an assertion.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated label ordering. Longer: reread Card 3 and explain its missing agreement evidence. Protect repair, living continuity and exit.

### Words and supports
Review Caribbean, empire, resources, enslavement and forced labor. Taíno names living people. Assertion means a statement someone makes. Read neutrally; accept child-directed tracing or dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 67 compares source evidence and origins. Return all papers. Error C needs inhabited-land evidence; error N needs assertion versus agreement; observed error L needs multiple harms. Share one priority across Return and repair and Evidence work's first five minutes.
