# Day 67: Where a record begins

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 66 papers with one cue each. Keep teacher-key.md and the generated answer key separate. Cover the entire exit master, including its reports. Use practice-67.md as teacher stimuli; children use today's page. All practice records, visitors and places are made up. No child represents a historical community.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 66's exit tickets and written tasks. If day 66's exit showed error N, read Side 1's new visitor notice and use [[s04-P67-4]]. Expect assertion, not recorded agreement. Error C: review day 66's Taíno homeland evidence; the Caribbean was inhabited. Observed error L: recall multiple harms. Repair one priority together if more than a third missed it; otherwise meet a group.

#### 5 min. Recall criteria
Review grade 3's corroboration: two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing. Review independent account: an account made by a separate teller, not a copy, also used on day 22. Evidence independence concerns origins, not different-looking paper.

Read Card 1's origin chain. **Ask.** [[s04-P67-1]] Children circle on their page before feedback. Expect the tide register. A copied entry adds no second observer, error K. Use the supplied chain, not matching words, to establish copying. No new model is introduced.

#### 20 min. Evidence work
First five finish the repair. Use the fresh storehouse record and [[s04-P67-6]] for error N; expect no recorded consent. For error C, its earlier residents establish inhabited land. Recall day 66's research summary for [[s04-P67-5]]; expect forced labor or violence for error L. Give feedback immediately; affirm living Taíno continuity.

Read Cards 2 and 3 separately. Children individually use [[s04-P67-2]] to choose a relevant record. Expect the invoice, because cloth concerns repair supplies; every source cannot answer every question, error B. Then [[s04-P67-3]] identifies ferry crews. Review source-sourcing from day 62: maker, date, audience, purpose. Use internal clues, not today's reader, error H, or a title alone, error M.

Fifteen parallel pairs explain one chosen clue, 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total. Give teacher feedback. Review source-log prompts and retained voyage-map labels without adding another response. Practice never compares the held landing reports or combines agreement, difference and independence.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's board routine. Group C's six explain their Card 2 choice, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Public pass allowed. Record names and one question-specific contribution, not merely a source-kind name, error S. Collect every page and cover practice.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-67.md now; say its reports are made up. Read [[s04-X67-1]] and [[s04-X67-2]] exactly. Children answer independently on the half slip. After class: thirty times sixty seconds, 30 minutes for all papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; leave unobserved blank. Return all papers on day 68.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy today's page and the covered exit reports, including the provenance line. Read neutrally. No historical letter or live page is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy today's page and the covered exit reports completely.
**Skip.** Public rotation and repeated partner turns.
- **5 min. Return.** Return day 66 papers. Read the visitor notice; use [[s04-P67-4]].
- **5 min. Recall.** Recall the copying rule. Read Card 1 and use [[s04-P67-1]].
  **Say.** Where does this chain begin? Expect the tide register.
- **15 min. Evidence.** Use the fresh storehouse record and [[s04-P67-6]]. Recall the research summary and use [[s04-P67-5]]. Read Cards 2 and 3 separately; use [[s04-P67-2]] and [[s04-P67-3]]. Discuss the clue after each answer.
- **5 min. Board.** Your child explains the chosen repair-supplies record. Expect the invoice's cloth detail.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the complete reports. Use [[s04-X67-1]] and [[s04-X67-2]] without hints. Consult the answer key afterward; return papers next lesson.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated partner explanations. Longer: rehear Card 2 and explain why the other record cannot answer its question. Protect repair and exit.

### Words and supports
Review corroboration, independent account, audience, purpose, source-log prompts, origins and copying history. Read neutrally; allow dictation. Content clues count as help.

### Tomorrow
Day 68 studies exchanges of plants, animals and diseases. Return all papers. Error K needs an origin-chain reminder; observed errors H and M need inside clues; error S needs a contribution rather than a kind name. Share one priority across Return and repair and Exchange work's first five minutes.
