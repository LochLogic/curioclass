# Teacher-only day 2 answers

Teacher use only. Keep this page away from student sheets.

Before class, read each answer beside its printed stimulus. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## s01-F2-1

Change river to lake.

Look for: Matches the hatched enclosed mark to lake, not river.

## s01-F2-2

Page-up is south.

Look for: Orients from the arrow; does not change a real geographic map arrow.

## s01-P2-1

Follows the solid line through the central land area.

Look for: Traces the named river line, not a leader or coast.

## s01-P2-2

The hatched enclosed Lake Superior outline traced.

Look for: Finds the leader endpoint and traces the enclosed water feature.

## s01-P2-3

The water west of the continent.

Look for: Locates the named ocean; no location pair or category explanation.

## s01-P2-4

The solid line marks a river; the hatch marks a lake. Accept one accurate observation.

Look for: Names a key mark and its meaning.

## s01-P2-R

Change water to high land.

Look for: Uses the supplied fresh key; independent of the exit symbols.

## s01-P2-E

Hudson Bay: broad inlet at 1. Lake Winnipeg: enclosed hatched water at 2.

Look for: Both features correctly marked on the map.

## s01-X2

Lake Winnipeg: its enclosed outline has the diagonal hatch that the key assigns to a lake.

Look for: Names Lake Winnipeg.; Links the enclosed hatched mark to the lake key, not just the name.

## s01-P2-RC

## s01-P2-RJ
