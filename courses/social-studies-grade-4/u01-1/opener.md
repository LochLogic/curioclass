# Section 1.1 opener

## The ideas

A legend assigns meaning; shape alone does not. A turned north arrow changes page directions. Physical maps select land and water; political maps select boundaries. A missing nation label cannot establish absence. Present lands and ancestral homelands differ. Inuit are an Indigenous people; Nunavut is a territory. A physical region groups shared features.

## Common errors and response

A: appearance replaces legend; B: page-up means north; C: bar count becomes ground distance; E: place categories collapse; F: nations are only past; J: only one map is useful; K: omission means empty land; M: preference replaces another reader's need; Q: physical categories are confused.

Many means more than 10 of 30. Repair one priority gap in Return and repair, five minutes. A second gap receives one five-minute group during Supported work. Others follow written cues and submit a fresh response. Never stack repairs in one slot or name a child as an error.
