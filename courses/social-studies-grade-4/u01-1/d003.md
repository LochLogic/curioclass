# Day 3: Landforms and living peoples

## Teacher page

### Before class
Continue day 1's observation rotation. Prepare the shared pieces listed above. Keep key-03.md teacher-only. Return day 2 papers, including s01-X2, with one private cue. Keep the new exit source separate until Exit ticket. Retain the record sheet and needed references.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 2's exit ticket s01-X2, Exit box, and observation and both exit responses. If many showed error A, use [[s01-P3-R]] for a fresh symbol repair. If error Q dominates, change the water tag to the key's category instead. For error B, review the arrow with a small group during Supported work. Everyone makes one fresh attempt; take the dominant gap only.

#### 10 min. Land and living peoples
Review grade 3's physical feature, legend and four-part map card. Review its region: places grouped by something shared. Extend this to a physical region, land grouped by shared physical features. Recall grade 2's Rocky Mountains as a reminder. Model [[s01-K3-1]], following the leader, then outline the patch. Introduce [[s01-P3-1]] separately.

Read Source 3N, Source 3C and Source 3I. Review the Navajo Nation and Cherokee Nation from grade 3. Introduce Inuit of Inuit Nunangat. Inuit are a people; Nunavut is a territory. Our map selects landforms. Its missing community labels tell us nothing about whether communities exist. Present lands and ancestral homelands are different descriptions; these cards do not map homeland boundaries.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Children attempt [[s01-F3-1]], then [[s01-P3-2]] independently. Collect this naming response for a component record and return it day 4. Afterward, separately match [[s01-P3-3]]. The shapes are made-up category cues, not land boundaries. Never pair the caption correction with locating two landforms. Use five minutes for one repair group of up to four; others compare their attempted correction with its source. Corrected silhouette work stays in folders. Hear six new children at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, while others work independently; record names. Collect captions for feedback tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the question board. Pairs compare their caption corrections in two simultaneous 45-second turns. Everyone may pass in public talk. Take up every caption. Add a source-supported present-tense detail beside the question about what maps leave out.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal Side 2 now. Alone: [[s01-X3]] on the map, keeping the half slip with it, and [[s01-P3-E]] in the Exit box. These are new landforms and a new map. Collect both. Mark after class: 30 children times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component on the record sheet. Hand back day 4.

### Other rooms
No internet is needed: use the printed credited cards. Without a printer, copy the locator and read cards aloud. Draw the exit inset only at exit time.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the landform patches and source cards. No map pins: use pencil marks.
**Skip.** The observation group and public sharing.
- **5 min. Return.** Read yesterday's cue and correct that work.
- **10 min. Land and people.** Trace [[s01-K3-1]]. Read the three cards aloud. **Say.** "Which two cards name Native nations?" Expect Navajo Nation and Cherokee Nation.
- **10 min. Repair.** Your child attempts [[s01-F3-1]], then matches [[s01-P3-3]]. **Say.** "What does the source establish today?" Expect that the Navajo Nation has lands today.
- **5 min. Discuss.** Compare the rewritten caption with Source 3N.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Side 2. Your child completes [[s01-P3-E]] and [[s01-X3]] alone. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce paired sharing, retaining all source cards and the independent exit. Longer: identify something each card establishes that the physical map cannot.

### Words and supports
Review: desert means dry land, hot or cold. Review grade 3's region; physical grouping extends it. Read labels aloud; use raised patterns and dictated captions. No child represents a people.

### Tomorrow
Day 4 returns day 3's captions and both exit responses. If many show error K, repair an inference about an omitted label. If error F dominates, correct a past-only caption from the taught card. If error A or error Q dominates, repair a category instead. Then assemble mountain belts across the two Americas.

### Credit
Ours. Source 3G supplies public-domain geographic outlines. Source 3N, Source 3C and Source 3I supply the credited factual paraphrases. Source 3D and Source 3S support exit locator positions.
