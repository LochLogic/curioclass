# Teacher-only day 3 answers

Teacher use only. Keep this page away from student sheets.

Before class, read each answer beside its printed stimulus. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## s01-P3-R

Dots mean hills.

Look for: Follows the supplied fresh key.

## s01-K3-1

The hatched western mountain patch traced.

Look for: Follows the leader to the patterned patch.; Uses a physical grouping, not a country border.

## s01-P3-1

Dotted patch matches desert.

Look for: Matches the dotted patch to desert.

## s01-F3-1

The Navajo Nation has lands in Utah, Arizona and New Mexico today.

Look for: Uses present tense and a fact stated by the named source.

## s01-P3-2

Navajo Nation and Cherokee Nation. Inuit are an Indigenous people; Nunavut is a territory.

Look for: Names Navajo Nation and Cherokee Nation.; Does not turn Nunavut into a Native nation.

## s01-P3-3

## s01-P3-E

The map selects landform information and omits nation labels. Missing labels cannot establish whether people live there.

Look for: Explains selection or omission.; Rejects the inference that no label means no inhabitants.

Look for: Marks both named landforms using the index and patterns.
