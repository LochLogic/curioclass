# Teacher-only day 1 answers

Teacher use only. Keep this page away from student sheets.

Before class, read each answer beside its printed stimulus. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## s01-P1-1

A shows water features; B shows country borders.

Look for: Names a visible detail on each map.

## s01-P1-2

Title, legend, north arrow and scale bar circled.

Look for: All four parts found; ungraded retrieval.

## s01-P1-3

Walking trail.

Look for: Uses the printed legend; ungraded retrieval.

## s01-P1-4

200 meters, because 4 times 50 is 200.

Look for: Counts four represented lengths; ungraded retrieval.

## s01-P1-5

East: north points left, so east points up.

Look for: Uses the arrow rather than the page edge; ungraded retrieval.

## s01-K1-1

How do map parts help us locate features? matches Map parts.

Look for: Recall: a supporting question helps answer the big question.; Read the supplied question.; Match it to Map parts; do not generate a two-reader question.

## s01-P1-6

Needed: map parts, distance, scale, boundaries, grid. Unrelated: pencil.

Look for: Five map-information strips together; pencil strip apart.

## s01-P1-7

One visible detail annotated with a defensible use.

Look for: Points to a printed feature or border.; Names what that detail helps a reader find.

## s01-X1

Which details does each reader need on a map? Accept another relevant smaller question.

Look for: Question addresses the two stated map needs.

## s01-P1-E

Paths help the student find a walking route. Country locations help the traveler compare countries.

Look for: Explains a defensible need for each reader.; Contrasts the two uses without relying on preference alone.
