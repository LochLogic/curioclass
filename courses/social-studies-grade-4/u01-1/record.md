# Unit 1 component record

Use one scale throughout: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
Access supports never count as content help. Blank means no evidence collected yet.
Never add a total. Note the date, content help and one next move.
Readiness is ungraded retrieval on days 1 and 12; record gaps and access notes only.

The daily paper evidence reaches every child. Oral observations supplement it.
Hear names 1 to 6 on day 1; 7 to 12 on day 2; 13 to 18 on day 3.
Hear names 19 to 24 on day 4; 25 to 30 on day 5.
Each six-child hearing takes 4.5 minutes within its named five-minute slot.
Later six-child hearings rotate through that same roster; date every hearing.

Use a separate cell for each named location, measurement, source link or reasoning component.
The assessment's A4b records source support from A4a; do not score the claim twice.
Day 16 records only the assigned fresh component. Return its written cue day 17.
