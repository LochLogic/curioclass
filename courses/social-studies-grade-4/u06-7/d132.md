# Day 132: Use feedback and try again

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read day 131's assessment and day 130 papers. Assign one priority and a written move per child; retain other gaps. Have cards-132, practice-132, response-132, opener, record, source-index and key-teacher. Use key-teacher for fresh items. Keep retries covered and separate from teaching. Print retry-line and retry-grid at matching source/response size. Use only the specific justice caption on this page.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Children privately read one cue from day 131's assessment. If many missed a component, assign its response group. Include day 130's error V or error I when relevant; do not stack another opening repair. Review the component scale without public ranking. No child must describe personal experiences.

#### 20 min. Response groups

Return day 130's written tasks: s06-P130-1, s06-P130-2, s06-P130-3, s06-W130-1, s06-W130-2, s06-P130-4, s06-P130-5. Use the existing private cue and repair time.

Return every day 131 assessment and all day 130 tickets, Exit boxes, practice and repair papers within this block. Retain error K, capital location, and error W, public use, in the status/offices meeting. Four sequential five-minute adult meetings: locating/dates; status/offices; maps/dated land; explanations/exchange/claims. Each child attends one priority meeting; others follow written moves independently. Each meeting holds at most four 45-second responses, three minutes, plus two minutes for modeling and handling. Children may pass publicly; written evidence reaches all.

Use cards-132's separate teaching cases. Review equal-year timeline from day 111 with [[s06-W132-1]]. For public records, review day 127 with [[s06-W132-2]]. Review key/location matching from day 114 with [[s06-W132-3]], using the printed Hill Basin clues. Review day 124's single-actor sale/purchase relation with [[s06-W132-4]]. Ask before showing key-teacher answers. Unrepresented status, land-history or locating gaps receive a written next move; do not claim these four tasks reassess them.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Cover group examples. Reveal only each child's assigned fresh component: [[s06-P132-1]], [[s06-P132-2]], [[s06-P132-3]] or [[s06-P132-4]]. These cases are made up; the justice's role is real. Use retry-line for dates and retry-grid for the map. Neutral reading or exact dictation is available. Collect all attempts. Begin checking during independent work, without content hints; note additional oral-support needs for follow-up.

#### 5 min. Reflect and hand on
Return every retry and assigned repair paper with one private evidence cue. Children point to a supported improvement in their own work. No additional judged response is required. Keep original, cue, correction and fresh attempt together. Retain all unfinished components and access needs for days 133/134; never rehearse their geographic source set.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy only the assigned text and trace the supplied grid or line. No visitor, live page or photograph is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy clues and trace the matching grid or line.
**Skip.** Class groups and roster turns.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 130 and day 131 papers. Choose one priority.
- **20 min. Response.** Use the matching teaching question on Side 1. Give feedback and let your child correct that one relation. Keep Side 2 hidden.
  **Say.** "Which detail supports your correction?" Expect a detail from the assigned teaching record.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Cover teaching. Reveal only the assigned retry: [[s06-P132-1]], [[s06-P132-2]], [[s06-P132-3]] or [[s06-P132-4]]. Your child answers independently.
- **5 min. Reflect.** Return one evidence cue. Save the first and fresh attempts together; list unresolved ideas for the next lesson.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated group responses, preserving the fresh attempt. Longer: use remaining time for the assigned written move, without borrowing future tasks.

### Words and supports
Review timeline, key, source, reasoning, branch and exchange. Enlarge or scribe without changing decisions.

### Tomorrow
Use record's single component scale, keeping unobserved components blank. Four teacher meetings hear at most sixteen turns, not thirty. Allow twenty seconds per retry, ten minutes for thirty: five during fresh work and five during reflection. All receive a same-day cue. Record unfinished marking for private completion, never as accomplished. Day 133 receives component/access notes and a new province atlas. Day 134 receives new history sources. Neither receives rehearsed answers.
