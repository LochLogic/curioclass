# Day 130: Recall with feedback

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare pages and five blank retrieval outlines from the regional outline masters. Keep atlas reference entries covered until checking. Have cards-130, practice-130, opener, source-index, key-teacher, record and day 129 papers. Use key-teacher for all items. Hold exit-130-stimulus, exit-130 and the Exit box covered. Reveal the half slip and box together only at the exit.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 129's written tasks: s06-P129-1, s06-W129-1. Use the existing private cue and repair time.

Return day 129's s06-X129a, s06-X129b, independent displays and repair papers. If many day 129 papers showed error Y, review makers using [[s06-W130-1]]. For error X, point to a detail rather than a preference. For error G, use [[s06-W130-2]]. All children mark their assigned choice; no drafting or revising prose.

#### 5 min. Retrieve locations
Review state and capital from days 108 to 110. Alone, label Maine, Kentucky, Nebraska, South Dakota and Nevada with recalled capitals on fresh outlines. Complete [[s06-P130-1]] by checking atlas-northeast, atlas-southeast, atlas-midwest and atlas-west. Change misses with a second mark. No speed target.

#### 10 min. Corrective pairing
Pairs locate each missed reference entry, point to its state boundary and cover it for another try. Those ready repeat the isolated Congress/branch match [[s06-P130-4]] and the record/question choice [[s06-P130-5]]. Review branches from day 126 and public records from day 127. No national three-building table or full admission set is rehearsed.

#### 15 min. Recall with feedback
Review natural resource: something from nature that people use. Recall specialize from grade 3: a business that makes mostly one kind of thing; day 124 extended this to workers. Use [[s06-P130-2]] and [[s06-P130-3]]. The shoemaker is made up. Partners take two simultaneous one-minute turns, pointing and explaining. Teacher hears six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes within this block. Public passing is allowed. Show current data answers for immediate correction, then repeat with the same isolated cards. Collect everyone's marked page; no paragraph writing.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Reveal exit-130-stimulus and its map; these records are made up. Children answer [[s06-X130a]] independently on the half slip and [[s06-X130b]] in the paper Exit box. Read neutrally or scribe exact words. Collect both.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace the five supplied outlines and copy text. Keep held sources separate. No live atlas or screen is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: trace supplied outlines and copy the questions.
**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 129 papers; choose one oral or pointing correction.
- **5 min. Locate.** Your child recalls five capitals, then checks [[s06-P130-1]] against the atlas entries.
- **10 min. Match.** Cover missed entries and recall again. Use [[s06-P130-4]] and [[s06-P130-5]].
- **15 min. Recall.** Complete [[s06-P130-2]] and [[s06-P130-3]] by marking or pointing.
  **Say.** "What does the shoemaker buy?" Expect: food.
  Give feedback and repeat; do not draft paragraphs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the new map. Your child answers [[s06-X130a]] and [[s06-X130b]] alone on paper. Save both for day 132.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated recall, preserving feedback and exit. Longer: cover the same reference entries and try again.

### Words and supports
Review state, capital, maker, natural resource, specialize, legislative and archive. Marking and pointing replace practice sentences. Exact dictation remains a paper exit.

### Tomorrow
Mark thirty sets at 45 seconds each, 22.5 minutes after class. Use record's single component scale. Error V means other needs go unmet; error I confuses national and state work. Error T misclassifies built resources, error K misidentifies capitals, error W misses public use. Day 131 checks independently; no opening repair. Return every paper in day 132 Response groups.

### Credit
Ours. Geographic references use checked public data in source-index; institutional facts use Sources s06-branches, s06-house and s06-capitol. Recall actors and records are made up.
