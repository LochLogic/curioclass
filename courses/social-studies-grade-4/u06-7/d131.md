# Day 131: Use new geographic evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Assemble the held packet from end-source-book, F1-locations, F2-cards, F4-sources, F5-source and F6-source. Include the source layers and response masters listed above. Keep key-teacher, record, source-index, opener and revised day 119 portfolios adult-only. Use key-teacher for current answers. Cover teaching examples. Retain geographic reference entries for consultation. Keep sources and responses at identical size. Read sensitive secular captions privately if requested.

### The day
#### 2 min. Orient
Review the independent-source routine without examples. Everyone works alone with new sources visible. Give neutral reading, enlarged maps or exact dictation. No causal hints or answer feedback. Day 130 tickets wait until day 132; no repair interrupts this assessment.

#### 5 min. Locate and date
Use [[s06-F1a]] through [[s06-F1g]] on F-locator/F-ocean. Use [[s06-F2a]] and [[s06-F2b]] on F2-line, with F2-cards. Children give concise labels for [[s06-F2c]] and [[s06-F2d]]. Regional atlas entries and island-reference stay available; locate from evidence, without speed scoring.

#### 13 min. Map evidence
Allow nine minutes for [[s06-F3d]] on atlas-I-response, consulting the five separate I layers and neutral factual captions. Dotted locators are not legal boundaries; arrows are not precise travel paths. Allow four minutes for [[s06-F3a]], [[s06-F3b]], [[s06-F3c]], [[s06-F3e]] on F3F/F3G/F3H/F3J-partial. No completed map is shown. Score only assigned additions; revised day 119 portfolios supply other full-map evidence. [[s06-F3u]] is an adult observation of F3d, never another prompt.

#### 9 min. Inquiry and claim
Use R and S for [[s06-F4q]], [[s06-F4g]], [[s06-F4c]], [[s06-F4s]] and [[s06-F4i]]. One short response or labeled diagram may answer them together. Do not imply an unshown soil zone or exact shipping route. Record [[s06-F4settle]] and [[s06-F4move]] from that same response, without additional asks.

#### 3 min. Economics
Ana and the dairy are made up. Use the two-row F5-source response for [[s06-F5w]] and [[s06-F5b]]. Accept plausible purchases meeting other needs; no unsupported barter requirement.

#### 3 min. Capital
Use F6-source for [[s06-F6a]], [[s06-F6b]] and [[s06-F6c]]. Concise labels suffice. No completed national building table or adult key is visible.

#### 5 min. Collect and reflect
Collect every response before discussion. Children privately point to one uncertain component; no public ranking. Begin sorting. Adult records [[s06-F2-se]], [[s06-F2-mw]], [[s06-F2-sw]], [[s06-F2-we]] from the same F2 timeline, without four further prompts. Leave unfinished components unobserved rather than assuming failure or success.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace supplied outlines and layers at matching size; copy the held text separately. No live page, photograph or visitor is required.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: trace matching sources and outlines; copy the held text.
**Skip.** Class sorting and public discussion.
- **40 min. Assessment.** Start with the assessment. Your child works independently through the student page and its held sources. Use the class's seven parts in order. Read neutrally or write exact dictated words; give no content hints. Collect all attempts before opening the adult key.
  **Say.** "Which part felt uncertain?" Expect any privately named component, not a corrected answer.
- **5 min. Reflect.** Save the attempt and note one feedback priority for tomorrow. Do not rehearse the retry cards.

### Shorter or longer
Keep the component order. Record unfinished work and access needs; allow separate completion rather than supplying answers.

### Words and supports
Review map conventions, source makers and evidence routines through neutral directions only. Sensitive land captions name the Mescalero Apache Tribe specifically. No reenactment or personal disclosure.

### Tomorrow
Mark after class: three minutes per child, ninety minutes for thirty, untested. Use record's single component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep every component separate, including original, feedback and revision evidence. Day 132 returns day 131's assessment and day 130 papers in Response groups. Assign one priority, then a fresh attempt; retain all other gaps.
