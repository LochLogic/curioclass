# Day 129: An independent evidence display

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and held half slips. Keep teacher-key and record adult-only; use current data keys for added questions. Have cards-129, practice-129, opener, source-index, question board and day 128 papers. Copy regional notes before returning them. Cover exit-129 and its stimulus. Remove paragraph frames; retain the checklist.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 128's s06-X128 and all three practice responses, plus assigned repair papers. If many day 128 papers showed error J or error X, use [[s06-W129-1]]. The workshop list and receipt are made up. For error G, point to one note's limits. Handle one priority per child within this block; meet smaller gaps during independent work.

#### 5 min. Prepare
Review grade 3's claim sheet and different-maker rule. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Recall the five-region cases from days 122 to 123. Read the checklist; supply no completed claim. The four park histories share a maker; Arizona's project account supplies the other. Review reduced-frame organization from day 100; use independent labels, never a paragraph frame.

#### 20 min. Independent closing response
Children work alone on [[s06-P129-1]], answering the question board's unit question. Independently arrange a claim banner, separate source annotations and reasoning arrows. This labeled display differs from the exit's prose response. Require two actual details and explained links; allow supported differences. Meet the smaller repair group for five minutes without composing a claim. Watch error Y: two pages from one maker.

#### 5 min. Share
Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Each child takes one minute explaining the display; passing publicly is allowed. Use three remaining minutes to inspect maker labels and collect every response. Teacher hears rotating six at 30 seconds each during pair work, three minutes total. Written feedback reaches everyone tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice and reveal exit-129-stimulus and its map. Records and makers are made up. Alone, children answer [[s06-X129a]] and [[s06-X129b]] on the half slip. Read neutrally or scribe exact words. Collect all slips.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy summaries, checklist and blank response space. Draw the held map separately. No screen is required.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy text; draw the held map separately.
**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 128 papers. Use [[s06-W129-1]] for a detail gap.
- **5 min. Prepare.** Read the summaries and checklist.
  **Say.** "Do two park histories give two independent makers?" Expect: no.
- **20 min. Organize.** Your child completes [[s06-P129-1]] with a claim label, source notes and arrows.
- **5 min. Share.** Listen to the display explanation.
  **Say.** "How does each detail support your answer?" Expect an explained connection for each maker.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the new records. Your child answers [[s06-X129a]] and [[s06-X129b]] independently. Save all papers.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping independent work and exit. Longer: annotate an alternative explanation without claiming all jobs are alike.

### Words and supports
Review claim, maker, reasoning and limit. Labels, arrows, enlarged text and exact dictation preserve independent decisions.

### Tomorrow
Mark thirty sets at 60 seconds each, thirty minutes after class. Use record's single component scale. Return all work on day 130. Error Y means one maker counted twice; error X means preference replaces reasoning; error G means a universal job claim. The opening uses oral choices and pointing, without prose revision, before isolated recall.

### Credit
Ours. Regional factual sources match day 128 and are listed in source-index. The workshop repair and exit records are made up.
