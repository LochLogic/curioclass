# Two accounts of railroad workers

These are our summaries of later accounts. Neither is an eyewitness statement.

## Source A
Maker: National Park Service.
Page updated: September 15, 2026.
The page invites readers to explore the park.
The park describes its aim to include overlooked builders.

Chinese workers held many railroad jobs, including masonry, carpentry and track work.
They received less pay than white workers.
In June 1867, Chinese workers stopped work together to seek better treatment.

## Source B
Maker: Library of Congress.
Guide updated: November 18, 2026.
The page supplies research books and links.

In June 1867, Chinese railroad workers sought better pay and shorter workdays.
Their strike did not achieve its demands then.
Their work helped make the railroad possible.

Both accounts discuss the same workers. Neither tells every worker's personal story.
