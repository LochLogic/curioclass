# Day 134: Build a claim from two accounts

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read key-checks-133-136 privately. Prepare component columns on record-checks-133-136. Print sources-check-134 and keep it unseen. Cover taught examples. Arrange neutral reading or exact dictation for access. After both checks, allow ninety minutes for thirty packets, three minutes each, an untested estimate.

### The day
#### 3 min. Orient
Day 133's assessment supplied map evidence; return it on day 135. Introduce independent history work without discussing answers. Content note: these accounts name unequal racial treatment and organized protest, without graphic detail. No role play or personal disclosure. Explain that our cards summarize later accounts, not workers' own words.

#### 12 min. Source labels
Children read the sources and independently complete [[C3-E134-A1]], [[C3-E134-A2]], [[C3-E134-A3]], [[C3-E134-B1]], [[C3-E134-B2]] and [[C3-E134-B3]]. Keep source labels visible. Distinguish access reading from content clues. Three short records per source sample origin, audience and purpose; accept brief phrases and an indicated clue.

#### 20 min. Claim and evidence
Children independently complete [[C3-E134-C]] and [[C3-E134-R]]. Allow a labeled evidence drawing with dictated reasoning. Keep sources visible and teacher answers covered. No partner checking. A claim may emphasize work, unequal treatment or organized action if both details support it. Do not supply an inference or preferred claim.

#### 5 min. Collect
Children reread their own responses. Collect every source-label and claim paper. Mark components after class with the single descriptive scale. B links A, so do not treat two institutions as independent eyewitnesses. If many miss maker/date, audience, purpose or relevant support, prioritize errors P, Q, R or W in day 135 groups. Write one private move per child across both checks.

### Other rooms
Copy both short accounts and response headings beforehand if printing is unavailable. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. No photograph, live page or personal family story is required.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy both source cards and response headings if printing is unavailable. Keep work in a folder.
**Skip.** Class collection and group discussion.
- **15 min. Assessment source labels.** Give sources-check-134 without explaining its evidence. Your child answers [[C3-E134-A1]], [[C3-E134-A2]], [[C3-E134-A3]], [[C3-E134-B1]], [[C3-E134-B2]] and [[C3-E134-B3]]. Read neutrally if needed.
- **20 min. Claim.** Your child answers [[C3-E134-C]] and [[C3-E134-R]] independently.
- **5 min. Save.** Keep all work. Read the teacher key privately afterward. Choose one useful next-step note across both check days for day 135.

### Shorter or longer
Preserve both sources and the claim. Leave unfinished components unobserved. Longer: reread the same evidence without added prompts.

### Words and supports
Review maker, date, audience and purpose from written days 62 and 63; claim and evidence from day 100. An update date belongs to the account, not the historical event. Separate reasoning from spelling and handwriting.

### Tomorrow
Day 135 returns all day 133 and 134 papers in Feedback. One priority selects M map, G resources, S sourcing or C claim work. Remaining gaps stay on the record.

### Credit
Ours. Sources C3-134A and C3-134B support summaries and metadata. Neither photographs nor source prose are reproduced.
