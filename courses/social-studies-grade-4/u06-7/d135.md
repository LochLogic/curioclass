# Day 135: Use one piece of feedback

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read both checks and key-checks-133-136. Assign one priority and written move per child on record-checks-133-136. Prepare only each child's teaching and held exit cards. Keep exits separate until the ticket block. Draw Teaching M bars at the stated lengths. For S2/S3 include the gate notice and catalog-label panel. All response records are made up; say so.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return day 134's assessment and every day 133 map/explanation paper. Children privately read one evidence cue. If many missed a component, assign its response meeting; prioritize one gap per child. Retain additional gaps. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; leave unobserved blank.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Four sequential five-minute meetings: M calibration/positions, G resource mechanisms, S source origins, C claim support. Model [[C3-K135-M]], [[C3-K135-G]], [[C3-K135-S]], [[C3-K135-S2]], [[C3-K135-S3]] or [[C3-K135-C]] after an attempt, with separate teaching cards. Ask before revealing the teacher key. Children apply written moves to original work; corrections are practice, not fresh evidence.

Each meeting hears two 45-second replies, ninety seconds, leaving three-and-a-half minutes for modeling and handling. Record eight names heard at most. Others follow the same printed cue and submit paper. Children may pass publicly. Every assigned paper gets feedback; no extra oral round is promised. Additional location/convention misses use source comparison on the returned map, without revealing future tickets.

#### 10 min. Fresh retry
Cover teaching. Reveal one assigned held card and matching Side 2 written task per child: [[C3-X135-M]], [[C3-X135-G]], [[C3-X135-S]], [[C3-X135-S2]], [[C3-X135-S3]] or [[C3-X135-C]]. S selects origin, audience or purpose, one task per child. Use the shared Retry box on Side 2. Everyone answers independently. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. A coached correction is not a fresh attempt.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Give everyone [[C3-X135-R1]], a new made-up plan/completion comparison. Children work alone for three minutes. Collect the common ticket and assigned written task retries during the remaining two minutes. Mark after class and choose one move for day 136. Keep the branch result separate from this common evidence check; never mark unassigned components mastered.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy only assigned teaching and separate held cards. Draw exact-length scale bars with a ruler. A paper folder replaces screens and devices.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy assigned cards if printing is unavailable. Use a ruler for M bars.
**Skip.** Class rotations and roster turns.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 134's assessment and day 133's map. Select one next step.
- **20 min. Repair.** Use the matching card and [[C3-K135-M]], [[C3-K135-G]], [[C3-K135-S]], [[C3-K135-S2]], [[C3-K135-S3]] or [[C3-K135-C]]. Give feedback, then correct original work.
  **Say.** "Which detail supports your correction?" Expect a relevant detail from the assigned record.
- **10 min. Fresh retry.** Cover teaching. Give the matching held card and Side 2 task: [[C3-X135-M]], [[C3-X135-G]], [[C3-X135-S]], [[C3-X135-S2]], [[C3-X135-S3]] or [[C3-X135-C]]. Your child answers alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[C3-X135-R1]] alone. Save both the written retry and common ticket. Read the teacher key privately afterward; retain one cue for day 136.

### Shorter or longer
Reduce repeated group replies; preserve the independent ticket. Longer: finish the assigned correction without borrowing confirmation cards.

### Words and supports
Review scale, resource, maker, audience, purpose and evidence in written-day meanings. Give exact dictation or larger text; keep evidence selection with the child.

### Tomorrow
Day 136 Feedback returns every assigned ticket. Errors M/Y, Z, P/Q/R or W select a written move and targeted repair. Confirm one component with a new case; retain other gaps.

### Credit
Ours. Source C3-135-own records invented response cases. No real person's experience is simulated.
