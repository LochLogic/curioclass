# Day 128: Gather regional evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and held half slips. Keep key-teacher and record adult-only; use key-teacher for all questions. Have cards-128, practice-128, opener, source-index and question board. Bring every day 127 paper. Keep exit-128 and its stimulus covered. Choose one repair priority per child.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 127 tickets, Exit boxes and all practice, retrieval and question-board papers. If many day 127 papers showed error P, review delegate tasks with [[s06-W128-1]]. For error W, use the made-up fountain record with [[s06-W128-2]]. Children answer only their assigned repair on paper; combine alternatives within five minutes.

#### 5 min. Retrieve the inquiry
Review the question board from grade 3 day 2. Read its unit question. Review grade 3 reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review its limit: what evidence cannot show. Recall grade 3's different-maker rule: two pages from one institution are one maker. Inspect the blank note headings. Give no worked response.

#### 20 min. Gather by hand
Review Paterson from day 122; the other four regional cases return from day 123. Use the student summaries and source-index. Historical slavery means people were forced, never willing trading partners. Alone, complete [[s06-P128-1]] and [[s06-P128-2]]. Meet the smaller repair group during the first five minutes. For titles without details, error J, or universal claims, error G, point back to the record without supplying an answer.

#### 5 min. Tentative answer
Use [[s06-P128-3]] as a display with separate labels and arrows. One maker must be the Bureau of Reclamation; other records share the park-service maker. **Ask.** [[s06-P128-2]] Expect a genuine missing fact. Hear two volunteers for 30 seconds each; public passing is allowed. Collect pages, returning copies of notes for tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover teaching. Reveal exit-128-stimulus and its map only now. These records are made up. Children answer [[s06-X128]] independently on the half slip. Neutral reading or exact dictation preserves the paper path. Collect all slips.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy summaries and note table; copy the held map separately. No live page is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy summaries and draw the held map separately.
**Skip.** Public sharing and class grouping.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 127 papers. Use one assigned fountain or delegate question.
- **5 min. Recall.** Read the unit question and note headings.
  **Say.** "Are two pages from one maker independent makers?" Expect: no.
- **20 min. Gather.** Your child makes five notes with [[s06-P128-1]] and [[s06-P128-2]]. Use arrows for [[s06-P128-3]].
  **Say.** "What remains unknown?" Expect a fact the records do not establish.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the made-up map and notice. Your child answers [[s06-X128]] alone. Save everything for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit volunteer sharing, keeping five notes and exit. Longer: test another possible answer against the notes.

### Words and supports
Review claim, reasoning, limit, maker and region. Read neutrally; enlarge print or scribe exact words.

### Tomorrow
Mark thirty sets at 60 seconds each, thirty minutes after class. Use record's single component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return every item on day 129. Error J means titles without evidence; error X means preference without support; error G means everyone has one job. One combined opening repair precedes independent organization.

### Credit
Ours. Summaries use Sources s06-paterson, s06-paterson-growth, s06-savannah, s06-minneapolis, s06-arizona-water and s06-fort-wood. Fountain facts are invented; delegate facts use Sources s06-house and s06-dc.
