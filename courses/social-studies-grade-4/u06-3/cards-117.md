# Day 117 teaching cards

## Community account first

The Mescalero Apache Tribe describes homelands across the Southwest. Its reservation was established in 1873.
The Tribe has its own elected government today. Its account names relocation in 1863.

## Historical context

The army forced Mescalero Apache people to Bosque Redondo in 1863. They left in 1865.
The Navajo Long Walk includes forced removals in 1864. Navajo people endured confinement until 1868.
Navajo leaders pressed for return. The 1868 treaty allowed return to part of their homeland.
The Navajo Nation has a living government today.

## Separate map layers

New Mexico includes dry plateau country, mountains and the Rio Grande.
Coronado's expedition entered this region in 1540. Albuquerque became a Spanish settlement in 1706.
Native peoples and governments were already there.
