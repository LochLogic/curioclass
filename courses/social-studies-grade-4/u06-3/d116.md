# Day 116: Resources and human work

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print cards-116 and student pages. Use practice-116 as an annotation master. Trace its source boundary into the three small layer boxes and side 2. Hold exit-116-stimulus and its grid. Give half slips matching blank grids with conventions space. Keep keys separate; use generated keys for marking. Prepare day 115 returns. The pear cards are made up, not a Kansas farm survey.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 115's exit and practice, including its retry. Review Return and repair. If many showed error Q, use [[s06-R116]]. Error E revises the separate wavy-line key. Error L aligns source and practice boundaries. Give one priority per child; further repairs use the first five Layer work minutes.

#### 6 min. Recall categories
Review natural resource: something from nature that people use, from grade 3. Review industry: all the work that gets or makes one kind of thing. Growing crops is farming. Processing means making a product from materials. Read cards-116: water is natural; a city is built. Schematic positions describe a given diagram, not a measured farm survey. Review counting east first, then north, on an empty square.

#### 19 min. Layer work
Children answer [[s06-P116-1]] from separate river and city sources. Use [[s06-P116-2]] on the water outline. Read the made-up [[s06-P116]]. Then read the Kansas source summaries once; use [[s06-P116-3]] on three separate outlines. These broad areas do not survey individual farms or parcels. Never combine them into the exit's grid shape. Hear six different children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes within work, using practice prompts. Record names; public passing is allowed. All submit paper. If many confuse categories, compare nature's materials with human activities before revision.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Reveal exit-116-stimulus now. Read its directions neutrally, including the made-up label. Children complete [[s06-X116]] on the half slip's matching grid. Locate all four features and add title, directions, scale and distinct key symbols. No grid placement is modeled. A scribe records specified positions. Collect responses.

#### 2 min. Close
Collect practice. Explain that tomorrow includes forced removal: people made to leave against their choice. Offer private discussion or quiet supported reading, without personal-history reports.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy separate outlines and the held blank grid. Keep source and response sizes matched. Never invent farm locations for the real state.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: trace outlines and draw the supplied blank grid.
**Skip.** Public observations.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 115 paper. Repair one component.
- **6 min. Recall categories.** Read cards-116 together.
- **16 min. Layer work.** Use [[s06-P116-1]], [[s06-P116-2]] and [[s06-P116-3]]. Read the made-up pear cards as a reminder.
  **Say.** "Which card shows processing?" Expect: the kitchen canning pears.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Reveal the made-up source. Your child completes [[s06-X116]] independently.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public observations. Longer: revisit separate category labels with their source evidence.

### Words and supports
Review natural resource and industry. Processing makes products from materials. Read labels without choosing symbols. Offer matching enlarged grids and exact dictation.
