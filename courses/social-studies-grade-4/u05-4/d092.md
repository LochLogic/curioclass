# Day 92: Choices under pressure

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and cards-092.md master. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-092-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-092.md. Bring day 91 tickets, practice and notes with private cues. Fold the retry until Motive work. Towns and children in explanatory cases are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return all day 91 papers, including board annotations. Hand back written tasks s05-P91-1, s05-P91-2, s05-P91-3, s05-F91-1, s05-F91-2, s05-K91-1, s05-F91-3, s05-F91-4 with private cues. If many day 91 tickets showed error S, use [[s05-F92-1]]. Track a boundary separately from a residence; these are made-up town districts, not countries or citizenship cases. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Finish one priority during Motive work; others read their cues.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review immigrant: a person who comes from another country to live; a visitor goes home. Use the made-up contrast, [[s05-K92-1]], then reveal [[steps:s05-K92-1]]. Read the historical teaching details. Explain economic opportunity as a chance to improve one's living conditions. Model [[s05-P92-2]] after children try. A difficult choice can involve serious pressures. Capture removes the person's choice of journey. State the distinction without rehearsing the reserved paired records. Never assume everyone in a group had one reason.

#### 17 min. Motive work
First five minutes finish repair with [[s05-F92-2]], a fresh district tracing. Alone, children annotate [[s05-P92-1]] and [[s05-P92-3]], using practice-092.md questions on their page. Use choice-notes.md for historical accounts only. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, detail and reason; enter one supplied source in source-log.md. Fifteen simultaneous pairs use [[s05-P92-4]] to compare the Irish rent pressure with the German opportunity, 45 seconds each child, 90 seconds total. Children revise their labels individually. Watch a job detail being mistaken for everyone's motive; point to the specific card. Do not assemble the later assessment's three-group and forced-arrival answer.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the growth question on question-board.md. Six rotating volunteers share one existing label, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; allow passing. Collect everyone's page and notes for day 93 feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Reveal exit-092.md's new departure records. Each child answers [[s05-X92-1]] independently on paper. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus thirty for other papers. Record components on record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all papers day 93.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the short summaries, sketches and tasks. No projector: point at paper. Printed sources replace a live page or visitor.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy summaries and tasks. No projector: use paper. Draw district sketches.
**Skip.** Public turns and repeated pair explanations.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 91 work; use [[s05-F92-1]].
- **8 min. Teaching first.** Read the made-up visitor contrast and historical details. Use [[s05-K92-1]] and [[s05-P92-2]].
- **17 min. Motive work.** Your child draws [[s05-F92-2]] and answers [[s05-P92-1]] and [[s05-P92-3]]. Add one source note. Discuss [[s05-P92-4]].
  **Say.** "How do the rent pressure and economic opportunity differ?" Expect: one makes staying harder; the other offers improvement elsewhere.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Reveal the new records and [[s05-X92-1]]. Your child answers alone. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated sharing, keeping independent labels and exit. Longer: refine an existing source note.

### Words and supports
Review immigrant and visitor. Read aloud; accept home-language rehearsal, labeled sketches and exact dictation. Discuss historical evidence without personal migration or family questions.

### Tomorrow
Day 93 studies forced transport and enslavement. Error I treats captivity as chosen employment. Return all day 92 work; annotate denied choice during Return and retrieval and Account work's first five minutes.
