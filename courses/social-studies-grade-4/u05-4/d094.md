# Day 94: Slavery's spread and division

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and cards-094.md master. Use the current generated key for revised practice, alongside teacher-key.md. Keep exit-094-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-094.md with the separate diagram. Its rectangles are an original schematic, not historical geography; omit the master footer's map-shape credit. Bring all day 93 work and unscored retrieval notes. Fold Side 2 until Division work.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 93 tickets, practice and board notes. Hand back written tasks s05-P93-3, s05-P93-4, s05-F93-1, s05-F93-2, s05-K93-1 with private cues. If many day 93 tickets showed error I, use [[s05-F94-1]]. Error P uses [[s05-F94-2]] instead. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Retrieval misses share the Massachusetts/Virginia reminder: slavery was legal in both, including northern Massachusetts. Review enslaved people: people forced to work who could not leave. Protect one priority; continue during Division work.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review admission: joining as a state; territory: land governed within a larger country. Explain division here as deep disagreement. Read the Missouri summary. **Ask.** [[s05-P94-1]] Wait, then model [[steps:s05-P94-1]]. Explain political balance as how much influence each side has in government. Read the later dispute and war context separately. Slavery was central to the division that led to war, not an unnamed quarrel. Teach the Civil War's years aloud, 1861 to 1865; keep its span separate from causal practice. Do not practice an applicant-map inference.

#### 17 min. Division work
First five minutes finish repair with [[s05-F94-3]] or [[s05-F94-4]], matching the priority. Others read written cues. Review cause-effect links: an earlier cause helps explain a later effect. Alone, connect [[s05-P94-2]]; then complete chronology task [[s05-P94-3]] using timeline.md. Review equal gaps for equal years; use rulers for marks, not map measurement. Review source log fields in source-log.md; enter one supplied attribution. Fifteen simultaneous pairs explain one link, 45 seconds each child, 90 seconds total. Children revise individually. Keep causal work separate from war-span recall and the unseen schematic.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the growth question on question-board.md. Six rotating volunteers share one existing link, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; allow passing. Collect everyone's page and notes for day 95 feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Reveal the separate schematic and exit-094.md. Children independently answer [[s05-X94-1]] and [[s05-X94-2]] on paper. Read labels neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus thirty for other papers. Record each component on record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work day 95.
