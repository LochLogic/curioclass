# Day 93: Captivity and people's actions

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and cards-093.md master. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-093-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-093.md. Bring every day 92 paper with a private cue. Keep Side 2 folded for retrieval. Read only the non-graphic summaries below, never the raw captivity accounts. Offer quiet private work without public speech.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and retrieval
Return day 92 tickets, practice and board notes. Hand back written tasks s05-P92-1, s05-P92-2, s05-P92-3, s05-F92-1, s05-F92-2, s05-K92-1, s05-P92-4 with private cues. Alone, children answer [[s05-P93-1]] and [[s05-P93-2]] before unfolding summaries. These recall grade 3's Massachusetts/Virginia examples, not all thirteen colonies. Record retrieval gaps without scores; route them to day 94. If many day 92 tickets showed error I, begin [[s05-F93-1]]; underline denied choice, not a job label. Otherwise give the written move to a small group. One priority continues during Account work.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review enslaved people: people forced to work who could not leave. Review petition: a written request to officials. Read the law and Sutton summaries. Name forced transport across the Atlantic, denied freedom and resistance through public requests. Teach that people maintained relationships and sought freedom; Sutton's request included her daughter. **Ask.** [[s05-K93-1]] Wait, then reveal [[steps:s05-K93-1]]. Read the separate free Black Boston caption. This history shows people acting, not only what was done to them. No role play or personal comparisons.

#### 17 min. Account work
First five minutes finish the priority repair with [[s05-F93-2]], a fresh law detail. Other children use written cues. Alone, complete [[s05-P93-3]] and [[s05-P93-4]] from the separate captions. Use choice-notes.md for historical details. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, detail and reason; enter one attribution in source-log.md. Fifteen simultaneous pairs explain one chosen annotation, 45 seconds each child, 90 seconds total. Children revise individually. Error P needs the explicit free-community detail. Keep the exit account and employment-label correction unseen.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the growth question on question-board.md. Six rotating volunteers share an existing annotation, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; allow passing. Collect everyone's page and notes for day 94 feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Reveal exit-093.md's new attributed account and caption. Each child answers [[s05-X93-1]] independently on paper. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus thirty for other papers. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, by component. Return all work day 94; retrieval remains unscored.
