# Day 93 teaching cards

Teacher reads: Massachusetts law permitted slavery in 1641. Virginia law also recognized slavery. Enslaved people could not freely leave. Belinda Sutton petitioned for a pension in 1783. Free Black Boston communities ran schools and businesses and opposed slavery.
