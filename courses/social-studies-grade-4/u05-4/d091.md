# Day 91: Residents and promised rights

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare the listed pages and cards-091.md master. Use the current generated key alongside teacher-key.md. Keep exit-091-sources.md adult-only; seal exit-091.md. Bring every day 90 paper with a private cue. Select six public volunteers. Council cases are made up. Fold Side 2 until Resident work.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 90 tickets, practice, repairs and board notes. Honor yesterday's evidence and payment handoff. If many day 90 tickets showed error G, use [[s05-F91-1]]: underline the evidence limit. Error N uses [[s05-F91-2]] instead. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Keep one priority; finish it during Resident work. Other children read their written cues.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review citizenship: a person counted as a member, from grade 3 day 130. Review treaty: agreement between governments. Read the Article IX summary without Article VIII. **Ask.** [[s05-P91-1]] Wait, then model [[steps:s05-P91-1]]. **Ask.** [[s05-K91-1]] Reveal its key only afterward. Explain property as things people own. Compare the overlay with the locator: modern state lines differ from acquisition lines. The map labels the purchase 1853; negotiations were finalized in 1854.

#### 17 min. Resident work
First five minutes finish one repair: [[s05-F91-3]] retries evidence limits; [[s05-F91-4]] retries constraints. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Use a ruler as a pointer, never to measure locator distances. Alone, answer [[s05-P91-2]] on the overlay and [[s05-P91-3]] on Side 2. Use growth-map.md for boundary notes and choice-notes.md for historical details only. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, detail and reason; record the supplied attribution in source-log.md. Fifteen simultaneous pairs explain one annotation, 45 seconds each child, 90 seconds total. Revise privately. Watch promises being treated as outcomes; return to the evidence needed.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the growth question on question-board.md. Six volunteers share an existing annotation, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; passing is allowed. Collect everyone's page and notes for day 92 feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Reveal exit-091.md with its new Article VIII summary. Each child answers [[s05-X91-1]] independently on paper. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus thirty minutes for other papers. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, separately by component. Return all work day 92.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy summaries and tasks; trace the supplied outline. No projector: point to paper. Printed sources replace live pages and visitors.

  **Say.** "Does this promise alone prove protection happened?" Expect: no; promises need evidence of fulfillment.

- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Give the sealed summary and [[s05-X91-1]]. Your child answers alone; discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated sharing. Keep repairs and exit. Longer: refine the evidence request.

### Words and supports
Review citizenship, treaty and reasoning. Read summaries aloud; allow home-language rehearsal and exact dictation. Keep public passing available.
