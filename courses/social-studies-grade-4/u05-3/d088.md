# Day 88: Two different Texas events

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print student pages and prepare cards-088.md and practice-088.md masters. Hold exit-088.md separately. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-088-sources.md adult-only. Bring day 87 papers, blank timeline.md and source-log.md headings. Draw a solid rectangle for the current garden, with a dotted old park edge crossing its middle. Also draw a fresh playground rectangle crossed by a dotted vertical pond edge. Both diagrams are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 87's exits, practice, map notes and board papers. Hand back written tasks s05-P87-1 and s05-P87-2 with their private cues. If many day 87 tickets showed error E, use [[s05-F88-1]] on the made-up diagram. Trace current and older boundaries separately; neither must follow the other. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Finish this priority during Event work; others read the event cards.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Day 87 located former Mexican lands. Today distinguish two changes in Texas. Independence means declaring separation from another country; teach this explicitly. Review annexation: adding land to a country's claimed control. Show the joining card alone. **Ask.** [[s05-K88-1]] Hear ideas before [[steps:s05-K88-1]]. Then read the separation card separately. Review enslaved people: people forced to work who could not leave. Read the cause card plainly. Preserving slavery was one motive; never present it as chosen work or every person's motive. No historical roles or personal questions.

#### 17 min. Event work
First five minutes finish the boundary repair and a fresh trace. Use [[s05-F88-2]] for the fresh attempt; turn to Side 2, then return. Others match event labels. Children independently complete [[s05-P88-1]]. Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks. Use timeline.md; each child places each event separately between the printed decades. Read the cause card for [[s05-P88-2]], then the Tejano choices for [[s05-P88-3]]. Define Tejano as a Texan of Mexican heritage in these accounts. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, detail and reason. Record one field from the printed source label. Fifteen parallel pairs take 45 seconds per child to explain one chosen match, 90 seconds total. Keep chronology, motive and choices in separate tasks.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the lands question. Six rotating volunteers share one existing event or position match, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; children may pass. Collect everyone's annotations for day 89 feedback. Cover completed work.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the new event record. Children independently answer [[s05-X88-1]] on the half slip. Read neutrally; exact dictation is access. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus thirty for other work, about one minute per child. Record separation/date, joining/date and motive: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all papers on day 89.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy summaries and tasks, drawing the equal-year line with ten-year marks. Keep the exit separate. Printed summaries replace live pages and visitors.

### At home

  **Say.** "Which word names joining?" Expect: annexation. Read the slavery definition without role play.
- **17 min. Event work.** Finish repair with [[s05-F88-2]]. Use [[s05-P88-1]], [[s05-P88-2]] and [[s05-P88-3]]. Place events separately on the equal-year line. Discuss one position match.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover work; reveal the new record. Your child answers [[s05-X88-1]] alone. Give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce partner repetition. Keep date matches, cause, differing choices and exit. Longer: verify one date's placement between decades.

### Words and supports
Review annexation, enslaved people, timeline and source log. New independence and Tejano receive definitions before use. Read aloud; permit home-language rehearsal and exact dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 89 studies the war and account makers. Return all day 88 papers. Error F needs separate actions/dates; error I needs forced versus chosen. Choose one priority in Return and repair and Account work's first five minutes.
