# Day 87: Former borders across modern states

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare the listed masters and student pages. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-087-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-087.md and exit-087-stimulus.md together. Bring day 86 papers. Cover the atlas except each teaching window; never teach with the exit crop. Explain that the garden record is made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 86's exits, practice and board papers. If many day 86 tickets showed error H, O or R, choose one link in [[s05-F87-1]]. Review cause-effect chain: a cause explains why; an effect follows. For error H seek a mechanism, error O check order, error R separate effects. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Continue that priority during Border work; others read the caption.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Day 86 connected movement with unequal effects; today read former Mexican lands. Review border: where one country ends and another begins. Teach the key explicitly: gray outlines show modern states; colored regions show historical acquisitions. Review treaty: agreement between governments. On teaching-locator.png expose only Wyoming. **Ask.** [[s05-K87-1]] Hear an answer, then show [[steps:s05-K87-1]]. Expose separate state windows, pointing to printed names. Do not rehearse the six-location exit set.

#### 17 min. Border work
First five minutes finish the repair and fresh correction. Others read the caption. Cover the model. Children answer [[s05-P87-1]] at the Colorado window, then [[s05-P87-2]] at the coast window. Point separately to Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, Utah and Nevada labels, tracing each old division. Read the southern-strip caption; distinguish former Mexican land from transfer dates. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, detail and reason. Use source-log.md and growth-map.md headings; claim-test.md supplies blank claim/detail headings. Fifteen parallel pairs each take 45 seconds per child to share one intersection, 90 seconds total.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the board question about whose lands Mexico lost. Six rotating volunteers share one existing annotation, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; passing is allowed. Collect every child's page and notes for day 88 feedback. Cover completed notes.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the separate overlay and half slip. Children independently complete [[s05-X87-1]] and [[s05-X87-2]] on paper. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus fifteen for practice. Record locations and boundary explanation separately: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Hand back all work on day 88.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace windows from the verified atlas; copy text and tasks. Copy the exit separately. No projector: point to the printed master. Printed summaries replace a live page or visitor.

### At home

  **Say.** "What does the fill change show?" Expect: two historical regions within one modern state.

- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover notes. Reveal the separate overlay. Your child answers [[s05-X87-1]] and [[s05-X87-2]] alone. Discuss feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce pair repetition. Keep teaching windows and exit. Longer: revisit the Colorado intersection.

### Words and supports
Review border, treaty and source log. Read aloud; allow home-language rehearsal and exact dictation. Review homeland: land a nation's people have known as home for a very long time. National claims do not erase homelands.

### Tomorrow
Day 88 distinguishes Texas independence and annexation. Return all day 87 papers. Error E needs separate current and historical lines: Return and repair, then Event work's first five minutes.
