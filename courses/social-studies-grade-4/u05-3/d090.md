# Day 90: What a record settles

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages, cards-090.md and practice-090.md masters. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-090-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-090.md and exit-090-stimulus.md together. Bring day 89's tickets, Exit box and practice with one private cue each. Cover completed comparisons. Supply claim-test.md and source-log.md blank headings. Town and council cases are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return all day 89 papers, including the Exit box and board annotations. Hand back written tasks s05-P89-1, s05-P89-2, s05-P89-3, s05-P89-4 and s05-P89-5 with their private cues. If many day 89 tickets showed error G, use [[s05-F90-1]] on the lamp record. Review the difference between a claim and evidence supporting it. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Continue this single priority during Evidence work; others reread the support details.

#### 5 min. Recall criteria
Review day 89's source limits and day 86's time order. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, detail and reason. Recall claim/detail/reason/limit headings from the earlier claim sheet. Children point to blank fields on claim-test.md; keep completed comparisons covered. Recall day 89's treaty caption: negotiation followed defeat; payment alone cannot show free choice. No new model or historical content.

#### 20 min. Evidence work
First five minutes finish one priority repair and a fresh correction. Use [[s05-F90-2]] for the fresh attempt; turn to Side 2, then return. Others select support independently. Read the separate support details for [[s05-P90-1]]. Children annotate only the single assertion. Use [[s05-P90-2]] on the new town register, then [[s05-P90-3]] on the recalled treaty context. Review treaty: agreement between governments. Test details individually; do not assemble the exit's two-account/map finding and open question. Fifteen parallel pairs take 45 seconds per child to explain one chosen detail, 90 seconds total. Each child revises an annotation using the blank claim/detail/reason/limit prompts. Watch error G, treating an assertion as proof, and error N, treating payment as free choice. Point back to the relevant detail, not a completed answer.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the growth question. Six rotating volunteers share one existing support annotation, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; passing is allowed. Collect everyone's page and notes for day 91 feedback. Cover completed work.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the new cause statements and locator card with the half slip. Children independently answer [[s05-X90-1]] and [[s05-X90-2]] on paper. Read neutrally; scribe exactly if needed. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus thirty for other papers, about one minute per child. Record location finding and open question separately: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 91.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy summaries, blank headings and tasks; copy the reserved locator separately. Printed summaries replace a live page, photograph or visitor. No devices are needed.

**Swap.** No printer: copy tasks and headings; trace the supplied locator separately and keep it covered.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated sharing. Keep independent annotations and exit. Longer: refine an existing limitation.

### Words and supports
Review reasoning and treaty. Read neutrally; allow home-language rehearsal and exact dictation. Public passing never removes the private paper task.
