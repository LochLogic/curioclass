# Day 89: Account makers and limits

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and cards-089.md and practice-089.md masters. Fold under the Exit box; seal exit-089.md separately. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-089-sources.md adult-only. Bring day 88 papers and blank source-log.md and claim-test.md headings. Use printed summaries instead of a visitor or live page. Club and town cases are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 88 papers and board annotations. Hand back written tasks s05-P88-1, s05-P88-2 and s05-P88-3 with their private cues. If many day 88 tickets showed error F or I, select one part of [[s05-F89-1]]. For error F separate leaving and joining; briefly return the event/date match. For error I review enslaved people: people forced to work who could not leave. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Finish one priority during Account work; others read the notice.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Day 88 separated Texas events and motives. Read the dated treaty caption. Review treaty: agreement between governments. Review perspective: two sides of one event. Review source log: maker, date, kind, audience, detail and reason. On the made-up notice, **Ask.** [[s05-P89-1]] Hear ideas, then [[steps:s05-P89-1]]. **Ask.** [[s05-K89-1]] Expect a dated park map; a clerk's claim needs testing. Review corroboration: two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing. Later scholarship can test evidence without being eyewitness testimony.

#### 17 min. Account work
First five minutes finish one repair and retry. Use [[s05-F89-2]] for the fresh attempt; turn to Side 2, then return. Others label the notice. Independently complete [[s05-P89-2]] and [[s05-P89-3]] from separate captions. Read regional voices aloud. Define Californio as a resident of Mexican California in these accounts. Review Tejano from day 88. Use [[s05-P89-4]] for one distinction. Review the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla as living peoples from day 80. Their public history concerns Oregon, not witnessing this war. Review consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made; national claims alone cannot show it. Read the troop-action and negotiation captions without completing a paired cause argument. Fifteen parallel pairs take 45 seconds per child to share one annotation, 90 seconds total. Use blank log and claim/detail/limit headings.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review whose lands were lost. Six rotating volunteers share one existing regional distinction, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; passing is allowed. Collect everyone's annotations. Cover completed work.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-089.md and the Exit box together. Children independently answer [[s05-X89-1]], [[s05-X89-2]] and [[s05-P89-5]] on paper. Read neutrally; allow exact dictation. After class allow forty-five minutes for thirty children's three responses, ninety seconds each, plus forty-five for practice and notes, ninety seconds per child. Record maker/date, agreement, map need and missing voice: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 90.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy short captions and tasks; prepare the exit and Exit box separately. Printed public-history summaries replace a visitor, photograph or live page.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy captions; keep both exit pieces covered.
**Skip.** Public turns and repeated pair talk.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 88 work; select one club repair.
- **8 min. Teaching first.** Read the notice. Use [[s05-P89-1]] and [[s05-K89-1]].
  **Say.** "Which evidence tests the boundary claim?" Expect: a dated park map.
- **17 min. Account work.** Finish repair with [[s05-F89-2]]. Use [[s05-P89-2]], [[s05-P89-3]] and [[s05-P89-4]]. Read the captions. Discuss one distinction, then cover notes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal both pieces. Your child answers [[s05-X89-1]], [[s05-X89-2]] and [[s05-P89-5]] alone. Give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce sharing. Keep captions and all exit work. Longer: revisit a source-kind match.

### Words and supports
Review perspective, corroboration, consent and source log. Read aloud; allow home-language rehearsal and exact dictation. Keep historical summaries attributed; no impersonation or personal disclosures.

### Tomorrow
Day 90 weighs support for war-cause statements. Return day 89's tickets, Exit box and practice. Error G needs one claim tested against evidence in Return and repair and Evidence work's first five minutes.
