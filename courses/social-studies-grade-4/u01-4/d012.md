# Day 12: Numbered lines and crossings

## Teacher page

### Before class
Cover the Exit panel on Side 2 with opaque scrap paper; keep it covered during practice. Prepare the generated teacher-only answer key and day 11 feedback. Use the section plan for background; today's printed references replace it. Label the ball with the two zero lines if needed.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 11's exit and practice page: s01-X11, s01-P11-E, s01-P11-R, s01-P11-1, s01-P11-2, s01-P11-3 and s01-P11-4, with one cue. Review county, nation and country from day 11. If many showed error E, error F or error L on day 11's exit, assign the matching record in [[s01-P12-R]]. Repair one dominant gap; others follow individual cues.

#### 10 min. Globe retrieval and repair
Review grade 3: equator, the line round the globe's middle; prime meridian, an agreed pole-to-pole line; hemisphere, half the globe. Review latitude lines running east/west and longitude lines running pole to pole. First five minutes: find both zero lines on the globe and complete [[s01-P12-1]]. Review grade 3's grid cell, letter then number. Next five: retrace D2 and the zero lines with everyone if needed; otherwise meet one group while others annotate. The retrieval stays in the folder.

#### 10 min. Numbered lines
Use Source 12A and Source 12B. Introduce degree as an angle measure, not a ground-distance unit. Latitude measures north/south from equator; longitude measures east/west from prime meridian. **Ask.** [[s01-K12-1]] Wait, then reveal [[steps:s01-K12-1]]. Follow existing star A's lines to their margins. The ordered coordinates name one crossing. Do not demonstrate placing a supplied pair.

#### 10 min. Supported work
Turn to Side 2, using Side 1's grid. Alone: [[s01-P12-2]] and [[s01-P12-3]]. Pairs each explain one line reading, 45 seconds each, simultaneously. After attempts, use the teacher answer key for feedback. Keep five minutes for day 11's second repair group using its assigned record. Collect numbered-line responses; other practice stays in the folder.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Uncover the Exit panel and [[s01-X12]]. Children mark independently on their grid; attach their half slip. Collect both. Mark after class: 30 children times 20 seconds, 10 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Return day 13 with the collected practice and one cue.

### Other rooms
No globe: draw a circle with equator and prime meridian. No printer: copy teaching grids separately; give the fresh exit grid only at exit time.

### At home
**Swap.** No globe: draw the two zero lines on a circle. No copies: draw separate grids.
**Skip.** Class collection and the second repair group.
- **5 min. Return.** Use your child's day 11 cue with [[s01-P12-R]].
- **8 min. Recall.** Complete [[s01-P12-1]] together.
- **10 min. Read.** Try [[s01-K12-1]], then read its steps. **Say.** "Which measurement comes first?" Expect: latitude.
- **7 min. Practice.** Your child attempts [[s01-P12-2]] and [[s01-P12-3]]. Discuss afterward.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover the Exit panel and [[s01-X12]]. Your child works alone. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: shorten pair explanations, keeping retrieval repair and both practice readings. Longer: compare a cell's area with a crossing's exact position.

### Words and supports
Review the zero lines and hemisphere. Coordinates: latitude first, longitude second. Intersection: crossing. Read margin labels aloud without locating the answer; provide raised lines or a scribe.

### Tomorrow
Day 13 returns all collected work. Error G reverses order; error P uses the wrong zero line. One dominant repair opens day 13; a second group meets during Supported work. Teacher tracing is withdrawn for routes and label repairs.

### Credit
Ours. Sources 12A and 12B support grid concepts. Source 12C and Source 12D support present-government reminders.
