# Section 1.4 opener

## The ideas

A cell has area; a numbered-coordinate location is an intersection. Latitude measures north/south from equator; longitude measures east/west from prime meridian. Lines run in a different direction from their measured change. Use latitude-first notation. Evidence must match its named map and support the claim. A limit concerns what the map cannot establish.

Keep every exit and assessment packet hidden until its named part. Prepare the matching key files for teacher use only. Gather pencils, folders and individual paper; add rulers for distance, blank strips for belts, and a globe or labeled ball for zero-line retrieval. Never measure a projected image.

## Common errors and response

G: coordinate order reverses; P: zero lines reverse; D/N/O: extent and locations are confused; H: claim lacks visible support; I: both details come from one source. Earlier letters return privately on day 16.

Many means more than 10 of 30. Repair one priority gap in Return and repair, five minutes. A second gap receives one five-minute group during Supported work. Others follow written cues and submit a fresh response. Never stack repairs in one slot or name a child as an error.
