# Day 13: Routes and accurate labels

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 12's grid and collected practice with one cue. Cover only the Exit panel on Side 2; keep it covered during practice. Prepare the generated teacher-only answer key. Use the section opener when it exists; printed route references provide today's fallback.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review day 12's coordinates and zero lines. If many showed error G or error P on day 12's exit, use the matching statement in [[s01-P13-R]]. Model one correction without supplying a point pair. Other children follow their cues. One dominant repair only. The reference repair stays in the folder.

#### 10 min. Line routes
Pairs trace premarked arrows for [[s01-P13-1]]. Each child takes one 45-second explanation turn, simultaneously across pairs. Repeat tracing silently, then compare the unchanged measurement. Review latitude, longitude and degrees from day 12; teacher tracing is withdrawn. Follow the whole line, not a cell. Route tracing stays in the folder.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Alone, complete [[s01-P13-2]], [[s01-P13-3]] and [[s01-P13-4]]. After each correction, pairs take two 45-second turns, three cycles totaling 4.5 minutes. Feedback follows each set from the teacher answer key; accuracy comes before ease. Reserve five minutes for a second day 12 repair group. No child produces a full point pair or plots a supplied pair in practice.

#### 5 min. Check explanations
Turn to Side 2, keeping the Exit panel covered. Everyone writes [[s01-P13-5]]. Hear six volunteers, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; public passing is allowed. Collect the three card corrections and all explanations, including those not heard. Their written evidence reaches everyone; oral comments are unscored.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Uncover the Exit panel and [[s01-X13]]. Children work alone: write Y on the half slip and mark Z on the grid. Collect both. Mark after class, 30 children times 30 seconds: 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Return day 14 with all collected work and one cue.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the route arrows on individual paper. Prepare the exit grid separately and withhold it. Read labels without tracing toward answers.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: draw the route and exit grids separately.
**Skip.** Public explanations and collection.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 12's cue; use [[s01-P13-R]].
- **8 min. Routes.** Trace both arrows for [[s01-P13-1]]. **Say.** "Which measurement stays unchanged along A?" Expect: latitude.
- **12 min. Repair.** Your child attempts [[s01-P13-2]] and [[s01-P13-3]]. Discuss after each.
- **5 min. Explain.** Your child completes [[s01-P13-5]]. Listen without tracing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover the Exit panel and [[s01-X13]]. Your child works alone; discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: hear three public explanations; keep all three correction sets and the exit. Longer: explain why movement along a parallel changes longitude.

### Words and supports
Review: coordinates, degrees, latitude, longitude, equator and prime meridian. Parallel: latitude line. Meridian: longitude line. These are alternate names for familiar lines, not new concepts. Keep the general reference available. A scribe records exact directions.

### Tomorrow
Day 14 returns day 13's exit and collected corrections. Error G reverses order; error P uses the wrong zero line. Repair one dominant gap first and another during Supported work. Then review evidence and reasoning from two maps.

### Credit
Ours. Source 12A and Source 12B support the line references. All route arrows are made up.

### Side 1
**I can use latitude and longitude accurately.**
Review: latitude measures north/south from equator; longitude measures east/west from prime meridian.
Order: latitude, then longitude. Numbers show degrees, an angle measure.
N means north; S, south; E, east; W, west.
Assigned reference error: longitude first; latitude zero is prime meridian.
