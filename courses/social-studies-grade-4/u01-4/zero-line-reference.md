# Zero lines and measured coordinates

| Measurement | Reference |
|---|---|
| Latitude | Degrees north or south from equator |
| Longitude | Degrees east or west from prime meridian |

Equator is zero latitude. Prime meridian is zero longitude.
Latitude lines run east and west. Longitude lines run from pole to pole.
Write latitude first, then longitude.
N means north; S, south; E, east; W, west.
