# Day 16: Feedback and a fresh repair

## Teacher page

### Before class
Prepare response-comparisons.md, the day 5 scale page, completed comparison strips, zero-line reference and record sheet. Choose one priority cue and one fresh component per child. Keep every retry card hidden during group comparisons; give each child only the assigned card later. Keep key-16.md and the generated key teacher-only. Verify retry-2.md's bar measures six centimeters. Prepare returned E14 and assessment work together.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return day 15's assessment and all day 14 work: s01-P14-R, s01-P14-1, s01-P14-2, s01-P14-3, s01-P14-4 and s01-X14 (E14). Review the component record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Prioritize one gap per child. If many show overlapping gaps, combine them under one move. Explain [[s01-P16-R]] using Side 1; never group children publicly by a score.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Four sequential five-minute adult groups; others work independently or in pairs on their written moves. Say the comparison places and market are made up. Review the relevant earlier skill before repair. Each group has one short comparison and at most two 45-second explanations, 90 seconds total; public passing is allowed.

Group 1, errors A/B/C/Q: review day 2's legend or day 5's scale. Match symbols or compare completed whole-bar strips; no strip construction. Group 2, errors E/F/L: review day 11's county/nation distinctions. Saskatchewan is an E11 reminder only. Group 3, errors G/P/D/N/O: review day 12's line labels or day 7's map windows. Group 4, errors H/I/J/K/M: review day 14: highlight supplied source/detail/reason links, without adding missing evidence. Compare the supplied examples using retained references, then children annotate the supplied comparisons. Collect every annotated comparison with the fresh attempt. No group sees its fresh card yet.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Privately reveal one component card per child: [[s01-P16-1]], [[s01-P16-2]], [[s01-P16-3]], [[s01-P16-4]], [[s01-P16-5]], [[s01-P16-6]] or [[s01-P16-7]]. For Card A, assign only symbol or direction. Allow the first two minutes for directions, never content hints. Everyone makes one written or constructed record alone. Collect all; no answer copying from returned assessments.

#### 5 min. Self-review
Turn back to Side 1. Children complete [[s01-P16-S]] without awarding scores; collect it with the private-cue response and fresh attempt. Keep folders for unit 2. Mark fresh repairs afterward: 30 children times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes. Use the same component scale. Return all collected work with written cues day 17 during Open the inquiry, one minute for reading cues.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy each assigned card separately. No ruler: provide a measured centimeter strip. No outside page or visitor is needed; printed government and geography references remain available.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy one assigned card separately. No ruler: use a measured strip.
**Skip.** The class groups and other cards.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Read the private cue together. Use [[s01-P16-R]].
- **15 min. Repair.** Follow that group's Side 1 move with the retained practice. **Say.** "Which reference supports your change?" Expect: the relevant legend, scale, line reference or map detail.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Reveal only the assigned card from its separate held card. Your child responds alone. Discuss afterward using the teacher answer key.
- **5 min. Self-review.** Complete [[s01-P16-S]]. Save the folder for day 17.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public explanations, keeping all four group visits and the independent attempt. Longer: find a second supporting detail in retained practice.

### Words and supports
Review the words for the assigned component only. Keep large labels, tactile lines and dictation available. Use only one marking scale; access support never counts as content help.

### Tomorrow
Day 17 opens: "How are Canada, Mexico and the United States tied together?" Return fresh repairs with one written cue; keep the folder. Children retrieve questions and evidence, then choose Canada or Mexico for inquiry.

### Credit
Ours. Source 16P supports political insets; Source 12D supports the government reference. All map windows and market layouts are made up.
