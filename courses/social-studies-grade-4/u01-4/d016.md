# Day 16: Feedback and a fresh repair

## Teacher page

### Before class
Give each child one private priority cue from E14 and day 15's assessment. Keep fresh Side 2 cards hidden. Prepare the generated teacher-only answer key. Retain the day 5 scale page and its verified 4-centimeter bar. The optional section opener may guide grouping; written moves below replace it.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return day 15's assessment, day 14's evidence sentences and E14. Review the component record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Prioritize one gap per child. If many show overlapping gaps, combine them under one move. Explain [[s01-P16-R]] using Side 1; never group children publicly by a score.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Four sequential five-minute adult groups; others work independently or in pairs on their written moves. Review the relevant earlier skill before repair. Each group has one short model and at most two 45-second explanations, 90 seconds total; public passing is allowed.

Group 1, errors A/B/C/Q: review day 2's legend comparison or day 5's measured bar. Group 2, errors E/F/L: review day 11's county/nation distinctions. Saskatchewan is an E11 reminder only. Group 3, errors G/P/D/N/O: review day 12's line labels or day 7's map windows. Group 4, errors H/I/J/K/M: review day 14's separate source-linked evidence sentences. Model using retained practice, then children repair their own practice response. No group sees its fresh card yet.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Privately reveal one component card per child: [[s01-P16-1]], [[s01-P16-2]], [[s01-P16-3]], [[s01-P16-4]], [[s01-P16-5]], [[s01-P16-6]] or [[s01-P16-7]]. For Card A, assign only symbol or direction. Allow the first two minutes for directions, never content hints. Everyone makes one written or constructed record alone. Collect all; no answer copying from returned assessments.

#### 5 min. Self-review
Children complete [[s01-P16-S]] on Side 1 without awarding scores; it stays in the folder. Keep folders for unit 2. Mark fresh repairs afterward: 30 children times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes. Use the same component scale. Return records with written cues day 17 during Open the inquiry.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy each assigned card separately. No ruler: provide a measured centimeter strip. No outside page or visitor is needed; printed government and geography references remain available.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy one assigned card separately. No ruler: use a measured strip.
**Skip.** The class groups and other cards.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Read the private cue together. Use [[s01-P16-R]].
- **15 min. Repair.** Follow that group's Side 1 move with the retained practice. **Say.** "Which reference supports your change?" Expect: the relevant legend, scale, line reference or map detail.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Reveal only the assigned card from Side 2. Your child responds alone. Discuss afterward using the teacher answer key.
- **5 min. Self-review.** Complete [[s01-P16-S]]. Save the folder for day 17.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public explanations, keeping all four group visits and the independent attempt. Longer: find a second supporting detail in retained practice.

### Words and supports
Review the words for the assigned component only. Keep large labels, tactile lines and dictation available. Use only one marking scale; access support never counts as content help.

### Tomorrow
Day 17 opens: "How are Canada, Mexico and the United States tied together?" Return fresh repairs with one written cue; keep the folder. Children retrieve questions and evidence, then choose Canada or Mexico for inquiry.

### Credit
Ours. Source 16P supports political insets; Source 12D supports the government reference. All map windows and market layouts are made up.
