# Day 14: Evidence from two maps

## Teacher page

### Before class
Use evidence-critiques.md for display; print matching practice only once. Prepare claim-reference.md, the zero-line reference, saved s01-X1/s01-P1-E and retained A/B maps. Keep the question board and record sheet. Separate Park Maps until the exit. Keep key-14.md teacher-only. Return day 13 papers, including s01-X13 and s01-X13b, with one cue.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return all day 13 papers: s01-P13-R, s01-P13-1, s01-P13-2, s01-P13-3, s01-P13-4, s01-P13-5, s01-X13 and s01-X13b. Review day 13's coordinates and zero lines. If many showed error G or error P on day 13's exit, use the matching reference repair in [[s01-P14-R]]. One dominant repair only; others follow individual cues. Corrected references stay in the folder.

#### 8 min. Evidence retrieval
Keep day 1's saved responses for the named two minutes in Supported work. Review grade 3's claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Review claim, evidence and reasoning from day 11. Read the supplied paragraph aloud; children annotate [[s01-P14-1]]. Discuss its detail-to-claim links. Keep annotations for collection today.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Review retained Physical Map A and Political Map B, both made by Curio Class for students. Keep the full reference frame visible. Under the given claim, children independently critique [[s01-P14-2]] and [[s01-P14-3]]. First two minutes: reread saved s01-X1 and s01-P1-E, the question and its reason. Partners then check source links, two 45-second turns. Teacher reserves five minutes for the second grid repair group. After attempts, give feedback from the teacher answer key.

#### 5 min. Share evidence
Everyone writes [[s01-P14-4]]. Hear six children, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total; anyone may pass publicly. Collect every practice response, including annotations and the reference repair. Add supported details to the question board without composing a full usefulness-and-limit paragraph.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket
Say the Park Maps are made up. Reveal Side 2 and [[s01-X14]]. Children independently write on the half slip, consulting both maps. Collect all slips. Mark afterward: 30 children times 90 seconds, 45 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Allow another 30 children times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for practice. Return all work day 16 with assessment feedback.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the retained map details and frame. Copy both Park Maps separately, withholding them until exit. Read labels without supplying comparisons.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: draw separate teaching and Park Maps.
**Skip.** The board and public sharing.
- **5 min. Return.** Use day 13's cue with [[s01-P14-R]].
- **8 min. Read.** Read the paragraph together; attempt [[s01-P14-1]].
- **12 min. Evidence.** Your child completes [[s01-P14-2]] and [[s01-P14-3]] using retained A/B. **Say.** "How does that detail support the claim?" Expect: a link to physical or political information.
- **3 min. Share.** Complete [[s01-P14-4]]. Listen, then discuss.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Park Maps and [[s01-X14]]. Your child writes alone; save feedback until day 16.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public sharing; keep both source-link critiques and independent exit. Longer: find a second visible detail for the same supplied claim.

### Words and supports
Review: claim, evidence, reasoning and limit. Keep claim-reference.md available, including its limit line. Read the paragraph aloud. Accept dictation that preserves the child's ideas; source pointing may support writing.

### Tomorrow
Day 15 uses unseen maps for the end-of-unit assessment. Do not repair E14 first. Its error H lacks evidence, error I uses one map only, and error N claims everything. Route these to day 16 with assessment feedback.
