# Day 14: Evidence from two maps

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 13's work with one cue. Prepare saved day 1 responses, the question board and retained A/B maps. Separate Side 2. Prepare the generated teacher-only answer key. When it exists, the section plan may guide preparation; the printed claim reference replaces it.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return s01-P13-2, s01-P13-3, s01-P13-4 and s01-P13-5. Review day 13's coordinates and zero lines. If many showed error G or error P on day 13's exit, use the matching reference repair in [[s01-P14-R]]. One dominant repair only; others follow individual cues. Corrected references stay in the folder.

#### 8 min. Evidence retrieval
Return day 1's saved question and reason privately. Review grade 3's claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Review claim, evidence and reasoning from day 11. Read the supplied paragraph aloud; children annotate [[s01-P14-1]]. Discuss its detail-to-claim links. Annotations stay in the folder.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Use retained Physical Map A and Political Map B, both made by Curio Class for students. Keep the full reference frame visible. Under the given claim, children independently write [[s01-P14-2]] and [[s01-P14-3]]. First two minutes: reread the saved day 1 responses. Partners then check source links, two 45-second turns. Teacher reserves five minutes for the second grid repair group. After attempts, give feedback from the teacher answer key.

#### 5 min. Share evidence
Everyone writes [[s01-P14-4]]. Hear six children, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total; anyone may pass publicly. Collect every evidence sentence and explanation. Add supported details to the question board without composing a full usefulness-and-limit paragraph.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket
Say the Park Maps are made up. Reveal Side 2 and [[s01-X14]]. Children independently write on the half slip, consulting both maps. Collect all slips. Mark afterward: 30 children times 90 seconds, 45 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Hold feedback until day 16 to preserve day 15's assessment.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the retained map details and frame. Copy both Park Maps separately, withholding them until exit. Read labels without supplying comparisons.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: draw separate teaching and Park Maps.
**Skip.** The board and public sharing.
- **5 min. Return.** Use day 13's cue with [[s01-P14-R]].
- **8 min. Read.** Read the paragraph together; attempt [[s01-P14-1]].
- **12 min. Evidence.** Your child completes [[s01-P14-2]] and [[s01-P14-3]] using retained A/B. **Say.** "How does that detail support the claim?" Expect: a link to physical or political information.
- **3 min. Share.** Complete [[s01-P14-4]]. Listen, then discuss.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Park Maps and [[s01-X14]]. Your child writes alone; save feedback until day 16.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public sharing; keep both source sentences and independent exit. Longer: find a second visible detail for the same supplied claim.

### Words and supports
Review: claim, evidence, reasoning and limit. Keep the full frame below available. Read the paragraph aloud. Accept dictation that preserves the child's ideas; source pointing may support writing.

### Tomorrow
Day 15 uses unseen maps for the end-of-unit assessment. Do not repair E14 first. Its error H lacks evidence, error I uses one map only, and error N claims everything. Route these to day 16 with assessment feedback.
