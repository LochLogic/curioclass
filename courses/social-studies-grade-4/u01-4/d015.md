# Day 15: End-of-unit map assessment

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the assessment form once, with its two faces, plus separate source sheets C, D, D supplement, E/F/G, coordinate grid, measurement inset and Card H/Madison inset. Verify the measurement bar at 2 centimeters and segment at 3; hand-draw exact copies if resized. Keep key-15.md teacher-only. Withhold all sources until assessment. Save day 14 feedback for day 16.

### The day
#### 11 min. Assessment Q1
Use separate source C and its measurement inset. Children work alone on [[s01-A1a]], [[s01-A1b]], [[s01-A1c]] and [[s01-A1d]]. Read directions neutrally within these minutes. The measurement inset is made up; its scale applies only there. No worked example, repair or self-marking. Keep C's title, key and index visible. Write feature names by the numbers.

#### 13 min. Assessment Q2
Use D, its seven-location supplement, the Madison inset, coordinate grid and Card H. Use D, its enlarged political insets, the nesting labels, coordinate inset and Card H. Alone: [[s01-A2a-outline]], [[s01-A2a]], [[s01-A2b]], [[s01-A2b-county]], [[s01-A2b-province]], [[s01-A2c]], [[s01-A2c-read]] and [[s01-A2d]]. Review references remain available, without worked cases or answers. Each child accesses all sources directly. Dictation records the child's exact relationships.

#### 6 min. Assessment Q3
Children use source E/F/G independently for [[s01-A3a]], [[s01-A3b]] and [[s01-A3c]]. E's library, park and street are made up; lake and wider locations are real. No response requires personal or household information.

#### 10 min. Assessment Q4
Use the response form for the paragraph, consulting separate C/D sources. Allow eight minutes for [[s01-A4a]]; assess [[s01-A4b]] from that same paragraph. Keep the same source sheets visible. Allow two minutes for [[s01-A4c]]. Score [[s01-A4c-interpretation]] from the visitor and other-reader work without another ask. Collect all packets. After class, mark each component using only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; no total. Score claim/reasoning once and multiple-source support once. Allow four minutes per child, two hours for 30, an untested estimate. Return day 16 with E14 and one combined priority move.

### Other rooms
No printer: hand-copy the response form and map sources, keeping the measured inset at actual size individually before class. No ruler: give a measured centimeter strip. Enlarge or raise lines; read labels without identifying answers. Additional access time does not mean content help.

### Shorter or longer
Keep all components; allow access time separately when needed. An early finisher rereads their own evidence without consulting another child's response.

### Words and supports
Review general word meanings from earlier days only. Read these neutral meanings on request: latitude, degrees north/south from equator; longitude, degrees east/west from prime meridian; province, a major Canadian division; county, a division within this example state; nation, a people with their own government; claim, what you argue; evidence, a supporting source detail. Never identify today's relationships. No rehearsal with a partner during this independent assessment.

### Tomorrow
Day 16 returns day 15's assessment and E14. Prioritize one gap: parts/scale, political terms, grid/extent, or evidence/limits. Error H, error I and error N from E14 join the same priority. Four five-minute response groups precede fresh private component repairs.

### Credit
Ours. Geographic Sources 15C0 to 15O support labels and locations. Source 15H supplies Card H's government facts. All cultural pins and the measurement segment are invented.
