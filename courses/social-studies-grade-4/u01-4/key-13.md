# Teacher-only day 13 answers

Teacher use only. Keep this page away from student sheets.

Before class, read each answer beside its printed stimulus. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## s01-P13-R

Order: latitude, longitude. Latitude zero: equator. Longitude zero: prime meridian.

Look for: Corrects only the assigned order or zero-line error; no point pair is produced.

## s01-P13-1

A: latitude unchanged. B: longitude unchanged.

Look for: A runs along a parallel. B follows a meridian. No full point coordinates required.

## s01-P13-2

Latitude: north/south from equator. Longitude: east/west from prime meridian.

Look for: Matches two supplied headings to separate correct margin descriptions.; No swapped-heading repair or coordinate pair.

## s01-P13-3

Latitude stays unchanged along Route C, which follows the 15 degrees north parallel.

Look for: Traces Route C along a parallel.; Names its unchanged measurement without producing a full coordinate pair.

## s01-P13-4

Coordinate degrees measure angles, not ground-distance units.

Look for: Explains degree labels with the general reference, not a supplied coordinate pair.

## s01-P13-5

Latitude matches north/south from equator; longitude matches east/west from prime meridian. Accept either justified match.

Look for: Links one matched heading to its zero-line reference.; All submit the heading match; six may explain for 45 seconds each.
