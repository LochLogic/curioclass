# Teacher-only day 14 answers

Teacher use only. Keep this page away from student sheets.

Before class, read each answer beside its printed stimulus. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## s01-P14-R

Latitude comes first and measures north/south from equator; longitude measures east/west from prime meridian.

Look for: Corrects assigned ordering or zero-line statement without reading or plotting an assessment point.

## s01-P14-1

Evidence: A marks Mississippi River; B bounds Mexico. Link: so their details differ.

Look for: Identifies source-linked details and the inference linking them to the given claim.

## s01-P14-2

Look for: Critiques a supplied claim/detail link without adding evidence or writing a new argument.

## s01-P14-3

## s01-P14-4
