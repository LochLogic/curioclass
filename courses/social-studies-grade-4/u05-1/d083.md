# Day 83: Canals, roads and people

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-083.md, practice-083.md master, record.md, question-board.md and source-log.md. Use canal-outline.md as the single shared canal reading, its two factual paragraphs only; no second canal text. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated keys and exit-083-sources.md adult-only. Return day 82 papers. Hold exit-083.md and exit-083-stimulus.md covered until Exit ticket.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 82's exit and question-board papers. Hand back every assigned written task, including practice and repair papers, with private feedback. For error B model identifying who made the made-up dock notice, then assign [[s05-F83-1]]. Error F uses [[s05-F83-2]]; error A uses [[s05-F83-3]]; error L uses [[s05-F83-4]]. Choose one priority per child. More than a third sharing it: one class move; otherwise small group. Others follow written cues in the same slot.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Read the single canal outline aloud. Review canal as a built waterway for boats; define freight as goods carried between places. A market is a place where people buy and sell. Transport can carry goods to buyers and people to settlements. It does not itself transfer a country's claim.
Read the separate National Road caption: freight wagons carried heavy goods across the Allegheny Mountains. Point to two isolated teaching sketches: a boat on water; a wagon on land. Do not join them into a complete route.
Read Loretta Metoxen's attributed Oneida land-loss account within the outline. Name her as a tribal historian. Review homeland. People already lived along the route; easier travel for newcomers could accompany land loss and displacement for Oneida people. This is an account of people, not empty land.

#### 17 min. Transport work
First five minutes finish the priority repair. Cover its model before the assigned response. For the remaining twelve minutes, show only the isolated sketches. Children use [[s05-P83-1]] for the road vehicle. Use [[s05-P83-3]] for the separate water link, then [[s05-P83-2]] for the historian's effect. No full journey or road/canal contrast.
Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Answer [[s05-P83-4]] individually. Review source log: record maker, date and relevant detail. Fifteen parallel pairs each take 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total, pointing to a selected detail. No child reenacts displacement or reports personal family experiences.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the travel question. Hear six new volunteers reading one detail from their paper, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Public passing is allowed. Record names; collect all papers. Everyone receives written feedback on day 84.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover models. Reveal the made-up farmer journey on the separate inset and the half slip. Read labels neutrally: Hudson means Hudson River. Children independently answer [[s05-X83-1]] and [[s05-X83-2]]. Accept an arrowed, labeled route with a dictated explanation. The water connection and inland road are separate journeys; no distance is inferred. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty exits, plus fifteen for practice and board papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; neutral reading or exact scribing is access.
