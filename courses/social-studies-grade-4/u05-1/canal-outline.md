# Single shared canal outline

The Erie Canal opened in 1825. Boats carried people and freight along its waterway. The canal supported trade between inland places and the coast.

Oneida historian Loretta Metoxen describes land loss and displacement linked to the canal. People already lived along the route.

This is the provisional version of the one shared canal reading. Use this outline if the shared reading is unavailable. Use one reading, never both. The route inset is held for the exit.
