# Day 80: Questions about growth

## Teacher page
### Before class
Send family.md; an adult student receives their own note and makes their own choice. Prepare record.md, question-board.md, source-log.md, growth-map.md and timeline.md. Put hook-rivers.svg beside teaching-locator.png; the river sketch is not a homeland boundary. Keep teacher-key.md and current generated keys adult-only. Cover exit-080.md until Exit ticket. Read cards-080.md aloud; use practice-080.md as preparation only.

### The day
#### 5 min. Hook
Pick up day 79's Tomorrow: open "Who gained and who lost as the United States grew?" Unfinished unit 4 work stays in folders. Review homeland: land a nation's people have known as home for a very long time. Point to the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla river-region card and today's government heading. These peoples and their government continue today. Use [[s05-K80]] before [[steps:s05-K80]]. No exact homeland boundary is claimed.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years, from grade 3 day 91. Children answer [[s05-P80-1]] and [[s05-P80-2]] privately. Review colony: a settlement far from the home country, which claims it. Do not score retrieval; flag gaps for day 81.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review supporting question: a smaller question that helps answer the big one. Present [[s05-P80-3]] before explaining the matches. A route alone cannot establish duration; departure and arrival times can. An event date needs a dated record. Model evidence selection, without composing a question about an omitted resident account.

#### 12 min. Question work
Review the question board. Teacher writes the five supplied questions from the student page. Fifteen parallel pairs use [[s05-P80-4]], each child taking 45 seconds, 90 seconds total. Co-write the board by placing their selected question cards and answer-day labels. Children do not rehearse the exit's question generation. Individually answer [[s05-P80-5]]; review the source log by recording maker, date and one relevant detail. Keep Growth Map notes and timeline in folders.

#### 5 min. Question board
Hear six different volunteers, one 45-second turn each: 4.5 minutes. They read their P80-5 evidence choice; passing is allowed. Record names. Collect every child's practice and board paper, including those unheard. All receive written feedback on day 81.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover teaching. Reveal the made-up visitor case on exit-080.md and [[s05-X80-1]], exactly as printed. Children answer independently; neutral reading or exact scribing is access. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty exits, one minute each, plus fifteen minutes for practice and board papers. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen; keep retrieval ungraded. Route error U, error L or retrieval gaps to day 81.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy tasks and trace verified masters; never guess boundaries. No display is needed.

**Swap.** No printer: copy the student tasks and trace the verified map master. Copy a covered exit.

  **Say.** "Does shading list residents?" Expect: no; the named peoples have homelands.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** Use [[s05-P80-1]] and [[s05-P80-2]]. Give reminders afterward.
- **8 min. Teaching.** Use [[s05-P80-3]]; discuss the evidence choices.
- **12 min. Questions.** Point to the five posted questions. Use [[s05-P80-4]] and [[s05-P80-5]] together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the made-up visitor case and [[s05-X80-1]]. Your child answers alone. Give a private next step afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce pair repetition. Keep retrieval, teaching and exit. Longer: name a second useful record.

### Words and supports
Review supporting question, homeland, colony and source log where used. Read all context aloud. Offer home-language rehearsal; keep exit reasoning independent.

### Tomorrow
Day 81 returns day 80 papers in Return and repair, then maps and dates growth. Error U needs evidence relevance; error L needs a named-resident reminder. One priority continues in Map work.
