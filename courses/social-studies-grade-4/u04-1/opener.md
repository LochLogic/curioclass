## Ideas and continuity

Voyage history uses different evidence for different questions. Material objects need find context. A source from the time may still contain mistakes or an imagined scene. Review supporting question, primary source, secondary source and source-sourcing from grade 3. Maker/date differ from later recorder/adapter. Audience and purpose need internal clues. The source log returns from earlier grade 4 work. Supply its blank headings independently.

## Errors, repair and evidence

Use the unit error register and this section's teacher key. Return all judged papers with one written priority. More than a third missing means whole-class repair; otherwise meet a group. Five Return and repair minutes and the first five work minutes share one priority and a fresh brief retry. Do not rehearse tomorrow's held stimulus. E77 content feedback waits until day 79; day 78 offers neutral access only. On day 79, Feedback holds five minutes and Response groups twenty. Four five-minute adult meetings hear at most two 45-second asks each. Everyone else receives a written move and submits a fresh paper attempt.

One scale throughout: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Unobserved stays blank. Preserve separate component evidence, without points or averages. Dictation and neutral reading are access supports; record content prompts separately.

## Talk and safety

Children may pass during public talk. In a five-minute board part, hear six 45-second turns, totaling four and a half minutes. Groups A to E rotate on days 60 to 64, 65 to 69, and 70 to 74. Written evidence reaches every child daily; no later individual oral assessment is promised. Fifteen parallel pairs need 90 seconds for one 45-second turn each. Day 76 sharing and day 79 reflection use those parallel turns.

Say that all invented people, towns and records are made up. Never invent Native identities or testimony. No reenactment, sacred designs, graphic imagery or personal-disclosure questions. Read the source cards neutrally. No source is claimed to represent every Native community. Families and students age 18 or older receive the choices in family.md; school rules also apply.

## Materials and two rooms

Thirty pencils and folders, printed cards and slips, one question board and a teacher record. Map tracing uses the original schematics. Ruler tasks use actual paper, not projected lengths. Print scaled SVGs at 100 percent and verify one centimeter physically. A home adult reads neutrally and takes the partner role. No student timer or score is used.
