# Day 47: Whose public account?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read both teacher-only keys. Return day 46 papers with one priority cue. Assign one repair row per child, B or J. Keep s03 exit 47's paired caption covered. Use this page's separate numbered labels as s03 living nations. Choose six unheard names on the s03 record. No live website or visitor is needed.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return every written task from day 46. Review its presence-versus-journey distinction. If many day 46 tickets showed error B or error J, address the shared priority. Otherwise meet that group. Use [[s03-P47-RB]] or [[s03-P47-RJ]], never both per child. Others revise returned classifications. Finish repairs in Account work's first five minutes.

#### 8 min. Public accounts
Review day 42: the Hopi Tribe is a living sovereign nation; Pueblo communities are distinct tribes. Introduce origin account: a named nation's own account of its beginnings. Today read only public, secular descriptions of people and movement. Do not retell sacred narratives or ask children about belief or ancestry.

Read label 1. **Ask.** [[s03-K47-1]] Wait, then reveal [[steps:s03-K47-1]]. Naming the teller keeps the account connected to its nation. Review the source log's maker, date and public audience. Archaeology and nation accounts stand beside each other; these labels do not rank them.

#### 17 min. Account work
First five minutes finish repairs. Children underline tellers on separate labels with [[s03-P47-1]], [[s03-P47-2]] and [[s03-P47-3]]. Then sort [[s03-P47-4]], [[s03-P47-5]] and [[s03-P47-6]]. The sort identifies each question's focus. A nation's account can itself use archaeological evidence, as Hunter's does; these are not competing boxes for entire sources.

Pairs each take 45 seconds to explain one sort, 1.5 minutes in parallel. Teacher feedback points to the question's focus and named teller. No joint museum labels are drafted. Collect every page.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the board routine. Everyone writes [[s03-P47-Q]]. Hear six single 45-second observations, 4.5 minutes, with a pass allowed. Use written notes if repairs need attention. Record the naming principle beside the archaeology questions.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal s03 exit 47's new paired caption: [[s03-X47]]. Children answer independently. After class allow 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes, to mark; add ten minutes for practice feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately. Return every paper on day 48.

### Other rooms
No screen: use printed labels. No printer: copy these labels and the separate exit card. Keep the paired caption unseen until the independent turn.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the separate labels and a covered exit card.
**Skip.** The class roster and unneeded repairs.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 46 feedback. Use the assigned B or J repair.
- **10 min. Read.** Read label 1. Use [[s03-K47-1]].
  **Say.** "Which words name the teller?" Expect Hopi Tribe.
- **15 min. Sort.** Your child underlines tellers with [[s03-P47-1]], [[s03-P47-2]] and [[s03-P47-3]]. Sort [[s03-P47-4]], [[s03-P47-5]] and [[s03-P47-6]]. Discuss the question's focus. No belief or family report is needed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-47.md's paired caption and [[s03-X47]]. Your child answers alone. Read the separate answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public sharing, keeping written notes. Longer: explain how one account can include several evidence types.

### Words and supports
Origin account; teller: the named author or nation speaking. Review source-log headings. Read neutrally; child-directed scribing records the child's attribution without supplying it.

### Tomorrow
Day 48 weighs route arguments and writes a claim. Error L makes one nation speak for everyone. More than ten misses: shared repair; otherwise a priority group. Return all papers in Return and repair and Claim work's first five minutes.
