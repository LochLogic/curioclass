# Day 45: A crossing and a separate passage

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read both teacher-only keys. Return day 44 exits and practice with one priority cue. Print s03 route layers; cover its final coast-tracing instruction today. Keep s03 exit 45 covered. Choose six unheard names on the s03 record. Use the student page's response spaces instead of duplicating the shared table.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review day 44's timeline: equal marks, thousand-year gaps, today at the right. If many day 44 tickets showed error A, reteach direction with [[s03-P45-R]]. Otherwise meet that group while others correct their returned work. Point along the retained line without placing new dates. These are made-up numbers. Finish one priority repair in Evidence sorting's first five minutes.

#### 8 min. Route model
Show only the undated teaching schematic. Teach Beringia as the land connection between Asia and North America. Lower sea level exposed land, according to Source 45A. A theory is an explanation tested against evidence. Migration means people moving between places.

**Ask.** [[s03-K45-1]] Wait, then reveal [[steps:s03-K45-1]]. Cover the southern label while pointing to the crossing; uncover it separately. A corridor means a passage. Land exposure and conditions farther south answer different questions. Review limit: what evidence cannot show, from grade 3. A drawing illustrates an idea; it is not evidence of an actual journey.

#### 17 min. Evidence sorting
First five minutes finish repairs. Pairs sort [[s03-P45-1]], [[s03-P45-2]] and [[s03-P45-3]] individually before comparing. Each child takes 45 seconds to explain one classification, 1.5 minutes per parallel pair. Ask for a question-category choice only, not a full route explanation. Teacher feedback points to land, ice or food; it never uses the exit inset. Collect every page.

Review the source log from day 42: identify the maker and the teaching schematic's purpose. It represents route questions, not a recorded journey.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the question board routine. Everyone writes [[s03-P45-Q]]. Hear six single 45-second contributions, 4.5 minutes, allowing a pass. If repair attention is needed, use written contributions instead. Add one question to the dates/routes strand.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal s03 exit 45, including its inset, only now: [[s03-X45a]] [[s03-X45b]]. Children answer independently. Collect. After class, mark both components at 30 seconds per child: 15 minutes for thirty. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately. Allow ten more minutes for practice feedback. Return all papers on day 46.

### Other rooms
No screen: use the teaching print. No printer: copy its separate labels and the complete exit inset privately. Keep dates and keys covered until the independent turn.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the teaching labels and a separate complete exit inset.
**Skip.** The class roster and unneeded repairs.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 44 feedback. Use [[s03-P45-R]] if needed.
- **10 min. Model.** Point to the land connection on the teaching sheet. Use [[s03-K45-1]].
  **Say.** "Which question does this label address?" Expect crossing between continents.
- **15 min. Sort.** Your child answers [[s03-P45-1]], [[s03-P45-2]] and [[s03-P45-3]]. Discuss one choice. Land concerns crossing; ice and food concern the southern passage.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover exit-45.md with its inset. Give [[s03-X45a]] and [[s03-X45b]]. Your child answers alone. Read the separate answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public sharing, retaining written contributions. Longer: compare the questions addressed by two sorted labels.

### Words and supports
Beringia; theory; migration; corridor. Review limit. Read labels neutrally; enlarged print and child-directed dictation preserve the reasoning.

### Tomorrow
Day 46 introduces coastal possibilities and dated evidence. Error C treats exposed land as proof of the whole inland route. More than ten misses: shared repair; otherwise meet the priority group. Return all papers in Return and repair and Evidence work's first five minutes.
