# Day 48: Claims with room for uncertainty

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read both teacher-only keys. Return day 47 papers with one priority cue. Keep s03 exit 48's complete diagram and dating note covered. Retrieve s03 migration evidence, s03 living nations and s03 timeline practice. Supply blank claim/detail/reason/uncertainty headings. Remove filled demonstrations before independent work. Choose six unheard names on the s03 record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return every written task from day 47. Review its separate tellers. If many day 47 tickets showed error L, use [[s03-P48-R]] together; otherwise meet that group. Others revise their returned author labels. Refer to retained labels as reminders only. Finish repairs in Claim work's first five minutes.

#### 5 min. Recall guide
Review reasoning from grade 3: how a detail supports a claim. Review limit: what evidence cannot show. Recall day 46's distinction between presence and a proven journey with [[s03-P48-G]]. Consult its summary, then cover completed examples. Review day 44's years-ago direction on the retained line, without placing new dates. Use the blank headings; uncertainty states what remains unknown. Nothing new is introduced here.

#### 20 min. Claim work
First five minutes finish repairs. Teams of three annotate [[s03-P48]] and [[s03-P48-2]]. Mark the supplied overclaim, consult Source 46A's summary and write individual corrected links. Each child selects one correction to develop under the blank headings. Review the source log: connect the detail to its maker.

Teacher feedback checks the link between detail and claim. Two defensible emphases: the points establish presence; a route remains unresolved. Neither proves a unique journey. A possible coast segment can follow a land crossing. Do not introduce a new site caption, route-test task or date pair. Collect each child's claim record.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the board routine. Partners each take 45 seconds explaining a revision, 1.5 minutes in parallel, then improve their record. During this same block the teacher hears six staggered 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes total; no queue. A public pass is allowed. If repairs need attention, collect written records instead. Post one supported, qualified claim.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal s03 exit 48's invented diagram and complete dating note: [[s03-X48a]] [[s03-X48b]]. Both paths are hypothetical. Children answer independently. After class allow 30 times 35 seconds, 17.5 minutes, for both components, plus ten minutes for claim feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately. Return every paper on day 49.

### Other rooms
No screen: use retained print. No printer: copy the supplied arguments and a separate complete exit diagram and note. Label its situation made up.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the arguments and a separate complete exit diagram and note.
**Skip.** Team roles, the roster and unneeded repair.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 47 feedback. Use [[s03-P48-R]] if needed.
- **5 min. Recall.** Use [[s03-P48-G]] and the retained research summary.
  **Say.** "What does this evidence leave unknown?" Expect the complete journey.
- **20 min. Revise.** Your child corrects [[s03-P48]] and [[s03-P48-2]]. Choose one to develop under claim, detail, reason and uncertainty. Read your child's connection between evidence and claim. Cover filled examples.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-48.md with its diagram and note. Give [[s03-X48a]] and [[s03-X48b]]. Your child answers alone. Read the separate answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: collect written records without public sharing. Keep both argument corrections and independent exit. Longer: compare the emphases of two supported claims.

### Words and supports
Review claim, detail, reasoning and limit. Use blank headings rather than completed sentences. Oral rehearsal or child-directed dictation preserves the child's reasoning.

More than ten misses: shared priority repair; otherwise a group, one component each.
