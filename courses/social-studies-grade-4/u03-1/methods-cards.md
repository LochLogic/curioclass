# Archaeology Methods

An archaeologist studies the past through material evidence.

An artifact is an object made and used long ago.

Context means a find's recorded location, layer and associations.

Review climate: what a place is usually like over many years.

Invented practice records; no real excavation:

| Method | Record needed |
|---|---|
| Controlled dig | Positions before soil moves |
| Artifact study | Close views of a shaped edge |
| Organic traces | Preserved seeds with recorded locations |
| Climate evidence | Plant traces compared across layers |
| Sky observation | Repeated horizon positions of a star |
| Teamwork | Different specialists' separately checked results |
