# Day 44: A line reaching thousands of years back

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read teacher-key.md and d044-key.html, including the three repair rows. Return all day 43 papers, including written tasks, with one priority cue. Assign only one of the three repair rows. Prepare s03 timeline practice and keep s03 exit 44 covered. Check equal tick spacing and readable labels on copied lines. Choose six unheard names on the s03 record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review day 43's private-feedback routine. If many day 43 tickets showed error F, K or O, teach the shared priority; otherwise meet that group. Use one assigned row: [[s03-P44-RF]], [[s03-P44-RK]] or [[s03-P44-RO]]. Others revise from written feedback using their methods chart. Alternatives share this slot and Label work's first five minutes. No repeated oral queue.

#### 8 min. Read the line
Review grade 3's timeline with equal marks for equal years. Today's unit is new: each gap represents one thousand years. Point to today, then move left, counting thousand-year gaps. These are invented number examples, not claims about real events.

Use preplaced points [[s03-P44-1]] and [[s03-P44-2]] before revealing [[steps:s03-P44-1]] and [[steps:s03-P44-2]]. Explain: fourteen gaps represent fourteen thousand years; six gaps represent six thousand. Larger years-ago counts reach farther into the past. To place a date, read the unit, find its matching tick and write beside it. Children do not place dates during practice.

#### 17 min. Label work
Use the first five minutes to finish day 43's priority repairs. Then children independently read [[s03-P44-3]], [[s03-P44-4]] and [[s03-P44-5]]. Point to the dots already on the supplied line. Use [[s03-P44-6]] to correct only the separate mistaken note, keeping every dot fixed. Collect all label work.

Partners take one 45-second turn each about how they read a label. Adult feedback points to the unit and today end, never gives reserved dates. Use the retained source log to record today's kind as an invented teaching diagram; its numbers are not historical evidence.

#### 5 min. Question board
Everyone answers [[s03-P44-Q]]. Hear six single 45-second contributions, 4.5 minutes total, with a public pass. Use written contributions if repair attention is needed. Add the interval meaning to the dates/routes strand; do not claim the line alone establishes a journey.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal s03 exit 44: [[s03-X44a]] and [[s03-X44b]] with its supplied line. Children place and circle independently. Collect. After class allow 30 times 25 seconds, 12.5 minutes, for marking both components. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately. Allow another ten minutes for practice feedback. Return exits and practice on day 45.

### Other rooms
No screen: use printed lines. No printer: use a ruler to copy equal ticks and the unit label; preserve all positions. Enlarge rather than squeeze labels. Keep teaching and exit lines separate.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy both teaching lines and the separate exit line with a ruler.
**Skip.** The six-child roster and repairs your child does not need.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 43's feedback. Use the assigned repair row only.
- **10 min. Read.** Point to today and the unit. Use [[s03-P44-1]] and [[s03-P44-2]].
  **Say.** "How many years does one gap represent?" Expect one thousand years.
- **15 min. Labels.** Your child reads [[s03-P44-3]] and corrects [[s03-P44-6]]. Dots stay fixed. Read the separate mistaken note aloud; the line itself is correct.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X44a]] and [[s03-X44b]] with the unseen supplied line. Your child answers alone. Consult the separate key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: collect written board notes without public sharing. Keep unit reading, label correction, repairs and exit. Longer: explain why years-ago counts increase toward the past.

### Words and supports
Thousand: 1,000. Review equal intervals. Years ago counts back from today. Pointing, enlarged lines and child-directed scribing preserve placement; content hints are recorded separately.

### Tomorrow
Day 45 introduces the land bridge theory and its evidence. Error A on day 44's exit needs the unit and today reference retaught. More than ten misses: shared teaching; otherwise a priority group. Return all papers across Return and repair and Evidence sorting's first five minutes, one fresh component per child.
