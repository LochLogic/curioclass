# Day 43: Records help researchers check ideas

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read teacher-key.md and d043-key.html, including repair and model rows. Return every day 42 paper, including written tasks, with one priority cue. Assign one repair per child. Prepare the six-method chart without reserved diagrams. Label Source 42A: National Park Service, 2023, public readers, explaining field methods. Select six different speakers on the s03 record. Keep s03 exit 43 covered until the exit slot.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review day 41's private-feedback routine. If many day 42 tickets showed error N, use [[s03-P43-RN]] together; otherwise meet that priority group. Century misses use [[s03-P43-RT]]; living-government misses use [[s03-P43-RG]] with the Hopi reminder. Alternatives share ten minutes across this slot and Method work's first five minutes. Others revise their question from written feedback. No oral queue.

#### 8 min. Method model
Introduce archaeologist: a researcher studying the past through material evidence. Review artifact and climate in grade 3's words on the chart. Model [[s03-K43-1]], then [[steps:s03-K43-1]], with invented sample records. No real digging.

Source 42A explains careful recording and samples. Undisturbed lower layers are usually older. Later digging can move material; depth alone is then insufficient. Define archaeological context as recorded location, layer and associated finds, distinct from day 20's source-setting context. Review shows, might suggest, cannot tell. Observation names a recorded detail; inference explains it.

#### 17 min. Method work
Finish repairs in the first five minutes. Pairs then match six missing records, [[s03-P43-1]] through [[s03-P43-6]], using s03 methods cards; each child records matches. Give each partner one 45-second turn about one match.

Explain limits separately: an edge shows shaping, not its maker's name; seeds show plant traces, not every meal. Source 43P supports using plant evidence to investigate environments. Climate concerns many years, not one day's weather. Sky records track repeated positions, not private meanings (Source 43S). Specialists compare different records; disagreement requires checks (Source 43T). No single specialist settles everything. Add one method detail to the retained source log. Collect all matches.

#### 5 min. Question board
Use [[s03-P43-Q]]. Hear six single 45-second contributions, 4.5 minutes total; public passing is allowed. If repairs still require attention, use written contributions instead. Add one method note to the board.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Give the complete reserved diagram card, [[s03-X43a]] and [[s03-X43b]], alone. Collect all papers. After class budget 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes. Mark each component shown, shown with help, not yet seen; note access separately. Allow another twenty minutes for practice feedback. Return exits and practice on day 44.

### Other rooms
Draw only the teaching records without a screen. Without printing, copy the complete exit diagrams and stems separately; preserve U's continuous layers and V's later pit. Never show them during practice.

### At home
**Swap.** No class set: share the chart. No printer: copy records and complete exit diagrams.
**Skip.** The speaker roster and unneeded repairs.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 42's cue. Use only the assigned repair.
- **10 min. Model.** Use [[s03-K43-1]]; uncover its steps afterward.
  **Say.** "Which record could another researcher check?" Expect measured position and a sample label.
- **15 min. Methods.** Your child uses the chart for [[s03-P43-1]], [[s03-P43-3]] and [[s03-P43-6]]. Discuss each method's limit. Read the definitions aloud if needed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s03-X43a]] and [[s03-X43b]] with their diagrams. Your child answers independently. Consult the separate key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: use written board contributions. Keep repairs, all six methods and exit. Longer: discuss another needed record.

### Words and supports
Review artifact: an object made and used long ago; climate: usual conditions over many years. Layer: a band of soil. Organic remains include preserved seeds. Astronomical observations are repeated sky-position records. Enlarge diagrams; allow neutral reading and dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 44 returns day 43's papers. Errors F, K or O receive one priority across Return and repair and Label work's first five minutes: complete records, disturbance, or independent checks. More than ten misses: teach the shared priority; otherwise meet that group. Then read a thousands-of-years line.
