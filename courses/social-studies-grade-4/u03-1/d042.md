# Day 42: Questions that need evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class
Prepare the named print. Keep teacher-key.md and d042-key.html teacher-only. Mark six names on the s03 record. Send the family note; adult students receive it themselves and choose their own consent. Prepare day 41's marked fresh routes and exit tickets with private cues. Display the retrieval strip without its response lines; student pages hold those responses. Keep the reserved exit covered.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Retrieval
Use the first minute to return day 41's assigned C1-P41-A, C1-P41-B, C1-P41-C, C1-P41-CP or C1-P41-D and C1-X41 with one written cue. Four retrieval minutes remain. Keep unresolved components on the record; no content repair is added. Alone: [[s03-P42a]] [[s03-P42b]] [[s03-P42c]]. Partners rehearse the supplied claim detail, 45 seconds each in parallel; each then marks [[s03-P42f]]. Review grade 3's equal marks for equal years, century meaning 100 years, and nation: a people with their own government. Read Source 42D's current Hopi statement. These responses are unmarked. Log misses for day 43; day 41's map needs stay on the private record.

#### 7 min. Hook
Pairs view the wall photograph and Pueblo Today summary on s03 hook cards. Read captions neutrally: Source 42B supplies background, Source 42C the present-day summary. Review grade 3's labels: shows, might suggest, cannot tell. Visible openings are observations; the photograph alone cannot supply people's thoughts. Accurate evidence affects what others learn; liking a picture supplies no historical evidence. Do not complete an importance explanation for a particular user.

#### 8 min. Question board
Post the unit question. Review supporting question: a smaller question helping answer the big one. Use [[s03-K42-1]], then [[steps:s03-K42-1]]. Review the question-board routine from grade 3. Co-write its four strands: methods, dates/routes, regions/objects, preservation; copy the answer-day ranges from the s03 question board. Each partner audits one supplied question, 45 seconds each, simultaneously across fifteen pairs.

#### 15 min. Question work
Alone: [[s03-P42d]] [[s03-P42e]]. Review the source log from day 19: maker, date, audience, purpose, kind, detail and reason. Children use one hook caption; unknown dates stay unknown. Hear six contributions, 45 seconds each, while others write. Collect everyone's question and evidence record. Feedback redirects opinions toward findable records; no completed importance-to-writer model. Children keep logs in folders.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Give s03 exit 42, [[s03-X42]] and [[s03-X42b]], first seen now. Children answer alone; collect all slips. After class allow 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes, for marking both items. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for each separate component; access support is separate. Allow another ten minutes for question-record feedback; use the same scale if recording a component. Return all papers on day 43 with one priority cue.

### Other rooms
No screen: share printed hook cards. No printer: copy stems and the strip; share the saved photograph. A drawing is labeled a drawing. No real digging or personal-history questions.

### At home
**Swap.** No class set: share one hook card. No printer: copy the strip and stems; view the saved photograph.
**Skip.** The six-child roster and wall display.
- **5 min. Look back.** Return day 41's route and exit with a written cue. Use [[s03-P42a]] and [[s03-P42c]]. Read the reminder before answering.
- **10 min. Questions.** View the hook cards. Read [[s03-K42-1]], then its steps.
  **Say.** "Which supplied question could this record help answer?" Expect the building-time question.
- **15 min. Record.** Your child completes [[s03-P42d]] and [[s03-P42e]]. Point to caption information for the log; leave unknown dates unknown.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X42]] and [[s03-X42b]] alone. Read neutrally, collect, then consult the teacher key privately.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public sharing, retaining question audits and exit. Longer: identify another record the same question needs.

### Words and supports
Review century, nation, supporting question and source log. Read captions aloud; offer enlarged print or child-directed dictation. Public passing is allowed.

### Tomorrow
Day 43 returns day 42's papers in Return and repair and Method work's first five minutes. Error N needs an evidence-seeking question or an accurate-information use; retrieval misses need a century or living-government reminder. More than ten misses: teach the shared priority; otherwise meet that group. Archaeology methods follow.
