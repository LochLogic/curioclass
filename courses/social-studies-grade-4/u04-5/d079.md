# Day 79: Improve one component

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 77's exit tickets and written tasks, and day 78's assessment, with one private component cue. Print teacher-key.md separately. Current generated item keys govern corrected rows. Assign Sourcing, Consequences, Chronology or Claims from actual misses. Separate response-moves.md from the unseen retry cards; give only one route per child. Set out unit 4 response and retry cards and component record. Keep each retry covered until Fresh attempt. All response cases are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Use one minute for day 77's errors K/F and four for day 78's assessment. Children read private cues and identify one priority, not a total. Review the component's criterion on returned work. Do not copy or redo its answer. Children with secure evidence extend the same criterion on a different route.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Meet four groups sequentially, five minutes each: Sourcing, Consequences, Chronology, Claims. Route errors A/B/H/M/S/U to Sourcing; C/D/E/L/N/O/Q to Consequences; F/G/P/T to Chronology; I/J/K/R/V/W to Claims. More than a third sharing one gap: use this same block for whole-class work and shorten other meetings.

Review source-sourcing with [[s04-P79-1]]: later interpretation comes from an old story, not observation. Review aim versus effect with [[s04-P79-2]]: seeks marks intention, not achieved benefit. Review equal-year intervals with [[s04-P79-3]]: ten years on each side. Review reasoning with [[s04-P79-4]]: replaced tiles support building care. Ask the assigned printed question before giving its contrast. Limit each meeting to two 45-second turns, 90 seconds total; public passing is allowed. Others follow the same written move privately. All submit their assigned classification. No fresh retry appears in teaching.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Each child receives only the matching unseen component: [[s04-R79-1]], [[s04-R79-2]], [[s04-R79-3]] or [[s04-R79-4]]. Children work alone or direct an exact scribe. Aim for six working minutes, then collect; allow unfinished work into Reflection without prompts. While children work, mark thirty group cards at eight seconds each, four minutes. Begin marking fresh papers as they arrive: thirty at eight seconds, four minutes. Estimates are untested; use Reflection for remaining papers. Both attempts use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; leave unobserved blank.

#### 5 min. Reflection
Return all assigned P79 and R79 papers with a written next-move cue, completing marking first if needed. Fifteen parallel pairs each take 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total, to point to changed evidence; passing is allowed. Every child marks improvement against the earlier criterion. Keep the original and fresh component beside each other; unresolved criteria stay in folders. No later oral turn is promised.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy only the assigned teaching card and a separate covered retry. For retry 3, preserve the marker's halfway position. No device is required.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the assigned teaching card and a covered retry.
**Skip.** Group rotations and repeated partner turns.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 77 papers and day 78's assessment. Choose one priority.
- **15 min. Response.** Use the matching group card; discuss its criterion.
  **Say.** "What does this detail support?" Expect the assigned card's supported classification.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Reveal only the matching retry. Your child answers alone. Use the private key afterward; return both papers with one written cue.
- **5 min. Reflection.** Your child points to improvement and keeps any unresolved criterion in the folder.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce pair repetition, keeping feedback and fresh evidence. Longer: explain how another detail could help the same criterion.

### Words and supports
Review source-sourcing, reasoning and interval in the assigned route only. Neutral reading and exact dictation are access. Record content help separately.

### Tomorrow
Day 80 opens "Who gained and who lost as the United States grew?" and writes supporting questions. Keep unfinished unit 4 criteria in the folder; no scheduled day 80 reteach is promised.
