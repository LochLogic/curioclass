# Day 78: Use unseen voyage evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print teacher-key.md and assessment-sources.md separately. Print the combined student page once, replacing the separate answer/source masters. Verify the blank strip exceeds twenty centimeters and grid ticks are one centimeter apart. Keep the unit 4 sealed assessment packet closed until class. Set out unit 4 centimeter rulers and component record. Prepare neutral reading or exact dictation. Remove taught packets and models.

### The day
#### 5 min. Read and settle
Pick up day 77's access arrangements only; its errors K and F wait for day 79. Read directions neutrally. Sources A to G summarize real records; no historical image is reproduced. Children work alone. Explain page navigation: Q1, Q2, Q5 and Q6 on Side 1; Q3 and Q4 on Side 2. Read sources without explaining their answers.

#### 10 min. Assessment Q1 and Q2
Use [[s04-E1a]], [[s04-E1b]], [[s04-E1c]] and [[s04-E1d]]: four source contributions, drawing limit and complementary uses. Then [[s04-E2a]], [[s04-E2b]] and [[s04-E2c]]: Norse identity/route, material versus saga evidence, encounter limits. Read captions neutrally; never supply the identity, contribution or limit. Return unfinished Q1/Q2 work during the last block.

#### 6 min. Assessment Q3
Children turn to Side 2. Use [[s04-E3a]], [[s04-E3b]], [[s04-E3c]] and [[s04-E3d]]: routes, named people, clue-based aim and documented consequence. The map is a locator, not a distance measure. No common royal-funding claim is required by this evidence.

#### 8 min. Assessment Q4
Use [[s04-E4a]], [[s04-E4b]] and [[s04-E4c]]. Children construct the century line vertically inside the blank strip, then draw aligned expedition bars on the grid. Preserve actual scale; never measure a projected image. Score the scale, historical positions, endpoints and overlap comparison separately.

#### 3 min. Assessment Q5
Return to Side 1. Use [[s04-E5a]] and [[s04-E5b]]: document maker/date, audience/purpose with internal clues. Neutral access only. Reporting conquest is supported; a support request is not required.

#### 8 min. Assessment Q6 and collect
Children use [[s04-E6a]] and [[s04-E6b]] for five minutes: one paragraph, E/G support, change/continuity and another reader's need. Last three minutes finish Q1/Q2 and collect everything. No additional exit. After class allow six minutes per child, three hours for thirty, untested. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for each component; unobserved stays blank. Record source support separately from claim quality. Return day 78's assessment on day 79.

### Other rooms
No printer: reproduce the combined sources, questions and diagrams on two sides, preserving physical scale. Read neutrally. No device is required.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the combined packet and true-scale grid.
**Skip.** Class settling and public talk.
- **40 min. Assessment.** Your child completes Q1 to Q6 alone using the combined student page, source summaries and ruler. Read wording neutrally; record dictated answers exactly. Offer no content clues. Allow unfinished source questions at the end. Consult the teacher key afterward and choose one priority for the next lesson.

### Shorter or longer
Keep every component. If access needs more time, record extra time without giving content help.

### Words and supports
Assessment access only: neutral reading, enlarged print and exact dictation. Record content prompts separately. Read the task order again when needed.

### Tomorrow
Day 79 returns day 78's assessment and day 77's papers. One priority routes each child to Sourcing, Consequences, Chronology or Claims, then a different unseen component. Keep unresolved criteria in the folder.

### Credit
Ours. Original summaries and schematics; source URLs and rights are in assessment-sources.md. All used pages opened November 23.
