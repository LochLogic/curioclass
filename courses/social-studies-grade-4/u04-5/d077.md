# Day 77: Recall source and time ideas

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 76 papers with one classification priority. Print teacher-key.md separately. Keep exit-77.md covered. Set out unit 4 teaching cards 77, source logs, voyage map, Voyage Accounts packet, question board and component record. Partners use today's recall page. Keep every assessment source sealed; content feedback from this exit waits for day 79.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
If day 76's exit showed error N, use [[s04-P77-4]]. Error J or observed error V uses [[s04-P77-5]]: a detail is not yet a reasoned claim. Error W uses [[s04-P77-6]]. All records are made up. Children classify one feature, without drafting or revising prose. More than a third missing: whole-class repair; otherwise meet a group.

#### 10 min. Recall sets
Review day 67's corroboration and grade 3's source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Children answer [[s04-P77-1]]; give immediate private feedback, copied index. Review grade 3's timeline: equal gaps for equal years. Use [[s04-P77-2]]: ten years. Review source kinds with [[s04-P77-3]]: later explanation. Children mark each classification; no speed score and no paired false-claim correction.

#### 15 min. Recall work
First five finish the priority repair, then classify a fresh record: [[s04-P77-7]], [[s04-P77-8]] or [[s04-P77-9]], matching the assigned route. Record a new response without revising yesterday's paragraphs.

**Ask.** [[s04-P77-3]] Fifteen parallel pairs each take 45 seconds per child, 90 seconds total, to explain the access clue. Passing is allowed. Expect later explanation, since the author summarizes an earlier voyage. Review source-log headings beside already taught Voyage Accounts and map labels; keep these in folders. Point to supporting questions on the question board. Collect all classifications.

#### 5 min. Prepare access
Check readable print, paper measures and neutral reading arrangements privately. Record E76 access needs. Content gaps wait for day 79. Do not show assessment sources or rehearse their answers.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-77.md now. Its claims are made up. Read [[s04-X77-1]] and [[s04-X77-2]] exactly. Children correct independently on the half slip. After class, thirty times ninety seconds, 45 minutes, covers all judged papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unobserved stays blank. Hold content feedback for day 79.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the practice records and a separate covered exit. Children mark paper classifications. Spoken reading is access, not an oral assessment.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy practice records and a covered exit.
**Skip.** Public sharing and repeated partner turns.
- **5 min. Return.** Return day 76 papers. Choose one classification route.
- **10 min. Recall.** Your child answers the three recall questions; give feedback each time.
  **Say.** "What kind is the later summary?" Expect later explanation.
- **10 min. Work.** Use the matching fresh classification. Check reading access for the next lesson.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-77.md. Your child answers [[s04-X77-1]] and [[s04-X77-2]] alone. Consult the private key afterward; hold content discussion until day 79.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated pair turns, preserving classification, feedback and exit. Longer: explain a classification orally, without new content.

### Words and supports
Review source-sourcing, corroboration, claim, detail, consent, timeline and interval. Neutral reading and exact dictation preserve independent decisions. No new writing frame is introduced.

### Tomorrow
Day 78 offers neutral access only. Errors K and F from day 77's exit enter day 79's response groups, with all papers returned there. Do not repair content during the assessment.

### Credit
Ours. Practice records are made up; no historical testimony is invented.
