# Day 97: Transfers and peoples today

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare pages, cards-097.md master and sealed exit pieces. Keep teacher-key.md and exit-097-sources.md adult-only. Use the current generated key for repairs and revised samples. Bring all day 96 work. Cover Side 2 until Community work. On teaching-locator.png show only Puerto Rico's inset; never measure its distances.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 96 tickets, practice, repairs and board notes. Review Return and repair: one priority. If many day 96 tickets showed error A, use [[s05-F97-1]]. Error B uses [[s05-F97-2]]; error F uses [[s05-F97-5]] instead. Review admission: joining as a state; consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Others use written cues. Continue in Community work.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Locate Puerto Rico. Read the local-government summary; autonomous means managing local affairs under outside rule. Never describe an empty island. Read the war record separately. Review treaty: agreement between governments. Explain cession: giving up a claim to another government; war forced this transfer. **Ask.** [[s05-P97-1]] Wait, then model [[steps:s05-P97-1]]. Review perspective: two sides of one event; governments' records cannot stand for everyone's views. Keep the war-method annotation separate from present life and Alaska's purchase.

#### 17 min. Community work
First five minutes finish one priority: [[s05-F97-3]], [[s05-F97-4]] or [[s05-F97-6]], matching the opening. Others use written cues. Alone, complete [[s05-P97-3]], then turn to Side 2. Read three public-activity notes with their access date. Complete [[s05-P97-2]]. Alaska Native peoples, Native Hawaiians and Puerto Ricans have continuing lives; one program cannot describe everyone. Fifteen simultaneous pairs rehearse one activity label, 45 seconds each child, 90 seconds total; revise alone. If a child says a people vanished, point to the current activity, without using the reserved music card.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review question-board.md and the growth question. Six rotating volunteers share an existing activity label, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record names; passing is allowed. Collect everyone's paper for day 98 feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Reveal exit-097-stimulus.md and exit-097.md. Children independently answer [[s05-X97-1]] and [[s05-X97-2]] on paper. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus thirty for other papers. Review record.md's scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work day 98.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy summaries and tasks; trace the supplied locator separately. No projector: point to the printed inset. Printed sources replace live pages or visitors.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated sharing. Keep repair, historical context, current notes and exit. Longer: refine an activity's attribution.

### Words and supports
Review treaty and perspective. Read neutrally; allow home-language rehearsal, exact dictation and private pauses. No historical roles or personal reports.
