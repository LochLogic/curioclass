# Day 158: A claim with evidence links

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-158 directions and integrated evidence-sheet/closing-display fields. Read opener and teacher-key; use current generated answers. Withdraw completed continuity-change charts and finished prose. Return day 157 work with cues. Place the two taught source summaries on Side 2; keep the exit and assessment sealed.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 157's s07-X157, s07-P157 and s07-F157. If many showed error F, request equals adoption, or error O, any body decides, compare [[s07-K158-R]] for five minutes. Everyone attempts fresh [[s07-F158]] for five. Otherwise meet up to six while others follow written cues. Unresolved error Q receives a constraint cue in the student's own record. No complete policy account is modeled. Keep repairs for day 160.

#### 5 min. Retrieval and source choice
Review grade 3's claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Review sourcing: who made it, when and why. EBC returns education support from day 139; FL returns the code record from day 152. Review: a second source needs a different maker; two details from one book are one source. Use locator and question-board to retain place and inquiry links. Blank checklist replaces completed continuity-change charts.

#### 15 min. Independent closing work
Children complete [[s07-P158]] alone as a stand-alone claim banner with separate evidence-sheet annotations and reasoning arrows. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review limit: what the evidence cannot show. Closing-display supplies the blank format, not finished prose. Each child selects an emphasis and a limit; do not supply an integrated paragraph. This work is unscored practice.

#### 5 min. Share
Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to explain a selected arrow. The other three minutes hold a written peer question and collection. Passing public talk is allowed. Collect every display for private feedback on day 160.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X158]] alone on a half-slip. Say both records are made up. Give four minutes independently, then collect. Neutral read-aloud or exact dictation preserves reasoning. After class: thirty times forty-five seconds, about twenty-three minutes. Use record's shown, shown with help, not yet seen scale; keep display feedback unscored. H/R repairs wait until day 160.

### Other rooms
Copy summaries and blank columns onto paper. Draw reasoning arrows without a screen.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy the source summaries and blank display fields.
**Skip.** Public sharing.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 157 papers. Compare [[s07-K158-R]], then try [[s07-F158]].
- **5 min. Recall.** Read the two source summaries and identify their different makers.
- **15 min. Create.** Your child completes [[s07-P158]] as a banner and separate notes.
  **Say.** "How does this detail support your claim?" Expect an explained connection, not just a title.
- **5 min. Share.** Be the partner. Keep one written question.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X158]] before its answer. Save everything for day 160.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated talk, not two sources or their links. Longer: identify a different supported emphasis.

### Words and supports
Review claim, reasoning, limit and sourcing. Allow enlarged lines, home-language rehearsal or exact dictation. A different supported claim is welcome.

### Tomorrow
Day 159 gives the unseen end-of-unit assessment with no repair. Keep day 158 papers for day 160: error H needs relevant evidence; error R needs explained details from both sources. Written cues reach every child then.
