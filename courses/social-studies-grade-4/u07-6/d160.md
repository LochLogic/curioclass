# Day 160: Responding and handing on evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-160 feature directions, evidence-sheet/repair-cards formats and reflection fields. Read opener, teacher-key and current answers. Assign one priority and fresh retry per child from day 159 and day 158 work. Keep Side 2 covered until Fresh retry. Set four groups of at most eight; prepare private written moves for all other gaps.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return day 159's assessment and day 158's s07-X158, s07-P158 and s07-F158 privately. Review grade 3's feedback, group, fresh-task and reflection routine. Children locate their priority with [[s07-P160]]. If many day 158 tickets show error H, preference, or error R, labels without links, assign evidence/responses first. This block identifies cues; repairs happen in groups. Review locator and question-board only as inquiry reminders.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Four sequential five-minute meetings: who built/gave what; causes/places; decisions; evidence/responses. At most eight per group. Each meeting holds two minutes comparing its example, then three for one assigned fresh [[s07-F160]] annotation. Others work in concurrent pairs from written cues, one minute each, then revise silently. If a gap exceeds eight, distribute that comparison to all and hear a rotating sample. No full oral round.

Who built/gave what: compare [[s07-K160-C]]; check error I or J through limited scope, never a claim that a nation disappeared. Causes/places: [[s07-K160-G]], error E, connections can reach elsewhere. Decisions: [[s07-K160-P]], error F/O/G/Q, use named responsibility and retain limits. Evidence/responses: [[s07-K160-A]], error H/R/S/T/U, locate evidence before asserting support. Review reasoning and scope. Each meeting allows at most two forty-five-second observations, ninety seconds within comparison/attempt time. Read every fresh paper later; do not practice held retry cases.

#### 10 min. Fresh retry
First uncover Side 2. Children independently answer only the assigned [[s07-R160-G]], [[s07-R160-P]], [[s07-R160-A]], [[s07-R160-C]] or [[s07-R160-H]]. Read neutrally; scribe exact words. MT is attributed to the Makah Tribe's public page. No answer copying. Other priorities retain a written move and remain not retried. A present-nation interpretation does not settle other missing group descriptions. Collect responses.

#### 5 min. Reflection and handoff
Use reflection's change, fresh detail and remaining-component fields, unscored. Give immediate private cues to a rotating six while others assemble draft, feedback, revision, assessment and retry evidence. After class, thirty responses times forty-five seconds require about twenty-three minutes. Return all remaining responses later on day 160 before handoff. Record retries on separate component records with their existing scales; never average away gaps. Keep practice unscored in folders. End-record/group-record carry every unresolved component to the next teacher. Record keeps exits separate. No mastery claim or extra day.

### Other rooms
Copy one assigned example and retry. Point to text without showing an answer or requiring a screen.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy the assigned feature and fresh card onto paper.
**Skip.** Public sharing and the other groups' tasks.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 159 and day 158 work. Choose one priority using [[s07-P160]].
- **20 min. Repair.** Compare the matching example on Side 1. Your child marks its feature, then the assigned row in [[s07-F160]]. Keep Side 2 hidden.
  **Say.** "Which feature helps your answer?" Expect the date, actor, connection or scope named in the cue.
- **10 min. Fresh retry.** Reveal only the assigned new card. Let your child answer independently.
- **5 min. Reflect.** Discuss the fresh response afterward. Save remaining needs with the portfolio.

### Shorter or longer
Trim public talk, not the fresh attempt. Longer: record another evidence need as open, without claiming another component passed.

### Words and supports
Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review limit: what evidence cannot show. Allow enlarged text, exact dictation or home-language rehearsal; keep the evidence demand.

### Tomorrow
This closes the course. Hand on private evidence and every unresolved component. One retry covers only its observed component; all other group and reasoning gaps remain visible.
