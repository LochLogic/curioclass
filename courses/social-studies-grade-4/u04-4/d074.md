# Day 74: Shared years, different places

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 73's exit and written tasks with one priority cue per child. Print teacher-key.md and the generated day key separately. Keep the complete exit-74.md and its grid covered. Set out unit 4 historical paired bars and unit 4 centimeter rulers. Verify one centimeter between historical year ticks. Use practice-74.md's source notes; today's page replaces its second question. Prepare two isolated repair scales of five centimeters per century.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
If day 73's exit showed error F, review equal years, not event spacing. Error T needs a proportional interval. Use [[s04-P74-3]] with a fresh scale. More than a third missing: whole-class repair; otherwise a group. Return every paper with one priority per child.

#### 5 min. Recall criteria
Review day 73's interval, simultaneous and single-bar construction. Review grade 3's shared-time idea: different places can share years. Children read the supplied historical bars; do not draw a pair or model the held ports. Criteria: follow one scale, preserve endpoints, read each location, and keep causal claims within the evidence.

#### 20 min. Timeline work
First five finish repair with [[s04-P74-4]]. Children then work alone on [[s04-P74-1]] using historical-bars.svg. Expect the span from 1541 to 1542; do not shade the following interval. Work independently on [[s04-P74-2]]: St. Lawrence. The bars describe Coronado's expedition in the Southwest and Cartier's third voyage in the St. Lawrence region. The voyage dates come from Sources CORONADO and CARTIER.

**Ask.** [[s04-P74-5]] Expect no connecting action established by these bars. Shared time cannot establish cause. Partners each explain this one evidence limit, 45 seconds each in fifteen parallel pairs, 90 seconds total. No paired activity-and-place comparison is rehearsed. Review day 62's source-log headings; add each maker. Keep taught summaries beside the map in the Voyage Accounts packet. Collect all papers and logs.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's board routine. Group E's six explain the bars' evidence limit, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Passing is allowed. Record names; everyone submits paper. Teacher returns to the unit question. Cover every example.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the complete exit-74.md and blank grid now. Say the ports and activities are made up. Read [[s04-X74-1]] and [[s04-X74-2]] exactly. Children construct and compare independently on paper. After class, thirty times seventy-five seconds, 37.5 minutes, covers all judged papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; unobserved stays blank. Return all work on day 75.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy supplied historical bars and the complete covered exit separately. Preserve centimeter spacing with a ruler. No ruler: use a verified paper centimeter measure. Never measure a projected image.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy historical bars and the complete held grid at actual size. No ruler: use a verified paper measure.
**Skip.** Public rotation and repeated partner turns.
- **5 min. Return.** Return day 73 papers. Use [[s04-P74-3]] and [[s04-P74-4]].
- **5 min. Recall.** Read the supplied historical bars and their place labels.
- **15 min. Work.** Use [[s04-P74-1]], [[s04-P74-2]] and [[s04-P74-5]].
  **Say.** "Do shared years alone establish cause?" Expect no connecting action established.
- **5 min. Board.** Your child explains that evidence limit. Keep taught summaries.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the complete made-up port record and grid. Use [[s04-X74-1]] and [[s04-X74-2]] alone. Consult the key afterward; return next lesson.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated partner explanations. Longer: describe additional evidence needed to establish a connection. Protect repair and exit.

### Words and supports
Review simultaneous, interval, century and source-sourcing. Read dates neutrally. Dictation records a child's chosen comparison; it never supplies dates or places.

### Tomorrow
Day 75 gathers evidence across voyages. Return all papers. Error G needs distinct place labels; error P needs causal evidence beyond shared time; error T needs endpoints matched to elapsed years. Share one priority across Return and repair and Evidence work's first five minutes.
