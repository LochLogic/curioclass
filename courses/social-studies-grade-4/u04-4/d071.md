# Day 71: A northern route

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 70's exit and written tasks with one priority cue per child. Print teacher-key.md separately; use the generated key for current items. Keep exit-71.md and its inset covered. Set out unit 4 Living Peoples packet. Copy outer-route labels onto separate paper tiles. Use practice-71.md for source references only. No ancestry inference, role play or personal-disclosure questions.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
If day 70's exit showed error O, revisit its present-community evidence. Error J needs an inside detail rather than a title. Review day 70's time-clue move using its returned evidence. More than a third missing: whole-class repair; otherwise meet a group. Return every paper; choose one priority per child.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Pairs inspect the outer-route labels. **Ask.** [[s04-P71-1]] Expect France, Newfoundland, Gulf. Then show that route on the accumulating map; add a separate enlargement for Newfoundland. Source CARTIER records this 1534 voyage and a later river journey. Explain that St. Lawrence Iroquoian names historical communities. Teach the name separately from any route question. Source CARTIER also records Cartier taking Donnacona and other captives to France in 1536. Captivity took away freedom. Do not model the held river inset or ask whose homeland a traced route entered.

#### 17 min. Route work
First five finish one repair with fresh [[s04-P71-4]] or [[s04-P71-5]]. Children then work alone on [[s04-P71-2]] and [[s04-P71-3]]. The council notice is made up. Expect no agreement evidence and the present. Review homeland and present lands from day 49: current lands need not cover a whole historical homeland. A present Wendat government account cannot assign every historical town to that nation.

Review day 62's source-log headings. Children copy Source CARTIER's maker and unknown publication date. Fifteen pairs rehearse their notice answers at the same time. In each pair, allow 45 seconds per partner, 90 seconds total. Collect responses and logs. Put only taught summaries into the two packets; map layers stay in folders.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's board routine. Group B's six explain their notice answer, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Public pass allowed. Teacher connects claims and captivity to the unit question. Record names; everyone submits paper. Cover teaching examples.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the complete exit-71.md now. Read [[s04-X71-1]] and [[s04-X71-2]] exactly. Children work independently on paper. After class, thirty times sixty seconds, 30 minutes, covers all judged papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; leave unobserved blank. Return all work on day 72.

### Other rooms

### At home

  **Say.** "Which label comes first?" Expect France.

  **Say.** "Does the notice show agreement?" Expect no agreement evidence.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated partner explanations. Longer: explain what agreement evidence the notice lacks. Protect repair and exit.

### Words and supports
Review homeland, present lands and source-sourcing. New: St. Lawrence Iroquoian, a historical community name. Read names neutrally; permit dictation without supplying content.

### Tomorrow
Day 72 traces Coronado's expedition. Return all papers. Missing route order needs separate stop labels. Error C needs evidence of existing communities; error N in practice needs assertion separated from agreement. Share one priority across Return and repair and Route work's first five minutes.
