# Day 72: An expedition and its effects

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 71's exit and written tasks with one priority cue per child. Print teacher-key.md separately and the generated key for current items. Keep the complete exit-72.md and locator covered. Set out unit 4 Living Peoples packet. Use practice-72.md's factual references only; today's page supplies tasks. Do not project the government homepage. Print its secular service statement only.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
If day 71's exit showed error C, review its evidence of existing residents. Error N from its practice needs assertion separated from agreement. For a misplaced route, revisit separate outer-route labels. More than a third missing: whole-class repair; otherwise a group. Return all papers with one priority each.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review day 65's voyage aims and day 68's aim-effect headings. An aim is an intended result; an effect is what happened. Source CORONADO records the 1540 departure from Compostela through Culiacán. Model only this early segment on the accumulating map. Explain entrada here as expedition. It continued north and returned in 1542.

Introduce A:shiwi, also called Zuni, using Source ASHIWI's own government account. People govern today. Name this community without rehearsing the held locator or its aim-effect contrast. Read the separate wealth-seeking statement; a search for riches does not establish that riches existed.

#### 17 min. Route work
First five finish one repair: fresh [[s04-P72-4]] for error C or [[s04-P72-5]] for error N. Then children work alone on [[s04-P72-1]], [[s04-P72-2]] and [[s04-P72-3]]. Expect departure order, aim, today. Point back to the matching sentence when a child substitutes an effect for an aim.

Fifteen pairs explain their aim classifications at the same time. In each pair, allow 45 seconds per partner, 90 seconds total. Review day 62's log headings; add maker and unknown publication date for Source CORONADO. Collect papers and logs. Retain taught departure and government summaries in the packets. No child locates Hawikuh or pairs its aim with displacement before the exit.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's routine. Group C's six explain the aim classification, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Passing is allowed. Teacher connects wealth-seeking with the unit question without supplying a held response. Record names; everyone submits paper. Cover examples.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Uncover the complete exit-72.md, its new locator and captions. Read [[s04-X72-1]] and [[s04-X72-2]] exactly. Children answer independently on paper. After class, thirty times sixty seconds, 30 minutes, covers all judged papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; unobserved stays blank. Return everything on day 73.

Shorter: omit repeated partner explanations. Longer: explain why seeking riches does not prove finding them. Protect repair and exit.

### Words and supports
