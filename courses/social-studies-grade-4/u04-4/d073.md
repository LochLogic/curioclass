# Day 73: Equal years, equal space

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 72's exit and written tasks with one priority cue per child. Print teacher-key.md and the generated day key separately. Keep exit-73.md covered. Print century-practice.svg at actual size; its full span must measure eighteen centimeters. Set out unit 4 practice century strips and unit 4 centimeter rulers. Use practice-73.md's source notes only; today's page replaces its event choices. Mark one activity start at 1100 on a spare practice strip.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
If day 72's exit showed error D, revisit its documented aim; error E needs its displacement caption. Revisit the matched evidence without rehearsing a paired contrast. Return all papers with one priority each. Otherwise retrieve grade 3 equal intervals. Give the printed interval reminder to everyone; do not add a second repair queue.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review grade 3's timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks. Review century, a hundred years. Introduce interval, time between marks. Review day 5's ruler measurement from zero. **Ask.** [[s04-P73-1]] Expect three centimeters. Then model a half-century offset with [[s04-P73-2]]: [[steps:s04-P73-2]]

Model [[s04-P73-6]]: split a century into four equal lengths; take three. Expect 2.25 centimeters. On the spare strip, model one duration bar with [[s04-P73-8]], extending from the supplied start by one quarter-century. Expect 0.75 centimeters. Align every year to the same scale. No full line with two internal markers or paired bars is modeled.

#### 17 min. Scale work
First five finish one fresh retry: [[s04-P73-9]] for error D, [[s04-P73-4]] for error E, or [[s04-P73-5]] for intervals. Children measure isolated practice offsets, then each places only the supplied Cartier event, [[s04-P73-3]], on a separate premarked strip. Expect 1.02 centimeters after 1500; accept within two millimeters. Assemble these single placements into the class chronology. Retain taught voyage summaries with the map; no held landing/conquest combination.

Introduce simultaneous as shared time, possibly different places. Read the made-up recital/race record. **Ask.** [[s04-P73-7]] Expect no causal evidence. Review grade 3's overlap idea without its earlier case. Pairs explain the missing connection, 45 seconds each, 90 seconds total. Collect all paper and source logs.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's routine. Group D's six explain one scale choice, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Public pass allowed. Record names; all submit paper. Cover examples.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-73.md. Read [[s04-X73-1]] exactly. Children construct independently on the half slip. After class, thirty times seventy-five seconds, 37.5 minutes, covers all judged papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; unobserved blank. Return everything on day 74.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated partner explanations. Longer: explain why approximate historical dates need approximate positions. Protect quarter-century work and exit.

### Words and supports
Review century, timeline and ruler measurement. New: interval; simultaneous. Use larger paper and larger labels while preserving physical distances. Dictation may label a child's chosen position, never choose it.

### Tomorrow
