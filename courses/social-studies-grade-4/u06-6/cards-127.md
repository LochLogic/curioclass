# Day 127 teaching cards

## Historical museum use

The Arts and Industries Building opened as a national museum in 1881.
Displays let people study objects and public history. This history does not establish current opening hours.

## Made-up town archive

A public record dates a town bridge's opening. People consult it to answer historical questions.
This town office and record are made up.

## District status

Washington, D.C. has local government. Residents vote for president.
D.C. has no senators. Its delegate lacks a vote on final House passage.
Committee work differs from that final vote. No statehood opinion is required.
