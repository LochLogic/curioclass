# Day 127: Public learning in the capital

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and held half slips. Use exit-127 as an adult assembly guide; its second item goes only in the Exit box. Keep cards-127, practice-127 and key-teacher adult-only. Use key-teacher for current answers and samples. Have opener, record, source-index, question board and all day 126 papers. Cover the caption and Exit box. Choose six volunteers or roster places for brief sharing.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 126 tickets, Exit boxes (s06-X126a) and s06-P126-1, s06-P126-2, s06-W126-1, s06-R126-1, s06-K126-1. If many day 126 tickets showed error I or retrieval gaps, review grade 3 day 27's nested-level model with [[s06-W127-1]]. Review grade 3 day 28's branch jobs within this same block. Name national/state/local separately; remove building arrows. Correct one priority per child; smaller gaps meet during work.

#### 10 min. Teach public resources
Review capital district from day 109: Washington, D.C. is the national capital district, not a state. Read cards-127's historical museum and district-status summaries separately. Source s06-museum is a public history page for readers, not today's visitor schedule. Review source maker, date, audience and purpose from day 122; use source-index. Review limiting claims to evidence from day 123.

Teach museum: objects preserved and displayed for learning. Archive: preserved records people can consult. The drill display and town bridge record are made up. **Ask.** [[s06-K127-1]] Hear ideas, then reveal [[steps:s06-K127-1]].

Teach monument: a structure for remembering a person or event; remembrance need not mean agreement. Give no monument practice case. Review delegate tasks from day 112. From Sources s06-dc and s06-house, state: D.C. has no senators; its delegate cannot vote on final House passage. The delegate can introduce bills and vote in committees. Residents have local government and presidential voting. Define final passage as the full House's decision on a bill. No policy debate or household disclosure.

#### 15 min. Compare by hand
Use practice-127 as an adult layout reference; the student page holds the shared display/record text. Alone, children complete [[s06-P127-1]] and [[s06-P127-2]]. Compare question-to-evidence links in pairs, without matching arrows supplied. During five minutes, meet the smaller repair group. Collect every annotated page; accept exact dictation.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the question board routine from grade 3 day 2. Use [[s06-Q127-1]] to answer what makes the capital different, through national work or public learning. Everyone writes. At most six share for 30 seconds each: three minutes, with two for handling. Passing publicly is allowed. Collect the notes.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover teaching. Reveal exit-127-stimulus only now. Alone, children answer [[s06-X127a]] on the half slip and [[s06-X127b]] in the Exit box. Read neutrally or scribe exact words. Collect both paper responses.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy shared text and held caption separately. No visitor, photograph or live page is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the shared text and questions, keeping the exit covered.
**Skip.** Class roster and public sharing.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 126 papers. Use [[s06-W127-1]] for one level gap.
- **10 min. Teach.** Read the short museum and district-status summaries from cards-127. Explain museum, archive and monument. Use [[s06-K127-1]].
  **Say.** "Can this record tell the bridge's length?" Expect: no, it dates the opening only.
- **15 min. Compare.** Your child completes [[s06-P127-1]] and [[s06-P127-2]] using the shared text. Compare source links together.
  **Say.** "Does the museum history give today's hours?" Expect: no.
- **5 min. Question board.** Write one supported note with [[s06-Q127-1]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the held caption. Your child answers [[s06-X127a]] and [[s06-X127b]] on paper alone. Keep all papers for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce volunteer sharing. Keep teaching, comparison and exit. Longer: name another question the town record leaves unanswered.

### Words and supports
Museum, archive, monument, delegate, final passage. Review government levels and source detail. Read neutrally; enlarge print. Judge ideas, not handwriting.

If many day 127 papers show error P, distinguish committee tasks from final House voting; for error W, connect a fresh record to a public question. Combine priorities within five minutes.
