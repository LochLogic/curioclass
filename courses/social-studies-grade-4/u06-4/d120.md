# Day 120: Maps and statehood check

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare the unseen response layout from M1-locations, M2-cards and A response masters. Keep mid-assessment as an adult checklist; use this day's printed timeline stems. Use generated keys alongside teacher-key. Keep mid-source-book adult-only. Mask D's category headings; retain its geographic marks. Prepare day 119 originals and written cues. Read no completed teaching response aloud. Keep source and response calibration lengths equal.

### The day
#### 1 min. Orient
Alone throughout. Review the written-cue revision routine from grade 3 day 119; explain only procedure. Sources remain available. Neutral reading and exact dictation preserve paper choices. No coaching or public turns.

#### 6 min. Locate
Children complete [[s06-M1a]] through [[s06-M1g]]. Use M-locator and M-ocean. Offer only the five requested state entries and seven island rows from the reference index. No completed response outline is shown. Reference sheets supply evidence, not scored student work.

#### 8 min. Dates and statehood
Allow four minutes for [[s06-M2a]] and [[s06-M2b]] using M2-cards and M2-line. Allow four for [[s06-M3a]] and [[s06-M3b]]. Do not supply an institution list or model paragraph. Read questions neutrally. Log requested content help separately.

#### 8 min. Northeast construction
Children complete [[s06-M4]] using A's three source layers. They construct the six-feature map with conventions on the fresh response. Teacher records M4u from that work, without another child task. Keep teaching maps covered.

#### 2 min. Dated-map reading
Children complete [[s06-M5c]] from D's separate geographic layers. Read only D's student context below, not the source book's classification sentences. Label the two modern outlines Arizona and New Mexico; label the arrow endpoint Bosque Redondo. These orient the stimulus without classifying it. Accept a private dictated answer; no historical enactment.

#### 10 min. Unaided revision
Return day 119 originals with cues. Children revise Florida, Iowa, Utah, Local and Northeast in that order, two minutes each. Use [[s06-M5b]], [[s06-M5m]], [[s06-M5w]], [[s06-M5l]] and [[s06-M5t]]. Preserve originals; add changes in another color. Sources remain visible. No answer is supplied. A child finding no needed change retains the complete record.

#### 5 min. Collect and record
Collect packet, originals, cues and revisions. Log missing components. Record M2-se, M2-mw, M2-sw and M2-we from M2a, without extra prompts. Detailed marking follows class; do not pretend all packets are marked here.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace supplied outlines and lines at matched sizes. Read teacher-held sources privately.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: use neutral dictation access without removing components. Longer: record unfinished components for the next response slot.

### Words and supports
Use familiar words without defining tested concepts. Read factual captions; do not identify correct categories. No personal experience is requested.

### Tomorrow

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for each component, with unobserved components blank. Day 121 returns all paper in its ten-minute Return and repair, one priority per child.
