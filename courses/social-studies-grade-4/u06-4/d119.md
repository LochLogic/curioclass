# Day 119: Fresh map records

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print the named masters. Keep key-teacher private; use current generated item keys for marking. Transfer response boundaries and calibration points into the four student map spaces at matching size. Prepare selected capital strips, source layers and Northeast cards. Cover the wall timeline and completed maps. Hold exit-119-stimulus, exit-119-line and exit-119 until their turn. Prepare day 118 returns.

### The day
#### 6 min. Return and retrieve

Return day 118's written tasks: s06-P118-1, s06-P118-2, s06-R118. Use the existing private cue and repair time.

Return day 118's exit, practice and reminder. Review Return and repair. If many showed error E, match a misplaced practice symbol to its key. Error L checks its source location; error R replaces a list with located marks. Give one priority during five minutes. Others independently complete the five Retrieval rows using separate reference entries. All take the final minute for [[s06-R119]], a made-up symbol retry. Collect paper; no public turns.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the held source and subdivided line now. Children independently complete [[s06-X119a]] and [[s06-X119b]] on one half slip. Read stems exactly; supply no placement hints. Collect before other work. No rehearsal corrects an event-box diagram today.

#### 18 min. Regional maps
Review day 114's map-scale check: source and response calibration lengths match. Independently complete [[s06-P119-1]], [[s06-P119-2]], then [[s06-P119-3]], six minutes each. Use separate B, C and E source layers, never a completed response. Read cards-119 once. Keep the documented soil sample separate from illustrative corn use and built processing. If many stall, note a criterion cue after collection; do not construct their record.

#### 7 min. Familiar-place map
Independently complete [[s06-P119-4]] from Local. Use the supplied Massachusetts public-place fallback, already encountered on day 113. Ask for no personal location. Preserve source positions and map conventions. No complete example is displayed.

#### 7 min. Northeast timeline
Review admission and ratification from days 111 and 112. Review equal-year spacing from day 111. Independently complete [[s06-P119-5]] with northeast-cards and northeast-line; use separate lanes at shared years. Collect all five originals. Covering the cooperative wall line preserves the independent reconstruction.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace source and response boundaries at the same size. Point to teacher-held source layers; read prose neutrally. No device is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: trace the supplied outlines and lines at matching sizes.
**Skip.** Class retrieval rotation; use two Retrieval rows.
- **4 min. Return and retrieve.** Return day 118 paper. Use [[s06-P119-R1]], [[s06-P119-R2]] and [[s06-R119]].
- **15 min. Regional maps.** Read source captions. Your child completes [[s06-P119-1]], [[s06-P119-2]] and [[s06-P119-3]].
- **7 min. Familiar-place map.** Use Local and [[s06-P119-4]].
- **7 min. Northeast timeline.** Cover completed work. Use [[s06-P119-5]]. Keep originals. Write one cue about a feature to inspect on each record, without giving its answer. Save revisions for tomorrow.
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the held line. Your child answers [[s06-X119a]] and [[s06-X119b]] alone. Put it away.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: shorten transitions, preserving every record and the exit. Longer: inspect source positions silently after collection.

### Words and supports
Review capital, physical feature, legend and natural resource. Review day 118's landmark, park and historic-site categories. Read stems neutrally. Exact dictation records the child's choices on paper. Enlarge sources and responses together.

### Tomorrow
Write one criterion cue per original: 150 records at thirty seconds, 75 minutes after class. Mark thirty exit slips at twenty seconds, ten more minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Day 120 returns five originals for unaided revision, with no repair. If many day 119 tickets show error C, reserve equal-year repair for day 121. Retrieval misses and all returned paper join day 121's ten-minute response.
