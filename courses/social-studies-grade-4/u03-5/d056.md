# Day 56: Connecting a claim and evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return every day 55 paper with one priority cue. Conceal s03 exit 56. Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key separate. Withdraw completed frames. Supply blank writing headings and corrected teaching summaries. Photograph and location record remain covered until the exit.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 55's written task and all practice sheets. If many day 55 tickets showed error H, classify [[s03-P56-R]] together. For error I, children mark the actual condition instead of the title. Otherwise meet the priority group; others annotate returned notes using written cues. Review evidence versus preference. Finish repairs in Writing work's first five minutes.

#### 5 min. Recall headings
Review grade 3's claim sheet and reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review limit: what evidence cannot show. Review day 46's uncertainty meaning. Reduce the frame to claim, evidence/reason, evidence/reason, limit. Children choose their own emphasis; give no completed paragraph.

#### 20 min. Writing work
First five minutes finish day 55 repairs. Independently use [[s03-P56]] with taught Archaeology Methods, Migration Evidence and Living Nations notes. Paragraph one states a method claim and connects a detail to it. Paragraph two adds another maker's relevant detail and a limit. Keep the emphasis on methods and limits, not a route/site/reader synthesis. Fifteen pairs rehearse for 45 seconds each child, 1.5 minutes in parallel; a child may pass publicly and write privately. Children finish independently. Collect every draft for descriptive feedback, not a second assessment result.

#### 5 min. Source audit
Review grade 3 day 150: a second source needs a different maker. A copied caption does not create another maker. Children compare their named makers with the retained source labels, then underline each actual detail and its reason. Review the question board's methods strand. For error I, require details rather than more titles. Withdraw all drafts before the exit.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the fresh photograph and separately authored location record: [[s03-X56]]. Children write independently. Use a visible raised form plus the location record; neither establishes private beliefs. After class allow 30 times 35 seconds, 17.5 minutes, plus fifteen for drafts. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; record access separately. Return everything on day 57.

### Other rooms
No screen: use individual prints. No printer: hand-copy the separate record, retaining access to the saved photograph. A drawing cannot replace photographic evidence; use the local image on the available display.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the headings and use the saved photograph beside its separate record.
**Skip.** Public sharing and roster.
- **5 min. Return.** Use [[s03-P56-R]] for the day 55 priority.
- **5 min. Recall.** Point to the blank writing headings.
- **20 min. Writing work.** Your child writes [[s03-P56]] from taught notes. Listen to a rehearsal, then let your child write independently.
  **Say.** "How does your detail support your claim?" Expect an actual connection, not a repeated title.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Hide the draft. Reveal exit-56.md and give [[s03-X56]] independently. Consult today's answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce rehearsal, keeping both paragraphs, reasons and exit. Longer: compare two defensible method emphases orally after collecting the exit.

### Words and supports
Review claim, reasoning, limit and uncertainty. Offer neutral reading and child-directed dictation. A blank access frame may remain: "This detail supports ____________________ because ______________________________."

### Tomorrow
Day 57 returns day 56 papers. Error E infers beliefs from form; error H overclaims; error I supplies titles alone. Use Return and repair plus Retrieval work's first five minutes for classification, without drafting or editing. More than ten misses: shared priority repair; otherwise a group.
