# Day 55: Choosing relevant evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Keep s03 exit 55 hidden. Return every day 54 paper, including the written task, with one priority cue. Withdraw completed examples. Lay out retained packets with their corrected author labels. Use only previously taught portions during practice. The catalog is made up. Keep teacher-key.md and today's data answer key separate from child print.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
If many day 54 tickets showed error F, use [[s03-P55-F]]; error G uses [[s03-P55-G]]. Error M in practice uses [[s03-P55-M]]. Alternatives share the slot. Review day 52's preservation and descendant meanings and day 43's context. Otherwise meet the priority group; others follow written cues on returned work. Finish in Evidence work's first five minutes.

#### 8 min. Evidence organization
Review grade 3's claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Review day 42's source log and question board. Supply blank maker/detail/strand headings. Children select their own taught source; no completed teacher entry is shown. Today's goal is choosing relevant evidence.

#### 17 min. Evidence work
First five minutes finish day 54 repairs. Independently use [[s03-P55]] repeatedly, one taught source at a time. Consult Archaeology Methods, the corrected Migration Evidence summary, and Living Nations labels. Keep notes separate rather than explaining a two-card combination. Check each maker against its actual label. Relevance means the detail helps a supporting question, not merely that its title sounds useful. For error I, point back to the detail field; for error H, to what the source actually reports. Collect every list for feedback.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the four supporting strands. Each child places one note under its strand. **Ask.** [[s03-P55]] Expect an accurately attributed detail in a relevant strand, such as recorded positions under methods. Hear six single 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, rotating unheard names; public pass allowed. Written notes reach everyone. Use this block for unfinished repairs if needed.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the separate master with both new source summaries: [[s03-X55a]] [[s03-X55b]]. Children answer independently on paper. The first detail supports knowledge of dated activity; the second documents cultural activity today, not unchanged ancient practices. After class allow 30 times 40 seconds, twenty minutes, plus ten for lists. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; access separately. Return all papers on day 56.

### Other rooms
No screen: use retained print. No printer: copy blank headings and the complete separate exit master. Preserve source labels; no real objects are handled.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the blank headings and separate exit summaries.
**Skip.** Public sharing and roster.
- **5 min. Return.** Choose your child's day 54 priority repair from the student page.
- **8 min. Organize.** Point to the maker, detail and strand headings.
- **17 min. Evidence work.** Use [[s03-P55]] with taught sources separately. Let your child choose details.
  **Say.** "Which question does that detail help?" Expect a relevant strand supported by the source's actual words.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-55.md. Give [[s03-X55a]] and [[s03-X55b]] independently. Consult the teacher answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public turns, retaining independent selection and both exit asks. Longer: organize another taught source separately.

### Words and supports
Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim, from grade 3 day 19. Review limit: what evidence cannot show, from grade 3 day 117. Read source labels neutrally; permit rehearsal and child-directed dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 56 writes and shares two linked paragraphs. Error H overclaims; error I gives titles without supporting details. Return every day 55 paper in Return and repair, finishing in Writing work's first five minutes. More than ten misses gets shared repair; otherwise meet a group.
