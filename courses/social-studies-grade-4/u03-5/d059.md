# Day 59: One next move

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read day 58's assessment and day 57 papers. Choose one priority per child, including missed reader-need components. Prepare s03 feedback privately. Keep teacher-key.md, today's data key and assigned retries separate from teaching examples. Label the catalog and notice as made up. Copy retained teaching-line labels and the Hopi author label into the designated spaces.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return every day 58 assessment paper privately with one next move; return day 57 exit tickets s03-X57a s03-X57b and recall sheets too. Review the response routine from grade 3 day 160: feedback, component repair, fresh attempt, folder reflection. Children mark the one assigned practice row, not every row. Errors are information, never public child examples.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Four sequential five-minute meetings: Chronology/routes for errors A/B/C/J/K, Evidence/methods for D/E/F/O/P, Continuity for G/L/M, Claim/source support for H/I/N. Day 57's content gaps join these groups. If many share a gap, keep the same meeting sequence with that larger group; others use their written moves.

Review only taught partial components. Chronology uses [[s03-P59-T]] on preplaced teaching labels. Methods uses [[s03-P59-M]] on complete catalog fields. Continuity uses [[s03-P59-C]] on a retained author label. Source support uses [[s03-P59-S]] on one recorded detail, not a two-source argument. Match the cue to the gap: larger years-ago means farther back; presence does not trace a journey; crossing and southward passage differ; land/coast segments can join; disturbance needs checking. Review region versus border, form versus belief, one find versus everyone, and independent checks. Review earlier Native life and living descendants separately. Read the row first, hear at most two 45-second turns per meeting, then give feedback. Unheard children show the marked field; written feedback reaches all. No retry card is visible during these meetings.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Reveal only the assigned card: [[s03-R1]], [[s03-R2]], [[s03-R3]] or [[s03-R4]]. For error N give [[s03-R4N]] instead. Every child answers independently on paper or directs a scribe. Five minutes holds one short component; the remaining five permit rereading and teacher checks of completed responses. Assess with shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for, recording access separately. Budget thirty times fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes, across these last five minutes and Reflection's first 2.5. Children who need longer retain unfinished status; do not infer success.

#### 5 min. Reflection
Turn back to Side 1 for reflection. Finish checks and return every fresh paper with one private written move during the first 2.5 minutes. Children use [[s03-P59-F]]; their reflection stays in the folder. Then pairs each take 45 seconds in parallel, 1.5 minutes; public pass allowed. **Ask.** [[s03-P59-F]] Expect a specific changed detail or remaining question. Unresolved components stay named, without a mastery claim or second oral queue.

### Other rooms
No screen: paper suffices. No printer: copy only the assigned retry card and its complete stimulus; copy teaching labels separately. Keep the retry hidden until independent work.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public reflection, protecting repair and independent response. Longer: revisit a written move after collecting the fresh response, without another judged task.

### Words and supports
Review context, source maker, reasoning and uncertainty. Use neutral reading and child-directed dictation. No child represents a nation or reports personal history.

### Tomorrow
Day 60 opens voyages and people already here, then supporting questions. Retain unresolved errors and written moves in the folder; no extra readiness task is promised. Every fresh response comes back today.
