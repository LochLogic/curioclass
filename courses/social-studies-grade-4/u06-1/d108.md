# Day 108: Reading a Northeast atlas

## Teacher page
### Before class
Teacher answers: key-teacher.md, kept separate. Print cards-108, atlas-northeast and reference-northeast-mainland. Print only S/C, state and capital columns; cover atlas boilerplate. Keep map titles and keys. Print student pages and half-slip exit-108; hold exit-108-stimulus and exit-108-river. Keep teacher-key and generated item keys private. Prepare day 107's returns, source-index and record.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 107's exit tickets and every written task, including retrieval, with one cue each. Review Return and repair: one priority, then a fresh retry. If many showed error X, use either [[s06-R108-consent]] or [[s06-R108-evidence]], not both. Everyone answers the assigned row on paper. Other gaps use the first five Locator work minutes. Neutral reading is available.

#### 9 min. Teaching first
Review state, a part of our country; capital, where government meets; region, states sharing something. Our grouping is one classroom choice. Review: political maps show borders; physical maps show land and water. An atlas is a collection of maps, review from day 18.
**Ask.** [[s06-K108-1]]
Show [[steps:s06-K108-1]] after children search. Review the map key: C and its dot identify a capital, not size. Point to Boston's coastal position without introducing the held river source.

#### 15 min. Locator work
Children use the page's nine separate strips for [[s06-P108-1]], finding each S and C mark before writing its missing capital. No blank outline is completed. Partners alternate pointing, working simultaneously. Then use the Massachusetts entry for [[s06-P108-2]]. Hear six single 45-second source observations during work; record names, allow passing. Collect paper from everyone, including children not heard. Other repairs occupy only the first five minutes.

#### 3 min. Source check
Partners trace one completed strip to its atlas row and C mark, 45 seconds each, simultaneously. Use the existing strip, with no new judged response.
Check: compare each missing capital with its atlas row. Underline a correction; teacher uses the original and correction separately.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-108-stimulus now, including the fresh outline and separate exit-108-river. Everyone answers [[s06-X108a]] and [[s06-X108b]] on paper. The index stays available; cover models. Allow neutral reading or exact dictation. Judge source-based position, not coordinate notation or pen precision. Mark all work afterward: 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Return on day 109 using shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component.

#### 2 min. Close
File locator notes after collection. Revisit day 107's question about where states are. Teacher keeps record and provenance.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace the supplied masters at matching sizes; copy only the short index columns. Keep held sources covered. No device is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: trace the supplied map and copy the short strips.
**Skip.** Public sampling and class collection.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 107 work. Choose one repair row.
- **8 min. Teaching first.** Use [[s06-K108-1]], then read its steps.
  **Say.** "Which code identifies the capital?" Expect: C3, Boston.
- **14 min. Locator work.** Complete [[s06-P108-1]] and [[s06-P108-2]]. Point from each row to its map marks together.
- **3 min. Source check.** Compare capitals with the atlas rows. Cover the demonstration.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-108-stimulus and river strip. Your child answers [[s06-X108a]] and [[s06-X108b]] alone, with the index available.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public sampling, retaining every locator and exit. Longer: verify a strip through both its state and capital marks.

### Words and supports
Review index: a list linking place names to map locations. Enlarge maps and use neutral descriptions or a scribe without locating answers. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow
Day 109 returns day 108's strips and exit, then locates Southeast and Midwest states. Error K uses the capital key; error L uses boundaries and source position. One opening priority; other needs use Locator work.

### Credit
Ours. Geographic sources and s06-hartford are recorded with URLs and checked status in source-index.
