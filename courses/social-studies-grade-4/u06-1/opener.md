# Section 6.1 opener

## Before class

Print each day's cards, practice sheet and held exit stimulus. Print the half-slip ticket separately. Print teacher-key.md for adults only. Use ../u06-1/record.md and ../u06-1/source-index.md. Source and response maps must print at the same size. A no-printer room copies or traces the supplied master, never guesses geography.

## Ideas

The inquiry needs evidence rather than a preferred picture. Separate a state, classroom region, capital and territory. Teach atlas lookup on individual entries. An ocean inset enlarges a place; page position does not locate it. All fifty states and capitals appear in the reference strips, with the anchor grouping.

## Words and continuity

big question: a question too large to answer at once; supporting question: a smaller question that helps answer the big one; region: a group of states that sit together and share something; our classroom five-region key is one way to group them; consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made; territory: land governed within a larger country.

These terms and routines were looked up in the subject ledger. Give independent reminders whenever an earlier grade 4 record is only planned. No lower-grade check case is reused here. Consent was taught on grade 3 day 125; the day 127 citation in the prose plan is a later review. Source-sourcing includes audience from day 126. The ledger marks equal-year spacing on day 91; do not presume a different planned timeline citation has been taught.

## Timing, turns and feedback

Each day has 40 minutes. Follow the named plan parts. Repair one priority in Return and repair; finish other needs within the first five work minutes. Never stack opening repairs. Six single 45-second teacher asks need 4.5 minutes in the work block. Partners talk simultaneously. Passing publicly is allowed. Everyone submits paper; name who was heard on the record. All others receive their written feedback on the next named day.

Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Leave unobserved components blank. Access support is not automatically content help. Every judged item has a named feedback route. Do not average away a missing map theme. Day 120 marking takes about two minutes per child after class; day 131 about three, untested.

## Paper and protection

The pupil page has at most 350 words and 15 words per sentence. Read source cards neutrally, at most 70 words per card. Use 14-point print, enlarged or tactile maps and a neutral scribe. Paper marks and dictated paper answers stay paper, never spoken items to save space. No tool is required. Cover completed practice before checking. Keep every held source arrangement out of teaching.

## Two rooms and care

One adult and one child use the same public records; the adult takes a partner turn. No timer or score is needed at home. Printed attributed summaries replace a missing photograph, page or visitor. No live page is required. State made-up stories and ordinary explanatory actors are made up. Native nations and their histories are real; no invented Native person or sacred material appears. No removal enactment or graphic account. Private discussion can meet the same historical goal. Never request home, family, money, religion, body or immigration reports. Students age 18 or older receive the family note and make their own leave-out or consent choice; school rules still apply.
