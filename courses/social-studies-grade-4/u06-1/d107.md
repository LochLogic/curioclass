# Day 107: Questions that need evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Teacher answers: key-teacher.md, kept separate. Read opener and source-index. Send family home, including the adult-student choice. Print cards-107, hook-107, student page and practice-107 as extra writing space. Hold exit-107-stimulus and exit-107 until Exit ticket. Keep teacher-key and the item-generated practice key separate. Set up record and five board headings.

### The day
#### 5 min. Retrieval
Day 106 promised our land-and-work question; retain its unresolved assessment record separately. Review grade 3 consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made. Children answer [[s06-R107a]] and [[s06-R107b]] alone. Read neutrally. The Declaration's writers complained about taxes without consent; this does not establish equal voting. Collect both responses for day 108.

#### 8 min. Hook
Show cards-107 and hook-107, our original diagrams. The farm is made up. Source A summarizes the park service's undated history for public readers; our diagrams explain it for learners. No photograph is claimed. Review sourcing: maker, date, audience and purpose.
**Ask.** [[s06-K107-hook]]
Then reveal [[steps:s06-K107-hook]]. Land offers clues; a picture alone cannot explain every work choice.

#### 7 min. Teaching first
Post: "Why do people in each region make and do what they do?" Review big question, too large to answer at once; supporting question, a smaller question helping answer it.
**Ask.** [[s06-K107-map]]
Then show [[steps:s06-K107-map]]. Think aloud: a useful question identifies missing evidence. Explain its use to another map reader without composing a worker-choice response.

#### 12 min. Question work
Fifteen pairs sort the five supplied questions under the page's headings. Everyone writes [[s06-P107-1]], [[s06-P107-2]] and [[s06-P107-3]]. Partners edit one question together; each child records evidence independently. First five minutes permit one retrieval repair. Hear six single observations, 45 seconds each, during work. Record speakers; anyone may pass. No worker-choice story appears here.

#### 3 min. Question board
Review the question-board routine. Post the five questions with answer days: location 108 to 110; statehood 111 to 113; maps 114 to 118; work 121 to 125; capital 126 to 127. Collect every page and annotation. Teacher keeps board and provenance.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the made-up worker record only now. Everyone answers [[s06-X107a]] and [[s06-X107b]] independently on exit-107, a half slip. A neutral reader or scribe preserves the paper task. Mark after class: 30 children times 30 seconds for all today's work, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; return all paper on day 108.

### Other rooms
Without printing, copy the short cards and diagrams; hold the exit record separately. No device is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the diagrams, page and covered exit record.
**Skip.** Class reports and posting.
- **5 min. Retrieval.** Use [[s06-R107a]] and [[s06-R107b]]. Review consent together.
- **8 min. Hook.** Read cards-107 and trace hook-107.
  **Say.** "Which diagram shows water powering machines?" Expect: Paterson's water-channel diagram.
- **7 min. Teaching first.** Use [[s06-K107-map]]. Read the explanation afterward.
- **10 min. Question work.** Use [[s06-P107-1]], [[s06-P107-2]] and [[s06-P107-3]]. Be the partner.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-107-stimulus. Your child answers [[s06-X107a]] and [[s06-X107b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public observations, keeping written questions and exit. Longer: test whether the chosen evidence answers the revised question.

### Words and supports
Review region: states sitting together and sharing something. Our five-region grouping is one classroom choice. Read cards aloud; allow home-language rehearsal and neutral dictation. No personal reports.

### Tomorrow
Day 108 locates Northeast states and capitals. Return day 107's retrieval, practice and exit. If many show error X, repair evidence relevance or consent, one priority; other needs use the first five work minutes.

### Credit
Ours. Factual summaries: s06-paterson and s06-consent; URLs and checked status in source-index.
