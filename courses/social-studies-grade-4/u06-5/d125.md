# Day 125: Follow work and a choice

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print student pages and half slips for s06-X125b. Prepare exit-125-stimulus; use exit-125 as an adult assembly guide. Its exchange table prints in the covered Exit box only. Keep cards-125 and practice-125 adult-only, omitting table practice. Mark with current generated keys; keep key-teacher as an adult reference. Have record, source-index, day 124 papers and thirty counters per child, or thirty pencil marks.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review: return day 124 tickets and practice s06-P124-1, s06-P124-2 and s06-P124-3 to every child. If many day 124 tickets showed error H or error V, use a made-up tailor/food-stall reminder: the tailor sells sewing work and purchases food. Distinguish main work from all needs within one repair. Children revise one priority; no extra oral queue.

#### 5 min. Recall the case
Review day 124's worker specialization and trade. Return Inez/mill arrow records; completed models stay covered. Children reconstruct [[s06-P125-1]] on separate arrows. Point to learned skills and equipment as reasons. Apply yesterday's ideas without modeling a complete two-actor table.

#### 15 min. Apply
Say the pay allocation is invented, never a recommendation. Review saving from grade 3 day 50: keeping money for later means it is still there. Use cards-125's allocation and [[s06-P125-2]]: eighteen needs, seven wants, five saving total thirty. Children build three counter groups, trace sales/purchases on their arrow record and check all counters. Review needs and wants as labels in this supplied story; never ask about families. Meet the repair group while others work.

#### 10 min. Explain a choice
Review opportunity cost from grade 3 day 57: the next-best thing given up. Apply [[s06-P125-3]] by moving two counters from wants to saving. Annotate before/after groups, what is unavailable now and what remains available later. Represent the changed allocation without rehearsing the exit's purchase question. Fifteen pairs each take two one-minute turns simultaneously. During remaining work hear six 45-second responses. Record names, allow public passing and collect all annotations.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-125-stimulus only now, with teaching covered. Alone, children complete [[s06-X125a]] in the student page's Exit box and [[s06-X125b]] on the half slip. The tall table belongs on the page; only the short choice item goes on the slip. Neutral reading and exact dictation preserve paper responses.

### Other rooms
No counters: make thirty pencil marks. No printer: copy arrows and covered exit table separately.

### At home
**Swap.** No counters: use thirty pencil marks. No printer: copy frames and questions.
**Skip.** Class repair group and roster.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's papers with one cue.
- **5 min. Recall.** Rebuild [[s06-P125-1]] without completed models.
- **15 min. Apply.** Set out eighteen needs, seven wants and five saving marks. Use [[s06-P125-2]].
  **Say.** "Is every mark accounted for?" Expect: yes, thirty in all.
- **10 min. Choice.** Use [[s06-P125-3]]. Move two wants marks to saving; annotate before and after.
  **Say.** "What remains available later?" Expect: saved counters, including the two moved.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the held record and Exit box. Your child answers [[s06-X125a]] and [[s06-X125b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce partner repeats, retaining actors, annotation and exit. Longer: trace payment opposite every purchase.

### Words and supports
Review specialize, trade, opportunity cost and saving. Enlarge arrows; use neutral dictation. Keep choices fictional.

### Tomorrow
Mark thirty sets at 40 seconds each, twenty minutes after class. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 126 returns all paper in Return and repair. If many day 125 Exit boxes show error V, reconnect sales with unmet needs. Individually remind children missing the next-best choice that saving remains available. Then study the national capital's three branch buildings.
