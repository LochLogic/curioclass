# Day 122: Questions about Northeast work

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print cards-122 with practice-122-soil, student pages and held exit-122-stimulus with exit-122-map. Keep exit-122 private. Use practice-122 as an adult checklist. Mark with current generated keys; keep teacher-key as an adult reference. Have record, source-index and day 121's marked papers. Printed summaries replace photographs or webpages.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review: return all day 121 tickets and practice. If many day 121 tickets showed error U or error G, draw a made-up hillside ditch draining a path. Contrast a feature list with a use-link and a claim that land dictates everyone's work. Children revise one priority; small groups finish during independent work.

#### 5 min. Question and sources
Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose, from grade 4 day 19. These park histories serve public visitors; publication dates are unspecified in the register. They describe historical work. Paterson is review of day 107. Review Salem wharves from grade 3 day 115; today uses different details. Read P and H on cards-122. Children try [[s06-P122-1]] before discussion. The places differ; H cannot corroborate one Paterson event.

#### 9 min. Worked explanation
Review natural resource and industry. Model P alone: natural river water, built channels, machinery, clustered factories, then street/building growth. This helps explain city growth without proving everyone's moving reason. Model H separately: built wharves extend waterfront access for goods handling. Review trade: goods move from where made to where sold. Children try [[s06-P122-2]], then reveal [[steps:s06-P122-2]]. Keep source notes separate.

#### 16 min. Research by hand
Alone, children underline the detail answering their question and fill the source/action/location/movement notes. Keep P and H in separate rows rather than combining a map and independent account. Write unknown when evidence is absent. Read the invented soil model: it demonstrates a possibility, not real soil geography. Children complete [[s06-P122-3]]. Meet the priority repair group during the first five minutes. Collect all paper; no individual oral queue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover models. Reveal the two made-up records only now. Alone, children complete [[s06-X122a]] and [[s06-X122b]] on a half slip. Neutral reading or exact dictation preserves the paper path.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace supplied masters and copy stems. Show teaching sources only on any shared screen.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: trace diagrams and copy questions.
**Skip.** Class repair group.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's papers with one cue.
- **5 min. Question.** Read P and H. Your child tries [[s06-P122-1]].
  **Say.** "What could this account help you find out?" Expect: a question about water and work.
- **9 min. Explain.** Follow P's water, channels and factories. Separately follow H's wharves and goods. Use [[s06-P122-2]].
- **16 min. Research.** Underline details. Keep each account's notes separate. Use the soil diagram and [[s06-P122-3]].
  **Say.** "Does this show real farming zones?" Expect: no, it is invented.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal held records. Your child answers [[s06-X122a]] and [[s06-X122b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce note copying, preserving evidence and exit. Longer: identify a question neither account answers.

### Words and supports
Review adapt, industry, natural resource and trade. Enlarge sources; accept neutral dictation.
