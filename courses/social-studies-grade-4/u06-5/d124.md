# Day 124: Specialization and purchases

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print student pages and held exit-124-stimulus; keep exit-124 private. Keep cards-124 and practice-124 teacher-held: read only Inez/mill paragraphs, never the held-pair instruction. Use student arrow frames, not the practice master's table. Mark with current generated keys; keep key-teacher as an adult reference. Have record, source-index and day 123 papers.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review: return day 123 tickets and practice s06-P123-1 and s06-P123-2 to every child. If many day 123 tickets showed error G or error U, draw a made-up quarry with a delivery road. Connect stone to delivery without claiming everyone cuts stone. Children revise one priority; remaining needs meet the teacher during independent work.

#### 5 min. Retrieve
Review goods and services from grade 3 day 50: a thing you can hold, work done for you. Children answer [[s06-P124-1]]. Review opportunity cost from grade 3 day 57: the next-best thing given up, not the price or every choice. Choosing one task gives up time for another. Request no personal money information.

#### 9 min. Teach two actors
Review specialize: a business that makes mostly one kind of thing. Extend to a worker concentrating on learned skills. Review trade: goods move from where made to where sold; work can be sold too. Say Inez and the mill are made up. Read their paragraphs from cards-124. Children try [[s06-P124-2]] before modeling: work leaves Inez, pay returns; food enters, payment leaves. Skills support her main work; purchases meet other needs. Treat the mill separately with [[s06-P124-3]] and its goods/purchase arrows. Equipment supports the mill's main work.

#### 16 min. Practice links
Alone, children finish separate arrow records. Add a reason beside each actor using supplied skills/equipment. This flow explanation differs from a completed table for a new pair. During parallel work hear six children for 45 seconds each using existing prompts; record names. Public passing is allowed. Collect everyone's paper, including from children not heard. If specialists supposedly supply everything, point back to purchases without supplying a complete answer.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover teaching records. Reveal the made-up exit-124-stimulus now. Alone, children complete [[s06-X124]] on a half slip with its blank table. Accept plausible purchases, including food for workers. Read neutrally; no answer is displayed.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy source paragraphs, arrow frames and exact stems. Keep held records separate.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy arrow frames and questions.
**Skip.** Class roster and public turns.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's paper with one cue.
- **5 min. Retrieve.** Use [[s06-P124-1]].
  **Say.** "Which is a thing, and which is work?" Expect: boots are goods; a haircut is a service.
- **9 min. Explain.** Read only Inez's and the mill's made-up paragraphs. Use [[s06-P124-2]] and [[s06-P124-3]].
- **16 min. Practice.** Draw separate work, goods and payment arrows. Your child adds reasons for main work.
  **Say.** "Why buy something else?" Expect: specializing does not meet every need.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the held record. Your child answers [[s06-X124]] alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce sharing, retaining both actors and exit. Longer: follow opposite payments for every goods arrow.

### Words and supports
Review goods, services, specialize, trade and opportunity cost. Neutral dictation and enlarged arrow frames preserve the task.

### Tomorrow
Mark thirty sets at 35 seconds each, seventeen and a half minutes after class. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 125 returns all papers. If many day 124 tickets show error H, include workers in trade; error V needs the unmet-needs link. Combine both in one tailor/food-stall reminder before following Inez's pay choices.

### Credit
Ours. All actors and exchanges are made up.
