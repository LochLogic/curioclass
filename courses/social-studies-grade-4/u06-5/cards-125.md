# Day 125 teaching cards

Inez and the mill are made up. Return their blank exchange arrows.

The worker receives thirty counters. One possible allocation is eighteen needs, seven wants, five saving.
These counters are invented. They are not a household recommendation.

New choice: move two wants counters to saving. The next-best current option is a game.
Saving keeps resources for later. It does not destroy pay.

Never ask children to report personal spending or family finances.
