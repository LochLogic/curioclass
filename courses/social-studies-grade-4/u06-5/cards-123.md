# Day 123 teaching cards

## Southeast: Savannah, nineteenth century

Savannah shipped cotton. Enslaved people were forced to produce much of that cotton.
They were people denied freedom, never goods or willing trading partners.

## Midwest: Minneapolis, nineteenth century

Millers used St. Anthony Falls for grain processing. Milling helped the city grow.

## Southwest: central and southern Arizona

Built delivery systems carry Colorado River water for farms and communities.
This does not establish why every community began there.

## West: Fort Vancouver, nineteenth century

A water-powered sawmill supplied carpenters. Extra lumber went to other places, including Hawaii.
These examples do not describe every resident's work.
