# Day 121: Resources and human choices

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare day 120's assessment and day 119's papers with one cue each. Print cards-121, student pages and held exit-121-stimulus with exit-121-map. Keep exit-121 covered. Use practice-121 as an adult checklist. Mark with current generated keys; keep teacher-key as an adult reference. Keep record and source-index private. Printed summaries replace webpages.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Review written-cue revision. Return day 120's assessment and day 119 papers to everyone. Honor yesterday's timeline promise: spacing gaps use equal-year marks on the original line. Spend five minutes on the common priority, then five on individual cue-led revision. Others repair their assigned component without copying a complete answer. Keep originals; route unfinished cues to day 123.

#### 5 min. Retrieve and hook
Review grade 3's change the land and adapt. Use Paterson's channels as a reminder of day 107, never a checked case. Children try [[s06-P121-1]] before discussion. Explain both using available water and changing its course. Record retrieval gaps for day 122.

#### 8 min. Teach a connection
Review natural resource: something from nature that people use. Review industry: all the work that gets or makes one kind of thing. Read cards-121's Paterson account. Separate natural water from built channels on the board. Usable power helps explain factories clustering and buildings growing there. Resources offer possibilities; skills, equipment and choices also matter. The account does not show every resident's job.

#### 12 min. Pairs and alone
Say the orchard is made up. Use practice-121-orchard and [[s06-P121-2]]. Fifteen simultaneous pairs each take two one-minute turns, four minutes including handling. Each child identifies the built feature on paper. During eight independent minutes, hear six children for 45 seconds each using the same prompt. Record names; public passing is allowed. Collect everyone's paper. A natural-feature answer gets a built-feature cue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Alone: reveal exit-121-stimulus and map; issue [[s06-X121]] on a half slip. Read neutrally; a scribe records the child's words. Allow the whole slot for writing and collection. Cover teaching charts.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace supplied diagrams and copy stems exactly. Use the board for teaching only.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: trace supplied diagrams and copy questions.
**Skip.** Class roster and group repairs.
- **10 min. Return and repair.** Return yesterday's work with one cue. Your child revises it.
- **5 min. Retrieve.** Read Paterson. Use [[s06-P121-1]].
  **Say.** "Does this use water, change the land, or both?" Expect: both.
- **8 min. Connect.** Point to natural water and built channels. Explain factory locations.
- **12 min. Practice.** Use the made-up orchard diagram and [[s06-P121-2]]. Take the partner turn.
  **Say.** "What did people build?" Expect: the crossing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the held map. Your child answers [[s06-X121]] alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce partner repeats, retaining written tasks and exit. Longer: distinguish a source detail from an inference.

### Words and supports
Review adapt, change the land, natural resource and industry. Enlarge diagrams; accept neutral dictation.

### Tomorrow
Mark thirty sets at 30 seconds each, fifteen minutes after class. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen; leave unobserved components blank. Day 122 returns every paper in Return and repair. If many day 121 tickets list features only, repair error U; deterministic explanations need error G repair. Use one fresh drainage example for both, then investigate Northeast work.
