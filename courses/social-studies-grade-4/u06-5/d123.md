# Day 123: Different resource connections

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print cards-123, student pages and held exit-123-stimulus with exit-123-diagrams. Keep exit-123 covered. Use practice-123 as an adult checklist. Mark with current generated keys; keep key-teacher as an adult reference. Have record, source-index, day 122 papers and unfinished day 120 cues. Use selected printed summaries, never whole source pages.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Review: return day 122 tickets and practice s06-P122-1, s06-P122-2 and s06-P122-3 to every child. If many day 122 tickets showed error J, error T or error X, draw a made-up reservoir with a built wall. A title identifies a record; details supply evidence. Separate natural water from built wall. For error X, revise the question toward available details. Children revise one priority in this single slot.

#### 8 min. Teach contrasts
Review industry and natural resource. Read cards-123 one account at a time. Nineteenth century means the 1800s; Arizona describes a modern delivery system. Savannah's cotton shipping depended on slavery: enslaved people were forced, never willing trading partners. Minneapolis connects waterpower and milling with city growth. Arizona's delivered water supports farms and existing communities, without explaining their founding. Fort Vancouver connects a sawmill with local building and distant lumber use. These original summaries use public government histories for visitors and project information for the public.

#### 17 min. Compare and repair
Children complete [[s06-P123-1]] with blank resource/action/settlement/movement headings, reviewed from day 122. Unknown is honest when evidence is missing. Use ten minutes for two sequential five-minute day 120 repair groups, at most four 45-second responses per group plus revision. Others work independently. Last seven minutes: complete [[s06-P123-2]]. Compare uses among today's accounts only; leave the Northeast comparison for the exit. Collect all paper.

#### 5 min. Explain limits
Review region from grade 4 day 3: a group of states that sit together and share something. Extend the reminder: places within one region differ. Review qualified claims: name what evidence supports and leaves unknown. A town's record cannot establish everyone's job. Revisit the unit question through account notes without showing a completed comparison paragraph.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the made-up C/D records and diagrams now. Children answer [[s06-X123]] alone on a half slip. Paterson means our earlier Northeast waterpower case; supply no completed link. Neutral reading is available.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy selected summaries, trace diagrams and copy the exact stem. No photograph or visitor is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy summaries and trace diagrams.
**Skip.** The two class repair groups.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's papers with one cue.
- **8 min. Read.** Read the four accounts. Explain that enslaved people were forced.
- **17 min. Record.** Your child completes [[s06-P123-1]] and [[s06-P123-2]]. Write unknown where evidence is missing.
  **Say.** "Does this tell us every resident's job?" Expect: no.
- **5 min. Limits.** Compare today's four accounts, keeping differences visible.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal C/D and their diagrams. Your child answers [[s06-X123]] alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce sharing, retaining all accounts, repairs and exit. Longer: identify missing evidence that could settle an unknown.

### Words and supports
Review region, industry and natural resource. Allow private historical discussion, enlarged print and neutral dictation.

### Tomorrow
Mark thirty sets at 40 seconds each, twenty minutes after class. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Day 124 returns all paper. If many day 123 tickets show error G, repair the everyone claim; a resource list needs error U repair. Use one fresh quarry-delivery sketch, then investigate specialization and trade.
