# Day 148: Southwest and West hazard records

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-148 as Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, locator, question-board, relationship-strips and record. Read opener; keep teacher-key and exit-masters separate. Use generated current item keys; these take precedence over teacher-key. Return day 147 papers with private location cues. Keep repair-cards' Evidence line ready. Cover the Side 2 Exit box until its own turn.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 147's papers: `s07-X147`, `s07-P147`, `s07-F147`. Review Return and repair. If many show error M, ignoring duration, or error S, naming only a hazard, compare [[s07-K148-R]] for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others act on cues. Everyone tries fresh [[s07-F148]] for five minutes before its key. Return source-scope gaps privately.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review Southwest and West locator labels and day 147's drought. Read Side 2's four made-up records aloud; these are not actual regional events. Source WF defines wildfire here as an unplanned vegetation fire. Explain that adults follow official clearance, not appearances. Water limits change human use, not weather. **Ask.** [[s07-K148]] Then show [[steps:s07-K148]]. Keep effects and actions separate rather than composing a complete exit explanation.

#### 14 min. Evidence investigation
Review sourcing: who made it, when and why. Curio Class made these records for learners in 2026. Alone for six minutes, children attempt [[s07-P148]] in evidence-sheet columns. Supply the unsupported claim "The watering rule creates rainfall" for marking. Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate supporting words. In six further minutes, revise separate marks. For error T, point to use/weather labels; for error P, point to official clearance. No rescue, reenactment or actual site visit occurs. Retain blank relationship-strips.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Collect every annotation and retry. Review question-board and region labels on locator. Hear at most four 45-second location observations, three minutes total; record names. Everyone may pass public talk. Keep papers for private cues.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X148a]] and the covered Exit box [[s07-X148b]]. Give three minutes alone, with neutral reading or exact dictation; collect all papers in the last minute. After class, allow 30 children times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for components and private cues. Review record's sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 149.

### Other rooms
Copy current records and tasks on plain paper. Keep the exit hidden. Use separate labels without a projector. No visitor, live event or personal story is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy current records and tasks without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** The class board and sampled public observations.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 147 papers. Compare [[s07-K148-R]], then attempt [[s07-F148]] before its key.
- **8 min. Read.** Read Side 2 together. Try [[s07-K148]], then reveal its steps.
- **14 min. Investigate.** Your child tries [[s07-P148]]. Be the location partner after independent marks.
  **Say.** "Does a watering rule change weather?" Expect: no, it changes water use.
- **4 min. Record.** Check one label; keep papers.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X148a]] and the covered Exit box [[s07-X148b]] independently before reading the key. Keep responses for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated sharing, preserving every record, the fresh retry and independent exit. Longer: refine one source-limit annotation.

### Words and supports
Review drought, hazard, response and sourcing. Clearance is official permission here. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. Use quiet paper study; never request personal disaster accounts.

### Tomorrow
Day 149 studies Johnstown dam failure and warnings. Return day 148 work. If many show error T, rules create rain, or error P, appearances establish safe access, use one ten-minute comparison/retry block; include priority error S or error O privately.

### Credit
Ours. Event records are made up; Source WF supports the wildfire gloss.
