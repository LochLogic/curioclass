# Day 150: Residence evidence and receiving places

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-150 as Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, locator, question-board, relationship-strips and record. Read opener; keep teacher-key and exit-masters separate. Use generated current item keys; these take precedence over teacher-key. Return day 149 papers with private location cues. Keep repair-cards' Evidence line ready. Keep the half-slip hidden until its turn.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 149's papers: `s07-X149`, `s07-P149`, `s07-F149`. Review Return and repair. If many show error C, excluding built-system causes, compare [[s07-K150-R]] for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six while others follow private cues. Everyone tries fresh [[s07-F150]] for five minutes before its key. Classify components only, without a missing-cause paragraph.

#### 5 min. Retrieval and orientation
Review day 149's displacement and technological labels, grade 3 day 93's cause-effect relationship, and sourcing: who made it, when and why. Review then/now evidence headings from grade 3 day 147. Retrieve locator and blank relationship-strips; introduce no model. Say: "Today's people, events and places are made up." Recall the printed map key: an arrow shows recorded movement.

#### 17 min. Settlement investigation
Alone for seven minutes, children attempt [[s07-P150]] in evidence-sheet columns. Mark residence words before inspecting each single after-event map from static-inventories. Label sending evidence and receiving evidence separately, using testimony for each. Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate words. In eight further minutes, revise annotations and mark unstated permanence. Wind damage and fire closure affect different records; no combined account is rehearsed. Offer private evidence-location cues. Keep relationship-strips uncompleted. These invented maps do not describe Johnstown.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Collect every annotation and retry. Review question-board; add the current evidence label. Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate a residence word; use the remaining two minutes to submit papers and record private-cue needs. Everyone may pass public talk.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X150]]. Give three minutes alone, with neutral reading or exact dictation; collect all papers in the last minute. After class, allow 30 children times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for components and private cues. Review record's sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 151.

### Other rooms
Copy current records and tasks on plain paper. Keep the exit hidden. Use separate labels without a projector. No visitor, live event or personal story is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy current records and tasks without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** The class board and sampled public observations.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 149 papers. Compare [[s07-K150-R]], then attempt [[s07-F150]] before its key.
- **5 min. Recall.** Read Side 2 together. Recall taught terms; show no new model.
- **17 min. Investigate.** Your child tries [[s07-P150]]. Be the location partner after independent marks.
  **Say.** "Which words show a changed residence?" Expect: now sleep in Bay Room, or moved to that guesthouse.
- **4 min. Record.** Check one label; keep papers.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X150]] independently before reading the key. Keep responses for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated sharing, preserving every record, the fresh retry and independent exit. Longer: refine one source-limit annotation.

### Words and supports
Review displacement, technological, sourcing and cause-effect. A receiving place is where people move in these records. Read neutrally, enlarge maps or scribe exactly. Never request family movements or disaster accounts.

### Tomorrow
Day 151 studies distant effects on people and deliveries. Return day 150 work. If many show error N, no relocation, or error E, origin only, protect one ten-minute Return and repair: five-minute residence/receiving-label comparison, then five-minute fresh retry. Related gaps share this block; written cues reach everyone.

### Credit
Ours. All event records are made up.
