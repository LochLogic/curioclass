# Day 146: Southeast hazards and different responses

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-146 as Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, locator, question-board, relationship-strips and record. Read opener; keep teacher-key and exit-masters separate. Use generated current item keys; these take precedence over teacher-key. Return day 145 papers with private location cues. Keep repair-cards' Evidence line ready. Keep the half-slip hidden until its turn.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Day 145 promised Southeast hazards. Return day 145's papers: `s07-X145`, `s07-P145`, `s07-F145`. Review Return and repair: one comparison and a fresh retry. If many show error D, land alone, spend five minutes comparing [[s07-K146-R]] before its steps. Otherwise meet up to six while others follow private cues. Everyone tries fresh [[s07-F146]] for five minutes before its key. Keep local-source gaps in private cues.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review region, an area that shares something, and the locator labels. Review hurricane and flood from grade 2 day 33. Introduce hazard, something that can cause harm, and response, action addressing harm or need. Say: "These Southeast records are made up, not regional history." Read Side 2. **Ask.** [[s07-K146]] Then show [[steps:s07-K146]]. Locate an actor without composing an effect-and-both-responses account.

#### 14 min. Evidence investigation
Review sourcing: who made it, when and why, from grade 3 day 95. Curio Class made these records in 2026 for learners. Review grade 3 day 93's cause-effect relationship; retain relationship-strips uncompleted. Alone for six minutes, children attempt [[s07-P146]] in evidence-sheet columns. Fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate actor words. For six further minutes, revise separate annotations. For error O, distinguish town crews from volunteers; for error S, locate an effect beyond the hazard name. Give private location cues, not complete explanations.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Review the question-board routine. Collect every sheet and retry. Hear at most four 45-second source-location observations, three minutes total; record speakers. Everyone may pass public talk. Retain locator and blank relationship-strips in Regional Voices.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X146]]. Give three minutes alone, with neutral reading or exact dictation; collect all papers in the last minute. After class, allow 30 children times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for components and private cues. Review record's sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 147.

### Other rooms
Copy current records and tasks on plain paper. Keep the exit hidden. Use separate labels without a projector. No visitor, live event or personal story is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy current records and tasks without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** The class board and sampled public observations.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 145 papers. Compare [[s07-K146-R]], then attempt [[s07-F146]] before its key.
- **8 min. Read.** Read Side 2 together. Try [[s07-K146]], then reveal its steps.
- **14 min. Investigate.** Your child tries [[s07-P146]]. Be the location partner after independent marks.
  **Say.** "Which words name officials?" Expect: town crews.
- **4 min. Record.** Check one label; keep papers.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X146]] independently before reading the key. Keep responses for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated sharing, preserving every record, the fresh retry and independent exit. Longer: refine one source-limit annotation.

### Words and supports
Hazard and response use the meanings above. Review sourcing and cause-effect. Read neutrally, enlarge print or scribe exactly. Do not request personal disaster accounts.

### Tomorrow
Day 147 studies Midwest tornadoes, floods and drought. Return day 146 work. If many show error S, hazard name only, or error O, one actor does everything, compare separate effect/actor labels and give a fresh retry in its ten-minute Return and repair.

### Credit
Ours. All event records are made up.
