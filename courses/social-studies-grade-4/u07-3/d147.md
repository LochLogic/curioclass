# Day 147: Midwest hazards and duration evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-147 as Side 2, evidence-sheet columns, locator, question-board, relationship-strips and record. Read opener; keep teacher-key and exit-masters separate. Use generated current item keys; these take precedence over teacher-key. Return day 146 papers with private location cues. Keep repair-cards' Evidence line ready. Keep the half-slip hidden until its turn.

### The day
#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 146's papers: `s07-X146`, `s07-P146`, `s07-F146`. Review Return and repair. If many show error S, hazard name without effect, or error O, one actor does everything, compare [[s07-K147-R]] and separate actor labels for five minutes. Otherwise meet up to six. Everyone tries fresh [[s07-F147]] for five minutes before its key; other gaps receive private cues.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review Midwest on locator, and day 146's hazard/response meanings. Review JO, the Joplin shelter account from day 137, only as a reminder. Introduce drought: less precipitation than usual over an extended time, from Source DR. Precipitation includes rain and snow. Read the invented duration strips; these are selected examples, not rules for every event. **Ask.** [[s07-K147]] Then show [[steps:s07-K147]]. Keep weather mechanisms in science.

#### 14 min. Evidence investigation
Review sourcing: who made it, when and why. Alone for six minutes, children attempt [[s07-P147]] using separate desk records. Copy one effect word group per record in evidence-sheet columns; never write a whole response chain. In two minutes, fifteen concurrent pairs take one minute per partner to locate duration labels. Use six remaining minutes to annotate missing evidence and revise. For error M, return to printed durations. Staff actions do not establish every resident's action. Review then/now evidence headings from grade 3 day 147. Retain blank relationship-strips, not completed accounts.

#### 4 min. Record and board
Collect every annotation and retry. Review question-board: add the current evidence label. Retain duration headings in Regional Voices. Record paper observations; no thirty-child spoken round. Everyone may pass public talk.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
First reveal [[s07-X147]]. Give three minutes alone, with neutral reading or exact dictation; collect all papers in the last minute. After class, allow 30 children times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for components and private cues. Review record's sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work on day 148.

### Other rooms
Copy current records and tasks on plain paper. Keep the exit hidden. Use separate labels without a projector. No visitor, live event or personal story is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy current records and tasks without a printer; point instead of projecting.
**Skip.** The class board and sampled public observations.
- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 146 papers. Compare [[s07-K147-R]], then attempt [[s07-F147]] before its key.
- **8 min. Read.** Read Side 2 together. Try [[s07-K147]], then reveal its steps.
- **14 min. Investigate.** Your child tries [[s07-P147]]. Be the location partner after independent marks.
  **Say.** "Which strip lasts longest?" Expect: the several-month rainfall shortage.
- **4 min. Record.** Check one label; keep papers.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s07-X147]] independently before reading the key. Keep responses for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer
Trim repeated sharing, preserving every record, the fresh retry and independent exit. Longer: refine one source-limit annotation.

### Words and supports
Drought uses the meaning above; duration means how long. Review hazard, response and sourcing. Read neutrally; accept home-language rehearsal or exact dictation. Do not request personal disaster accounts.

### Tomorrow
Day 148 studies Southwest hurricanes/drought and West drought/wildfire. Return day 147 work. If many show error M, ignoring duration, or error S, hazard name alone, compare fresh duration/effect labels and retry within one ten-minute Return and repair.

Ours. Source JO supports the Joplin reminder; Source DR supports the drought definition.
