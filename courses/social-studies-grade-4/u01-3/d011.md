# Day 11: Place terms and living nations

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 10's exit and board correction with one cue. Separate Side 2, including Card N. Prepare the generated teacher-only answer key. Use the optional section opener when available; the printed government card is the fallback for a live page or visitor.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 10's s01-X10 and s01-P10-4. Review state and country. If many showed error E on day 10's exit, use [[s01-P11-R]], then trace the outer country boundary. Otherwise children retry independently from their written cues.

#### 10 min. Terms and government
First three minutes: review day 7's claim, evidence and reasoning. Use [[s01-P11-1]] with both practice maps. Retain the full paragraph frame below. These places and maps are made up.

Next seven minutes: review city, state and country, and kindergarten's nesting model. Introduce county as a division within this example state. **Ask.** [[s01-K11-1]] Wait, then show [[steps:s01-K11-1]]. Separately review Cherokee Nation's present government using Source 11C. Review nation: a people with their own government. Explain: a Native nation can govern itself within the United States, without being an independent country. Keep nation out of the nesting diagram.

#### 15 min. Supported work
Children independently repair [[s01-P11-2]], then annotate the separate government card with [[s01-P11-3]]. Pairs compare after attempts; show the teacher answer key afterward. Teacher holds five minutes for at most four children: for error E, use the arrow key; for error F, find the present-government detail; for error L, distinguish nation from independent country. Use one priority. Others follow their cues.

#### 5 min. Compare corrections
Each pair takes two 45-second turns on [[s01-P11-4]]. Public passing is allowed. Collect every diagram and government annotation. Review the question board and attach an accurate correction.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the Tucson inset and [[s01-X11]] on the half slip. Children label and explain independently. Reveal Card N and [[s01-P11-E]] in Side 2's Exit box, with its own writing space. Collect both. Mark after class: 30 children times 60 seconds, 30 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Return all collected work day 12 with one cue.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy practice maps and a separate exit inset on individual paper. Copy source cards faithfully; read them aloud without supplying the judgment. Paper cards replace a live site or visitor.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: draw the diagrams separately and copy both source cards.
**Skip.** The public board and class collection.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 10's cue; try [[s01-P11-R]].
- **10 min. Examine.** Use [[s01-P11-1]] and [[s01-K11-1]]. **Say.** "What does the arrow mean?" Expect: is inside.
- **15 min. Correct.** Your child attempts [[s01-P11-2]] and [[s01-P11-3]]. Read Source 11C together. Take both partner turns for [[s01-P11-4]]. Discuss afterward.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Side 2 and [[s01-X11]]. Your child answers independently, then completes its Exit box, [[s01-P11-E]]. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit posting the correction; keep independent work and both exit records. Longer: explain why a nation's government does not belong on a universal place ladder.

### Words and supports
Review: continent, a very large land area; city, a large town; country, people, land with borders, a government and laws. Review state, province, nation, evidence and reasoning. County: a division within this example state. Read labels aloud; accept labelled arrows or dictated explanations without giving relationships.

### Tomorrow
Day 12 returns day 11's exit, Exit box and annotations in Return and repair. For error E, repair containment; for error F, find present government; for error L, distinguish nation and country. Use one dominant repair; a second group meets during Supported work. Globe-grid retrieval has its own part before latitude and longitude.

### Credit
Ours. Sources 11C and 11N are nation-authored government paraphrases. Sources 11P and 11G support the exit inset. Source 11B supports the nation/country distinction.
