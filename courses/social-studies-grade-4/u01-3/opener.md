# Section 1.3 opener

## The ideas

A province or territory belongs within Canada. State, county and city relationships depend on the example; there is no universal nesting ladder. A Native nation has its own government and need not be an independent country. Nation is never inserted into the invented city/county/state chain.

## Before class

Print the named shared pieces: canada-trails.md; mexico-caribbean-trails.md; containment-government.md. Keep every exit and assessment packet hidden until its named part. Prepare the matching key files for teacher use only. Gather pencils, folders and individual paper; add rulers for distance, blank strips for belts, and a globe or labeled ball for zero-line retrieval. Never measure a projected image.

## Common errors and response

D: all extents retain detail; E: divisions and countries are confused or containment is reversed; F: present Native government is denied; H: preference replaces evidence; L: nation means only independent country; N: a map is complete.

Many means more than 10 of 30. Repair one priority gap in Return and repair, five minutes. A second gap receives one five-minute group during Supported work. Others follow written cues and submit a fresh response. Never stack repairs in one slot or name a child as an error.

## Evidence and time

All days are 40 minutes. Every child submits individual paper evidence. Public talk is optional. Two simultaneous 45-second pair turns use 90 seconds. A six-child hearing uses 4.5 minutes within five. The record roster names the rotation. Every collected item receives a written cue on its handbacks.csv day.

Save strip construction, heading repair and missing-evidence completion for their independent checks. No exercise uses personal addresses, identities, money, religion or family histories. Name made-up people and places aloud as made up. Printed credited source cards replace unavailable visitors, local pages and photos.
