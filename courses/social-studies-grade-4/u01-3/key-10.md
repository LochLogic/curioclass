# Teacher-only day 10 answers

Teacher use only. Keep this page away from student sheets.

Before class, read each answer beside its printed stimulus. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## s01-P10-R

A province is a division within Canada.

Look for: Corrects the province/country relation without repeating the day 9 exit case.

## s01-K10-1

The trail reaches Jalisco in main-map cell B3.

Look for: Read the name and panel.; Follow column B, row 3.; Trace the division boundary within Mexico.

## s01-K10-2

Cuba is a Caribbean country, not a state in Mexico.

Look for: Read the Cuba reference and its separate outline.; Replace Mexican state with Caribbean country.

## s01-P10-1

Circle C3; replace it with B3.

Look for: Uses the main map and index; corrects the supplied route rather than performing a three-place search.

## s01-P10-2

Look for: Uses the boundary to reverse the false containment relation.

## s01-P10-3

Look for: Uses region and country accurately; accepts the grouping explanation without naming additional places.

## s01-P10-4

Look for: Marks the corrected region statement, rather than writing it a second time. Collect and return day 11.
