# Day 84: Railways change access

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare cards-084.md, the student page and practice-084.md master. Keep teacher-key.md, the generated key and exit-084-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-084.md and exit-084-stimulus.md separately. Bring day 83 papers, record.md and question-board.md. Copy only the teaching notes from the student page onto the board.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 83's exits, practice and question-board papers. If many day 83 tickets showed error C, model vehicle/function sorting; assign [[s05-F84-1]]. Error L instead receives [[s05-F84-2]]. Review homeland: the land a nation's people have known as home for a very long time. Choose one priority per child. More than a third means a class move; otherwise meet a small group. Others follow private written cues.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Read cards-084.md's factual sentences aloud, leaving its model for work time. Source s05-RAIL describes shorter travel and western settlement; Sources s05-IRISH and s05-CHINESE name railway builders. Chinese and Irish people helped build railways. Existing peoples and homelands were there before these tracks.
Define railway as a route with tracks for trains. Review freight: goods carried from place to place. Review passenger: a person carried on a ship; extend it to train travel. Use only the separate cloth-delivery note. **Ask.** [[s05-K84-1]] Hear two ideas, then show [[steps:s05-K84-1]]. Review trade: goods move from where made to where sold. Faster access supports trade and settlement; it does not buy a country's territory.

#### 17 min. Railway work
First five minutes finish the priority repair, covering its model before the fresh response. Others read their journey notes. During the remaining twelve minutes, read the invented journey durations from cards-084.md. These numbers explain a model, not a measured historical trip. Children individually complete [[s05-P84-1]] and [[s05-P84-2]]. Symbols identify a load or traveler here; no movement arrows appear. Fifteen parallel pairs each take 45 seconds per child to compare responses, 90 seconds total. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Each child completes [[s05-P84-3]].

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the board's travel question. Six rotating volunteers each read their P84-3 response, at most 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes total. Passing is allowed. Record names and collect every child's paper for feedback on day 85.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover teaching examples. Reveal the new diagram and half slip together. Read labels neutrally. Children independently answer [[s05-X84-1]]. Exact dictation is available. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty exits and fifteen for other papers. Record each effect separately: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Neutral reading is access. Return everything on day 85.

  **Say.** "How can arriving sooner help a seller?" Expect: cloth can reach buyers sooner.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce pair repetition. Keep repair, access reasoning and exit. Longer: rehearse the P84-3 explanation with a partner.

### Words and supports
Railway; review freight, passenger, homeland and reasoning. Read labels aloud; allow home-language rehearsal and exact scribing.
