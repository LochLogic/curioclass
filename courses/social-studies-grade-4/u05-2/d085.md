# Day 85: Purposes and daily work

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages, cards-085.md and practice-085.md masters. Keep teacher-key.md for exits only; use the current generated key for practice. Keep exit-085-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-085.md with its captions. Bring day 84 papers, record.md and question-board.md. Tell children the repair cases and evidence strip are made up. The student page is the single child copy of the teaching summaries.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 84's written tasks, exits and board papers with private cues. If many day 84 tickets showed error C, model a vehicle carrying goods, then assign [[s05-F85-1]]. For error L, review homeland and assign [[s05-F85-2]] instead. More than a third means a class move; otherwise small group. One priority continues in Motive work. Others read the teaching summaries.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review purpose: what people wanted. Apply that meaning to each moving decision. A person's moving purpose needs evidence about that person. Read the Spalding purpose sentence only. Missionary work means trying to spread one's religion; children discuss the historical purpose without religious activity. **Ask.** [[s05-P85-1]] Take ideas before showing [[steps:s05-P85-1]].
Read the Taylor summary separately. Reporting means gathering and sharing news. Children answer [[s05-P85-2]]. Explain the comparison method without filling a comparison: name each supported aim, then state a similarity or difference. One woman's or man's aim cannot stand for everyone of that gender. These records establish purposes, not Taylor's route.

#### 17 min. Motive work
First five minutes finish the priority repair and fresh response, covering the repair model. For the remaining twelve minutes, read Source s05-TASKS' summary. Define wagon train: wagons traveling together. The Oregon and California trail tasks included fuel collection, ox-team driving and caring for injured people. Women did these tasks too. Children independently match the separate evidence strip in [[s05-P85-3]]. Read the after-arrival Spalding detail, then use [[s05-P85-4]]. A settlement is a place where people establish homes. This activity happened after arrival, among Nez Perce people. No mission-building detail is used.
Fifteen parallel pairs each take 45 seconds per child to point to a task detail, 90 seconds total. Children complete [[s05-P85-5]] privately. Do not request family or religious disclosures.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the board's motives question. Six rotating volunteers read P85-5 responses, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Passing is allowed. Record names; collect all papers for day 86 feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover teaching summaries. Reveal the new captions and half slip. Children independently answer [[s05-X85-1]] and [[s05-X85-2]]. Read neutrally; an exact scribe preserves their words. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets and fifteen for other papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component; access alone is not help. Return all on day 86.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the summaries and assigned tasks; copy the exit separately. No visitor or live page is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the page and keep the exit captions covered.
**Skip.** Public turns and repeated pair talk.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 84 work. Use one assigned F85 task.
- **8 min. Teaching first.** Read each purpose summary separately. Use [[s05-P85-1]] and [[s05-P85-2]].
  **Say.** "What did Spalding want to do?" Expect: missionary work.
- **17 min. Motive work.** Finish repair. Read the trail summary. Use [[s05-P85-3]] and [[s05-P85-4]]. Discuss [[s05-P85-5]]. Explain wagon train and settlement.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover teaching cards. Reveal the new captions. Your child answers [[s05-X85-1]] and [[s05-X85-2]] alone. Discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce pair repetition. Keep purposes, trail tasks, settled activity and exit. Longer: rehearse P85-5 with a partner before writing.

### Words and supports
Purpose, missionary, reporting, wagon train, settlement. Read summaries aloud; permit home-language rehearsal and exact dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 86 studies causes and unequal effects. Return day 85 papers. Error D needs individual evidence rather than a gender rule; a missing trail detail needs a separate task reminder. Use Return and repair and Cause work's first five minutes.
