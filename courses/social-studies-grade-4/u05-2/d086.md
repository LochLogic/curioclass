# Day 86: Causes and unequal effects

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare student pages and cards-086.md and practice-086.md masters. Keep teacher-key.md, generated keys and exit-086-sources.md adult-only. Seal exit-086.md with its captions. Bring day 85 papers, branches.md, record.md and question-board.md. Copy blank branch headings; no completed chain remains during the exit. Tell children the repair people, town arrow and market notices are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 85's written tasks, exits and board papers. If many day 85 tickets showed error D, remind children to use individual evidence, then assign [[s05-F86-1]]. For a missing trail detail, briefly recall day 85's fuel, ox-team and care tasks instead. More than a third means a class move; otherwise small group. Choose one priority, continuing into Cause work. Others read the trade summary.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review cause-effect chain: Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___. Review unit 4's intended-gain and documented-harm branches; supply fresh blank headings. A cause helps explain why something happens. An effect is a change that follows. Order alone does not explain a cause.
Read the trade summary from Source s05-TRIBAL, the Confederated Tribes of the Umatilla Indian Reservation's public history. Review exchange: movements between regions, with unequal effects possible. This historical account belongs to the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, living peoples recalled from day 80. Use only its trade detail. **Ask.** [[s05-K86-1]] Hear ideas before showing [[steps:s05-K86-1]]. Exchanges provided a way to get useful items. Test one link rather than completing a movement-and-harm chain.

#### 17 min. Cause work
First five minutes finish the priority repair and fresh response; cover the model. Others annotate the trade detail. In the remaining twelve minutes, children independently complete [[s05-P86-1]]. Review timeline order: earlier before later. A later action cannot cause an earlier action. Use the separate town sequence in [[s05-P86-3]] to reject order without mechanism, error H.
Read the nonreserved early-arrivals caption and use [[s05-P86-2]]. Then use the made-up market notices in [[s05-P86-4]] to match distinct effects to groups, error R. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Fifteen parallel pairs each take 45 seconds per child to point to one supporting detail, 90 seconds total. Record effects separately under blank group headings. No historical role play or personal disclosures.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the board's effects question. Six rotating volunteers share one P86-4 match, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Passing is allowed. Record names; collect all papers for day 87 feedback. Cover completed branch notes.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the new Trail Card only now. Children independently answer [[s05-X86-1]] on paper. Read neutrally; permit exact dictation. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets and fifteen for other papers. Record mover reason and resident effect separately: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Neutral reading is access. Hand back all papers on day 87.
