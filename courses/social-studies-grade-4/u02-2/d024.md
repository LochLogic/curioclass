# Day 24: Offices and their work

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md, teacher-only key-exits.md and the generated key. The student page incorporates government-sources.md, government-table.md, practice-24.md and blank source-log.md fields. Use topic-notes.md to retain individual findings. Prepare question-board.md, record.md and all day 23 papers. Print no duplicate source/table sheets. Keep exit-24.md hidden until the exit.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return s02-X23, s02-P23, s02-P23-R and s02-P23-1, including the dated finding. If many showed error M, peoples only in the past, use [[s02-P24-R]]. If error I predominates, revisit each child's dated finding and its counted group instead. Others annotate existing notes. Choose one priority and continue it in Research work; do not replay the exit identification.

#### 8 min. Read office records
Read government-sources.md one country at a time. Review prime minister as government leader from grade 2, without reusing its national case. Review the president's country-level office from grade 3. Define Parliament and courts through their stated work. Explain confidence as support to continue governing. Say constitutional monarchy names a constitutional system with a monarch; parliamentary government depends on the elected House's support. Use the records, not current leaders or selection procedures. Do not supply a completed table or comparative sentences.

#### 17 min. Research work
First five minutes extend day 23's priority; others begin [[s02-P24]]. Work in teams of three, each with individual paper. Members find evidence together; everyone records every country. Fill one office and function at a time, then source labels and form. Review source log: maker, date, audience, purpose, detail and use. Undated means no publication date is supplied, not today's year. Circulate to check functions against sources. Collect each child's table and repair for day 25.

#### 5 min. Check functions
Partners each take 45 seconds pointing from one existing cell to its source, 90 seconds total. Check functions rather than names alone. Public passing is allowed. Keep comparative similarity/difference sentences for the independent exit. Retain each child's government notes; add one supported office finding to the question board.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-24.md, exactly [[s02-X24]]. Every child answers independently on paper; neutral reading and dictation are available. Collect all. Mark afterward: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; 30 times 90 seconds, 45 minutes, for tables and repairs. Record each component as shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Keep access separate from content help.

### Other rooms
Copy the source records and blank table onto paper. If displaying one copy, read each record again on request. Keep the separate exit covered.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: fold paper for the table and copy source records.
**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.
- **5 min. Return.** Read feedback. Use [[s02-P24-R]], or correct the dated finding's counted group.
- **8 min. Records.** Read the country records separately. Point to each office's stated work.
- **17 min. Research.** Your child completes [[s02-P24]]. **Say.** "Which source supports this cell?" Expect the country's named government page and its matching office detail. Keep comparison sentences for the exit.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X24]]. Your child answers alone; discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public reporting, keeping all countries and functions. Longer: verify another existing table cell against its named source.

### Words and supports
Parliament makes laws in the Canadian record. Courts interpret laws and resolve disputes. Executive means carrying out laws. Provide neutral reading, dictation and enlarged cells. Judge government knowledge separately from grammar.

### Tomorrow
Day 25 researches cities and land. Return s02-X24 and all day 24 practice. Error D, misnaming an office or mismatching its function, enters Return and repair and the first five Research work minutes, using fresh evidence.
