# Government office cards

## Canada
Form: parliamentary democracy within a constitutional monarchy.
Head of government: prime minister; the government needs the elected House's confidence.
Lawmakers: Parliament makes laws.
Courts: interpret laws and resolve disputes.

Original factual summary: [democratic institutions](https://www.canada.ca/en/democratic-institutions/services/democracy-canada.html) and [court system](https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/csj-sjc/ccs-ajc/01.html).

## Mexico
Form: federal presidential republic.
Head of government: president leads the executive branch.
Lawmakers: Congress makes laws.
Courts: interpret laws and administer justice.

Original factual summary: [political system](https://www.ine.mx/sobre-el-ine/sistema-politico-electoral/).

## United States
Form: federal presidential republic.
Head of government: president leads the executive branch.
Lawmakers: Congress makes laws.
Courts: interpret and apply laws.

Original factual summary: [government branches](https://www.usa.gov/branches-of-government).

Confidence means support to continue governing. Executive means carrying out laws.
Read each office separately. No current leaders or judge-selection details are needed.
