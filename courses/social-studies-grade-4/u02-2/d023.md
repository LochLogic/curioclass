# Day 23: People and language notes

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md; use the generated key for added items. Prepare people-sources.md for reading aloud, excluding teacher metadata. The student page incorporates practice-23.md, source-log.md and topic-notes.md fields; print no duplicate sheets. Retain question-board.md and record.md. Withdraw filled teaching logs. Keep exit-23.md hidden until the exit. Bring day 22's marked papers.

### The day
#### 5 min. Warm-up, Return and repair
Return s02-X22 and all day 22 practice: s02-P22, s02-P22-R, s02-P22-1, s02-P22-2, s02-P22-3, s02-P22-4 and s02-P22-5. Yesterday checked sources and their limits. If many showed error D, unsupported conclusions, or error N, titles without details, choose one priority through [[s02-P23-R]]. Otherwise meet one group while others write. This classroom inventory is made up. Extend the same priority during Research work.

#### 10 min. Shared source introduction
Read the shared introduction in people-sources.md. First Nations, Inuit and Métis are distinct living peoples; the Haida Nation is specifically named. Review Inuit from day 3 without repeating its case. Name the organizations behind each account. Explain the binational organization speaks about its own work, not everyone in Mexico. Read the Afro-Mexican recognition line. Keep the reserved language association hidden. Review population using grade 3's meaning: how many people live in a place, from a dated count or estimate. Read both dated language findings; they count specified groups, not total populations.

#### 15 min. Research work
First five minutes extend day 22's repair; others begin independently. Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose, and day 22's blank note frame. Children retain their chosen country and complete [[s02-P23]] and [[s02-P23-1]]. Supply the named maker orally for the shared introduction. Each child records evidence, not a copied class report. Use separate people and language rows; never rehearse paired identification plus a universal-speaker limit. Collect both sides for day 24.

#### 5 min. Notes and scope
Partners each have 45 seconds to point to a dated finding's counted group, 90 seconds total. They check their existing notes, adding no new response. No household language survey. Public passing is allowed. Add one supported research answer to the question board. Do not display a complete report.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exit-23.md, exactly [[s02-X23]]. Everyone answers independently on paper. Read neutrally or take dictation without content cues. Collect all. Mark afterward: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; 30 times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for the practice bundle. Use record.md's shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; record access separately.

### Other rooms
Read the source summaries aloud and copy blank headings onto paper. Hand-copy the separate exit only when needed; keep its wording unchanged.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy the blank note headings and read the source summaries aloud.
**Skip.** Public sharing and class grouping.
- **5 min. Return.** Read yesterday's feedback. Complete [[s02-P23-R]].
- **10 min. Sources.** Read the shared introduction and the two dated findings. Keep the exit hidden.
- **15 min. Research.** Your child chooses their country's finding and completes [[s02-P23]] and [[s02-P23-1]]. **Say.** "Which group was counted?" Expect the card's named group, not everybody in the country. Keep the year beside the finding.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X23]]. Your child answers alone; discuss after collecting it.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit public contributions, preserving repair and individual notes. Longer: check a note's maker and counted group against the source again.

### Words and supports
Review population and source log. A language is a way people communicate. Use each people's printed name. Enlarge text, read neutrally or take dictation; never infer a child's identity.

### Tomorrow
Day 24 researches government offices in a table. Return s02-X23 and every day 23 practice paper. Error I, everyone inferred from one example, or error M, past-only peoples, enters Return and repair and the first five Research work minutes.
