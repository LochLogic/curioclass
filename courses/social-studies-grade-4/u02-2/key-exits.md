## s02-X23

The Gabriel Dumont Institute develops Michif resources for Métis language work. This establishes current language work, not every Métis person's speech.

Look for: Bounded source-based inference; no universal speech claim.

Samples, shown then needing work: It documents Michif language work by a Métis institute, not everyone's speech. | Every Métis person speaks Michif.

## s02-P23

A supported present-day people detail with its maker: for example, the Council of the Haida Nation describes protecting Haida culture today. Accept a relevant detail from the binational Indigenous organization, naming that maker and its own stated scope.

Look for: Accurate detail; named maker; people described in the present. Do not require a universal-language conclusion.

Samples, shown then needing work: The Haida Nation's council describes its work today. | All Indigenous peoples are one people.

## s02-P24

Canada: parliamentary democracy within a constitutional monarchy; prime minister leads government; Parliament makes laws; courts interpret laws and resolve disputes. Mexico and United States: federal presidential republics; presidents lead executive branches; Congress makes laws; courts interpret/apply laws or administer justice. Source for each country. No comparison sentences required.

Look for: Each country has its correct offices, functions and source; record each component separately.

Samples, shown then needing work: Canada: Parliament makes laws. | The prime minister is a president.

## s02-P25

Canada: Ottawa in Ontario, Canada; Rideau Canal, a built waterway and historic landmark; wood, a natural resource. Mexico: Mexico City, Mexico; Chapultepec hill, natural land; castle museum, historic landmark; water, a natural resource. Cite the selected locator and relevant source cards. A canal is not land or water made by nature.

Look for: City, feature, resource and landmark separately source-linked; distinguish a built canal from a natural hill. Do not infer a river junction or city climate.

Samples, shown then needing work: Ottawa; canal waterway; cite canal page. | This map proves warmth.

Samples, shown then needing work: Water used for agriculture supports growing crops. | Everyone works in one industry.
