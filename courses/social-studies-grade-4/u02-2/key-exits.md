# Section 2.2 teacher keys

Teacher only. Read before class; keep out of student packets.
One scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Access support is not content help.

## s02-X23

Métis Nation; Michif; language work is not a claim about every member's speech.

Look for: People/language named; universal inference rejected.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Métis; Michif; it does not say everyone speaks it. | Every Métis person speaks Michif.

## s02-X24

Shared lawmakers/courts; prime minister/House confidence versus presidents. Accept supported alternatives.

Look for: Supported similarity and difference.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: All have courts; Canada has a prime minister rather than a president. | Canada's prime minister is its president.

## s02-X25

City marker beside meeting of Red and Assiniboine rivers.

Look for: Uses dot and joining river lines, not climate.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: The river lines meet beside Winnipeg's dot. | The map proves it is always warm.

## s02-X26

Cargo handling at Montreal's listed port; no evidence of every resident's job.

Look for: Place/service link with bounded employment inference.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Montreal's port handles cargo; this does not describe all residents. | Everyone in Montreal loads ships.

## s02-P23

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: The Haida Nation's council describes its work today. | All Indigenous peoples are one people.

Look for: Performs named component using supplied evidence; written feedback; content separate from grammar.

Samples, first shows evidence, second needs work: Canada: Parliament makes laws. | The prime minister is a president.

## s02-P25
