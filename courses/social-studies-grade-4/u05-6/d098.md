# Day 98: Growth map and timeline

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare pages and sealed exit-098.md. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-098-sources.md private. Bring all day 97 papers. Use cards-098.md as a master. Print the invented bar and route together at equal magnification; check one segment matches one bar, each two centimeters at actual size. Keep historical locators out of distance measuring.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 97 tickets, written tasks, repairs and question-board paper. Review Return and repair: one priority. If many day 97 tickets showed error T, use [[s05-F98-1]]; error M uses [[s05-F98-2]]. These club and town cases are made up. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Others use written cues. Finish during Map work.

#### 5 min. Recall
Review map scale: equal bar lengths represent equal distances. Lay a paper strip along the printed bar, then count whole bars along the route. Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks, from grade 3. Review source-log.md: maker, date, detail and question. Identify the atlas maker, not a witness to consent.

#### 20 min. Map work
First five minutes finish the priority with fresh [[s05-F98-3]] or [[s05-F98-4]]. Alone, use [[s05-P98-1]] on timeline.md, then [[s05-P98-2]] and [[s05-P98-3]]. Transfer separate place labels to growth-map.md using teaching-locator.png. Review the separate date cards; keep acquisition and statehood distinct. Give one location at a time, without a method grid. Point to the checklist.md evidence reminder. Watch for equal spacing between events instead of between years; point back to decade marks.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review question-board.md and the growth question. Six rotating children may share one existing label, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Record names; passing is allowed. Every child marks one secure label and one gap on their page. Collect all papers and source logs.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Open exit-098.md for independent [[s05-X98-1]]. Read the original historical summary neutrally; ceding means giving up claimed control. Never coach the inference. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus fifteen for other papers. Review record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, separately by component. Return everything day 99.

### Other rooms
No printer: trace the supplied locator; copy short texts and a ten-year line. Draw route and bar together. No projector: point to paper. Printed summaries replace live pages.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy texts and trace the locator if printing is unavailable. Draw equal route segments and a matching bar.
**Skip.** Public turns and repeated pair talk.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 97 work; choose one repair.
- **5 min. Recall.** Review equal bar lengths and equal-year marks.
- **20 min. Map work.** Finish repair. Your child uses [[s05-P98-1]], [[s05-P98-2]] and [[s05-P98-3]]. Point to the supplied atlas, not guessed boundaries.
  **Say.** "What do equal timeline gaps represent?" Expect: equal years.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Give exit-098.md with [[s05-X98-1]]. Your child answers alone; discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: cut repeated public sharing. Keep map, scale, equal-year construction and exit. Longer: check a label against its source.

### Words and supports
Review territory: land governed within a larger country; admission: joining as a state. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. No personal reports.

### Tomorrow
Day 99 links gains with effects. Return all day 98 work. Error A confuses acquisition with admission; error H offers a date without a mechanism. Use one status distinction or isolated causal link in Return and repair and Link work's first five minutes.

### Credit
Ours. Original summaries use Sources s05-ATLAS, s05-OR, s05-AK, s05-HAWAII, s05-PRTREATY and s05-SPWAR. Atlas outlines are public domain.
