# Day 103: Feedback and a fresh component

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read day 102's assessment and day 101 cues. Put one private priority note on each packet. Prepare four rosters and group-records.md. Print only each child's assigned fresh-attempt.md section; seal it. Keep teacher-key.md and current generated key adult-only. Use record.md's existing component scale. Match groups to evidence; other gaps receive a private source-specific written move from the returned packet.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return day 102's assessment. Review the respond routine from grade 3: feedback note, component group, fresh task and reflection. Each child reads one priority note. Error I needs the force-versus-choice source reminder; error R needs separate affected-group evidence. Return day 101 cues alongside the relevant component. No public error labels or personal histories.

#### 20 min. Groups
Hold four sequential five-minute meetings, at most eight children each; repeat a priority component when needed. These cases are made up. Reserve two minutes for its move, ninety seconds for at most two 45-second responses, and ninety seconds for written feedback, ten seconds per group paper. Record names. Everyone submits paper. While waiting, children locate the evidence named by their packet cue and annotate one missing component, without copying a full correction.

Access uses [[s05-P103-group-access]]: mark endpoints, then trace movement between them. Sources uses [[s05-P103-group-sources]]: underline each maker's observation. Review: a second source needs a different maker; a copy adds no independent observation. Residence uses [[s05-P103-group-residence]]: hold the school's position while moving the district line. Continuity uses [[s05-P103-group-continuity]]: mark the date separately from the public activity. If many still miss, use the same concrete feature and record content help; do not show the fresh case.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Cover group work. Reveal only the assigned made-up record: [[s05-P103-access]], [[s05-P103-sources]], [[s05-P103-residence]] or [[s05-P103-continuity]]. Children answer independently on fresh-attempt.md. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. These probe one component, not the whole failed assessment item. Keep the remaining historical or reasoning gaps recorded separately.

#### 5 min. Self-check and handoff
Children mark one secure idea and one remaining question. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Within every pair, partner one speaks for 45 seconds, then partner two for 45 seconds. The whole room finishes this round in 90 seconds; passing is allowed. During this independent closure, allow eight seconds per assigned short paper, four minutes for thirty, to mark and write one component cue. Return all fresh attempts today; group papers returned during Groups. Review record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; blank means unobserved. Keep unfinished evidence blank and retain its gap. Close folders without promising another repair slot.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the assigned source record and a blank response space. No projector: use paper. Source summaries replace unavailable live pages or visitors.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy just the assigned record if printing is unavailable. Keep the fresh case covered.
**Skip.** Group rotations and public talk.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 102's assessment. Read one priority cue together.
- **15 min. Groups.** Use the matching group task on the student page. Point to its concrete evidence, then cover it.
  **Say.** "Which detail addresses your cue?" Expect: endpoints, each maker's observation, fixed school location or dated activity.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Reveal the matching fresh-attempt.md section. Your child answers independently; read neutrally if needed.
- **5 min. Self-check and handoff.** Return the fresh paper with one cue. Your child records a secure idea and a remaining question. Keep both with the assessment.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: cut repeated pair sharing. Keep feedback, group move, independent attempt and same-day return. Longer: identify further evidence needed for the remaining question.

### Words and supports
Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review the source-maker distinction. Neutral reading and exact dictation are access; record content help separately. No personal reports.

### Tomorrow
Day 104 starts course-level check 2 with unseen evidence about first peoples and primary sources. Day 103's remaining gaps stay in the component record; no readiness or reteaching block is promised there.

### Credit
Ours. All group and fresh-attempt records are made up. No invented Native people or nations are used.
