# Day 106: Feedback and fresh evidence

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read teacher-only key-checks-104-106.md. Put one private cue on each returned day 104 and day 105 packet. Form four rosters using record-checks-104-106.md. Print only each child's assigned fresh-106.md section and cover it. Never reveal a fresh attempt during teaching.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return day 104's assessment in two minutes and day 105's assessment in three. Children read one priority cue across both. Review day 103's written routine: feedback, component group, fresh task, reflection. No public error labels or personal reports. Preserve other gaps.

#### 20 min. Groups
Run four sequential five-minute meetings, at most eight children each. Use two minutes for the model, ninety seconds for two 45-second responses, and ninety seconds for written moves. Record speakers; passing is allowed. Everyone submits paper. Other children underline evidence named by their cue, without copying a full answer.

Dates, errors N/O: use [[C2-K106-date]], then [[steps:C2-K106-date]]. A dated object does not trace a journey. Clues, errors P/Q/R: use [[C2-K106-clue]], then [[steps:C2-K106-clue]]. Separate original metadata from the inside clue. Kinds, errors S/T: use [[C2-K106-kinds]], then [[steps:C2-K106-kinds]]. Causes, errors U/V: use [[C2-K106-cause]], then [[steps:C2-K106-cause]]. Trace cause toward effect, tail to head. All cases are made up. If many share a gap, give its move to the class, then use remaining meetings for other priorities. Do not show fresh cases.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Cover group examples. Reveal only the assigned fresh-106.md section: [[C2-E106-date]], [[C2-E106-clue]], [[C2-E106-kinds]] or [[C2-E106-cause]]. Children answer independently. These held assessment rows sample only the assigned reasoning, not recovery of every historical component. Read neutrally or scribe exactly; record content help separately.

#### 5 min. Return and reflect
Children write a secure idea and remaining question while the teacher marks short fresh papers: eight seconds each, four minutes for thirty. Return them with one cue during the final minute. Use shown, shown with help or not yet seen; unobserved stays blank. Retain unresolved historical gaps. Group papers received moves during Groups. There is no additional exit ticket or promised repair slot.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy only the assigned fresh section with response lines. No screen or device is needed. Cover paper models before independent work.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy only the assigned fresh-106.md section if printing is unavailable. Keep it covered.
**Skip.** Group rotations and public responses.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 104 and day 105 work with one next-step note.
- **15 min. Groups.** Use the matching group example on the student page, then cover it.
  **Say.** "Which detail addresses your note?" Expect: the earlier date, requested action, permission versus outcome, or water entering the roof.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Reveal only the matching fresh-106.md section. Your child answers alone; read neutrally if needed.
- **5 min. Return and reflect.** Read the teacher key privately and give one cue. Keep the response with a secure idea and remaining question.

### Shorter or longer
Keep feedback, matched model, fresh attempt and same-day return. Longer: identify evidence still needed for the remaining question.

### Words and supports
Review years ago, primary source, audience, purpose, cause and effect in the written days' meanings. Supply the missing move through its model. No planned-only ledger row is assumed taught.

### Tomorrow
Day 107 opens the question about why people in each region make and do different things. Retain today's record; no new assessment or repair slot is promised there.

### Credit
Ours. Every group and fresh case is made up, with no invented Native person or nation.
