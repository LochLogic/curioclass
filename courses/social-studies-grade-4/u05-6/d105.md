# Day 105: Course-level check 2, sources and exchange

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print sources-105.md and the student page. Read key-checks-104-106.md privately. Keep sources unseen until Orient. Prepare record-checks-104-106.md. Reserve sixty minutes after class for thirty packets, two minutes each, untested. Printed cards replace unavailable websites; no visitor or unseen photograph is needed.

### The day
#### 3 min. Orient
Keep day 104's assessment private until day 106. It checked dated evidence and a letter. Keep those answers private until day 106. Review only independent work and access arrangements. Today's new selections include our schematic map and event strip.

#### 15 min. Source comparison
Alone, children use Sources C and D for [[C2-E105-1]] and [[C2-E105-2]]. Read source labels neutrally. Record oral responses exactly when needed, without identifying a contribution or limit. These questions ask comparison, not a memorized Cabot biography. Royal authority does not establish residents' consent.

#### 17 min. Exchange
Children use Map E and Timeline F for [[C2-E105-3]], [[C2-E105-4]] and [[C2-E105-5]]. Keep both visible. No map measurement is needed. Separate cause, crop effect and animal effect. Do not give a causal arrow in speech. The event strip explicitly has unequal intervals. Allow labelled arrows or sentences answering the same stems.

#### 5 min. Collect
Children inspect their work; collect without revealing answers. After class use the day 104 component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Unobserved stays blank. Note access separately. Error S treats both sources as eyewitnesses; error T turns permission into an outcome. Error U reverses causation or gives only order; error V reverses movement. Combine both days' evidence for one private priority cue. If many miss, prioritize that component in day 106 Groups.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy source cards, labelled arrows, event strip and stems before class. No devices are needed. Read or enlarge text; scribe exact responses without causal hints.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy sources-105.md and the response page when printing is unavailable. Keep work in a folder.
**Skip.** Class collection and public sharing.
- **3 min. Assessment set-up.** Keep day 104 answers private.
- **15 min. Source comparison.** Your child answers [[C2-E105-1]] and [[C2-E105-2]] using Sources C and D.
- **17 min. Exchange.** Your child answers [[C2-E105-3]], [[C2-E105-4]] and [[C2-E105-5]] using Map E and Timeline F. Read without hints.
- **5 min. Collect.** Keep the work. Read the teacher key privately. Choose one next-step note across days 104 and 105 for day 106.

### Shorter or longer
Keep each component; unfinished evidence stays unobserved. Longer: reread existing responses without new questions or answer sharing.

### Words and supports
Review primary and secondary source from day 60; cause and effect from day 86. A source's age alone does not establish its contribution. Neutral reading and dictation are access. Judge mechanism separately from English grammar.

### Tomorrow
Day 106 Feedback returns days 104 and 105. Groups uses fresh made-up component cases for errors N to V. Each child then answers one assigned fresh component, without repeating the historical check.

### Credit
Ours. Source rows C2-105C, C2-105Cdate, C2-105D and C2-105EF document factual references. No existing map, prose, activity or image is adapted.
