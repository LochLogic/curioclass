# Day 101: Separate facts before the assessment

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare pages and sealed exit-101.md. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key, cards-101.md answer master and exit-101-sources.md adult-only. Bring day 100 papers. Assign six optional recall turns. Keep record.md and the access roster private. Cover worked arguments; the unseen assessment remains sealed.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 100 tickets, written tasks, source logs, repairs and question-board paper. Review Return and repair: one priority. If many day 100 tickets showed error K, use [[s05-F101-1]]; error Q uses [[s05-F101-2]]; error R uses [[s05-F101-3]]. All repair cases are made up. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Others use written cues. Classify support; do not revise prose.

#### 5 min. Recall
Review the partner routine: choose one set, answer alone, compare choices, then request an adult check. Keep date, vehicle and meaning sets separate. Review timeline.md for chronology only; cover it before recall. Use checklist.md to recall relevant evidence. Explain the procedure, without a new historical model or full assessment rehearsal.

#### 20 min. Recall work
First five minutes finish the priority with fresh [[s05-F101-4]], [[s05-F101-5]] or [[s05-F101-6]]. Fifteen simultaneous pairs use [[s05-P101-1]], [[s05-P101-2]] and [[s05-P101-3]] separately. Each writes before comparing; adult verifies against cards-101.md and the private key. No speed score. Review forced transport and division through separate meanings, not a combined causal argument. Hear six rotating children, one existing response each, 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes total. Record names; passing is allowed. For a miss, point to its specific source reminder. Review source-log.md: maker, date, detail and question.

#### 5 min. Access check
Collect every recall sheet and repair. Privately confirm neutral reading, exact dictation or enlarged print for day 102. Review question-board.md: retain one unanswered question in folders. Cover all completed displays. Do not preview held maps, source selections or labels.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Say the Review Label and Record is made up. Reveal exit-101.md; children independently answer [[s05-X101-1]] on paper. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus fifteen for other papers. Review record.md's scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all work with one written cue before day 102; content reteaching waits until day 103.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy separate choice sets and a blank response sheet, keeping adult keys apart. No projector: point to paper. Printed source reminders replace live pages.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy the separate choice sets and keep the adult key apart if printing is unavailable.
**Skip.** Public turns and repeated partner comparisons.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 100 work; choose one repair.
- **5 min. Recall.** Review the separate-set routine.
- **20 min. Recall work.** Finish the matching fresh retry. Your child answers [[s05-P101-1]], [[s05-P101-2]] and [[s05-P101-3]] before you check privately.
  **Say.** "Which vehicle belongs with a canal?" Expect: boat.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Say the new record is made up. Reveal it with [[s05-X101-1]]. Your child answers alone; discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce partner comparisons. Keep all separate sets, access check and exit. Longer: verify a recall choice against its source.

### Words and supports
Review enslaved people: people forced to work who could not leave. Review division: deep disagreement over slavery. No personal reports or historical role play.

### Tomorrow
Day 102 administers the end-of-unit assessment. Return all day 101 papers before it begins, with one private written cue. Error I treats force as chosen work; error R claims equal benefit. Use tickets for access planning only; teach repairs on day 103.

### Credit
Ours. Historical recall uses Sources s05-MEX, s05-ROAD, s05-RAIL and s05-KN. Repair and exit records are made up.
