# Day 100: Claims and source details

## Teacher page
### Before class
Prepare pages, separate claim-test.md records and sealed exit pieces. Keep teacher-key.md, current generated key and exit-100-sources.md private. Bring all day 99 work. Use cards-100.md as a master; the student page supplies attributed summaries. Cover worked arguments and sentence frames. Keep the canal and letter unseen until the exit.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 99 tickets, written tasks, repairs, source logs and question-board paper. Review Return and repair: one priority. If many day 99 tickets showed error H, use [[s05-F100-1]]; error O uses [[s05-F100-2]]; error R uses [[s05-F100-3]]. Cases are made up. More than a third means whole class; otherwise small group. Others use written cues.

#### 5 min. Recall checklist
Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review: a second source needs a different maker; two details from one book are one source. Retrieve the checklist.md criteria: claim, source, detail, because, limit. Withdraw sentence frames. Children recall criteria; give no model argument or completed two-source response.

#### 20 min. Closing work
First five minutes finish one priority with fresh [[s05-F100-4]], [[s05-F100-5]] or [[s05-F100-6]]. Alone, use separate question headings and [[s05-P100-1]] on claim-test.md. For [[s05-P100-2]], annotate the rival claim with one relevant advocacy detail. Review source-log.md: maker, date, detail and question. Keep the historical event separate in living-peoples.md. These are separate one-source responses, not a combined argument. During the final two minutes, fifteen simultaneous pairs each share one narrow claim, 45 seconds per child. Watch for titles without details; point to the relevant record.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review question-board.md. **Ask.** Which detail supports your narrow claim? Hear six rotating volunteers, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Record names; passing is allowed. Collect every response, repair and annotation for descriptive feedback. The closing response is practice, not a scored performance.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Cover practice. Say the Transfer Records are made up by two independent invented makers. Reveal exit-100-stimulus.md and exit-100.md. Independently answer [[s05-X100-1]]. Read neutrally or scribe exactly. After class allow thirty minutes for thirty tickets, one minute each, plus fifteen for other work. Review record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, separately by component. Return all papers day 101.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy short records and blank headings, holding the exit texts separately. No projector: point to paper. Use the checked printed summaries if live pages are unavailable.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy texts and headings if printing is unavailable; trace the canal picture separately.
**Skip.** Public turns and repeated partner talk.
- **5 min. Return and repair.** Return day 99 papers; choose one repair.
- **5 min. Recall checklist.** Review claim, source, detail, because and limit.
- **20 min. Closing work.** Finish the matching fresh retry. Your child completes [[s05-P100-1]] and [[s05-P100-2]] separately.
  **Say.** "What shows that public government continues?" Expect: the government lists a council, laws or court.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Explain that the new records are made up. Reveal them with [[s05-X100-1]]. Your child writes alone; discuss afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: cut repeated sharing. Keep separate responses, repairs and exit. Longer: identify a limit of one supporting detail.

### Words and supports
Review claim and reasoning. Neutral reading, exact dictation and home-language rehearsal preserve independence. No home or cultural identity reports.

### Tomorrow
Day 101 recalls separate facts before the assessment. Return all day 100 papers. Error K gives preference without support; error Q gives titles without distinct details; error R claims equal benefit. Classify one supporting detail or group effect in Return and repair and Recall work; no prose revision.
