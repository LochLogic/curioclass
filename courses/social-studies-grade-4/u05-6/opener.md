# Section 5.6 opener

## Before class

Print the day’s teaching cards and practice sheet. Print its half-sheet ticket separately and hold it until the exit. Print teacher-key.md for adults only. Never include an answer in a student packet. Use the unit record in u05-1/record.md. Send the English/Spanish family note on day 80.

## Ideas and teaching

Keep separate chronology and causal mechanism, source title and supporting evidence, national gain and resident loss. The end-of-unit assessment uses held source selections. A defensible argument can emphasize different supported effects. Score reasoning separately from English, spelling and handwriting.

Each day is 40 minutes. Follow the named parts on its plan. Preserve teaching time: Return and repair holds one five-minute priority move; the first five work minutes finish it and a fresh retry. More than a third missing means a shared move; otherwise use a small group. Other gaps receive written moves within that same work block.

Practice classifies, annotates or tests single components. Keep held exit and assessment cases out of teaching and repair. Cover completed examples before independent work. No three-mode assessment explanation, four-row assessment grid, reserved river-trade chain or complete historical two-source argument is rehearsed. Public question-board talk is optional, six single 45-second asks; collect everyone’s paper. Never attempt thirty serial turns in five minutes.

## Words and continuity

Review supporting question: a smaller question that helps answer the big one. Review homeland: the land a nation’s people have known as home for a very long time. Review consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made. Review perspective: two sides of one event. Review corroboration: two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. A second source needs a different maker; two details from one book are one source. Supply independent source-log and map-scale reminders. Do not presume a planned routine has been learned.

New distinctions receive plain explanations before use: territory is land governed within a larger country; admission is joining as a state; annexation brings land under a country; treaty is an agreement between governments; citizen is a legal member of a country. Review immigrant: a person who comes from another country to live. Never call Native nations immigrants. Review petition: a written request to officials.

## Expected errors

- A: Statehood and acquisition are conflated. Repair: Admission makes a member state; purchase and annexation do not.
- B: Foreign transfer proves resident consent. Repair: Seek the affected nation’s own agreement.
- C: Transport itself adds territory. Repair: Transport changes access for movement and trade.
- D: Gender determines motives. Repair: Use each person’s documented purpose.
- E: Requested map places or historical and modern boundaries are conflated. Repair: Check location and distinguish dated national lines from modern state lines.
- F: Independence annexation and war happened together. Repair: Name distinct events and dates.
- G: All accounts are equally established. Repair: Test each assertion against independent evidence.
- H: Dates alone prove cause. Repair: Explain a mechanism with evidence.
- I: Enslavement was chosen employment. Repair: Captivity denied freedom and choice.
- J: Civil War division was unrelated to slavery. Repair: Connect western slavery disputes to division.
- K: Preference alone supports a claim. Repair: Connect evidence and reasoning.
- L: Territorial gains were empty land. Repair: Name residents and governments already there.
- M: Peoples vanished after transfer. Repair: Read dated public activity in the present.
- N: Payment after defeat proves a free sale. Repair: Military defeat constrained negotiations.
- O: A later event caused an earlier one. Repair: A cause must precede its effect.
- P: All Black people were enslaved. Repair: Free Black lives disprove the universal claim.
- Q: Two titles equal two-source support. Repair: Use a relevant detail from each distinct maker.
- R: Every group benefited equally. Repair: Track gains and losses separately by group.
- S: Nationality changes only through a journey. Repair: A governing border can move around a home.
- T: All gains used the same method. Repair: Distinguish purchase agreement annexation and war.
- U: Only liking maps makes the inquiry useful. Repair: Another reader needs missing evidence.
