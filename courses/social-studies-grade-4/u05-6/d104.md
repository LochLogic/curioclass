# Day 104: Course-level check 2, evidence and a letter

## Teacher page
### Before class
Read teacher-only key-checks-104-106.md. Print sources-104.md separately; keep it unseen. Prepare record-checks-104-106.md with component columns. Cover teaching displays. Allow sixty minutes after class for thirty packets, two minutes each, an untested estimate. Printed summaries replace unavailable live pages or photographs.

### The day
#### 3 min. Orient
Day 103 closed the unit assessment response. Keep remaining gaps in its record. Review only independent work. Hand out today's new sources. Say Source A's proposed claim is made up; its research finding is real. No content rehearsal.

#### 12 min. Dated evidence
Children work alone on [[C2-E104-1]] and [[C2-E104-2]]. Keep Source A visible. Read labels neutrally or record exact dictation. Observe chronology separately from route uncertainty. Give access support without selecting evidence or interpreting dates.

#### 20 min. Letter record
Children use Source B for [[C2-E104-3]], [[C2-E104-4]] and [[C2-E104-5]]. Keep questions separate. Allow pointing to clues followed by exact dictation. Do not show the answer key or confirm an inference. The source is an original factual summary, not a purported quotation.

#### 5 min. Collect
Children reread their own responses; collect every paper. After class record components as shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Unobserved stays blank. Access reading is not content help. Flag reversed dates, a proven-route claim, host/writer confusion or an inference without a clue as errors N to R. Write one private cue for day 106. If many miss, prioritize its group then; tomorrow stays a cold check.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the exact cards and five stems beforehand. No device or screen is needed. Enlarge text or scribe exactly. No oral round of thirty answers is planned.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy sources-104.md and the response page if printing is unavailable. Use a folder instead of a class record.
**Skip.** Class collection and group talk.
- **3 min. Assessment set-up.** Read directions without giving clues.
- **12 min. Dated evidence.** Your child answers [[C2-E104-1]] and [[C2-E104-2]] alone.
- **20 min. Letter record.** Keep Source B visible for [[C2-E104-3]], [[C2-E104-4]] and [[C2-E104-5]]. Read neutrally if needed.
- **5 min. Collect.** Keep the work. Read the teacher key privately afterward. Save one next-step note for day 106; do not rehearse tomorrow's sources.

### Shorter or longer
Keep all five items; unfinished evidence stays unobserved. Longer: reread the same sources without new prompts.

### Words and supports
Review written days: years ago, day 44; theory, day 45; primary source, day 60; audience and purpose, days 62 to 63. Preserve those meanings. Judge history separately from spelling and handwriting.

### Tomorrow
Day 105 compares two kinds of source on another voyage and explains exchange causes and effects. Return day 104 work in day 106 Feedback; errors N to R select response priorities.
