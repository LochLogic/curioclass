# Day 102: End-of-unit assessment

## Teacher page
### Before class
Print response and source sheets; keep teacher-key.md private; mark with teacher-key.md. Prepare assessment-context.md for neutral reading within each question's minutes. Hold assessment-a-river.md until Q8. Cover taught displays. Return day 101 written tasks [[s05-P101-1]], [[s05-P101-2]], [[s05-P101-3]], repairs and tickets with private cues. Have each child's reading, enlargement or exact-dictation support ready.

### The day
#### 1 min. Orient
Distribute the unseen packet. Explain paper responses and map marking. If day 101 tickets showed error I or error R, retain the private cue for day 103. Provide access without content coaching.

#### 3 min. Q1
Read Source B context for Q1; children use assessment-b.md for [[s05-E1-1]], [[s05-E1-2]] and [[s05-E1-3]]. Read neutrally; accept brief explanations.

#### 4 min. Q2
Read Source A directions, including location, year, method and country. Children mark separate locators and complete four acquisition rows: [[s05-E2-1]], [[s05-E2-2]], [[s05-E2-3]], [[s05-E2-4]]. Then [[s05-E2-5]]. Method labels can repeat. Do not supply national parties.

#### 3 min. Q3
Read Source C, including purposes, noon rest and mission construction. Children answer [[s05-E3-1]], [[s05-E3-2]] and [[s05-E3-3]]. Identify missionary work only as a historical purpose.

#### 2 min. Q4
Read Source F neutrally, including choice under pressure, forced transport and free Black lives. Children use [[s05-E4-1]] and [[s05-E4-2]]. Do not request personal histories.

#### 2 min. Q5
Read Source E; children answer [[s05-E5-1]] and [[s05-E5-2]]. No causal cues beyond the supplied context.

#### 4 min. Q6
Children mark the modern outlines for [[s05-E6-1]], then use [[s05-E6-2]] and [[s05-E6-3]]. Distinguish the later southern strip on the overlay; do not provide missing dates.

#### 5 min. Q7
Read Source D's attributed government excerpt and later scholarship. Children use [[s05-E7-1]] and [[s05-E7-2]] with the disputed-border locator. Repeat neutrally; do not decide between accounts for them.

#### 4 min. Q8
Read Source A's reserved river-trade context; display assessment-a-river.md now. Children answer [[s05-E8-1]] and [[s05-E8-2]]. Give no worked chain or correction.

#### 8 min. Q9
Read Source G's two distinct records and their makers. Children answer [[s05-E9-1]] once, naming two records and supporting details. Keep the interpretation independent.

#### 4 min. Reread and collect
Offer neutral rereading. Collect all sheets; no self-marking or answer display. Reserve 120 minutes after class, four minutes per child, to read thirty packets and record components, an untested estimate. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Assess [[s05-E9-2]], [[s05-E9-3]] and [[s05-E9-4]] from Q9 alone. Record reading access separately from content help; never average components. Return all work day 103.

### Other rooms
No printer: prepare source outlines by tracing the supplied maps, then copy short response prompts. No projector: use printed sources. Keep teacher context and keys separate.

### At home
**Swap.** Trace supplied outlines and copy prompts if printing is unavailable. Keep adult context and keys separate.
**Skip.** Class collection routines; retain every question.
- **40 min. Assessment.** Give the unseen packet. Follow Q1 to Q9 and their minute allocations, including neutral context readings. Your child writes or dictates independently. Repeat a reading without explaining the answer.
  **Say.** "Which source reading would you like repeated?" Expect: a source label or no request.
Afterward keep the work for day 103. Do not add teaching during the assessment.

### Shorter or longer
Keep every question. Apply established access arrangements; record any additional time separately. Do not add content prompts.

### Words and supports
Use already established access supports. Do not introduce vocabulary or explain content during this assessment.

### Tomorrow
Day 103 responds to day 102's assessment. Return each packet with one priority component cue. Route access, source independence, changed governance or continuing activity to the matching group; give other content gaps a private written move. Retain remaining gaps for course-level checks without promising a repair slot.

### Credit
Ours. Sources A to G are original summaries and attributed selections in assessment-context.md. Federal atlas outlines are public domain. No source image or private life is required.
