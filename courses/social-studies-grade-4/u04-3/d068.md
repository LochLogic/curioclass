# Day 68: Movements and effects

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 67 papers with one cue. Keep the complete exit master covered. Use teaching cards as teacher references; today's page supplies practice. Print the generated answer key separately; it replaces old practice answers. Lay out the blank branch sheet. All copying chains, crews and canal records are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 67 promised exchanges. First return its papers. If day 67's exit showed error K, review independent account: a separate teller, not a copy. Use [[s04-P68-4]]. For errors H or M, use the inside address in [[s04-P68-6]]. Error S: explain that this notice records a repair request. More than a third missing: whole-class repair; otherwise meet a group. Work on one priority.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review hemisphere: a half of the globe, grade 3 day 4. Introduce exchange as movements between regions; movements need not benefit everyone. Read the direction facts from Source DIRECTIONS on Side 1. Point to the Americas and Europe on the retained map without adding a voyage route.

Review grade 3 day 93's cause-effect chain and day 98's perspective: two sides of one event. Read the made-up crew record. **Ask.** [[s04-K68-1]] Then reveal [[steps:s04-K68-1]]. Add intended-gain and documented-harm branches on the blank organizer. Consequence means an effect. An aim cannot establish a result. Retain this model in folders.

#### 17 min. Exchange work
First five minutes finish the priority repair; [[s04-P68-5]] gives a fresh origin retry. For address errors, retry the original notice after feedback. Then children work alone on [[s04-P68-1]] and [[s04-P68-2]], drawing one arrow and sorting two labels. Expect the supplied directions; reverse arrows need origin-first reading.

Use [[s04-P68-3]] for one bounded canal effect. Expect blocked entry, not equal gain, error E. Review day 66: forced labor and violence also harmed Taíno people, Source HARMS. Disease explains no entire history, error L. People are never goods. Fifteen pairs explain their canal answer, 45 seconds each, 90 seconds total. Give feedback; review source-log headings without another written task.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's board routine. Group D's six explain their canal answer, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. A public pass is allowed. Record names; collect every page. Cover practice before the exit.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the complete exit-68.md now, including its diagram text. Read [[s04-X68-1]] exactly. Children answer independently. After class, allow thirty times sixty seconds, 30 minutes, for all judged papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; leave unobserved blank. Return all papers on day 69.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the page and complete covered exit onto paper. Read neutrally. A screen, visitor or photo is unnecessary.

  **Say.** Which line gives the aim? Expect clearing the walkway.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: omit repeated pair explanations. Longer: explain why the crew's aim cannot prove its result. Protect repair and exit.

### Words and supports
Review independent account, hemisphere, cause-effect chain and perspective. New: exchange, consequence, intended gain, documented harm. Read neutrally; allow dictation. Record content prompts as help.
