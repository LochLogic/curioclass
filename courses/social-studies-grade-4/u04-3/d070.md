# Day 70: Community accounts today

## Teacher page
### Before class
Return day 69's exit ticket and written tasks with one cue. Keep the complete exit covered. Use practice-70.md's factual summaries and source footers only; today's page replaces its old tasks. Print the generated answer key separately. Set out retained papers. No sacred images, conquest reenactment or questions about children's identities. The library labels are made up.

### The day
#### 5 min. Return and repair
If day 69's exit showed error Q, review alliance and distinct roles. Return its caption with a cue naming the missed force; do not repeat it as a new test. Use [[s04-P70-4]] for a fresh role check. Missing route stops: revisit day 69's separate location labels on the map. More than a third missing: whole-class repair; otherwise meet a group. One priority per child.

#### 8 min. Teaching first
Review day 66's Taíno living-community statement, using Source TAINO. Political defeat does not mean disappearance. Introduce Nahua and Nahuatl as the people and language named by Source NAHUA. Read the two attributed summaries on Side 1 separately; publication dates are unknown, not today's check date.

Model finding a time clue in the made-up library sentence on Side 2: now establishes present time. A title alone cannot establish the activity. Review source-sourcing from day 62, maker and date first. Remember day 60's named Penobscot case only; today's evidence concerns these two sources. No one account represents every community member.

#### 17 min. Community work
First five finish repair; [[s04-P70-5]] gives a fresh role retry. Children then work alone on [[s04-P70-1]] and [[s04-P70-2]]. Expect present and today. Distinguish a time word from an action word. Do not ask for paired community activities before the exit.

Read the made-up labels. **Ask.** [[s04-P70-3]] Expect the sentence reporting teaching now, not the title, error J. Give feedback. Fifteen parallel pairs explain their chosen detail, 45 seconds each, 90 seconds total. Review day 68's branch headings; no continuity paragraph is modeled. Keep the two taught summaries in the Living Peoples packet, never the held exit. Collect responses and source logs; retained organizers stay in folders.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review day 60's board routine. Group A's six explain their evidence-detail choice, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; public pass allowed. Return to what voyages meant for people already here. Teacher connects earlier harms with present accounts without a child synthesis task. Record names; every child submits paper. Cover examples.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the complete exit-70.md now, including both new cards. Read [[s04-X70-1]] exactly. Children answer independently on the half slip. After class, thirty times sixty seconds, 30 minutes, covers all judged papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; unobserved stays blank. Return all papers on day 71.
