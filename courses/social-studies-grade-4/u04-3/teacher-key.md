# Section 4.3 teacher key

Teacher only. Before class, print this key separately. Never display held answers early.

Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen; leave unobserved components blank.

## s04-X68-1

Answer: Maize added a food crop elsewhere; introduced smallpox caused severe Native losses.

Look for: Distinct crop and disease effects, without equal-benefit or single-cause claims.

Samples: Maize added a food crop elsewhere; introduced smallpox caused severe Native losses. | Everyone gained equally.

## s04-X69-1

Answer: Coast through Tlaxcala toward Tenochtitlan.

Look for: Connects labeled coastal and inland stops in order.

Samples: Coast through Tlaxcala toward Tenochtitlan. | Cortés alone did everything on this route.

## s04-X69-2

Answer: Spanish forces and allied Tlaxcalteca forces fought Mexica defenders.

Look for: Names allies and defenders; other Native allies may be acknowledged.

Samples: Spanish forces and allied Tlaxcalteca forces fought Mexica defenders. | Cortés fought alone.

## s04-X70-1

Answer: Taíno artists maintain artistic traditions; Eduardo de la Cruz writes books in Nahuatl.

Look for: Links each secular activity to its community and card.

Samples: Taíno artists maintain artistic traditions; Eduardo de la Cruz writes books in Nahuatl. | Both peoples vanished.

## s04-P68-1

Answer: Americas toward Europe.

Look for: Uses supplied origin/direction, without the held opposite-flow effect pair.

Samples: Americas toward Europe. | Every movement benefited everyone equally.

## s04-P68-2

Answer: Both go Europe toward Americas.

Look for: Groups entries by supported direction; people never in goods columns.

Samples: Both go Europe toward Americas. | People can be sorted as goods.

## s04-P68-3

Answer: Boats cannot enter that inlet while the gate is closed.

Look for: A single bounded direct effect.

Samples: Boats cannot enter that inlet while the gate is closed. | Every resident benefits equally.

## s04-P69-1

Answer: Coast, Tlaxcala, Tenochtitlan.

Look for: Place ordering only, not the held trace-plus-actors task.

Samples: Coast, Tlaxcala, Tenochtitlan. | Cortés acted alone.

## s04-P69-2

Samples: Mexica defenders. | All Native communities took one side.

## s04-P69-3
