# Day 38: Use feedback on one fresh component

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md. Use record.md to assign one priority and matching card. Keep cards sealed. Prepare written moves and four sequential groups. Exception M: its Haida source appeared on days 23 and 24. Use it only as an unscored reminder; retain M as not retried. Assign another fresh card only for another documented gap.

### The day
#### 5 min. Warm-up, Feedback
Return day 37's assessment, day 36's s02-X36, s02-P36, s02-P36-R, s02-P36-D1, s02-P36-D2 and s02-P36-D3, and day 29's s02-E7, s02-E8, s02-E8b, s02-E11 and s02-E12. Review grade 3's feedback, group, fresh-task and reflection routine. Give one private priority; children read feedback. No public rankings or household examples.

Use [[s02-P38-R]] to check that each child understands the private cue. Children name, point to or dictate the component in parallel. Keep this routine unscored; judge content only on the assigned fresh card.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Meet four groups sequentially, five minutes each. Choosing sources: review maker, purpose and complementary experiences. Checking facts: review checkable/opinion and corroboration, two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing. Trade: review named inputs, changed purchases and group effects. Claim/explanation: review reasoning, how a detail supports a claim, with two makers kept distinct. On returned work, children highlight a supplied feedback detail or label one field. Do not rehearse a complete repair response. Others follow these written moves independently. Each group hears at most two 45-second explanations, leaving 3.5 minutes for guidance. The last group may instead hear six unresolved day 29 findings at 45 seconds each, leaving 30 seconds to change speakers. Its usual two explanations then stop; other children there use written moves. Record unheard findings as not retried. Do not reveal fresh cards yet.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Give each child only the assigned fresh card: [[s02-P38a]], [[s02-P38b]], [[s02-P38c]] or [[s02-P38d]], or its single-priority variant named in opener.md, except M. For M alone, retain not retried and use the earlier record as a reminder. Read neutrally. Children answer independently with a new detail and reason; they may direct a scribe or use aided speech without an adult queue. Do not practice a card before judging it. Collect each assigned response. If it misses the priority, record the remaining need rather than supply the answer.

#### 5 min. Self-review
Pairs each get 45 seconds simultaneously, explaining one change from their work; passing publicly is allowed. Reflect without assigning a scale. Keep the folder for day 39's map course-level check. After class mark thirty assigned cards at 60 seconds each, 30 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component, access separate. Return fresh attempts and the cue sheet privately at day 39's opening; written cue only.

### Other rooms
Hand-copy only the assigned card if printing fails. Printed feedback replaces screens; no outside resource is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: copy only the assigned fresh card and response space.
**Skip.** Four class meetings and public sharing.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 29, 36 and 37 work. Choose one next step.
- **15 min. Respond.** Locate the missed component in returned work. Review that idea without copying an answer.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Give only the assigned [[s02-P38a]], [[s02-P38b]], [[s02-P38c]] or [[s02-P38d]], or its assigned variant. Your child answers alone. For M alone, review the earlier record and leave the judgment open.
- **5 min. Reflect.** **Say.** "What changed in your explanation?" Expect a new relevant detail or clearer evidence link. Keep the folder for day 39.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce optional sharing, retaining the fresh attempt. Longer: explain the existing revision more precisely, without another judged task.

### Words and supports
Review source-sourcing, corroboration, checkable, opinion, reliance, evidence and reasoning. Use neutral reading, enlarged text and child-directed dictation. No second scale or team judgment.

### Tomorrow
Day 39 begins course-level check 1, part 1, using physical and political maps. Privately return the assigned main or variant card and s02-P38-R with one written cue before that check; keep unresolved needs on record.md. No unit 3 readiness task.

### Credit
Ours. The main routes and most variants are made up. The M reminder uses the credited real Haida Nation governance record.
