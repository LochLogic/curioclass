### The day
#### 5 min. Warm-up, Return and repair
Return s02-X35, s02-P35, s02-P35-R and s02-E9. If many day 35 exits showed error G, preference, error N, titles only, or error F, everyone gains, use [[s02-P36-R]] with all; otherwise one group. #### 10 min. Definition sets
Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Children match [[s02-P36-D1]], then check. Review grade 3 specialize and grade 2 import/export with [[s02-P36-D2]], then check. Review grade 3 reasoning with [[s02-P36-D3]], then check. Each partner gets one 45-second turn across these sets, simultaneously across pairs; no speed score.
Check: Maker created it; audience receives it; purpose is intended use.
Check: Import comes in; export goes out; specialize means makes mostly one kind.
Check: Evidence supplies a relevant detail; reasoning links it to the claim.

#### 15 min. Evidence work
First five minutes finish the day 35 repair by matching an existing component, not rewriting. Others begin [[s02-P36]]. Review day 19's made-up equipment notice and metadata, information about a source. Read the printed labels; children match labels independently. Review checkable and opinion as supplied labels, not fresh claims to classify. If many confuse date and audience, point to the fields and repeat matching. Collect all matches; keep assessment and exit cases hidden.

#### 5 min. Prepare access
Children try today's larger text or neutral read-aloud. Confirm how pointing, dictation and breaks will work tomorrow. Give no assessment examples, source preview or reasoning prompts. Children keep the meanings recalled; collect today's work for later feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal exactly [[s02-X36]]. Children answer independently on the half slip. Offer access only. After class allow thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits and thirty times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for matches. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component, with access separate. Return day 38; give no targeted content repair on day 37.
