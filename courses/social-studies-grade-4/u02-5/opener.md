# Section 2.5 opener: Build a claim from distinct evidence

## Before class
Read teacher-only key-exits.md. Print only today's selected sources and sheets.
Keep held cards and keys out of practice packets. Say all invented people and scenes are made up.
Use the unit record in u02-1, one copy per student. Section records continue the same scale.

## The ideas
Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Gather relevant details under supplied claims on day 34. Day 35 revises one weak link in a supplied paragraph before the independent exit. Keep the entire port and bicycle packet unseen through day 36. The day 37 sources and prompts together fit 350 student words. E6 supplies the argument; E10 records its source support without another response or duplicate claim score. The response day addresses one missed component. A fresh component card is withheld until after group practice. Record unrepaired gaps honestly.

## Errors and response
G: preference replaces a defensible claim. N: titles replace details from both sources. F: an argument erases a losing group. C/D: the proposed verifier lacks the information needed.
More than a third missing: teach one priority to all. Otherwise meet one group.
Return all papers with one written move in the named slot. Normal repairs use five opening minutes and five work minutes.
Day 29 has only five opening repair minutes. Never replace the model with a repair.
Judge content separately from grammar, handwriting and access support.
Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Leave unattempted components blank.

## Turns and feedback
Pairs take two 45-second turns in parallel. Six teacher-heard comments take 4.5 minutes.
Collect paper from everyone else and return it the next named day.
Days 28 and 29 each reserve 25 minutes: thirty findings take 22.5 minutes, transitions 2.5.
Private desk findings use the same roster. Retain parallel listener replies; E8b judges them once.
Day 38 holds four sequential five-minute groups, one priority per student.

## Supplies and access
claim-notes; selected established sources; importance-record; sealed sources-37 and assessment-37; unit component record; one assigned response-a/b/c/d card; teacher-only key.
Read selected sources neutrally. Use enlarged maps, dictation and labeled drawings without reasoning hints.
Forty minutes; 600 teacher words and 350 student words at most; student sentences at most 15 words.
Prepared print and original diagrams replace screens, visitors and unopened photographs.
For browser-check facts, keep the logged source limit; no live dependency is required to run the paper task.
Never ask for household, language, money, religion, body or immigration disclosures.
Public talk may be passed; offer a private response. Adults aged 18 or older receive their own choice.
School rules still apply.

## Keep for later
Keep each child's notes and feedback together. Withdraw completed source-log models before research.
Reserve exit cases until their own day, the full assessment through day 36, and fresh repair cards until day 38.
No readiness sheet is needed: days 17, 18 and 30 retrieve earlier skills.

## Assign one fresh priority
Main response-a serves A. Use response-a-b, response-a-j or response-a-q for B, J or Q.
Main response-b serves C or D. Use response-b-i, response-b-k, response-b-l or response-b-m for I, K, L or M.
Main response-c serves F. Use response-c-e or response-c-o for E or O.
Main response-d serves G or N. Use response-d-h or response-d-p for H or P.
Print only that card. Score only its assigned component on the unit scale.
All variants remain held until the fresh-attempt block. Do not show them in group practice.
The M variant uses a real current Haida Nation governance record, never an invented Native person.
Keep remaining gaps open; do not infer that one card repaired every component.
