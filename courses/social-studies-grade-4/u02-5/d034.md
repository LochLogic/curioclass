# Day 34: Gather evidence and keep its limits

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only key-exits.md alongside current generated answers. Prepare practice-34.md within the page and claim-notes.md headings. Select day 33's country-relations article and United States goods table from trade-sources.md. Bring goods-map.md, topic-notes.md (s02-u02-2-topic-notes), source-log.md, question-board.md and record.md. Keep exit-34.md with pottery-map.svg sealed. Return all day 33 papers with one written priority.

### The day
#### 5 min. Warm-up, Return and repair
Return day 33's s02-X33, s02-P33, s02-P33-F, s02-P33-R, s02-P33-S and s02-P33-B. Review changed purchases and group effects. If many showed error F, everyone gains, or error E, goods without a purchase link, use [[s02-P34-R]] with all; otherwise one group. Others read feedback. The bakery is made up.

#### 8 min. Evidence organization
Review grade 3 reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review its claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Use these familiar headings as notes, without a filled paragraph. Explain tentative as may change with evidence. Begin [[s02-P34]] under the given headings. Keep titles separate from details. The diagram and article share one underlying maker.

#### 17 min. Evidence work
First five minutes extend the day 33 repair; others sort established notes. Then all continue [[s02-P34]] individually. Use the article, goods table and retained research notes. Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Read selected extracts aloud; each child records a detail and its maker. National totals cannot establish every group's experience. Use only notes linked to checked source rows; otherwise use the two selected records. If many supply only titles, point back to an actual record entry, without composing a complete argument.

#### 5 min. Question board
Review the question board. Pairs point from an existing note to the supporting question it helps answer. Each partner gets 45 seconds simultaneously across pairs; public passing is allowed. Teacher notes one unanswered question. Collect every list, including silent children's papers. No extra judged question or speaking queue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Reveal the half slip: exactly [[s02-X34]], with its diagram. Children answer alone. Neutral reading, enlarged print and dictation supply access without reasoning hints. Collect all. After class allow thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; thirty times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for practice. Use record.md's shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Return all papers day 35.

### Other rooms
Printed extracts replace screens. Copy headings and diagram by hand if printing fails; reserve the slip until exit.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: draw the organizer and copy selected records. Hide the exit.
**Skip.** Public board reports and class collection.
- **5 min. Return.** Return day 33 papers; use [[s02-P34-R]] for one purchase link.
- **8 min. Organize.** Review claim, detail and reason headings. Read selected records aloud.
- **12 min. Evidence.** Your child sorts notes with [[s02-P34]]. Distinguish each maker from the detail.
- **5 min. Connect.** Point to one existing note. **Say.** "Which question does this help answer?" Expect a match supported by the note's actual information.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X34]] alone. Keep it for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce optional sharing, preserving repair and exit. Longer: sharpen an existing limit note.

### Words and supports
Review claim, evidence and reasoning from day 14; reasoning links a detail to a claim. Tentative means may change with evidence. Accept labeled drawings or dictation; separate language support from reasoning.

### Tomorrow
Day 35 returns s02-X34, s02-P34 and s02-P34-R. Error G, liking instead of evidence, or error N, titles without details, receives five opening minutes and five Claim work minutes. Children write from organized notes.
