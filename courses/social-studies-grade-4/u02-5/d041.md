# Day 41: Respond to course-level check 1

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read teacher-only key-d041.md. Use exit-d041.md for the complete ticket. From days 39 and 40, select one priority per child. Form four groups: symbols/direction, distance, places/grid, source judgment. Cut apart routes A, B, C, CP and D; assign one. Prepare half-sheet exits. Verify route B's bar is 2 centimeters and U-to-V is 3 centimeters. Prepare written moves for children awaiting their group. Teaching cards stay teacher-only.

### The day
#### 5 min. Feedback
Return day 39's assessment and day 40's assessment privately. Children read one cue and locate the missed component. Include unresolved day 38 source needs when choosing the priority. Review the written day 38 feedback routine: same scale, one next step. No public ranking.

#### 20 min. Response groups
Meet four groups sequentially, five minutes each. If many share a gap, use that group's slot as whole-class teaching; it replaces that group. Others follow their written move on returned work. Never show fresh routes yet.

Symbols/direction: use [[C1-K41-1]] then [[steps:C1-K41-1]]. Draw a north arrow pointing up beside that teaching key. Rotate it right and trace directions. Distance: use [[C1-K41-2]] then [[steps:C1-K41-2]]. Places/grid: use [[C1-K41-3]] then [[steps:C1-K41-3]]; explain a province within Canada and state within Mexico from returned work. Source judgment: use [[C1-K41-4]] then [[steps:C1-K41-4]]. Error K: judge claims separately. Error L: printed does not mean proved. Review only entries written earlier; explicitly teach any remaining prerequisite before the fresh route.

Hear a rotating six across all groups, at most two 45-second explanations per group. This leaves at least three and a half minutes for teaching. Papers supply evidence from everyone. Record names heard, not an oral queue.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt
Give one unseen route per child: [[C1-P41-A]], [[C1-P41-B]], [[C1-P41-C]], [[C1-P41-CP]] or [[C1-P41-D]]. Children answer alone; collect all cards. Each route includes its own reference. They may direct a scribe. Prior feedback must not supply this case's answer. If the priority remains unseen, retain it in the folder rather than giving another answer to copy.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Give exit-d041.md, the new half-sheet map and [[C1-X41]], alone. Collect it. After class use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component for routes and exit, separate from access notes. Allow thirty minutes for thirty children. Keep original papers, cue, fresh route and exit together. The exit checks a contradicted location claim; it supplements the assigned route evidence.

### Other rooms
No screen is needed. Copy only each child's assigned route. Provide ruler-measured route B even without printing. The made-up diagrams replace real photos, visitors and live pages.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy one route and the complete exit-d041.md ticket. Route B needs a ruler; measure its bar and segment.
**Skip.** Four group meetings and public talk.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Return days 39 and 40. Choose one next step.
- **15 min. Teach.** Read the matching teaching card from key-d041.md. Use its answer afterward.
  **Say.** "What does this reference tell us?" Expect the card's corrected statement.
- **10 min. Fresh attempt.** Give one assigned [[C1-P41-A]], [[C1-P41-B]], [[C1-P41-C]], [[C1-P41-CP]] or [[C1-P41-D]]. Your child answers alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[C1-X41]] and its new map. Keep all papers together.

### Shorter or longer
Reduce optional adult explanations, keeping all four teaching slots, fresh attempt and exit. Longer: clarify the written cue after collecting evidence.

### Words and supports
Review legend, direction, scale, province, state, latitude, longitude, checkable, opinion and evidence. Use enlarged diagrams and neutral reading. Only content help changes the recorded evidence label. A child may pass publicly.

### Tomorrow
Day 42 opens its historical inquiry. Its first Retrieval minute privately returns day 41's marked fresh route and exit with one written cue. Keep unresolved map and source needs on the private record for later teaching; this distribution adds no content repair. A reversed direction on the exit needs a north-arrow cue.

### Credit
Ours. Response maps and records are made up. Route CP uses real geographic names and categories, logged in C1-geo-admin and C1-province. No personal household information is requested.
