# Day 37: Use unseen evidence independently

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md, teacher-only key-exits.md and current generated answers. Print sources-37.md separately with end-atlas.svg; incorporate assessment-37.md stems within the student page. Keep keys separate. Bring record.md and revised day 29 reports, finding records and listener replies. Prepare agreed access supports. Sources A/B are factual summaries; remaining classroom scenes are made up.

### The day
#### 4 min. Read packet
Reveal the unseen source sheet and page. Read neutrally, including diagram labels. A combines a regional department map, 1969, and undated provincial place-name records. B combines undated federal port records; 2026 labels date our summaries. Other scenes are invented. Errors C and D from day 36 wait for day 38. No model, hints or repair today. Students work independently throughout.

#### 3 min. Assessment Q1
Children answer [[s02-E1]], using A/B. Record location/cargo here; retained s02-E7 and s02-E8 carry revised research and presentation evidence.

#### 6 min. Assessment Q2
Children answer [[s02-E2a]], [[s02-E2b]], [[s02-E2c]], [[s02-E2d]] and [[s02-E2e]]. These sample kinds/views, gathered details and origin/format/context limits. Accept concise labeled notes.

#### 3 min. Assessment Q3
Children answer [[s02-E3a]], [[s02-E3b]] and [[s02-E3c]]. Record distinction, actual evidence comparison and bounded credibility separately.

#### 4 min. Assessment Q4
Children answer [[s02-E4a]] and [[s02-E4b]]. Record supported strength and order, detail and accuracy repairs.

#### 7 min. Assessment Q5
Children answer [[s02-E5a]] and fill [[s02-E5b]]'s table. Record specialization/growth/disruption separately from group effects in each country.

#### 8 min. Assessment Q6
Children answer [[s02-E6]]. Record claim/reasoning once. [[s02-E10]] records only this response's distinct-source support; no second response.

#### 5 min. Complete and collect
Children check for blanks without a key. Collect all. After class allow four minutes per child, two hours for thirty. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; record access separately. Combine revised day 29 evidence and day 35's s02-E9 without rescoring them. Assign one priority for day 38. If many miss a component, route it to its response group; do not reteach now.

### Other rooms
Print or hand-copy the source sheet, page and diagram; no live page is required. Keep answers adult-only.

### Tomorrow
Day 38 returns day 37's assessment and day 36's items. Four sequential groups address Choosing sources, Checking facts, Trade and Claim/explanation. Return day 29 components; give one fresh component attempt per child.
