# Unit 3 family note: comparing to one half and one

## English

For the next two school days your child is comparing fractions by asking: is it more or less than one half? More
or less than one?

**Half of what?** Half of a small pizza and half of a large pizza are both one half, but they are not the same
amount of food. Two fractions can only be compared when they are parts of the same whole.

**Try this at home, five minutes.** Fold a small sheet of paper and a large one in half. Ask your child: "Which
half is bigger? Are both of them one half?" Then use a ruler: is 5/8 inch more or less than half an inch? Ask how
they know each time.
