# Stretch 10 record sheet: multiplicative comparison

One row a student, for days 79 to 89; print two for a class over 30. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to H
(the unit openers); s for a slip (a fact or adding slip with the right setup); x for any other wrong answer; or a
dash for not reached. R1 to R3: the readiness check, day 79. E80 to E87: the exits; on day 82 a tick means all
three sentences, on day 86 both answers, on day 87 all three look-fors. End 1 to 8: the end check, day 88; items 7
and 8 take one tick for each look-for on the key (listed under the table), marked after class and handed back on
day 89. Each head names the standard's part it records.
