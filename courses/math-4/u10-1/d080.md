# Day 80: Times as many

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page both sides, the strip sheet, the exit slips and the key. Count 30 cubes into a bag for
each pair. Put the frame card on the wall, where it stays all stretch. Put the display page on the projector.
Have day 79's readiness results beside you, with the names of students who missed item 1.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: count by threes and fours

Whole class, then alone. Count aloud by threes to 30, then by fours to 40. Students write each count on the back
of the strip sheet. Today's copies are counted this way.

**If the readiness check showed gaps on item 1** (the 30 chairs in 5 rows): nothing moves today. If many missed
it, day 81 opens with the whole-class reteach in the unit opener. If a few did, list them for a small group in
day 81's warm-up. Today, pair each of them with a partner who got item 1 right. Gaps on items 2 and 3 wait for
days 83 and 87.

#### 10 min. Teaching first: 4 times as many, beside 4 more

Whole class, the teacher building at the front. Students fill the two frames on side 1 as you go. The display
shows each step on its own screen.

**Example 1** ([[s10-K80-1]]). [[steps:s10-K80-1]]

**Example 2** ([[s10-K80-2]]). [[steps:s10-K80-2]]

Leave both builds side by side all day. Say once, plainly: "Times also means occasions, as in three times a day.
Here it means copies." A student may say the comparison in a home language first.

#### 25 min. Build, draw, say: six comparisons

Pairs with cubes for the first three, then alone, drawing only. The six are on side 2 ([[s10-P80-1]],
[[s10-P80-2]], [[s10-P80-3]], [[s10-P80-4]], [[s10-P80-5]], [[s10-P80-6]]).

For 1 to 3, one partner builds the small strip, the other builds the copies under it, counting copies aloud.
Both draw the two strips on the strip sheet, a square for each cube, then fill the frame. Swap jobs each time.
Cubes go back in the bag after 3.

For 4 to 6 there are too many to build. Students draw each copy as a box with its number written inside, and
count by that number. This is the strip picture the stretch keeps.

Watch for a small strip plus single cubes on the end (error A: 5 for the first), and one copy too many (error
D: 8 for the first). Ask as you walk: "Which strip is one copy? How many copies did you make?" If many make D,
the class counts copies aloud with you, starting with the first strip as copy one.

#### 10 min. Fix two wrong answers

"Fix it", side 1 ([[s10-F80-1]], [[s10-F80-2]]). Pairs, two minutes each to say what went wrong, then the
class. The display shows each wrong working beside the right one. Ray added 8 squares: 8 times as many is 8
copies. Lin drew the 3-strip and then four more in one row: in the long row, the first strip is copy one.

If many still add, pairs build both with cubes, "8 more than 3" and "8 times as many as 3", and say which is
which.

#### 5 min. Pull it together

Whole class, at the two builds from teaching first.

**Ask.** "Where is the 4 in your cubes? Where is the 3?" Expect: "the 3 is the cubes in one strip"; "the 4 is
how many copies". Then point to the 7: "Where is the 4 here?" Expect: "4 single cubes on the end". Show the
copies first, then the single cubes.

#### 5 min. Exit: the slip

Alone, on paper ([[s10-X80]]). Read it aloud once. A student may build it with cubes first, then draw. Record
results on the record sheet under E80. Cubes are put away for the stretch.

### Other rooms

- **No cubes or counters.** Students draw squares on the strip sheet, or cut paper squares; the pairs work stays
  the same, one drawing the small strip and one the copies.
- **One projector, no camera.** Build at the front, then draw the same strips on the board.
- **One child at home.** Beans, coins or bricks. You set out 3; the child sets out 4 times as many, then 4
  more, and says which is which. Then swap roles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop comparison 6 and the challenge. Never drop the fixes, the frames or the cubes for 1 to 3.
Longer: the challenge ([[s10-C80-1]]), drawn with each copy as a box marked 7.

### Words and supports

Word Wall cards: times as many as, copy, strip, more than. Frames: "__ is __ times as many as __." "__ is __
more than __." Access: cubes that snap, sentences read aloud, frames to fill, not copy.

### Tomorrow

On the slip, 7 (error A): the strips show a 2 and a 5 added. 12, or six copies drawn (error D): one copy too
many. "2 is 5 times as many as 10" (error C): roles swapped. Day 81 opens with the readiness reteach if many
missed item 1. Otherwise its first five minutes show the commoner of A and D beside the right one. If both are
needed, that error is talked through inside day 81's notice task. C is fixed on day 81 anyway. Keep the strip
sheets: day 81's notice task starts from today's strips.
