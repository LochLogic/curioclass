# Unit 11.1 record sheet: area and perimeter formulas

One row a student. Each cell gets a tick, or the error letter of the wrong answer (A to E, from the unit
opener). R1 to R4: the readiness check, day 88. E89 to E92: the exits; on day 90 a tick means both parts, by
either path. End 1 to 7: the end check, day 93; items 5 and 6 get one tick for each look-for (item 5: the
length, the right measure for each part, the units; item 6: two sides that add to the same, a reason from
length plus width). A column with more than a third letters is the next day's opening.
