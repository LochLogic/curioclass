# Section 11.1 record sheet: area and perimeter formulas

One row a student; print two for a class over 30. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to F (section opener); s
for a slip (a fact, adding, fraction or zero slip with the right setup); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash
for not reached. R1 to R4: the readiness check, day 88. E89 to E92: the exits; on day 90 a tick means both
parts, by either path. End 1 to 7: the end-of-unit assessment, day 93; items 5 and 6 take one tick for each look-for on the
key (listed under the table). Each head names the standard's part it records.
