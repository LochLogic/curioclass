# Day 90: Find the missing side

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page both sides, the facts check part 2 sheet and the key. Open Community Plot Planner on each
device in Lot planner. Put the display page on the projector.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: facts check, part 2

Alone. The 36 quotients from unit 1, untimed. Collect at 5 minutes; not reached is not wrong. Mark the sheets after class. After the facts check, students under 32 of 36 on day 89 keep their 9
by 9 table out for the lesson.

**If the readiness check showed gaps** (more than a third missed item 1 or 2): the warm-up goes to the reteach in
the section opener, with five minutes from "On your own" and the partner check; the facts check moves to day 91, and
those two questions are dropped; day 91's fixes 3 and 4 ask the same two parts.

**If day 89's tickets showed many A** (area and perimeter swapped): Ava's rug again, "covers" or "goes around"; the facts check moves to day 91. Day 89's 18 (error F) and "24 meters" (error E) also sit near you for the
examples. With the reteach also here, the reteach comes first and A goes to day 91's warm-up.

#### 10 min. Teaching first: two worked examples

Whole class. Students fill the two frames on side 1 as you go. The display shows each figure,
then each step on its own screen.

**Example 1** ([[s11-K90-1]]). The missing side is a missing factor. Each square stands for one square foot.
[[steps:s11-K90-1]]

**Example 2** ([[s11-K90-2]]). [[steps:s11-K90-2]]

Write the four-side equation first, every time: it stops errors B and D.

#### 20 min. Community Plot Planner: the five notes

Alone, in Lot planner. Each family note gives one number and one side; the student finds the missing side, writes
the equation, then tags the plot with the measure the note did not give. Students write each fence
equation as four sides in the Plot notes table on side 2, even where the tool shows 2 × (5 + ?) = 30
([[s11-T90-1]], [[s11-T90-2]], [[s11-T90-3]], [[s11-T90-4]], [[s11-T90-5]]).

Watch for a tag marked "counted" or a plot "built with squares" (the pieces came back), and a missing side that
is the note's two numbers added or taken away (54 or 42 for the Lee family: error C). Ask as you walk: "Which
number is fixed? Which side is missing? What equation says that?"

**Stop at 20 minutes**, lot finished or not: the notes, equations and tags are the job.

#### 15 min. On paper: fix two, then two alone

"Fix it", side 1 ([[s11-F90-1]], [[s11-F90-2]]), 6 minutes. For Jo's, one minute of turn-and-talk in pairs, then
the class. The display shows each wrong working beside the right one. Jo subtracted: area is rows times columns,
so ask "5 times what is 35?". Max took off one long side and stopped: a fence has two long sides and two short ones.

"On your own", side 2 ([[s11-P90-1]], [[s11-P90-2]]), 6 minutes, alone, no grid. Then 3 minutes in pairs:
partners swap and check each answer by putting it back in the equation ("Does 3 × 9 give 27?"). A mismatch gets
a question mark, not the answer.

If many miss the area question: rows of 3 on grid paper until 27 squares are used. If many miss the fence
question: fill in 13 + __ + 13 + __ = 42 together.

#### 5 min. Pull it together

Whole class.

**Ask.** "For the garden bed we never took anything away. For the goat pen we took away and then halved. Why?"
Expect: "area is rows times columns, so it is what times 7"; "the fence has two of each side". Both began with an equation for this rectangle with one unknown.

**Ask.** "How can you check a missing side with no one to tell you?" Expect: put it back in the equation.

#### 5 min. Exit: On your own in Community Plot Planner, or the ticket

Alone. With devices: "On your own" from the tool's header, no hints, the same two parts as the ticket with other
numbers ([[s11-X90t1]], [[s11-X90t2]], [[s11-X90t3]], [[s11-X90t4]]). Students show the end screen; write area and
perimeter results on the record sheet under E90. No devices: the exit ticket ([[s11-X90a]], [[s11-X90b]]).

### Other rooms

- **No devices, or the tool not yet built.** The 20 minutes become the paper path: the five notes in the table on
  side 2, area notes shaded in rows on grid paper, fences sketched with four labelled sides, each checked by
  multiplying back or adding the four sides. Watch for errors C and D; ask the same three questions.
- **One projector.** You run the tool; the class writes each equation on side 2 before you type it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop the partner check (students check their own) and the challenge. Never drop the fixes or the written
equations, and never give the grid back for "On your own". Longer: the challenge ([[s11-C90-1]]), then the
extension ([[s11-C90-2]]).

### Words and supports

Word Wall cards: area, perimeter, length, width, square foot, equation. Frames on the display: "The missing side
is __ because __ × __ = __." "The two short sides are __ feet together, so each one is __ feet."
