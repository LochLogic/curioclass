# Day 93: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the end-of-unit assessment both sides, the key and the record sheet. Open the display; show the answers only at the
marking. Put grid paper out for the groups.

### The day

#### 30 min. End-of-unit assessment

Alone, unaided: no grid paper, tiles or times table. Read each item aloud once. Early finishers check each section. At 30
minutes, pencils down. An item not reached is recorded as not reached, not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Pencils away; show the answers to 1 to 4 and 7. Students tick or circle each in pen or
colored pencil. Items 5 and 6 come back in the last ten minutes.

**Groups, 15 minutes.** Hands up by item sets the groups. A student who missed several joins the group for the
first one missed. Each group fixes its item in pen beside the first try, then checks it: put the side back in the
equation, or add the four sides again. Each group's task is on the display.

- Item 1 (error C): rows of 4 on grid paper until the 28 squares are used.
- Item 2 (error D): fill in 16 + __ + 16 + __ = 50 together.
- Items 3 and 4 (error B): trace and name all four sides, then write l + w + l + w. A right setup with a wrong
  sum is an adding slip: redo the sum only.
- Item 7 (error A or E): sketch it, shade the inside, trace the edge in another color. Area is the shaded part,
  in square centimeters. A student whose day 92 written task missed look-for 1 or 4 joins this group.

Students who missed nothing do the challenge ([[s11-C93-2]]), then the extension ([[s11-C93-3]]), then check a
group's fixes. Collect the checks.

#### 10 min. Items 5 and 6 together

Hand back the checks. Pairs read each other's items 5 and 6 and ask the two questions on the display. Students
add to their own answers in pen; you judge the pencil first try after class.

**Ask.** "Which part asked for the inside, and which for the edge?"

**Ask.** "Do your two sides add to 19? Why does that make the perimeter the same?"

When unit 12's readiness check exists, it takes these ten minutes and items 5 and 6 return on day 94 instead.

### Other rooms

- **No projector.** Write the answers and the group tasks on a covered part of the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No projector: mark from the key. Grid paper: paper squares or a string.

**Skip.** The groups and the challenge.

- **30 min. End-of-unit assessment.** Items 1 to 7, alone, with no squares or times table. Read each item aloud
  once. Not reached is not wrong.
- **15 min. Go over it, one to one.** Mark items 1 to 4 and 7 together from the key. Fix each missed one in pen
  beside the first try: [[s11-E1]] with 28 squares in rows of 4; [[s11-E2]] by filling in
  16 + __ + 16 + __ = 50.

    **Say.** For [[s11-E6]]: "Do your two sides add to 19? Why does that keep the perimeter the same?" Expect:
    length plus width is half the fence.

    Read items 5 and 6 after, ticking each look-for on the key alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the groups to ten minutes, never the check or the marking. Longer: the extension
([[s11-C93-3]]) for everyone who finishes a fix.

### Words and supports

The defaults hold: items read aloud once, large print offered, a scribe or an oral answer on items 5 and 6, a
sketch drawn from a student's words.
