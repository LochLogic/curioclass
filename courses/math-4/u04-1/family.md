# Unit 4 family note: adding parts of the same size

## English

For the next three weeks your child is adding and taking away fractions whose parts are the same size, then
mixed numbers such as 2 1/3, then stories.

**Same size parts.** Two eighths and three eighths make five eighths. The parts are still eighths, so the answer
is 5/8, not 5/16.

**Wholes are parts too.** One whole is three thirds, so 2 1/3 = 3/3 + 3/3 + 1/3 = 7/3.

**Try this at home, five minutes.** Ask your child why two eighths and three eighths make five eighths, not five
sixteenths. Later in the unit, ask them to show you how 2 and 1/3 can be written as thirds. If you know a quick
rule, hold it back: the class finds it from the pieces.
