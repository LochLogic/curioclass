# Unit 3 record sheet: equivalent fractions and comparing

One row a student, for days 9 to 25; print two for a class over 24. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to K
(the section openers); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for not reached. R1 to R4: the readiness check, day 9.
E10 to E23: the exits; a tick means every part of the ticket. W12 to W23: the written tasks, judged after class and
handed back the next day (W23 when pencils go down on day 24): one tick a look-for (listed under the table). End 3.1a to 3.4a: the end-of-unit
assessment, day 24; 3.1b and 3.3a take one tick a look-for, marked after class and handed back on day 25. Each head
names the parts of the standard it records.
