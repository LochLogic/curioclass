# Day 86: Which kind of comparison?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page both sides, about 8 strip sheets, the exit slips and the key. Write the warm-up
problem on the board. Put the display page on the projector.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: one grade 3 two-step problem

Alone, in notebooks. [[s10-P86-9]] Students write two short equations (56 stickers, then 47), then one with a letter, and say what the
letter stands for. Watch for 56, one step only: ask "Is that what the question asked?"

#### 3 min. Day 85's exit back

Whole class. **If day 85's slips showed many C** ("Jon read 4 times as many"): say the answer as a full sentence,
"Pat read 4 times as many pages as Jon", and test it: draw Jon's 5-strip, then four copies. The long strip is
Pat's, so Pat's name goes first. Otherwise one student reads the right sentence and points to the longer strip.
Students who wrote 15 or 100 sit near you for the sort.

#### 5 min. Teaching first: two problems that look alike

Whole class. Students fill the two frames on side 1 as you go. Ask "Copies or extra?" before any working.

**Example 1** ([[s10-K86-1]]). [[steps:s10-K86-1]]

**Example 2** ([[s10-K86-2]]). [[steps:s10-K86-2]] Say steps 2, 4 and 5 only; the display shows them all.

**Ask.** "Both problems use 8 and 6. Why are the answers so different?" Expect: "times as many is 6 copies of
8"; "more than is 8 and 6 extra".

Say: from today, draw the strip only when you are stuck. When you are sure, say what it would look like.

#### 18 min. Sort cards on paper (Growth Spurt, Which one?, when it exists)

Alone. The eight cards on side 1 ([[s10-P86-1]], [[s10-P86-2]], [[s10-P86-3]], [[s10-P86-4]], [[s10-P86-5]],
[[s10-P86-6]], [[s10-P86-7]], [[s10-P86-8]]). For each card: who has more? What does the
story say or ask: times as many (copies), or more than (extra)? The student says what the strip would look like, then writes the card
number in the sort table. A student who cannot say it draws it on the strip sheet. Nobody solves yet.

At 12 minutes, read the kinds aloud from the key, card by card; students tick or fix their own. Cards 2, 5 and 7
are "more than". On card 7 say: "49 is 7 copies of 7, and also 7 and 42 extra. The question decides: it asks how
many more."

Check (sort): Listen to each kind. Tick it or fix it.

Watch for card 7 sorted "times as many": 49 and 7 look like a division. Ask "Does it ask how many more, or how
many times?" On card 2, ask "Longer by copies, or by 7 feet?" If many miss cards 5 and 7, draw card 7 both ways
on the board and ask which picture the words make.

#### 12 min. Solve four cards, then a partner check

Alone, 9 minutes: cards 3, 4, 5 and 7 on side 2, each with a letter equation and a sentence answer. Then 3
minutes in pairs: partners swap and check by rereading the story with the answer in it ("Is 34 pages 9 fewer
than 43?"). A mismatch gets a question mark, not the answer.

Watch for 144 on card 3 (error B): ask "Who has more? The answer must fit the longer strip." On 392 for card 4,
ask "392 times? Is the dog as heavy as 392 cats?"
Watch for 52 on card 5 (error C): Sam read fewer pages than Ivy. If many miss card 3, draw 36 as four equal
pieces with s under one piece.

#### 10 min. Written task: two stories, the same children

Alone, 7 minutes: [[s10-W86]] Look-fors: [[steps:s10-W86]]

Then 3 minutes, whole class: two students read theirs; the class says which is which.

**Ask.** "How did you make the 2 mean copies in one story and extra in the other?" Expect: "times as many as" in
one, "more than" in the other.

Read the rest after class against the key's samples.

#### 7 min. Exit: the slip

Alone. [[s10-X86v2]] Read it aloud once. A strip only if stuck, on the back. Collect the slips for tomorrow.

### Other rooms

- **No devices, or the tool not yet built.** The sort cards are the paper path. When
  it exists: Which one? for the same 18 minutes, stop at 18. Students still say the strip in words before they
  tap a kind.
- **One projector.** Read one card at a time aloud; students hold up one finger for times as many, two for
  more than, then show the key screen.
- **One child at home.** Read each card aloud. The child says the kind and builds it with beans only if
  unsure. Solve cards 3 and 7 together.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: solve cards 3 and 7 only; keep the whole sort, the written task and the exit. Longer: the challenge ([[s10-C86-1]]), then a card of the student's own for a
partner to sort.

### Words and supports

Word Wall cards: times as many, times as much, more than, longer than, how many times. Frames, printed on side 2:
"______ has ___ times as many ______ as ______." "______ has ___ more ______ than ______." Read any card aloud when asked; a student may retell it
in a home language first.

### Tomorrow

Day 87 opens with readiness item 3's reteach if many missed it; otherwise its first 5 minutes go to this exit.
On the slip, melon 96 (error B), or melon 20 or squash 96 (error A, the two kinds mixed): day 87 redraws the
pumpkin as 4 copies of the melon, then the squash as the pumpkin plus a piece of 4. Squash 10: the 4 went on the
melon; reread whose weight. The two-stories task goes back in day 87's first five minutes. Both stories one kind
(error A): those students join the A redraw. Check first, in day 87's problems, the students who drew most strips
today.
