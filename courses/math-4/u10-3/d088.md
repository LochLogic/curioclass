# Day 88: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the end check both sides, the key, the record sheet and stretch 11's readiness check. Have the marked day
87 slips ready to hand back. Open the display; show the answers only at the marking. Put strip sheets out.
Cover the comparison frame on the wall.

### The day

#### 30 min. End check

Hand back the day 87 slips at the door, face down. Alone, unaided: strips may be drawn, no cubes or times
table. Read each item aloud once. At 30 minutes, pencils down; an item not reached is not reached, not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Pencils away; show the answers to 1 to 6. Students tick or circle each in pen. Items 7 and
8 you judge after class; students answer the two questions on the F to H screen, and a "no" joins that group.
So does a day 87 slip with a look-for missed.

**Groups, 15 minutes.** Hands up sets the groups; a student in two goes to the earlier one. Each group fixes
its items in pen beside the first try. Tasks are on the display.

- A and D (13 or 48 on 3, 28 on 4, 24 on 5, an addition on 1 or 2, Lee and Kim alike): draw the cat as an
  8-strip, the dog as copies under it. Count copies aloud, the first as one. Beside it, draw 5 more than 8.
- B and C (128 on 4, 81 on 5, roles swapped on 1 or 2): sort items 3, 4 and 5 by what is unknown. Ask "who
  has more?" and mark the longer strip first.
- F to H (7, 8, or the day 87 slip: 90, 248, 4,650, or a letter never named). Did your letter say what it stands for? Is your answer near an estimate?
  Write the two steps as two equations, then join them, multiplying first.

Students who missed nothing do [[s10-C88-1]], then [[s10-C88-2]]. Collect the checks.

#### 10 min. Stretch 11's readiness check

Alone, with grid paper for item 4. Read each item aloud once. Collect at 10 minutes.

### Other rooms

- **No projector.** Write the answers and group tasks on a covered part of the board.
- **One child at home.** Give the check, go over each miss with strips, then the readiness check.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups ten minutes, never the check, marking or readiness check. Longer: both challenges for anyone
who finishes a fix.

### Words and supports

The defaults hold; items 1, 7 and 8 may be answered aloud. A drawn strip is offered, not cubes.

### Tomorrow

After class: record 1 to 6 from the pencil first try, then judge 7 and 8, each look-for alone, and mark R1 to R4.
