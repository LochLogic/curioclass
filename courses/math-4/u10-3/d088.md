# Day 88: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print everything on the print list. Open the display; show the answers only at the marking. Put strip sheets out for the groups.
Cover the comparison frame on the wall.

### The day

#### 30 min. End check

Keep the day 87 slips until pencils go down. Alone, unaided: strips may be drawn in the work boxes, no strip
sheets, cubes or times table. Read each item aloud once. At 30 minutes, pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Pencils away; hand back the day 87 slips (and any day 86 slips); show the answers
to 1 to 6. Students tick or circle each in pen. You judge 7 and 8 after class; students answer the two questions on the F to H screen, and a "no" joins that group.

**Groups, 15 minutes.** Hands up sets the groups; a student in two joins the earlier one. Each group fixes its items in pen. Tasks are on the display.

- A and D (13 or 48 on 3, 28 on 4, 24 on 5, an addition on 1 or 2, Lee and Kim alike or
  swapped, or day 86's slip: melon 20 or squash 96): draw the cat as an
  8-strip, the dog as copies under it. Count copies aloud, the first as one. Beside it, draw 5 more than 8.
- B and C (128 on 4, 81 on 5, roles swapped on 1 or 2, or day 86's slip: melon 96): sort items 3, 4
  and 5 by what is unknown. Ask "who has more?" and mark the longer strip first. C: say the bigger amount first
  and write it alone: 210 = 7 × 30.
- F to H (7, 8, or a day 87 look-for missed, as error G). Underline the question. Is your answer what it asks? Did your letter say what it stands for? Write the two steps as two equations, then join them, multiplying first.

Students who missed nothing do [[s10-C88-1]], then [[s10-C88-3]]. Collect the checks.

#### 10 min. Stretch 11's readiness check

Alone, with grid paper for item 4. Read each item aloud once.

### Other rooms

- **No projector.** Answers and group tasks on a covered part of the board.
- **One child at home.** The defaults, then the readiness check.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups ten minutes, never the check, marking or readiness check. 

### Words and supports

The defaults hold; items 1, 7 and 8 may be answered aloud.
