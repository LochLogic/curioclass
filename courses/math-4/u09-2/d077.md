# Day 77: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print everything on the print list. Mark the day 76 tickets; judge the written tasks. Open the display; show the check's answers only at
the marking. Take down the place names.

### The day

#### 10 min. Mixed set (Road Trip Logbook when it exists)

Alone, on the day 77 mixed set: [[s09-T77-1]], [[s09-T77-2]], [[s09-T77-3]], [[s09-T77-4]], [[s09-T77-5]],
[[s09-T77-6]], [[s09-T77-7]], [[s09-T77-8]], [[s09-T77-9]], [[s09-T77-10]]. When it exists: Road Trip
Logbook's Trip ledger, the same ten, stopped at 10 minutes. Until then, paper, worked on grid paper: each
answer is checked against the display key. Not finished is fine.

Check: Right on the first try? Tick it. If not, fix it in pen.

#### 30 min. End-of-unit assessment

Keep the day 76 tickets and written tasks until pencils go down. Alone, unaided. At 30 minutes, pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Hand back the day 76 tickets and written tasks. Show the answers screen; students tick or
circle each unit assessment item in pen. You judge 3 and 6 after class. A ticket with 23,776 or 22,675 (I) or 37,324
(F), or a written task with zeros as 10s, joins that group.

**Groups, 15 minutes.** Students find their wrong answer on the groups screen and go to that group; a student in
two goes to the subtracting one. A wrong answer on no list: bring it to me. Each group fixes its items in pen on
the column sheet.

- A and D (short by 10, 100, 1,000 or 10,000 on 1 or 2, 111,225, or two digits in one column): item 1 in expanded
  form beside the algorithm, each carry said by its value: "15 ones is 1 ten and 5 ones." Then fix 1 and 2.
- E (22,335 or 11,225 on 1): estimate first; it must pass 100,000. Fix 1.
- B (1,253,595 on 2, 127,625 on 5, or Dev's 15,998 let stand on 3): place names over the columns; say both
  numbers aloud, estimate, then redo 2 and 5.
- F to H (55,254, 55,854, 44,854, 45,754 or 54,754 on 4, 647,163 on 5, Rosa's 6,532 let stand on 6): in item
  4, ask "can I take 6 from 1?"; ungroup everything first, then subtract and add back.
- I (563,047 or 552,037 on 5, 5,578 on 6): say 8,004 as 7 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 14 ones before any column. Fix
  6, then 5, each checked by adding.

Students who missed nothing do [[s09-C77-1]], then [[s09-C77-2]]. Collect the checks.

### Other rooms

- **No projector.** The mixed set's key, the answers and the group tasks on a covered part of the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No projector: mark from the key in this view. Grid paper: lined paper turned sideways.

**Skip.** The mixed set, the groups and the challenge.

- **30 min. End-of-unit assessment.** Items 1 to 6, alone, with no help. Read an item aloud once if asked. Not
  reached is not wrong.
- **15 min. Go over it, one to one.** Mark items 1, 2, 4 and 5 together from the key. Fix each missed one in pen
  beside the first try: [[s09-E1v2]] with each small 1 said by its worth; [[s09-E5v2]] by renaming the whole top
  number in one line first, then adding back.

    **Say.** For [[s09-E6]]: "Add Rosa's answer to 2,536. Do you get 8,004?" Expect: no, 9,068, so she is
    wrong; 5,468.

    Read items 3 and 6 after, ticking each look-for on the key alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the mixed set to six problems, three of each kind, and groups ten minutes. Never the check or the
marking.

### Words and supports

The defaults hold, plus large-square grid paper and typed columns; items 3 and 6 may be answered aloud.
