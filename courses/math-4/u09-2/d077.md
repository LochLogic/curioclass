# Day 77: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print everything on the print list. Mark the day 76 slips. Open the display; show the check's answers only at
the marking. Take down the place names.

### The day

#### 10 min. Mixed set (Road Trip Logbook when it exists)

Alone, on the day 77 mixed set: [[s09-T77-1]], [[s09-T77-2]], [[s09-T77-3]], [[s09-T77-4]], [[s09-T77-5]],
[[s09-T77-6]], [[s09-T77-7]], [[s09-T77-8]], [[s09-T77-9]], [[s09-T77-10]]. It is the warm-up. When it exists:
Road Trip Logbook's Trip ledger, the same ten, stopped at 10 minutes. Until then, paper: each answer is checked
against the key on the display once done, ticked or fixed in pen. Students write a right-first-try count,
compared only with their own last one. Not finished is fine; the sheets stay with students.

Check: Right on the first try? Tick it. If not, fix it in pen.

#### 30 min. End check

Keep the day 76 slips until pencils go down. Alone, unaided. Read each item aloud once. At 30 minutes, pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Pencils away; hand back the day 76 slips; show the answers to 1, 2, 4 and 5. Students tick
or circle each in pen. You judge 3 and 6 after class. A day 76 slip with 23,776 (I) or 37,324 (F) joins that
group.

**Groups, 15 minutes.** Hands up sets the groups; a student in an adding group and a subtracting group goes to
the subtracting one. Each group fixes its items in pen on the column sheet. Tasks are on the display.

- A and D (short by 10, 100, 1,000 or 10,000 on 1 or 2, or two digits in one column): item 1 in expanded
  form beside the algorithm, each carry said by its value: "15 ones is 1 ten and 5 ones." Then fix 1 and 2.
- B (1,253,595 on 2, 127,625 on 5, or Dev's 15,998 let stand on 3): place names over the columns; say both
  numbers aloud, estimate, then redo 2 and 5.
- F to H (55,254, 55,854, 44,854, 45,754 or 54,754 on 4, 647,163 on 5, Rosa's 6,532 let stand on 6): in item
  4, ask "can I take 6 from 1?" Ungroup everything first, saying what each place became, then subtract. Check
  by adding.
- I (563,047 on 5, 5,578 on 6): say 8,004 as 7 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 14 ones before any column. Fix
  6, then 5, each checked by adding.

Students who missed nothing do [[s09-C77-1]], then [[s09-C77-2]]. Collect the checks.

### Other rooms

- **No projector.** The mixed set's key, the answers and the group tasks on a covered part of the board.
- **One child at home.** The defaults.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the mixed set to six problems, three of each kind, and groups ten minutes. Never the check or the
marking.

### Words and supports

The defaults hold, plus large-square grid paper and typed columns; items 3 and 6 may be answered aloud.
