# Unit 3 family note: comparing two fractions

## English

For the last six school days of this unit your child is comparing two fractions by giving them parts of the same
size, or the same number of parts. The unit ends with a short assessment.

**Same size parts.** Which is more, 2/3 or 7/12? Cut each third into 4: 2/3 = 8/12. Now both are twelfths, and 8
twelfths is more than 7, so 2/3 > 7/12.

**Try this at home, five minutes.** Use a ruler. Ask: "Which is longer, 3/4 inch or 5/8 inch?" Your child finds
both marks, then renames 3/4 as eighths to check. Ask them to read the answer aloud with the sign, "3/4 is greater
than 5/8", and say how they know.
