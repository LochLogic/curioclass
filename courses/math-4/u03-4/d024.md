# Day 24: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Stack the day 23 papers by name, held until pencils go down. Cover the Word Wall's comparing cards. Strip
envelopes stay in desks until the groups; put plain paper out.

### The day

#### 30 min. End-of-unit assessment

Alone: no strips, cards or printed lines; a sketched line is allowed. Read each item aloud once. At 30 minutes, pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Pencils away; hand back the day 23 papers; show answers to 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7.
Students mark in pen, matching wrong answers to the group list. Say: "Raise a hand if your item 6 sentence
differs; I will check it."

**Groups, 15 minutes.** Hands up sets the groups (in two: join the earlier). Fixes go in pen in the
fixes box. Tasks are on the display.

- A, C and K (item 1: 4/6, 9/6 or 8/12; item 3: 6/100; item 4: first blank 7, second 5, third 11; item 7: only
  the bottom renamed): fold a thirds strip into sixths; lay it on the line from 0,
  count one whole's parts, then on past 1. For item 7, cut one sixth into 5 and count the parts in
  5/6.
- B and E (item 1: 6/5; item 3: 8/12, 3/5 left out, or "no"; item 4: first blank 13, second 11, third 101): mark
  your answer and the given fraction on one line. Adding moves the point; cutting does not. (ii): name both
  with smaller numbers. Left out 3/5: group the tenths in twos.
- D, G and J (item 7, or a day 23 ticket saying Ben, a turned sign, or sixths as bigger pieces): tenths beside
  twelfths strips: more parts, each smaller. Say each pair as parts of a size, then as a sentence; the open side
  faces the greater.
- H (item 6 "yes", or a day 23 "cannot tell"): fold two strips of different length in half; lay the halves
  together. "Half of what?" Then the trail story: one trail, one whole, so you can tell.

Students who missed nothing do [[s03-C24-1]], then [[s03-C24-2]], then check a group's fixes.

#### 10 min. Unit 4's readiness check

Alone, on paper, no strips: unit 4's readiness check, four items, each read aloud once. Collect the sheets and the assessment pages at the end.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip envelope: fold paper strips.

**Skip.** The groups, the challenges, and unit 4's readiness check (give it the next morning).

- **30 min. End-of-unit assessment.** Items 1 to 7, alone, no strips, each read aloud once.
- **15 min. Go over it, one to one.** Mark items 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 together from the key; judge 2 and 5 from its
  look-fors. Fix each missed one in pen beside the first try: [[s03-E1]] by folding a thirds strip into sixths
  and counting the parts from 0; [[s03-E4]] by asking how many parts each part was cut into, top and bottom
  alike; [[s03-E6]] by folding two strips of different length in half.

    **Say.** For [[s03-E7v2]]: "Read each answer aloud as a sentence. Does the open side face the greater
    fraction?" Expect: 5/6 is greater than 7/10; 3/10 is less than 1/3; 7/4 equals 14/8.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups ten minutes, never the assessment, marking or readiness check. Longer: the challenges.
