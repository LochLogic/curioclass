# Facts check, part 1: products

## Student

Write each answer. Skip any you do not know, and come back if there is time.

## Teacher

The 36 products of the facts from 2 × 2 to 9 × 9, each pair once (stretch 1, s01.md). Given in a warm-up on paper:
part 1 on day 3 and again on day 89, part 2 on day 4 and again on day 90. No clock is shown. Collect at 5 minutes;
an item not reached is left blank, not marked wrong. Count the right answers. A blank is not marked x; it is simply
not counted. Write the number right under F1 on the stretch 1 record sheet. The mark (ours, untested): 32 of 36. Under 32
right keeps a 9 by 9 table out for that day's lesson, whatever the reason. Fact help goes into the warm-ups, not
the lessons.
