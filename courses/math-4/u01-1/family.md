# Unit 1 family note: factors and multiples

## English

For the next six school days your child is finding every pair of numbers that multiply to make a number. For
36 the pairs are 1 × 36, 2 × 18, 3 × 12, 4 × 9 and 6 × 6. These are its factor pairs.

**A factor pair is a rectangle.** 36 squares can make a rectangle 4 rows by 9. Turned on its side it is 9 by 4,
the same rectangle, so 4 × 9 and 9 × 4 count once.

Later in the week your child learns that 36 is a multiple of 4, and that a number with only one rectangle, like
7, is called prime.

**Try this at home, five minutes.** Put out 24 coins, beans or buttons. Ask your child how many different
rectangles they can make, with every row the same length. Ask how they know they found them all.
