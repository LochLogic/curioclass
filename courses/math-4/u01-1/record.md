# Unit 1 record sheet: factors and multiples

One row a student, for days 1 to 7; printed on day 1, two for a class over 17. Each cell gets a tick; an error letter A to G
(the section openers); s for a slip (a fact slip with the right search); x for any other wrong answer; or a dash for
not reached. R1 to R3: the readiness items, day 1 (R1 is the number right out of 20). E1 to E5: the exits; on
day 2 a tick means all three look-fors, on days 3 and 4 both parts. W3 and W5: the written tasks of days 3 and 5,
one tick for each look-for on the row (listed under the table). F1 and F2: the facts check, parts 1 and 2 (days
3 and 4), the number right out of 36. End 1 to 6: the end-of-unit assessment, day 6; items 5 and 6
take one tick for each look-for, marked after class and handed back on day 7. Each head names the standard's
part it records.
