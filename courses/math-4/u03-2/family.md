# Unit 3 family note: why multiplying top and bottom works

## English

For the next four school days your child is learning the rule for equal fractions, and why it works.

**Cutting every part.** Cut each fifth of a strip into 2 pieces. There are now 2 times as many pieces in the whole,
and 2 times as many in the shaded part: 2/5 = (2 × 2)/(2 × 5) = 4/10. The pieces are smaller, so the amount is the
same. Going back the other way, pieces are put in equal groups: 4/10 = 2/5.

**Try this at home, five minutes.** Use a ruler. Find 1/2 inch. Ask your child why 2/4 inch and 4/8 inch land on
the same mark. Then ask: "Is 4/8 four times as much as 1/2?" Listen for the reason: the same mark, so the same
amount.
