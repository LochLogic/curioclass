# Day 8: Shape patterns, and the 10th step without drawing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print everything on the print list. Put the display page on the projector. Write the two shape tapes on a covered
part of the board: "Start at 3 tiles. Add 2. 3, 5, 7" and "Start at 2 tiles. Add 5. 2, 7, 12". Sort day 7's
tickets by error. Lay out squares and sticks for each pair.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: factor pairs and multiples, or the reteach

Alone, top of side 1 ([[s02-W8-1]], [[s02-W8-2]], [[s02-W8-3]]). Read the answers aloud at 4 minutes; students
check their own in pen.

Students who missed a readiness item sit with you for the opener's reteach: item 1 or 2, skip count by 6 on the 1
to 100 chart; item 3, counters put in pairs.

#### 5 min. Reteach, or E7 back

**If many missed a readiness item** (more than a third): the warm-up and these 5 minutes are its reteach, whole
class.

**Otherwise, if day 7's tickets showed many C or E** ("It adds 6", or 65 as the reason): cover the rule on the
board and list what is true of 29, 35, 41 by themselves. Then dot pairs for one jump of 6: three pairs, so the one
left over stays alone. Finish together: "It must keep going because __".

**Many A** (6 or 35 first): circle the start on five rules on the board, write it first, then jump. These students
build step 1 first in every shape today and write its count before any jump.

#### 3 min. Fix it 1: two numbers are not a feature

Whole class, on day 7's doubling list (recording page, side 1, pattern 4). [[s02-F8-1]] [[steps:s02-F8-1]] If many
named a feature from two or three numbers: write eight numbers before saying anything.

#### 8 min. Teaching first: columns of 2

Pairs build with squares; you build under the camera. Students write the counts in shape pattern 1 on the
recording page.

**Example 1** ([[s02-K8-1]]). [[steps:s02-K8-1]]

**Ask** (after step 4). "How many squares in the 10th step? Can you tell without drawing it?" Expect: 20, and "2,
then 9 more twos". If you hear 22: count the jumps from step 1 to step 3, two, not three.

#### 12 min. Rule Machine, on paper: two shape tapes

Alone, on the recording page, side 2, the shape tapes ([[s02-T8-1]], [[s02-T8-2]]). When it exists: Rule Machine,
the shape tapes, practise with feedback. Until then, uncover tape 1 on the board. Students copy the start, the rule
and the three counts, write the next five, then the 10th step on side 1. At 5 minutes show the list and the 10th
step; each checks their own in pen. Then tape 2 the same way.

Watch for 20 or 50: the start left out (error A); ask "how many tiles in step 1?" For 23 or 52, ten jumps: ask
"how many jumps from step 1 to step 3?"

**Stop at 12 minutes.**

#### 22 min. By hand: sticks, a plus sign, and shapes that repeat

**Sticks, 7 minutes.** Pairs build stick squares, steps 1 to 3, in shape pattern 2 on the recording page: 4, 7,
10. Then Fix it 2 on side 2 ([[s02-F8-2]]), pairs, then the class. [[steps:s02-F8-2]]

**Ask.** "How many sticks in the 10th shape? Can you tell without drawing it?" Expect: 4, then 9 more threes, 31.
Set 30 (ten threes) and 34 (ten jumps) against step 1's 4 sticks. If many still draw: say step 10 in words
first, "the first shape and how many jumps?"

**On your own, 9 minutes.** Side 2, alone. The plus sign ([[s02-P8-1]]): squares in hand, shape pattern 3 on the
recording page. Then the repeating shapes ([[s02-P8-2]]), drawn in a row in the box. Watch for 40 or 41 on
the plus sign, as on the tapes, and "it adds 4" as the feature (error C): cover the rule. Squares at the 3rd, 6th:
ask "which shape is 2nd?"

**Written task, 6 minutes.** Side 2, alone ([[s02-W8]]). Collect it to judge after class with its two look-fors:
says Sam is right; says the triangles sit at the multiples of 3 and 30 is 3 × 10. A student who drew thirty shapes
has the first only (error B).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Alone, on paper ([[s02-X8]]). Read it aloud once. A tick needs the five counts, a feature that is not the rule, and
a reason about the rows of 3. Record results on the record sheet under E8.

### Other rooms

- **No squares or sticks.** Squares drawn on the recording page; sticks as pencil marks, a short line a side.
- **No printer.** Write the tapes, the stems and the frames on the board; students rule boxes in a notebook.

### At home

**Swap.** No squares: paper squares torn from scrap. No sticks: toothpicks or pencil marks. No projector: point at
the page.

**Skip.** The warm-up, the reteach, the tapes, the repeating shapes and the written task.

- **5 min. Fix it 1.** [[s02-F8-1]]: the child writes eight numbers before saying what is true.
- **10 min. The example.** Read [[s02-K8-1]] together. Build steps 1 to 3 with paper squares; the child writes each
  count under its step on the recording page, shape pattern 1.

    **Say.** "How many squares in the 10th step? Can you tell without drawing it?" Expect: 2, then 9 more twos, 20.

- **8 min. Fix it 2.** Build stick squares, steps 1 to 3, with toothpicks. Then [[s02-F8-2]].

    **Say.** "How many sticks in the 10th shape? Can you tell without drawing it?" Expect: 4, then 9 more threes,
    31.

- **8 min. On your own.** [[s02-P8-1]], on shape pattern 3: count first, then a feature and why.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** The child answers [[s02-X8]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop tape 2 and the repeating shapes. Never drop Fix it 2 or the written task; every shape today ends
with "the start and how many jumps". Longer: a pair makes its own shape rule with sticks or squares, builds steps
1 to 3, and finds its 10th step; the other pair checks it with "the start and 9 jumps".

### Words and supports

Word Wall card: step, beside the rule, pattern and term cards from day 7. Frames, on side 1: "I notice __." "The
10th step is the start and __ jumps." Access: a student who cannot build directs a partner; reasons may be said
aloud to you or a scribe.

### Tomorrow

On the ticket, 3, 6, 9 first (error A), or all five steps drawn and counted (error B): watch these students on
day 9's items 1, 4 and 6; a miss puts them in the A and B group. "It adds 3" (error C): the C and D group if they
miss item 2. More numbers as the reason: read beside items 3 and 5. The written task is judged tonight and handed
back on day 9.
