# Day 8: Shape patterns, and the 10th step without drawing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print everything on the print list. Put the display page on the projector. Write the two shape tapes on a covered
part of the board: "Start at 3 tiles. Add 2. 3, 5, 7" and "Start at 2 tiles. Add 5. 2, 7, 12". Sort day 7's
tickets by error. Lay out squares and sticks.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: factor pairs and multiples, or the reteach

Alone, top of side 1 ([[s02-W8-1]], [[s02-W8-2]], [[s02-W8-3]]). At 4 minutes, show the answers screen; students
check their own in pen.

Students who missed a readiness item sit with you for the opener's reteach: item 1 or 2, skip count by 6 on the 1
to 100 chart; item 3, counters put in pairs.

#### 5 min. Reteach, or E7 back

**If many missed a readiness item** (more than a third): the warm-up and these 5 minutes are its reteach, whole
class.

Otherwise, hand back day 7's tickets. Show the answer to E7 on the board: 29, 35, 41, 47, 53, 59, all odd, an odd
number plus 6 is odd. Students fix in pen beside the first try.

**If many C, D or E** ("because it adds 6", "take turns", or 65 as the reason): cover the rule and list what is
true of 29, 35, 41 by themselves; take turns fails at 35. Then dot pairs for one jump of 6: three pairs, so the one
left over stays alone. Finish together: "It must keep going because __".

**Many A** (6 or 35 first): circle the start on five rules (start 5, add 2; start 7, add 10; start 4, add 6; start
10, add 3; start 6, add 4), write it first, then jump. These students build step 1 first in every shape today.

#### 3 min. Fix it 1: two numbers are not a feature

Whole class, on day 7's doubling list (recording page, side 1, pattern 4). [[s02-F8-1]] [[steps:s02-F8-1]] If many
named a feature from two or three numbers: students look at the eight numbers already in pattern 4 and say what is
true of them before anyone says odd or even.

#### 8 min. Teaching first: columns of 2

You draw the steps on the board, and pairs build with squares. Students write the counts in shape pattern 1 on
the recording page.

**Example 1** ([[s02-K8-1]]). [[steps:s02-K8-1]]

**Ask.** After step 3: "How many squares in the 10th step? Can you tell without drawing it?" Expect: 20, and "2,
then 9 more twos". If you hear 22: count the jumps from step 1 to step 3, two, not three.

#### 10 min. Rule Machine, on paper: two shape tapes

Alone, on the recording page, side 2, the shape tapes ([[s02-T8-1]], [[s02-T8-2]]). When it exists: Rule Machine,
the shape tapes, practise with feedback. Until then, uncover tape 1 on the board. Students copy the tape, write the
next five counts, then the 10th step on side 1. At 5 minutes show the answers; each checks their own in pen. Then tape 2 the same way.

Watch for 20 or 50: the start left out (error A); ask "how many tiles in step 1?" For 23 or 52, ten jumps: ask
"how many jumps from step 1 to step 3?"

**Stop at 10 minutes.**

#### 24 min. By hand: sticks, a plus sign, and shapes that repeat

**Sticks, 7 minutes.** Pairs build stick squares, steps 1 to 3, in shape pattern 2 on the recording page: 4, 7,
10.

**Ask.** "How many sticks in the 10th shape? Can you tell without drawing it?" Pairs write a guess first. Expect:
4, then 9 more threes, 31. Set 30 (ten threes) and 34 (ten jumps) against step 1's 4 sticks. If many still draw:
say step 10 in words first, "the first shape and how many jumps?"

Then Fix it 2 on side 2 ([[s02-F8-2]]) as the check, pairs, then the class. [[steps:s02-F8-2]]

**On your own, 11 minutes.** Side 2, alone. The plus sign ([[s02-P8-1]]): squares in hand, shape pattern 3 on the
recording page. Then the repeating shapes ([[s02-P8-2]]), drawn in a row in the box. Watch for 40 or 41 on
the plus sign, and "it adds 4" as the feature (error C): cover the rule. Squares at the 3rd, 6th:
ask "which shape is 2nd?"

**Written task, 6 minutes.** Side 2, alone ([[s02-W8]]). Collect it to judge after class with the key's two
look-fors. A student who drew thirty shapes has the first only (error B).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Alone, on paper ([[s02-X8v2]]). Read it aloud once. Tick each look-for alone: the four counts and 36 for the 10th
step; a feature that is not the rule; a reason that says what one row of 3 does to it. Record under E8.

### Other rooms

- **No squares or sticks.** Squares drawn on the recording page; sticks as pencil marks, a short line a side.
- **No printer.** Write the tapes, the stems and the frames on the board; students rule boxes in a notebook.

### At home

**Swap.** No squares: paper squares torn from scrap. No sticks: toothpicks or pencil marks. No projector: point at
the page.

**Skip.** The warm-up, the reteach, the tapes and the repeating shapes.

- **5 min. Fix it 1.** [[s02-F8-1]]: the child writes eight numbers before saying what is true.
- **10 min. The example.** Read [[s02-K8-1]] together. Build steps 1 to 3 with paper squares; the child writes each
  count under its step on the recording page, shape pattern 1.

    **Say.** "How many squares in the 10th step? Can you tell without drawing it?" Expect: 2, then 9 more twos, 20.

- **8 min. Fix it 2.** Build stick squares, steps 1 to 3, with toothpicks. Then [[s02-F8-2]].

    **Say.** "How many sticks in the 10th shape? Can you tell without drawing it?" Expect: 4, then 9 more threes,
    31.

- **6 min. On your own.** [[s02-P8-1]], on shape pattern 3: count first, then a feature and why.
- **3 min. Written task.** [[s02-W8]], alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** The child answers [[s02-X8v2]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop tape 2 and the repeating shapes. Never drop Fix it 2 or the written task; every shape today ends
with "the start and how many jumps". Longer: pairs make a shape rule, build steps 1 to 3, find the 10th, and swap
to check.

### Words and supports

Word Wall card: step. Frames, on side 1: "I notice __." "The
10th step is the start and __ jumps." Access: a student who cannot build directs a partner; reasons may be said
aloud to you or a scribe.

### Tomorrow

On the ticket, 3, 6, 9 first or the 10th as 30 (error A), or all ten steps drawn (error B): watch these students
on day 9's items 1, 4 and 6; a miss puts them in the A and B group. "It adds 3" (error C): the C and D group if they
miss item 2. More numbers as the reason: read beside items 3 and 5. The written task is judged tonight and handed
back on day 9.
