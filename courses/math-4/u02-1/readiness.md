# Ready for number patterns

## Student

Write your answers on this sheet. For item 2, you may write or draw.

## Teacher

The last ten minutes of day 6, after the day's exit ticket. Alone, on paper, all written; no counters and no
chart. Read each item aloud once. Mark after class, before day 7, and write each result on the unit 2 record sheet
(R1 to R3): a tick, the error letter where the key gives one, or x for any other wrong answer. Item 1 needs all
four numbers for a tick, and item 3 all three even numbers with no odd one circled.

"Many" means more than a third of the class. All three items serve section 2.1, and their reteach is in its
opener. It runs in day 8's warm-up and first five minutes: for the whole class if many missed an item, otherwise
for the students who missed it, during the warm-up. The results are in hand on day 7, so you know whom to watch.

- **Item 1, jumping by the same amount.** The signs: 19, 20, 21, 22 (counting on by ones), or one jump of 6
  slipped with the rest built on it (a slip: count back by 6 to check). What it means for day 7: every list that
  day is made by the same jump, and a student who cannot yet jump by 6 spends the lesson on the adding, not on
  what the numbers show. Watch these students on day 7's first rule. Reteach: skip count by 6 on a 1 to 100
  chart, then ask "what is true of every number you landed on?"
- **Item 2, a reason from pairs.** The signs: "Yes, because 12, 20 and 28 are even" (examples, not a reason,
  error E), or "No". What it means: day 7's reasons are this kind of reason, and E is the error day 7 meets head
  on. A student who gave examples here is the one to ask "how do you know about the ones you have not written?"
  Reteach with item 1: four rows of dots, put in pairs; none is ever left over.
- **Item 3, even and odd.** The signs: 3, 11, 15 and 17 circled (even and odd swapped), or one even number missed
  or one odd number circled. What it means: even and odd are the features students notice most on days 7 and 8.
  A student who cannot yet tell them apart has nothing to notice. Reteach: counters put in pairs; none left over
  is even, one left over is odd. Give these students counters on day 7, before the reteach.

Day 7 does not wait for the reteach: it opens with dot pairs, the same move as items 2 and 3. Keep the record
sheet where you will see it on days 7 and 8.
