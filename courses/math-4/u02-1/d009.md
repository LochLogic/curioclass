# Day 9: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Stack the day 8 written tasks by name, held until pencils go down. Squares and counters stay away until the
groups. Have day 8's tickets and unit 2's record sheet beside you.

### The day

#### 30 min. End-of-unit assessment

Alone, unaided: no squares, counters, dots or 1 to 100 chart. Read each item aloud once. At 30 minutes, pencils
down; not reached is not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Pencils away; hand back the day 8 written tasks, look-fors ticked; show the answers to 1,
2, 4 and 6. Students mark in pen, matching wrong answers to the group list.

Check: [[s02-E1]], [[s02-E2]], [[s02-E4]], [[s02-E6v2]]

**Groups, 15 minutes.** Hands up sets the groups (in two: join the first). If day 8's tickets showed error A
(3, 6, 9 first) or B (all five steps drawn), and the same student missed 1, 4 or 6, they go to A and B. A day 8
ticket with C ("It adds 3") and a miss on item 2 goes to C and D. Fixes go in pen in the fixes box.

- A and B (item 1: 9, 18, 27 or 17, 26, 35; item 4: 5, 10, 15, or a miscount after drawing; item 6: 42, 126, 378, or
  adding; written task: thirty shapes drawn, look-for 2 missed): circle the start in each stem, write it first,
  then jump. Item 4: build step 1 with squares, one on a row of 5, and write 6. Say step 6 in words first, "the
  first shape and how many jumps?": 6 + 5 × 5 = 31. Item 6: say "times 3" at each jump. Written task: count by
  threes to 30.
- C and D (item 2: "It goes up by 11" or "They are all even"; item 6: "They triple"): cover the rule. Write odd or
  even under every number of item 2 before saying anything, then list what is true of the numbers themselves.

Students who missed nothing do [[s02-C9-1]], then [[s02-C9-2]], then check a group's fixes.

#### 10 min. Unit 3's readiness check

Alone, on paper, no strips: unit 3's readiness check, four items, each read aloud once. Collect it and the
assessments at the end.

### Other rooms

- **No projector.** Write the answers to 1, 2, 4 and 6 and the group tasks on a covered part of the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No projector: mark from the key. No paper squares: coins or squares torn from scrap.

**Skip.** The groups, the challenges, and unit 3's readiness check (give it the next morning, before day 10).

- **30 min. End-of-unit assessment.** Items 1 to 6, alone, with no squares or coins. Read each item aloud once.
  Not reached is not wrong.
- **15 min. Go over it, one to one.** Mark 1, 2, 4 and 6 together from the key; read 3 and 5 against its
  look-fors. Fix each missed one in pen beside the first try: [[s02-E1]] by circling the start and writing it
  first; [[s02-E4]] by building step 1 with coins, one on a row of 5.

    **Say.** For [[s02-E2]]: "Cover the rule. Is each number odd or even?" Expect: even, odd, even, odd, even;
    they take turns.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups ten minutes, never the assessment, marking or readiness check. Longer: the challenges.

### Words and supports

Items read aloud once; large print offered; item 4 may be built with squares and drawn by a scribe; items 3 and 5
may be said aloud to a scribe.

### Tomorrow

After class: record 1, 2, 4 and 6 from the pencil first try, with error letters; judge 3 and 5 by look-for. Mark
unit 3's readiness check (its teacher notes) onto unit 3's record sheet, R1 to R4, so day 10 opens with the
results beside you.

Item 3: [[steps:s02-E3]]

Item 5: [[steps:s02-E5]]

Hand 3 and 5 back at the start of day 10, look-fors ticked, with the "meets" answer shown; students fix in pen
during the warm-up. More numbers as the reason: a dot-pairs sketch of one jump, then "It must keep going because
__". Unit 2's errors come back on Check 1 (day 42). Students cut out unit 3's strip sheets as morning work before
day 10.
