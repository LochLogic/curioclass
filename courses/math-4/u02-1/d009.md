# Day 9: Show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Stack the day 8 written tasks by name, held until pencils go down. Squares stay away until the groups. Have day 8's tickets, unit 2's record sheet and unit 3's record sheet beside you.

### The day

#### 30 min. End-of-unit assessment

Alone, unaided: no squares, counters, dots or 1 to 100 chart. Read each item aloud once. At 30 minutes,
pencils down; not reached is not wrong.

#### 20 min. Respond: mark, then fix in groups

**Mark, 5 minutes.** Pencils away; show the answers to 1, 2, 4 and 6. Students mark in pen and find their
group.

Check: [[s02-E1]], [[s02-E2]], [[s02-E4v2]], [[s02-E6v2]]

**Groups, 15 minutes.** By the marking: a wrong number on 1, 4 or 6 goes to A and B; a miss on 2, or "They
triple" on 6, to C and D; in both, join the first. If day 8's tickets showed A, B or C, watch those
first. Fixes go in pen in the fixes box.

- A and B (item 1: 9, 18, 27 or 17, 26, 35; item 4: 5, 10, 15, 20 and 45, or all nine steps drawn; item 6: 42, 126,
  378, or adding; day 8's written task: you drew all thirty shapes): circle the start in each stem, write it
  first, then jump. Item 4: build step 1 with squares, one on a row of 5, and write 6. Say step 9 in words first,
  "the first shape and how many jumps?": 6 + 8 × 5 = 46. Item 6: say "times 3" at each jump. Hand back the day 8
  written tasks here, look-fors ticked: count by threes to 30.
- C and D (item 2: "It goes up by 11" or "They are all even"; item 6: "They triple"): cover the rule. Write odd or
  even under every number of item 2 before saying anything, then list what is true of the numbers themselves.
  For item 6, write what is true of 14, 42, 126 and 378 that "times 3" never said.

Students who missed nothing do [[s02-C9-1]], then [[s02-C9-2]], then check a group's fixes.

#### 10 min. Unit 3's readiness check

Alone, on paper, no strips: unit 3's readiness check, four items, each read aloud once. Collect it and the
assessments at the end.

### Other rooms

- **No projector.** Write the answers to 1, 2, 4 and 6 and the group tasks on a covered part of the board.
- **No printer.** Write the six items on the board; students answer in a notebook, item 4's drawing included.

### At home

**Swap.** No projector: mark from the key. No paper squares: coins or squares torn from scrap.

**Skip.** The groups, the challenges, and unit 3's readiness check (give it the next morning, before day 10).

- **30 min. End-of-unit assessment.** Items 1 to 6, alone, with no squares or coins. Read each item aloud once.
  Not reached is not wrong.
- **15 min. Go over it, one to one.** Mark 1, 2, 4 and 6 together from the key; read 3 and 5 against its
  look-fors. Fix each missed one in pen beside the first try: [[s02-E1]] by circling the start and writing it
  first; [[s02-E4v2]] by building step 1 with coins, one on a row of 5.

    **Say.** For [[s02-E2]]: "Cover the rule. Is each number odd or even?" Expect: even, odd, even, odd, even;
    they take turns.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups ten minutes, never the assessment, marking or readiness check. Longer: the challenges.

### Words and supports

Items read aloud once; large print offered; item 4 may be built with squares and drawn by a scribe; items 3 and 5
may be said aloud to a scribe.

### Tomorrow

After class: record 1, 2, 4 and 6 from the pencil first try, with error letters; judge 3 and 5 by look-for. Mark
unit 3's readiness check (its teacher notes) onto unit 3's record sheet, R1 to R4, so day 10 opens with the
results beside you.

Item 3: [[steps:s02-E3v2]]

Item 5: [[steps:s02-E5]]

Hand 3 and 5 back in day 10's first five minutes, look-fors ticked, with the "meets" answers shown; students fix
in pen. More numbers as the reason: a dot-pairs sketch of one jump, then "It must keep going because __". Unit 2's
errors come back on Check 1 (day 42).
