# Stretch 9 family note: adding and subtracting to a million

## English

For the next six school days your child is adding and subtracting numbers up to a million, in columns, the way
you probably learned.

**Ask what the little 1 at the top is worth.** In 3,746 + 2,538, the 6 ones and 8 ones make 14 ones. That is 1
ten and 4 ones, so the little 1 over the tens is one ten. Over the thousands it would be one thousand.

**Subtracting works the same way, backwards.** When a column is too small, one of the next place is broken into
ten. Your child may call it borrowing, regrouping or ungrouping: all are fine.

**Try this at home, five minutes.** Before your child adds two big numbers, ask for a rough guess first. After
subtracting, ask them to add the answer back to check it.
