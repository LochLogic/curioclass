# Day 89: Chapter 9: the narrator's view and mine

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a prepared detail card and a talk slip for each child, and the discussion record. Make six groups of five and post the order. Put the discussion moves chart where all can see it. Mark the chapter 8 vote page in the book (about 190 words). Choose three pairs to hear from the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P89-2]]: split, check each chunk, then blend and write. Review: you split three-syllable words on day 81. Then once and people, [[s03-P89-3]]; mark the odd part. Review: you mapped people on day 81. Once is new. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 9, [[s03-P89-4]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its sentence, [[s03-P89-5]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one ending-rule line, [[s03-P89-6]]. Review: you used the three ending rules on day 81.

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Agreement on slates. Review: you learned agreement on day 88. Children write the sentence with the right verb, [[s03-P89-7]], then the sentence with the right pronoun, [[s03-P89-8]]. *15 min.* Chapter 9 is read aloud later in the day, so pairs reread the chapter 8 vote page now, [[s03-P89-9]]. One child reads and one listens with the feedback card, then they swap. Review: you used the feedback card on day 2. Hear three pairs at 3 minutes each and float for the rest. Fill the heard-six column.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*2 min.* Hand back the day 88 strips, s03-X88. Say one new pair of sentences, [[s03-P89-10]]: "The small birds sings. It hops." Children mark the subject, then say both sentences so the verbs and the pronoun match. This is error Y and error Z. If many strips showed either, say a second pair whole class: "Two cooks stirs the pot. It smells good." *3 min.* Pairs say the five discussion moves from the chart, [[s03-P89-11]]. Review: you learned them in unit 1.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 9 aloud (about 780 words): the club checks once more, and Ada is said to worry too much. *4 min.* Pairs choose a place in chapter 9 where the narrator gives a judgment and say the words that show it, [[s03-P89-12]]. Each child marks one detail there and fills the top of the prepared detail card, [[s03-P89-13]]. Review: the narrator tells the story and may judge a character; you used the view table on day 84. *4 min.* Model with one volunteer group, [[s03-K89-1]]. Four roles: one reads a marked detail, one waits, one asks a question that links to what was said, one says what became clearer. The frame: "The narrator thinks ___; I think ___ because ___." Fill it for Ada's worry: the narrator thinks she worries too much; I think she is careful, because checking found the switched labels. *4 min.* Pairs say their first sentence with the frame, [[s03-P89-14]], and each child writes a linked question on the card. Watch for error E, the narrator's view said as your own with no reason.

#### 30 min. Formal discussion window

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each at the teacher's table, 1 minute a child. Review: you held a discussion window on day 9. At the table each child reads the marked detail and asks the linked question, [[s03-P89-15]], then answers a classmate's question, [[s03-P89-16]]. Record five boxes for each child on the discussion record: marked detail, turn, linked question, answer, what it clarified. Mark each box on the record scale, alone, with help or not yet seen. Watch for error O (a question unrelated to the talk), error Q (a vague answer such as "stuff happens") and error N (speaking over a classmate).
The other 25 children write at their seats. First they write how their view of Ada's worry differs from the narrator's, [[s03-P89-17]], 15 minutes. As each group returns from the table, its children fill the talk slip, [[s03-P89-18]]: what the talk made them see, and where it came from. Watch for error P, the same idea with no learning named. Last, in 5 minutes, they write what "worries too much" tells about the narrator, [[s03-P89-19]]. A group that finishes early rereads its passage. A late group is not extended past the window; note who was not reached.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X89v2]]. Then children write the practice item in the Exit box on the student page, [[s03-P89-1v2]]; it is handed back with the slip. Shown: a view of the child's own that differs from the narrator's, with a reason from the chapter. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the narrator think? What do you think?" Not yet seen: the narrator's or a character's view given as the reader's (error E). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the discussion partner, asks one linked question and one request for a detail, and writes one note on the explanation. One projector: show the discussion moves chart and the frame. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No chapter book at home: any book with a narrator.
**Skip.** The slate words, the pair reread and the group talk.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you one point from the talk and answers [[s03-P89-17]] aloud.
  **Say.** "What did the narrator think? What do you think, and why?" Expect: the narrator thinks Ada worries too much; the child gives a view with a reason.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X89v2]] and [[s03-P89-1v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the card, the talk window and the exit; cut the slate words. Longer: write a second explanation using a different passage.

### Words and supports

Narrator, view, because, link, explain. Frame: "The narrator thinks ___; I think ___ because ___." Audio beside print; large print; a reading buddy in each group; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 90 returns the day 89 exit and the Exit box item, s03-X89v2 and s03-P89-1v2, and repairs error E: label whose words give each judgment, then give your own with a reason. It begins with the skills word check, then reads chapter 10 closely for motives and for a phrase that means more than it says. The slate work, [[s03-P89-2]], [[s03-P89-3]], [[s03-P89-4]], [[s03-P89-6]], [[s03-P89-7]] and [[s03-P89-8]], the spoken work, [[s03-P89-5]], [[s03-P89-9]], [[s03-P89-10]], [[s03-P89-11]], [[s03-P89-12]], [[s03-P89-14]], [[s03-P89-15]], [[s03-P89-16]] and [[s03-K89-1]], and the card, explanation, slip and word, [[s03-P89-13]], [[s03-P89-17]], [[s03-P89-18]] and [[s03-P89-19]], stay in the folder. The discussion record goes to the teacher's file.
