# Section 3.2 opener: how a change grows, who says what, and agreement (days 87 to 90)

## What the section does

For four days the class reads chapters 7 to 10. Children say how a later action grows from an earlier problem, make a verb agree with its subject and a pronoun with the noun it stands for, hold a small-group discussion about the narrator's view and their own, and read closely for a character's reason and a phrase that means more than it says. Day 87 also holds a brief invented episode set in the chapter world, written alone in one sitting.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 87 is a partner-rereading day with the episode task. Day 88 is the agreement lesson: a worked pair of sentences, then four printed sentences to repair. Day 89 is a discussion day: six groups of five meet at the teacher's table for five minutes each, one minute a child, while the others write. Day 90 replaces the long-word block with a short word check, heard one child at a time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A later part grows from an earlier one.** Name the earlier problem, then the action that answers it, with a word such as so or because.
- **A verb matches its subject.** One reader checks; two readers check. A pronoun matches the noun it stands for.
- **The narrator is a voice of its own.** A narrator may judge a character. A character may judge another. The reader may judge differently, and gives a reason.
- **A discussion has a part to play.** Bring a marked detail, wait your turn, ask a question that links to what was said, and say what the talk changed.
- **A phrase can mean more than its words.** Read it against what happens next.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** States a trait without an action or consequence. Repair: Draw action to result, then name the supported trait. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Y:** Uses a verb form that disagrees with its subject. Repair: Match the subject number to the verb form. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Z:** Uses a pronoun that does not agree with its intended antecedent. Repair: Mark the intended referent before selecting its pronoun. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **E:** Attributes one person's view to another. Repair: Label whose words express each judgment. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **D:** Reads a nonliteral phrase as a physical event. Repair: Compare the physical reading with the surrounding event. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **P:** Repeats an initial idea without explaining learning from discussion. Repair: Name one peer detail that changed or strengthened understanding. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
