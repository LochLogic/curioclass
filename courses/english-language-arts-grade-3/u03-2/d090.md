# Day 90: Chapter 10: motives and a phrase that says more

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child and the word check sheet, day 90. Mark two places in chapter 10 of the class book copy: the opening sentences (about 45 words, unseen by the class) for the reading check, and the page from "Near the end of the hour" to "explain it to me" (about 160 words). Pick two children to read a log line.

### The day

#### 15 min. Skills word check

Whole class, then one at a time. *5 min.* Children write on sheet 90A while you say the words. Words, [[s03-P90-1]]: say each word once in a sentence; they write it. Sentence, [[s03-P90-2]]: say "Ada thanked her friend for the help." in phrases, twice; they write it. Affixes, [[s03-P90-3]]: they write what each ending or beginning means. *10 min.* Hear 12 children read, 45 seconds each (9 minutes), one at a time at your table, [[s03-P90-4]]; the rest read their own book silently. The reading is the opening of chapter 10, the first sentences (about 45 words), from the class book copy: the child reads from the book, you print nothing, and the class has not heard it yet. Choose the 12 from the heard-six column and those not yet heard this unit. Mark the word check sheet, day 90. The other 18 are heard on day 91 (12 children) and day 92 (6 children), 45 seconds each, on the opening of chapter 12 instead, because chapter 10 has now been read aloud; the handbacks rows name them. Pattern repair is not today; it goes to day 91.

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P90-5]]. No conferences. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each. Fill the heard-six column.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 89 slips and Exit box items, s03-X89 and s03-P89-1. Error E is the narrator's view said as a character's. Say one new pair, [[s03-P90-6]]: the narrator says the cook is lazy; ask whose view that is. Then a second, [[s03-P90-7]]: the child gives their own view with a reason. Children mend their slip with a partner. If many slips showed error E, say one more whole class, with a different story. *2 min.* Known roots, [[s03-P90-8]]: helpless, joyful, unkind. Say the root and the ending for each. Review: you used -ful and -less on a known root on days 26 and 27.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 10 aloud (about 920 words). *8 min.* Pairs reread the dark page, one reading and one listening, then swap. *4 min.* Share: three pairs say what "in the dark" means on that page, [[s03-P90-9]]. Watch for error D, a phrase read as physical ("the room was dim"): ask what Ada did not know.

#### 20 min. Writing

*4 min.* Model, [[s03-K90-1]]: why Ben puts the plan on the top shelf. Ask the question, find the sentence that answers it, and say the motive: he wants it safe. *8 min.* Children write one question about a character's motive in chapter 10 and an answer with the place it comes from, [[s03-P90-10]]. *8 min.* Chart for "in the dark," [[s03-P90-11]]: the words, what they could mean, the clue from the page, and the meaning chosen. New today: a phrase can mean more than it says. Watch for error D.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: cupboard. It is a compound word: cup and board, yet it is not for cups only. Review: you learned compound words in grade 2 on day 51. Children say a clause with cupboard, [[s03-P90-12]], then name its two small words, [[s03-P90-13]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P90-14]]: say why Ada does not erase the arrow. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X90]]. Shown: gives the motive, then the sentence or place that shows it. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does it say Ben did?" Not yet seen: a nonliteral phrase read as physical (error D). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 10 aloud, is the reread partner, and hears the 20-second reading. One projector: show the chart frame. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No chapter book at home: any book with a story.
**Skip.** The slate words and the pair reread.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you why Ada does not erase the arrow.
  **Say.** "What does the arrow stand for?" Expect: the first plan, a first mistake that she keeps as a clue.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X90]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the word check, the dark page and the exit; cut the chart. Longer: write a second question about a different character.

### Words and supports

Motive, nonliteral, cupboard, in the dark. Audio beside print; large print; the word check read aloud by a helper; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 91 returns the day 90 exit, s03-X90, and repairs error D: say what the phrase means, then what it did not mean. It begins with the pattern repair from the word check sheets, hears the next 12 children read the chapter 12 opening, which no one has heard (45 seconds each; the last 6 go to day 92), and moves to the next section. The slate work and the sheet, [[s03-P90-1]], [[s03-P90-2]], [[s03-P90-3]] and [[s03-P90-8]], the log work, [[s03-P90-5]], the spoken work, [[s03-P90-4]], [[s03-P90-6]], [[s03-P90-7]], [[s03-P90-9]], [[s03-P90-12]], [[s03-P90-13]], [[s03-P90-14]] and [[s03-K90-1]], and the question and chart, [[s03-P90-10]] and [[s03-P90-11]], stay in the folder.
