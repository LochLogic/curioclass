# Exit cards, days 87 to 90

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 87 (s03-X87), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Earlier: Pia's seed tray sat on a shelf she could not reach. Later: Pia pulled over a sturdy box and watered the seeds." Ask: How does the later action grow from the earlier problem?
- **Day 88 (s03-X88), Exit.** Invented for practice. Draft: "The tired hikers rests. It drinks water." The word It stands for the hikers. Ask: Rewrite both sentences so the verbs and the pronoun agree.
- **Day 89 (s03-X89), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Jonah wants to float the sailboat once more. Ivy says the last float found a leak. The narrator says Jonah fusses over nothing." Ask: Who says Jonah fusses over nothing? Write the name.
- **Day 89 (s03-P89-1), Student page, practice.** Invented for practice. Card: "Mara asks to test the bridge model again. Colt says the first test held. The narrator says Mara checks too often." Ask: Write your own view of Mara's request, with a reason from the card.
- **Day 90 (s03-X90), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Arlo hands Nell a drawing full of tiny lines. 'I can't make heads or tails of this,' she says. She asks him to point to the door." Ask: Why does Nell ask Arlo to point to the door? Use what "heads or tails" means.

The student-page practice item (day 89) goes on the day's student page with a blank line under it, and is marked by the teacher with the same look-fors as the exit.
