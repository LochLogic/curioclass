# Exit cards, days 87 to 90

Strips marked From the book print lines from the unit's own books, with the chapter named, and carry no invented label. For a card marked Invented for practice, say aloud "This card is made up for practice" and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 87 (s03-X87v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, the end of chapter 7: "Ada still had a last arrow to fix." "Monday," she said. "We'll look at the arrow on Monday." "Both of us." Ask: What does Ada plan because of the last arrow?
- **Day 88 (s03-X88), Exit.** Invented for practice. Draft: "The tired hikers rests. It drinks water." The word It stands for the hikers. Ask: Rewrite both sentences so the verbs and the pronoun agree.
- **Day 89 (s03-X89v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 5: "That's wasting time," said Ada. It was the narrator's opinion as well. The narrator thought Ben was wasting time. Ask: Ben has asked to see the arrow. Ada and the narrator think he is wasting time. Write how your own view, as the reader, differs from theirs.
- **Day 89 (s03-P89-1v2), Student page, practice.** From the book. Book 1, chapter 5: "It might matter," said Ben. "I'd like to see what's wrong with it. If we don't look, how will we know what to change?" Ask: Write your own view of Ben's request. Give a reason from the chapter.
- **Day 90 (s03-X90v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 10: "Maybe it's hard to see your own mistakes," said Jun. "They hide in plain sight." Ask: What does Jun mean? Write one sentence.

The student-page practice item (day 89) goes on the day's student page with a blank line under it, and is marked by the teacher with the same look-fors as the exit.
