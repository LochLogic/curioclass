# Day 87: Chapter 7: how a change grows from earlier events

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a chapter map frame for each child. Mark two pages in the book: the end of chapter 6 (from "Ms. Alvarez looked at it", about 190 words) and the chapter 7 page from "Over there" to the note (about 240 words). Choose the three pairs you will hear from the heard-six column. Have the day 86 strips ready to hand back.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P87-1]]: split, check each chunk, then blend and write. Review: you split exactly on day 84. Then though and answer, [[s03-P87-2]]; mark the odd part. Review: you mapped answer on day 81. Though is new: its ough does not say what it says in thought. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 7, [[s03-P87-3]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its sentence, [[s03-P87-4]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one ending-rule line, [[s03-P87-5]]. Review: you used the three ending rules on day 81.

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Grammar on slates, from the second sign in chapter 6. Children write the subject of "The careful reader checks the arrow," [[s03-P87-6]], then what its verb does, [[s03-P87-7]]. Review: you named a subject on day 84, and the five jobs on day 86. *15 min.* Partner rereading of the end of chapter 6, [[s03-P87-8]]. One child reads and one listens with the feedback card, then they swap. Review: you used the feedback card in unit 1; the card asks for accuracy, phrases and expression, and never for speed. Hear three pairs at 3 minutes each (six children, 9 minutes) and float for the rest. Fill the heard-six column. If a child reads word by word, read one sentence in phrases and ask for the same.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 86 strips, s03-X86. Error X is a class named with no job. Say one new sentence, "Ben looked up. He smiled.", and ask what He does, [[s03-P87-9]]: it stands in for Ben, so the name is not said again. Children mend their own strips with a partner. If many strips showed error X, say it once more whole class with "The dog barked. It ran." *2 min.* Pairs open their chapter 6 maps and say what was not settled at the end of chapter 6, [[s03-P87-10]]: the last arrow.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 7 aloud (about 880 words). Stop after "This time, though, she had seen it first." and model, [[s03-K87-1]]. Make three marks on the board for the three earlier times Ben pointed out a problem and Ada saw it last, then one mark for this time. Say: "A change grows from earlier events. Three times Ada saw last. Now she sees first." Read on. *8 min.* Pairs reread the page from "Over there" to the note, one reading and one listening, then swap. *4 min.* Share: three pairs name the action that changes the corner, [[s03-P87-11]]. Watch for error C, a trait with no action ("Ada is kind"): ask "What did she do?"

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Alone, fill the first four boxes of the chapter 7 map, [[s03-P87-12]]. Then write the link, [[s03-P87-13]]: "Chapter 6 ended with ___. Chapter 7 begins with ___." Watch for error B, chapter 7 put first. *15 min.* N03, a made-up scene written alone in one sitting. Say the task: a new reader cannot reach a book on a low shelf; show what Ada does and why. The scene uses one fact from chapter 7: the table moved, Ada's note, or the red bear book. Plan card, 3 minutes, [[s03-P87-14]]; draft, 10 minutes, [[s03-P87-15]]; reread, 2 minutes. Children write Made up at the top. Collect it after the exit. Evening note for each child, the one change that would help most, as on day 86. The scene is read for four things: a chapter 7 fact used correctly, an action that causes the next event, events in order, a closing that settles the problem.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Model, [[s03-K87-2]]: in "with a lot of confidence", confidence means being sure of yourself, and the sentence says Ada was sure the table fit. Each child says a clause about Ada that uses the word, [[s03-P87-16]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P87-17]]: say what in chapter 7 changed because Ada saw the table first. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X87v2]]. Shown: names the earlier problem and links the later action to it. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does Ada still have to fix?" Not yet seen: a trait with no action (error C), or a fact the chapter does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 7 aloud, is the reread partner, and hears the reread. The child writes the scene. No printer: copy the map boxes onto a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No chapter book at home: any book with a story.
**Skip.** The slate words and the pair reread.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you what Ada did in chapter 7.
  **Say.** "What changed because Ada saw the table first?" Expect: she moved the table so Ben could reach his book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X87v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the plan card, a draft of five sentences and the exit. Longer: write a second ending that changes the corner a different way.

### Words and supports

Confidence, earlier, later, change, scene, made up. Large print; the exit read aloud twice; a scribe for the scene; record any help. Preview the optional independent classic for sensitive moments.

### Tomorrow

Day 88 returns the scene with one note and one change, and repairs error C on the day 87 exit, s03-X87v2: name the action and what it did, then the trait. It reads chapter 8 and teaches agreement. The slate work, [[s03-P87-1]], [[s03-P87-2]], [[s03-P87-3]], [[s03-P87-5]], [[s03-P87-6]] and [[s03-P87-7]], the spoken work, [[s03-P87-4]], [[s03-P87-8]], [[s03-P87-9]], [[s03-P87-10]], [[s03-P87-11]], [[s03-P87-16]], [[s03-P87-17]], [[s03-K87-1]] and [[s03-K87-2]], and the map, link and plan card, [[s03-P87-12]], [[s03-P87-13]] and [[s03-P87-14]], stay in the folder. The scene, [[s03-P87-15]], goes to the teacher after the exit.
