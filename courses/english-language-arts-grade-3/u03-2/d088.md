# Day 88: Chapter 8: verbs and pronouns that match

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one note on each made-up scene the evening before, as on day 86. Print the student page for each child. Keep the key for the sentences to repair, key-sentences-to-repair-88, with you; it is not printed. Copy the two model sentences (K88-1 and K88-2 below) on the board. Mark the chapter 8 page where the labels are switched (about 190 words). Pick four children for conferences and two to read a log line.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P88-1]]: split, check each chunk, then blend and write. Review: you split three-syllable words on day 81. Then group, [[s03-P88-2]]; mark the odd part. Review: you mapped would in grade 2 on day 54; ou says the same sound here. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 8, [[s03-P88-3]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its sentence, [[s03-P88-4]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one ending-rule line, [[s03-P88-5]]. Review: you used the three ending rules on day 81.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P88-6]]. The teacher holds four 4-minute conferences, [[s03-P88-7]] (16 minutes, one record each). Two children read a log line aloud, [[s03-P88-8]], 2 minutes each. Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the scenes, each with its note. Children read the note and make the one change in the margin, [[s03-P88-9]], keeping the first words beside it. Review: you used one note, one change on day 86. Recopying with no change is error L. *2 min.* Hand back the day 87 strips, s03-X87. Error C is a trait with no action. Say one card, [[s03-P88-10]], and ask what the later action did about the earlier problem: the jar could fall, so Lena set a book beside it. If many strips showed error C, say a second card whole class: "Earlier, the gate stuck. Later, Tam pushed it with a board."

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 8 aloud (about 800 words): the vote, the labels switched, the mistake found. *8 min.* Pairs reread the page where the labels are switched, [[s03-P88-11]], one reading and one listening, then swap. After the second reading each pair says what the mistake is. *4 min.* Share: three pairs read one sentence from the page and say the job of two of its words, [[s03-P88-12]]. Watch for error X, a class named with no job ("took is a verb"): ask what it does.

#### 20 min. Writing

New today: agreement. *6 min.* First, children say the five jobs, [[s03-P88-13]] (review of day 86). Then model two checks on the board. Check 1, [[s03-K88-1]]: copy Ben's line from chapter 8, then change it: "The quiet readers shares a plan now." The subject is readers, more than one, so the verb is share. Say it both ways: one reader shares; two readers share. Check 2, [[s03-K88-2]]: "Ada pressed the labels onto the shelf. It stayed there." The word It stands for labels, more than one, so it is They. A pronoun matches the noun it stands for. *8 min.* Children revise four printed sentences, [[s03-P88-14]], [[s03-P88-15]], [[s03-P88-16]] and [[s03-P88-17]]. Watch for error Y (the verb still wrong) and error Z (the pronoun still wrong). *6 min.* Each child writes one sentence of their own about the vote, [[s03-P88-18]], and writes the job of two words under it, [[s03-P88-19]].

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: label. Review: you learned label in kindergarten, a word written beside a part of a drawing; that is a noun. It is also a verb, to put a label on something. Children say one sentence about chapter 8 with label as a noun, [[s03-P88-20]], then one with label as a verb, [[s03-P88-21]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P88-22]]: read your revised sentence aloud and say which word you changed and why. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X88]]. Shown: the verbs and the pronoun all match the hikers. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Who rests? How many?" Not yet seen: the verb or the pronoun left unchanged (error Y or error Z). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 8 aloud and is the reread partner; the child revises the sentences. One projector: show the two model sentences and the four printed sentences. No printer: copy the four sentences onto the board for slates.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The slate words and the pair reread.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child fixes [[s03-P88-14]] and [[s03-P88-16]] on the page and says which word they changed.
  **Say.** "How many people vote? So does the verb say vote or votes?" Expect: vote.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X88]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the note, the two model checks, two of the four sentences and the exit. Longer: write a second sentence of their own with a pronoun and check it.

### Words and supports

Subject, verb, pronoun, match, label. Large print for the printed sentences; the notes read aloud; one picture of one reader and one of two readers; record any help.

### Tomorrow

It reads chapter 9 and holds the first formal talk, on the narrator's view and the reader's own. The slate work, [[s03-P88-1]], [[s03-P88-2]], [[s03-P88-3]] and [[s03-P88-5]], the log work, [[s03-P88-6]], [[s03-P88-7]] and [[s03-P88-8]], the spoken work, [[s03-P88-4]], [[s03-P88-10]], [[s03-P88-11]], [[s03-P88-12]], [[s03-P88-13]], [[s03-P88-20]], [[s03-P88-21]], [[s03-P88-22]], [[s03-K88-1]] and [[s03-K88-2]], and the revisions, [[s03-P88-9]], [[s03-P88-14]], [[s03-P88-15]], [[s03-P88-16]], [[s03-P88-17]], [[s03-P88-18]] and [[s03-P88-19]], stay in the folder.
