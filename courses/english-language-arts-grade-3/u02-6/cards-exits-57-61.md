# Exit cards, days 57 to 61

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each paper card aloud twice before the children write; a spoken card is shown first at the child's own turn at the table. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 57 (s02-X57), spoken, three children.** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "A marsh frog has webbed back feet. It lives in tall grass at a pond's edge. When a hungry bird comes near, it dives under the water." Ask: Say which fact belongs under habitat.
- **Day 57 (s02-X57b), paper, every child.** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "A marsh frog has webbed back feet. It lives in tall grass at a pond's edge. When a hungry bird comes near, it dives under the water." Ask: Write the fact that belongs under habitat as a brief note. End it with the label marsh frog book, p. 21.
- **Day 58 (s02-X58), spoken, three children.** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Many frogs live in a bog, soft wet ground where moss grows. A resting frog's ventral side, the underside of its body, touches the ground." Ask: Say what bog means.
- **Day 58 (s02-X58b), paper, every child.** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Many frogs live in a bog, soft wet ground where moss grows. A resting frog's ventral side, the underside of its body, touches the ground." Ask: Write what ventral means.
- **Day 59 (s02-X59), paper.** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "A tree frog has wide, sticky toe pads. Rain makes the leaves wet and slippery. Many animals hunt for frogs on the ground." Ask: Write how the toe pads help this frog stay safe. Use because.
- **Day 60 (s02-X60), spoken, three children.** This card is invented for practice. The strip comes from the river frog book, page 9, heading Home. Strip: "River frogs lay eggs in slow, shallow water at the river's edge. The tadpoles hide among the stones." Ask: Say one question about river frogs that this strip answers.
- **Day 60 (s02-X60b), paper, every child.** This card is invented for practice. The strip comes from the river frog book, page 9, heading Home. Strip: "River frogs lay eggs in slow, shallow water at the river's edge. The tadpoles hide among the stones." Ask: Fill a note card with a brief note from the strip. Give its box and the label river frog book, p. 9.
- **Day 61 (s02-X61), spoken, three children.** This card is invented for practice. Request: what a chorus frog looks like. An invented wildlife page has three headings: Description, Habitat, Food. Ask: Say which heading you open first.
- **Day 61 (s02-X61b), paper, every child.** This card is invented for practice. Request: where a chorus frog lays its eggs. The strip comes from a wildlife page, heading Habitat. Strip: "A chorus frog lays eggs in cool, shallow pools in the woods." Ask: Fill a note card for the request. Give its box and the label wildlife page, Habitat.
