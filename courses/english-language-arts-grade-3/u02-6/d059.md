# Day 59: Write a linked explanation of how a glass frog's body and home help it live

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the Description paragraph for each child, Habitat paragraph cut off. Mark the three pairs you will hear on the page 11 reread.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 58's exit (s02-X58 and s02-X58b), error D (the clue in the sentence is ignored): judge a child's meaning, [[s02-P59-1]]. *7 min.* Week 12. Write four new words on slates, [[s02-P59-2]]; choose -ment or -ous and say what each ending adds, [[s02-P59-3]]. Map some: children mark the letters that do not sound the way they look, [[s02-P59-4]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Review from day 57: a plural that changes and an idea noun. Children write the plural of foot, [[s02-P59-5]], and choose the idea noun, [[s02-P59-6]]. *15 min.* Partner rereading of page 11 with the feedback card, no speed: read it aloud twice, [[s02-P59-7]]. Listen to three pairs for 3 minutes each; the rest give each other feedback.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Review from day 58: the body paragraph against the habitat paragraph; children say one difference to a partner. *3 min.* Read the rest of page 11 and page 12 aloud, pausing at each sentence, and leave out the wrestling lines. *9 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: what supports the claim that the frog is hard to see? Pairs point to the words, [[s02-P59-8]] (6 minutes); class share (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*3 min.* Model joining a trait to why it helps, with so, [[s02-K59-1]]; then say a definition in your own words, [[s02-K59-2]]. *7 min.* Each child fills the noun sheet: a regular plural, [[s02-P59-9]]; the plural of leaf, [[s02-P59-10]]; and an idea noun used as an idea, [[s02-P59-11]]. The sheet is dated 59 and kept.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: say your topic sentence and two facts you will use, [[s02-P59-12]]; the partner names the link word they heard (4 minutes). Three volunteers, 2 minutes each (6 minutes). Anyone may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

Silent. Each child writes one paragraph on how the grainy Cochran frog's body and home help it live, from pages 11 and 12 and the Description paragraph, with a topic sentence, two facts, a definition, linking words and a closing sentence, [[s02-P59-13]]. One sitting. Record each look-for on the record sheet: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X59]]. Shown: the pads hold on to wet leaves, linked to staying safe with because. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the pad do on a wet leaf?" Not yet seen: the cause and the result reversed, or no link.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult sits apart, reads nothing during the writing, and records any help. No printer: copy the noun sheet by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed page: use any animal book and lined paper.
**Skip.** The exit.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child tells you why a green frog on a leaf is hard to see, using so. Then writes two sentences about it.
  **Say.** "Why does the color help the frog?" Expect: the color blends into the leaves, so hungry animals do not find it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X59]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the topic sentence, two facts and a closing sentence; leave the definition for tomorrow. Longer: add a sentence on why the stream matters to the eggs.

### Words and supports

Trait, habitat, transparent, definition, plural. Frames: "The frog is hard to see because ___." Large print; the pages read aloud again for a child who needs it, except during the paragraph; a scribe, with spelling left as written; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 60 is the mid check on locating information and taking notes. It opens with day 59's exit, [[s02-X59]], error Y (draw the cause to result arrow). The paragraph, [[s02-P59-13]], and the noun sheet, [[s02-P59-9]] to [[s02-P59-11]], go to the teacher for written feedback and come back on day 61; the slate work and pointing, [[s02-P59-1]] to [[s02-P59-6]] and [[s02-P59-8]], stays in the folder.
