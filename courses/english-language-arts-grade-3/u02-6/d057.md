# Day 57: Take brief notes under body, habitat and survival, with the page

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the note card, day 57, for each child, and the question card, days 57 to 66, for each pair. Write the three note box definitions on the board: body (a part of the frog), habitat (where it lives), survival (what it does to stay safe). Return the day 56 paragraphs, each with one written change. Pick six names from the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 56's exit (s02-X56v2), error AJ (the drawing is ignored): children point on the page 7 diagram to the froglet, then to the page 6 sentence that matches, [[s02-P57-4]]. *7 min.* Week 12. Review: you met -ment and -ous on days 53 to 55. Show develop and development, danger and dangerous: -ment makes the act or result, -ous makes full of; blend the base first, then check the meaning. Say [[s02-P57-2]]. Children write development and dangerous on slates, [[s02-P57-1]]. Map eyes: children mark the letters that do not sound the way they look, [[s02-P57-3]].

#### 20 min. Hand-back and rereading

*5 min.* Hand back the day 56 paragraph. Each child marks the one change its note asks for, [[s02-P57-5]]; day 58 makes it. *15 min.* Partner rereading of page 8 with the feedback card, no speed: read it aloud twice, [[s02-P57-6]]. Listen to three pairs for 3 minutes each; the rest give each other feedback.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*2 min.* Review: the note slip from grade 2, a fact in a few words with its source tag. *4 min.* Read pages 8 and 9 aloud; children follow with a finger. *4 min.* Ask the research question from the question card: how does a frog's body fit where it lives? Say what relevant means, [[s02-K57-4]]. Model judging three notes on the board, each with its page: [[s02-K57-1]], [[s02-K57-2]] and [[s02-K57-3]]. Then judge a child's box choice, [[s02-K57-7]]: the definition chooses the box, not a word in the note. *5 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: which words answer the question? Pairs point to three phrases, [[s02-P57-7]] (3 minutes); class share (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Show a regular plural (tadpole, tadpoles) and a plural that changes (foot, feet), [[s02-K57-5]]. Review: you made irregular plurals in grade 2, day 54 (children, teeth). Leaf, leaves is the sentence item, [[s02-P57-8]]. Show the two-question test for an abstract noun: can you touch it, and is it an idea? Model kindness, [[s02-K57-6]]. *5 min.* Children write two sentences, [[s02-P57-8]] and [[s02-P57-9]].

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: say your research question and one relevant fact, [[s02-P57-10]]; the partner says which box fits and why (4 minutes). While the pairs talk, then through the rest of the part, hear six children not yet heard on day 55's exit, one at a time, 90 seconds each: show page 4 and read each passage aloud twice, then ask its item (9 minutes).

#### 20 min. Writing

*7 min.* Each child numbers five phrases on pages 8 and 9, each under eight words, [[s02-P57-11]]. *5 min.* Then writes each number in its box on the note card, and the book and page on the source line, [[s02-P57-12]]. *5 min.* Partners swap, find each phrase on its page and tick the real ones, [[s02-P57-13]]. *3 min.* Each child says why one number belongs in its box, [[s02-P57-14]].

Check: the partner finds each phrase on its page and ticks it only when its words are there.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X57b]]. While they write, call three children to the table one at a time, 60 seconds each: show page 8 and ask [[s02-X57v2]]. The rest are seen on [[s02-X57b]]. Shown: the home words, lives mostly in water, said aloud, and a short note under the right box with the label. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the fact tell you?" Not yet seen: the eyes chosen (error X), or a whole sentence copied.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult takes the partner role and ticks the phrases against the book page. No printer: draw the three boxes by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed page: use any frog or animal book and a lined page.
**Skip.** The exit.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child reads page 9 aloud twice and picks three short phrases that tell about a frog's body or home. Together, tick each one on the page.
  **Say.** "Which box does it go in: body, habitat or survival?" Expect: the box that matches what the fact tells.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X57v2]] aloud and [[s02-X57b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: number three phrases, not five; the swap stays. Longer: write one note in full for a phrase on page 9, then check it against the three notes on the board.

### Words and supports

Note, relevant, body, habitat, survival, source. Frames: "This fact goes under ___ because it tells ___." Large print; the page read aloud once more for a child who needs it, except where the child is reading it for the swap; a scribe for the box words; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 58 hears the exit, [[s02-X57v2]] and [[s02-X57b]], and opens on the page 10 glass frog. Children make the one change to their day 56 paragraph. The notes, [[s02-P57-11]] to [[s02-P57-13]], the two sentences, [[s02-P57-8]] and [[s02-P57-9]], and the slate and pointing work, [[s02-P57-1]], [[s02-P57-3]] and [[s02-P57-4]], stay in the folder; the marked paragraph, [[s02-P57-5]], goes to day 58.
