# Day 61: Use feedback to gather evidence about an unusual frog

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the note card, day 57, for each child. Read the day 60 sheets and write one note on each; tally the error letters from the end items and the exits. Pick four names for conferences from the heard-six column. Set out the day 60 sheets, the day 59 paragraph and noun sheet, ready to hand back.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 60's exit (s02-X60 and s02-X60b), error W (no focused question): children write a question, not a fact, [[s02-P61-1]]. *7 min.* Week 12, regrouped by the errors on the check: the -us ending and the dropped e. Say payment and curious, [[s02-P61-2]] (2 minutes); then a longer base, improvement and hazardous: blend the base, add the ending, check the meaning, [[s02-P61-3]] (3 minutes). Map water: children mark the letters that do not sound the way they look, [[s02-P61-4]] (2 minutes).

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children read from their logs. Hold four conferences of 4 minutes: the child tells one new fact and where the words are. Add the four to the heard-six column. The last 4 minutes: each child writes a dated line, [[s02-P61-5]].

#### 10 min. Feedback and return and repair

*3 min.* Hand back day 60's assessment, the sheets (s02-A39v2, s02-A40, s02-A41, s02-A42, s02-A43v2, s02-A44v2, s02-A45v2, s02-A46v2, s02-A47v2, s02-A48, s02-A49) and the exits (s02-X60 and s02-X60b), with the written notes, and the day 59 paragraph (s02-P59-13) and noun sheet. Each child finds the one letter on the sheet. *4 min.* Repair the commonest letter at the board with a new case. *3 min.* Groups by letter: A, a fact the text does not give (point to the words); D, a meaning that ignores its sentence (reread for the clue); V, a search that ignores the headings (open the heading first); W, no question (write it first); X, a note in the wrong box (the definition chooses); Z, a paragraph job named without checking (read what it tells).

#### 20 min. Written repair

*8 min.* Each child makes the one change the note asks for on the day 60 sheet, on a new page, [[s02-P61-6]]. *1 min.* Read the Habitat paragraph aloud to the class; define no word in it. If a child asks, say the stream and forest words are what the note needs. *11 min.* Each child fills a note card for one new note, [[s02-P61-7]].

#### 10 min. Reading and reread

*2 min.* Review: the three boxes, body, habitat and survival (days 57 to 60). *4 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: the excerpt and page 11; which words tell what hides the frog, [[s02-P61-8]]. *4 min.* Each child writes that fact as a brief note with its box and label, [[s02-P61-9]].

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Review: category (grade 1), a group of things that go together; say it for the note boxes, [[s02-K61-1]]. Brief (day 57): [[s02-K61-3]]. New: locate, to find where something is, [[s02-K61-2]]. *5 min.* Children write two sentences, [[s02-P61-10]] and [[s02-P61-11]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: read the repaired note aloud; the partner names its category and how they know, [[s02-P61-12]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X61b]]. While they write, call three children to the table, 60 seconds each: show the frog book's contents and ask [[s02-X61v2]]. Shown: Frogs and Toads named, and a short note under habitat with the label. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the request ask for?" Not yet seen: another section opened first (error V), or a sentence copied.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hands back the sheets and reads the Habitat paragraph aloud once. No printer: copy the note card by hand. No excerpt: use any short wildlife page.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal page.
**Skip.** The exit.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child reads the Habitat paragraph aloud to you and says one place a glass frog needs.
  **Say.** "Where does the glass frog live?" Expect: forests with streams.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X61v2]] aloud and [[s02-X61b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one repair and the new note; record the rest as not reached. Longer: a second note from the Description paragraph, with its box.

### Words and supports

Category, locate, brief, note, source, habitat. Frames: "This note goes under ___ because it tells ___." Large print; the paragraph read aloud again; a scribe for the notes; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 62 hears the exit, [[s02-X61v2]] and [[s02-X61b]], and repairs error V first: open the heading that matches the request. It starts the frog book as the second source. The repaired sheet, [[s02-P61-6]], and the note, [[s02-P61-7]], go to the teacher and come back on day 62. The slate and sentence work, [[s02-P61-1]] to [[s02-P61-5]], [[s02-P61-8]] to [[s02-P61-12]] and [[s02-K61-1]] to [[s02-K61-3]], stays in the folder.
