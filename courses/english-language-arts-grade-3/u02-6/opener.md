# Section 2.6 opener: finding facts in the frog book and taking notes (days 57 to 61)

## What the section does

Over five days the class learns to take brief notes under body, habitat and survival, each with a source label. Day 60 checks locating and noting; day 61 repairs the notes.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes: reading and research, writing, a skills block and a five-minute exit. The exits are one ask each and unscored; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- A note is brief and labelled: a short phrase and its source, not a copied sentence.
- A heading or index word is chosen from the question.
- A word meaning comes from the clue in its own sentence.

## The errors, in plain words

- **X:** A note is in the wrong category. Repair: choose the category by what the fact tells. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **D:** A meaning ignores its sentence clue. Repair: find the clue words in the same sentence. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **Y:** A causal relationship is reversed. Repair: draw the cause to result arrow. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **W:** Research has no focused question. Repair: turn the topic into one question the page answers. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **V:** A search ignores useful text features. Repair: pick the heading from the request. 3 minutes, next day's opening.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
