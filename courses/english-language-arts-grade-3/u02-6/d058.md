# Day 58: Read the glass-frog page and explain its body and habitat words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the Description paragraph of the excerpt for each child; cut off the Habitat paragraph. Write trait, need and habitat on three cards. Pick four names for conferences from the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 57's exit (s02-X57 and s02-X57b), error X (a box chosen by a word in the note): judge a child's box choice, [[s02-P58-1]]. *7 min.* Week 12. Read and spell -ment and -ous on new bases in frog sentences: write excitement, enjoyment and nervous on slates, [[s02-P58-2]]; say what -ous means, [[s02-P58-3]]. Map many: children mark the letters that do not sound the way they look, [[s02-P58-4]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading

*16 min.* Hold four conferences of 4 minutes while the rest read from their logs: the child reads three sentences aloud, [[s02-P58-5]]. Add the four to the heard-six column. *4 min.* Each child writes a log line, [[s02-P58-6]].

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Show the cards. Habitat is the place where an animal lives (day 53). A trait is a way an animal looks or acts, [[s02-K58-1]]. Review: a need is what a living thing must have (kindergarten), [[s02-K58-2]]. *5 min.* Read page 10 and the start of page 11 aloud. Children point to one trait, [[s02-P58-7]], and one need, [[s02-P58-8]]. *7 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: how does the body paragraph differ from the habitat paragraph? Pairs point to one body sentence, [[s02-P58-9]], and one home sentence, [[s02-P58-10]] (4 minutes); class share (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*1 min.* Read the Description paragraph aloud; do not read the Habitat paragraph. *4 min.* Model transparent from the book sentence, [[s02-K58-3]]. Model dorsal from the belly sentence and the class line "dorsal: the upper side", [[s02-K58-4]]. *5 min.* Each child writes what speckling means, [[s02-P58-11]], and underlines the clue, [[s02-P58-12]]. Permanent, vulnerable and undisturbed are not taught; if asked, say the word is not needed today.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: say how the body paragraph differs from the habitat paragraph and show the words, [[s02-P58-13]] (4 minutes). Three volunteers, 2 minutes each (6 minutes). Anyone may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*8 min.* Each child makes the one change from the day 57 note in the day 56 paragraph, on a new page, not a copy, [[s02-P58-14]]. *12 min.* Write about the grainy Cochran frog: one sentence on its body with transparent and bones, [[s02-P58-15]], and one on its home with habitat and leaves, [[s02-P58-16]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X58b]]. While they write, call three children to the table one at a time, 60 seconds each: show the strip, ask [[s02-X58]]. The rest are seen on [[s02-X58b]]. Shown: the meaning from the clue words. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which words in the same sentence tell you?" Not yet seen: a meaning that ignores the sentence.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the Description paragraph aloud, then the child reads it again. No printer: read the paragraph from the screen and copy the sentences with speckling by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed paragraph: use page 10 of any animal book.
**Skip.** The exit.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child reads page 10 aloud twice. Together, find one word that tells how the frog looks and one that tells what it needs.
  **Say.** "What does transparent mean? Which words tell you?" Expect: you can see through it, from the same sentence.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X58]] aloud and [[s02-X58b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one sentence, not two; the underline stays. Longer: write a third sentence joining the body fact to the home with so.

### Words and supports

Trait, need, habitat, transparent, dorsal, speckling. Frames: "The words ___ tell me it means ___." Large print; the paragraph read aloud again for a child who needs it, except on the exit; a scribe for the meaning; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 hears the exit, [[s02-X58]] and [[s02-X58b]], error D (find the clue words in the same sentence). The revised paragraph, [[s02-P58-14]], goes to the teacher. The log line, [[s02-P58-6]], the speckling work, [[s02-P58-11]] and [[s02-P58-12]], the sentences, [[s02-P58-15]] and [[s02-P58-16]], and the slate work, [[s02-P58-2]] and [[s02-P58-4]], stay in the folder.
