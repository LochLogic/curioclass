# Day 60: Check on locating information and brief notes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Save the local keyword page on the one device and print one results card for each group of three. Print the student page and the full excerpt, both paragraphs, for each child. Teach nothing new.

### The day

#### 30 min. 60A and the one device

Silent. Each child writes 60A, [[s02-A39v2]], [[s02-A41]] and [[s02-A42]]. Ten groups of three take the device, 3 minutes a group, 1 minute a child, [[s02-A40]]; record each child's keyword and link. The rest keep writing. Give no hint.

#### 20 min. 60B

Silent, one sheet: [[s02-A43v2]] to [[s02-A49]], on frog book pages 12, 15 and 16 (15 and 16 new), the new Habitat paragraph and Panel B. Record each part: shown, shown with help (anything read aloud) or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Silent collection

Children reread their sheets and add nothing. Collect the sheets.

#### 30 min. Skills (retrieval only)

*3 min.* Return and repair day 59's exit (s02-X59v2), error Y (cause and result reversed): children underline the result, [[s02-P60-1]]. *12 min.* Dictate "The frog was sitting beside the shining stream" (3 minutes). Children write hopeful and hopper with the reason (3 minutes), correct camoflage in a source note with the beginning dictionary (2 minutes), then write payment, shipment, pavement, curious and enormous with each ending's meaning (4 minutes). *5 min.* Independent reading; hear five children read the week's words, 1 minute each. *10 min.* Formal discussion in six groups of five, sources open: how does where a glass frog lives help explain its body? Round 1, 1 minute a child: share one marked detail (5 minutes). Round 2: ask a linked question or answer one (5 minutes). Sit with two groups, 5 minutes each, and record those ten children.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X60b]]. While they write, call three children to the table, 60 seconds each: show the strip, ask [[s02-X60]]. Shown: a question the strip answers, and a note of a few words with its box and the label. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the strip tell you?" Not yet seen: a statement for a question, or a sentence copied.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads no item aloud where reading is the evidence, takes the device turn and records support. No device: the child names a heading and a keyword on the results card. No printer: copy Panel B by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any frog or animal book.
**Skip.** The exit.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the Habitat paragraph and answers [[s02-A39v2]] alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X60]] aloud and [[s02-X60b]] on paper, alone.
- **25 min. Read.** Your child reads from the log book and tells you one thing they wondered.
  **Say.** "What did you wonder?" Expect: one question about the book.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the discussion is one round. Longer: write a second question about glass frogs and name the heading to open.

### Words and supports

Panel, heading, keyword, note, relevant, habitat. Large print; items read aloud except where reading is the evidence; a scribe. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 returns day 60's sheets, [[s02-A39v2]] to [[s02-A49]], and the exits, [[s02-X60]] and [[s02-X60b]], with written notes. It also returns day 59's exit (s02-X59v2), paragraph (s02-P59-13) and noun sheet. The slate work, [[s02-P60-1]], stays in the folder. Repairs go to the next teaching lesson, never during the check.

> Glass frogs have transparent skin beneath their bodies. Transparent means that light can pass through something so that objects behind it can be seen. Parts of their bodies can be seen through their bellies.

> Their habitats include forests with running streams. Habitat means the place an animal lives. The grainy Cochran frog, one kind of glass frog, blends into leaves during the day. Its eggs rest on leaves over a stream. After hatching, tadpoles drop into the water when it rains. The stream provides a place for the tadpoles to grow.
