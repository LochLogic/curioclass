# Section 4.5 opener: research and the first draft (days 137 to 141)

## What the section does

Five days start the water research project and the opinion essay. Children use headings and a keyword to find information, take brief notes with a source tag, plan an opinion, draft its opening and reasons, and meet in a formal discussion. Short reading and writing tasks made on 137 and 138 are kept and scored later.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 137 uses devices in two short turns, or printed pages where there are none, and makes the research notes. Day 138 plans the essay. Days 139 to 141 draft an opening, a first reason and a second reason, with a plural check, a revision and a discussion. Each day ends with a five-minute exit on paper. Nothing in this section is scored on the day.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Headings and keywords help you find a fact.** Use them, then keep only what answers your question.
- **A brief note is a few words plus a source.** Copying a whole page is not a note.
- **Sort each note under one question.** Access, demand, pollution, solutions.
- **An opinion needs a choice and a reason from the sources.** Liking is not a reason.
- **A link word names a relationship.** Because, so and for example do different jobs; choose the one that is true.
- **Tell the reader what the reader needs.** A reader who does not know a word needs it explained first.

## The errors, in plain words

- **AT:** Ignores the parts of a word when working out its meaning. Repair: build the meaning from the base and the part.
- **A:** Adds a fact the notes never give. Repair: point to the note.
- **M:** Supports an opinion with liking only. Repair: replace liking with a source fact.
- **N:** Uses a link word that says the wrong thing. Repair: name the relationship first.
- **Q:** Copies a whole source. Repair: shorten to a few words.
- **R:** Adds a plain s to an irregular plural. Repair: say the singular and the irregular plural together.
- **AH:** Changes the subject in a discussion. Repair: begin with the peer's claim.
- **AQ:** Files a note under the wrong category. Repair: match the note to the question.
- **W:** Ignores what the reader needs. Repair: add the background first.
- **L:** Gives a topic with no choice. Repair: add the choice the essay will defend.
- **AO:** Writes s where es is needed. Repair: compare a short and a long plural.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored and a check day stays unscored on the day.
