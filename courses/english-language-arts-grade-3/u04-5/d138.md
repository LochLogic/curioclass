# Day 138: Plan an opinion essay from the water sources

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the opinion plan page for each child. Write the four look-fors on the board: position, two reasons, link words, close. Sort the day 137 pages, note sheets and exit slips with one comment each. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column; start with children not heard on day 136.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Say against and answer twice each; against has ai said /e/, answer has a silent w. Children write both, [[s04-P138-1]]. *3 min.* Say: -ment and -ful are word parts that build a new word. Children build equipment and helpful, [[s04-P138-2]]. Review: you added -ment and -ful on days 128 and 27. *3 min.* Children add -ed to three words, [[s04-P138-3]]. Review: you used the -ed ending rules on day 126. Watch for a doubled letter missed.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P138-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying what they read, what they wondered and one detail, [[s04-P138-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P138-6]]. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 137 exit ticket, s04-X137, with one comment each. The commonest slip is likely a whole card copied, error Q: say the note in a few words and name the source. *2 min.* Read the four look-fors on the board aloud together. Say: our plan needs all four.

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return the day 137 page, with the card answers, and the research notes, each with one comment. Children read the comment silently and mark one thing to change, [[s04-P138-7]]. The change is made on day 140. Say: this is a first look and not a grade. Collect the page and the notes again after the 5 minutes; they come back on day 139.

#### 10 min. Reading

Children read the card Channel Checks again on the day 137 page. Say: use the sentences around a word to work out what it means. Children say what restores means, [[s04-P138-8]]. Review: you used the sentence clue for a word on day 127. Then read the story strip aloud: two lines, one that Lena says and one that the narrator tells. Children say which line the narrator tells, [[s04-P138-9]]. Review: you told a narrator from a character on day 133. Collect both answers; written feedback comes back on day 139.

#### 25 min. Plan (O04)

*3 min.* Model one list line on the board with a topic the children are not planning: "Position: our class should explain pollution first. Reason 1: rain carries oil and litter into streams (Water on Earth, section 7)." Say the line is a list, not a paragraph. Review: you filled a four-box chart for an opinion on day 98. *18 min.* Children plan on the opinion plan page, a list with no paragraph frame: topic, opinion, two reasons each with a source fact and its source, link words and a closing sentence. The plan page is a working page kept in the folder, not a judged item. Say the facts come from the research notes, not from memory. First each child does the tick check on the half plan on the student page, [[s04-P138-13]] (the half plan has a position and one reason only), then finishes it: reason 2, which supports the position, [[s04-P138-10]]; the link word, [[s04-P138-11]]; and the closing sentence, [[s04-P138-12]]. Confer with children who have no source fact; a liking is not a reason, error M. *4 min.* Children check the plan against the four look-fors and tick only what it has, [[s04-P138-14]]. Keep the plans in the folder.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child says the plan aloud to a partner: the position, then each reason with its source, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P138-15]]. Listen in on three pairs.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence about a conclusion. Children circle the words that tell what a conclusion does, [[s04-P138-16]]. Check the circled words against the sentence. Review: you met conclusion on day 136.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X138]]. Support is recorded. Shown: one sentence that picks a choice. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which one will the class explain first?" Not yet seen: a topic with no choice, error L. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the log line, plays the partner for the plan talk and sets the exit on paper. One projector: show the four look-fors and one model line; children plan on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed plan page: write on a plain sheet.
**Skip.** The log line and the word in context.

- **15 min. Plan.** Read section 6 of Water on Earth aloud.
  **Say.** "What should a class explain first, and why?" Expect a choice with a fact from the page. Your child writes an opinion and one reason on a plain sheet.
- **10 min. Words.** Say against and answer; your child writes them, [[s04-P138-1]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X138]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the plan, the check against the look-fors and the exit; cut the -ed rule and the word in context. Longer: add a third reason with a source.

### Words and supports

Opinion, position, reason, source fact, link word, conclusion, plan. Frames (for the spoken sentence): "Our class should explain ___ first because ___ (source)." "My plan has ___ reasons." A printed plan list on a card; extra time at the plan; a drawing with a spoken reason may stand in; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 139 opens with the commonest slip on s04-X138, likely a topic with no choice, error L: add the choice the essay will defend. It then drafts the topic and opinion sentence and the first reason from the plan, checks plurals and uses an abstract noun. The plans go in the folder.
