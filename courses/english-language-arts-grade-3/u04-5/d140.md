# Day 140: Revise the research and draft a reason for a new reader

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child. Put the day 137 pages, note sheets and comparisons, with their comments, on the desks. Write on the board: what does the reader not yet know? Choose five children to confer with; start with children not heard on day 139.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Say heart and early twice each; both have ear, said /ar/ in heart and /ur/ in early. Children write both, [[s04-P140-1]]. *5 min.* Say: un- is a word part at the front; it changes the meaning. Say the parts of unfair, un and fair. Children build unfair and unclear, [[s04-P140-2]]. Review: you added un- to a base on day 11. Watch for the base respelled.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P140-3]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child naming a book, a question and one detail, [[s04-P140-4]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P140-5]]. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 139 exit ticket, s04-X139, and the day 139 page with a tick on each written task. Write dish and dishes side by side and say both. The commonest slip is likely dishs, error AO: compare dish and dishes before editing another plural. *2 min.* Ask: "What does the reader not yet know?" Children answer for the topic they chose. Say: a reader needs the idea explained before the fact.

#### 15 min. Revise

Children use the comments from days 138 and 139. Each child makes one written change to the research notes, [[s04-P140-6]]; one to the comparison, [[s04-P140-7]]; and one to the card answers, [[s04-P140-8]], marking each change. Review: you revised from a written note on day 112. Walk round; for a note that is a whole sentence, error Q, say it in a few words with its source. Keep the pages in the folder.

#### 25 min. Draft (O04)

*3 min.* Model on the board with a topic the children are not writing: "Groundwater is water under the ground. Wells reach it (Water on Earth, words to know)." Say the first sentence tells the reader the idea and the second gives the source detail. Review: you explained a word for a younger reader on day 23. *2 min.* Children pick the better of two openings for a reader who does not know runoff, [[s04-P140-9]]. *20 min.* Children write one reason paragraph for a reader who does not know their topic: first what the idea means, then one source detail. The paragraph is a working page kept in the folder, not a judged item. Confer with five children, about 4 minutes each, each saying what the reader did not know and where the paragraph tells it, [[s04-P140-10]]. Watch for a slogan or a liking in place of the idea, errors W and M. Keep the drafts in the folder.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child reads the reason paragraph to a partner and asks what the partner knows now, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P140-11]]. Listen in on three pairs.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Write unsafe. Say: un- and safe are two parts. Children say what unsafe means from its parts, [[s04-P140-12]]. Check it on the board: not safe. Say the same move works on unfair and unclear. Review: you read un- words by their parts on day 18. Watch for more safe, error AT.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X140]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a sentence that says what the screen does for this reader. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What did the screen catch?" Not yet seen: a slogan or a liking, errors W and M. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the log line, is the partner for the reading aloud and confers on the draft. One projector: show the model and the board question; children revise and draft on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No comments to hand: reread your child's notes and choose one to shorten.
**Skip.** The log line and the revision.

- **15 min. Reason.** Read section 7 of Water on Earth aloud.
  **Say.** "What does a reader not yet know about this?" Expect an idea such as pollution or runoff. Your child writes what the idea means and one detail with its source.
- **10 min. Words.** Say unsafe; your child says what it means from its parts, [[s04-P140-12]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X140]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reason paragraph, the word in context and the exit; cut the long words and the revision of the card answers. Longer: add a second detail to the paragraph.

### Words and supports

Reader, idea, detail, source, revise, unsafe. Frames: "The reader does not know ___." "___ means ___. For example, ___ (source)." The model on a card; extra time; a drawing with a spoken explanation may stand in; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 141 returns s04-X140 with a comment at the draft, prepares a passage and holds a formal discussion, practices the link words because, so and for example, and drafts a second reason linked to the opinion. The reason paragraph and revised pages go in the folder.
