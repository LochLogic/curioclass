# Day 139: Draft the opening and the first reason

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child. Put the plan pages and ruled paper on the desks. Sort the day 137 card answers, with feedback, for the second look. Keep the four look-fors on the board. Choose six children to confer with; note the children you do not reach.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Say thought and straight twice each; thought has ough said /aw/ and straight has aigh said /ate/. Children write both, [[s04-P139-1]]. *3 min.* Say: a word that ends in x adds es to make more than one. Children write two plurals, [[s04-P139-2]]. Review: you used the ending rules for -s and -es on day 132. *3 min.* Children draw lines between the chunks of two long words, [[s04-P139-3]]. Review: you split four-chunk words on day 128. Watch for a cut inside a chunk.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Wednesday

*5 min.* Say: an abstract noun names an idea you cannot touch. Children circle the abstract nouns in a list, [[s04-P139-4]]. Review: you learned abstract nouns on day 57 and used them on day 136. *15 min.* Partners reread the first paragraph of Water that stays to each other, twice each, in phrases, [[s04-P139-5]]. The listener marks one look-for on the feedback card. Listen to three pairs and give each one comment. Review: you reread with the feedback card on day 134.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 138 exit ticket, s04-X138, and the day 138 page with a tick on each written task. The commonest slip is likely a topic with no choice, error L. Say: add the choice the essay will defend. Children add the choice to the sentence they wrote. *2 min.* Hand the day 137 card answers back (collected on day 138) for a second look; children check that they understand the comment before the revision on day 140.

#### 5 min. Plural check

Say the plural of each word with your partner: child, mouse, tooth, foot; each says the four, then uses two in a spoken sentence, [[s04-P139-6]]. Say the singular and the plural together. Review: you said irregular plurals together on day 136. Listen to three pairs.

#### 30 min. Draft (O04)

Children write from the plan; the facts come from the research notes. Opening sentence with the topic and the opinion, [[s04-P139-7]]. Then the first reason with a fact and its source, [[s04-P139-8]]. Say: use one plural noun and one abstract noun where they fit, then mark them, [[s04-P139-9]]. Review: you wrote an opinion with a reason and a link on day 98. Confer with six children, 4 minutes each (24 minutes), each saying how the first reason fits the opinion, [[s04-P139-10]]. Start with children not heard on day 138. Watch for a liking in place of a source fact, error M; the others write.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child reads the first reason aloud to a partner and says its source, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P139-11]]. Listen in on three pairs.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Say: for example introduces one case. Children finish one sentence with a case, [[s04-P139-12]]. Review: you met for example on day 103. *2 min.* Check one finished sentence on the board: the case must be a case of the idea before it.

#### 10 min. Exit

*5 min.* Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X139]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a reason from the source with dishes spelled in full. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What did the helper do?" Not yet seen: dishs, error AO, or a liking, error M. Record on the record sheet. *5 min.* Children reread their draft, change one plural or one link word and mark it, [[s04-P139-13]]. Keep the drafts in the folder.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner for the reread and the plural check, and confers on the draft for the whole conference time. One projector: show the plan and one opening sentence; children draft on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No plan page: use a plain sheet.
**Skip.** The reread and the word in context.

- **15 min. Draft.** Read section 6 of Water on Earth aloud.
  **Say.** "What is your first reason, and where did you read it?" Expect a fact from the page and its name. Your child writes the opening and the reason, [[s04-P139-7]] and [[s04-P139-8]].
- **10 min. Words.** Say box and fox; your child writes the plurals, [[s04-P139-2]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X139]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the opening, the first reason and the exit; cut the long words and the reread. Longer: add a link phrase with for example to the first reason.

### Words and supports

Plural, abstract noun, example, reason, source, topic. Frames: "Our class should explain ___ first because ___ (source)." "For example, ___." The plan page and a printed plural card (child, children; mouse, mice) for any child; extra time; a drawing with a spoken reason may stand in; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 140 opens with the commonest slip on s04-X139, likely dishs, error AO: compare dish and dishes before editing another plural. It then names what a reader does not yet know, revises the research notes, the comparison and the card answers after the feedback, and drafts one reason paragraph with a source detail. The drafts go in the folder.
