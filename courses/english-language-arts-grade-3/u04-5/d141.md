# Day 141: Hold a discussion and draft a second reason

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child. Make six groups of five; put children not heard on day 132 in the groups you will record. Write the discussion moves on the board: look, wait, link, ask for a detail, say what you understand now. Open the record sheet at the discussion record.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Say young and world twice each; young has ou said /u/ and world has or said /ur/. Children write both, [[s04-P141-1]]. *6 min.* Say: -ly tells how; -ful means full of. Children add -ly to glad and -ful to thank and hope, [[s04-P141-2]]. Review: you added -ly and -ful on days 134 and 27. Watch for a double l in -ful, error x.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P141-3]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying what they read and what it made them wonder, [[s04-P141-4]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P141-5]]. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 15 min. Prepare and read

*5 min.* Each child chooses one passage from the water texts that helps their opinion and marks the evidence in it, [[s04-P141-6]]. Review: you marked a passage for a claim on day 105. *5 min.* Partners reread the marked passage to each other in phrases, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over; the listener says what it tells, [[s04-P141-7]]. *5 min.* Each child writes one question that asks the group for a detail, [[s04-P141-8]]. Review: you asked for a detail in a discussion on day 130.

#### 10 min. Discussion

Six groups of five talk at once, 2 minutes a turn; each child speaks once. The talk itself is not judged. After it, each child writes one thing they understand now and the passage that shows it, [[s04-P141-9]]. Say the moves: look, wait, link, ask for a detail, say what you understand now. Review: you used the norms look, wait and link on day 130. Children not heard on day 132 are heard first. You may note the five boxes for two groups as your own notes: a prepared passage, a link to a peer, a request for detail, an explanation after listening, a turn kept. Every child may pass; no child is held up as the example of a mistake.

#### 5 min. Link practice

Children join three pairs with the link word shown, [[s04-P141-10]]. Say: because gives the reason, so gives the result, for example gives one case. Review: you used the link words by relation on day 103. Watch for a link word that misstates the relationship, error N.

#### 20 min. Draft (O04)

Return the day 140 exit ticket, s04-X140, with a comment as the drafting begins. Children write a second reason paragraph from the plan: a reason, one source detail and a link phrase that joins it to the opinion, [[s04-P141-11]]. Review: you explained an idea before the fact on day 140. Confer with children whose reason is a liking, error M, or whose link word misstates, error N. Keep the drafts in the folder.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence about a link. Children circle the words that tell what a link does, [[s04-P141-12]]. Check them against the sentence. Review: you met link words on day 98.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X141]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a reply to the peer that joins the notes to the opinion with a fitting link word. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What do the notes say?" Not yet seen: a liking, error M, a link word that misstates, error N, or a change of subject, error AH. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the group, hears the child say what the passage shows and links to one point; the child drafts and answers the exit on paper. One projector: show the discussion moves; children work in groups of five with the printed passages.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed texts: use any short page about water.
**Skip.** The log line and the link practice.

- **15 min. Reason.** Read section 8 of Water on Earth aloud.
  **Say.** "Which fact would help your opinion, and what word joins them?" Expect because, so or for example. Your child writes a second reason with a source, [[s04-P141-11]].
- **10 min. Words.** Say young and world; your child writes them, [[s04-P141-1]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X141]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the passage, the discussion, the second reason and the exit; cut the long words and the word in context. Longer: write a link phrase for each of the three link words.

### Words and supports

Passage, evidence, link, because, so, for example, peer. Frames: "I add to ___: ___." "What detail shows ___?" "Now I understand ___." Sentence frames may be faded as children are ready; a printed list of the moves; extra time; a drawing with a spoken reason may stand in; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 142 begins the next section. It returns the day 141 draft with a written move for error L, M or N, then drafts a conclusion tied to the opinion and edits irregular plurals. The drafts and the discussion pages go in the folder.
