# Exit cards, days 137 to 141

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 137 (s04-X137), Exit.** Invented for practice. Wells card: A cap keeps dirt out of a well. Taps card: A tap is turned off when it is not used. Ask: Write a brief note for solutions from the Wells card. Name its source.
- **Day 138 (s04-X138), Exit.** Invented for practice. Sign: "Please keep fuel out of the river." Ask: Which should a class explain first: stopping fuel before it enters, or testing after? Write one opinion sentence.
- **Day 139 (s04-X139), Exit.** Invented for practice. Plan: "Explain why care matters." Source: "A helper stacked one clean dish on top of another dish." Ask: Write a first reason sentence from the source. Use the plural of dish.
- **Day 140 (s04-X140), Exit.** Invented for practice. Source: "The intake screen caught twigs. Workers checked it after rain." Reader: "I do not know what a screen does." Ask: Write one sentence that tells this reader what the screen does.
- **Day 141 (s04-X141), Exit.** Invented for practice. Notes: "A cracked lid lets dirt in. A new lid keeps dirt out." Opinion: "Explain lid care first." Peer: "I think lids do not matter." Ask: Write one reply to your peer. Join the notes to the opinion with a link word.

Every exit sheet prints the card and the ask only, with a ruled space for the answer. The look-fors live in items.csv and the record sheet, never on the child's page.
