# Day 137: Research a water issue and keep brief notes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, with the card Channel Checks on side 2, and the research note sheet. Set the fifteen devices at a search box with the three texts, or lay out the highlighted prints. Pair the children: one half takes the first device turn. Keep the four-category sheet from day 128 beside each note sheet. Open the record sheet.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Say through, then country twice each. Children write both, [[s04-P137-1]]. Review: you met through on day 83. Country is new: ou says /u/. *3 min.* Say the -ing rule: drop a silent e, double the last letter after a short vowel, keep the y. Children add -ing to three words, [[s04-P137-2]]. Review: you used these ending rules on day 130. *3 min.* Say research and evidence in chunks, then children write them, [[s04-P137-3]]. Review: you split these words on day 134. Watch for a word spelled by its sounds.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Monday

*5 min.* Say: a pronoun stands for a noun. It stands for one; they stands for more than one. Children write it or they in two gaps, [[s04-P137-4]]. Review: you matched it and they to their nouns on days 127 and 129. *15 min.* Partners reread the first paragraph of How a Town Cleans Its Water to each other, twice each, in phrases, [[s04-P137-5]]. The listener marks one look-for on the feedback card. Listen to three pairs and give each one comment. Review: you reread with the feedback card on day 134.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 136 page with a tick on each written task (s04-P136-1 to s04-P136-19) and the exit ticket s04-X136. The commonest slip was likely error R, a plain s on an irregular plural. Say foot and feet, then tooth and teeth, together; children say them back. Review: you said irregular plurals together on day 136. *2 min.* Ask: "Which heading finds how workers clean the water?" Children point to The steps in the print. Say the keyword: steps.

#### 25 min. Reading

*5 min.* Feature search. Children use the headings of Water on Earth, [[s04-P137-6]]. Say: "Which heading and keyword find this fastest?" Review: you used a heading to find a section on day 54. *10 min.* Silent comparison. Children reread two short sections and write a shared point and a different point, [[s04-P137-7]]. Review: you found a shared point, an idea and not a word, on day 126. *10 min.* Read the card Channel Checks aloud once. Children answer four questions on side 2, [[s04-P137-8]], [[s04-P137-9]], [[s04-P137-10]] and [[s04-P137-11]]. Review: you read a diagram beside its text on day 121. Collect the card answers for written feedback; they come back on day 138.

#### 20 min. Research

*3 min.* Model one note: demand, under eight words, with its source: "More homes need more water (Water on Earth, section 6)". Say the source tag is a few words, the book and the section. Review: you wrote a note with a source tag on day 57. *2 min.* Children choose one water issue from access, demand or pollution, [[s04-P137-12]]. *10 min.* Two turns of 5 minutes at a device, or on the highlighted print. Each child writes at least four brief notes on the note sheet, each with a source and one category from access, demand, pollution or solutions. The notes are working pages kept for day 144, not a judged item. Review: you sorted notes into the four categories on day 128. *5 min.* Children file four given notes under a category, [[s04-P137-13]]. Watch for a whole sentence copied on the sheet, error Q, a note filed under the wrong category, error AQ, or a fact the text never says, error AP. Keep the sheets in the folder.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child reads one note aloud to a partner and says its source and category, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P137-14]]. Listen in on three pairs.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X137]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a few words that name the cap and the Wells card. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What is the source?" Not yet seen: the whole card copied (error Q) or a category that does not fit (error AQ). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner for the reread and the talk, and the child answers the exit on paper. One projector: show the headings once; children work on the highlighted print and the two device turns become two print turns.

### At home

**Swap.** No devices: use a printed page of Water on Earth.
**Skip.** The partner reread and the card.

- **15 min. Notes.** Read section 5 of Water on Earth aloud.
  **Say.** "What is one thing that makes water hard to reach?" Expect a long walk or waiting. Your child writes a few words and the book name.
- **10 min. Words.** Say through and country; your child writes them, [[s04-P137-1]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X137]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the card answers, the notes and the exit; cut the -ing rule and the pronoun gaps. Longer: write a fifth note under a second category.

### Words and supports

Research, evidence, source, category, pronoun, issue. Frames: "A brief note is a few words and its source." "I filed this under ___ because ___." A card with the four category names; a word strip with a picture for each; extra time at the device turn; typing may replace pencil; a drawing with a spoken note may stand in; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 138 returns the card answers and the research notes with one comment each, reads the card again and the story strip, and plans the opinion essay as a list of a position and two reasons with sources. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X137, likely a whole sentence copied: shorten it to a few words with its source.

**Channel Checks.**

A blocked channel stopped rain from reaching a garden tank. Workers unblocked it before the next rain.

The next rain reached the tank because its path was clear. Clearing a path restores movement. Inspecting it afterward checks whether movement continues.

Diagram: roof, then channel, then tank, joined by arrows. Leaves are crossed out at the channel.

Glossary: channel, a path carrying water. Blocked, stopped from passing.
