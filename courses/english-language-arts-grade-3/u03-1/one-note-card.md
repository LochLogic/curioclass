# One-note, one-change card (day 86, used again on days 93, 99 and later)

Print one for each child, half a page. The original answer stays beside the revision. Recopying with no change is not a revision.

- The note I read on my paper: ____________________
- The one change I will make: ____________________
- Why this change helps the reader: ____________________
- My revised answer is written beside the first one.
