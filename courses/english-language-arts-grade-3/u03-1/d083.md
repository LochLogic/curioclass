# Day 83: Read chapter 3 and say how it builds on chapter 2

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a chapter map frame for each child. Return the day 82 strips and exit boxes face down. Mark the page where Nia and Sam see the table (from "At that moment, the classroom door opened" to the notebook question). Pick four children for conferences and two to read a log line. Mark them in the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P83-1]]: split, check each chunk, then blend and write. Then through and laugh, [[s03-P83-2]]; mark the odd part. Review: you mapped through and laugh in grade 1. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 3, [[s03-P83-3]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its paragraph, [[s03-P83-4]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one ending-rule line, [[s03-P83-5]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P83-6]]. The teacher holds four 4-minute conferences, [[s03-P83-7]] (16 minutes, one record each). Two children read a log line aloud, [[s03-P83-8]], 2 minutes each. Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 82 strips and exit boxes, s03-X82 and s03-P82-1. Repair error E (one person's view given to another): the strip beside its repair, [[s03-K83-1]]. *1 min.* Pairs look at the chapter 2 map and say the last thing that happened, [[s03-P83-9]]. *1 min.* Pairs say one thing chapter 3 could add, [[s03-P83-10]]. Nothing is scored.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 3 aloud (about 890 words). Stop once to point at the small table. *8 min.* Pairs reread the page where Nia and Sam have no place for two (about 230 words), [[s03-P83-11]]: one reads, one listens, then swap. *4 min.* Share. Three pairs say what "my plan hit a wall" means, in one sentence, [[s03-P83-12]]. Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Children map chapter 3 on the next frame, [[s03-P83-13]]. Leave the link row blank today. *5 min.* A new idea: a chapter builds on the one before it when a choice in the first causes a trouble in the next. Children draw one arrow on their map from the choice in chapter 2 to the trouble in chapter 3 and write so or because on it, [[s03-P83-14]]. Watch for error B: the arrow running backward. *10 min.* Children reread the sentence where the plan hits trouble and write why Ada's plan had to change, [[s03-P83-15]]. Watch for error C: a feeling with no action.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Phrase: hit a wall. Review: the context and figurative chart from unit 1. Children draw a real wall, then write what Ada meant beside it, [[s03-P83-16]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P83-17]]: one partner says the choice in chapter 2, the other says the trouble in chapter 3. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X83]]. Shown: the earlier choice, a cause word and the later change, in order. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What did Kai choose first?" Not yet seen: the later change given as the cause (error B), or a motive the card does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 3 aloud and is the reread partner; the child draws the arrow. No printer: draw the frame on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The slate words and the pair rereads.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and writes one log line.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you how chapter 3 grew out of chapter 2.
  **Say.** "What choice in chapter 2 caused the trouble?" Expect: the small table and no room for two.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X83]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the arrow and the exit. Longer: draw the fourth plan Ada makes after the trouble.

### Words and supports

Chapter, choice, trouble, builds on, hit a wall. Frames: "The choice was ___, so ___." and "Ada meant her plan ___." Large print; a scribe for the explanation; oral rehearsal before the share; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 84 returns the day 83 strip, s03-X83, and repairs error B first: put the choice before the change. It reads chapter 4 and tells the narrator's view from a character's and the reader's. The slate work, [[s03-P83-1]], [[s03-P83-2]], [[s03-P83-3]] and [[s03-P83-5]], the log work, [[s03-P83-6]], [[s03-P83-7]] and [[s03-P83-8]], the spoken work, [[s03-P83-4]], [[s03-P83-9]], [[s03-P83-10]], [[s03-P83-11]], [[s03-P83-12]], [[s03-P83-17]] and [[s03-K83-1]], and the map, the arrow, the explanation and the drawing, [[s03-P83-13]], [[s03-P83-14]], [[s03-P83-15]] and [[s03-P83-16]], stay in the folder.
