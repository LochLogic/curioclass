# Exit cards, days 81 to 86

Strips marked From the book print lines from the unit's own books, with the chapter named, and carry no invented label. For a card marked Invented for practice, say aloud "This card is made up for practice" and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 81 (s03-X81v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 1: "Ben walked across the courtyard and into the empty classroom. Ada followed him, because she could not stand to wait." Ask: Copy the words that tell why Ada followed Ben.
- **Day 82 (s03-X82v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: the article, paragraph 5: "I kept every old draft in the same folder, and I think this is very useful. An old draft shows what I changed and why." Ask: What does the writer think about keeping old drafts? Write one sentence.
- **Day 82 (s03-P82-1v2), Student page, practice.** From the book. The article, paragraph 7: "Making a series takes patience. It takes notes, drafts, questions and many small checks. That is only my view, and you may have a different one. What do you think a series needs most?" Ask: What do you think a series needs most? Give a reason from the article.
- **Day 83 (s03-X83v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 1: "The first plan was wrong, she knew that. It had a door problem." Chapter 2: "At the end of lunch, Ada picked up the first plan and began to crumple it." Ask: How does the next chapter grow from the door problem?
- **Day 84 (s03-X84v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 2: "A wrong plan is no use to anyone," said Ada. Ben held out his hand. "Then may I have it?" Ada gave it to him. She thought he was being silly, and so, if we are honest, did the narrator. Ask: Copy the sentence that gives the narrator's view of Ben.
- **Day 84 (s03-P84-1v2), Student page, practice.** From the book. Book 1, chapter 2: Ben clips the old drawing in his folder. "If we ever forget why we moved the corner, we can look." Ask: Write your own view of keeping it. Give a reason from the chapter.
- **Day 85 (s03-X85v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 5: "So she had hidden the plan. She had put it at the bottom of her backpack. And all morning she had carried it, as heavy as a rock." Ask: What does "as heavy as a rock" mean here? Use a clue.
- **Day 86 (s03-X86), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "The brown cat sleeps. It dreams." Ask: What job does It do in the second sentence?

The two student-page practice items (days 82 and 84) go on the day's student page, each with a blank line under it. They are marked by the teacher with the same look-fors as the exit.
