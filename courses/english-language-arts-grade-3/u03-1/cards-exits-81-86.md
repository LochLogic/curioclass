# Exit cards, days 81 to 86

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 81 (s03-X81), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Tara set a wet paint sign by the bench. Leo read it. He pressed his thumb on the bench to check. His thumb came away green." Ask: Copy the sentence that tells what happened because Leo pressed the bench.
- **Day 82 (s03-X82), Exit.** Invented for practice. Passage: "The town painted a mural on the library wall. I think one weekend was the right time because many hands helped. Ten people finished before the rain." Ask: What does the writer think about one weekend? Write one sentence.
- **Day 82 (s03-P82-1), Student page, practice.** Invented for practice. Passage: "The class pond has no fence. I believe a fence is needed because small children visit it. Mr. Orr says a bright sign is enough." Ask: Write your own view of the fence, with a reason from the passage.
- **Day 83 (s03-X83), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Earlier: Kai picks a thin vase for his sunflowers. Later: The tall sunflowers lean and the vase tips over." Ask: How does the later part grow from the earlier choice?
- **Day 84 (s03-X84), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Hugo says the pot has a crack. The narrator says Hugo is rude. Dina says, 'I can fix it.'" Ask: Copy the sentence that says Hugo is rude.
- **Day 84 (s03-P84-1), Student page, practice.** Invented for practice. Card: "Zoe wants a second bake sale table. The narrator says Zoe is bossy. Ned says, 'It would help us.'" Ask: Write your own view of Zoe's idea. Give a reason from the card.
- **Day 85 (s03-X85), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Wren has news to share. Her brother puts down his book and turns to her. 'Go ahead, I'm all ears,' he says." Ask: What does "I'm all ears" mean here? Use a clue from the card.
- **Day 86 (s03-X86), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "The brown cat sleeps. It dreams." Ask: What job does It do in the second sentence?

The two student-page practice items (days 82 and 84) go on the day's student page, each with a blank line under it. They are marked by the teacher with the same look-fors as the exit.
