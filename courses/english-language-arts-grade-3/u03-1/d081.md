# Day 81: Read chapter 1 and start the chapter map

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and one chapter map frame for each child, and the hook drawings for the room. Read chapter 1 once yourself. Mark the door page (from "Ben stood in the doorway" to "I don't know yet"). Pick four children for conferences and two to read a log line. Mark them in the heard-six column. Day 80 ended Check 1 and taught nothing for this unit.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P81-1]]: split each into chunks, check each chunk, then blend and write it whole. A new skill: three-syllable words are split into chunks first. Then people and answer, [[s03-P81-2]]; mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. Review: you mapped people in grade 2 and answer on day 40. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 1, [[s03-P81-3]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its paragraph, [[s03-P81-4]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one line of ending rules on slates, [[s03-P81-5]]. Review: you met drop the e, double the last letter and change y on day 33.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P81-6]]. Review: the reading log and the four-minute conference, from day 4. The teacher holds four 4-minute conferences, [[s03-P81-7]] (16 minutes, one record each). Two children read a log line aloud, [[s03-P81-8]], 2 minutes each. Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*2 min.* Show the two hook drawings of one reading corner. Ask the series question once, aloud: "Would you recommend this series to a friend, and why?" Nothing is scored; the class will answer it in the weeks ahead. Pairs tell which corner they would use and what in the drawing says so, [[s03-P81-9]]. *3 min.* Retrieve the parts of a tale on The Lion and the Mouse, [[s03-P81-10]]: pairs, 30 seconds each. Review: you used a plan card with a situation, events in order and an ending in unit 2.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 1 aloud (about 790 words). Stop twice to point at the two plans. A chapter is one part of a book; each chapter builds on the one before it. *8 min.* Pairs reread the door page (about 190 words), [[s03-P81-11]]: one reads, one listens, then swap. *4 min.* Share. Three pairs say the problem in one sentence, [[s03-P81-12]]. Expect: the door swings into the rug, so the first plan fails. Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Model the start of the chapter map on the board, [[s03-K81-1]]: the title, the two plans and the problem. *7 min.* Children copy the frame and mark the two plans and the opening problem, [[s03-P81-13]]. *8 min.* Children write one or two sentences, [[s03-P81-14]]: what did Ben's pointing at the door change? Watch for error B: the change given as the cause. Watch for error A: an action the chapter never gives.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: plan. Pairs, 1 minute each, [[s03-P81-15]]: what it means in chapter 1 (a drawing and an idea of where things go), and one other use of the word.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P81-16]]: which of the two plans would you try first, and what in the chapter makes you say so? Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X81v2]]. Shown: the copied words give the reason, because she could not stand to wait. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Why did Ada go in?" Not yet seen: what Ada did copied as the reason (error B), or a reason the chapter does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 1 aloud and is the reread partner; the child fills the map. No printer: draw the frame on a folded sheet and write the hook drawings on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The slate words and the pair rereads.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and writes one log line.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you the two plans from chapter 1 in three sentences.
  **Say.** "Which plan would you try first?" Expect: a plan and a reason from the chapter.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X81v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the map and the exit. Longer: draw the corner from the second plan.

### Words and supports

Chapter, plan, series, problem, three-syllable, chunk. Frames: "The first plan went wrong because ___." and "I would try plan ___ because ___." Large print; a scribe for the map; oral rehearsal before the share; record any help.
