# Day 84: Read chapter 4 and tell the narrator's view from a character's and yours

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a view table and a chapter map frame for each child. Return the day 83 strips face down. Mark the nuisance page of chapter 4 (from "Mateo tried to squeeze through the gap" to "the room that is wrong"). Pair the class and set the feedback cards. Choose three pairs to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P84-2]]: split, check each chunk, then blend and write. Then enough and whole, [[s03-P84-3]]; mark the odd part. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 4, [[s03-P84-4]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its paragraph, [[s03-P84-5]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one ending-rule line, [[s03-P84-6]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Grammar retrieval on slates, two sentences from chapter 3. A new word: the subject is who or what the sentence is about. Write the subject and the verb, [[s03-P84-7]] and [[s03-P84-8]]. *15 min.* Pairs reread the start of chapter 3 with the feedback card, no speed target, [[s03-P84-9]]. The teacher hears three pairs for 3 minutes each (six children, 9 minutes) and floats for the rest. Review: partner rereading with the card, from day 17.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 83 strips, s03-X83. Repair error B (the later change given as the cause): put the choice first and join it with so or because, [[s03-K84-1]]. *2 min.* Retrieve from grade 2: pairs, 30 seconds each, using the discussion moves chart (the four steps from grade 2 plus the fifth move you added in grade 3), [[s03-P84-10]]. Review: you learned point of view in grade 2, what a character thinks and wants; the narrator is the teller, from day 41.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 4 aloud (about 805 words): a fourth plan, the shelf blocks the door, and the narrator calls Ben's warning a nuisance. *8 min.* Pairs reread the nuisance page (about 200 words), [[s03-P84-11]]: one reads, one listens, then swap. *4 min.* Share. Three pairs say whose word nuisance is and point to the sentence, [[s03-P84-12]]. Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Model a sort on the board, [[s03-K84-2]]: strips go in the character says row or the narrator says row of the view table. *8 min.* Children sort four strips from chapter 4 into the first two rows of their own table, [[s03-P84-13]]. *7 min.* Each child fixes one wrong label, [[s03-P84-14]]. Leave the I think row for the talk. Watch for error E: the narrator's word given to a character.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: nuisance. Pairs, 1 minute each, [[s03-P84-15]]: what it means and whose word it is.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P84-16]], with the frame "The narrator thinks ___; I think ___ because ___." Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X84]]. Then children answer [[s03-P84-1]] on the page, in the exit box. Shown: the sentence that gives the view to the narrator. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Who says Hugo is rude?" Not yet seen: a character's words given as the narrator's (error E), or a fact the card does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 4 aloud and is the reread partner; the child fills the table. No printer: draw the table as three rows on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No chapter copy: use any story with a teller.
**Skip.** The slate words and the feedback card.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and writes one log line.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you what the narrator thinks of Ben's warning and what they think.
  **Say.** "Whose word is nuisance?" Expect: the narrator's.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X84]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the table, the nuisance page and the exit. Longer: write the narrator's view of a second character.

### Words and supports

Narrator, character, view, nuisance, subject, verb. Frames: "The narrator thinks ___." and "I think ___ because ___." Large print; a scribe for the table; oral rehearsal before the share; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 85 is a check day on a new passage and needs nothing from day 84; no reteaching happens on day 85. Day 86 returns the day 84 strip and exit box, s03-X84 and s03-P84-1, and repairs error E first: label whose words give each view. The slate work, [[s03-P84-2]], [[s03-P84-3]], [[s03-P84-4]], [[s03-P84-6]], [[s03-P84-7]] and [[s03-P84-8]], the spoken work, [[s03-P84-5]], [[s03-P84-9]], [[s03-P84-10]], [[s03-P84-11]], [[s03-P84-12]], [[s03-P84-15]], [[s03-P84-16]], [[s03-K84-1]] and [[s03-K84-2]], and the table, [[s03-P84-13]] and [[s03-P84-14]], stay in the folder.
