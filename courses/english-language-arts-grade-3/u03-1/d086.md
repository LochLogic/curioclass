# Day 86: Use feedback and read chapter 6: what each word does

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one note on each day 85 packet the evening before: the one change that would help most. Print the student page and a one-note card for each child. Sort the packets, the day 84 strips and exit boxes, and the day 85 strips into groups by error letter, at most four groups. Mark the signs page of chapter 6 (the four signs). Pick four children for conferences and two to read a log line.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P86-1]]: split, check each chunk, then blend and write. Then friend and thought, [[s03-P86-2]]; mark the odd part. Review: you mapped thought on day 82. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 6, [[s03-P86-3]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its paragraph, [[s03-P86-4]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one ending-rule line, [[s03-P86-5]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P86-6]]. The teacher holds four 4-minute conferences, [[s03-P86-7]] (16 minutes, one record each). Two children read a log line aloud, [[s03-P86-8]], 2 minutes each. Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Returned feedback

Hand back day 85's assessment, the packets s03-A1 to s03-A5, each with its note. Children read the note and write the one change they will make on the one-note card, [[s03-P86-9]]. A new routine: one note, one change.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

*2 min.* Whole class: one wrong answer from the packet beside its correction, [[s03-K86-1]]. Say why the correction fits the words. *6 min.* Hand back the day 84 strips and exit boxes, s03-X84v2 and s03-P84-1v2, and the day 85 strips, s03-X85v2. At most four teacher groups by error letter take 1.5 minutes each; everyone else applies the same move in pairs. Error E (a view given to the wrong person): label whose words give each judgment, [[s03-P86-10]]. Error D (a phrase read as a picture): say what the phrase really means and the clue, [[s03-P86-11]]. *2 min.* Close in pairs: tell your partner the one change you are making, [[s03-P86-12]].

#### 25 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 6 aloud (about 930 words): the four signs, spoken by the characters. *8 min.* Pairs reread the signs page (about 200 words), one reads and one listens, then swap. *9 min.* Function. A word class has a job: a noun names, a verb acts, an adjective describes, an adverb tells how, and a pronoun stands in for a noun. Model a sort on the board, [[s03-K86-2]]. Children match four words to their jobs, [[s03-P86-13]], and circle one verb, [[s03-P86-14]]. Watch for error X: the class named with no job.

#### 10 min. Writing

Revise one packet answer with the one-note card, [[s03-P86-15]]. The first answer stays beside the revision. Recopying with no change is error L.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P86-16]]: say a sentence of your own with a noun and an adverb; your partner points to each. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X86]]. Shown: what the word does, not only its class name. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What would the sentence say without it?" Not yet seen: the class named with no job (error X). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 6 aloud and is the reread partner; the child writes the revision. No printer: write the one-note card on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The slate words and the pair reread.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you what a noun and a verb do.
  **Say.** "What does a word like slowly do?" Expect: it tells how.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X86]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the note, the revision and the exit. Longer: find a word in the signs for each class.

### Words and supports

Noun, verb, adjective, adverb, pronoun, job, note, change. Frame: "___ is a ___; it ___." Large print; the notes read aloud; a scribe for the revision; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 87 returns the day 86 strip, s03-X86, and repairs error X first: say what the word does in its sentence. It reads chapter 7 and explains how its change grows from earlier events. The slate work, [[s03-P86-1]], [[s03-P86-2]], [[s03-P86-3]] and [[s03-P86-5]], the log work, [[s03-P86-6]], [[s03-P86-7]] and [[s03-P86-8]], the spoken work, [[s03-P86-4]], [[s03-P86-12]], [[s03-P86-16]], [[s03-K86-1]] and [[s03-K86-2]], and the card and revision, [[s03-P86-9]], [[s03-P86-10]], [[s03-P86-11]], [[s03-P86-13]], [[s03-P86-14]] and [[s03-P86-15]], stay in the folder.
