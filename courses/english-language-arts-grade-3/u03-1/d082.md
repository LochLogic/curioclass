# Day 82: Read chapter 2 and the series article, and tell the writer's view from yours

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a chapter map frame and the series article for each child. Return the day 81 strips face down. Mark the second-plan page of chapter 1 (from "She went back to the picnic table" to "I don't know yet"). Pair the class and set the feedback cards. Choose three pairs to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say three words for slates, [[s03-P82-2]]: split, check each chunk, then blend and write. Then thought and eyes, [[s03-P82-3]]; mark the odd part. Review: you mapped eyes on day 57. *3 min.* Dictate one sentence from chapter 2, [[s03-P82-4]]. *4 min.* In pairs, one word in its paragraph, [[s03-P82-5]]: read it by chunks, then say what it means. Last, one ending-rule line, [[s03-P82-6]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Grammar retrieval on slates, two sentences from chapter 1: write the noun, the verb and the adjective, [[s03-P82-7]] and [[s03-P82-8]]. Review: you named nouns, verbs and adjectives in grade 1. *15 min.* Pairs reread the second-plan page of chapter 1 with the feedback card, no speed target, [[s03-P82-9]]. The teacher hears three pairs for 3 minutes each (six children, 9 minutes) and floats for the rest. Review: partner rereading with the card, from day 17.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 81 strips, s03-X81v2. Repair error B (the action copied as its reason): say what Ada did, then why, [[s03-K82-1]]. *2 min.* Retrieve from grade 2: pairs say one way two versions of a fable can differ (grade 2, the fable versions), [[s03-P82-10]]. Say what an author does: an author wants readers to think or feel something.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Read chapter 2 aloud (about 870 words): Ben clips the old drawing into the PLANS folder. *6 min.* Pairs read the series article, taking turns by paragraph, [[s03-P82-11]]. *3 min.* Pairs mark the words that show what the writer thinks, [[s03-P82-12]]. A new idea: a writer's view is what the writer thinks; the writer marks it with words such as I think, I believe and the best. *3 min.* Share. Three pairs read aloud the words that show the writer's view, [[s03-P82-13]]. Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*4 min.* Model the sort on the board, [[s03-K82-2]]: a fact and the writer's view. *5 min.* Children map chapter 2 on the next frame, [[s03-P82-14]]. *6 min.* Children write beside each marked sentence whose view it is, [[s03-P82-15]]. *5 min.* Each child writes one question the article can answer and copies the sentence that answers it, [[s03-P82-16]]. Watch for error A: a question the article cannot answer.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: series. Pairs, 1 minute each, [[s03-P82-17]]: what it means in the article, and one series from home or school that is not a book of this unit.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P82-18]]: read one sentence you marked, say it is the writer's view, then say what you think about the same idea. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X82v2]]. Then children answer [[s03-P82-1v2]] on the page, in the exit box. Shown: the writer's own view, named as the writer's. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which words show what the writer thinks?" Not yet seen: a different view given as the writer's (error E), or a claim the passage does not make (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the article aloud and is the reread partner; the child marks the opinion words. No printer: write the article on the board and mark it together.

### At home

**Swap.** No article copy: use the opinion in any short note.
**Skip.** The slate words and the feedback card.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and writes one log line.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you one thing the article's writer thinks and one thing they think.
  **Say.** "Which words show what the writer thinks?" Expect: I think or I believe.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X82v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the article, the marking and the exit. Longer: write a second question the article answers.

### Words and supports

Series, author, view, opinion, fact, draft. Frames: "The writer thinks ___." and "I think ___ because ___." Large print; the article read aloud once; oral rehearsal before the share; record any help.
