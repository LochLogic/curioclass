# Day 85: Check: chapter 5, text answers, a character's actions and a phrase

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the packet for each child, with P85 once on side 2. Close the chapter books until the reading part. Say aloud: "This passage is made up for practice." Plan to answer nothing about content today. Day 84's strips and exit boxes are not returned today; they go back on day 86.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, retrieval only

No new pattern and no teaching. *10 min.* Long-word retrieval on paper: write six three-syllable words in chunks and whole, [[s03-P85-1]], then eight irregular words, [[s03-P85-2]]. *5 min.* Ending rules on slates, [[s03-P85-3]]. *15 min.* Independent reading from the log book, [[s03-P85-4]]. No conferences.

#### 20 min. Chapter unaided

Children read chapter 5 alone and silently (about 840 words; about 7 minutes at 120 words a minute, then rereading). The teacher answers nothing about the content.

#### 30 min. Items

Silent. P85 is printed once on side 2 of the packet; every item points to it. Read an item aloud on request, never the passage. Item 1, [[s03-A1]], 8 minutes. Item 2, [[s03-A2]], 8 minutes. Item 3, [[s03-A3]], 6 minutes. Item 4, [[s03-A4]], 2 minutes. Item 5, [[s03-A5]], 6 minutes. Mark with the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record the support used.

#### 5 min. Silent verification

Children reread their own answers once, [[s03-P85-5]]. The teacher answers nothing and gives no hint. Collect the packets at the end of this part and take them home to write one note on each; day 86 returns them.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X85v2]]. Shown: the meaning, a worry Ada carried, with a clue from chapter 5. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Why did she hide it?" Not yet seen: the plan read as really heavy (error D), or a meaning the chapter does not support (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each item aloud on request and writes what the child says, marking it as read aloud. No printer: write P85 and the items on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The retrieval words.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the passage on the packet and answers items [[s03-A1]], [[s03-A2]], [[s03-A3]], [[s03-A4]] and [[s03-A5]] alone, on paper. Read an item aloud if asked, never the passage. Give no hint.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X85v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chapter, the items and the exit. Longer: none; this is a check day.

### Words and supports

Question, answer, sentence, feeling, phrase. Large print; items read aloud on request; a scribe for the answers; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 86 returns the packet with one written note on each child's work, s03-A1 to s03-A5, and the day 85 strip, s03-X85v2, and repairs the commonest error first. It also returns the day 84 strip, s03-X84v2 and s03-P84-1v2. It reads chapter 6. The retrieval work, [[s03-P85-1]], [[s03-P85-2]], [[s03-P85-3]], [[s03-P85-4]] and [[s03-P85-5]], and the packet stay in the teacher folder.
