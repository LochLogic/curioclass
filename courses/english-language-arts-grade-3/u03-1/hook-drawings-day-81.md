# Hook drawings, day 81

Print two drawings, side by side on one page. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot.

- **Drawing 1.** A corner of a classroom. A rug on the floor, a low bookcase against the window, a beanbag by the wall, and a door that opens across the rug.
- **Drawing 2.** The same corner. The bookcase has been turned to face the wall, the beanbag sits beside it, and a clear strip of floor runs from the door to the rug.

Ask the series question once, aloud: would you recommend this series to a friend, and why? Children then say which drawing makes a corner they would use and what in the picture makes them say so. No answer is scored.
