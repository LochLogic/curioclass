# Section 3.1 opener: a first chapter book (days 81 to 86)

## What the section does

For six days the class reads the first six chapters of book 1 of a chapter-book series, one chapter a day, and keeps a chapter map. Children learn to ask a question the words can answer and prove it with a sentence, to say how a later part grows from an earlier one, to read a nonliteral phrase against the scene, to tell the narrator's view from a character's and from their own, and to name what a word does in a sentence. Day 85 is a check on a new passage, and day 86 returns it with one note and one change.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Days 81, 83 and 84 teach a chapter with a worked example; day 82 adds a short article about how series are made; days 82 and 84 are partner-rereading days. Day 85 is silent: skills retrieval only, chapter 5 read alone, five items on a printed passage, then a silent reread of answers. Day 86 returns the packet and teaches what each word class does. Every day ends with a five-minute exit on paper, alone, and the exit of one day is repaired in the next opening.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Evidence comes from the page.** A good answer points to a sentence. Ask: where does it say so?
- **A chapter builds on the one before.** The later part follows from a choice or a problem in the earlier part.
- **Words can mean more than they say.** A phrase such as a figure of speech is read against what is happening, not as a picture of its words.
- **Three views can sit in one passage.** The narrator, a character and the reader may each judge the same person. Label whose words give each judgment.
- **A word class has a job.** A noun names, a pronoun stands in for a noun, a verb acts, an adjective describes and an adverb tells how.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** Invents evidence absent from the text. Repair: Underline a supporting sentence before answering. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **B:** Reverses the order of linked parts. Repair: Number the earlier cause before the later change. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **E:** Attributes one person's view to another. Repair: Label whose words express each judgment. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **C:** States a trait without an action or consequence. Repair: Draw action to result, then name the supported trait. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **D:** Reads a nonliteral phrase as a physical event. Repair: Compare the physical reading with the surrounding event. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **X:** Names a word class without explaining its function. Repair: Explain what the word does generally and here. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **L:** Treats neat recopying as revision. Repair: Change one explanation in response to feedback. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
