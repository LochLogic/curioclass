# Day 106: Check the review and the talk

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the audit card for each child. Make six groups of five and post the order. Have the word check sheet, day 105, from each child with its note and the discussion record. Keep the title rule card out of sight until the audit is collected.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, retrieval only

*6 min.* The word sheets come back with their notes. Pairs read once, other, many and any from the corrected card, [[s03-P106-6]]. Children write six words the teacher says, on numbered lines, [[s03-A25]]. Then they write stopped, hoping and happiness from stop, hope and happy, each change named, [[s03-A26]]. Watch for AG, AH, AJ. *4 min.* Ending rules on slates, [[s03-P106-1]]. *2 min.* Hand back s03-X105 (error P); each child names one detail a partner gave, [[s03-P106-2]]. *18 min.* Own reading, then the log line, [[s03-P106-3]]. Meanwhile call each child to the table, 20 seconds each, to read said, does, were and their, [[s03-A24]]. Hear the six children from day 105 first. No conferences.

#### 25 min. Audit and discussion window

Everyone completes the audit card, [[s03-A13]] to [[s03-A17]], [[s03-A28]], [[s03-A23]] and [[s03-A27]] (21 minutes, the directions read silently in the first 5). Six groups of five come to the table, 4 minutes each, 48 seconds a child, with the prepared claim card: [[s03-A18]] to [[s03-A22]]. Record five boxes on the discussion record. Watch for errors M to Q. Mark each row once: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Edit and publish

Bring out the title rule card. Children copy the revised review in a clear hand, [[s03-P106-4]], the draft and the revision beside it.

#### 10 min. Silent verification

Each child rereads the copy against the audit card and the title rule card and fixes up to two misses in the margin, [[s03-P106-5]].

#### 5 min. Collection

*2 min.* The plan, draft, question, note, revision, published review and audit card go in the folder and are collected. *3 min.* A child who has not finished a row finishes it; the rest read quietly.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the half slip aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X106]]. Shown: a reason that names what Pia does. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What happens in the book that proves it?" Not yet seen: the opinion again (error I) or a fact with no reason (error H). Record it.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the discussion items aloud. The audit and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The slates and the group talk.

- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s03-A13]] to [[s03-A17]], [[s03-A28]], [[s03-A23]], [[s03-A25]], [[s03-A26]] and [[s03-A27]] on paper, alone, from their review. Give no hint.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then tells you which reason in their review is strongest.
  **Say.** "What happens in the book that shows it?" Expect: an event, such as Ben waits for Ada.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X106]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the audit, the talk and the exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Audit, review, reason, closing. Frame: "I recommend ___ because ___." Large print for the card; directions read aloud on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 107 hands back the day 106 assessment: the audit card with its note. It repairs error I from the exit, s03-X106: ask what happened in the book that proves the reason. Then the class reads a two-scene script and the poem Pond Voices. The log and word work, [[s03-P106-1]] to [[s03-P106-3]] and [[s03-P106-6]], and the editing, [[s03-P106-4]] and [[s03-P106-5]], stay in the folder.
