# Exit cards, days 105 to 107

Strips marked From the book print lines from the unit's own books, with the chapter named, and carry no invented label. For a card marked Invented for practice, say aloud "This card is made up for practice" and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. An exit slip is a half slip holding about two items. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 105 (s03-X105), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A reader says, "The Boat Club and Sled Club books are the same. Both have a club." Boat Club, page 6: Orin mends a torn sail alone. Sled Club, page 9: Wren asks three friends to hold the ropes. Ask: Write one sentence you could say to this reader. Use a page.
- **Day 106 (s03-X106), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A review says, "I recommend the Moon Club because it is a good series." In book 1, Pia shares her map when a friend gets lost. Ask: Write one new reason for the review. Use what happens in the book.
- **Day 107 (s03-X107v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: the script, scene 1, Ada: "I am going to erase it and start again, by myself." Scene 2, Ada: "It works because we checked it together." Ask: How does the second scene answer the first?
- **Day 107 (s03-X107bv2), Exit box.** From the P107 panel (reserved, never taught). Print Leaf Boats in the exit box: "A leaf rests by the garden stone. / Its curled brown edges lie alone." "Rain lifts the leaf beyond the stone. / It sails where little streams have grown." Ask: How does the second stanza develop the first?
