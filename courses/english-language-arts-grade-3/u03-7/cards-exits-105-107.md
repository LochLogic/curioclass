# Exit cards, days 105 to 107

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. An exit slip is a half slip holding about two items. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 105 (s03-X105), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A reader says, "The Boat Club and Sled Club books are the same. Both have a club." Boat Club, page 6: Orin mends a torn sail alone. Sled Club, page 9: Wren asks three friends to hold the ropes. Ask: Write one sentence you could say to this reader. Use a page.
- **Day 106 (s03-X106), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A review says, "I recommend the Moon Club because it is a good series." In book 1, Pia shares her map when a friend gets lost. Ask: Write one new reason for the review. Use what happens in the book.
- **Day 107 (s03-X107), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: The first scene. [In the kitchen.] Ivo: 'The stew needs salt.' Mae: 'I will look in the pantry.' The second scene. [Mae returns.] Mae: 'The salt was on the top shelf.' Ivo: 'Now the stew tastes right.' Ask: Write one sentence that tells how the second scene answers the first.
- **Day 107 (s03-X107b), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: Poem, The Clock. First stanza: The clock stands still upon the wall. / Its hands do not move at all. Second stanza: A key is turned, a spring is wound. / The hands go round with a ticking sound. Ask: Write one sentence that tells how the second stanza develops the first.
