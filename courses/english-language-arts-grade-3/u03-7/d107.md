# Day 107: Read the script and the poem: how later parts build on earlier ones

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the script and the frog poem copies for each child, and a feedback card for each pair. Have the review audit cards with their notes and the exit slips; sort the exits by letter. Mark scene 2 in the script from "Good morning, Ada." to "Second arrow, past the shelf.", about 118 words.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say performance, carefully and direction. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P107-1]]. Review: you wrote carefully in chunks on day 101 and direction on day 42. Performance is new. Then break and great, [[s03-P107-2]]: mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. These two are new. *3 min.* One slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P107-3]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (day 81). *4 min.* Pairs read each other's chunks aloud: each chunk must blend. Watch for error AI, a chunk that cannot be blended, and error AG, a studied word spelled by its sound.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading with a feedback card

Say: words in square brackets are stage directions, notes for the performer. They are not spoken. This is new. Pairs, scene 2, the marked part, a part each: Ada and Ben. Review: the routine from day 2: phrases and expression, no speed. Each child reads their part with the directions unspoken, [[s03-P107-4]] (3 minutes); the partner marks one phrase read well and one place for more expression on the card, [[s03-P107-5]] (1 minute). Swap parts and do it again. Then each pair reads the passage once more together (4 minutes). That is 12 minutes with 3 to change over. Say nothing about speed.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Pairs say the job of the underlined word in two narrator sentences from the script: take, and hard, [[s03-P107-6]]. Review: a verb acts and an adjective describes (day 86).

#### 5 min. Returned feedback

Hand back the day 106 assessment: the audit cards, each with its note and its marks. Each child reads the note and marks the one explanation to revise, [[s03-P107-7]]. Watch for error L, the same words written more neatly.

#### 10 min. Return and repair after the check

*2 min.* Whole class, error I, from s03-X106: write I recommend the Moon Club because it is a good series. Ask what happened in the book that proves it, [[s03-P107-8]]. If another letter is commonest on the audit (H, J, K, L or R; AG, AH or AJ for words; AK for the dated writing), use its wrong example from the card instead. *6 min.* At most four teacher groups by letter, 1.5 minutes each. Everyone else works in pairs: one says a reason from their review, the other asks what happened in the book that proves it, [[s03-P107-9]]. *2 min.* Close in pairs: tell your partner the one change you will make and why, [[s03-P107-10]].

#### 25 min. Reading

*12 min.* The script, both scenes. The teacher reads the Narrator and four volunteers read Ada, Ben, Priya and Mateo, scene 1 then scene 2. Any child may pass. The class follows the brackets silently to hear where the directions fall. *6 min.* Pond Voices: the teacher reads it aloud; pairs reread a stanza each. Review: a stanza is a group of lines in a poem (day 40). *7 min.* Link teaching. A scene is a part of a play, as a stanza is a part of a poem. Scene is new. Model, [[s03-K107-1]]: an arrow from Ben's question in scene 1 to the part of scene 2 that acts on it, labelled asked, checked. Model, [[s03-K107-2]]: an arrow from stanza 1 to stanza 2, labelled peeps, answers. Children draw one arrow in the script margin, [[s03-P107-11]], and one in the poem margin, [[s03-P107-12]]. Watch for error B, an arrow that starts at the later part.

#### 10 min. Revise one explanation

Review: one note, one change, the first version beside it (day 105). Each child changes the explanation they marked, [[s03-P107-13]]. It is scored from the work handed in.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each, 1 to change: tell your partner one thing scene 2 shows that scene 1 did not, [[s03-P107-14]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X107]]. The second item, [[s03-X107b]], is in the Exit box on the student page and goes to the teacher with the slip. Shown: the later part answers or develops the earlier one, in order. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the first part need? What does the second do about it?" Not yet seen: the later part first (error B) or a thin answer, "It is about a clock" (error T). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads one scene aloud, the child reads the other, and they draw the two arrows together. One projector: show the script and the poem. The arrows and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed script: read the poem and any two chapters of your child's book.
**Skip.** The feedback card and the grammar check.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you what happened in one scene or poem this week, in order.
  **Say.** "What happened first, and what came after?" Expect: two parts in order, the later one following the first.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X107]] and the Exit box item, [[s03-X107b]], on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the arrows, the revision and the exit; cut the slate line and the talk. Longer: draw a third arrow for two chapters of book 1.

### Words and supports

Scene, stanza, stage direction, later, earlier. Frames: "Scene 1 asks ___. Scene 2 ___." "Stanza 1 says ___. Stanza 2 ___." Audio beside print for the script; large print for the poem; a part read aloud before it is performed; record any help.
