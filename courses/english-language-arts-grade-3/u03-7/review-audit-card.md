# Review audit card (days 106 and 107)

Print one for each child, two sides. Silent, with the dated folder open. The directions are read silently in the first five minutes. The teacher scores each row once on the 2, 1, 0 scale after the folder is collected.

1. Introduction and opinion. Read your review. Mark where it names the books, gives an opinion, begins reasons.
2. Reasons. Read your review. Copy the reason that supports your opinion.
3. Links. Read your review. Circle the word or phrase that links your opinion to a reason.
4. Closing. Read your review. Mark the closing and say who might like the books.
5. Process. Compare your draft and revision. Write one change that helps a reader.
6. Organization. Read your review. Mark grouped reasons and the sentence on why to try the series.
7. Title. Write the title with capitals as a title needs: a series for every reader.

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of row 7.
