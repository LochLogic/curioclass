# Day 105: Prepare for the series talk, rehearse it and change the draft

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep key-word-check-sheet-105 with you. Print the student page, the word check sheet, day 105, and a prepared claim card for each child, and the discussion record. Make six groups of five and post the order. Put the discussion moves chart where all can see it. Have the day 104 drafts with their written notes and the exit slips. Write each child's longer word (from days 103 and 104) on a card for the table. Choose the six children heard on day 106.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word check

*2 min.* Say the six words, each twice: since, running, hoped, tries, sadness, many; children write them, [[s03-P105-1]]. Then say hug, bake and lazy, each with the longer word it makes. Children write each longer word and what changed, [[s03-P105-2]]. Review: you did the ending rules on day 104. Children read the passage on the sheet silently and answer two questions, [[s03-P105-3]] and [[s03-P105-4]]. Review: you learned what un- and -ful mean in unit 1. *8 min.* Call children to the table one at a time, 24 children at 20 seconds each. Each reads once, other, many and any, [[s03-P105-5]], then the longer word on their card, [[s03-P105-6]]. The other six are heard at the table on day 106. Review: you chunked long words on days 103 and 104. Record each reading. Watch for error AJ, an irregular word read by its letters, and error AI, a chunk that cannot be blended. Write one note on each sheet tonight, and keep the six names.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P105-7]]. Review: you filled this line on day 98. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one thing noticed, [[s03-P105-8]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Read the note

*2 min.* Hand back the day 104 exit slips. Whole class, error K, from s03-X104: a last line, "Anyway, my lunch was good." The reader still wants to know what to do about the books. Children reread their two reasons and tell a partner who might like the books, [[s03-P105-9]]. *3 min.* Hand back the drafts, each with one written note on each part. Each child marks the one change the note asks for, [[s03-P105-10]], and keeps the first version beside it. Review: one note, one change (day 100). Watch for error L, the same words written more neatly.

#### 5 min. Roles and preparation

Read the discussion moves chart: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Review: you learned the five moves in unit 1 and held a window on day 98. *2 min.* Model, [[s03-K105-1]]: say a claim, a detail with its page, then a question built from the claim's last word. Ask which move each part is. *3 min.* Each child circles one question frame on the card, [[s03-P105-11]]: "What happened just before that?" "Which word on the page made you choose it?" "Who else is in that part?"

#### 10 min. Prepare

*3 min.* Each child marks one passage of about 150 words in each book, rereads both, and writes the book and page of each on the card, [[s03-P105-12]]. *7 min.* They write the detail from one page, [[s03-P105-13]], the claim, [[s03-P105-14]], and one question for a neighbor's claim, [[s03-P105-15]]. Review: you wrote a linked question on day 98. Watch for error M, a claim with no page, and error O, a question that does not link.

#### 5 min. Hard-part reread

Children reread one marked passage and underline the words a speaker could point to, [[s03-P105-16]]: an action or a line of talk, never "it is good".

#### 25 min. Revise and rehearse (writing 20, talk 5)

Six groups of five come to the teacher's table, 4 minutes each, 48 seconds a child. Review: you held this window on day 98. The speaker says the claim, the detail and its page, [[s03-P105-18]] (15 seconds). A neighbor asks a linked question, [[s03-P105-20]] (10). The speaker answers, [[s03-P105-21]] (15), and says what became clearer, [[s03-P105-22]] (8). Everyone waits for the speaker, then takes the agreed turn, [[s03-P105-19]]. Record five boxes on the discussion record: marked detail, turn, linked question, answer, what it clarified. Mark each alone, with help or not yet seen. Watch for error N, speaking over a classmate, error M and error P, the first idea again with nothing learned. A late group is not extended: a child not reached has blank boxes, and the day 106 window is the first hearing. The others write at their seats for 20 minutes: they change the part of the review the note marked, [[s03-P105-17]], the first version beside it. As each group returns, children fill the last card line, [[s03-P105-23]].

#### 5 min. Word in context

Evidence: the line or page that shows an idea is true. Review: you learned evidence in kindergarten: the line that says it. Each child writes one sentence about a book that uses the word, [[s03-P105-24]]. The sentences are collected.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X105]]. Shown: a page and a detail from each club, and how the books differ. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What happens on page 6, and what happens on page 9?" Not yet seen: no page, "They are different" (error M), or the reader's idea repeated (error P). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the claim, asks one linked question, hears the answer and says one thing it made clearer. Read the dictated words aloud and have the child read the four words aloud. One projector: show the discussion moves chart. The card, the revision and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No second book: use any two books your child knows.
**Skip.** The word check, the conferences and the group talk.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Prepare.** Your child marks one page, then writes one detail from it, [[s03-P105-13]].
  **Say.** "Which page shows that?" Expect: a page and something that happens on it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X105]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the card, the rehearsal, the revision and the exit; cut the log line and the word sentence. Longer: write a second detail from the other book.

### Words and supports

Evidence, claim, detail, page, linked question. Frames: "I would recommend the series because ___." "On page ___, ___." "What happened just before that?" Audio beside print for the marked passages; the claim said aloud before it is written; large print for the card; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 106 is a check. It begins with skills retrieval only: the word sheet comes back with its note, [[s03-P105-1]] to [[s03-P105-6]], and the day 105 exit, s03-X105, returns with error P repaired: name one detail a partner gave that changed or kept your idea. Then children audit their review folder, hold the revised discussion in six groups, and publish the review. The log work, [[s03-P105-7]] and [[s03-P105-8]], the note work, [[s03-P105-9]] to [[s03-P105-11]], the reading work, [[s03-P105-12]], [[s03-P105-13]] and [[s03-P105-16]], the spoken work, [[s03-P105-18]] to [[s03-P105-22]] and [[s03-K105-1]], and the card writing, [[s03-P105-14]], [[s03-P105-15]] and [[s03-P105-23]], stay in the folder. The revision, [[s03-P105-17]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P105-24]], go to the teacher with the folder. The word sheet and the discussion record go to the teacher tonight. Six children are heard at the table on day 106.
