# Section 3.7 opener: the discussion, the check and the script (days 105 to 107)

## What the section does

Day 105 prepares a text-based discussion about the series, rehearses it in small groups and revises the review from the teacher's written note. Day 106 is the check: the child audits the review folder, takes part in the revised discussion and publishes the review. Day 107 returns the check, repairs the commonest error and opens the next unit of work: a two-scene script and a returning poem, read as parts that build on each other.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 105 holds a one-at-a-time word check at the teacher's table while the class works alone. Day 106 is silent work with the folder; six groups of five meet the teacher for four minutes each. Day 107 holds reading aloud, link teaching and one revised explanation.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Come prepared.** A marked page and one linked question let a child join a talk with evidence.
- **Listen, then speak.** Wait for the speaker to end, then use the turn cue.
- **Ask to understand.** A good question comes from the speaker's own last word.
- **Say what became clearer.** Repeating the first answer shows nothing was learned.
- **A scene and a stanza are parts.** The second part acts on or grows from the first.

## The errors, in plain words

- **M:** Brings no page or detail. Repair: mark one passage before taking a turn. 5 minutes.
- **N:** Speaks before the speaker ends. Repair: rehearse the turn cue once in the group. 5 minutes.
- **O:** Asks an unrelated question. Repair: build the question from the speaker's last word. 5 minutes.
- **P:** Repeats the first idea. Repair: name one detail a classmate gave. 5 minutes.
- **B:** Puts the later part first. Repair: number the earlier part before the later. 5 minutes.
- **S and T:** No place for the scene, or a thin response. Repair: name who is where, then add a line or an action. 6 minutes.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The discussion record holds five boxes for each child. Exits are unscored.
