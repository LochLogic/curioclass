# Exit cards, days 153 to 156

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card, except the day 154 and day 156 cards, which name a real text. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 153 (s04-X153), Exit, unscored.** Invented for practice. Source: "The crew tested the valve before reopening it." Listener: "Which words tell me the order?" Ask: Write one complete sentence that names the order words.
- **Day 153 (s04-X153b), Exit, second card, unscored.** Invented for practice. Sentence: "The crew tested the valve // before reopening it." Ask: Write what your voice does at the //.
- **Day 154 (s04-X154v2), Exit, unscored.** This is from How a Town Cleans Its Water. It says: "Each step prepares the water for the next one. The coagulation step makes clumps possible. The stirring step makes the clumps big enough to sink. The resting step removes the clumps, so the filters have less to catch." Ask: Write the main idea of this part.
- **Day 155 (s04-X155), Exit.** Invented for practice. Source: "Repairing the drain let water flow again." Listener: "I missed why the repair mattered." Ask: Write one complete sentence that explains why it mattered.
- **Day 156 (s04-X156), Exit.** Notes: "Wait at the water point: 30 to 40 minutes. Zone: Mixed Farming. Report: Water Paths in Kitui County." Listener: "Which source gave that number?" Ask: Write a complete sentence that names the source and what it reported.

Every exit sheet prints the card and the ask only, with a ruled space for the answer. The look-fors live in items.csv and the record sheet, never on the child's page.
