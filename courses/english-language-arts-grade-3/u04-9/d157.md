# Day 157: Check 2, language and the literary packet, first fifteen children heard

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 156's assessment and written tasks (s04-P156-1, s04-P156-2, s04-P156-3 and s04-X156) stay in the unit record and go home with the course record on day 160. Put the thirty children in the spring hearing order; mark the first fifteen for today and the rest for day 158. List each child with a clean recording from the last ten days, with its date. Keep set A face down.

### The day

#### 20 min. The language card

All thirty, silent, on paper. Say once: "Today's texts are new and made up for practice. Nothing was taught from them. You may pass any ask." Each child answers sheet 157A, [[c2-A157L1]] to [[c2-A157L16]]. Collect the sheets when the time is up.

#### 25 min. The literary packet

All thirty, silent. The only help is the titles read aloud. Each child reads the two Pip chapters, the two scenes and the poem in the packet booklet and answers sheet 157B, [[c2-A157a]], [[c2-A157d]], [[c2-A157e]], [[c2-A157f]], [[c2-A157g]], [[c2-A157b]], [[c2-A157h]], [[c2-A157l]], [[c2-A157m]], [[c2-A157n]], [[c2-A157i]], [[c2-A157k]], [[c2-A157j]] and [[c2-A157o]]. Collect the sheets.

#### 45 min. Oral window, first fifteen

Children 1 to 15, 3 minutes each, at your table; the rest read their own book, unmarked. Use set A and the passage Mo's Kite. In order: the purpose and first reading, [[c2-A157r5]]; the sentence with wind, [[c2-A157r7]]; the affix words, [[c2-A157r1]]; the -tion and -ous words, [[c2-A157r2]]; the long word, [[c2-A157r3]]; the unusual words, [[c2-A157r4]]; then the reread, [[c2-A157r6]]. A child with a clean recording from the last ten days skips the reread; write its date. Give no help beyond the stuck word, and collect each card after each child. A child not reached goes on the missing-evidence list for day 158.

After class, mark every ask and list the gaps by letter from the course register. Day 159 acts on them.

### Other rooms

One child: the language card, the packet and the oral window in order, about 60 minutes. No printer: copy the packet by hand in large print. A scribe and pointing count for the reading items; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use any two short stories about the same child and a short poem.
**Skip.** The oral window.

- **20 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[c2-A157L1]] to [[c2-A157L16]] alone.
- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the packet and answers [[c2-A157a]] to [[c2-A157o]] alone.
  **Say.** "Show me the words that tell you." Expect: the child points to the line.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear the oral window over two days only if every child is still heard once; no item is cut or made easier. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: chapter, scene, stanza, narrator, lesson, setting, plot. Frames: "The lesson is ___." "The picture shows ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used. Large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 158 is the second check day: two new articles, a paragraph, and the second fifteen children heard. Day 157's sheets and records wait for day 159, which returns them with one written note and teaches each gap in small groups.

### Credit

Ours: written for Curio Class.
