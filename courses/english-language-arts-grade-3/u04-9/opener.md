# Section 4.9 opener: announcements, two new texts and the close of the unit (days 153 to 156)

## What the section does

Four days close the unit. Children record a revised announcement in small groups, read two new water texts and compare them, rehearse and repair one gap, and then give a short live announcement, ask and answer questions, and listen to a spoken report with a chart and a drawing. The unit record is made from the work of these days and of earlier ones.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Days 153, 154 and 156 are check days with silent routines and no teaching; day 155 is a working day for repair and rehearsal. On 153 and 156 the group turns run one group at a time while the other children prepare quietly, so the teacher can listen and tick. Each day ends with a five-minute exit on paper.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A pause is made with the voice.** The written cue stays unspoken.
- **A clarification is a complete sentence.** It names the source and what the source said.
- **Compare two texts by what they share and then what differs.**
- **A main idea covers the details.** A single detail is not the main idea.
- **Nearby water is not the same as safe water.** The texts separate distance from safety.
- **A discussion needs studied evidence.** Bring one located passage, listen, link to the speaker, say what the talk changed.
- **A repair answers a specific comment.** Reread the hard part silently, name a strategy, then change something.

## The errors, in plain words

- **Z:** Rushes so the listener loses the order. Repair: phrase at the meaning boundary.
- **AA:** Answers with a fragment. Repair: restate with a subject and a verb.
- **AB:** Says the written cue. Repair: pause instead.
- **AC:** Watches an adult type. Repair: the child types an attributed line.
- **AD:** Waits for the teacher to read the hard part. Repair: reread silently and name one strategy first.
- **AK:** Guesses a long word from its first letter. Repair: read every syllable, then check the sentence.
- **A, B, F:** adds an unstated fact; takes a detail as the main idea; lists only similarities or only differences.
- **AE to AJ:** an isolated number as the listening idea; no studied evidence; interrupting; changing the topic; repeating a claim; saying yes alone to a request for detail.
- **S to Y:** carried from 4.8; each is shown beside its correction on 155.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the component sheet and the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored on every day of this section. New performances on 156 are not scored; the earlier records are scored after class.
