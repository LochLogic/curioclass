# Day 160: Revise the opinion paragraph and close the course

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have each child's opinion paragraph from day 158 with its note, the day 159 exit slip and exit box, and the reading log. List the children whose rereading record still needs a sample.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return the opinion paragraph with its note and the day 159 exit slip and exit box (c2-X159v2 and c2-P159-16). Hand back the day 80 exit slip and exit box too (c1-X80 and c1-P80-11), to go home with the course record. Each child reads the note and finds the part it names: the opinion, a reason, a linking word or the closing.

#### 40 min. Revise the paragraph

Each child changes the one part the note names, [[c2-P160-1]] on sheet 160A, and rereads the whole paragraph aloud in a quiet voice to a partner. The teacher meets the children whose note named the opinion or the closing, with the paragraph group card.

#### 25 min. Rereading and transition records

The teacher hears each child who still needs a rereading sample, one minute each, from the reading log and a book of the child's choice. Everyone else fills the reading log's last line and lists one book to read next. Records are unjudged folder work.

#### 10 min. Exit

Hand out the strip. One ask, [[c2-X160]]. Children answer alone, in silence.

#### 10 min. Close

Children put every sheet in the course folder. Say one thing each child can do now that they could not do on day 1, and name the three records that go home. Hand back day 156's assessment and written tasks (s04-P156-1, s04-P156-2, s04-P156-3 and s04-X156) from the unit record with the course record.

### Other rooms

One child: the note, the one change, the reread and the exit in order, about 70 minutes. A scribe and pointing count; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: reread the returned paragraph and fix one part.
**Skip.** The reread to a partner.

- **20 min. Fix.** Your child reads the note and fixes the part it names, [[c2-P160-1]].
- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads a book of their choice and tells you what it was about.
  **Say.** "What will you read next?" Expect: the child names a book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[c2-X160]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: change only the part the note names. Longer: none; the day is full.

### Words and supports

Words: opinion, reason, link, closing, revise. Frames: "The note says ___." "That is why ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used.

### Tomorrow

This is the last day of the course; nothing is planned after it. The exit slip, [[c2-X160]], goes into the course record and home with the family, which reads it with the record.
