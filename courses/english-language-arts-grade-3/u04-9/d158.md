# Day 158: Check 2, the article pair and the opinion paragraph, second fifteen children heard

## Teacher page

### Before class

Take the spring hearing order: today are children 16 to 26, then anyone the day 157 list says was not reached. Keep set B of the oral cards face down. Day 157's items, the sheets and the oral records, stay in the folder with today's; both days are returned together on day 159.

### The day

#### 25 min. The article pair

All thirty, silent. The only help is the titles read aloud. Each child reads The Story Walk and The Reading Tent and answers sheet 158A, [[c2-A158a]], [[c2-A158c]], [[c2-A158d]], [[c2-A158b]], [[c2-A158i]], [[c2-A158e]], [[c2-A158f]], [[c2-A158g]] and [[c2-A158h]]. Say once: "These articles are new. Nothing was taught from them. You may pass any ask." Collect the sheets.

#### 20 min. The opinion paragraph

All thirty, silent, one sitting. Each child writes one paragraph for Mr. Park on sheet 158B, [[c2-A158W1]], then marks it, [[c2-A158W2]], [[c2-A158W3]] and [[c2-A158W4]]. The articles stay on the table. There is no revising today. Collect the sheets.

#### 45 min. Oral window, second fifteen

Children 16 to 26, 4 minutes each (3 minutes of asks and 1 minute to change over, 44 in all), at your table; the rest read their own book, unmarked. Use set B and the passage Lena's Bookmark. In order: the purpose and first reading, [[c2-A158r5]]; the sentence with bow, [[c2-A158r7]]; the affix words, [[c2-A158r1]]; the -sion and -ous words, [[c2-A158r2]]; the long word, [[c2-A158r3]]; the unusual words, [[c2-A158r4]]; then the reread, [[c2-A158r6]]. A child with a clean recording from the last ten days skips the reread; write its date. Give no help beyond the stuck word. Children 27 to 30, and any child still not heard, go on a list for you to hear in day 159's group time.

After class, mark every ask on both days and list the gaps by letter from the course register. Day 159 acts on them.

### Other rooms

One child: the articles, the paragraph and the oral window in order. No printer: copy the articles by hand in large print, headings kept. A scribe and pointing count; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No booklet: use any two short articles on one topic.
**Skip.** The oral window.

- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child reads both articles and answers [[c2-A158a]] to [[c2-A158i]] alone.
  **Say.** "Show me where it says so." Expect: the child points to the words.
- **20 min. Assessment.** Your child writes the paragraph and marks it, [[c2-A158W1]] to [[c2-A158W4]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: spread the oral window over the next day only so every child is heard once; no ask is cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: article, writer, opinion, reason, compare, because, also. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "That is why ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used. Large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 159 returns day 157's items and today's with one written note and teaches each gap in small groups by letter. The paragraph is revised on day 160. It is a response day, not a new text.

### Credit

Ours: written for Curio Class.
