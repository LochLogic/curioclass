# Day 154: Compare two water articles

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, P154A and P154B and a half slip for each child. Put each child's day 153 page, ticked, on the desk. Keep each child's retained day 137 comparison and portfolio card in the folder for marking after class. This is a check: nothing is taught, and the two articles have not been read or named before today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*1 min.* Hand back the day 153 page, s04-P153-1 and s04-P153-2, ticked; day 153's assessment comes back on day 155 with its comment. *9 min.* Silent retrieval. Children reread the long words they starred on day 153 and the ending rules in the word record. Nothing is said aloud.

#### 20 min. Skills block, check day

*15 min.* Silent log reading. Record who read; no conferences. *5 min.* Each child writes one log line, [[s04-P154-2]]. Unscored.

#### 5 min. Silent preparation

Children open the studied water text they brought, write its title and mark one sentence to point to on day 156, [[s04-P154-1]]. Unscored.

#### 15 min. Compare two texts

Hand out P154A and P154B. Children read both silently, then answer [[s04-A45]]. Say only the ask. Review: you wrote what two texts share on day 127. Watch for differences only, error F.

#### 10 min. Ask and answer

Children answer [[s04-A46]]. Say only the ask. Review: you answered a question in one complete sentence on day 146. Watch for a claim the article does not make, error A.

#### 10 min. Main idea

Children answer [[s04-A47]]. Say only the ask. Review: you found a main idea on days 127 and 134. Watch for a small detail as the main idea, error B.

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Children answer [[s04-A48]] alone. Read the ask aloud again if a child asks, never the articles. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Watch for a child who waits for you to read the hard part, error AD.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X154]]. Unscored. Shown: the notice's idea, covered soil reduces runoff. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What is the whole notice about?" Not yet seen: a detail as the idea, error B. Collect the pages, slips and folders. After class, score the retained comparison and the card from the folder: [[s04-A49]], [[s04-A50]], [[s04-A51]], [[s04-A52]], [[s04-A53]], [[s04-A54]], [[s04-A55]], [[s04-A56]] and [[s04-A57]].

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each ask aloud, never the articles, and the child writes on paper; no help. One projector: show nothing.

### At home

**Swap.** No studied text: use any book.
**Skip.** The preparation.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Your child reads from their own book and writes one log line, [[s04-P154-2]]. Then your child says the main idea and one detail aloud. Give no help.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a second chapter or page, then tells you one new fact.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X154]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check. Longer: a third comparison point.

### Words and supports

Compare, share, differ, main idea, detail. Read the asks aloud again for any child who asks, never the articles; large print with a line guide; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 155 is a working day: the written comments on days 153 and 154 come back, and children rehearse the live explanation and repair one gap. The pages, slips and folders stay with you for marking.
