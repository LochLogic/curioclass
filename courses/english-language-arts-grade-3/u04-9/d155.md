# Day 155: Rehearse and repair one gap

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a half slip for each child, and an announcement feedback card for each pair. Sort the returned work into three piles for each child: day 152, day 153, day 154, each with its written comment. Set fifteen devices; the paper script is the fall-back. Tally on the record sheet the four errors seen most across days 153 and 154. Nothing new is taught: this is repair.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Hand back the day 152 dictation sheet and heard-words card with written moves: s04-A39 and s04-A40, error AR, say sit and sitting together, then double before -ing; s04-A34 and s04-A38, error AK, read every syllable, then check the sentence; s04-A35, error AS, say said with its spoken sounds, then reread it. *5 min.* Children add -ing to stop, plan and drip, [[s04-P155-1]]. Then in pairs they say collection, protection and pollution by syllable, then whole, [[s04-P155-2]]. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

*16 min.* Four conferences of four minutes, one child at a time; the rest read silently. *4 min.* Two children read their log line aloud in clear phrases, [[s04-P155-3]]; two other children read on the next log day. Review: you read your log line aloud on day 151.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return the written feedback on day 153's assessment and day 154's assessment, and the day 153 exit, s04-X153 and s04-X153b, and the day 154 page, s04-P154-1 and s04-P154-2, ticked. Show the four commonest errors from the tally beside their corrections, groups of four at most: Z, AA, AB, AC, AK, AD, A, B, F, N, O, R, S, T, U, V, W, X, Y, AR and AS are the ones to choose from. Say the move once; children do not copy it.

#### 5 min. Opening

*2 min.* Hand back the day 152 letter and its written move, s04-A24 to s04-A27, error N or O, and the page, s04-A28 to s04-A33, and log lines, s04-A36 and s04-A37, error A. *3 min.* Children read the comment on each pile and write the one gap they will close today, [[s04-P155-10]].

#### 20 min. Rehearse live explanations

*10 min.* Two device turns of 4 minutes, with 1 to switch. The first fifteen children record the announcement and listen once, [[s04-P155-4]]. The other fifteen say it to a partner, who ticks the feedback card, [[s04-P155-5]]. Then they swap. Review: you recorded a turn on day 149 and read with the feedback card on day 2. Without devices, hear six children and tick the card. *10 min.* Everyone says the announcement again to a new partner with one change from the comment, [[s04-P155-6]]. Watch for the cue said as words, error AB.

#### 15 min. Close one gap

Children reread the hard part of a source silently and write the strategy they used, [[s04-P155-8]]. Then each repairs the gap with the written move: a reading gap by rereading aloud to a partner, a speaking gap by saying the line again, a writing gap by changing the line. This work is the child's own: unscored, kept in the folder. Confer with three small groups of at most four. Watch for a child who waits for you to read the hard part, error AD.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Partners each say the announcement and answer one question in one complete sentence, [[s04-P155-7]]. 2 minutes each, 1 to change over. Watch for a fragment, error AA.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence with explain. Children circle what explain means in it, [[s04-P155-9]]. Review: you learned explain in kindergarten, to say how or why.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X155]]. Unscored. Shown: a sentence that gives the consequence, water could flow again. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What happened because of the repair?" Not yet seen: a fragment, error AA; a cue said inside the sentence, error AB. Record on the record sheet. Collect the pages, slips, cards and scripts; the exit and the rehearsal comment go back on day 156.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the listener and the partner; the child says the announcement twice, once with one change. No device: hear six children and tick the feedback card. One projector: show nothing.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: your helper listens.
**Skip.** The word in context.

- **10 min. Words.** Your child adds -ing to stop, plan and drip, [[s04-P155-1]].
- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then says one detail aloud.
- **10 min. Say it.** Your child says the announcement once, then once with one change.
  **Say.** "Which source told you that?" Expect: a named text and what it said.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X155]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the gap repair, one rehearsal and the exit. Longer: a child says the announcement to a third listener.

### Words and supports

Rehearse, explain, repair, gap, strategy. Frame: "My gap is ___. I will ___." A word strip; a printed script; headphones; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 156 is the last check day of the unit. Six groups of five hear a short report with a chart and a drawing, each child gives a 30-second live announcement and asks or answers one question linked to a speaker. It ends with the returned comment, one change and the exit. The exit and the rehearsal comment go back first.
