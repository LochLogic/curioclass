# Day 153: Record the announcement

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the page, a half slip and a feedback card for each child. Put each child's day 152 Exit box and practice address on the desk, with the script. Set the recorder at your table; open the component sheet and device log. Write six groups of five on the board. Nothing is taught.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Day 152's assessment goes back on day 155. *2 min.* Hand back the Exit box, s04-X152b: name care, the idea, error S. Hand back the practice address, s04-P152-1, ticked: the comma goes between the city and the state, error Y. *6 min.* Silent retrieval: children reread the long words in the word record and star three to ask about on day 155. *2 min.* Hand out the half slip face down. Read the two cards aloud twice, [[s04-X153]] and [[s04-X153b]]. Say: write only when you are not at the recorder.

#### 20 min. Skills block, check day

*15 min.* Silent log reading. Record who read; no conferences. *5 min.* Each child writes one log line, [[s04-P153-1]]. Unscored.

#### 60 min. Six groups, ten minutes each

Children not at the recorder reread the script, ring each bracket cue and write how many, [[s04-P153-2]]; then write the exit alone. Each child's turn is 2 minutes (5 children, 10 minutes; 6 groups, 60). Review: you recorded a turn on day 149.

*45 s.* The child reads the revised announcement into the recorder, [[s04-A41]]. *30 s.* Ask "What does that step do?" The child answers in one complete sentence, [[s04-A42]]. *15 s.* The child says one line again with a pause where a bracket cue is shown and not said, [[s04-A43]]. *30 s.* The child shows the typed pieces on the device, [[s04-A44]]; tick the three boxes on the page. Review: you typed and replied on days 148 and 149. Mark the device log.

Tick facts, pace and complete sentences on the feedback card; the written comment follows after class. Mark the component sheet. Watch for speech so fast the order is lost, error Z; a fragment, error AA; the cue said as words, error AB; an adult typing, error AC.

Collect the pages, slips, cards, scripts and the device log. Exits are unscored. Shown: a sentence naming the order words, before reopening it; a voice that pauses at the mark and does not say it. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does your voice do?" Not yet seen: a fragment, error AA; yes alone, error AJ; the mark read aloud, error AB; no stop, error Z.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult listens to the script and one sentence answer. One projector: show nothing.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: your helper listens and ticks the card.
**Skip.** The log line.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Your child shows you the typed and published pieces and the reply, [[s04-A44]]. Then your child reads the script aloud, pausing at each bracket, and answers "What does that step do?" in one sentence. Give no help.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes one log line, [[s04-P153-1]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X153]] and [[s04-X153b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Announcement, script, pause, cue, clarify. Read each card again for any child who asks; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 154 is a check day on two new water articles: nothing is taught. Children read page A and page B silently, compare them and give each main idea. Comments return on day 155.
