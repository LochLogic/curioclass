# Group cards, days 159 and 160 (teacher only)

One card for each group move, never a check text and never an exit card. Each card for a reading or article group prints a few lines from a text the class has studied, named on the card; the stems of c2-P159-1v2 to c2-P159-5v2, c2-P159-14v2 and c2-P159-15v2 hold the exact lines. The other cards are made up: for those, say "This card is made up for practice."

- **Reading groups.** Evidence: New Well, Shared Work, page 7. Lesson: the same story, page 9. Part builds on part: the same story, pages 2 and 3. Views: the same story, page 5. Likes and differences: The Courtyard Club, chapter 1 of each book. Ice (made up): Dad said, "Let us break the ice."
- **Language groups.** "The dogs barks." Lena: Lena said, "Come in." Cat: "A cat is ___ than a mouse." Hats: "the boys hats" and "the boys' hats."
- **Word groups.** Affix cards: replay, hopeless. Long words: unhappiness, rewritten. Reread card: "Dan fed the hen. It ate the corn."
- **Article groups.** Writer's view: How a Town Cleans Its Water, Step 3 and What I think. What both texts say: Water on Earth, section 5, and Water Paths in Kitui County, More than distance.
- **Paragraph group (day 160).** Each child's own paragraph from day 158, with the returned note.
