# Group cards, days 159 and 160 (teacher only)

One card for each group move. Each card is new, never a check text and never an exit card. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice."

- **Reading groups.** Snack: "Jo ate a snack. Jo was hungry because lunch was late." Hen: "Hen kept pushing past the line. At last nobody saved her a seat." Map: "Part A: Ty found a map. Part B: Ty followed the map." Ana: "Ana said, 'I am not scared.' The narrator said her knees shook." Lost: "Story A: A pup got lost in a park. Story B: A kitten got lost in a mall." Ice: Dad said, "Let us break the ice."
- **Language groups.** "The dogs barks." Lena: Lena said, "Come in." Cat: "A cat is ___ than a mouse." Hats: "the boys hats" and "the boys' hats."
- **Word groups.** Affix cards: replay, hopeless. Long words: unhappiness, rewritten. Reread card: "Dan fed the hen. It ate the corn."
- **Article groups.** Park: "The park is open from nine to five. The park is the nicest place in town." Frogs and toads: "Text A: Frogs lay eggs in ponds. Text B: Toads lay eggs in strings."
- **Paragraph group (day 160).** Each child's own paragraph from day 158, with the returned note.
