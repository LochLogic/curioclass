# Day 156: Present, explain and act on feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a half slip for each child, and one display for each group. Put each child's returned day 155 work on the desk. Open the discussion record and component sheet. Write six groups of five on the board. Nothing is taught.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Silent retrieval: children reread the long words from the returned day 155 work.

#### 20 min. Skills block, check day

*15 min.* Silent log reading. Record who read; no conferences. *5 min.* Each child writes one log line, [[s04-P156-3]]. Unscored.

#### 3 min. Silent preparation

Children open the studied passage marked on day 154 and mark one sentence to point to.

#### 42 min. Six groups, seven minutes each

Six groups of five come to your table in turn, 7 minutes each (6 groups, 42 minutes). Children not at the table write one question to ask about their studied passage, [[s04-P156-1]]. Then they reread the passage silently.

At the table: *30 s.* Read the report aloud once; the group looks at the chart and the drawing. *1 min 40 s.* Each child, 20 seconds, says the main idea with one detail from the chart and one from the drawing, [[s04-A58]]. *2 min 30 s.* Each child, 30 seconds, gives a live announcement, [[s04-P156-4]]. *2 min 30 s.* Each child, 30 seconds, asks or answers one question, linked to the speaker and with one added detail, [[s04-P156-5]]. Fill the five boxes of the discussion record. Review: you held a five-box discussion on days 130 and 141. Today's talk is unscored. Watch for a number alone as the idea, error AE; a preference, error AF; interrupting, error AG; a new subject, error AH; the first claim repeated, error AI; yes alone, error AJ.

#### 15 min. Final response

*4 min.* Hand back the day 155 exit, s04-X155, and the rehearsal comment, error AA: restate a fragment as a sentence. Children read the comment. *6 min.* Each child makes one change to the announcement or the response and writes it, [[s04-P156-2]]. *5 min.* Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X156]]. Unscored. Shown: the report named and what it said. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which report?" Not yet seen: a fragment, error AA. Collect the pages, slips, records and recordings. After class, score the retained discussion records: [[s04-A59]], [[s04-A60]], [[s04-A61]], [[s04-A62]] and [[s04-A63]].

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the group. One projector: show the chart and drawing; one display passes between groups.

### At home

**Swap.** No display: the adult describes a chart and a picture.
**Skip.** The log line.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Your child says a one-minute announcement.
  **Say.** "Which source told you that?" Expect: a named text and what it said.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes one log line, [[s04-P156-3]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X156]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Report, chart, drawing, announcement, source. Read each ask again for any child who asks, never a passage; a printed script; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 157 begins the spring oral check in a new unit; nothing from this one is taught there. The day 156 work stays with you for the unit record.
