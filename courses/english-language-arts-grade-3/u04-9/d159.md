# Day 159: Respond to Check 2

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark day 157's and day 158's items. Write one note on each child's sheets naming the one gap to fix first, by letter from the course register. Sort the children into the groups below. Print the group cards and the exit strip. The group cards and the board work are not collected. Hear any child still not heard in the oral window at the start of Word groups, from the hearing order list.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return day 157's and day 158's sheets with the note, and the paragraph for day 160. Each child reads the note and picks the group it names. Nothing here is scored again.

#### 25 min. Reading groups

Small groups by gap, 4 minutes each; the rest read on. Use the cards. Text evidence, [[c2-P159-1v2]]. Lesson, [[c2-P159-2v2]], spoken. Part builds on part, [[c2-P159-3v2]]. Narrator and character views, [[c2-P159-4v2]], spoken. Likes and differences, [[c2-P159-5v2]]. Word meaning, [[c2-P159-6]]. Teach one move in each group, then the child tries the card.

#### 20 min. Language groups

Small groups by gap, 5 minutes each. Verb that matches, [[c2-P159-7]]. Spoken words, [[c2-P159-8]]. The right ending, [[c2-P159-9]]. Who owns it, [[c2-P159-10]]. Teach one move, then each child tries the card on the board.

#### 20 min. Word groups

Small groups by gap, 5 minutes each. Parts that add meaning, [[c2-P159-11]], spoken. Splitting long words, [[c2-P159-12]], spoken. Rereading so it sounds like talking, [[c2-P159-13]], spoken. Teach one move, then each child tries the card.

#### 10 min. Article groups

Small groups by gap, 5 minutes each. Writer's view, [[c2-P159-14v2]]. What both texts say, [[c2-P159-15v2]]. Teach one move, then each child tries the card.

#### 10 min. Exit

Hand out the strip. One ask, [[c2-X159v2]]. Then children answer [[c2-P159-16]] in the exit box on the page. Children answer alone, in silence.

### Other rooms

One child: the note, the one move, the card and the exit in order, about 60 minutes. A scribe and pointing count; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheets: reread a returned page and fix one thing.
**Skip.** The group cards.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and tells you one thing it said.
  **Say.** "Where in the book does it say so?" Expect: the child points to the words.
- **10 min. Fix.** Your child reads the note and fixes the part it names.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[c2-X159v2]] on paper, then [[c2-P159-16]] in the exit box, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: take only the group the child's note names. Longer: none; the day is full.

### Words and supports

Words: writer, reader, narrator, chapter, scene, stanza, compare, because. Frames: "The note says ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used.

### Tomorrow

Day 160 returns the day 159 exit slip and exit box, [[c2-X159v2]] and [[c2-P159-16]], and the opinion paragraph, then revises the paragraph, completes the rereading and transition records, and closes the course. Nothing from this check is new there.
