# Check 2 oral cards, days 157 and 158 (teacher only, unseen until the day)

Say each time: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing here was taught. You may pass any ask." Print each word card with its words in large letters, one card to a child's turn. Keep every card face down and collect it after each child, so the second fifteen children never see set A. Day 157 uses set A (children 1 to 15 of the spring hearing order); day 158 uses set B (children 16 to 30, then anyone not heard). The passages are g3-c2-o157 (Mo's Kite) and g3-c2-o158 (Lena's Bookmark); print each with its title and nothing else.

## Set A, day 157

- Affixes (c2-A157r1): unpack, helpful.
- Latin suffixes (c2-A157r2): conversation, nervous.
- A long word to split (c2-A157r3): unbreakable.
- Words that are not the way they look (c2-A157r4): says, climb, knot.
- Self-correction sentence (c2-A157r7): the third sentence of Mo's Kite, the one with the word wind.

## Set B, day 158

- Affixes (c2-A158r1): disagree, careless.
- Latin suffixes (c2-A158r2): decision, famous.
- A long word to split (c2-A158r3): disagreement.
- Words that are not the way they look (c2-A158r4): does, both, mountain.
- Self-correction sentence (c2-A158r7): the second sentence of Lena's Bookmark, the one with the word bow.

## The three minutes of one child

Turn the title card, 20 seconds (purpose, c2-A157r5 or c2-A158r5). First reading, 60 seconds. Tell what it said, 10 seconds. The sentence with the tricky word, 10 seconds. The word cards, 55 seconds (r1 to r4). The reread, 25 seconds (r6). A child with a clean recording from the last ten days skips the reread: write its date in the record. Never add help beyond the stuck word.
