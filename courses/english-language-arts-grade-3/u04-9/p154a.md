**P154A. Distance Is One Part of Access**

In July 2025, a report described water collection in different parts of Kitui County, Kenya. The report did not give one distance for every person.

In the Marginal Mixed Farming zone, the average round trip was five to seven kilometers. Some places had longer journeys. In the Mixed Farming zone, round trips ranged from two to four kilometers. These ranges describe particular areas at the time of the report. They do not tell us how far one child walks today.

Distance is only part of access. A person may reach a water point and still wait to collect water. Broken equipment can also affect the supply.

Another Kenyan account described a community group called Kyeni Kya Wasya. The group helped to build sand dams and wells. Such local work belongs in an account of water access, alongside the difficulties.

Having water nearby and knowing whether it is safe to drink are different questions. A distance report alone cannot answer both.
