# Sort cards (day 14)

Twelve cards, cut apart. Pairs sort them under **A story** or **Information**, and say one reason for each.

- A girl waits for a tall ladder to be brought to the tree. (story)
- A market opens at eight and closes at noon. (information)
- The pony slowed down, and the boy slowed with him. (story)
- A school bus makes four stops along the river road. (information)
- The new kitten hid behind a boot and would not come out. (story)
- A library card lets a person borrow books for three weeks. (information)
- The wind was loud, so Nell read in a whisper. (story)
- A weekly newspaper tells people what is on in town. (information)
- Tomas hoped the rain would stop before the walk. (story)
- A map shows where places are. (information)
- Pia sorted her seeds into small paper cups. (story)
- Many towns keep a place where people may read free. (information)
