# Day 17: Experts and what keeps readers from books

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the expert card and annotation sheet for each child and the bar graph for each pair. Make ten groups of three and give each expert one section: By Road in England, By Boat in Bangladesh, By Bus and by Mail in New Mexico, or How It Worked. Find the day 17 line of exit cards 14 to 19. Make the heard-six list.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair and hand-back.* Repair the day 16 exit (s01-X16). Error H is a fact that does not belong with the topic. Write three facts on the board: "Pip has a red bike. The bike has a bell. Pip's cousin likes soup." Ask for a topic sentence that fits two of them (Pip has a red bike with a bell), then ask which one does not fit and why. Hand back the day 16 exit (s01-X16) and the gathered facts (s01-P16-1) with one comment each. The day 16 paragraphs (s01-P16-2) stay with the teacher for day 20.

*7 min. Five moves.* Review: you learned five discussion moves in grade 3, on day 1. Pairs say them from the chart on the wall without looking: Prepare, Wait, Ask for detail, Link, Explain. Each child says one move and one thing to say for it. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*10 min. Partner rereading.* Review: in unit 1.2 you gave a partner feedback on the card. Pairs reread By Bus and by Mail in New Mexico (103 words). Each child reads twice, about 2 minutes 30 seconds, and the partner gives feedback, 30 seconds. That is about 6 minutes for a pair. Never say speed. Listen to two pairs, 3 minutes each, and write them in the heard-six column.

*10 min. Possessives.* Review: in grade 3, on days 2 and 12, you learned the possessive with one owner. Say each, children write it on a slate: the hat of the teacher (the teacher's hat), the nest of the bird (the bird's nest), the leash of the dog (the dog's leash), the book of the girl (the girl's book). Ask: "Who owns it? Where does the apostrophe go?" Expect: after the owner, before the s.

#### 20 min. Model

*8 min. Listening to an expert.* This is new: a listener states the main idea and two details of what an expert reads aloud. Say: "Do not look at the page. Listen to the whole section first." Read By Road in England aloud once as the expert. Then model the listener with [[s01-K17-1]] and show the steps with [[steps:s01-K17-1]]. Say it back: the main idea first, then two details, one at a time. Write them on the board.

*12 min. Words and a graph.* A graph is another way to say something. Review: you read a bar graph in grade 2 mathematics. Show the printed bar graph, books returned to the class basket in week one. Read the title, then the label on each axis, then one bar. Use [[s01-K17-2]] and [[steps:s01-K17-2]]. Teach nothing new about graphs. Then show the small map of Books on the Move and ask what the words say and what the map adds. Say: "The graph and the map each give only what they show."

#### 25 min. Hand work

*12 min. Expert card.* Each child prepares as the expert for the assigned section, on the expert card, with [[s01-P17-1]]. Mark one detail word for word. Write the main idea as a whole sentence and two details. Review: on day 15 you wrote a main idea and two details. Walk and look. If a child writes only the topic, ask: "What does the whole section say? Say it as a sentence."

*13 min. Annotations.* Each child writes two lines for the graph with [[s01-P17-2]] and two lines for the map with [[s01-P17-3]]: what the words say and what the image adds. Judged after class with the look-fors in the items. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Talk

Ten groups of three. Each expert takes 3 minutes: 1 minute to read the section aloud; 1 minute for the listeners to state the main idea and two details; 1 minute for the group to say what keeps readers from books in that place. Three experts at 3 minutes is 9 minutes, with 1 minute to change. Listen to two groups, six children, and write them on the record sheet. Anyone may pass. If a child talks while a partner is reading (error R), say: "Wait for the cue." If a child asks about something unrelated (error S), ask: "Which detail did your partner mark?" Ask: "What keeps readers from books in this place?"

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 17 card aloud once and let children read it. Children write alone: [[s01-X17]]. Unscored. Shown: a question about what keeps readers from books, drawn from the text. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the text say makes books hard to reach?" Not yet seen: a comment instead of a question, or a question about something else.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the graph on the board and copy the expert card by hand. No groups of three: the adult is the listener, then reads a section aloud and the child states its main idea and two details. No projector: keep the graph on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No article: any short book of facts, read aloud by a family member.
**Skip.** The possessives and the group talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Read one section of Books on the Move aloud to an adult.
  **Say.** "What is the main idea?" Expect: one whole sentence and two details.
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X17]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the possessives; keep the expert card, the groups and the exit. Longer: a child finds one more graph in a book and says what it adds to the words.

### Words and supports

Expert, listener, main idea, detail, graph, axis. Frame: "The main idea is ___. One detail is ___." A child may speak, point or draw on day 17, and the support is recorded. Large print; the sections read aloud; a spoken description of the map and graph. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 opens with 3 minutes on this exit. Error Q is a comment, not a question about a detail: mark one detail before speaking and say it aloud once. The day 17 exit goes back with one comment. Day 18 rereads both articles for main ideas and what the images add.
