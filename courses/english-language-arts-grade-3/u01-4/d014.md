# Day 14: An article tells facts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print Books on the Move with its map for each pair. Cut the sort cards, twelve for each pair. Find the day 14 line of exit cards 14 to 19. Write the umbrella strip on the board: "Raj shared his umbrella with Dee on the path. Dee smiled, and both stayed dry." Sort the day 12 maps and paragraphs (s01-P12-1, s01-P12-2) and the day 13 item results for hand-back, one comment on each. Make the heard-six list from the record sheet.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*5 min. Repair and hand-back.* Repair the day 12 and day 13 exits (s01-X12 and s01-X13). Error B is a topic given as the message ("It is about an ant"). Error C is the action and its result reversed. Read the umbrella strip aloud. Ask: "What did Raj do, and what came of it?" Write the answer with an arrow: Raj shared, so both stayed dry. Ask which came first. While pairs copy it, hand back the day 12 maps and paragraphs and the day 13 item results, one comment each. The day 13 end-item errors are in the comments.

*5 min. Paragraph topic.* Review: you found a paragraph's topic in grade 2. Read aloud: "Our class garden has three beds. One bed grows beans. One bed grows peas. The last bed grows squash." Ask: "What is the topic?" Expect: our class garden. Ask for one fact. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*10 min. Partner rereading.* Review: in unit 1.2 you gave a partner feedback on the card. Pairs reread By Boat in Bangladesh (85 words). Each child reads twice, about 2 minutes, and the partner gives feedback, 30 seconds: one box ticked, one thing that went well, one next try. That is about 5 minutes for a pair. Never say speed. Listen to two pairs, 3 minutes each, and write them in the heard-six column.

*10 min. Joining sentences.* Review: in unit 1.3 you joined sentences with so. Say two short sentences and children write one sentence on a slate with a link word: "The bus stops. Readers line up." ("The bus stops, so readers line up."); "The river is wide. The boat has a flat bottom."; "Mia is tired. She reads in bed." Ask: "Which link word fits, so or because?" Expect: the one that makes sense when read aloud.

#### 20 min. Model

*4 min. Read.* Read Books on the Move aloud, children following with a finger. It is 383 words. Real places are named plainly. Ask nothing about a child's own library.

*16 min. An article is not a story.* Show the headings first. Say: "An article explains with facts. A story has a character with a problem." Set the article beside a page of Reading Beside Grandmother. Ask which has headings. Ask who has a problem in each. Use [[s01-K14-1]] and show the steps with [[steps:s01-K14-1]]. Mark the headings and one fact about a real place, and note that no character faces a problem.

Then the defined words. Say each word, find its sentence in the first paragraph, and say it again in your own words: carrier (the vehicle or the person that brings books to readers), route (the path it travels), stop (a place along the route where readers borrow and return books). Read [[s01-K14-2]] and show the steps with [[steps:s01-K14-2]] for the stop list under By Road in England: place and time. Last, read [[s01-K14-3]] with [[steps:s01-K14-3]]. Say: "The heading tells where to look."

#### 25 min. Hand work

*15 min. Sort.* Pairs sort the twelve cards under A story or Information with [[s01-P14-1]], and say a clue for each card. Walk and look. If a pair sorts by a word in a card, not by what the card does, ask: "Does a character act or feel here, or does it give a fact?" Judged after class with the look-fors in the item.

*10 min. The chart.* Pairs start the main-idea chart for Books on the Move with [[s01-P14-2]]. Fill the topic and one detail from the first section only. Leave the main idea column empty: it comes on day 15. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs sit together. Each child takes 2 minutes to say one thing the article explains (4 minutes in all). Then 3 minutes for the partner to ask for a detail ("Which section tells that?"). Then 3 minutes: hear three volunteers at 1 minute each. Review: in unit 1.1 you learned five discussion moves. Anyone may pass. Ask: "Which sentence in the article could you check?"

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 14 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X14]]. Unscored. Shown: names a fact the strip gives and says no character faces a problem. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the strip tell, and does anyone have a problem?" Not yet seen: a fact that is not on the strip, or only that it is about a boat.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the sort cards by hand onto paper slips and the article onto the board. No projector: keep the map on paper. No pairs: the adult is the partner for the rereading and the sort.

### At home

**Swap.** No article: any short book of facts, with its headings.
**Skip.** The partner rereading and the sort.

- **10 min. Read.** Read one section of Books on the Move aloud to an adult.
  **Say.** "What is one fact this section gives?" Expect: a fact the words give.
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X14]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the joining sentences and the chart; keep the sort and the exit. Longer: a child finds two headings in a book from home and says what each promises.

### Words and supports

Article, story, heading, carrier, route, stop, stop list. Frame: "It is information because ___." Preview the map with a spoken description. Large print; the article read aloud for a child who cannot read it; a scribe for the chart, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 opens with 3 minutes on this exit. Error A is a fact the text does not give ("The boat comes on Sunday"): point to the sentence first, then answer. Error B is a topic given as the reason. Day 15 reads Books on Horseback.
