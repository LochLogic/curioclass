# Day 16: A first paragraph about books for far-away readers

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print both articles for each pair and lined paper for each child. Find the day 16 line of exit cards 14 to 19. Sort the day 15 chart rows, main ideas and exits (s01-P15-1, s01-P15-2, s01-X15v2) for hand-back, with one comment written on each tonight. Draw two columns on the board for the list of needs.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair and hand-back.* Repair the day 15 exit. Error B is a topic given as the main idea ("It is about horses"). Show two cards: "It is about vans." and "The van brings books to Elm Street each Friday." Ask which says what the whole text shows, and why. Expect: the second, a whole claim. Hand back the day 15 chart rows, main ideas and exits with one comment each.

*7 min. Informative writing.* Review: you wrote a topic, two facts and an ending in grade 2. Say the notes: a tadpole hatches from an egg; it grows back legs; it becomes a frog. On slates, children write one topic sentence, two facts and an ending. Unscored. Say: "The topic comes first. The ending says what the facts show."

#### 20 min. Skills

*10 min. Week 4 words.* Say each word once, clap its chunks (car-ri-er, re-mote, head-quar-ters, re-plen-ish) and children write it on a slate: carrier, route, stop, remote, headquarters, replenish, borrow, return. Ask: "Which words start with re-? Read the base first, then add the prefix." Expect: return (re- plus turn). Say: remote and replenish only start with re-; they are not a base plus re-. Decode them by chunks, and check the sense.

*10 min. Joining facts.* Review: you used so and also to join sentences in unit 1.3 and on day 14. Say two facts about one thing and children write one sentence on a slate: "A tadpole has a tail. It swims." ("A tadpole has a tail, and it swims."); "Bees live in a hive. They make honey."; "The class has a blue box. It holds paper." Ask: "Do the two facts belong together?" Expect: yes, they are about one thing. Say: a link word joins facts that belong to one idea.

#### 40 min. Hand work

Nothing is taught today. This is the first informative paragraph, written in one sitting with no frame and no model. The teacher reads it for needs. It is not scored. It is kept for day 20.

*10 min. Gather.* Each child copies three facts from the two articles about how people far from a library get books onto the lines of the student page, with [[s01-P16-1]]. Pairs may reread the articles. Each child writes their own three facts.

*30 min. Write.* Each child writes a paragraph, alone, with [[s01-P16-2]]. No frame, no model, no help with the words. Walk and look. If a child asks what to write, say: "Say your topic, then your facts." Record what you see on the record sheet: a topic, related facts together, a link word, a closing. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. If facts about one idea are mixed with others (error H), if a link joins unrelated ideas (error I), if the ending turns to a new topic (error J) or if the writing is a private list (error K), note it for the list of needs. Say nothing about it now. Collect the paragraphs for day 20.

#### 15 min. Talk

*8 min. Pairs.* Each child takes about 1 minute 30 seconds to read their paragraph aloud. The partner takes about 2 minutes 30 seconds to say one thing they learned. Review: you used discussion moves in grade 3, on day 1. Anyone may pass.

*7 min. Needs.* The whole class lists on the board what writers need next. Write what children say in two columns: what the paragraph did, what it needs. Do not name a child. Keep the list for day 20.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 16 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X16]]. Unscored. Shown: a short paragraph that names the topic, gives the two facts that belong and leaves out the window fact. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which facts are about how the class recycles paper?" Not yet seen: all three facts, or only one.

### Other rooms

No printer: children copy the facts from the board. No pairs: the adult hears the paragraph and says one thing learned. No shared list: keep it on one sheet of paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No articles: any book of facts about one subject.
**Skip.** The slate words and the talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Read your paragraph aloud to an adult.
  **Say.** "What would you add?" Expect: one more fact or a closing.
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X16]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the joining facts; keep the gather, the paragraph and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second paragraph on the other article and compares the two.

### Words and supports

Topic, fact, link word, ending, paragraph. Frame: none for the paragraph, by design. A scribe for a child who cannot write, noted. Large print; the articles read aloud. Record any help; a paragraph written with help is marked shown with help.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 opens with 3 minutes on this exit. Error H is a fact that does not belong with the topic: sort the facts under the topic, then copy the group. The day 16 exit (s01-X16) and the gathered facts (s01-P16-1) go back with one comment each. The paragraphs stay with the teacher for day 20; the list of needs stays on paper.
