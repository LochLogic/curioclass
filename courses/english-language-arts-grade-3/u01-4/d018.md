# Day 18: What the words and the images tell

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print both articles with their images and the glossary and dictionary page, one for each pair. Write four words on the board: replenished, unfinished, librarian, headquarters. Find the day 18 line of exit cards 14 to 19. Sort the day 17 cards and annotations (s01-P17-1, s01-P17-2, s01-P17-3) and exits (s01-X17) for hand-back. Make the conference list of four children.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair and hand-back.* Repair the day 17 exit. Error Q is a comment, not a question about a detail ("I like boats"). Show a strip, "The bike path is closed on Mondays," and two cards: "I like bikes." and "Why is the path closed on Mondays?" Ask which card asks about a detail the strip gives, and mark the detail. Hand back the day 17 expert cards, annotations and exits with one comment each.

*7 min. Context clue.* Review: you used a sentence clue in grade 1 and on day 7. Write: "The street was deserted. No cars, no people, only the wind." Ask: "What does deserted mean? Which words tell you?" Expect: empty, because no cars and no people. Children answer on slates. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*4 min. Week 4 words.* Review: in unit 1.2 you made word maps. Pairs map one week 4 word each, from carrier, route, stop, remote, headquarters, replenish, borrow, return: the word, its chunks, a short meaning, a sentence. Four minutes, quickly.

*16 min. Reading conferences.* Four different children from the list, 4 minutes each: one page read aloud (1 minute), one question answered from the words (1 minute 30 seconds), one next step in the log (1 minute 30 seconds). Write them in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs. If a child reads a word by its first letters (error P), say the chunks, blend, and check the sense.

#### 20 min. Model

*10 min. Words and images.* Reread both articles with the images. Review: on day 15 you read a caption, a map title and a key. First By Boat in Bangladesh: read the words, then the map title and key. Use [[s01-K18-1]] and show the steps with [[steps:s01-K18-1]]. Ask: "Does the map say the boats are the best way everywhere?" Expect: no, it shows where. Then How It Worked: read the words, then the caption. Use [[s01-K18-2]] and [[steps:s01-K18-2]]. Say: "An image adds what it shows. Say no more than it supports." Ask what the words give that the image does not.

*10 min. Four word strategies.* Review: on day 7 you used a sentence clue, on day 11 a root and an ending, and on day 11 a dictionary entry. Four words, one strategy each. Read replenished in its sentence with [[s01-K18-3]] and [[steps:s01-K18-3]]: the clue is saddlebags with books and rode out again. Cover un- on unfinished with [[s01-K18-4]] and [[steps:s01-K18-4]]: read the base first, then add the prefix. Find library inside librarian with [[s01-K18-5]] and [[steps:s01-K18-5]]. Review: help gave helpful and helpless on day 11. Last, open the glossary for headquarters with [[s01-K18-6]] and [[steps:s01-K18-6]]. Say aloud: "Find the word, read every sense, choose the one that fits the sentence."

#### 25 min. Hand work

*13 min. Words and images.* Pairs fill a two-line table for each article, what the words say and what the image adds, with [[s01-P18-1]]. Walk and look. If a pair says the picture shows how every carrier felt (error V), ask: "What does the caption say? Say no more than that."

*6 min. Look up.* Each child finds one word on the printed glossary with [[s01-P18-2]] and one on the dictionary page with [[s01-P18-3]], at the same time. Read the whole entry or every sense, then copy the one that fits the article. If a child writes a guess (error G), ask: "Which line did you read?"

*6 min. Main idea.* Each child picks the main idea card for each article with [[s01-P18-4]] and says why it is a whole claim. If a child picks the topic card (error B), ask: "Does it say what the whole article shows?" Judged after class with the look-fors in the items. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs sit together. Each child takes 2 minutes to say what an image tells that the words do not (4 minutes in all). Then 3 minutes for the partner to ask for a detail ("Which image do you mean?"). Then 3 minutes: hear three volunteers at 1 minute each. Review: you used discussion moves in grade 3, on day 1. Anyone may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 18 card aloud twice. Show the two images. Children write alone: [[s01-X18]]. Unscored. Shown: one whole sentence for the main idea that also uses the map. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the whole strip show, and what does the map add?" Not yet seen: only a topic ("It is about a van"), or a claim beyond the strip and the map.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the glossary and dictionary entries by hand. No projector: keep the images on paper. No pairs: the adult is the partner for the table and the talk.

### At home

**Swap.** No article: any short text with a picture and its caption.
**Skip.** The word maps, the conferences and the talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Pick one word you do not know in your book and use a clue to guess it.
  **Say.** "Which words gave the clue?" Expect: words in the same sentence.
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X18]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the word maps; keep the table, the look-ups and the exit. Longer: a child writes what a caption would need to say to prove one more thing, and what it cannot prove.

### Words and supports

Caption, glossary, dictionary, prefix, headquarters, replenished. Frame: "The words say ___. The image adds ___." Preview the images with a spoken description. Large print; the articles read aloud; a scribe for the table, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 is a Check day with no repair slot. Day 19 hands nothing back. This exit (s01-X18) and its errors, V (an image read beyond what it shows) and B (only that both are maps), go to the opening of day 20 with the day 19 results. Day 19 holds no teaching: silent skills, reading, seven items and one exit.
