# Section 1.4 opener: articles about books on the move (days 14 to 19)

## What the section does

Children read two short articles, one about book carriers today and one about the packhorse librarians of Kentucky in the 1930s. They learn that an article explains with facts, find its main idea and two details, group facts into a paragraph, listen to an expert, and use the words with a photograph, a map and a graph. Day 19 is a Check day on a fresh panel.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 14 sorts story and information; day 15 starts the main-idea chart and the map title and key; day 16 writes a paragraph; day 17 prepares an expert card and a discussion; day 18 uses a glossary and a dictionary page. Day 19 holds no teaching: silent skills, reading, seven items, silent review and one exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **An article gives facts.** A story has a character facing a problem; an article explains.
- **A main idea is a whole claim.** Two details support it.
- **A caption and a map add facts the words may not give.** A caption does not prove more than it says.
- **A glossary entry is for the word in the sentence.** Check it; do not guess.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, B, V:** a missing fact, a topic given as the main idea, an image read as proof of more than it shows.
- **H, I, J, K:** facts left ungrouped, a link joining unrelated ideas, an ending on a new topic, writing a younger reader cannot follow.
- **Q to U:** a missing detail, an interruption, an unrelated question, an opening opinion repeated, a vague answer.
- **F, G, AC:** a word clue ignored, a look-up skipped, a word part read the wrong way.

## Marking

Same scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Repairs go to the next teaching lesson, never during the check.
