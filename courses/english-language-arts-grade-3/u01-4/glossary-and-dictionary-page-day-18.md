# Glossary and dictionary page (day 18)

## Glossary, printed beside the article

- **bookmobile:** a vehicle fitted out as a small library.
- **headquarters:** the main place a group works from.
- **saddlebag:** a bag that hangs over the back of a horse.

## Dictionary page

Four entries, in alphabetical order.

- **borrow** (verb): to take something and give it back later.
- **remote** (adjective): far from other places.
- **return** (verb): to bring something back.
- **route** (noun): the path a person or vehicle follows.

Say aloud: "Find the word, read every sense, choose the one that fits the sentence."
