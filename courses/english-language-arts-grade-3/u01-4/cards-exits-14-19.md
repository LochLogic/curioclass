# Exit cards, days 14 to 19

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children write. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 14 (s01-X14).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "The Reed Lake book boat ties up at the dock each Saturday. A sign by the dock lists its hours. Readers check the sign before they walk down." Ask: Explain how you know this strip is information, not a story.
- **Day 15 (s01-X15).** Invented for practice. Picture and map: a bookmobile at a school. Caption: "The bookmobile stops at the school every Tuesday." Ask: Write the main idea. Draw the bookmobile at a school gate, and a keyed map of Ridgemoor with a title, a school and a bookmobile stop marked.
- **Day 16 (s01-X16).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "The class recycling box: Paper goes into the blue box. The box was moved by the window last week. A helper takes the full box to the big bin." Ask: Copy the two facts that belong in a paragraph about recycling paper.
- **Day 17 (s01-X17).** This text is invented for practice. Read (aloud once, and printed): "Few roads reach the farms at Reed Lake. The book boat ties up at the dock each Saturday, so families can borrow books." Ask: Write one question for the expert about what keeps readers from books here.
- **Day 18 (s01-X18).** Invented for practice. Strip: "The Maple Street van visits three schools. It parks outside each on Thursday." Image: a map with three schools and a van route. Ask: Write the main idea of the strip and its map. Draw a small keyed map with three marked schools joined by a route line.
- **Day 19 (s01-X19).** This sidebar is invented for practice. Read: "A caption is a short explanation below a picture. A useful caption names the place. Copying means making a copy." Ask: Explain what useful means in the sidebar, using its -ful ending.

## Pictures and maps for these cards

- **Day 15.** A drawn bookmobile at a school gate. A keyed map of Ridgemoor with a school and a bookmobile stop marked, a title and a short key.
- **Day 18.** A small keyed map with a title, three marked schools and a route line joining them.
