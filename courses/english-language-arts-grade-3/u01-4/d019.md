# Day 19: Check on a new article panel

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the panel page. Make the picture from the art spec. Mark a record sheet column for each item. Find the day 19 line of exit cards 14 to 19.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Nothing is taught today. The day 18 table, look-ups and main ideas (s01-P18-1, s01-P18-2, s01-P18-3, s01-P18-4) stay with the children and go back on day 20. Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each word once and children write it. Week 4: carrier, route, stop, remote, headquarters, replenish, borrow, return. Then un-, re- or dis- on a base, said as the prefix and base: un- kind, re- play, dis- like. Ten minutes: partners reread a favorite page, twice each, quietly. Ten minutes: each child adds a line to the reading log.

#### 10 min. Reading

Each child reads the panel twice, silently, with its picture, caption, map and glossary. One word or phrase may be marked. Say nothing about the words or the pictures.

#### 35 min. Items

Children work alone, in writing. Teach nothing and give no hint. Record each child on the record sheet while they write: [[s01-A12]] (6 minutes, RI.3.1), [[s01-A13]] (8, RI.3.2), [[s01-A14]] (5, RI.3.7), [[s01-A15]] (4, L.3.4a), [[s01-A16]] (4, L.3.4b), [[s01-A17]] (4, L.3.4c) and [[s01-A18]] (4, L.3.4d). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and write down any prompt. No grade is given.

#### 5 min. Silent review

Each child rereads the panel and their answers and may change one. Silent.

#### 5 min. Collection and transition

Collect the student pages, then read the day 19 card aloud twice for the exit.

#### 5 min. Exit

Children write alone: [[s01-X19]]. Unscored. Shown: says -ful means full of, and useful is full of use. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the ending -ful mean?" Not yet seen: only that it is about copying, or useful as without use.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the panel by hand. A child who cannot see the picture hears the factual description from the art spec: a wagon with book shelves, pulled by two horses on a dirt road, with a man driving. No pairs: the adult is the partner for the rereading.

### At home

**Swap.** No panel page: any short article, read twice.
**Skip.** The partner rereading and the slate words.

- **10 min. Assessment.** First, your child reads the panel page twice and answers [[s01-A12]] and [[s01-A13]] alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Then your child answers [[s01-X19]] alone.
- **20 min. Read.** Read with an adult for 20 minutes.
  **Say.** "What does your book explain?" Expect: a main idea in a sentence.
- **10 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the log line. Longer: a child reads a second article and says its main idea with two details.

### Words and supports

Panel, caption, map, glossary, county. Large print; a spoken description of the picture; items read aloud except where reading is the evidence; a scribe for the answers, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 20 opens with 3 minutes on this exit and the item results (s01-X19, s01-A12 to s01-A18). Error B is a topic given as the main idea ("It is about horses"): connect two details to one complete claim on a new strip. Day 20 also repairs day 18's exit, error V (an image read beyond what it shows). Repairs go to the next teaching lesson, never during the check.
