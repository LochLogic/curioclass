# Art spec: day 19 panel pictures

- **Locator map.** An original plain outline map of the United States with the state of Maryland shaded and labelled, and a dot for Hagerstown in the western part of the state. A title ("Where Maryland is") and a short key. No other state names needed.
- **Picture of the wagon.** No photograph of the 1905 book wagon with a confirmed public-domain or federal source is on file: the images on the Western Maryland Regional Library and Appalachian History pages (sources g3-u1-f6 and g3-u1-f7) are credited to the library's collection with no rights statement. Draw an original illustration, and no photograph: a horse-drawn wagon with shelves of books along its sides, two horses in harness, a man holding the reins, and a dirt road. Caption: "A drawing of a horse-drawn book wagon, Hagerstown, Maryland, about 1905."
- **Image description.** A short factual description for any child who cannot see the picture: a wagon with book shelves, pulled by two horses on a dirt road, with a man driving. Nothing about what it means.
