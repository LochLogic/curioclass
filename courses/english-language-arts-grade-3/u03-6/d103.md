# Day 103: Write the second reason and join ideas with a link word

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, and one link sheet for each pair. Keep the key for the link sheet, key-link-pairs-day-103, with you; it is not printed. Have the day 102 exit slips and each child's reason 1. Write the link words on the board: because, so, therefore, since, for example. Pick the four children for conferences by the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say unworried, forgetful and explaining. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P103-1]]. Then people and would, written whole, [[s03-P103-2]]. Review: you split a long word into chunks and blended each one on day 81. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P103-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P103-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (day 81). *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk and says what curious means, then they swap, [[s03-P103-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AI, a word split so a chunk cannot be blended, and error AA, a guess from the first letters.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P103-6]]. Review: the routine from day 81. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each): ask which detail they used in reason 1 and what it shows. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and recall

*2 min.* Hand back the day 102 exit slips. Whole class, error I, from s03-X102: write They are loyal because they are friends beside Wren waits on the rocks for Kip. Ask what happened in the book that proves it. *3 min.* Pairs tell what each link word does, [[s03-P103-7]]. Review: because gives a cause and so gives a result (day 6); since opens a reason and therefore follows it (day 98). New today: for example gives an instance, one case of something.

#### 10 min. Choose the link

*4 min.* Show one reason joined two ways, [[s03-K103-1]]: Mateo shrugged because Ada would not say who got the tree seat, and Mateo shrugged because the beans grew tall. Say both ideas, then choose cause, result or example. The first fits; the second joins ideas that are not related (error J). *6 min.* Pairs take the link sheet. For each of the three pairs of sentences, they write cause, result or example beside the pair, [[s03-P103-8]]. Then they sort the five link words on the board under cause, result or example, [[s03-P103-9]]. No link is written into a blank.

#### 5 min. Hard-part reread

Children reread the last lines of chapter 3 of book 2, from "It's better," said Ben carefully, to the end, and write on side 2 what they tell a reader who is deciding about the books, [[s03-P103-10]]. Nothing is marked in the book.

#### 20 min. Draft reason 2

Say before they write: say both ideas, then choose the link; a link between ideas that do not relate fails (error J). *15 min.* Children write reason 2, [[s03-P103-11]]: a link joins the opinion to the reason, another joins the reason to its detail, and a sentence says what the detail tells a reader. Watch for error J and error I. *5 min.* They reread it and mark one detail not yet explained, [[s03-P103-12]].

#### 10 min. Link check in pairs

*6 min.* Partners name the relation of each link in the other's paragraph, cause, result or example, [[s03-P103-13]]; 3 minutes each. *4 min.* Each writer changes one link, with the first version kept beside it, [[s03-P103-14]]. Watch for error L, the same link written more neatly.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Link: a word that joins two ideas. Each child writes one sentence about a book that uses the word, [[s03-P103-15]]. The sentences are collected.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X103]]. Shown: a link that shows the second sentence as a result of the first. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Did the first sentence cause the second, or the second cause the first?" Not yet seen: because (error B) or for example or but (error J). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the pair partner for the link sheet and the link check. One projector: show the link words and the two ways of joining a reason. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No slate: write the ending words on paper.
**Skip.** The conferences and the link check.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child says two sentences about their day joined by because, and two joined by so.
  **Say.** "Which part is the cause and which is the result?" Expect: the part after because is the cause; the part after so is the result.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X103]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the link sheet, reason 2 and the exit; cut the slate line and the word sentence. Longer: write a third reason joined with for example.

### Words and supports

Link, cause, result, example. Frames: "___ because ___." "___, so ___." "For example, ___." Audio beside print for the reread; large print for the link words; the paragraph told aloud before it is written; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 104 begins with the day 103 exit, s03-X103, and repairs error J: say both ideas, then choose cause, result or example. Then it drafts the closing, trades drafts with a partner and changes the draft after the partner's question. Reason 2 [[s03-P103-11]] goes in the dated folder. The word work, [[s03-P103-1]] to [[s03-P103-5]], the log work, [[s03-P103-6]], the spoken work, [[s03-P103-7]], [[s03-P103-9]] and [[s03-P103-13]], the link work, [[s03-P103-8]], [[s03-P103-10]], [[s03-P103-12]] and [[s03-P103-14]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P103-15]], stay in the folder.
