# Exit cards, days 101 to 104

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. An exit slip is a half slip holding about two items. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 101 (s03-X101), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: "The Rain Boot Gang, book 1. Four friends mend a leaky bus shelter. They learn that careful work takes patience." Ask: Write one opening sentence for a review of this series. Name the series and give your opinion.
- **Day 102 (s03-X102), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: "I recommend the Tidepool Twins because they are loyal friends. Details from book 1: (a) Wren waits on the rocks for Kip. (b) The twins live in a blue house. (c) Kip's shoe is red." Ask: Write one sentence: the detail that proves loyalty and what it shows.
- **Day 103 (s03-X103), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: "The Pocket Moon books. Nell packs a flashlight before every trip. ___ she is never left in the dark." Ask: Write the linking word that fits the blank. The second sentence is a result.
- **Day 104 (s03-X104), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A partner's note on a review of the Hill Street Gang books. The last line says, "Anyway, my lunch was good." The note says, "Your last line is about lunch. What do you want me to do about the books?" Ask: Write a new last line for the review that answers the note.
