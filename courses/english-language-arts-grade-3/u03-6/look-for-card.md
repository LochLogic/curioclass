# Review look-for card (day 104)

Print one for each pair, half a page. The writer reads the whole draft. The partner names one look-for that was met and asks one question linked to a word in the draft. The question goes in the margin.

- The opening names both books and gives an opinion.
- Two reasons are named in order.
- Each reason has a detail from a book.
- The links fit.
- The closing says who might like the series.
