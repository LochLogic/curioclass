# Day 102: Write the first reason and learn the title rule

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a title rule card for each child. Have the day 101 exit slips and each child's plan page and introduction. Put the title model where all can see it, and keep the printed review on the desk. Mark the passage in book 2, chapter 4, from "On Friday morning, Ada began to ask" to "Ada wrote it down", about 120 words. Write the model paragraph on the board.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say questioning, preparing and usefulness. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P102-1]]. Then thought and enough, written whole, [[s03-P102-2]]. Review: you split a long word into chunks and blended each one on day 81. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P102-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P102-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (day 81). *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk and says what different means, then they swap, [[s03-P102-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AI, a word split so a chunk cannot be blended, and error AA, a guess from the first letters.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading with a feedback card

Pairs, the marked passage. Review: the partner rereading and the feedback card are the routine from day 2: phrases and expression, no speed. Each child reads the passage aloud and then reads it again, [[s03-P102-6]] (3 minutes); the partner marks one phrase read well and one place for expression on the card, [[s03-P102-7]] (1 minute). Swap. That is 12 minutes with 3 to change over. Say nothing about speed.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Pairs say the job of each named word, [[s03-P102-8]]. Review: a noun names, a verb acts, an adjective describes, an adverb tells how, a pronoun stands in for a noun (day 86). Pairs check each other against the five jobs.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and recall

*2 min.* Hand back the day 101 exit slips. Whole class, error H, from s03-X101: write Four friends mend a leaky bus shelter, beside I think the series is worth reading because the friends are patient. Ask which says what the writer thinks. Write the opinion first, then add the story as proof. *3 min.* Pairs point to one reason and one detail in the printed review, [[s03-P102-16]]. Retrieve: a reason says why; a detail says what happened.

#### 10 min. Model a reason paragraph

*7 min.* On the board, model one reason about the setting, not a reason the children are likely to choose, [[s03-K102-1]]. Say each sentence's job aloud: the reason says why, the detail says what is there, the last sentence says what the detail tells a reader. The paragraph is on side 1. *3 min.* Pairs underline the reason, the detail and what it tells, [[s03-P102-9]].

#### 10 min. Title capitals, taught

*3 min.* Show the four rules on the title model, [[s03-K102-2]]: the first word and the important words start with a capital; a, an, the, and, but, or, for, of, in, on, at, to and by stay lowercase unless first; the word after a colon starts with a capital. Review: you copied this title on day 100; copying only. *5 min.* On slates, children fix three invented titles, [[s03-P102-10]]; partners check against the rule card. Say that up is not on the card's list, so it takes a capital. Watch for error R, important words left lowercase, and error AM, small words given capitals. *2 min.* Children write the heading of their review with both book titles and check each against the card, [[s03-P102-11]].

#### 20 min. Draft reason 1

*3 min.* Children reread the strongest detail they marked on the plan page. *17 min.* Children write reason 1, [[s03-P102-12]], in three labelled lines: Reason, Detail and page, What it tells a reader. Keep the model on the board. Watch for error I, a reason that repeats the opinion, and error H, a detail copied with no reason.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, 1 to change. Read your reason, [[s03-P102-13]]. Your partner names the detail and says what it shows, [[s03-P102-14]]. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Detail: something that happened or is there in a book. Each child writes one sentence about a book that uses the word, [[s03-P102-15]]. The sentences are collected.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X102]]. Shown: the detail that proves loyalty and what it shows. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which detail shows she is loyal?" Not yet seen: a detail copied with no meaning (error H) or the opinion said again as the reason (error I). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the pair partner, marks the card and is the talk partner. One projector: show the model paragraph and the title model. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No slate: write the titles on paper.
**Skip.** The feedback card and the grammar words.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Find.** Your child finds one title at home and says which words start with a capital.
  **Say.** "Which small words stay small?" Expect: a, the, and, for, of, in, on, at, to, by, unless first.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X102]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the title rule, reason 1 and the exit; cut the slate line and the word sentence. Longer: write a second reason about a different book.

### Words and supports

Detail, reason, title, capital. Frames: "I recommend ___ because ___." "For example, ___. This shows ___." Audio beside print for the marked passage; large print for the rule card; the reason told aloud before it is written; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 103 begins with the day 102 exit, s03-X102, and repairs error I, a reason that repeats the opinion: ask what happened in the book that proves it. Then it drafts reason 2 and joins ideas with fitting link words. Reason 1 [[s03-P102-12]] and the heading [[s03-P102-11]] go in the dated folder. The word work, [[s03-P102-1]] to [[s03-P102-5]], the reading, [[s03-P102-6]] and [[s03-P102-7]], the spoken work, [[s03-P102-8]], [[s03-P102-13]], [[s03-P102-14]] and [[s03-P102-16]], the model work, [[s03-P102-9]] and [[s03-P102-10]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P102-15]], stay in the folder.
