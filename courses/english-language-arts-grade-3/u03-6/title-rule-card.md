# Title rule card (days 102, 104 and 106)

Print one for each child, half a page. Taught on day 102 with the printed title model, read again on 104 and 106.

1. The first word starts with a capital.
2. The important words start with a capital.
3. These small words stay lowercase unless they come first: a, an, the, and, but, or, for, of, in, on, at, to, by.
4. The word after a colon starts with a capital.

The title model: The Courtyard Club: The Missing Plan

Check your heading: Does the first word start with a capital? Did I keep the small words lowercase? Is the word after the colon a capital?
