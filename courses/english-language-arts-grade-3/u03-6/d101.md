# Day 101: Plan the review and write its opening

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a plan page for each child. Have sheet O01 with its one-line note on each child's desk to return; it is a guide for today's plan and is not repaired. Have the day 100 exit slips. Put the title model where all can see it, and keep the printed review on the desk. Pick the four children for conferences by the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say disagreement, remembering and carefully. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P101-1]]. Then laugh and answer, written whole, [[s03-P101-2]]. Review: you split a long word into chunks and blended each one on day 81; you wrote irregular words on day 99. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P101-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P101-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (day 81). *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk and says what recommend means, then they swap, [[s03-P101-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AI, a word split so a chunk cannot be blended, and error AA, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P101-6]]. Review: the log line and the four-minute conference are the routine from day 81. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each): ask which book they would give a friend first and what happens in it that shows why. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and recall

*3 min.* Hand back the day 100 exit slips. Whole class, error K, from s03-X100: write the weak review's last line, My cousin has a dog named Biscuit, beside the model's last line. Ask which line goes back to the reasons. Then ask what a closing line goes back to, and who it names. *1 min.* Return sheet O01 with its note; children read it silently. *1 min.* Pairs point to the sentence in the printed review that gives the opinion and say why a review needs it first, [[s03-P101-7]].

#### 20 min. Choose and plan

*3 min.* Children reread their marked passages from the two books, at most 250 words in all, and look again at their two-book maps from section 3.4. Review: you built the maps on days 94 and 97. *7 min.* Each child chooses an opinion and two reasons, and notes the page of a detail that proves each. Say before they write: a reason says why, with something that happened in the books; a reason that repeats the opinion adds nothing (error I). Review: a reason tells why (day 98). *10 min.* On the plan page, which has no frame beyond its labels, children write the titles, the opinion, reason 1 and reason 2 with their pages, a link for each and a closing idea, [[s03-P101-8]]. The titles are copied from the title model; the rule for them is taught on day 102. The strongest reason goes last. Watch for error I and error H, a retelling with no opinion.

#### 20 min. Draft the introduction

*2 min.* Say the three jobs of an opening: it names the series and both books, it says what I think, and it says that two reasons follow. Children keep a finger on the opinion sentence of the printed review. *13 min.* Children write the introduction on side 2, [[s03-P101-9]], in three labelled lines, not one sentence: Books, I think, Next. Watch for error H and error I. *5 min.* Beneath it they list the two reasons in order, the strongest last, [[s03-P101-10]]. These become the headings for days 102 and 103.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, 1 to change. Read your introduction, [[s03-P101-11]]. Your partner says the opinion and what the introduction says comes next, [[s03-P101-12]]. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Recommend. Review: you used recommend on day 100. *3 min.* Each child writes one sentence about a book that uses the word, [[s03-P101-13]]. *2 min.* Any three children who wish read theirs. The sentences are collected.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X101]]. Shown: one sentence that names the series and gives an opinion. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What do you think of this series?" Not yet seen: a retelling of the story (error H) or an opinion given as its own reason (error I). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the pair partner and the talk partner, and says the opinion and what comes next from the introduction. One projector: show the title model and the printed review. The plan page and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No slate: write the ending words on paper.
**Skip.** The conferences and the heard-six column.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you which book they would give a friend first and one reason.
  **Say.** "What happens in the book that shows it?" Expect: an event from the book, not just "it is good".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X101]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the plan page, the introduction and the exit; cut the slate line and the word sentence. Longer: write a second opening for a reader who prefers a different kind of book.

### Words and supports

Recommend, opinion, reason, introduction. Frames: "I recommend ___ because ___." "I have two reasons." Audio beside print for the marked passages; large print for the plan page; the introduction told aloud before it is written; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 102 begins with the day 101 exit, s03-X101, and repairs error H, a retelling with no opinion: write the opinion first, then add the story as proof. Then it models one reason paragraph, teaches the title rule and drafts reason 1 with a detail from the books. The plan page [[s03-P101-8]], the introduction [[s03-P101-9]] and the reason list [[s03-P101-10]] go in the dated folder. The word work, [[s03-P101-1]] to [[s03-P101-5]], the log work, [[s03-P101-6]], the spoken work, [[s03-P101-7]], [[s03-P101-11]] and [[s03-P101-12]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P101-13]], stay in the folder.
