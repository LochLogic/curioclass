# Day 104: Write the closing, trade drafts and change the draft

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, and one look-for card for each pair. Have the day 103 exit slips and each child's whole draft so far. Mark the passage in book 2, chapter 5, from "Ada watched Priya take a blanket" to "he flopped on it", about 140 words. Keep the title rule card on each desk.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say discussing, improvement and conclusion. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P104-1]]. Then through and whose, written whole, [[s03-P104-2]]. Review: you split a long word into chunks and blended each one on day 81. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P104-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P104-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (day 81). *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk and says what peaceful means, then they swap, [[s03-P104-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AI, a word split so a chunk cannot be blended, and error AA, a guess from the first letters.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading with a feedback card

Pairs, the marked passage. Review: the routine from day 2: phrases and expression, no speed. Each child reads the passage aloud with expression and then reads it again, [[s03-P104-6]] (3 minutes); the partner marks one phrase read well and one place for more expression on the card, [[s03-P104-7]] (1 minute). Swap. That is 12 minutes with 3 to change over. Say nothing about speed.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Pairs. Each child reads one sentence from their own draft and says its subject and its verb, [[s03-P104-8]]. Review: the verb matches its subject (day 88). If they do not match, the writer fixes the verb on the spot.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and recall

*2 min.* Hand back the day 103 exit slips. Whole class, error J, from s03-X103: write Ben asks questions. Therefore the beans grow tall. Say both ideas. They do not relate. Then write Ben asks questions, so Ada's plan improves, and name it a result. *3 min.* Children look at their two reasons and tell a partner who might like the series, [[s03-P104-9]].

#### 20 min. Draft the conclusion

Say before they write: a closing goes back to the reasons; it does not start a new thought (error K). *3 min.* Children reread their two reasons. *12 min.* Children write the closing in three labeled lines, [[s03-P104-10]]: Still (the opinion in new words), Who (who might like the series) and Try it (an invitation). *5 min.* They check the heading and every title in the draft against the title rule card, [[s03-P104-11]]. Review: the title rule from day 102. Watch for error R and error AM.

#### 10 min. Peer exchange

Pairs, with the look-for card. Review: the rules for a discussion from unit 1: one speaker at a time, listen with care, stay on the topic. The writer reads the whole draft, [[s03-P104-12]] (2 minutes). The partner writes on the card one look-for that was met and one question linked to a word in the draft, [[s03-P104-13]], and asks it, [[s03-P104-14]] (1 minute). The writer answers, [[s03-P104-15]] (1 minute). Swap. That is 8 minutes with 2 to change over. Watch for error O, a question unrelated to the draft. The card is clipped to the draft.

#### 15 min. Revise after the question

Review: one note, one change, the first version beside it (day 100). Each child changes one reason using the partner's question and marks the closing line the question touches, [[s03-P104-16]], with the first version kept beside the change. Watch for error L, the same words written more neatly. Collect each draft with its card at the end.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Conclusion: the part of a review that ends it and goes back to the opinion. Each child writes one sentence about a book that uses the word, [[s03-P104-17]]. The sentences are collected.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X104]]. Shown: one closing line that answers the note and names who might like the books. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What do you want the reader to do about the books?" Not yet seen: a line unrelated to the books (error K) or the same line written more neatly (error L). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner. Read the draft aloud, say one look-for you heard, ask one question about a word, and write it on the card before the child changes one thing. One projector: show the look-for card and the title rule card. Drafts and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No coloured pencils needed.
**Skip.** The feedback card and the grammar check.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child reads their draft aloud to you, and you ask one question about it.
  **Say.** "Which word in your draft made me ask that?" Expect: a word from the draft.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X104]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the closing, the peer exchange, the revision and the exit; cut the slate line and the word sentence. Longer: change a second reason using the same question.

### Words and supports

Conclusion, closing, look-for, question. Frames: "I still recommend ___." "Try it if you like ___." "Which word shows ___?" Audio beside print for the reread; large print for the look-for card; the closing told aloud before it is written; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 105, after its word check and independent reading, returns the day 104 draft with one written note on each part, including the title, and the day 104 exit, s03-X104, and repairs error K: reread the reasons and name who might like the books. Children then make the one change the note asks for, with the first version beside it, and start to prepare the series discussion. The word work, [[s03-P104-1]] to [[s03-P104-5]], the reading, [[s03-P104-6]] and [[s03-P104-7]], the spoken work, [[s03-P104-8]], [[s03-P104-9]], [[s03-P104-12]], [[s03-P104-14]] and [[s03-P104-15]], the card work, [[s03-P104-13]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P104-17]], stay in the folder. The closing [[s03-P104-10]], the title check [[s03-P104-11]] and the revision [[s03-P104-16]] go to the teacher with the draft.
