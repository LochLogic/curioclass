# Section 3.6 opener: drafting the book review (days 101 to 104)

## What the section does

For four days children write one book review of the series, a paragraph at a time. Day 101 plans it and writes the introduction. Day 102 writes the first reason with a detail from the books and teaches how to write a title. Day 103 writes the second reason and joins ideas with fitting linking words. Day 104 writes the closing, trades drafts with a partner, and changes the draft after the partner's question.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. A long-words warm-up opens every day. Days 101 and 103 hold independent reading and short conferences. Days 102 and 104 hold partner rereading with a feedback card. Every day ends with a short written exit. Children keep a dated folder: the plan page, the draft, the partner's question, the teacher's written note.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **An opinion says what I think; a reason says why.** A reason that only repeats the opinion adds nothing.
- **A detail says what happened.** A reason needs a detail from a book and a sentence on what the detail shows a reader.
- **A linking word joins ideas that truly relate.** Because gives a cause, therefore and so give a result, since gives a cause first, for example gives an instance.
- **A closing returns to the opinion.** It names who might like the series and invites them to try it.
- **A title has a rule.** The first word and the important words start with a capital; small words such as a, an, the, and, but, or, for, of, in, on, at, to and by stay lowercase unless first.
- **A revision answers a question.** One question, one change, and the first version stays beside it.

## The errors, in plain words

- **H:** Retells the story and gives no opinion. Repair: write the opinion first, then add the story as proof. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **I:** Gives the opinion again as its own reason. Repair: ask what happened in the book that proves it. 7 minutes.
- **J:** Joins ideas that do not relate. Repair: say both ideas, then choose cause, result or example. 7 minutes.
- **K:** Ends with something unrelated to the books. Repair: reread the reasons and name who might like the series. 5 minutes.
- **L:** Recopies neatly and calls it a revision. Repair: make one change from the question, original beside. 5 minutes.
- **R and AM:** Title capitals missed or overdone. Repair: compare with the title rule card. 5 minutes.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored. The draft is judged together with the note and the revision on day 106.
