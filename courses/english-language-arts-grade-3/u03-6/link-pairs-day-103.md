# Link pairs, day 103

Print one for each pair, one side. Each pair is two sentences from book 2 with the link left blank. Pairs choose the link and say whether it gives a cause, a result or an example. Words to choose from are on the board: because, therefore, so, since, for example.

1. Chapter 3. Ben did not want Jun beside Nia and Sam. ____________ they whisper a lot about their dragon book.
2. Chapter 3. Ben asked Jun where he wanted to sit. ____________ Ada put a card on the far bench that said QUIET READING.
3. Chapter 4. The answers were nothing like the names. ____________ Priya had not wanted the sunny cushion.
