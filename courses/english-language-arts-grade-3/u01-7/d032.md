# Day 32: Draft the first proof paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the feedback card, day 29, and the day 32 exit card. Copy the four-sentence strip from page 4 of "The Boat Book Day" on the board. Chart the link words also, and, but, so. List six new children for recording and the six recorded on day 31 for one-minute rerecordings. Set the device out of reach.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Hand-back.* Return the day 31 exit (s01-X31, s01-X31b) and the practice pages (s01-P31-1, s01-P31-2, s01-P31-3). If many listed the details with no reason (error H), say: "Give a reason that both details support." Children underline the reason in one given answer.

*7 min. Phrasing.* Review: partner rereading with the feedback card (day 29). Pairs read the four-sentence strip from page 4 of "The Boat Book Day", from "At last Lia sees the boat" to "river animals", twice each, one turn at a time, and tick one box, [[s01-P32-1]]. The six recorded yesterday read their notes silently and choose the sentence to rerecord.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* Review: -ly (grade 2 day 37): how or in what way. Write slow and slowly, quick and quickly. Children say each meaning, then write [[s01-P32-2]].

*5 min. Grammar.* Review: join two facts with and, also or but (grade 3 days 14 to 29). Write: "Nadia was tired. She kept reading." Children join them, [[s01-P32-3]]. Both and and but fit; but fits best, because the second fact goes against the first. Accept either with a comma and a reason.

*7 min. Partner rereading.* Pairs reread the 88 words of "Sam Meets the Book Bus" from "Inside, the bus looks like a library that has grown wheels" to "it is book two", twice each (about 45 seconds a read), and tick the feedback card. Listen to two pairs.

#### 40 min. Hand work

*5 min.* Reread the plan frame and the boat detail. *25 min.* Write the first proof paragraph, [[s01-P32-4]]: a boat detail, a fact that shows it, a link word, and why it supports the strategy. If a word may be new to a younger reader, say what it means. *5 min.* Read aloud to yourself. *5 min.* Date and file.

*Recording (24 minutes of the 40).* Six new children are recorded for 3 minutes each (purpose, 60 to 80 words of a story passage, one reread sentence, one picture). The six recorded on day 31 each rerecord one sentence in one minute (6 minutes). No writing conferences today. Write one line after class for each recorded child naming one move heard.

#### 15 min. Talk

Pairs: each child reads their paragraph, 2 minutes, and the partner writes one link word and one fact, [[s01-P32-5]], 2 minutes (8 minutes). Then the class chart "which link fits?" (7 minutes): show "Lia walks the boards slowly. ___ she watches each step." with also, but and so, and decide with [[s01-K32-1]]. **Ask.** "How does your detail show the strategy at work?" Expect: the detail and the small step it shows.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 32 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X32]]. Unscored. Shown: says checking helps Rosa pack the right thing, in a way a younger reader could follow. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the list help her not forget?" Not yet seen: restates the strip (error K), or adds that the list names the day (error A).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the strips and writes the paragraph as the child speaks. No device: practice aloud and write the note. No printer: write the four sentences on a card. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No draft: say the paragraph aloud.
**Skip.** The link choice.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the paragraph aloud, [[s01-P32-4]], and points to the link word.
  **Say.** "Which word links your facts?" Expect: also, and or but.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child says what quickly means, [[s01-P32-2]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X32]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the grammar to one pair of facts; keep the paragraph and the exit. Longer: children write a second link choice and say why.

### Words and supports

Link word, detail, fact, proof, slowly, quickly. Frames: "The detail is ___. It shows ___." "Also ___." Large print; a scribe for the paragraph, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X32). Error K is a step with no explanation: say how the step helps, so a younger reader can follow. The recording notes come back. Paragraphs go to day 33.
