# Day 30: Read a reading contract and plan my own

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the plan frame, day 30, the goal card, day 29, the strategy menu, day 29, the affix cards, the word check card and the day 30 exit card. Chart the model contract and mark its seven parts on a key. List six children for the last word-record rotation (24 have been heard) and two for conferences. Mark each child's goal card from day 29.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Hand-back.* Return the day 29 exit (s01-X29, s01-X29b). If many gave a goal with no move (error X), say: "A goal needs a move. Let the voice rise at a question mark." Each child reads their goal card and checks it names a strategy.

*7 min. Affix cards.* Review: un-, re-, dis-, pre-, mis-, -ful and -less (grade 3 days 11 to 27). Hold up each card; every child says its meaning and writes one word on a slate: unhappy, reopen, disagree, preview, misread, careful, homeless. Check as a class. Unscored. Do not use the dictation words or their bases today.

#### 20 min. Skills

*10 min. Word check.* First 3 minutes: say each of the eight words twice and children write them on the first lines of the page, [[s01-P30-1]]. Then 7 minutes: hear six children, one minute each, from the word check card (the last rotation of six). The rest write [[s01-P30-2]], [[s01-P30-3]] and [[s01-P30-4]]. Meets: reads untwist and hopeful and explains not and full of; reads enough, laugh and through by checking the letters. Miss: un- means again (error AC); a word guessed from its first letters (error P). Mark nothing for a child you do not hear today. Regroup the pattern teaching on day 31. Retest the diagnosed grade 2 pattern in the same slot.

*10 min. Conferences.* Two 5-minute reading conferences while the rest read from their logs.

#### 20 min. Text and model

*5 min. Read.* Read "The Boat Book Day" aloud (about 4 minutes), then one minute to say who the story is about.

*10 min. The model.* Show "Taking Small Steps" on the chart and read it aloud. New here: a reading contract is a short plan with parts. Read each part and mark it with the key: topic, definition, detail, link, detail, conclusion, question. Use [[s01-K30-1]]: "Which sentence tells a younger reader what a strategy is?" Then children mark the model on their own copy, [[s01-P30-5]]. Say: "A younger reader needs the definition."

*5 min. Recording demonstration.* Nobody records today. Use the device. Say your purpose aloud: "I will read two sentences so a listener can follow." Read two sentences of page 7, reread one with more feeling, then show the picture on that page and say what detail it clarifies. Children say what they heard.

#### 25 min. Hand work

*5 min.* Reread pages 2 to 7 of "The Boat Book Day" and the day 29 goal card. *10 min.* Each child fills the plan frame, [[s01-P30-6]]: a strategy from the menu, what it means for a younger reader, a boat detail with its page and a bus detail with its page from the stories and the class chart, a link word, a closing idea and one question for the reader. *10 min.* Partners check each other's plan against the model's parts, [[s01-P30-7]]. Nothing is drafted today.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs: each child 2 minutes on their plan and 2 minutes on the feedback question (8 minutes). Then the class lists good feedback questions (2 minutes). **Ask.** "Which part of the model tells a younger reader what the strategy is, and which tells how it works?" Expect: the definition, and the details.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 30 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X30]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on slates: [[s01-X30b]]. Unscored. Shown: names the strategy and explains it for a younger reader, and says definition. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does this sentence do for the reader?" Not yet seen: restates the strip, or adds a fact it does not give.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the word check at once and uses the plan frame aloud, writing as the child speaks. No printer: copy the model onto a card. A child may say the plan frame; the adult writes it and notes the support.

### At home

**Swap.** No copy: say the plan aloud.
**Skip.** The feedback question.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the model contract aloud and points to the definition.
  **Say.** "What does a strategy mean?" Expect: a plan that helps you do something.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you their plan, [[s01-P30-6]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X30]] and [[s01-X30b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the affix cards to four; keep the word check, the model, the plan frame and the exit. Longer: children write a second feedback question for their plan.

### Words and supports

Reading contract, strategy, definition, detail, link, conclusion, question. Frames: "My strategy is ___." "It means ___." Large print; the model read aloud again; a scribe for the plan frame, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 31 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X30, s01-X30b). Error K is a sentence with no name or explanation for the reader: name the strategy and explain it so a younger child can follow. The word check results decide the pattern retaught on day 31. Plans go to day 31 for the introduction.
