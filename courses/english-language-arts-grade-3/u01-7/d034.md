# Day 34: Draft a conclusion and rehearse a passage

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the feedback card, day 29, the recording pictures and sign-up sheet and the day 34 exit card. Copy the four-sentence strip from page 3 of "Reading Beside Grandmother" on the board. List six new children for recording, six for one-minute recordings and six for one-minute rehearsals with you. Set the device out of reach.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Hand-back.* Return the day 33 exit (s01-X33). If many joined a fact the card did not give (error I), write the card's facts on the board; children find and mark the one fact the link used that is not there. *7 min. Phrases.* Pairs draw a slash where a listener could pause in the strip, from "Ama finishes a page" to "She reads slowly", [[s01-P34-1]], then read it with and without the pauses. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* Review: -ness (day 31), -ly (day 32) and -able (day 33). Children make three words and say each meaning, [[s01-P34-2]].

*5 min. Grammar.* Review: link two facts with because (kindergarten u03-1). Write: "Ama checks the word. She wants it to make sense." Children join them, [[s01-P34-3]].

*7 min. Partner rereading.* Pairs reread the 91 words of "Reading Beside Grandmother" from "Grandmother takes a strip of bright paper" to "climb another page", twice each (about 45 seconds a read), and tick the feedback card. Listen to two pairs.

#### 40 min. Hand work

*5 min. Model.* Show how to choose a passage for recording: read the 67 words from "Ama and Grandmother sit side by side at the table" to "beside Ama" aloud with a purpose. Hold up the picture of a girl and an older woman at a table and ask which picture clarifies the detail with [[s01-K34-1]]. *15 min.* Write the conclusion, [[s01-P34-4]]: what the two details show about the strategy, ending on the strategy. *20 min.* Rehearse: 8 minutes in pairs, twice each with one comment, [[s01-P34-6]]; 4 minutes to choose a picture and label it, [[s01-P34-5]]; 3 minutes to read the conclusion aloud; 5 minutes to date and file.

*Recording (24 minutes of the 35 working minutes).* Six new children are recorded for 3 minutes each, and six rerecord one sentence in one minute each. The six children not yet heard rehearse for you for one minute each (6 minutes) and record in section 1.8; write a note after class. That is 30 minutes with 5 spare. No writing conferences today. Write one line after class for each recorded child naming one move heard.

#### 15 min. Talk

Pairs: each child reads the contract aloud so far, 2 minutes, and the partner names the strategy and the two details, 2 minutes (8 minutes). Then the class decides what a conclusion adds (5 minutes). Collect the folders (2 minutes). **Ask.** "What does your conclusion add that the details did not say?" Expect: what the two details show together.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 34 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X34]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on slates: [[s01-X34b]]. Unscored. Shown: closes on the contract topic and says what the ribbon and hook make possible; says to pause at the end marks so a listener hears each sentence. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What would the listener hear if the sentences ran together?" Not yet seen: closes on a new topic (error J), or says to read faster (error X).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the rehearsal and writes the conclusion as spoken. No device: rehearse aloud and write the note. No printer: copy the strip onto a card. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No passage: read any page twice.
**Skip.** The picture label.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the chosen passage aloud twice and says what changed the second time, [[s01-P34-6]].
  **Say.** "Where did you pause?" Expect: at the end marks.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child reads the conclusion aloud, [[s01-P34-4]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X34]] and [[s01-X34b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the grammar and one reading in the pair rehearsal; keep the conclusion and the exit. Longer: children rehearse a second passage.

### Words and supports

Conclusion, rehearse, passage, picture, clarify. Frames: "My conclusion shows ___." "The picture clarifies ___." Large print; a scribe for the conclusion, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 belongs to section 1.8 and checks the reading and recording rows without an opening. Day 36 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X34, s01-X34b). Error J is a closing on a new topic: end on the contract topic. Error X is reading faster: pause at each end mark. The contract drafts go to section 1.8; the chosen passage and picture stay with the child.
