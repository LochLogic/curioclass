# Look-for card (days 31 to 34)

A small card for each pair, to use as partners read each other's draft. Each look-for is a yes or a not yet. Say aloud: "Look for one thing at a time."

- My partner's topic sentence names a strategy.
- Related facts are together.
- A reason joins two facts.
- A link word joins related facts only.
- A younger reader could try it tomorrow.

Write one sentence to your partner: "Your ______ is clear because ______."
