# Section 1.7 opener: a reading contract (days 30 to 34)

## What the section does

Children read a model reading contract, then write their own: a topic sentence, two grouped explanations with facts, a closing sentence and a question. They choose a strategy for their own reading, practise a passage from one of the three stories, and record it on days 34 and 35. They learn the -ed and -ing endings with silent e and with doubled letters, and the day 30 word record is completed here.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 30 reads the model contract, plans, and hears six children read the word list. Day 31 drafts the topic and groups the reasons. Day 32 drafts the first explanation. Day 33 drafts the second explanation with linking words. Day 34 drafts the closing sentence and rehearses the passage for listeners.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A strategy is a plan that helps.** A contract names it, explains it and says when it will be used.
- **A reason needs two details.** The details alone are a list; the reason joins them.
- **A younger reader needs a meaning.** The first time a word may be new, say what it means.
- **A link joins related facts.** Also and so join facts that belong together.
- **A closing sentence says what the plan makes possible.**
- **A reading passage is read for sense.** A reader pauses at an end mark, rereads a stumble and checks a word with its letters and the sentence.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, H, I, J, K:** a fact not given, facts without a reason, a link joining unrelated ideas, a closing on a new topic, writing a younger reader cannot follow.
- **AC, P:** a word part read the wrong way, a word guessed.
- **O, X:** a suffix without its spelling change, reading that races past the end marks.

## Marking

Same scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading records are filed on the check sheet for days 35 and 36. Scores come only from the records, never from an exit.
