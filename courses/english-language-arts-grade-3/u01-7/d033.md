# Day 33: Draft the second proof paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the day 33 exit card. Make the possessive cards, day 33 (one owner, many owners). Chart the -ed and -ing rules and the -able examples. List six new children for recording and the six recorded on day 32 for one-minute rerecordings. Set the device out of reach.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Hand-back.* Return the day 32 exit (s01-X32) and the practice pages (s01-P32-1, s01-P32-2, s01-P32-3, s01-P32-4, s01-P32-5). If many wrote a step with no explanation (error K), say: "Say how the step helps, so a younger reader can follow." Children add one how-it-helps clause to a given sentence.

*7 min. Possessives.* Review: one owner takes 's, many owners take s' (days 2, 12, 17 and 20). Show the possessive cards. Children count the owners first, then place the apostrophe in five phrases, [[s01-P33-1]]. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* First 4 minutes: Review: you met -able in grade 2, day 103. Now write it yourself: -able means able to be. Write break and breakable, bend and bendable; children say "Breakable means able to be broken." Then they write [[s01-P33-2]]. Next 4 minutes: Review: drop a silent e before -ed or -ing (bake, baked, baking); double the last letter after a short vowel (tap, tapped, tapping) (grade 1 u03-8 day 109, grade 2 u02-4 day 48, grade 3 day 8). Children write [[s01-P33-3]].

*12 min. Conferences.* Independent reading from the logs; two 4-minute conferences while the rest read.

#### 40 min. Hand work

*8 min.* Word-work sentences, [[s01-P33-4]]. They stay in the folder; you mark them on day 36. For the library sentence, use the dictionary first (Review: dictionary check, day 20; guide words, day 27). *22 min.* Write the second proof paragraph, [[s01-P33-5]]: a bus detail from "Sam Meets the Book Bus", a fact that shows it, a link word, and why it supports the strategy. *5 min.* Read aloud to yourself. *5 min.* Date and file.

*Recording (24 minutes of the 40).* Six new children are recorded for 3 minutes each; the six recorded on day 32 rerecord one sentence in one minute each. No writing conferences today. Write one line after class for each recorded child naming one move heard.

#### 15 min. Talk

Pairs: each child reads their paragraph, 2 minutes, and the partner says what the fact shows, [[s01-P33-6]], 2 minutes (8 minutes). Then the class compares two sentences, one with a link and one without, with [[s01-K33-1]] (7 minutes). **Ask.** "What does the link word tell a reader about the two facts?" Expect: that they belong together, or why one follows.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 33 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X33]]. Unscored. Shown: explains how the facts help Tomas with also or so, joining only facts the card gives. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the wall card tell him?" Not yet seen: joins a fact the card lacks (error I), or adds running (error A).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the strip and writes the answers as spoken. No device: practise aloud and write the note. No dictionary: the adult reads the entry. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No draft: say the paragraph aloud.
**Skip.** The word-work sentences.

- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you the rule for smiled and the rule for sitting, [[s01-P33-3]].
  **Say.** "What happens to the e?" Expect: it drops before -ed or -ing.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the paragraph aloud, [[s01-P33-5]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X33]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the possessives to three phrases; keep the paragraph and the exit. Longer: children write a third possessive phrase of their own.

### Words and supports

Owner, apostrophe, breakable, link, fact. Frames: "The fact shows ___." "So ___." Large print; the rules on a card; a scribe, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X33). Error I is a link that joins a fact the card does not give: link only facts the card gives. The paragraphs go to day 34.
