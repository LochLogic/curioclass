# Day 31: Draft the introduction to my contract

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the look-for card, days 31 to 34, and the day 31 exit card. Read the day 30 word check and exit results; sort the children into three branches by the pattern missed most (prefix, suffix, irregular word). List the six children to be recorded and six for one-minute practice turns. Set the device out of reach.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Hand-back.* Return the day 30 exit (s01-X30, s01-X30b), the word check and the practice pages (s01-P30-1, s01-P30-2, s01-P30-3, s01-P30-4, s01-P30-5, s01-P30-6, s01-P30-7). If many wrote a sentence with no name or no explanation (error K), show three sentences about a boy who checks a bus card each night; children tick the one that names the strategy and explains it so a younger child can follow. Do not use the exit's strip.

*7 min. Reteach by branch.* Three minutes on the pattern missed most, in three groups. Prefix group: untie, retell, mislead, prepay, disagree, each said with its meaning. Suffix group: careless, thankful, helpless, careful, thankless. Irregular group: sure, build, busy, great, guess. Then a five-word dictation and one sentence from the group's list on slates. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* New here: -ness means the state of being. Write kind and kindness, sad and sadness; say each. Children read each pair and say: "Kindness means the state of being kind." Then they write [[s01-P31-1]]. Only the plain base word takes -ness here; the spelling change comes later.

*12 min. Conferences.* Independent reading from the logs; two 4-minute conferences while the rest read.

#### 40 min. Hand work

*5 min.* Reread the plan frame and the page of the boat detail. *25 min.* Write the introduction, [[s01-P31-2]]. Frame: "My strategy is ___. It means ___. For example, ___." Group the boat reason together and the bus reason together. *5 min.* Reread aloud to yourself. *5 min.* Date the draft and file it.

*Recording (24 minutes of the 40).* The device stays with you. Six children are recorded for 3 minutes each, one after the other: each states a purpose, reads 60 to 80 words of a story passage of their choice, rereads one sentence, and shows one picture from the story. Six other children take a one-minute practice turn with a rerecording sentence (6 minutes). Everyone else writes. No writing conferences today. After class write one line for each recorded child naming one move heard, such as "paused at each end mark". The line goes on the record sheet; no scoring.

#### 15 min. Talk

Pairs: each child reads their introduction aloud, 2 minutes, and the partner ticks the look-for card, [[s01-P31-3]], 2 minutes (8 minutes). Then the class lists what a younger reader needs (7 minutes). **Ask.** "What does a younger reader need from the first lines?" Expect: the strategy named and what it means.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 31 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X31]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on slates: [[s01-X31b]]. Unscored. Shown: gives a reason both details support, and a topic sentence naming checking questions as the strategy. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What do both details help him do?" Not yet seen: lists the details with no reason (error H), or adds a teacher the card does not give (error A).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the look-for card with the child and writes the answers as spoken. No device: practice aloud and write the note. No printer: write the frame on a card. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No draft: say the introduction aloud.
**Skip.** The look-for card.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the introduction aloud, [[s01-P31-2]].
  **Say.** "Is the strategy clear to you?" Expect: yes, or one thing missing.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child says what darkness means, [[s01-P31-1]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X31]] and [[s01-X31b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the reteach to the largest branch; keep the words, the draft and the exit. Longer: children add a question for the reader at the end of the introduction.

### Words and supports

Strategy, introduction, reason, group, kindness. Frames: "My strategy is ___." "It means ___." Large print; a scribe for the draft, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X31, s01-X31b). Error H is facts listed with no reason: give a reason that both details support. The six recorded today read their notes. Drafts go to day 32.
