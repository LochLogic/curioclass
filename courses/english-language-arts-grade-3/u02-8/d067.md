# Day 67: Complete notes on body, habitat and survival from two sources

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a note card for each child. Tally the day 66 strips and find the commonest error letter. Set out the day 66 strips, the day 65 sheets with their notes and the heard-six column. List the children whose day 66 spoken exit was not heard.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back the day 66 strips, s02-X66, s02-X66bv2 and s02-X66c. Repair error W (a question that asks for everything): at the board, narrow a new broad question to one animal and one detail, [[s02-P67-1]]. Each child checks their own narrowed question from day 66 the same way. *7 min.* Week 14. Review: you learned -tion in grade 2 (nation, section, collection). Today the base changes first. Read explanation, information, introduction and relation, name each base and say what changed, [[s02-P67-2]]. Inform takes -ation with nothing lost; relate loses its e before -ion. Then write information and relation, [[s02-P67-3]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Retrieval on slates. Review: a plural that changes (day 59), an abstract noun, an idea you cannot touch (day 57), and an irregular past verb (day 43). Write the plural of leaf and tooth, [[s02-P67-4]]; say the idea noun, [[s02-P67-5]]; write the past of swim, [[s02-P67-6]]. *15 min.* Partner rereading of page 17, Putting It Together (about 250 words), with the feedback card and no speed, [[s02-P67-7]]. Review: you used the feedback card in unit 1. The teacher listens to three pairs for 3 minutes each and records only; the other pairs give feedback with the card.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Retrieve the three note boxes on slates, [[s02-P67-8]]. *3 min.* Reread page 12 aloud, from "Notice how the frog's home and body work together." Model one note with its source, [[s02-K67-1]]: a brief phrase, then the book and page. **Ask.** "Where can each note be checked?" *6 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: each child picks two of their notes and finds the source and page that show each one, [[s02-P67-9]]. Expect: the book, page 12, or the excerpt under Description or Habitat. Watch for error A: a note no page gives. *3 min.* Class share. Do not define permanent, vulnerable or undisturbed in the excerpt; a note may copy the words it needs.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*4 min.* Review: you learned related, link and evidence in kindergarten, and relevant on day 57. Model one sentence that uses all three: "The color is related to hiding; the evidence is on page 11." *6 min.* Each child writes one sentence about the glass frog with its source in brackets, [[s02-P67-10]].

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs, 2 minutes a child, [[s02-P67-11]]: say which note you checked and where you found it; the partner says whether the page really shows it. While they talk, call about five children whose day 66 exit was not heard, 45 seconds each, and ask s02-X66 or s02-X66bv2. *6 min.* Three volunteers, 2 minutes each, say a note and its page. Anyone may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*8 min.* Each child revises the day 65 sheet with the one change its written note asks for, [[s02-P67-12]]. This is the revision opportunity for the day 65 sheet; it goes to the teacher for scoring on day 68. *12 min.* Complete the note card, [[s02-P67-13]]: body, habitat and survival each hold at least two notes under eight words, each with its source label, one from the book and one from the excerpt where the source gives it. Watch for error X: a home fact under body.

**Shared shape.** The note card shares the shape of E67 (a brief note with its source). E67 uses a new case, and nothing practised or shown today uses it.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X67]], [[s02-X67b]] and [[s02-X67c]]. Shown: each note in its own box with the right source. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which source says it?" Not yet seen: a home fact under body or a body fact under survival (error X), or a source that does not give the fact (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult takes the partner role in the rereading and the talk and points to the page for each note. No printer: draw the three boxes on a folded page and write the student page items on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal book.
**Skip.** The slate work and the partner reading.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads page 12 aloud.
  **Say.** "Where can this note be checked?" Expect: the book, and the page that shows it.
- **20 min. Notes.** Your child fills the note card, [[s02-P67-13]]: one body note, one home note and one survival note, each with its source.
  **Say.** "Which box does this note go in?" Expect: body for a part, habitat for a place, survival for how it stays safe.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X67]], [[s02-X67b]] and [[s02-X67c]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision, the note card and the exit; record the sentence and the talk as not reached. Longer: write a note that both sources give, with both labels.

### Words and supports

Note, source, evidence, related, link, relevant, body, habitat, survival, information, relation. Frame: "My note says ___. It is in ___, page ___." Large print; a scribe for the notes; the page read aloud again; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 returns the day 67 strip, [[s02-X67]], [[s02-X67b]] and [[s02-X67c]], and repairs error X first: sort the note by what the fact tells. It teaches the three-box plan for a page and begins supported typing. The revised day 65 sheet is scored then. The note card stays in the folder. The board and slate work, [[s02-P67-1]] to [[s02-P67-6]], [[s02-P67-8]] and [[s02-K67-1]], the spoken work, [[s02-P67-7]], [[s02-P67-9]] and [[s02-P67-11]], and the sheets, [[s02-P67-10]], [[s02-P67-12]] and [[s02-P67-13]], stay in the folder.
