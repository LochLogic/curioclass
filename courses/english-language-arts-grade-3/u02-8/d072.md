# Day 72: Use feedback and write a conclusion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a feedback slip for each child. Write four notes on each slip: the day 71 sheet, the day 70 discussion, the day 69 typed line and the essay folder. Tally the day 71 strips and find the commonest letter. Write the five names for the final clips.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back the day 71 strips, s02-X71 and s02-X71b. Repair error D (a word meaning taken from nowhere): underline the clue beside the word, then restate the meaning, [[s02-P72-12]]. *7 min.* Week 14. Retrieve the meanings of the common prefixes and suffixes on cards, [[s02-P72-1]]; then read longer words from the essay and say what each means, [[s02-P72-2]]. Review: you met un-, re- and -ful in grade 2.

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Grammar retrieval on slates: a more exact word for a general one, [[s02-P72-3]]. *15 min.* Pairs reread page 11 with the feedback card, [[s02-P72-4]]; no teacher listens today. Five children make their final clips of the poem with its display, 3 minutes each, [[s02-P72-5]].

#### 10 min. Feedback and return and repair

*3 min.* Hand back day 71's assessment, s02-A67 to s02-A76, and one slip a child with a note on each of four things. *4 min.* Repair the commonest letter from the day 71 strips at the board. *3 min.* Children go to groups by the letter on their slip.

#### 10 min. Written repair

Each child repairs the middle paragraph with the one note that names it, [[s02-P72-6]]. The day 71 sheet and the typing are revised on day 73. Watch for error AC: the paragraph copied again with no change.

#### 10 min. Reread

*4 min.* **Ask.** "What has the explanation established?" Pairs reread the essay so far and the sources and point to the words that show the body and the home work together, [[s02-P72-7]]. Expect: the green color blends into the leaves of its home. *6 min.* Each child writes the one question the conclusion must answer, [[s02-P72-8]].

#### 5 min. Word and sentence work

*2 min.* Model closing sentences, [[s02-K72-1]], and changing a general word, [[s02-K72-2]]. Review: you used a closing sentence on day 68 and the precise word on day 45. *3 min.* Each child changes one word in the conclusion plan, [[s02-P72-9]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs: read your planned conclusion aloud, [[s02-P72-10]]; the partner says what it closes.

#### 15 min. Conclusion drafting

Each child drafts the conclusion paragraph and links it to the topic sentence and its two groups, [[s02-P72-11]]. The essay then has an introduction, a middle paragraph and a conclusion in draft. Watch for error Q: a new unrelated topic in the close.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X72]] and [[s02-X72b]]. Shown: a last sentence that returns to the frog and says how it answers the feedback. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the note ask?" Not yet seen: a sentence that stays on a new topic (error Q) or a copy with no change (error AC). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the reading partner. No device: read the poem to the teacher and record a paper stand-in. No printer: write the four notes on a page.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal book.
**Skip.** The partner reading and the clips.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the essay so far aloud.
  **Say.** "What did the conclusion tell you again?" Expect: how the frog's body and home work together.
- **20 min. Write.** Your child repairs the paragraph, [[s02-P72-6]], and drafts the conclusion, [[s02-P72-11]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X72]] and [[s02-X72b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the conclusion and the exit. Longer: write a second closing sentence that uses a more exact word.

### Words and supports

Feedback, repair, conclusion, closes, prefix, suffix, exact, unusual, quietly, movement. Frame: "So the ___ and the ___ work together." Large print; a scribe for the conclusion; the page read aloud again; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 73 returns the day 72 strip, [[s02-X72]] and [[s02-X72b]], and repairs error Q first: choose the sentence that closes the frog explanation. The draft essay and the conclusion stay in the folder, and the teacher holds the feedback slips. The board and slate work, [[s02-P72-3]], [[s02-P72-9]], [[s02-P72-12]], [[s02-K72-1]] and [[s02-K72-2]], the spoken work, [[s02-P72-1]], [[s02-P72-2]], [[s02-P72-4]], [[s02-P72-5]], [[s02-P72-7]] and [[s02-P72-10]], and the sheets, [[s02-P72-6]], [[s02-P72-8]] and [[s02-P72-11]], stay in the folder.
