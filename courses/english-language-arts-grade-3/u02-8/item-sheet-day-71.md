# Item sheet, day 71

Print one sheet for each child, ruled. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. Silent.

## Panel D

> The western water-holding frog sheds layers of skin in its burrow, a hole in the ground. These layers form a cocoon, a covering around the frog. Inside it the frog can remain inactive for months until rain comes.
>
> After summer rains it breeds in temporary water. Females lay large groups of eggs there.
