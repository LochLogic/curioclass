# Day 71: Check a body paragraph written alone

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child. Tally the day 70 strips and find the commonest error letter. Set out the check sheet and the item sheet. Write the names for the recordings.

### The day

#### 35 min. 71A

Silent. Children read Panel D on the printed sheet and answer alone: *20 min.* the paragraph, [[s02-A67]], [[s02-A68]], [[s02-A69]], [[s02-A70]] and [[s02-A119]]; *8 min.* the three panel questions, [[s02-A74]], [[s02-A75]] and [[s02-A76]]; *7 min.* the last three, [[s02-A71]], [[s02-A72]] and [[s02-A73]]. Nothing is read aloud; if the panel is read to a child, record it as help. The teacher answers nothing about content.

#### 15 min. Recording rotation

Five children make their final recordings of the poem with its display, 3 minutes each, at the one device, [[s02-P71-3]]. The other twenty-five read silently from their logs and may reread their own sheet.

#### 5 min. Silent verification

Children reread their own sheet and add nothing the teacher has said. Collect the sheets.

#### 30 min. Skills

*3 min.* Hand back the day 70 strips, s02-X70, s02-X70b and s02-X70c. Repair error A (a claim no line gives): read a claim, point to the line that says it, or say none does, [[s02-P71-5]]. *7 min.* Week 14, retrieval only, no new pattern: write information and conclusion as your teacher says each, [[s02-P71-1]]. *15 min.* Five other children make practice clips of the poem with its display, 3 minutes each, [[s02-P71-2]]; the rest read from their logs. *5 min.* Each child adds a dated line to the log, [[s02-P71-4]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X71]] and [[s02-X71b]]. Shown: two sentences that use while and because, and the meaning of mottled with its clue words. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which words sit beside it?" Not yet seen: because joining clauses that do not explain each other (error AB), or a meaning with no clue (error D). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult sits apart and answers nothing about content. No device: read the poem to the teacher and record a paper stand-in. No printer: copy Panel D on the board and write the items beside it.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal book.
**Skip.** The recordings and the silent check.

- **20 min. Assessment.** Your child reads Panel D and answers [[s02-A71]], [[s02-A72]] and [[s02-A74]], alone.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from the log book.
  **Say.** "What did you wonder?" Expect: any honest wondering about the book.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X71]] and [[s02-X71b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sheet, the exit and the repair; record the recordings as not reached. Longer: reread the sheet and mark one place to improve; do not change it.

### Words and supports

Panel, paragraph, define, cocoon, burrow, because, while, information, conclusion. No frames on the check; a scribe or the panel read aloud is recorded as help.

### Tomorrow

Day 72 returns the day 71 strip, [[s02-X71]] and [[s02-X71b]], and repairs error D first: underline the clue, then restate the meaning. It hands back a note on four things and each child repairs the middle paragraph with the note that names it. The sheets stay with the teacher. The board and slate work, [[s02-P71-1]], the spoken work, [[s02-P71-2]], [[s02-P71-3]] and [[s02-P71-5]], and the log line, [[s02-P71-4]], stay in the folder.
