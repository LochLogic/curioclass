# Section 2.8 opener: planning and writing the page (days 67 to 72)

## What the section does

The class sorts notes from two sources, plans a page in three boxes, types one line at the one device, tests a claim against the sources, and closes an explanation after feedback.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes: reading and research, writing, a skills block and a five-minute exit. The exits are one ask each and unscored; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- Notes sort into groups before they are written.
- A claim needs a source that says it.
- A closing returns to the topic.

## The errors, in plain words

- **X:** A note is in the wrong category. Repair: sort the note by what the fact tells. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **AA:** Related facts are left ungrouped. Repair: sort the notes into body and home piles. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **AD:** Watching another person type counts as keyboarding. Repair: say the keys you pressed, line by line. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **A:** A fact is asserted without source support. Repair: point to the line in the source before saying it. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **D:** A meaning ignores its sentence clue. Repair: find the clue words in the same sentence. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **Q:** An ending introduces an unrelated topic. Repair: keep the closing on the topic. 3 minutes, next day's opening.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
