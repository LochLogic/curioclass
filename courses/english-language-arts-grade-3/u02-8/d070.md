# Day 70: Discuss which details explain survival and draft the middle paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child and a discussion card for each pair. Make the discussion record: a row for each child with five boxes (marked detail, turn, linked question, answer, what the talk clarified). Write the six groups of five and the five names for the poem clips. Open the book to page 12 and the excerpt at each table.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back the day 69 typed line, s02-X69, on the shared page; the page stays on the device. Repair error AD (a line watched, not typed): the child says the keys they pressed to type their line, word by word, [[s02-P70-10]]. *7 min.* Week 14. Read a mixed list and name each base, [[s02-P70-2]]; then write migration and brightness, [[s02-P70-1]]. Migrate loses its e before -ation.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Five children make their final recordings of the poem with its display, 3 minutes each, at the one device, [[s02-P70-3]]; each had a practice clip on day 68, so the teacher gives one note on it first. The rest read from their logs. The last 5 minutes: each child writes a dated line, [[s02-P70-4]].

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*5 min.* Prepare source details. Each child marks two details in the book and the excerpt that explain how the glass frog lives, [[s02-A61]]. The teacher records the first box. *4 min.* Reread the excerpt aloud (211 words, 2 minutes) and page 12 (about 180 words, 2 minutes). Do not define permanent, vulnerable or undisturbed. **Ask.** "Which claim has support in both sources?" *3 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: find the claim and the two places that say it, [[s02-P70-6]]. Expect: glass frogs live near running water, from page 11 or 12 of the book and from the excerpt under Habitat. *3 min.* Class share, each pair pointing to both places.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*4 min.* Model two claims. One has support in both sources, [[s02-K70-1]]; one has support in only one, [[s02-K70-2]]. Then model joining two details within one group with also, another and but. Review: you used also and and in unit 1 and on day 59; another is a new joining word today. *6 min.* Each child writes one linked sentence from their marked details, [[s02-P70-7]]. Watch for error AB: a joining word between details that do not belong together.

#### 30 min. Discussion and the middle paragraph

Formal discussion is new today: a prepared talk where each child waits for a turn, links to a peer and explains after listening. Six groups of five, each with 5 minutes at the teacher's table, 1 minute a child. A child states a marked detail and where it is; the next child asks a linked question ("You said ___; which words show it?"); the first child answers with a detail. Pairs keep the discussion card on the table for the frames; each frame is faded after the first group. Record each child's five boxes: [[s02-A61]], [[s02-A62]], [[s02-A63]] or [[s02-A65]], [[s02-A66]] and [[s02-A64]]. Back at their seat, each child writes on a slip what the talk made clearer, [[s02-P70-8]]; the slip is the record for the fifth box and takes a minute of the paragraph time. Six groups of 5 minutes use all 30.

The other twenty-five children, quietly and with the sources open, plan and draft the middle paragraph: the glass frog's body and home, with the details that explain how it lives, [[s02-P70-9]]. They use the 10 minutes of the talk slot and the 20 minutes of the writing slot together. Watch for error AA: related facts left apart.

**Shared shape.** The model, worked example and practice here share the shape of E70 (say whether the sources support this claim) and of its check item; the exit and the check item use new cases, so none of them is shown or practiced on the same case.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X70]], [[s02-X70b]] and [[s02-X70c]]. Shown: no, with the reason that the excerpt is about glass frogs; a detail that shows it; one sentence that joins both details with a related joining word. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the excerpt cover?" Not yet seen: accepting a claim the source does not make (error A), a bare answer with no detail (error AO), a joining word between unrelated details (error AB). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the discussion partner, states one marked detail and waits for the child's linked question. No device: the final recording is read to the teacher and noted; record it as a paper stand-in. No printer: write the student page items on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal book.
**Skip.** The recording and the group talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child rereads page 12.
  **Say.** "Which detail explains how the frog lives?" Expect: a detail about the leaf, the stream or its green color.
- **20 min. Write.** Your child marks two details, [[s02-A61]], and writes the middle paragraph, [[s02-P70-9]].
  **Say.** "Which words show it?" Expect: the child points to the page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X70]], [[s02-X70b]] and [[s02-X70c]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the marking, the discussion turn and the exit; the paragraph is finished on day 72. Longer: add a detail from the second source to the paragraph.

### Words and supports

Claim, support, source, detail, link, also, another, but, discussion, migration, brightness. Frames on the discussion card: "I want to add that ___." "I have a question about what you said: ___." "After listening to you, I understand ___." Large print; a scribe for the slip; the text read aloud again; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 71 returns the day 70 strip, [[s02-X70]], [[s02-X70b]] and [[s02-X70c]], and repairs error A first: point to the line in the source before saying it. Then it is the check: each child writes a body paragraph alone from a new panel, silently, and answers questions about the panel. The middle paragraph draft and the slips stay in the folder; the teacher holds the discussion record. The board and slate work, [[s02-P70-1]], [[s02-P70-10]], [[s02-K70-1]] and [[s02-K70-2]], the spoken work, [[s02-P70-2]], [[s02-P70-3]] and [[s02-P70-6]], and the sheets, [[s02-P70-4]], [[s02-P70-7]], [[s02-P70-8]] and [[s02-P70-9]], stay in the folder.
