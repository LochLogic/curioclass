Teacher note. Printed beside a marking key; the teacher reads it and marks seven parts on day 30: 1 topic (Taking small steps is a strategy for a hard task); 2 definition (A strategy is a plan...; it means doing one small piece at a time); 3 detail (Lia); 4 link (Also); 5 detail (Sam); 6 conclusion (Both children got there...; I will read one page, then the next); 7 question (Could a younger reader follow my explanation?). Before use, confirm the two details against the stories (the boards in g3-u1-01, the stone walls in g3-u1-02).

## Text

Taking Small Steps. A strategy is a plan that helps you do something. Taking small steps is a strategy for a hard task. It means doing one small piece at a time. Lia walks the long boards one slow step at a time, and she reaches the boat. Also, Sam counts the stone walls one by one, and he reaches the book bus. Both children got there because they did not try to do everything at once. When a book looks hard, I will read one page, then the next. Could a younger reader follow my explanation?
