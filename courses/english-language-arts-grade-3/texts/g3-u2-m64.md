## Part 1. A Glass Frog on a Leaf (the model to check)

The grainy Cochran frog is a glass frog that lives near streams. During the day it rests flat on a leaf. Its green color blends into the leaves around it, so it is hard to see. Because it is hard to see, a hungry animal is less likely to find it. The frog's body and its home work together.

*Link to check on page 11: "Its green color blends into the leaves around it. This makes it hard to see." The teacher points to these two sentences and says the link is supported. The next link, hard to see so less likely to be found, is on the same page.*

## Part 2. A Frog by the Stream (the draft to revise and edit)

The grainy Cochran frog live near a stream. It is green, and it is the most small frog on the leaf. Many leafs hang over the water. My dog likes to swim in the pond.

*Marked for the teacher to model: a weak link (add a because or so that joins the green color to being hard to see), an off-topic ending (the dog sentence; replace it with one about the frog's home), one verb (live, lives), one comparison (the most small, the smallest, or smaller) and one plural (leafs, leaves).*
