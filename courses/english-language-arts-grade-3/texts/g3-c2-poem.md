# Lamp Light (two stanzas)

Lamp Light

The lamp is small. The house is still.
I tuck my toes beneath the quilt.
One book is open on my knees,
and the first page waits for me.

Soon the words are running, running,
over roofs and across the sky.
The small lamp glows, my eyes are wide,
and the night is full of story.
