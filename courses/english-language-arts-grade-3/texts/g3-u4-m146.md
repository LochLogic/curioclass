# Cleaning River Water in Four Steps

River water holds dirt, so workers clean it in four steps. First, they add a chemical that makes the dirt stick together. Next, they stir the water slowly, and the clumps grow. Then the water rests in a tank. Because the clumps are [Pause before heavy.] heavy, they sink. Last, the water passes through sand and gravel, and the filters catch what is left. Four steps give clearer water for homes.
