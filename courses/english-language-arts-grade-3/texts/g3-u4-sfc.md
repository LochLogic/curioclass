# The School Card and the Farm Card

## The school card

A school needs water for drinking and for its kitchen. Children fill their bottles each day. The cooks rinse pots with water too.

## The farm card

A farm needs water for its fields and for drinking. Farmers fill the troughs each morning. The barn is washed with water too.
