Up the Hill

Mark and his dad hiked up a steep trail to the top of a hill. The wind blew hard, so they zipped their coats. Near the top, they spotted a small bird gliding over the trees. It circled twice and then dropped out of sight. Mark grinned. "Let's come back next weekend and bring a snack," he told his dad.
