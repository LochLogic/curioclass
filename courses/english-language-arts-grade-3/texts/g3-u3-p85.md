# Ben at the Table

Ada hid the plan because she feared its arrow was wrong. Ben waited by the empty table. Ada sighed; her plan had fallen flat. "My heart sank," Ada said. Ben asked to see the arrow before she erased it. The narrator says, "Ben was wasting time." Ada finally unfolded the page.
