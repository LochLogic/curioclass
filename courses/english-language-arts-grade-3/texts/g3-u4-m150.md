# To the Water Office

Room 6 Water Group
40 Birch Lane
Lake Bend, MT 59000

Dear Water Office,

Our class studied how a town cleans its water. We read about four steps that make muddy water clear. We learned that clean water protects health. Safety matters to every family. Teamwork matters too, because many workers share each job. Men and women check each step. Our two classes would like to learn more about your work. Please tell us how your workers check the filters.

Thank you,
Room 6 Water Group
