Stage directions are in square brackets; the class reads them silently or as the director's job and never speaks them as dialogue (days 108, 116 to 119). The Narrator speaks a few lines that give opinions, as in the book, so readers can separate the Narrator's view from a character's and their own. Scene 2 acts on the question Ben asks in scene 1, which is what the second scene adds (day 107). Keep the practice passages for days 117 and 118 apart from this play; they are new texts.

# Checking the Plan

**Characters:** Narrator, Ada, Ben, Priya, Mateo.

**Setting:** Room 6, a classroom, and the picnic table in the garden behind it. Both scenes take place at school.

## Scene 1: The Empty Table

[The picnic table in the garden at lunchtime. Ben sits with his blue folder open in front of him. Priya and Mateo stand nearby. Ada waters a bean plant with her back to them.]

**Narrator:** Ada had a plan. Nobody could see it. That was the problem.

**Ben:** Ada, where is the plan?

[Ada does not answer. She goes on watering the same bean plant.]

**Ben:** Ada?

**Ada:** I am busy.

**Priya:** Ms. Alvarez wants to see it today.

**Ada:** [She sighs and sets down the watering can.] I know. My plan has fallen flat. I looked at it last night and my heart sank.

**Mateo:** Your heart sank? [He looks worried.] Are you all right?

**Priya:** It is a way of speaking, Mateo. It means she felt bad.

**Ada:** I think the last arrow is wrong. I am going to erase it and start again, by myself.

[Ben pauses before he answers. He holds up the folder.]

**Ben:** Could I see the arrow first?

**Ada:** Why? It is wrong. If I erase it, it will not matter.

**Ben:** It might matter. If we do not look at what is wrong, how will we know what to change?

**Narrator:** This, the Narrator thinks, was wasting time. The plan was due that afternoon. A person in a hurry would simply erase.

[Ben says nothing more. He sits and waits. Priya sits beside him. After a moment, Mateo sits on the grass.]

**Ada:** [Speaking softly.] Nobody has ever waited for me before.

**Ben:** I do not mind waiting.

[Ada kneels, opens her backpack and takes out a folded paper. She holds it for a moment against her chest. Then she unfolds it and sets it on the table.]

**Ada:** There. Now you can look.

**Ben:** [He leans over the page.] Thank you.

## Scene 2: Before Everyone Comes

[Room 6, early the next week, before the first bell. Sun comes through the windows. A cupboard stands against one wall. Ada kneels beside a large drawing spread on the floor. Ben enters with the folder.]

**Narrator:** Some questions take a few days to answer. This was one of them.

**Ben:** Good morning, Ada.

**Ada:** Good morning. I kept it hidden because I wanted it right.

**Ben:** [He kneels and traces the last arrow with a finger. It leads straight to the cupboard.] This arrow points at a cupboard. Nobody can walk into a cupboard.

**Ada:** [A pause. She looks at the cupboard and then at the page.] Then it is wrong. I was right to worry.

**Ben:** Maybe. Or maybe you were right to ask for help. May we try it before everyone comes?

[Ada hands him the pencil. They stand and walk from the door along the wall, following the arrows with their eyes.]

**Ada:** First arrow, along the wall.

**Ben:** Second arrow, past the shelf.

**Ada:** Third arrow, past the cupboard. [She stops. The cupboard door is closed in front of her.] And here the arrow sends us into the door.

[She turns the arrow on the drawing so that it bends toward the window corner.]

**Ada:** A question can find what a careful drawing misses.

[Priya and Mateo come in. They see the drawing and the two friends.]

**Priya:** What are you doing?

**Ada:** Checking. Will you walk it with us?

**Mateo:** [He groans, then grins.] Checking again? Fine. Which way?

**Narrator:** The Narrator thinks that this is tiresome. Walking a route over and over is hard on the feet.

**Ada:** [She smiles.] I think it is useful.

[The four of them walk the route together. At the last arrow they turn toward the window corner and stop. Sunlight falls on the green beanbag. Ada holds up the drawing so everyone can see it.]

**Ben:** It works.

**Ada:** It works because we checked it together.

[The bell rings. The curtain falls.]
