## Why the Poison Dart Frog Is So Colorful

Long, long ago, deep in South America, a boy named Ranna lived by himself in a secret, magical cave. From outside, the cave looked dull, rocky and gray. Inside, its walls glittered with rubies, diamonds and sapphires.

Ranna shared the cave with three pet frogs named Drab, Ash and Grey. Their skin was a dull gray-green. Drab was tiny, Ash was tinier, and little Grey was the tiniest of all.

One day Ranna was playing a hunting game with his frog friends. Without meaning to, he knocked over a torch that lit the cave. It fell onto the dry grass of his bed, and the grass caught fire!

"Help!" Ranna cried. "Please, someone help me!" He ran to the cave's entrance, but flames filled it. He bent to pick up Drab, Ash and Grey. His small friends hopped away from him. He chased them, and they hopped faster, deeper and deeper into the cave.

Then Ranna noticed something odd. "It is getting cooler," he thought. Ahead of him he saw a giant crack in the cave wall. He scooped up the three frogs and wriggled through the crack. Outside, he took a deep breath of fresh air. Then he looked at his friends to make sure they were safe.

Ranna gasped. Drab, Ash and Grey were safe, but they were not dull anymore! Drab was ruby red with flecks of gold. Ash was sapphire blue with streaks of black. Grey shimmered like a rainbow. The jewels in the cave walls had melted and coated the frogs in brilliant colors.

"No one will ever forget how brave you were," said Ranna.

And to this day, the children and grandchildren of those three little frogs have skin the color of gems.
