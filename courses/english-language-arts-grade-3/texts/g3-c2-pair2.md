# The Bus Stop Shelf (Pip, chapter 2)

Chapter 2: The Bus Stop Shelf

A week later, Pip built a second shelf at the bus stop. Gran helped her nail the boards.

That afternoon the wind blew hard. Books flew across the sidewalk. Pip's face fell.

"Try again," said Gran.

Pip found a strap and tied the books down. A bus driver stopped and read the sign. He took a book with a blue cover and smiled. Pip stood tall.
