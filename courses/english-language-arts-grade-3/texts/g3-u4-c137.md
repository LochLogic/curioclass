# Channel Checks, with a Story Strip

## Channel checks

A blocked channel stopped rain from reaching a garden tank. Workers unblocked it before the next rain.

The next rain reached the tank because its path was clear. Clearing a path restores movement. Inspecting it afterward checks whether movement continues.

**Diagram:** three boxes in a row, left to right: roof, channel, tank. An arrow joins each box to the next. Leaves are crossed out at the channel.

**Glossary:** *channel*, 1. a path carrying water. 2. a station that sends out television shows. *blocked*, stopped from passing.
