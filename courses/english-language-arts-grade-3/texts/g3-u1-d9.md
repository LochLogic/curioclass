## Text

Boat. Lia returned her book to the boat. She asked the librarian for another story about the river. Her friend had forgotten the return day, so Lia wrote the next date on a card for her.

Bus. Sam put his finished book in his bag before breakfast. He walked with his father to the book bus. They reached its stop before it left. Sam chose a new book and showed his father its cover.
