# Day 114 Panels

Panel P114v (book 2 companion)

Ben wanted the reading circle moved to the shade. Ada wanted to check every seat first. The narrator calls her delay silly. Ben calls it fair.

Panel P114 (article companion, slot 04)

A writer may keep familiar characters but change their problem. I think every series should return to the same setting. That lets readers notice smaller changes. Another reader might prefer a new place.

Panel P114c (Story C)

In the school library Ada wants every shelf labeled by color. Ben says some readers cannot tell the colors apart. They add shapes to the labels, and every reader finds a shelf.

Panel P114d (Story D)

On the playground in spring Ben wants one team game for everyone. Ada asks who wants a quieter choice. They set up a game and a drawing table, and every child joins something.
