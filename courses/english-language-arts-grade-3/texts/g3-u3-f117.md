# Where to Read

The reading club had an invitation for Friday. Everyone would meet on the garden bench at noon. Then a sudden rain came and soaked the wood. Ada looked at the wet bench and sighed. "We need another place," she said. Ben pointed across the yard. "The covered doorway is dry, and it has room for all of us." Ada smiled. They carried the books there together and sat down in a circle. When the rain stopped, the club kept reading.
