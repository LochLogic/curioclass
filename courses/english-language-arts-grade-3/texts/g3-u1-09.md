## Text

**Nia's Library Notice**

Nia made a reading copy of an old library notice. Some letters were faint, so light that she could barely see them. Her first copy was a foggy window. She enlarged the words to make them readable. The printer put the bigger words on two sheets. She left a margin along each edge so no words were cut off. Now readers could see every line.

**Glossary**

margin: the empty strip along a page's edge.
