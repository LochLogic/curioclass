Teacher note. Six strips, all true inside the practice. Three groups hide in them: what the box is (1, 2), how people use it (3, 4) and why it works (5, 6). Not a frog or a library-boat case. The child chooses, groups and links; no strip is a model sentence.

# The Book-Swap Box

1. A book-swap box is a small box with a door and shelves.
2. It stands outside, near a bus stop or a park.
3. A person takes a book and may leave a book.
4. Nobody needs a card or pays a fee.
5. The door keeps rain off the books.
6. A sign tells people how to use the box.
