# The Story Walk (article A)

The Story Walk

What It Is
A Story Walk puts a picture book on a path. Each page stands on a post. Families walk from post to post and read the story as they go.

Why Families Like It
Walking gets children moving. It also gets grown-ups reading with them. The path is free and open all day. The book stays up for a month. A Story Walk is the best way to share a book outdoors.
