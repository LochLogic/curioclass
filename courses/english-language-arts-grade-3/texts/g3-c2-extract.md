# The Club Table (two scenes)

Scene 1: The school library, morning

Dee set up a sign for a book club. Rob read it and frowned.

"Nobody wants to read at lunch," he said.

Dee only smiled. "I do," she said.

Scene 2: The same table, noon

Three children sat down with books. Rob stood far off and watched. He tapped his foot.

Then he slid a chair closer. "Maybe one page," he said.

Rob did not want anyone to see he was curious.
