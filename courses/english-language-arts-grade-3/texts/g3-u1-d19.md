## Text

In April 1905, a library in Hagerstown, Maryland, sent a book wagon into the countryside. Two horses, Dandy and Black Beauty, pulled it. Joshua Thomas, the library's janitor, drove. Many families lived in isolated places, far from other homes and far from the library. The wagon rolled along dusty country roads to reach them. At each stop, Mr. Thomas unpacked the books, and families chose some to borrow. In its first six months, the wagon gave out over 1,000 books. The library wanted books to travel to readers in the county, not wait in town.

Glossary: janitor: a person who cleans and takes care of a building; county: a part of a state with its own local government.
