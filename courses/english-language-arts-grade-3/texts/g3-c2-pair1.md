# A Shelf for Neighbors (Pip, chapter 1)

Chapter 1: A Shelf for Neighbors

Pip set a shelf on Gran's porch. She filled it with six books and a sign. The sign said, "Take one. Bring it back."

Pip sat on the step and waited. Morning passed. Noon passed. Nobody came, and her smile sank.

"Be patient," said Gran. "A shelf is like a garden."

At dusk a boy stopped. He picked a book with a red cover. Pip's heart did a flip, and she grinned.
