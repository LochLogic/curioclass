# Lena's Bookmark

Lena made a bookmark from a ribbon. She tied a bow at the top. Then she checked the knot twice. It was finished. She slid it into her book and held it up proudly. Her brother grinned. "Can I have one?" he asked. Lena grabbed more ribbon.
