# Where to Practice

After lunch, the drama group began its rehearsal in the classroom. Each partner read a line, but the noise from the next table was loud. Ben could not hear Ada's words. "We need another place," he said. Ada picked up the collection of scripts. They walked to the empty hallway, where every voice sounded clear. Ben read his line, and Ada answered right away. Soon the partners knew all of their lines. The teacher smiled at the calm, quiet work.
