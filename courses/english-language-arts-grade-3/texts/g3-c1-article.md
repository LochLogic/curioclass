# The Hill Town Book Cart

Contents: Why a Cart? (page 1). A Cart Day (page 1). Who Helps (page 2). What the Cart Brings (page 2).

## Why a Cart? (page 1)

The Hill Town library stands in the valley. Many families live up on the hill. The road is steep, so the trip is long. The library built a book cart to bring books up the hill.

## A Cart Day (page 1)

First, helpers load the cart with books. Next, the cart rolls up the hill. At each stop, families choose books. Last, the cart goes back down with the books people returned.

## Who Helps (page 2)

Volunteers push the cart. They give their time and are not paid. They also read aloud to the youngest children. Because volunteers help, the cart can visit every week.

## What the Cart Brings (page 2)

The cart carries picture books, maps and cookbooks. Families may ask for a book by name. It comes on the next visit. The cart is especially popular with children.

Sidebar (page 2): Cart facts. Eight stops. One visit each week. About 120 books on board.
