Read whole on day 1 (map bridge: Manaus and the Rio Negro on a world map), again on 2 to 5, and returned to on 29 to 32, 34 to 37 and 39. ## Text

[page 1]
Lia lives in Manaus, a city in Brazil. The Rio Negro flows past it, wide and brown as strong tea. In the rainy months the river climbs and covers some of the paths. Then boats are the buses.
Once a month, a library comes to Lia's landing on a boat. Today is Boat Book Day.
Picture: A girl of about eight with a long dark braid stands on a wooden porch built on tall posts. Wide brown water spreads below her. A green line of rainforest trees edges the far bank under low, bright clouds.

[page 2]
The night before, Lia prepares. She chooses the book she will return, the one about river animals. She slips the date card inside it so she will remember the day. Then she sets the book on the table by the door.
"Why so early?" asks her little sister, Nina.
"The boat does not stay long," says Lia. "I want to be ready to borrow something new."
Picture: A kitchen at night with a hanging lamp. Lia sets a thick blue book on a table by the door, and a small card sticks out of its pages. A younger girl in pajamas watches from a hammock.

[page 3]
In the morning, water covers the path to the landing. Neighbors have laid long boards across it. Lia walks the boards one slow step at a time. Her heart beats like a drum.
Halfway across, she notices something. One board is loose, and it wobbles.
"Careful!" Lia calls to the people behind her. She kneels, pushes the board straight, and holds it still with her foot. One by one, her neighbors cross. Nina holds her hand the whole way.
Picture: A line of neighbors, grown-ups and children, walks on long boards over brown water between houses on posts. Lia kneels at the front, pressing a loose board flat with her foot. Her sister holds her hand.

[page 4]
At last Lia sees the boat, tied to the landing. It has shelves along both walls and a roof like a big hat. Inside, a librarian named Dona Ivone stamps the books.
Lia gives her the book about river animals. Dona Ivone opens the cover and finds the date card. "Right on time," she says. Lia stands a little taller.
Picture: A white boat with a curved green roof is tied to a floating wooden landing. Through a wide window we see shelves full of books. A woman with gray curls and round glasses holds a stamp. Lia hands her a blue book.

[page 5]
Lia's friend Cami stands at the shelves, frowning. "I want a book about birds," she says, "but I do not know which one."
Lia does not choose for her. She asks, "Which birds do you like best?"
Cami talks about birds with bright feathers and loud voices. Lia listens. Then she finds a book with big, shiny pictures and holds it out. "Try this one."
Cami's face lights up like a lamp.
Picture: Inside the boat library, two girls stand close together at a shelf. One points at a picture of a bird with bright feathers. The other listens with her head tilted. Rain taps on the window behind them.

[page 6]
Lia chooses a book about the rainforest for herself. Its first sentence is long and hard.
When the rain stops, the river grows quiet, and the whole forest seems to listen.
Lia reads it once, and some words trip her. She reads it again, slowly, and pauses at the comma. Now the sentence makes a picture in her mind. She explains it to Nina. "The forest is so still, it seems to have ears."
"Say it again!" says Nina.
Picture: Lia and Nina sit on a bench at the back of the boat with an open book between them. Lia points under a line of words. Nina's eyes are wide. Green leaves press against the window.

[page 7]
A horn toots. The boat must go. Dona Ivone calls, "See you next month!"
Lia writes the new date on a card and tucks it inside her book. Then she follows her neighbors home along the boards. This time the loose board stays still under their feet.
Picture: The boat pulls away from the landing with its horn blowing. Lia stands on the boards holding her new book against her chest and waves. Her neighbors walk home behind her in the soft rain.

[page 8]
That night, the lamp glows in the window and the river hums. Lia reads aloud while Nina listens.
The boat had brought the books across the water. Lia had made the way across the boards. Together, the books had come all the way home.
Picture: Lia and Nina in the hammock under the lamp, the book open across their knees. Outside the window, the dark river shines under a few stars.
