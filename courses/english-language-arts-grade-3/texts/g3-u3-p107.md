# The Torn Notice and Leaf Boats

Scene 1. [Beside a torn notice.]
Ben: "Only half the title remains."
Ada: "I will find the other half."

Scene 2. [Ada returns with the scrap.]
Ada: "The missing words are here."
Ben: "Now I can read the whole title."

Leaf Boats

A leaf rests by the garden stone.
Its curled brown edges lie alone.

Rain lifts the leaf beyond the stone.
It sails where little streams have grown.
