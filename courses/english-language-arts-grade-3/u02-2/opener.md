# Section 2.2 opener: drafting a frog tale (days 43 to 47)

## What the section does

For five days each child drafts one tale of their own from the plan card: the narrator, the situation and the beginning on day 43, speech, action and feeling in the middle on day 44, and time links and an ending on day 45. Day 46 is a check on cold reading: a poem, a myth and a note with hard words. Day 47 returns the work with feedback and children revise.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Days 43 to 45 teach a verb form, speech marks and the comma, and the three-time line, and children apply each to their own draft. On day 45 the teacher also hears each child read a short word list. Day 46 is silent: three item sheets, then a silent reread. Day 47 gives back the day 45 word check and the day 46 sheets with a note.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Verbs change for time.** Some add -ed; some change inside. Check the form against the time the sentence tells.
- **Speech has marks.** The spoken words go inside speech marks, with a comma before the closing mark.
- **An ending closes the problem.** It answers what the tale opened.
- **A word can be read by its parts.** A base and an ending say what the word means.

## The errors, in plain words

- **L:** An irregular verb receives a regular ending. Repair: Rehearse the irregular pair in a sentence. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **S:** Writing leaves the intended explanation unclear. Repair: Add the missing explanation for its purpose. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **P:** An event has no developed character response. Repair: Expand it with dialogue and a response. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **U:** Dialogue lacks speech punctuation. Repair: Mark spoken words before adding quotation marks and comma. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Q:** An ending introduces an unrelated topic. Repair: Close the established problem or explanation. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **O:** Narrative events have an incoherent order. Repair: Order event cards before adding time links. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **A:** A fact is asserted without source support. Repair: Locate the exact source sentence. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Y:** A causal relationship is reversed. Repair: Draw a cause-to-result arrow from the source. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
The day 45 word check and the day 46 items are scored on the foundational and end columns of the record sheet.
