# Day 45: Draft time links and a closing that follows the events

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, draft pages, three-time line and the word check, day 45, for each child. Find the day 45 line of the exit cards. Print the word check card large. Mark three names on the heard-six column for the spoken exit ask. Choose twelve children for the word check hearing.

### The day

#### 15 min. Word check (inside the skills block)

*3 min.* Return and repair, on day 44's exit (s02-X44), error U (speech with no marks or comma): children add marks and a comma to a line, [[s02-P45-1]]. Error P (an event with nothing said or felt): add what Frog says. *7 min.* The word check on paper: the teacher dictates three words and a sentence; each child writes them and tells in a tale sentence what the ending adds. Review: -tion and -sion (grade 2). Children write mention and tension on slates, [[s02-P45-2]]. *5 min.* The hearing: twelve children, one at a time, about 2 minutes 15 seconds each with the changeover, read the check card aloud, [[s02-A1]], [[s02-A2]], [[s02-A3]]. Only twelve fit: eight are heard on day 46 and the rest on day 48. The hearing runs on through the independent reading. The teacher does not mark the word check; each hearing goes on the record sheet as shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Everyone reads from their logs, no teaching, while the hearing goes on. No conferences today, so the heard-six column loses its six. Each child adds one log line.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs (review: day 42). Pairs reread the last three paragraphs of Why the Frog Has a Long Tongue and point to the words. **Ask.** "How does the ending settle the contest?" Expect: Kikker won the contest, and soon every frog caught flies with a flick of the tongue, [[s02-K45-1]]. *4 min.* Class share. *3 min.* Retrieval on slates: the class writes mention and tension again from memory, then checks them against the board.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Review: simple tenses, past, present and future (grade 1). The teacher shows one verb on a three-time line, hop, hopped, will hop, and says: the word changes to tell when. Children fill the line for croak, [[s02-P45-3]]. *5 min.* Each child writes one verb from their own draft in all three boxes and underlines the part that changed, [[s02-P45-4]].

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs, 2 minutes a child: read your ending aloud; the partner says whether it closes the problem, [[s02-P45-5]]. *6 min.* Three volunteers read their endings, 2 minutes each. Anyone may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Judge a closing: a tadpole cannot find a pond, a turtle carries it to one. Children mark the ending that closes the problem, [[s02-P45-7]]. *15 min.* Each child drafts time links and a closing in their own tale, [[s02-P45-6]]: First, Then and At last before the events, and a last sentence that follows them. Watch for error Q (a closing about something else): ask what problem the tale began with. Watch for error S (words with no sentence): ask who, where and why.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 45 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X45]]. While they start, hear three children from the heard-six column, one at a time, 45 seconds each, [[s02-X45b]]; the other children's time links are read from the draft. Unscored. Shown: a sentence that follows the three events. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What happened to Frog in the end?" Not yet seen: a closing about something else (error Q) or words with no sentence (error S).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the word check and is the partner for the reread and the talk. No three-time line: draw three boxes on paper. No printer: copy the exit card onto the board. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No three-time line: draw three boxes on paper.
**Skip.** The word check and the volunteers.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the whole draft aloud to you.
  **Say.** "Which words tell when each event happens?" Expect: First, Then, At last.
- **10 min. Mark.** Your child underlines each time word.
- **10 min. Write.** Your child writes a closing sentence, [[s02-P45-6]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X45]] alone, then answers [[s02-X45b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the volunteers and the judging; keep the draft and the exit. Longer: a child writes the closing two ways and chooses one.

### Words and supports

Time link, closing, past, present, future. Large print; the draft said aloud before it is written; a scribe for ideas, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 is the check: the reading and word items, in sections, with the retrieval of -tion and -sion first. Day 47 hands back this exit, [[s02-X45]], and the word check. Error Q (a closing about something else): reread the start and close the same problem. Error S (words with no sentence): add who, where and why. The student page and draft pages stay in the folder.
