# Day 47: Use reading feedback while revising the tale's situation and character

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write a note on each day 46 sheet and on each heard child's day 45 word check. Sort the sheets into four piles by error letter. Print the student page and draft pages for each child and a feedback card for each pair. Mark three names on the heard-six column for the spoken exit ask. Check the recording list: five names for the fourth window.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair, on day 46's exit (s02-X46), error E (certainty words treated as the same), error A and error x: place wondered, suspected and knew on the certainty line from least sure to most sure, [[s02-P47-1]]. Review: the certainty line (day 40). Then point to the guess word on the card. *7 min.* Week 10: children write attention, discussion and expression on slates, [[s02-P47-2]]. Review: -tion and -sion (grade 2). Review: an irregular word has a part that does not sound the way it looks (day 40). Children mark that part of above, [[s02-P47-3]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Review: regular and irregular verbs (day 43). Children write the past form of four verbs on slates, [[s02-P47-6]]. *15 min.* The fourth recording window: five children, 3 minutes each, swap included, on stanza one of Under the Leaf, [[s02-P47-4]]. Review: a recording states its purpose, reads, and rereads one line after the replay (day 42). Every other pair rereads stanza three with the feedback card, in phrases and with expression, no speed, [[s02-P47-5]]. The partner's card is the feedback today.

#### 10 min. Feedback and return and repair

*3 min.* The teacher returns the day 46 sheets (day 46's assessment) with notes, errors A to G and O, and the day 45 word check to the children who were heard, with a note: compare the base and the ending, then read the words again. Day 45's exit comes back too: error Q (a closing about something else) or error S (words with no sentence). *4 min.* The teacher repairs the commonest letter on the board. *3 min.* Four groups by letter, one repair item each, three minutes: what a tale teaches, [[s02-P47-7]]; a nonliteral line, [[s02-P47-8]]; a certainty word, [[s02-P47-9]]; a word part, [[s02-P47-10]]. Children in no group repair the sheet's own item.

#### 20 min. Written repair

Each child revises the beginning of their tale on draft pages, using the note and the repair, [[s02-P47-11]]: show where the narrator is and why. Watch for error S (a bare line with no reason): ask where and why. Watch for error Y (the friend and the call in the wrong order): ask who calls first.

#### 10 min. Reread

*4 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs (review: day 43). Pairs reread the end of Why the Frog Has a Long Tongue and point to the words. **Ask.** "Which action shows kindness despite teasing?" Expect: Kikker showed the other frogs the exercises that made his tongue long, [[s02-K47-1]]. *6 min.* Review: a trait is what a character's actions show (day 6). Each child writes three sentences about Kikker: his trait, [[s02-A15]], his goal, [[s02-P47-12]], and how one action changes the contest, [[s02-A16]]. Written today, returned on day 48.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*4 min.* Review: an exact verb (grade 2). In a line of the model beginning, replace went with a more exact verb, leaped, and say why. *6 min.* Each child changes one verb in the revised beginning, [[s02-P47-13]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: read the revised beginning aloud; the partner says what the narrator wants, [[s02-P47-14]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 47 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X47]]. While they start, hear three children from the heard-six column, one at a time, 45 seconds each, [[s02-X47b]]; no card. Unscored. Shown: place and reason in two sentences. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Where is she, and why does she call?" Not yet seen: a bare line with no reason (error S) or events out of order (error Y).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner for the reread and the talk, and hears the recording read aloud. No device: skip the recording; each child reads stanza one aloud, noted. No feedback card: the partner names one smooth phrase. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No draft pages: copy the beginning onto one sheet.
**Skip.** The slate words and the recording.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the revised beginning aloud.
  **Say.** "Where is the narrator, and why?" Expect: the place and the reason, in the child's words.
- **20 min. Write.** Your child writes three sentences about Kikker: trait, goal and one action, [[s02-A15]], [[s02-P47-12]], [[s02-A16]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X47]] alone, then answers [[s02-X47b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the groups and the exact-verb model; keep the revision and the exit. Longer: a child revises the middle of the tale with the same note.

### Words and supports

Feedback, revise, trait, goal, exact verb. Large print; the note read aloud, recorded as help; a scribe for ideas, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X47]]. Error S (a bare line with no reason): add the missing reason, then reread it to a partner. Error Y (the friend and the call in the wrong order): say who calls first, then put the sentences in that order. The draft pages and Kikker sentences stay in the folder; the Kikker sentences return on day 48.
