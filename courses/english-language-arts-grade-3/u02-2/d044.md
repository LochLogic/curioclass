# Day 44: Draft the middle of your frog tale with speech, action and feeling

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and draft pages for each child and a feedback card for each pair. Find the day 44 line of the exit cards. Check the recording list: five names for today, the third window. Mark three names on the heard-six column for the spoken exit ask. Write the four unmarked lines of [[s02-P44-8]] on the board.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair, on day 43's exit (s02-X43), errors L and S. Error L (an -ed on a changed verb): children say the past form of sleep in a sentence about a tadpole, [[s02-P44-1]]; any child who made error L fixes their own exit sentence. Error S (words with no sentence): add who, where and why. *7 min.* Review: -tion and -sion (grade 2). Say mention, division and motion once each; children write them on slates, [[s02-P44-2]]. Review: an irregular word has a part that does not sound the way it looks (day 40). Children mark that part of laughter, [[s02-P44-3]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Review: regular and irregular verbs (day 43). Children match four verbs with their past forms, [[s02-P44-4]]. *15 min.* The third recording window: five children, 3 minutes each, swap included, on stanza two of Under the Leaf, [[s02-P44-6]]. Review: a recording states its purpose, reads, and rereads one line after the replay (day 42). Every other pair rereads the taunt paragraph of Why the Frog Has a Long Tongue with the feedback card, in phrases and with expression, no speed, [[s02-P44-5]]. The partner's card is the feedback today.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* On the board, mark one place to pause in a taunt, [[s02-P44-7]]. *8 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: pairs reread the taunt paragraph and point to the words. **Ask.** "How do the taunts affect Kikker?" Expect: he dives deep under the water and his cheeks burn with embarrassment, [[s02-K44-1]]. *4 min.* Class share.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Review: quotation marks mark the words a person says (grade 1 and day 27). New today: where the comma and the end mark go. Write two lines from the tale: "Maybe a ladder!" called another. Then Old Bullfrog croaked, You have no chance. Mark the spoken words first, then add the marks. The ! or ? or a comma sits inside the closing mark when the speaker comes after: "Go home, Tiny," called a frog. *5 min.* Children correct four unmarked lines on slates, [[s02-P44-8]].

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs, 2 minutes a child: read one spoken line aloud so your partner hears how the speaker feels, [[s02-P44-9]]. *6 min.* Three volunteers perform a line, 2 minutes each. Anyone may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

Each child drafts the middle of the tale on draft pages, [[s02-P44-10]]: one event with a spoken line in speech marks, an action, a thought and a feeling. Watch for error P (an event with no spoken words or feeling): ask what the frog says. Watch for error U (speech with no marks): mark the spoken words first.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 44 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X44]]. While they start, hear three children from the heard-six column, one at a time, 45 seconds each, [[s02-X44b]]; the other children's sequence is read from the draft. Unscored. Shown: speech in marks with the comma inside, and one action or feeling. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does Frog say?" Not yet seen: speech with no marks (error U) or an event with nothing said or felt (error P).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner for the reread and the lines, and hears the recording read aloud. No device: skip the recording; each child reads stanza two aloud to a partner, noted. No feedback card: the partner names one phrase that sounded smooth. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No device or feedback card: read stanza two aloud twice to an adult.
**Skip.** The slate words and the recording.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the taunt paragraph aloud to your child.
  **Say.** "How do the taunts affect Kikker?" Expect: he dives under the water and feels embarrassed.
- **10 min. Marks.** Your child adds speech marks and commas, [[s02-P44-8]].
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes the middle of the tale, [[s02-P44-10]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X44]] alone, then answers [[s02-X44b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the pause mark and the volunteers; keep the marks, the draft and the exit. Longer: a child adds a second speaker who answers the first.

### Words and supports

Spoken line, speech marks, comma, taunt, embarrassment. Large print; a scribe for ideas, noted; the taunts read aloud twice. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 45 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X44]]. Error U (speech with no marks or comma): mark the spoken words first, then add the marks and the comma. Error P (an event with nothing said or felt): add what Frog says and how she feels. Day 45 then adds time links and a closing sentence to the draft. The draft pages stay in the folder.
