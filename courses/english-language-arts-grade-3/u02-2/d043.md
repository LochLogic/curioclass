# Day 43: Draft the beginning of your frog tale

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, draft pages and the two tale copies for each child, and the verb cards for each pair. Find the day 43 line of the exit cards. Mark three names on the heard-six column for the spoken exit ask. Write hop, hopped, swim and swam on the board after the pairs sit.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair, on the commonest letter in day 42's exit (s02-X42), error O. Children number three events out of order and say which comes first, [[s02-P43-1]]; then add First, Then and At last. *7 min.* Review: -tion and -sion (grade 3, days 41 and 42). Say invention, tension and confusion once each; children write them on slates, [[s02-P43-2]]. Review: an irregular word has a part that does not sound the way it looks (day 40). Children mark that part of young, [[s02-P43-3]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children read from their logs. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), then each child adds a log line (4 minutes). Add the four to the heard-six column and mark each shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*6 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs (review: day 42). Pairs reread the first two paragraphs of Why the Frog Has a Long Tongue and point to the words. **Ask.** "How does the opening make the goal clear?" Expect: the words about wanting to win, [[s02-K43-1]]. *3 min.* The class names the words. *6 min.* Show the start of Why the Poison Dart Frog Is So Colorful beside it. Review: the narrator is the teller (day 41); a tale may be told from outside, or by an I who is in it. Name who tells each tale, the sentence that gives the situation, and what is wanted. The Ranna opening gives a situation and no goal yet; the long-tongue opening gives both.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*2 min.* New today: regular and irregular verbs. Hop becomes hopped by adding -ed: regular. Go becomes went, a changed word: irregular. Show swim and swam, say the wrong form that sounds right, swimmed, and cross it out. *3 min.* Children mark a wrong past form, [[s02-P43-4]]. *5 min.* Pairs sort the eight verb cards into regular and irregular and say each past form in a sentence about a frog, [[s02-P43-5]]. The teacher checks the piles and does not mark.

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs, 2 minutes a child, swap included: tell the beginning of your tale; the partner says back who tells it and where it starts, [[s02-P43-8]]. *6 min.* Six volunteers, 1 minute each. Anyone may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*17 min.* Each child drafts the narrator, the situation and the beginning from the plan card, on draft pages, [[s02-P43-7]]: who tells it, where it starts, what is wanted, the first event. *3 min.* Underline two past verbs and mark R or I, [[s02-P43-6]]. Watch for error S (a list of words): ask who, where and why. Watch for error L (an -ed on a changed verb).

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 43 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X43]]. While they start, hear three children from the heard-six column, one at a time, 45 seconds each, [[s02-X43b]]; the other children's narrator and situation are read from the draft. Unscored. Shown: two sentences that follow the card, one -ed verb, one changed verb. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which verb changed inside?" Not yet seen: a regular ending on a changed verb (error L) or words with no sentence (error S).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner for the reread and the talk, and sorts the verb cards with the child. No verb cards: write eight verbs on slips. No printer: copy the exit card onto the board. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No plan card: write the plan on one sheet. No verb cards: write the pairs on slips.
**Skip.** The conferences and the pair sort.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the first two paragraphs of the long-tongue tale aloud.
  **Say.** "What does Kikker want?" Expect: to win the contest.
- **10 min. Verbs.** Your child marks the wrong form, [[s02-P43-4]], then names one -ed verb and one that changes inside.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes the beginning, [[s02-P43-7]], and marks two past verbs, [[s02-P43-6]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X43]] alone, then answers [[s02-X43b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the sort and the volunteers; keep the reread, the draft and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second opening line told by the friend.

### Words and supports

Narrator, situation, regular, irregular, past form. Large print; the draft said aloud before it is written; a scribe for ideas, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X43]]. Error L (an -ed on a changed verb): say the pair in a sentence, then fix your own sentence. Error S (words with no sentence): add who, where and why. Day 44 then drafts the middle of the tale with a spoken line, an action, a thought and a feeling. The draft pages stay in the folder.
