# Verb cards, day 43

Print one set for each pair, cut apart. Eight cards, each with the verb on the front and its past form on the back: walk, walked, jump, jumped, splash, splashed, fix, fixed, sit, sat, see, saw, give, gave, write, wrote. Pairs sort the eight into regular (the past form adds -ed) and irregular (the past form changes in another way), then read each past form aloud in a sentence about a frog. The teacher checks the two piles and does not mark.

Four are regular and four irregular. Do not add any verb a child writes in an exit or an end item.
