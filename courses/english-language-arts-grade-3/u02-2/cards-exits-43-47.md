# Exit cards, days 43 to 47

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 43 (s02-X43).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "I waited at the edge of a dry pool. My friend needed a damp hiding place." Ask: Write two sentences that tell what the narrator did next. Use one regular past verb and one irregular past verb. Spoken ask for the six children heard today (s02-X43b): Tell who is telling this and where the telling starts.
- **Day 44 (s02-X44).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Frog reached the bank. Her friend was still behind her. She wanted him to hear her warning." Ask: Write what happens next. Put what Frog says in speech marks, and add one thing she does or feels. Spoken ask for the six heard today (s02-X44b): Tell three things Frog does next, in order.
- **Day 45 (s02-X45).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Frog found a hollow log. His friend climbed inside. Rain began outside." Ask: Write one closing sentence that follows these events. Spoken ask for the six heard today (s02-X45b): Say the three events again with a time word before each.
- **Day 46 (s02-X46).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "The path was empty. I guessed my friend had gone home. The porch light was off." Ask: Write the word that shows the speaker is not sure.
- **Day 47 (s02-X47).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Little Frog wanted company. She called from a dark hollow. Her friend followed the sound." Ask: Rewrite the first two sentences to show where Frog is and why she calls. Spoken ask for the six heard today (s02-X47b, no card): Tell one thing your feedback note said and how you changed your beginning.
