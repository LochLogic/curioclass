# Day 46: Check poem and myth reading, word meaning and the tale's message

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the item sheets, day 46: the three sheets, each on its own page, and Latona at the Pool for each child. Find the day 46 line of the exit cards. Choose eight children for the word check hearing.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills (retrieval only)

*10 min.* Retrieval, no new pattern. Review: -tion and -sion (grade 2). The teacher says station, decision and mention once each; children write them on slates, [[s02-P46-1]]. *20 min.* Independent reading from the logs, no teaching. Eight more children are heard for the word check begun on day 45, about 2 minutes 15 seconds each with the changeover; this replaces the conferences. The rest are heard on day 48. Each log gets one line.

#### 45 min. Items

Silent, each on its own sheet. Sheet 46A, 18 minutes: Latona at the Pool is read alone, [[s02-A4]], [[s02-A5]], [[s02-A6]]. If the text is read aloud, record it as help. Sheet 46B, 12 minutes: the invented poem panel, [[s02-A7]], [[s02-A8]], [[s02-A9]], [[s02-A10]]. Sheet 46C, 15 minutes: the invented notes, [[s02-A11]], [[s02-A12]], [[s02-A13]], [[s02-A14]]. The teacher collects every sheet at the end of the part, reads no stem and gives no hint. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Silent verification

Children reread their own answers. The teacher answers nothing about content. After class, sort the sheets into piles by error letter (A, B, C, D, E, F, G and O); the piles make day 47's four groups.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 46 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X46]]. Unscored.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult sits apart and reads nothing aloud. Large print on request, recorded as help. No printer: copy the sheets by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at the child's level.
**Skip.** The slate words.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Your child tells you what the assessment sheets were like, with no hints from you.
  **Say.** "What did you wonder about?" Expect: one thing, said in a sentence.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from the log book.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes station, decision and mention, then reads them to you.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X46]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the sheets keep their time. Longer: a child finishing early rereads Latona and checks each answer.

### Words and supports

Pool, glossary, protect, cautious. Large print, recorded as help. No hint is given.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 hands back this exit, [[s02-X46]], and the three sheets. Error E (certainty words treated as the same): place wondered, suspected and knew on a line. Error C (a claim the text does not make): reread and copy the line. Error A (a claim the card does not make): point to the words. Error x (a word about the path, not about being sure): find the guess word. The sheets stay with the teacher; children keep the folder.
