# Word check, day 45

The teacher hears each child read these aloud, one at a time, about 2 minutes 15 seconds each with the changeover, while the others read from their logs. Twelve children are heard on day 45, eight on day 46 and the rest on day 48. Score the ending, the whole word and the sentence separately on the record sheet.

1. For kindness, slowly and readable, say what each ending adds to its word.
2. Read this sentence aloud: Frog's action needed revision.
3. Read this sentence aloud: Frog said the rain was enough.

Print the words and sentences large on a card, one set for each reading. Do not read the words to the child first.
