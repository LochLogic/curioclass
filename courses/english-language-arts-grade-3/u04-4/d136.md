# Day 136: Use your feedback, then read an opinion model and write plurals

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, with the opinion model on side 2. Sort the P135 pages by the commonest letter on the view and phrase items: J, K or AU. Make at most four repair groups. Write on the board: J, K, AU. Keep the word record from day 135 open at the commonest missed pattern. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words and word repair

*4 min.* Teach again the commonest missed pattern on the word record: say each word in chunks, then children write the words they missed, [[s04-P136-1]]. *3 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P136-2]]. Review: you split long words into chunks from day 81 on; you used therefore and since on day 98. *3 min.* Say since and example twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P136-3]]. Review: you met for example on day 103. Watch for a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P136-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying what they read, what they wondered and one detail, [[s04-P136-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P136-6]]. Fill the heard-six column. Start with children not heard on day 133. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return day 135's assessment, the page P135, with one comment each, and the exit tickets of days 134 and 135 (s04-X134 and s04-X135) with one comment each. Hand back the day 134 page with a tick on each written task. Children read the comment silently and mark one thing to fix, [[s04-P136-7]]. Say: the check was unscored, so this is a first look and not a grade.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Groups by the commonest letter on P135, at most four groups. *2 min.* Each group reads the right line on its own copy. Group J: "I say pond first. The writer says both jobs." Group K: "Staff watch the gauge carefully." Group AU: "The article asks for a bucket for every house." *6 min.* Each child redoes the one item for the group, a different case from P135.

- Group J, [[s04-P136-8v2]]. Say: label each sentence writer or me, with the rows of the view label sheet.
- Group K, [[s04-P136-9]]. Say: test both readings against the next sentence.
- Group AU, [[s04-P136-10v2]]. Say: reread the sentence that states it.

*2 min.* Each child shows the revision to a partner from the group and says what changed, [[s04-P136-11]]. Review: you did return and repair on days 125 and 132. Do not reopen the P135 passage.

#### 15 min. Read

*6 min.* Read A Rain Garden First aloud once, then children mark its four parts, [[s04-P136-12]]: the topic, the position, the because and the close. Review: you marked an opinion model on day 120. *9 min.* Say: a plural noun names more than one; an abstract noun names an idea you cannot touch. Children circle each plural noun, [[s04-P136-13]], and underline the abstract noun, [[s04-P136-14]]. Review: you learned irregular plurals and abstract nouns on days 57 and 59. Watch for a singular noun circled.

#### 15 min. Write

*4 min.* Model two on the board: man and men, and the sentence "Safety matters to a village." Say the singular and the plural together. *11 min.* Children write six lines. Plurals first, [[s04-P136-15]]. Then two sentences that begin with an idea, [[s04-P136-16]] and [[s04-P136-17]]. Watch for a plain s added to an irregular plural, error R. A child who needs it may say the sentence aloud first.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child reads the four plural pairs to a partner, the singular first, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P136-18]]. Say: say the singular and the plural together. Listen in on three pairs; no child is held up as the example of a mistake.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read the sentence about a conclusion. Children circle the words that tell what a conclusion is, [[s04-P136-19]]. *2 min.* Read the entry on the board and check it against the circled words: a conclusion is the part that ends a piece of writing and closes what the writer said. Review: you met conclusion on day 66 as a closing sentence.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X136]]. Support is recorded. Shown: geese. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What do we call more than one goose?" Not yet seen: gooses or geeses (error R). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hands back the day 135 page, takes the part of the group and says the right line; the child redoes the repair item, marks the model and answers the exit on paper. One projector: show the model; children mark on paper and the plural pairs go on the board one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed model: read any short opinion paragraph together.
**Skip.** The group repair and the word in context.

- **15 min. Read.** Read A Rain Garden First aloud together.
  **Say.** "What does the writer want first, and why?" Expect a rain garden first, because dry soil drinks slowly.
- **10 min. Plurals.** Say each singular and ask for the plural. Your child writes the four plurals, [[s04-P136-15]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X136]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the plural writing and the exit; cut the long words and the partner talk. Longer: write a closing sentence for the model, or a third idea sentence.

### Words and supports

Plural, abstract noun, conclusion, position, neighbour, since. Frames: "The writer believes ___. I think ___." "The plural of ___ is ___." The model may be read aloud again for any child; a card with child, children; woman, women; mouse, mice; large print for the card; a drawing may carry a sentence with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 137 begins the next section. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X136, likely error R: say the singular and its irregular plural together. It then starts research on a chosen water issue and possible solutions in the four categories. The P135 pages and the plural lines go in the folder.
