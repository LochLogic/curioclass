# Exit cards, days 133 to 136

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 133 (s04-X133), Exit.** Invented for practice. Story: "Sam wants the biggest bucket. The narrator says sharing the buckets is kind." Article: "The writer asks that every house gets a bucket." Ask: Whose words say sharing is kind? Write the name and the words.
- **Day 134 (s04-X134), Exit.** Invented for practice. First paragraph: "First a net lifts big leaves from a pond. Next a rake pulls out the weeds." Second paragraph: "The writer says a pond needs both jobs. Leaves and weeds are different kinds of mess." Ask: Write a response to the writer. Say if you agree. Give two reasons with a link word. Finish with a closing sentence.
- **Day 135 (s04-X135), Exit.** Invented for practice. Sentence: "Staff keep an eye on the pond gauge each day." Ask: Say in your own words what keep an eye on means here.
- **Day 136 (s04-X136), Exit.** Invented for practice. Line: "One goose stands by the gate. Two ____ stand by the gate." Ask: Write the word that fits the gap.

Every exit sheet prints the card and the ask only, with a ruled space for the answer. The look-fors live in items.csv and the record sheet, never on the child's page.
