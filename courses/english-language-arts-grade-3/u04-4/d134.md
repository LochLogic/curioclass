# Day 134: Follow the steps and the words that join them, then answer the writer

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the town text and the feedback card for each child. Return the day 133 exit slips with one comment each as children sit. Sort them by the commonest slip: the narrator, Mr. Kioko, or right. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column. Write on the board: first, next, then, after that, because, so.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P134-1]]. Review: you split long words into chunks from day 81 on. *3 min.* They add -ly or -ous to three words, [[s04-P134-2]]. Review: you added -ly on days 32 and 127 and learned -ous on day 54; fame loses its silent e. *3 min.* Say heard and learn twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P134-3]]. Both are new: ear saying /ur/. Watch for a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Wednesday

*5 min.* Show "The plant cleans water. It sends the water to homes." Say: it stands for the plant. Children write the word that stands for the workers, [[s04-P134-4]]. Review: you learned on day 86 that a pronoun stands for a noun, and on day 88 that it matches the noun. *10 min.* Partner rereading: each child reads the first paragraph of the town text aloud twice, with the partner listening, 5 minutes each way, [[s04-P134-5]]. Review: you did partner rereading with the feedback card on days 2 and 132. Listen in on three pairs, 2 minutes each; that is six children heard. Give one comment on phrases or expression, not on speed. *5 min.* Each child ticks the feedback card for their partner and says one thing that went well, [[s04-P134-6]].

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 133 page with a tick on each written task. The commonest letter on the day 133 exit is likely error AM: a character's words given to the narrator. Write two lines from the story on the board: "That is not fair" and "the plan was fair." Write the speaker beside each: Mutheu, the narrator. Say: name who says it before you say what they think. Children read the slip comment and mark one thing to fix. *2 min.* Partners say what a character, a narrator and a writer each are, one line each, [[s04-P134-7]]. Review: you labelled these views on day 133.

#### 20 min. Read and annotate

*4 min.* Read steps 1 to 4 of the town text aloud once; children follow on their copy. *5 min.* Pairs reread the steps in turn, one step each, 2 minutes each way and 1 to change over, [[s04-P134-8]]. *11 min.* Children mark the print. Circle the word that opens each step, [[s04-P134-9]]. Then in step 3 circle the cause word and underline the cause, [[s04-P134-10]]. Say: first, next, then and after that tell the order; because tells the cause. Review: you used time words on day 41 and so and because on day 14. Watch for step names circled, not the order words.

#### 15 min. Paragraph links

*3 min.* Model one link on the board with a different text: "The tap was off. So the bucket stayed dry." The second sentence is joined to the first by so, and so shows cause. Children copy the frame "joined to ___ by ___." *9 min.* Pairs fill two rows. Step 3 uses a word from step 2, [[s04-P134-11]]. A sentence in the paragraph Why the order matters is joined by so, [[s04-P134-12]]. *3 min.* Each child writes the main idea of the town text in one line, [[s04-P134-13]]. Review: you gave a main idea on days 121 and 127.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child tells a partner which link word helped most in the town text, and why, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P134-14]]. Listen in on three pairs; no child is held up as the example of a mistake.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read the sentence with therefore. Children write a word that does the same job, [[s04-P134-15]]. *2 min.* Say: therefore means for that reason, the same job as so. Review: you used therefore and since as link words on day 98.

#### 10 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X134]]. This is O03: it replaces the usual exit. Support is recorded. Shown: says if they agree as their own view, two reasons that use the net, the rake or the mess, a link word and a closing sentence. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the card say the net and the rake each do?" Not yet seen: the reader's view given to the writer (error J), a claim the card does not make (error A), liking alone (error M) or no closing (error O). Record on the record sheet. Keep the slip in the folder.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the steps aloud once, hears the rereading and takes the part of the partner; the child circles the order words, does the two link rows and answers the exit on paper. One projector: show the town text one step at a time; children mark on paper and partners talk in pairs.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed text: read any short how-it-works page with steps.
**Skip.** The long words and the word in context.

- **15 min. Read.** Read the steps together, twice, taking turns.
  **Say.** "Which words tell you what comes first, next and then?" Expect first, next, then, after that.
- **10 min. Mark.** Your child circles the order words and the cause word, [[s04-P134-9]] and [[s04-P134-10]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X134]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the marking, the two link rows and the exit; cut the long words and the partner talk. Longer: write a third reason in the exit, or a second link word.

### Words and supports

Link word, cause, therefore, order, evidence, research. Frames: "First ___. Next ___. Then ___." "It happens because ___." "I agree because ___. So ___." The town text may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the card; a drawing may carry a reason with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 135 is the unscored check: a word check, silent reading, then one new passage with four asks. The exit tells you who put the reader's view in the writer's mouth, error J; that comes back on day 136 with the passage. The slip goes in the folder.
