# Day 135: Check point of view and word meaning in a new passage

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, with the check passage (P135) on side 2. Tell children to keep side 2 face down until the passage part. Make a word card with five words: build, heard, famous, development, important. This is a check: no help with the items, no scoring today. Do not read the passage before the passage part. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column and a word record beside it. Day 134's exit slips stay in the folder.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word check

*4 min.* Say each of four words twice; children write them, [[s04-P135-1]]. Unscored. Do not give a spelling. *6 min.* While the others match three words to their meanings, [[s04-P135-3]], hear six children read the word card aloud, one minute each, [[s04-P135-2]]. Start with children not heard on days 133 and 134. Mark each miss on the word record. The commonest missed pattern is taught again on day 136.

#### 20 min. Silent independent reading

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P135-4]]. Move among them; no conferences. Record who read the whole time.

#### 15 min. Read the panel

Children turn to side 2. *6 min.* They read At the Source silently. *3 min.* Read it aloud once. *6 min.* They reread it silently. Children mark nothing and nothing is explained.

#### 35 min. Items

One ask at a time, in order. Say only the ask. *10 min.* [[s04-A10]]. *10 min.* [[s04-A11]]. *8 min.* [[s04-A12]]. *7 min.* [[s04-A13]]. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Unscored this time. The day 131 page is not scored again.

#### 5 min. Independent review

Children reread their own answers and fix one thing beside the first answer, [[s04-P135-5]]. Collect the pages.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X135]]. Support is recorded. Shown: watch carefully, or check. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What would staff do each day to a gauge?" Not yet seen: a body-part meaning (error K). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the passage aloud once and the child answers on paper; the word card is read aloud to the adult. The items stay on paper. One projector: show the passage, then the page; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed passage: any two short paragraphs of your own on one topic.
**Skip.** The word card and the review.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Your child reads side 2 of the page, then answers [[s04-A10]] to [[s04-A13]] alone. Give no help.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes the log line, [[s04-P135-4]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X135]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check; each ask stays. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Prevention, litter, stream, passage, view, check. Read the passage aloud again for any child who asks; record any help; extra time for the items, up to 50 minutes; a scribe; large print.

### Tomorrow

Day 136 hands the passage back with one comment each, then repairs the commonest slip among J, K and the text-detail error. It also teaches the missed word pattern from today and the plural nouns. The pages, [[s04-A10]] to [[s04-A13]], stay with the teacher for marking. The word work goes in the folder.
