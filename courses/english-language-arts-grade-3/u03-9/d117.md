# Day 117: Read a new passage aloud, first fifteen

## Teacher page

### Before class

Split the class list: the first 15 are heard today; the second 15 work alone and write the exit. Print F117, directions at the top; its key is on key-oral-forms-117-118 and is not printed. Return the day 116 retelling and note to the second 15. Fold each script-to-delivery sheet so only lines 1 and 4 show.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word retrieval

Silent. Say direction, hesitate and performance; children write each whole and circle the hardest part, [[s03-P117-1]] (6 minutes). Say their and said; children write each and mark the odd part, [[s03-P117-2]] (4 minutes). Review: direction and hesitate (day 113), performance (day 107), their (day 108). Watch for error AG.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Silent reading from the log book, alone. No conferences. Children write the log line, [[s03-P117-3]]. Watch for error AK.

#### 60 min. Oral rotation, first fifteen

Fifteen children, four minutes each, at the table with F117. Score each part 2, 1 or 0 (shown, shown with help, not yet seen) on the oral record sheet:

- *0.5 min.* Directions read silently; the child says why the reading place changes, [[s03-A46]]. Watch for error AC.
- *2 min.* The child reads F117 and again, the speech as a speaker would, [[s03-A47]]. Watch for error AB.
- *0.75 min.* Offer carrying for covered; the child checks the sentence, [[s03-A48]]. Watch for error AD.
- *0.75 min.* The child reads invitation, sudden and covered by chunks, then finds one in F117, [[s03-A49]]. Watch for errors AA and AI.

At the close of each turn, hand over written decoding feedback: one word to redivide, one sentence to reread. Children waiting read their own book.

The second 15 work alone. *5 min.* Read the note on the retelling; add a subject and a verb to each sentence, [[s03-P117-4]]. *2 min.* Return E116, reread the last action and close the scene, [[s03-P117-5]]. *5 min.* Fill lines 1 and 4 of the script-to-delivery sheet, [[s03-P117-6]]. *5 min.* Say the chosen line to a partner so the voice shows the bracket, [[s03-P117-7]]. *5 min.* The half slip, read aloud twice: [[s03-X117]] and [[s03-X117b]]. The rest is silent reading and quiet rehearsal. Watch for errors AF, V, AE, AA and AI. Collect the sheets and slips at the close.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears one page read twice and notes how the second reading differed.

### At home

**Swap.** The sheets stay at school; use the log book.
**Skip.** The word work.

- **15 min. Assessment.** Your child reads one page of their own book aloud twice, then says why the scene happens where it does. Give no hint.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book.
- **10 min. Say.** Your child says their report once at a pace you can follow.
  **Say.** "What was one fact you heard?" Expect: one fact from the report.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the four parts of each turn; shorten the alone work. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Passage, reread, chunk, blend, bracket, voice. A child who needs more time may read to the teacher alone in a quiet corner; large print; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 118 hears the second fifteen and hands back the day 117 assessment as written decoding feedback; the first fifteen revise a saved reading from it. E117 returns to the second 15, likely error AE: keep the bracket unsaid. The word work, [[s03-P117-1]] to [[s03-P117-3]], and the changes, [[s03-P117-4]] to [[s03-P117-6]], stay in the folder.
