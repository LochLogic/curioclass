# Presentation cards (days 114, 115, 116 and 119)

Print four blank cards for each child on day 114 and four more on day 115, index-card size. One sentence on each card, with a mark // where the voice pauses.

Structure cards, day 114:
- Card 1, the situation of scene 1: ____________________
- Card 2, the action that was changed: ____________________
- Card 3, the change and its effect: ____________________
- Card 4, the ending: ____________________

Key-point cards, day 115: one fact, one descriptive detail and one pause mark on each card, chosen from the retained review and the scene folder, with the title and one grammar point that helps a listener follow.

Revised for facts and detail on day 116 and read once, silently, on day 119.
