# Day 114: Chapters, scenes, stanzas and different views

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the sheet and the panel page for each child. Have the scene folder, the scene audit card and the two-book map ready. Seat children apart. At the close, hand back the day 113 assessment, the scene folder and audit card, and the G112 sheet, each with its written note.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word retrieval

Silent. Say whispering, performance and direction; children write each whole and circle the hardest part, [[s03-P114-1]] (6 minutes). Say again and their; children write each and mark the odd part, [[s03-P114-2]] (4 minutes). Review: whispering and direction (day 113), performance (day 107), again and their (day 108). Watch for error AG.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Silent reading from the log book, alone. No conferences and no teaching. Children write the log line, [[s03-P114-3]]. Watch for error AK, a log line with no date.

#### 12 min. Item 1, chapter, scene and stanza (RL.3.5)

Silent. The directions on the sheet are read silently in the first 3 minutes. Each child answers [[s03-A38v2]] from the P114t panel. Review: chapter, scene and stanza (days 89, 90, 91, 94, 96 and 97). Mark 2, 1 or 0 (shown, shown with help, not yet seen). Watch for error B.

#### 7 min. Item 2, three views (RL.3.6)

Silent. Panel P114v, [[s03-A39]]. Review: narrator and character views (days 84 and 89). Watch for error E.

#### 7 min. Item 3, author and reader (RI.3.6 and RI.3.1)

Silent. Panel P114, [[s03-A40]] then [[s03-A41]]. Review: author and reader views (day 82). Watch for errors A and E.

#### 9 min. Item 4, two books (RL.3.9)

Silent, the two-book map open. Panels P114c and P114d, [[s03-A42]]. Six claims, each judged alone. Review: theme, setting and plot (days 94 to 98). Watch for error F.

#### 17 min. Presentation cards, structure

Silent, unscored. Each child plans four cards for the day 119 report, [[s03-P114-4]]. New: the report cards, and the // mark where the voice pauses. Cards go in the scene folder. Watch for error AF.

#### 8 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X114v2]]. Support is recorded. Shown: the tomatoes Ben waited for in chapter 1 turn red and are shared in chapter 2. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What happened first?" Not yet seen: the later part first (error B) or tomatoes from a shop (error A). Record it. Collect the sheets and folders.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each item aloud on request, never a panel.

### At home

**Swap.** The folder stays at school; use the log book and the panel page.
**Skip.** The word work and the cards.

- **15 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s03-A39]] and [[s03-A41]] on paper, alone. Give no hint.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then tells you what the next chapter added to the one before.
  **Say.** "What did the second part add?" Expect: the first event, then what came after it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X114v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the items and the exit; cards wait. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Chapter, scene, stanza, narrator, author, reader, theme, setting, plot. Frames for the cards: "First ___", "Ada changed ___", "This matters because ___". Directions read aloud on request; large print; a scribe; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 115 checks the G112 title and sentences and prepares key-point cards; children read the G112 note then. s03-X114v2 comes back in day 116's Return and retrieve, likely error B: number the earlier chapter first. The word work, [[s03-P114-1]] to [[s03-P114-3]], stays in the folder.
