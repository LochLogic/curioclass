# Day 118: Read a new passage aloud, second fifteen

## Teacher page

### Before class

The second 15 are heard today; the first 15 work alone and write the exit. Print F118, directions at the top; its key is on key-oral-forms-117-118 and is not printed. Hand back the day 117 assessment: return E117 slips with notes to the second 15, and the written decoding feedback to the first 15.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word retrieval

Silent. Say whispering, direction and hesitate; children write each whole and circle the hardest part, [[s03-P118-1]] (6 minutes). Say break and great; children write each and mark the odd part, [[s03-P118-2]] (4 minutes). Review: whispering, direction and hesitate (day 113), break and great (day 107). Watch for error AG.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Silent reading from the log book, alone. No conferences. Children write the log line, [[s03-P118-3]]. Watch for error AK.

#### 60 min. Oral rotation, second fifteen

Fifteen children, four minutes each, at the table with F118, the same four parts as day 117, scored 2, 1 or 0 (shown, shown with help, not yet seen) on the oral record sheet:

- *0.5 min.* Directions read silently; the child says why the partners move, [[s03-A50]]. Watch for error AC.
- *2 min.* The child reads F118 and again, the speech as a speaker would, [[s03-A51]]. Watch for error AB.
- *0.75 min.* Offer removal for rehearsal; the child checks the sentence, [[s03-A52]]. Watch for error AD.
- *0.75 min.* The child reads collection, rehearsal and empty by chunks, then finds one in F118, [[s03-A53]]. Watch for errors AA and AI.

At the close of each turn, hand over written decoding feedback: one word to redivide, one sentence to reread. Children waiting read their own book, then read the note on E117 and fix the bracket answer once, [[s03-P118-5]] (2 minutes).

The first 15 work alone. *10 min.* Read the decoding feedback from day 117; divide the marked word into chunks and say it, [[s03-P118-4]]. *5 min.* Fill lines 1 and 4 of the script-to-delivery sheet with a new script line, [[s03-P118-7]]. *5 min.* Say the report to a partner, pausing where the cards say //, [[s03-P118-6]]. *5 min.* The exit, in writing, from the half slip: [[s03-X118]] and [[s03-X118b]]. Read each card aloud twice at the start of the five minutes. The rest is silent reading. Watch for errors AA, AI, AE and AN. Collect the sheets and slips at the close.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears one page read twice and notes how the second reading differed.

### At home

**Swap.** The sheets stay at school; use the log book.
**Skip.** The word work.

- **15 min. Assessment.** Your child reads one page of their own book aloud twice, then says why the scene happens where it does. Give no hint.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book.
- **10 min. Say.** Your child says their report once to you.
  **Say.** "What does the script do with a bracket?" Expect: the voice shows it, and the bracket words are not said.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the four parts of each turn; shorten the alone work. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Passage, reread, chunk, blend, bracket, voice. A child who needs more time may read to the teacher alone in a quiet corner; large print; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 119 is the report window and hands back the day 118 assessment as written decoding feedback. The first 15 read back E118, likely error AE: keep the bracket unsaid. The word work, [[s03-P118-1]] to [[s03-P118-3]], and the changes, [[s03-P118-5]] and [[s03-P118-7]], stay in the folder.
