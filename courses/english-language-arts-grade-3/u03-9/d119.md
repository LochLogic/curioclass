# Day 119: Present the scene reports

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make six groups of five. Set the table. Hand back the day 118 assessment: the written decoding feedback from days 117 and 118, and the E118 slips to the first 15. Have each child's presentation cards ready.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word retrieval

Silent. Say performance, hesitate and whispering; children write each whole and circle the hardest part, [[s03-P119-1]] (6 minutes). Say again and their; children write each and mark the odd part, [[s03-P119-2]] (4 minutes). Review: performance (day 107), hesitate and whispering (day 113), again and their (day 108). Watch for error AG.

#### 20 min. Independent apply

Silent. Each child reads the decoding feedback from day 117 or 118, rereads the marked sentence, then divides the marked word and says it, [[s03-P119-3]] (5 minutes). The first 15 also fix the bracket answer on E118 once, [[s03-P119-4]] (2 minutes). The rest is quiet rehearsal, [[s03-P119-5]]. Watch for errors AA, AE and AN.

#### 3 min. Silent preparation

Each child reads the cards once.

#### 42 min. Report window

Six groups of five, seven minutes each, at the table. Each child, scored 2, 1 or 0 (shown, shown with help, not yet seen) on the report record sheet:

- *1 min each, 5 minutes.* A report of one revised action in the scene and its effect, with a pause where the script marks one, [[s03-A54]]. Watch for error AN.
- *12 seconds each, 1 minute.* One clarifying question from the teacher: "What does Ada do with the page?" The child answers in a full sentence, [[s03-A55]]. Watch for error AF.
- *12 seconds each, 1 minute.* The child says how the script's pause is spoken and why the word is skipped, [[s03-A56]]. Watch for error AE.

The other 25 continue the independent apply. The day 117 and 118 oral records and the day 116 rehearsal are reused, not rescored. A child may report to the teacher alone.

#### 5 min. Same-day feedback

Each child reads the teacher's note and marks one change, [[s03-P119-6]]. Watch for error Q.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X119]]. Support is recorded. Shown: The names tell a listener which place is which. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the listener need?" Not yet seen: a fragment (error AF) or a vague answer (error Q). Collect the sheets and slips.

#### 5 min. Reflection

Each child writes or says one thing the scene taught about checking and one to do next time, [[s03-P119-7]]. Never scored.

### Other rooms

One child: the parent hears the report and asks one question.

### At home

**Swap.** The folder stays with the teacher.
**Skip.** The word work.

- **15 min. Assessment.** Your child tells you one thing they changed in their scene, then answers your question "Why does that change matter?" in a full sentence. Give no hint.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X119]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the report and the exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Report, fact, detail, pace, pause, clarify. Frames: "First I changed ___", "This matters because ___". Cards may be drawn and spoken; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 120 begins the water book and the clean freshwater question. The scene folder, cards and reflection go home or to the class library; the records stay with the teacher. The word work, [[s03-P119-1]] and [[s03-P119-2]], the fix, [[s03-P119-4]], the note, [[s03-P119-6]], and the reflection, [[s03-P119-7]], stay in the folder.
