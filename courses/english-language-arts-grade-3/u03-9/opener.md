# Section 3.9 opener: transfers, a review and a report (days 114 to 119)

## What the section does

The last six days check what the unit taught and ask for a spoken report. Day 114 is a transfer check on how later chapters, scenes and stanzas build on earlier ones, on different views, and on comparing two stories; children also plan presentation cards. Day 115 audits the title and a short grammar sheet and prepares key-point cards. Day 116 rehearses the scene report. Days 117 and 118 are oral readings of a new passage, half the class each day. Day 119 is the report window, with same-day feedback and a short reflection.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Days 114 and 115 are silent check days at the desk. Day 116 holds teacher listening while the class reads and rehearses. Days 117 and 118 hold four-minute turns at the teacher's table for fifteen children while the others work alone. Day 119 holds six groups of five at seven minutes each.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Parts build.** A later chapter, scene or stanza answers, completes or changes an earlier one.
- **Views are different.** The narrator, a character, the author and the reader can each judge differently; label whose words show which.
- **Likeness and difference.** A comparison names both, from the page.
- **A title has capitals by rule.** Important words start with capitals; small words stay lowercase unless first.
- **A word class has a job.** Name what the word does, not only its name.
- **Speaking differs from writing.** A script's bracket becomes a pause or a voice, never read aloud.
- **A report is for a listener.** Facts, a descriptive detail, a pace a listener can follow, in complete sentences.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, B, E, F:** An answer not in the text; later part first; one view given for another; likenesses only. Repair: underline support, number the earlier part, label whose words, add a contrast. 5 to 7 minutes.
- **R and AM:** Title capitals. Repair: compare with the title model. 5 minutes.
- **X, Y and Z:** Function not named; agreement missed. Repair: say what the word does, then match subject and referent. 7 minutes.
- **AA, AB, AC and AD:** Guessing from a first letter, rushing past a pause, not explaining the event, accepting a word that clashes. Repair: in the 119 independent apply time. 5 minutes.
- **AE, AF and AN:** A direction spoken; a fragment; a report said in one breath. Repair: bracket the direction, add a subject and a verb, mark the pause points. 5 minutes.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored. Oral and report records stay with the teacher.
