# Day 115: Titles, word jobs and key points

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the sheet for each child. Hand back the day 114 assessment with its notes. Have each child's day 112 G112 sheet with its feedback, the day 114 transfers, the day 106 review with its audit card, and blank key-point cards. The fresh lines are on the student page, not the G112 sheet.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word retrieval

Silent. Say hesitate, whispering and performance; children write each whole and circle the hardest part, [[s03-P115-1]] (6 minutes). Say break and great; children write each and mark the odd part, [[s03-P115-2]] (4 minutes). Review: hesitate and whispering (day 113), performance, break and great (day 107). Watch for error AG.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Silent reading from the log book, alone. No conferences and no teaching. Children write the log line, [[s03-P115-3]]. Watch for error AK.

#### 5 min. Read the G112 note

Silent. Each child reads the note on the G112 sheet and changes one thing in the title and one thing in a sentence, [[s03-P115-4]]. The first version stays beside. Watch for error L, a neater copy.

#### 20 min. G112 audit and revision

Silent. First 5 minutes: finish any row of the G112 table left open (s03-P112-8). Then the fresh lines are written and scored: the title, [[s03-A43]] (1 minute); the word jobs, [[s03-A44]] (7); the agreement sentences, [[s03-A45]] (7). Every child gets the full 15 minutes. Review: title capitals (days 102 and 104); word class jobs (days 86 and 110); agreement (days 110 and 112). Mark 2, 1 or 0 (shown, shown with help, not yet seen). Watch for errors R, AM, X, Y and Z.

#### 27 min. Cards, key points

Silent. *5 min.* Each child checks the retained day 106 review against the audit card; reused, not rescored (it rests on day 104 feedback and day 105 revision). *10 min.* Each child changes one thing in one day 114 answer, the first kept beside it, [[s03-P115-5]]. *12 min.* Key-point cards, [[s03-P115-6]], then the top and last cards, [[s03-P115-7]]. New: the key-point card, one fact, one descriptive detail and a // pause mark. Watch for error AF.

#### 8 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X115]]. Support is recorded. Shown: The Cat on the Garden Wall. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which small words stay lowercase?" Not yet seen: every word lowercase (error R), or On with a capital (error AM). Record on the record sheet. Collect the folders.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each item aloud on request.

### At home

**Swap.** The folder stays at school; use the log book.
**Skip.** The word work and the card edits.

- **15 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s03-A43]] and [[s03-A45]] on paper, alone. Give no hint.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then says what changed in their scene and why it matters.
  **Say.** "What changed, and why does it matter?" Expect: one changed action and its effect.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X115]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the fresh lines and the exit; one card only. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Title, capital, noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, key point. Frames: "This word tells ___", "A fact: ___", "A detail: ___". Directions read aloud on request; large print; answers by mouth to a scribe; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 116 rehearses the scene report and hands back the day 115 assessment with E112 to E115, each with its commonest letter: U, Z, B and AM. The word work, [[s03-P115-1]] to [[s03-P115-3]], the changes, [[s03-P115-4]] and [[s03-P115-5]], and the cards stay in the folder.
