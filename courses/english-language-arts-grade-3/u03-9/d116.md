# Day 116: Rehearse the scene report

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the sheet for each child. Hand back the day 115 assessment. Return the four exit slips, E112, E113, E114 and E115, each with its commonest letter beside the answer: U, Z, B and AM. Fold the class list in two: the first 20 are heard during independent reading, the last 10 during the rehearsal. Keep a pencil and the report record at the table. Seat children apart.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Silent. Say explain, pause and reread; children add -ing to each and write it, [[s03-P116-1]] (6 minutes). Say said and again; children write each and mark the odd part, [[s03-P116-2]] (4 minutes). Review: the ending rules (days 81 and 107), again (day 108). Watch for errors AH and AG.

#### 20 min. Independent reading, with rehearsal listening

Silent reading from the log book. The teacher sits at the table and listens to the first 20 children, a minute each, say their report from the cards, [[s03-P116-6]]. Each child comes when called. Written feedback follows class: one fact heard, one place to slow. No log conferences today. Watch for error AN.

#### 5 min. Return and retrieve

Return the four slips. Each child fixes each answer once, the first kept beside, [[s03-P116-3]] (4 minutes). Then the class says what a listener needs a report to give: facts, a detail and a pace (1 minute). Watch for errors U, Z, B and AM.

#### 20 min. Reading and rereading

Children reread scene 1 and scene 2 of the play. They mark two facts, one descriptive detail and one place to pause, and leave the stage directions unspoken, [[s03-P116-4]]. Review: stage direction, a note in brackets, not spoken (days 107 and 108). Watch for error AE.

#### 20 min. Rehearse the report

*10 min.* Each child revises the cards for facts and detail and marks // where the pace slows, [[s03-P116-5]]. *10 min.* Each child rehearses aloud at the desk. The teacher listens to the last 10 children, a minute each, [[s03-P116-6]]. These 30 one-minute turns are preparation and are not scored. Review: a report in complete sentences at a pace a listener can follow (day 108).

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each, 1 minute to change over: say the report once at a pace the partner can follow; the partner says one fact heard, [[s03-P116-7]]. Any child may pass and say it to the teacher alone later.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Each child writes one sentence about the script that shows what hesitate means, [[s03-P116-8]]. Review: hesitate (days 108 and 113). Watch for error AF.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X116]]. This brief scene retelling is product N10, and it replaces the usual response. Support is recorded. Shown: events in order with a time word, and an ending that closes the scene. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What happens first, then last?" Not yet seen: a fragment (error AF), or a new problem opened at the end (error V). Record on the record sheet. The retelling and cards go to the teacher; a written note comes back before day 117.

### Other rooms

One child: the parent listens and says one fact heard.

### At home

**Swap.** The cards stay at school; use the log book.
**Skip.** The word work.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book.
- **15 min. Say.** Your child says their report once, slowly, to you.
  **Say.** "Tell me one fact and one detail." Expect: a fact about the scene and a detail you can picture.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X116]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the return, the rehearsal and the exit; skip the word in context. Longer: a second pair talk with a new partner.

### Words and supports

Rehearse, report, fact, detail, pace, pause. Frames: "First I changed ___", "This matters because ___", "I pause here because the script says ___". Cards may be drawn and spoken; a child who needs more time may report to the teacher alone; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 117 listens to the first fifteen children read a new passage. The teacher's written note on the retelling returns then, with s03-X116, likely error V or AF: reread the last action and close it, or add a subject and a verb. The cards and the retelling go to the teacher today. The word work, [[s03-P116-1]] and [[s03-P116-2]], the fixes, [[s03-P116-3]], the marks, [[s03-P116-4]], the cards, [[s03-P116-5]], and the sentence, [[s03-P116-8]], stay in the folder.
