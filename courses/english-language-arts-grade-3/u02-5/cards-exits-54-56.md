# Exit cards, days 54 to 56

None of these exits is invented: each asks on the frog book. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 54 (s02-X54v2).** No insert card: the exit is on the frog book. Open the frog book's contents and index. Ask: Which page would you open to learn what webbing does for a frog?
- **Day 54 (s02-X54bv2).** No insert card: the exit is on the frog book. Open the frog book's contents and index. You found a page for webbing. Ask: Name the part of the book that told you the page.
- **Day 55 (s02-X55v2 and s02-X55bv2).** No card: both ask on page 4 of the frog book (items.csv).
- **Day 56 (s02-X56v2).** No card: the exit asks on page 6 and the page 7 diagram (items.csv).
