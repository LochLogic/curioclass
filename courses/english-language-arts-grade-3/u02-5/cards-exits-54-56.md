# Exit cards, days 54 to 56

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 54 (s02-X54).** This card is invented for practice. Open the frog book's contents and index. Ask: Which page would you open to learn what webbing does for a frog?
- **Day 54 (s02-X54b).** This card is invented for practice. Open the frog book's contents and index. You found a page for webbing. Ask: Name the part of the book that told you the page.
- **Day 55 (s02-X55).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Dry weather lasted all month. This made the stream shrink. Fish gathered in the last deep pool." Ask: Say what the word This points back to in the second sentence.
- **Day 55 (s02-X55b).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Dry weather lasted all month. This made the stream shrink. Fish gathered in the last deep pool." Ask: Say how the first two sentences connect.
- **Day 56 (s02-X56).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "A seed soaks up water and swells. Next, the root comes out. Later, a shoot grows up." Diagram: three small drawings numbered 1, 2 and 3 with arrows. Ask: Explain in two sentences how the seed changes. Use a time word and the diagram.

Day 56 diagram: three small plain drawings in a row: a dry seed in soil (1), a seed with a thin root coming out (2), a small plant with a short shoot (3), with an arrow from each to the next.
