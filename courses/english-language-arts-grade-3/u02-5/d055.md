# Day 55: Close-read the amphibian section: what This points to, and how sentences connect

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the book copy for each child. Mark four names for conferences and three for the spoken exit on the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 54's exit (s02-X54 and s02-X54b), error V (a page from the wrong part of the book): children judge two ways of finding streams, [[s02-P55-1]]. *7 min.* Review: -ment and -ous (days 53 and 54). Say movement, enjoyment, famous and nervous; children write each on a slate, [[s02-P55-2]]. Map through: children mark the letters that do not sound the way they look, [[s02-P55-3]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children read from their logs. Hold four conferences of 4 minutes: the child reads four sentences twice, smoothly, [[s02-P55-11]]. Add the four to the heard-six column. Last 4 minutes: each child writes one log line.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* On slates children name the stage that comes right after the egg, [[s02-P55-4]]. *3 min.* Read What Is an Amphibian? (pages 3 and 4) aloud, leaving out the lines about what animals eat. *6 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: how does the word This refer to the sentence before it? New: a word like This or That can point back to what came before. Pairs find two sentences on page 3 that begin with a pointing word and circle it, [[s02-P55-5]]; then judge a claim about what This points to, [[s02-P55-6]]. *3 min.* Class share.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*4 min.* Model amphibian, moist and double life from the book's own sentences. *6 min.* Each child writes what moist means in their own words, with the sentence from page 3 that shows it, [[s02-P55-7]].

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs, 2 minutes a child: given the main idea "Amphibians need water or damp places to live", mark the two details that support it, [[s02-P55-8]]; say one aloud; the partner checks it against the page. *6 min.* Three volunteers, 2 minutes each, say the detail and the words that show it.

#### 20 min. Writing

Each child writes yes or no to the cause and result claim, [[s02-P55-9]]; then writes a short paragraph: the main idea, then the two details just marked, [[s02-P55-10]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hear three children from the heard-six column, one at a time, about 90 seconds each: read the strip aloud twice and ask the first card, [[s02-X55]]; then read the second card, [[s02-X55b]]. Each card is first seen at the child's own turn. Six more are heard on day 56 and six on day 57, in the Talk part; the rest are seen on [[s02-P55-6]] and [[s02-P55-9]]. The rest read silently. Shown: This points to the dry weather, and dry weather is the cause and the stream shrinking is the result. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What happened first?" Not yet seen: a stream or fish named, or two sentences with no link.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the exit aloud on day 55. No printer: copy the pages by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed book: read page 3 from the screen.
**Skip.** The conference and the spoken exit.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child reads pages 3 and 4 aloud to you.
  **Say.** "What does the word This point to in one place?" Expect: the sentence just before it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X55]] and [[s02-X55b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: circle one pointing word, not two. Longer: write a second paragraph on skin or eggs.

### Words and supports

Amphibian, moist, detail, cause, result. Preview moist with a wet cloth; frames: "This points back to ___." "The cause is ___ and the result is ___." Large print; extra time; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X55]] and [[s02-X55b]], error Y (no arrow from cause to result): draw the arrow from the sentences. The book copy goes to day 56; the paragraph stays in the folder.
