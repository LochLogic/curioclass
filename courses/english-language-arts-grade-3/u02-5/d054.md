# Day 54: Use headings, the index and a diagram to answer questions about frogs

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the book copy for each child, and a lookup card for each pair. Mark six names on the heard-six column for the spoken exit. Mark three pairs to listen to in the rereading.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 53's exit (s02-X53v2), error B (one word instead of a whole idea): children mark the whole idea, [[s02-P54-1]]. *7 min.* Review: the ending -ous says /us/ (grade 2). New: it means full of, or having. Show famous on the board: full of fame, [[s02-K54-1]]. Say famous, nervous and dangerous; children write each on a slate, [[s02-P54-2]]. Map double from page 1 of the book: children mark the part that does not sound the way it looks, [[s02-P54-3]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Review: comparisons (grade 1) and verb forms. Children write the word on the board, [[s02-P54-4]], and the two past forms, [[s02-P54-5]]. *15 min.* Partners reread stanza three of Under the Leaf twice, smoothly, saying one thing they heard, no speed target, [[s02-P54-6]]. Listen to three pairs, one child at a time for about 45 seconds each; the rest give feedback in turn.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*2 min.* Children order egg, tadpole and frog on slates, [[s02-P54-7]]. *4 min.* Read From Egg to Frog (pages 5 and 6) aloud, tracing each arrow on the page 7 diagram and naming first, then, next. *2 min.* Children find the words that say why a tadpole needs water (it has gills and breathes underwater). *5 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: where does a label explain an arrow? Pairs find the arrow from the eggs to the tadpole and underline its sentence on page 5, [[s02-P54-8]]. *2 min.* The class names them. Read the line on page 7 that says why the diagram shows no numbers.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*2 min.* Review: a heading tells what a section is about (grade 1): the heading on page 5 names the life cycle, and the contents says page 5. *5 min.* Review: contents and index (grades 1 and 2). Model two ways to find a page. Contents: sections in order with their first pages; index: small topics in order with every page. Judge two ways of finding the amphibian part, [[s02-K54-2]]; then trace gills in the index: pages 5 and 6. *3 min.* Children write contents or index for each question, [[s02-P54-9]], and box the entry for a question about metamorphosis, [[s02-P54-10]].

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs, 2 minutes a child: write a question the book can answer, [[s02-P54-11]], and ask it; the partner finds the page in under a minute and writes the page and the words on the lookup card. *6 min.* Three volunteers, 2 minutes each, ask the class.

#### 20 min. Writing

Each child checks three claims with the index and writes yes or no with the pages, [[s02-P54-12]], [[s02-P54-13]] and [[s02-P54-14]]. The three questions of the plan are replaced by these, so no practice repeats the exit.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X54v2]]. While they start, hear the six named children, 45 seconds each, one at a time: read the second card, [[s02-X54bv2]]. Shown: page 8 or 17, and the index named. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which part lists every page?" Not yet seen: the contents alone, or a page with no support. The other children are heard on day 55.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult sits apart and hears the rereading. No printer: copy the index by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed book: use the page numbers on the screen.
**Skip.** The spoken exit and the lookup card.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child uses the contents and index to find a page about gills and reads it aloud.
  **Say.** "Which part of the book told you the page?" Expect: the index.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X54v2]] and [[s02-X54bv2]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: answer two claims, and name the third for day 55. Longer: write a question that needs two pages and find both.

### Words and supports

Contents, index, heading, diagram, famous. Preview index with the book open; frames: "The index lists ___ on page ___." Large print; extra time; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X54v2]] and [[s02-X54bv2]], error V (a page from the wrong part): choose a heading or an index entry from the question. The book copy goes to day 55. The sheets stay in the folder.
