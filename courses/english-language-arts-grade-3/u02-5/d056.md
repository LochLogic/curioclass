# Day 56: Explain in a paragraph how a tadpole changes, with time and cause links

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the answer sheet and the panel A sheet for each child. Return the day 52 tales and the repair pages. Mark two names for conferences on the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 55's exit (s02-X55v2 and s02-X55bv2), error Y (no arrow from cause to result): children mark the cause in a given sentence, [[s02-P56-1v2]]. *7 min.* Review: -ment and -ous (days 53 to 55). Say amusement, agreement, joyous and poisonous; children write each on a slate, [[s02-P56-2]]. Map because: children mark the letters that do not sound the way they look, [[s02-P56-3]].

#### 20 min. Skills: tale revision and reading

*8 min.* Each child puts the one change the note asked for into the day 52 tale, using the day 53 repair page, [[s02-P56-4]]; it is scored with the tale. *8 min.* Hold two conferences of 4 minutes: the child reads three sentences twice, smoothly, [[s02-P56-5]]. Add the two to the heard-six column. *4 min.* Log reading.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Review: time words (kindergarten). On the page 7 diagram, name the time links first, then, next and at the same time; children point to each as it is named. *3 min.* Read pages 6 and 7 aloud and show the diagram. *6 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: how do the diagram and the words show the same change? Pairs point to the arrows and to the sentence about the tail. *3 min.* Class share.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Model joining two facts with so and with while; also and another add one more fact. The teacher marks the two joins, [[s02-K56-1]] and [[s02-K56-2]]. *5 min.* Review: you joined facts with and and but in grade 1 (days 40 and 42), grade 2 (Both/But) and grade 3 (days 27 and 32). Children join four pairs on slates, with and, but, because and while, [[s02-P56-6]], [[s02-P56-7]], [[s02-P56-8]] and [[s02-P56-9]].

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: say the topic sentence and two facts you will use; the partner names the time link they heard (4 minutes). While the pairs talk, then through the rest of the part, hear six children not yet heard on day 55's exit, one at a time, 90 seconds each: show page 4 and read each passage aloud twice, then ask its item (9 minutes).

#### 20 min. Writing

Silent. *5 min.* Hand out the answer sheet; children open the book to pages 8 and 9, not yet read in class. Read nothing aloud. Children answer the three end items alone, [[s02-A36v2]], [[s02-A37v2]] and [[s02-A38v2]]. *15 min.* Then hand out the panel A sheet; each child writes one paragraph on how a tadpole changes, using Panel A and its diagram, with a topic sentence, facts and a definition, linking words and a closing sentence, [[s02-P56-10]]. One sitting. Record each part on the record sheet: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Children open pages 6 and 7; read the ask aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X56v2]]. Shown: two sentences, a time word, and an order that matches the last two drawings, froglet then grown frog. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the froglet do first?" Not yet seen: one sentence, or the order reversed.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult sits apart and reads nothing aloud. No printer: copy Panel A by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed sheet: draw the four stages on lined paper.
**Skip.** The exit.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child tells you how a tadpole changes, in order, using first, then and at the same time.
  **Say.** "What caused the tail to get shorter?" Expect: the body takes the tail in as the frog grows.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X56v2]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the topic sentence and two facts; record the rest as not reached. Longer: add a sentence on why the pond matters to a tadpole.

### Words and supports

Tadpole, froglet, gills, lungs, cause. Large print; the panel read aloud for a child who needs it, except on the end items; a scribe, with spelling left as written where it is the evidence. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 hears the exit, [[s02-X56v2]]. The paragraph, [[s02-P56-10]], and the revised tale, [[s02-P56-4]], go to the teacher for written feedback and come back on day 57. The slate and board work, [[s02-P56-1v2]] to [[s02-P56-3]] and [[s02-P56-6]] to [[s02-P56-9]], stays in the folder.
