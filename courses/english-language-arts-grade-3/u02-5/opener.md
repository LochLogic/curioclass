# Section 2.5 opener: finding facts in the frog book (days 54 to 56)

## What the section does

For three days the class uses the frog book as a tool. Day 54 finds facts with the contents, the index and a life-cycle diagram. Day 55 reads the amphibian section closely and says what a word like This points back to. Day 56 revises the day 52 tale with the one change its note asked for, and writes one explanatory paragraph from a short panel and its diagram.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 54 practises a question, the index entry, the page and the words. Day 55 reads pages 3 to 4, names the main idea and a detail, and writes a short paragraph. Day 56 joins facts with linking words and writes the Product I05 paragraph in one sitting from a panel and its diagram.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A book has a map.** The contents shows sections, the index shows pages for a topic, and the diagram shows an order.
- **A pointing word points back.** This stands for the idea in the sentence before.
- **A cause comes before its result.** Say which is which, then link them.
- **A diagram and its words say one thing.** Use both.

## The errors, in plain words

- **V:** A search ignores useful text features. Repair: Choose a heading or search term from the question. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **A:** A fact is asserted without source support. Repair: Locate the exact source sentence. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Y:** A causal relationship is reversed. Repair: Draw a cause-to-result arrow from the source. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **B:** A topic replaces a complete central idea. Repair: Join two key events into a complete lesson statement. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Z:** A paragraph connection is named without checking content. Repair: Compare each paragraph's job before choosing the connection. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **AJ:** An explanation ignores the illustration's evidence. Repair: Join one visible detail to one textual detail. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Q:** An ending introduces an unrelated topic. Repair: Close the established problem or explanation. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
