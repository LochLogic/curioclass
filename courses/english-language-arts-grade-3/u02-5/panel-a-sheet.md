# Panel A sheet, day 56

Print one for each child with the diagram below the panel, then ruled lines for the paragraph.

Panel A: "Many frogs begin as eggs in water. Tadpoles hatch. Gills allow these young animals to breathe underwater. Later, legs and lungs develop while the tail is absorbed. The stages do not all take the same length of time."

Diagram: egg, tadpole, froglet with legs and a short tail, adult, with arrows.

Product I05: write one paragraph on how a tadpole changes. Use a topic sentence, facts and a definition, linking words, and a closing sentence.
