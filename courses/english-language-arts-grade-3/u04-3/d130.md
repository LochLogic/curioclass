# Day 130: Read about pollution and talk about what the texts show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, sections 7 and 8 of the water book and the town text for each child. Print six discussion records, one for each group of five. Make the groups before class; mix who sits with whom. Return the day 129 slips with one comment each. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column. Choose two groups to listen to in the discussion.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P130-1]]. Review: you split long words into chunks from day 81 on; you learned -ous on day 54 and protect on day 127. *3 min.* They add -ing to three words, [[s04-P130-2]]. Review: you dropped a silent e and doubled a last letter on day 33; carry keeps its y. *3 min.* Say believe and above twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P130-3]]. Review: you met believe on day 98. New: above has o saying /u/ and ve saying /v/. Watch for a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P130-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying what they read and one detail that fits, [[s04-P130-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P130-6]]. Fill the heard-six column. Start with children not heard on day 128. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 129 page with a tick on each written task. The commonest letter on the day 129 exit is likely error C, two sentences with no link. Show a pair of short sentences on the board and ask: which gives the cause, and which the result? Say it with the answer first, then the word that joins them. Children reread the slip comment and mark one thing to fix. *2 min.* Partners say the three discussion norms to each other, [[s04-P130-7]]. Review: you used look, wait and link in the formal discussions on days 98 and 105. Point to the five boxes on the record: passage, wait, ask, link, what the talk taught.

#### 15 min. Read and prepare

*3 min.* Read section 7 aloud once, then unpack it: rain runs over the ground and carries things into water; harmful things in water are pollution. *6 min.* Children read sections 7 and 8 silently. They may also use the town text from days 126 and 127. *6 min.* Model first: read one sentence from section 8 aloud, "Fixing a leaky pipe helps, too." Say what it shows, "small jobs can save water", and ask a peer a question: "What do you mean by small jobs?" Then each child marks one passage in section 7 or 8, or in the town text, and copies its first four words, [[s04-P130-8]]. The passage must be one they can read aloud and explain.

#### 15 min. Formal discussion

*3 min.* Groups of five set the norms: one speaker at a time, look at the speaker, wait for the floor. *10 min.* Each child takes a 2-minute turn: read your passage and say what it shows, [[s04-P130-9]]. Others may ask one question about the passage, [[s04-P130-10]], and the speaker answers. Anyone may link their own idea to a peer by name, [[s04-P130-11]]. Frames: "Can you say more about ...?" and "I agree with ... because ...". Listen to two groups with the discussion record and tick each box as it happens; that is ten children heard. *2 min.* Each child says what the talk taught them, [[s04-P130-12]]. Passing is fine, and no one is held up as the example of a mistake. Children not heard today are recorded on day 132.

#### 15 min. Write (N07)

*2 min.* Remind children of the label: write "Invented." at the top, because the episode is made up and is not a research note. Review: you wrote a labeled invented episode on day 128. *11 min.* Each child writes a short episode, [[s04-P130-13]]. One event and one fact from section 8. Children who need it may draw the episode and label each picture. *2 min.* Children reread and point to the word invented and to the fact. Watch for a claim written as a research fact, error AP. Collect the episodes for the folder.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read the sentence from the glossary: pollution is something harmful in water. Children draw one example and label it, [[s04-P130-14]]. *2 min.* Pairs show each other the drawing and the label and say what is harmful in it. Review: you used a glossary on day 18.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X130]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a question that asks for more detail about the claim. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What would you like to know more about?" Not yet seen: a yes-or-no question only (error AJ) or a change of subject (error AH). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the log conference, then takes the part of the group; the child reads a passage and the adult asks "Can you say more?". The child writes the episode and the exit on paper. One projector: show sections 7 and 8 one at a time; groups talk in six places in the room.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed sections: read any short account of keeping water clean.
**Skip.** The word in context.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads their own book and writes the log line, [[s04-P130-4]].
  **Say.** "What did you notice?" Expect one fact.
- **10 min. Talk.** Your child reads one passage from section 7 or 8 and says what it shows. Ask: "Can you say more?"
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X130]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the discussion, the episode and the exit; cut the suffix work. Longer: link to two peers by name in the discussion, and write a third sentence in the episode.

### Words and supports

Pollution, protect, solution, passage, norm, invented. Frames: "Can you say more about ___?" and "I agree with ___ because ___." A word strip of the three norms; sections 7 and 8 may be read aloud again for any child; large print; a drawing may carry the episode with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 131 is the check on two new passages: reading, a question, a main idea, two details and a short grammar strip. It is silent and unscored on the day. Today's exit tells you who needs a prompt for asking a question. Day 132 hands the check back.
