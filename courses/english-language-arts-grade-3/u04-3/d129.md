# Day 129: Read the population excerpt and compare it with the book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the excerpt for each child, and one chart for each pair. Pairs are fixed: one child takes the book column, one the excerpt column. Hand back the day 128 slips with one comment each; the four-category sheets come out of the folder. Choose three pairs to listen to in the skills block. Write on the board, ready to cover: housing developments, roads, shopping areas, factories, land disturbed, stress on water.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P129-1]]. Review: you split long words into chunks from day 81 on, and population on day 128. Growth has one chunk. *3 min.* They add -ion to three words, [[s04-P129-2]]. Review: you learned -tion and -sion on day 41, and added -ion on day 128. *3 min.* Say again and move twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P129-3]]. These are new: again has ai saying /e/; move has ve saying /v/ and o saying /oo/. Watch for a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Thursday

*5 min.* Pronouns. Review: you learned on day 86 that a pronoun stands in for a noun, and that it matches the noun, one thing or many, on day 88. Children write it or they in two gaps, [[s04-P129-4]]. *10 min.* Partner rereading: each child reads section 6 of the water book aloud twice, with the partner listening, 5 minutes each way, [[s04-P129-5]]. Review: you did partner rereading with the feedback card on day 2. Listen in on three pairs, 2 minutes each; that is six children heard. Give one comment on phrases or expression, not on speed. *5 min.* Each child ticks the feedback card for their partner and says one thing that went well, [[s04-P129-6]].

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 128 page with a tick on each written task. The commonest letter on the day 128 exit is likely error AQ, a note filed by one word. Say: match the note to the question each category asks. Access asks who can reach water; demand asks how much people want; pollution asks what harms the water; solutions asks what people do. Children check their two notes on the four-category sheet against those questions, reread the slip comment and mark one thing to fix. *2 min.* Each child says the main idea of section 6 to a partner, 1 minute each, [[s04-P129-7]].

#### 25 min. Read and compare

*5 min.* Preview three words. Slew is a large number. Development is building on land; review: you added -ment to develop on day 57. Population is all the people in a place; review: you split it on day 128. *2 min.* Read the excerpt aloud once, slowly. *6 min.* Unpack each clause on the board with the covered words. More people move in: a large number of things must be built, homes, roads, shops and places for work. Building disturbs the land. And the water is under stress, because everyone must be supplied. Children say each clause back in their own words. The excerpt is dense; children do not decode it alone. *4 min.* Children reread it silently with the board beside them. *8 min.* Pairs fill the chart. First, model one line on the board from section 4 of the book: "People use water at home, on farms and in factories (book)". Each child then fills their own column, [[s04-P129-8]]. Together the pair writes the shared point in the middle row, [[s04-P129-9]]. Review: you found a shared point as an idea both texts make, not a shared word, on day 126. Leave the charts in the folder.

#### 15 min. Write

*5 min.* Each child says two sentences to a partner, one starting "Both" and one telling a difference, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over. *10 min.* Each child writes the two sentences, [[s04-P129-10]] and [[s04-P129-11]]. Review: you wrote a "Both texts" sentence on day 127. Check each sentence against the texts: point to the line. Watch for a claim neither text makes, error A, and for two shared words with no idea, error F.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child reads the difference sentence to a partner, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P129-12]]. Passing is fine.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read the slew sentence in the excerpt again. Children write one or many, [[s04-P129-13v2]]. *2 min.* Say which words in the sentence show it: a list of many things. Slew is not a verb here. Review: you used a sentence clue on day 18.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the passage aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X129v2]]. Support is recorded. Shown: one sentence with so or because that puts the cause first. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which happened first?" Not yet seen: a sentence with no link (error C) or the result put first (error E). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the excerpt aloud and unpacks it, hears the rereading and gives the feedback; the child fills both columns of the chart on paper. The exit stays on paper. One projector: show the excerpt one paragraph at a time with the covered words; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed excerpt: read any short account of a growing town together.
**Skip.** The partner talk and the word in context.

- **15 min. Read.** Read section 6 aloud twice, taking turns.
  **Say.** "What is it mainly about?" Expect a sentence about the whole page.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes the Both sentence and the difference sentence, [[s04-P129-10]] and [[s04-P129-11]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X129v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chart, one sentence and the exit; cut the grammar. Longer: add a second key detail for each text.

### Words and supports

Population, development, slew, growth, stress, supply. Frame: "Both say ___ , but the book says ___ and the excerpt says ___." The excerpt is read aloud again for any child; large print; a drawing may carry a detail with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 130 reads about pollution and holds the first formal discussion. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X129v2, likely error C: say which sentence is the cause and which the result. Then children say the discussion norms: look, wait, link. The two-column chart stays in the folder.
