# Day 128: Read about demand and write notes for the four categories

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the four-category sheet and section 6 of the water book for each child. Return the day 127 exit slips with one comment each. Draw four boxes on the board: access, demand, pollution, solutions, with a plain picture of a tap, a crowd, a dirty stream and a bucket. Write the four headings of sections 5 to 8 on a strip. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P128-1]]. Review: you split long words into chunks from day 81 on, and community into four chunks on day 127. Population has four chunks; pollution has three. *3 min.* They add -ment or -ion to four words, [[s04-P128-2]]. Review: you learned -ment on day 53, and -tion and -sion on day 41. Agree keeps both e letters before -ment. *3 min.* Say because and water twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P128-3]]. Review: you mapped water on day 61. New: in because, the au says /o/ and the se says /z/. Watch for a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P128-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one thing noticed that fits it, [[s04-P128-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P128-6]]. That is six children heard. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on. A child who is not heard today is heard first on day 130.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 127 page with a tick on each written task. Children read the comment on the day 127 slip. The commonest letter is error B, one step given as the whole idea. Put the passage's four lines on the board and test one wrong sentence against each line: does it cover every line? *2 min.* New idea for notes: a research note says where its fact came from. Review: you wrote a note card with a source line on day 57. Pairs tell each other how a note shows its source, 1 minute each, [[s04-P128-7]]. Say: the tag goes after the note, in brackets, like (book).

#### 15 min. Read and sort

*5 min.* Read section 6 aloud once (2 minutes); children reread it silently (3 minutes). Review: you learned demand on day 123. Supply is new today: the amount of water that is available. *4 min.* Model with the strip of four headings on the board. For each heading, ask which category it belongs under and say the category by its job, not by one word in the heading: access is reaching water, demand is how much people want, pollution is something harmful in water, solutions is what people do to help. Then write one demand note on the board with its tag: "Homes need water (book)". Say: a note is a few words, under eight. *6 min.* Each child writes two notes on the four-category sheet from section 6, one for the Demand box, [[s04-P128-8]], and one for the Solutions box, [[s04-P128-9]]. Look for a demand fact in the Solutions box, error AQ.

#### 25 min. Write (N06)

*4 min.* Model a label and a first line on the board. Write "Invented." at the top, then: "At the school garden, the hose gave only a trickle." Say: invented means made up. It is a story, so it never goes in the research notes. Review: you wrote an invented episode on day 95. *18 min.* Each child writes a short episode, [[s04-P128-10]]. One event, one family, one fact from section 6. Children who need it may draw the episode and label each picture. *3 min.* Children reread. Each points to the word invented at the top and to the fact from section 6. Watch for a claim written as a research fact, error AP. Collect the episodes for the folder.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child reads the first line of the episode to a partner, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P128-11]]. Passing is fine.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read the sentence in section 6 that starts "Supply is". Children circle the words that tell what supply means here, [[s04-P128-12v2]]. *2 min.* Read the glossary entry for supply and check it against the circled words. Review: you used a sentence clue and then the glossary on day 18. Supply as a noun only; the verb is not taught today.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X128]]. Then they turn to the exit box on the page and write alone, [[s04-X128b]]. Support is recorded. Shown: pollution, and a word that is in the note. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which word in the note shows it?" Not yet seen: access because of creek (error AQ), or a word the note does not hold (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the log conference and the first line; the child writes the notes and the episode on paper. The exit stays on paper. One projector: show section 6 and the four boxes; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed section: use any short account of water.
**Skip.** The partner talk and the word in context.

- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads their own book and writes the log line, [[s04-P128-4]].
  **Say.** "What did you notice?" Expect one fact.
- **15 min. Notes.** Read section 6 together. Your child writes the Demand note and the Solutions note, [[s04-P128-8]] and [[s04-P128-9]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X128]] on paper, alone, then [[s04-X128b]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the notes, the episode and the exit; cut the suffix work. Longer: write one more note for the Access box from the book, with its tag.

### Words and supports

Access, demand, pollution, solutions, supply, source tag, invented. Frame: "A note for ___: ___ (book)." A word strip of the four categories with pictures stays on the desk; section 6 may be read aloud again; large print for the cards; a drawing may carry the episode with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 129 reads the population excerpt and compares it with the book. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X128, likely error AQ: match the note to the category question before filing it. Then children say the main idea of section 6 aloud. The four-category sheet stays in the folder.
