# Section 4.3 opener: sorting facts and talking about them (days 128 to 132)

## What the section does

Five days teach children to sort brief notes into four categories, to see how one sentence explains another, to take part in a formal group discussion, and to name what a word does in a sentence. Day 131 is a check on two new passages, unscored, with grammar on a short strip. Day 132 returns it and ends with an opinion paragraph.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 128 sorts notes and writes a note card. Day 129 reads a short government excerpt and compares it with the book. Day 130 prepares and holds a formal discussion in groups with a five-box record. Day 131 is silent: reading, items, a silent review and an exit. Day 132 returns the passages, repairs the commonest slip, discusses and ends with an opinion paragraph.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A note is brief and says where it came from.** A few words with a source tag, filed under one category.
- **One sentence can explain another.** Find which sentence gives the cause and which gives the result.
- **A discussion needs preparation.** Bring a located passage, wait for the floor, ask about a peer's claim and link your own.
- **A word has a job.** A noun names, a pronoun stands for a noun, a verb acts, an adjective describes, an adverb tells how.
- **Subject and verb agree, and a pronoun matches its noun.** Say singular or plural before choosing.

## The errors, in plain words

- **F:** Gives only a shared point or only differences. Repair: rebuild a two-row table, shared row first.
- **G:** Names a word class without saying its job. Repair: say the job before the name.
- **H:** Uses a verb that does not match its subject. Repair: find the subject and match the verb.
- **I:** Uses a pronoun that does not match its noun. Repair: draw an arrow back to the noun.
- **AP:** Treats an invented episode as a research fact. Repair: label it invented and keep it out of the notes.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored and a check day stays unscored on the day.
