# Day 132: Use the check, then talk and choose an issue to explain first

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, sections 6 to 8 of the water book and the excerpt for each child. Print six discussion records. Make the groups before class; put children not heard on day 130 first in the order. Sort the day 131 pages (P131) by the commonest letter on the comparison and word-job items: F, G, H or I. Make at most four repair groups. Write on the board: fact, invented.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P132-1]]. Review: you split long words into chunks from day 81 on. *3 min.* They add -s or -es to three words, [[s04-P132-2]]. Review: you learned the -s and -es rule on day 122; carry changes y to i. *3 min.* Say group and eight twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P132-3]]. Both are new: group has ou saying /oo/; eight has eigh saying /ai/. Watch for a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Tuesday

*5 min.* Word jobs. Review: you learned on day 86 what a noun, a pronoun, a verb, an adjective and an adverb do. Children write what two words tell us in one sentence, [[s04-P132-4]]. Say the job first, then the name. *10 min.* Partner rereading: each child reads the first paragraph of section 8 aloud twice, with the partner listening, 5 minutes each way, [[s04-P132-5]]. Review: you did partner rereading with the feedback card on day 2. Listen in on three pairs, 2 minutes each; that is six children heard. Give one comment on phrases or expression, not on speed. *5 min.* Each child ticks the feedback card for their partner and says one thing that went well, [[s04-P132-6]].

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return day 131's assessment, the pages P131, and the exit slips with one comment each. Children read the comment silently and mark one thing to fix, [[s04-P132-7]]. Hand back the day 130 page and the day 131 work with a tick on each written task. Say: the check was unscored, so this is a first look and not a grade.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Groups by the commonest letter on the day 131 page, at most four groups. *2 min.* Each group reads the right line on its own copy. *6 min.* Each child redoes the one item for the group, a different case from the day 131 page. Groups are F, G, H and I.

- Group F, [[s04-P132-8v2]]. Say: write the shared point first, then the difference.
- Group G, [[s04-P132-9]]. Say: say what the word does before its name.
- Group H, [[s04-P132-10]]. Say: find the subject, say one or many, then match the verb.
- Group I, [[s04-P132-11]]. Say: draw an arrow back to the noun.

*2 min.* Each child compares the redo with a partner from the group. Review: you did return and repair on day 125. Do not reopen the day 131 passages.

#### 5 min. Model

Show the error to watch: an invented note treated as a fact. Put a fact note beside an invented line on the board: "Demand is how much water people want" and "Mina's well sang to her". Say: invented is fine in a story, with its label; it never goes in the research notes. Review: you labelled your episodes on days 128 and 130. Children label four lines fact or invented, [[s04-P132-12]]. Pass the page to a partner and check; no child is named as the one who missed.

#### 5 min. Prepare

Each child marks one passage from sections 6 to 8 to bring to the discussion, and copies its first four words, [[s04-P132-13]]. It must be one they can read aloud and explain.

#### 10 min. Discussion

Groups of five, 2 minutes a turn. Each child reads the passage and says what it shows about access, demand or pollution, [[s04-P132-14]]. Others may ask one question about the passage, [[s04-P132-15]], and link their own idea to a peer by name, [[s04-P132-16]]. Listen to two groups with the discussion record, starting with children not heard on day 130; that is ten children. The last box is filled on day 141. Passing is fine, and no one is held up as the example of a mistake. Children not heard today are heard first on day 141.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read the sentence about an issue. Children circle the words that tell what an issue is, [[s04-P132-17]]. *2 min.* Read the entry on the board and check it against the circled words: an issue is a problem or topic people talk about. Review: you used a sentence clue and then a glossary on day 18.

#### 10 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the cards aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X132]]. Support is recorded. Shown: an opinion, two reasons from the cards, a link word and a closing sentence. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the card say?" Not yet seen: a shared point only (error F), liking a card (error M), no closing (error O) or a claim not on the cards (error A). Record on the record sheet. This is O09; keep it in the folder.

#### 10 min. Reread

Each child reads section 6 aloud to a partner, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P132-18]]. Then each says the main idea of the excerpt to the partner, [[s04-P132-19]]. Each child writes one detail from section 6 or the excerpt with its source tag, [[s04-P132-20]]. Passing is fine.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hands back the day 131 page, hears the rereading and takes the part of the group; the child does the repair item for the commonest letter and the exit on paper. One projector: show the cards one at a time; children write on paper and groups talk in six places.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed sections: read any short account of a water problem together.
**Skip.** The group repair and the word in context.

- **15 min. Read.** Read section 6 aloud twice, taking turns.
  **Say.** "Which issue about water should a class explain first, and why?" Expect an opinion with a reason.
- **10 min. Notes.** Your child writes one detail with its source tag, [[s04-P132-20]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X132]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the discussion and the exit; cut the long words. Longer: write a third reason in the exit, or link to two peers by name.

### Words and supports

Issue, fact, invented, passage, opinion, reason. Frames: "I think ___ should come first because ___. For example, ___. So ___." Sections 6 to 8 may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the cards; a drawing may carry a reason with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 133 begins the next section. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X132, likely error F: write the shared point first, then the difference. The episodes and the opinion piece go in the folder.
