# Day 131: Check two new water passages for points, details and word jobs

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, with the check passages (P131) on side 2. Tell children to keep side 2 face down until the passage part. The word strip is on side 1 under a fold line. This is a check: no help with the items, no scoring today. Do not read the passages before the passage part. Return the day 130 exit slips with one comment each as children sit; nothing from them is repaired today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word retrieval

Say each of eight words twice; children write them, [[s04-P131-1]]. Then they fold the page along the line, unfold it, read the eight words on the strip silently and mark the hardest, [[s04-P131-2]]. Unscored. Do not give a spelling.

#### 20 min. Silent independent reading

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P131-3]]. Move among them; no conferences. Record who read the whole time.

#### 10 min. Read the panels

Children turn to side 2. *6 min.* They read The Repair List and The Check List silently. *4 min.* Read both aloud once. Children mark nothing.

#### 40 min. Items

One ask at a time, in order. Say only the ask. *15 min.* [[s04-A5]]. *8 min.* [[s04-A7]]. *7 min.* [[s04-A6]]. *10 min.* [[s04-A8]] and [[s04-A9]]. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Unscored this time.

#### 5 min. Independent review

Children reread their own answers and fix one thing beside the first answer, [[s04-P131-4]]. Collect the pages.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X131]]. Support is recorded. Shown: Shade. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does steadily keep the path cool?" Not yet seen: sunlight (error I). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each passage aloud once and the child answers on paper. The items stay on paper. One projector: show each passage, then the page.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed passage: any two short accounts of one topic of your own.
**Skip.** The word dictation and the review.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Your child reads side 2 of the page, then answers [[s04-A5]] to [[s04-A9]] alone. Give no help.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes the log line, [[s04-P131-3]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X131]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check; each ask stays. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Dependable, repair, committee, treatment, inspect, report. Read the passages aloud again for any child who asks; record any help; large print.

### Tomorrow

Day 132 hands the page back with one comment each, then repairs the commonest slip on the comparison and the word jobs. The pages, [[s04-A5]] to [[s04-A9]], stay with the teacher for marking. The word work, [[s04-P131-1]] and [[s04-P131-2]], goes in the folder.

### Side 2

**A. The Repair List.**

The water committee wants a dependable supply. A damaged pipe has stopped water reaching one collection point. Residents list repairs and choose which work to do first. They post the list at the collection point so everyone can see it. They explain that repairing the pipe can bring the collection point back into use.

**B. The Check List.**

The plant workers want a dependable supply. A damaged pump has stopped water entering one treatment tank. Workers list repairs and choose which work to do first. Workers record each repair in a log so the next shift knows what was done. They explain that repairing the pump can bring the treatment tank back into use.

Strip on page B: The careful workers inspect the pump slowly. They report the damage.
