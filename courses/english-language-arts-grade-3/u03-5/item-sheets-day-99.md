# Item sheets, day 99

Print the two passages on one page, then the items on a second page, for each child. Silent. The books are open to those pages. Read an item aloud on request, never a passage. Items 1 and 2 are written on the sheet; item 3 is written on the child's day 97 recount, revised in the independent preparation block with the first version beside it.

## Passage A (P99a)

Ada spread her drawing on the classroom floor. "I kept it hidden because I wanted it right," she said. Ben traced the arrow toward the closed cupboard. "May we try it before everyone comes?" Ada handed him the pencil. They walked from the door to the reading corner, stopping at the cupboard. Ada turned the arrow. "A question can find what a careful drawing misses," she said. When the others arrived, they followed the changed route together.

## Passage B (P99b)

Ada stood in the courtyard with her seating drawing. "I put everyone under the tree," she said. Ben held up his book. "But I cannot hear my partner when everyone talks there." Ada lowered her pencil. "Where would you choose?" Ben pointed to the far bench. Together they marked one place for partners and another for quiet reading. "A question can improve a careful drawing," Ada said. "And one place need not suit everyone." The readers chose their places.
