# Day 98: Which book first? Reasons from the changes, and a talk

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a reasons list and prepared card and a talk slip for each child, and the discussion record. Make six groups of five and post the order. Put the discussion moves chart where all can see it. Draw four boxes on the board: opinion, reason, link word, closing line. The day 97 recount sheets are with you: write one note on each tonight; they go back on day 99.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say opinion, exciting and family. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P98-1]]. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81, and you wrote opinion on day 89. Then believe, [[s03-P98-2]]: mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. Review: you met believe in kindergarten, days 23 and 57. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P98-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P98-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter. *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk, the other says what excited means, then they swap, [[s03-P98-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AI, opinion split o-pi-nion, and error AA, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P98-6]]. Review: you filled this line on day 95. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each). Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 97 slips and exit box (s03-X97v2, s03-P97-1v2). Error G, an event given as the message: write Ada moved the yellow beanbag beside asking others helps a plan work, and ask which one would still be true in another story. Then a fresh pair on the board, [[s03-P98-7v2]]. *2 min.* Retrieve opinion. Review: in grade 2 you gave an opinion and a reason joined by because. Pairs pick the stronger of two reasons for liking apples and say why, [[s03-P98-8]]. Hear six. Watch for error I, the opinion said again as its reason.

#### 20 min. Teaching and preparation

*8 min.* Teach the four boxes on the board. An opinion says what I think. A reason says why, and it comes from a change in the books, not from "it is good". A link word joins them: you know because; today add since, which opens the reason, and therefore, which comes after it. A closing line goes back to the opinion. Fill the boxes for book 1 and book 2, [[s03-K98-1]]. Opinion: I recommend book 1 first. Reason with since: since Ada hides her plan and later asks everyone to check it, readers see her change. Reason with therefore: in book 2 she says yes to Ben's help at once; therefore the change that began in book 1 lasts. Closing: that is why I would give book 1 to a friend first. Ask which box holds the event and which holds what Ada thinks.
*6 min.* On the reasons list, children write their pick and one prepared reason from a change in an arc, with since or therefore, [[s03-P98-9]], then one question for a partner that links to the reason, [[s03-P98-10]]. Review: you asked a linked question on day 89. Watch for error I, and for error A, an event neither book holds.
*4 min.* Pairs rehearse, 2 minutes each child: the reason, [[s03-P98-11]], then the question, [[s03-P98-12]]. Watch for error O, a question that does not link.
*2 min.* Restate the discussion moves chart and the roles at the table. Review: you learned the five moves in unit 1.

#### 30 min. Formal discussion window

Six groups of five, 4 minutes each at the teacher's table, with 1 minute to change groups (30). Review: you held a discussion window on day 89. The question: which book would you recommend first to a friend, and why? Each child says the reason and asks the linked question, [[s03-P98-13]], then answers a classmate's question, [[s03-P98-14]]. Record three boxes for each child live on the discussion record: turn, linked question, answer. Mark each box alone, with help or not yet seen. After class, fill the other two boxes, marked detail and what it clarified, from the reasons list. Watch for error N, speaking over a classmate. A late group is not extended past the window; note who was not reached and hear them on day 100.
The other 25 write at their seats; each child is away from the seat for one 4-minute turn. First, 5 minutes, the word in context, [[s03-P98-17]]. Review: in grade 2 you learned that a reason tells why. Children write a sentence about a book that uses reason. Then, 15 minutes, two reasons for their pick on the reasons list, one from each book's arc, one with since and one with therefore, [[s03-P98-15]]. When a child is back and the reasons are written, they fill the talk slip, [[s03-P98-16]]: what the talk changed or kept. Watch for error P, the same idea with no learning named. Group 6 returns as the part ends and fills the talk slip in the first 2 minutes of the exit part.

#### 5 min. Exit

Group 6 fills the talk slip first, 2 minutes. Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X98]]. Shown: an opinion, because, and an event from the card that fits the reader. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What happens in the book that proves it?" Not yet seen: the opinion said again (error I), or an event the card does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the rehearsal partner and the discussion partner, and asks one linked question. One projector: show the four boxes and the discussion moves chart. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No book 2: any two books your child knows.
**Skip.** The slates, the conferences and the group talk.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Recommend.** Your child tells you which book they would give a friend first, and writes one reason, [[s03-P98-9]].
  **Say.** "What happens in the book that shows why?" Expect: an event, such as Ada hides her plan and later asks everyone to check it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X98]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the four boxes, the two reasons and the exit; cut the slate words. Longer: write a third reason that starts with for example.

### Words and supports

Opinion, reason, recommend, since, therefore, closing line. Frames: "I recommend ___ because ___." "Since ___, ___." "___; therefore, ___." Audio beside print; the reason said aloud before it is written; large print for the reasons list; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 99 is a check: skills retrieval only, then two printed passages with both books open, six comparison claims and one question, with no teaching. The day 97 recount comes back with one note in the last block. Day 100 repairs error I from the exit, s03-X98. The word work, [[s03-P98-1]] to [[s03-P98-5]], the log work, [[s03-P98-6]], the spoken work, [[s03-P98-4]], [[s03-P98-7v2]], [[s03-P98-8]], [[s03-P98-11]] to [[s03-P98-14]] and [[s03-K98-1]], and the writing, [[s03-P98-9]], [[s03-P98-10]], [[s03-P98-15]], [[s03-P98-16]] and [[s03-P98-17]], stay in the folder. The discussion record goes to the teacher's file.
