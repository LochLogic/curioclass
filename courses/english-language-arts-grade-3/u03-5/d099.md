# Day 99: Check: two passages, theme, setting and plot

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the packet for each child. Open the book 1 copy at chapter 11 (the paragraph from "Ada spread her drawing") and the book 2 copy at chapter 5 (its first paragraph). Say aloud: "These passages are made up for practice." Answer nothing about content. Have the day 97 recounts, each with its note, for the last block.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, retrieval only

No new pattern and no teaching. *10 min.* Long-word retrieval on paper: six words in chunks and whole, [[s03-P99-1]], then six irregular words, [[s03-P99-2]]. *5 min.* Ending rules on slates, [[s03-P99-3]]. *15 min.* Independent reading from the log book, then the log line, [[s03-P99-4]]. No conferences.

#### 15 min. Source reading

Silent. Children reread P99a and P99b on side 1 of the packet, with both books open to those pages and their finished maps beside them. Answer nothing about content.

#### 30 min. Items

Silent, on paper. Read an item aloud on request, never a passage. Item 4, [[s03-A10]], 22 minutes: a likeness and a difference for theme, setting and plot, six claims. Item 5, [[s03-A11]], 8 minutes. Mark each of the six claims on its own, with the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record the support used. Collect the packets; write one note on each tonight.

#### 10 min. Independent preparation

Return each day 97 recount and message sheet with its note. Children revise it with the one-note, one-change card, [[s03-P99-5]]: one change in meaning, the first version kept beside it. Recopying with no change is error L. The revised recount is scored once from the work handed in, [[s03-A12]], and is not part of the 30 minutes.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X99v2]]. Shown: one likeness and one difference in one sentence with but. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What is the same? What is different?" Not yet seen: likenesses only (error F), or an event the books do not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each item aloud on request and writes what the child says, marking it as read aloud. No printer: write P99a, P99b and the items on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The slate line.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Your child reads P99a and P99b, then answers [[s03-A10]] and [[s03-A11]] alone on paper. Read an item aloud if asked, never a passage. Give no hint.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then tells you how two stories they know are alike and different.
  **Say.** "What is the same, and what is different?" Expect: one likeness and one difference, joined with but.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X99v2]] on paper, alone. The recount, [[s03-A12]], is revised at school.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the items and the exit; cut the slate line. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Theme, setting, plot, alike, different. Frame: "In both, ___, but in one ___ and in the other ___." Large print for the passages; extra time inside the 90 minutes; answers by mouth; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 100 hands back day 99's assessment, the packet with its note, and repairs error F, a likeness with no difference, from the exit, s03-X99v2, and error I from the day 98 exit, s03-X98. Then children study a complete printed review and write a brief recommendation. The word work, [[s03-P99-1]] to [[s03-P99-3]], the log work, [[s03-P99-4]], and the revision, [[s03-P99-5]], stay in the folder.

> P99a. Ada spread her drawing on the classroom floor. "I kept it hidden because I wanted it right," she said. Ben traced the arrow toward the closed cupboard. "May we try it before everyone comes?" Ada handed him the pencil. They walked from the door to the reading corner, stopping at the cupboard. Ada turned the arrow. "A question can find what a careful drawing misses," she said. When the others arrived, they followed the changed route together.

> P99b. Ada stood in the courtyard with her seating drawing. "I put everyone under the tree," she said. Ben held up his book. "But I cannot hear my partner when everyone talks there." Ada lowered her pencil. "Where would you choose?" Ben pointed to the far bench. Together they marked one place for partners and another for quiet reading. "A question can improve a careful drawing," Ada said. "And one place need not suit everyone." The readers chose their places.
