# Day 100: Use the note, then study a book review and write one

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a sheet O01 for each child. Have the day 99 packets with their notes, and the day 98 and day 99 exit slips. Put the title model where all can see it. Mark nothing in the books. Pick the four children for conferences from the table turns not reached on day 98, then the rest by the heard-six column. Choose three children to share a label.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say revising, capital and idea. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P100-1]]. Then pretty and whole, [[s03-P100-2]]. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81; you wrote busy and believe on day 98. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P100-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P100-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i. *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk, the other says what important means, then they swap, [[s03-P100-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AI, a word split so a chunk cannot be blended.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P100-6]]. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each); the first go to any child not reached at the table on day 98: hear the reason and the linked question, then answer a classmate's. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Returned feedback

Hand back day 99's assessment, the packets with the written note on each, from items s03-A10 to s03-A12. Children read the note and mark the one change they will make, [[s03-P100-7]]. The packets are scored once, from the work handed in; the repair and the change are not scored again.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

*2 min.* Whole class, error F, from the day 99 slips (s03-X99v2): write both are about Ada and both are in school beside both are school places, but one is indoors beside a cupboard and the other is outdoors by a tree. Ask what the second says that the first does not: a difference. *6 min.* Groups by error letter, while everyone else works in pairs: swap packets, find a likeness with no difference and write the difference beside it with but, [[s03-P100-8]]. Error I, from the day 98 slips (s03-X98), 3 minutes: write I recommend it because it is a good story beside I recommend The Moss Path because Dev asks every hiker for one clue, and ask what happened in the book that proves it. Then two more groups, 1.5 minutes each, for the commonest letters on the packets (error G, a detail given as a theme, and error A, an event neither book holds). *2 min.* Close in pairs: each child says the move they used, [[s03-P100-9]].

#### 25 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Read the printed review aloud once (about 150 words). Children follow on side 2. *8 min.* Pairs label it with four colours, [[s03-P100-10]]: opinion, reasons, link words, closing. Review: in grade 2 you named these parts, and on day 98 you used them for book 1. Expect: the opinion in the first sentence, two reasons after First and Second, the link words First, Second, As a result, Also, and the closing after For these reasons. *8 min.* Model one revision on the weak review beneath it, [[s03-K100-1]]: read the note, make one change, keep the first beside it. Review: one note, one change, from day 93. *3 min.* Children copy the series title and the two book titles from the title model for their heading, [[s03-P100-12]]. This is copying only; the title rule is taught on day 102. *3 min.* Hear three children read a label aloud and say the words it marks, [[s03-P100-11]]. Watch for error K in the weak review's last line, an unrelated thought.

#### 10 min. Writing

Say before they write: a reason says why, with an event; a reason that repeats the opinion adds nothing (error I). One sitting, on sheet O01: a heading, an opinion, two reasons from changes in the books with a link word, and a closing line that goes back to the reasons, [[s03-P100-13]]. The sheets go to the teacher. Watch for error L, a recopy of the day 98 reasons with no change.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child. Read your recommendation, [[s03-P100-14]]. Your partner names the strongest reason and says where it is from, [[s03-P100-15]]. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X100]]. Shown: one closing line that names a kind of reader and fits the reasons on the card. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What are the two reasons about?" Not yet seen: a line unrelated to the reasons (error K). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the pair partner and the talk partner, and writes one note on sheet O01 before the child changes one thing. One projector: show the review and the title model. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No coloured pencils: underline each part differently.
**Skip.** The slates, the conferences and the repair groups.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you one reason they would give for a series they like.
  **Say.** "What happens in the books that shows it?" Expect: an event from the books, not just "it is good".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X100]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the labels, sheet O01 and the exit; cut the slate words and the share. Longer: write a recommendation of the books for a different reader.

### Words and supports

Review, recommend, reason, link word, closing, heading. Frames: "I recommend ___ because ___." "First, ___. Second, ___." "For these reasons, ___." Audio beside print; large print for the review; the recommendation told aloud before it is written; record any help.

### Side 2

> The Lantern Lane Friends. Book 1: The Lost Key. Book 2: The Big Swap. I recommend The Lantern Lane Friends to readers who like friends solving problems together. First, the main character changes from one book to the next. In The Lost Key, Mina keeps every worry to herself, but in The Big Swap she asks her friends for help. This shows that a character can grow. Second, the problems get bigger. The lost key only closes one small shed, while the swap changes the whole street. As a result, I wanted to read both books in one weekend. Also, the setting stays the same, so I could notice what was different. For these reasons, you should start with The Lost Key and then read The Big Swap.
