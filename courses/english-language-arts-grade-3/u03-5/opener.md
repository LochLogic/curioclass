# Section 3.5 opener: a recommendation and a check (days 98 to 100)

## What the section does

For three days the class turns from comparing to recommending. Children prepare a reason from a change in the books and a question for a partner, discuss which book to recommend, sit a check on theme, setting and plot, and then study a complete printed book review and write a brief recommendation of their own.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 98 teaches the parts of an opinion piece and runs a formal discussion: six groups of five meet at the teacher's table for five minutes each while the others write two reasons. Day 99 is a check: skills retrieval only, two printed passages with the books open, six comparison claims and one question, then a revision block for the day 97 recount. Day 100 returns the packet and studies a model review.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **An opinion says what I think; a reason says why.** A reason repeated as the opinion adds nothing.
- **A link word joins them.** Because, since, for example and therefore should join ideas that really relate.
- **A closing line returns to the opinion.** It may say who might like the books.
- **A comparison has six claims.** A likeness and a difference for each of theme, setting and plot, each from the page.
- **A revision changes meaning.** One note, one change, and the original stays beside it.

## The errors, in plain words

- **I:** Repeats the opinion as its reason. Repair: Explain what feature makes the recommendation sensible. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **F:** Gives similarities without differences. Repair: Add a contrasting detail in each comparison dimension. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **G:** Treats a single event as a theme. Repair: Explain what the event suggests beyond itself. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **K:** Ends with an unrelated thought. Repair: Return the ending to the recommendation. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **L:** Treats neat recopying as revision. Repair: Change one explanation in response to feedback. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
