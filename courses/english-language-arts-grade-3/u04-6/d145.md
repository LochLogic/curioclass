# Day 145: Check a revised essay, plurals and abstract nouns

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child. Put each day 144 essay, with its written feedback, on the desk; the moves for L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, AO and AQ are written on it. This is a check: no help with the items, and nothing is taught.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Say each of eight words twice; children write them, [[s04-P145-1]]. Then they look at the strip on the page and circle the hardest, [[s04-P145-2]]. Unscored. Do not give a spelling.

#### 20 min. Skills block, check day

*15 min.* Children read their own book silently. *5 min.* They write one log line, [[s04-P145-3]]. No conferences. Record who read the whole time.

#### 10 min. Revise the essay

Hand back the day 144 essay with its written feedback, which is day 144's assessment, items s04-A14 to s04-A19. Hand back the day 144 tasks s04-P144-1, s04-P144-2, s04-P144-3 and s04-P144-4, and the exit slip s04-X144, with a tick on each. Hand back the day 143 exit slip, s04-X143, with one comment: name the relationship before the link word. Children read the feedback, replace one link word with one that fits its reason, and mark it. Then they answer [[s04-A20]]. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Plural audit

Children turn to side 2 and answer [[s04-A21]]. Say only the ask.

#### 10 min. Abstract noun audit

Children answer [[s04-A22]]. Say only the ask.

#### 10 min. Trace the process

Children open the essay folder and answer [[s04-A23]]. Say only the ask.

#### 10 min. Independent audit

Children reread the revised essay with the look-for card of four and tick the four rows, [[s04-P145-4]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X145]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: a sentence that drops the false reason and uses the source words. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the source say?" Not yet seen: the draft recopied, error T, or a link word that misstates, error N. Record on the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Reflection

Children write one sentence, [[s04-P145-5]]. Collect the student pages, the essays and the exit slips.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult gives each ask and sits by; no help. The items stay on paper. One projector: show nothing; the work is on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No essay folder: use any earlier draft and its comment.
**Skip.** The word dictation.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Your child reads the comment on the essay, changes one link word, then answers [[s04-A20]] to [[s04-A23]] alone. Give no help.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes the log line, [[s04-P145-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X145]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check; each ask stays. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Revise, feedback, link word, plural, abstract noun, folder. Read each ask aloud again for any child who asks; a scribe for the sentences; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 146 begins the next section. It opens with the written moves from today's feedback, five minutes on each pile, then children give a short spoken announcement. The essay, the feedback and the folder stay with the teacher. The word work, [[s04-P145-1]] and [[s04-P145-2]], goes in the folder.
