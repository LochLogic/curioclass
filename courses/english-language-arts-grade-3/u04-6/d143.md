# Day 143: Give a peer look-for and revise the essay

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child and one peer look-for strip for each pair. Put the day 142 drafts, with the written feedback, on the desks. Write on the board: name the relationship first, then the link word. Choose five children to confer with; start with children not conferred with on day 142. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Say hop and hopping, then hope and hoping. Say: a short vowel and one letter after it, so the letter doubles. Children add -ing to hop and tap, [[s04-P143-1]]. Review: you added -ing to run on days 130 and 137. Watch for no doubling, error AR. *5 min.* Review: in kindergarten and grade 2 you knew were by heart. Say were and many twice each; were has ere said /er/, many has a said /e/. Reread each in a sentence. Children write both, [[s04-P143-2]]. Watch for a word spelled by its sound, error AS.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P143-3]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child naming a book, a question and one detail, [[s04-P143-4]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P143-5]]. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return the day 142 exit slip, s04-X142, with one comment (3 minutes): each child reads the comment and compares the close with the draft on the card, error O. Return the exit box, s04-X142b, with one comment (2 minutes): say wolf and wolves together, glass and glasses together, errors R and AO. Hand back the day 142 page with a tick on each task and the draft with its written move for O, R or AO.

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: name the relationship first. A reason answers why; a result tells what follows. Write on the board: which relationship does your link word show? Children read a pair and write the word, [[s04-P143-7]]. Say: because shows a reason; so shows a result. Review: you used so and because on day 141. Watch for the relationship misnamed, error N.

#### 15 min. Peer critique

Pairs. Each child reads the essay aloud to the partner in phrases, 2 minutes each, [[s04-P143-8]] (4 minutes). The partner fills the peer look-for strip, 3 minutes each, [[s04-P143-9]]: the link word heard, how the reason and the opinion are related, and yes or not yet (6 minutes). Each child asks one question about the essay, [[s04-P143-10]], and answers the partner's with a detail, [[s04-P143-11]], 2 minutes each (4 minutes). One minute to change over. Say the norms: look, wait, link. Review: you asked for a detail and linked to a peer on day 141. Every child may pass. No child is held up as the example of a mistake.

#### 25 min. Revise and publish

Children make one change from the peer look-for and one from the teacher, and write each beside the line it changes. Then they fix the one thing the written move names and mark it, [[s04-P143-6]]. Review: you revised from a comment on day 140. They edit plurals against the plural strip, then copy the essay neatly. Confer with five children for 4 minutes each (20 minutes) while the rest write; the talk is a note, not a mark. Watch for a copy with nothing changed, error T. The published essay and the strip go in the folder.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence about revising. Children write what revise means in it, [[s04-P143-12]]. Check the answers against the sentence. Review: you revised a draft on day 140.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X143]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a new sentence that joins the notes to the opinion with a link word that fits. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the source say?" Not yet seen: the draft recopied, error T, or a link word that misstates, error N. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner, hears the essay, fills the strip and asks one question; the child revises and answers the exit on paper. One projector: show the strip once; the pair work is one pair with the parent; the work is on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strip: the parent says the link word heard and whether it is a reason or a result.
**Skip.** The long words.

- **15 min. Look-for.** Your child reads the essay aloud to you.
  **Say.** "Which link word did I hear, and how are the reason and the opinion related?" Expect because for a reason, or so for a result. Your child changes one sentence, [[s04-P143-6]].
- **10 min. Word.** Say hop and hopping; your child adds -ing to hop and tap, [[s04-P143-1]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X143]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the peer look-for, the revision and the exit; cut the long words and the word in context. Longer: ask your partner a second question and answer it with a source detail.

### Words and supports

Relationship, reason, result, link, revise, peer. Frames: "The link word I heard is ___." "It shows a ___." "Where does the source say ___?" A printed strip and look-for card; the plural strip; extra time for the copy; the peer note may be written; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 144 is a check day: nothing is taught. Children choose two sources from the research folder and write a new essay alone in one sitting. The published essay and the strip stay in the folder. The day 143 exit slip goes back on day 145.
