# Section 4.6 opener: the opinion essay, finished and checked (days 142 to 145)

## What the section does

Four days finish the water opinion essay. Children draft a closing sentence, edit plurals, give and take one peer look-for, and publish the essay. Then two check days follow: an essay written alone in one sitting on 144, and a revision of it on 145 after written feedback. Everything that was taught earlier in the unit is used again; nothing new is introduced on a check day.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Days 142 and 143 are working days: a long-words block, a skills block, then drafting, peer talk and revision. Days 144 and 145 are check days with silent routines, no teaching, and short audits of what was learned. Each day ends with a five-minute exit on paper. Exits are unscored; the check-day items are scored from the work itself.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A conclusion goes back to the opinion.** It does not start a new topic.
- **A link word names how two ideas relate.** Because shows a reason; therefore shows a result. Name the relationship first, then choose the word.
- **Revision answers feedback.** A clean copy with nothing changed is not a revision.
- **Some plurals are irregular.** Say the one and the more than one together before editing.
- **An abstract noun names an idea, not a thing.** Thirst and cleanliness name ideas.
- **Sustained writing leaves a trail.** A plan, a draft and a revision, each dated, are the evidence.

## The errors, in plain words

- **L:** Gives a topic with no opinion. Repair: add the choice the essay will defend.
- **M:** Supports an opinion only with liking. Repair: replace liking with a source reason.
- **N:** Uses a link word that says the wrong thing. Repair: name the relationship first.
- **O:** Ends with something unrelated. Repair: return to the opinion in the last sentence.
- **P:** Reports a preference as something learned. Repair: name one thing the sources showed.
- **Q:** Copies a whole source. Repair: shorten to a few words and a source tag.
- **R:** Adds a plain s to an irregular plural. Repair: say the one and the more than one together.
- **AO:** Writes s where es is needed. Repair: compare a short and a long plural.
- **S:** Names a thing as an abstract noun. Repair: ask whether you can touch it.
- **T:** Copies the draft out clean and calls it revised. Repair: show one change made in answer to feedback.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored on every day of this section; the independent essay and the revision are scored from the work, with the support used recorded.
