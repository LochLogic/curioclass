# Exit cards, days 142 to 145

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 142 (s04-X142), Exit.** Invented for practice. Draft: "Clean cups are worth explaining. A clean cup keeps germs away." Ask: Write one closing sentence that fits this draft.
- **Day 142 (s04-X142b), Exit, second card.** Invented for practice. Line: "Two wolf rested by the road. Two glass sat by the sink." Ask: Write the line again with both plural words right.
- **Day 143 (s04-X143), Exit.** Invented for practice. Source: "Inspection found the damaged seal." Draft: "The seal was found damaged, therefore inspection causes damage." Peer: "Your connector reverses the reason." Ask: Write one new sentence that fixes the link.
- **Day 144 (s04-X144), Exit, unscored.** Invented for practice. Opinion: "Our class should explain refill stations first." Reason: "A station lets children fill a bottle at school." Ask: Write one sentence that joins them with a link word.
- **Day 145 (s04-X145), Exit, unscored.** Invented for practice. Draft: "Pumps matter because they create rain." Feedback: "The source does not say that." Source: "Pumps move available water." Ask: Write the corrected sentence.

Every exit sheet prints the card and the ask only, with a ruled space for the answer. The look-fors live in items.csv and the record sheet, never on the child's page.
