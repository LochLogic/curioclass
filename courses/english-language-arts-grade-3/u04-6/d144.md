# Day 144: Check an opinion essay written alone

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and two essay sheets for each child. Put each research folder on its desk. Tell children to keep side 2 face down until the essay part. This is a check: no help with the items, and nothing is taught. Day 143 ended with its exit; nothing from it is repaired today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Say each of eight words twice; children write them, [[s04-P144-1]]. Then they fold the page along the line, unfold it, read the eight words on the strip and mark the hardest, [[s04-P144-2]]. Unscored. Do not give a spelling.

#### 20 min. Skills block, check day

*15 min.* Children read their own book silently. *5 min.* They write one log line, [[s04-P144-3]]. No conferences. Record who read the whole time.

#### 10 min. Source selection

Children open the research folder, choose two sources and mark the notes to use, [[s04-P144-4]]. Say only the ask.

#### 35 min. Independent essay (O05)

Read the prompt on side 2 aloud once. Children write on the essay sheets, one part at a time, then mark it. Say only the ask. *10 min.* They write the opening and the opinion, then answer [[s04-A14]]. *15 min.* They write the reasons with a source fact each, then answer [[s04-A15]]. *5 min.* They join each reason to the opinion with a link word, then answer [[s04-A16]]. *5 min.* They write the close, then answer [[s04-A17]]. The essay is the product; it is kept for feedback, not scored from the page alone. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Research audit

Children show the question and one thing learned from the folder, [[s04-A18]].

#### 5 min. Notes audit

Children show brief notes with their sources, sorted into access, demand, pollution and solutions, [[s04-A19]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X144]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: a sentence that joins the opinion and the reason with a link word that shows a reason. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which word tells why?" Not yet seen: liking alone, error M, or a link word that misstates, error N. Record on the record sheet. Collect the student pages, the essay sheets and the exit slips at the end of the day.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult gives the prompt and sits by; no help. The items stay on paper. One projector: show the prompt once.

### At home

**Swap.** No research folder: use any short notes about water.
**Skip.** The word dictation.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Give your child the prompt on side 2. Your child writes a short essay alone, then answers [[s04-A14]] to [[s04-A17]]. Give no help.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes the log line, [[s04-P144-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X144]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check; each ask stays. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Opinion, reason, source, link word, close, research. Read the prompt aloud again for any child who asks; a scribe for the essay; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 145 hands the essay back with written feedback, then children revise one link word and answer short audits on plurals and abstract nouns. The essay stays with the teacher for feedback. The word work, [[s04-P144-1]] and [[s04-P144-2]], goes in the folder.
