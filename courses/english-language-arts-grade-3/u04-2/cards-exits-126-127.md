# Exit cards, days 126 to 127

A card marked invented: say aloud "This card is made up for practice," and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. A card from the unit's texts names its text and part and is not marked invented. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 126 (s04-X126v2), Exit.** A is from Water on Earth. It says: "Supply can shrink, too. When little rain falls, rivers and wells may hold less water." B is from Water Paths in Kitui County. It says: "Rain comes in seasons, and many rivers hold water only after a storm. ... some rivers and ponds had started to dry up after the poor rains." Ask: Write one important point both texts share.
- **Day 126 (s04-X126bv2), Exit box on the student page.** Use texts A and B on your slip. Write one detail only B has.
- **Day 127 (s04-X127v2), Exit.** From Water on Earth, section 8: "People can protect water in many ways. They can conserve it, which means using less and wasting less. Turning off the tap while brushing teeth saves water. Fixing a leaky pipe helps, too." Ask: Write the main idea of this part in one sentence.

Every exit sheet prints the card and the ask only, with a ruled space for the answer. The look-fors live in items.csv and the record sheet, never on the child's page.
