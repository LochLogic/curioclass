# Section 4.2 opener: how a town treats its water (days 126 and 127)

## What the section does

Two days read a short account of how a town cleans its water and set it beside the account of walking to reach water. Children say what both texts share, what only one gives, and what a whole text is mainly about.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes, with the same long-word and skills blocks as before. Day 126 reads the two texts in turn and builds a two-column chart, shared point first. Day 127 reads in pairs, finds the main idea of the treatment text and writes it in one sentence. Each day ends with a five-minute exit on paper.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Comparing starts with what is shared.** Name one point both texts make before naming a difference.
- **A difference is a detail only one text gives.** Say which text, and use its words.
- **A main idea joins the steps.** A list of steps is not yet an idea; the idea says what the steps do together.
- **Do not add claims.** If a text does not say it, it is not evidence.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** Adds a claim the text never makes. Repair: point to the line first.
- **C:** Lists steps without a joining idea. Repair: join each step to the idea with so.
- **F:** Gives only differences or only similarities. Repair: write the shared point first.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored and a check day stays unscored on the day.
