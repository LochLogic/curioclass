# Day 127: Reread both texts and say what each is mainly about

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and both texts for each child. Return the day 126 exit slips with one comment each, and the day 126 page with a tick on each written task. Put the pair charts from day 126 on the desks and a feedback card with each pair. Choose three pairs to listen to in the skills block. Draw three boxes on the board: idea, detail, detail.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P127-1]]. Review: you split long words into chunks from day 81 on; you learned -ment on day 53. Community has four chunks, one more than before. *3 min.* They add -ly to three words, [[s04-P127-2]]. Review: you added -ly in grade 2, and you changed y to i on day 63; easy changes the same way. *3 min.* Say once and would twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P127-3]]. Review: you met both words on days 89 and 42.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Tuesday

*5 min.* Pronouns. Review: you learned on day 86 that a pronoun stands in for a noun. Children write it or they in two gaps, [[s04-P127-4]]. *10 min.* Partner rereading: each child reads the first paragraph of "Water that stays" in the Kitui account aloud twice, with the partner listening, 5 minutes each way, [[s04-P127-5]]. Review: you did partner rereading with the feedback card on day 2. Listen in on three pairs, 2 minutes each; that is six children heard. Give them one comment on phrases or expression, not on speed. *5 min.* Each child ticks the feedback card for their partner and says one thing that went well, [[s04-P127-6]].

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Children read the comment on the day 126 slip and mark one thing to fix, [[s04-P127-7]]. The commonest letter is error C, a detail given as the shared point. Say: a shared point is an idea both cards make. *2 min.* Each child says one point the two texts share from the chart, to a partner, [[s04-P127-8]].

#### 10 min. Pair reading

Pairs read the town text from the start of "Why the water needs cleaning" to the end of the paragraph before Step 1, about 150 words, one child a paragraph in turn, [[s04-P127-9]]. Each child then rereads one sentence to check a word, and tells the partner which word.

#### 15 min. Main idea

*4 min.* Model with the town text on the board. Show three idea cards: a list of the four steps, one detail, and one sentence that covers the whole text. Test each card: does it cover every part? Pick the whole-text card. Then write one detail beside it joined with so. Review: you picked the better main idea card on day 15 and filled a main-idea chart then. *11 min.* Pairs do the Kitui account: each child picks the card that covers the whole account, [[s04-P127-10]], then writes one detail joined to the idea with so, [[s04-P127-11]]. Leave the class chart on the wall.

#### 15 min. Write

*5 min.* Each child writes one detail from the town text, joined to its idea with so, [[s04-P127-13]]. *7 min.* Each child writes one sentence that starts "Both texts" and says what the two main ideas share, [[s04-P127-12]]. *3 min.* Children check each sentence against the texts: point to the line. Watch for a claim neither text makes, error A.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child says which detail in each text helps its idea most, and why, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P127-14]]. Passing is fine.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read the sentence about boreholes. Children circle the words that tell what a borehole is, [[s04-P127-15]]. *2 min.* Read the glossary entry for borehole and check it against the circled words. Review: you used a sentence clue and then the glossary on day 18.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X127]]. Support is recorded. Shown: one sentence that joins the filling, the rising float and the shut-off. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Does your sentence cover all three?" Not yet seen: one step as the whole idea (error B) or the steps listed with no idea (error C). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hears the rereading and gives the feedback; the child does the main idea and the Both sentence on paper. The exit stays on paper. One projector: show each text one paragraph at a time; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed texts: read any two short accounts of water together.
**Skip.** The pair reading and the partner talk.

- **15 min. Read.** Read the first paragraph of "Water that stays" aloud twice, taking turns.
  **Say.** "What is it mainly about?" Expect a sentence about the whole paragraph.
- **15 min. Idea.** Your child picks the idea card and writes the Both sentence, [[s04-P127-10]] and [[s04-P127-12]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X127]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the main idea, the Both sentence and the exit; cut the grammar. Longer: write a second detail for each text, each joined with so.

### Words and supports

Main idea, detail, treatment, community, protect, borehole. Frame: "___ happened, so ___." The town text may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the cards; a drawing may carry a detail with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 128 reads about how much water people want and sorts what it reads under research categories. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X127, likely error B: test a sentence against every step of the card. The class main-idea chart stays on the wall.
