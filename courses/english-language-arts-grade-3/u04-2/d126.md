# Day 126: Read how a town cleans its water, then compare it with the Kitui account

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, both texts and the day 125 table for each child, and one chart for each pair. Pairs are fixed: one child takes the town column, one the Kitui column. Choose the six children heard at the table in the log block. Sort the day 125 exit slips by the commonest letter. Day 125 is handed back today in the opening.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P126-1]]. Review: you split long words into chunks on day 81, and important on day 123. Then they add -ed to three words, [[s04-P126-2]]. Review: you learned the ending rules on day 101 and used them on day 125. *6 min.* Say answer and people twice each, once inside a short sentence; children write them, [[s04-P126-3]]. Review: you mapped both words on day 81. Watch for a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P126-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one thing noticed that fits it, [[s04-P126-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

*3 min.* The commonest letter from day 125's exit is error A, a claim with no line behind it. Show the rule: point to the line first, and if there is none, say so. Each child does [[s04-P126-6]] alone. *2 min.* Children reread the table from day 125. Do not change it; the account column is copied from it today. Hand back the day 125 page with a tick on each written task.

#### 20 min. Read

*4 min.* Read How a Town Cleans Its Water aloud once, children following with a finger. *4 min.* Children reread it silently. *3 min.* Pairs skim the Kitui account for the table and the last section; they do not reread it all. *3 min.* Each child matches the four steps to their jobs, [[s04-P126-7]]. *6 min.* The class starts the chart on the board with you: read the town text's first section, say its most important point, tag it "town", and point to the line it comes from. Review: you charted important points and key details on day 123, and you tagged a source on a note on day 57. Name no shared point yet.

#### 20 min. Write

*3 min.* Model one cell. Fill the Kitui column's important point from the day 125 table, say its tag, and point to its line in the account. *8 min.* Pairs fill the chart, one important point and one key detail in each column, [[s04-P126-8]]. Each child fills one column and checks the other's by pointing to the line. *5 min.* Each child sorts six lines alone: both texts, town text only or Kitui account only, [[s04-P126-9]]. A shared point is an idea both texts make, not a shared word. *2 min.* Children check each line they marked both by pointing to it in each text. Watch for a point with no line, error A, and for a sort with no both, error F.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child picks a line they marked both and tells a partner where each text says it, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P126-10]]. Passing is fine.

#### 5 min. Word in context

*3 min.* Read Step 4 aloud. Children underline the words that tell what a filter does, [[s04-P126-11]]. *2 min.* Read the glossary entry for filter and check it against the underlined words. Review: you used a sentence clue and then the glossary on day 18.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the cards aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X126]]. Then they turn to the exit box on the page and write alone, [[s04-X126b]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a shared point both cards state, and one detail only B has. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the card say?" Not yet seen: a claim not on the cards (error A), a detail given as the shared point (error C) or differences only (error F). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the town text aloud, the child fills one chart in two columns and sorts the six lines. The exit stays on paper. One projector: show the town text and the chart one at a time; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed texts: read any short account of how water is cleaned, and any account of walking to water.
**Skip.** The conferences and the partner talk.

- **15 min. Read.** Read the town text aloud together. Your child matches the steps, [[s04-P126-7]].
  **Say.** "Which line says that?" Expect a finger on a line.
- **15 min. Sort.** Your child sorts the six lines, [[s04-P126-9]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X126]] on paper, alone, then [[s04-X126b]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the sort and the exit; cut the long words. Longer: add a third column for a detail neither text gives, marked "not in the texts".

### Words and supports

Important point, key detail, source tag, shared, filter, treatment plant. Talk frame: "I marked line ___ both. The town text says ___ and the account says ___." The town text may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the chart; a drawing of a tap or a filter may carry a detail with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 127 rereads both texts in pairs and finds what each text is mainly about. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X126, likely error C: give a shared point, not just one detail. The chart, [[s04-P126-8]], is kept for day 127. The sort and the log line stay in the child's folder.
