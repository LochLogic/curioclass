# Bus story event cards (day 10 opening)

Four cards, cut apart, for ordering. Pairs put them in order and say the arrow words: first, then, next, last.

1. Sam checks the bus card and sets off with his first book.
2. Sam counts the stone walls to make a game of the climb.
3. Mrs. Patel says book two is out on loan, and then Hannah brings it in.
4. Sam walks home and begins the new book by the lamp.
