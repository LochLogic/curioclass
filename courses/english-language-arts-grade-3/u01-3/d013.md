# Day 13: Check on a new tale

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the tale page: The Crow and the Pitcher, credited "A fable credited to Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece, in Milo Winter's 1919 telling", with the new illustration beside it. Make the crow picture from the art spec; without a printer, draw it by hand: a small crow, a tall narrow-necked pitcher, pebbles at its feet. Mark a record sheet column for each code. Find the day 13 line of exit cards 10 to 13.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Nothing is taught today. The day 12 maps and paragraphs stay with the children and go back on day 14. Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each word once and children write it. Week 3: knot, mountain, adventure, supper, lamp, bookmark, tablecloth, tired. Then un-, re- or dis- on a base, said as the prefix and base: un- like, un- fold, re- paint, dis- trust. Ten minutes: partners reread a favorite page of the grandmother story, twice each, quietly. Ten minutes: each child adds a line to the reading log.

#### 12 min. Reading

Each child reads The Crow and the Pitcher twice, silently, with its illustration. One phrase may be marked. Say nothing about the words or the picture.

#### 31 min. Items

Children work alone, in writing. Teach nothing and give no hint. Record each child on the record sheet while they write: [[s01-A6]] (6 minutes, RL.3.1), [[s01-A7]] (8, RL.3.2), [[s01-A8]] (5, RL.3.3), [[s01-A9]] (4, RL.3.4), [[s01-A10]] (4, L.3.5a) and [[s01-A11]] (4, RL.3.7). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and write down any prompt. No grade is given.

#### 7 min. Silent review

Each child rereads the tale and their answers and may change one. Silent.

#### 5 min. Collection and transition

Collect the student pages, then read the day 13 card aloud twice for the exit.

#### 5 min. Exit

Children write alone: [[s01-X13]]. Unscored. Shown: says what the action shows, with the beetle's action. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What did the beetle do, and what came of it?" Not yet seen: only that it is about an ant.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the tale by hand and draw the illustration. No pairs: the adult is the partner for the rereading.

### At home

**Swap.** No tale page: any short fable, read twice.
**Skip.** The partner rereading and the slate words.

- **10 min. Assessment.** First, your child reads the tale page twice and answers [[s01-A6]] and [[s01-A7]] alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Then your child answers [[s01-X13]] alone.
- **20 min. Read.** Read with an adult for 20 minutes.
- **10 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the log line. Longer: a child reads a second fable and says its message in one sentence.

### Words and supports

Tale, message, trait, moral. Large print; a spoken description of the illustration; a scribe for the answers, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 opens with five minutes on the day 12 exit and this exit: error B (a topic given as the message, "It is about an ant"). It also hands back the day 12 maps and paragraphs (s01-P12-1, s01-P12-2) and the item results with one comment each.
