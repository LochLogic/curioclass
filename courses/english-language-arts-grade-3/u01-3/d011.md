# Day 11: Words that are not meant exactly, again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Find the day 11 line of exit cards 10 to 13. Keep the day 7 context and figurative chart on the board, with room to add rows. Print the dictionary page, day 11, one for each pair. Sort the day 10 maps and sentences (s01-P10-1, s01-P10-2) for hand-back, one comment on each. Make two lists from the record sheet: four children for conferences and the heard-six children still to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

Three minutes repair day 10's exit, and hand back the day 10 maps and sentences with one comment each. If many children missed it, use error C (the events or the action and its result reversed). Show a new strip: "Dina spilled the juice. She wiped the table." Order the two events with arrows, then say them with so: "Dina spilled the juice, so she wiped the table." Ask which came first.

Then seven minutes on slates, unscored. Review: you learned un-, re- and dis- in grade 2. Say a base and a prefix, children write the new word and say what it means: un- with happy, re- with fill, dis- with agree, re- with wrap. Ask: "What does the prefix add?" Expect: not, again, the opposite of. Read the base first, then add the prefix.

#### 20 min. Skills

*4 min. Week 3 words.* Review: in unit 1.2 you made word maps. Four more words: lamp, bookmark, tablecloth, tired. Say each, clap its chunks (book-mark; ta-ble-cloth), and children write it on a slate. Bookmark and tablecloth are two words joined: ask for each part.

*16 min. Reading conferences.* Four different children from the list, 4 minutes each, as on day 10: one page read aloud (1 minute), one question answered from the words (1 minute 30 seconds), one next step in the log (1 minute 30 seconds). Write them in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs. If a child reads a word by its first letters (error P), say the chunks, blend, and check the sense.

#### 20 min. Model

Reread pages 1, 3 and 5 aloud, children following with a finger. Review: on day 7 you made a chart with three columns: the phrase, what it means, the clue. Keep it on the board.

*8 min. Two phrases.* Add the mountain wears a white tablecloth (page 1). Say the words exactly. Ask if a mountain can wear a tablecloth. Say what it means: a cloud lies across the top. Mark the clue: the wind pushes the clouds over the mountain, and the picture shows a white cloud. Then a knot of letters (page 3) with [[s01-K11-1]], and show the steps with [[steps:s01-K11-1]]. Mark the clue: Grandmother stopped, and Ama said to cut it into pieces.

*5 min. A known root.* Review: in unit 1.2 you learned that the ending -less takes the base away. Write help, helpful, helpless in a row. Read [[s01-K11-2]], with its steps in [[steps:s01-K11-2]]. Say the root, then the ending, then the whole word. Ask which word says Grandmother had no one to help her. Do not say it for them.

*4 min. A picture's color.* Show the page 3 picture. Read [[s01-K11-3]] and show the steps with [[steps:s01-K11-3]]. Ask: would the kitchen feel the same in cold gray light? Say: the color does work the words do not.

*3 min. A dictionary entry.* Show the entry for knot on the dictionary page. Read both senses. Say aloud: "Find the word, read every sense, choose the one that fits the sentence." Ask which fits a knot of letters. Say it fits the idea of a tangle, not a real cord.

#### 25 min. Hand work

*12 min. Three phrases.* Pairs reread pages 3, 5 and 6 and add three phrases to their charts with [[s01-P11-1]]: her laugh is as wide as the sky, a mountain is climbed one step at a time, like a little flag. For each, say what it means and mark the clue. Walk and look. If a child takes a phrase as a real event (error E), say: "Can that really happen? Say the words, then what is meant."

*4 min. Look up.* Each child gets one word from bookmark, lamp, supper or knot and finds it on the dictionary page with [[s01-P11-2]]. Read every sense, then copy the one that fits the story.

*9 min. Page 3.* Each child works alone with [[s01-P11-3]] and the words and picture table. Review: you filled a words and picture table in unit 1.2. Fill three columns: what the words say, what the picture shows, what the picture adds. Walk and look. If a child names a thing in the picture only (error D), ask: "What does it add that the words do not say?" Judged after class with the look-fors in the item. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet. Hand back on day 12.

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: in grade 2 you used the four-step chart (turn, pair, link, clarify); in grade 3 the chart adds a fifth move. Pairs sit together. Each child takes 2 minutes to say one phrase, its meaning and its clue (4 minutes in all). Then 3 minutes for the partner to ask for a detail ("Which page tells that?"). Then 3 minutes: hear three volunteers at 1 minute each, and write two of them in the heard-six column with the four from the conferences. Anyone may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 11 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X11]]. Unscored. Shown: says what the picture adds and links it to the words. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the picture show that the words do not?" Not yet seen: the phrase read as a real thing, or a pictured person named with nothing added.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the dictionary entries and the three phrases onto paper by hand. No projector: keep the page 3 picture on paper. No pairs: the adult is the partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No dictionary page: any dictionary, or one sense of a word said aloud.
**Skip.** The conferences and the talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Reread pages 3 and 5 of the grandmother story aloud, or any short story.
  **Say.** "Which phrase is not meant exactly, and what does it mean?" Expect: the words, what they mean, and a clue.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child fills the table for page 3. [[s01-P11-3]]
- **5 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X11]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chart, the dictionary entry and the exit; cut the three phrases to two. Longer: a child writes the meaning of one more phrase from a book and marks its clue.

### Words and supports

Lamp, bookmark, tablecloth, tired, helpless. Frame: "It means ___. The clue is ___." Preview each picture with a short spoken description. Large print; a spoken description of the picture for a child who cannot see it; a scribe for the table, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 opens with three minutes on this exit and hands back the phrase charts, look-ups and tables (s01-P11-1, s01-P11-2, s01-P11-3) with one comment each. The error to watch is E (a phrase read as a real event): put the literal and the intended reading side by side. Day 12 reads The Lion and the Mouse, a tale credited to Aesop.
