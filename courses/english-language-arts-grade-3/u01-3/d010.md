# Day 10: Recounting the grandmother story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Find the day 10 line of exit cards 10 to 13. Give each pair a recount map sheet (a strip folded in four) and cut one set of bus event cards for each pair. Sort the day 8 exits and the day 9 notes (s01-X8, s01-X9, s01-A1 to s01-A5) for hand-back, one comment on each. Make two lists from the record sheet: the four children for conferences and the heard-six children still to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

Five minutes repair, and hand back the day 8 exits and day 9's assessment (the discussion notes) with one comment each. Count the letters and teach only the commonest of four. The other three get a written comment.

If error B (a topic given as the message): show a new strip, "Tariq wrote to his aunt every week. He saved coins in a jar to buy a stamp." Ask: "What do his actions show?" Write the frame "The words ___ show ___." Connect two details to one claim: saving coins and writing every week show that he works to keep in touch.

If error K (a list with no events): write "Bring a bag. Be early." Ask: "Which event shows that?" Replace the list with one event and what came of it.

If error Q (no prepared detail, "I like Joao"): say "Mark one detail before you speak, then say it aloud once." Practise on one line of the day 9 notes.

If error T (an opening opinion repeated, "I still think the same"): ask what your partner said that you can add to or change. Say it again, with the new detail.

Then five minutes with the four bus event cards, unscored. Review: in unit 1.2 you put events in order with arrows. Pairs order the cards for the bus story, then say them with the arrow words: first, then, next, last. Ask which two cards one so can join.

#### 20 min. Skills

*4 min. Week 3 words.* Review: in unit 1.2 you made word maps. Today four new words: knot, mountain, adventure, supper. Say each, clap its chunks (mountain: moun-tain; adventure: ad-ven-ture; supper: sup-per), then children write it on a slate. The k in knot is silent. Pins go on the sound and spelling wall.

*16 min. Reading conferences.* Review: in grade 2 you had a four-minute reading conference. Four children, 4 minutes each: the child reads one page of their book aloud (1 minute), you ask one question and they answer from the words (1 minute 30 seconds), and you set one next step in the log (1 minute 30 seconds). Write the four in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs, quietly. If a child reads a word by its first letters (error P), say the chunks, blend, and check that it makes sense.

#### 20 min. Model

*5 min. Reading the whole story.* Read Reading Beside Grandmother aloud (426 words, about 4 minutes), stopping at the pictures on pages 1, 3 and 4 so that children say what they see. Children follow with a finger. Say the setting once: Cape Town, South Africa. Grandmother is learning to read as an adult, and she is shown with dignity. Ask no one about their family's reading.

*15 min. The board recount.* Draw four small boxes joined by arrows on the board. Use the pages 1 to 3 events. Reread each in turn with the children, then write it in one line and show the steps with [[steps:s01-K10-1]]. Box 1, page 1: the wind rattles the window. Box 2, page 2: after supper they read. Box 3, page 3: Grandmother meets a knot of letters. Box 4, page 3: Ama says cut it into pieces, and she reads adventure. At each arrow say the link: "The wind rattled, then they read." "She met a knot of letters, so Ama said cut it into pieces." Ask which arrows are so and which are then, and why. Then write one line in the Beginning box of the recount map sheet: reading after supper, a knot of letters, cut into pieces. Pairs copy it onto their own sheet. The board stays up for the hand work.

#### 25 min. Hand work

*15 min. The map.* Pairs reread pages 4 to 6 (about 175 words, two quiet reads each) and finish the Middle, Middle and End boxes with [[s01-P10-1]]. The Middle boxes hold the skipped line and the tired eyes. The End box holds the strip in the book. Arrows join the boxes. Walk and look. If a pair writes only "Ama reads", ask: "What happened next because of it?"

*10 min. One sentence.* Each child works alone with [[s01-P10-2]]: pick two joined events on the map and write one sentence, the cause first, with so or because. Frame: "___ happened, so ___." Walk and look, and do not write for them. If a child writes the result first, ask: "Which came first?" Judged after class with three look-fors: two events from the map, the cause first, a link word. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet. Hand back the maps and sentences with one comment on day 11.

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: in grade 2 you used the four-step chart (turn, pair, link, clarify); in grade 3 the chart adds a fifth move. Pairs sit together. Each child takes 2 minutes to say their sentence and the page it came from (4 minutes in all). The cue is the one from day 1: finish by saying "Your turn." Then 3 minutes for the partner to ask for a detail ("Which page tells that?") and for the speaker to answer. Then 3 minutes: hear three volunteers at 1 minute each, and write two of them in the heard-six column with the four from the conferences. Anyone may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 10 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X10]]. Unscored. Shown: the three events in order, with so or then. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What happened first? Then what?" Not yet seen: only what Kofi likes, or the events out of order.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the map strip by folding paper into four and write Beginning, Middle, Middle, End on it. No projector: write the story's line on the board for each box. No pairs: the adult is the partner and orders the four bus cards alone.

### At home

**Swap.** No story pages: any short story, with the events said aloud in order.
**Skip.** The conferences and the talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Read pages 1 to 3 of the grandmother story aloud, or any short story.
  **Say.** "What happened first, then next, then last?" Expect: three events in order, joined by so or then.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes one sentence with so: the cause first, then what came of it. [[s01-P10-2]]
- **5 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X10]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the board recount and the exit; cut the map to one Middle box. Longer: a child recounts pages 4 to 6 aloud to an adult in four sentences, with a link word in each.

### Words and supports

Knot, mountain, adventure, supper. Frame: "___ happened, so ___." Preview each picture with a short spoken description. Large print; the story read aloud for a child who cannot see the pictures; a scribe for the sentence, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 11 opens with three minutes on this exit and hands back the maps and sentences (s01-P10-1, s01-P10-2) with one comment each. The error to watch is C (the action and its result reversed): order the two events with arrows, then say so. Day 11 rereads pages 1, 3 and 5 for phrases that are not meant exactly.
