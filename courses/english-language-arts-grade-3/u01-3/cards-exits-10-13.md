# Exit cards, days 10 to 13

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children write. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 10 (s01-X10).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "Kofi lost his place in the rhyme sheet. His teacher pointed to the last word they had said. Kofi found the next line and went on." Ask: Tell what happened to Kofi from first to last.
- **Day 11 (s01-X11).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "The new sentence was a locked door to Kofi. He read it slowly until its meaning opened." Picture: Kofi sits very still, forehead on his fist, eyes on the page. Ask: What does the picture add to the words locked door?
- **Day 12 (s01-X12).** This tale is invented for practice. Read: "Two goats met on a narrow bridge. Each wanted to cross first, and neither would step back. Then one goat knelt, and the other stepped over her. Both crossed safely. 'Giving way kept us both moving,' said the goat." Ask: Explain the message of the tale, using what happened and the goat's last words.
- **Day 13 (s01-X13).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "An ant could not cross a puddle. A beetle pushed a leaf across it. The ant crossed the floating bridge and thanked the beetle." Ask: What does the beetle's action show?
