# Recount map sheet (days 10 and 12)

A long strip folded in four. Draw **Beginning**, **Middle**, **Middle** and **End** boxes, with an arrow between each. Under the strip draw one line: "The ___ happened, so ___."

Partners hold the map on day 10. On day 12 each child holds their own and transfers it to the tale.
