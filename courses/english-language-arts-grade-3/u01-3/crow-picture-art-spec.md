# Art spec: The Crow and the Pitcher illustration (day 13)

New original illustration for the day 13 check. Plain black line drawing with one flat color, thick lines, no shading.

- A small crow beside a tall pitcher with a narrow neck.
- A small heap of pebbles at the crow's feet.
- The pitcher is much taller than the crow, so the size difference is the main thing the eye sees.
- No text in the picture. No caption.

Not a copy of any older illustration of the tale.
