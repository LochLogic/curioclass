# Day 152: Check the letter, abstract nouns and an address

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the dictation sheet for each child. Put each retained day 151 letter, with your written move, on its desk with the folder. Keep the page face down until the dictation sheets are collected; its item 1 is answered on the dictation sheet. Have the heard-words card, the town text and a feedback card for each pair ready. This is a check: no help, nothing taught.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*6 min.* Hand out the dictation sheet only. Say each of six words twice; children write one on each line: said, does, sitting, smiled, cries, happiness. Then say: write a sentence on line 7 with sitting in it; the sentence is about water in a channel, [[s04-A39]]. Collect the dictation sheets. Do not give a spelling. *4 min.* Children turn over the student page and write the ending rule, [[s04-A40]]. Unscored this time; the support used is recorded.

#### 20 min. Skills block, check day

*10 min.* Call each child to you for 20 seconds, one at a time (30 children, 10 minutes). The child reads remembering and blockage, [[s04-A34]], then said and does, [[s04-A35]], from the heard-words card. Mark each word on the card: read right, read after rereading, or not yet. Meanwhile the rest read steps 3 and 4 of the town text twice to a partner in low voices, [[s04-A37]]. The partner fills the feedback card and ticks only what was heard; read the cards at hand-in. *10 min.* Silent log reading. Children write one line saying why they read and what a detail showed, [[s04-A36]]. Then they write one line about a word they reread to fix, [[s04-A38]]. Record who read the whole time.

#### 24 min. Revise the letter

*3 min.* Hand back the day 151 slip, s04-X151, with one comment: add the source detail to a vague sentence, error V. *1 min.* Hand back the Exit box, s04-X151b: mark the boundary between the city and the state before the comma, error Y. *1 min.* Hand back the day 151 tasks, s04-P151-2, s04-P151-3, s04-P151-6, s04-P151-7, s04-P151-8, s04-P151-9, s04-P151-10 and s04-P151-13, with a tick on each; say bush and bushes together, error AO. *6 min.* Hand back the day 151 letter with its written move. Children read the move, sort the sentences by topic if a group is mixed, error U, and change the one thing it names. *13 min.* One ask at a time; say only the ask. *4 min.* [[s04-A24]]. *4 min.* [[s04-A25]]. *2 min.* [[s04-A26]]. *3 min.* [[s04-A27]]. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Watch for facts about two things in one group, error U, and a link word that joins a fact to an invitation.

#### 4 min. Plan fit

Children answer [[s04-A28]]. Say only the ask. Watch for the invitation before the purpose, error W.

#### 4 min. Range

Children open the writing folder and answer [[s04-A29]]. Say only the ask. Watch for one sitting offered as sustained writing, error X.

#### 6 min. Process

Children open the letter folder and answer [[s04-A30]]. Say only the ask. Watch for a clean recopy offered as a revision, error T.

#### 6 min. Plural audit

Children answer [[s04-A31]]. Say only the ask. Watch for an irregular plural spelled with a plain s, error R.

#### 6 min. Abstract noun audit

Children answer [[s04-A32]]. Say only the ask. Watch for a touchable thing marked as the idea, error S.

#### 5 min. Address audit

*1 min.* Children look at the practice address on side 2 and draw a line where its comma belongs, [[s04-P152-1]]. Unscored. Do not correct it. *4 min.* Children read the address block of their own letter and check each comma, [[s04-A33]]. Say only the ask. Watch for the comma left out, error Y.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read each card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X152]] and [[s04-X152c]]. Then children write the Exit box on the page, [[s04-X152b]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: a sentence that gives the purpose, to find damage; the comma between the city and the state; a sentence that names care as the idea. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the reader need first?" Not yet seen: the invitation before the purpose, error W; no comma, error Y; cover named as the idea, error S. Record on the record sheet. Collect the pages, letters, folders, dictation sheets, heard-words card and slips for marking. The marking is on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each ask aloud, hears the four words, says the six dictation words, and the child answers on paper; no help. The letter stays at home. One projector: show nothing; the work is on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No letter folder: use any earlier letter or paragraph and its comment.
**Skip.** The four words and the partner reading.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Your child reads your comment on the letter, changes one thing, then answers [[s04-A24]] to [[s04-A27]] and [[s04-A31]] to [[s04-A33]] alone. Give no help.
- **10 min. Words.** Say said, does, sitting, smiled, cries and happiness twice each; your child writes them in a numbered list, [[s04-A39]].
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes one line on why they read, [[s04-A36]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X152]] and [[s04-X152c]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check; each ask stays. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Letter, purpose, address, comma, plural, abstract noun, folder. Read each ask aloud again for any child who asks, never the source; a quiet room for the heard words; a scribe for the sentences; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 153 is a check day on the announcement: nothing is taught. Six groups record a revised announcement in turn while the rest prepare, and you tick three look-fors for each child. The day 152 pages, letters, folders and slips stay with you for marking; the written moves come back later in the repair, with no reteaching.
