# Day 151: Draft and edit the letter

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and a cue plan template for each child. Put each letter plan, the day 149 listening cards with a written move, and the day 150 slips on the desks. Open each pair's device file with the day 149 script. Set fifteen devices and six recording devices; paper is the fall-back. Open the device log. Choose six groups of five. The letter goes to a classroom water office only.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* In pairs, children read build, thought and heavy and say each in a sentence, [[s04-P151-1]]. Review: you mapped build on day 133, thought on day 102 and heavy on day 146. Unscored. *4 min.* Say class and classes, wish and wishes: -es comes after s, sh, ch and x. Children write the plural of bush and church, [[s04-P151-2]]. Review: you added -es to wish on day 132. Watch for a plain s where -es is needed, error AO. *1 min.* The class says the ending rule: a short vowel and one letter after it, double the letter; drop a silent e; after a consonant, change y to i before -ed or -es. Review: you said it on day 150. *2 min.* Children fix one word in the pair work.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P151-3]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child naming a book, a question and one detail, [[s04-P151-4]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P151-5]]. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 150 slip, s04-X150, with one comment. Where the comma was left out, children mark the boundary between the city and the state first, then put the comma, error Y. Hand back the day 150 page with a tick on each task. *1 min.* Hand back the Exit box, s04-X150b and s04-X150c: touch the object or name the idea, error S, and say calf and calves together, error R. *1 min.* Read the purpose note on the board: the classroom water office needs the purpose of a step before the invitation. Say: the reader learns why, then is asked to come.

#### 20 min. Reading

*10 min.* Hand back the two day 149 listening cards, s04-P149-8 and s04-P149-9, with a written move (2 minutes). Read each account aloud once more (2 minutes). Children read the move and fix the main idea or a detail on the card, and mark the change, [[s04-P151-6]] (6 minutes). Review: you listened for a main idea and details on days 17, 146 and 149. Watch for a small detail named as the main idea, error B, and a clean recopy, error T. *10 min.* Children reread the source for their letter, the town text or the water book. They mark the hardest part and write one fact from it with its source tag, [[s04-P151-7]]. Review: you wrote a note with a source tag on day 137.

#### 20 min. Draft and edit (I11)

*11 min.* Children draft the letter on lined paper from the plan, [[s04-P151-8]]: the address block with its comma, the issue, facts grouped by topic joined with a link word such as also or another, the purpose of the step before the invitation, and a closing that invites one question. Review: you used link words on days 146 and 148. Then they draw a line between the city and the state in the address block and tick the comma, [[s04-P151-9]], and check each plural against the strip. Watch for facts about two things in one group, error U. *9 min.* The device window, taken inside the drafting: the first fifteen children use a device each for 4 minutes: they type one change to the day 149 script that makes a fact clearer, [[s04-P151-10]], and record the revised script with one clarifying sentence and a pause at the changed cue, [[s04-P151-11]]; speaking is about 90 seconds at most. Then 1 minute to switch, and the other fifteen take 4 minutes. The children not on a device keep drafting and editing. Mark the device log: the change typed and the file saved. Review: you typed on a device on days 137, 148 and 149. Watch for a change that is the same line typed again, error T, and a cue said aloud, error AB. Without a device, children write the change on the template and read it to a partner. After class, write one move on each letter for day 152.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Six groups of five, one recording device a group, 1 minute each child. Each child says one revised line, then answers one question from the group in one complete sentence, [[s04-P151-12]]. The recordings are an unscored note, kept for day 153, not a mark. Every child may pass. No child is held up as the example of a mistake. Watch for a fragment, error AA.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence with group. Children circle what group means in it, [[s04-P151-13]]. Review: you sorted things into groups in kindergarten. Say: to group is to put the facts about one thing together.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X151]]. Then children write the Exit box on the page, [[s04-X151b]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: a sentence about the foam layer that keeps the filter group and starts with a link word; the comma between the city and the state. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the source say about foam?" Not yet seen: a sentence with no link word, error V, a sentence about something else, error U, or no comma, error Y. Record on the record sheet. Collect the drafts, plans and slips for written feedback before day 152.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the listener and asks one question about the script; the child drafts the letter, edits the address, and answers the exit on paper; a phone may record one minute and stays at home. One projector: show nothing; the device turn is two print turns.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: say the changed line aloud and your helper listens. No lined paper: any page.
**Skip.** The long words and the log.

- **15 min. Draft.** Your child writes the letter from the plan: the address with its comma, two facts about one thing joined with also or another, and a closing question, [[s04-P151-8]].
  **Say.** "Which facts belong together?" Expect: facts about the same step.
- **10 min. Address.** Your child draws a line between the city and the state, then ticks the comma, [[s04-P151-9]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X151]] on paper, alone, then the Exit box, [[s04-X151b]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the draft, the address edit and the exit; cut the listening card and the word in context. Longer: a child drafts a second closing sentence for a different reader.

### Words and supports

Draft, group, link word, purpose, address, edit. Frames: "Our group explains ___. First ___. Another ___. Please bring a question about ___." A word strip; the plural strip; an address card with the comma marked; a scribe or typing for the draft; headphones for the recording turn; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 152 is a check day: nothing is taught. It begins with four words read to you and a dictation, then children read your written feedback, revise the retained letter, and audit its plurals, an abstract noun and its address. The day 151 slips go back that day. The letter, the plan and the recordings stay with you.
