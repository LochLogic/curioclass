# Section 4.8 opener: a letter to a water office (days 150 to 152)

## What the section does

Three days plan, draft and check a short letter to a water office. Children use a model letter, group related facts, link them, name an idea with an abstract noun, write a closing invitation, and put the comma in an address. The third day is a check day: the letter is revised after written feedback, and heard words and a dictation are read and written.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 150 plans from the model, day 151 drafts and edits, and day 152 checks. Day 151 also revises last week's script in short typed turns. Day 152 is silent and unhurried: no teaching, with short audits of the letter, the plurals, the abstract noun and the address. Each day ends with a five-minute exit on paper, and nothing is scored on day 150 or 151.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A letter has a plan.** Address, issue, grouped facts, one idea noun, an invitation.
- **Group by topic.** Facts about one thing sit together, then the next group.
- **Give the reader the purpose first.** The invitation comes after.
- **An abstract noun names an idea.** Ask: can I touch it? If not, it may be an idea noun.
- **Commas in an address.** A comma goes between the city and the state, and none between the state and the number.
- **Irregular plurals** are said as a pair, the one and the more than one.

## The errors, in plain words

- **S:** Names a thing as an abstract noun. Repair: touch it or name the idea.
- **R:** Adds a plain s to an irregular plural. Repair: say the pair together.
- **Y:** Leaves out the comma between city and state. Repair: mark the boundary first.
- **U:** Mixes unrelated facts in one group. Repair: sort by topic.
- **V:** Says something vague. Repair: add a definition and a source detail.
- **W:** Invites before giving the purpose. Repair: state the purpose first.
- **N, O, T, X:** carried from earlier sections; use the written move on the feedback, with no reteaching on the check day.
- **AK, AS, AR:** word errors from the long-words block; each group regrouped by its commonest error.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored on every day of this section. On the check day the letter, the address and the word items are scored from the work and the support used is recorded.
