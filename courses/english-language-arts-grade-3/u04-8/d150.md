# Day 150: Plan a letter from a model

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the letter model and the letter plan list for each child. Put the day 149 scripts, with the written feedback, on the desks. Make three word slips from the day 149 tallies: long words (error AK), hard words (error AS) and doubled letters (error AR). Choose five children to confer with in the plan. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column. The letter and its address are made up for practice; no real office gets a letter.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*1 min.* Say the ending rule and the class says it back: a short vowel and one letter after it, double the letter; drop a silent e; after a consonant, change y to i before -ed or -es. Review: you learned the ending rules on day 81 and used them on day 143. *9 min.* Three groups by the commonest slip on day 149, 3 minutes each at your table. Group one reads population, development and pollution one syllable at a time, then says each, [[s04-P150-1]]. Review: you divided these words on day 128. Group two reads again, enough and touch, then says each in a sentence, [[s04-P150-2]]. Review: you mapped enough on day 84 and touch on day 149. Group three says stop, drip and plan with -ing and spells each aloud, [[s04-P150-3]]. Review: you added -ing to hop and tap on day 143. Each child reads one word, about 15 seconds a child; it is an unscored note. The other groups read their words in pairs, then cover them and write each from memory, [[s04-P150-4]]. Watch for a long word guessed from its first letter, error AK, a word read by its usual sound, error AS, or no doubling, error AR.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P150-5]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child naming a book, a question and one detail, [[s04-P150-6]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P150-7]]. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening

*4 min.* Return the day 149 scripts with one comment each. Each child shows a partner one change they made from the comment, error T. *1 min.* Hand back the day 149 exit slip, s04-X149, with one comment: the cue is shown, not said, error AB.

#### 20 min. Teach from the model

*5 min.* Hand out the letter. Read its first two lines, the address and Dear Water Office, then the whole letter once while children follow. Pairs reread it in phrases. Say: the first two sentences say what the class studied. Children draw a line under them, [[s04-P150-8]]. Say: facts about one thing sit together; the invitation comes last. Then children find classes and women and write each singular, [[s04-P150-9]]. Review: you learned woman and women on day 136 and kept them on the plural strip from day 142. Watch for the invitation marked as what the class studied.

*8 min.* Ask the planned question: can I touch it, or is it an idea? Point to a pipe: you can touch it. Say safety and teamwork: you cannot touch them; they are ideas. Write touch and idea on the board. Say: an idea noun can tell why a thing matters. Children circle the two words they cannot touch, [[s04-P150-10]]. Review: you learned abstract nouns on day 57 and used them on days 136, 139 and 142. Watch for a touchable word called an idea, error S.

*7 min.* Write the model's last address line on the board with no comma. Say: first find where the city ends and the state begins; the comma goes there; no comma goes before the number. Put the comma in. Children circle the line with the comma in the right place, [[s04-P150-11]]. Watch for a comma left out, error Y.

#### 20 min. Plan (I11)

Hand out the plan list. Say: the plan comes first and the letter follows it. Children copy the class address card from the page into the plan, [[s04-P150-12]]; the card is made up, and a real letter would use the school's own address. They fill the plan: the issue from the day 148 announcement; two facts about one thing, each with its source in the town text or the water book; a link word such as also or another; one abstract noun and the idea it names; a closing that invites one question, [[s04-P150-13]]. Confer with five children for 4 minutes each (20 minutes) while the rest plan. These are children's own work: unscored, kept in the folder. Watch for facts about two things mixed in one group, error U.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Pairs say the plan aloud in order, 2 minutes each, with 1 to change over, [[s04-P150-14]]. Move among them; it is an unscored note. Every child may pass. No child is held up as the example of a mistake.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence with invite. Children circle what invite means in it, [[s04-P150-15]]. Review: you learned invite in kindergarten, to ask someone to join. Say: an invitation asks the reader to come or to answer.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X150]]. Then children write the Exit box on the page, [[s04-X150b]] and [[s04-X150c]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: the comma between the city and the state and none before the number; a sentence that names conservation as the idea; calves for two calf. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Where does the city end?" Not yet seen: no comma, error Y; a touchable word called an idea, error S; calfs, error R. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the model with the child, asks the planned question, and the child writes the plan and the exit on paper; the letter stays at home. One projector: show nothing; the model is on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed model: the parent writes a three-line letter. No plan list: fold a page into five boxes.
**Skip.** The long words.

- **15 min. Model letter.** Your child reads the letter aloud to you.
  **Say.** "Can I touch it, or is it an idea?" Expect: safety and teamwork are ideas you cannot touch. Your child circles the two idea words, [[s04-P150-10]].
- **10 min. Plan.** Your child fills the plan list, [[s04-P150-13]]. Help with nothing but the question "Which two facts go together?"
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X150]] on paper, alone, then the Exit box, [[s04-X150b]] and [[s04-X150c]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the plan and the exit; cut the log day to the line and one conference, and the word in context. Longer: a child writes a second address with the comma for a different town.

### Words and supports

Letter, address, group, invite, safety, teamwork, comma. Frames: "Our group explains ___. First ___. Another ___. Please bring a question about ___." A word strip with pictures; an address card with the comma already marked for rehearsal; a scribe or typing for the plan; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 151 begins by returning the day 150 slips and the Exit box with a comment, and a note on what the water office needs first. Children draft the letter from the plan, edit the address, and take a short device turn to revise the day 149 script. The model and the plan stay in the folder.
