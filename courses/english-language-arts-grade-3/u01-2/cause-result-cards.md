# Cause and result cards (day 6)

Six sentence cards, cut apart. Pairs put two cards in order and say "so" or "because".

- The path was wet. / We walked slowly.
- The bell rang. / The class lined up.
- She packed the bag early. / She was ready to go.

Teacher note: each pair of cards makes one cause and one result; the cause card comes first when the pair says "so".
