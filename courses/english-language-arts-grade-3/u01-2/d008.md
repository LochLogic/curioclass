# Day 8: Writing what Sam's actions show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Find the day 8 line of exit cards 5 to 9. Print the two preparation pages, one pair of pages for each child, each marked "invented for practice". Sort the day 6 maps and the day 7 sorts and look-ups for hand-back, one comment on each. Make two lists from the record sheet: the two conference groups of up to five, and the heard-six children still to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

Three minutes repair day 7's exit, and hand back the day 7 sorts and look-ups (s01-P7-1, s01-P7-2, s01-P7-3, s01-P7-4, s01-P7-5) with one comment each. If many children missed it, use the letter you saw most.

If error D (a pictured thing named with no job): show a new picture beside the words "Omar looked at the steps." The picture shows a tall stair, Omar small at the bottom with his hat in his hand. Draw two columns: The words say, The picture adds. Fill the first, then ask what the picture adds that the words do not say. Expect: the stair is very long, so the climb looks hard.

If error E (a phrase read as a real event): write "The bag weighed a ton." Say what the words say, then what is meant: very heavy. Write them side by side.

Then seven minutes on slates, unscored. Review: in grade 2 you added -ed and -ing to a base word. Say each word in a sentence, then children write it. (1) Four week 2 words: lane, steep, stone, week. Children say the sound or chunk before writing. (2) Add -ed to plan (planned, the n is doubled) and -ing to jump (jumping). Say the base word, then the new word. Pins on the sound and spelling wall stay up.

#### 20 min. Skills

*7 min. Partner rereading.* Review: in grade 2 you reread with a partner for expression; the feedback card is new in grade 3. Pairs reread page 6 (about 70 words, 35 seconds a pass). Each child reads twice, 70 seconds, and the partner gives feedback, 30 seconds: one box ticked, one thing that went well, one next try. That is 3 minutes 20 seconds each, 6 minutes 40 seconds for a pair. Never say speed. Listen to two pairs and write them in the heard-six column.

*13 min. Conference groups.* Review: in grade 2 you read in a small group for the diagnosed gap. Two groups of up to five children, 6 minutes each, one minute to change. In each group, every child blends two week 2 words by chunks (30 seconds) and reads one new sentence aloud (20 seconds): five children, 4 minutes 10 seconds. Words: village, valley, lane, steep, wall, climb. Sentences: "Walk up the steep lane." "A stone wall runs by the valley." For the last 1 minute 50 seconds, the group reads one line of the bus story together and says what it means. If a child reads a word by its first letters (error P), say the chunks, blend, and check the sense. The rest read from their logs.

#### 40 min. Hand work

Each child works alone first. Nothing is taught new today.

First 25 minutes. Review: on day 3 you wrote a paragraph with a topic, a fact, a link and an ending. Each child writes a response for a younger reader, from their own day 6 map and page 6, using [[s01-P8-1]]. Put the writing frame card beside the page: Topic, Fact, Link, Ending. The frame to start: "The words ___ show ___." Each child writes a claim, two events as proof, a link word and a closing line. Walk and look, and do not write for them. If a child writes only "It is about books" (error B), ask: "What did Sam do, and what happened next?" If a child writes a list, "bring a bag, be early" (error K), ask: "Which event shows that?" Judged after class with the four look-fors. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet. Hand back with a comment on day 9.

Then 15 minutes with the two preparation pages, boat and bus, with [[s01-P8-2]]. Each page is short. Read each page twice in a quiet voice. Children underline one detail on each page that shows how the person gets ready to borrow again. Then each child says one claim aloud, quietly, to themselves: "The words ___ show ___." Walk and listen. If a child marks a whole page (error Q), say: "Choose one word group."

#### 15 min. Talk

Review: in grade 2 you used the four-step chart (turn, pair, link, clarify); in grade 3 the chart adds a fifth move. Pairs sit together. Each child takes 3 minutes with a prepared detail: the claim, then the words it came from (6 minutes in all). The cue is the one from day 1: the speaker finishes by saying "Your turn," and the partner waits for those words. Walk and listen for a talk over the cue (error R): say the cue and ask the pair to start again.

Then, for 9 minutes, hear three pairs, 3 minutes each, which is 1 minute 30 seconds for each of six children. Write each in the heard-six column. The other pairs swap roles and rehearse again. Anyone may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 8 card aloud twice. Children write three sentences alone: [[s01-X8]]. Unscored. Shown: says what the actions show, uses two events as proof, with a link word, for a younger reader. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What did he do? What happened because of it?" Not yet seen: only what the strip is about, or a list with no events.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the two preparation pages onto paper by hand and read the strip aloud. No projector: keep the picture on paper. No pairs: the adult is the partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No preparation pages: use any short passage and mark one detail.
**Skip.** The partner rereading, the conference groups and the talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Reread your two marked preparation pages aloud twice.
  **Say.** "Which detail did you mark, and what does it show?" Expect: a detail copied from the page, with what it shows.
- **15 min. Write.** Reread your response from today; add one thing you left out, or say it aloud to an adult.
- **5 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X8]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the response and the exit; cut the 15 minutes of preparation pages to one page. Longer: a child writes a second response on Lia and checks it against the four look-fors.

### Words and supports

Message, proof, link, ending. Frames: "The words ___ show ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." Preview the preparation pages with a picture for each. Large print; a spoken description of each picture for a child who cannot see it; a scribe for the response, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 is the group discussion; it has no repair slot. Error B is a topic given as the message ("The story is about books"). Error K is a list with no events as proof ("Bring a bag, be early"). Day 9 hands back the response and the marked pages with one comment each. Day 10 opens with five minutes on this exit.
