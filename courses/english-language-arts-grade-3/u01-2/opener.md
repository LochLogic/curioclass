# Section 1.2 opener: Lia, Sam and the message (days 5 to 9)

## What the section does

Children reread the boat story and read *Sam Meets the Book Bus*. They recount each story in order, say what it teaches from the events, and learn to tell a phrase that is not meant exactly from one that is. On day 7 they use a sentence clue, a known word part and a printed dictionary page. Day 8 prepares a talk; day 9 is the first formal group discussion, which is also a Check day.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. A short repair opens the day, then reading, a model, hand work and one exit. Day 9 holds no teaching: silent skills, independent preparation, six group windows of seven minutes, and one exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A message comes from what a character does and what results, not from the topic.** Put the events side by side, then say what they show with "so".
- **Some phrases are not meant exactly.** Say the literal reading, then the intended one, and mark the clue.
- **A cause comes before its result.** Say "so" or "because" in the right order.
- **In discussion, bring a detail and name what you learned.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **B:** a topic given where a message is wanted. Join two events to one claim.
- **C:** an action and its result reversed. Number the events and draw an arrow.
- **E:** a phrase read as a physical event. Put the literal and intended readings side by side.
- **F, G:** a word clue ignored, a look-up skipped. Mark the clue; open the page.
- **Q to U:** a missing detail, an interruption, an unrelated question, an opening opinion repeated, a vague answer.

## Marking

Same scale as 1.1: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reading logs guide conferences; they are not comprehension scores.
