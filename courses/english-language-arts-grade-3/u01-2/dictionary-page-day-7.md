# Dictionary page (day 7)

Four entries, in alphabetical order, printed on one page. Each pair looks up one word.

- **lane** (noun): a narrow road or path.
- **pack** (verb): to put things into a bag or box. **pack** (noun): a bag carried on the back.
- **steep** (adjective): rising or falling sharply.
- **wall** (noun): an upright structure of stone, brick or wood that closes in or divides a space.

Say aloud: "Find the word, read every sense, choose the one that fits the sentence."
