# Phrase cards (days 7, 8 and 11)

Four cards from the bus story. The back of each card holds the clue strip.

| Phrase | Intended meaning | Clue |
|---|---|---|
| legs like jelly | tired and wobbly | after the long steep climb |
| clouds like gray wool hats | clouds sitting low and round on the hilltops | the clouds sit on the hilltops |
| a library that has grown wheels | a bus that holds books and is a library | the shelves inside |
| stones like a puzzle | stones that fit tightly together | each wall is built of fitted stones |

Pairs sort the cards into "meant exactly" and "not meant exactly", say each intended meaning, and mark the clue.
