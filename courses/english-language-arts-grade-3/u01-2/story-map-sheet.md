# Story-map sheet with a message box (days 5 to 8, 10, 12, 13)

Fold a sheet in thirds. Draw three boxes across the top: **Who**, **Want**, **Where and when**. Under them draw a row of boxes for **Events**, joined by arrows. At the foot draw one wide box labelled **Message** and a smaller line beneath it: "The events show ___."

The teacher holds the map on day 5; partners hold it on day 6; each child holds their own on day 8. It returns in 1.3 for the grandmother story.
