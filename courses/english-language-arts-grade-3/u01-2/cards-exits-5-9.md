# Exit cards, days 5 to 9

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children write. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 5 (s01-X5).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "Lia's friend could not carry all her books. Lia held half of them until they reached home. Together they carried every book safely." Ask: Write one sentence. What does Lia's action show about helping?
- **Day 6 (s01-X6).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "Sam missed the first stop. He checked the route card and walked to the next one. There he borrowed the book he needed." Ask: Explain how Sam walking on to the next stop changed what happened.
- **Day 7 (s01-X7).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "The line felt like a mile long to Tara, but she kept waiting." Picture: Tara is at the back of a long line. She looks tired. Her shoulders are low. She holds her coins tightly. Ask: What does like a mile long tell about Tara's wait? Use the words and the picture.
- **Day 8 (s01-X8).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "Sam found a wet book beside the stop. He dried its cover and returned it to the librarian. Another reader could borrow it." Ask: Write three sentences for a younger reader. What do Sam's actions show about shared books? Use the events as proof.
- **Day 9 (s01-X9).** This strip is invented for practice. Read: "Mina could not carry her books to the stop. Her arms were full of bags. Her neighbor Joao set the books in a wheelbarrow and pushed it up the lane." Ask: Write one sentence on what your partner's answer showed you.
