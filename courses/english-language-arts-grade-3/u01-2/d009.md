# Day 9: Talking about the boat and the bus

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make six groups of five. Set one table with five chairs. Mark the record sheet with a column for each of SL.3.1a, b, c, d and SL.3.3. Find the day 9 line of exit cards 5 to 9.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Nothing is taught today. As children settle, hand back the day 8 responses and marked pages (s01-P8-1, s01-P8-2) with one comment each. Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each week 2 word once and children write it: village, valley, steep, stone, climb, week, wall, bus. Ten minutes: partners reread a favorite page of the bus story, twice each, quietly. Ten minutes: each child adds a line to the reading log.

#### 13 min. Independent preparation

Each child rereads the two preparation pages and the details marked on day 8. On a note card, each writes three words for one detail and three words for one question to ask a peer. Silent.

#### 42 min. Group windows

Six groups of five, 7 minutes each. The topic: how Lia and Sam get ready to borrow again. Each child gets about 80 seconds: a prepared claim, 25 seconds; one linked question for a peer, 15 seconds; one answer with detail, 25 seconds; one sentence on what became clearer, 15 seconds. Five children take 6 minutes 40 seconds, and 20 seconds is left to change groups. Teach nothing and give no hint. Use the cue "Your turn" from the discussion chart.

Record each child on the record sheet while they talk, with the end items: [[s01-A1]] (the claim, SL.3.1a), [[s01-A2]] (the cue and turns, SL.3.1b), [[s01-A3]] (the linked question, SL.3.1c), [[s01-A4]] (what became clearer, SL.3.1d), [[s01-A5]] (a question to the speaker, SL.3.3) and [[s01-A73]] (an answer, SL.3.3). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and write down any prompt. No grade is given. Collect the note cards and exit sentences at the end of the day. The 25 children away from the table read silently from their logs.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 9 card aloud twice. Pairs tell each other what Joao's action did for Mina, with a detail from the strip, for 2 minutes. Then each child writes alone: [[s01-X9]]. Unscored. Shown: names what the partner said, with a detail, and what it showed. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What did your partner add?" Not yet seen: only "It was good."

### Other rooms

No table: use one corner. No pairs: the adult is the partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: use any short story and mark one detail.
**Skip.** The group window.

- **5 min. Assessment.** First, your child answers [[s01-X9]] alone, with the adult as partner.
- **20 min. Read.** Read with an adult for 20 minutes.
- **10 min. Say.** Say one detail you used and one thing the talk made clear.
  **Say.** "What became clearer?" Expect: a plain sentence about what a partner added.
- **10 min. Read.** Add a line to your reading log.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the log line. Longer: a child chooses a second detail.

### Words and supports

Prepare, cue, link, explain. Frames: "You said ___; I would add ___." "Now I see ___ because ___." Large print; a spoken description of each picture; a scribe for the exit sentence, noted. A child may speak or point in the window. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 opens with five minutes on E8 and E9: error Q (an opinion with no detail, "I like Joao"), and error T (an old view repeated, "I still think the same"). Day 10 reads the grandmother story whole.
