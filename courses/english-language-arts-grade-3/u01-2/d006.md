# Day 6: What Sam does to get his book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the six cause and result cards for each pair. Make one story map sheet for the board and one for each pair. Find the day 6 line of exit cards 5 to 9. Sort the day 5 maps and message sentences and write one comment on each. List your four conference children from the record sheet, skipping anyone you heard on day 5.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

Three minutes repair day 5's exit, and hand back the day 5 maps and message sentences (s01-P5-1, s01-P5-2) with one comment each. If many children missed it, use the letter you saw most.

If error B (a topic given as the message): read a new strip: "Ben's seed pot was dry. He watered it each morning for a week. A green shoot came up." **Ask.** What did Ben do, and what happened because of it? Expect: he watered it every morning, so a shoot came up. Write "It is about plants" beside it and ask what is missing. Children write one claim with so: "Watering every day made the seed grow."

If error C (the events reversed): number the two events on the board and draw an arrow from first to second. Read it with so. The cards below practise the same order.

Then seven minutes. Review: in grade 2 you wrote a sentence with because, and put what happened first. Pairs put out the six cause and result cards. Each pair puts two cards in order and says it with so, then with because: "The bell rang, so the class lined up." "The class lined up because the bell rang." The cause comes first with so. Do all three pairs of cards. Unscored. Walk and listen.

#### 20 min. Skills

*4 min. Words.* Review: in grade 2 you mapped a tricky word before reading it. Map two bus-story words. Write climb: c-l-i-m, with i saying its long sound, as in time, and the b at the end making no sound; underline the b. Write wall: w-a-ll, where a says /aw/. Children say the sounds, blend, and say a sentence. Then read lane, stone, steep, week and bus by their patterns, never by guessing (error P): lane and stone have a silent e; steep and week have the vowel team ee; bus is a closed syllable. Pin climb and wall on the sound and spelling wall.

*16 min. Four conferences.* Review: in grade 2 you held four-minute reading conferences. Hear four children, 4 minutes each (16 minutes). Ask why this book, hear the child read a page aloud twice, and say one thing that went well and one next try. The four children go on the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs.

#### 20 min. Model

Read the whole bus story aloud (543 words, about 5 minutes), showing each picture. Say first: "This story is made up. Only the valley is real. It is Swaledale, in England." Say plainly that the weather is wet and the walk is long and that the story shows an ordinary day. Anyone may step out and come back. Do not ask children about their own travel or their own libraries.

Then start the map on the board (15 minutes). Review: in grade 2 you used a story map with events joined by arrows. Fill the top boxes: **Who:** Sam. **Want:** book two, before the bus leaves. **Where and when:** Swaledale, a Thursday, the bus stays one hour. Then the events, each in a box with an arrow:

- Sam plans: he checks the bus card and packs the first book.
- The hill is steep and his legs feel like jelly.
- He could turn back. He makes a counting game with the walls.
- He reaches the village and the bus.
- Book two is out, then Hannah brings it back, so Sam borrows it.

Say: "Sam wants book two. That want is his motivation. What he does shows what he is like, and that is a trait." Write trait beside the map. Show the idea with [[steps:s01-K6-1]]. Ask first and let children answer before you reveal each step.

**Ask.** What does Sam want, and what does he do to get it? Expect: he wants book two; he plans, climbs the hill and makes a counting game.

Leave the Message box empty until the end. Say: "Sam kept going. What did that change?" Write the result in the box, then the message: "Sam made a plan and kept going, so he reached the book he wanted."

#### 25 min. Hand work

Partners work at once with their own sheet. First 15 minutes: they complete the map for pages 3 to 6 with [[s01-P6-1]]. Each event gets a box, an arrow and the words that show it. Walk and look. If a pair adds a fact the page does not give (error A), put a finger on the sentence and ask where it is.

Then 10 minutes: each child writes two lines with [[s01-P6-2]]: what Sam wants, then how his effort changed what happened, with so. Each child points to the words used. Judged by you with four look-fors: what Sam wants, what he does, what changed, so in the right order. If a child writes "He likes books" (error B), ask: "What did he do?" If a child writes the result first (error C), number the events.

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: in grade 2 you used the four-step chart (turn, pair, link, clarify); in grade 3 the chart adds a fifth move. Pairs talk all at once. Each child takes 2 minutes to say what Sam's effort changed and to name the words used (4 minutes in all). Then the partner asks for a detail and links back, "You said ___; I would add ___," for 3 minutes. Anyone may pass. Then three volunteers share one sentence each, 1 minute each (3 minutes). Listen to two pairs and add them to the heard-six column beside the four conference children.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 6 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X6]]. Unscored. Shown: what Sam wants, what he does, how that changed the result, with so in order. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What did he want? What did he do?" Not yet seen: only what Sam likes, or the events reversed.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the map boxes on scrap paper and say the cause cards aloud. No projector: build the map on the board. No pairs: the adult is the partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No story pages: use any story where a character works to get something.
**Skip.** The conferences, the cause cards and the partner talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Reread pages 3 and 4 aloud twice, the second time like talking.
  **Say.** "What does Sam want, and what does he do to get it?" Expect: he wants book two; he climbs and counts the walls.
- **15 min. Write.** Answer [[s01-P6-2]] in two lines.
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together; say what a character wants.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X6]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the cause cards to one pair and keep the map, the two lines and the exit. Longer: a child maps page 7 and says what the sunlight on the walls changes.

### Words and supports

Motivation, trait, effort, result. Frames: "Sam wants ___. He ___, so ___." Preview hill, bus, village, valley and wall with a picture for each. Large print; a spoken description of each picture for a child who cannot see it; a scribe for the two lines, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 7 opens with three minutes on this exit. Error C is the action and result reversed ("He borrowed the book and so he missed the stop"). Error B is a trait given with no link to the events ("He likes books"). Day 7 rereads the bus story for word meaning and what the pictures add.
