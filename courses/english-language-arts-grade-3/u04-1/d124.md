# Day 124: Check a new water-book panel for questions, main idea and vocabulary

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, with P124 on side 2. Tell children to keep side 2 face down until the panel part. Cut nothing; the word strip is on side 1 under a fold line. This is a check: no help with the items, no scoring today. Do not read the passage before the panel part. Day 123 ended with its exit; nothing from it is repaired today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word retrieval

Say each of eight words twice; children write them, [[s04-P124-1]]. Then they fold the page along the line, unfold it, read the eight words on the strip silently and mark the hardest, [[s04-P124-2]]. Unscored. Do not give a spelling.

#### 20 min. Silent independent reading

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P124-3]]. Move among them; no conferences. Record who read the whole time.

#### 15 min. Read the panel

Children turn to side 2. *6 min.* They read Keeping Rain Nearby silently. *3 min.* Read it aloud once. *6 min.* They reread. Children mark nothing. Read the diagram line aloud with the passage, once.

#### 35 min. Items

One ask at a time, in order. Say only the ask. *10 min.* [[s04-A1]]. *10 min.* [[s04-A2]]. *8 min.* [[s04-A3]]. *7 min.* [[s04-A4]]. Record the support used for each child: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Unscored this time.

#### 5 min. Independent review

Children reread their own answers and fix one thing beside the first answer, [[s04-P124-4]]. Collect the pages.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X124]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a meaning that fits the creek card, and a clue from the card. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which words tell you what the water was doing?" Not yet seen: a meaning for current that is the time now, or no clue. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the passage aloud once and the child answers on paper. The items stay on paper. One projector: show the passage, then the page.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed passage: any short informational paragraph of your own.
**Skip.** The word dictation and the review.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Your child reads side 2 of the page, then answers [[s04-A1]] to [[s04-A4]] alone. Give no help.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then writes the log line, [[s04-P124-3]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X124]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: not for a check; each ask stays. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Supplies, regularly, before, after, evidence, reflect. Read the passage aloud again for any child who asks; record any help; large print.

### Tomorrow

Day 125 hands the page back with one comment each, then repairs the commonest slip. The pages, [[s04-A1]] to [[s04-A4]], stay with the teacher for marking. The word work, [[s04-P124-1]] and [[s04-P124-2]], goes in the folder; the passage is kept for day 131.

### Side 2

**Keeping Rain Nearby.**

A school roof sends rain into a covered tank. The stored water supplies the school garden during a dry week. Keeping some rain nearby reduces the water the garden needs from other sources.

Before water reaches the tank, a screen catches leaves. The garden worker checks the cover regularly. The water is for plants. This account does not say it is drinking water.

Diagram: roof, screen, covered tank, garden. An arrow joins each box to the next. Caption: Rain moves through the screen before storage.

Words to know: cover means a top that closes a container.
