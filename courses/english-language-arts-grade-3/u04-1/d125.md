# Day 125: Use reading feedback, then read the access account beside the water book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, section 5, the Kitui account and the table for each child. Mark each day 124 page with one comment. Sort the day 123 exit slips and boxes with them. Choose the six children heard at the table today. Sort the day 124 pages by the commonest letter, in at most four groups. Day 124 was the check; today hands it back.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P125-1]]. You teach access today; important was on day 82. Then they add -ed to three words, [[s04-P125-2]]. Review: you learned the ending rules on day 101. *6 min.* Say water and source twice each; children write them, [[s04-P125-3]]. Watch for error AK, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P125-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one thing noticed that fits it, [[s04-P125-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Feedback

Hand back day 124's assessment with one comment each: every written task, and the four answers. Hand back the day 123 slip and box with them. Children read the comment silently and mark one thing to fix, [[s04-P125-6]]. Do not explain the comments aloud.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Groups by the commonest letter on the day 124 page, at most four groups. *2 min.* Each group reads the right line on its own copy. *6 min.* Each child redoes the one item for the group, a different case from the passage: section 4 or Diagram 2. Groups are A, B, C, D and E.

- Group 1, [[s04-P125-7]].
- Group 2, [[s04-P125-8]].
- Group 3, [[s04-P125-9]].
- Group 4, [[s04-P125-10]].
- Group 5, [[s04-P125-11]].

*2 min.* Each child compares the redo with a partner from the group. Review: you did return and repair on day 93. Do not reopen the day 124 passage; it is kept for day 131.

#### 20 min. Read

*8 min.* Read section 5, Access to Clean Water, aloud once, then children reread it silently. They write one line that says what it is about, [[s04-P125-12]]. Review: you said the gist of an opening on day 53. *12 min.* Read the Kitui account aloud in parts, then silently. Read the zone table together. Each child reads the table in the account, [[s04-P125-13]]. Do not ask where a child's water comes from. The account names real places and their sources and solutions; read it plainly.

#### 12 min. Notes

Children fill the book and account table: one important point and one key detail for each text, [[s04-P125-14]]. Review: you charted important points and key details on day 123. Pairs compare tables, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, and each says one point they both wrote, [[s04-P125-15]]. Watch for a point with no detail beside it.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child says whether the book and the account agree about access, and one way, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P125-16]]. Passing is fine.

#### 8 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the cards aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X125]]. Support is recorded. Shown: an opinion, two reasons from the cards, a link word and a closing sentence. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the card say?" Not yet seen: a fact not on the cards (error A), a shared point only (error F), liking a card (error M) or no closing (error O). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult hands back the day 124 page, reads section 5 and the account aloud, and the child fills the table. The exit stays on paper. One projector: show the account and the table one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed account: read section 5 from any copy of the water book you have.
**Skip.** The group repair and the partner talk.

- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads section 5 aloud, then writes one line, [[s04-P125-12]].
  **Say.** "Do the book and the account agree?" Expect: both say reaching water can take time and effort.
- **10 min. Table.** Your child fills the table, [[s04-P125-14]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X125]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the table and the exit; cut the long words. Longer: write a third key detail for each text.

### Words and supports

Access, important, detail, round trip, zone, key detail. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. For example, ___." Section 5 may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the table; a drawing may carry a detail with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 126 rereads the access account beside the book. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X125, likely error A: point to the line on the card before writing a claim. The book and account table, [[s04-P125-14]], is kept for day 126. The line, [[s04-P125-12]], goes to the teacher with the exit slips.
