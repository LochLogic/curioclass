# Day 123: Read how people use water and chart important points and key details

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and section 4 for each child. Look at the day 122 exit slips and pick the commonest slip, likely error E, a label read from the line and not from the picture. Choose the six children heard at the table today. Draw Diagram 2 on the board, and a three-column chart beside it: only the school card, both cards, only the farm card.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P123-1]]. Review: you met -ment on day 53 and -ous on day 54, and you split three-syllable words from day 81. *5 min.* Say water, source and treatment twice each; children write them, [[s04-P123-2]]. Treatment is the new spelling today. Watch for error AK, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P123-3]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one detail that helped it, [[s04-P123-4]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

*3 min.* Say the day 122 slip without a name: one answer read the label from the line and said nothing the picture shows. Point to the board. The line says what the tap does; the picture shows which part comes first and which comes next. *2 min.* Each child traces the arrows on Diagram 2 with a finger and says first, next and last, [[s04-P123-5]]. Hear about six; the rest say it to a partner.

#### 20 min. Read and chart

*6 min.* Read section 4, How People Use Water, aloud once, then children reread it silently. Ask each pair to tell one way people use water, [[s04-P123-6]]. *14 min.* Read the school card and the farm card on the page aloud. Review: in grade 2 you compared the main points of two texts on one topic. You teach the chart today: an important point is what the cards share; a key detail is a fact only one card has. Model one row on the board. Children fill their own chart, [[s04-P123-7]]. Put the shared point in the middle column. Do not fill the middle column first for them. Watch for a shared point put under only one card: model one matched row with that pair, then they try the next row.

#### 20 min. Write

*5 min.* Each child chooses one detail from the chart and copies the words from its card, [[s04-P123-8]]. *10 min.* Children write why the detail is a key detail, using because, [[s04-P123-9]]. Review: you joined ideas with link words on day 8. *5 min.* Pairs read each other's sentence and point to its words on the card, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P123-10]]. Watch for a sentence about section 4 and not the cards.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child says which detail best helps a point on the chart, and why, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P123-11]]. Passing is fine. Watch for a detail picked because it is liked.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence on the page. Children find the clue and write what demand means, [[s04-P123-12]]. Demand is new today. Say it, and use it once about water.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read both texts aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X123v2]]. Then they turn to the exit box on the page and write alone, [[s04-X123bv2]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a point both texts share, and a detail only text B has. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What do both texts say people build?" Not yet seen: a detail named as the shared point, a point that fits one card, or a copied card line. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads section 4 and the two cards aloud, and the child fills the chart. The chart, the writing and the exit stay on paper. One projector: show the cards and the chart one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed book: read section 4 from any copy of the water book you have.
**Skip.** The table reading, the conferences and the partner talk.

- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads section 4 aloud, then tells you one way people use water.
  **Say.** "What do the school card and the farm card both say?" Expect: both need water for drinking.
- **10 min. Chart.** Your child fills the chart, [[s04-P123-7]].
- **5 min. Write.** Your child answers [[s04-P123-9]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X123v2]] and [[s04-X123bv2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chart, the writing and the exit; cut the long words and the word in context. Longer: add a third card of your own and chart it.

### Words and supports

Demand, treatment, important point, key detail, shared, only. Frame: "Both cards say ___. Only the ___ card says ___." Section 4 may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the cards; a drawing may carry a detail with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 124 is the check for this section: a new text, read cold. Nothing from today's exit is repaired there. The exit slip, [[s04-X123v2]], and the exit box, [[s04-X123bv2]], go back on day 125 with the day 124 comments. The chart, [[s04-P123-7]], and the sentence, [[s04-P123-9]], stay in the folder; the word, [[s04-P123-12]], goes to the teacher with the exit slips.
