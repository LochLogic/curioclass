# Day 120: Read the water book's opening and ask why clean freshwater matters

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 119 ended unit 3; today starts unit 4. Hand back day 119's assessment with its comments at the door. Print the student page and section 1 of the water book for each child. Write the four words reflect, explain, water and source on cards. Put out the glass of water and the salt and fresh cards. Choose the six children heard at the table today; the next six are heard on day 121.

### The day

#### 10 min. Word check

*4 min.* Say each word twice in a sentence: reflect, explain, water, source. Children write them, [[s04-P120-1]]. Then they draw a line from each word to its meaning, [[s04-P120-2]]. *6 min.* The six chosen children come to the table one at a time, one minute each, and read the four cards, [[s04-P120-3]]. Review: source from grade 1, day 110, and explain from kindergarten, day 39. Water and source are the irregular words: the a in water says o, and ou in source says or. Record each reading. Watch for error AK, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P120-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one thing noticed, [[s04-P120-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Hook

Hold up the glass of water. Then show the two cards, salt and fresh. Ask the class, [[s04-P120-6]]. Take two answers, then say the word together: freshwater. Fresh means very little salt. Say only that today.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Children write three places where water is found, [[s04-P120-7]]. Review: you learned where water is found in grade 2 science. Do not ask where their own water comes from. Watch for a place repeated.

#### 17 min. Read and annotate

*2 min.* Read section 1, Why Water Matters, aloud. *3 min.* Children reread it silently. *5 min.* Pairs each tell one key detail, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P120-8]]. Review: you stated details in grade 3 unit 1. *7 min.* Each child writes one line that says what section 1 is about, [[s04-P120-9]]. Review: you said the gist of an opening on day 53. Watch for error B, one word such as water.

#### 15 min. Model and frame

Show the model paragraph on the student page, [[s04-K120-1]]. Read it aloud once. Review: you built this on days 98 and 100: an opinion, two reasons with link words, and a closing line. The frame is: opinion, reason 1, reason 2, link word, closing. Children mark the parts on their page, [[s04-P120-10]]. They only mark today; they do not write a paragraph yet. Watch for error AL if anyone says the section tells us fresh water is safe: the section says what freshwater is, nothing more.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child tells a partner an opinion of the opening in one sentence, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P120-11]]. Passing is fine. Watch for error L, a fact with no opinion.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence on the page. Test fresh and water apart, then together, [[s04-P120-12]]. Freshwater is new today. Watch for error AL, "water you can drink".

#### 8 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X120]]. Support is recorded. Shown: an opinion, two reasons from the sign, a link word and a closing sentence. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the sign tell a visitor?" Not yet seen: fresh means safe to drink (error AL), liking the sign (error M), no opinion (error L), reasons not from the sign (error N) or no closing (error O). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the cards, hears the four words, and reads section 1 aloud. The log, the model and the exit stay on paper. One projector: show the model and the cards one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed book: read section 1 from any copy of the water book you have.
**Skip.** The table reading, the conferences and the partner talk.

- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads section 1 aloud, then writes one line, [[s04-P120-9]].
  **Say.** "What is section 1 mostly about?" Expect: everyone needs water, and freshwater has very little salt.
- **10 min. Model.** Your child marks the model, [[s04-P120-10]].
- **5 min. Word.** Your child answers [[s04-P120-12]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X120]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the model and the exit; cut the retrieval and the word in context. Longer: write a third reason beside the model.

### Words and supports

Freshwater, source, reflect, explain, opinion, reason. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. For example, ___." Section 1 may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the model; a drawing may carry a reason with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 121 reads where water is found. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X120, likely error AL: say what fresh means and what it does not tell you. The word work, [[s04-P120-1]] to [[s04-P120-3]], the retrieval, [[s04-P120-7]], the model marks, [[s04-P120-10]], and the word sentence, [[s04-P120-12]], stay in the folder. The one line, [[s04-P120-9]], goes to the teacher with the exit slips.
