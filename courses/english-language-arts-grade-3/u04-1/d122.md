# Day 122: Answer questions about how water moves, with the book's words and Diagram 2

## Teacher page

### Before class

The water book diagram art is owed: a person draws Diagram 2 before layout.

Print the student page, section 3, Diagram 2 and a half-page three-box chart for each child. Pair the children and give each pair a feedback card. Look at the day 121 exit slips and pick the commonest slip, likely error B, one small detail as the idea. Draw Diagram 2 large on the board.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Children split two words into syllables, [[s04-P122-1]]. Then they add -s or -es to three words, [[s04-P122-2]]. You teach the -s and -es rule today: after s, sh, ch, x or z, add -es. Say it with push and reach, then let them try flow. *5 min.* Say water and source twice each; children write them, [[s04-P122-3]]. Watch for error AK, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Tuesday

*5 min.* Children say the noun and the verb in a short sentence, [[s04-P122-4]]. Review: you named nouns and verbs on day 86. *15 min.* Partner rereading of section 3. Each child reads it aloud in turn to a partner, 5 minutes each, and the partner listens for phrases, [[s04-P122-5]]. Then each child marks the partner's feedback card, [[s04-P122-6]]. Review: you used the feedback card from day 2. Walk the room and listen to three pairs.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Say the day 121 slip without a name: one sentence named a small detail as the whole idea. Ask the class what the whole passage is about, then what a detail is. The idea covers every line; a detail covers one. *2 min.* Children say yesterday's idea from section 2 in one sentence. Do not hand it over.

#### 15 min. Read and answer

*6 min.* Read section 3, How Water Moves, aloud once. Then model three questions, 2 minutes each, [[s04-K122-1]]. You teach the frame today: "The text says ... so ...". Say the answer in those words each time and point to the line. *5 min.* Pairs ask each other one question the section can answer; the partner answers with The text says, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P122-7]]. *4 min.* Each child puts a finger on Diagram 2 and reads the five labels in the order of the arrows, [[s04-P122-8]]. Review: you read arrows and labels on day 54. Watch for a label read out of order.

#### 15 min. Four word strategies

Each word gets one way in, 2 minutes modelled and 1 minute for children on their page. Say each way aloud before the word.

- Context: read the sentence around condenses and write what it means, [[s04-P122-9]]. The clue is in the sentence; the strategy is new today.
- Affix: easy and -ly make easily, [[s04-P122-10]]. You learned the -ly pattern as an ending; today it is a meaning clue.
- Known root: evaporates is in section 3, so use it for evaporation, [[s04-P122-11]].
- Glossary: find groundwater in the words to know and copy its meaning, [[s04-P122-12]]. You met groundwater on day 121.

Keep the order. Say the strategy before the word, so children learn the way in, not four separate meanings. Watch for a guess that never looks at the sentence.

#### 15 min. Write

*10 min.* Children fill the three-box chart for Diagram 2: the label, what the arrow shows, what the words add, [[s04-P122-13]]. Model the first row on the board. Do not model the row a child will need alone. *5 min.* Each child writes one sentence from the chart about the water cycle, [[s04-P122-14]]. Watch for a sentence that repeats the label and adds nothing.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child reads the chart to a partner, row by row, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P122-15]]. Passing is fine. Watch for a chart read as a list of labels, with no arrows.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X122v2]]. Support is recorded. Shown: the label Filtration, and one thing the diagram shows that the line does not. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What do the arrows tell you?" Not yet seen: a label picked from the line only (error E), a repeat of the line (error AN), or no second part. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads section 3 aloud, then listens to the child read it; the feedback card is filled by the adult. The chart and the exit stay on paper. One projector: show Diagram 2 and the chart one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed book: read section 3 from any copy of the water book you have.
**Skip.** The partner reading, the feedback card and the partner talk.

- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads section 3 aloud, then reads the five labels on Diagram 2 in the order of the arrows.
  **Say.** "What happens first?" Expect: the sun warms the water, so some evaporates.
- **10 min. Chart.** Your child fills the chart, [[s04-P122-13]].
- **5 min. Word.** Your child answers [[s04-P122-9]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X122v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the chart and the exit; cut the long words and the noun and verb. Longer: write a second sentence from the chart.

### Words and supports

Evaporates, condenses, easily, evaporation, groundwater, label, arrow. Frame: "The text says ___, so ___." Section 3 may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the diagram; a drawing may carry a box with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 123 reads how people use water. It opens with the commonest slip on s04-X122v2, likely error E: a label read from the line, not from the picture. The long words, [[s04-P122-1]] to [[s04-P122-3]], the chart, [[s04-P122-13]], and the sentence, [[s04-P122-14]], stay in the folder. The feedback card, [[s04-P122-6]], goes back to its pair.
