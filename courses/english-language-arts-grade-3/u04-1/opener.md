# Section 4.1 opener: the water book (days 120 to 125)

## What the section does

For six days the class reads a short informational book about water on Earth, one section a day, and begins to compare it with a second account about how far people walk to reach water. Children practise asking a question the words can answer, finding the main idea, reading a diagram with its labels, and using a chart to join important points to key details. On days 120 and 125 the exit is a short opinion paragraph. Day 124 is a check on a new passage that stays unscored; day 125 returns it with one comment and a chance to redo one item.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Every day opens with ten minutes of long words, then twenty minutes of the independent reading block, either four short reading conferences or partner rereading. Days 121 to 123 each teach one section of the book with a worked example. Day 124 is silent: word retrieval, reading alone, one new passage, four items, a silent review, then the exit. Day 125 returns the passage, repairs the commonest slip in small groups, reads a section of the book and the second account, and ends with an opinion paragraph. Each exit is on paper, alone, and the commonest slip of one day is repaired in the next opening.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **The words on the page are the evidence.** A good answer points to a sentence. Ask: where does it say so?
- **The main idea covers the whole text.** A single detail is only one part. Test a sentence by asking whether it fits every line.
- **A detail supports the idea when you can say how.** Joining a detail to the idea with the word because shows the link.
- **A picture and its words work together.** Labels and arrows show order; the words explain why. Use both.
- **A word can have more than one meaning.** Read the whole sentence with each meaning and keep the one that fits.
- **Fresh is not the same as safe.** Water can be free of salt and still need treatment. Say what the text says, no more.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** Adds a fact the text never gives. Repair: point to the line before writing.
- **B:** Names one small detail as the main idea. Repair: test a sentence against every line.
- **C:** Lists details without saying how they support the idea. Repair: join each detail with because.
- **D:** Picks a familiar meaning that does not fit the sentence. Repair: read the whole sentence with each meaning.
- **E:** Reverses before and after. Repair: trace the arrows with a finger.
- **AK:** Guesses a long word from its first letter. Repair: read every syllable, then check with the sentence.
- **AL:** Treats fresh water as safe to drink. Repair: separate not salty from safe.
- **AN:** Uses the picture without its words. Repair: label the picture, then add one word from the line.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored and a check day stays unscored on the day.
