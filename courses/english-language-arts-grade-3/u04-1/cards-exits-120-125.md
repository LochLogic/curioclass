# Exit cards, days 120 to 125

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 120 (s04-X120), Exit.** Invented for practice. Sign: "Salt water fills the bay. A creek brings fresh water to the shore. Visitors may not know if the creek water is safe to drink." Ask: Would you tell a visitor to read this sign first? Write your opinion, two reasons from the sign, a link word and a closing sentence.
- **Day 121 (s04-X121), Exit.** Invented for practice. Caption: "In spring, snow on the hills melts. The meltwater runs into a stream. The stream fills a lake." Ask: Write one sentence that says what the caption is mainly about.
- **Day 122 (s04-X122), Exit.** Invented for practice. Diagram: "A Rain Barrel". Labels: gutter, barrel, tap. Arrows: gutter to barrel to tap. Line: "The tap lets out water for the garden." Ask: Which label comes right after the barrel? Write it, then write one thing the picture shows that the line does not.
- **Day 123 (s04-X123), Exit.** Invented for practice. First card: "A bakery needs water for dough and for washing trays. Bakers fill the tubs each morning." Second card: "A car wash needs water for cars and for washing floors. Workers spray the cars all day." Ask: Write one important point both cards share.
- **Day 123 (s04-X123b), Exit.** Invented for practice. First card: "A bakery needs water for dough and for washing trays. Bakers fill the tubs each morning." Second card: "A car wash needs water for cars and for washing floors. Workers spray the cars all day." Ask: Write one detail that only the bakery card has.
- **Day 124 (s04-X124), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "The creek's current was strong near the bridge. Hikers held a rope to cross." Ask: What does current mean here? Write a clue from the card.
- **Day 125 (s04-X125), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card A: "A school on a hill carries cans of water from a market tap. The walk takes an hour." Card B: "A town school has a tap in the yard. The water is tested each week." Ask: Which card would you use to start a lesson on access to water? Write your opinion, two reasons from the cards, a link word and a closing sentence.

Every exit sheet prints the card and the ask only, with a ruled space for the answer. The look-fors live in items.csv and the record sheet, never on the child's page.
