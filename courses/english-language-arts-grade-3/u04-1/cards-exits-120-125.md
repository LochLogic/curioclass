# Exit cards, days 120 to 125

A card marked invented: say aloud "This card is made up for practice," and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. A card from the unit's texts names its text and part and is not marked invented. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 120 (s04-X120), Exit.** Invented for practice. Sign: "Salt water fills the bay. A creek brings fresh water to the shore. Visitors may not know if the creek water is safe to drink." Ask: Would you tell a visitor to read this sign first? Write your opinion, two reasons from the sign, a link word and a closing sentence.
- **Day 121 (s04-X121v2), Exit.** This is from Water on Earth, section 7. It says: "Rain that runs over the ground picks up what it finds. It can carry litter, oil from roads, fertilizer from lawns, and waste ... The runoff carries these things into streams, lakes, and groundwater." Ask: Write one sentence that says what this part is mainly about.
- **Day 122 (s04-X122v2), Exit.** From How a Town Cleans Its Water. Diagram: "The steps in order." A row of tanks labeled Coagulation, Flocculation, Sedimentation and Filtration. Arrows: river, then each tank in that order, then a pipe "to homes." Line: "Then the water rests in a large tank." Ask: The line tells about Sedimentation. Which label comes right after it? Write it, then write one thing the diagram shows that the line does not.
- **Day 123 (s04-X123v2), Exit.** Text A, from Water on Earth: "Communities improve access. They dig wells, build tanks, lay pipes, and repair pumps." Text B, from Water Paths in Kitui County: "Many communities build sand dams. ... The water sinks into the sand and stays underground. People reach it through a well." Ask: Write one important point both texts share.
- **Day 123 (s04-X123bv2), Exit.** Use texts A and B on your slip. Write one detail only text B has.
- **Day 124 (s04-X124), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "The creek's current was strong near the bridge. Hikers held a rope to cross." Ask: What does current mean here? Write a clue from the card.
- **Day 125 (s04-X125), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card A: "A school on a hill carries cans of water from a market tap. The walk takes an hour." Card B: "A town school has a tap in the yard. The water is tested each week." Ask: Which card would you use to start a lesson on access to water? Write your opinion, two reasons from the cards, a link word and a closing sentence.

Every exit sheet prints the card and the ask only, with a ruled space for the answer. The look-fors live in items.csv and the record sheet, never on the child's page.
