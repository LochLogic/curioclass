# Day 121: Read where water is found and say what section 2 is mainly about

## Teacher page

### Before class

The water book diagram art is owed: a person draws Diagram 1 before layout.

Print the student page, section 2 and Diagram 1 for each child. Look at the day 120 exit slips and pick the commonest slip, likely error AL, fresh read as safe. Choose the six children heard at the table today; they are the six not heard yesterday. Draw an empty main-idea chart on the board: one box in the middle, three lines out.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Children split three words into syllables, [[s04-P121-1]]. Then they write water and source and circle the part that is not spelled as it sounds, [[s04-P121-2]]. Then they add -ing to three words, [[s04-P121-3]]. Review: you split long words from day 81 on, and you learned the ending rules on day 101. *6 min.* The six chosen children come to the table, one minute each, and read reflect, explain, water and source from the cards. Record each reading. Watch for error AK, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P121-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one thing noticed, [[s04-P121-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

*3 min.* Say the day 120 slip without a name: one answer said fresh means safe. Ask the class to finish, [[s04-P121-6]]. Say the repair in two lines: fresh tells us how little salt, not whether it is safe. Then ask one child to say the first thing the section needs us to know about water, and take another. *2 min.* Each child says the section 1 line again in one whole sentence, [[s04-P121-7]]. Hear about six; the rest say it to a partner. Do not praise the sentence for being long.

#### 20 min. Read

*2 min.* Read section 2, Where Water Is Found, aloud once. *4 min.* Children reread it silently. *6 min.* Pairs read Diagram 1 beside the section, 2 minutes each way and 1 to change over. Each child says what the big bar and the thin bar show, [[s04-P121-8]]. Review: you read arrows and labels on day 54. *8 min.* Build the main-idea chart on the board. Read three sentences about the section and mark the one that fits every line, [[s04-P121-9]]. Put that idea in the middle box. Children copy it, then find three details in the section and join each to the idea, [[s04-P121-10]]. Review: you made a main-idea chart on day 15. Watch for error B, one small detail named as the idea.

#### 20 min. Write

*5 min.* Each child says one because sentence to a partner before writing, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P121-11]]. Review: you joined ideas with link words on day 8. *15 min.* Children write two details from section 2 under the idea given on the page, each joined with because, [[s04-P121-12]]. Walk the room. Say a detail with the page covered; do not hand over a sentence. Watch for a detail that is a number alone, with no because.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Each child tells a partner which detail best supports the idea and why, 2 minutes each and 1 to change over, [[s04-P121-13]]. Passing is fine. Watch for a detail picked because it is liked.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence on the page. Children find the clue and write what source means, [[s04-P121-14]]. Source is the word you met yesterday: say it, spell its ou, and use it once.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the passage aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X121v2]]. Support is recorded. Shown: one sentence that covers the rain picking things up and carrying them into water. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Does your sentence fit every line?" Not yet seen: one small detail as the idea (error B), a sentence that says lake water is safe (error AL), two sentences, or a copied line. Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads section 2 aloud and the child points to the bars on Diagram 1. The chart, the writing and the exit stay on paper. One projector: show the chart and Diagram 1 one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed book: read section 2 from any copy of the water book you have.
**Skip.** The table reading, the conferences and the partner talk.

- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads section 2 aloud, then looks at Diagram 1.
  **Say.** "What does the thin bar show?" Expect: freshwater, which is a small part.
- **10 min. Chart.** Your child fills the chart, [[s04-P121-10]].
- **10 min. Write.** Your child writes two details with because, [[s04-P121-12]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X121v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the chart and the exit; cut the long words and the word in context. Longer: add a third detail with because.

### Words and supports

Freshwater, source, groundwater, frozen, idea, detail. Frame: "The idea is ___ because ___." Section 2 may be read aloud again for any child; large print for the diagram; a labelled drawing may carry a detail with a spoken sentence; record any help.

### Tomorrow

The word work, [[s04-P121-1]] to [[s04-P121-3]], the chart, [[s04-P121-10]], and the word sentence, [[s04-P121-14]], stay in the folder. The two details, [[s04-P121-12]], go to the teacher with the exit slips.
