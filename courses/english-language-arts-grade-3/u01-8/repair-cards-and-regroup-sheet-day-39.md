# Repair cards and regroup sheet (day 39)

## Repair cards

Four sets, one for each group by letter. A child takes the card for the commonest letter on their own results and repairs one missed response. The first form stays in the folder under the repair.

- **Evidence (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, V, W, Z, AC, AD).** Put a finger on the line before you answer. Say what the words say, then what they mean here. Check a word part or the glossary before you decide. Read a label or a clue before you use a number.
- **Writing (H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, AB).** Group facts that belong together. Link only related facts. End on the same topic. Explain it for a younger reader. Change the meaning, not only the copy. Count the owners, then place the apostrophe. Mark the base word and say the ending rule.
- **Decoding (P, X).** Blend the word by its parts, then check it with the sentence. Pause at each end mark so a listener hears where a sentence stops.
- **Discussion (Q, R, S, T, U, Y).** Mark a detail before you speak. Wait for the cue. Begin with your partner's words. Add something new. Give the detail when it is asked for. Name the pictured detail the passage explains.

## Fresh cards (one for each group, used in the last five minutes)

Each card is invented for practice. It asks the child to find, judge or mark, never to answer a short story question.

- **Evidence.** Read: "Mina left the key under the mat so her brother could get in. He found it after school." Judge this answer: "Mina wanted him to be late." Mark the words on the card that show if it is right.
- **Writing.** Two closing lines for a note on packing a bag. A: "Packing the night before helps me leave on time." B: "My cousin has a red bike." Tick the line that stays on the topic.
- **Decoding.** Draw a slash between the parts of each word, then read it: kindness, rewashed, helpless. Draw a slash after each end mark in: "Pip opens the box. It is empty!"
- **Discussion.** A partner says: "The boy walks to the bus every Friday." Tick the reply that links to it. A: "I like pizza." B: "You said every Friday. What does he carry?"

## Regroup sheet

A list for the teacher, one row for each child: the one repair the child took, the next unit's group, and a note on any supported records. The sheet goes to the next unit with the folders.
