# Exit cards, days 35 to 39

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the day 39 card. Days 35 to 37 use the child's own work. Day 38's exit is the first two tale items, with no extra time. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 35 (s01-X35).** This request is invented for practice. Listener request: "Help me hear where one sentence ends." Use your own retained recording. Ask: Write the word where your listener hears one sentence end.
- **Day 36 (s01-X36).** The child's contract and its written feedback. Ask: What will you change in your bookmark plan because of one feedback note?
- **Day 37 (s01-X37).** Reader request: "Explain getting ready so I can try it tomorrow." Ask: Revise one explanation in your contract so this reader could try it.
- **Day 38.** The tale items are the exit; no extra card.
- **Day 39 (s01-X39).** Read: "Eli read the page number as seven. His partner said, 'That is not the page we are on.' Eli looked again. The page said eleven." Ask: What should Eli do next time to catch a mistake in a number?
- **Day 39, spoken (s01-X39b).** Say aloud, with no card: "Tell your partner one thing you changed from your first plan to your bookmark." Hear six children.
- **Day 39, spoken (s01-X39c).** The first forty-two words of the Bangladesh section of "Books on the Move", from "In the Chalan Beel region" to "library boats", printed on a card for you. Ask: Read these forty-two words alone, with no help. One child at a time, one minute each.
