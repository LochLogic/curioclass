# Day 39: Share the work, repair it and regroup

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the repair cards and fresh cards, the day 39 exit cards, the forty-two-word card and the regroup sheet. Mark each child's commonest letter on the day 37 and day 38 results. Sort the folders into four groups by letter. List the six children with an AA record.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and share

*6 min. Return.* Hand back all the evidence: day 37's assessment (s01-A45 to s01-A55) and the exit (s01-X37), then day 38's assessment (s01-A56 to s01-A70) and the bookmarks, with the recording and group notes. If many wrote a recopy or an answer a younger reader could not follow (error K), say: "What did the reader need that was not there?" (2 minutes). Each child marks one letter (2 minutes). *4 min. Share.* Partners show the bookmark and say what they changed from the plan, 2 minutes each, [[s01-X39b]]. Hear six children as you move; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 30 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* Review: -ness (day 31), -ly (day 32), -able (day 33), un- and re- (day 30). Say each word once and children write it on slates: kindness, slowly, washable, replay, unkind, careful. *5 min. Grammar.* Review: link two facts with because (day 34). Write two facts about the boat library and join them. *7 min. Partner rereading.* Review: partner rereading with the feedback card. Pairs read the 86 words of "Books on the Move" from "Its State Library started" to "for six weeks", twice each, and tick the card. Listen to two pairs. *10 min.* Independent reading with two conferences of 5 minutes.

#### 20 min. Apply repairs

Four groups by letter: evidence (errors A, B, C, D, E, F, G, V, W, Z, AC, AD), writing (errors H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, AB), decoding (errors P, X) and discussion (errors Q, R, S, T, U, Y). Lead decoding for 15 minutes; the other groups work in pairs from their written repair cards. Each child repairs one missed response, [[s01-P39-1]], and keeps the first form below it. Then 5 minutes: each child reads the fresh card for the repair and does it, [[s01-P39-2]]. Evidence group: error A is an answer that is not from the text. Writing group: error K is a contract a younger reader could not follow; error M is a recopy. Discussion group: error Y is a display that does not fit.

#### 20 min. Discuss revisions

Three minutes: each child marks one detail in "Books on the Move", [[s01-P39-3]]. Twelve minutes: six groups of five talk at once on how the carriers change who can read. Spend 2 minutes with each group and hear one child, the heard-six child, for the record. This fills the 12 minutes with no spare: if a visit runs over, take that child's turn first tomorrow. Then 5 minutes: read the day 39 card aloud twice and children write alone, [[s01-X39]]. Unscored. Shown: says to look again at the number and check it with a partner or the page. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What did his partner say?" Not yet seen: says to read faster (error X).

#### 10 min. Regroup

*4 min.* Assign each child to a group for the next unit by the earliest unmet prerequisite from the component scores, on the regroup sheet. *6 min.* The six children with an AA record read the forty-two-word card alone, one minute each, [[s01-X39c]]; the rest read. This fills the six minutes: if one runs over, finish that child on day 40. Accurate components receive independent reading.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult returns the work, repairs one response with the card and hears the bookmark share and the forty-two words. Last day of the unit: show the bookmark and say what changed. No printer: copy the repair card by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No bookmark: say one thing the unit taught you.
**Skip.** The slate words.

- **10 min. Tell.** Your child says one thing the unit taught them about getting past what stops us reading, [[s01-X39b]].
  **Say.** "What changed?" Expect: one thing they changed, with a reason.
- **10 min. Repair.** Your child reads the repaired response aloud, [[s01-P39-1]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X39]] and [[s01-X39b]] alone, and reads one page aloud, [[s01-X39c]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the grammar and one reading; keep the repair, the fresh card and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second repair for another note.

### Words and supports

Repair, share, regroup, carrier, route. Frame: "I repaired ___ because ___." Large print; the repair card read aloud; a scribe, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 40 begins the frog poems. The regroup list and the folders go on; the repaired response stays in the folder. Today's exit (s01-X39) and the repair practice (s01-P39-1, s01-P39-2) go back in the opening of the next unit.
