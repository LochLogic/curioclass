# Bookmark strip (day 38)

A tall strip of paper, about 5 cm by 20 cm, for each child. The bookmark publishes the contract.

- **Top:** my strategy, in a few words.
- **Middle:** one step and its reason.
- **Bottom:** my closing sentence.
- **Back:** my name and the date.

Children copy only from their revised contract. A child may add a small drawing.
