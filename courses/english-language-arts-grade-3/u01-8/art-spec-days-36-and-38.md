# Art spec: day 36 presentation and day 38 panel diagram

## Day 36 (text g3-u1-d36s)

- **Picture.** An original plain line drawing of a child taking a used sheet from a tray. A short title, "Using both sides".
- **Graph.** An original bar graph with the title "Paper sheets used". The horizontal axis is labeled "Day" with Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday. The vertical axis is labeled "Number of sheets", equal steps of one from 0 to 12. The bars show 12, 9 and 6.
- **Image description.** "A child takes a used sheet from a tray." And: "Bars fall from twelve on Monday to nine on Tuesday and six on Wednesday."

## Day 38 (text g3-u1-09)

- **Diagram.** One original diagram in two parts side by side. Left, labeled "First copy": one sheet with tiny, pale words. Right, labeled "Second copy": two sheets with larger, dark words and a bracket marking the blank strip along one edge, labeled "margin". An arrow runs from left to right.
- **Image description.** "A first copy with small faint words on one sheet, and a second copy with bigger dark words on two sheets, with the edge strip marked margin."
- Print "invented for practice" at the foot of both.

## Day 38 tale picture (text g3-u1-04, The Foolish, Timid Rabbit; item s01-A59)

- **Picture.** One new original plain line drawing with one flat color, thick lines, no shading. A long crowd of rabbits, a deer, a fox and an elephant runs along a path, mouths open and eyes wide. In front of them, on a small rise, the Lion stands still with his head up and his tail low. No text in the picture. No caption. No motion lines, so the item asks what the crowd and the calm Lion add, not what a drawing trick adds.
- **Image description.** "A crowd of frightened animals runs along a path toward a Lion who stands calm on a small rise."
- Not a copy of any older illustration of the tale.
