# Day 38: Check the tale, the panel and the bookmark

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the tale packet, the panel with diagram and glossary, the new tale picture and the bookmark strip. Find the revised contracts and the bookmark plans. Set out pencils. Teach nothing today.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each word once and children write it: careful, unkind, helpless, replay, slowly, kindness. Mark nothing. Twenty minutes: each child reads from their log.

#### 10 min. Tale reading

Alone, silently: "The Foolish, Timid Rabbit" (595 words, about 10 minutes). Read it aloud to anyone who needs it and record the support. Say nothing about it.

#### 8 min. First tale items

Alone, in writing, with no hint: [[s01-A56]] (3 minutes) and [[s01-A57]] (5 minutes). These two are the exit: no extra card. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and write down any prompt.

#### 7 min. Remaining literary items

[[s01-A58]] (3 minutes), [[s01-A59]] with the new picture (2 minutes) and [[s01-A63]] (2 minutes). Alone, in writing.

#### 5 min. Panel reading

Alone, silently: the library notice panel, with its diagram and glossary, twice. Say nothing about it.

#### 9 min. Informational items

[[s01-A60]] (3 minutes), [[s01-A61]] (4 minutes) and [[s01-A62]] (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Vocabulary items

Five items at 2 minutes each: [[s01-A64]], [[s01-A65]], [[s01-A66]], [[s01-A67]] and [[s01-A68]]. Items may be read aloud only where reading is not the evidence.

#### 8 min. Bookmark publication

Each child writes the bookmark from the plan and the revised contract, [[s01-A71]], on the strip: the strategy, one step with its reason and a closing sentence, in forty words or fewer. No new essay. Then sign and date the back. After class, hear each child's two readings from your notes, [[s01-A69]] and [[s01-A70]]: does the child explain the lion's check and the copying steps without an adult retelling the text? Do not score them again.

#### 3 min. Collection

Collect the sheets and the bookmarks.

### Other rooms

One child: a quiet hour and a half; read each item aloud only where reading is not the evidence, and note support. No printer: copy the tale and the panel by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No bookmark: any strip of paper.
**Skip.** The slate words.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the panel and answers [[s01-A60]] alone.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads a book silently, then tells you what it was mainly about.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child says what the bookmark says and why, [[s01-A71]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the slate words to three; keep the items and the bookmark. Longer: a child reads a second short text and gives its main idea.

### Words and supports

Tale, panel, diagram, glossary, bookmark. Frame: "The bookmark shows ___." Large print; the tale read aloud for those who need it, recorded as support; a scribe, noted.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 returns the day 38 sheets (s01-A56 to s01-A70) and the bookmarks, with your notes. The day 37 marks go to the repairs. Error A is an answer that is not from the text: find the words that say it. The bookmarks go back to the children to share.
