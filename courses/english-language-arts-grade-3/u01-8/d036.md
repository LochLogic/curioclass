# Day 36: Use feedback, listen and talk in groups

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the look-for card, the discussion page, the presentation picture and graph and the day 36 exit card. Write your feedback on each contract before class. Teach nothing today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Hand-back.* Return the day 34 exits (s01-X34, s01-X34b), the day 35 exit (s01-X35) and day 35's assessment (s01-A35 to s01-A38) with the day 35 pages (s01-P35-1, s01-P35-2). Error J is a closing on a new topic. Error X is reading faster. Error W is a recording that explains nothing. Error Y is a display that does not fit. Each child finds their letter. *4 min.* Partners swap contracts and tick the look-for card, [[s01-P36-2]]. *3 min.* Read your written feedback silently.

Check: a tick only where the contract shows the strategy and the grouped reasons.

#### 20 min. Skills, silent

Independent reading from the logs, with silent retrieval cards. Say nothing about the words.

#### 9 min. Presentation and response

Present the script twice with the picture and the graph, about 3 minutes in all. Children prepare silently for 3 minutes. Then write alone for 3 minutes: [[s01-A43]]. Give no hint. Shown: the main idea, a spoken detail, a picture detail and a number with its label.

#### 9 min. Bookmark plan

Children plan the bookmark from the contract, [[s01-P36-1]]. Then they change the plan to answer one written note, [[s01-A72]]. Then read the day 36 card aloud twice. Children answer alone: [[s01-X36]]. Unscored. Shown: names a change in meaning that answers a real note. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which note did you answer?" Not yet seen: a recopy (error M).

#### 42 min. Group windows

Six groups of five, 6 minutes of talk and 1 to move. In turn, each child tells a detail from the discussion page, [[s01-A39]]; everyone speaks twice. Each child asks a partner one question, [[s01-A41]], and says what the talk made clearer, [[s01-A42]]. Another child asks the speaker about her brother, [[s01-A44]]. Record taking turns by watching, not asking, [[s01-A40]]. Write down any prompt. The window fills all seven minutes: if one runs over, stop it and record what you heard. The other groups work on the plan and read. Collect the pages.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the speaker and the group; take the answers aloud, noted. No printer: draw the graph by hand. No groups of five: take turns with one partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No graph: read any chart in a book.
**Skip.** The group talk.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Your child tells you the one change they made to their plan, [[s01-P36-1]].
  **Say.** "Which note did you answer?" Expect: the note and the change.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you one detail they would share about the discussion page.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads aloud from their log.
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X36]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tick only the strategy. Longer: a child writes a second closing line.

### Words and supports

Presentation, graph, bar, feedback, plan. Frames: "I changed ___ because ___." Large print; the graph described in words, never the answer; a scribe, noted.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 hands back the exit (s01-X36), the plan (s01-P36-1, s01-A72) and the partner marking (s01-P36-2) in the revision part. Error M is a recopy: change what the contract says, move the fact under its reason. The contract and feedback stay for the revision.
