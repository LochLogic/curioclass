# Section 1.8 opener: records, a revision and a regroup (days 35 to 39)

## What the section does

Four check days hold the unit's records: the story recordings on day 35, the discussion on day 36, the contract revision, conventions and dictation on day 37, and the tale and panel items on day 38. Day 39 shares products, repairs what the records show and regroups for the next unit.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Days 35 to 38 hold no teaching: silent skills, then the records, and a short exit. Day 39 returns and shares, repairs in the skills block, applies the repairs to one piece, and discusses revisions.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A recording is read for sense.** The listener hears where a sentence ends and sees one pictured detail.
- **A revision changes the meaning.** A recopy is not a revision.
- **A discussion builds on others.** Ask for clarification, link a reply, give detail.
- **A check measures what was taught.** Nothing new is taught on a check day.

## The errors, in plain words

- **W, Y:** a passage not explained, a display that does not belong to it.
- **M, H, K:** a recopy, facts left apart, writing a younger reader cannot follow.
- **R, S, T, U:** interrupting, an unrelated question, a repeated opinion, a vague answer.
- **N, AB, G:** a possessive without an apostrophe, an apostrophe for the wrong number of owners, a reference not consulted.
- **A, B, E, X, P:** a fact not given, a topic as the point, a phrase read literally, racing, guessing.

## Marking

Same scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A miss is reteached on day 39. Never a name beside a mistake.
