# Day 35: Check the recordings and prepare the group talk

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the discussion page and the day 35 exit card. Take the sign-up sheet from day 34: the six children not yet recorded are today's six. Test the device in a quiet corner. Teach nothing today.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each word once and children write it: kindness, slowly, washable, replay, unkind, careful, helpless, breakable. Review: -ness (day 31), -ly (day 32), -able (day 33), un- and re- (day 30). Twenty minutes: each child reads from their log.

#### 18 min. Final recordings

You sit at the device. Six children take 3 minutes each, alone and unaided: say a purpose (30 seconds), read the chosen passage (90), reread one sentence with more expression (30), and show the display and say what it clarifies (30). After a misread say only "Check that word." Score after class from the recording: [[s01-A35]], [[s01-A36]], [[s01-A37]] and [[s01-A38]]; write the support used. Six turns fill 18 minutes with no spare: hear any overrun in the 2-minute independent slot. The others mark one detail on the discussion page for 5 minutes, [[s01-P35-2]], then read their logs for 13.

#### 30 min. Group draft exchanges

Six groups of five, in turn: 4 minutes of talk, 1 to move. Each child tells the marked detail and why, [[s01-P35-3]], about 45 seconds each. You score nothing; the evidence is on day 36. The others reread the page. After class note one move heard from the discussion chart for each group.

#### 2 min. Independent work

Children label the display card, [[s01-P35-1]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 35 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X35]]. Unscored. Shown: names the last word of a sentence where the voice pauses. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Where does a listener hear one sentence stop?" Not yet seen: "I read well" (error W).

#### 5 min. Collection

Hand back the day 34 practice pages with your notes: s01-P34-1, s01-P34-2, s01-P34-3, s01-P34-4, s01-P34-5 and s01-P34-6. Collect the display cards, the marked pages and the exits. The recordings stay on the device.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the listener and the group; record on a phone, or hear the reading and write a note with the same four parts. No device: write that note. No printer: copy the page onto a card. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: the adult listens and writes one line.
**Skip.** The recording and the group talk.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the discussion page and marks one detail, [[s01-P35-2]].
  **Say.** "Which detail will you tell, and why?" Expect: a detail from the page and a reason.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads their passage aloud twice and pauses at each end mark.
- **10 min. Label.** Your child writes one label for their picture, [[s01-P35-1]].
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X35]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the slate words to four; keep the recordings and the exit. Longer: a child who has recorded writes a second label.

### Words and supports

Purpose, pause, display, detail, label. Frame: "My picture shows ___." Large print; the page read aloud; a scribe, noted.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X35) and the recording notes. Error W is a recording that explains nothing: say where the pause is. Error Y, on the label (s01-P35-1), is a display that does not fit the passage: name the detail first, then pick the picture. Day 36 also uses the discussion page.
