# Day 37: Check the contract revision and the spelling

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the day 37 exit card. Set out the beginning dictionary, lined paper and the process folders. Find the day 36 exits and each child's written feedback. Find the dated list of earlier short explanations in each folder. Teach nothing today.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each word once and children write it: careful, unkind, helpless, replay, slowly, kindness. Mark nothing. Twenty minutes: each child reads from their log.

#### 30 min. Contract revision

Hand back the day 36 exit (s01-X36), day 36's assessment (s01-A39 to s01-A44, s01-A72), the plan (s01-P36-1) and the partner marks (s01-P36-2). Error M is a recopy. Children read their feedback and revise the contract alone, in writing, for 25 minutes. Give no hint. In the last 5 minutes they audit the draft in silence. After class, mark the revised contract for [[s01-A45]] (grouped under the strategy), [[s01-A46]] (two accurate details and what they mean), [[s01-A47]] (linking words), [[s01-A48]] (the purpose of the strategy) and [[s01-A49]] (a younger reader could use it). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Process annotation

Children mark the earlier plan, the peer note, your note and the revision that followed, [[s01-A51]]. You also look at the dated pieces in the folder, [[s01-A50]], as a spoken record.

#### 15 min. Conventions

Review: one owner (day 2) and more than one owner (unit 1.5). Read the dictation three times, slowly, with a pause: "Our driver's bus was stopping. The two girls' books were ready, and she was hoping to help." Twelve minutes. Score after class: driver's and girls', [[s01-A52]]; hoping, [[s01-A53]]; stopping, [[s01-A54]]. Then three minutes with the beginning dictionary: children correct one spelling, [[s01-A55]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 37 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X37]]. Unscored. Shown: a change in meaning that a younger reader could follow. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What step could this reader not do?" Not yet seen: a recopy (error M).

#### 5 min. Collection

Collect the contracts, dictations, the folders and the exits.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the dictation three times and marks the contract by the five look-fors, noted. No dictionary: copy one page. No printer: copy the page by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No contract: read any piece you wrote aloud.
**Skip.** The dictation.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the revised contract aloud and says what changed, [[s01-A49]].
  **Say.** "Could a younger reader follow it?" Expect: yes, with a reason.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the page they wrote.
- **10 min. Write.** Your child writes two sentences with a person's or a group's belongings.
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X37]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the slate words to three; keep the revision and the exit. Longer: a child revises a second paragraph.

### Words and supports

Revise, feedback, contract, dictionary, owner. Frame: "I changed ___ because ___." Large print; the dictation read aloud; a scribe for the revision, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 is another check. The marks from today (s01-A45 to s01-A55, s01-X37) go to day 39 for repairs; the revised contract comes back on day 38 for the bookmark. Error K is a contract a younger reader cannot follow: add one step.
