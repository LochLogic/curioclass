# Exit cards, days 1 to 4

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children write. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 2 (s01-X2).** Say once, slowly, and let the children write: "Our baker's cat was sitting on the step."
- **Day 3 (s01-X3).** This card is invented for practice. Read: "Lia opened the pond book. The first page named a frog she had seen. She read its name twice, then showed the page to her sister." Ask: Explain to a younger child what Lia did with the frog page. Write three or four sentences.
