# Screen cards (days 2 and 3)

These are placement records, not grades. Print one card for each child.

## Long-word reading card

Twelve words, read aloud one on one, two minutes each: napkin, tiger, cradle, sunset, pumpkin, hundred, remake, unkind, playful, dolphin, backpack, table. Note which syllable pieces a child blends and which a child guesses.

## Spelling screen (whole class, written)

Say each word once, then in a sentence, and let the children write it: rain, seed, float, napkin, tiger, cradle, baking, running, rerun, unfair, said, again. Then say once: "We were running to see the tiger again." Record each word right or wrong and the pattern missed (vowel team, syllable, ending rule, tricky word).

## Oral reading and writing sample

The passage for the oral reading is the text g3-u1-scr, read once unseen, followed by a retell. The writing sample uses the frame card on day 3. Both are unscored.
