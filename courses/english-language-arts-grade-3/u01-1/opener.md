# Section 1.1 opener: books that travel (days 1 to 4)

## What the section does

For four days the class reads *The Boat Book Day*, an invented picture-book story about a girl whose library comes by boat. Children ask and answer questions with the words and the pictures, learn the five moves of prepared partner talk, take the start-of-year screens, and begin a reading log. The question for the unit, how do people get the books they want, stays on the wall.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 1 teaches prepared talk and the words-and-picture table. Days 2 and 3 hold the screens: the long-word card, the spelling screen, one oral reading and one short writing sample. They place children in groups; they are never graded. Day 4 teaches choosing a book by its purpose and starts the log. Every day ends with one exit, one ask on a new case.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A question has a place in the text.** Point to the words, then say what they show.
- **Words and pictures work together.** The picture often adds what the words leave out. Ask what it adds, not what it contains.
- **Talk has five moves.** Prepare a detail, wait, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Anyone may pass.
- **Choose a book by its purpose.** Name the question first, then look for the words that match.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail the card does not give. Put a finger on the sentence, then redo the answer.
- **D:** a pictured thing named with no contribution. Say what it adds to the words.
- **N, O:** a missing apostrophe, a missing double letter. Ask who owns it; mark the short vowel.
- **K:** a list where an explanation is wanted. Add the missing explanation for a younger child.
- **AD:** a book chosen by its cover. Name the question first.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits and screens are unscored.
