# Feedback card (days 2, 4, 7, 8, 12, 14, 17)

For partner rereading. After each reading, the listener ticks one box.

- **Accurate:** read the words on the page.
- **In phrases:** stopped at the ends of sentences and said groups of words together.
- **Expressive:** let the voice show how the words feel.

Never talk about speed. Say one thing that went well and one next try.
