# Day 3: Hearing you read and write

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the oral-reading passage out of sight: children must meet it cold. List the 13 children you hear first from the record sheet, then the 12 for the long-word card and the 10 for the second oral slot. Print a writing frame card for each child and the long-word reading card from the screen cards. Find the day 3 line of exit cards 1 to 4. Put a plain sheet of notes by you and hold a watch.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

Three minutes repair day 2's exit. If many children missed it, use the letter you saw most.

If error N (no apostrophe in a possessive): write "the nurse's bag". **Ask.** Who owns the bag? Expect: the nurse. Say the owner, then put the apostrophe after it and add s. Children write "the pilot's cap" and "Dad's keys" on slates.

If error O (a missing double letter before -ing): write hop. Mark the short vowel with a small curve, double the last letter, then add -ing: hopping. Children do the same with clap and skip. Review: you added -ing to words in grade 1; only the doubling is new today.

Then four minutes. Review: in grade 2 you joined two short sentences into one with and. Write "Lia chose a book. Nina listened." Children say "Lia chose a book, and Nina listened." Then "Kai hung up his coat. Mia found her seat." Pairs join it. Then each child retells two sentences from page 3 to a partner who has not read it. **Ask.** Who is the listener, and what do they need to know? Expect: someone who was not there; who did what, in order. Last, map water (a says /o/, er says /er/) and pin it on the wall with friend, heart, once, neighbors, says and does.

#### 20 min. Oral samples, first slot

You hear 13 children, 1.5 minutes each (19.5 minutes). Everyone else reads from the tub. For each child: hand the unseen passage, say "Read it once, then tell me what happened." Let the child finish each sentence. Never correct mid-sentence and never read a word. If a child stops, wait five seconds, say "Go on", and write down the word. Look at your watch only to note the pace for your record; never say it to the child. When the reading ends, say "Tell me what happened." Write on the notes sheet: words missed, words self-corrected, whether it sounded like talking, and whether the retell has events in order. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet. A prompted reading is recorded with its prompt, never as independent (error AA); hear that child again unaided later. This is a placement record and gives no grade.

#### 55 min. Rotations

*First 15 minutes, whole class.* **Ask.** Who is the listener, and what do they need to know? Expect: a younger child who has not read the story; the facts in order, in sentences. Hand out the writing frame card: topic, fact, link, ending. Review: in grade 2 you wrote a topic, facts and an ending. Think aloud once on the board with a different topic, how Lia helped Cami: Topic: "Lia helped her friend Cami find a book." Fact: "Lia asked which birds Cami liked." Link: "Then". Fact: "Lia listened and found a book with shiny pictures." Ending: "Lia helped by listening." Children then write [[s01-P3-1]] on their frame card in about 10 minutes. Unscored.

*Then 40 minutes.* You hear the 12 children who have not yet done the long-word card, 2 minutes each (24 minutes), then 10 more oral samples (15 minutes); say Go on after five seconds as on day 2. That is 39 of the 40 minutes, so you have almost no time to change children: call the next child as you finish. If time runs out, the last oral samples wait for the conference slot on day 5; tell those children. Everyone else reads from the tub first, then works from the menu on the student page.

Judged by you with four look-fors: a topic sentence, facts from the story only, a link word, a closing sentence. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet; never a grade.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 3 exit card aloud twice. Children write three or four sentences: [[s01-X3]]. Unscored. Shown: two facts from the card only, a link word, easy for a younger child to follow. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What did she do first? What did she do next?" Not yet seen: a list of words.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the four frame boxes on the board. No watch: count slowly in your head. No projector: no change.

### At home

**Swap.** No story pages: use any page of any book.
**Skip.** The oral samples, the watch and the menu.

- **10 min. Reread.** Your child rereads a favourite page twice, the second time like talking.
  **Say.** "Tell me what happened, as if I was not there." Expect: who, what, in order.
- **15 min. Explain.** Answer [[s01-P3-1]] in two or three sentences.
  **Say.** "What is the next fact?" Expect: a fact from the story after the word then.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X3]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the menu and keep both samples and the exit. Longer: children write the ending sentence for a fifth fact in [[s01-P3-1]].

### Words and supports

Listener, topic, fact, link, ending, younger. Frames: "Lia ___." "Then she ___." "This shows ___." A scribe for the explanation is fine; a scribe for the spelling sample is not. A drawn plan first, then the words. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 4 opens with three minutes on this exit. Error K is a list of words such as "frog, page, sister" with no explanation for a younger child. Error A is a fact the card does not give, such as the page going to the child's mother. Day 4 launches book choice and the reading log.
