# Discussion chart (days 1 to 9, and 17)

Five moves, one line each, with a picture clue. Taught by the teacher on day 1, held by the teacher through day 4, then by partners.

1. **Prepare.** Mark one detail in the text and be ready to say it.
2. **Wait.** Listen until the speaker finishes. Use the agreed cue to take a turn.
3. **Ask for detail.** "Which part do you mean?"
4. **Link.** "You said ___; I would add ___."
5. **Explain.** "Now I see ___ because ___."

Sentence frame for evidence: "The words ___ show ___." Anyone may pass.
