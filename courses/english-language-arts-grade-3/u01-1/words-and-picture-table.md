# Words and picture table (days 1, 3 to 7)

Two columns, two rows, drawn on a plain sheet. Children fill it from a marked page.

| The words say | The picture shows |
|---|---|
| (a short quote from the page) | (what the picture adds that the words do not say) |
| | |

Say aloud: "The words say ___, and the picture shows ___."
