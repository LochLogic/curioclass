# Day 4: Choosing a book and keeping a log

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the tub into three piles by topic. Cut one back-words card: "This book explains how bees make honey." Print a reading log for each child. Find the day 4 line of exit cards 1 to 4. List your four conference children from the record sheet and two from the pairs.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

Three minutes repair day 3's exit, and hand back the writing frame cards (s01-P3-1) with one comment each. If many children missed it, use the letter you saw most.

If error K (a list of words, no explanation): read a new card: "Lia held the map. She put her finger on the river, then showed the way to her brother." **Ask.** What did Lia do for her brother? Expect: she showed him the way on the map. Children write two sentences with then.

If error A (a fact the card does not give): read the same card and cover it. **Ask.** Where did the map go next? Expect: the card does not say.

Then seven minutes. **Ask.** Why do you read? Expect: to find out something, to enjoy it, to learn how. Say: "A purpose is the reason you pick up a book." Children say one purpose to a partner. Write three on the board. Retrieve words: friend, heart, once, says and does on slates, unscored.

#### 5 min. Grammar

Review: in grade 2 an apostrophe and s shows one owner. Write "Lia's book" and "Nina's hand". **Ask.** Who owns the book? Expect: Lia. Children write "Cami's page" and "Dad's cup" on slates. Look and hold up; no scores.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Pairs reread pages 7 and 8 (91 words, about a minute a pass). Review: in grade 2 you reread with a partner for expression; the feedback card is new in grade 3: accurate, in phrases, expressive. Each child reads twice, the partner ticks one box and says one thing that went well and one next try. Never say speed. Walk and hear three pairs, 3 minutes each; write them in the heard column. The others finish by 12 minutes, then choose from the tub.

#### 20 min. Model

Say: "Today you choose a book for a reason." Show the back-words card and think aloud with [[s01-K4-1]]: "I hold one book. I read the back words and ask what question it can answer." Point to the words explains how bees make honey and turn them into a question. Show the wrong way once, about a book: "If I judge this book by its bright cover, I have not asked what it can answer." Not about a child. Then show the three log columns, left empty: title, my purpose, one detail that told me. Say what each column asks for.

#### 25 min. Hand work

Eight minutes: browse the tub, choose one book, and say your purpose to a partner. Five minutes: read the first page. Six minutes: write the first log line on the reading log. For the last six minutes everyone reads silently. Review: in grade 2 you held reading conferences. Meanwhile you hold four conferences, 4 minutes each: ask the child why this book, read a page aloud, then say one thing that went well and one next try. Write them in the heard column. Some children may finish early; they read on. Unscored.

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: in grade 2 you used the four-step chart (turn, pair, link, clarify); in grade 3 the chart adds a fifth move. Pairs tell each other the book chosen and the reason, using "My purpose is ___ because ___." Partner links: "You chose it because ___." Each child takes about 90 seconds in both directions, both pairs at once. Anyone may pass. Hear two pairs.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 4 card aloud twice. Children write a log line: [[s01-X4]]. Unscored. Shown: the right card is chosen, a question is written, and the words on the card are named. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What do you want to find out?" Not yet seen: only a title.

### Other rooms

No printer: children write the three log columns on scrap paper. No tub: use any books in the room. No projector: write the back words on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No log sheet: use scrap paper.
**Skip.** The talk with the teacher, the partner talk and the card.

- **10 min. Choose.** Pick a book from home for a reason; say the reason aloud.
  **Say.** "What do you want to find out?" Expect: a question about the topic.
- **10 min. Read.** Read the first page, then write one log line: title, your question and one detail.
- **10 min. Reread.** Reread one page twice, the second time like talking.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X4]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the browse to five minutes and keep the model, one log line and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second log line for a book chosen by a different purpose.

### Words and supports

Purpose, title, cover, log, detail. Frames: "I want to find out ___." "The card says ___." Large print and a spoken list of the three cards for a child who cannot read them; a scribe for the log line is fine, and noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 5 opens with three minutes on this exit. Error AD is choosing a card by its cover or its name alone, with no question. Day 5 reads the boat-library story whole.
