# Day 1: A library that comes by boat

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story, eight pages with pictures, one set for each pair. Pin the discussion chart and the unit question where everyone can see them. Cut the three word cards, the nine syllable cards and the four word-map cards. Find a world map or an atlas page of South America with a title and a key. Choose today's six from the first group on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Show the page 1 picture alone and give pairs a minute to say what they see. Then read page 1 aloud (55 words). Pairs match the word cards Lia, river and landing to a picture detail and say it: "The word river matches the wide brown water." Review: in grade 2 you said what the words say and what the picture shows. Write the unit question on the board and read it aloud: "How do people get past what keeps them from books and school?" Do not answer it. Say: "We keep asking this all unit."

Teach the listening cue: when a speaker finishes, the speaker says "Your turn." A listener waits for those words.

Content note, about a minute: say plainly that in Manaus, Brazil, the river rises in the rainy months and covers some paths, and people lay boards and ride boats. The story shows careful, ordinary days. Anyone may step out and come back.

**Ask.** What does the picture show that page 1 does not say in words? Expect: a porch on tall posts, green trees on the far bank, low clouds.

#### 8 min. Map bridge

Do not assume children have read a map yet. Hold up the world map. Say: "A title tells what the map shows. A key tells what a colour or a symbol means." Read the title aloud and read one line of the key. Find Brazil, then the dot for Manaus. If your map does not show the river, say the Rio Negro flows past the city. Pairs take turns: one partner points to the title and says what it tells, the other points to the key and says what one symbol means. Then each pair finds Brazil. Pairs work at once, about one minute a child. Walk to your six.

#### 14 min. Read and ask

Read the whole story aloud (502 words), pausing to show each picture. Say first: "This story is made up. Only the city and the river are real." About 7 minutes.

Review: in grade 2 you used who, where and why questions. Model one why question on the board with [[steps:s01-K1-1]]. Point to the sentence as you answer. About 4 minutes.

Pairs answer [[s01-P1-1]] and [[s01-P1-2]] and point to the words each time. Three minutes. Listen to your six.

**Ask.** How do you know? Expect: a finger on the sentence, and the words read aloud.

#### 30 min. Skills

*10 min. Syllables.* Review: in grade 2 you sorted closed, open and silent-e syllables by the letters after the vowel. Pairs sort nine cards from the story: lamp, slips, tucks, stamps (closed); she, so (open); date, home, time (silent e). Say what the letters after the vowel tell you before you read each card. Blend, then check it makes sense.

*10 min. Tricky words.* Review: in grade 2 you mapped tricky words. Map four words from the story on the board: friend, heart, once, neighbors. Say it, tap each sound, and put a line under the part that surprises you: ie says /e/ in friend; ear says /ar/ in heart; the o in once says /wu/; eigh says /a/ in neighbors. Children read each word by sound and never guess it from the first letters (error P). Pin the four words on the sound and spelling wall.

*10 min. Reading.* Children read from the picture-book tub. No logs yet. Hear nobody; walk and look.

#### 20 min. Hand work

Children mark three pages with two colours (pages 4, 5 and 8): one color under words that tell, the other round picture details. About 10 minutes.

Model first with page 2 on the board: [[steps:s01-K1-2]]. Say: "The words say ___, and the picture shows ___."

Then each child fills two rows of the words and picture table, one from page 5 and one from page 8: [[s01-P1-3]] and [[s01-P1-4]]. About 10 minutes. If a child only lists a thing in the picture (error D), ask: "What does it add to the words?" Each child marks one detail to talk about.

#### 5 min. Prepared talk

Review: in grade 2 you used turn, pair, link and clarify, and "Which part do you mean?". Today the chart has five moves: prepare a detail, wait, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Read the written script aloud with a volunteer adult or a pair acting it. Script: "My detail is on page 2: Lia slips the date card inside the book. Your turn." "Which part do you mean?" "Where she sets the book by the door. Your turn." "You said the book sits by the door; I would add that she is ready early." "Now I see Lia plans ahead because the card helps her remember." About 2 minutes. Pairs each take one turn of about 45 seconds, both at once. Anyone may pass. Hear your six, 30 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Exit

Children reread page 3 and look at its picture, then write one sentence and underline the words or circle the picture detail they used: [[s01-X1v2]]. Unscored. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet. Shown: the water is named and a pointer is made. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does page 3 say?"

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the table on the board and read the story aloud with each picture held up. No projector: hold up the page 1 picture. No map: draw a box for the title and a small key with two symbols.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed story: read pages 1 and 2 from the paper the teacher sends. No coloured pencils: use a pencil.
**Skip.** The map, the sorting and the pair talk.

- **10 min. Read.** Read pages 1 and 2 aloud, one page each.
  **Say.** "What does the picture show that the words do not say?" Expect: a porch on posts over brown water; a lamp and a card sticking out of the book.
- **10 min. Words.** Answer [[s01-P1-1]] and [[s01-P1-2]]. Point to the words.
- **10 min. Table.** Fill [[s01-P1-3]] together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X1v2]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the tub reading to 4 minutes and fill only one table row. Keep the model, the talk and the exit. Longer: a child defends a different detail from the story that answers the same question.

### Words and supports

River, boat, library, borrow, return. Frames: "The words ___ show ___." "The words say ___, and the picture shows ___." Preview the five words before reading. Pair with a partner who shares a language where you can; accept a drawn answer first. Large print and a spoken description of each picture for a child who cannot see it. Why the flood is named plainly: it is true, and the story shows people doing ordinary careful things, so children can stay calm. Why the exits are unscored: they show what to repair tomorrow, and a mark would turn them into a grade.

### Tomorrow

Day 2 opens with three minutes on this exit. If many missed it, say which letter: error A (a fact page 3 does not give, such as "The boat is late") or error D (a picture object named with no contribution, such as "The picture has people and boards"). Day 2 then holds the first spelling and long-word screens.
