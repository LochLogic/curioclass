# Writing frame card (day 3, and returns on day 8)

Four boxes down a half sheet.

- **Topic:** One sentence that says what you will explain.
- **Fact:** A fact from the text, copied exactly.
- **Link:** A word that joins the next fact: then, so, also, another.
- **Ending:** One sentence that says what the facts show.
