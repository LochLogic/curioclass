# Day 24: Check on the access paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the source card, day 24, and the audit card, day 24. Mark a record sheet column for each item. Find the day 24 exit card. Set out each child's dated folder.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Nothing is taught today. The day 23 exit (s01-X23, s01-X23b) stays with you and goes to day 25. Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each word once and children write it, un-, re-, dis-, pre- and mis- words (unwrap, rebuild, preview, misread). Children also say each prefix meaning to themselves. Twenty minutes: each child reads from their log.

#### 12 min. Items

Children work alone, in writing, on the source card. Teach nothing and give no hint. Record each child: [[s01-A19]] (6 minutes, RI.3.1) and [[s01-A20]] (6 minutes, RI.3.2). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and write down any prompt.

#### 20 min. Product audit

Silent. Eight minutes: each child lays out the dated folder and ticks the audit card. Twelve minutes: each child rereads the revised paragraph and notes once more. Nothing is changed or added today. After class, score the folder paragraph on the record sheet: [[s01-A21]] grouped facts, [[s01-A22]] accurate facts and a defined word, [[s01-A23]] links within a group, [[s01-A24]] a closing sentence, [[s01-A25]] a younger reader could follow it, [[s01-A26]] the dated short and long writing in the folder.

#### 18 min. Independent reading

From the logs, quietly.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 24 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X24]]. Unscored. Shown: says the map gives a path and a name for each viewing spot. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which words on the strip say that?" Not yet seen: an answer from a guess about the topic.

#### 5 min. Collection

Collect the folders, the source card pages and the exits.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the source card by hand. A child may speak an answer and the adult writes it; record the support. One child: a quiet half hour, then the adult files the dated pages.

### At home

**Swap.** No source card: any short article, read twice.
**Skip.** The slate words.

- **10 min. Assessment.** First, your child reads the card and answers [[s01-A19]] alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Then your child answers [[s01-X24]] alone.
- **20 min. Read.** Read with an adult.
  **Say.** "What does your book explain?" Expect: a main idea in a sentence.
- **10 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the log line. Longer: a child reads a second short card and gives its main idea with two details.

### Words and supports

Source card, folder, audit card, dated. Large print; items read aloud except where reading is the evidence; a scribe for the answers, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X24). Error A is an answer from a guess: answer from the strip. Day 25 returns the day 24 results (s01-A19 to s01-A26) and repairs the commonest letter, H, a paragraph that mixes groups. Repairs go to the next teaching lesson, never during the check.
