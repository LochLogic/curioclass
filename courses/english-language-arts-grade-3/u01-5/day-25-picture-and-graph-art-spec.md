# Art spec: day 25 picture and graph (text g3-u1-d25)

- **Picture.** An original plain line drawing of a shelf with three baskets, labelled Animals, Space and Maps, with a few books standing in each basket. No people. A short title, "Our classroom library".
- **Graph.** An original bar graph with the title "Books borrowed". The horizontal axis is labelled "Month" with March, April and May. The vertical axis is labelled "Number of books", equal steps of one from 0 to 5. The bars show 3, 4 and 5.
- **Image description.** For a child who cannot see the picture: "A shelf holds three baskets labelled Animals, Space and Maps, each with a few books." For the graph: "Bars rise from three in March to four in April and five in May."
- Print "invented for practice" at the foot of both. Nothing here is a real service.
