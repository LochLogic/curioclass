# Product audit card (day 24)

Teacher look-fors for the revised paragraph, scored on the record sheet: a topic sentence and grouped facts; accurate facts and a defined word; links within a group; a closing sentence; a younger reader could follow it; the dated pieces show short and long writing. Each is marked 2, 1 or 0.

A child who is absent for part of the work has the pieces they did, noted with the dates.
