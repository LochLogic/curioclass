# Day 23: Revise for purpose, links and a closing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page. Put the frame away; children hold their own plan. Find the day 23 exit card. Sort the day 22 exits for hand-back. Make cards of two related facts for the opening. List three conference children. Rule the board chart "What does a younger reader need?"

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair.* Hand back the day 22 exit and revision (s01-X22, s01-P22-1, s01-P22-2, s01-P22-3) with one comment each. If many copied the model back (error M), show the four sentences and ask: "Which note did this change use?" Expect: none, so the meaning has not changed.

*7 min. Linking words.* Unscored. Show cards with two related facts. Children choose also, another, and or but and say why. Review: in grade 2 you joined sentences to give reasons and to show Both/But. Today also and but join facts.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Prefix sort.* Children sort cards of pre-, mis-, un-, re- and dis- words by prefix and say each meaning, then spell six studied words on a slate: preview, misread, unwrap, rebuild, disconnect, prewash.

*12 min. Reading conferences.* Three children from the list, 4 minutes each. Write them in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs.

#### 40 min. Hand work

*8 min. Reread.* The frame is withdrawn. Partners hear each other's paragraph aloud and say what a younger reader would not know.

*20 min. Revise.* Each child revises their paragraph with [[s01-P23-1]]. Add the missing explanation (purpose), make each link join facts of one group, and write the closing sentence. If a child explains a word with the word itself (error K), ask: "What would a younger reader see in your words?"

*8 min. Edit.* Review: you wrote one owner, like the baker's cat, in unit 1.1, and many owners today. Children write [[s01-P23-2]], then [[s01-P23-3]]. Count the owners first. Each child checks their own paragraph for an owner and a doubtful spelling, then checks one spelling in the dictionary with [[s01-P23-4]].

*4 min. File.* Label the final copy "Revised, day 23" and file it with the plan, draft, note and change log.

#### 15 min. Talk

Pairs: peer critique for purpose, each child 4 minutes as writer and 4 as listener (8 minutes). Then 7 minutes: the class fills the chart "What does a younger reader need?" **Ask.** "What would a younger reader need to follow your paragraph?" Expect: a word explained and a link that joins facts of one group.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 23 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X23]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on their slates: [[s01-X23b]]. Unscored. Shown: explains rinse in plain words and keeps the reminders in order. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What would a younger reader not know?" Not yet seen: the strip copied back. For the closing strip, shown: one sentence about the pots and brushes, not a new topic.

### Other rooms

No dictionary: use any alphabetical word list. No pairs: the adult hears the paragraph and says what a younger reader would not know. No cards: say the pairs of facts aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No revised paragraph: tell the closing sentence aloud.
**Skip.** The peer critique.

- **10 min. Closing.** Your child reads the paragraph and says the closing sentence, [[s01-P23-1]].
  **Say.** "What do the facts show?" Expect: that books reached readers far away.
- **15 min. Read.** Read for fifteen minutes and tell one fact your book gave.
- **5 min. Spell.** Your child checks one spelling, [[s01-P23-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X23]] and [[s01-X23b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the linking cards; keep the revision, the edit, the filing and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second closing sentence and says which one is better.

### Words and supports

Purpose, closing sentence, younger reader, owner, apostrophe. Frames: "A younger reader would not know ___." "So ___." Large print; the paragraph read aloud; a scribe for the revision, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 24 is a check and holds no opening. The folder is the child's until collection. After class read this exit (s01-X23, s01-X23b) and send it to day 25. Error K is a reminder with no meaning: say what it means so a younger reader can use it. Error J is a closing about another topic: end on the same topic.
