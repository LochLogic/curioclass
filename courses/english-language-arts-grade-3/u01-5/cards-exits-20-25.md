# Exit cards, days 20 to 25

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children write. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. The cards are never used in a worked example.

- **Day 20 (s01-X20).** Five note strips, one fact each: Water the herbs. Pick ripe berries. Pull the weeds. Carry the berries to the kitchen. Turn the soil. Ask: Write two groups. Give each group a heading that says its purpose.
- **Day 21 (s01-X21).** Read: "A den is a sheltered home for a fox. This den has two doors, one on each side of the hill." Ask: Explain this den to a younger reader.
- **Day 21, second card (s01-X21b).** Read: "The wren builds its nest in spring." "The wren feeds its chicks bugs." Ask: Join the two facts into one sentence with a link word.
- **Day 22 (s01-X22).** Read: "Visitors read the park sign. Ducks are protected. The sign names the rules. Protected birds are left alone." Notes: partner, "put the sign facts together"; teacher, "say what protected means". Ask: Rewrite the four sentences using both notes.
- **Day 23 (s01-X23).** For a younger reader. Read: "Rinse the pots. Also, dry the brushes. Shelf." Ask: Rewrite this so a younger reader can use it.
- **Day 23, second card (s01-X23b).** Read: "The helpers rinse the pots. Also, they dry the brushes. Then they put both on the shelf." Ask: Write one closing sentence for the paragraph.
- **Day 25 (s01-X25).** Read: "The art club made two clay bowls and six clay cups today." Two labelled bars show 2 and 6, and a drawing shows both kinds. Ask: How many more cups than bowls did the club make?
- **Day 25, second card (s01-X25b).** Read: "Fin is a goldfish. Fin eats flakes. Fin swims in a bowl. My bike is red." The note says: Your last sentence is about another topic. Ask: Which closing sentence fixes the note? A: "My bike is fast." B: "Fin has food and a bowl to live in." C: "Fin is a fish."

## Pictures for these cards

- **Day 20.** Five plain rectangles, each with one note in large letters and a blank line beneath.
- **Day 25.** A bar graph with the title "Clay made today", a horizontal axis labelled "Kind" with the bars "bowls" and "cups", a vertical axis labelled "Number made" with equal steps of one from 0 to 6, and bars 2 and 6. A small drawing of a bowl and a cup sits beside the graph.
