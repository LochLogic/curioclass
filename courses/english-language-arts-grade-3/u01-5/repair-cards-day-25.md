# Repair cards (day 25)

Four small cards, one for each repair. Each child takes the card for the commonest letter on their day 24 results and repairs one sentence in their folder paragraph.

- **Grouping (H).** My facts about one thing are together. I can name the group in a few words.
- **Linking (I).** My link word joins two facts about the same thing. I read both facts aloud to check.
- **Closing (J).** My last sentence is about the same topic. It says what the facts make possible.
- **Younger reader (K).** I explained the word a younger reader may not know. I wrote the meaning in plain words.

Write the repaired sentence below its first form. Keep both in the folder.

## Fresh cards (one for each repair, used in the last five minutes)

Each card is invented for practice. The child writes one sentence that meets the repair card.

- **Grouping (H).** Notes: "The crate holds atlases." "The lamp is yellow." "The crate holds maps." Write the two crate notes together as one sentence.
- **Linking (I).** Facts: "Mei sweeps the hall." "Mei empties the bins." Join them with a link word that fits.
- **Closing (J).** Read: "The scouts sweep the hall. Also, they empty the bins." Write a closing sentence for the paragraph.
- **Younger reader (K).** Read: "Sharpen the pencils. Tray." Rewrite it so a younger reader can use it.
- **Met every look-for.** Write a second group of facts for your own paragraph, with its own heading.
