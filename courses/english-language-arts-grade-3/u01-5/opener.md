# Section 1.5 opener: writing a paragraph about getting books (days 20 to 24)

## What the section does

Children plan, draft, revise and finish one informative paragraph about a challenge of getting books, using the two articles as sources. They learn to group related facts under one heading, to explain a word for a younger reader, to link facts with also, another, and and but, and to end with a closing sentence. They place the apostrophe in a possessive by counting the owners, and they check a spelling in a beginning dictionary. Day 24 is a check day. Day 25 returns the results and holds a short, formal class discussion.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 20 plans from a model and ten note strips; day 21 drafts; day 22 uses one peer note and one teacher note to revise; day 23 revises for purpose, links and the closing sentence. Day 24 holds no teaching: silent skills, two reading items, an audit of the child's own paragraph folder, and one exit. Day 25 gives the feedback back, repairs one sentence, and holds the discussion.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A paragraph is facts in groups.** Each group has a heading that says what the group is for.
- **A younger reader needs a meaning.** A word the reader may not know is explained once, in plain words.
- **A link joins related facts.** Also, another, and and but join facts within one group, never two unrelated facts.
- **A closing sentence says what the facts make possible.** It does not start a new topic.
- **A possessive counts the owners.** One owner takes apostrophe and s; many owners take s and then the apostrophe.

## The errors, in plain words

- **H, I, J, K:** facts left ungrouped, a link joining unrelated ideas, a closing on a new topic, writing a younger reader cannot follow.
- **A, B:** a fact the source does not give; a topic offered as the point.
- **M:** a copy treated as a revision.
- **N, AB:** an apostrophe left out, or placed for the wrong number of owners.
- **Z:** the bars of a graph read with the wrong labels.

## Marking

Same scale for every exit: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Repairs go to the next teaching lesson, never into the check. Day 24 scores come from the folder only.
