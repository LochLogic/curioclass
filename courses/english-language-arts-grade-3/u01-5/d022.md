# Day 22: Revise with a peer look-for and a note

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page. Write each draft's one-line note. Prepare a phrase strip for each pair and mark a 90-word article section. Find the day 22 exit card. Sort the day 21 exits for hand-back. List two pairs to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair.* Hand back the day 21 exits (s01-X21, s01-X21b) with one comment each. Hand back the day 21 drafts too (s01-P21-1, s01-P21-2). If many gave a fact the strip does not give (error A), show: "A den is a sheltered home for a fox." Ask: "Does it say how many foxes live there?" Expect: no, so it is not an answer. If many joined facts about different things (error I), show: "They rode mules. Also, the program began in 1935." Ask: "Are both facts about the same thing?" Expect: no, so also does not fit.

*7 min. Phrases.* Unscored. Partners read a four-sentence strip with phrase marks, then swap: read in phrases, stop at each end mark.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. The prefix mis-.* Review: you learned pre- in kindergarten. Mis- is new: it means wrongly. Say misread, misspell, mislead. Children write each, circle mis-, and say it means wrongly. Say: "Say the part's meaning, then test it in the word."

*5 min. Also and but.* Review: in grade 2 you joined sentences to give reasons and to show Both/But. Today also and but join facts. Say two facts and children join them. "The carriers rode mules. They carried books." "The roads were bad. The carriers went anyway." Say: "Also adds to the same group. But shows a turn."

*7 min. Partner rereading.* Partners reread the 90-word section twice each with the feedback card: accurate, in phrases, expressive. No speed talk. Listen to two pairs, 3 minutes each, and write down who was heard.

#### 40 min. Hand work

*5 min. The note.* Each child reads the teacher's note on their draft.

*10 min. Peer look-for.* Show the model on the page. **Ask.** "Which revision did what the note asked?" with [[s01-K22-1]]. Expect: Revision A, because it puts the two crate facts side by side; Revision B only copies the sentences. Then partners swap drafts. Each looks for the one thing the note names and writes yes or not yet, and where, with [[s01-P22-1]].
Check: the partner points to the sentence the note names and says yes or not yet, and the writer agrees it is that sentence.

*20 min. Revise.* Each child revises the draft for the note with [[s01-P22-2]]: move, add or explain one fact. Circulate. If a child recopies the same words (error M), ask: "What changed in the meaning?"

*5 min. Change log.* Each child writes what changed and where with [[s01-P22-3]]. File it with the draft.

#### 15 min. Talk

Pairs, all at once. Each child takes a 6-minute turn as writer: say the note in your own words, answer the partner's one question about it with a detail, and hear what the partner would change (12 minutes). Then two volunteers share a revision, 1.5 minutes each (3 minutes). **Ask.** "What does your partner's note ask you to change in the meaning?" Expect: a fact moved, added or explained, said in the writer's words.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 22 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X22]]. Unscored. Shown: uses both notes, with the sign facts together and protected explained. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which note did you not use?" Not yet seen: the four sentences copied back, or only one note used.

### Other rooms

No partner: the adult reads the draft for the note and writes yes or not yet. No strips: write four short sentences on the board. No feedback card: say the three looks aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No draft to hand: say the note aloud and revise by speaking.
**Skip.** The partner rereading.

- **10 min. Read aloud.** Your child reads the revised paragraph aloud.
  **Say.** "What might a younger reader not know?" Expect: one word or step.
- **5 min. Log.** Your child reads the change log, [[s01-P22-3]].
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X22]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the also and but minutes and the volunteers; keep the note, the revision, the change log and the exit. Longer: a child revises for a second note from the board.

### Words and supports

Note, revise, change log, peer, look-for. Frames: "The note asks me to ___." "I moved ___ next to ___." Large print; the note read aloud; a scribe for the revision, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X22). Error M is a recopy: use both notes to change the model, not copy it. Day 23 withdraws the frame; each child holds their own plan.
