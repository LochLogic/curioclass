# Day 25: Use feedback and talk about a library talk

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the repair cards, day 25, and the day 25 picture and graph. Mark each child's commonest day 24 letter on the record sheet. Put the day 24 results and the day 23 and 24 exits in a folder for hand-back. Set up the chart, and six groups of five. Find the day 25 exit card. List three conference children.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair.* Hand back the day 24 exit (s01-X24) with one comment each. If many answered from a guess about the topic (error A), show the strip and ask: "Which words say that?" Expect: the words on the strip.

*7 min. Feedback target.* Each child names the one change the notes of days 22 and 23 asked for and finds it in their folder. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* Say week 5 words once and children write each on a slate: preview, prewash, misread, misspell, unwrap, rebuild. Say the prefix meanings.

*12 min. Reading conferences.* Three children from the list, 4 minutes each. Write them in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs.

#### 10 min. Feedback

Return day 24's assessment (s01-A19 to s01-A26) and the day 23 exits and revision (s01-X23, s01-P23-1, s01-P23-2, s01-P23-3, s01-P23-4). Each child reads theirs, marks the one letter to repair, and writes a target in the folder (5 minutes). The teacher's list of the commonest letters goes on the board (5 minutes). Say what a reminder means so a younger reader can use it (error K) and end on the same topic (error J).

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Four groups by letter: grouping (error H), links (error I) and closing (error J), younger reader (error K), and a group that met every look-for. Lead the largest group for 15 minutes; the others work in pairs from the repair cards. Each child repairs one sentence with [[s01-P25-3]] and keeps the first form below it. Then 5 minutes: each child reads the fresh card for their repair and writes one sentence with [[s01-P25-4]]. The group that met every look-for writes a second group of facts with its own heading.

#### 25 min. Discussion

*3 min. Listen.* Read the practice talk aloud twice, with the baskets picture and the books graph. *5 min. Prepare.* Each child marks one detail with [[s01-P25-1]] and rehearses a claim with a partner. *12 min. Talk.* Six groups of five talk at once. Use the five moves on the discussion chart: prepare a detail, wait, ask, link, explain. One child in each group is the heard-six; spend 2 minutes with each group and record that child's moves. **Ask.** "What do the words, the picture and the bars each tell, and what do all three tell together?" Expect: a detail from each, then a claim built from all three. If a child reads a bar without its label (error Z), say: "Read the label under each bar first." *5 min. Write.* Each child writes one sentence on what the talk made clearer with [[s01-P25-2]].

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 25 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X25]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on their slates: [[s01-X25b]]. Unscored. Shown: four more, from six cups and two bowls. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does each bar say?" Not yet seen: four, with no unit, or the bar for two and six added. For the goldfish strip, shown: chooses B and says it stays on the fish.

### Other rooms

No groups of five: use pairs. No printed cards: read each repair aloud and write it on a scrap. One child: the adult is the group and records the moves.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture or graph: say a small count of books by month and ask for the largest.
**Skip.** The repair cards.

- **10 min. Repair.** Your child reads the target in the folder and says it aloud.
  **Say.** "What is your target?" Expect: one change, said in your child's words.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you one thing the group talk made clearer, [[s01-P25-2]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X25]] and [[s01-X25b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the week 5 words and one conference; keep the feedback, the repair, the talk and the exit. Longer: the group that met every look-for reads their second group aloud to a partner.

### Words and supports

Target, repair, label, bar, claim. Frames: "I see now that ___ because you said ___." "The bar for ___ shows ___." Large print; the repair card read aloud; a scribe for the sentence, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 26 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X25, s01-X25b). Error Z is a bar read without its label: read the label on each bar before comparing. The repaired paragraph stays in the folder for day 37.
