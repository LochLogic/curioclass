# Day 21: Draft the access paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page. Lay each plan frame beside the two articles. Find the day 21 exit card. Sort the day 20 exits for hand-back. List three conference children. Keep a board space headed "What a reader needs".

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair.* Hand back the day 20 exit (s01-X20) with one comment each. If many left the five notes in one list or split them wrongly (error H), show two cards: "carry the berries to the kitchen" and "pick ripe berries". Ask: "Are these two for the same purpose?" Expect: yes, both are about the berries, so they go together under one heading.

*7 min. Spelling.* Unscored. Say six week 4 words and children write each on a slate: carrier, route, stop, remote, borrow, return. Then say unwrap, rebuild and disconnect as prefix and base. Children write each and use one in a sentence.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. The prefix pre-.* Review: you met pre- in kindergarten (precut). Pre- means before. Say preview, prewash, pretest. Children write each on a slate, circle pre-, and say it means before. Say: "Say the part's meaning, then test it in the word."

*12 min. Reading conferences.* Three children from the list, 4 minutes each: one page aloud, one question answered from the words, one next step in the log. Write them in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs.

#### 40 min. Hand work

*5 min. Reread.* Each child rereads their plan frame and the two articles.

*30 min. Write.* Each child writes the access paragraph from the frame with [[s01-P21-1]], using only the two articles. Circulate and hold writing conferences. If a child writes a fact the articles do not give (error A), ask: "Which sentence in the articles says that?" This first full draft is not scored.

*5 min. Read back.* Each child reads the paragraph and underlines the sentence that explains a word, with [[s01-P21-2]]. They circle one link word. After class, write one line on each draft: a question that asks for a change in meaning (about 2 minutes a draft).

#### 15 min. Talk

Pairs, all at once. Each child reads their paragraph aloud (1.5 minutes each) and the partner says one thing they learned (2.5 minutes each): 8 minutes in all. Then 7 minutes: the class lists on the board what a reader needs that a first draft often lacks. **Ask.** "What did your partner's paragraph tell you that you did not know?" Expect: a fact from the paragraph, said in the listener's words.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 21 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X21]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on their slates: [[s01-X21b]]. Unscored. Shown: the den answer explains the word and says what the two doors give. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What would a younger reader not know?" Not yet seen: the strip copied back, or a fact the strip does not give. For the wren strip, shown: one sentence with a link word joining both facts.

### Other rooms

No printed articles: read each aloud, have the child tell the facts, then write. No pairs: the adult is the partner for the talk. No board: use a sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No paragraph yet: tell the facts aloud from the frame.
**Skip.** The pair talk.

- **10 min. Read aloud.** Your child reads the paragraph aloud.
  **Say.** "What would you add?" Expect: one fact or one explained word.
- **5 min. Check.** Your child underlines the sentence that explains a word, [[s01-P21-2]].
- **15 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X21]] and [[s01-X21b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the prefix minutes and the board list; keep the draft, the read-back and the exit. Longer: a child adds a second group of facts with its own heading.

### Words and supports

Draft, paragraph, definition, link word, group. Frames: "A carrier is ___." "Also, ___." Preview the articles with their pictures. Large print; the frame read aloud; a scribe for the draft, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 22 opens with 3 minutes on these exits (s01-X21, s01-X21b). Error A is a fact the strip does not give: answer from the strip only. Error I is a link joining facts about different things: join only facts about one thing. Each child reads the one-line note on their draft first.
