# Day 20: Plan a paragraph from groups of facts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the note strips and frame, day 20. Cut the strips sheet apart; keep each pair's two piles and four heading cards together. Put the model and the two library boat plans on the board, with the list of needs: topic sentence, grouped facts, link word, closing sentence. Find the day 20 exit card. Sort the day 18 and 19 exits and items, and the day 16 paragraphs. List three conference children.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair and hand-back.* Hand back the day 18 exit (s01-X18) and the day 19 exit and items (s01-X19, s01-A12 to s01-A18), with one comment each. If many children made error V on day 18's exit (an image read beyond what it shows), show a picture of a book bike with the caption "Saturday at the market" and ask: "Does the picture say every child likes it?" Expect: no, it only shows a bike and a market day. If many made error B on day 19's items (a topic given as the main idea), show two cards: "It is about a bike." and "A book bike brings picture books to the market." Ask which says what the whole text shows. Expect: the second, a whole claim. Other letters go to conference groups.

*7 min. Revise and edit.* Review: you revised and edited a paragraph in grade 2. Children fix the paragraph in [[s01-P20-1]] and say why. Expect: a capital letter where a sentence starts, and the drum sentence crossed out because it is not about snails. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words and a dictionary.* Five minutes: say each word once and children write it on a slate: remote, replenish, borrow, then unwrap, rebuild, disconnect, said as prefix and base (un- wrap, re- build, dis- connect). Review: you read the base first, then added the prefix, in unit 1.4. Three minutes: say photograph. Review: you used a dictionary in grade 2 and on day 11. Children try foto and photo on the slate, find the guide words at the top of the page, and copy the right spelling with [[s01-P20-2]]. Say: "If a word does not look right, try another spelling, then check the page."

*12 min. Reading conferences.* Three children from the list, 4 minutes each: one page read aloud (1 minute), one question answered from the words (1 minute 30 seconds), one next step in the log (1 minute 30 seconds). Write them in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs. If a child reads a word by its first letters (error P), say the chunks, blend, and check the sense.

#### 20 min. Model

*1 min. Read.* Read the Bangladesh boat section of Books on the Move aloud (about 95 words). Say: "Today we plan a paragraph. The topic is library boats, not the carriers. First see how a plan is made."

*11 min. A plan made of groups.* Show Plan A on the board: eight notes about the boats in the order they came. Show Plan B: the same notes in two groups, "How the boats travel" and "What readers find on board". **Ask.** "Why does Plan B help a reader more?" with [[s01-K20-2]]. Expect: the facts for one purpose are together, and each heading says what its group is for. Say the rule once: "Ask of each note, what is it for? Notes with one purpose go together, and the heading names the purpose." Point to the main-idea chart: a heading is the main idea of a group in a few words. Then read the model paragraph on the page, and children mark each part. **Ask.** "Which sentence tells the whole topic?" with [[s01-K20-1]]. Expect: the first. **Ask.** "Which word links two facts of one group?" Expect: Also, circled. **Ask.** "Which sentence tells a younger reader what a word means?" Expect: the reference book sentence, boxed. **Ask.** "Which sentence closes?" Expect: the last, starred, because it says what the boats make possible. Review: you wrote a topic, facts and a closing on day 16.

*5 min. Possessives.* Review: you wrote one owner, like the baker's cat, in unit 1.1. New today: many owners. One boat has a roof: the boat's roof, with the apostrophe before the s. Three boats each have a roof: the boats' roofs, with the apostrophe after the s. Say: "Count the owners first. One owner, apostrophe then s. Many owners, s then apostrophe." Children write [[s01-K20-3]], then [[s01-K20-4]]. If a child leaves out the apostrophe (error N) or places it for many owners (error AB), ask: "How many boats own the roof?"

*3 min. Also and but.* Review: in grade 2 you joined sentences to give reasons and to show Both/But. New today: also and but join facts. Say each pair and children say one joined sentence: "The boat has flat bottoms. It crosses shallow water." "Few roads reach the village. A boat can reach it." "Readers borrow books. They read for pleasure." Say: "Also adds a fact to the same group. But shows a turn."

#### 25 min. Hand work

*5 min. Needs.* Hand back the day 16 paragraphs. Each child rereads theirs beside the list of needs on the board and writes the one need it still lacks with [[s01-P20-3]]. No grade is given.

*12 min. Sort the carrier strips.* Pairs take the ten strips, already in two piles. Two strips are in the wrong pile. Pairs mark them with [[s01-P20-4]], move them, then choose a heading card for each pile with [[s01-P20-5]]. Walk and look. If a pair picks "Books" for a pile (a topic, not a purpose), ask: "What is this pile for?" Judged after class with the look-fors in the items.

*8 min. Plan frame.* Each child copies the two headings, writes a topic sentence, circles two link words, writes one word a younger reader may not know with its meaning, and writes one closing idea, with [[s01-P20-6]]. Nothing is drafted today. Date the frame and file it in the folder with the day 16 paragraph.

#### 10 min. Talk

All pairs at once. Each child takes 2 minutes to tell the partner the two groups and why each strip belongs (4 minutes in all). Then 3 minutes: the partner asks for a detail ("Which strip tells that?"). Then three volunteers share, 1 minute each. Anyone may pass. **Ask.** "Which notes belong together, and what are they for?" Expect: the notes with one purpose, and the heading names that purpose.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 20 card aloud twice. Show the five strips. Children write alone: [[s01-X20]]. Unscored. Shown: the three plant-care notes together, the two berry notes together, each heading saying what its group is for. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What is each group for?" Not yet seen: all five in one list or in the order given, or two groups that part the berry notes.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the ten strips on scraps and the four heading cards on four more; copy the model on the board. No pairs: the adult is the partner for the strips and the talk. No dictionary: use any alphabetical list of words.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strips: cut a page into strips and write one fact on each.
**Skip.** The conferences and the pair talk.

- **10 min. Model.** Your child reads the model paragraph and underlines its topic sentence, [[s01-K20-1]].
  **Say.** "What is the whole paragraph about?" Expect: library boats bring books to villages that roads do not reach.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say photograph. Your child looks it up in a dictionary, [[s01-P20-2]].
- **15 min. Read.** Read the Kentucky article aloud and say which facts go together. Then read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X20]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the also and but minutes and the conferences; keep the model, the possessives, the strips, the frame and the exit. Longer: a child sorts the library boat notes under two other headings and says which pair of headings a reader would like better.

### Words and supports

Heading, group, topic sentence, link word, closing sentence, apostrophe, owner. Frames: "This group is for ___." "The heading is ___ because every note ___." Preview library boats with a picture. Large print; the strips read aloud and touched; a scribe for headings, never for spelling evidence, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X20). Error H is notes left in one list, in the order given, or a split that parts the two berry notes: group the strips by what each group is for, then name it. Day 21 is application: each child drafts the access paragraph from their frame. The frame and the day 16 paragraph stay in the folder.
