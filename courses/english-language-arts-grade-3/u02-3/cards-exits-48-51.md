# Exit cards, days 48 to 51

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 48 (s02-X48).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Three frogs heard a crack. One spoke. The other two moved." Ask: Revise "The other two moved" so a reader sees how they moved. Use more or most.
- **Day 48 (s02-X48b).** Same card, read to three children. Ask: Say one line the frog who spoke might say, and say where its quotation marks go.
- **Day 49 (s02-X49).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "The frog found a shaded pool. The bank was dry. Its friend needed shade." Ask: Write the resolution in two or three sentences. Start with a time word. Use and in one sentence. Use because or when in another.
- **Day 50 (s02-X50).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Frog shouted softly to avoid waking her friend." Peer note: Make the action fit the effect. Ask: Rewrite the sentence with a verb that fits the peer note.
- **Day 50 (s02-X50b).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Frog shouted softly to avoid waking her friend." You rewrote it with a new verb. Ask: Say why your new verb fits.
- **Day 51 (s02-X51).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Show how the leaf helps me understand where the frog waits." Ask: Point to a label on your leaf that shows where the frog waits. Read it.
- **Day 51 (s02-X51b).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Show how the leaf helps me understand where the frog waits." Ask: Say one phrase from your recording that shows where the frog waits.
