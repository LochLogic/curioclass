# Day 49: Revise the resolution and the time links, and join sentences

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and draft pages for each child, and a feedback card for each pair. Find the day 49 line of the exit cards. Check the recording list: five names for today, the fifth window, and that the device saves locally. Write the three practice lines of [[s02-P49-1]] on the board.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair, on day 48's exit (s02-X48), error N (a form for three used for two, or an invented form): the line on the board, children mark the wrong part and say it right, [[s02-P49-1]]. Any child who made error U on the line of speech adds the marks and the comma in the margin. *7 min.* Week 10: children write location, reaction and confusion on slates, [[s02-P49-11]]. Review: -tion and -sion (day 41). Review: an irregular word has a part that does not sound the way it looks (day 40). Children mark that part of one, [[s02-P49-12]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Review: a simple sentence and a compound sentence joined with a comma and and (grade 2). Children join two tale sentences on slates, [[s02-P49-2]]. Then they mark the whole sentence, [[s02-P49-3]]. *15 min.* The fifth recording window: five children, 3 minutes each, swap included, on stanza three of Under the Leaf, [[s02-P49-4]]. Review: a recording states its purpose, reads, and rereads one line after the replay (day 40). Every other pair rereads stanza two with the feedback card, in phrases and with expression, no speed, [[s02-P49-5]]. The teacher hears no pairs.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*8 min.* Review: hard-part reread (day 40). Pairs reread Why the Poison Dart Frog Is So Colorful from the torch to the crack and point to the order words. **Ask.** "What makes the escape sequence coherent?" Expect: the events come in the order they happen, and the words One day, Then and Outside tell the order, [[s02-K49-1]]. *4 min.* The class shares what they pointed to. *3 min.* Show three sentences from the same lines: Ranna found a crack. Ranna found a crack, and he wriggled through. Ranna wriggled through because the flames filled the entrance. Say: the first is simple, the second is compound, the third is complex. New: a complex sentence has a part that starts with because or when and cannot stand alone; it hangs on a whole sentence.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Review: a joining word joins two parts (grade 1). Model and, but and so on Kikker practiced. His tongue grew strong: so fits, because the second part is the result. Then because and when on The frogs laughed. Kikker dove deep: Kikker dove deep because the frogs laughed. When the frogs laughed, Kikker dove deep. Say: join only parts that belong together. *5 min.* Children choose a joining word for three pairs on slates, [[s02-P49-6]], then mark the whole sentence, [[s02-P49-7]]. Error AB (because joins unrelated ideas) and error AG (a part that cannot stand alone used as a sentence) are watched here.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: tell the ending of your tale in order; the partner checks the time words, [[s02-P49-8]]. Then three volunteers tell their ending and the class names the time words, 2 minutes each. Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

Each child revises the resolution in the draft so the events follow in order with time words, [[s02-P49-9]], then adds one sentence with and and one with because or when, [[s02-P49-10]]. Watch for error O (events out of order): lay the events in order, then add the time words. Watch for error Q (an ending about something else): reread the start and close the same problem. Watch for errors AB and AG. The draft stays in the folder with a dated note.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 49 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X49]]. Unscored. Shown: time word first, one sentence joined with and, one with because or when, events in order. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What happened first?" Not yet seen: error AB, error AG or error O.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner for the reread and the talk, and hears the recording read aloud. No device: skip the recording; each child reads stanza three aloud, noted. No feedback card: the partner names one smooth phrase. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No slate: use paper. No device: your child reads stanza three of Under the Leaf aloud to you.
**Skip.** The recording and the partner reread.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the revised resolution aloud.
  **Say.** "Which words tell the order?" Expect: time words such as First and Then, in the child's words.
- **20 min. Write.** Your child writes one sentence with and and one with because or when, [[s02-P49-10]]. Then the child marks the whole sentence, [[s02-P49-7]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X49]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the volunteers and the slate joins; keep the revision and the exit. Longer: a child writes a complex sentence with when in the tale.

### Words and supports

Resolution, time words, join, complex. Frame, printed on the student page: And, but and so join two whole sentences. Because and when start a part that needs a whole sentence. Large print; a scribe for ideas, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X49]]. Error AG (a part used as a sentence): attach it to a clause with a whole thought. Error AB (because joins unrelated ideas): choose related parts before joining. Error O (events out of order): order the events, then add time words. Day 50 applies the peer note and the teacher note to a verb chosen for effect and holds the sixth recording window. The draft pages stay in the folder.
