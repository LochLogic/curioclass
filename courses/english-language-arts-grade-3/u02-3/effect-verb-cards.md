# Effect-verb cards, day 50

Print one set for each pair, cut apart. Four cards. Each has a frog sentence with a blank, the effect the writer wants, and two verbs to choose between. Children choose, read the sentence aloud with each verb, and say why.

1. The frog ___ across the log to show off. Effect: proud. Verbs: strutted, crept.
2. The frog ___ into the pond with a loud splash. Effect: heavy and loud. Verbs: slid, plunged.
3. The tadpole ___ away from the shadow. Effect: quick and frightened. Verbs: darted, drifted.
4. The old frog ___ to the lily pad. Effect: slow and tired. Verbs: plodded, skipped.
