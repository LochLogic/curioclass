# Day 50: Use a peer note and a teacher note to choose a verb for effect

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one short note on each child's draft (about 30 minutes outside class): one verb to change, or one time word to fix. Print the student page and draft pages for each child and a set of effect-verb cards for each pair. Find the day 50 line of the exit cards. Check the recording list: five names, the sixth window; after today all 30 children have been recorded once. Mark three names on the heard-six column for the spoken exit ask.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair, on day 49's exit (s02-X49), error AG (a part used as a sentence), error AB (because joins unrelated ideas) and error O (events out of order). Children make a part into a whole sentence, [[s02-P50-1]]. *7 min.* Week 10: children write confession, version and conclusion on slates, [[s02-P50-2]]. Review: -tion and -sion (day 41). Review: an irregular word has a part that does not sound the way it looks (day 40). Children mark that part of through, [[s02-P50-3]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading and recording

*15 min.* The sixth recording window: five children, 3 minutes each, swap included, on stanza three of Under the Leaf, [[s02-P50-4]]. Review: a recording states its purpose, reads, and rereads one line after the replay (day 40). The rest read from their logs, no teaching. No conferences today. *5 min.* Everyone adds one log line.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Review: regular and irregular verbs (day 43). Children write the past form of jump and catch on slates, [[s02-P50-5]]. *7 min.* Review: an exact verb (day 47). Pairs reread the rain in stanza two of Under the Leaf and the mouth snapping shut in Kikker's tale, and point to the verb in each. **Ask.** "Which precise verb changes the reader's picture?" Expect: tiptoeing shows rain falling lightly, drop by drop; snapped shows a mouth closing fast, [[s02-K50-1]]. *5 min.* The class shares what each verb shows.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Read The frog ___ down the path, glad and full of energy twice, once with bounced and once with trudged, and say what each makes the reader see, [[s02-K50-2]]. Say: choose the verb that gives the feeling the sentence wants. *5 min.* Pairs choose a verb for the effect on each of four cards and say why, [[s02-P50-6]]. Error R (a verb that works against the effect) is watched here.

#### 10 min. Talk

Groups of three, 3 minutes a child: read one paragraph of the draft; the next speaker waits for the reader to finish, then links a question to what was read; the reader notes one change in the margin, [[s02-P50-7]]. Review: the discussion moves (day 40). The teacher circulates; the heard-six column is not used. The last minute: children take back their drafts and notes.

#### 20 min. Writing

Each child uses the peer note and the teacher's note: change one verb to fit the effect, [[s02-P50-8]]; check the time words from yesterday still show the order, [[s02-P50-10]]; write what the note asked and what changed, [[s02-P50-9]]. Watch for error AC (recopying treated as revision): ask what the note asked for and where the change is. Watch for error R. Each draft is dated and kept in the folder with the plan, the notes and the earlier draft; a copied final alone is not kept (error T).

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 50 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X50]]. While they start, hear three children from the heard-six column, one at a time, 45 seconds each, [[s02-X50b]]; read the second card to each. Unscored. Shown: a quiet verb in place of shouted softly, and a reason from the effect. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What is the sentence trying to do?" Not yet seen: error AC (the sentence copied), error R (a loud verb kept for a quiet effect) or no reason from the effect.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner and the peer, and hears the recording read aloud. No device: skip the recording; each child reads stanza three aloud, noted. No cards: read the four sentences aloud. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No slate: use paper. No device: your child reads stanza three of Under the Leaf aloud to you.
**Skip.** The recording and the groups of three.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the old verb and the new verb in the revised sentence aloud.
  **Say.** "Which verb fits the feeling?" Expect: the new verb, with the feeling it gives.
- **20 min. Write.** Your child changes one verb in the draft, [[s02-P50-8]], and writes what changed, [[s02-P50-9]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X50]] alone, then answers [[s02-X50b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the cards; keep the verb change and the exit. Longer: a child chooses a verb for the opposite effect and says what changes.

### Words and supports

Effect, verb, peer note, teacher note. Frame, printed on the student page: The note asked me to ___. I changed ___ to ___. Large print; the note read aloud, recorded as help; a scribe for ideas, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X50]] and [[s02-X50b]]. Error R (a verb that works against the effect): read two verbs and choose the one that fits. Error AC (recopying treated as revision): name the change the note asks for, then make it. Day 51 is a check: a reading note on the saved clip, a note about words, and a language card. The dated folder goes with each child to day 52.
