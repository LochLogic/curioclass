# Section 2.3 opener: revising with care (days 48 to 51)

## What the section does

For four days each child revises their tale: the problem and response with speech and a comparison on day 48, the resolution and time links on day 49, and words chosen for their effect on day 50. Day 51 is a check: reading a poem aloud from a saved clip, explaining a drawn display, and a few language items.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Days 48 to 50 teach a comparison with -er, -est, more and most, joining clauses with and, but, so, because and when, and choosing a verb to match an effect, and children apply each to the draft. Day 50 ends the recording windows, so every child has been recorded once. Day 51 is silent except the saved clip: three item sheets, a drawn labelled leaf as the display, and a reading note.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A comparison uses a pattern.** Two things: -er or more. Three or more: -est or most.
- **A clause can be joined.** and, but and so join equal clauses; because and when add a reason or a time.
- **A verb carries a feeling.** The same action reads differently with a quiet verb and a loud one. Ask which fits what the sentence wants.
- **A display helps a listener.** A label on a drawing points to the words it explains.

## The errors, in plain words

- **P:** An event has no developed character response. Repair: Expand it with dialogue and a response. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **N:** A comparison has an invalid form. Repair: Match its noun or action to the comparison pattern. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **AB:** A conjunction or linking word joins unrelated ideas. Repair: Choose related clauses before joining. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **AG:** A dependent clause is used as a complete sentence. Repair: Attach it to a clause with a complete thought. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **O:** Narrative events have an incoherent order. Repair: Order event cards before adding time links. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **AC:** Recopying is treated as revision. Repair: Make the meaning change requested in feedback. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **R:** A word contradicts its claimed effect. Repair: Read two choices and choose the intended effect. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **K:** A display does not clarify the recording. Repair: Match one label to a spoken detail. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
The day 51 items are scored on the end columns of the record sheet.
