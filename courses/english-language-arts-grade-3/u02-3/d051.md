# Day 51: Check a poem reading, words chosen for effect, and verbs and comparisons

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the item sheets, day 51, and a display leaf sheet for each child. Take the word check cards for the two children not yet heard. Mark six names on the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills (retrieval only)

*3 min.* Return and repair, on day 50's exit (s02-X50 and s02-X50b), error R (a verb that works against the effect) and error AC (recopying treated as revision): children mark the verb that fits, [[s02-P51-2]]. *7 min.* No new pattern. Review: -tion and -sion (day 41). Children write collection, vacation and invention on slates, [[s02-P51-1]]. *20 min.* Independent reading from the logs, no teaching. Hear the last two children for the word check begun on day 45, about 2 minutes 15 seconds each, which completes the word check.

#### 35 min. Items

Silent, each on its own sheet; no stem is read and no hint given. Sheet 51A, 5 minutes: each child writes in the reading note on the display leaf sheet, [[s02-A17]], [[s02-A18]], [[s02-A20]]. After class, mark [[s02-A19]] and [[s02-A21]] from each saved clip and drawn leaf, by phrasing and expression with no speed target; a clip of stanza one or two is judged on the last two lines it holds. Sheet 51B, 10 minutes: the invented note, which uses patient (review: grade 2), [[s02-A22]], [[s02-A23]]. Sheet 51C, 20 minutes: the invented language card, [[s02-A24]], [[s02-A24b]], [[s02-A25]], [[s02-A26]], [[s02-A26b]], [[s02-A27]], [[s02-A27b]]. Record each part on the record sheet with the unit's one scale: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Portfolio preparation, in silence

Review: a display and a label on a drawn scene (day 40). Each child draws the place the frog waits on the display leaf sheet and labels it. Then each child puts the folder in order and dated: plan, draft, notes, revision. Error K (a display that does not clarify the recording): a mountain on a leaf page.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the day 51 card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X51]]. While they start, hear six children from the heard-six column, 45 seconds each, [[s02-X51b]]; read the second card to each. Unscored. Shown: a label that names where the frog waits, and a phrase from the clip that says it. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Where does the frog wait?" Not yet seen: error K or a phrase that does not say where the frog waits.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult sits apart and hears the child read Under the Leaf. No device: the reading to the teacher is the clip. No printer: copy the sheets by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed sheets: copy the items onto paper.
**Skip.** The saved clip, the word check and the spoken exit ask.

- **15 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s02-A22]], [[s02-A23]], [[s02-A24]] and [[s02-A27]] alone, with no hints.
  **Say.** "Which word tells how sure the speaker was?" Expect: knew, because the speaker saw the frog.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads for 20 minutes and tells you one phrase to read slowly.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X51]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear the six exit asks on day 52. Longer: a child draws a second label on the leaf.

### Words and supports

Label, display, reading note, patient. Large print; items read aloud except decoding; extra time. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X51]] and [[s02-X51b]]. Error K (a label that does not show the hiding place): match one label to a spoken detail. Each child carries the dated folder to day 52.
