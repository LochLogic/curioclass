# Display leaf sheet, day 51

Print one for each child. A large blank leaf outline fills most of the page, with room beside it for three or four label lines, and a box below headed Reading note. The child draws the place the frog waits, labels it, and in the note writes one word they decoded, the clue that checked it, and that they reread.
