# Event cards

Print one set for each pair, cut apart. Six cards; each has a number on the front and a time word on the back: First, Next, Then, After that, Later, At last. Children write a short event on the front of three or four cards, shuffle them, and a partner puts them in order and reads the time words on the back to check.

Front: a box with the number 1 to 6 in the corner and room for one short event.
Back: one time word, large.
