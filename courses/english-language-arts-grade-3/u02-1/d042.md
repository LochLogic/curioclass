# Day 42: Read the colorful-frog tale and plan a tale of your own

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a plan card and a tale copy for each child, and a set of event cards for each pair. Write the day 42 exit card on the room copy. Check the recording list: five names for today. Write the muddled plan card from the student page on the board only after the pairs are seated.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair, on the commonest letter in day 41's exit (s02-X41), error Y. Write three events from the Kikker tale out of order. Children number them and draw the arrows, cause first. *7 min.* Review: -tion and -sion (grade 2). Say direction, collection and extension once each; children write them on slates, [[s02-P42-6]]. Review: an irregular word has a part that does not sound the way it looks (day 40). Map would, [[s02-P42-7]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Review: a possessive with one owner takes 's (day 12). Children write on slates the cave that belongs to Ranna and the lake that belongs to Kikker, [[s02-P42-5]]. *15 min.* The second recording window: five more children, 3 minutes each, swap included, [[s02-P42-9]]. Review: recording (day 31). Every other pair rereads stanza two of Pond Voices with the feedback card, phrases and expression, no speed, [[s02-P42-8]]. The card is the partner's feedback today.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*2 min.* Ask on slates: which event made Kikker's tongue strong, [[s02-P42-11]]. Unscored. *3 min.* Say first: the fire in this tale hurts nobody. Read Why the Poison Dart Frog Is So Colorful aloud once. Gloss scooped, wriggled and brilliant with a gesture or picture as they come. *6 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs: "Which event turns danger into escape?" Pairs reread from "the frogs hop deeper" to the crack and point to the words, [[s02-K42-2]]. *4 min.* Class share.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*6 min.* On the tale copy, the teacher marks and names the parts: who tells it, the sentence that tells the situation, [[s02-K42-1]], three events and the ending. Do not draw a card. *4 min.* Pairs number three events from the tale on their page, [[s02-P42-1]]. Check with the event cards: the front holds the event, the back holds its time word.

#### 10 min. Talk

*3 min.* The teacher models a recording on Pond Voices stanza one: state the purpose, read, hear the replay, change one pause. Then draw the reeds and label where the peep comes from. *4 min.* Pairs, about 105 seconds each, swap included: tell your plan and ask your partner whether the events are in order, [[s02-P42-10]]. *3 min.* Three volunteers read a plan, 1 minute each. The class checks the order with event cards. Anyone may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

*4 min.* Pairs judge a muddled plan card and mark the event that is out of order, [[s02-P42-2]]. *13 min.* Each child chooses one feature from the list and plans a different invented tale on the plan card: who tells it, the situation, two or three events in order, the ending, [[s02-P42-4]]. This is the first sitting of the frog tale, written as a card and not as the tale. The list leaves out marks and colors; the exit is a fresh prompt. *3 min.* Add a time word to each event, [[s02-P42-3]]. The card stays in the folder for days 43 to 50.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the exit card and read nothing aloud. Children write alone, [[s02-X42]]. Unscored. Shown: names who tells it, a situation, two events in order, and an ending that explains the marks. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What happens first?" Not yet seen: the ending before its cause (error O) or a list of words with no situation (error S).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the tale aloud, marks the three parts with the child and helps the child choose a feature. No event cards: write the events on slips of paper. No device: skip the recording and read stanza two aloud. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No plan card: write the plan on one sheet.
**Skip.** The slate words.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the colorful-frog tale aloud with your child.
  **Say.** "Which event turns danger into escape?" Expect: the frogs hop deeper and Ranna finds the crack.
- **10 min. Plan.** Your child reads the plan card aloud and adds one time word to each event, [[s02-P42-3]].
- **10 min. Write.** Your child fills in or improves the plan card, [[s02-P42-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X42]] alone, from the strip you hold.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the possessives and the talk; keep the reread, the plan card and the exit. Longer: a child draws a picture of the ending.

### Words and supports

Scooped, wriggled, brilliant, escape, plan. Frame: "First ___. Then ___. That is why ___." Large print; the tale read aloud twice; a scribe for ideas, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 43 opens with 3 minutes repairing the order of events from today's exit, [[s02-X42]], then drafts a narrator, a situation and a beginning for the frog tale from the plan card. The plan card stays in the folder for days 43 to 50 and goes on to section 2.2.
