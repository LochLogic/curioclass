# Section 2.1 opener: frog poems and a tale to learn from (days 40 to 42)

## What the section does

For three days the class reads frog poems and two short tales that explain why a frog has a feature. The compelling question goes on the chart: How does where a frog lives shape how it looks and acts? Children learn that a poet can say a thing in a way that is not exactly true and still mean something plain, they number the events of a tale in order, and they plan a tale of their own on a card. The recording window opens on day 40 with one poem, five children at a time.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 40 reads the poems and hears one group of five in talk. Day 41 reads the long-tongue tale and maps its events with arrows. Day 42 reads the colorful-frog tale, rehearses reading a stanza with a partner, and ends with each child making a plan card for a tale of their own. Each day ends with a one-ask exit on a made-up card.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Words can mean more or less than they say.** A wind that "whispers" does not speak. Ask what the words say, then what the poet means.
- **A tale explains by events.** One event leads to the next, and the last one is why the frog is as it is. Say the because.
- **A plan comes before a draft.** Who tells it, the starting situation, two or three events in order, the ending.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** Nonliteral language is read as a physical event. Repair: Contrast the literal object with contextual meaning. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **A:** A fact is asserted without source support. Repair: Locate the exact source sentence. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **Y:** A causal relationship is reversed. Repair: Draw a cause-to-result arrow from the source. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **B:** A topic replaces a complete central idea. Repair: Join two key events into a complete lesson statement. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **O:** Narrative events have an incoherent order. Repair: Order event cards before adding time links. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **S:** Writing leaves the intended explanation unclear. Repair: Add the missing explanation for its purpose. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
