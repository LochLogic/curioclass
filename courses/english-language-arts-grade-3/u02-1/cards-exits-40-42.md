# Exit cards, days 40 to 42

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 40 (s02-X40).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "Raindrops drummed upon a leaf. Frog leaped below to find relief." Ask: Tell a partner why the frog leaped below the leaf. Use the word drummed.
- **Day 41 (s02-X41v2).** No card: the exit asks on the last paragraph of the tale (items.csv).
- **Day 42 (s02-X42).** This card is invented for practice. Card: "A grey frog hid beneath a painted leaf. Rain carried the colors onto its back. At sunrise it wore the leaf's bright marks." Ask: Plan a different tale about why a frog has marks. Write who tells it, the starting situation, two events in order and the ending.
