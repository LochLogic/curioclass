# Day 65: Check: write a complete paragraph from a new panel

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, with Panel C on side 1. Clear the desks of notes, the book and the excerpt. Rule a paragraph page for each child. Set the strip for the exit and the record sheet. Pick four names for conferences from the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 40 min. 65A

Silent. Each child reads Panel C and answers alone. Part 1, 6 minutes: [[s02-A50]]. Part 2, 24 minutes: [[s02-A51]], [[s02-A52]], [[s02-A53]] and [[s02-A54]], one paragraph on the paragraph page, with an optional labeled sketch. Part 3, 10 minutes: [[s02-A55]] to [[s02-A59]] and [[s02-A60]]. Read nothing aloud; if Panel C is read to a child, record it as help. Answer nothing about content. Record each part on the record sheet: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Silent verification

Children reread their own sheets. They may add a missed link word or label in the second pen and add nothing the teacher has said. Collect the sheets and the paragraph pages.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X65]] and [[s02-X65b]]. Shown: a question the strip answers, and a one-sentence answer with because. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the strip tell you?" Not yet seen: a question the strip does not answer, a statement, or the cause and result reversed (error Y).

#### 30 min. Skills (retrieval only)

*3 min.* Return and repair day 64's exit (s02-X64, s02-X64b, s02-X64c, s02-X64d and s02-X64e), error L (a regular ending on an irregular verb): say the right form aloud, then write it, [[s02-P65-3]]. *7 min.* Week 13 spelling rules on slates, no new pattern, [[s02-P65-1]]. *20 min.* Independent reading. Hold four conferences of 4 minutes, recording only and teaching nothing, and add the four to the heard-six column. The last 4 minutes: each child writes a dated line, [[s02-P65-2]].

### Other rooms

One child: the adult leaves the table during 65A and the silent verification, and records any help. No printer: the sheet must be printed; Panel C on the board is not enough for a check.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal book.
**Skip.** The conferences and the spelling.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Your child reads Panel C and answers [[s02-A50]], [[s02-A51]], [[s02-A52]], [[s02-A53]], [[s02-A54]], [[s02-A55]], [[s02-A56]], [[s02-A57]], [[s02-A58]], [[s02-A59]] and [[s02-A60]] alone, in silence.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from the log book.
  **Say.** "What did you wonder?" Expect: one question about the book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X65]] and [[s02-X65b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the assessment is never shortened; the skills part is. Longer: none on a check day.

### Words and supports

Panel, prey, temporary, link, question. Large print; items read aloud except where reading is the evidence; a scribe for the paragraph; the sketch may replace one written sentence. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 66 hands back the day 65 sheets, [[s02-A50]] to [[s02-A60]], with written notes, and returns the day 65 exit, [[s02-X65]] and [[s02-X65b]], repairing error Y first: draw the cause to result arrow. It repairs the commonest letter from the sheets, and the groups go by letter. The day 65 revision is made on day 67. The spelling and log work, [[s02-P65-1]] to [[s02-P65-3]], stays in the folder.
