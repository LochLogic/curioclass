# Day 62: Add notes from the frog book and say what each source adds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the note card for each child. Lay out the day 61 repair page and note card, s02-P61-6 and s02-P61-7, with the day 60 sheets, the day 59 noun sheet and the day 60 spelling sheet, each with one written note. Set the book copy and the excerpt on every desk and one question-starter card on every pair desk. Pick three children for the spoken exit from the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 61's exit (s02-X61 and s02-X61b), error V (a search that ignores the headings): hand back the strips, then write one new request on the board, [[s02-P62-1]]. *4 min.* Hand back the day 61 repair page and note card, s02-P61-6 and s02-P61-7, and the three sheets, each with its note. Each child makes the one change the spelling note asks for, [[s02-P62-12]]. *3 min.* Week 13. Review: you spelled famous and nervous on day 54 movement on day 53 and excitement on day 58. Say the four, write them on slates, [[s02-P62-2]], then ask [[s02-P62-3]]. The silent e drops before -ous and stays before -ment.

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Each child makes the one change the noun sheet note asks for, [[s02-P62-11]]. *15 min.* Partner rereading of pages 10 to 12: one child reads a page, the partner says one phrase that sounded smooth and one word to try again, then swap pages. No speed. Sit with three pairs for 3 minutes each and listen for phrases and expression.

Check: the partner names one smooth phrase, found on the page, and one word to try again.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Review: a source label has the source and where in it (day 57). Write a label for a book fact on page 11, [[s02-P62-4]], and for a fact in the excerpt's Habitat paragraph, [[s02-P62-5]]. *3 min.* Read the Habitat lines on page 12 and the excerpt's Habitat paragraph aloud. Define no word in either. *9 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs, both pages open. **Ask.** "What does each source add?" Each child marks with a pencil one detail only the book gives, [[s02-P62-6]], and one only the excerpt gives, [[s02-P62-7]] (6 minutes). Then share (3 minutes). Expect the eggs held on a leaf over the stream, or the tadpoles dropping into it, from the book; and the range from southern Mexico into northern Argentina from the excerpt. Watch for a detail that both pages give.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*3 min.* Say unusual from the heading on page 13: not like most others, [[s02-K62-1]]. Say surface from the excerpt's Description: the outside of something, [[s02-K62-2]]. Both are new today. *7 min.* Each child writes one sentence about the glass frog with unusual or surface and gives the page, [[s02-P62-8]]. Walk the room; a sentence with surface may name the frog's skin.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, both sources open, the question-starter card between them. One child is the speaker for 2 minutes 30 seconds: name one detail and its source. The partner asks one question linked to it, [[s02-P62-9]], and the speaker answers from the page, [[s02-P62-10]]. Then swap (10 minutes). Listen to four pairs, 2 minutes each, for the question rule only; no record. The scored discussion is day 70.

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Fix the one answer your note names on the day 60B sheet, [[s02-P62-13]]. *5 min.* Fix the box or label your note names on a day 60A note, [[s02-P62-14]]. *10 min.* Three more notes for the note set. Each child finds the words on the page, marks them, and gives the box and label, [[s02-P62-15]], [[s02-P62-16]] and [[s02-P62-17]], then copies each onto a note card. Watch for error X: a note in the wrong box.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strips to every child and read them aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X62b]]. While they write, call three children to the table, 90 seconds each: show the strips and ask [[s02-X62]] and [[s02-X62c]]. Shown: the wildlife page named, then Habitat, a fact only the book tells, and on paper a brief note with its box and label. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which strip says it?" Not yet seen: the book named for the eggs (error A), or a fact the strip does not give.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult takes the partner role and asks the starter questions. No printer: copy the note cards by hand. No excerpt: use any short wildlife page beside a book page on the same animal.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal page.
**Skip.** The partner reading and the pair exchange.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads pages 10 to 12 aloud, then the Habitat paragraph.
  **Say.** "What does each page add?" Expect: the book says the eggs hang on a leaf over a stream; the other page says where glass frogs live.
- **15 min. Notes.** Your child finds the words on the page and fills [[s02-P62-15]], [[s02-P62-16]] and [[s02-P62-17]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X62]] and [[s02-X62c]] aloud and [[s02-X62b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one revision, two notes, and record the rest as not reached. Longer: a fourth note from the excerpt's Description paragraph, with its box and label.

### Words and supports

Source, label, note, habitat, unusual, surface. Frames: "The book adds ___. The page adds ___." Large print; a factual description of each drawing; a scribe for the notes; the pages read aloud again; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 63 hears the exit, [[s02-X62]], [[s02-X62b]] and [[s02-X62c]], and repairs error A first: keep each note to what its source says. Children draft a paragraph from the notes. The revised sheets, [[s02-P62-11]] to [[s02-P62-14]], stay in the folder; the teacher scores the day 60 items from them before day 63 and records the marks. The slate, board and spoken work, [[s02-P62-1]] to [[s02-P62-10]], [[s02-K62-1]] and [[s02-K62-2]], and the three note cards stay in the folder.
