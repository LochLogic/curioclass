# Day 63: Draft a paragraph with a topic, grouped facts and links

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child. Score the day 60 items (s02-A39 to s02-A49) from the revised sheets in the folders, and record them. Read the day 62 strips and tally the error letters. Pick four names for conferences from the heard-six column. Set the question card on every pair desk.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 62's exit (s02-X62, s02-X62b and s02-X62c), error A (a fact the strip does not give): hand back the strips, then ask the class which note is not in a new strip, [[s02-P63-1]]. *7 min.* Week 13. Review: you changed y to i for cries and stories in grade 2, and you double the last letter after a short vowel (day 33). Say carry and carried: the y changes to i before -ed, and before -ness too, [[s02-K63-1]]. Dictate swimming, flapped, carried, happiness and tininess; children write them on slates, [[s02-P63-2]], then say which rule each of swimming and carried used, [[s02-P63-3]]. Last, the irregular word through: mark the letters that do not sound the way they look, [[s02-P63-4]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children read from their logs. Hold four conferences of 4 minutes: the child tells one new fact and where the words are. Add the four to the heard-six column. The last 4 minutes: each child writes a dated line, [[s02-P63-16]].

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Review: a clause is a doer and what it does. Write the complete clause on the slate, [[s02-P63-5]]. *3 min.* Read page 12 aloud, about 190 words, with no word defined. Ask [[s02-P63-6]] and take two more sentences the same way. *9 min.* Hard-part reread, in pairs. **Ask.** "Which two facts belong together?" Each pair points to two facts on page 12 that go in one group, [[s02-P63-7]], and says why, [[s02-P63-8]] (6 minutes). Then share (3 minutes). Expect the eggs on a leaf and the tadpoles dropping into the stream, both about where the young grow. Watch for two facts from different groups.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*4 min.* Review: you joined facts with and, but and because in unit 1 (day 32). Show a joined pair that fits, [[s02-K63-2]], and one that does not, [[s02-K63-3]]. The test is the same each time: does the second part belong with the first, and does the joining word fit? *6 min.* Children judge four joined sentences on the page, [[s02-P63-9]] to [[s02-P63-12]]. Watch for error AB, a joining word that links ideas that do not belong.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: tell your topic sentence and two facts that belong together, [[s02-P63-13]]; the partner says whether they belong and why, [[s02-P63-14]] (4 minutes). Then three volunteers, 2 minutes each, with the class saying whether the facts belong (6 minutes).

#### 20 min. Writing

Product I07, first draft. Each child writes one paragraph about the grainy Cochran frog from the note set, [[s02-P63-15]]. The paragraph has a topic sentence that names two groups, body and home; the body facts together; the home facts together; a definition; two joined related clauses; and a closing sentence. Teacher moves: ask a child to say the groups before writing; point to the notes under each box. Watch for error AA: body facts mixed into the home group.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X63]]. Shown: two notes that belong together, one sentence, and a joining word that fits. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Does the second part belong with the first?" Not yet seen: a joining word between unrelated notes (error AB) or notes from two groups (error AA).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult takes the partner role in the pair work and checks that the two facts belong together. No printer: copy the four sentences on the board. No frog book: use any short animal page.

### At home

**Swap.** No slate: write on paper.
**Skip.** The conferences and the volunteers.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads page 12 aloud.
  **Say.** "Which two facts belong together?" Expect: two facts about where the young frogs grow, such as the eggs on a leaf and the tadpoles dropping into the stream.
- **20 min. Write.** Your child marks [[s02-P63-9]] to [[s02-P63-12]], then writes the paragraph, [[s02-P63-15]], from the notes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X63]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the topic sentence and one group, and record the rest as not reached. Longer: add one more related fact to a group with also.

### Words and supports

Topic sentence, group, belong, link, definition, because. Frames: "These facts belong together because ___." Large print; a scribe for the paragraph; the page read aloud again; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 64 hears the exit, [[s02-X63]], and repairs error AB first: test the joining word, does the second note belong with the first. If day 63's tickets showed error AA instead, sort the notes into the two boxes before any revising. Children revise the paragraph and edit it. The draft, [[s02-P63-15]], and the page, [[s02-P63-9]] to [[s02-P63-12]], stay in the folder. The slate and spoken work, [[s02-P63-1]] to [[s02-P63-8]], [[s02-P63-13]], [[s02-P63-14]], [[s02-P63-16]] and [[s02-K63-1]] to [[s02-K63-3]], stays in the folder.
