# Day 64: Revise the paragraph and edit verbs, tenses and comparisons

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child. Read the day 63 drafts and mark, in the margin, one verb, one comparison and one plural for each child to edit. Tally the error letters from the day 63 strips. Copy the two model paragraphs on the board or ready them on the projector. Set out the day 63 drafts.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 63's exit (s02-X63), error AB (a joining word between ideas that do not belong): hand back the strips, then ask whether the parts fit in a new sentence, [[s02-P64-1]]. *7 min.* Week 13, mixed check. Each child underlines every -ment, -ous, -ness, -ing or -ed word in the day 63 draft, [[s02-P64-2]], and says the base and the rule for one of them, [[s02-P64-3]]. Last, write the base word of hopping, nervous and kindness on slates, [[s02-P64-13]].

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Grammar on slates. Review: you sorted regular and irregular verbs on day 43 and compared two with -er and three with -est on day 48. Cross out the three that are not words, [[s02-P64-4]], and circle the word for two frogs, [[s02-P64-5]]. *15 min.* Partner rereading of the excerpt: one child reads the Description paragraph, the other the Habitat paragraph (211 words in all), then swap. Read permanent, vulnerable and undisturbed as written and define none. The partner says one smooth phrase and one word to try again. Sit with three pairs for 3 minutes each.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Review: today and yesterday forms of a verb (day 45). Circle the yesterday sentence, [[s02-P64-6]]. *12 min.* Hard-part reread. **Ask.** "Does the source support the revised connection?" Show the model paragraph, Part 1, and read it aloud. Point to one link in it, that the frog is hard to see because its color blends in, and to the words on page 11 that support it, [[s02-K64-1]] (3 minutes). Pairs then check one link in their own drafts against the page, [[s02-P64-7]], and change the link if the page does not say it (6 minutes). Class share (3 minutes). Watch for error A: a link that says more than the page.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*5 min.* Show Part 2, the short draft. Mark and name each flaw without fixing it. The weak link, where "it is green" has no reason joined to it. The comparison in "the most small frog", [[s02-K64-2]]. The plural in "many leafs", [[s02-K64-3]]. The last sentence about the dog, [[s02-K64-4]]. Say that the rule decides each one, not how it sounds. *5 min.* Each child circles one verb, one comparison and one plural in their own draft, [[s02-P64-14]].

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child: read the link you revised aloud, [[s02-P64-8]]; the partner says whether the page supports it and points to the words, [[s02-P64-9]] (4 minutes). Three volunteers, 2 minutes each, with the class pointing to the words (6 minutes).

#### 20 min. Writing

Product I07, final. *10 min.* Revise the links so the page supports each one, [[s02-P64-10]], and rewrite the closing sentence so it fits the topic sentence, [[s02-P64-11]]. Watch for error Q: a closing sentence about a new animal. *10 min.* Edit the verbs, tenses, comparisons, plurals and abstract nouns you marked, [[s02-P64-12]]. A clean copy of the first paragraph only if time allows.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X64e]]. While they write, call children to the table, 50 seconds each, about six: show the strip, ask [[s02-X64]], [[s02-X64b]], [[s02-X64c]] and [[s02-X64d]] in turn. Shown: sat, ran, smaller, and sentence 4 named. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Say the sentence aloud first." Not yet seen: sitted, runned, more smaller, or the bike sentence kept (errors L, N and Q). The other children's evidence is the paper strip and the edited paragraph, [[s02-P64-12]]; the next six come to the table in the next heard-six rotation.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult takes the partner role and points to the words on the page. No projector: copy the two models on the board. No printer: write the student page items on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No projector: point at the page.
**Skip.** The partner rereading and the volunteers.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the paragraph from day 63 aloud.
  **Say.** "What does your closing sentence do?" Expect: it comes back to what the paragraph is about.
- **20 min. Revise.** Your child fixes one link, [[s02-P64-10]], the closing sentence, [[s02-P64-11]], and circles and edits one verb, one comparison and one plural, [[s02-P64-14]] and [[s02-P64-12]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X64]], [[s02-X64b]], [[s02-X64c]] and [[s02-X64d]] aloud and [[s02-X64e]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: revise the links and the closing sentence, edit one marked word, and record the rest as not reached. Longer: write a clean copy of the whole paragraph.

### Words and supports

Revise, edit, link, source, support, comparison, plural. Frames: "The page supports my link because ___." Large print; a scribe for the revision, not for the edit of verbs, tenses and comparisons; the pages read aloud again; record any help.
