# Exit cards, days 62 to 66

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 62 (s02-X62).** This card is invented for practice. Two strips. Book, page 5: "A meadow frog freezes in dry grass." Wildlife page, Habitat: "Meadow frogs need still, shallow water for eggs." Ask: Say which source tells where the eggs are laid.
- **Day 62 (s02-X62b).** This card is invented for practice. Use the two strips on the day 62 card. Ask: Write one brief note from the wildlife page. Give its category and source label.
- **Day 63 (s02-X63).** This card is invented for practice. Four notes: long back legs; can jump far; lives in tall grass; the grass hides it. Ask: Pick two notes that belong together. Join them in one sentence with and or because.
- **Day 64 (s02-X64).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Yesterday the frog sitted on a log. Then it runned to the pond. It is the more small frog by the pond. My cousin has a blue bike." Ask: Say the right word for sitted in sentence 1.
- **Day 64 (s02-X64b).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Yesterday the frog sitted on a log. Then it runned to the pond. It is the more small frog by the pond. My cousin has a blue bike." Ask: Say the right word for runned in sentence 2.
- **Day 64 (s02-X64c).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Yesterday the frog sitted on a log. Then it runned to the pond. It is the more small frog by the pond. My cousin has a blue bike." Ask: Say the right words for more small in sentence 3. Say the frog is small next to one other frog.
- **Day 64 (s02-X64d).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Yesterday the frog sitted on a log. Then it runned to the pond. It is the more small frog by the pond. My cousin has a blue bike." Ask: Say which sentence does not fit the paragraph.
- **Day 65 (s02-X65).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Green treefrogs live near lakes and streams. Many green plants grow there. The frogs are hard to see among them." Ask: Write one question that this strip answers.
- **Day 65 (s02-X65b).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Green treefrogs live near lakes and streams. Many green plants grow there. The frogs are hard to see among them." Ask: Answer your question in one sentence. Use because.
- **Day 66 (s02-X66).** This card is invented for practice. Feedback request: "Your paragraph says the horned frog is bigger than a toad but gives no size." Ask: Say a focused research question from this feedback.
- **Day 66 (s02-X66b).** This card is invented for practice. Feedback request: "Your paragraph says the horned frog is bigger than a toad but gives no size." Ask: Say which index word or heading you would open to find the size.
- **Day 62 (s02-X62c).** This card is invented for practice. Use the two strips on the day 62 card. Ask: Write one thing only the book strip tells.
- **Day 64 (s02-X64e).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "Yesterday the tadpole growed legs." Ask: Write the right word for growed.
- **Day 66 (s02-X66c).** This card is invented for practice. Feedback request: "Your paragraph names the glass frog's home but not what it eats." Ask: Write a focused research question from this feedback.
