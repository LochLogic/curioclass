# Exit cards, days 62 to 66

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 62, the two strips for s02-X62b.** This card is invented for practice. Two strips. Book, page 5: "A meadow frog freezes in dry grass." Wildlife page, Habitat: "Meadow frogs need still, shallow water for eggs." (s02-X62v2 asks on the frog book and the excerpt instead; items.csv.)
- **Day 62 (s02-X62b).** This card is invented for practice. Use the two strips on the day 62 card. Ask: Write one brief note from the wildlife page. Give its category and source label.
- **Day 63 (s02-X63).** This card is invented for practice. Four notes: long back legs; can jump far; lives in tall grass; the grass hides it. Ask: Pick two notes that belong together. Join them in one sentence with and or because.
