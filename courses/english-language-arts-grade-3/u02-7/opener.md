# Section 2.7 opener: two sources, linked ideas and editing (days 62 to 66)

## What the section does

The class adds the wildlife page as a second source, joins notes that belong together, edits verbs and comparisons, asks and answers a question from a strip, and writes a focused research question from feedback.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes: reading and research, writing, a skills block and a five-minute exit. The exits are one ask each and unscored; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- Each source adds something; say what.
- A joining word works only for ideas that belong together.
- Irregular verbs and comparison forms are checked against a rule.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** A fact is asserted without source support. Repair: keep each note to what its source says. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **AB:** A conjunction or linking word joins unrelated ideas. Repair: test the joining word: does the second note belong with the first. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **L:** An irregular verb receives a regular ending. Repair: say the right form of the verb aloud before writing it. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **Y:** A causal relationship is reversed. Repair: draw the cause to result arrow. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **W:** Research has no focused question. Repair: narrow the question to one frog and one detail. 3 minutes, next day's opening.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
