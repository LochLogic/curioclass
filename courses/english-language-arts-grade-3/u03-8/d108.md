# Day 108: Plan a revised scene and compare a spoken model with its script

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the script and a scene plan card for each child. Have the day 107 exit slips and Exit box items, sorted by letter, with the commonest letter written beside each correction. In scene 1, find Ada's line after "Speaking softly." and the three speakers from "Priya:" to "Mateo:".

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say whispered, hesitate and direction. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P108-1]]. Review: you wrote direction in chunks on day 107 and met hesitate in grade 2. Whispered is new. *3 min.* Children write again and their, [[s03-P108-2]]: mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. Review: in grade 1 you met their (day 124) and in grade 2 you knew it by heart (days 38 and 93); again is new. *2 min.* One slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P108-3]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter (day 107). *2 min.* Pairs read each other's chunks aloud: each chunk must blend. Watch for error AI, AG and AH.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P108-4]]. Review: you filled this line on day 105. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes), each child saying a purpose and one thing noticed, [[s03-P108-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

*2 min.* Hand back the day 107 slip, s03-X107, and the Exit box item, s03-X107b. Beside each correction write the commonest letter; likely error B, the later part first: number the earlier scene or stanza before the later one. *3 min.* Pairs, 1 minute each, 1 to change over: tell your partner how scene 2 answers a question that scene 1 asks, [[s03-P108-6]].

#### 12 min. The spoken model and the script

*3 min.* The teacher reads scene 1 aloud; the class follows the brackets silently. Review: a stage direction is a note for the performer in brackets, not spoken (day 107). *7 min.* Say: a listener hears a voice pause, go soft or go up. A reader sees brackets, commas, question marks, a new line and a speaker's name that do the same job. This is new. Model, [[s03-K108-1]]: read Ada's line after "Speaking softly." with a soft voice and the bracket unsaid; then read it again saying the bracket, so children hear that it is wrong. Model, [[s03-K108-2]]: the three speakers from "Priya:" to "Mateo:", where a new line and a name show a new voice. Children draw a wavy line under one bracket that tells a voice what to do, [[s03-P108-7]] (2 minutes). Pairs name one difference between hearing a scene and reading it, [[s03-P108-8]] (2 minutes). Watch for error AE, a direction read as speech. *2 min.* Reread Ben's question, "Could I see the arrow first?", once, so children hear the voice rise.

#### 8 min. Plan a revised scene

Review: you made a plan card for a frog tale in unit 2 (day 42): who is where, events in order, an ending. Today the card tells scene 1 as a story with one action changed. Model, [[s03-K108-3]]: Priya, not Ben, asks to see the arrow. Fill each row aloud in order. Review: you chose a word for effect on day 41. Model, [[s03-K108-4]]: said becomes murmured, because the question is soft. Model, [[s03-K108-5]]: tell the plan in four complete sentences with a pause after each.

#### 20 min. Plan

Each child picks a different action to change; no one copies Priya. Row 1 is a drawing: who is where, each label written, [[s03-P108-9]]. Then the card: the changed action and the reason, [[s03-P108-10]]; the events in order, [[s03-P108-11]]; one spoken line, [[s03-P108-12]]; one action or thought, [[s03-P108-13]]; the ending, [[s03-P108-14]]; one stronger word, [[s03-P108-15]]. Circulate. Watch for errors S, T, U, V and W. The card goes in the scene folder.

#### 5 min. Report

Pairs, 2 minutes each, 1 to change over: tell the plan in four complete sentences at a pace the partner can follow, [[s03-P108-16]]. Watch for error AF, a fragment, and AN, too fast.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Each child writes one sentence about Ada that uses hurry, [[s03-P108-17]]. It comes from the Narrator in scene 1.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X108]]. The second item, [[s03-X108b]], is in the Exit box on the student page and goes to the teacher with the slip. Shown: the voice pauses and the bracket stays unsaid; the opening says who and where. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the voice do? Is the bracket spoken?" or "Who is there? Where are they?" Not yet seen: the bracket spoken (error AE), a fragment (error AF) or no place (error S). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads scene 1 aloud, the child follows the brackets, and they fill the card together. One projector: show the script and the plan card. The card and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed script: use two pages of dialogue from your child's book.
**Skip.** The slate line and the report to a partner.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you one scene from their book with one action changed, in four sentences.
  **Say.** "What did you change, and why?" Expect: one changed action with a reason, in whole sentences.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X108]] and the Exit box item, [[s03-X108b]], on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the spoken model, the plan card and the exit; cut the word in context and shorten the report. Longer: write the four report sentences on the back of the card.

### Words and supports

Stage direction, bracket, plan card, murmured, hurry. Frames: "The voice ___ at the bracket." "In the ___, ___ is at the ___." "First ___. Then ___. In the end ___." Audio beside print for the script; a part read aloud before it is performed; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 109 drafts the opening and first events of the revised scene. It begins by repairing the commonest letter from the exit, s03-X108 and s03-X108b, likely error AE: bracket the direction before the line is read. The word work, [[s03-P108-1]] to [[s03-P108-3]], the reading and spoken work, [[s03-P108-5]], [[s03-P108-6]], [[s03-P108-8]], [[s03-P108-16]] and the models, [[s03-K108-1]] to [[s03-K108-5]], stay in the folder. The log line, [[s03-P108-4]], the marked bracket, [[s03-P108-7]], the plan card, [[s03-P108-9]] to [[s03-P108-15]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P108-17]], go to the teacher.
