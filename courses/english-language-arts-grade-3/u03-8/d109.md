# Day 109: Draft the beginning of the revised scene

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the script and the chapter 5 passage copy for each child, and a feedback card for each pair. Have the scene plan cards from day 108, and the day 108 slips and Exit box items sorted by letter. In scene 1 mark the passage from "Ben pauses before he answers." to "There. Now you can look.", about 140 words. In chapter 5 mark "Here is the truth." to "heavy as a rock.", about 155 words.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say proceeding, quietly and performer. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P109-1]]. Review: you wrote chunks in this warm-up all unit; the chunk words are new. *3 min.* Children write said and were, [[s03-P109-2]]: mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. Review: in kindergarten (day 146) and grades 1 and 2 you knew said and were by heart. *2 min.* One slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P109-3]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (days 107 and 108). *2 min.* Pairs read each other's chunks aloud. Watch for error AI, AG and AH.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading with a feedback card

Pairs, the marked part of scene 1, a part each: Ada and Ben; the child reading Ada also reads the Narrator. Review: you did this on day 107: the brackets stay unspoken, and you read in phrases with expression, with no race for speed. Each child reads their part, [[s03-P109-4]] (3 minutes); the partner marks what was read well and what needs more expression on the card, [[s03-P109-5]] (1 minute). Swap parts and do it again, with more expression on the second reading. Then each pair reads the passage once more together (4 minutes). That is 12 minutes with 3 to change over. Say nothing about speed.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Pairs say the job of the two underlined words in a Narrator sentence from scene 1: person and simply, [[s03-P109-6]]. Review: a noun names, an adverb tells how an action is done (grade 2); a verb acts and an adjective describes (day 86).

#### 5 min. Recall situation

*2 min.* Hand back the day 108 slip, s03-X108, and the Exit box item, s03-X108b, the commonest letter beside each correction; likely error AE: bracket the direction before the line is read. *3 min.* Pairs, 1 minute each, 1 to change over: tell your partner who is where when scene 1 opens, [[s03-P109-7]]. Review: a situation names who is where (the plan card, day 42).

#### 20 min. Reading and rereading

*4 min.* The class rereads scene 1 aloud in parts, brackets unspoken; any child may pass. *3 min.* The teacher reads the chapter 5 passage aloud while children follow. *3 min.* Children mark one detail of place and one word that shows Ada's feeling. *10 min.* Hard-part reread: children reread the passage and the scene and write a margin note on the place detail, [[s03-P109-8]], and one on the feeling word, [[s03-P109-9]]. Each note says how the opening they will write could use it. Circulate; ask any child with only a copied word, "How could your opening use it?"

#### 20 min. Draft the opening

Each child rereads the day 108 plan card (2 minutes), then drafts the opening so a listener can answer "Where are they, and what is about to happen?" The opening, [[s03-P109-10]] (6 minutes); the first two events in order with time words, [[s03-P109-11]] (6 minutes); one sentence on how the opening connects to scene 1, [[s03-P109-12]] (3 minutes); one word chosen from the opening and what it adds, [[s03-P109-13]] (3 minutes). Watch for error S, no where, and error U, a reversed time word.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each, 1 to change over: read your opening aloud, and your partner says where the scene is, [[s03-P109-14]].

#### 5 min. Word in context

Each child writes one sentence about Ada that uses heavy, [[s03-P109-15]]. It comes from the chapter 5 passage.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X109]]. The second item, [[s03-X109b]], is in the Exit box on the student page and goes to the teacher with the slip. Shown: the words that tell the listener where Orin is; a thing Orin says or does that shows doubt. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Where is Orin when it begins?" or "What does Orin say or do before he asks?" Not yet seen: a name with no place (error S) or an action that does not show doubt (error W). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the marked scene and the passage aloud, and the child drafts. One projector: show the passage and the plan card. The draft and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed passage: use one page from the start of your child's book.
**Skip.** The slate line and the feedback card.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child says the opening of their scene aloud.
  **Say.** "Where do you think the scene is?" Expect: the place, named from what the child said.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X109]] and the Exit box item, [[s03-X109b]], on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the passage, the opening and the exit; cut the word in context and the grammar retrieval. Longer: draft a third event with a time word.

### Words and supports

Opening, situation, listener, heavy, hesitate. Frames: "The scene is in the ___ because ___." "My opening starts ___, as scene 1 does." Audio beside print for the passage; a part read aloud before it is performed; record any help.

The word work, [[s03-P109-1]] to [[s03-P109-3]], the reading and spoken work, [[s03-P109-4]], [[s03-P109-5]], [[s03-P109-6]], [[s03-P109-7]] and [[s03-P109-14]], stay in the folder. The margin notes, [[s03-P109-8]] and [[s03-P109-9]], the opening draft, [[s03-P109-10]] to [[s03-P109-13]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P109-15]], go to the teacher; the opening draft is returned on day 110 for a reread.
