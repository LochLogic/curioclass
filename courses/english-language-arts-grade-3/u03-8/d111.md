# Day 111: Use a partner's question to revise the scene

## Teacher page

### Before class

Collect the G110 sheets with the scene drafts. Print the student page, the script, a peer question card and a one-note, one-change card for each child. Have the scene drafts, and the day 110 slips and Exit box items sorted by letter. Post the discussion moves chart. Choose pairs; a child with no partner pairs with the teacher.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say surprise, hopefully and directions. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P111-1]]. Review: you wrote direction in chunks on days 107 and 108; directions adds an ending. Surprise and hopefully are new. *3 min.* Children write said and again, [[s03-P111-2]]: mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. Review: days 108 and 109. *2 min.* One slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P111-3]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (days 107 to 110). *2 min.* Pairs read each other's chunks aloud. Watch for error AI, AG and AH.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P111-4]]. Review: you filled a log line on days 105, 108 and 110. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes), each child saying a word they liked and why, [[s03-P111-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Read the peer question

Partners swap drafts. Each child reads the other's draft (2 minutes), then writes one question in its margin, [[s03-P111-6]], with the frame on the peer question card: "I cannot hear or see ___ when ___." (2 minutes), and hands the draft back (1 minute). Watch for error O, a question that does not come from the writer's words.

Check: the partner finds the frame filled in and a question that comes from the writer's own words.

#### 20 min. Reading, rereading and clarifying

*8 min.* Hard-part reread. Children reread Ada's lines in scene 1 and mark the words that show how she responds to Ben, [[s03-P111-7]]. *12 min.* Respectful linked exchange. Review: the group rules on the discussion moves chart (days 98 and 105). Pairs, 5 minutes each, 1 to change over, 1 to set up. The writer says which response the question means: a spoken line, an action or a thought, [[s03-P111-8]]. The partner asks one question linked to the writer's own words, [[s03-P111-9]]. The writer answers in complete sentences, [[s03-P111-10]]. Any child may pass. Watch for error N, an interruption, and error AF, a fragment for an answer.

#### 20 min. Revise dialogue and description

*2 min.* Hand back the day 110 slip, s03-X110, and the Exit box item, s03-X110b, the commonest letter beside each correction; likely error T: add an action, a thought or a spoken line to a named feeling. *18 min.* Each child uses the one-note, one-change card. Review: one note, one change, the first version beside it (day 105). Change one spoken line to answer the question, [[s03-P111-11]] (6 minutes); change or add one action or thought, [[s03-P111-12]] (6 minutes); write a margin note on one word chosen and its effect, [[s03-P111-13]] (6 minutes). Keep every speech mark and comma. Watch for error L, the same words written more neatly.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each, 1 to change over: tell your partner what you changed and why it answers the question, [[s03-P111-14]].

#### 5 min. Word in context

Each child writes one sentence about Mateo that uses tiresome, [[s03-P111-15]]. It comes from the Narrator in scene 2.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X111]]. Shown: an action or a spoken line a listener could see or hear, such as rubbing a sleeve or asking "Is it too late?" Shown with help: after one prompt, "What would Orin do, or say, that you could see or hear?" Not yet seen: a feeling named again (error T) or the same line recopied (error L). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the draft and writes the margin question, then plays the partner in the exchange. One projector: show the peer question card and the discussion moves. The draft, the revision and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed script: use any short scene your child has written, or a page of their book.
**Skip.** The slate line and the partner exchange.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child reads their scene aloud.
  **Say.** "Is there anything you cannot hear or see yet?" Expect: one thing a listener cannot yet hear or see, named by the child.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X111]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the margin question, the revision of one line and the exit; cut the talk and the word in context. Longer: answer a second question from the partner and revise again.

### Words and supports

Question, response, spoken line, action, thought, tiresome. Frames: "I cannot hear or see ___ when ___." "The question means ___." "I changed ___ because ___." Audio beside print for the script; a part read aloud before it is performed; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 112 applies the teacher's note to time words, word choice and agreement. It begins by repairing the commonest letter from the exit, s03-X111, likely error L: make one change from the question, the original kept beside it. The word work, [[s03-P111-1]] to [[s03-P111-3]], the reading and spoken work, [[s03-P111-5]], [[s03-P111-8]] to [[s03-P111-10]] and [[s03-P111-14]], and the marked lines, [[s03-P111-7]], stay in the folder. The log line, [[s03-P111-4]], the margin question, [[s03-P111-6]], the revision, [[s03-P111-11]] to [[s03-P111-13]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P111-15]], go to the teacher; the draft and the G110 sheet come back with a written note on day 112.
