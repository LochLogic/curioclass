# Day 110: Draft the dialogue, the description and the closing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the script, the chapter 5 passage copy and a G110 sheet for each child. Have the day 109 opening drafts, and the day 109 slips and Exit box items sorted by letter. In chapter 5 mark "Ben did not seem to mind being slow." to "too hot.", about 200 words. On the board write: Ben asked, "Could I see the arrow first?"

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say deciding, helpfully and explaining. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P110-1]]. Review: you have written chunks in this warm-up since day 81; these three words are new. *3 min.* Children write does and their, [[s03-P110-2]]: mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. Review: their (day 108). Does is new. *2 min.* One slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P110-3]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (days 107 to 109). *2 min.* Pairs read each other's chunks aloud. Watch for error AI, AG and AH.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P110-4]]. Review: you filled a log line on days 105 and 108. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes), each child reading the line and saying what the words told them, [[s03-P110-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Recall closure

*2 min.* Hand back the day 109 slip, s03-X109, and the Exit box item, s03-X109b, the commonest letter beside each correction; likely error S: name who is where, then the one word that shows the feeling. *3 min.* Pairs, 1 minute each, 1 to change over: tell your partner what closes the action in scene 1, [[s03-P110-6]]. Review: an ending that closes the action (the plan card, day 108).

#### 20 min. Reading and rereading

*1 min.* Review: you used quotation marks with the comma inside on day 44. Children copy the board line on the slate and check each mark against the board, [[s03-P110-7]]. *4 min.* Pairs reread Ben's lines in scene 1, brackets unspoken, a part each; any child may pass. *3 min.* The teacher reads the chapter 5 passage aloud while children follow. *12 min.* Children mark where an action sits beside Ben's spoken words and write a margin note on one: how does it show his response? [[s03-P110-8]]. Circulate; ask anyone who wrote "Ben is nice", "What does Ben do?"

#### 20 min. Draft dialogue, description and ending

*15 min.* Each child rereads the day 109 opening (2 minutes), then drafts what the characters say with speech marks and commas, [[s03-P110-9]] (5 minutes); one action and one thought that show a response, [[s03-P110-10]] (4 minutes); and the closing, [[s03-P110-11]] (4 minutes). Watch for error AL, speech without marks; error T, a feeling alone; error V, a new problem at the end. *5 min.* The G110 sheet, on the readers, silently. Review: you learned the job of each word class on day 86 and agreement on day 88. One sentence with a noun, a pronoun, a verb, an adjective and an adverb, [[s03-P110-12]]; the table with what each word does here and in general, [[s03-P110-13]]; a singular and a plural sentence, [[s03-P110-14]]. A child who has not finished the table says so; it is finished on day 112. Watch for error X, a class named with no job, and error Y, a verb that does not match.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each, 1 to change over: read your dialogue aloud, and your partner says which words are spoken, [[s03-P110-15]].

#### 5 min. Word in context

Each child writes one sentence about Ada that uses secret, [[s03-P110-16]]. It comes from the chapter 5 passage.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X110]]. The second item, [[s03-X110b]], is in the Exit box on the student page and goes to the teacher with the slip. Shown: the speech marks and the comma around the spoken words; an action or thought that shows what Rafe does. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which words are spoken?" or "What does Rafe do or think?" Not yet seen: no marks (error AL) or a feeling word alone (error T). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the passage aloud, and the child drafts and fills the sheet. One projector: show the passage and the board line. The draft, the sheet and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed passage: use one page of dialogue from your child's book.
**Skip.** The slate line and the G110 sheet.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child reads their dialogue aloud.
  **Say.** "Which words are spoken?" Expect: the words inside the speech marks.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X110]] and the Exit box item, [[s03-X110b]], on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the passage note, the draft and the exit; cut the talk and the word in context. Longer: draft a second spoken line with a thought beside it.

### Words and supports

Dialogue, speech marks, response, closing, secret. Frames: "The speech marks go around ___." "___ shows how ___ feels because ___." Audio beside print for the passage; a part read aloud before it is performed; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 111 uses a partner's question to revise the dialogue and detail. It begins by repairing the commonest letter from the exit, s03-X110, and the Exit box item, s03-X110b, likely error T: add an action, a thought or a spoken line to a named feeling. The word work, [[s03-P110-1]] to [[s03-P110-3]], the reading and spoken work, [[s03-P110-5]], [[s03-P110-6]], [[s03-P110-7]] and [[s03-P110-15]], stay in the folder. The log line, [[s03-P110-4]], the margin note, [[s03-P110-8]], the scene draft, [[s03-P110-9]] to [[s03-P110-11]], the G110 sheet, [[s03-P110-12]] to [[s03-P110-14]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P110-16]], go to the teacher; the draft and the G110 sheet come back with a written note on day 112.
