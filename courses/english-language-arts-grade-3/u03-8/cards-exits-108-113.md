# Exit cards, days 108 to 113

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. An exit slip is a half slip holding about two items. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 108 (s03-X108), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: [Wren pauses.] Wren: Did you take the mittens? Ask: Write what a reader's voice does at the bracket.
- **Day 108 (s03-X108b), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: Sana asks Rafe to hold the tape measure. Rafe says yes. Ask: Write one opening sentence for a scene about this. Tell who is where.
- **Day 109 (s03-X109), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A listener asks where Orin is. The opening says: Orin stood by the shed door. 'Er, could you hold this?' he asked. He looked at his shoes. Then he grinned. Ask: Write the words that tell the listener where Orin is.
- **Day 109 (s03-X109b), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: The opening says: Orin stood by the shed door. 'Er, could you hold this?' he asked. He looked at his shoes. Then he grinned. Ask: Write one thing Orin says or does that shows he hesitates.
- **Day 110 (s03-X110), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: Rafe tapped the shelf. Was the space too small? Then he said let us measure it. Ask: Rewrite the last sentence with speech marks and a comma.
- **Day 110 (s03-X110b), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: Rafe tapped the shelf. Was the space too small? Then he said let us measure it. Ask: Write one action or thought that shows Rafe's response.
- **Day 111 (s03-X111), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A peer note says, "I cannot hear or see that Orin is worried." Ask: Write one action or spoken line that shows Orin is worried.
- **Day 112 (s03-X112), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: A draft says, "Before the class folded the tables, Sana swept the floor." The writer meant that the class folded the tables first. Ask: Rewrite the sentence so the time word shows that order.
- **Day 113 (s03-X113), Exit.** Invented for practice. Strip: The twins packed lunches. Then she walked to the bus. Ask: Change one word so the pronoun matches the twins.
