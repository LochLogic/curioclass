# Day 113: Check the revised scene

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the audit card for each child. Have each child's scene folder with the plan, draft, question, note and revision, and the G110 sheet. The day 112 grammar record is already collected. Seat children apart.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Silent. Say whispering, direction and hesitate; children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P113-1]] (6 minutes). Say again and their; children write each and mark the odd part, [[s03-P113-2]] (4 minutes). Review: direction and hesitate (days 107 and 108), again and their (day 108). Whispering is new. Watch for error AI and AG.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Silent reading from the log book, alone. No conferences and no teaching. Children write the log line, [[s03-P113-3]]. Watch for error AK, a log line with no date.

#### 40 min. Portfolio audit

Silent. Each child completes the audit card, row by row, against the scene folder; the directions are read silently in the first 3 minutes of the first row. Rows 1 to 6 and 9 use the scene: situation, [[s03-A29]]; dialogue and description, [[s03-A30]]; time words, [[s03-A31]]; closing, [[s03-A32]]; word effect, [[s03-A33]]; process, [[s03-A34]]; speech, [[s03-A37]]. Rows 7 and 8 are fresh sentences: word classes, [[s03-A35]]; agreement, [[s03-A36]]. The scene is not rewritten. Give no hint. Mark each row once: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Watch for errors S to Z and AL.

#### 12 min. Independent preparation

*5 min.* Each child rereads the audit card against its directions and fixes up to two rows beside the first answer, [[s03-P113-4]]. *5 min.* Each child marks one passage in the play, one in a book and one in the article for the day 114 cards, with a one-line reason for each, [[s03-P113-5]]. *2 min.* The scene folder and audit card are collected.

#### 8 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X113]]. Support is recorded. Shown: a pronoun that matches the twins, they. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Who walked to the bus? How many people?" Not yet seen: she left in place (error Z) or it (error Z). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the directions aloud on request and records the help. The audit, the marks and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** The scene folder stays at school: answer the fresh rows only.
**Skip.** The long-words check and the marked passages.

- **15 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s03-A35]] and [[s03-A36]] on paper, alone. Give no hint.
- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then tells you the order of events in one chapter.
  **Say.** "What happened first, and what happened after?" Expect: two events in order, with a time word.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X113]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the audit rows and the exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Audit, folder, scene, closing, word class. Directions read aloud on request; large print for the card; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 114 opens the unit's check on the plays, books and the article. The scores and a written note on the scene folder come back in section 3.9. It returns s03-X113 on day 116, likely error Z: find the people the pronoun stands for, then match it. The word work, [[s03-P113-1]] and [[s03-P113-2]], the log line, [[s03-P113-3]], and the fixes, [[s03-P113-4]], stay in the folder. The marked passages, [[s03-P113-5]], go to the teacher for the day 114 cards.
