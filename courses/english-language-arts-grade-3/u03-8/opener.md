# Section 3.8 opener: revising a scene (days 108 to 113)

## What the section does

Children revise one scene from the class script across six days. Day 108 compares the teacher's spoken reading with the printed script and plans the revised scene. Day 109 drafts the opening and the first events. Day 110 drafts the dialogue, the description and the ending, and writes a short grammar sheet about the readers. Day 111 uses a partner's question to revise. Day 112 applies the teacher's note to time words, word choice and agreement. Day 113 is the check: the child audits the folder.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Every day opens with a long-words warm-up; the other time goes to reading, one drafting or revising block, a short pair talk and a written exit. The scene folder holds the plan, the draft, the partner's question, the teacher's note and the revision, and the original always stays beside the change.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A script is for a performer.** A bracketed direction is not spoken; the voice or the body does it.
- **A scene opens with a situation.** Who is where, and what is about to happen.
- **A response shows feeling.** A spoken line, an action or a thought shows it; the feeling word alone does not.
- **Time words order events.** Before, after, first and then must match what happened first.
- **Words carry effect.** A gentle word fits a gentle moment.
- **Subjects and verbs agree.** A pronoun matches the people it stands for.

## The errors, in plain words

- **S:** No situation. Repair: name who is where when the problem opens. 5 minutes.
- **T:** A feeling with no response. Repair: add an action, a thought or a spoken line. 6 minutes.
- **U:** A reversed time link. Repair: place two events on an arrow, then choose the word. 5 minutes.
- **V:** A new problem at the end. Repair: reread the last action and close it. 5 minutes.
- **W:** A word whose effect clashes. Repair: say two verbs aloud against the feeling. 5 minutes.
- **X:** A word class named without its job. Repair: say what the word does here. 5 minutes.
- **Y and Z:** Verb or pronoun does not match. Repair: mark the subject and the referent, then choose. 6 minutes.
- **AE and AF:** A direction read as speech; a fragment for a clarification. Repair: bracket the direction; rehearse with a subject and a verb. 5 minutes.
- **AL:** Speech without marks. Repair: mark the spoken words, then place the marks and comma. 6 minutes.
- **L and O:** Neat recopying; an unrelated question. Repair: model one margin change; build the question from the writer's own line. 5 minutes.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Exits are unscored. The scene folder is audited once on day 113.
