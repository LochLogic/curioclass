# Scene audit card (days 113 and 114)

Print one for each child, two sides. Silent, with the scene folder beside the card. The directions are read silently in the first three minutes of the first row. The scene is not rewritten. The teacher scores each row once on the 2, 1, 0 scale.

1. Situation. Read your scene. Mark who is there, where they are and what the problem is.
2. Dialogue and description. Read your scene. Mark one spoken line, one action, one thought and one feeling.
3. Time words. Read your scene. Circle each time word and say what it orders.
4. Closing. Read your scene. Mark the closing that settles the problem.
5. Word effect. Read your scene. Choose one word for effect and explain what it adds.
6. Process. Set your plan, feedback, revision and edits side by side. Write one change and why.
7. Word classes. In "The proud painters share a brush. They work carefully." write each class's job.
8. Agreement. Write a singular and a plural sentence about painters and brushes. Agree verbs and pronouns.
9. Speech. Look at your scene. Write one line of speech with its commas and quotation marks.
