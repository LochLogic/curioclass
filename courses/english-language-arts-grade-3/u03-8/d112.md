# Day 112: Apply the teacher's note to time words, word choice and agreement

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the script, a G112 sheet, a one-note, one-change card for each child and a feedback card for each pair. Have the scene drafts and G110 sheets with your written notes, and the day 111 slips sorted by letter. In scene 2 mark "He kneels and traces the last arrow" to "the arrow sends us into the door.", about 128 words.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Say exciting, suddenly and thoughtfully. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P112-1]]. Review: you have written chunks in this warm-up all unit; these three words are new. *3 min.* Children write were and does, [[s03-P112-2]]: mark the part that does not sound the way it looks. Review: days 109 and 110. *2 min.* One slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P112-3]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i (days 107 to 111). *2 min.* Pairs read each other's chunks aloud. Watch for error AI, AG and AH.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading with a feedback card

Pairs, the marked part of scene 2, a part each: Ben and Ada. Review: you did this on days 107 and 109: brackets unspoken, phrases, expression, no race for speed. Each child reads their part, [[s03-P112-4]] (3 minutes); the partner names on the card one place where the voice did what the print shows, [[s03-P112-5]] (1 minute). Swap parts and do it again. Then each pair reads the passage once more together (4 minutes). That is 12 minutes with 3 to change over. Say nothing about speed.

#### 5 min. Distinct grammar record

Silently, on the G112 sheet, about the partners. Review: you wrote a title with capitals on day 102, and a sheet like this on day 110. A new title, [[s03-P112-6]]; one sentence with a noun, a pronoun, a verb, an adjective and an adverb, [[s03-P112-7]]; the table, [[s03-P112-8]]; a singular and a plural sentence about the partners and seats, [[s03-P112-9]]. Children write what they can; this sheet is kept as a separate record and is revised on day 115. Watch for error AM, error X and error Y.

#### 5 min. Read the teacher note

Hand back the draft and the G110 sheet with the written note. Each child marks one change on the draft, [[s03-P112-10]], and one on the G110 sheet, [[s03-P112-11]]. Watch for error Y, a mismatch the note names: mark the subject and the referent, then choose the form.

#### 20 min. Reading and rereading

*4 min.* The class rereads scene 2 aloud in parts, brackets unspoken; any child may pass. *8 min.* Children find the three words that number the arrows and write them in order, [[s03-P112-12]], then mark one word chosen for effect and write what it adds, [[s03-P112-13]]. *8 min.* Pairs read two sentences aloud and say what the time words add to the order of events, [[s03-P112-14]]. Review: a time word tells which event comes first and which comes next (day 108).

#### 20 min. Revise

*2 min.* Hand back the day 111 slip, s03-X111, the commonest letter beside each correction; likely error L: make one change from the question, the original kept beside it. *18 min.* Each child uses the one-note, one-change card, the first version kept beside each change. Change the time words so the events are in order, [[s03-P112-15]] (5 minutes); change one word for effect, [[s03-P112-16]] (3 minutes); fix an agreement, [[s03-P112-17]] (4 minutes); rewrite the G110 table with all five word jobs, and finish any part left blank on day 110, [[s03-P112-18]] (6 minutes). Watch for error U, a reversed time word, and error L, the same words written more neatly.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each, 1 to change over: tell your partner what you changed and why, [[s03-P112-19]].

#### 5 min. Word in context

Each child writes one sentence about the route that uses useful, [[s03-P112-20]]. It comes from Ada in scene 2.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X112]]. Shown: a time word that puts the first event first, such as after or then. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Which happened first? Put an arrow between the two events." Not yet seen: the reversed word left in place, or while, which does not show the order (error U). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads scene 2 aloud, the child follows the time words, and they revise together. One projector: show scene 2 and the G112 sheet. The revision, the sheets and the exit stay on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed script: use a page of your child's own writing that has order words.
**Skip.** The slate line, the G112 sheet and the feedback card.

- **20 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book. No writing is needed.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you what they changed in their scene.
  **Say.** "What did you change, and why?" Expect: one change with a reason, in whole sentences.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X112]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the time words, the revision and the exit; cut the talk and the word in context. Longer: change a second time word and say why.

### Words and supports

Time word, order, effect, agree, useful. Frames: "Before ___, ___ happened first." "I changed ___ to ___ because ___." Audio beside print for scene 2; a part read aloud before it is performed; record any help.

### Tomorrow

The G112 sheet table, [[s03-P112-8]], is finished on day 115 in the G112 audit (5 minutes). Day 113 is the check: each child audits the scene folder on the scene audit card, silently, with a silent word check first. The G112 sheet, [[s03-P112-6]] to [[s03-P112-9]], is collected and carried to day 115. The word work, [[s03-P112-1]] to [[s03-P112-3]], the reading and spoken work, [[s03-P112-4]], [[s03-P112-5]], [[s03-P112-14]] and [[s03-P112-19]], and the notes, [[s03-P112-10]] and [[s03-P112-11]], stay in the folder. The marked words, [[s03-P112-12]] and [[s03-P112-13]], the revision, [[s03-P112-15]] to [[s03-P112-18]], and the word sentence, [[s03-P112-20]], go to the teacher in the scene folder; s03-X112 is returned on day 116.
