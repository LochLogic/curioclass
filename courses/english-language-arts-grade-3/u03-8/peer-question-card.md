# Peer question card (day 111)

Print one for each child, half a page. Partners swap drafts and each writes one question in the margin of the other's draft.

Frame: I cannot hear or see ____________ when ____________.

The respectful linked exchange, in order:
1. The writer says which response the question means: a spoken line, an action or a thought.
2. The partner asks one question linked to the writer's own words.
3. The writer answers in complete sentences.

The group rules are on the discussion moves chart.
