# Day 52: Check a fresh frog tale written alone

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a tale page and the two model tales for each child, and the exit cards. Take the record sheet, unit 2. Name the tale, the folder and the exit as the three things handed in. Mark six names on the heard-six column for day 53.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills (retrieval only)

*3 min.* Return and repair day 51's exit (s02-X51 and s02-X51b), error K (a label that does not show the hiding place): children judge a label against a described drawing, [[s02-P52-1]]. *10 min.* No new pattern. Review: -tion and -sion (grade 2). Say action, fiction and television; children write each on a slate, [[s02-P52-2]]. *5 min.* Review: comparisons (grade 1). Children finish the sentence on the board, [[s02-P52-3]]. *3 min.* Hand back day 51's assessment, items s02-A17 to s02-A27, and its two practice sheets (s02-P51-1 and s02-P51-2), with one note each. *9 min.* Partners reread Why the Frog Has a Long Tongue aloud, in turn. No recording, no teaching.

#### 40 min. Tale

Silent. Children reread both model tales (8 minutes), plan on the tale page (4 minutes) and write alone (28 minutes). Read the prompt aloud once, then answer nothing about content. After class, mark each part from the tale on the record sheet, one scale: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Help is recorded only for a scribe or extra time. The tale is judged on [[s02-A28]] (narrator and situation, events in order), [[s02-A29]] (speech, action, thought, feeling), [[s02-A30]] (time words), [[s02-A31]] (a closing that closes the problem), [[s02-A33]] (situation, change, outcome) and [[s02-A34]] (speech marks and a comma). Child-written, on sheet 52B: [[s02-A32]].

#### 5 min. Folder

Each child sets the fresh tale beside the dated folder and writes the one difference, [[s02-A35]].

#### 10 min. Silent verification

Children reread their tale. The teacher answers nothing about content and gives no hint. A child may fix a letter or a mark; a new sentence is not added.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the card and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X52]]. Then the second card, read twice, [[s02-X52b]]. Each card is first seen at the child's own turn. Collect the tale, the folder and the exit cards at the end of the day.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the prompt once and sits apart. No device or printer: copy the prompt by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed page: use lined paper.
**Skip.** The folder and the exit.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Your child reads one of the model tales, then plans and writes a short tale alone.
  **Say.** "What changes first, and what does the ending explain?" Expect: the situation changes, and the ending explains the frog feature.
- **Read.** Your child reads for 20 minutes from the log book.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the plan and the first three events; record that the closing was not reached. Longer: a child adds a second line of speech.

### Words and supports

Narrator, situation, closing, dialogue. Large print; the models may be read aloud; a scribe for the tale, with spelling and marks left as written where they are the evidence. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X52]] and [[s02-X52b]], error Q (a closing that starts a new problem): close the problem the tale opened. The tale returns with one written note; the folder goes to day 53. The slates and sheets stay in the folder.
