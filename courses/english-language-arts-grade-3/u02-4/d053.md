# Day 53: Use a note to repair the tale, and read the frog book for gist

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one note on each tale, naming one part and one change. Print the student page, the repair page and the book copy of pages 1 and 2 for each child. Mark six names for the spoken exit on the heard-six column, and four for conferences.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Return and repair day 52's exit (s02-X52 and s02-X52b), error Q (a closing that starts a new problem): children mark the ending that closes a given problem, [[s02-P53-1]]. *7 min.* New: the ending -ment means the act or result of. Show movement on the board and say it means the act or result of moving, [[s02-K53-1]]. Say movement, treatment and statement; children write each on a slate, [[s02-P53-2]]. Then map the irregular word Earth from page 1: children mark the part that does not sound the way it looks, [[s02-P53-3]].

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children read from their logs. Hold four conferences of 4 minutes: the child reads four sentences twice, smoothly, [[s02-P53-4]]. Add the four to the heard-six column. Last 4 minutes: each child writes one line in the log.

#### 10 min. Feedback

Hand back the tale and its note (3 minutes); hand back day 52's assessment, the end items s02-A28 to s02-A35, with the note. Each child reads the note and underlines the part of the tale it names, [[s02-P53-5]] (3 minutes). Repair on the board the letter most children missed on day 52 (4 minutes).

#### 20 min. Written repair

Each child writes that part again on the repair page, with the one change the note asks for, [[s02-P53-6]]. Copying without the change does not count (error AC). The whole-tale revision is on day 56.

#### 10 min. Reread

Read pages 1 and 2 of Frogs: Bodies and Homes aloud, without the figure notes (4 minutes). Model: which tells the gist of the habitat paragraph, (a) Habitat or (b) A habitat gives a frog what it needs; the teacher marks (b) and says it is a whole idea, [[s02-K53-2]]. In pairs, reread the three paragraphs on page 2 and underline the words that say why a green frog's color may help in a green forest, [[s02-P53-7]] (4 minutes); class share (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

New: habitat. Read the definition sentence on page 1. Each child writes a sentence with habitat in it and says it to a partner, [[s02-P53-8]] (6 minutes). The teacher shows a topic turned into a whole idea: Frogs becomes A frog's body and home go together (4 minutes).

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 1 minute a child: mark the sentence that tells what the opening section is mostly about, [[s02-P53-9]]; then tell the partner which words on page 2 show it. The partner points to them.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the bat card. While the rest read, hear the six named children one at a time, about 45 seconds each; read the card aloud twice, and the child says one sentence, [[s02-X53]]. The others are heard on days 54 and 55. Shown: one sentence naming bats resting by day and flying by night. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What are all three sentences about?" Not yet seen: one detail only.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner and reads page 1 aloud. No printer: copy the book copy by hand. Record any help.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed book: read page 1 aloud from the screen.
**Skip.** The spoken exit and the conference.

- **25 min. Read.** Your child reads pages 1 and 2 aloud to you.
  **Say.** "Say in one sentence what this part is mostly about." Expect: a frog's body and its home go together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X53]] aloud, in one sentence.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write only the change itself on the repair page. Longer: write the gist in two ways, short and long, and say which keeps more of the text.

### Words and supports

Gist, habitat, movement, note. Preview habitat, stream and tree with pictures. Frames: "This section is mostly about ___ and ___." Large print; extra time; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 opens with 3 minutes on this exit, [[s02-X53]], error B (one word instead of a whole idea): join two ideas into one sentence. The tale and repair page stay in the folder for day 56; the book copy goes to day 54.
