# Tale page, day 52

Print one two-sided page for each child. The first side is plain ruled lines with a plan box across the top (narrator, situation, two events, ending). The second side continues the ruled lines. Leave room at the foot of side two for the line: The word I chose for its effect is ______ because ______.
