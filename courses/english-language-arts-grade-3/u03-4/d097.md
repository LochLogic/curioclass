# Day 97: Chapter 5 of book 2: finish both maps and recount the two books

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one note on each day 96 episode the night before. Print the student page and a recount and message sheet for each child. Mark the book 2 chapter 5 page where Mateo chooses and Ms. Alvarez asks how Ada knew (from "Mateo came last" to "I asked", about 210 words). Keep the first paragraph of chapter 5 out of the reread. Pick the three pairs to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say conclusion, therefore and beginning. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P97-2]]. Then whose, [[s03-P97-3]]. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P97-4]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P97-6]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i. *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk, the other says what everyone means, then they swap, [[s03-P97-5]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AA, a word guessed from its first letters, and error AI, beginning split as be-ginn-ing.

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

Partner-rereading day. *5 min.* Grammar on slates. Review: you marked the subject on day 84, and on day 86 you learned that a pronoun stands in for a noun. Children mark the subject and the verb, [[s03-P97-7]], then write the noun a pronoun stands for, [[s03-P97-8]]. *15 min.* Pairs reread the page of book 2 chapter 4 where Ada and Ben read the answers (about 160 words), taking turns by paragraph, then swap, [[s03-P97-9]]. Review: you reread with a partner and a feedback card on day 2, listening for phrases and expression, not speed. Hear three pairs not heard on day 94, 3 minutes each (6 children, 9 minutes), and float for the rest. Fill the heard-six column.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 96 episodes (s03-X96) with the one note. Review: one note, one change, the first words kept. Children write the one change in the margin, [[s03-P97-10]]. Error S, no situation: add one opening sentence that names who is where when the problem opens, then reread the first lines. *2 min.* Retrieve setting. Review: you named a setting for each book on day 94. Each child says both settings from the map, [[s03-P97-11]].

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*4 min.* Read book 2 chapter 5 aloud once (about 500 words). Do not reread, copy or ask about its first paragraph; day 99 uses it. *8 min.* Pairs reread the page where Mateo chooses and Ms. Alvarez asks how Ada knew, one reading and one listening, then swap, [[s03-P97-12]]. *4 min.* **Ask.** Three pairs say the message of chapter 5, [[s03-P97-13]]. Expect: asking people what they want lets a plan work for everyone. *4 min.* Model one finished map box, the Alike box of the plot row, with a detail from each book, [[s03-K97-1]]. Say the chapter where each detail is.

#### 20 min. Writing

*8 min.* Finish the map. In the setting row and the plot row, write one likeness and one difference with a detail, [[s03-P97-14]]. *12 min.* The recount and message, on the recount and message sheet. Children write what happened in book 1 in three or four sentences, [[s03-P97-15]], and its message with two details, [[s03-P97-16]]; then the same for book 2, [[s03-P97-17]] and [[s03-P97-18]]. Last, children copy their two messages into the theme row of the map. Watch for error G, an event given as a message; if many do it, sort three statements into what happened and what it teaches (the day 95 sort). Children may use their chains from day 96. The sheets go to the teacher for written feedback at the end of the day and come back on day 99.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: conclusion. Review: on day 66 you learned that a conclusion is the closing sentence of a paragraph; at the end of a story it is the closing part where the problem is settled. Each child says what it is to a partner, [[s03-P97-19]]. Hear six.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child. Read the message you wrote for one book, [[s03-P97-20]], and ask which detail shows it, [[s03-P97-21]]. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X97v2]]. Then children answer the exit box on the student page, [[s03-P97-1v2]]. Shown for the slip: a message that fits both books, asking others helps a plan work. Shown for the exit box: a difference between the two settings in the lines. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What do both stories teach?" or "What is different about the places?" Not yet seen: an event given as the message (error G), a likeness given as a difference (error F), or a fact the books do not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the reread partner and the talk partner. One projector: show the map. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No book 2: any two stories your child knows.
**Skip.** The slates, the hearing and the margin change.

- **10 min. Words.** Say the words and the sentence in [[s03-P97-2]] and [[s03-P97-4]] one at a time. Your child writes them.
- **10 min. Map.** Your child finishes the setting row and the plot row of the map, [[s03-P97-14]].
- **10 min. Message.** Your child writes the message of one book and two details, [[s03-P97-16]].
  **Say.** "What do the two books teach that is the same?" Expect: asking questions or asking people helps a plan work.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X97v2]] on paper, alone, then [[s03-P97-1v2]] in the exit box.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the map, the recount of one book and the exit; cut the grammar and the word in context. Longer: add a sentence saying which book you would read first, and why.

### Words and supports

Theme, setting, plot, conclusion. Frames: "In both books, ___." "In book 1 ___, and in book 2 ___." "The message is ___ because ___." Audio beside print; the recount told aloud to a helper; record any help.
