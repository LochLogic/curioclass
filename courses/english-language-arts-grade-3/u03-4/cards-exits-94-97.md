# Exit cards, days 94 to 97

Strips marked From the book print lines from the unit's own books, with the chapter named, and carry no invented label. For a card marked Invented for practice, say aloud "This card is made up for practice" and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 94 (s03-X94v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 1: Ben opened the door. "It swung into the room and across the floor, and it did not stop until it hit the place where Ada had drawn the rug." Book 2, chapter 1: Ben reads the plan "line by line, from the gate to the cushions. He did not find a single mistake." Ask: How are the plots alike and different? Use but.
- **Day 95 (s03-X95v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 8: "And the door lost," said Jun gently. "But you can still sit by it." Mateo thought about this. "So losing a vote doesn't mean losing the thing?" "Not if we share," said Ada. Ask: What is the message? Give one detail.
- **Day 96 (s03-X96), Exit.** Invented for practice. The club plans a one-day book swap. Write a short episode about it. Show checking before acting and asking before acting.
- **Day 97 (s03-X97v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 11: "I think the reason is that I didn't draw it alone." Book 2, chapter 4: "Most people don't know what other people want," said Ben. "That's why you ask." Ask: What do both books teach?
- **Day 97 (s03-P97-1v2), Student page, practice.** From the book. Book 1, chapter 5: "At lunch on Thursday, the Courtyard Club met on the picnic table." Book 2, chapter 3: "That night, Ada sat at her kitchen table and made twenty-four name cards." Ask: Write one way the settings differ.

Day 96 (s03-X96) is the task card for the episode of the day: print the stem above on a half page and read it once. The children's finished episode is the exit; there is no second card.
