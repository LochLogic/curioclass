# Exit cards, days 94 to 97

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 94 (s03-X94), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Story A: In the garage, Jo wires a doorbell. The button sticks. Story B: On the porch, Jo wires a doorbell. Rain stops it." Ask: Write one sentence that says how the plots are alike and different. Use the word but.
- **Day 95 (s03-X95), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Teo wants to run every leg of the relay alone. By the last leg he is too tired and drops the baton. Next race he lets each friend run one leg, and they win." Ask: Write one sentence that gives the message and one detail that shows it.
- **Day 96 (s03-X96), Exit.** Invented for practice. The club plans a one-day book swap. Write a short episode about it. Show checking before acting and asking before acting.
- **Day 97 (s03-X97), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Story A: In the gym, Owen forgot his shoes and sat out. After that he packed his bag the night before. Story B: In the yard, Owen forgot his hat and got too hot. After that he packed his bag the night before." Ask: Write one sentence that tells what both stories teach.
- **Day 97 (s03-P97-1), Student page, practice.** Invented for practice. Card: "Story A: Dee waits at the bus stop in the rain. She reads under an umbrella. Story B: Dee waits at the train stop in the sun. She reads on a bench." Ask: Write one way the two settings differ.

Day 96 (s03-X96) is the task card for the episode of the day: print the stem above on a half page and read it once. The children's finished episode is the exit; there is no second card.
