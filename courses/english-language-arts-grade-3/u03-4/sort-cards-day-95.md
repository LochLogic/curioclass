# Event or message sort cards (day 95)

Print one set for each pair, cut apart. Six cards about one pet in a classroom. Pairs sort each card into "what happened" or "what it teaches". Print "invented for practice" at the foot. Answers: cards 1, 3 and 5 are events; cards 2, 4 and 6 are messages.

1. The class fish was put in a new bowl on Monday.
2. A pet needs care that does not wait for someone to notice.
3. Two children took turns filling the water.
4. When many people share a job, a plan keeps it fair.
5. The fish swam to the front of the bowl.
6. A small job done each day matters.
