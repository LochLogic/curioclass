# Day 95: Chapter 3 of book 2: tell what happened from what it teaches

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child and one set of sort cards for each pair, cut apart. Hand back the day 94 slips with error F marked. Mark the book 2 chapter 3 page where Ben asks about the quiet readers (from Ben's question to "Ask him," said Ada). Pick the four conference children and the two children for the log line. Keep the draft pages ready to collect.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say discover, decided and unhappy. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P95-1]]. Then very and heart, [[s03-P95-2]]. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P95-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P95-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i. *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk, the other says what carefully means, then they swap, [[s03-P95-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AA, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P95-6]]. Review: you filled this line on day 93. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each). Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*2 min.* Error F, from the day 94 slips (s03-X94v2): a likeness with no difference. Children marked F add a difference to their sentence, joined with but. *3 min.* Retrieve event and message. Review: on day 5 the story map with a message box kept what happened apart from what it teaches. Pairs sort the six sort cards into what happened and what it teaches, [[s03-P95-7]].

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*6 min.* Read book 2 chapter 3 aloud (about 640 words, 5 minutes, then 1 minute for one stop). **Ask.** After "I should have done that from the start": what will Ada do now? Expect: ask each reader where they want to sit. *8 min.* Pairs reread the page where Ben asks about the quiet readers (about 150 words), taking turns by paragraph, then swap, [[s03-P95-8]]. *6 min.* Model. Review: a message is what the actions and results show, which you learned on day 5; an event is only what happened. Show an event beside a message, [[s03-K95-1]], and two details that show the message, [[s03-K95-2]]. Then say that a theme is a message that comes back in more than one book, and show book 1 beside chapter 3, [[s03-K95-3]]. Write what happened in one box and what it teaches in another; the message could fit a different story.

#### 20 min. Writing

*15 min.* One episode, alone, in one sitting, [[s03-P95-9]]. Plan card 3 minutes, draft 10, reread 2. Review: you made a plan card on day 42, with the situation, events in order and an ending; you put time words in your tale on day 41; you put the comma inside the quotation marks on day 44. Children write invented at the top. The episodes go to the teacher tonight and come back on day 96 with one note. If many children open with talk and no situation, show one opening sentence naming who is where (error S). *5 min.* Children write two details from chapter 3 that show a message, [[s03-P95-10]], then the message they show, [[s03-P95-11]]. Watch for error G, an event given as the message.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: message. Review: you learned message on day 5. Pairs tell each other how a message differs from an event, [[s03-P95-12]]. Hear six.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child. Say the message that the details of chapter 3 give, [[s03-P95-13]], then how book 1 gives the same one, [[s03-P95-14]]. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X95v2]]. Shown: a message that could fit another story and one detail that shows it. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the detail show beyond itself?" Not yet seen: an event given as the message (error G), or a fact the chapter does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the reread partner and the talk partner, and sorts the cards beside the child. One projector: show the map. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No book 2: any story your child knows.
**Skip.** The slates, the conferences, the sort and the episode.

- **10 min. Words.** Say the words and the sentence in [[s03-P95-1]] and [[s03-P95-3]] one at a time. Your child writes them.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and fills the line in [[s03-P95-6]].
- **10 min. Message.** Your child writes two details and the message they show, [[s03-P95-10]] and [[s03-P95-11]].
  **Say.** "What does chapter 3 teach that could fit another story?" Expect: asking people what they need helps a plan fit them.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X95v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the model and the exit; cut the sort and the word in context. Longer: write the message of book 1 under the message of chapter 3 on the map, theme row.

### Words and supports

Event, message, theme. Frames: "The message is ___ because ___." "In chapter 3, ___ shows it." Audio beside print; the sort cards read aloud by a helper; record any help.

### Tomorrow

It hands back the day 95 episode, [[s03-P95-9]], with one note and one change, then reads book 2 chapter 4 and sets the events of the two books in chains. The word work, [[s03-P95-1]] to [[s03-P95-5]], the log work, [[s03-P95-6]], the spoken work, [[s03-P95-4]], [[s03-P95-7]], [[s03-P95-8]], [[s03-P95-12]] to [[s03-P95-14]], [[s03-K95-1]], [[s03-K95-2]] and [[s03-K95-3]], and the message lines, [[s03-P95-10]] and [[s03-P95-11]], stay in the folder.
