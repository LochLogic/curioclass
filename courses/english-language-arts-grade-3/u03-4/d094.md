# Day 94: Chapter 2 of book 2: compare the setting and the plot with book 1

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a two-book map and a two-book opening chart for each child. Hand back the day 93 slips with error F marked. Mark two pages: the book 1 door page (chapter 4, from "May I just try something?" to "somewhat muffled", about 170 words) and the book 2 argument page (chapter 2, from "Priya walked to the best chair" to "It won't be me," said Mateo, about 220 words). Pick the three pairs to hear.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say difference, interest and favorite. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P94-1]]. Then move and great, [[s03-P94-2]]. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P94-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P94-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i. *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk, the other says what wonderful means, then they swap, [[s03-P94-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AA, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

Partner-rereading day. *5 min.* Grammar on slates. Review: you marked the subject on day 84 and matched the verb to it on day 88. Write the sentence on the board; children mark the subject and the verb, [[s03-P94-6]]. Then a second sentence on the board; children choose the verb that matches, [[s03-P94-7]]. *15 min.* Pairs reread the page of book 2 chapter 1 where Ada says yes to Ben (about 140 words), taking turns by paragraph, then swap, [[s03-P94-8]]. Review: you reread with a partner and a feedback card on day 2, listening for phrases and expression, not speed. The listener marks the card. Hear three pairs, 3 minutes each (6 children, 9 minutes), and float for the rest. Fill the heard-six column.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*2 min.* Error F, from the day 93 slips (s03-X93): a likeness with no difference. Children marked F add a difference to their sentence, joined with but. Review: you said Both ___. But ___. in grade 2. *3 min.* Retrieve setting. Review: setting is where and when a story happens; you learned it in kindergarten and used it in grade 2. Pairs name one setting detail from book 1, [[s03-P94-9]].

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*6 min.* Read book 2 chapter 2 aloud (about 600 words, 5 minutes, then 1 minute for questions about words). *8 min.* Pairs reread the argument page of chapter 2 with the book 1 door page beside it, one child reading each, then swap, [[s03-P94-10]]. *3 min.* **Ask.** Three pairs say it in one sentence, [[s03-P94-11]]: what is the new problem? Expect: two readers want the same chair. *3 min.* Write the two problems side by side on the board, one line each, and read them once.

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Say that the plot is what happens in a story: its events and its problem. Model the setting row and the plot row on a copy of the map, [[s03-K94-1]] and [[s03-K94-2]]. Review: the story map with a message box on day 5 gave each part of a story a box; the two-book map gives each book a box. Say each alike box and each different box as its own line, and name the chapter where the detail is. *10 min.* Children fill the setting row and the plot row on their own map, with the day 93 chart and both books open, [[s03-P94-12]]. Watch for error A, a detail the page does not give; repair with a finger on the page. *5 min.* Children write two separate lines: one sentence for how the problems are alike, [[s03-P94-13]], and one for how they differ, [[s03-P94-14]].

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: difference. Say that it names how two things are not the same. Review: you learned similar and different in grade 2. Each child says a sentence about the two problems to a partner, [[s03-P94-15]]. Hear six.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child. Tell one way the two settings are alike, [[s03-P94-16]], then one way they differ, [[s03-P94-17]]. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X94]]. Shown: one likeness and one difference, in one sentence with but. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What is the same? What is different?" Not yet seen: a likeness only (error F), or a detail the card does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the reread partner and the talk partner. One projector: show the map. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No book 2: any two stories your child knows.
**Skip.** The slates, the hearing and the repair.

- **10 min. Words.** Say the words and the sentence in [[s03-P94-1]] and [[s03-P94-3]] one at a time. Your child writes them.
- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads chapter 2 of book 2 with you, taking turns by paragraph.
- **10 min. Map.** Your child fills the setting row and the plot row, [[s03-P94-12]].
  **Say.** "How are the two problems alike, and how do they differ?" Expect: both are plans that meet a problem; one is a wrong drawing and one is two readers wanting a chair.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X94]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the map and the exit; cut the grammar and the word in context. Longer: add the third row, theme, for one book only, as a guess to check on day 95.

### Words and supports

Setting, plot, alike, different, difference. Frames: "In book 1 the problem is ___." "In book 2 the problem is ___." Audio beside print; the two problems read aloud by a helper; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 95 begins with the day 94 exit, s03-X94, and repairs error F, a likeness with no difference, then reads book 2 chapter 3 and tells what happened from what it teaches. The word work, [[s03-P94-1]] to [[s03-P94-5]], the grammar, [[s03-P94-6]] and [[s03-P94-7]], the spoken work, [[s03-P94-4]], [[s03-P94-8]] to [[s03-P94-11]], [[s03-P94-15]] to [[s03-P94-17]], [[s03-K94-1]] and [[s03-K94-2]], and the map and its lines, [[s03-P94-12]] to [[s03-P94-14]], stay in the folder.
