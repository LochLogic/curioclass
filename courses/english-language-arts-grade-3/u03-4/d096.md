# Day 96: Chapter 4 of book 2: link the events of the two books, then write an episode

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one note on each day 95 episode the night before. Print the student page for each child. Print the day 96 card as a half slip for each child and keep it face down until the exit. Mark the book 2 chapter 4 page where Ada asks each reader where to sit (from "Where would you like to sit?" to "warm grass on her toes", about 150 words). Pick the three conference children and the one child for the log line.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say example, organized and finally. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P96-1]]. Then answer, [[s03-P96-2]]. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P96-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P96-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i. *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence chunk by chunk, the other says what curious means, then they swap, [[s03-P96-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AA, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 15 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P96-6]]. Three 4-minute conferences (12 minutes, three children, one record each). One child reads a log line aloud (3 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*2 min.* Hand back the day 95 episodes. Review: you used one note, one change and the first words kept on days 86 and 93. Children read the note and write the one change in the margin, [[s03-P96-7]]. *1 min.* Error G, from the day 95 slips (s03-X95v2): an event given as the message. Ask what the detail shows beyond itself, then say the message. *2 min.* Retrieve the plan card. Review: you made a plan card for a tale on day 42. Pairs name its parts for an episode, [[s03-P96-8]].

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*5 min.* Read book 2 chapter 4 aloud (about 530 words, 4 minutes, then 1 minute for questions about words). *8 min.* Pairs reread the page where Ada asks each reader where to sit (about 150 words), taking turns by paragraph, then swap, [[s03-P96-9]]. *4 min.* **Ask.** Three pairs name one answer that changed Ada's plan, [[s03-P96-10]]. Expect: fourteen readers wanted the shade, so Ada moved nearly everyone under the tree. *3 min.* Show the first link of each chain, one event and the event it leads to, [[s03-K96-1]]. Say that a link is one event leading to the next.

#### 20 min. Writing

*10 min.* Linked events. Children write four boxes for each book, in order, on Side 2 of the student page, [[s03-P96-11]] and [[s03-P96-12]]. Then they mark one link that is alike in the two chains, [[s03-P96-13]], and one that is different, [[s03-P96-14]]. Clip the page to the map. Watch for error B, an event put before its cause. *10 min.* Dialogue. Review: you put the comma inside the quotation marks on day 44. Children write four lines of talk between two readers who cannot find a ball, [[s03-P96-18]], on a draft page, naming the speaker and using the marks. This practice is not the exit.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: example. Say that it is one case that shows what you mean. Each child says a sentence about a problem in the books, [[s03-P96-15]]. Hear six.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child. Read your four lines of talk, [[s03-P96-16]], then say which words show who speaks, [[s03-P96-17]]. Any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Exit

*8 min.* Hand out the half slip and read it aloud twice: a short episode in the series world, [[s03-X96]], labelled invented. Plan card 2 minutes, draft 6. Review: the situation, the events in order, dialogue with its quotation marks and comma, time words and an ending. Shown: a situation with who is where, checking and asking both before the action, dialogue with its marks, time words in order and a closing that settles the problem. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Who is where when the problem begins?" Not yet seen: no situation (error S), time words in the wrong order (error U), or an unrelated problem opened at the end (error V). Collect; one note comes back on day 97. *2 min.* Then children answer the exit box on the student page alone, [[s03-P96-19v2]]. Shown for the box: a link in both books, Jun is asked and the plan changes. Not yet seen: a link from one book only, or a person the books do not give (error A). Record both on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the reread partner and the talk partner. One projector: show the map and the chains. The episode stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No book 2: any two stories your child knows.
**Skip.** The slates, the conferences and the margin change.

- **10 min. Words.** Say the words and the sentence in [[s03-P96-1]] and [[s03-P96-3]] one at a time. Your child writes them.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and fills the line in [[s03-P96-6]].
- **10 min. Chains.** Your child writes four events for each book, [[s03-P96-11]] and [[s03-P96-12]].
  **Say.** "Where do the two chains match?" Expect: someone speaks and Ada changes her plan in both.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child writes the episode on the half slip, [[s03-X96]], alone, then [[s03-P96-19v2]] in the exit box.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chains and the exit; cut the dialogue and the word in context. Longer: add a fifth box to each chain.

### Words and supports

Link, example, situation. Frames: "First, ___. Then, ___." "In book 2, ___ leads to ___." Audio beside print; the plan card read aloud; record any help.

### Tomorrow

It reads book 2 chapter 5 and finishes both maps for theme, setting and plot. The word work, [[s03-P96-1]] to [[s03-P96-5]], the log work, [[s03-P96-6]], the margin change, [[s03-P96-7]], the spoken work, [[s03-P96-4]], [[s03-P96-8]] to [[s03-P96-10]], [[s03-P96-15]] to [[s03-P96-17]] and [[s03-K96-1]], the dialogue, [[s03-P96-18]], and the chains, [[s03-P96-11]] to [[s03-P96-14]], stay in the folder.
