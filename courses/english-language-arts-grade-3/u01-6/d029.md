# Day 29: Repair the explanation and set a reading goal

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the feedback card, day 29, the goal card, day 29, the strategy menu, day 29, and the repair cards, day 29. Mark each child's commonest letter on the day 28 results. Find the day 29 exit card. List six word-record children for rotation 4 and a group of four for each repair.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

Review: on day 25 you named a target from your paragraph feedback. Each child names that target and shows where they used it in the day 28 explanation, in the folder. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* First 2 minutes: Review: -ful full of and -less without. Say careful, helpless, thankful, useless; children write each on a slate. Then hear one child a minute for the word record, rotation 4 of 6, with the word check; the rest write each word in a sentence. This fits with no slack; if a turn runs over, finish that child on day 30.

*5 min. Grammar.* Review: join two facts with and, also or but. Children write two facts about the boat library and join them.

*7 min. Partner rereading.* Review: partner rereading with the feedback card. Pairs reread page 3 of "The Boat Book Day" and the first two sentences of page 4 (88 words, about 45 seconds), twice each, and tick the card. You listen to two pairs.

#### 30 min. Return and repair

*5 min. Return.* Hand back the day 27 exit (s01-X27) and practice pages (s01-P27-1, s01-P27-2, s01-P27-3, s01-P27-4). If many chose line two, or gave no reason (error A), say: "Which line says I, and what does that show?" (3 minutes). Return day 28's assessment (s01-A27 to s01-A32) and the exit (s01-X28) with the notes on the explanation; each child marks one letter (2 minutes).

*20 min. Groups by letter.* Four groups: reading (errors A and B), grouping (error H), links and closing (errors I and J), and a group that met every look-for. Lead the largest group for 15 minutes; the others work in pairs from the repair cards. Give the reading group 8 minutes first: a topic word is turned into a question and an answer from the words, as on the day 28 exit (error B). Each child repairs one sentence of their explanation with [[s01-P29-1]] and keeps the first form below it. The group that met every look-for writes a second group of facts with its own heading.

*5 min. Fresh card.* Each child reads the fresh card for their repair and writes one sentence with [[s01-P29-2]].

#### 20 min. Strategy and goal

*8 min. Model.* Review: you met strategy in grade 2, day 82. New here: a strategy is a move that helps you read. Show the five on the strategy menu. Use [[s01-K29-1]] and [[s01-K29-2]] to match a detail to a strategy (2 minutes each). Then show one hard sentence from page 6 of "The Boat Book Day", reread it slowly and say which strategy you chose and where. *12 min. Goal card.* Each child reads two records, the notes on their explanation and your reading note, and fills the goal card with [[s01-P29-3]]. Frame: "My strategy is ___. I will use it when ___. My evidence is ___."

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs: each child 2 minutes to tell the goal and the evidence; 1 minute to move. **Ask.** "What is your strategy, and what in your work shows you need it?" Expect: one strategy and one record that shows it.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 29 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X29]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on slates: [[s01-X29b]]. Unscored. Shown: says faster does not help and names a move. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What would make the sentence sound like a question?" Not yet seen: calls the goal a good one, or says faster would help (error X).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult returns the results and works from the repair cards alone. No printer: write the five strategies on a card. A child may speak the goal card and the adult writes it; record the support.

### At home

**Swap.** No card: say the goal aloud.
**Skip.** The grammar.

- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you their goal and one place they will use it, [[s01-P29-3]].
  **Say.** "What shows you need it?" Expect: a record of their own work.
- **10 min. Repair.** Your child reads the repaired sentence aloud and says what it fixed.
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X29]] and [[s01-X29b]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the grammar and the second pair listening; keep the repair, the goal card and the exit. Longer: the group that met every look-for reads their second group aloud to a partner.

### Words and supports

Strategy, goal, evidence, repair, record. Frames: "My strategy is ___." "The record shows ___." Large print; the repair card read aloud; a scribe for the goal card, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X29, s01-X29b). Error X is a goal with no move: a goal needs a move, so let the voice rise at a question mark. The goal card goes to day 30's opening. The repaired explanation stays in the folder.
