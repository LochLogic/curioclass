# Day 27: Tell Washington's own words from the retelling

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the voice and retelling chart, day 27, the dictionary page, day 27, and the feedback card. Mark each child's day 26 exit for hand-back. Find the day 27 exit card and keep it. List six word-record children for rotation 2.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair.* Hand back the day 26 exit (s01-X26v2, s01-X26b) and the practice pages (s01-P26-1, s01-P26-2, s01-P26-3, s01-P26-4) with one comment each. If many said only that he wanted to learn (error W), point to the context line and ask: "What does it say that the sentence does not?" Expect: freedom had come, so learning was allowed.

*7 min. Retrieval.* Review: on day 26 you wrote a main idea and read context. From memory, each child writes the main idea and one context fact on a slate, then checks both against the day 26 chart. Unscored.

#### 20 min. Skills

*8 min. Words.* First 2 minutes: Review: -ful full of and -less without. Say playful, thankful, useful, useless; children write each and say its meaning. Then hear one child a minute for the word record, rotation 2 of 4, with the word check; the rest spell the four words and use two in sentences. This fits with no slack: if a turn runs over, the sixth child is heard first on day 28.

*5 min. Grammar.* Review: join two facts with a link word, and or also. New here: but joins two facts that pull against each other. Say "Booker could not go in the day, but he found a teacher at night." Children join two facts of their own with and, then with but.

*7 min. Partner rereading.* Review: partner rereading with the feedback card, for phrases and expression, never speed. Pairs reread the span from "Booker wanted more than anything" to "read the book eagerly" (92 words, about 45 seconds), twice each, and tick the card. You listen to two pairs, 3.5 minutes each.

#### 20 min. Text and model

*4 min.* Model with [[s01-K27-1]]. A line is Washington's own account if it says I and sits in quotation marks. Say once: "Account" means his own telling of events here. It is not a bank, and it is not a story told by someone else. *4 min.* [[s01-K27-2]]: Review: a sentence clue tells a word's meaning. *4 min.* [[s01-K27-3]]: Review: literal means said exactly; nonliteral means meant another way. *4 min.* [[s01-K27-4]]: Review: guide words show which words are on a page. *4 min.* [[s01-K27-5]]: Review: a known word inside a longer word is a clue, as care is in careful.

#### 25 min. Hand work

*8 min.* Pairs do [[s01-P27-1]] on the voice and retelling chart. *7 min.* Each child fills the table in [[s01-P27-2]]. *4 min.* Each child does [[s01-P27-3]] on the dictionary page. *6 min.* Each child writes [[s01-P27-4]].

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs: each child 2 minutes on which words are Washington's and how they know. **Ask.** "How can a reader tell Washington's own words from the retelling?" Expect: the word I and the quotation marks. *6 min.* Make the class chart "How can a reader tell?" with five children giving one clue each, 1 minute each, and 1 minute to write it up.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 27 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X27v2]]. Unscored. Shown: picks line one and says it says I. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "Which line says I?" Not yet seen: line two, or no reason (error A).

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the retelling aloud and marks W and R together. No printer: copy the dictionary page by hand, six entries. A child may speak an answer and the adult writes it; record the support.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: underline the quoted lines in the text with a pencil.
**Skip.** The dictionary page.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the annotated retelling aloud, saying the quoted lines in a different voice.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child shows how to tell Washington's own words, [[s01-P27-1]].
  **Say.** "How can you tell?" Expect: it says I and has quotation marks.
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X27v2]] alone, from the card the teacher sends home.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the grammar and one word strategy; keep the W and R marks, the word notes and the exit. Longer: a child finds a second word in the dictionary page and reads its entry aloud.

### Words and supports

Account, quotation marks, devour, determined, alphabet, freedom. Frames: "It says I, so ___." "It means ___ here." Large print; the retelling read aloud; a scribe for the table, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 is a check day with no opening. The day 27 exit (s01-X27v2) and practice pages (s01-P27-1, s01-P27-2, s01-P27-3, s01-P27-4) go to day 29's return and repair. Error A is choosing the retelling or giving no reason: say how the I voice shows his own words.
