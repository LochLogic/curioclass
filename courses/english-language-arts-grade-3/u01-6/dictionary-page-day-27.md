# Dictionary page (day 27)

A printed page for each pair. Guide words at the top: **alone** and **also**. Entries run in order.

- **alone** (adjective): with no one else.
- **along** (preposition): from one end to the other of something.
- **aloud** (adverb): in a voice that can be heard.
- **alphabet** (noun): the letters of a language, in their set order.
- **already** (adverb): before now.
- **also** (adverb): too; in addition.

Say aloud: "The two words at the top tell you which words are on the page. Say the letters in order to get to the word."

Print the guide words large. A child who cannot read the page may have it read aloud.
