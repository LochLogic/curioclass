# Strategy menu (day 29)

A wall chart and a copy for each child. Five strategies, each with a detail from the boat story and one from the bus story.

| Strategy | In the boat story | In the bus story |
|---|---|---|
| Get ready before | Lia sets her library book by the door the night before. | Sam checks the bus card and wraps his book in a bag. |
| Take small steps | Lia walks the boards one slow step at a time. | Sam walks to one wall, then the next. |
| Read slowly and reread | Lia reads the long sentence again, slowly, and pauses at the comma. | Sam reads the first chapter slowly. |
| Ask and listen | Lia asks Cami which birds she likes, then listens. | Sam asks Mrs. Patel for book two. |
| Mark where to begin | Lia tucks a new date card inside her book. | Sam crosses off a day on the bus card. |

Say aloud: "A strategy is a move that helps. A goal names the move and the place to use it."
