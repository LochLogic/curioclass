# Context bridge cards and picture timeline (day 26)

Eight minutes at the start of day 26, before the retelling. Read the context text aloud once. Then children place four cards on the timeline in order. The teacher names slavery and freedom plainly, says "enslaved people" and does not ask what it was like or for family stories.

## Four cards (one fact each)

1. Booker was born in 1856 on a farm in Virginia.
2. In April 1865 he heard he was free.
3. His family moved to Malden, West Virginia.
4. In 1872 he set out for a school called Hampton Institute.

## The picture timeline

A straight line with four marks in order: 1856, 1865, a mark for the move to Malden, and 1872. Each mark has a box for one card. A child may say the order aloud instead of placing the cards.

## Words said aloud once

- **enslaved:** forced to work without pay and not free to leave.
- **emancipation:** being set free.
