# Repair cards (day 29)

Five small cards. Each child takes the card for the commonest letter on their day 28 results and repairs one sentence of their explanation.

- **Reading (A, B).** My sentence says what the panel says. I put a finger on the words first. My main idea is a whole sentence, not a topic word.
- **Grouping (H).** My facts about one thing are together. I can name the group in a few words.
- **Links (I).** My link word joins two facts about the same thing. I read both facts aloud to check.
- **Closing (J).** My last sentence is about the same topic. It says what the facts show.
- **Met every look-for.** I write a second group of facts for my explanation, with its own heading.

Write the repaired sentence below its first form. Keep both in the folder.

## Fresh cards (one for each repair, used in the last five minutes)

Each card is invented for practice. The child writes one sentence that meets the repair card.

- **Reading (A, B).** Read: "Omar's aunt kept the key on a hook by the door. At night she hung her coat over it." Write the main idea as one whole sentence.
- **Grouping (H).** Notes: "The shelf holds jars." "The rug is red." "The shelf holds tins." Write the two shelf notes together as one sentence.
- **Links (I).** Facts: "Rosa folds the cloths." "Rosa stacks the bowls." Join them with a link word that fits.
- **Closing (J).** Read: "The class waters the beans. Also, they measure each stem." Write a closing sentence for the paragraph.
- **Met every look-for.** Write a second group of facts for your own explanation, with its own heading.
