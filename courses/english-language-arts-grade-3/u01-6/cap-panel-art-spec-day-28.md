# Art spec: day 28 cap panel (text g3-u1-d28)

- **Picture.** An original plain line drawing of a school door with a row of pegs inside. A few simple caps and hats hang on the pegs. One peg is empty. No faces, no people shown in detail.
- **Image description.** For a child who cannot see it: "A school doorway with pegs on the wall. Most pegs hold a hat or a cap. One peg is empty."
- **Rule.** The picture adds nothing the panel does not say and does not show a cap being made. The panel is read twice, alone, on day 28 only. Do not print "invented for practice": the panel retells a real event in our words.
