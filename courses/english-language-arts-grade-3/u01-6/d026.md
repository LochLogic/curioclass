# Day 26: Read about Booker and the world he lived in

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, both texts, the context and what Booker did chart, day 26, for each pair, and the context bridge cards and timeline, day 26. Find the day 26 exit card and keep it. Read the context text yourself first. List two conference children and six word-record children.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening

*3 min. Repair.* Hand back the day 25 exit (s01-X25, s01-X25b) with one comment each. If many read a bar without its label (error Z), show the graph and ask: "What does each label say?" Expect: the label first, then the bar.

*7 min. Context bridge.* Read "Before Booker Learned to Read" aloud once (about 1.5 minutes). Say plainly: enslaved means forced to work without pay and not free to leave; emancipation means being set free. Then pairs place the four bridge cards on the timeline in order (3.5 minutes); a child may say the order aloud. Name slavery and freedom plainly and do not ask what it was like or for anyone's family. About 1 minute of slack.

#### 20 min. Skills

*3 min. Retrieval.* Review: in section 1.4 you found a main idea, one whole sentence, and details that support it, on the main-idea chart. Each child says the main idea of "Books on Horseback" in one sentence. Unscored.

*10 min. Words.* First 4 minutes: Review: -ful means full of and -less means without, taught earlier on care and help. Write on slates: careful, careless, helpful, helpless. Say each meaning. Then enslaved and determined: say each, clap its parts, mark the part that is odd (the -ed that sounds like d). Last 6 minutes: hear one child a minute for the word record, rotation 1 of 4, with the word check (day 30 card); the rest write the four -ful and -less words in a sentence each. This fits with no slack: if a turn runs over, the sixth child is heard first on day 27.

*7 min. Reading conferences.* Two children from the list, 3.5 minutes each. Write them in the heard-six column. The rest read from their logs.

#### 20 min. Text and model

*6 min. Read.* Read "How Booker T. Washington Learned to Read" aloud while children follow (about 3 minutes). Then preview devour and determined in the text (3 minutes). Devour is explained in its paragraph; determined is in the last paragraph. Account waits for day 27.

*14 min. Model.* On the chart, model the two columns. Review: a main idea is a whole sentence the details support. Use [[s01-K26-1]] to sort one sentence (4 minutes), [[s01-K26-2]] to choose the main idea (5 minutes) and [[s01-K26-3]] to find the sentence for the first detail (5 minutes). Say what you do: ask who did something, Booker or the world he lived in.

#### 25 min. Hand work

*5 min.* Each child rereads the retelling silently and does [[s01-P26-1]]. *12 min.* Pairs complete the chart with [[s01-P26-2]]; the first detail is already marked from the model. *8 min.* Each child does [[s01-P26-4]] (2 minutes), then writes [[s01-P26-3]] (6 minutes).

#### 10 min. Talk

*4 min.* Pairs: each child 2 minutes on how the details support the main idea. **Ask.** "How does the context text help you understand what Booker wanted?" Expect: a fact from the context, then what he wanted. Frame: "The words ___ explain ___." *6 min.* Six volunteers share, 50 seconds each, with 1 minute slack.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the day 26 card aloud twice. Children write alone: [[s01-X26v2]]. Then say aloud, and children answer on slates: [[s01-X26b]]. Unscored. Shown: uses the context and the sentence and adds no fact. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the context line say about freedom?" Not yet seen: only "he wanted to learn" (error W), or a teacher or school neither gives (error A). For the spoken question, shown: says the number 18.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads both texts aloud, then the chart is filled together. No printer: copy the four bridge cards by hand. A child may speak a chart answer and the adult writes it; record the support.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: say each detail and its sentence aloud.
**Skip.** The timeline.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child rereads the first two paragraphs of the retelling aloud.
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you one detail and the sentence it comes from, [[s01-P26-1]]. Do not ask about family history.
  **Say.** "Which sentence says that?" Expect: the whole sentence, read aloud.
- **10 min. Read.** Read any book together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X26v2]] and [[s01-X26b]] alone, from the card the teacher sends home.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the word-record turns to the six listed and one conference; keep the bridge, the model, the chart and the exit. Longer: pairs add a fourth detail to the chart with its sentence.

### Words and supports

Enslaved, emancipation, freedom, determined, context, detail. Frames: "The words ___ explain ___." "The main idea is ___." Large print; both texts read aloud; the chart filled by speaking, with a scribe, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 opens with 3 minutes on this exit (s01-X26v2, s01-X26b). Error W is saying only what he wanted: use the context line with the sentence to explain the event. Day 27 rereads Washington's own account and marks his words.
