# Day 28: Check on the cap panel and the explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the cap panel picture, day 28, and the reading group lines, day 28. Find the day 28 exit card. Set out lined paper and the folders. List six word-record children for rotation 3. The day 27 exit and practice pages stay with you and go back on day 29. Teach nothing today.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, silent

Ten minutes on slates, silent: say each word once and children write it. useful, endless, preview, misspell, unwrap, rebuild, thankful, helpless, prewash, misread. Twenty minutes: each child reads from their log; six children are heard at one minute each for the word record, rotation 3 of 4, with the word check. No teaching.

#### 8 min. Panel reading

Each child reads the cap panel alone, silently, twice. Say nothing about it.

#### 12 min. Items

Children work alone, in writing. Give no hint. Record each child: [[s01-A27]] (6 minutes, RI.3.1) and [[s01-A28]] (6 minutes, RI.3.2). Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and write down any prompt.

#### 25 min. Explanation

Silent. Twenty minutes: each child writes the explanation for [[s01-A29]] in one sitting, with a topic, grouped facts, one definition, one link and a closing sentence. Unscored practice. After class, read it for [[s01-A30]], [[s01-A31]] and [[s01-A32]]. Five minutes: children write alone [[s01-X28v2]], from the day 28 card, read aloud twice. Unscored. Shown: a question about what he wanted, answered by the day-school. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the sentence say he wanted?" Not yet seen: a topic word only, "school" (error B), or a fact the sentence does not give (error A).

#### 10 min. Reading groups

Six groups of five. Each child reads a two-sentence card twice (about 30 words, 15 seconds) and gets one comment on phrasing from the group, about 80 seconds each, 7 minutes for five. Allow 3 minutes to move and start. Sit with one group and record phrasing. Unscored; day 35 holds the unit fluency record.

#### 5 min. Collection

Collect the items, the explanations and the exits.

### Other rooms

One child: a quiet half hour; the adult reads each item aloud only where reading is not the evidence, records support and returns notes next day. No printer: copy the panel by hand. No groups of five: read to the adult twice.

### At home

**Swap.** No panel: any short paragraph, read twice.
**Skip.** The slate words.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the panel and answers [[s01-A27]] alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s01-X28v2]] alone.
- **20 min. Read.** Read with an adult.
  **Say.** "What does your book explain?" Expect: one sentence on what it explains.
- **10 min. Write.** Add a line to your reading log.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the dictation to six words. Longer: a child reads a second short paragraph and gives its main idea.

### Words and supports

Panel, explanation, group, link. Large print; items read aloud except where reading is the evidence; a scribe for the answers, noted. Record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 29 returns day 28's assessment (s01-A27 to s01-A32) and the exit (s01-X28v2), with the notes on the explanation. Error B is a topic word for a main idea: turn it into a question and an answer from the words. Repairs go to the next teaching lesson, never during the check.
