# Goal card (day 29)

A card each child fills in from two records of their own work: a day 28 reading note and the feedback card.

- **My strategy:** (choose one: get ready before, take small steps, read slowly and reread, ask and listen, mark where to begin)
- **When I will use it:** ______________________
- **My evidence (one record that shows I need it):** ______________________
- **My goal for the next two weeks:** ______________________

The card goes to day 30's opening. A child may speak the card and the teacher writes it.
