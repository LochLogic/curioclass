# Exit cards, days 26 to 29

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain print on white. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children write. Answers are in items.csv. The sentences on the cards for days 26 to 28 are Washington's own, kept out of every shared page, so the cards are held by the teacher until the exit.

- **Day 26 (s01-X26).** Context line and one sentence from his own book, with the word longing glossed as a strong wish. Ask: Use the context and the sentence to explain why he could begin to read. Then say aloud (s01-X26b): In the retelling, what was the first thing Booker learned at the furnace?
- **Day 27 (s01-X27).** Two lines, one his own and one a retelling. Ask: Explain which line is Washington's own account.
- **Day 28 (s01-X28).** One sentence from his own book, with set upon glossed as determined about. Ask: Write one question about what Washington wanted that this sentence answers.
- **Day 29 (s01-X29).** Read: "Mina read Where is the key? in a flat voice. Her partner said it did not sound like a question. Mina wrote this goal: I will read faster." Ask: Explain why this goal does not help Mina. Then say aloud (s01-X29b): Mina has twenty pages to read by Friday and feels she cannot. Which strategy on the menu fits what Mina needs?

The wording of every card is the wording in items.csv; copy it from there when printing.
