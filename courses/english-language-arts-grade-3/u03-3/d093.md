# Day 93: Use the notes, then compare the openings of book 1 and book 2

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one note on each day 92 packet the night before. Print the student page, a two-book opening chart and a one-note, one-change card for each child. Draw a chart of two columns on the board, headed alike and different. Keep the day 91 and day 92 exit slips for the repair part. Mark two places: the first two paragraphs of book 1 chapter 1 (about 90 words) and the first two paragraphs of book 2 chapter 1 (about 63 words). Pick the two children for the log line.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

Three prompts on paper. *3 min.* Say recommend, underline and happily. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P93-1]]. Then build and believe, [[s03-P93-2]]. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81 and again on day 91. *3 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P93-3]], in phrases, twice; children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P93-5]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i. *4 min.* In pairs, one child reads the sentence from book 2 chapter 1 chunk by chunk, the other says what remembered means, then they swap, [[s03-P93-4]]. Check the meaning after decoding. Watch for error AA, a word guessed from its first letters.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P93-6]]. Four 4-minute conferences (16 minutes, four children, one record each). Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes). Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Returned feedback

Hand back the day 92 packets with the written note on each, from day 92's items, s03-A6 to s03-A9. Children read the note and mark the one change they will make on that answer, [[s03-P93-7]]. Error AC is an answer that repeats what the passage says without explaining it. The packets are scored once, from the work handed in; the revision is not scored again.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

*3 min.* Error B, from the day 91 slips (s03-X91v2): the move put before its cause. Lay the day 91 slip lines in order, number the first, then say the link again. Then the chapter 7 table, [[s03-P93-9v2]]. *2 min.* Error C, from the day 92 slips (s03-X92v2): put one wrong answer beside its correction, Ada is good beside Ada put in two chairs, so Nia and Sam read side by side. Ask what the second says that the first does not: an action and what it caused. Then the chapter 8 labels, [[s03-P93-8v2]]. *4 min.* Two groups of 2 minutes each, by error letter on the packets (the two commonest of error A, error E, one person's view given to another, and error AC), while everyone else applies the same move to their own answer in pairs. *1 min.* One pair says the move they used.

#### 25 min. Reading and reread

*2 min.* Retrieve comparing: pairs name two ways a pencil and a marker are alike and two ways they differ, [[s03-P93-11]]; write them on the chart. Review: you compared two things in grade 2, alike and different. *6 min.* Read book 2 chapter 1 aloud (about 580 words, 5 minutes, then 1 minute for one stop). **Ask.** After "she did not say no": what does Ada do that she would not have done a few weeks ago? Expect: she says yes to Ben's help. *8 min.* Pairs reread the opening of book 1 chapter 1 and the opening of book 2 chapter 1, one reading and one listening, then swap. *9 min.* Model the who row of the chart, [[s03-K93-1]]. Children fill where the first scene is and the problem, [[s03-P93-12]]. Watch for error A, an event the opening does not give.

#### 10 min. Writing

Children revise one packet answer with the one-note, one-change card, [[s03-P93-10]]: one change made from the note, the first answer kept beside it. Recopying with no change is error L; model one change in the margin if many do it.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P93-13]]: point to one row of the chart where the openings match and one where they differ, and read the boxes. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X93v2]]. Shown: one likeness and one difference, in one sentence with but. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What is the same? What is different?" Not yet seen: likenesses only (error F), or a detail the books do not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 1 of book 2 aloud and is the reread partner. One projector: show the chart. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No book 2: any two stories your child knows.
**Skip.** The slates, the conferences and the repair groups.

- **10 min. Words.** Say the words and the sentence in [[s03-P93-1]] and [[s03-P93-3]] one at a time. Your child writes them.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and fills the line in [[s03-P93-6]].
- **10 min. Compare.** Your child tells you who is in each of two stories and where each begins.
  **Say.** "How do the two beginnings match, and how do they differ?" Expect: a likeness, such as the same people, and a difference, such as the place or the problem.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X93v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the opening chart and the exit; cut the retrieval. Longer: compare the endings of the two books on the same chart.

### Words and supports

Alike, different, opening, revise. Frames: "In book 1 the who is ___." "In book 2 the who is ___." Audio beside print; the chart read aloud by a helper; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 94 begins with the day 93 exit, s03-X93v2, and repairs error F, a likeness with no difference, then reads book 2 chapter 2 and adds to the chart. The word work, [[s03-P93-1]] to [[s03-P93-5]], the log work, [[s03-P93-6]], the spoken work, [[s03-P93-4]], [[s03-P93-8v2]], [[s03-P93-9v2]], [[s03-P93-11]], [[s03-P93-13]] and [[s03-K93-1]], and the revision, [[s03-P93-7]], [[s03-P93-10]] and [[s03-P93-12]], stay in the folder.
