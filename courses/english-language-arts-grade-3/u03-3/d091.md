# Day 91: Chapter 11: what the turn changes on the arc

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and an arc sheet for each child. On chart paper draw a rising and falling arc labeled situation, problem, events, turn, ending. Mark chapter 1 under situation (two plans for the reading corner) and chapters 2 to 4 under problem (Ada's plans keep a mistake she cannot find). Open the day 90 word check sheets. Pick the 12 children not yet heard. Mark the first three sentences of chapter 12 in the book 1 copy; the class has not heard them.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*4 min.* Repair first. Look across the day 90 word check sheets and find the one pattern most children missed: an odd spelling, an ending change or an affix meaning. Teach only that pattern: say a word, show the part that does not sound the way it looks, children write it twice on slates. Nothing new until this is done.
Week 19 begins: three-syllable words with familiar parts. Review: you split a long word, checked each chunk and blended it on day 81, and you learned dis- in grade 2.
*2 min.* Say recurring, similar and disagree. Children write each in chunks, then whole, [[s03-P91-1]]. Recurring has re- and a doubled r before -ing; similar has three chunks, sim-i-lar. Then again and learn, [[s03-P91-2]]. Review: you mapped again in grade 1.
*2 min.* Say the sentence, [[s03-P91-4]], in phrases, twice, and children write it. Then one slate line of ending rules, [[s03-P91-3]]. Review: drop the e, double the last letter, change y to i, from days 33 and 81.
*2 min.* In pairs, one child reads the last sentence of chapter 11 chunk by chunk and the other says what expected means, then they swap, [[s03-P91-5]]. Check the meaning after decoding: it must fit the sentence. Watch for error AD, a close word accepted.

#### 20 min. Independent reading and conferences

Log day. Children read their own book and fill the log line, [[s03-P91-6]]. *9 min.* Hear 12 children read the opening of chapter 12 (its first three sentences, about 50 words, not yet read to the class), 45 seconds each, one at a time at your table (s03-P90-4). The child reads from the book 1 copy; print nothing. Mark the word check sheet, day 90. The last 6 are heard on day 92. Review: the word check hearing from day 45. *8 min.* Two 4-minute conferences, one record each. Review: the four-minute reading conference from day 4. *3 min.* Two children read a log line aloud, 1.5 minutes each. Fill the heard-six column. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair and retrieve

*3 min.* Hand back the day 90 slips, s03-X90v2. Error D is a phrase read as a physical event. Read Jun's line beside the page before it: Ada looked at the arrow for days and could not see it. Ask what Ada could not see. Then a fresh card, [[s03-P91-7]]. Review: a phrase can mean more than it says, from day 90. If many slips showed error D, say one more phrase with a different card. *2 min.* Retrieve the plan card, [[s03-P91-8]]. Review: you made a plan card in unit 2 on day 42 and used it on day 81. Pairs say the three boxes and where a problem box goes. The arc is that card with a problem and a turn added; nothing else is new.

#### 20 min. Reading and reread

*6 min.* Read chapter 11 aloud whole (about 650 words). *2 min.* Two stops. **Ask.** After "I want it there": what did Ada do to the arrow, and what did she not do? Expect: she turned it and did not erase the old arrow. **Ask.** After "That's three things": what does Ada ask the friends to do? Expect: walk the route and say anything strange. *8 min.* Pairs reread the page from "By the time Priya, Jun and Mateo had followed it" to "three things we wouldn't have known", one reading and one listening, then swap. *4 min.* Share: three pairs say what each friend found, [[s03-P91-9]]. Watch for error A, a fact the page does not give.

#### 20 min. Writing

*5 min.* Model, [[s03-K91-1]]. Point to the arc: chapters 1 to 4 are marked. Ask whether an action in chapter 5 changes the problem. It does not: Ada hides the plan and the last arrow is still wrong, so chapter 5 is an event. Say: an event adds to the story; the turn is where an action changes what happens next. *10 min.* Children place chapters 5 to 11 on their arc sheets, [[s03-P91-10]]. They may use their chapter map frame sheets from days 81 to 87 and the book. Leave the ending box empty: chapter 12 is read tomorrow. *5 min.* Children write the two sentences, [[s03-P91-11]], on the arc sheet. Watch for error B, before and after reversed.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Word: route. Review: you learned route and stop on day 14. In chapter 11 it is the way Ada and Ben walk from the door to the reading corner, stopping at the cupboard. Children say what it means, then draw a one-line route with two stops in the box on the page, [[s03-P91-12]].

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes each child, [[s03-P91-13]]: which chapter is the turn, and what makes it the turn? Any child may pass. Listen for chapter 11 with an action, not a position.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X91v2]]. Shown: what the walks found, then what Ada changed, in that order. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What did the friends find first?" Not yet seen: the change put before the cause (error B), or a detail the chapter does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads chapter 11, is the reread partner and draws the arc on paper. One projector: show the arc. The exit stays on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No chapter 11: your child tells the story so far, then reads any story book.
**Skip.** The slates, the reread and the hearing.

- **10 min. Words.** Say the words and the sentence in [[s03-P91-1]] and [[s03-P91-4]] one at a time. Your child writes them.
- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and fills the line in [[s03-P91-6]].
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells the story so far in five short sentences, then fills [[s03-P91-10]].
  **Say.** "Which part changed what happens next?" Expect: Ada turned the arrow and asked the friends to check the route.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X91v2]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the reading, the arc and the exit; cut the route drawing. Longer: place a favorite book on an arc and name its turn.

### Words and supports

Arc, turn, route. Frame: "The turn is chapter ___ because ___." Audio beside print; large print for the arc; the arc sheet read aloud by a helper; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 92 is a check day: skills retrieval with the last 6 children heard on the chapter 12 opening, chapter 12 read alone, four items, and the day 92 exit. The day 91 exit, s03-X91v2, is not repaired tomorrow; its repair, error B, goes back on day 93. The word work, [[s03-P91-1]] to [[s03-P91-5]], the log work, [[s03-P91-6]], the spoken work, [[s03-P91-7]], [[s03-P91-8]], [[s03-P91-9]], [[s03-P91-13]] and [[s03-K91-1]], and the arc sheet, [[s03-P91-10]], [[s03-P91-11]] and [[s03-P91-12]], stay in the folder.
