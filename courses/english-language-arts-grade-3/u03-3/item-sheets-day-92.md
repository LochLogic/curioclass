# Item sheets, day 92

Print two sheets for each child. Silent. Sheet A holds P92 and items 1 to 3. Sheet B holds C12, which children read alone after chapter 12, and item 4. Read an item aloud on request, never a passage. Each passage is printed once; every item points to it.

## P92

In chapter 10, Ben found that the arrow pointed away from the door. In chapter 11, Ada asked everyone to check a new plan. In chapter 12, they followed it together and reached the reading corner. The narrator calls checking tiresome. Ada calls it useful.

## C12

The group reached the reading corner. Ben clipped the checked plan beside the doorway. Ada stopped him from putting the first drawing away. "Hang that beside it," she said. "Then we can see what changed." The two pages fluttered together whenever someone opened the door. Ada watched another reader stop to compare them, then take the clear route to the books. She left both pages hanging.
