# Day 92: Check: chapter 12, characters and how the ending grows

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the packet for each child. Close the chapter books until the reading part, except the one open at your table for the hearing. Say aloud: "These passages are made up for practice." Answer nothing about content. Day 91's slips and arc sheets are not returned today; they go back on day 93. Open the word check sheet, day 90, at the last 6 children.

### The day

#### 30 min. Skills, retrieval only

No new pattern and no teaching. *10 min.* Long-word retrieval on paper: six words in chunks and whole, [[s03-P92-1]], then eight irregular words, [[s03-P92-2]]. *5 min.* Ending rules on slates, [[s03-P92-3]]. *15 min.* Independent reading from the log book, then the log line, [[s03-P92-4]]. No conferences. While the others read, hear the last 6 children read the opening of chapter 12 (its first three sentences, about 50 words, not read to the class) from the book 1 copy, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes (s03-P90-4). Mark the word check sheet, day 90.

#### 20 min. Chapter 12, unaided

Children read chapter 12 alone and silently (about 890 words), then reread. Answer nothing about the content.

#### 30 min. Items

Silent. P92 is printed once on side 1 and C12 once on side 2; every item points to its passage. Read an item aloud on request, never a passage. Item 4, [[s03-A6]], 8 minutes. Item 5, [[s03-A7]], 8 minutes. Item 6, [[s03-A8]], 7 minutes. Item 7, [[s03-A9]], 7 minutes, on C12; children keep the C12 passage folded under until this item. Mark with the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record the support used.

#### 5 min. Silent verification

Children reread their own answers once, [[s03-P92-5]]. Give no hint. Collect the packets and take them home to write one note on each; day 93 returns them.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s03-X92]]. Shown: the last action (rolled the ball to the waiting children), then what it caused (they began together). Shown with help: after one prompt, "What did Cal do at the end?" Not yet seen: a trait with no action (error C), or an event the card does not give (error A). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads each item aloud on request and writes what the child says, marking it as read aloud. No printer: write P92 and the items on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any book at home.
**Skip.** The retrieval words and the hearing.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Your child reads P92, then C12, and answers [[s03-A6]], [[s03-A7]], [[s03-A8]] and [[s03-A9]] alone, on paper. Read an item aloud if asked, never the passage. Give no hint.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book, then tells you what might come next.
  **Say.** "What comes next?" Expect: a guess from the story.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s03-X92]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chapter, items and exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Chapter, narrator, judgment. Large print; items read aloud on request; a scribe; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 93 returns the packet with one written note on each child's work, s03-A6 to s03-A9 (error AC), and the day 92 exit, s03-X92 (error C), and the day 91 exit, s03-X91 (error B), and repairs the commonest error first, with small groups for error E and error AC. It then reads the first chapter of book 2. The retrieval work, [[s03-P92-1]], [[s03-P92-2]], [[s03-P92-3]], [[s03-P92-4]] and [[s03-P92-5]], and the packet stay in the teacher folder.
