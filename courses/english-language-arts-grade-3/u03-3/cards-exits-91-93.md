# Exit cards, days 91 to 93

Each strip below prints lines from the unit's own books, with the chapter named; it carries no invented label. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 91 (s03-X91v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 11: "But Ada did not mind. She darkened the second arrow. She moved the yellow beanbag." Ask: What did the friends' walks change in the plan?
- **Day 92 (s03-X92v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 12: Nia and Sam "sat side by side at the round table, one chair each, reading with their heads close." "I fixed that one on Wednesday," said Ada. Ask: What did Ada's Wednesday fix cause?
- **Day 93 (s03-X93v2), Exit.** From the book. Strip: book 1, chapter 1: "She drew the long wall with the windows and the short wall with the cupboard. She drew the door." Book 2, chapter 1: "The courtyard was a small square of grass with a big maple tree in the middle." Ask: How are the settings alike and different? Use but.
