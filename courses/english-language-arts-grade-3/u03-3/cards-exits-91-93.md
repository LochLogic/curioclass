# Exit cards, days 91 to 93

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 91 (s03-X91), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Earlier: Rae suggests testing the new path with a rolling ball. Later: The ball rolls into the pond, so Rae moves the path uphill." Ask: What did the test change in Rae's plan?
- **Day 92 (s03-X92), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Cal held the only ball at recess because he wanted the first kick. At the end he rolled it to the waiting children. They began together." Ask: What did Cal's last action cause?
- **Day 93 (s03-X93), Exit.** Invented for practice. Card: "Story A: Bo builds a sand fort near the waves. A wave takes the front wall. Story B: Bo builds a sand fort near the dunes. The wind takes the flag." Ask: Write one sentence that says how the settings are alike and different. Use the word but.
