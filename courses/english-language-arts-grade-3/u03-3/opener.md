# Section 3.3 opener: the arc and the ending (days 91 to 93)

## What the section does

For three days the class finishes book 1 and starts book 2. Children place each chapter on a narrative arc, say what chapter 11 changes, read chapter 12 alone and answer a packet about the ending, and then, on day 93, use the written note on that packet to revise one answer and begin comparing the openings of the two books.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes. Day 91 teaches the arc with a worked chapter. Day 92 is a check: skills retrieval only, chapter 12 read alone, items on a printed passage and on the chapter, silent verification. Day 93 returns the packet, repairs one error in the whole class and in small groups, reads the first chapter of book 2 and starts a two-column opening chart.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **An arc has a situation, a problem, events, a turn and an ending.** The turn is where an action changes what happens next.
- **An ending grows from earlier chapters.** Name the earlier chapter and the later one, and what links them.
- **A character can change.** What a character does at the end can differ from the start. Describe the action and what it caused.
- **Whose judgment is it?** The narrator, a character and the reader may each name a quality. Keep each one with its speaker.
- **A comparison needs a difference.** A likeness alone is half a comparison.

## The errors, in plain words

- **B:** Reverses the order of linked parts. Repair: Number the earlier cause before the later change. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **C:** States a trait without an action or consequence. Repair: Draw action to result, then name the supported trait. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **E:** Attributes one person's view to another. Repair: Label whose words express each judgment. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **AC:** Reads without explaining the text's meaning. Repair: Reread to answer one literal question from the words. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **A:** Invents evidence absent from the text. Repair: Underline a supporting sentence before answering. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.
- **F:** Gives similarities without differences. Repair: Add a contrasting detail in each comparison dimension. 5 minutes, next day's opening repair.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
