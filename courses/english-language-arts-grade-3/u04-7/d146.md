# Day 146: Listen to a model announcement

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, a listening card and the printed model for each child. Sort the day 145 essays into piles by the move written on them (L to R, AO); put the biggest pile's essays where you will sit. Put the day 145 word work, s04-P145-1 and s04-P145-2, in each child's folder. Say aloud: the model is made up for practice.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Say announce, deliver and clarify twice each. Children draw lines between the syllables, [[s04-P146-1]]. Then say skip, bake and copy. Say: after a short vowel the letter doubles, a silent e is dropped before -ed only, and y changes to i. Children add -ed, [[s04-P146-2]]. Review: you added -ed to wish and name on day 142. *5 min.* Say heavy and again twice each; heavy has ea said /e/, again has ai said /e/. Children write both, [[s04-P146-3]]. Review: you mapped again on day 129. Watch for a word spelled by its sound.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P146-4]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child naming a book, a question and one detail, [[s04-P146-5]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P146-6]]. Fill the heard-six column of the record sheet. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening

Return the essays with their written feedback, which is day 145's assessment, items s04-A20 to s04-A23. Hand back the day 145 page tasks s04-P145-3, s04-P145-4 and s04-P145-5 with a tick on each, and the exit slip s04-X145. Children read the move on their essay and fix the one sentence it names, then mark the change, [[s04-P146-7]]. Say: the move is a note for the writer, not a new essay. The biggest pile works with you on one shared sentence; the others work alone. Watch for a sentence copied again with no change, error T. The essays go back in the folder.

#### 20 min. Hook and model

*3 min.* Say: I will read one line two ways. Read "The tank holds rain from the roof, so we water the garden" once flat and fast, then again with a pause after roof and after so. Children tell a partner which way they could follow, [[s04-P146-8]]. Every child may pass.

*5 min.* Say: this is a model announcement, made up for practice. Children keep the model face down and the listening card ready. Read it once, at a talking pace. Children turn it over, underline the sentence that tells the main idea and number two details, [[s04-P146-9]]. Review: you stated a main idea and two details from a talk on day 17. Watch for a step sentence underlined as the main idea, error B.

*4 min.* Show the worked example. Say: the model has a bracket. A bracket is a note for the voice, so it is shown, not said. Read "Because the clumps are [Pause before heavy.] heavy, they sink" twice. First read the bracket words aloud; children say that is wrong. Then pause before heavy and say nothing for the bracket; children say that is right. Review: you read stage directions in brackets on day 107; they were not spoken. Watch for the cue read as words, error AB.

*5 min.* Read the model a second time. Children write on the listening card the word after the pause and one slow phrase, [[s04-P146-10]]. Fill only the first line, the pause line and the slow-phrase line today. The other lines stay blank for day 149.

*3 min.* Children point to the slow phrase on the card and say it to a partner. Say: a pause lets the listener catch the word.

#### 10 min. Reading

*3 min.* Children read "The steps" in the town text silently. *4 min.* They read it aloud to a partner, one step each, in phrases, [[s04-P146-12]]. Review: you read with a partner and a feedback card from day 2. Hear six children. *3 min.* Children find the step that matches one line from the model, [[s04-P146-11]]. The model used four facts from this page.

#### 10 min. Writing

*4 min.* Children circle the line that tells the main idea of the model, [[s04-P146-13]]. Then the link, [[s04-P146-14]]. Say: name the relationship first. A reason answers why. Review: you named the relationship before the link word on day 143. Watch for therefore joining a reason wrongly, error N. *3 min.* Children circle the ending that closes the announcement, [[s04-P146-15]]. Review: you closed an opinion on day 142. Watch for no close, error O. *3 min.* Children turn an answer of one word into a sentence, [[s04-P146-16]]. Watch for a fragment, error AA.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Pairs. One child asks, "What happens in the last step?" and the other answers in one complete sentence, [[s04-P146-17]]; then they swap. Two minutes each, 1 to change over. Every child may pass. No child is held up as the example of a mistake. Review: you told a plan in complete sentences on day 108.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence about a delivery. Children circle what delivery means in it, [[s04-P146-18]]. Check the answers against the sentence. Say: delivery here is how a speaker says the words.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child. Read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X146]]. Then they turn to the exit box on the page. Read the first card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X146b]]. Then say the second question, which is written on the page, and children answer in a sentence, [[s04-X146c]]. All three are unscored; support is recorded. Shown: a main idea and a detail in two sentences, a pause that is not said, a complete sentence. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What is the whole piece about?" Not yet seen: a detail as the main idea, error B; the cue said aloud, error AB; a fragment, error AA. Record on the record sheet. Collect the slips, the pages and the cards.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult reads the model aloud and the child marks the card and the page; the partner talk is said to the adult. One projector: show nothing; the model is read aloud and the work is on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed model: read aloud any short how-it-works paragraph and name its main idea.
**Skip.** The log conference.

- **15 min. Listen.** Read the printed model aloud once, then again. Your child underlines the main idea, [[s04-P146-9]].
  **Say.** "What is the main idea, and which detail proves it?" Expect: water is cleaned in four steps; a chemical makes the dirt stick.
- **10 min. Word.** Say heavy and again; your child writes both, [[s04-P146-3]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X146]], [[s04-X146b]] and [[s04-X146c]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the second read and the exit; cut the log conferences to two. Longer: a child reads the model aloud once with the bracket as a pause.

### Words and supports

Announce, listener, delivery, pause, bracket, clarify. Frame: "The main idea is ___. One detail is ___." A word strip; the model may be read from a printed card; a scribe; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 147 is an application day: nothing new is taught. It opens with the day 146 slips handed back, then children think about what a listener already knows. Each child chooses one issue and plans the key points for it. The printed model, the listening card and the word work go in the folder.
