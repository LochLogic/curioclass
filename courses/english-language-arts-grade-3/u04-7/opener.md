# Section 4.7 opener: listening and planning an announcement (days 146 to 149)

## What the section does

Four days turn the water research into a short spoken announcement. Children listen to a model and mark its main idea, details and delivery. They plan key points for one listener, write and type a script, then add cues for the voice and revise after a peer's question. Some of the work is typed in short turns, and some is recorded in small groups.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes: a long-words block, a skills block, then the day's work. Day 146 is the model, day 147 the plan, day 148 the script and short typed turns, and day 149 the cue plan, a recorded turn and a small-group discussion. Where there are no devices, a paper copy does the same job. Each day ends with a five-minute exit on paper. Nothing is scored on the day.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A model shows what to aim at.** Name the main idea, the details and what the voice does.
- **Plan for the listener.** Begin at what this listener does not yet know.
- **Facts come first, then the claim.** Give the source for each key point.
- **A cue is written, not spoken.** A bracket asks the voice to pause or slow; the words in it stay on the page.
- **A clarification is a complete sentence.** A fragment leaves the listener guessing.
- **Feedback changes the plan.** A peer's question is answered with a changed line.

## The errors, in plain words

- **B:** Takes a small detail as the main idea. Repair: ask what the whole piece is about.
- **A:** Adds a fact the model never states. Repair: point to the words.
- **V:** Says something vague in place of a definition and a detail. Repair: add both.
- **W:** Ignores what the listener needs. Repair: write what this listener knows first.
- **AA:** Answers with a fragment. Repair: restate it with a subject and a verb.
- **AB:** Says the written cue aloud. Repair: pause instead.
- **T:** Copies the plan out unchanged. Repair: show one change from the feedback.
- **L to R and AO:** carried from 4.6; use the written move on one sentence.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored. Record typed turns and recordings on the device log: the file saved, the text typed, the question asked.
