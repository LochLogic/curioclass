# Day 147: Plan the key points for a listener

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the plan list for each child, and one feedback card for each pair. Put the day 146 slips and pages on the desks. Put the printed model in each child folder. Choose five children to confer with; start with those not conferred with on day 146. Open the record sheet at the heard-six column.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Say listener, purpose and announcement twice each. Children draw lines between the syllables, [[s04-P147-1]]. Say: a key point is the main thing a listener must keep. Then say stir, shine and try. Children add -ing, [[s04-P147-2]]. Review: you added -ing on days 130 and 143. *5 min.* Say ocean and busy twice each; ocean has c said /sh/, busy has u said /i/. Children write both, [[s04-P147-3]]. Watch for a word spelled by its sound.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Tuesday

*5 min.* Grammar. Write on the board: a script is written to be read aloud. Children circle the sentence that fits a script, [[s04-P147-4]]. Say: we say um when we talk; we leave it out of a script. Review: you told a plan in complete sentences on day 108. *15 min.* Pairs reread the two passages What I think and Why the order matters in the town text, each child reading to the other in phrases, then again with expression, [[s04-P147-5]]. The listener fills the feedback card and ticks only what was heard, [[s04-P147-6]]. Review: you read with a partner and the feedback card from day 2. No speed talk. Hear six children and fill the heard-six column.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 146 page with a tick on each task, s04-P146-1, s04-P146-2, s04-P146-3, s04-P146-4, s04-P146-7, s04-P146-9, s04-P146-10, s04-P146-11, s04-P146-13, s04-P146-14, s04-P146-15, s04-P146-16 and s04-P146-18. Hand back the slips s04-X146, s04-X146b and s04-X146c, each with one comment. Children read the comment. Where a slip is a fragment, they say it again as a sentence with a subject and a verb. Error AA. Where the cue was said, say: a cue is shown, not said. *2 min.* Say: a listener knows some things about water already. Children tell a partner one thing a listener knows and one thing they need first, [[s04-P147-7]]. Review: you gave a short explanation for a listener on day 3. Every child may pass.

#### 20 min. Reading

*10 min.* Children choose one issue from the research folder and mark one passage in the water book, the Kitui County account or the town text that gives its key facts, [[s04-P147-8]]. *5 min.* They underline the hardest part and write one word beside it: reread, ask or draw, [[s04-P147-9]]. Decode the word, never guess it. *5 min.* Each child says the hard part to a partner in one complete sentence, [[s04-P147-10]]. Review: you read a source and noted a few words on days 137 and 140.

#### 20 min. Plan

Children fill the plan list for their issue: the main idea, three key points each with a source tag, the order, and a close, [[s04-P147-11]]. Say: begin at what the listener does not know. Say: the plan lists points; the script comes tomorrow. Confer with five children for 4 minutes each (20 minutes) while the rest write; the talk is a note, not a mark. Watch for a point the listener knows already, error W; for a copied paragraph, not a few words. The plan is unscored and stays in the folder. After class, write one comment on each plan for day 148.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Pairs. Each child says the main idea and three key points, [[s04-P147-12]], and the partner asks one question in a complete sentence, [[s04-P147-13]]. Two minutes each, 1 to change over. Every child may pass. No child is held up as the example of a mistake. Review: you asked a linked question on day 141.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence with source. Children circle what source means in it, [[s04-P147-14]]. Review: you wrote a source tag on a note on days 128 and 137.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X147]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: a sentence that starts at what the listener does not know and uses the source words. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What does the listener not know?" Not yet seen: a point the listener knows, error W, or a fragment, error AA. Record on the record sheet. Collect the slips, the pages and the cards; the plans stay in the folders.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner, hears the plan and asks one question; the child answers the exit on paper. One projector: show nothing; the work is on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No research folder: use any short page about water.
**Skip.** The partner reading.

- **15 min. Plan.** Your child chooses one fact about water and fills the plan list, [[s04-P147-11]].
  **Say.** "What does your listener already know?" Expect: something simple, such as that people drink water. Then: "What do they need first?"
- **10 min. Read.** Your child marks the hardest part of a page and writes reread, ask or draw, [[s04-P147-9]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X147]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the plan, the partner talk and the exit; cut the partner reading to one passage. Longer: a child writes a second plan for a different listener.

### Words and supports

Key point, listener, source, plan, order, close. Frame: "The listener knows ___. They need ___ first." A word strip; the plan may be filled with a scribe; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 148 is an application day: nothing new is taught. It opens with the day 147 slips handed back, then children read the model again for its beginning, linked facts and close. They write a script from the plan; some type one short turn and the rest write on paper.
