# Day 149: Make a cue plan and record a turn

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, two listening cards and a cue plan for each child. Put the day 148 slips on the desks and open each pair's device file so the peer question shows. Set fifteen devices, one for each pair; the paper script is the fall-back. Open the device log and the discussion record. Mark the first and second device turn on the record sheet. Choose six groups of five for the discussion.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Say carefully, helpful and slowly twice each. Children draw lines between the syllables, [[s04-P149-1]]. Then say slow, care and brave. Say: the e stays before -ful and -ly. Children add -ly or -ful, [[s04-P149-2]]. Review: you added -ly and -ful on days 127 and 141. Then say swim, wave and play. Children add -ing, [[s04-P149-4]]. *5 min.* Say touch and double twice each; both have ou said /u/. Children write both, [[s04-P149-3]]. Watch for a word spelled by its sound.

#### 20 min. Skills block, Wednesday

*5 min.* Grammar. Say: we say gonna when we talk, but a script says going to. Children circle the sentence that belongs in a script, [[s04-P149-5]]. Then they circle the cue line that is shown and not said, [[s04-P149-12]]. Say: a cue is shown, not said. Review: you read stage directions that were not spoken on day 107. Error AB. *15 min.* Pairs reread the script, each child reading to the other in phrases, [[s04-P149-6]]. The listener fills the feedback card and ticks only what was heard, [[s04-P149-7]]. Review: you read with a partner and the feedback card from day 2. No speed talk. Hear six children.

#### 15 min. Reading

*5 min.* Hand back the day 148 slips, s04-X148, with one comment each; children read the peer's typed question on the screen or slip. Where a slip was vague, children add a meaning and a source detail aloud. Error V. *10 min.* Listening response. Say: I will read two short accounts once each. Pencils down while I read. Read "Water that stays", the sand dam paragraph, once. Children write the main idea and one detail on a listening card, [[s04-P149-8]]. Read "Why the order matters" once. Children write the main idea and one detail on a second card, [[s04-P149-9]]. Review: you listened for a main idea and details on days 17 and 146. Collect the cards. Watch for a small detail as the main idea, error B.

#### 15 min. Cue plan and rehearse

*6 min.* Ask the planned question: which words would you slow down so the fact is not lost? Children fill the cue plan with three lines of the script, each with a cue in brackets and what the voice does, [[s04-P149-10]]. They read the peer's question and change one line to answer it, [[s04-P149-11]]. These are children's own work: unscored, kept in the folder. Watch for a plan copied again, error T. *9 min.* Device turns of 4 minutes, with 1 to switch (4, 4 and 1). The first fifteen children record the script from the page, [[s04-P149-13]], answer the peer's question in one complete sentence, [[s04-P149-14]], say one line with a pause where the cue plan shows one and no cue words, [[s04-P149-15]], then type one sentence to the peer about what changed, [[s04-P149-16]]. Speaking is about 90 seconds at most. The other fifteen rehearse aloud in pairs with a pause at each bracket, [[s04-P149-17]]. Then they swap. Mark the device log: the file saved and the reply typed. Without a device, hear six children and tick the feedback card, and the rest rehearse. After class, write component feedback for day 150.

#### 20 min. Discussion

*2 min.* Say: the two accounts we heard; think of one question about them. Pass out nothing new. *18 min.* Six groups of five take turns at your table, 3 minutes each (6 groups, 18 minutes). Each child asks or answers one question about the two accounts in one complete sentence, [[s04-P149-18]], about 36 seconds each. Start one device to record the group; the other devices stay off. Fill the five boxes of the discussion record for each child as an unscored note; this talk is not judged and is not a mark. The rest of the class rehearses aloud in pairs with the cue plan, [[s04-P149-17]]. Review: you held a five-box discussion on days 130 and 141. Every child may pass. No child is held up as the example of a mistake. Watch for a fragment, error AA.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the sentence with clarify. Children circle what clarify means in it, [[s04-P149-19]]. Review: you learned clarify in kindergarten, to ask again so you understand. Say: to clarify is to make a hard part clear for the listener.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X149]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: the plan line written again with the last fact slow and the cue shown, not said. Shown with help: after one prompt, "What did the peer lose?" Not yet seen: the cue said aloud, error AB, or the plan copied unchanged, error T. Record on the record sheet. Collect the slips, pages, cards, scripts and cue plans for written feedback before day 150.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the listener; the child reads the script, answers the question aloud and writes the reply; a phone may record one minute and stays at home. One projector: show nothing; the turns are on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: say the script aloud and your helper ticks a card.
**Skip.** The grammar.

- **15 min. Cue plan.** Your child marks three places in the script with a bracket and says what the voice does, [[s04-P149-10]].
  **Say.** "Which words would you slow down so the fact is not lost?" Expect: the last fact or a number.
- **10 min. Say it.** Your child reads the script aloud and pauses at each bracket, without saying the cue.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X149]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the cue plan, one device turn and the exit; cut the listening response to one account. Longer: a child reads the script to a third listener.

### Words and supports

Cue, bracket, clarify, pause, slow, reply. Frame: "I will slow down ___ so the listener keeps ___." A word strip; a printed card of the script; a partner may read the cue plan aloud; headphones for the recording turn; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 150 begins the next section, on a letter. It opens with the day 149 script feedback, one comment each, and your hand-back of the day 149 slips; children show one change they made from a comment. Then a model letter to a water office is the new text. The script, cue plan and recordings stay with you.
