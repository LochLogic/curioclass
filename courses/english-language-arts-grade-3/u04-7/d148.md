# Day 148: Write and type a script from the plan

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child and one slip for each pair. Put the day 147 plans, each with your comment, and the day 147 slips on the desks. Set fifteen devices, one for each pair, each with a blank file named for the pair. Open the device log: the text typed, the file saved, the question asked. Choose which child in each pair types first.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*5 min.* Say statement, movement and excitement twice each. Children draw lines between the syllables, [[s04-P148-1]]. Then say state, move and excite. Say: the e stays before -ment. Children add -ment, [[s04-P148-2]]. Then say trim, ride and fry. Children add -ing, [[s04-P148-4]]. Review: you added -ing on days 130 and 147; you added -ment on days 128 and 138. *5 min.* Say island and whole twice each; island has an s that is not said, whole has a w that is not said. Children write both, [[s04-P148-3]]. Watch for a word spelled by its sound.

#### 20 min. Skills block, log day

Children read their own book and write the log line, [[s04-P148-5]]. Four conferences of 4 minutes (16 minutes), each child naming a book, a question and one detail, [[s04-P148-6]]. Two children read a log line aloud, 2 minutes each (4 minutes), [[s04-P148-7]]. Fill the heard-six column. Review: you kept this log from day 4 on.

#### 5 min. Opening

*3 min.* Hand back the day 147 slips, s04-X147, with one comment each. Children read the comment; where the first point repeats what the listener knows, they write what this listener knows above the plan. Error W. *2 min.* Children look at the model and underline the beginning, circle one link word and box the close, [[s04-P148-8]]. Review: you saw the model's pause and close on day 146. Say: our scripts need the same three parts.

#### 20 min. Reading

*10 min.* Children reread the sources for their three key points and put a star by the line that backs each one, [[s04-P148-9]]. Decode a hard word, never guess it. *5 min.* Each child reads a partner's plan and ticks yes or not yet for the order of the facts, [[s04-P148-10]]. Review: you gave a peer look-for on day 143. *5 min.* Hand back each plan with your one comment. Children read it and write the one change they will make, [[s04-P148-11]]. Watch for a plan copied out again, error T.

#### 20 min. Draft and type

Children write the script from the plan: a beginning, linked facts and a close, [[s04-P148-12]]. Say: say where a fact came from, with the source tag or the words according to. The script is unscored and stays in the folder; it is read from, never learned by heart. The typed turn and the paper turn each last 8 minutes, with 4 minutes to switch and save (8, 8 and 4). In the first turn the first child of each pair types one paragraph of the script and names its source, [[s04-P148-13]], then types one question to the partner about the partner's plan, [[s04-P148-14]]. Light use: practice with feedback. The other child drafts on paper. Then they swap. Mark the device log: text typed, file saved, question asked. Watch the device room and do not type for a child; if a file is lost, the paper script is the fall-back. Without a device, a child writes the paragraph and the question on the slip. Review: you typed and saved one line on day 68.

#### 5 min. Partner talk

Pairs. Each child reads the paragraph aloud in phrases, [[s04-P148-15]], then says what the typed question asks in one complete sentence, [[s04-P148-16]]. Two minutes each, 1 to change over. Every child may pass. No child is held up as the example of a mistake. Review: you read in phrases on day 143.

#### 5 min. Word in context

Read the two sentences about the plant text. Children circle the one that shows where a fact came from, [[s04-P148-17]]. Say: according to tells the listener where a fact came from.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the half slip to every child and read the card aloud twice; children write alone, [[s04-X148]]. Unscored. Support is recorded. Shown: a beginning, the handle fact joined to why it matters with a link word, and a closing. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Why does the handle matter?" Not yet seen: a vague claim in place of the fact, error V; liking alone, error M; or no closing, error O. Record on the record sheet. Collect the slips, the pages and the scripts; the typed files stay on the devices in class.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the peer; the child writes the script, then types a paragraph and a question if there is a device, or writes them on a slip. One projector: show nothing; the work is on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: write the paragraph and the question on a slip.
**Skip.** The log conference.

- **20 min. Script.** Your child writes a script from the plan: a beginning, linked facts, a close, [[s04-P148-12]].
  **Say.** "Where did that fact come from?" Expect: the source named, such as the plant text.
- **10 min. Word.** Your child adds -ment to state, move and excite, [[s04-P148-2]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s04-X148]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the script, the typed turn and the exit; cut the log conferences to two. Longer: a child reads the script aloud with one pause chosen.

### Words and supports

Script, source, according to, paragraph, question, save. Frame: "According to ___, ___." A word strip; a printed card of the script; a scribe may type; headphones are not needed today; extra time within the day; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 149 is an application day: nothing new is taught. It opens with your hand-back of the day 148 slips and each peer's typed question. Children listen to two short accounts, add cues for the voice to the script, record one short turn and join a small-group talk.
