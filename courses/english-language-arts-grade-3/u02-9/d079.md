# Day 79: Check 1, the article and the source trail, second fifteen children heard

## Teacher page

### Before class

Take the winter hearing order: today are children 16 to 30, then anyone the day 78 list says was not reached. Keep set B of the oral cards face down. Keep day 78's items, the sheets and the oral records, in the folder; they are returned on day 80.

### The day

#### 25 min. The article

All thirty, silent. The only help is the title read aloud. Each child reads The Hill Town Book Cart, with its headings, and answers sheet 79A, [[c1-A79a]] to [[c1-A79j]]. Collect the sheets when the time is up. Say once: "This article is new. Nothing was taught from it. You may pass any ask."

#### 20 min. The source trail

All thirty, silent. Each child uses the contents, the index, the sidebar and the search page in the booklet to answer sheet 79B, [[c1-A79k]] to [[c1-A79o]]. Collect the sheets.

#### 45 min. Oral window, second fifteen

Children 16 to 30, 3 minutes each, at your table; the rest read their own book, unmarked. Use set B and the passage Yara's Reading Tent. In order: the purpose and first reading, [[c1-A79s]]; the sentence with close, [[c1-A79u]]; the affix words, [[c1-A79p]]; the -sion and -ous words, [[c1-A79q]]; the unusual words, [[c1-A79r]]; then the reread, [[c1-A79t]]. A child with a clean recording from days 70 to 76 skips the reread; write its date. Give no help beyond the stuck word. A child still not heard goes on a list for you to hear in day 80's group time.

After class, mark every ask on both days. Day 78's items stay with day 79's for the one written note each child gets. List gaps by letter from the course register, A to Y.

### Other rooms

One child: the article, the trail and the oral window in order. No printer: copy the article by hand in large print, headings kept. A scribe and pointing count; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No booklet: use any short article with headings and ask your own questions.
**Skip.** The oral window.

- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the article and answers [[c1-A79a]] to [[c1-A79j]] alone.
  **Say.** "Show me where it says so." Expect: the child points to the words.
- **20 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[c1-A79k]] to [[c1-A79o]] with the page parts.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: spread the oral window over the next day only so every child is heard once; no ask is cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: article, heading, contents, index, sidebar, link, volunteers. Frames: "The main idea is ___." "I would type ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used. Large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 80 returns day 78's items and today's with one written note, and teaches each gap in small groups by letter. It is a response day, not a new text.
