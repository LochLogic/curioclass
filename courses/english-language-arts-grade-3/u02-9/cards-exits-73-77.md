# Exit cards, days 73 to 77

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every exit is one ask; read each card twice before the children answer. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 73 (s02-X73).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "The frog is green. The frog is hard to see in leaves." Peer note: "Connect the facts." Teacher note: "Choose a precise word." Ask: Ask your peer one question that links to the note.
- **Day 73 (s02-X73b).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "The frog is green. The frog is hard to see in leaves." Peer note: "Connect the facts." Teacher note: "Choose a precise word." Ask: Revise the two sentences. Use a precise word.
- **Day 73 (s02-X73c).** This card is invented for practice. Strip: "The frog is green. The frog is hard to see in leaves." Peer note: "Connect the facts." Teacher note: "Choose a precise word." Ask: Say why your change helps.
- **Day 74 (s02-X74).** This card is invented for practice. Reader question: "Which source supports your explanation of the stream?" Ask: Point to the source and the page or heading in your folder.
- **Day 74 (s02-X74b).** This card is invented for practice. Reader question: "Which source supports your explanation of the stream?" Ask: Copy one categorized note from your folder with its label.
- **Day 74 (s02-X74c).** This card is invented for practice. Ask: Say how your second draft makes the evidence clearer than the first.
