# Day 74: Check: the revised essay, the notes and the glass frog excerpt

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, with an index card in one of three forms. Clear desks but for the folder and the log. Set the device at the keyword page. Write the five names for the recordings and set the exit strip.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back day 73's strip, s02-X73b, s02-X73 and s02-X73c. Repair error AB (a joining word that links unrelated ideas): pairs choose the join where the second idea belongs, [[s02-P74-6]]. *7 min.* Retrieval only, no new pattern: read three week 15 words and name each base, [[s02-P74-5]].

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Slates: the past of fall, [[s02-P74-1]], and a plural that changes, [[s02-P74-2]].

#### 15 min. Recording

Five children make their recordings of the poem with the display, 3 minutes each, [[s02-P74-3]]. The rest read silently from their logs.

#### 25 min. Retained essay, notes and the excerpt

Silent. Part 1, 10 minutes: each child lays out the folder in order, the day 71 body paragraph included, and answers [[s02-A77]], [[s02-A78]], [[s02-A79]], [[s02-A80]], [[s02-A81]], [[s02-A82]] and [[s02-A83]] from it. Part 2, 15 minutes: each child rereads the Habitat paragraph of the glass frog excerpt alone and answers [[s02-A85v2]], [[s02-A86v2]], [[s02-A87]], [[s02-A88v2]], [[s02-A89v2]] and [[s02-A90v2]]. Answer nothing about content; record any help.

#### 30 min. Location rotations

Ten groups of three take 3 minutes each at the device, 1 minute a child. With the printed excerpt beside the device, each child names the heading to open to find where glass frogs live, [[s02-A84v2]]; give procedural help only. The children not at the device write their index card page, [[s02-P74-4]] (a third of the sheets carry [[s02-P74-4b]] or [[s02-P74-4c]] instead; each has its own key), then finish the sheet or read their logs silently. Watch for error V: a page opened at random.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X74b]], then [[s02-X74v2]] and [[s02-X74c]] on the page. Shown: a labelled note, a named source with its page, what the second draft adds. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Where in your folder is it?" Not yet seen: no label (error A), no text features used (error V), a recopy (error AC). Collect the folders, sheets and strips when the exit is done.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult leaves the table and records any help. No printer: the excerpt copies must be printed.

### At home

**Swap.** No log book: any animal book.
**Skip.** The recordings and the rotation.

- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s02-A77]] to [[s02-A83]] from the folder, then rereads the Habitat paragraph of the excerpt and answers [[s02-A85v2]] to [[s02-A90v2]] alone, in silence.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads from the log book.
  **Say.** "What did you wonder?" Expect: one question about the book.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X74b]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the long words part, never the assessment. Longer: none on a check day.

### Words and supports

Excerpt, source, label, category, index, keyword. Large print; items read aloud except where reading is the evidence; a scribe for the essay items; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 returns the day 74 strip, [[s02-X74b]], [[s02-X74v2]] and [[s02-X74c]], and repairs error V first: pick the heading or the page before opening the book. The folders and sheets come back with notes on day 75. The spoken work, [[s02-P74-3]] and [[s02-P74-5]], and the board work, [[s02-P74-1]], [[s02-P74-2]] and [[s02-P74-6]], stay in the folder. Check: [[s02-A77]] to [[s02-A90v2]].
