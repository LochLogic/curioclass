# Check 1 oral cards, days 78 and 79 (teacher only, unseen until the day)

Say each time: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing here was taught. You may pass any ask." Print each word card with its words in large letters, one card to a child's turn. Keep every card face down and collect it after each child, so the second fifteen children never see set A. Day 78 uses set A (children 1 to 15 of the hearing order); day 79 uses set B (children 16 to 30, then anyone not heard). The passages are g3-c1-o78 (Odo's Reading Spot) and g3-c1-o79 (Yara's Reading Tent); print each with its title and nothing else.

## Set A, day 78

- Affixes (c1-A78n): rename, boldly.
- Latin suffixes (c1-A78o): election, gracious.
- Words that are not the way they look (c1-A78p): heart, once, neighbors.
- Self-correction sentence (c1-A78s): the third sentence of Odo's Reading Spot, the one with the word close.

## Set B, day 79

- Affixes (c1-A79p): refill, quietly.
- Latin suffixes (c1-A79q): invasion, ambitious.
- Words that are not the way they look (c1-A79r): friend, practice, library.
- Self-correction sentence (c1-A79u): the third sentence of Yara's Reading Tent, the one with the word close.

## The three minutes of one child

Turn the title card, 20 seconds (purpose). First reading, 60 seconds. Tell what it said, 10 seconds. The sentence with close, 15 seconds. The three word cards, 50 seconds. The reread, 25 seconds. A child with a clean poem recording from days 70 to 76 skips the reread: write its date in the record. Never add help beyond the stuck word.
