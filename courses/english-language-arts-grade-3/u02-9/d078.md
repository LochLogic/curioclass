# Day 78: Check 1, the fable and the label, first fifteen children heard

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the thirty children in the winter hearing order and mark the first fifteen for today; the other fifteen are heard on day 79, and nobody is heard twice. List each child with a clean poem recording from days 70 to 76, with its date. Print the pages for every child. Keep set A of the oral cards face down.

### The day

#### 3 min. Opening

Return the day 77 strip (s02-X77, s02-X77b and s02-X77c) and repair error A of unit 2: point to the sentence that gives the detail. Then say once: "Today's texts are new and made up for practice. Nothing was taught from them. You may pass any ask." Mark every ask shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 22 min. The fable

All thirty, silent. The only help is the title read aloud. Each child reads The Goat Who Would Not Look (about 115 words) and answers sheet 78A, [[c1-A78a]] to [[c1-A78i]]. Collect the sheets when the time is up.

#### 20 min. The label

All thirty, silent, one sitting. Each child reads the six strips and writes one label for the book-swap box on sheet 78B, [[c1-A78j]] to [[c1-A78m]]. A small labeled drawing is allowed where it helps. There is no revising today. Collect the sheets.

#### 45 min. Oral window, first fifteen

Children 1 to 15, 3 minutes each, at your table; the rest read their own book, unmarked. Use set A and the passage Odo's Reading Spot. In order: the purpose and first reading, [[c1-A78q]]; the sentence with close, [[c1-A78s]]; the affix words, [[c1-A78n]]; the -tion and -ous words, [[c1-A78o]]; the unusual words, [[c1-A78p]]; then the reread, [[c1-A78r]]. A child with a clean recording skips the reread; write its date. Give no help beyond the stuck word, and collect each card after each child. A child not reached goes on the missing-evidence list for day 79.

After class, mark every ask and list the gaps by letter from the course register, A to Y. Day 80 acts on them.

### Other rooms

One child: the fable, the label and the oral window in order, about 60 minutes. No printer: copy the fable and strips large by hand. A scribe and pointing count for the reading items; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No fable sheet: use any short fable and ask your own questions on a plain page.
**Skip.** The oral window.

- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the fable and answers [[c1-A78a]] to [[c1-A78i]] alone.
  **Say.** "Show me the words that tell you." Expect: the child points to the line.
- **20 min. Assessment.** Your child reads the strips and writes the label, [[c1-A78j]] to [[c1-A78m]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear the oral window over two days only if every child is still heard once; no item is cut or made easier. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: fable, lesson, picture, label, topic, link. Frames: "The lesson is ___." "The picture shows ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used. Large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 79 is the second check day: a new article, and the second fifteen children heard. Day 78's sheets and records wait for day 80, which returns them with one written note and repairs each gap by letter. The strip and the cards stay in the folder.

### Credit

Ours: written for Curio Class.
