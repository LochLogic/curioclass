# Day 76: Check: the tale, a fresh fable and the frog card

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the fable and the frog card sheet for each child. Put a dictionary on each table. Write the five names for the recordings and set the exit strip.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back day 75's strip, s02-X75 and s02-X75b, and day 75's assessment, s02-A91 to s02-A104, with one written note each. Repair error AE (an irregular plural kept with a regular ending): check the whole word in the dictionary, [[s02-P76-1]]. *7 min.* Retrieval only: read the prefix and suffix words of the week and say one in a sentence, [[s02-P76-2]].

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Slates: a comma in speech, [[s02-P76-3]], and an exact word for a general one, [[s02-P76-4]].

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Silent, with one log line, [[s02-P76-5]].

#### 10 min. Narrative audit (76A)

Silent. Each child lays out the day 52 tale with its note (day 53) and revision (day 56) and answers [[s02-A112]] to [[s02-A118]] from it.

#### 20 min. The fresh fable (76B)

Silent, unscored. Each child reads Part 2 of The Frogs Who Wished for a King (about 286 words, 4 minutes); the only help is the title line read aloud. Then [[s02-A105]] and [[s02-A106]] (3 minutes), [[s02-A107]] (3), [[s02-A108]] (4), [[s02-A109]] and [[s02-A110]] (6). 

#### 15 min. Final recordings (76C)

Five children make their final recordings of the poem with the display, 3 minutes each, [[s02-A111]]. The other twenty-five draft the frog card on paper, [[s02-P76-6]] to [[s02-P76-9]]: a tale's message, one word and its meaning, one book fact with its page, and a labeled picture.

#### 10 min. Card publication

Each child makes the final copy of the frog card. Say once, as a review from grade 1, day 63: "Imagined means made up in a tale. Sourced means the book says it." The tale's message goes in the box headed From the tale, the fact in the box headed From the book. Credit the tale on the card.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X76]], then [[s02-X76b]] on the page. Shown: each part labeled, the picture detail named. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Does the book say it?" Not yet seen: a made-up cause called a source fact (error A), or a detail that does not match (error K). Collect the sheets, cards and strips when the exit is done.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult leaves the table. No device: record the recording "not yet seen".

### At home

**Swap.** No fable copy: use any short fable. No device: skip the recording.
**Skip.** The recordings and the dictionary.

- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s02-A112]] to [[s02-A118]] from the tale, then reads the fable and answers [[s02-A105]] to [[s02-A110]] alone.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child tells you the message of one tale.
  **Say.** "Where in the text is it?" Expect: the child points to the lines.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X76]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the long words part. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Tale, fable, message, imagined, sourced, label. Large print; a scribe for the card text; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 77 returns the day 76 strip, [[s02-X76]] and [[s02-X76b]], and repairs error K first: match each picture detail to a line. The sheets and cards come back with notes on day 77. The board and slate work, [[s02-P76-1]], [[s02-P76-3]] and [[s02-P76-4]], and the spoken work, [[s02-P76-2]], stay in the folder. The card pieces, [[s02-P76-6]] to [[s02-P76-9]], go in the frog book. Check: [[s02-A105]] to [[s02-A118]].
