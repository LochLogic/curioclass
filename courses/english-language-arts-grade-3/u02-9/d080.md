# Day 80: Respond to Check 1

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark day 78's and day 79's items and write one note on each child's sheet naming the one gap to fix first. Sort the children into the groups below by letter from the course register. Print the group cards and the exit strip. Hear any child still not heard in the oral window, at the start of Word groups.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return day 78's and day 79's sheets with the note. Each child reads the note and picks the group it names. Nothing here is scored again.

#### 25 min. Reading groups

Small groups by gap, 4 minutes each, and the rest of the class reads on. Use the cards. Text questions, [[c1-P80-1]]: copy the words that tell. Lesson, [[c1-P80-2]], spoken. Nonliteral words, [[c1-P80-3]]. Order and cause, [[c1-P80-4]]. Headings and index, [[c1-P80-5]], spoken. Word meaning from the sentence, [[c1-P80-6]]. Teach one move in each group, then the child tries the card on the board.

#### 20 min. Word groups

Small groups by gap, 4 minutes each. Parts that add meaning, [[c1-P80-7]], spoken. Words that are not the way they look, [[c1-P80-8]], spoken. Rereading so it sounds like talking, [[c1-P80-9]], spoken. Teach one move, then each child tries the card.

#### 25 min. Revise the label

Each child reads the returned note on the label from day 78 and changes one part the note names, [[c1-P80-10]] on sheet 80A. The teacher circulates and conferences with the children whose note named the topic sentence or the closing.

#### 10 min. Exit

Hand out the strip. The strip holds one ask, [[c1-X80]]. Then children answer [[c1-P80-11]] in the exit box on the page. Children answer alone, in silence.

#### 5 min. Close

Children put the sheets in their folder. Say one thing a child did better today than on day 78.

### Other rooms

One child: the note, the one move, the card, the label and the exit in order, about 70 minutes. A scribe and pointing count; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheets: reread your returned label and fix one part.
**Skip.** The group cards.

- **15 min. Fix one thing.** Your child reads the note and fixes the part it names on sheet 80A, [[c1-P80-10]].
- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads from their own book and tells you one thing it said.
  **Say.** "Where in the book does it say so?" Expect: the child points to the words.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[c1-X80]] on paper, then [[c1-P80-11]] in the exit box, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: take only the group the child's note names. Longer: none; the day is full.

### Words and supports

Words: lesson, label, heading, index, topic sentence, closing. Frames: "The note says ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used.

### Tomorrow

Day 81 begins unit 3: long words, then reading chapter 1 of the new book. Nothing from this check is taught there. The exit slip stays with you for the course record; the sheets stay in the folder.
