# Day 75: Check: publish a line and the language card

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the language card for each child. Put a beginning dictionary on each table. Open the shared page at the device with a peer comment waiting under each line. Write one note on each day 74 sheet tonight. Set the exit strip and the record sheet.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back day 74's strip, s02-X74b, s02-X74 and s02-X74c, and day 74's assessment, s02-A77 to s02-A90, with one written note each. Repair error V (a page opened at random): say what you do first to find webbing, [[s02-P75-1]]. *7 min.* Regrouping practice, no new pattern: groups by the pattern each missed, base marked first, [[s02-P75-4]].

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Slates: a tense word, [[s02-P75-2]], and a conjunction, [[s02-P75-3]]. Review: simple tenses, day 45; complex sentences, day 49.

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Silent from the log, with one log line, [[s02-P75-5]]. The teacher sets up the device.

#### 30 min. Publication rotations

Six groups of five take 4 minutes each at the one device, with 1 minute to change groups (30). Each child types one reply to the peer comment and saves, [[s02-A91]]. Review: you typed and saved on day 69. Give procedural help only. The other twenty-five reread their spelling and sentence pieces and the word lists silently. Watch for error AD: a child who watches a partner type.

#### 25 min. Language card

Silent, on paper, with a beginning dictionary on each table. In the first 2 minutes read the dictation sentence "The beetle was running beside the sparkling pond." aloud twice to everyone; children write it as item 11, [[s02-A102]]. The other items, [[s02-A92]] to [[s02-A101]], [[s02-A103]], [[s02-A120]] and [[s02-A104]], follow the card in order. Answer nothing about content; record help. The card is fresh.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X75]], then [[s02-X75b]] on the page. Shown: the corrected sentence, and the entry word they used. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the editor ask?" Not yet seen: a plural kept with a regular ending (error AE), or a guess with no entry checked (error G). Collect the card, the sheets and the strips when the exit is done.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult leaves the table and records any help. No device: record the typing items "not yet seen". No dictionary: record the dictionary items "not yet seen".

### At home

**Swap.** No device: type nothing. No dictionary: any word book.
**Skip.** The rotation.

- **25 min. Assessment.** Your child answers [[s02-A92]] to [[s02-A104]] alone, in silence. Read [[s02-A102]]'s sentence aloud twice.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the essay aloud.
  **Say.** "Which detail do you remember?" Expect: one detail from the essay.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X75]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the long words part, never the assessment. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Tense, conjunction, plural, dictionary, entry, publish, sentence. Large print; items read aloud except spelling and dictation; one-handed typing allowed; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 76 returns the day 75 strip, [[s02-X75]] and [[s02-X75b]], and repairs error AE first: check the dictionary entry for the whole word. The card and sheets come back with notes on day 76. The board work, [[s02-P75-2]] and [[s02-P75-3]], the spoken work, [[s02-P75-1]] and [[s02-P75-4]], and the log line, [[s02-P75-5]], stay in the folder. Check: [[s02-A91]] to [[s02-A104]] and [[s02-A120]].
