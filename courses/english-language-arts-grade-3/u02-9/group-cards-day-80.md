# Group cards, day 80 (teacher only)

One card for each group move on day 80. The reading cards are lines from unit 2 texts the class has read, never a check text and never the exit card; the word cards are made up for practice. Say so each time.

- **Reading groups, from the unit 2 texts.** Mud: Latona at the Pool, paragraph 7, "They stamped their feet. They kicked up mud. Soon the clear water turned thick and brown." King: The Frogs Who Wished for a King, paragraphs 3 and 4, with the moral line covered. Sun: Latona at the Pool, paragraph 2, "The sun beat down on the stones." Eggs: the frog book, page 12, the three sentences on laying, hatching and dropping into the stream. Toads: the frog book's contents and the index on page 19. Parched: Latona at the Pool, paragraph 4, "Please, may I have a drink? My children and I are parched."
- **Word groups.** Affix cards: unlock, painful, replant. Words that are not the way they look: said, could, people. Reread card: "The rain stopped. Then the sun came out."
- **Label group.** Each child's own label from day 78, with the returned note.
