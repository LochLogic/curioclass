# Group cards, day 80 (teacher only)

One card for each group move on day 80. Each card is new, never a check text and never the exit card. Say each time: "This card is made up for practice."

- **Reading groups.** Beans: "The class planted beans. The beans grew because the sun was warm." Fox: "Fox kept boasting, so nobody asked him to play." Rain: Dad said, "It is raining cats and dogs." Seeds: "First the seeds were sown. Next rain fell. Last the sprouts came up." Headings page: the headings Rain, Wind and Snow, and the index entry rain 4, 7. Sailors: "The sailors were weary. They sat down and rested."
- **Word groups.** Affix cards: unlock, painful, replant. Words that are not the way they look: said, could, people. Reread card: "The rain stopped. Then the sun came out."
- **Label group.** Each child's own label from day 78, with the returned note.
