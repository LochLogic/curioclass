# Day 73: Revise with a peer note and a teacher note

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and the editing checklist for each child. Set the shared page open at the device with the line list. Lay each child's folder with the day 72 slip on top. Write the model sentence and the three joins on the board, covered. Pick the three children for practice clips from the recording list; the other two dated children wait for day 76. Set the strip for the exit.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back day 72's strip, s02-X72 and s02-X72b. Repair error Q (a closing on a new topic): pairs say which sentence closes the explanation, [[s02-P73-1]]. *7 min.* Week 15. Dictate two words and mark each base, [[s02-P73-2]]; then read three words and name each base, [[s02-P73-3]]. Review: you met -ment, -ous, -tion and -ness words on days 69 and 70.

#### 10 min. Device turns (skills conferences)

Ten children each take one minute at the one device. Each uses the day 72 note to change the typed line and saves it, [[s02-P73-4]]. The teacher stands at the device and writes what changed. The others read from their logs. Review: you typed and saved a line on day 69.

#### 10 min. Independent reading and recording

Three children make a practice clip of the poem with its display, 3 minutes each, [[s02-P73-5]]. The rest read and write one log line, [[s02-P73-6]]. One minute to settle. The two other dated children make a written display and scene note and record on day 76.

#### 15 min. Reading and reread

*3 min.* Retrieve an exact word on slates, [[s02-P73-7]]. Review: you changed a general word on day 72. *2 min.* **Ask.** "What will the added words tell us?" Read the board sentence, "The tadpoles drop from the leaf.", then show the revision, [[s02-K73-1]]. *6 min.* Pairs reread the revised sentence and point to the words first, [[s02-P73-8]]. Expect: from the leaf into the stream, and when the rain comes. *4 min.* **Ask.** "What did the changed clause clarify?" Expect: when the tadpoles drop and where they land. Page 12 of the book says it too.

#### 10 min. Word and sentence work

*4 min.* Model joining two sentences with and, then with because, [[s02-K73-2]], and test each join: does the second idea belong with the first? Review: you wrote complex sentences with because and when on day 49. Then model a precise word, [[s02-K73-3]]. *6 min.* Each child joins two sentences in the essay and underlines the joining word, [[s02-P73-9]], and changes one general word to a precise one, [[s02-P73-10]]. Watch for error AB: a joining word that links unrelated ideas.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 2 minutes a child. Read your sentences with the peer note from day 72. The partner asks one question that links to the note, [[s02-P73-11]]; the child says what the answer changed, waiting for the partner to finish first, [[s02-P73-12]]. The teacher hears a rotating six and marks the heard-six column (4 minutes). Three volunteers share their change with the class, 2 minutes each (6 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Writing

Twenty children, the twenty who did not have a skills turn, each take a one-minute device turn in order, [[s02-P73-4]]; with the ten from the skills part, that is 30 turns, one for every child. The ten who had a skills turn start the paper revision at once. Everyone else revises the essay on paper, the day 71 body paragraph included, from the day 72 note: change the meaning, not only the words, [[s02-P73-13]]. Then edit with the checklist: plurals that change, [[s02-P73-14]], and one noun for an idea, [[s02-P73-15]]. Watch for error AC: the paragraph copied again with no change. Review: you met plurals that change and idea nouns on days 57 and 59.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X73b]], then [[s02-X73]] and [[s02-X73c]] on the page. Shown: one revised sentence with a precise word and a linked cause, and a question that links to the peer note. Shown with help: right after one prompt, "What does the peer note ask?" Not yet seen: a copy with no change (error AC), a link between unrelated ideas (error AB), or a question that ignores the note (error AM). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the partner and asks the linked question. No device: type nothing; write the changed line on paper and record "typing not yet seen". No printer: copy the checklist onto the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: write the changed line on paper. No log book: any animal book.
**Skip.** The device turns and the clips.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the essay aloud and shows you the day 72 note.
  **Say.** "What does the note ask you to change?" Expect: a sentence or a word named in the note.
- **20 min. Write.** Your child revises, [[s02-P73-13]], and joins two sentences, [[s02-P73-9]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X73b]] and [[s02-X73]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision, one joined pair and the exit. Longer: revise a second paragraph with a second note.

### Words and supports

Revise, peer, note, precise, clause, join, clarify, collection, amazement. Frame: "I changed ___ because the note said ___." Large print; a scribe for the essay, not for the typing; the notes read aloud; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 74 returns the day 73 strip, [[s02-X73b]], [[s02-X73]] and [[s02-X73c]], and repairs error AB first: test the link, do the two ideas belong together. Day 74 is a check, silent on paper: children bring the folder with the revised essay, the dated notes and the categorized notes. The board and slate work, [[s02-P73-1]], [[s02-P73-2]], [[s02-P73-7]], [[s02-K73-1]], [[s02-K73-2]] and [[s02-K73-3]], the spoken work, [[s02-P73-3]], [[s02-P73-5]], [[s02-P73-8]], [[s02-P73-11]] and [[s02-P73-12]], and the sheets, [[s02-P73-6]], [[s02-P73-10]], [[s02-P73-13]], [[s02-P73-14]] and [[s02-P73-15]], stay in the folder. The joined sentences, [[s02-P73-9]], and the typed line, [[s02-P73-4]], stay on the page and in the folder.
