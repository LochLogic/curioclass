# Day 77: Repair, discuss and compile the frog book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page, the discussion slip and an evidence slip for each child. Write on each evidence slip the commonest letter from the checks and one repair. Set one repair card for each letter. Mark the six groups of five. Print the typed pages beforehand; the device is not used today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Long words

*3 min.* Hand back day 76's strip, s02-X76 and s02-X76b. Repair error K (a picture detail that does not match the line): choose the picture that matches, [[s02-P77-1]]. *7 min.* Week 16. Dictate two words and mark each base, [[s02-P77-2]]; read three words and name each base, [[s02-P77-3]]. Review: you met -ment, -ous, -tion and -ness words on days 69, 70 and 73.

#### 20 min. Rereading and grammar

*5 min.* Grammar retrieval on slates: the word that compares two, [[s02-P77-4]]. *15 min.* Partner rereading of page 12 with the feedback card, no speed target, [[s02-P77-5]]. The teacher listens to three pairs for 3 minutes each. A clean individual recording from day 76 may stand as the winter sample; day 79 hears only missing samples.

#### 10 min. Return evidence and feedback

*3 min.* Hand back day 76's assessment, s02-A105 to s02-A118, and one slip a child with the commonest letter from the checks and one repair. *4 min.* Model one repair on a fresh strip, [[s02-K77-1]]: the claim has no source, so find the page by the index. *3 min.* Children go to the repair card for the letter on their slip. The cards cover error A, AA, AB, AC, AD, AE, AF, AG, AH, B, C, D, E, G, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, U, V, W, X, Y and Z.

#### 30 min. Formal discussion, with the repair and the compile running at the same time

Six groups of five take 5 minutes each at the teacher's table, 1 minute a child. **Ask.** "How does where a frog lives shape how it looks and acts?" Each child states one marked source detail and says where it is, [[s02-P77-6]], waiting for their turn, [[s02-P77-7]]. The next child asks a linked question, [[s02-P77-8]]; the first answers with a detail, [[s02-P77-9]]. Each child then writes on a slip what the talk clarified, [[s02-P77-10]]. Expect: the leaf, the stream and the green color working together. The teacher marks five boxes: detail, turn, linked question, answer, what became clearer. Any child may pass. The other twenty-five apply the one repair from their slip to the essay or the card (20 minutes), [[s02-P77-11]], then place their finished pages in the book (10 minutes). Watch for error AC: a page copied again with no change.

#### 15 min. Compile and share

Put the pages in order with a contents page and a cover. Pairs reread one page and say which source detail changed their explanation, [[s02-P77-12]]. Class share. The whole class takes part.

#### 5 min. Exit

Hand out the strip to every child and read it aloud twice; children write alone, [[s02-X77]], then [[s02-X77b]] and [[s02-X77c]] on the page. Shown: a sentence with a source detail, a named repair, and how the page is clearer. Shown with help: after one prompt, "Where does the source say it?" Not yet seen: a claim with no source (error A), a recopy (error AC), or no part in the work (error AD). Record on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: the adult is the discussion partner; the child reads the whole book aloud once and the adult writes one sentence about what became clearer. No printer: write the slips on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No book copy: use the child's essay.
**Skip.** The groups and the page 12 reread.

- **15 min. Talk.** Your child tells you one source detail and where it is.
  **Say.** "How does where a frog lives shape how it looks and acts?" Expect: a detail about the leaf or the stream.
- **15 min. Repair.** Your child writes what the talk clarified, [[s02-P77-10]], and repairs one page, [[s02-P77-11]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers [[s02-X77]] on paper, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the discussion and the exit. Longer: write a second repair with a more exact word.

### Words and supports

Repair, clarify, source, detail, contents, cover, week 16 words. Frames: "The source says ___ on ___." and "I repaired ___ so the page ___." Large print; a scribe for the page; oral rehearsal before the discussion; record any help.

### Tomorrow

Day 78 is a check on new texts and needs nothing from this unit; the book stays in the room and each child keeps a copy of their own page. It returns the day 77 strip, [[s02-X77]], [[s02-X77b]] and [[s02-X77c]], and repairs error A first: point to the sentence that gives the detail. The board and slate work, [[s02-P77-1]], [[s02-P77-2]] and [[s02-P77-4]], the spoken work, [[s02-P77-3]], [[s02-P77-5]], [[s02-P77-6]], [[s02-P77-7]], [[s02-P77-8]], [[s02-P77-9]], [[s02-P77-12]] and [[s02-K77-1]], and the slip and repair, [[s02-P77-10]] and [[s02-P77-11]], stay in the folder.
