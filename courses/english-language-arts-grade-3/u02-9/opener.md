# Section 2.9 opener: revising, language and the frog book (days 73 to 77)

## What the section does

The class asks linked questions, revises with a peer and a teacher note, checks language with a card and a dictionary, separates imagined from sourced, and compiles the frog book.

## How the days run

Each day is 90 minutes: reading and research, writing, a skills block and a five-minute exit. The exits are one ask each and unscored; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- A question links to the note it follows.
- Revision changes something; copying does not.
- Imagined and sourced parts are labelled.

## The errors, in plain words

- **AB:** A conjunction or linking word joins unrelated ideas. Repair: test the link: do the two ideas belong together. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **V:** A search ignores useful text features. Repair: pick the heading or page before opening. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **AE:** An irregular plural retains its singular form. Repair: check the dictionary entry for the whole word. 3 minutes, next day's opening.
- **K:** A display does not clarify the recording. Repair: match each picture detail to a line. 3 minutes, next day's opening.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet, dated. Exits are unscored.
