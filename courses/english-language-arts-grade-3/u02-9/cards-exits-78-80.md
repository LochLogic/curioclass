# Exit cards, days 78 to 80

Only day 80 has an exit; days 78 and 79 hold the check items themselves. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card. Plain black type on white. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv and wrongs.csv, not here.

- **Day 80 (c1-X80, with c1-P80-11 in the exit box on the student page).** This card is invented for practice. Card text (print it above the ask, once): "Mr. Diaz made a quiet corner in the hall. A rug keeps the corner soft and quiet. A lamp makes it easy to read. Children who need quiet go there for five minutes. Then they walk back to class." Ask one: Read the card. Why is there a rug? Copy the words that tell you. Ask two, printed in the exit box on the student page and not on the strip (c1-P80-11): Join the two ideas with a linking word: The corner has a rug. The corner has a lamp.
