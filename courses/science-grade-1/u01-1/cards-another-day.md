# Another day's shadow marks (day 3)

One set for each pair of children (15 sets). Say aloud before they start: "These cards are invented. A real saved record can take their place if our class kept one." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. If you hold a saved record of your own class's stick from another sunny day, use it instead and say which day it is from.

**Set.** Three cards in a row, each showing a pencil in clay on a windowsill with a date box and a small clock picture:
- Card 1, early in the day: a shadow dot on the left spot.
- Card 2, later: a shadow dot on the middle spot.
- Card 3, later still: a shadow dot on the right spot.
The date on this set is a different day from day 2's record, so the date boxes differ. The cards are mixed up for the children to put in order.

**How the pair uses them.** Put the three cards in order by the clock pictures, lay them beside day 2's record and say whether the dots went the same way. Key: yes, left, middle, right, both days. The teacher names it: the same thing happens again and again, a pattern. If your stick runs the other way, the set can be turned, and the teacher says "ours is the other way round."
