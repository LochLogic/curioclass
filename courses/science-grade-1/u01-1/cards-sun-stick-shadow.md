# Sun, stick and shadow cards (day 2)

Three cards in a row for each group. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. The cards run left to right; our stick may run the other way, and that is fine.

**Card S, the sun.** A round sun with short straight rays. An arrow from it points toward the right.
**Card T, the stick.** A pencil standing upright in a lump of clay. Beneath it, a ground line.
**Card W, the shadow.** A flat dark shape lying along the ground line, long and thin.

**How the group uses them.** Lay them left to right as sun, stick, shadow, with the sun on the left and the shadow on the right. Then turn the whole row so the shadow is on the left: the sun card moves to the right of the stick. The group says: "The shadow is on the side away from the sun, because the stick blocks the sunlight." For each of the group's two dots, the group lays the cards so the shadow matches the dot, and points to where the sun card goes.
