# Exit cards, days 1 to 4, and the weather strip: teacher key (days 1, 2, 3, 4)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-exits-1-4.md.

- Day 1 retrieval strip (not an exit) / Spoken: **Key:** sunny (the first and third squares). A miss: match the two sunny pictures together, record today's weather once.
- Day 1 cards: the lamp-post / Spoken, item s01-X1a: **Key:** the shadow. Wrong: "the sun stays put, so nothing changed" is error A. Touching the post is shown with help.
- Day 1 cards: the lamp-post / Spoken, item s01-X1b: **Key:** the pole, the sun, an arrow for the light and a shadow on the lawn; where the sun goes is the child's own idea.
- Day 1 cards: the lamp-post / Spoken, item s01-X1c: **Key:** will the swing's shadow move again tomorrow. Wrong: the fun card or the color card has no letter (watching cannot answer fun; one look tells the color).
- Day 2 cards: the bench and the fence post / Spoken, item s01-X2a: **Key:** the sun on the left.
- Day 2 cards: the bench and the fence post / Spoken, item s01-X2c: **Key:** three different times well apart, with the post kept in one place. Wrong: three looks at one moment is error A.
- Day 2 cards: the bench and the fence post / Spoken, item s01-X2b: **Key:** the left spot. Wrong: the middle, "it stays" is error A. The right, "it goes back," has no letter.
- Day 3 cards: the sandbox pole / Spoken, item s01-X3a: **Key:** the right spot. Wrong: the middle, "it stays where it is" is error A.
- Day 3 cards: the sandbox pole / Spoken, item s01-X3b: **Key:** I think it is on the right, because last week it went left, middle, right. Wrong: "because the sun stays there" is error A; "because I want it" has no letter.
- Day 4 card: the pale moon (item s01-X4c) / Spoken: **Key:** yes. Wrong: no, "the moon comes out only at night" is error C.
