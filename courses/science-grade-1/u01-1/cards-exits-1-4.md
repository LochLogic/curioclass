# Exit cards, days 1 to 4, and the weather strip

Each exit has picture cards that every child touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Children and grown-ups drawn have different skin tones and hair, and no names. The cards run left to right. Our stick may run the other way round; if so, say "ours is the other way round." Each drawing is its own view: never ask children to compare directions between two cards. The day 4 shadow card and the day 4 moon-strip card are in cards-held-back-day4.md, not here.

Ground spots, wherever they appear: three round places on one ground line, called left, middle and right. The left and right spots are far from the post; the middle spot is close beside it. A shadow dot sits on one of them.

## Day 1 retrieval strip (not an exit)

**Strip.** Three squares in a row: a sun, a cloud, a sun. Date boxes under each.

**Spoken:** "This strip is invented for practice. Touch the weather that happened twice." **Key:** sunny (the first and third squares). A miss: match the two sunny pictures together, record today's weather once.

## Day 1 cards: the lamp-post

**Card 1a, morning.** A lamp-post at a bus stop (a plain shelter and a bench). Its shadow lies on the ground to the left of the post. A clock picture and a date box in the corner. A small girl stands by the shelter.
**Card 1b, after lunch.** The same post, shelter and girl. The shadow lies on the ground to the right of the post. A clock picture and a date box in the corner.
**Three touch cards:** the post alone; the shadow alone (a flat dark shape); the bus stop shelter alone.

**Spoken, item s01-X1a:** "Touch what is different on the two cards." **Key:** the shadow. Wrong: "the sun stays put, so nothing changed" is error A. Touching the post is shown with help.

**Spoken, item s01-X1b:** the same two cards, with a date box and a small drawn clock under each. "The girl wrote the date and drew a clock under each drawing. Say why." **Key:** so we know when each was made and which came first.

## Day 2 cards: the bench and the fence post

**Card 2a.** A school bench in a yard. Its shadow lies on the ground to the left of the bench.
**Three touch cards:** the sun drawn on the left of the bench; the sun drawn on the right of the bench; the sun drawn straight above the bench.

**Spoken, item s01-X2a:** "Touch where the sun is." **Key:** the sun on the right.

**Card 2b.** Two small cards and a blank row. Small card one: a fence post with a dot on the left ground spot (a clock picture with a morning sky). Small card two: the same post with a dot on the middle ground spot (a clock picture a little later). The blank row: three ground spots in a row, no dots, so no spot is marked as the answer.
**Three touch cards:** a dot on the left spot; a dot on the middle spot; a dot on the right spot.

**Spoken, item s01-X2b:** "On the blank row, touch where the third dot goes." **Key:** the right spot. Wrong: the middle, "it stays" is error A. The left, "it goes back," has no letter.

## Day 3 cards: the sandbox pole

**Strip 1, kept from last week.** A pole in a sandbox with a dot on the left spot, a dot on the middle spot and a dot on the right spot. A date box.
**Strip 2, from yesterday.** The same pole, as three small cards: a dot on the left spot; a dot on the middle spot; a flap card for the third look. A date box. Below it, a blank row of three ground spots with no dots and no flap, so no spot is marked as the answer.
**Three touch cards:** left; middle; right.

**Spoken, item s01-X3a:** "On the blank row, touch where the covered dot is." **Key:** the right spot. Wrong: the middle, "it stays where it is" is error A.

**Spoken, item s01-X3b:** the same two strips. "Say: I think ___, because I saw ___." **Key:** I think it is on the right, because last week it went left, middle, right. Wrong: "because the sun stays there" is error A; "because I want it" has no letter.

## Day 4 card: the pale moon (item s01-X4c)

**Card 4c.** A rooftop with a chimney, drawn at lunchtime: a bright sky, the sun with short rays beside a pale moon drawn as a round shape with a dotted edge. A clock picture reading midday.
**Two touch cards:** a tick (yes); a cross (no).

**Spoken:** "A pale moon is over a rooftop at lunchtime, and the sun is shining beside it. Can the moon be in the day sky? Touch yes or no." **Key:** yes. Wrong: no, "the moon comes out only at night" is error C.
