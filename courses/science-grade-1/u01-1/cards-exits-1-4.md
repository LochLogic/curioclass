# Exit cards, days 1 to 4, and the weather strip

Each exit has picture cards that every child touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Children and grown-ups drawn have different skin tones and hair, and no names. The cards run left to right, except where a card says it runs right to left. Our stick may run the other way round; if so, say "ours is the other way round." Each drawing is its own view: never ask children to compare directions between two cards. The day 4 shadow card and the day 4 moon-strip card are in cards-held-back-day4.md, not here.

Ground spots, wherever they appear: three round places on one ground line, called left, middle and right. The left and right spots are far from the post; the middle spot is close beside it. A shadow dot sits on one of them.

## Day 1 retrieval strip (not an exit)

**Strip.** Three squares in a row: a sun, a cloud, a sun. Date boxes under each.

**Spoken:** "This strip is invented for practice. Touch the weather that happened twice." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

## Day 1 cards: the lamp-post

**Card 1a, morning.** A lamp-post at a bus stop (a plain shelter and a bench). Its shadow lies on the ground to the left of the post. A clock picture and a date box in the corner. A small girl stands by the shelter.
**Card 1b, after lunch.** The same post, shelter and girl. The shadow lies on the ground to the right of the post. A clock picture and a date box in the corner.
**Three touch cards:** the post alone; the shadow alone (a flat dark shape); the bus stop shelter alone.

**Spoken, item s01-X1a:** "Touch what is different on the two cards." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

**Spoken, item s01-X1b:** no cards. "Draw a flagpole on a school lawn, the sun, an arrow for its light, and the shadow." Children draw in the exit box on the student page. The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

**Card 1c.** A swing set in a sunny school yard, its shadow on the ground.
**Three touch cards:** a swing shadow with a small calendar showing tomorrow and a question mark (will the swing's shadow move again tomorrow?); a smiling child on a swing (is the swing fun?); a paint pot beside the swing (what color is the swing?). Read each card's question aloud as you show it.

**Spoken, item s01-X1c:** "Touch the question we can find out by watching the swing's shadow again and again." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

## Day 2 cards: the bench and the fence post

**Card 2a.** A school bench in a yard. Its shadow lies on the ground to the right of the bench.
**Three touch cards:** the sun drawn on the left of the bench; the sun drawn on the right of the bench; the sun drawn straight above the bench.

**Spoken, item s01-X2a:** "Touch where the sun is." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

**Card 2b.** Two small cards and a blank row. Small card one: a fence post with a dot on the right ground spot (a clock picture with a morning sky). Small card two: the same post with a dot on the middle ground spot (a clock picture a little later). The blank row: three ground spots in a row, no dots, so no spot is marked as the answer.
**Three touch cards:** a dot on the left spot; a dot on the middle spot; a dot on the right spot.

**Spoken, item s01-X2c:** no cards. "A new gate post stands by the fence. You will mark the tip of its shadow three times today. Say when you will look." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

**Spoken, item s01-X2b:** "On the blank row, touch where the third dot goes." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

## Day 3 cards: the sandbox pole

**Strip 1, kept from last week.** A pole in a sandbox with a dot on the left spot, a dot on the middle spot and a dot on the right spot. A date box.
**Strip 2, from yesterday.** The same pole, as three small cards: a dot on the left spot; a dot on the middle spot; a flap card for the third look. A date box. Below it, a blank row of three ground spots with no dots and no flap, so no spot is marked as the answer.
**Three touch cards:** left; middle; right.

**Spoken, item s01-X3a:** "On the blank row, touch where the covered dot is." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

**Spoken, item s01-X3b:** the same two strips. "Say: I think ___, because I saw ___." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

## Day 4 card: the pale moon (item s01-X4c)

**Card 4c.** A rooftop with a chimney, drawn at lunchtime: a bright sky, the sun with short rays low on one side, and a pale moon drawn as a round shape with a dotted edge on the far side of the sky. A clock picture reading midday.
**Two touch cards:** a tick (yes); a cross (no).

**Spoken:** "A pale moon is over a rooftop at lunchtime, and the sun is shining on the other side of the sky. Can the moon be in the day sky? Touch yes or no." The key is in key-cards-exits-1-4.md.
