# Day 3: Does it do it again?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out fifteen sets of dated shadow-mark cards, mixed up, four covered-dot strips and four which-side cards for each pair. Hold the sandbox-pole strips back. Choose today's six. Key: key-cards-another-day.md. Key: key-cards-exits-1-4.md.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; no safety steps. Say aloud that the cards are invented for practice.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Return and repair (review from kindergarten). Error A from day 2's exit: show the three dots of day 2 on the chart. All trace the order in the air while one child traces the chart, saying left, middle, right. Then hand back day 2 records.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

**Ask.** [[s01-P3-3]] "Say the order of the three dots, using the clock pictures." The class reads day 2's record aloud together.

#### 8 min. Another day's marks

Pairs put the three cards of another day in order by the clock pictures (2 minutes), lay them beside day 2's record and say whether the dots went the same way (3 minutes). Name it: the same thing happens again and again, a pattern (review). If our stick runs the other way, say "ours is the other way round." Hear the six, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Expect: yes, the same way.

#### 8 min. Rehearse a prediction

Say all four strips are invented. Strips 2 and 4 run the other way, so use the order of the first two dots.

**Ask.** [[s01-P3-1]] "Touch where the covered dot is, and say: I think ___, because I saw ___." Pairs take one strip at a time and lift the flap (4 minutes). Hear the six, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Expect: the next spot on, because the two dots went that way. The middle, "it stays": error A; lay the two dots in order and walk a finger on.

#### 4 min. Where is the sun?

**Ask.** [[s01-P3-2]] "Touch the side the sun is on, and say: opposite the shadow." Pairs take four cards (3 minutes). A pair that touches the shadow's side lays day 2's sun, stick and shadow cards under the card and slides the sun across. Hear the six, 10 seconds each.

#### 3 min. Add to the chart

Add: it did the same on two days. The class dictates; you scribe.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." **Ask.** [[s01-X3a]] "Touch where the covered dot is." All point. **Ask.** [[s01-X3b]] "Say: I think ___, because I saw ___." The six answer, 25 seconds each. Expect: right; because last week it went left, middle, right. The middle, or "the sun stays there": error A. "Because I want it": shown with help.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw dots on scrap paper and cover one with a sticky note.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw dots on paper; cover one with a sticky note.

**Skip.** The pairs and the chart.

- **6 min. Read it.** Your child says the order of the three dots from the first look.
- **8 min. Another day.** Draw three dots from another day's looks beside the first; ask if they went the same way.
  **Say.** "Did the dots go the same way?" Expect: yes. Say: that is a pattern.
- **8 min. Covered dot.** Draw a post with two dots; cover the third spot.
  **Say.** "I think ___, because I saw ___." Expect: the next spot on, because the dots went that way.
- **4 min. Which side?** Draw a tree with its shadow; ask where the sun is. Expect: opposite the shadow.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3a]] and [[s01-X3b]]: cover the third dot on a new post; ask where it is, and why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two strips, two cards. Longer: make a strip for a friend to predict.

### Words and supports

Pattern, again, covered, opposite. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error A, day 4 opens with Return and repair: the chart's three dated dots, a child runs a finger along them, the class says what the next dot would do. Day 4 puts timed moon pictures in order.
