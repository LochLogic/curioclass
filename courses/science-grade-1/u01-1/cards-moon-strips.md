# Timed moon pictures (day 4)

Six identical sets, one for each group of five, all invented. Say aloud: "These pictures are invented for practice. The moon does not rise at the same clock time every day." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines. Not to scale. Every picture shows one school view: the gym roof on the left, the flagpole in the middle, the far trees on the right. Each picture has a clock picture and a date box, and an adult reads the clock aloud. Mix the order in the row before handing out.

**One set is four pictures, in time order:**
1. A bright afternoon sky. A pale moon (a round shape with a dotted edge) low above the gym roof. Clock picture: three in the afternoon.
2. An evening sky, a little darker. The moon above the gym roof and the flagpole, higher. Clock picture: six in the evening.
3. A dark sky. The moon high above the flagpole. Clock picture: nine at night.
4. A dark sky. The moon lower above the far trees. Clock picture: midnight.

**The fallback look.** If the moon is not up in school hours, use one extra card: a pale moon low above the gym roof in a bright sky, a clock picture and a date box, said aloud to be a saved picture. The live look uses the moon log and the real landmarks, with backs to the sun and the sun screened by a building.
