# Which-side cards (day 3)

Four cards for each pair, all invented. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines. Each card shows one object in a yard with a flat dark shadow lying on the ground on one side. No sun is drawn. The pair touches the side the sun is on and says "opposite the shadow."

| Card | Object | Shadow lies | The sun is on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a tree | to the right | the left |
| 2 | a mailbox | to the left | the right |
| 3 | a kickball on grass | to the right | the left |
| 4 | a bicycle standing up | to the left | the right |

None of these is the bench of the day 2 exit. A pair that touches the shadow's side: lay the sun, stick and shadow cards from day 2 under the card and slide the sun to the other side.
