# Day 2: Where will the next dot go?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, fix six sticks (pencil upright in clay, base chalked) on taped paper shadow records by the door, one a group. Check that the sun reaches every spot at all three looks. Keep day 1's teacher morning tracing.

### Safety and set-up

Never look at the sun; eyes on the ground. Stay with the teacher. Clay and chalk stay out of mouths. Cloud: write "cloud" and say aloud the marks are not complete. Clean-up, 3 minutes: tub, wipes, sink wash with lunch.

### The day

#### 2 min. Look 1

At arrival. Groups take turns, 20 seconds each: one child chalks a dot at the shadow's tip. Read the clock once; groups draw the clock picture.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Return and repair is review from kindergarten. Error A: show day 1's tracing and its after-lunch dot in order; walk a finger from first to second: "the shadow moved." A third or fewer: a small group (2 minutes) while the rest get day 1 drawings back.

#### 5 min. Plan the three looks

Keep, change, watch (review): keep the stick, spot and paper; change only the time; watch the tip. Each group draws a landmark in the top box. Hear the six say what we keep, 15 seconds each.

#### 2 min. Look 2

Late morning. A different child chalks the dot.

#### 7 min. Which side is the sun?

Groups lay the cards sun, stick, shadow, then turn the row (2 minutes 30). Say: "The shadow lies on the side away from the sun, because the stick blocks the sunlight."

**Ask.** [[s01-P2-1]] "Where will the next dot be, and what makes you think so?" Each child draws the third dot in a new colour (1 minute 30), then tells a partner "I think ___, because I saw ___" (1 minute). Hear the six, 20 seconds each. Expect: past the second, because the first two went that way. The middle: error A.

#### 2 min. Look 3

After lunch. A third child chalks the dot.

#### 5 min. Check and revise

Children compare their drawn dot with the third; a miss is a result. Revise the class chart with the three dots and a sentence the class dictates.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented cards." **Ask.** [[s01-X2a]] "Touch where the sun is." All point. **Ask.** [[s01-X2b]] "Touch where the third dot goes." The six answer, 25 seconds each. Expect: the sun on the right; right. The middle, "it stays": error A. Left: shown with help.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Sticks, clay, chalk to the tub; wipes.

### Other rooms

No yard or sun: saved marks, said aloud to be from another day.

### At home

**Swap.** No yard: a sunny sill. No clay: tape a pencil to a cup. No cards: draw the sun, stick and shadow.

**Skip.** The groups, the cards and the chart.

- **6 min. Look 1.** In the morning stand the pencil on a paper; dot the shadow's tip and write the time.
- **6 min. Look 2.** Later, dot it again. Your child draws the third dot in a new colour on the student page.
  **Say.** "Where will the next dot be, and what makes you think so?" Expect: past the second, because the first two went that way.
- **6 min. Which side?** Draw the sun, stick and shadow. Never look at the sun.
  **Say.** "Where is the sun?" Expect: on the side away from the shadow.
- **6 min. Look 3.** After lunch, dot it and compare.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2a]] and [[s01-X2b]]: draw a bench with its shadow on one side; ask where the sun is. Then draw two dots on a post; ask where the third goes.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the landmark. Longer: find a post at school and say which side its shadow will be on after lunch.

### Words and supports

Look, dot, landmark, away from the sun. Pointing and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error A, day 3 opens with Return and repair: the three dated dots on the chart, a finger walked along them, the order said aloud. Day 3 compares another day's marks.
