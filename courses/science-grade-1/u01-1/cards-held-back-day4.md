# Held-back exit cards, day 4

Keep these out of sight until the day 4 exit. They are not used on days 1 to 3 or in the hook. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. The moon strip runs left to right; the birdhouse post runs right to left. Each drawing is its own view.

## The moon strip (item s01-X4a)

**Strip.** A field with a gate on the left, a windmill in the middle and a silo on the right. Three looks in a row:
- Look 1: the moon low above the gate, a clock picture.
- Look 2: the moon high above the windmill, a clock picture.
- Look 3: covered with a paper flap, a clock picture on the flap.
Not to scale; the clock times differ on purpose.
**Three touch cards:** the moon low above the gate; the moon high above the windmill; the moon lower above the silo.

**Spoken:** "Touch where the moon will be at look three." The key is in key-cards-held-back-day4.md.

## The birdhouse post (item s01-X4b)

**Cards.** Two small cards and a blank row. Small card one: a birdhouse on a post with a shadow dot on the right ground spot. Small card two: the same post with a shadow dot on the middle ground spot. The blank row: three ground spots in a row, no dots, so no spot is marked as the answer.
**Three touch cards:** left; middle; right.

**Spoken:** "On the blank row, touch where the next dot goes." The key is in key-cards-held-back-day4.md.
