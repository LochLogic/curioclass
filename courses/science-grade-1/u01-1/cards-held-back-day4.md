# Held-back exit cards, day 4

Keep these out of sight until the day 4 exit. They are not used on days 1 to 3 or in the hook. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. The cards run left to right. Each drawing is its own view.

## The moon strip (item s01-X4a)

**Strip.** A field with a gate on the left, a windmill in the middle and a silo on the right. Three looks in a row:
- Look 1: the moon low above the gate, a clock picture.
- Look 2: the moon high above the windmill, a clock picture.
- Look 3: a flap covering the picture.
**Three touch cards:** low above the gate; high above the windmill; lower above the silo. Not to scale; the clock times differ on purpose.

**Spoken:** "Touch where the moon will be at look three." **Key:** lower above the silo. Wrong: high above the windmill, "it stays" is error B. Low above the gate, "it goes back," has no letter.

## The birdhouse post (item s01-X4b)

**Card.** A birdhouse on a post, three ground spots in a row. A shadow dot on the left spot and a shadow dot on the middle spot. The right spot is empty.
**Three touch cards:** left; middle; right.

**Spoken:** "Touch where the next dot goes." **Key:** the right spot. Wrong: the middle, "it stays" is error A. The left has no letter.
