# Exit cards, days 5 and 6: teacher key (days 5, 6)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-exits-5-6.md.

- Day 5 / Item s01-X5a: **Key:** the sky is too bright to see them; they are still there. Wrong: "the stars went away" is error D.
- Day 5 / Item s01-X5b: **Key:** the clear dark night.
- Day 5 / Item s01-X5c: **Key:** the noon sky with the pale moon. Wrong: the night moon is error C; the noon sky with no moon has no letter.
- Day 6 / Item s01-X6a: **Key:** middle. Wrong: left, "it stayed" is error A; right has no letter.
- Day 6 / Item s01-X6b: **Key:** high above the barn. Wrong: low above the pond, "it stayed" is error B; lower above the windmill has no letter.
- Day 6 / Item s01-X6c: **Key:** no stars to see; the stars are still up there. Wrong: stars to see has no letter.
