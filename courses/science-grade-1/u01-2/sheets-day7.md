# Day 7 sky sheets

Held back until day 7. Say "these sheets are invented for practice." Five sheets plus a farm's star cards, one set for each group of five. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines. Each look has a clock picture.

**Sheets 1 and 2, the sun.** The same view: a tree on the left, a roof in the middle, a hill on the right. Two different days. The sun at 9 a.m. low above the tree, at noon high above the roof, at 3 p.m. lower above the hill.
**Sheet 3, the sun, covered.** The same view, a third day. 9 a.m. low above the tree, noon high above the roof; 3 p.m. covered.
**Sheet 4, the moon.** A pole on the left, a barn in the middle, a ridge on the right. 8 p.m. low above the pole, 11 p.m. high above the barn, 2 a.m. lower above the ridge.
**Sheet 5, the moon, covered.** 5 a.m. low above the pole, 8 a.m. high above the barn; 11 a.m. covered.
**A farm's star records.** Four cards, each with a sun or moon symbol in the corner to match the circle row on the response sheet: clear day, no stars; clear dark night, stars; clear day, no stars; clear dark night, stars. The next, a clear dark night, is covered.

Keys: E1 lower above the hill, from sheets 1 and 2. E2 lower above the ridge, from sheet 4; circling sheets 4 and 5 also meets; the morning moon is accepted as a moon. E3 stars; on the day records the stars are not seen, not gone. An extra circle on sheet 3 (E1) or on the day records (E3) still meets. Error letters: A for E1, B and C for E2, D for E3.
