# Another place's sky records (day 6)

Three stations, two sets of each station, six in all, all invented. Say aloud: "These are invented records from another school. Real records from another place have not been shown to us, so we say what the cards show." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, with a clock picture on each card. Each card is its own view. A flap covers the last card at each station.

**Station 1, the sun.** A flagpole at a hill school, three ground spots. This card runs right to left. Dot right (morning), dot middle (noon), the left spot covered (afternoon). The key is in key-cards-other-place.md.

**Station 2, the moon.** A church tower on the left, a bridge in the middle, a water tower on the right. Look one: the moon low above the church tower. Look two: high above the bridge. Look three covered. The key is in key-cards-other-place.md.

**Station 3, the stars.** A harbor school. A clear dark night with stars, a bright day with none, a second clear dark night with stars, and the next clear dark night covered. The key is in key-cards-other-place.md.

Each group predicts, says "I think ___, because I saw ___," and lifts the flap. Hear a rotating six. A group that predicts the opposite: lay the cards in order and walk a finger. Errors: A at station 1, B at station 2, D at station 3.
