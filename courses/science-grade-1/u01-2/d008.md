# Day 8: Fix it and try a fresh prediction

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 7, write short feedback on each response sheet and sort the sheets into at most four piles: error A and B, error C, error D, no repair. Set out the timed moon picture sequence, yarn, the day and night sky card sets, the fresh sky cards and prediction strips. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Feedback names what the pictures show.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return the day 7 sheets. Read the class feedback aloud; each child looks at their own feedback box, and an adult reads it to any child who asks. The six hear theirs, 20 seconds each. Send children to their table.

#### 10 min. Compare records and revise

Each table gets 2 minutes with you; others follow picture directions. Day 6's marks and strip stay on hand. Children revise their day 7 reason by pointing, retelling or redrawing. Note the revised reason on the sheet for up to six children a table, 20 seconds each. Revisions never change day 7's cells.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-1]] Error A and B: "Trace the pictures with a finger. Where did it go?" Lay yarn along the timed moon picture sequence. Expect: a new place each time.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-2]] Error C: "What do the daytime pictures show?" Expect: the moon in the day sky.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-3]] Error D: "The day card has no stars. What does that mean?" Expect: not seen, but still there. Use the day and night sky card sets. Start from day 7's items.

#### 10 min. Fresh predictions (unscored)

Say "These cards are invented for practice." Read each prompt aloud; children mark the prediction strip: sun 3 minutes, moon 2, stars 2. Hear six reasons. Then the exit, 3 minutes: **Ask.** [[s01-X8a]] "Touch where the sun will be at look three." [[s01-X8b]] "Touch what the card says to her." [[s01-X8c]] "Touch what the blank night shows." All touch; the six answer, two to an item. Expect: lower above the mill; the moon can be in the day sky; stars. High above the bridge: error A. "Only at night": error C. No stars: shown with help.

#### 5 min. Collect

Sheets and strips in. Read the question board once and close it. Scoring follows after class from the revised day 7 work.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the fresh cards on scrap paper. One adult: two tables, the rest on picture directions.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a sun low over a canal, high over a bridge, and a blank third place.

**Skip.** The tables, the sheets and the board.

- **6 min. Look back.** Trace any missed picture in order.
  **Say.** "Where did it go, picture by picture?" Expect: a new place each time.
- **6 min. The day moon.** Draw a pale moon over a bridge at two in the afternoon.
  **Say.** "Can the moon be in the day sky?" Expect: yes.
- **6 min. Stars.** Draw a bright day with no stars.
  **Say.** "What does no stars mean here?" Expect: not seen, but still there.
- **7 min. Try one.** Your child draws where the sun will be next.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8a]], [[s01-X8b]] and [[s01-X8c]]: ask where the sun goes next, whether the moon is only out at night, and what a blank clear dark night shows.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair only the groups that missed. Longer: predict a cloudy night's card and say what the cloud hides.

### Words and supports

Path, next, day sky, not seen, still there, evidence. Pointing, retelling and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Unit 1 ends here. Unit 8's day 9 opens the daylight question and starts a dated light-or-dark chart. Nothing from this day waits for a repair there.
