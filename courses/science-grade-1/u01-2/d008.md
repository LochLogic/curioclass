# Day 8: Fix it and try a fresh prediction

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 7, write short feedback on each sheet and sort into four piles: A and B, C, D, no repair. Set out the timed moon pictures, yarn, the sky card sets, the fresh cards and prediction strips. Choose today's six. Key: key-cards-day8-fresh.md. Key: key-cards-sky-day-night.md.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 7's assessment, read feedback, then revise (kindergarten review). Read the class feedback aloud; an adult reads any child's box on request. The six hear theirs, 20 seconds each.

#### 10 min. Compare records and revise

Each table gets 2 minutes with you for the repair ask only; others follow picture directions. Day 6's marks and strip stay on hand. Children revise their day 7 reason by pointing, retelling or redrawing. Note the revised reason on the sheet for two children a table; the rest you note from the sheet after class. Revisions never change day 7's cells.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-1]] Error A and B: "Trace the pictures with a finger. Where did it go?" Lay yarn along the timed moon picture sequence. Expect: a new place each time.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-2]] Error C: "What do the daytime pictures show?" Expect: the moon in the day sky.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-3]] Error D: "The day card has no stars. What does that mean?" Expect: not seen, but still there. Use the sky card sets.

#### 10 min. Fresh predictions (unscored)

Say "These cards are invented for practice." Show the three practice cards (the exit cards stay hidden). Read each prompt aloud; children mark the prediction strip: the practice sun card 2 minutes, the practice moon card 2; the practice star card is said aloud, 1 minute. Hear six reasons, 15 seconds each. Then show the exit cards; the exit, 3 minutes: **Ask.** [[s01-X8a]] "Touch where the sun was at look two." [[s01-X8b]] "What would you tell her? Touch one." [[s01-X8c]] "Touch what the blank night shows." All touch; the six answer, two to an item. Expect: the middle card, over the pine; the moon can be in the day sky; stars. Low above the cornfield, "it stayed": error A. "Only at night": error C. No stars: not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Collect

Sheets and strips in. Read the question board once and close it. Scoring follows after class from the revised day 7 work.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the fresh cards on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a sun low over a cornfield, a blank middle place, and the sun lower over a train station.

**Skip.** The tables, the sheets and the board.

- **6 min. Look back.** Trace any missed picture in order.
  **Say.** "Where did it go, picture by picture?" Expect: a new place each time.
- **6 min. The day moon.** Draw a pale moon over a bridge at two in the afternoon.
  **Say.** "Can the moon be in the day sky?" Expect: yes.
- **6 min. Stars.** Draw a bright day with no stars.
  **Say.** "What does no stars mean here?" Expect: not seen, but still there.
- **7 min. Try one.** Your child draws where the sun will be next.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8a]], [[s01-X8b]] and [[s01-X8c]]: ask where the sun was at the covered middle look, whether the moon is only out at night, and what a blank clear dark night shows.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair only the groups that missed. Longer: predict a cloudy night's card and say what the cloud hides.

### Words and supports

Path, next, day sky, not seen, still there, evidence. Pointing, retelling and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Unit 1 ends here. Unit 8's day 9 opens the daylight question and starts a dated light-or-dark chart. Nothing from this day waits for a repair there.
