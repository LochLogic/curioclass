# Fresh cards, day 8

Unscored. Say "these cards are invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines. Each card is its own view.

## Practice cards (the prediction strip; not the exit)

Three practice cards, shown before the exit cards, which stay out of sight until the exit.

**Practice sun card.** A pond on the left, a shed in the middle, a barn on the right. Look one: the sun low above the pond. Look two: high above the shed. Look three covered. Children draw the sun in each box of the prediction strip.

**Practice moon card.** A playground: swings on the left, a slide in the middle, a fence on the right. Look one, 7 a.m.: a pale moon high above the swings. Look two, 9 a.m.: lower above the slide. Look three, 11 a.m., covered. Children draw the moon in each box of the prediction strip.

**Practice star card.** A desert school's records: a clear dark night with stars, a sunny day with none marked, a clear dark night with stars, and a sunny day covered. Children say what the covered day shows: no stars to see, though they are still up there.

## Exit cards

**Item s01-X8a.** A sun strip: a cornfield on the left, a tall pine in the middle, a train station on the right. Look one: the sun low above the cornfield. Look two covered. Look three: the sun lower above the train station. Touch cards: low above the cornfield; high above the tall pine; lower above the train station. The key is in key-cards-day8-fresh.md.

**Item s01-X8b.** A pale moon high over a bridge at two in the afternoon, a clock picture. A girl says the moon is only out at night. Spoken: "What would you tell her? Touch one." Touch cards: the moon can be in the day sky; the moon is out only at night. The key is in key-cards-day8-fresh.md.

**Item s01-X8c.** A hill-top station: a clear dark night with stars, a clear day with nothing marked, a clear dark night covered. Touch cards: stars, no stars. The key is in key-cards-day8-fresh.md.
