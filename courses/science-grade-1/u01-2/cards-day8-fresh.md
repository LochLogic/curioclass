# Fresh cards, day 8

Unscored. Say "these cards are invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines. Each card is its own view.

**Item s01-X8a.** A sun strip: a canal on the left, a bridge in the middle, a mill on the right. Look one: the sun low above the canal. Look two: high above the bridge. Look three covered. Touch cards: low above the canal; high above the bridge; lower above the mill. **Key:** lower above the mill. Wrong: high above the bridge is error A.

**Item s01-X8b.** A pale moon high over a bridge at two in the afternoon, a clock picture. A girl says the moon is only out at night. Touch cards: the moon can be in the day sky; the moon is out only at night. **Key:** the moon can be in the day sky. Wrong: the other is error C.

**Item s01-X8c.** A hill-top station: a clear dark night with stars, a clear day with nothing marked, a clear dark night covered. Touch cards: stars, no stars. **Key:** stars. Wrong: no stars is error D.
