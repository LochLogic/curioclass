# Day 7: Show what we know about the sky

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set a group of five at each of six tables with the day 7 sky sheets. Give each child a response sheet. Keep the class pattern chart from day 6 on the wall. Put the day and night sky card sets out for waiting children. Ask a helper adult to move round. Open the record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Children may pass; a partner may point. Calm sheets for children who fear the dark.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "These sheets are invented for practice. Use the pictures. I will not help with the answers." Read the three prompts: [[s01-E1]] "Where will the sun be at 3 p.m. on sheet 3? Draw its path, mark the covered place and point to the sheet that shows you." [[s01-E2]] "Where will the moon be at 11 a.m. on sheet 5?" [[s01-E3]] "Touch what the next clear dark night will show, and point to the records that tell you."

#### 6 min. Paper responses

Two minutes an item, prompts read aloud again. Each child draws the path on the response sheet, marks the covered place, and touches the sheet that shows it. Nothing is scored or fed back.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six groups of five, three minutes each. Each child gives one item's reason aloud, 30 seconds: S, M and T rotate through the group. Write the spoken reason in the feedback box in checklist words or a short note. Mark each look-for on the record sheet: S shown, H shown with help, N not yet seen. Only the first unaided try counts; write H when you steadied or read a card; leave a cell blank if you did not see it. The other items are marked from the sheet. Note error A for a sun that stays, error B for a moon that stays, error C for a morning moon rejected, error D for stars gone by day. Waiting children use the day and night sky card sets quietly; the helper moves round.

#### 3 min. Collect

Sheets in; give no feedback. Day 7 reteaches nothing.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the sheets on scrap paper. One projector: show one sheet at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheets: draw three sun pictures over a tree, roof and hill, and two clear dark nights.

**Skip.** The group turns and the record sheet.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Draw a sun low over a tree and high over a roof; your child says where it goes next. Do the same for a moon. Then show two clear dark nights with stars and ask what the next night shows. Do not help.
  **Say.** "Where will it be next, and how do you know?" Expect: the next place along, from the pictures.
- **10 min. Look back.** Look at any missed one together and trace the pictures with a finger.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the night sky with stars. 

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep all three items; shorten the waiting-card work. Longer: none; nothing is added to a check.

### Words and supports

Path, covered, next, evidence, stars, not seen. Read every prompt aloud; enlarged sheets, a scribe, pointing and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 returns each sheet with written feedback and repairs error A, error B, error C and error D. It starts from day 7's items.
