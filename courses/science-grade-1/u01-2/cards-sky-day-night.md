# Day and night sky cards (day 5)

Six identical sets of eight cards, one for each group of five, all invented. Say aloud: "These cards are invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Every card shows the same campsite: a tent, a tree and a hill. Each has a clock picture. An adult reads the clock aloud.

| Card | Sky | Stars marked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | clear dark night | yes |
| 2 | sunny morning | no |
| 3 | clear dark night, a pale moon | yes |
| 4 | sunny afternoon | no |
| 5 | clear dark night | yes |
| 6 | bright noon, a pale moon | no |
| 7 | clear dusk | yes |
| 8 | cloudy night | no |

The group sorts day and night, then puts a finger on the cards where stars show. The key is in key-cards-sky-day-night.md.
