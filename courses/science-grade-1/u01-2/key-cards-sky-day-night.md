# Day and night sky cards (day 5): teacher key (days 5, 6, 7, 8)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-sky-day-night.md.

- Day and night sky cards (day 5): Key: cards 1, 3, 5 and 7. Card 8 is night but cloudy: no stars show, because the clouds cover them. Error D: a child says the stars went away in the day; point to a bright-sky card and say "we could not see them, but they are still there." Error C: a child says the moon is only for night; point to card 6 and its clock.
