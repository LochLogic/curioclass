# Day 6: Do our patterns hold at another place?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set three stations with two copies of the other-place sky records, last card under a flap. Pair the six groups. Hold the exit cards back. Have the timed moon picture sequence and the day and night sky card sets ready. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Error C from day 5's exit: look at the daytime moon picture in the timed moon picture sequence and say what it shows (2 minutes). Error D: show a bright-sky card from the day and night sky card sets and say "not seen, but still there" together (2 minutes).

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read the class pattern chart's predictions aloud (review).

**Ask.** [[s01-P6-1]] "What do we think the next clear dark night will show?" Hear three. Expect: stars, as before.

#### 12 min. Test elsewhere

Say: "These records are invented, from another school. We say what the cards show." Two groups to a station, 4 minutes, then rotate. Each group predicts, says "I think ___, because I saw ___", then lifts the flap. Hear six children, 20 seconds each.

**Ask.** [[s01-P6-2]] Sun station: "Where will the third dot be?" Expect: right. The middle, "it stays": error A.

**Ask.** [[s01-P6-3]] Moon station: "Where will the moon be at look three?" Expect: lower above the water tower. High above the bridge: error B.

**Ask.** [[s01-P6-4]] Star station: "What does the covered night show?" Expect: stars. No stars: error D.

#### 6 min. Did our patterns hold?

**Ask.** [[s01-P6-5]] "Did our patterns hold at another place? How do you know?" This answers the question on the board. Expect: on these cards, yes. Say: "Real records may differ." Children dictate the closing explanation and you scribe it: where the sun seems to be changes, so the shadow falls on the other side.

#### 4 min. Revise the shadow account

**Ask.** [[s01-P6-6]] "Draw your account again: sun, stick, shadow, arrow. Where is the sun, compared with the shadow?" Expect: the sun on the side opposite the shadow. Scribe a label for the six.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented cards." **Ask.** [[s01-X6a]] "Touch where the third dot goes." [[s01-X6b]] "Touch where the moon will be at look three." [[s01-X6c]] "Touch what the next card shows." All touch; the six answer, two to an item. Expect: right; lower above the lighthouse; stars. The middle, "it stays": error A. High above the boat shed: error B. No stars: shown with help.

#### 1 min. Pack

Cards to the tubs.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three dots left, middle, and a blank; two moons on a strip; two clear dark nights with stars.

**Skip.** The stations, the chart and the board.

- **8 min. Dots.** Draw a stick with two shadow dots. Your child says where the third goes.
  **Say.** "Where will the third dot be?" Expect: the next place along.
- **8 min. The moon.** Draw a moon high over a house and low over a tree. Your child says where it goes next.
  **Say.** "Where will the moon be next?" Expect: further along, lower.
- **6 min. Stars.** Show two clear dark nights with stars and a blank one.
  **Say.** "What will the next one show?" Expect: stars.
- **3 min. Draw.** Your child draws a stick, its shadow and the sun on the other side.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6a]], [[s01-X6b]] and [[s01-X6c]]: ask for the third dot, the moon's next place and the next night.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two stations. Longer: say which record helped most.

### Words and supports

Pattern, predict, other place, again, opposite. Pointing and home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 7 is the check and reteaches nothing. Error A and error B go to day 8 with the written feedback.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
