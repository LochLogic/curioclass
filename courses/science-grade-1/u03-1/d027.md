# Day 27: Look again and tell it fresh

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 26, fix one unit 3 feedback cue card to each child's record, tied to the component it most needs. Set the repair cards by group. Choose the groups by letter from day 26's assessment and day 25's exit.

### Safety and set-up

Children sit. Any child may pass; wrong examples are read as invented sentences.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read feedback and revise

Hand back day 26's assessment records. Read the cues and prompts aloud: "Point to the mark and say what you saw." "Is it still the same kind if one thing is different?" Children say it to a partner, 15 pairs, about 20 seconds each way, then revise the marked record. A child who can point to a mark and say the observation shows it. You and the helper listen at four pairs.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs: error B and error C from day 25's exit, and the gaps day 26's items showed. At most three groups by letter, three minutes each with you and one minute to move. Error A: find a difference beside a similarity. Error B: find shared features in two different-looking ones. Error C: trace the labeled seed path on the day 22 maple card with a finger. Others use the picture directions on the repair cards.

#### 7 min. Exit: fresh account

Unscored. Say "Invented." Show the dandelion parent and young, then the goose and gosling. **Ask.** [[s03-X27a]] "Point to something that is the same on the dandelion parent and the young one." **Ask.** [[s03-X27b]] "Point to something that is not the same." **Ask.** [[s03-X27c]] "Point to something that is the same on the goose and the gosling." **Ask.** [[s03-X27d]] "Point to something that is not the same." Each child marks the response sheet; hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Expect the jagged edges shared and the young leaf shorter; the flat beak and feathers shared and the white neck band against the white wing tip. "Plants cannot have young" is error C; "so it is another kind" is error B; "they are copies" is error A.

#### 3 min. Collect and clean-up

Collect the records and response sheets once.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cue cards on scrap paper. One projector: one picture at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say each cue aloud and point.

**Skip.** The partner talk, the repair groups and the record sheet.

- **10 min. Look back.** Read the cue together and fix one mark on the day 26 pictures.
- **7 min. Repair.** Trace a seed path with a finger; find a difference beside a similarity.
  **Say.** "Is it still the same kind if one thing is different?" Expect: yes; the shared features stay.
- **13 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X27a]], [[s03-X27b]], [[s03-X27c]] and [[s03-X27d]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision and one pair of asks. Longer: find a second feature that is alike on the dog and the puppy.

### Words and supports

Parent, young, kind, alike, different, feature, mark. Frame: "Alike in ___; different in ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 closes unit 3. The revised day 26 record is scored after class; the fresh account is never scored. Day 28 begins unit 4 with a blurry guitar band and a changing sound.
