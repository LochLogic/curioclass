# Exit cards, days 22 to 25 and 27

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a model or a packet, and each shows new individuals, never a family from a teaching day. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each marking. Every card is captioned invented for practice and gives the parent in its caption. Animals are drawn in side view at the same size. Every child answers on paper or by pointing; the teacher hears a rotating six and writes who on the record sheet. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 22: M22E

A different kind of plant from the maple. The parent EP has an oval blade with a smooth edge, 85 by 40 mm. Young EY1 has a blade 35 by 17 and young EY2 a blade 65 by 30 (length then width, teacher only). Show each whole plant with roots, stem and its blade, and a dotted seed path from EP to each young plant. Give each child the three blades loose and unaligned, with a tape line.

**Item s03-X22a.** Spoken: "Point to something that is the same on the parent plant and its young." **Key:** the oval, smooth-edged blade, or the stem, or the roots. Pointing only to a difference is shown with help.

**Item s03-X22b.** Spoken: "Lay the three blades with their bases together, from shortest to longest." **Key:** EY1, EY2, EP. Blades with the bases apart are shown with help and a length prompt.

## Day 23: D23E

A black dog and a black puppy, both with floppy ears, fur, four legs and a long tail. The dog has one white forepaw; the puppy has a white chest. Caption: "The dog is the puppy's parent."

**Item s03-X23a.** Spoken: "Point to one thing that is the same on the parent and the puppy." **Key:** floppy ears, fur, four legs, a long tail or black fur. Wrong: "They are exact copies, everything is the same" is error A.

**Item s03-X23b.** Spoken: "Point to one thing that is not the same, to add to our chart." **Key:** the white forepaw and the white chest. Wrong: "Nothing is different, it is a copy" is error A.

## Day 24: C24E

A white cat and a white kitten, both with triangular ears, fur, four legs and a long tail. The cat has a black patch on its head; the kitten has a black patch on its tail. Caption: "The cat is the kitten's parent."

**Item s03-X24a.** Spoken: "Point to two things that are the same on the cat and the kitten." **Key:** any two of triangular ears, fur, four legs, a long tail, white fur. Wrong: "The kitten has a patch on its tail, so it is not a cat like her" is error B.

**Item s03-X24b.** Spoken: "Point to one thing that is not the same." **Key:** the head patch against the tail patch. Wrong: "That makes them different animals" is error B.

## Day 25: V25E

A maple family and a cat family. Maple parent VP has a blade 75 by 65 mm; young VY1 30 by 25 and VY2 60 by 50; another maple adult VA 85 by 72. All have five pointed lobes. A seed path runs from VP to each young plant. Cat parent VC is black with white paws; young VK1 is black with a white muzzle and VK2 black with a white chest; another cat adult VCA is black with a white tail tip. All cats have triangular ears, fur, four legs and a long tail.

Two printed groupings, both shown and neither a model to revise. **Grouping 1:** eight boxes, each plant and each cat alone in its own box, headed because each looks a little different. **Grouping 2:** two boxes, the four maples together and the four cats together. Give each child the loose blades VY1, VY2 and VP and a tape line.

**Item s03-X25a.** Spoken: "Touch the grouping that is better." **Key:** grouping 2. Wrong: grouping 1, "the little one is a different thing because it is smaller," is error B.

**Item s03-X25b.** Spoken: "Tell me why you chose your grouping." **Key:** the maples share five pointed lobes and the cats share triangular ears, fur, four legs and a long tail, though the blades differ in length and the cats differ in markings. Wrong: "the small maple does not go with the big one because plants do not have babies" is error C; "the cat with the white tail is not like the other" is error B.

**Item s03-X25c.** Spoken: "Lay the three blades with their bases together, from shortest to longest." **Key:** VY1, VY2, VP. A row with the bases apart is shown with help.

## Day 27: P27E and A27E (unscored)

**P27E.** A maple parent with a blade 95 by 80 mm and its young plant with a blade 45 by 38, both with five pointed lobes, a seed path from parent to young. **A27E.** A brown dog and a brown puppy, both with floppy ears, fur, four legs and a long tail; the dog has a white muzzle and the puppy has white paws. Caption: "The dog is the puppy's parent."

**Items s03-X27a to s03-X27d.** Spoken, one ask each: "Point to something that is the same on the maple parent and the young one." "Point to something that is not the same." Then the same two asks for the dog and the puppy. **Key:** the five pointed lobes are shared and the young blade is shorter and narrower; floppy ears and fur are shared, and the dog's white muzzle is against the puppy's white paws. Wrong: "That is not its young, plants have no babies" is error C; "The young one looks different, so it is another kind" is error B; "They are copies" is error A.
