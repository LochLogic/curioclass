# Exit cards, days 22 to 25 and 27

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a model or a packet, and each shows new individuals, never a family from a teaching day. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each marking. Every card is captioned invented for practice and gives the parent in its caption. Animals are drawn in side view at the same size. Every child answers on paper or by pointing; the teacher hears a rotating six and writes who on the record sheet. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 22: M22E

A different kind of plant from the maple. The parent EP has an oval blade with a smooth edge, 85 by 40 mm. Young EY1 has a blade 35 by 17 and young EY2 a blade 65 by 30 (length then width, teacher only). Show each whole plant with roots, stem and its blade, and a dotted seed path from EP to each young plant. Give each child the three blades loose and unaligned, with a tape line.

**Item s03-X22a.** Spoken: "Point to something that is the same on the parent plant and its young." **Key:** the oval, smooth-edged blade, or the stem, or the roots. Pointing only to a difference is shown with help.

**Item s03-X22b.** Spoken: "Lay the three blades with their bases together, from shortest to longest." **Key:** EY1, EY2, EP. Blades with the bases apart are shown with help and a length prompt.

## Day 23: D23E

A gray squirrel and a young gray squirrel, both with round ears, fur, four legs and a bushy tail. The parent has a white chest patch; the young one has a white tail tip. Caption: "The squirrel is the young one's parent."

**Item s03-X23a.** Spoken: "Point to one thing that is the same on the parent and the young one." **Key:** round ears, fur, four legs, a bushy tail or gray fur. Wrong: "They are exact copies, everything is the same" is error A.

**Item s03-X23b.** Spoken: "Point to one thing that is not the same." **Key:** the white chest patch and the white tail tip. Wrong: "Nothing is different, it is a copy" is error A.

## Day 24: C24E

A white mouse and a young white mouse, both with round ears, fur, four legs and a long thin tail. The parent has a gray patch on its back; the young one has a gray patch on its ear. Caption: "The mouse is the young one's parent."

**Item s03-X24a.** Spoken: "Point to two things that are the same on the mouse and the young one." **Key:** any two of round ears, fur, four legs, a long thin tail, white fur. Wrong: "The young one has a patch on its ear, so it is not a mouse like her" is error B.

**Item s03-X24b.** Spoken: "Point to one thing that is not the same." **Key:** the back patch against the ear patch. Wrong: "That makes them different animals" is error B.

## Day 25: V25E

A pig family and a willow family, new to the class. Pig parent PP is pink with a black ear; young PY1 is pink with a black patch on the back and PY2 pink with a black patch on a back leg; another pig adult PA is pink with a black spot on the nose. All have a snout, four legs and a curly tail. Willow parent WP has a long narrow blade 110 by 15 mm; young WY1 45 by 7 and WY2 80 by 11; another willow adult WA 130 by 17. All have long narrow blades with pointed tips. A seed path runs from WP to each young plant. No pig or willow card is used in any teaching day.

Two printed groupings, both shown and neither a model to revise. **Grouping 1:** two boxes with no heading, the four big adults, pigs and willows, in one and the four small young ones in the other. **Grouping 2:** two boxes, the four willows together and the four pigs together.

**Item s03-X25a.** Spoken: "Touch the grouping that puts the same kind together." **Key:** grouping 2. Wrong: grouping 1, "the little one is a different thing because it is smaller," is error B.

**Item s03-X25b.** Spoken: "Tell me why those go together even though they do not all look the same." **Key:** the willows share long narrow blades with pointed tips and the pigs share a snout, four legs and a curly tail, though the blades differ in length and the pigs differ in markings. Wrong: "the small willow does not go with the big one because plants do not have babies" is error C; "the pig with the black ear is not like the other" is error B.

## Day 27: P27E and A27E (unscored)

**P27E.** A dandelion parent with a leaf 120 by 30 mm and its young plant with a leaf 60 by 15, both with jagged toothed edges, a seed path from parent to young. **A27E.** A gray goose and a gray gosling, both with a flat beak, feathers, two legs and webbed feet; the goose has a white neck band and the gosling has a white wing tip. Caption: "The goose is the gosling's parent."

**Items s03-X27a to s03-X27d.** Spoken, one ask each: "Point to something that is the same on the dandelion parent and the young one." "Point to something that is not the same." Then the same two asks for the goose and the gosling. **Key:** the jagged toothed leaf edges are shared and the young leaf is shorter and narrower; the flat beak and feathers are shared, and the goose's white neck band is against the gosling's white wing tip. Wrong: "That is not its young, plants have no babies" is error C; "The young one looks different, so it is another kind" is error B; "They are copies" is error A.
