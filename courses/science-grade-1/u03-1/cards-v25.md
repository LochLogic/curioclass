# Kind packets V25 (day 25)

Six packets, two each for maple, dog and cat, printed from M22, D23 and C24. They add no new animals or plants: the same individuals are laid out again in rows so a group can compare young with young and adults with adults. Say aloud: "These are invented for practice."

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Captions are as on the first cards.

## Each packet shows

- **Row 1, the parent.** The parent card of the kind.
- **Row 2, young with young.** Both young side by side.
- **Row 3, adults with adults.** The parent beside the other adult of the kind.
- **Row 4, the contrasting kind.** For maple, the pine; for dog, the fish; for cat, the dog parent.

Maple packets also hold the loose blades MY1, MY2 and MP and the blade of MA, from M22, so the group can line up the three on tape and lay MP beside MA. Two blades that differ by a small amount are laid beside each other and called a little longer; do not force an order.

## Spoken

"Compare young with young. Compare adults with adults. Which kind is different, and what feature tells you?" Each group tells one same and one different. The class asks: "Which evidence changed our model?" Then the class dictates one shared account that joins plants and animals and returns to the schoolyard question without saying where the seedlings came from.
