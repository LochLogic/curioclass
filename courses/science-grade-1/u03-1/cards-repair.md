# Repair cards (days 24, 25 and 27)

Three cards, printed one set for each group of five and one large set for the teacher. The wrong sentences are read aloud as invented sentences: no child is the example of a mistake. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Each card and strip carries the printed words "invented for practice" where a child sees it.

## Card A: alike beside not exactly (day 24, day 27 group A)

The dog sheet D23 with the dog parent and one puppy ringed. Two sentence cards beside it. Wrong, read aloud: "The young are exact copies." Right, read aloud: "They share fur, but their markings differ." Each child marks one same and one not-the-same on the dog packet.

## Card B: still one kind (day 25, day 27 group B)

Two cats from C24 side by side, with different patches. Wrong, read aloud: "A different patch means it cannot be a cat." Right, read aloud: "Their patches differ, but their other shared features show both are cats." Children point to the features that stay the same: triangular ears, fur, four legs and a long tail.

## Card C: the seed path (day 27 group C)

The family card of the maple from M22, with the dotted arrow and its seed picture from the parent to each young plant. Wrong, read aloud: "Plants cannot have young." Right, read aloud: "This picture's arrow shows a seed from this parent growing into the young plant." Each child traces the path with a finger.

## Picture directions (day 27, for the children not with the teacher)

One strip of three pictures for each group letter, no words but the big word under each picture: a ring (find a difference beside a similarity), two linked animals (find shared features in two that look different), a finger on the dotted path (trace the seed path). The teacher teaches nothing new.
