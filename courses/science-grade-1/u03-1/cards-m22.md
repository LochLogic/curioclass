# Maple family cards M22 (days 22 and 25)

Printed one packet for each group of five (six packets) and one large set for the teacher. Say aloud before you show them: "These plants are invented for practice. They are not our schoolyard trees." Each card is headed invented for practice. These are drawn plants, not tracings of real ones, and they say nothing about where any real seedling came from. A caption supplies the parent and the seed path; nobody works a parent out from looks or from where a plant stands.

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Draw each plant whole, with roots, a stem and true leaves, and draw its one blade beside it. Name the markings in outline and label them with a word. A small corner label (MP, MY1, MY2, MA) is for the adult; children say the parent, the small young plant, the other young plant and the other adult. No conclusion and no arrow of evidence is drawn on a card.

## The cards

- **Family card.** The maple parent MP at the top and its two young plants MY1 and MY2 below it. A dotted arrow with a small seed picture runs from MP to each young plant. Caption: "A seed from the maple parent grew into each young plant."
- **Other adult card.** MA alone. Caption: "Same kind, another adult."
- **Pine card.** A pine plant with clusters of needles and no broad blade. Caption: "A different kind."

All four maples share five pointed lobes on the blade. The pine shares none.

## The blades

Three loose life-size blades with flat bases, so they sit on a tape line, cut by an adult. Teacher-only dimensions in millimetres, length then width, to check before cutting:

| Blade | Length by width |
|---|---|
| MY1 (young) | 40 by 35 |
| MY2 (young) | 55 by 48 |
| MP (parent) | 70 by 60 |
| MA (other adult, drawn on its card; used only to compare with MP) | 80 by 70 |

The gap between neighbouring blades in the ordered set is at least 15 mm, so the tips differ by eye. Order: MY1, MY2, MP. Print at actual size and do not scale. No stalks, no units read.

## Day 22 spoken

The teacher lays two blades with their bases on the tape and says: "Both have five pointed lobes, so they are alike." Then the group lays the three blades and says what is the same and what is not. Read the caption that shows the seed path. For the pine: "What feature tells it is a different kind?" Key: needles. On day 25 the same cards come back in the V25 packet.
