# Unit 3 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, draw or lay out the answer. Note the day 22 nest-picture retrieval in the margin beside the name, with the same scale. Mark who you heard in the rotating six on each day. The End columns are the observation on day 26: one cell for each of the six components and one for the support the child used. Components 1 to 5 come from the paper marks and component 6 from the oral turn; credit a paper component only if the child can point to the mark and say the observation. Day 27 gives a child the chance to revise the day 26 record; score the revised earlier work, and never score the day 27 fresh account. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied the cards or read a card aloud, and say so.
