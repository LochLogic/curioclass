# Exit cards, days 22 to 25 and 27: teacher key

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-exits-22-27.md. Blade sizes are teacher-only millimeters, length then width, to check before cutting. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 22: M22E

Blades: EP (parent) 85 by 40; EY1 (young, drawn only) 35 by 17; EY2 (young) 65 by 30; EA (another adult, a small one) 50 by 24. The loose blades are EA, EY2 and EP, at least 15 mm apart.

s03-X22a. Key: the oval, smooth-edged blade. The stem or the roots alone is shown with help, with the prompt "What about the leaf?". Pointing only to a difference is shown with help.

s03-X22b. Key: EA, EY2, EP. The small adult goes first, so "young are small" does not give the order; only the tips do. Blades with the bases apart are shown with help and a length prompt.

## Day 23: D23E

s03-X23a. Key: the brown foal, which shares the long face, mane, legs and hooves. Wrong: the calf has no letter; "the star means it is not hers" is error B.

s03-X23b. Key: thumb down; the foal has a white star the parent does not. Wrong: thumb up, "they are exact copies", is error A.

## Day 24: C24E

s03-X24a. Key: both lambs. Wrong: only the lamb with no patch, "the patch makes it not a sheep", is error B; the duckling has no letter.

s03-X24b. Key: the black face, black legs and white wool card. Wrong: the black patch card, "it makes the lamb different", is error B; the grass card has no letter.

## Day 25: V25E

Willow blades: WP 110 by 15; WY1 45 by 7; WY2 80 by 11; WA 130 by 17.

s03-X25a. Key: grouping 2. Wrong: grouping 1, "the little one is a different thing because it is smaller," is error B.

s03-X25b. Key: the willows share long narrow blades with pointed tips and the pigs share a snout, four legs and a curly tail, though the blades differ in length and the pigs differ in markings. Wrong: "the small willow does not go with the big one because plants do not have babies" is error C; "the pig with the black ear is not like the other" is error B.

## Day 27: P27E and A27E (unscored)

Dandelion leaves: parent 120 by 30, young 60 by 15.

s03-X27a to s03-X27d. Key: the jagged toothed leaf edge or the long leaf shape is shared and the young leaf is shorter and narrower; the flat beak and feathers are shared, and the goose's white neck band is against the gosling's white wing tip. Wrong: "That is not its young, plants have no babies" is error C; "The young one looks different, so it is another kind" is error B; "They are copies" is error A.
