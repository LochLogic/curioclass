# Oak family and rabbit family cards (day 26)

Keep these sealed until day 26. They appear in no teaching day. Say aloud: "These families are invented for practice." Each card is headed invented for practice and gives the parent in its caption; parentage is never worked out from looks or place. Captions give no comparison. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no conclusion or evidence arrow on any card. Each child has their own printed set of both family cards and three loose life-size oak blades, and marks the cards one spoken ask at a time: a joining line, a red, blue or green ring, or an X. Sizes, keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-day26.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

## Oak family (O26)

Show each whole plant with roots, a stem and its labeled blade, and a dotted seed path from the oak parent OP to each young plant. Every oak blade has rounded side lobes and a rounded end lobe, with a flat base. OP, OY1 and OY2 have three side lobes on each side; the other adult OA, captioned "Same kind, another adult", has four on each side and is drawn the same size as OP. The young blades are drawn smaller than the parent's. On the card, OY1's labeled blade is drawn shorter than OY2's, in the same ratio as the loose blades (50 and 70 mm). A fern OF, captioned "A different kind", has fronds of many small leaflets and no lobed blade. Three loose life-size blades, OY1, OY2 and OP, are cut by an adult to the sizes in the key.

## Rabbit family (R26)

All rabbits and the fish are drawn in side view at the same size. Every rabbit has long upright ears, fur, four legs and a short round tail.

| Individual | Markings |
|---|---|
| RP, rabbit parent | gray with white forepaws |
| RY1, young | gray with a white chest |
| RY2, young | gray with a white muzzle |
| RA, another adult | gray with one white ear tip |
| RF, a fish, caption "A different kind" | fins, scales and a forked tail |

Captions: "The rabbit parent and its two young." Under each rabbit a label, read aloud: "parent" under RP, "young" under RY1 and RY2, "Same kind, another adult" under RA. A dotted line runs from RP to each young, as the oak seed path does; all rabbits stay the same size. The fish is drawn as a different fish from the pond fish of day 13's read-aloud.

## The sitting

Two minutes to launch; twelve to lay and tape the blades and mark both cards, one spoken ask at a time; then six groups of five take three minutes each, 30 seconds a child and 30 seconds to change groups. At the turn each child answers the two "exact copies" asks, pointing to their marks; the teacher writes the spoken reason on the child's record. Waiting children draw one thing they saw on the back of their record, then sort the day 23 and 24 packets onto the class chart from picture directions. Nobody is helped with the answer.
