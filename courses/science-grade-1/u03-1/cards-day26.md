# Oak family and rabbit family cards (day 26)

Keep these sealed until day 26. They appear in no teaching day. Say aloud: "These families are invented for practice." Each card is headed invented for practice and gives the parent in its caption; parentage is never worked out from looks or place. Captions give no comparison. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no conclusion or evidence arrow on any card. Each child has their own printed set of both family cards and three loose life-size oak blades, and marks the cards with links and rings.

## Oak family (O26)

Show each whole plant with roots, a stem and its labelled blade, and a dotted seed path from the oak parent OP to each young plant. Every oak blade has three rounded side lobes on each side and a rounded end lobe, with a flat base. Teacher-only dimensions in millimetres, length then width, to check before cutting:

| Individual | Blade |
|---|---|
| OP, oak parent | 90 by 45 |
| OY1, young | 50 by 25 |
| OY2, young | 70 by 35 |
| OA, another adult, caption "Same kind, another adult" | 100 by 50 |
| ON, a pine, caption "A different kind" | clusters of needles, no broad lobed blade |

The loose blades are OY1, OY2 and OP, with at least 20 mm between neighbours. Order: OY1, OY2, OP. OY2 is wider than OY1, and OA is wider and longer than OP.

## Rabbit family (R26)

All rabbits and the fish are drawn in side view at the same size. Every rabbit has long upright ears, fur, four legs and a short round tail.

| Individual | Markings |
|---|---|
| RP, rabbit parent | grey with white forepaws |
| RY1, young | grey with a white chest |
| RY2, young | grey with a white muzzle |
| RA, another adult | grey with one white ear tip |
| RF, a fish, caption "A different kind" | fins, scales and a forked tail |

Captions: "The rabbit parent and its two young." The fish is drawn as a different fish from the pond fish of day 13's read-aloud.

## The sitting

Two minutes to launch; six to lay the blades and mark both cards; then six groups of five take four minutes each, about 48 seconds a child. Each child points through the plant claim and the animal claim and says the evidence; the teacher writes the spoken reason on the child's record. Waiting children finish their marks and then sort the day 23 and 24 packets onto the class chart from picture directions. Nobody is helped with the answer.
