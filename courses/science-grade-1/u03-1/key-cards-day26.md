# Oak family and rabbit family cards: teacher key (day 26)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-day26.md. Teacher-only dimensions in millimeters, length then width, to check before cutting. Blades are compared by length only, with the bases together; widths and whole-plant portrait sizes are never scored; the labeled blade lengths on the oak card are.

| Individual | Blade |
|---|---|
| OP, oak parent | 90 by 45, three side lobes a side |
| OY1, young | 50 by 25, three side lobes a side |
| OY2, young | 70 by 35, three side lobes a side |
| OA, another adult (drawn on its card, not loose) | 90 by 45, four side lobes a side |
| OF, a fern | fronds of many small leaflets, no lobed blade |

The loose blades are OY1, OY2 and OP, at least 20 mm apart.

## Keys, in the order the asks are spoken

- s03-E26a, lay and tape the blades: OY1, OY2, OP. Bases apart or the parent first: shown with help.
- s03-E26b, a line joining something the same on the oak parent and a young oak: the rounded lobes, three side lobes a side, the rounded end lobe, the flat base or the blade shape; roots or a stem also counts.
- s03-E26c, red ring, the two young oaks: one young blade is shorter than the other. A child who points to the loose OY1 and OY2 blades instead of ringing is shown.
- s03-E26d, blue ring, the two adult oaks: OA has four side lobes a side, OP three. "It is a different kind" is error B.
- s03-E26e, an X on the different kind: the fern. An X on a young oak "because it is smaller" is error B.
- s03-E26g, a line joining something the same on the rabbit parent and a young rabbit: long upright ears, fur, gray fur, four legs or a short round tail.
- s03-E26h, green ring, the parent and a young rabbit: white forepaws against a white chest or a white muzzle. "They are copies" is error A.
- s03-E26i, red ring, the two young rabbits: the white chest against the white muzzle.
- s03-E26j, blue ring, the two adult rabbits: white forepaws against the white ear tip. "That one is not a rabbit" is error B.
- s03-E26k, an X on the different kind: the fish.
- s03-E26f, at the turn, "Use your marks: are the young oaks exact copies of their parent?": no, with a pointed shared feature and a difference. "Yes, copies" is error A; "plants cannot have young" is error C. A bare "no" is shown with help.
- s03-E26l, at the turn, "Use your marks: are the young rabbits exact copies of their parent?": no, with a pointed shared feature and a difference. "Yes, copies" is error A; "a different patch means it cannot be a rabbit" is error B.
