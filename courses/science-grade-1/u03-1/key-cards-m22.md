# Maple family cards M22: teacher key (days 22 and 25)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-m22.md.

Teacher-only dimensions in millimeters, length then width, to check before cutting:

| Blade | Length by width |
|---|---|
| MY1 (young) | 40 by 35 |
| MY2 (young) | 55 by 48 |
| MP (parent) | 70 by 60 |
| MA (other adult, drawn on its card; used only to compare with MP) | 80 by 70 |

The gap between neighboring blades in the ordered set is at least 15 mm, so the tips differ by eye. Order: MY1, MY2, MP.

Pine card: "What feature tells it is a different kind?" Key: needles, no lobes.
