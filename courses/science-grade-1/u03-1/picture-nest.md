# The nest pictures (day 22 retrieval)

Two frames for the teacher, printed large. Say aloud first: "These pictures are invented for practice." They bring back kindergarten's habit of a claim and the evidence for it, before the leaves. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except the day label. One actor in each frame: a small round-bodied bird. No real nest is shown.

## Frame 1, morning

**Picture:** A fork in a tree branch with nothing in it. A small bird stands on the branch with one twig in its beak. On the ground below the branch lies a loose pile of twigs. A clock picture reads morning.

## Frame 2, afternoon

**Picture:** The same fork, the same branch. The twigs are now woven into a cup shape in the fork. The bird sits beside the cup. On the ground below, only two twigs are left. A clock picture reads afternoon.

## Spoken

"What changed, and what did you see that shows it?" Key: the twigs were gathered into a nest; the pile on the ground is smaller and the cup is in the fork. Then point to leaf and stem on the retained parts chart. A miss: the teacher says one "I saw ___, so ___" and the child tries again in the investigation. Retrieval, not scored: note S, H or N in the margin of the record sheet. Keep the nest out of every exit: day 22's exit is a plant family.
