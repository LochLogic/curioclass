# Feedback picture-cue cards (days 26 and 27)

A small card for each cue, printed and cut, so a child can hear a cue read aloud and see it. After day 26 the teacher fixes one card to each child's record, tied to the component that record most needs. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. A cue points at the next look; it never gives the answer.

- **Link** (a line between two leaves): what is the same on the parent and its young?
- **Ring** (a ring round one marking): what is not the same, so they are not exact copies?
- **Young and young** (two small plants side by side): what is the same and what is different between the two young?
- **Adult and adult** (two big plants side by side): what is the same and what is different between the two adults?
- **Another kind** (one odd one out): which feature tells it is a different kind?
- **Alike but not exactly** (two arrows pointing at each other): say what is alike and what is not.

The spoken prompts read to the whole group on day 27: "Point to the mark and say what you saw." "Is it still the same kind if one thing is different?" Each child hears the cue, points to the mark and says the observation; a child who can shows it. The paper marks carry components one to five and the oral turn carries component six.
