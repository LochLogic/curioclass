# Cat family cards C24 (day 24)

Printed fifteen copies, one for each pair, and one large copy for the teacher. Say aloud before you show it: "This cat family is invented for practice. No cat is in our room." The parent and the young are given by the captions, never worked out from looks. Each card and strip carries the printed words "invented for practice" where a child sees it.

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Every cat is drawn in side view at the same size, with triangular ears, fur, four legs and a long tail. Name each marking in outline with a word label.

## One sheet, five animals in a row

1. **Cat parent (CP).** Gray, with dark stripes. Caption: "Cat parent."
2. **Kitten (CY1).** Gray, with white paws. Caption: "A kitten of the cat parent."
3. **Kitten (CY2).** Gray, with a white chest. Caption: "Another kitten of the cat parent."
4. **Other adult cat (CA).** Black, with a white muzzle. Caption: "Same kind, another adult."
5. **Contrast: the dog parent from D23 (DP).** The same drawing. Caption: "A different kind."

## Spoken

Nothing new is taught. Pairs repeat day 23's routine: a link line for each shared feature and a ring round each difference, and the pair replaces a vague label ("looks the same") with a named feature. The cats share triangular ears, fur, four legs and a long tail; their markings differ. Ask: "Is it still a cat if one thing is different?"
