# Dog family cards D23 (days 23 and 24)

Printed fifteen copies, one for each pair, and one large copy for the teacher. Say aloud before you show it: "This dog family is invented for practice. No dog is in our room." The dog in unit 2's turning frames is a different, single dog on a rug; none of these is that dog. The parent and the young are given by the captions, never worked out from looks.

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no children drawn. Every dog is drawn in side view at the same size, with floppy ears, fur, four legs and a long tail, so the markings carry the comparison. Name each marking in outline with a word label.

## One sheet, five animals in a row

1. **Dog parent (DP).** Brown, with a white chest. Caption: "Dog parent."
2. **Puppy (DY1).** Brown, with white paws. Caption: "A puppy of the dog parent."
3. **Puppy (DY2).** Brown, with a white muzzle. Caption: "Another puppy of the dog parent."
4. **Other adult dog (DA).** Black, with white paws. Caption: "Same kind, another adult."
5. **Fish.** In side view, with fins, scales and a forked tail, drawn as a different fish from the pond fish of day 13's read-aloud. Caption: "A different kind."

A small corner label (DP, DY1, DY2, DA) is for the adult. No conclusion is drawn on the sheet.

## Spoken

The teacher models one link and one difference: "Alike in floppy ears; different in the white marking." Pairs draw a link line for each shared feature and a ring round each difference. The dog and each puppy share floppy ears, fur, four legs and a long tail; the markings differ. Day 24 keeps this packet for the repair.
