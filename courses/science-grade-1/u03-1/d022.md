# Day 22: Are the little trees like the big one?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut blades to key-cards-m22; keep key-cards-exits-22-27 and key-picture-nest for the keys (all teacher only). Set six M22 maple family packets with comparison strips and masking tape. Lay the pressed leaves by the seedling photographs. Hang the unit 3 question board, comparison chart and part-and-job chart. Day 21's assessment is scored after class.

### Safety and set-up

Only you touch the pressed leaves; a child with a plant-dust allergy sees the photograph. Nothing is tasted. No scissors.

### The day

#### 3 min. Hook: the leaves and the photograph

Hold the dated pressed leaves beside the dated photograph. **Ask.** "Are the little trees under our big tree like it? Exactly like it?" Hear three guesses; confirm none. Say: "Under the tree is not the same as from the tree." Post the question; leave it open.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Nest pictures: invented for practice. **Ask.** "What changed, and what did you see that shows it?" Expect: twigs made a cup, a smaller pile below. A miss: say one "I think ___, because I saw ___" (review, kindergarten's frame). Then point to leaf and stem on the parts chart.

#### 3 min. Teacher model

Say: "These plants are invented for practice, not our schoolyard trees." Point to the flat part of each leaf: "This flat part of a leaf is its blade." Lay the parent and one young blade, bases together on the tape. Say: "I think they are alike, because I saw five pointed lobes on both." Read the caption: a seed from the maple parent grew into each young plant.

#### 7 min. Groups: line up and order

Six groups of five. Three children each lay a blade, bases on the line; the fourth says which tip is longest, the fifth which is shortest. Move the jobs on one place and repeat. Hear one child per group, a minute each.

#### 3 min. Records

Each child draws the parent blade and one young blade, links the lobes with a line and rings one difference. Labels are dictated.

#### 3 min. A different kind

Show the pine card. **Ask.** "What feature tells you this is a different kind?" Expect: needles, no lobes.

#### 5 min. Start the comparison model

The class dictates "alike in ___; different in ___" on the chart. **Ask.** "Alike in what?" Expect: five pointed lobes, stem, roots. **Ask.** "Different in what?" Expect: the young blades are shorter.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say "Invented." **Ask.** [[s03-X22a]] All point; the six say it, 20 seconds each. A stem or roots alone: shown with help, then ask "What about the leaf?" **Ask.** [[s03-X22b]] "Lay the three blades bases together, shortest to longest." Check six layouts now; the rest are checked after class. Bases apart: shown with help.

#### 3 min. Hand washing and clean-up

Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw blades on scrap paper. One projector: one picture at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed blades: your child lays three paper leaf shapes you cut, in three lengths.

**Skip.** The group jobs, the board and the record sheet.

- **5 min. The puzzle.** Look at one leaf from a plant at home or a picture of it and say what you see.
- **10 min. Line up.** Tape a line. Your child lays three blades with bases on it.
  **Say.** "Which tip is longest?" Expect: the parent blade, then the middle, then the shortest.
- **5 min. Alike and different.** Your child says one thing alike and one different.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X22a]] and [[s03-X22b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: records by dictation; keep the blades and exit. Longer: count lobes.

### Words and supports

Parent, young, kind, feature, blade, alike, different, shorter, longer, lobes. Frame: "Alike in ___; different in ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 23's Open names one shared feature for children shown with help, then asks whether a dog and its puppy show the same pattern.
