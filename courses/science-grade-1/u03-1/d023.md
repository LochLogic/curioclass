# Day 23: Is a puppy like its parent?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print 15 D23 dog family copies, one for each pair. Hang the class comparison chart, the unit 3 question board and the part-and-job chart, with the maples still on the chart. Choose today's six pairs. Keep key-cards-exits-22-27 (teacher only) for the exit keys.

### Safety and set-up

Children work seated. No live animal comes into the room; the dogs are drawings. Any child may pass, and a partner may point instead.

### The day

#### 4 min. Open

Read the board question: "Does another family show the pattern?" Day 22's chart: maple young are alike in lobes, different in length. Class names ears from the part-and-job chart; you name fur. The schoolyard trees stay unanswered.

#### 4 min. Teacher model

Say: "This dog family is invented for practice. The captions say who is the parent." Show the large D23 sheet. Say: "Alike in floppy ears; different in the white marking." The fish is a different kind.

#### 7 min. Pairs: compare

Fifteen pairs, one copy each. Pairs link each feature the dog parent shares with a puppy and ring each difference; then each child marks their own record. Expect ears, fur, legs and tail linked; the white markings ringed. "They are exact copies" is error A; ask "What is not the same?"

#### 4 min. Share

Hear five pairs, 40 seconds each, then a minute for challenges. **Ask.** "What did you see that supports the claim that they are alike?" Expect: floppy ears on both. Partners may challenge: "Is anything different?"

#### 5 min. Revise the model

Add the dogs beside the maples. **Ask.** "Does the dog family agree with the maples?" Expect: yes, alike in ears and fur, different in the white marking, as the maples are.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." **Ask.** [[s03-X23a]] "Touch this horse's foal." **Ask.** [[s03-X23b]] "Pip says it is an exact copy. Tell Pip one way the foal is like its parent and one way it is not." Hear six, 20 seconds each. Expect: foal; a mane like it, but a white star. "They are the same" is error A.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Copies into envelopes, tables wiped.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the family on scrap paper. One projector: one picture at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed sheet: draw a dog and a puppy, each with floppy ears, fur, four legs and a tail, and one white mark in a different place on each.

**Skip.** The pairs, the share and the chart.

- **5 min. Look.** Your child names what they see on each drawing.
- **10 min. Compare.** Your child draws a line between each feature that is the same and rings what is not.
  **Say.** "What is the same on both, and what is not?" Expect: ears and fur are the same; the white mark is in a different place.
- **5 min. Say it.** Say "alike in ___; different in ___" together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X23a]] and [[s03-X23b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one puppy only, and skip the fish; keep both exit asks. Longer: find a second feature the second puppy shares, such as the tail.

### Words and supports

Parent, puppy, alike, different, feature, exact copy, ears, fur, paw. Frame: "Alike in ___; different in ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error A, "they are exact copies", is repaired first thing tomorrow: day 24 lays the wrong and right sentences beside the dog sheet, and each child marks one same and one not the same. Day 24 then tries the same routine on a cat family.
