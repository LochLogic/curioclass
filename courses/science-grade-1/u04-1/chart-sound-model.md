<div style="margin-top:.15in;font-size:14pt">Teacher: a movable wall chart you hold. Day 28 starts it with the children's strips under making sound and the first question. Day 29 adds the felt throat and puts "Can sound make something move too?" on the board, parked to day 32. Day 30 adds the drum and puts "Does it have to shake to sound?" on the board for day 31. Day 31 revises the stopping arrow under stopping sound ("it stopped shaking, so the sound stopped"). On day 32 the third arrow is added only if both cases support it, and the table question goes up when a child asks it; a null result is kept as a result. Children use the arrows on day 33 and revise on day 34, and the chart is kept for day 36. Every entry is in the children's own words, written by the teacher; none is a conclusion before the evidence.</div>
