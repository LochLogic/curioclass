# The toy-cart pictures (day 28 retrieval)

Say aloud first: "These pictures are invented for practice." They bring back kindergarten's push before the guitar. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each card. One actor in each picture: a child's hand.

## The three cards

- **Card 1, push.** A small toy cart at rest on a floor, with a taped mark behind it. A hand is flat against the back of the cart, with an arrow pointing the way the cart would go.
- **Card 2, hold.** The same cart at rest on the tape mark. A hand lies flat on top of the cart and an arrow points down.
- **Card 3, nothing.** The same cart at rest. No hand is drawn.

## Spoken

"Mark the push that could start it moving." Key: card 1, the hand pushes the cart. Then "What did we watch in our tower test?" Key: a hand pushes the cart and we watch whether the tower stays standing under the agreed fan test. A miss: the teacher pushes a classroom box gently and the child marks its new place. Retrieval, not scored: note S, H or N in the margin of the record sheet.
