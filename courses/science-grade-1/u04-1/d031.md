# Day 31: Does holding it still stop the sound?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check each band and fit it low. Set a guitar and a drum at each group of five. Count the glasses and set a damp cloth or wipe at each group.

### Safety and set-up

Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Glasses on for band turns; guitars low, away from faces; one gentle pluck per turn. Today only, a fingertip rests lightly on the paper top to still the drum.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Read the chart aloud. **Ask.** [[s04-P31-1]] "What did the drum, the band and your throat do?" Hear three. Expect: they shook, and they made a sound. Hand-backs in: none from day 30.

#### 4 min. Plan

Say: "On day 28 I stilled the band without comparing; today we compare." Glasses on. Partners say the stopping test aloud: "We change free or gently held, we keep the same band and the same gentle start, we watch the blur and the sound." Hear four pairs.

#### 14 min. Stopping test

In groups of five, each child plucks the band and listens, then plucks it again and gently holds it still (about 1 minute); then taps the drum and, after a moment, rests a fingertip lightly on the paper (about 40 seconds). Others mark their free-and-held half: a big sound mark, a small one or none. Hear a rotating six.

#### 5 min. Rug

**Ask.** "What did you change, and what stayed the same?" Expect: free or held; the same band and the same pluck. Children say "it stopped shaking, so the sound stopped", with an evidence arrow: "I saw ___, so ___." Revise the stopping card on the chart in their words. "It still rang loudly" is error A: lay the free and held marks side by side.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Wipe and bag glasses; count bands.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Play the bell recording beside its cards. **Ask.** [[s04-X31a]] "Pip says: if I hold the bell's body still, the sound will stop. Touch the picture that is a fair test." All touch. **Ask.** [[s04-X31b]] "What do you expect to hear when the bell's body is held gently?" Hear the six, 20 seconds each. Section 4.1 closes.

### Other rooms

No printer: rule scrap paper into free and held boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No guitar or drum: a band over a shoebox, a paper-covered tub. No glasses: you pluck, the child watches from a distance.

**Skip.** The partner plan, the rug and the record sheet.

- **4 min. Plan.** Say it together: "We change ___, we keep ___, we watch ___."
- **14 min. Test.** Pluck free, then pluck and gently hold it. Tap the drum, then rest a fingertip on it. Mark big, small or no sound.
  **Say.** "What did you change, and what stayed the same?" Expect: free or held; the same pluck.
- **5 min. Say it.** "It stopped shaking, so ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X31a]] and [[s04-X31b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the band only; keep the plan, rug and exit. Longer: hold the drum at the rim, then the top; which is quieter?

### Words and supports

Free, held, still, quiet, stopped. Frames: "It stopped shaking, so ___." "I saw ___, so ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error A, that it still rings loudly when held, goes to day 32: children open the strip's band row and say "I held it, I heard ___." Day 32 tests whether sound moves a paper strip and rice.
