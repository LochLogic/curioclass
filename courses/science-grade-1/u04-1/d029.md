# Day 29: Does our throat agree with the band?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check each band again and fit it low. Count the glasses. Put the hand wipe tub on each table and hang the class sound chart and question board. Keep the exit cards face down.

### Safety and set-up

Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Glasses on for every band turn; guitars low, away from faces; one gentle pluck per turn. The throat touch is light, optional and one's own, after a hand wipe. Clean-up is 2 minutes: glasses wiped and bagged, bands counted.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Point to the day 28 chart. **Ask.** [[s04-P29-1]] "What did the band do when it sounded?" Hear three. Expect: it went blurry, it shook. Hand-backs in: none. If more than a third missed day 28's exit, lay the spoon cards: the spoon free, then held. Say "I held it, I heard ___." Fewer miss: two minutes at a table later.

#### 4 min. Plan

Partners say the test: "We change ___, we keep ___, we watch ___." Expect: change the band for the throat; keep a gentle start; watch and listen. Each pair marks the plan boxes on the test chart. Hear four pairs.

#### 10 min. Band test

Glasses on. Each child in a group of five plucks once (45 seconds) while the others watch and listen; five turns take under 4 minutes. Everyone marks the band row: I saw, I heard. The others draw while they wait. Expect a blur and a sound. Hear a rotating six.

#### 7 min. Throat test

Hand wipe (1 minute). Together and quietly, each child hums "mmm" with fingertips on their own throat (1 minute); a child may whisper or pass. Children draw what they felt in the throat row (3 minutes). Partners compare the band row and the throat row (2 minutes). Nothing is marked on hearing.

#### 3 min. Rug

**Ask.** "What is alike about the band and your throat?" Expect: both shake, both make a sound. Add the felt throat to the chart in their words. Post a new question, parked for day 32: "Can sound make something move too?"

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Glasses wiped and bagged, bands counted.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Play the comb recording beside its cards. **Ask.** [[s04-X29]] "Pip says anything that moves makes a sound. Touch the two comb pictures you would compare to test Pip." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. The still comb with either is shown with help.

### Other rooms

No printer: rule scrap paper into the chart boxes. One projector: show one chart.

### At home

**Swap.** No guitar: a band over a shoebox, held low. No glasses: you pluck, the child watches from a distance.

**Skip.** The partner plan, the rug and the record sheet.

- **5 min. Plan.** Say it together: "We change ___, we keep ___, we watch ___."
- **10 min. Band.** Pluck once and fill the band row aloud.
  **Say.** "What did you see, and what did you hear?" Expect: a blur; a sound.
- **6 min. Throat.** Wipe hands. Hum "mmm" with a fingertip on the throat; draw what you felt.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X29]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one turn each on the band, keep the throat and exit. Longer: a partner hums while the child feels the partner's throat, with permission.

### Words and supports

Plan, change, keep, watch, felt, alike. Frames: "We change ___, we keep ___, we watch ___." "Alike in ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If more than a third touched the still comb, day 30's warm-up repairs it. Day 30 repeats the test on a paper-covered box drum.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
