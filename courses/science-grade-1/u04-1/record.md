# Unit 4 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, draw or place the answer. Note the day 28 toy-cart retrieval in the margin beside the name, with the same scale and no score. Mark who you heard in the rotating six on each day, in the heard column. The End columns are the observation on day 33: for each of the two cases, one cell for each of the six components and one for the support the child used. Components 1 to 5 come from the paper marks and component 6 from the oral turn; credit a paper component only if the child can point to the mark and say the observation. A case taken from held-back records is marked indirect. Day 34 gives a child the chance to revise the day 33 record; score the revised earlier work, and never score the new day 34 work. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied the cards or read a card aloud, and say so. Keep this sheet for day 36, which reuses day 33's plan, procedure and own-data record without a new score on it.
