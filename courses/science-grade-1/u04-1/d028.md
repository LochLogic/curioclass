# Day 28: Why does the band go blurry?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 27 closed unit 3 and handed nothing on; this starts unit 4. Check each band and fit it low on its shoebox. Count the glasses. Hang the chart and the board.

### Safety and set-up

Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Everyone near a guitar wears eye protection; guitars stay low, away from faces; one gentle pluck per turn. Clean-up is 2 minutes: children wipe and bag their glasses, you count the bands.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook

Glasses on. Pluck once, low, then still the band gently with one finger; twice. Children say what they notice. Then ask aloud, giving no cause: "Why does the band go blurry when the guitar plays, and why does the sound stop when we touch it?" Post it on the board.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Say "These pictures are invented for practice." **Ask.** "Mark the push that could start the cart moving." Expect card 1. **Ask.** "What did we watch in our tower test?" Expect: whether the tower stays up. A miss: push a box gently and the child marks its new place. Unscored; note S, H or N in the margin.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Pluck once. Fill three boxes aloud on the big strip: "I did pluck. I saw the band go blurry. I heard a sound that got quiet when I touched it." Children point to each box. Pip says, "The hand makes the sound." Write that guess in Pip's words, untested.

#### 12 min. Pluck and draw, in groups of five

Six groups. Each child plucks once and stills once (45 seconds) and says the three boxes (20 seconds); five children take under 6 minutes while the others draw their own strip. A child may pass or direct a partner. No blur: pluck slightly more, low, or show the dated photograph and say so. Hear a rotating six.

#### 4 min. Rug

Strips go under making sound; notices go on the board. **Ask.** "What makes the sound, and what did you see?" Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Expect "the hand" or "the band"; write both, untested. Teach "vibrate": fast hands, back and forth.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Glasses wiped and bagged, bands counted.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Play the spoon recording beside its cards. **Ask.** [[s04-X28]] "Pip says the hand is still making the sound. Touch the picture that could show whether the spoon itself is making it." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. The harder tap or lone spoon is shown with help.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strip on scrap paper. One projector: show one strip.

### At home

**Swap.** No guitar: stretch a household band over a shoebox, held low. No glasses: you pluck, the child watches from a distance.

**Skip.** The groups, the rug and the record sheet.

- **10 min. Pluck.** Pluck once, then still it with a finger. Say the three boxes.
  **Say.** "What did you do, what did you see, what did you hear?" Expect: plucked; the band went blurry; a sound that got quiet.
- **10 min. Draw.** Draw the strip; say "I think ___, because I saw ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X28]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the rug's notices; keep the strip and exit. Longer: a child directs a partner's pluck.

### Words and supports

Pluck, still, blur, vibrate, sound, quiet. Frames: "I did ___. I saw ___. I heard ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If more than a third missed the exit, day 29's warm-up repairs it. Day 29 plans a test with a partner and adds the child's own throat.
