# Day 28: Why does the band go blurry?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 27 closed unit 3; this starts unit 4. Record the held-back trials before each exit and day 33, unseen (the card files). Check each band and fit it low on its shoebox. Count the glasses and set a damp cloth or wipe at each group. Keep key-cards-exits-28-31 (teacher only) for the exit keys and key-picture-cart (teacher only) for the retrieval keys.

### Safety and set-up

Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Everyone in the room wears eye protection while guitars are out; guitars stay low; one gentle pluck per turn. Bands are intact and non-latex (check for latex allergies). Wipe and bag shared glasses.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook

Glasses on. Pluck once, low, then still the band gently with one finger; twice. Children say what they notice. Ask, giving no cause: "Why does the band go blurry when the guitar plays, and why does the sound stop when we touch it?" Post it on the board.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Say "These pictures are invented for practice." **Ask.** "Mark the push that could start the cart moving." Expect card 1. **Ask.** "What did we watch in our tower test?" Expect: whether the tower stays up. Unscored.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Say: "Remember kindergarten's strip, before, touch, after. This one says I did, I saw, I heard." Pluck once. Fill three boxes aloud on the big strip: "I did pluck. I saw the band go blurry. I heard a sound that got quiet when I touched it." Pip says, "The hand makes the sound." Write that guess, untested. Teach "vibrate": fast hands, back and forth.

#### 12 min. Pluck and draw, in groups of five

Six groups. Each child plucks once (30 seconds) and says the three boxes (20 seconds); only you still the band. Others draw their own strip. A child may pass. No blur: pluck slightly more, low. Hear a rotating six.

#### 4 min. Rug

Post three children's strips under making sound, and keep the rest. **Ask.** "What makes the sound, and what did you see?" Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Write both guesses, untested.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Glasses bagged; bands counted.

#### 3 min. Exit

As in kindergarten (review): picture cards, said aloud to be invented for practice, and a rotating six heard. Say "Invented for practice." Show the doorstop card and read: "A spring doorstop sticks out from the bottom of a wall. A finger flicks its tip and lets go. We hear a sound." **Ask.** [[s04-X28]] All touch. **Ask.** [[s04-X28b]] Partners say it (30 seconds); hear the six, 15 seconds each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strip on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No guitar: stretch an intact non-latex band over a shoebox, held low. No glasses: you pluck, the child watches from a distance.

**Skip.** The groups, the rug and the record sheet.

- **10 min. Pluck.** Pluck once; you still it with a finger. Say the three boxes.
  **Say.** "What did you do, what did you see, what did you hear?" Expect: plucked; the band went blurry; a sound that got quiet.
- **10 min. Draw.** Draw the strip; say "I think ___, because I saw ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X28]] and [[s04-X28b]], answered alone, from the doorstop card or a picture of it: touch the part that moves fast back and forth, then say what makes the sound and what you saw.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the rug's notices. Longer: a child directs a partner's pluck.

### Words and supports

Pluck, still, blur, vibrate, sound, quiet. Frames: "I did ___. I saw ___. I heard ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If more than a third missed the exit, day 29's warm-up repairs it with the doorstop card. Day 29 plans a test with a partner and adds the child's own throat.
