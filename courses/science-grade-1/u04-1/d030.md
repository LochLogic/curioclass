# Day 30: Will a drum agree?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set a paper-covered box drum at each group of five with its top tight. Hang the sound chart and question board. Keep key-cards-exits-28-31 (teacher only) for the keys.

### Safety and set-up

Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Taps are gentle, fingertips only, no sticks. A fingertip rests on the box rim, never on the paper top. Nothing flies, so no glasses are needed. Clean-up is 2 minutes: drums back in the tub.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Read the band and throat rows of the class chart aloud. **Ask.** [[s04-P30-1]] "Will a drum agree with the band and the throat? Say a guess." Hear five and write each guess in the child's words, untested. Hand-backs in: none. If more than a third touched the still comb on day 29, lay the swept, plucked and still comb cards: "A still comb is not moving, so it cannot test Pip." Fewer miss: two minutes at a table.

#### 4 min. Plan

Partners say the same plan: "We change the thing we sound, we keep a gentle tap, we watch and feel." Each pair marks the plan boxes on the test chart. Show the fingertip on the rim, never on the paper top.

#### 13 min. Drum test

Say once: "We tap three times to be surer it happens every time." Each child taps three times, gently (about 1 minute), feels the rim and listens, then marks the drum rows: saw, felt or not felt, heard. Five children take 5 minutes at one drum; the rest is marking while they wait. A tap not felt is marked "not felt", and the child taps once more. Hear a rotating six.

#### 6 min. Compare

Groups lay their band, throat and drum rows side by side and point to what is alike (3 minutes); hear three groups. Revise the chart in the children's words. Post "Does it have to shake to sound?" for day 31.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Drums back in the tub.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Play the tray recording beside its cards. **Ask.** [[s04-X30]] "Pip tapped this tray once and says that is enough to know how it works. Touch the picture of what to do to be surer." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. The harder tap or a different tray is shown with help. A child who says it keeps ringing when held is met on day 31.

### Other rooms

No printer: rule scrap paper into the chart rows. One projector: show one chart.

### At home

**Swap.** No drum: stretch paper over a tub, held low. Use fingertips only.

**Skip.** The partner plan, the compare part and the record sheet.

- **4 min. Plan.** Say it together: "We change ___, we keep ___, we watch ___."
- **12 min. Drum.** Tap three times, gently. Rest a fingertip on the rim, never on the paper top. Mark each tap felt or not felt.
  **Say.** "Did the drum agree with the band?" Expect: yes, it shook and made a sound.
- **5 min. Compare.** Put the band, throat and drum rows side by side and point to what is alike.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X30]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two taps each; keep the compare and the exit. Longer: tap near the rim and in the middle, and say what is alike.

### Words and supports

Drum, tap, rim, felt, not felt, agree. Frames: "The drum ___ like the band." "I felt ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who repeats a test to be surer. No repair is planned for it; say the three-tap line again whenever a test is repeated. Day 31 tests whether holding the band or drum still stops the sound.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
