# Exit cards, days 28 to 31

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a chart or a test, and each shows a new object, never the guitar or the drum of the teaching days. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic with apparatus labels only and supplies no result. Beside each card the teacher plays a real, dated recording made by an adult: classroom-level sound, smooth edges, nothing sharp, continuous sound and printable frames, and no fabricated observation. If a valid recording cannot be made, the card is asked alone and the exit is marked indirect. Every child marks a card on paper or points; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 28: the craft stick (R28)

Picture, invented for practice: a craft stick held down at a table edge and flicked, its free end a blur, with the caption "A craft stick is flicked at a table edge. Its free end is a blur. We hear a sound." **Item s04-X28.** Spoken: "Pip says the hand is making the sound. Touch the part that is moving fast back and forth while we hear it." Touch cards: the craft stick's free end; the hand; the table. **Key:** the craft stick's free end. The hand or the table is shown with help, no letter; the hand is Pip's first idea.

## Day 29: the comb (C29)

Recording: a plastic comb swept slowly through the air, then its teeth plucked. **Item s04-X29.** Spoken: "Pip says anything that moves makes a sound. Touch the two comb pictures you would compare to test Pip." Cards: (1) the comb swept slowly; (2) the comb with its teeth plucked; (3) the comb lying still. **Key:** cards 1 and 2. A still comb with either card is shown with help, no letter.

## Day 30: the tray (T30)

Recording: a tray tapped once. **Item s04-X30.** Spoken: "Pip tapped this tray once and says that is enough to know how it works. Touch the picture of what to do to be surer." Cards: (1) the same gentle tap, several times; (2) one much harder tap; (3) a different tray. **Key:** card 1. Cards 2 and 3 are shown with help. A child who says it keeps ringing when held is error A, met first on day 31.

## Day 31: the bell (B31)

Recording: a bell rung with its body free, then (kept for after the item is answered) rung again with its body gently held, the handle in the same place. Play only the free ring until s04-X31b has been answered. **Item s04-X31a.** Spoken: "Pip says: if I hold the bell's body still, the sound will stop. Touch the picture that is a fair test." Cards: (1) the same ring, body free, then body held; (2) a soft ring with the body free, a hard ring with the body held; (3) ring it harder. **Key:** card 1; card 2 changes two things. **Item s04-X31b.** Spoken, to the rotating six: "What do you expect to hear when the bell's body is held gently?" **Key:** much quieter, or no sound. "It would still ring loudly when held" is error A.
