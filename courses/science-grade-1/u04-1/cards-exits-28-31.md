# Exit cards, days 28 to 31

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a chart or a test, and each shows a new object, never the guitar or the drum of the teaching days. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic with apparatus labels only and supplies no result. Beside each card the teacher plays a real, dated recording made by an adult: classroom-level sound, smooth edges, nothing sharp, continuous sound and printable frames, and no fabricated observation. If a valid recording cannot be made, the card is asked alone and the exit is marked indirect. Each recording is also printed as a dated three-frame record card (before, during, after) that a child can read: the object drawn still or with short motion lines as the adult saw it, a printed heard mark (three short curved lines by a drawn ear) when a sound was heard and none when not, and a sound on or sound off label. Until the recordings exist, run from paper: the adult runs the trial before the day and draws the three frames by hand with its date. Every child marks a card on paper or points; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-exits-28-31.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

## Day 28: the doorstop (R28)

Picture, invented for practice: a spring doorstop sticking out from the bottom of a wall, a finger drawn just after flicking its tip and now away from it, short motion lines on each side of the spring. Caption: "A spring doorstop sticks out from the bottom of a wall. A finger flicks its tip and lets go. We hear a sound." **Item s04-X28.** Spoken: "Pip says the finger is making the sound. Touch the part that is moving fast back and forth while we hear it." Touch cards: the doorstop spring; the finger; the wall.

**Item s04-X28b.** The same picture. Spoken: "Say: I think ___ makes the sound, because I saw ___."

## Day 29: the comb (C29)

Recording: a plastic comb swept slowly through the air, then its teeth plucked. **Item s04-X29.** Spoken: "Pip says anything that moves makes a sound. Touch the two comb pictures you would compare to test Pip." Cards: (1) the comb swept slowly; (2) the comb with its teeth plucked; (3) the comb lying still.

## Day 30: the tray (T30)

Recording: a tray tapped once. **Item s04-X30.** Spoken: "Pip tapped this tray once and says that is enough to know how it works. Touch the picture of what to do to be surer." Cards: (1) the same gentle tap, several times; (2) one much harder tap; (3) a different tray.

## Day 31: the bell (B31)

Recording: a bell rung with its body free, then (kept for after the item is answered) rung again with its body gently held, the handle in the same place. Play only the free ring until s04-X31b has been answered. **Item s04-X31a.** Spoken: "Pip says: if I hold the bell's body still, the sound will stop. Touch the picture that is a fair test." Cards: (1) the same ring, body free, then body held; (2) a soft ring with the body free, a hard ring with the body held; (3) ring it harder. **Item s04-X31b.** Spoken, to the rotating six: "What do you expect to hear when the bell's body is held gently?"
