# Day 39: What lets us see the wall, and the lamp?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the flashlight, the cool enclosed lamp, the cave picture sequence, the paper evidence chart, the repair cards, days 39 to 42, and the held-back exit cards, days 38 to 42. Test the dimming once: half, never dark.

### Safety and set-up

Never shine a light into eyes or look at the sun. You alone switch the lamp and flashlight, aimed at a wall, away from faces. Children look at the lamp from the side, briefly, never into its beam. Seats by the door are open to anyone; the exit stays lit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error A from day 38. Say "Invented for practice." Show a closed paper bag with a sticker inside, then the bag open. Someone might say "We waited, so the sticker showed up." Children point to the picture where the light changed. If many made error A on day 38's exit, lay the shut and open pictures side by side. Few: up to six sit at the demonstration table.

#### 3 min. Rug

Point to the question board: "What if the thing makes light?" The class names today's three places: the box, the room, the cave pictures.

#### 9 min. The gently dimmed room

Dim by half. Switch on the lamp and the flashlight, aimed at a wall. Children look at the lamp from the side and at the wall near it. **Ask.** "What lets us see the wall?" Expect: the lamp shines on it. **Ask.** "What lets us see the lamp?" Expect: it makes its own light. Hear six, 30 seconds each: 3 minutes. Name "makes its own light" and post it under sources.

#### 9 min. Cave pictures

Read each caption aloud. Each group of five has the three cave cards. Children point to what the light shines on, then to what makes the light: 5 children x 3 cards x 10 seconds is 2 minutes 30 seconds a group, all groups at once.

#### 5 min. Evidence chart

You place three picture cards in the boxes: the wall near the lamp, the lamp, the cave wall. Read each aloud; each child rings "shines on it" or "makes light" under it. Wall and cave wall: shines on it; lamp: makes light.

#### 2 min. Lights up

Raise the room; lamp and flashlight go in the tray.

#### 2 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s05-X39]] "Does the string need another lamp shining on it, for us to see it? Say yes or no." All answer together; hear six, 20 seconds each. "Yes, it needs a lamp" is error B.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw three boxes on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No lamp: a switched-on bike light or torch, held away from faces, in a dim room; never shine it into eyes; an adult handles the light and secures its battery compartment.

**Skip.** The cave pictures and the repair.

- **10 min. Look.** In a dim room, a grown-up switches on a torch aimed at a wall. Look from the side.
  **Say.** "What lets us see the wall? What lets us see the torch?" Expect: the torch shines on the wall; the torch makes its own light.
- **10 min. Sort.** Find two things that make light and two that do not.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X39]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the cave pictures to one card. Longer: find a thing at home that makes its own light.

### Words and supports

Shines on it, makes its own light, lamp, wall, cave. Frame: "The ___ makes its own light." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error B from today's exit goes to day 40's Return and repair: a book light off, then on, inside a closed suitcase. Day 40 revises the drawing with a second arrow and writes one sentence.
