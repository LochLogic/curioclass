# Day 39: What lets us see the wall, and the lamp?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the flashlight, the cool enclosed lamp, the cave picture sequence, the paper evidence chart, the repair cards, days 39 to 42, and the held-back exit cards, days 38 to 42. Test the dimming once: half, never dark. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-38-42, key-cards-records and key-cards-repair.

### Safety and set-up

Never shine a light into eyes or look at the sun. You alone switch the lamp and flashlight, aimed at a wall, away from faces. Children look at the lamp from the side, briefly, never into its beam. Seats by the door are open to anyone; the exit stays lit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error A from day 38. Say "Invented for practice." Show a closed paper bag with a sticker inside, then the bag open. Someone might say "We waited, so the sticker showed up." Children point to the picture where the light changed. Many with A: lay the two pictures side by side. Few: up to six sit at the demonstration table.

#### 3 min. Rug

Point to the question board: "What if the thing makes light?" Name today's three places: box, room, cave pictures.

#### 9 min. The gently dimmed room

Dim by half. Switch on the lamp and the flashlight, aimed at a wall. Children look at the lamp from the side and at the wall near it. **Ask.** "What lets us see the wall?" Expect: the lamp shines on it. **Ask.** "What lets us see the lamp?" Expect: it makes its own light. Hear six, 30 seconds each. Name "makes its own light" and post it under sources.

#### 8 min. Cave pictures

Read each caption aloud. Each group of five has the three cave cards. Children point to what the light shines on, then to what makes the light; all groups at once, 10 seconds a card.

#### 5 min. Evidence chart

First hold up the three box records from day 38 (cards-records.md): the toy is seen only when light reaches it. Then hold up each evidence card in turn, the wall near the lamp, the lamp, the cave wall, and read it aloud; each child rings "shines on it" or "makes light" under card 1, 2 and 3. Wall and cave wall: shines on it; lamp: makes light.

#### 2 min. Lights up

Raise the room; lamp and flashlight go in the tray.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s05-X39]] "Does the string need another lamp shining on it, for us to see it? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no, on show me." Mark every child; hear six say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: no, the lights make their own light. "Yes, it needs a lamp" is error B.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw three boxes on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No lamp: a switched-on bike light or flashlight, held away from faces, in a dim room; never shine it into eyes; an adult handles the light and secures its battery compartment.

**Skip.** The cave pictures and the repair.

- **10 min. Look.** In a dim room, a grown-up switches on a flashlight aimed at a wall. Look from the side.
  **Say.** "What lets us see the wall? What lets us see the flashlight?" Expect: the flashlight shines on the wall; the flashlight makes its own light.
- **10 min. Sort.** Find two things that make light and two that do not.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X39]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the cave pictures to one card. Longer: find a thing at home that makes its own light.

### Words and supports

Shines on it, makes its own light, lamp, wall, cave. Frame: "The ___ makes its own light." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error B from today's exit goes to day 40's Return and repair: a book light off, then on, inside a closed suitcase.
