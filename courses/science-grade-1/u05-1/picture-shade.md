# Old shade picture and before-and-after pairs (day 38 retrieval)

Say aloud: "This picture is like our kindergarten sun and shade looks (review)." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Teacher voice only; the child points. Keys and wrong answers are in key-picture-shade.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

**Picture S1.** A school playground. A round sun with short lines in one top corner. A big tree whose shade falls on one bench; one child sits on the sunny bench and another child on the shaded bench. Word under the sun: sun.

**Ask 1.** "Point to what makes the light here."

**Pair P1 and pair P2.** Two pictures of the same sandy patch, drawn as a before and an after. In pair P1 the patch is in the sun and then covered by a card roof, and nothing else changes. In pair P2 a sandy patch in the sun is drawn beside a different patch of soil in the shade.

**Ask 2.** "Which before-and-after drawing showed us our idea was true?"
