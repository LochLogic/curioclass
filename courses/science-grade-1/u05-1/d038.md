# Day 38: Did the toy go away?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 37 hands on nothing. Lay out the peephole shoebox and toy, the flashlight, the paper account sheet and the held-back exit cards, days 38 to 42. Tape the seams the day before. Cut a light hole about 2 cm across in each box top, away from the peephole, and cover it with a tape tab; the leak test is done with the tab on. Draw the headings seeing, sources, barriers and "our question" on paper. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-38-42 and key-picture-shade.

### Safety and set-up

Never look at the sun or shine a light into anyone's eyes. Flashlights are held low and aimed down into the box, never at a face or the peephole; you secure each battery compartment.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook

The rotating six each look through the peephole with the flap shut and say "I see ...", then with it open: two looks of 12 seconds, 2.5 minutes in all. **Ask.** "Did the toy go away? How do you know?" Expect: it stayed; we saw it when the flap was open. Do not explain.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Say "This picture is like our kindergarten sun and shade looks (review)." **Ask.** "Point to what makes the light here." Expect: the sun. **Ask.** "Which before-and-after drawing showed us our idea was true?" Expect: the one with one change.

#### 5 min. Notices to question

Post one notice under "seeing". Read aloud and post: "Why can't we see the toy inside the box until we open the flap?"

#### 11 min. Boxes in groups

Six groups of five: looker, flashlight holder, recorder, or sit by the door. Keep, change, watch is review from kindergarten. Plan aloud (2 minutes): "We keep the toy and where we look. We change only the light. We watch the toy." Each child takes three 15-second looks: flap shut, tab on; flap open; flap shut, tab peeled back, the flashlight shining down through the light hole at the toy. With three boxes, two groups share one (7.5 minutes); with one box, only the six look. Each child draws seen or not seen for each look. "We waited and it showed up" is error A.

#### 6 min. Our first drawing

Model flap shut: the toy, where we look, "not seen"; then flap open with an arrow for light and "seen". Say "The toy stayed. Our seeing changed." Each child draws their own on side 2; pointing counts. Hear the six.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Flashlights off and trayed; lids shut; count boxes.

#### 3 min. Exit

Exit with picture cards, as in kindergarten (review). Say "This is invented for practice." Show the three pairs of cards. **Ask.** [[s05-X38]] "Touch the pair that checks whether light lets us see." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each, and mark who. Pair 2, the long wait, is error A; pair 3 changed two things.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the boxes on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No shoebox: a carton with a hole and a flap. No flashlight: a phone light held low, aimed down, never at a face. Cut or poke a small light hole in the carton top, away from the looking hole.

**Skip.** The retrieval.

- **5 min. Look.** Look through the hole with the flap shut, then open.
  **Say.** "Did the toy go away?" Expect: no, it stayed; we could not see it.
- **15 min. Try.** Look three times: flap shut; open; shut with the flashlight shining down through the light hole. Draw seen or not seen each time.
- **5 min. Draw.** Draw the toy, an arrow for light, and seen.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X38]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two looks, shut and open.

### Words and supports

Toy, flap, light, seen, not seen, dark (inside the shut box). Frame: "When the flap is open, light shines on the toy." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error A from today's exit goes to day 39's Return and repair, a closed paper bag with a sticker inside.
