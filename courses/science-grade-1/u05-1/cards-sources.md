# Ten source cards (day 42)

Say aloud: "These are invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Each card is one thing alone, no scene. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-sources.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

**Make their own light (five):** the sun (a round sun with short lines; looked at only in the picture), a switched-on flashlight, a campfire, a candle flame, a traffic signal showing a lit light.

**Need light from elsewhere (five):** a pillow, a spoon, a rock, a book, a shoe.

Each group lays ten cards under two headings, makes its own light and needs light from elsewhere; each child places two and says why.
