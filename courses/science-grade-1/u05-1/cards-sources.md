# Ten source cards (day 42)

Say aloud: "These are invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Each card is one thing alone, no scene.

**Make their own light (five):** the sun (a round sun with short lines; looked at only in the picture), a switched-on flashlight, a campfire, a candle flame, a traffic signal showing a lit light.

**Need light from elsewhere (five):** a pillow, a spoon, a rock, a book, a shoe.

Each group lays ten cards under two headings, makes its own light and needs light from elsewhere; each child places two and says why. **Key:** the first five under the first heading, the second five under the second. The cards are not exit items; no card shows a sock, key, button, lantern, toy car, night light, string of lights, firefly or leaf.
