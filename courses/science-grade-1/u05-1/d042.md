# Day 42: What if the light cannot get in?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the peephole shoebox and toy, the flashlight, the paper account sheet, the self-luminous source picture cards, the repair cards, days 39 to 42, and the held-back exit cards, days 38 to 42.

### Safety and set-up

Never shine a light into eyes or look at the sun; it appears only on a card. Flashlights are held low, aimed down into the box, never at a face or the peephole; you secure each battery compartment. Any child may sit by the door. The exit stays lit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error B from day 41. Say "Invented for practice." Show a bike light in a closed drawer, off, then on, a glow seen through a crack. Someone might say "The bike light needs a lamp to be seen." Children point to the picture where nothing else is lit. If many made error B on day 41's exit, lay the off and on pictures side by side. Few: up to six sit at the demonstration table.

#### 3 min. Rug

Return to the box. **Ask.** "What if the light cannot get in, or cannot get out?" Hear guesses.

#### 8 min. Sort the cards

Say "These are invented for practice." Each group lays the ten cards under "makes its own light" and "light shines on it". Each child places two and says why: 2 cards x 25 seconds is 50 seconds a child, 4 minutes 10 seconds for five. Hear the rotating six, 20 seconds each. Five cards make their own light; five do not.

#### 10 min. Box and blocked light

Groups predict aloud, 1 minute. Each child then takes three 15-second looks, aimed down: flap shut with the flashlight shining in at the gap; the same with a cardboard held in front of the flashlight; flap open with the flashlight off. That is 45 seconds a child, 3 minutes 45 seconds for five. Each child draws seen or not seen. Expect: seen, not seen, seen.

#### 4 min. Add to the account

**Ask.** "What changes if we block the light?" Expect: the toy is not seen. Say "block means stop light getting to a thing" (kindergarten's block, review, extended to a lamp). Each child adds the word block and a card between the flashlight and the toy. Post "what blocks light?" for day 43.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Holders switch off and tray the flashlights; lids shut.

#### 3 min. Exit

The rotating six heard, record sheet names who, is review from kindergarten. Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s05-X42]] "We shut the curtain so no outside light comes in. Touch the one we can still see." All touch; hear six, 20 seconds each. "Neither, it needs the window" is error B; the button after a wait is error A.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No box: a carton with a hole. No flashlight: a phone torch, held low.

**Skip.** The card sort.

- **5 min. Name.** Find a thing that makes its own light and a thing that does not.
- **15 min. Block.** Look into the box: flap shut with the torch shining in; the same with a card in front of the torch; flap open, torch off. Draw seen or not seen.
  **Say.** "What changes when we block the light?" Expect: the toy is not seen.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X42]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the sort. Longer: block the light with a second thing.

### Words and supports

Block, shines on it, makes its own light, seen, not seen. Frame: "When we block the light, the toy is ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error A and error B from today's exit go to day 43's Return and repair, three minutes each. Day 43 then tests clear plastic, wax paper and cardboard in a fixed beam of light.
