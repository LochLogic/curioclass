# Box, room and cave records (days 39 and 40)

Say aloud: "These are pictures of real looks, made by an adult, and some are photographs from a cave." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Captions are read aloud; nobody reads them.

## Box records (from the real day 38 looks)

Three cards from the class box, each dated: flap shut (nothing seen), flap open (toy seen), flap shut with the flashlight held at the gap and aimed down (toy seen). If the box leaks, say aloud that the cards are recordings and indirect.

## Room records (from the real day 39 demonstration)

Three cards from the gently dimmed room, each dated: a lit lamp seen from the side; the wall near the lamp, bright; the wall far from the lamp, dim. Nothing is looked at straight on into a beam.

## Cave records

**Card C1.** A photograph of visitors seen as dark shapes in flashlight beams in a cave. Credit under it: National Park Service. Caption read aloud: "Visitors silhouetted in flashlight beams."

**Card C2.** A photograph of headlamps shining from a distance under a rock ledge in a cave. Credit: National Park Service. Caption read aloud: "Headlamps shine from a distance under a rock ledge in Coronado Cave."

**Card C3.** Our own line drawing of one flashlight beam on a cave wall, the wall bright where the beam lands. Credit: Curio Class.

Source and rights: sources.csv row 8F, checked 2026-11-19. The photographs are previewed by the adult before use; if one does not show the beam clearly, it is cut and the drawing carries the day.

**Questions** (spoken): "What does the light shine on? What makes the light?" **Keys:** C1 and C2: the light shines on the cave and the visitors or ledge; the flashlight or headlamp makes the light. C3: the wall is lit; the flashlight makes the light.
