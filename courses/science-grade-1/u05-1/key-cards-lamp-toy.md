# Lamp and toy picture sequence (day 41): teacher key

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-lamp-toy.md. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

- Questions. Key: the lamp makes its own light; the duck is seen only when the lamp is on, so it needs light from elsewhere. Say each aloud, then partners say the sentence "We can see the duck when the lamp is on because it was not seen when the lamp was off" or a child's own words.
