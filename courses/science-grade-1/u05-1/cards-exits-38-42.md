# Exit cards, days 38 to 42

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or says yes or no; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet.

## Day 38: the sock (item s05-X38)

Spoken: "Pip wants to know whether light lets us see a sock. Touch the pair that checks whether light lets us see." Pairs: (1) a sock in a shut lunch box, then the same sock with the lid open; (2) the same sock in a shut lunch box, then the shut box again after a long time by the clock; (3) a sock in a shut box, then a different toy in an open box. **Key:** pair 1. Pair 2 is error A. Pair 3 is shown with help, no letter.

## Day 39: the lights on a fence (item s05-X39)

Card: a string of little battery lights switched on along a fence in a dark yard. Spoken: "Does the string need another lamp shining on it, for us to see it? Say yes or no." **Key:** no, it makes its own light. Yes is error B.

## Day 40: the key (item s05-X40)

Card pair: a key hanging in a shut shed; the door open and the key seen. Finishing cards for "We can see the key when ___": light reaches it; we wait a long time; the door is blue. **Key:** light reaches it. The long wait is error A. The blue door is shown with help.

## Day 41: the glow lantern (item s05-X41)

Cards: first, a glow lantern with the room light on; second, the lantern with the room light off, still seen; third, a glossy toy car with the room light on. Spoken: "Touch the picture that shows the lantern makes its own light." **Key:** the second card. The first card is error B. The car is shown with help.

## Day 42: the button and the night light (item s05-X42)

Card: a button on a table and a switched-on night light. Spoken: "We shut the curtain so no outside light comes in. Touch the one we can still see." **Key:** the night light. "Neither, the night light needs the window" is error B. The button after a wait is error A.
