# Exit cards, days 38 to 42

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or shows thumb up or down; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-exits-38-42.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

## Day 38: the sock (item s05-X38)

Spoken: "Pip wants to know whether light lets us see a sock. Touch the pair that checks whether light lets us see." Pairs: (1) a sock in a shut lunch box, then the same sock with the lid open; (2) the same sock in a shut lunch box, then the shut box again after a long time by the clock; (3) a sock in a shut box, then a different toy in an open box.

## Day 39: the lights on a fence (item s05-X39)

Card: a string of little battery lights switched on along a fence in a dark yard. Spoken: "Does the string need another lamp shining on it, for us to see it? Say yes or no."

## Day 40: the key (item s05-X40)

Card pair: a key hanging in a shut shed; the door open and the key seen. Three finishing cards, one big phrase each, read aloud: "light reaches it"; "we wait a long time"; "the door is blue". Spoken: "We can see the key when ___. Touch the card that finishes our sentence."

## Day 41: the battery lantern (item s05-X41)

Three cards: the switched-on battery lantern in a dark room, still seen; the same lantern with the room light on; a glossy toy car with the room light on. Spoken: "Touch the picture that shows the lantern makes its own light."

## Day 42: the button and the night light (item s05-X42)

Card: a button in a shut box with the lid on, and a switched-on night light standing on the table beside the box. Spoken: "A button in a shut box and a switched-on night light. Touch the one we can see."
