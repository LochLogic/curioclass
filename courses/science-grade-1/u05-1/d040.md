# Day 40: When can we see a thing, and why?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the paper account sheets (seeing drawings) from day 38, the paper light-observation records with the cave picture sequence, the paper sentence-frame sheets, the repair cards, days 39 to 42, and the held-back exit cards, days 38 to 42. Write six word labels on card: toy, lamp, wall, flap open, light, cave. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-38-42, key-cards-records and key-cards-repair.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; children sit. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error B from day 39. Say "Invented for practice." Show a closed suitcase with a book light inside, off, then on, a glow at the lid. Someone might say "The book light needs a lamp to be seen." Children point to the picture where nothing else is lit. Many with B: lay the two pictures side by side. Few: up to six sit at the demonstration table.

#### 3 min. Rug

Read the board: "What lets us see? What about things that glow?"

#### 8 min. Revise the drawing

Each group lays out the box, room and cave records. On the board, draw a lamp with short lines around it for "makes its own light", and an arrow from the lamp to a wall for "light shines on it". Each child adds the lamp or flashlight with the lines, and keeps the first arrow for the wall, on their day 38 drawing. Walk and listen.

#### 12 min. A supported sentence

Model (3 minutes): "We can see the wall when the lamp shines on it, because we saw it bright near the lamp." Partners say their own, 30 seconds each. Walk and listen: in grade 1 a spoken sentence meets the goal, so mark the ones you hear. Dictation (6 minutes): write for the rotating six, 40 seconds each. The rest copy or trace labels into the frame and draw the proof; a helper adult may scribe. Judge on three look-fors: names the thing seen; says light reaches it or it makes its own light; the because points to something the child saw. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Scientists find out

Say and post: "We tested, we looked, we drew."

#### 3 min. Exit

Exit with picture cards, as in kindergarten (review). Say "Invented for practice." Show the shed pictures and lay out three finishing cards for "We can see the key when ___": light reaches it; we wait a long time; the door is blue. Read each card aloud. **Ask.** [[s05-X40]] "Touch the card that finishes our sentence." All touch, no partner turn; mark every child. Hear the six give the because, 20 seconds each. Expect: light reaches it, because the door opened. "We wait a long time": error A.

#### 2 min. Put away

Collect the sheets and drawings.

### Other rooms

No printer: the sentence on scrap paper, with the frame written on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No records: use your child's day 38 drawing and look at a lamp and a wall. An adult switches the lamp; look from the side briefly, never straight into a bright bulb.

**Skip.** The partner talk and the board.

- **8 min. Look back.** Find the drawing; add the lines around the lamp.
  **Say.** "How do we know the lamp makes its own light?" Expect: we see it with nothing else lit.
- **12 min. Sentence.** Your child says "We can see the ___ when ___ because ___"; you write it exactly and do not correct. They draw the proof.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X40]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one sentence, no partner talk. Longer: a second sentence about the lamp.

### Words and supports

Shines on it, makes its own light, because, proof. Frame: "We can see the ___ when ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and dictation count.

### Tomorrow

Error A from today's exit goes to day 41's Return and repair: a closed pencil case with a sticker inside.
