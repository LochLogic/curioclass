# Day 41: A new lamp and a toy beside it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the enclosed lamp and unlit toy picture sequence (one set a group), the paper sentence-frame sheet, the two new picture cards, the repair cards, days 39 to 42, and the held-back exit cards, days 38 to 42. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-38-42, key-cards-lamp-toy and key-cards-repair.

### Safety and set-up

You alone switch the lamp. Children look from the side, never into a beam. Seats by the door are open to anyone; the exit stays lit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error A from day 40. Say "Invented for practice." Show a closed pencil case with a sticker inside, then the case with the lid open. Someone might say "We waited, so the sticker showed up." Children point to the picture where the light changed. Many with A: lay the two pictures side by side. Few: up to six sit at the demonstration table.

#### 3 min. Rug

Read the board: "Does a new lamp need light from elsewhere? Does the toy next to it?"

#### 10 min. Lamp off and on beside an unlit toy

Each group has the four dated cards: lamp off and on, twice, the duck beside it, seen through the day 38 box peephole. Or run it live in the box: switch the lamp through the flap, close it, and the six look. **Ask.** "Point to the lamp. Does it need another lamp shining on it, for us to see it?" Expect: no, it makes its own light. **Ask.** "Point to the duck. What lets us see the duck?" Expect: the lamp shines on it; it is not seen when the lamp is off. Hear the rotating six (review) say why, 30 seconds each.

#### 8 min. Say it again

Two new cards, spoken only. Card 1: the bear in a dark room, flashlight on. Card 2: the truck in the tunnel, closed then open. For each: look 1 minute; partners say "We can see the ___ when ___ because ___", 30 seconds each; hear three of the six. Expect: light shines on the bear; light gets into the open tunnel.

#### 4 min. Strengthen the drawing

Each child adds the lamp with short lines around it, and the toy with an arrow for light shining on it.

#### 3 min. Exit

Exit with picture cards, as in kindergarten (review). Say "Invented for practice." Show three cards: the switched-on battery lantern in a dark room, still seen; the same lantern with the room light on; a glossy toy car with the room light on. **Ask.** [[s05-X41]] "Touch the picture that shows the lantern makes its own light." All touch, no partner turn; mark every child. Hear the six say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: the lantern in the dark room. The lantern with the room light on is error B.

#### 2 min. Put away

Lamp off; collect the sheets.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No lamp: a flashlight and a toy in a shut box with a looking hole, flashlight off then on. An adult switches the flashlight and secures the batteries; aim it low, never at eyes or faces.

**Skip.** The two new cards.

- **5 min. Repair.** Open a closed box with a sticker in it. Which look had the light?
- **15 min. Lamp and toy.** Flashlight off, then on, beside a toy in the shut box.
  **Say.** "Does the flashlight need another lamp to be seen? What lets us see the toy?" Expect: it makes its own light; the flashlight shines on the toy.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X41]]: draw the three cards (a switched-on battery toy light with the room light on, the same light with the room light off, a toy car); your child touches the one that shows the light makes its own light.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fold the lamp case into day 40.

### Words and supports

Lamp, toy, shines on it, makes its own light, because. Frame: "We can see the ___ when ___ because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error B from today's exit goes to day 42's Return and repair: a bike light in a closed drawer, seen through a crack.
