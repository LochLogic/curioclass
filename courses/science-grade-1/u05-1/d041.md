# Day 41: A new lamp and a toy beside it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the enclosed lamp and unlit toy picture sequence (one set a group), the paper sentence-frame sheet, the two new picture cards, the repair cards, days 39 to 42, and the held-back exit cards, days 38 to 42.

### Safety and set-up

You alone switch the lamp. Children look from the side, never into a beam. Seats by the door are open to anyone; the exit stays lit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error A from day 40. Say "Invented for practice." Show a closed pencil case with a sticker inside, then the case with the lid open. Someone might say "We waited, so the sticker showed up." Children point to the picture where the light changed. If many made error A on day 40's exit, lay the shut and open pictures side by side. Few: up to six sit at the demonstration table.

#### 3 min. Rug

Read the board: "Does a new lamp need light from elsewhere? Does the toy next to it?"

#### 10 min. Lamp off and on beside an unlit toy

Each group has the four dated cards: lamp off, lamp on, lamp off, lamp on, the duck beside it. Or run it live. **Ask.** "Point to the lamp. Does it need another lamp shining on it, for us to see it?" Expect: no, it makes its own light. **Ask.** "Point to the duck. What lets us see the duck?" Expect: the lamp shines on it; it is not seen when the lamp is off. Hear the rotating six say why, 30 seconds each: 3 minutes. A card not clear: repeat once, keep both.

#### 8 min. Say it again

Two new cards, spoken only, not written. Card 1: the bear in a dark room, torch on. Card 2: the truck in the tunnel, closed then open. For each: look 1 minute; partners say "We can see the ___ when ___ because ___", 30 seconds each; hear three children, 20 seconds each: the rotating six in all. Expect the bear is seen when the torch shines on it; the truck when the tunnel is open and light gets in.

#### 4 min. Strengthen the drawing

Each child adds the lamp with short lines around it, and the toy with an arrow for light shining on it.

#### 3 min. Exit

As in kindergarten (review): the exit with picture cards, invented for practice, said aloud. The rotating six heard, with the record sheet naming who, is also review. Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s05-X41]] "Touch the picture that shows the lantern makes its own light." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. The first card, the lantern with the room light on, is error B.

#### 2 min. Put away

You switch off the lamp. Collect the sheets.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No lamp: a torch and a toy in a dim room, torch off then on. An adult switches the torch and secures the batteries; aim it low, never at eyes or faces.

**Skip.** The two new cards.

- **5 min. Repair.** Open a closed box with a sticker in it. Which look had the light?
- **15 min. Lamp and toy.** Torch off, then on, beside a toy.
  **Say.** "Does the torch need another lamp to be seen? What lets us see the toy?" Expect: it makes its own light; the torch shines on the toy.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X41]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fold the lamp case into day 40.

### Words and supports

Lamp, toy, shines on it, makes its own light, because. Frame: "We can see the ___ when ___ because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error B from today's exit goes to day 42's Return and repair: a bike light in a closed drawer, seen through a crack. Day 42 sorts ten picture cards and asks what changes when light is blocked.
