# Lamp and toy picture sequence (day 41)

Say aloud: "These are pictures of real looks, made by an adult." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. One set per group of five; one teacher demonstration is the same set live.

Apparatus: a cool enclosed battery lamp with a clear dome, no cord, and an unlit wooden duck beside it, on a table in a gently dimmed room, the lamp low and not aimed at faces. Four dated cards: (L1) lamp off, nothing seen; (L2) lamp on, the lamp bright and the duck seen; (L3) lamp off again, nothing seen; (L4) lamp on, the duck seen from the same place. If a card is not clear, repeat once and keep both.

**Questions** (spoken): "Point to the lamp. Does it need light from elsewhere? Point to the duck. Does the duck?" **Key:** the lamp makes its own light; the duck is seen only when the lamp is on, so it needs light from elsewhere. Say each aloud, then partners say the sentence "We can see the duck when the lamp is on because it was not seen when the lamp was off" or a child's own words.

If there is no lamp, use the cards and say they are recordings and indirect. Cards show real looks only; nothing is filled in.
