# Lamp and toy picture sequence (day 41)

Say aloud: "These are pictures of real looks, made by an adult." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. One set per group of five; one teacher demonstration is the same set live. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-lamp-toy.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

Apparatus: a small cool enclosed battery lamp with a clear dome, no cord, and an unlit wooden duck beside it, inside the day 38 lined peephole box with the flap shut and the light hole taped, so no room light gets in. Each card is the look through the peephole. Four dated cards: (L1) lamp off, nothing seen; (L2) lamp on, the lamp bright and the duck seen; (L3) lamp off again, nothing seen; (L4) lamp on, the duck seen from the same place. If a card is not clear, repeat once and keep both.

**Questions** (spoken): "Point to the lamp. Does it need light from elsewhere? Point to the duck. Does the duck?"

If there is no lamp, use the cards and say they are recordings and indirect. Cards show real looks only; nothing is filled in.
