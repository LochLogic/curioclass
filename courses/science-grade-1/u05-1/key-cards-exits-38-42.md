# Exit cards, days 38 to 42: teacher key

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-exits-38-42.md. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

- Day 38: the sock (item s05-X38). Key: pair 1. Pair 2 is error A. Pair 3 is shown with help, no letter.
- Day 39: the lights on a fence (item s05-X39). Key: no, it makes its own light. Yes is error B.
- Day 40: the key (item s05-X40). Key: light reaches it. "We wait a long time" is error A. "The door is blue" is shown with help, no letter. The six heard give the because: the door opened and then the key was seen.
- Day 41: the battery lantern (item s05-X41). Key: the lantern in the dark room. The lantern with the room light on is error B (it may be seen only because the room light shines on it). The toy car is shown with help, no letter. The six heard say why: it makes its own light, so nothing else has to shine on it.
- Day 42: the button and the night light (item s05-X42). Key: the night light. "Neither, the night light needs another lamp" is error B. The button after a wait is error A.
