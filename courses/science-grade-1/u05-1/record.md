# Unit 5 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, draw or place the answer. Note the day 38 and day 43 retrieval in the margin beside the name, with the same scale and no score. Mark who you heard in the rotating six on each day, in the heard column. The End columns are the observation on day 46: for item 1, one cell for each of the four components and one for the support the child used; for item 2, one cell for each of the eight components and one for the support. Components are credited from the paper marks and the oral turn; credit a paper component only if the child can point to the mark and say the observation. A case taken from held-back records is marked indirect. Day 47 gives a child the chance to revise the day 46 record; score the revised earlier work, and never score the new day 47 work. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied the cards or read a card aloud, and say so.
