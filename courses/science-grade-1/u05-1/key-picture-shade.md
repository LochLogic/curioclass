# Old shade picture and before-and-after pairs (day 38 retrieval): teacher key

Not printed for children. Goes with picture-shade.md. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

- Ask 1. Key: the sun. A miss: point back to the class chart from kindergarten and lay a finger on the sun.
- Ask 2. Key: pair P1: the same patch, one thing changed. A miss: lay the two pairs side by side and point to what changed in each. This retrieves the old evidence account only; it does not test warming, and no claim about warming is made today.
