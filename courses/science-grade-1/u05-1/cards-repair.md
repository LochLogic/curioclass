# Repair cards, days 39 to 42

Keep these in an envelope. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Each repair is one pair of pictures and one wrong sentence beside one right sentence. Children point to the picture where the light changed (error A) or where nothing else is lit (error B). The wrong sentence is a first idea, said as "someone might say", and nobody is the example of a mistake.

## Error A (days 39 and 41)

**Day 39: the paper bag.** Picture 1: a closed paper bag with a star sticker drawn inside it, shown by dotted lines. Picture 2: the same bag open, the sticker seen. Wrong: "We waited, so the sticker showed up in the dark bag." Right: "We saw the sticker when the bag was open and light got in."

**Day 41: the pencil case.** Picture 1: a closed pencil case with a sticker inside. Picture 2: the same case with the lid open, the sticker seen. Same wrong and right sentences with "pencil case".

## Error B (days 40 and 42)

**Day 40: the book light.** Picture 1: a closed suitcase with a small book light inside, switched off, nothing seen. Picture 2: the same suitcase, the book light switched on, a glow seen at the lid edge. Wrong: "The book light needs a lamp to be seen." Right: "The book light shows with nothing else lit, so it makes its own light."

**Day 42: the drawer.** Picture 1: a closed drawer with a bike light inside, switched off. Picture 2: the same drawer, the light on, a glow seen through a crack. Same wrong and right sentences with "bike light".

Keys: A: picture 2 is where the light changed. B: picture 2 is where nothing else is lit. These cards are never exit or end items.
