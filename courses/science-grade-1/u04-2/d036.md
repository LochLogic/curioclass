# Day 36: Fits or does not fit, and what the strip did

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 35 sent the second check here. Set six table sets face down: three blades on a tape line, the goat family and hen, the sound records. Day 33's record and day 26's record stay on the shelf: they are not scored again today. Hang the sound chart. Read the cards once and keep them sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Only you work the speaker, on a dry table, if you run a record live, cord taped down. Paper confetti bits stay on the plate, away from the speaker, swept up after. A trial that fails is still a result. With no live trial, use the adult-made records and mark them indirect. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The goat and hen pictures are invented for practice; the sound records are real adult recordings. You may pass." Seat six groups of five.

#### 6 min. Lay out the cards

Each child gets the goat family and hen cards and lays the three linden blades on the tape line, bases together. Read Pip's two claims aloud once each: "The young are exact copies." "The second kid is not a goat because of its white ear." Nothing is asked or marked yet; give no help.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six groups come to the teacher table, three minutes each: five children at 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to move. Each child brings their blades and cards. Ask each child once, aloud: [[c1-L36a]] "Does Pip's blade claim fit or not? Point to what shows it." [[c1-L36b]] "Does Pip's goat claim fit or not? Point to what shows it." Then with the baking tray records, [[c1-V36b]] Waiting groups draw the confetti plate with the sound off and the sound on on side 2, unscored; they do not touch the blades again.

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts. Six groups of five at 30 seconds a child fill the eighteen minutes.

#### 5 min. Records

Lay the confetti plate records and the class sound chart at each table. Say each ask once; each child marks side 2. [[c1-V36a]] [[c1-V36c]] "Our chart has three arrows. Put an X on the arrow the confetti records help show." Marked after class from the page.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Collect the pages and blades. Wipe the tables. Cover the cards.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper. No tape: lay the blades along a ruled line.

### At home

**Swap.** No blades: a leaf from a grown plant and two from its young, or seedlings; with none, skip the leaf claim. No confetti: a paper strip hung from a chair.

**Skip.** The group turn and the unit 4 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Lay three leaves bases together and say the young are exact copies; ask fits or not. Then tap a pot lid, hold it, and ask what the lid did. Compare a strip near a playing phone, off and on, with nothing touching. Do not help.
  **Say.** "What did the strip do, and why?" Expect: it moved only when the sound was on.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fits, does not fit, alike, different, sound off, sound on, arrow. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 35's assessment and today's pages are marked after class. Day 37 hands back each page with a picture cue, repairs the largest error groups from days 35 and 36 by letter, and tries a fresh short task.
