# Day 36: Fits or does not fit, and what the strip did

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 35 sent the second check here. Set six table sets face down: three blades on a tape line, the goat family and hen, the sound records. Day 33's record and day 26's record stay on the shelf: they are not scored again today. Hang the sound chart. Read the cards once and keep them sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Only you work the speaker, on a dry table, if you run a record live; the cone is never held against an ear or shared by mouth. Tissue stays away from the speaker openings. A trial that fails is still a result. With no live trial, use the adult-made records and mark them indirect. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The pairs and records are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Seat six groups of five.

#### 6 min. Claim cards

Each child gets the goat family and hen cards and lays three blades on the tape line, bases together. Read Pip's claim aloud once: "The young are exact copies." [[c1-L36a]] "Does it fit or not? Point to what shows it." Then [[c1-L36b]]. Children point, mark and say. Walk and mark. Give no help.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six groups come to the teacher table, three minutes each: five children at 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to move. Ask each child once, aloud: the blade claim again with the tips pointed [[c1-L36a]]; the lid, tapped and then held [[c1-V36b]]; and the two records you ask to see [[c1-V36a]]. Other groups lay the blades and goat cards side by side and draw what the tips show, unscored.

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts. Six groups of five at 30 seconds a child fill the eighteen minutes.

#### 5 min. Records

Each child touches the arrow the strip records help show and says why [[c1-V36c]], then draws sound off and sound on on the page. Walk and mark.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Collect the pages and blades. Wipe the tables. Cover the cards.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper. No tape: lay the blades along a ruled line.

### At home

**Swap.** No blades: three leaves of one plant. No cone: a rolled paper cup. No tissue: a paper strip hung from a chair.

**Skip.** The group turn and the unit 4 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Lay three leaves bases together and say the young are exact copies; ask fits or not. Then tap a pot lid, hold it, and ask what the lid did. Compare a strip near a playing phone, off and on, with nothing touching. Do not help.
  **Say.** "What did the strip do, and why?" Expect: it moved only when the sound was on.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fits, does not fit, alike, different, sound off, sound on, arrow. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 35's assessment and today's pages are marked after class. Day 37 hands back each page with a picture cue, repairs the largest error groups from days 35 and 36 by letter, and tries a fresh short task.
