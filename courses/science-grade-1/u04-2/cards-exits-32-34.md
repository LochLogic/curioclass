# Exit cards, days 32 and 34

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a chart or a test, and each shows a new object, never the strip, the rice or the day 33 cases. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic with apparatus labels only and supplies no result. Beside each card the teacher shows a real, dated recording made by an adult: classroom-level sound, foil folded, smooth edges, nothing sharp, and no fabricated observation. The day 34 fringe records (one with a hand near it, held back as the unfair comparison) and the drum record must be separately collected observations. If no valid record is available, the fresh check stays unfinished and is said so. Every child marks a card on paper or points; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet.

## Day 32: the foil (F32)

Recording: a foil square hangs from a stand while a speaker plays and a card is waved in the air beside it. **Item s04-X32.** Spoken: "Pip says the waving card moved the foil. Touch the picture that is a fair check of whether the sound moved it." Cards: (1) the speaker off and then on, the card held still, nothing touching the foil; (2) a hand pressing the foil while the speaker plays; (3) the card waving with the speaker off. **Key:** card 1. "Sound cannot move foil" is error B. The pressing hand is error C. Card 3 is shown with help, no letter.

## Day 34: the fringe and the drum (G34 and D34)

Recordings: two records of a new paper fringe hanging from a stand, each with the speaker off and then on and the fringe in the same place; in record 1 a hand is held near the fringe, in record 2 nothing is near it. A new drum record: a paper-covered drum tapped gently, and tapped again and gently held. The class sound chart, with its two arrows, is beside the records.

**Item s04-X34a.** Spoken: "Touch the record that is a fair check of whether sound moves the fringe." **Key:** record 2, nothing near the fringe. Record 1 is error C. "Sound cannot do that" is error B.

**Item s04-X34b.** Spoken: "Touch the arrow on our chart that the fringe record helps show." **Key:** "it sounds, so it makes things shake". The other arrow is shown with help. "It is unproven because sound cannot move things" is error B.

**Item s04-X34c.** Spoken: "Touch the arrow on our chart that the drum record helps show." **Key:** "it shakes, so it sounds". The other arrow is shown with help. "The held drum still sounds" is error A.
