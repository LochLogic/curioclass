# Exit cards, days 32 and 34

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a chart or a test, and each shows a new object, never the strip, the rice or the day 33 cases. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic with apparatus labels only and supplies no result. Beside each card the teacher shows a real, dated recording made by an adult: classroom-level sound, foil folded, smooth edges, nothing sharp, and no fabricated observation. The day 34 fringe, fan and cup records must be separately collected observations. If no valid record is available, the fresh check stays unfinished and is said so. Each recording is also printed as a dated three-frame record card (before, during, after) that a child can read: the object drawn still or with short motion lines as the adult saw it, a printed heard mark (three short curved lines by a drawn ear) when a sound was heard and none when not, and a sound on or sound off label. Until the recordings exist, run from paper: the adult runs the trial before the day and draws the three frames by hand with its date. Every child marks a card on paper or points; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-exits-32-34.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

## Day 32: the foil (F32)

Recording: a foil square hangs from a stand while a speaker plays and a card is waved in the air beside it. **Item s04-X32.** Spoken: "Pip says the waving card moved the foil. Touch the picture that is a fair test of whether the sound moved it." Cards: (1) the speaker off and then on, the card held still, nothing touching the foil; (2) a hand pressing the foil while the speaker plays; (3) the card waving with the speaker off.

## Day 34: the fringe, the fan and the cup (G34, N34 and C34)

Recordings, each separately collected and dated by an adult: (1) a new paper fringe hanging from a stand, speaker off and then on, the fringe in the same place and nothing near it; (2) the same fringe with the speaker on and a small hand fan blowing on it; (3) a plastic cup tapped gently, then tapped again and gently held. The class sound chart, with its three arrows, is beside the records. Never invent a result: look at the fringe record first, and if the fringe did not move, the arrow is not shown and the class says so.

**Item s04-X34a.** Spoken, with the fan record: "Does this record show that sound moved the fringe? Touch yes or no."

**Item s04-X34b.** Spoken: "Touch the arrow on our chart that the fringe record helps show."

**Item s04-X34c.** Spoken: "Touch the arrow on our chart that the cup record helps show."
