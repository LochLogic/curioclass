# Day 32: Can sound make something move too?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the tissue strip on separate supports from the speaker. Seal a few grains under the cover and set the bowl on its own stool, not on the speaker's table. Keep key-cards-exits-32-34 (teacher only) for the exit key. Try a steady low sound once.

### Safety and set-up

You alone handle the speaker, rice and loose film, on a dry table. Sound stays at classroom level, never near ears. Nothing is eaten. Children sit back and do not blow.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error A from day 31. Children open the day 31 sheets at the strip's band row and say "I held it, I heard ___." Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. Expect: much quieter. "It still rang loudly" is error A: lay the free and held marks side by side.

#### 5 min. Plan

Keep, change, watch is review from kindergarten. **Ask.** [[s04-P32-1]] "Pip wants to test it. What do we keep, change and watch?" Expect: the strip in the same place and touch nothing; sound off or on; the strip. Press the strip with a finger, then wave a card beside it: it moves both times. Say: "A touch or moving air can move it too, so we keep them out; only the sound changes." If asked about the table, show the strip's stand and the rice's stool.

#### 8 min. Strip demonstration

Each group names an asker, who says "off"; all watch 10 seconds; then "on", 10 seconds. Six groups, about 30 seconds each. Children draw the boxes, circling moved, did not move or not clear.

#### 6 min. Rice demonstration

The same plan and boxes, the grains under the cover. If nothing moves clearly, show the motion sequence cards: "This is a recording, not our test." Note it as indirect.

#### 3 min. Rug

**Ask.** [[s04-P32-2]] "What did the strip do with the sound off, and on?" **Ask.** [[s04-P32-3]] "What did the rice do with the sound off, and on?" Add "it sounds, so it makes things shake" to the chart only if both moved with sound on and not off; otherwise write what each did. "Sound can't move anything" is error B: lay the off and on boxes side by side.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Speaker off; film bagged.

#### 3 min. Exit

You learned this exit in kindergarten (review): picture cards, said to be invented for practice, and a rotating six heard. Say "Invented for practice." Show the foil recording beside its cards. **Ask.** [[s04-X32]] All touch; hear the six. The pressing hand is error C.

### Other rooms

No printer: rule scrap paper into four boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No speaker: a phone playing a steady low sound, held away from ears. No rice: the strip only, hung from a chair back apart from the phone.

**Skip.** The rice and the rug.

- **4 min. Plan.** "We change the sound, off or on. We keep the strip still. We watch it."
  **Say.** "Could the table be moving it?" Expect: it might, so the strip hangs apart.
- **15 min. Test.** Sound off, watch; sound on, watch; once more. Draw both boxes. No touching, no blowing.
- **5 min. Say it.** "When the sound was on, the strip ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X32]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the strip only. Longer: which case was clearer?

### Words and supports

Sound off, sound on, moved, did not move, not clear, recording. Frame: "When the sound was on, it ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Errors B and C go to day 34. Day 33 is a check: groups plan, test and record a paper drum and a paper strip.
