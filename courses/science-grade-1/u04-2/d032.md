# Day 32: Can sound make something move too?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the tissue strip on separate supports from the speaker. Seal a few grains under the cover. Try a steady low sound once; if the strip stays still, ready the motion sequence cards. Print the sound off, sound on records.

### Safety and set-up

You alone handle the speaker, rice and loose film, on a dry table. Sound stays at classroom level, never near ears, never raised to force a result. Nothing is eaten. Children sit back, touch nothing and do not blow. No eye protection needed.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error A from day 31. Children open the day 31 sheets at the strip's band row and say "I held it, I heard ___." Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. Expect: much quieter. "It still rang loudly" is error A: lay the free and held marks side by side.

#### 3 min. Plan

Point to "Can sound make something move too?" **Ask.** [[s04-P32-1]] "Pip wants to test it. What do we change, keep and watch?" Expect: sound off or on; the strip in the same place; the strip. If a child asks "Could the table move it?", board it and show the separate supports.

#### 8 min. Strip demonstration

Each group names an asker, who says "off"; all watch 10 seconds; then "on", 10 seconds. Six groups, about 30 seconds each. Children draw the boxes, ringing moved, did not move or not clear. A not clear pair is repeated once, both kept.

#### 8 min. Rice demonstration

The same plan and boxes, the grains under the cover. If nothing moves clearly, show the motion sequence cards: "This is a recording, not our test." Note it as indirect.

#### 3 min. Rug

**Ask.** [[s04-P32-2]] "What did the strip and the rice do with the sound off, and on?" Hear three. Add "it sounds, so it makes things shake" to the chart only if both moved with sound on and not off; otherwise write what each did. "Sound can't move anything" is error B: lay the off and on boxes side by side.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Speaker off and film bagged by you. Records into folders.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Show the foil recording beside its cards. **Ask.** [[s04-X32]] "Pip says the waving card moved the foil. Touch the picture that is a fair check of whether the sound moved it." All touch; hear the six. The pressing hand is error C.

### Other rooms

No printer: rule scrap paper into four boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No speaker: a phone playing a steady low sound, held away from ears. No rice: the strip only, hung from a chair back apart from the phone.

**Skip.** The rice and the rug.

- **4 min. Plan.** "We change the sound, off or on. We keep the strip still. We watch it."
  **Say.** "Could the table be moving it?" Expect: it might, so the strip hangs apart.
- **15 min. Test.** Sound off, watch; sound on, watch; once more. Draw both boxes. No touching, no blowing.
- **5 min. Say it.** "When the sound was on, the strip ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X32]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the strip only. Longer: which case was clearer?

### Words and supports

Sound off, sound on, moved, did not move, not clear, recording. Frame: "When the sound was on, it ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error B and error C go to day 34, since day 33 teaches nothing. Day 33 is a check: groups of five plan, test and record an unfamiliar paper drum and a paper strip beside the speaker.
