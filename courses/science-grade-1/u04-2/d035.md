# Day 35: New skies, new parts, a seed dish cover

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out six table sets of the check cards face down. Tape a start line at each group's table with the marker beside it and a seed dish, with a branch card, at the end. Cut stiff card into half sheets. Hang the sky chart. Read the cards once and keep them sealed. Day 34 kept the chart; day 36 holds the day 33 records, so none is used here.

### Safety and set-up

Craft materials are soft and blunt: paper, card and tape only. Nothing is worn on a body. The dish is empty: no real seeds. The marker slides once, slowly, along the table. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The cards are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Seat six groups of five.

#### 12 min. Make the model and cover

Read the seed dish problem aloud: a dish of seeds on a yard table, a branch that may slide against it, and birds that must reach the seeds. [[c1-C35a]] "Tell me which part of the snail you would copy, by the job it does there." Each child makes a paper cover, draws the dish and the cover on the page [[c1-C35b]], and slides the marker once from the taped line, then circles what the check showed [[c1-C35c]]. Walk and mark. Give no help.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six groups come to the teacher table, three minutes each: five children at 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to move. Ask each child once, aloud, with cards in hand: the covered dot or moon [[c1-S35a]] or [[c1-S35b]]; the frog [[c1-A35b]]; the basil arrow [[c1-P35b]]; and "point through your sketch, how does the shape do its job?" [[c1-C35d]]. Other groups work at their own tables with the check cards: touch the record that shows when stars are seen [[c1-S35c]], tell what the roots do on the basil card [[c1-P35a]], and tell what the snail shell does [[c1-A35a]]. At the turn each child gives you those three answers in one go, a touch and two short sentences, about 10 of the 30 seconds; the five asks above take the other 20. You mark them.

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

#### 2 min. Collect and clean up

Collect the pages and the covers. Wipe the tables.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper. No stiff card: fold a sheet of paper into an arch.

### At home

**Swap.** No marker: a crayon. No dish: a cup. No stiff card: a cereal box side.

**Skip.** The group turn and the unit 4 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Tell the seed dish problem and ask once: "Which part of a snail do you copy?" Your child makes the cover, slides a crayon toward it once, and draws what happened. Ask where a shadow dot goes after two dots in a row and what a plant did when its window moved. Do not help.
  **Say.** "How does the shape do its job?" Expect: it stands over the dish with one open side.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Cover, gap, open side, arch, arrow, shown with help. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 closes the first check; the sheets are marked after class. Day 36 is the second check, on resemblance and on what a sound does to a strip. Day 37 hands each sheet back with a picture cue.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
