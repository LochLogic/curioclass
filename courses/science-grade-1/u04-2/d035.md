# Day 35: New skies, new parts, a seed dish cover

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out six table sets of the check cards face down. Tape a start line at each group's table with the marker beside it and a seed dish, with a branch card, at the end. Cut stiff card into half sheets. Hang the sky chart. Read the cards once and keep them sealed. Day 34 kept the chart; day 36 holds the day 33 records.

### Safety and set-up

Craft materials are soft and blunt: paper, card and tape only. Nothing is worn on a body. The dish is empty: no real seeds. The marker slides once, slowly, along the table. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 2 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The cards are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Seat six groups of five. Each table turns its check cards face up.

#### 6 min. Picture marks

Each child marks side 2 of the student page, which prints the pictures from the table cards. Say each ask once, then wait about a minute; give no help. [[c1-S35a]] [[c1-S35b]] [[c1-S35c]] [[c1-P35b]] [[c1-A35b]] These are marked after class from the page.

#### 9 min. Make the model and cover

Read the seed dish problem aloud: a dish of seeds on a yard table, a branch that may slide against it, and birds that must reach the seeds. Each child makes a paper cover, draws the dish and the cover on side 1 [[c1-C35b]], and slides the marker once from the taped line, then circles what the slide showed [[c1-C35c]]. Go table by table, about 75 seconds a table: ask each child once, [[c1-C35a]] "Tell me which part of the snail you would copy, by the job it does there," 15 seconds each. The helper watches each slide so the circles can be checked against it. Give no help.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six groups come to the teacher table, three minutes each: five children at 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to move. Each child brings their cover and page. Ask once, aloud: [[c1-C35d]] "Point through your sketch: how does the shape of your cover do its job?" Then, with the basil card, [[c1-P35a]] Then, with the snail card, [[c1-A35a]] Three short answers fill the 30 seconds. Collect each child's page and cover as the group leaves. Waiting groups finish their covers and drawings, then draw one thing they saw on the back of the page, unscored.

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper. No stiff card: fold a sheet of paper into an arch.

### At home

**Swap.** No marker: a crayon. No dish: a cup. No stiff card: a cereal box side.

**Skip.** The group turn and the unit 4 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Tell the seed dish problem and ask once: "Which part of a snail do you copy?" Your child makes the cover, slides a crayon toward it once, and draws what happened. Ask where a shadow dot goes after two dots in a row and what a plant did when its pot was turned. Do not help.
  **Say.** "How does the shape do its job?" Expect: it stands over the dish with one open side.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Cover, gap, open side, arch, arrow, shown with help. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 closes the first check; the sheets are marked after class. Day 36 is the second check, on resemblance and on what a sound does to a strip. Day 37 hands each sheet back with a picture cue.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
