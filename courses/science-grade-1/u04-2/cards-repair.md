# Repair cards (day 34)

Three cards, printed one set for each group of five and one large set for the teacher. The wrong sentences are read aloud as invented sentences: no child is the example of a mistake. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture.

## Card A: free beside held (error A)

The day 33 ruler record, flicked free and then gently held, laid beside the day 31 free-and-held strip. Wrong, read aloud: "The held ruler keeps sounding." Right, read aloud: "When we held it still, the sound got quiet." Children lay the free-and-held pair on the day 33 ruler record and say "I held it, I heard ___".

## Card B: off beside on (error B)

The fixed-paper sound-off and sound-on records laid side by side. Wrong, read aloud: "Sound can't move anything." Right, read aloud: "When the sound was on, the paper moved; when it was off, it did not." Children point to which box shows motion.

## Card C: no touch (error C)

The day 33 ribbon record with nothing touching, beside the ribbon with a hand pressing it. Wrong, read aloud: "I held it while it played and it moved, so sound did it." Right, read aloud: "We did not touch it, and it moved only when the sound was on." Children lay the no-touch comparison and name what stayed the same.

## Picture directions

One strip of three pictures for each group letter, no words but the big word under each picture: free beside held, off beside on, no touch. The teacher teaches nothing new.
