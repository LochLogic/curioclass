# Day 33 records: the unfamiliar drum, the ruler and the ribbon

Keep these sealed until day 33. They appear in no teaching day. Say aloud: "These are recordings of real tests." Each card is headed with the date, plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no result written on any card. Each card is a real, unlabelled record made by an adult, never an invented result; with no valid record, leave that evidence incomplete.

## Item 1 records (the ruler beside an unfamiliar paper drum)

Apparatus: a plastic ruler held flat on a table edge with its free end under 10 cm, flicked with a fingertip, beside an unfamiliar paper-covered drum. Records, one card each: ruler flicked alone; drum tapped alone with one gentle fingertip tap; equal ruler flicks with the free end free; equal ruler flicks with the free end gently held. Each is marked recording. Children ask for these under their plan and mark which they chose; the unfamiliar drum is also tapped gently by the group for a direct comparison. Records are marked indirect.

## Item 2 records (the ribbon beside a speaker)

Apparatus: a thin paper ribbon hung from a stand beside a speaker that plays, shown with the ribbon on the same table and with the ribbon on a separate support. Records, one card each: ribbon on the shared table, sound on; ribbon on a separate support, sound off then on, in a fixed position, no touching; and a trial with a hand pressing the ribbon. Children choose the comparisons they ask for.

## Check before use

The teacher checks every record before use. No hands, breath or fan move the ribbon during sound-only trials. Unknown results are never filled in. The ribbon is never used elsewhere in days 28 to 34, and is not tissue, foil, card or fringe.
