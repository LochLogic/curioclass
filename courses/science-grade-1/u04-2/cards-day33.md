# Day 33 records: the unfamiliar drum, the ruler and the ribbon

Keep these sealed until day 33. They appear in no teaching day. Say aloud: "These are recordings of real tests." Each card is headed with the date, plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no cause or claim written on any card. Each card is a real, unlabelled record made by an adult, never an invented result; with no valid record, leave that evidence incomplete.

## Item 1 records (the ruler beside an unfamiliar paper drum)

Apparatus: a plastic ruler held flat on a table edge with its free end under 10 cm, flicked with a fingertip, beside an unfamiliar paper-covered drum. Records, one card each: ruler flicked alone; drum tapped alone with one gentle fingertip tap; equal ruler flicks, one left to ring; equal ruler flicks, one gently stilled by a fingertip on the free end right after the flick. Each is marked recording. Each item 1 card is a three-frame record like the motion sequence cards: before, during and after, each frame headed with the date the adult made it. The during frame shows what the adult observed: the ruler's free end drawn blurred when it moved, and a printed heard mark (three short curved lines by the ear of a drawn listener) when a sound was heard, one small curved line when it was quieter, and no mark when nothing was heard. A key strip at the foot of each card shows the three marks so a child can read them. The mark records what was heard; it names no cause. The finished cards come from real trials an adult runs and records; until they exist, run from paper: an adult runs the four trials before day 33 and draws each card by hand with the date and the heard mark. Children ask for these under their plan and mark which they chose; the unfamiliar drum is also tapped gently by the group for a direct comparison. Records are marked indirect.

## Item 2 records (the ribbon beside a speaker)

Apparatus: a thin paper ribbon hung from a stand beside a speaker that plays, shown with the ribbon on the same table and with the ribbon on a separate support. Records, one card each: ribbon on the shared table, sound on; ribbon on a separate support, sound off then on, in a fixed position, no touching; and a trial with a hand pressing the ribbon. Each card is a three-frame record (before, during, after, each dated), the during frame showing the ribbon drawn still or with short motion lines, as the adult saw it, and a printed sound on or sound off label. Until the finished cards exist, an adult runs the trials before day 33 and draws each card by hand with its date. Children choose the comparisons they ask for.

## Check before use

Print one set of each item's records for the teacher table and three sealed sets of each for the waiting groups: groups 1, 3 and 5 take item 2 from records at their table, groups 2, 4 and 6 item 1. The teacher checks every record before use. No hands, breath or fan move the ribbon during sound-only trials. Unknown results are never filled in. The ribbon is never used elsewhere in days 28 to 34, and is not tissue, foil, card or fringe.
