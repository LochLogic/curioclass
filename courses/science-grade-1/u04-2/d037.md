# Day 37: Fix one mark and try a fresh card

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 36, fix one picture cue to each child's sheets, tied to what it most needs. Count the error letters A to L and note the three largest groups. Set the repair cards by letter. Keep the exit cards sealed. Check the streamer exit's two arrow cards.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; children sit. Any child may pass. Wrong sentences are read as invented; no child is the example.

### The day

#### 8 min. Read feedback and revise

Hand back day 35's assessment and day 36's assessment, the sheets. Say the three commonest cues aloud (2 minutes). Each child finds their own cue, points to the mark and says what they saw, then fixes one mark with a pencil (6 minutes). Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. New work is unscored; the first try is kept.

#### 12 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs: error letters A to L. The three largest letter groups come to you, 3 minutes each and 1 minute to change. Every other child works at the table whose repair card matches their cue, from its picture directions; a helper may take one table. The moves, by letter: A and B, say the dots or moon pictures in time order, placing nothing. C, lay a day card beside a night card and say hidden, not gone. D and E, the root close-up, then the two sill pictures, saying what changed. F, the frog cards in order, saying what each part does. G, say what the arch's shape does with the gap above the dish. H and I, three blades with the tips marked; a goat family and a hen. J, lay the held tray record beside the free one; say the sound stopped. K and L, say what moved on the confetti records off and on, and that nothing touched the plate.

#### 7 min. Exit: fresh task

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[c1-X37a]] **Ask.** [[c1-X37b]] **Ask.** [[c1-X37c]] **Ask.** [[c1-X37d]] All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. Sun staying put is error A; a picture on the lid is error G; exact copies is error H; sound cannot do that is error K. With no valid records, the task is unfinished, and you say so.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect the sheets. Note on the unit 4 record sheet who fixed a mark.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper. No helper adult: the pictured cards carry the small groups.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say the cue aloud. No cards: draw each on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the unit 4 record sheet.

- **10 min. Look back.** Find the picture cue and fix one mark on the day 35 or day 36 page.
- **10 min. Repair.** Lay the two sill pictures side by side, or the strip off and on.
  **Say.** "What changed, and what did it do?" Expect: the window moved and the leaves turned, or the strip moved only with sound.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[c1-X37a]], [[c1-X37b]], [[c1-X37c]] and [[c1-X37d]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear the six only. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fix, cue, fresh, alike, arrow. Frame: "I fixed ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 hands on nothing: the fresh task is never scored. Day 38 opens unit 5 with a box puzzle about seeing.
