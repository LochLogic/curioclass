# Day 37: Fix one mark and try a fresh card

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 36, fix one picture cue to each child's sheets, tied to what it most needs. Count the error letters A to L and note the four largest groups. Set the repair cards by letter. Keep the exit cards sealed. Day 36 left the pages for today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; children sit. Any child may pass. Wrong sentences are read as invented; no child is the example.

### The day

#### 8 min. Read feedback and revise

Hand back day 35's assessment and day 36's assessment, the sheets. Say the three commonest cues aloud (2 minutes). Each child finds their own cue, points to the mark and says what they saw, then fixes one mark with a pencil (6 minutes). Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. New work is unscored; the first try is kept.

#### 12 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs: the twelve error letters A to L, by letter. Four groups, 3 minutes each with you, taken from the four largest; the other letters use the pictured repair cards, 1 minute each inside the 12, and a helper adult may take a group. Error A and B group: slide a finger along the dots, then place the covered one. Error C group: lay a day card beside a night card. Error D and E group: the root close-up and two sill pictures. Error F and G group: frog cards in order, then an arch beside a picture card. Error H and I group: three blades, tips marked; a goat family and a hen. Error J, K and L group: lid tapped and held; strip off and on; the fair pair.

#### 7 min. Fresh task and exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[c1-X37a]] "Touch where the 2 o'clock dot will be." **Ask.** [[c1-X37b]] "Touch the lid that can do the job." **Ask.** [[c1-X37c]] "Touch the picture that shows Pip is wrong." **Ask.** [[c1-X37d]] "Touch the arrow the streamer records help show." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. Sun staying put is error A; a picture on the lid is error G; exact copies is error H; sound cannot do that is error K. With no valid records, the task is unfinished, and you say so.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect the sheets. Note on the unit 4 record sheet who fixed a mark.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper. No helper adult: the pictured cards carry the small groups.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say the cue aloud. No cards: draw each on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the unit 4 record sheet.

- **10 min. Look back.** Find the picture cue and fix one mark on the day 35 or day 36 page.
- **10 min. Repair.** Lay the two sill pictures side by side, or the strip off and on.
  **Say.** "What changed, and what did it do?" Expect: the window moved and the leaves turned, or the strip moved only with sound.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[c1-X37a]], [[c1-X37b]], [[c1-X37c]] and [[c1-X37d]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear the six only. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fix, cue, fresh, alike, arrow. Frame: "I fixed ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 hands on nothing: the fresh task is never scored. Day 38 opens unit 5 with a box puzzle about seeing.
