# Feedback picture-cue cards (days 33 and 34)

A small card for each cue, printed and cut. After day 33 the teacher fixes one card to each child's record, tied to the component that record most needs. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. A cue points at the next look; it never gives the answer.

- **Question** (a question mark): what did we want to find out?
- **Plan** (a hand pointing at two pictures): what did you ask to see, and what stayed the same?
- **Free and held** (a thing, then a hand holding it): what happened when it was held?
- **Off and on** (two boxes): what did the strip do with sound off, and with sound on?
- **No touch** (a hand crossed out): was anything touching it?
- **Arrow** (an arrow between two boxes): which way does the arrow go, and what record shows it?

The spoken prompts read to the whole group on day 34: "Point to the mark and say what you saw." "What stayed the same?" Each child hears the cue, points to the mark and says the observation; a child who can shows it. The paper marks carry components one to five and the oral turn carries component six.
