# Motion sequence cards (days 32 and 34)

These are the no-kit record, used when the speaker cannot move a sample at classroom volume, and on day 34. Each card is made by an adult from a real, dated recording of the actual strip or rice, printed as three frames in a row: sound off, sound on, sound off again. Each frame is headed with the date and the words sound off or sound on, and the whole card is headed recording. No frame is drawn from imagination, and a card with no valid recording is left blank and said to be unfinished: never invent success. Say aloud, "This is a recording, not our test." Plain frames, no arrows and no caption that gives the result; the child says what moved. The strip card shows the strip hanging from its own stand, apart from the speaker; the rice card shows the grains on film under the raised clear cover. Children mark the sequence in the sound off and sound on boxes of their two-box record, and the teacher notes the result as indirect.

Day 34 uses a paper sound-and-motion set in the same form: a drum-and-tap record and a hanging fringe record, three frames each, each frame dated, to lay beside the class sound chart. These are separate observations from the exit records.
