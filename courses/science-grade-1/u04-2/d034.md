# Day 34: Look again and tell which way the arrow goes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fix one picture cue to each child's sheet. Set the repair cards and the open day 33 records by group A, B and C. Print the revised accounts. Keep the exit cards sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; children sit. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read feedback and revise

Hand back day 33's assessment. Tell the three commonest comments aloud (2 minutes). Each child finds their own cue, points to the mark and says what they saw, then draws the three arrows the right way round on the revised account (8 minutes). Only the rotating six dictate a sentence, 20 seconds each; the others write or draw "it shakes, so it sounds" under the arrow. New work is unscored.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs: errors B and C from day 32, and day 33's gaps. Three groups, 3 minutes each with you; the two moves take 30 seconds each. Error A group: lay the day 33 ruler record, free then gently held, beside the day 31 free and held strip; "I held it, I heard ___." Error B group: lay the sound off and sound on records side by side. Error C group: lay the day 33 ribbon record with nothing touching beside the ribbon with a hand pressing it; name what stayed the same.

#### 7 min. Exit: fresh evidence

As in kindergarten (review): picture cards, said aloud to be invented for practice, and a rotating six heard. Lay the chart's three arrows beside the fringe record, the fan record and the cup record. Look at the fringe record first; if it did not move, ask [[s04-X34a]] only. **Ask.** [[s04-X34a]] "Look at the fan record. Does this record show that sound moved the fringe? Touch yes or no." **Ask.** [[s04-X34b]] "Touch the arrow the fringe record helps show." **Ask.** [[s04-X34c]] "Touch the arrow the cup record helps show." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. "The held cup still sounds" is error A; "sound cannot move things" is error B; yes to the fan record is error C.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect the sheets. Mark answered questions on the board.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three arrows on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say the cue and point. No records: use your child's day 33 drawings.

**Skip.** The repair groups.

- **10 min. Look back.** Find the picture cue and fix one mark.
- **10 min. Repair.** Lay the sound off and sound on drawings side by side.
  **Say.** "Which box shows it moving?" Expect: the sound on box, when nothing touched it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X34a]], [[s04-X34b]] and [[s04-X34c]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair one letter. Longer: plan a fair test for a new thing beside the speaker.

### Words and supports

Arrow, record, sound off, sound on, held, no touch, fair test. Frames: "It shakes, so it sounds." "It sounds, so it makes things shake." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 closes unit 4. The revised day 33 sheets are scored after class; the fresh check is never scored. The chart and each day 33 record are kept for day 36. Day 35 is a check on new sky sequences and a material solution.
