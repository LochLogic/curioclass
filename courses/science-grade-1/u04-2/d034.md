# Day 34: Look again and tell which way the arrow goes

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 33, fix one picture cue to each child's sheet, tied to what it most needs. Set the repair cards and the open day 33 records by group A, B and C. Hang the class sound chart and question board. Print the revised accounts. Keep the exit cards sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; children sit. Any child may pass. Wrong sentences are read as invented; no child is the example.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read feedback and revise

Hand back day 33's assessment, the two-panel sheets. Tell the three commonest comments aloud (2 minutes). Each child finds their own cue, points to the mark and says what they saw, then draws both arrows the right way round on the revised account and dictates a sentence (8 minutes). Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. New work is unscored.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs: error B and error C from day 32, and the gaps day 33 showed by letter. Three groups, 3 minutes each with you; the moves fill the tenth minute. Others use the repair cards' picture directions; a helper adult may take a group. Error A group: lay the day 33 held record beside the day 31 free and held strip; "I held it, I heard ___." Error B group: lay the sound off and sound on records side by side; point to the box that shows motion. Error C group: lay the no-touch record beside the hand-pressing record; name what stayed the same.

#### 7 min. Fresh evidence and exit

Say "Invented for practice." Lay the chart's two arrows beside the two fringe records and the drum record. **Ask.** [[s04-X34a]] "Touch the record that is a fair check of whether sound moves the fringe." **Ask.** [[s04-X34b]] "Touch the arrow the fringe record helps show." **Ask.** [[s04-X34c]] "Touch the arrow the drum record helps show." All touch; hear the six, 20 seconds each. "The held drum still sounds" is error A; "sound cannot do that" is error B; the hand-near record is error C. With no valid records, the fresh check is unfinished, and you say so.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect the sheets. Revisit the board; mark the questions answered.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two arrows on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say the cue and point. No records: use your child's day 33 drawings.

**Skip.** The repair groups.

- **10 min. Look back.** Find the picture cue and fix one mark.
- **10 min. Repair.** Lay the sound off and sound on drawings side by side.
  **Say.** "Which box shows it moving?" Expect: the sound on box, when nothing touched it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X34a]], [[s04-X34b]] and [[s04-X34c]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair one letter. Longer: plan a fair check for a new thing beside the speaker.

### Words and supports

Arrow, record, sound off, sound on, held, no touch. Frames: "It shakes, so it sounds." "It sounds, so it makes things shake." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 closes unit 4. The revised day 33 sheets are scored after class; the fresh check is never scored. The chart and each day 33 record are kept for day 36. Day 35 is a check on new sky sequences and a material solution.
