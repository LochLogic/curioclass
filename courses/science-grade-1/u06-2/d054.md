# Day 54: Show what your device does

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the two marks, 3 and 6 strips, in the one closed lane with a low card at each. Seal the message card, three quick. Station receivers' cards. Put each child's prepared device, paper sketch and paper trial record in group trays. Choose the order of the six groups.

### Safety and set-up

One group in the lane, under your eye; a helper adult watches the waiting groups and never helps with answers. String low and gently straight, walk round it. Light aimed down at the low card, never at eyes, battery compartments secured by you; sound at classroom level. Cups rest outside the ear. No emergency patterns. Cups are adult-punched. A broken device: mark device failed. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. Nobody helps today, and you may pass. A miss is part of what we find out." The message is the helper's: picture is ready, three quick, sent for the first time today. Groups of five take places.

#### 18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each

One group in the lane: five 30-second turns plus 30 seconds to change over. In its turn a child sends three quick at the near mark, then at the farther mark, with the cup re-set and the string pulled gently straight between marks: [[s06-E54b]]. The why and the judgment come in Records, not in the lane. The receiver at each mark holds up a picture before the sender's card is shown; roles pass on so every child receives. No coaching. Cup devices note tight or not. Mark each child at once on the unit 6 record. Credit a mark only if it is what the receiver marked. Waiting groups, at their desks and unscored, place the six familiar device-evidence cards on the three-box picture and draw a reason for one. Write who was heard.

#### 6 min. Records

Every child finishes the paper trial record: draws in the box how the device carries the message (sender, device, receiver, with an arrow) and rings what the two marks show (near and farther, near only, not yet). A spoken or drawn why meets the goal. Hear the rotating six say theirs, 30 seconds each, and note the model words used: sender, device, receiver, the friend's mark. After class, mark item 2's why and judgment from each record, and [[s06-E54a]] from the day 53 sketch and the made device; a drawing with no built part is error B.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Records are collected and kept with the devices for day 55.

### Other rooms

No lane: pictured invented trials; practice reading records only. No printer: draw the record's boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: paper sheets end to end.

**Skip.** The group turns and the cards.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Lay 3 strips for near and 6 for farther. Your child sends three quick with their device at each mark; you mark a picture first, then see the card. Do not help. Then your child shows the picture of sender, device and receiver and tells how the device carries the message.
  **Say.** "How does your device carry the message?" Expect: the sender, then the device, then the friend; the friend's mark may differ.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the two sends and the why; keep the order. Longer: none; a check stays a check.

### Words and supports

Sender, device, receiver. Frame: "My device carries ___ to ___." Pointing counts; read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Gaps go to day 55 by letter: error A with error F, error C with error D, error B with error E. Day 55 returns day 54's assessment.
