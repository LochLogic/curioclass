# Day 53: A new problem, a better device

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pre-cut the cover, stand, shutter and drum-surface pieces. Put out each child's student-built message device and paper revised sketch. Choose today's six. Hide the held-back exit cards. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-52-55.

### Safety and set-up

Cups are pre-punched by an adult only; string stays low, no loops on bodies. No test today. Light is never aimed at eyes; sound stays at classroom level. The new pattern is not an emergency pattern. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs in from day 52's exit: error F. Say "Invented for practice." Show repair card F: the flap light with a one-try record beside a two-try record. Children say which tells more. Expect: the two tries.

#### 3 min. The new problem

Say "Invented for practice." Show the display-helper case card: a helper at a display table far across the room must be told that a picture is ready. The class agrees the new pattern, three quick, and that the farther mark will be six strips. **Ask.** "Why do people send messages far away?" Children point to the bell or the display table. Expect: a friend far away knows without shouting. Add improve to the design-cycle chart, after test.

#### 22 min. Prepare

Five minutes: each child draws a dated sketch of their device, and in the box "the trial that told me" the chart trial that says to add a part. Thirteen minutes: each child adds or changes one designed part on their device, a cover, a stand, a shutter or a drum surface, with pre-cut pieces and tape. A borrowed flashlight is not building. A partner's making part goes in the last box. While they build, move round and take each child's sentence "I added ___ because ___", 20 seconds each, and hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each: **Ask.** "What did you add, and which trial told you to?" A part with no trial behind it: ask which trial. Four minutes: each child revises their own sketch to show what was built and why it should help.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Store devices; collect pieces.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Show the trial record card first and read its two rows aloud. **Ask.** [[s06-X53a]] "The record shows what got through the wall. Draw a device that could get the message to the friend" (70 seconds), in the side 1 box. **Ask.** [[s06-X53b]] "Touch the row of the record that tells you your device should work." All touch; mark every child. Hear three say why. After class, fix one picture cue to each sketch.

### Other rooms

No cups: a drum surface or flap from a cereal box. No printer: draw the sheet's boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No cups or cardboard: a paper tube and a flap cut from a cereal box.

**Skip.** The spoken check and the partner box.

- **6 min. The new problem.** Say: "A helper far across the room needs to know a picture is ready."
  **Say.** "Why do people send messages far away?" Expect: so a friend far off knows without walking over.
- **15 min. Make it better.** Your child draws the device, names the trial that told them, adds one part, and draws it.
  **Say.** "What did you add, and which trial told you to?" Expect: a part, and a trial like the friend marked a different card.
- **3 min. Say it.** Write the words: I added ___ because ___.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X53a]] and [[s06-X53b]]: read the record aloud first: "The drum tap got through the wall; the lamp blink did not." Your child draws a device to reach a friend behind a cardboard wall, then says which part of the record tells you it should work.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the redraw. Longer: a second note of which trial.

### Words and supports

Improve, shutter, stand, cover, wall. Frame: "I added ___ because ___." Pointing and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exits show who chooses a feature with no trial behind it (shown with help). Day 54 checks today's device and sketch, with no coaching.
