# Repair cards, days 52, 53 and 55

Keep these in an envelope. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Each repair is one picture pair or one picture and one wrong sentence beside one right sentence. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

**C (day 52): the untried mark.** A paper drum on a stool at the sender line of the lane, the lane seen from above. Marks at two strips (near) and five strips (farther) each have a small matching pair of pictures; a third, dotted mark far beyond, drawn with a question mark, is labelled not tried. Wrong: "It worked near, so it works anywhere." Right: "It worked at the near mark; we tried the farther mark too, and only those two marks are tested." Children say which marks were tested.

**D (day 52): one change or two.** Two trial pairs of a drum on a stool. In the first, the same drum is tapped the same soft way at the near mark and at the farther mark. In the second, the drum is tapped softly at the near mark and hit hard at the farther mark. Wrong: "It sounded different far away because it was far." Right: "Two things changed, the distance and how hard the drum was hit, so distance alone cannot be the reason; tap the same way and change only the distance." Children point to the pair with one change.

**F (day 53): one try or two.** A flap light, with two records. The first record has one box, a match. The second has two boxes, a match and a match. Wrong: "It worked once, so it always will." Right: "It worked twice the same way; try it again to be surer." Children say which record tells more.

## Day 55 group moves (fresh cards, same errors)

**Group A with F.** Three pairs of small pictures, sender's card then friend's mark: a lamp-blink pair that match, a pair that differ, a pair that match. Children say same or not the same for each. Then a record of a box drum with one try, beside a record with two tries; children say which tells more.

**Group C with D.** Two result sheets for a flap light laid side by side, near and farther, with a question mark box for a mark not tried. Then two pictured pairs: in the first the same card and the same signal at both marks; in the second the card stays and the flap is taken off at the farther mark. Children say what was tested and point to the pair with one change.

**Group B with E, a written move.** Five pictures in a row, each with one big word: sketch, made device, fix, strips, count. A sketch beside a made device with one part missing; an arrow to fix; six strips laid touching along a taped line; a count box. Children follow the pictures on their own, then recount.
