# Day 55: Fix a step and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the day 54 records by gap: error A with error F, error C with error D, error B with error E. Lay the lane with paper floor markers. Set out the repair cards and the fresh message card. Hide the held-back exit cards. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-52-55.

### Safety and set-up

One group in the lane, under your eye. String low and gently straight. Light aimed down at the low card, never at eyes, batteries secured; sound at classroom level. Cups rest outside the ear. No emergency patterns. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 8 min. Read feedback and revise

Two minutes: read the three most common day 53 sketch comments aloud. Six minutes: each child finds the cue on their sketch and corrects or adds one picture or label. New work is unscored. Hear six.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs in from day 54's assessment, by letter. Say "Invented for practice." Group A with F, 4 minutes with you: sender's cards beside friends' marks, same or not the same, then a one-try record beside a two-try record. Group C with D, 4 minutes: near and farther results side by side, then point to the pair with one change. Group B with E works alone from the written move: the sketch beside the made device, then six strips laid touching along a taped line and recounted.

#### 9 min. Exit: fresh trial

Six minutes. A child whose send or receipt failed on day 54 repairs the named step (signal, aim or string, distance, receiver) and tries once at the failed mark with the fresh message card, about 20 seconds each, up to eighteen trials. The message is new, so the fixed step is what we test. The new mark replaces the day 54 value, marked revised, and earns nothing else. A miss is evidence. Record any beyond eighteen as unfinished. The others redraw the three-box picture with arrows. Three minutes. Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X55a]] "Touch the step to fix." All point. **Ask.** [[s06-X55b]] "Point to the step you repaired, and then to what the friend marked after." Hear six who repaired.

#### 3 min. Collect and close

Collect sketches, products and trial records for day 67. **Ask.** "How can we tell a friend across the playground it is time to come in, without shouting?" Write the class's one answer as sheets come in, and beside it three or four dictated words.

### Other rooms

No lane: pictured trials, said aloud to be invented; nothing is scored.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: paper sheets end to end.

**Skip.** The group repair and the lane.

- **8 min. Fix the sketch.** Your child fixes one picture.
- **10 min. Fix a step.** Your child names one step that went wrong, fixes it and tries once with a new message: one long, one quick.
  **Say.** "Which step did you fix, and what did the friend mark after?" Expect: the string, aim, signal or distance, and the friend's actual mark.
- **5 min. Say it.** Write your child's answer to: how can we tell a friend across the playground it is time to come in, without shouting?
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X55a]] and [[s06-X55b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one repair and one try. Longer: a second step fixed.

### Words and supports

Repair, step. Frame: "I fixed the ___, and the friend marked ___." Pointing counts.
