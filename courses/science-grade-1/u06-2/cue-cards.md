# Feedback picture-cue cards (day 55)

After day 53, fix one small picture cue to each child's revised sketch. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. The cues are: a star beside a feature that names the trial behind it; a question mark beside a feature with no trial behind it; a small hand beside a part that is really made; a pencil beside a part that is only drawn; an arrow from the changed part toward the receiver where the sentence about how it should help is missing. Each cue has one big word. One cue per child, kept to what the child drew. A child who can point to the cue and say what to change shows it. The teacher tells the three most common cues aloud on day 55.
