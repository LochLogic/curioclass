# Familiar device-evidence cards (day 54, waiting children)

Say aloud: "These are cards from our own days." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. While a group takes its turn, other children place six familiar cards on the sender, device and receiver picture on their own, unscored and without help, then draw a reason for one card.

1. The box drum being tapped gently (device).
2. The flashlight with a cardboard flap (device).
3. The cup pair with the string gently straight (device).
4. The line up card held at the chest (sender's message).
5. The page with a picture marked by a friend (receiver).
6. The results chart with a near row and a farther row (receiver's marks).

The teacher notes which box each card went in and which model words the child used. No result is written on any card.
