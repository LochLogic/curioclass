# Display-helper case card (day 53)

Say aloud: "This is invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture.

**Picture 1.** A classroom seen from above. At the near wall a child stands at a line with a device on a small table. At the far wall a helper sits at a display table with a flat frame on it. Between them, the lane with two marks, drawn dotted. Big words: picture is ready. The printed line across the top reads: invented for practice.

**Picture 2.** Two small pictures side by side. On the left, a bell hanging by a school door, with a child walking toward it. On the right, a display table with one framed picture. Under them one big question for the teacher to read aloud: why do people send messages far away? The class answers by pointing to one picture or the other and saying why. No phone, radio or screen is drawn.

**Picture 3.** The new pattern, three quick dashes, drawn on a card. Big words: ready.
