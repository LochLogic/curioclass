# Exit cards, days 52, 53 and 55

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first, except for the child's own record on day 55. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or says yes or no; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. None shows a repair card, the trial cards or the day 54 pieces. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-exits-52-55.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

## Day 52 (items s06-X52a and s06-X52b)

**X52a.** Pip the puppet holding a clapper made of two wooden blocks, and one finished trial: the sender's card and the friend's matching mark. Pip's speech line, read once: it will work every time. Spoken: "Is Pip sure yet? Say yes or no."

**X52b.** Two records. The first: a clapper, two boxes, the friend's mark matching the sender's card in both. The second: a rattle can, two boxes, a match in the first and a miss in the second. Spoken: "Touch the record that gives a reason to depend on the device."

## Day 53 (items s06-X53a and s06-X53b)

**X53a.** Shown after the X53b trial record card has been read aloud. A card: a friend sitting behind a cardboard wall, a helper on the other side. Spoken: "A helper must tell the friend the paint is dry. The record shows what got through the wall. Draw a device that could get the message to the friend." Each child draws in the exit box on side 1 of the student page.

**X53b.** A trial record card, shown first and read aloud: two rows. Lamp blink behind the wall: the friend marked nothing. Drum tap behind the wall: the friend marked the right picture. Spoken: "Touch the row of the record that tells you your device should work."

## Day 55 (items s06-X55a and s06-X55b)

**X55a.** A pictured failed trial: a flap light aimed up at the ceiling, not at the low card by the friend, and the friend's mark not matching the sender's card. Three cards: a hand aiming the light down at the low card; a child with an open mouth shouting; a different message card. Spoken: "Touch the step to fix."

**X55b.** The child's own trial record. Spoken: "Point to the step you repaired, and then to what the friend marked after." No card is shown. The practice message is new, one long then one quick, drawn as a long dash and a short dash, and was not used on days 48 to 54. The child sends it once after their own repair. Apply only the letter that matches the claim the child makes (A, C, D or F); other answers are recorded in plain words.
