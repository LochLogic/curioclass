# Exit cards, days 52, 53 and 55

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first, except for the child's own record on day 55. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or says yes or no; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. None shows a repair card, the trial cards or the day 54 pieces.

## Day 52 (items s06-X52a and s06-X52b)

**X52a.** Pip the puppet holding a clapper made of two wooden blocks, and one finished trial: the sender's card and the friend's matching mark. Pip's speech line, read once: it will work every time. Spoken: "Is Pip sure yet? Say yes or no." **Key:** no, try it again. Yes is error F.

**X52b.** Two records. The first: a clapper, two boxes, the friend's mark matching the sender's card in both. The second: a rattle can, two boxes, a match in the first and a miss in the second. Spoken: "Touch the record that gives a reason to depend on the device." **Key:** the clapper record. The rattle can record is error F.

## Day 53 (items s06-X53a and s06-X53b)

**X53a.** Two cards of a friend sitting behind a cardboard screen. A lamp blinks on the left side; a drum is tapped on the left side. No friend's mark is drawn on either card. Spoken: "Touch the one that can get the message through the screen." **Key:** the drum. The lamp is shown with help, and the screen blocking light is said aloud.

**X53b.** Two sketches. The same small pictured trial, in which the light drifted off the card, sits beside both. The first has a stand under a flashlight keeping it aimed at a card. The second has a brighter flashlight. Spoken: "Touch the feature a trial record supports." **Key:** the stand. The ribbon is shown with help.

## Day 55 (items s06-X55a and s06-X55b)

**X55a.** A pictured failed trial: a cup pair with the string drooping on the floor and the friend's mark not matching the sender's card. Three cards: a hand pulling the string gently straight; a child with an open mouth shouting; a different message card. Spoken: "Touch the step to fix." **Key:** the string. The shouting and the changed message are shown with help.

**X55b.** The child's own trial record. Spoken: "Point to the step you repaired, and then to what the friend marked after." No card is shown. The practice message is new, one long then one quick, drawn as a long dash and a short dash, and was not used on days 48 to 54. The child sends it once after their own repair. Apply only the letter that matches the claim the child makes (A, C, D or F); other answers are recorded in plain words.
