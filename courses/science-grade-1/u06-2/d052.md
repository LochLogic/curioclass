# Day 52: Again, and the same?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape the lane as the lane map shows: sender line, near mark at 2 strips, farther mark at 5 strips, a low card at each mark, a walking route round the string. Put out each child's student-built message device and paper results chart. Choose today's six. Hide the held-back exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

One group in the lane, under your eye. String low and gently straight; walk round it. Light aimed down at the low card, never at eyes; you secure each battery compartment. Sound at classroom level, away from ears. No emergency patterns. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs in from day 51's exit: error C and error D. Say "Invented for practice." Three minutes, repair card C: the wrong sentence beside the right one. Children say which marks were tried. Three minutes, repair card D: two drum pairs. Children point to the pair with one change.

#### 2 min. Rug

Add a heading to the question board: dependable. Keep, change, watch (review from kindergarten; only the distance changes). **Ask.** "Does the same device give the same mark when we try it again?" Say: "Dependable means the same each time, so far."

#### 18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each

The same lane, device and card as each child's day 51 chart, one group of five at a time. Each child sends once at the near mark and once at the farther mark: ten sends of 15 seconds is 2 minutes 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to change over. A receiver at each mark holds up a line up or wait card before the sender's card is shown. A fourth child draws that mark in the friend marked box beside the day 51 mark. The chart notes tight or not. A mark that cannot be told: send once more, keep both. Waiting groups sort the invented trial cards into same both times and not the same. Say "These cards are invented for practice." Write who was heard.

#### 3 min. Compare

Each child looks at the two marks for near, then farther, and circles same or not the same. **Ask.** "Did the friend mark the same both times?" Expect: yes where circled same; not the same is evidence, not a poor mark. Hear six.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Devices stored; adults collect string.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X52a]] "Is Pip sure yet? Say yes or no." All show thumb up or down. **Ask.** [[s06-X52b]] "Touch the record that gives a reason to depend on the device." Hear six, 20 seconds each. Yes to Pip, or the match and a miss, is error F.

### Other rooms

No lane: use the invented trial cards, said aloud to be invented, and practise reading the chart only; nothing is scored.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: paper sheets laid end to end.

**Skip.** The group turns and the sorting.

- **5 min. Set the marks.** Lay 2 strips for near and 5 for farther.
- **15 min. Send twice.** Your child sends the same card with the same device at each mark. You mark a picture first, then see the card.
  **Say.** "Did you get the same mark both times?" Expect: the same or not the same, said honestly.
- **5 min. Compare.** Your child draws the two marks side by side and circles same or not the same.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X52a]] and [[s06-X52b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one send at each mark. Longer: a third try at the farther mark.

### Words and supports

Dependable, repeat. Frame: "The friend marked ___ both times." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who takes one try as proof (error F). Day 53 opens with that for 3 minutes, then brings a new problem.
