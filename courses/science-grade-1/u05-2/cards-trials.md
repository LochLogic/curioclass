# Trial cards (days 43, 44 and 45)

Say aloud: "These are pictures of real trials, made by an adult." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is made from a real trial with the flashlight and the screen taped in place, each dated, and records the screen as it was: nothing is filled in, and a missing card is left missing and said so.

Cards, one trial each, in the same beam: (T0) the empty opening; (T1) clear plastic; (T2) wax paper; (T3) cardboard; (T4) the plastic mirror, with a front screen and a side screen; (T5) smoothed foil, with both screens. Children read from the real picture: a bright patch, a dim patch with no clear edge, a shadow where the patch was, or a patch on the side screen. On day 45 each group lays its cards into three hoops (lets light through, lets some through, blocks light), puts the mirror card and the foil card in the blocks hoop if no light shows behind, and tags them turns light if the side screen is bright.

**Expected pattern, to be matched against the real pictures:** clear plastic, a nearly full patch; wax paper, a dim patch; cardboard, a shadow; a smooth mirror, a side patch and none behind. Score nothing against this; the pictures decide. If the day 43 and 44 trials fail or a card cannot be made, the dated photographs are said to be recordings and marked indirect.
