# Repair cards, days 43 to 47

Keep these in an envelope. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Each repair is one picture pair or one picture and one wrong sentence beside one right sentence. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

**A (day 43): the toy chest.** Picture 1: a shut toy chest with a toy inside, shown dotted. Picture 2: the lid lifted, the toy seen. Wrong: "We waited, so the toy showed up in the dark chest." Right: "We saw it when the lid opened and light got in."

**B (day 43): the flashlight under a blanket.** Picture 1: a lump under a blanket, nothing else lit. Picture 2: the same, a glow at the edge. Wrong: "The glow under the blanket needs a lamp." Right: "The flashlight shows at the edge, so it makes its own light."

**E (day 44): the taped light.** One picture of a flashlight held in a hand with arrows showing it moving, and one picture of the flashlight with a tape mark under it and a piece changed. Wrong: "Move the light for each piece so we can see." Right: "Tape the light and change only the piece." Children point to the picture where only the piece changes.

**C (day 45): behind and beside.** A picture of a fixed mirror in a beam with a dark front screen and a bright side screen. Wrong: "The patch beside the mirror proves light went through it." Right: "Behind the mirror it was dark and the side screen was bright, so it turned the light." Children point to the screen that would show light going through.

**D (day 47): the mirror card.** The three hoops and a mirror card. Wrong: "The mirror needs its own group." Right: "The mirror goes with the ones that block, and it can also turn the light: that is a different question." Children put the card in the blocks hoop and mark turns light on a line apart.

**E (day 47): the fixed stand.** A picture of the stand with a taped place for the flashlight and a taped place for the screen and three pieces to try; children mark what stays still and what changes.
