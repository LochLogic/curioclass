# Familiar evidence cards (day 46, waiting children)

Say aloud: "These are cards from our own days." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. While a group takes its turn, other children match six familiar cards on their own, unscored and without help: the lamp beside the wooden duck (makes light, and the duck needs light from elsewhere), a flashlight beam on a cave wall, the wall near the lamp, clear plastic in the beam (bright patch), wax paper in the beam (dim patch), cardboard in the beam (shadow). Each child puts each card under one of five labels, shines on it, makes its own light, lets light through, lets some through, blocks light, and draws a reason on the back. Cards are taken from the real cards of days 41, 43, 44 and the cave sequence; none shows the day 46 records or pieces. Nobody is told the result.
