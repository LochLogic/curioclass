# Exit cards, days 43 to 45 and 47

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or says yes or no; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. None shows the strip, the class pieces or the day 46 records.

## Day 43 (items s05-X43a and s05-X43b)

**X43a.** Three trial cards of unfamiliar pieces in the same beam: baking paper (a dim patch), a cereal-box piece (a shadow and no patch), a clear sandwich bag (a bright patch). Spoken: "Touch the one that blocked the light and made a shadow." **Key:** the cereal-box piece. The baking paper and the bag are shown with help, no letter.

**X43b.** Spoken: "Pip tried three new pieces. Each time Pip moved the flashlight closer, so the patch was easy to see. Is that a fair test? Say yes or no." **Key:** no; keep the light still and change only the piece. Yes is error E.

## Day 44 (item s05-X44)

Cards: a new shiny tray tried in the beam, a bright patch on the side screen and no patch behind it. Spoken: "Pip says the light went through the tray. Is Pip right? Say yes or no." **Key:** no, the tray sent the light a new way. Yes is error C. A fourth group for the tray is error D.

## Day 45 (items s05-X45a and s05-X45b)

**X45a.** A fresh six-card record, invented: three cards where light went through, one where some went through, two where it was blocked, one of those also sending light to the side. Three hoops. Spoken: "Touch the hoop with the fewest cards." **Key:** the lets some through hoop, one card. A fourth hoop for the side card is error D. Other hoops are shown with help.

**X45b.** Two plan pictures: the flashlight and the screen taped in place; the flashlight held and moved. Spoken: "Touch the plan that is fair." **Key:** the taped one. The moved one is error E.

## Day 47 (items s05-X47a and s05-X47b), fresh cards, unscored

**X47a.** A firefly glowing on a night with no moon and no lamp, and a leaf hanging beside it. Spoken: "Touch the one we can see with no other light." **Key:** the firefly. The leaf after a wait is error A; neither is error B.

**X47b.** Two trial cards: a kitchen-roll sheet in the beam with a dim patch behind it; a baking-tin lid in the beam with a patch at the side and none behind. Spoken: "Touch the piece where light went through." **Key:** the kitchen-roll sheet. The lid is error C. If the cards are not valid, the check stays unfinished and is said so.
