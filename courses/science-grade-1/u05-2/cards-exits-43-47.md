# Exit cards, days 43 to 45 and 47

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or shows thumb up or down; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. None shows the strip, the class pieces or the day 46 records. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-exits-43-47.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

## Day 43 (items s05-X43a and s05-X43b)

**X43a.** Three trial cards of new pieces in the same beam, each card showing the piece and its screen only: a small wooden board (no patch on the screen), a sheet of tracing paper (a dim patch), a clear page protector (a bright patch). The results are not said aloud. Spoken: "Touch the piece that blocked the light."

**X43b.** Spoken: "Pip tried three new pieces. Each time Pip moved the flashlight closer, so the patch was easy to see. Is that a fair test? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no, on show me."

## Day 44 (item s05-X44)

Cards: a new shiny tray tried in the beam, a bright patch on the side screen and no patch behind it. Spoken: "Pip says the light went through the tray. Is Pip right? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no, on show me."

## Day 45 (items s05-X45a and s05-X45b)

**X45a.** A fresh six-card record, invented. Five cards are laid in three hoops (lets light through, lets some through, blocks light). The sixth card is held up: a shiny steel bowl turned on its side in the beam, no patch on the screen behind, a bright patch on the side screen. Spoken: "Touch the hoop this card goes in."

**X45b.** Two plan pictures; the flashlight is taped in place in both. Plan 1: the screen is taped in place too. Plan 2: the screen is moved for each piece. Spoken: "Touch the plan that is fair."

## Day 47 (items s05-X47a and s05-X47b), fresh cards, unscored

**X47a.** A firefly glowing on a night with no moon and no lamp, and a leaf hanging on a far branch across the picture, too far for the firefly's glow to reach. Source for the firefly: sources.csv 9L. Spoken: "Touch the one we can see with no other light."

**X47b.** Two trial cards: a kitchen-roll sheet in the beam with a dim patch behind it; a baking-tin lid in the beam with a patch at the side and none behind. Spoken: "Touch the piece where light went through."
