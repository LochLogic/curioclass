# Feedback picture-cue cards (day 47)

After day 46, fix one small picture cue to each child's response sheet. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. The cues are: a star drawn by a mark that shows what the screen did; a question mark by a box that needs what was seen; an arrow by a picture where the light's path is missing; a taped-light symbol where the light and screen stayed still; a two-line mark where the mirror's turning of the light is kept apart from the three groups. Each cue has one big word. One cue per child, kept to what the child drew. A child who can point to the cue and say what was seen shows it. The teacher tells the three most common cues aloud on day 47.
