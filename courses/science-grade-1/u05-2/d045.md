# Day 45: Sort, count and plan

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, soften every cardboard stand piece, make the test-record cards from the real day 43 and 44 trials, and set out the hoops, count charts, masking tape and peer-plan sheets for each group. Hide the held-back exit cards. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-43-47.

### Safety and set-up

Stand pieces are blunt, edges trimmed. Build on a low, stable surface. Paper hoops lie flat; no string loops. The room stays lit; flashlights stay trayed.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error C from day 44's exit. Say "Invented for practice." Show a fixed mirror in a beam, a dark front screen and a bright side screen. Read the wrong sentence ("The patch beside the mirror proves light went through it") beside the right ("Behind the mirror it was dark and the side screen was bright, so it turned the light"). Children point to the screen that would show light going through.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Math 1.MD.C.4 (review): you sorted into groups and counted each in math this fall. Say "Invented for practice." Lay seven animal cards in three groups: 4 fish, 2 frogs, 1 bird. **Ask.** "How many in each group?" Expect: 4, 2 and 1. Six answer.

#### 11 min. Sort and count

Each group lays its test-record cards into the three hoops: lets light through, lets some through, blocks light. Where no light shows behind, the mirror and the foil go in the blocks hoop, with a turns light tag if the side screen is bright. Calling it a fourth, mirror-like kind is right if no light showed behind. Each child lays one card and says why, 30 seconds, five at once. Count each hoop on the chart. **Ask.** "Which hoop has the most? Which has the fewest?" Expect: the real cards decide; a tie is the same.

#### 6 min. Build the stand

Each group tapes flashlight and screen places on a cardboard stand. Each child tapes one piece. Shelve it for day 46.

#### 6 min. Peer plan

On the peer-plan sheet (keep, change, watch: kindergarten, review) each child draws what to change (the piece), keep still (light, screen) and watch (the screen) for pieces not yet met. A partner points to each box and says it back; unscored.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Stands to the shelf; tape off.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Lay five cards of a new record in the three hoops and hold up the sixth: a shiny steel bowl turned on its side in the beam, no patch behind, a bright patch on the side screen. **Ask.** [[s05-X45a]] "Touch the hoop this card goes in." All touch; mark every child. Show the two new plan pictures. **Ask.** [[s05-X45b]] "Touch the plan that is fair." All touch. Hear six say why, 20 seconds each. A new hoop because light went through is error D; the plan with the moved screen is error E.

### Other rooms

No hoops: three circles of string or drawn on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No hoops: three paper circles. No cardboard: use a book stack.

**Skip.** The group count and swap.

- **8 min. Sort.** Draw four quick pictures with your child: clear plastic, wax paper, cardboard, a shiny spoon. Lay them into three circles: through, some, blocks.
  **Say.** "Where does the spoon go?" Expect: blocks, with a turns light mark.
- **12 min. Plan.** Tape a place for the flashlight and the paper. Your child draws the plan on the student page.
  **Say.** "What do we keep still? What do we change?" Expect: the light and paper; the piece.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X45a]] and [[s05-X45b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tape the stand marks yourself.

### Words and supports

Count, most, fewest, stand, plan. Frame: "I keep ___ still and change ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error D and error E from today's exit wait for day 47, because day 46 is a check and reteaches nothing.
