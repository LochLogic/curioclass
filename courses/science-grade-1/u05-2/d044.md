# Day 44: Through, or a new way?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, edge-tape the mirrors, smooth the foil, tape each flashlight to its taped mark for the flashlight and screen, and set a side screen beside each beam. Rehearse the mirror beam onto the side screen. Add a turns the light line to the class seeing chart and question board. Hide the held-back exit cards, days 43 to 47. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-43-47.

### Safety and set-up

Flashlights stay on their marks, below faces, aimed at the screen: never at an eye or the sun. You secure each battery compartment. Mirrors are plastic only, never glass; no lasers; you set each mirror's angle so its beam falls on the side screen, never at a face. Dim the room gently; any child may sit by the door.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand-back in: error E from day 43's exit. Say "Invented for practice." Show two pictures: a flashlight held in a hand with arrows, and a flashlight with a tape mark under it and a piece changed. Read the wrong sentence ("Move the light for each piece so we can see") beside the right ("Tape the light and change only the piece"). Children point to the picture where only the piece changes.

#### 3 min. Rug

Two questions, asked apart. **Ask.** "Does a mirror let light through?" Take guesses. **Ask.** "Does a mirror do something else?" Tell nothing.

#### 6 min. Plan

Add a side screen beside the beam, below faces. Keep the light on its mark and the first screen still. **Ask.** "What do we change?" Expect: the piece. Each piece gets two marks. **Ask.** "Behind the piece: does light go through?" **Ask.** "At the side: does light go a new way?" 

#### 12 min. Test

Five pieces a group: mirror, foil, clear plastic, wax paper, cardboard. Each child places one, 40 seconds each; three groups share each of the two mirror-and-foil sets. A waiting group points on its day 43 chart to the piece that let light through. Groups mark both screens. Expect: a mirror, no patch behind and a patch at the side; the screens decide for foil. If a beam fails, repeat once, then use the dated trial photographs as recordings.

#### 4 min. Record

On the test chart each child marks the mirror and foil in the shadow column, since no light shows behind, and answers the turns the light line apart: yes or no.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Lights off into the tray; you count pieces.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s05-X44]] "Pip says the light went through the tray. Is Pip right? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no, on show me." Mark every child; hear six say why, 20 seconds each. Thumb up is error C.

### Other rooms

One mirror: you demonstrate and children read the screens.

### At home

**Swap.** No mirror: the back of a large shiny spoon, held steady. No foil: skip it.

**Skip.** The group sharing.

- **5 min. Set up.** Tape the flashlight. Put one paper behind the beam, one at the side.
- **15 min. Test.** Dim the lights gently. Hold the spoon back in the beam, then clear plastic, wax paper and cardboard. Draw what each screen shows on the student page.
  **Say.** "Did light go through? Did it go a new way?" Expect: the spoon, no patch behind and a patch at the side, so a new way.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X44]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: mirror and cardboard only; keep both screens and the exit. Longer: tip a plastic mirror; never aim light at faces.

### Words and supports

Mirror, through, a new way. Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error C from today's exit goes to day 45's Return and repair: compare the screens behind and beside a fixed mirror. Error D waits for day 47.
