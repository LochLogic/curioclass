# Day 43: What does a piece do to a beam of light?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, trim sample edges, tape each flashlight to its taped mark for the flashlight and screen, secure batteries, and try each piece. Lay out a set for each group, with the retrieval pictures for day 43, the repair cards, days 43 to 47, and the light test charts. Keep the held-back exit cards, days 43 to 47, hidden. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-43-47 and key-picture-retrieval.

### Safety and set-up

Flashlights stay on their marks, below faces, aimed at the screen: never at an eye or the sun. Plastic stays out of mouths. Dim the room gently, never dark; keep the exit lit; any child may sit by the door.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs in: error A and error B from day 42's exit. Say "Invented for practice." Error A, 3 minutes: the shut toy chest, then the lid lifted; children point to the picture where light got in. Error B, 3 minutes: the blanket pictures; children point to the picture where nothing else is lit. Many with either: lay the pictures side by side.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Say "These pictures are like our kindergarten tests (review)." **Ask.** "In our ball test, what did we change and watch?" Expect: the push; how far the ball went. **Ask.** "Which picture shows the cover blocking sunlight?" Expect: the cardboard cover. Hear six, one ask each.

#### 2 min. Rug

**Ask.** Under barriers on the board: "What lets light through?"

#### 5 min. Plan

Pairs choose which piece goes in the beam first. Look at the empty opening first: the beam alone makes a patch. Keep, change, watch is review. **Ask.** "What will we keep still?" Expect: the light on its mark, and the screen. **Ask.** "What will we change?" Expect: the piece. **Ask.** "What will we watch?" Expect: the screen. Children draw the three.

#### 11 min. Test

Groups of five. Each child places one piece in the beam, 40 seconds each; two repeat a piece. The group names the patch. Expect: clear plastic, a nearly full patch; wax paper, a dim patch; cardboard, a shadow. A failed beam: use the dated trial photographs, said to be recordings.

#### 3 min. Record

Read each row aloud. Each child ticks the light test chart: bright patch, dim patch or shadow, for each piece.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Lights off into the tray; you count pieces back.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." Show the three new trial cards: a small wooden board, a sheet of tracing paper, a clear page protector, each with its screen. **Ask.** [[s05-X43a]] "Touch the piece that blocked the light." All touch; mark every child. **Ask.** [[s05-X43b]] "Is that a fair test? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no, on show me." Mark every child; hear six say why, 20 seconds each. Thumb up is error E.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the chart on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No flashlight: a phone light taped to a book stack, held low. No wax paper: a paper napkin.

**Skip.** The retrieval pictures.

- **5 min. Plan.** Tape the flashlight; lean white paper as the screen.
  **Say.** "What do we keep still? What do we change?" Expect: the flashlight and paper; the piece.
- **15 min. Test.** Dim the lights gently. Hold clear plastic, wax paper (or the napkin) and cardboard in the beam one at a time. Your child ticks the chart.
  **Say.** "What does the screen show?" Expect: bright patch, dim patch, shadow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X43a]] and [[s05-X43b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pieces; keep the empty opening and the exit.

### Words and supports

Patch, bright, dim, shadow, fair. Frame: "I kept ___ still and changed ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error E from today's exit goes to day 44's Return and repair: tape the light spot on a picture and change only the piece.
