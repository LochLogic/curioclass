# Day 46: Show what you found

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, trim every piece, edge-tape the mirror, rehearse each trial and note what the screens really show, and make and date the four cupboard records, one set a group. Set the day 45 stand, flashlight and two screens at one observation table. Seal the cupboard records until Records. Lay out response sheets, day 45's peer plans, each group's familiar evidence cards and the heard record.

### Safety and set-up

Flashlight on the stand, below faces, aimed at the screen: never at an eye or the sun. The mirror is plastic only, never glass; its beam falls on the side screen. Dim gently, never dark; the exit stays lit; any child may sit by the door. Only the group at the table handles light; others use paper. You secure each battery compartment.

### The day

#### 2 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. Recordings are called recordings. You may pass." Groups of five take places. No reteaching; hand-backs in: none.

#### 4 min. Picture plans

Hand back day 45's peer plan sheet: it already shows what stays still. On the response sheet each child writes 1 to 4 by the four printed piece pictures for the order, and rings one guess under each: bright, dim or shadow. At the table, run the group's most common order and say so.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six turns of 3 minutes at the table: 50 seconds of shared trials, where each child places one piece (one is repeated so all five act) and all four effects are seen; five linked 24-second accounts; 10 seconds to change over. Each child's 20-second account is the same [[s05-E46b]]: place your piece and say what the two screens showed (behind: bright patch, dim patch or shadow; at the side: a patch or none). Then the child touches the chart hoop for the piece [[s05-E46c]] and says keep or the change [[s05-E46d]]. Mark each child on the scale. After class, score all four effects from each child's what I saw row on the response sheet. Waiting children match the familiar cards under five labels and draw a reason, unscored. A failed trial: use the dated photographs.

#### 4 min. Records

Each group opens its four cupboard records. **Ask.** [[s05-E46a]] Every child touches the lit and unlit looks and draws in both rows when we saw the label and the little light, and why. The rotating six dictate the reason, 20 seconds each. After class, mark every child on the scale from the drawings.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Lights off; pieces, screens, pages collected.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the response boxes on paper. One projector: show each trial large, one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No stand: a flashlight taped to a book stack. No mirror: a shiny lid, never glass. Use only a plastic mirror, keep the flashlight below faces, never aim light or reflections at eyes.

**Skip.** The group turns.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Your child picks an order for a clear cup wall piece, tissue paper, a dark cloth square and a small plastic mirror, guesses each and tests one at a time with the flashlight taped; do not help. Then your child shows when a label and a small light in a shut cupboard can be seen, and draws or tells why.
  **Say.** "What did the screens show, and why can we see the label?" Expect: clear, a bright patch; tissue, dim; the dark cloth, a shadow; mirror, a patch at the side; the label only when light reaches it.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pieces and the seeing account. Never cut the plan. Longer: one more piece.

### Words and supports

Patch, through, shadow, mirror, fair. Frame: "I kept ___ still and changed ___." Pointing, drawing or dictation counts.

### Tomorrow

Gaps by error letter go to day 47: error A and error B in a group together, error C and error D in a group, error E on a fixed-stand picture.
