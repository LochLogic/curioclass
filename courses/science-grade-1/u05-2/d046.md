# Day 46: Show what you found

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, trim every piece, edge-tape the mirror, rehearse each trial and write down what the screens really show. Set the day 45 stand, flashlight and two screens at one observation table. Seal the lit and self-luminous picture records (the cupboard label and battery light cards) until the turns. Lay out the response sheets, the familiar evidence cards for each group and the record of who was heard.

### Safety and set-up

Flashlight on the stand, below faces, aimed at the screen: never at an eye or the sun. The mirror is plastic or edge-taped; its beam falls on the side screen. Dim the room gently, never dark; the exit stays lit and any child may sit by the door. Only the group at the table handles light; others use paper.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. Recordings are called recordings. You may pass." Groups of five take places. No reteaching; hand-backs in: none.

#### 3 min. Picture plans

On the response sheet each child marks the order of the four new pieces, a guess for each, and what stays still.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six turns of 3 minutes at the table: 60 seconds of shared trials, where each child places one piece (one is repeated so all five act) and all four effects are seen; five linked 20-second accounts; 20 seconds to change over. Accounts: child 1, the label seen with and without outside light; child 2, the little battery light off and on; child 3, clear plastic and tissue paper; child 4, cardboard and its shadow; child 5, the mirror, the side screen, what to add to the chart. Each is a [[s05-E46a]] or [[s05-E46b]] step. Waiting children match the familiar evidence cards under five labels and draw a reason, unscored. Mark each child on the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. If a trial fails, use the dated photographs as recordings.

#### 3 min. Records

Each child marks the four effects seen and draws or dictates why they saw it. You write dictated words.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Lights off and counted; pieces and screens collected.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the response boxes on paper. One projector: show each trial large, one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No stand: a torch taped to a book stack. No mirror: a shiny tray.

**Skip.** The group turns.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Your child picks an order for clear plastic, tissue paper, cardboard and a shiny tray, guesses each and tests one at a time with the torch taped; do not help. Then your child shows when a label and a small light in a shut cupboard can be seen, and draws or tells why.
  **Say.** "What did the screens show, and why can we see the label?" Expect: clear, a bright patch; tissue, dim; cardboard, a shadow; tray, a patch at the side; the label only when light reaches it.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pieces and the seeing account. Never cut the plan. Longer: one more piece.

### Words and supports

Patch, through, shadow, mirror, fair. Frame: "I kept ___ still and changed ___." Pointing, drawing or dictation counts.

### Tomorrow

Gaps by error letter go to day 47: error A and error B in a group together, error C and error D in a group, error E on a fixed-stand picture. Day 47 returns picture comments and checks a fresh case.
