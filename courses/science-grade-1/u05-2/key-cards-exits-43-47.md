# Exit cards, days 43 to 45 and 47: teacher key

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-exits-43-47.md. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

- X43a. Key: the small wooden board. The tracing paper and the page protector are shown with help, no letter.
- X43b. Key: thumb down, no; the six heard say keep the light still and change only the piece. Thumb up is error E.
- Day 44 (item s05-X44). Key: thumb down, no; the six heard say the tray sent the light a new way. Thumb up is error C. A child who calls the tray a fourth, mirror-like kind of piece is right if they say no light went behind it.
- X45a. Key: the blocks light hoop, with a turns light tag. A child who asks for a fourth, mirror-like hoop and says no light showed behind is also right. A child who sorts it out of the no-light group because light went through it to the side is error D. The lets light through hoop is error C. The lets some through hoop is shown with help.
- X45b. Key: plan 1, the taped screen. Plan 2 is error E (moving a set-up part between pieces).
- X47a. Key: the firefly. The leaf after a wait is error A; neither is error B.
- X47b. Key: the kitchen-roll sheet. The lid is error C. If the cards are not valid, the check stays unfinished and is said so.
