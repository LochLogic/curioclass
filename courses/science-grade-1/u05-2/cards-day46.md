# Day 46 records and pieces (held back until the check)

Keep these sealed until day 46. They appear in no teaching day. Say aloud: "These are recordings of real tests." Each card is headed with the date, plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no result written on any card. Each is a real, unlabelled record made by an adult, never an invented result; with no valid record, leave that evidence incomplete.

## Item 1 records (the cupboard label and the battery light)

Apparatus: a cupboard with a paper label on its inside wall and a small battery light on the shelf, in a room that can be made dark. Label records, one card each: the label with outside light absent, the little light off; the label with outside light present, the little light off. Light records, one card each: the little light off, no outside light, the label out of the picture or shielded; the little light switched on, no outside light, the label out of the picture or shielded. Children mark both objects' appearances; they are not told a conclusion. The label records are taken with the little light off.

## Item 2 pieces

Four new pieces: clear plastic sheet, cardboard sample, tissue paper, and a differently shaped plastic mirror, with the day 45 stand, two screens and the flashlight. None of these pieces was used on days 43 to 45. Children choose the order and predict each effect. The second screen is moved into the redirected beam and stays below faces. Each sample is compared with the empty opening.

## Check before use

The teacher rehearses every trial the day before and records what the screens really show. Nothing is filled in. Edges are trimmed and the mirror is plastic or edge-taped, no glass and no lasers.
