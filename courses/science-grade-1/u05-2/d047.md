# Day 47: Fix it and say why

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 46, fix a picture cue only to the sheets with gaps and the six heard, kept to what the child drew (about 20 minutes of prep); the rest hear the three most common comments in class. Sort those sheets by the gaps in day 46's items: error A with error B, error C with error D, error E. Set out the hoops, the mirror card and the fixed-stand picture. Hide the held-back exit cards, days 43 to 47. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-43-47.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; paper hoops lie flat; any child may pass.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read feedback and revise

Two minutes: read the three most common comments aloud. Eight minutes: each child finds their own picture cue and adds or corrects one picture or label on the earlier account and on the day 46 records. New work is unscored. Hear six, 20 seconds each; pointing to the cue and saying what was seen shows it.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs in: gaps from day 46's items, and errors C, D and E from day 45's exit. Say "Invented for practice." Group A with B, 4 minutes with you: the toy chest shut and open, then the flashlight under the blanket, pointing to where nothing else is lit. Group C with D, 4 minutes: the screens behind and beside the fixed mirror, then the mirror card goes in the blocks hoop or a mirror-like group, and turns light is marked on a line apart. Error E group marks the fixed-stand picture: what stays still, what changes. Two minutes to move. Others link printed pictures.

#### 7 min. Exit: fresh evidence

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s05-X47a]] "Touch the one we can see with no other light." **Ask.** [[s05-X47b]] "Touch the piece where light went through." Hear six, 20 seconds each. The leaf after a wait is error A; neither is error B; the lid is error C. If the cards are not valid, the check stays unfinished and is said so.

#### 3 min. Collect and close

**Ask.** "Why can we see the toy when the flap opens?" Expect: light gets in and shines on it. **Ask.** "Why do wax paper and cardboard differ?" Expect: wax paper lets some light through; cardboard blocks it and makes a shadow. Write the class's one answer as children hand in sheets.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No hoops: three paper circles.

**Skip.** The group sequence.

- **8 min. Fix.** Find the picture cue on the sheet and fix one picture.
- **10 min. Repair.** Show the toy chest shut and open.
  **Say.** "Why can we see the toy now?" Expect: light got in and shone on it.
- **5 min. Say it.** Write your child's answer to why wax paper and cardboard differ.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X47a]] and [[s05-X47b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group sequence; keep the revision and the exit. Longer: draw the box and screen with a sentence.

### Words and supports

Fix, cue, through, shadow, mirror. Frame: "We saw ___ because ___." Pointing and dictation count.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 starts unit 6. The record sheet and the revised account are kept as unit 5's record.
