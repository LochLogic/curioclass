# Day 63: Slide the strips

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print an account sheet each. Set one large set of three strips and a tape line for each group. Cut the day 9 report card flat for Pip. Seal the exit cards. Choose six not heard at day 62's exit to dictate, six more for the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. Never look at the sun.

### The day

#### 4 min. Open

Pip holds the report card: "It was light at half past five, so the day was long." **Ask.** "Touch the card that tells how long the whole day was light, the report or the strip." Expect the strip. One moment is error C.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand-back from day 62's exit, error A: lay Pip's equal bars under the real strips. **Ask.** "Point to the shorter light part." Expect winter's. Few missed: one group.

#### 10 min. Slide and compare

Groups of five slide the large strips along the tape line until the sunrise pictures meet. As with the leaf bases and tips in unit 3, the sunset picture that sits farther is the longer light part. Winter against fall, 4 minutes; winter against spring, 4 minutes; in each, five children slide in turn, 45 seconds each. **Ask.** "Point to the shorter light part." Expect winter's, both times. Each child colours the shorter light part on their account sheet, 2 minutes.

#### 3 min. Test a claim

Pip: "Winter has more daylight than spring." **Ask.** "Point to the light parts that tell if Pip is right." Expect winter's is shorter. Pip is wrong on purpose (error B).

#### 4 min. Revise the model

Redraw the class model's three boxes from the full strips, winter's light part shorter. The report stays out: it is one moment.

#### 4 min. Dictate

Six children each dictate a line for the account, 30 seconds each; the rest write fall, winter and spring under their strips. Scribe it and read it back, 1 minute. Frame: "Winter has less daylight than fall and spring; these light parts show it."

#### 4 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Show the three strips, the equal-sun card and the noon report. Everyone touches, 1 minute: **Ask.** [[s08-X63a]] "Touch the cards that tell us about the whole day." The rotating six, 30 seconds each: **Ask.** [[s08-X63b]] "Winter's light part: is it longer, shorter or the same as fall's and spring's?" Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Expect: shorter. "The same" is error A; "longer" is error B; the noon report is error C; the equal-sun card is shown with help.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Count strips back; keep the token.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each strip on a bar of one fixed length. No tape: use a table edge.

### At home

**Swap.** No large strips: use the three on the account sheet. No tape line: use a table edge.

**Skip.** Pip, the groups and the dictation turns.

- **6 min. One moment.**
  **Say.** "It was light at half past five. Does that tell how long the whole day was light?" Expect: no, one moment; the strip shows the whole day.
- **10 min. Slide.** Slide the winter strip against fall, then against spring, until the sunrise pictures meet.
  **Say.** "Which light part is shorter?" Expect: winter's.
- **4 min. Colour.** Your child colours the shorter light part.
- **5 min. Account.** Write your child's words about the seasons under the strips.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X63a]] and [[s08-X63b]], asked aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: slide winter against fall only. Never cut the slide or the exit. Longer: why two "light" reports cannot show equal days.

### Words and supports

Longer, shorter, whole day, one moment, fall, winter, spring. Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the seasons are the same (error A), winter is longer (error B) or the noon report tells the day (error C). Day 64 is a check on new strips and reteaches nothing; gaps go to day 65's tables, 4 minutes each.
