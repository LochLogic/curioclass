# Day 63: Slide the strips

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print an account sheet each. Set one large set of three strips and a tape line for each group. Cut the day 9 report card flat for Pip. Seal the exit cards; cut each exit strip out singly so it slides. Choose six not heard at day 62's exit to dictate, six more for the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. Never look at the sun.

### The day

#### 4 min. Open

Pip holds the report card: "It was light at half past five, so the day was long." **Ask.** "Thumbs up if this card tells how long the whole day was light, thumbs down if not." Expect thumbs down. Lay the strip beside the token: the strip is the whole day, the report one moment. Thumbs up is error C.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand-back from day 62's exit, error A: lay Pip's equal bars under the real strips. **Ask.** "Point to the shorter light part." Expect winter's. Few missed: one group.

#### 10 min. Slide and compare

Groups of five slide the large strips along the tape line until the sunrise pictures meet. The sunset picture that sits farther is the longer light part. Winter against fall, then winter against spring, 4 minutes each; five children slide in turn, 45 seconds each. **Ask.** "Point to the shorter light part." Expect winter's, both times. Each child colors the shorter light part on their account sheet, 2 minutes.

#### 3 min. Test a claim

Pip: "Winter has more daylight than spring." **Ask.** "Point to the light parts that tell if Pip is right." Expect winter's is shorter. Pip is wrong on purpose.

#### 4 min. Revise the model

Redraw the class model's three boxes from the full strips, winter's light part shorter.

#### 4 min. Dictate

Six children each dictate a line for the account, 30 seconds each; the rest write fall, winter and spring under their strips. Scribe and read it back, 1 minute. Frame: "Winter has less daylight than fall and spring; these light parts show it."

#### 4 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Show the three strips, the equal-sun card and the noon report. Everyone touches, 1 minute: **Ask.** [[s08-X63a]] "Touch the cards that tell us about the whole day." The rotating six, 30 seconds each, slide the exit strips, sunrise pictures together, and answer one ask at a time: **Ask.** [[s08-X63b]] "Slide winter against fall. Winter's light part: is it longer, shorter or the same as fall's?" **Ask.** [[s08-X63c]] "Now slide winter against spring. Is it longer, shorter or the same as spring's?" Mark each on the scale. Expect: shorter, both times. "The same" is error A; "longer" is error B; the noon report is error C; the equal-sun card is shown with help.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Count strips back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each strip on a bar of one fixed length. No tape: use a table edge.

### At home

**Swap.** No large strips: use the three on the account sheet. No tape line: use a table edge.

**Skip.** Pip, the groups and the dictation turns.

- **6 min. One moment.**
  **Say.** "It was light at half past five. Does that tell how long the whole day was light?" Expect: no, one moment; the strip shows the whole day.
- **10 min. Slide.** Slide the winter strip against fall, then against spring, until the sunrise pictures meet.
  **Say.** "Which light part is shorter?" Expect: winter's.
- **4 min. Color.** Your child colors the shorter light part.
- **5 min. Account.** Write your child's words about the seasons under the strips.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X63a]], [[s08-X63b]] and [[s08-X63c]], asked aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: slide winter against fall only. Never cut the slide or the exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Longer, shorter, whole day, one moment, fall, winter, spring. Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 64 is a check on new strips and reteaches nothing; errors A, B and C go to day 65's tables, 4 minutes each.
