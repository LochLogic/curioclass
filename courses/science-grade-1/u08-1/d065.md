# Day 65: Fix it and try fresh strips

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 64's sheets and day 63's exit by gap into four tables: A, B, C, D. Lay each table's cards from the repair cards. Seal the exit cards. Choose six children for the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. Never look at the sun. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback and revise

Say the three most common picture comments aloud, 1 minute. Each child finds the cue on their day 64 sheet and fixes one picture or label, 4 minutes. New work is unscored; the original is kept.

#### 12 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs by letter from day 63's exit and day 64's assessment. A: Pip's equal bars under the real strips; point to the shorter light part. B: season labels against the strips; winter goes on the shorter. C: the report card and the strip; touch the one that shows the whole day. D: trace the light part between its two pictures. Visit the three largest tables, 4 minutes each: all point together, then hear up to six, 20 seconds each. Other tables work the picture move cards alone. One child follows the same moves with an adult.

#### 8 min. Fresh comparison

Groups color the light parts on the fresh October 20, January 20 and April 20 strips and agree which is shortest, pointing, 6 minutes. Each group says its pointing in 20 seconds, 2 minutes; scribe the class answer in the sheet's last box. Unscored.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Lay the three exit strips in a row, no dates and no labels. Pip holds up the third strip, the longest light part: "This one is winter's." Everyone at once, 1 minute: **Ask.** [[s08-X65a]] "Thumbs up if Pip is right, thumbs down if not." Mark each child. Expect thumbs down. Then hold up the middle strip. The rotating six, 20 seconds each, at the strip: **Ask.** [[s08-X65b]] "Touch the daylight on this strip." Mark on the scale. Expect: the light part between the sunrise and sunset pictures. Thumbs up, "winter has the most daylight", is error B, or error D if the child says its dark part is smallest; touching a dark part is error D.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect strips. Keep the revisions and the day 64 record for day 67.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strips on a bar of one fixed length. No cue cards: say each comment aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue: say the comment aloud. No strips: draw three bars of one length, one with a clearly shorter white part.

**Skip.** The tables and groups.

- **6 min. Fix.** Your child fixes one picture or label on the day 64 sheet.
- **8 min. Trace.** Trace a light part from the sunrise picture to the sunset picture.
  **Say.** "Which part is the daylight?" Expect: the light part between the pictures.
- **9 min. Fresh strips.** Color each light part.
  **Say.** "Which light part is shortest, and which season is it?" Expect: January's, winter.
- **2 min. Write.** Write your child's answer in the last box.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X65a]] and [[s08-X65b]], asked aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two tables, then the fresh strips. Never cut the fresh strips or the exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fix, light part, shorter, whole day, winter. Offer "has less daylight" as a word support. Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

No gaps carry on. Day 66 is the first year-end check.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
