# Day 64: A check on new strips

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 63 leaves no work here. Print a response sheet each. Seal a strip set and the evening-only report for each group. Lay out the familiar seasonal evidence cards for waiting groups. Set the record sheet's heard column and End columns beside you. Choose the order of the six groups.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. Never look at the sun; strips are prepared records. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check, and we call it one. You may pass. The report is invented for practice. The strips are real records from another place, not our own. Nobody helps today except one neutral re-reading." Hand out the sets and sheets.

#### 6 min. Mark

Each child colors the light part of all three strips and circles the shorter light part for winter against fall and for winter against spring. The report lies on the table with no hint. Circulate; do not coach.

#### 18 min. Six group turns

3 minutes each: five 30-second turns, then 30 seconds to change over. Each child says the pattern, pointing to the record or records that show it: [[s08-E64a]]. Mark at once on the End columns, one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Four look-fors: colors all three light parts; compares winter with fall; compares winter with spring (winter is the shortest of the three counts for both); points to the full-day strips, not the report. Only shown counts; after one neutral re-reading it is shown with help. Write who was heard. Note error A (all the same), error B (winter has more), error C (the report as evidence) and error D (the dark part as daylight). Waiting groups finish their sheets, then match the familiar cards and draw a reason; do not help them.

#### 3 min. Collect and clean up

Collect the sheets and strips. After class, about 25 minutes, fix one picture cue from cue-cards.md to each sheet. Keep the record for day 67.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strips on a bar of one fixed length. One projector: show the strips large; the turns run in order at one table.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strips: draw three bars of one length, 24 cm each, and make the white parts 10.9 cm for Oct. 20, 9.6 cm for Jan. 20 and 13.6 cm for Apr. 20, starting 6 cm, 7.1 cm and 4.9 cm from the left.

**Skip.** The group turn and the waiting cards.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Your child colors the light part on each strip and circles the shorter one in each pair, with the evening report beside them. Do not help or hint.
  **Say.** "What do these records show about winter daylight? Point to what shows it and tell me why." Expect: winter's light part is shorter than fall's and spring's; the three strips, not the report.
- **10 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check stays a check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Every prompt is read aloud. Pointing, dictation and enlarged strips count. Fall, winter, spring, shorter, whole day, one moment.

### Tomorrow

Gaps go to day 65 by letter: error A, B, C and D each have a table, with a picture cue on each sheet. This day reteaches nothing.
