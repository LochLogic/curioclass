# Check cards, days 66 to 68 (year-end)

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 66. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a date, a time, or one short word strip per caption that the teacher reads aloud, no brand names, children and grown-ups of many skin tones and abilities. Every case is said aloud to be invented for practice. Six table sets.

## Day 66: seeing and materials

- **Closet looks.** A toy on a closet shelf: look 1, at night, door open and every lamp in the house off; look 2, door open with the hall lamp on, shining on it. A headlamp in a dark tent with no other light is the second case.
- **Plan cards.** Three picture cards: the lamp, the screen, the piece.
- **Piece sets.** Per group: a lamp, a screen, a stand and four pieces cut with taped edges: a clear folder piece, a coffee filter, an egg-carton piece, and a plastic mirror. Piece cards to sort under bright patch, dim patch and dark shadow, three per child. A record of a mirror tilted in the beam with a patch on a side screen, and the same side screen with no mirror and no patch. A record of a folded towel square with a dark shadow and no patch.

## Day 67: message, sources and daylight

- **Message.** Near mark and farther mark cards, per group a strip of tape for each. The agreed message table ready (one long, two short). Sender, device and receiver picture cards in a row. A card of a light flashing with no meaning card.
- **Animal pages.** A lamb and a ewe: words the lamb bleats, a picture of the ewe walking to the lamb. A chick and a hen: words the chick peeps, a picture with the beak open. A pumpkin plant caption and a seed diagram.
- **Strips from a third place.** Seattle, January 10 and April 10, 2026 (strips-key.md), drawn at 1 cm to the hour on one fixed 24 cm bar and lined up at the sunrise pictures for S67a. Pip's claim, said aloud: winter and spring have the same daylight. For S67b, a sky-watch picture like unit 1's, dated January 10: the sun low over a roof, with a clock showing three o'clock, beside the January 10 strip.

## Day 68: fresh exit cards

- **X68a.** A lamp, its beam, a mirror tilted in the beam, a screen straight ahead behind the mirror and a screen at the side; no patch drawn. Each child draws the patch on side 2 of the student page.
- **X68b.** A drum device record: the near mark with the friend's right mark, the farther mark with two beats marked when three were sent. Two answer cards: works far; not yet.
- **X68c.** A child's drawing dated April 10: a post with a long shadow behind it and a clock at eight in the morning; beside it the April 10 full-day strip (above).
- **X68d and X68e.** Words, read aloud: On a crowded beach, an elephant seal pup is apart from its mother. The pup calls. The mother knows her pup's call, and she comes and finds it. One picture: the mother seal moving across the sand to the pup. The fact rests on the National Park Service, Point Reyes (sources.csv 13Q).

## Feedback cue cards (days 66 and 67, for day 68)

One picture cue card for each mark that most children need to fix: two tent looks; a headlamp in a dark tent; lamp and screen marked still with a piece marked changes; three patch cards; mirror on and off; sender device receiver; near and far cards; young card then adult card; a words strip beside a still picture; plant, seed, young plant; two light parts side by side; a report card beside a full-day strip. Cue cards say nothing in words.

## Check before use

The teacher checks every record before use. Light is never pointed at eyes. Unknown results are never filled in.
