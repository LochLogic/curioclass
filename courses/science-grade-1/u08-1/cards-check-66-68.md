# Check cards, days 66 to 68 (year-end)

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 66. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a date or a time, no brand names, children and grown-ups of many skin tones and abilities. Every case is said aloud to be invented for practice. Six table sets.

## Day 66: seeing and materials

- **Tent looks.** A toy in a tent: look 1, flap shut and no lamp; look 2, a lamp outside shining in. A headlamp in a dark tent with no other light.
- **Piece sets.** Per group: a lamp, a screen, a stand and four pieces cut with taped edges: a clear folder piece, a coffee filter, an egg-carton piece, and a plastic mirror. Piece cards to sort under bright patch, dim patch and dark shadow. A record of a mirror beside the beam with a patch on a side screen, and the same side screen with no mirror and no patch. A record of a cardboard piece with a dark shadow.

## Day 67: message, sources and daylight

- **Message.** Near mark and farther mark cards, per group a strip of tape for each. The agreed message table ready (one long, two short). Sender, device and receiver picture cards in a row. A card of a light flashing with no meaning card.
- **Animal pages.** A lamb and a ewe: words the lamb bleats, a picture of the ewe walking to the lamb. A chick and a hen: words the chick peeps, a picture with the beak open. A pumpkin plant caption and a seed diagram.
- **Strips from a third place.** Winter and spring day strips with sunrise and sunset pictures, lined up at the sunrise pictures, and an evening report that says it was light at half past five.

## Day 68: fresh exit cards

- Mirror tray with a patch on a wall, and the same tray with no mirror.
- Near mark answered; farther mark untried.
- A note about bright sky at four o'clock and a full-day strip.
- A calf and a cow: the words the calf bawls, a picture of the cow licking the calf.

## Feedback cue cards (days 66 and 67, for day 68)

One picture cue card for each mark that most children need to fix: two tent looks; a headlamp in a dark tent; lamp and screen marked still with a piece marked changes; three patch cards; mirror on and off; sender device receiver; near and far cards; young card then adult card; a words strip beside a still picture; plant, seed, young plant; two light parts side by side; a report card beside a full-day strip. Cue cards say nothing in words.

## Check before use

The teacher checks every record before use. Light is never pointed at eyes. Unknown results are never filled in.
