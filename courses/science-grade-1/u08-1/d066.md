# Day 66: A tent, four pieces and a beam

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 65 handed on no gaps. Set six table sets: a flashlight aimed at a screen on a taped mark, the four pieces, the side screen and the piece cards. Trim the piece edges. Rehearse the four pieces in the beam and note what each shows. Keep the tent pictures sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Never shine a light into anyone's eyes or look at the sun; you secure each battery compartment. Egg-carton pieces are new; check for egg allergies, an allergic child uses plain card; keep them out of mouths. Mirrors are plastic only, never glass; no lasers. The dark is a dimmed corner; any child may use the pictures instead. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The tent pictures and the pieces are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Seat six groups of five.

#### 4 min. Plans

Say: "Pip will test three pieces." Each child draws only the lamp, one piece and the screen on "My tent and my lamp", with a "stays still" mark and a "changes" mark. Then, with the lamp, screen and piece cards: **Ask.** [[c2-P66a]] "Touch the one thing that changes." **Ask.** [[c2-P66b]] "Touch the things that must stay still." Every child touches on their own drawing; hear the rotating six at the cards, 20 seconds each.

#### 12 min. Group trials

Each child in turn puts each of the three pieces that are not the mirror in the beam, about 30 seconds a piece, and marks all three piece cards under bright patch, dim patch or dark shadow from their own screen [[c2-T66a]]; then turns the mirror once, tilted in the beam, onto the side screen (20 seconds) and touches what the mirror did on the two mirror records [[c2-T66b]]. About 2 minutes a child, 10 a group. Stand at one table for each 2 minutes. Give no help. Mark T66a from the piece cards after class; mark T66b as you see it, on the scale. Hear the rotating six say what one piece did to the light.

#### 8 min. Records and seeing account

Each child touches the look where the toy is seen [[c2-V66a]], touches yes or no for whether the headlamp can be seen alone [[c2-V66b]], and touches keep or change for the account when the folded towel square record is shown [[c2-T66c]]. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each, for the spoken reasons; judge the rest from the page.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Collect the pages, wipe the tables and wash hands. Pieces back in the tub, flashlights off.

### Other rooms

No flashlight: use the piece records. No mirror: use the mirror record and say it is a picture.

### At home

**Swap.** No flashlight: a phone light, never at eyes. No mirror: a foil-wrapped card. No filter: a paper napkin.

**Skip.** The group trial and the unit 8 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Ask once: what stays still and what changes when you test pieces. Shine a lamp on a wall through a coffee filter, a clear cup wall piece and a folded towel square (an adult cuts the cup piece and tapes the edges; keep the lamp below faces), and your child sorts them under bright patch, dim patch and dark shadow. Ask where the toy is seen and whether a headlamp can be seen alone. Do not help.
  **Say.** "What did each piece do to the light?" Expect: the clear piece let it through, the filter let some through, the towel square blocked it (try each with your lamp first).
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Lit, own light, patch, shadow, lets light through, blocks light, turns the light, keep, change. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.
