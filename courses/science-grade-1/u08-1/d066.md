# Day 66: A tent, four pieces and a beam

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 65 handed on no gaps. Set six table sets: a flashlight aimed at a screen on a taped mark, the four pieces, the side screen and the piece cards. Teacher trims the piece edges. Before class, rehearse the four pieces in the beam and write down what each shows, so you can check the key. Keep the tent pictures sealed. Read the cards once.

### Safety and set-up

Never shine a light into anyone's eyes or look at the sun; you secure each battery compartment. Egg-carton pieces are new; check for egg allergies, an allergic child uses plain card; keep them out of mouths. Mirrors are plastic or edge-taped; no lasers. The darkness is a dimmed corner, and a picture alternative is always open to any child. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The tent pictures and the pieces are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Seat six groups of five.

#### 4 min. Plans

Say: "Pip will test three pieces." Each child draws what stays still and what changes for their own four pieces on "My tent and my lamp", with an arrow from the lamp to the toy and from the headlamp to the eye, and a partner points back to each. Then ask [[c2-P66a]] once for all: "Touch what stays still and what changes." Hear the rotating six, 20 seconds each.

#### 12 min. Group trials

Groups work in parallel while you walk. Each child in turn places one piece in the beam, about two minutes each, marks the piece card for the piece they put in under bright patch, dim patch or dark shadow from their own screen, and says whether it let light through, let some through or blocked it [[c2-T66a]]; stand at one table for each 2 minutes; the mirror piece is asked apart, with the side screen [[c2-T66b]]. Give no help. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Records and seeing account

Each child touches the look where the toy is seen [[c2-V66a]], says if the headlamp can be seen alone [[c2-V66b]], and touches keep or change for the account when the folded towel square record is shown [[c2-T66c]]. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each, for the spoken reasons; judge the rest from the page.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Collect the pages, wipe the tables and wash hands. Pieces back in the tub, flashlights off.

### Other rooms

No flashlight: use the piece records. No mirror: use the mirror record and say it is a picture.

### At home

**Swap.** No flashlight: a phone light, never at eyes. No mirror: a foil-wrapped card. No filter: a paper napkin.

**Skip.** The group trial and the unit 8 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Ask once: what stays still and what changes when you test pieces. Shine a lamp on a wall through a coffee filter, a clear cup wall piece and a folded towel square (an adult cuts the cup piece and tapes the edges; keep the lamp below faces), and your child sorts them under bright patch, dim patch and dark shadow. Ask where the toy is seen and whether a headlamp can be seen alone. Do not help.
  **Say.** "What did each piece do to the light?" Expect: the clear one let it through, the card blocked it.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Lit, own light, patch, shadow, lets light through, blocks light, turns the light, keep, change. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow
