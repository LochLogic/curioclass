# Repair cards (days 62, 63 and 65)

Large plain cards, one big word under each picture. They use only the class strips and Pip's cards from the teaching days, never an exit case or a day 64 strip.

## Error A: every season has the same daylight (days 62, 63, and the A table on day 65)

Pip's three equal bars laid under the class strips for fall, winter and spring. Children point to the light part that is shorter. Words on the card: the same, shorter.

## Error B: winter has more daylight (day 63, and the B table on day 65)

The three season labels laid against the class strips. The label winter goes on the strip whose light part is shorter. Children point and say it.

## Error C: one evening report is the whole day (day 62 if many, and the C table on day 65)

The day 9 report card beside the day 9 strip. A card for one moment, with a clock-less moment picture, and a card for the whole day. Children touch the card that shows the whole day.

## Error D: the dark part is daylight (day 62 if many, and the D table on day 65)

Two class strips. A finger traces from the sunrise picture to the sunset picture along the light part. A card says: daylight is the light part.

## The picture move cards (day 65, the fourth table works alone)

Three small cards: point to the light part; slide the sunrise pictures together; point to the shorter light part.
