# Feedback picture-cue cards (day 65)

The written feedback on each day 64 sheet is a small picture cue fixed to it after class. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, one word under each. The teacher reads the three most common comments aloud on day 65. A cue names a thing to fix and never gives a score.

1. A pointing hand on the white part of a strip, between a sunrise picture and a sunset picture: point to the light part.
2. Two strips with their sunrise pictures together and a hand on the shorter one: slide, then point to the shorter.
3. A season label above a strip: does the label match the light part?
4. A strip beside a single report card, with a ring round the strip: the strip shows the whole day.
5. A hand beside a speech mark: tell me how you know.
