# Unit 8 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, color or slide the answer. Note the day 9 and day 62 weather-watch retrieval in the margin beside the name, with the same scale and no score. Mark who you heard in the rotating six on each day, in the heard column. The End columns are the observation on day 64: one cell for each of the four look-fors and one for the support the child used. Credit a look-for only from the paper marks and the child's own turn, and credit the evidence look-for only when the child points to the full-day strips and not to the evening-only report. Only S counts; H means after the one neutral re-reading. Day 65 gives a child the chance to revise the day 64 sheet from the picture comment; the original is kept, and the new day 65 work is never scored. Keep the day 64 record for day 67. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied the cards or read a card aloud once more, and say so.
