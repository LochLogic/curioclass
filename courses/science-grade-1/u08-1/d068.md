# Day 68: Fix one mark, try a fresh card, hand on the record

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 67, fix one picture cue to each child's page and record. Count the error letters M to W and note the four largest groups. Set the unit 8 repair cards by letter. Keep the exit cards sealed. Rule the hand-off record, one line a child.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; children sit. Light is never pointed at eyes; sounds stay at classroom level; no emergency patterns. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 8 min. Read feedback and revise

Hand back day 66's assessment and day 67's assessment, the pages and the trial records. Say the three commonest cues aloud (2 minutes). Each child finds their cue, points to the mark, says what they saw and fixes one mark (6 minutes). Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. New work is unscored.

#### 12 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs: the eleven error letters M to W, by letter. Four groups, 3 minutes each with you, from the four largest; the other letters use the pictured cards, 1 minute each inside the 12. Error M and N group: the tent looks side by side. Error O and P group: mirror on and off; lamp and screen marked still. Error Q and R group: flash card beside the meaning card; near and farther cards. Error S and T group: young card then adult card; words beside a still picture. Error U group: plant, seed, young plant. Error V and W group: two light parts side by side; report beside the full-day strip.

#### 7 min. Fresh task and exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[c2-X68a]] "Touch what the mirror did." **Ask.** [[c2-X68b]] "Touch what you do next." **Ask.** [[c2-X68c]] "Touch the card that shows how long the day was light." **Ask.** [[c2-X68d]] "Say what the calf did, what the cow did and why." All touch or say; hear the six, 20 seconds each. A patch meaning light through is error O; works near so works farther, error R; the note as the whole day, error W; only the cow acting, error S.

#### 3 min. Collect and hand-off record

Collect the pages. On the hand-off record write each child's sources, models and what remains for grade 2. No family report is needed.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say the cue aloud. No cards: draw each on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the unit 8 record sheet.

- **10 min. Look back.** Find the picture cue and fix one mark on the day 66 page or the day 67 record.
- **10 min. Repair.** Lay the mirror on and mirror off pictures side by side.
  **Say.** "What did the mirror do?" Expect: it turned the light to the side, and the patch went when it was gone.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[c2-X68a]], [[c2-X68b]], [[c2-X68c]] and [[c2-X68d]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear the six only. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fix, cue, fresh, turns the light, farther, whole day, young. Frame: "I fixed ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 closes the year: the fresh task is never scored, and the hand-off record goes in the folder for the next teacher.
