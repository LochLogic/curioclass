# Day 68: Fix one mark, try a fresh card, hand on the record

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 67, fix one picture cue to each child's page and record. Count the error letters M to W and note the four largest groups. Set the unit 8 repair cards by letter. Set the year-end cue cards (cards-check-66-68.md) by letter; keep the fresh exit cards sealed. Rule the hand-off record, one line a child.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; children sit. Light is never pointed at eyes; sounds stay at classroom level; no emergency patterns. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 8 min. Read feedback and revise

Hand back day 66's assessment and day 67's assessment, the pages and the trial records. Say the three commonest cues aloud (2 minutes). Each child finds their cue, points to the mark, says what they saw and fixes one mark (6 minutes). Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. New work is unscored.

#### 12 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs by letter, M to W. Four groups, 3 minutes each with you, from the four largest; the other letters use the pictured cards, 1 minute each inside the 12. Error M and N group: the tent looks side by side. Error O and P group: mirror on and off; lamp and screen marked still. Error Q and R group: flash card beside the meaning card; near and farther cards. Error S and T group: young card then adult card; words beside a still picture. Error U group: plant, seed, young plant. Error V and W group: two light parts side by side; report beside the full-day strip.

#### 7 min. Exit: a fresh task

Say "Invented for practice." Read each card's words aloud. **Ask.** [[c2-X68a]] "Draw where the patch of light lands." Each child draws on side 2. **Ask.** [[c2-X68b]] "Touch works far or not yet." **Ask.** [[c2-X68c]] "Touch the card that shows how long the day was light." **Ask.** [[c2-X68d]] "Say what the pup does." **Ask.** [[c2-X68e]] "Say what the mother does when the pup calls." All draw, touch or say; hear the six, 20 seconds each. A patch drawn behind the mirror is error O; works far because it worked near, error R; the shadow drawing as the whole day, error W; only the mother acting, error S.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect the pages and the fresh cards. After class (about 15 minutes), write each child's sources, models and what remains for grade 2 on the hand-off record.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw each on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say the cue aloud. No cards: draw each on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the unit 8 record sheet.

- **10 min. Look back.** Find the picture cue and fix one mark on the day 66 page or the day 67 record.
- **10 min. Repair.** Lay the mirror on and mirror off pictures side by side.
  **Say.** "What did the mirror do?" Expect: it turned the light to the side, and the patch went when it was gone.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[c2-X68a]], [[c2-X68b]], [[c2-X68c]], [[c2-X68d]] and [[c2-X68e]], each asked aloud, one at a time, answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear the six only. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fix, cue, fresh, turns the light, farther, whole day, young. Frame: "I fixed ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

The fresh task is never scored; the hand-off record goes in the folder for the next teacher.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
