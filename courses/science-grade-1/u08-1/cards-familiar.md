# Familiar seasonal evidence cards (day 64, for the waiting groups)

Six large cards a group, from our own days. Three strips from the class archive, each with its date only, and three season labels. Waiting children match each strip to its season label, then draw on the back of the sheet why winter has less daylight. They do not help the group at the teacher's table. Remove torn cards.
