# Day 67: A message, a page of young and a season claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 66 sent the second check here. Set six table sets with the kept device and its dated plan; the plan and device stand for making and are not scored again. The day 60 and day 64 records stay on the shelf, unscored. Tape a near mark and a farther mark in a clear supervised lane. Keep the new pages sealed.

### Safety and set-up

String stays low and children walk around it. Light is never pointed at eyes or at the sun; you secure each battery compartment. Sounds stay at classroom level; cups rest lightly outside the ear. Messages are not emergency patterns; the message is "table ready". Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The message and the pages are new and invented for practice. You may pass." The message is "table ready": one long, two short. Seat six groups of five.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six groups come to the lane, three minutes each: five children at 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to move. Each child sends the message once at the near mark and once at the farther mark while a friend receives and marks [[c2-M67a]]. Then **Ask.** "Touch what the friend marked at each mark." Nothing else is asked at the lane. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

Waiting groups match the familiar unit 7 and unit 8 cards on their own, unscored; the new pages stay sealed.

#### 6 min. Record completion

Whole class, about 40 seconds an item. Read each stem, and each caption or word strip, aloud; every child touches or marks on side 2; mark each child on the scale. **Ask.** [[c2-M67b]] "Touch the sender, the device and the receiver in order." **Ask.** [[c2-M67d]] "Which is the signal, and which is the message?" **Ask.** [[c2-M67c]] "Touch what the records show: works at the near mark only, or works at every mark." **Ask.** [[c2-A67a]] (the lamb page). **Ask.** [[c2-A67b]] (the chick page). **Ask.** [[c2-A67c]] (the pumpkin caption). **Ask.** [[c2-S67a]] "Thumbs up if Pip is right, thumbs down if not." **Ask.** [[c2-S67b]] "Touch the one that shows how long the day was light." Hear a rotating six across the items, one or two at each, for the spoken answers.

#### 3 min. Clean up

Collect the pages and trial records. Roll the string, lift the marks.

### Other rooms

No lane: lay the near mark and the farther mark on a table. No device: use the sender, device and receiver cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No lane: a hallway with two taped marks. No device: a flashlight, never at eyes, or a spoon on a pot. Keep sounds at classroom level, away from ears, and any string low.

**Skip.** The group turn and the unit 8 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Your child sends one long and two short to you at a near mark and a farther mark, and you mark what you saw or heard. Ask what the signal was and what the message was. Say the lamb and ewe words, then draw a lamb and a ewe walking to it. Draw two bars of one length, one with a clearly shorter white part, and ask which season has more daylight. Do not help.
  **Say.** "Did it work at both marks?" Expect: it worked where the friend marked right; a mark not tried is not known.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Message, signal, sender, receiver, near, farther, words, picture, light part. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 67 closes the year-end checks; the pages are marked after class. Day 68 hands back day 66's assessment and day 67's assessment with a picture cue, and tries a fresh short task.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
