# Day 67: A message, a page of young and a season claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 66 sent the second check here. Set six table sets with the kept device and its dated plan; the plan and device stand for making and are not scored again. The day 60 and day 64 records stay on the shelf, unscored. Tape a near mark and a farther mark in a clear supervised lane. Keep the new pages sealed.

### Safety and set-up

String stays low and children walk around it. Light is never pointed at eyes or at the sun; you secure each battery compartment. Sounds stay at classroom level, never near ears. Messages are not emergency patterns; the message is table ready. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The message and the pages are new and invented for practice. You may pass." The message is table ready: one long, two short. Seat six groups of five.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Six groups come to the lane, three minutes each: five children at 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to move. Each child sends the message once at the near mark and once at the farther mark while a friend receives and marks [[c2-M67a]]; then touches sender, device, receiver [[c2-M67b]] and tells the signal from the message [[c2-M67d]]; then says which marks the records prove [[c2-M67c]]. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

Other groups work the animal pages [[c2-A67a]], [[c2-A67b]] and [[c2-A67c]] and the strips [[c2-S67a]] and [[c2-S67b]], pointing, touching and drawing on the page.

#### 6 min. Record completion

Each child finishes the page. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each, for the spoken reasons; judge the rest from the page.

#### 3 min. Clean up

Collect the pages and trial records. Roll the string, lift the marks.

### Other rooms

No lane: lay the near mark and the farther mark on a table. No device: use the sender, device and receiver cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No lane: a hallway with two taped marks. No device: a flashlight, never at eyes, or a spoon on a pot. Keep sounds at classroom level, away from ears, and any string low.

**Skip.** The group turn and the unit 8 record sheet.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Your child sends one long and two short to you at a near mark and a farther mark, and you mark what you saw or heard. Ask what the signal was and what the message was. Read the lamb and ewe words, then show the picture. Show two strips and ask which season has more daylight. Do not help.
  **Say.** "Did it work at both marks?" Expect: only the marks tried can be judged.
- **5 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Message, signal, sender, receiver, near, farther, words, picture, light part. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, a scribe and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 67 closes the year-end checks; the pages are marked after class. Day 68 hands back day 66's assessment and day 67's assessment with a picture cue, and tries a fresh short task.
