# Invented trial cards (days 51 and 52, waiting groups)

Say aloud: "These cards are invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Cards are small and cut apart, one set a group.

## Day 51: sender's card and friend's mark (understood, not understood)

Each card has two small pictures joined by a thin arrow: on the left the sender's card, on the right the friend's mark, a corner word near or far, and a drawn box beneath for the child to draw a guess. Two kinds of picture: line up (three children in a line) and wait (a flat hand held up).

1. near: line up, then line up.
2. far: wait, then line up.
3. far: wait, then wait.
4. near: line up, then wait.
5. near: wait, then wait.
6. far: line up, then line up.

Key for the teacher: the pictures match on cards 1, 3, 5 and 6 (understood); they differ on cards 2 and 4 (not understood).

## Day 52: friend's marks on two tries (same both times, not the same)

Each card shows a device (a box drum, a flap light or a cup pair), one small sender's card, and two small boxes for the friend's mark on try one and try two.

1. box drum: line up, line up.
2. flap light: line up, wait.
3. cup pair: wait, wait.
4. box drum: wait, line up.
5. flap light: wait, wait.
6. cup pair: line up, wait.

Key for the teacher: same both times on cards 1, 3 and 5; not the same on cards 2, 4 and 6.
