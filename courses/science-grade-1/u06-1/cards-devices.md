# Three kinds of device (days 48 and 49)

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Four large cards the teacher holds up, one at a time.

**Cup pair, string straight.** Two paper cups on their sides, a string joining their bottoms, the string drawn as a straight gentle line. One child speaks quietly toward one cup from the side, not into it; another holds the other cup slightly away from the ear. Words under it: cup pair. An arrow from the cup mouth to the word catch, and an arrow along the string to the word carry.

**Cup pair, string slack.** The same two cups, the string drawn as a drooping curve that touches the floor. Words under it: slack. No arrow.

**Flashlight with a flap.** A flashlight lying on a table, aimed low at a card on the floor. A cardboard flap is taped at its front, hinged so it can lift. One short dash of light is drawn on the card. Words under it: flap. An arrow from the flap to the words short light.

**Box drum.** A small box with a paper top, a hand tapping the top gently. Two small marks beside it show the top is shaking. Words under it: box drum. An arrow from the top to the word shakes.
