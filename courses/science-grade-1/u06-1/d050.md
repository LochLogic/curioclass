# Day 50: Measure with strips

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape one line on the floor for each group, long enough for six strips. Set six identical strips and two markers at each line. Cut each cup string to reach the farther mark with room to spare. Choose today's six. Hide the exit cards. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-48-51.

### Safety and set-up

Walk around devices and strings, kept low. Devices are held for planning, not sent: no light at eyes, no sound near ears. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Repair for error B, day 49's exit. Say "Invented for practice." Show repair card B, the plan beside the made flap. Read the wrong sentence ("My plan is my device, I am done") beside the right. Children touch the one that can send now. A child with only a plan finishes the missing part with your help: tape on a pre-cut flap or a pre-cut drum top.

#### 4 min. Strips

Nearer and farther are review from kindergarten; today the nearer mark is called the near mark. Lay three identical strips on a taped line, first with a gap, then touching. **Ask.** "Which tells how long the path is?" Expect: the touching one. Teach: strips touch end to end, no gaps, no overlaps; the count is the path's length. No tape measure.

#### 10 min. Measure

Groups of five, six strips each. Each child lays one strip along the group's taped line, touching, to a farther mark of 5 strips. All count aloud. Then two children lay the near mark at 2 strips and set a marker; all count aloud. Gaps: recount.

#### 8 min. Plan the trial

Partners fill a plan card (keep, change, watch: kindergarten, review): the device, the message (line up or wait), keep the same (message, signal, string tight), change (the distance only), watch (the friend's mark). They draw in the boxes. Partners swap and say each box back.

Check: only the distance changes.

#### 2 min. Share

A rotating six say "I will keep ___, change ___, watch ___", 20 seconds each. Name who on the record sheet.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect strips; tape the markers down.

#### 4 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X50a]] "Touch the row that tells how long the path is." Three rows of blocks: one with a gap, one with a block on top of its neighbor, one touching. All touch; mark every child. **Ask.** [[s06-X50b]] "Count the crayons out loud. How many crayons long is the path?" Each child counts in a whisper and shows the number on fingers; mark every child. **Ask.** [[s06-X50c]] "Which one should Sam keep the same?" Hear six, 20 seconds each. The row with a gap is error E.

### Other rooms

No strips: cut newspaper into equal strips.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: equal paper strips cut from a page.

**Skip.** The group lines and the share.

- **5 min. Strips.** Lay three strips with a gap, then touching. No strips: a row of coins or real paper clips with a gap, then touching.
  **Say.** "Which tells how long the path is?" Expect: the touching one.
- **10 min. Measure.** Lay strips touching from a start to a mark. Count them aloud. Set a nearer mark at 2 strips.
- **7 min. Plan.** Your child draws what to keep, change and watch.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X50a]], [[s06-X50b]] and [[s06-X50c]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the farther mark only.

### Words and supports

Strip, end to end, gap, near, farther, keep, change, watch. Frame: "The path is ___ strips." Counting aloud and pointing count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who counts a gap as a strip (error E). Day 51 opens with error E.
