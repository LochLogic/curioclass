# Day 50: Measure with strips

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape one line on the floor for each group, long enough for six strips. Set six identical strips and two markers at each line. Cut each cup string to reach the farther mark with room to spare. Choose today's six. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

Walk around devices and strings, kept low. Devices are held for planning, not sent: no light at eyes, no sound near ears. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

If many gave error B on day 49's exit, this is the repair. Say "Invented for practice." Show repair card B, the plan beside the made flap. Read the wrong sentence ("My plan is my device, I am done") beside the right. Children touch the one that can send now. A child with only a plan finishes the missing part with your help.

#### 4 min. Strips

Near and farther are review from kindergarten. Lay three identical strips on a taped line, first with a gap, then touching. **Ask.** "Which tells how long the path is?" Expect: the touching one. Teach: strips touch end to end, no gaps and no strips on top of each other; the count of strips is how long the path is. No tape measure, no part strips.

#### 10 min. Measure

Groups of five, six strips each. Each child lays one strip along the group's taped line, touching, to a farther mark of 5 strips. The group counts them aloud together. Then the group sets the near mark at 2 strips with a marker. Each child lays one strip: 5 x 30 seconds is 2 minutes 30 seconds. Fix gaps by asking the group to recount.

#### 8 min. Plan the trial

Partners fill a plan card (keep, change, watch: kindergarten, review): the device, the message (line up or wait), keep the same (message, signal, string tight), change (the distance only), watch (the friend's mark). They draw in the boxes, each holding their own device. Partners swap and point to each box and say it back.

Check: keep the message, the signal and the string the same; change only the distance; watch the friend's mark.

#### 3 min. Share

A rotating six say "I will keep ___, change ___, watch ___", 20 seconds each. Name who on the record sheet.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect strips; tape the markers down.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X50a]] "Touch the row that measures the path." **Ask.** [[s06-X50b]] "Count the clips in that row out loud. How many clips long is the path?" Hear six, 20 seconds each. A counted space is error E.

### Other rooms

No strips: cut newspaper into equal strips.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: equal paper strips cut from a page.

**Skip.** The group lines and the share.

- **5 min. Strips.** Lay three strips with a gap, then touching.
  **Say.** "Which tells how long the path is?" Expect: the touching one.
- **10 min. Measure.** Lay strips touching from a start to a mark. Count them aloud. Set a nearer mark at 2 strips.
- **7 min. Plan.** Your child draws what to keep, change and watch.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X50a]] and [[s06-X50b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the farther mark only.

### Words and supports

Strip, end to end, gap, near, farther, keep, change, watch. Frame: "The path is ___ strips." Counting aloud and pointing count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who counts a gap as a strip (error E). Day 51 opens with error E for 4 minutes, then each group sends the same card at the near and farther marks.
