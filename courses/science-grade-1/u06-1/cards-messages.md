# Message cards (day 48)

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Five sets, one set a group of five, each set two cards. Every pattern is only a practice pattern; none copies a real emergency signal.

**Line up.** A picture of three children standing one behind another in a line. Under it the big words: line up. Pattern: two quick taps or two quick blinks, drawn as two short dashes.

**Wait.** A picture of one child holding a flat hand up. Under it the big words: wait. Pattern: one slow tap or one long blink, drawn as one long dash.

**The pattern nobody agreed.** One card for the teacher only, held up after the class has marked its guess: three dashes, quick, slow, quick. It has no picture and no word under it.
