# Day 49: Sketch it, then make it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pre-punch every cup; cut each string and flap card. Set out each group's supplies. Choose today's six. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

String stays low; walk around it, never loop it on a body. Only an adult punches cups. Scissors are blunt, used seated, counted back. Light never at eyes, battery compartment secured by you; drum at classroom level; speak toward a cup from the side, never into it. No real emergency patterns. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

If many gave error A on day 48's exit, this is the repair. Say "Invented for practice." Show repair card A: the sender's card (wait) beside the friend's mark (line up). Read the wrong sentence ("The friend saw the lamp blink, so it worked") beside the right one. Every child touches same or not the same.

#### 6 min. Three kinds

You and an adult show, close up: a cup pair with the string gently straight, then slack; a flashlight blinked behind a cardboard flap, aimed low; a box drum tapped. Name each part and its job, as review of shape and job: the cup catches the shake, the string carries it, the flap makes a short light, the drum top shakes. A slack string does not carry the shake.

#### 4 min. Sketch

On the sketch page each child draws the device they will make, the message it sends (line up or wait) and one arrow from a shape to its job.

#### 13 min. Make

Groups of five. Each child makes one device from their sketch: a cup pair with the string ends taped flat inside the cups, a flap for the group's flashlight, or a drum top on a small box. Only flap makers cut, seated, two scissors a group. A flap maker tries the flap on the shared flashlight for 30 seconds, aimed at a low card: 5 x 30 seconds is 2 minutes 30 seconds. A child with only a plan keeps building.

#### 3 min. Share

**Ask.** "Which part of your device does which job?" A rotating six hold up their device and answer, 20 seconds each. Expect: a cup catches the shake; a flap makes a short light.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Count the scissors. Adults collect loose string. Each device gets a name and goes on the shelf.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X49a]] "Touch the one that can send a message now." **Ask.** [[s06-X49b]] "Touch the flap that is ready to send a message." Hear six, 20 seconds each. The plan is error B.

### Other rooms

No cups: a small box and a sheet for a drum. No flashlight: a drum or cup pair.

### At home

**Swap.** No cups: a drum from a box and paper. No torch: skip the flap.

**Skip.** The group supplies and the share.

- **6 min. Look.** Tap a box drum. Show two cups joined by string, slack and then straight.
  **Say.** "Which string will carry the shake?" Expect: the straight one.
- **6 min. Sketch.** Your child draws the device and one arrow from a shape to its job.
- **12 min. Make.** Make one drum top on a small box, or the cup pair with tape inside.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X49a]] and [[s06-X49b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: everyone makes the drum. Longer: sketch a second device by the other kind.

### Words and supports

Sketch, shape, job, cup pair, flap, drum. Frame: "The ___ does the job of ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who takes a plan for a device (error B). Day 50 opens with error B for 3 minutes, then measures the farther mark with identical paper strips and plans a near and farther trial.
