# Exit cards, days 48 to 51

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or says yes or no; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. None shows a repair card, a device card or the lane map.

## Day 48 (items s06-X48a and s06-X48b)

**X48a.** A garden gate with a girl at it and a friend standing far across a lawn. Three cards: the girl taps a flowerpot softly with a spoon; the girl holds a lamp low, aimed at a card on the ground; the girl shines a flashlight at the friend's face. Spoken: "Touch a safe way to send a signal that is not shouting." **Key:** the flowerpot tap or the low lamp. The flashlight at the face is shown with help and the rule that light never points at eyes is said aloud again.

**X48b.** The friend at the gate holds a page on which the wait picture is marked. A separate card shows what the girl's hidden card said: line up. Spoken: "Did the friend understand the message? Say yes or no." **Key:** no. Yes is error A.

## Day 49 (items s06-X49a and s06-X49b)

**X49a.** Two cards: a paper plan of a drum drawn flat on a page, and a drum made from a small box. Spoken: "Touch the one that can send a message now." **Key:** the made drum. The plan is error B.

**X49b.** Two cards of paper cups joined by string: in the first the string lies on the floor in loops; in the second the string runs straight and gentle. Spoken: "Touch the pair where the string is ready to carry a message." **Key:** the straight string. The loops are shown with help.

## Day 50 (items s06-X50a and s06-X50b)

**X50a.** A new path drawn on the floor from above. Two cards of identical paper strips laid along it: in the first there is a gap between two strips; in the second the strips touch end to end. The path is four strips long. Spoken: "Touch the row that measures the path." **Key:** the touching row. The gap row is error E.

**X50b.** The touching row again. Spoken: "Count the strips in that row out loud. How many strips long is the path?" **Key:** four strips. A count that includes the gap is error E.

## Day 51 (items s06-X51a and s06-X51b)

**X51a.** Two cards of a trial pair, each showing a near mark and a farther mark. In the first, the same card and the same signal (two quick dashes) appear at both marks. In the second, two quick dashes at the near mark and one long dash at the farther mark. Spoken: "Touch the pair that shows whether distance changes what the friend gets." **Key:** the first pair. The second pair is error D.

**X51b.** Pip the puppet and a device that worked at the near mark. Three cards: Pip tries the same device and signal at the farther mark; Pip says it works anywhere and sits down; Pip changes the signal and tries far away. Spoken: "Touch what Pip should do to find out about the farther mark." **Key:** the first card. Stopping is error C; changing the signal is error D.
