# Exit cards, days 48 to 51

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or says yes or no; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. None shows a repair card, a device card or the lane map.

## Day 48 (items s06-X48a and s06-X48b)

**X48a.** A garden gate with a girl at it and a friend standing far across a lawn. Three cards: the girl taps a flowerpot softly with a spoon; the girl holds a lamp low, aimed at a card on the ground; the girl shines a flashlight at the friend's face. Spoken: "Touch a safe way to send a signal that is not shouting." **Key:** the flowerpot tap or the low lamp. The flashlight at the face is shown with help and the rule that light never points at eyes is said aloud again.

**X48b.** The friend at the gate holds a page on which the wait picture is marked. A separate card shows what the girl's hidden card said: line up. Spoken: "Did the friend understand the message? Say yes or no." **Key:** no. Yes is error A.

## Day 49 (items s06-X49a and s06-X49b)

**X49a.** Two cards: a paper plan of a clapper drawn flat on a page, and a clapper made from two cardboard strips. Spoken: "Touch the one that can send a message now." **Key:** the made clapper. The plan is error B.

**X49b.** Two cards of a cardboard flap on a flashlight: in the first the flap is taped flat over the whole lens; in the second the flap hangs on one taped edge and can lift. Spoken: "Touch the flap that is ready to send a message." **Key:** the flap that can lift. The flap taped flat is shown with help.

## Day 50 (items s06-X50a and s06-X50b)

**X50a.** A new path drawn on the floor from above. Two cards of identical paper clips laid along it: in the first there is a space between two clips; in the second the clips sit in a line, each touching the next. The path is four clips long. Spoken: "Touch the row that measures the path." **Key:** the touching row. The space row is error E.

**X50b.** The two rows again, the one with the space and the touching one. Spoken: "Count the clips in the touching row out loud. How many clips long is the path?" **Key:** four clips. A count that includes the space is error E.

## Day 51 (items s06-X51a and s06-X51b)

**X51a.** Two cards of a trial pair, each showing a near mark and a farther mark. In the first, two quick dashes at the near mark and one long dash at the farther mark. In the second, the same card and the same signal (two quick dashes) appear at both marks. Spoken: "Touch the pair that shows whether distance changes what the friend gets." **Key:** the second pair. The first pair is error D.

**X51b.** Pip the puppet and a flap light that worked at the near mark. Three cards: Pip says it works anywhere and sits down; Pip changes the signal and tries far away; Pip tries the same flap light and signal at the farther mark. Spoken: "Touch what Pip should do to find out about the farther place." **Key:** the third card. Stopping is error C; changing the signal is error D.
