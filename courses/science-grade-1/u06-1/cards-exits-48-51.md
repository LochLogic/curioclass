# Exit cards, days 48 to 51

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Every card is a schematic and supplies no result. Every child touches a card or shows thumb up or down; the teacher hears a rotating six, 20 seconds each, and writes who on the record sheet. None shows a repair card, a device card or the lane map. Keys and wrong answers are in key-cards-exits-48-51.md (teacher only, never printed for children).

## Day 48 (items s06-X48a, s06-X48b and s06-X48c)

**X48a.** A garden gate with a girl at it and a friend standing far across a lawn. Three cards: the girl taps a flowerpot softly with a spoon; the girl rings a small hand bell held low at her side; the girl shines a flashlight at the friend's face. Spoken: "Touch a safe way to send a signal that is not shouting."

**X48b.** The friend at the gate holds a page on which the wait picture is marked. A separate card shows what the girl's hidden card said: line up. Spoken: "Did the friend understand the message? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no, on show me."

**X48c.** The same garden gate card. Spoken: "The girl wants her friend to line up. Say the problem she needs to solve."

## Day 49 (items s06-X49a and s06-X49b)

**X49a.** Three cards: two cups joined by a long straight string with its ends taped inside the cups; a small box with its paper top lying off beside it; a flashlight with a cardboard flap taped flat over the whole lens. Spoken: "Touch the one that could send a message now."

**X49b.** The same three cards. Spoken: "Say why the cups with the straight string could send a message now."

## Day 50 (items s06-X50a and s06-X50b)

**X50a.** A new path drawn on the floor from above. Three cards of identical wooden blocks laid along it: in the first there is a gap between two blocks; in the second one block lies partly on top of its neighbor; in the third the blocks sit in a line, each touching the next, none on top. Spoken: "Touch the row that tells how long the path is."

**X50b.** A different path with five identical crayons laid end to end along it, each touching the next. Spoken: "Count the crayons out loud. How many crayons long is the path?"

**X50c.** A card: Sam with a cup pair, a near mark and a farther mark, and two message pictures, wait at the near mark and line up at the farther mark. Spoken: "Sam will send wait at the near mark, then line up at the farther mark. Two things change: the distance and the message. Which one should Sam keep the same?"

## Day 51 (items s06-X51a and s06-X51b)

**X51a.** Two cards of a trial pair, each showing a near mark and a farther mark. In the first, two quick dashes at the near mark and one long dash at the farther mark. In the second, the same card and the same signal (two quick dashes) appear at both marks. Spoken: "Touch the pair that shows whether distance changes what the friend gets."

**X51b.** Pip the puppet and a flap light that worked at the near mark. Three cards: Pip says it works anywhere and sits down; Pip changes the signal and tries far away; Pip tries the same flap light and signal at the farther mark. Spoken: "Touch what Pip should do to find out about the farther place."
