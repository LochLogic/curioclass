# Day 48: A signal is not a message

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a near and a far floor marker. Set the drum, flashlight and pictures at the near one. Choose today's six. Hide exit cards. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-48-51.

### Safety and set-up

Practice patterns only. Drum at classroom level. The flashlight is yours, aimed down at a low card, never at eyes. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Day 47 ended unit 5. Show the old ramp and tower pictures: "Point to the problem and the test. Point to a sketch feature and say its job." Drum picture: "Point to the part that shakes." Box-light picture: "Point to the light source." Hear six, 20 seconds each. Unscored.

#### 6 min. Hook

"What does the recess bell tell the playground?" With the class at the far marker, tap four slow taps on the drum from the near one. **Ask.** "Who heard it? Who knows what it said?" Expect: most heard; few know. Pip: "What did it mean?" Post the question: "How can we tell a friend across the playground it is time to come in, without shouting?"

#### 6 min. Sender, signal, receiver

Show the three-box picture: sender, device, receiver. Message: what the sender wants the friend to know. Signal: the sound or light that goes across. Say "Invented for practice." The class agrees two messages, each with a picture and a pattern: line up (two quick taps or blinks), wait (one slow tap or long blink). **Ask.** "What did the friend need besides hearing a sound?" Expect: to know what it means. Each child draws the three boxes on the student page.

#### 7 min. Send and mark

Tap "line up" with the card hidden. Every child marks the sound row before the card is shown. Repeat with the flashlight, in the light row. Then tap three taps nobody agreed, quick, slow, quick; children draw a guess in the last box. Six say what they marked, 20 seconds each.

#### 4 min. The problem

The class says the problem and the test: the friend marks the same picture as the sender's card. Open the question board: meaning, reach, dependable.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Devices collected.

#### 3 min. Exit

Exit with picture cards, as in kindergarten (review). Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X48a]] "Touch a safe way to send a signal that is not shouting." All touch; mark every child. **Ask.** [[s06-X48b]] "Did the friend understand the message? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no, on show me." Mark every child. Thumb up is error A. **Ask.** [[s06-X48c]] "Say the problem the girl needs to solve." Hear the rotating six on this one only, 20 seconds each.

### Other rooms

No drum: a spoon on a box. No printer: draw the cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No flashlight: a lamp held low. Never aim a flashlight or lamp at faces or eyes.

**Skip.** The marking page and the board.

- **5 min. Taps.** Tap four slow taps on a box from across the room.
  **Say.** "What did that tell you?" Expect: nothing yet; we did not agree.
- **10 min. Agree.** Agree two quick taps means line up, one slow tap means wait. Your child draws sender, device and receiver on the student page.
- **7 min. Mark.** Hide a card and send it. Your child points to a picture before you show it.
  **Say.** "Which picture do you think it was?" Expect: the matching one.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X48a]], [[s06-X48b]] and [[s06-X48c]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sound only. Longer: a chime.

### Words and supports

Message, signal, sender, receiver. Frame: "The friend marked ___; the card said ___."

### Tomorrow
