# Day 48: A signal is not a message

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a near and a far floor marker. Set the drum, flashlight and pictures at the near one. Choose today's six. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

Practice patterns only. Drum at classroom level. The flashlight is yours, aimed down at a low card, never at eyes. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Day 47 closed unit 5 with its record kept. Show the old ramp and tower pictures: "Point to the problem and the test. Point to a sketch feature and say its job." Drum picture: "Point to the part that shakes." Box-light picture: "Point to the light source." Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. Unscored.

#### 6 min. Hook

"What does the recess bell tell the playground?" Expect: time to come in. With the class at the far marker, tap four slow taps on the drum from the near one. **Ask.** "Who heard it? Who knows what it said?" Expect: most heard; few know. Pip: "What did it mean?" Post the question: "How can we tell a friend across the playground it is time to come in, without shouting?"

#### 6 min. Sender, signal, receiver

Show the three-box picture: sender, device, receiver. Message: what the sender wants the friend to know. Signal: the sound or light that goes across. Say "Invented for practice." The class agrees two messages, each with a picture and a pattern: line up (two quick taps or blinks), wait (one slow tap or long blink). **Ask.** "What did the friend need besides hearing a sound?" Expect: to know what it means. Each child draws the three boxes on the student page.

#### 7 min. Send and mark

Tap "line up" with the card hidden. Every child marks the sound row before the card is shown. Repeat with the flashlight aimed down at a low card, in the light row. Then tap three dashes nobody agreed; children draw a guess in the last box. Many differ. Six say what they marked, 20 seconds each.

#### 4 min. The problem

The class says the problem and the test: the friend marks the same picture as the sender's card. Open the question board: meaning, reach, dependable. Put the guesses under meaning.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect devices.

#### 3 min. Exit

The rotating six heard, record sheet names who, is review from kindergarten. Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X48a]] "Touch a safe way to send a signal that is not shouting." **Ask.** [[s06-X48b]] "Did the friend understand the message? Say yes or no." Hear six, 20 seconds each. Yes is error A. Light never points at eyes.

### Other rooms

No drum: a spoon on a box. No printer: draw the cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No torch: a lamp held low. Never aim a torch or lamp at faces or eyes.

**Skip.** The marking page and the board.

- **5 min. Taps.** Tap four slow taps on a box from across the room.
  **Say.** "What did that tell you?" Expect: nothing yet; we did not agree.
- **10 min. Agree.** Agree two quick taps means line up, one slow tap means wait. Your child draws sender, device and receiver on the student page.
- **7 min. Mark.** Hide a card and send it. Your child points to a picture before you show it.
  **Say.** "Which picture do you think it was?" Expect: the matching one.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X48a]] and [[s06-X48b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sound only. Longer: a chime.

### Words and supports

Message, signal, sender, receiver. Frame: "The friend marked ___; the card said ___."
