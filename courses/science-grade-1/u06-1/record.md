# Unit 6 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, draw or place the answer. Note the day 48 retrieval in the margin beside the name, with the same scale and no score. Mark who you heard in the rotating six on each day, in the heard column. The End columns are the observation on day 54: for item 1, the made work from day 53, one cell for each of the four components and one for the support the child used; for item 2, the two trials, one cell for each of the six components and one for the support. Credit a component only from the paper marks and the child's own turn, and credit a trial mark only if it is what the receiver marked, not what the sender's card said. An honest failed trial is evidence. Day 55 gives a child the chance to revise the day 53 sketch from the picture comment and to try once more at a failed mark; the new mark replaces the day 54 value for send and receipt, marked revised, and earns nothing else. Never score the other new day 55 work. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied the cards or read a card aloud, and say so.
