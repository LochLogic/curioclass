# Sender, device, receiver picture (day 48, kept on the wall)

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Three large boxes in a row, joined left to right by two thick arrows. Box 1 holds a child with a small card held to the chest, the card face hidden. Big word under it: sender. Box 2 holds a plain box drum with two short tap marks beside it, and under the box the words box drum, flashlight, cup pair as three small pictures to swap in. Big word: device. Box 3 holds a child with a page and a pencil, one ear toward the device. Big word: receiver. A dotted line runs from the device box to the receiver box, with the big word signal over it. Under the whole row a bracket from the first box to the card reads: message. Nothing on the picture says what any message is.
