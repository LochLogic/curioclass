# Repair cards, days 49 to 51

Keep these in an envelope. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children, one big word under each picture. Each repair is one picture pair or one picture and one wrong sentence beside one right sentence. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

**A (day 49): card and mark.** Two children stand at opposite ends of a hallway; one holds a lamp aimed low at a card on the floor. First picture: the sender's card, a flat hand held up (wait). Second picture: the page the friend marked, three children in a line (line up). Wrong: "The friend saw the lamp blink, so it worked." Right: "The friend marked line up and the card said wait, so the blink was noticed and the message was not understood." Children touch same or not the same.

**B (day 50): the plan and the made flap.** First picture: a paper plan of a cardboard flap for a flashlight, drawn as a flat sketch with an arrow from the flap to the words short light. Second picture: a made cardboard flap taped on a flashlight lying on a table. Wrong: "My plan is my device, I am done." Right: "My plan shows what to make; my made device sends the message." Children touch the one that can send now, then a child with only a plan finishes the missing part with help.

**E (day 51): the taped line.** One picture of a taped line on the floor with a start mark and an end mark, and six empty strip places drawn dotted along it. Wrong: "Strips with a gap still count; five strips." Right: "Strips touch end to end, no gaps or overlaps; count only the strips." Children lay six real strips touching along their own taped line and recount aloud.
