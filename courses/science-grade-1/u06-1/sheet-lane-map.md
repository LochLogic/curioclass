# Lane map (days 51 and 52)

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, diverse children. A long strip of floor seen from above, taped at both edges. At the left end a thick line with the big words sender line. Two taped marks along it: the first is drawn as two strips laid touching, with the big word near at its end; the second is drawn as five strips laid touching, with the big word farther at its end. A small child figure with a page stands at each mark; they are the receivers. A thin line for the string runs from the sender line along the middle of the lane, drawn gently straight. Beside the lane, a dotted path with small footprints and the big words walk here shows the way around the string. A caution sign is not drawn; the teacher reads the safety lines aloud.
