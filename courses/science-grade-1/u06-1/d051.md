# Day 51: Near and farther

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape the one closed lane: sender line, near mark at 2 strips (about 1 m), farther mark at 5 strips (about 2.5 m), a low card at each mark, a walking route round the string. Time one practice turn. Choose today's six. Hide the exit cards. Teacher keys: key-cards-exits-48-51.

### Safety and set-up

One group in the lane, under your eye; a helper adult watches the waiting groups. String low and gently straight; walk round it, no pulling. Light aimed down at the low card, never at eyes; you secure each battery compartment. Sounds at classroom level. Cups rest outside the ear. No emergency patterns. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

If many gave error E on day 50's exit, this is the repair. Show repair card E: the wrong sentence ("Strips with a gap still count; five strips") beside the right. Groups lay six strips touching and recount.

#### 3 min. Set-up

Each child picks a face-down message card, line up or wait, keeps it hidden, and holds their plan card (keep, change, watch: review).

#### 18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each

One group at a time in the lane. Both receivers stand at their marks all turn. Each child sends their card at the near mark, then the farther mark, same signal, same device; roles turn after a child's two sends. A receiver holds up a line up or wait card before the sender's card is shown; the fourth child ticks a pre-drawn card. Each cup pair has one string, cut by an adult for the farther mark; at the near mark the sender winds the spare on the string-winding card so it stays gently straight. The chart notes tight or not. If a turn runs long, the farther send goes first next turn; never cut a child's send. Other groups sort the invented trial cards: understood means the friend marked what the card said. Say "Invented for practice." They draw a guess for the farther mark. Write who sent.

#### 4 min. Chart

Each child checks the friend marked boxes show what the receiver marked, not the sender's card, circles tight or not, leaves the again column blank, and draws two arrows: "I did ___, so the friend ___" for near and farther. **Ask.** "What did you keep the same and what did you change?" Expect: the card, signal and device; only the distance. Partners say it; scribe a rotating six, 20 seconds each.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Store devices.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X51a]] "Touch the pair that shows whether distance changes what the friend gets." **Ask.** [[s06-X51b]] "Touch what Pip should do to find out about the farther place." Hear six, 20 seconds each. A changed signal is error D. Stopping at near is error C.

### Other rooms

No lane: use the invented trial cards and practice the chart only.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: paper sheets laid end to end.

**Skip.** The group turns and the sorting.

- **5 min. Set the marks.** Lay 2 strips for near and 5 for farther.
- **12 min. Send.** Your child sends the same hidden card at each mark. You mark a picture first, then see the card.
  **Say.** "What did you keep the same and what did you change?" Expect: the card and signal; only the distance.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the marks and two arrows.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X51a]] and [[s06-X51b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one send each. Longer: a second card.

### Words and supports

Near, farther, understood, tight. Frame: "I did ___, so the friend ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 repairs errors C and D, then repeats the trial.
