# Day 51: Near and farther

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape the one closed lane: sender line, near mark at 2 strips, farther mark at 5 strips, a low card at each mark, a walking route round the string. Choose today's six. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

One group in the lane, under your eye. String low and gently straight; walk round it, no pulling. Light aimed down at the low card, never at eyes. Sound at classroom level. No emergency patterns. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

If many gave error E on day 50's exit, this is the repair. Show repair card E: the wrong sentence ("Strips with a gap still count; five strips") beside the right. Each group lays six strips touching along a taped line and recounts aloud.

#### 3 min. Set-up

Read the lane map aloud. Each child draws a hidden message card, line up or wait, on the back of their plan card, which holds keep, change and watch.

#### 18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each

One group at a time in the lane. Each child sends their card once at the near mark and once at the farther mark, same signal, same device. A receiver at each mark holds up a line up or wait card before the sender's card is shown. A fourth child draws that mark on the sender's chart, then the card. Roles turn each send. Ten sends of 15 seconds is 2 minutes 30 seconds, plus 30 seconds to change over: 3 minutes. Cup senders wind spare string on the string-winding card so it stays gently straight; the chart notes tight or not. Other groups sort the invented trial cards into understood and not understood. Say "These cards are invented for practice." Write who sent.

#### 4 min. Chart

Each child checks the friend marked boxes, circles tight or not, and draws two arrows: "I did ___, so the friend ___" for near and farther. **Ask.** "What did you keep the same and what did you change?" Expect: the card, signal and device; only the distance. Partners say it; scribe a rotating six, 20 seconds each. Keep the charts for day 52.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Store devices; adults collect string.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented for practice." **Ask.** [[s06-X51a]] "Touch the pair that shows whether distance changes what the friend gets." **Ask.** [[s06-X51b]] "Touch what Pip should do to find out about the farther mark." Hear six, 20 seconds each. A changed signal is error D. Stopping at near is error C.

### Other rooms

No lane: use the invented trial cards, said aloud to be invented, and practise the chart only. A drum not heard far: the receiver marks could not tell; keep it and send once more.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: paper sheets laid end to end.

**Skip.** The group turns and the sorting.

- **5 min. Set the marks.** Lay 2 strips for near and 5 for farther.
- **12 min. Send.** Your child sends the same hidden card at each mark. You mark a picture first, then see the card.
  **Say.** "What did you keep the same and what did you change?" Expect: the card and signal; only the distance.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the marks and two arrows.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X51a]] and [[s06-X51b]], answered aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one send at each mark. Longer: a second card at both marks.

### Words and supports

Near, farther, understood, tight. Frame: "I did ___, so the friend ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who assumes it works anywhere (error C) or changes two things (error D). Day 52 opens with both for 3 minutes each, then repeats the receiver trial from these charts.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
