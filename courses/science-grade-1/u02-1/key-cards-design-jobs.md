# Shape and job cards (day 15): teacher key (day 15)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-design-jobs.md.

- The match sets (six sets, one for each group of five) / Spoken: Key: tendril to thin pole; webbed foot to water; broad paw to soil; hooked burr to cloth. The pretty four fit no job here: they are lovely, and their shape does not do these jobs. A child who matches a pretty card by its color is showing error D, and the repair is to hold the two cards side by side and say "its shape does the job."
