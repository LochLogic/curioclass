# Day 11: What do the parts do, and what does the shoot show?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check for plant allergies; plan photographs for any child who needs them. Lay six trays and the root close-up cards. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Never taste a plant part; no sap, no digging. Only you lift the class plant. Two sinks, 15 children each at about 12 seconds is 3 minutes; with one sink, wipes at the tables.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error A (day 10's E10b): trace the chart's water arrow into the root with a finger and say "roots take in water and hold." Whole class if more than a third made it; otherwise a small group.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read the part-and-job chart aloud (review). **Ask.** [[s02-P11-1]] "Point to a plant part and say its job." Hear three.

#### 8 min. Look at the parts

Each group finds a stem, a leaf and a root on its tray and cards: "Put a finger on the part that holds. Put a finger on the part that takes in water." For one minute you alone lift the class plant out of its pot over a tray, so roots in soil are seen. The six stay with you, one minute each.

#### 8 min. Dated shoot pictures and the evidence arrow

Say strip A is invented for practice: it shows what leaning looks like, not what our plant did. Point along it in date order. Model one arrow: from what we saw (leaning toward the lamp) to what it shows (a plant responds to light). **Ask.** [[s02-P11-2]] "Draw your arrow from what you saw to what it shows." Name no cause. Hear six, 20 seconds each. Look-fors: the arrow starts at something seen and ends at a response.

#### 4 min. Add to the chart

The class dictates: roots hold and take in water; the stem holds leaves up; leaves face the light; a flower helps make seeds (not every plant has one); a fruit keeps seeds safe. Each row gets an evidence arrow. The parts of one plant work together.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." **Ask.** [[s02-X11a]] "Touch the part that takes in water." **Ask.** [[s02-X11b]] "Touch the part that faces the light." **Ask.** [[s02-X11c]] "Touch the picture of the shoot on Thursday." All touch; the six say why, 15 seconds each. Expect: roots; leaves; leaning right. Still straight: error B.

#### 3 min. Hand washing and clean-up

Trays, cards and plant away; wash hands.

### Other rooms

No plant: photographs of a stem and leaves.

### At home

**Swap.** No class plant: a houseplant or a picture. No cards: draw roots, stem and leaves.

**Skip.** The trays and the chart.

- **6 min. Find the parts.** Look at a plant together. Never taste it. Wash hands after.
  **Say.** "Which part holds, and which takes in water?" Expect: the roots do both.
- **8 min. The shoot.** Draw three pictures of a shoot, straight, then leaning toward a lamp on the left. Draw an arrow from the leaning one to "a plant responds to light."
  **Say.** "What did you see, and what does it show?" Expect: it leaned toward the lamp, so a plant responds.
- **6 min. Add a job.** Draw a stem and a leaf and say each job.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X11a]], [[s02-X11b]] and [[s02-X11c]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: share trays; never the arrow or the exit. Longer: name a part and job on the tray.

### Words and supports

Root, stem, leaf, job, responds, I saw ___, so ___. Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error B, day 12 opens with Return and repair: strip A laid in date order and a finger walked along it. Day 12 adds leaves and a second strip.
