# Dated shoot strips (days 11 and 12)

Two strips for the class, each printed one for each child to draw an evidence arrow on. If the class has its own dated photographs of a shoot by a window that really did lean, use those and ignore these. If the class pictures show no lean, say so plainly and use these, and say aloud: "These pictures are invented for practice. They show what leaning looks like, not what our plant did." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Each frame has a day label and a clock picture so the order can be read without words, and a lamp on a stand beside the cup.

## Strip A, day 11: one shoot, one lamp (three frames)

A radish shoot in a clear cup on a windowsill, three leaves and a short stem. The lamp stays on the left of the cup in every frame.
1. **Mon 6:** the shoot stands straight. The clock picture reads morning.
2. **Tue 7:** the shoot has a slight lean toward the lamp.
3. **Wed 8:** the shoot clearly leans toward the lamp, its leaves reaching to the left.

Under the strip, a blank box for the child's arrow: from "what we saw" (the leaning picture) to "what it shows" (the shoot responds to light). The teacher models one arrow first. No cause is named. Spoken: "Point along the strip in date order. What did you see?"

## Strip B, day 12: a changed light (five frames)

A marigold seedling in a cup. A lamp stands on the right of the cup in the first three frames, and moves to the left in the last two.
1. **Mon 13:** the seedling stands straight, lamp on the right.
2. **Wed 15:** the seedling leans right, toward the lamp.
3. **Thu 16:** the lamp is carried to the left; the seedling still leans right.
4. **Fri 17:** the lamp is on the left; the seedling has begun to straighten.
5. **Sat 18:** the seedling leans left, toward the new lamp side.

Under the strip, a box with the frame "I saw ___, so ___." Each child points along the strip in date order, draws one arrow and says the frame. Never say why a plant leans. No frame on either strip is used in an exit.
