# Exit cards, days 10 to 15: teacher key (days 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-exits-10-15.md.

- Day 10 / Item s02-X10a: **Key:** the stem. A wrong touch is shown with help.
- Day 10 / Item s02-X10b: **Key:** no, roots take in water and hold the plant. Wrong: yes is error A.
- Day 11 / Item s02-X11a: **Key:** the roots.
- Day 11 / Item s02-X11b: **Key:** the leaves. A wrong touch is shown with help.
- Day 11 / Item s02-X11c: **Key:** leaning to the right. Wrong: still straight, "plants do not move," is error B.
- Day 12 / Item s02-X12a: **Key:** the joined leaf. A wrong touch is shown with help.
- Day 12 / Item s02-X12b: **Key:** toward the hole. Wrong: straight up is error B; away from the hole has no letter.
- Day 13 / Item s02-X13a: **Key:** squeak, ears, legs. Wrong: squeak, legs, ears is error C; squeak, nose, mouth is shown with help.
- Day 13 / Item s02-X13b: **Key:** thumb down; the ears hear, the legs carry. Wrong: thumb up is error C.
- Day 14 / Item s02-X14a: **Key:** the eyes. Wrong: the fox or the feet has no letter.
- Day 14 / Item s02-X14b: **Key:** the tail. Wrong: the eyes is error C; the mouth has no letter.
- Day 15 / Item s02-X15a: **Key:** fall slowly through the air. Wrong: make a garden look pretty is error D; dig into the soil has no letter.
- Day 15 / Item s02-X15b: **Key:** thumb down; copy the wide flat wing shape, not the picture. Wrong: thumb up is error D.
