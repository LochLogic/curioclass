# Plant-part cards (days 10 to 12)

Printed once, one set in an envelope for each child, and one large set for the teacher. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Each card is one big picture and one big word under it, so a child can match a picture to its word without reading more than the one word. Say aloud that the squash plant on these cards is the one in the day 10 picture book.

## The five part cards (days 10 and 11)

Each card shows the whole squash plant in light gray, with the one part drawn in thick black so it stands out.
1. **roots:** thick roots with thin side roots spreading in soil, the soil cut away. Word: root.
2. **stem:** the long stem rising from the ground to the leaves. Word: stem.
3. **leaves:** three broad leaves, all turned toward a small sun in the corner. Word: leaf.
4. **flower:** one open yellow flower. Word: flower.
5. **fruit:** one squash fruit on the ground, with a small cut-open picture of seeds inside. Word: fruit.

On day 10 each child draws a plant, places the five cards on it and circles the part they think helps it stand. On day 11 the same cards go on the part-and-job chart.

## The root close-up cards (day 11)

Three cards, one set for each group, to look at beside the real stem and leaf trays. No card shows a plant dug out of the ground by a child.
- **Close-up 1:** the thick root of a squash plant with thin side roots, in a cut-away of soil. A drop of water is drawn in the soil beside the thin roots, with an arrow into them.
- **Close-up 2:** a clump of grass with its root mass holding a block of soil together. The roots are drawn thick and many.
- **Close-up 3:** a seedling lifted gently out of a pot, with its roots wrapped round the soil ball. A hand holds the pot.

**Spoken for all three:** "Find the roots. Put a finger on the part that holds. Put a finger on the part that takes in water." The key is in key-cards-plant-parts.md.
