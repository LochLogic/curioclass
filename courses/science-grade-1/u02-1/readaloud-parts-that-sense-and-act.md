# Parts That Sense and Act

A picture read-aloud for day 13. Six pages, one animal in each scene, one part and one job on each. Before you show it, say who made it and what it is: "We made this picture book for our class. The animals in it are pretend, and no animal is in our room today." Show one page at a time and read the words under it. After each page, name the topic and ask for the detail ("What does the hare do with its ears?"). A child may point instead of speaking. Children do not read; the only printed word is the title on page 1.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except the title. The hare is tall-eared with a white tail, the same animal on pages 1 to 5. Page 6 has the fish alone. Each picture shows only the one animal.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A hare sits in a meadow, ears up. A sound line, three curved marks, comes from the edge of the page where a gate swings. A wide title across the top: Parts That Sense and Act.

**Read:** A hare sits in the meadow. A gate clicks. The hare's ears hear it.

**Topic:** ears. **Detail:** they hear.

## Page 2

**Picture:** The same hare running, back legs stretched out behind it and front legs stretched out ahead. Dust puffs at its feet.

**Read:** The hare runs. Its legs carry it away.

**Topic:** legs. **Detail:** they carry it.

## Page 3

**Picture:** The hare stops at the meadow's far side and looks. A patch of clover is drawn ahead of it. Dotted lines run from its eyes to the clover.

**Read:** The hare looks. Its eyes see the clover.

**Topic:** eyes. **Detail:** they see.

## Page 4

**Picture:** A close view of the hare's face. Small curved arrows go into its nostrils from a flower beside it.

**Read:** The hare breathes in. Its nostrils take in air.

**Topic:** nostrils. **Detail:** they take in air.

## Page 5

**Picture:** The hare eats clover. A small stream runs beside it, and the hare bends to drink from it. Its mouth is open.

**Read:** The hare eats and drinks. Its mouth takes in food and water.

**Topic:** mouth. **Detail:** it takes in food and water.

## Page 6

**Picture:** A fish alone in a pond, drawn large. Its body is covered in rows of overlapping scales.

**Read:** A fish swims in a pond. Scales cover its body.

**Topic:** scales. **Detail:** they cover the body.

## For the teacher

The hare's chain is the picture: a gate clicks (what happened), its ears hear (the part that senses), its legs carry it (the part that moves). Say it as a chain and do not explain how. Add the question "Which jobs could help people?" to the question board after page 6. Keep the animal out of the exit: day 13's held-back card is a different animal.
