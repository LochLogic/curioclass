# Animal part cards and chain cards (days 13 and 14)

Six sets in envelopes, one for each group of five, and one large set for the teacher. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Say aloud that no animal is in the room and that the animals on the cards are from the day 13 picture book.

## The six part cards (day 13)

Each card shows its animal in light grey with the one part drawn in thick black.
1. **ears:** the hare's tall ears turned toward a sound line. Word: ears.
2. **legs:** the hare's back legs stretched out, running. Word: legs.
3. **eyes:** the hare's eye, with dotted lines to a patch of clover. Word: eyes.
4. **nostrils:** the hare's nose, with curved arrows going in. Word: nostrils.
5. **mouth:** the hare's open mouth at the clover and the stream. Word: mouth.
6. **scales:** a fish with rows of overlapping scales. Word: scales.

**Sort by job (day 13).** Four heading cards, each a picture with one word: **sense** (an eye and an ear), **move** (a pair of running legs), **take in** (a mouth and an arrow in), **cover** (a scaly fish). The key is in key-cards-animal-parts.md.

## The chain cards (days 13 and 14)

Three cards in a row with arrows between them, one set for each group and one large for the teacher: **what happened** (a blank box and a small ear shape), **the part that senses** (a blank box with an eye and an ear), **the part that moves** (a blank box with a pair of legs). The teacher models the chain on the hare: a gate clicks (what happened), ears (the part that senses), legs (the part that moves). Children lay the chain on the animal side of the chart. On day 14 they lay it on the dog frames.

**Spoken:** "What happened? Which part senses it? Which part moves?" Key for the hare: a gate clicks, ears, legs. Say it as a chain, a picture, with no talk of how. The picture of the hare running and the gate are teaching cases; none is used in an exit.
