# Day 12: Leaves, and a shoot with a moved lamp

## Teacher page

### Before class

Gather only identified, non-toxic tree leaves, with their stems, onto six trays. Note each leaf's tree and the date on a label. Keep the press and its weights to hand. Choose today's six. Key: key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

### Safety and set-up

Never taste a plant part. Use known non-toxic leaves with no sap or mold; an allergic child uses photographs. Only you handle press weights. After class, set up for unit 3's hook: you alone press leaves from the big tree and the seedlings under it, each labeled with place and date, beside the dated seedling photographs. They show where seedlings grew, not their parent. Hand wash: wipes at tables at clean-up; at the next transition, groups wash at the sinks, scrubbing at least 20 seconds.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error B (day 11's E11c): lay strip A in date order and walk a finger along it: "it leaned toward the lamp." Whole class if more than a third made it; otherwise a small group.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read the chart with its evidence arrows aloud (review). **Ask.** [[s02-P12-1]] "Point to the shoot arrow and say what we saw." Hear three.

#### 9 min. Leaf trays

Each group finds, on its tray, where a leaf joins its stem. **Ask.** [[s02-P12-2]] "Put a leaf picture on the light row of the chart." One child from each group sticks one; the rest point from their seats. The six stay with you, one minute each. Say: the stem holds the leaf up and the leaf faces the light, while the roots on the root close-up cards take in water.

#### 11 min. A second shoot strip

Say strip B is invented for practice. A lamp stands on the right for three days, then is carried to the left. Each child points along the strip in date order and draws one evidence arrow. **Ask.** [[s02-P12-3]] "Say I saw ___, so ___." Partners say it in turn; hear six, 20 seconds each. Expect: I saw it lean right, then left, so it follows the light. Name no cause.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." **Ask.** [[s02-X12a]] "Touch the leaf that the plant can use to face the light." Show the box card. **Ask.** [[s02-X12b]] "Touch where the sprout points on Thursday." All touch; the six say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: the joined leaf; toward the hole. Straight up: error B. Another card: shown with help.

#### 3 min. Hand washing and clean-up

Trays and leaves away; hand wipes; sinks at the next transition.

### Other rooms

No leaves: photographs of a leaf joined to its stem.

### At home

**Swap.** No leaf: draw one joined to a stem. No strip: draw five pictures of a seedling, lamp on the right, then the left.

**Skip.** The trays, the chart and the press.

- **6 min. A leaf.** Look at one leaf on its stem. Never taste it. Use a leaf from a plant you know well, or a picture; wash hands after.
  **Say.** "Where does the leaf join the stem?" Expect: at the end.
- **9 min. The strip.** Draw the seedling leaning right, then left after the lamp moves.
  **Say.** "What did you see, and what does it show?" Expect: it leaned toward the lamp, so it responds to light.
- **5 min. Your arrow.** Your child draws an arrow from the leaning picture to what it shows.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X12a]] and [[s02-X12b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: share trays; never the arrow or the exit. Longer: find a second leaf and say its job.

### Words and supports

Leaf, stem, joins, lamp, leaned, I saw ___, so ___. Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error B, day 13 opens with Return and repair: strip B laid in date order and a finger walked along it.
