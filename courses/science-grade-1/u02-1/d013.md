# Day 13: Parts that sense and parts that move

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out six sets of part and chain cards, and the large sets. Choose today's six. Key: key-cards-animal-parts.md. Key: key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No animal is in the room; say so.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error B (day 12's E12b): lay strip B in date order and walk a finger along it: "it leaned toward the moved lamp." Whole class if more than a third made it; otherwise a small group.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read the plant side of the chart aloud (review). **Ask.** [[s02-P13-1]] "Say one need a plant part helps meet." Hear three. Expect: roots, water; leaves, light.

#### 6 min. Read-aloud: Parts That Sense and Act

Say: "We made this book. The animals in it are pretend; no animal is in our room." Show six pages, one animal in each scene, five about the hare and one about a fish: a hare hears a gate click and runs; its eyes see clover; its nostrils take in air; its mouth takes in food and water; a fish's scales cover it. After each page, name the topic and ask for the detail. **Ask.** [[s02-P13-2]] "What do the hare's ears do?" Expect: they hear the gate. Then add to the question board: "Which jobs could help people?"

#### 9 min. Sort the part cards

Each group sorts the six part cards under sense, move, take in and cover. **Ask.** [[s02-P13-3]] "Which cards go under sense?" The six stay with you, one minute each. Expect: ears and eyes sense; legs move; nostrils and mouth take in; scales cover.

#### 6 min. Sensing-to-action arrows

Model the chain on the hare card: a gate clicks (what happened), the ears (the part that senses), the legs (the part that moves). Say it is a picture, with no talk of how. **Ask.** [[s02-P13-4]] "Lay the chain on the hare card." Groups lay it; then each group adds it to the animal side of the chart.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." Read the caption aloud: "A cat hears a toy squeak behind the sofa and walks over to it." Show the three chains. **Ask.** [[s02-X13a]] "Touch the chain that tells the story." **Ask.** [[s02-X13b]] "Pip says the cat's ears carried it to the toy. Is Pip right? Thumb up or thumb down." All answer; the six say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: squeak, ears, legs; thumb down. Squeak, legs, ears, or thumb up: error C. Another chain: shown with help.

#### 2 min. Pack

Cards into envelopes.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six parts on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw an ear, an eye, a leg, a nose, a mouth and a scale.

**Skip.** The groups and the chart.

- **6 min. The story.** Tell it: a hare hears a gate click and runs. Draw the hare.
  **Say.** "What did the ears do?" Expect: they heard the gate.
- **9 min. Sort.** Draw the six parts and sort them into sense, move, take in and cover.
  **Say.** "Which parts sense?" Expect: ears and eyes.
- **6 min. The chain.** Draw three boxes: a gate clicks, the ears, the legs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X13a]] and [[s02-X13b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four pages; never the chain or the exit. Longer: the six give a chain of their own.

### Words and supports

Ears, eyes, legs, nostrils, mouth, scales, sense, move, job. Frame: "The ___ senses; the ___ moves." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error C, day 14 opens with Return and repair: the chain card, "eyes see, legs carry". Day 14 uses a second animal.
