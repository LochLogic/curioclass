# Shape and job cards (day 15)

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Every job is a general human job. Say aloud that the cards are made by us. Say what a copied shape means: we copy the shape that does the job, never a picture of the animal.

## The four examples (teacher cards, shown before groups choose)

Each example is a pair: a part's shape card and a job card, with the word **shape** on one and **job** on the other.
1. **A nut's hard shell and keeping something safe.** The shell is a closed, round, hard cover. The job card shows a small box with something safe inside.
2. **Spreading roots and staying upright.** Wide roots under a tree. The job card shows the paper tower standing on a wide base.
3. **Overlapping feathers and keeping rain out.** A bird's feathers laid in rows like roof tiles. The job card shows rain sliding off a covered doorway.
4. **A hare's wide ear and detecting.** A tall, wide ear turned toward a sound. The job card shows a person listening from behind a wall, and the teacher's paper ear cone, held up and never put to an ear or mouth. Its sound test is for a later unit.

## The match sets (six sets, one for each group of five)

Each set holds **four job cards and eight part cards.** Groups match each job to the part whose shape could do it, and draw one arrow from the shape to the job.

**Job cards.** hold on to a thin pole; push through water; dig into soil; stick to rough cloth.

**Part cards that fit by shape (4):**
- a vine's curling tendril, wrapped round a thin stick (hold on to a thin pole);
- a frog's wide webbed back foot, spread in water (push through water);
- a mole's broad front paw with long flat claws, in soil (dig into soil);
- a seed burr covered in small hooks, caught on a sleeve (stick to rough cloth).

**Part cards that are pretty but do not fit any of these jobs (4):** pink rose petals; yellow daisy petals; a shiny red cherry; striped tulip petals.

**Spoken:** "Which part's shape could do this job? Draw one arrow from its shape to the job." The key is in key-cards-design-jobs.md.

The held-back day 15 exit uses a different job (keep a small paper tool safe in a bag) and a different part (a beetle's hard wing cover), so neither the job nor the wing cover is on these sets.
