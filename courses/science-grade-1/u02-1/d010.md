# Day 10: Which parts could help it stand?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Stand the tower on a low clear table with a taped mark. Cover the fallen tree's root end in the photograph so only standing trunks show. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Only you fan, gently, away from faces. Mind paper edges.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read the unit 1 pattern chart aloud (review); day 9's strips wait until day 62. **Ask.** [[s02-P10-1]] "Point to one pattern and say what happens again and again." Hear three.

#### 7 min. Hook

Show the photograph, trunks standing; say some trees do fall in storms. Fan the tower, three strokes from the mark: it tips. Say the problem, what we want to be different (the tower falls), as in kindergarten (review). Before any roots appear, ask aloud: "How does a tree stay standing in a storm, and can we copy it to keep our tower from tipping?"

**Ask.** "What do you notice?" Scribe each notice with a small picture on the question board, under holding, sensing or copying. 

#### 3 min. Retrieval (unscored)

Say "Invented for practice." Show a potted plant by a window, and a pond edge; need, water, light, food are kindergarten's (review). **Ask.** "Touch the picture where a plant can get water and light." **Ask.** "Say what a land animal needs there." Expect: water, food. Point at the fallen tower. **Ask.** "Say the problem people want to solve." Expect: keep it standing.

#### 8 min. Read-aloud: Which Parts Could Help It Stand?

Say "We made this book; the garden is pretend." Show five pages, one part and one job each: roots, stem, leaves, flower, fruit. After each, name the topic and ask for the detail. **Ask.** [[s02-P10-2]] "What do the roots do?" Expect: hold the plant and take in water. Say "roots take in water and hold", never "eat". After page 5 ask "Which part helps it stand?"

#### 7 min. Parts drawing

Each child draws a plant, places the five cards on it, copies one word label or dictates it, and circles the part they think helps it stand. The six stay with you, one minute each. Start the chart from their words: a picture and a guessed job per row.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." **Ask.** [[s02-X10a]] "Touch the part of the plant that is like the tube." All touch. **Ask.** [[s02-X10b]] "Do roots eat the soil? Touch yes or no." All touch; the six say what roots do, 20 seconds each. Expect: the stem; no, roots take in water and hold. Yes: error A. Another card: shown with help.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Tidy up.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the parts on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tower: a paper tube on a plate. No cards: draw the five parts.

**Skip.** The chart and the board.

- **5 min. Tip it.** Fan the tube gently, away from faces, until it tips.
  **Say.** "What do you notice?" Expect: it tipped; any notice.
- **8 min. Five parts.** Draw roots, stem, leaves, flower and fruit.
  **Say.** "What do the roots do?" Expect: hold the plant and take in water.
- **7 min. My plant.** Your child draws a plant on the student page and circles the part that helps it stand.
- **5 min. Wrong idea.** A girl says roots eat the soil.
  **Say.** "Do roots eat the soil?" Expect: no, they take in water and hold.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X10a]] and [[s02-X10b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three read-aloud pages, never the exit. Longer: children say the problem in their own words.

### Words and supports

Root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit, job, hold. Frame: "The ___ helps by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error A, day 11 opens with Return and repair: the water arrow traced into the root on the chart.
