# Exit cards, days 10 to 15

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a read-aloud or a match set. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each touch card. Each drawing is its own view. Every child touches a card; the teacher hears a rotating six and writes who on the record sheet. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 10

**Item s02-X10a.** A bean plant in a pot, leaning over, drawn beside a straight paper tube standing on its end. Touch cards: leaves; stem; flower. Spoken: "Touch the part of the plant that is like the tube." **Key:** the stem. A wrong touch is shown with help.

**Item s02-X10b.** A girl, Lena, stands at a clear pot of beans, with the soil and the roots showing. A speech mark beside her. Touch cards: yes; no. Spoken: "Lena says the roots eat the soil. Do roots eat the soil? Touch yes or no." **Key:** no, roots take in water and hold the plant. Wrong: yes is error A.

## Day 11

**Item s02-X11a.** A pea shoot in a clear cup on a sill, drawn whole: roots, stem, leaves and a small flower. Touch cards: roots; leaves; flower. Spoken: "Touch the part that takes in water." **Key:** the roots.

**Item s02-X11b.** The same picture. Spoken: "Touch the part that faces the light." **Key:** the leaves. A wrong touch is shown with help.

**Item s02-X11c.** Three pictures of a pea shoot on a sill. Picture 1, Monday morning: the lamp is on the left, the shoot is straight. Picture 2, Monday afternoon: the lamp is now on the right of the cup, the shoot is still straight. Picture 3 is covered. Touch cards: still straight; leaning to the right. Spoken: "The lamp stayed on the right until Thursday. Touch the picture of the shoot on Thursday." **Key:** leaning to the right. Wrong: still straight, "plants do not move," is error B.

## Day 12

**Item s02-X12a.** A vine by a window. One leaf is joined to its stem and tilted toward the window. One leaf has fallen and lies on the sill in the same light by the window. Touch cards: the joined leaf; the fallen leaf. Spoken: "Touch the leaf that fits our light job." **Key:** the joined leaf. A wrong touch is shown with help.

**Item s02-X12b.** A sprout in a cup on a sill. Picture 1: the lamp is on the left, the sprout is straight. Picture 2: the lamp is now on the right. Picture 3 is covered. Touch cards: still straight; leaning right. Spoken: "The lamp stayed on the right for three days. Touch the picture of the sprout after three days." **Key:** leaning right. Wrong: still straight is error B.

## Day 13

**Item s02-X13a.** A lizard on a rock. A shadow passes over it. The lizard runs under a stone. Read this caption aloud: "A shadow passes over. The lizard runs under a stone." Touch cards: eyes; legs; mouth. Spoken: "Touch the part that sees the shadow." **Key:** the eyes. A wrong touch is shown with help.

**Item s02-X13b.** The same card. Spoken: "Touch the part that carries it under the stone." **Key:** the legs. Wrong: the eyes is error C.

## Day 14

**Item s02-X14a.** A duck at a pond's edge. A fox shape appears at the bank. The duck swims away. Touch cards: the fox; the eyes; the feet. Spoken: "Touch the card that goes in the first box of the chain." **Key:** the fox. A wrong touch is shown with help.

**Item s02-X14b.** The same card. Touch cards: eyes; feet; beak. Spoken: "Touch the part that moves the duck away." **Key:** the feet. Wrong: the eyes is error C.

## Day 15

**Item s02-X15a.** A job card: keep a small paper tool safe in a bag. Two part cards: a beetle's hard wing cover, curved like a lid; a flower's blue petals. Touch cards: the wing cover; the petals. Spoken: "Touch the part whose shape could keep the tool safe." **Key:** the wing cover. Wrong: the petals, "they are pretty," is error D.

**Item s02-X15b.** The same cards. Spoken: "Draw one arrow from the part's shape to its job, or say how the shape helps." **Key:** the wing cover covers the tool like a lid. Wrong: "draw a beetle on the bag so it is safe" is error D.
