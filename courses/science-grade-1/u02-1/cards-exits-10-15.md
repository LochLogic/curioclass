# Exit cards, days 10 to 15

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a read-aloud or a match set. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each touch card. Each drawing is its own view. Every child touches a card; the teacher hears a rotating six and writes who on the record sheet. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 10

**Item s02-X10a.** A bean plant standing upright in a pot, drawn beside a straight paper tube standing on its end. Touch cards: leaves; stem; flower. Spoken: "Touch the part of the plant that is like the tube." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

**Item s02-X10b.** A girl, Lena, stands at a clear pot of beans, with the soil and the roots showing. A speech mark beside her. Touch cards: yes; no. Spoken: "Lena says the roots eat the soil. Do roots eat the soil? Touch yes or no." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

## Day 11

**Item s02-X11a.** A pea shoot in a clear cup on a sill, drawn whole: roots, stem, leaves and a small flower. Touch cards: roots; leaves; flower. Spoken: "Touch the part that takes in water." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

**Item s02-X11b.** The same picture. Spoken: "Touch the part that faces the light." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

**Item s02-X11c.** Three pictures of a pea shoot on a sill. Picture 1, Monday morning: the lamp is on the left, the shoot is straight. Picture 2, Monday afternoon: the lamp is now on the right of the cup, the shoot is still straight. Picture 3 is covered. Touch cards: still straight; leaning to the right. Spoken: "The lamp stayed on the right until Thursday. Touch the picture of the shoot on Thursday." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

## Day 12

**Item s02-X12a.** A vine by a window. One leaf is joined to its stem and tilted toward the window. One leaf has fallen and lies on the sill. Touch cards: the joined leaf; the fallen leaf. Spoken: "Touch the leaf that the plant can use to face the light." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

**Item s02-X12b.** A closed box drawn from the side, cut away so the inside shows, with one hole on its left side and a lamp shining outside the hole. Picture 1, Monday: a small straight bean sprout in the box. Picture 2, Thursday: covered. Touch cards: the sprout bending toward the hole; the sprout straight up; the sprout bending away from the hole. Spoken: "Touch where the sprout points on Thursday." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

## Day 13

**Item s02-X13a.** A cat by a sofa; a toy mouse behind the sofa. Read this caption aloud: "A cat hears a toy squeak behind the sofa and walks over to it." Three chains of three boxes each: squeak, ears, legs; squeak, legs, ears; squeak, nose, mouth. Spoken: "Touch the chain that tells the story." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

**Item s02-X13b.** The same card. Spoken: "Pip says the cat's ears carried it to the toy. Is Pip right? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

## Day 14

**Item s02-X14a.** The duck chain card (the fox, the eyes, the feet), each box also cut as its own touch card, beside a fish card: a fish in a pond, a heron's shadow falling on the water, then the fish under a lily pad. Spoken: "Which box of the duck's chain stays the same for the fish? Touch it." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

**Item s02-X14b.** The same two cards, and three touch cards: a fish tail; fish eyes; a fish mouth. Spoken: "Touch the fish's part that moves it under the lily pad." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

## Day 15

**Item s02-X15a.** A part card: a seed with a wide, flat, thin wing on one side. Three job cards: fall slowly through the air; dig into the soil; make a garden look pretty. Spoken: "Touch the job its shape could do." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.

**Item s02-X15b.** The same seed card, and a second card: a paper ball with a picture of the seed glued on it. Spoken: "Pip wants a paper helper that falls slowly, so Pip glued a seed picture onto a paper ball. Will the picture make the ball fall slowly? Thumb up for yes, thumb down for no." The key is in key-cards-exits-10-15.md.
