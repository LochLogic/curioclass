# Day 14: A second animal, and parts that work together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out six sets of dog frames, chain cards and animal part cards. Draw the paper class model on chart paper and hang it beside the chart: two boxes, plant (roots take in water, leaves face the light) and hare (ears sense, legs move). Choose today's six. Key: key-cards-animal-parts.md. Key: key-cards-exits-10-15.md. Key: key-frames-dog-turns.md.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No animal is in the room; say so.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error C (day 13's E13b): point along the chain card: "eyes see, legs carry."

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read the hare's chain (review). **Ask.** [[s02-P14-1]] "Say what happened, the part that senses and the part that moves." Expect: a gate clicks, ears, legs.

#### 10 min. A second animal

Say: "These pictures are invented for practice. A real dog is not in our room." Show the four dated frames and read each caption: the dog lies still; a voice calls from the kitchen; the dog lifts its head and turns its ears; the dog walks toward the kitchen. Show the card "We think the dog heard the voice, because we saw its ears turn" and say it is what we think. Say: "Like kindergarten's I think ___, because I saw ___." **Ask.** [[s02-P14-2]] "Lay the chain cards on the frames." The six stay with you.

#### 7 min. Compare and revise the class model

**Ask.** [[s02-P14-3]] "Which parts did the hare and the dog both use to sense and to move?" Expect: ears, legs. Ask once: "What did the hare and the dog both do?" Expect: each responds. Join the two boxes with the arrow "each part has a job" and write respond under both: the shoot leans, the dog turns. A system: parts that are linked and need each other (kindergarten, review). The class dictates; you scribe.

#### 4 min. Shoot and animal side by side

Lay strip B beside the dog frames. **Ask.** [[s02-P14-4]] "Say one sentence about the shoot and an animal side by side." Scribe one sentence. Expect: each responds to what is around it.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." Show the duck chain and the fish card: a heron's shadow falls on the water, and the fish swims under a lily pad. **Ask.** [[s02-X14a]] "Which box of the duck's chain stays the same for the fish? Touch it." All touch. **Ask.** [[s02-X14b]] "Touch the fish's part that moves it under the lily pad." All touch; the six say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: the eyes; the tail. The eyes move it: error C.

#### 2 min. Pack

Envelopes.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four frames on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No frames: draw four pictures of a dog lying down, hearing a call, lifting its head, walking.

**Skip.** The groups and the model.

- **7 min. Four pictures.** Look at or draw the dog frames.
  **Say.** "What happened, and which part senses it?" Expect: a voice called; the ears.
- **8 min. The chain.** Draw three boxes: a voice calls, the ears, the legs.
  **Say.** "Which part moves the dog?" Expect: the legs.
- **5 min. Both.** Draw a shoot leaning toward a lamp beside the dog.
  **Say.** "How are they alike?" Expect: each responds to what is around it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X14a]] and [[s02-X14b]]: draw three boxes: a fox, the eyes, the feet. Tell your child: a fish sees a heron's shadow and swims under a lily pad. Ask which box stays the same for the fish, then which part moves the fish: tail, eyes or mouth.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: share frames; never the exit. Longer: lay a chain on a frame of your own.

### Words and supports

Ears, legs, eyes, sense, move, responds, we think. Frame: "The ___ senses; the ___ moves." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error C, day 15 opens with Return and repair: the chain card again.
