# Day 14: A second animal, and parts that work together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out six sets of dog frames, chain cards and animal part cards. Hang the paper class model beside the chart: a plant on the left, the hare on the right. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No animal is in the room; say so.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error C (day 13's E13b): point along the chain card: "eyes see, legs carry." Whole class if more than a third made it; otherwise a small group.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read the hare's chain aloud (review). **Ask.** [[s02-P14-1]] "Say what happened, the part that senses and the part that moves." Hear three. Expect: a gate clicks, ears, legs.

#### 10 min. A second animal

Say: "These pictures are invented for practice. A real dog is not in our room." Show the four dated frames and read each caption, which says only what happened: the dog lies still; a voice calls from the kitchen; the dog lifts its head and turns its ears; the dog walks toward the kitchen. Show the card "We think the dog heard the voice" and say it is what we think, since we cannot ask the dog. **Ask.** [[s02-P14-2]] "Lay the chain cards on the frames." Groups lay them. The six stay with you, one minute each.

#### 7 min. Compare and revise the class model

**Ask.** [[s02-P14-3]] "Which parts did the hare and the dog both use to sense and to move?" Expect: ears, legs. Join the plant side and the animal side of the class model with arrows to the need each part helps meet: parts that work together (review). The class dictates the change; you scribe it.

#### 4 min. Shoot and animal side by side

Lay strip B beside the dog frames. **Ask.** [[s02-P14-4]] "Say one sentence about the shoot and an animal side by side." Scribe one sentence. Expect: the shoot leans toward the lamp and the dog turns toward the voice; each responds to what is around it.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." **Ask.** [[s02-X14a]] "Touch the first arrow: fox to eyes, or feet to eyes?" **Ask.** [[s02-X14b]] "Touch the part that moves the duck away." All touch; the six say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: fox to eyes; the feet. The eyes move it: error C. Another card: shown with help.

#### 2 min. Pack

Frames and cards into envelopes.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four frames on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No frames: draw four pictures of a dog lying down, hearing a call, lifting its head, walking.

**Skip.** The groups and the model.

- **7 min. Four pictures.** Look at or draw the dog frames.
  **Say.** "What happened, and which part senses it?" Expect: a voice called; the ears.
- **8 min. The chain.** Draw three boxes: a voice calls, the ears, the legs.
  **Say.** "Which part moves the dog?" Expect: the legs.
- **5 min. Both.** Draw a shoot leaning toward a lamp beside the dog.
  **Say.** "How are they alike?" Expect: each responds to what is around it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X14a]] and [[s02-X14b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: share frames; never the side-by-side or the exit. Longer: lay the chain on a frame of your own.

### Words and supports

Ears, legs, eyes, sense, move, responds, we think. Frame: "The ___ senses; the ___ moves." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error C, day 15 opens with Return and repair: the chain card again. Day 15 matches jobs people need done to parts whose shapes could help.
