# Day 18: Build it, test it, change one thing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep day 16's marks. Give each group a tower, tape and a test record. Pre-cut thick card. Key: key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

### Safety and set-up

A helper adult works beside you from Build to Retest and watches the scissors. Children cut seated; scissors are counted out and in. Safety glasses go on for cutting and for the fan test. Only you fan, gently, away from faces; stop if anything lifts. Keep the test table low and clear.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error D (day 17's exit): point to the colored pattern beside the outward supports: "the supports hold it up; the pattern does not." Whole class if more than a third made it; else a small group.

#### 7 min. Build

Each child builds the sketched support: paper strips taped on a cardboard base. Check each build as it is finished, 10 seconds each, before it is tested.

#### 6 min. Test

Same test as day 16. One group at a time brings its five supports, about a minute a group: stand the tower on each, then fan. **Ask.** [[s02-P18-1]] "Say what happened." The group record circles stood or tipped by support number; read it by number, never by name. Other groups improve their sketches.

#### 4 min. Revise

Each child changes one thing on the support and on the sketch. **Ask.** [[s02-P18-2]] "What one thing will you change on your support?" Expect: wider feet. A stuck-on root picture: error D.

#### 4 min. Retest

Retest only the changed supports (one change, then retest, as in kindergarten, review), tipped ones first, up to 20 at about 12 seconds each, same marks. **Ask.** [[s02-P18-3]] "Your support stood once. Does that mean it always stands?" Expect: no, sometimes, every time (kindergarten words, review).

#### 3 min. Closing explanation

Scribe the class's words. **Ask.** [[s02-P18-4]] "How did the spreading supports help the tower?" Expect, from the record: wide supports that touched the table were harder to tip. Say cardboard feet only model roots, and some trees fall.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." Show the three flag sketch cards. **Ask.** [[s02-X18a]] "Touch the sketch whose strips will hold the flag up." All touch; mark the six. Show the two-frame card. **Ask.** [[s02-X18b]] "The fanned flag tipped. Say what you would change." Partners say it (20 seconds); hear three. Expect: the strips spread out to the table; longer strips to the table, then test again. A stuck-on root picture: error D.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Scissors seated and counted; glasses wiped and bagged (review).

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the group test record on scrap paper. No fan: wave a folded sheet three times from the mark, and say so.

### At home

**Swap.** No fan: an adult waves a folded sheet three times, away from faces. No cardboard: fold thick paper for the base.

**Skip.** The group record and the class explanation.

- **8 min. Build.** Your child builds the sketched support from a base and paper strips taped on. An adult cuts thick card.
- **8 min. Test.** An adult stands the tower on the support and waves the paper three times from the same mark.
  **Say.** "What happened?" Expect: it stood or it tipped, as it was seen.
- **6 min. Change.** Change one thing on the support and the sketch, and test again.
  **Say.** "It stood once. Does that mean it always stands?" Expect: no, test again.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X18a]] and [[s02-X18b]]: draw three flags for your child: two strips spread out to the table, two strips that end above the table, roots drawn on the flag. Your child touches the one that will stand, then says what to change if a flag with short strips tipped.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: test and retest three supports a group; never the exit. Longer: predict which change will help most, then test it.

### Words and supports

Build, test, stood, tipped, change, wider. Frame: "It stood" or "It tipped, so I will ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error D, day 19 opens with Return and repair: the stuck-on picture beside wider supports. Day 19 picks new problems.
