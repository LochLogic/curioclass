# Exit cards, days 16 to 19 and 21

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a read-aloud, a match set or a model sketch. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each touch card. Every child marks a card; the teacher hears a rotating six and writes who on the record sheet. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 16

**Item s02-X16a.** Two paper lighthouse plans, drawn side by side. Plan 1: wide root-like feet that end above the table, a gap drawn under each foot. Plan 2: wide root-like feet that rest on the table. Touch cards: plan 1; plan 2. Spoken: "Touch the plan whose feet can hold it up." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

**Item s02-X16b.** The same two plans. Spoken: "On plan one, draw one foot that can hold it up." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

**Item s02-X16c.** The same two plans. Spoken: "Say why plan one, as it was drawn, tips over." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

## Day 17

**Item s02-X17a.** A sketch of a paper lamppost with two straight legs and a head, no supports. Touch cards: outward supports; one thicker leg; a colored pattern. Spoken: "Touch what to add so a partner can see how it stands." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

**Item s02-X17b.** The same sketch. Spoken: "Say what the shape-to-job arrow should say." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

## Day 18

**Item s02-X18a.** Three sketch cards of a paper flag: two strips spread out to the table; two strips that end above the table; roots drawn on the flag. Spoken: "Touch the sketch whose strips will hold the flag up." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

**Item s02-X18b.** A card with two frames: the flag standing with its strips ending above the table, then the same flag tipped after the fan test. Spoken: "The fanned flag tipped. Say what you would change." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

## Day 19

**Item s02-X19a.** A part card the class has not seen: a camel's long eyelashes, a row of long hairs over the eye that keeps sand out. A kit for each child: a slot card (a card with a window about 5 centimeters across), one paper scrap about 2 centimeters square, and three paper strips about 1 centimeter wide. Spoken: "Lay the strips across the window the way the eyelashes keep sand out." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

**Item s02-X19b.** The same kit, with the child's shape. Spoken: "Say which part you copied and how its shape does the job." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

## Day 21 (unscored)

**Item s02-X21.** A bird grasping a paper berry with a long beak, and beside it a proposed paper beak whose two strips cannot meet. Touch cards: strips that close around the berry; a picture of a bird stuck on the strips; the strips colored red. Spoken: "Touch the change that could help it pick up a paper berry." The key is in key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.
