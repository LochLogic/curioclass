# Exit cards, days 16 to 19 and 21

Keep these out of sight until each day's exit. None is used in a hook, a read-aloud, a match set or a model sketch. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each touch card. Every child marks a card; the teacher hears a rotating six and writes who on the record sheet. A wrong touch with no letter is shown with help.

## Day 16

**Item s02-X16a.** Two paper lighthouse plans, drawn side by side. Plan 1: wide root-like feet that end above the table, a gap drawn under each foot. Plan 2: wide root-like feet that rest on the table. Touch cards: plan 1; plan 2. Spoken: "Touch the plan whose feet can hold it up." **Key:** plan 2, the feet on the table. A wrong touch is shown with help.

**Item s02-X16b.** The same two plans. Spoken: "Draw one more foot and say what it does." **Key:** a foot reaching the table that holds the lighthouse up. Wrong: draws roots on the lighthouse wall, "now it looks like a tree" (D).

## Day 17

**Item s02-X17a.** A sketch of a paper lamppost with two straight legs and a head, no supports. Touch cards: outward supports; a coloured pattern; a label that says strong. Spoken: "Touch what to add so a partner can see how it stands." **Key:** outward supports. Wrong: the coloured pattern (D).

**Item s02-X17b.** The same sketch. Spoken: "Say what the shape-to-job arrow should say." **Key:** wide feet hold it up. Wrong: "it looks like a tree so it stands" (D).

## Day 18

**Item s02-X18a.** A paper signpost that tipped in its test, drawn lying on its side. Touch cards: wider spreading supports; a taller post; a stuck-on picture of roots. Spoken: "Touch the change you would try." **Key:** wider spreading supports. Wrong: the stuck-on picture (D).

**Item s02-X18b.** The same cards. Spoken: "Say what you would draw on its sketch." **Key:** wider supports touching the table, said as a guess about what will happen, not a result. A wrong answer is shown with help.

## Day 19

**Item s02-X19a.** A desk slot, drawn with scraps of paper to keep out and a wavy line for air to let through. Touch cards: a row of blunt bumps set close together over the slot; a solid sheet over the slot; a picture of thorns glued flat over the open slot. Spoken: "Touch the sketch that keeps the scraps out and lets air through." **Key:** the row of bumps. Wrong: the glued picture (D). The solid sheet is shown with help, no letter.

**Item s02-X19b.** The same cards. Spoken: "Say how its shape does the job." **Key:** the bumps are close enough that scraps cannot pass and air can. Wrong: "the thorn picture makes it work" (D).

## Day 21 (unscored)

**Item s02-X21.** A bird grasping a paper berry with a long beak, and beside it a proposed paper beak whose two strips cannot meet. Touch cards: strips that close around the berry; a picture of a bird stuck on the strips; a longer strip. Spoken: "Touch the change that could help it pick up a paper berry." **Key:** strips that close around the berry. Wrong: a picture of a bird stuck on the strips (D). This case is not scored and is not used on any earlier day.
