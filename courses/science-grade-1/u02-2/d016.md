# Day 16: Sketch a support for the tower

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a tower mark and a fan mark on a low table. Cover the chart's sketch box with a note. Choose six.

### Safety and set-up

Only you fan, gently, away from faces; stop if anything lifts. Handle edges carefully; nothing is cut.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error D (day 15's exit): lay the wing cover card beside the petals on the design chart and say: "its shape does the job." Whole class if more than a third made it; otherwise a small group.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Point along the chart: problem, try an idea, test it. **Ask.** [[s02-P16-1]] "Say our problem." Hear three.

#### 5 min. The test we will use

Fan the plain tower from the fan mark, three gentle strokes. It should tip; if it stands, move the mark one tape-length nearer and fan again. The class says: same marks, same three strokes, stood or tipped. One stand is not every time.

#### 4 min. Model a sketch

Lift the note: problem, sketch, build, test. A sketch is a plan drawing: the thing, the part we add, where it touches, one arrow from shape to job. From the roots row of the part-and-job chart, sketch a tree with spreading roots, an arrow to "holds it up", and a dictated label. **Ask.** [[s02-P16-2]] "Where do the roots touch?" Expect: the ground.

#### 12 min. Sketch

Each child holds up base pieces to choose a wide shape, then sketches on the paper sketch sheet the tower with a root-like support and one arrow from shape to job. **Ask.** [[s02-P16-3]] "How could a wide shape help it stand?" Hear six, 2 minutes each, and write who on the record sheet; scribe any label a child says. Expect: it touches the table in more places, so it is harder to tip. Roots drawn on the tower's wall, "it looks like a tree": error D.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." Show the two lighthouse plans. **Ask.** [[s02-X16a]] "Touch the plan whose feet can hold it up." **Ask.** [[s02-X16b]] "Draw one more foot so the lighthouse can stand." All touch or draw; the six show it, 30 seconds each. Expect: plan two; a foot that reaches the table. Roots drawn on the lighthouse wall, "now it looks like a tree": error D.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Base pieces into envelopes, sketches into the folder for day 17.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the lighthouse plans on scrap paper. No fan: wave a folded sheet three times, and say so.

### At home

**Swap.** No fan: an adult waves a folded sheet of paper three times, away from faces. No cardboard: draw four base shapes on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the chart and the six heard.

- **6 min. The test.** An adult stands a paper tower on a mark and waves the paper three times. It tips. Say: same place, three waves, stood or tipped.
- **4 min. A sketch.** Draw a tree with wide roots and an arrow from the roots to "holds it up".
  **Say.** "Where do the roots touch?" Expect: the ground.
- **12 min. Sketch.** Your child sketches the tower with a root-like support and one arrow from the shape to the job.
  **Say.** "How could a wide shape help it stand?" Expect: it touches the table in more places, so it is harder to tip.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X16a]] and [[s02-X16b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: no group sharing; never the exit. Longer: sketch a second support.

### Words and supports

Sketch, shape, job, wide, touch, stood, tipped. Frame: "This shape ___, so ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error D, day 17 opens with Return and repair: the root picture on the wall beside the foot that touches the table. Day 17 pairs swap sketches, and a partner says how the shape does its job.
