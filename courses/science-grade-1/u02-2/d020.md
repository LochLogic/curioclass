# Day 20: Show what we know and make a cover

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set six tables of five with tape, and one marker table at the teacher. Open the sealed problem card. Check the shoot pair or drawn fallback (sheets-day20.md). Keep day 19's chart up, no model answer.

### Safety and set-up

A helper adult watches tools in Sketch, make, check; no help with answers. Children cut seated; scissors are counted out and in; glasses on for cutting. Pieces are soft and blunt, and nothing is worn. The marker slides slowly, stops on contact, with an adult near. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 2 min. Launch

Say: "These pages are invented for practice. I will not help with the answers." Read the problem card: a small paper vase stands on a museum table; a visitor's marker may slide along the table; the vase must stay untouched. Make a cover that stands over the vase without resting on it, leaves a gap above it and has one open side so a visitor can see in. Paper, cardboard and tape only. Show the three criteria pictures.

#### 14 min. Evidence account and design

Row [[s02-E20a]]. Read seven prompts aloud, 1 to 2 minutes each; children mark the evidence page. Mark the part that takes in water; the part that holds leaves up; the leaves that face the light. Draw one arrow from where the light moved to the shoot's later bend. Mark the part of the tortoise that covers its body. Draw one arrow from the movement nearby to the tortoise pulling in. Then say or draw which outside part to copy, by the job it does. Captions say what is seen, never the job. Note error A (roots eat the soil), error B (a plant cannot move or respond) and error C (the eyes move the legs). Nothing is scored or fed back.

#### 12 min. Sketch, make, check

Row [[s02-E20b]]. Each child, alone or in a pair with each contribution named on the page, sketches the cover on the cover page and makes it. The marker table opens at minute 4: each child slides the marker once from the taped line, 15 seconds each, 30 children in 8 minutes. Circle on the page: touched the vase, gap above, one open side. Record both on the record sheet. Expect an arch that stands clear. A flat cover on the vase fails the gap; a tortoise picture used only as decoration is error D.

#### 2 min. Collect and clean up

Pages and covers into folders, scissors counted, glasses wiped. Give no feedback.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pages on scrap paper. One projector: show one picture at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: draw four boxes, roots in water, leaves on a stem, a shoot before and after a lamp moved, and a tortoise before and after something moved nearby.

**Skip.** The marker table, the record sheet and the pairs.

- **26 min. The assessment.** Read each prompt aloud and do not help. Your child marks the four pictures and draws the two arrows. Then your child draws a small vase and makes a paper cover that stands over it with one side open. Slide a card slowly to it.
  **Say.** "How does the shape of your cover do its job?" Expect: it stands over the vase, keeps the card off, and the open side lets someone see in.
- **6 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none; nothing is added to a check.

### Words and supports

Cover, gap, open side, arch, copy, arrow. Read every prompt aloud; enlarged pages, a scribe, pointing and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 returns each page and cover with picture-cue feedback and hears each child's account. Error A, error B, error C and error D are repaired by letter.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
