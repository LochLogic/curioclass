# Nature-shape picture cards (day 17)

Six cards for each pair, in an envelope, and one large set for the teacher. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. The teacher says aloud that these are drawings and that no plant or animal is in the room. A partner uses them to say "this shape ___, so ___".

1. **spreading roots:** a plant with roots running wide under the soil, arrows from the root tips down and out. Word: roots. Shape: wide and spreading. Job: hold the plant up.
2. **hard shell:** a nut with its hard curved shell, one half cut away to show the seed inside. Word: shell. Shape: curved and hard. Job: keeps the seed safe.
3. **overlapping feathers:** a bird's wing with feathers laid over one another like roof tiles, and drops running off. Word: feathers. Shape: laid over one another. Job: keep rain out.
4. **wide ear:** a hare's tall wide ear turned toward a sound line. Word: ear. Shape: wide and open. Job: catches sound.
5. **wide base:** a young tree with a wide trunk flaring at the bottom, drawn beside a thin straight stick of the same height. Word: base. Shape: wide at the bottom. Job: stands steady.
6. **pine cone:** a pine cone with its scales laid over one another, a seed under one scale. Word: scales. Shape: laid over one another. Job: keep the seeds covered.

Shape and job cards, for each pair: two blank cards with a space to write or point, headed **shape** and **job**, and a small arrow card. Cards 1 to 4 were seen on day 15 or before; cards 5 and 6 are new here and are not used in any exit. None of the six is a lighthouse, lamppost, signpost, desk slot or bird beak.
