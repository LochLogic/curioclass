# Day 19: A new problem, a copied part

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the class chart, the part-and-job chart and the design chart. Give each group its cards. Keep day 18's records to hand. A second adult watches the scissors.

### Safety and set-up

Children cut seated; scissors are counted out and in; glasses on for cutting. Pieces are soft and blunt, with no sharp spikes, and nothing is worn on a head or body.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error D (day 18's exit): lay the stuck-on picture beside wider supports: "the wide shape holds it up; the picture does not." Whole class if more than a third made it.

#### 4 min. The class chart

Fill the chart's top row from day 18's group records: the tower, the part we copied (roots), the shape we tested (wide, touching the table). Fill two more rows from the part-and-job chart, the last box open. **Ask.** [[s02-P19-1]] "How did the shape we copied do its job?"

#### 4 min. Choose a problem

Say the problems are invented for practice. Read the three cards aloud: keep a paper egg from being bumped; keep paper rain off a paper garden bed, with a way in for a hand; tell a paper cart a wall is near. **Ask.** [[s02-P19-2]] "What must your shape do?" Each child picks one card; note the choices on the class list.

#### 14 min. Sketch and make

Each child sketches the problem, picks one of the eight part-and-job cards, and makes a soft blunt shape of paper and card, seated. Each then checks the shape against the problem card by pointing to the part that does the job. Hear six at their places, 1 minute each; write who on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Say it

Pairs tell each other the part they copied. **Ask.** [[s02-P19-3]] "Why did you copy that part?" Hear six, 30 seconds each. Expect: its shape does the job. "It is pretty," or "it looks like the animal": error D.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." Show the desk slot cards. **Ask.** [[s02-X19a]] "Touch the sketch that keeps the scraps out and lets air through." **Ask.** [[s02-X19b]] "Say how its shape does the job." All touch; the six say it, 30 seconds each. Expect: the row of bumps, close enough that scraps cannot pass and air can. The glued thorn picture: error D. The solid sheet: shown with help.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Scissors counted in, glasses wiped and bagged, shapes into folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw one problem and three parts. No scissors: tear paper; if cutting, blunt child scissors, seated, with an adult watching.

**Skip.** The chart, the groups and the six heard.

- **4 min. Choose.** Draw a paper egg that must not be bumped.
  **Say.** "What must your shape do?" Expect: keep the egg from being bumped.
- **4 min. Part cards.** Draw three parts: a nut shell, feathers and a hare's ear. Say the job of each shape.
- **14 min. Make.** Your child picks a part (a nut shell, feathers, a hare's ear) and makes a soft blunt shape of paper and card for the problem.
  **Say.** "Why did you copy that part?" Expect: its shape does the job.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X19a]] and [[s02-X19b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one problem and no pair talk; never the exit. Longer: make a second shape for a second problem.

### Words and supports

Problem, copy, part, protect, keep out, detect, soft, blunt. Frame: "I copied ___ because its shape ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 20 reteaches nothing, so error D waits for day 21: Return and repair with the working shape, point and say its job. Day 20 is an observation day with a new problem and no new teaching.
