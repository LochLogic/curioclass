# Day 17: Can a partner say how your shape works?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put out 15 envelopes of nature-shape cards, one for each pair, and hang the design chart and the design cycle chart. Hand back the sketches. Choose three pairs to hear. Key: key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and pencils only. Pairs sit side by side. Clean-up is 2 minutes inside the day.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error D (day 16's exit): point to the foot that touches the table beside the roots drawn on the lighthouse wall: "the foot holds it up; a root picture does not." Whole class if more than a third made it; otherwise a small group.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Say the cards are drawings and no plant or animal is in the room. Show the roots card and say its shape and job, with the children's yes. Then show the wide base card. **Ask.** [[s02-P17-1]] "How does the shape of the tree base do its job?" Hear three.

#### 14 min. A partner explains

Pairs swap sketches. The partner points to the shape on the sketch and says "this shape ___, so ___", using the cards. **Ask.** [[s02-P17-2]] "Point to your partner's shape. How does it do its job?" If the partner cannot say it, the author redraws or relabels, and a label may be dictated. Then swap roles. Hear three pairs, 4 minutes each, and write who on the record sheet. Expect: it spreads out and touches the table, so it stands. "It looks like a tree, so it stands": error D.

#### 5 min. Class share

Two authors who say yes show their sketches; the class says "this shape ___, so ___". Draw the arrow again on the board. **Ask.** [[s02-P17-3]] "Which way does the arrow point?" Expect: from the shape to the job. Say it reads like day 11's evidence arrow: I saw ___, so ___.

#### 3 min. Choose

Each child circles the one part of their sketch they will build on day 18.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "Invented." Show the lamppost sketch. **Ask.** [[s02-X17a]] "Touch what to add so a partner can see how it stands." **Ask.** [[s02-X17b]] "Say what the shape-to-job arrow should say." All touch; mark the six. Partners say it; hear three, 30 seconds each. Expect: outward supports; wide feet hold it up. The colored pattern, or "it looks like a tree so it stands": error D.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Sketches into the folder for day 18, cards into envelopes.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six cards on scrap paper and the lamppost on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a tree with a wide base beside a thin stick.

**Skip.** The pairs, the class share and the six heard.

- **6 min. Look at the cards.** Look at the wide base card, or your drawing of it.
  **Say.** "How does the shape of the base do its job?" Expect: it is wide at the bottom, so it stands.
- **14 min. Explain the sketch.** Your child points to the shape on their sketch and says "this shape ___, so ___". You are the partner. If you cannot say it, your child redraws or relabels.
  **Say.** "Show me the shape. How does it do its job?" Expect: it spreads out and touches the table, so it stands.
- **3 min. Choose.** Your child circles the part to build.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X17a]] and [[s02-X17b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one explanation each way and no class share; never the exit. Longer: a second sketch for the same support, drawn from another side.

### Words and supports

Shape, job, partner, point, arrow, wide, spread. Frame: "This shape ___, so ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error D, day 18 opens with Return and repair: the colored pattern beside the outward supports. Day 18 builds the circled support from card and paper and tests it with three gentle strokes.
