# Day 19: problem cards and part-and-job cards

Three problem cards for each group of five and one large set for the teacher, then the part-and-job cards. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Say aloud that each problem is invented for practice and that nothing here is worn.

## The three problem cards

1. **Protect.** A paper egg sits on a card table. A paper block may bump it. Picture: the egg, a small arrow from the block. Must do: keep the paper egg from being bumped. The shape must be soft and blunt.
2. **Keep out.** A paper garden bed, drawn from above. Paper rain, drawn as small blue marks, falls toward it. Must do: keep paper rain off the bed, and leave a way for the gardener's hand to reach in.
3. **Detect.** A paper cart rolls on a card road toward a wall. Must do: tell the cart a wall is near, before it touches. The paper cart cannot be pushed hard.

The teacher notes each child's choice on the class list. No child is asked to be the example in a picture.

## The part-and-job cards (eight, for each group)

1. **nut shell:** curved and hard, keeps a seed safe.
2. **spreading roots:** wide and spreading, hold a plant up.
3. **feathers:** laid over one another, keep rain out.
4. **hare ear:** wide and open, catches sound.
5. **pine cone scales:** laid over one another, keep seeds covered.
6. **insect feelers:** thin and long, touch what is near.
7. **beetle wing cover:** curved like a lid, covers the wings.
8. **fish scales:** overlapping and smooth, cover the body.

Each card shows the picture, the part word and one line of shape and job. Four fit the three problems by shape (shell and wing cover protect; feathers and scales keep out; feelers detect), and the others are good for other jobs. Sketches may combine two. Cards 1 to 4 were seen on day 15 or before; none is a thorn, a row of bumps, a lighthouse, a lamppost, a signpost or a bird's beak.
