# Day 21: Tell how your cover works

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fix one or two cue cards to each child's page, chosen from day 20's record. Set six places, each with a marker, tape, the strips and the chain cards. Key: key-cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

### Safety and set-up

A helper adult works beside you through the group turns. Scissors serve only the cover adjustment: seated, counted, glasses on. Nothing is worn. The marker slides slowly, stops on contact, with an adult near. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 2 min. Launch

Say "Invented." Hand back day 20's assessment, each page and cover with its cues. A cue points at the next look, never at the answer.

#### 5 min. Read feedback and revise

Read the shared prompts aloud: "Point to the part that does the job." "Show how your cover keeps the marker off." "Show where a visitor can see in." Each child revises the page and adjusts the cover. Slide the marker once more for a changed cover, up to ten, 15 seconds each.

#### 18 min. Group turns

The two end rows. Six groups of five, three minutes each, about 36 seconds a child. Read the roots, stem, shoot arrow and tortoise arrow from each child's evidence page, and the marker, gap and open side from the circles on the day 20 cover page, before the turn. Then ask two things only. **Ask.** [[s02-E21a]] "Why did you copy the part you chose?" **Ask.** [[s02-E21b]] "Show how your shape keeps the vase safe and lets a visitor see." Write the spoken reason on the child's sheet and mark the record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Waiting children match the dated shoot strips and chain cards to the part-and-job chart and draw a reason; the helper moves round. Expect the shell's protection joined to the cover's shape. "The tortoise picture makes it work": error D.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Up to four groups work at once from the charts, a minute with each, 4 minutes shared; others use their own cue. Error D from day 19's exit: point to the working shape and say its job. Error A: trace the water arrow into the root. Error B: lay the shoot strips in date order and walk a finger along them. Error C: point along the chain card, "eyes see, legs carry."

#### 3 min. Exit

Unscored. Say "Invented." Show the bird and the two strips that cannot meet. **Ask.** [[s02-X21]] "Touch the change that could help it pick up a paper berry." All touch; the six say it, 20 seconds each. Expect: strips that close around the berry. A bird picture stuck on the strips: error D.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect pages and covers once, for scoring; scissors counted in, glasses wiped and bagged.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cue cards on scrap paper. One projector: show one picture at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say each cue aloud and point.

**Skip.** The group turns, the record sheet and the groups by letter.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Your child points to the part they copied on their page, to the cover's shape and to its open side, and draws the arrow. Do not help.
  **Say.** "Show me how your cover works." Expect: the arch stands over the vase and keeps the marker off, and the open side lets someone see in.
- **10 min. Look back.** Read the cues together and fix the cover once.
- **5 min. Bird and beak.** [[s02-X21]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none; nothing is added to a check.

### Words and supports

Cover, gap, open side, arch, point, copy. Read every cue and prompt aloud; enlarged pages, a scribe, pointing and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 closes unit 2; scoring follows after class from the first try and the revision. Day 22 opens unit 3.
