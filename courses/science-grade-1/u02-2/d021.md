# Day 21: Tell how your cover works

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fix one or two cue cards to each child's page, chosen from day 20's record. Set six places, each with a marker, tape, the strips and the chain cards. Ask a helper adult to move round.

### Safety and set-up

Scissors serve only the cover adjustment: seated, counted, glasses on. Nothing is worn. The marker slides slowly, stops on contact, with an adult near. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 2 min. Launch

Say the problem is invented for practice. Hand back day 20's assessment, each page and cover with its cues. A cue points at the next look, never at the answer.

#### 5 min. Read feedback and revise

Read the shared prompts aloud: "Point to the part that does the job." "Show how your cover keeps the marker off." "Show where a visitor can see in." Each child revises the page and adjusts the cover. Slide the marker once more for a changed cover, up to ten, 15 seconds each.

#### 18 min. Group turns

Rows [[s02-E21a]] and [[s02-E21b]]. Six groups of five, three minutes each, about 36 seconds a child. Each child points through the page and cover in the prepared order: the part I copied, the shape, the open side, the arrow. Write the spoken reason on the child's sheet and mark the record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Waiting children match the dated shoot strips and chain cards to the part-and-job chart and draw a reason; the helper moves round. Expect the shell's protection joined to the arch, and the marker, gap and open side read from the cover. "The tortoise picture makes it work": error D.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Up to four groups work at once from the charts, a minute with each, 4 minutes shared; others use their own cue. Error D from day 19's exit: point to the working shape and say its job. Error A: trace the water arrow into the root. Error B: lay the shoot strips in date order and walk a finger along them. Error C: point along the chain card, "eyes see, legs carry."

#### 3 min. Exit

Unscored. Say "Invented." Show the bird and the two strips that cannot meet. **Ask.** [[s02-X21]] "Touch the change that could help it pick up a paper berry." All touch; the six say it, 30 seconds each. Expect: strips that close around the berry. A bird picture stuck on the strips: error D.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect pages and covers once, for scoring; scissors counted in, glasses wiped and bagged.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cue cards on scrap paper. One projector: show one picture at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No cue cards: say each cue aloud and point.

**Skip.** The group turns, the record sheet and the groups by letter.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Your child points to the part they copied on their page, to the cover's shape and to its open side, and draws the arrow. Do not help.
  **Say.** "Show me how your cover works." Expect: the arch stands over the vase and keeps the marker off, and the open side lets someone see in.
- **10 min. Look back.** Read the cues together and fix the cover once.
- **5 min. Bird and beak.** [[s02-X21]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; keep every part of the check. Longer: none; nothing is added to a check.

### Words and supports

Cover, gap, open side, arch, point, copy. Read every cue and prompt aloud; enlarged pages, a scribe, pointing and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 closes unit 2. The record sheet holds each child's account, and scoring comes after today from the first try and the revision. Day 22 opens unit 3 with the seedling puzzle, comparing parent and young plant leaves, and retrieves the parts chart.
