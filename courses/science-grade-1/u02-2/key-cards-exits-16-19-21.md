# Exit cards, days 16 to 19 and 21: teacher key (days 16, 17, 18, 19, 21)

Not printed for children. Goes with cards-exits-16-19-21.md.

- Day 16 / Item s02-X16a: **Key:** plan 2, the feet on the table. A wrong touch is shown with help.
- Day 16 / Item s02-X16b: **Key:** a foot reaching the table that holds the lighthouse up. Wrong: draws roots on the lighthouse wall, "now it looks like a tree" (D).
- Day 16 / Item s02-X16c: **Key:** its feet end above the table, so they cannot hold it up. Wrong: "it does not look like a tree" (D); "it is too tall" has no letter.
- Day 17 / Item s02-X17a: **Key:** outward supports. Wrong: the colored pattern (D); one thicker leg (shown with help).
- Day 17 / Item s02-X17b: **Key:** wide feet hold it up. Wrong: "it looks like a tree so it stands" (D).
- Day 18 / Item s02-X18a: **Key:** the strips spread out to the table. Wrong: the strips that end above the table (shown with help); the roots drawn on the flag (D).
- Day 18 / Item s02-X18b: **Key:** longer or wider strips that reach the table, then test again. Wrong: a stuck-on picture of roots is error D. Any other answer is shown with help.
- Day 19 / Item s02-X19a: **Key:** strips in a row across the window, like eyelashes, with gaps smaller than the scrap and the window still open. The teacher looks at six now and checks the rest after class. Wrong: eyelashes drawn on the card instead (D). A solid cover is shown with help, no letter.
- Day 19 / Item s02-X19b: **Key:** the camel's eyelashes; the gaps are small enough that the scrap cannot pass and open enough that air can. Wrong: "it looks like eyelashes, so it works" (D).
- Day 21 (unscored) / Item s02-X21: **Key:** strips that close around the berry. Wrong: a picture of a bird stuck on the strips (D); the strips colored red (shown with help). This case is not scored and is not used on any earlier day.
