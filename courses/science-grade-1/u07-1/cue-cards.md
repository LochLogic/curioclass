# Feedback picture-cue cards (day 61)

The written feedback on each day 60 sheet is a small picture cue fixed to it after class. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one word under each. The teacher reads the three most common comments aloud on day 61.

1. A pointing hand beside a drawn young animal shape: point to what the young does.
2. A pointing hand beside a drawn adult animal shape: point to what the adult does.
3. A small page and a small picture side by side: say the words or the picture.
4. A question mark beside a sound mark: a mark is a picture of a sound.
5. A grown plant shape, a seed and a young plant shape in a row: made, then grew.

A cue names a thing to fix and never gives a score.
