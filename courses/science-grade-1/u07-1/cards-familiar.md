# Familiar robin and deer evidence cards (day 60, waiting children)

Say aloud: "These are cards from our own days." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. While a group takes its turn, other children place the cards on the robin and deer chains on their own, unscored and without help, then draw a reason for one card.

1. Young robins with open mouths (the young does).
2. The adult robin putting food in a mouth (the adult does).
3. The fawn lying still in grass (the young does).
4. The adult deer standing over the fawn (the adult does).
5. A young robin with food (stays alive).
6. The fawn standing (stays alive).

The teacher notes which place each card went in and which of the words young, adult and stays alive the child used. No result is written on any card.
