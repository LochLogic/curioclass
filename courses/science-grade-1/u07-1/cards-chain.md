# Signal and response picture cards (day 59)

Large pre-cut cards, one set for each group of five, kept in an envelope. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. Say first: "These cards are from our own robin and deer pages."

## Chain cards

1. Robin, the young does: young robins with open mouths and call marks. Word: cheeps.
2. Robin, the adult does: the adult putting food into a mouth. Word: feeds.
3. Robin, so the young stays alive: a young robin with food. Word: stays alive.
4. Deer, the young does: the fawn lying still in grass. Word: hides.
5. Deer, the adult does: the adult standing over the fawn. Word: feeds milk.
6. Deer, so the young stays alive: the fawn standing. Word: stays alive.

Groups lay the robin chain, then the deer chain beside it: young does, adult does, stays alive. Pip's cheeping claim is tested against the two chains.

## Sort cards (the repair for error C)

Six cards from the robin and deer packets, sorted into "we can see it" and "the words tell it": open mouths (see), cheeping (words), food in the beak (see), the fawn lying in grass (see), the fawn is quiet (words), the adult comes back later (words). No result is written on any card.
