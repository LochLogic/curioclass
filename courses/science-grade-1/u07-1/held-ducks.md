# Day 60 duck case (held back, invented for practice)

Printed "invented for practice" on the text and on every frame, and said aloud. Used only on day 60. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. The adult duck is labeled parent and the small ducks young. The text is read once; the pages are handed out; no meaning is given for either sequence.

## Words, read aloud once

These ducklings hatched from eggs laid by this adult duck. The adult calls to keep the ducklings together, and they answer. When the adult gives a danger call, the ducklings usually stop moving. Staying still can help keep them safe from an animal hunting them.

## Sequence 1, three frames (calls and answers)

1. In tall plants, the adult duck with a call symbol (one arc mark) by its beak. The young are not in view.
2. The adult at the edge of the frame, its arc mark still by its beak. The young, hidden among the plants, each with two small marks by its beak.
3. The adult and the young together, side by side, no call symbols.

## Sequence 2, three frames (a danger episode)

1. The adult duck with a danger call symbol (a jagged mark) by its beak. The young swim nearby.
2. The young are beside the plants, not moving. The jagged mark has gone.
3. A hunting animal, far off at the edge of the picture, walks past. The young are still beside the plants.

## Call symbol convention

Say aloud, before the sequences are handed out: "A mark by a beak is a picture of a call. A picture of a call is not the sound." Do not say what either sequence means. These are two pictures of behavior the words report, not two separate watchings that prove it.

## Keys (teacher only)

The words report that both adult and young call, that the young stop moving at the danger call, and that staying still can keep them safe. The pictures show the calling marks, the reuniting, and the young still beside the plants while the hunter passes. Only the words say why. The adult calls and the young answer or stay still, so each acts and each can help the young stay alive.
