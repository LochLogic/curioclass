# Day 59: The robin and the deer, side by side

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the signal and response picture cards for six groups. Set out Pip, the fawn page and the class model. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work on the rug and at tables. Say first: "These cards are from our own robin and deer pages." Aisles clear; replace damaged cards. No live animals. Any child may pass. Two clean-up minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Open

Pip says, "Only the grown-up helps the babies stay alive." Children point on the robin and deer rows to a young action. If many missed day 57's exit (error A), name the cheep and the lying still and trace each to staying alive.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 58's exit (error C), sort the six sort cards into "we can see it" and "the words tell it": open mouths, cheeping, food in the beak, the fawn lying in grass, the fawn is quiet, the adult comes back later. Children place them; the class says each.

#### 5 min. Robin cards

Groups of five lay the robin chain: the young does, the adult does, stays alive. Say a cheep is a signal, as in unit 6, a sound that goes across; it is not an agreed message.

#### 5 min. Deer cards

Groups lay the deer chain beside the robin chain. Check with the fawn page.

#### 5 min. Same and different

Pip says, "All young animals call for food." Children point to what is the same (the young acts, the adult comes, the young stays alive) and to one thing that is different (cheeps and hides).

#### 5 min. Revise the model

The class dictates; you scribe: the young and the adult both act, their actions can help the young stay alive, and different animals do different things. Fill the third box.

#### 4 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Read the penguin and alligator words; show the cards. One minute, everyone touches: **Ask.** [[s07-X59a]] "Touch what the chick does." **Ask.** [[s07-X59b]] "Touch what the adult penguin does." **Ask.** [[s07-X59c]] "Do the penguin and the alligators do exactly the same things? Touch yes or no." Then six children, 30 seconds each, marked heard, dictate [[s07-P59a]] about the robin: "The young ___, the adult ___, this helps because ___." Scribe it. "Yes" is error B.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Card sets counted back into envelopes.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the picture cards large by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the robin and deer chains on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and Pip.

- **10 min. Two chains.** Your child draws the robin: the young cheeps, the adult feeds, the young stays alive. Then the deer: the fawn hides, the adult feeds milk, the fawn stays alive.
  **Say.** "What is the same? What is different?" Expect: the young acts, the adult comes, the young stays alive; cheeping and hiding differ.
- **10 min. Your words.** Your child finishes: "The young ___, the adult ___, this helps because ___." Write it down.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X59a]], [[s07-X59b]] and [[s07-X59c]], asked aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: lay the robin chain, then the deer, and skip Pip's second claim. Never cut the model or the exit. Longer: a child names a third difference.

### Words and supports

Same, different, signal, helps. Frame: "The young ___, the adult ___, this helps because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Errors A and B go to day 61's tables. Day 60 is a check on new duck and sunflower pages: children may pass, and it is called a check.
