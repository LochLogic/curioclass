# Fawn picture sequence (day 58)

Three frames, no words, one packet for each group of five. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. The same deer as the fawn page, drawn from a different side so the frames are not copies of the page pictures. Say first: "These pictures were drawn for our class."

1. **Frame 1.** The fawn lies in grass, head low. At the edge of the picture the adult deer walks away.
2. **Frame 2.** A wide meadow. The fawn is a small shape in the grass, still. The adult is far off, head down.
3. **Frame 3.** The adult is back beside the fawn, which is standing, its head under the adult.

Children point to one link and say which shows it, the words or the picture. The frames show the fawn lying down, the adult far away, and the adult back. They cannot show that the fawn is quiet, or why lying still helps. Those are things only the words tell.
