# Robin page (day 57)

A two-caption page, read aloud by the teacher, one copy for each group of five and one large. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Say first: "We made this page for our class." Children mark what the young does and what the adult does on their chart box.

## Caption 1

**Picture:** Four young robins in the nest, beaks open wide, call marks by each.

**Read:** The young robins cheep and open their mouths when the adult lands.

## Caption 2

**Picture:** The adult robin bending over the nest, a worm in its beak going into one open mouth.

**Read:** The adult robin brings food and puts it in a mouth. Food helps the young robins stay alive.

## For the teacher

Many animals care for their young, not all, and not in the same way. Point out that the picture shows open mouths and a worm, and the words tell about the cheeping. Do not say how the young learn to cheep, and do not say every cheep gets food. Ask: "What does the young do? What does the adult do?"
