# Day 61: Fix it and try a fresh page

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 60's items by gap and set four tables: A, B, C, and D with E. Lay each table's cards from the repair cards and the signal and response picture cards. Hide the exit cards. Choose six children not heard on day 59.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. No live animals. Any child may pass. Two clean-up minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback and revise

Say the three most common picture comments aloud (1 minute). Each child finds the cue on their day 60 sheet and fixes one picture or label (4 minutes). New work is unscored; the original is kept.

#### 12 min. Return and repair

Hand-backs from day 59's exit (errors A and B) and day 60's assessment, by letter. Visit the three largest tables, 4 minutes each. A: trace the young action and the adult action to staying alive. B: robin and deer chains side by side, one difference named, stays alive kept. C: sort cards into "we can see it" and "the words tell it". D and E: the seed sequence, then the food arrow marked "not for plants", with separate prompts. The fourth table works the picture move cards alone. One child follows the same moves with an adult.

#### 8 min. Fresh case and closing

Groups mark the fresh robin page and stills: the young, the adult, and the words or the picture for each (5 minutes). Meanwhile take the dictated closing from the six children, 30 seconds each (3 minutes): "The young ___, the adult ___, this helps because ___." Scribe it, then circulate.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Read the bat words; show the one drawing. One minute, everyone shows: **Ask.** [[s07-X61a]] "Pip says this picture shows the pup calling, so we do not need the words. Is Pip right? Thumb up or thumb down." Two minutes, a rotating six say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: thumb down, a picture makes no sound, so the words tell us it called. Thumb up, "you can see it calling", is error C.

#### 2 min. Collect and close

While pages are collected, the class dictates one answer to the compelling question. Close the board. Keep the revised accounts for day 67.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the page by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cues: say the comment aloud.

**Skip.** The tables and the groups.

- **8 min. Fix.** Your child fixes one picture on the day 60 sheet.
- **12 min. Fresh page.** Read a new robin page aloud. Your child points to the young, the adult, and says if the words or the picture show it.
  **Say.** "What does the young do? What does the adult do? Words or picture?" Expect: cheeps; brings an insect; the words tell the cheeping.
- **5 min. Say it.** Write your child's answer to: why does a baby bird cheep when its parent lands?
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X61a]], asked aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one table, then the fresh page. Never cut the exit. Longer: a second fix.

### Words and supports

Fix, words, picture, young, adult. Frame: "The young ___, the adult ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

No gaps carry on. Day 62 reopens the fall daylight question; unit 7's records wait for day 67.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
