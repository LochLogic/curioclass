# Repair cards (days 57, 58, 59 and 61)

Every card is a large plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, one big word under each picture. They use only the robin, the deer and the bean from the teaching days, never an exit case.

## Error A: only the adult helps (days 57, 59 and 61)

Two cards. The robin chick with its mouth open and call marks, word: cheeps. The fawn lying still in grass, word: hides. Children trace each to staying alive.

## Errors D and E: the seed sequence (day 58, and the D and E table on day 61)

The bean seed sequence drawn large: grown plant, arrow labeled made, seed, arrow labeled grew, young plant. Beside it a separate card: a food arrow going from a plant to a plant, marked not for plants. Children point and say it. A second card for error E: the words "only animals have young" beside the grown plant, seed and young plant.

## Error C: seen or heard (day 59, and the C table on day 61)

The six sort cards of cards-chain.md. Cards for the two headings: we can see it, the words tell it.

## Error B: same and different (day 61)

The robin and deer chains side by side. A card for one difference: cheeps beside hides. A card for one thing the same: stays alive.

## The picture move cards (day 61, the fourth table works alone)

Three small cards: point to the young, point to the adult, point to the picture or the words that shows it.
