# Unit 7 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, draw or place the answer. Note the day 56 retrieval in the margin beside the name, with the same scale and no score. Mark who you heard in the rotating six on each day, in the heard column. The End columns are the observation on day 60: the plan's item 1 components are split over End 1 (six components and the support the child used), End 4 (words against pictures, one component and the support) and End 5 (the pattern in both sequences, heard as the adult's part and the young's part, and the support); for item 2, one cell for each of the two components and one for the support; End 3 is the every-animal thumbs answer, one cell, kept apart from the ten points, so error B can be routed to its day 61 table. Credit a component only from the paper marks and the child's own turn, and credit the evidence components only when the child points to the marked words or picture. A case taken from a held-back page is marked indirect. Day 61 gives a child the chance to revise the day 60 sheet from the picture comment; the original is kept, and the new day 61 work is never scored. Keep the day 60 record for day 67. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied the cards or read a card aloud once more, and say so.
