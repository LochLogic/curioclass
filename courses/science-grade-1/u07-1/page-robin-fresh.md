# Fresh robin page and stills (day 61)

One page and two stills for each group of five, used for the fresh case. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Different pictures from days 56 to 58: a different nest on a porch beam, two young, and the adult bringing a small insect, not a worm. Say first: "We made this page for our class."

## The page

**Picture:** A nest on a beam under a porch roof. Two young robins in it, beaks open, call marks by each.

**Read:** In a nest on a beam, two young robins cheep. The adult robin brings a small insect and puts it in one mouth.

## Still 1

An adult robin on a fence post, a small insect in its beak, the nest on the beam in the background.

## Still 2

The adult robin on the nest rim, bending over two young with open beaks. No call marks.

## For the teacher

Groups mark the young, the adult, and the words or the picture for each. The words say the young cheep; neither still can show a sound. Nothing says that every cheep gets food. The exit for this day is a held-back alligator case, so keep this page free of alligators.
