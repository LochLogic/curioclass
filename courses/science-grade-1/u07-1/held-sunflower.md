# Day 60 sunflower case (held back, invented for practice)

Printed "invented for practice" under the picture and said aloud. Used only on day 60. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. A different plant from unit 3's maple and from day 57's bean.

## Words, read aloud

This grown sunflower made seeds. One seed grew into this young sunflower.

## Diagram

Three stages, out of order, left to right: a young sunflower with two leaves; a tall grown sunflower with a big flower head; one seed. No arrows and no words, so nothing can be copied: the child draws the link. No food, no carrying and no guarding.

## Key (teacher only)

The young sunflower is the one with two leaves. A seed produced by the grown plant grew into it. No feeding. An arrow from the grown sunflower giving food to the young one is error D. Sun, rain or soil drawn around the young plant is not an error; credit the seed link if it is there. "a grown plant cannot make young" is error E.
