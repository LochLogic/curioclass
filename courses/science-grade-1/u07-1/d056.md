# Day 56: Why does the baby bird cheep?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 55 ended unit 6 and handed nothing on. Print three-box pages and six packets. Rule the question board: young, adult, staying alive. Cover the photograph's caption. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. No live nest, no nest visit, no handling of wild animals. Any child may pass. Two clean-up minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook

Show the photograph; hear three notices. Say, "We made the next account. It is not a film of one nest." Read the Cheep at the nest read-aloud. **Ask.** "Why does a baby bird cheep with its mouth open when its parent lands on the nest?" Post three guesses. Say once: a photograph cannot make a sound; the words tell about the cheeping.

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Two minutes: "Mark the old forecast picture that helped us ask about a storm. Point to the animal on our parts chart that sensed and responded." Two minutes: card M22, invented for practice: "Mark the grown plant and the young plant. What does the seed arrow mean?" Expect: a seed grew into the young one. If many miss, trace the arrow; repeat day 57. Note error E for "no young".

#### 3 min. Teacher models a question

Show frame 2 large. Ask, "What is the young bird doing?" Answer from the picture: beak wide open. Ask the same of page 3's words: they cheep. Call marks draw a cheep; they are not a sound.

#### 8 min. Groups look and ask

Six groups of five, one packet each. Five minutes, a minute a child: each points and says "I see ___." Three minutes: the group agrees one question; post it under young, adult or staying alive.

#### 5 min. Start the model

Draw on the class model: open mouth, adult with food, a third box with a question mark. Each child draws the same.

#### 4 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Show the alligator cards. One minute, everyone touches: **Ask.** [[s07-X56a]] "Touch the words that say what the young do." **Ask.** [[s07-X56b]] "Touch the picture that shows what the adult does." Three minutes, a rotating six at 30 seconds, marked heard: **Ask.** [[s07-X56c]] "Say one thing you want to find out about how this helps them." "The babies do nothing" is error A.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Packets into envelopes. Collect pages.

### Other rooms

No display: hold up one large print.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: draw a nest, open-beaked young and an adult with a worm.

**Skip.** The groups and the board.

- **8 min. Look.** Show a picture of a bird feeding its young. Your child says "I see ___" three times.
  **Say.** "What is the young doing? What is the adult doing?" Expect: the young opens its mouth; the adult brings food.
- **7 min. Ask.** Your child asks one question about it. Write it down.
- **10 min. Draw.** Fold paper in three. Draw the open mouth, the adult with food, and a question mark.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X56a]], [[s07-X56b]] and [[s07-X56c]], asked aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the plant half of the retrieval. Never cut the exit. Longer: a second question.

### Words and supports

Young, adult, cheep. Frames: "I see ___." "I want to find out ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Children who say the adult does it all (error A) work the robin frames first on day 57.
