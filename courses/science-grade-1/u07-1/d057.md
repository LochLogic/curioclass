# Day 57: Robin, fawn and bean pages

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a chart for each child and the three pages for six groups. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. Say first: "A real fawn alone in the grass is fine, and we never touch it." No live animals. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Open and repair

If many missed day 56's exit (error A), three minutes: show the robin frames from the repair cards; trace the open mouth and the cheep to the food. If few, they work with you at the rug. Then one minute: **Ask.** "Do deer act like robins?" Post it.

#### 5 min. Robin page

Say, "We made this page for our class." Read both captions. **Ask.** "What does the young do? What does the adult do?" Each child marks the robin row of the chart. Expect: cheeps and opens its mouth; brings food.

#### 5 min. Deer page

Read all three captions. Children mark the deer row. Name hiding quietly as something the young does. Expect: lies still; comes back and feeds milk.

#### 3 min. Plant caption

Read the bean plant page, invented for practice. Say once: "The grown plant is the parent plant, as in unit 3." Hear three children point to the grown plant, the seed and the young plant. Say no food arrow is drawn: the plant did not feed it like a robin.

#### 2 min. Chart

Scribe a robin row and a deer row on the class chart from the children's marks.

#### 5 min. Revise the model

Add the deer row to the class model. Post the next question: do the pictures agree with the words?

#### 4 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Read the alligator words (30 seconds). One and a half minutes, everyone draws: **Ask.** [[s07-X57a]] "In the adult box, draw what the adult does." **Ask.** [[s07-X57b]] "In the young box, draw what the young do." Then read the pea words and lay the three pea pictures in a row, no arrows: **Ask.** [[s07-X57c]] "Touch the picture the young pea plant came up from." All touch; hear a rotating six say why, 20 seconds each. An empty young box, "she does it", is error A; the grown plant because "it fed the young one" is error D; "a plant has no young" is error E.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect charts.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the chart on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: draw a robin, a fawn and a bean plant.

**Skip.** The groups and the class chart.

- **12 min. Robin and fawn.** Tell your child: a young robin cheeps and opens its mouth, and the adult brings food. A fawn lies still and quiet, and the adult comes back to feed it milk. Your child draws each.
  **Say.** "What does the young do? What does the adult do?" Expect: cheeps; brings food. Lies still; feeds milk.
- **10 min. Plant.** Draw a grown bean plant, a seed and a young plant. Arrows say made, then grew. No food arrow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X57a]], [[s07-X57b]] and [[s07-X57c]]: your child draws what the adult does and what the young do for new words, then touches the plant picture.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read two deer captions. Never cut the plant caption or the exit. Longer: say how the animals differ.

### Words and supports

Hide, feed, seed, grown plant, young plant. Frame: "The young ___, the adult ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Errors D and E (plant) go first to day 58, five minutes; error A goes to day 59.
