# Day 58: Do the pictures agree with the words?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a chart for each child and six fawn packets. Set out Pip, day 56's robin feeding photograph, the repair cards and the robin page beside frame 3. Hide the exit cards.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. Say first: "These pictures were drawn for our class; the robin photograph is a real one from day 56." No live animals. Any child may pass. Two clean-up minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Open

Point to the board question: do the pictures agree with the words? Pip says, "I can hear the baby birds in the photograph!" Children say what the photograph shows and what it cannot show.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 57's exit (errors D and E), show the bean plant page redrawn large with the arrows made and grew, beside a food arrow marked "not for plants". Children point and say it. Error A, if many, moves to day 59's open.

#### 4 min. Teacher model

Set the robin page beside frame 3. Say, "The words say the adult brings food. The frame shows food in the beak." Put a words mark and a picture mark on the chart's adult row.

#### 8 min. Groups compare the deer

Six groups of five: a fawn page and the fawn sequence each. Five minutes, a minute a child: each points to one link and says which shows it, the words or the picture. Three minutes: the group names one thing the words tell that the frames do not.

#### 2 min. Share

Four groups say that one thing, 30 seconds each; the other two groups point to theirs on the class model during the revise. Expect: the fawn is quiet; lying still helps keep it hidden.

#### 4 min. Revise the model

Each child puts a words mark or a picture mark on each link of their chart. The class model gets the same marks.

#### 6 min. Exit

Say, "Invented for practice." Read the sea otter words; show the one picture. **Ask.** [[s07-X58a]] "Say one thing the words tell that our picture does not show." Everyone whispers it to a partner, 1 minute; then a rotating six say it to you, 30 seconds each, marked heard. Expect: the mother wraps the pup in kelp, and our picture has no mother. "The picture shows her wrapping it" is error C.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect the six packets.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the frames large by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: draw three frames of a fawn alone, far from the adult, and back.

**Skip.** The groups and the sharing.

- **10 min. Words and picture.** Read the fawn page aloud. Your child looks at the three frames.
  **Say.** "Which shows the fawn lying still, the words or the picture?" Expect: the picture shows it lying down; only the words say it is quiet.
- **10 min. Marks.** On a chart of words and picture, your child marks which shows each link.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X58a]], asked aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two frames of the fawn. Never cut the teacher model or the exit. Longer: a child says why two pictures may come from one account.

### Words and supports

Words, picture, show, tell. Frame: "The words tell ___. The picture shows ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error C (a picture proves a sound) goes to day 59, five minutes of sorting cards. Day 59 lays the robin and deer chains side by side.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
