# Day 60: Ducks and a sunflower

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day 59 class model stays on the wall, unscored. Print a response sheet per child and the duck and sunflower pages, sealed. Choose the order of the six groups. Set the record sheet's heard column and its End columns beside you.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. No live animals. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 1 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check, and we call it one. You may pass. The ducklings and the sunflower are invented for practice. Nobody helps today." Asked, reread once, neutrally; write H.

#### 2 min. Read and show

Read the duck words once: "A mark by a beak is a picture of a call. A picture of a call is not the sound." Give no meaning for either sequence. Hand out sheets and both sequences.

#### 3 min. Mark

Each child marks the young and the adult in words and pictures. Circulate; do not coach. Then "pencils down" until the plant case.

#### 19 min. Six group turns, about 3 minutes each

Five 36-second turns, 10 seconds to change. Each child points to a marked place and gives a survival reason [[s07-E60a]], then **Ask.** [[s07-E60d]] "Say one thing the duck words tell that the duck pictures do not." **Ask.** [[s07-E60e]] "What happens again in both duck picture sequences?" Mark at once in the End columns: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Credit evidence only when the child points to the marked words or picture. Write who was heard. Note error A (only the adult acts) and error C (a picture proves a sound). Waiting groups place the robin and deer cards alone and draw a reason on scrap, unscored; sheets stay down.

#### 3 min. Every animal

To the whole class: **Ask.** [[s07-E60c]] "Thumbs up for yes, thumbs down for no: does every animal have to call to keep its young safe?" All show at once (30 seconds); mark each child. A rotating six say why, 20 seconds each, marked heard. Thumbs up, "every animal has to call", is error B.

#### 4 min. Plant case

Read the sunflower words with the diagram, invented for practice. Each child draws, from words and diagram, how the young sunflower got there: [[s07-E60b]]. After class read the drawings: an arrow from the grown sunflower giving food to the young one is error D; sun, rain or soil drawn around the young plant is not an error; "grown plants cannot make young" is error E.

#### 2 min. Collect

Collect the sheets. After class, fix a day 61 cue card to each sheet. Keep the record for day 67.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the sheet's boxes by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: draw three duck frames with a call mark by the beak, and a sunflower, a seed and a young sunflower.

**Skip.** The group turns and the cards.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Read the duck words once; your child marks the young and the adult in the pictures. Do not help. Then read the sunflower words.
  **Say.** "How do the ducks stay safe? Where did the young sunflower come from?" Expect: the adult calls and the young stop moving; a seed from the grown plant grew into it.
- **10 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: ducks only, then the sunflower. A check stays a check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Young, adult, words, picture, seed. Frame: "The young ___, the adult ___, this helps because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Gaps go to day 61 by letter: error C at the C table, errors A and B at their tables, errors D and E at the plant table. Day 61 returns day 60's sheets with a picture comment.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
