# Day 60: Ducks and a sunflower

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 59 ended with the paper class model, which stays on the wall unscored. Print a response sheet for each child and the duck and sunflower pages; keep both sealed. Choose the order of the six groups. Set the record sheet's heard column and its End columns beside you.

### Safety and set-up

Seated paper work. No live animals. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 2 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check, and we call it one. You may pass. The ducklings and the sunflower are invented for practice. Nobody helps today, except one neutral re-reading of the words."

#### 3 min. Read and show

Read the duck words once. Say: "A mark by a beak is a picture of a call. A picture of a call is not the sound." Give no meaning for either sequence. Hand out the sheets and the two sequences.

#### 4 min. Mark

Each child marks the young and the adult in the words and in the pictures. Circulate; do not coach.

#### 18 min. Six group turns, 3 minutes each

A group at your table: five 30-second turns, then 30 seconds to change over. Each child points to a marked place, gives one survival reason and says whether every animal must call: [[s07-E60a]]. Mark at once on the record sheet's End columns, on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Credit the evidence components only when the child points to the marked words or picture. Write who was heard. Note error A (only the adult acts), error B (every animal must call) and error C (a picture proves a sound). Waiting groups finish their sheets, then place the familiar robin and deer evidence cards on their own and draw a reason, unscored.

#### 5 min. Plant case

Read the sunflower words with the diagram, invented for practice. Each child marks the young sunflower and draws how it got there: [[s07-E60b]]. After class read the drawings: a food arrow is error D; "grown plants cannot make young" is error E.

#### 2 min. Collect

Collect the sheets and the sequences. After class fix a picture cue from the day 61 cue cards to each sheet. Keep the record for day 67.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the sheet's boxes by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: draw three duck frames with a call mark by the beak, and a sunflower, a seed and a young sunflower.

**Skip.** The group turns and the cards.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Read the duck words once; your child marks the young and the adult in the pictures. Do not help. Then read the sunflower words.
  **Say.** "How do the ducks stay safe? Where did the young sunflower come from?" Expect: the adult calls and the young stop moving; a seed from the grown plant grew into it.
- **10 min. Look back.** Praise one thing. Correct nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: ducks only, then the sunflower. A check stays a check. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Young, adult, words, picture, seed. Frame: "The young ___, the adult ___, this helps because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Gaps go to day 61 by letter: error C at the C table, errors A and B at their tables, errors D and E at the plant table. Day 61 returns day 60's sheets with a picture comment.
