# Robin nest picture sequence (days 56 and 58)

Four frames, no words, one packet for each group of five and one shown large. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. The same robin family as the read-aloud: one adult, four young. Say aloud before use: "These pictures were drawn for our class. They show what robins do. They are not a film of one nest." A drawn call mark is a picture of a cheep, not a sound.

1. **Frame 1.** The nest in the tree fork. Four young with beaks closed. The adult flies toward the nest, a worm in its beak, still a little way off.
2. **Frame 2.** The adult has landed on the rim. All four young have beaks wide open, with call marks beside each. (Day 56: the teacher models "What is the young bird doing?" on this frame.)
3. **Frame 3.** The adult bends over one open mouth with the worm. The other three beaks stay open.
4. **Frame 4.** The adult is gone. The young sit with beaks closed. One has a bulge in its throat.

Day 56: groups look, point and say "I see ___". Day 58: the teacher sets one robin page beside frame 2 or 3 to show a words mark and a picture mark. The frames show what can be seen: open mouths, a worm, an adult on the rim. They cannot show a cheep or a reason.
