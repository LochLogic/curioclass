# Our science safety rules (Grade 1, the whole year)

A teacher page: each rule once, with its days. Each day's Safety and set-up says what that day needs. Give a short safety talk before every hands-on part; watch every task throughout. Cut any task you cannot run safely; every day has a paper path.

## The stop rule (every day)

Anyone, child or adult, may say "Stop." Hands go flat on the table and eyes come to you. Stop for a spill, a hurt child, rising dust, a loose cord, a snapped band or thunder. Start again only when you say so.

## Eye protection (days 18 to 21, 28, 29, 31, 33)

- Impact-rated safety glasses or goggles, child size, marked Z87.
- Everyone in the room wears them, you too, from set-up to clean-up, while bands are stretched, the ruler is flicked or children cut.
- One pair per child, in a named bag. After each use, wash with soap and water and dry fully before bagging.
- Without enough pairs for all, you pluck or flick and children watch from their seats.

## Hand washing (days 1, 2, 4, 11, 12, 22, 29, 66)

Wash with soap and running water, scrubbing at least 20 seconds, after clay, chalk, outdoor looks and plant parts. When thirty cannot wash at once, they wipe hands at the table, then wash before the next food or break.

## Allergies: dust, plants, latex and foods (days 1, 2, 11, 12, 22, 28 to 31, 66)

- Before the year, get the nurse's list: asthma, dust, plants, latex and foods.
- A child on the list uses the pictures; any child may choose them.
- A child with asthma or dust sensitivity makes pencil dots, not chalk (days 1, 2).
- Bands are intact and nonlatex; balloons and rubber toys are not used (days 28 to 31).
- Egg-carton pieces come from a new, unused carton; a child with an egg allergy uses plain card (day 66). No food is used or tasted.

## Plants, soil and mold (days 10 to 12, 20, 22)

- Only identified, nontoxic plants and leaves, with no sap or thorns. Never taste a plant part. A stand-in houseplant is checked nontoxic first (day 11).
- No digging and no loose soil in class; only you lift the class plant from its pot over a tray (day 11).
- Pressed leaves are checked the day before for dust and mold, and a moldy leaf is thrown out; you hold them and children look (days 12, 22).

## Outdoor looks and adult ratios (days 1, 2, 4)

- Children stay with you; count them out and back in.
- Never look at the sun, and no binoculars or other lenses; backs to the sun on day 4. Sunglasses do not make looking safe.
- In lightning, extreme heat or cold, skip the look and use the saved tracing.
- Ask a helper adult to move round on the cutting, band, ruler and string lane days (18 to 21, 28, 29, 31, 33, 49, 51, 52, 54).

## Heat and lamps (days 38 to 46, 48 to 55, 66 to 68)

- No heater, kettle or flame all year.
- You alone switch the lamp and flashlights. Light is aimed at a wall, a screen or a low card, never at eyes or the sun; children look at a lamp from the side, briefly.
- Flashlights take AA or AAA cells only, never button cells (a swallowed one can badly burn a child's throat within hours); you secure every battery compartment.
- The room is dimmed, never dark; the exit stays lit (days 38 to 47, 66).

## Electricity (days 32, 33, 36)

Only you plug in or work the speaker, on a dry table, its cord taped down out of walkways and unplugged after class. Sound stays at classroom level, never near ears.

## Magnets, small parts and string (days 28 to 33, 36, 45, 47, 49 to 55, 67)

- No magnets are used this year.
- Rice stays sealed under wrap (day 32); confetti stays on its plate (day 36). Nothing small goes in a mouth, nose or ear.
- Sorting hoops are paper and lie flat (days 45, 47).
- String is short, kept low and walked around, never looped on a body; adults cut it and collect it (days 49 to 55, 67).
- Cup devices: the sender speaks into the cup in a normal voice; the receiver holds the cup lightly against the outside of the ear, never pressed in; nobody tugs the string while a cup is at an ear, and both say "ready" first (days 49, 51, 52, 54, 55, 67).

## Glass and sharp edges (days 16 to 21, 43, 44, 46, 49, 53, 66)

- No glass anywhere, home swaps included. Mirrors are plastic only, edges taped if rough, no lasers (days 44, 46, 66).
- Children cut seated with blunt scissors, counted out and in. You pre-cut thick card and pre-punch cup holes (days 49, 53). An adult trims cut plastic (days 43, 46, 66).

## Clean-up (every hands-on day)

Clean-up has its own minutes. Wipe spills at once. Glasses are washed and dried before bagging; scissors, string, pieces and devices are counted back; hands are washed as above.
