# This week in US History I

## English

This week we study the Civil War from the record left by people who lived it: Lincoln's letters, a nurse's journal,
Harriet Tubman's own letter about guiding Black soldiers on a raid that freed hundreds, and photographs.
We also read two 1862 land laws and an 1863 treaty that shows whose land it was.
We ask of every source: who made it, when, for whom, and what it cannot tell us.

We name slavery and violence plainly. At the end of the week we test a claim made after the war, that slavery was not its
cause, against what Confederate leaders said in 1861.

Midweek your student revises an essay on why the war came. Ask them: what did you change, and what evidence made you
change it?

A question for the table: why might a letter written during the war tell us something different from a book
written twenty years later?
