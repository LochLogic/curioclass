Douglass' Monthly and as a broadside in Rochester, New York, to recruit Black men for the 54th Massachusetts.

**Source D. William Morris Smith, photograph, Company E, 4th United States Colored Infantry, at Fort Lincoln,
District of Columbia, 1865.** Public domain. Library of Congress. [image: about thirty soldiers in uniform, standing
in rows with rifles, officers in front; a posed company photograph]

**Source E. Thomas Nast, "Franchise. And Not This Man?", Harper's Weekly, August 5, 1865.** Public domain. Library
of Congress. [image: Columbia, the figure for the nation, rests her hand on the shoulder of a Black Union soldier

Gloss: *franchise*, the right to vote.
