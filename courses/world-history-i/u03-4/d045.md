# Day 45: Evidence of power

## Teacher page

### Before class

On each assessment paper, write one A1 triplet by hand, rotating: form A, hereditary rule, dynasty, empire; form B, kingdom, empire, feudal society; form C, dynasty, kingdom, hereditary rule. Cut out F1 and F2 from each packet and fold each shut. Keep papers and packets covered until the revision ends. The folders stay with you: A2 was scored last night from the revised maps and written tasks (s03-P44-1, s03-P44-2, s03-P44-3).

### The day

#### 5 min. Claim revision

Return each student's day 44 claim, s03-W44, with its written feedback, then [[s03-P45-1]], alone: change what the feedback questions, keep the first version, note the change. Only the claim comes back today. Collect the revised claims before handing out the paper; A2d scores them.

#### 40 min. Assessment

Students work alone on lined paper, numbering each answer by item. Every student answers all three survey items; none is skipped. [[s03-A2-v2]] is scored from the folder: students write nothing for it.

Pace: [[s03-A1-v2]] 5 minutes, [[s03-A2a]] 3, [[s03-A2b-v2]] 2, [[s03-A2d]] 5, [[s03-A3-v2]] 6, [[s03-A4]] 6, [[s03-A5a]] 6, [[s03-A5b]] 7.

Read sources aloud on request, without explanation of the content; a one-line word explanation is not coaching. Read each translator's word once, then use its substitute from the opener's list. The printed text stays as printed. For A5b, students open only the card they chose after writing their question and reason. No coaching. Allow a scribe and extra time; record the support.

#### 5 min. Mark

Pencils down. Read the key aloud for A2a, A2b and A2d only; students circle, in another colour, what they would change, keeping the first answer. Collect everything, packets included.

After class, score each look-for in its own record-sheet cell: 2, accurate with explained evidence; 1, partial; 0, unsupported or mistaken. Keep language apart from content. F1 shows the deputy's authority and guards, not a tax payment. Allow about 3 hours for 30 students with A2, an untested estimate. Then sort the 0 and 1 cells into at most four response groups with the opener's table.

### Other rooms

No printer: hand-copy the paper and insets; copy F1 and F2 onto separate folded cards. Read the key aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: hand-copy the paper, form A and the insets; copy F1 and F2 onto separate folded cards.

**Skip.** Nothing is skipped; every survey item is answered.

- **45 min. Assessment.** First, return yesterday's claim and give five minutes to revise it from your written feedback. Then uncover the paper and packet for forty minutes alone, on lined paper, numbering each answer. Your child chooses F1 or F2 before opening it. A2 is scored from the map already handed in.
- **5 min. Review.** Read the three survey answers together; your child circles, in another colour, what to change.
  **Say.** "Which part would you change?" Expect: a named component and the evidence for the change.

### Shorter or longer

Extra time extends completion; nothing is added.

### Words and supports

Say "enslaved people" in our words. The salt miners at Taghaza and the armed men at Mali's court were enslaved; Source C names the inland peoples Kilwa raided, and their voices are missing here (error J: people as the subject, the force named). Ibn al-Athir records killing, with no supported total. Mongke's words and Dugha's song reach us through visitors. Nobody speaks for Mali, the Swahili coast or Mongolia, or acts in character.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 returns the marked assessment by component, the day 45 packet and goods cards open for reference, and s03-X44-v2 with error P. Groups come from the opener's table: A, B, I, N, L, K or D, H, J, C, O, E, F, G and P, at most four, given on slips. Then the units 1 to 3 timeline review.
