# Day 45: Evidence of power

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare three masters, printing only the assigned triplet in A1's blank: form A, hereditary rule, dynasty, empire; form B, kingdom, empire, feudal society; form C, dynasty, kingdom, hereditary rule. Rotate copies. Lay out lined paper. Return day 44's claim with written feedback and the map folder. Keep assessment papers and sources-45.md covered until revision ends; fold F1 and F2 separately. Open the answer key and record sheet privately.

### The day

#### 5 min. Claim revision

Alone, [[s03-P45-1]]. Use day 44's revision box; keep both versions. Feedback on error D separates conquest's killing from trade gains. Collect claims and day 44's revised maps before handing out the paper. Only these revised products enter A2 and A2d.

#### 40 min. Assessment

Students work alone. [[s03-A2]] scores the collected map, without redrawing. A1 samples power terms; A2 samples geography, politics, economy, culture and external links. Accept the previously used sketches labelled not to scale, without measured distances; accept a sourced absence of alliance evidence.

Pace: [[s03-A1]] 6 minutes; [[s03-A2a]] 3; [[s03-A2b]] 2; [[s03-A2d]] 4; [[s03-A3]] 5; [[s03-A4]] 6; [[s03-A5a]] 6; [[s03-A5b]] 8. For A1, ask why the words help compare power. Skip only the mapped option's survey; everyone supplies A2d's revised claim. Spare survey minutes support reading. A3 samples directions and relays; A4, knowledge and limits; A5a, interpretation traced to evidence; A5b, inspected further evidence.

Read sources aloud without explanation. Read only titles of folded cards until a choice is recorded, then the chosen card privately if needed. No coaching or answer display. Allow a scribe and extra time, recording support; preserve independent reasoning.

#### 5 min. Mark

Pencils down. Read the answer key for A2a, A2b and A2d only. Students circle possible changes with another pencil, preserving original answers. Collect everything. After class, score each component 2, accurate with explained evidence; 1, partial; 0, unsupported or mistaken. Use row look-fors and record sheet A1 to A5b columns; distinguish language from content. Enter 0/1/2 instead of ticks in assessment columns. Score A2d's claim component for everyone. F1 shows authority and guards, not a tax payment. Allow about 40 minutes after class, untested.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy papers, assigned triplets and insets; share handwritten sources, keeping folded cards concealed. Read the answer key aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: hand-copy the paper, form A triplet and insets. Share the source packet; fold each further card separately.

**Skip.** Group sorting and repeated survey reading.

- **45 min. Assessment.** First, give five minutes to revise yesterday's claim from written feedback. Collect it and the revised map. Then uncover the paper and packet for forty minutes alone. Follow the class item order; omit only the mapped option's survey. Read sources neutrally if needed. Keep answers hidden; show only the further-card titles until your child records a choice. Your child opens and investigates that card. Keep original answers.
- **5 min. Review.** Read the three survey answers together. Circle possible changes separately. Write feedback afterwards; tomorrow, revisit missed components together, then correct one alone.
  **Say.** "Which part would you change?" Expect: a specific component and evidence explaining the change.

### Shorter or longer

Use skipped-survey minutes for reading; preserve revision and inquiry. Extra time extends completion, without adding content.

### Words and supports

Read packet glosses neutrally. Use enslaved people in our words. Taghaza's miners, Mali's armed court guards and people taken in Kilwa's raids were coerced; their voices are missing here. Ibn al-Athir records killing, without a supported total. Trade gains cannot compensate. African states were African-built and ruled. Mongke's words and Dugha's work reach us through visitors. Mali, Timbuktu, the Swahili coast, Shona communities and Mongolia live today; nobody speaks for them or acts in character.

### Tomorrow

Return day 45's assessment and revision notes by component for day 46's feedback and response groups. Keep day 44's exit, s03-X44, for error I's labour-and-cost response. More than a third missing a component needs class repair; otherwise group support. Review units 1 to 3 on the parallel timeline, then correct independently.
