# Day 43: Mapping power, part 2

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return day 42's exit, s03-X42-v2, marked for error I. Set out folders, rulers and the option sheets. Print the pages; open the answer key and record sheet. Have the parallel timeline ready, or draw two rows on the board.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Students do [[s03-P43-1]] alone on their returned day 42 ticket, then tell a partner.

**Ask.** Who works, and who carries a cost? Expect a named producer (the salt diggers) and a cost (the carrying, or the king's share). Error I gives the river alone. If more than a third missed it yesterday, trace producer, carrier and collector together; otherwise confer during map work.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Model one culture line on the board, with its source and makers beyond the ruler: "Mali, about 1352: village women sold millet, milk and rice to travellers (Source B)." For option b, the Swahili language and Kilwa's coral-stone mosque, built on the coast, are the coast's own culture; the Shona builders of Great Zimbabwe are a link inland. For option d, Source J's heading records Christians, Muslims and Buddhists debating at Mongke's court. Then model one absence note: "Alliance: no source on my sheet records one."

**Ask.** Which link to another region does your source record, and which does it not? Expect Source C's gold route from the interior; the Mongol card records an alliance sought, not made.

#### 22 min. Alone: the map, part 2

[[s03-P43-2]]

Students add to the squared-paper map from day 42 and write the explanations on Side 1. One line a link is enough; every link needs a source or an absence note. Where a source gives a journey time, write it on the route; journey times are not distances.

Confer with the look-fors, without marks. For error K, sort the sheet's finds into made here and brought here; Africans built and ruled these towns. Present-day borders are error B. A distance no source gives is an evidence question: ask which source says so. For error N, list everyone the source shows taking part before the ruler.

#### 8 min. Pairs: share

Partners from different options swap maps and student pages; each writes one question about a link or a date in the box on the partner's Side 2. Swap back; authors answer from their sheet or name the missing evidence.

**Ask.** Whose power does your map show, and whose does it leave out? Expect: rulers and routes are drawn; producers, carriers and enslaved people need a label or an absence note.

#### 5 min. Timeline

[[s03-P43-3]]

Students draw two separate bands on Side 2: Mali from about 1235, the Yuan 1271 to 1368. The card gives no end for Mali; they overlap from 1271 to at least 1352, when Ibn Battuta visited. During the exit minutes, two volunteers add the bands to the class timeline.

**Ask.** Does sharing years make two states one? Expect: separate states can exist at the same time. For error H, align both bands on one year scale with separate labels.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

[[s03-X43-v2]], alone, on paper. Collect tickets and folders: the map, Side 1, the partner question and the day 42 ticket with the warm-up arrow.

After class, record the exit's three look-fors. Write map feedback in short phrases, about 5 minutes a map: dated geography, politics, economy, culture, each link, and a source limit. Accept honest absence notes. No marks yet.

### Other rooms

Without a printer, read the sheets aloud and students copy only their own work. A ruled grid replaces squared paper; two rows on paper replace the wall timeline.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: read the sheets together. No wall timeline: draw two rows.

**Skip.** Sharing with a partner.

- **4 min. Warm-up.** Use yesterday's ticket for [[s03-P43-1]].
  **Say.** "Who works, and who carries a cost?" Expect: a producer, and the carrying or the king's share.
- **5 min. Sources.** Reread the chosen sheet aloud.
  **Say.** "Which link is recorded?" Expect: a sourced trade or war link; a missing alliance is noted, not invented.
- **16 min. Map.** Add [[s03-P43-2]] to yesterday's map alone. Tonight, write one short line each on dates, politics, economy, culture, each link and a source limit; no marks.
- **5 min. Timeline.** Draw [[s03-P43-3]] together. The card gives no end for Mali.
  **Say.** "Are these one state?" Expect: two states in the same years.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X43-v2]], alone. Keep the ticket and the map for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one partner question only; keep the map work, timeline and exit. Longer: check another option's link and name its limit.

### Words and supports

Alliance: an agreement between powers. Exchange: ideas, practices and people moving, not only goods. Culture: language, music, building, belief and learning. Students may dictate. Gloss translators' words once. Mali, the Swahili coast, the Shona and Mongolia are living places and peoples; no student speaks for them.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 returns the map with written feedback, with s03-P43-1, s03-P43-3 and s03-X43-v2 clipped to it: five minutes to read, eight to revise. Error H merges two states; error K reads imports as foreign founders. An H miss redraws the bands on the back of the ticket; a K miss sorts made here from brought here. Then the discussion and the closing claim.
