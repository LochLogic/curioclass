# Day 46: Repair an explanation, connect the years

## Teacher page

### Before class

Arrange day 45's assessment with written feedback by component, revised maps and claims, and day 44's exit, s03-X44. Lay out the retained sources, answer key and record sheet. Sort response groups by the most frequent missed component and error. Keep marks private; announce work areas by repair task. Open the parallel timeline from day 5, including day 43's additions. Set out lined paper for corrections and dated bands.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return day 45's assessment and revision notes, including the revised closing claim, s03-P45-1, plus day 44's exit. Students read feedback silently, identify a component to repair and join its group. An error I miss on s03-X44 joins the labour-and-cost group. Preserve first versions, written feedback and revisions: the map was revised on day 44 before A2 scoring; the closing claim was revised on day 45 before A2d scoring.

#### 25 min. Response groups

In groups, [[s03-P46-1]]. Spend five minutes modelling the most common repair, fifteen applying it with sources, then five sharing the reason for a change. If more than a third missed a component, model that repair for everyone; otherwise teach the affected group. Others begin with their assigned move. Use the returned assessment's own question, not a new invented case.

Assign the relevant move; groups need only their own:

- Error A: apply overlapping terms, naming each relationship.
- Error B: date the map; replace present-day borders with supported towns and routes. Accept schematic maps labelled not to scale.
- Error C: trace goods in both directions using the goods cards.
- Error D: set Source E's killing beside Source D's enforced money; record harms and trade gains separately. Gains never compensate for conquest; give no unsupported death total.
- Error E: check maker, date, audience and transmission. Ibn Battuta dictated later; the atlas maker worked from reports.
- Error F: connect each clause of interpretation S to evidence in Source P1 or Source P2.
- Error G: inspect the chosen further card, record a detail and revise the inquiry. Source F1 shows guards and authority, not a tax payment.
- Error H: align separate dated bands; overlap does not mean one state.
- Error I: trace workers, carriers, rulers and costs using Source P1. Wealth depended on labour.
- Error J: name coercion: enslaved miners at Taghaza, armed enslaved guards at Mali's court, and people taken in Kilwa's raids. Their own voices are missing here.
- Error K: distinguish local building and rule from imported objects. Africans built and ruled African states.
- Error L: separate court display from subjects' consent.
- Error M: trace accounts to a shared origin before counting witnesses.
- Error N: identify contributors beyond rulers: Dugha's work in Source F2 and the visitor's account carrying it.
- Error O: mark regional markets and relays, rather than one end-to-end journey.

Students without misses strengthen a source limit through the same practice row: compare Source C's building description with its coral-stone gloss, distinguishing the visitor's claim from surviving evidence. Keep corrections as practice, without a new score; record the improvement separately from day 45's marks. Practice pages stay with students.

#### 15 min. Review: units 1 to 3

Pairs complete [[s03-P46-2]] on lined paper beside the parallel timeline. First scan entries from each of units 1 and 2; then connect unit 3. Use the timeline's existing dates and labels, not recalled guesses. Trace the named network between regions; simultaneous developments alone do not prove contact.

After pairs explain their choices, compare with the timeline and source-sheet fact cards. Correct dates with evidence. For error H, redraw separate bands on a shared year scale. Keep the completed comparison covered during the exit; students independently choose another pairing for it.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Alone, [[s03-X46]], using returned sources and the timeline, without group corrections or the answer key. Provide lined paper for a corrected explanation and two dated bands. A student without a component marked 1 or 0 independently strengthens an accurate component with evidence and a limit. Collect tickets; use the three look-fors on the record sheet. Do not score today's first-written practice.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the two practice rows and exit onto paper; reuse handwritten sources. No wall timeline: copy its existing entries onto lined paper before class. Read sources aloud neutrally; a scribe records the student's reasoning.

### At home

**Swap.** No class timeline: copy earlier dated entries from your child's retained timeline notes onto paper. No printer: copy today's questions; reuse yesterday's papers and sources.

**Skip.** Group sorting and the class sharing round.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Return yesterday's written feedback and day 44's exit. Choose the main repair together.
- **15 min. Repair.** Use [[s03-P46-1]] together, following the written feedback. Preserve the original answer. With no misses, strengthen a source limit.
  **Say.** "Which evidence changes your explanation?" Expect: a source detail and its connection to the correction.
- **10 min. Review.** Use [[s03-P46-2]]. Point to earlier entries from units 1 and 2, then unit 3.
  **Say.** "Where do the years overlap?" Expect: shared years in different regions; a named network.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X46]], alone. Cover joint corrections; keep sources available. With no misses, strengthen an accurate component independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten the sharing round, preserving each repair, review and independent exit. With longer time, use the same practice row to explain a second source limit.

### Words and supports

Interpretation: an explanation built from evidence. Coercion: force denying people's choices. Read glosses aloud; accept pointing before explanation to distinguish language needs from content gaps. Nobody acts in character or speaks for a people. Mali, Timbuktu, the Swahili coast, Shona communities and Mongolia are living places.

### Tomorrow

Read corrections and timeline explanations separately. Error H appears when bands merge or Mali is placed only after Mongol rule; retain these tickets for focused chronology support. An unsupported correction needs sourcing support. Day 47 is course-level check 1's first day: an independent sourced claim paragraph from two unseen sources on an interaction of 500 to 1500 drawn from units 1 and 2. Use today's evidence to arrange access and later response, without coaching answers during the check.
