# Day 46: Repair an explanation, connect the years

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet's 0 and 1 cells, choose at most four groups with the opener's table (the four most missed components). Write each student's group and case on a slip; marks stay private. Others work in pairs from the written move. Lay out the marked day 45 assessment, the day 45 packet and goods cards (now open for reference), the day 42 option sheets and day 44's exit, s03-X44-v2. Have the parallel timeline ready.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return the marked assessment by component, the revised claim s03-P45-1, the packet and s03-X44-v2. Students read the feedback silently and go to the group on their slip. An error P miss on the day 44 exit joins the P group: name who gained and who paid.

#### 25 min. Response groups

[[s03-P46-1-v2]]. Each group uses its move on the new case on its slip, from a source already taught, then each student fixes or strengthens their own answer in one sentence with a source detail. Spend five minutes modeling the move with the largest group if more than a third missed it; then fifteen applying; then five sharing what changed.

The moves, from the opener's table:

- A: two words for power applied to one ruler.
- B: a dated map beside a present-day one; name what moved.
- C and O: trace one route both ways, market to market.
- D: harms and gains in two columns; gains from trade do not erase the killing.
- E: ask of each claim, could the maker have seen this? Polo told his book to Rustichello.
- F: trace each half of a statement to its source.
- G: open the source, copy one detail, revise the question.
- H: separate dated bands on one year scale.
- I: who dug, carried, collected and gained.
- J: people as the subject, the force named: the salt miners held in slavery, the armed enslaved men at Mali's court, the inland peoples Kilwa raided. Their own voices are missing.
- K: sort made here from brought here; Africans built and ruled these towns.
- L: who made the account, for whom, and whose view is missing.
- N: list everyone the source shows taking part.
- P: name who gained and who paid.

A student with no 0 or 1 cells strengthens one source limit: Source C's "wood" beside Kilwa's coral-stone mosque. Corrections are practice; record improvement apart from day 45's marks. Practice pages stay with students.

#### 15 min. Review: units 1 to 3

Pairs do [[s03-P46-2-v2]]: review units 1 and 2 entries beside unit 3 on the parallel timeline, for days 47 and 48. Use the timeline's dates, not guesses; shared years alone do not prove contact. Close by naming the five checks on one unit 1 source on the timeline: day 47 asks a sourced claim on units 1 and 2.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

[[s03-X46-v2]], alone, on paper, from the timeline only, choosing a pairing not made in the review. Collect tickets; record the three look-fors. It goes back on day 49.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the slips, the case and the exit onto paper. No wall timeline: copy its entries onto lined paper before class.

### At home

**Swap.** No class timeline: copy the earlier timeline entries onto paper.

**Skip.** Groups and sharing; work from the missed component with the most 0 and 1 cells.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Return the marked assessment, the packet and day 44's exit. Choose the main repair together.
- **15 min. Repair.** [[s03-P46-1-v2]], using the case for that component. Keep the first answer.
  **Say.** "Which evidence changes your answer?" Expect: a source detail and how it changes the answer.
- **10 min. Review.** [[s03-P46-2-v2]], with one unit 1 or 2 entry and one unit 3 entry.
  **Say.** "Do the years overlap, and what linked them?" Expect: dated bands; a network named, or none recorded.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X46-v2]], alone. Keep it for day 49.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the sharing minutes. Longer: a second source limit with the same move.

### Words and supports

Interpretation: an explanation built from evidence. Coercion: force that takes away people's choices. Read glosses aloud; accept pointing before explaining. Nobody acts in character or speaks for a people.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 is course-level check 1: a sourced claim on units 1 and 2 from unseen sources. s03-X46-v2 goes back on day 49, error H: two developments merged, or Mali placed only after Mongol rule; an H miss joins the timeline group then.
