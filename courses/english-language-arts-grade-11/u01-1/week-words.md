# Week 1 word cards

Plain meanings, written by Curio Class. Check each against how the word is used in the story or the account. The 1828 dictionary can give an older sense; day 4 shows how to read an entry.

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| melancholy | sad and gloomy |
| scruples | doubts that make a person hold back from something that may be wrong |
| covenant | a serious agreement, made before witnesses or before God |
| communion | sharing; in a church, the shared bread and wine, and the people who share it |
| pious | devoted to religion, or showing it |
| indicted | formally charged with a crime |
| testimony | what a witness says, under oath, about what they saw or know |
| spectre | a ghost; in 1692, a shape said to be a person's spirit |
