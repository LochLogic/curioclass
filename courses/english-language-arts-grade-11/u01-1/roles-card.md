# Roles card

- **Evidence checker:** asks, Where does the text say that? and notes the line.
- **Questioner:** asks one question that probes the reasoning.
- **Synthesizer:** says what two speakers agree and what conflicts.
- **Invites other views:** asks someone who has not spoken what they think.
- **Timer:** keeps the pace and nothing else.

The group agrees the roles aloud before talking.
