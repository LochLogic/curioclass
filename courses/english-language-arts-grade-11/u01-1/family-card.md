# Family card: conceive and perceive

Both words are built on a Latin root, cap or cept, that means take. Each family has a verb, a noun and an adjective. The ending shows the job.

| Verb | Noun | Adjective |
|---|---|---|
| conceive | conception | conceivable |
| perceive | perception | perceptible |

The parts of speech were checked against the 1828 dictionary entries.
