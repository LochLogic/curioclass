A reminder: a family has different forms with different jobs. Conceive is a verb, conception is a noun, conceivable is an adjective. In "all that our nature can conceive of sin", conceive means to imagine or form an idea of, and the 1828 entry for conception, sense 3, says "apprehension of any thing by the mind; the act of conceiving in the mind".
