# Day 4: Check a word against its sentence and a printed entry

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 3's exit goes back today with error A marked. Print the entry cards for pairs and the exit sheet (it holds the entry for grave; no one sees it before the exit). Print the entry cards: cards 1 and 2 (ocular, conception) go to pairs, and cards 3 and 4 (awful, presently) are projected for the class.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Return day 3's exit. Students sort [[s01-P4-1]] alone, one sentence a row.

**Ask.** Which sentence only reports, which implies, and which claims more than the account can show? Expect: the testimony about the pinching is reported; "little occasion to prove" implies Mather takes her guilt as settled; that Bishop herself pinched them is unproved, since witnesses reported a Shape. If many (more than a third) missed day 3's exit, rehearse one more sort together.

#### 2 min. Retrieval

Grade 9 families: students answer [[s01-R7]]. Retrieval only. A miss: show the family card.

#### 3 min. Word-family slot

Conceive, conception, conceivable, tied to grade 10's perceive: students do [[s01-P4-2]] with the family card, naming the noun and adjective and using one form about Brown.

**Ask.** What does the ending tell you? Expect: ception is a noun, able an adjective. A miss: show the perceive family on the card.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Students answer [[s01-R6]] on the staff paragraph (read on day 1, so now open), then check ocular against card 1 and say which clue helped. A miss: model infer then verify.

**Ask.** Which clue helped, the sentence or the entry? Expect: the sentence gave a trick of sight; the entry says depending on the eye.

#### 16 min. Teaching first and worked example

Teach the word-check: 1, infer from the sentence; 2, name the part of speech and a related form; 3, read the 1828 entry; 4, verify against the sentence. Students try [[s01-K4-1]] on conceive with card 2; then model with [[steps:s01-K4-1]]. Do not move on until each step is named.

Project awful (Brown: all the sounds of the benighted wilderness pealing in awful harmony): infer, then read the entry. Senses 1 and 2 both fit; the note on common people's use shows the 1828 sense is not the modern one. Project presently (Mather: they were presently struck down): at present does not fit; soon after and immediately both do, so the sentence alone cannot choose. Webster's 1828 dictionary records how words were used then; it does not settle modern meaning.

**Ask.** Which step caught your first guess out? Expect: step 4, rereading the sentence with the entry's sense.

#### 10 min. Practice

Pairs do [[s01-P4-3]] steps 1, 2 and 4 on one word from a different paragraph, then each student picks a word needed from the day's reading and does the same; step 3 stays open in the log. Begin the word log: one line a word with its source sentence (3 minutes). In the last 2 minutes, look up at most three chosen words at the projector, one at a time, from entries checked first, never invented.

#### 4 min. Week 1 words

Pairs check the eight week 1 words against context and the card, four each, and one writes a reuse sentence with [[s01-P4-4]].

#### 5 min. Exit

[[s01-X4]], alone, on the exit sheet.

### Other rooms

No devices are needed. Without a printer, read the card entries aloud twice and students copy the line they use.

### At home

**Swap.** No projector: read the entry from the card; no printer: copy the entry line.

**Skip.** The projected words and the pairs.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Sort [[s01-P4-1]] together.
  **Say.** "Is it reported, implied or unproved?" Expect: pinching testimony reported; Bishop herself pinched them, unproved.
- **8 min. Example.** Try [[s01-K4-1]], then read its answer together.
  **Say.** "Which step checks the guess?" Expect: step 4, reread the sentence with the entry's sense.
- **8 min. Practice.** Do [[s01-P4-3]] together.
- **9 min. Words.** Do [[s01-P4-4]], then add two words from the independent book to the log.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Answer [[s01-X4]] alone on the exit sheet.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: model awful only and leave presently for the log. Longer: pairs check a second week 1 word through all four steps.

### Words and supports

Infer: work out from clues. Verify: check the guess against the sentence and the entry. Part of speech: the job a word does. Entry cards: 1 ocular, 2 conception, 3 awful, 4 presently. Conceive in paragraph 59 means "imagine"; conception, sense 3, "apprehension of any thing by the mind", is the same sense. Frame: My guess is __ because the sentence says __; the entry says __, so my guess stays or changes because __. Access: large print for the entries; the entries read aloud; a scribe for the exit; a bilingual glossary except on the exit. Read-aloud help is recorded apart from independent evidence. The student page and word log stay in the folder.

### Tomorrow

Return [[s01-X4]] in day 5's warm-up, errors C, E and F; the most common takes the warm-up and the others take a card in preparation. Also in day 5's preparation part, error H: a small group works with the teacher while the rest prepare, each picking a word from the independent book. Day 5 holds a first seminar.
