# This unit in grade 11 English

## English

Our class is reading Young Goodman Brown, a story that leaves one big question open: did the forest meeting really happen, or was it a dream or a trick of the mind? We learn to tell what a story shows from what it only suggests, and to defend more than one reading with exact words. We also read a short 1693 account of a witchcraft trial and ask what an account claims and what it cannot prove, and we look up older words in a printed 1828 dictionary.

At home, ask: "What does the story actually say, and what are we guessing?" Please also keep up 20 to 30 minutes of independent reading a night. Nothing needs to be signed or sent back.
