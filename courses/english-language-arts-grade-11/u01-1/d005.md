# Day 5: Which uncertainties belong to the texts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 4's exit goes back today with errors C, E and F marked. Print the prep sheets, half-sheets and roles cards; mark six groups of five. The last sentence of the story and the first sentence of the excerpt are the exit: do not mark or discuss them. Open the answer key and the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Return day 4's exit. Count errors C, E and F; the most common takes the warm-up, the others a card at the start of preparation. Students do [[s01-P5-1]] alone: test two senses of a word against one new sentence. If many (more than a third) missed day 4's exit, do one more word together.

**Ask.** Which words of the sentence show the sense? Expect: some grave divine of the New England churches describes a minister, so grave is an adjective for a person, serious, not a burial place.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Pairs rehearse [[s01-R8]] once: paraphrase a partner before disagreeing. Unscored. A miss: model the evidence turn, "You said ..., and in the text ..." and the pair replays it.

#### 8 min. Preparation

Partners prepare two marked passages, one from Brown and one from Mather, on the prep sheet: passage, what it shows, a question that probes the evidence. Use the Two readings page and the chart. A small group (error H, if many) works with the teacher for the first 5 minutes: each picks a word from the independent book and finds its source sentence.

#### 5 min. Protocol

Groups of five choose roles from the roles card: evidence checker, questioner, synthesizer, the person who invites other views, and a timer used only as a pace cue. They agree a goal and a deadline and write both on the half-sheet.

**Ask.** What will you do if two people want the same role? Expect: agree aloud and decide together; no one assigns every role.

#### 12 min. Seminar

Student led, six groups at once: Which uncertainties belong to the texts, and which come from us? Everyone may pass. The checker records each speaker on the half-sheet. Listen to two groups for about 3 minutes each, rotating by day, and take no marks; day 5 is instruction, not scored.

#### 2 min. Step back

Each group rereads one hard clause from its exchange and says what it settled.

**Ask.** What did the clause settle, and what did it leave? Expect: it settled who is speaking; it left open whether what they report is true.

#### 10 min. Weekly paragraph

Alone, 7 minutes: [[s01-P5-2]], one paragraph for a peer, in the four look-fors: the claim answers the question; one short exact phrase from each text; stated, inferred and unproved kept apart; what stays uncertain is named. Then a peer reads it and writes one margin note against the look-fors (3 minutes). Collect it for written feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit

[[s01-X5]], alone, on paper, with the two held sentences opened.

### Other rooms

No devices are needed. Without a printer, copy the prep lines from the board; groups may be pairs plus one.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: write the prep lines in the notebook.

**Skip.** The roles, the half-sheet and the group talk.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Do [[s01-P5-1]] together.
  **Say.** "Which sense fits, and which words show it?" Expect: serious, because it describes a divine, a minister.
- **8 min. Prepare.** Choose one passage from each text, as for the prep sheet.
- **8 min. Talk.** The parent takes the other side.
  **Say.** "Where does the text say that?" Expect: a marked passage and what it shows.
- **12 min. Write.** Write [[s01-P5-2]] alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Answer [[s01-X5]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups skip the step back and the peer note stays. Longer: write the question the other group should have asked.

### Words and supports

Evidence checker: asks where the text says it. Synthesizer: says what two speakers agree and where they conflict. Probe: ask for the reasoning, not a repeat. Frame: __ says __, but __ says __, and what would settle it is __. Access: large print; the passages read aloud; the paragraph spoken to a scribe; the pages may be filled with a partner. Read-aloud help is recorded apart.

### Tomorrow

Return [[s01-X5]] in day 6's Return and repair part, errors A and L; take the more common one. Error A: two sure-sounding writers taken to prove each other right; error L: two positions listed with no link. If many (more than a third) missed it, join two attributed positions with but and name one missing piece of evidence. Day 6 also returns the paragraph with written feedback for revision on day 8.
