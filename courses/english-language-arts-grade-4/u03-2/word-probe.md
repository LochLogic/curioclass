# Private word probe

Use one word per student. Show the isolated word first and then its sentence. Read the direction from the item. Fold over or cover the other lines. Record what the child actually read and how they checked it. Allow 45 seconds per child. Give 30 seconds for the group change.

| Day | Group | Word | Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 89 | 1 | obstruction | The obstruction blocked their path. |
| 92 | 2 | instruction | The instruction showed us where to start. |
| 93 | 3 | destruction | The storm caused destruction in the park. |
| 94 | 4 | instructive | The map was instructive, so we kept it. |
| 96 | 5 | restructure | We will restructure the shelf to hold bigger books. |

On day 89, say obstruction for the numbered dictation line. Use a sentence orally if a child needs context. On day 90 return the dictation and allow a reference check in the word repair. Keep private oral results separate from the dictation. The key is the printed word beside the child's group. Accept accurate reading with an audible retry. Keep each later word private until the group is called.
