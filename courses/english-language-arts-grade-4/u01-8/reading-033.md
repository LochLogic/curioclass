# Day 33 reading

I put space after argument  
because the door seemed to wait.  
I put space after ground  
because the wedge did not move.  
You said a reader might hear it differently.  
That had not occurred to me.  
I had imagined the page  
was a set of instructions  
that every voice would obey.  

Omar read it fast.  
The door sounded angry.  
Leila read it slowly.  
The wedge sounded patient.  
Both readers used my words.  

I wanted to tell Omar  
he had read it wrong.  
Then I asked him to show me  
where the anger came from.  
He pointed to argument.  
I had put that word there myself.  

Now I am wondering  
whether I want the door to argue.  
It might simply be heavy.  
The wedge might be stubborn  
instead of patient.  
I did not expect  
a scratched piece of yellow rubber  
to make this much trouble.  
I have left the poem open  
on my desk,  
which is exactly what the wedge  
would have done with the door.
