# Day 33: Reading choices and writing choices

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-only day, inspection and exit keys. Return X32/P32 and actual rehearsal/source notes. Set six fixed tables of five with sources, discussion notes and role assignments. Keep the exit folded. Retain December 5's first 120 words and Williams paragraph 2 only. Prepare record columns for each child's actual question, answer, linked comment, role and listening review. Public talk permits passing; provide a private exchange within that child's scheduled turns.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 32's X32, P32 and rehearsal notes. If many day 32 tickets showed A or O, inspect [[s01-P33-1]]: locate the unsupported claim before opening the inspection key. One earliest repair fits these five minutes; do not add a second group. Review evidence and inference from day 18. Review day 1 roles: facilitator invites, evidence-finder locates, questioner asks, responder answers, reviewer accounts for listening. Assign one action to each child; everyone still asks and answers.

#### 5 min. Finish explanation

Use the unfinished day 32 folder for [[s01-P33-folder]]. Review the grade 3 dated-folder routine: plan, draft, feedback, revision, edit. Own drafts remain unjudged. Collect a dated copy for individual written feedback after class, ready before day 34. A missing actual source detail receives a locating note, not an invented connection supplied by the teacher.

#### 45 min. Formal discussion

Review grade 3 discussion moves: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Groups remain seated. The teacher visits six tables in order, seven-and-a-half minutes each. Use [[s01-P33-talk]] diagnostically; no score for a practiced whole explanation.

Each child has two forty-five-second allowances. In the first circuit, briefly identify a prepared source detail and ask the next child a specific follow-up. In the second, answer the received question, link to the peer's actual idea, and name what listening clarified. Five children times two turns times forty-five seconds equals seven-and-a-half minutes. Give role cues on the table; take brief notes during speech. Begin at the first table, without added introductions or movement between groups. A private exchange uses the same allowance; do not assume speech from paper.

Waiting groups quietly finish their own unjudged explanation, source notes and discussion preparation. Their listening-review slips retain ideas actually heard, not invented peer remarks. Keep actual day 20 pending components and day 26 R26 observations alongside today's notes. Paper does not prove unheard speech. Give brief written diagnostic feedback during the next independent reading slot or after class; revised preparation on day 34 makes eligible records available for day 38. If a role or component is still missing, leave it pending.

#### 5 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X33]] exactly as printed. Four minutes alone on the half slip, one to collect. No extra question or oral response. After class, allow thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes for thirty, to check source action, revision action and the supported connection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record support separately. Return this and P33 on day 37.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review scrib/script, writing, from day 31 through [[s01-W33]]. Pairs read every printed part before opening teacher-only steps. Review grade 3 dictionary checking with guide words: locate script, compare its complete spelling, and choose the writing sense. The reference check stays unjudged in the folder. Hear partners concurrently; do not promise thirty teacher hearings.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Review the taught December 5 span and Williams paragraph 2, under 200 words together. Read independently, then log an actual source location. Use remaining time to adapt the prepared paper visual/explanation for an unfamiliar second reader. Keep a copy if no already available school reader responds; mark response pending. No visitor, contact or extra performance is assumed. The teacher dates feedback on discussion notes; complete individual explanation notes after class.

### Other rooms

Use retained leaves and blank lined pages. Copy current stems without keys and fold the exit separately. A board replaces the display. A private live exchange fits the assigned allowance.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy current stems if printing is unavailable. Use retained leaves and a spelling reference. The adult takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read the returned note. Try [[s01-P33-1]], then check together.
  **Say.** "Which claim lacks support?" Expect: the claim about winning a race.
- **5 min. Finish.** Complete [[s01-P33-folder]] on lined paper. Keep the source location.
- **10 min. Discuss.** Exchange one actual source question and answer. Keep notes. The adult writes one specific feedback note afterward.
- **10 min. Read.** Reread the taught leaves. Read [[s01-W33]] and check its spelling. Keep a paper version for another reader.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X33]]. Your child responds alone; keep the slip for day 37.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: leave the second-reader copy unfinished; preserve all discussion allowances and the exit. Longer: inspect whether a visual clarifies the source connection rather than adding decoration.

### Words and supports

Review source location, evidence, inference, presentation, visual and revision from earlier grade 4 days. Review grade 3 discussion, folder and dictionary routines. Enlarge print, provide signing or content scribing, and record help. Independent reading remains the child's. No personal loss story is requested.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 receives day 33's written presentation feedback and discussion notes for independent revision. Error S means recopying without a meaningful response. Do not reteach during collection. X33 errors A, unsupported praise, and O, claimed copying absent from the texts, wait for day 37 Feedback. Unheard components stay pending.
