# Day 35: Read an unfamiliar poem and story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only administration and assessment keys. Release only Dust of Snow and The Lunch Table today. Preserve stanzas; number poem lines 1 to 8 and story paragraphs at The folded, Lend a hand, Before I lifted. Print rued means regretted. Assign the first fifteen conferences. Retrieve actual ten-day records and taught rereading leaves.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch

Keep day 34's assessment for day 37; no answers today. Give the new leaves and directions without interpretation. No personal loss comparison is requested.

#### 25 min. Reading collection

Alone: theme/summary seven minutes; encounter six; comparisons four; sayings four; relationships four. Include approximately two minutes for both whole readings. Use Side 1 then Side 2. The ten separate stems share these allocations. Collect originals. [[s01-A35-range]] uses dated understanding across prose, drama and poetry; this short task cannot certify reading-band mastery.

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Review the taught December 5 span, first 120 words. Children reread and log an actual location. No reserved answers or new assessment case practice.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Children file dated work and quietly review their own collection completeness. Prepare individual written component notes after class for day 37. No answer teaching or thirty conferences here. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for each component; note help separately. Judgment follows independent correction on day 38.

#### 30 min. Reading conferences

Hear only the first fifteen, two minutes each. Review grade 3 rereading with phrasing feedback. Use taught December 5's first sixty words and The Missing Line first-person paragraph 1. Reuse matching individual ten-day entries. [[s01-A35-RF-purpose]], [[s01-A35-RF-phrasing]] and [[s01-A35-RF-confirm]] record only actual purpose/meaning, accurate expressive successive prose/poetry readings and letter/context confirmation. Hear missing short components; do not force four whole readings into two minutes. Unheard parts stay pending. Others independently review familiar un- with [[s01-W35]] and consult the teacher-only key afterward; no simultaneous repair group.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact prompts and the two whole texts. Preserve reference numbers. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy current leaves and prompts. The adult replaces the listening teacher.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reading.

- **25 min. Assessment reading.** Give both new leaves and the student page. Your child answers alone. Keep the keys covered.
- **5 min. File.** Keep originals for written feedback before day 37. Avoid answer coaching.
- **10 min. Reread.** Hear the taught December and prose spans twice as needed. Record only actual reading. Try [[s01-W35]], then check together.
  **Say.** "What does un- do here?" Expect: it reverses rolling.
- **5 min. Read.** Return to the taught December span and keep a dated log.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: finish filing later. Longer: familiar independent reading. Preserve collection and conference allowances.

### Words and supports

Review grade 4 theme, summary, comparison and word relationships. Review grade 3 rereading. Enlarge print; record scribing or direction help. Independent reading stays the child's.

### Tomorrow

Day 36's assessment collects a new informational text and retained portfolio, then hears the other fifteen. Day 35's items wait for day 37: B/C theme versus topic/opinion; D unsupported description; Y/Z literal readings; AA/AJ word relationship or degree. Actual RF feedback joins that return.
