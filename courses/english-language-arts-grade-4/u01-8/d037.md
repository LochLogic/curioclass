# Day 37: Use feedback and prepare a clear explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return X33/P33, days 34 to 36 reading originals and written component feedback, actual reading observations, and day 36's eligible essay/presentation judgments. Count earliest unmet letters. Select the four most frequent, ties by register order; assign four five-minute groups. Mark each child's repair choice. Set taught December, story and scene leaves out separately from returned assessments. Open teacher-only day and inspection keys. Select six missing speech components and five due rereaders.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Feedback

Return s01-X33 and s01-P33-1 from day 33 with prepared notes. Hand back day 34's assessment, day 35's assessment and day 36's assessment, including the audit results. Use [[s01-R37-priority]] on the response sheet to identify one priority. This is one shared five-minute return. Review grade 3 folder tracing: keep original, note and later revision together. Prioritize one earliest unmet component per child. If many X33 tickets showed A or O, route the unsupported-claim group first. Actual RF and day 36 audit notes use the same return; no thirty conferences here.

#### 20 min. Response groups

Four groups receive five teacher minutes each on their selected letter, using a different taught case. Others use assigned written moves in pairs, then the teacher-only day key. Each child completes only the marked choices; do not make everyone finish the menu. All repair choices except P37 stay in the folder, uncollected. The teacher addresses the precise selected fault, not every fault in its family.

Routing: A/O use [[s01-F37-source]]; B/C/D use [[s01-F37-meaning]] to distinguish a topic from an event-supported claim. E uses [[s01-F37-form]]; F uses [[s01-F37-teller]]; G uses [[s01-F37-direction]]. Review these earlier grade 4 distinctions. G locates one direction in the taught scene without staging a mismatch or completing a comparison.

J/K/L/M/AH/AI/AK use [[s01-F37-role]], followed by review of the actual assigned role: invite, locate, ask, answer, review. Point to the preceding idea, wait for completion, and keep a note of what listening changed. Do not supply a scripted full exchange. H reviews [[s01-W37-review]] with whole-word checking; I/AM/AN mark phrases in the familiar December span, then reread for meaning, with no new individual hearing here.

N/P use [[s01-F37-link]] and group only notes about that same hinge topic. Q/S/AE/AB use [[s01-P37-1]]: a precise object label changes meaning, unlike neat tracing or decoration. R uses [[s01-F37-end]]. T/U use [[s01-F37-sentence]], locating the dependent clause and complete thought. V/AF inspect the capital B in option B's Because; AG/W inspect the paired quotation marks and introducing comma in The Missing Line's paragraph 2. X/AL review grade 3 dictionary checking against the actual script entry, letter by letter. AD orders actual dated folder artifacts. Y/Z review a comparison in taught December 5 through its surrounding lines; AA/AJ use [[s01-F37-degree]] to separate an opposite feeling from a stronger positive feeling. These are feature inspections, not completed assessment answers.

**Ask.** Which supplied response changes the information? Use [[s01-P37-1]] before opening its inspection key. Expect: naming hinge identifies the object. Return that judged choice day 38; own draft work remains unjudged. If a child's additional fault cannot receive instruction in this window, record it pending; do not pretend a generic move repaired every need.

#### 25 min. Independent correction

Alone, use [[s01-R37-folder]] on response sheets beside original day 34/35/36 work and X33. Address the written note using actual sources, without a displayed assessment sample answer. Keep changed wording and dates visible for judgment after day 38. Children who finish one priority may correct another returned component; unfinished corrections stay pending. Other minutes clarify the own explanation and paper visual, unjudged. No new essay or convention replacement is requested.

#### 10 min. Rehearsal

Review day 31's presentation and clarifying visual. Concurrent pairs use [[s01-R37-talk]]. Prepare the already revised version, no new scored draft. Hear at most six missing spoken components at forty-five seconds each, four-and-a-half minutes within ten. The rest use peer notes; paper does not prove speech. Retain actual source/role/question/answer observations with dates and support for day 38. Permit passing and a private teacher exchange within the assigned allowance. No simultaneous repair group.

#### 10 min. Word work

After assessment collection, teach tract as pulling through [[s01-W37]]. Box tract in each printed word, then read the whole words. A tractor pulls; traction concerns a grip that allows pulling without slipping. The root helps with these meanings, but does not settle every sense of every longer word. Pairs read before seeing teacher-only steps.

Review grade 3 pre-, before, and mis-, wrongly, through [[s01-W37-affix]]. Read both whole words and match meanings before opening the key. Consult the actual dictionary entry for doubtful spelling. New tract learning is not retroactive evidence on earlier assessments.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading with phrasing feedback and actual missing-component observations. Keep expression meaningful, without a speed target. Use December 5's first sixty words and The Missing Line first-person paragraph 1, under 110 words together. Partners state purpose, reread successively, and check letters plus context. Hear only five due readers at two minutes each, ten within fifteen; reuse matching ten-day evidence. Record actual missing purpose, accuracy, expression and confirmation components. Unheard parts stay pending. No repair group during teacher listening.

#### 5 min. Independent reading

Use the taught December span and log. Date and retain the prepared folder for day 38's writing-over-time audit; any new writing stays unscored. Keep the revised explanation and visual ready. No fresh exit is added.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy current choices without keys. Retain taught leaves, assessment originals and blank correction paper. A board replaces the display. Private speech uses the same scheduled allowance.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy assigned choices and use lined paper for the response sheet. The adult takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Group rotations and public sharing.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Lay out originals and written notes. Choose one priority.
- **5 min. Repair.** Try [[s01-P37-1]], then check together.
  **Say.** "Which response names the object?" Expect: change thing to hinge.
- **10 min. Correct.** Use [[s01-R37-folder]] alone with the original sources. Keep both versions.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Hear the prepared explanation with its visual. Give one specific listening note.
- **5 min. Word work.** Try [[s01-W37]] and [[s01-W37-affix]], then read their meanings together.
- **5 min. Reread.** Use the familiar December and prose spans. Keep the prepared folder for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: correct only the priority component, recording others pending. Preserve groups and the individual preparation sequence. Longer: correct the next returned component independently; no new scored performance.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, inference, theme, narrator, direction, revision and visual. Review grade 3 discussion, dictionary, folder and rereading routines. New root: tract, pulling. Enlarge print; accept signing or content scribing and record support. Independent decoding remains the child's. No personal loss disclosure is requested.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 uses the prepared version and actual eligible discussion records. Bring original feedback and day 37's independent corrections. Return P37 in Feedback; finalize corrected reading components after class. Missing oral evidence remains pending unless one of the six scheduled components can be heard. No extra writing sitting or repeated full performance is promised.
