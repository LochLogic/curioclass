# Day 36: Read information and retain the writing trail

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only administration and assessment keys. Separate the complete Two Ways to Find a Poem Text leaf, preserving paragraph numbers. Prepare the other fifteen readers and actual ten-day records. Gather day 27 revised essays/convention slips with day 26 written feedback, and day 34 revisions with day 33 feedback. Keep all teacher audit rows off student copies. Teacher keys: assessment-key-05.md, assessment-key-06.md, assessment-key-09.md, assessment-key-10.md, assessment-key-11.md, assessment-key-12.md, assessment-key-13.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch

Keep day 35's assessment for day 37. Release today's new informational leaf and directions only. This is an invented system, not a real library description.

#### 15 min. Informational collection

Alone, use [[s01-A36a-fact]] and [[s01-A36a-infer]] within ten minutes; [[s01-A36a-sentence]] gets five. Include about one reading minute. Retain originals for day 37 feedback, independent correction, and day 38 judgment. These sample stated information, supported inference and sentence contribution; no full reading-band conclusion.

#### 10 min. Folder submission

Review the grade 3 dated-folder trace. Children submit actual day 27 eligible revisions, convention slips, day 34 presentation revision, discussion notes, source tags and visual. No new essay or scored convention writing. Audit after class using teacher-only components:

[[s01-A36b-topic]] [[s01-A36b-groups]] [[s01-A36b-develop]] [[s01-A36b-links]] [[s01-A36b-precise]] [[s01-A36b-close]] [[s01-A36b-audience]] [[s01-A36b-process]]

[[s01-A36b-sentence]] [[s01-A36b-fragment]] [[s01-A36b-runon]] [[s01-A36b-name]] [[s01-A36b-opening]] [[s01-A36b-speech-comma]] [[s01-A36b-speech-marks]] [[s01-A36b-quote-comma]] [[s01-A36b-quote-marks]] [[s01-A36b-reference]] [[s01-A36b-spelling]]. The retained day 34 presentation revision, s01-A34a, has no additional sitting. Missing dated feedback/revision means pending evidence, never invented completion.

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Review December 5's taught first 120 words. Children read, log the actual location and date, then continue familiar leaves. Keep actual breadth/support evidence without claiming full-band mastery.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Quiet filing and completeness review. Prepare individual component notes after class for day 37. No answer coaching. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen, recording support separately. Return audit results alongside reading feedback tomorrow.

#### 30 min. Reading conferences

Hear the other fifteen, two minutes each. Review grade 3 rereading with phrasing feedback. Use December 5's first sixty words and The Missing Line first-person paragraph 1. Reuse matching ten-day evidence. [[s01-A36-RF-purpose]], [[s01-A36-RF-phrasing]] and [[s01-A36-RF-confirm]] record only actual meaning, expressive accurate successive readings, and letter/context confirmation. Hear missing short components; unobserved parts stay pending. Others review familiar dis- through [[s01-W36]], consulting the teacher-only key afterward. No simultaneous repair group or new tract teaching.

### Other rooms

Copy current prompts and both numbered paragraphs. Use retained folder work. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy the new leaf and prompts. The adult hears missing reading components.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reading.

- **15 min. Assessment reading.** Give the new leaf and its three prompts. Your child works alone; keep keys covered.
- **10 min. Folder.** Gather actual revised essay, convention slips, presentation and their earlier written notes. Keep dates. Add no replacement essay.
- **10 min. Reread.** Hear the familiar December and prose spans. Keep observations of actual reading. Try [[s01-W36]], then check together.
  **Say.** "What does dis- mean here?" Expect: a negative meaning, not liking.
- **10 min. Read and file.** Use taught leaves and the dated log. Prepare a written component note for day 37 without teaching answers.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: finish filing later. Longer: familiar reading. Preserve every collection and listening allowance.

### Words and supports

Review grade 4 fact, inference, paragraph and source location; grade 3 folder/rereading routines. Enlarge print and record help. Independent reading stays the child's.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 responds to days 34 to 36 assessments, X33 and audit feedback. Day 36's items O/A need accurate facts/support; sentence contribution needs both paragraphs. Prioritize one component, retain originals, then revise independently. Familiar-pattern practice continues; tract is taught only after collection.
