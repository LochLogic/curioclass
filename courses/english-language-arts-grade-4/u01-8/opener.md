# Section 1.8 opener

## What the section does

Revise explanations, read cold cases, and present eligible prepared work. Days 33 to 38, ninety minutes daily.

## Ideas and continuity

Explicit means directly stated in the source. Inference means an unstated conclusion supported by source details. Biography is factual life writing, distinct from a poem speaker. Establish these meanings independently because earlier grade 4 plans are drafts. Reader, purpose and form determine information a new reader needs. Keep supporting information beside the idea it explains.

Review grade 3: heading means the main idea of a group of notes, saying what the group is for. Review the dictionary check using guide words. Review peer look-for and teacher note: a partner checks one named feature; the writer moves, adds or explains a fact. Review the folder trace: plan, draft, feedback, revision, edit. Reuse these routines with current work.

Essay structure is opening, two aspect sections, and a related ending. Inspect one faulty component at a time. Sentence-combining preserves complete thoughts when joining or separating clauses. Own drafting remains unjudged until designated collection; judged practice chooses or repairs a different supplied component.

## Word work

Day 20 establishes schwa as an unstressed vowel sound whose spelling must be checked, using pencil. Day 22 establishes stress as a syllable given greater emphasis: photograph/photography shifts stress; nation/national does not. Day 31 teaches scrib/script as writing; day 37 teaches tract as pulling through tractor/traction, after assessment collection. Familiar patterns and affixes are retrieved with a brief explanation when prior teaching is uncertain. No new Skills Block cycle is claimed. Keep day 29 probe words off earlier practice.

## Errors and response

- A: An inference has no supporting detail. Repair: More than 10 of 30: Trace the inference back to one stated detail. One five-minute repair on a different taught case; prioritize earliest component.
- E: Page shape alone determines literary form. Repair: More than 10 of 30: Compare the actual structural features of the three forms. One five-minute repair on a different taught case; prioritize earliest component.
- H: A familiar word is substituted without reading the whole printed word. Repair: More than 10 of 30: Read every syllable and word part, then reread the sentence to check the substitution. One five-minute repair on a different taught case; prioritize earliest component.
- O: Claimed evidence is not in the text. Repair: More than 10 of 30: Check each claimed fact against its source location. One five-minute repair on a different taught case; prioritize earliest component.
- S: Revision is treated as neat recopying. Repair: More than 10 of 30: Make one meaning-changing response to written feedback. One five-minute repair on a different taught case; prioritize earliest component.

Return papers on dates in handbacks.csv. One earliest need receives five Opening minutes; others use one workshop group or independent choices. Days 18 and 28 reserve ten Return and repair minutes: five instruction, five correction. No teacher repair group runs during listening. Give written individual draft feedback after class, ready before revision.

## Before class and materials

Print authorized reading leaves, current inspection/exit cards, source tags, feedback sheet, lined folder pages, and record continuation. Teacher-only pieces: inspection-key.md and exit-key.md; section 1.6 also uses convention-key.md. Read keys before class; keep them off student copies. Use pencils and dictionaries or a printed reference. A board replaces a projector. Paper figures replace performers. No visitor or recording is required.

## Evidence, access and care

Use one component scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Record language help separately from content errors. Unheard behavior stays blank. Public talk permits passing; a private exchange fits the scheduled turn. No personal loss, religion or family experience is requested. Preview pet loss in Ben's notebook, Frost's father's death, Rossetti's illness/death and religion, Hughes's Black identity, and Dickinson's publication without permission when those readings occur. No racial role play or personal parallels. Offer an ordinary object for original writing. A student eighteen or older receives the family note and makes their own choices under school rules.

Whole profiles run 380 to 401 words, about four reading minutes. Print across leaves with at most 350 words each; preserve whole poems. Do not distribute manuscripts containing reserved companions. Day 34 uses The Last Token; day 35 first releases Dust of Snow and The Lunch Table; day 36 uses Two Ways to Find a Poem. Earlier replacement companions remain unused.

At home, use thirty-five minutes: five retrieve, ten read, ten inspect, five respond, five reread. The adult replaces the partner, keeps dated work and describes help without scores. Use assigned leaves only. A second-audience copy stays in the folder if no already available reader responds; mark response pending.
