# Day 38: Share the prepared poem and explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only administration and assessment keys. Fix six groups of five and the nearby private-exchange position. Set out prepared versions, visuals and actual day 33/37 discussion records after feedback/revised preparation. Select at most six missing spoken components. Keep teacher-only keys separate. No visitor or recording is required. Teacher keys: assessment-key-03.md, assessment-key-13.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch

Receive day 37's revised work and prepared folder. Use the prepared version only; no last-minute changes are judged. Public sharing permits passing; a private teacher exchange uses the scheduled minute. No family-loss disclosure is requested.

#### 30 min. Presentations

Six seated groups of five, five minutes each, one minute per child including poem, explanation and visual. The teacher observes sequentially using [[s01-A38a-report]], [[s01-A38a-pace]], [[s01-A38a-clear]] and [[s01-A38a-visual]] within the same turn. Retain actual evidence/support. Waiting children inspect taught whole XXII through [[s01-P38-1]], then read familiar leaves quietly. No simultaneous repair group or additional full performance.

#### 10 min. Discussion

Reuse eligible actual day 33 exchanges after feedback and revised preparation. Teacher-only components: [[s01-A38b-prep]], [[s01-A38b-turn]], [[s01-A38b-role]], [[s01-A38b-question]], [[s01-A38b-answer]], [[s01-A38b-link]], [[s01-A38b-review]]. Hear at most six missing forty-five-second components, four-and-a-half minutes within ten. Others discuss concurrently and complete listening notes. Paper never proves unheard speech; missing evidence stays pending. Review the familiar roles and linked-question routine without coaching assessed answers.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Return P37 with day 37's notes. Share brief component next steps privately in writing. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help separately. Review the grade 3 dated folder and eligible revision audit for writing over time. Collect prepared versions and listening notes at the end of Feedback. [[s01-A38c]] audits the retained folder after class, without new writing. Finalize eligible day 34/35/36 independent corrections after class, alongside original attempts and feedback. No thirty conferences inside this slot.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review familiar re-, again, through [[s01-W38]]. Pairs read every part, then consult the teacher-only key. Review grade 3 dictionary checking; reference spelling stays unjudged. No new root or dictation.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Review December 5's first 120 words and Williams paragraph 2, under 200 words together. Read and log actual locations. Finish the second-reader paper adaptation. If no already available reader responds, keep it and mark response pending. No contacts or extra performance are promised. Collect student pages and dated folders at the end of Independent reading.

### Other rooms

Use paper visuals, copied current stems and retained taught leaves. Private presentation fits the assigned minute. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy current stems and use a paper visual. The adult is the listener.

**Skip.** Group rotations and public sharing.

- **10 min. Assessment sharing.** Hear the prepared poem/explanation once, including its visual. Use the prepared version; keep actual listening notes.
- **5 min. Discussion.** Ask about one actual source detail. Keep what became clearer.
  **Say.** "Which reading detail shaped this choice?" Expect: an actual source detail connected to a specific own-poem choice.
- **10 min. Folder.** Keep original, written feedback, revision and dated reading work together. Give a written next step.
- **5 min. Word work.** Try [[s01-W38]], then check together.
- **15 min. Read.** Use taught leaves. Try [[s01-P38-1]], then check together. Keep the second-reader copy; another listener is optional.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: finish the second-reader copy later. Longer: familiar independent reading. Preserve all observation allowances.

### Words and supports

Review grade 4 evidence, presentation and clarifying visual; grade 3 discussion, folder and dictionary routines. Enlarge print, allow signing/content scribing, and record help. Independent reading remains the child's.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 launches animal defenses. Return day 38's finalized component/support record, dated folder and P38 during Notebook. Missing components remain pending. Keep the written next step; no added repair lesson or extra oral turn is promised.
