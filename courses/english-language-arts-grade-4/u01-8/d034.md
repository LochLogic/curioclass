# Day 34: Read a new case and revise from feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only administration and assessment keys. Separate four The Last Token Text leaves; exclude teacher notes. Prepare paper figures and rehearse only the fixed teacher actions privately. Return day 33's individual written explanation feedback and discussion notes. Select five due readers and eligible ten-day records. Keep the taught rereading leaves separate from the cold reading. Set out lined paper and actual prepared versions.

### The day

#### 20 min. Cold reading collection

Alone, children first see all four The Last Token versions and the exact prompts. Four shared allocations include reading: action/inference four minutes; form six; narrator access four; oral/staging six. The whole set takes about two reading minutes, included. Read first-person prose exactly, stressing last token and pausing before stepped out. With paper figures, follow standing/gripping direction 1; slide the token before Ivo finishes, contrary to direction 2. Follow everything else. No child must act.

Use [[s01-A34b-action]], [[s01-A34b-infer]], [[s01-A34b-form]], [[s01-A34b-narrate]], [[s01-A34b-oral]] and [[s01-A34b-stage]]. No hints or answer discussion. Retain all pages. [[s01-A34b-evidence]] audits actual references in those responses, without another sitting. These sample RL.4.1, RL.4.5, RL.4.6, RL.4.7 and W.4.9a separately. No full-standard conclusion follows one case.

#### 25 min. Presentation collection

Receive day 33's explanation version, written feedback and diagnostic discussion notes. Alone, complete [[s01-A34a]]. Revise the actual explanation, source connection and preparation in response to that note. Keep original, feedback, revision and visual together; date the response. Error S is neat recopying without meaning change. Do not coach a new answer. The eligible revision is judged in day 36's retained-work audit, not during today's writing.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Quietly file all versions and identify unfinished components without discussing answers. The teacher checks collection completeness, not thirty individual conferences. Prepare component-specific written reading notes after class for day 37 correction. Day 33's X33 remains held for day 37. Use the single component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help separately. This feedback slot contains no reteaching.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review scrib/script from day 31 through [[s01-W34]]. Pairs try first, then consult the teacher-only day key. Review grade 3 dictionary checking with guide words. Compare every letter in the actual inscription entry; reference work stays unjudged in the folder. Do not introduce a new root.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Review day 22's complete-thought boundary with [[s01-K34]]. Choose before opening the teacher-only key. A dependent although clause needs a complete thought. This supplied choice is unjudged retrieval, not a new convention sample for the portfolio.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading with feedback on phrases and expression, without a speed target. Use December 5's first sixty words and The Missing Line first-person paragraph 1, under 110 words together. Partners state purpose and reread successively, checking letters plus context.

Hear five due children at two minutes each, ten within fifteen. Reuse matching individual ten-day evidence; hear only missing components. [[s01-A34-RF-purpose]], [[s01-A34-RF-phrasing]] and [[s01-A34-RF-confirm]] remain teacher observation rows. Record actual passage meaning, accuracy, phrasing, successive readings and confirmation. No staged error or forced four whole readings. Missing evidence stays pending. No repair group runs during listening.

### Other rooms

Copy exact current prompts and authorized leaves. Use paper figures for the fixed performance. Retain paragraphs, line numbers and scene directions. The assessment requires no device or outside recording.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy current prompts and leaves. Fold paper figures. The adult supplies the fixed performance from teacher-only administration directions.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.

- **20 min. Assessment reading.** Give the four new leaves and items 1 to 6. Include the prescribed oral reading and staging. Your child responds alone; keep answers covered.
- **10 min. Revision.** Use [[s01-A34a]] with yesterday's written note. Keep both versions and the visual.
- **5 min. Retrieve.** Try [[s01-W34]] and [[s01-K34]], then check together.
  **Say.** "Which choice completes the thought?" Expect: B adds we can see.
- **5 min. Reread.** Use the taught December and prose spans. Describe only reading actually heard. Keep dated work for day 37 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: finish quiet filing later; keep every collection allocation. Longer: read familiar leaves independently, without rehearsing tomorrow's reserved texts.

### Words and supports

Review source location, inference, verse, narrator, dialogue and directions from earlier grade 4 days. Review grade 3 dictionary and rereading routines. Enlarge print or scribe content; record help. Do not read independent-reading words for the child. Avoid personal parallels.

### Tomorrow

Day 35's assessment uses a new poem and story plus the first fifteen reading conferences. Retain day 34's items for day 37 Feedback and independent correction: A/O unsupported or absent evidence, E form by print alone, F narrator as author, G direction mismatch. Do not rehearse these answers tomorrow. RF notes join the same return.
