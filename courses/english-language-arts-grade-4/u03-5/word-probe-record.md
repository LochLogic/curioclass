# Individual word probe record

Write the group and date. Ask each reader to read both words alone. Then show the sentences one at a time. Record what you actually hear. Allow 45 seconds per child and 30 seconds for the group handover. Six readers per group fit in five minutes. Give each later group a fresh pair in its existing word block.
