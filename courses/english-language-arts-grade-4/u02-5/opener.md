# Section 2.5 opener: Plan two story paths

## Teach how a branch works

The situation establishes who is where when the problem opens. The narrator is the teller of a story. A branch is one route after a choice. A transition relates events in time, a sensory detail helps the reader imagine a sight, sound or touch, and a resolution gives an outcome connected to the problem.

Use an incomplete arrow to introduce direction. Hold back the whole story until the independent reading on day 63. Give ten minutes for both routes and five minutes for the half-slip exit. Return the exit on day 64. Retrieve the narrative arc as the plan card with a problem and a turn added, then let children add a fork to their folder. Retrieve reader, purpose and form as the rhetorical situation. Keep personal maps and plans as dated folder work.

## Connect research to a choice

Separate the factual introduction from the fictional story. Children use research to keep the animal's action plausible. Inspect one unsupported action, unclear phrase, event dependency or unrelated outcome at a time. For error AA, trace what happens before the next event. For error AK, compare the action with a located source. For error Q, check how much the source lets the writer claim. For errors AB, W and AJ, find a vague detail, disconnected ending or missing reason in a different fragment.

Keep wildlife undisturbed. Read about defenses and use diagrams or notes. Children can pass during public talk and respond privately. Hear six short planning responses and collect written marks from the other 24. On day 68, use six groups of five for 7½ minutes each. Each child has up to 45 seconds for a prepared detail and linked question, then 30 seconds for a linked answer. Keep 1¼ minutes per group for roles, notes and transition. One child starts turns, one finds the passage and one asks for clarification. Rotate the jobs. Record what each child actually says and does.

## Before class

Set out the story, saved animal passages, notebooks, fork maps and half slips. Keep the generated answer keys on the teacher table. Copy only the numbered dictation blanks for day 66. Set out the discussion turn record for day 68. Use the unit record for scoring. On days 64 and 67, prepare one taught prose segment and the whole verse for partner rereading. Use saved copies if a live page fails and plain paper for the map if copies run out.
