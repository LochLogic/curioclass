## Text

### Greek hero in a Roman tale: Heracles and Cacus

While Heracles slept, Cacus stole some of his cattle. Cacus dragged them backward into a cave. The footprints appeared to lead away from it. Heracles followed the misleading tracks, but the search was fruitless. The cattle remained hidden.

Then the rest of the herd passed the cave. The stolen animals called out from inside. Heracles heard them and discovered their hiding place. Even a hero famous for Herculean strength needed a clue. The footprints could not give him that clue.

### Akan tradition: Anansi and the Wisdom Pot

Anansi wanted to keep all wisdom for himself. He gathered it into a pot. He tried to hide it high in a tree. The pot hung in front of him and blocked his climb.

His son Kweku Tsin watched him struggle. At last, the boy suggested hanging the pot behind him. Anansi thought his pot held all wisdom. Yet his son had supplied advice the pot had not given him.

Angry, Anansi threw the pot down. It broke on a rock, and wisdom spread throughout the world.

### Japanese tradition: The Sparrow Gifts

A kind old man visited a fairy sparrow he had cared for. As he left, she offered him a large box or a small one. He chose the small box because he could carry it more easily. At home he discovered treasure inside.

His wife wanted more and went to demand the larger box. She left without thanking the sparrow. Expecting greater riches, she opened it on the road. Frightening creatures sprang out, and she ran home in fear. The larger gift had disappointed her greedy expectation.
