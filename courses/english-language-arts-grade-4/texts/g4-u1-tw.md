## Text

### Paragraph 1

In this invented reading room, poem cards have two labels. One label names a subject, such as journeys or weather. The other names a feature, such as repeated sounds. A poem about rain may have a weather label and a repeated-sounds label.

### Paragraph 2

A paper list records every title beside both labels. A visitor can begin with a subject or a feature. The list points to the same card from either starting place. Each card stays in one numbered pocket. The list gives its pocket number.
