## Teacher notes

All situations are invented. Point separately to baker, baker, guest, basket, roll, kitchen, time, reason. Whom receives welcomes; whose signals possession. Where, when and why introduce place, time and reason clauses. These are supplied sentences, never sentence-pair joining practice. For an isolated antecedent error, inspect the claim that who points to apron; it points to baker.
