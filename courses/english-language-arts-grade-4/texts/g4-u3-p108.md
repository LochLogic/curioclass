### Morning Opening Hours

This example and its arrival log are invented for practice.

Publish morning opening hours for the lock in this example. Its arrival log records three boats before noon and none during the afternoon. The log covers just one day.

Morning service would let those carriers pass when they actually arrive. They would not have to wait for an afternoon opening. That pattern supports my proposal. But a single day cannot establish the best hours for a whole season.
