## Text

### Cast

Ben: a student with an unfinished poem.
Mina: a classmate.

### Setting

A low step outside a classroom doorway. Voices and footsteps can be heard from the hall.

### Scene

[Ben sits on the low step with his poem face down. Mina approaches and stops beside him.]

BEN: I cannot find my ending.

[Mina points toward the doorway. She leaves Ben's paper in his hands.]

MINA: What can you hear?

[Ben stays still. From the hallway come a quick shuffle, a pause, then several heavy steps.]

BEN: Shoes. Some quick ones. Some heavy ones.

[Mina waits quietly. Ben turns over his paper and writes.]

BEN: The hallway wears a coat of footsteps.

[Ben lifts the poem so Mina can read it. Mina smiles.]

MINA: I can hear that coat.

BEN: I walked through it this morning. I did not hear it then.

[They remain beside the step as the sounds of the hall continue.]
