## Reading notes

Meaning: an ordinary object receives concentrated attention; what depends on it remains unstated. Structure: four couplets divide wheelbarrow and rainwater. Language: glazed suggests a shiny coating. Knowledge and support: describe a wheelbarrow, compare red and white, and read across breaks. Preserve lower case and absence of end punctuation.

so much depends  
upon  

a red wheel  
barrow
