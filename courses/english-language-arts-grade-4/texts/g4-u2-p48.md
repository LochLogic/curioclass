## Text

### Paragraph 1

First a nearby animal touches the crab. Next the crab pulls back into its empty snail shell. Finally, only its claw tips remain visible.

### Paragraph 2

The crab's rear body is soft. The discarded shell surrounds that body part. Because of this covering, a hard barrier separates the soft body from a predator.

### Paragraph 3: listening transcript

The shell gives a hard covering, while withdrawal moves exposed parts farther inside. Covering and movement protect differently, but work together.

### Paragraph 4

A growing crab becomes too large for its shelter. Finding a larger empty shell solves the problem of a shelter that no longer fits.
