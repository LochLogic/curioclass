At rehearsal, Imani could not remember the storyteller's ending. Last week, the visitor had spoken without notes. Theo opened the class recording. They heard the ending and a pause they had forgotten. Imani wrote what the pause helped her notice. Theo saved a second copy in the class folder. On Friday, another group listened. Their ending sounded different from Imani's, so they returned to the recording together. They kept both versions of their notes beside it.
