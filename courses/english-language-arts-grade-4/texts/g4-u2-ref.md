## Text

### Card 1: word parts

Photo means light.
Graph means write or record.
Tele means far.
Photograph combines photo and graph.

### Card 2: point

Point (POYNT) can mean a sharp tip or a main idea.

### Card 3: hinge

Hinge (HINJ): a joint allowing a part to turn.

### Card 4: exposed

Exposed (ik-SPOHZD): uncovered here.

### Card 5: photograph

Photograph (FOH-tuh-graf): a picture recorded with light.
