### Information for Arrivals

This is an invented modern planning model, not an actual 1817 proposal.

Include a public information desk in canal-town plans. Imagine a newcomer reaching town with a cart. The newcomer does not know which landing accepts farm goods. Asking at several buildings takes time.

One shared desk could give directions to the landings. It could also display notices of boat departures. The town would have to pay someone to keep those notices current. An empty desk would not help.

A shared location could serve several landings instead of staffing each separately.
