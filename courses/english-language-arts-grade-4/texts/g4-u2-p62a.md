## Text

A Texas horned lizard can be difficult to notice on bare ground. Its patterned skin may look like the soil around it. Some populations have yellowish coloring; others are reddish or dark brown. Camouflage means blending with the surroundings. A predator may overlook a lizard whose colors resemble the ground. This protection depends on the background. The same colors need not hide it everywhere.
