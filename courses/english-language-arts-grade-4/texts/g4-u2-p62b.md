## Text

Color is only part of the Texas horned lizard's protection. Its broad body is flattened. When the lizard lies against the earth, it makes a small shadow. Spines along its sides also interrupt the outline of its body. For this low-shadow position, the lizard must lie close to the ground. Shape and position can make it harder for a predator to notice the animal.
