## Text

### Paragraph 1

An adult woodland box turtle has an upper shell and a lower shell. The lower shell has a hinge. A hinge is a joint that allows a part to move. When the turtle draws in its head and legs, the lower shell can move. It can close much of the opening. A hard covering and a movable part work together.

### Paragraph 2

The shell's markings provide a different kind of help. Yellow and orange patches can blend with sunlight and shadow on the forest floor. Protection may involve being hard to notice as well as being hard to reach. A photograph can record markings, but it cannot alone explain how the hinge moves.
