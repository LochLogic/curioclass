# Day 9: Read new texts independently

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print N9, December 20's whole The Tray with eight numbered lines; P9, its whole comparison paragraph; and S9, its whole scene. Print each once on the separate reading copy. Keep these covered until Launch. Prepare teacher-only assessment-key.md and day answer key. Gather Hope revisions and retained reading observations. Set aside The Missing Line's taught first paragraph and first verse stanza for later rereading.

### The day

#### 10 min. Launch

Distribute new N9/P9/S9 now, sources open. Explain response spaces and private work. Short answers use two lines; theme and summary have larger boxes. Reading occurs within item times. No hints, interpretation teaching or day 8 error E repair today.

#### 35 min. Written check

Alone: D9a, [[s01-A09a-action]] and [[s01-A09a-infer]], eight minutes; D9b, [[s01-A09b-theme]] and [[s01-A09b-summary]], ten; D9c, [[s01-A09c]], twelve; D9d, [[s01-A09d]], five. Collect unchanged first attempts. Each response samples only its named component, not whole-standard mastery.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Scan thirty papers at twenty seconds each, ten minutes, writing an unmet-component note. Children use [[s01-P9-4]] in the remaining five. No supplied answer, reteach or scored correction. Return finalized Hope revisions privately: thirty times fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes before class. Keep first attempts and notes for day 10.

#### 10 min. Word work

Use [[s01-P9-1]] silently, review of days 5 and 6's familiar chunks and dictionary spelling. Write familiar practice in notebooks. Check against references; work stays in folders. No new teaching.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Use [[s01-P9-2]], review of day 4's sentence beginnings and familiar name capitals. Check independently against the day answer key after attempting. This work stays in folders.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

After checkpoint collection, use [[s01-P9-3]]. Review partner feedback: phrases and expression, no speed. Retain actual [[s01-A09-RF-purpose]], [[s01-A09-RF-phrasing]] and [[s01-A09-RF-confirm]] observations from the preceding ten days. Hear only five due missing-component readers at two minutes each; five minutes remain for transitions/feedback. Keep prose and verse evidence separate; unheard components stay blank. No second screen.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact prompts and source lines without a printer. Share printed taught rereading copies only after collection.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy exact prompts and N9/P9/S9 once, preserving poem lines.

**Skip.** The class listening rotation.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Uncover the new reading copy. Spend five minutes on directions, then thirty-five responding. Your child answers [[s01-A09a-action]], [[s01-A09a-infer]], [[s01-A09b-theme]], [[s01-A09b-summary]], [[s01-A09c]] and [[s01-A09d]] alone, using the texts.
- **5 min. Reflect.** Keep the first attempt unchanged. Give a written component note. Try [[s01-P9-4]].
  **Say.** Which part needs another look? Expect the child to identify a part from the note.

Retain actual reading observations for s01-A09-RF-purpose, s01-A09-RF-phrasing and s01-A09-RF-confirm; these add no written task. Keep dated work. Return Hope's revision privately.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve check allocations; extend access time separately and record it. No extra assessed task.

### Words and supports

Review taught terms only. Large print, signing or a composition scribe may provide access; record help. Never supply an interpretation.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 Return and repair returns day 9's assessment and notes, plus s01-X08 and inspected choices. Errors A/O need located evidence, B/C message/event distinctions, E structural features. Prioritize one component: five minutes on another taught case, five independent revision. Judgment after class day 11: thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes; return day 12; actual reading feedback also returns day 12. Day 10 reads part 5 and the whole Hughes poem.
