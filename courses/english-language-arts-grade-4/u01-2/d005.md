# Day 5: Reading leaves a writing choice

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print whole Frost, notebook part 3, the taught Williams poem, and current cited choice cards only. Keep future manuscript companions out. Print s01-X05 exactly on one half slip; keep it covered. Teacher-only answers: day answer key and exit-key.md. Gather day 4's tickets, Exit boxes and role notes, retained story map, reading log, sound and spelling wall, and unit 1 component record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return day 4's s01-X04, s01-P4-X1, inspected practice and role notes. If many day 4 tickets showed error E, use [[s01-P5-3]] to locate one actual line feature. Reread it with meaning rather than treating short print as an explanation. Others privately inspect their returned work. Error Y waits for the single Choice workshop group. Carry the story map and log forward from section 1.1.

#### 15 min. Read and notice

Whole class tracks the complete Frost poem, 108 words, then all notebook part 3, about 1,050 words. About ten reading minutes leaves five for glosses and noticing. Explain queer as unusual here, downy as soft, and harness bells as bells on the horse's gear. Content: stopping to admire woods, obligations, comparisons, and permission to record another person. No personal disclosure is needed.

Pairs locate [[s01-P5-1]]. **Ask.** [[s01-P5-1]] Expect the line naming promises; what the promises are remains unstated. Review evidence: the line or page that shows an idea is true, from day 2. Review day 3 infer: work out an unstated idea using text details. Match a claim to words before accepting it.

#### 10 min. Feature demonstration

Review day 4 verse, writing arranged in deliberate lines, and rhythm, the pattern of sounds and stresses heard while reading. Review line break from grade 1: where one line ends and the next begins; it puts a clue at a line end. Review stanza from grade 3: a group of lines in a poem.

Pairs first attempt [[s01-K5-1]]. Reveal [[steps:s01-K5-1]]. Teach meter as a recurring pattern of stressed and unstressed syllables. A reader's voice can vary; regular meter is not required of every poem. Then pairs attempt [[s01-P5-2]]. Inspect the repeated ending only, without composing a full two-detail comparison.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

Alone then pairs, complete [[s01-P5-5]]. Review theme from grade 3 as a message that comes back in more than one book; day 3 extended this to a supported message within one text. A topic names the subject; a theme says something about it. Locate lovely and promises without displaying a finished theme-and-summary response.

Return to the two separate choices in s01-P5-1 and s01-P5-2. Give feedback on the located line or feature. The teacher's one five-minute group uses [[s01-P5-4]] for day 4 error Y. Others mark their own located evidence, without another teacher turn. Collect the selected cards for day 6.

#### 10 min. Notebook

Use [[s01-P5-6]] privately. Review day 1 image: words that help a reader imagine a sensory detail. Children may sketch a feature or try a different phrasing, without a whole comparison. The dated note stays unjudged in the folder. Partners may pass on sharing. Do not print or display a completed exit-shaped response.

#### 5 min. Exit

Release [[s01-X05]] for the first time. Children read and respond alone on paper: four minutes, then one to collect. Its short poem and response print once, exactly as items.csv gives them. Use one roomy response area on the half slip. Do not read a sample answer aloud. Record the paired details and reading-to-writing connection separately using shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review the five familiar syllable types from grade 1, one known word each, using [[s01-P5-7]] and the sound and spelling wall. Read letters and chunks before checking meaning. Pairs cover, spell and reference-check the named word. Independent decoding remains the child's. No new foundations cycle or irregular-word set is introduced.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P5-8]]. Review the day 1 reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Children choose only accessible taught copies. Teacher marking of the exit and inspected choices takes thirty children times twenty seconds, ten minutes. At most four missing-component conferences take two minutes each, eight minutes; two minutes remain for transitions. This is no repeated fall screen. Keep actual evidence and support on the unit 1 component record.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Hand-copy current exact cards and share whole reading copies with tracking. Use a board copy of the familiar wall. Keep the exit private until response time; do not distribute an entire manuscript.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the current short prompts and share reading copies.

**Skip.** Class conferences and the role rotation.

- **5 min. Repair.** Inspect day 4's returned work. Try [[s01-P5-3]].
- **12 min. Read.** Read whole Frost and part 3 together, sharing the reading load. Try [[s01-P5-1]].
  **Say.** Are the promises named? Expect no; their nature is unstated.
- **8 min. Notice.** Try [[s01-K5-1]] before its steps, then [[s01-P5-2]].
  **Say.** Which ending repeats? Expect Frost's final line.
- **5 min. Write.** Use [[s01-P5-6]] privately. Keep the dated note.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X05]]. Your child responds alone. Keep it for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce notebook elaboration and voluntary sharing; keep full readings, feature teaching and exit. Longer: test a second natural phrasing of Frost's first line. Inspected practice returns day 6; private drafts stay unjudged in the folder.

### Words and supports

Review poem, poet, image, evidence, infer, theme, verse and rhythm from section 1.1. New meter names the regular stress pattern. Preserve source punctuation and line breaks. Use large print, a reading partner, tactile line separators, or a composition scribe; record help. Do not supply the exit interpretation.

### Tomorrow

Return s01-X05 and inspected cards in day 6 Opening. Error A lacks a paired supporting detail; error O supplies an absent fact. Locate one different taught feature in Opening; other needs share one Choice workshop group. Missing evidence remains blank until observed. Day 6 reads whole A Birthday, revisits November 12, and teaches comparison names and word relationships. Pass the dated notebook, map, log and actual-observation record onward.
