# Day 8: Three forms, one moment

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print The Missing Line's three whole tellings and whole scene only, excluding reserved companions. Gather Hope's first responses with written feedback, current feature and intensity labels, discussion moves chart and role notes. Prepare floor-level seating, paper figures and soft taps. Cover s01-X08. Teacher-only keys: choice-key.md, exit-key.md, assessment-key.md and day answer key. Arrange a willing reader or perform both voices yourself; acting is optional. Teacher keys: choice-key-01.md, choice-key-04.md, choice-key-05.md. Print current sheets listed above.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 7's poem meaning feedback sheet with component feedback. Children independently revise s01-A07-meaning beneath the retained first attempt. If many day 7 interpretations showed error Y, each rereads the cited lines and responds to the private unmet-component note. No class answer or new model. Collect both attempts for day 9 finalization. Day 7 error Z and the exit ticket wait for Choice workshop.

#### 10 min. Read versions

Read The Missing Line's three whole tellings, 291 words, and whole scene, 150 words. Four reading minutes leave six for labels. Content: an unfinished poem, a wish for privacy, listening, and a classmate who waits. No child must share an unfinished draft.

Use [[s01-P8-3]] to review grade 3 point of view: character, narrator, article writer and reader as four labeled views. Review narrator as the teller. Earlier outside narrators were examples, not a rule that narrators never say I. Teach first-person narration: a participant tells using I. Teach third-person narration: an outside teller uses names or third-person pronouns. Thoughts may appear in either; this third-person telling reports outward events. Use [[s01-P8-1]] to locate a pronoun outside dialogue.

#### 15 min. Hear and see

Teach drama as writing for performance and cast as its characters or roles. Try [[s01-K8-1]], then reveal [[steps:s01-K8-1]]. Review setting from day 3: where and when a story happens. Review grade 3 dialogue, words spoken by characters, and stage direction, a note for the performer in brackets, not spoken.

Read the complete first-person prose aloud exactly, softly on the hope in paragraph 1, pausing after each sound in paragraph 2. Try [[s01-P8-4]]. Stage the whole scene exactly: paper face down, Mina points without taking it, shuffle, pause, heavy steps, then Ben writes and lifts; Mina smiles. Keep actors seated at floor level. Two paper figures provide the fallback.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-2]] Expect Mina waits. Connect the visible waiting to the printed direction, without composing a full three-form comparison. Everyone has the printed text; repeat a missed action without requiring a child to perform.

#### 20 min. Choice workshop

Return day 7's s01-X07 and inspected choices. One five-minute group uses [[s01-P8-9]] if many tickets showed error Z. Others independently use [[s01-P8-5]] and the feature and intensity labels, one form at a time. Review grade 1: prose versus poem, same events in lines or in sentences; review verse, rhythm and meter from days 4 and 5. A prose sentence can also have rhythm; free verse need not have regular meter. No whole comparison model.

Hear only children 13 to 18 in two-minute linked role exchanges, twelve minutes total. Review day 1 role actions and discussion moves. The questioner uses [[s01-P8-11]]; the responder locates its words. Hear preparation, invitation, linked question/answer and listening reflection. Others audit notes concurrently. Give six dated written feedback notes at thirty seconds each in the remaining three minutes. Public passing leads to a private live exchange, never inferred oral evidence from paper.

#### 5 min. Notebook

Use [[s01-P8-6]] and [[s01-P8-10]]. Children date one observation and audit their own role labels. Private notebook work stays unjudged in folders. The record holds only actual exchanges; retain written feedback for day 12 Choice workshop.

#### 5 min. Exit

Release [[s01-X08]] for the first time, alone on paper. Print its exact prose, verse and scene once on the half slip, preserving breaks. Four minutes respond, one collects. One roomy box accepts three brief feature notes linked as a comparison. Record actual features and comparison separately with shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No model or key appears on the child's ticket.

#### 10 min. Word work

Use [[s01-P8-7]], review of closed chunks and vowel teams from days 5 and 6. Read chunks before checking meaning; cover, spell and check against the dictionary. Record a correction on the sound and spelling wall. Other practice self-checks against the day answer key after attempting it. No new cycle or reserved word instruction.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P8-8]], review of the carried reading log. Choose accessible taught notebook entries or tellings. Teacher marks thirty children times thirty-five seconds: twenty-five for the exit and five each for the two selected choices, 17.5 minutes. Leave 2.5 for notes and transitions; no additional conference. Keep support and component evidence separate. The Hope revision is finalized day 9, not modeled here.

### Other rooms

Live reading replaces recordings. The adult moves two paper figures if no reader volunteers. Share current whole copies with tracking; hand-copy exact private tickets without a printer. Use a board instead of the wall.

### At home

**Swap.** No reader or recording: move two paper figures and read both voices. Share whole copies.

**Skip.** The class role rotation and group repair.

- **5 min. Revision.** Return the Hope sheet with a written component note. Your child revises alone.
- **10 min. Read.** Read all three taught tellings and the whole scene. Try [[s01-P8-1]].
- **10 min. Perform.** Read and stage the printed versions exactly. Try [[s01-P8-2]] and [[s01-P8-4]].
  **Say.** Which action matches the direction? Expect Mina waits without taking the page.
- **5 min. Notice.** Try [[s01-P8-5]] and [[s01-P8-6]]. Keep the private note.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X08]]. Your child responds alone. Keep it for day 10.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce notebook elaboration; keep all readings, actual performance, scheduled role turns and exit. Longer: find another printed direction and observe its action. Self-checked work stays in folders; inspected choices return day 10.

### Words and supports

Review point of view, narrator, setting, dialogue, stage direction, verse, rhythm and meter. New drama, cast and first/third-person narration name today's distinctions. Provide large print, signing, printed descriptions, tactile separators or a composition scribe; record help. Paper cannot replace evidence of listening or speaking.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 uses new N9/P9/S9 only after launch. It returns the finalized Hope meaning record during Feedback, without a class answer. Keep s01-X08 and inspected choices for day 10 Return and repair. Error E uses page size instead of structural features: one existing repair route locates a feature in a different taught text. Day 9 has no reteach. Pass actual role evidence and dated feedback to day 12.
