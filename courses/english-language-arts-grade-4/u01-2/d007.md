# Day 7: Sayings and a second reading

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print whole Hope and the permitted part 4 entries. Keep December 13 and The Tray covered. Retain A Birthday for the comparison-object choice. Print today's response sheets, feature labels and word cards. Gather day 6 papers. Select five due readers, reusing matching records. Teacher keys: assessment-key-01.md, choice-key-01.md, choice-key-03.md, choice-key-04.md. Keep all keys private.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 6's s01-X06, s01-X06b, s01-X06c and inspected practice. If many day 6 tickets showed error Y, [[s01-P7-4]] matches another taught comparison to its real object. Review day 6 literal/nonliteral: words meant exactly or another way. Other children inspect feedback privately. Errors AA and AJ wait for the alternative Choice workshop group, one route per child.

#### 15 min. Read and track

Read whole Hope, 69 words, and notebook part 4 except December 13 and The Tray. Release December 13 only for the exit; the day completes part 4 through December 19. Ten reading minutes leave five for tracking and glosses. Gloss gale as strong wind, abash as make uneasy, and extremity as great difficulty. Content: a failed handle, discouragement, sayings, an imaginative bird, and visitors. Children need not disclose personal worries.

Review line breaks and rhythm from day 4. Track across a line ending when the sentence continues. Read with natural phrasing without giving a complete interpretation of Hope. The independent meaning response comes before any teacher explanation of that comparison.

#### 10 min. Expressions

Use [[s01-P7-2]] to select one comparison object in the already-taught Rossetti poem.

Review grade 3's sentence clue: the sentence beside a word tells what it means. Introduce idiom as an expression whose customary meaning differs from its word-by-word reading. Use [[s01-P7-1]] before revealing its meaning label. **Ask.** [[s01-P7-1]] Expect beginning again, from the failed-handle context.

Introduce adage as a customary saying offering general wisdom, not a guarantee; proverb as a customary saying offering guidance. Categories can overlap. Pairs match [[s01-P7-3]] and [[s01-P7-5]] with the feature and intensity labels. The lighter circle improves Omar's letter; practice does not guarantee a perfect result. Do not introduce today's exit expression.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

First, children answer [[s01-A07-meaning]] independently on the poem meaning feedback sheet, six minutes with the whole poem open. No completed answer, meaning label or partner response precedes it. Collect the first attempt for written feedback after class. This is a taught-text interpretation, not a cold reading claim.

Then use [[s01-P7-4]] only if not completed in Opening. The five-minute teacher group uses [[s01-P7-10]] for day 6 errors AA or AJ. Review synonym, antonym and degree from day 6. Others check their expression selections using the day answer key after attempting them. Keep new Hope meaning work separate from these old comparison choices.

#### 10 min. Notebook

Use [[s01-P7-6]], alone, then pairs if willing. Review day 1's discussion moves: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Partners each take a concurrent 45-second turn on one phrasing choice. The dated note stays unjudged in the folder. No public sharing is required.

#### 5 min. Exit

Release [[s01-X07]] alone on paper for the first time. Print its exact stem on the half slip, with one roomy response box. Four minutes respond, one collects. The response needs a conventional meaning and an action that supports it. Record these separately using shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Do not infer a misunderstanding from a blank response.

#### 10 min. Word work

Use [[s01-P7-7]], review of day 6's silent-e and vowel-team reading and dictionary spelling. Read letters and chunks before checking context. Children self-check with the dictionary and sound and spelling wall, then continue taught reading. Teacher marks the thirty exits at fifteen seconds each, 7.5 minutes, and s01-P7-1 at five seconds each, 2.5 minutes. No simultaneous teacher-led spelling turns.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading: phrases and expression, no speed. Use [[s01-P7-8]], then whole Hope twice, with a partner's phrasing suggestion before rereading. Children may read privately instead of publicly.

Hear only five due children, two minutes each, ten total plus five for transitions and feedback. Review actual purpose, meaning, accuracy, pace, expression and confirmation records. Reuse matching observations from the preceding ten days; hear only missing components on short taught spans. Correct a real difficulty, then hear the reread. Never stage a mistake or infer unheard prose reading from verse.

#### 5 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P7-9]] and review the carried reading log. Choose accessible taught copies only. No additional conference today. After class, allow thirty times twenty seconds, ten minutes, for written Hope feedback. Name an unmet component without supplying the interpretation; retain the first response for tomorrow's independent revision.

### Other rooms

Share whole reading copies with tracking. Hand-copy only current exact prompts without a printer. A board copy replaces the wall. Keep the exit hidden and the teacher keys separate.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy current prompts and share whole readings.

**Skip.** The class listening rotation and group repair.

- **5 min. Repair.** Inspect day 6 feedback and try [[s01-P7-4]].
- **12 min. Read.** Read whole Hope and part 4 together. Keep December 13 covered until the exit. Stop before The Tray.
- **8 min. Match.** Try [[s01-P7-1]] and [[s01-P7-5]].
  **Say.** What does square one mean here? Expect: the failed handle makes work feel like starting again.
- **5 min. Meaning.** Your child answers [[s01-A07-meaning]] alone on the separate sheet. Keep it for a written note. Then reread a short taught passage together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read December 13 now, then uncover [[s01-X07]]. Your child responds alone; keep the dated slip.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce voluntary notebook sharing; preserve whole readings, independent meaning response and exit. Longer: test another phrasing of December 19. Self-checked practice stays in folders; inspected choices return day 8.

### Words and supports

Review metaphor from day 6; new idiom, adage and proverb name conventional expressions. Large print, signing, tactile line dividers and a composition scribe provide access; record help. Independent word reading remains the child's. Give no meaning cues during the independent response.

### Tomorrow

Return Hope's first attempt with written feedback in day 8 Opening for independent revision; finalize day 9. Return s01-X07 and inspected choices in day 8 Choice workshop. Error Z reads the saying literally: match December's taught expression to its context in one five-minute group. Day 8 reads and performs The Missing Line's complete taught versions, keeping checkpoint companions covered.
