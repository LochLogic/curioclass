# Section 2.3 opener: Research an expert animal

## Ask, locate, note, group, list, revisit

Each group reads one expert article. Give them stable paragraph or heading locations, one short passage per leaf when needed. Relevant means a fact that helps answer the research question. A category is a group of things that go together. Children choose categories after consulting the sources. Use the note card with source tag to keep title, author and location with an idea. Show one misplaced note or one missing tag for inspection.

Use the saved selection beside its matching article. The pufferfish and armadillo groups use the printed book and saved digital article. Each expert group finishes the article by day 55, opening the reserved spans only when their exits release them. Keep packet B covered for day 57. Use the print copies when the devices fail and record unfinished digital evidence.

## Prepare and collect

Make two groups of fifteen for device turns on days 53 and 54. Open local digital copies and put the matching paper copies at seats. On day 54 consult print and digital references in the planned turns. Keep the held skunk material for the exit and use different entries for strategy practice. File the dated independent strategy record and day 55 correction together for day 74.

On day 56 release the new print text and its saved digital companion. Keep reading and independent notes inside the collection allocation. Children supply their own question answers, grouping and locations. Errors J, K, L and T indicate repeated aspects, unrelated details, missing source information and ungrouped notes. Mark one priority for the next repair.
