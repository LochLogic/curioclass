# Section 2.2 opener: Explain with words and displays

## Read the relationship

Use the text-structure map to ask how two ideas fit together. Chronology gives an order. Cause and effect links an action and its result. Comparison explains a likeness or difference. Problem and solution explains the difficulty and what addresses it. Ask children to explain the relationship itself. One signal word can occur in several structures.

Arrows may show an order or a connection. Graphs show counts in units. Read the legend and axes before comparing bars. Ask what the display adds to the words. Correct an isolated label, unit or relationship. Keep Graph 1 covered through day 46's teaching. Use the practice count card earlier in that day. Reserve the new listening selection, diagram and graph for day 48.

## Set up the work

Keep the listening selection separate from the print copy. Read it twice and collect the response before releasing the transcript where the check calls for that sequence. File the first attempt, written feedback and later independent correction. Before discussion, assign a facilitator, evidence-finder and questioner and explain each job from the discussion moves card. Each learner has a contribution and linked exchange. On day 50 hear six for the spoken exit and collect a paper explanation from everyone. The remaining twenty-four individual speaking turns take eighteen minutes during day 51 Independent reading.

For errors P and AL, name the unit and counted variable before interpreting a bar. For errors C and S, distinguish a judgment or copied phrase from a faithful restatement. For errors AD and U, point first to what the display or second source adds. Use the named repair slot and different material for a retry.
