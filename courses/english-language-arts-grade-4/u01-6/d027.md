# Day 27: Revise independently and retain the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-only day key, convention-key.md and the unit assessment guide/key. Return each folder with its day 26 dated notes, original convention slips and source leaves. Keep X26 covered until day 28. Select five due readers; reuse matching ten-day observations. The student page carries the collection prompt; no duplicate assessment page. Set out references and blank revision paper. Teacher keys: assessment-key-04.md, assessment-key-07.md, assessment-key-08.md, assessment-key-09.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch

Retrieve day 26's folder and written notes. If many still need repair, record the need for day 28; supply no new teaching. Explain collection and clarify directions without supplying answers. Preserve all earlier versions and supports used.

#### 40 min. Writing collection

[[s01-A27-topic]] Children independently revise from existing feedback. Suggested shared allowances: topic/grouping eight minutes, development ten, links six, precision five, conclusion six, process five. These total forty, with no forced stops. Revise original convention slips within this sitting. Mark one meaning change and connect it to the dated note. Retain everything as D27a for day 36 judgment.

Teacher-only component records, not extra student tasks: [[s01-A27-groups]] [[s01-A27-develop]] [[s01-A27-links]] [[s01-A27-precise]] [[s01-A27-close]] [[s01-A27-audience]] [[s01-A27-process]]. Conventions: [[s01-A27-sentence]] [[s01-A27-fragment]] [[s01-A27-runon]] [[s01-A27-name]] [[s01-A27-opening]] [[s01-A27-speech-comma]] [[s01-A27-speech-marks]] [[s01-A27-quote-comma]] [[s01-A27-quote-marks]] [[s01-A27-reference]] [[s01-A27-spelling]]. Judge eligible retained evidence once per component: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record help separately.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Quietly file plan, draft, dated note, revision and edited slips. Collect folders; prepare new written component feedback after class, about 120 minutes for thirty folders. Return day 28. Do not teach corrections or score a response to today's new note. Preserve the day 27 version unchanged for day 36.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review day 22's familiar [[s01-W27-1]] and [[s01-W27-2]] in pairs. Say each pair naturally and consult the reference. Unjudged retrieval only; no new pattern or probe word.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Review day 24's choices [[s01-K27-1]] and [[s01-K27-2]]. Partners select and consult the teacher key afterward. These different cases do not alter collected slips.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review familiar partner rereading with Williams profile paragraph 2 and whole XXII. Pairs state purpose, reread successively with meaningful phrasing, and check letters plus context. Hear five due children at two minutes each, ten within fifteen. Reuse matching ten-day observations; no concurrent repair group.

Teacher records actual missing evidence in [[s01-A27-RF-purpose]], [[s01-A27-RF-phrasing]] and [[s01-A27-RF-confirm]]. Unheard components stay pending for day 29's existing rotation. Never infer speech from paper or stage a misreading.

### Other rooms

Notebook revision pages replace forms. Use printed references and taught source leaves, preserving lines.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: use notebook versions, copied directions and a printed reference. The adult is the reading partner.

**Skip.** Class rotations and same-day answer teaching.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Your child completes [[s01-A27-topic]] alone, using day 26's written notes. Keep the plan, draft, notes, revised essay and original convention slips together. Include a marked meaning change and its connection to the note. Keep this version for later judgment.
- **5 min. Reread and file.** Reread Williams paragraph 2 and whole XXII together for meaning. Keep actual reading observations with the folder.
  **Say.** "What are you reading to find?" Expect: a purpose connected to the passage and a matching detail.

### Shorter or longer

Keep the collection intact. Longer: reread a taught source quietly after filing.

### Words and supports

Review familiar folder, revision and reading routines. Give established access supports and record them; independent spelling stays the child's. Public speech permits a private turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 returns day 27's assessment feedback with s01-X26-1, s01-X26-2, s01-P26-1 and s01-P26-listen. Its ten-minute Return and repair holds five minutes on a different taught fault and five for independent response. Error S needs meaningful revision; R/O returns to topic relevance. Keep day 27's version for day 36 judgment. Then publish the essay and plan an original poem.
