# Day 24: Develop the first aspect

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return X23 and P23-1 together. Set out plans, folders, authorized profile/poem leaves and dictionaries. Open teacher-only inspection-key.md, convention-key.md, exit-key.md and day key. The student page holds today's convention slips; print no duplicate slip set. Cover Side 2 until collection and keep the exit separate. Select five due readers, reusing matching ten-day records. Have day 22 actual reading feedback ready.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 23's exit and inspection. If many show AE, inspect P23-1's missing subject without composing an opening. For O, check one claim against its source. Repair one earliest need only; route others to Choice workshop. Keep own openings unjudged.

#### 10 min. Source rereading

Review day 19 quotation: exact source words inside paired marks. Read Williams profile paragraph 2, under 100 words, once from the shared leaf. **Ask.** [[s01-P24-1]] Expect children to locate the contradicted limitation; the paragraph names other writing forms. Do not compose a fact/quotation paragraph together.

Children choose [[s01-P24-2]] before discussion. Inspect the introducing comma and both quotation boundaries. The wrong option lacks both features; it cannot diagnose either fault alone. Retrieve both punctuation uses with [[s01-K24-3]], then [[steps:s01-K24-3]]. Say that this supplied dialogue is invented, unlike a source quotation copied exactly.

#### 25 min. Choice workshop

First seventeen minutes: children privately draft their first body paragraph from their own chosen profile/poem and day 23 plan. Review day 22 develop: add information that explains an idea. Keep facts, definitions or examples beside the aspect they explain. A quotation must match the source. Do not require a definition where none helps. Their paragraphs remain unjudged folder work.

During this writing, use one five-minute group for the earliest remaining X23 need. If more than ten share it, use that slot with everyone. Final eight minutes: unfold Side 2 and independently collect C24-name, C24-opening, C24-speech and C24-text, first seen now. No answer coaching. Keep originals for feedback day 26, revision day 27 and judgment day 36. Collect P24-1/P24-2 too.

#### 10 min. Folder work

Review grade 3's dated folder process. Children attach source paragraph or poem-line locations and privately check whether their first aspect stays grouped. Preserve the opening and every draft; no teacher score. Collect the essay version after class for written component feedback before day 27.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X24]], first seen now. Seven minutes: independently develop the paragraph on the half slip. Three minutes: collect and mark thirty at six seconds each. Look for connected development, the supplied fact and exact quotation. Accept defensible readings of the image without inventing a light source. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Record O, N, W or AG only when visible. Return day 25 Opening.

After class, allow five minutes for thirty paired inspection choices at ten seconds each. Prepare convention feedback for day 26, about eight minutes at sixteen seconds per child, without writing corrected answers on slips.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review schwa from day 20 and the familiar re- base from day 19. Use [[s01-W24]], then [[steps:s01-W24]]. Pairs locate entries with guide words, cover, write and compare. Keep rehearsal unjudged. If many guess the weak vowel, point back to the entry before a second attempt.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Review day 21 sentence-opening and proper-name capitals using [[s01-K24-1]] and [[s01-K24-2]]. Children choose, then reveal [[steps:s01-K24-1]] and [[steps:s01-K24-2]]. These are selections, not repairs of the independent slips. Keep choices unjudged.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading: phrases and expression, no speed target. Use Williams profile paragraph 2 and whole XXII, about one minute for a first reading. Partners state a purpose, read successive times for sense and use letters plus context to confirm words.

Return day 22 actual reading feedback. Teacher hears five due children at two minutes each, ten within fifteen; reuse matching preceding-ten-day observations. Record actual purpose/meaning, prose and verse accuracy/rate/expression, and confirmation or correction. Unheard components remain blank for the next scheduled window, day 27. No repair group runs during listening. Passing in public talk is allowed; hear a private turn instead.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy short prompts, with slips and exit covered separately. Notebook columns replace source tags and folder forms. Preserve paragraph numbers and whole poem lines; a printed reference replaces a dictionary.

### At home

**Swap.** Without printing, copy the prompts separately. Use notebook drafts and a printed reference.

**Skip.** Class rotations and teacher marking.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 23 feedback.
- **10 min. Inspect.** Reread the source and try [[s01-P24-1]] and [[s01-P24-2]].
  **Say.** "Which choice marks the borrowed words correctly?" Expect: A, with a comma and paired marks.
- **10 min. Write.** Keep the body paragraph unjudged. Uncover and complete the four convention slips on Side 2 alone.
- **5 min. Reread.** Read the taught prose and whole poem twice for meaning. Consult [[s01-W24]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X24]]. Your child answers alone. Keep dated work for feedback and later revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce draft recopying, preserving sources, convention collection, rereading and exit. Longer: privately test another source detail's relevance to the first aspect.

### Words and supports

Review develop, aspect, quotation and guide words. Review phrase: words read together for meaning. Enlarge leaves, allow signing or content scribing and record help. Keep independent spelling and reading the child's. No personal parallels are requested.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 returns X24 and P24-1/P24-2. If many show O, inspect the contradicted limitation again; if W/AG dominates, compare quotation boundaries. Use one earliest Opening repair only. Develop the second body paragraph and independently collect sentence/reference slips. Day 24 convention slips receive notes day 26 and revision day 27.
