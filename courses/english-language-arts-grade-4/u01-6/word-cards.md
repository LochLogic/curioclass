# Word meanings

**run-on:** complete thoughts fused without a fitting boundary.

**feedback:** a note about one feature to improve.

**edit:** correct conventions while keeping the intended meaning.

**contrast:** show how things differ.

**synonym:** a word with a similar meaning.

**antonym:** a word with an opposite meaning.

**idiom:** a saying whose meaning differs from its separate words.

**adage:** a familiar saying offering an observation or advice.
