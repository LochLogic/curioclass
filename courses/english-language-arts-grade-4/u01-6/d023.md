# Day 23: Plan for an unfamiliar reader

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return X22, P22-1/P22-2 and actual R11 feedback together. Set out day 21 source-tagged notes, the section 1.5 faulty essay and current reading leaves. Open teacher-only inspection-key.md, exit-key.md and the day key. Cover the exit until response time. Print each taught source once, excluding reserved companions. Provide blank folder pages and a dictionary. Keep all plans and drafts unjudged.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 22's tickets and inspection choices. If many showed error N, inspect the faulty essay's mixed first body paragraph. If error T dominates, reuse day 22's complete-thought choice. Repair only the earliest need; others use Choice workshop. Return actual R11 feedback without turning unheard speech into evidence. Review day 22 essay structure: opening, two aspects and a related ending.

#### 10 min. Source inspection

Pairs retrieve their chosen profile and poem from day 21. Review aspect: one focus within the subject. Read at most 200 already-taught words, about two minutes at 120 words per minute. Locate one note's source tag; do not infer undocumented motives.

**Ask.** [[s01-P23-1]] Children choose privately before discussion. Expect the missing subject, not more unrelated information. Inspect the fault without composing a complete introduction. Leave the independent choice visible for collection.

#### 25 min. Choice workshop

Review grade 3 heading: the main idea of a group of notes, saying its purpose. Retrieve day 21's grouping plan. Children name their own unfamiliar reader, group notes under two aspects and draft an opening privately on folder pages. The reader needs the poet named and the explanation's focus. These drafts stay in the folder, unjudged; no completed introduction is displayed.

Teacher takes one five-minute group for the earliest unmet X22 component, using the faulty essay. If more than ten missed the same component, use that slot with the whole class. Others keep writing. Collect P23-1 independently before revealing its key.

#### 10 min. Folder preparation

Review grade 3's folder process: plan, draft, feedback, revision, edit. Date the plan and opening. Attach source locations and keep life information distinct from poem features. Each child checks the named reader against the opening privately. Teacher notes support and unfinished work, without judging the draft. Keep day 21 notes beside today's plan.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X23]], first seen now. Seven minutes: write independently on the half slip. Three minutes: collect and mark thirty responses at six seconds each. Record introduction and focus with one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Accept equivalent source-true openings. Error AE leaves the subject unnamed; O invents a fact. Hand back day 24 Opening. Mark P23-1 after class at four seconds each, two minutes, for the same return.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review day 20 schwa and day 19 re- plus a known base. Use [[s01-W23]], then [[steps:s01-W23]]. Pairs read both words, consult the entries, cover, write and compare every letter. Review grade 3 guide words for locating an entry. This is unjudged rehearsal. If many guess the weak vowel, return to the printed entry and compare its full spelling.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children reread a taught profile or poem and update the reading log. Review the log's date, title, purpose and actual detail. Hear at most five two-minute conferences about source locations, ten minutes total. These are support conferences, not extra individual reading checks. Others continue reading quietly.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the short inspection and exit prompts separately. Use notebook columns for source tags and dated feedback. Preserve verse lines and profile paragraph numbers; use only taught leaves.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the short prompts separately and use notebook folder pages. Use a printed spelling reference.

**Skip.** Class observation rotations and teacher marking.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 22 feedback and inspect its faulty component.
- **10 min. Read and inspect.** Reread a taught source. Try [[s01-P23-1]].
  **Say.** "What information is missing?" Expect: the subject.
- **10 min. Draft.** Your child groups source notes and drafts an opening for a named reader. Keep it unjudged.
- **5 min. Words.** Read [[s01-W23]], consult the reference, then compare spellings.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X23]]. Your child answers alone. Keep dated work.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce conference time and private recopying, preserving the plan, source check and exit. Longer: inspect whether an extra note belongs with either aspect, without adding a judged paragraph.

### Words and supports

Review reader/purpose/form, aspect, heading and essay structure from days 18 to 22. Review feedback as the teacher note, and edit as convention correction within the familiar folder process. Enlarge leaves, offer signing or content scribing, and record help. Independent spelling stays the child's. Public sharing permits passing.

### Tomorrow

Day 24 returns X23 and P23-1. If many show AE, inspect the supplied opening's missing subject; for O, locate source evidence before accepting a claim. One Opening repair only. Children develop the first body paragraph using their dated plan; their own draft remains unjudged.
