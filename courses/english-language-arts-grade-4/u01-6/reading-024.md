# Day 24 reading

On the way home I tried  
to collect noises in my pockets.  
The crossing button clicked.  
A suitcase rattled over cracks.  
Someone shook a wet umbrella  
beside the bakery door.  
I had no way to bring them back.  
By dinner the suitcase was gone.  
Only the umbrella stayed,  
a small indoor rain  
falling where the floor was already dry.
