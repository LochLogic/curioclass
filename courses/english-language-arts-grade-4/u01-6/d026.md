# Day 26: Close the explanation and read feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare dated individual notes on each essay and day 24/25 convention originals, about 120 minutes for thirty essay folders plus the slip review. Name a need without supplying a corrected answer. Return X25 and P25 choices. Open teacher-only inspection-key.md, convention-key.md, exit-key.md and day key. Select six due discussion children from day 20's pending list. Set out sources, blank folder pages, references and record continuation. Cover X26.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 25's exit and P25-1/P25-2. If many show P, inspect the false rain link; for Q, compare thing and stanza. Use one earliest repair only. Others retain the second body draft for private source inspection. Review day 22 conclusion: the last part closes what the writer has explained.

#### 10 min. Source inspection

Children reread at most 200 taught words from their chosen profile/poem, about two reading minutes. Review the day 21 source-tag routine. Check a draft detail against its exact location, without inventing the poet's motives. Prepare one located note for discussion. Own essay work remains unjudged; no completed ending is shown.

#### 20 min. Discussion

Review grade 3 moves: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link and explain what became clearer. Review the peer look-for: check one named feature, then move, add or explain a fact. Three minutes: prepare source notes and assign a turn inviter in each group of five.

Seven minutes: groups discuss concurrently using [[s01-R26]]. Each child has one forty-five-second turn, including a question or relevant response; five turns take three minutes forty-five seconds per group. The inviter makes room for each turn. Teacher visits the six due children, forty-five seconds each, four-and-a-half minutes plus movement. Record only heard preparation, role, question/response and linked contribution. Public speech may be passed; offer the same private turn. No repair group runs during listening.

Five minutes: everyone independently completes [[s01-P26-listen]]. Paper shows listening understanding only. Five minutes: children privately draft their own related conclusion on folder pages, unjudged. Keep all drafts. Other twenty-four oral records remain pending for the existing day 33 discussion window, using eligible observations when available.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

**Ask.** [[s01-P26-1]] Expect removal of the misplaced body detail. Inspect placement within the body, not a whole ending, before the independent exit.

Return prepared essay and convention notes from days 24/25. Review grade 3 revision: moving, adding or explaining information changes meaning. Children read the dated notes and mark the work each note concerns, without copying a supplied correction. Keep first attempts and feedback for tomorrow. Teacher uses one five-minute group for the earliest unmet X25 component, or the whole class if more than ten share it. Do not complete an ending or correct convention slips together. Collect P26-1 and listening reflections.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X26-1]] and [[s01-X26-2]], first seen now, together on a half slip. The shared stimulus is read once; both stems remain exact. Six minutes: independently produce a close and explain the unrelated ending's failure. Four minutes: collect and mark thirty pairs at eight seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. R indicates an unrelated close; O indicates falsely claiming that the bus sentence fits. Return day 28 Return and repair, with D27 feedback. Give no X26 answer coaching tomorrow.

After class, mark P26-1 and the listening reflection together at twelve seconds each, six minutes, for day 28 return. Keep unheard oral components blank.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review day 22 stress, the syllable given greater emphasis. Retrieve [[s01-W22]] and [[s01-W22-2]], then their teacher-only steps. Photograph/photography moves the strongest stress from first syllable to second. Nation/national keeps first-syllable stress. A suffix does not always move stress. Pairs say both pairs twice naturally, consult entries and compare spelling. No first teaching or individual screen. If many stress every syllable equally, model the whole familiar word before another try.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Reread a taught source and update the familiar log. Teacher checks that every child has dated notes before tomorrow's revision and records remaining needs. Keep the plan, earlier drafts, convention first attempts and teacher feedback together. Missing feedback is prepared before day 27; do not replace it with a peer's opinion.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy current prompts separately and cover the exit. Notebook columns replace folder forms and source tags. Use a printed reference and preserve poem lines. One adult takes the discussion partner's role.

### At home

**Swap.** No group: the adult is the partner. Copy prompts without printing; use notebook drafts and a reference.

**Skip.** Class rotations and teacher marking.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 25 feedback.
- **10 min. Read and discuss.** Use a located source note with [[s01-R26]].
  **Say.** "Which source detail supports that idea?" Expect: an actual located detail; record uncertainty honestly.
- **10 min. Inspect and draft.** Try [[s01-P26-1]] and [[s01-P26-listen]]. Keep the conclusion draft unjudged. Read the prepared teacher notes.
- **5 min. Words.** Read [[s01-W22]] and [[s01-W22-2]] naturally; compare spellings.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X26-1]] and [[s01-X26-2]]. Your child answers alone. Keep dated versions for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce private recopying and reading-log time; preserve discussion, written feedback and both exits. Longer: privately locate another source detail for a remaining draft need.

### Words and supports

Review conclusion, source, peer look-for, feedback, revision and stress. Use the familiar folder meanings. Enlarge leaves, allow signing or content scribing and record help. Independent spelling stays the child's. Offer an ordinary subject; no personal disclosure is required.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 uses today's dated essay and convention feedback for independent revision. Preserve X26 until day 28, with P26-1, listening reflection and actual discussion feedback. D27 retains all versions for day 36 judgment. Day 28's ten-minute Return and repair addresses X26/D27: R or O returns to relevance; S requires a meaningful response to the note. No new answer coaching during day 27 collection.
