# Day 25: Develop and link the second aspect

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return X24 and P24-1/P24-2. Return the day 24 page briefly for inspection feedback, then retain it for day 26 convention notes. Open teacher-only inspection-key.md, convention-key.md, exit-key.md and day key. Student Side 2 supplies today's convention slips; print no duplicate set. Keep it folded until collection. Set out source-tagged plans, authorized reading leaves, folders and references. Cover the half-sheet exit separately. Do not display any convention key sentence.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 24's exit and inspection choices. If many showed error O, review [[s01-K25-R1]] from day 24; for errors W/AG, review [[s01-K25-R2]] instead. Repair one earliest need only. Others enter Choice workshop with the dated plan. Rehearse the chosen review concurrently; hear at most two forty-five-second samples. Convention slips wait for day 26 feedback.

#### 10 min. Source rereading

Children reread at most 200 taught words from their chosen profile/poem, about two reading minutes. Retrieve day 21 source locations and check the second aspect's notes. Review aspect: one focus within the subject. Keep an actual life fact separate from an inference about inspiration. Do not turn an unsupported motive into a fact. Teacher checks source locations privately, without completing a body paragraph.

#### 25 min. Choice workshop

Review day 14 precise: specific enough to identify the intended feature. **Ask.** [[s01-P25-1]] Expect stanza, the familiar group of verse lines. Children mark their choice before discussion; do not replace every vague word for them.

Review day 22 fitting links. **Ask.** [[s01-P25-2]] Expect removal of the false causal link; notebook color cannot explain the rain. Inspect that single relationship without rewriting a paragraph. Also and another add information; for example introduces an instance; because needs a supported cause.

Children privately draft the second body paragraph on folder pages, keeping it unjudged. Connect their second aspect to source details with meaningful links and precise words. Teacher runs one five-minute group for the earliest remaining X24 need. More than ten with the same need receive whole-class repair in that slot. Collect P25-1/P25-2 before revealing the key.

#### 10 min. Convention collection

Unfold Side 2 only now. Independently complete C25-sentence, C25-fragment and C25-runon. Then complete C25-reference and C25-spelling as one reference-check sequence, using a genuinely doubtful spelling from current taught-source work. Retain the first attempt, entry, reference identity and checked result. If no word is doubtful, record that honestly; consultation evidence stays pending. No invented uncertainty and no dictated words. Do not coach an answer.

Collect originals. Prepare written notes after class for day 26, about ten minutes at twenty seconds per child. Keep the five components separate on the descriptive record. The child revises independently day 27; judgment is day 36.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X25]], first seen now. Seven minutes: independently revise on the half slip. Three minutes: collect and mark thirty responses at six seconds each. Look for a logical link or clear reference and a precise term replacing the vague noun. Preserve the original meaning. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; distinguish P from Q. Return day 26 Opening. After class, mark thirty pairs of inspection choices at eight seconds each, four minutes, for the same return.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review day 20 schwa and day 19 known-base reading with [[s01-W25]], then [[steps:s01-W25]]. Pairs consult the reference, cover, write and compare all letters. Retrieve grade 3 guide words to locate entries. Keep rehearsal unjudged and separate from diagnostic slips. If many guess the reduced vowel, compare the printed entry before another attempt.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Reread a taught source and update the familiar log with date, title, purpose and actual detail. Keep both body drafts and source tags together. Teacher prepares a list of component needs from actual work; no extra reading screen. Preserve the earlier versions for tomorrow's written feedback.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the short prompts with Side 2 and the exit covered. Notebook columns replace folder forms and source tags. Use a printed spelling reference. Preserve poem lines and profile paragraph numbers.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy prompts separately and use notebook folder pages. A printed reference replaces a dictionary.

**Skip.** Class groups and teacher marking.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 24 feedback.
- **10 min. Read and inspect.** Reread a taught source. Try [[s01-P25-1]] and [[s01-P25-2]].
  **Say.** "Does notebook color explain the rain?" Expect: no; because falsely claims a cause.
- **10 min. Write.** Keep the second body draft unjudged. Unfold Side 2 and complete its five convention entries alone.
- **5 min. Words.** Use [[s01-W25]] and compare reference spellings. Keep dated first attempts.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X25]]. Your child answers alone. Keep the work for feedback and later revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce private recopying and reading-log decoration; keep source checks, conventions and exit. Longer: privately check another link in the child's own draft without adding a judged paragraph.

### Words and supports

Review precise, stanza, aspect, fragment and run-on. A complete thought needs an appropriate boundary, as on day 22. Enlarge print, allow signing or content scribing, and record help. Independent spelling stays the child's. Public sharing permits passing.

### Tomorrow

Day 26 returns X25 and P25-1/P25-2. If many show P, inspect the false rain link again; for Q, compare vague and specific nouns. Use one earliest Opening repair. Give prepared teacher notes on drafts and convention slips during Choice workshop. Discussion uses actual sources; conclusions remain unjudged drafts.
