# Unit 2 record sheet: Animal defenses

Use the same scale for each independent exit or assessment component. Enter **2** for independently shown, **1** for partly shown or shown with help, and **0** when an observed attempt does not show the criterion. Leave unobserved or unfinished components blank. In the margin or on the evidence sheet, note support, date and item ID. Keep spoken and paper observations in separate entries. Mark retrieval as a dated note without a score.

For checks, retain an original, a dated written move and a later independent revision. Score the eligible evidence only after correction. Attach the device and discussion records. Use actual individual observations and record missing evidence as pending. Copy the evidence sheet by day or by child to keep a short set of components together.
