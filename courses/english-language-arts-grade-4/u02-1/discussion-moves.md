# Discussion moves card

**Facilitator.** Invite someone to begin.

**Evidence-finder.** Find a detail and its location.

**Questioner.** Ask about a speaker's idea.

Everyone takes a turn. Listen to the person speaking.

| Before talking | After talking |
|---|---|
| Detail and location ____________ | One shared idea ____________ |
| Question ______________________ | What I understand now _________ |

Use a detail. Ask or answer a linked question.
