# Teacher key for individual word reading and dictation

Keep this copy with the teacher. Use the exact prompts in s02-A44l and s02-A44m. For the reading turn, reveal the two isolated words to the assigned child first. Then uncover their sentences. Ask the child to show how the written parts help with reading. Listen for the complete word including its ending. Give written feedback before the next independent reading. Hold the words and sentences from each child until the first turn.

**photographic**  
**retraction**

The photographic record caught a brief movement.  
The claw moved inward during retraction.

For the dictation, say **protection** and **attraction**, in that order. Show the student only the numbered blanks. Allow the child to identify and check a doubtful spelling with a reference. Record the original and correction separately.
