# Section 2.4 opener: Write an informative essay

## Organize an explanation

Use essay-structure from unit 1. The opening establishes the subject. Sections group details by their job in the explanation. Links express how one detail relates to another. A conclusion returns to the explanation. Use the paragraph-frame and rhetorical-situation from unit 1: consider reader, purpose and form before choosing what to include.

Inspect one weak preview, misplaced detail, misleading link or unrelated ending at a time. Give dated peer and adult feedback. Children retain their own plan, draft, feedback and revision without a judgment until the named later collection. Errors T, U, V and W call for more purposeful groups, connected sources, a fitting link and a related ending. Use accurate action, feeling and state words. Check can, may and must against the condition expressed.

## Prepare devices and paper

Prepare fifteen devices and saved passages. Cohort A types on day 60, cohort B on day 61. Set aside five minutes for private exchange, then thirty uninterrupted minutes for a one-page sitting. Save the child's actual duration and page evidence. Capture the dated comment, response, revised text and publication. If a device fails, continue the literacy work on paper and leave the missing digital evidence open. A paper gallery is available.

Keep the two day 62 passages separate and covered until launch. Use the print first source and the saved digital second source. Collect a new independent explanation in the forty-minute block. File the original and written feedback for day 63 independent correction and day 64 judgment. The earlier expert essay follows its own feedback and revision dates.
