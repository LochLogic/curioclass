# Following language and theme

A summary is a brief account of important events without personal opinion. A theme states a message the events develop. Help readers move from a topic to a message by checking one claim against a particular action. When a figure or saying is read literally, return to the surrounding action and test that reading.

Keep the two dates leaf with the released installment. Use one meaning card at a time. Make the clause connection visible by tracing a relative pronoun to a person, thing or possession. Trace a relative adverb to a place, time or reason. Let students keep their own summaries in the folder while they inspect isolated errors on different texts.

Use the discussion roles and an actual written detail. Compare formal language for visitors with informal wording for partners who share context. Both serve a purpose. Pair partners for brief turns, and keep paper reflections separate from live observations.

Collect the day 134 word pattern probe in the word block. Correct the observed pattern in the next word block with a different word. Return the reading exit in the repair block. Leave unfamiliar checked words out of the earlier word work. Keep all original marks and corrections dated.
