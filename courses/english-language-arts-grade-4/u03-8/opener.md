# Revising and discussing judgments

Keep the dated process essay in the child's folder. Use a separate envelope for the independent opinion piece. On day 115 give out the new tale set and language sample in their assigned parts. On 116 open only the two assigned new source passages at the independent task. Collect the unfinished argument and return that same draft for continuation on 117. Read the separate listening passage aloud twice on 117. Collect every listening response before distributing its printed text. Review firsthand, an account from someone who was there, and secondhand, an account drawn from other sources.

Use the unit record scale. Match each marked criterion to actual evidence. Keep the earlier written feedback with the original and revised paragraphs. Reuse matching individual rereading and critique records within their allowed window. Leave an unobserved criterion blank. Written listening notes document what a child heard. Record spoken evidence on the private observation sheet.

On 118, give children an individual priority and time to revise. Cycle five children at a time on managed devices. During each five-minute turn, children open their own files, revise, exchange a short comment and save. Use the class’s existing audience arrangement. If devices are unavailable, exchange paper versions with the same reader choices and note the digital work still due. Use the publication log to record actual actions. The independent sentence exit stays separate from publication practice.

On 119, run six sequential discussion groups of five. Give each group five minutes. Each child has one 45-second speaking turn. Use the remaining 75 seconds for handover and an unfinished observation if it fits. Collect each child’s individual paper. In the private word block, hear thirty readers for 45 seconds each and allow two and a half minutes for directions and handover. Keep the materials closed until the assigned parts. Assemble the private word card from the unit plan and keep it beside the teacher record. Return a written priority privately and record further needs for the next unit.

Canal sources describe loss of land, construction danger and illness. Guide children to speak as present-day readers. Invite a pass during public talk and offer a private way to show evidence.
