# Section 2.7 opener: Revise, check and share two routes

## Keep the collections separate

On day 74, hold back the cold text and labeled display until collection. Read the listening paragraph twice without explanation. Collect the paraphrase before giving children the transcript. Give children the remaining reading and response time in the same cold transfer part. Keep the separate grammar sample covered until its five-minute collection. Use the generated teacher key privately. Return the cold work on 75. Use the later dated revision when judging it on 76.

Audit only the eligible dated transition, ending and precise-language revisions. A transition relates events in time. A conclusion follows the narrated events. For error V, identify the real order before changing the link. For error W, check what the closing detail has to do with the event. For error AJ, connect the located evidence to the reason. Use the same unit record scale for each independent component. Keep an unfinished or unobserved entry open.

On 75, gather the actual folder and device evidence. Children can mark pages independently while the teacher checks the evidence after class. Read only the reserved half slip during its five-minute exit slot. Return its written move in day 76's launch. Keep the second branch condition unseen until that launch. Collect the full branch within 40 minutes. Return one written move for independent revision on 77 before marking eligible evidence.
