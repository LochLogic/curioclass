# Private fall reading cards

Teacher only. Print one current card at a time. No answers are printed here.

Day 1, children 1 to 9: Read the gate revision aloud. What makes the noise? Read fantastic. Reread the first line.

Day 2, children 10 to 16: Read the first two September 11 stanzas. What do Ben's hands do? Read volcanic. Reread one sentence.

Day 3, children 17 to 25: Read the October 16 passage from Instead I said through We went to reading. What changes? Read magnetic. Reread.

Day 4, children 26 to 30: Read Williams's poem. What is beside the wheelbarrow? Read Atlantic. Reread one pair of lines.

Two teacher minutes per child, sixty total across four days. Use the actual printed
passage and word. Record observed meaning, decoding, phrasing and help on record.md.
Other children use the familiar word cards independently. No simultaneous teacher-led dictation.
