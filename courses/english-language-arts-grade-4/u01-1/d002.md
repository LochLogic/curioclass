# Day 2: Find the words behind an idea

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use choice packet for the supplied task order. Print complete taught reading copies: part 1 and the complete Williams biography. Lay out the R3 paw picture, reading log, skill cards, sound and spelling wall, and unit 1 component record. From fall reading cards and section 1.1 independent exit cards, print only today's cards. Gather day 1 tickets and role notes. Cover the exit stimulus and prepare the log sample. Teacher keys: practice-key-02.md, practice-key-03.md. Print current sheets listed above.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 1's s01-X01, inspected practice, and role notes. If many day 1 tickets showed error Q, use [[s01-P2-1]] to compare the weak gate caption with its specific alternative. Otherwise children inspect their own word choice privately. No child rewrites the exit as a model. Feedback names one next step; notebooks remain unjudged.

#### 15 min. Read and locate

Whole class reads complete part 1 of A Notebook of Small Things, 1,032 words, about nine minutes, tracking the print. Preserve line breaks. Review poem, detail, and poet from day 1. Review evidence: the line or page that shows an idea is true, from grade 3. Readers may pass in public talk.

Use the last three minutes for [[s01-R3]]. Review grade 3: the words say and the picture shows, what a picture adds to the words. Cover the drawing first, locate Tansy's action, then reveal the R3 paw picture. Error O invents a message: reconnect the drawing to the actual words.

#### 10 min. Evidence choices

Pairs attempt [[s01-K2-1]] before the teacher demonstrates [[steps:s01-K2-1]]. Match the supplied claim to its supporting phrase. **Ask.** Which phrase belongs with reluctance? Expect held wrist. A phrase about Leila's boots does not support dislike. For error A, reread the action and reject the unsupported match. Keep the demonstration a matching task, without a complete detail-to-thought explanation.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

Review the grade 3 reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Alone, inspect [[s01-P2-2]], then start today's log using a located detail from part 1. Fix its inaccurate detail by checking the gate revision. Collect the inspected log line; mark 30 at ten seconds each, five minutes here. Return it on day 3. The other ten minutes hold directions, logging, and the repair group. Children choose an accessible taught entry for later reading. Hold one five-minute repair group for either error A or error O while others locate and log. No child receives both this group and the same Opening repair.

#### 10 min. Notebook

Read the complete Williams biography in Five Poets at Work with shared tracking, 388 words, about four minutes. Use only this profile, excluding its reserved companion. Use [[s01-P2-3]] to distinguish a factual reading purpose from a personal preference. Review purpose for reading from day 1. Source 2A and Source 2B support the profile's life facts; make no new life claims.

Children answer [[s01-P2-4]] privately, copying a phrase from September 15. The dated note stays in the folder, unjudged. It is not a composed inference response. Ask permission before reading anyone's notebook.

#### 5 min. Exit

All answer [[s01-X02]] alone on the half-sheet ticket, exactly as printed. Release the journal line now for the first time. Allow four minutes for reading and writing, one for collection. No peer help or answer key is visible. Marking takes 30 children times 20 seconds, ten minutes in Partner rereading below. Record the two component columns on the common shown, shown with help, not yet seen scale.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading: phrases and expression, no speed. Keep one purpose: understand why the hands stay down. Use [[s01-P2-5]], a 28-word selection. Both children read twice in concurrent pairs. The listener offers one useful phrasing or accuracy suggestion; the reader checks the letters and rereads. Partners discuss the hands' action to keep meaning central. Reuse each child's matching fall observation; no extra teacher listening group today. While pairs work, spend ten minutes marking tickets and five minutes preparing handbacks and support notes.

#### 15 min. Fall rotation

Hear only children 10 to 16 using [[s01-P2-6]], two minutes each, fourteen minutes plus one for transitions. Reading, meaning, unfamiliar long-word decoding, and a successive sentence reread share one turn. The other children work independently on [[s01-P2-7]], review of familiar closed/open chunks and reference spelling. Attend to letters before confirming meaning. Error AL keeps the misspelling after checking: point to the letter difference. Keep unjudged word practice in folders; no simultaneous teacher-led spelling task.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Share full reading copies and track a teacher read-aloud. Hand-copy the paw mark and task choices. Each child needs an exact private exit and visible or tactile words for decoding. Loose paper can hold the log and notebook.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: share reading copies and copy the choices on paper.

**Skip.** The class fall rotation and group repair.

- **5 min. Repair.** Inspect yesterday's ticket privately. Try [[s01-P2-1]].
  **Say.** Which caption names the sound? Expect the loose latch clacked.
- **12 min. Read.** Share complete part 1. Try [[s01-R3]] with a drawn paw mark.
  **Say.** Which words support it? Expect Tansy stepped on this notebook.
- **8 min. Choose.** Try [[s01-K2-1]] before reading its steps. Share the complete Williams profile; try [[s01-P2-3]].
- **5 min. Record.** Inspect [[s01-P2-2]], then date a log. Reread the short selection together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X02]]. Your child responds alone. Keep it dated.

### Shorter or longer

After unjudged attempts, use practice-key.md for partner or self feedback. Private notebook work stays unjudged in the folder. Return inspected practice on day 3 with the log and exit; keep one descriptive scale on the teacher record.

Shorter: reduce optional notebook elaboration, preserving the complete readings, choices, partner rereading and exit. Longer: locate another part 1 phrase for an existing supplied claim, without composing an exit-shaped explanation.

### Words and supports

Review the grade 3 evidence and picture wording above. Review day 1 image and poet. Offer shared tracking, large print, home-language discussion, or a scribe; record support. Read the exit stimulus aloud only when recorded as reading support; never explain its interpretation. Keep independent word decoding independent.

### Tomorrow

Return s01-X02 and inspected log lines on day 3. Error A is an unsupported thought; Opening matches a different taught action to a claim. Error O adds a command never printed; Choice workshop deletes an invented event. Day 3 reads complete part 2, reviews the story map, and introduces infer and summary. Retain actual fluency observations for later use.
