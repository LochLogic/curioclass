# Reading poems and becoming a writer

## English

We will read poems and an original notebook story. Children will notice details, discuss ideas, and revise writing. Later, the story names a cat's illness and death plainly. We preview this gently. Children may use ordinary or invented subjects. They never need to share personal loss or private family experiences. They may pass during public talk and respond privately. Please contact the teacher about a reading support or a quiet alternative. A student who is 18 or older receives this note and makes their own leave-out or consent choice. School rules still apply. Optional: enjoy a familiar poem together. No report from home is requested.
