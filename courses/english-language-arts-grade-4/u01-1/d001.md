# Day 1: Words that help a reader hear

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use choice packet for the supplied task order. Print the gate versions, biography headings, skill cards, and current independent exit cards only. Use complete taught reading copies without later entries. Send the family note. Set out the discussion moves chart and role notes, sound and spelling wall, reading log, and unit 1 component record. Fall reading cards supply the private fall reading card. Number children privately, 1 to 30. Cover the exit. Teacher keys: practice-key-01.md. Print current sheets listed above.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Whole class tracks the two gate versions, about 40 words. This is course day 1; there is no preceding day or readiness check. Pairs try [[s01-K1-1]] before the demonstration. **Ask.** Which version helps you hear? Expect the revision, especially loose latch and clacked. Reveal [[steps:s01-K1-1]]. Introduce image as words that help a reader imagine a sensory detail. Post: How does reading poems help someone become a writer?

#### 10 min. Retrieval

Use three minutes for explicit teaching and [[s01-R1]], alternating brief partner turns. Teach explicitly: a pronoun takes a noun's place; I names the person speaking. Introduce Ben as the notebook speaker, addressing his teacher. Point to I in the revision before children try. Use [[s01-R2]] for the remaining seven minutes. Review familiar word chunks and the ending ing. Teach a complete sentence: it tells who or what and what happens. Contrast The gate with The gate rattled. Children then write their own sentence for R2. Point back to Ben's name if the speaker is confused. Keep this writing sample ungraded in the folder. Do not interpret a fragment as a failure to understand the story.

#### 15 min. Discussion moves

Review the grade 3 discussion moves chart: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Add five responsibilities: facilitator invites, evidence-finder locates, questioner asks, responder answers, reviewer accounts for listening. Pairs inspect [[s01-P1-1]], [[s01-P1-2]], and [[s01-P1-3]] against the gate revision without producing a complete exit response.

Teacher hears children 1 to 6 in linked exchanges, two minutes per child, twelve minutes total. Include preparation and one responsive turn in each window. Other pairs take concurrent turns, then mark their chosen role and a located gate word on the page. Everyone may pass publicly; offer a private exchange later. Paper never proves oral skill. Record only heard actions; give these six dated written feedback during the last three minutes. Collect everyone's role notes for day 2.

#### 12 min. Choice workshop

Use [[s01-P1-4]]. Review grade 3 purpose for reading: choose a book by the question it must answer, not by the cover. Compare only on-file notebook and biography headings; save whole biographies for tomorrow. Review the grade 3 reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Children begin a log using the gate reading. For error Q, use one five-minute group on the gate words. While others log independently, mark the four choice responses together: 30 papers at ten seconds, five minutes. Two minutes introduce the purpose task. Return the choice page on day 2.

#### 10 min. Notebook

Alone, [[s01-P1-5]]. A poet writes poems. Review poem: lines and sound, may have no events. Ben writes to his teacher; children may choose their own imagined audience. Do not request family information or read a notebook publicly without permission. Notebook work stays in the folder, unjudged. Offer one private word-choice comment when invited.

#### 5 min. Exit

Release [[s01-X01]] alone on paper, exactly as printed. Allow three minutes to write and two to collect. Its tall response box uses half a sheet. No model, peer discussion, or answer key appears on the ticket. Teacher marking is 30 children times 20 seconds, ten minutes during independent Skills below. Record shown, shown with help, or not yet seen for each component, never points.

#### 30 min. Skills

Review grade 1 closed/open syllables: a closed syllable ends in a consonant and usually has a short vowel; an open syllable ends in a vowel and usually has a long vowel. Use [[s01-P1-6]]. Review the sound and spelling wall: the week's irregular words marked by the class. Supply the reference word latch for a letter-by-letter comparison. Others work independently and reread the gate; no teacher-led group runs alongside screening.

Hear children 1 to 9 on [[s01-P1-7]], privately, two minutes each: eighteen minutes. Show the reading and unfamiliar word only at the child's turn. Listen to decoding before offering context; record meaning, accuracy, phrasing, self-correction, and actual spelling support. One short successive rereading fits the same turn. Mark exits for ten minutes while everyone continues independently; two minutes remain for transitions. Children are screened once across days 1 to 4.

### Other rooms

No devices are needed. Hand-copy the short gate versions and task choices on the board. Copy each exit privately, with its exact stem and a tall writing box. Share taught reading copies; each screened child still needs visible or tactile words. Use loose paper for a notebook and log.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the gate versions and choices. Use loose paper for a notebook.

**Skip.** The class role rotation and fall screen.

- **5 min. Retrieve.** Read the gate versions. Explain: I names Ben, the speaker. Try [[s01-R1]].
  **Say.** Who speaks? Expect Ben; I or my shows his voice.
- **8 min. Compare.** Try [[s01-K1-1]] before reading its steps together.
  **Say.** Which words help you hear? Expect loose latch and clacked.
- **7 min. Choose.** Inspect [[s01-P1-1]] and [[s01-P1-2]]. Trade the two roles.
- **10 min. Notebook.** Try [[s01-P1-5]]. Let private writing stay private. Reread the gate together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X01]]. Your child writes alone. Keep it dated.

### Shorter or longer

Unjudged practice gets partner or self feedback from practice-key.md after each attempt. Notebook work stays unjudged in the folder. Return inspected paper with day 2 tickets; record only observed components.

Shorter: reduce optional notebook decoration and repeat reading. Keep the choices, diagnostic turns, and exit. Longer: compare another supplied gate word with its vague counterpart, without composing a model passage.

### Words and supports

Review detail: something that happened or is there in a book, the proof for a reason. Use the grade 3 discussion wording above. Introduce poet and image through the gate. Offer large print, signing, or a scribe for composition; log all support. A scribe does not supply a word choice. Independent decoding remains the child's.

### Tomorrow

Return s01-X01 and role notes on day 2. Error Q is a vague image: use the weak gate caption in Opening for five minutes. Other needs go to Choice workshop or Fall rotation, one route per child. Day 2 reads complete part 1, locates evidence, and inspects a reading log. Retain diagnostic and role records for the later discussion windows.
