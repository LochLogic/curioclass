# Day 3: Actions, feelings, and important events

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print complete part 2, student page, private fall card, and covered half-sheet exit. Keep the generated day answer key teacher-only. Set out day 2 tickets and inspected log lines. Prepare a blank story map: who, want, events joined by arrows, message. Put the supplied event labels on the board. Preview the episode about writing another person's worry without permission. No personal disclosure is requested.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return day 2's s01-X02 and inspected log lines. If many day 2 tickets showed error A, use [[s01-P3-1]] with a different taught detail from September 15. Children match the supported label and reject an invented teacher order. Individual returned responses remain private. Error O needs wait for Choice workshop's separate five-minute route, rather than adding another repair here.

#### 15 min. Read and map

Whole class reads complete part 2, 1,026 words, about nine minutes with tracking. Students may pass in public talk. Review grade 3 story map with a message box: who, want, events joined by arrows, message. Review setting: where and when a story happens; locate classroom and October. Use [[s01-R4]] for three minutes, ordering supplied labels on the map rather than composing a summary.

Review grade 3 message: what the actions and results show, argued from events, not the topic. Offer two message labels: good writing permits sharing anything; ask before sharing another person's private experience. Children select the latter using the refusal and removal. Review theme as a message that comes back in more than one book; one episode alone does not establish recurrence across books. Do not ask children for a personal parallel.

#### 10 min. Meaning choices

Introduce infer: work out an unstated idea using text details. Introduce summary: a brief account of important events without personal opinion. Connect these names with familiar action and message work. Try [[s01-K3-1]], then demonstrate [[steps:s01-K3-1]]. Review grade 3 trait; one moment does not prove always. Point to the later question that changes the supplied choice. **Ask.** Which label fits the later question? Expect respects her choice. Keep the model a label match, without a complete inference and summary response.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

Pairs use [[s01-P3-2]] and [[s01-P3-3]] on the student page. Review event and response: what happens, then what a character says or does about it. Reorder the supplied actions and cross out the opinion; match words and actions separately. Teacher collects this practice for day 4. For error C, point to an action the preference cannot replace. For error D, compare the early mistake with the later question. One five-minute group uses [[s01-P3-4]] for day 2 error O. Everyone else works independently; no child gets two routes for one miss.

#### 10 min. Notebook

Alone, [[s01-P3-5]]. Review day 1 image and poet through a private word-choice note. Writing stays in the folder, unjudged; it is neither a modeled summary nor a judged inference. A fictional subject is welcome. Children can decline sharing, including with peers. Invite them to reread a taught entry if they finish.

#### 5 min. Exit

Release [[s01-X03]] alone on paper, exactly as printed, preserving its lines. This new poem is first seen now. The half-sheet ticket holds two response spaces. Four minutes for reading and responding, one for collection. Marking takes 30 children times 20 seconds, ten minutes during independent Skills below. Use the same shown, shown with help, not yet seen scale with separate feeling/evidence and summary columns; no third theme demand.

#### 30 min. Skills

Hear only children 17 to 25 using [[s01-P3-6]], two minutes each, eighteen minutes. Reading, meaning, unfamiliar long-word decoding, and one successive reread share the turn. Preserve all chunks before context; record actual attempts and help. The other children independently use [[s01-P3-7]], review of familiar closed/open chunks and reference spelling from days 1 and 2. Then they read taught entries. Mark exits for ten minutes while all continue independently; two minutes remain for transitions. No second fall screen or simultaneous teacher-led activity.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Share complete reading copies for a read-aloud with tracking. Copy the map headings and supplied labels on the board. Each child still receives a private exact exit and visible or tactile words for decoding. Loose paper can hold the dated notebook and log.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: share the reading copy. Write the supplied labels on scraps.

**Skip.** The class fall rotation and group repair.

- **5 min. Repair.** Inspect yesterday's ticket privately; try [[s01-P3-1]].
  **Say.** Which label matches? Expect notices something useful.
- **12 min. Read.** Share complete part 2. Try [[s01-R4]] with the labels.
  **Say.** Which action comes first? Expect writes about Leila.
- **8 min. Choose.** Try [[s01-K3-1]] before reading its steps. Use [[s01-P3-2]].
  **Say.** Which phrase is an opinion? Expect I like notebooks.
- **5 min. Notebook.** Try [[s01-P3-5]]. Keep writing private if requested. Reread a taught entry.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X03]]. Your child responds alone. Keep it dated.

### Shorter or longer

Uncollected practice stays in the folder with immediate descriptive feedback. No points are assigned.

Shorter: trim optional notebook elaboration, preserving complete reading, choice tasks and the exit. Longer: match another supplied trait label against an early and later action without composing an assessed response.

### Words and supports

Review grade 3 story-map, message, theme, and trait wording. Review evidence and detail from days 1 and 2. Infer and summary name today's new work. Offer large print, signing, oral rehearsal, or a scribe; record support. No one must disclose another person's worry. Independent word decoding remains the child's.

### Tomorrow

Return s01-X03 and collected choice work on day 4. Error A is an unsupported feeling; Opening selects a supported label from part 2. Error C substitutes opinion for events; Choice workshop reorders supplied taught actions. Error D concerns an unchanging trait claim, also repaired there. Day 4 reads the complete Williams poem, marks separate features, and finishes the fall rotation. Keep the map and log for day 5.
