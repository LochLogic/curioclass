# Unit 1 assessment answer key

Teacher only. Keep this key separate from student pages.
Accept other defensible responses supported by the actual text.
Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen for every component.
A blank means no observation. Record support and dates.

## s01-A07-meaning

Text: g4-u1-05; lines 1 to 8

Prompt: What does thing with feathers suggest in Hope?

Answer: Hope is imagined as a persistent singing bird, suggesting encouragement through difficulty.

Look for: figurative referent hope | persistence through trouble | feedback 7, revise 8, finalize 9

Samples: Hope keeps encouraging someone through trouble, like the singing bird. | A real bird lives inside a person.

Wrong: A real bird lives inside a person. (Y).

## s01-A09a-infer

## s01-A09b-summary

Look for: brief event sequence | no personal opinion | feedback 9, revise 10, score 11

Wrong: I enjoy gardening. (C).

## s01-A09c

Text: g4-u1-01 g4-u1-08 g4-u1-09; reserved N9/P9/S9 whole set

Prompt: Compare N9, P9, and S9 using their structural features.

Answer: N9 has verse lines, down/brown rhyme and recurring beat, with four main beats in the opening lines. P9 narrates in a paragraph. S9 names cast/classroom setting, dialogue and bracketed directions. Do not demand identical meter throughout.

Look for: features from all three | verse/rhythm/meter | prose paragraph | cast, setting, dialogue, directions | feedback 9, revise 10, score 11

Samples: N9 rhymes down/brown in verse. P9 uses a paragraph. S9 labels actors and bracketed actions. | Paper size decides their forms.

Wrong: Paper size decides their forms. (E).
