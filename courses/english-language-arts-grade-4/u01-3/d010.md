# Day 10: Prepare, listen, and link

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare current choice cards, role feedback sheet and complete taught reading copies. Print January part 5 only, plus the whole Dream Variation. Keep future entries covered. Set out returned day 9 papers and s01-X08 with inspected day 8 choices. Open teacher-only choice-key.md, exit-key.md and the day answer key. Keep the exit card teacher-held until Exit exchange. Mark children 19 to 24 for workshop and 25 to 30 for exit listening.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Honor day 9's Tomorrow: return assessment feedback and day 8 work. If many, more than ten, missed evidence, use [[s01-P10-6]]. Choose only one common repair: evidence, message/opinion [[s01-P10-7]], or form [[s01-P10-8]]. Review theme: a message that comes back in more than one book. Day 3 extended this to a supported message within one text. Teach on this already-taught story for five minutes; children independently correct their own returned check for five. Never display a completed assessment answer. X08 uses the self-directed form choice. Collect revised day 9 work for judgment after class day 11.

#### 15 min. Read and listen

Whole class follows the complete January part, 1,047 words, and Dream Variation, 77 words. Read aloud with tracking, about ten minutes. Content includes Ben's Black self-identification, room to move/rest and respecting a declined invitation. No identity acting or private disclosure. Review stanza: a group of lines in a poem, from day 5. Pairs try [[s01-P10-4]], two thirty-second turns concurrently. Hear two volunteers briefly; public talk may be passed.

#### 10 min. Role-note audit

Review day 1's discussion moves chart: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Review its five assigned role actions. Use [[s01-P10-3]], then [[s01-P10-1]] and [[s01-P10-2]]. Children fix separate fragments before hearing feedback. Error J needs a located source, error K a finished turn, error AK a performed role. Question/response choices address errors AH/AI; unrelated remarks show error L, ignored listening error M. These are isolated repairs, never a full inspiration discussion model.

#### 17 min. Choice workshop

Children complete current choice cards independently, then [[s01-P10-9]] in notebooks, unjudged. Teacher hears children 19 to 24 for two minutes each, twelve minutes total. Use the supplied rest question, January space reply and waiting reflection from the cards. Each child performs their assigned role in a brief exchange; teacher supplies the other turn. Record actual preparation, role, question, answer, link and listening evidence, leaving unheard components blank. Give each a written component target on the role feedback sheet. Remaining five minutes hold transitions and written feedback, no extra teacher group.

#### 8 min. Exit exchange

Read [[s01-X10]] exactly now, including its fresh source. Allow one minute of mental preparation. No writing for this spoken item. Pairs speak concurrently; six due children exchange with the teacher, forty-five seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Reserve the remaining time for directions and transitions. Record actual speech only, with support. Collect earlier paper notes separately; they cannot prove speech. Children may use a private live exchange. Children 1 to 12 receive this oral turn day 11; 13 to 24, day 15.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review day 1's closed/open chunks and reference spelling with [[s01-P10-5]]. Pairs read each word, compare letters with dictionaries, and correct independently. No new pattern cycle. Work stays in folders. If guessing replaces letters, cover the context, read every chunk, then restore the sentence.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P10-10]], review of the reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Read taught copies only. Dated reactions and logs stay in folders, unjudged. Teacher adds written role feedback from actual observations without another listening screen.

After class, mark thirty choice sheets at twenty seconds each, ten minutes. Return them day 11 Opening. Use one scale everywhere: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record separate component columns, dates and support; never diagnose an unheard turn.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy current short choices and exact reading text without a printer. Read the teacher-held exit card aloud. Complete texts are separate reading copies; the short task sheet does not replace them.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy current choices and read complete texts aloud together.

**Skip.** Class listening rotations.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use the private day 9 note and correct one component independently.
- **12 min. Read.** Share complete part 5 and Dream Variation.
- **8 min. Choose.** Try [[s01-P10-1]] and [[s01-P10-2]].
  **Say.** Which question links to rest? Expect: Which words show rest? Which reflection follows the peer? Expect: Now I notice waiting.
- **5 min. Write.** Try [[s01-P10-9]]; date and keep the unjudged reaction.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X10]] aloud for the first time. Allow silent preparation, then converse. Expect a link between touching grooves and imagined paths. Keep a dated note of actual speech, separate from paper preparation.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce optional notebook length while preserving repair, complete readings and exit turns. Longer: locate a second pace choice without composing a full assessed response.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, the line or page that shows an idea is true, from day 2. Review the named roles. Large print, line dividers, signing or a private live exchange provide access; record help. Do not supply a spoken answer.

### Tomorrow

Return X10 notes and inspected choices in day 11 Opening. Error O needs actual words before an interpretation; choose one five-minute repair, with other needs on independent cards. Day 11 reads part 6 and compares narrator access. Judge day 9 independent corrections after class day 11: thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes; return finalized feedback day 12. Return today's six workshop role targets for day 12 revised preparation. Finalize oral component records day 14 from actual evidence.
