# Day 11: Check the teller's knowledge

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare complete February part 6, excluding A Whole Afternoon. Print whole taught The Missing Line tellings and its whole taught scene; exclude reserved companions. Lay out story map with a message box and current source-label cards. Keep half-sheet X11 covered. Open teacher-only choice-key.md, exit-key.md and the day answer key. Set out returned day 10 choices, role feedback sheet and oral notes. Mark children 1 to 12 for retained X10 listening. Teacher keys: choice-key-01.md, choice-key-02.md, choice-key-03.md, choice-key-04.md. Print current sheets listed above.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 10's notes and choices. If day 10's exit showed error O, use the taught story action choice [[s01-P11-9]]: locate words before interpreting. Repair only this component for five minutes; other needs get independent choice cards in workshop. Never model the shell response. Retain day 9 corrections for judgment after class today.

#### 15 min. Read and trace

Whole class reads complete part 6, 1,005 words, about nine minutes with tracking. Use [[s01-P11-3]] before feedback; locate the two separate judgments rather than composing a portrait. Perspective means a way of seeing or thinking. Ben and Mina judge the rehearsal differently. Inspiration means a detail, reading or response that sparks a writing choice; locate it when reading, without rehearsing an assessment response.

Review grade 3's story map with a message box: who, want, events joined by arrows, message. Use [[s01-P11-7]] privately, unjudged. The new step is dating actions to trace a changing attitude. Compare a label with an action, rather than completing a paired explanation.

#### 10 min. Narrator access

Reread The Missing Line's three complete taught tellings, 291 words, about three minutes. Review day 8's narrator, the teller of a story, and its first-person participant versus third-person names/pronouns. Review point of view: character, narrator, article writer and reader as four labeled views.

**Ask.** [[s01-K11-1]] Children choose alone before the model. Then show [[steps:s01-K11-1]]. The author creates a teller; the reader interprets the words. A character's perspective and the narration form are different labels. Sort separate hope, action and guess fragments with source-label cards; do not produce a complete two-telling comparison.

#### 10 min. Live replay

Review day 8's description/direction matching. Read the complete first-person prose aloud: quiet hope in paragraph 1, pauses after hallway sounds. Stage the whole taught scene accurately, with teacher and willing reader or paper figures. Remain at floor level; soft taps supply sounds. All children locate [[s01-P11-4]] before feedback. Optional face-up opening: identify the changed action, then replay accurately. No reserved staging difference is used.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

Alone, children answer [[s01-P11-1]], [[s01-P11-2]] and [[s01-P11-6]], using current choices and labels. Reveal feedback only after attempts. The third-person telling here reports outward events; other third-person tellings can report thoughts. Error F assumes narrator equals author; error O invents Mina's thought. One five-minute teacher group repairs the earliest unmet component on these taught fragments. Others revise their own map privately. No complete paired model or character portrait.

#### 5 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X11]] now. Children answer alone on the half slip, with its new tellings open. It asks for change and narrator knowledge in one comparison. Collect all thirty. Mark after class at twenty-five seconds each, 12.5 minutes, returning day 12 Opening. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen, with separate look-for columns and support notes.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review r-controlled syllables from grade 1's far-mer work and consonant-le from grade 2's handle work. Use [[s01-P11-5]]. Review grade 3's three-syllable division: split into chunks, check each chunk, then blend. Model re/mem/ber; read all letters before checking context. Pairs try each word in concurrent turns, then check reference spelling letter by letter. Familiar chunk spellings may sound reduced in a whole word; confirm the full pronunciation. Work stays in folders.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P11-8]] with familiar copies. Review reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Teacher hears children 1 to 12 on retained s01-X10, forty-five seconds each, nine minutes. Repeat its exact source and ask; use retained mental preparation, no written response. Record actual speech and private feedback. This replaces optional conferences. Unheard children 13 to 24 are due day 15. Other children read and log quietly.

After class, inspect thirty choice sheets at twenty seconds each, ten minutes; return day 12. Judge day 9 independent corrections at ninety seconds each, forty-five minutes; return finalized written and actual reading feedback day 12. Map and word work stay in folders, unjudged.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy current choices and exact source text without a printer. Two paper figures replace actors. Complete reading copies remain separate from the short task sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or actors: copy short choices and use two folded-paper figures.

**Skip.** Class listening rotation.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 10's private note. Locate evidence on a taught page.
- **12 min. Read.** Share complete part 6, then all three taught tellings.
- **8 min. Inspect.** Try [[s01-K11-1]] and [[s01-P11-2]].
  **Say.** Which label fits the hope? Expect private thought. Can third-person tellers reveal thoughts? Expect yes; check each actual telling.
- **5 min. Words.** Try [[s01-P11-5]], checking the dictionary together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X11]] for an independent paper comparison. After collection, expect both keep then share; the first gives relief, the third gives Sima's reason. Keep dated feedback for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: omit the optional altered replay; preserve accurate performance, complete reading and exit. Longer: locate an additional action for a dated map, unjudged.

### Words and supports

Review setting, where and when a story happens; event, what happens; response, what a character says or does. Large print, tactile separators, signing or a scribe support composition; record help. Public talk and acting may be passed. Independent decoding stays the child's.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 Opening returns X11, inspected choices, day 9 finalized correction and actual reading feedback. If many show error F, repair narrator/author with the taught hope label, never Raj's map. Other needs use one Choice workshop group. Day 12 locates thoughts, words, actions, setting and turning events, then revises earlier role feedback. Return X10 listening feedback privately; unheard turns stay pending, never inferred from paper.
