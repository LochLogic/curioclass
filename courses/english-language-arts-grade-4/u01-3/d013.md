# Day 13: Explain change with evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print N9, The Tray, eight numbered lines, and fresh N13, A Whole Afternoon, ten numbered lines, once each. Cover N13 until Launch. Withdraw filled maps. Open teacher-only assessment-key.md and day answer key. Gather actual role observations, written feedback and day 12 revised notes. Prepare six groups of five, record columns and private speaking arrangements.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch

Return day 12 role targets. Uncover N13; children read independently and mark one detail. N9 is familiar baseline; N13 supplies fresh reading evidence. Sources stay open. No X12 repair or supplied interpretation.

#### 10 min. Written check

Alone: D13a, [[s01-A13a-stated]] and [[s01-A13a-infer]], four minutes shared. D13b, [[s01-A13b]], six minutes. Record s01-A13b-evidence from that same description, no extra writing. Collect unchanged first attempts. These sample stated/inferred change, connected character depth and located literary evidence.

#### 30 min. Discussion window

At each group's own turn, read [[s01-A13c-prep]] exactly. Observe six sequential groups for five minutes each: five children times forty-five seconds, 3.75 minutes; seventy-five seconds remain for missing linked exchanges and notes. Others read taught entries or inspect role cards independently. No rehearsed response.

Record preparation, turn, role, question, answer, link and listening review in the seven s01-A13c columns. Reuse matching actual observations from the preceding ten days with written feedback and revised preparation. Unheard components stay blank; paper cannot prove speech. Private live exchange replaces a public turn within its allocation.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Scan thirty written responses at twenty seconds each, ten minutes. Give private component notes, no answers or scored revision. Children use [[s01-P13-1]] for five minutes. Keep actual discussion notes. Use one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; separate components, dates and supports.

#### 10 min. Word work

Use [[s01-P13-2]] silently, review of days 10 and 11 patterns/reference spelling. Work stays in folders, unjudged. No new check-word practice.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P13-3]] with taught copies, review reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. No additional teacher screen. Logs stay in folders.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact prompts and source lines. Preserve numbering and verse line breaks.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy exact prompts and N9/N13 once, preserving lines. Be the discussion partner.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Uncover N13. Your child reads and marks a detail independently for five minutes. Allow four minutes for [[s01-A13a-stated]] and [[s01-A13a-infer]], then six for [[s01-A13b]]. The evidence component uses that same description. Sources stay open. Finish with fifteen minutes for the oral discussion card, s01-A13c-prep. Take a linked turn. Retain earlier actual components, feedback and revised notes.
  **Say.** Discuss what inspires Ben. Use the entry and respond to another reader's idea. Expect a located link to observation or Mina's response. Record actual speech.
- **15 min. Feedback.** Give a component note and use [[s01-P13-1]]. Keep the original unchanged. Bring work to day 14 repair; do not teach its answers now.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve check allocations; record extended access separately. No extra assessed task.

### Words and supports

Review taught terms only. Offer large print, signing, private speech or a composition scribe; record help. Never supply interpretations.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 Opening returns X12 and day 12 choices. Return and repair returns D13: five minutes teaching on different taught fragments, five independent correction. Error A needs evidence; error D needs connected details. Finalize oral records from actual observations after feedback, never presumed speech. Judge written revisions after class day 15, thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes; return day 16. Day 14 reads part 7 and gathers inspiration evidence.
