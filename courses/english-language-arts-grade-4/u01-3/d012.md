# Day 12: Locate details before a discussion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out current choice and source-label cards, story map with a message box, role feedback sheet and complete taught reading copies. Keep X12 covered and N13 out of packets. Prepare whole February 6 and February 10 entries and Dream Variation. Set out finalized day 9 written/reading feedback and returned day 11 work. Open teacher-only choice-key.md, exit-key.md and the day answer key. Mark children 25 to 30 for role exchanges and five due readers.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return day 11's X11 and choices, plus finalized day 9 corrections and actual reading feedback. Return day 11's actual X10 oral notes privately. If day 11's tickets showed error F, repair with [[s01-P12-9]] on the taught story, never Raj's map. Review narrator as teller; I may belong to a fictional participant. One repair only; other needs enter Choice workshop. Do not add a second reading screen for returned feedback.

#### 10 min. Reread

Read complete February 6 and 10 entries and whole Dream Variation, about 500 words total, roughly five minutes. Track words with shared reading support. Review stanza: a group of lines in a poem. Children silently mark a thought, word or action on a taught entry, without composing a full portrait. Preview the text's noisy room and choice of quiet space; no compulsory acting or personal disclosure.

#### 15 min. Locate and label

Review setting, where and when a story happens; event, what happens; response, what a character says or does. Teach turning event: an event that changes what a character does or understands. It needs a before-and-after connection in the actual text.

**Ask.** [[s01-K12-1]] Children locate before seeing [[steps:s01-K12-1]]. Moving cards responds to noise; by the window locates that action. Use separate source-label cards, not a finished character portrait. Children try [[s01-P12-1]] and [[s01-P12-3]]. Classify one fragment at a time as thought, spoken words, action, setting or turning event. Review grade 3's story map with a message box: who, want, events joined by arrows, message. Children add labels privately; maps stay unjudged.

#### 20 min. Choice workshop

Use [[s01-P12-2]], then [[s01-P12-7]]. Review day 1's discussion moves chart: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Each child dates one revision of earlier written role feedback. Teacher hears children 25 to 30 in two-minute supplied-turn exchanges, twelve minutes total. Use the rest question from day 10 and today's kitchen-space reply; children perform their assigned role. Record actual question, answer, role and link separately, then give a written target. Do not rehearse an inspiration discussion.

The next five minutes hold one repair group: the earliest unmet narrator/evidence component on a taught fragment. Other children work independently. Three minutes remain for transitions. Revise preparation from feedback without treating the paper as speech.

#### 5 min. Notebook

Use [[s01-P12-4]]. Date private notes and keep them in folders. A child's own description remains unjudged, with no prescribed three-part preparation response.

#### 5 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X12]] now. Children read its new case and prepare notes alone on one half slip with a tall response box. Collect all thirty. Mark after class at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen, separate look-for columns and access help. Return day 14 Opening; no repair teaching on day 13.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review yesterday's three-syllable analysis, r-controlled/consonant-le and reference spelling with [[s01-P12-5]]. Pairs divide, blend and check each letter, then correct independently. Letters and chunks come before context; a familiar guess is error H, an unchanged spelling mismatch error AL. Word work stays in folders, unjudged.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review partner rereading with feedback: phrases and expression, no speed, learned in grade 3. Use [[s01-P12-6]]. Use the complete taught texts as reading copies; the first prose paragraph and first verse stanza are short practice spans, each under 120 words. Partners name a purpose, read, receive one specific note and reread. All pairs work concurrently.

Hear only five due readers at two minutes each, ten minutes; five minutes remain for partner feedback and transitions. Cycle six groups of five over three weeks. Reuse matching observations from the preceding ten days. Record actual purpose/meaning, prose and verse accuracy/rate/expression, and confirmation or self-correction separately. Unheard evidence stays blank. No planted errors or additional individual screen.

#### 5 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P12-8]] with familiar copies. Review reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Keep logs and drafts in folders. After class, inspect thirty choice sheets at twenty seconds each, ten minutes, and six role notes at thirty seconds each, three minutes. Return choices day 14; role targets return day 13 Launch. Retain actual observations, written feedback and revised preparation together.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy current choices and short exact source leaves without a printer. Read complete taught texts together. Separate reading copies preserve whole texts without crowding the task sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy current choices and exact reading leaves. No partner: take the other turn.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use day 11's note and the taught narrator claim.
- **10 min. Read.** Share complete February 6 and 10 entries and Dream Variation.
- **8 min. Locate.** Try [[s01-P12-1]] and [[s01-P12-3]].
  **Say.** Is arranging reading an action or setting? Expect action. Is Mina's concern reported or guessed? Expect reported thought.
- **7 min. Reread.** Try [[s01-P12-6]]. Give one phrasing note and hear the phrase again.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X12]]. Your child writes independently. After collection, expect repeated trying, hall/music-table setting and the low note changing frown to grin. Keep dated notes for day 14; give no new case practice tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: shorten private notebook writing, preserving label instruction and exit. Longer: locate another independent detail, without composing a scored portrait.

### Words and supports

Review evidence and perspective from earlier days. Provide line separators, large print, signing or a composition scribe; record support. Public speaking may be passed for a private live exchange. Independent decoding remains the child's; oral evidence requires actual speech.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 first reads N13 and uses N9 as familiar baseline. Children bring written role targets and revised preparation, without advance N13 practice. X12 error D needs several located details; hold repair until day 14 Opening, alongside inspected day 12 choices. Other errors receive one existing workshop route later. No X12 reteach on assessment day. Finalize the prepared oral record day 14 from actual observations, feedback and revision.
