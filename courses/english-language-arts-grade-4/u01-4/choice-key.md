## s01-P14-probe-paper

Text: original task; no source text; original probe sentence: After returning, I placed the torn page carefully beside my notebook.

Prompt: Mark familiar parts in returning and carefully.

Answer: re-turn-ing; care-ful-ly. Base turn/care, prefix re- and suffixes ing/ful/ly.

Look for: five-minute paper probe within ten Word work minutes | five assigned oral samples across the block, one minute each; others pending for existing later rotations | feedback 14, correction/extension 15 | paper cannot show speech

Samples: re / turn / ing; care / ful / ly | care / ful / y

Wrong: care / ful / y (AL). Omitting the second l produces carefuly, not carefully.

## s01-P14-probe-oral

Text: original task; no source text; original probe sentence: After returning, I placed the torn page carefully beside my notebook.

Prompt: Read returning and carefully alone and in the printed sentence.

Answer: Read every letter and familiar part, preserving returning and carefully; then confirm meaning in the printed sentence.

Look for: five-minute paper probe within ten Word work minutes | five assigned oral samples across the block, one minute each; others pending for existing later rotations | feedback 14, correction/extension 15 | paper cannot show speech

Samples: Observer: both whole words decoded fully alone and in the sentence. | Says caring for carefully without reading every part.

Wrong: Says caring for carefully without reading every part. (H). Distinct paper analysis and actual oral evidence.
