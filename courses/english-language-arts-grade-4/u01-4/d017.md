# Day 17: Explain inspiration for an unfamiliar reader

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy N9, The Tray, eight numbered lines, and N13, A Whole Afternoon, ten lines, once each. Remove filled plans and sign models. Open teacher-only assessment-key.md and day answer key. Prepare blank lined paper, written-feedback sheets and five due reading records. Use the day answer key's source-specific reading and literary-evidence examples.

### The day

#### 10 min. Launch

Read the two D17 directions exactly. Sources remain open. This is a new writing task on familiar reading, not a cold-reading score. Keep day 16 tickets for day 18; no repair teaching today. Children reread and locate details independently. Supply no model or interpretation.

#### 35 min. Writing check

Alone, answer [[s01-A17-topic]] on lined paper. One sitting covers eight written component records. Budget: topic/groups eight, development ten, links six, precision five, close six minutes. Children choose their drafting order; these are allocations, not forced pauses. Audience and evidence add no tasks. Collect unchanged first attempts.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Scan thirty responses, twenty seconds each, ten minutes. Give one dated written note on the earliest unmet component. Children file first attempts and notes in five minutes. No answer model, teaching or scored correction. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; blanks remain unobserved. Revise day 18; finalize after class day 19, thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes.

#### 10 min. Word work

Silently use [[s01-P17-1]], review of days 14 to 16 parts and spelling. Work stays in folders, unjudged. No new instruction.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Independently use [[s01-P17-2]], familiar capitals. Keep it unjudged. Quotation instruction remains day 19.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review day 14's taught spans: The Missing Line first-person prose paragraph 2 and February 14 verse. Partners reread for meaning with feedback. Retain matching preceding-ten-day observations. Hear only five due missing-component readers, two minutes each, ten within fifteen. Record actual purpose/meaning, accurate successive prose/verse phrasing and letter/context confirmation in the three RF columns. No staged errors or duplicate diagnostic. Unheard cells wait for existing rotations. Private written RF feedback returns day 19.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact prompts and numbered sources. Plain lined paper replaces the response page.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy exact directions and number source lines. Use plain lined paper.

**Skip.** Class rotations. Do not show answer models.

- **35 min. Assessment.** Give N9/N13 and [[s01-A17-topic]]. Your child reads and writes independently. Sources stay open. Retain the first attempt unchanged.
- **10 min. Feedback.** Give one written component note without an answer. File both versions. Revision belongs to day 18.
  **Say.** Which note will you use for your later revision? Expect: your child identifies the named component, without revising now.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve the sitting. Record extended access separately; add no task.

### Words and supports

Review taught terms only. Offer enlarged sources, signing and content scribing; record help. Independent reading stays the child's.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 Return and repair combines D17 feedback and X16: five minutes instruction on a different taught case, five independent revision. Start with the earliest unmet component. Finalize day 19; return day 20. No additional sitting.
