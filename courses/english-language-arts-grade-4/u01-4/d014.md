# Day 14: Notice the evidence behind a poem

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy only part 7, March 4 through April 18, preserving verse lines and rights. Gather day 12 choices, X12, day 13 first attempts and actual oral observations. Separate teacher-only choice-key.md, exit-key.md, pattern-key.md and day answer key. Use choice-cards.md to prepare student-page prompts; print no duplicate set. Print the sign text only. Select five due word readers and five due rereaders from the record. Cover future companions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return day 12 choices and X12. Review connected character details from day 12. If many day 12 tickets showed error D, pairs select connected actions in [[s01-P14-3]], using February 3 instead of the held case. Expect practicing with Dad because visitors asked questions. Others inspect their dated note. One repair only, not another discussion assessment.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 13's assessment. Five minutes: review February 3. **Ask.** [[s01-P14-4]] Expect Mina's evidence of successful visitor reading and drawing. Error A needs located evidence; error D needs connected actions. Five minutes: children independently correct D13 writing from their component note. Keep first attempts unchanged. Finalize D13 oral columns from actual observations, feedback and revised notes only. No paper-to-speech inference or extra screen.

#### 15 min. Read and gather

Preview plainly: Tansy is old and unwell; the later ending includes her death. Offer supported quiet reading and a quiet return. No personal loss story is requested.

Read all part 7, 1,005 words, about nine minutes with tracking. Review evidence and inspiration from day 11. Pairs label a supplied March 4 note in [[s01-P14-5]], rather than compose an explanation. Expect the chair and poem notes, not happiness alone. Use [[s01-P14-1]] with April 18 open. Delete the publication claim; do not infer every private note is a finished poem. Judge only selection and location. After class inspect selections, thirty times forty seconds, twenty minutes.

#### 15 min. Paragraph inspection

Introduce rhetorical-situation: reader, purpose, form. Review grade 2 audience: the listeners a reader reads for. Here written explanations also have readers. Use the fictional sign inspection model, separate from held writing cases.

**Ask.** [[s01-K14-1]] Pairs choose before the teacher answer. Expect visitors, explaining display use, informative paragraph. Review grade 3 informative paragraph: topic, grouped facts, link word, closing. Add precise nouns and links expressing the actual relationship. Organize means put related information together. An aspect is one part of a topic. A biography tells a person's life; this notebook is fiction.

Inspect one weakness at a time. The lunch sentence breaks grouping. Try [[s01-K14-2]] before showing its isolated repair. Expect also for another related fact, not a false because. A useful heading can name display use; a picture is optional. Never assemble a complete repaired paragraph.

#### 10 min. Choice workshop

Pairs answer [[s01-P14-2]] on the sign card, then receive feedback. Expect a noun naming actual materials, not stuff, error Q. Own writing notes remain unjudged folder work. Teach one five-minute group on the earliest unmet component; others finish selections. Collect choice cards for day 15. Additional needs wait for existing workshops.

#### 5 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X14]] only now. Children answer alone on half-sheet tickets, with the exact printed stem. Collect thirty. After class mark detail and connection: thirty times twenty seconds, ten minutes. Error O invents a rhyme demand; error A lacks evidence. Write one component note. Return day 15 Opening.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review grade 3 base, prefix, suffix and three-syllable chunk reading. In the first five minutes everyone completes [[s01-P14-probe-paper]] on pattern-probe.md, its sentence printed once. Across the block hear only five assigned children on [[s01-P14-probe-oral]], one minute each. Paper work continues concurrently; other oral cells remain blank.

The last five minutes review the dictionary check with guide words: locate, compare every letter, correct. Use only probe words. After class give written feedback, thirty times fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Correct or extend in day 15 Word work. Omitted letters and incomplete chunks need different notes.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading with feedback. Use [[s01-P14-6]] with separate taught reading copies. Partners each reread and give one accuracy or expression observation. Teacher hears only five due children, two minutes each, ten minutes within fifteen. Reuse matching preceding-ten-day records. Note actual purpose, meaning, accuracy, rate, expression and letter/context confirmation; never stage an error. Private oral feedback returns day 15 with existing papers. Source punctuation stays intact outside the task-sheet limit.

#### 5 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P14-7]]. Review reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Accessible taught copies and private log stay in the folder. No added screen.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy current cards and the exact exit stem. Preserve verse line breaks. A board replaces the display.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy today's cards and exact ticket; read the separate text together.

**Skip.** Class rotations and the oral sampling schedule.

- **5 min. Return.** Read dated feedback; correct one D13 component independently.
- **12 min. Read.** Share all part 7. Preview illness without requesting personal stories.
- **8 min. Inspect.** Try [[s01-P14-1]] and [[s01-P14-2]] with sources open.
  **Say.** Which note goes beyond the entry? Expect: publishing every poem is unsupported.
- **5 min. Reread.** Share the taught February 14 lines. Give one specific reading observation.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X14]]. Your child answers alone. Keep dated work.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce pair reporting, preserving complete reading, repairs and exit. Longer: locate another unsupported supplied claim without writing a model paragraph.

### Words and supports

Review evidence and inspiration. New: reader, purpose, form, precise, organize, aspect. Offer enlarged lines, signing, quiet reading and content scribing; record help. Decoding remains independent. A child may pass publicly and respond privately.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 Opening returns X14 and all inspected selections. If many invent evidence, repair error O using a different taught case; if details are unlinked, repair error A. Word work returns paper and actual oral probe feedback. Judge D13 revised writing after class day 15, thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes; return day 16. Tomorrow finishes part 8 and inspects related endings.
