# Day 16: Plan for a reader who needs the details

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out finalized D13 writing and day 15 feedback. Print complete January 17 and February 14 entries only, with rights and line breaks. Keep N9/N13 and all filled organizers away from planning. Open teacher-only choice-key.md, exit-key.md and day answer key. Cover today's half-sheet exit. Retain blank notebook paper for unjudged plans and the writing feedback sheet for dated notes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Return day 15's tickets and inspected choices, finalized day 13 written assessment and actual day 10 oral feedback. If many X15 tickets invented cancellation or a fixed screen, repair error O using [[s01-P16-3]] on the taught rehearsal. If many gave unrelated summaries, target error C with that same relevant-detail selection. Repair one earliest need only. No second D13 correction sitting.

#### 10 min. Retrieval

Review grade 3 informative paragraph: topic, grouped facts, link word, closing. R8 takes two minutes, [[s01-R8]], on the weak sign model. Expect the clear topic and a matching protective cover or return instruction. If missed, connect just one detail to the named topic.

R9 takes three minutes. Use [[s01-P16-2]] to order plan, draft, feedback, revision, then [[s01-R9]] to compare two folder labels. Review grade 3's writing process through the essay folder: plan, draft, feedback, revision, edit. The revision names what helps the visitor; the draft's things is vague. A miss prompts a side-by-side comparison, not a new model paragraph. Retrieval is unscored.

Use the remaining five minutes to retrieve day 14's rhetorical-situation: reader, purpose, form. Review audience: the reader or listeners. Partners use [[s01-P16-8]] to name visitors, explanatory purpose and paragraph form. Do not supply an inspiration plan.

#### 15 min. Source inspection

Pairs reread complete January 17 and February 14, about five minutes total. These taught entries stay open. Use [[s01-P16-4]]; expect unsupported because the sign opens conversation rather than answers everything. Mark selection and located evidence only.

Review the familiar terms in context: a biography tells a person's life; this notebook is fiction. A quotation keeps exact source words. Introduce names the topic; an example illustrates it. Organize groups facts, an aspect is one part, audience names readers, purpose names the writing's job. These meanings were established before today; no new decoding set.

Partners sort only supplied labels: instructions, visitor choices, unrelated lunch. This is relevance inspection, not a complete Ben explanation. Do not use N9 or N13, completed plans, or the held exit case.

#### 20 min. Choice workshop

Use [[s01-P16-1]] on the student page with the separate sign text. Judge only the misplaced detail. Review grade 3 plan made of groups: notes sorted by purpose under headings. Revisit the partial groups on [[s01-P16-9]]: cover with instructions, or cover with lunch. The first serves visitor use; the second mixes topics, error N. No complete plan model.

Children use [[s01-P16-5]] privately with a selected taught entry. Own plans stay unjudged in folders. Remove supplied examples before they plan. The teacher leads one five-minute group on the earliest need while others plan independently. Collect inspected student pages for day 18 feedback. Own plans and retrieval notes stay in folders.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X16]] only now. Children read the unfamiliar case and plan independently for an unfamiliar reader. Use the exact stem on a half sheet; supply a separate blank continuation leaf if needed. No filled frame or source hint. Allow all ten minutes for reading and response. Collect thirty.

After class mark topic, relevant selection and order with related close, thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen minutes. Error N copies unrelated facts without grouping. Note one earliest component. X16 returns day 18 Return and repair with D17; day 17 has no repair teaching. This is writing transfer on a new case, not a cold-reading score for the familiar novel.

#### 10 min. Word work

Apply familiar four-syllable division from yesterday with [[s01-P16-6]]. Children attempt independently, then use the reference: in-vi-ta-tion. Review dictionary guide-word lookup and compare the whole spelling, not just its first letter. The known ending can sound like shun; do not erase its letters. Explain one correction or confirm an accurate attempt. Keep attempts unjudged in folders. No assessment words are practiced.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Use [[s01-P16-7]] with accessible taught copies. Review the dated reading log. Children keep it privately. Up to five due missing-component conferences at two minutes each fit within twenty, ten minutes total. Use the same taught February 14 verse and The Missing Line prose paragraph 2 as day 14. Observe only missing components; reuse recent matching actual evidence. Remaining time is reading, not another screen.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy short prompts and the exact exit stem. Preserve whole separate reading copies and verse line breaks. Plain paper replaces a printed planning frame.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the short prompts and exact ticket; use plain notebook paper.

**Skip.** Class listening rotations.

- **5 min. Return.** Inspect day 15 feedback. Select one relevant rehearsal detail in [[s01-P16-3]].
- **10 min. Retrieve.** Order [[s01-P16-2]], then compare sign draft and revision in [[s01-R9]].
  **Say.** What improved? Expect: the revision names the return instruction and its use.
- **10 min. Read and plan.** Reread one complete taught January 17 or February 14 entry. Use [[s01-P16-5]] privately.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X16]]. Your child plans alone. Keep the dated response for later feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public sharing, preserving independent plans and the full exit. Longer: inspect a different supplied irrelevant detail; do not rehearse tomorrow's sources.

### Words and supports

Review reader, purpose, form, audience, topic, aspect, organize, evidence, quotation, biography, introduce and example. Offer large print, movable blank notes and content scribing without choosing evidence. Record help. Independent word reading stays the child's. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 writes one independent paragraph from N9/N13 with sources open. Do not teach from X16 before that sitting. Return X16 and inspected selections on day 18 Return and repair. If many show error N, combine the earliest grouping need with D17 feedback in that single ten-minute slot. Other needs use later workshops. Keep own practice plans unjudged and separate from assessment writing.
