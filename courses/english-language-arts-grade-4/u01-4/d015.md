# Day 15: An ending can hold more than one feeling

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy only whole part 8, May 6 through June 16, with its rights line. Set out dated probe feedback and D13 corrections. Use choice-cards.md for the student-page prompts; print no duplicate cards. Keep teacher-only pattern-key.md, choice-key.md, exit-key.md and day answer key separate. Retain the day 10 shell card and preparation for children 13 to 24; the earlier section's exit-key.md stays private. Cover today's exit until response time. Teacher keys: choice-key-01.md, choice-key-02.md, choice-key-03.md. Print current sheets listed above.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 14's tickets, inspected selections and actual rereading feedback. If many X14 responses invented a claim, repair error O using [[s01-P15-3]] with the taught February 14 entry. Expect Ben leaves the unknown writer alone. If many lacked evidence, repair error A by locating that line. Repair only one need; remaining needs enter one workshop group. Never display yesterday's held response as a model.

#### 15 min. Read ending

Preview plainly: Tansy dies. The text gives no medical details. Offer supported private reading, company or quiet and a quiet return. No one shares a personal loss account.

Read all part 8, 1,025 words, about nine minutes, tracking print. Pause for quiet reflection; children may mark a page privately or leave the notebook closed. Review Ben's writing choices from part 7. The booklet is complete, his notebook has a title and blank pages, and he still misses Tansy. Sharing a poem does not require sharing every page.

#### 10 min. Resolution labels

Review resolution: how the story's difficulty ends or changes. Use [[s01-P15-5]] in pairs; label action, result and remaining feeling. Accept the chosen gate page and private cat poem without claiming grief disappears. Use [[s01-P15-2]] on the choice card. Judge only the match, not a whole explanation. Error C replaces source work with an unrelated response; error O invents an event. Do not demonstrate the held puppet case or a complete ending explanation.

#### 15 min. Conclusion inspection

Use [[s01-P15-1]] with the sign model. Pairs choose before feedback. Expect the visitor-related ending; cold lunch is unrelated, error R. Explain that a close returns to the explanation's purpose. It need not repeat every sentence.

Review day 14 reader, purpose, form, grouping and precision. Introduce means name the topic for the reader; an example is a case showing the idea. Review quotation as exact words, without teaching new quotation punctuation. Use [[s01-K15-2]] to contrast addition with a false cause. Use [[s01-P15-6]] to name the actual return instructions. A heading helps only if it identifies the subject. Never produce a complete corrected paragraph.

Review grade 3 four-chunk long words using [[s01-K15-1]]. Children attempt first; model in-for-ma-tion, four syllables, then compare every letter with the dictionary entry. Pronunciations can vary; preserve sounds and spelling, not an arbitrary visual cut.

#### 10 min. Choice workshop

Partners revisit one sign ending or link from today's cards and receive feature-specific feedback. Own conclusions remain unjudged folder work. A five-minute teacher group treats the earliest unmet need; other children independently correct only the selected sign fault. Collect inspected cards for day 16. No full Ben paragraph or plan is judged.

#### 5 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X15]] only now. Each child reads the new case and answers alone on the half sheet. Collect thirty; after class mark thirty times twenty seconds, ten minutes. Look for an effective response and actual result, not an invented repair. Give one written component note; return day 16 Opening.

#### 10 min. Word work

First five minutes: return the paper probe and actual oral feedback. Review grade 3 familiar parts, then independently correct returning or carefully on the feedback card. Children already accurate explain a base and affix. Error H omits or substitutes sounds; error AL drops a letter despite checking. Paper cannot prove oral decoding.

Next five minutes: reread and check information with the reference, reviewing guide-word lookup. No new word set or reserved assessment words. Keep corrected probe versions together; children retain the folder work. Missing oral evidence waits for existing rotations.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children use [[s01-P15-4]] with accessible taught copies. Review reading log: date, title, my purpose, one detail. Logs stay in folders.

Hear children 13 to 24 on retained s01-X10, forty-five seconds each, nine minutes within twenty. Give the exact shell card orally only at each child's own turn. Use retained mental preparation; do not request writing. Judge actual prepared contribution only. This replaces optional conferences. Children 25 to 30 were due day 10 and children 1 to 12 day 11; retain actual evidence, never presumed completion. Write private oral feedback for day 16.

After class judge D13 independent corrected writing, thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes. Retain first attempt, note and revision; return day 16. Inspect today's selections, thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen minutes. Use one record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; blank is unobserved.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact current prompts. Read complete part 8 together when independent reading needs support. Preserve source punctuation and verse lines.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: hand-copy the current cards and exact ticket. Read the complete part together.

**Skip.** The class oral rotation; retain any already given oral feedback.

- **5 min. Return.** Read feedback and correct one familiar word on the probe card.
- **12 min. Read.** Share all part 8. Offer quiet without asking about personal loss.
- **8 min. Inspect.** Try [[s01-P15-1]] and [[s01-P15-2]].
  **Say.** Does the ending return to the sign's purpose? Expect: helping visitors read does; lunch does not.
- **5 min. Reread.** Reread a chosen taught page together. Keep any private notes private.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X15]]; your child answers alone. Keep dated work.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce workshop reporting, keeping complete reading and the oral window. Longer: inspect another sign noun's precision, not a new paragraph.

### Words and supports

Review audience, evidence, resolution, reader, purpose, form and word parts. Introduce and example name jobs in the sign explanation. Offer large print, signing, quiet reading and content scribing. Record help; decoding stays independent. Respect a pass in public talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 Opening returns X15, inspected choices, finalized day 13 writing and actual day 10 oral feedback. If many invent cancellation or a fixed screen, repair error O using another taught case. Unrelated summaries need error C repair. Only one repair fits Opening. Day 16 retrieves information writing and the folder process, then plans independently. The class has read the whole novel.
