# Day 31: Make the idea clear for a listener

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-only day, inspection, exit and assessment keys. Return D30 feedback, X29/P29 and corrected actual probe notes. Separate the eligible day 30 revision from new revisions. Copy presentation fragments once on the student page; draw the labeled hinge beside Fragment B. Keep exit covered. Select five missing-component readers for existing conferences. Retain the taught December 5 leaf and set out references.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 30's assessment with written feedback, plus s01-X29, s01-P29-1 and s01-P29-2. If many day 29 tickets showed Q, use [[s01-F31-1]]; O returns to the child's printed moment, without inventing a cause. S in D30 needs a meaning change connected to the prior note. One earliest repair only; others enter the workshop group. Review the grade 3 folder trace. Preserve the eligible day 30 version for judgment; today's new changes stay unjudged.

#### 10 min. Presentation inspection

Three minutes retrieve grade 3 main idea and supporting details using [[s01-R12]]. Read fragments A, B and C in their supplied order, without arranging a finished introduction. Children mark on paper; pairs may rehearse simultaneously. Hear at most two actual forty-five-second samples, ninety seconds within three minutes. Everyone else has paper evidence only.

Then seven minutes inspect [[s01-P31-1]] and [[s01-K31-1]]. For K31, first rush and blur Fragment C; then repeat it with distinct words and a followable pace. Children choose before seeing [[steps:s01-K31-1]]. A listener needs the topic and relevant details, clear words and time to understand. Model only the delivery change. Do not compose an assessed introduction.

#### 15 min. Source and visual

Review December 5's first 120 words, about one reading minute. It shows persistence, without asking anyone to disclose personal trouble. On the board, place a simple gate-hinge drawing beside the shared creak fragment. Children choose [[s01-P31-2]] before seeing its key. A visual supports an idea when a feature helps the listener understand it; decoration alone does not do that work.

Review inspiration: something that sparks a writing choice. Children privately begin [[s01-P31-folder]] from their own poem and actual taught reading. Identify the reader's unfamiliarity and keep the source location. Own drafts stay unjudged in the folder.

#### 20 min. Choice workshop

Pairs inspect their supplied crossed-out lunch choice and visual choice using inspection-key.md after responding. Teacher records those two feature choices, returning them day 32. For one five-minute repair group, revisit the earliest Q/S/O/AE fault through the supplied weak subject or irrelevant detail. Others continue their own introduction privately, date it and retain it unjudged. They may draw an optional visual and label what it helps explain. Do not require recording equipment or a visitor.

After class allow three minutes per D30 folder, ninety minutes total, to finalize the eligible revision's two components, then return its judgment day 32. New day 30 or day 31 feedback responses do not replace that retained evidence.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X31]] exactly as printed. Eight minutes independent writing, two collection. No added visual or oral task. After class allow thirty seconds per response, fifteen minutes for thirty, to check orientation and a supported inspiration. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help separately. Return X31, R12 and P31 day 32 Opening. Spoken R12 results exist only for the two heard samples.

#### 10 min. Word work

Teach scrib/script as roots meaning writing. Script can mean written words; an inscription is writing marked on a surface. Use [[s01-W31]] before [[steps:s01-W31]], then [[s01-W31-2]] to connect both root forms. Read aloud naturally, attending to all syllables.

Review known un-, re- and dis- from grade 3 through [[s01-W31-3]]. Children try first, then consult the teacher key and their reference. Review grade 3 dictionary checking with guide words; record the actual inscription entry and compare letters. Do not use reserved probe words for group rehearsal.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children reread their taught December 5 leaf and keep the familiar log. Up to five missing-component conferences take two minutes each, ten within twenty. Hear pending day 29 word samples privately on the original card, then independent corrections from existing written notes where available. A new first hearing receives written feedback for day 32; it cannot count as an already corrected sample. Unheard evidence stays blank. No concurrent repair group.

### Other rooms

The board or copied fragments replace a display. Draw the labeled hinge by its matching fragment. Blank paper replaces lined forms. Fold the exit until response time. A private live exchange replaces public speaking.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy fragments and questions without keys. Draw the hinge. Use blank paper and a spelling reference. The adult replaces partner and display.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public performance.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read returned feedback and try [[s01-F31-1]].
- **10 min. Inspect.** Read the fragments. Try [[s01-R12]], [[s01-P31-1]] and [[s01-P31-2]], then check.
  **Say.** "What does the hinge picture explain?" Expect: the source of the creak.
- **10 min. Draft.** Begin [[s01-P31-folder]] privately. Keep the dated draft in the folder.
- **5 min. Words.** Try [[s01-W31]] with its answer, then reference-check inscription.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X31]]. Your child writes alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: leave own visual unfinished; keep source, feature inspection and exit. Longer: explain why the supplied decorative alternative fails to clarify the idea.

### Words and supports

Review main idea, supporting detail, inspiration, revision and expression. Presentation means an explanation for listeners; visual is something shown; pace is speaking speed. Effect means the difference a choice makes. Support means help an idea make sense. Use enlarged fragments, movable cards, signing or content scribing, recording help. Children may pass publicly.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 connects the poem to its reading influence. Return X31, R12/P31 and finalized D30 records in Opening. AE needs a named topic for a new reader; O needs a source-supported idea. AB needs a meaningful visual feature; AC needs a followable sequence. If many show these, prioritize one supplied fault for five minutes; route the rest to the independent choice work. Keep unheard oral evidence pending.
