# Day 31 reading

Leila read about her brother's boots.  
There was a line about mud  
leaving little islands on the stairs.  
I could see those stairs.  
I have never been inside her house.  

You asked what helped us see.  
Omar said boots.  
Mina said islands.  
I wanted to say  
that the mud moved from outdoors  
into the place where people live,  
and that this made the brother seem real.  
But the sentence in my head  
had too many corners.  

I wrote it instead.  
You read it while we worked.  
Beside it you wrote,  
Which words made you think that?  

I drew an arrow back to the islands.  
An arrow is less frightening  
than a whole explanation.  
It can point  
while I keep my mouth closed.  

At lunch Leila asked  
why I had not said anything.  
I told her I liked her stairs.  
She said the stairs were ordinary.  
That was the strange part.  
She had made ordinary stairs  
appear in our classroom  
without carrying a single board.  
I put a small star beside my arrow.  
No one needed to see that either.
