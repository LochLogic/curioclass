# Day 29: Draft a poem with deliberate choices

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-only day, inspection, exit and assessment keys. Return X28/P28. Copy December 5: A bird that stays, first 120 words only, without later companions; retain whole Hope. Keep exit covered. Select five due probe readers, then five different due rereaders from the record. Prepare lined poem pages and references. The probe card goes only to its reader, without a key.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return [[s01-X28]] and s01-P28-1. If many day 28 tickets showed Q, review the supplied boot-image choice; E uses the mitten ending choice from day 28. One earliest need only, then a private correction to returned work. Other needs go to the workshop group. Today turns the unjudged plan into a draft.

#### 10 min. Reread

Review whole Hope and the taught December 5 entry's first 120 words, 189 words total, about two reading minutes. Warm suggests comfort and encouragement here, not a measured temperature. The entry shows persistence without constant cheerfulness. Review figurative comparison through [[s01-P29-1]]. Children mark the claim before the teacher opens inspection-key.md. Do not require a personal parallel. Keep pet-loss entries outside this reading selection.

#### 10 min. Image inspection

Children choose [[s01-P29-2]] alone, then discuss in pairs. Reveal its steps only after choices. Review line breaks and imagery from day 28: A holds bangs at the ending. B can create a different pause, but does not meet this particular sound focus. Demonstrate how to inspect that one choice, without supplying a whole poem. Collect both judged inspection choices for day 31 return.

#### 25 min. Choice workshop

Children draft [[s01-P29-folder]] alone, using yesterday's plan, without a required rhyme or personal story. Own drafts stay in the folder, unjudged. Pairs may point to one detail they can picture; review grade 3 peer look-for and teacher note, checking only the named feature. Each may pass in public talk or confer privately.

Use one five-minute repair group for the earliest unresolved Q/E/Y need, on the already supplied feature choices. Others write independently. During the final five minutes, children date and submit plan and draft with their chosen taught-source title/location. After class, allow three minutes per folder, ninety minutes total, for individual written feedback ready before day 30. Give a precise unmet criterion without supplying revised words.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X29]], exactly as printed, for eight minutes of independent writing and two of collection. No added explanation is required. After class allow forty-five seconds per poem, about twenty-three minutes for thirty children. Record image and line-choice evidence with the single component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record support separately. Return on day 31, keeping day 30 free of answer teaching.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review familiar prefixes using [[s01-W29-1]]. Five minutes of familiar practice run alongside five assigned one-minute oral turns on [[s01-A29-word]]. Each assigned reader first sees the full probe on the card during their turn. Read both words alone and in its context; note actual word parts and accurate reading. Never infer spoken performance from paper. The other twenty-five have no probe result yet. Their sheet holds familiar-prefix notes only.

Return an individual written note before day 30 Word work. Corrections use that block's first five minutes. Missing hearings wait for day 31's existing reading conferences; no extra class screen. Review grade 3 dictionary checking for a doubtful spelling in the child's own draft; it is independent of the reading probe.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Review complete-thought boundaries with [[s01-K29-1]]. Pairs choose, then check against the teacher key. This familiar convention inspection is unjudged; no assessed sentence is rehearsed.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading with feedback: phrases and expression, no speed target. Use whole Hope and the same December 5 span. Partners choose a purpose, reread successively and compare letters with context when confirming a word. Hear five different due children at two minutes each, ten within fifteen. Reuse matching observations from the preceding ten days, including day 27's pending components. Record purpose/understanding, accuracy/phrasing and actual confirmation separately. A pending prose component waits for day 32; today's verses cannot supply it. No simultaneous repair group and no second whole-class assessment.

### Other rooms

Use the board and copied stems without keys. Hand-copy the assigned text spans once. Blank lined paper replaces poem pages. Keep the exit and probe folded until their own independent turns.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy questions and lined pages; retain taught texts. Use a spelling reference. The adult replaces partner and board.

**Skip.** Class rotations and the oral probe unless your child is assigned today.

- **5 min. Repair.** Correct yesterday's returned plan from its note.
- **10 min. Read.** Reread Hope and December 5's first 120 words. Try [[s01-P29-1]].
  **Say.** "Does warm give a temperature?" Expect: no, it suggests comfort.
- **5 min. Inspect.** Try [[s01-P29-2]], then check together.
  **Say.** "Which ending holds the sound?" Expect: A holds bangs.
- **10 min. Draft.** Begin [[s01-P29-folder]]. Keep dated versions together. Write one note about a detail needing clarity, without supplying words.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X29]]. Your child writes independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: continue the own draft during later folder work; keep inspection, feedback and exit. Longer: inspect a different effect of the supplied alternate ending, without a complete model poem.

### Words and supports

Review imagery, line break, figurative comparison, prefix and revision. Warm means comfort in this reading. Review the grade 3 peer and dictionary routines. Provide enlarged print, line separators or content scribing; record help. Independent decoding is heard without reading the word to the child first.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 independently revises the poem using today's written feedback. X29 and P29 return day 31: Q needs a clear subject; O needs the given moment; Y needs the figurative sense. Day 30 Word work independently corrects actual probe feedback, especially H, skipping parts. Do not teach a new answer during the collection. Retain all poem versions for day 31 judgment.
