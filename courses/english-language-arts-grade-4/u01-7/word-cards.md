# Word meanings

**presentation:** prepared sharing for listeners.

**visual:** something shown to clarify an idea.

**pace:** how quickly speech moves.

**expression:** using the voice to convey meaning.

**explain:** make an idea clear with details.

**support:** give evidence for an idea.

**revision:** a change to earlier writing.

**effect:** what a change does for a reader.
