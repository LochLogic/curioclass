# Day 28: Plan what readers will notice

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-only inspection-key.md, exit-key.md and the day answer key. Return D27 feedback, X26 slips and P26 notes together. Preserve day 27's essay for day 36. Print only whole taught poems, one per leaf; exclude reserved companions. Keep the exit covered. Set out references, lined poem pages and the record continuation. Choose five children needing missing reading evidence.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Pick up day 27's Tomorrow: publish the essay, then plan a poem. Return the folder, [[s01-X26-1]], [[s01-X26-2]], s01-P26-1 and actual s01-P26-listen notes. Children read dated feedback silently and locate the earliest need. If many day 26 tickets showed error S, prioritize meaning changes; R/O needs a relevant ending or actual source evidence. Repairs have their own part next.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Five minutes of instruction, five of independent correction. Review the grade 3 folder trace: plan, draft, feedback, revision, edit. Use [[s01-F28-1]] on the board. Children choose first; then show [[steps:s01-F28-1]]. A useful change answers the written note. For R/O, return privately to the child's already marked source and remove an unsupported ending. Other needs use their written notes independently. Keep all versions, including the unchanged day 27 version.

#### 10 min. Read poems

Review five taught whole poems: Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening, XXII, Dream Variation, Hope and A Birthday. Their 377 words take about four minutes aloud; use six for silent rereading and attention to features. Preview Dream Variation's Black self-affirmation without identity acting. For A Birthday, retrieve dais, vair and halcyon from its reading notes. No personal parallel is requested. Review verse lines, rhythm as sound movement, and meter as a recurring stress pattern. Not every poem has regular meter.

#### 10 min. Feature inspection

Pairs choose [[s01-P28-1]] before consulting the teacher's separate key. Record only the supplied-image choice. Review line break: where one line ends and the next begins. Use [[s01-K28-1]], then [[steps:s01-K28-1]], to model how an ending can hold attention. Do not build a complete poem plan. Review prose and drama through [[s01-K28-2]]. Show its steps after choices. Page outline alone does not determine form.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

Children begin [[s01-P28-folder]] alone on lined pages; this stays in the folder, unjudged. An object in the room is enough; no personal disclosure. Reserve five minutes for one earliest-need group using the supplied fault again. Others independently publish a clean paper essay from their corrected version, then continue planning. Retain all earlier essays for day 36. Label the poem plan with today's date, reader and purpose. Do not mark it against today's exit.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X28]] for independent paper work, exactly as printed in data. Allow eight minutes to plan; collect in two. Do not ask for an additional explanation. After class, allow thirty seconds per child, fifteen minutes total, to read image and line-ending evidence. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen, recording support separately. Return X28 and P28 on day 29 Opening.

#### 10 min. Word work

Return actual day 27 reading feedback without adding a screen. Review familiar prefixes with [[s01-W28]], then its steps. Review day 22 stress using [[s01-W28-2]]. Pairs try, then consult the teacher key.

Review grade 3 dictionary check of a doubtful spelling with guide words using [[s01-W28-3]]. Stress concerns emphasis; an unstressed vowel may need a spelling check. Each child records the actual reference entry used. Keep probe words off practice.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children reread an assigned taught poem and keep the familiar reading log. Hear at most five missing-component conferences, two minutes each, ten within twenty. Use the actual due-reader list; unheard evidence stays blank. Review confirming a word through all letters and context. No repair group runs during listening. Other children continue reading independently.

### Other rooms

Copy current inspection stems and ruled lines on paper without their keys. Copy each taught poem once, preserving lines. The board replaces the display; keep exits folded until response time.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy current questions and ruled lines. Use the taught poem leaves and spelling reference. The adult replaces the partner and display.

**Skip.** Class rotations and the publication queue.

- **5 min. Repair.** Try [[s01-F28-1]], then consult its answer.
  **Say.** "Which change answers the note?" Expect: return cards names what returns.
- **10 min. Read.** Reread the five taught poems together.
- **10 min. Inspect.** Choose [[s01-P28-1]] and [[s01-K28-1]], then check together.
  **Say.** "Which ending holds the action?" Expect: A ends with drips.
- **5 min. Plan.** Begin [[s01-P28-folder]]. Keep the dated plan in the folder.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X28]]. Your child answers alone. Keep it for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: finish neat essay publication later; keep repair and the exit. Longer: inspect the alternate line ending's effect without composing a model poem.

### Words and supports

Review image: words suggesting sensory experience; line break; verse; rhythm; meter; revision. Review the grade 3 dictionary routine. Enlarged lines, movable separators, signing or content scribing preserve access. Record help. Public talk permits passing or a private turn; independent word reading stays the child's.

### Tomorrow

Day 29 drafts the original poem. Return X28 and P28 in Opening. Q means the image lacks an identifiable subject; E means spacing replaces deliberate language. If many show either, use one supplied-feature choice for five minutes, then give the child a private retry on the returned plan. Other needs use the workshop group. No held exit case enters practice.
