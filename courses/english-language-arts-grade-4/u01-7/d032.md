# Day 32: Connect a writing choice to a reading

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-only day, inspection and exit keys. Return X31, P31/R12 and finalized D30 records. Retain taught December 5 and Williams paragraph 2 leaves, excluding future companions. Set out source tags and feedback. Keep the exit covered. Select six explanation listeners today and five different due rereaders. Keep day 31's new probe notes for independent correction, without an extra hearing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return s01-X31, s01-R12, s01-P31-1, s01-P31-2 and finalized day 30 records. If many day 31 tickets showed AE, review [[s01-F32-1]]; O returns to the printed source before accepting a cause. One earliest repair only. Other needs use today's supplied choice independently. No group instruction runs during teacher listening. Own introductions remain unjudged.

#### 10 min. Reread sources

Review source locations and the distinction between a text's words and our inference. Each child rereads up to eighty words of their own poem with either the taught December 5 span, first 120 words, or Williams profile paragraph 2. This keeps the total at most 200 words, under two reading minutes. The remaining minutes locate a relevant actual detail and place its source tag beside a writing choice. No biography fact proves a poet's undocumented motive. Review the earlier source-tag routine, recording title and line, entry or paragraph.

#### 25 min. Choice workshop

Alone, children answer [[s01-P32-1]] on the supplied irrelevant-detail fault, then use inspection-key.md after responding. Record only this choice for day 33 return. Model crossing out the irrelevant information, without writing a completed two-poem connection. Review the meaningful-visual choice from day 31 as needed.

Children develop [[s01-P32-folder]] privately on lined pages. Own explanation stays unjudged. Keep the reader and purpose from yesterday, and actual details from source and own poem. A relationship may concern a subject, comparison, sound or form; resemblance alone does not prove a cause. Children name their own actual reading influence.

Pairs rehearse concurrently with [[s01-P32-listen]]. Hear six assigned children, forty-five seconds each, four-and-a-half minutes within this workshop. Keep actual source/sequence notes, no judgment of the own draft. The remaining children submit an unjudged source note; it does not count as heard speech. No simultaneous teacher repair group. Return notes to all day 33 with roles for that day's discussion; later written feedback and revision precede presentation judgment.

#### 10 min. Folder preparation

Children date and file introduction, explanation draft, own poem, source tags and visual plan. Keep the source detail beside the claim it supports. Leave unfinished writing visible for day 33's five-minute finish. Collect for that handoff, without a score. Day 33 provides individual written feedback before day 34 revision. Do not substitute today's rehearsal notes for that written feedback sequence.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X32]], exactly as printed. Eight minutes independent paper response, two collection. No oral follow-up or extra part. After class, allow thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes for thirty, to check an actual detail from both texts and a supported relationship. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen, recording support separately. Return X32 and P32 on day 33 Opening.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review scrib/script from day 31 using [[s01-W32-2]], then familiar re- through [[s01-W32]]. Children try before consulting the teacher-only steps. Review grade 3 dictionary checking with guide words; compare the actual inscription entry with the copied letters. Use five of these minutes for independent responses to actual day 31 probe notes where needed; others continue familiar roots. No reserved probe rehearsal and no new pattern.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Review the complete-thought boundary from day 22 through [[s01-K32-1]]. Pairs choose before the teacher key is opened. A dependent clause needs a complete thought; this supplied convention choice is unjudged.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading with feedback, phrases and expression without a speed target. Use December 5's first 120 words and Williams profile paragraph 2, under 200 words together. Pairs state purpose, reread successively, and check whole words through letters plus context. Hear five due children at two minutes each, ten within fifteen. Reuse matching ten-day observations. Record only actually heard purpose/understanding, accuracy/phrasing and confirmation; unheard components remain pending. No teacher repair group during listening.

### Other rooms

Copy current stems without keys. Use retained taught leaves, source tags and blank lined paper. The board replaces a display. Keep the folded exit separate. Private live rehearsal fits the scheduled turn when public talk is declined.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy current questions and use blank lined paper. Retain taught source leaves and a spelling reference. The adult replaces partner and board.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public presentation.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read the returned note; try [[s01-F32-1]].
- **10 min. Read.** Reread the own poem selection and either taught source. Keep source locations.
- **5 min. Inspect.** Try [[s01-P32-1]], then check together.
  **Say.** "Does the price detail support this explanation?" Expect: no, it is unrelated.
- **10 min. Write and file.** Begin [[s01-P32-folder]]. Keep the dated explanation, introduction, poem and visual plan together. Leave unfinished parts for tomorrow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X32]]. Your child writes alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: leave the own explanation unfinished for day 33; preserve evidence inspection, rehearsal and exit. Longer: test whether the planned visual clarifies the named relationship or only decorates it.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, inference, inspiration, source location, presentation, support and visual. A source location lets another reader find the same words. Review the grade 3 folder and dictionary routines. Provide enlarged print, signing or content scribing, recording support; independent reading stays the child's. Ordinary subjects and private exchanges remain available.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 finishes the explanation, then holds a formal discussion about reading's influence on writing. Return X32/P32 and all rehearsal/source notes in Opening. A needs a relationship supported by details; O needs accurate evidence from each actual text. If many show either, use one supplied irrelevant-detail choice within five minutes. Unheard speech stays pending for scheduled discussion turns. Individual day 33 feedback leads to independent day 34 revision, without scoring the new draft now.
