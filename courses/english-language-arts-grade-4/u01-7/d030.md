# Day 30: Revise the poem from written feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return day 29's individual written poem feedback and actual probe notes. Keep the prior plan and draft attached. Open the current teacher-only day answer key and assessment guide/key. Set out references and lined paper. The student page supplies the collection prompt; do not duplicate assessment pages. Keep X29 for day 31. Preserve a copy of today's revision.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch

Review the familiar folder routine without modeling a revision. Children read the prior note. If many day 29 papers showed missing images or unrelated content, record a day 31 repair need; give no answer teaching now. Clarify directions only.

#### 40 min. Poem collection

[[s01-A30-revise]] Alone, children revise from day 29's written feedback and explain one clarifying change. Suggested allowances: reread note five, revise twenty-five, explain five, check and file five. Preserve selected taught-source location. This is retained-process evidence, not a cold reading check.

The teacher records [[s01-A30-audience]] from the same revision for the chosen purpose and reader. W.4.5 samples meaningful response to prior feedback; W.4.4 samples clear images/order suited to reader and purpose. No extra student question. Judge the eligible revision on day 31 using shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record support separately.

#### 15 min. Feedback

Children quietly file all versions. New comments do not enter today's evidence. After class, allow three minutes per folder, ninety minutes for thirty, to compare the prior note, draft and revision and prepare day 31 written feedback. No reteaching or new response sitting.

#### 10 min. Word work

First five: assigned children independently correct or extend actual day 29 probe feedback. A missing hearing stays pending; it receives no invented result. Others review [[s01-W30-1]] independently with references. Final five: everyone continues familiar word-part work. No new pattern teaching.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Review familiar reading-log work using whole Hope or the taught December 5 span. Children read quietly and keep their source locations. No added assessment screen or new text.

### Other rooms

Blank lined paper and copied directions replace forms. Use retained taught leaves and a printed reference.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the prompt on lined paper. Retain taught leaves and a spelling reference.

**Skip.** Class collection and any new answer teaching.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Your child completes [[s01-A30-revise]] independently from yesterday's written feedback. Keep the plan, draft, feedback, revision, selected source location and change explanation. Clarity is recorded from this work through [[s01-A30-audience]], without another response.
- **5 min. File and reread.** Keep actual probe corrections with the folder. Reread a taught poem together.
  **Say.** "Where does your folder keep yesterday's note?" Expect: beside the draft and today's revision. Keep later comments separate.

### Shorter or longer

Keep the collection intact. Longer: reread a taught source quietly after filing.

### Words and supports

Review revision and the grade 3 folder trace. Use established access supports and record them. Independent decoding/spelling remains the child's. No personal disclosure or public performance required.

### Tomorrow

Day 31 returns D30 feedback and X29/P29. S means recopying without a meaning change; Q means an unclear subject; O means unsupported content. One earliest need fits Opening, others the workshop. Finalize only the eligible day 30 revision; new feedback needs later independent work. Pending probe hearings fit existing reading conferences.
