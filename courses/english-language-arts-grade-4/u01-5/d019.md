# Day 19: Details support an inference

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out Williams and XXII without companions. Prepare covered reading-019.md and exit-019.md. The student page holds the separate Rossetti inference; extra-records.md is an alternative only. Read teacher-only inspection-key.md, exit-key.md and extra-records-key.md. Return day 18 tickets and practice feedback. Prepare five due fluency records; reuse matching ten-day evidence. Retain D17 versions for after-class judgment.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 18's s01-X18-2 slips and inspection work. If many showed error A, inspect the familiar only-poems claim, [[s01-K19-3]], against paragraph 2. If error O dominates, locate the actual supporting sentence instead. Repair one earliest need, never repeat the held profile. Other needs go to Choice workshop. Review explicit as directly stated and inference as an unstated conclusion supported by details.

#### 10 min. Reread

Use five minutes for the five pending day 14 word-reading samples, children 16 to 20. Use the retained pattern-probe.md card, one minute each, recording actual decoding and support. Other children begin familiar reading. The remaining five minutes hold the whole Williams/XXII rereading and one claim inspection.

Reread the whole Williams profile, 388 words, and whole XXII, 16 words, about four reading minutes. Review biography versus poem speaker. **Ask.** [[s01-K19-2]] Pairs locate paragraph 4 before [[steps:s01-K19-2]]. His medical work supplies context; it does not prove what caused this poem. Explain that a detail must bear on the proposed idea. Children reject the supplied claim without composing an inference explanation.

#### 15 min. Quotation boundaries

Review grade 3 paired quotation marks for spoken words. Use [[s01-P19-1]] as an unjudged first inspection; hold the key until children choose. Show the comma separating the speaker introduction, opening marks, final period and closing marks. Contrast direct speech with copying words from a text.

**Ask.** [[s01-K19-1]] Locate the words in paragraph 2. Reveal [[steps:s01-K19-1]] one boundary at a time. A quotation is exact source wording inside paired marks. The comma belongs after the introductory word states; the borrowed sentence's period stays inside the final marks. This convention does not turn a guess into a fact.

#### 20 min. Choice workshop

Alone, revisit P19-1 once, then hand in the final selection. Other work: check private source notes for unsupported claims or missing quotation boundaries. Review grade 3's peer look-for: a partner checks only the feature named on section 1.5 feedback. Writers revise by adding, moving or explaining a fact. Own notes stay unjudged. One five-minute teacher group repairs the earliest remaining day 18 need using the taught only-poems fault. More than ten misses: begin the workshop with that group repair for everyone; keep independent source work afterward.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X19]] only now. Children read reading-019.md and explain independently on the half slip. Seven minutes response; three minutes marking, thirty times six seconds. Judge a located detail supporting a qualified inference about Dickinson, using the teacher-only key. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; note support separately. Return day 20. After class, mark P19-1 and grammar selections, ten seconds per child together, five minutes.

#### 10 min. Word work

Return day 17's actual reading feedback privately. Review grade 3 re-, meaning again, in [[s01-W19]]. Reveal [[steps:s01-W19]]. Partners blend the prefix and base, then reread naturally. Review day 18 guide-word spelling checks; read, cover, write and verify replay. Rehearsal stays unjudged in the folder. If many drop a printed part, uncover the prefix and base separately before blending again.

#### 5 min. Grammar retrieval

Review day 1 complete thoughts and familiar name/sentence capitals. Alone, use [[s01-P19-2]]. Collect the selection for day 20 return; model no new convention. Children who finish reread their notes silently.

#### 15 min. Partner rereading

Review grade 3 partner rereading: purpose, smooth phrases, expression and actual correction. Use Williams paragraphs 1 and 2 and whole XXII. A phrase is words read together for meaning. Partners read successively, point to a troublesome word, check letters and context, and reread naturally. No invented error is required.

Listen only to five due children, two minutes each, ten within fifteen. Record actual purpose/understanding, accurate meaningful prose/verse phrasing and self-correction when seen. Reuse matching ten-day observations. Unheard components remain blank. No teacher repair group runs while listening. Others receive descriptive partner feedback without a second individual screen.

Reserve the final three Partner rereading minutes for the separate independent inference [[s01-X18-1]]. Open its Exit box only after X19. Children read the selected Rossetti sentences and respond on paper. Collect with X19; return both day 20, with one priority repair. The ten teacher-listening minutes remain intact.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy short tasks and preserve numbered reading leaves. A board replaces a projector. Copy a small spelling reference if no dictionary is available.

### At home

**Swap.** Use notebook tags and hand-copied prompts without a printer. A spelling reference replaces a dictionary.

**Skip.** Class reading rotations and teacher judgment.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read the day 18 note and inspect [[s01-K19-3]].
- **10 min. Reread.** Read the whole Williams profile and XXII. Inspect [[s01-K19-2]].
  **Say.** Does his job prove the poem's cause? Expect: no; paragraph 4 warns against that claim.
- **10 min. Quotation.** Try [[s01-K19-1]] and [[s01-P19-1]]. Compare exact words and both boundaries with the adult key.
- **5 min. Words.** Read [[s01-W19]]. Check replay in the reference. Reread one taught passage smoothly.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X19]], then complete the separate Exit box, [[s01-X18-1]]. Work independently. Keep dated work and describe help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: collect fewer private notes, retaining both quotation uses and the exit. Longer: inspect another boundary in the same source quotation without composing an assessed explanation.

### Words and supports

Review explicit, inference, biography, quotation and phrase in use. Enlarge source lines, permit signing or content scribing, and record help. Independent reading and word recognition stay the child's. Public sharing permits passing.

### Tomorrow

Day 20 returns X19, P19-1 and P19-2. If many give error A, distinguish source context from proven cause using the taught faulty claim. For error O, locate what the profile actually says. Repair one need in Opening; route others to Choice workshop. Finalize day 17 revisions after class today, thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes; return privately day 20. Next: whole chosen profiles, evidence discussion and essay-subject selection.
