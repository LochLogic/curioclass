# Day 18: What the biography actually says

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print Williams across two 14-point leaves: paragraphs 1 to 3, then 4 to 5. Keep XXII available. Set aside covered reading-018.md and exit-018.md. Retain day 17 paragraphs, feedback sheets and day 16 tickets. Read the teacher-only unit assessment key, inspection-key.md, exit-key.md and day key. Attach the section record to the teacher component record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Review factual answers from a located sentence. Pairs use [[s01-R10]] for three minutes, with the familiar Williams paragraph 1. Hear at most two children, 45 seconds each; other rehearsal stays unjudged. Do not infer oral evidence from writing. Review day 14 reader, purpose and form: who needs the information, why, and in what kind of writing. A biography tells a real person's life; a poem speaker need not be that person.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Honor day 17's assessment feedback and day 16's tickets. Return all day 16 inspection work too. Prioritize the earliest unmet component. Five minutes: inspect [[s01-K18-1]] and reveal [[steps:s01-K18-1]]. Review keeping information beside the idea it explains. For another component, select just its supplied sign fault from [[s01-K18-3]]. Reveal only the matching part of [[steps:s01-K18-3]]. Name the fault without completing a paragraph.

Five minutes: children independently revise their retained D17 paragraph using their written note. Preserve the first attempt. Review the grade 3 folder trace: plan, draft, feedback, revision, edit. Review grade 3's teacher-note routine: move, add or explain a fact, then log the change. Collect both versions for day 19 judgment; no judgment today.

#### 10 min. Read biography

Read the complete 388-word Williams profile, about four reading minutes. Children read silently first; reread difficult sentences together. Review evidence as located words supporting an idea. Teach explicit: directly stated in the source. Teach inference: an unstated conclusion supported by details. A reasonable idea must still fit the text. No one labels or sorts claims yet.

#### 10 min. Source inspection

**Ask.** [[s01-K18-2]] Let pairs locate the actual sentence before showing [[steps:s01-K18-2]]. Removing only restores the source's meaning. A poem's imagination cannot establish a biographical fact. More than ten unsupported choices: reread paragraph 2 together and point to the omitted kinds of writing.

#### 15 min. Choice workshop

Alone, inspect [[s01-P18-1]] on the student page. Collect this supplied-fault response. Then children make private biography notes using section 1.5 source tags. Review grade 3 source tags: keep a paragraph or poem line beside the note. Their own notes stay unjudged in the folder. One five-minute group repairs the earliest remaining D17/X16 need using a different taught sign feature. Others reread or check source locations independently. Use section 1.5 feedback for dated descriptive notes.

#### 10 min. Exit

Release reading-018.md and one half-sheet ticket for [[s01-X18-2]]. These selected Rossetti sentences and two claims are first seen now. Children classify independently. Allow seven minutes to read and label; collect one ticket per child. Mark thirty tickets at six seconds each, three minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record support separately. Return day 19. The separate supported-inference record is collected on day 19, not today. After class, mark P18-1 at ten seconds each, five minutes; return day 19.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review day 16's invitation and four-syllable division with [[s01-W18]], then [[steps:s01-W18]]. Blend every syllable before checking meaning. Review grade 3 dictionary guide words: find the entry and compare every letter. Pairs read, cover, write and check invitation, then retain attempts unjudged. No individual reading screen here. If many skip a syllable, uncover one chunk at a time before blending again.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Children reread the taught profile or familiar notebook entries and update the reading log. Do not distribute new companions. Hear at most five missing-component conferences, two minutes each, ten total. This is separate from the earlier repair group. Use existing actual records when they fit; leave unheard components blank.

### Other rooms

A board replaces a projector. Hand-copy short prompts and number the profile paragraphs. Preserve the whole profile across two leaves. Use the notebook for tags and feedback when printed sheets are unavailable.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the short tasks and use notebook source tags. Use a spelling reference.

**Skip.** Class rotations and teacher marking.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use the retained day 17 note and [[s01-K18-1]]. Revise independently; keep both versions.
- **10 min. Read.** Read the whole Williams profile together. Reread paragraph 2.
- **10 min. Inspect.** Try [[s01-K18-2]] and [[s01-P18-1]]. Consult the adult key afterward.
  **Say.** Which printed words contradict the claim? Expect: paragraph 2 names other kinds of writing.
- **5 min. Words.** Read [[s01-W18]], then check invitation in the reference.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover reading-018.md and [[s01-X18-2]]. Your child labels independently. Keep dated work.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce independent note volume, preserving the whole profile, repair and exit. Longer: locate a second source sentence contradicting the only-poems claim.

### Words and supports

Review biography, reader, purpose and evidence. Review grade 3 source tag: preserve the profile paragraph or poem line beside each evidence note. Explicit means directly stated; inference means an unstated, supported conclusion. Enlarge text or offer signing and content scribing; record help. Independent reading stays the child's. Public talk permits passing. No personal hardship disclosure is requested.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 returns X18, P18-1 and R10 feedback. If many confuse direct wording with interpretation, repair error A using the only-poems fault. For invented evidence, error O, locate the actual paragraph sentence. One Opening repair only; other needs use Choice workshop. Day 19 rereads the profile and teaches exact quotation boundaries. Finalize D17 corrections after class day 19, thirty times ninety seconds, forty-five minutes; return day 20.
