# Day 20: Choose a poet through evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare one chosen whole non-Williams profile per child, across two 14-point leaves, and its whole taught poem. Offer Frost, Hughes, Dickinson or Rossetti. Exclude all companions and later poems. Print the student page and covered half-sheet exit. Open teacher-only day and section keys. Prepare source tags, section 1.5 feedback and six actual-observation spaces on the teacher component record. Return day 19 papers and finalized day 17 paragraphs privately.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 19's s01-X19 exit ticket, P19-1 and P19-2, plus finalized day 17's assessment and written feedback. If many showed error A, inspect the familiar patient-cause claim, [[s01-K20-3]], against paragraph 4. If error O dominates, locate the actual statement before accepting a note. Review the difference between documented context and a proven cause. Repair one need only; others use Choice workshop.

#### 10 min. Whole profile reading

Children read one complete non-Williams profile, 380 to 401 words, and its matching whole poem. This is about five reading minutes at 120 words a minute; allow rereading and brief private notes afterward. Match Frost with Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening, Hughes with Dream Variation, Dickinson with Hope, Rossetti with A Birthday.

Preview the selected text plainly: Frost's father's death; Hughes's Black identity; Dickinson's publication without permission; Rossetti's religion, illness and death. No personal parallel or racial role play. A child may choose another available profile. Review biography versus poem speaker, and retain paragraph/line locations beside notes.

#### 10 min. Evidence and capitals

Inspect [[s01-K20-1]] without completing an essay choice. Reveal [[steps:s01-K20-1]] after children find paragraph 1. A later setting cannot replace the documented birthplace.

Review familiar sentence and name capitals, then extend to the poem title in [[s01-K20-2]]. Reveal [[steps:s01-K20-2]]. For three minutes, establish the day's word case: pencil. Review grade 2 schwa, a weak vowel sound in an unstressed part. In pencil, the second vowel is reduced; sound alone cannot reliably supply its letter. Point to i in the reference, then blend pen-cil naturally.

#### 20 min. Discussion

Assign a four-paragraph essay for a reader unfamiliar with the poet: an opening, two body paragraphs on distinct aspects of inspiration, and a related ending. Use biography evidence and a poem example; do not invent a life event. Subject choices and own planning remain unjudged.

Review grade 3's discussion moves: mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, explain what became clearer. Form six concurrent groups of five. Give roles: facilitator, source finder, questioner, connector and listener; everyone still contributes and may pass publicly. Allow three minutes preparation, eight discussion, five written reflection and four for transitions/subject choice.

Use [[s01-P20-2]]. Each child has one question and one response, about 45 seconds each: five times ninety seconds is seven-and-a-half minutes per concurrent group. Teacher hears only a rotating six actual contributions, 45 seconds each, four-and-a-half minutes within eight. Record only components actually heard, never every component from one turn. The rest's source-tagged preparation and listening reflection show paper work only. Write individual feedback after class. Continue missing observations in existing day 26 and day 33 discussion windows.

#### 5 min. Choice workshop

Alone, answer [[s01-P20-1]]. Collect the supplied-feature selection. Review the earlier Frost fault if more than ten children still replace evidence with guesses. Own essay choices remain private folder work. Do not model choosing between competing poets.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X20]] only now. Seven minutes: read the new brief and justify a choice independently. Three minutes: collect and mark thirty half slips at six seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen, recording support separately. Return day 21. After class, mark P20-1 at ten seconds each, five minutes, and return it with the tickets.

#### 10 min. Word work

Use [[s01-W20]], then [[steps:s01-W20]]. Review the schwa case introduced earlier today: read pencil, consult the reference, cover, write and compare every letter. Review day 18's invitation division orally in concurrent pairs, without an individual screen. Explain that a known base can preserve spelling when a sound weakens; a reference resolves doubt. Practice stays unjudged in folders. If many guess letters from sound, reopen the actual entry rather than repeat the sound louder.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Read the taught chosen profile or poem; update the reading log. One five-minute group repairs the earliest remaining day 19 need with the known source fault. No teacher listening screen runs concurrently. Children retain their unjudged subject choice and source tags for day 21.

### Other rooms

A board and hand-copied prompts replace a projector. Supply complete numbered profile leaves, preserving poem lines. Notebook columns replace source tags and feedback sheets.

### At home

**Swap.** Use notebook tags and hand-copied prompts without a printer. A reference entry replaces a dictionary.

**Skip.** Class observation rotations and teacher marking.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use day 19 feedback with [[s01-K20-3]].
- **10 min. Read.** Choose one non-Williams profile and its whole poem. Read both.
- **10 min. Inspect and discuss.** Try [[s01-K20-1]] and [[s01-P20-1]]. Discuss [[s01-P20-2]] together. Keep your child's essay choice unjudged.
  **Say.** Where does the source support that claim? Expect: a matching paragraph or poem line.
- **5 min. Words.** Read [[s01-W20]], then verify pencil in the reference.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X20]]. Your child answers independently. Keep dated work and note help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce private note volume, preserving whole reading and discussion turns. Longer: inspect the same faulty claim against a second relevant source paragraph.

### Words and supports

Review inspiration, evidence, biography, speaker and source tags. Review grade 2 schwa: an unstressed vowel sound whose spelling needs checking. Keep personal histories private. Offer enlarged print, signing or content scribing; record help. Independent reading stays the child's.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 returns X20, P20-1 and written feedback on actual discussion contributions. If many showed error A, inspect the Frost birthplace fault against its sentence. One Opening repair; other needs use Choice workshop. Next: gather and group source-tagged facts, quotations and examples in private notes. Remaining discussion observations go to the scheduled day 26 and day 33 windows, without inferring speech from reflections.
