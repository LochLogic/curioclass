# Day 20 reading

Robert Frost
Frost and his family moved to England in 1912. There he met other poets, including Edward Thomas, whose writing influenced him.

Emily Dickinson
Emily Dickinson did much of her writing at her family's home in Amherst. The home offered her a room of her own and space to garden. A conservatory allowed her to tend plants through the year.
