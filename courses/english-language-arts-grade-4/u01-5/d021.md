# Day 21: Keep related evidence together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return day 20 tickets, P20-1 and written feedback on actual discussion contributions. Keep whole chosen profiles and poems available, excluding companions. Mark each child's relevant biography paragraphs totaling at most 200 words with the whole poem; use the same taught source. Prepare source tags and section 1.5 feedback. Print student pages and covered half-sheet exits. Open teacher-only inspection-key.md, exit-key.md and the day answer key.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and Opening

Return day 20's work. If many showed error A, inspect the familiar Frost birthplace claim, [[s01-K21-1]], against paragraph 1. Contrast the printed birthplace with the later setting. Repair one need only; other needs go to Choice workshop. Review day 20's essay assignment: explain one poet's inspiration to a reader unfamiliar with the poet, using life evidence and a poem example.

#### 10 min. Source rereading

Children reread their chosen whole poem and the marked biography paragraphs, at most 200 words altogether. For a longer poem, select a shorter relevant prose span. This takes about two reading minutes, leaving time to locate details. Review day 18 source tags: preserve the text title and paragraph or line beside each note. Review day 19 exact quotations; paraphrased notes must not wear quotation marks. Children gather their own notes without judgment.

#### 10 min. Note inspection

Review grade 3 heading: the main idea of a group of notes, saying what the group is for. Review day 14 aspect: one part of a topic considered together. Keep facts about one aspect together, as in the earlier plan made of groups. Do not produce a completed grouping example.

**Ask.** [[s01-P21-1]] Pairs inspect the supplied misplaced note; keep the final selection for independent completion later. Refer to Williams paragraph 1 to confirm the fact, then distinguish truth from relevance to a particular group. A true fact can still be in the wrong place. More than ten misses: point to Poem features, reread the note and contrast its subject with the label.

#### 25 min. Choice workshop

Five minutes: children independently complete P21-1 and [[s01-P21-2]]. Retrieve day 20 sentence/name/title capitals first, without adding a new rule. Collect these supplied-fault responses together.

Fifteen minutes: children gather and group their own facts, exact quotations and poem examples under useful headings on source tags or notebook pages. Their own plans stay unjudged in folders, for day 23 planning. Review keeping supporting information beside the idea it explains. Review grade 3 peer look-for and teacher note: a partner checks only the named source location, then the writer moves, adds or explains a fact and logs the change on section 1.5 feedback.

One five-minute teacher group repairs the earliest remaining day 20 need using the familiar claim fault. Other children reread a taught source or verify a note independently. No public ranking or compulsory sharing of a personal experience.

#### 10 min. Exit

Uncover [[s01-X21]] only now. Alone, children create two headings and place the new notes beneath them. Allow seven minutes for reading and grouping, followed by three minutes to collect and scan thirty slips, six seconds each. Accept equivalent accurate headings; do not require the key's exact words. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record support separately. Return day 22. After class, mark the two practice selections together at ten seconds per child, five minutes; return day 22.

#### 10 min. Word work

Review day 20 schwa and reference spelling with [[s01-W21]], then [[steps:s01-W21]]. Pairs read pencil, locate the entry, cover, write and compare every letter. Review day 19's known base in [[s01-W21-2]]: read play before adding re-. Check replay in the reference. Hearing a reduced vowel does not tell every spelling. Practice stays unjudged in folders; no extra individual screen. If many omit a letter, point to each reference letter before covering it again.

#### 20 min. Independent reading

Reread the taught chosen profile or poem; update the reading log. Children keep grouped notes and source tags for day 23, without scoring their draft. Hear at most five missing-component conferences, two minutes each, ten total. Reuse eligible actual evidence; leave unheard behavior blank. Do not run another teacher repair group while conferencing.

### Other rooms

A notebook replaces source tags and feedback sheets. Hand-copy exact short tasks and preserve source paragraph numbers. A board replaces a projector. Use a printed reference entry when no dictionary is available.

### At home

**Swap.** Use notebook notes and hand-copied tasks without a printer. Use a printed spelling reference.

**Skip.** Class conferences and teacher marking.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use day 20 feedback and [[s01-K21-1]].
- **10 min. Reread.** Read the chosen whole poem and marked biography paragraphs. Keep source locations.
- **10 min. Inspect.** Try [[s01-P21-1]] and [[s01-P21-2]]. Then group your child's own notes, unjudged.
  **Say.** Does the note match the group label? Expect: the medical degree concerns life, not a poem feature.
- **5 min. Words.** Read [[s01-W21]] and verify pencil with the reference.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X21]]. Your child groups independently. Keep dated work and describe help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: gather fewer private notes while retaining both sources and locations. Longer: check whether another private note fits its current heading; do not provide a completed grouping model.

### Words and supports

Review heading, aspect, evidence, quotation and schwa in the meanings used above. Offer enlarged movable note strips, signing or content scribing; record help. Independent word reading stays the child's. A private exchange may replace public sharing.

### Tomorrow

Day 22 returns X21, P21-1 and P21-2. If many show error N, inspect the familiar misplaced medical-degree note. If error O dominates, return to the printed source before accepting a heading's claim. Repair one need in Opening; others use Choice workshop. Day 22 inspects a faulty four-paragraph essay and teaches complete-thought boundaries. Keep day 21's own source-tagged notes unjudged for day 23.
