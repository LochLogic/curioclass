# Word meanings

**explicit:** directly stated in the source.

**inference:** an unstated conclusion supported by details.

**quotation:** the source's exact words.

**heading:** the main idea of a group of notes, saying what the group is for.

**develop:** add evidence and explanation.

**precise:** specific enough to identify the intended subject or feature.

**conclusion:** an ending related to the explanation.

**fragment:** an incomplete thought punctuated as complete.

**complete:** expressing a whole thought.

**revise:** change meaning or organization.

**publish:** make selected writing available to readers.

**phrase:** words read together as a meaningful group.
