# Invented mixing practice records

These records are invented for practice. They are not promised classroom outcomes.

## F54: known classroom materials

Treatments: a small baking-soda portion plus 5 mL vinegar.
Controls: the same portion plus 5 mL water.
Match portions, temperature, twenty-second stirring and sixty-second observation.

| Trial | Treatment observation | Control observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bubbles still visible | No continuing bubbles |
| 2 | Bubbles still visible | No continuing bubbles |
| 3 | Bubbles still visible | No continuing bubbles |

Record observations only. Keep an actual failed trial beside its supplied alternative.

## F55: known classroom materials

Treatments: 10 mL milk plus 2 mL lemon juice.
Controls: 10 mL milk plus 2 mL water.
Both start at the same temperature. Stir twenty seconds; observe after two minutes.

| Trial | Treatment observation | Control observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Visible clumps | Uniform appearance |
| 2 | Visible clumps | Uniform appearance |
| 3 | Visible clumps | Uniform appearance |

Record observations only. Clumping alone does not settle formation.
