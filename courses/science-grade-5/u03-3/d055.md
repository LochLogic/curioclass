# Day 55: Compare without overclaiming

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read today's teacher-only answer keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv. Cover exit-55. Privately check milk, citrus and glove allergies. Refrigerate milk until preparation; preportion equal-temperature milk and liquids shortly before class. Pretrial safely. Prepare three matched dry steel-wool pairs, cups and labels for adult setup. Print only F55 if needed. Put trial-log, change-model boxes, waste and washing supplies within reach.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles through cleanup. No tasting or sniffing; milk cups remain open. Adult alone handles wire with gloves and closes display cups; children never open them. Without allergy clearance, protection or washing, use records and leave conducting unobserved.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 54's tickets, error G. Use [[s03-P55g]]: compare two minutes, annotate two, submit one observation in one. If many day 54 tickets showed error G, more than ten, replace the group move with whole-class repair. Review observation versus inference: air introduced by pouring is a recorded possibility, not proof of formation.

#### 11 min. Milk trials

Six groups work in parallel. Two setup minutes: goggles, cup labels and portions. Each treatment receives 10 mL milk plus 2 mL lemon juice; controls receive 10 mL milk plus 2 mL water, measured in 1 mL steps. Match starting temperature, cups and intervals. Use day 54's five roles, rotating each pair; every child handles safely and records all pairs. Start pairs at minutes two, four and six. Stir twenty seconds; observe two minutes after addition. [[s03-P55]] records starting appearance; [[s03-P55t]] records results. A failed reading stays. One retry starts by minute eight, ends by ten; otherwise append F55, printed and announced as invented for practice. Never promise clumps. Teacher initials personal actions on record.

#### 5 min. Rust setup

Adult alone dampens one steel-wool portion in each matched pair, leaving its partner dry. Use equal portions, matched air-filled cups and location; close and date all six cups. Children do [[s03-P55s]] in change-model margins. This is a setup for day 57, with no guaranteed change. Keep displays closed afterwards.

#### 8 min. Component reporting

Review starting/resulting observations using [[s03-P55q]], in pairs. One minute each, then hear rotating six at forty-five seconds each, four-and-a-half minutes. Public passing is welcome. Remaining time holds feedback. The reading explains that milk clumps can gather existing material; clumps alone cannot establish formation, error E. Do not diagnose two flawed comparisons in practice.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X55]] on exit-55, alone. Collect before discussing. After class, thirty forty-five-second readings require twenty-two-and-a-half marking minutes. Review record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return on day 56. Practice feedback is immediate; pages stay in folders.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Adult collects milk waste and wire equipment. Review staggered washing: thirty forty-second washes at two sinks take ten minutes. Allow two for collection, wiping and goggle care. Goggles remain on until splashes are cleared; displays stay closed.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: use only F55 for observations; conducting stays unobserved. Teacher describes the dry/damp setup without claiming it was conducted. No printer: copy prompts exactly and keep exit unseen.

### At home

**Swap.** Use invented F55 without allergy clearance, goggles or washing. Use drawn wire-cup labels instead of wire handling. Copy log boxes without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public hearing.

- **5 min. Recall.** Do [[s03-P55g]].
- **12 min. Compare.** Use three safe pairs or F55. Do [[s03-P55]] and [[s03-P55t]].
- **10 min. Label and clear.** Do [[s03-P55s]] and [[s03-P55q]]. Adult collects liquids; wash hands and clean goggles.
  **Say.** "Did clumps alone settle formation?" Expect: no; existing material can gather into clumps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X55]] alone. Afterwards discuss its teacher key and give a next-step note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reporting; retain all trials, setup, exit and cleanup. Longer: locate a source statement supporting caution about clumps.

### Words and supports

Review variable, control, trial, observation, inference and uncertainty. Stable trays and a scribe preserve decisions; proxy handling remains unobserved.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 returns error I, confounded conditions, and error E, mixing guarantees formation, in two five-minute repairs. Then separate sand and salt and compare recovery with new-property evidence. View wire displays on day 57.
