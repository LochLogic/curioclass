# Does a changed appearance mean a new substance?

## 1. What we see

Materials can become hard to see when mixed. Their appearance alone cannot show that material vanished.

## 2. What tests show

Compare starting and resulting properties. Keep comparison conditions matched. Repeat trials to check consistency. A new property can support a new-substance claim. One sign alone may leave questions open.

## 3. A useful limit

Acid can gather existing milk material into clumps. Clumps alone do not establish a new substance. A separation method can fail without making anything new.
