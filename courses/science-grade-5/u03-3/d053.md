# Day 53: Plan a matched comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read today's teacher-only answer keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv. Cover exit-53. Set out empty cups and measures; no wet mixing today. Prepare the fixed stick at its recorded place and observation time. Use a supervised nearby site so movement fits five minutes; otherwise use H53. Lay out planning-sheet and shadow-record, with source-lined-notes fields retained on the plan.

### Safety and set-up

Observe shadows, never the sun. Adult supervises movement and stores chalk and stick. Never taste lab equipment.

### The day

#### 5 min. Shadow setup and reading

Review day 36's fixed-stick routine: same stick, location and sun-time convention. Two readers align the ruler at the stick base and shadow tip, reading to 1 cm. Everyone does [[s03-P53h]]. Record date, clock time and direction landmark. Cloudy, unclear or unsafe: retain missing actual reading; put H53, 31 cm, in a separate row, printed and announced as invented for practice. No repeated outdoor attempt.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 52's tickets. Error F: [[s03-P53f]], five minutes. Error I: [[s03-P53i]], five minutes. Each move compares two minutes, annotates two and returns one choice in one. If many day 52 tickets showed either error, more than ten, the same slot becomes whole-class repair. Review unseen material from day 7 and grade 4 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing.

#### 18 min. Build a valid plan

Six groups arrange three empty cup pairs. Alone, each child does [[s03-P53]] on planning-sheet: equal adult-portioned baking-soda amounts; 5 mL vinegar versus water, using 1 mL measure steps; same starting temperature, twenty-second stirring, sixty-second observation. Record starting and resulting appearance, including bubble presence at the set interval. Review variable, control and trial from day 52. Groups rehearse dry roles; teacher checks each plan. No liquids are mixed and no flawed plans are supplied.

#### 6 min. Source-lined notes

Review two-column notes: source statement and source location, [[s03-P53n]]. Children locate paragraph 2 of the retained reading and connect its statement to their own plan. Repeating checks consistency; it does not guarantee truth. Give immediate descriptive feedback, error x for a guarantee claim. Public talk may be passed; simultaneous pairs use one minute each.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s03-X53]] on exit-53, alone. Collect without discussion. After class, thirty forty-five-second readings require twenty-two-and-a-half marking minutes. Use the review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record. Return on day 54. Practice feedback is immediate; plans and shadow records stay in folders.

#### 2 min. Dry cleanup

Children stack empty cups and file plans beside change-model. Adult stores stick, chalk and ruler. Retain actual and supplied records separately.

### Other rooms

No safe outdoor access: use labeled H53 without observation credit. No projector or printer: read shared text and copy exact prompts, keeping the exit fresh.

### At home

**Swap.** Without outdoor access, use invented H53, keeping the actual row missing. Draw plan boxes without a printer. Empty cups may be drawn.

**Skip.** Public talk and group rotation.

- **5 min. Shadow.** Do [[s03-P53h]] from the actual or supplied record.
- **7 min. Repair.** Do [[s03-P53f]] and [[s03-P53i]].
- **15 min. Plan.** Do [[s03-P53]] and [[s03-P53n]]. Use dry cups only.
  **Say.** "Why repeat the comparison?" Expect: to check consistency and notice unusual results.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X53]] alone. Afterwards discuss its teacher key and give a next-step note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce dry role rehearsal, preserving individual plans and exit. Longer: locate another reading statement supporting a fixed condition.

### Words and supports

Review variable, control, trial, observation, inference and uncertainty. Enlarged plans and a scribe preserve each child's choices.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 returns error I, two changes isolate a cause, in five minutes. Then carry out three paired fizzing trials using these valid plans and record actual outcomes.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Source S03-PE4 and Source S03-safety. H53 is invented for practice.
