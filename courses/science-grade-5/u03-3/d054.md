# Day 54: Keep every trial

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read today's teacher-only answer keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv. Cover exit-54. Check ingredients, allergies, protection and washing privately. Pretrial small portions, then prepare equal baking-soda pinches and measured liquids. Label fresh cup pairs treatment/control, one through three; keep a spare pair. Display only F54 if needed, never F55. Set out trial-log, change-model boxes, waste tray and cleaning supplies.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles stay on through cleanup. No tasting, sniffing or blowing powder. Open cups only, never tight lids. Adult handles spills and waste. Without allergy clearance, protection or washing, use records; conducting remains unobserved.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 53's tickets, error I. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Use [[s03-P54i]]: compare two minutes, annotate two, submit one mark in one. If many day 53 tickets showed error I, more than ten, use this same slot for whole-class repair.

#### 5 min. Preparation

Review day 13 fizzing, without balloons here. Bubbles are gas pockets; pouring can also introduce air, error G. Review variable, control and trial. Six groups check equal powder portions and 5 mL liquid using 1 mL measure steps. Match starting temperature, cups, stirring and observation interval. Teacher checks goggles and open cups before mixing.

#### 15 min. Paired trials

Six groups work in parallel: three setup minutes, then four minutes per fresh pair. Roles: treatment pourer, control pourer, treatment stirrer, control stirrer, recorder. Rotate roles each pair; every child handles safely and records individually. Stir gently twenty seconds. Observe starting appearance, then bubble presence sixty seconds after liquid addition; clock has whole-second steps. Everyone does [[s03-P54]] for all pairs. Teacher initials one identified action per child on record. Retain no-fizz or unclear results. Retry one failed pair within its remaining cycle; if unresolved, append F54, printed and announced as invented for practice. Never overwrite actual results.

#### 4 min. Model

Review day 52 starting/resulting boxes. Add dated cup/trial tags, [[s03-P54m]], alone. Record what was seen, not gas identity. Teacher checks observation versus inference without rehearsing a causal paragraph plus identification limit.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X54]] on exit-54, alone. Collect before discussing. After class, thirty thirty-second readings need fifteen marking minutes. Review record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return on day 55. Give practice feedback now; pages stay in folders.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Review staggered washing from day 47. Adult collects open cups at the waste tray. Thirty forty-second washes at two sinks use ten minutes; collection and goggle cleaning use two. Keep goggles on until cups and splashes are cleared.

### Other rooms

No kit: use only F54; children record supplied observations, without conducting credit. No projector: read the clock and records aloud. No printer: copy exact prompts, holding exit unseen.

### At home

**Swap.** Use invented F54 when safe portions, goggles or washing are unavailable. Copy log boxes without a printer. Keep actual failures separate.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public reporting.

- **5 min. Recall.** Do [[s03-P54i]].
- **15 min. Compare.** Follow the same three-pair procedure, or use F54. Do [[s03-P54]].
  **Say.** "What did you actually observe?" Expect: cup-labeled observations, including absent bubbles.
- **7 min. Record and clear.** Do [[s03-P54m]]. Adult collects liquids; wash hands and clean goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X54]] alone. Afterwards discuss its teacher key and give a next-step note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce model sharing; preserve all trials, exit and cleanup. Longer: locate an unclear observation without replacing it.

### Words and supports

Review variable, control, trial, observation, inference and uncertainty. Stabilize trays; adult proxy handling leaves that action unobserved. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 returns error G, any bubble proves new material, in five minutes. Then compare milk mixtures and prepare matched damp/dry displays for day 57.

### Credit
