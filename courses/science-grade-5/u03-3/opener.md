# Section 3.3: Testing what a change can establish

## Purpose and science

Introduce control, variable and trial directly before application. A fair test keeps the same, changes one thing and watches one thing. A control is the matched comparison without the tested treatment. Repeated trials check consistency. Material can remain as unseen parts despite clear appearance. Milk clumps can gather existing material; they do not alone establish a newly formed substance. Teach observations separately from inferences.

## Safety and fallback

Ask milk, citrus and powder allergies privately before handling; use records if needed. No tasting, sniffing or heating. Goggles through cleanup; open cups only. Teacher alone handles steel wool with gloves and closes display cups. Days 54/55 need twelve minutes for staggered washing and collection. Teacher pretrials quantities and checks supplier safety sheets; absent safe protection or washing means paper records.
