# Day 52: What can a change show?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the teacher-only answer keys for today's item rows in items.csv and wrongs.csv. Keep exit-52 covered. Display the reading once and change-repair-cards as no-kit reversible-change records. Use source-lined-notes and change-model layouts on the student page's back. Sort day 44's change notes and day 51's tickets. No substances or heating today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 51's tickets, error A. Review property: color tells color and nothing else. Pairs point using [[s03-P52a]]. Compare two minutes, annotate orally two, submit one pointer choice in one. If many, more than ten, missed day 51's exit, replace group repair with the same whole-class move.

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Review grade 4 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Review day 7's unseen-material account, [[s03-P52b]]; dots represent material too small to see, not actual part sizes. Children point individually before pair talk. Correct error F by retaining the material label.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Introduce variable, a condition that can change, and control, a matched comparison without the tested change. Review trial from grade 4. Read [[s03-K52-1]] before revealing [[steps:s03-K52-1]]. A new-substance claim needs starting/resulting property evidence under repeated matched conditions. An appearance change alone leaves the claim open.

#### 10 min. Change repair

Return day 44 item c1-R44c notes. Review grade 2: went back, stays changed, attempted return; heating or cooling causes a change. Display the chocolate and paper reminders, [[s03-P52r]], four minutes. Trace attempted-return arrows four; answer fresh [[s03-P52w]] two. All supplied records are invented for practice. Ready children explain orally why an unsuccessful return attempt cannot settle formation, error H.

#### 12 min. Reading and notes

Read changed-appearance-reading aloud. Review grade 4 observation and inference, then day 46's two-column notes: source statement and source location. Children do [[s03-P52]] and [[s03-P52c]]; a source statement is not this class's observation. Explain filter, which retains undissolved solid; filtrate, liquid passed through; uncertainty, what evidence cannot settle. Review day 5 residue, material remaining. Label a teacher-drawn filter route without predicting today's exit.

#### 5 min. Model

Children do [[s03-P52d]] using separate change-model boxes. Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second turns use four-and-a-half minutes; others point in pairs. Public passing is welcome. Do not combine model drawing with a proposed recovered-material test.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X52]] on exit-52; children work alone. Collect before discussing. After class, thirty forty-five-second readings take twenty-two-and-a-half marking minutes. Review the record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return on day 53. Give practice feedback now; pages stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No projector: read the shared records aloud. No printer: copy exact prompts, keeping exit covered until collection time. No practical action is claimed today.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy prompts and note boxes without a printer. Use the supplied records; no heating.

**Skip.** Class return, public hearing and group rotation.

- **5 min. Recall.** Do [[s03-P52b]] by pointing.
- **10 min. Changes.** Read the invented reminder records. Do [[s03-P52r]] and [[s03-P52w]].
  **Say.** "Which property returned?" Expect: firmness returned after cooling the wax.
- **12 min. Read.** Read the shared passage together. Do [[s03-P52]] and [[s03-P52c]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X52]] alone. Afterwards use its teacher key for a next-step note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce pair reporting, preserving repair, reading and exit. Longer: name one limitation of the paper record orally.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, property, observation, inference and residue. New: variable, control, filter, filtrate, uncertainty. Enlarge boxes; a scribe records the child's decisions.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 returns error F, material vanished, and error I, two changes isolate a cause, in two five-minute repairs. Then record shadows and plan three matched mixing trials.
