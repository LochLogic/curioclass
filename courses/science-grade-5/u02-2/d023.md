# Day 23: Exchanges inside one jar

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys from child papers. Prepare jar-log.md and covered exit-23.md using exits-23-26.md. Have day 22 papers and record.md ready. Privately check plant, soil, mold and protective-material allergies. Inspect plants and soil for animals. Stage six trays and protective sets. Missing safe protection or washing uses jar-panels.md J23, printed and announced invented for practice.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 22's s02-X22. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. If many show error A, mostly soil, or error C, no minerals, share the first five-minute soil/mineral contrast. Review day 19: minerals are small necessary matter inputs. Error D, light as material, receives the second five. Each move: contrast two minutes, individual correction two, collection one. Above ten misses: whole class; otherwise targeted, with ready children locating evidence limits.

#### 5 min. Teaching and safety

Review system, grade 1 day 11: plant parts work together. Extend to plant, air, soil and water. **Ask.** [[s02-K23-1]] Wait for predictions, then [[steps:s02-K23-1]] Explain exchange: matter passes between parts, including gases entering and leaving plants. Review observation and inference, grade 4 day 16: seeing leaves differs from explaining unseen routes. Goggles, gloves and aprons on before handling. No tasting, sniffing, added chemicals or animals.

#### 5 min. Jar setup

Six groups work simultaneously. Each child's spoon turn lasts thirty seconds: five turns take two-and-a-half minutes. Place preportioned soil around the staged plant. Teacher closes six jars within two minutes; remaining time checks lids. Keep jars shaded and closed, with no release or mold culture. Paper users inspect J23 instead; assembly remains unobserved.

#### 11 min. Predict interactions

First minute: return day 22's s02-P22-1 feedback. Alone, [[s02-P23-1]] on the student page. Use jar-log.md for the dated visible detail, jar ID and actual/supplied label. Review day 5's dated change line: retain originals. Label arrows predicted or source-supported. If the view is unclear, retain that reading, retry once, then use J23 separately. No weighing today. Pairs explain one prediction, forty-five seconds each simultaneously; public passing is allowed. Collect prediction pages; logs stay in folders.

#### 10 min. Clean-up and washing

Wipe trays and store closed plant jars. Two sinks take fifteen thirty-second waves, seven-and-a-half minutes; movement and wiping take two-and-a-half. Paper-only observers need no soil-handling queue. Teacher sanitizes goggles after class per label before reuse. Isolate deteriorating jars unopened; adults dispose under school procedures.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X23]] alone now. Read its exact stem neutrally. Children draw and explain independently on the half slip. Practice used one jar, without paired chambers or weights. After class allow thirty times twenty-five seconds, twelve minutes thirty seconds marking, plus ten minutes descriptive prediction feedback. Record only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; blanks remain missing evidence.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: J23 retains the model task; label it invented. No printer: copy current stems and one jar outline, keeping the exit covered. Logs stay separate from supplied evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** No jar, protection or safe washing: use J23 on paper. No printer: copy its views.

**Skip.** Group assembly and public reports.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 22 feedback. Separate minerals from light's energy role.
- **10 min. Model.** Use [[s02-K23-1]]. Point at the one jar outline.
  **Say.** "What could move between these parts?" Expect: water into roots or gases between plant and air.
- **10 min. Predict.** Do [[s02-P23-1]]. Date the visible detail on jar-log.md. Mark J23 invented when used. Keep predictions separate.
- **5 min. Compare.** Explain one arrow. Dots stand for unseen parts; light supplies energy.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X23]] alone. Discuss using the current key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce partner reporting, preserving setup, safety, washing and exit. Longer: distinguish a visible detail from an inferred route.

### Words and supports

Review system, boundary, observation, inference and day 1's dots. Exchange means matter passes between parts. Enlarge outlines; a child may dictate onto individual paper.
