# Day 26: Defend one evidence link

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Prepare component-locators.md day 26 task and covered exit-26.md using exits-23-26.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys separate. Bring day 25 papers, record.md and completed source-chart.md. Give each pair either historical-card.md H18 or input-records.md A21; never combine them into a packet. No pumpkin or chief-source argument is displayed before exit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 25's s02-X25. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. If many show error I, contrast familiar A21's matched conditions with its one changed air condition for two minutes. Children correct their individual choice for two minutes; collect for one. Above ten misses: whole class; otherwise targeted while ready children locate an unmeasured input share. Growth alone does not isolate a source.

#### 12 min. New practice comparison

Read the invented-for-practice record aloud: **Ask.** [[s02-P26-1]] Children answer alone, then compare orally in pairs, forty-five seconds each simultaneously. Review day 19's mineral: a small necessary material input. Soil-free does not mean mineral-free. Air, water and minerals remain matter inputs; light supplies energy. The record gives no exact shares. Review day 19's dry plant weight: drying removes water-content differences but does not identify the incoming sources. Collect this individual response for descriptive feedback.

#### 14 min. Evidence challenge

First minute: return day 25's s02-P25-1 feedback. Each pair uses one familiar record separately. **Ask.** [[s02-P26-2]] Begin a single-source statement with its locator on Side 2. Review claim-evidence-reasoning from day 18. H18 challenges chiefly soil but leaves tree water content unmatched. A21 changes air while other inputs match, supporting an air contribution without measuring its fraction. Each partner explains for forty-five seconds. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns inside five minutes, naming who. Every child retains their own written response.

#### 10 min. Strengthen a component

Children improve that one statement and its limit without a supplied sentence frame. Partners check their own record for an omitted control or measurement. Review day 5's dated change line: retain original marks and date changes. They need not invent a weakness where a control is already stated. Teacher gives descriptive feedback as available; no heard response means no oral evidence. This component statement and any feedback stay in the folder. Do not rehearse a full chief-source account.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X26]] only now; read the exact invented-for-practice stem neutrally. Each child writes one argument independently on the half slip. After class: thirty times twenty-five seconds, twelve minutes thirty seconds marking, plus ten minutes descriptive s02-P26-1 feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; blanks remain missing evidence. Accept chiefly air/water linked to taught science and both supplied comparisons. Records support contributions without measuring exact shares or directly measuring water's contribution.

### Other rooms

No printer: display the first invented record, then one familiar source at a time. Children write independently on plain paper. Keep the combined exit record covered. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the current tasks and one familiar record onto paper.

**Skip.** Public reports and rotating hearing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use day 25 feedback and familiar A21. Locate its changed condition.
- **10 min. Inputs.** Do [[s02-P26-1]].
  **Say.** "Which inputs remain without soil?" Expect: air, water and minerals; light supplies energy. Exact shares remain unknown.
- **15 min. One source.** Do [[s02-P26-2]] with either H18 or A21. Child points to the evidence and improves one statement, keeping its limit. Preserve and date changes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X26]] alone. Discuss with the current key afterward. The records do not directly measure water's contribution.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reporting, preserving the new practice record, individual component and exit. Longer: make the component's evidence locator more precise without adding another source.

### Words and supports

Review mineral, dry plant weight, source locator, claim-evidence-reasoning and evidence limit. Read records aloud. Enlarge tables or accept dictation onto individual paper. Public passing remains available.

Return s02-P26-1 descriptive feedback inside Location sort, one minute. Keep s02-P26-2 in folders. Then connect growing plants to where dead plant matter goes.
