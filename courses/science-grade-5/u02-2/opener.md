# Section 2.2: Boundaries and evidence limits

## Purpose and science

A boundary selects what counts inside the system. A jar closed to matter can still contain exchanges among plant, air, soil and water. Light enters as energy. Teach gas exchange explicitly on day 23. Visible droplets do not establish every route; short survival does not prove self-sustaining conditions. Dots model parts too small to see, without molecular names.

## Before class

Print the section's shared sheets, current exits-23-26.md when present, teacher-keys.md and practice-teacher.md. Keep answer keys separate from child pages. Print only each current day's sheet sections. Hold exits and end-of-unit cases unseen until administration. Supplies: Jar-panels, jar-log and component-locators. Six plastic lidded jars, small non-toxic plants, preportioned damp bagged potting soil, spoons and trays. Protection and soap/water/towels are required for handlers.

## Running the section

Day 23 begins with ten repair minutes, then five safety minutes, five setup, eleven predictions, ten cleanup/washing and four exit. Five simultaneous 30-second spoon turns per group take two-and-a-half minutes; adult closes jars. Two sinks hold fifteen 30-second waves, with movement time. Day 24 practice is oral/pointing to unchanged models, with individual circled source locators. It never drafts a revision before the fresh exit.

Every student may pass in public talk. Hear rotating six and mark who, with paper evidence from everyone. Planned-only prerequisite records are not proof of completed teaching; supply direct illustrated explanations. Read records aloud before independent reading. Student pages stay within 350 words, with plain printed stems and 15-word sentences.

## Safety and alternatives

J23 replaces unsafe or missing setup. Keep predictions separate from observations. Containers remain closed, shaded and isolated if deteriorating. No animals, release, mold culture, tasting or sniffing.

Handling days use the equipment and protection in their day blocks. Check plant/soil/mold and nonlatex-material precautions privately. Adult handlers use eye protection for splash/dust hazards, gloves and apron; wash afterward. Missing protection or safe washing means paper records. Never taste or sniff samples. Never look at the sun. Teacher stores or disposes of deteriorating closed containers under school procedures.

## Common errors and evidence

J: no gas exchange. I: survival or growth identifies source. B/D: water-only or light-as-material. Give each listed letter its actual five-minute slot; return all papers.

The practice sheets keep evidence components separate. Do not demonstrate a complete held answer. Use the source chart to distinguish observation, source mechanism and invented practice. General facts are checked in the course's S02 source rows. Exact practice species relationships and numbers are constructed unless the card says historical.
