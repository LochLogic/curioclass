### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 24's s02-X24. Review return and repair from grade 4 day 17. If many show error B, water only, spend five minutes pointing to day 18's taught air/leaf route with water held equal in the familiar air comparison. Error D, light becomes material: spend five separating energy from matter labels. Each move has two-minute contrast, two-minute individual correction and one-minute collection. Above ten misses: whole class; otherwise targeted. Ready children name the limits of a source locator.

#### 12 min. Evidence limits

First minute: return day 24's s02-P24-1 locator feedback. Review relevant and sufficient evidence from grade 4 day 105: relevant bears on this claim; sufficient gives enough support. Use H18 alone. **Ask.** [[s02-P25-1]] Children first locate one measurement, then name missing information on their own page. The tree-weight row reports a large gain; plant water content was not matched. Accept matched initial/final dry whole-plant samples as a proposed measurement. Drying destroys a sample, so use matched samples, not the same living plant twice. This is a paper proposal, not today's procedure.

#### 14 min. Defend one link

Pairs use that same single record. Each explains their s02-P25-1 response for forty-five seconds, simultaneously across pairs. Review day 18's claim-evidence-reasoning: connect a claim to its located detail. A missing measurement should address the named uncertainty. Review rotating six from grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns inside five minutes; record who. All children keep individual paper evidence. Error I overclaims from growth; point back to the unmeasured input or unmatched water content. No uncontrolled-watering verdict or full chief-source argument is rehearsed.

#### 5 min. Dated annotation

Review day 5's dated change line: retain originals, date a second-color addition. Add the same evidence limit to source-chart.md, beside its H18 locator. Keep the original supported statement. Collect individual s02-P25-1 responses; charts stay in folders. Harvesting and drying remain on days 35 and 37.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X25]] only now. Read the exact invented-for-practice stem neutrally; each child marks one choice independently. No explanation is required. After class: thirty times ten seconds, five minutes marking, plus ten minutes descriptive practice feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; blank remains missing evidence. Error I means the child explicitly selects that the uncontrolled record identifies all sources.
