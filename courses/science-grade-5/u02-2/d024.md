# Day 24: Trace familiar routes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Use component-locators.md day 24 only. Have unchanged models and each child's completed source-chart.md ready. If a locator is missing, copy the familiar record label and location before class; supply no completed route drawing. Prepare covered exit-24.md from exits-23-26.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys separate. Bring day 23 papers and record.md. No soil or plants are handled.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 23's s02-X23. Review return and repair from grade 4 day 17. If many show error J, spend the first five distinguishing a sealed outer lid from exchanges between plant and inside air. Error I receives five: upright leaves or survival alone cannot identify sources. Each move: two-minute familiar contrast, two-minute individual pointing or oral correction, one-minute check. Above ten misses: whole class; otherwise targeted. Children keep writing unchanged.

#### 12 min. Oral routes

Review day 1's dots: symbols for parts too small to see. Review day 18's root/water and leaf/air structure-function links. Pairs use unchanged willow models. **Ask.** [[s02-P24-2]] First round: water. Second: air. Each round gives both partners forty-five seconds, simultaneously across pairs, with time to locate and point. Expect water into roots or air matter into leaves; dots are not visible grains. Error D requires the separate light-energy label. No drawing, drafting or written revision.

#### 14 min. Evidence locators

First two minutes: return day 23's s02-P23-1 descriptive feedback. Point at observation versus prediction labels without editing. Alone, **Ask.** [[s02-P24-1]] Circle an existing locator on source-chart.md. W19 places roots in mineral water; A21's matched-water air comparison supports an air contribution. These records support inputs; exact entry routes also use taught science. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second explanations within five minutes and name who on record.md. Others continue pointing practice. Collect every child's circled locator; oral feedback stays in folders.

#### 5 min. Self-comparison

Children silently compare unchanged models with their existing locators. Point to the selected route, its evidence and the separate light label. Partners may repeat the same oral route task or pass. Keep all writing unchanged. No incomplete-diagram repair is practiced.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X24]] only now on exit-24.md, including its starter drawing. Read the exact stem neutrally; children revise independently on the half slip. Collect before discussion. After class: thirty times twenty seconds, ten minutes marking, plus ten minutes descriptive locator feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; blank evidence stays blank. An omitted route alone does not establish an error letter.

### Other rooms

No printer: display familiar locator labels separately and let children circle one copied label. Draw the held exit starter only at exit time. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy existing locator labels and the exit starter onto separate paper.

**Skip.** Public reports and the rotating hearing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 23 feedback. Point out internal exchanges inside one boundary.
- **10 min. Routes.** Point along one unchanged route at a time, water then air.
  **Say.** "What do the dots stand for?" Expect: parts too small to see. Water enters roots; air matter enters leaves.
- **10 min. Locate.** Do [[s02-P24-1]] beside the completed source chart. Circle one existing locator. Keep the drawing unchanged.
- **5 min. Compare.** Child points to a route and its supporting locator.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X24]] alone, with its starter drawing. Discuss the current key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated reports, preserving both oral rounds, locator and exit. Longer: distinguish a record-supported input from its taught entry route, still orally.

### Words and supports

Review dots, matter, energy, exchange and source locator. Read labels aloud. Enlarge unchanged models or allow pointing; preserve independent exit drawing, with dictation onto paper if needed. Every child may pass publicly.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 returns day 24's s02-X24: error B, water alone, first five minutes; error D, light as matter, second five. Return s02-P24-1 in Evidence limits, one minute. Children then locate supporting measurements and identify evidence limits in a familiar source.
