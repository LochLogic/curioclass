# Day 78: Connecting the unit evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Bring day 77 slips and three practice responses. Retrieve australia-diagram.md, completed evidence-chart.md copies, class hourly/monthly shadow records, D70.md and N71.md. If class hourly measurements are missing, use S67.md; if monthly measurements are missing, state the gap. Label supplied records invented for practice. Prepare mechanism-locators.md and correct light-path-card.md. Cover the current exit and assessment records.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 77 s04-X77, s04-P77-scale, s04-P77-travel and s04-P77-disk. Review return and repair: five minutes each for errors E and F. Error E: locate sun within star category and its nearer distance. Error F: locate fixed-patch evidence beside the correct spreading component. Each round holds two minutes comparing, two tracing, one explanation. Over ten matching misses makes that round whole-class. Others inspect a limit; public talk may be passed.

#### 5 min. Recall
Review gravity, the pull toward Earth's center. Show the invented Australia diagram and recall the classroom fall separately. Review observation versus inference: a supplied diagram is not a firsthand fall measurement. Review the shadow-stick anchor using original dated records. If monthly observations exist, describe their differences only; otherwise retain an explicit evidence gap. No mechanism for seasonal shadow changes is added.

#### 16 min. Component rehearsal
Eleven minutes: pairs visit separate completed component charts, one at a time: gravity, daily shadows, monthly daylight, seasonal stars and controlled light. Allocate two minutes per chart and one for routing. Each partner points for thirty seconds; remaining time locates the original record. Cover each chart before the next. No writing draft or integrated response.

Five minutes: review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second single-component reports fit. Everyone completes [[s04-P78-locate]] on side 1, naming only one chart. Collect all thirty locators. Correct examples stay separate; never display a fresh combined record board for rehearsal.

#### 10 min. Unit consensus
Teacher reconnects familiar evidence orally: two minutes each for gravity, daily spin, yearly orbit, distance/spreading, and model boundaries. Children point to existing components. Review spin: daily shadows and light-dark cycling. Review orbit: matched seasonal night views; the moon has its own orbit. Describe monthly daylight/shadow differences without claiming orbit alone explains their seasonal cause. Keep measured records distinct from model diagrams. No student synthesis draft or revised writing.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s04-X78]] now. Three minutes alone, one collecting. After class, thirty ninety-second exits plus thirty fifteen-second locators take fifty-two-and-a-half minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Match only stated claims to errors A/C/D/G/F; describe other gaps. Hold every paper and all feedback until 81. No repair or hints on 79/80.

### Other rooms
No class archive: use S67, D70 and N71 separately, invented for practice. Monthly shadow evidence stays missing. No printer: copy one locator and today's held exit onto paper.

### At home
**Swap.** Use supplied component records if earlier papers are absent; name them invented for practice.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public reports.

- **8 min. Return.** Revisit day 77 category and spreading notes separately.
- **7 min. Recall.** Inspect the fall diagram and original shadow records separately.
- **10 min. Locate.** Show one component at a time. Complete [[s04-P78-locate]].
  **Say.** "Which observation supports this component?" Expect a specific matching record entry.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X78]] alone. Keep feedback for day 81.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce pair repetition, retaining repairs, every consensus component and exit. Longer: locate an unanswered question without inventing evidence.

### Words and supports
Review gravity, spin, orbit, apparent brightness and observed/supplied. Read records neutrally. A scribe records the child's choices; enlarge diagrams without adding mechanism labels.

### Tomorrow
Day 79 independently graphs unfamiliar sky records. Keep E78 and locator feedback until day 81 Private feedback. Route error A to gravity, C to daylight, D to orbit, G to spin and F to light, within 81's four-round priority ceiling.
