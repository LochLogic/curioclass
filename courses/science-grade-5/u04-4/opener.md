# Section 4.4: Distance and light

## Purpose and science

Fixed-output spreading light delivers less per equal patch farther away. Output, area and method matter. Sun is nearest star; stars differ in distance. Use equal-disk images for apparent size separately. Never rank unlike stars from distance alone.

Use argument with gravity/cause or stars/scale; use graphs with sky patterns. Separate observations from model inferences.
