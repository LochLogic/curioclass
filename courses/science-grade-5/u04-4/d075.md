# Day 75: Light at different distances

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md; prepare the generated model key. Set distance markers at 90 and 180 cm using 1 cm ruler marks. Outline one receiving patch on the opaque screen. Inspect enclosed lights and lit routes. Prepare light-log.md, light-path-card.md and L75.md. Bring day 74 tickets/locators, D70.md strips and section 4.3 desk pieces. Keep today's exit covered.

### The day

#### 15 min. Return and repair
Return day 74 s04-X74 and s04-P74-locate privately. Review return and repair: three five-minute rounds. Error C: compare D70 daylight durations. Error D: move Earth above/below sun, fixed far markers. Error G: hold Earth's center still and turn its observer through light/dark. Each round holds two minutes comparing, two tracing, one explanation. More than ten matching misses replaces that group visit with whole-class teaching. Others retrieve separate correct components. Public talk may be passed.

#### 7 min. Light instruction
Three minutes: review grade 4 source, object, receiver: separate light-model roles. Trace source to screen to eye separately. Four minutes: introduce apparent brightness, how bright a source looks at the observer. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Specify fixed lamp output, receiving patch, room light and viewing method. Review the light-ray model; extend its outward arrows to spreading paths using [[s04-K75-1]], then [[steps:s04-K75-1]]. Children copy the correct component, without revising a faulty diagram.

#### 10 min. Controlled demonstration
Six seated groups watch together. Adult reads 90/180 cm at 1 cm steps. Keep one lamp, its setting, patch, room light and viewing position fixed. Move lamp between marks; swap matching lamps and repeat each lamp's comparison. Never compare two lamps without the same-lamp check. Everyone records [[s04-P75-actual]] in the light-log frame on side 1. Retry poor contrast once; keep unclear and the date. Then show L75, saying "invented for practice"; supplied relative units are not measured by eyes.

#### 6 min. Component labels
Alone, [[s04-P75-control]]. **Ask.** Which fixed condition did you label? Expect output, patch, room light or method. Connect that condition to attributing the observed change to distance. Review claim-evidence-reasoning: an observation supports a claim through a reason. Tag the correct light-path-card model with observed or supplied evidence; keep seeing paths separate from this brightness component. No competing-record selection.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence
Reveal [[s04-X75]] now. Three minutes alone, one collecting all thirty slips and student pages. After class, thirty exits at forty-five seconds plus sixty practice entries at fifteen seconds take thirty-seven-and-a-half minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error F denies a distance effect; describe control/path omissions separately. Return on 76.

#### 3 min. Cleanup
Adult switches off and stores lights. Children collect paper and distance marks with routes fully lit.

### Other rooms
No apparatus: compare L75's supplied readings, announced as invented for practice. No printer: copy the current page and held slip onto paper. Preserve actual unclear readings separately.

### At home
**Swap.** Without lights or ruler, use L75; say it is invented for practice. Draw the page if needed.

**Skip.** Group reports.

- **8 min. Return.** Revisit day 74 notes with separate sky components.
- **7 min. Model.** Trace source-screen-eye. Copy [[s04-K75-1]].
- **10 min. Compare.** Adult moves one enclosed light; child completes [[s04-P75-actual]] and [[s04-P75-control]].
  **Say.** "Which condition stayed fixed?" Expect lamp output, patch, room light or method.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X75]] alone. Consult the separate key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated labeling discussion, retaining repairs, demonstration and exit. Longer: distinguish a failed observation from an invented fallback.

### Words and supports
Review light-ray and source-screen-eye. Apparent brightness describes how bright something looks. Enlarge the path card; read records neutrally. Frame: "The fixed patch receives __ because __."

### Tomorrow
Day 76 returns E75 and both practice entries in five-minute Return and repair. Error F receives a fixed-patch comparison. Control or path gaps get private notes. Then audit controls without equal-total arrays.

### Credit
Ours. Background: Sources S04-distance and S04-light. Exact fallback values are invented for practice.
