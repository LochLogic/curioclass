# Day 77: Very different distances

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md; prepare the generated scale key. Bring day 76 slips, audits and footprints. Prepare travel-card.md, equal-disks.md and grain-position-card.md as separate displays. Inspect the sealed grain-of-sand comparison card; substitute its printed dot if damaged. No loose grains. Keep current exit covered. The no-kit relative-distance map is the position card's separation rule.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return day 76 s04-X76, s04-P76-audit and s04-P76-footprint. Review return and repair. For error F, spend two minutes on fixed-patch evidence, two on correct spreading paths and one explaining. Over ten matching misses replaces the group with whole-class teaching. Others strengthen one model limit. Retain unclear actual observations.

#### 7 min. Teaching scale
Three minutes: compare the supplied whole-number distances, one centimeter and more than two kilometers, using familiar units; no conversion calculation. Four minutes: introduce light-year, the distance light travels in one year. Read travel-card: sunlight takes about eight minutes to Earth; the next closest star's light takes over four years. The sun belongs to the star category; other stars have different distances. Travel time for light provides distance evidence, not how long someone watched.

#### 19 min. Separate scale cases
Six minutes: show grain-position-card. State the model rule: Earth-sun separation is one centimeter; next star lies beyond two kilometers. Grain marks Earth's position, not its size. Body sizes are not to scale. No walking. Everyone completes [[s04-P77-scale]].

Six minutes: put that card away; use travel-card alone for [[s04-P77-travel]]. Shorter light travel supports a nearer source. Do not rank unlike stars' brightness by distance alone.

Seven minutes: put travel away; show the invented equal-disks record for [[s04-P77-disk]]. **Ask.** Which image covers more space? Expect the nearer equal disk. Equal actual sizes stay unchanged; an image can look larger nearby. No brightness or travel readings accompany this record.

#### 9 min. Argument components
Four minutes: review spreading from 75 and claim-evidence-reasoning from unit 2. Locate one observation-to-reason link at a time; teacher holds the correct component. Five minutes: review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second reports fit; public passing is allowed. Everyone returns paper. Keep travel, disk and light components separate, without a complete observatory repair argument.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence
Reveal [[s04-X77]] now. Three minutes independent paper, one collecting. After class, thirty sixty-second exits and ninety ten-second practice entries take forty-five minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error E excludes sun from stars; error F denies distance effects. Note unsupported image/distance links descriptively. Return all on 78.

#### 1 min. Cleanup
Collect sealed cards. Leave damaged grain packaging closed; use the dot.

### Other rooms
No grain or distance equipment: the printed position dot represents location. No projector: show enlarged paper cards separately. Copy only today's page and held exit if printing is unavailable.

### At home
**Swap.** Use a drawn position dot and copied cards; no grain or outdoor walk is needed.

**Skip.** Public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Revisit day 76 notes with the correct spreading component.
- **7 min. Scale.** Read the position rule and complete [[s04-P77-scale]].
- **8 min. Travel.** Read travel-card; complete [[s04-P77-travel]].
- **7 min. Disks.** Put travel away; complete [[s04-P77-disk]].
  **Say.** "Which image covers more space?" Expect the nearer equal disk.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X77]] alone. Read the separate key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public reports, retaining all three separate cases and exit. Longer: explain why a position marker cannot show actual body sizes.

### Words and supports
Review apparent brightness and distance. Light-year names distance. Read decimal travel facts aloud without calculation; use the rounded values. Frame: "This model represents __, but leaves out __."

### Tomorrow
Day 78 returns E77 and all three practice responses in ten-minute Return and repair. Error E gets nearest-star category work; error F gets spreading evidence, five minutes each. Then retrieve mechanisms separately before the teacher's unit consensus.
