# Component practice answer keys

Teacher only. Print separately from student sheets.

## s02-P27-1

Air/water incoming; attached stem retained; detached twig detached but still matter.

Look for: locations sorted without disappearance

## s02-P27-2

A visible location/appearance notice, not an unsupported mass-loss claim.

Look for: visible notice without complete model

## s02-P28-1

Dated actual/supplied label and visible detail; unseen gases/microbes not called observations.

Look for: visible detail and provenance

## s02-P28-2

Grass to cricket; cricket to lizard, on separate cards. Constructed relationships.

Look for: food-to-eater direction on isolated edges

## s02-P29-1

Gases to air; released water to environmental water; returned minerals to soil/water.

Look for: separate destinations without all-becomes-soil claim

## s02-P30-1

Dated visible edge/color/fragments with actual/invented label, no inferred weight or gas result.

Look for: visible detail and provenance

## s02-P30-2

Correct source locator for decomposer/environment route; source-supported rather than directly observed.

Look for: relevant mechanism locator

## s02-P31-1

15 mL with 1 mL steps; illustrated, not class measurement.

Look for: 15 mL and 1 mL interval

## s02-P31-2

Remains may accumulate; mineral return may decrease over time. Prediction, not observation.

Look for: removal linked to conditional change
