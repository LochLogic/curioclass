# Section 2.3: Following departing matter

## Purpose and science

Matter remains when a part detaches or breaks into fragments. A food web connects organisms through food-to-eater transfers. Producers make food using light energy and environmental air/water matter; consumers obtain food from organisms. Decomposers use remains/wastes and return matter to environmental pools. Plants can use that material again. Gas/water/waste exchanges occur across living boundaries. Reuse need not reach the same plant. Jars do not supply evidence for consumer animals or unseen microbes.

## Safety and alternatives

J23/L30 replace unavailable views; retain actual failure reasons separately. Teacher collects safe weathered leaves without unknown animal waste, never wets or incubates litter. No pupil litter contact. Closed displays stay closed; unsafe displays use paper.

Handling days use the equipment and protection in their day blocks. Check plant/soil/mold and nonlatex-material precautions privately. Adult handlers use eye protection for splash/dust hazards, gloves and apron; wash afterward. Missing protection or safe washing means paper records. Never taste or sniff samples. Never look at the sun. Teacher stores or disposes of deteriorating closed containers under school procedures.
