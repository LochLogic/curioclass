# Separate organism and destination cards

Invented for practice. Each row is a separate card.
Do not join these into a complete practice web.

| Card | One supplied link |
|---|---|
| M1 | Grass provides food for a cricket. |
| M2 | A cricket provides food for a lizard. |
| M3 | Water reaches roots. |
| M4 | Part of air enters leaves. |
| M5 | Dead material supports decomposers. |
| M6 | Released gases enter surrounding air. |
| M7 | Released water enters environmental water. |
| M8 | Returned minerals enter soil or water. |

A producer makes food using light energy, air and water matter.
A consumer obtains food from other organisms.
A decomposer breaks down dead material and wastes.
These meanings do not identify organisms inside a sealed class jar.
