# Day 30: Keep visible changes separate

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Prepare observation-columns.md, meadow-cards.md and dated photographs or leaf-sequence.md L30. Cover exit-30.md from exits-27-31.md. Separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Bring day 29 feedback and record.md. Check allergies privately. Teacher alone prepares a safe weathered-leaf display, without unknown animal waste, using listed protection and washing afterward. Never wet or incubate litter. Missing safe materials: use L30; isolate deteriorating containers unopened.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 29's s02-X29. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. First five: error E, familiar twig matter remains after detaching. Second five: error J, familiar gas arrows cross living boundaries even when unseen. Each block: two-minute contrast, two-minute individual correction, one-minute collection. Above ten misses per letter: whole class; otherwise targeted. Keep original marks with day 5's dated change line. Missing arrows get follow-up, not automatic letters.

#### 5 min. Observation preparation

Review observation, inference, grade 4 day 16. Separate the closed display, actual dated outdoor photographs and source mechanisms. The display is not the outdoor sequence. Containers stay closed: no pupil litter contact, tasting or sniffing. Read L30 aloud as invented for practice when earlier photographs are missing. Its date labels show order, not elapsed days. Do not call invented drawings class evidence.

#### 12 min. View and record

Three minutes organize six groups of five. Six ninety-second lens views fit the remaining nine minutes. Alone, children do [[s02-P30-1]] in observation-columns.md. Date and label actual or supplied. Record an edge, color or visible fragments; fragment count is not a weight or decay rate. Unclear view: try one safe angle within the turn, keep the failure reason, then record L30 separately. A single present view does not establish an earlier change. Collect individual records for descriptive feedback.

#### 12 min. Separate source annotation

First minute: return day 29's s02-P29-1 feedback. Review day 28's decomposer meaning and environmental returns. Read the separate mechanism card and familiar meadow outputs. **Ask.** [[s02-P30-2]] Expect Source S02-CYCLE's gas/water/waste statement linked to an appropriate route, marked source-supported. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns within five minutes; record who. Partners take one forty-five-second turn each concurrently. Passing is allowed. Keep observation and mechanism columns separate; do not rehearse a model revision plus limit. Collect annotations.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Adult stores the unopened display and lenses, wipes the table and bags wipes. Children touched only dry paper/lenses. Accidental contents contact stops work for washing and paper replacement.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X30]] only now. Read its exact stem neutrally. Children revise independently on half slips. Fragments retain matter; later returns are inferred, and drawings cannot establish gas movement or weight loss. After class: thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen minutes marking, plus ten minutes practice feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record.md. Error E denies retained matter; error J denies gas exchange. Omission alone receives follow-up.

### Other rooms

L30 replaces unsafe or missing views, labeled invented for practice. No printer: draw its sequence and current mechanism card separately. Keep the exit covered.

### At home

**Swap.** Use L30 instead of collecting litter. Copy current sheets if printing is unavailable.

**Skip.** Group queues and public reports.

- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 29 feedback. Revisit retained matter, then gas exchange.
- **8 min. Observe.** Read L30 as invented for practice. Do [[s02-P30-1]], keeping its label.
  **Say.** "What visible detail can you record?" Expect: torn edges or fragments, not a measured weight loss.
- **13 min. Source.** Read the separate mechanism card. Do [[s02-P30-2]]. A source mechanism is not a directly observed gas route.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X30]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce reports, preserving observations, source labels and exit. Longer: specify a measurement that could test one inferred route.

### Words and supports

Review observation, inference, decomposer and exchange. Enlarge views; read the mechanism aloud. Accept dictation on individual paper.

### Tomorrow

Day 31 returns day 30's s02-X30: error E first five minutes, error J second five. Return s02-P30-1 and s02-P30-2 inside Component claims, one minute. Then compare jar and outdoor boundaries while retaining observation limits.
