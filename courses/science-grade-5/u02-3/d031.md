# Day 31: Test what a model explains

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Prepare prediction-panels.md, observation-columns.md, meadow-cards.md, jar-log.md, jar-panels.md J23 and leaf-sequence.md L30. Cover exit-31.md using exits-27-31.md. Separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Bring day 30 feedback and record.md. Check plant, soil and mold allergies privately; paper observers start with supplied records. Isolate unsafe displays unopened. No litter contact, tasting, sniffing or new specimen preparation.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 30's s02-X30. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. First five: error E, detached twig/fragments still contain matter. Second five: error J, familiar gas routes remain possible when unseen. Each block: two-minute contrast, two-minute individual correction, one-minute collection. Above ten misses per letter: all; otherwise targeted. Review day 5's dated change line: retain original marks. Omission alone receives follow-up.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

One minute: review volume and milliliter from day 3, using prediction-panels.md's separate invented scale. **Ask.** [[s02-P31-1]] Expect 15 mL, neighboring marks 1 mL apart; no finer reading. Four minutes: review day 23's included-parts boundary. A closed jar retains matter internally; an outdoor study area allows exchanges across its edges. Models change when evidence challenges them. Review observation and inference, grade 4 day 16: source-supported unseen routes are inferred. Do not demonstrate a two-record cycle revision.

#### 8 min. Current views

Teacher demonstrates jar and leaf views, four minutes each. Children do [[s02-P31-3]], keeping observations separate in observation-columns.md; date jar-log.md too. Retain an unclear reason, try one safe angle, then use J23 or L30 separately, printed and announced invented for practice. Never open containers. A supplied illustration is not a new class measurement. Do not infer weight loss or identify microbes from appearance.

#### 6 min. Decomposer prediction panels

Read the invented panels: only decomposer presence changes; regular dead-material supply continues. Review day 28's decomposer and matter-cycle meanings. **Ask.** [[s02-P31-2]] Children predict alone, then partners explain for forty-five seconds each concurrently. Expect possible buildup and reduced mineral return over time, not measured results. These paper predictions replace the absent simulation; no real organisms are removed. Collect scale responses and predictions for descriptive feedback.

#### 10 min. Component claims

First minute: return day 30's s02-P30-1 and s02-P30-2 feedback. **Ask.** [[s02-P31-4]] Use one familiar meadow route or one direct-view detail separately. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns within five minutes; record who. Partners take one forty-five-second turn each simultaneously; passing is allowed. Everyone retains an individual tagged component. A source mechanism cannot become an observation because its arrow fits. Preserve proposals without assembling the exit's combined cycle.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Adult stores unopened displays, wipes the table and bags wipes. Children handled dry paper only. Accidental contact stops work for washing; replace handling with paper.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X31]] only now. Children independently draw and label on half slips; read the exact stem neutrally. Accept observed records with resolution limits and inferred reuse routes. After class: thirty times thirty-five seconds, seventeen minutes thirty seconds marking, plus ten minutes practice feedback. Record only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error E denies retained matter; error J denies gas exchange. Missing work is missing evidence.

### Other rooms

No safe displays: J23/L30, labeled invented, replace firsthand views. No printer: copy current panels separately. Keep the exit covered.

### At home

**Swap.** Use J23 and L30 instead of real specimens. Copy current panels if printing is unavailable.

**Skip.** Public hearing and class demonstrations.

- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 30 feedback; revisit retained matter and gas routes.
- **5 min. Scale.** Do [[s02-P31-1]].
  **Say.** "What does one marked step represent?" Expect: 1 mL on this invented scale.
- **7 min. Views.** Do [[s02-P31-3]], labeling supplied records separately.
- **6 min. Predict.** Do [[s02-P31-2]]. These are predictions, not observations.
- **3 min. Tag.** Do [[s02-P31-4]] for one component.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X31]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce reports, preserving boundary meanings, predictions and exit. Longer: identify a measurement that could test one inferred route.

### Words and supports

Review milliliter, observation, inference, decomposer, matter cycle and boundary. Enlarge scale marks; accept dictation on individual paper.
