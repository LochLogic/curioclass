# Teacher answer keys

Teacher only. Keep covered until all responses are collected.

## s02-X27

Air enters leaves; water/minerals enter roots. Shed leaf matter reaches ground. Add an unresolved question about later destinations or decomposition.

Look for: incoming and outgoing matter routes | genuinely unresolved fallen-matter question

Meets: Air and water enter; leaves carry matter to ground. Where will that matter go later?

Does not yet: Fallen leaves become nothing

## s02-X28

Environmental water to roots and air to leaves; tree food to caterpillar to bird. Legend: matter passes from food to eater. Invented relationships are not animals observed in jars.

Look for: water/air inputs to tree | tree-caterpillar-bird food-to-eater links

Meets: Water and air enter the tree. Tree food passes to caterpillars and then birds.

Does not yet: Plants exchange no gases with air

## s02-X29

Remains/waste to decomposers, outputs to sediment/soil, water and air, then environmental matter available to plants again. Not all matter becomes soil.

Look for: decomposer/environment returns | plant reuse and gas/water exchanges

Meets: Decomposers use remains and waste. Material returns to environmental pools and can enter plants again.

Does not yet: Dead matter disappears

## s02-X30

Fragments retain matter. Later decomposer/environment routes are inferred. Gas movement or unseen transport is not established by pictures. No measured leaf-weight loss follows.

Look for: fragment matter retained and proposed returns labeled inferred | names a route pictures cannot establish

Meets: Fragments still hold matter. I infer later returns, but pictures cannot establish gas movement.

Does not yet: The missing whole leaf proves matter vanished

## s02-X31

Observed: water present, no resolved level change; outdoor leaves look smaller. Infer decomposer/environment returns and plant uptake with gas/water routes labeled inferred. Neither record establishes every route, complete water retention or leaf-weight loss.

Look for: both records with their measurement limits | reuse cycle with observed/inferred labels

Meets: The marked level stays the same and leaves look smaller. I infer returns through decomposers and environmental pools; unseen gas routes remain inferred.

Does not yet: Smaller leaves mean matter became nothing
