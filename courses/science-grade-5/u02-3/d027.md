# Day 27: Follow a detached part

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Prepare bean-location-cards.md, a familiar plant diagram and covered exit-27.md using exits-27-31.md. Separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Bring day 26 feedback and record.md. Display only the current component task, never the full exit model.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 26's [[s02-X26]]. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. If many show error A or error B, contrast incoming air, water and small mineral inputs for two minutes. Day 26's gain exceeds soil loss; its matched air comparison challenges water alone. Children correct their evidence locator for two minutes; collect for one. Above ten misses: all; otherwise targeted.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review day 18's willow input model and day 23's plant boundary. Review: air is matter, from day 1; minerals are small necessary inputs, from day 19. Show an invented fallen-twig sketch beside, separate from, that model. Model a notice using [[s02-P27-2]]: the detached twig lies on a tray. Detaching changes its location, not its existence. Keep its later destination open; do not demonstrate a cycle or a complete input/output response.

#### 18 min. Location sort

First minute: return day 26's s02-P26-1 descriptive feedback. Read the bean cards aloud as invented for practice. Alone, children do [[s02-P27-1]]. Partners explain one label each, forty-five seconds per child simultaneously. Teacher revisits uncertain labels: incoming air/water, retained stem, detached twig. Each child then completes their own [[s02-P27-2]]. Collect both for descriptive feedback, without another marking scale. Keep the three locations separate rather than drawing a combined model.

#### 10 min. Question board

Review question board: notices become questions, from day 18. Turn to Side 2. **Ask.** [[s02-P27-3]] Partners take forty-five seconds each simultaneously. Expect a later-location or breakdown question; a prediction alone needs question wording. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns within five minutes; record who. Passing is allowed. Everyone keeps an individual question in their folder. Post unresolved routes without answering them yet.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X27]] only now. Each child draws and writes independently on the half slip. Read its exact stem neutrally. After class: thirty times twenty-five seconds, twelve minutes thirty seconds marking, plus ten minutes practice feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record.md. Accept supported equivalent arrows and an unanswered question. Error E requires a disappearance claim; a missing route alone receives follow-up.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw separate cards on the board; children record on paper. Copy the exit only at its turn. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the separate cards onto paper. Use drawings, without collecting plants.

**Skip.** Public hearing and the class board.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 26 feedback. Revisit air and water inputs.
- **8 min. Notice.** Show the invented twig sketch. Do [[s02-P27-2]].
  **Say.** "Where is the detached twig?" Expect: on the tray; detaching did not make it vanish.
- **12 min. Sort.** Do [[s02-P27-1]], keeping locations separate. Discuss one label each.
- **6 min. Question.** Do [[s02-P27-3]]. Keep the question open.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X27]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports, preserving the sort, question and exit. Longer: specify evidence needed for one open question.

### Words and supports

Incoming, retained, detached. Review matter and system boundary. Enlarge cards; accept dictated words on individual paper.
