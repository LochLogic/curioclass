# Day 29: Locate environmental returns

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Lay out meadow-cards.md output cards separately, air/water/soil destination labels and observation-columns.md mechanism card. Cover exit-29.md from exits-27-31.md. Separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Bring day 28 feedback and record.md. No complete return cycle is displayed before the exit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 28's s02-X28. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. If many show error J, contrast familiar leaf/air incoming and outgoing routes for two minutes. Review exchange from day 23: invisible does not mean absent. Children correct individually for two minutes; collect for one. Above ten misses: all; otherwise targeted. An omitted arrow alone gets a prompt, not a letter.

#### 10 min. Evidence retrieval

First two minutes: return day 28's s02-P28-1 and s02-P28-2 descriptive feedback. Review producer, consumer, decomposer and matter cycle from day 28. Read the separate mechanism card aloud. Partners locate the supplied statements about remains, gases, water and wastes. Review observation and inference, grade 4 day 16: these mechanisms are source-supported, not processes seen inside our jar. Do not combine the component cards into a return/reuse model.

#### 16 min. Destination sort

Read M6, M7 and M8 as invented for practice. **Ask.** [[s02-P29-1]] Children match alone, then compare in pairs. Each partner explains one destination for forty-five seconds simultaneously. Expect gases to surrounding air, water to environmental water, minerals to soil or water. Distinguish eating food from releasing wastes or leaving dead remains. Review day 19's mineral meaning: necessary matter, not energy-supplying food. Teacher checks that no label says all dead matter becomes soil. Keep three transfers separate. Collect the individual matches for descriptive feedback.

#### 10 min. Route hearing

**Ask.** [[s02-P29-2]] Use one output card only. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns within five minutes, recording who. Other pairs take two simultaneous forty-five-second turns, one each; passing is allowed. Children retain their individual source-tagged edge in folders. A card label supports the invented route, while Source S02-CYCLE supports the general exchange mechanism. Teacher retains separate component labels without drawing a full cycle.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X29]] only now. Read its exact stem neutrally; children extend the supplied boxes independently on half slips. Accept supported routes through decomposers to environmental matter and plant reuse. Matter need not reach the same plant. After class: thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen minutes marking, plus five minutes practice feedback. Record only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error E requires disappearance; error J requires denying gas exchange. Missing work remains missing evidence.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw separate output cards and labels on the board. Children copy one route per line. Reveal exit boxes only at the independent turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy current cards onto paper if printing is unavailable. No samples are collected.

**Skip.** Public hearing and the class chart.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 28 feedback. Revisit gas routes.
- **8 min. Retrieve.** Read the familiar roles and mechanism card from day 28.
- **12 min. Sort.** Do [[s02-P29-1]].
  **Say.** "Where does this released material go?" Expect: gases to air, water to environmental water, minerals to soil or water.
- **6 min. One edge.** Do [[s02-P29-2]] with one card. Keep its label.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X29]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public hearing, preserving individual matches and exit. Longer: explain why a particular output belongs with its destination.

### Words and supports

Review decomposer, matter cycle, exchange and mineral. Read cards aloud. Enlarge destination labels; permit dictation onto individual paper.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 returns day 29's s02-X29 in ten-minute Return and repair: error E first five, error J second five. Return s02-P29-1 in Separate source annotation, one minute. Keep s02-P29-2 in folders. Then observe leaf changes separately from source mechanisms.
