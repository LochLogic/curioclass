# Day 28: Separate views and food links

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Prepare current meadow-cards.md and observation-columns.md sections, jar-log.md and jar-panels.md J23. Separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Cover exit-28.md using exits-27-31.md. Bring record.md and day 27 papers. Check plant, soil and mold allergies privately; paper observers start with J23. Isolate deteriorating jars; never open, taste or sniff contents. No litter handling or new jar assembly.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 27's s02-X27. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. If many show error E, spend two minutes contrasting the familiar detached twig with disappearance. Children correct individually for two minutes; collect for one. Above ten misses: all; otherwise targeted. Missing arrows alone get descriptive follow-up.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Read J23 views and meadow card M1 as invented for practice. **Ask.** [[s02-K28-1]] Then [[steps:s02-K28-1]]. Teach producer: makes food using light energy and air/water matter; consumer: obtains food from organisms; decomposer: breaks down remains and wastes. Teach food web as linked food-to-eater transfers. Use separate cards, never a completed web. From the mechanism card, explain separate gas/water outputs and mineral returns. Plants can reuse environmental matter: a matter cycle need not return to the same plant. Review exchange from day 23; light supplies energy, not matter.

#### 12 min. Closed-jar observation

Three minutes organize six groups of five and label records. Each group gets one ninety-second lens view of the closed display, nine minutes total. Children do [[s02-P28-1]] in observation-columns.md and date jar-log.md. Review observation, inference, grade 4 day 16. Record only visible details or an unclear view. Retry one safe angle within the turn; retain the failure reason, then label J23 separately as invented for practice. A jar view cannot identify unseen organisms or establish feeding links.

#### 14 min. Separate links

First minute: return day 27's s02-P27-1 and s02-P27-2 feedback. Alone, [[s02-P28-2]] using M1 and M2 separately. Partners explain one food-to-eater direction each, forty-five seconds simultaneously. Teacher circulates for reversed arrows. Then point separately to M3 root/water and M4 leaf/air inputs; do not assemble a multi-input web. Collect individual observations and matches for descriptive feedback.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Adult stores unopened jars and lenses, wipes the table and bags wipes. Children handled dry paper/lenses only. Accidental contents contact stops work for washing; use paper afterward.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X28]] alone. Children independently draw on half slips; read the exact stem neutrally. After class: thirty times twenty-five seconds, twelve minutes thirty seconds marking, plus ten minutes practice feedback. Record only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Accept equivalent supported food-to-eater and environmental-input arrows. Error J needs an explicit no-gas claim; a blank is missing evidence.

### Other rooms

No safe jar: J23, labeled invented, replaces firsthand observation. No printer: copy current cards separately and record on paper. Keep the exit covered.

### At home

**Swap.** Use J23 instead of a jar. Copy current cards if printing is unavailable.

**Skip.** Group queues and class sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 27 feedback; detached matter remains matter.
- **8 min. Sources.** Read J23 and M1. Do [[s02-K28-1]].
  **Say.** "Which record supplies the feeding link?" Expect: M1, not the jar view.
- **8 min. Observe.** Do [[s02-P28-1]], marking the view supplied and invented. No opening containers.
- **10 min. Links.** Do [[s02-P28-2]]. Read the role meanings. Explain separate gas, water and mineral returns; plants can reuse environmental matter.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X28]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce partner reports, preserving roles, observation and exit. Longer: find which separate card supports an uncertain arrow.

### Words and supports

Producer, consumer, decomposer, food web, matter cycle. Review observation and inference. Read cards aloud; enlarge them or accept individual dictation.
