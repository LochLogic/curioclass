# Day 41: Repairing evidence links

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated answer keys separate. Prepare original papers with private component notes, four assigned letter moves and one covered retry per child. Print repair-sources.md, repair-returns.md, repair-energy.md and repair-interactions.md by assignment. Prepare retry-s.md, retry-r.md, retry-e.md or retry-k.md, reflection.md, record.md and assessment-record.md. Retrieve day 38's checklist and day 4's pot log.

### The day

#### 5 min. Private feedback

Return day 38's s02-X38, two minutes; day 39's s02-E1, one; day 40's s02-E2/s02-E3, two. Honor yesterday's handoff: no original points change. Explain routing directly: largest gap first, at most four letter moves, one fresh retry. Review observation and inference: a supplied record is not a class observation. Introduce today's routing without assuming an earlier response routine.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Review return and repair. Four five-minute rounds: two compare the packet's wrong claim with its correct component; two individually locate/annotate that component; one date a correction beside the original. Teacher visits sources, returns, energy, then interactions; other groups use correct component records, followed by private locator feedback. Assign a different unresolved letter each round, or deepen the same locator if needed. More than ten stated misses: one whole-class move replaces a round. Leave extra gaps unresolved.

Sources: [[s02-P41-A]], [[s02-P41-B]], [[s02-P41-C]], [[s02-P41-D]], [[s02-P41-I]]. Review day 18's claim-evidence-reasoning and light distinction, day 19's minerals and day 21's controls. Returns: [[s02-P41-E]], [[s02-P41-J]]. Review day 28's decomposer and day 29's matter-cycle. Supply missing picture details orally: M8 returns minerals to soil or water; M6 arrows go from living things to air. Energy: [[s02-P41-F]], [[s02-P41-G]], [[s02-P41-H]]. Review days 34 to 36: sunlight through food, building/repair matter and outward warmth. Draw T9 as living thing to surrounding warmth. Interactions: [[s02-P41-K]]. Review day 33's invented R33 shared grass dependency. These are isolated locators, never complete retry models. Packet labels are invented reminders, not new historical measurements.

#### 15 min. Fresh retry

Reveal only the assigned sheet now: sources [[s02-R41S]], returns [[s02-R41R]], energy [[s02-R41E]] or interactions [[s02-R41K]]. Say records are invented for practice. Ten minutes independent response, five independent rereading, then collect. Use neutral reading/dictation support without suggested routes. No additional science exit or rescoring.

#### 5 min. Reflection and handoff

Return s02-P38-1 and recorded s02-P38-2/s02-P38-3 feedback during the first minute. Everyone writes [[s02-P41-1]] concurrently with optional public contributions: rotating six heard, record sheet names who, forty-five seconds each, four and a half minutes. Review that routine; passing is allowed. File original, correction and retry separately; archive day 4's pot log without new measurement. After class: thirty times one minute, thirty teacher minutes, for retry/reflection feedback. Return both through individual folders on day 41. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record.md only; original points stay on assessment-record.md. Record unaddressed gaps.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy only assigned packet/route and reflection. No devices or live materials are needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the assigned packet and retry onto blank paper; use pencil dates instead of folders.

**Skip.** Public turns and other children's routes.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read private notes. Keep the first answers.
- **15 min. Repair.** Use three assigned letter moves, five minutes each. Point to the packet's correct component.
  **Say.** "Which component changes your answer?" Expect its evidence link, not just a copied claim.
- **15 min. Retry.** Give only the assigned task: [[s02-R41S]], [[s02-R41R]], [[s02-R41E]] or [[s02-R41K]]. Allow ten minutes alone, then five rereading. Keep answers covered until collection.
- **5 min. Reflect.** Use [[s02-P41-1]]. Return a brief evidence note today. Keep any remaining question visible.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: assign fewer repair letters, retaining the fresh retry. Longer: strengthen an evidence limit orally.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, matter, energy, decomposer and legend. Read neutrally; allow dictated independent reasoning. Frame: "This evidence supports ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 42 starts course-level particle storyboards and weight-evidence graphs. Retain unresolved A/B/C/D/I sources, E/J returns, F/G/H energy and K dependencies in individual folders. No additional unit 2 repair lesson is promised.
