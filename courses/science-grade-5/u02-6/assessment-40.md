# Day 40: linked ecosystem model

**s02-E2**

Invented for practice. A dune garden has grasses and shrubs. Beetles eat leaves. Mice eat seeds. Owls eat mice. Fungi and bacteria break down dead remains and wastes. Water enters soil. Organisms exchange gases with air. Build a model explaining different needs and matter returns for plant reuse. Add an introduced snail eating seedlings, without predators in this invented garden. Revise your model to explain two linked consequences.
