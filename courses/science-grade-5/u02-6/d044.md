# Day 44: Repair evidence links, then look ahead

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read check1-44-teacher.md. Prepare day 42 and day 43 papers with private next steps, two priority letters each. Group by particle, measurement, plant or arrow gap. Print only assigned repair44-cards.md routes. Keep exit and readiness covered. Open check1-record.md; retain day 16's practical record separately. Do not erase original evidence.

### The day

#### 5 min. Private feedback

Return day 42's assessment, two minutes; day 43's assessment, two minutes. Read private next steps for one minute. Honor yesterday's handoff: retain first answers, choose two largest gaps. Review return and repair from day 41. Do not combine these marks with original unit assessment points.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Four parallel five-minute rounds: two rounds per priority gap. First round: teacher contrasts the relevant correct feature on repair44-cards.md with the child's stated wrong; second round: child locates that feature and dates a correction beside the original. All write concurrently. Teacher visits particles, measurement, plants, then arrows, four minutes each with one minute routing. Within visits, review rotating six heard, record sheet names who: at most six 45-second turns total, not thirty individual explanations. Passing is allowed; everyone submits paper.

Particles, errors A/B/C: review unseen symbolic parts, retained count and dissolved material from unit 1. Use [[c1-P44a]], accepting the assigned card's count, scale or dissolved feature. Measurement, errors D/E/F/G: review complete boundaries, equal recorded totals, faithful bars and checks of anomalies; [[c1-P44b]]. Plants, errors H/I/K/L: review chief air/water sources, roots/leaves, small minerals and limits; [[c1-P44c]]. Arrows, errors J/M/N/O/P: review light as energy, decomposer returns, food material/energy and outward warmth; [[c1-P44d]]. Explain any missing convention directly before use. These are component locators, never the exit's whole flawed-account revision. More than ten stated misses: one whole-class move replaces that round. Log unresolved gaps; promise no extra response day. Corrected locator work stays with students in folders today.

#### 10 min. Exit

Give [[c1-X44]] alone, seven minutes; collect, one; give locator feedback and put dated corrections in folders, two. Say the toy record is invented for practice. No peer checking or teacher hints. This is a new linked explanation from appearance and whole-case evidence, unlike the separate feature locators. No readiness teaching precedes the ticket.

#### 10 min. Readiness

Reveal three invented records: [[c1-R44a]], two minutes; [[c1-R44b]], three; [[c1-R44c]], three. Review only procedures for using separate readiness rows, not the science. The final two minutes collect and separate the three rows for diagnosis; do not total them or claim thirty papers were individually diagnosed in two minutes.

After class: thirty times ninety seconds for readiness and thirty times one minute for exit, seventy-five teacher minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen throughout check1-record.md. Give written next steps. Return exit feedback through individual folders today. Route properties and purpose separately to day 46's two ten-minute repairs; change evidence to day 52's ten-minute repair. Readiness records sample written grade 2 skills; any newly found gap receives direct teaching there. Practical measurement remains unobserved where day 16 lacked it.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy only assigned cards and exact prompts. No second color: box corrections. No equipment or devices are needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the assigned card and prompts; box changes if no second color is available.

**Skip.** Other repair routes, public turns and class sorting.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read day 42 and day 43 notes. Choose two gaps.
- **15 min. Repair.** Use assigned card locators from [[c1-P44a]], [[c1-P44b]], [[c1-P44c]] or [[c1-P44d]]. Keep first answers.
  **Say.** "Which feature helps your correction?" Expect the assigned feature with its evidence link.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Give [[c1-R44a]], [[c1-R44b]] and [[c1-R44c]] independently. Keep separate next steps.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[c1-X44]] alone. Read its answer together afterward and return a brief note today.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair one gap, preserving exit and readiness. Longer: strengthen one correction; no additional assessment.

### Words and supports

Review matter, energy, evidence, complete system and property. Readiness revisits grade 2 flexibility, material-purpose evidence and a change returning. Read neutrally; accept drawings, dictation and home-language reasons. Frame: "This evidence changes ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 45 opens unit 3 by observing four unknown powders and proposing safe identification tests. Today's readiness notes shape day 46's properties/purpose repairs and day 52's change repair.
