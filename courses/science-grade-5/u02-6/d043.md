# Day 43: Which plant claims fit the evidence?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read check1-43-teacher.md; keep it private. Print the cards within the two-sided page. Cover existing models. Retain day 42's assessment without feedback. Use the same private check1-record.md, with separate columns for today's components. Cards are original invented records, not observations of this class.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Keep day 42's promised change of shape: evidence-card sorting and rebuttal labels replace an extended written argument. Review only independent-work procedures. Announce records as invented for practice. Read directions neutrally. Explain that sorting means writing card letters beside claims; children need no scissors. No hint about the correct sort.

#### 28 min. Evidence sort and rebuttals

Students use the printed cards for [[c1-E43a]]. Allow six minutes reading, eight sorting and fourteen for evidence-linked labels. They may attach more than one card to a claim and use one card twice. Labels combine records with the plant-source explanation; artistic arrangement earns nothing. Keep this fresh case unrehearsed. Air and complete-system ideas are earlier knowledge; do not reveal the day 42 answer key.

#### 10 min. Arrow samples

Five minutes each for [[c1-E43b]] and [[c1-E43c]]. Students annotate the supplied partial maps and caption. These sample environmental returns and food uses; they do not constitute a full ecosystem or energy-model assessment. Accept a labeled route and short caption rather than an extended paragraph. No peer discussion or answer display.

#### 2 min. Independent rereading

Students check whether their own labels refer to card letters. Do not suggest missing scientific ideas. Neutral dictation support remains available.

#### 2 min. Collection

Collect all pages. No separate exit. Keep original answers intact for day 44's Private feedback, alongside day 42's marked papers.

After class: thirty children times two minutes, sixty teacher minutes. On check1-record.md, use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for eleven components only. A supported response is recorded as shown with help; neutral access support alone does not supply the science. Mark stated errors H to P, then write one private next step. Do not infer letters from blanks. No new score is added to the original unit 2 assessment. Prioritize two gaps per child for day 44.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the same cards and claims on paper before class. No board or devices are required. Permit oral dictation privately without explaining the science.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy the evidence cards and claims if no printer is available. Card letters replace cutting.

**Skip.** Class collection and other children's work.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Give [[c1-E43a]], [[c1-E43b]] and [[c1-E43c]]. Read unfamiliar words without science hints. Say every case is invented for practice. Keep answers covered until the work is finished.
- **5 min. Keep the first answers.** Put today's pages beside day 42's pages. Save feedback for day 44.
  **Say.** "Are all the labels attached to a page?" Expect the original sort and both arrow samples, without corrections.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: preserve all three items and neutral access support. Longer: reread, without a model answer.

### Words and supports

Review claim, evidence, mineral, matter and energy from unit 2. A rebuttal challenges a claim using evidence. Read that direction before work without modeling an answer. Labels, drawings, dictation and home-language reasoning count.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 returns both check days, repairs two priority gaps and holds a fresh exit. Its final ten minutes check readiness for material properties and changes.
