# Day 39: plant evidence

**s02-E1**

Invented for practice. A radish investigation reports dry whole-plant weights of 2 g, then 28 g. Dry soil changes from 600 g to 599 g. Shed material was collected separately. Another radish grows without soil in water containing 1 g of added minerals. Its dry weight changes from 2 g to 24 g. Matched air groups have equal water, minerals, temperature and light. They finish at 16 g in ordinary air. They finish at 25 g with more of the air part plants use. Write one argument for the main matter sources using all three comparisons. Include entry routes and explain sunlight's different role.
