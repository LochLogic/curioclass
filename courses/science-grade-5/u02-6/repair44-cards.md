# Component cards for day 44

Print only assigned routes. All records are invented for practice.
These are teaching records. Keep the fresh exit covered.

**Particle route.**
A capped tube keeps its air when compressed.
Before and after drawings use the same symbolic dot count.
Dots stand for parts too small to see, far smaller than a visible grain.
The compressed drawing places dots closer together.
A separate liquid model retains dissolved parts throughout the water.
Recovered solid supports retaining that unseen material.

**Measurement route.**
A sealed test includes liquid, lid and container each time.
Its complete totals match before and after a temperature change.
Each bar ends at its recorded total.
A lower reading has several possible causes.
Checking the scale and repeating the complete-system reading tests a scale explanation.

**Plant route.**
Two plant groups receive equal water and light; useful air differs.
A change in growth supports investigating the changed air input.
Air and water provide most new matter; small mineral inputs still matter.
Water enters roots, and part of the air enters leaves.
These comparisons alone do not give exact source shares.

**Arrow route.**
Sunlight supplies energy for making plant food.
Food supplies body-building matter and energy for growth, repair, movement and warmth.
Energy leaves living things as warmth rather than cycling through soil.
Dead material goes through decomposers to environmental pools that plants can use again.
