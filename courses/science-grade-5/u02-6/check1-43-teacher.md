## c1-E43a

H challenges the chiefly-soil claim: plant dry gain is 37 g while dry soil loss is 1 g. W challenges soil necessity: soil-free dry gain is 31 g, exceeding 1 g minerals added. A challenges water alone: the equal-condition air groups differ by 13 g. Together the comparisons support air and water as chief sources, without exact shares. Water enters roots, part of the air enters leaves, minerals are small inputs, and sunlight supplies energy for making food rather than plant material.

Components: soil comparison and implication | soil-free comparison and implication | controlled air comparison and limitation | chiefly air/water claim | root water and leaf air routes | minerals distinguished from chief sources | light energy distinguished from matter.

Meets: H gives 37 g plant gain but 1 g soil loss. W gives 31 g gain with only 1 g minerals and no soil. A keeps water equal but changing useful air changes growth by 13 g. Air and water provide most matter through leaves and roots; minerals are small inputs. Light supplies food-making energy. Exact fractions remain unknown. | Not yet: Soil made nearly all the plant. (error H)

- Most plant gain is soil. Error H: Plant gain far exceeds soil loss.
- Water alone supplied all gain. Error I: Omits controlled air evidence.
- Sunlight turns into plant material. Error J: Confuses energy with matter.
- Taller growth identifies the exact source fraction. Error K: Growth does not isolate a quantitative source share.
- No soil means no minerals are present. Error L: Ignores the supplied mineral input.

## c1-E43b

A route from dead leaves through decomposers to environmental air, water or soil, then to plant reuse, challenges disappearance. Accept any scientifically accurate environmental return with a reuse connection; no whole-web coverage claim.

Components: environmental return and reuse.

Meets: Dead leaves to decomposers to soil minerals to plant roots shows matter reused. | Not yet: Nothing remains after decay. (error M)

- Dead leaves vanish into nothing. Error M: Omits environmental return.

## c1-E43c

Food carries sunlight-derived energy used for growth, repair, movement and warmth; food matter also builds and repairs bodies. Energy leaves as warmth rather than cycling from soil back to plants. No full new model is required.

Components: food energy origin and uses | food matter for building and repair | outward warmth without energy cycling.

Meets: Food carries energy first from sunlight for growth, repair, movement and warmth. Its matter builds and repairs the animal. Energy leaves as warmth rather than cycling through soil. | Not yet: Soil returns the energy to plant food. (error N)

- Energy cycles back through soil to plants. Error N: Applies matter reuse to energy.
- The animal receives food energy directly from sunlight. Error O: Omits the plant-food link.
- Food supplies no building material. Error P: Treats food only as fuel.
