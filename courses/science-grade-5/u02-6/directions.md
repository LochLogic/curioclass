# Neutral assessment directions

Teacher only. On day 39 read the invented label and record without explaining relationships. Give three minutes for directions, 37 for Q1 and five for independent checking. On day 40 give three minutes for directions, 28 for Q2 and 14 for Q3. Cover teaching displays and notebooks. Read records on request neutrally; use enlarged copies or a scribe without suggested links. Collect independent papers before any feedback. No extra exit ticket is given.

Day 41: return E38 and the three original checks in five minutes. Keep original points. Assign at most four letter moves, one per five-minute round. One whole-class move replaces a round. Correct component packets support the three groups the teacher is not visiting. Give a child only one assigned retry: ten minutes response and five checking. Collect all. The final five minutes holds process reflection and six optional 45-second contributions. No complete held retry is modeled. Teacher later reads all papers and returns brief feedback in individual folders on day 41, without claiming another lesson of repair.
