# Day 39: An independent plant argument

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and directions.md. Use assessment-39.md as the record master; print this student page only, with the record once. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md, generated answer keys and assessment-record.md in the teacher folder. Cover notebooks and teaching displays. Arrange established enlargement, neutral reading or dictation support. Keep day 38's tickets covered.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Day 38 ended with separate evidence components and promised today's unfamiliar plant argument. Begin independently, without returning its exit or checklist. Read directions.md and the invented label aloud. Read assessment-39.md neutrally. Children may request rereading or procedural help; do not interpret comparisons. Each child works alone.

#### 37 min. Independent assessment

Administer [[s02-E1]], the assessment-39.md record printed on the student page. Children write their own evidence-linked argument and include entry routes in words or drawing. Keep all three comparisons visible on each child's paper. Do not display a completed account, supply a starting claim or use partner checking. Record access support privately. If many struggle, retain their evidence for day 41 rather than teaching during the assessment. Missing responses remain incomplete evidence, not automatically error letters.

#### 5 min. Independent checking

Children reread the printed task and their own response silently, adding anything they choose. No answers are shown. Collect both response sides together, including incomplete work. After class, allow thirty times sixty seconds, thirty teacher minutes, to mark Q1's six two-point components on assessment-record.md. Use current item look-fors and teacher-keys.md: 2 accurate and linked, 1 accurate but incomplete, 0 absent or incorrect. Total 12; absence stays blank. Hold marked work until day 41.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy assessment-39.md exactly onto individual papers, with blank response space. No devices or investigation equipment are needed. A scribe records the child's words without suggesting a cause.

### At home

**Swap.** Without a printer, copy assessment-39.md and supply two blank response sides. Cover earlier work.

**Skip.** Class collection and public discussion. Keep feedback until day 41.

- **3 min. Directions.** Read the invented record neutrally. Explain where the child writes.
- **37 min. Independent assessment.** Give [[s02-E1]]. Read again on request without suggesting an answer.
- **5 min. Independent checking.** The child rereads and adds to the response alone. Keep every page for day 41; do not teach from the answer key today.

### Shorter or longer

Keep independent reasoning time. Shorten distribution by laying covered papers out beforehand. An early finisher rereads the same task; add no scored extension.

### Words and supports

Read words as printed. Give established reading, enlarged-print or dictation support without causal hints. Do not reteach science vocabulary today. Judge reasoning, not spelling or drawing skill.

### Tomorrow

Day 40 assesses an unfamiliar ecosystem model, then its energy extension. No Q1 or day 38 feedback occurs before those papers are collected. Return s02-E1 in day 41 Private feedback, one minute. Stated mostly-soil, water-only, mineral-free and sunlight-material claims route to errors A, B, C and D; unsupported claims invite a follow-up rather than an inferred letter.

Ours. Assessment records are invented for practice.
