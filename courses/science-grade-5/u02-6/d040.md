# Day 40: One model, two kinds of arrows

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Use assessment-40.md as the record master; print this student page only. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md, current generated answer keys and assessment-record.md teacher-only. Cover notebooks, displays and day 39 papers. Prepare established neutral reading, enlargement or dictation support. Keep the energy extension covered until its part.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Day 39 promised today's new ecosystem model. Hold day 39's assessment, s02-E1, for day 41 Private feedback. No return and repair occurs; hold day 38's feedback too. Say the dune garden record is invented for practice. Read its relationships neutrally. Each child constructs an independent model; clarify procedure without supplying scientific routes. No equipment or live organisms are used.

#### 28 min. Independent matter model

Give [[s02-E2]]. Children draw and label their own model, then revise it for the newcomer and explain the consequences. Read the printed record again on request without hints. Judge different needs, boundary exchanges, feeding and reuse through the environment. Do not interpret a blank arrow as a stated misconception. If many struggle, note their unsupported links for day 41; keep this part independent.

#### 14 min. Independent energy extension

Reveal [[s02-E3]]. Children add the requested style and legend to their own matter model, with explanations on side 2. Leave both printed tasks available. Reserve the last two minutes within this part for silent rereading and collection. No peer checking or displayed answers. After class, allow thirty times two minutes, sixty teacher minutes, for Q2/Q3. With day 39's thirty minutes, original marking totals ninety minutes. Use current item look-fors beside teacher-keys.md. On assessment-record.md enter Q1 /12, Q2 /14 and Q3 /10, total /36. Two-point components earn 2 accurate/linked, 1 accurate/incomplete, 0 absent/incorrect; one-point components earn 1 or 0. Leave absence blank. Return on day 41.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy assessment-40.md exactly onto individual papers with drawing and explanation space. Keep Q3 covered until its part. A scribe records the child's own routes and explanations without interpreting them.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy assessment-40.md onto two response sides. Cover the second task at first.

**Skip.** Class collection and discussion. Keep answers and feedback covered until day 41.

- **31 min. Independent matter assessment.** Begin with three minutes reading the invented record without explaining relationships. Give [[s02-E2]] for twenty-eight minutes drawing and revising alone.
- **14 min. Independent energy extension.** Reveal [[s02-E3]]. The child extends the same model and rereads it alone. Keep both sides for feedback on day 41. Do not teach the answer today.

### Shorter or longer

Prepare papers beforehand to preserve reasoning time. Early finishers reread their own explanations. Add no scored extension or new check.

### Words and supports

Read printed vocabulary neutrally, without defining a causal relationship. Established dictation, enlargement and description supports keep the reasoning independent. Spelling and drawing skill are not scored.

### Tomorrow

Return s02-E2 and s02-E3 in day 41 Private feedback, two minutes, alongside Q1 and day 38's ticket. Route stated disappearance, no-gas-exchange and no-newcomer-effect claims through errors E, J and K. Route cycling energy, fuel-only food and direct sunlight-to-animal claims through errors F, G and H. Missing links receive component feedback. Day 41 provides four repair rounds and one assigned fresh retry; original points remain.

### Credit

Ours. Ecosystem relationships are invented for practice.
