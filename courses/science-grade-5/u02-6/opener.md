# Section 2.6: Independent evidence and response

## Purpose and science

The assessment samples argument and linked models on fresh constructed records. Q1 asks evidence-linked plant inputs and light distinction. Q2 asks needs, matter exchanges, decomposer reuse and conditional newcomer effects. Q3 extends that model with sunlight-food energy, four uses, material for growth/repair and outward warmth. Use the private answer keys and component allocations. Drawing quality, spelling and reading speed are not science constructs.

## Before class

Print the section's shared sheets, teacher-keys.md and practice-teacher.md. Keep answer keys separate from child pages. Print only each current day's sheet sections. Hold exits and end-of-unit cases unseen until administration. Supplies: Assessment-39, assessment-40, neutral directions, four repair packets, four assigned retry sheets, reflection, teacher-keys, formative record and original assessment-record. Cover all prior displays and notebooks on the assessment days.

## Running the section

Day 39: directions three, Q1 thirty-seven, independent checking five. Day 40: directions three, Q2 twenty-eight, Q3 fourteen. No teaching or E38 feedback. Score 12/14/10 components for 36 points, then return on day 41. Its four five-minute rounds permit at most four letter moves per child. One whole-class repair replaces a round. Assign one fifteen-minute retry route, then five reflection minutes.

Every student may pass in public talk. Hear rotating six and mark who, with paper evidence from everyone. Planned-only prerequisite records are not proof of completed teaching; supply direct illustrated explanations. Read records aloud before independent reading. Student pages stay within 350 words, with plain printed stems and 15-word sentences.

## Safety and alternatives

Read records neutrally or use established scribe/enlargement supports without causal hints. No equipment is required. Keep absences blank. Return retry/process feedback in individual folders on day 41 after class; retain unresolved gaps rather than promising a later repair lesson.

Handling days use the equipment and protection in their day blocks. Check plant/soil/mold and nonlatex-material precautions privately. Adult handlers use eye protection for splash/dust hazards, gloves and apron; wash afterward. Missing protection or safe washing means paper records. Never taste or sniff samples. Never look at the sun. Teacher stores or disposes of deteriorating closed containers under school procedures.

## Common errors and evidence

Sources A/B/C/D/I, returns E/J, energy F/G/H, interactions K. Correct component packets support independent groups. Preserve original points; fresh retry categories live only on the formative record.

The practice sheets keep evidence components separate. Do not demonstrate a complete held answer. Use the source chart to distinguish observation, source mechanism and invented practice. General facts are checked in the course's S02 source rows. Exact practice species relationships and numbers are constructed unless the card says historical.
