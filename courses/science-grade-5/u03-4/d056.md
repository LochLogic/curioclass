# Day 56: Follow separated materials

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current teacher-only answer keys, including added practice rows. Cover exit-56. Check sand and food allergies privately. Stage six filters over stable cups on trays, labeled sand/salt pinches and water. Prepare a matching dried filtrate without classroom heating. Integrate actual-fallback-log and change-model fields below; display separation-picture once. Set out washing supplies.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles through cleanup; no tasting, sniffing, airborne dust or loose metal filings. Adult collects solids. Without protection, allergy clearance or washing, use S56 and leave handling unobserved.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 55's tickets: error I then error E, five minutes each. Use [[s03-P56i]], then [[s03-P56e]]: compare two minutes, annotate two, submit one in one. If many day 55 tickets, more than ten, show either error, replace its group move with whole-class repair. Review grade 4 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Review observation versus inference.

#### 13 min. Separation

Six groups work concurrently. Two setup minutes: review day 52's filter, filtrate and uncertainty labels on apparatus. Add 20 mL water using 1 mL marks. Five one-minute roles: measure, add portions, stir gently, pour through filter, inspect retained/passed material. Rotate observation access; everyone records [[s03-P56log]]. Allow six minutes for drainage, matching dried-dish viewing and recording. A wet or unclear result stays; retry once within this block. Then supply S56, announced and printed as invented for practice, separately from actual readings. Never promise recovered crystals.

#### 6 min. Model and photographs

Use the original magnetic-separation-diagrams instead of photographs, [[s03-P56m]]. No filings handled. Trace one successful route using separation-sequence, [[s03-P56]]. Review unseen material from day 52 and recovered residue from unit 1 day 6. A filter's inability to retain dissolved material limits this method. Keep original change-model marks; review grade 4 second-color revision with dates and initials. Practice follows successful routes only.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X56]] alone on exit-56; collect before discussion. After class, thirty forty-five-second readings take twenty-two-and-a-half minutes. Review record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return error F/H notes on day 57. Practice receives immediate feedback and stays in folders.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Adult collects liquid and sand waste. Thirty forty-second washes at two sinks take ten minutes. Use two more for trays, wiping and goggle care. Review goggles wiped and bagged. Keep protection on until splash work ends.

### Other rooms

No kit: use S56 and original diagrams, labeled invented; handling remains unobserved. No printer: copy prompts exactly, keeping exit covered. The diagrams supply all visual evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe kit, use invented S56 and magnetic-separation-diagrams. Copy boxes without a printer.

**Skip.** Group roles and public talk.

- **5 min. Recall.** Do [[s03-P56i]] and [[s03-P56e]].
- **15 min. Separate.** With protection and allergy clearance, filter sand/salt gently. Use [[s03-P56log]] and [[s03-P56]]. Otherwise trace S56.
  **Say.** "Where did dissolved material go?" Expect: through the filter with water; drying can leave residue.
- **10 min. Trace and wash.** Do [[s03-P56m]]. Adult collects waste; wash and clean goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X56]] alone. Then use its teacher key for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce diagram discussion; retain separation, exit and cleanup. Longer: trace the other material without adding a new claim.

### Words and supports

Review filter, filtrate, residue, uncertainty and unseen material. Review day 45's property-table labels and units. Stabilize funnels; allow neutral reading and a scribe while preserving student decisions.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 returns day 56's error F, clear means absent, and error H, failed separation proves formation. Two five-minute component repairs precede closed-display observations and model revision.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Source S03-PE4 and Source S03-safety. S56 and magnetic diagrams are invented for practice.
