# Day 58: Join the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current teacher-only answer keys. Cover exit-58. Arrange recorded class property and comparison tables from days 45 to 57, keeping actual/invented labels. Retain original notes. Prepare a blank teacher consensus chart and question board. Use today's separate component prompts; hold later assessment records unseen.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 57's tickets, error G. Use [[s03-P58g]]: compare two minutes, point to one recorded source for two, retain the component in one. If many day 57 tickets, more than ten, show error G, replace the group move with whole-class repair. Do not evaluate the fresh exit's two flawed claims during practice.

#### 16 min. Oral property rehearsal

Review property tables from days 45 to 51, [[s03-P58p]]. Pairs take four three-minute rounds: powder, metal, mineral, liquid. Each child has forty-five seconds to locate one recorded distinction; partners locate its test conditions. Use remaining four minutes for teacher feedback and uncertainties. Distinguish color, scratch resistance, magnet response, dissolving and supplied conduction records. Identify powders only where actual records distinguish candidates; the kitchen puzzle can remain partly unresolved. Never use laboratory samples in food. No new inventory packet.

#### 10 min. Change evidence hearing

Use [[s03-P58]] orally with days 54 to 57's existing notes. Review grade 4 observation versus inference. Six voluntary forty-five-second turns fit five minutes; review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Pairs use remaining five minutes to locate one correct starting/resulting or control tag. Public passing is welcome. Rehearse single correct components; no written revision or paired flawed-claim response.

#### 10 min. Consensus chart

Teacher records oral agreements. Review day 45's property-evidence table and day 52's unseen-material model: identify through distinguishing measured properties, preserve unseen material, compare controlled repeats and name evidence limits. Repeated changed properties support change; unsuccessful separation alone cannot settle it. Review question board: notices become questions. Attach evidence to each answer and retain uncertainty. Children point to existing notes without rewriting them.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s03-X58]] alone on exit-58, then collect. Thirty sixty-second readings need thirty after-class minutes. Review record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Hold error A/H feedback until day 61; no assessment-day reteaching. Practice feedback was immediate; papers stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No projector: draw the teacher chart. Missing earlier charts: read the labelled component records already supplied on days 56/57 and retained property notes. Do not manufacture actual observations or claim an unresolved identity.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw a consensus chart without a projector. Use retained supplied records when apparatus records are missing.

**Skip.** Public hearing.

- **5 min. Recall.** Do [[s03-P58g]].
- **12 min. Properties.** Use [[s03-P58p]] orally with retained powder, metal, mineral and liquid notes.
- **12 min. Changes.** Use [[s03-P58]] orally. Adult writes the consensus chart; child points to evidence.
  **Say.** "Does failed separation settle formation?" Expect: no; a method can fail on an existing mixture.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X58]] alone. Keep it for day 61 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public hearing, preserving both rehearsals and exit. Longer: add an uncertainty to the teacher chart through discussion, without another child revision.

### Words and supports

Review property, hardness, conduction, control, uncertainty and unseen material. Enlarge retained records. Neutral reading and a scribe preserve independent exit decisions.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 assesses personal measurements and new material records. Cover completed models and notebooks. Day 58's error A, shared color identifies, and error H, failed spoon separation proves formation, wait for day 61's repair rounds. No further unit 3 teaching precedes assessment.
