# Day 57: Compare dated changes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current teacher-only answer keys. Cover exit-57 and controls-cards results. Check labels/dates against day 55's starting record. Arrange six closed steel-wool display cups for concurrent group viewing; supply hand lenses. Integrate rust-records and actual-fallback-log fields below. Keep dated starting properties beside cups, not completed conclusions. Leave wire sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Teacher alone moves and stores wire cups. Children never open displays or touch wire. Use R57 for damaged, unclear or unavailable displays; retain actual failures separately.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 56's tickets: error F with [[s03-P57f]], then error H with [[s03-P57h]]. Five minutes each: compare two, annotate two, submit one. If many day 56 tickets, more than ten, show either error, its whole-class version replaces the group move. Review day 52's unseen material and method limits separately, never conflicting-repeat diagnosis in practice.

#### 6 min. Paper controls

Use controls-cards, [[s03-P57c]], before uncovering results. Review grade 4 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Children choose alone, compare with a partner, then reveal the invented three-pair record. This paper comparison replaces the unbuilt simulation; no digital tool. Locating a fixed feature differs from judging the exit's two records.

#### 9 min. Closed-display inspection

Use day 55's dated starting properties. Six groups inspect concurrently: two orientation minutes, five one-minute lens turns and two recording minutes. Each child adds [[s03-P57]] to rust-records. Match observation dates, cup and portion sizes. Retain an unclear view, retry once within the turn, then append R57, announced and printed as invented for practice. Actual cups may remain unchanged; never invent a result.

#### 14 min. Model revision

Use [[s03-P57model]] on retained change-model margins. Review grade 4 observation, inference and second-color revision: preserve originals, dates and initials. Draw known starting steel wool and later surfaces, with damp/dry comparison tags. Known material, starting properties and repeated controls support a moisture-linked change consistent with rust; brown color alone cannot identify it. Do not assign an untested gas role. Six voluntary forty-five-second turns fit five minutes; review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Others annotate, then receive feedback. Public passing is welcome.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X57]] alone on exit-57, then collect. Thirty forty-five-second readings take twenty-two-and-a-half after-class minutes. Review record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return error G on day 58. Practice feedback is immediate; diagrams stay in folders.

#### 2 min. Dry cleanup

Adult stores closed cups. Children file diagrams and lenses. No child handles wire.

### Other rooms

No displays: R57 supports interpretation only, not personal investigation credit. No printer: copy prompts and draw tables; conceal exit. Paper controls fully replace the unbuilt simulation's reasoning task.

### At home

**Swap.** Use invented R57 instead of wire cups. Copy controls and tables without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public hearing.

- **5 min. Recall.** Do [[s03-P57f]] and [[s03-P57h]].
- **6 min. Controls.** Do [[s03-P57c]] before uncovering results.
- **12 min. Observe and model.** Do [[s03-P57]] and [[s03-P57model]] with actual closed displays or R57.
  **Say.** "Which condition differed?" Expect: dampness, with cup, portion and dates matched.
- **7 min. Explain and file.** Compare model tags; preserve original observations. Adult stores closed cups.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X57]] alone, then discuss its teacher key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public turns; retain comparisons, model, exit and storage. Longer: locate another dated control observation without declaring identity from color.

### Words and supports

Review control, trial, observation, inference and uncertainty. A lens stand and enlarged records preserve decisions; a scribe records the child's own words.

### Tomorrow

Day 58 returns day 57's error G, bubbles alone prove formation, in one five-minute repair. Then rehearse property and change components orally and build the teacher's consensus chart.
