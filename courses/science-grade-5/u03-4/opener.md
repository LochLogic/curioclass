# Section 3.4: Separation and change evidence

## Purpose and science

A filter retains undissolved solid while liquid passes. Filtrate is the liquid that passes; residue is material left behind. Filtration cannot alone decide whether the retained solid existed before mixing. A failed separation may show a method limit. Compare known starting/resulting properties and repeated controls. Rust background applies to known steel wool; do not call any unidentified brown scrap rust.
