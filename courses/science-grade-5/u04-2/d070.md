# Day 70: Daylight across the months

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Prepare the day's item key, including the comparison for s04-P70-bars. Prepare today's half slip from exits.md with blank paired-bar space. Lay out D70.md, daylight-axes.md and single-day-strip.md. Bring day 69's tickets and both component notes, plus the formative record. Use D70's invented data; there is no checked numerical public extract. Keep completed graphs covered during the exit.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 69 s04-X69, s04-P69-lit and s04-P69-mark privately. Review return and repair. If many tickets show error B, spend five minutes aligning previously observed shadow marks and sunlight sides: two locating, two tracing, one preserving notes. For error G, spend five minutes sketching a fixed-center Earth with one observer turning into and out of lamp light. Keep yearly orbit out of the daily explanation. Over ten matching misses makes that move whole-class. Others inspect model limits; no extra repair minutes.

#### 7 min. Daylight instruction

Review grade 1 daylight: from sunrise to sunset; one moment and the whole day. An evening report is not the whole duration. Review grade 4 duration, elapsed sending time; shift explicitly from sending seconds to daylight hours.

Announce D70: invented for practice, a constructed site. Values are rounded whole-hour practice values, not precise local observations. Explain that actual records can retain minutes. Review day 69 spin as the daily light-dark explanation. Monthly duration differences are a different pattern; no seasonal mechanism is required.

#### 16 min. Daylight-only graph

Ten minutes alone: [[s04-P70-bars]] on daylight-axes. Review grade 5 day 9 data-graph: shared zero, equal scale, labels and units. State that one half of a two-hour interval is one hour. Teacher confers on endpoints before accepting comparisons. Use the pattern evidence described below when marking the comparison.

Six minutes: put monthly graph aside. **Ask.** [[s04-P70-strip]] Child shades remaining boxes before discussion. Expect eight dark hours because twenty-four minus sixteen is eight. Show the complete day boundary; keep this single-day strip separate from monthly graphs. Explain a light/dark key, using outline for light and hatching for dark. Do not make paired monthly practice bars.

#### 8 min. Pattern talk

Three minutes: pairs point to evidence in their daylight-only graph for their written comparison. Expect nine to fifteen to thirteen hours: rise, then fall among these samples. Three samples do not establish every month's value. If many claim equal duration, trace the distinct bar ends before retrying the comparison.

Five minutes: review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second reports fit; passing is allowed. All thirty return graphs, comparison and separate strip. Explain that spin supports daily cycling, while these samples describe seasonal duration without explaining its cause.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Reveal [[s04-X70]] only now. Three minutes alone on prepared axes, one to collect. After class: thirty forty-five-second exit readings plus thirty thirty-second practice scans take thirty-seven-and-a-half minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Explicit equal-duration claims get error C. Error G applies only to a volunteered yearly-orbit claim, never an omitted spin explanation. Return on 71.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy D70, blank axes and separate strip onto squared paper. Give held exit separately. No outdoor observation or digital tool is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy axes on squared paper. Draw twenty-four boxes for the separate strip.

**Skip.** Public reporting and group rotation.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 69 notes. Sketch the fixed-center spin model.
- **7 min. Duration.** Announce D70 invented; distinguish one moment from the whole day.
- **12 min. Graph.** Child does [[s04-P70-bars]], then puts it aside for [[s04-P70-strip]].
  **Say.** "How many hours remain dark?" Expect eight, since twenty-four minus sixteen is eight.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X70]] alone. Discuss its key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer reports; retain separate strip, graph and exit. Longer: identify which unsampled months remain unknown.

### Words and supports

Review daylight, duration and spin. Read records neutrally. Enlarge axes; use hatching rather than color. A scribe preserves the child's comparisons.
