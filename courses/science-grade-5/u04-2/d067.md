# Day 67: A fixed stick, moving marks

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md, checking today's CSV answers. Prepare only today's half slip from exits.md. Lay out shadow-log.md and S67.md. Install a blunt stick at a clear accessible site; inspect weather and route. Prepare dated background readings before and after class, using one clock convention. Keep observed records separate from supplied records. Bring day 66's tickets, locator notes and formative record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 66 s04-X66 and s04-P66-locate privately. Review return and repair. If many tickets claim down always means page-bottom, review day 62 center and centerward gravity. Rotate a correct tilted disk for two minutes, trace the centerward arrow for two, retain notes in one. Over ten matching misses makes this whole-class; others inspect observation labels. Leave gravity and pick up day 66's unresolved shadow question.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Review grade 1: shadow, the shade a stick makes where it blocks sunlight; away from the sun, opposite the shadow. **Ask.** [[s04-K67-sun]] Wait before revealing [[steps:s04-K67-sun]].

Teach apparent: how a position looks to an observer. Spin means Earth turns around its own axis. Offer spin as an initial explanation, not proof from one mark. A higher apparent sun makes a shorter shadow on level ground; a lower sun makes a longer shadow. Review grade 4 centimeter, ruler zero, whole centimeter. Read at 1 cm steps; never invent decimals. Observe ground only; never look at the sun, even through tinted glass.

#### 18 min. Field record

Three minutes travel and safety; stay clear of the fixed stick. Six representatives, one per group, each take at most one minute to mark and measure the current tip. Teacher handles compass. Label north, east, south and west on a ground sketch; diagonals lie between them. Use approximate directions, not degrees. Shared repeats at one time are not different hourly readings.

Nine minutes: all complete [[s04-P67-raw]] using shadow-log. Preserve date, stick/site, time convention and source. A fuzzy tip gets a whole-centimeter interval. Retry once within the slot, retain unclear or missing, then announce S67: invented for practice. Read one supplied row at a time. Never merge it into actual observations. Teacher's other timed readings remain dated background routines.

#### 8 min. Initial model

Three minutes alone: [[s04-P67-next]]. Keep the raw mark separate from the predicted location. Review grade 4 observation versus inference. Teacher labels the initial spin idea separately from evidence. No combined two-reading explanation is rehearsed.

Five minutes: review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second reports fit; anyone may pass. Everyone returns paper. Record descriptive prediction notes without calling an untested prediction wrong.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Reveal [[s04-X67]] only now. Three minutes alone, one to collect. After class, thirty thirty-second exit readings plus thirty twenty-second page scans take twenty-five minutes. Review the single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Initial models receive descriptive notes. Only an explicit unchanging-direction claim is error B. Return all on 68.

#### 3 min. Cleanup

Adult collects kit in one minute. Six handlers wash at two sinks, three per sink, forty seconds each: two minutes. Others handled paper only.

### Other rooms

Unsafe weather, inaccessible ground or absent kit: use S67, announced invented. No printer: copy records and blank log; give exit separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Use S67 instead of outdoor kit. Copy records without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 66 notes. Point toward the familiar disk's center.
- **7 min. Light.** Do [[s04-K67-sun]].
  **Say.** "Where would sunlight come from?" Expect north, opposite the southward mark.
- **10 min. Record.** Announce S67 is invented. Read one row. Child completes [[s04-P67-raw]].
- **5 min. Predict.** Child does [[s04-P67-next]]. Keep prediction separate from observation.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X67]] alone. Discuss its key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports, retaining observation, prediction and exit. Longer: examine uncertainty at a fuzzy tip.

### Words and supports

Apparent, spin, direction. Review shadow and opposite. Read records neutrally; offer tactile arrows and a scribe preserving choices.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 returns all papers in Return and repair. Error B triggers five minutes aligning timed ground directions. Then graph hourly lengths and inspect monthly comparability.
