# Section 4.2: Changing shadows and daylight

## Purpose and science

Spin changes apparent sun direction/height and daily illumination. Shadows point opposite incoming light. Describe monthly differences without their seasonal mechanism. Keep hourly and monthly patterns distinct.

Use argument with gravity/cause or stars/scale; use graphs with sky patterns. Separate observations from model inferences.

## Running the section

Teach apparent/spin and ruler conventions on 67; baseline and units on 68. Monthly comparison requires same stick, site and sun-time convention. D70 is invented, not a public numerical extract.

Explain conventions directly when earlier teaching is missing or only planned. Hear rotating six, forty-five seconds each inside five minutes. Record who; anyone may pass. Everyone returns paper. Read neutrally; a scribe preserves child choices. Student sentences have fifteen words at most.

## Safety and fallback

Ground observations only; never look at sun, even through tinted glass. Teacher installs blunt stick at accessible clear site. Six one-minute representative readings are shared. Ruler step 1 cm; fuzzy tips become intervals. Retry once, retain missing/unclear, switch to S67/S68. Six handlers wash at two sinks, forty seconds each, two minutes plus one for kit. On 69 use enclosed light, screen and lit routes; no eyes/lasers. Failed kit uses original lamp frames.

Announce and print invented for practice on constructed records. Keep actual dates and failed readings separately. Desk models are not astronomical observations. No visitor, unopened photo or local page is required.
