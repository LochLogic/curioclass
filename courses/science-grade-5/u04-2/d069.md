# Day 69: Testing a spinning Earth

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md with today's item key. Use the corrected screen-direction answer in the item key. Prepare today's half slip from exits.md. Lay out lamp-sequence.md and screen-direction.md. Fix an enclosed flashlight toward the screen. Put the globe in its beam with a paper observer mark; test whether the lit boundary is visible. Keep routes lit. If kit fails, use the supplied model frames, announced invented. Bring day 68's papers and formative record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 68 s04-X68, s04-P68-bars and s04-P68-conditions privately. Review return and repair. If many tickets show error B, sketch timed ground directions from the returned record: locate two minutes, trace two, retain notes one. Over ten matching misses makes this whole-class. If only graph gaps dominate, use this slot for zero and labels instead; do not add another repair. Others inspect comparable conditions.

#### 7 min. Model test

Review day 67 spin and apparent, including the longer shadow when sunlight appears lower. Spin changes the observer's orientation to incoming light. Fix lamp and globe center. Mark one observer. Turn the globe without moving its center, pausing at one position. Everyone predicts illumination before the light reveals it. **Ask.** [[s04-P69-lit]] Expect the lamp-facing observer lit; the far side blocked by Earth. Explain the daily alternation while holding orbital position fixed. No lasers or beams toward eyes. The disk alternative is a diagram with a shaded half, not a sphere-light measurement.

#### 19 min. Component predictions

Seven minutes: pairs inspect one lamp-sequence frame, with the other covered. Each records an illumination prediction, then checks against the teacher's demonstration. Preserve the original and date any revision. If kit fails, reveal each invented frame separately: frame A faces the lamp; frame B faces away. The distant frame uses the same leftward lamp position convention.

Six two-minute group visits remain seated while teacher brings the visible model into view. Each group gets one paused observer position, predictions, then illumination evidence. All children write on their own page; no thirty-child spoken queue. Do not make a chronological shadow-to-darkness log.

Use screen-direction separately: [[s04-P69-mark]]. Expect the mark right of the peg, away from the lamp and along beam travel. This single lit-position record tests the opposite-side idea. It supplies no measured shadow length. No additional peg experiment is required.

#### 7 min. Model evidence tags

Two minutes: teacher dates the revised spin explanation. Review grade 4 observation versus inference: separate lamp-model observation from astronomical inference. Lamp illumination is model evidence; it is not an observation of Earth from space. The small model does not establish astronomical scale or seasonal daylight causes.

Five minutes: review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second reports fit; permit passing. Children identify the observation supporting their prediction; all return the two component notes. Keep shadow direction separate from illumination states.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Reveal [[s04-X69]] only now. Three minutes alone, one to collect. After class: thirty thirty-second exit readings plus thirty thirty-second scans of both component notes take thirty minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only explicit unchanging-direction claims get error B; yearly orbit causing daily cycling gets error G. Missing links get descriptive notes. Return all on 70.

#### 3 min. Cleanup

Teacher switches off and stores light, globe and screen. Children clear paper and check lit routes. No dark walking.

### Other rooms

No globe or working light: use the invented frames and separate screen record. No printer: copy diagrams without answers; supply held exit separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Use the invented lamp frames instead of kit. Copy them without a printer.

**Skip.** Group visits and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 68's notes; inspect one graph label.
- **10 min. Predict.** Child does [[s04-P69-lit]] for one covered-frame position. Reveal afterward.
  **Say.** "Does lamp light reach this observer?" Expect yes on the facing side, no behind Earth.
- **10 min. Direction.** Give the separate screen record and [[s04-P69-mark]]. Discuss model limits.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X69]] alone. Discuss its key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports; retain component tests and exit. Longer: explain a limit without adding seasonal causes.

### Words and supports

Review spin, apparent, observation and inference. Offer large observer marks, tactile half-disks or a neutral scribe. Child chooses illumination and direction.

### Tomorrow

Day 70 returns E69 and both component notes. Its ten-minute Return and repair holds five minutes for error B and five for error G. Then graph daylight duration across months.
