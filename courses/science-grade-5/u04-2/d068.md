# Day 68: Showing hourly and monthly patterns

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md against current CSV answers. Prepare today's half slip from exits.md, including both blank grids and arrow space. Lay out S67.md, S68.md, shadow-log.md and length-axes.md. Inspect stick, route and weather. Bring day 67's tickets and both practice pages, plus the formative record. Keep supplied monthly records distinct from actual archives. Display no held graph.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 67 s04-X67, s04-P67-raw and s04-P67-next prediction notes privately. Review return and repair. If many tickets show error B, align S67's timed directions with ground-mark sketches: two minutes locating, two tracing, one retaining notes. Over ten matching misses makes this whole-class; others inspect uncertainty labels. Do not combine direction and monthly graphs during repair.

#### 8 min. Graph instruction

Review grade 5 day 9 data-graph labels and zero baseline. Three-minute retrieval: **Ask.** [[s04-K68-pencils]] Let children draw before showing steps. Five minutes: [[steps:s04-K68-pencils]]. Review grade 4 bar graph: bars start at the bottom line. For horizontal bars, that zero line stands at the left. Explain equal intervals, centimeter labels and ending between numbered gridlines. New time labels replace pencil labels; no coordinates. Review day 45 pattern chart: shared features. Here describe a relationship across times.

#### 18 min. Measure and graph

Six one-minute representative readings of the current shadow are shared to everyone. Review day 67 centimeter, ruler zero, whole centimeter. Observe ground only; never look at the sun. Teacher supervises clear stick space. Use day 67's fixed ground-direction marks. Put readings in shadow-log with date and clock convention. Retry unclear once within each turn; retain failures and announce S67 invented fallback.

Twelve minutes alone: [[s04-P68-bars]] on length-axes, using the explicitly supplied S67 series. Keep actual readings in their separate log. Show how each small fifth of a five-centimeter interval is one centimeter. Direction stays in the raw record. Monthly lengths stay in S68's raw table. Never build a combined daily/monthly display in practice.

#### 7 min. Evidence review

Two minutes pairs: [[s04-P68-conditions]]. Inspect date, fixed stick/site and local solar noon. Ordinary clock time alone may shift with daylight saving. Use S68 when archives lack comparable conditions; announce invented, never collected. Solar noon supplies the same sun-time convention.

Five minutes: review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second reports fit; passing is allowed. All return graphs and condition notes. Teacher gives descriptive label feedback; no combined graph is shown.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Reveal [[s04-X68]] only now. Three minutes alone on prepared grids, one to collect. Read the exact stem neutrally. Accept separate labeled displays and direction words or arrows keyed by time. After class: thirty forty-five-second exit readings and thirty thirty-second practice scans take thirty-seven-and-a-half minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Explicit unchanging-direction claims are error B; inaccurate bars alone receive descriptive notes. Return on 69.

#### 3 min. Cleanup

Adult collects kit in one minute. Six handlers wash at two sinks, forty seconds each: two minutes. Others handled paper only.

### Other rooms

Use supplied S67/S68 if kit, archive or safe access fails. No printer: copy blank axes onto graph paper, keeping exit data covered.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy axes onto squared paper. S67/S68 replace outdoor kit and missing archives.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 67 notes and locate one timed direction.
- **8 min. Bars.** Child tries [[s04-K68-pencils]]. Then show its steps.
  **Say.** "Where does each bar start?" Expect at the same zero line.
- **12 min. Graph.** Announce S67 invented. Child does [[s04-P68-bars]] and [[s04-P68-conditions]]. Keep direction and monthly records separate.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X68]] alone with blank grids. Discuss its key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce reports; preserve graph time and exit. Longer: audit an uncertain endpoint without claiming false precision.

### Words and supports

Review apparent, spin and direction. Explain baseline as the shared zero line. Enlarge grids or provide raised axes; scribe without choosing bar endpoints.

### Tomorrow

Day 69 returns graphs, condition notes and E68 in Return and repair. Error B gets five minutes aligning timed directions; graph gaps get private notes. Then test the spin model with a fixed lamp.
