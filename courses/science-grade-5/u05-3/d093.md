# Day 93: Equal shares of all water

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return s05-X92 and s05-P92 feature notes, including s05-P92-2. Separate teacher-only exit-key.md. Cover Archive A. Prepare the first loose strip with 100 equal intervals using graph paper. Keep the hundred-square paper for non-water retrieval only. Copy the source table once, on the student page. No class measurement of Earth's water is possible; these are supplied estimates.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair. If many made error D in day 92's exit, use five minutes to distinguish an inland salty lake from ocean. Otherwise that group receives support while others reread notes. Next five minutes: descriptive location corrections, especially wet underground gaps and land ice. Over ten gaps makes the corresponding route whole-class. Return all papers, preserving originals; no added individual conference.

#### 5 min. Retrieve equal shares
Review day 92: a hundredth is one of 100 equal portions. Read the invented mosaic aloud as invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-P93-2]] on hundred-square paper. Pairs point to their one marked tile. If parts become unequal, overlay equal cells before counting. This non-water grid is separate from water-strip work. No percentage calculation.

#### 7 min. Read Earth evidence
Read the student table, supported by Source S05-water. Review fresh water's little dissolved salt and salt water's abundant dissolved salt. Almost all salt water is ocean water; salty groundwater and inland lakes also belong. Supplied estimates describe amounts, not map areas. **Ask.** [[s05-P93-3]] Expect salt water, including the inland and underground stores. These estimates cannot establish any town's available supply.

#### 10 min. Apply to strips
Review day 92's named whole, equal units and zero. Children use [[s05-P93]], placing the supplied partition on a linear strip. Use graph-paper spacing to keep equal units. Write the whole and category labels; use different hatching for each category. Teacher retains the consensus strip, children retain their evidence notes. If a salt label becomes ocean only, reread the category list. Do not assemble a grid-and-bars package or repair an atlas.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. The next six explain one strip choice, forty-five seconds each; passing is allowed. Partners previously explain simultaneously, not to the teacher. All thirty hand in strips during the remaining half minute. Record oral evidence only for those heard.

#### 8 min. Paper exit
Reveal [[s05-X93]] and its blank display. Read exactly; say this archive is invented for practice, not an Earth estimate. Children construct independently. Keep all practice covered. After class: thirty tickets at forty seconds, twenty marking minutes; thirty strips at ten seconds, five minutes. Review the single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error D excludes non-ocean salt water. Return papers on day 94.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy only the table and one strip for practice. Copy the held fifty-cell grid and matching axes separately. Neutral reading and larger cells preserve the same construction task.

### At home
**Swap.** No printed strips: draw equal intervals on squared paper. Copy the held display separately.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class collection.

- **5 min. Return.** Review day 92's location feedback.
- **5 min. Retrieve.** Child uses [[s05-P93-2]] on a hundred-square grid.
- **7 min. Evidence.** Read today's table; use [[s05-P93-3]].
- **10 min. Strip.** Child completes [[s05-P93]].
  **Say.** "What water belongs in the salt category?" Expect ocean, salty groundwater and salty lakes.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Give [[s05-X93]] and its held display, alone. Keep it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce oral reports, preserving strip work and exit. Longer: partners discuss [[s05-C93-1]] orally, without a new written record.

### Words and supports
Review whole and equal share. Enlarge unit marks; a neutral scribe follows the child's choices. Keep category prompts away from the exit.

### Tomorrow
Day 94 returns s05-X93 and s05-P93 strips, including s05-P93-2 and s05-P93-3. Its ten-minute Return and repair gives error D five minutes, followed by five for equal-unit or copying corrections. Then lift the fresh subset into its own named whole.
