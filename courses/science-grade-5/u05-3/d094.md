# Day 94: A fresh-water whole

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return s05-X93 and s05-P93 strips, including s05-P93-2 and s05-P93-3. Separate teacher-only exit-key.md. Cover the fresh-drawer exit. Retain the all-water strip and its heading. Prepare a removable fresh piece, an enlarged fresh-only strip and loose category labels. Print the fresh-only table once on the student page. Earth shares are supplied estimates; do not present them as class measurements.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair. If day 93's tickets showed error D, spend five minutes including inland salt stores in the salt category. Otherwise support that group while others read feedback. Use the next five minutes for descriptive equal-unit or copying corrections. Over ten gaps makes that route whole-class. Return strips without extra conferences; preserve originals.

#### 7 min. Reopen subset model
Review day 92's cloth subset and day 93's all-water partition. Lift the fresh piece while keeping its parent strip visible. Link it to the enlarged fresh-only strip. Each of its 100 new equal units refers to fresh water. Enlarging adds no water. Retain both whole labels. Paper supplies the full explanation while the optional display is unavailable; zero digital minutes. If a child keeps the all-water heading, trace the fresh piece back to its parent before relabeling.

#### 15 min. Apply to nested strips
Read today's supplied table. Review day 92's combined ice and full remainder categories. Combined ice includes polar land ice, glaciers and permanent snow, excluding sea ice. Review soil moisture, water held in soil; it joins the remainder, alongside ground ice. Pairs arrange category strips using graph-paper intervals; hundred-square paper is an unmarked spacing guide, not a category grid. **Ask.** [[s05-P94]] Children independently label the fresh-only strip and place the card. Partners each explain for forty-five seconds. If error F drops other stores, restore the full category label. Teacher retains consensus; children retain their labels and source locator. No grid-and-bars package.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear the next six, forty-five seconds each; anyone may pass. Each points to the whole label or one category choice. All thirty return labeled strips during the final half minute. Record oral evidence only for those actually heard.

#### 8 min. Paper exit
Reveal [[s05-X94]] with its unused display. Read exactly; say invented for practice, not another Earth estimate. Children work alone. Collect half tickets. After class: thirty tickets at forty seconds, twenty minutes; thirty strip labels at ten seconds, five minutes. Review one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error E retains the all-water whole; error F reduces the remainder to lakes and rivers. Return papers on day 95.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw loose strips and labels; copy the held grid and axes separately. Enlarge or use raised equal intervals. A neutral scribe follows choices without supplying category names.

### At home
**Swap.** No strips: draw the parent strip and a separate enlarged fresh strip. Copy the held display separately.

**Skip.** Public reporting and class collection.

- **5 min. Return.** Review day 93's category feedback.
- **7 min. Reopen.** Lift the fresh piece; keep its parent visible.
- **15 min. Apply.** Read today's table. Child completes [[s05-P94]].
  **Say.** "What water belongs in this whole?" Expect fresh water alone, still part of all water.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Give [[s05-X94]] with its new display, alone. Keep it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce partner reporting, preserving the subset trace and exit. Longer: partners discuss [[s05-C94-1]] orally, without a new written record.

### Words and supports
Review whole, share and surface and other fresh water. Keep both whole headings visible during practice. Use larger loose labels; remove prompts for exit.

### Tomorrow
Day 95 returns s05-X94 and s05-P94. Ten-minute Return and repair: five minutes tracing the changed whole for error E, then five restoring the full remainder for error F. Next, read supplied small endpoints and align one swamp strip.
