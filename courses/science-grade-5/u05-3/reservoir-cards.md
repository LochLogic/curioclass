# Separate water-store cards

Read one card at a time. These are original schematic descriptions.

| Store | Separate description |
| --- | --- |
| Ocean | Connected salt water surrounds continents. |
| Polar land ice | Ice covers land in Greenland and Antarctica. |
| Glacier | A mass of land ice moves slowly. |
| Groundwater | Water sits in gaps below the land surface. |
| Lake | Water collects in an inland hollow; some lakes are salty. |
| River | Water flows along a channel. |

Dry gaps do not hold groundwater. Sea ice is separate from land ice.
