# Supplied Earth-water evidence

These are rounded estimates, not class measurements.
Use equal shares, with the whole named each time.

| Whole: 100 all-water shares | About this many |
| --- | --- |
| Salt water | 97 |
| Fresh water | 3 |

Salt includes ocean water, salty groundwater and salty lakes.
Almost all salt water is in the ocean.

| Whole: 100 fresh-water shares | About this many |
| --- | --- |
| Ice caps, glaciers and permanent snow | 69 |
| Fresh groundwater | 30 |
| Surface and other fresh water | 1 |

Polar land ice belongs in the combined ice category.
The last category includes more than lakes and rivers.
Examples include soil moisture and ground ice.

| Supplied small-store endpoint | Parts per 100 fresh-water shares |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh lakes | 0.26 |
| Rivers | 0.006 |
| Swamps | 0.03 |

Numbers are supplied; do not calculate conversions or percentages.
Use the same zero, scale and fresh-water units for all inset bars.
No atmospheric quantity is asked.

Facts: [Water-distribution table](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/where-earths-water),
Shiklomanov, 1993. Original table wording and drawings.
