# Day 92: Water stores above and below land

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return s05-X91 and s05-P91 locator slips. Separate teacher-only exit-key.md. Cover the held map. Lay out five reservoir cards, withholding the named-place polar row for the later assessment. Draw a separate invented polar-land-ice sketch. Board: an invented triangle mountain symbol with its meaning; a sixteen-square cloth; a separate zero-to-ten axis. Say all board records are invented for practice. Use original schematics when maps are unavailable; never claim they show actual places.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair. If day 91's tickets showed error A, spend five minutes tracing an action and its evidence on returned papers. Next five minutes: error C, point to the observed air effect despite unseen air. More than ten gaps makes that route whole-class; otherwise support that group while others reread evidence. Return locator slips without extra conferences. Review the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Retrieve map language
Review key, a box that tells what each mark means; liquid, water that flows; solid, water that keeps its shape. Ice and snow are solid water. Everyone answers [[s05-P92-2]] independently, then points with a partner. Review lake: water collected in a hollow. If symbols confuse, connect just the triangle to its meaning before moving on.

#### 8 min. Teach locations and representations
Four minutes: show five separate cards and the invented polar-land-ice sketch. Introduce reservoir/store, groundwater in underground gaps, slowly moving land glacier and polar ice. Distinguish polar land ice from sea ice. Review day 88's little-salt fresh water and abundant-salt water. Lakes and groundwater can be salty. Four minutes: model [[s05-K92-1]], then [[s05-K92-2]], revealing steps after each ask. [[steps:s05-K92-1]] [[steps:s05-K92-2]] Review graph units from unit 1 and scale labels from unit 4; directly explain water shares and changed wholes. One of 100 equal portions is one hundredth. Map area does not show stored amount.

#### 9 min. Initial model
First two minutes: define combined ice as ice caps, glaciers and permanent snow. Define surface and other fresh water as the remainder beyond combined ice and fresh groundwater, including ground ice, frozen water in the ground. Soil moisture means water held in soil. Next seven minutes: pairs use one card at a time, [[s05-P92]]. **Ask.** [[s05-P92]] Each sketches one separate store and adds a location note, not a complete locating map. Both partners explain for forty-five seconds each. Teacher assembles a location list, not a finished exit sheet. If groundwater becomes one giant underground lake, redraw separate wet spaces. Children retain feature notes; teacher retains the initial consensus. Sources S05-D92-water and S05-D92-ice support real store facts; schematics remain invented.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear the next six, forty-five seconds each; passing is allowed. All thirty tag notes invented schematic and hand them in during the remaining half minute. Record listening only for those heard.

#### 8 min. Paper exit
Reveal the unused map with [[s05-X92]] exactly as printed. Say invented for practice. Children label independently on the half ticket. After class: thirty tickets at thirty seconds, fifteen minutes; thirty feature notes at ten seconds, five minutes. Give descriptive initial-model feedback; use the formative scale for exits. Error D calls inland salty water ocean. Return all papers on day 93.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the separate practice components; copy the covered exit separately. Enlarge labels or scribe neutrally. No samples, soil or ice are handled.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class collection.

- **7 min. Retrieve.** Use [[s05-P92-2]] and the triangle symbol.

- **6 min. Locate.** Child uses [[s05-P92]] on one card.
  **Say.** "Where could this water sit?" Expect the card's location, including underground gaps when chosen.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s05-X92]] with its new map, alone. Keep it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce pair reporting, preserving component modeling and exit. Longer: partners discuss [[s05-C92-1]] orally, without a new written record.

### Words and supports
Store: a place holding water. Whole: everything included. Share: one equal portion. Offer large separate sketches; withdraw location prompts for exit.
