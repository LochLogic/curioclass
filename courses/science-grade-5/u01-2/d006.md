# Day 6: What the dried dishes show

## Teacher page

### Before class
Keep d006-key.html teacher-only. Return day 5's exit and practice with descriptive feedback. Check dish labels, preparation records, dryness and contamination. Use matched dishes made with the same water and containers. Ask privately about food allergies. Prepare fresh-mixture supplies, goggles, washing points and N6. Use the formative class list: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Add error notes separately, without points.

### Safety and set-up
Wear goggles from setup through cleanup; never taste or heat. Keep substances away from eyes and mouths. Review hand-lens handling from grade 2: hold above the dish and adjust distance, without touching residue. Use records without safe handling, rinsing water or eyewash.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen
Return day 5's s01-X5 and practice papers: s01-P5-1, s01-P5-2 and s01-P5-3. Review dissolve, residue and unseen parts from day 5. If many showed error C, put an empty-liquid drawing beside a retained-material drawing. State the board claim: dissolved matter is gone. Use today's dried-dish comparison to decide which account has support; do not repeat the filter exit. Leave error B's full repair for day 7.

#### 18 min. Residue investigation
Six groups of five work concurrently. First three minutes: inspect labels and preparation dates. Identify which dish began with salt water and which with plain water.

Next five minutes: [[s01-P6-1]], observation portion. Each child gets a 30-second lens turn per pair of dishes, 2.5 minutes per group; record between turns. The lens reveals residue detail, not unseen parts. Preserve dampness or unexpected deposits. If samples are damp, unmatched or contaminated, record that limitation and read N6 aloud as invented for practice. Never replace an observation with N6.

Next five minutes: teacher portions water and salt into fresh dishes. Each group stirs gently and labels the date and contents. Observe without tasting; do not expect today's dish to dry now. Final five minutes: [[s01-P6-2]], keeping observed appearance separate from the proposed unseen-scale model.

#### 14 min. Test the claim
Apply day 5's model and evidence routine. Finish [[s01-P6-1]] with the comparison and claim test. Keep salt in the proposed clear-liquid model; connect it to recovered residue. Plain-water comparison limits the idea that every dish leaves that solid. If actual dishes are inconclusive, say so; reason separately from invented N6. Explain only that water dries away; no account of how it leaves is required.

Pairs explain their dish evidence, two 45-second turns each. The teacher circulates and offers descriptive feedback, without claiming to hear all 30. Error C: compare the claim with recovered material. Date additions beside the original drawing. Collect practice pages; return day 7. After class allow 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes, for descriptive feedback.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s01-X6]] alone on paper. This invented cooling cycle is first seen now; no heating demonstration occurs. After class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes marking with the teacher answer key. Use the class list's three categories; note errors separately. Return day 7 in Reopen.

#### 4 min. Clean-up
Adults secure labeled fresh dishes away from food and collect lenses. Review damp wiping and handwashing from day 2. Four parallel washing queues allow about 25 seconds per child. Collect goggles; adults complete sanitizing and required contact time after class, before reuse.

### Other rooms
No dishes, lens or safe supplies: use N6 explicitly as invented, with no handling claimed observed. No printer: copy the table; reveal the exit only at its turn.

### At home
**Swap.** Without safe dishes, lens, goggles or washing facilities, read invented N6. No printer: copy the table and hide the exit.

**Skip.** Group rotations; preparing a new dish when using records.

- **5 min. Reopen.** Read day 5 feedback; revisit the board claim.
- **12 min. Compare.** Do [[s01-P6-1]] using dishes or N6. Keep actual observations separate.
- **10 min. Model.** Do [[s01-P6-2]], using today's appearance or N6's fresh stage.
  **Say.** What challenges the gone claim? Expect recovered salt-water residue beside plain water without residue.
- **3 min. Put away.** Wash hands and secure materials; the adult cleans goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X6]]; work alone. Discuss with the teacher answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: shorten pair reporting, retaining observation, claim test and exit. Longer: locate the preparation detail making the dish comparison useful.

### Words and supports
Review: residue, dissolve and unseen parts from day 5; evidence from grade 3. Frame: I changed ___ because ___ showed ___. Read dish labels aloud. A partner may describe visible detail; accept a labeled drawing or dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 7 returns s01-X6 and both practice tasks in Reopen. If many show error C, reconnect recovered salt with retained material. Combine this with day 5's error B feedback in one five-minute slot, choosing the larger gap. Keep dated originals for consensus model 1.
