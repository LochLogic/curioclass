# Day 5: Clear water, unseen sugar

## Teacher page

### Before class
Keep d005-key.html teacher-only. Preportion water and sugar; ask privately about food allergies. Prepare comparison cups, goggles and washing points. Print S1/S2 below. Lay out day 4 feedback. Prepare dated salt-water and plain-water dishes if absent; use day 6's fallback if damp. Prepare a formative class list: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Add error notes separately, without points.

### Safety and set-up
Adults and children wear goggles through mixing and cleanup. No tasting; keep materials from eyes and mouths. Use records without rinsing water, eyewash or safe handling. Sanitize goggles after collection, completing contact time after class before reuse.

### The day

#### 5 min. Notice dissolving
Review day 4's revised air drawings: visible emptiness did not establish absent air. Six groups stir sugar into water gently. Take five 20-second stirring turns per group, concurrently. Watch grains; stop handling for spills or unsafe behavior.

#### 12 min. Investigate
Alone, [[s01-P5-1]]. Compare plain water with the mixture. Describe appearance before explaining unseen parts. Keep unexpected grains in the record. Stir once more; if still cloudy, retain that observation and use S1 separately, labelled invented for practice. Clear means see-through, not empty. Dissolve means material spreads through water in parts too small to see. A grain is visible; model dots stand for much smaller unseen parts. The drawing proposes an explanation, not a sighting of those parts.

#### 10 min. Two-source reading
Review: source means a card or page giving information, from grade 4. Evidence supports a claim, from grade 3. Read S1/S2 aloud as invented accounts; nobody tastes.

**Ask.** [[s01-K5-1]]

Model finding a line in each account, then linking them verbally, without drawing a liquid model.

[[steps:s01-K5-1]]

Pairs use [[s01-P5-3]], two 30-second turns each. All point to one line; public sharing is optional. These accounts support a model but do not show unseen parts directly.

#### 10 min. Revise
First five minutes: return day 4's papers and s01-X4a/s01-X4b. If many showed errors A/I, revisit retained syringe air. For error x, distinguish running readings from additions on returned work. Use the same five-minute slot, targeting each child's gap.

Remaining five minutes: [[s01-P5-2]], using S2 alone. Add dots in an enlargement of the liquid; label them unseen sugar parts. Date the change beside the original, without erasing. This is the paper dot panel; no viewer is needed. Error B: a drawn dot is not a visible grain. Error C: loss from sight does not establish loss of matter. Collect practice pages for descriptive feedback tomorrow; after class allow 30 times 20 seconds, 10 minutes.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s01-X5]] for the first time. Alone, paper only; read without hints. After class: 30 tickets times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes marking. Use the class list's three categories; note errors separately. Return day 6 in Reopen.

#### 4 min. Clean-up
Review damp wiping and handwashing from day 2. Adults collect cups and goggles; wash hands in four parallel queues, about 25 seconds each including movement. Adults finish rinsing and sanitizing after class. Children retain the model evidence folder.

### Other rooms
No materials: use S1/S2 as invented records, not observations. No printer: copy practice text; reveal the exit only at its turn. No projector: read the printed accounts.

### At home
**Swap.** Without safe supplies, goggles or washing facilities, use invented S1/S2. No printer: copy practice; hide the exit.

**Skip.** Group rotations and class feedback queues.

- **5 min. Notice.** Compare the cups, or read S1.
- **10 min. Observe.** Do [[s01-P5-1]]; keep observation apart from explanation.
- **10 min. Read.** Read S1/S2 and [[s01-K5-1]]. Do [[s01-P5-3]].
  **Say.** What does each account add? Expect appearance from S1 and reported sweetness from S2.
- **7 min. Revise.** Do [[s01-P5-2]] with S2 alone. Date the change; retain the first drawing.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X5]]. Answer alone; discuss using the teacher answer key afterward. Wash and put away supplies together.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public sharing, keeping observation, reading, revision and exit. Longer: add an observation-versus-explanation label to existing work.

### Words and supports
Review: source, evidence and unseen air parts. Residue means solid left when liquid dries. Frames: I changed ___ because ___ showed ___. Source ___ says ___. Read accounts aloud; allow labelled drawings or dictation without suggestions.

### Tomorrow
Day 6 returns today's exit and practice in Reopen. If many show error C, compare empty and retained-material drawings with residue evidence. Error B feedback returns again in day 7's Reopen. Keep the question board: Where is dissolved material?
