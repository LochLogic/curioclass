# Section 2.5: Following food energy

## Purpose and science

Sunlight energy enters plant food, then transfers through feeding. Food supplies material for growth and repair and energy for growth, repair, movement and warmth. Material use and energy use are different accounts of a process that can need both. Matter can be reused; energy passes outward as warmth, with new sunlight input. Separate arrow styles and a legend prevent treating energy as a matter cycle. Source is where transferred energy comes from; recipient receives it.

## Safety and alternatives

P35 is fresh plant 18 g, not dry gain. S37 is dried soil 480/479 g, a small uncertain difference. H36 is supplied shadow 27 cm. Retry once within the station; keep damp/unreadable actual status and supplied lines separate. No heating.

Handling days use the equipment and protection in their day blocks. Check plant/soil/mold and nonlatex-material precautions privately. Adult handlers use eye protection for splash/dust hazards, gloves and apron; wash afterward. Missing protection or safe washing means paper records. Never taste or sniff samples. Never look at the sun. Teacher stores or disposes of deteriorating closed containers under school procedures.
