## s02-X34

Sunlight to plant/nectar food to insect to bird, with distinct energy-arrow style and legend.

Look for: connected sunlight-food-insect-bird energy path | distinct energy style and legend

Meets: Dashed energy arrows run sunlight to plant food to insect to bird. Solid arrows mean matter.

## s02-X35

Plant-made grain provides chick growth/repair material and energy originally captured from sunlight. The nearby plant is context, not a hidden second ask.

Look for: food material for growth and repair | food energy traced to sunlight through plants

Meets: Grain supplies matter to build and repair the chick. Its energy originally came from sunlight captured by plants.

Does not yet: Food provides energy but no body material

## s02-X36

Matter: air/water into hazelnut-producing plant, food matter into dormouse growth/repair. Energy: sunlight through food to dormouse for growth, repair, motion and warmth, then outward warmth. Separate legends. Shadows: same stick, place, method and sun time; record weather/clock changes.

Look for: separate matter/energy with four energy uses and outward warmth | same stick/place/method/sun time shadow controls

Meets: Food matter builds and repairs; sunlight energy passes through food for all four uses and leaves as warmth. Keep stick, place, method and sun time fixed.

Does not yet: Sunlight becomes body material

## s02-X37

Chiefly air/water inputs with small minerals. Shed leaves retain matter returned through decomposers/environment for possible reuse. Taught evidence supports sources; the short record alone does not establish every input/unseen route.

Look for: chief air/water and small mineral inputs | shed matter through environmental returns and plant reuse

Meets: Air and water supply most matter. Shed leaves retain matter that decomposers return to environmental pools for plant reuse.

Does not yet: Soil supplies nearly all new plant matter

## s02-X38

Correct light to energy; air/water are matter inputs. Continue dead-snail matter through decomposers/environment for reuse. Energy passes sunlight-reeds-snails-ducks then outward warmth, not back to reeds. Food material builds/repairs; energy supports growth, repair, motion and warmth.

Look for: repairs light/matter and dead-matter faults | repairs energy cycle and includes food material/four energy uses

Meets: Light supplies energy, air/water supply matter. Decomposers return remains. Food energy supports four uses and leaves as warmth; food matter builds and repairs.

Does not yet: Sunlight is plant material
