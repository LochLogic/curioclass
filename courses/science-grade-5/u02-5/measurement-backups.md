# P35, H36 and S37: supplied readings

Invented for practice. These are separate records, not one combined investigation.
Keep them apart from actual unreadable or missing readings.

| Record | Supplied reading | Instrument step | What was measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| P35 | 18 g | 1 g | Fresh harvested plant, with tray tared |
| H36 | 27 cm | 1 cm | Shared fixed-stick shadow |
| S37 | 480 g before; 479 g after | 1 g | Dried soil on the same tared scale |

P35 includes water. It does not establish dry plant gain.
S37's small difference may lie within the comparison's measurement uncertainty.
Do not subtract fresh plant weight from dry-soil weight.
Damp actual soil stays marked damp; never heat it in class.
