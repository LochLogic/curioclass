# Day 35: Fresh weight and food uses

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Cover exit-35.md using exits-34-38.md; separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Print current energy-components.md cards and P35 from measurement-backups.md. Set harvest-log.md and jar-log.md beside the station; stage labeled pots and trays. Have jar-panels.md J23 views ready. Check allergies privately. No safe protection or washing: use records throughout.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 34's s02-X34 and s02-P34-2 feedback. Review return and repair. If many showed error H, work together; otherwise target that group. Two minutes contrast direct sunlight with T2's plant-food source, two individual [[s02-P35-3]], one collect. Expect grass food. Keep the source/recipient cards separate.

#### 5 min. Weighing preparation

Review tare, day 4: zero the empty tray before recording contents. Review whole-gram resolution, day 8: the scale changes in 1 g steps. Review comparable water content, day 19: fresh weight includes water. It cannot establish dry gain. Teacher wears eye protection, nonlatex gloves and apron, finishes damp plant separation, and avoids dust. Children handle dry paper only. No tasting; jars remain closed.

#### 12 min. Harvest records

Review harvest, grade 3 day 42: taking plant parts for use. Teacher shares six fresh-plant weighings, one minute each, after two setup minutes. Read each stable display to 1 g; announce tray ID and date. **Ask.** [[s02-P35-1]] Everyone records all readings. Four remaining minutes cover one retry and spreading soil thinly in labeled trays for day 37. Retain unreadable results. Print and say P35 is invented for practice, 18 g fresh plant on a 1 g scale; record it separately. Never call it class growth.

#### 5 min. Closed-jar view

Review observation and inference, grade 4 day 16. Teacher shows closed jars; children do [[s02-P35-4]]. If unclear, try a safe viewing angle once, then retain the reason. Missing or unsafe jars use J23 views, announced invented for practice. Review the day 23 jar system-model: visible droplets do not reveal every internal route. Do not open or sniff jars.

#### 9 min. Food-use sort

First minute: return day 34's s02-P34-1 labels. Review day 34 food matter and energy. Read invented T3 to T8 separately. **Ask.** [[s02-P35-2]] Pairs sort; each child records card IDs independently. Expect material for building/repair and energy for the four uses. A process may need both; classify the card's specified use. Error G: point to the building-material card. Collect all records and sorts.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Adult damp-wipes equipment, contains soil, removes protection and washes with soap/water; use towels and waste bag. Children file dry papers. Contact means stop and wash, replacing remaining observations with records. No pupil sink queue.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X35]] alone on half slips. Read the exact stem. After class: thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen minutes marking; ten more for practice feedback. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error G denies food material; silence alone is not that claim.

### Other rooms

No equipment: use P35 and J23, invented for practice. Keep actual failures separate. No printer: copy current cards, tables and covered exit.

### At home

**Swap.** No pots, jars, scale or protection: use invented P35 and J23. Copy cards instead of printing.

**Skip.** Six-tray station, public work and sample handling when using records.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 34 feedback. Do [[s02-P35-3]].
- **10 min. Weight.** Use P35 for [[s02-P35-1]].
  **Say.** "What does this weight include?" Expect water, so it is not dry gain.
- **5 min. View.** Use J23 for [[s02-P35-4]].
- **10 min. Sort.** Read T3 to T8 and do [[s02-P35-2]].
  **Say.** "Does growth need both?" Expect material and energy; each card names one use.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X35]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sort discussion, preserving six readings, safety and exit. Longer: explain why fresh/dry weights cannot be subtracted as growth.

### Words and supports

Review tare, whole-gram resolution, observation, inference and food uses. Read records aloud. Dictation preserves individual reasoning.
