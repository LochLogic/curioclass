# Component practice answer keys

Teacher only. Print separately from student sheets.

## s02-P34-1

Sunlight to grass food or grass food to cricket, on separate cards; tail is source, head recipient.

Look for: source/tail and recipient/head

## s02-P35-1

Whole-gram fresh weight, tared tray/pot ID/date, or unreadable. Fresh weight includes water, not dry gain.

Look for: within 1 g step | fresh rather than dry

## s02-P35-2

Building/repair material cards: material. Energy for moving, growing, repair or warmth: energy. Processes may need both; classify the specified use.

Look for: stated matter/energy use distinguished

## s02-P36-1

Whole-centimeter actual reading/controls or unresolved reason. H36 separately supplied.

Look for: 1 cm step and unresolved status retained

## s02-P36-2

Matter movement, energy transfer or outward warmth, as specified on that isolated card.

Look for: one correct legend

## s02-P37-1

Whole grams with tray/pot ID/date and drying status. Do not compare unlike wet/dry states.

Look for: 1 g steps and drying status

## s02-P37-2

Relevant source label; observation distinguished from inferred mechanism.

Look for: relevant return source

## s02-P38-1

Relevant locators on unchanged, separate input/return/transfer panels; no prose revision.

Look for: correct locators on separate unchanged panels
