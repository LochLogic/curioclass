# Separate energy and food-use cards

Invented for practice. Each row is a separate card.

| Card | One component |
|---|---|
| T1 | Sunlight energy enters grass food. |
| T2 | Energy in grass food transfers to a cricket. |
| T3 | Food material builds new body parts. |
| T4 | Food material repairs body parts. |
| T5 | Food energy supports movement. |
| T6 | Food energy supports warmth. |
| T7 | Food energy supports growth. |
| T8 | Food energy supports repair. |
| T9 | Energy leaves living things as warmth. |

Source means where transferred energy comes from.
Recipient means what receives that energy.
Use solid arrows for matter and dashed arrows for energy.
Each task uses a separate arrow or card, never a complete overlay.
