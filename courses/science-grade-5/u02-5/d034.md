# Day 34: Where food energy starts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Print T1/T2 only from energy-components.md. Cover exit-34.md using exits-34-38.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys separate. Bring day 33 feedback and record.md. Leave later cases covered.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 33's s02-X33 and correction feedback, s02-P33-3 and s02-P33-4. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. If many, more than ten, showed error K, contrast shared food with an unaffected-other-eater claim together; otherwise target that group. Two minutes contrast, two individual correction using [[s02-P34-2]], one collection. Expect either eater may lack food. No predator model is reconstructed.

#### 8 min. Retrieval and teaching

Day 33 left food energy unresolved. Review transfer, source: where transferred energy comes from, and recipient: what receives it, grade 4 day 16. Spend three minutes pointing tail to source and head to recipient. Review day 18: sunlight supplies food-making energy; air and water supply matter. Review food web, day 32: linked feeding routes from food to eater. Teach legend: a key for arrow styles. Solid means matter; dashed means energy. Food contains matter and usable energy.

#### 16 min. Component arrows

Read T1/T2 aloud as invented for practice. **Ask.** [[s02-K34-1]] Pause for placement before revealing [[steps:s02-K34-1]]. Model only T1, then remove it. Pairs inspect T2 separately. **Ask.** [[s02-P34-1]] Children draw two isolated arrows, each in its own space. Expect sunlight to grass food on T1; grass food to cricket on T2. Do not join or overlay them. For error H, point back to T2's food source.

#### 12 min. Evidence talk

First minute: return day 33's s02-P33-1 and s02-P33-2 feedback. Each child displays one isolated labeled arrow and points to its T-card evidence. Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who, grade 4 day 16: six forty-five-second turns within five minutes; others inspect their labels silently. Passing is allowed. Collect everyone's labels. Clarify source versus recipient without building a complete chain.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X34]] only now. Read its exact stem neutrally. Children work alone on half slips, using the supplied solid matter chain. Leave drawing room below it. After class: thirty slips at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes marking, plus ten for labels. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record.md. Error H requires a stated direct-sunlight claim; an omitted arrow needs follow-up.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy separate T cards and response spaces. Keep the exit covered until use.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy T1/T2 and response spaces if printing is unavailable. Point at paper instead of a board.

**Skip.** Public hearing and classmates' displays.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 33 feedback. Do [[s02-P34-2]].
- **8 min. One arrow.** Read invented T1. Try [[s02-K34-1]], then show its steps.
  **Say.** "Where does this arrow start?" Expect sunlight, its source.
- **10 min. Separate cards.** Do [[s02-P34-1]] using T1 and T2 separately.
  **Say.** "What receives energy on T2?" Expect the cricket, through grass food.
- **7 min. Evidence.** Let the child point to each card's supporting words.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X34]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce the hearing, keeping component matches and exit. Longer: explain why reversed arrows misidentify recipients.

### Words and supports

Review transfer, source, recipient and food web. Legend explains line styles. Read cards aloud; accept dictated labels. Review nectar, grade 2 day 65, only when reading the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 returns s02-X34 in Return and repair: error H needs the plant-food link. Return s02-P34-2 feedback with it. Return s02-P34-1 inside Food-use sort, one minute. Then the teacher lifts and weighs plants while children record food's material and energy uses.
