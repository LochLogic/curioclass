# Day 36: Food uses and outward warmth

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md; separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Cover exit-36.md using exits-34-38.md. Set current energy-components.md cards apart. Prepare harvest-log.md and H36 from measurement-backups.md. Retrieve day 20's shadow record and fixed site; check safe access, supervision and the earlier sun-time convention. If travel cannot fit five minutes, use H36.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 35's s02-X35 and s02-P35-3. Review return and repair. For error G, contrast food as energy only with T4's repair material: two minutes contrast, two individual [[s02-P36-3]], one collection. More than ten misses: whole class; otherwise targeted. Expect material for repaired parts, with energy also needed.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Review day 34's sunlight energy and matter distinction. Model separate T3/T4 material destinations, then T5 to T8 energy uses: motion, warmth, growth, repair. Each process may need both; each card highlights one use. **Ask.** [[s02-K36-1]] Take responses before [[steps:s02-K36-1]]. Explain energy passes outward as warmth; plants need new sunlight energy. Error F applies matter reuse to energy. Never combine these components into a complete animal food-origin model.

#### 5 min. Monthly shadow record

Review monthly shadow record, day 3, and day 20's demonstration. Within five minutes, including movement, teacher marks the tip using the same fixed stick/place and earlier sun time; record clock changes. Measure from stick base to tip with the 1 cm ruler. **Ask.** [[s02-P36-1]] Share one reading with everyone. Look at shadows only, never the sun; supervise the nearby site. Cloudy, unsafe or unclear: retain reason, retry only within time, then announce H36, invented for practice: 27 cm at 1 cm steps. Missing date/time stay unspecified.

#### 15 min. Separate uses

First minute: return day 35's s02-P35-1, s02-P35-2 and s02-P35-4 feedback. Review day 35's specified material/energy uses. Pairs inspect one separate T card at a time. **Ask.** [[s02-P36-2]] Each student selects an isolated legend and records its card ID. Expect solid material for T3/T4, dashed energy for T5 to T9. If sunlight is called body material, error D, contrast incoming air/water matter with light energy. Do not rehearse the combined exit model or shadow-control answer alongside it.

#### 7 min. Legend hearing

Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear six forty-five-second turns within five minutes, each explaining one isolated arrow. Everyone may pass. Other students check their own card against its printed statement. Collect legends and shadow records from everyone; reserve two minutes for collection and clarification.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Adult stores ruler and chalk; fixed stick stays in place. Children file dry cards and return safely.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X36]] only now; both parts stay on paper. Read exact stem. Leave a large model area and two control lines on the half slip. After class: thirty times forty-five seconds, twenty-two and a half minutes marking; ten for practice. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Diagnose stated errors D, F and G, not omissions alone.

### Other rooms

No outdoor access: H36, separately labeled invented for practice. No printer: copy current cards and response spaces; hold exit unseen.

### At home

**Swap.** No fixed stick, safe site or ruler: use invented H36. Copy cards if needed; point at paper instead of a board.

**Skip.** Public hearing and group movement.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 35 feedback. Do [[s02-P36-3]].
- **8 min. Outward warmth.** Read T9. Try [[s02-K36-1]], then show its steps.
  **Say.** "Where does this arrow end?" Expect surrounding warmth, without returning to plant food.
- **5 min. Shadow.** Use [[s02-P36-1]] with the actual reading or separate H36.
- **12 min. Separate uses.** Read T3 to T8. Do [[s02-P36-2]].
  **Say.** "What does this card name?" Expect material or energy, according to its words.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X36]] alone. Discuss with the teacher key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce hearing, preserving food uses, shadow slot and exit. Longer: distinguish a material-return arrow from T9 without joining them.

### Words and supports

Review source, recipient, legend and shadow: where light is blocked, grade 1 day 1. Describe line styles aloud. Dictation or tactile arrows preserve independent reasoning.
