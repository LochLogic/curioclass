### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 37's s02-X37 and correction feedback, s02-P37-3, s02-P37-4 and s02-P37-5. Review return and repair, with oral/pointing contrasts only. First five minutes, error A: soil chiefly supplies growth versus day 18's air/water account; [[s02-P38-2]]. Second five, error E: disappearance versus C3 returns; [[s02-P38-3]]. Each block has two minutes contrast, two individual silent pointing then simultaneous partner turns, one transition. More than ten stated errors: whole-class support; otherwise target that group. No written model correction.

#### 5 min. Question board

Review day 18's plant inputs, day 29's matter returns and day 36's outward warmth. Read the retained question board beside the separate correct panels. Link one question to one component at a time. Review observation and inference: source-chart.md retains evidence limits, not proof of every route. Distinguish checked science sources from invented practice illustrations. Keep unresolved class readings unresolved; do not invent a consensus observation.

#### 16 min. Accuracy rehearsal

First minute: return day 37's s02-P37-1, s02-P37-2 and s02-P37-6. Review legend: solid matter, dashed energy. Partners point to C1, then separately C3, C4, C5 and C6. Alternate forty-five-second explanations concurrently; both partners get each component within eight minutes. **Ask.** [[s02-P38-1]] Each child circles locators on the existing unchanged panels and records panel IDs on the checklist. Expect S02-WEB for incoming plant matter, S02-CYCLE for returns, T1/T3/T4/T9 as invented illustrations. Use remaining time to clarify unsupported locators. No draft, revision or integrated flawed model appears in practice.

#### 10 min. Consensus hearing

Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who: six forty-five-second evidence turns fit five minutes. Each explains one component and its locator; passing is allowed. Everyone submits the locator checklist. In the remaining five minutes, teacher records oral agreements and questions on separate panels. Agreement follows evidence; it is not a vote about science. Keep plant inputs, matter returns and energy transfers separate. No complete combined model is constructed.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X38]] and its flawed diagram only now. Read exact stem neutrally; children correct independently on half slips. Use drawing space rather than copying the stem. After class: thirty times forty-five seconds, twenty-two and a half minutes marking; ten for checklists. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Stated sunlight material, disappearance and cycling-energy claims are errors D, E and F. Missing evidence invites follow-up. Hold all exit feedback until day 41.
