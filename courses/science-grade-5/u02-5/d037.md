# Day 37: Dry-soil readings and evidence limits

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Cover exit-37.md using exits-34-38.md; separate teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys. Stage labeled trays, scale, harvest-log.md and jar-log.md. Set S37 from measurement-backups.md and J23 views from jar-panels.md apart. Display only separate C1/C3 from correct-panels.md; keep T3/T9 from energy-components.md for repairs. Check allergies privately. Missing safe protection or washing means records only.

### The day

#### 15 min. Return and repair

Return day 36's s02-X36 and s02-P36-3. Review return and repair: three five-minute blocks, each two-minute contrast, two-minute individual correction, one-minute collection. First error D: light energy versus air/water matter, [[s02-P37-3]]. Second error F: outward warmth versus cycling energy, [[s02-P37-4]]. Third error G: food building material versus energy only, [[s02-P37-5]]. Each block goes whole class if more than ten stated that error, otherwise to its group. Others locate the same correct component. Do not rebuild a complete animal model.

#### 4 min. Dryness and tare

Review day 35 tare and day 8 whole-gram resolution: zero the empty tray; read 1 g steps. Teacher checks comparable drying and original tray IDs. Damp, spilled or uncertain samples remain unresolved; do not invent dry weights. Compare only like quantities, with handling losses noted. Teacher uses eyewear, gloves, apron and appropriate dust protection. Never stir, blow or sniff soil; observers sit away. No tasting; jars stay closed.

#### 8 min. Soil station

Teacher shares six one-minute tared readings, identifying tray and drying status. **Ask.** [[s02-P37-1]] Everyone records whole grams. Use two contingency minutes for one retry. If drying or weighing fails, retain reason; print and announce S37, invented for practice: 480 g before, 479 g after on a 1 g scale. Its 1 g difference may lie within comparison uncertainty. Never subtract fresh day 35 plant weight from dry soil. No classroom heating.

#### 4 min. Closed-jar view

Review day 23's jar system-model and grade 4 observation/inference. Show closed jars; do [[s02-P37-6]]. One safe-angle retry, then actual unclear reason and separate J23, invented for practice, if needed. Visible droplets do not establish unseen gas routes. Preserve earlier records.

#### 5 min. Separate evidence locators

First minute: return day 36's s02-P36-1 and s02-P36-2. Review roots/water and leaves/air, day 18. Point to C1 with Source S02-WEB. Separately review decomposer, day 28, and matter-cycle returns, day 29; read C3 with Source S02-CYCLE. **Ask.** [[s02-P37-2]] Each child tags a plant-source locator and a separate return locator. Expect the named science sources, not jar droplets as proof of decomposition. Collect all records. No combined input-return model is practiced.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Adult contains soil, damp-wipes trays, removes protection and washes using soap/water and towels. Never sweep dry. Children file dry records. Accidental contact means stop and wash; replace observation with records.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X37]] alone. Children revise its stated partial shrub-to-shed-leaves route independently on half slips. Read exact stem without interpreting it. After class: thirty times forty-five seconds, twenty-two and a half minutes marking; ten for practice. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Stated soil-only and disappearance claims are errors A and E; omissions need follow-up.

Shorter: limit viewing commentary, preserving repairs, readings and exit. Longer: name a measurement that would improve comparability.
