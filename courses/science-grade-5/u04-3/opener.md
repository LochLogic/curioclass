# Section 4.3: Changing night view

## Purpose and science

Earth orbit changes matched outward night views; spin explains nightly apparent motion. Moon own orbit changes its background position approximately monthly. No phases or seasonal mechanism. Not all stars disappear seasonally.

Use argument with gravity/cause or stars/scale; use graphs with sky patterns. Separate observations from model inferences.
