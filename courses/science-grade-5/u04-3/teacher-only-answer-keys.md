# Teacher-only solved evidence and samples

Teacher only. Keep apart from every child packet.

## s04-X71

Answer: One symbol per evening: winter C=3,D=0; summer C=0,D=3. Include key/group/season labels and explicit zeros. Cause not required; omissions alone are not D.

Look-fors: two accurate series | key and labels | zeros distinct from missing

Meets: One dot is one evening. Winter C three/D zero; summer C zero/D three. | Not yet: Daily Earth spin alone explains different seasonal night-star records. (error D)
