# Day 71: Counting clear-night views

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Prepare the generated key for the added pattern item. Bring day 70's exit, graph and strip notes. Prepare thirty student pages and held current-day half slips with graph space. Use component-tags.md's evidence-location frame on side 2, without its day 74 question. Keep future tickets covered. Put graph paper beside each slip.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 70 s04-X70, s04-P70-bars and s04-P70-strip privately. Review return and repair. If many tickets show error C, spend five minutes locating unequal daylight endpoints on their returned graphs. For volunteered error G, spend five minutes sketching a fixed-center Earth with an observer turning into sunlight. Over ten matching misses makes that move whole-class; otherwise work beside a small group. Ready children audit labels. Preserve originals.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Connect yesterday's monthly daylight question to which stars show at matched nighttime. Announce N71 invented for practice, not local observations. Teach star group: selected stars tracking a view, not stars physically joined. Visible here means above the clear, unobstructed horizon at that time. Review grade 1: not seen is not gone.

Review grade 5 day 45's pattern chart: different materials share a property. Explain this new visibility key directly: Y, zero and missing have different meanings. Teach pictograph notation directly: one symbol stands for one observed evening. Labels name season and group; the symbol key states its unit. Do not model a two-series graph.

#### 16 min. Chart components

Eight minutes: model reading Winter 1's conditions before its Harbor mark. Pairs trace the other winter rows, then each records [[s04-P71-tally]]. Exclude the cloudy extra record; label it missing, never zero.

Eight minutes: put raw charts aside. **Ask.** [[s04-P71-key]] Each labels the separate symbol example before discussion. Expect two clear observed evenings. Use visibility-key.md's convention, never a two-series construction. Review day 9 data-graph labels; new counts are evenings, not grams. If many include Winter 5, return to its conditions flag before counting again.

#### 8 min. Pattern notes

Three minutes alone: [[s04-P71-pattern]], using raw Harbor records only. Review observation, inference from grade 4: supplied records describe visibility; its cause remains a question. Five minutes: review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second reports fit; passing is allowed. All thirty submit both sides. Keep Ridge raw; never graph the groups together during practice.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Reveal [[s04-X71]] now: three minutes alone, one to collect. Its graph uses the half slip's blank graph space. After class, thirty forty-five-second exit readings plus thirty thirty-second practice scans take thirty-seven-and-a-half minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record graph omissions descriptively; error D requires the stated spin-alone claim. Return all work on 72.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy raw N71 and the separate symbol example once on paper. Give the held exit separately. All work stays indoors; no night visit or digital tool.

### At home

**Swap.** No graph paper: draw spaced rows on plain paper. Copy N71 if needed.

**Skip.** Public reports and class rotation.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 70 notes; compare the daylight endpoints.
- **7 min. Records.** Announce N71 invented. Explain Y, zero and missing.
- **12 min. Components.** Child does [[s04-P71-tally]] and [[s04-P71-key]].
  **Say.** "What do two dots represent?" Expect two clear observed evenings.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X71]] alone, with blank graph space. Discuss the answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports, keeping both component tasks and exit. Longer: discuss why cloudy records cannot establish invisibility.

### Words and supports

Star group, visible, symbol key. Read records neutrally. Enlarge symbols; a scribe preserves the child's decisions.

### Tomorrow

Day 72 returns E71 and both component tasks plus pattern notes. Its five-minute Return and repair prioritizes graph labels or error D, never both full repairs. Then teach orbit and test outward night views.
