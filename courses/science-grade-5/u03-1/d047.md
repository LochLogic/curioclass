# Day 47: Compare measured portions

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only answer keys. Keep exit-47 covered; exits-45-47 is a teacher index. Prepare six sets of four preweighed coded powders; retain actual weights, creator/date and identities privately. Set out property-table, actual-fallback-log, record and powder-cards P47 fallback only. Mark five child roles per group, rotating across cups.

### Safety and set-up

The day before, check ingredients and allergies privately. No tasting, sniffing or blowing. Goggles stay on through twelve-minute cleanup. Teacher handles spills and collects solids. Without protection, allergy clearance or two sinks, use records without child handling; mark practical actions unobserved. Measuring spoons transfer powders; they do not establish equal weights.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 46's tickets. Say the component record is invented for practice. If many chose by shine, error A, use [[s03-P47r]]: compare two minutes, annotate two, submit one. More than ten misses gets the same whole-class move. Ready children locate the distinguishing property. Review property and grade 4 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Practice stays in folders; give feedback during visits.

#### 14 min. Measuring investigation

Six groups work in parallel. First six minutes: one one-minute teacher scale visit per group verifies one portion; other weights come from the dated preparation log. Teacher reviews tare, 1 g steps and units, demonstrated on day 46. Each child measures 20 mL water, thirty seconds each: four test cups plus one water-only checking cup. Each measures cup height, twenty seconds each. Review ruler zero and whole centimeters from day 20; cup height describes equipment, not hardness. Five water and five ruler turns total about four minutes per group; one visiting child still fits six minutes.

Last eight minutes: run four two-minute cup cycles. Add one coded portion, stir gently thirty seconds, observe sixty seconds after stirring starts; use remaining time to record and rotate roles. Keep water source, portion weight, stirring and interval fixed. Review dissolve from day 5: material spreads into unseen parts. Do not promise particular outcomes or identities. [[s03-P47]] records personal water readings; [[s03-P47o]] records outcomes. A clock holds scientific conditions, never student speed.

#### 10 min. Evidence table

Complete property-table and actual-fallback-log with quantities, conditions, creator/date and source: personal action, teacher reading or invented record. Retain one uncertain reading and retry once within four minutes. If still unclear, preserve both and use P47 separately: say it is invented for practice. **Ask.** [[s03-P47c]] compares two complete rows; do not identify materials or ask for missing measurements. Announce its fallback pair as invented for practice. Teacher visits groups during six minutes of recording; feedback stays beside originals. More than ten unit omissions gets a brief unit-label reminder within this part.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X47]] on exit-47, independently on paper. Read neutrally; collect before discussion. After class: thirty times forty seconds equals twenty teacher minutes for tickets, plus thirty times twenty seconds equals ten for practice review. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record. Return tickets on day 48; practice feedback goes into folders today.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Stagger six groups through two sinks: thirty 40-second washes need ten minutes. Two further minutes hold teacher cup collection and goggle care. Goggles stay on until samples are cleared. Dry waiting children file original and supplied records separately.

### Other rooms

No kit: use P47, labelled invented, without practical credit. Without a printer, copy exact prompts and blank tables. Keep the exit unseen.

### At home

**Swap.** Use P47's invented record instead of powders. Copy prompts and tables without a printer.

**Skip.** Group roles and scale visits.

- **5 min. Return.** Discuss day 46's note. Do [[s03-P47r]].
- **12 min. Record.** Read P47 as invented. Do [[s03-P47]] using its supplied water amount, then [[s03-P47o]]. Label both supplied.
  **Say.** "Which condition stayed fixed?" Expect a recorded portion, water amount, stirring or interval.
- **10 min. Compare.** Do [[s03-P47c]] with two actual complete rows if available. Otherwise use its invented pair. Keep source labels.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X47]] alone, then discuss its key and return a brief note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce reporting, preserving measurements, observation cycles, exit and cleanup. Longer: compare another pair of complete observations.

### Words and supports

Review dissolve, fair test, tare, g, mL and ruler zero. Enlarge marks; use a scribe for original decisions.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 returns day 47's tickets: error A, a single property proves identity; error B, weights without grams. Its ten-minute Return and repair holds both five-minute moves before metal/liquid comparisons and the teacher circuit demonstration.
