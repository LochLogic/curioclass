# Day 45: Lookalikes need tests

## Teacher page

### Before class

Send family home. Read current teacher-only answer keys, including new rows on the generated key. Keep exit-45 covered. Display jar-drawing with jar-picture. Prepare four coded cups per group; retain identities privately. Set out property-table, actual-fallback-log, record and the day 45 portion of powder-cards. Use exits-45-47 only as a teacher index.

### Safety and set-up

The day before, check ingredients and allergies privately. Six groups of five: four tiny portions on trays, one lens each. Goggles on through twelve-minute cleanup. No tasting, sniffing, blowing or cooking. Lab samples never enter food. Without protection, allergy clearance or two working sinks, use records with no child handling or practical credit.

### The day

#### 7 min. Opening

Keep day 44's promise: begin unit 3 with four lookalike powders. Display the invented kitchen puzzle, never a food decision. State the unit question: how can tests distinguish materials, and when does mixing make something new? Review question board: notices become questions. Everyone writes [[s03-P45q]]. Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who: six 45-second turns within five minutes; passing allowed. Collect all thirty papers.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Review property from grade 2: something we can see or feel; a test can show more. Color tells color. Review its pattern chart: different materials share a property. **Ask.** [[s03-K45-1]] using counters; hear responses before revealing [[steps:s03-K45-1]]. Review grams, g, from day 1 and milliliters, mL, from day 3; explain weight and water-volume labels directly. Review grade 4 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Preview dissolving as review from day 5.

#### 12 min. Observation and planning

Six parallel groups use five one-minute lens turns; each child inspects four cups. Write [[s03-P45]] with four labelled rows, then [[s03-P45t]]. Observe without naming identities. Remaining seven minutes hold planning and teacher visits. If appearance becomes an identity claim, error A, return to the observed property. Record creator/date and actual or supplied status in actual-fallback-log. Say supplied powder-cards are invented for practice.

#### 4 min. Model

Review grade 2 sorting chart: one column per property, one row per sample. Start property-table with each child's observations and separate proposed tests. Review day 9 data-graph labels; explain these new column meanings directly. Post questions still unanswered. First ideas stay visible; papers stay in folders.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X45]] on exit-45 for independent paper responses. Read neutrally; no hints. Collect before any discussion. After class, thirty times forty seconds equals twenty teacher minutes for tickets, plus thirty times twenty seconds equals ten for practice. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record. Return tickets on day 46; practice feedback goes into folders today. Assign error A/B only from a stated claim.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Groups stagger through two sinks: thirty 40-second washes take ten minutes. Two further minutes hold teacher powder collection and goggle care. Goggles remain on until samples are removed. Dry waiting students file tables. Teacher wipes spills; stop handling if dust escapes.

### Other rooms

No kit: read only day 45 powder-cards as invented records. No printer: copy the diagram, blank tables and exact current prompts. No projector is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Use day 45 powder-cards instead of loose powders. Copy tables and exact prompts without a printer.

**Skip.** Public turns and group handling.

- **5 min. Notice.** Look at jar-drawing. Do [[s03-P45q]].
- **8 min. Model.** Do [[s03-K45-1]] with colored counters or drawings.
  **Say.** "What can color tell us?" Expect color alone, not identity.
- **15 min. Observe and plan.** Read the four invented observations. Do [[s03-P45]] for each cup and [[s03-P45t]]. Keep first ideas.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X45]] alone. Discuss its key afterward and return a brief note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reporting, preserving individual observations, planning, exit and cleanup. Longer: add a second safe test.

### Words and supports

Review property, fair test, dissolve, g and mL. Read neutrally; enlarge records or scribe the child's decisions.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 returns day 45's tickets: error A, color proves identity; error B, weight without units. Two five-minute repairs precede source-lined notes and day 44's separate properties/purpose repairs.
