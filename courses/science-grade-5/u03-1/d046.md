# Day 46: Better tests need better records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only answer keys. Prepare day 45's tickets and day 44's separate readiness notes. Keep exit-46 covered. Lay out property-cards, property-table, source-lined-notes and record. Open exits-45-47 as teacher index only. Check the scale and measure; use the invented recording-model fallback if unavailable.

### Safety and set-up

Prepare dry buttons and plain water the day before. Teacher alone handles demonstration equipment, wearing splash goggles for pouring. No powders, scratching, live circuit or warm water today. Preview records replace these apparatus. No tasting. Wipe water spills immediately; children have no wet contact.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 45's tickets. Say the supplied component records are invented for practice. First five minutes: error A, color proves identity; [[s03-P46rA]] locates one distinguishing property. Next five: error B, weight without units; [[s03-P46rB]] locates a unit. Each move: compare two minutes, annotate two, submit one. More than ten misses replaces the group move with whole-class repair. Ready students annotate the complete component records. Review the formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. All practice stays in folders; teacher gives feedback during visits.

#### 5 min. Recording model

Review source from grade 4: a card or page that tells you something. Review its two-column notes: source statement and source location. **Ask.** [[s03-K46-1]] before revealing [[steps:s03-K46-1]]. Review tare from day 35: zero the empty cup, then add dry buttons. Review whole-gram steps from day 8, mL from day 31, and retained original readings. Explain each mark directly. Teacher reads actual whole g, mL and thermometer marks. Retry an unstable reading once; keep both, then separately label the supplied fallback invented for practice. Children copy one source-lined observation.

#### 10 min. Properties repair

Return day 44's c1-R44a with its next step. Review grade 2 property words: rough, smooth, bends, stiff. All use property-cards records: [[s03-P46]], four minutes; [[s03-P46b]], four; [[s03-P46c]], two. Review sorting chart and shared-property pattern chart. These sort components, never select a material and propose another test. Ready children describe one safe single-property observation instead of repeating a mastered sort.

#### 10 min. Purpose repair

Return day 44's c1-R44b. Review grade 2 choice model: job, property tested, what the test showed, our choice. Read the equal-drop record four minutes; [[s03-P46d]], four; [[s03-P46e]], two. Compare identical sheet areas and drop sizes. No further-test proposal here. Ready children explain the evidence limit: the record covers this test only.

#### 6 min. Property preview

Use [[s03-K46-2]] and its invented single-property records. Children label each property; teacher reveals [[steps:s03-K46-2]] afterward. Model a gentle scratch with a finger tracing the diagram, never real scratching. Trace the complete dry tester diagram and disconnecting step. Hardness is scratch resistance; reflectivity is shine. Magnetic response is observed pull. Electrical conduction permits current in a complete loop. Review grade 4 heat transfer, warmer toward cooler; name heat conduction through a material. Add source/test columns to property-table.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X46]] on exit-46; everyone answers independently. Collect without peer feedback. After class: thirty times forty seconds, twenty teacher minutes for tickets; reserve another twenty for practice in folders, forty seconds per child. Return practice notes in folders today and tickets on day 47. Use record's single formative scale, not points. An unsupported answer receives a follow-up before a letter.

### Other rooms

No instruments: use recording-model fallback, labelled invented. No printer: copy the exact student records and prompts. Paper is the full path.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy records without a printer. Use invented recording-model values instead of equipment.

**Skip.** Public turns and mastered repair repetitions.

- **5 min. Return.** Discuss day 45's note. Do [[s03-P46rA]] and [[s03-P46rB]].
- **7 min. Notes.** Do [[s03-K46-1]].
  **Say.** "Where did this observation come from?" Expect its trial or card location.
- **8 min. Sort.** Do [[s03-P46]], [[s03-P46b]] and [[s03-P46c]].
- **8 min. Purpose and preview.** Do [[s03-P46d]], [[s03-P46e]] and [[s03-K46-2]]. Discuss the teacher key afterward.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X46]] alone, then discuss and return a brief note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce mastered repetitions, preserving both repair routes and the exit. Longer: add a source location to another observation.

### Words and supports

Review source, property, stiff, fair test and g/mL. Enlarge marks; accept dictation without suggesting the test.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 returns day 46's tickets. If many chose by shine, error A, locate a distinguishing property in five minutes. Then compare measured powder portions under fixed conditions.
