# Day 106: build two planned filters and check a fair test

## Teacher page

### Before class
Separate teacher-keys.md and today's held-exits.md. Use the current-day data key for all items; it supersedes shared keys. Retrieve day 104 sketches and day 105 controls. Per group of five, prepare two covered-edge funnels/cups, equal quarter-cup portions of rinsed sand and gravel per filter, paper supports and cloth. Set trays, goggles, nonlatex gloves, towels, waste containers, wipes and soap/water. Check allergies, eyewash, supervision and six existing wash places. Otherwise select construction-records.md before class.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Honor day 105's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return E105 and component pages privately. Five minutes each: error E, [[s06-P106-repair-E]], then error F, [[s06-P106-repair-F]]. Contrast unmatched versus matched conditions, then one result versus retained repeats. More than ten misses uses the whole-class round; otherwise assigned children repair while others inspect sketches. Collect fresh corrections and log unresolved needs.

#### 5 min. Safety and setup
Review prototype: a model built to test an idea. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Check protection and covered edges. Adults alone cut, rinse, portion or repair. No tasting, blowing dust or face touching. Use clean classroom materials only. Stop for missing protection, damage or uncontrolled spills. Review system-model arrows: input passes through media toward collection; building has not shown performance.

#### 15 min. Construct
Six parallel groups build from approved sketches: seven minutes per prototype, one transition. Each gets one paper support, quarter-cup sand and quarter-cup gravel; only cloth count changes. Keep height and supports fixed. Rotate accessible assembly actions; adults install wet media as necessary. Each child records [[s06-P106-assembly]] and [[s06-P106-inventory]]. Teacher names actual actions on action-roster.md. Records-only groups inspect construction-sequence.svg through construction-records.md and annotate components; construction remains unobserved. Do not match unfamiliar plans to built records during practice.

#### 3 min. Fairness inspection
Teacher visits six groups for 25 seconds each, with 30 seconds movement. Inspect one fixed portion using the inventory response. Review design-cycle: define, sketch, build, test, compare. Hold unsafe or mismatched apparatus for adult correction before trials. Everyone submits a control note; no public speaking queue.

#### 7 min. Cleanup
Two minutes: collect solids in sediment waste containers and wipe trays. One: review goggles wiped and bagged, in parallel. Four: remove gloves and wash with soap/water. Thirty 45-second turns at six existing places take 3.75 minutes. Keep protection through splash risk; no solids down sinks. Adult stores labeled prototypes.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Cover practice. Reveal [[s06-X106-1]] and [[s06-X106-2]] together on one half slip. Read exact stems, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection. Matching gaps get descriptive feedback; unequal inputs show error E. After class: thirty slips at 25 seconds take 12.5 minutes; component pages at ten seconds take five. Use one formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep practical observation separate.

### Other rooms
No safe equipment or adequate adults: use labeled construction records for dry annotations. No devices needed. No printer: copy response spaces and exact exit stems, covered until release.

### At home
**Swap.** Missing safe apparatus, protection or washing: use construction-records.md; construction remains unobserved.
**Skip.** Group roles and station circuit.
- **8 min. Repair.** Return day 105 work; use [[s06-P106-repair-E]] and [[s06-P106-repair-F]].
- **5 min. Prepare.** Adult checks protection, covered edges and rinsed portions.
- **15 min. Build.** Child follows both approved sketches and records [[s06-P106-assembly]] and [[s06-P106-inventory]].
  **Say.** "Check one media portion against your classroom sketch." Expect matching quantity, or a mismatch needing correction.
- **7 min. Cleanup.** Adult collects solids; child wipes goggles and washes hands. Never taste.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice; child answers [[s06-X106-1]] and [[s06-X106-2]] independently. Return feedback on day 107.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce duplicate discussion, keeping construction, inspection, safety and exit. Longer: audit another fixed component without testing performance.

### Words and supports
Review prototype, fair test and design-cycle. Offer enlarged sketches, adapted grips and neutral scribing. Record assistance separately from science understanding.

### Tomorrow
Day 107 returns E106, component pages and repair slips. If many accepted unequal inputs, error E gets five minutes matching a condition. Matching gaps receive private drawing feedback. Carry approved controls and labeled prototypes into three trials each; keep actual actions separate from paper reasoning.
