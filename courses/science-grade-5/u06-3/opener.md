## Science and continuity

Trial means one try under stated conditions. Fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing.
Failure means an observed goal not met under a stated test condition.
Keep five roles distinct: pour, time, volume, category, fault/reset. Rotate them across trials.
Name every child's actual contribution; a shared successful filter is not individual evidence.
Use six serial slips, one per trial, alternating A/B. No compiled reconstruction practice precedes day 107.
T107 is a deliberately imperfect invented fallback, printed one sequential slip at a time.
A blocked outlet can give zero collected volume while cloudiness is not readable.
That is different from an unreadable volume, which is unresolved rather than zero.
On 108 the teacher plots all actual volumes before redesign; children audit one dot at a time.
Half-cup marks set precision. Check the physical half-cup threshold before rounding for plotting.
Between-tick volumes are estimates. Cloudiness remains a separate ordered category.
