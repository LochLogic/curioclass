# Day 107: run repeated fair trials and keep every result

## Teacher page

### Before class
Separate teacher-keys.md and today's held-exits.md. Use the current-day data key; scale-v2 replaces the old prompt and key. Retrieve approved controls and checked prototypes. Per group: tray, half-cup measuring cup, timer, six labeled cups, goggles and nonlatex gloves. Adult prepares stirred classroom soil-water, towels, sediment waste, wipes and soap/water. Check allergies, covered edges, eyewash, six wash places and adequate adults. Otherwise prepare separate sequential archive-T107.md slips.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Return privately: s06-X106-1, s06-X106-2, s06-P106-assembly, s06-P106-inventory, s06-P106-repair-E, s06-P106-repair-F.
Honor day 106's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return E106, component pages and repair slips privately. If many accepted unequal inputs, contrast unmatched/matched intervals with [[s06-P107-repair-E]]; otherwise assign repairs while others inspect controls. Give matching gaps descriptive feedback. Collect corrections. Final minute checks goggles, gloves and stable trays. No tasting; adult handles mixture and edges.

#### 23 min. Controlled trials
Review trial: one test; fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Six parallel groups use five setup minutes, then six three-minute rounds alternating A1/B1/A2/B2/A3/B3. Review day 105's controls: prewet each with one cup and drain 60 seconds. Adult freshly stirs/dispenses equal one-cup inputs before each round, staggering stations across one minute. Pour ten seconds within 60 seconds collection; settle twenty seconds at equal depth/light. Read eye-level half-cup steps; check the physical half-cup threshold before rounding. Between marks means estimate. One safe reread inside the round, then unresolved with reason. Review cloudiness: ordered categories, never an average or drinking-safety measure. Drain 60 seconds between trials; replace no media.

Rotate pour, time, volume, category and fault/reset roles. Each child keeps one [[s06-P107-serial]] response per round on a blank slip, using trial-slip.md as the shared field legend, never a compiled table. Teacher observes one group each round and records named actions on action-roster.md. Timer measures collection. Unobserved actions remain absent. Stop unsafe handling. Records-only groups receive T107 slips sequentially, read aloud as invented for practice; never mix supplied and actual readings. Keep blocked-outlet zero volume with unreadable cloudiness.

#### 5 min. Evidence limits
Review milliliter (mL): a unit for water volume. Draw equal 20 mL steps from zero through the seventh mark; use [[s06-P107-scale-v2]]. Keep this pictured instrument separate from cups, without conversion. Discuss one slip at a time. One result cannot establish consistency, error F; deleting an inconvenient result is error G. No winner table or shuffled reconstruction practice.

#### 7 min. Cleanup
Two minutes for sediment waste/surfaces; one for parallel goggle wiping/bagging; four for glove removal and soap/water washing. Thirty 45-second turns need six existing places. Review goggles wiped and bagged. Keep protection through splash risk, solids out of sinks. Adults store prototypes and original slips.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Cover practice. Reveal [[s06-X107]] on a half slip with six blank record rows. Read exactly, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection. After class: thirty records at 35 seconds take 17.5 minutes; 180 serial slips at eight seconds take 24 minutes; repair/scale pages at ten seconds take five. Review the sole scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Retain actual observations separately.

### Other rooms
Insufficient protection, adults or wash capacity: use sequential T107 records; conducting remains unobserved. No devices needed. No printer: copy one serial slip at a time and the exit at release.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce discussion, keeping all trials and cleanup. Longer: inspect another unresolved reading's limit.

### Words and supports
