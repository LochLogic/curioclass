# Day 9: Weigh ice through melting

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep d009-key.html teacher-only. Trial a very small ice piece melting at room temperature. Prepare six leak-checked bags and dry towels. Label six separate cooling bags, partly filled with water and ample expansion space. Reserve their before-readings card for day 10. Prepare graph paper with whole-gram endpoint room; do not use the shared sheet's compressed graph. Cover the exact exit stem on a blank half sheet. Retain day 8 papers for return.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 8's s01-X8, s01-P8-1 and s01-P8-2. Repair error D using the familiar dry inventory: keep the cup after moving pieces. Ready children annotate retained objects on their returned inventory. Give endpoint feedback now; its full F repair has a separate slot below. Store these corrected papers in folders.

#### 18 min. Melting measurements

Review grade 2's solid, liquid, melt, warmer. Review day 8's complete boundary, whole grams and repeat-reading routine. Use [[s01-P9-1]]. Six one-minute before visits and six after visits take twelve minutes; six minutes between rounds allow melting and transitions. Room warmth only. Keep bags sealed; dry exteriors before weighing.

At each visit zero the empty scale, verify the unchanged check object, and read the complete bag twice in 1 g steps. Actual readers initial; everyone keeps the raw record, date, scale and state. Handling evidence names only observed children.

Leaks, unstable readings or remaining ice stay recorded. Retry once safely within the slot. If unsuccessful, set aside the attempt and use N8 separately. Print and say invented for practice. Supplied readings never establish practical skill. Keep a surprising stable result; do not force equal numbers. The graph will test retained matter through changing form using standard grams.

#### 15 min. Graph components and prepare cooling

First five minutes: repair error F by tracing a returned day 8 bar to its recorded endpoint. Ready children trace their own record. Next seven: [[s01-P9-2]], using one complete record with no rival contents-only values. Put bars on the same zero-based gram axis; label stages and endpoints. If repeats disagree, mark both values and label instability rather than silently choose one.

Review rotating six heard from grade 4: six 45-second evidence turns fit 4.5 minutes while others graph. Record names; everyone may pass. Everyone hands in paper for descriptive feedback, 30 times 10 seconds after class, five minutes. Return day 10.

Last three minutes, set up day 10: teacher weighs the six labeled water bags, 30 seconds each, recording two before readings on the dated card. Keep bags and card together. Freeze after class; teacher alone places and removes bags. Failed preparation uses N9 tomorrow.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s01-X9]] alone. This held case first appears here. After class: 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes marking. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen on the formative record continuation; add error letters. Return day 10.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Six captains dry stations in parallel. Collect bags without opening them; wipe slippery spills immediately. No tasting. Store cooling cards for day 10.

### Other rooms

No scale, ice or safe bag: N8, explicitly invented, replaces measurement. No freezer: reserve N9 for tomorrow. No projector: share raw readings on paper. No printer: copy stems exactly and use graph paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No scale or melting materials: use N8, invented for practice. No freezer: use N9 tomorrow. No printer: copy the questions, keeping the exit hidden.

**Skip.** Group queues and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 8 feedback and keep every inventory object.
- **12 min. Measure.** Keep a small ice bag sealed and dry. Complete [[s01-P9-1]], or use labeled N8.
  **Say.** What belongs in both readings? Expect the same bag and all its contents.
- **10 min. Graph.** Complete [[s01-P9-2]]. Point from each bar to its raw reading.
- **3 min. Prepare.** Weigh and label a partly filled sealed water bag. You freeze it afterward, leaving expansion space.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X9]] for independent work. Discuss afterward using the teacher answer key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports; keep raw readings, graph, cooling setup and exit. Longer: describe the scale's unresolved smaller changes.

### Words and supports

Review: melt, solid, liquid, whole grams, boundary, graph endpoints. Enlarge axes; allow a scribe without science hints. A child may direct handling.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 returns s01-X9 and both practice papers in Return. Five minutes repair D/E using familiar apparatus or melting evidence; five repair F. Then weigh the same prepared bags after freezing.
