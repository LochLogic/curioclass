# Day 11: Keep the salt apparatus together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep d011-key.html teacher-only. Check allergies before salt handling. Preportion six trays; each holds cup, 50 g water, salt in its holder, and spoon. Provide goggles, rinsing water, eyewash and four supervised washing points; otherwise choose adult wet handling. Without safe eye protection or rinsing provision, use N10. Prepare towels and labeled goggle sanitizer. Print the exact exit with graph space. Retrieve day 6 residue evidence and day 10 papers.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 10's s01-X10, s01-P10-1 and s01-P10-2. First five minutes: D/E alternatives use familiar complete-bag inventory or freezing readings. Teacher leads the largest gap; others locate matching evidence in parallel. Next five: F endpoint tracing, raw value beside plotted height. Ready children explain the whole-gram limit. Store corrected papers in folders.

#### 20 min. Mix, measure and wash

Review day 5's dissolve: material spreads through water in parts too small to see. Review day 8's boundary and zero/check routine. Use [[s01-P11-1]]. Goggles stay on for everyone handling or nearby, from setup through cleanup. No tasting; report spills immediately.

Six one-minute before and six after visits take twelve minutes. At each visit zero empty, verify the unchanged check object, and read twice at the 1 g step. Initially weigh tray, cup, water, unmixed salt, holder and spoon together. Stir during the two-minute mixing interval; retain every object afterward. Dry exteriors before weighing; actual readers initial. Everyone records date, scale, inventory and both readings.

Six minutes allow eight 45-second washing waves at four supervised points, with dry recording between turns. Without those points, teacher alone handles wet materials; children use shared authentic readings, handling unobserved. Keep goggles on nearby; handlers wash hands.

Spills, visible undissolved salt or unstable readings stay recorded. Retry once safely within the slot; then use N10 separately, printed and said aloud as invented for practice. Never erase a mismatch or claim invented measurements as observed. Equal whole-gram readings mean no detected change at that step.

#### 7 min. Graph and caption

Complete [[s01-P11-2]] using one complete salt record, no rival contents-only record. Review day 9's zero-based gram axes, common intervals, stage labels and endpoints. Mark both repeated values if unstable. Review day 6's recovered salt orally: residue supports retaining unseen dissolved salt. Link that familiar evidence to today's measured quantities; appearance alone cannot establish loss.

Pairs each take 45 seconds concurrently to point to a reading. Everyone writes a dated caption. Teacher circulates without claiming thirty individual oral checks. After class allow 30 times 10 seconds, five minutes descriptive feedback. Return papers day 12.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Cover practice records. Reveal [[s01-X11]] alone, first sight of both records. After class allow 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes marking. On the formative record continuation use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen, plus error letters. Return day 12.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Teacher collects liquids. Six captains wipe trays and sanitize five goggles each, following labeled contact time. Teacher finishes after class if necessary; no reuse until dry. Keep goggles on through nearby wet cleanup; wash hands after handling.

### Other rooms

Missing safe kit: N10, explicitly invented, replaces wet work. Missing washing points: adult handles liquids and salt. No projector: share records on paper. No printer: copy exact stems; hide the exit.

### At home

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce pair discussion, preserving complete readings, caption, washing and exit. Longer: explain what unchanged whole-gram readings leave unresolved.

### Words and supports

Review: dissolve, boundary, total, whole grams, residue and graph endpoints. Allow enlarged records, directed handling or dictated captions without science hints.
