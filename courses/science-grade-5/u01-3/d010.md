# Day 10: Weigh the same bag after freezing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep d010-key.html teacher-only. Match each frozen bag to its dated day 9 card and original scale. Check seams; retain preparation failures. Teacher retrieves bags shortly before their visits. Prepare dry towels and N9. Use the student page's open graph space, avoiding the shared compressed graph. Copy the exact exit onto a blank half sheet with graph room and a judgment line. Keep every exit covered.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 9's s01-X9, s01-P9-1 and s01-P9-2. First five minutes: teacher leads the largest D/E gap with familiar complete-bag evidence. D: keep the same bag. E: compare changed appearance with the actual complete readings, without forcing equality. Other children locate apparatus or readings in parallel. Next five minutes repair F: trace a returned bar's endpoint to its value. Ready children identify actual versus supplied evidence. Store corrected papers in folders.

#### 15 min. Cooling measurements

Review grade 2's freeze, solid, liquid, cooler; retrieve day 9's bag preparation. Use [[s01-P10-1]]. Six two-minute visits take twelve minutes; three minutes cover transitions. Keep each bag sealed, dry its exterior, and use yesterday's scale. Review day 8's zero/check routine. Zero empty, verify the unchanged object, then read the complete frozen bag twice at the 1 g step. Actual readers initial; everyone copies dated original and current readings.

If freezing failed, seams leak or readings will not settle, retain the status and values. Retry once safely within the visit. Then use N9 on a separate record, printed and said aloud as invented for practice. Unobserved handling stays blank with its reason. Do not substitute a prediction for a measurement.

#### 13 min. Component comparison

Complete [[s01-P10-2]] with one complete record. Review day 9's paired bars on one zero-based gram axis; use stage labels and endpoints matching raw values. Annotate the location of one reading. If repeats disagree, mark both values and label instability. No split-apparatus sums or missing totals appear in practice.

Compare this familiar bag with yesterday's melting evidence orally. Review rotating six heard from grade 4: hear a different six, 45 seconds each, within five minutes. Record names; everyone may pass. All others annotate while listening. In simultaneous pairs each partner gets 45 seconds to point to evidence.

Use standard grams to compare amount through changing form. Equal whole-gram readings support no detected change at that step; they cannot rule out a smaller difference. Keep unexpected results and check apparatus, dryness and scale. After class, descriptive feedback takes 30 times 10 seconds, five minutes. Return both papers day 11.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s01-X10]] alone, first sight of its separated after-readings. After class allow 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes marking. Use the formative record continuation's single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Add error letters; return day 11.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Six captains towel stations dry in parallel; teacher stores sealed bags. Never puncture them. No tasting; wipe slippery spills immediately.

### Other rooms

No prepared frozen bag, freezer or working scale: N9, explicitly invented, replaces measurements. Never claim children observed freezing. No projector: show dated cards. No printer: copy exact stems and use graph paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No frozen bag or scale: use N9, labeled invented for practice. No printer: copy questions and hide the exit. Only you use the freezer.

**Skip.** Group queues and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 9 feedback; trace a graph endpoint.
- **10 min. Weigh.** Dry the sealed bag. Keep its original card and record [[s01-P10-1]].
  **Say.** What must match yesterday's comparison? Expect the same complete bag and scale.
- **12 min. Graph.** Complete [[s01-P10-2]]. Point from one endpoint to its raw record. Keep unexpected readings.
- **3 min. Store.** Keep bags sealed, wipe spills and retain records.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X10]] for independent work. Discuss afterward with the teacher answer key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports, preserving measurements, evidence annotation and exit. Longer: explain why a changed shape alone cannot establish lost matter.

### Words and supports

Review: freeze, solid, liquid, cooler; whole grams, complete boundary and graph endpoints. Enlarge the record; permit dictated annotations without reasoning hints.
