# Section 1.3: weigh the whole system

## Purpose and continuity

Days 8 to 11 connect measuring and graphing with retained matter. Supply consensus model 1 directly: air and dissolved material persist when unseen. Earlier section 1.2 days are planned, so explain if unavailable. Review system, parts that work together, from grade 1. Here the weighing boundary includes every object on the scale. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing, from grade 3. Review zero-based bar graphs from grade 2 and scaled bars from grade 3. Teach gram axes and whole-gram steps explicitly on day 8. No conversions.

## What the evidence means

Include apparatus and initially separate ingredients. Compare the same complete boundary on the same scale. A 1 g step cannot resolve every smaller change. Equal readings mean no detected change at that step. Keep surprising readings; check zero, dry exteriors and unchanged apparatus. Retry once safely, then retain the attempt beside the separately labeled fallback.

Day 8 uses a dry cup, card pieces and bag: inventory and order actions, then draw single component bars. Day 9 weighs sealed bags during room-temperature melting. Teacher records cooling bags before freezing after class. Day 10 weighs those same bags frozen. Day 11 retains tray, cup, salt, water, holder and spoon through mixing. Measurement and graphing join the retained-matter idea with standard gram quantities.

## Evidence and repairs

Every student returns paper. Six one-minute before/after scale visits fit twelve minutes; frozen bags use six two-minute visits. Only actual readers initial readings. Group roles do not certify every child's weighing skill.

Review rotating six heard: six 45-second public turns fit five minutes. Record names. Everyone else submits an annotation and gets feedback next day. Anyone may pass. Partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously.

Use record.md's single formative scale. Day 8 returns the earlier model ticket in two five-minute routes: air/apparatus first, scale/residue second. Day 9 returns boundary work in five minutes. Days 10 and 11 have ten-minute returns: apparatus/temperature for five, endpoints for five. Alternative routes share time. Day 12 receives day 11 papers.

A: gas treated as nothing. B: dots treated as grains. C: clear means empty. D: omitted apparatus. E: temperature creates or removes matter. F: bar height disagrees with its number. Repair through familiar evidence, never the upcoming held case. Language support does not change the scientific demand.
