# Day 8: Keep everything in the comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep d008-key.html teacher-only. Cut six sets of inventory strips. Check the whole-gram scale on a level surface; retain an unchanged dry check object's reading. Prepare separate zero-based axes for single bars, large enough for whole-gram endpoints. Print the exit directly from its data stem, leaving drawing and explanation space. Do not use a prefilled practice graph. Gather day 7's consensus model and papers.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 7's s01-X7 and s01-P7-1. Review consensus model 1: unseen air and dissolved material remain. First five minutes: repair A/I with familiar ball/syringe evidence, or D with an object inventory. Second five: repair B with the grain/unseen-part inset, or C with day 6's residue record. Teacher leads the largest gap; others annotate familiar evidence in parallel. Ready children locate evidence on the existing model. No held exit case returns as practice.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review grade 1's system: parts work together. Here a complete weighing system includes contents and apparatus. Teach boundary: everything included; total: all included weight. Review day 1's gram, zero and repeated readings. Teach 1 g steps; equal readings mean no detected change, not exact equality.

Move dry pieces from cup to bag; retain everything. Review grade 3's fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Keep apparatus and scale fixed. Review inventory, a list of observed things, from grade 2; list these objects.

Review grade 2's bars starting at the bottom line. Teach gram axes from zero, equal intervals, stage labels before/after. Model [[s01-K8-1]], announced invented for practice. Reveal [[steps:s01-K8-1]] after children locate the value. Error F: a label cannot replace the correct endpoint. Two stage bars would share one axis; today practice keeps bars separate.

#### 21 min. Plan and component practice

Use [[s01-P8-1]] and physical strips. Six two-minute scale visits take twelve minutes; nine remain for zero checks and discussion. Zero the empty scale; verify the check object. Weigh the inventory twice, transfer pieces, retain cup, then reweigh twice. Read whole grams. Retain instability; retry once, then use N7's invented inventory drawing without claiming measurement.

Waiting children complete [[s01-P8-2]] on separate axes. Check six group plans during visits; each child submits their own inventory and order. Do not draw paired weighing plans. Practice single-bar endpoints, never a held exit's case or response. Give descriptive feedback after class: 30 papers times 10 seconds, five minutes; return day 9.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s01-X8]] alone, first sight. After class: 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes marking. On the formative record continuation use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen, plus error letters. Return day 9; keep practical evidence blank when unobserved.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Return dry cups, pieces and bags; wipe the scale. Keep model evidence folders.

### Other rooms

Missing scale or kit: print and say N7 is invented for practice. Order strips without a weighing claim. No projector: draw the single-bar example. No printer: copy stems exactly; hide the exit.

### At home

**Swap.** No scale or cups: use N7, explicitly invented for practice. No printer: copy the questions, hiding the exit.

**Skip.** Group queues and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 7 feedback beside its familiar records.
- **8 min. Inventory.** Move dry pieces from cup to bag, keeping everything. Do [[s01-P8-1]].
  **Say.** What stays in the comparison? Expect cup, pieces and bag at both stages.
- **10 min. Bars.** Work [[s01-K8-1]], then [[s01-P8-2]] on separate axes. Use zero, grams and equal intervals.
- **4 min. Store.** Keep the inventory for tomorrow. Return dry materials.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X8]]. Let your child work alone; discuss afterward with the teacher answer key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce discussion, preserving visits, component practice and exit. Longer: explain why whole grams leave smaller differences unresolved.

### Words and supports

Review: system, inventory, fair test, gram, bar graph. New: boundary, total, 1 g steps. Enlarge axes; allow dictated reasoning without science hints.

Day 9 returns s01-X8 and both practice papers in Return. Repair D using retained cup and bag; graph endpoints receive their own five-minute route. Then measure a sealed ice bag as it melts.
