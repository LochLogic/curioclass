# Day 89: Water moves land material

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 88's s05-X88-1, s05-X88-2, s05-X88-3 and s05-P88/s05-P88-compare. Separate exit-key.md; cover today's ticket and Exit box. Check sand/glove allergies through confidential adult records. Portion damp sand, stabilize six tray stations and check eyewash. Lay out protection, towels, soap, waste container and approved goggle-cleaning supplies. Fewer than six wash points means adult-only wet handling. Missing protection means dry T89. Keep teacher consensus drawings for day 91. On the covered ticket, add starting arrows: land to water; water to land.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
If many made error A in day 88's exit, review one action on a retained strip for five minutes. Otherwise support that group while others locate evidence. Next five minutes: error C, use day 85's supplied air-container reminder, not a new experiment. Unseen air occupies space; observable effects support its action. Return all papers; preserve originals. Over ten gaps makes the corresponding route whole-class. Review return and repair and the single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 6 min. Safety and plan
Review [[s05-K89]], then reveal [[steps:s05-K89]]. Review weathering: breaking rock material down; erosion carries loosened material. Review runoff, rain that runs over the ground; watershed, land draining to a common outlet. Review day 88: little dissolved salt does not establish drinking safety. Everyone near wet materials wears goggles and nonlatex gloves through cleanup. No blowing or tasting. Stop unsafe handling; contain pours and wipe spills. Dry observers have equal recording roles.

#### 15 min. Observe and record
Six groups work simultaneously. Five two-minute roles: shape a damp slope; pour gently; locate grain patches; compare positions; check entries. All observe throughout and complete their own tray record, [[s05-P89]]. **Ask.** [[s05-P89]] Expect visible locations and direction, with uncertainty retained. Read locations from the tray itself; infer no speed or mass. One gentle retry fits the check role. Still unclear: keep the entry and use T89 separately, said aloud as invented for practice. Last five minutes: tag evidence and compare raw positions. Teacher retains one water-to-land action arrow supported by today's record, separate from raw sketches. No berm, notch or reciprocal-arrow revision is practiced.

#### 7 min. Cleanup
Two minutes: collect sediment into solid waste and wipe trays. Five: remove gloves and wash with soap. Six parallel wash points allow five 45-second users each, plus transition. With fewer, children stay dry; adult cleanup replaces handling. Teacher alone disposes of sand, never down sinks, and cleans goggles after class for the cleaning agent's required contact time.

#### 7 min. Paper exit
Reveal [[s05-X89-2]] on the half ticket and [[s05-X89-1]] in the covered Exit box. Say invented for practice. Children work alone. Collect both papers. After class: thirty children times forty seconds, twenty marking minutes; inspect thirty tray records for twenty seconds each, ten more minutes. Use the single formative scale. Error A lacks linked actions. Assign error B only when a child actually calls transport weathering. Return all evidence on day 90.
