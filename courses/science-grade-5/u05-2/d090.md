# Day 90: Maps and source accounts

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 89 papers s05-X89-1 and s05-X89-2, plus s05-P89. Lay out the unit 5 Pine Basin map and watershed records, tracing paper and evidence notes. Separate teacher-only exit-key.md. Cover held-map-90.svg, the half ticket and student-page Exit box. Keep the municipal source account separate from invented Pine Basin. No local extract or visit is needed.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
If many made error A in day 89's exit, spend five minutes connecting one action to one observation on the returned paper. Otherwise support that group while others check their evidence tags. Next five minutes: error B, review stationary breaking versus carried loose grains using yesterday's separate cases. Over ten gaps makes that route whole-class. Return s05-P89 without extra conferences. Preserve original observations. Review return and repair and the single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Reopen map/source evidence
Review runoff, rain that runs over the ground; key, a box that tells what each mark means; watershed, land draining to a common outlet. Review day 88: low salt alone does not establish drinking safety. Use isolated [[s05-K90]], then [[steps:s05-K90]]. Say invented for practice. Review the tray's downhill direction without copying its exit. Read the dated municipal account's source statement separately. Its April 8, 2026 account identifies Cedar and Tolt watersheds; it establishes no present conditions. Pine Basin's separate storage-pond note is invented. Do not connect these two places.

#### 15 min. Component evidence work
Pairs use [[s05-P90]]. **Ask.** [[s05-P90]] Expect key matches and the claim's source label. Five minutes: match the three separate map symbols using tracing paper. Five: locate the dated account and distinguish it from the invented note. Five: each child retains individual key matches and one source locator. No connected height route or complete map/source-limits response is rehearsed. If marks are unclear, redraw the same isolated symbols larger. If a locator is missing, point to the dated heading before students choose. No household water questions.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six children each get forty-five seconds to show one symbol match or source locator; passing is allowed. Collect all thirty individual records in the remaining half minute. Record only those actually heard. Other children have practiced with partners, not received an oral judgment.

#### 7 min. Paper exit
Uncover [[s05-X90-1]] and held-map-90.svg on the half ticket; reveal [[s05-X90-2]] in the separate Exit box. Read invented labels aloud. Children answer alone on paper. Collect both pieces. After class, thirty children times forty seconds means twenty marking minutes. Inspect thirty practice records for twenty seconds each, ten further minutes. Use the single scale. Error A gives disconnected labels; error G claims safety without testing evidence. Return all on day 91.

### Other rooms
No tracing paper: copy isolated symbols on plain paper. No printer: draw components on the board and keep exit records covered on separate paper. Enlarged copies and neutral reading preserve choices.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies or tracing paper: draw Pine Basin's separate symbols and copy the dated source note.

**Skip.** Six public reports and class collection.

- **5 min. Return.** Revisit day 89's feedback and raw record.
- **8 min. Reopen.** Use [[s05-K90]] and its steps; read both source notes separately.
- **15 min. Components.** Child completes [[s05-P90]].
  **Say.** "Which record supports the supply claim?" Expect the source note, with its real date or invented label.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s05-X90-1]] with its map and [[s05-X90-2]] in the separate Exit box. Child answers alone. Keep both papers.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: trim public sharing, retaining components and independent responses. Longer: discuss a question the map cannot answer.

### Words and supports
Review source labels and evidence tags. Point to the key while reading; do not trace the held route for children.

### Tomorrow
Day 91 returns both exits and s05-P90. Its ten-minute opening treats error A, disconnected route labels, and error G, fresh assumed safe, five minutes each. Then orally rehearse existing displays; the teacher records consensus.

### Credit
Ours. Background: Sources S05-watershed-alt, S05-supply and S05-safe. Pine Basin and exit maps are invented for practice.
