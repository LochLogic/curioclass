# Day 113: Explain one evidence component

## Teacher page

### Before class
Separate teacher-keys.md and today's held-exits.md; use current-day data answer keys. Keep held-graph-113.svg covered until independent evidence. Retrieve day 112's tickets, component pages and listening roster, day 100's anchor/question board, day 107's serial slips or separate T107, and day 110's V baseline/retests. Display design-cycle components and existing source locators. Do not print duplicate component-note responses.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return day 112's s06-X112, source, limit and both fresh repair pages privately. Review return and repair. Honor its Tomorrow: first five minutes error A, [[s06-P113-repair-A]]; next five error D, [[s06-P113-repair-D]]. More than ten misses selects that whole-class round; otherwise assign components privately. Oral corrections and brief marks only; preserve first responses without written revision.

#### 8 min. Evidence retrieval
Review source and two-column notes, source statement and source location. Return to day 100's question board and original parking-lot anchor, labeled invented for practice. Teacher points separately to existing consensus arrows, source locators and test conditions. Review system-model, cause and effect, data-graph and design-cycle: define, sketch, build, test, compare. No new explanation or sample report is composed.

#### 17 min. Partner fluency
Fifteen simultaneous pairs work through four rounds. Each partner has 45 seconds per round: twelve minutes of talk, five to change evidence. Round one uses [[s06-P113-point]]. Round two uses [[s06-P113-repeat]], preserving every T107/V trial. Round three points to one fixed condition in the earlier plan. Round four points to one recorded failure. Review fair test, trial, criterion, constraint and failure in existing records. Keep rounds separate; no complete exit-shaped explanation or written drafting. Teacher records who was actually heard, not all partner turns.

**Ask.** Which trial could challenge that claim? Expect an identified unfavorable or unresolved trial, with its note retained. If none challenges that particular claim, accept this after the full record is inspected.

#### 5 min. Consensus hearing
Continue day 112's rotating-six roster. Six voluntary 45-second component turns take 4.5 minutes; all thirty submit component locators in 30 seconds. Teacher points to the existing shared model and limits, without dictating a report. Passes remain unheard; carry the roster forward.

#### 5 min. Fresh paper evidence
Cover practice and reveal [[s06-X113]] with held-graph-113.svg, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection minute. This first response is not a drafted/revised report. Half ticket only, with source-linked arrows and short notes. After class, thirty at 40 seconds take twenty minutes; component pages at ten seconds take five. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only. Error B lists disconnected parts; error C reports a future result as observed.

### Other rooms
No actual records: use T107 and V110, labeled invented and kept separate. No devices: paper graph. No printer: copy the graph behind a cover and reveal it with the exact exit stem.

### At home
**Swap.** No partner: adult points and listens. No actual records: use labeled T107/V110 separately.
**Skip.** Public hearing and prose revision.
- **8 min. Repair.** Return day 112 work. Complete [[s06-P113-repair-A]] and [[s06-P113-repair-D]].
- **7 min. Retrieve.** Point separately to existing source locations and test conditions.
- **15 min. Components.** Use [[s06-P113-point]] and [[s06-P113-repeat]], then one control and failure.
  **Say.** "Which trial could challenge that claim?" Expect an identified unfavorable or unresolved repeat, retained with its note.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Reveal the held graph and [[s06-X113]]; child works alone. Return all pages on day 114.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce evidence-switching time, keeping every component and independent evidence. Longer: locate another observation that could challenge one component claim.

### Words and supports
Review projection, an expected outcome still needing observations; source, trial, fair test, failure, criterion and constraint. Enlarge records and read neutrally. Accept pointing during component talk; independent arrows remain the child's decisions.

### Tomorrow
Day 114 returns E113 and component pages. Error B gets five interaction-arrow minutes; error C gets five observation/projection minutes. Carry the listening roster into plan-component reports and the monthly shadow observation.
