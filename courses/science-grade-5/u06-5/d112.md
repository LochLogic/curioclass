No complete combined claim is modeled or rehearsed.

**Ask.** Which source supports this connection? Expect the relevant W line and section, or the explicitly invented W112 log location. Use the data answer key for component acceptance.

Review rotating six heard. Continue day 111's listening roster: six voluntary component reports at 45 seconds use 4.5 minutes; thirty pages are collected in 30 seconds. A pass remains unheard. Others submit source/data notes. No public complete report.

Cover practice. Reveal [[s06-X112]] only now. Read its exact stem and invented label, then allow four independent minutes and one collection minute. Use a half ticket.
