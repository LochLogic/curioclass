#### 15 min. Audit components

#### 5 min. Monthly shadow
Review day 100's fixed-stick length/direction routine and monthly shadow record. One setup minute, two simultaneous observation minutes with six representatives, one recording minute including a 30-second report, one return/cleanup minute. Supervise walking; never look at the sun. Read tip-to-base length within 1 cm ruler steps, using the direction card. All thirty record date, shared time, length/unit and direction. Fuzzy or missing tip stays unresolved with reason. S114, 41 cm northward, is a separate record printed/said invented for practice. One reading cannot establish a seasonal pattern.

#### 5 min. Share and handoff
Continue day 113's roster: six voluntary 45-second control reports, then collect thirty component pages in 30 seconds. Passes remain unheard. Days 115/116 collect independent evidence; reveal no cases. Shadow logs stay in folders, descriptively noted without an exit scale.
