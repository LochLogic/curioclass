# Day 61: Repair components and try afresh

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current teacher-only answer keys. Sort day 58, day 59 and day 60 originals privately. Assign four component moves at most and one fresh retry matching each largest remaining gap. Prioritize day 58's A/H within these rounds. Cut only assigned repair-components excerpts; hold retry and next-readiness unseen. Use the compact next-readiness-pictures adaptation below; insert assigned records and one retry on side 1. Keep points, formative evidence and coverage on separate records.

### The day

#### 5 min. Private feedback

Return day 59's assessment for two minutes and day 60's assessment for three; include retained day 58 tickets. Preserve original points, never rank children publicly. Review day 17's private-feedback and fresh-retry routine. Explain today's route: find your assigned letter, locate its evidence, date a correction, then try one fresh slip. Read individual next-step notes quietly.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Four five-minute rounds maximum, [[s03-P61]]. Teacher visits one parallel packet group per round: identifying A/C/D, quantities B, unseen E/F, causal G/H/I. Within each round use one assigned letter: compare two minutes, annotate two, retain the correction in one. Component-locators reveal only the relevant feature after the attempt. More than ten misses makes that letter move whole-class, replacing a round. Secure children locate a limit in that same component.

Day 58 A locates a distinguishing property; H locates a method limit, in separate five-minute moves. Use familiar component cards only, never held retry cases or complete assessment responses. Review grade 4 second-color revision, retaining original observations and initials. Extra gaps stay unresolved; do not promise every letter a repair.

#### 10 min. Fresh retry

Give one assigned unseen slip: [[s03-R61P]] for A; [[s03-R61Q]] for B/D; [[s03-R61M]] for F; [[s03-R61I]] for I; [[s03-R61C]] for C; [[s03-R61E]] for E; [[s03-R61G]] for G; [[s03-R61H]] for H. All thirty work independently and submit. No peer key. Record fresh evidence separately using review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Paper retry cannot establish missing practical coverage. After class, thirty forty-five-second readings take twenty-two-and-a-half minutes. Return private retry notes after class today; original forty-point assessment remains unchanged.

#### 10 min. Next readiness

Reveal next-readiness with next-readiness-pictures. Three minutes each: [[s03-N61a]], [[s03-N61b]], [[s03-N61c]]. Read neutrally, no teaching or shared explanations. These check older noncontact force, sky and daylight ideas through new invented records. Collect, then use one minute to sort by row. Review formative scale on a separate readiness record, no unit 3 letters or total. After class, thirty thirty-second readings need fifteen minutes. Return private notes today; send three row results to day 63's Noncontact support, Sky support and Daylight support, ten minutes each. No added repair on day 62.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy assigned card, retry and readiness exactly, revealing each only in its part. Copy diagrams with arrows and full-day strips. No apparatus or digital tool.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy assigned cards and drawings without a printer. Keep keys separate until independent work ends.

**Skip.** Parallel group movement.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 58/59/60 originals privately; explain one next step.
- **15 min. Repair.** Use up to three assigned component cards with [[s03-P61]]. Preserve originals.
  **Say.** "Which feature supports your correction?" Expect the assigned component from component-locators.
- **10 min. Retry.** Give one assigned fresh slip alone. Use its teacher key afterward for descriptive feedback.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Give [[s03-N61a]], [[s03-N61b]] and [[s03-N61c]] neutrally with pictures. Keep separate row notes for day 63.

### Shorter or longer

Retain feedback, retry and readiness. Fewer component rounds leave explicit unresolved gaps. Longer: discuss a limit after collection, without changing original points.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, property, control and unit. Neutral reading, enlarged diagrams and dictation preserve the child's decisions; no scientific hints on retry/readiness.

### Tomorrow

Day 62 starts a model of down using falling objects. Day 63 receives readiness gaps in three named supports. Unit 3 points stay fixed; unresolved reasoning and practical gaps remain on separate notes.

### Credit

Ours. Fresh records and pictures are invented for practice.
