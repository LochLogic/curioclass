# Day 59: Independent material evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current teacher-only answer keys; cover inventory side and all completed models. Prepare six coded safe trays with teacher identities recorded privately. Check food/mineral allergies, sample edges and scale function. Portion powder to give readable whole grams. Stage empty tare cups, ruler and magnet beside scale. Prepare practical-action-checklist and separate coverage-record. Keep sinks visible from station. Set out soap, towels and wipes.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles through splash cleanup. No tasting, sniffing, dust, scratching, heating or live conduction. Sealed liquids stay closed. Teacher collects solids. Without safe protection/washing, supply no-kit property records and mark practical components unobserved.

### The day

#### 28 min. Observation window

Honor day 58's handoff: no feedback from its exit. Give one minute neutral directions for [[s03-E1]]. Six groups of five visit four minutes each. Five forty-five-second personal turns plus fifteen reset seconds fit each visit. Each child tares an empty cup, weighs the powder portion, reads mineral length and observes magnet response; teacher initials each actual action. Waiting peers prepare labeled record fields independently, without answers or discussion. Observe sealed liquid visually. No supplied number earns personal credit.

Retain failed readings in actual-fallback-log; retry or enlarge a safe portion within the personal slot. If unresolved, leave that component blank and mark unobserved separately. Earlier groups wash during later visits: five forty-second washes at two sinks fit two minutes. Stagger one group per slot; final group washes for two minutes after visits. Collect practical records in one minute. Total: one plus twenty-four plus two plus one.

#### 17 min. Independent paper

Uncover inventory side, [[s03-E2P]], [[s03-E2M]], [[s03-E2R]], [[s03-E2L]]. Say the records are invented for practice. Children work alone; neutrally read or scribe without hints. Collect without discussion. No exit ticket. After class, reserve thirty-five marking minutes: Q1 four points, Q2 four per material, twenty possible today. Use the points-only assessment-record; retain missing practical coverage separately. Return day 59 and day 60 originals together on day 61. Day 58 evidence stays retained, without new points.

### Other rooms

No kit: reasoning remains assessable; practical actions remain unobserved on coverage-record. No printer: copy exact prompts, concealing inventory until its part. No projected keys.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe kit, use supplied records and leave practical evidence unobserved. Copy prompts without a printer.

**Skip.** Group queue.

- **40 min. Assessment.** First 20 minutes: give [[s03-E1]] neutrally with a safe coded tray. Watch personal weighing, length and magnet actions; wash afterward. Next 20 minutes: give [[s03-E2P]], [[s03-E2M]], [[s03-E2R]] and [[s03-E2L]] alone. Collect for day 61 feedback. Keep keys closed until collection; withhold coaching between assessment days.
  **Say.** "Record your own observations." Expect: an independent labeled record, with failed readings retained. Give no scientific hints.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve personal turns and independent time. Missing acts stay unobserved; additional time supports access without scientific coaching.

### Words and supports

Use previously taught vocabulary without reteaching. Neutral read-aloud, enlargement and dictation preserve decisions; proxy actions do not earn practical credit.

### Tomorrow

Day 60 assesses controlled collaboration and new-substance reasoning. No day 59 feedback then. Return errors A/B/C/D and practical coverage notes privately on day 61; do not assume a wrong reason from a blank.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Source S03-PE3, Source S03-mineral, Source S03-dissolving and Source S03-copper. Inventory records are invented for practice.
