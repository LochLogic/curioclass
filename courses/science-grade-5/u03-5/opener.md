## Running the section

Each assessment day: one minute directions, six four-minute group visits, two final-wash minutes and one collection minute, then seventeen uninterrupted paper minutes. On 59, five 45-second personal scale slots plus fifteen seconds reset fit each group visit; length and magnet actions happen while peers wait. On 60, stage three paired cup sets and stagger starts; three children mix, two have named control-measuring/recording actions, all record individually. On 61 preserve five feedback minutes, four five-minute repairs, one ten-minute assigned retry and ten minutes next readiness.

Every student may pass in public talk. Hear rotating six and mark who; individual paper reaches everyone. Explain conventions directly wherever earlier teaching was only planned. Allow neutral read-aloud or a scribe. Keep 15-word student sentences and 350 total printed student words per day.

## Safety and fallback

Teacher stages smooth checked samples and portions. Goggles, trays, two sinks, soap/towels and approved wipes ready. Earlier groups wash during later visits, one group per two-minute slot; last group washes before paper. Retry failed readings within the personal slot or mark unobserved. Never credit an invented measurement as firsthand. No heating, live conduction, cleaners, tasting or child wire handling. Ask food/mineral allergies privately; use paper when safe handling is unavailable.

## Common errors and response

Letters A to I apply only when the stated claim matches. An unexplained answer gets a follow-up, not an assumed letter. Use the scheduled five-minute component move: compare two minutes, annotate two, submit one. More than ten misses replaces that group move with whole-class repair. Never add time or preview a held full response.
