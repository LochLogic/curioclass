# Day 60: Independent change evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current teacher-only answer keys. Cover follow-up side and completed models. Check food allergies privately. Prepare six safe trays: three matched open cup pairs, each with 15 mL water, three 1 g salt portions. Use 1 mL marked measure and verified scale during preparation. Keep water temperatures equal. Stage practical-action-checklist, separate coverage-record and washing supplies; sinks stay visible.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles through cleanup. Mix gently; no tasting, sniffing, cleaners, heating or sealed mixtures. Teacher collects waste. Without protection, allergy clearance or washing, supply records; conducting stays unobserved.

### The day

#### 28 min. Observation window

Honor day 59's handoff: day 59's assessment stays sealed until day 61. No feedback or day 58 repair. One minute neutral directions, [[s03-E3]]. Before visiting, each child independently plans variable, fixed conditions and three-trial reason. Six groups visit four minutes each; peers wait independently without discussing answers. Use practical-action-checklist for named actions.

Three children add salt and stir one treatment each. Fourth child stirs controls; fifth checks control water measures and records them. Start pairs thirty seconds into the visit, then at ninety and one hundred fifty seconds. Stir each cup twenty seconds; observe thirty seconds after stirring ends. All independently record both starting/resulting cups for all three pairs. Teacher initials actual acts; group success cannot substitute. Preserve failed readings or changed quantities in actual-fallback-log; one within-slot retry, otherwise retain uncertainty. Supplied fallback is labeled invented and never earns conducting credit.

Earlier groups wash during later visits, one group per two-minute slot. Five forty-second washes at two sinks fit two minutes. Final group uses two minutes after visits; teacher collects in one. One plus twenty-four plus two plus one equals twenty-eight.

#### 17 min. Independent paper

Reveal [[s03-E4a]], [[s03-E4b]], [[s03-E4c]] and [[s03-E4d]] on follow-up side. Announce invented records. No demonstrations of these solutions. Work alone; read neutrally, with no hints. Collect originals without discussion. No exit. After class, reserve forty marking minutes: Q3 four points, Q4 sixteen, twenty today and forty overall. Use assessment-record points only; keep unobserved practical components blank with separate coverage-record notes. Preserve denominator and original points. Return on day 61; thirty-five marking minutes from day 59 plus forty today gives seventy-five total.

### Other rooms

No kit: paper reasoning remains assessable, conducting unobserved. For fallback, invent no numbers: record salt grains initially and clear water afterward as an explicitly invented qualitative record. No printer: copy exact prompts with follow-up concealed.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe kit, use an invented qualitative record: salt grains initially, clear water afterward. Leave conducting unobserved. Copy prompts without a printer.

**Skip.** Group queue.

- **40 min. Assessment.** First 23 minutes: give [[s03-E3]] without hints. Child plans, performs safe paired comparisons with adult help moving cups, and independently records. Note personal acts; wash afterward. Next 17 minutes: give [[s03-E4a]], [[s03-E4b]], [[s03-E4c]] and [[s03-E4d]] alone. Collect; hold answers for day 61 feedback.
  **Say.** "Keep your own record." Expect: an independent record of the observed trial results. Do not explain the science.

### Shorter or longer

Keep trials and independent paper. If a turn fails, record the coverage gap. Allow established access time without hints.

### Words and supports

Use taught vocabulary without reteaching. Enlarge records, read neutrally or scribe student decisions. Physical assistance leaves proxy components unobserved.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 returns day 59, day 60 and day 58 work privately. Route matching errors A to I through four component rounds, then one fresh retry. Next readiness ends that day; unresolved practical gaps stay recorded.
