# Teacher practical-action checklist

Teacher only. Record actual actions, never supplied numbers.

Day 59: six groups of five, four minutes each.
Each group has five 45-second personal scale turns and fifteen reset seconds.
Length and magnet work proceed beside the scale while peers wait.
Initial each personally observed action. Retain actual failed readings.

Day 60: stage three paired trials, each cup with 15 mL water.
Treatments receive 1 g salt. Fix starting temperature, twenty-second stirring and observation interval.
Observe thirty seconds after stirring. Stagger starts within each four-minute group visit.
Three children mix treatment pairs; two perform named control-measuring or recording actions.
All independently plan before their visit and record starting/resulting properties.

One minute directions plus 24 visits plus two final washing plus one collection equals 28.
Earlier groups wash during later visits, one group per two-minute slot at two sinks.
Then allow seventeen uninterrupted paper minutes, with no feedback or coaching.
