# Day 50: Trace warmth through a sample

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only answer keys. Keep exit-50 covered and exits-48-51 private. Set out temperature-records, property-table, actual-fallback-log and record. Pretest the contact thermometer and spoons. Mark equal immersion depths and equal handle reading locations. If dimensions cannot match or the thermometer cannot resolve handle warming, plan T50 as supplied evidence.

### Safety and set-up

Teacher alone handles comfortably warm water, spoons and thermometer on the spill tray. Never boil water. Goggles on; wipe spills immediately. Children remain dry observers and never test warmth by touching. No tasting. Use records if protection, suitable apparatus or safe preparation is unavailable. Keep teacher washing and goggle care inside cleanup.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 49's tickets. Error A: [[s03-P50a]]. Error D: [[s03-P50d]]. Each five-minute move: compare two, annotate two, submit one for immediate feedback. More than ten misses replaces that move with whole-class repair. Review day 46 hardness; scratch resistance is not length. Practice stays in folders.

#### 13 min. Warmth demonstration

Review grade 4 heat: energy transfer from a warmer object to a cooler object. Review temperature, read from the thermometer to the nearest mark. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing.

Run two five-minute trials, one spoon per trial. Reset water amount and starting temperature; match spoon dimensions, starting handle temperature, depth, reading location and room conditions. Teacher records water and starting handle readings to 1 degree Celsius, starts contact, and reads the handle after three minutes using the same contact method. A teacher clock fixes exposure, never student speed. Children enter [[s03-P50]] on temperature-records, one reading at a time.

Three minutes hold recording and one unclear-reading retry. Preserve unclear attempts in actual-fallback-log. If differences remain unresolved, report that honestly, then announce T50 as invented for practice. Its starting water is warmer than both handles. Supplied readings earn no firsthand measurement credit.

#### 8 min. Component reporting

Pairs complete [[s03-P50r]] using temperature-records, then [[s03-P50f]] separately. **Ask.** [[s03-P50r]]. Expect water to handle when water starts warmer. **Ask.** [[s03-P50f]]. Expect starting water temperature. Keep transfer direction and fixed-condition work separate; no combined endpoint-and-controls explanation. Give immediate feedback; pages stay with students.

#### 2 min. Model

Review the property-evidence table. Teacher adds labelled thermal readings with units and source, preserving unclear results. These quantities support comparisons under the recorded conditions; they do not name the material uniquely.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X50]] on exit-50, paper, alone. Read neutrally; collect before discussion. After class, thirty times forty-five seconds gives twenty-two-and-a-half marking minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record. Return on day 51.

#### 8 min. Cleanup

Teacher collects and washes wet apparatus. Dry observers file papers and wipe goggles. If anyone contacted samples or water, switch to records and stagger their washing here. No thirty-child wet activity.

### Other rooms

No suitable thermometer or matched spoons: use T50, labelled invented for practice. No printer: copy exact prompts and blank tables. Keep the exit covered.

### At home

**Swap.** Replace demonstration equipment with temperature-records T50, said aloud to be invented. Copy tables without a printer.

**Skip.** Live warm-water handling and group reporting.

- **8 min. Return.** Discuss day 49's note. Do [[s03-P50a]] and [[s03-P50d]].
- **12 min. Read.** Copy T50 readings with units using [[s03-P50]]. Label them supplied.
- **7 min. Trace.** Do [[s03-P50r]] and [[s03-P50f]] separately.
  **Say.** "Where does energy start?" Expect: warmer water, moving toward the cooler handle.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X50]] alone. Then discuss its answer key and return a note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce reports, preserving readings, source labels, exit and cleanup. Longer: locate another condition already matched in the recorded comparison.

### Words and supports

Review heat, temperature, degrees Celsius and fair test. Enlarge thermometer marks; accept dictated paper responses. Preserve every child's decision.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 returns day 50's tickets. Its five-minute Return and repair addresses error I, changing water warmth while comparing materials. Then children rehearse property components and personal scale use before independent table revision.

### Credit
