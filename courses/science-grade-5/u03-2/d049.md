# Day 49: A scratch is not a length

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only answer keys. Keep exit-49 covered and exits-48-51 private. Prepare six labeled trays of two known teaching specimens, checked for safe gentle scratching. Set out mineral-records, property-table, actual-fallback-log and record. Check the ruler's zero and whole-centimeter marks. Do not prepare an unknown-matching practice tray.

### Safety and set-up

The day before, reject sharp, dusty or chipped specimens. Goggles remain on through cleanup. Rub gently over trays; never break, blow dust or taste. Stop for chips or loose dust; adult collects waste. Without safe samples, protection or washing, use H49, explicitly invented for practice; practical actions remain unobserved.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 48's tickets. Error A: [[s03-P49a]]. Error C: [[s03-P49c]]. Each five-minute move compares two minutes, annotates two, submits one for immediate feedback. More than ten misses replaces that move with whole-class repair. Review grade 2 property and day 48 magnetic response. Keep these as separate components; no two-sample limits-and-tests response. Practice stays in folders.

#### 15 min. Mineral observations

Explain the specimen meaning directly: a mineral specimen is a solid sample studied for its properties. This differs from unit 2's nutrient meaning. Review day 46 hardness: resistance to a gentle scratch, and grade 2 gentle scratch testing. Demonstrate two minutes. Children compare the known specimens directly. No unknown/reference matching.

Six groups work simultaneously. Five two-minute turns take ten minutes: each child aligns ruler zero, reads length to 1 cm, then gently tries fingernail and teacher-checked coin on a clear surface. Review ruler zero and whole centimeter from day 20. Use the lens to distinguish a groove from a surface streak; stop rather than force a mark. Record [[s03-P49]] on mineral-records and [[s03-P49l]] on today's page. Record the scratching tool separately under Test on property-table. Three remaining minutes hold recording and one unclear-result retry.

#### 4 min. Model

Review observation versus inference and the property-evidence table. Keep length with cm and scratch response without cm. Retain original results, creator/date and tool in actual-fallback-log. If a retry remains unclear, preserve it and use H49 separately, announced as invented for practice. Do not infer mineral identity from size. Teacher gives feedback while visiting; pages stay with students.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X49]] on exit-49, paper, alone. Read neutrally; collect before discussion. After class, thirty times thirty seconds gives fifteen marking minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record. Return on day 50.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Adult collects mineral waste without raising dust. Stagger thirty forty-second washes at two sinks, ten minutes. Two minutes remain for collection and goggle care. Keep goggles on until trays are cleared.

### Other rooms

No kit: use H49 rather than unprovided photographs. No printer: copy exact prompts and blank tables. Label invented evidence; do not award handling credit.

### At home

**Swap.** Replace specimens and tools with mineral-records H49, announced as invented for practice. Copy tables without a printer.

**Skip.** Group turns and live scratching.

- **8 min. Return.** Discuss day 48's note. Do [[s03-P49a]] and [[s03-P49c]].
- **12 min. Observe.** Read H49. Do [[s03-P49]] and [[s03-P49l]], labeling both supplied.
  **Say.** "Which entry needs cm?" Expect: length; a scratch response is not a length.
- **7 min. Record.** Keep tool, scratch response and length separate on property-table. Keep source labels.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X49]] alone. Then discuss its answer key and return a note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce reporting; preserve every personal measurement, scratch turn, exit and cleanup. Longer: inspect one uncertain groove again without adding force.

### Words and supports

Mineral specimen means a solid sample here. Review hardness, property and whole centimeter. Enlarge marks; a scribe records the child's decisions. Proxy handling stays unobserved.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 returns day 49's tickets. Ten-minute Return and repair holds error A, shared appearance proves identity, and error D, hardness measured in cm. Then teacher warmth readings extend the property table.

### Credit
