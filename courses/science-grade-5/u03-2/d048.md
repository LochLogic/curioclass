# Day 48: Separate the properties

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current teacher-only answer keys; keep exit-48 covered and exits-48-51 private. Prepare six trays of checked smooth metals and sealed water/oil vials. Retain identities, creator and date. Set out property-records, property-table, actual-fallback-log and record. Test the bulb loop with a known conducting strip.

### Safety and set-up

Check oil allergies beforehand. Goggles stay on through cleanup. No tasting, sharp edges or opened vials. Teacher alone connects the low-voltage tester, always through its bulb, disconnecting promptly. Never connect a battery directly across metal or touch liquids with the tester. Without safe apparatus, protection or washing, use invented M48 records; handling remains unobserved.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 47's tickets. Error A: [[s03-P48a]]. Error B: [[s03-P48b]]. Each five-minute move: compare two, annotate two, submit one for immediate feedback. More than ten misses replaces that move with whole-class repair. Practice stays in folders. Review grade 2 property: something we can see or feel about a material; a test can show more. Review day 46 reflectivity, magnetic response and electrical conduction.

#### 7 min. Circuit demonstration

Review grade 4 circuit: a complete conducting path through a load and both battery terminals. Assemble the bulb loop. Test the known strip before each sample, then each metal; allow one retry. **Ask.** [[s03-P48c]]. Children record teacher observations. A failed checking strip leaves the result unresolved. Retain failed/unclear attempts in actual-fallback-log; use separately labelled M48 if needed, announced as invented for practice.

#### 10 min. Property stations

Six groups work simultaneously. Five one-minute turns each let every child use the magnet and inspect sealed vials and metal shine. Review grade 3 aluminum, a silver metal a magnet does not pull, beside steel, pulled. Actual samples govern. Record [[s03-P48]] on property-records and [[s03-P48s]] on today's page. Remaining five minutes hold comparison and source labels. Show supplied water-mixing observations; never open vials. No unknown-identification rehearsal.

#### 2 min. Model

Review day 45's property-evidence table and grade 2 pattern chart: different materials share a property. Add magnet, shine and circuit rows with conditions and sources. Review observation versus inference; preserve uncertainty. Teacher tags the question board, reviewing notices become questions.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s03-X48]] on exit-48, paper, alone. Read neutrally; collect before discussion. After class, thirty times forty-five seconds gives twenty-two-and-a-half marking minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record. Return on day 49. Give practice feedback during visits today.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Six staggered groups use two sinks: thirty forty-second washes take ten minutes. Two minutes remain for adult sample collection and goggle care. Keep goggles on until apparatus is cleared.

### Other rooms

No kit: M48, explicitly invented for practice. No printer: copy exact prompts and blank tables. Keep the exit unseen until collection time.

### At home

**Swap.** Replace equipment with property-records M48, said aloud to be invented. Copy prompts without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation and live circuit handling.

- **7 min. Return.** Discuss day 47's note. Do [[s03-P48a]] and [[s03-P48b]].
- **12 min. Observe.** Read M48. Do [[s03-P48c]], [[s03-P48]] and [[s03-P48s]], marking evidence supplied.
  **Say.** "Does every metal in this record pull?" Expect: no; steel pulls and copper does not.
- **8 min. Record.** Add separate property rows and their source labels to property-table.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X48]] alone. Then discuss its answer key and return a short note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce station reporting, preserving personal turns, records, exit and cleanup. Longer: repeat one unclear observation, retaining both results.

### Words and supports

Review circuit, reflectivity, magnetic response, electrical conduction and property. Enlarge records; allow neutral reading or dictated paper responses.

### Tomorrow

Day 49 returns day 48's tickets. Its ten-minute Return and repair separates error A, appearance proves identity, from error C, every metal pulls. Then children observe mineral scratch behavior and measure length.

### Credit
