# Section 3.2: Several properties support identification

## Purpose and science

Magnetism is not shared by every metal. Test a known conductor before interpreting a failed bulb result. A circuit is a complete conducting path through a load and both battery terminals. Hardness means resistance to a gentle scratch; it is not length. Heat is energy transfer from a warmer object to a cooler object. Read temperature to the nearest instrument mark. Match sample dimensions and exposure for a material comparison.
