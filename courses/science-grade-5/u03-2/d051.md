# Day 51: Put the evidence together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only answer keys. Keep exit-51 covered and exits-48-51 private. Set out record, teacher consensus chart and retained property-table and actual-fallback-log. Prepare thirty dry cups beside the 1 g scale, spoon, powder and tray. Check stable whole-gram readings with a comfortably visible portion beforehand. No practice identification table is revised today.

### Safety and set-up

Check powder ingredients and allergies privately beforehand. Teacher adds powder; children only place cups, tare and read. Goggles remain on through cleanup. No tasting, sniffing or blowing. Collect powder as solid waste. Without protection, allergy clearance, washing or a working scale, use a teacher-supplied invented reading; practical scale actions remain unobserved.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 50's tickets. Error I: [[s03-P51f]]. Compare for two minutes, annotate orally by pointing for two, submit a pointer choice for one. More than ten misses replaces that move with whole-class repair. Review grade 4 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. No endpoint interpretation plus controls response.

#### 16 min. Component rehearsal

Review day 46 tare: account for the empty tray before recording contents. Here the empty cup is tared. At one supervised station, thirty twenty-five-second dry turns total twelve-and-a-half minutes. Each child does [[s03-P51t]]: places a cup, tares, receives adult-added powder, then reads whole grams. Observe actions without a spoken explanation. Remaining three-and-a-half minutes hold setup and brief retries. Retain an unstable result; retry once only within this allowance. If unresolved, record tare observed, reading unresolved. Fallback display: 7 g, explicitly invented for practice; no live weighing credit.

Meanwhile pairs use existing notes, [[s03-P51]], pointing without writing. Review day 45's property-evidence table and data-graph labels from day 9: quantities need units. Existing results stay unchanged. Teacher records scale observations on record; no new child practice table.

#### 6 min. Evidence hearing

Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear six forty-five-second turns, four-and-a-half minutes, using [[s03-P51q]]; allow passing. Others locate components in their own notes, then exchange pointer choices in pairs. Teacher joins correct components; children draft nothing. Every child supplies independent paper at exit, including those not heard.

#### 2 min. Consensus

Review grade 2 pattern chart: different materials share a property. Teacher alone updates the consensus chart from existing observations. Keep actual observations apart from inference. Children keep their notes unchanged. Do not show a wrong identification table or rehearse its correction.

#### 4 min. Notebook evidence

Reveal [[s03-X51]] on exit-51, paper, alone. Children make their first independent table revision here. Review grade 4 routine: revise in a second color, keeping original observations and initials. A two-row table or clear paragraph is acceptable. Collect before discussion. After class, thirty times forty-five seconds gives twenty-two-and-a-half marking minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen on record. Return on day 52; practice feedback is immediate and pages stay in folders.

#### 12 min. Cleanup

Adult collects cups and powder. Stagger thirty forty-second washes at two sinks, ten minutes. Two minutes remain for collection and goggle care. Goggles stay on until powder is cleared.

### Other rooms

No kit: point to existing readings, labeled actual or supplied. No scale action credit. No printer: copy prompts exactly, holding the exit unseen. Teacher keeps action gaps separately from scientific explanations.

### At home

**Swap.** Without a scale or safe powder setup, point to existing supplied readings. Copy prompts without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation, live powder handling and public hearing.

- **5 min. Return.** Discuss day 50's note. Do [[s03-P51f]] orally.
- **12 min. Locate.** Do [[s03-P51]] using existing notes, without writing.
- **10 min. Explain.** Do [[s03-P51q]] aloud.
  **Say.** "Can different materials share an appearance?" Expect: yes; another tested property can differ.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s03-X51]] alone. Then discuss its answer key and return a note.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reporting; preserve personal scale turns, exit and cleanup. Longer: locate another distinguishing observation orally, without revising practice notes.

### Words and supports

Review property, tare, grams, observation and inference. Enlarge scale digits; dictated paper preserves the child's exit decision. Adult handling leaves practical action unobserved.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 returns day 51's tickets. Error A, white proves identity, receives its five-minute Return and repair. The class then introduces evidence for testing changed-material claims, using the reversible-change repair and reading.
