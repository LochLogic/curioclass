# Day 96: What the water graphs can establish

## Teacher page

### Before class
Prepare returned day 95 s05-X95-1, s05-X95-2, s05-P95 and s05-P95-2. Separate teacher-only exit-key.md. Cover the ticket and its unit 5 supplied graph record. Reopen existing paper charts, with each whole named. Use supplied estimates, never classroom measurements. Enlarge an unreadable axis; do not guess a reading. No wet equipment, tasting or household questions.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair. Honor day 95's Tomorrow: return both exit pieces and endpoint slips. If day 95's tickets showed error E, use five minutes tracing the fresh subset to its parent whole. Next five minutes address error H: align zero, units and tick spacing on single-store strips. More than ten gaps makes that route whole-class; others reread their corrected labels. Preserve originals. Do not repeat the held paired-display task.

#### 8 min. Teach access limits
Review day 88: fresh water has little dissolved salt; low salt does not establish tested safety. Review day 90: a map and a water-supply account establish different things. Use the existing fresh-groundwater strip. Its size establishes a global store, without showing a working local intake or testing results. Apply these distinctions without discussing anyone's home. Teacher demonstrates [[s05-P96]] orally: groundwater is a large fresh store; the graph alone cannot establish a town's supply. Keep the three-graph explanation for independent work.

#### 15 min. Component practice
Pairs use the access component sheet, [[s05-P96]]. Three rounds: all-water salt feature, fresh-store feature, then the single swamp endpoint. Review whole and share from day 92, inset from day 95. Each partner takes forty-five seconds per round, ninety seconds simultaneously across pairs. They record separate source locators, not a joined paragraph. Teacher circulates during remaining time. For error D, retain non-ocean salt stores; for error G, separate salt evidence from safety evidence. Paper supplies all feedback; the optional display is unavailable, zero digital minutes.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear the next six component claims, forty-five seconds each; students may pass. All thirty return locator sheets in the final half minute. Record listening only for those heard.

#### 7 min. Paper exit
Close practice. Reveal [[s05-X96]] and the covered graph sheet together. Read the exact stem; say the town is invented for practice. Children independently write one explanation on the half slip, using both overview graphs and the inset. Collect graph record, ticket and page. After class: thirty tickets at forty seconds, twenty minutes; thirty locator sheets at ten seconds, five minutes. Review one formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all on day 97.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy practice features separately, then uncover the held graph record and exact stem. Provide neutral reading or scribing without supplying a claim.

### At home
**Swap.** No board or printer: draw each practice feature on paper; copy held graphs separately.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class collection.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 95's whole and scale feedback.
- **8 min. Review.** Point to the groundwater strip.
  **Say.** "What does this graph establish?" Expect a large fresh store, without local access or safety evidence.
- **15 min. Practice.** Use [[s05-P96]] on three separate features. Keep source locators.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Reveal held-evidence-96.md and [[s05-X96]]. Child answers alone; keep for next-day feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated partner reports, preserving each evidence type and the exit. Longer: revisit the limit in [[s05-P96]] using another existing feature.

### Words and supports
Review fresh water, salt water, groundwater, whole, share and inset. Enlarge matching scales together. Neutral reading preserves the child's interpretation.

### Tomorrow
Day 97 returns s05-X96 and s05-P96 locator sheets. Its Return and repair gives error D five minutes on salt-category members, then error G five on salt versus safety evidence. Separate system and water stations reopen the unit question.
