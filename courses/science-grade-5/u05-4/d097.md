# Day 97: Join the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 96 tickets and locator sheets. Separate teacher-only exit-key.md; cover today's half ticket and fold the student-page Exit box closed. Set three stations: mountain/tray mechanisms, water-store dominance, named wholes. Keep original source and invented-record labels. Use existing notes, not new measurements. Cover complete assessment and retry packets. No household questions or wet equipment.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair. If day 96's tickets showed error D, spend five minutes sorting ocean and non-ocean salt-category members from the existing table. Next five minutes: error G, separate low-salt evidence from testing and access evidence. More than ten gaps makes that route whole-class; others reread feedback. Return s05-X96 and s05-P96 without individual conferences. Use separate features, never a complete access paragraph.

#### 7 min. Reopen anchor components
Review day 87's land-air actions and day 89's water-land observations. Name the parts and observed action in each panel. In the mountain record, moist air rises; clouds and rain form along its path. Air continues beyond the ridge with less moisture. Water follows slopes and carries loose grains. Review erosion, carrying loosened material. Reopen day 94's separate nested strips: the fresh-only whole comes from the all-water fresh subset. Keep each claim beside its own source. When a tray note is unclear, retain it and use the separately tagged invented T89 fallback; never silently replace evidence.

#### 15 min. Consensus rehearsal
Pairs rotate through three five-minute stations, [[s05-P97]]. Each child takes forty-five seconds at each station, ninety seconds per pair; all pairs work simultaneously. Use remaining time for source-locator notes and movement. Teacher records the joined consensus alone: land shapes air routes; water changes land; large water stores do not establish ready fresh supply. Keep two systems per action panel. Children point to one taught component at a time, without drafting a joined explanation. For missing actions, return to observation tags; for error E, trace the fresh subset. No dune or equal-height-bar case appears.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear the next six source locators, forty-five seconds each; passing is allowed. Collect all thirty station sheets in the final half minute. Claim listening only for those heard. Close displays and evidence folders.

#### 8 min. Paper exit
Reveal [[s05-X97-1]] on the half ticket and open the student-page Exit box [[s05-X97-2]]. Read exact stems. Say the dune record is invented for practice. Each child writes independently; neutral reading supplies no mechanism or whole. Collect. After class: thirty paired ticket/page records at forty-five seconds, 22.5 minutes; thirty locator sheets at ten seconds, five minutes. Review one formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Hold exit feedback until day 99; no return or repair on day 98.
