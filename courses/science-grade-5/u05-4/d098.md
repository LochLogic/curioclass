# Day 98: Independent models and water graphs

## Teacher page

### Before class
Separate teacher-only assessment-key.md and score-record.md. Copy two student sides; inspect marked endpoints and readable ticks. Prepare enlarged masters when needed. Put day 97 s05-P97 notes inside closed folders. Keep E97 feedback covered until day 99. No models, keys, completed bars, household questions or apparatus.

### The day

#### 15 min. Independent model assessment
Honor day 97's Tomorrow: no repair or exit feedback. Return closed folders without interpretation. Read directions neutrally; say island observations are invented for practice. Alone, children complete [[s05-Q1a]], [[s05-Q1b]], [[s05-Q1c]], [[s05-Q1d]] and [[s05-Q1e]]. Allow an annotated drawing and brief comparison answers. Read words without explaining an action. Do not diagnose publicly or supply a model.

#### 8 min. All-water graph assessment
Alone, [[s05-Q2a1]] and [[s05-Q2a2]]. Earth shares are supplied estimates, not class measurements. Endpoints are marked; children construct bars and choose labels. No calculations or optional-grid conversions are required. An unreadable copy gets an enlarged blank axis, never a suggested answer.

#### 10 min. Fresh-only graph assessment
Alone, [[s05-Q2b1]] and [[s05-Q2b2]]. Children complete the next graph and use the ice-location record. Maintain independent choices; no hints about categories or the whole.

#### 7 min. Small-reservoir assessment
Alone, [[s05-Q2c1]], [[s05-Q2c2]] and [[s05-Q2c3]]. Children draw on matching axes and explain the display's claims. Neutral reading and scribing preserve their reasoning. No teaching or response begins here.

#### 5 min. Review and collection
Children reread their own responses, then hand in both sides. Collect all thirty; no extra prompt or exit. After class: thirty records at two minutes each, sixty minutes. Use assessment-key.md and the separate points-only score-record.md, maximum 24. Award printed component points only; unattempted items remain missing evidence, with no incomplete total. Preserve original responses and points. Choose up to three priority letters for day 99; copying/location gaps receive descriptive feedback. E97 uses its separate formative record.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy exact stems and proportional blank axes onto two sides. Use neutral reading, tactile axes or a scribe. Do not explain mechanisms.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy the two student sides and blank axes. No class set: use one copy.

**Skip.** Class collection; keep both sides for day 99.

- **15 min. Models.** Alone: [[s05-Q1a]], [[s05-Q1b]], [[s05-Q1c]], [[s05-Q1d]], [[s05-Q1e]].
- **8 min. All water.** [[s05-Q2a1]], [[s05-Q2a2]].
- **10 min. Fresh water.** [[s05-Q2b1]], [[s05-Q2b2]].
- **7 min. Inset.** [[s05-Q2c1]], [[s05-Q2c2]], [[s05-Q2c3]].
- **5 min. Review.** Child checks the paper alone. Adult reads words neutrally, without hints. Keep originals; use the separate key afterward. Save feedback for day 99.

### Shorter or longer
Keep all parts and their order. Follow individual access arrangements; record unfinished evidence instead of rushing or teaching an answer.

### Words and supports
Read printed words neutrally. Enlarge axes consistently. A scribe records the child's labels and explanations without adding content.

### Tomorrow
Day 99 returns Q1/Q2 original points and E97 feedback privately. Errors A/B/C route to system components; D/E/F/G/H to water components. Three five-minute repairs fit the existing fifteen minutes; log further gaps. Preserve original points through revision and one fresh retry.

### Credit
Ours. Sources S05-water and S05-ice support estimates and ice locations. Invented island observations retain Sources S05-coast, S05-life and S05-temperature for their scientific mechanisms.
