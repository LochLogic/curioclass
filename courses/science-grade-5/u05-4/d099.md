# Day 99: Repair, revise and try a new case

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return Q1/Q2 original points and E97 feedback privately. Mark up to three priority repair letters per child. Separate teacher-only keys; use current item-row keys for revised D/G practice. Keep retry sheets covered and fold the Side 2 graph closed. Assign one route by the Fresh transfer map; multiple gaps use the highest priority. Print student drawings without answer labels. No household disclosures or apparatus.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback
Honor day 98's Tomorrow. Return day 98's assessment and day 97 s05-X97-1/s05-X97-2. Children privately locate feedback and assigned letters. Original points remain unchanged; missing evidence stays missing. Review the separate formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair. Three five-minute rounds maximum per child; use assigned Side 1 components. Teacher visits three priority groups for four minutes each, plus three one-minute routing/check intervals. Others attempt their packets independently. During each routing minute, read needed corrections from the teacher key; children compare after attempting. More than ten with one gap makes its round whole-class, replacing a visit. A links action/evidence; B reviews weathering versus erosion; C cites air effects; D sorts salt members; E names wholes; F retains the full remainder; G separates salt-test and contamination evidence; H compares scale units. No retry record appears. Log extra gaps.

#### 10 min. Individual revision
Review second-color revision, keeping original observations and initials. Children annotate their own Q1/Q2/E97 responses using repaired components. Preserve original answers and points. Teacher checks evidence labels during circulation; use descriptive revision notes, no replacement score. Missing observations cannot be invented.

#### 10 min. Fresh transfer
Map: A/B systems, C air, D salt, E/F/H distribution, G access. One minute neutral reading, eight independent work, one collection. Reveal exactly one assigned half-sheet route: [[s05-R99-systems]], [[s05-R99-air]], [[s05-R99-distribution]], [[s05-R99-salt]] or [[s05-R99-access]]. Say records are invented for practice. Distribution uses the covered Side 2 graph; other routes answer on the half slip. Never show other routes or a key. Collect all thirty retries and revisions. After class: thirty retries at thirty seconds, fifteen minutes; revisions at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes; repair pages at ten seconds, five minutes. Use the single formative scale on record.md, separate from original points.

#### 5 min. Retrieval and handoff
Review grade 4: refine means make a change intended to improve a design. Natural resource means material or energy supply found in nature. Use the new invented Side 2 pictures with [[s05-P99-design]] and [[s05-P99-resource]]. Children mark one change and one resource link; collect all notes. A changed design still needs test evidence before claiming improvement. Forward uncertain links, without a readiness score or extra conference.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy assigned stems and blank retry axes separately. Draw the two handoff pictures without answer labels. A neutral scribe preserves independent choices.

### At home
**Swap.** No group or printer: copy only assigned repair rows and one covered retry. No second color: circle additions.

**Skip.** Group visits and public collection.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read day 98 and E97 notes privately.
- **10 min. Repair.** Try up to three assigned Side 1 questions; adult checks the separate key afterward.
  **Say.** "Which evidence changed your explanation?" Expect the matching observation, whole or test distinction.
- **8 min. Revise.** Child adds corrections while keeping originals.
- **5 min. Handoff.** Use [[s05-P99-design]] and [[s05-P99-resource]] with Side 2 pictures.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Give only the assigned route: [[s05-R99-systems]], [[s05-R99-air]], [[s05-R99-distribution]], [[s05-R99-salt]] or [[s05-R99-access]]. Child answers alone; distribution uses the covered Side 2 graph. Adult keeps it for private written feedback after day 100.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: assign fewer repair components, preserving revision and fresh transfer. Longer: reread the evidence behind the existing revision; no extra retry.

### Words and supports
Review interaction arrows, weathering, erosion, fresh, whole, remainder, scale, refine and natural resource. Read neutrally; original and later evidence stay distinguishable.

### Tomorrow
Day 100 opens storm-runoff protection models. Private written retry, revision and repair feedback returns after class on day 100 in the Retrieve handoff folder, zero added lesson minutes. Send today's design/resource notes and unaddressed letters to day 103 Return and repair. Promise no extra repair block.

### Credit
Ours. All new pictures and records are invented for practice.
