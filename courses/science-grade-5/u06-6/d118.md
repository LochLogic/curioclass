# Day 118: What can a model and graph show?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Day 117's exit ticket and written task feedback already returned after class on day 117.
Read key-118.md, today's teacher-only answer key. Print proportional blank axes, never completed bars. Cover earlier displays; conceal day 119 sources. Prepare individual records. Day 117 feedback already returned there; no repair enters today's sitting. Source C5-118-water supplies estimates and ice locations. Data tables and blank axes occupy both sides of the single student sheet.

### Safety and set-up
Paper and pencils only. No living plants, soil, water or equipment handled. Collection takes the final minute. No personal water questions.

### The day

#### 15 min. Independent model assessment
Honor day 117's Tomorrow. Say the observation records are invented for practice; Earth estimates are sourced. One minute neutral directions, seven for [[c5-E118-model]], seven across [[c5-E118-coast]], [[c5-E118-life]] and [[c5-E118-air]]. Children work alone. Read words without explaining arrows. No teaching, feedback or public turns.

#### 8 min. All-water graph
Alone: [[c5-E118-all]]. Supply endpoints without conversions. Children construct bars and annotations. Replace a spoiled copy with an identical blank axis. Graph units are equal shares, not huge water volumes.

#### 10 min. Fresh-water graph
Alone: [[c5-E118-fresh]]. The location note supplies polar land ice; children decide its graph category. Preserve independent choices. No atmospheric quantity is asked.

#### 7 min. Small-reservoir inset
Alone: [[c5-E118-inset]] and [[c5-E118-limit]]. Matching axes use the fresh-only whole. Read neutrally; do not supply an access conclusion.

#### 5 min. Review and collection
Four minutes inspection, one collection. No exit or key display. After class, thirty records at ninety seconds take forty-five minutes. Use key-118.md and record-118-120.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen for each component; no points or totals. Record access help separately. Tag errors AF/AG/AH for interaction, transport or air; AI/AJ/AK/AL/AM for membership, whole, remainder, safety or scale. Copying slips get descriptive feedback. Assign at most two priority system/graph moves for day 120, retaining further gaps.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy stems, data and proportional blank axes onto two sides. Enlarge axes consistently; a neutral scribe follows the child's decisions. This supplied-record assessment needs no instrument fallback.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy both sides and blank axes. No class set: one copy suffices.
**Skip.** Class collection and public reports.

- **8 min. All water.** [[c5-E118-all]].
- **10 min. Fresh water.** [[c5-E118-fresh]].
- **7 min. Inset.** [[c5-E118-inset]] and [[c5-E118-limit]].
- **5 min. Review.** Child checks alone. Adult reads neutrally; keep the separate key hidden. Save feedback for day 120.

### Shorter or longer
Keep every component. Record unfinished evidence; follow established access arrangements without rushing or teaching answers.

### Words and supports
Read terms as printed without definitions that solve items. Enlarge all axes proportionally. Scribes preserve exact student directions.

### Tomorrow
Day 119 uses new community sources and an air-cushion planning memo. Keep today's answers and feedback private until day 120 Return and repair. No repair interrupts day 119.

### Credit
Ours. Source C5-118-water supplies estimates and polar ice locations. Sources C5-118-coast, C5-118-life and C5-118-air check science behind invented observations.
