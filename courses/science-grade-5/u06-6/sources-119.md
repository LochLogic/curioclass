# Day 119: Source cards

## Source A: A campus account
Seattle University, *Rain Gardens of SU*, undated.
https://www.seattleu.edu/life-at-seattle-u/our-campus/campus-gardens/rain-gardens/

**A1.** Campus gardens collect runoff in engineered soil and slowly refill groundwater.

**A2.** At Lee Miley Rain Garden, excess water enters the combined sewer when capacity is reached.

## Source B: A federal benefits description
United States Environmental Protection Agency, *Benefits of Green Infrastructure*, updated November 19, 2026.
https://www.epa.gov/green-infrastructure/benefits-green-infrastructure

**B1.** Green infrastructure captures and absorbs stormwater, reducing runoff.

**B2.** It can restore wildlife habitat and support natural systems.
