### Before class
Keep teacher-keys.md teacher-only; use current-day data answer keys. Source-cards-115.md and assessment-115.md are prompt masters for today's two-sided page, not extra child pages. ### The day

#### 5 min. Directions
Honor day 114's handoff: no return or reteach today. Issue unseen source page and independent response spaces. Review the source-location routine only through neutral directions: source labels and line identifiers remain available. Read printed sources, captions and unfamiliar words neutrally. All scientific choices belong to the child. No practice frame, answer display or class discussion.

#### 24 min. Independent source report
Alone, [[s06-Q1a]]. Eight minutes to read/annotate, sixteen to draw and report. Both source excerpts remain available; print shared text once. No feedback or probing. Record reading/scribing assistance descriptively. A neutral scribe writes the child's explanation exactly. Do not suggest a route, source pair or limit.

#### 12 min. Independent impacts
Alone, two minutes each: [[s06-Q1b1]], [[s06-Q1b2]], [[s06-Q1b3]], [[s06-Q1b4]], [[s06-Q1b5]], [[s06-Q1b6]]. Read the invented label with each case. Each response produces an affected part, action and causal connection; no answer-choice chart or public turns. No personal household or disaster examples.

#### 4 min. Evidence status
Alone, [[s06-Q1c]]. Children retain sources and use line references. Collect papers at the close; offer no answers. After class, thirty children at three minutes each require ninety minutes of descriptive feedback. Hold originals and feedback until day 117. One assessment scale only, 32 possible points across both sittings, finalized after revision: today's components total twenty. No categorical marks on that record. Missing work remains identified.
