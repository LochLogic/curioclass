# Day 120: Repair and hand on the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class
Retained handoff references: day 118.
Read key-120.md and key-120-components.md, today's teacher-only answer keys. Return days 118/119 privately with component feedback. Assign at most four five-minute moves, at most two from either day. Gather course-model records and day 116's unchanged action record. Conceal the pump exit. Print one half ticket; the student page's Exit box holds its drawing.

### Safety and set-up
Dry paper and pencils only. No handled plants, soil, loads or water. Use the last minute for filing. No personal disclosures or public comparisons.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback
Honor day 119's Tomorrow. Return day 118's assessment and day 119's assessment; read private priorities. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Original responses remain visible and unchanged. Public talk may be passed; all submit paper.

#### 20 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair from day 117. Four five-minute moves use parallel routes: systems [[c5-P120-systems]], graphs [[c5-P120-graphs]], sources [[c5-P120-sources]], testing [[c5-P120-testing]]. Children attempt assigned components, inspect private feedback, then annotate corrections. Teacher visits each route for three minutes, uses four minutes transitioning and four inspecting work. More than ten with one gap replaces that route visit with a whole-class move. No extra time. By priority, read one substitute component aloud; the child uses the matching response box: error AG [[c5-P120-erosion]]; error AI [[c5-P120-salt]]; error AK [[c5-P120-other]]; error AL [[c5-P120-safe]]; error AM [[c5-P120-scale]]; error AS [[c5-P120-faults]]; error AT [[c5-P120-step]]; error AU [[c5-P120-action]]. Each replaces a five-minute move, never adds one. New cases only; keys stay teacher-held. These assisted repairs do not establish independent success. Log unaddressed needs.

**Ask.** Which observation supports your arrow or claim? Expect a specific recorded action or value. Partners exchange two 45-second explanations simultaneously within a move; no thirty-child speaking queue.

#### 10 min. Revise and hand off
Five minutes second-color revision, preserving original measurements and actions. Five minutes [[c5-P120-handoff]] with the table. Select existing particle, conserved-matter, system and fair-trial records or write evidence still needed. Review only what written evidence establishes; directly explain a missing convention as new before using it. Particle models infer unseen matter, not visible grains. Record secure observations and unanswered questions without assuming mastery. Day 116's observations and missing conducting remain unchanged.

#### 10 min. Fresh transfer
Cover repairs. Reveal [[c5-X120]], printed and said invented for practice, on a half ticket; use the Exit box for the model. Nine independent minutes, one collection/filing. No feedback until completion. After class: thirty exits at forty seconds take twenty minutes; thirty handoffs at twenty seconds take ten. Use the same three-category scale for exit components; handoffs are descriptive. Return private written feedback with final folders. Keep AR/AO needs and unresolved gaps separate from original check judgments.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy assigned prompts, four handoff rows and a concealed exit. Scribes preserve decisions; copied tables never supply conducting evidence.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy assigned rows and covered exit. No second color: circle additions. Missing record: write evidence still needed.
**Skip.** Route visits and public reports.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Read days 118/119 notes together.
- **15 min. Repair.** Up to three assigned rows: [[c5-P120-systems]], [[c5-P120-graphs]], [[c5-P120-sources]] or [[c5-P120-testing]].
  **Say.** "Which observation supports your claim?" Expect a specific action or value.
- **10 min. Revise and hand off.** Preserve originals; complete [[c5-P120-handoff]] with retained work.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Child answers [[c5-X120]] alone, drawing in the Exit box. Give private feedback afterward; keep questions with the folder.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fewer repairs, preserving revision, handoff and transfer. Longer: inspect an existing locator; do not invent missing observations.

### Words and supports
Review interaction, whole, source and fair test in assigned work. Explain unfamiliar conventions directly. Read neutrally; record access assistance separately.

### Tomorrow
The course ends. Hand on retained model evidence, unchanged practical observations and unresolved questions for future retrieval. No additional work or conference is promised.

### Credit
Ours. All new component records and the pump case are invented for practice.
