# Day 116: Test unfamiliar catchers

## Teacher page

### Before class
Separate teacher-keys.md; use current-day data answer keys. Hide problem, footprint and Q2 fallback until directions. Follow station-preparation.md: rehearse cycles, check allergies, covered edges, clean mixture, protection and two supervised wash places. Select records if safe timing/supervision fails. Assessment-116.md supplies layout, not duplicate pages. Keep action roster, assessment record and stored formative record separate. Hold day 115 work for 117.

### The day

#### 6 min. Setup and transitions
Honor day 115: independent evidence, no reteach. Two minutes neutral directions/safety; four distributed reset/transition minutes outside turns. Only active five handle. Goggles, nonlatex gloves, stable trays; no tasting, dust blowing or face touching. Adult prepares/repairs edges and mixture. Stop damage or uncontrolled spills. Review fair-test routine through neutral directions without supplying decisions.

#### 24 min. Observed stations and waiting work
Six groups of five, four minutes each, [[s06-Q2a]]. Reveal problem/footprint. Each records a planning decision simultaneously, 45 seconds. Six alternating 25-second cycles use 150 seconds: two-second pour, collect until five seconds, read/reset. First five pours belong to different children; sixth repeats the first. Teacher names each observed action on the roster. Everyone retains six mL readings/faults, thirty seconds; finish, fifteen seconds. Read 1 mL steps and whole seconds. One safe reread within cycle, then unresolved/reason. No winner supplied.

Waiting children start Q2b independently. Finished groups remove gloves and wash: five 45-second turns at two supervised places fit 2.25 minutes within the next turn. Goggles stay through splash risk, never shared. Use records if supervision cannot cover washing.

#### 10 min. Independent explanations
Alone, [[s06-Q2b]], with retained measurements/problem. Collect every record/model without feedback. Actual failures count. No-kit: announce fallback invented for practice; planning/interpretation remain assessable, conducting unobserved. Never combine supplied and actual values.

#### 5 min. Cleanup
One adult waste/surface minute, one parallel goggle wipe/bag minute, three final-group soap/water washing minutes. Keep solids out of sinks. After class, thirty at three minutes take ninety minutes for feedback, held until 117. Today's twelve possible points join yesterday's twenty after revision. One 32-point scale; missing practical evidence stays blank, total pending.

### Other rooms
Unsafe apparatus, washing or supervision: labeled Q2 records, conducting unobserved. No printer: copy problem and trace a 16 cm by 8 cm footprint. No device needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Missing safe apparatus/protection: use Q2's invented records; conducting remains unobserved. Adult prepares apparatus and follows classroom safety controls.
**Skip.** Group transitions; keep hygiene.
- **40 min. Assessment.** Read problem/footprint neutrally. Child completes [[s06-Q2a]], deciding a plan and conducting six safe trials with adult collaboration. Measure the same water-collection interval each trial. Child completes [[s06-Q2b]] independently using retained results. Collect work without feedback. Clean apparatus and wash hands in the final five minutes. Return evidence on day 117.

### Shorter or longer
Keep every action, repeat and hygiene. Failed timing: stop safely; retain incomplete evidence. Finish interpretation with separately labeled supplied records.

### Words and supports
Review criterion, constraint, fair test and trial through neutral reading. Adapt grips; scribe chosen readings. Record help and personally directed actions.

### Tomorrow
Day 117 returns days 114/115/116 originals. Errors E/F/G/H get five-minute testing components within twenty repair minutes. Preserve actions/values during revision.

### Credit
