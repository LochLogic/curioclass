# Day 119: Correct a claim and plan a comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read key-119.md, today's teacher-only answer key. Print sources-119.md separately from response pages. Conceal earlier work and keys. Attach day 116's unchanged practical record before class, including assistance and unobserved actions. Do not recollect, rescore or fill gaps. Day 118's assessment stays sealed until day 120.

### Safety and set-up
Paper and pencils only. No cushions, loads, syringes, plants, soil or liquids handled. Filing uses the last minute. No home, health or disaster disclosures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions
Honor day 118's Tomorrow. Say the proposal, impact records and design brief are invented for practice; source accounts are real. Distribute sources and blank pages. Read neutrally. No feedback or public turns. Day 116's record remains teacher-held and unchanged.

#### 19 min. Leaflet correction
Alone: [[c5-E119-leaflet]]. Five minutes reading, fourteen drawing and explaining. Source A describes its institution's installation; Source B gives general benefits. Children decide how these different accounts support the proposed action. Read authors, dates and lines without interpretation. Do not rehearse a corrected leaflet.

#### 9 min. Human impacts
Alone: [[c5-E119-impacts]]. Five brief records require part/action connections: ninety seconds each plus ninety seconds inspection. The stream record also reaches the ocean. No choice bank or personal disclosure.

#### 4 min. Evidence status
Alone: [[c5-E119-status]]. Children correct the unsupported local-result caption. Accept defensible limits; do not prompt the observation/projection distinction during assessment.

#### 8 min. Fair-test memo
Alone: [[c5-E119-plan]]. Seven planning minutes, one collection. This unfamiliar air-retaining design draws on air occupying space and controlled comparisons. It samples planning, not conducting or full engineering mastery. After class: thirty papers at two minutes take sixty minutes. Use key-119.md and the single record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, for each component, without points or totals. AN/AO identify disconnected explanation/model; AP prediction as observation; AL safety; AU mismatched action; AQ/AR/AS/AT controls, repetition, deleted faults or unsupported readings. Choose at most two source/test repair moves for day 120; retain remaining needs.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy source cards separately from response spaces. Enlarge text or use neutral reading/scribing. A written plan cannot replace an observed practical action.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy sources and response pages. No practical record: leave conducting unobserved.
**Skip.** Class collection and record transfer.
- **5 min. Assessment directions.** Read neutrally; keep keys hidden.
- **19 min. Leaflet.** Child reads both sources and answers [[c5-E119-leaflet]] alone.
- **9 min. Impacts.** [[c5-E119-impacts]].
- **4 min. Caption.** [[c5-E119-status]].
- **8 min. Plan.** [[c5-E119-plan]]. Keep the separate key hidden until work is complete. Save feedback for day 120.

### Shorter or longer
Keep each component. Follow access arrangements; record unfinished evidence rather than giving hints or demanding speed.

### Words and supports
Read authors, source lines and directions neutrally. Scribes record student decisions. No extra scientific definitions during independent work.

### Tomorrow
Day 120 returns days 118 and 119 privately. Four five-minute repair moves cover systems, graphs, sources and testing. Retain unresolved gaps in the descriptive handoff. Day 116 conducting evidence stays unchanged.

### Credit
Ours. Sources C5-119A and C5-119B support source-card paraphrases. The proposal, impact records and cushion brief are invented for practice.
