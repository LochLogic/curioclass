# Day 21: What can an air comparison show?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print exit-21.md using exits-18-22.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and current answer keys private; use record.md. Prepare only A21 from input-records.md, paper source chart from source-chart.md and question board. A21 is invented, not extracted published data. Have day 20's exit and logs ready. No gas equipment, plants or chemicals are handled.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 20's s02-X20. If many treat light as material, error D, use five minutes contrasting day 18's light-energy label with its separate water route. For error I, use five minutes comparing H20's height information with its inability to identify inputs. Each move: two minutes contrast, two independent correction, one collection. Ready children name what remains unmeasured. Arithmetic gets separate descriptive feedback; do not repeat today's held comparison designs.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review fair test, grade 4 day 2: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Read A21 aloud: invented for practice. More useful air means more of the air part plants use; no gas names or gas trial.

Point to equal water, minerals, light and temperature. Air treatment differs. Dry plant weights are 11 g and 17 g, in 1 g steps. Review whole-gram resolution from day 8. Explain that the 6 g difference supports air's contribution under these conditions. Equal water challenges water-only, error B; it does not measure air's percentage. Review sufficient evidence, grade 4 day 105.

#### 18 min. Single-study evidence

Return day 20's s02-P20-1 s02-P20-2, two minutes. **Ask.** [[s02-P21-1]] Children tag their own records, then compare with a partner. Each partner gets 45 seconds, simultaneously across pairs.

**Ask.** [[s02-P21-3]] Give two minutes before revealing that A21's recorded endpoints are supplied observations within an invented investigation. Review grade 4 day 13's prediction: an expected result. The different-plant claim is a prediction, not another observation. Neither becomes actual classroom evidence.

Hear rotating six, review grade 4 day 16, for at most 45 seconds each within five minutes. Note who spoke; every child returns paper. Remaining time corrects source locators and unsupported claims. Keep only one study in view; no choice between competing designs.

#### 5 min. Question board

Review question board from day 18: attach support and uncertainty to a question. Keep Source S02-WEB's checked general explanation separate from invented A21 numbers. **Ask.** [[s02-P21-2]] Each child proposes a measurement, without performing it. A relevant example measures dry plant weights under changed minerals with other inputs equal. It is a proposal, not a result. Collect all three practice responses.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

**Shared shape.** Practice tags one supplied study. The exit chooses between two completed comparisons and explains the choice.

Reveal [[s02-X21]], exact stem, invented for practice, now. Everyone responds alone on paper. After class: 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes exit marking; 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes descriptive practice feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Blanks stay missing evidence. Error B keeps water-only despite the comparison; error I assigns air the result when water also changes. Return all on day 22.

### Other rooms

No projector: copy A21 and the current source chart onto the board. No printer: copy today's records, questions and blank spaces; keep the exit covered until use. No wet equipment is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No display or printer: copy A21's table and today's questions onto paper.

**Skip.** Public reports and class rotations.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 20 feedback. Separate light energy from matter inputs.
- **10 min. Compare.** Read A21, explicitly invented. Do [[s02-P21-1]].
  **Say.** "What changes while water stays equal?" Expect: the useful air supply; other stated conditions match.
- **10 min. Tag.** Do [[s02-P21-3]]. Discuss supplied observations versus a prediction. Do [[s02-P21-2]] as a proposal only.
- **5 min. Source.** Locate A21's record and uncertainty. Explain that its numbers are not published results.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X21]] alone; discuss afterward with the answer key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce oral reports; retain individual tags, proposal and exit. Longer: improve a measurement proposal's controls, without comparing competing completed designs.

### Words and supports

Review fair test and sufficient evidence. Observation: recorded result. Prediction: an expected result not yet observed. Useful air: the air part plants use to build matter. Read records aloud; accept dictated paper tags.

### Tomorrow

Day 22 returns day 21's exit: error B first five minutes, using equal-water air evidence; error I second five, separating controlled observations from overclaiming. Practice s02-P21-1 s02-P21-2 s02-P21-3 returns during Evidence stations, two minutes. Then write separate statements at soil, water and air stations and revise component model notes.
