# H18: a reported tree investigation

This is a summary of a historical report.

| Reading | At planting | Five years later |
|---|---|---|
| Willow weight | 5 pounds | About 169 pounds |
| Dried soil | 200 pounds | Less than one pound below 200 pounds |

The report describes watering with rainwater or distilled water.
The soil was dried before both soil weighings.
The report does not say that both tree weights were dry.
Fallen leaves were not weighed.
The report concluded that water supplied all growth.
That conclusion goes beyond this comparison.
Air was not controlled.

Source H18: the historical willow account, translated in 1662, page 109.
An original summary; no conversions are needed.
