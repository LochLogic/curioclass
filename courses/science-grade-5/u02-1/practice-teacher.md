# Component practice answer keys

Teacher only. Print separately from student sheets.

## s02-P18-1

About 164 pounds tree gain and less than one pound re-dried soil loss.

Look for: locates both reported changes

## s02-P18-2

Tentative air into leaves and water/minerals into roots, sunlight distinguished as energy.

Look for: possible inputs with uncertainty

## s02-P19-1

Water availability can limit growth. No inherited change is established.

Look for: water linked to growth without inheritance claim

## s02-P19-2

Less specified food may reduce feeding/survival, not a guaranteed outcome.

Look for: food need linked to conditional consequence

## s02-P19-3

Mineral supply differs; complete group has 6 g greater dry weight. This is constructed, not a published result.

Look for: locates changed minerals and dry-weight result

## s02-P20-1

Pot ID/date, whole-centimeter reading or unreadable with reason; actual and supplied records separate.

Look for: agreed zero/top and ruler steps | provenance retained

## s02-P20-2

Date, sun time/clock note, stick/place, whole-centimeter reading or unreadable. H20 separate.

Look for: records shared reading and controls

## s02-P21-1

Air differs; water, minerals, light and temperature match. A21 is invented.

Look for: locates changed air and equal conditions

## s02-P21-2

A relevant controlled measurement, such as matched dry whole-plant weights while varying one input.

Look for: relevant measurement and controlled comparison

## s02-P22-1

An accurate statement from that station alone, with record/source locator and limited claim.

Look for: one accurate source-linked statement
