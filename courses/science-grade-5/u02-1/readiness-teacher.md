# Readiness directions and answer keys

Teacher only. Use existing day 17 rows s01-N17a/b: four minutes each, two for collection/sorting. No points.

**s01-N17a:** Water availability can affect growth; inherited differences are not established. Record separately from unit 1 points.

Look for: links water availability to shown growth | uses supplied environmental comparison without claiming inheritance

Samples: More available water could support greater growth. The record does not establish inherited differences. | They must have different inherited traits.

**s01-N17b:** Less specified food can reduce survival opportunities; not a guarantee that every grazer dies. Record separately from unit 1 points.

Look for: links disappearing plants to less food | gives possible survival consequence without guaranteeing death

Samples: Less food may make survival harder; it does not prove every grazer dies. | Losing food cannot affect survival.

Day 19: four minutes water/growth, four resources, two fresh explanations. Teach planned-only links explicitly. More than ten misses makes that move whole-class. Otherwise target a group; ready children check controls.
