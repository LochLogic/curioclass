# Day 22: Revise what each record supports

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print exit-22.md from exits-18-22.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and current answer keys private; record.md holds feedback. Set three paper stations: historical-card.md, input-records.md W19/M19, and A21. Copy source-chart.md locator headings onto the student page. Keep records separate; do not display a complete combined argument. Have day 21 papers and original willow drawings ready. No specimen handling.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 21's s02-X21. If many retain water-only, error B, spend five minutes revisiting A21's equal water and changed air. Error I: spend five distinguishing a changed input from an isolated cause, using A21's stated controls and its limits. Each move: two minutes contrast, two individual correction, one collection. Ready children identify unmeasured input shares. Do not present today's held vine record.

#### 16 min. Evidence stations

Three four-minute paper stations plus four minutes for transitions. Use the first two transition minutes to return day 21's s02-P21-1/2/3. Six groups visit three stations, two groups per station.

**Ask.** [[s02-P22-1]] Children write one separate source-linked statement at each station. H18: compare reported changes while keeping dry-weight and fallen-leaf limits. W19/M19: distinguish available inputs from measured results; minerals remain present in soil-free growing. A21: equal water and changed air support air contribution without measuring a percentage. Mark invented cards as invented for practice. No single synthesis paragraph or integrated routes/light model here.

#### 10 min. Oral challenges

Pairs read their separate station statements, one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six at most 45 seconds each within five minutes; note who. Everyone will submit source locators.

Use two separate oral exchanges: **Ask.** [[s02-P22-2]] Expect sunlight's food-making energy role. Then **Ask.** [[s02-P22-3]] Expect mineral inputs can arrive in water. No written response to either spoken item. Rehearsal is unscored; absence of a heard answer is missing evidence. Keep oral feedback notes in individual folders. Error D turns light into matter; error C rejects minerals.

#### 5 min. Model notes

Review day 5's dated change line: retain originals and date each correction in another color. Children revise separate components of their original willow drawing using the locator statements. Review relevant and sufficient evidence, grade 4 day 105. No complete combined account is modeled. Collect station statements; drawings and oral feedback stay in folders.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X22]] only now. Read the exact invented-for-practice stem neutrally; everyone answers alone on the half slip. After class: 30 times 25 seconds, twelve minutes thirty seconds exit marking; ten minutes descriptive station feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Blank stays missing evidence. Accept limited claims: the supplied comparisons support contributions, not exact shares or mineral necessity. The general chiefly-air-and-water account comes from taught science, not a measured percentage here.

### Other rooms

No printer or station space: copy three separate records and current stems on the board; groups point to each station in sequence. Keep the exit hidden. No device or wet kit is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer/display: copy the three separate records and locator headings onto paper.

**Skip.** Station movement and public reports.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 21 feedback. Locate equal water and changed air on A21.
- **15 min. Components.** Do [[s02-P22-1]], keeping one statement per record.
  **Say.** "What does this record support?" Expect: a source-linked observation with a limit, without an exact input percentage.
- **10 min. Revise.** Keep the original willow drawing. Date separate corrections. Explain sunlight's food-making energy role. Explain minerals can arrive in water. Keep these as separate exchanges.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X22]] alone. Discuss afterward using the current answer key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports, preserving three station statements, revisions and exit. Longer: improve a locator or qualify an overclaim; do not rehearse the held integrated package.

### Words and supports

Review source locator, incoming matter, energy and dated change line. Read station records aloud first. Enlarge the drawing or accept dictated corrections onto paper. Every child may pass during public talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 returns day 22's s02-X22 in ten-minute Return and repair. First five: error A, mainly soil, and error C, no minerals, share one soil/mineral contrast. Second five: error D, light as stuff. Station statement s02-P22-1 returns in Predict interactions, one minute. Then set up plant-only jars and predict interactions. No harvest or weighing is promised.
