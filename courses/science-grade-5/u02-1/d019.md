# Day 19: What grows without soil?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print current sheets from exits-18-22.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and d019-key.html private; record.md holds feedback. Prepare prerequisite-pictures.md and input-records.md W19/M19. Stage historical-card.md and source-chart.md. Sort day 17 readiness notes and day 18 papers. Check allergies privately. Inspect six class bean pots and the sealed water-grown plant display prepared by teacher. Set hand-washing supplies, damp wipes and waste bag; eye protection, nonlatex gloves and aprons protect adult handlers. No safe setup: paper.

### The day

#### 10 min. Prerequisite teaching

Return day 17's separate s01-N17a s01-N17b notes. Review surroundings, grade 3 day 47, and needs meet conditions, day 69. Explicitly teach the evidence-to-effect links without assuming those skills were completed.

Use prerequisite pictures: four minutes for water/growth, four for prey/survival. Less available water can limit growth; less specified food can limit survival. Neither result is guaranteed. **Ask.** [[s02-P19-1]] and [[s02-P19-2]]. Reserve two minutes for independent explanations. More than ten gaps: teach that link to all; otherwise support a small group while ready children identify uncontrolled conditions on the same pictures.

#### 5 min. Evidence return

Return day 18's s02-X18 with descriptive notes. If many assign one source from equal totals, error I, contrast H18's observed changes with its uncontrolled inputs. Two minutes contrast, two for individual correction, one collection. Ready children locate omitted observations. Initial drawings stay descriptive.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review day 5's information source versus incoming material. Use source-chart.md: H18 reports soil change; W19 shows available inputs, not measured growth. Model a locator for each, keeping statements separate. Soil-free growing includes small necessary mineral inputs. Minerals are matter, not energy-supplying food.

Read M19: invented for practice, not published data. **Ask.** [[s02-P19-3]] before revealing [[steps:s02-P19-3]]. Dry weight excludes water held inside plants. Drying destroys a sample, so initial and final dry comparisons need matched samples. Fresh weights need comparable water content. New parts can use existing plant material; appearance alone cannot establish net gain. No cutting or weighing-plan rehearsal.

#### 15 min. Observe and compare

Return day 18's s02-P18-1 s02-P18-2, one minute. Teacher moves displays past six seated groups, one minute each. Children observe without contact. Missing or unsafe displays: use W19, announced invented; mark observation unavailable.

Compare actual visible features with the diagram, without treating either as long-term growth evidence. Two 45-second partner statements run simultaneously. Hear rotating six, review grade 4 day 16, within five minutes; note who. Collect all three individual practice responses. Do not ask for the held exit's storage-and-weighing account.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Teacher stores closed displays, wipes trays and washes hands using soap, running water and towels. Observers have no washing queue. Accidental contact stops for washing; paper replaces further handling. No tasting or sniffing; no classroom chemical trial.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

**Shared shape.** Practice compares displays and mineral treatments separately. The exit critiques new leaves and proposes whole-cutting weighing.

Reveal [[s02-X19]] now; read the exact stem neutrally. All respond alone on paper. After class: 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes exit marking; 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes descriptive practice feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen on the formative record. Missing evidence stays blank. Error I overclaims from new leaves; error C rejects minerals. Return all on day 20.

### Other rooms

No displays or safe protective equipment: use W19 and label it invented for practice. No printer/projector: copy current stems and diagrams on paper or board, keeping exits hidden.

### At home

**Swap.** No plants or protection: use W19. No printer/display: copy today's picture records and questions. Handle no soil or liquid.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports.

- **8 min. Needs.** Read the invented pictures. Do [[s02-P19-1]] and [[s02-P19-2]].
  **Say.** "What changed for the living thing?" Expect: water availability or prey supply, affecting growth or survival.
- **10 min. Sources.** Compare H18 and W19 separately. Explain that soil-free growing still includes minerals. Dry weights exclude internal water; drying destroys samples.
- **12 min. Minerals.** Read M19 and do [[s02-P19-3]]. Keep invented records separate from observations. Explain that new parts may use existing material.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X19]] alone; discuss afterward using the current answer key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce oral display reports, preserving prerequisite support, mineral comparison and exit. Longer: locate one uncontrolled input, without planning the held cutting test.

### Words and supports

Mineral: a small necessary material input. Dry weight: weight after water removal. Source chart: idea and its record. Read stimuli aloud; permit enlarged text and dictated paper responses.

### Tomorrow

Day 20 returns day 19's exit: error I, growth does not locate sources, first five minutes; error C, minerals remain necessary, second five. Practice s02-P19-1 s02-P19-2 s02-P19-3 returns in Height observations, two minutes. Then measure existing plants and make the monthly shared shadow record.
