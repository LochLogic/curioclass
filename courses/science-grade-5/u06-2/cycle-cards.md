# Teacher-held design-cycle components

Teacher only. Reveal stages separately; do not supply a complete goal-and-cycle student response.

Define the problem and checkable criteria.

Sketch alternatives within constraints.

Build a prototype matching its drawing.

Test fairly with repeated trials.

Compare every result and locate failure evidence.

Change one feature, predict its effect, and retest under matching conditions.

These are general components, not an answer to the held display-filter case.
