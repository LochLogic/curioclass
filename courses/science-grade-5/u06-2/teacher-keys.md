Teacher only. Keep this guide separate from every student copy.
Read the section opener for teaching moves; use this guide to prepare and mark.
The student's stems are in items.csv and the day pages.
Read each question before revealing its answer.

## Day 103

- **s06-P103-repair-A.** Water interacts with plants or soil while moving through the wetland.
- **s06-P103-repair-B.** Rainwater moves from roof into barrel.
- **s06-P103-repair-C.** Projection; future observations are needed.
- **s06-P103-retrieve.** Examples: lid opening triggers an alarm; messages keep agreed meanings; water designs face matched flow conditions.
- **s06-P103-measure-v2.** 40 milliliters.
- **s06-P103-sort.** Criterion, a way to check the need is met.
- **s06-P103-limit.** Constraint, the familiar limit on a design.
- **s06-X103.** Example: Fewer counted flecks after filtering equal samples. At most one mesh square and one frame. Link plan, build, test, compare, change and retest to the measured goal and limits. Accept another workable visible-fleck or volume criterion.

## Day 104

- **s06-P104-repair-D.** Appearance cannot establish drinking safety; never taste.
- **s06-P104-feature.** Cloth-layer count; all other portions/supports/outlets match.
- **s06-P104-contribution.** Specific labeled personal decision; group success is not individual evidence.
- **s06-P104-design.** Two labeled sketches: one versus two cloth layers. Each has one quarter-cup of sand and one quarter-cup of gravel. Paper support and outlet match.
- **s06-X104-2.** Repeated fair trials must establish actual performance. A forecast proves neither reliable success nor drinking safety.
- **s06-X104-1.** R meets retention/material limits but misses volume. S predicts meeting every stated goal and limit.

## Day 105

- **s06-P105-repair-D.** Whether it is safe to drink remains untested; never taste.
- **s06-P105-repair-F.** One trial does not show the range or consistency of repeated performance.
- **s06-P105-plot.** Two marks at 1 cup; one at 1.5, with cup units.
- **s06-P105-measure.** 80 milliliters: four steps of 20 mL.
- **s06-P105-repeat.** Variation is evidence; retaining every result shows consistency of performance.
- **s06-P105-control.** Input, mixture, position, reset or interval, with fair-comparison reason.
- **s06-X105.** Change hole spacing only. Fix input volume, scrap amount/size, mixture, pour position, duration and reset. Alternate at least three trials each. Record volume in a stated unit and escaping scrap counts. Repeats reveal variation; retain every failure.
