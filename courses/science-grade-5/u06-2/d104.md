# Day 104: Two designs before building

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the unit 6 section 6.2 opener. Separate unit 6 section 6.2: teacher keys, teacher-held design-cycle components and held independent paper. Prepare separate component notes, Classroom design sketches, Separate classroom goal and limit cards and formative record. Ask material allergies privately. Thirty children with one teacher use labeled schematic records without handling. Physical materials require adequate existing supervision, protection and six existing wash places. Add no facilities or staffing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Honor day 103's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return the named papers privately: s06-X103, s06-P103-sort, s06-P103-limit, s06-P103-repair-A, s06-P103-repair-B, s06-P103-repair-C, s06-P103-retrieve, s06-P103-measure-v2. Error D: [[s06-P104-repair-D]]. More than ten misses makes this whole-class; otherwise visit the assigned group while others label existing criteria. Collect fresh slips. Appearance is separate from safety; never drink output.

#### 5 min. Safety and setup
Review prototype, media, criterion and constraint. Six groups of five use records by default: teacher draws covered portions and cloth layers, clearly invented for practice. If safe handling is available, inspect teacher-portioned clean sand and gravel, cloth squares and paper filter supports on spill trays. Wear individual splash goggles. No tasting, dust blowing or face touching. Stop for missing protection, damage or uncontrolled spills. Teacher alone portions materials; no environmental soil or runoff.

#### 25 min. Generate alternatives
Review design-cycle chart through teacher-held components. Use Classroom design sketches, [[s06-P104-design]], for two alternatives differing only in cloth-layer count. Each receives one quarter-cup sand and one quarter-cup gravel from equal shared portions, with matching paper support, funnel and outlet. One versus two cloth layers is the only change. [[s06-P104-feature]] labels it. Each child records a specific contribution, [[s06-P104-contribution]]. Teacher checks each against the same classroom material limit; no numerical forecast scoreboard. Pairs explain predicted water routes orally. An extra layer might retain material or slow flow; predictions need tests. Keep the supplied-forecast comparison unseen. Review rotating six heard: continue day 103's roster. Six 45-second reports take 4.5 minutes inside this part; passes remain unheard, all submit paper. Retain versioned sketches/quantities for days 105/106. Records supply no handling evidence.

#### 5 min. Cleanup
Records path: file drawings and review the pictured cleanup sequence without pretending it occurred. Handling path: two minutes close media and wipe trays/goggles in parallel. Thirty 30-second soap-and-water wash turns across six existing places take 2.5 minutes; half a minute resets places. Use sediment waste containers; keep solids from sinks. Goggle wipes, soap, water and drying supplies must be ready. Adult handles waste. Missing capacity selects records before class.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Close sketches, cards and displays. Reveal [[s06-X104-1]] on a half slip and [[s06-X104-2]] in the student Exit box. Read exactly, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection. After class: thirty slips at 40 seconds take twenty minutes; component pages at 10 seconds take five; sketches at 20 seconds take ten. Review the sole formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error F treats forecasts as reliability; error D infers safety.

### Other rooms
No kit or inadequate supervision: use invented schematic portions. No printer: copy sketch frame and stems. Construction and handling evidence remain absent on records work.

### At home
**Swap.** No safe materials, goggles or sink: use invented drawings. No printer: copy the sketch frame.
**Skip.** Public reports and group roles.
- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s06-P104-repair-D]] after day 103 feedback.
- **5 min. Prepare.** Adult checks allergies/protection or selects drawings.
- **15 min. Sketch.** Draw two cloth-layer alternatives; use [[s06-P104-feature]] and [[s06-P104-contribution]].
  **Say.** "How might another layer change movement?" Expect possible slower passage or more retention, needing tests.
- **5 min. Put away.** Adult closes media; wipe surfaces/goggles, then wash with soap and water. Drawing-only work needs filing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Child independently completes [[s06-X104-1]] and [[s06-X104-2]]. Return private feedback on day 105.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated reports, keeping both designs and cleanup. Longer: trace an existing predicted route without adding features.

### Words and supports
Review filter, prototype, media, criterion, constraint and prediction. Offer large drawings, neutral reading and individually labeled contributions. Group success does not establish each child's contribution.

### Tomorrow
Day 105 returns today's pages, sketches and E104. Errors D/F each get five minutes: appearance versus safety, then forecasts versus repeated evidence. Carry versioned quantities and exact listening roster into dry planning. No water is poured tomorrow.

### Credit
