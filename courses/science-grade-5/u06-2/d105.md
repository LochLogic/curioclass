# Day 105: Plan repeated fair tests

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the unit 6 section 6.2 opener. Separate unit 6 section 6.2: teacher keys and held independent paper. Use current rows for separate component-note keys. Set out Classroom design sketches, Separate classroom goal and limit cards, teacher-held design-cycle components, Dry control and trial-order strips, Separate measurement cards and original schematic, Cloudiness comparison reference and original schematic. For each group of five: two protected funnels, two empty cups, one half-cup-marked measuring cup and stable tray. Adult checks covered edges. No water poured today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Honor day 104's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return its pages, versioned sketches/quantities and exit privately. First five minutes: error D, [[s06-P105-repair-D]]. Next five: error F, [[s06-P105-repair-F]]. More than ten misses makes the component whole-class; otherwise visit assigned children while others audit labels. Collect fresh slips; log unresolved needs.

#### 10 min. Instruments and repeats
First three minutes: review line plots, one mark per trial, [[s06-P105-plot]]. Teach supplied half-unit spacing explicitly if needed; place measurements over ticks before marking. Review variable: a condition that can change, from day 52. Review milliliter (mL): a unit for water volume. Use [[s06-P105-measure]] with the separate 20 mL picture; do not convert to cups. Demonstrate eye-level cup reading from zero with half-cup steps. Check the half-cup target before plot rounding; between marks are estimates. Review ordered cloudiness categories; match depth, light and twenty-second settling, never average. One safe reread; persistent ambiguity is unresolved with reason. Invented backup stays separate. Use [[s06-P105-repeat]] to connect repeats with variation.

#### 17 min. Dry control stations
Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing; trial means one test. Six parallel groups rehearse separate controls on dry apparatus. Six minutes ordering paper strips replace the unbuilt tool's comparison job. Rehearse freshly stirred one-cup inputs, same pour position, ten-second pour inside a 60-second collection, identical initial prewet/drain and resets, alternating A1/B1/A2/B2/A3/B3. Fix prewet to one cup and drain for 60 seconds; between trials drain 60 seconds without replacing media. Timer measures water movement on trial days, not student speed. Match sample depth, light and settling. [[s06-P105-control]] records one control/reason, never a complete plan. Review design-cycle components. Start a blank retained table: trial, volume/cups, cloudiness, fault. Label A1/B1/A2/B2/A3/B3; leave results blank. Store approved dry controls and day 104 sketches for 106. Review rotating six heard: continue day 104's roster, six 45-second reports inside this part. Everyone submits paper; passes remain unheard. No conducting credit today.

#### 3 min. Put away
File sketches and serial strips by group. Return dry apparatus to stable trays. Adult checks and repairs edges; children never cut. No wet cleanup queue.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Cover practice and displays. Reveal [[s06-X105]] on a half slip. Read exactly, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection. After class: thirty plans at 40 seconds take twenty minutes; component pages at 10 seconds take five. Review the sole formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error E changes another condition; error F treats one result as reliable.

### Other rooms
No apparatus: draw separate instruments and control strips; children point through dry actions. No devices: paper supplies all comparison work. No printer: copy response spaces and keep the held case covered.

### At home
**Swap.** No cup/funnel: draw dry apparatus and marked scales. No printer: copy strips.
**Skip.** Public reports and group roles.
- **8 min. Repair.** Use [[s06-P105-repair-D]] and [[s06-P105-repair-F]].
- **10 min. Measures.** Use [[s06-P105-plot]], [[s06-P105-measure]] and [[s06-P105-repeat]].
- **8 min. Rehearse.** Point through separate controls; use [[s06-P105-control]].
  **Say.** "Which condition could hide the design's effect?" Expect an unmatched input, mixture, interval or reset, with its reason.
- **3 min. Put away.** File sketches; adult checks edges. Pour no water today.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Child independently completes [[s06-X105]]. Return private feedback on day 106.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated reports, keeping measurement and independent planning. Longer: audit an existing control for a precise reset rule.

### Words and supports
Review variable, fair test, trial, milliliter, cloudiness and design-cycle. Offer large marks, tactile strips and neutral reading; a scribe preserves the child's plan.

### Tomorrow
Day 106 returns today's pages and E105. Errors E/F each get five minutes in Return and repair: match a condition, then justify repeats. Send approved dry controls and day 104 sketches/quantities into construction. Keep planning evidence separate from conducting evidence.
