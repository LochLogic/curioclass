# Day 103: Define a filter problem

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the unit 6 section 6.2 opener. Separate unit 6 section 6.2: teacher keys, teacher-held design-cycle components and held independent paper. Use current rows for separate component-note keys. Set out Separate classroom goal and limit cards, Cloudiness comparison reference and its original schematic, Separate measurement cards and cylinder schematic. Bring day 99 handoff notes, returned day 102 papers and formative record. No water handling today.

### The day

#### 15 min. Return and repair
Honor day 102's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return the named papers privately: s06-X102, s06-X101-1, s06-X101-2, s06-P102-soil, s06-P102-status, s06-P102-repair-A, s06-P102-repair-BI, s06-P102-observed. Three five-minute rounds: error A, [[s06-P103-repair-A]]; B, [[s06-P103-repair-B]]; C, [[s06-P103-repair-C]]. Day 101 status notes share C's round; day 99 notes inform retrieval without extra minutes. More than ten misses makes the component whole-class; otherwise visit its assigned group while others work independently. Collect fresh slips; log unresolved needs.

#### 5 min. Retrieve comparisons
Review grade 4's lid alarm, BEND/ROLL/PRESS messages and three-design water-route model. Pairs use [[s06-P103-retrieve]] for four minutes. Review criterion: a way to check the need is met; constraint: the familiar limit on a design. Use one minute to reconnect goals, limits and matched comparisons. These are reminders, not earlier assessment cases.

#### 8 min. Define the problem
Review filter: a material that retains undissolved solid while liquid passes, from day 56. Review milliliter (mL): a unit for water volume, from day 31. Use [[s06-P103-measure-v2]] with the 20 mL picture. Define prototype: a model built to test a design idea; media: materials placed inside a filter. Classroom targets are ours, invented for practice: improve one cloudiness category, collect half a cup in 60 seconds, avoid bypass, use at most one cup of media. No target tests drinking safety. The illustrated reference is a schematic, not a photograph or calibrated measurement.

#### 12 min. Component practice
Pairs classify separate cards with [[s06-P103-sort]] and [[s06-P103-limit]]. Review design-cycle chart: define, sketch, build, test, compare. Teacher reveals components separately, adding failure evidence, one change and retest. Children explain stages orally, without a complete goal-and-cycle drawing. Teach cloudiness categories 0 to 3 as ordered descriptions, not equal intervals. Match depth, light and settling; ambiguity stays a range or unresolved, never averaged. Review rotating six heard: continue day 102's roster, six 45-second reports inside this part. Everyone submits paper; passes remain unheard. Error D confuses appearance with safety: never drink classroom-filter water.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Cover practice and cycle. Reveal [[s06-X103]] on a half slip. Read exactly, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection. After class, thirty drawings at 40 seconds take twenty minutes; component pages at 10 seconds take five. Review the record's only scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing evidence is not an inferred misconception.

### Other rooms
No devices: use original printed schematics. No printer: draw reference and instrument marks, copying stems exactly. No physical filter or water is required.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy separate cards and marked cylinder. No group: adult listens neutrally.
**Skip.** Public reports and teacher group visits.
- **10 min. Repair.** Use [[s06-P103-repair-A]], [[s06-P103-repair-B]] and [[s06-P103-repair-C]].
- **5 min. Recall.** Use [[s06-P103-retrieve]].
- **12 min. Components.** Use [[s06-P103-measure-v2]], [[s06-P103-sort]] and [[s06-P103-limit]]. Read cycle stages separately.
  **Say.** "Which observation would show the collection goal was met?" Expect half a cup in the stated interval. Appearance does not show safety.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Child independently completes [[s06-X103]]. Return private feedback on day 104.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated stage explanations, keeping repairs and exit. Longer: inspect the reference for ambiguity without inventing precision.

### Words and supports
Review criterion, constraint, filter and milliliter. Define prototype, media and cloudiness directly. Offer large marks, neutral reading and a scribe preserving decisions.

### Tomorrow
Day 104 returns today's pages and E103. Error D gets five minutes separating appearance from safety. Groups sketch alternatives before construction. Carry exact listening roster; check allergies, protection, supervision and wash capacity before selecting handling or records.
