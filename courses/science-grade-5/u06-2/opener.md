## Science and continuity

A criterion is a way to check the need is met. A constraint is the familiar design limit.
A prototype is a model built to test an idea. Media are materials placed inside a filter.
Review filter: a material that retains undissolved solid while liquid passes.
Review variable: a condition that can change. A milliliter is a unit for water volume.
Explain conventions directly, even when earlier written teaching exists.
Classroom targets are ours and untested: improve one cloudiness category, collect half a cup in 60 seconds,
avoid bypass, and use at most one cup of media. Keep all four visible during classroom work.
Illustrated cloudiness references are original model categories, not calibrated photographs.
Use equal sample depth, light and settling time. An ambiguous match is a range or unresolved.
Do not average categories or infer drinking safety.
