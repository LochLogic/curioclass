# Day 33: A changed food supply

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Print only rabbit-strips.md background and R33 table. Cover exit-33.md using exits-32-33.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys separate. Bring day 32 feedback and record.md. Keep all cases as paper work.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 32's s02-X32 and correction strips s02-P32-2 and s02-P32-3. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. First five: error E, matter returns rather than vanishes; do [[s02-P33-3]]. Second five: error J, released gases reach air; do [[s02-P33-4]]. Each block: two-minute familiar component contrast, two-minute individual correction, one-minute collection. If many, more than ten, showed that error, work together; otherwise target that group. Everyone repairs a component, without reconstructing the hedge model.

#### 5 min. Record boundaries

Day 32 located different needs; now compare a changed resource supply. Read rabbit-strips.md's short background, supported by Source S02-RABBIT: rabbits eat plants, compete for resources and have predators in Australia. Point to the report title and pages as its locator. Then announce R33: invented for practice, with no measured populations. Its predator-free patch is made up. Review predator, grade 3 day 59. Explain introduced here: newly brought into this patch. Discuss organism relationships without blaming peoples.

#### 16 min. Resource comparison

First minute: return day 32's s02-P32-1 feedback. Read R33 strips A to D aloud, keeping them separate. Review day 32's resource meaning and food web: food-to-eater routes. Pairs compare A with B for five minutes. **Ask.** [[s02-P33-1]] Expect B, with its less-uneaten-grass evidence. Each child records independently. Separately inspect C for five minutes, linking the resident eater to its food. Final five: preserve the separate locators and resolve unsupported claims. No predator addition or completed two-consequence diagram is practiced.

#### 10 min. Conditional claims

Review observation and inference, grade 4 day 16: constructed statements are not class observations. **Ask.** [[s02-P33-2]] Each child writes one conditional dependency statement with a strip label. Explain conditional: it may happen if the stated conditions hold. Expect less grass may leave less food for the resident eater, C. Partners each explain for forty-five seconds, concurrently. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns within five minutes; name who. Passing is allowed. Collect all thirty statements and locators for feedback. Keep the food-energy question open for tomorrow.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X33]] only now, including its supplied model. Read the exact stem neutrally. Children revise independently on half slips. After class: thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen minutes marking; ten more minutes for practice feedback. Review the single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error K says only the newcomer can change. Missing links invite follow-up, not an automatic letter. Accept conditional, supported alternatives.

  **Say.** "Which record supports this dependency?" Expect B for reduced grass after rabbit feeding.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports; preserve separate resource comparisons, individual statements and exit. Longer: qualify the statement if grass growth changes.

### Words and supports

Review predator, resource, food web, observation and inference. Introduced means newly brought here; conditional means dependent on stated conditions. Read records aloud; accept dictated individual paper.
