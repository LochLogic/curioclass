# Day 32: Locate different needs

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md. Lay out partial-meadow.md and retained meadow-cards.md tables, not their response sections. Cover exit-32.md using exits-32-33.md. Keep teacher-keys.md, practice-teacher.md and generated keys separate. Bring day 31 feedback and record.md. Use no living materials.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 31's s02-X31. Review return and repair, grade 4 day 17. First five: error E, fragments retain matter; do [[s02-P32-2]]. Second five: error J, gases cross living boundaries; do [[s02-P32-3]]. Each block: contrast two minutes, individual correction two, collect one. If many, more than ten, showed that error, work together; otherwise target that group. Everyone corrects individual paper; no complete exit model is demonstrated.

#### 6 min. Prerequisite support

Day 31 asked where matter returns; today locate different organisms' needs. Review producer, consumer and decomposer, day 28, using the role meanings. Review day 23's exchange: gases and water enter organisms; gases, water and wastes leave. Review day 29's matter cycle: environmental matter can be reused. Explain food web directly: linked feeding routes from food to eater. Keep M1 and M2 separate. Review predator, grade 3 day 59: an animal that hunts other animals for food. N2 supplies this role here.

#### 15 min. Needs locator

First minute: return day 31's s02-P31-1 and s02-P31-2 feedback. Announce and read the tables: invented for practice. Six groups of five inspect N1 to N5 as separate components. Review kindergarten's what a place gives: food, water, air, light, land. Resource means a needed supply. Model locating N4's water-to-roots link and tag. **Ask.** [[s02-P32-1]] Each child chooses another component and writes independently. Compare different needs without joining a full return model. If a link is reversed, trace matter from resource to receiver.

#### 10 min. Evidence hearing

Review observation and inference, grade 4 day 16. These supplied links are invented, not class observations. Partners each explain their locator for forty-five seconds, concurrently. Review rotating six, grade 4 day 16: hear six forty-five-second turns within five minutes; name who in record.md. Children may pass. Others check their own resource, receiver and locator. Collect all thirty locator notes for descriptive feedback; teacher retains the separate component chart.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X32]] only now. Read the exact stem neutrally; children model independently on half slips. After class: thirty times thirty-five seconds, seventeen minutes thirty seconds marking; ten more minutes for practice feedback. Review the scale from grade 4 day 16: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error E denies retained matter; error J denies gas exchange. An omitted arrow invites a question before assigning a letter.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy component tables and individual response spaces; keep the exit covered. No display is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the card tables and response spaces if printing is unavailable.

**Skip.** Group sorting and the public hearing.

- **10 min. Repair.** Return day 31 feedback. Do [[s02-P32-2]] and [[s02-P32-3]].
- **6 min. Meanings.** Read the separate role and exchange cards together.
- **10 min. Locate.** Point to N4's water and roots. Do [[s02-P32-1]] using another strip.
  **Say.** "Which resource does this organism need?" Expect the chosen organism's resource and strip label.
- **5 min. Explain.** Let the child explain the link; keep separate components separate.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s02-X32]] alone. Discuss with the teacher answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports; retain repairs, meanings, individual locator and exit. Longer: explain why a component needs its resource.

### Words and supports

Review producer, consumer, decomposer, predator, exchange and matter cycle. Explain food web directly. Read tables aloud; accept dictated labels on individual paper.
