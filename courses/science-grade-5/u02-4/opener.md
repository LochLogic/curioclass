# Section 2.4: Needs and changing relationships

## Purpose and science

A resource dependency connects one organism to something it needs. Different organisms have different needs and linked food routes. A changed food supply can affect more than one organism. These are conditional predictions, not guaranteed counts. Predator means an animal that hunts other animals for food. Review the established meaning. The real rabbit report describes plant feeding, competition and predators. R33 alone supplies the invented predator-free local setting.

## Running the section

Day 32 receives ten repair minutes for E31, then supplies role/exchange explanations directly. Each learner locates one resource link on a partial web. K returns on day 34 in five minutes.

Every student may pass in public talk. Hear rotating six and mark who, with paper evidence from everyone. Planned-only prerequisite records are not proof of completed teaching; supply direct illustrated explanations. Read records aloud before independent reading. Student pages stay within 350 words, with plain printed stems and 15-word sentences.

## Safety and alternatives

A room without specimens uses the identical records. Read aloud, enlarge or describe diagrams, or scribe onto individual paper. No personal home, illness or disaster accounts.

Handling days use the equipment and protection in their day blocks. Check plant/soil/mold and nonlatex-material precautions privately. Adult handlers use eye protection for splash/dust hazards, gloves and apron; wash afterward. Missing protection or safe washing means paper records. Never taste or sniff samples. Never look at the sun. Teacher stores or disposes of deteriorating closed containers under school procedures.

## Common errors and evidence

E/J: missing returns or denied gas exchange. K: new eater affects nobody else. Practice uses separate dependency locators, never a new-predator two-consequence revision.

The practice sheets keep evidence components separate. Do not demonstrate a complete held answer. Use the source chart to distinguish observation, source mechanism and invented practice. General facts are checked in the course's S02 source rows. Exact practice species relationships and numbers are constructed unless the card says historical.
