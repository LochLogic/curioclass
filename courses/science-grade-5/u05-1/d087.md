# Day 87: Revisiting the mountain explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 86's ticket and s05-X86-2 Exit box, s05-P86 ordering record, s05-P86-climate, s05-P86-key, s05-P86-recheck and s05-P86-extension. Keep originals and annotations. Prepare two covers for separate action strips, unit 5 evidence notes and the question board. Hold the day 87 exit unseen. Separate exit-key.md from copies. Retain record.md and the source-labeled anchor records.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
If many made error A in day 86's exit, use five minutes to review the difference between names and an action on separate strips. Otherwise teach that group while others locate supporting tags. Next five minutes: error C, unseen air excluded, uses the supplied bag account again; air occupies space even unseen. Over ten matching misses means whole class. Return s05-P86 and climate notes within these routes. Describe timescale or key gaps privately; preserve all original marks. No extra climate lesson is promised.

#### 8 min. Worked sequence
Review the Cascades records and day 86's correct action strips. Read supplied motion records as invented for practice; the regional west/east contrast has Source S05-cascades. Use [[s05-K87-stages]], then reveal [[steps:s05-K87-stages]] one at a time. Children point without copying a finished explanation. **Ask.** [[s05-K87-stages]] Expect the two separate traces in its steps. Keep the land-air connection to two systems; water gets a separate trace. Do not revise a flawed statement here.

#### 15 min. Apply orally
Paper replaces the unavailable rain-shadow display for all fifteen minutes, with zero digital minutes. Use [[s05-P87]]. First show approach and clouds/rain; cover rising air. Then show rise and continued travel; cover clouds/rain. For each round, allow two minutes to predict, ninety seconds for alternating forty-five-second partner turns, and ninety seconds to uncover and locate evidence. All fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Final five minutes: children record stage order and a locator on unit 5 evidence notes; teacher updates the consensus board. Reveal [[steps:s05-P87]] after both predictions. If unsupported guesses persist, return to the bordering strips.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear six forty-five-second turns; passing is allowed. Use the last half minute to collect thirty stage-order slips and evidence notes. Keep the teacher's consensus separate from individual evidence. Use the single familiar formative scale; hearing partners does not mean the teacher heard everyone.

#### 7 min. Paper exit
Reveal [[s05-X87]], the first contradicted-explanation revision. Read its invented label aloud. Children revise alone without strips or frames. Collect thirty half tickets. After class allow thirty seconds per child, fifteen teacher minutes, using exit-key.md. Inspect thirty practice packets for thirty seconds each, fifteen additional minutes. Look for the action, evidence and continuing air; error A names parts without connecting them. Exclusion of air gets a descriptive note here. Return all work on day 88.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the correct strips, use blank paper covers, and read record labels. A neutral scribe records the child's choices. Retain the same paper exit; no simulation is needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No strip copies: write each correct stage separately and cover it with paper.

**Skip.** Public reporting and class collection.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 86's feedback together; point to the supporting strip.
- **8 min. Sequence.** Try [[s05-K87-stages]], then read its steps. Trace air and water separately.
- **15 min. Predict.** Use both fixed covers in [[s05-P87]]. Child predicts before each reveal, then records stage order.
  **Say.** "What shows air continuing past the ridge?" Expect the continued-travel strip, supported by its record.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice; give [[s05-X87]] alone. Keep the original and return feedback next meeting.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public reporting, retaining both predictions and exit. Longer: locate another supporting observation for a familiar stage.

### Words and supports
Review moist air, ridge, slope and interaction. Provide large separate arrows. Frame during practice: "My stage __. Evidence __." Remove it for the exit.

Day 88 returns day 87's s05-X87 and s05-P87 notes. Error A, names without an action, receives its first five-minute repair. The next five minutes preserve independent evidence correction. Then compare ocean interactions with the mountain-air example.
