# Climate readiness: teacher key

Teacher only. The long-term map describes climate: P usually wet, Q usually dry.
Yesterday is one weather record; it cannot overturn the many-year pattern.
Distinguish daily-weather dependence from map-key inversion.
A response using yesterday to rename climates is the daily-weather gap, not automatically a key inversion.
Do not repeat the check on 85. Use three minutes timescales, four symbol matching, three fresh oral comparisons on 86.
