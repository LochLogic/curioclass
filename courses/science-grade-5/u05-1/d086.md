# Day 86: Following air across land

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 85's tickets, s05-P85, s05-P85-retrieve, s05-P85-sort and unit 5 evidence notes. Sort day 84's climate readiness into timescale and key-reading gaps. Prepare unit 5 climate support sheet and separate climate-support-key.md. Cut action strips apart; retain anchor records. Keep the half ticket and the lower independent block on side 1 covered. Separate exit-key.md from child copies.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
If many made error A in day 85's exit, use the first five minutes for a whole-class action move; otherwise teach that group while others sort component labels. Use [[s05-K86-arrow]], then [[steps:s05-K86-arrow]]. Second five: error C uses the supplied bag account from day 85, read afresh. Air occupies space even unseen. Over ten matching gaps means whole class. Children add a correction beside originals. Preserve day 85's uncertainty; do not repair by copying a completed exit panel.

#### 8 min. Teach from evidence
Read anchor-records.md: the Cascades record describes a moist west and drier east. Distinguish the schematic from actual rainfall measurements. Introduce moist air, air containing water. Review day 85's land, air, ridge, slope and interaction. Reveal separate action-strips.md lines after reading the observations. **Ask.** [[s05-K86-arrow]] Expect [[steps:s05-K86-arrow]]. Explain clouds and rain accompanying rising air, then continued travel with less moisture. Trace water separately. No completed mountain profile is modeled.

#### 10 min. Climate support or extension
Use day 84's c2-R84-climate results, not a new readiness check. Missing results mean direct teaching; over ten gaps means everyone. Review grade 3 climate: the range of usual weather across many years, including year-to-year changes. Three minutes: [[s05-P86-climate]]. Four: review key, a box explaining marks, through [[s05-P86-key]]. Three: [[s05-P86-recheck]], choose a record for seasonal planning. After attempts, reveal their steps using climate-support-key.md for the first two. Others use [[s05-P86-extension]], compare timescales without a daily/usual contradiction task. Collect all support sheets for day 87.

#### 5 min. Evidence ordering
Pairs return to the top of side 1 for [[s05-P86]] using separate correct strips. Each partner has forty-five seconds simultaneously across pairs; two minutes to order, one to attach a locator, thirty seconds to retain the record. Do not annotate a profile. If labels replace actions, reread one motion strip.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear six forty-five-second explanations, then collect thirty ordering records. Public passing is allowed. Review one record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record only the six actually heard; no second scale.

#### 7 min. Paper exit
Reveal [[s05-X86-1]] on the half ticket and uncover side 1?s lower block: held-profile-86.svg with [[s05-X86-2]] in its Exit box. Read invented labels aloud; children annotate the profile and explain alone. Both responses use the same seven minutes. Collect thirty tickets and student pages. After class allow forty-five seconds per child, 22.5 teacher minutes, using exit-key.md. Inspect thirty practice packets for thirty seconds each, fifteen additional minutes. Record error A or error C only when supported; climate mistakes get descriptive notes. Return both items and all practice on day 87.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy separate strips and support key symbols, holding the exit profile unseen. Use tactile land contours or neutral scribing; children choose actions and evidence.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: draw separate strips; keep the exit profile covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and group routing.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 85's feedback; point to the separate land-air action strip.
- **8 min. Evidence.** Read the anchor observations; child orders [[s05-P86]].
  **Say.** "Which observation supports that action?" Expect a matching record label, not just parts.
- **10 min. Climate.** Try [[s05-P86-climate]], [[s05-P86-key]] and [[s05-P86-recheck]]. Explain the answers afterward.
- **5 min. Connect.** Child points to the continuing air strip and separate falling-water route.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s05-X86-1]] and [[s05-X86-2]] alone. Save both for feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public sharing, preserving support and exit. Longer: compare the two extension records again using their timescale labels.

### Words and supports
Frame: "Action __. Evidence __." Read all captions neutrally. Keep practice arrows separate; remove frames for the exit.

### Tomorrow
Day 87 returns day 86's ticket, s05-X86-2 Exit box, s05-P86 and climate sheets. Its ten-minute opening repairs error A, disconnected parts, and error C, air excluded. Climate notes join the matching route descriptively. Correct-stage predictions precede the first flawed-explanation revision.
