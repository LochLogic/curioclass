# Day 88: Ocean water acts on other systems

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return day 87's s05-X87 and s05-P87 stage-order notes. Prepare unit 5 evidence notes, action words and retained action strips. Keep the day 88 half ticket and student-page Exit box covered until independent work. Separate exit-key.md from child copies. Use record.md; do not distribute the ocean-cards.md cases or later assessment records as practice.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
If many made error A in day 87's exit, use five minutes to review an action between land and air on separate correct strips. Otherwise teach that group while others locate evidence tags. Over ten gaps means whole class. Spend five further minutes on individual evidence corrections beside original marks. Return s05-P87 within this route. Keep climate or reading gaps descriptive. Review the single scale, shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Preserve the original explanation; no full exit revision is rehearsed.

#### 8 min. Teach ocean interactions
Review land, water, air and living things; explain two at a time. Use [[s05-K88-actions]] and reveal [[steps:s05-K88-actions]] separately, with gestures and labels. Say these accounts are invented for practice, not class measurements. Review habitat, a place where living things find what they need. Floating plant life uses water as a living place. Explain that slow ocean warming and cooling reduces nearby air extremes; no seasonal comparison record is practiced. Introduce watershed, land draining to a common outlet. Define fresh water as low in dissolved salt, salt water as abundant in dissolved salt. Low salt alone does not establish drinking safety; no tasting or treatment activity.

#### 15 min. Evidence sorting
Pairs spend four minutes reading isolated action words in [[s05-P88]], four sorting them by affected system, two locating the teaching label on unit 5 evidence notes. Then use [[s05-P88-compare]]: two simultaneous forty-five-second partner turns, with thirty seconds to point to evidence. Last three minutes: retain each individual's match and locator. **Ask.** [[s05-P88-compare]] Expect [[steps:s05-P88-compare]]. Practice sorts words and compares familiar components orally; it never produces three new coastal explanation panels. If only names appear, return to the action word.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Hear six forty-five-second oral comparisons; passing is allowed. Collect thirty sorting records in the remaining half minute. Record who was heard without assuming partners were heard by the teacher. Return all records on day 89.

#### 7 min. Paper exit
Reveal [[s05-X88-1]] on the half ticket, then [[s05-X88-2]] and [[s05-X88-3]] in separate student-page Exit boxes. Read invented labels aloud. All three are independent paper responses within these same seven minutes. Collect both pieces from thirty children. After class allow forty-five seconds per child, 22.5 teacher minutes, using exit-key.md and the one formative scale. Inspect thirty practice packets for thirty seconds each, fifteen additional minutes. Error A omits actions; error C excludes unseen air. Hand back all three items and sorting records on day 89.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy isolated words on separate paper; keep the Exit box unseen. Use neutral dictation or enlarged labels. Printed accounts replace field readings; no equipment is handled.

### At home
**Swap.** No copies: write the isolated action words on slips; keep exit prompts covered.

**Skip.** Public reporting and class collection.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 87's feedback; locate the action strip together.
- **8 min. Connect.** Use [[s05-K88-actions]] and its steps. Explain watershed and fresh versus safe.
- **15 min. Sort.** Child answers [[s05-P88]]; compare one action through [[s05-P88-compare]].
  **Say.** "What changes in each connection?" Expect two actions, each linking its own pair of systems.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s05-X88-1]] and both separate Exit boxes, [[s05-X88-2]] and [[s05-X88-3]], alone. Keep both papers for feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce sharing, preserving teaching and three paper responses. Longer: compare another isolated action with the familiar mountain strip.

### Words and supports
Frame: "Water __. The affected system is __." Read labels neutrally; withdraw the frame before independent work. Keep each connection separate.

### Tomorrow
Day 89 returns day 88's three items, s05-P88 and s05-P88-compare sorting records. Its ten-minute Return and repair addresses error A, parts without actions, and error C, unseen air excluded, five minutes each. Then use an erosion tray or its observation fallback to revise water-land interactions.
