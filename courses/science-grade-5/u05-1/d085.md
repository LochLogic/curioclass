# Day 85: A blue Earth and contrasting slopes

## Teacher page

### Before class
Prepare unit 5 anchor records, component cards and evidence notes. Open the Earth photograph linked in anchor-records.md; keep its credit. Without it, use the original schematic, explicitly invented for practice. Keep the day 85 exit covered and exit-key.md teacher-only. Prepare record.md; initial ideas receive descriptive notes, without a scale mark.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook and question board
Day 84 ended with climate readiness; save those results for day 86. Show the Earth photograph, then the separate Cascades landscape record, then the invented dry-intake town. Read invented labels aloud. Review the question board: notices become questions. Before explanations, everyone writes [[s05-P85]]. Hear six volunteers, thirty seconds each; collect thirty first notices. Keep the town question general, without household disclosures. Do not draw a comparison model yet.

#### 3 min. Retrieval
Review grade 4 weathering: breaking rock material down; erosion carries loosened material. Children answer [[s05-P85-retrieve]] by pointing or writing. Reveal [[steps:s05-P85-retrieve]]. Review day 1: air is matter, has weight and occupies space. Explain the new supplied bag reminder, [[s05-K85-air]], then [[steps:s05-K85-air]]. No bag is handled. A miss gets these separate contrasts, not a scored diagnosis.

#### 8 min. Teach components
Review kindergarten's land, water, air and living things using separate component-cards.md close-ups. Introduce ridge, a high line of land; slope, land rising or falling; interaction, one system affecting another. Review the day 23 jar system-model: choose a boundary and connect parts through actions. Today select land and air; keep water observations separate. Review uncertainty: what evidence cannot settle yet. Show words and separate arrow marks, without completing the held wide/close panel.

#### 14 min. Evidence work
Pairs use [[s05-P85-sort]] and unit 5 evidence notes. Spend four minutes locating records, six sorting observations from questions, two alternating partner explanations, two preserving labels and open questions. Each partner has forty-five seconds; all pairs speak simultaneously. **Ask.** [[s05-P85-sort]] Expect [[steps:s05-P85-sort]]. If guesses replace observations, point back to the printed record. Keep the causes unsettled; no complete practice panel.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six forty-five-second turns take 4.5 minutes; passing is allowed. Use the remaining half minute to collect every evidence sheet and student page. Teacher keeps the board; children receive their notes on day 86. Hearing six does not establish thirty oral performances.

#### 7 min. Paper exit
Reveal only [[s05-X85]], exactly as printed in held-exits.md. Read neutrally; children draw alone. Collect thirty half tickets. After class allow thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes, for descriptive feedback using exit-key.md. Inspect thirty practice packets for thirty seconds each, fifteen additional minutes. Note missing actions as error A and exclusion of unseen air as error C. Retain uncertainty, rather than demanding the later mechanism. Return everything on day 86.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy component labels and prompts, leaving the exit covered. No photograph: read the labeled invented schematic aloud. No factual rainfall is measured today.

### At home
**Swap.** No photograph: use the labeled invented schematic. No copies: draw separate component labels.

**Skip.** Class collection and rotating speakers.

- **8 min. Notice.** Read the anchor records; child answers [[s05-P85]].
  **Say.** "What can you see, and what remains a question?" Expect an observation and an unsettled question.
- **5 min. Recall.** Try [[s05-P85-retrieve]] and [[s05-K85-air]]. Read the steps afterward.
- **15 min. Sort.** Use [[s05-P85-sort]]. Point to the source together; keep guesses separate. Explain ridge, slope and interaction using separate labels.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s05-X85]] alone. Keep the drawing for next meeting's feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: hear fewer public turns, preserving independent noticing and exit. Longer: locate another uncertainty in the same record.

### Words and supports
Read captions aloud. Offer large labels and neutral dictation. Frame: "My observation __. Record __. My question __."

### Tomorrow
Day 86 returns day 85's exit and s05-P85, s05-P85-retrieve and s05-P85-sort notes. Its first five-minute move repairs error A, names without action; its second repairs error C, unseen air excluded. Climate support uses day 84's results within its own ten minutes.
