# Day 111: compare original and revised evidence using every requirement

## Teacher page

### Before class
Use the current-day data key for all items; it supersedes shared keys. Separate teacher-keys.md and today's held-exits.md. Keep held-hopper-plot.md covered until the final eight minutes. Retrieve V baseline/retests, earlier A/B plots, goal-cards.md and the listening roster from 109. Set line-plot paper, pencils, second color, folders and the formative record. Shared notes are prompt masters; use today's page for component responses and independent Exit box.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Return privately: s06-P110-retests, s06-X110, s06-P110-dry, s06-P110-mark, s06-P110-repair-H.
Honor day 110's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return E110, retest records and component/repair pages privately. First five minutes: error E, changed versus matching pour position, [[s06-P111-repair-E]]. Next five: error G, erasing versus retaining a cloudier repeat, [[s06-P111-repair-G]]. More than ten misses uses that whole-class round; otherwise assigned repairs run beside single-slip checks. Keep actual readings unchanged. Collect corrections and log unresolved needs.

#### 5 min. Compare components
Review criterion: a way to check the need is met; constraint: the familiar limit on a design. Retrieve separate unit goals: improve appearance one category, collect at least half a cup in 60 seconds, avoid leaks; media limit one cup. Review prototype and system-model. Point to each goal separately on goal-cards.md. Give no complete recommendation model. Appearance alone does not establish drinking safety.

#### 17 min. Evidence stations
Teacher graphs actual V baseline/retests first, keeping group conditions separate. If needed, use V110 supplied records labeled invented for practice, without mixing them with measurements. Review line plots: one identified mark per volume, cup units and equal half-cup steps. Keep unresolved values beside the axis with reasons; preserve estimates. Cloudiness remains ordered and separate, never averaged.

Pairs audit single marks against serial evidence, [[s06-P111-dot]]. Separately inspect only the no-bypass criterion, [[s06-P111-criterion]], then switch components. Include another group's outcomes only with conditions named. Review failure: observed goal not met under a stated test condition. An absence of recorded bypass differs from proof of no possible bypass. Compare original/revised observations orally in separate components. Never graph and recommend a complete case during practice. Review design-cycle: retest evidence can reveal continued failure; an untested change remains a proposal.

#### 5 min. Share
Review rotating six heard; continue day 109's roster. Six 45-second component reports take 4.5 minutes; collect all thirty notes in 30 seconds. Passes remain unheard. Pass original V evidence to 112; never promise drinking safety or guaranteed future results.

#### 8 min. Paper exit
Cover practice. Reveal [[s06-P111-plot]] in the student-page Exit box for four minutes. Then reveal [[s06-X111]] on a half slip for three minutes; collect both in one minute. Read exact stems, invented for practice; no feedback between components. Keep the hopper record available during recommendation. After class: thirty paired responses at 50 seconds take 25 minutes; component pages at ten seconds take five. Review one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error F selects a first result; error D claims drinking safety. Missing goals/limits get descriptive feedback.

### Other rooms
No measured V evidence: use separate labeled V110 records, with conducting unobserved. No devices needed. No printer: copy blank axes first; hide hopper data until release.

### At home
**Swap.** Missing actual V evidence: use V110 records, labeled invented for practice. No plot paper: draw equally spaced ticks.
**Skip.** Public reports.
- **8 min. Repair.** Return day 110 work; use [[s06-P111-repair-E]] and [[s06-P111-repair-G]].
- **5 min. Goals.** Adult points to each separate classroom requirement.
- **14 min. Audit.** Adult plots retained V readings; child completes [[s06-P111-dot]] and [[s06-P111-criterion]] separately.
  **Say.** "Inspect one V trial for the no-bypass criterion." Expect its recorded observation, or unknown.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Reveal [[s06-P111-plot]] first, then [[s06-X111]]; give no feedback between them. Return both on day 112.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce duplicate audits, keeping every plotted reading and both independent items. Longer: identify an extra controlled test needed for a stronger claim.

### Words and supports
Review criterion, constraint, line plot and design-cycle. Enlarge axes and describe records neutrally. A scribe records the child's decisions without checking them.

### Tomorrow
Day 112 returns E111, the held plot and component/repair pages. If many selected a first result, error F gets five minutes; drinking-safety claims, error D, get five more. Send all V baseline/retests into a limited claim combining community evidence and filter results.
