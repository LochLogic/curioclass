# Day 110: revise a filter and retest under matching conditions

## Teacher page

### Before class
Separate teacher-keys.md, held-V110.md, fallback-V110.md and today's held-exits.md; conceal V. Retrieve A/B sketches and controls. Prepare six protected V stations, replacement cloth, stirred classroom soil-water, half-cup cups, timers, labeled cups, trays, goggles and nonlatex gloves. Set towels, sediment waste, wipes and soap/water. Check allergies, eyewash, edges, supervision and six wash places. Missing capacity selects records. Use the current-day data key for all items; it supersedes shared keys.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Honor day 109's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return E109 and component/repair pages, including s06-P109-change, privately. If many proposed unrelated changes, contrast stable support with color change, error H, [[s06-P110-repair-H]]. Otherwise assign repairs beside sketch inspection. Collect corrections; log unresolved needs.

#### 5 min. Safety and readings
Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Adults alone repair edges and supply clean classroom mixture. Goggles/gloves on; stable trays, no tasting, dust blowing or face touching. Stop for damage, missing protection or uncontrolled spills. Review half-cup marks: [[s06-P110-mark]]. Check the threshold before rounding; between marks means estimate. Missing cup: draw the labeled no-kit surface below its half-cup mark, invented for practice. Review cloudiness references at equal depth/light after twenty-second settling. One safe reread, then unresolved/reason, never invented zero.

#### 11 min. Dry revision components
Six parallel groups reposition/replace the single earlier A/B feature. Each child completes [[s06-P110-dry]]. Review prototype and design-cycle. Inspect one fixed control at a time; adults check protected apparatus. Store A/B revisions separately, explicitly untested. No repeated trials or causal route-writing rehearsal. V evidence remains covered.

#### 12 min. Fresh practical exit
Reveal [[s06-P110-retests]] in the student-page Exit box. Read exactly, baseline invented for practice. Two minutes inspect/adjust V cloth, two prewet/setup, three two-minute trial/reset cycles, two readings/rereads. Match one-cup prewet and 60-second drain. Adult stirs/dispenses each one-cup input; ten-second pours inside equal 60-second collections. Begin the next 60-second drain while comparing a separate sample after twenty-second settling. Keep depth/light fixed; replace no media. Rotate pour/time/volume/category/fault roles; teacher observes each group thirty seconds within cycles on action-roster.md. Each child records all trials and an actual action without feedback. Preserve baseline and failures. Records path: child chooses adjustment before sequential fallback-V110.md release, labeled invented for practice. Conducting remains unobserved; never merge supplied with actual results.

#### 7 min. Cleanup
Two minutes sediment waste/surfaces, one parallel goggle wiping/bagging, four glove removal and soap/water washing. Thirty 45-second turns at six places take 3.75 minutes. Review goggles wiped and bagged. Keep protection through splash risk; no solids down sinks. Adults store V evidence, separate from untested A/B.

#### 5 min. Independent exit record
Without feedback, reveal [[s06-X110]] on a half slip. Use retained baseline/retests for the drawing, four minutes plus collection. No hints or feedback accompany the independent drawing. After class: thirty combined records at 45 seconds take 22.5 minutes; component pages at ten seconds take five. Review one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error E changes conditions; error G hides a repeat. Unobserved actions stay absent.

### Other rooms
Inadequate adults, wash places or protection: use reserved V records after the adjustment decision. No devices required. No printer: copy held records at release, retaining baseline and responses.

### At home
**Swap.** Missing safe apparatus/protection: release fallback-V110.md after the adjustment decision. Conducting stays unobserved.
**Skip.** Group roles and station circuit.

  **Say.** "Retest the cloth adjustment three times, keeping the baseline record." Expect every result and actual actions, or labeled supplied records.

- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Complete [[s06-X110]] without feedback. Return both records on day 111.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce dry discussion, preserving independent trials and cleanup. Longer: inspect one control's precision after evidence collection.

### Words and supports
Review prototype, cloudiness and system-model. Enlarge marks and adapt grips; log neutral assistance. Never suggest the independent revision.

### Tomorrow
Day 111 returns E110, retests and component/repair pages. If many changed conditions, error E gets five minutes; hidden repeats, error G, get five more. Carry baseline and every retest into plots and criterion audits; keep A/B success unclaimed.
