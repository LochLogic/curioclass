# Day 14: Investigate before naming a cause

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and d014-key.html private. Prepare day 13 feedback. Inspect bag seams; dry bag and tray exteriors. Set out the scale, unchanged dry check object and towels. Print the anomaly sheet headings integrated below. Cut N13 anomaly strips; assign one per group, keeping the three systems separate. Each strip is invented for practice. Do not prepare deliberate spills or collector-gain demonstrations. Hide exit cards.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 13's s01-X13, s01-P13-1 and s01-P13-2. First five minutes: if many show error A, review day 1's air-as-matter evidence, then point out possible open/retaining routes from day 13. Second five: error F, trace actual gram values to bar endpoints. Ready children locate an unresolved raw trial; no one rewrites readings to make a pattern.

#### 20 min. Investigate one anomaly

Review day 8's complete weighing boundary, whole-gram resolution and zero/check routine. Six groups have two-minute scale visits, twelve minutes total. At each visit zero empty, verify the unchanged dry check object, and read the same complete dry sealed bag repeatedly at 1 g steps. Record actual values and actors. Keep variation; no forced agreement. No tasting or opening bags. Leak: stop, towel the spill and use records. Retry unstable readings once within the visit; retain the attempt and reason.

Everyone uses [[s01-P14-1]] on one assigned record. Eight other minutes hold inventory comparison and annotations while visits rotate. Use an actual unresolved sequence when available; otherwise N13 separately, printed and announced invented for practice. Its three separate systems show repeated 101/102/101 g, a documented 123/120 g spill, or 134/130 g after removing a 4 g support. One child investigates one strip. No matching grid, paired-offset example or collector-gain record appears.

Look for an observation separated from a possible cause. A documented spill supports one explanation but may leave its amount uncertain. The removed-support record changes the weighed boundary. Require a controlled next action; record no outcome that was not observed.

#### 9 min. Evidence hearing

Review rotating six heard, from grade 4: six voluntary 45-second accounts fit five minutes; record names. Everyone locates evidence and finishes an individual annotation in the other four minutes. Review second-color revision from grade 3: keep original observations and initials. Error G: separate a lower reading from its proposed cause. After class allow 30 times 15 seconds, seven-and-a-half minutes feedback. Return annotations day 15.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Hide practice. Reveal [[s01-X14]] alone; every child writes independently. After class allow 30 times 25 seconds, twelve-and-a-half minutes marking on the formative record continuation's single categorical scale. Keep error letters separate. Return day 15.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Captains towel dry surfaces. Teacher stores intact sealed bags and removes leaks.

### Other rooms

No scale or safe bags: use one N13 strip, labeled invented for practice; firsthand measuring remains unobserved. No projector: share paper. No printer: copy one strip and exact hidden exit.

### At home

**Swap.** Missing scale or bags: use one invented N13 strip. No printer: copy that strip and hide the exit.

**Skip.** Six-group queues and public hearing.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 13 feedback; trace an endpoint.
- **15 min. Investigate.** Do [[s01-P14-1]] for one assigned strip. Keep the original record unchanged.
  **Say.** "What is observed, and what remains uncertain?" Expect: exact readings are observed; an unchecked cause remains possible.
- **10 min. Evidence.** Point to the observation and controlled next action. Revise your explanation beside the original in another color.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X14]], answered alone. Discuss after collection.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public reports, retaining observations, controlled next action and exit. Longer: explain which observation could separate two remaining causes.

### Words and supports

Review: anomaly, weighing boundary, whole grams, graph endpoints and second-color revision. Read records aloud without suggesting causes. Allow enlarged records or a scribe. Public passing is welcome.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 returns s01-X14 and s01-P14-1. Its five-minute Return repairs error G's unsupported gas claim and error D's omitted support orally. Then rehearse familiar evidence and the ball explanation, without written practice revision.
