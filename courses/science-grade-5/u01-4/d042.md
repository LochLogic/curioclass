# Day 42: Matter behind changed appearances

## Teacher page

### Before class
Day 41's exit ticket feedback already returned after class on day 41.

Read check1-42-teacher.md; keep it private. Prepare two model panels and blank gram axes, 0 to 200 g, small divisions 5 g. Retrieve day 16's original practical record unchanged, including missing-action notes. Cover models and answer displays. Give each child a private check1-record.md. Confirm day 41 written task papers already carry feedback; keep them stored.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Pick up day 41's handoff: particle storyboards and weight-evidence graphs begin today. Unresolved unit 2 notes remain in folders. Announce all supplied cases and readings as invented for practice. Review only procedures: work independently, retain first marks, neutral reading or scribing is available. Explain panel and axis locations without a science example. No public turns or live measurements today.

#### 14 min. Particle storyboards

Give seven minutes each for [[c1-E42a]] and [[c1-E42b]]. Students create annotated models, joining unseen matter with the visible records and contrasting scales. Do not coach an explanation. Neutral reading preserves the demand; a child may dictate their own labels privately. These cases remain unseen until this part.

#### 18 min. Weight graphs

Students answer [[c1-E42c]] independently. Twelve minutes graphing and six for captions. The supplied records replace unsafe or impractical live heating and cooling, explicitly as invented records. They provide graphing and interpretation evidence only. No instrument reading is claimed as observed today. Keep each caption beside its change. Read a stem without emphasizing which apparatus to include.

#### 8 min. Measurement note

Students answer [[c1-E42d]]. Keep original readings visible while they propose a test of the claim. Allow prose or an annotated remeasurement diagram; score its evidence and fair comparison, not writing length.

#### 2 min. Collection

Collect all paper, including the graph side. No additional exit. Keep answers covered through day 43. Carry day 16's firsthand weighing record forward unchanged, without recollecting or scoring it again; absent actions remain not yet observed. Today cannot replace them.

After class: thirty children times two minutes, sixty teacher minutes. Use check1-record.md and the separate answer key's sixteen components. One scale only: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark observed evidence, not drawing polish. Record explicit errors A to G; a blank response does not establish an error letter. Write one private next step, then route these papers to day 44's Private feedback. Keep the practical record separate in its original form.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy stems and blank panels exactly, with ruled axes. No projector or equipment is needed. A scribe records the child's independent words without suggestions.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the page and draw blank axes on ruled paper. Use a folder for retained papers.

**Skip.** Class collection and public talk.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Give [[c1-E42a]], [[c1-E42b]], [[c1-E42c]] and [[c1-E42d]]. Say the records are invented. Read words neutrally if needed. Keep the answer key closed.
- **5 min. Save the evidence.** Put every first answer and graph together. Preserve day 16's original weighing record separately. Keep feedback until day 44.
  **Say.** "Is every page together?" Expect the two models, graph and measurement note, without correcting them.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: spread independent time across the same items; omit none. Longer: allow rereading without coaching.

### Words and supports

Review particle-model, unseen parts, grams, complete system and graph from unit 1. Read aloud neutrally; allow enlarged axes, drawing, dictation or home-language explanations. Do not supply science labels.

### Tomorrow

Day 43 uses fresh plant-evidence cards and brief matter and energy arrow samples. Today's marked papers wait for day 44.

### Credit

Ours. Source C1-AIR checks unseen air; Source C1-TOTAL checks retained matter. All supplied cases and measurements are invented for practice.
