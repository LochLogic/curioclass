# Day 15: Rehearse the evidence account

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and d015-key.html private. Retrieve day 14 tickets and annotations. Display familiar syringe, recovered-salt, melting, freezing and salt-mixing records separately. Retrieve the original ball account. Preserve dates and actual/invented labels; supplied fallback records remain explicitly invented for practice. Prepare removable evidence tags on the teacher's model. Print locator headings integrated below and hide the exit. Do not show tomorrow's assessment cases.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 14's s01-X14 and s01-P14-1. If many show error G or error D, point through the familiar removed-support evidence: the object left the weighed boundary. Review day 14's observation/cause distinction. Children explain orally or point; no practice writing revision. Ready children locate the supporting inventory.

#### 21 min. Consensus rehearsal

Review consensus: a shared explanation supported by evidence, from grade 4. Review day 7's retained air and clear-liquid account. Five three-minute partner rounds take fifteen minutes. Rounds use syringe compression, recovered salt, melting, freezing and salt mixing, separately. Within each round both partners take 45 seconds; the remaining 90 seconds locate and compare the familiar evidence. No new combined change-record card appears.

Review day 3's compressed air: retained unseen parts occupy less space. Review day 6's salt residue: clear liquid can retain dissolved parts. Review days 9 to 11's complete graphs: changing appearance need not change total grams. Error B confuses symbolic dots with visible grains. Error E assigns created or lost matter to temperature alone. Oral explanations must connect their account to the recorded observation.

Six teacher synthesis minutes attach removable oral evidence tags to the existing class model. Retain original drawings unchanged. Repeated patterns support the explanation while whole-gram readings leave smaller changes unresolved.

#### 15 min. Anchor explanation

Review day 1's flat and pumped ball. Partners rehearse why added air explains the gain: air has weight and occupies space. Give each child one familiar evidence link from the five rounds. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously; both take 45 seconds. They then compare their oral account against earlier evidence, without drafting or revising writing.

Review rotating six heard from grade 4. Hear six 45-second voluntary turns inside five minutes; note names. All children complete [[s01-P15-1]] by circling an existing record and pointing to its evidence. Remaining time permits evidence location and teacher follow-up. No speed target. After class allow 30 times 10 seconds, five minutes descriptive locator feedback, returned day 17.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Hide familiar models. Reveal [[s01-X15]], fresh individual written evidence on blank outlines. No dots or answer captions are preprinted. Collect before discussion. After class allow 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes marking on the formative record continuation's single categorical scale; add error letters separately. Hold feedback until after day 16 assessment, returning day 17.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to separate paper records. No printer: copy locator choices and exact exit with blank outlines; hide it until use. Missing class observations: retain their labeled invented fallbacks.

### At home

**Swap.** No class display: use familiar folder records. No printer: copy locator choices and the hidden exit outlines.

**Skip.** Public reports and group rotations. You are the speaking partner.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 14 feedback. Point through the removed-support record.
- **15 min. Rehearse.** Discuss syringe, salt residue, melting, freezing and salt mixing separately.
  **Say.** "Which observation supports your explanation?" Expect: a specific reading, retained air or recovered salt.
- **10 min. Ball.** Rehearse why added air increased weight. Do [[s01-P15-1]] by circling familiar evidence. Keep drawings unchanged.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X15]] for independent work. Save feedback until day 17.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce synthesis reports; preserve both partner turns and exit. Longer: explain one limit of the evidence without inventing a new reading.

### Words and supports

Review: consensus, unseen parts, dissolved material, boundary and whole-gram resolution. Offer enlarged records and pointing; do not supply a fresh model. Public passing is welcome.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 independently assesses models, weighing and graphs. Hold today's error B/C/E feedback until day 17. No warm-up or coaching precedes assessment. Day 17 returns the locator and s01-X15 alongside day 16's assessment for routed repairs.
