# Day 12: Plan before mixing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and d012-key.html private. Retrieve day 11's tickets, logs and graphs. Lay out dry inventories and the unchanged check object. Ask privately about food and balloon allergies before tomorrow's handling. Inspect bottle necks and balloons; teacher alone fits balloons. Print N11 apparatus drawings, explicitly invented for practice, for missing or unsafe kit. Use trial-plan.md headings integrated below. No mixing today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 11's s01-X11, s01-P11-1 and s01-P11-2. First five minutes: three-minute complete salt inventory for error D, then two-minute recovered-residue link for error C. Review day 6's residue: clear liquid can retain unseen salt. Second five: error F, trace day 11's actual endpoint to its reading. If many need either move, lead it for everyone; ready children locate supporting evidence.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing, from grade 3. Review day 8's weighing boundary and zero/check routine. Teach open weighing boundary: gas can cross out. Gas-retaining weighing boundary includes the attached balloon. Anomaly means a result needing investigation.

Use [[s01-K12-1]], then reveal [[steps:s01-K12-1]]. Demonstrate zero and check-object verification separately. A lower reading alone cannot identify its cause. A small loss may escape a 1 g scale.

#### 16 min. Plan trials

Six groups author one assigned plan under blank headings, [[s01-P12-1]]. Three groups plan open trials; three plan retaining trials, one trial per group. Specify equal quarter-teaspoon baking soda and tablespoon vinegar portions, same scale, dry exteriors and retained supports. Each child writes a prediction with a reason. Include two readings, spill/leak checks, one safe retry and a separately labeled fallback. Never remove the empty holder. No competing completed-plan cards appear.

#### 8 min. Safety check

Visit six groups for one minute each; use two minutes for common directions. Children point through their own inventories. Teacher demonstrates fitting a dry nonlatex balloon, without mouth inflation. Tomorrow requires splash goggles through cleanup, rinsing water, eyewash and washing provision. Never a rigid lid or tasting. If kit or protection fails, use the invented N11 drawings and tomorrow's supplied record.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s01-X12]] alone. Children judge independently; no prior view of its plans. After class allow 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes marking. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen on the formative record continuation; add error letters separately. Plan feedback takes 30 times 10 seconds, five more minutes. Return both day 13.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect dry apparatus. Teacher stores substances separately for tomorrow.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: use N11, printed and announced as invented for practice. No printer: copy blank headings and exact exit stems, keeping the exit covered. No projector: point to paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Missing kit or scale: use the invented N11 drawings. No printer: copy headings and hide the exit.

**Skip.** Group visits and public reporting.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 11 feedback; trace one graph endpoint.
- **5 min. Inventory.** Do [[s01-K12-1]] with dry objects or drawings.
  **Say.** "What stays for the next weighing?" Expect: bottle, holder, spoon and tray.
- **15 min. Plan.** Complete [[s01-P12-1]]. Plan three trials per condition. Explain that no mixing happens today. You fit any dry balloon; never inflate by mouth.
- **5 min. Safety.** Point through protection, spill checks and the supplied-record alternative. Put equipment away.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X12]], answered alone. Use the private key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public planning reports; retain predictions, safety checks and exit. Longer: explain what equal whole-gram readings might leave unresolved.

### Words and supports

Review: fair test, boundary, whole grams, residue and endpoints. New: open, gas-retaining, anomaly. Allow dictated plans without supplying the science. Everyone may pass publicly.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 returns the plan and s01-X12. Error D omits apparatus; error G treats every decrease as gas escape. Its five-minute Return uses a dry inventory and alternative-cause check before small trials.

### Credit

Ours. N11 is invented for practice.
