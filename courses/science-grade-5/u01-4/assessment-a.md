# Assessment

Invented for practice.

Case A: A packing cushion is full of air and sealed. A book is set on it. The cushion flattens but stays sealed. Case B: A sealed pouch holds water. Inside is a small packet of a solid that dissolves. The packet opens. The solid spreads out, and the water turns clear. (a) Draw the cushion before and after the book. (b) Draw the pouch before and after the packet opens. Use dots for parts too small to see. (c) Explain what your dots show for each case. Say why you cannot see the parts.
