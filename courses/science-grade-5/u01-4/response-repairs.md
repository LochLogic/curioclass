# Day 17: familiar evidence repairs

Use only your assigned group. Keep original observations and initials.
Review second-color revision: change an explanation beside the original without erasing evidence.

## Model group: A, B, C

Use familiar ball, syringe and recovered-salt records.
Five minutes: locate evidence that air occupies space and has weight.
Five minutes: compare a visible grain with an inset of unseen parts.
Five minutes: locate residue evidence for retained dissolved matter.
Revise one flagged component in the remaining five minutes.

## Boundary and graph group: D, F

Use your familiar cup, card pieces and bag inventory.
Five minutes: trace every object through both weighings.
Five minutes: trace a familiar graph endpoint to its recorded value.
Use ten remaining minutes for one second-color revision and an evidence limit.

## Temperature group: E, F

Use familiar complete melting and freezing bag records.
Five minutes: separate changed appearance from changed total weight.
Five minutes: trace bar endpoints to original gram readings.
Use ten remaining minutes for one flagged revision and an evidence limit.

## Evidence-limit group: G, D, F

Use the familiar removed-support strip and your raw graph.
Five minutes: separate an observed reading from a proposed cause.
Five minutes: inventory apparatus and identify the removed support.
Five minutes: check an endpoint against its original reading.
Revise one flagged component in the remaining five minutes.

The teacher visits each group for four minutes within this twenty-minute part.
Annotated familiar evidence supports other group minutes. No new chemical trials.
After repairs, attempt one assigned fresh retry independently.
