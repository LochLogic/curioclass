# Day 17: Repair one gap, then try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and d017-key.html private. Sort day 16's assessment and held day 15 papers by largest gap. Prepare familiar evidence for four simultaneous groups: model A/B/C, boundary/graph D/F, temperature E/F, evidence-limit G/D/F. Print response repairs and one assigned fresh retry per child, keeping readiness separate and hidden. Select only that child's route from Side 1, Use current item stems, including the stated trapped air; shared cards supply response space only. Prepare both records; original points remain unchanged.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Privately return day 16's assessment and held day 15 s01-X15 and s01-P15-1. Give assessment four minutes, held work one. Separate original assessment points from formative marks and missing practical evidence. Assign one largest-gap group per child. If many show one gap, lead its familiar repair while other groups follow their supplied directions.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Use response-repairs.md with familiar folder evidence. Four four-minute teacher visits plus four routing minutes fit twenty; children stay in one group. Model A/B/C uses ball/syringe, scale inset and recovered salt, three five-minute moves. Boundary/graph D/F uses complete inventory and endpoints, two five-minute moves. Temperature E/F uses melting/freezing totals and endpoints, two moves. Evidence-limit G/D/F uses observed/proposed cause, removed support and endpoints, three moves. Other group time repairs one flagged original component.

Review second-color revision from grade 3: keep original observations and initials. Review days 7, 11 and 14's models, complete readings and controlled checks. Retain original points; add descriptive feedback only. Extension identifies an evidence limit. No chemical trials or rehearsed retry cases.

#### 10 min. Fresh retry

Assign one: [[s01-R17a]] for air/scale, [[s01-R17b]] for boundary/graph, [[s01-R17c]] for temperature, [[s01-R17d]] for dissolving, or [[s01-R17e]] for causal limits. All are invented for practice; each child receives only the assigned case. Eight independent minutes, then two to collect and note completion/help. After class allow 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes marking on the formative record continuation's single categorical scale. Return day 18. Paper never replaces missing firsthand weighing.

#### 10 min. Next readiness

Reveal [[s01-N17a]], then [[s01-N17b]], five minutes each. Every child answers on paper. Read exact stems aloud if needed, without hints or preteaching. Cases and pictures are invented for practice. Review targets are surroundings, grade 3 day 47, and needs meet conditions, grade 3 day 69; these labels guide teacher records after collection. Mark each prerequisite descriptively and separately after class: 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes total. Send support/extension notes to day 19. Add no unit 1 points.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy one assigned retry with blank drawing/graph space and both hidden readiness stems. Use supplied familiar evidence if original work is unavailable; record absence. No projector or wet kit is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy only the assigned retry and readiness cards. Missing original evidence: use familiar supplied records.

**Skip.** Group visits. Keep readiness covered until its part.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read day 16 feedback and held day 15 work privately.
- **15 min. Repair.** Choose the largest gap. Point to familiar evidence; revise one explanation in another color, keeping originals.
  **Say.** "Which observation supports your repair?" Expect: a relevant original reading or model-evidence link.
- **10 min. Retry.** Give only one: [[s01-R17a]], [[s01-R17b]], [[s01-R17c]], [[s01-R17d]] or [[s01-R17e]]. Let your child answer alone. Discuss later.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Reveal [[s01-N17a]], then [[s01-N17b]], five minutes each. Read without hints. Keep these notes separate from unit 1 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve one repair, independent retry and final readiness. Reduce public feedback; extension names an evidence limit, without extra checks.

### Words and supports

Review: second-color revision, boundary, unseen parts and endpoints. Keep readiness vocabulary support neutral. Dictation or enlarged cards preserve each task.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 returns the assigned s01-R17 retry in three minutes of Hook and retrieval. Then compare historical tree/soil records and make an initial matter account. Separate s01-N17a/b gaps go to day 19's ten-minute Prerequisite support/extension, not day 18. No repeat lab is promised.
