# Day 13: Keep every raw reading

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and d013-key.html private. Return-ready day 12 plans and tickets. Confirm private allergy checks, safe bottles and nonlatex balloons. Preportion each trial: quarter teaspoon baking soda, tablespoon food vinegar. Provide goggles for all nearby, rinsing water, eyewash, towels and labeled sanitizer. Four supervised washing points are required for child wet handling; otherwise teacher handles liquids. Print N11 and N12 fallbacks, both invented for practice. Keep exit hidden.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 12's s01-X12 and s01-P12-1. If many omitted apparatus, error D, trace the dry inventory. For error G, separate a lower reading from its proposed cause; missing apparatus remains an alternative. Review day 12's open and gas-retaining boundaries. Both moves share this five-minute inventory check; ready groups locate their controls.

#### 22 min. Paired trials

Use [[s01-P13-1]]. Review day 8's zero/check routine and whole-gram resolution. Goggles stay on from setup through nearby cleanup. No tasting, mouth inflation or rigid lids. Teacher alone fits balloons, powder held above vinegar before weighing. Open groups retain powder in a separate holder; every holder, spoon and support stays on its tray.

Three minutes: teacher fitting/checks. Six 45-second before visits and six after visits share one scale, nine minutes total. Zero empty, verify the unchanged check object, dry exteriors and read twice at 1 g steps. Tip powder only after the first reading; allow two minutes for reaction/observation. Three groups open and three retaining give three trials each. Compare each trial with its own start, not another bottle's starting weight. Actual actors alone initial.

Eight minutes: dry recording and washing, eight 45-second waves at four points plus two movement minutes. Everyone copies six raw pairs. Spills/leaks stop handling. Retain failed or unstable readings; retry once safely within the visit, then use N12 separately, printed and said invented for practice. Missing protection means records only. Never enlarge amounts to force a loss. Equal readings can miss a smaller loss.

#### 9 min. Pool and interpret

Post all raw pairs. Four minutes: each child graphs one assigned trial with [[s01-P13-2]], leaving unresolved readings visible. Review day 9's common gram intervals and exact endpoints. Review day 1's unseen air model orally: point to possible routes on apparatus, without paired graph/model panels. Review rotating six heard from grade 4: six 45-second voluntary accounts fit five minutes. Record names; everyone adds an uncertainty note. After class allow 30 times 10 seconds, five minutes paper feedback; return day 14.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Hide class records. Reveal [[s01-X13]] for independent graph-and-model work. After class: 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes marking. Use the formative record continuation's single categorical scale, with error letters separately. Return day 14.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Teacher collects residues under local disposal directions. Six captains wipe trays and sanitize five goggles each, following full labeled contact time. Teacher finishes after class if necessary; no reuse until dry. Keep protection during nearby wet cleanup. Anyone handling residues washes again.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: use N11/N12 as invented records. Adult-only mixing leaves student handling unobserved. No projector: share paper records. No printer: copy the log, grid and exact hidden exit.

### At home

**Swap.** Without scale, goggles, rinsing provision or safe kit, use N12, invented for practice. No graph paper: draw a grid. No printer: copy hidden stems.

**Skip.** Group queues and public reporting.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 12 feedback; point through the inventory.
- **15 min. Trials.** Do [[s01-P13-1]] with supplied N12 values. Keep their invented label. Do not initial invented handling.
  **Say.** "Could equal readings miss a smaller loss?" Expect: yes, the scale reads whole grams.
- **7 min. Graph.** Do [[s01-P13-2]] for one trial; preserve its values.
- **3 min. Put away.** Store records and equipment.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X13]] for independent work. Discuss after collection.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: use supplied records if safe trials cannot fit; retain uncertainty, graph and exit. Longer: locate evidence needed to distinguish two possible causes.

### Words and supports

Review: open, gas-retaining, boundary, whole grams, graph endpoints and unseen parts. Enlarge axes or allow dictation without hints. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 returns s01-X13 and both practice papers. First five minutes repair error A, gas treated as nothing; second five repair error F, mismatched endpoints. Then investigate one anomaly without forcing agreement.
