# Section 1.4: investigate a changed reading

## Purpose and continuity

Days 12 to 17 join unseen matter with complete-system graphs. Supply earlier planned models directly if unavailable. Observation is distinct from inferred cause. Teach open/retaining boundaries and anomaly on day 12. An anomaly is a result needing investigation.

Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Review consensus: a shared explanation supported by evidence, from grade 4. Review rotating six heard and second-color revision with original observations retained. Earlier planned practice does not prove mastery.

## Compare and investigate

Plan three small open trials and three retaining trials on one scale. Six groups each perform one assigned trial. Equal portions, retained supports and unchanged apparatus matter. Compare each trial with its own starting reading. Different starting apparatus weights need not match across conditions. Keep all raw values. Gas is matter; a collector stays inside the weighed boundary, while an open boundary permits matter to leave. A 1 g scale may miss small losses. Matching readings do not rule out smaller changes.

Day 14 investigates incomplete anomalies. A lower reading alone cannot identify gas escape. Check zero, unchanged objects, dry exteriors, leaks and spills. Never force agreement. Optional old soil-tray inspection is teacher only, two minutes during ready work.

## Prepare and protect

Print trial-plan.md, n11-apparatus.md, raw-log.md, n12-trials.md, anomaly-sheet.md, n13-anomalies.md and evidence-locator.md. Section 1.3's record continues formative marks. Keep all checks and keys private until use.

Inspect plastic bottles, trays, holders, measuring spoons and nonlatex balloons. Ask privately about allergies before foods or balloon contact. Each trial uses a quarter teaspoon baking soda and tablespoon food vinegar. Never increase quantities to force detectable loss. Teacher fits balloons with powder above vinegar before weighing. Open trials keep powder in a retained separate holder. No rigid lid, mouth inflation or tasting. Goggles protect adults and children from setup through cleanup. Keep rinsing water and eyewash accessible. Follow local disposal directions and secure substances.

One scale: six 45-second visits before and six after take nine minutes. Fitting/checks take three, reaction/observations two, washing/recording eight. Four washing points hold eight 45-second waves plus movement. If provision fails, teacher handles liquids; students share authentic readings without handling credit. Five-minute cleanup may leave goggle sanitizing for after class, with full contact time before reuse.

## Evidence, assessment and response

Six 45-second public turns fit five minutes; record names. All thirty return paper. Public talk may be passed. Day 15 partners rehearse familiar components orally, each taking 45 seconds. They circle an existing evidence reference, with no practice writing revision. Hold the fresh exit until after assessment.

Day 16 lasts 45 minutes. Print assessment-a.md, assessment-b.md and practical-card.md; keep assessment-key.md private. Teacher dates complete dry chilled-pouch readings twice beforehand. Six groups of five receive four observed minutes each, plus four transition minutes. Five 45-second personal turns fit each visit. Waiting students work independently. Final seventeen minutes finish paper. Do not coach or share answers. Room warmth only; inspect seams and dry exteriors. Failed practical evidence remains blank with reason. N14 permits paper interpretation only.

Record twenty original assessment points on this section's record.md: one per specified component, zero for observed unmet components, blank for unavailable evidence. Record incomplete possible points honestly. Formative marks stay on a separate sheet; preserve original assessment evidence for later checks.

Day 17 privately returns assessment and held exit. Four parallel groups use one largest-gap route each. Four four-minute teacher visits plus four routing minutes fit twenty. Familiar annotated evidence guides other group minutes. Retain originals; revise one flagged component in a second color. A ten-minute assigned fresh retry earns a formative mark, no second assessment score. Last ten minutes: separate prerequisite readiness records. Forward to day 19 support/extension. Return retry feedback on day 18.

A/B/C repair unseen matter and scale; D/F complete boundaries and graphs; E/F temperature claims; G/D/F unsupported causal claims. Five-minute components run inside simultaneous groups, never four sequential class rotations. Every child receives paper feedback.
