# Day 16: Explain unfamiliar changes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep assessment-key.md and d016-key.html teacher-only. Hold day 15's feedback. Earlier, record each complete dry chilled pouch twice, with date, pouch identity, scale and 1 g step. Inspect seams, leave expansion space and warm sealed at room temperature. Prepare six groups' earlier reading cards, scale and towels. Print the two-sided assessment and separate teacher assessment record. N14 is invented for practice and supplies no firsthand weighing evidence.

### The day

#### 28 min. Practical observation window and independent paper

No warm-up, teaching or coaching. Day 15's familiar rehearsal ends before these fresh cases. Start all children independently on [[s01-E1]], [[s01-E3]] and [[s01-E4]]. Announce supplied cases as invented for practice; actual pouch readings remain authentic. Read stems or scribe without science hints.

Six groups of five visit the station for four minutes each, twenty-four minutes, plus four transition minutes. Each child gets 45 seconds for [[s01-E2]]: zero empty, weigh the whole dry pouch, read twice and record in whole grams. Five turns take three minutes 45 seconds. Teacher observes all thirty, recording only performed actions. Children write their comparisons while waiting; they never share answers.

Wipe exteriors during transitions. No opening, tasting or external heat. Teacher removes leaks and wipes spills. Keep unstable attempts and their reasons; no coached repeat. Unavailable action evidence stays blank. No kit: use N14 for interpretation only; three practical-action cells remain blank, not yet observed. Paper cannot certify measuring.

#### 17 min. Independent paper completion

Allow nominally eight minutes for Q1, seven for Q3 and two for Q4; station waiting time also holds paper work. Collect during the final minute. No additional exit, peer checking or answer display.

After class allow 30 times two minutes, one hour marking. Q1: eight components; Q2: four; Q3: six; Q4: two. Use the separate assessment record's one-point-per-component scale, zero for observed unmet components, blank with reason for unavailable evidence. Report an incomplete denominator when needed. Preserve original points and papers; route largest gaps to day 17. Error A/B/C: unseen matter; D/F: boundary/graph; E/F: temperature; G/D/F: causal evidence.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: N14, explicitly invented, supports interpretation only. No printer: hand-copy exact stems, blank panels and gram axes. No projector is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No scale or safe pouch: use invented N14; weighing remains unobserved. No printer: copy exact stems, blank panels and axes.

**Skip.** Group queues and public reporting. Keep answers private throughout independent work.

- **28 min. Independent assessment.** Offer [[s01-E1]], [[s01-E2]], [[s01-E3]] and [[s01-E4]]. One four-minute station visit fits here; use the remaining time for paper. Prepare the dry chilled pouch's earlier readings yourself. Let it warm sealed at room temperature. Do not coach, open or taste.
- **17 min. Finish.** Allow independent paper completion, then collect. Keep original answers for day 17 feedback. Use the teacher key afterward; missing measurement remains missing.

### Shorter or longer

Keep the full assessment window. An unfinished response stays unfinished; add no questions or science hints.

### Words and supports

Use established access arrangements: enlarged print, exact-stem reading or a scribe. Record help without changing scientific demands. No vocabulary rehearsal today.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 privately returns day 16's assessment and held day 15 papers. Its Feedback and parallel Return routes address error A/B/C, D/F, E/F or G/D/F. Each child revises one gap, attempts one fresh retry, then completes separate next-unit readiness.

### Credit

Ours. Supplied cases are invented for practice; pouch readings are actual when measured.
