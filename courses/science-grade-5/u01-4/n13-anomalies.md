# N13: separate anomaly strips

Invented for practice. These are three separate systems.
Cut the strips apart. Investigate only your assigned strip.

## Strip 1

An unchanged sealed bag is weighed three times.
The whole-gram readings are 101 g, 102 g and 101 g.
The record gives no zero-check result.

## Strip 2

A complete system reads 123 g.
A spill is documented before the next reading of 120 g.
The record gives no weighing of the spilled liquid.

## Strip 3

A complete system reads 134 g.
Its 4 g support is removed.
The next reading is 130 g.
