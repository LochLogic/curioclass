# Unit 1 readiness look

## Teacher note

For the teacher. Day 1, 6 minutes for the child plus 4 to mark M and P, whole class on paper. Read the task aloud once. Give each child the envelope of six card pieces from the handout (readiness-pieces). This is an access look, not a score. Sort the sheets afterward on the record sheet: write M for a missing-piece gap (a piece left out or added) and P for a property-use gap (the writing says stiff is good but not why). Day 2 uses both letters.
