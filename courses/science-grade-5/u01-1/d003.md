# Day 3: Air takes less space

## Teacher page

### Before class
Keep the generated d003-key.html teacher-only.
Inspect six capped syringes and 30 pairs of protective eyewear. Set six fixed shadow sticks on marked spots, safely nearby. Use a paint pen, no nails. Check ruler intervals. Return day 2 feedback on s01-X2a and s01-X2b. Cover both checks; keep the teacher-only key separate. Display day 1 readings and retain dated drawings.

### Safety and set-up
No needles. Press gently away from faces; never remove a cap under pressure. Stop at resistance. Use N3 for leaks, damaged equipment or missing protection. Supervise outdoors; never look at the sun. Use the record if the route is unsafe.

### The day

#### 5 min. Syringe safety
Teacher demonstrates safe handling. All wear eyewear. Teach volume, space occupied, and mL, milliliter. Read at the plunger edge nearest the tip. One-milliliter marks allow whole-milliliter readings; use actual marked intervals on other syringes. Model one reading aloud.

#### 23 min. Squeeze and compare
Two minutes: review day 1's air model and readings. If day 1 tickets showed error A, contrast an empty drawing with air-entering evidence. Two more minutes return day 2 checks: error J tracks missing pieces; error K links stiffness to a job, one minute each.

Ten minutes in six simultaneous groups: [[s01-P3-1]]. Each child gets 45 seconds to read, gently press and reread: five turns take 3 minutes 45 seconds per group. Others record and observe. A child may direct a partner. Retain actual readings. For leaks or unclear marks, stop and use N3: invented for practice, 25 mL then 15 mL. Say which evidence is invented.

Five minutes: [[s01-K3-1]], invented for practice. Try first, then model one annotated drawing, rather than paired answer boxes.

[[steps:s01-K3-1]]

Four minutes: [[s01-P3-2]].

**Ask.** What evidence says the syringe was not empty? Expect occupied volume and resistance while the tip stayed closed. Review: dots stand for unseen parts, from day 1. Smaller space supports closer spacing, not disappearing air, error I. The dots are a model; we did not see particles. Add evidence beside the question board's air question.

#### 10 min. First shadow reading
Review: shadow, a blocked-light region, and ruler zero with whole centimeters. Allow two minutes out, two for shared instruction, four for groups measuring simultaneously, two to return. [[s01-P3-3]]: align zero at the stick base; read the clear tip to the nearest centimeter. Record fixed spot, date and clock time. Save this first record, not a seasonal conclusion.

Keep stick, height, upright position, place and clock convention fixed monthly. Note daylight-saving changes. No clear tip or no sun: write unreadable. Separately read N3's 40 cm as invented; do not insert it as an observation.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s01-X3a]] on the half slip and [[s01-X3b]] in the Exit box. Answer alone. After class, 30 times 12 seconds means 6 minutes marking against the key. Return both day 4; tick or error per item, no score.

#### 3 min. Clean-up
Release pressure before returning syringes. Collect rulers and chalk. Review wiping and handwashing in two waves. Keep drawings in folders.

### Other rooms
No safe syringe or outdoor access: read N3 aloud as invented for practice. No printer: copy practice, revealing checks only when students answer. Never substitute a reserved pouch or bottle case.

### At home
**Swap.** No safe syringe or eyewear: use invented N3, 25 mL then 15 mL. No outdoor stick or clear sun: mark unreadable, then discuss invented 40 cm separately. No printer: use paper.

**Skip.** Group rotations.

- **12 min. Volumes.** Do [[s01-P3-1]] and [[s01-P3-2]] from safe readings or N3.
  **Say.** What shows air was inside? Expect occupied volume and resistance.
- **8 min. Model.** Do [[s01-K3-1]].
  **Say.** What changes? Expect space and spacing, not retained air.
- **10 min. Shadow.** Do [[s01-P3-3]]. Never look at the sun. Record place and clock time.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X3a]] and [[s01-X3b]]. Answer alone; discuss with the key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: shorten public discussion. Keep safe turns, model, record and checks. Longer: add a model-limit label.

### Words and supports
Frame: I know the syringe was not empty because ___. Read marks aloud. Allow dictation and directed handling.
