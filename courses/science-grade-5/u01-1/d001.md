# Day 1: What entered the ball?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Trial the ball within its rated pressure; choose N1 if the gain is unresolved. Check the scale reads whole grams. Prepare six card pieces per envelope. Keep exit cases covered. Print teacher-only keys separately. Display the question board and send the family note.

### Safety and set-up
Teacher alone handles the needle and pump. Stay below rated pressure; keep the ball in its tray. Everyone wears protective eyewear during pumping. Stop for damage, leaks or missing protection; use N1. No tasting.

### The day

#### 12 min. Hook
This is the course's first day. Alone, predict with [[s01-P1-1]]. Zero the tray, weigh the ball twice, pump safely, remove the needle and reweigh twice. Share whole-gram readings before explaining. Complete [[s01-K1-1]].

[[steps:s01-K1-1]]

**Ask.** What went into the ball? Expect air; a stable gain supports air having weight. Error A assigns air no matter. Review: fair test, keep, change, watch, from kindergarten. Keep the same ball and tray; change added air.

Retain unstable or unchanged readings. Check zero and position once; never pump extra to force a gain. If unresolved, print and read N1: invented for practice, 420 g then 424 g, gain 4 g. Distinguish it from observations.

#### 17 min. Draw and compare
Six minutes alone: [[s01-P1-2]]. Four minutes paired comparison; everyone may pass publicly. Review kindergarten's question board: turn notices into questions. Post the unit question: What is everything made of, if we cannot see the pieces? Does any of it vanish when it changes?

Compare an empty-ball account with an air-entering account. Review: evidence, what I saw, and claim, what I think, from kindergarten. Offer dots as a new model for unseen air parts, [[s01-P1-3]]. Explain that dots are enlarged symbols; error B treats them as visible grains. A flat ball may already contain air. Keep originals dated, never erased.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s01-X1]] only now. Read exactly; students draw and finish independently. Collect unscored. After class, allow 30 children times 12 seconds, 6 teacher minutes, for feedback against the key. Return day 2; record a tick or error A/D, never points.

#### 2 min. Clean-up
Teacher secures needle and pump. Collect eyewear and wipe the tray. Pages stay in folders.

#### 10 min. Readiness check
Teach no rebuild example first. Reveal [[s01-R1]] on the readiness sheet. Six minutes alone with card pieces; then 4 minutes to inspect 30 sheets, 8 seconds each. Record M and P as access flags, not scores. Sort for day 2.

### Other rooms
No safe equipment: use N1, labelled and read aloud as invented. No printer: copy practice on paper; reveal exit and readiness stems only at their turns.

### At home
**Swap.** No safe ball, pump, scale or eyewear: read invented N1, 420 g then 424 g. No printer: copy questions onto paper.

**Skip.** Group talk and class sorting.

- **5 min. Predict.** Do [[s01-P1-1]].
- **10 min. Read.** Use [[s01-K1-1]] with N1 or safely gathered readings.
  **Say.** What went in? Expect air; N1 gains 4 g.
- **15 min. Draw.** Do [[s01-P1-2]] and [[s01-P1-3]].
  **Say.** What does a dot stand for? Expect a part too small to see.
- **5 min. Exit.** Reveal [[s01-X1]]; let the child answer alone. Discuss afterward using the teacher key.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce public sharing. Keep readings, model, exit and readiness. Longer: add labels to the original drawing.

### Words and supports
New: matter has weight and takes space; gram, g, labels scale readings. Read displays aloud. Frame: The ball got heavier because ___. Drawing or dictation counts.

### Tomorrow
Day 2 returns day 1 tickets and readiness flags. Error D gets two minutes in Rebuild; error B gets one closing minute. Error A is revisited day 3 beside today's readings. M/P select support.
