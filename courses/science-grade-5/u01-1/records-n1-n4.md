# Records N1 to N4: constructed practice records

These records are invented for practice. They are not observations and not photographs. Use them only when the real ball, blocks, syringe or pot are missing.

**N1: a flat and a pumped ball.** Empty scale reads 0 g. The flat ball on the scale reads 420 g. After 40 pumps the ball reads 424 g, a gain of 4 g.

**N2: six blocks rebuilt.** Six numbered blocks make a bridge, then a wall. All six are in both. Block 3 is long and flat, so it spans the gap.

**N3: air in a closed syringe, and a first shadow.** The syringe holds 25 mL of air. Pressed, it holds 15 mL. The stick's first shadow is 40 cm long at the clock time written on the sheet.

**N4: a weighed bean pot.** Empty pot, 0 g after zero. Dry soil, 100 g. Beans, 102 g on the scale, so 2 g added. Water, 132 g on the scale, so 30 g added. Total 172 g. Record each line as added.
