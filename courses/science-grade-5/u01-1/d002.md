# Day 2: Same pieces, new thing, and soil to dry

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the class list for plant and mold allergies. Seat gap M together and gap P together. Count six blocks into a bag for each group. Label one drying tray for each group. Have two unlike balls ready. Set out the scale, damp towel, nonlatex gloves and hand-washing supplies. Print the pages. Return day 1's exit ticket (s01-X1) and page (s01-P1-3), marked for errors A, B and D. Open the record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

Groups of five, one table each. Soil: check allergies first; keep it in trays on a towel, spread thin; wipe up with a damp towel, never a brush; nonlatex gloves on; children with asthma stand back, mask offered; the teacher handles soil for anyone who should not. Wash hands after soil. Count the blocks back to six. The trays stay dated and labelled until day 4. Clean-up takes 5 minutes inside the day.

### The day

#### 10 min. Support or extension

Alone or in small groups, by day 1's marks, with the card pieces.

Children with gap M count their six pieces, rebuild them as a new object, and count again. Children with gap P test a long flat piece across two books, first flat and then folded, and say which holds a small eraser. Both then write [[s01-P2-1]]. In the last 3 minutes everyone answers [[s01-C2-1]]; if a dot was called a grain (error B), add: a dot stands for parts far too small to see.

#### 16 min. Rebuild

**If more than a third made error D:** hold up two unlike balls, or draw them, for 2 minutes. Ask what else differs besides air. Expect: size, rubber, the ball's own weight. Weigh the same ball.

The worked example, 4 minutes: [[s01-K2-1]]. Students try first, then model.

[[steps:s01-K2-1]]

Groups of five, 8 minutes: build one thing from six blocks, rebuild it as another, and list on [[s01-P2-2]] the job of every block number. If a block is left out, find it together.

**Ask.** Are all six still there? Expect: yes, point to each. Parts are kept when a whole changes. Blocks show parts and wholes, but not the true size of unseen parts. Board: this day answers what a part is, and what a whole is.

#### 10 min. Spread soil

Allergy check first, gloves on. Groups spread a thin layer of soil on their labelled, dated tray and set it aside. It dries for two school days. Weigh one tray with its soil together, without pressing zero, and write the grams on its label; day 4 weighs it again.

While they work, try [[s01-K2-2]]. Tell it as a story with invented numbers.

[[steps:s01-K2-2]]

**Ask.** Why dry the soil before we weigh it? Expect: water weighs too, so the bag weight is soil plus water. Only the dry weight is the soil. Reading 120 g as dry soil is error H. The open tray lets water go into the air; we weigh closed things from day 8.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

[[s01-X2a]] and [[s01-X2b]], alone, on paper. Collect them; they are not scored.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Count the blocks back to six. Set the trays aside. Wipe tables with a damp towel; wash hands. Pages stay in their folders.

### Other rooms

No devices are needed. With no blocks, use six numbered cards or record N2 (six blocks, a bridge, then a wall). With no tray, a paper plate holds the soil.

### At home

**Swap.** No blocks: use six cards, or six coins. No tray: use a plate.

**Skip.** The group build and the two balls.

- **8 min. Rebuild.** Do [[s01-K2-1]], then [[s01-P2-2]] with six toy blocks.
  **Say.** "Are all six still there?" Expect: yes, point to each one.
- **10 min. Soil.** Spread a little soil thinly on a plate. Do [[s01-K2-2]].
  **Say.** "Why dry the soil first?" Expect: water weighs too, so only the dry weight is soil.
- **12 min. Practice.** Do [[s01-P2-1]] with six pieces of card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2a]] and [[s01-X2b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop the card test. Never drop the block list, the soil story or the exit. Longer: rebuild a third time and list the jobs again.

### Words and supports

Piece: one part of a whole. Dry: no water left in it. Frame: "This piece helps because ___ so the ___ can ___." Read every question aloud. Accept a drawing or dictation for jobs.

### Tomorrow

Read the exits. A shelf of five pieces is error J and "stiff is good" with no reason is error K: day 3's Squeeze and compare spends one minute on each. A damp weight written as the dry weight is error H: day 4 repairs it inside the pot part. Day 3 squeezes air in a closed syringe and draws it with dots.
