# Day 2: Same pieces, different jobs

## Teacher page

### Before class
Keep the generated d002-key.html teacher-only.
Sort day 1 readiness M/P flags. Return day 1 feedback. Check plant and mold allergies privately. Prepare numbered blocks and labeled trays. Weigh empty trays beforehand. Set out gloves and hand-washing supplies. Keep teacher-only key separate and both paper checks covered.

### Safety and set-up
Avoid soil dust. Use nonlatex gloves; the teacher handles soil if needed. Offer a dust-free work position. Clean with a damp towel, never dry sweeping. No tasting; wash hands afterward.

### The day

#### 10 min. Support or extension
Review: grade 2's parts-and-whole sketch and stiff, bends easily. Explain taking apart and rebuilding directly; do not assume the planned rebuild routine was taught.

Four minutes: M groups match and count pieces; P groups test the same card spanning two books under one eraser. Swap flat and folded positions; explain the observed support. Four minutes: [[s01-P2-1]] independently. Ready children start [[s01-C2-1]]. Last two minutes: compare limits of the block model. If day 1 drawings showed error B, spend one minute distinguishing visible blocks from unseen air parts.

#### 16 min. Rebuild
If day 1 tickets showed error D, use two minutes comparing unlike balls: rubber, size and starting weight can differ. Review kindergarten's fair test; return its feedback.

Four minutes: try [[s01-K2-1]], then demonstrate physically.

[[steps:s01-K2-1]]

Eight minutes in six groups: [[s01-P2-2]], with a numbered job list, no shelf drawing. Every child tracks one block; the fifth also tracks the sixth. Two minutes compare explanations. Error J loses a piece; error K names a property without its job.

**Ask.** What can these blocks show about air parts? Expect retained parts in a changed whole; visible blocks cannot show unseen size. Keep the air model from day 1.

#### 10 min. Spread soil
Groups spread soil thinly into dated drying trays, 5 minutes, as a set-up for day 4. Weigh one complete tray twice; record its empty-tray weight separately. Keep the reading, including water, labeled damp.

Five minutes: [[s01-K2-2]], invented for practice, not a measured result. Try before showing steps.

[[steps:s01-K2-2]]

**Ask.** What else is included in this reading? Expect tray and water as well as soil. Error H counts damp material as dry soil. Drying establishes a consistent starting condition; air-dried soil can retain moisture. Do not promise two days guarantees dryness.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s01-X2a]] on the half slip, then [[s01-X2b]] in the student Exit box. Alone, paper only. Collect both. After class: 30 times 12 seconds, 6 minutes, for key-based feedback. Return day 3; one tick or error per item, no score.

#### 5 min. Clean-up
Count blocks back to six. Store trays safely. Review damp wiping and handwashing. Use available sinks in two waves; nonwashing students label trays and clear tables. Keep student work in folders.

### Other rooms
No blocks: six numbered scrap cards, or record card N2, invented for practice. No safe soil: draw the tray set-up and retain the invented record. No printer: reveal checks on the board only at their turns.

### At home
**Swap.** No blocks: numbered cards. No safe soil or tray: draw the planned set-up. No printer: copy practice and hide checks.

**Skip.** Class sorting and group reporting.

- **10 min. Rebuild.** Try [[s01-K2-1]] and [[s01-P2-1]].
  **Say.** Where is each piece? Expect all six accounted for.
- **10 min. Explain.** Use [[s01-P2-2]] and [[s01-C2-1]].
  **Say.** What cannot blocks show? Expect air parts are too small to see.
- **10 min. Soil.** Read invented [[s01-K2-2]]. Explain tray, soil and water. Spread a little soil only with gloves and allergy clearance; wash hands.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X2a]] and [[s01-X2b]]. Answer alone, then discuss the key.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce reporting. Keep tracing, model limits and both checks. Longer: rebuild again without adding pieces.

### Words and supports
Frame: This piece helps because ___ so the ___ can ___. Read stems aloud; accept dictated jobs and labeled drawings.

### Tomorrow
Day 3 returns both checks. Errors J/K get one minute each in Squeeze and compare. Error H waits for day 4's Plant beans. Day 3 measures enclosed air and starts the shadow record.
