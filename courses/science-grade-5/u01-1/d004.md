# Day 4: Revise the air drawing, and weigh a bean pot

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check that the day 2 soil is dry: crumbly, pale, not sticking to a clean dry finger. Weigh the day 2 tray with its soil together, as before, and write the drop on its label. If the soil is not dry, use a prepared dry sample or record N4, invented. Check beans are untreated. Set two scales. Return day 3's s01-X3a and s01-X3b, marked. Print the pages. Open the record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

Allergy checks first, especially legumes; never eat the beans. Keep soil in trays; wipe up with a damp towel, never a brush; children with asthma stand back, mask offered. Nonlatex gloves; wash hands. The teacher alone squeezes the capped bottle, gently, away from faces. Dry the scales before weighing. Pots stand in saucers. The teacher keeps the plants. Clean-up takes 5 minutes inside the day.

### The day

#### 5 min. Compare drawings

Students set the day 1 and day 3 air drawings side by side and write [[s01-P4-1]].

#### 13 min. Revise the air model

Squeeze the sealed bottle gently so all can see it give.

**Ask.** What stays the same when air is squeezed, and what changes? Expect: the dots stay; the space and spacing change.

Students revise their drawing on [[s01-P4-2]]: the same number of dots, a new spacing, and the caption "I changed ___ because ___ showed ___." They keep the first drawing and add the revision beside it. Dots are parts too small to see, never grains.

**If many made one error.** For the last 3 minutes, repair the error most children made on day 3's ticket. Error I: draw twelve dots in a wide box, then in a narrow box, on the dot panel, and count both. Error L: lay today's record beside a stick's mark and name what stays fixed. The other gets one picture and one sentence for a small group. Board: did anything leave?

#### 18 min. Plant beans in weighed soil

First, 3 minutes. Set the day 2 tray's first weight beside its weight now (or, if the drop is under 5 g, bag soil 140 g beside dried 118 g). Which weighs soil? Expect: the dried weight; the wet reading has water in it (error H, day 2's s01-X2b).

Then 4 minutes: [[s01-K4-1]]. Model zeroing the scale with the empty pot, then each line.

[[steps:s01-K4-1]]

Groups, 11 minutes, three at each scale for a 3 minute turn; waiting groups confirm their soil is dry and finish their caption. At the scale: zero the empty pot, add the dry soil, then the beans, then water, writing each line on the pot log, [[s01-P4-3]]. Each line is the grams added. Wet-soil weight is never used.

**Ask.** Why do we log the water on its own line? Expect: it is not soil; we can find the soil, beans and water apart later.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

[[s01-X4a]] and [[s01-X4b]], alone, on paper. Collect them unscored.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Wipe soil into a tray with a damp towel. Label the pots with group and date and set them in the window. Wipe the scale and wash hands. Drawings and logs stay in their folders.

### Other rooms

No devices are needed. With no scale, use record N4, invented for practice. With no bottle, squeeze a sealed zip bag of air.

### At home

**Swap.** No scale: use record N4 for the pot. No pot: use a cup. No bottle: press a sealed zip bag gently, away from faces.

**Skip.** The group repair and the planting.

- **5 min. Compare.** Set the old drawings side by side. Do [[s01-P4-1]].
  **Say.** "What stayed the same?" Expect: the dots; only their spacing changed.
- **10 min. Revise.** Squeeze a sealed bottle gently. Do [[s01-P4-2]].
  **Say.** "Did any air go away?" Expect: no, the same dots sit closer.
- **15 min. Pot.** Do [[s01-K4-1]], then [[s01-P4-3]] if you can weigh.
  **Say.** "Why log the water apart?" Expect: water is not soil.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X4a]] and [[s01-X4b]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the wet and dried sort unless the tickets showed it. Never cut the revision, the pot log or the exit. Longer: draw what the dots do if the syringe is pulled wider, and say what would test it.

### Words and supports

Zero: press the scale's zero with the empty pot on it. Added: the grams put in this time. Dry: no water left. Frame: "I changed ___ because ___ showed ___." Read every reading aloud. Accept dictation for the caption.

### Tomorrow

Read the exits and record the error letters. A squeezed bottle drawn with no dots or fewer dots shows air being lost. A running scale reading copied as grams added shows the pot log is not understood. Day 5 starts section 1.2: sugar is stirred into water, and these drawings are revised to show where the sugar goes. Keep the drawings and the pot log.
