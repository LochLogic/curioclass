# Day 4: Revise from evidence and record additions

## Teacher page

### Before class
Keep the generated d004-key.html teacher-only.
Check soil baseline and untreated seed packaging. Ask privately about plant, mold and legume allergies. Set two scales, gloves and hand-washing supplies. Return day 3 checks, s01-X3a and s01-X3b, and day 2 soil feedback. Print corrected N4 below instead of the shared card's unexplained total. Keep teacher-only key separate; hide both checks.

### Safety and set-up
Keep soil contained; avoid dust. Wear nonlatex gloves. Teacher handles materials for anyone needing a dust-free position. No tasting. Wipe with a damp towel and wash hands. Keep water away from scales; use records if safe handling is unavailable.

### The day

#### 5 min. Compare drawings
Review: day 1's model and day 3's syringe evidence. [[s01-P4-1]] alone. Distinguish added air in the ball from unchanged enclosed air during compression; different containers need not share a dot count. Keep both dated originals.

#### 13 min. Revise the air model
Review the actual syringe readings, or their explicitly invented fallback. No bottle demonstration: that case belongs to today's independent check.

**Ask.** What stays the same when enclosed air is squeezed? Expect retained air; smaller occupied space supports closer spacing.

Seven minutes: [[s01-P4-2]], one annotated model and evidence caption, not paired exit drawings. Label dots as unseen parts; the model connects measured space with a scale we cannot see. Four minutes paired explanation, with passes allowed. Last two minutes: return day 3 feedback. Error I: use the syringe record to reject lost air. Error L: compare the fixed-place record with its setup details. Give each repair one minute; targeted follow-up happens during recording.

#### 18 min. Plant beans in weighed soil
Three minutes: revisit day 2's soil feedback, especially error H. Complete tray readings include the tray; subtract its recorded weight. A wet baseline includes water. Use the prepared air-dried stock if tray soil remains damp; otherwise use corrected N4. Dry means the operational air-dried baseline, not proven absence of all moisture.

Four minutes: [[s01-K4-1]], invented for practice. Explain differences from successive readings, then show steps.

[[steps:s01-K4-1]]

Six groups share two scales: three two-minute turns per scale take 6 minutes. Zero the empty pot. [[s01-P4-3]]: record soil and loose beans separately, reading twice. Waiting groups finish captions. Never predict seed mass; flag unresolved additions. Check unstable readings once, then use N4 separately. Final five minutes: two minutes to sow beans and label pots together; three one-minute turns per scale to add water and record its grams. Each group gets 3 scale minutes total. Rotate recording roles. The teacher maintains plants for unit 2.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Reveal [[s01-X4a]] on the half slip and [[s01-X4b]] in the Exit box. Alone, paper only. After class: 30 times 12 seconds, 6 minutes marking with the key. Return day 5 in Revise; tick or error per item, no points.

#### 5 min. Clean-up
Contain soil with a damp towel. Label and store pots in saucers. Review handwashing in two waves. Retain drawings and pot logs for day 5 and unit 2.

### Other rooms
No safe soil, dry stock or scale: use corrected N4 below, read aloud as invented. Do not claim planting or measurement was observed. No printer: copy practice; reveal checks only at their turns.

### At home
**Swap.** No safe soil, seeds, gloves, pot or scale: use corrected invented N4 below. No printer: copy questions and table.

**Skip.** Group rotations and planting when using records.

- **5 min. Compare.** Do [[s01-P4-1]].
- **10 min. Revise.** Revisit syringe evidence for [[s01-P4-2]].
  **Say.** What changed? Expect space and spacing; enclosed air remained.
- **15 min. Record.** Do [[s01-K4-1]] and [[s01-P4-3]], using actual readings or N4.
  **Say.** Why subtract the earlier reading? Expect only the new addition is wanted.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X4a]] and [[s01-X4b]]. Answer independently. Discuss with the key afterward.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce paired sharing, retaining revision, recording and checks. Longer: add evidence labels to the revised model.

### Words and supports
Review: gram, zero, unseen parts and air-dried baseline. Frame: I changed ___ because ___ showed ___. Read marks aloud; allow dictation.

### Tomorrow
Day 5 returns today's checks inside Revise. Errors A/I lose enclosed air; error x copies running readings as added amounts. Carry feedback and revised drawings into the dissolving investigation. Keep the pot log for unit 2.
