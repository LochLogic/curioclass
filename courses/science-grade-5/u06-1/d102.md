# Day 102: Protection changes connected parts

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the unit 6 section 6.1 opener. Separate unit 6 section 6.1: teacher keys and held independent paper. Use current rows for today's separate component notes. Set out Separate resource-protection cards, Runoff noticing record and its original schematic. Prepare day 101 papers, unfinished day 100 slips, question board and formative record. Read facts aloud without requesting family experiences.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Honor day 101's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return the named papers privately: s06-X101-1, s06-X101-2, s06-X100-1, s06-X100-2, s06-P101-locator, s06-P101-status, s06-P101-repair-B, s06-P101-repair-I, s06-P101-link. First five minutes address error A with [[s06-P102-repair-A]]. Second five address unfinished day 100 B/I with [[s06-P102-repair-BI]]. More than ten misses makes the component whole-class; otherwise use assigned parallel work. For B, point to the arrow's action, not just its labels. Collect fresh slips and log unresolved needs.

#### 8 min. Evidence-status reminder
Review observation, inference, projection and value. Use [[s06-P102-observed]] and [[s06-P102-status]], separately. Children try before keys are revealed. Discuss recorded evidence versus future hopes orally. These labels describe different kinds of claims; testable effects need observations. Values express what matters. Do not practice a two-source drawing with all status labels.

#### 17 min. System components
Review cause and effect, Earth interaction panels and runoff. Read Separate resource-protection cards one at a time: soil, water use, habitat, litter, air, space. Explain unfamiliar factual content directly from each card. Pairs use [[s06-P102-soil]]. Teacher alone revises separate panels, connecting land cover and soil movement; water use and supply; living things and habitat; litter transport and ocean; releases and air; debris and near-Earth space. Include farming, industry and daily public actions without blame. No six-card matching sheet or integrated child report. Review question board: notices become questions; retain source locations with each component. Evidence can test effects, not decide community priorities. The invented public-pipe example stays separate from real source facts.

#### 5 min. Share and handoff
Continue day 101's rotating six heard roster, review. Six 45-second source-backed component reports take 4.5 minutes; all others submit paper. Passes remain unheard. Each heard child explains one already rehearsed arrow, not a complete report. Teacher links the anchor to the next question: what could classroom-filter measurements establish? Appearance alone cannot establish drinking safety; day 103 defines measurable goals.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Cover practice. Reveal [[s06-X102]] for independent work on a half slip. Read its exact stem and invented label. Four minutes work, one collection. After class, thirty drawings at 40 seconds take twenty minutes; component pages at 10 seconds take five. Review the sole formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Describe source connections and status labels separately within that scale.

### Other rooms
No projector: use printed cards individually. No printer: copy short component facts and source locations; draw blank panels. Keep the held case unseen until its turn.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: adult copies today's facts and blank response spaces. No partner: explain components to the adult.
**Skip.** Public reports and group panel collection.
- **8 min. Repair.** Use [[s06-P102-repair-A]] and [[s06-P102-repair-BI]] after day 101 feedback.
- **7 min. Status.** Use [[s06-P102-observed]] and [[s06-P102-status]].
- **12 min. Components.** Read protection cards separately; use [[s06-P102-soil]].
  **Say.** "Which source supports that arrow?" Expect the soil card and reduced soil movement.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Child independently completes [[s06-X102]]. Return private feedback on day 103.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated oral reports, retaining all source components and exit. Longer: locate the source behind an existing arrow.

### Words and supports
Review source, watershed, wetland, observation, projection and value. Provide large arrows and neutral reading. Separate factual evidence from preference without judging the preference.

### Tomorrow
Day 103 returns today's pages and exit. Errors A/B/C each get five minutes: combine information, connect parts, distinguish projection. Day 101 status notes share C's round. Carry day 99 design/resource notes and exact listening roster.

### Credit
Ours. Factual cards have checked source rows; invented cases are labeled.
