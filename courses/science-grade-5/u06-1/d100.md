# Day 100: Where does runoff go?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the unit 6 section 6.1 opener. Separate unit 6 section 6.1: teacher keys from held independent paper. Use current item-row keys for practice. Print the family note and today's component notes only. Check secured stick, direction reference and safe walking route. Open the public image in the Runoff noticing record. Prepare the formative record and question board. No household water questions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook first
Alone, inspect the public image before explaining it: [[s06-P100-notice]]. Accept visible details; invisible hazards are not identifiable. If unavailable, use the original schematic, invented for practice, and mark photograph evidence missing. Review observation and inference: seeing differs from explanation. Collect notices.

#### 5 min. Retrieve and explain
Pick up day 99's design/resource handoff. Review natural resource: material or energy supply found in nature. Pairs use [[s06-P100-resource]] and [[s06-P100-fresh]] for three minutes. Review day 95's accessible fresh-water distinction. Use two minutes to explain drainage. Review watershed: land draining to a common outlet, from day 88. After class, return day 99 retry, revision and repair feedback in the Retrieve folder, zero added conference minutes. Return the named papers privately: s05-P99-A, s05-P99-B, s05-P99-C, s05-P99-D, s05-P99-E, s05-P99-F, s05-P99-G, s05-P99-H, s05-P99-design, s05-P99-resource.

#### 8 min. Source and route components
Review runoff: rain that runs over the ground, from grade 2. Review source: a card or page that tells you something. Review cause and effect and Earth interaction panels: parts linked by observed actions. Pairs try [[s06-P100-arrow]] before the teacher reveals its key. Review action arrows; mark uncertain links differently. The town schematic is invented for practice; its intake leads to treatment. Review question board: notices become questions. Teacher poses the unit question about protecting water and testing filters; retain unresolved ideas.

#### 17 min. Evidence work
Pairs inspect one anchor component at a time, [[s06-P100-route]]. Teacher holds the initial system model; children label one component only. Do not practice an obstructed route map or compose protection inquiries. Review rotating six heard, record sheet names who. Six 45-second component reports take 4.5 minutes inside this part; all thirty submit paper. Passes remain unheard. Error B lists parts without an action; revisit the arrow row. Error I needs an action affecting the recorded route.

#### 5 min. Monthly shadow
Review fixed-stick observation from day 3. One shared demonstration: ruler zero at base, nearest 1 cm, direction reference, [[s06-P100-shadow]]. Travel and reading take four minutes; clearing and return take one. Never look at the sun. Unsafe route or unclear tip: unresolved with reason. Read separate S100, invented for practice; never copy it into actual data. Shadows are background, not runoff evidence.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Cover practice. Reveal [[s06-X100-1]] on a half slip and [[s06-X100-2]] in the student Exit box. Read exactly, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection. After class: thirty slips at 30 seconds, fifteen minutes; Exit boxes at 10 seconds, five minutes; component pages at 10 seconds, five minutes. Review the sole formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Initial-model descriptive notes stay separate.
