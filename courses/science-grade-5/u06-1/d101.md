# Day 101: What can these sources tell us?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the unit 6 section 6.1 opener. Separate unit 6 section 6.1: teacher keys and held independent paper. Use current rows for corrected practice and attribution keys. Print today's separate component notes only through the student page. Open Community and wetland source cards. Use checked supply and wetland accounts; do not reconstruct the blocked policy. Prepare returned day 100 papers, anchor components, question board and formative record.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Honor day 100's Tomorrow. Review return and repair. Return notices, component pages and both exit papers privately. First five minutes: error B, [[s06-P101-repair-B]]. Next five: error I, [[s06-P101-repair-I]]. More than ten misses makes that component whole-class; otherwise visit the assigned group while others work independently. Collect fresh slips. Keep unresolved needs for day 102, without recopying the exit.

#### 8 min. Evaluate sources
Review source: a card or page that tells you something. Review watershed: land draining to a common outlet. Define wetland: land covered or saturated by water for some or all of the year. Identify maker, date, purpose and location before content. Review two-column notes: source statement and source location. Pairs try [[s06-P101-link]] before the teacher reveals an oral causal connection. Define projection: an expected outcome that still needs observations. Define value: what people consider important. Evidence tests effects; it does not choose values.

#### 17 min. Source workshops
Pairs read Card S and Card W separately, with neutral reading available. Complete [[s06-P101-locator]]. The city account reports April expectations, not today's supply. The federal card explains wetland functions; neither establishes every local outcome or drinking safety. Add supported components to the question board, review: notices become questions. Do not compose paired attributions or a shared inquiry before the held exit. Error A needs a connection rather than disconnected facts; revisit the oral link. No policy-specific detail is taught.

#### 5 min. Share
Continue day 100's rotating six heard roster, review. Six 45-second source-locator reports total 4.5 minutes; all submit paper. Passes remain unheard. Individually use [[s06-P101-status]]. Review observation versus projection: future expectation is not a measured outcome. Preserve the source date.

#### 5 min. Paper exit
Close sources and practice. Reveal [[s06-X101-1]] on a half slip and [[s06-X101-2]] in the student Exit box. Read exactly, invented for practice. Four independent minutes, one collection. Thirty slips at 30 seconds each need fifteen teacher minutes after class; component pages at 10 seconds need five. Review the sole formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Attribution and shared inquiry receive separate descriptive notes.

### Other rooms
No devices: printed source cards hold both accounts. No printer: copy today's facts, locators and stems. Keep the held slip unseen until its turn.

### At home
**Swap.** No printer: copy source facts and response spaces. No partner: adult reads neutrally.
**Skip.** Public reports and listening roster.
- **8 min. Repair.** Use [[s06-P101-repair-B]] and [[s06-P101-repair-I]] after day 100 feedback.
- **10 min. Read.** Read sources separately; use [[s06-P101-locator]] and [[s06-P101-link]].
  **Say.** "How can storage change later flow?" Expect slower release, not guaranteed safety.
- **8 min. Status.** Use [[s06-P101-status]]. Separate April expectations from present observations.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Cover practice. Child independently answers [[s06-X101-1]] and [[s06-X101-2]]. Return private feedback on day 102.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce repeated locator reports. Keep repairs and unseen exit. Longer: reread one existing source location for its limits.

### Words and supports
Review source, watershed, observation, inference and two-column notes. Define wetland, projection and value explicitly. Read factual cards aloud; accept faithful paraphrase and neutral scribing.

### Tomorrow
Day 102 returns today's pages and exit. Error A gets five minutes connecting information; unfinished day 100 B/I share a second five-minute round. Save evidence-status uncertainty for day 103's error C round. Carry the exact listening roster forward.

### Credit
Ours. Source cards are original factual summaries with source-row provenance.
