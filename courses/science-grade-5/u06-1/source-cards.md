## Card W: scientific account

United States Environmental Protection Agency.
[Why are Wetlands Important?](https://www.epa.gov/wetlands/why-are-wetlands-important)
Source row: S06-wetlands. Original summary; no source passage or image copied.

W1. Wetlands can store water and release it more slowly.
W2. Wetland plants and soil interact with water moving through the watershed.
W3. Wetlands provide habitat and can improve water quality.
Location: introduction, Wetlands and Nature, and Flood Protection.
These functions do not establish drinking safety.

## Words

A watershed is land draining to a common outlet.
Wetlands have water covering or saturating soil for some or all of the year.
Definition source: [What is a Wetland?](https://www.epa.gov/wetlands/what-wetland), S06-wetland-definition.
