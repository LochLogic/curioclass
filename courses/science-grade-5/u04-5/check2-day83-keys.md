# Day 83 teacher-only answers

Teacher only. Keep apart from every student packet. Use check2-record.md scale, never points.

## c2-E83-distance
Choose row A: sun is a star, much nearer than J/K, which are differently distant. Minutes versus years supports much nearer; thirteen versus twenty-one supports different distances.

Look-fors: sun is star | much nearer | other stars differently distant | travel-time evidence

Meets: A: sun is a star, much nearer by minutes versus years. Thirteen and twenty-one years show different J/K distances. | Not yet: B: sun is not a star. (error X)

## c2-E83-light
Choose farther/fainter and ray R. Same output spreads over more area farther away, delivering less to an equal patch. Link controlled evidence to the much nearer sun appearing brighter. No J/K brightness ranking from distance alone.

Look-fors: farther lamp fainter with readings | R spreads light | less per equal patch | nearer sun brighter

Meets: Farther/fainter; R. Four versus sixteen supports spreading light and less per equal patch farther away. The much nearer sun appears brighter. | Not yet: Distance cannot change apparent brightness at fixed output. (error Y)

## c2-E83-size
Nearer objects can appear larger; link the equal-disk record to the much nearer sun. This compares apparent size, not actual stellar sizes or brightness.

Look-fors: nearer linked to apparent size | equal-disk evidence | model limit

Meets: The nearer disk looks larger in the same camera. The much nearer sun appears larger too; actual stellar sizes are not compared. | Not yet: A larger image proves the sun physically exceeds every other star. (error Z)

## c2-E83-shadow
Lengths decrease 48 to 24 cm, then increase to 36 cm. Directions change west, north, northeast. Supplied-display interpretation only.

Look-fors: decrease then increase | west to north to northeast

Meets: Shorter then longer; west changes through north to northeast. | Not yet: A fixed stick always has one shadow direction. (error AA)

## c2-E83-daylight
June: fourteen daylight, ten dark hours. December: nine daylight, fifteen dark. June has five more light and five fewer dark hours. No seasonal cause required.

Look-fors: more daylight in June | less darkness in June | accurate values or comparison

Meets: June has five more daylight hours and five fewer dark hours. | Not yet: Daylight duration is identical in both months. (error AB)

## c2-E83-stars
L appears on all three March nights and no September nights; M reverses, at matched place/time. Zero is not visible then, not destroyed. This does not establish a full graphing result.

Look-fors: L March only in record | M September only in record | avoids destruction claim

Meets: At this place and clock time, L appears in March; M in September. | Not yet: L ceased to exist in September. (error AC)
