# Day 80: Independent evidence arguments

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Prepare end-day80.md as the two-sided student page, one copy of each stem. Keep completed force, sky and light models covered. Hold day 78 and day 79 papers, keys and feedback privately. Prepare courses/science-grade-5/u04-1/record.md for access notes only. No apparatus is needed; the supplied map is a model, not a claim that these falls were observed.

### The day

#### 3 min. Neutral directions
Hold day 79's assessment privately for return on 81, with no feedback today. Today each child answers two new arguments alone. Say these records are invented for practice. Read the supplied records neutrally. A scribe preserves the child's own selections, drawing and explanation. Do not supply mechanism labels or discuss prior errors.

#### 20 min. Q2
Children independently complete [[s04-Q2]] with the supplied map on side 1. Record access support only. No push, fall or cord demonstration. Do not indicate which records to select or what direction an arrow should take. Allow drawing plus short writing as the child's own argument.

#### 20 min. Q3
Children independently complete [[s04-Q3]] on side 2. Retain their evidence argument and light-path drawing together. Read values and units without interpreting their relationships. Keep earlier ray diagrams and model components covered. Give no category hint or unlike-star brightness ranking.

#### 2 min. Check and collect
Children independently check and hand in both sides, without discussion. After class, thirty two-minute readings take sixty teacher minutes. Use the key's ten-point components per argument, twenty today and thirty-six across both assessment days. Keep original points. Error A means page-bottom down; error E excludes sun from stars; error F denies a distance effect. Describe other missing components rather than assigning invented letters. Keep points separate from the formative record. Return Q2/Q3 with Q1a-d and E78 on 81.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the exact current stems and supplied disk onto paper, preserving blank argument space. Enlarge the map or supply tactile positions without arrows. No screen or digital tool is required.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy the current stems and blank map if printing is unavailable.

**Skip.** Group routines and public talk. Cover all earlier completed models.

- **45 min. Assessment.** Use three minutes for neutral directions. Child answers [[s04-Q2]] and [[s04-Q3]] independently, twenty minutes each. Finish with two minutes checking and collecting. Read without scientific hints; a scribe records the child's decisions. Keep the separate key private, then prepare one next step for each needed component. Hold feedback until day 81.

### Shorter or longer
Keep both complete argument tasks and their time. Honor established access accommodations without coaching. Early finishers independently check evidence choices; add no new scored questions.

### Words and supports
Use familiar paper-response access choices. Read the record exactly and neutrally. Do not define a tested mechanism through a helpful example during this sitting.

### Tomorrow
Day 81 returns E78, Q1a-d, Q2 and Q3 in Private feedback. Priority components receive four five-minute Return and repair rounds at most. Error A routes to centerward evidence, E to star category, F to controlled light. Other gaps get descriptive routes; one largest-gap retry follows.

### Credit
Ours. Exact survey and observatory records are invented for practice. Background: Sources S04-gravity, S04-distance and S04-light.
