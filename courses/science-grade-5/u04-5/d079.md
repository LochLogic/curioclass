# Day 79: Independent sky records

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Prepare end-day79.md as the two-sided student page with its blank axes and no duplicate questions; supply graph paper if a larger graph is needed. Keep completed models, current keys, day 78 papers and all day 80 records covered. Have courses/science-grade-5/u04-1/record.md for access notes, without formative judgments during the sitting. Check enlarged or tactile copies preserve all data without answers.

### The day

#### 3 min. Neutral directions
Day 78 connected familiar evidence; today each child works independently on unfamiliar records. Say that all supplied site records are invented for practice. Review independent paper work; explain page order, graph spaces and access choices. Read neutrally if requested; give no scientific hints. No E78 or locator feedback: hold errors A, C, D, G and F for 81. Public explanations are not required.

#### 12 min. Q1a
Children independently complete [[s04-Q1a]] using the supplied zero-based graph. Keep the stem visible; no answer or model display. Record only access support. Do not point toward a particular motion or direct a direction arrow. Extra graph paper preserves the same task.

#### 12 min. Q1b
Children independently complete [[s04-Q1b]]. The unfilled grid supports two series per month. Do not suggest comparisons or interpret the light-dark pattern. A scribe writes only the child's decisions; no revision of reasoning by the adult.

#### 12 min. Q1c
Children independently complete [[s04-Q1c]]. Preserve the child's group key and zero values. Read group letters, months and counts without interpreting them. Do not connect a motion to a timescale for the child.

#### 4 min. Q1d
Children independently complete [[s04-Q1d]]. Accept a short paragraph or labeled model explanation reflecting the child's own choices. No apparatus or class consensus is shown.

#### 2 min. Check and collect
Children review their own work unaided. Collect all thirty papers without answer discussion. After class, allow thirty two-minute readings, sixty teacher minutes, using only the points components in the separate key: five each for Q1a/b/c, one for Q1d, sixteen today. Keep observations and originals. Describe missing evidence and record matching errors B/C/G/D/H privately. Store points apart from the formative record. Return all component notes on 81, not 80.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the exact current stems and blank scales onto two paper sides. Use enlarged graph paper or tactile axes without completed marks. No digital tool is required.

### At home
**Swap.** Draw blank axes or use squared paper; copy today's stems exactly.

**Skip.** Public talk and group routines. Keep completed models and keys out of sight.

- **45 min. Assessment.** Use three minutes for neutral directions. Child answers [[s04-Q1a]], [[s04-Q1b]] and [[s04-Q1c]], twelve minutes each, then [[s04-Q1d]] for four. Allow two minutes for independent checking and collect. Read neutrally without hints; a scribe preserves the child's decisions. Keep the separate key private. Hold all feedback until day 81.

### Shorter or longer
Preserve all task time and components. Established access accommodations may extend a child's sitting privately; do not replace evidence with a coached answer. Secure early finishers review silently.

### Words and supports
Review only directions for using already familiar graph paper. Read labels and units neutrally. No mechanism word bank or sentence frame is supplied during assessment.

### Tomorrow
Day 80 collects two independent evidence arguments. Hold E78, locator and Q1a-d feedback for day 81 Private feedback. Route B to timed arrows, C to durations, G to daily spin, D to seasonal orbit and H to moon motion within its four-round ceiling.

### Credit
Ours. Exact records are invented for practice. Background: Sources S04-spin, S04-daylight, S04-stars and S04-moon.
