## c2-E82-plan
Compare salt addition with water-only controls across three pairs. Fix water amount, salt dose within treatments, cup type, stirring and observation interval. Repeats check consistency. Accept safe equivalent matched plans.

Look-fors: salt addition changed | relevant fixed conditions | three paired trials and reason

Meets: Only salt addition differs: 10 mL water per cup, 1 g salt per treatment. Same cups, stirring and interval; three pairs check consistency. | Not yet: Warm salt cups and cold controls isolate salt addition. (error V)

## c2-E82-log
Use actual before/after observations in six labeled cells and a personally observed action. Changes, unchanged results and unresolved readings count when accurate. Membership alone cannot show conducting.

Look-fors: identified safe personal action | three matched pairs | actual starting and resulting properties

Meets: I stirred treatment 2. Our six cells keep actual starting and resulting observations, even if grains remain. | Not yet: My partner performed every action; I copied the supplied record. (error U)

## c2-E82-claim
The claim is unsupported. Matched recovery properties are consistent with retained salt; clarity alone does not establish new material. Keep actual trial evidence separate. Recovery is not a universal no-reaction test.

Look-fors: warranted verdict | starting/resulting property comparison | controls and limits

Meets: Clarity is insufficient. Recovered grains match salt and controls leave none. These records do not support the new-substance claim. | Not yet: Every mixture makes a new substance, so clarity proves formation. (error W)
