# Day 84: Using feedback and checking climate readiness

## Teacher page

### Before class
Mark originals with check2-teacher-keys.md. Prepare check2-record.md, up to two assigned repair blocks and one matching half-sheet retry per child. Hold retries and climate prompt out of sight. Bring the dated shadow log. Check weather, safe access, stick and the established sun-time convention. Preserve a missing actual reading if the scheduled time cannot be reached.

### The day

#### 5 min. Private feedback
Return day 82 and day 83 papers: two minutes for materials notes, three for sky notes. Retain originals; use a second color for corrections. Review the single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing practical evidence stays not yet observed. Each child identifies one priority claim; no individual conference queue.

#### 15 min. Return and repair
Three five-minute rounds; assign at most two component repairs, then compare an annotation with evidence. Teacher visits three groups for four minutes each, with three routing minutes. More than ten matching misses replaces one round with whole-class support. Everyone keeps paper; public talk may be passed.

Use [[c2-P84-properties]] for error T; distinguish personal evidence for U. Use [[c2-P84-controls]] for V/W, [[c2-P84-light]] for X/Y/Z and [[c2-P84-pattern]] for AA/AB/AC. For X/Z or AB/AC, inspect the matching returned record first. **Ask.** Which record supports your correction? Expect the assigned property's test, controlled contrast or sky observation. Show the selected answer only after the attempt. Keep originals visible.

#### 10 min. Fresh retry exit ticket
Cover repairs. Give exactly one unseen route: [[c2-X84-material]], [[c2-X84-change]], [[c2-X84-light]] or [[c2-X84-pattern]]. Assign the priority claim, not every missed component. Children answer alone. Collect thirty slips; append a private note after class that day. Original results remain.

#### 5 min. Monthly shadow
Review the written day 53 fixed-stick routine. Use same stick, site and sun time. One minute to access the adjacent site, two helpers at thirty seconds each, one minute to share/record, one minute to return, one minute margin. Observe ground only, never the sun; adult supervises. Read to 1 cm; retry unclear once within the helper's turn. Everyone writes [[c2-P84-shadow]] in the dated log. Cloud, unsafe access or wrong time: retain missing; announce H84, invented for practice, 38 cm north, separately.

#### 10 min. Climate readiness
Last ten minutes only. Reveal [[c2-R84-climate]]: six independent minutes, then four minutes inspecting thirty papers, eight seconds each. Give no climate teaching or answers. Sort AD, one-day reasoning, and AE, reversed key, separately. Day 86 uses its existing ten-minute support.

### Other rooms
No outdoor access: H84 supplied record, never an actual monthly point. No printer: copy assigned blocks only. Adapt ruler grips or use a scribe without changing evidence choices.

### At home
**Swap.** No class stick: use H84, labelled invented, with actual monthly reading missing. No second color: underline corrections.

**Skip.** Group rotations and public reporting.

- **5 min. Return.** Review days 82 and 83 privately.
- **10 min. Repair.** Choose one: [[c2-P84-properties]], [[c2-P84-controls]], [[c2-P84-light]] or [[c2-P84-pattern]].
  **Say.** "Which record supports your correction?" Expect a matching observation, not a copied answer.
- **8 min. Retry.** Give the matching unseen [[c2-X84-material]], [[c2-X84-change]], [[c2-X84-light]] or [[c2-X84-pattern]] alone.
- **2 min. Shadow.** Write [[c2-P84-shadow]], keeping H84 separate.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Child answers [[c2-R84-climate]] alone. Keep it for day 86; explain no answers yet.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one repair, preserving retry, shadow and final readiness. Longer: add no new meeting.

### Words and supports
Frame: "My correction __. Supporting record __." Allow dictation. Climate words are read neutrally during readiness.

### Tomorrow
Day 85 opens Earth's systems. Day 86 receives climate readiness: three minutes compare long-term/daily records, four map-key practice, three fresh comparison. Teach everyone if over ten miss; otherwise target the gap group. Other unresolved gaps remain recorded.

### Credit
Ours. H84 and printed cases invented for practice. Background: Sources C2-properties, C2-mixing, C2-distance, C2-light, C2-stars and C2-climate.
