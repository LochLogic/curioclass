# Day 83: Recommending an evidence-based sky display

## Teacher page

### Before class
Day 82's assessment remains held for day 84, without feedback today.
Print two-sided pages. Keep check2-day83-keys.md separate. Prepare check2-record.md. Cover the sky displays until their part. Do not return day 82 feedback during this assessment.

### The day

#### 3 min. Independent directions
Yesterday, day 82 checked materials. Today uses a new observatory's instrument-cover and camera records. Announce all records and displays are invented for practice. Read directions neutrally. No apparatus or outdoor observation occurs. Children work alone; dictation preserves their choices.

#### 8 min. Distance display
Children complete [[c2-E83-distance]] in the recommendation table. Require evidence within the same row, rather than a second long argument. Assess sun category, much nearer sun and differently distant stars. Travel times compare distances without powers of ten.

#### 12 min. Brightness recommendation
Children complete [[c2-E83-light]]. They choose a brightness relation and a ray model, linking the selected trial evidence to the sun. Do not explain the ray choices during the check. Watch privately for error X, excluding sun from stars, and error Y, denying a distance effect. Keep omissions distinct from stated errors.

#### 8 min. Apparent size
Children complete [[c2-E83-size]] in the last table row. The equal-disk camera record tests apparent size reasoning, not a claim about every star's actual size. No answer display before collection.

#### 12 min. Supplied sky displays
Uncover side 2. Four minutes each: [[c2-E83-shadow]], [[c2-E83-daylight]], [[c2-E83-stars]]. Children annotate supplied graphs rather than construct them. This samples pattern interpretation only; do not report full 5-ESS1-2 performance. Different graph contexts and recommendation choices replace the recent written arguments and graph construction.

#### 2 min. Collect
Collect both sides from every child. No exit ticket. Mark after class with check2-day83-keys.md and check2-record.md, about sixty teacher minutes for thirty children. Retain original evidence and append private notes for day 84. Record errors Z, AA, AB or AC only when the stated claim matches.

### Other rooms
No printer: reproduce the recommendation choices and proportional bars on the board, with labels. Keep teacher answers separate. Enlarge bars or provide raised outlines and neutral label reading. No device needed.

### At home
**Swap.** No class display: point to the printed bars. Parent reads labels neutrally; keep teacher answers hidden.

**Skip.** Public reporting and group transitions.

- **3 min. Assessment directions.** Explain the invented records.
- **8 min. Distance.** Answer [[c2-E83-distance]] alone.
- **12 min. Light.** Answer [[c2-E83-light]] alone.
- **8 min. Size.** Answer [[c2-E83-size]] alone.
- **12 min. Patterns.** Answer [[c2-E83-shadow]], [[c2-E83-daylight]] and [[c2-E83-stars]] alone.
- **2 min. Keep.** Save originals for day 84. Discuss answers only after the work is complete.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: shorten directions, retaining evidence and all three pattern samples. Longer: add no assessed task.

### Words and supports
Apparent means how something looks to an observer. Offer large print, a scribe and neutral rereading. The child's evidence choices must remain independent.

### Tomorrow
Day 84 returns both assessment days. Error T or V/W routes to materials or control repair; X/Y/Z to light; AA/AB/AC to patterns. Monthly shadow and climate readiness keep their reserved time.

### Credit
Ours. Records invented for practice. Background: Sources C2-distance, C2-light and C2-stars.
