# Day 82: Testing unfamiliar material claims

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read check2-station.md and the separate check2-teacher-keys.md. Prepare the six group trays and check2-record.md. Check food allergies before salt; use paper if needed. Pretest portions, scale and timing. Reject sharp metals or dusty minerals. Label unknowns privately. Keep all answers covered.

### The day

#### 4 min. Directions and safety
Day 81 repaired sky evidence; today checks unfamiliar materials. Explain paper layout without solving anything. Announce all printed reference records are invented for practice. Goggles cover eyes; no tasting or smelling. Only listed salt and water are mixed, over trays. Sealed liquids stay sealed. This is independent evidence, with collaboration only during trials.

#### 28 min. Observed investigation
Six groups of five visit for four minutes each; four transition minutes total. Follow check2-station.md's exact turn budget. Each child personally measures and observes [[c2-E82-measure]]. Record the actual action and unit, never a copied value. Each has one identified mixing action in [[c2-E82-log]].

Waiting children independently complete [[c2-E82-plan]], then identification rows. Each group reconciles its plans before running three pairs. No scientific hints. Other groups' readings cannot replace a child's action. Finished groups wash at two sinks: three waves of twenty seconds, one minute during the next visit. Wiping uses transitions.

If an instrument fails, retry once within that turn, retain failure and use the station's invented fallback separately. No practical credit for supplied evidence.

#### 10 min. Independent evidence
Complete [[c2-E82-powder]], [[c2-E82-metal]], [[c2-E82-mineral]], [[c2-E82-liquid]], the trial log and [[c2-E82-claim]]. The claim uses a supplied follow-up, not a guaranteed classroom result. Collect every child's two sides. No exit ticket; these assessment items supply evidence.

#### 3 min. Clean-up
Last group washes for one minute. Close samples, wipe trays, clean goggles and dry tables for two minutes. Adult empties cups. Mark afterward using check2-record.md's single scale; allow sixty teacher minutes. Assign error T for color-only claims, V for confounded conditions and W for automatic formation claims; U only for copied evidence claimed as personal.

### Other rooms
No kit: use check2-station.md's invented fallback. Personal measuring and conducting remain not yet observed. No printer: copy only the student records and blanks. A scribe records choices without hints.

### At home
**Swap.** No group equipment: use the station's invented record; personal practical acts remain unobserved. Parent handles liquid disposal.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public reporting.

- **5 min. Prepare.** Read safety and directions; keep answers hidden.
- **15 min. Investigate.** Child plans [[c2-E82-plan]], measures [[c2-E82-measure]] if equipped, and runs [[c2-E82-log]] with you.
  **Say.** "What did you actually observe?" Expect only the child's recorded properties.
- **15 min. Identify.** Complete [[c2-E82-powder]], [[c2-E82-metal]], [[c2-E82-mineral]] and [[c2-E82-liquid]] alone.
- **7 min. Decide.** Answer [[c2-E82-claim]] alone.
- **3 min. Finish.** Wash, wipe and retain the work for day 84 feedback.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: reduce transitions, never remove personal acts or controlled repeats. Longer: add no new assessed task.

### Words and supports
Read labels neutrally. Accept dictated reasons and adapted grips. Reference, property and control keep their earlier meanings. Supply no example answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 83 checks a new distance recommendation and supplied sky displays. Retain today's originals. Day 84 returns days 82 and 83 in Private feedback, then repairs at most two priority components.

### Credit
Ours. Records are invented for practice. Background: Sources C2-properties, C2-mixing and C2-safety.
