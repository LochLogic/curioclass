## c2-P84-light
Light spreads over more area farther away; less reaches an equal patch at fixed output.

Look-fors: larger spread | less per equal patch

Meets: Farther away the same light spreads across more area; less reaches our equal patch. | Not yet: Distance cannot affect brightness. (error Y)
