## C: complete-day contrast

Invented strips: February daylight eight hours; August daylight sixteen hours.

```
February: LLLLLLLLdddddddddddddddd
August:   LLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLdddddddd
```

Each character is one hour. L is daylight; d is darkness.
