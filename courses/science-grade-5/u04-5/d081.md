# Day 81: Repairing and trying fresh evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read opener.md and teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Sort day 78 to 80 papers by priority component. Prepare score-record.md and the separate formative record. Select at most four repair-components.md blocks and one fresh-retry.md route per child. Keep retries hidden. Retrieve orbit-locator.md, earth-sun-moon-pieces.md, lamp-sequence.md and L75.md for isolated repair. Prepare earlier-earth.md's rain/map figures. Compose assigned blocks on one additional side; keep teacher keys separate.

### The day

#### 5 min. Private feedback
Return day 78 s04-X78 and s04-P78-locate, day 79's assessment Q1a-d, and day 80's assessment Q2/Q3. Allow one minute for E78, two for Q1 notes, two for Q2/Q3. Review private feedback, one largest-gap parallel repair route and fresh assigned retry from day 61. Preserve original points in score-record, thirty-six possible. Explain today's four-round ceiling; not every gap will be resolved. Review formative scale separately: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No public ranking.

#### 20 min. Return and repair
Review return and repair. Assign up to four five-minute component rounds per child, based on their own work. Teacher visits four groups for four minutes each; four routing minutes complete the twenty. Other children follow their selected isolated cards. Over ten matching misses makes one round whole-class instead of that visit.

Match cards: error A centerward [[s04-P81-repairA]]; error B timed arrows [[s04-P81-repairB]]; error C unequal daylight [[s04-P81-repairC]]; error D Earth orbit [[s04-P81-repairD]]; error E star category [[s04-P81-repairE]]; error F fixed conditions [[s04-P81-repairF]]; error G daily spin [[s04-P81-repairG]]; error H moon orbit [[s04-P81-repairH]]; error I nightly viewing direction [[s04-P81-repairI]]. Show only assigned components with their familiar supports, never retry records.

Each round: compare two minutes, annotate two, submit one. Review: revise the record in a second color, keeping the original observations and initials. **Ask.** Which record supports your correction? Expect the matching card observation. Unmatched omissions get descriptive notes. Keep unresolved gaps.

#### 15 min. Fresh retry
Reveal one assigned route only: [[s04-R81-gravity]], [[s04-R81-sky]], [[s04-R81-stars]], [[s04-R81-moon]] or [[s04-R81-light]]. Everyone answers their largest-gap route independently. Cover repairs; collect all thirty cards. Secure children state one limit within their assigned standard. A single route provides evidence only for what it asks; original assessment points stay unchanged.

#### 5 min. Earlier Earth review
Review grade 4 weathering: breaking rock material down; carrying is different. Use earlier-earth's invented rain diagram and [[s04-P81-rain]]. Then review key, a box that tells what each mark means, using [[s04-P81-map]]. Read and annotate together, no disaster stories. These retrieval marks stay in folders; note transport gaps for day 85 and map gaps for 92. This is not unit 5 readiness.

After class: thirty sixty-second retries, up to 120 ten-second annotations, and sixty ten-second retrieval scans take at most sixty minutes. Return private retry/repair notes on day 81 after class. Retain unresolved gaps for the day 83 sky check; add no repair meeting.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy assigned blocks and one retry onto paper. Use isolated enlarged/tactile components or a scribe preserving choices. No device or apparatus needed.

### At home
**Swap.** Copy only assigned component blocks and one fresh-retry card. Use drawn paper model pieces.

**Skip.** Group routing and public talk.

- **5 min. Return.** Read original feedback privately.
- **12 min. Repair.** Use selected repair-components cards; keep originals visible and annotate in another color.
  **Say.** "Which record supports your correction?" Expect a matching observation on the assigned card.
- **12 min. Retry.** Hide repairs. Child completes one assigned fresh-retry card alone. Return a private next-step note afterward.
- **6 min. Earlier Earth.** Use [[s04-P81-rain]] and [[s04-P81-map]] with the page figures.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: retain highest-priority repairs and full fresh retry. Longer: explain a same-standard model boundary; add no new scored task.

### Words and supports
Review gravity, spin, orbit and apparent brightness as assigned. Review weathering and map key in the final part. Read labels neutrally. Revision box: "My correction __. Supporting record __."

### Tomorrow
Day 82 checks unfamiliar materials and new-substance claims. No new unit 5 readiness today; that belongs to 84. Keep unresolved sky components with original points and retry notes for day 83.

### Credit
Ours. Supplied records are invented for practice. Background: Sources S04-gravity, S04-spin, S04-daylight, S04-stars, S04-moon, S04-distance and S04-light.
