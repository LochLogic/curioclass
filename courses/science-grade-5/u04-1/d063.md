# Day 63: Three evidence supports

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current CSV answers before teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Use the corrected E63 observing-period guidance. Prepare today's half slip from exits. Sort day 61's three row results using readiness.md; missing results mean explicit support for everyone. Return day 62 descriptive notes. Keep apparatus put away. Use the compact support records below, adapted from the three named pieces, without duplicate sheets.

### The day

#### 3 min. Opening

Return day 62 s04-X62, s04-P62-label, s04-P62-actual and s04-P62-hook privately. Honor its Tomorrow: keep all three readiness blocks intact. Review grade 4 return and repair. Explain three routes. Review formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Initial-model descriptive notes remain separate.

#### 10 min. Noncontact support

Return day 61 s03-N61a notes. Three minutes: read separate invented magnet/clip and rubbed-plastic/paper records. Review grade 3 non-contact force arrow across a gap and grade 4 contact, touching. Four minutes: model how air separates objects while movement supports interaction. Three minutes: alone, [[s04-P63-gap]]. Ready children locate evidence limits. Everyone submits paper; no thirty spoken turns.

#### 10 min. Sky support

Return day 61 s03-N61b notes. Three minutes: review grade 1, the moon can be in the day sky; not seen is not gone. Read sun-moon-strips as invented pattern cards. Four minutes: model continuing the sun's direction beyond chimney without claiming exact positions. Three minutes: independently [[s04-P63-path]]. Ordinary stars can be hidden in bright daytime sky; darkness permits seeing them, subject to clouds and direction. Keep gravity separate.

#### 10 min. Daylight support

Return day 61 s03-N61c notes. Three minutes: review grade 1, more daylight, less daylight. Four minutes: read complete-day strips, counting light and remaining dark symbols separately. Each full day has twenty-four hour symbols. These invented strips simplify light and dark; twilight can remain bright after sunset. Three minutes: [[s04-P63-day]]. Longer light leaves less remaining darkness in a fixed full day. No observing-duration calculation or cause of seasons.

#### 8 min. Model return and repair

Five minutes for day 62 error A: compare a correct tilted disk for two, annotate original labels for two, submit in one. If over ten of thirty claim page-bottom everywhere, make this whole-class instead of parallel groups. Others locate evidence limits. Preserve originals. Remaining three minutes review centerward labels and gravity without contact. Show only correct force-arrow cards; cover explanatory text before identifying labels.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Give [[s04-X63]] unseen, alone: three minutes and one collecting thirty slips and pages. After class, thirty forty-five-second exit readings plus thirty twenty-second practice scans take thirty-two-and-a-half minutes. Use current look-fors: centerward rightward pull without contact; winter's longer potential observing period. Do not equate all sun-below-horizon time with fully dark sky. Return on day 64, with A/C in its ten-minute slot.
