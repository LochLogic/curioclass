# Section 4.1: Which way is down?

## Purpose and science

Earth's pull near its surface points approximately toward its center. A freely hanging stationary weight aligns with local down; tension supports it. Name the gravitational force, not net force or a motion path. Supply round Earth; these falls do not prove its shape.

Use argument with gravity/cause or stars/scale; use graphs with sky patterns. Separate observations from model inferences.

Teacher alone handles soft ball and short enclosed soft-weight cord, below waist over tray. Mark observer line; teacher and nearby observers wear impact goggles. No swinging or throwing. Three cleanup minutes cover retrieval and parallel approved-wipe goggle care. No kit: original drop-cord-records, explicitly supplied.
