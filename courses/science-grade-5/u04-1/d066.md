### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 65 s04-X65, s04-P65-fragments and s04-P65-link. Review return and repair. If many tickets show error A, compare a correct tilted disk for two minutes, trace centerward direction for two, retain notes in one. Do this orally or by pointing; no revised writing in fluency practice. Over ten matching misses makes this whole-class. Others locate evidence limits. Preserve originals. Missing causal links alone get descriptive notes.

#### 5 min. Recall

Review day 62 center and plumb line; day 64 free-versus-constrained evidence. Review grade 3 gravity, a pull down, extended here toward Earth's center. Retrieve grade 4 force arrow: starts on the thing pushed, points the way the force goes. All shown arrows are correct. Children point to origin, direction and force name. No wrong-arrow correction in practice.

#### 21 min. Evidence rehearsal

Six minutes: pairs inspect two separate correct practice diagrams below, reading one observation label each. Six minutes: silently locate labels with [[s04-P66-locate]], then partners point to a supporting feature. Four minutes: teacher distinguishes observed ball motion from gravitational arrow. Cord support holds its weight still; gravity still acts. No written explanation is drafted or edited.

Last five minutes: rotating six heard, forty-five seconds each; record who and permit passing. Review grade 4 routine: rotating six heard, record sheet names who. All thirty return locator notes. If many choose a model label instead of an observation, indicate text reporting what happened, then let them point again.

#### 10 min. Gravity consensus

Teacher fills evidence-chart, preserving original observations and dating consensus. Five minutes connect separately located fall and free-cord evidence to Earth's center-directed pull. Children point to evidence locations without rehearsing a new paired-fall argument. Three minutes mark limits: round Earth supplied, tied cords not independent direction evidence, no numerical gravity. Two minutes revisit shadow-puzzle's unresolved change. Gravity explains falling; today's records do not explain changing shadows. Next investigate hourly length and direction.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s04-X66]] for three minutes alone; collect thirty slips and locator pages in one. This independent transfer corrects an unfamiliar diagram using prior learning. After class, thirty thirty-second exit readings plus thirty fifteen-second locator scans take twenty-two-and-a-half minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Reserve error A for an explicit universal page-bottom claim; wrong origin or page-right alone gets descriptive notes. Return on 67.
