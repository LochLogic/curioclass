# Day 65: Connect evidence to a claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read current item answers before teacher-only-answer-keys.md. Prepare today's half slip from exits. Sort day 64 private notes. Copy only the one-site fragments to the student page; keep keys covered. Display correct slanted diagram and evidence-chart. No apparatus is used.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 64 s04-X64, s04-P64-free, s04-P64-observe and s04-P64-label. If many tickets show error A, rotate a correct tilted disk: compare two minutes, annotate two, retain one. Over ten matching misses makes this whole-class. Others locate what cord records cannot establish. Review return and repair; retain originals and private notes. No held paired-fall records.

#### 6 min. Argument components

Review day 18 claim-evidence-reasoning and day 62's use: claim, supporting observation and why they connect. Explain again with one slanted site. Earth pulls inward is the claim. Free weight hangs inward is the observation. Gravity explains alignment links cause and effect. A still weight has support as well as gravity. Children read fragments before the key is revealed.

#### 22 min. Fragment workshop

Four minutes: pairs sort [[s04-P65-fragments]] without an answer display. Four minutes: locate the observation in drop-cord-records' single-site description. Five minutes: reveal [[steps:s04-P65-fragments]], modeling why alignment supports the proposed pull without proving Earth's shape. Four minutes: everyone independently completes [[s04-P65-link]], annotating relevance instead of drafting a paired argument.

Final five minutes: rotating six heard, forty-five seconds each; record who, permit passing. All thirty return paper. Review grade 4 observation versus inference: hanging is observed or supplied; Earth's pull is the explanatory model. If many merely repeat the claim, locate the particular observed alignment. No second site or held record appears.

#### 8 min. Correct-model rehearsal

Display the existing slanted earth-disk arrow and one-site evidence-chart. Children point to object, center and supporting observation, then repeat with a partner. All arrows are correct. Teacher states the limit: a supplied disk is a model, not proof of Earth's round shape. Check relevance annotations privately. No new material or integrated two-fall argument.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Reveal [[s04-X65]] only now. Three minutes alone, one collecting thirty slips and pages. Read neutrally. After class, thirty forty-five-second exit readings plus thirty twenty-second practice scans take thirty-two-and-a-half minutes. Review scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Give descriptive gap notes unless the child actually claims universal page-bottom down. Return on day 66.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy fragments and response spaces; give held exit separately. No projector: display a correct practice disk on the board. No apparatus is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy fragments and disk without a printer. Adult replaces partner.

**Skip.** Public reports and class sorting.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 64 notes; locate center on the familiar disk.
- **8 min. Fragments.** Review claim, observation and reason. Child does [[s04-P65-fragments]] before checking.
  **Say.** "Which fragment reports what happened?" Expect: free weight hangs inward.
- **12 min. Connection.** Child does [[s04-P65-link]]. Point to evidence on the correct one-site diagram.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X65]] alone. Check afterward using its current answer.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports; keep relevance and exit. The plan's alternate path substitutes day 66 consensus rehearsal and E66 for today's rehearsal and E65, never both exits. Longer: discuss evidence limits.

### Words and supports

Review claim, observation, reason, gravity and center. Read fragments aloud. A scribe records the child's links without adding them.

### Tomorrow

Day 66 returns E65 and fragment/link notes. If many tickets show error A, trace center on a correct disk for five minutes. Then locate evidence on correct diagrams and record consensus. Missing links get descriptive notes.

### Credit

Ours. One-site records and fragments are invented for practice.
