# Day 62: Which way is down?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Day 61's exit ticket and written task feedback already returned after class on day 61.

Read current items.csv answers before teacher-only-answer-keys.md; use current rows if they differ. Prepare today's half slips from exits. Send family.md home separately. Inspect enclosed weight, cord and ball. Mark observer line beside low tray. Set goggles and wipes for parallel cleanup. Display only today's diagrams; cover exits. Keep actual-log separate from supplied records.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook and retrieval

Pick up day 61's Tomorrow: start falling-object models; readiness results wait until day 63. Whole class watches one protected low drop. Teacher and nearby observers wear impact goggles. No throwing or swinging. Show australia-diagram, explicitly invented for practice, then shadow-puzzle separately. Review grade 1 shadow stick: morning tracing beside its after-lunch shadow. **Ask.** [[s04-P62-hook]] Expect both supplied, plus a question about changing shadows. Post the unit question about falling objects, shadows and stars.

Within three minutes, review grade 4 force arrow: starts on the thing pushed, points the way the force goes. It names a force on an object, not energy's destination or its travel path.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Review grade 3 force, a push or a pull on one object; gravity, a pull down. Teach center, the middle of the supplied round-Earth model. Near Earth's surface, its gravitational pull points approximately centerward. Supply round Earth; these falls do not prove its shape.

Show earth-disk's single slanted site. **Ask.** [[s04-P62-label]] Wait, then reveal [[steps:s04-P62-label]]. Teach plumb line: freely hanging cord and weight. A stationary free cord aligns with local down; its support holds the weight up. Gravity still acts. No force calculation.

#### 16 min. Observation records

Four setup minutes, then six groups of five watch teacher-only demonstrations for two minutes each. Others work quietly on their records. Everyone completes [[s04-P62-actual]], including date and conditions. Observe release and stationary cord separately. No numeric instrument reading is claimed. Repeat an obscured view once; unresolved stays missing. Without safe kit, use drop-cord-records, saying invented for practice and supplied, never actual observation.

Review day 18 claim-evidence-reasoning: claim, supporting observation and why they connect. Gravity is the proposed cause of falling. Keep observations when explanations change.

#### 7 min. Model labels

Alone, finish the existing-arrow label, retaining initial ideas. Hear rotating six, forty-five seconds each within five minutes; anyone may pass. Review grade 4 routine: rotating six heard, record sheet names who. All thirty return paper. Teacher keeps the correct model and evidence-chart. If many confuse motion with force, locate arrow origin and force name.

#### 4 min. Fresh evidence

Reveal [[s04-X62]] only now. Three minutes alone, one collecting thirty slips. Read neutrally; accept child-directed scribing. Initial models get descriptive notes, not demanded consensus. After class, thirty thirty-second exit readings plus thirty fifteen-second practice scans take twenty-two-and-a-half minutes. Return on 63.

#### 3 min. Cleanup

Adult retrieves ball and contains cord. Goggle users wipe and bag in parallel with approved wipes.

### Other rooms

No kit: supplied drop-cord-records. No printer: copy current practice and exit exactly, on separate paper. Keep held cases covered.

### At home

**Swap.** Use supplied diagrams instead of ball, cord or goggles. Copy without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public reports.

- **7 min. Notice.** Read australia-diagram and shadow-puzzle as invented. Do [[s04-P62-hook]].
  **Say.** "Which records were supplied?" Expect: both printed records, unlike a witnessed drop.
- **8 min. Model.** Explain center and plumb line. Child does [[s04-P62-label]] before checking its answer.
- **12 min. Record.** Child does [[s04-P62-actual]] using a labeled supplied fallback. Discuss force versus motion.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Give [[s04-X62]] alone. Give private descriptive feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports; retain records and exit. Longer: distinguish cord support from Earth's pull.

### Words and supports

Center, plumb line, gravity. Enlarge diagrams; read neutrally and scribe chosen directions. Public talk is optional.

### Tomorrow

Day 63 returns practice notes in Opening. If many tickets show error A, page-bottom everywhere, rotate a correct slanted disk in Model return and repair. Preserve all three ten-minute readiness supports.

Ours. Supplied diagrams and exact shadow values are invented for practice.
