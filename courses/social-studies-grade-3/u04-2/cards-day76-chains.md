# Day 76: two page summaries for the chains

Each says only what its page says. Where a page is silent, the child writes "the page does not say". A seasonal camp is a place people stay in one season.

**Narragansett seasonal homes.** The tribe's own page says that in earlier times members had two homes: a winter home and a summer home. In summer the tribe moved to the shore. Summer shelters on the coast were made of bark and woven mats. A winter longhouse held up to 20 families. Today the Narragansett Indian Tribe is a federally recognized Tribal Nation in Rhode Island. Credit: the Narragansett Indian Tribe's Early History and Tribal Government pages.

**Penobscot River.** The Penobscot Nation's own lessons call the river the heart of Penobscot culture. For thousands of years the Penobscots have relied on Maine's rivers for food, drinking water and travel. Credit: the Penobscot Nation's own pages.
