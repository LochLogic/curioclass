# Penobscot map, a teaching sketch

A teaching sketch, not to scale. It is not the Nation's own map; the Nation's own map is the authority, and the teacher may print it beside this one. Shading is approximate, with no hard edge.

```
   MAINE                                        (dashed line = state border)
   - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
   |                                                 |
   |        ~ ~ ~ the Penobscot River ~ ~ ~          |
   |     ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::          |
   |     ::::::::::::::::::::::::[#]:::::::          |
   |        ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~         |
   - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
   Key:  :::: = the river area the Penobscots rely on (a sketch, not the homeland's edge)
         [#]  = Indian Island, the Nation's offices
         - - -  = the state border
```
