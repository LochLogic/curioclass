# Map legend, three marks

Name: ______________________

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| ::::: (shading) | An area a map marks for a nation, such as a homeland or the river area its people rely on. It is not exact. |
| [#] (square) | A place named on the nation's page today, such as its offices. It is one place, not the whole homeland. |
| - - - - (dashed line) | A state border. It is another map. |
