# Teacher key, exit cards days 75 and 76

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-75-76.md); never hand to children.

- Day 75, ask 1: Penobscot Nation, Narragansett Indian Tribe, Wampanoag Tribe of Gay Head (Aquinnah), in any order, by dots 1, 2 and 3. Not Maine, Rhode Island and Massachusetts (A). Ask 2: An X on the square (the town hall), not on the shading (the park). Not an X over all the shaded area (T).
- Day 76: Canoes on the river carry people and food between homes and gardens. Not "the river alone decided where they live" (C); not "every nation uses canoes the same way" (J); not "there is a river there" (L).

Marking: shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. The day 76 written task (two chains and one sentence) has three look-fors: a feature named from the page, a practice named from the page, and no claim that the feature alone decided.
