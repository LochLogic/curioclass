# Exit cards, days 75 and 76

Print "made up, for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken.

## Day 75: Exit map, two panels

Panel 1. A rough New England outline with three numbered dots. Clue words by each dot.

```
   Maine        (1) by a river
   Vermont  New Hampshire
        Massachusetts
   Connecticut   Rhode Island
        (2) on the shore        (3) on an island
```

Ask 1: Exit map, panel 1. Write the names of the three nations marked.

Panel 2. A made-up map of Pine Lake Park, a made-up town. Made up, for practice.

```
   +--------------------------------+
   |  ::::::::::::::::::            |
   |  ::::::   LAKE   ::::::        |
   |  :::::::::::::::::[#]          |
   +--------------------------------+
   ::::  = the park     [#]  = the town hall     +---+ = county line
```

Ask 2: Exit map, panel 2. Put an X on the town hall.

## Day 76: Alder Bend Card

Neighbors today in a made-up town, Alder Bend, use canoes to carry food between their homes and riverside gardens.

Ask: Alder Bend Card, invented. How does the river help connect homes and gardens?
