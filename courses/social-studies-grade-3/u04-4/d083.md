# Day 83: What a nation shares, and how we know

## Teacher page

### Before class
Tally day 82's exit by error C (the grass decided) or L (there is grass); the key is key-exits-77-82.md. Print a table for each child, four cards for each pair. Draw three boxes on the board: before, action, after. Keep key-exits-83-85.md out until slips are in.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 82's exit slips (s04-E82, s04-E82b) and written task (s04-P82a to s04-P82d), each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Spend four minutes on the more-missed of C or L. C: "The grass decided" beside "Summer grass feeds the cows, so the farm puts the shed beside it." L: "There is grass" beside the same right answer. The other letter meets you at the side table.

#### 8 min. A contribution and a biography
New word: a contribution is something a nation shares or makes that goes on today. Read Card 1 aloud: the Penobscot Nation shares a River unit on its own curriculum page (you met it in section 4.2). Say: "The Nation shares this, its page says." Read Card 2 aloud, Carol A. Dana. Review: a biography is the true story of one person's life, told by someone else (unit 1). Fill the before, action, after boxes on the board from the card only; say that is all it says. Children write [[s04-P83a]].

#### 10 min. Sort four cards
Pairs read all four cards: River, Dana, basket (Makah) and wild rice (Menominee; review of day 79). Pairs fill the table: kind, nation and source. Children write [[s04-P83b]], then fix a weak card, "Nations shared things," with the frame "The ___ Nation ___. The source is ___.": [[s04-P83c]]. Each card is one nation's own example, never every nation's. If a pair says Native peoples shared nothing useful (error U), have them read the River card and name the knowledge in it. Hear six pairs, a minute each. On the question board answer "What contributions continue?" Children write [[s04-P83f]].

#### 15 min. Write with sources
Each child picks the better of two cards and says why: [[s04-P83d]]. Then one sentence: [[s04-P83e]]. Scribe allowed. Look-fors: a nation, a source, present tense where the page says it continues. Mark as before.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Contribution Card, first seen here. Read the card and asks once. Every child writes [[s04-E83]] and [[s04-E83b]]. Key: key-exits-83-85.md. Mark each as before.

**Shared shape.** The model and practice read four cards that name a nation and a source; the exit gives one new Penobscot Nation card and asks what it documents and where it comes from.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the table on the board and read the cards aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed table: draw four rows by hand.

**Skip.** The sort of the four cards.

- **8 min. Two cards.** Read Card 1 and Card 2. [[s04-P83a]] **Say.** "What does the Nation share?" Expect: its River unit, or its language lessons.
- **12 min. The table.** Fill the table. [[s04-P83b]] [[s04-P83c]] (fix with "The ___ Nation ___. The source is ___.")
- **15 min. Write.** [[s04-P83d]] [[s04-P83e]] [[s04-P83f]]
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card once. [[s04-E83]] [[s04-E83b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one card from the table, [[s04-P83d]] only. Longer: add a fifth card from another page and name its source.

### Words and supports
Words: contribution, nation, source, kind, today. Frame: "The ___ Nation ___. The source is ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 84 teaches sovereignty, a nation's right to govern itself. It opens with whichever of error G (every nation shared the same thing) or O (they shared long ago, and that is all) more children made today.
