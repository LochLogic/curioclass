# Day 85: Asking questions of two sources

## Teacher page

### Before class
Tally day 84's exit for error F (a tribal government is a town department). Print the student page and the event cards, two Cherokee cards per pair. Keep key-exits-83-85.md out until the slips are in.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 84's slips (s04-E84, s04-E84b) and written task (s04-P84a to s04-P84e), each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. If error F was common, take four minutes: "A tribal government is a town department" beside "A tribal government speaks for its own people; its page says it has its own laws and elections." Point at the day 84 table. The E83 leftovers (errors G and O) meet you at the side table during the pairs part.

#### 8 min. Two sources, one event
Model with Bridge Day, made up for practice. Read the photograph card: a newspaper photographer who was there, dated 8 June. Read the club history: written in 2010 by a member who was not there; it says the bridge opened in June. Review from unit 4.1: primary and secondary, for one question. The photograph is primary for the opening; the history is secondary. Review from grade 2: two tellers who each saw it and agree are more trustworthy, and a copy is the same teller. Here only one teller saw it. A question connects a source to the event: "When did the bridge open?" Asking does not check anything.

#### 10 min. Ask and compare
Pairs reread the two day 78 cards, the Cherokee Nation's and the Eastern Band's. Children write [[s04-P85a]], [[s04-P85b]] and [[s04-P85c]]. Hear six pairs, a minute each. Say the removal plainly; any child may pass, and no child is asked to read the removal cards aloud.

#### 15 min. Before, after and the makers
Each child writes [[s04-P85d]]. Read event cards 1 to 3 aloud once, plainly, and set them out already in order; then pairs lay cards 4 to 6 before or after those three, with no dates drawn and no strip. Each child writes [[s04-P85e]]. On the question board answer "How can sources help us know?" Children write [[s04-P85f]]. Mark as before: a question about the event, a before and after in words, no claim that one source proves a cause.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Boat Photo and Harbor Note, made up, for practice, first seen here. Read it once. Every child writes [[s04-E85]] and [[s04-E85b]]. Key: key-exits-83-85.md. Return written feedback by day 86.

**Shared shape.** The model and practice read a photograph and history, then two real pages and event cards; the exit gives two new made-up sources and asks a question and which came first.

### Other rooms
No printer: read the cards aloud; write the six events on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No event cards: write the six events on slips of paper.

**Skip.** The six event cards.

- **12 min. Two accounts.** Read the two Cherokee cards aloud once, plainly. [[s04-P85a]] [[s04-P85b]] [[s04-P85c]] **Say.** "What do both cards agree on?" Expect: the Trail of Tears.
- **10 min. Makers.** [[s04-P85d]] **Say.** "Who made each card?" Expect: two different governments.
- **12 min. Events.** Lay the six event cards in order. [[s04-P85e]] [[s04-P85f]]
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Read the two sources once. [[s04-E85]] [[s04-E85b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: [[s04-P85b]] and [[s04-P85e]] only. Longer: ask a second question of the two cards.

### Words and supports
Words: question, source, agree, differ, before, after, maker. Frame: "___ came before ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 86 begins section 4.5 with a short claim that answers the unit question with named sources. It opens by checking the supporting-question record and returning today's written feedback.
