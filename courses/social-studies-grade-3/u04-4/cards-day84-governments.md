# Day 84: sovereignty and the governments card

The teacher says it once: "Sovereignty is a nation's right to govern itself." Nations existed before the United States and still govern today. Governments talk to one another. There are legal limits, and the teacher holds them.

**The Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians.** The Eastern Band has a Principal Chief and a Tribal Council. The Eastern Band's page says Cherokee is a sovereign nation. It has its own laws, elections, government and institutions. The Eastern Band is at Cherokee, North Carolina, in the Qualla Boundary. Credit: the Eastern Band's own Government page.

**The Navajo Nation (the day 80 card).** Council delegates meet in the Council Chambers to talk about problems and pass laws. In 1991 the government was reorganized into three branches.

**Our town and our state.** The teacher uses the class's unit 2 cards.

The table compares four governments: who leads, who decides, who it speaks for. Do not impose a pattern. The class says what each page says.
