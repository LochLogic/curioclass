# Teacher key, exit cards days 83 to 85

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-83-85.md); never hand to children.

- Day 83, ask 1: In the Nation's Homeland unit, students study maps with place names in Penobscot and learn what they mean, then use what they learn to find their way along the river. Not "every nation shared the same thing" (G); not "Native peoples gave nothing useful" (U); not "they shared names long ago, and that is all" (O). Ask 2: The Penobscot Nation's own curriculum page.
- Day 84, ask 1: Its leaders spoke for the people to state officials. Not "a tribal government is a town department" (F). Ask 2: two separate governments that meet and speak with each other, government to government. Not "the tribe is part of the state" (F).
- Day 85, ask 1: A question about the boats, such as why more boats were at the dock later. Not a question about which picture is prettier (Q); not "I asked why, so I know why" (E). Ask 2: The photograph, dated 2 May, shows two boats before the note, dated 4 May, counts five. Neither proves why.

Marking: shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. The day 83 written task (two cards and one sentence) is read with the key. The day 85 written feedback is returned before day 86 where possible.
