# Bridge Day cards, made up, for practice

Print "made up, for practice" at the bottom of each card. The teacher models these on day 85.

**Bridge Day photograph.** A crowd walks onto a new footbridge. Made by: a newspaper's photographer, who was there. Date: 8 June.

**Bridge Club History.** A short history of the club, written in 2010 by a club member who was not there that day. It says the bridge opened in June.

The model: ask what the history is secondary for, and what the photograph is primary for. Two tellers who each saw it, and agree, are two sources. A copy of one account is the same teller. A question connects a source to the event. Asking does not check.
