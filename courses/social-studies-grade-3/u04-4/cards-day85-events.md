# Day 85: six event cards

Each card is one event. There are no dates drawn and no strip. The teacher reads cards 1 to 3 aloud once, plainly, and sets them out already in order; no child is asked to read these cards aloud, and any child may pass. Pairs lay cards 4 to 6 before or after those three. Each child writes "______ came before ______" and one question the cards raise.

1. The Cherokee people lived across much of the Southeast.
2. The United States government forced the Cherokee people to leave.
3. Many Cherokee people died on the Trail of Tears.
4. Some Cherokee people remained in the East and rebuilt in North Carolina.
5. Today the Cherokee Nation governs in eastern Oklahoma.
6. Today the Eastern Band has a Principal Chief and a Tribal Council.

Removal accounts: the two day 78 cards, the Cherokee Nation's and the Eastern Band's. Pairs ask one question of the pair, mark what agrees and what differs, and say whether the makers are separate.
