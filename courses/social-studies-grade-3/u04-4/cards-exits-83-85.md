# Exit cards, days 83 to 85

Print "made up, for practice" in small letters on the made-up card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken.

## Day 83: Contribution Card

The Penobscot Nation has a Homeland unit in its lessons. Students study maps with place names in Penobscot and explore what the names mean. Then they use what they learn to find their way along the Penobscot River.

Source of this card: the Penobscot Nation's own curriculum page.

Ask 1: Contribution Card. What contribution does the Nation's page document?
Ask 2: Contribution Card. Name the source that shows it.

## Day 84: Eastern Band Notice

On 16 April 2025 the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians posted a notice. Principal Chief Michell Hicks and other tribal leaders went to Raleigh. They met the governor, state officials and lawmakers. They spoke for the tribe's priorities. The notice says they want to stay in touch at state and federal levels.

Ask 1: Eastern Band Notice. What shows the Eastern Band is acting as a government?
Ask 2: Eastern Band Notice. Does the notice show that a law changed?

## Day 85: Boat Photo and Harbor Note, made up, for practice

The photograph was taken at the dock by a visitor. It is dated 2 May. It shows two boats. The harbor note was written by the harbor office. It is dated 4 May. It counts five boats at the dock.

Ask 1: Boat Photo and Harbor Note, invented. Ask one question about both sources.
Ask 2: Boat Photo and Harbor Note. Which came first, the photograph or the note?
