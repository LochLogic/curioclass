# Techiman Traders Record, facts from a Ghanaian newspaper, 2023

Our own words, not quoted. Teacher reads each chunk aloud once. These are two chunks of one report; the facts on how the market began and who runs it are held back for day 67.

Techiman is a market in Ghana where many traders sell.

## Chunk 1

A vegetable trader named Jennifer Owusu-Mensah says business is good in the market. Traders come to it from all over West Africa. She says shoppers can buy many kinds of goods there, from food to building materials.

## Chunk 2

A man named Kusi Appiah thinks it is one of the best markets in the region. He says traders still face problems: rubbish is handled badly, some women sell food without stall space, streetlights do not work, and safety is a worry. He asked the police for more patrols and asked the town's assembly to act. (Our note: big markets in any country have problems like these.)

The report names two people. Not every trader is said to agree.
