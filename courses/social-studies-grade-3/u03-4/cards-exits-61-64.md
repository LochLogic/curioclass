# Exit cards, days 61 to 64

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken.

## Day 61: Origin Label Card

A label on a box says "Tea. Grown in Sri Lanka." A shop in our town sells the tea. The map legend: an arrow shows a good moving to a place.

Ask 1: Origin Label Card, invented. Trace the tea from its country to our town.
Ask 2: Origin Label Card. How does the shop depend on the grower?

## Day 62: Cocoa Card

A cocoa grower sells dried beans to a chocolate maker. The chocolate maker sells the grower a bean dryer (made up).

Ask 1: Cocoa Card, invented. What does the grower need from the chocolate maker?
Ask 2: Cocoa Card. What does the chocolate maker need from the grower?

## Day 63: Ghana Exit Locator

Three shaded shapes on a plain outline map of West Africa, marked only A, B and C, with no names on them. The wall locator map from day 63 sits beside it.

Ask 1: Ghana Exit Locator. Which shape is the country Ghana today?
Ask 2: Ghana Exit Locator. How do you know?

Exit box, extra. Trader Card, invented: a trader sells peppers to cooks and buys peppers from a farmer. Print "made up, not from Ghana" on the card.
Trader Card, invented. Name one way she earns or spends.

## Day 64: Marsh End and Dunmore cards

Marsh End market (made up): it has covered stalls where people sell fruit. Dunmore market (made up): it has shared bays where people set out cloth.

Marsh End and Dunmore cards. Write one likeness, with a detail from each.
