# Day 62: Trade works both ways

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 61's slips for error P and error I and pick the more-missed. Draw the Hill Street Cafe card, labeled made up: a cafe here buys bananas from Ecuador and sells banana bread. Draw the Corner Creamery card, labeled made up: a creamery here buys vanilla from Madagascar and sells ice cream. Keep key-exits-61-64.md away until slips are in. Before day 66, write one cue on each child's cue sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 61's slips (s03-E61, s03-E61b). Whole class, the more-missed. Error P: "the arrow shows how they depend" beside "the arrow shows the goods move; each side also needs what the other gives". Error I: "now nobody here works" beside "the bought bananas become banana bread the cafe bakes here". Use the Hill Street Cafe card. The other goes to the side table during Two-way arrows.

#### 8 min. A dated order history
Review: a buyer and a seller, from kindergarten. New: Brightside Snacks (made up) is in our state; a lime grower in Mexico sells it limes. Show the strip. Year 1: 100 boxes ordered and paid for. Year 2: 300 boxes. Say: the maker relies on the grower for limes; the grower relies on the maker's orders for sales. Each side needs the other more as orders grow. Each child writes [[s03-P62a]]. Hear a rotating six.

#### 10 min. Two-way arrows
Pairs fill the blank sheet: limes go from the grower to the maker, money goes back. Each child writes [[s03-P62b]] and [[s03-P62c]]. Visit a rotating six pairs. The side table of up to six takes the other of error P or I for 5 minutes, using the Corner Creamery card.

#### 8 min. Local work stays
Review: an import is a good that comes into a country (grade 2). The imported limes arrive, and the maker mixes and packs the snack here. Local work does not end. Pairs trace the Corner Creamery card: vanilla comes in, and the creamery makes the ice cream here. Each child writes [[s03-P62d]]. Hear a rotating six.

#### 6 min. Prices
Review: price, from kindergarten. Fewer limes, or more orders, may lead the grower to set a higher price. Buyers may buy less, or choose another seller, so a seller cannot charge just anything. No graphs. Each child writes [[s03-P62e]]. The page stays in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the quarter slip with the Cocoa Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E62]] and [[s03-E62b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** Pairs show limes and money going opposite ways and what a late delivery changes; the exit asks what each side needs from the other, in a card with new goods.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the strip on the board; pairs draw two arrows on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: write Year 1, 100 boxes and Year 2, 300 boxes on paper.
**Skip.** The prices note.

- **10 min. Model.** Say: "A grower in Mexico sells limes to Brightside Snacks. Year 1 is 100 boxes, Year 2 is 300." Draw limes going one way, money the other.
- **20 min. Arrows.** Your child writes [[s03-P62a]], [[s03-P62b]], [[s03-P62c]], [[s03-P62d]] and [[s03-P62e]].
  **Say.** "Who relies on whom in Year 2?" Expect: each side relies on the other more.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E62]] [[s03-E62b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: leave out the prices. Longer: say what a late delivery changes for the cafe and for the Ecuador grower.

### Words and supports
Words: relies on, orders, grower, maker, goods, money, late, price. Frame: "The maker needs ___ from the grower." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 63 opens with the more-missed of error I (now nobody here works) and error P (the arrow explains the need), 5 minutes. Day 63 turns to a present-day market in Ghana, a country in West Africa.
