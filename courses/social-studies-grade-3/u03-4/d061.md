# Day 61: What is made here, what comes from far away

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 60's slips for errors H and AB and pick the more-missed. Draw the Maple Bank card, labeled made up: $100 saved a year, $5 interest. Draw the three arrows on each pair's map. Keep key-exits-61-64.md away until slips are in. The cards are made up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 60's slips (s03-E60, s03-E60b, s03-E60c). Whole class, the more-missed. Error H: "$105 is the interest" beside "$5 is the interest; the $100 is the deposit". Error AB: "interest is a free gift" beside "the borrower pays it". Use the Maple Bank card. Error O goes to the side table during Map work.

#### 5 min. Retrieval: trade
Review: you learned trade in grade 2: goods move from where they are made to where they are sold, and an arrow on a map shows the move. A made-up town buys rice grown in another country. Partners say [[s03-P61a]], one minute each way. Hear a rotating six. A miss joins the Map work side table.

#### 8 min. Read an origin label
New: an origin label says what a good is and which country it came from. Read each card aloud: Bananas, grown in Ecuador; Vanilla, grown in Madagascar; Olive oil, made in Spain. Model on the bananas, tracing the printed arrow with a finger: "The map shows an arrow from Ecuador to our town. I can find it because the legend says an arrow shows a good moving to a place." Review: the legend trace is from day 7. New: a buyer relies on the country that sends the good. Each child writes [[s03-P61b]] for the vanilla card.

#### 10 min. Map work
Pairs put each card on its country, trace the printed arrow with a finger and say the legend trace. Each child writes [[s03-P61c]] for one card. Drawing an arrow waits for the exit. Visit a rotating six pairs. The side table of up to six meets for 5 minutes: error O, or the retrieval miss (trace one printed arrow, then a fresh one).

#### 5 min. Made here or from far away
Set the Local Production Record's Finch Street Bakery card (made up, bread baked here) beside the Bananas and Vanilla cards. If a card names a far country, it is from far away; if a business here made it, it is made here. Each child writes [[s03-P61d]] for the olive oil card.

#### 4 min. Savings check-in
Review: the class savings ledger, from day 53 (day 57's line is s03-P57c). Made up, nothing collected. The third $20 goes in; shade box three. Each child writes [[s03-P61e]]. The $60 is kept, not gone (error AD). The page stays in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Origin Label Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children draw [[s03-E61]] and write [[s03-E61b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** Pairs read printed arrows and say the legend trace; the exit asks each child to draw a new arrow and say how a buyer needs a supplier.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the map on the board and use chalk arrows.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: use a drawn oval for the country and a dot for the town.
**Skip.** The savings line.

- **10 min. Model.** Read: "Bananas. Grown in Ecuador." Trace the arrow with a finger. Say: "The map shows an arrow to our town. The legend says an arrow shows a good moving."
- **20 min. Labels.** Your child writes [[s03-P61b]], [[s03-P61c]], [[s03-P61d]] and [[s03-P61e]].
  **Say.** "Why might a town buy rice from far away?" Expect: the town needs rice; the grower sells it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E61]] [[s03-E61b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: two cards, not three. Longer: pairs find a fourth country.

### Words and supports
Words: origin label, grown, made, legend, arrow, relies on. Frame: "The map shows ___. I can find it because the legend says ___." Pointing and drawing are answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 62 opens with the more-missed of error P (the arrow explains the need) and error I (now nobody here works), 5 minutes, the other at the side table. Day 62 follows one made-up order history.
