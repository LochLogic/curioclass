# Day 63: A market in Ghana today

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 62's slips for error I and error P and pick the more-missed. Draw the Kite Shop card, labelled made up: a kite shop here buys cloth from a maker in another country. Post the locator map. The newspaper is the Daily Graphic, 24 June 2023 (U3J). Keep key-exits-61-64.md away until slips are in. The Record is in our words, never quoted; add nothing to it.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 62's slips (s03-E62, s03-E62b). Whole class, the more-missed. Error I: "the grower stops growing" beside "the buyer's work here goes on, with the bought goods". Error P: "the beans" alone beside "each side needs something from the other". Use the Kite Shop card.

#### 4 min. Retrieval: a country report
Review: a country report, from grade 2, tells where a place is, who lives there and how people work. Partners say [[s03-P63a]], one minute each way. Hear a rotating six. A miss takes the three-part frame at the side table during Earn and spend.

#### 5 min. Which Ghana?
Pairs find West Africa on their day 61 world map with legend. Then point to the locator map. Ghana is a country in West Africa today, on the Gulf of Guinea; Ivory Coast lies to its west, Burkina Faso to its north and Togo to its east. Review: the legend trace is from day 7. One sentence: a different empire with the same name lay farther northwest long ago; grade 6 studies it. We do not study it today.

#### 14 min. The Techiman Traders Record
Review: who made it, when and why (day 18). It is a Curio Class summary of a report in a Ghanaian newspaper, 2023 (3 minutes). Techiman is a market in Ghana where many traders sell. Read chunk 1 aloud (2): Jennifer Owusu-Mensah, a vegetable trader. Pairs name who is in it and what she says; each child writes the first half of [[s03-P63b]] (4). Read chunk 2 aloud (2): Kusi Appiah and his concerns. Each child writes the second half (3). Not every trader is said to agree.

#### 5 min. Earn and spend
Review: earn and pay (kindergarten); money in and out (day 53). Selling brings money in; buying sends it out. Model: "The Record shows a vegetable trader, so customers buy her goods." The "so" is our thinking, not the Record's words. Pairs finish [[s03-P63c]] about a person or the market. Visit a rotating six pairs. The side table of up to six takes the country-report miss.

#### 4 min. Say it
Partners say: "The Record shows ___, so ___. It does not say ___." Each child writes [[s03-P63d]]. The page stays in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Ghana Exit Locator, first seen here. Leave the locator map up. Read each ask once. Children write [[s03-E63]] and [[s03-E63b]] on the slip and [[s03-E63c]] in the exit box. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** Pairs read a map by its labels and a record by its people; the exit asks which of three unnamed shapes is Ghana, and a trader card is new.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the map on the board and read the Record aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: draw an outline and a dot for Ghana on the coast of West Africa.
**Skip.** The country-report question.

- **10 min. Model.** Read the Record aloud. Say: "The Record shows a vegetable trader, so customers buy her goods."
- **20 min. Record.** Your child writes [[s03-P63b]], [[s03-P63c]] and [[s03-P63d]].
  **Say.** "What does the Record not say?" Expect: what every trader thinks.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the cards and each ask once. [[s03-E63]] [[s03-E63b]] [[s03-E63c]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: chunk 1 only. Longer: pairs name one thing the Record leaves out.

### Words and supports
Words: market, trader, Ghana, earn, spend. Frame: "The Record shows ___, so ___." Pointing and drawing are answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 64 opens with the more-missed of error R (the old empire is Ghana today) and error J (one trader speaks for all), 5 minutes, the other at the side table. Day 64 compares two records.

### Credit
Facts from a report in the Daily Graphic, 24 June 2023, in our words. The Kite Shop is made up.
