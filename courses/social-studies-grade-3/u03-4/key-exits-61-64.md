# Teacher key, exit cards days 61 to 64

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-61-64.md); never hand to children.

- Day 61, ask 1: An arrow from Sri Lanka to our town, drawn with the legend. Ask 2: The shop needs the tea to serve its customers. Not "the arrow explains it" (P).
- Day 62, ask 1: The bean dryer, or a buyer who pays for the beans. Ask 2: The cocoa beans, or money for the dryer. Not "the grower needs nothing" (P).
- Day 63, ask 1: Shape A: shaded on the southern coast, as Ghana sits on the locator map. Not the old empire (R). Ask 2: It touches the sea, like Ghana on the locator map. Shape B lies inland far to the northwest (where the old empire lay) and shape C lies inland to the north. Extra, in the Exit box: She earns when she sells to cooks; she spends when she buys from the farmer. Not "she speaks for every trader" (J).
- Day 64: Both set aside space for trading: Marsh End has covered stalls and Dunmore has shared bays. Not "all markets have covered stalls" (J); not a liking only (K).
