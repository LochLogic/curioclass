# Lime order history, day 62 (made up)

Print "made up, for practice". A grower in Mexico sells limes to a made-up snack maker, Brightside Snacks.

- Year 1: Brightside orders 100 boxes of limes and pays the grower.
- Year 2: Brightside orders 300 boxes of limes and pays the grower.

Goods go one way and money goes the other.

## Pairs

In Year 2, who relies more, the grower or the snack maker? Draw the two-way arrows. Which way does the money go? A delivery is late: what changes for each side?

## Local work

Imported limes become a local snack. Does local work end? No: a local maker still mixes and packs.

## Why can a seller not charge just anything?

Buyers can choose another seller.
