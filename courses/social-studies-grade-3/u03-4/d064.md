# Day 64: Two records, two ways to get what we need

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 63's slips for error R and error J and pick the more-missed. Draw the Valmora card, labelled made up: Map 1, "Valmora, an old kingdom, long ago"; Map 2, "Valmora, a country today". Have the two-source model cards, day 64, and the question board ready. Keep key-exits-61-64.md away until slips are in. No new content today: it applies day 55's and day 63's records.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 63's slips (s03-E63, s03-E63b, s03-E63c). Whole class, the more-missed. Error R: "the old empire is Ghana today" beside "the country today is the one the map names today". Use the Valmora card and revise Map 1's caption. Error J: "one seller speaks for every seller" beside "one card shows one seller". The other goes to the side table during Compare the records.

#### 7 min. A two-source model
Review: the claim frame, from the Local record and the Class record (day 45): "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___." Teacher models on made-up cards. Card 1: a hardware shop sells nails and paint to builders, with nails from a factory in the next state. Card 2: a fish stall sells fresh fish to cooks, brought by boats that morning. "I think the shop gets what it needs from farther away than the stall, because Card 1 shows nails from the next state and Card 2 shows fish from that morning." Each child writes [[s03-P64d]] with a different claim of their own. Hear a rotating six.

#### 12 min. Compare the records
Review: shows, suggests, cannot tell (day 9). Pairs fill the table on the Local Production Record and the Techiman Traders Record. Row 1: how each says people get what they need. Each child writes [[s03-P64a]]. Row 2: one same and one different, each with a detail. Each child writes [[s03-P64b]]. Visit a rotating six pairs. The side table of up to six takes the other of error R or J for 5 minutes.

#### 6 min. Share
Groups of four, two pairs. Each pair reads one same and one different, about 40 seconds each. The listener says the detail back. Hear a rotating six groups.

#### 7 min. Limit the claim
Review: who made it, when and why. A dated report supports only its time; one market is not all of Ghana; two cards are not all of our town. Say what each source cannot tell. Each child writes [[s03-P64c]]. Add the class's answer to the question board: how do places depend on trade? Day 65 gives the sentence.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the quarter slip with the Marsh End and Dunmore cards, first seen here. Read the cards and the ask once. Children write [[s03-E64]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The model gives one difference between two made-up shops; pairs compare real records on how each gets what it needs; the exit asks for a likeness between two new made-up markets.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the table on the board; read each record aloud twice. The question board is chart paper.

### At home

**Swap.** One record: use the Techiman Traders Record and say what it does not tell.
**Skip.** The share.

- **10 min. Model.** Read both cards aloud. Say: "I think the shop gets nails from farther away than the stall gets fish."
- **20 min. Compare.** Your child writes [[s03-P64d]], [[s03-P64a]], [[s03-P64b]] and [[s03-P64c]].
  **Say.** "What does one market not tell us?" Expect: what every market is like.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the cards and the ask once. [[s03-E64]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one row of the table. Longer: say what the table's same and different could mean for Day 65's claim.

### Words and supports
Words: record, same, different, cannot tell, market, claim, detail. Frame: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 65 opens with the more-missed of error K (no detail from the source) and error J (one record is all), 5 minutes. Day 65 writes a short claim with one or two pieces of evidence.

### Credit
Ours. The hardware shop, the fish stall and Valmora are made up; the Records follow our own summaries.
