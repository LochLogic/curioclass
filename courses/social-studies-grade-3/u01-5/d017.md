# Day 17: How a group's way of life shapes what it does with land

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use the Pine Bend Card in part 3. A nation's own passage may replace it only after you add a sources.csv row from that nation's own site and it passes the sensitivity read. Put the nation's name on the wall as its page gives it, and the frame card. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 16 to 18 (key-exits-16-18.md), out of the room until slips are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 16's exit slips with the written cue. If many made error Q, show a board pair: Cobb Corner, 1990, an empty lot; 2020, a swing set and a sandbox; pairs say what people did. If many made error P, pairs finish "The crosswalk by the school is there because people ___." The other letter goes to a small group in part 4.

#### 8 min. Culture and land
Review: in grade 2 you learned that culture is what people make, do and share, and that people change the land or adapt to it. Read the Dry Hollow Card aloud (invented for practice). Say the frame card: "This group ___, so it ___." Model: This group holds a spring work day, so the creek runs. The work day is what the group does and shares: culture. The cleared creek bed is a change to the land.

**Ask.** What does this group do, and so what happens to the land? Expect: it clears the creek each spring, so the creek runs in summer.

#### 12 min. A nation's own words
Read the second passage aloud in two pieces, with its maker and date (the Pine Bend Card unless a sourced passage replaces it). Pairs fill the frame on the Nation Card: "This nation ___, so it ___." Where the page gives no reason, write "the page does not say why." Do not explain the answer. Hear four pairs. Say once: this is one nation's page, not every nation's. Each child writes [[s01-P17]]; it stays in the folder.

**Ask.** What does the page say the nation does, and why? Expect: its words, no more.

#### 10 min. Before, after, stayed

Read the Elm Lane Card aloud (invented for practice). Pairs rule a sheet into three boxes and fill Before, After and Stayed from the card. The small group takes its repair first, for 5 minutes, then fills the chart with the teacher. Each child writes [[s01-P17b]]; it stays in the folder.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Take the frame card down. Read the Garden Group Card from cards-exits-16-18 once. Every child writes [[s01-E17]] and [[s01-E17b]] on the half slip. The card is first seen here.

**Shared shape.** Dry Hollow is read aloud with its frame, and the chart sorts a street into before, after and stayed. The exit asks each child to name a shared practice and say how it helps explain new beds, with no frame given.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the three boxes on the board and read the cards aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No nation page: use a street or club you know that does one thing together each year.

**Skip.** The nation passage.

- **10 min. A group and a place.** Think of a group that does one thing together, such as a team or a club.
  **Say.** "What does the group do, and what does it do to a place?" Expect: its shared practice, then a change to a place.
- **20 min. Before, after, stayed.** Draw a place near you in three boxes: before, after, stayed.
  **Say.** "What stayed the same?" Expect: one thing that did not change.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-E17]] [[s01-E17b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fill only the Stayed box. Longer: a second frame sentence for Elm Lane.

### Words and supports

Words: culture, nation, stayed, "the page does not say why". Frames: "This group ___, so it ___." Accept pointing, drawing and dictation.

### Tomorrow
If many wrote that the families changed nothing (error Q), day 18 names what people did in a dated pair; if many listed features (error P), it links one feature to what people did. Day 18: an early map beside a modern one.
