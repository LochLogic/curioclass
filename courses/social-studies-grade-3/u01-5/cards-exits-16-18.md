# Exit cards, days 16 to 18

Print "invented for practice" at the bottom of each card. Every child answers in writing; the day's six are also heard.

## Day 16: Park Change Card

A paved strip beside a school becomes a planted rain garden. The town wanted it to soak up rain. Ask: what did people change on the land? What did the people want the change to do?

## Day 17: Garden Group Card

Families in a made-up neighbourhood pass on seed-saving skills and share seeds each spring. The new garden beds are filled with these plants. Ask: what practice do the families share? How does that practice help explain the new garden beds?

## Day 18: Millpond Map Pair

Two made-up maps of the same place, dated 1900 and 2000. A footpath is only on the 2000 map. The pond is on both. Ask: name one change. Name one thing that stayed the same on both maps.
