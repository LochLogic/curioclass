# Teacher key, exit cards days 16 to 18

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-16-18.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 16: they replaced a paved strip with planted ground; to soak up rain water.
- Day 17, E17: they pass on seed-saving skills, or they share seeds each spring (either one is right). E17b: the skills or the shared seeds give them plants to fill the new beds.
- Day 18: the footpath was added; the pond is on both.

## Practice card keys (teacher only)

- Day 16, land change cards: each card names a change people made and an aim; the rain garden on the exit card is a new case.
- Day 16, sort cards: 1, 3 and 5 are made by people; 2, 4 and 6 are made by nature.
