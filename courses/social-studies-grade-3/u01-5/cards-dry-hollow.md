# Dry Hollow Card (day 17, teacher's model)

Invented for practice. Dry Hollow is a made-up valley. The families there hold a work day each spring to clear the creek bed. Because they clear it, the creek runs in the summer. Model: This group holds a spring work day, so the creek runs.

## Pine Bend Card (second made-up card, the day 17 part 3 default)

Invented for practice. Pine Bend is a made-up river town. The families there keep a rule: they leave the river edge uncut. Because they leave it uncut, berries grow along the edge each summer. Frame: This group leaves the river edge uncut, so berries grow there.
