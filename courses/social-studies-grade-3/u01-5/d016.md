# Day 16: How people changed the land

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the local nation's whole page. Choose three to five sentences on land care that pass the sensitivity read (docs/pipeline/briefs/sensitivity-read.md); copy them word for word with address and date. Write the nation's name on the wall. If none pass, pairs sort the five cards again by effect. Cut each card's aim line into a strip. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 16 to 18 (key-exits-16-18.md), out of the room until slips are collected.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 15's exit tickets and written tasks with the written cue. 3 minutes: review, in grade 2 you learned that people change the land; each child writes [[s01-R16]]; it stays in the folder. 7 minutes: for error G, pairs sort three claims about a board sketch (a bay, a pier) under the wall card: the map shows a pier; it might help boats stop; who built it cannot be told. For error P, pairs say "The map shows ___. It might help because ___." The other letter goes to a small group in part 4.

#### 7 min. Five ways people change land
New words: purpose (what people wanted) and effect (what else happened). A wetland is land where water covers or sits near the soil's surface for part or all of the year (U.S. EPA, Source 16A). Show the field card: people planted beans to feed the town; the wild grass was gone. Pairs match the dam, road, wetland and park pictures to aim strips. Read each effect: the stream below the dam ran lower; hill trees were cut; frogs in the wet place left; the lot's weeds were mowed. Each child writes [[s01-P16]]; it stays in the folder.

**Ask.** What did people want, and what else happened? Expect: an aim, then a second effect.

#### 11 min. One nation's land care
This part is taught, not checked: no exit asks it. Say the nation's name, and that its people live here today. Read the first piece with its maker and date; pairs fill the nation and what its page says it does with the land. Read the second; pairs fill what for. Where the page is silent, write "the page does not say." Name burning, farming or trails only if the page does. Say once: another nation's page may differ. Hear four pairs. Each child writes [[s01-P16c]]; it stays in the folder.

**Ask.** What does the page say the nation does, and what for? Expect: its words only.

#### 7 min. Made by people or by nature

Review: in grade 1 you sorted what people made from what was already there. Pairs sort the six cards: "Made by ___. I can tell because ___." The small group takes its repair, then sorts cards 1 and 2. Each child writes [[s01-P16b]]; it stays in the folder.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Read the Park Change Card from cards-exits-16-18 once. Every child writes [[s01-E16]] and [[s01-E16b]] on the half slip.

**Shared shape.** Practice matches strips, names a second effect and sorts by maker; the exit asks for change and aim from a card heard once.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the cards on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No Land Change Cards: use a path, fence or garden bed you can see.

**Skip.** The nation passage.

- **10 min. Look.** Find one thing near you that people made, such as a path, wall or fence.
  **Say.** "Did people or nature make it, and how can you tell?" Expect: a mark of people, such as a straight edge.
- **20 min. Wanted and happened.** Draw it before and after, and write what people wanted.
  **Say.** "What else happened when people changed it?" Expect: a second effect, not the aim.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-E16]] [[s01-E16b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: match three cards. Longer: a second card with one good thing and one hard thing.

### Words and supports
Words: purpose, effect, wetland, nation. Frames: "Another effect was ___." "Made by ___. I can tell because ___."

### Tomorrow
If many wrote that people did nothing (error Q), day 17 names what people did in a dated pair; if many listed features (error P), it links one feature to what people did. Day 17: culture and land.
