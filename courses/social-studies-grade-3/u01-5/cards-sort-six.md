# Sort cards: made by people or made by nature (day 16)

Invented for practice. Six new cards to sort under made by people and made by nature.

1. A path worn through the grass by many walkers.
2. A gully cut by a spring flood.
3. A stone wall along a field.
4. A sand bar left by a river.
5. A fence around a garden.
6. A fallen tree across a trail.
