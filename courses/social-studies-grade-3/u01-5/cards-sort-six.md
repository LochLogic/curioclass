# Sort cards: made by people or made by nature (day 16)

Invented for practice. Six new cards to sort under made by people and made by nature.

1. A path worn through the grass by many walkers. (people)
2. A gully cut by a spring flood. (nature)
3. A stone wall along a field. (people)
4. A sand bar left by a river. (nature)
5. A fence around a garden. (people)
6. A fallen tree across a trail. (nature)

Teacher key: 1, 3, 5 people; 2, 4, 6 nature.
