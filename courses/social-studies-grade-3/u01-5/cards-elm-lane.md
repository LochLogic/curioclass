# Elm Lane Card (day 17, before, after, stayed chart)

Invented for practice. Elm Lane is a made-up street. Before: a corner shop and a row of elms. After: the shop became a library and two elms were cut to widen the sidewalk. Stayed: the row of the other elms and the lane's name.

Chart: three boxes, Before, After, Stayed. Pairs fill each from the card.
