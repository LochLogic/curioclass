# Nation Card (days 16 and 17)

One page for a pair. Boxes: The nation (named by its own name) ______. What its own page says it does with the land ______. What for ______. Where the page is silent write: the page does not say.

The nation passages are not printed here. They are written only after a sensitivity read passes; until then the day 17 pair work uses the made-up cases. Nothing ceremonial or sacred. Present tense.
