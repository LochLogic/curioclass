# Exit cards, days 117 to 121

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. Day 118 has no exit.

## Day 117: Inquiry Link Card (made up)

Big question: Could two families share one garden? Source 1, a bed chart made by the garden club: the garden has four beds. Source 2, a note written by the Cho family: the Reyes family and the Cho family each need two beds.

Ask: Inquiry Link Card. For each source, write its maker and how it helps answer the big question.

## Day 119: Overlap Card (made up)

A strip with two bands. The ferry ran from 1700 to 1740. The wharf began in 1720 and ran on.

Ask 1: Overlap Card. Did the ferry and the wharf run at the same time?
Ask 2: Overlap Card. Say the years.

## Day 119: fresh timeline records (teaching, made up)

Market hall ran from 1690 to 1730. Rope walk ran from 1710 to 1750. Dock opened in 1760 and still runs. Place all three bands on the paper strip with ten-year marks. Which two overlapped? The market hall and the rope walk, from 1710 to 1730.

## Day 121: Fresh Passages (exit, made up)

Print "made up" on every passage. Each child gets the passage for their group. Under each: E121 and E121b.

Passage A. Made up. The rope loft ran from 1640 to 1700. The sail loft ran from 1660 to 1680.
Passage B. Made up. A poster for Gull Harbor says: Visit our calm bay! A town log for Gull Harbor lists three wrecks on the rocks last year.
Passage C. Made up. A flood washed out a bridge in a valley town. The town voted to build a ferry. The ferry owner then raised the fare.
Passage D. Made up. A church book of 1700 lists: Edith, free. Tom, enslaved by a merchant. Hal, a servant for seven years. The book lists only those baptized.

Ask 1: Fresh Passage. A missed idea is marked. Fix your answer with evidence.
Ask 2: Fresh Passage. Write in your own words why the fix is right.
