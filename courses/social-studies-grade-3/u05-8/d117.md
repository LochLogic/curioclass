# Day 117: Two sources, one claim

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the student page, the claim sheet, the four detail cards and the exit slips with the Inquiry Link Card; the key is key-exits-117-121.md. Quill Cove and the garden card are made up; say so. Say each nation by its own name, in the present tense.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (W from E116)
Hand back day 116's slips and records (s05-E116, s05-P116a, s05-W116a, s05-P116b, s05-P116c, s05-P116d) with a cue; route records are kept for day 119. If many missed W (water alone built a port), take 10 minutes: read the made-up Quill Cove record, a tidal creek, a net loft and a Saturday market. Each child fills three blanks [[s05-W117a]]. If few missed it, they do this for 5 minutes at the side table; the rest read the question board.

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Read the unit question and the question board. Review (day 85): two tellers who each saw it agree; an account and its copy are one teller. Review (day 19): a claim has evidence and a because. Review (day 103): the harvest card gave Winslow's side only. No writing.

#### 10 min. Gather
Review (day 95): you read Smith and the Massachusett Tribe's page; today are two new lines. Smith was a visitor writing in London in 1616 for readers at home (U5N). Read his words once: "Heere are many rising hilles, and on their tops and descents many corne fields, and delightfull groues." Plain words: hills with many cornfields and pleasant groves. The Massachusett Tribe at Ponkapoag is a nation that lives today; this is its own page (U5H). In our words: women took part in decisions with men and elders. Today a tribal council and an elected board govern. Pairs fill the table [[s05-P117e]].

#### 6 min. Pairs: two sources
Show the four detail cards. Say: a source is counted by its maker. Each pair gets two spoken asks, [[s05-P117c]] and [[s05-P117d]]. Hear a rotating six pairs, 45 seconds each; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Pairs not reached answer on day 118.

#### 8 min. The link
Read the day 90 record once: the path question helped because its answer was evidence for the big question. New term: a limit says what the evidence cannot show. Review (unit 1): claims that show, might suggest or cannot tell. Pairs choose two cards from different makers. Each child fills the claim sheet: claim, both sources with makers, a detail from each, and the limit [[s05-P117a]] [[s05-P117b]]. Leave "how it supports" for day 118. Practice record; written cue after class; revised on day 119. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the slip with the Inquiry Link Card, new today. Read it once. Each child writes [[s05-E117]]. Key: key-exits-117-121.md. Hand the slips back on day 118.

**Shared shape.** The table, spoken asks and claim sheet use Smith and the Tribe's details; the exit is an invented garden card with two sources, asking each maker and how it helps.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the four cards on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you read each source aloud; your child may skip any part.

**Skip.** The Quill Cove repair, if day 116 went well.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read the Quill Cove record. [[s05-W117a]]
- **10 min. Two sources.** Read Smith's words, then the Tribe's. [[s05-P117e]]
- **6 min. Cards.** Read the four cards. [[s05-P117c]] [[s05-P117d]] **Say.** "Who made each card?" Expect: Smith made cards 1 and 2; the Massachusett Tribe made cards 3 and 4.
- **10 min. A claim.** [[s05-P117a]] [[s05-P117b]]
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card once. [[s05-E117]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: keep the table and the claim; drop the repair and the cards. Longer: add a third detail and name its maker.

### Words and supports
Words: source, maker, detail, claim, limit. Frame: "I think (claim) because (source) shows (detail). This does not prove (limit)." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 118 has each child write a claim from two named sources, with a limit; the slips come back first.
