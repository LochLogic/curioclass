# Day 124: Fix one idea, then a start page

## Teacher page

### Before class
Score days 122 and 123 from key-check2.md and write on each check 2 sheet one note: a circled missed idea and one sentence. Sort children into at most four groups by the largest gaps: A sources, B timeline, C the treaty and what it meant, D claim and two sources. Put each group's practice card on its table. All fresh cards are made up; say so. Print the unit 6 start page. Keep key-exits-124.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 122's assessment and day 123's assessment, the check 2 sheets with their notes (and day 121's slips, s05-E121 and s05-E121b): sc2-E1, sc2-E1d, sc2-E1b, sc2-E1e, sc2-E1c, sc2-E2, sc2-E2b, sc2-E2c, sc2-E3, sc2-E3b, sc2-E3c, sc2-E4, sc2-E4b and sc2-E4c. Each child reads the note silently or with a partner. Say: a fix needs a new answer and a reason, not a copy.

#### 15 min. Groups
Each group meets you for four minutes in turn; the others work in pairs from the practice card on their table. Hear each child in your group once, about 45 seconds each: Group A, [[sc2-P124a]]; Group B, [[sc2-P124b]]; Group C, [[sc2-P124c]]; Group D, [[sc2-P124d]]. Name each child heard on the record.

#### 10 min. Fresh task
Hand each child the Exit Card for their group on the half slip. Each child fixes the idea marked on their sheet: [[sc2-X124]] and [[sc2-X124b]]. A scribe or a spoken answer is offered. Read the ask for the marked idea aloud from key-exits-124.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Unit 6 start page
The last ten minutes. Hand out the unit 6 start page, two symbol cards. Read each card aloud once, then its question once: [[s06-RD1]] and [[s06-RD1b]]. Each child answers with a few words or a circle; a child may answer at the side table. Give no score. On the record sheet note what each child did and what help was used. Keep the pages for day 126.

**Shared shape.** The group practice asks are spoken, one fresh case each. The exit uses a different Exit Card and asks for a written fix with a detail.

### Other rooms
No groups: read each note aloud to one child; they fix the idea on the Exit Card for their note.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; the parent reads the note aloud.

**Skip.** The group asks and the start page.

- **5 min. The note.** Read the note on the check 2 sheet.
- **15 min. Try again.** Pick a made-up card that fits the note and talk it through. **Say.** "What did you miss, and what would you add?" Expect: the idea named in the note, with a detail from the card.
- **10 min. The fix.** Write the new answer and a detail from the card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc2-X124]] [[sc2-X124b]] Tell the adult what you fixed.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fix one idea only; drop the group asks. Longer: fix a second marked idea on another card.

### Words and supports
Words: note, fix, evidence, card, symbol. Frame: "I fixed ___ because the card says ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 125 opens unit 6 with a question: whose agreement does a government need? Today's start page results wait for day 126.
