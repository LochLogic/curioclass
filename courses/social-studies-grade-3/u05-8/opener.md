# Section 5.8 opener: the inquiry, the claim, the review and the end check

## What the section does

Five days of 40 minutes. Day 117: gather evidence from two sources by two makers and link a supporting question to the big question. Day 118: write a short claim with two named sources and a limit; no exit. Day 119: written feedback, revisions and a fresh timeline. Day 120: the end-of-unit check from two dossiers. Day 121: the response.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Two sources.** A source is counted by its maker. Two details from one maker are one source (AG). Day 117 uses two makers: John Smith, a visitor writing in 1616 for readers at home (sources.csv U5N), and the Massachusett Tribe at Ponkapoag, a nation that lives today and keeps its own records (U5H). The plan names Mourt's Relation and a Pamunkey passage; the first was refused (403) and is cut, and the Pamunkey lines are all used on days 98 and 99.
- **A claim stays inside its evidence.** The frame is claim, evidence, because, and a limit: "this does not prove ___" (J). The big question is "Could the colonists and the Native nations have shared the land?" A good claim says sharing was possible in some ways and under pressure in others, and names what the evidence cannot show.
- **A supporting question helps only if it connects** (AH). Reread the day 90 record, the path.
- **Timelines.** Overlap is the years two developments share (K).
- **The end check.** Two dossiers, each read aloud once, the card a task names just before the task. Dossier A is a made-up colonial town record with one real card, the law card. Dossier B is a made-up neighbors' record with one real card, the Atkins record. Everything marked made up is labeled. No Native nation or person is made up: Captain U leads a made-up fishing town.
- **Day 121.** Each child gets a note on the day 120 sheet, then work from the written move for their group, then a fresh task. Copying an answer does not fix a missed idea.

## The errors in plain words

AG is counting two details from one maker as two sources. AH is saying a question helps when it does not connect. J is letting wishes prove what happened. K is thinking a new thing ended the old at once. Copying an answer is not fixing it.
