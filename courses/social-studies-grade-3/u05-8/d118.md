# Day 118: Write a claim

## Teacher page

### Before class
Mark day 117's slips and records with a cue. Print the student page and a fresh claim sheet; day 117's sheet stays in the folder. Board list, with makers: Smith (1616, a visitor); the Massachusett Tribe at Ponkapoag (its own page); the day 98 Pamunkey Indian Tribe history page (its own page); the day 103 harvest card (Winslow). Seloy and the 1621 alliance are not listed; the day 120 check uses them. Fern Row and the pond are made up. No exit today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (J, AG or AH from E117 and 117)
Hand back the slips and records (s05-E117, s05-P117a, s05-P117b, s05-W117a, s05-P117e), 5 minutes, one cue each. Then 5 minutes on AH, the most common miss: read the made-up Pond Card aloud and show the right beside the wrong: "It helps because it has words" beside "It helps because its answer is evidence for the big question." Each child writes [[s05-W118a]]. Pairs not heard on day 117 answer their ask, s05-P117c and s05-P117d, at the side table during the write part.

#### 3 min. Step back
Read the blank claim sheet once; do not model it. Read the unit question aloud. Review (day 117): a source is counted by its maker; a limit says what the evidence cannot show.

#### 14 min. Write
Each child writes a closing claim [[s05-P118a]] on the claim sheet: pick two sources with different makers from the board, name each maker, give one detail from each, say how each supports the claim, and end "This does not prove ___." A claim may run longer than one sentence. Offer the frame on the page. While they write, a group of six meets you at the side table for the made-up Fern Row cards (Card 1, Reed School paper: the classes shared the library on Mondays. Card 2, Reed School paper: the classes took turns at the art table. Card 3, Elm School paper: the classes argued over the music room), [[s05-W118b]] and [[s05-W118c]], spoken, about 45 seconds each. Then hear a rotating six at their desks, 45 seconds each, and name them on the record. A child may say a claim aloud while you write it.

#### 8 min. Share
Pairs read to each other. The partner names the two sources and their makers [[s05-P118b]]. Then three children read aloud, each with a different supported claim; the class listens before revising. A child may pass.

#### 4 min. Revise
Each child adds to or fixes the claim in pencil below the first. Do not erase the first.

#### 1 min. Collect
The sheets go to the folder. They are not marked today.

### Other rooms
No printer: fold a page into the claim sheet's lines. No board: pass the source list on a card.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you read each source card aloud; your child may skip any part.

**Skip.** The pond repair, if day 117 went well.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read the Pond Card. [[s05-W118a]]
- **15 min. The claim.** Choose two sources by different makers. [[s05-P118a]] **Say.** "What does this not prove?" Expect: what the sources cannot show, such as what would have happened.
- **8 min. Share.** Read your claim aloud. [[s05-P118b]]
- **7 min. Revise.** Add one thing below the first claim.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: keep the claim with one detail from each source; drop the share. Longer: add a second limit or a third source with its maker.

### Words and supports
Words: source, maker, detail, claim, limit, prove. Frame on the page. Read the cards aloud; pointing, drawing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 119 returns the claim sheet with a written note, then each child revises earlier records and tries a fresh timeline before the unit check.
