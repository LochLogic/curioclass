# Day 117 cards: two sources, four details

Print "made up, for practice" on invented cards. Read each line aloud once.

## Repair (W from day 116)

A fresh made-up port record, Pike Cove: a long beach, a market and a boat yard. Connect harbor, labor and trade.

## Retrieval

The unit question and the question board. From unit 4: two tellers who each saw it agree; an account and its copy are one teller. The claim frame from unit 1: claim, evidence, because.

## Gather: two sources

Source 1. John Smith, A Description of New England, London, 1616. A visitor writing for readers at home. (sources.csv U5N.) Exact words for the teacher: "Heere are many rising hilles, and on their tops and descents many corne fields, and delightfull groues." Plain summary: there were hills with many cornfields and pleasant groves.
Source 2. The Massachusett Tribe at Ponkapoag. A nation that lives today and tells its own history. (U5H.) Plain summary: women took part in decisions with men and elders. Today a tribal council and an elected board govern.

Table: source, maker, detail, what it shows about sharing the land.

## Pairs: four detail cards, two from each maker

Card 1. Smith: many hills had cornfields.
Card 2. Smith: the groves were pleasant to see.
Card 3. Massachusett Tribe: women gathered shellfish, greens and herbs.
Card 4. Massachusett Tribe: women made pottery and baskets.

Each pair names the maker of each detail and says how many sources there are. Two details from one maker are one source (AG).
