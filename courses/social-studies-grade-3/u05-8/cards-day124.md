# Day 124: fresh cards for the four groups

Every card is made up, for practice; print that at the bottom. Group A is sources, group B is the timeline, group C is the treaty and what it meant, group D is claim and two sources. The practice asks are spoken and use the first card of each group. The exit slip uses the second card (the Exit Card) and is written; print it on a half slip with the two exit asks, [[sc2-X124]] and [[sc2-X124b]]. No Exit Card names a real nation.

## Group A: sources

Practice card, Fresh Source Card. A postcard written in 1950 by a visitor to Mill Dock, for a friend. Ask (spoken): [[sc2-P124a]]

Exit Card A. The question is: how did Heron Pier get its lamp? Source 1: a pier keeper's diary, 1931, written that week: "The new lamp was lit tonight." Source 2: a school book, 2019, written for children: "Heron Pier got its lamp in 1931."

## Group B: timeline

Practice card, Fresh Timeline Card. A school opened in 1962. Ask (spoken): [[sc2-P124b]]

Exit Card B. Kestrel Hill, a made-up town. 1801, the town is founded. 1865, the first church opens. 1912, the fire hall opens. 1990, the park opens.

## Group C: the treaty and what it meant

Practice card, Fresh Agreement Card. The Moss Cove Co-op gives a field to a railway and keeps its mill pond. Ask (spoken): [[sc2-P124c]]

Exit Card C. Gull Rock Agreement, 1900, made up. The Harbor Company may build a pier. The Fishers' League gives up its old dock and keeps its right to land boats on the beach.

## Group D: a claim from two sources

Practice card, Fresh Pair Card. Source 1, a flyer, 2024, by the library, for families: "Sixty children came to the reading fair." Source 2, a photo caption, 2024, by the school: "Parents joined the fair." The claim: the fair was popular. Ask (spoken): [[sc2-P124d]]

Exit Card D. The question is: why did Salt Rock build a seawall? Source 1: a harbor board notice, 1998, for residents: "Storms flooded the lower street three times in 1996 and 1997. The board will build a seawall." Source 2: a harbor museum label, 2019, for visitors: "The seawall opened in 1999. Since then the lower street has stayed dry in storms."

## The exit slip, half page

Say: use the Exit Card for your group. Each child writes [[sc2-X124]] and [[sc2-X124b]].
