# Day 123: Course check 2, part 2

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-check2.md and key-check2.md; the key stays with you. Print cards 3 and 4. Say the nation by its own name, the Makah Tribe, in the present tense: it lives at Neah Bay today. The two sources are real and printed in our words; say so. This part uses unit 4's nation and place, not the colonies assessed on day 120. Never score a first attempt. Keep day 122's sheets with you.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions
Say: pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each source once, just before its questions. You may pass any question. No timer is shown.

#### 8 min. Sources
Read Source A and Source B aloud once. Say: a treaty is an agreement between nations; cede means give up. Say: Source A is our wording of the 1855 treaty; Source B is our wording of the Makah Tribe's own page, written for readers today.

#### 12 min. The treaty
Each child writes [[sc2-E3]], [[sc2-E3b]] and [[sc2-E3c]]. Both sources stay in view.

#### 12 min. The claim
Each child writes [[sc2-E4]], [[sc2-E4b]] and [[sc2-E4c]]. A spoken answer, a pointed answer or a scribe is offered to any child, without supplying the claim or a detail.

#### 3 min. Marking
Each child checks that every question has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most six who left one blank say it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record.

#### 3 min. Collection
Collect the sheets and cards. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-check2.md, together with day 122. Day 122's assessment goes back with this one on day 124. Write the missing part by name (sources, timeline, treaty, claim), not a total.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy each source large by hand and read it aloud from your copy; a child may hear it again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read each source aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** Day 122's sheet.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Read both sources once, then each question once. Do not help. **Say.** "What did the Makah keep in the treaty?" Expect: the right to fish and hunt whales and seals at their usual places. [[sc2-E3b]] [[sc2-E4]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the treaty questions and the claim. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: treaty, claim, source, detail. Read every source and question aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 124 hands both parts back with one note each, then each child fixes one missed idea on a fresh card. The last ten minutes are unit 6's start page.

### Credit
Ours. Source A follows the treaty text on the Washington State Governor's Office of Indian Affairs page (C2A); Source B follows the Makah Tribe's page (C2B). Both are reworded.
