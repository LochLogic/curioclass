# Day 120: Unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-day120.md; key-day120.md stays with you. Chalk card A4 (a 1650 kitchen, a girl hauling a bucket from a well, a woman churning butter) and the trade diagram (Ports M, N, O, with arrows) on the board, covered. The law, alliance, Atkins and Seloy cards are real; the rest is made up, and you say so. Say each nation by its own name, in the present tense. Person look first: read the Boston exhibit page on Atkins before printing B3 (checked 2026-11-21).

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions
Say: eight tasks, pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each card once, just before its task. If you finish, check that every task has an answer. You may pass any task. Reading aloud takes about 350 words in all and sits inside the 30 minutes.

#### 4 min. Q1
Read cards A1 and B1. Each child writes [[s05-Q1]] and [[s05-Q1b]].

#### 4 min. Q2
Read cards A2 and B2. Each child writes [[s05-Q2]] and [[s05-Q2b]].

#### 3 min. Q3
Read card A3. Each child writes [[s05-Q3]].

#### 3 min. Q4
Uncover card A4 and read it. Each child writes [[s05-Q4]].

#### 3 min. Q5
Read card A5 and then card B3. Each child writes [[s05-Q5]] and [[s05-Q5b]].

#### 4 min. Q6
Read cards A6 and A7. Each child writes [[s05-Q6]] and [[s05-Q6b]].

#### 3 min. Q7
Uncover the diagram and read card A8. Each child writes [[s05-Q7]].

#### 6 min. Q8
Read cards A9 and B4, then the task. Each child writes [[s05-Q8]], [[s05-Q8b]], [[s05-Q8c]] and [[s05-Q8d]]. A spoken answer, a pointed answer or a scribe is offered to any child, without supplying reasoning.

#### 5 min. Check and collect
Each child checks that every task has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most six children who left a task blank answer it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record.

#### 2 min. Collect
Collect the sheets and the folders. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-day120.md; maps and spoken answers count. Score the revised records from the folder: [[s05-R1]], [[s05-R1b]], [[s05-R2]], [[s05-R3]], [[s05-R4]], [[s05-R5]], [[s05-R6]], [[s05-R7]], [[s05-R8]], [[s05-R9]]. Never score day 119's first attempts; day 121 returns the sheets.

### Other rooms
No chalkboard: draw card A4 and the diagram on paper and hold each up. A child who needs it hears every card read again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read each card aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** The folder records.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Read each card once, then each question once. Write your child's answer if they ask. Do not help. **Say.** "Why does a harbor help a port town grow?" Expect: ships can load in sheltered water, and workers and trade gather there. [[s05-Q1]] [[s05-Q2]] [[s05-Q3]] [[s05-Q4]] [[s05-Q5]] [[s05-Q6]] [[s05-Q7]] [[s05-Q8]]
- **5 min. Your records.** Your child shows each revised record. [[s05-R1]] [[s05-R3]] [[s05-R5]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: tasks 1, 3, 5 and 8, one card each. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: dossier, card, claim, source, cause. Read every card and stem aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 121 returns each sheet with one note, then each child fixes one missed idea on a fresh card. The note and the group follow what the check showed.

### Credit
Ours. The law card and the Atkins record follow City of Boston Archaeology (U5R). The alliance card follows Pilgrim Hall; the Aquinnah line follows the Wampanoag Tribe of Gay Head (Aquinnah). The Seloy card follows the Florida Museum. All other cards are made up.
