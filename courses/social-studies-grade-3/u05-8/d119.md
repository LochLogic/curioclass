# Day 119: Review and revise

## Teacher page

### Before class
Before class, write a one-line cue on each record and on day 118's claim sheet, and circle nothing on the strips yet. Put in each folder: days 90, 104, 106, 107, 108, 110, 113, 116 and 117, and day 118's claim sheet. Print the student page and the exit slips with the Overlap Card; the key is key-exits-117-121.md. Take down the claim frame; leave the blank frames. The Overlap Card and the strip records are made up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Written feedback
Hand out the folders. Each child reads the cues silently or with a partner: day 90 (s05-E90, s05-E90b, s05-P90a, s05-P90b, s05-P90c, s05-P90d), day 104 (s05-E104, s05-E104b), day 106 (s05-E106, s05-P106a), day 107 (s05-E107, s05-P107a), day 108 (s05-E108, s05-P108a), day 110 (s05-E110, s05-P110a), day 113 (s05-E113, s05-E113b, s05-P113d), day 116 (s05-E116, s05-P116a) and day 117 (s05-E117, s05-P117a, s05-P117b). Day 118's claim sheet (s05-P118a) is read, not redone. Anyone from the Fern Row group not heard on day 118 answers one spoken ask now (s05-W118b, s05-W118c).

#### 20 min. Revisions
No new teaching. Each child revises, in colored pencil below the first answer, the records whose cue says "not yet", and finishes any missing record, about 2 to 3 minutes each. Only the revision is scored. Move child to child; a child who scored 0 on a first-half record says one answer aloud [[s05-P119d]]. Hear a rotating six, about 45 seconds each, and name them on the record.

#### 5 min. Fresh timeline
Review (day 91): equal marks for equal years. Review (days 100 and 106): a band is a line from a start year, and two bands can run at the same time. Read three made-up trade records: the market hall ran from 1690 to 1730; the rope walk ran from 1710 to 1750; the dock opened in 1760 and still runs. Each child draws all three bands on the strip and says which two overlapped [[s05-P119a]].

#### 5 min. Timeline cue and revision
Circle one missed criterion on each strip, spacing or overlap, about ten seconds a child, starting with the first finished. Each child then revises the strip alone. Only the revision is scored.

#### 3 min. Review
Pairs whisper each answer; then call on one pair each time. First, [[s05-P119b]]. Second, review (day 114): the triangle is a simplified picture of some sea routes, not every voyage [[s05-P119c]].

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the slip with the Overlap Card, new today. Read it once aloud. Each child writes [[s05-E119]] and [[s05-E119b]]. Key: key-exits-117-121.md. The slips go back on day 121. Take the strip, the cause chart and the claim frame down.

**Shared shape.** The strip practice uses three made-up records and asks which two overlapped; the exit gives a different pair and asks about the same time and the years.

### Other rooms
No paper timeline strips: draw a line, mark each ten years and draw the bands as lines.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you read each cue aloud; your child may skip any part.

**Skip.** The review questions.

- **6 min. Cues.** Read the cues on the records together.
- **19 min. Revise.** Pick two records marked "not yet" and add a new answer in color below the first.
- **8 min. Strip.** Draw three bands on a strip. [[s05-P119a]] **Say.** "What does overlap mean?" Expect: two things at the same time.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card once. [[s05-E119]] [[s05-E119b]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: revise two records and draw the strip; drop the review. Longer: add a fourth record and find a second overlap.

### Words and supports
Words: overlap, band, strip, route, revise. Read every cue aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 120 is the unit check: eight tasks from two source packets, pencil only, no talk, each read aloud once. The exit slips come back on day 121.

### Credit
Ours. The records, the Overlap Card and the strip are made up.
