# Day 122: Course check 2, part 1

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-check2.md and key-check2.md; the key stays with you. Print cards 1 and 2. Both cards are made up: say so. Unit 5 was assessed on day 120, so this part asks the timeline skill on a made-up town, not on unit 5's events. Never score a first attempt. Say each word plainly: primary, secondary, decade, century.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions
Say: pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each card once, just before its questions. You may pass any question. No timer is shown.

#### 16 min. Sources
Read card 1 aloud once. Say: primary means made at the time by someone who was there; secondary means made later. Each child writes [[sc2-E1]], [[sc2-E1d]], [[sc2-E1b]], [[sc2-E1e]] and [[sc2-E1c]]. The card stays in view.

#### 16 min. Timeline
Read card 2 aloud once. Say: a decade is ten years; a century is one hundred. Each child writes [[sc2-E2]], [[sc2-E2b]] and [[sc2-E2c]]. A spoken answer, a pointed answer or a scribe is offered to any child, without supplying the answer.

#### 3 min. Marking
Each child checks that every question has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most six who left one blank say it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record.

#### 3 min. Collection
Collect the sheets and cards. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-check2.md. Write the missing part by name, not a total.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy each card large by hand and read it aloud from your copy; a child may hear a card again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read each card aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** Day 121's folder.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Read each card once, then each question once. Do not help. **Say.** "How is a diary written that day different from a label written later?" Expect: the diary is primary, the label secondary. [[sc2-E1]] [[sc2-E2]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: card 1 and the first question of card 2. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: primary, secondary, decade, century, timeline. Read every card and question aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 123 is part 2 of the check: two short sources about the Makah Tribe and a treaty, and a claim with a detail from each. Day 124 hands both parts back.
