# Day 121 cards: fresh cases for the fix

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. None of these is in a dossier or a teaching piece.

## Fresh strip (Group A)

Salt house: 1670 to 1710. Cooper's yard: began 1690 and ran on. Place both on a ten-year strip. They share 1690 to 1710.

## Fresh passage (Group B)

Made up. A flyer for Elm Quay says: Come to Elm Quay! Safe docks and busy markets. A harbor clerk's ledger for Elm Quay lists docks, a market and a tax on every ship.


## Fresh passage (Group C)

Made up. A hard frost killed the wheat in a river town. The town voted to buy grain from a nearby town. Then a lord claimed the town's grain store as his own.


## Fresh passage (Group D)

Made up. A shipyard list shows three people who worked there: Wren, free, paid by the day; Cobb, a servant with a four-year term; Teague, enslaved, held for life. A caption says the list shows one yard.
