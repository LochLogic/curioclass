# Teacher key: end-of-unit sheet

Teacher only. Never hand to children. Score each prompt 2, 1 or 0 after class. Letters are the error register.

- Q1: an interest behind his choice, such as a fort to protect the colony. Not "he ruled everyone, Native nations too" (AC).
- Q1b: an interest behind her choices, such as trade for tools while keeping the fishing grounds. Not "because she ruled everyone there" (AC).
- Q2: the cornfield was lost under water, caused by the mill dam raising the river. Not "one cause explains every loss" (N) or "they chose to lose it" (F).
- Q2b: a storehouse burned, caused by the fight over a boundary: a different cause from A. Not "the dam caused it too" (N).
- Q3: a servant worked a set time; an enslaved person was held for life and denied freedom. Not "enslaved workers could leave after a term" (Q).
- Q4: water now comes from a tap; butter is made by machine or bought. Any change the drawing shows. Not "home and work are the same now" (AB).
- Q5: no. It was legal in Massachusetts too. Not "yes, only southern colonies" (H).
- Q5b: some Africans were free, like Zipporah Potter Atkins, who bought a house in 1670. Not "all Africans were enslaved" (R).
- Q6, Q6b: sheltered water let ships load, and work and trade gathered there. Not "the water alone built it" (W).
- Q7: traders carried people by force and sold them; their forced labor grew the tobacco. Not "everyone travelled by choice" (T).
- Q8: a claim that stays inside the evidence, such as sharing was possible for a time (the 1621 alliance card, A9) but broke down where relations worsened (the Seloy card, B4); with a limit. Not a wish with no evidence (J).
- Q8b: one detail from A and one from B. Not two details from A (AG).
- Q8c: two named sources, each linked to the claim. Not titles alone (AG).
- Q8d: it names the evidence the supporting question gave. Not "it helped because it had words" (AH).
- R1 to R9: scored from the revised folder; the look-fors are in the unit plan and the record sheet.
