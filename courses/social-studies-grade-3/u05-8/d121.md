# Day 121: Fix one idea

## Teacher page

### Before class
Before class, score day 120 from key-day120.md and write on each sheet one note: a circled missed idea and one sentence. Sort children into at most four groups by the largest gaps (A timeline, B sources and claims, C causes and effects, D lives and law). Put each group's written move and fresh card on its table. The fresh cards and the Fresh Passages are made up; say so. Keep key-exits-117-121.md with you.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 120's assessment, the sheets with their notes: s05-Q1, s05-Q1b, s05-Q2, s05-Q2b, s05-Q3, s05-Q4, s05-Q5, s05-Q5b, s05-Q6, s05-Q6b, s05-Q7, s05-Q8, s05-Q8b, s05-Q8c, s05-Q8d and the revised records s05-R1 to s05-R9. Hand back day 119's slips and strips too (s05-E119, s05-E119b, s05-P119a). Each child reads the note silently or with a partner. Say: a fix needs a new answer and a reason, not a copy.

#### 20 min. Groups
The group meets you for 5 minutes in turn; the other groups work in pairs from their written move, on the fresh card, and stop before the move's last line (the explaining is the exit's work). Hear each child in your group once, about 45 seconds each: Group A, [[s05-P121a]]; Group B, [[s05-P121b]]; Group C, [[s05-P121c]]; Group D, [[s05-P121d]]. Group A also builds a fresh overlap of two invented bands for the K gap from day 119. Name each child heard on the record.

#### 10 min. Fresh task
Hand each child the Fresh Passage for their group on the exit slip (cards-exits-117-119.md, made up). Each child fixes the missed idea marked on their sheet, using a detail from the passage: [[s05-E121]] and [[s05-E121b]]. A scribe or a spoken answer is offered. While they write, hear any child not yet heard in a group, about 45 seconds each, up to six. Key: key-exits-117-121.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Reflection
Each child says to a partner what they fixed and why. Nobody is made the example of a mistake. Close the folders; the unit is done.

**Shared shape.** The group cards and the spoken asks use one fresh case each; the exit gives a different Fresh Passage and asks the child to fix a marked idea with a detail from it.

### Other rooms
No groups: read each note aloud to one child; they fix the idea on the Fresh Passage for their note.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; the parent reads the note aloud.

**Skip.** The group asks.

- **5 min. The note.** Read the note on the day 120 sheet.
- **15 min. Try again.** Pick a made-up passage that fits the note and talk it through. **Say.** "What did you miss, and what would you add?" Expect: the idea named in the note, with a detail from the passage.
- **10 min. The fix.** Write the new answer and a detail from the passage.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-E121]] [[s05-E121b]] Tell the adult what you fixed.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fix one idea only; drop the group asks. Longer: fix a second marked idea on another passage.

### Words and supports
Words: note, fix, evidence, passage, overlap. Frame: "I fixed ___ because the passage says ___." Read every passage aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 122 is course check 2: primary and secondary sources, and a short timeline. Day 121's exit tells you who still needs the idea; none is scored against the unit.

### Credit
Ours. All passages and cards are made up.
