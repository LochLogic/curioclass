# Written moves for day 121 groups

Teacher only. Each group works from its written move on a fresh case (cards-day121-fresh.md). No dossier text is reused.

## Group A: timeline and centuries (A, AA, K)

Read the strip. Mark each band with its years. Say which years two bands share. Fix any missed idea on the fresh strip.

## Group B: sources and claims (C, J, AG, AH)

Read the fresh passage. Name the maker and why it was made. Say what it leaves out. Build a claim that stays inside the evidence and ends "this does not prove ___".

## Group C: causes and effects (F, N, W, T)

Read the fresh passage. Write each effect with its own cause. Say whether each loss was a choice, a pressure or a seizure. Connect harbor, labor and trade on the fresh port record.

## Group D: lives and law (H, R, S, Q, G, O)

Read the fresh passage. Say who was free and who was enslaved, and where. Say what the record shows and what it cannot show. Name one change and one thing that stayed.

The explaining is the exit's work (E121b); the moves stop before it.
