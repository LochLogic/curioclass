# Teacher key, day 124 (practice and exit)

Teacher only. Mark each shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Ask the marked idea from the child's sheet on the Exit Card.

- P124a: primary, written in 1950 by someone there. Not "secondary".
- P124b: the 1960s. Not the 1900s.
- P124c: its mill pond. Not the field.
- P124d: a detail copied from Source 1, sixty children came. Not "the fair is fun".
- Exit Card A: Source 1 is primary, a diary kept that week; Source 2 is secondary, written in 2019; a good question for Source 1 is who lit the lamp.
- Exit Card B: the founding is the earliest; the church is in the 1860s; the fire hall in the 1910s; 1801 is the 1800s, the nineteenth century; the park (1990) is the 1990s, the twentieth century.
- Exit Card C: the League gave up its old dock and kept its beach landing right; the pier is the Harbor Company's gain; for the League the agreement meant losing a dock and keeping the way to fish.
- Exit Card D: a claim such as the seawall kept the street dry; a detail from Source 1, three floods in 1996 and 1997; a detail from Source 2, the street has stayed dry since 1999.
- X124: a new answer that differs from the sheet and fits the card. Not the same answer again. X124b: a detail from the Exit Card, said or written.
