# Day 149: Questions about people who made a difference

## Teacher page

### Before class
Lay out the choice cards you supplied: eight to ten people or groups, women and men, each with a one-line note and two named sources. Leave out any card held for the end-of-unit check on day 159. Where a card names enslavement, say people were forced to work and were not paid, plainly; read no slur. Keep key-exits-149.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 148's slips, strips, charts and answers with their cues: s07-E148, s07-E148b, s07-P148a, s07-P148b, s07-P148c, s07-P148d, s07-P148e. If many gave 1905 to 1950 or said the new tram ended the old (error K), draw new made-up bands (invented for practice): a market, 1900 to 1930, and a library, opened 1915. A group of up to 6 who still wrote "everyone" (error D) reread one supported claim.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: in unit 1 you used before, action, after for a person's change, and again in unit 4 on day 83. Each child writes on a slip one thing a person from an earlier biography did and what it changed. Read six slips; the record names who was read.

#### 8 min. Two kinds of question
Show the made-up Tomas Card (invented for practice): Tomas Reyes, a baker who lit the ovens before dawn, and a garden club. Say: some questions ask for looks, names or dates; some ask for an action, a change or what went on. Put six on the board in two columns and say why each goes where. Looks, names, dates: "How tall was Tomas?" "When was Tomas born?" "What is the club's name?" Action or change: "Why did Tomas light the ovens before dawn?" "What did the club do with the empty lot?" "What do people still do in the garden today?" The last three model a question about the person, the club and change. Each child fixes [[s07-P149a]].

#### 9 min. Choose a person
Read each choice card aloud once. Each child then reads their own pick, and writes [[s07-P149b]]. Several may choose the same. A relative is never required; nothing is asked about home.

#### 10 min. Three questions
Each child fixes three weak questions for the chosen person or group, writing the new question: [[s07-P149c]], [[s07-P149d]] and [[s07-P149e]]. Frame: "How did ___ change ___?"

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the quarter slip. Read the made-up Reading Room Card once, then the ask once: [[s07-E149]]. Key: key-exits-149.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice fixes a weak question on a made-up baker; the three fixes on the child's own pick take the same form; end item Q3 asks a new question about cards no child has seen; the exit gives a new made-up library card with three questions and asks which one asks about an action.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the questions frame on plain paper. Read the choice cards from your own copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No choice cards: use the cards the teacher sent.

**Skip.** The repair, the stop cards and the swap.

- **10 min. Tomas.** Read the Tomas Card. [[s07-P149a]] **Say.** "What would you want to know about what he did?" Expect: a what, how or why question about his work.
- **10 min. Choose.** Read the cards. [[s07-P149b]]
- **10 min. Questions.** [[s07-P149c]] [[s07-P149d]] [[s07-P149e]] **Say.** "Does it ask what someone did or what changed?" Expect: yes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E149]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fix the weak question, choose, write one question. Longer: write a second question.

### Words and supports
Words: biography, action, change, continued. Frames: "I want to know how ___ changed ___." "What did ___ do?" Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 150 hands back E149 and P149a to P149e. The exit tells you who chose a looks or a name question as the action question (error P); they replace a fresh appearance question with one about an action on day 150. Day 150 takes notes on the chosen person from two sources.

### Credit
Ours. The choice cards are teacher-supplied and credited aloud; Tomas Reyes, the garden club and the Reading Room Card are made up.
