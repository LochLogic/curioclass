# Exit cards, days 150 to 152

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. Each exit is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-150-152.md (teacher only). The notes, the biography and the sharing record are folder records (retained evidence), not exits.

## Day 150: Source Pair Card (made up, for practice)

Source 1: a museum page about a baker, made by a museum. Source 2: another page about the same baker, made by the same museum.

Ask: Source Pair Card. Do these two sources have different makers?

## Day 151: Mara Card (made up, for practice)

A. In 1890 the school grew. B. In 1880 Mara opened a school. C. In 1900 the school closed.

Ask: Mara Card. Write the letters in time order.

## Day 152: Sharing Card (made up, for practice)

A. Two partners read their summaries to each other. B. A poster hangs in the room. C. A librarian hears a summary at the library desk.

Ask: Sharing Card. Which one shares with someone outside the class: A, B or C?
