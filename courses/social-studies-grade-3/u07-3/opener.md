# Section 7.3 opener: people who made a difference

## What the section does

Four days of 40 minutes. Day 149 asks questions about a person and a group and each child chooses a person. Day 150 takes notes from two sources by different makers. Day 151 writes a short biography in time order. Day 152 shares it with someone outside the class.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A good question asks about action or change** (P). What color or what name is not enough.
- **Names and order alone do not explain a contribution** (E). The biography joins an action to a change with "so".
- **Sharing means someone outside the class** (F). A partner or a poster is not that.

## The errors in plain words

E is a list of dates with no change in it. F is thinking talk with a partner counts as sharing outside the class. P is a question about looks or names.

## Supplies

Paper, pencils, the notes frame, the biography template, the exit card in cards-exits-149.md and the key in key-exits-149.md (teacher only). The choice cards (eight to ten people, each with two sources by different makers) are teacher-supplied and are not written here.
