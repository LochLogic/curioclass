# Day 150: Notes from two sources

## Teacher page

### Before class
Give each child the two source cards for their pick (teacher-supplied, at most 120 words each, different makers). Write the Tomas Reyes example on the board so it can come down on day 151. Put small paper date cards by the wall strip. Keep key-exits-150-152.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 149's slips and questions with their notes: s07-E149, s07-P149a, s07-P149b, s07-P149c, s07-P149d, s07-P149e. If many chose a question about looks or a name (error P), show a fresh made-up question, "What color was the baker's cart?", and let children replace it with one about an action. The rest start the retrieval slip.

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: in unit 4 a copy adds nothing; in unit 5 a claim had two sources with their makers. A second source needs a different maker; two details from one book are one source. Each child writes on a slip one reason why. Read six; the record names them.

#### 6 min. Model once
Show the made-up Tomas Reyes sources (invented for practice). Source 1: the City Report, made by the city, 1910: Tomas lit his ovens before dawn. Source 2: a letter by a mill worker, 1912: "I bought hot rolls from Tomas on the way to the mill." Fill the frame on the board: name and maker, one dated detail each. Then draw one arrow from his action to its change and say why: "Tomas baked before dawn, so mill workers could eat before their shifts." Say: so joins an action to a change. This is the only filled example.

#### 16 min. Notes
Read the two cards aloud to a small group of up to 8 at the side table for the first 6 minutes. Each child fills the frame: [[s07-P150a]], [[s07-P150b]], [[s07-P150c]], [[s07-P150d]], and puts the person's dates on a card at the wall strip. The last 10 minutes, walk and write one-line cues on each frame: about ten seconds a child, five minutes for 30. Practice, revised on day 158.

#### 6 min. Links and questions
Each child draws one arrow from an action to a change and writes [[s07-P150e]]. Then [[s07-P150f]] and [[s07-P150g]]. Frame: "___ so ___."

#### 3 min. Partner check
Partners find each other's two sources and say which detail came from which, 1 minute. Then hand out the quarter slip. Read the made-up Source Pair Card once, then the ask once: [[s07-E150]]. Key: key-exits-150-152.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The model fills the notes frame for a made-up baker from two sources with different makers, and children fill it from their own two; the exit gives a new card of two pages by one museum and asks if the makers differ.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the frame on plain paper. Read the two cards aloud from your own copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No class cards: use the two sources the teacher sent.

**Skip.** The retrieval, the stop cards and the partner check.

- **5 min. Why two?** **Say.** "Why must the second source have a different maker?" Expect: two details from one maker are one source.
- **15 min. Notes.** Read both sources aloud. [[s07-P150a]] [[s07-P150b]] [[s07-P150c]] [[s07-P150d]] **Say.** "Who made it, and when?" Expect: the maker and the date from the card.
- **10 min. Links.** [[s07-P150e]] [[s07-P150f]] [[s07-P150g]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E150]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one source in full, then the link. Longer: test a third source against one detail.

### Words and supports
Words: source, maker, detail, so. Frames: "Source 1 says ___; Source 2 says ___." "___ so ___." Use a color for each source; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 151 hands back P150a to P150g and E150. The exit tells you who counted two pages by one maker as two sources (error I); the notes tell you who listed names and dates with no change (error E). Day 151 writes the notes in time order.

### Credit
Ours. The two source cards are teacher-supplied and credited; Tomas Reyes, the City Report, the mill worker's letter and the Source Pair Card are made up.
