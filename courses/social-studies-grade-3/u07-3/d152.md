# Day 152: Sharing it with someone outside the class

## Teacher page

### Before class
Return each child's day 151 paragraph and their day 148 interim answer. Print the card and sharing record sheets. The card carries a first name only: no photograph, school or class name. Ask nothing about home; a child with no family adult nearby will use a school adult on day 158. Keep key-exits-150-152.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 151's paragraphs and slips with their cues: s07-P151a, s07-P151b, s07-E151. If many listed dated facts with no change (error E), read a fresh made-up paragraph (invented for practice): "In 1920 Lila Ortiz opened a flower stall. In 1925 she moved it to Pine Street." Each child fixes one line so it says what changed because of her action. Others read their cues.

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: in unit 2 you used the claim frame, "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." Say it back. Each child reads their day 148 interim answer to a partner.

#### 10 min. The card
Each child writes [[s07-P152a]]: a summary of at most 60 words with the person's first name, the action and the change. Then [[s07-P152b]], the answer to the big question, "Who has made our community what it is?" Words or a drawing are answers.

#### 12 min. Rehearse
Say: a partner hears a rehearsal; a listener outside the class is the one who counts. Pairs say the summary in one minute, then the partner asks one question and writes it on the rehearsal line; 4 minutes each way, then switch. Hear six children one at a time at the side table; the record names who was heard. The rehearsal line is not the sharing record.

#### 6 min. Plan the listener
Model one line aloud: "A neighbor will hear it on Thursday and ask me a question." Each child writes [[s07-P152c]]: the kind of listener, a family adult or neighbor or a school adult on day 158, and the day. The question line, [[s07-P152d]], is left empty until the summary is heard. Nothing is asked about home.

#### 3 min. Check the sheets
Hand out the quarter slip. Read the made-up Sharing Card once, then the ask once: [[s07-E152]]. While children write, walk and initial each listener line, about six seconds a child. Key: key-exits-150-152.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice writes a card, rehearses with a partner and names a real listener; the exit gives a new made-up card of three ways of sharing and asks which one reaches someone outside the class.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the card and sheet on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class card: write the summary on plain paper.

**Skip.** The repair, the partner rehearsal and the stop cards.

- **15 min. The card.** [[s07-P152a]] [[s07-P152b]] **Say.** "Who has made our community what it is?" Expect: the child's answer from day 148.
- **10 min. Say it.** The child says the summary to you. Ask one question. [[s07-P152d]] **Say.** "How did that change what people could do?" Expect: the change in the summary.
- **5 min. Plan.** [[s07-P152c]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E152]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: write the summary in three sentences and name the listener. Longer: say it to a second listener and write a second question.

### Words and supports
Words: summary, listener, outside the class. Frames: "My person is ___. Because ___, ___." "I think ___ helped make our community because the ___ shows ___." Read every card aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 153 begins the informed action: how one person alone and one group together took on a regional or world problem. Its Return names who still needs a listener. The exit tells you who thought partner talk or a poster shares beyond the class (error F). It hands back P152a, P152b, P152c and E152, and P152d once a listener has heard.
