# Day 151: Writing the biography in time order

## Teacher page

### Before class
Take down the Tomas Reyes example from day 150; nothing is modelled on a child's own person today. Keep the blank time-order template where all can see it. Put out each child's notes frame. Print the quarter slips. Keep key-exits-150-152.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 150's frames and slip with their cues: s07-P150a, s07-P150b, s07-P150c, s07-P150d, s07-P150e, s07-P150f, s07-P150g, s07-E150. If many counted two pages by one maker as two sources (error I), show two fresh made-up pages by one baker's guild and ask whose pages they are. If many listed names and dates with no change (error E), read fresh made-up notes: Nan Okafor read to children at the library (1925 library book); more children borrowed books (1927 newsletter). Join them: "Nan read to children, so more children borrowed books." Then each child retries alone: Ben Cho opened a bike shop (1930 shop book); more children rode to school (1932 newsletter). The rest begin the time words.

#### 3 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: first, next, last in kindergarten. New: then, later and so, which say what came after and what it led to. In pairs, each child says one sentence about their morning with first, then and later.

#### 4 min. The template
Show the blank template and read its lines aloud: first (source 1), then (source 2), so, later, one more detail. Say: the order of the lines is the order in time, so put the earliest date first.

#### 20 min. Write
Each child writes [[s07-P151a]] from their notes: five to seven sentences in time order, the person, one dated detail from each source named, and one sentence on what changed because of the action. Words, a labelled drawing or a scribe are answers. Walk and write one cue on each paragraph, about ten seconds a child, five minutes for 30. Hear no one aloud.

#### 5 min. Partner read
Each child reads the paragraph to a partner. The partner points to the place where the action leads to the change. If it is missing, the writer adds one so: [[s07-P151b]].

#### 3 min. Revise one line
In two minutes each child changes one line on a clean copy. Practice; the biography is revised on day 158. In the last minute, hand out the quarter slip. Read the made-up Mara Card once, then the ask once: [[s07-E151]]. Key: key-exits-150-152.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The paragraph is a folder record written from the child's own notes in time order, and the exit gives a new made-up card with three dated lines in the wrong order and asks for the order.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the template lines on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class template: copy the lines on the page.

**Skip.** The repair, the stop cards and the partner read.

- **5 min. Words.** **Say.** "Tell me about your morning with first, then and later." Expect: three steps in order.
- **20 min. Write.** [[s07-P151a]]
- **5 min. Read it.** Read it to the adult. [[s07-P151b]] **Say.** "Which line shows what changed because of the action?" Expect: the line with so.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E151]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: write the first, then and so lines only. Longer: add a sentence about what stayed the same.

### Words and supports
Words: time order, first, then, later, so. Frames: "First ___. Then ___. So ___. Later ___." Read every line aloud; a scribe or a labelled drawing is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 152 hands back P151a, P151b and E151 with their cues. The exit tells you who put the lines in the order they were listed (error E); they join fresh dated facts to a change on day 152. Day 152 shares the biography with someone outside the class.
