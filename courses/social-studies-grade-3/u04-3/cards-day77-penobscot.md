# Day 77: Penobscot case card

One nation, one example. Present tense. Read aloud twice, with the credit.

**The Penobscot Nation.** The Penobscot Nation is in Maine. The Nation calls the Penobscot River the heart of Penobscot culture. The Penobscots have relied on the rivers of Maine for food, drinking water and travel for thousands of years. Today the Nation has a Chief and a Council. Its offices are at Indian Island, Maine.

Credit: the Penobscot Nation's own pages.
