# Day 79: Menominee case card

One nation, one example. It does not stand for the whole Midwest. Present tense. Only where the Nation lives and where it lived. Read aloud twice, with the credit.

**The Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin.** The Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin governs from the Village of Keshena. That is on the Menominee Indian Reservation, about 45 miles northwest of Green Bay, Wisconsin. The page says the Menominee once lived across the area now called Wisconsin and parts of Michigan and Illinois. Today less than half of the people are able to live on the Reservation. The page does not say more.
