# Day 80: Navajo case card, the river account and a schematic

One nation, one example. Present tense. No sacred mountains, stories, designs or ceremony. Read aloud twice.

**The Navajo Nation.** The Navajo Nation is in the Southwest. It governs itself today. Council delegates meet in the Council Chambers to talk about problems and pass laws. In 1991 the government was reorganized into three branches. Credit: the Navajo Nation's own History page.

**The San Juan River.** The San Juan River begins in southern Colorado. It flows west through a narrow farming valley below Navajo Dam. The Navajo Indian Irrigation Project is a network of irrigation facilities. It takes water from Navajo Dam and Reservoir. The page says it is meant to serve the Navajo Nation. It waters a large farm of more than 110,000 acres. Credit: the Navajo Nation Water Rights Commission, in our words.
