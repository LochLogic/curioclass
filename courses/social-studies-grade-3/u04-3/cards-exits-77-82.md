# Exit cards, days 77 to 82

Print "made up, for practice" in small letters on the made-up cards. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken.

## Day 77: Mattamiscontis Stream Card

The Penobscot Nation works on streams in the Mattamiscontis area. For about ten years this work has centered there. The Nation is rebuilding lake outlets. More than twenty stream connectivity projects are finished. Alewife and blueback herring are fish. As their numbers grow, the Nation expects more harvest for its people.

Ask 1: Mattamiscontis Stream Card. Why does the Nation work along this stream?
Ask 2: Mattamiscontis Stream Card. Is this work in the past or now?

## Day 78: Choctaw Card

The Choctaw people have called the Southeast home for centuries. In the 1830s the United States sought to remove the Choctaw from Mississippi, and many Choctaw people were forced away. Many went west to what is now Oklahoma. About 4,000 Choctaw people stayed in Mississippi. Today the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma has its offices in Durant, Oklahoma. The Mississippi Band of Choctaw Indians governs itself in Mississippi.

Ask 1: Choctaw Card. Why are the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma lands in Oklahoma?
Ask 2: Choctaw Card. Did every Choctaw family leave Mississippi?

## Day 79: Shawano Lake Card

A college in Wisconsin writes that wild rice camps were common on Shawano Lake until as recent as the early 1900s. People harvested wild rice at these camps. This is a passage from the College of Menominee Nation.

Ask 1: Shawano Lake Card. Why did people camp by the lake?
Ask 2: Shawano Lake Card. Does the card show where all Menominee people lived?

## Day 80: Navajo Exit Locator

Passage: "The Navajo Nation extends into the states of Utah, Arizona and New Mexico." This is from the Navajo Nation's own History page.

```
   Colorado    Utah
   New Mexico  Arizona
        (.)  presence only, no territorial boundaries
```

Ask 1: Navajo Exit Locator. Write the states the passage names.
Ask 2: Navajo Exit Locator. Do those state edges mark the homeland edge?

## Day 81: Shore Case

Families in a made-up town live beside a calm bay today. They keep their boats on the sand there. Each morning they fish beyond the bay.

Ask 1: Shore Case, invented. Why do people live near that bay?
Ask 2: Shore Case. Name one thing the people do that helps explain it.

## Day 82: two short cards

**Potter Card, invented.** In a made-up town, a clay bank lies beside a creek. Potters dig the clay and make bowls and jars there.

**Dairy Farm Card, invented.** At a made-up farm, cows graze on upland grass in summer. The farm puts a summer shed beside the grass.

Ask 1: Potter Card, invented. How do the clay bank and pottery work explain the place?
Ask 2: Dairy Farm Card, invented. How do upland grass and grazing explain the shed?
