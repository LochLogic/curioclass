# Wants cards and trade card, day 53

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Three wants cards (teacher holds them up)

- The baker holds bread and wants shoes.
- The cobbler holds shoes and wants a table.
- The carpenter holds a table and wants a hat.

Nobody wants bread, so no pair can swap. Barter is a trade with no money. Then each of the three is given a few counters as money: each sells for money and buys what they want. Paying gives up money, so someone gives up something.

## Fresh trade card

A shopper pays $4 to the kite maker for a kite. The kite maker pays $4 to the soup cook for lunch.

Pairs mark who pays money to whom.

## Sentence frame

Money helps because ____ can ____.

Barter is one comparison, shown once. Do not say that everyone once swapped goods.
