# Local Production Record, made-up edition

Print "made up, for practice" on both cards. Replace them, or add cards, from two or more pages of our town's own businesses. Print the maker, the page and the date read.

## Card 1: Finch Street Bakery (made up)

Made by Curio Class, 2026. Finch Street Bakery bakes bread from flour and sells it to neighbors. It uses an oven, a mixer and what its bakers learned.

## Card 2: Alder Bike Stand (made up)

Made by Curio Class, 2026. Alder Bike Stand fixes bicycle tyres for riders. It uses a pump, a tyre lever and what its mechanic learned.

## Table the pairs fill from each card

What the business makes or does. A good or a service. One natural, one human and one capital resource.
