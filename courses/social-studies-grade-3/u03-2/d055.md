# Day 55: A survey of businesses near us

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 54's slips for error F and error M and pick the more-missed. If you have two public pages of our town's own businesses, summarize them on the two cards, with maker, page and date; otherwise use the made-up pair. Keep key-exits-53-55.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 54's slips (s03-E54, s03-E54b). Whole class, the more-missed. Error F: "the profit is $12" beside "$12 are sales; take away the costs". Error M: "the stew, because it sells for more" beside "pears leave more after costs". The other goes to the survey side table.

#### 5 min. The survey table, modeled
Nothing is new today. Review: the three baskets from day 51, good and service from day 50, hoped-for profit from day 54. Fill one row for the day 51 bakery: it makes bread, a good; wheat is natural, the bakers' know-how human, the oven capital; it might make more of the bread that sells, because it hopes to earn.

#### 16 min. Survey two businesses
Read each business card aloud once; the maker and date are on each. Pairs fill row 1 and row 2 of the survey table, each child writing [[s03-P55a]], [[s03-P55b]] and [[s03-P55c]] for the first card. Visit a rotating six pairs, about 1.5 minutes each. A side table of up to six takes the other of error F or error M for 5 minutes. Fast pairs may add a third and fourth business: this is the part to cut.

#### 6 min. Share rows
Groups of four, two pairs. Each person reads one row; the listener names one resource of any kind, about 40 seconds each.

#### 5 min. What might a seller choose?
**Ask.** What might this seller choose, and why do we say might? Expect: a choice about what to make or ask, and might because the hope of profit may not come true. For each business the class names one thing; two go on the board, said with "might".

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Clock Repair Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E55]] and [[s03-E55b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The survey reads business cards and fills a table; the exit gives one new card and asks two plain questions. The Clock Repair Card is new.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the two cards on the board; children copy the table.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. No local pages: use the made-up cards.

**Skip.** The groups of four and the side table.

- **10 min. Model.** Fill one row for a bakery: what it makes, three resources, one choice.
- **20 min. Survey.** Read each card once. Your child writes [[s03-P55a]], [[s03-P55b]] and [[s03-P55c]].
  **Say.** "What might this seller choose, and why might?" Expect: a choice; the profit may not come.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E55]] [[s03-E55b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one business, not two. Longer: a third and fourth business.

### Words and supports
Words: survey, business, makes, does, resource, might. Frame: "This seller might choose ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 56 opens with: every business has to choose how to use what it has. First, the more-missed of error V (clock repair named a good) and the skill named as the capital resource, 5 minutes; the other at the side table.

### Credit
Ours. The made-up cards are invented for practice; any local card is our summary of its public page.
