# Cost and sales cards, day 54

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Sales are the money buyers pay. Profit is what is left after costs.

## Teacher model, a candle stall

A candle costs $3 to make and sells for $8. Sales $8, costs $3, profit $5.

## Pairs

- Key ring: costs $2, sells for $6. Profit?
- Cookies: costs $7, sell for $13. Profit?

## Sentence frame

A seller hopes to ____, so she chooses ____.

A hope of profit may shape what a seller makes. It does not promise that buyers will come. Profit is not a way to judge a person.
