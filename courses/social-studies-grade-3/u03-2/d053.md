# Day 53: Why money helps

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 52's slips for error S. Draw the goal bar on chart paper: four boxes of $20, $80 in all, for display supplies, made up. Nothing is collected. Keep key-exits-53-55.md away until slips are in. The ledger and the student page stay in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 52's slips (s03-E52, s03-E52b). If many gave error S: "a workshop and a specialist shop are the same" beside "one workshop divides the tasks of one product; a shop specializes". Contrast the boat workshop's three tasks with the sail shop and boat shop, two businesses.

#### 8 min. Trading without money
Review: you learned trade in kindergarten, an agreed swap where both say yes. Hold up three wants cards, made up: the baker holds bread and wants shoes; the cobbler holds shoes and wants a table; the carpenter holds a table and wants a hat. Nobody wants bread, so no pair can swap. New word, barter: a trade with no money. Say it once; do not say everyone once swapped goods.
**Ask.** Why could nobody in the three-person trade get what they wanted? Expect: nobody wanted what the next person held. Each child writes [[s03-P53a]].

#### 8 min. Add money
Review: you learned buy and sell in kindergarten. Give each of the three a few counters as money. Show three picture cards for the baker's day: bread is made, bread is sold for a counter, a counter buys shoes. The class puts the cards in order and says who gives up what at each step. Paying gives up money, so someone gives up something; free is error B. Pairs read a fresh trade card and mark who pays whom; visit a rotating six pairs. Each child writes [[s03-P53b]].

#### 12 min. Our class savings plan
Show the made-up plan: $80 toward display supplies, four boxes of $20. Review: you learned a budget in grade 1 with three jars. A budget here is money in, money out, money kept. Model week 1 for 3 minutes: In $20, Out $0, Kept $20. Each child fills the week 1 line of the ledger, [[s03-P53d]], in 5 minutes. Partners read it to each other, 1 minute each. The class shades one box. No child says what they have of their own. Saved money kept is still there; "it disappeared" is error AD.

#### 4 min. Say why money helps
Partners finish "Money helps because ___ can ___," 1 minute each way; hear a rotating six. Each child writes [[s03-P53c]]. Then write today's answer to question 2 on the question board.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Farmer Card and the Savings Card, first seen here. Read each card and ask once. Children write [[s03-E53]] and [[s03-E53b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice names who pays whom and a ledger line; the exit asks how money helps one person and whether saved money is still there. Both cards are new.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the ledger on the board; children copy the line.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use coins or buttons. No class: you are the partner.

**Skip.** The partner reading and the goal bar.

- **10 min. No money.** Read the three wants cards. Your child writes [[s03-P53a]].
  **Say.** "Why could nobody swap?" Expect: nobody wanted the next thing.
- **10 min. With money.** Pay with counters. Your child writes [[s03-P53b]] and [[s03-P53c]].
  **Say.** "Why does money help?" Expect: a seller can take it and buy from someone else.
- **10 min. A ledger.** Make up a goal. Your child writes the week 1 line, [[s03-P53d]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read each card and ask once. [[s03-E53]] [[s03-E53b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one pair of cards for the trade. Longer: a second week on the ledger.

### Words and supports
Words: barter, money, budget, ledger, in, out, kept. Frame: "Money helps because ___ can ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 54 opens with the more-missed of error B (money makes it free) and error AD (saved money disappeared) from today's exit. The other goes to a small group in the profit work.

### Credit
Ours. The cards and the savings plan are invented for practice; nothing is collected.
