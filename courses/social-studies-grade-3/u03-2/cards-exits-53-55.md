# Exit cards, days 53 to 55

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken.

## Day 53: Farmer Card

A farmer grows pumpkins. A bootmaker wants no pumpkins. The farmer wants new boots.

Ask 1: Farmer Card, invented. How can money help her get the boots?
Ask 2: Savings Card, invented. A club saves $30 in a jar. Is it gone?

## Day 54: Market Card

At a market, a seller can make stew or pears. Stew costs $9 and sells for $12. Pears cost $3 and sell for $7.

Ask 1: Market Card, invented. Which leaves more money after costs, stew or pears?
Ask 2: Market Card. How much more is left?

## Day 55: Clock Repair Card

A clock repairer fixes clocks. She uses what she learned and a workbench.

Ask 1: Clock Repair Card, invented. Is clock repair a good or a service?
Ask 2: Clock Repair Card. Which is the capital resource: the skill or the workbench?
