# Teacher key, exit cards days 53 to 55

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-53-55.md); never hand to children.

- Day 53, ask 1: She sells the pumpkins for money and pays the bootmaker. Not "money means nobody gives anything up" (B). Ask 2: No. It is kept in the jar; the club gave up spending it now. Not "it disappeared" (AD).
- Day 54, ask 1: The pears: $7 less $3 leaves $4. The stew leaves $12 less $9, which is $3. Not "stew, because it sells for more" (M). Ask 2: $1 more. Not "the sale price is the profit" (F).
- Day 55, ask 1: A service: work done for someone. Not "a good". Ask 2: The workbench: equipment people made and use to make things. Not "the skill" (a skill is human).
