# Teacher key, exit cards days 3 to 6

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-3-6.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 3: both circles on the right places; the flat card stretches sizes, bends shapes or cuts the edges.
- Day 4: Africa; northern; western.
- Day 5: C3; latitude.
- Day 6: M, because near the pole it gets less direct sunlight; no, how much rain is a different fact from how warm it is.

## Practice card keys (teacher only)

- Days 5 and 6, World Grid Card: Lima is B2 (south of the equator and west of the prime meridian, between 30 south and 30 north; column B is 120 to 60 degrees west). Oslo is D3 (north of 30 north, east of the prime meridian, column D 0 to 60 degrees east).
