# Exit cards, days 3 to 6

Print "invented for practice" at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Every child answers in writing; the day's six are also heard.

## Day 3

New Globe View Card and a new flat world card, each showing Asia, Africa and the Indian Ocean with 1-to-3-word labels. Ask: Circle Asia on both. Circle the Indian Ocean on both. Write one way the flat world card changes something from the globe.

## Day 4

A new World Outline Card with the equator and the prime meridian already drawn, and a dot on the west coast of Africa labeled with a letter. Say aloud that the dot marks Dakar, Senegal. Ask: Write its continent. Is Dakar in the northern or southern hemisphere? Is Dakar in the eastern or western hemisphere?

## Day 5

An Exit Grid: columns A to D from west to east, rows 1 to 4 from south to north, a star in C3. Ask: Write the cell name. Which kind of line runs east to west, latitude or longitude?

## Day 6

Lowlands Card, invented for practice. Two lowlands: M is near the North Pole and has steady rain; N is near the equator and has almost none. Ask: Which lowland gets more direct sunlight, M or N? Someone says N must be wetter because it is hot. Is that right?
