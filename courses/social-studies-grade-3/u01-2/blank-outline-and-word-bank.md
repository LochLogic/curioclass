# Blank outline and word bank

A flat world outline with the seven continents and five oceans unlabelled, each with a small numbered circle. Under it a word bank of twelve words: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America, South America, Arctic Ocean, Atlantic Ocean, Indian Ocean, Pacific Ocean, Southern Ocean. Children write the names in the circles. Note: the Southern Ocean is the water round Antarctica.
