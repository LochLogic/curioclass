# World Outline Card

A flat world outline with a lightly drawn border, no lines on it yet. Children draw the equator as a line across the middle with a red crayon and the prime meridian as a line down through the map with a blue crayon, then label the four half-worlds north, south, east and west. Teacher note: the equator is the 0 degree latitude line; the prime meridian is the 0 degree longitude line (NOAA, sources 1A, 1B).
