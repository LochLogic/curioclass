# World Grid Card (days 5 and 6, teaching)

Invented for practice. A flat world outline with six columns lettered A to F from west to east, three rows numbered 1 to 3 from south to north. Each column is 60 degrees across from 180 degrees west; row 1 is south of 30 south, row 2 is between 30 south and 30 north, row 3 is north of 30 north. No latitude or longitude numbers are printed on the card.

Teaching use: the teacher places a dot for Lima, Peru and the pair names its cell and its continent. Then a dot for Oslo, Norway.

Teacher key: Lima is B2 (south of the equator and west of the prime meridian, between 30 south and 30 north; column B is 120 to 60 degrees west). Oslo is D3 (north of 30 north, east of the prime meridian, column D 0 to 60 degrees east).
