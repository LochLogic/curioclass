# Classroom grid map (day 5, teaching)

A wall grid or floor grid: columns A to D from west to east, rows 1 to 4 from south to north. Mark the classroom wall with the cell names. The teacher puts a paper star on one cell and asks children to say its name, letter first, then number. For teaching put the star on B3, and a second paper shape on C1. Children call the cell, then place a token where the teacher names a cell (A4, then D3).

Teacher note: the exit uses a different printed grid and a different cell.
