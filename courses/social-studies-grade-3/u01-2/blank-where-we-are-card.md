# Where We Are card

One page for a pair. Boxes to fill in:

- Our continent: ______
- Our hemispheres: north or south ______; east or west ______
- Our cell on the class world grid: ______
- How sunny our town is, from the weather page: ______
- How much rain our town gets: ______

The teacher fills the town's two weather facts from a free, credited page. No child writes about their own home.
