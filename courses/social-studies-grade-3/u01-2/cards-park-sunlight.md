# Park cards: Death Valley and Great Smoky Mountains (day 6)

Two cards with credit. Print each fact on the card; read aloud once. Sources: National Park Service pages 6A, 6B, 6C, read 2026-11-21; locations from source 6D.

**Card 1. Death Valley National Park (California and Nevada).** The park gets under 2 inches of rain in a year. Summer temperatures can reach 120 degrees Fahrenheit. Four mountain ranges stand between the valley and the sea and block much of the rain.

**Card 2. Great Smoky Mountains National Park (Tennessee and North Carolina).** In the lowlands the park gets about 55 inches of rain in a year. Higher up it can get about 85 inches.

Ask: Which park gets more rain? Does warm weather tell us how much rain a place gets? Teacher note: rain and warmth are two different facts about a place. Credit: National Park Service.
