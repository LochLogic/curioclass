# Exit cards, days 130 to 132

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. Each exit is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-130-132.md (teacher only).

## Day 130: Oneida Card (real, original summary) and New Jersey Card (made up)

Oneida Card. In 1777 Congress sent the Oneidas a message. It promised them protection. (Source: Oneida Indian Nation, First Allies, original summary.)

Ask 1: Oneida Card. Does the message say Oneida people voted in state elections?

New Jersey Card (made up, for practice). In 1790 a free woman in New Jersey owns the property the law asks for and has lived in her county for a year.

Ask 2: New Jersey Card. Could this woman vote in 1790?

## Day 131: Voting Law Card (made up, for practice)

In 1805 a state law says only men who own land may vote. Reza was born in that state and owns none.

Ask 1: Voting Law Card. Is Reza still a citizen of the state?

Ask 2: Voting Law Card. What does this law decide?

## Day 132: Monument Card (made up, for practice)

A town council ordered a statue in 1890. The caption says it was made "to remind residents of fair courts".

Ask 1: Monument Card. Why was the statue made?

Ask 2: Monument Card. Does the caption prove everyone was treated equally?
