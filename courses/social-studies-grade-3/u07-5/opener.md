# Section 7.5 opener: review, check and response

## What the section does

Three days of 40 minutes. Day 158 returns the written feedback, revises two flagged components and retries one skill in observed pairs. Day 159 is the end-of-unit check from a historical map and sources not seen before. Day 160 responds to the check and closes the year.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Day 159 is the check.** The map and cards A to E are used on no teaching day. Only revised records are scored for the other goals.
- **An overlap is not a replacement** (K). A route and a library can both exist in the same years, and an overlap alone does not prove a cause (M).
- **Cause needs evidence** (M). Route records and library records, not one stop.

## The errors in plain words

D is thinking one record shows everyone's experience. K is thinking a new thing ended the old one. M is thinking an overlap proves a cause.

Paper, pencils, the revision sheet, the retry look-for sheet, the packet (packet-day159.md; key-day159.md is teacher only), the exit cards in cards-exits-158-160.md and the key in key-exits-158-160.md.
