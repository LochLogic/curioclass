# Day 160: Fix it, show it, close the year

## Teacher page

### Before class
Score the day 159 sheets (key-day159.md). On each sheet circle one missed idea and write one sentence. Sort children by their 0 and 1 cells into four groups, named in Groups. Make one repair card with a made-up weak line for each group, and a written move for the pairs. Find children not ticked on the day 158 look-for sheet. Keep key-exits-158-160.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 159's assessment, each sheet with its circled note: s07-Q1, s07-Q1b, s07-Q1c, s07-Q2, s07-Q2b, s07-Q3, s07-Q4, s07-Q4b, s07-Q4c, s07-Q5, s07-Q5b, s07-Q5c, s07-Q5d and the scored records s07-R1 to s07-R8c. Each child reads the note once and says the sentence to a partner. No new content.

#### 15 min. Groups
Four groups by component: people and sources (errors A, B, D, J, N); maps (errors C, O, Q); explanation and questions (errors E, P, I, K); action (errors F, G, H, L, M). Meet them in turn, about three minutes each and a half minute to move; say "switch" as a pace cue. Each child in your group takes one turn of about 20 seconds: say the weak line on the group's card again so it is right, [[s07-P160a]]. A child not ticked in the day 158 retry restates and combines on a fresh card instead: s07-P158b, s07-P158c. Partner reviews not heard on day 158 are heard here too: s07-P158e. The rest work in pairs from the written move: they read the weak line aloud and say it right to each other. Hand back the day 158 revisions, group lines and exits: s07-P158a, s07-P158d, s07-E158, s07-E158b.
Group cards, each made up: people and sources, "Everyone who lived here long ago chose to come." Maps, "The map shows every road that ever was." Explanation and questions, "He was famous and kind." Action, "We told one friend, so it worked for everyone."

#### 10 min. Fresh task
Say: this strip is made up, for practice. Read the Bus Card once. Each child writes on the slip: [[s07-E160]] [[s07-E160b]]. Then the third ask goes in the Exit box on the student page: [[s07-E160c]]. Key: key-exits-158-160. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. A child still unsure after this says it at the side table.

#### 10 min. Portfolio reflection
Each child picks the part they changed most, writes what changed and why it is stronger: [[s07-P160b]]. Then each child tells a partner, 30 seconds each. Say a closing line: the folders go to grade 4. The year closes.

**Shared shape.** The groups say a weak line right. The fresh task reads a strip, says may and names evidence. The reflection names one part and gives a reason.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the Bus Card strip by hand. Teaching one child: read the sheet note together, repair one line and do the Bus Card aloud with the adult writing.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child look through the folder together.

**Skip.** The partner talk.

- **10 min. Notes.** Read the teacher's note on the unit check aloud.
- **15 min. Fix one.** Read a weak line aloud; your child says it right. **Say.** "What is missing?" Expect: who, which source or a reason.
- **10 min. Your folder.** Your child picks the part they changed most. [[s07-P160b]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Bus Card once. [[s07-E160]] [[s07-E160b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the Bus Card and one repair. Longer: test another source against a claim.

### Words and supports
Words: overlap, may, evidence, record. Frames: "The route and the library both ran from ___ to ___." "The stop may have helped because ___." "We need ___ records." Read every card aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
This is the last day of unit 7 and of the year. The folders and the record sheet go to the grade 4 handoffs.

Ours. The Bus Card and the repair cards are made up.
