# Day 158: Make our work stronger

## Teacher page

### Before class
Write one line on each record: which look-for it shows or misses. Handed back today: the day 157 claims and sheets with cues, and the records from section 7.1 (day 141 on) to 7.4. Print the look-for sheet: a row for each child, three ticks (restated, offered a combination, voted after everyone spoke). Form five groups of six. Keep key-exits-158-160.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Written feedback
Hand back each folder with the notes: s07-P157a, s07-P157b, s07-P157c, s07-P157d, s07-E157 and the records s07-P141a, s07-P142d, s07-P147a, s07-P148e, s07-P150e, s07-P151a, s07-P152a. Say: a **criterion** is a look-for your work is judged by. Review: you used look-fors to revise a record on day 46 and revised after a one-line cue on day 119. Each child reads the notes and circles the two that flag a change.

#### 15 min. Revise
First, on the made-up lines on the student page, each child circles the better: [[s07-P158a]]. Then each revises their own two flagged components on a clean copy, a new line each (unjudged folder work). Call up to six children whose cue says error E or I to the side table for five minutes. For E, read "She was famous and kind" and ask what she did. For I, read a made-up claim with two sources and no details; each says a detail to add. A school adult hears up to six children who have no outside listener on their record sheet, about two minutes each, and initials the record; a second adult takes any more.

#### 10 min. Observed retry
Say: the question is made up, for practice: what name should our class book have? Three names are on the sheet. Groups of six hear each child's plan. Review: restate, compromise and the private vote from day 155. Sit with each group for two minutes, one group after another. In your two minutes each child takes about 20 seconds: restate another's plan, [[s07-P158b]], then offer a way to combine two plans, [[s07-P158c]]. Tick the look-for sheet; say "switch" as a pace cue. Then the group votes privately and one child writes the group's correcting line: [[s07-P158d]]. Groups waiting read the three names. The last group finishes its line in the first minute of Review. Children not reached are heard first on day 160.

#### 8 min. Review
Read the question board's five answers and the evidence list aloud. No new content. Each pair: one child tells the other, in 30 seconds, which one source best shows who made the community, and why, then they swap: [[s07-P158e]].

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read each ask once: [[s07-E158]] [[s07-E158b]]. Key: key-exits-158-160. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Collect folders and slips.

**Shared shape.** The practice picks the better of two lines; the exit asks the child to name a criterion and write their own change.

### Other rooms
Teaching one child: read the notes together; the retry is the child and an adult. No printer: copy the notes by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child read the notes together.

**Skip.** The group retry.

- **8 min. Notes.** Read the teacher's notes aloud; circle two. [[s07-P158a]]
- **15 min. Revise.** Your child writes a new line for each flagged note.
- **10 min. Two plans.** Hear your child's plan for a book name; say it back. **Say.** "What could we use from both plans?" Expect: a way to join parts.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E158]] [[s07-E158b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: revise one component and tell the other aloud. Longer: reread the revision against a look-for and change one more line.

### Words and supports
Words: criterion, revise, restate, compromise. Frames: "I changed ___ because ___." "I heard you say ___." "We could use ___ from both." Read every note aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 159 is the end-of-unit check: pencil only, packets read aloud, no new content. The exit tells you who still copied a note (error E). Day 160 hands back E158, E158b, P158a, P158d and the retry ticks; children not reached restate and combine first there.

Ours. The class book names are made up, for practice.
