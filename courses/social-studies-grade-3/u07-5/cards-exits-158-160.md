# Exit cards, days 158 and 160

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. Each exit is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-158-160.md (teacher only).

## Day 158

Ask 1: Name one rubric criterion you changed in your work.

Ask 2: Write the change you made.

## Day 160: Bus Card (made up, for practice)

On a strip, a bus route band runs 1940 to 1960. A library band runs 1930 to 1990. The library has a stop on the route.

Ask 1: Bus Card. In which years did the bus route and the library both run?

Ask 2: Bus Card. Say how the stop may have helped people reach the library.

Ask 3: Bus Card. What evidence is needed before calling the route a cause?
