# Day 158 sheets: revision, retry and review

## Revision

Two components were flagged on the written note. Rewrite each on a clean copy with a new line. Do not copy the note.

## Retry (observed pairs)

Say back another child's plan in your own words. Offer one way to combine two plans. Write one correcting line on the group's contribution record. The teacher's look-for sheet ticks: restated, offered a combination, voted after everyone spoke.

## Review

Say which one source best shows who made the community, and why.
