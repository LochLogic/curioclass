# Teacher key, exit cards days 158 and 160

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-158-160.md. Marking: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- E158: a named criterion, such as a reason that joins an action to a change. Not "I left it as it was" (E).
- E158b: the new line or detail the child added, such as a so line. Not "the claim still names two sources and no details" (I).
- E160: 1940 to 1960. Not "the library replaced the bus route in 1940" (K).
- E160b: the stop may have helped riders get to the library. Not "the library proves everyone rode the bus" (D).
- E160c: route records and library records, such as who rode and who visited. Not "the stop proves the route caused it" (M).
