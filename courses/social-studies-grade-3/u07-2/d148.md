# Day 148: The class timeline: what changed, what stayed

## Teacher page

### Before class
Check the wall strip: equal marks for equal years, with the cards of days 142 to 147 (the nation, the town, the old sheet, the interview's remembered time, the photograph). Say only what each card says. A ruler and a stop card on each desk. Keep key-exits-146-148.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 147's slips and records with their notes: s07-E147, s07-E147b, s07-P147a, s07-P147b, s07-P147c, s07-P147d, s07-P147e, s07-P147f. If many thought one image shows everyone (error D), read two made-up records (invented for practice): Ms. Reed, recorded 2001, says, "There was a bakery on my street." A photograph from 1930 shows five people at a fair table. Children sort four claims, supported or not: "Ms. Reed remembers a bakery on her street"; "Every street had a bakery"; "Five people stood at the table"; "The whole town went to the fair."

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: in units 5 and 6 you drew strips with equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks. Say back together: equal gaps for equal years; overlap, two things there at the same time.

#### 8 min. The class timeline
Walk along the wall strip and read each card once. Pick two cards. **Ask.** "What changed between these two? What stayed?" Expect a change and one thing that went on, from the cards only. Do it twice. The interview sits at the time remembered, not the recording day.

#### 8 min. Observed strip
Review: bands from a start year, unit 5. Each child marks a strip 1870 to 1920 with a ruler, a mark every ten years, then draws three made-up bands (invented for practice): [[s07-P148a]]. Then each child shades where the ferry and the bridge both stood and names both: [[s07-P148b]]. If many draw the bands one after another (error K), draw them on the board and say: the ferry was still running when the bridge opened.

#### 8 min. What changed, what stayed
Each child picks two wall-strip cards and writes [[s07-P148c]] and [[s07-P148d]]. While they write, walk and write one cue on each strip, on spacing or overlap: about ten seconds a child, five minutes for 30.

#### 5 min. Interim answer
Each child writes [[s07-P148e]] from the nation's account and the interview; a drawing is an answer. Kept for day 152.

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read the made-up Strip Card once, then each ask once: [[s07-E148]] and [[s07-E148b]]. Key: key-exits-146-148.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice draws three made-up bands and shades one overlap; the exit gives two bands in words, asks their overlap and a change.

### Other rooms
No wall strip: lay the cards on a table in year order.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strip: draw it with a ruler. No class wall strip: use the interview and the photograph from yesterday.

**Skip.** The retrieval, the stop cards and the shared timeline.

- **15 min. Strip.** Draw a strip, a mark every ten years, 1870 to 1920. [[s07-P148a]] [[s07-P148b]] **Say.** "Show me where the ferry and the bridge both stood." Expect: the child points to the shaded part.
- **10 min. Changed and stayed.** [[s07-P148c]] [[s07-P148d]] **Say.** "What changed, and what went on?" Expect: one change and one thing that stayed.
- **5 min. My answer.** [[s07-P148e]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E148]] [[s07-E148b]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: draw the ferry and the bridge only. Longer: add a fourth made-up band.

### Words and supports
Words: timeline, overlap, changed, stayed. Frame: "I think ___ helped make our community because the ___ shows ___." Read every card aloud; a taped strip or a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 149 hands back E148, E148b and P148a to P148e with their cues. The exit tells you who merged the bands or thought the new tram ended the old (error K); they read new dated bands on day 149, which asks questions about people who made a difference.
