# Day 147: Photographs and traditions as sources

## Teacher page

### Before class
Choose a labeled local photograph and one public civic tradition with a dated photograph; no sacred ceremony, no Native ceremony unless the nation's page shares it. Children share only an everyday tradition (food, game, song) their family shares gladly. Both teacher-supplied; no holiday or symbol facts. Ask no child about family. Keep key-exits-146-148.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 146's slips and records with their notes: s07-E146, s07-E146b, s07-P146r, s07-P146a, s07-P146b, s07-P146c, s07-P146d, s07-P146e, s07-P146f. If many put the recording year as the time remembered (error N), read a made-up card: recorded in 1998, Mr. Vale says, "My father drove the bakery cart down Elm Street." Children label both dates (recorded 1998; remembered, earlier) and order them. A group of up to 6 who thought one speaker shows everyone (error D) says whose memory it is.

#### 5 min. Overlap model
Say: Review: in unit 5 you drew timeline bands and saw what overlaps. Draw a strip, 1850 to 1890, ten-year marks, equal gaps, with a made-up ferry, 1850 to 1880, and a made-up school, opened 1860 (invented for practice). **Ask.** "What was going on at the same time?" Expect: from 1860 to 1880 the ferry ran and the school was open. Say: a new thing does not end an old one the day it starts.

#### 3 min. Interview contribution
Each child writes [[s07-P147a]] from their own interview or the Local Interview; a child with neither copies one line you read and names its speaker.

#### 8 min. Old photographs
Show the photograph beside its label. Review (grade 2): label, account. Ask who made it, when, where. Each child copies the label: [[s07-P147b]]. Then pairs read the made-up Mill Street Card and sort three statements: [[s07-P147c]]. Review: in unit 1 you sorted claims into shows, suggests and cannot tell. S shows, G is a hint, C cannot tell.

#### 9 min. A tradition and its community
Read the community's own account aloud, naming who made it and when. Show the dated photograph beside it. **Ask.** "What does the photograph show?" Say: the photograph shows what is there; the people who keep the tradition tell what it means to them, and the account is their telling. Each child writes [[s07-P147d]]: a question about how it began, who began it or what people do.

#### 8 min. Change-and-continuity record
Review: continuity-change chart, unit 5. Each child writes, from the interview and one photograph, [[s07-P147e]] and [[s07-P147f]], each with a source and no "everyone". Practice, revised on day 158.

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read the made-up Procession Card once, then the ask once: [[s07-E147]]. Then read the card again and the second ask once for the Exit box: [[s07-E147b]]; it goes back on day 148. Key: key-exits-146-148.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice sorts three statements on a made-up bakery photograph and writes a question for an account; the exit gives a new Procession Card; the second ask is in the Exit box.

### Other rooms
No projector: pass the photograph and account around in pairs.

### At home

**Swap.** No class photograph or account: use the ones the teacher sent.

**Skip.** The overlap model, the stop cards and the pair work.

- **5 min. A line.** [[s07-P147a]]
- **10 min. Photograph.** Read the Mill Street Card. [[s07-P147c]] **Say.** "Does the photograph show it, hint at it, or not tell?" Expect: the sign shows bakery; the loaves sold, not tell.
- **10 min. Tradition.** Read the account. [[s07-P147d]] **Say.** "What does the photograph show?" Expect: only what is in it.
- **5 min. Record.** [[s07-P147e]] [[s07-P147f]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E147]] [[s07-E147b]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the sort and one record line. Longer: test the photograph.

### Words and supports
Words: photograph, label, account, shows, suggests, cannot tell. Frame: "The photograph shows ___." Read every card aloud; enlarge the photograph and say what is in it; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 148 hands back E147, E147b and P147a to P147f. The exit tells you who thought one image shows everyone (error D); they sort claims on two fresh records on day 148, which sets the cards on the class timeline.

### Credit
Ours. The photograph, tradition and account are teacher-supplied and credited aloud; Mr. Vale, the ferry, the school and the Mill Street Card are made up.
