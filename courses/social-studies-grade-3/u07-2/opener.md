# Section 7.2 opener: memories, photographs and change over time

## What the section does

Three days of 40 minutes. Day 146 reads a supplied interview and asks open questions. Day 147 reads old photographs and a community's own account of a tradition. Day 148 sets records on the class timeline and finds what changed and what stayed.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **An interview has two dates** (N). The day it was recorded is not the time the speaker remembers.
- **One memory or one image is not everyone's** (D). A photograph shows what is in it; what it meant to the people in it needs their own account.
- **A timeline is drawn to scale, and records overlap** (K). A newer way does not end an older one the day it starts.

## The errors in plain words

D is thinking one memory or picture shows everyone. K is thinking a new thing ended the old one at once. N is thinking the interview date is when the events happened.

## Supplies

Paper, pencils, rulers, the class wall strip, the exit cards in cards-exits-146-148.md and the key in key-exits-146-148.md (teacher only). The supplied interview and the old photographs are teacher-supplied; the interview is a transcript (sources.csv U7S, a neighborhood oral history, rights unclear, so print only the short facts the teacher has checked).
