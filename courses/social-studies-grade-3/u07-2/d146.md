# Day 146: Asking a speaker about the past

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the Local Interview transcript first; it is teacher-supplied, so say only what it says. Jot its speaker, recording day and time remembered. Ask no child about home; any child may decline any question. Put a stop card on each desk. Keep key-exits-146-148.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 145's slips and records with their notes: s07-E145, s07-E145b, s07-P145a, s07-P145b, s07-P145c, s07-P145d, s07-P145e, s07-P145f. Read four made-up cards (invented for practice); children sort each chosen or forced and say why. Dev read a mill notice and decided to move. A captain made Sam work on a ship, unpaid; Sam could not leave. Ivan was paid weekly to build boats and could quit. Ada was taken from home and made to work unpaid; she could not leave. Say: Sam and Ada were enslaved, people who were forced. If many thought a forced move was chosen (error B) or enslaved people were paid (error J), sort whole class; otherwise a group of up to 6 sorts while the rest begin the retrieval slip.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: practice interview with a visiting school adult, grade 2. New: an interview asks a speaker about the past. An answer comes with the speaker, the interview date (the day it was recorded) and the time remembered (what the speaker talks about). Each child writes [[s07-P146r]]. Read six slips; the record names them.

#### 12 min. The supplied interview
Review (grade 2): open question and follow-up. Model an open question, which starts with what, how or why: "What was the town square like?" Then a follow-up: "What did people do there?" Read the Local Interview aloud once. Each child fills the record: [[s07-P146a]], [[s07-P146b]], [[s07-P146c]], [[s07-P146d]], [[s07-P146e]]. **Ask.** "Which day was this recorded, and which time does the speaker talk about?" Expect: two different times. Say: this is one speaker's memory; others may differ.

#### 8 min. Practise
Pairs take turns, 4 minutes each way, with the made-up recall cards on the page: one reads a card and answers only from it, or says "the card does not say"; the other asks an open question and a follow-up. Each child writes [[s07-P146f]]. Hear three pairs; the record names them.

#### 7 min. Choose questions
Read the take-home sheet's three questions aloud. Each child marks the ones to ask, writes their own or declines. A child with no interview at home uses the supplied one; nothing is asked about home. Say: the adult writes the speaker, the interview date and the time remembered.

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read the made-up Interview Dates Card once, then each ask once: [[s07-E146]] and [[s07-E146b]]. Key: key-exits-146-148.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The record, kept in the folder, fills fields from the supplied interview; the practice writes a question with no dates; the exit gives a new made-up card and asks which time it remembers.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the record frame on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class transcript: use the one the teacher sent.

**Skip.** The repair, the stop cards and the pair talk.

- **10 min. Hear it.** Read the interview aloud. [[s07-P146a]] [[s07-P146b]] [[s07-P146c]] [[s07-P146d]] [[s07-P146e]] **Say.** "Which day was it recorded, and which time does the speaker talk about?" Expect: two different times.
- **10 min. Ask.** Read one recall card. [[s07-P146f]] **Say.** "Ask what, how or why." Expect: a question that starts with what, how or why.
- **5 min. Remember.** [[s07-P146r]]
- **5 min. Choose.** Pick one question from the sheet.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E146]] [[s07-E146b]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the record and one recall card. Longer: write a second follow-up.

### Words and supports
Words: interview, speaker, open question, follow-up. Frame: "The speaker is ___. It was recorded on ___. The speaker remembers ___." Read every card aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 147 hands back E146, E146b, P146r and P146a to P146f. The exit tells you who gave the recording year as the time remembered (error N) or thought one speaker shows everyone (error D); they label both dates on a fresh recollection on day 147, which reads photographs and a tradition.
