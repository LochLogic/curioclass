# Exit cards, days 146 to 148

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. Each exit is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-146-148.md (teacher only).

## Day 146: Interview Dates Card (made up, for practice)

In 2015 a woman is recorded. She says, "When I was a girl, we skated on the pond behind the school."

Ask 1: Interview Dates Card. Was the skating in 2015 or earlier?

Ask 2: Interview Dates Card. Does it show every child in town skated there?

## Day 147: Procession Card (made up, for practice)

A photograph shows people walking down a street with banners. The label says only the year and the street.

Ask 1: Procession Card. What does the photograph need the community's own account to explain?

Ask 2: Procession Card. Does it show that everyone in town joined?

## Day 148: Strip Card (made up, for practice)

On a strip, a horse tram band runs 1905 to 1935. An electric tram band runs 1920 to 1950.

Ask 1: Strip Card. In which years did both trams run?

Ask 2: Strip Card. Did the horse tram stop when the electric tram opened?
