# Repair pictures, day 126

Made up, for practice. The teacher reads every caption aloud. Used in the 10-minute return and repair part, for errors A and B from the readiness page.

## Picture sort

Three pictures: a flag on a pole; a tall stone memorial tower in a capital city; a lunch box. Ask: name one that stands for the whole country and one that is only an object. A flag stands for the country. A national memorial stands for the country's shared memory. A lunch box is only an object. (Item: [[s06-P126a]])

## Fresh pair

A pencil box and a flag of the country. Which one stands for the country?

## Banner card

A town banner with a heron and no caption. Ask: write a caption that gives the heron a meaning, such as "The heron is patient and calm, like our town." The meaning is an idea, not the name of the bird. (Item: [[s06-P126b]])

## Second caption (if many missed B)

A school flag with a lantern. The caption says: "The lantern shows we help each other find the way." Ask what the lantern stands for.
