# Teacher key, exit cards days 125 to 127

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-125-127.md. Marking: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- E126: the shut harbor adds to their sense of unfair treatment, so they wanted a government of their own with a say. Not "they wanted no government at all" (C).
- E126b: 1774 sits between 1770 and 1780, nearer 1770 (four centimeters after the 1770 mark), two years before the 1776 card. Not one step after the last card whatever the years (H).
- E127 (the idea about power): the people's agreement, the consent of the governed. Not "nothing, whoever is in charge decides" (T).
- E127b: government is meant to protect their rights and they should have a say in it. Not "the leader owns their rights" (O).
