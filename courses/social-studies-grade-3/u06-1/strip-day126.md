# Timeline strip, 1700 to 1810

Student page. Print one strip for each child, or draw it by hand on a long paper strip.

Rule one long line. Make one centimetre a year. Put a mark every ten years and write 1700, 1710, 1720 and on to 1810. Equal years get equal gaps.

Place cards by their years. Day 126: Plymouth, 1620, is too far left to draw (it would be 80 centimetres before the strip begins), so the teacher marks it with an arrow at the left edge. 1776 sits between 1770 and 1780, six centimetres after the 1770 mark; check that gap with a ruler. Draw your card for 1776 and fill the four-box label under it.

Later days add 1787, 1791 and a band from 1776 to 1807. Keep the strip in your folder.
