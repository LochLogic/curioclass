# Unit 6 start page: two symbol cards

## Student

For the teacher. This is one short page, not a test. It is asked on day 124 in the last ten minutes. Read each card aloud once, then read the question aloud once. Every child answers on the page with a few words or a circle. A child who prefers may answer at the side table. Note on the record sheet what each child did and what help was used. Give no score. Made up, for practice.

Symbol Card (made up, for practice). Two pictures: a flag on a pole, and a school chair.
