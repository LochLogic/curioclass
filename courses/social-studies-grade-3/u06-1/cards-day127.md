# Cards for day 127

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. The teacher reads each card aloud once.

## Voice sort: four lines (made up, for practice)

Sort each line into "wanted no rules" or "wanted a voice".
1. We want a say in what taxes we pay.
2. We want no one to make any rule at all.
3. We want to choose people to speak for us.
4. We want no one to tell us anything, ever.

Item: [[s06-P127a]].

## The words

Read the equality line twice with a plain gloss: "all men are created equal" means all are meant to be equal; the writers also wrote of rights, "Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness." Read the consent line twice: governments get their power from the people's agreement. The teacher says plainly that in 1776 many people, including enslaved people and women, were not treated as equal, and day 130 asks who.

## Arden Lake boat rule (made up, for practice)

The neighbors chose the Arden Lake council. The council wrote a rule: no motors on the lake. Ask: where did the council's power come from?

## Word wall and chart

Two words on the wall: democratic principle (an idea about how a people's government should work, such as equality or consent) and civic virtue (a good habit shown by what a person does, such as fairness). Sort six phrases ours into two columns:
- everyone is equal
- the people agree
- telling the truth to the council
- helping a neighbor carry a ladder
- power comes from the people
- listening before deciding

Principles: everyone is equal, the people agree, power comes from the people. Virtues: telling the truth, helping a neighbor, listening before deciding. Item: [[s06-P127b]].

## Claim line

Finish: Government needs ___ because ___. Item: [[s06-P127c]].
