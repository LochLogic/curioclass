# Cards for day 125

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. The teacher reads each card aloud once.

## Hook card

Title: The Declaration of Independence. Read aloud: "governments are instituted among Men, deriving their just powers from the consent of the governed" (National Archives transcript, public domain).

Our two lines beside it: People agree to be governed. A government needs their agreement.

Ask the class: Whose agreement does a government need?

## Room 5 reading corner (made up, for practice)

Version one: the teacher announces who sits where in the reading corner. Version two: the class talks about the corner and agrees on where each group sits. Ask: Which one had the children's agreement?

Word card for the wall: consent means agreeing to how decisions are made. Consent does not mean liking every rule.

## Two ways to be fair (reminder card from unit 2)

Everyone votes on each rule. Or chosen people decide.

## Shop card (made up, for practice)

A shop owner hears of a new rule about opening hours. The class says who else cares about the rule and why, in the frame: "___ cares because ___."

## The six supporting questions for the board

Why independence (day 126)? What did the writers believe (day 127)? What makes a government plan (day 128)? What rights were listed (day 129)? Whose voices counted (days 130 and 131)? What do symbols promise (day 132)?
