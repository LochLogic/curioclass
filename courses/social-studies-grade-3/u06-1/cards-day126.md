# Cards for day 126

The teacher reads each card aloud once. Sources: National Archives (public domain), sources.csv U6A and U6B.

## Four-box label for the Declaration

Maker: a committee of the colonists' leaders. Date: 1776. Audience (who it was written for): the king and the wider world. Purpose: to give reasons for separating.

Audience is the new word: who a source is written for.

## Taxation card

Declaration, 1776, read aloud: "For imposing Taxes on us without our Consent."

Plain words: taxes are money people pay to a government. The writers complained that taxes were set without their agreement. Ask: why did this feel unfair? Link to consent. The colonists did not want no rules; they wanted a say. The war is named only as "later, in grade 5".

## Strip

Student page: the paper strip from strip-day126.md. The class places 1620 (Plymouth, from unit 5) and 1776 and checks the gap with a ruler. Each child draws and labels one card for 1776 and fills the four-box label. Item: [[s06-P126c]].
