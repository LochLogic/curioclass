# Exit cards, days 125 to 127

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. Each is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-125-127.md (teacher only).

## Day 125: Bus Driver Card (made up, for practice)

A bus driver is told not to speak at the town meeting.

Ask: Bus Driver Card. Why might the driver care whose agreement the town needs?

## Day 126: Harbor Card, 1774 (real, original summary)

In 1774 a law shut the harbor of Boston to shipping. Ships could not land or load goods there. Later that year the First Continental Congress, a meeting of colonists' delegates, called laws like it unjust and cruel. (Source: Declaration and Resolves of the First Continental Congress, 1774, Avalon Project.)

Ask 1: Harbor Card. How might this help explain why they declared independence?

Ask 2: Strip Card. Mark 1774 where it belongs on your strip.

## Day 127: Town Leader Card (made up, for practice)

A town leader says everyone must obey him because he is in charge. No one chose him.

Ask 1: Town Leader Card. Using the Declaration's idea about power, what is missing?

Ask 2: Town Leader Card. Why does that matter to the people there?
