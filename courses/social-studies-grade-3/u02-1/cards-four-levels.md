# Four levels (day 27)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Outlines, nested

Four paper circles, the largest last: SCHOOL, TOWN, STATE, COUNTRY.

## Leader cards

Principal. Mayor. Governor. President. (The school committee is named here and taught on day 30.)

## Power and duty

A power is what a leader may do. A duty is what a leader must do.

## Need cards

1. A new roof for the Oakridge school.
2. A street light out on Birch Street.
3. A long road that joins towns.
4. Keeping the whole country safe from attack.

Pairs place each need on the circle that looks after it and say why one level cannot do all four.
