# Day 25: Whose rules, and whose needs?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Fill blank-local-record-teaching.md from our town's own official page: maker, title, date, link, why it was made, about 60 words that say one thing the town government does, and the rights line. Word the class's turn-taking rule on a chart. Post the unit 2 question board. Check day 24's readiness answers for oral support. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 25 to 27 (key-exits-25-27.md), out of the room until the exit cards are collected.

### The day

#### 7 min. Hook: our turn-taking rule
Whole class. Hand back day 24's exit slips with one cue. Point to the class turn-taking rule. Review: who made it, when and why (day 18). **Ask.** Who made this rule? Who can change it? Whose needs does it help? Expect: our class or the teacher made it; either can change it; it helps every child who wants to speak. A child may pass. Post the big question: Who should make the rules in our town?

#### 13 min. The town's record
Pairs. Show the Local Government Record. Before reading, say who made it, when and why (review, day 18); where the page says nothing, the answer is "the record does not say." Read the paragraph once. Review: a government is leaders and workers who make and carry out a community's rules (grade 1). Pairs fill the table (blank-day25-table.md): for our class rule and our town's rules, who makes it, who can change it, who it reaches. Seven minutes; visit six pairs, a minute each. Two pairs share. **Ask.** Who does a town's rule reach? Expect: everyone who lives, walks, works or visits there, not only voters. If many write grown-ups only (error D), name a child who walks to school.

#### 9 min. Whose needs count?
Pairs. Oakridge is made up. Its rule: dogs on a leash at Oakridge Pond. Show the six pond cards. Model with the runner: "The runner needs room to pass without a dog in the way." Each pair picks a different card and finishes [[s02-P25a]], aloud, then in writing (4 minutes). Two pairs share. **Ask.** Whose needs should count? Expect: everyone the rule reaches. A liking ("I like dogs," error W) is not a need: ask what the person does on the path.

#### 8 min. What do we wonder?
Review: the wonder card (day 1). Each child writes one wonder about who decides, [[s02-P25b]], 4 minutes; a child may pass or tell a scribe. Read six aloud, grouped. Wonder cards stay in the unit folder.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the quarter slip with the Larkfield Card, first seen here. Read the card and ask once. Every child writes or draws [[s02-E25]]. A scribe takes up to 3 children, 45 seconds each. Mark the record sheet.

**Shared shape.** The pond cards ask what a person needs from a rule. The exit is a new case: why a decision reaches one child.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the table headings on the board; answer on lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class chart: use the rules of a board game. No town page: your adult finds a short paragraph on the town's website.

**Skip.** The pairs and the shared sentences.

- **8 min. A rule we know.** Look at a game rule or a park sign.
  **Say.** "Who made it? Who can change it? Who does it reach?" Expect: someone made it for a reason; the person in charge can change it; it reaches everyone who plays or visits.
- **12 min. The town's rules.** Read a short paragraph from the town's page; fill the table sheet.
- **10 min. Pond cards.** Pick one card. [[s02-P25a]]
  **Say.** "What does this person need?" Expect: a need, not a liking.
- **5 min. Wonder.** [[s02-P25b]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-E25]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fill two table rows; keep the pond cards and the exit. Longer: a second pond card.

### Words and supports
Words: rule, record, reach, need, wonder. Frame: "This person needs ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. No one reports on home or family.

### Tomorrow
Day 26 writes the supporting questions together and teaches why groups make rules. If many wrote "I like buses" (error W) or "only grown-ups who vote care" (error D) on day 25's exit, day 27's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one.
