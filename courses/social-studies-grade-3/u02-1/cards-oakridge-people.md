# Oakridge Pond people cards (day 25)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Six picture cards, cut apart. Oakridge made a rule: dogs must be on a leash at Oakridge Pond.

1. A runner on the pond path.
2. A person pushing a stroller on the pond path.
3. A person using a wheelchair on the pond path.
4. A person walking a dog.
5. A bird watcher sitting by the water.
6. A child learning to ride a bike.

Each pair picks one card and finishes: "A pond rule matters to this person because ___."
