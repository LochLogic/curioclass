# Teacher key, exit cards days 25 to 27

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-25-27.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 25: the route decides how Oskar gets to school, so a rule about it reaches him; a child who rides is affected. Not "I like buses". Not "only voters care" (D).
- Day 26, ask 1: any group the plan reaches, such as people who live on Tanner Street, drivers, shops on the street. Ask 2: a reason tied to the plan, such as cars cannot drive through, noise, or more customers. Not "I like markets" (W).
- Day 26, Park Card: to keep walkers safe; a rule can keep people safe, settle a problem or protect a freedom. Not "rules were always there" (S).
- Day 27: ten towns share the lake, so the need is shared and no one town should test it for all; each level looks after needs of its own size. Not "the mayor does it all" (A).
