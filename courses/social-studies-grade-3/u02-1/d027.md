# Day 27: Why we have governments, and what each level does

## Teacher page

### Before class
Lay out the level outlines and cards: four nested circles (school, town, state, country), leader cards, four need cards. Find Mexico and the United States on the class map. Have the Local Government Record, teaching edition, and the unit 2 question board in view. Write one cue on each day 26 slip. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 25 to 27 (key-exits-25-27.md), out of the room until the exit cards are collected.

### The day

#### 8 min. Return and repair
3 min: show the map; read the slip once (grade 2): [[s02-T27]]. 5 min: hand back day 25's exit slips and day 26's exit slips; teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right. W: "I like buses" beside "Riders need safe routes, so a rule reaches them." D: "Only grown-ups who vote care" beside "A child walks and rides, so the rule reaches children." S: "Rules were always there" beside "A group made this rule for a reason." x: "The park likes being strict" beside "The park wants walkers safe."

#### 13 min. Four levels
Pairs. A level of government is a school, town, state or the country; each looks after needs of its own size, and none is worth more. Review (grade 1): the mayor leads a town, the governor a state, the President the country. The principal leads a school. Review: a duty is a job a member is expected to do. New: a power is what an official may do. Model a made-up Oakridge rule card: "The mayor may choose the day of the town fair. The mayor must tell the town first." Place the town circle and mayor card; pairs place principal, governor and President, then finish [[s02-P27a]] and [[s02-P27b]]. Error A from day 26 is taught here. **Ask.** What is a power, and what is a duty? Expect: a power is what an official may do; a duty is what an official must do.

#### 10 min. What each level does
Pairs. Four made-up need cards: a school roof, a streetlight, a long road that joins towns, keeping the whole country safe. Say that the President leads the armed forces (Source: USA.gov, Branches of the U.S. government). Pairs put each card on its level and write [[s02-P27c]], [[s02-P27d]], [[s02-P27e]], [[s02-P27f]]. **Ask.** Why can one level not do all four? Expect: each looks after needs of its own size; a school roof is not a need of the whole state. Meanwhile up to 5 children who missed the map slip meet the teacher, 45 seconds each.

#### 6 min. The record as a source
Reread the day 25 Local Government Record: who made it, when and why (review, day 18). **Ask.** What does it say our town government does? Expect: what the record names. Write the class's answer to "Why have governments?" on the board; model: one family cannot make rules for a town. Each child finishes [[s02-P27g]]; pages stay in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the quarter slip with the State Card, first seen here. Read it once. Every child writes or draws [[s02-E27]]. A scribe takes up to 3 children, 45 seconds each.

**Shared shape.** The need cards place a need on a level; the exit is a new case.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the circles on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No circles: draw four rings on paper. No map: find Mexico and the United States in an atlas.

**Skip.** The small group and the shared sentences.

- **5 min. Look back.** Find Mexico and the United States. [[s02-T27]]
- **12 min. Four levels.** Place principal, governor and President on the rings. [[s02-P27a]] [[s02-P27b]]
  **Say.** "What does may mean? What does must mean?" Expect: may is a power; must is a duty.
- **10 min. Needs.** [[s02-P27c]] [[s02-P27d]] [[s02-P27e]] [[s02-P27f]]
  **Say.** "Why can one level not do all four?" Expect: each looks after needs of its own size.
- **4 min. Why governments.** [[s02-P27g]]
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-E27]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: [[s02-P27c]] to [[s02-P27e]] only. Longer: a second state need.

### Words and supports
Words: level, power, duty, government. Frame: "The ___ looks after ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 28 names the leaders at each level, the branches and tribal nations as governments. If many made error A on day 27's exit (one official does every job), day 28 sorts office, place and job cards.
