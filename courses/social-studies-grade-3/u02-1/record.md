# Unit 2 record sheet: who decides here

Write each child's name down the left, one row for each child, and keep one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never write a count of reminders.

Columns: the readiness items R1 to R8 (day 24; no score, only the support used); one tick and a note for each exit E25 to E46; the day 47 prompts Q1 to Q8; and the record rows Q9 to Q19, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when it is missing; leave blank when not seen.

Record rows: Q9 (day 34, rights and duties), Q10 (day 36, five settings), Q11 (day 37, comparison chart), Q12 and Q13 (the day 40 slip, with the partner mark and the listening notes, not revised), Q14 (day 39, values record), Q15 (day 41 card), Q16 (day 43 table, the Menominee column), Q17 and Q18 (day 26 record), Q19 (day 44 record). Score from the day 46 revision, except Q12 and Q13.

Days 25 to 27, 33 to 37 and 38 to 40 exits are heard from the day's six, rotating through the class. Day 48's fresh redo is not scored on this sheet; note only what help was used. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell.
