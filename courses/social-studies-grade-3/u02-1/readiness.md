# Unit 2 readiness check: Sam, Class Vote and Sign Team cards

## Student

For the teacher. This is a short check on grade 1 ideas, not a test. It is given on day 24 in the last ten minutes (the cards are read in unit 1's day 24 piece). Read each made-up case aloud once, then each question aloud once. Every child answers on the page by writing a few words or labelling a drawing. A child may answer at the side table. Note on the record sheet what each child did and what help was used. Give no score and no grade. The results guide the five-minute repair on day 26.
