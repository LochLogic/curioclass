# Why groups make rules (day 26)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Three picture cards, one person each.

1. Nia crosses a street. A rule tells cars to stop. The rule keeps ___ safe.
2. Two children want one swing. A rule says take turns. The rule settles a ___.
3. A club member wants to speak. A rule says each member gets a turn. The rule protects a ___ to speak.
