# Electing helpers: the day 26 repair

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Picture sequence: the plant corner

1. BEFORE: Willow Class has a plant corner. Nobody waters the plants. Some leaves are dry.
2. ACTION: The class chooses two helpers. Each child casts one secret ballot. Pia gets 6 ballots. Omar gets 2. Pia is the watering helper.
3. ROLES: Pia waters. Omar draws the labels. Lena checks the labels for mistakes.
4. AFTER: The plants have water and labels. The corner looks good.

Say: What did Pia do? What changed? One quality Pia showed? Who won, and why is a second ballot for one child unfair? How does each role help the shared goal?

## Fresh card to check

Room 3 chooses a helper to set out the chairs. Dani gets 5 ballots. Lou gets 3. Each child cast one ballot.

Ask: Who wins? Is it fair to count one child's ballot twice?
