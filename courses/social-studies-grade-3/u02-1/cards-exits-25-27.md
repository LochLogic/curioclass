# Exit cards, days 25 to 27

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written; some are also heard.

## Day 25: Larkfield Card

Oskar is eight. He rides bus 4 to school in Larkfield. The town council may change bus 4's route next year.

Ask: Why might Oskar care who decides bus 4's route?

## Day 26: Tanner Street Card

Larkfield will hold a Saturday market on Tanner Street. Shoppers will come. The street will be closed to cars.

Ask 1: Name one group, besides shoppers, who might want a say.
Ask 2: Why might that group want a say?

## Day 27: State Card

Ten towns in Larkfield's state share one lake. Each town runs its own beach. The state tests the lake's water.

Ask: Why might the state, not one town, test the lake's water?


## Day 26: Park Card
A park has a path for walkers and bikes. The park made a rule: bikes must stop for walkers.
Ask: Why might the park make this rule?
