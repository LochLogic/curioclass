# Cards for day 129

The teacher reads each card aloud once. Source: National Archives, Bill of Rights transcript (public domain), sources.csv U6D.

## Why a list

The Bill of Rights is the first ten amendments, added to the Constitution after it began. Congress proposed amendments in 1789 and ten were approved on 15 December 1791. Amendment means a change or addition. In their short introduction the members of Congress spoke of extra clauses that would explain and limit what the government could do. 1791 goes on the strip, four years after 1787.

## One right, closely

First Amendment, read aloud: "abridging the freedom of speech, or of the press". Plain words: the government may not stop people from speaking or printing their ideas. Pairs say one thing the clause lets a person do. Each child then answers the speech card on the student page (made up case). Item: [[s06-P129a]]
