# Exit cards, days 128 and 129

Print "made up, for practice" on the invented card. Each is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-128-129.md (teacher only).

## Day 128: Kestrel Isle Card (made up, for practice)

On Kestrel Isle, judges decide whether a person broke a law.

Ask 1: Kestrel Isle Card. Which job is it: makes laws, carries out laws or decides cases?

Ask 2: Kestrel Isle Card. In our national plan, who does that job?

## Day 129: Phrase Card (real, from the Bill of Rights)

First Amendment, 1791. A phrase: "prohibiting the free exercise thereof".

Words to know: exercise here means to practice or live out. Thereof means "of it", here of religion.

Ask 1: Phrase Card. What does this phrase protect?

Ask 2: Phrase Card. Does it set up Congress, list a right or explain independence?
