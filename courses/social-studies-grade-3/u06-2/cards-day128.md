# Cards for day 128

The teacher reads each card aloud once. Sources: National Archives, sources.csv U6C and U6E (public domain); Massachusetts Constitution, U6R.

## 1787 order strip (three cards, in order)

1. May 1787. Delegates met in Philadelphia.
2. They wrote a plan of government. Delegates signed it on 17 September 1787.
3. The states voted on it in their own conventions. Nine of the thirteen had to say yes.

Beside it: the Declaration (1776) gave reasons. The Constitution sets up a government.

## Three crops with job labels

- Article I, first sentence: "All legislative Powers herein granted shall be vested in a Congress of the United States." Job: Congress makes laws.
- Article II, first sentence: "The executive Power shall be vested in a President of the United States of America." Job: a President carries out laws.
- Article III, first sentence: "The judicial Power of the United States, shall be vested in one supreme Court, and in such inferior Courts as the Congress may from time to time ordain and establish." Job: courts decide cases.

The class sorts each into the job baskets from unit 2. The plan splits the work so one person does not do it all.

## Beside our state

The class's state plan from unit 2's chart: Massachusetts has two branches of lawmakers, a Governor and courts. Same three baskets, one for the state and one for the country. A teacher in another state swaps in the class's own chart. Pairs name one way they are alike and one way they differ.
