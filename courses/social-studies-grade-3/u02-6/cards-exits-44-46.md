# Exit cards, days 44 to 46

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written; some are also heard.

## Day 44: Cedarbank Card

The big question: Who should decide the rules for the Cedarbank fishing dock? A supporting answer: most people who use the dock after school are children.

Ask: How does this supporting answer help the big question?

## Day 45: Rule Exit Pair

Card A, Fennmoor Charter: The town council may make and change the town ice rink's rules.

Card B, Meeting Notes: Residents told the meeting the rink door is too narrow for wheelchairs.

Ask: Write one sentence: who decides the rink's rules? Use a detail from each card.

## Day 46: Ferndale Card

In Ferndale, neighbors speak for ten minutes about a new dog park. Then the council votes.

Ask: Who has the final say on the dog park?
