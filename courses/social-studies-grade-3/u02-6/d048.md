# Day 48: Fix it and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class
Sort the children into four groups from the record sheet, at most about 8 each: authority and funding; rights and taking part; deciding; constitutions and claims. Write each child's group on their cue. Copy the four written moves from written-moves-day48.md, one for each group. Cards are new cases, not on day 47.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 47's assessment (the answer sheets), the revised records and the day 40 slip (error G; s02-P40a, s02-P40b, s02-P40c), with their cues. Each child reads the cues and marks the biggest gap.

#### 20 min. Grouped reteach
Meet each group for 5 minutes in turn; the other groups work in pairs from their written move.
Group 1, authority and funding: say who decides, who runs the work and who pays. Then, for error K from day 46's exit (E46), sort speaking and voting on a fresh notice: who tells the council what people need, and who votes.
Group 2, rights and taking part: say a right with its duty; name one way a child can take part.
Group 3, deciding: say the other child's reason back, give your own, then one equal private ballot.
Group 4, constitutions and claims: name a power and a limit on a plan. Then, for error J and error V from day 45's exit (E45), say a claim with a different detail from each of two records, not a title and not a liking. Five minutes.
A child who missed most items: the adult goes over the missed items together instead, and redoes one aloud.

#### 5 min. Fresh redo
Each child answers the card for their group on the student page, alone, and hands it in. Group 1: [[s02-F48a]], [[s02-F48b]]. Group 2: [[s02-F48c]], [[s02-F48d]]. Group 3: [[s02-F48e]], [[s02-F48f]]. Group 4: [[s02-F48g]], [[s02-F48h]]. Read each card once. This is a redo, not a new exit; it is judged, handed back and not scored on the record sheet. A child who needs it speaks to an adult at the side table.

#### 10 min. Unit 3's readiness check
Two cards, read aloud, 5 minutes each. Jo Card: [[s02-R9]], [[s02-R9b]], [[s02-R9c]]. Ren Card: [[s02-R10]], [[s02-R10b]], [[s02-R10c]]. Both cards are made up. Record each gap separately on the record sheet before day 49. Day 50 takes the gaps.

**Shared shape.** The four redo cards share the shape of the group each follows; each is a new case, not on day 47. Review: you learned choices and saving in grade 1; the Jo and Ren cards check them, and the readiness check on grade 1 choices and saving is not new teaching.

### Other rooms
No printer: write each card on the board; children answer on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. Skip the groups; go over the missed items together.

**Skip.** The record sheet.

- **5 min. Cues.** **Say.** "Which cue helps most?" Expect: one named gap.
- **20 min. Fix it.** Go over the missed items together. Say each answer in your own words.
- **5 min. Redo.** Read one redo card aloud. Your child answers alone.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Read the Jo and Ren cards once. Your child answers each ask.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one card, not two. Longer: write a new card for a made-up club or town.

### Words and supports
Words: gap, redo, power, limit, ballot, save. Frame: "I changed my answer because ___." Pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are all answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 49 opens unit 3 with what our school needs each day. The readiness gaps from today guide who sits nearest the teacher; day 50 repairs them.

### Credit
Ours. The redo and readiness cards are invented for practice.
