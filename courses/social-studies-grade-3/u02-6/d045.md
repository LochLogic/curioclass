# Day 45: Our claim, in a sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class
Copy the Brindle bridge name card and the day 45 claim strip from cards-day44-45.md and blank-day45-claim.md. Have the Local Government Record, class edition, and the Class Decision Record in each pair's folder. Keep the teacher key (key-exits-44-46.md) away until slips are collected. Take the frame card down during the second part.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 44's exit slips. Teach the more-missed of error W and error V, wrong then right, whole class. Error W: "The dock has many boards" beside "Children use it, so their needs should be heard." Error V: "I think the mayor, because I like him" beside "The council decides; the Council Page shows its power." Ask who the rule touches, then how the answer helps the big question.

#### 4 min. The frame, then it comes down
Brindle is made up. Read the Brindle bridge name card once: "The Brindle Council Page says the council may name the town's bridges." Say: "I think the council picks the new bridge name because the Council Page shows it may name bridges." Read the frame card once, then take it down. Children keep the pencil-tip copy on their claim strip.

#### 8 min. Say it first
Each child says a claim to a partner using the Local Government Record and the Class Decision Record, one minute each. Then once with a new partner, one minute each. Listen to a rotating six and note what each child's claim names.

#### 10 min. Write it
Children write one sentence on the claim strip: [[s02-P45a]], [[s02-P45b]] and [[s02-P45c]]. Spoken or dictated is allowed. Sit at the side table for up to 4 children who need a scribe, about 2 minutes each; the others write alone. The strip is kept.

#### 7 min. Share and revise
Groups of four, norms card in hand (listen, say it back, give a reason; you learned it on day 38). Each child reads a claim and the listener says back the detail, one minute a turn, 4 minutes. Then each child changes one word or detail they liked in another claim, 3 minutes.

#### 3 min. Our answer
Write the class answer under the big question on the board: the people allowed to decide have the **final say**, the person or group that makes the decision. People telling leaders what they think and need is **public voice**. Speaking is not voting. Write two supported balances side by side, each with a detail from the records. Close the question board.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the quarter slip with the Rule Exit Pair, first seen here. Read both cards and the ask once. Children write [[s02-E45]].

**Shared shape.** The Brindle model gives one card and a claim from it; the exit gives two cards and asks for one sentence with a detail from each. The exit is a new pair: the Rule Exit Pair.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the claim strip on the board; children copy it.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. No folder: use two papers about your town.

**Skip.** The side table, the groups of four.

- **5 min. Read.** Read two papers aloud, slowly.
- **8 min. Say it.** **Say.** "Who decides?" Expect: a claim with a detail from each paper. Switch.
- **10 min. Write it.** [[s02-P45a]] [[s02-P45b]] [[s02-P45c]]
- **7 min. Share.** Say back one detail. Change one word.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read both cards and the ask once. [[s02-E45]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one detail, not two. Longer: write a second claim that balances the first.

### Words and supports
Words: claim, record, detail, final say, public voice. Frame: "I think ___ because the Local record shows ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 46 reads written cues and revises eight records, then repairs the more-missed of error J (two titles, no details) and error V. If many made J or V on day 45's exit, that repair sets the wrong beside a right answer, with a fresh detail.

### Credit
Ours. The Brindle card and the Rule Exit Pair are invented for practice. The records are the unit's own.
