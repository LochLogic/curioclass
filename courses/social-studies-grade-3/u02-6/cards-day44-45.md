# Day 44 and 45 cards

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on the model cards.

## The claim frame card (up on day 44, taken down on day 45)

I think ___ because the ___ shows ___, which means ___.

On day 45 the pencil-tip copy: I think ___ because the Local record shows ___ and the Class record shows ___.

## Day 44 oral model: Brindle's band shell

Question: Who should decide the band shell rules? Card 1: The Brindle Council Page says the council may make and change the band shell rules. Card 2: Notes say children use the band shell on Saturdays. The teacher says a claim aloud in the frame.

## Day 45 model: Brindle's bridge name

Question: Who picks the new bridge name? Card 1: The Brindle Charter says the council may name bridges. Card 2: Notes say neighbors asked for a name that honors the creek. The teacher says one more claim, then the frame comes down.

## Words

Link: how a supporting answer helps the big question. People a rule touches: the people who use it or follow it. Final say: the person or group that makes the decision. Public voice: people telling leaders what they think.
