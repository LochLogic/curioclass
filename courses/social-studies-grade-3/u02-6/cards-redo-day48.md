# Fresh redo cards, day 48

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. One item for each group. Not scored on the record sheet.

**Wexley Card.** The council decided to charge a small fee to use the roller rink. The mayor's staff open it each morning. Ask: Who decided to charge the fee? Who pays it?

**Garnet Club Card.** A club says only members over twelve may speak at meetings. Ask: Does this protect everyone's freedom to speak? What could change?

**Class Pet Card.** Two children disagree about a class pet's name. One says the other's idea is silly. Ask: What next? How should the class decide?

**Club Plan Card.** A made-up club plan says: Members may speak at meetings. The president may not spend over ten dollars alone. Ask: Name one power. Name one limit.
