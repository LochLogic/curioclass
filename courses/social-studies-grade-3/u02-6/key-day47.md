# Teacher key, day 47

Teacher only. Never given to children.

- Q1: to make shared rules, run services and meet needs no one family can meet. Q1b to Q1d: the body, the official and the way on the Local record page (sample: Town Council; Town Manager; submit public comments). Not "every official has the same job" (A); not "only voters" (D).
- Q2 to Q2d: council, mayor, courts; mayor town, governor state, President country.
- Q3: property tax for supplies; Q3b: the water fee for pipes; Q3c: the government collects, then spends. Not "free" (C); not "gifts" (L). No arithmetic is scored.
- Q4: the council; carts blocked the footpath; it keeps the path open for everyone. Not S.
- Q5: leaders need to hear what people need; a child may learn, ask, or speak at public comment with an adult. Not Y, not D.
- Q6, Q6b: the year and the right on the State card. Not R, not M.
- Q7: a part and job on the State card. Q7b: delegates, 1787, the states ratified. Not AD.
- Q8: a claim with one accurate detail; Q8b: a different accurate detail from each record, linked. Not V, not J.
- Records: Q9 to Q19 are scored 2, 1 or 0 on each look-for from the revision, or from the day 40 slip for Q12 and Q13 (listening notes for Q13).
