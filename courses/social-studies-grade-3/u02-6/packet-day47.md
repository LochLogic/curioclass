# Day 47 packet

Teacher reads the Local record, the State card and the Class record aloud once, then each prompt aloud once at its start. Pages are each under 350 words. Printed "made up, for practice" on the Town model, Funding model and Rule record. No keys on any page.

## Page 1: Local record (assessment edition)

Fill from our town's own official page that was not taught. Print the maker, the page, the date read and the link. Sample from a real town, Amherst (replace it with our town). The Town Council has 13 councilors. The Council appoints the Town Manager. The Manager is the chief executive officer. People may submit public comments to the Town Council.

## Page 2: State card (assessment edition)

Our state's own official page, a short clause and an adoption note, in plain words. Default (Massachusetts): voters approved the plan in 1780. It sets up lawmakers, a governor and judges. Article XIV of the Declaration of Rights protects people from unreasonable searches and seizures. Teacher: if our state is not Massachusetts, replace it from our state's page.

## Page 3: Class record

The Class Decision Record from day 40, with no names.

## Page 4: Town model (made up, for practice)

Thistle Town is made up. The council makes the town's laws. The mayor carries out the laws. Courts decide cases. The state has a governor. The country has a President.

## Page 5: Funding model (made up, for practice)

Harborbrook is made up. Property-tax money buys school supplies. The water fee pays for fixing pipes. The town collects the money and then spends it.

## Page 6: Rule record (made up, for practice)

Harborbrook Footpath Law. Made by the town council. Carts were blocking the footpath. The law keeps the footpath open for everyone.
