# Written moves, day 48

One for each of four groups. Letters first seen on day 47 are used here.

1. **Authority and funding** (A, C, L, K): the adult says who decides, who runs the work, who pays; one fresh sort of speaking and voting on a notice.
2. **Rights and taking part** (D, M, S, Y): say a right with its duty, and one way a child can take part.
3. **Deciding** (G, Q, AC, F, O, AA): say it back, give a reason, one equal private ballot.
4. **Constitutions and claims** (H, AB, R, AD, J, V): a power and a limit on a plan; a claim with a different detail from each of two records.

One child's moves: the adult goes over the missed items together and redoes one orally.
