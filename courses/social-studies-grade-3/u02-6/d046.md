# Day 46: Back to our records

## Teacher page

### Before class
Write one cue on each child's eight records and on the claim strip from day 45 on the cue sheet, records: present, partly or missing against each record's look-fors. Put it in each folder: about 2 minutes a child, about 60 minutes for 30 children, after day 45 and outside the 40 minutes; the cue sheet is untested. Keep the teacher key (key-exits-44-46.md) away until slips are in. The day 40 work is not revised: its cues are on the cue sheet, days 39 and 40.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the written cues
Hand back day 45's exit slips, the claim strip from day 45 (error J; s02-P45a, s02-P45b, s02-P45c) and the folders. Each child reads the cues and stars the records that need work first on the revision list.

#### 25 min. Revise the records
Independent work: eight records, about 3 minutes each, starred ones first. A record with every look-for present is checked, not rewritten. No new content. Sit at the side table for up to 6 children who need a scribe or an item read aloud; the others work alone. Revisions are scored on day 47. Handbacks go here: the day 26 record (error W), the day 34 record (error M), the day 36 record (error Z), the day 37 record (error P), the day 39 record (error Q), the day 41 card (error AD) and the day 43 table (error I), the day 44 record (error W; s02-P44a, s02-P44b, s02-P44c).

#### 8 min. Retrieval and repair
Five minutes, whole class: teach the more-missed of error J and error V from day 45's exit, wrong then right. Error J: "Card A and Card B say it" beside "Card A gives the council power; Card B shows a need." Error V: "I think the mayor, because I like him" beside "The council decides; Card A shows its power." Three minutes, pairs: say the four level cards (school, town, state, country), one rule's story from the day 33 rule and one way to take part from the day 37 board answer, half a minute each, then switch.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the quarter slip with the Ferndale Card, first seen here. Read the card and the ask once. Children write [[s02-E46]].

**Shared shape.** The retrieval cards name a level, a rule and a way to take part; the exit gives a made-up case and asks who has the final say. The exit is a new case: the Ferndale Card.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw four circles on the board for the levels; children point as they say each.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. No cues: reread each record and add one thing.

**Skip.** The side table.

- **5 min. Read.** Read each record aloud and star the ones to fix first.
- **25 min. Revise.** Change each starred record. A good record stays as it is.
- **5 min. Say it.** **Say.** "Name the four levels." Expect: school, town, state, country. Then one way to take part.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and ask once. [[s02-E46]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: revise the starred records only. Longer: say what changed in each record and why.

### Words and supports
Words: record, cue, revise, final say, public voice. Frame: "I changed ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read any item aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 47 is the end-of-unit check: eight prompts from a packet of town sources you have not seen, read aloud once, and your revised records. Day 48 repairs error K (speaking is not voting) in its first group if many made it on today's exit.

### Credit
Ours. The Ferndale Card is invented for practice. The records are the unit's own.
