# Section 2.6 opener: our answer to "Who should make the rules in our town?"

## What the section does

Five days of 40 minutes. Day 44 gathers evidence: pairs choose two records from the unit's folder that answer a supporting question, and each child fills the inquiry record (a supporting question, a record and its answer, and the link). Day 45 writes and shares one claim on the frame, naming the Local Government Record and the Class Decision Record with a detail from each. Day 46 revises eight records after written cues. Day 47 is the end-of-unit assessment. Day 48 responds with four grouped reteaches and a fresh redo, then gives unit 3's readiness check.

## How the days run

Day 44 and 45 open with a five-minute return and repair; the exits are in cards-exits-44-46.md. Day 46 reads the cues, revises records for twenty-five minutes (a record with all looks present is checked, not rewritten), then a retrieval and repair, then an exit. Day 47: the packet (5 minutes), eight unaided prompts (30 minutes), reread and hand in (5 minutes). Day 48: feedback (5), grouped reteach (20), fresh redo (5), unit 3's readiness check (10).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A supporting answer is not the final answer.** It earns its place by helping: a link.
- **A title is not evidence. A detail is (J).** "Card A and Card B say it" names two titles and no details.
- **Final say belongs to those who are allowed to decide. Public voice tells them what people need.** Speaking is not voting (K).
- **Elected leaders, a direct vote or a supported balance can all be defended.** The claim needs a detail, not a liking (V). No party preference. No real officeholder is judged.

## Marking day 47

Items Q1 to Q8 are marked on the key (key-day47.md): shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Eleven records (days 26, 34, 36, 37, 39, 40, 41, 43, 44) are scored from the revision and the day 40 slip by part on one scale: 2 (look-for present), 1 (partly), 0 (missing). Nothing is self-marked. Plan about three minutes a child after class; about ninety minutes in all (ours, untested). Written advice cannot stand in for the day 40 retry.

## The errors in plain words

W is an answer that does not link. V is a liking in place of a claim. J is two titles with no details. K is speaking taken as voting. AD, T, I and U come from section 2.5 and are repaired on day 44. Wrong ideas are read from "someone might say" cards, never from a named child.

## Supplies for the section

The frame card, the question board answers, the unit folder of records, the day 44 record, the claim strip, the cue sheet, the level cards, the day 47 packet and key, the score sheet, the four written moves, the redo cards, exit cards, the record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Chart paper for the board, index cards, a folder for each child. The teacher reads every record and prompt aloud once.
