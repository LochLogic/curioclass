# Teacher key, exit cards days 44 to 46

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-44-46.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 44: children use the dock, so their needs should be heard by the deciders. Not "the dock has many boards" (W); not "I like it, so children should decide" (V).
- Day 45: the council decides (Card A gives it the power) after hearing residents; Card B shows a need, the door is too narrow for wheelchairs. Not "Card A and Card B say it" (J); not "the mayor, because I like him" (V).
- Day 46: the council. Neighbors speak to it. Not "everyone who spoke" (K).
