# Day 47: Unit 2 check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Make the packet from packet-day47.md. Fill page 1 from our town's own official page and page 2 from our state's page: print the maker, page, date read and link. Check each passage against the key. Keep the teacher key (key-day47.md) in your folder. Nothing from days 44 to 46 stays on the wall.

### The day

#### 5 min. The packet
Hand out the packets and answer sheets. Say: answer alone, pass any prompt you like, no one helps, each prompt is read once. Read the Local record, the State card and the class record page aloud once. Thistle Town, Harborbrook and the footpath law are made up and say so.

#### 30 min. Eight unaided prompts
Read each prompt once at its start, with a spoken pace cue at each change and no clock shown.

They are judged from records: [[s02-Q9]], [[s02-Q10]], [[s02-Q11]], [[s02-Q14]], [[s02-Q15]], [[s02-Q16]], [[s02-Q17]], [[s02-Q18]], [[s02-Q19]] from the revised records; [[s02-Q12]] and [[s02-Q13]] from the day 40 slip, which is not revised.

#### 5 min. Reread and hand in
Collect the packets and answer sheets: children finish any prompt, reread, and hand in both with a name on each. Nothing is self-marked.

### Marking
After class, mark every item and every record look-for 2, 1 or 0 on the day 47 score sheet, using the teacher key; spelling and handwriting are not scored. About 3 minutes a child, about ninety minutes in all (ours, untested). Record results by part on the unit 2 record sheet. Day 48 hands back the cues from the cue sheet, records.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the packet pages by hand on paper; keep each page short.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. The class record is your home's own two-person decision: write the date, the question, what you chose and why.

**Skip.** The sheets for the teacher.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Read each packet page aloud once, then each prompt once. Write your child's answer word for word. Do not help. **Say.** "Who should make our town's rules?" Expect: a claim with a detail from each record.
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: none; every part is asked once. Longer: your child tells one thing they would change in a record.

### Words and supports
Words: government, council, tax, fee, rule, right, constitution, claim. Frame: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." Read aloud, enlarge type; pointing, speech and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 48 hands back cues on the packet and the revised records, reteaches in four groups, and gives a fresh redo, then unit 3's readiness check. If many made error K on day 46's exit, the first group repairs it.

### Credit
Ours. The Town model, Funding model and Rule record are made up, for practice. The Local record and the State card come from official pages, credited on each page.
