# Class idea, ballot rules and slips (days 38 to 40)

## Idea slip (day 38)

One small choice our class could make together. Safe to carry out. Nothing about anyone's home.

My idea: ______________________________________________

## Screened idea card (day 39)

Four ideas chosen by the teacher from the box. The class drops one counter each in a cup for the idea to decide. The top idea becomes the question with two options. The rest go on a later list.

Idea 1: __________  Idea 2: __________  Idea 3: __________  Idea 4: __________

## The ballot rules card (day 39, used on day 40)

1. One ballot each.
2. The same rules for everyone.
3. The ballot is private.
4. A tie means we talk more, then a runoff.

## Leaning slip (day 39)

Mark one: A   B   not sure. Reason card: write one reason, no name.
