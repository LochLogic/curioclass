# Teacher key, exit cards days 38 to 40

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-38-40.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 38, ask 1: Ben wants time to finish reading. Not "I agree with Ana" (G); not "Ben is boring" (Q); not "silly" (AC). Ask 2: any reason with because that does not insult.
- Day 39, ask 1: she tripped on a bag. Ask 2: safety. Ask 3: any need such as a crowded door; not "a classmate who disagrees does not care about safety" (Q).
- Day 40, ask 1: no, one ballot each, counted once; not "fair because Hugo is popular" (O) or "his friends asked the loudest" (F). Ask 2: count Hugo's ballot once.
