# Section 2.4 opener: talking a choice through, then deciding fairly

## What the section does

Three days of 40 minutes. Day 38 teaches three habits for talking a choice through: listen, say it back (restate), give a reason. Children also learn two words for a view: experience (something that happened to you in our class or school) and value (something that matters to you). Day 39 holds a real, small, safe class choice: children choose the question, mark a private leaning, talk it through in groups of six and fill a values record. Day 40 hears the less-supported view first, then the class decides by one equal private ballot and writes a Class Decision Record.

## How the days run

Each day opens with a five-minute return and repair for the most-missed letter of the day before, then teaches or applies, then ends with an exit card (cards-exits-38-40.md). Day 39's values record is practice, kept for written feedback and revised on day 46. Day 40's slip is the scored retry for D2.Civ.7 and D2.Civ.9. It is not revised.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Restating shows you listened. It does not mean you agree.** "I hear you say you want longer recess to play. I think recess should stay."
- **A different view comes from a different experience or value, not a worse person.**
- **Hearing the less-supported view first does not reveal anyone's ballot.** Count the marks, say which option had fewer, with no names.
- **An equal ballot is one each, counted once, whoever the child is.** A tie is a reason to talk more, not to give anyone two ballots.
- **The choice is real, small and safe to carry out** (a Friday game, a reading time, a plant's name). The class chooses it. Ideas that touch anyone's rights, identity, access or punishment are set aside kindly. An experience is something that happened in our class or school; no one tells about home.

## How to hear thirty children

One adult cannot hear thirty. Each child gives a written slip and a partner's mark; the teacher listens to a rotating six each day (day 38 six, day 39 six different, day 40 six different). Where there is no slip and no listening, write "not yet seen"; never guess. Written cues on the values record say present, partly or missing against four look-fors: (1) my view with its reason; (2) an experience from our class or school, or a value; (3) another child's reason, said right; (4) no judging the person.

## The errors in plain words

G is refusing to restate because you disagree. Q is taking a different view to mean a bad person. AC is mocking a view. AA is voting at once with no reasons heard. F is letting the loudest voice count. O is giving extra ballots to a popular child. P and K come from day 37 and day 32 and are repaired on day 38. Wrong ideas are read from "someone might say" cards, never from a named child.

## A room with nothing

Scrap paper slips, a box, counters or blocks, chart paper for the cards. A child may pass in talk. Signing, pointing to a reason card and a communication device count as taking part. Being quiet is not failing.
