# Exit cards, days 38 to 40

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written; some are also heard.

## Day 38: Practice Pair Card

Ana says, "Recess should be longer so we can play." Ben says, "Keep it the same so we finish our reading."

Ask 1: Say Ben's reason in your own words.
Ask 2: Give your own reason about recess. Use because.

## Day 39: Book Box Card

Zoe wants the class book box moved near the door. She says, "Last week I tripped on a bag walking to it. I want everyone to reach books safely."

Ask 1: What happened to Zoe?
Ask 2: What does Zoe care about?
Ask 3: Give one reason a classmate might want a different place.

## Day 40: Room 9 Card

Room 9 voted on a Friday game. Hugo's friends asked the teacher to count his ballot three times because he is popular. The teacher did.

Ask 1: Was the vote fair?
Ask 2 (about our own class vote): Why are the reasons with fewer marks heard first?
