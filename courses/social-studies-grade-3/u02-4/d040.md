# Day 40: Hear every view, then decide fairly

## Teacher page

### Before class
Sort day 39's leaning slips and reason cards into A and B piles; note which option had fewer, with no names. Print the ballot slips and the Class Decision Record chart. Cover the ballot box. Keep the teacher key (key-exits-38-40.md) away until slips are collected. Today's listening six: a group not heard on day 38 or 39.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback and repair
Hand back day 39's exit slips and written task, the values record. Two minutes: each child reads their written cue and picks one thing to do better; the record is revised on day 46. Three minutes: teach the most-missed of error Q, error AC and error AA, wrong then right. Error Q: "Sam is not a nice person" beside "Sam values playing outdoors." Error AC: "That idea is silly" beside "I hear a different reason. Here is mine." Error AA: "We voted at once" beside "We heard a reason from each side, then voted."

#### 10 min. Hear the less-supported view first
Say which option had fewer marks, with no names. Read that option's reason cards aloud, about 2 minutes. In groups of six each child says one of those reasons back before giving their own, about 45 seconds each, 5 minutes. Sit with your listening six. Then up to 6 volunteers, 30 seconds each, say one reason they had not thought of. Others pass.

#### 10 min. Talk it through
Groups of six, reason cards on the desks. Speak in turn; point or sign. Eight minutes of talk, then 2 minutes of private marks: "My view held" or "My view changed."

#### 5 min. The private ballot
Review (grade 1): a secret ballot is marked in private, dropped in a covered box, and two counters count. Read the ballot rules card: one ballot each, the same rules for all, private, a tie means talk more, then a runoff. Children mark one slip and drop it in the covered box in two lines, about 6 seconds each, 3 minutes. Two counters read the marks aloud; you record, 2 minutes. A tie takes 1 minute of talk and a runoff, and the record is written in 3 minutes.

#### 8 min. Reflect and record
Three minutes: review (grade 1): a decision record names the date, problem, choices, how we decided, result and reason. Write the Class Decision Record with the class: date, question, options, result, one reason for each side, a continuing concern, what we will do. Post the answer to "How can we decide fairly?" on the unit 2 question board. Five minutes: each child fills [[s02-P40a]], [[s02-P40b]] and [[s02-P40c]] on the slip; the partner marks the third. The slips go in the folder, not revised.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Room 9 Card, first seen here. Read the card and the two asks once. Children write [[s02-E40]] and [[s02-E40b]].

**Shared shape.** The ballot rules and the class vote share one rule with the exit, one ballot each counted once; the exit is a made-up room where it is broken.

### Other rooms
No box: fold slips into a hat or a bag. No printer: copy the slip lines from the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the group. No box: use a cup and two folded papers.

**Skip.** The covered box, the groups of six, the chart.

- **12 min. Hear the other side first.** Pick a small choice with two options. State the reason for the option your child likes less. **Say.** "I heard you say ___ because ___." Expect: your child says that reason back, then gives their own.
- **6 min. Decide.** Each marks one folded slip in private. Count each once.
- **12 min. The slip.** [[s02-P40a]] [[s02-P40b]] [[s02-P40c]] You are the partner.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and asks once. [[s02-E40]] [[s02-E40b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: skip the private marks. Longer: a runoff.

### Words and supports
Words: ballot, tie, runoff, restate. Frame: "I heard you say ___ because ___." Pointing and a scribe are answers.
