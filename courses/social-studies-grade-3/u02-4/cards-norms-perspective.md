# Norms, perspective and the practice choice (day 38)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every case card.

## The norms card (kept on the wall)

1. Listen.
2. Say it back: "I hear you say ___ because ___."
3. Give a reason.

## Tumbleford Room 7 (teacher model)

Room 7 is made up. Mo wants more story time. Eli wants more art. The teacher models listening, saying it back without agreeing, and giving a reason.

## The three-column card

| | Mo | Eli |
|---|---|---|
| View (what I want, and why) | More story time, to hear the ending. | (the class fills this in) |
| Experience (what happened in our class) | Story time stopped in the middle of a chapter. | |
| Value (what matters to me) | Hearing a whole story. | |

## The practice choice card (threes)

Three roles: speaker, listener who says it back, checker with the norms card. Choice: which song to start our Friday with, a slow song or a fast song. Three rounds of 2 minutes with a minute to switch.
