# Day 38: Listen, say it back, give a reason

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the norms card, three-column perspective card, Tumbleford Room 7 card, retrieval slips, day 38 and choice cards. Keep the teacher key (key-exits-38-40.md) away until slips are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 37's exit slips. Teach the more-missed of error P and error K, wrong then right. Error P: "A bench vote is an election" beside "An election picks a person; a bench vote decides a matter." Error K: "Everyone at the meeting also votes" beside "They speak; the council votes." The other letter goes to the side table.

#### 3 min. Retrieval
Review (grade 1): a problem is a place that needs fixing, never a child. Read the slip once; children write [[s02-T38]] and [[s02-T38b]]. A miss goes to the side table.

#### 9 min. Teaching first
Say Tumbleford Room 7 is made up: Mo wants more story time, Eli wants more art. Review (kindergarten): listen to the speaker, wait for a turn; the class turn-taking rule chart is on the wall. Show the norms card: listen, say it back, give a reason. Take your turn first. Say Mo back: "I hear you say you want more story time because you want the ending." Then give a reason of your own. **Ask.** Did I say I agree with Mo? Expect: no, I said what Mo said. Restate means to say another's view back fairly, without agreeing or arguing. Children try it on a neighbor.

#### 4 min. Words for a view
Review (grade 1): perspective is what makes sense to the people who live it. Two new words: an experience is something that happened to you in our class or school; a value is something that matters to you. Fill Mo's column of the three-column perspective card: story time stopped mid-chapter; hearing a whole story. **Ask.** What matters to Eli? Expect: making things.

#### 10 min. Practice in threes
Ten threes: speaker, listener who says it back, checker with the norms card. Choice: a slow or a fast song to start Friday. Three rounds of 2 minutes, a minute to switch, so each child holds each role. Any child may pass. Side table, 5 minutes: the retrieval-miss group, up to 5, one minute each to say a fresh problem back. Next 5: the second-letter group, up to 5, on a fresh notice. They are your listening six; with no group, listen to six threes.

#### 4 min. Ideas for a class choice
Each child writes [[s02-P38a]] on a scrap slip; pairs put one in the box. An idea is small, safe and about the class. Screen the box tonight.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Practice Pair Card, first seen here. Read it once. Children write [[s02-E38]] and [[s02-E38b]].

#### 2 min. Close the board
Add "How can we decide fairly?" to the unit 2 board. Say tomorrow we choose one idea.

**Shared shape.** Practice has three children share a choice aloud; the exit puts two views on a card and asks a written restate and a reason.

### Other rooms
No printer: use the board and scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No second child: you are the partner. No wall: set the norms card on the table.

**Skip.** The threes, the idea box.

- **3 min. Retrieval.** Read the slip. [[s02-T38]] [[s02-T38b]]
- **10 min. Listen, say it back, give a reason.** Read Mo and Eli. Take a turn and say Mo back, without agreeing.
  **Say.** "I hear you say ___ because ___." Expect: the child says your view back fairly.
- **12 min. Words for a view.** Your child fills Eli's column: view, experience, value.
- **5 min. An idea.** [[s02-P38a]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and asks once. [[s02-E38]] [[s02-E38b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: two rounds in threes. Longer: a second choice card.

### Words and supports
Words: restate, experience, value. Frame: "I hear you say ___ because ___." Nobody tells about home.

### Tomorrow
Day 39 holds a real class discussion and names the experiences and values behind each view. Its Return and repair sets the most-missed of day 38's exit beside a right answer: error G (will not say it back), error Q (a different view means a bad person) or error AC (mocking). The slips stay in the folder.
