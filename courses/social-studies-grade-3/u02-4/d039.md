# Day 39: Talk a real choice through

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the screened idea card (four ideas from the box), ballot rules card, leaning slips and reason cards. Keep the teacher key (key-exits-38-40.md) away until slips are collected. Today's listening six: a group not heard on day 38.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 38's exit slips. Teach the most-missed of error G, error Q and error AC, wrong then right. Error G: "I won't say it back, I disagree" beside "I hear you say you want longer recess to play. I think recess should stay." Error Q: "Sam is not a nice person" beside "Sam values playing outdoors." Error AC: "That idea is silly" beside "I hear a different reason. Here is mine." If it was G, five more minutes come in the discussion.

#### 4 min. Choose the question
Read the screened idea card. Each child drops one counter in the cup for the idea to decide, in two lines (about 5 seconds each). Read the totals. The top idea becomes the question with two options, A and B.

#### 4 min. Norms and ballot rules
Read the norms card together. Review (grade 1): a ballot is a paper where one person marks a choice; a secret ballot is marked in private. Read the ballot rules card: one ballot each, the same rules for all, private, a tie means talk more, then a runoff.

#### 5 min. First leaning, private
Each child marks a leaning slip, A, B or not sure, and writes one reason on a card with no name. Nobody sees another child's slip. After class sort the reason cards into A and B piles.

#### 12 min. Talk it through
Five groups of six, a norms card on each desk. Each child gives a view and a reason, then the next child says it back before giving their own, about a minute each, 6 minutes. Then 4 minutes of open talk, and 2 minutes to stop and share one reason you had not thought of. Sit with your listening six; if G was most missed, they restate first for 5 minutes. Any child may pass, point or sign.

#### 7 min. The values record
Review (day 38): an experience is something that happened to you in our class or school; a value is something that matters to you. Children fill [[s02-P39a]], [[s02-P39b]], [[s02-P39c]] and [[s02-P39d]]. Five minutes writing; two minutes a partner marks "my reason was said right: yes or not yet". Nothing from home. After class write one cue for each child on the cue sheet: listening, saying it back, reasons, one ballot.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Book Box Card, first seen here. Read the card and the first two asks once. Children write [[s02-E39]] and [[s02-E39b]]. Then read the third ask once; children write [[s02-E39c]] in the Exit box on the student page.

**Shared shape.** The record is the child's own view; the exit is a made-up card.

### Other rooms
No counters: use paper slips. No printer: copy the four boxes from the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the group. No counters: use two coins.

**Skip.** The cup, the groups of six.

- **6 min. Choose and lean.** Pick one small choice at the table, with two options. Each of you marks A or B and says one reason.
  **Say.** "I hear you say ___ because ___." Expect: your child says your reason back before giving their own.
- **12 min. Talk it through.** Take turns for a view, a reason and a say-it-back.
- **12 min. The values record.** [[s02-P39a]] [[s02-P39b]] [[s02-P39c]] [[s02-P39d]] Nothing from home.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and asks once. [[s02-E39]] [[s02-E39b]] [[s02-E39c]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: skip the open talk. Longer: a second choice.

### Words and supports
Words: experience, value, restate, ballot. Frames: "My view is ___ because ___." "I care about ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 40 hears the less-supported view first, then the class decides by one private ballot. If many made error Q on day 39's exit (a classmate who disagrees does not care about safety), day 40's Return and repair sets it beside a right answer; error AC and error AA come from the groups. Written cues come back on day 40.
