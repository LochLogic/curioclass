## The Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin (credited original summary)

The Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin is a nation that governs itself today. It has a written constitution. The constitution has an article on a Tribal Legislature. It has another on a Tribal Judiciary. It also has a part on keeping powers separate. The constitution has been amended. This is a different plan from a state's. Credit: the Tribe's own pages and the National Indian Law Library's index of its constitution. Summaries only; the Tribe keeps its copyright.

Pairs say how the plan sets up its parts, in its own way.
