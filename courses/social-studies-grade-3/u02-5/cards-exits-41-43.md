# Exit cards, days 41 to 43

Print "invented for practice" in small letters on the day 41 card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written; some are also heard.

## Day 41: Council Card

A made-up council says, "Nothing can stop us."

Ask: Why does a constitution matter for a government?

## Day 42: State Rights Exit Card

An unused short clause from our state's official constitution page, in 20 to 30 words, different from the clause taught. Default (Massachusetts): A person who is wronged should be able to use the laws for a fair fix.

Ask 1: What right does the clause protect?
Ask 2: Do you get this right only if you follow every rule?
Ask 3: Did every state write its plan in 1780? Say how you know.

## Day 43: Menominee Card

The Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin has its own court system today. It has a lower court and a supreme court. Credit: the Tribe's own pages.

Ask 1: Must the Menominee plan copy a state's three parts?
Ask 2: What does the card show about the Menominee courts?
