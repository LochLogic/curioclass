# Teacher key, exit cards days 41 to 43

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-41-43.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 41: it sets up the government and says what it may and may not do, so it limits the council. Not "a list of rules for kids" (H); not "the council can do anything because people chose it" (AB).
- Day 42, ask 1: the right on the overlay clause (default: a fair fix through the laws). Ask 2: no, it belongs to everyone (M). Ask 3: no, each state wrote its own plan in its own year, for example Massachusetts 1780 and Colorado 1876 (R).
- Day 43, ask 1: no. The card shows the nation's own arrangement. Not "yes, every government has the same three parts" (U). Ask 2: alike, a lower and a supreme court; different, the courts are the nation's own, today. Not "a government long ago" (T); not "part of the town" (I). Ask 3: the Mayor, who puts the rules to work. Not the Council (x).
