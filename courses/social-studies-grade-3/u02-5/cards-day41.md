# Day 41 cards

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on the Willowmere card.

## Willowmere's plan (teacher model, three lines)

1. It sets up a council and a mayor.
2. It says what the council may do and what it may not do.
3. It lists one right.

Beside it: our class rule list has rules, but no parts and no limits.

## Someone might say (H)

"Our class rule list is a constitution."

## The speech safeguard strip

Three cards in order.

1. Congress proposed it.
2. The states approved it.
3. It became part of the Constitution.

The speech line says Congress may not make a law that cuts off the freedom of speech. Sources: National Archives, the Bill of Rights and the Constitution transcript.
