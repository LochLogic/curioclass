# Exit cards, days 100 to 104

Print "made up, for practice" on invented cards. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. Answers are spoken, pointed or written.

## Day 100: Passenger Comparison (made up)

Ann belonged to a church that was not allowed to pray in its own way in England. Her church sailed across the sea to start over. Bo was hired to work on the ship.

Ask 1: Passenger Comparison, invented. Why did Ann cross?
Ask 2: Passenger Comparison. Why was Bo not a Pilgrim?

## Day 101: Compact Exit Card

A list of four signers of the Compact: John Carver, William Bradford, Edward Winslow, Myles Standish. Forty-one names are signed in all. (Avalon text, U5P.)

Ask: Compact Exit Card. Name one group of people who did not sign the Compact.

## Day 102: Winter Care Card (made up)

A family card: in a cold winter, four in a family fall sick. One is left to gather wood, carry water and care for the others.

Ask: Winter Care Card. How does sickness make a family's work harder?

## Day 103: Later Relations Exit Card

Read aloud once. In 1675 came King Philip's War. Many Wampanoag people died. (Mashpee Wampanoag Tribe timeline, U5E.) The 1621 gathering was one meeting.

Ask: Later Relations Exit Card. What does the 1675 war show about one harvest gathering?

## Day 104: Colony Overlap Card (made up)

Two bands. Colony R ran from 1630 to 1660. Colony S began in 1650.

Ask 1: Colony Overlap Card. Was R there when S began?
Ask 2: Colony Overlap Card. Say how you know.
