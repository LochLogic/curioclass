# Day 102: Hardships and who faced them

## Teacher page

### Before class
Draw the Hardship Table on the board with three columns: who, hardship, job. Copy the Hale and Pruitt records. Keep key-exits-100-104.md away until slips are in. Keep a class list for who was heard. Our source tells how many died, not who did each chore, so every family record is made up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair (E from E101)
Hand back day 101's slip (s05-E101) marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Write the wrong, "The Compact governed everyone," beside the right, "A rule reaches the people who made it." Pairs sort the four Chess Club claims, made up. Each child writes [[s05-P102a]]. If few made error E, the missing children work at the side table.

#### 10 min. Hardships
Review (day 97): a role is the job a person did. A hardship is something hard that a family must face. Source: about half of the passengers died in the first winter. Read the Hale record, made up: the garden failed. Fill the table with the class: who, hardship, job. **Ask.** "Who faced this hardship?" Expect: the whole family, each with a job. Each child copies the Hale frame.

#### 8 min. Women and children
Read the Pruitt record, made up: the house stayed cold. Add rows with the class. Women, men and children each have jobs; no row belongs to men alone. Say: a hardship falls on the whole family.

#### 12 min. Pairs
Pairs read the Moss, Wick and Dunn records, made up, and say who did what. Each child writes [[s05-P102b]], [[s05-P102c]] and [[s05-P102d]]. Hear a rotating six pairs, about a minute each, and list their names.

#### 3 min. Record sentence
Each child writes [[s05-P102e]]. It goes in the record folder; the teacher gives a written cue after class, and it is revised on day 104. The other practice answers stay in the record folder.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip with the Winter Care Card, new today. Read it once. Each child writes [[s05-E102]]. Key: key-exits-100-104.md.

**Shared shape.** Model and practice read made-up family records and name who did each job; the exit is a new card about one winter's work and asks how a change in who can work makes the work harder.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the claims and the records onto the board and read each aloud once.

### At home

**Swap.** No folder: keep the sentence on a sheet to show the teacher.

**Skip.** The Chess Club claims.

- **5 min. Claims.** [[s05-P102a]]
- **10 min. The Hale family.** Read the Hale record. **Say.** "Who faced this hardship?" Expect: the whole family, each with a job.
- **10 min. Three records.** Read each record. [[s05-P102b]] [[s05-P102c]] [[s05-P102d]]
- **5 min. Your sentence.** [[s05-P102e]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Winter Care Card once. [[s05-E102]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the three records to two; the Hale frame and the sentence stay. Longer: make up a family record about a hard day and name each job.

### Words and supports
Words: hardship, role, job, family. Frame: "The ___ family faced ___. ___ did ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 103 opens with error V (only men's work mattered) from today's exit, five minutes, with fresh care and shelter records, then places 1621 on the strip.
