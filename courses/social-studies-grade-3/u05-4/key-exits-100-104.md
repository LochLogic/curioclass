# Teacher key, exit cards days 100 to 104

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-100-104.md. Marking: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- E100: to worship in her church's own way, apart from the Church of England. Not "for land" (AF).
- E100b: Bo was hired for work. Pilgrims were the church group that worshiped in their own way, apart from the Church of England. Not "everyone on the ship was a Pilgrim" (M) and not "Bo crossed to worship in the church's way too" (AF).
- E101: Native nations, or any group not on the signer list. The Compact was an agreement among its signers, and the card says forty-one names are signed. Not "it governed everyone" (E).
- E102: fewer people can gather wood, carry water and care for the sick. Not "only men's work mattered" (V).
- E103: it did not make lasting peace; the 1675 war shows later relations need other evidence. Not "it made peace forever" (D).
- E104: yes. The years 1650 to 1660 are on both bands. Not "S replaced R" (K).
