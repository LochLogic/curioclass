Teacher only. Never hand to children. Score each prompt 2, 1 or 0 after class from the sheet and the revised folder.

- M1: spaced by years. P is in the 1500s; Q and R are in the 1600s and closer together. Not "all three one step apart" (A).
- M1b: the sixteenth century. Not the fifteenth (AA).
- M1c: 1625 to 1650. Q ran to 1650 and R began in 1625, so both were there in those years. Not "R replaced Q" (K) and not 1610 to 1625 (K).
