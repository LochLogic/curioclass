# Day 101: The Mayflower Compact and self-government

## Teacher page

### Before class
Hang the wall strip. Draw the Reef Swim Club table on the board with two columns: who makes the rule, whom it reaches. Keep key-exits-100-104.md away until slips are in. Keep a class list for who was heard. The Compact is called an agreement among its signers; do not say who could vote.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (AF and M from E100)
Hand back day 100's slip and Record card (s05-E100, s05-E100b, s05-P100a) marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen; the card goes back in the folder for day 104. Write the wrong, "Everyone crossed for one reason," beside the right, "Each group had its own reason." Review (day 100): Pilgrims are the church group; hired workers are not. Pairs read Pia's and Joss's cards, made up, and say each reason. Each child writes [[s05-P101a]], [[s05-P101b]] and [[s05-P101c]]. If few made error AF or M, they work at the side table.

#### 4 min. Timeline
Review (day 91): one mark is ten years. Place the Compact on the wall strip at 1620, a second card on the same mark as the crossing. **Ask.** "How many years after Jamestown began?" Expect: thirteen. Each child writes [[s05-P101d]] on the strip in the record folder.

#### 8 min. Self-government
The Reef Swim Club is made up. Its members make the rules for the club, vote and pick a leader. Review: vote (kindergarten, grade 1), to choose by counting each member's pick. Fill the table with the class: the members make the rule; it reaches the members, not the town pool next door. Say: self-government means the people of a group make the rules for the group. **Ask.** "Who makes this rule, and whom does it reach?" Expect: the members make it and it reaches the members.

#### 8 min. The Compact
Read the label first: made by Curio Class from a university's text, to show what the Compact said. Read the summary once. Fill the same table with the class. The summary names only the signers; the class says whom it does not name. Each child writes [[s05-P101e]] and [[s05-P101f]].

#### 8 min. Pairs: whom does it reach
Pairs read six claims, made up, and sort them into reaches and does not reach. Each child writes [[s05-P101g]]. Repair error E on the spot: a rule reaches the people who made it. Hear a rotating six pairs, about a minute each, and list their names.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip with the Compact Exit Card, new today. Read it once. Each child writes [[s05-E101]]. Key: key-exits-100-104.md.

**Shared shape.** Model and practice fill a who-makes-it and whom-it-reaches table and sort claims; the exit gives a new signer list and asks what self-government means.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the summary and the six claims onto the board and read each aloud once.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: draw a line and mark 1607 and 1620.

**Skip.** The claims.

- **8 min. Passengers.** Read the cards. [[s05-P101a]] [[s05-P101b]] [[s05-P101c]]
- **12 min. Club and Compact.** Read the club and the summary. **Say.** "Who makes the rule, and whom does it reach?" Expect: the people who made it, and only them. [[s05-P101e]] [[s05-P101f]]
- **5 min. Strip.** [[s05-P101d]]
- **5 min. Claims.** [[s05-P101g]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Compact Exit Card once. [[s05-E101]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the claims to 4 minutes; the table and the Compact stay. Longer: make up a club and its rules; say whom they reach.

### Words and supports
Words: self-government, rule, vote, Compact, signer, reach. Frame: "The rule reaches ___ but not ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 102 opens with error E (the Compact governed everyone) from today's exit, five minutes, with a fresh set of claims about a chess club, then turns to hardships.
