# Mid-unit sheet: three tasks

Print on one page. The teacher reads the directions aloud once. Pencil only. No talk. Every colony and voyage on this page is made up unless it says Champlain. The key is in key-day105.md.

Name: __________

Task 1. The strip.
Colony P began in 1585. Colony Q began in 1610. Colony R began in 1625. All are made up, for practice.
Ask: On your strip, place P, Q and R.
Ask: Colony P began in 1585. Which century is that?
Ask: Colony Q lasted to 1650. Which years did Q and R share?

Task 2. The map.
A printed map shows Europe, the Atlantic Ocean and North America. Voyage Card, made up. A ship left Europe in 1580 and sailed west.
Ask: Point to North America, Europe and the Atlantic on the map.
Ask: Voyage Card. Give one reason Europeans sailed west.

Task 3. The route.
A map shows dots for France and Quebec. It shows the Atlantic Ocean and the river.
Ask: Champlain sailed from France in 1608 to Quebec. Trace his route with arrows.

Check that every task has an answer. Hand in the sheet.
