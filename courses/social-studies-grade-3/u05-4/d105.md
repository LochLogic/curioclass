# Day 105: Mid-unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Make the day 105 mid-unit packet from packet-day105.md: the paper timeline strips, the map and the route map with a blank route line. Every colony and voyage on it is made up, for practice, except Champlain. Each child's revised record folder stays on the desk. Check that no page holds a key. Keep the teacher key (key-day105.md) in your folder. Cover the wall strip, 1500 to 1700, ten-year marks, and the bands; no packet case has been taught.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions
Say: three tasks, pencil only, no talk, no one helps. Each prompt is read once. Your folder is on your desk; an adult scores it later. Nothing is marked by you.

#### 30 min. Three tasks
Read each task once at its start, with a spoken pace cue at each change and no clock shown.
Task 1, the strip, 10 minutes: each child writes [[s05-M1]], [[s05-M1b]] and [[s05-M1c]]. Hear no one; the sheet is the record.
Task 2, the map, 10 minutes: each child writes [[s05-M2]] and [[s05-M2b]]. A child who needs to point and speak answers to an adult in a separate slot of 2 minutes after the task, at most 6 children, with the answers written down; list their names.
Task 3, the route, 10 minutes: each child writes [[s05-M3]].

#### 5 min. Check and collect
Each child checks that every task has an answer, which is a check for blanks, not a mark. Collect the sheets.

#### 2 min. Collect records
Collect the folders. These items are scored after class from the revised records, so nothing new is written: [[s05-M4]], [[s05-M4b]], [[s05-M5]], [[s05-M5b]], [[s05-M6]], [[s05-M7]], [[s05-M8]].

### Marking
After class score every item 2, 1 or 0 on the day 105 score sheet with key-day105.md; M4 to M8 from the revised folder. Spelling and handwriting are not scored. About 4 minutes a child (ours, untested). Record results by letter for day 106.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the strip, the map and the route map by hand on paper; keep each page short.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. Nothing here asks about your home.

**Skip.** The sheets for the teacher.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Read each task once, then each question once. Write your child's answer if they ask. Do not help. **Say.** "Why did Europeans sail west?" Expect: for trade, wealth, land or their religion. [[s05-M1]] [[s05-M1b]] [[s05-M1c]] [[s05-M2]] [[s05-M2b]] [[s05-M3]]
- **10 min. Your records.** Your child shows each revised record. [[s05-M5]] [[s05-M8]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: none; every part is asked once. Longer: your child tells one thing they would change in an answer.

### Words and supports
Words: century, strip, route, reason, record. Frame: "I think ___ because ___." Read aloud; enlarge type and maps; pointing, speech and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 106 hands back the sheets with a note and a letter; error K from day 104's exit is repaired first, then a five-minute response to the check.

### Credit
Ours. Every colony and voyage on the packet is made up except Champlain, whose route is from a reference page on explorers.
