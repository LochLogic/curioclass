# Day 104: Revise the records and review the timeline

## Teacher page

### Before class
Write a one-line cue on every record in each folder, saying what is shown and one thing to add. Hang the wall strip with the three bands. Keep key-exits-100-104.md away until slips are in. Keep a class list for who was heard.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair (written feedback)
Hand out the folders with the records marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen: s05-E93, s05-P93a, s05-P93b, s05-P93c, s05-P93d, s05-E95, s05-P95a, s05-P95b, s05-P95c, s05-P95d, s05-P95e, s05-P95f, s05-E96, s05-E96b, s05-P96a, s05-P96b, s05-P96c, s05-P96d, s05-P96e, s05-E100, s05-E100b, s05-P100a, s05-E101, s05-E102, s05-P102e, s05-P103a, s05-E103. Children read their cues silently or with a partner.

#### 25 min. Independent revisions
Each child revises the records whose cue says not yet, at least four, writing in colored pencil below the first answer; about three minutes each. Review (day 96): shows, might suggest, cannot tell. Error D (one gathering made peace permanent) from day 103's exit is repaired first: each child writes [[s05-P104d]], then revises the ordering record, s05-P103a. No new teaching. Each child writes [[s05-P104e]] last. The practice answers stay in the record folder. Hear a rotating six, about 45 seconds each, and list their names.

#### 8 min. Timeline review
On the wall strip, show the three bands: St. Augustine from 1565, Jamestown from 1607, Plymouth from 1620. Children draw the bands on the paper strip. Say which began first and about how many tens apart. Say one nation each colony lived beside: the Spanish at Seloy, a Timucua town; the English and the Powhatan nations; the English and the Wampanoag. Each child writes [[s05-P104a]], [[s05-P104b]] and [[s05-P104c]].

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip with the Colony Overlap Card, new today. Read it once. Each child writes [[s05-E104]] and [[s05-E104b]]. Key: key-exits-100-104.md.

**Shared shape.** The review names which band starts first, how far apart two starts are and one neighbor nation for each colony; the exit gives two new bands and asks whether one was there when the other began.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the bands on the board and read the Town cards aloud once.

### At home

**Swap.** No folder: use the week's sheets.

**Skip.** The timeline review.

- **7 min. Cues.** Read each cue together; your child picks the records to revise.
- **15 min. Revise.** Your child adds to each answer in a colored pencil. [[s05-P104d]] [[s05-P104e]]
- **8 min. Bands.** [[s05-P104a]] [[s05-P104b]] [[s05-P104c]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Colony Overlap Card once. [[s05-E104]] [[s05-E104b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: revise four records; the bands and the exit stay. Longer: revise every record whose cue says not yet.

### Words and supports
Words: cue, revise, band, neighbor. Frame: "I changed ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 105 is the mid-unit check: three tasks on paper, pencil only. Error K (a new colony ended the older one) from today's exit is picked up on day 106.

### Credit
Ours. The neighbor nations are the ones taught on days 97, 98 and 103. The Town cards are made up.
