# Exit cards, days 113 to 116

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. Answers are spoken, drawn or written.

## Day 113: Port Work Card (made up)

Rafe builds boats. Wyn fishes. Wyn has no coins but has a full net of fish.

Ask 1: Port Work Card. How do Rafe and Wyn need each other?
Ask 2: Port Work Card. Wyn has no coins. How can Wyn pay Rafe?

## Day 114: Route Card (made up)

A ship carries indigo, a blue dye, from the island of Isle Corra to a port. The indigo was grown on Isle Corra's plantations by people who were held there against their will and never paid.

Ask 1: Route Card. Who grew the indigo on Isle Corra?
Ask 2: Route Card. How were they made to work?

## Day 115: Two-Harbor Exit Map (made up)

A map of two harbors, Cask Bay and Reed Harbor. Cask Bay has sheltered water, a fish market and fishers' boats. Reed Harbor has sheltered water, a boat yard and stacks of timber.

Ask 1: Two-Harbor Exit Map. What does the water at each harbor give?
Ask 2: Two-Harbor Exit Map. What do the people at each harbor do?

## Day 116: Route Card (made up)

A map. Goods gather at Marlow Quay, a made-up port with deep water and a long dock. Ships arrive from far away.

Ask: Route Card. Why did goods gather at Marlow Quay?

The keys are in key-exits-113-116.md. Do not give them to children.
