# Day 157: What came of it, and our claim

## Teacher page

### Before class
Note what you saw happen with the contribution since day 156: who read or used it, and what they did. If nothing yet, "we saw nothing yet" is true. Gather the nation's page and the interview from your teacher-supplied copies, the labor card and the action record. Keep key-exits-154-157.md. Collect each child's claim and sheet for a cue before day 158.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 156's records with one cue line each: s07-P156a, s07-P156b, s07-P156c, s07-P156d, s07-E156. If many thought finishing shows it worked (error M), seat up to six at the side table. Read a made-up case: a class made a reminder card about walking in line and predicted classmates would walk in line. Two children looked at it. Each child says one thing we saw and one we do not know, 40 seconds each. The rest read the question board.

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Read aloud the board's five answers and the evidence list: the Local Nation Account (day 142), the Labor History Card (day 145), the Local Interview (day 146) and the action record. Review: the claim frame from day 86. Ask six children one source each, 40 seconds each; the record names who was heard.

#### 8 min. Look back
Say what you saw and heard of the contribution, in plain words. Review: grade 2's Action Record: what we did, what happened, what we would change. Each child fills the three-column sheet: [[s07-P157a]], [[s07-P157b]] and [[s07-P157c]]. Say: finishing is not the same as it working; what we do not know goes in the last column.

#### 15 min. Write the claim
Show a filled example claim from earlier in the course for two minutes, then take it down. Show the blank frame. Write one class claim on the board together from the board's answers. Each child uses the nation's page and the interview, checks against the labor card and the action record, and adds a detail from each of two sources: [[s07-P157d]]. People beyond the founders count. Words or a drawing with a scribe are answers.

#### 6 min. Hear another and revise
Pairs read their claims, 3 minutes, then revise one line, 1 minute. At the 4-minute mark hand out the quarter slip with the made-up Claim Card; children finish it in Collect. Use the norms card: each partner restates the other's claim, says which contribution mattered and why, and the writer changes one line.

#### 2 min. Collect
Read the card once and the ask once: [[s07-E157]]. Key: key-exits-154-157. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Collect the claims and sheets; write a cue on each before day 158.

**Shared shape.** The practice compares predictions with what was seen and writes the child's own claim from the project's sources; the exit gives a new made-up claim and asks what is missing from it.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the frame and the three columns on plain paper; read the sources from your copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No class contribution: the child writes the three columns about the small paper item.

**Skip.** The pair reading.

- **8 min. Look back.** [[s07-P157a]] [[s07-P157b]] [[s07-P157c]] **Say.** "What do we not know?" Expect: a longer effect.
- **20 min. The claim.** Read two sources aloud together. [[s07-P157d]]
- **5 min. Revise.** Read the claim aloud; change one line.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Claim Card once. [[s07-E157]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one detail from one source and a scribe for the second. Longer: test a third source against the claim.

### Words and supports
Words: predicted, saw, unknown, claim. Frame: "I think ___ helped make our community because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___." Read every source aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 158 returns the cues and revises the portfolio. The exit tells you who still writes a claim with no detail (error I). Day 158 hands back P157a to P157d and E157.
