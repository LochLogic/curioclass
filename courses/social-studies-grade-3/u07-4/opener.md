# Section 7.4 opener: informed action, alone and together

## What the section does

Five days of 40 minutes. Day 153 looks at how one person alone and one group together took on a regional or world problem. Day 154 nominates a local problem from the project evidence and weighs ways to help. Day 155 decides by a fair process. Day 156 makes the class's one contribution. Day 157 looks back and writes a claim answering the unit question.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **One action does not solve every scale** (G). A person alone and a group together each reach some people and not others.
- **A prediction needs a reason** (L). "It will work because we want it" is a wish, not a prediction.
- **Finishing is not impact** (M). The class records what it saw, what it predicted and what it does not know.
- **A fair process hears everyone** (H). Every plan is heard, a compromise is offered, and the vote is private. A majority may not silence others.
- **A claim needs details from two sources** (I). A source's title is not a detail.

## The errors in plain words

G is thinking one action fixes the whole problem. H is thinking the most hands win. I is a claim with no detail. L is thinking a wish is a reason. M is thinking finishing shows it worked.

## Supplies

Paper, pencils, scissors, tape, the nomination card, the strategy comparison, the decision record, the action record, the three-column sheet and the claim frame. The exit cards are in cards-exits-153.md and the key in key-exits-153.md (teacher only). No travel, no fundraising and no required public contact; the teacher screens options for forty minutes on day 156.
