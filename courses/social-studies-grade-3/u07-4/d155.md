# Day 155: Deciding by a fair process

## Teacher page

### Before class
Set out the norms card, the ballot rules card, the Class Decision Record, a covered box and one paper ballot per child. Make six slips, three reasons and three wishes, such as "A wider rug lets more children sit, because it is wider." Form five groups of six; pick two counters. Keep key-exits-154-157.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 154's comparison with one cue line each: s07-P154a, s07-P154b, s07-P154c, s07-P154d, s07-P154e, s07-P154f, s07-E154. If many gave a wish as a reason (error L), seat up to six at the side table; each reads one slip and says reason or wish, 40 seconds each. The rest read the norms card.

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Review: the norms card from day 38: listen, say it back, give a reason. Review: restate, say another person's view back fairly (day 38). Review: the ballot rules card from day 39: one ballot each, the same rules for all, private, a tie means talk more then a runoff. Review: the Class Decision Record from day 40. Ask six children one card each, 40 seconds each; the record names who was heard.

#### 10 min. Hear each plan
Each group of six: every child tells a plan for the chosen problem and one reason, one minute each, six minutes. Then each child restates the plan before theirs, about 40 seconds each, four minutes. Sit with two groups for five minutes each; the record names who was heard. Say: any plan that cannot finish in 40 minutes with our supplies needs a smaller part.

#### 8 min. A compromise
New today: a compromise is a plan that takes useful parts of other plans. Groups find one plan that uses a useful part of two others and write one reason for each view heard. Join two other groups, so four of five are heard by the end; the fifth is heard on day 158. Each child writes [[s07-P155a]], [[s07-P155b]] and [[s07-P155c]].

#### 8 min. Decide
One reporter from each group tells its plan, 40 seconds each. Read the ballot rules card aloud. Each child marks one ballot in private and drops it in the covered box, about five seconds a child. Two counters read the marks aloud. A tie means one minute of talk, then a runoff. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Decision record
Fill the Class Decision Record for the class on the board. Each child writes [[s07-P155d]], how the class decided, in 2 minutes. Words, a drawing or a scribe are answers. Keep the last 3 minutes for the exit: hand out the quarter slip with the made-up Vote Card, read the card once and the ask once: [[s07-E155]]. Key: key-exits-154-157.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The record is practice and is revised on day 158.

**Shared shape.** The practice carries out a fair process on the class's own plans: hear every plan, restate, compromise, vote in private; the exit gives a new made-up vote card and asks whether the class should vote now, and why.

### Other rooms
No box: use a shoebox with a lid. No printer: copy the record lines on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No groups: the adult tells a plan too, and the child restates it.

**Skip.** The counters and the runoff.

- **12 min. Hear plans.** The child tells a plan and a reason; you restate it. Then you tell one; the child restates it.
- **10 min. A compromise.** [[s07-P155a]] [[s07-P155b]] [[s07-P155c]] **Say.** "What part of each plan is useful?" Expect: one useful part from each.
- **8 min. Decide.** Each marks a paper in private; count. [[s07-P155d]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Vote Card once. [[s07-E155]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: hear and restate three plans. Longer: write a second compromise.

### Words and supports
Words: view, restate, compromise, ballot. Frames: "I heard ___; I would add ___." "A plan that uses ___ and ___ is ___." Read every card aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 156 makes the contribution the class chose. The exit tells you who thinks the biggest group should decide at once (error H). Day 156 hands back P155a to P155d and E155.
