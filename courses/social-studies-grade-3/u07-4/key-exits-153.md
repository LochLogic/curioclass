# Teacher key, exit cards day 153

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-153.md.

- E153: an action one child could take, such as asking to play in a shady spot. Not "a group poster will fix the whole problem" (G).
- E153b: an action a group could take, such as many children signing one note asking the adults for shade. Not "the group will win because we want it" (L).
- P153d (Exit box): a result joined to the action by a reason. Not "it will work because we want it" (L).
