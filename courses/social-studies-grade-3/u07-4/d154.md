# Day 154: Choosing a problem and weighing ways to help

## Teacher page

### Before class
Put the question board, the evidence list and the screen card where you can reach them: finishes in day 156's 40 minutes, supplies the class has, in school, no travel, no fundraising, no hazardous cleanup, no required public contact. Put out one dot per child. You screen; the class chooses. Keep key-exits-154-157.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 153's work with one cue line each: s07-P153a, s07-P153b, s07-P153c, s07-P153e, s07-E153, s07-E153b, s07-P153d. If many thought one action fixes the whole problem (error G), seat up to six at the side table: "A class recycles its own paper. Does that fix all the school's waste?" Each says what is still needed, 40 seconds each. If many gave a wish as a reason (error L), read three made-up predictions aloud and sort them into reasons and wishes. The rest read the question board.

#### 3 min. Retrieval
Read aloud the board's answers and the evidence list: the nation's page, the labor card, the interview and the biographies. Ask six children one line each, 30 seconds each; the record names who was heard.

#### 7 min. Nominate
Say: a problem is a place or a situation that needs fixing, never a person. Model, made up: "A made-up town page says the path to the pond is muddy after rain." Each child writes [[s07-P154a]], a problem found in a line of the project's evidence, and [[s07-P154b]], who it touches. Nothing about home. Grade 2's flood-help work (day 33) may be one nomination only.

#### 6 min. Pairs practise
While you sort the cards against the screen card and join duplicates, pairs talk through a fresh made-up problem: a classroom with no place for wet boots. Each child says to a partner: "A likely result is ___ because ___." No one writes.

#### 5 min. Choose the problem
Read the two or three screened problems. Take one reason for each. Each child places one dot, about five seconds a child. A tie means more talk, then another dot. 

#### 14 min. Strategy comparison
For the chosen problem each child writes [[s07-P154c]], [[s07-P154d]] and [[s07-P154e]]. For [[s07-P154f]] read two limits on the board for each option, one that says whom it does not reach and one wish; each child circles the better one. Use "Alone I could ___." and "A group could ___; a limit is ___." Keep the last 4 minutes for the exit: hand out the quarter slip with the made-up Sign Card, read the card once and the ask once: [[s07-E154]]. Key: key-exits-154-157.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The comparison is practice; it is revised after written feedback.

**Shared shape.** The practice nominates from real evidence, predicts aloud on a made-up wet boots problem and writes options and circles the better limit; the exit gives a new made-up sign card and asks for one limit of the child's sign.

### Other rooms
No dots: raise one hand at each choice, then count. No printer: copy the comparison lines on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class vote: the adult names two problems and the child picks one.

**Skip.** The side table and the dots.

- **10 min. Nominate.** Read a line from one of the project's sources. [[s07-P154a]] [[s07-P154b]] **Say.** "Who does it touch?" Expect: people, never your own family.
- **20 min. Compare.** [[s07-P154c]] [[s07-P154d]] [[s07-P154e]] [[s07-P154f]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Sign Card once. [[s07-E154]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one result and one limit for the group option only. Longer: a third option weighed against the first two.

### Words and supports
Words: nominate, problem, option, likely. Frames: "Alone I could ___; likely ___ because ___." "A group could ___; a limit is ___." Read every card aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 155 decides which way to help, using the norms card, the ballot rules card and a private vote. The exit tells you who thinks a sign fixes every problem. Day 155's Return repairs a wish given as a reason (error L). Day 155 hands back P154a to P154f and E154.
