# Exit cards, day 153

Print "made up, for practice" on the card. Slip 1 is a half slip with two asks. The third ask goes on the student page as the Exit box and is handed back on day 154. The key is in key-exits-153.md (teacher only).

## Day 153: Shade Card (made up, for practice)

At a made-up school, children wait for the bus in the hot sun. No shade is near the stop.

Ask 1: Shade Card. Name one thing you could do alone.

Ask 2: Shade Card. Name one thing a group could do.

## Exit box (student page)

Shade Card: say a likely result of the group's action, and why.
