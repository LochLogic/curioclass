# Exit cards, days 154 to 157

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. Each exit is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-154-157.md (teacher only). The strategy comparison, decision record, action record and closing claim are folder records (retained evidence), not exits.

## Day 154: Sign Card (made up, for practice)

A child draws a sign for the school door. It asks people to walk, not run, on the ramp.

Ask: Sign Card. Predict what will happen once the sign is up. Give a reason.

## Day 155: Vote Card (made up, for practice)

Eight children like a red banner. Three like a blue banner. Three have not been heard.

Ask: Vote Card. Should the class vote now? Say why.

## Day 156: Display Card (made up, for practice)

A class finished a display of old photographs on Friday. Nobody has visited it yet.

Ask: Display Card. Does finishing the display show visitors learned from it?

## Day 157: Claim Card (made up, for practice)

A child writes: "I think workers made our community because they are important."

Ask: Claim Card. Rewrite the claim with a detail from one of our sources.
