# Day 156: Making the class's contribution

## Teacher page

### Before class
Post the chosen plan from day 155 on the wall. Lay out scissors, tape, markers and paper in trays, counted. Write six role cards per group: maker, labeler, checker, reader, carrier, placer. No role needs lifting, small print or speaking in public. Choose where the contribution will be set so its audience will see it. No water, travel, fundraising or public contact. Keep key-exits-154-157.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 155's records with one cue line each: s07-P155a, s07-P155b, s07-P155c, s07-P155d, s07-E155. If many took the biggest group as the decider (error H), seat up to six at the side table. Read a fresh made-up case: three plans for a class garden box, one child talking most. Each child restates one plan, about 40 seconds each. The rest check the plan on the wall.

#### 3 min. The plan and the roles
Read the chosen plan aloud once. Hand each child a role card. Say: everyone has a job, and any child may pass.

#### 26 min. Make
Groups make the contribution with the trays' supplies. Guide; do not do it for them. If a group runs late, it finishes only the label; nothing is carried past this day. Stop card on each desk.

#### 4 min. Action record
Write on the board what we made and who will use it. Each child writes [[s07-P156a]], [[s07-P156b]], [[s07-P156c]] and [[s07-P156d]]. Words, a drawing or a scribe are answers. At the 2-minute mark hand out the quarter slip with the made-up Display Card, read the card once and the ask once: [[s07-E156]]. Key: key-exits-154-157.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Clean up
Children finish the exit slip in the first minute while one counter per tray collects. Count scissors, tape and markers back into the trays. Paper scraps go to the reuse bin. Set the contribution where its audience will see it.

**Shared shape.** The practice makes the class's own contribution and records what we made, who will use it, an expectation with a reason and one limit; the exit gives a new made-up finished card and asks whether finishing shows anyone learned.

### Other rooms
No trays: count the tools in and out from one box. No printer: copy the four lines on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class contribution: the child and adult make one small paper item for another family adult.

**Skip.** The role cards and the clean up count.

- **5 min. The plan.** Read the plan aloud. Pick who does what.
- **20 min. Make.** Make it with paper, tape and markers.
- **8 min. The record.** [[s07-P156a]] [[s07-P156b]] [[s07-P156c]] [[s07-P156d]] **Say.** "What do you expect, and why?" Expect: a likely result and a reason.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Display Card once. [[s07-E156]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: finish only the label. Longer: add one more helper role for a second group.

### Words and supports
Words: contribution, role, expect. Frames: "We made ___ for ___." "I expect ___ because ___; one limit is ___." Read every card aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 157 looks at what came of the contribution and writes the closing claim. The exit tells you who thinks finishing shows it worked (error M). Day 157 hands back P156a to P156d and E156.

### Credit
Ours. The garden box case and the Display Card are made up.
