# Day 153: One person alone, a group together

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the strips and the half slips. Write the bike rack strip on the board, boxes empty. Put each child's day 152 sheets on their desk. Keep key-exits-153.md. 

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 152's sheets with one cue line each: s07-P152a, s07-P152b, s07-E152, s07-P152c. A child with no listener line writes one now: a family adult, a neighbor or a school adult on day 158. s07-P152d comes back once a listener has heard. If many thought a partner or a poster shares beyond the class (error F), read a made-up case: Kai tells his partner; Rosa tells a neighbor. Ask: who is outside the class?

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Review: in grade 2 you filled an Action Record: what we did, who answered, what we would change. Review: in unit 2 you filled a Class Decision Record and a four-box strip. Ask six children one box each, 40 seconds each; the record names who was heard.

#### 8 min. One person alone
Read the Nell strip aloud once. Say: new today, an action is what a person did; a reason is why; a result is what came of it; a limit is what the page does not tell us. Each child fills the strip: [[s07-P153a]].

#### 7 min. A group together
Review: enslaved people were held for life and denied freedom (day 109). Hand out the stop card. Read the Freedom Association strip aloud once. Say: people seeking freedom made their own choices; the group helped them. Each child fills the strip: [[s07-P153b]]. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Model a prediction
Say: a prediction says a likely result and why. Model, made up and invented for practice: the school bike rack is full by eight. Alone: one child could ask the office about it. Group: the class could write one letter asking for a second rack. Result: a second rack may come, because the letter shows many bikes left out. Limit: a letter does not build a rack. "It will work because we want it" is a wish, not a reason.

#### 8 min. Independent practice
Read the Litter Card and its caption once. Each child writes [[s07-P153c]], then circles one line in [[s07-P153e]]. Pairs say why they chose it.

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read the made-up Shade Card and the asks once. Each child writes [[s07-E153]] and [[s07-E153b]] on the slip, then [[s07-P153d]] in the Exit box. Key: key-exits-153.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice reads two cases and fills strips, models a bike rack, writes a limit and picks the better of two lines; the exit gives a new shade case and asks for an action alone, an action in a group and a result with a reason.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the strips on plain paper; read the cards from your copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No second paragraph: use the Nell strip only.

**Skip.** The retrieval slips and the stop card.

- **8 min. One person alone.** Read the Nell strip. [[s07-P153a]] **Say.** "Why did he write it?" Expect: to inspire change.
- **7 min. A group together.** Read the Freedom Association strip. [[s07-P153b]]
- **5 min. Model.** Say the bike rack strip aloud, filling each box.
- **10 min. Practice.** Read the Litter Card. [[s07-P153c]] [[s07-P153e]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Shade Card once. [[s07-E153]] [[s07-E153b]] [[s07-P153d]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fill only the result and limit boxes on the second strip. Longer: write a second group action for the litter case.

### Words and supports
Words: alone, together, action, reason, result, limit, predict. Frames: "Alone I could ___." "A group could ___." "A likely result is ___ because ___." Read every card aloud; a scribe is an answer; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 154 turns to our own project: each child names a problem found in its evidence. The exit tells you who thinks one action fixes the whole problem (error G) or a wish is a reason (error L). Day 154 hands back P153a, P153b, P153c, P153e, E153, E153b and P153d.
