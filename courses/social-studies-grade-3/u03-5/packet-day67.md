# Day 67 packet

Teacher reads Packet A, B, C and D aloud once, then each prompt aloud once at its start. Pages are each under 350 words. Printed "made up, for practice" on Packets A, C and D. No keys on any page.

## Page 1: Packet A, Workshop Record (made up, for practice)

Lee runs a local workshop. Wood from trees, joining skills and a saw make benches. The same skills and tools repair chairs. Lee has $28 for either display shelves or a sign. Each costs $28, and Lee cannot buy both. Lee ranks the shelves first and the sign second.

A bench costs $18 to make and has a $25 tag price. A box costs $6 to make and has a $10 tag price. Buyers get $3 off for collecting on time. Late collection adds a $2 storage fee. Lee repairs Farah's chair. Farah pays money, and Lee buys bread with it.

Packet A. Lee chooses the shelves. Name one benefit of the shelves.
Packet A. What is Lee's opportunity cost?
Packet A. Why can Lee not have both?
Packet A. Which one is the reward?
Packet A. Which one is the penalty?
Packet A. What might the penalty make a buyer do?
Packet A. What kind of resource is the wood?
Packet A. What kind of resource is the joining skill?
Packet A. What kind of resource is the saw?
Packet A. Why does money help Lee get bread?
Packet A. At the tag price, how much does a bench leave after costs?
Packet A. How much does a box leave after costs?
Packet A. How might that shape what Lee makes?
Packet A. Give a detail from A that supports your claim.

## Page 2: Packet B, facts from a Ghanaian newspaper (2023)

Our own words, not quoted. Techiman is a market in Ghana. It began in 1944 as a small market next to a chief's palace. Women are the main ones who run it today. They work in groups, one group for each kind of goods.

Packets A and B. Write one claim: how does our community get what it needs?
Packet B. Give a different detail from B that supports your claim.

## Page 3: Packet C, Trade Record (made up, for practice)

A Canadian supplier sells hinges to Lee's workshop in the United States. The supplier buys US-made tools from Lee. A route arrow runs from Canada to Lee's workshop. Over time both place more orders, and both rely more on deliveries and buyers across the border.

Packet C. What does Lee's workshop need from the supplier?
Packet C. What does the supplier need from Lee?
Packet C. Why do they rely on each other more over time?

## Page 4: Packet D, three needs (made up, for practice)

Need 1: bus drivers want a savings place their own group owns, where members also borrow.
Need 2: Omar wants a safe place to keep his savings and earn interest. He does not want to own any part of it.
Need 3: a baker pays a small fee each month so that a listed fire loss is paid.

Packet D, need 1. Which place fits: a bank, a credit union or insurance?
Packet D, need 2. Which place fits?
Packet D, need 3. Which place fits?
Packet D. Say what the credit union does.
