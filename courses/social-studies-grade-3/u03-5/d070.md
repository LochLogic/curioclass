# Day 70: Course check 1, part 2

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-check1.md and key-check1.md; the key stays with you. Print cards 4 and 5. Both cards are made up: say so. Unit 3 was assessed on day 67, so this part asks its skill on a town land choice, not on unit 3's cases. Never score a first attempt. Keep day 69's sheets with you; they are scored together after class.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions
Say: pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each card once, just before its questions. You may pass any question. No timer is shown.

#### 14 min. Pinewick Card
Read card 4 aloud once. Say what each word means: a cost is what is paid or given up; a benefit is a good thing a choice gives. Each child writes [[sc1-E4]], [[sc1-E4b]] and [[sc1-E4c]]. The card stays in view.

#### 18 min. Source Card
Read card 5 aloud once. Say: a claim is a sentence that answers the question. Each child writes [[sc1-E5]], [[sc1-E5b]] and [[sc1-E5c]]. The card stays in view. A spoken answer, a pointed answer or a scribe is offered to any child, without supplying the claim or the detail.

#### 3 min. Marking
Each child checks that every question has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most six who left one blank say it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record.

#### 3 min. Collection
Collect the sheets and cards. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-check1.md, together with day 69. Write the missing part by name (map, grid, leaders, choice, claim), not a total.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy each card large by hand and read it aloud from your copy; a child may hear a card again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read each card aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** Day 69's sheet.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Read each card once, then each question once. Do not help. **Say.** "What do we give up when we choose one thing?" Expect: the next best choice, the opportunity cost. [[sc1-E4]] [[sc1-E4c]] [[sc1-E5]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the Pinewick Card and the claim. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: cost, benefit, opportunity cost, claim, source. Read every card and question aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 71 hands both parts back, day 69's assessment and today's, with one note each, then each child fixes one missed idea on a fresh card. The groups follow the gaps you wrote.

Ours. Both cards are made up.
