# Day 69: Course check 1, part 1

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-check1.md and key-check1.md; the key stays with you. Print cards 1 and 2. Card 3 has no page: you say each ask once. Both cards are made up, so say so. Collect day 68's redo cards with the notes you wrote. This check comes right after unit 3, so none of its cases come from unit 3. Never score a first attempt.

### The day

#### 2 min. Hand back
Hand back day 68's redo cards (s03-F68a, s03-F68b, s03-F68c, s03-F68d, s03-F68e, s03-F68f, s03-F68g, s03-F68h, s03-F68i, s03-F68j, s03-F68k), each with the note you wrote. Each child reads the note silently and puts the card away.

#### 2 min. Directions
Say: pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each card once, just before its questions. You may pass any question. No timer is shown.

#### 12 min. Town map
Read card 1, the Wren Hollow Map, aloud once. Each child writes [[sc1-E1]], [[sc1-E1b]], [[sc1-E1c]], [[sc1-E1d]] and [[sc1-E1e]]. The card stays in view.

#### 12 min. World grid
Read card 2 aloud once, then the World Warm Card. Each child writes [[sc1-E2]], [[sc1-E2b]], [[sc1-E2c]], [[sc1-E2d]] and [[sc1-E2e]].

#### 9 min. Leaders
Say each ask once, then wait. Each child writes one thing for [[sc1-E3]], [[sc1-E3b]] and [[sc1-E3c]]. A child who needs to say an answer may tell it to you or a scribe.

#### 3 min. Collection
Each child checks that every question has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most three who left one blank say it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record. Collect the sheets and cards. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-check1.md. Write the missing part by name, not a total.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy card 1 large by hand and read each card aloud from your copy; a child may hear a card again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read each card aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** The day 68 cards.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Read each card aloud once, then each question once. Do not help. **Say.** "What does a President do for the country?" Expect: carries out the country's laws. [[sc1-E1]] [[sc1-E2]] [[sc1-E3c]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the town map and one leader ask. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: legend, landmark, hemisphere, power. Read every card and question aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 70 is part 2 of the check: a cost, a benefit and the opportunity cost of a town choice, and a claim with a detail from a source not seen before. Day 71 hands back both parts.

### Credit
Ours. The cards are made up; Mexico City's place on the globe follows its city page (source 4G).
