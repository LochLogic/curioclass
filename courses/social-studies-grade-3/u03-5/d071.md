# Day 71: Fix one idea

## Teacher page

### Before class
Score days 69 and 70 from key-check1.md and write on each check 1 sheet one note: a circled missed idea and one sentence. Sort children into at most four groups by the largest gaps: A map and world grid, B leaders, C cost and choice, D claim and source. Put each group's practice card on its table. All cards are made up; say so. Keep key-exits-71.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 69's assessment and day 70's assessment, the check 1 sheets with their notes: sc1-E1, sc1-E1b, sc1-E1c, sc1-E1d, sc1-E1e, sc1-E2, sc1-E2b, sc1-E2c, sc1-E2d, sc1-E2e, sc1-E3, sc1-E3b, sc1-E3c, sc1-E4, sc1-E4b, sc1-E4c, sc1-E5, sc1-E5b and sc1-E5c. Each child reads the note silently or with a partner. Say: a fix needs a new answer and a reason, not a copy.

#### 20 min. Groups
Each group meets you for five minutes in turn; the others work in pairs from the practice card on their table. Hear each child in your group once, about 45 seconds each: Group A, [[sc1-P71a]]; Group B, [[sc1-P71b]]; Group C, [[sc1-P71c]]; Group D, [[sc1-P71d]]. Name each child heard on the record.

#### 10 min. Fresh task
Hand each child the Exit Card for their group on the half slip. Each child fixes the idea marked on their sheet: [[sc1-X71]] and [[sc1-X71b]]. A scribe or a spoken answer is offered. Read the ask for the marked idea aloud from key-exits-71.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Reflection
Each child says to a partner what they fixed and why. Nobody is made the example of a mistake. Close the folders.

**Shared shape.** The group practice asks are spoken, one fresh case each. The exit uses a different Exit Card and asks for a written fix with a detail.

### Other rooms
No groups: read each note aloud to one child; they fix the idea on the Exit Card for their note.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; the parent reads the note aloud.

**Skip.** The group asks.

- **5 min. The note.** Read the note on the check 1 sheet.
- **15 min. Try again.** Pick a made-up card that fits the note and talk it through. **Say.** "What did you miss, and what would you add?" Expect: the idea named in the note, with a detail from the card.
- **10 min. The fix.** Write the new answer and a detail from the card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc1-X71]] [[sc1-X71b]] Tell the adult what you fixed.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fix one idea only; drop the group asks. Longer: fix a second marked idea on another card.

### Words and supports
Words: note, fix, evidence, card. Frame: "I fixed ___ because the card says ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.
