# Teacher key, day 68 redo cards

Teacher only. Never given to children.

- Sketch Card: The sketchbook, the next choice.
- Wall Card: The free turn. The $2 charge is the penalty.
- Weaver Card: The loom.
- Roof Card: Anyone with a roof can pay the roof mender in money, who then buys cheese; the cheese maker does not need roof work, so no one has to want the other's thing.
- Lantern and chime: The chime: $5 against $2.
- Town Card: The safe place for savings.
- Chair Card: The maker needs wood, and the wood yard needs the maker's orders.
- Post Office and Farm Stand: Both give people a place to get what they need: a sorting counter, tables under a roof.
- Elm and Ash: $5 more, for the same $100 and year.
- Street Tree Card: A named group, such as neighbors by the street or the crew who waters the trees, with a reason.
- Honey Card: The price, $4, is a fact. Too much is a view.
