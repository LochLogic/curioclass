# Teacher key, day 71 (practice and exit)

Teacher only. Mark each shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Ask the marked idea from the child's sheet on the Exit Card.

- P71a: south. The arrow points to the right edge, so the left of the page is south. Not "up".
- P71b: state. Not country.
- P71c: the car wash, the next best choice given up. Not "the money".
- P71d: a detail such as 120 swam or 50 were children. Not "the pool is nice".
- Exit Card A: legend, circle the diamond (old school); direction, the school is north of the post office, since the arrow points up and the school is up the page; scale, 2 bars; grid, Cape Town is D1; hemisphere, southern and eastern; warm or cold, a place near the equator gets more direct sunlight.
- Exit Card B: Ms. Okoye is a state leader; the governor carries out state laws, so she signs the highway plan; the mayor does a town job, a sidewalk plan. Not "the President".
- Exit Card C: a cost, the $5,000 or the door not bought; a benefit, new books for readers; the opportunity cost, the new door. Not "the $5,000".
- Exit Card D: a claim such as many families use the splash pad; a detail, 600 people used it or 200 were children; who made it, the park board, for neighbors.
- X71: a new answer that differs from the sheet and fits the card. Not the same answer again. X71b: a detail from the Exit Card, said or written.
