# Day 67: Unit 3 check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Make the packet from packet-day67.md: pages 1 to 4, and page 5 with each child's own revised Clinic exit practice record (day 49), supporting-question record (day 50) and day 66 savings sheet. Packets A, C and D print "made up, for practice". Packet B is our words, not quoted, and names no newspaper on the page. Check that no page holds a key. Keep the teacher key (key-day67.md) in your folder. Nothing from days 49 to 66 stays on the wall.

### The day

#### 5 min. Packet
Hand out the packets and answer sheets; the day 66 sheet goes back inside page 5, with its cue (s03-P66a, s03-P66b, s03-P66c, s03-P66d, s03-P66e). Say: answer alone, pass any prompt you like, no one helps, each prompt is read once. Read Packets A, B, C and D aloud once. A, C and D are made up. Packet B tells what one report says about the Techiman market.

#### 30 min. Eight unaided prompts
Read each prompt once at its start, with a spoken pace cue at each change and no clock shown.
Q1, 3 minutes: [[s03-Q1]], [[s03-Q1b]], [[s03-Q1c]].
Q2, 4 minutes: [[s03-Q2]], [[s03-Q2b]], [[s03-Q2c]].
Q3, 3 minutes: [[s03-Q3]], [[s03-Q3b]], [[s03-Q3c]].
Q4, 3 minutes: [[s03-Q4]].
Q5, 4 minutes: [[s03-Q5]], [[s03-Q5b]], [[s03-Q5c]].
Q6, 4 minutes: [[s03-Q6]], [[s03-Q6b]], [[s03-Q6c]], [[s03-Q6d]].
Q7, 3 minutes: [[s03-Q7]], [[s03-Q7b]], [[s03-Q7c]].
Q8, 6 minutes: [[s03-Q8]], [[s03-Q8b]], [[s03-Q8c]].
A child who needs to point and speak answers to an adult in a separate slot of 2 minutes after the period, at most 6, with the answers written down. Q9 to Q11 are not asked. They are judged from the revised records after written feedback: [[s03-Q9]], [[s03-Q9b]], [[s03-Q9c]] from the day 66 sheet, [[s03-Q10]] from the day 49 record and [[s03-Q11]] from the day 50 record.

#### 5 min. Reread and Q3 marking
Children reread and finish. Then read Q3 with the class: each answer and its kind of resource. Children tick their own answer in the second color and do not change it. Collect the packets and answer sheets, a name on each.

### Marking
After class, mark every item and every record look-for 2, 1 or 0 on the day 67 score sheet, using the teacher key; spelling and handwriting are not scored. The records are scored from the day 66 revisions, not before. About 3 minutes a child, about ninety minutes in all (ours, untested). Record results by part on the unit 3 record sheet and by letter for day 68's groups.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the packet pages by hand on paper; keep each page short.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. Nothing here asks about your home.

**Skip.** The sheets for the teacher.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Read each packet page aloud once, then each prompt once. Write your child's answer word for word. Do not help. **Say.** "How does a community get what it needs?" Expect: a claim with a detail from each packet.
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: none; every part is asked once. Longer: your child tells one thing they would change in a record.

### Words and supports
Words: resource, money, profit, reward, penalty, opportunity cost, interest, bank, credit union, insurance, trade, claim. Frame: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." Read aloud, enlarge type; pointing, speech and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 68 hands back the packet and the revised records with notes, reteaches in four groups, and gives a fresh redo card. If many made error K on day 65's exit, the claims group takes it first.

### Credit
Ours. Packets A, C and D are made up. Packet B gives facts from a Ghanaian newspaper (2023), in our own words, not quoted.
