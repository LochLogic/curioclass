# Day 65: Our answer, with two records

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 64's slips (s03-E64) and pick the more-missed of error K and error J. Write the frame on a card: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." Have the day 64 tables, both records, the question board and the exit slips ready. Keep key-exits-65.md away until slips are in. No new content: it applies days 45 and 64.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 64's slips (s03-E64), with the tables and sentences back (s03-P64a, s03-P64b, s03-P64d) and a written cue; the folder page too (s03-P64c). Whole class, the more-missed. Use the fish stall card from day 64 (made up), one card only. Error K: "the stall is good" beside "the card shows fish brought by boats that morning". Pairs say the detail back to each other, one minute. Error J: "all markets work this way" beside "this market, in this report, does". If J was more-missed, K waits for day 68's K group; if K was, the rest of J is heard in day 66's cue reading.

#### 4 min. The frame, read once
Review: you wrote a claim on a frame on day 45 and compared two records on day 64. Read the card aloud once, then take it down. Children keep their day 64 tables in front of them.

#### 8 min. Say it first
Pairs say a claim aloud from the Local Production Record: how does our town get what it needs? Each child says it to a partner, then again to a new partner, about 90 seconds a round. Hear a rotating six pairs.

#### 10 min. Write one sentence
Each child writes [[s03-P65a]] on the student page, or tells it to an adult who writes it down. The detail comes from the table. A child who is stuck looks at the table. 

#### 6 min. Share and revise
Pairs swap pages and read each other's sentence aloud. Each child then writes [[s03-P65b]], and [[s03-P65c]] from the Techiman Traders Record. The partner checks each detail is in its record. Each child changes one word and writes [[s03-P65d]]. Hear a rotating six pairs.

#### 4 min. Our answer
Review: the question board (day 45, day 64). Build the class answer, two supported emphases side by side: what our town's record shows about how people here get what they need, and what the Techiman Traders Record shows. No ranking of places, no percent. One market is not all of Ghana; a dated report supports only its time; two cards are not all of our town.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the quarter slip with the Greengrocer and Depot cards, first seen here. Read the cards and the ask once. Children write [[s03-E65]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The repair gives one detail from one made-up card; pairs write a claim from one real record; the exit asks for a claim from two new made-up places.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the table on the board; read each record aloud twice. The question board is chart paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tables: read each record aloud twice.

**Skip.** The share.

- **5 min. Look back.** Read both records aloud. Say what each shows.
- **25 min. Write.** Your child writes [[s03-P65a]], then [[s03-P65b]], [[s03-P65c]] and [[s03-P65d]].
  **Say.** "What does one market not tell us?" Expect: what every market is like.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the cards and the ask once. [[s03-E65]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one detail from each record, said aloud, not written. Longer: write a second sentence naming what the records cannot tell.

### Words and supports
Words: claim, because, detail, record, shows. Frame: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___." Pointing, drawing and dictation are all answers. Read any item aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 66 closes the class savings ledger at Kept $80 and revises the day 49, 50 and 60 records from their cues; the rest of error J is heard in the cue reading. Error K from today's slips goes to day 68's K group.

### Credit
Ours. The fish stall, Greengrocer and Depot are made up. Facts about Techiman are from a Ghanaian newspaper (2023), in our words, not quoted.
