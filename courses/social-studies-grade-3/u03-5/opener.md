# Section 3.5 opener: bringing it together

## What the section does

Four days of 40 minutes. Day 65 writes one claim sentence about how our town and Techiman get what they need, using one detail from the Local Production Record and a different detail from the Techiman Traders Record, and revises one word. Day 66 closes the class savings ledger and revises three records: the day 49 record (who else needs an answer), the day 50 record (two readings of "needs") and the day 60 cue (interest and two offers). The unit plan labels day 66 Fluency, but the content is revision. Day 67 is the assessment: the packet, read aloud, with the revised records scored. Day 68 hands the results back in small groups and redoes one fresh item for each missed idea.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A claim needs a detail from a source.** Two sources, one detail from each. A liking or a title is not a detail.
- **Revision is cue, then change.** The cue written on days 49, 50 and 60 is read on day 66. The child revises, the revision is scored on day 67 as an end item.
- **Interest closes the ledger.** The class ledger ends at $80 in, $0 out. Interest is payment for using money.

## The errors in plain words

K is the idea that a source can be named without a detail from it. AC treats a checkable fact as only an opinion. The 3.1 to 3.4 letters return on day 68 as redo cards.

## Supplies

The day 66 revision sheets, the packet (read aloud), the redo cards for day 68, exit cards.

## A room with nothing

Chart paper, the child's own earlier slips, the teacher reading the packet aloud once. A parent may sit in on days 67 and 68; nothing on the packet names a family or a country.
