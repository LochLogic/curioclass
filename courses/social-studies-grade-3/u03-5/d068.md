# Day 68: Fix it and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class
Sort the children into four groups from the day 67 marks by biggest gap, at most eight each, and write the group on each note. Copy the written moves, four groups (written-moves-day68.md), one for each group. Keep key-day68.md away until the redo is in. The cards are new cases, not on day 67.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 67's assessment (the packet and answer sheet, s03-Q1 to s03-Q8) and the revised records, each with a written note. Each child reads the note and marks the biggest gap.

#### 25 min. Grouped reteach
Meet each group for 6 minutes in turn, one minute in all to move; the other groups work in pairs from their written move. At about 45 seconds a child, each child in a group of eight is heard once. Groups follow the component table: 1 choices, rewards and resources (Q1, Q2, Q3); 2 money, profit and interest (Q4, Q5, interest); 3 banks and trade (Q6, Q7); 4 claims and sources (Q8, who needs to know, fact or view). A ninth child joins the group of the next-biggest gap. Group 1 takes written moves 1 and 2, group 2 move 3, group 3 move 4, group 4 move 5.
Group 1: look at the missed answer, then say the idea back: what is given up, what the reward is, which kind of resource (s03-Q1b, s03-Q2b, s03-Q3b).
Group 2: say why money helps, what a sale leaves after costs, and how much more one offer pays for the same $100 and year (s03-Q4, s03-Q5b).
Group 3: match a need to a bank, a credit union or insurance; say how a maker and a supplier rely on each other (s03-Q6b, s03-Q7b).
Group 4: the K group. Link two fresh details to a claim and retry (s03-Q8b, s03-E65, s03-P65a). Then a one-minute sort of a fact and a view, and who else needs to know. A child who missed most items: the adult goes over the missed items together instead, and redoes one aloud.

#### 10 min. Fresh redo
Each child answers the card for their group on the student page, alone, and hands it in: [[s03-F68a]], [[s03-F68b]], [[s03-F68c]], [[s03-F68d]], [[s03-F68e]], [[s03-F68i]], [[s03-F68f]], [[s03-F68g]], [[s03-F68h]], [[s03-F68j]], [[s03-F68k]]. One card each. Read each card once. This is a redo, not scored on the record sheet; it is judged and handed back. A child who needs it speaks to an adult at the side table.

**Shared shape.** The group sorts use the child's own packet answers; each redo card is a new made-up case on the same idea, not on day 67.

### Other rooms
No printer: write each card on the board; children answer on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. Skip the groups.

**Skip.** The record sheet.

- **5 min. Note.** Read the note together. **Say.** "Which gap is the biggest?" Expect: one named idea.
- **20 min. Fix.** Go over the missed answers together. Say each in your own words.
- **10 min. Redo.** Pick the one card for that gap. Read it once. Your child answers alone: [[s03-F68a]] to [[s03-F68k]].

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one missed answer, one card. Longer: write a new made-up card for the same idea.

### Words and supports
Words: gap, redo, claim, detail, interest, opportunity cost. Frame: "I changed my answer because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 69 begins the course check: read a town map with its legend, directions and scale, find a place on a world map, and name one power of a leader at each level of government. Today's redo cards come back with a note.

### Credit
Ours. The redo cards are made up, for practice.
