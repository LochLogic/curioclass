# Written moves, day 68

One for each of five groups. Letters first seen on day 67 are used here.

1. **Choices and savings** (Y, Z, C, D, B, AD): the adult goes over the Sketch Card and the savings ledger.
2. **Rewards and money** (Q, E, A, W, AA): the Wall Card and the Weaver Card.
3. **Exchange and profit** (B, F, M, H, AB, O): the Roof Card and the Lantern and chime.
4. **Banks and trade** (G, N, P, I, R): the Town Card and the Chair Card.
5. **Claims and sources** (K, J, AC, T): the Post Office and Farm Stand cards, the Elm and Ash card, the Street Tree Card and the Honey Card.

One child's moves: the adult goes over the missed items together and redoes one orally. E65's K repair goes to the fifth group.
