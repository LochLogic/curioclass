# Teacher key, course check 1 (days 69 and 70)

Teacher only. Keep apart from packet-check1.md. Score each part once: 2 when every look-for appears, 1 when some appear without a false claim, 0 when absent or contradicted. Write the missing part by name (map, grid, leaders, choice, claim), not a total. Spelling and handwriting are not scored. Read each answer for meaning.

- Day 69 read-aloud notes. Card 1: a landmark is a place people know and visit, here the old mill; a physical feature is made by nature. Card 2: the equator is the line around the middle of Earth; the prime meridian is the line that splits east from west; places near the equator get more direct sunlight than places near a pole.
- E1: circles the triangle. Not the blue line (picked by its look, not the legend).
- E1b: south. Not east or "right" (the page's right taken as east).
- E1c: about 3 bars. Not "3 steps".
- E1d: Linden Creek (the wavy blue line). Not the school or mill.
- E1e: the old mill. Not the creek.
- E2: B2. Not 2B.
- E2b: northern. E2c: western.
- E2d: K. Not L. E2e: more direct sunlight near the equator. Not a reason about rain.
- E3: a town job such as making town policy, proposing the town budget or picking a town manager (sources U2H, U2R). Not a job for the whole country.
- E3b: leads the state's executive branch and carries out state laws (sources U2A, U2D). Not a country-wide job.
- E3c: leads the executive branch and carries out the country's laws (source U2A). Not "decides court cases".
- Day 70 read-aloud notes. Card 4: tax money is the money the town collects; a cost is what is paid or given up; a benefit is a good thing a choice gives.
- E4: tax money for the playground, or the lot. Not a good thing.
- E4b: a good thing, such as a place for children to play. Not a cost.
- E4c: the parking lot. Not "the $20,000".
- E5: a claim that answers, such as families use the library at night. Not "I like the library".
- E5b: 240 people came, 90 of them children, or the late hours. Not a detail the card does not say.
- E5c: the Pinewick Library; families in the town. Not "a child".
