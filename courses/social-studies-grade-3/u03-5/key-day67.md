# Teacher key, day 67

Teacher only. Never given to children.

- Q1: A benefit such as display space for benches. Q1b: The sign, the next-best choice. Q1c: Lee has only $28, and each one costs $28. Not "Lee can buy both" (Y); not "everything else is the cost" (Z); not "Lee is lazy" (C).
- Q2: The $3 off for collecting on time. Q2b: The $2 storage fee for late collection. Q2c: Collect on time. It does not force anyone. Not a mixed pair (Q); not "it forces" (E).
- Q3: Natural. Q3b: Human. Q3c: Capital. Not "the saw is money" (A).
- Q4: Farah can pay money, and the bread seller takes money, so the bread seller need not want a chair repair. Not "money means nobody gives anything up" (B).
- Q5: $7: $25 less $18. Q5b: $4: $10 less $6. Q5c: Lee may make more benches, which leave more, if buyers will pay; a reasoned other choice counts. Not "the $25 price is the profit" (F); not the highest price wins (M).
- Q6: A credit union: its members own it, and they save and borrow there. Q6b: A bank, which keeps savings and lends; Omar does not want to own any part of it. Q6c: Insurance, which pays covered losses from shared payments. Q6d: It is owned by its members, who save and borrow there. Not W, AA, G or N.
- Q7: The hinges. Q7b: Lee's orders, and the US-made tools it buys. Q7c: More orders each way mean each needs the other more. Not an arrow alone (P); not "imports end local work" (I).
- Q8: A defensible claim that links the two sources to getting needs, with a detail from each. Q8b: A detail from A, such as Lee repairs chairs and makes benches. Q8c: A different detail from B, such as women run the market in groups, one for each kind of goods, or it began in 1944 beside a palace. Not a liking or a title only (K).
- Records, scored 2, 1 or 0 on each look-for from the day 66 revision (not before): Q9: The $8 offer. Q9b: $3 more, for the same $100 and year. Q9c: Payment for using money. Q10: A named group and a reason, such as workers needing supplies. Q11: Supplies today, or saving for later repairs, each with a reason; another defensible pair of readings with reasons also counts.
