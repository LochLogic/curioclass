# Fresh redo cards, day 68

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. One item for each group. Not scored on the record sheet.

**Sketch Card.** A child has $9. A harmonica costs $9. A sketchbook costs $9. The child buys the harmonica. Sketch Card, invented. The child buys the harmonica. What is given up?

**Wall Card.** A climbing wall gives a free turn to anyone who helps pack the ropes. It charges $2 when a rope is left out. Wall Card, invented. Which is the reward?

**Weaver Card.** A weaver uses a loom and her coins. Weaver Card, invented. Name the capital resource.

**Roof Card.** A roof mender wants cheese. A cheese maker wants a wall painted. Roof Card, invented. Why does money help the roof mender get cheese?

**Lantern and chime.** Lantern: cost $9, sells $11. Chime: cost $2, sells $7. Which leaves more?

**Town Card.** A town needs a safe place for savings and a way to pay for hail damage to roofs. Town Card, invented. Which need could a bank help with?

**Chair Card.** A chair maker buys wood from a wood yard, which sells wood to the maker. Chair Card, invented. How do the chair maker and wood yard rely on each other?

**Post Office and Farm Stand cards.** The post office has a sorting counter. The farm stand has tables under a roof. Post Office and Farm Stand cards. Write one likeness with a detail from each.

**Elm and Ash.** Elm pays $4 on $100. Ash pays $9. How much more does Ash pay?

**Street Tree Card.** A town plants shade trees. Street Tree Card, invented. A town plants shade trees. Who besides walkers needs to know?

**Honey Card.** A jar of honey costs $4. Sam says that is too much. Honey Card, invented. A jar costs $4. Sam says too much. Which is a fact?
