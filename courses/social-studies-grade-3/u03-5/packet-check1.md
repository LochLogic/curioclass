# Course check 1: the cards for days 69 and 70

Print each card on its own page. Every card is made up, for practice: print "made up, for practice" at the bottom of each. The teacher reads each card aloud once, just before its questions. The key is in key-check1.md (teacher only).

## Day 69, card 1: Wren Hollow Map

A small map of a made-up town, Wren Hollow. The north arrow points to the left edge of the page. The legend: a star is the library; a square is the school; a triangle is the old mill, a landmark; a circle is the market; a wavy blue line is Linden Creek. The library is in the middle of the page. The school is up the page from the library, 3 bars away. The market is on the right side of the page from the library. The old mill stands beside the creek, near the top. The scale bar shows one bar, about one block.

## Day 69, card 2: World Grid Card and World Warm Card

A flat world outline with six columns lettered A to F from west to east and three rows numbered 1 to 3 from south to north. The equator is a line across the card. The prime meridian is a line down the card between columns C and D. A dot marks Mexico City, Mexico, in column B, row 2, above the equator line and left of the prime meridian. Credit: Mexico City's place on the globe from its city page (source 4G).

World Warm Card: the same outline with two dots. Dot K sits near the equator. Dot L sits near the South Pole.
