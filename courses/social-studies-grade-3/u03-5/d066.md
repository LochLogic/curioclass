# Day 66: Finish the plan, fix the records

## Teacher page

### Before class
Cues are on the Clinic exit practice record (day 49), the supporting-question record (day 50) and the day 60 record (cue sheet). Fold the day 66 savings sheet so only its last three lines show (revise day 49, revise day 50, the one change); the savings lines are on the student page. Redraw the class ledger and goal bar with three lines in: Kept $20, $40, $60, and the fourth $20 box empty. Nothing is scored today; only the revisions are scored, on day 67.

### The day

#### 5 min. Cues
Hand back day 65's slips (s03-E65) and sentence checks (s03-P65b, s03-P65c, s03-P65d). Hear the rest of error J (s03-P64c): "one market is not all of Ghana" beside "this report shows one market", one minute. Then each child reads the cues on the day 49, 50 and 60 records and stars the one that matters most. Review: you learned to revise from a written cue on day 46.

#### 10 min. Inquiry revisions
Children revise in the second colour. Four minutes on the day 49 record (s03-E49, s03-E49b): name a group and a reason. Six minutes on the day 50 record (s03-P50a, s03-P50b, s03-P50c, s03-P50d): two readings of "needs" and the link to the big question. A record whose look-fors are all present stays as it is. Write the one change and why on the sheet.

#### 5 min. A fresh savings try
Review: the two-bank table from day 60 and the ledger lines from days 53 to 61. A made-up ledger and two made-up banks, read aloud once. Each child writes [[s03-P66a]], [[s03-P66b]], [[s03-P66c]] and [[s03-P66d]].

#### 5 min. Teacher written check
Every child checks their own fresh try against three look-fors on the student page and ticks yes, partly or not yet. Then collect the sheets. After class, write present, partly or missing beside each look-for and return the sheet with the cue inside the day 67 packet (s03-P66a to s03-P66e). Children with a not yet tick are seen first in the next part.

#### 10 min. Independent savings revision
Each child reads the day 60 cue and revises their interest sentence (s03-P60a, s03-P60b) in the second colour: who pays and who gets it; deposit and interest kept apart. Then they check their class ledger lines. Confer first with any child who ticked not yet.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
The fourth $20 goes in. Each child writes [[s03-P66e]]. Then read the unit's words aloud once: scarcity, opportunity cost, reward, penalty, money, profit, bank, credit union, insurance, interest, natural, human and capital resources, goods and services, trade, relies on. Pairs pick one word and say it in their own words, 30 seconds each way.

**Shared shape.** There is no exit ticket today. The two offers on the student page are the offers the day 67 record asks about: this sheet is the record that day 67 judges, so the practice and that judgment share a case on purpose.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the ledger and the two offers on the board; children answer on paper. Use pencil and pen for the two colours.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. Read the cues together.

**Skip.** The teacher check.

- **5 min. Read.** Your child reads each cue aloud. **Say.** "Which cue helps most?" Expect: one named change.
- **15 min. Fix.** Your child changes one thing in each record, in a second colour.
- **15 min. Try.** Read the ledger and the two banks aloud once. Your child writes [[s03-P66a]], [[s03-P66b]], [[s03-P66c]], [[s03-P66d]] and [[s03-P66e]].

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: revise the day 60 sentence only. Longer: write what you would tell a friend about interest.

### Words and supports
Words: cue, revise, ledger, interest, deposit, goal. Frame: "I changed ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read any item aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 67 is the end-of-unit assessment: a packet read aloud once, eight prompts, unaided. Today's revised records come back inside it. Nothing new is taught.

### Credit
Ours. The ledger, Bank Moss and Bank Reed are made up.
