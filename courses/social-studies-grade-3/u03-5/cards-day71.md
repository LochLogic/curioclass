# Day 71: fresh cards for the four groups

Every card is made up, for practice; print that at the bottom. Group A is map and world grid, group B is leaders, group C is cost and choice, group D is claim and source. The group practice asks are spoken and use the first card of each group. The exit slip uses the second card (the Exit Card) and is written; print it on a half slip with the two exit asks, [[sc1-X71]] and [[sc1-X71b]].

## Group A: map and world grid

Practice card, Fox Pond Map. The north arrow points to the right edge of the page. The pond is on the left side of the page from the farm. Ask (spoken): [[sc1-P71a]]

Exit Card A. Larch Lake Map. The arrow points up. Legend: a flag is the post office; a diamond is the old school, a landmark; a wavy line is the lake. The school is 2 bars from the post office. The card also holds a world outline with the grid of card 2 and one dot for Cape Town, South Africa, in column D, row 1 (source 4F).

## Group B: leaders

Practice card, Fresh Level Card. A state road plan is signed. Ask (spoken): [[sc1-P71b]]

Exit Card B. Ms. Okoye is the governor of Lakeshore, a made-up state. She signs a plan to fix a state highway. The mayor of Cedar Pond, a made-up town, signs a plan for a town sidewalk.

## Group C: cost and choice

Practice card, Fresh Choice Card. A club can run a bake sale or a car wash. It picks the bake sale. Ask (spoken): [[sc1-P71c]]

Exit Card C. Lakeview, a made-up town, has $5,000 for its library. It can buy new books or a new door. It picks the books.

## Group D: claim and source

Practice card, Fresh Source Card. Town Pool Notice, July 2025, made by the pool for swimmers. "Last week 120 people swam. 50 of them were children." Ask (spoken): [[sc1-P71d]]

Exit Card D. Park Board Notice, June 2025, made by the park board for neighbors. "The splash pad opened May 1. By June 1, 600 people had used it. 200 of them were children."

## The exit slip, half page

Say: use the Exit Card for your group. Each child writes [[sc1-X71]] and [[sc1-X71b]].
