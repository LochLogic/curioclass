# Day 87: review card sets, fronts only

Four rounds of six minutes. Tables hold at most eight children. Pairs use these self-checking cards, with the answer face down (the backs are in key-day87-review.md, a separate piece). All cases are new; none is in an exit or in the day 88 packet.

## Round 1: the nations

**Nations Card.** Four names: Maine, Penobscot, Rhode Island, Narragansett. Write N by each Native nation and S by each state.

## Round 2: the sort

**Ferry Day cards, made up, for practice.** The question: "Who rode the ferry on its first day?"

- Ticket Stub. Kept by a rider on the first day.
- Ferry Poster. Printed before the first day by the ferry company.
- Town History. Written in 2015 by a historian who was not there.

For each card say: primary or secondary, and why.

## Round 3: the map

**Reedham Card, made up, for practice.** In the made-up town of Reedham, people cut reeds at a marsh each spring. The town craft shop makes baskets from the reeds.

Write one chain: People ______, so they lived near ______.

## Round 4: what we gave and how we govern

Four cards from earlier days: the Penobscot River unit, the Carol A. Dana card, the Eastern Band card and the Navajo Nation card.

Name a nation and one thing it shares. Name one way a tribal government governs.
