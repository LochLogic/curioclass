# Day 87: Review before the check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Set four tables of at most eight and put one set of review cards, fronts only, at each. Put the teacher key, day 87 review cards (key-day87-review.md), in your folder. Return each child's day 72 exit card with its written note, and day 86's slips with their notes. Take down filled organizers; leave the blank strips and the frame. Keep key-exits-86-87.md out until the slips are in.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback and revision
Hand back the day 72 exit (s04-E72, s04-E72b) with its written note, and day 86's slips and written task (s04-E86, s04-E86b, s04-P86a to s04-P86e, s04-P86g to s04-P86i), each marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Each child reads the notes. Anyone whose supporting-question check was missing an idea or link retries it at the first table, unscored, during round 1.

#### 5 min. Importance revision
Each child rewrites the day 72 answer in a pencil of a second color: a named group and a reason: [[s04-P87a]]. This revised card is what the unit check scores for its last item. Scribe allowed.

#### 28 min. Review
One minute to start, then four rounds of six minutes, a minute to move between. Pairs at a table check each other with the cards, answer face down. At each round you sit at one table and hear the six children whose exits show most gaps; a record column names them, and each is heard about 45 seconds. Round 1, the nations: [[s04-P87b]]. Round 2, the sort: the Ferry Day cards, [[s04-P87c]]. Round 3, the map: the Reedham Card, [[s04-P87d]]. Round 4, what we share and how we govern: [[s04-P87e]]. These are reviews of work you taught on days 75 to 85; nothing is taught as new.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Aquinnah Locator, first seen here. Read the picture and both asks once. Every child writes [[s04-E87]] and [[s04-E87b]]. Key: key-exits-86-87.md. Mark each shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The review rounds use made-up and earlier cards at tables; the exit gives one new map with a wrong label and asks the child to fix it and point.

### Other rooms
No tables: pairs take the four rounds one after another from the cards; keep the backs with the adult.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each round's question on paper.

**Skip.** The second color.

- **7 min. Your note.** Read the note on the day 72 card together. [[s04-P87a]] **Say.** "Who else needs the answer to our big question?" Expect: a named group and a reason.
- **25 min. Four rounds.** Read each round, then [[s04-P87b]] [[s04-P87c]] [[s04-P87d]] [[s04-P87e]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the map once. [[s04-E87]] [[s04-E87b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: two rounds. Longer: a child writes one new card for a round.

### Words and supports
Words: nation, homeland, primary, secondary, chain, share, government. Frame: "The ___ Nation ___. The source is ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 88 is the unit check, from records the class has not seen. Bring a pencil; answer alone; any prompt may be passed.

### Credit
Ours. Facts: the earlier cards named in round 4. The Ferry Day and Reedham cards are made up.
