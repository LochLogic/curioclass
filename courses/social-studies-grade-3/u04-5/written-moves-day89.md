# Written moves, day 89

One for each of four groups. At most eight children in a group. Six minutes each, with one minute to move. One diagnosed letter for each child.

1. **Nations and maps** (A, B, S, T, J, K): the adult goes over the Name Bank Card, the Reed Park Map and the Pair Card.
2. **Land, place and removal** (C, L, I, P): the adult goes over the Fish Camp. Card 1 of the day 78 Cherokee cards is read aloud once, plainly, with no detail. A child may pass.
3. **Sources** (D, M, E, Q): the adult goes over the Harbor Race Records.
4. **Claims, giving and governing** (H, V, R, G, O, U, F): the adult goes over the two nation cards, the Language Card and the Park Display. Q8 on day 88 is the first check for H and V.

The adult goes over the missed items together and redoes one orally.
