# Day 88: Unit 4 check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Make the packet from packet-day88.md: pages 1 to 4, and page 5 with each child's own revised day 72 exit. Records A and C print "made up, for practice". Check that no page holds a key. Keep the teacher key (key-day88.md) in your folder. Take down everything from days 72 to 87; no packet record has been taught.

### The day

#### 5 min. Records
Hand out the packets and answer sheets. Say: answer alone, pass any prompt you like, no one helps, each prompt is read once. Read Records A, B, C and D aloud once, about sixty words each, without explaining anything. A and C are made up.

#### 30 min. Eight unaided prompts
Read each prompt once at its start, with a spoken pace cue at each change and no clock shown.

Q2, 3 minutes: [[s04-Q2]].

Q6, 3 minutes: [[s04-Q6]], [[s04-Q6b]].

Q8, 6 minutes: [[s04-Q8]], [[s04-Q8b]], [[s04-Q8c]].
A child who needs to point and speak answers to an adult in a separate slot of 2 minutes after the period, at most 6, with the answers written down. Q9, 2 minutes in the last part: [[s04-Q9]], [[s04-Q9b]]. Children answer them from their own revised day 72 exit on page 5; they are scored from that card, not from a new answer.

#### 5 min. Collect and check
Children answer Q9 and Q9b, reread and finish. Collect the packets and answer sheets with a name on each, and check that every sheet is in.

### Marking
After class mark every item 2, 1 or 0 on the day 88 score sheet with key-day88.md; spelling and handwriting are not scored. About 3 minutes a child (ours, untested). Record results by part on the unit record sheet and by letter for day 89's groups.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the packet pages by hand on paper; keep each page short.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. Nothing here asks about your home.

**Skip.** The sheets for the teacher.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Read each packet record aloud once, then each prompt once. Write your child's answer word for word. Do not help. **Say.** "How have the Native nations of our region shaped this place?" Expect: a claim with a detail from each of two records.
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: none; every part is asked once. Longer: your child tells one thing they would change in an answer.

### Words and supports
Words: nation, homeland, season, contribution, source, primary, secondary, claim, detail. Frame: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___." Read aloud; enlarge type and maps; pointing, speech and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 89 hands back the packet with a note and a letter, reteaches in groups, and gives one fresh redo item. The largest group meets first.

### Credit
Ours. Records A and C are made up. Records B and D give facts from the Narragansett Indian Tribe's and the Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin's own websites, in our own words.
