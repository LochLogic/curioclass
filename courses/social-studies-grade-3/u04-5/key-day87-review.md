# Teacher key, the day 87 review cards

Teacher only. The backs of the self-checking cards. Never given to children.

- Nations Card: Penobscot N, Narragansett N, Maine S, Rhode Island S. A nation is a people with their own government; a state is a part of our country. Not a state named as a nation (A).
- Ferry Day cards: the Ticket Stub is primary (a rider who was there). The Ferry Poster is not primary for who rode. The Town History is secondary (looks back). Reasons are about the maker. Not "old means primary" (D).
- Reedham Card: People cut reeds and make baskets in spring, so they lived near the marsh. Not "the marsh decided" (C); not "there is a marsh" (L).
- Round 4: any nation with something its own page shows it shares; any tribal government with who leads it, taken from the earlier cards. Not "every nation shared the same thing" (G).
