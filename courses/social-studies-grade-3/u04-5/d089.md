# Day 89: Fix it and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class
From the day 88 marks, sort the children into four groups of at most eight by their largest gap and write the group and one letter on each note (the groups are in written-moves-day89.md: nations and maps; land, place and removal; sources; claims, giving and governing). Copy the written moves, one for each group. Keep key-day89.md away until the redo is in. The redo cards are new cases, not on day 88. Offer enlarged high-contrast maps and tactile markers.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 88's assessment (the packet and answer sheet, s04-Q1 to s04-Q9b) and day 87's review sheet and exit (s04-P87a, s04-P87b, s04-P87c, s04-P87d, s04-P87e, s04-E87, s04-E87b), each with a written note and a letter if there is one. Each child reads the note and marks the biggest gap.

#### 25 min. Grouped reteach
Meet each group for 6 minutes in turn, one minute in all to move; the groups not with you work in pairs from their written move. At about 45 seconds a child, each child in a group of eight is heard once. Group 1 goes over the Name Bank Card, the Reed Park Map and the Pair Card. Group 2 goes over the Fish Camp; read Card 1 of the day 78 Cherokee cards aloud once, plainly and briefly, a child may pass. Group 3 goes over the Harbor Race Records. Group 4 goes over the Language Card, the two nation cards and the Park Display; H and V (Q8) take the two cards first. A child who missed most goes over the missed items with you instead and redoes one aloud.

#### 10 min. Fresh redo
Each child answers the new item for the component they missed, alone, and hands it in: [[s04-F89a]], [[s04-F89b]], [[s04-F89c]], [[s04-F89d]], [[s04-F89e]], [[s04-F89f]], [[s04-F89g]], [[s04-F89h]], [[s04-F89i]], [[s04-F89j]], [[s04-F89k]], [[s04-F89l]], [[s04-F89m]], [[s04-F89n]], [[s04-F89o]], [[s04-F89p]], [[s04-F89q]], [[s04-F89r]]. Read each card once. One component each; Q8 for those with H or V. This is a redo, not scored on the record sheet; it is judged and handed back. A child who needs it speaks to an adult at the side table.

**Shared shape.** The group work uses the child's own day 88 answers; each redo card is a new case on the same idea, not on day 88.

### Other rooms
No printer: write each card on the board; children answer on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. Skip the groups.

**Skip.** The record sheet.

- **5 min. Note.** Read the note together. **Say.** "Which gap is the biggest?" Expect: one named idea.
- **20 min. Fix.** Go over the missed answers together. Say each in your own words.
- **10 min. Redo.** Pick the one card for that gap. Read it once. Your child answers alone: [[s04-F89a]] to [[s04-F89r]].

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one missed answer, one card. Longer: write a new made-up card for the same idea.

### Words and supports
Words: gap, redo, claim, detail, homeland, source. Frame: "I changed my answer because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 90 opens unit 5 with a question and a retrieval task. Today's redo cards come back with a note.

### Credit
Ours. The Fish Camp, Reed Park Map, Harbor Race Records and Park Display cards are made up, for practice. The Pair Card and Language Card use the Mashpee Wampanoag Tribe's and the Cherokee Nation's own pages, in our words.
