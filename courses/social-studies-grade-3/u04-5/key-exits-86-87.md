# Teacher key, exit cards days 86 and 87

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-86-87.md); never hand to children.

- Day 86, ask 1: A claim about how the town uses its river, such as the town shares its knowledge of the river with its neighbors. Not a liking (x); not "the cards prove my answer" (H). Ask 2: A detail from card A, such as the town's land lies along the river. Not a title only (H). Extra: a different detail from card B, such as the town teaches its neighbors river travel. Not a second detail from card A (V).
- Day 87, ask 1: The markers are common lands that the Tribe's page names, not the whole homeland. Not "the markers are the whole homeland" (T). Ask 2: The shading across the whole island, where the page says ancestors have lived, not only the markers. Not the three markers (T).

Marking: shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. The claim on day 86 has three look-fors: it answers the stated question, it names a detail from each of two records, and it gives a reason.
