# Exit cards, days 86 and 87

Print "made up, for practice" in small letters on the made-up cards. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken. Reed River is a made-up town. Say so aloud, and do not use a real nation's details.

## Day 86: Reed River cards, made up, for practice

**Card A. The Reed River Town Council's map, made up.**

```
   +--------------------------------+
   |   ::::::::::::::::::::::::     |
   |   ~~~ Reed River ~~~~~~~~~     |
   |   ::::::::::::::::::::::::     |
   +--------------------------------+
   ::::  = the town's land
```

**Card B. A museum account, made up.** The Reed River Museum says Reed River town teaches river travel to its neighbors. Every summer visitors learn from the town's own guides.

Ask 1: Reed River cards, invented. Write a claim about how the town uses its river.
Ask 2: Reed River cards. Give one detail from card A that supports your claim.

Extra, in the Exit box: Reed River cards. Give a different detail from card B.

## Day 87: Aquinnah Locator

```
   NOEPE (the island)
   ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
   ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
   [#] [#] [#] ::::::::::::::::::::
   ::::  = homeland         [#]  = common lands, named on its page
   Label: "These three markers are the whole homeland."
```

The three markers read: Gay Head Cliffs, Herring Creek, Lobsterville.

Ask 1: Aquinnah Locator. The label calls three markers the whole homeland. Fix the label.
Ask 2: Aquinnah Locator. Point to where ancestors have lived.
