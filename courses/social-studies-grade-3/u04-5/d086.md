# Day 86: Our claim, with two sources

## Teacher page

### Before class
Write the five supporting questions on the board and, beside them, the words shaped, settle and contribution. Print the frame card and the slip. Keep key-exits-86-87.md out until the slips are in.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval and supporting-question check
Hand back day 85's slips (s04-E85, s04-E85b) and written task (s04-P85a to s04-P85f) with the written feedback, read and not redone. Then read aloud: "Pick one of our five questions. Name a word in it that can be read two ways, say the two ways, and say how its answer helps the big question." Children write [[s04-P86a]], [[s04-P86b]] and [[s04-P86c]] on the check card. Scan the cards. A missing idea or link gets a written note and an unscored retry on day 87. Day 85's errors Q and E wait for day 87 or day 89.

#### 8 min. Choose evidence
Model choosing, never a finished claim. Hold up the Local Homeland Record, the day 72 nation card, and think aloud: which detail shows the Nation along the river? Then the day 83 contribution card: which detail shows it sharing today? Say a detail comes from one record, and two lines from one card are one source. Children write [[s04-P86d]] and [[s04-P86e]].

#### 10 min. Say a claim
Pairs say a claim aloud: "I think ___." Each partner says back the other's emphasis in their own words, without arguing: [[s04-P86f]]. Emphases to hear: knowledge, settlement, governing. Hear six pairs, a minute each; a record column names who was heard. Nothing is written.

#### 12 min. Write the claim and revise
Review: you wrote claims with a detail from each of two sources in unit 1, and used reasoning to say how a detail supports a claim. Each child writes the frame "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___" with a detail from each record: [[s04-P86g]]. After hearing another emphasis, each child changes one thing: [[s04-P86h]]. Scribe or dictation allowed. Mark as before: it answers the question, names a detail from each of two records, and gives a reason.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Reed River cards, first seen here. Say Reed River is a made-up town. Read the cards and asks once. Every child writes [[s04-E86]] and [[s04-E86b]]. Children then write [[s04-P86i]] in the Exit box on the student page. Key: key-exits-86-87.md. Mark each shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The model and practice choose details from the class's own two records; the exit gives two new made-up cards from one town and asks a claim and a detail.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the frame on the board; read the records aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed frame: write it on paper.

**Skip.** The saying aloud in pairs; say your claim to your child instead.

- **8 min. The check.** [[s04-P86a]] [[s04-P86b]] [[s04-P86c]] **Say.** "Which word can be read two ways?" Expect: shaped, settle or contribution.
- **10 min. Choose.** [[s04-P86d]] [[s04-P86e]]
- **14 min. The claim.** [[s04-P86g]] [[s04-P86h]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Reed River cards once. [[s04-E86]] [[s04-E86b]] [[s04-P86i]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the claim only, [[s04-P86g]]. Longer: write a claim from the other emphasis too.

### Words and supports
Words: claim, detail, evidence, emphasis, source. Frame: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 87 returns the written note on the day 72 exit, revises it, and reviews the nations, the sort, the map and what we share and how we govern. Missed checks get an unscored retry.

Ours. Facts: the Penobscot Nation's curriculum pages. The Reed River cards are made up.
