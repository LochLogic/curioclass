## The two nation cards (the Pair Card, the Language Card and the two nation cards)

**Card 1. The Mashpee Wampanoag Tribe.** The Mashpee Wampanoag Tribe is also called the People of the First Light. Its people have lived in what is now Massachusetts and eastern Rhode Island for more than 12,000 years. The Tribe's office is in Mashpee, Massachusetts. The Tribe runs a Language department. It holds a weekly lunch and language event. Source: the Tribe's own website. The page lists a Tribal Council.

**Card 2. The Cherokee Nation.** The Cherokee Nation once held a large territory across much of the Southeast. Today the Cherokee Nation uses its treaty rights and its own authority over the Cherokee Nation Reservation in eastern Oklahoma. Source: the Cherokee Nation's own page.

Ask: Pair Card. Name one way the two homelands differ.
Ask: Pair Card. Name one way one nation governs itself today.
Ask: Language Card. Name one contribution the Nation's page shows.
Ask: Language Card. Name the source that shows it.
Ask: Two nation cards. Write one claim that answers our unit question.
Ask: Two nation cards. Give a detail from card 1.
Ask: Two nation cards. Give a different detail from card 2.
