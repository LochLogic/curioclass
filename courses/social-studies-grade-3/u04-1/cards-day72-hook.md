# Day 72 hook: the nearest nation's card

For the teacher. Print a town physical map and one card of about 50 words from the nearest nation's own page. If the school is not near the Penobscot Nation, make the card from the nearest nation's own pages in the same way. Use its own name, the present tense, and say where the words come from. Do not invent a card. Do not name a child's family or home.

## Sample card, for a school in the Northeast

The Penobscot Nation says the Penobscot River is the heart of Penobscot culture. For thousands of years the Penobscots have used Maine's rivers for food, drinking water and travel. Today the Nation has a Chief and a Council. Its offices are at Indian Island, Maine.

Read aloud: "These are words from the Penobscot Nation's own pages."

## What the children do

Children set the map beside the card. Each says what the map shows and what the card shows. Then they say what each one tells about who shapes this place.
