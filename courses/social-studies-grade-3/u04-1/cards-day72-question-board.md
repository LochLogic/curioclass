# The question board, for the teacher

Post this on day 72 and keep it up to day 86.

**Big question:** How have the Native nations of our region shaped this place, then and now?

**Supporting questions, with their days:**

1. Whose homelands are here? (day 75)
2. Why settle here? (day 82)
3. What contributions continue? (day 83)
4. Who governs today? (day 84)
5. How can sources help us know? (day 85)

On day 72 the class writes these together. Children pick one for their own record. Pairs read the word "shaped" two ways: it changed the land, or it shared knowledge of the land.
