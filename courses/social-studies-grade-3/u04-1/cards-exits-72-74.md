# Exit cards, days 72 to 74

Print "made up, for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken. The day 72 card is kept in the folder and comes back on day 87.

## Day 72: Library Display Card

A library display tells about a nation near us. It speaks only of the past. Families come to learn about their region.

Ask 1: Library Display Card, invented. Who besides our class needs our big question answered?
Ask 2: Library Display Card. Why does that group need it?

## Day 73: Meeting Records Card

Record 1 is a drawing. A person sitting in the town meeting made it that evening. Record 2 is a history. A historian who was not there wrote it years later.

Ask 1: Meeting Records Card, invented. For "Who came?", which record is primary?
Ask 2: Meeting Records Card. Why is that record primary?

## Day 74: Snow Records Card

Record 1 is a note. A neighbor wrote it on the morning the snow fell. Record 2 is a town weather history. It is in an old, worn book.

Ask 1: Snow Records Card, invented. Which record is firsthand for that snowfall?
Ask 2: Snow Records Card. Why is it firsthand?
