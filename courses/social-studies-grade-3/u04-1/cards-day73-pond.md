# Pond Day cards, made up, for practice

Print "made up, for practice" at the bottom of each card. The teacher models these on day 73.

**Pond Day photograph.** Children carry buckets near a pond. Made by: a parent who came to help. Date: 4 May.

**Pond Map.** A map of the pond, drawn later by the class. Made by: the class. Date: 20 May.

The question for the model: "Who took part in Pond Day?"

Added by pairs:

**Jug Card.** A clay jug. Made in 1970 by a potter.

**Boat Builder's Telling.** A recorded telling, made in 2019 by a boat builder, about building boats on the shore.
