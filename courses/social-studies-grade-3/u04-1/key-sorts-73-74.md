# Teacher key, the matching of days 73 and 74

Teacher only. Never given to children. Practice items ask for the maker (days 73 and 74); the primary or secondary talk below is for the spoken discussion and the exit.

## Day 73, six cards, "Who took part in the Harvest Fair?"

1. Fair Photograph: primary. Taken at the fair by a visitor who was there.
2. Helper Sheet: primary. Helpers wrote their own names at the fair.
3. Newspaper Story: secondary. The reporter was not there and repeats others.
4. School History Book: secondary. Written long after, looking back.
5. Baker's Telling: primary for her own stall. She tells what she knows herself.
6. Stall Plan: secondary for who took part. It is primary for a different question, "Where did the committee plan the stalls?" Say so aloud.

Reasons are about the maker and the question. Never age, never looks. Not "the oldest is primary" (D). Not "primary means true" (M).

## Day 74, four cards, "How do these sources show Native people's use of plants?"

1. Interview, David Grignon: primary. He tells what he knows himself, in 2014.
2. Canoe basket: primary for that maker's cedar-bark craft in 1984 to 1985. It is not primary for earlier practice. A new basket cannot show how an earlier one was used.
3. Museum label: secondary. The museum describes the basket; it did not make it.
4. Textbook page: secondary. Curio Class wrote it later, looking back at the other sources.
