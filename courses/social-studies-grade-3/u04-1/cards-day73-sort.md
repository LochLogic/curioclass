# Six cards for the sort, made up, for practice

Print "made up, for practice" at the bottom of each card. One card each: its maker, its date, and its kind. The stated question for the whole set is: "Who took part in the Harvest Fair?"

1. **Fair Photograph.** Taken at the fair by a visitor. Dated the day of the fair.
2. **Helper Sheet.** A sign-up sheet that helpers wrote their names on at the fair.
3. **Newspaper Story.** Written a week later by a reporter who was not there. It repeats what other people said.
4. **School History Book.** A book of the town's history, printed in 2020.
5. **Baker's Telling.** A recording made in 2018. A baker says what happened at her own stall.
6. **Stall Plan.** A map the fair committee drew before the fair. It shows where stalls will stand.

Each pair matches the six cards to their makers and says whether each maker was at the fair. Card 6 can answer a second question: A, who took part in the fair, or B, where the stalls were planned.
