# Day 72: Whose land is this?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Day 71 ended the course check; its results say who needs the map key again, which day 75 takes. Print the nearest nation's card from its own page (sample in cards-day72-hook.md; never invent one). Cover the five supporting questions and post them. Have a town physical map and the Rain Map cards. Keep key-exits-72-74.md out until the slips are in.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook
Set the town map beside the card. Read the card aloud: "These are words from the nation's own pages." Review (unit 1): the legend trace, "The map shows ___. I can find it because the legend says ___." Review (day 28): the nation card. **Ask.** What does each one tell us about who shapes this place? Expect: the map shows rivers and land; the card says the Nation uses the rivers and is here today. Nobody is asked about home.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
Review (grade 2): climate is what a place is usually like over many years. Hand back day 71's slips (sc1-X71, sc1-X71b). Hand each pair the Rain Map card (made up); read it once. Pairs answer [[s04-P72a]] and [[s04-P72b]]. If many matched by a name or shape, repair first in Questions.

#### 8 min. Local homeland
New today: a homeland is the land a nation's people have known as home for a very long time. Model on the card: the Nation's page says the Penobscots have relied on Maine's rivers for thousands of years. A climate region is a weather word; a homeland comes from a nation's own account. Point to "today": the Nation lives and governs now, so we speak in the present. Pairs say: "The map shows ___. The card says ___."

#### 10 min. Questions
Review (grade 1): a big question and its smaller questions. Uncover the board and read the five supporting questions with their days. Review (days 20 and 26): a word can have two readings. Each pair copies one question and fills the record: [[s04-P72c]], [[s04-P72d]], [[s04-P72e]], [[s04-P72f]]. For "shaped" the readings are: it changed the land; it shared knowledge of the land. Hear six pairs, a minute each, and write their names on the record sheet so the next round hears six others. If many missed the Rain Map, compare rain symbols first, then a fresh pair, and keep only [[s04-P72c]] and [[s04-P72f]].

#### 10 min. Importance record
Review (day 2): who else cares, "This matters to ___ because ___." Read the choices aloud. Each child circles [[s04-P72g]] and [[s04-P72h]], then tells a partner a reason in their own words.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Library Display Card, first seen here. Read the card and the two asks once. Every child writes [[s04-E72]] and [[s04-E72b]]. Keep the slips: they return on day 87. Key: key-exits-72-74.md.

**Shared shape.** The Rain Map goes from pictures to a climate clue, the hook from a map and a card to who shapes this place; the importance record circles given groups and reasons, and the exit asks the child to name a group and a reason for a library display the class has not seen.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the Rain Map on the board and read the card aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No town map: draw the street, park or school block. No nation card: read the sample card from the teacher.

**Skip.** The record's last two lines.

- **8 min. A map and a card.** Look at the map, then the card. **Say.** "Who shapes this place?" Expect: the Nation, today.
- **5 min. Rain Map.** [[s04-P72a]] [[s04-P72b]]
- **10 min. A question.** Pick one from the board. [[s04-P72c]] [[s04-P72d]] [[s04-P72e]] [[s04-P72f]]
- **10 min. Who else.** [[s04-P72g]] [[s04-P72h]]
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card once. [[s04-E72]] [[s04-E72b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the record keeps [[s04-P72c]] and [[s04-P72f]]. Longer: write a second supporting question.

### Words and supports
Words: nation, homeland, shaped, climate, today. Frame: "The map shows ___. The card says ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 73 teaches primary and secondary sources. It opens with the Seed Pot Records, then whichever of error R (only the teacher needs it) or K (the Nation is gone) more children made today.

### Credit
