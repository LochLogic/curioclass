# Seed Pot Records, made up, for practice

Print "made up, for practice" at the bottom of each card.

**Record 1.** A photograph. It shows three seed pots on a sill. Made by: Mr. Ames, a photographer. Date: 12 March.

**Record 2.** A note. It says: "We planted three pots today." Made by: Ms. Lee, a teacher. Date: 12 March.

Cards for pairs. Pairs say who made each, when, and what the two agree on.
