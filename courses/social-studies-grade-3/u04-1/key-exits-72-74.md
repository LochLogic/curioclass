# Teacher key, exit cards days 72 to 74

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-72-74.md); never hand to children.

- Day 72, ask 1: A named group beyond the class, such as families, visitors, neighbors or library staff. Not "only our teacher" (R). Ask 2: They need to know the nation lives and works here today, not only in the past. Not "nobody needs it because the nation is gone" (K). The card is saved and revised on day 87.
- Day 73, ask 1: Record 1, the drawing made at the meeting. Ask 2: It was made at the meeting by a person who was there. Not "it is the older paper" (D); not "it must be true" (M). The answer is marked on the because, not on the pair.
- Day 74, ask 1: The neighbor's note. Ask 2: The neighbor wrote it the morning the snow fell, as a person who saw it. Not "the book, because it looks oldest" (D); not "the note must be right" (M).
