# Day 93 cards: reasons and meetings

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. The teacher reads each card aloud once.

## Four reason cards

- Trade. Some Europeans hoped to buy and sell goods across the sea.
- Riches. Some hoped to find things worth a lot of money.
- Land. Some hoped for land to farm and build on.
- Religion. Some hoped to live and worship as their faith asked.

Say plainly: people already lived on every coast they reached.

## The chain strip

Four boxes in a row: reason, voyage, meeting, effect.

## Salt Cove (worked, made up)

A ship from far away reaches Salt Cove. Fishers live there. The fishers watch the ship come in. The sailors want fresh water.

What each group saw: a strange ship; a town with drying racks. What each learned: news of the other's land. What each wanted: water and safe passage; to know who the visitors were. Chain: a reason (water), a voyage, a meeting, an effect (each learns about the other).

## Fern Point (pairs, made up)

A ship reaches Fern Point. A town of people stands on the shore. The visitors want to trade cloth. The townspeople want to see what the cloth is like.

Each pair fills the chain strip: reason, voyage, meeting, one effect.

## Repair row (from day 92)

Year cards: 1790, 1805, 1990, 2024, 1850. Say the century aloud for each.
