# Day 94 cards: routes and map labels

Read each card aloud once. Cabot is not on any teaching card.

## Repair (Z from day 93): two accounts of one coast, made up

A visitor's letter (made up): I saw a bay. I saw smoke from many fires. I wrote to tell my friends at home.
A townsperson's note (made up): This is our town. Our fields run to the shore. We fish from the point.

Pairs find a farm or a town in each account.

## Worked trace: Verrazzano

In 1524 Giovanni da Verrazzano sailed for the king of France. He crossed the Atlantic and sailed along the coast from Florida to New Brunswick. (Dictionary of Canadian Biography, Verrazzano, sources.csv U5L.)

Say aloud: the tail starts at France. The arrow crosses the water. It follows the coast to New Brunswick.

## Pairs trace: Henry Hudson

In 1609 Henry Hudson reached New York Bay. (National Park Service, sources.csv U5T.) On the pair map, draw an arrow from the Europe dot to the New York Bay dot. Say the start, the direction and the end aloud.

AE repair: Hudson's route ends at New York Bay, not at Plymouth. Verrazzano's ends at New Brunswick.

## French and Dutch labels

Two labels only: New France (Quebec was founded in 1608) and New Netherland (Dutch New Amsterdam in 1625). Pairs find each on the map and say which direction it lies from Plymouth. No history is taught.

## Repair for B, wrong beside right

Wrong: an arrow with the head at the start dot. Right: the tail is at the start dot and the head is at the end dot.
