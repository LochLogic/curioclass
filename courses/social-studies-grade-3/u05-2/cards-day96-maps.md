# Day 96 cards: an early map and a new one

Print "made up, for practice" on every map. No real early map is used until a map page is read.

## Repair (C from day 95), made up

A flyer for Lantern Beach and the town's own page for the same beach. Pairs say what each says and what each leaves out.

## Worked map pair: Kettle Sound, made up

Early map labels: Great Water; Kettle Bay; Two Rivers; Marsh End (a drawn town of nine houses); a blank space inland.
Current map labels: Kettle Sound; Kettle Bay; Two Rivers; Harbor Street; a blank space inland.

Lay the early map over the current one. Shared: the bay and the two rivers. Changed: Great Water is now Kettle Sound. Marsh End, a drawn town, is gone from the new map.

Say "north" if the early map is turned.

## Pairs: Ferry Bay, made up

Early map labels: Wide Mouth; Stone Point; Reed Creek; Long Shed (a drawn building).
Current map labels: Ferry Bay; Stone Point; Reed Creek; Dock Road.

Pairs name one shared feature and one label that changed, and write both on the relationship strip.

## Shows, suggests, cannot tell: three labels on a made-up map

- A new name: Harbor View.
- A drawn town: six houses by a river.
- A blank space.

Sort: the drawn town shows people lived there. The new name suggests it. The blank space cannot tell.

Z repair: blank or new does not mean empty.
