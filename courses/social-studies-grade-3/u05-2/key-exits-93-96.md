# Teacher key, exit cards days 93 to 96

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-93-96.md. Marking: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- E93: any effect that treats the coast as lived on, such as each group getting goods or learning about the other. Not "the land was empty" (Z).
- E94: an arrow from the England dot west across the Atlantic, arrowhead at the coast dot. Not the head at England (B). Cabot's exact landfall is not asked (disputed, cut).
- E95: the land was lived on and farmed. Smith: cornfields and at least thirty homes along a river. Massachusett page: children kept birds from the fields. Not "Smith knows what everyone wanted or thought" (C).
- E96: shared: Gull Point or Elm Brook. Different: Round Water is now Larch Bay, or Fish Houses is now Pier Lane. Not "the new labels mean no one lived there" (Z).
