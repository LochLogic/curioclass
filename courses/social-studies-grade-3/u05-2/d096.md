# Day 96: An early map beside a new one

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print a Ferry Bay pair for each pair of children, with a loop of tracing paper. Put the Kettle Sound maps on the projector. Keep key-exits-93-96.md away until slips are in. Keep a class list for who was heard.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (C from E95)
Hand back day 95's slip and practice (s05-E95, s05-P95a, s05-P95b, s05-P95c, s05-P95d, s05-P95e, s05-P95f) marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Read the flyer and the beach page again, both made up. Pairs say what each says and what each leaves out. Show the wrong, "The explorer wrote it, so it tells all of it," beside the right: each account tells part. A nation's own page tells its own story. If few made error C, they work at the side table.

#### 12 min. Worked map pair
Say that both maps are made up. The early Kettle Sound map: Great Water, Kettle Bay, Two Rivers, Marsh End (a drawn town of nine houses), a blank space inland. The current map: Kettle Sound, Kettle Bay, Two Rivers, Harbor Street, a blank space inland. Lay the early map over the current one. Shared: the bay and the two rivers. Changed: Great Water is now Kettle Sound. **Ask.** "Which label is on both maps?" Expect: Kettle Bay or Two Rivers. Review (unit 1): north is where the arrow points; if the early map is turned, turn it until its arrow matches. Marsh End is drawn on the early map and not named on the new one; a drawn town shows people lived there.

#### 10 min. Pairs
Pairs lay the Ferry Bay early map over the current one on tracing paper and fill the relationship strip with the shared and changed places they see. Each child writes [[s05-P96a]] and [[s05-P96b]]. Hear a rotating six pairs, about a minute each, and list their names. If the day is compressed, this part shortens to 4 minutes.

#### 6 min. Shows, might suggest, cannot tell
Review (unit 1): shows, might suggest, cannot tell. The claim is "people lived here." Read three labels on a made-up map: a drawn town, a new name, a blank space. Each child writes [[s05-P96c]], [[s05-P96d]] and [[s05-P96e]]. Repair error Z: a blank space or a new name does not mean the land was empty. Post the answer to "Whose account and map?" on the question board.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip with the Coast Exit Pair, new today: an early map and a current map. Read the labels once. Each child writes [[s05-E96]] and [[s05-E96b]]. Key: key-exits-93-96.md.

**Shared shape.** Model and practice lay an early map over a new one and match, find and sort labels; the exit is a new pair of maps and asks for one shared place and one changed label.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw both maps on the board; hold two pages to a window to lay one over the other.

### At home

**Swap.** No tracing paper: hold the two pages to a window.

**Skip.** The pair round and the class board.

- **15 min. The map pair.** Read the labels of both Ferry Bay maps. [[s05-P96a]] [[s05-P96b]]
- **15 min. Sort.** **Say.** "Does a drawn town show people lived here?" Expect: yes, it shows it. [[s05-P96c]] [[s05-P96d]] [[s05-P96e]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the labels once. [[s05-E96]] [[s05-E96b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one map pair, not two. Longer: make a map of your street then and now, made up, and say what changed.

### Words and supports
Words: shared, changed, label, drawn, blank. Frame: "___ is now ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read every label aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 97 opens with error Z (a blank space or a new name means the land was empty) from today's exit, ten minutes, with two accounts of one coast, before the first colonies.
