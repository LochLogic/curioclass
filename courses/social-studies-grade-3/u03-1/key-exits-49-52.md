# Teacher key, exit cards days 49 to 52

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-49-52.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 49, ask 1: A named group other than the patients, such as cleaners, drivers or nurses. Not "only the patients" (T). Ask 2: A reason tied to the clinic need, such as cleaners need clean sheets or drivers need a plan for on-time delivery. Practice record: write one cue, read on day 66 and scored as the revised Q10.
- Day 50, ask 1: The potter: a mug is a thing you can hold. Ask 2: The plumber: fixing a sink is work done for someone. Not "both are goods" (V).
- Day 51, ask 1: The wrench: a tool people made and use to make things. Not "the wrench is money" (A). Ask 2: The water. Not "the wrench is natural because it is metal" (U). Extra in the Exit box: Her skill at finding faults: knowledge she learned.
- Day 52, ask 1: The workshop: a writer, an illustrator and a binder each do one task on the same book. Ask 2: it does mainly one kind of work, repairs, and leaves other work to other businesses. Not "they are the same" (S).
