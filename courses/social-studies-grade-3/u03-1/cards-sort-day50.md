# Goods and services sort, day 50

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Twelve picture cards of kinds of local business, one picture and one name on each. The teacher models four first with two hoops (goods, services) and marks who makes it and who uses it.

## Modeled by the teacher

A shoe shop. A window washer. A bus company. A bakery.

## Twelve cards for the pairs

Florist. Bike repair stand. Farm stand. Laundry. Bookshop. Candle maker. Taxi. Tree trimmer. Pizza shop. Music teacher. Toy shop. Boat painter.

Pairs sort the twelve into goods and services and mark producer (who makes or does it) and consumer (who uses it). Some cards could be both a good and a service; the pair says why it chose.

## Retrieval cards, grade 2 and grade 3 practice

Bus Card, invented. A bus company drives riders across town.

Fishing Crew Card, invented. A fishing crew uses fish, a boat and fishing know-how.
