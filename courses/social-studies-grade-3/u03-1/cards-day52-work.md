# Shared work cards, day 52

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## One workshop, many tasks (teacher)

A boat workshop: one worker cuts the wood, one sands it and one paints it. They make one toy boat together. Division of labor: the team divides the tasks of one product.

## Two businesses, one kind of work each (teacher)

A sail shop makes only sails. A boat shop makes only hulls. To specialize is to make mostly one kind of thing. Each needs what the other makes.

## Eight job cards for the pairs

Sort each into "inside one workshop" or "between businesses".

1. In the coat workshop, one person cuts the cloth.
2. In the coat workshop, one person sews the seams.
3. In the coat workshop, one person presses the coat.
4. A button maker sells buttons to many coat shops.
5. A thread shop sells thread to many workshops.
6. A cloth mill weaves cloth for many tailors.
7. A zipper maker makes only zippers.
8. A coat label printer prints only labels.

## Pinecrest Main Street (made up)

A picture of a made-up street with these businesses along it: a shoe repair counter, a cheese shop, a cloth shop, a fruit stand, a key cutter, a bakery, a ticket booth for the bus and a candle shop. Pairs find two specialist businesses and say what each must get from somewhere else.
