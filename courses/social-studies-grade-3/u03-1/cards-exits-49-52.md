# Exit cards, days 49 to 52

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken; some are also heard. Day 49's exit is a practice record, read with a cue and revised on day 66.

## Day 49: Clinic Needs Card

A clinic needs clean sheets every week. It also needs its deliveries on time.

Ask 1: Clinic Needs Card, invented. Who besides patients needs to know how we meet needs?
Ask 2: Clinic Needs Card. Why does that group need to know?

## Day 50: Potter and Plumber Card

A potter makes mugs and sells them at a stall. A plumber fixes sinks in people's homes.

Ask 1: Potter and Plumber Card, invented. Which one sells a good?
Ask 2: Potter and Plumber Card. Which one does a service?

## Day 51: Repairer Card

A repairer fixes garden carts. She uses water to wash off dust, her skill at finding faults and a wrench.

Ask 1: Repairer Card, invented. Which one is the capital resource?
Ask 2: Repairer Card. Which one is the natural resource?

Exit box, extra: Repairer Card. Which one is the human resource?

## Day 52: Book Team Card

In one workshop a writer, an illustrator and a binder each do one task on the same book. A shop down the street only repairs books.

Ask 1: Book Team Card, invented. Which one divides the work of one book?
Ask 2: Book Team Card. Explain what makes the repair shop a specialist.
