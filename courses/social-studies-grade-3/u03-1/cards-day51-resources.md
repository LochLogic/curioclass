# Resource cards and traces, day 51

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Three boxes: natural, human, capital.

## The bakery trace (teacher)

Bread comes from flour. Flour is made from wheat. Wheat grows on a farm. Natural: the wheat. Human: what the bakers know and can do. Capital: the oven and the mixer. Flour is made from wheat, so it is traced back and not put in natural.

## Twelve resource cards for the pairs

Wheat. Rain. Soil. How to knead dough. How to grow wheat. The driver's know-how for a safe delivery. Oven. Mixer. Tractor. Flour mill machine. Fence around the field. Bakery shelves.

Pairs sort the twelve into the three baskets.

## A tool is not money

Oven card. Coin card. The oven is used to make bread; the coin is used to pay. Six cards for pairs to sort into "used to make things" and "used to pay": oven, rolling pin, a $5 bill, mixer, coins in a jar, a ladle.

## A service needs resources too

The lawn service (teacher): the grass is natural, the owner's skill is human, the mower is capital.

For pairs, a fresh service card: Boat Rental Card. A boat rental uses a pond, rowboats and the owner's know-how about rowing and safety. Name the three kinds.
