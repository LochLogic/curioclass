# Section 3.1 opener: what it takes to meet a need

## What the section does

Four days of 40 minutes. Day 49 opens the unit's big question, How does our community get what it needs? The class looks at its own School Needs Record (paper, water and cleaning) and traces who supplies each need and who uses it. Pairs name another group who needs to know, and each child writes a wonder card. Day 50 gives a short repair for the unit 3 readiness gaps (choices and saving from grade 1), sorts goods and services, co-writes the six supporting questions and starts the supporting-question record. Day 51 sorts resources into three baskets: natural, human and capital. Day 52 shows how work is shared inside one workshop and between specialist businesses.

## How the days run

Day 49: hand back, the School Needs Record, trace who supplies it, who else needs the answer, wonder card, the big question, the exit. Day 50: return and repair, goods and services, a pairs sort, the board, the record, the exit. Day 51: return and repair, trace a bakery, sort resource cards, a tool is not money, a service needs resources, the exit. Day 52: return and repair, one workshop, two businesses, a job-card sort, our town street, the exit. The exits are in cards-exits-49-52.md and the teacher key is key-exits-49-52.md.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A big question matters to people besides the ones it serves first.** Cleaners, drivers and office staff need the answer to how a school gets paper, water and cleaning.
- **A supplier is the person or business that gets a need to the people who use it.**
- **A good is a thing you can hold; a service is work done for someone.** A person can make, sell and also buy.
- **Natural resources come from nature, human resources are learned knowledge and skill, capital resources are equipment people made and use to make things.** Capital never means money. Flour is made from wheat, so it is traced back and not placed in natural. A skill is learned, and it says nothing about anyone's worth.
- **Work can be shared inside one workshop (division of labor) or between businesses that each make mostly one kind of thing (specialization).** No job belongs to a kind of person.

## The errors in plain words

T is the idea that only the people served need to know. V is the idea that a good and a service are the same. A is the idea that a tool is money. U is the idea that a made tool is a natural resource. L is the idea that only goods need resources. S is the idea that a workshop and a specialist shop are the same. Y, Z, AD and G come from the readiness check and are repaired on day 50. Wrong ideas are read from "someone might say" cards, never from a named child.

## Supplies for the section

The School Needs Record (made with the school office), need strips, wonder cards, chart paper for the board, picture cards for the sort, resource cards, three boxes for the baskets, job cards, the town street picture, ten counters a pair, exit cards, the record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Chart paper for the board, index cards for the sorts, three boxes or envelopes for the baskets, bottle caps for counters. The teacher draws the cards and reads each one aloud.

No child reports on home, family jobs or money. A child may pass in talk. No school worker is named or questioned in class unless they agreed beforehand.
