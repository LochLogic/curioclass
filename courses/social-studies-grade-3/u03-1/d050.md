# Day 50: Goods, services and our questions

## Teacher page

### Before class
Pick the more-missed readiness gap. Read day 49's slips for error T and list a group of up to six. Set out the card sets, the board and the hoops. Keep key-exits-49-52.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
The readiness cards (s02-R9, s02-R9b, s02-R9c, s02-R10, s02-R10b, s02-R10c) named the gap. If more than a third made it, run this for the whole class: model 2 minutes, guide 2, a fresh choice 1. Otherwise a named group of up to six has it at the side table; the class does the retrieval alone.
Choices (error Y, error Z): two same-price picture choices beside eight counters; four counters buy one, the other is given up, the thing wanted next. Fresh choice: $11, two things; each child says what is given up.
Saving, review from grade 1 (error AD, error G): split ten counters between spending now and keeping for a goal; saved money is still there. Fresh goal $14, start $6; name where savings are kept, a bank.
Pairs say [[s03-P50f]] aloud in the guide minutes, 1 minute each.

#### 5 min. Goods, services, producers and consumers
Review: you learned goods and services in grade 1, and producer and consumer in grade 2. Sort four cards into two hoops: a shoe shop, a window washer, a bus company, a bakery. Say who makes or does it and who uses it.

#### 10 min. Pairs sort
Pairs sort 12 business cards into goods and services and mark producer and consumer; a card that fits both is said with a reason. Visit a rotating six. Then each child writes [[s03-P50e]] from the Bus Card.
Side table, 5 minutes each: first the error T group reads their day 49 slips (s03-E49, s03-E49b), names one more group for a school library and gives a reason; then the other readiness gap if it names a group.

#### 8 min. Co-write the board
Post the big question; a supporting question helps answer it. Co-write six on the board with their days: who produces what (52), why people use money (53), what shapes what sellers make (54), what rewards and penalties push choices (58), what banks and other places do with money (60), how places depend on trade (64).

#### 10 min. Supporting-question record
Model on the Pinecrest library, made up: "How does the library get what it needs?" Needs can mean books to lend now, or money to fix the roof later. Each child picks one board question and writes [[s03-P50a]], [[s03-P50b]], [[s03-P50c]] and [[s03-P50d]]; drawing and dictating count. Visit a rotating eight.
**Ask.** Which word in our big question could mean two things? Expect: needs, with two readings and a reason each. "Needs means needs" has one reading: ask for another.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Potter and Plumber Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E50]] and [[s03-E50b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The sort uses business cards; the exit two named workers the sort never used. Cue the record in writing before day 66.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the 12 cards on index cards; two circles on the floor are the hoops.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use coins or pasta. No class: you are the partner.

**Skip.** The side table and the board.

- **10 min. Goods and services.** Sort six things you see, such as a bakery, a bus ride, a toy shop, a haircut: made to hold, or work done.
  **Say.** "Who makes it, and who uses it?" Expect: a maker and a user.
- **10 min. A question.** Your child writes [[s03-P50a]], [[s03-P50b]], [[s03-P50c]] and [[s03-P50d]].
  **Say.** "Which word could mean two things?" Expect: needs.
- **10 min. Bus Card.** Your child writes [[s03-P50e]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E50]] [[s03-E50b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: six cards in the sort. Longer: a second question on the record.

### Words and supports
Words: good, service, producer, consumer, supporting question. Frame: "Needs could mean ___ or ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 51 opens by sorting fresh product and repair pictures if many said the plumber sells a good, or both are goods (error V), and then names the resources a business uses.
