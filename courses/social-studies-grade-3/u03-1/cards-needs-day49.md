# Need strips, day 49

Invented for practice where a strip is not from the School Needs Record. Cut the sheet into strips. Five pairs get each need. The teacher reads each strip aloud once.

## Strip 1: Paper

Need: paper for writing and drawing.
Supplied by: ______________
Used by: ______________
Draw or write one arrow from the supplier to the users.

## Strip 2: Water

Need: water to drink and to wash hands.
Supplied by: ______________
Used by: ______________
Draw or write one arrow from the supplier to the users.

## Strip 3: Cleaning

Need: a clean room, hall and sink.
Supplied by: ______________
Used by: ______________
Draw or write one arrow from the supplier to the users.

## Teacher model, "matters to others" for paper

The office staff need to know when paper is low. The art teacher needs to know when a box will arrive. Each has a reason to care about the answer.

Fill the blanks on each strip from the School Needs Record. Supplier is the new word: the person or business that gets a need to the people who use it.
