# Day 49: What does our school need?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Fill in the School Needs Record with the school office: maker, date, and who supplies and who uses paper, water and cleaning. Name no worker unless they agreed. Cut the need strips, five pairs a need. Keep key-exits-49-52.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 3 min. Hand back
Hand back the redo cards (s02-F48a, s02-F48b, s02-F48c, s02-F48d, s02-F48e, s02-F48f, s02-F48g, s02-F48h) with their notes: read, not redone. Seat the children the readiness cards (s02-R9, s02-R9b, s02-R9c, s02-R10, s02-R10b, s02-R10c) named nearest you; day 50 repairs them.

#### 6 min. The School Needs Record
Show the record and read it aloud once. Review: you learned who made it, when and why in unit 1.
**Ask.** Who made it, when, and why? Expect: our teacher with the school office; the date on it; to see what our school needs each day. Each child writes [[s03-P49d]]; speaking counts.

#### 10 min. Trace who supplies it
New word, supplier: the person or business that gets a need to the people who use it. Model paper: the office orders it, pupils and teachers use it, one arrow runs from the office to the classroom. Review: you learned need in kindergarten. Pairs fill one strip from the record and draw one arrow; each child writes [[s03-P49a]], [[s03-P49b]] and [[s03-P49c]]. Visit a rotating six pairs, 1 minute each. An arrow from user to supplier: ask who gets the need to whom.

#### 7 min. Who else needs the answer?
Review: you learned "This matters to ___ because ___" in unit 1. Model for paper: the office staff need to know when it is low; the art teacher needs to know when a box will arrive.
**Ask.** Who else needs to know how our school gets paper, water and cleaning, and why? Expect: a group besides the pupils served first, with a reason tied to the need. Only the people served, or no reason, is error T.
Pairs say a group and a reason, 1 minute each way; hear a rotating six. Each child writes [[s03-P49e]].

#### 5 min. Wonder card
Review: you wrote a wonder card in unit 1. Each child writes or draws [[s03-P49f]]. Six volunteers read theirs aloud; write them on the wonder strip. A child may pass.

#### 6 min. The big question and two words
Post "How does our community get what it needs?" This opens the question board; supporting questions come day 50. Say need and supplier again with two drawings: a box carried to a school door, a hose filling a cooler.
**Ask.** Which drawing shows a supplier, and who uses what it brings? Expect: either one, with supplier and user named.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Clinic Needs Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E49]] and [[s03-E49b]]. Keep the slips; cue each in writing before day 66. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** Practice uses our school's needs with a frame; the exit is a clinic card with two plain asks, new to the child.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the needs on index cards and the record on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No School Needs Record: write three lines yourself (paper, water, cleaning; who supplies, who uses). No class: you are the partner.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the wonder strip.

- **10 min. Trace it.** Pick one need. Your child writes [[s03-P49a]], [[s03-P49b]] and [[s03-P49c]].
  **Say.** "Who gets this need to the people who use it?" Expect: the supplier.
- **10 min. Who else?** Your child writes [[s03-P49e]].
  **Say.** "Who else needs to know, and why?" Expect: a group and a reason.
- **10 min. Wonder.** Your child writes [[s03-P49f]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E49]] [[s03-E49b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one strip a pair. Longer: a second group and reason.

### Words and supports
Words: need, supplier, record, matters. Frame: "This matters to ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 50 repairs the day 48 readiness gaps and opens the question board. If many wrote only the people served (error T), a group of up to six meets you at the pairs sort for 5 minutes, with a school library case.

### Credit
Ours. The cards are invented for practice; the record is our class's own.
