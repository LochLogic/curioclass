# Question board, day 50

Invented for practice. Chart paper. The teacher writes the big question and leaves room beside each supporting question for the day it is answered.

Big question: How does our community get what it needs?

1. Who produces what? (answered day 52)
2. Why do people use money? (day 53)
3. What shapes what sellers make? (day 54)
4. What rewards and penalties push choices? (day 58)
5. What do banks and other places do with money? (day 60)
6. How do places depend on trade? (day 64)

Say it again on day 50: a big question and a supporting question are different. A supporting question helps answer the big one.
