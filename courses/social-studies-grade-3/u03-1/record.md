# Unit 3 record sheet: how our community gets what it needs

Write each child's name down the left, one row for each child, and keep one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never write a count of reminders.

Columns: the readiness items R9 to R10c (day 48; no score, only the support used); one tick and a note for each exit E49 to E65; the day 67 prompts Q1 to Q8; and the record rows Q9 to Q11, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when it is missing; leave blank when not seen.

Record rows: Q9, Q9b and Q9c (day 66, the two offers, the difference, and interest), Q10 (day 49 record, revised on day 66), Q11 (day 50 record, revised on day 66). Score from the day 66 revision after the written cue, and not before. The day 49 exit and the day 50 record are practice until then.

Exits E49, E50, E51, E52 and E53 to E65 are heard from the day's six, rotating through the class. Day 68's fresh redo is not scored on this sheet; note only what help was used. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell.
