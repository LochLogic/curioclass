# Day 51: What a business uses

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 50's slips for error V. Mark three boxes natural, human and capital. Keep key-exits-49-52.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 50's slips (s03-E50, s03-E50b): if many gave error V, say "a hat and a haircut are both goods" beside "a hat is a good; a haircut is a service". Whole class sorts fresh pictures: a kite, mending a scarf, a loaf, a piano tuning. Each child points to good or service.

#### 8 min. Trace a bakery
Review: you traced bread back to wheat in grade 2, and learned natural resource and human resource there. Trace a made-up bakery: bread, flour, wheat on a farm. Natural: the wheat. Human: what the bakers know and can do, learned and never a person's worth. New word, capital resource: equipment people made and use to make things, the oven and the mixer. Capital here is not the city from grade 1, and not money.
Flour is made from wheat, so trace it back, not into natural.
**Ask.** Flour is made from what natural resource? Expect: wheat, from a farm. Each child writes [[s03-P51a]].

#### 10 min. Sort the cards
Pairs sort 12 resource cards from the bakery and the farm into the three baskets. Visit a rotating six pairs. Side table of up to six, 5 minutes: whoever put the oven or tractor in natural (error U) traces it back to metal from the ground and the people who learned to make it.

#### 6 min. A tool is not money
Set an oven card beside a coin card. **Ask.** Which one is used to make bread? Expect: the oven; the coin is used to pay. Pairs sort six cards into used to make things and used to pay. Each child writes [[s03-P51b]]. A coin or bill put under capital is error A.

#### 8 min. A service needs resources too
Work a lawn service, made up: the grass is natural, the owner's skill human, the mower capital. A service uses resources as a bakery does. Pairs name each kind for the Boat Rental Card, read aloud once. Each child writes [[s03-P51c]], [[s03-P51d]] and [[s03-P51e]]. Only the bakery having resources is error L.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Repairer Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E51]] and [[s03-E51b]] on the slip and [[s03-E51c]] in the exit box. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** Practice asks what kind a named thing is; the exit gives a kind and asks for the thing. The exit card is new.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the 12 cards on index cards; three envelopes are the baskets.

### At home

**Swap.** No baskets: draw three boxes. No class: you are the partner.

**Skip.** The side table and the lawn service.

- **12 min. Bakery.** Trace bread back to wheat. Your child writes [[s03-P51a]].
  **Say.** "Which is natural: the wheat, the oven or the flour?" Expect: wheat; flour is made from it.
- **12 min. Boat Rental.** Read the card once. Your child writes [[s03-P51c]], [[s03-P51d]] and [[s03-P51e]].
  **Say.** "Is a coin used to make things?" Expect: no, to pay.
- **6 min. Oven or coin.** Your child writes [[s03-P51b]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E51]] [[s03-E51b]] [[s03-E51c]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: eight resource cards. Longer: trace an oven back to the ground and to the people who learned to make it.

### Words and supports
Words: natural, human, capital, resource, tool. Frame: "The ___ is a ___ resource because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 52 opens with whichever was more missed: the water for the capital resource (error A), or the wrench for the natural one (error U). The other goes to the side table. Then it shows how work is shared.

### Credit
Ours. The bakery, lawn service, Boat Rental Card and Repairer Card are invented for practice.
