# Unit 3 readiness check: Jo Card and Ren Card

## Student

For the teacher. This is a short check on grade 1 ideas, not a test. It is given on day 48 in the last ten minutes, after the unit 2 response. Read each made-up card aloud once, then each question aloud once. Every child answers on the page by writing a few words or labelling a drawing. A child may answer at the side table. Note on the record sheet what each child did and what help was used. Give no score and no grade. The results guide the five-minute repair on day 50.

**Jo Card (made up).** Jo has $8. Paper costs $8. A puzzle costs $8. Jo buys the paper.

**Ren Card (made up).** Ren wants a $12 book. Ren has $5. Ren saves $2 and skips a snack.
