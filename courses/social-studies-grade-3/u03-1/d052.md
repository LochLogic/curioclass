# Day 52: Sharing the work

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 51's slips for error A and error U and pick the more-missed. Draw the Pinecrest Main Street picture large. Keep key-exits-49-52.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 51's slips (s03-E51, s03-E51b, s03-E51c). Whole class, the more-missed. Error A: "the water is the capital resource" beside "the capital resource is a tool people made and use to make things; money is used to pay". Error U: "the wrench is natural because it is metal" beside "people made the wrench; the metal came from the ground". The other goes to the side table in the sort.

#### 7 min. One workshop, many tasks
A made-up boat workshop: one worker cuts the wood, one sands it, one paints it, all for one toy boat. Say what each does. New words: division of labor, a team dividing the tasks of one product. Each child writes [[s03-P52b]].

#### 8 min. Two businesses, one kind of work
A sail shop makes only sails; a boat shop makes only hulls, made up. Review: you learned specialize in grade 1, each worker learning one part well. Here a business specializes when it makes mostly one kind of thing. Each needs what the other makes. Every worker still uses learned skill, a human resource; practice builds knowledge and decides no one's worth.
**Ask.** What does the sail shop need from the boat shop? Expect: a hull. Each child writes [[s03-P52a]].

#### 10 min. Pairs sort
Pairs sort 8 job cards into inside one workshop and between businesses. Visit a rotating six pairs. Side table of up to six, 5 minutes: the other of error A or error U from day 51's slips. Calling a workshop and a specialist shop the same is error S.

#### 7 min. Our town street
On the made-up Pinecrest Main Street picture, pairs find two specialist businesses and say what each must get from somewhere else; hear a rotating six. Each child writes [[s03-P52c]]. Write today's answer to question 1 on the question board. End: the sail shop has sails and needs a hull, so how does it get one? Day 53 gives money.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Book Team Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E52]] and [[s03-E52b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** Practice names one task, one need and one business; the exit asks which of two does each kind of work. The exit card is new.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the job cards on index cards; draw the street on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No envelope of cards: write each job on a scrap. No class: you are the partner.

**Skip.** The side table and the street.

- **10 min. A workshop.** Read about the boat workshop. Your child writes [[s03-P52b]].
  **Say.** "What does each worker do?" Expect: cut, sand, paint.
- **12 min. Two businesses.** Your child writes [[s03-P52a]].
  **Say.** "What does the sail shop need?" Expect: a hull.
- **8 min. A street.** Read the street list. Your child writes [[s03-P52c]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E52]] [[s03-E52b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: four job cards. Longer: a third business that needs a sail and a hull.

### Words and supports
Words: divide, specialize, workshop, task. Frame: "The ___ needs ___ from the ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 53 gives money. It opens with error S if many missed today's exit: the repair shop for the workshop, or the workshop for the specialist. Jobs inside one workshop beside two specialist businesses, 5 minutes.

### Credit
Ours. The workshops, shops, street and Book Team Card are invented for practice.
