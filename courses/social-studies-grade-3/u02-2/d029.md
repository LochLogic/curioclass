# Day 29: How towns and cities are set up

## Teacher page

### Before class
Hang the Our towns chart (Boston, Boulder, Lexington; the last row, our town, waits for day 32). Have the three town form cards and the six role cards ready. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 28 to 32 (key-exits-28-32.md), out of the room until the exit cards are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 28's exit slips and Exit boxes. Teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right. A: "The President makes all the laws" beside "Congress makes laws, the President carries them out, courts decide cases." If you heard in talk "a nation is a town" or "the nation was long ago", teach that first: read one present-tense line from the nation card.

#### 7 min. Same jobs in a town
Say: a town also makes rules and has people who run its daily work. Review (day 28): a state and the country split their jobs three ways; a town may split them another way. Review (grade 1): elected means chosen by voters. New: hired means chosen to do a job. Show one picture: a voter marking a ballot for a council member, and a library worker the town hired. **Ask.** Which person was elected, and which was hired? Expect: the council member was elected; the library worker was hired. New: a council is a group chosen to make a town's or city's laws. Each child writes [[s02-P29g]].

#### 15 min. Three real forms
Read one town form card every 2 minutes, saying its maker, date and link first (review: who made it, when and why, day 25): Boston, Boulder, Lexington (Source U2I, U2J, U2H). Name the form each card shows: mayor and council; council and manager; Select Board and Town Meeting. New: a town manager is hired to run a town's daily work; a town meeting is where voters gather and decide for the town. After the cards, pairs add each town's row to the Our towns chart, 3 minutes a row: who makes the rules, who runs the daily work. Each child then writes [[s02-P29a]], [[s02-P29b]] and [[s02-P29c]]. Practice pages stay in the folder.

#### 10 min. Which form?
Pairs sort the six role cards onto the three forms. Card 4 ("Elected to vote on the city laws") fits two forms: both Boston and Boulder have an elected council. Each child writes [[s02-P29d]] and [[s02-P29e]]. **Ask.** Does every town do it the same way? Expect: no; use the chart. Each child writes [[s02-P29f]].

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Town Form Card, first seen here. Read it and the two asks once. Every child writes [[s02-E29]] and [[s02-E29b]].

**Shared shape.** The chart rows and role cards go from a town or a role to its form; the exit asks which person in a new town holds a job.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the chart onto chart paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: draw three columns on paper, one for each town.

**Skip.** The role cards.

- **9 min. Elected or hired?** Say what each word means. [[s02-P29g]]
- **14 min. Three towns.** Your adult reads each town card aloud. [[s02-P29a]] [[s02-P29b]] [[s02-P29c]]
  **Say.** "Who makes the rules, and who runs the daily work?" Expect: a council or board makes them; a mayor or manager runs the work.
- **8 min. Which form?** [[s02-P29d]] [[s02-P29e]] [[s02-P29f]]
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Town Form Card once. [[s02-E29]] [[s02-E29b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: [[s02-P29a]] to [[s02-P29c]] only. Longer: find a fourth town's form on its own web page.

### Words and supports
Words: council, manager, town meeting, elected, hired. Frame: "___ is hired by ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 30 shows who governs the public schools: a school committee and the superintendent it hires. If many made error B on day 29's exit (a town taken for a mayor town) or error A (the manager taken for the voter), day 30's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one.
