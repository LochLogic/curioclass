# Six role cards: which form? (day 29)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Sort each card onto one of three forms: MAYOR AND COUNCIL, COUNCIL AND MANAGER, SELECT BOARD AND TOWN MEETING.

1. Hired to run the daily work for the council.
2. Elected members meet at a Town Meeting and vote on money.
3. Leads the city and proposes the budget.
4. Elected to vote on the city laws.
5. Elected to make policy and oversee the town.
6. Appointed by the council to carry out its policies.

Then answer: Does every town do it the same way? Use the chart.
