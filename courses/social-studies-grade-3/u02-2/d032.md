# Day 32: Our town's government, and a town in another state

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print our town's packet (government page, meeting notice, who runs daily work) and a matching packet from Lexington or Boulder, whichever is in another state (Source U2H, U2J); each page shows its maker and date. Choose two unused notices for the exit, one from each town, each voting on a new rule; write the answers in the key. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 28 to 32 (key-exits-28-32.md), out of the room until the exit cards are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 31's exit slips. Teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right. C: "The repair was free" beside "Someone collects money, then spends it." If many made error L, sort three fresh made-up slips on the board: a bus fare for one ride (fee), money all owners must pay (tax), a coin dropped in a collection box (gift).

#### 4 min. Find the building
Review (unit 1): the legend names a map's symbols. Pairs find the town hall on the unit 1 town map and say which legend symbol shows it. Each child writes [[s02-T32]]. A miss: up to 5 children meet you during the next part for symbol matching, 1 minute each.

#### 14 min. Our own town
Read our town's packet aloud, one page at a time, saying each page's maker and date first (review: who made it, when and why, day 25). Titles only, no names. Pairs fill boxes A to C of the Local Government Record, class edition: [[s02-P32a]], [[s02-P32b]], [[s02-P32c]]. Where a page does not say, write "the page does not say." On the meeting notice, point out who speaks and who votes: people may speak; the body the notice names votes.

#### 13 min. A town in another state
Read the matching packet aloud the same way. Pairs fill box D: [[s02-P32d]]. Then check the Our towns chart: fill the row for our town, and look again at the other town's row. **Ask.** How are our town and the other town alike and different? Expect: both have a body that votes on rules; the forms differ. Add that answer to "Who decides locally?" on the unit 2 question board (review, day 26), keeping day 27's answer. Short version: skip the map part, give this part 6 minutes, keep the exit. Record pages stay in the folder; read them, write one cue on each box, and keep them for days 44 and 45.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with Notices A and B, first seen here. Read each notice and its ask once. Every child writes [[s02-E32]] and [[s02-E32b]].

**Shared shape.** The packets and the record go from a town page to its body and its way to take part; the exit asks which body votes in a new notice from each town.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the page titles and makers on the board and read the pages aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No town map: skip that step. No second town: use the Lexington card from day 29.

**Skip.** The Our towns chart.

- **4 min. Find the building.** Find the town hall on a town map. [[s02-T32]]
- **14 min. Our town.** Find your town's government page. Read it aloud. [[s02-P32a]] [[s02-P32b]] [[s02-P32c]]
  **Say.** "Who makes the rules, and who runs the daily work?" Expect: the titles on the page.
- **13 min. Another town.** Read a second town's page. [[s02-P32d]]
  **Say.** "How are the two towns alike and different?" Expect: both have a body that votes; the forms differ.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Read both notices once. [[s02-E32]] [[s02-E32b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: boxes A and B only. Longer: a third town's page in a box of its own.

### Words and supports
Words: packet, notice, council, manager, vote. Frame: "The ___ votes. The ___ runs the daily work." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 33 opens why a rule was made. If many made error K on day 32's exit (a visitor taken to vote) or error B (one town form taken for every town), day 33's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one.

### Credit
Ours. The exit notices are invented by Curio Class. Facts: each town's own official website.
