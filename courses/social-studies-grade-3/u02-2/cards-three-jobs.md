# The three jobs, national government (day 28)

Three cards, one job each. Print the source on each card.

**Card 1: makes laws.** Congress makes the laws for the whole country. It has two parts: the Senate and the House of Representatives.

**Card 2: carries out laws.** The President leads the executive branch. The executive branch carries out the laws.

**Card 3: decides cases.** The Supreme Court and other federal courts decide cases about laws.

A branch is one part of a government with its own job.

Three baskets: MAKES LAWS. CARRIES OUT LAWS. DECIDES CASES.

In our state, the state constitution names a legislative power, an executive power and a judiciary power. A lawmaker may make laws, not carry them out.
