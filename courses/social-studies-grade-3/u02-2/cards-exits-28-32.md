# Exit cards, days 28 to 32

Print "invented for practice" on each card except Notices A and B. Each answer is written; some are also heard.

## Day 28: Job Card

Brynn is a state lawmaker. Cole is the governor. Tali is a judge in a state court.

Ask 1: Who makes the state's laws: Brynn, Cole or Tali?
Ask 2: Who carries out the state's laws?
Ask 3: Who decides cases about the state's laws?

## Day 29: Town Form Card

In Quill Town, voters chose Ezra. Ezra votes on town laws. Marit was hired to run the town's daily work.

Ask 1: Which person is the town manager?
Ask 2: What job shows who the manager is?

## Day 30: School Card

Residents elected Tess and six others. They vote on the school budget. They hired Ormond to oversee all the schools. Rex runs one school building.

Ask 1: Who is on the school committee?
Ask 2: Who leads the daily running of all the schools?

## Day 31: Town Hall Card

A made-up state collects a tax. It gives some to a town to fix the town hall's heating. A child says the repair was free.

Ask 1: Who spent the state's money on the heating?
Ask 2: A child says the repair was free. What paid for it?

## Day 32: Town Exit Notices

Notice A (our town; the teacher replaces this default). Notice of Meeting. The Alder Town Council will meet Tuesday at 7 pm in the town hall. The public may speak for two minutes each. The Council will vote on a new rule for the town park.

Notice B (the comparison town; the teacher replaces this default). Notice of Town Meeting. The voters of Fenn Town will gather Saturday at 10 am in the school gym. The voters will vote on the budget and on a new rule for the town pool. Anyone may watch.

Ask 1: Notice A. Name the body in this notice that votes on rules.
Ask 2: Notice B. Name the body in this notice that votes on rules.
