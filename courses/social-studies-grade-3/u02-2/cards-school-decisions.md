# Who decides at school? (day 30)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Six decision cards. Sort onto three columns: OUR TEACHER, OUR PRINCIPAL, THE COMMITTEE OR SUPERINTENDENT

1. Where to sit today.
2. A rule for the whole school building.
3. The school calendar for the whole district.
4. The daily bus plan for all the schools.
5. Who goes first to the water fountain this week.
6. Who is hired to lead all the schools.

## Role chips

COMMITTEE MEMBER. SUPERINTENDENT. PRINCIPAL. Say how each gets the job, as the district page says.

## Cedar Hill district (invented)

The Cedar Hill school committee has five members. They vote on the budget and the calendar. They hired a superintendent. The superintendent runs the schools each day.
