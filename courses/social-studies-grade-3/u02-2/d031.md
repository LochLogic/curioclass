# Day 31: How taxes and fees pay for services

## Teacher page

### Before class
Set out the four service cards, the jar and a pile of counters, the tax or fee cards and the strips. No child says what anyone pays; no amounts are used. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 28 to 32 (key-exits-28-32.md), out of the room until the exit cards are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 30's exit slips. Practice pages stay in the folder. Teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right. Error A: "Rex is the superintendent" beside "Rex runs one school building. The superintendent, hired by the committee, leads all the schools." Or: "The committee runs the buses each day" beside "The committee sets policy; the superintendent runs the daily work."

#### 9 min. What do services cost?
Show the four service cards beside the jar. For each, ask what it needs: people, materials, money. Say: free to use is not free to provide. Each child writes [[s02-P31a]].

#### 10 min. Tax or fee?
Review (grade 1): a tax is money many people put in together for what everyone uses, like coins in one jar. New: a fee is money paid by the person who uses one service. A tax is required; a gift is chosen. New: a property tax is money collected from owners of buildings and land. It is one source of school money, not the only one: schools also get money from the state and the country (Source U2L, National Center for Education Statistics; Source U2M for fee and property tax). Pairs sort the eight made-up payment cards. Each child writes [[s02-P31b]], [[s02-P31c]] and [[s02-P31d]].

#### 8 min. Collect, then spend
Show the three-box strip: payers, collector, spender. Trace snow clearing once: many people of the town put counters in the jar (payers); the town government holds the jar (the collector); it spends counters on the snow clearing (the spender). Then say: a state may collect a tax and send some of its money to a town's schools, and the town spends it on school books (Source U2L). **Ask.** Who collects the money, and who spends it? Expect: the government collects it, then spends it; they may be different governments.

#### 5 min. A fresh service
Pairs fill a strip for a new made-up service, a town street sweeper: each child writes [[s02-P31e]], [[s02-P31f]] and [[s02-P31g]]. Two pairs share, 1 minute each; others pass.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Town Hall Card, first seen here. Read it and the two asks once. Every child writes [[s02-E31]] and [[s02-E31b]].

**Shared shape.** The service cards, payment cards and strip go from a service to who pays, collects and spends; the exit asks which government spent a tax in a new case.

### Other rooms
No jar: draw a jar on the board and use pencil marks for counters.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use coins, beans or buttons. No cards: write each payment on a slip.

**Skip.** The tax or fee cards.

- **8 min. What it costs.** Name four things a town gives that need money, such as clean water. [[s02-P31a]]
- **12 min. Tax or fee?** Your adult reads each payment card. [[s02-P31b]] [[s02-P31c]] [[s02-P31d]]
  **Say.** "What is the difference between a tax and a fee?" Expect: a tax is paid by many for what many use; a fee is paid by the one who uses a service.
- **12 min. Collect, then spend.** Use the jar. [[s02-P31e]] [[s02-P31f]] [[s02-P31g]]
  **Say.** "Who collects the money, and who spends it?" Expect: the government collects it, then spends it.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Town Hall Card once. [[s02-E31]] [[s02-E31b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: [[s02-P31b]] and [[s02-P31c]], then the strip. Longer: fill a second strip for school books.

### Words and supports
Words: tax, fee, property tax, collect, spend. Frame: "The ___ collects the money, then the ___ spends it." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 32 finds our own town's government on its official pages and compares it with a town in another state. If many made error C on day 31's exit (the repair taken as free), day 32's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one. If many made error L (a tax taken as a gift), sort tax, fee and gift cards.
