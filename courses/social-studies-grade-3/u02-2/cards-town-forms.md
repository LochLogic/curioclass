# Three real town and city forms (day 29)

Maker, date and link are read aloud before each card.

## Card 1: Boston, Massachusetts (mayor and council)

City of Boston website, read 2026-11-21. The City Council has 13 councilors, elected by Boston's citizens every two years. The council writes and changes local laws. It approves the city's budget each year. The Mayor is the chief executive officer of the City.

## Card 2: Boulder, Colorado (council and manager)

City of Boulder website, read 2026-11-21. Boulder has a council-manager form of government. The elected City Council sets policies. A city manager, appointed by the council, administers them.

## Card 3: Lexington, Massachusetts (Select Board and Town Meeting)

Town of Lexington website, read 2026-11-21. An elected five-member Select Board makes policy and oversees the town. A full-time Town Manager, appointed by the Select Board, runs daily work. About 200 elected members of the Town Meeting appropriate funds and revise bylaws.
