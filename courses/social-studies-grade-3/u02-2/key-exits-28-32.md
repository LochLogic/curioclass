# Teacher key, exit cards days 28 to 32

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-28-32.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 28: Brynn makes the laws; Cole carries them out; Tali decides cases. Not "the governor makes all the laws" (A).
- Day 29: Marit is the manager, hired to run the daily work. A title alone ("she is called the manager") does not say the job (X). Ezra is the councillor, chosen by voters to vote on town laws. Not "Marit is the mayor" (B).
- Day 30: Tess and the six others are the school committee. Ormond, hired by them, is the superintendent and leads the daily running. Rex runs one building. Not "Rex is the superintendent" (A).
- Day 31: the town spent it. The state collected the tax and gave some to the town. The repair was paid by tax money from many people, so it was not free (C) and not a gift (L).
- Day 32, default cards: Notice A, the Alder Town Council; Notice B, the voters at Fenn Town Meeting. The people who speak or watch do not vote (K). The two bodies differ (B). If the teacher replaced the notices, the overlay governs: the body each new notice says votes on rules.
