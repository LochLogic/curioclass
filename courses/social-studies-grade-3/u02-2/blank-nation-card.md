# Nation card (day 28)

About 50 words, read aloud once, about the tribal nation nearest the school. The text is from the nation's own page, by its own name, in the present tense. The teacher fills the lines before the day. Where the page does not say, the card says "the page does not say."

## Fallback card, if the nearest nation's page is not ready

The Navajo Nation lists its government on its own website. The page lists an Executive branch, with an Office of the President. It lists a Legislative branch, with the Navajo Nation Council. It lists a Judicial branch, with the Supreme Court of the Navajo Nation. The Navajo Nation governs itself today.
