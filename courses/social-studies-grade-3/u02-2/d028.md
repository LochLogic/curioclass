# Day 28: Three jobs of a government, and a nation that governs itself

## Teacher page

### Before class
Set out the three baskets (MAKES LAWS, CARRIES OUT LAWS, DECIDES CASES), the three-jobs cards (cards-three-jobs), day 27's leader cards and the Bayview cards. Fill the nation card from the nearest tribal nation's own page, or use its fallback. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 28 to 32 (key-exits-28-32.md), out of the room until the exit cards are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 27's exit slips; teach error A: write "The mayor tests it" beside "Ten towns share the lake, so the state looks after it. Each level looks after needs of its own size." Whole class.

#### 14 min. Leaders and the three jobs
3 min. Review (grade 1, day 26): place the mayor, governor and President cards on town, state and country. Review (day 27): a power is what an official may do; a duty, what an official must do.

11 min. The national government is the worked example. Read each national card aloud once with its source line (Source U2A, USA.gov; Source U2B, the National Archives) and put it in its basket. New: a branch is one part of a government with its own job. Congress is a legislature: lawmakers. Say: "A lawmaker may make laws, not carry them out. The President may carry them out, not make them." Say a state has the same three jobs (Source U2D); a town may share them another way (days 29 to 32). **Ask.** Which job does each person do? Expect: Congress makes laws, the President carries them out, courts decide cases. Each child writes [[s02-P28a]] to [[s02-P28c]].

#### 8 min. A made-up country
Pairs. Say that Bayview is made up; show its three picture cards. Pairs sort the eight job cards into the baskets, 5 min; each child then writes [[s02-P28d]] and [[s02-P28e]]. Meanwhile up to 5 children who missed day 27's country slip meet you, 1 minute each. Practice pages stay in the folder.

#### 10 min. A nation that governs itself
Read the nation card aloud once, with its maker, date and link first (review: who made it, when and why, day 25). Review (grade 1, grade 2): a nation is a people with their own government; this one has its own leaders and laws, set up by itself, today. You learned in grade 1 that the Navajo Nation elects a President today, a reminder only. Another nation sets up its government its own way. Pairs finish [[s02-P28f]] from the card; where the page does not say, write "the page does not say." No child speaks for a nation. Six children, 45 seconds each, say one line the page says; note them on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Job Card, first seen here. Read it and two asks; every child writes [[s02-E28]] and [[s02-E28b]]. Then unfold the Exit box, read the third ask; each child writes [[s02-E28c]].

**Shared shape.** The cards go from a job to its basket; the exit goes from a job to the person who holds it, in a new set of people.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw three baskets on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No baskets: draw three boxes. No nation page: your adult reads the fallback card.

**Skip.** The Bayview card sort.

- **4 min. Look back.** Who leads a town, a state and a country?
- **10 min. Three jobs.** Your adult reads each national card aloud. [[s02-P28a]] [[s02-P28b]] [[s02-P28c]]
  **Say.** "Which job does each person do?" Expect: Congress makes laws, the President carries them out, courts decide cases.
- **8 min. Bayview.** [[s02-P28d]] [[s02-P28e]]
- **10 min. A nation.** Read the nation card. [[s02-P28f]]
  **Say.** "Does this nation have its own leaders and laws?" Expect: yes, today.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Job Card once. [[s02-E28]] [[s02-E28b]] [[s02-E28c]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: [[s02-P28a]] to [[s02-P28c]] only. Longer: write a fourth Bayview card and say which job it does.

### Words and supports
Words: branch, legislature, lawmaker, court, nation. Frame: "The ___ makes the laws." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 29 compares how towns and cities are set up. If many made error A on day 28's exit (one person does every job), day 29's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one.
