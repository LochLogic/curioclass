# Tax or fee? (day 31)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Sort each card into TAX or FEE.

1. A ticket to swim at the town pool.
2. Money many owners pay each year for schools and roads.
3. A charge to park in the town beach lot.
4. Money many people pay together for fire engines.
5. A ticket to enter the town museum.
6. Money owners of buildings and land pay to the town.
7. A charge to rent the town hall room for a party.
8. Money a state collects from many people and shares with towns.

A tax is paid by many people for what many use. A fee is paid by the person who uses one service.
