# Bayview (day 28)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

Bayview is a made-up country. It has three picture cards: a LAWMAKING COUNCIL, a CHIEF, a COURT.

## Eight job cards, to sort into three baskets

1. Writes a new law for Bayview.
2. Carries out the new law.
3. Hears a case about the law.
4. Votes on a law.
5. Leads the people who carry out laws.
6. Decides who was right in a case.
7. Changes an old law.
8. Makes sure the law is followed.

Pairs put each card in a basket: MAKES LAWS, CARRIES OUT LAWS, DECIDES CASES.
