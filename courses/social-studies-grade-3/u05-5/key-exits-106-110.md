# Teacher key, exit cards days 106 to 110

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-106-110.md. Marking: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- E106: lost grazing meadow, caused by farms spreading; or illness, caused by the ship's fever. Each effect has its own cause. Not one cause for both (N), and not "they chose to leave" for a seizure (F).
- E107: V kept the town safe; W kept the town's shared pasture. Not "V ruled both" (AC).
- E108: some children learned letters at home; it does not show every home. Not "every home" (G).
- E109: Tom. Hester cannot, because enslavement denied her that choice. Not "both of them" or "both are servants" (Q).
- E110: children learned to read then and now. A learner and a place to learn also fits. Not only "everything changed" (O).
