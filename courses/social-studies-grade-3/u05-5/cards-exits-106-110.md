# Exit cards, days 106 to 110

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. Answers are spoken, pointed or written. No Native nation or person is made up on any of these cards.

## Day 106: New Cause Card (made up)

In a made-up town, families grazed sheep on a river meadow for many years. New farms spread over the meadow. Then a ship brought a fever, and many people fell ill.

Ask: New Cause Card. Name one effect on the people there, and its cause.

## Day 107: New Leader Pair (made up)

Governor V ran a town. Captain W led a farm town that shared a pasture. V kept the town safe. W kept the town's shared pasture.

Ask: New Leader Pair. What did each leader want to keep safe?

## Day 108: New Object Caption (made up)

Caption: A prayer book kept on a shelf. A child in this family learned letters from it at home. Other homes may have done differently.

Ask: New Object Caption. What does it show about learning at home?

## Day 109: New Work Terms Card (made up)

Tom is a servant with a five-year term. Hester is an enslaved woman.

Ask: New Work Terms Card. Who is free to leave after five years, Tom or Hester?

## Day 110: New Learning Exit Pair (made up)

Then: in a made-up town in 1650, a child learned letters at a neighbor's kitchen table. Now: a child learns letters in a classroom with a teacher.

Ask: New Learning Exit Pair. What stayed the same?

The keys are in key-exits-106-110.md. Do not give them to children.
