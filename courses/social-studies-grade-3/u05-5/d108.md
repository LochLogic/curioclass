# Day 108: Homes and schooling

## Teacher page

### Before class
Hand back day 107's written tasks with a cue. Write the house caption: "A reconstruction at a museum village: a house built on a timber frame, furnished with reproductions. It shows what the first houses may have looked like." Write the school summary (below). Print the exit slip. No child describes their own home.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair (AC from E107)
If many missed day 107's exit (error AC, one leader rules all), all 5 minutes go here; if few, those children use the side table. Read: Mayor Dale was chosen by a town's families. Captain Orla was hired by a ship's owner. Both are made up. Each child writes [[s05-W108a]].

#### 5 min. Who made it?
Review: you asked who made it, when and why on day 95. A reconstruction is a building made later from evidence; it is later evidence, not a photograph of then. A painting or drawing is a later picture. An artifact is an object made and used long ago.

#### 10 min. A house
Show and read the house caption. **Ask.** "Who made this house, and when?" Expect: a museum, in our time, from evidence. **Ask.** "Does one house show every family's home?" Expect: no, only this one. Each child writes [[s05-P108c]].

#### 10 min. School and learning
Read the summary once. Boston Latin School was founded in 1635. A 1642 law required parents to see that children learned to read. Most children learned with a hornbook, a printed page on a thin board under a sheet of horn. Schooling was aimed mainly at boys; girls were not shut out of every town. Pairs fill three columns, who, where and with what. Each child writes [[s05-P108b]] and [[s05-P108d]]. Hear a rotating six, about 45 seconds each.

#### 8 min. Record
Each child writes [[s05-P108a]]. Practice; cue after class; revised on day 119. Hear a rotating six, about 45 seconds each, and list their names.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the slip with the New Object Caption, new today. Read it once. Each child writes [[s05-E108]]. Key: key-exits-106-110.md.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the caption and summary on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No museum picture: use the caption alone.

**Skip.** The pairs.

- **5 min. Repair.** [[s05-W108a]] **Say.** "Does Dale lead Orla?" Expect: no.
- **12 min. House.** Read the caption. [[s05-P108c]]
- **13 min. School.** Read the summary. [[s05-P108b]] [[s05-P108d]] [[s05-P108a]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card once. [[s05-E108]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: keep the house, one school box and the exit. Longer: add what the summary does not tell.

### Words and supports
Words: reconstruction, artifact, hornbook, caption. Frame: "The caption shows ___." Read aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers.
