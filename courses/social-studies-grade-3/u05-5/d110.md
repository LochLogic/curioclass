# Day 110: Then and now

## Teacher page

### Before class
Hand back day 109's written tasks with a cue. Write a short record of this classroom: desks, books, light, heat. Have the day 108 house caption and school summary ready. Print the exit slip. No child describes their own home.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (Q from E109)
If many missed day 109's exit (error Q, servant and enslaved are the same), all 10 minutes go here: the Work Terms Table filled again from two new made-up cards, never acted out. Cal serves three years and is then free. Mina is held for life. Each child writes [[s05-W110a]]. If few missed it, those children take 5 minutes at the side table, while the rest retrieve the day 108 home and learning record.

#### 8 min. The chart
You learned the then-and-now chart, with a row for what stayed, in kindergarten and again in grade 3, unit 1. Worked case, from memory: light to read by. Then, a candle or lamp. Now, electric light. Changed: the source of light. Stayed: people need light to see and read. Fill the chart with the class.

#### 10 min. Pairs: two pairs of cards
Pairs fill the chart for two made-up pairs. Pair 1: washing clothes by hand at a river, and a washing machine. Pair 2: the day 108 house, built on a timber frame, and a present-day house with electric light (made up, not a child's home). Each child writes [[s05-P110c]] and [[s05-P110d]]. Hear a rotating six pairs, about 45 seconds each.

#### 10 min. Record
Read the day 108 school summary beside the record of this classroom. Each child writes [[s05-P110a]]. Say: schools are not called the same; name the change and the thing that stayed. Practice; cue after class; revised on day 119. Hear a rotating six, about 45 seconds each, and list their names.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the slip with the New Learning Exit Pair, new today. Read it once. Each child writes [[s05-E110]]. Key: key-exits-106-110.md.

**Shared shape.** The chart pairs name a change and what stayed for light, washing and a house; the record uses a school summary; the exit gives a made-up kitchen-table lesson beside a classroom.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the chart on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No classroom: use your table or a room you read in.

**Skip.** The pairs.

- **7 min. Repair.** [[s05-W110a]] **Say.** "Who is free after a set time?" Expect: Cal.
- **12 min. Chart.** Light, washing and a house. [[s05-P110c]] [[s05-P110d]]
- **11 min. Record.** Read the school summary. [[s05-P110a]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card once. [[s05-E110]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: keep one pair, the record and the exit. Longer: add one more change.

### Words and supports
Words: then, now, changed, stayed. Frame: "One change is ___. One thing that stayed is ___." Read aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers.

### Tomorrow
