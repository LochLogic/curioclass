# Before, action and after boxes

One page, three boxes side by side, each about 6 cm tall, with a line at the bottom of each for a few words.

| BEFORE | ACTION | AFTER |
|---|---|---|
| What was it like? | What did the person do? | What changed? |

Print the title "Before, action and after" at the top. Used on days 1 and 2 only, then withdrawn.
