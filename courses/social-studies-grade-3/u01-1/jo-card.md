# Jo Card

Invented for practice. Jo and the garden are made up. Read this aloud once.

Before, a public garden had no signs on its paths. Jo asked the gardeners where signs would help. Jo organized sign-making and put the signs up with adults. Afterwards, visitors found their way.

Print "invented for practice" at the bottom of the card.
