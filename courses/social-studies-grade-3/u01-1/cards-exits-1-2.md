# Exit cards, days 1 and 2

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written; the day's six are also heard.

## Day 1: Sign Card

Rae lives by the old bridge. People walked past it and did not know its stories. Rae asked the neighbors for the bridge's stories and put them on a sign at the bridge. After the sign went up, visitors stopped to read.

Ask: What did Rae do about the old bridge? What changed at the old bridge after the sign went up?

## Day 2: Bike Rack Card

The class wants to put a bike rack at the school. The class says: "We want the rack in the best spot." A map of the school shows the front door, the walking path and the side wall.

Ask: Who besides students might care where the rack goes? Why might that person or group care? Which word in "the best spot" could two people read two ways?
