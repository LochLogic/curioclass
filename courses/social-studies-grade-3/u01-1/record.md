# Unit 1 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left, one row for each child, and keep one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Never write a count of reminders.

Columns: the readiness items R1 to R3 (day 1, Jo Card: no score, only the support used); one tick and a note for each exit E1 to E24; the day 23 end looks Q1 to Q13, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when it was heard and not yet seen; leave blank when not heard. A child who needs the side table has the exit heard there; note it.

Day 1 and day 2 exits are heard from the day's six, rotating through the class. Q10 and Q11 are scored after class from the revised day 2 record. Q12 and Q13 are scored from the revised question record. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell.
