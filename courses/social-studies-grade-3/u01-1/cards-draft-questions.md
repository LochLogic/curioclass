# Draft question cards (day 2 pairs)

Invented for practice. Eight cards for each pair. Pairs sort them under two headings: helps answer our big question, and does not.

Big question: Why is our town where it is?

1. Where does our town sit on a map? (helps)
2. Is there a river or lake near the town? (helps)
3. What is the best pizza? (does not)
4. Why did people choose to live here? (helps)
5. What is your favorite color? (does not)
6. Which roads cross in our town? (helps)
7. Who has the fastest bike? (does not)
8. What land or water is close by? (helps)

Teacher key: cards 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8 help; 3, 5 and 7 do not.
