# Draft question cards (day 2 pairs)

Invented for practice. Eight cards for each pair. Pairs sort them under two headings: helps answer our big question, and does not.

Big question: Why is our town where it is?

1. Where does our town sit on a map?
2. Is there a river or lake near the town?
3. What is the best pizza?
4. Why did people choose to live here?
5. What is your favorite color?
6. Which roads cross in our town?
7. Who has the fastest bike?
8. What land or water is close by?
