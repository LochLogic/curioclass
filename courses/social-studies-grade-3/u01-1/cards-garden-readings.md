# Garden question cards (day 2)

Invented for practice. The model question is "Where should the class garden go?" (a made-up class garden).

Two priority cards (what someone wants most; each can be checked on a plan):
- "Best means sunniest."
- "Best means nearest the tap."

One fact card (one reading, found by the arrow): "The tap is east of the shed."

Four cards for pairs to sort into fact or priority:
1. The class most wants flowers for bees.
2. The shed is next to the fence.
3. The helpers most want a garden that is easy to water.
4. The garden bed is four steps long.
