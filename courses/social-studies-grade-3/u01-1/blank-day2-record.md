# Day 2 record

Two boxes and a line for each child's name. Kept in the folder. It comes back for written feedback and is revised on day 22.

**Box A. Who else cares?** Write one person or group, besides you, who might care why our town is here. Then write why they might care.

**Box B. A word with two readings.** Look at "a good place for a town". Write one word people could read two ways. Write the two ways.

No child writes about their own home.
