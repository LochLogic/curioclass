# Library volunteer strips (day 2 worked example)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every strip. Three strips, cut apart.

1. BEFORE: Few children came to the library on Saturdays. The books were hard to reach.
2. ACTION: A volunteer named Ines asked families which books they wanted and built a low shelf.
3. AFTER: More children came on Saturdays and borrowed books.

Model aloud: someone who cares about the library is the librarian, who wants children reading, and the families, who want books they like.
