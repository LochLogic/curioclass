# Teacher key, exit cards days 1 to 2

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-1-2.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 1: Rae asked the neighbours for the bridge's stories and put them on a sign; visitors can read the bridge's past.
- Day 2: another person or group (the adults who keep the doorway clear, neighbours who walk the path), with a use tied to the spot; the word is best (nearest the door, or out of the walkers' way).

## Practice card keys (teacher only)

- Day 2, draft question cards: cards 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8 help; 3, 5 and 7 do not.
- Day 2, garden question cards: 1 and 3 are priorities; 2 and 4 are facts; the fact card is "The tap is east of the shed."
