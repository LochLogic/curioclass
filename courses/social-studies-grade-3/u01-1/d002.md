# Day 2: Who else cares?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The garden piece prints its two priority cards with the word best, which is today's exit word: write those two strips yourself with good. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 1 to 2 (key-exits-1-2.md) out of the room until the slips are collected.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Gap H, from day 1's exit (the Sign Card) and the Jo page. Read the library volunteer strips. Children order the three strips, name the action and its result, then tell a partner about a new card, read once: "Before, the bench by the field was broken. Pilar, a made-up girl, wrote a note to the principal. A helper fixed it. After, children could sit." Whole class if more than a third missed Jo; otherwise a small group of up to 8 at the side table, 1 minute a pair, adding the question-and-source cards for anyone not yet seen on the wonder card. The others sort the 8 draft question cards in pairs: "helps answer our big question" or "does not".

#### 9 min. Who else cares?

Model with a made-up class garden: "This matters to the helpers who water on weekends, because they need to reach the tap. It matters to the cook, because herbs must be easy to carry in." Pairs do the same for our question. Each child writes box A, about 3 minutes. The record is kept for a written cue before day 22.

**Ask.** Who else might care why our town is here, and why? Expect: another person or group with a use, such as families or people who plan roads. "I like it" is error O: ask who else.

#### 8 min. Two readings

Show two strips: "Good means sunniest." "Good means nearest the tap." Each can be checked. Show the fact card: "The tap is east of the shed." Pairs sort the four garden sort cards into fact or priority. Each child writes box B, about 3 minutes.

**Ask.** Which word in "a good place for a town" can two people read two ways? Expect: good, with two things it could mean (near water, or safe from floods). A direction such as north is error J: it has one reading.

#### 9 min. Co-write the supporting questions

If pairs sorted in the first part, read the sorted cards aloud, 2 minutes; otherwise sort now, 4 minutes. Review: in grade 1 a supporting question was a smaller question that helps answer the big one. Post each with its day: Where are we on Earth? (day 6). What does our town map show? (day 10). How did land and water help place towns? (day 15). How have people changed this place? (day 18). Whose needs does a good location serve? (day 20).

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Read the Bike Rack Card once, then each ask once. Every child writes the first two answers in the exit box on the student page ([[s01-E2]], [[s01-E2b]]) and the third on the half slip ([[s01-E2c]]). The card is first seen here.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No envelope of cards: write each question on its own scrap. No projector: read from the page.

**Skip.** The pair talk, the board and the small group.

- **9 min. A helper.** Read about Pilar and the broken bench; put before, action and after in order.
  **Say.** "What did she do? What changed?" Expect: she wrote a note; children could sit.
- **10 min. Who else cares?** Pick one question about your town. Write box A.
  **Say.** "Who else might care, and why?" Expect: another person or group and a reason.
- **11 min. Two readings.** Say "good means sunniest" and "good means nearest the tap." Write box B.
  **Say.** "Which word could be read two ways?" Expect: good, with two meanings.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-E2]] [[s01-E2b]] [[s01-E2c]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort four draft cards, not eight. Longer: a second person who might care, with a different use.

### Words and supports

Words: matters, person, group, use, reading, mean. Frames: "This matters to ___ because ___." and "'___' could mean ___ or ___." A pointed card or a home language is an answer.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error O on today's slip ("I like bikes"), day 3 opens with it read from a card, five minutes. If many gave error J (a direction word), a small group sorts one mapped fact and two priorities. Day 3 then compares a globe with a flat map.
