# Biography fallback card: Frederick Law Olmsted and the Back Bay Fens

Use only if no local town page is ready. Read aloud once. Sources: the National Park Service page on the Back Bay Fens and the page on the Frederick Law Olmsted National Historic Site, read 2026-11-21.

Frederick Law Olmsted was a landscape architect, a person who plans parks. He is no longer living. In 1878 he designed the Back Bay Fens, a park in Boston, Massachusetts. Before the park, the place was described as the foulest marsh and muddy flats in Massachusetts. Olmsted's plan joined two jobs: it handled the dirty water, and it made a park for people. Today the Back Bay Fens is part of a chain of Boston parks that the National Park Service describes as the Emerald Necklace.

Ask: What did he do? What changed? Who else might care about that park?
