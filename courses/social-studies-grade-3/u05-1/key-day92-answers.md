# Teacher key: day 91 and 92 card sets

Teacher only. Never hand to children.

- Alder Bay on the scaled strip: 1520 at mark 2, 1570 at mark 7, 1590 at mark 9, 1670 at mark 17, counting from 1500 as mark 0. 1570 to 1590 is two tens, twenty years.
- Pell Harbor: dock to school, three tens; school to bakery, fifteen tens (150 years); dock to bakery, eighteen tens.
- Centuries: 1815, the nineteenth; 1999, the twentieth; 2026, the twenty-first.
- Cobble Ford: well to mill, three tens; mill to gate, twelve tens.
- Dunmore Landing: oven 1505 first; oven to bell, six tens; bell to pump, three tens; pump to shed, four tens; shed to ferry post, three tens; ferry post to barn, three tens.
- Century mat: 1715 and 1788, eighteenth; 1805 and 1861, nineteenth; 1912 and 1999, twentieth; 2004 and 2026, twenty-first.
