# Day 91 cards: Alder Bay and Pell Harbor

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. Plain black line on white.

## Alder Bay (hook and model)

- market field, 1520
- first bridge, 1570
- mill, 1590
- school, 1670

Strip A spaces the four cards one hand apart each. Strip B spaces them by years, one mark for ten years. Ask: which strip shows how long between? On strip B, 1570 to 1590 is two marks, twenty years. 1590 to 1670 is eight marks.

## Pell Harbor (pairs)

- dock, 1515
- school, 1545
- bakery, 1695

Pairs place the three cards on their strips and say how many tens apart each pair of cards is.

## Century cards

Year cards for the century part: 1815, 1999, 2026. The teacher counts the hundreds aloud, then one more. Wrong and right for AA: "The 1800s are the eighteenth century" beside "1800 to 1899 is the nineteenth century: eighteen hundreds are done, so count one more."
