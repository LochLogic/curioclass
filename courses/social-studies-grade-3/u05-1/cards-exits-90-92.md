# Exit cards, days 90 to 92

Print "made up, for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken. The day 90 card is kept in the folder and comes back on day 119 (revised) for the day 120 record.

## Day 90: Mill Creek Dock Card

The town of Mill Creek plans a dock on the creek. The town clerk keeps the plan. People fish and boat on the creek, and a family sells berries beside the creek path. The clerk must answer one question: should the town build the dock?

Ask 1: Mill Creek Dock Card, invented. Who besides the town clerk needs the answer?
Ask 2: Mill Creek Dock Card. Why does that group need the answer?

## Day 91: Hollis Cove Strip Card

Hollis Cove built its first inn in 1640. It built its first library in 1680.

Ask: Hollis Cove Strip Card. Put both on your strip, one mark for ten years.

## Day 92: Voyage Dates Card

A record lists two voyages. One is dated 1530. The other is dated 1550.

Ask: Voyage Dates Card, invented. A record dates two voyages to 1530 and 1550. Which century?

The teacher key is in key-exits-90-92.md. Do not give it to children.
