# Day 92: Place events and count centuries

## Teacher page

### Before class
Cut the Dunmore Landing cards and the century mat. Make a face-down answer card for each pair from key-day92-answers.md. Return day 91's slips and strips with a written note. Keep the keys away until slips are in. No new content today; nothing is drafted.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 91's slip, strip and practice (s05-E91, s05-P91a, s05-P91b, s05-P91c), marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Show the wrong beside the right for error A: the Alder Bay cards spaced one hand apart each, beside cards spaced by years. Everyone builds a fresh line from the three Cobble Ford cards (made up): well 1525, mill 1555, gate 1675 [[s05-P92a]], and checks it against the answer strip. If few made error A, they work at the side table and the rest begin round 1.

#### 12 min. Round 1: place and measure
Pairs deal six Dunmore Landing cards (made up): oven 1505, bell 1565, pump 1595, shed 1635, ferry post 1665, barn 1695. Each child places them on a strip: [[s05-P92b]]. Each pair picks two cards, says which came first and how many tens apart, and each child writes [[s05-P92c]]. The partner checks against the face-down answer card. Hear a rotating six pairs, about a minute each, and list their names.

#### 8 min. Round 2: centuries
Pairs sort eight year cards onto the century mat (columns: eighteenth, nineteenth, twentieth, twenty-first): 1715, 1788, 1805, 1861, 1912, 1999, 2004, 2026. Say each century aloud: count the hundreds, then one more. Each child then writes [[s05-P92d]]. No card from the 1500s is used.

#### 8 min. Round 3: a card for a partner
Each child writes a year after 1700 on a blank card, [[s05-P92e]], and its century on the back, [[s05-P92f]]. The partner places it on the century mat and checks the back. This round is the compressible block: drop it and the day runs 32 minutes.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the quarter slip with the Voyage Dates Card, first seen here. Read it once. Each child writes [[s05-E92]]. Key: key-exits-90-92.md.

**Shared shape.** Practice places made-up cards on a strip and sorts later years onto a mat; the exit gives two voyage dates from a century not practised today and asks for their century.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the mat on the board; children copy the columns.

### At home

**Swap.** No answer cards: check against the answers the guide gives.

**Skip.** The partner round and the class check.

- **10 min. Cobble Ford.** [[s05-P92a]] **Say.** "How many tens from the well to the mill?" Expect: three tens.
- **12 min. Dunmore Landing.** [[s05-P92b]] [[s05-P92c]]
- **10 min. Centuries.** [[s05-P92d]] **Say.** "Count the hundreds, then one more. What century is 1805?" Expect: the nineteenth.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card once. [[s05-E92]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: drop round 3. Longer: a card set for a made-up town of your own on a second strip.

### Words and supports
Words: decade, century, first, apart. Frame: "___ is in the ___ century." Pointing and a scribe are answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow
Day 93 opens with error AA (counting the 1500s as the fifteenth century) from today's exit, with a fresh row of year cards, then finds Europe, the Atlantic and North America.
