# Day 90 cards: retrieval, the shore, the worked record

Print "made up, for practice" at the bottom of each made-up card.

## Retrieval cards (grade 2, why people move)

Card 1. The Okafor family (made up). A parent took a new job in another town. The family chose to move.
Card 2. The Lindqvist family (made up). The town closed their building for repairs. The family had to leave.
Ask: Why might a family move, and how is being made to move different? Answer, for the teacher: people may move for work or safety; being made to move takes away choice.

## The shore card

A drawn shoreline with a small town, a few houses and a boat. Under it, read aloud once: In 1565 Spanish colonists settled at Seloy, a Timucua town in what is now Florida. (Florida Museum account, sources.csv U5A.) Pairs talk for two minutes: how could settlement change life for people already there?

Teach the word: a colony is a settlement far from the home country, which the home country claims.

## The worked record (made up)

Big question: "Could skaters and walkers share the new path?"
1. A question: Who uses the path most?
2. A fact it needs: when each group is there.
3. One idea read two ways: "share" means use the path together, or each keep a part.
4. The link: the answer tells us which way of sharing could work.

## Y wrong and right

Wrong: "Only our teacher needs the answer." Right: "The people who walk the path need it, so they know if it will be safe."
