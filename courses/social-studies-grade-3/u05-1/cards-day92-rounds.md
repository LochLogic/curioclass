# Day 92 cards: Cobble Ford, round cards, century mat

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. None of these years is used on a day 91 card or an exit.

## Round 0: Cobble Ford (repair)

- well, 1525
- mill, 1555
- gate, 1675

Each child builds a fresh scaled line from the three cards and checks it against the answer strip.

## Round 1: Dunmore Landing, six dated cards

oven 1505, bell 1565, pump 1595, shed 1635, ferry post 1665, barn 1695.

Pairs place them, say which came first, and say how many tens apart for two chosen pairs of cards. The partner checks against a face-down answer card.

## Round 2: century mat

Year cards: 1715, 1788, 1805, 1861, 1912, 1999, 2004, 2026. No card from the 1500s. The mat has four columns: the eighteenth, nineteenth, twentieth and twenty-first centuries. Pairs sort and say the century aloud.

## Round 3: a card for a partner

A blank card with a line for a year and a line on the back for its century. Each child writes a year from 1700 on and its century on the back.
