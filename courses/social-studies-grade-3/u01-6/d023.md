# Day 23: Unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the packet for each child. Build map L from the town map, the early map and a photograph, as the packet says, and make the overlay for [[s01-Q1]], [[s01-Q1b]], [[s01-Q1c]], [[s01-Q6]], [[s01-Q8]] and [[s01-Q8b]]. Keep the teacher key (key-day23.md) and the overlay out of the room until the packets are handed in. Take every frame and card from days 19 to 22 off the wall. Choose up to 6 children who may need to speak an answer.

### The day

#### 5 min. Packet out
Hand back day 22's written task to the folder. Hand out the packets and answer sheets. Say: answer alone, pass any prompt you like, no one helps, each prompt is read once. Read the labels on map L aloud without explaining them. The Flood Garden Card, the Boat Parade Card and the cards of G, W and C are made up and say so.

#### 30 min. Nine prompts, unaided
Read P1 and P2 aloud once each, about a minute each. Then read each prompt once, with a spoken pace cue at each change and no clock shown. The ids are on the sheet.
Q1, 3 minutes: [[s01-Q1]], [[s01-Q1b]], [[s01-Q1c]] on map L.
Q2, 4 minutes: [[s01-Q2]], [[s01-Q2b]], [[s01-Q2c]], [[s01-Q2d]], [[s01-Q2e]] on the sheet's blank grid.
Q3, 3 minutes: [[s01-Q3]] to [[s01-Q3f]] on cards W and C.
Q4, 2 minutes: [[s01-Q4]] to [[s01-Q4f]], circled and written on map R.
Q5, 4 minutes: [[s01-Q5]], the states on the political map.
Q6, 2 minutes: [[s01-Q6]], [[s01-Q6b]], circled on the three views.
Q7, 3 minutes: [[s01-Q7]], [[s01-Q7b]], [[s01-Q7c]].
Q8, 3 minutes: [[s01-Q8]] to [[s01-Q8g]].
Q9, 4 minutes: [[s01-Q9]] to [[s01-Q9d]], from P1 and P2.
A child who needs to point and speak answers to an adult in a separate slot of 2 minutes after the period, at most 6, with the answers written down. Q10, Q11, Q12 and Q13 are not asked. They are judged from records: [[s01-Q10]], [[s01-Q10b]], [[s01-Q11]] from the day 2 record as revised; [[s01-Q12]], [[s01-Q12b]], [[s01-Q12c]], [[s01-Q13]] from the day 20 record as revised; [[s01-Q2f]], [[s01-Q2g]] from the day 10 map as revised on day 22.

#### 5 min. Collect and close
Collect the packets and answer sheets and check each has a name. Nothing is self-marked. After class, mark each look-for 2, 1 or 0 on the record sheet and each component 0 or 1, with the key; spelling and handwriting are not scored.

### Other rooms
No printer: read each prompt aloud and write answers on lined paper; show maps L and R and the cards from the front.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use a map of your town and one short page about a river.

**Skip.** The scribe slot and the records.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Read each prompt once and give no help; your child writes or points. Use the sheet's questions in order.
- **12 min. Go over it.** Look together at what was missed and say the right idea once. Your child tries one part again.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: keep one item from each prompt, never the claim. Longer: none; this is a check.

### Words and supports
Words: legend, north arrow, bar, hemisphere, region, claim. Prompts and labels are read aloud. Pointing, speaking and a scribe are fine; the choices stay the child's.

### Tomorrow
Day 24 hands back day 23's assessment by component and regroups the class to reteach and redo what it showed, then gives the readiness check for unit 2.

### Credit
Ours. The park map, the climate cards, the Flood Garden Card and the Boat Parade Card are invented by Curio Class. Elwha River sources are credited in the packet.
