# Retrieval cards, day 22

Invented for practice. Six fresh cards a partner checks against the answer overlay: a legend, a north arrow, a bar, a region, a feature kind and a source detail. Each is a new case, none used elsewhere in the unit.

1. A legend card: the key says a triangle is a tent. Which symbol is the tent?
2. A north arrow card: the arrow points to the bottom edge. Which side of the page is north?
3. A bar card: the bar fits three times between the tent and the lake. About how many bars?
4. A region card: Georgia. Which region?
5. A feature card: the key shows a blue oval. What kind of feature?
6. A source card: a map shows a ford and a photograph shows wagon ruts. Name one detail.

Teacher key: the triangle; the lower side; about 3 bars; Southeast; a lake; the ford, or the wagon ruts.
