# Parallel redo items (day 24)

Invented for practice. A fresh quarter page for each missed component. The case is new; the shape matches the check component. Not scored; for the child to try again.

- Map parts: Pebble Lane Map. A legend says a square is a hut. Circle the hut. Which way is the hut from the tree if the arrow points down?
- Place on Earth: a dot on a card marks a city; is it north or south, east or west?
- Regions and features: name the region of a state, name a river on a map.
- Land and water: a made-up town beside a lake; how might the lake help explain the town?
- Change and stay: two made-up maps; name one change and one thing that stayed, each with its date.
- Claims and questions: a made-up map and a photograph; write a claim with one detail from each.
