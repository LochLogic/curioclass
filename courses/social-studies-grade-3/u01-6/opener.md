# Section 6 opener: using evidence and reviewing the unit

## What the section does

Six days, 19 to 24. Day 19 writes a claim from two sources, a map and a photograph, with the claim frame. Day 20 writes supporting questions and a question record. Day 21 writes a claim using four sources on the Source Table, and the compelling question closes. Day 22 returns written feedback and the children revise the day 10 map, the day 2 record and the day 20 question record, then a fresh map exit. Day 23 is the check: a withheld packet, not seen before, answered alone. Day 24 hands back what the check showed and gives parallel redo items for each missed component.

## How the days run

Short repair, worked example, pairs work, then a printed exit card (cards-exits-19-22.md; the day 24 exit is in the same file). The day's six are also heard. Day 23 has no exit: it is the check.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A claim uses a source's detail.** I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___.
- **A feature seen proves nothing alone.** The claim goes beyond what is seen, and a source detail supports it.
- **A question is made with a source in mind.** Which word could people read two ways? Which two facts does it need?
- **Feedback is written first, then used.** A revision is judged against the feedback cue.
- **The check is a packet children have not seen.**

## The errors in plain words

- **G:** a visible feature proves a conclusion the source does not support.
- **J:** every disagreement is just opinion.
- **K:** a claim needs no relevant source details.
- **O:** an unrelated response answers an inquiry question.
- **H:** a name or title explains a contribution.

## Supplies for the section

The Fountain Square pair, the claim frame, the Source Table, the question record, the feedback cue sheet, the retrieval cards, the day 23 packet, written move cards, parallel redo items, the record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Read the sources aloud and write the claim frame on the board.
