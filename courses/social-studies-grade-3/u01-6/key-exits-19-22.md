# Teacher key, exit cards days 19 to 22

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-19-22.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 19: I think people can walk or roll up the east ramp to the entrance. The map shows a ramp on the east side; the photograph shows the ramp reaching the entrance.
- Day 20: a fact the question needs; a word such as best or help with two readings; two of the unit's sources, each one that could give the fact.
- Day 21: I think the town might be there because people could meet and trade at the road crossing. Map A shows the town at a crossing; Record B says a market opened beside it.
- Day 22: about 5 bars.
- Day 24: west (the arrow points to the left edge, so the lower side of the page is west).
