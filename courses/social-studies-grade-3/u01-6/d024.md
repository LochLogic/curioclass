# Day 24: Fix what the check showed

## Teacher page

### Before class
Fill the record sheet from day 23. Make four groups: locate (error A, C, L); map conventions (error B, D, F, S); relationships (error E, G, P, Q, R); evidence and questions (error H, I, J, K, M, N, O). Put the written move cards and a redo quarter page for each missed component at each table. A child who missed nothing takes the extension. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 19 to 22 (key-exits-19-22.md), out until the exit is collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Give each child day 23's assessment results by component, 0 or 1, and one written note. Each child circles one component to fix. Show the answer key on the projector after the packets are handed back.

#### 20 min. Grouped reteach
Each group sits with the teacher for 5 minutes in turn: locate, map conventions, relationships, evidence and questions. The other groups work in pairs from the written move cards for their letters. The map conventions group also takes day 22's exit: if many made error D on day 22's exit (bars read as steps), lay a strip along a fresh route and count whole bars. Hear each child's reasoning once.

#### 5 min. Parallel redo
Each child tries a fresh redo quarter page for a missed component, about 3 minutes; it is not scored with the check. Then every child writes [[s01-E24]] on the quarter slip beside the Pine Fold Map; read the ask once. The map is first seen here.

#### 10 min. Unit 2's readiness check
Review of grade 1. Read three made-up cases aloud, each once. Children answer on the sheet by writing, drawing or telling a scribe. Score reasoning, not reading speed.
Sam Card: "The crosswalk by the school was faded and hard to see. Sam told the principal and helped families make a poster asking the town to paint it. Now the lines are bright." [[s02-R1]], [[s02-R2]], [[s02-R3]].
Class Vote Card: "A class chooses a materials helper. Each voter casts one secret ballot. Ari gets 7 ballots and Ren gets 4." [[s02-R4]], [[s02-R5]], [[s02-R6]].
Sign Team Card: "A group prepares a public garden sign. One person gathers facts, another draws and another checks the words." [[s02-R7]], [[s02-R8]].
Results guide oral support on day 25.

**Shared shape.** The Pine Fold Map is a new case. The redo cards share the shape of each check component; each is a new case, not scored with the check.

### Other rooms
No projector: read the answer key aloud by component. No printer: write the redo on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use a page from any past map task.

**Skip.** The groups and the scribe.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at one missed idea.
  **Say.** "What is the right idea?" Expect: the move card's one-line fix.
- **15 min. Try again.** Redo one part on a fresh map or claim.
  **Say.** "How did you know?" Expect: the key, the arrow or a detail from a source.
- **10 min. Unit 2 check.** Read the three cases aloud; your child answers ([[s02-R1]] [[s02-R4]] [[s02-R7]]).
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-E24]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one redo, one case read aloud. Longer: a second redo from the extension.

### Words and supports
Words: component, redo, leader, vote, ballot, goal. Pointing, drawing, speaking and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 25 opens unit 2 with the rules students already know at school and in town. The readiness answers guide oral support there.
