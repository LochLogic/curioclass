# Exit cards, days 19 to 22 and day 24

Print "invented for practice" at the bottom of each card. Every child answers in writing; the day's six are also heard.

## Day 19: Court Pair

A made-up court with a map and a photograph. The map shows a ramp on the east side. The photograph shows the ramp reaching the entrance. Ask: write one claim about how people enter the court; name both sources, and one detail each gives.

## Day 20

Use the board's five supporting questions. Ask: pick one you did not choose today and write one fact it needs and two sources that could give it; in the same question write one word people could read two ways.

## Day 21: Town Exit Pair

A made-up Map A of a town at a road crossing and a made-up Record B that says a market opened beside that crossing. Ask: write one claim: why might the town be there? Write the detail you used from Map A and from Record B.

## Day 22

A fresh bar map with two marked places five bars apart. Ask: how many whole bars apart are the two marked places?

## Day 24: Pine Fold Map

Invented. A map of a farm with a barn, a well on the lower side of the page from the barn, and a north arrow that points to the left edge. Ask: which way is the well from the barn? Use the arrow.

The teacher key is in key-exits-19-22.md, a separate piece. Do not give it to children.
