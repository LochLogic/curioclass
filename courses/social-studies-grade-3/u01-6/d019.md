# Day 19: A claim from a map and a photograph

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the Fountain Square pair; both sources are made up, and you say so. Put the claim frame card on the wall. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 19 to 22 (key-exits-19-22.md), out of the room until slips are collected.

### The day

#### 12 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 18's exit slips and its written tasks with the written cue, 1 minute. Retrieval, 3 minutes. Review: in grade 2 you named a source and what it shows. Read: "A photograph shows bicycles on a ferry." Partners say what claim it supports and name the source.

Eight minutes for the more-missed error on day 18's exit. If many made error I (a blank place called empty): set the early map beside one line from the Nation Card page, read in the present tense. Pairs say what each adds. If many made error R (one change ends everything): use the Orchard Lane pair, invented for practice. 1980 shows a pond, a lane and a field; 2010 shows the pond, the lane and a playground. If neither was common, take error I.

#### 7 min. Who made it, and what it shows
Show the Fountain Square pair. Review: who made it, when and why. Read the credits: the map, made by the made-up parks office in 2019 to help visitors; the photograph, taken by a made-up visitor in 2021. Use the shows, suggests, cannot tell card from day 9. Shows: the stop and four paths; the stand and people waiting. Suggests: people use the stop. Cannot tell: how many ride.

**Ask.** Who made each source, when and why? Expect: the made-up parks office, 2019, to help visitors; a made-up visitor, 2021.

#### 8 min. A spoken claim
Review: since kindergarten you have said "I think, because". Model: "I think people arrive by bus, because the map shows a bus stop on the south side and the photograph shows people waiting there." A claim is what you say is true; evidence is a detail you can point to in a named source. New word: reasoning says how the detail supports the claim. Pairs each say a claim, 1 minute each way.

**Ask.** What does each source show, and what do you think because of it? Expect: a detail from each, then "I think".

#### 8 min. One written claim
Each child writes the claim they said, on the frame ([[s01-P19]]). The small group of up to 6 takes its repair first, 5 minutes, then writes with you.

Check: a partner reads the sentence aloud. Does it name the map and the photograph, with one detail from each?

#### 5 min. Exit ticket
Give each child the Court Pair beside the half slip; read both asks once. Every child writes [[s01-E19]] and [[s01-E19b]]. The pair is first seen here.

**Shared shape.** The Fountain Square pair is spoken first. The exit is a new pair, with no partner.

### Other rooms
No printer: sketch the map and describe the photograph on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pair: use a map and a photograph of one park or street.

**Skip.** The retrieval, the repair and the partner check.

- **10 min. Two sources.** Set a map and a photograph of one place side by side.
  **Say.** "Who made each one, and what does it show?" Expect: a maker and a detail.
- **20 min. A claim.** Say a claim, then write it ([[s01-P19]]).
  **Say.** "Why do you think that?" Expect: how a detail supports it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-E19]] [[s01-E19b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one claim said, not two. Longer: a second claim from the same pair, with what the pair cannot tell.

### Words and supports
Words: claim, evidence, reasoning, source, shows. Frame: "I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___." A spoken claim, a drawing or a scribe is an answer.

### Tomorrow
If many named both sources with no detail from either (error K), day 20 opens by linking one detail from each of two sources to a new claim; if many went past what the sources show (error G), it sorts three claim cards into shows, suggests and cannot tell. Day 20 then lists the facts a supporting question needs.
