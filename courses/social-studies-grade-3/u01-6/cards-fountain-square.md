# Fountain Square pair (day 19)

Invented for practice. A map and a photograph of one made-up place, Fountain Square. The map shows a bus stop on the south side and four paths into the square. The photograph shows a covered stand by the bus stop and people waiting there. Ask pairs: write a claim. I think ___ because the ___ shows ___ and the ___ shows ___. Model: I think people arrive by bus because the map shows a bus stop on the south side and the photograph shows people waiting there.
