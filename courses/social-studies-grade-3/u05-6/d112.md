# Day 112: Their own words

## Teacher page

### Before class
This day tells hard history. Put a stop card on each desk; any child may point to it or pass. No role play. Hand back day 111's written tasks and exit slips with a cue. Print the exit slip. Read the clause aloud as written; read no slur, and say "Black people" for the source's older word.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (H or R from E111)
If many missed day 111's exit on error H (slavery was only in the South), take 10 minutes: read the made-up record, "In Hartwell, a northern port, enslaved men worked on the wharf in 1700"; pairs name the place and say what it shows; each child writes [[s05-W112a]]. If many missed error R (all Africans in Boston were enslaved), take 5 minutes: read two made-up records, Nia kept a shop and could leave; Omar was held for life; each child writes [[s05-W112b]]. If few missed either, those children use the side table.

#### 7 min. Who wrote it?
Review: who made it, when and for whom (day 95). Read: on 25 May 1774 a great number of enslaved Black people of Massachusetts sent a petition to the Governor, Council and House of Representatives. A petition is a written request to officials. Each child writes [[s05-P112a]].

#### 9 min. The request
Read the clause aloud once, then the summary. Clause: "we have in common with all other men a naturel right to our freedoms without Being depriv'd of them by our fellow men." Summary: like everyone else, we have a natural right to be free, and no one should take it from us. Naturel means natural; depriv'd means taken away. To resist is to act against being enslaved. Each child writes [[s05-P112b]]. Hear a rotating six, about 45 seconds each.

#### 6 min. Building lives
Say: enslaved people were skilled workers, in crafts and at sea as well as in homes and on farms; their lives were lives, not only suffering. Review: in grade 2 you read about Venture Smith, captured as a child, held, and later buying his freedom. The petition writers wrote as people with rights. Nothing about families or faith is told here beyond what the pages say.

#### 6 min. Record
Each child writes [[s05-P112c]]. Hear a rotating six, about 45 seconds each, and list their names.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the slip with the Petition Exit Fragment, new today. Read it once with its summary. Each child writes [[s05-E112]]. Key: key-exits-111-112.md.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the summary on the board; read the clause aloud once.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: keep the source line, the summary and the exit. Longer: add one kind of skilled work.

### Words and supports
Words: petition, resist, clause, natural right. Frame: "They say ___." Read aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.
