# Exit cards, days 111 and 112

Print "made up, for practice" only where marked. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. Answers are spoken or written.

## Day 111: Charlestown Labor Card

Read aloud once. In Charlestown, near Boston, Jack and Acton were enslaved potters at the Parker pottery in 1723. (City of Boston Archaeology, U5R.)

Ask 1: Charlestown Labor Card. Does it support "slavery was only in the South"?
Ask 2: Charlestown Labor Card. Say why.

## Day 112: Petition Exit Fragment

Read aloud once, with the summary. The writers wrote: "that you will accordingly cause an act of the legislative to be pessed." (Founders' Constitution, U5Q.) Summary: they ask that the lawmakers pass a law.

Ask: Petition Exit Fragment. What did the writers do to resist?

The keys are in key-exits-111-112.md. Do not give them to children.
