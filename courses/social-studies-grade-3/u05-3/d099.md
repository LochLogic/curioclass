# Day 99: Two papers about the 1622 war

## Teacher page

### Before class
Hang the wall strip. Write the dates 1607, 1622 and 1646 on cards for the board. Keep key-exits-97-99.md away until slips are in. Keep a class list for who was heard. The war is named plainly; no role play, no graphic words.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (C from E98)
Hand back day 98's slip and practice (s05-E98, s05-P98a, s05-P98b, s05-P98c, s05-P98d, s05-P98e, s05-P98f, s05-P98g, s05-P98h) marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Write the wrong, "The explorer's page tells all of it," beside the right, "Each page tells part." Review (day 95): who made it, when and why. Pairs read the Beach Pair on the page, both made up, and say what each says and leaves out. Each child writes [[s05-P99a]] and [[s05-P99b]]. If few made error C, they work at the side table.

#### 5 min. Timeline
Review (day 91): one mark is ten years. Place 1622 on the wall strip. **Ask.** "How many years after 1607?" Expect: fifteen, one and a half tens. Each child writes [[s05-P99c]] on the strip in the record folder.

#### 12 min. The 1622 war, two papers
Say plainly: Powhatan people and the English at Jamestown fought in 1622. Put the dated cards in order on the board. Say what the Pamunkey Indian Tribe's history page gives: the English settled in 1607 and conflict began; a second war began in 1622; a 1646 treaty ended the third war. Dates first, then what led to what; a war does not just happen. Made up, to practice: in 1990 a town built a footbridge, in 1992 walkers said it was too narrow, in 1995 the town widened it. Pairs say the order aloud; the early events came first. Read Label 1 and Label 2 on the page once each. The label comes first: maker, date, purpose. We have not read the company's report. A paper made to defend a side tends to say what helps that side. Review (day 96): shows, might suggest, cannot tell. Each child writes [[s05-P99d]] and [[s05-P99e]].

#### 8 min. Pairs
The quiet-room pair is made up. Pairs read both papers and say who made each. Each child writes [[s05-P99f]] and [[s05-P99g]]. Hear a rotating six pairs, about a minute each, and list their names. If the day is compressed, this part shortens to 4 minutes.

#### 3 min. Board evidence
Review (day 90): the question board. Add "two sides, two papers" to it. The board's "What undermined it?" waits for day 106.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip with the Pier Rule Pair, new today. Read the papers once. Each child writes [[s05-E99]] and [[s05-E99b]]. Key: key-exits-97-99.md.

**Shared shape.** Model and practice read maker labels, match a paper to its maker and sort claims; the exit is a new pair of papers and asks why each maker might say it that way.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the pairs and the labels onto the board, and read each aloud once. Roll paper makes the strip.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: draw twenty marks, one for each year.

**Skip.** The pair round and the question board.

- **10 min. The Beach Pair.** Read both papers aloud. **Say.** "Does one paper tell all of it?" Expect: no, each tells part. [[s05-P99a]] [[s05-P99b]]
- **10 min. Two labels.** Read Label 1 and Label 2. [[s05-P99d]] [[s05-P99e]]
- **10 min. Quiet room.** Read both papers. [[s05-P99f]] [[s05-P99g]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Pier Rule Pair once. [[s05-E99]] [[s05-E99b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the pairs part to 4 minutes; the two labels and the strip stay. Longer: write two made-up papers about a playground rule, each with a maker.

### Words and supports
Words: paper, maker, purpose, war, treaty. Frame: "Paper ___ was made by ___ to ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read every paper aloud once.

### Tomorrow
Day 100 opens with error C (one account tells all of it) from today's exit, five minutes, with one more pair of maker and purpose labels, then places Plymouth (1620) on the strip.

### Credit
Ours. The war facts are in our words from the Pamunkey Indian Tribe's history page; the company label is in our words from a university edition's catalogue page. The Beach Pair, the footbridge, the quiet-room pair and the Pier Rule Pair are made up.
