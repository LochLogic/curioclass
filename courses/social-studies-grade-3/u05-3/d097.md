# Day 97: St. Augustine, and who answered to whom

## Teacher page

### Before class
Hang the wall strip. Draw the Leader Card frame on the board: Name, Nation, Role, Decision, Answered to. Keep key-exits-97-99.md away until slips are in. Keep a class list for who was heard.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair (Z from E96)
Hand back day 96's slip and practice (s05-E96, s05-E96b, s05-P96a, s05-P96b, s05-P96c, s05-P96d, s05-P96e) marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Write the wrong, "The map is blank there, so no one lived there," beside the right, "A blank only means that map does not show it." Read the two Gull Cove accounts on the page, both made up. Each child writes [[s05-P97a]] and [[s05-P97b]]. If few made error Z, they work at the side table.

#### 7 min. Timeline and hook
Review (day 91): one mark is ten years. Place 1565 between the 1560 and 1570 marks. Read, from the National Park Service: on 8 September 1565 Pedro Menendez de Aviles and 800 Spanish settlers founded St. Augustine. Review (day 90): Seloy. Read, from the Florida Museum: the Spanish settled at Seloy, a Timucua town, and made its council house into a fort. Wrong: "No one was there before the colony." Right: "A Timucua town was there." Say Timucua by that name; the page tells 1565 only, so do not say they are gone or are not. Each child writes [[s05-P97c]] on the strip in the record folder.

#### 12 min. Worked leader cards
New: a leader card holds a name, a nation, a role (review, grade 1 day 6: the job a person did), a decision (a choice the person made; you learned about decisions in kindergarten) and who the leader answered to (the group the leader reports to). Fill Card 1 on the board: Pedro Menendez de Aviles; Spain; led the Spanish settlers; founded St. Augustine; answered to, blank. **Ask.** "Which group did Menendez lead?" Expect: the Spanish settlers. A blank box is honest: the page does not say. Fill Card 2: name, blank; nation, Timucua; role, the people of Seloy, with a council house; decision, blank; answered to, blank. **Ask.** "Did Menendez lead the people of Seloy?" Expect: no; they are the Timucua, with their own town. Each child writes [[s05-P97d]] and [[s05-P97e]].

#### 9 min. Pairs: who answered to whom
Brook School and Hill School are made up. Pairs read the page. Each child writes [[s05-P97f]] and [[s05-P97g]]. Hear a rotating six pairs, about a minute each, and list their names. Catch error AC at once: "Brook's principal ruled Hill too." Right: each leader answers to their own group.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the quarter slip with the Seloy Exit Card, new today. Read the card once. Each child writes [[s05-E97]]. Key: key-exits-97-99.md.

**Shared shape.** Model and practice fill a card, read a blank box and sort whom a leader answered to; the exit is a new line about Seloy and asks what the Timucua could decide.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw both frames and the Gull Cove accounts on the board; children copy. Roll paper makes the strip.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: draw ten marks, one for each ten years.

**Skip.** The pair round and the class board.

- **10 min. Two accounts.** Read the Gull Cove accounts aloud. [[s05-P97a]] [[s05-P97b]]
- **10 min. Leader card.** Read Card 1 and Card 2. **Say.** "Did Menendez lead the people of Seloy?" Expect: no, they are the Timucua. [[s05-P97d]] [[s05-P97e]]
- **10 min. Who answers?** Read Brook and Hill. [[s05-P97f]] [[s05-P97g]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Seloy card once. [[s05-E97]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the pairs part to 4 minutes; the strip and both cards stay. Longer: make a made-up leader card for a club leader and say who they answer to.

### Words and supports
Words: role, decision, answers to, Timucua, blank. Frame: "___ answers to ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read every card aloud once.

### Tomorrow
Day 98 opens with error AC (one leader's say ruled everyone) from today's exit, five minutes, with new made-up leader cards, then places Jamestown on the strip.

### Credit
Ours. The St. Augustine facts are in our words from the National Park Service and the Florida Museum. Gull Cove, Brook School, Hill School and Ms. Ito are made up.
