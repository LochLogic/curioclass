# Day 98: Jamestown and Wahunsonacock

## Teacher page

### Before class
Hang the wall strip. Lay out the four reason cards from day 93. Draw the Leader Card frame on the board with an Interest box added. Keep key-exits-97-99.md away until slips are in. Keep a class list for who was heard.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair (AC from E97)
Hand back day 97's slip and practice (s05-E97, s05-P97a, s05-P97b, s05-P97c, s05-P97d, s05-P97e, s05-P97f, s05-P97g) marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Write the wrong, "Menendez ruled the people of Seloy," beside the right, "Each led their own people." Pairs read the page, made up. Each child writes [[s05-P98a]] and [[s05-P98b]]. If few made error AC, they work at the side table.

#### 5 min. Timeline
Review (day 91): one mark is ten years. Place 1607 on the wall strip; **Ask.** "About how many tens from 1565 to 1607?" Expect: about four; forty-two years. Each child writes [[s05-P98c]] on the strip in the record folder.

#### 8 min. Why people came
Review (day 93): the four reason cards, made up, and the cause-effect strip. Read, from a reference page: in 1607 the Virginia Company founded James Fort. **Ask.** "What did the company want?" Expect: our page does not say; write the question on the board and pick no reason. Read, from the Pamunkey Indian Tribe's own history page: the English settled in 1607 and conflict began. Each child writes [[s05-P98d]].

#### 12 min. Wahunsonacock and the Virginia Company
Read, from the Pamunkey Indian Tribe's history page: Wahunsonacock, also called Powhatan, led about thirty groups; the Pamunkey were one of six core tribes. The page spells the name Wahunsonacock, and we use the nation's spelling. Read, from the tribe's reservation page: the Pamunkey Indian Tribe is a sovereign nation today, on its reservation. Review (day 97): the leader card. Fill Card A, Wahunsonacock (Powhatan; led about thirty groups), and Card B, the Virginia Company (England; founded James Fort). New meaning: an interest is what a person or group wants or needs, not the bank word from grade 1. Example, made up: Ms. Bell runs a skate park and wants the ramp safe, so she closes it for a week. **Ask.** "Which card tells the Powhatan side of 1607?" Expect: Card A. Review (kindergarten): two sides of one event. Each child writes [[s05-P98e]] and [[s05-P98f]].

#### 8 min. Pairs
Pairs read the page, made up, and say what each wants. Each child writes [[s05-P98g]] and [[s05-P98h]]. Hear a rotating six pairs, about a minute each, and list their names.

#### 2 min. Exit
Hand out the quarter slip with the Pamunkey Exit Card, new today. Read the card once. Each child writes [[s05-E98]]. Key: key-exits-97-99.md.

**Shared shape.** Model and practice read cards, match a want to a choice and fill blank boxes; the exit is a new line from the Pamunkey page and asks why a leader made one choice.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the two frames and the made-up cases on the board; children copy. Roll paper makes the strip.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: draw ten marks, one for each ten years.

**Skip.** The pair round and the question board.

- **10 min. The two cards.** Read Card A and Card B. **Say.** "Which card tells the Powhatan side?" Expect: Card A. [[s05-P98e]] [[s05-P98f]]
- **10 min. Wants and choices.** Read Mr. Kato and Mrs. Aziz. [[s05-P98g]] [[s05-P98h]]
- **10 min. Years.** Use a strip. [[s05-P98c]] [[s05-P98d]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the Pamunkey card once. [[s05-E98]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the pairs part to 4 minutes; the strip and both cards stay. Longer: write a made-up leader card with an interest and a decision.

### Words and supports
Words: interest, decision, answers to, Jamestown, blank. Frame: "___ wants ___, so ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers. Read every card aloud once.

### Tomorrow
Day 99 opens with error C (one account tells all of it) from today's exit, ten minutes, with two papers on one beach rule, then places 1622 on the strip and reads two papers about the war.

### Credit
Ours. The Jamestown, Wahunsonacock and Pamunkey facts are in our words from the Pamunkey Indian Tribe's pages and a reference page. Lake Pool, Chess Club, Ms. Bell, Mr. Kato and Mrs. Aziz are made up.
