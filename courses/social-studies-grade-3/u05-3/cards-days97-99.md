# Days 97 to 99 cards: leaders, Jamestown, two papers

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. Read each line aloud once.

## Day 97 repair (Z from day 96)

A visitor's old map and a town's own map of one coast, made up. Pairs find a farm or a town in each.

## Day 97 hook

Place 1565 on the wall strip. Read: On 8 September 1565 Pedro Menendez de Aviles and 800 Spanish settlers founded St. Augustine. (National Park Service, sources.csv U5B.)

Read again: the Spanish settled at Seloy, a Timucua town. They made its council house into a fort. (Florida Museum, U5A.) The Timucua are named by their own name.

## Worked leader cards

Card 1. Name: Pedro Menendez de Aviles. Nation: Spain. Role: led the settlers. Decision: founded St. Augustine. Answered to: the Spanish crown (the page does not say; leave the box blank and say why).
Card 2. Name: not in our record. Nation: Timucua. Role: people of Seloy, with a council house. Decision: blank. The page names no Timucua leader for Seloy.

Discuss: a blank box is honest. The cards differ in how full they are.

## Pairs: Brook School and Hill School, made up

Brook School: the principal answers to the district office far away. Hill School: the school council answers to the families who send their children.

Each pair sorts who each leader answers to and writes two lines.

## Day 98 hook

Place 1607 on the wall strip. Jamestown is later than St. Augustine: 1565 to 1607 is forty-two years. About four tens.

Read: In 1607 the Virginia Company founded James Fort. (U5L.)

## Wahunsonacock and the Pamunkey

Read, from the Pamunkey's own history page: Wahunsonacock, also called Powhatan, led about thirty groups. The Pamunkey were one of six core tribes. (U5C.) Read: the Pamunkey Indian Tribe is a recognized nation today, on its own reservation. (U5D.)

Card A. Name: Wahunsonacock. Nation: Powhatan; the Pamunkey were one of six core tribes. Role: led about thirty groups. Decision: blank. Interest: blank. The page names no decision or interest in our record.
Card B. Name: the Virginia Company. Nation: England. Role: a company. Decision: founded James Fort in 1607. Interest: blank, because the page is silent.

Say: Perspective. Two sides of one event.

## Pairs: Captain Ruiz and Elder Pita, made up

A fishing town, made up. Captain Ruiz runs the boats and decides when they go out. Elder Pita keeps the town's records and decides who may fish near the rocks. Captain Ruiz wants to fish more days. Elder Pita wants the fish to last.

Fill a card for each: role, one decision, the interest that explains it.

## Day 99 repair (C from day 98)

A pair of papers about one beach rule, made up: a lifeguard's notice and a swimmers' letter. Pairs say who made each, why, and what each leaves out.

## Day 99 hook

Place 1622 on the wall strip. 1607 to 1622 is fifteen years. One and a half tens.

## The 1622 war, said plainly

Powhatan people and the English at Jamestown fought in 1622. (Pamunkey history, U5C: a second war began in 1622.)

Dated order on the board: 1607, the English settle. 1622, a second war begins. 1646, a treaty ends the third war.

X repair with a dock quarrel, made up: In 1990 a town built a new dock. In 1992 the fishers said it blocked their boats. In 1995 the town moved the dock. Put the dates in order first, then say what led to what.

## Two papers

Paper from the Pamunkey Indian Tribe: its own history of the war, read aloud (U5C). No graphic words. Children underline one claim.

Label card: Made by the Virginia Company in 1622; Edward Waterhouse wrote it as the company's official response to the 1622 fighting, to reassure investors and migrants. It is the company's side. (U5O.) Not read in full. Maker, date and purpose come first.

## Pairs: the quiet-room pair, made up

Paper 1, by the library staff, says the reading room should stay silent. Paper 2, by the reading club, says the room should allow low talk. Pairs say who made each and why each might say it that way.

## Board

Add "two sides, two papers" to the question board.
