# Day 36: Working together

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the ten five-setting cards, but make two changes: print cards 9 and 10 as these, so no card matches the exit. Card 9: "The town plows every road after a storm with shared trucks and workers." (benefit). Card 10: "Council members disagree about how many plow trucks to buy." (difficulty). Set out five mats: family, school, workplace, volunteer group, government. Keep the teacher key, key-exits-33-37.md, out of the room until the exit slips are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 35's exit slips. Teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right. Error Y: "Leaders know what everyone needs" beside "The letters told the board there was a need." Error D: "Only voters' letters count" beside "A child can learn, ask and speak with an adult." If many made error Y, compare a fresh made-up pair on the board: a park bench plan before and after neighbors wrote in.

#### 7 min. A benefit and a difficulty
Review (grade 1): a benefit is what a choice gives you. Review (grade 1): to cooperate is to work together. New: a difficulty is what makes working together hard. Model with a class puppet show (invented): six children make it. Benefit: more hands finish the puppets sooner. Difficulty: only some can practice at recess. How they handle it: they choose a time when everyone can come. Say that every group has both. **Ask.** What helps and what is hard when people work together? Expect: a benefit and a difficulty.

#### 10 min. Five settings
Pairs sort the ten cards onto the five mats, a benefit and a difficulty on each. Read each card aloud first. The family is made up. No child tells about their own home. Visit pairs and ask "Is that a benefit or a difficulty?" Review (grade 1): a volunteer is a person who chooses to help a group's work and is not paid.

#### 12 min. The record
Each child fills five rows: [[s02-P36a]], [[s02-P36b]], [[s02-P36c]], [[s02-P36d]], [[s02-P36e]]. Eight minutes writing; use the sorted cards for ideas, and a new idea is welcome. Meanwhile up to 5 children say their rows at the side table, 1 minute each. Records stay in the folder. Write one cue on each by day 45: a benefit and a difficulty that fit each setting. Children revise on day 46.

#### 3 min. Handling it
Each child says to a partner one difficulty and one way a group handles it. A child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Pool Card, first seen here. Read the card and the two asks once. Every child writes [[s02-E36]] and [[s02-E36b]].

**Shared shape.** Practice sorts cards onto five mats and fills five rows; the exit is one town swim program, answered in two sentences. No card uses a pool.

### Other rooms
No mats: draw five boxes on the board and read the cards aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No mats: make five piles on the table.

**Skip.** The partner talk, the side table.

- **8 min. A benefit and a difficulty.** Read the puppet show.
  **Say.** "What helps and what is hard?" Expect: a benefit and a difficulty.
- **10 min. Five settings.** Sort the ten cards into five piles.
- **14 min. The record.** [[s02-P36a]] [[s02-P36b]] [[s02-P36c]] [[s02-P36d]] [[s02-P36e]]
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and asks once. [[s02-E36]] [[s02-E36b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: three settings in the record. Longer: a benefit and a difficulty for a sixth setting.

### Words and supports
Words: benefit, difficulty, cooperate, family, workplace. Frame: "Working together helps because ___. It is hard when ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 37 compares three ways of deciding. If many made error E on day 36's exit (working together taken as always easy), day 37's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one. If many made error Z (one difficulty for every setting), set "people do not get along" beside a difficulty that fits the town plan.
