# Match the rule (day 33)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. Six rule cards and six problem cards. Pairs match each rule to its problem and name one thing it protects.

## Rule cards

1. Walk, do not run, in the school hall.
2. Wash your hands before you eat lunch.
3. Wear a helmet on the school scooter track.
4. Put the paints away when you finish.
5. Raise your hand to speak in circle time.
6. Line up at the door before recess ends.

## Problem cards

A. Many hands touched the food and some children got sick.
B. Children fell off the scooters and got hurt.
C. Children bumped into each other in a crowded hall.
D. The paints dried out and could not be used again.
E. Everyone talked at once and no one could be heard.
F. Children came in late and the class waited.
