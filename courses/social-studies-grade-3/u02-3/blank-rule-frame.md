# Rule pages (day 33)

## The school rule

The school's own rule for walking in the halls, from the handbook, or "the class and teacher" for a classroom rule. Fill before the day.

Rule: ______________________________   Where it is written: ______________________

## The town law

One law from our town's own code page, with maker, date and link, chosen and checked by the teacher.

Law: ______________________________   Maker: ____________   Date: ____________   Link: ____________

If the page says why the law was made, read it. If it does not, children write: the page does not say.

## Fallback: Tumbleford (invented for practice)

Tumbleford is a made-up town. Its council made a law: no bicycles on the boardwalk. Bicycles had bumped into people who were walking. The law keeps the boardwalk safe for walkers.
