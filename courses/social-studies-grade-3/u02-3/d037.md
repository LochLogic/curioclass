# Day 37: Three ways of deciding

## Teacher page

### Before class
Print the three deciding cards and the chart. Have the unit 2 question board ready for "How can people participate?" Keep the teacher key, key-exits-33-37.md, out of the room until the exit slips are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 36's exit slips. Teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right. Error E: "Working together is always easy" beside "Working together helps; it is hard when views differ." Error Z: "People just do not get along" beside "People want the pool open on different days." If many made error E, pairs add a difficulty to the benefit on a fresh made-up card, in the same five minutes: "Four neighbors share one ladder."

#### 9 min. Three ways of deciding
Say each way and what it decides; nothing is new. Review (kindergarten): a vote is a choice by blocks or hands when we still do not agree. A class vote decides a matter, and every member votes. Review (day 29): a council is a group chosen to make a town's or city's laws; elected means chosen by voters; a town meeting is voters who gather and decide for the town. Review (day 35): public comment is a time at a public meeting when people may speak. Speakers do not vote. Review (grade 1): an election is voting to choose a leader for a job; a vote on a matter chooses a thing. You may say the grade 1 kite club picked a captain and a kite color. **Ask.** Who decides, and what do they decide? Expect: in an election, voters decide who gets the job.

#### 18 min. The comparison chart
Read the three deciding cards aloud. Pairs talk, then each child fills the chart, three rows: [[s02-P37a]], [[s02-P37b]], [[s02-P37c]]. For each, write who discusses, who decides and what is decided. Visit 8 pairs, about 1 minute each; every pair is seen once by day 38. The chart stays in the folder. Write one cue on each by day 45: all three cells in every row. Children revise on day 46.

#### 5 min. Close the board
Add the answer to "How can people participate?" on the unit 2 question board. Take it from day 35's five ways: vote, go to a meeting, serve on a board, volunteer, stay informed, and learn and ask with an adult. Then say tomorrow's work: how a class talks a choice through and hears every view.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Rilla Card, first seen here. Read the card and the two asks once. Every child writes [[s02-E37]] and [[s02-E37b]].

**Shared shape.** Practice puts one decision on each card and fills a chart; the exit puts two votes on one card in a new town and asks which is the election and how the two differ.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the chart on the board, read the cards aloud, and children copy it.

### At home

**Swap.** No second person: you are the partner.

**Skip.** The visit, the board.

- **9 min. Three ways of deciding.** Read the three cards.
  **Say.** "Who decides, and what do they decide?" Expect: voters decide who gets a job.
- **18 min. The chart.** [[s02-P37a]] [[s02-P37b]] [[s02-P37c]]
- **5 min. Taking part.** Your child names two ways people take part.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and asks once. [[s02-E37]] [[s02-E37b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: two rows. Longer: a fourth row for a decision your child knows.

### Words and supports
Words: vote, election, council, town meeting, elected. Frame: "In ___, ___ decide ___." Nobody says who in their home votes. Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 38 learns how a class talks a choice through and hears every view, using today's class vote. If many made error P on day 37's exit (a vote on the bell schedule taken for an election), day 38's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one. If many made error K (a person who speaks also votes), sort speaking and voting on a fresh notice.
