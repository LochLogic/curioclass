# Group rules (day 34)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Teacher model: the Brindle sandbox

The Brindle sandbox is for everyone. The group rule is: put the toys away when you leave. Each person's duty keeps the freedom for the next child to play.

## Two cards for pairs

**Garden club card.** The garden club agreed: water only your own row, then close the gate. Fill: the rule, each person's duty, whose freedom it protects.

**Recess ball card.** The recess ball group agreed: pass the ball to someone who has not had it. Fill: the rule, each person's duty, whose freedom it protects.

## A right is not a prize

A child forgets a duty once. Does the right stop? What does the group do?
