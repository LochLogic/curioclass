# Exit cards, days 33 to 37

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Each answer is written; some are also heard.

## Day 33: Rule Record Card

A town record (made up): Rule 12. No loud music after 9 at night. Made by the town council, March 4.

Ask 1: Rule Record Card, invented. What problem might have led to Rule 12?
Ask 2: Rule Record Card. What does Rule 12 protect?

## Day 34: Reading Group Card

A reading group agrees: one person talks at a time. Jun forgot and talked over someone.

Ask 1: Reading Group Card, invented. What duty does each person get?
Ask 2: Reading Group Card. Whose freedom does that duty protect?
Ask 3: Reading Group Card. Jun talked over someone. Should the group take away his turns?

## Day 35: Service Card

Residents wrote letters asking for a coat bin at the town hall. The board read the letters before it voted to add one. Dara says, "Leaders already know what everyone needs."

Ask: Service Card, invented. Why did the board need the residents' letters?

## Day 36: Pool Card

The town government plans a summer swim program at the town pool.

Ask 1: Pool Card, invented. Name one benefit of working together on the swim program.
Ask 2: Pool Card. Name one difficulty of planning that swim program together.

## Day 37: Rilla Card

On Saturday, voters in Rilla choose between two people for a seat on the school committee. On Tuesday, the committee votes yes or no on a new bell schedule.

Ask 1: Rilla Card, invented. Which vote is an election?
Ask 2: Rilla Card. On Saturday, what do the voters decide?
