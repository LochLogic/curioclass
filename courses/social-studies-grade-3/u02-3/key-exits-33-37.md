# Teacher key, exit cards days 33 to 37

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-33-37.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 33: loud music at night kept neighbors from sleeping; the rule protects neighbors' sleep and quiet at night. Not "rules were always there" (S).
- Day 34: each person listens while one talks; it protects the others' freedom to speak; no, the group should not take away Jun's turns; Jun keeps the right and the group reminds Jun (not "Jun loses the right", M).
- Day 35: the letters told the board about a need, so it could answer. Not "leaders already know" (Y); not "only voters letters count" (D).
- Day 36: a benefit such as the town pays together for lifeguards and a pool no one family could; a difficulty tied to the plan, such as people want different days or times. Not "always easy" (E); not "people do not get along" for every setting (Z).
- Day 37: Saturday's vote is the election; the voters decide who fills the school committee seat, a person. Ask 2: Tuesday's committee vote decides a matter and Saturday's voters choose a person; not "both are elections" (P).
