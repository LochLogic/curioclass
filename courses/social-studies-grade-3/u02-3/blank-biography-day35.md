# Biography page, day 35

Read aloud once, about 200 words. A documented person from the town's past who served on a board or volunteered, from the town's historical society or official history page. The teacher reads the whole page first, checks the source, the date and the rights line. The person is no longer living. No living officeholder and no campaign page.

## Fallback, invented for practice

Hollis Greer is a made-up person from Tumbleford, said aloud to be made up. Hollis served on the town clock committee for twelve years. The old clock stopped. Hollis asked neighbors to help. Forty volunteers cleaned and fixed it. The clock rang again.
