# Five settings (day 36)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card. The family is made up. Nobody tells about their own home. Ten cards, two for each mat: FAMILY, SCHOOL, WORKPLACE, VOLUNTEER GROUP, GOVERNMENT.

1. A made-up family shares the chores, so all the chores get done. (benefit)
2. The made-up family disagrees about who does the dishes. (difficulty)
3. A class project gets finished sooner when four children share it. (benefit)
4. The four children have different ideas for the poster. (difficulty)
5. A bakery crew fills many orders by sharing the work. (benefit)
6. The crew members work different hours and miss each other. (difficulty)
7. A volunteer group of twenty can clean the whole beach in one morning. (benefit)
8. Only a few of the twenty can come every week. (difficulty)
9. A town can pay together for a new park that no family could build. (benefit)
10. People in the town want the park in different places. (difficulty)

## Five mats

FAMILY. SCHOOL. WORKPLACE. VOLUNTEER GROUP. GOVERNMENT.
