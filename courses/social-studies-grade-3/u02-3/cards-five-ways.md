# Five ways to take part, and two service cards (day 35)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Five ways

1. Vote.
2. Go to a meeting.
3. Serve on a board.
4. Volunteer.
5. Stay informed.

Public comment is a time at a public meeting when people may speak.

## Two service cards

**Before.** A town board plans to close the recycling center at 3 o'clock every day. Many workers cannot come before 3.

**After.** Residents spoke at a board meeting. The board heard that workers could not come. The board voted to keep the center open until 6 on Thursdays.

Say what changed and why the board needed to hear.
