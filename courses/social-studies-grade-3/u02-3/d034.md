# Day 34: Rights and duties

## Teacher page

### Before class
Set out the group rule cards, the sandbox model, the four-box strip and the records. Write one "someone might say" card: "Mina forgot one toy, so she can never use the sandbox again." Have day 26's library slips ready for the side table. Keep the teacher key, key-exits-33-37.md, out of the room until the exit slips are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 33's exit slips. Teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right. Error S: "Nobody made it; rules were always there" beside "The town council made Rule 12 after loud music kept neighbors awake." If many made error S, order a fresh strip: no running on the Brindle pool deck; children slipped; the pool staff made it.

#### 8 min. Rights and duties
Review (grade 1): a right is something every person here may do or have. Review (day 26): a freedom is something a person is free to do. Review (grade 1): a duty is a job a member is expected to do for the group. New today: every person has duties, not only officials. Model on the sandbox: everyone may use it; the group rule is to put toys away when you leave; each person's duty keeps the freedom for the next child. Fill the strip, with the group as maker. **Ask.** Whose freedom does your duty protect? Expect: the next child's, or others'. Then pairs say to each other: "My duty is ___, so the next child is free to ___." Any pair may pass. Meanwhile up to 5 children who missed day 26's library items meet you at the side table, 1 minute each, to revise the unfair library rule aloud.

#### 10 min. Two group rules
Read the garden club card and the recess ball card aloud. The maker of each rule is the group. Pairs fill: [[s02-P34e]], [[s02-P34f]], [[s02-P34g]], [[s02-P34h]].

#### 4 min. A right is not a prize
Read the forgot-a-duty card: Mina forgot one toy. Show the "someone might say" card. **Ask.** What does the group do? Expect: remind her, help her, ask her to try again. Say: a right is not a prize for obeying.

#### 10 min. The record
Each child picks one rule card (sandbox, garden club or recess ball) and fills the record: [[s02-P34a]], [[s02-P34b]], [[s02-P34c]], [[s02-P34d]]. Seven minutes writing. Meanwhile up to 5 children say theirs at the side table, 1 minute each. Records stay in the folder. By day 45 write one cue on each: the rule, a duty, a freedom kept, the right not made a prize. Children revise on day 46.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Reading Group Card, first seen here. Read the card and the three asks once. Every child writes [[s02-E34]] and [[s02-E34b]] on the slip, then [[s02-E34c]] in the Exit box on the student page.

**Shared shape.** Practice fills frames (the duty, the freedom) on sandbox, garden and ball cards; the exit asks the same ideas as questions on a reading group, in a new case.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the record lines on the board and children copy them.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one card in "Two group rules".

### Words and supports
Words: right, freedom, duty, responsibility, group. Frame: "My duty is ___, so ___ is free to ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 35 shows how a democracy depends on people taking part. If many made error M on day 34's exit (a right taken away for a missed duty), day 35's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one.
