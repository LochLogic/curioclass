# Day 35: People who take part

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the whole biography page first: a person from our town's past, no longer living, who served on a board or volunteered; the page names a deed and what it changed, with its maker, date and rights line. Fill the biography page, day 35, or use the invented Hollis Greer. Have unit 1's biography answer ready. Keep the teacher key, key-exits-33-37.md, out of the room until the exit slips are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 34's exit slips. Teach the most-missed letter, wrong answer then right; error M first. Error M: "He talked over someone, so the group takes his turns away for good" beside "No. He keeps the right to speak; the group reminds him of the duty." If many made error M, revise a rule that takes a right away for one slip: the pool rule "run once and never swim again" becomes "run once and walk back to the start."

#### 3 min. Retrieval
Review (unit 1): a biography is the true story of one person's life, told by someone else; the before, action and after boxes show a change. Read the retrieval slips once. Each child writes [[s02-T35]] and [[s02-T35b]]. A miss: up to 5 children meet you at the side table during the next part, with the before, action and after cards, 1 minute each.

#### 10 min. A person who took part
Read the biography aloud once, about 200 words, after saying who made the page and when. With the fallback, say aloud that Hollis is made up. Pairs fill the boxes: [[s02-P35a]], [[s02-P35b]], [[s02-P35c]]. During this, the retrieval group meets you. Two pairs share. The practice page stays in the folder.

#### 9 min. Five ways to take part
Show the five cards: vote, go to a meeting, serve on a board, volunteer, stay informed. New: public comment is a time at a public meeting when people may speak. Speaking is not voting: people may speak; the board votes. Pairs match the person's action to a card: [[s02-P35d]]. Each child writes [[s02-P35e]]. A child may take part with an adult.

#### 7 min. Why leaders need us
Read the Before card, then the After card. Pairs write [[s02-P35f]]. **Ask.** Why did the leaders need to hear from people? Expect: they did not know the need until people told them.

#### 3 min. Our town
From day 32's Local Government Record, box C: each child says to a partner one way people take part in our town. A child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Service Card, first seen here. Read the card and the ask once. Every child writes [[s02-E35]].

**Shared shape.** Practice is a person's deed and a board that changed its hours after residents spoke at a meeting; the exit is a coat bin, residents' letters and a made-up child's claim.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the five ways on the board and read the cards aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No local biography: use the Hollis Greer card.

**Skip.** The retrieval slip, our town, the partner talk.

- **14 min. A person who took part.** Read the biography page. [[s02-P35a]] [[s02-P35b]] [[s02-P35c]]
- **11 min. Five ways.** Show five ways: vote, meeting, board, volunteer, stay informed. [[s02-P35d]] [[s02-P35e]]
  **Say.** "Is speaking at a meeting voting?" Expect: no, the board votes.
- **9 min. Why leaders need us.** Read both service cards. [[s02-P35f]]
  **Say.** "Why did the leaders need to hear?" Expect: they did not know the need.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and ask once. [[s02-E35]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: cut "Our town".

### Words and supports
Words: take part, volunteer, board, public comment, resident. Frame: "The leaders needed to hear because ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 36 names a benefit and a difficulty of working together. If many made error Y on day 35's exit (leaders taken to know every need), day 36's Return and repair sets that answer beside a right one. If many made error D (only voters count), set that beside "a child can learn, ask and speak with an adult."

### Credit
Ours. The service cards, the Service Card and the Hollis Greer card are invented by Curio Class. The local biography comes from the town's own page, chosen by the teacher.
