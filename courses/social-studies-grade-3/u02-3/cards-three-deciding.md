# Three ways of deciding (day 37)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

**Card 1.** Room 3 picks a name for the class plant by vote. Every child in the room votes.

**Card 2.** The Brindle council hears speakers about a stop sign. Then the elected council members vote.

**Card 3.** Brindle voters choose a clerk. Voters mark one ballot each.
