# Day 144 cards: model, made up, and growth note frame

## River Town model (made up, for practice)

Pell Landing is a made-up town. A note says: "Pell Landing grew after a mill opened by the river in 1860. People came to run the mill wheel and grind grain. A road to the mill made a market grow."

Ask: Where did Pell Landing grow? What work did people do there? When did it grow?

## Growth note frame

The town grew where ______ because ______ and people ______.
