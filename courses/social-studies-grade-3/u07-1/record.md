# Section 7.1 records

Each record is practice, revised on day 158 after the written feedback. The teacher marks shown, shown with help or not yet seen, one scale for all of them.

## Day 141: supporting-question record

Words or a drawing. Four boxes: a question from the board, a fact it needs, one idea in it that people read two ways, and how it connects to the big question.

## Day 142: research note

The nation's page says ______. The town record says ______.

## Days 143 and 144: map record

Date of my sheet: ______. How far the state reached on my sheet: ______. What my sheet leaves out: ______. One way today's outline differs: ______. Then the growth note: place, work, time.

## Day 145: population and movement record

Two years and two counts. More or fewer? Who decided how each person came: the traveler, or someone else by force.
