# Day 141: Who has made our community what it is?

## Teacher page

### Before class
Day 136 closed unit 6; days 137 to 140 were the course check, so nothing is repaired today. Choose an old image of your town and a current photograph of a public place; the Long Wharf engraving is the fallback. Check the crop, caption and date; say only what the caption says. Write the five supporting questions on cards. Keep key-exits-141-145.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook
Show the old image and the photograph side by side. Pairs talk: whose work made this place, and whose story can these pictures not tell? Hear two pairs. Pass allowed. Return day 140 slips sc3-X140, sc3-X140b.

#### 7 min. Retrieval task
Say: Review: in unit 1 you used the legend on the town map, and you filled a Nation Card from the local nation's own page. Each child points to the public place's symbol in the legend, then writes [[s07-P141r1]] and [[s07-P141r2]]. Hear a rotating six at the side table, about 20 seconds each, and name them on the record. A miss goes to the day 142 side table for five minutes of unit 1 and unit 4 work.

#### 8 min. Why a question matters to others
Say: a big question matters to people besides us. Model one: next year's third graders reading a plaque would use the answer, because they want to know who made their school's street. Do not offer a new neighbor as an audience. Pairs say theirs aloud, then each child circles the better reason, [[s07-P141a]]. If the reason names no one else (error I), ask who else would use it. If it says everyone agrees (error D), say some people name this and others may name more.

#### 8 min. Co-write
Say: Review: you learned supporting question in grade 1, and you used the question board in units 1 and 2. On the board, post the five questions with the day each is answered and the evidence it needs: Who lived here first (day 142; a nation's own page, the town's record)? Why did towns grow (days 143 to 145; maps and notes)? What do memories and photographs reveal (days 146 to 148; people's stories and pictures)? Who shaped change (days 149 to 152; cards on people and groups)? How can we contribute (days 153 to 157; what we learned)? Show two contribution cards: a project people remember, and quiet work that goes on. Say we keep both meanings all unit.

#### 9 min. Supporting-question record
Each child chooses one question from the board and fills four boxes in words or a drawing: [[s07-P141b]], [[s07-P141c]], [[s07-P141d]], [[s07-P141e]]. A scribe is offered. Collect them; write one note on each for day 142. Practice, revised on day 158.

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read the card aloud once: [[s07-E141]]. Key: key-exits-141-145.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice is a pick of the better of two printed reasons; the exit asks each child to write their own reason for a new neighbor.

### Other rooms
No pictures printed: describe each aloud and draw one on the board. No board: lay the five cards on a table.

### At home

**Swap.** No old picture: use a photograph of any public place. No board: lay cards on the table.

**Skip.** The rotating six.

- **5 min. Look.** Look at the picture. **Say.** "Whose work made this place?" Expect: any person or group who built or kept it.
- **10 min. Who cares.** [[s07-P141a]]. **Say.** "Who else would want the answer, and why?" Expect: another person and a reason that is theirs.
- **15 min. Questions.** [[s07-P141b]] [[s07-P141c]] [[s07-P141d]] [[s07-P141e]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E141]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: write the question and the fact it needs only. Longer: a second audience for the same question.

### Words and supports
Words: question board, audience, contribution. Frame: "Another person who would use the answer is ___ because ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 142 sets the nation's own page beside the town's record to answer who lived here first. The exit tells you who gave a reason with no one else in it (error I); they get the day 142 repair. It hands back the retrieval slips and the supporting-question records.

### Credit
Ours. The pictures are the teacher's choices, used with their captions.
