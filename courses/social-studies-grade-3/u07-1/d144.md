# Day 144: Why a town grew where it did

## Teacher page

### Before class
Choose a short growth note for one early town of our state, not the town on the day 159 sheet. For the second place, read the city's own history page and each nation's own page the note names; Denver is the plan's example. Say only what the pages say; no fact about it is printed here. Write one note on each day 143 record. Keep key-exits-141-145.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 143's slips and records with their notes: s07-E143, s07-E143b, s07-P143a, s07-P143b, s07-P143c, s07-P143d, s07-P143e. If many took a sheet's edge for the state's edge (error C), show a fresh made-up sheet titled "Lark Valley, the river towns only." Pairs say what it leaves out. If many wrote that the state always had today's outline (error Q), their record names one difference from today. Others read their notes.

#### 5 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: in unit 5 you made the harbor chain, what the water gives, what people do and what moves. Say it back over the unit 5 port record. Say: Review: in unit 1 you learned river, harbor and pass as words for land and water. Each child points to one on the record or the outline.

#### 6 min. Model, made up
Read the River Town model: Pell Landing, a made-up town. Fill a place and work card on the board: the place gave a river to turn the mill wheel; people's work was grinding grain. Ask: where did it grow, what work did people do, and when? Say: a place gives something, and people's work uses it.

#### 9 min. A second place
Read the second-place note aloud. Read each nation's own page first, and say that the nation is a living nation today, where its page says. A town's growth is a settler town's growth, not the first living in a place. Fill the same card: what the place gave, what people did. **Ask.** "What did the place give, and what did people do?" Expect: the place and the work the note names. Compare the harbor chain with this place.

#### 8 min. Own state
Pairs read the growth note for one early town of our state and fill three boxes: [[s07-P144a]], [[s07-P144b]] and [[s07-P144c]]. Each child writes their own. If a box is silent, write "the note does not say."

#### 4 min. Growth note
[[s07-P144d]] Frame: The town grew where ___ because ___ and people ___. Practice, revised on day 158. Collect it.

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read the Cask Lake Note once, then each ask once: [[s07-E144]] and [[s07-E144b]]. The card is made up; say so. Key: key-exits-141-145.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice fills place, work and time from a made-up mill town and two real notes; the exit gives a made-up note with a lake, a railroad stop and a brick works, and a child who says the lake is the only reason.

### Other rooms
Read each note aloud from your copy; draw the card on the board and fill it with the class.

### At home

**Swap.** No note for our town: use the River Town model.

**Skip.** The second place and the repair.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the town note aloud. **Say.** "Where did it grow, and what work did people do?" Expect: the place and the work the note names.
- **20 min. Boxes.** [[s07-P144a]] [[s07-P144b]] [[s07-P144c]] [[s07-P144d]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E144]] [[s07-E144b]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fill place and work only. Longer: add a second thing that helped the town grow.

### Words and supports
Words: growth, place, work. Frame: "The town grew where ___ because ___ and people ___." Read every note aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 145 asks why people moved here and how work changed. The exit tells you who named water as the whole reason (error O); they get the day 145 repair with a fresh pond. It hands back E144, E144b and P144a to P144d.
