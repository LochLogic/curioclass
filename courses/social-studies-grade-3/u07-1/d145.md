# Day 145: Who came here, and how work changed

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the Local Record cards and the Labor History Card first; they come from sources you have read, and say only what they say. Name enslaved people plainly as people who were forced to work and were not paid, never beside goods. Ask no child about their family's moves or legal status. Put a stop card on each desk. Keep key-exits-141-145.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Hand back day 144's slips and records with their notes: s07-E144, s07-E144b, s07-P144a, s07-P144b, s07-P144c, s07-P144d. If many named water as the whole reason (error O), read this made-up card: Ash Pond opened a canning factory in 1900 and hired many workers, and a road reached markets that wanted cans. Children join the pond to the workers and buyers. Others read their notes.

#### 4 min. Retrieval
Say: Review: in grade 2 you learned chosen, they decided, and forced, they could not stay safely or freely. You also learned that "a nation of immigrants" does not describe people who were here first or brought by force; Native nations are not immigrants.

#### 7 min. Population
Say: population is how many people live in a place. Show the cards: Boston, 1980, a census count of 562,994; Boston, 2024, an estimate of 673,822 (another town uses its own dated figures). Put both on the wall strip. Each child writes [[s07-P145a]] and [[s07-P145b]]. Say: an estimate is not an exact count.

#### 9 min. Forced and chosen
Say plainly: some people were brought here by force and made to work and were not paid; they were enslaved, people who were forced. Some workers were free and were paid. Some people chose to come. If the Local Record says so, say that people who were forced also resisted and built lives. Fill the first row with the class: Anika, made up, came for a bakery job and decided. Pairs read two Local Record cards and say who decided how each person came. Each child fixes [[s07-P145c]]. If many thought everyone chose (error B) or all workers were free (error J), reread the cards. Anyone may pass.

#### 9 min. Labor History Card
Read the Labor History Card aloud. Pairs fill two columns, work earlier and work now. Each child writes [[s07-P145f]]. Frame: "People worked at ___. Later they worked at ___ because ___."

#### 3 min. Record
Each child copies one time someone was taken by force and one time someone chose from the Local Record: [[s07-P145d]], [[s07-P145e]]. Practice, revised on day 158.

#### 3 min. Exit
Hand out the half slip. Read the made-up Lena and Joel Cards once, then each ask once: [[s07-E145]] and [[s07-E145b]]. Key: key-exits-141-145.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The practice fixes a false line and copies two lines from the local record; the exit gives two new made-up cards and asks which move was forced and how the child knows.

### Other rooms
No wall strip: lay the two number cards on a table in year order.

### At home

**Swap.** No local cards: use the cards the teacher sent.

**Skip.** The repair, the stop cards and the wall strip.

- **10 min. Counts.** [[s07-P145a]] [[s07-P145b]] **Say.** "Is the later count more or fewer, and is it exact?" Expect: more; the later one is an estimate.
- **15 min. Moves.** Read the cards. **Say.** "Who decided how they came?" Expect: the traveler, or someone else by force. [[s07-P145c]] [[s07-P145d]] [[s07-P145e]]
- **5 min. Work.** [[s07-P145f]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-E145]] [[s07-E145b]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: do the counts and one move only. Longer: write a second sentence on how work changed.

### Words and supports
Words: population, forced, chosen. Frames: "Who decided how they came?" "People worked at ___. Later they worked at ___ because ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 146 opens the interviews, with questions families choose. The exit tells you who thought a forced move was chosen (error B) or that enslaved people were paid (error J); they sort four fresh cards on day 146. It hands back E145, E145b and P145a to P145f.

### Credit
Ours. The population figures follow the City of Boston report; Anika, Lena, Joel, Ash Pond and the two exit cards are made up.
