# Exit cards, days 141 to 145

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. Each exit is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-141-145.md (teacher only).

## Day 141

Ask: Why might a new neighbor care who shaped this town?

## Day 142: Founding Card (made up, for practice)

Town record (made up): "Made a town in 1722."

Old Hill Farm book (made up): "The Hartley family farmed here before the town was made, and we farm here today."

Ask 1: Founding Card. Must one of the two records be wrong?

Ask 2: Founding Card. Which record tells who lived there before 1722?

## Day 143: Map Title Card (made up, for practice)

A sheet drawn in 1790 is titled "Orchard Province, without the northern district."

Ask 1: Map Title Card. Does this sheet show how far the whole province reached?

Ask 2: Map Title Card. Circle the words in the title that tell you.

## Day 144: Cask Lake Note (made up, for practice)

Cask is a made-up town. A note says it grew after a railroad stop opened in 1885. It says a brick works hired many workers. A child says, "It grew only because of the lake."

Ask 1: Cask Lake Note. Does the lake alone explain why the town grew?

Ask 2: Cask Lake Note. Name one other thing that helped the town grow.

## Day 145: Lena and Joel Cards (made up, for practice)

Lena's card: Lena was taken from her home by force. She was made to work and was not paid.

Joel's card: Joel's family took a train to the city because a factory there was hiring.

Ask 1: Lena and Joel Cards. Was Lena taken by force, or did she choose to come?

Ask 2: Lena and Joel Cards. How do you know?
