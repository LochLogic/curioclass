# Day 56: Not enough for everything

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 55's slips for error V and error A and pick the more-missed. Draw the shelf strip, six places in a row, on chart paper. Keep key-exits-56-60.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 55's slips (s03-E55, s03-E55b). Whole class, the more-missed. Error V: "clock repair is a good" beside "a repair is work done for someone: a service". Error A: "the skill is the capital resource" beside "the workbench is capital; the skill is human". Use the three baskets from day 51: the workbench goes in capital. The other goes to the side table during Benefit and cost cards.

#### 8 min. Not enough room
Show the shelf strip. A baker (made up) has one oven shelf with six places for trays. She wants four trays of bread, three of rolls and two of cookies. Lay nine counters beside six places: three have no place. New word, scarcity: not enough time, money or space to do everything we want. Pairs put six counters on their own strip and choose which trays to bake.
**Ask.** Why can the baker not do everything? Expect: the shelf has only six places.

#### 7 min. It is not about effort
Read four sentences from the board. The class answers "a limit" or "not a limit" with a thumb. Limit: "The shelf has six places." "She has one afternoon to bake." Not a limit: "The baker is lazy." "There is flour in the store." A second baker works twice as hard and has the same shelf: still six places. Laziness is not a limit (error C); the flour exists, the shelf is short (error X). No one is the example. Each child writes [[s03-P56d]].

#### 12 min. Benefit and cost cards
Review: you learned the benefit and cost card in grade 1, what I get and what it cost. Today's card has a third box, what I give up. Each pair has eight counters and two choices of five counters each: a set of markers or a bird feeder (made up). They cannot have both. Pairs fill card 1 for the markers, each child writing [[s03-P56a]], [[s03-P56b]] and [[s03-P56c]], then card 2 for the bird feeder. Visit a rotating six pairs. A side table of up to six takes the other of error V or error A for 5 minutes.

#### 5 min. Say it
"I choose ___. I get ___. It costs ___. I give up ___." Partners say it, one minute each way. Hear a rotating six.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Gardener Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E56]] and [[s03-E56b]] on the slip and [[s03-E56c]] in the exit box. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The cards compare two buys with counters; the exit gives one gardener, one afternoon and no counters. The Gardener Card is new.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the strip on the board; children copy the three questions onto paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use nine bottle caps or coins from a game. Nothing real is spent.
**Skip.** The four-sentence sort.

- **10 min. The shelf.** Lay nine paper trays beside six places. Say: "Three have no place. That is scarcity: not enough room for everything."
- **20 min. Two cards.** Your child has eight counters. Markers cost five and a bird feeder costs five. Your child writes [[s03-P56a]], [[s03-P56b]] and [[s03-P56c]] for the markers.
  **Say.** "Why can we not have both?" Expect: only eight counters, not enough.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E56]] [[s03-E56b]] [[s03-E56c]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: one card, the markers. Longer: a third choice, a book at three counters; which two could be had?

### Words and supports
Words: scarcity, choice, benefit, cost, limit, give up. Frame: "I choose ___. I give up ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 57 opens with the more-missed of error D (the $6 named as what she gave up) and error Y (she can do both), 5 minutes; the other at the side table. Then children rank three choices and name only the next-best one given up.
