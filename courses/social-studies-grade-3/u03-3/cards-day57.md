# Day 57 cards

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Teacher model, a class trip fund

Tickets, a book and a game. The class ranks them and picks the tickets. The opportunity cost is the book, the next best, not the game.

## Three choices for the pairs to rank

A jump rope, a ball and a box of chalk. The pair ranks them first, second and third, chooses one and names what they gave up and why not the third. The next-best choice is the opportunity cost.

## Frame

I choose ____. The opportunity cost is ____ because ____ was next best.

## Ledger

The class ledger gets its second line on day 57. The savings are kept, not gone: the class gave up spending them now.
