# Day 58: What pushes a choice

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 57's slips for error Z and error D and pick the more-missed. Make sort cards 1, 2 and 5 to 8 in the style of cards 3 and 4: three rewards, three penalties. Have the question board ready: today adds its answer. Keep key-exits-56-60.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 57's slips (s03-E57, s03-E57b). Whole class, the more-missed. Error Z: "the shelf, the plant and the lamp" beside "only the shelf, the next best". Error D: "the shelf costs the most" beside "next best, not the price". Work a fresh set of three: rank, choose, name the second.

#### 8 min. Pushes on a choice
Read the six cards of cards-day58.md once each (made up). Say the rink's $2 is for keeping skates past closing. New word, incentive: something that pushes a choice. A reward is a good thing that may make people more likely to act. A penalty is a cost that may make them less likely. A push is not a force: some people will still do the other thing (error E).
**Ask.** Which cards are rewards, and which are penalties? Expect: the cookie, the notebooks and the sticker are rewards; the rest are penalties.

#### 12 min. Sort and predict
Each pair (about 15 in a class) sorts its eight cards into reward and penalty and says one "might" for each: "This might make people ___." Each child writes [[s03-P58a]] and [[s03-P58b]] for cards 3 and 4. Visit a rotating six pairs, about 1.5 minutes each. A side table of up to six takes the other of error Z or error D for 5 minutes.

#### 8 min. A sale price and a late fee
Review: you learned fee in unit 2, money paid by the person who uses one service; and hoped-for profit on day 54. Side by side: a sale price is a reward to buyers, and the seller hopes for more sales and so for profit. A late fee is a penalty for being late. It is not the fee for using a service (error Q if mixed). Each child writes [[s03-P58d]].
**Ask.** Who might not change what they do? Expect: some buyers will skip the sale; some people will pay the fee.

#### 4 min. Say it
"This reward or penalty might make people ___." Partners say it about one card, then each child writes [[s03-P58c]]. Hear a rotating six.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Canoe Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E58]] and [[s03-E58b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The sort cards hold one push each; the exit gives one canoe stand with two pushes. The Canoe Card is new.

### Other rooms
No printer: read the six cards aloud; write cards 3 and 4 on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read the two made-up lines below. No class: you are the partner.
**Skip.** The sale price and late fee comparison.

- **10 min. Model.** Read: "A cafe gives a free cookie after ten coffees. A park fines $20 for leaving trash." Say: "The cookie is a reward. The fine is a penalty."
- **20 min. Sort.** Read cards 3 and 4 once. Your child writes [[s03-P58a]], [[s03-P58b]] and [[s03-P58c]].
  **Say.** "What might it make people do, and might some not?" Expect: a likely choice; some will still do the other.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E58]] [[s03-E58b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: four cards, not eight. Longer: make up a reward and a penalty for a clean room, and say who might not change.

### Words and supports
Words: incentive, reward, penalty, might, late fee. Frame: "This reward or penalty might make people ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 59 opens with the more-missed of error Q (a reward and a penalty mixed up) and error E (a push makes everyone act), 5 minutes. Then children meet a bank again, a credit union and insurance, and match needs to them.
