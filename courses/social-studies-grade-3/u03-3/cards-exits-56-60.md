# Exit cards, days 56 to 60

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Each answer is written, drawn or spoken.

## Day 56: Gardener Card

A gardener can weed her beds or re-pot her herbs. She cannot do both today. Weeding makes the beds look neat. Re-potting pays $6.

Ask 1: Gardener Card, invented. Name one benefit of weeding.
Ask 2: Gardener Card. What does she give up if she weeds?

## Day 57: Reading Corner Card

A class ranks four ideas for its reading corner: cushions first, the shelf second, the plant third, the lamp fourth. It picks the cushions.

Ask 1: Reading Corner Card, invented. Cushions are chosen. What is the opportunity cost?
Ask 2: Reading Corner Card. How do you know?

## Day 58: Canoe Card

A canoe stand gives a free paddle to anyone who rents for a whole day. It charges $3 for a late canoe.

Ask 1: Canoe Card, invented. Which one is a penalty?
Ask 2: Canoe Card. What might the penalty make a renter do?

## Day 59: Two cards

Sedge Credit Union Card, invented. Sedge Credit Union lets the neighbors who joined it save and borrow. Tern Insurance Card, invented. Tern collects a fee from many people.

Ask 1: Sedge Credit Union Card, invented. Who owns Sedge Credit Union?
Ask 2: Tern Insurance Card. What does Tern do with the money people pay?

## Day 60: Oak and Fir cards

Bank Oak pays $1 a year on $100. Bank Fir pays $3. Which pays more?
Same $100, same year. How much more does the better bank pay?

Exit box, extra: What is interest?
