# Day 59 cards (teacher reads aloud)

Invented for practice where marked.

## Eastside Bank (made up)

A bank keeps savings safe and lends. It normally pays interest on savings.

## Riverbend Credit Union (made up)

A credit union is owned by its members. Members elect its board. One member's savings becomes another member's loan.

## Shore Insurance (made up)

Many people pay a little into a shared fund. The fund pays for the losses an agreement lists. It does not cover every loss.

## Coverage card (made up)

The agreement lists a storm-damaged window only. An ordinary repair is not listed. Insurance is not a savings jar.

## Need cards for the pairs

Six need cards (made up), two for each place: Pat wants to keep wages safe until they are needed. A workers' club owns a shared fund and its members borrow from it. A shop owner wants help if a flood spoils stock. Match each need to a bank, a credit union or insurance, and say what it does. Make the other three cards in the same style.
