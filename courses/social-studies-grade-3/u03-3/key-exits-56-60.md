# Teacher key, exit cards days 56 to 60

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-56-60.md); never hand to children.

- Day 56, ask 1: The beds look neat. Ask 2: Re-potting the herbs, the other job, not the $6. Not "$6" (D).
- Day 57, ask 1: The shelf: it was ranked second, next best after the cushions. Not "the whole list" (Z). Ask 2: It was ranked second, the next best after the cushions. Not "it costs the most" (D).
- Day 58, ask 1: The $3 charge for a late canoe. Not the free paddle (Q). Ask 2: Bring the canoe back on time. Some may still pay it. Not "everyone will" (E).
- Day 59, ask 1: Its members, the people who joined. Not "the bank that owns it" (W). Ask 2: It pays for the losses its agreement lists. Not "it saves it for them" (AA).
- Day 60, ask 1: Bank Fir. Ask 2: $2 more. Extra, in the Exit box: Payment for using money. Not "the whole balance" (H).
