# Day 59: Banks, credit unions and insurance

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 58's slips for error Q and error E and pick the more-missed. Make the three institution cards: each place with one line on what it does, from the lines below. Keep key-exits-56-60.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 58's slips (s03-E58, s03-E58b). Whole class, the more-missed. Error Q: "the sticker is a penalty" beside "a fine is a penalty; a sticker is a reward". Error E: "a reward makes everyone do it" beside "a reward might make some people do it". Use fresh cards: a free ride after ten bus trips, $2 for a late class book. The other goes to the side table during Match the needs.

#### 6 min. The bank, again
Review: you learned bank and loan in grade 1: a bank keeps savers' money safe, and a loan is money lent that the borrower repays. Eastside Bank (made up) keeps savings safe and lends money that is repaid. Say "normally" for interest: savings accounts normally pay it. A bank does not only give gifts (error G). Each child writes [[s03-P59a]].

#### 8 min. A credit union
Riverbend Credit Union (made up) is owned by its members, the people who joined. They save and borrow there, and one member's savings becomes another member's loan. Members elect its board. Source: mycreditunion.gov, checked; it does not describe banks.
**Ask.** How is it different from a bank? Expect: its members own it. Not the bank (error W). Each child writes [[s03-P59b]].

#### 8 min. Insurance
Shore Insurance (made up): many people pay a little into a shared fund, and the fund pays for the losses an agreement lists. Show the coverage card: the agreement lists a storm-damaged window only, so an ordinary repair is not paid (error N). Insurance is not a savings jar (error AA). Each child writes [[s03-P59d]].

#### 10 min. Match the needs
Pairs match the six needs on the student page to a bank, a credit union or insurance, and say what that place does. Two needs go to each place. Each child writes [[s03-P59c]]. Visit a rotating six pairs, about 1.5 minutes each. A side table of up to six takes the other of error Q or error E for 5 minutes, and error G if many.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Sedge Credit Union Card and the Tern Insurance Card, first seen here. Read each card and ask once. Children write [[s03-E59]] and [[s03-E59b]] on the slip and [[s03-E59c]] in the exit box. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** Pairs match a need to a place; the exit gives new places. The two cards and Harbor Bank are new.

### Other rooms
No printer: write the three place lines on the board; read the six needs aloud and children write the place beside each number.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read the three made-up lines below. No class: you are the partner.
**Skip.** The coverage card.

- **10 min. Model.** Read: "A bank keeps savings safe and lends. A credit union is owned by its members. Insurance pays for the losses its agreement lists."
- **20 min. Write.** Your child writes [[s03-P59a]], [[s03-P59b]] and [[s03-P59d]], then matches the six needs. [[s03-P59c]]
  **Say.** "Which place fits, and what does it do?" Expect: a bank, a credit union or insurance, and its job.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read each card and ask once. [[s03-E59]] [[s03-E59b]] [[s03-E59c]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: match four needs, not six. Longer: make up a need for each place.

### Words and supports
Words: bank, credit union, member, insurance, loan, covered. Frame: "A ___ fits because it ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 60 opens with the more-missed of error W (the credit union belongs to the bank) and error AA (insurance keeps savings), 5 minutes. Then children learn what interest is and compare two made-up banks by dollars a year on the same $100.

### Credit
Ours. Facts about credit unions follow the National Credit Union Administration's public page; insurance follows a public encyclopedia page. Every place and card is made up for practice.
