# Day 56 cards

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Oven shelf (teacher model)

A baker has one oven shelf with room for six trays. She wants four trays of bread, three of rolls and two of cookies. Nine trays cannot fit six places. That is scarcity: not enough time, money or space to do everything we want.

## Two benefit and cost cards for the pairs

Each pair has eight counters. Card 1: a set of markers, five counters. Card 2: a bird feeder, five counters. They cannot buy both.

## Questions on the benefit and cost card

What do I get? What does it cost in money? What do I give up?

## Sentence card

The baker is lazy. A limit comes from time or money, not from laziness. The pair says whether the card names a limit.
