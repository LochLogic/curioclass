# Day 58 cards

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Six cards the teacher reads (invented)

A cafe gives a free cookie after ten coffees. A rink charges $2 for keeping skates past closing. A shop sells notebooks at a lower price in August. A park fines $20 for leaving trash. A sticker goes to a tidy desk. A library charges $1 for a lost book. Reward: a good thing that may make people choose something. Penalty: a cost that may make people avoid something.

## Eight cards for the pairs to sort

Card 3: Plums cost less on Saturday. Card 4: The rink charges $5 for a lost skate. Cards 1, 2 and 5 to 8 are fresh cards the teacher makes in the same style. Each pair sorts into reward and penalty and writes one "might" for each.

## Frame

This reward or penalty might make people ____.

An incentive may change a choice. It does not make everyone act the same way. A fee for a service is a price, not a penalty.
