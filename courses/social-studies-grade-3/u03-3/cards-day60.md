# Day 60 cards

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Why interest?

A baker (made up) borrows $100 for a year and pays back the $100 and $6 for using it. That payment is interest: payment for using money. A bank normally pays interest to people who keep savings there. It is not the whole balance and it is not a gift: someone pays it.

## Teacher model, two made-up banks

Bank Birch pays $2 a year on $100. Bank Cedar pays $6 on $100. Same $100, same year. Cedar pays $4 more.

## Frame

At Bank ____ the same $100 earns ____ more in a year.

## Net-maker card

A net-maker borrows $100 and pays back $105. The extra $5 is the interest. The net-maker pays it; the lender gets it.
