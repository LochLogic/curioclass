# Day 57: What we give up

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read day 56's slips for error D and error Y and pick the more-missed. Draw the mat on the board: three cards in a row, a star over the one chosen, an arrow to the one given up. Find the goal bar from day 53. Keep key-exits-56-60.md away until slips are in. The student page stays in the child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair
Day 56's slips (s03-E56, s03-E56b, s03-E56c) and the benefit and cost cards. Whole class, the more-missed. Error D: "the $6 is what she gave up" beside "she gave up the other job; a price is not a job". Error Y: "she can do both" beside "one afternoon holds one job". The other goes to the side table during Rank and name.

#### 9 min. Rank, choose, name the next-best
Review: you learned the choose-and-give-up mat in grade 1, three ranked cards with one chosen and one given up, and opportunity cost in grade 2, the best other thing we gave up. Review: rank, first, second and third: put choices first, second and third. Model a class trip fund (made up): tickets, a book and a game. The class ranks them, tickets first, and picks the tickets. The opportunity cost is the book, the next best. It is not the game, and it is not the price (error Z, error D).
**Ask.** What did we give up, and which was next best? Expect: the book, ranked second.

#### 12 min. Rank and name
Each pair ranks the jump rope, the ball and the chalk, chooses one and says what they gave up and why not the third. Each child writes [[s03-P57a]]. Visit a rotating six pairs, about 1.5 minutes each. A side table of up to six takes the other of error D or error Y for 5 minutes. Fast pairs rank a set you make.

#### 7 min. Savings check-in
Review: you learned the budget, money in, money out and money kept, on day 53. The second $20 goes in the made-up ledger: In $20, Out $0, Kept $40, and the goal bar gets a second box. Nothing is collected. Saving gives up spending it now; the $40 is not lost (error AD). Each child writes [[s03-P57c]] and [[s03-P57d]]. New routine: beside the ledger each child marks confident, needs practice or revised. The marks are not scored.

#### 4 min. Say it
"I choose ___. The opportunity cost is ___ because ___ was next best." Partners say it about their own ranking, one minute each way. Hear a rotating six. Then each child writes [[s03-P57b]].

#### 3 min. Exit ticket
Hand out the half slip with the Reading Corner Card, first seen here. Read the card and each ask once. Children write [[s03-E57]] and [[s03-E57b]]. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

**Shared shape.** The class ranks three made-up buys and pairs rank their own; the exit prints a ranking of four for a reading corner and asks the child to read it. The Reading Corner Card is new.

### Other rooms
No printer: draw the three picture cards; write the ledger line on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No class: you are the partner. No ledger: draw the four boxes.
**Skip.** The ledger marks.

- **10 min. Model.** Rank a book, a game and a snack, made up. Pick one. Say: "The opportunity cost is the next best, not all the rest."
- **20 min. Rank.** Your child ranks a jump rope, a ball and chalk, picks one and writes [[s03-P57a]]. Then the ledger: In $20, Out $0, Kept $40. [[s03-P57c]] [[s03-P57d]]
  **Say.** "What did you give up, and was it next best?" Expect: one thing, ranked second.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read the card and each ask once. [[s03-E57]] [[s03-E57b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: skip the savings check-in. Longer: rank four choices and name the next best.

### Words and supports
Words: rank, opportunity cost, next best, given up, kept. Frame: "I choose ___. The opportunity cost is ___ because ___ was next best." Pointing, drawing and a scribe are all answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 58 opens with the more-missed of error Z (everything not chosen) and error D (the price), 5 minutes. Then children sort rewards and penalties and say what each might make people do.
