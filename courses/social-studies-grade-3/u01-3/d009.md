# Day 9: What a landmark tells about the past

## Teacher page

### Before class

Find the legend's historic landmarks on the town map; if it marks none, use the town page's list. Public places only, no homes. Have the day 1 landmark photograph with its dated caption and credit. Put up the shows, suggests, cannot tell card (shows, might suggest, cannot tell) on the wall. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 7 to 10 (key-exits-7-10.md), out of the room until the slips are collected.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many made error D on day 8's exit, lay the strip along a fresh route and count again. Otherwise do one fresh bar count and one direction from the north arrow. Hand back day 8's exit tickets and written task (the exit box) with the written cue.

#### 9 min. Historic landmarks on the map

A historic landmark is a place the town remembers because of its past. Find them by their legend symbols. Review: you found places by their legend symbol on day 7 and day 8. Pairs find three on the town map and say which way each is from the school, using the north arrow.

**Ask.** Which part of the map tells us a symbol is a historic landmark? Expect: the legend.

#### 14 min. One landmark's past

Show the day 1 landmark's photograph and read its dated caption aloud, with its credit. Review: in grade 2 you checked a caption against its picture and said "The source says ___; it cannot tell ___." New here is a middle kind of claim. On the shows, suggests, cannot tell card: "the caption shows" is what its words or the picture say; "it might suggest" is a guess that fits what you see; "we cannot tell" is what no one wrote. Model three statements about this landmark: its date (shows), what the picture hints at (might suggest), who built it or why (cannot tell). Read a card: "Someone might say, 'The photograph proves the town was founded here for the bakery.'" Set beside it: "The caption shows a bakery stood on Cedar Street; it cannot tell why the town began."

**Ask.** Does the photograph tell us why people built it? Expect: no, we cannot tell unless the caption says.

Pairs sort four statement cards (invented for practice) under the three headings on the wall card, giving a reason from the caption or photograph.

#### 7 min. A sentence

On the day 1 landmark, each pair writes: "The photograph might suggest ___. The caption cannot tell ___." The teacher reads three aloud, with no one named as a mistake.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Read the card once, then the ask once. Every child writes on the half slip ([[s01-E9]], [[s01-E9b]]). The card is first seen here.

**Shared shape.** The statement cards sort claims under three headings from a made-up bakery caption. The exit reads a new caption, then finds its landmark on a new map.

### Other rooms

With one projector, show the photograph and caption; pairs use printed cards. With no printer, copy the four statements onto strips of paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No landmark photograph: use any old photograph with a dated caption.

**Skip.** The sort and the pair sentence.

- **10 min. A landmark.** Find a historic landmark on the town map.
  **Say.** "What is a historic landmark?" Expect: a place remembered because of its past.
- **15 min. The caption.** Read the caption aloud.
  **Say.** "What does it show? What can it not tell?" Expect: its words; what nobody wrote.
- **5 min. One sentence.** Write: "The photograph might suggest ___. The caption cannot tell ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-E9]] [[s01-E9b]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort two cards, not four. If day 10 folds into this day, the sentence becomes a 7-minute sketch of the school grounds. Longer: a second landmark.

### Words and supports

Words: historic landmark, caption, shows, might suggest, cannot tell. Frame: "The photograph might suggest ___. The caption cannot tell ___." Accept a pointed card as an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 draws the school grounds. If many wrote a builder, an age or a reason the caption does not give (error G), it opens by sorting three claim cards into shows, might suggest and cannot tell.
