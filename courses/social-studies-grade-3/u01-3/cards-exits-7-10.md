# Exit cards, days 7 to 10

Print "invented for practice" at the bottom of each card. Plain black line on white. Every child answers in writing; the day's six are also heard.

## Day 7: Clover Court Map

Title Clover Court. Legend: a ring with a dot is the fountain; a short bar is the bench; a gap in a line is the gate; a blue square is the flower bed. A north arrow points to the right edge. Bench on the upper side of the page and the fountain in the middle, two bars apart. Ask: use the legend to circle the fountain; which way is the bench from the fountain; about how many bars apart.

## Day 8: Reed Pond Card

A map with a pond, a creek, a hill and a gate, with the pond and gate four bars apart. Ask: about how many bars apart are pond and gate; name one physical feature on the card; which part told you what that symbol stands for.

## Day 9: Landmark Exit Caption

A photograph card of a depot with the caption "Mill Road Depot, 1912: freight trains stopped here." Beside it, a small new town map with a legend shows the depot and four other places by symbol, none named. Ask: what past use does the caption say? Then: use the legend to find the depot, and circle it.

## Day 10: Stone Yard Plan

Words only: "Stone Yard has a gate on the west side and a well on the east side, with a path between. One grid step is 5 metres." Children draw the map and add a legend, a north arrow and a scale bar for one grid step.
