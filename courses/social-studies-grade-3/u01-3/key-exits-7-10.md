# Teacher key, exit cards days 7 to 10

Teacher only. Keep apart from the exit cards (cards-exits-7-10.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 7: the ring with a dot; west (the arrow points to the right edge, so the upper side is west); about 2 bars.
- Day 8: about 4 bars; pond, creek or hill; the legend.
- Day 9: freight trains stopped there; the depot symbol on Mill Road circled, found from the legend.
- Day 10: gate west, well east, a path between; a legend naming both symbols; an arrow labeled N; a bar labeled 5 metres.

## Practice card keys (teacher only)

- Day 9, landmark statement cards: 1 and 2 show; 3 might suggest, only if the photograph shows people walking; 4 cannot tell. Children give a reason from the caption or photograph.
