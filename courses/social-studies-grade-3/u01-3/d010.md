# Day 10: A map of our school grounds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Plan a short look at the grounds from the door, public parts only, within school supervision policy; if it rains, use the aerial photograph and read its credit. Choose a wall to pace. Draw the Fern Hollow draft on the board. Keep the shows, suggests, cannot tell card up. Keep the teacher key, exit cards days 7 to 10 (key-exits-7-10.md), out of the room until the slips are collected.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Two minutes: each child sketches a reading corner's door and shelf (review: in grade 2 you drew a classroom map). Hand back day 9's exit tickets with the written cue.

Eight minutes, if many made error G on day 9: three claim cards on a made-up caption, "Harbor Street Fish Market, 1932: boats unloaded here," with a photograph of stacked crates. Sort them under its three headings: "Boats unloaded here in 1932" (shows); "Fish were sold here" (might suggest); "The market began because the town grew" (cannot tell). If error G was rare, place object symbols on a fresh sketch (5 minutes) and list four features of the grounds (3); children who missed it join a small group in part 3.

#### 8 min. The school grounds

Look at the grounds from the door; the teacher names north. Review: in grade 2 you paced a wall. The teacher paces a long wall; the class counts and picks one bar of whole paces. Each child lists four features and which way each is from the door.

**Ask.** Which way is the first feature from the door? Expect: a direction by the north arrow, not by the top of the page.

#### 11 min. Draw the map

On the school-grounds frame each child draws the four features in place, then fills the four boxes: title, legend, north arrow and a bar labeled with its pace count. Practice, not scored. The teacher cues six children; each school-grounds map is kept for a written cue before day 22, where it is revised and scored. The small group takes its repair.

#### 6 min. Fern Hollow Trail

Read the card: a trailhead sign in the south, a bridge in the middle, a picnic table near the north end; one grid step is 10 meters. The board holds a child's draft: the table in the south, a legend of two colors, no north arrow, a bar saying "3 steps." Pairs say what is wrong in each part; one pair redraws it.

**Ask.** What does the bar need to say? Expect: a distance on the ground, in meters.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

While slips are collected, add one sentence from days 7 to 9 under "What does our town map show?" on the question board. Read the description once, then each ask once. Every child draws on the half slip ([[s01-E10]], [[s01-E10b]], [[s01-E10c]], [[s01-E10d]]). The description is first seen here.

**Shared shape.** The school-grounds map is drawn from looking, and Fern Hollow is a draft to fix. The exit asks each child to draw a new place from words alone.

### Other rooms

No walk: map the room.

### At home

**Swap.** No school grounds: use a public park or the library's front yard. Never a home.

**Skip.** The class repair and the Fern Hollow draft.

- **10 min. Look.** Stand at a door and look out.
  **Say.** "Which way is each thing from the door?" Expect: north, south, east or west by the arrow.
- **20 min. Draw.** Draw four places with a title, a legend, a north arrow and a bar.
  **Say.** "What does the legend tell us?" Expect: what each symbol stands for.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-E10]] [[s01-E10b]] [[s01-E10c]] [[s01-E10d]] Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: draw three features, not four. If this day folds into day 9, the drawing becomes a short sketch there. Longer: a second bar.

### Words and supports

Words: title, legend, north arrow, bar, pace. A drawing with spoken labels is an answer.

### Tomorrow

If many put places on the wrong sides on the exit (error L), day 11's first part rebuilds labeled columns and rows and places a new cell. The maps get written feedback before day 22.

### Credit

Ours. Every map and card here is invented by Curio Class for practice.
