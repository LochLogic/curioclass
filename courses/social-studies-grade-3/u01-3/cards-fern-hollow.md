# Fern Hollow Trail card (day 10)

Invented for practice. A made-up place described in words: "Fern Hollow Trail has a trailhead sign in the south. A bridge crosses a stream in the middle. A picnic table is near the north end. One step on the grid is 10 metres." Children draw it on the school-grounds frame model in pairs, checking each of the four parts.
