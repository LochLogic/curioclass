# Maple Park Map (day 7 teaching)

Invented for practice. Print "invented for practice" at the bottom. A small map with title "Maple Park", a legend (a circle with a cross is the swing set, a triangle is the shelter, a green square is the tree grove), a north arrow pointing up, and a scale bar. Place the swings in the northwest, the shelter in the east, the grove in the south, with a straight path from the swings to the shelter.

Model: Which part tells what the triangle stands for? Which way is the shelter from the swings? The legend answers the first; the north arrow answers the second.
