# Landmark statement cards (day 9)

Invented for practice. A made-up caption: "Cedar Street Bakery, 1950: the oven was on the left." Four statement cards to sort under shows, might suggest, cannot tell.

1. The caption says the bakery stood on Cedar Street. (shows)
2. The caption says 1950. (shows)
3. People may have come to the bakery on foot. (might suggest, only if the photograph shows people walking)
4. What the bakery sold. (cannot tell)

Teacher key: 1 and 2 show; 3 might suggest; 4 cannot tell. Children give a reason from the caption or photograph.
