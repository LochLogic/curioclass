# Route cards (day 8)

Six cards of routes between public places on the town map. The teacher writes the places from the real map: for example, from the library to the town hall, from the school to the park. Each card asks: which way do you go? About how many bars is it? The teacher fills the answers on the overlay only. No made-up local history; places are named only as the town map shows them.
