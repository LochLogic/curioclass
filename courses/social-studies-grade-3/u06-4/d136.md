# Day 136: Fix one idea

## Teacher page

### Before class
Score day 135 from key-day135.md and write on each sheet one note: a circled missed idea and one sentence. Sort children into at most four groups by the largest gaps, one letter each. Put each group's written move and fresh card on its table. The fresh cards are made up; say so. Keep key-exits-133-136.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 135's assessment, the sheets with their notes: s06-Q1, s06-Q1b, s06-Q1c, s06-Q2, s06-Q2b, s06-Q2c, s06-Q2d, s06-Q2e, s06-Q3, s06-Q3b, s06-Q4, s06-Q4b, s06-Q5, s06-Q5b, s06-Q5c, s06-Q5d, s06-Q5e and the records s06-R1, s06-R2, s06-R2b, s06-R3, s06-R4, s06-R4b, and day 134's slips and work (s06-E134, s06-P134a, s06-P134b). Each child reads the note silently or with a partner. Say: a fix needs a new answer and a reason, not a copy.

#### 20 min. Groups
Each group meets you for five minutes in turn; the others work in pairs from their written move on the fresh card. Hear each child in your group once, about 45 seconds each, and name them on the record. Groups by letter: C, T, E, D, J, S, K, P, G, I and Q, N, H and M, R. Each group's move is its letter's written move; never use packet text. For R, use the made-up Cedar State clauses: where power comes from and who may elect are two questions.

#### 10 min. Fresh task
Read the Gloss Cards aloud once, then each ask once: [[s06-E136]] and [[s06-E136b]]. Then each child fixes the idea marked on their sheet on a fresh caption, clause or dated card from their group's table and writes why the fix is right, [[s06-P136a]]. A scribe or a spoken answer is offered. Key: key-exits-133-136.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Reflection
Each child says to a partner what they fixed and why. Nobody is made the example of a mistake. Close the folders; the unit is done.

**Shared shape.** The group cards each use one fresh case; the exit gives two new statements from a state plan and asks whether they prove everyone voted.

### Other rooms
No groups: read each note aloud to one child; they fix the idea on the fresh card for their letter.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; the parent reads the note aloud.

**Skip.** The group cards.

- **5 min. The note.** Read the note on the day 135 sheet.
- **15 min. Try again.** Pick a made-up card that fits the note and talk it through. **Say.** "What did you miss, and what would you add?" Expect: the idea named in the note, with a detail from the card.
- **10 min. The fix.** [[s06-P136a]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-E136]] [[s06-E136b]] Tell the adult what you fixed.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fix one idea only. Longer: fix a second marked idea on another card.

### Words and supports
Words: note, fix, gloss, evidence. Frame: "I fixed ___ because the card says ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 137 is course check 3. Its civics task asks what consent of the governed means for our town's or state's government. Today's exit tells you who still mixes up where power comes from and who may vote.

### Credit
Ours. The gloss cards follow the Massachusetts constitution (U6R) in original wording; the fresh cards are made up.
