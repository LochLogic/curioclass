# Written moves, day 136 groups

Teacher only. Each group meets the teacher for five minutes by its letter; the others work in pairs from the written move. Every move uses fresh cards, never packet text. Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card.

- C: a fresh card about a complaint with a taxation feel; ask what the people wanted instead, and link it to a voice, not to no rules.
- T: a fresh invented ruler who asks and one who only commands; ask whose agreement is missing.
- E: three fresh details to sort to the Declaration, the Constitution and the Bill of Rights.
- D: two fresh cases sorted into citizenship or voting.
- J: two new cases, one before 1807 and one after, in a state where property decided the vote.
- S: four fresh phrases sorted into what a person does, what a government follows and a feeling.
- K: fresh provisions sorted to makes laws, carries out laws and decides cases.
- P: order three notes about drafting and approval.
- G: link two fresh details to a claim with the frame withdrawn.
- I and Q: a fresh invented case; ask the difference between agreement and liking a rule, or between agreement and kindness.
- N: a fresh law card and an affected person; say who cares and why.
- H and M: a fresh dated line drawn to scale with two overlapping bands.
- R: two fresh invented glosses (made up, for practice) beside a fresh state clause: where power comes from and who may vote are two different questions.
