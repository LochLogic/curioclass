# Day 140: new cards for the four groups

Every card is made up, for practice. Group A is maps, group B is leaders and consent, group C is cost and choice, group D is claim and two sources; each child joins the group of the idea still marked on their day 139 slip. The practice asks are spoken and use the first card of each group. The exit slip uses the second card (the Confirm Card) and is written; print it on a half slip with the two exit asks, [[sc3-X140]] and [[sc3-X140b]].

## Group A: maps

Practice card, Another Map Card. A farm map. A scale bar shows one bar. The gate is 3 bars from the barn. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P140a]]

Confirm Card A. Dune Cove Map. The arrow points up. Legend: a triangle is the library; a square is the school; a wavy line is the creek. The library is north of the school. The creek is 2 bars from the school.

## Group B: leaders and consent

Practice card, Another Rule Card. A town votes on a new park. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P140b]]

Confirm Card B. Dune Cove asks neighbors to vote on a new bus stop. The vote is yes. The town manager carries the plan out.

## Group C: cost and choice

Practice card, Another Choice Card. A club buys a tent for its camp. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P140c]]

Confirm Card C. A town has $4,000 for its park. It can buy a bench or plant a tree. It plants the tree.

## Group D: claim and two sources

Practice card, Another Source Card. A letter written in 1960 by a harbor worker, that year. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P140d]]

Confirm Card D. The question is: when did Dune Cove light its first street lamp? Source 1: a lamplighter's note, 1911, written that night: "Tonight the first lamp was lit." Source 2: a school book, 2018, for children: "The first lamp was lit in 1911."

## The exit slip, half page

Say: use the Confirm Card for your group. Each child writes [[sc3-X140]] and [[sc3-X140b]].
