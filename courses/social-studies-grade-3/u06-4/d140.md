# Day 140: Redo and confirm

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read the day 139 slips and mark in pencil which idea each child still needs: sort into at most four groups again, A maps, B leaders and consent, C cost and choice, D claim and two sources. Put each group's practice card on its table. All cards are made up; say so. Keep key-exits-140.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 139's exit slips, sc3-X139 and sc3-X139b, with a line on each: one thing now shown and one still to do. Each child reads it silently. Say: today you redo the idea once more on a new card.

#### 20 min. Groups
Each group meets you for five minutes in turn; the others work in pairs from the practice card on their table. Hear each child in your group once, about 45 seconds each: Group A, [[sc3-P140a]]; Group B, [[sc3-P140b]]; Group C, [[sc3-P140c]]; Group D, [[sc3-P140d]]. Name each child heard on the record.

#### 10 min. Confirm
Hand each child the Confirm Card for their group on the half slip. Each child answers the idea still marked on their slip: [[sc3-X140]] and [[sc3-X140b]]. A scribe or a spoken answer is offered. Read the ask aloud from key-exits-140.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Reflection
Each child says to a partner what they now know that they did not on day 137. Nobody is made the example of a mistake. Close the folders.

**Shared shape.** The group practice asks are spoken, one new case each. The confirm uses a different Confirm Card and asks for a written answer with a detail.

### Other rooms
No groups: read each line aloud to one child; they redo the idea on the Confirm Card for their line.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; the parent reads the line aloud.

**Skip.** The group asks.

- **5 min. The line.** Read the line on the day 139 slip.
- **15 min. Try again.** Pick a made-up card that fits the line and talk it through. **Say.** "What do you know now that you did not before?" Expect: the idea named in the line, with a detail from the card.
- **10 min. The redo.** Write the answer and a detail from the card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc3-X140]] [[sc3-X140b]] Tell the adult what you confirmed.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: redo one idea only; drop the group asks. Longer: redo a second idea on another card.

### Words and supports
Words: note, redo, confirm, card. Frame: "I know ___ because the card says ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.
