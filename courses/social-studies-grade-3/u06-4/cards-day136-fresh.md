# Fresh cards for day 136, by letter

Print "made up, for practice" on every card. Every card here is invented and uses no packet text. The teacher reads each card aloud once.

- C. In 1768 a made-up town taxes every cart that crosses its bridge. The carters say nobody asked them. Ask: what did the carters want instead?
- T. Mara, a made-up ruler, asks market sellers before she sets a fee. Dov, a made-up ruler, sets the fee and tells them. Ask: whose agreement is missing?
- E. Three details: it lists complaints against a king; it names the court that decides quarrels; it says people may print newspapers. Sort each to the Declaration, the Constitution or the Bill of Rights.
- D. A 1810 town book counts Ada, born in town, as one of its people. A 1810 rule says only dock-tax payers vote on the dock. Sort each to citizenship or voting.
- J. Cedar State is made up. Until 1806 any adult with a small farm could vote there. In 1806 it let only men vote. Edith, 1800, has a small farm. Her niece, 1810, has one. Who could vote?
- S. Sort four phrases to what a person does, what a government follows and a feeling: he went back every week until the council listened; no one may be treated worse for who they are; she felt angry; the government follows the rules it wrote down.
- K. A council writes a rule for ferry tickets; a mayor sees ferry rules kept; a court decides if an owner broke a ferry rule. Sort each to makes laws, carries out laws or decides cases.
- P. Order three notes: the towns of Alder County each voted on the plan; delegates from the towns met and wrote a plan; the plan was put to work.
- G. Claim: the class shares its chores. Details: each child picks a chore from a jar every week; the room has a window. Link the detail that fits, with the frame taken away.
- I and Q. Tomas dislikes a new library rule, but the town voted for it after hearing everyone. A made-up king is kind and gives gifts but never asks. Ask: how is agreement different from liking a rule, and from kindness?
- N. A 1800 rule raises the ferry fee. Mina rides the ferry; Jo is its captain. Ask: who cares about the rule, and why?
- H and M. A well was used from 1795 to 1830. A pump was added in 1815. Draw both bands to scale and say when both were used.
- R. In made-up Cedar State's plan, one clause says all power belongs to the people. Another says adults who own land may elect the council. Ask: where does power come from, and who may elect? Are those the same question?
