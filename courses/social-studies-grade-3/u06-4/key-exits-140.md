# Teacher key, day 140 (practice and confirm)

Teacher only. Mark each shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

- P140a: 3 bars. Not "3 steps".
- P140b: the people have a say. Not "the mayor likes it".
- P140c: a good thing the tent gives, such as a dry place. Not a cost.
- P140d: primary, written in 1960 by someone there. Not "secondary".
- Confirm Card A: legend, circle the triangle (library); direction, the library is north of the school; scale, 2 bars.
- Confirm Card B: the neighbors have a say; the vote is how they give consent; the manager carries it out.
- Confirm Card C: a cost, the $4,000 or the bench not bought; a benefit, shade from a tree; the opportunity cost, the bench.
- Confirm Card D: a claim such as the first lamp was lit in 1911; a detail from Source 1, tonight the first lamp was lit; from Source 2, the year 1911; Source 1 is primary and Source 2 is secondary.
