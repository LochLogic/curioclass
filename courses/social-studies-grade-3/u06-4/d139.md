# Day 139: Fix one idea

## Teacher page

### Before class
Score days 137 and 138 from key-check3.md and write on each check 3 sheet one note: a circled missed idea and one sentence. Sort children into at most four groups by the largest gaps: A maps, B leaders and consent, C cost and choice, D claim and two sources. Put each group's practice card on its table. All cards are made up; say so. Keep key-exits-139.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback
Hand back day 137's assessment and day 138's assessment, the check 3 sheets with their notes (and day 136's slips, s06-E136 and s06-E136b): sc3-E1, sc3-E1b, sc3-E1c, sc3-E1d, sc3-E1e, sc3-E2, sc3-E2b, sc3-E2c, sc3-E2d, sc3-E2e, sc3-E3, sc3-E3b, sc3-E3c, sc3-E4, sc3-E4b, sc3-E4c, sc3-E5, sc3-E5b, sc3-E5c, sc3-E5e, sc3-E5d and sc3-E5f. Each child reads the note silently or with a partner. Say: a fix needs a new answer and a reason, not a copy.

#### 20 min. Groups
Each group meets you for five minutes in turn; the others work in pairs from the practice card on their table. Hear each child in your group once, about 45 seconds each: Group A, [[sc3-P139a]]; Group B, [[sc3-P139b]]; Group C, [[sc3-P139c]]; Group D, [[sc3-P139d]]. Name each child heard on the record.

#### 10 min. Fresh task
Hand each child the Exit Card for their group on the half slip. Each child fixes the idea marked on their sheet: [[sc3-X139]] and [[sc3-X139b]]. A scribe or a spoken answer is offered. Read the ask for the marked idea aloud from key-exits-139.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Reflection
Each child says to a partner what they fixed and why. Nobody is made the example of a mistake. Close the folders.

**Shared shape.** The group practice asks are spoken, one fresh case each. The exit uses a different Exit Card and asks for a written fix with a detail.

### Other rooms
No groups: read each note aloud to one child; they fix the idea on the Exit Card for their note.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; the parent reads the note aloud.

**Skip.** The group asks.

- **5 min. The note.** Read the note on the check 3 sheet.
- **15 min. Try again.** Pick a made-up card that fits the note and talk it through. **Say.** "What did you miss, and what would you add?" Expect: the idea named in the note, with a detail from the card.
- **10 min. The fix.** Write the new answer and a detail from the card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc3-X139]] [[sc3-X139b]] Tell the adult what you fixed.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: fix one idea only; drop the group asks. Longer: fix a second marked idea on another card.

### Words and supports
Words: note, fix, evidence, card. Frame: "I fixed ___ because the card says ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 140 hands back today's slips, then each child redoes the idea once more on a new card and confirms it.

### Credit
Ours. All cards are made up.
