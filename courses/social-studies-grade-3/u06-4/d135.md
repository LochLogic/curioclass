# Day 135: Unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-day135.md and key-day135.md; the key stays with you. Print the five packet pages. For a state other than Massachusetts, swap in the class's own state passages on page 5. Collect the revised records and strips from day 134. Never score a first attempt. Say each nation by its own name, in the present tense.

### The day

#### 1 min. Directions
Say: pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each packet once, just before its questions. You may pass any question. No timer is shown.

#### 35 min. Assessment
Five steps in order, each read from the key and packets as named. Each child answers on the sheet.

1. Q1, 8 min. Read the word notes for packet A from the key, then packet A once. Each child writes [[s06-Q1]], [[s06-Q1b]] and [[s06-Q1c]].
2. Q2, 10 min. Read the word notes for packets B and C, then both once. Each child writes [[s06-Q2]], [[s06-Q2b]], [[s06-Q2c]], [[s06-Q2d]] and [[s06-Q2e]].
3. Q3, 5 min. Read packet D once, with its word note. Each child writes [[s06-Q3]] and [[s06-Q3b]].
4. Q4, 5 min. Read the own-state card once. Each child writes [[s06-Q4]] and [[s06-Q4b]].
5. Q5, 7 min. Each child writes the claim, a detail from packet A with its reasoning, a detail from packet D and what D adds: [[s06-Q5]], [[s06-Q5b]], [[s06-Q5c]], [[s06-Q5d]] and [[s06-Q5e]]. Packets stay in view. A spoken answer, a pointed answer or a scribe is offered to any child, without supplying reasoning.

#### 2 min. Marking
Each child checks that every question has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most six children who left one blank answer it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record.

#### 2 min. Collection
Collect the sheets. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-day135.md, and write the missing part with its letter, not a total. Score the revised records and strip from the folder: [[s06-R1]], [[s06-R2]], [[s06-R2b]], [[s06-R3]], [[s06-R4]], [[s06-R4b]]. Never score day 134's first attempts.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the packets large by hand and read each aloud from your copy; a child may hear a packet again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read each packet aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** The folder records.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Read each packet once, then each question once. Do not help. **Say.** "What does consent of the governed mean?" Expect: people have a say in how they are governed. [[s06-Q1]] [[s06-Q2]] [[s06-Q3]] [[s06-Q4]] [[s06-Q5]]
- **5 min. Your records.** Your child shows each revised record. [[s06-R2]] [[s06-R3]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: packets A and D with Q1, Q3 and Q5. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: packet, claim, detail, source. Read every packet and question aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 136 returns each sheet with one note, then each child fixes one missed idea on a fresh card. The groups follow the letters you wrote on the sheets.

### Credit
Ours. The packets quote the National Archives (U6AP, U6CP, U6DP) and follow the Massachusetts Historical Society (U6P), the New Jersey Historical Commission (U6F) and the Massachusetts constitution (U6R).
