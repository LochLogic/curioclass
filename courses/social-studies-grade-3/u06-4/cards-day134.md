Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. The teacher reads each card aloud once.

## Fresh line (retrieval model, made up, for practice)

A bridge stood from 1790 to 1810. A flood came in 1835. Draw the bridge band and the flood on a line with ten-year marks. The gap from 1810 to 1835 follows the years.
