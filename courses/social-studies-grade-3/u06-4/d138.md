# Day 138: Course check 3, part 2

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-check3.md and key-check3.md; the key stays with you. Print card 5. The port and both sources are made up: say so. Unit 6 is not the topic. Never score a first attempt. Keep day 137's sheets with you.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions
Say: pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each source once, just before its questions. You may pass any question. No timer is shown.

#### 6 min. Sources
Read Source A and Source B aloud once. Say: Source A is a letter from 1924; Source B is a book from 2005.

#### 14 min. The claim
Say: a claim is a sentence that answers the question. Each child writes [[sc3-E4]], [[sc3-E4b]] and [[sc3-E4c]]. Both sources stay in view. A spoken answer, a pointed answer or a scribe is offered to any child, without supplying the claim or a detail.

#### 12 min. Agree and differ
Each child writes [[sc3-E5]], [[sc3-E5b]], [[sc3-E5c]], [[sc3-E5e]], [[sc3-E5d]] and [[sc3-E5f]].

#### 3 min. Marking
Each child checks that every question has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most six who left one blank say it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record.

#### 3 min. Collection
Collect the sheets and cards. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-check3.md, together with day 137. Day 137's assessment goes back with this one on day 139. Write the missing part by name (map, leaders, consent, choice, claim, sources), not a total.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy the two sources large by hand and read them aloud from your copy; a child may hear them again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read both sources aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** Day 137's sheet.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Read both sources once, then each question once. Do not help. **Say.** "How is a letter written that month different from a book written later?" Expect: the letter is primary, the book secondary. [[sc3-E4]] [[sc3-E5c]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the claim and the two source types. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: claim, source, primary, secondary, agree, differ. Read every source and question aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 139 hands both parts back with one note each, then each child fixes one missed idea on a fresh card.

### Credit
Ours. The port, the letter and the book are made up.
