# Exit cards, days 133, 134 and 136

Print "made up, for practice" on the invented cards. Day 135 has no exit; it is the check. Each exit is a half slip. The teacher reads each stem aloud once. The key is in key-exits-133-136.md (teacher only).

## Day 133: Detail Card (made up, for practice)

Question: Do the people of Willow Park have a say about the park?

Detail X: A sign at the gate lists the park rules.

Detail Y: Each year neighbors vote on new park rules at the meeting.

Ask 1: Detail Card. Which detail answers the question, X or Y?

Ask 2: Detail Card. Write a claim that answers the question, using that detail.

## Day 134: Overlap Card (made up, for practice)

A mill ran from 1785 to 1835. A canal opened in 1810 and still runs.

Ask: Overlap Card. When did the mill and the canal run together?

## Day 136: Gloss Cards (from the Massachusetts Constitution, original wording)

Gloss 1 (Declaration of Rights, Article V): the people hold the power, and officials answer to them.

Gloss 2 (Declaration of Rights, Article IX): people who meet the qualifications the plan sets have an equal right to elect officials.

Ask 1: Gloss Cards. Do these two statements prove everyone could vote?

Ask 2: Gloss Cards. Use both glosses to explain your answer.
