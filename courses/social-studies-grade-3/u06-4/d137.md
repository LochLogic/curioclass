# Day 137: Course check 3, part 1

## Teacher page

### Before class
Read packet-check3.md and key-check3.md; the key stays with you. Draw and print cards 1 to 4; all are made up, so say so. Unit 6 was assessed on day 135, so the consent question is asked on our own town or state, not on the founding papers. Never score a first attempt. Say each word plainly: legend, consent, cost, opportunity cost.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions
Say: pencil only, no talk, no one marks their own page. I read each card once, just before its questions. You may pass any question. No timer is shown.

#### 3 min. Hand back
Hand back day 136's fix cards, s06-P136a, with the note on each: one circled idea and one sentence. Each child reads it silently, then the cards are put away.

#### 12 min. Maps
Read card 1 once. Each child writes [[sc3-E1]] and [[sc3-E1b]]. Read card 2 once. Each child writes [[sc3-E1c]], [[sc3-E1d]] and [[sc3-E1e]]. The cards stay in view.

#### 10 min. Leaders and consent
Read each ask once: [[sc3-E2]], [[sc3-E2b]], [[sc3-E2c]], [[sc3-E2d]] and [[sc3-E2e]]. A spoken answer, a pointed answer or a scribe is offered to any child, without supplying the answer.

#### 8 min. The Birchfield Card
Read card 4 once. Say what each word means: a cost is what is paid or given up; a benefit is a good thing a choice gives. Each child writes [[sc3-E3]], [[sc3-E3b]] and [[sc3-E3c]].

#### 3 min. Marking
Each child checks that every question has an answer: a completeness check, not a mark. At most six who left one blank say it aloud at the side table, about 45 seconds each; name them on the record.

#### 2 min. Collection
Collect the sheets and cards. After class score each part once, 2, 1 or 0, from key-check3.md. Write the missing part by name, not a total.

### Other rooms
No printer: copy each card large by hand and read it aloud from your copy; a child may hear a card again.

### At home

**Swap.** One parent and one child; you read each card aloud once and write what your child says.

**Skip.** Day 136's card.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Read each card once, then each question once. Do not help. **Say.** "What does consent of the governed mean?" Expect: people have a say in how they are governed. [[sc3-E1]] [[sc3-E2d]] [[sc3-E3c]]
- **5 min. Finish.** Your child rereads and finishes. Do not help.

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: the maps and the Birchfield Card. Longer: none; the check stays the same length.

### Words and supports
Words: legend, mayor, governor, consent, cost, opportunity cost. Read every card and question aloud; pointing, drawing, speech and a scribe are answers; a child may pass.

### Tomorrow
Day 138 is part 2 of the check: two sources about one event, and a claim. Day 139 hands both parts back.

Ours. All cards are made up.
