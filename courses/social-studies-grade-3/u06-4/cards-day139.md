# Day 139: fresh cards for the four groups

Every card is made up, for practice. Group A is maps, group B is leaders and consent, group C is cost and choice, group D is claim and two sources. The practice asks are spoken and use the first card of each group. The exit slip uses the second card (the Exit Card) and is written; print it on a half slip with the two exit asks, [[sc3-X139]] and [[sc3-X139b]].

## Group A: maps

Practice card, Fresh Map Card. The arrow points to the left edge. The dock is on the left side of the page from the mill. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P139a]]

Exit Card A. Pike Island Map. The arrow points up. Legend: a flag is the post office; a circle is the lighthouse, a landmark; a wavy line is the lake. The lighthouse is 3 bars from the post office, to its right.

## Group B: leaders and consent

Practice card, Fresh Leader Card. A governor signs a plan for a state bridge. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P139b]]

Exit Card B. Pike Island holds a vote on a new ferry. Most neighbors vote yes. The mayor of Pike Island says the plan will go ahead.

## Group C: cost and choice

Practice card, Fresh Choice Card. A school picks new desks over a new rug. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P139c]]

Exit Card C. A town has $6,000 for a playground. It can buy a slide or a swing set. It buys the slide.

## Group D: claim and two sources

Practice card, Fresh Pair Card. Source 1, a harbor notice, 1938, by the harbor board for fishers: "Forty boats came to the spring fleet." Source 2, a photo caption, 1938, by the town: "Boats filled the harbor." The claim: the fleet was large. Ask (spoken): [[sc3-P139d]]

Exit Card D. The question is: when did Pike Island open its school? Source 1: a school bell log, 1952, written that day: "The school opened Monday with 22 children." Source 2: a town history, 2012, written for readers today: "The school opened in 1952."

## The exit slip, half page

Say: use the Exit Card for your group. Each child writes [[sc3-X139]] and [[sc3-X139b]].
