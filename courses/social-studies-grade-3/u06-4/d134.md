# Day 134: Revise the records and the strip

## Teacher page

### Before class
Before class, write one line on each child's day 125, 128 and 131 records, about 30 seconds each, across days 126 to 133: what is there, what is missing, nothing more. Keep key-exits-133-136.md. Nothing new is taught today; no timer is shown.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval, with the H and M model
Say the six supporting questions together. Review: on day 119 and again on day 130 you drew bands on a strip with ten-year marks. Draw the fresh line: a bridge stood from 1790 to 1810; a flood came in 1835. The gap from 1810 to 1835 is drawn longer than the bridge band because the years are more. Gaps follow years; bands may sit together. Say once that one thing starting did not end another.

#### 5 min. Written feedback
Hand back the day 125, 128 and 131 records with their notes (s06-P125a, s06-P128a, s06-P131a). Each child reads the one-line note. Pairs may read a note aloud. Say: a revision is a new line, not a copy.

#### 15 min. Revise the records
Five minutes each: who else cares about the big question (125); who drafted and who approved the national plan (128); the three-question sort (131). Words only, [[s06-P134b]]. Hand back day 133's slips and claims (s06-E133, s06-E133b, s06-P133a, s06-P133b); the G and Q children take a fresh pair of details at the small-group table, five minutes, frame withdrawn. The notes do the work; hear nothing aloud.

#### 5 min. Fresh strip
Each child draws a strip with ten-year marks, puts 1787 and 1791 on it by their years, and draws the New Jersey band from 1776 to 1807 under them, [[s06-P134a]]. Use the ruler.

#### 5 min. Teacher written check
Walk the room and write one cue on each strip: order, spacing or overlap, about ten seconds a child. Children read quietly; no one waits for you.

#### 5 min. Revision
Three minutes: each child revises the strip on a clean copy from the cue. Then the Overlap Card, read aloud once with its ask once: [[s06-E134]]. Key: key-exits-133-136.md. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Revised records and strips are scored with day 135, not marked here.

**Shared shape.** The practice redraws the same strip and rewrites old records; the exit gives two new dated bands and asks when both ran.

### Other rooms
No ruler: fold a paper strip into ten equal parts for the years.

### At home

**Swap.** No clean strip: use the back of the page.

**Skip.** The fresh line.

- **10 min. Notes.** Read the notes on the three records. **Say.** "What is missing?" Expect: the part the note names.
- **15 min. New lines.** [[s06-P134b]]
- **10 min. The strip.** [[s06-P134a]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-E134]]

### Shorter or longer
Shorter: revise two records and the strip. Longer: add a second band on the strip and say which years it shares.

### Words and supports
Words: overlap, note, revise. Frame: "Both ran from ___ to ___." Read every note aloud; pointing and a scribe are answers.

### Tomorrow
Day 135 is the unit check, unaided, from packets read aloud. It scores the revised records and strip from this folder. The exit tells you who needs the overlap idea first.

### Credit
Ours. The bridge, flood, mill and canal are made up.
