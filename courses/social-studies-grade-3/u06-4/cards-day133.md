# Cards for day 133

Print "made up, for practice" on every invented card. The teacher reads each card aloud once.

## Club claim (teacher example, made up, for practice)

The Reading Club. Claim: The club shares its books. Detail 1: Members take turns taking each book home. Detail 2: The club meets on Tuesdays. The teacher links the detail that fits to the claim, then the frame is taken down.

Sources to pick from: the Declaration's consent line, an article of the Constitution, the speech clause, a New Jersey rule, a symbol caption.

## The petition, in words (teacher says; never read aloud as a quote)

In January 1777 a group of Black men in Massachusetts sent a petition to the Council and House. Prince Hall and seven other Black men signed it. They said all people have a natural right to freedom. We have a handwritten copy, so the words we have come through that copy. Ask: what was the promise, and what was the practice?
