# Teacher key, day 139 (practice and exit)

Teacher only. Mark each shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Ask the marked idea from the child's sheet on the Exit Card.

- P139a: north. The arrow points to the left edge, so the left edge is north and the dock is north of the mill. Not "up".
- P139b: state. Not town.
- P139c: the new rug, the next best choice given up. Not the desks.
- P139d: a detail such as forty boats came. Not "boats are fun".
- Exit Card A: legend, circle the circle (lighthouse); direction, the lighthouse is east of the post office; scale, 3 bars.
- Exit Card B: consent means the neighbors have a say; the vote is how they give it; the mayor carries the ferry plan out. Not "the mayor decides alone".
- Exit Card C: a cost, the $6,000 or the swing set not bought; a benefit, children have a slide; the opportunity cost, the swing set.
- Exit Card D: a claim such as the school opened in 1952; a detail from Source 1, 22 children came on Monday; from Source 2, the year 1952; Source 1 is primary and Source 2 is secondary.
- X139: a new answer that differs from the sheet and fits the card. Not the same answer again. X139b: a detail from the Exit Card, said or written.
