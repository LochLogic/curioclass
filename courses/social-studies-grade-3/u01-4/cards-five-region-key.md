# Five-region key (ours)

Our classroom grouping of the 50 states. Other maps may group states differently.

- **Northeast:** Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland.
- **Southeast:** Virginia, West Virginia, Kentucky, Tennessee, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Florida, Alabama, Mississippi, Arkansas, Louisiana.
- **Midwest:** Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, Minnesota, Iowa, Missouri, North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska, Kansas.
- **Southwest:** Oklahoma, Texas, New Mexico, Arizona.
- **West:** Montana, Wyoming, Colorado, Idaho, Utah, Nevada, Washington, Oregon, California, Alaska, Hawaii.
