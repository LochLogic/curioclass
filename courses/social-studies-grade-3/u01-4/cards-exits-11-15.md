# Exit cards, days 11 to 15

Print "invented for practice" at the bottom of each card. Every child answers in writing; the day's six are also heard.

## Day 11: Exit Map 1

A political map with one state marked: Missouri (use Tennessee for classes in the Midwest) and the five-region key printed beside it. Ask: write the region of the key that holds it; circle the marked state's name in its region list.

## Day 12

A new outline of our own region. Ask: write the states that touch our state. 

## Day 13

A new Southwest outline with an eight-word bank: Oklahoma, Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, Colorado, Arkansas, Southwest, Rocky Mountains. Ask: label the Southwest's states from the bank, using the key.

## Day 14: Fenmark Panel

Invented for practice. A made-up panel with four places marked A to D and a key. The key shows a row of brown triangles and a wavy blue line, and names the shapes of land and water in words. Drawing notes are in key-exits-11-15.md. Ask: use the key to say what kind of feature each place is.

## Day 15: Hillford Map and Photo

Invented for practice. A map and a photograph of a town where a road crosses a river bend by a bridge. Ask: what could help explain where Hillford is?

The teacher key is in key-exits-11-15.md, a separate piece. Do not give it to children.
