# Gull Cove card (day 14, teacher model)

Invented for practice. A made-up harbor map: a bay shaped like a curve of water, a cape on the north side, a pier, a few boats, and a key (a wavy blue line for a creek, a grey bar for the pier, a blue half-circle for the harbor). The teacher shows how to read the kind of feature from the key.
