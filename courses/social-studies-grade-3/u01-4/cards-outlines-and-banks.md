# Outlines and name banks (days 12 and 13, teaching)

Invented for practice. Day 12 teaching: an outline of the West region with Montana marked, and a worked example: Montana touches Idaho, North Dakota, South Dakota and Wyoming (source: Montana page, read 2026-11-21; Canada is also on its northern border and is not a state). Pairs find the states that touch Utah on a second outline.

Day 13 teaching: an outline of the Midwest with a bank of names that also holds names that do not belong (a Southeast state, a landform, a region name). Pairs label every Midwest state from the key and cross out the names that do not belong.

Teacher key: the key's lists. A bank word that is a region name or a landform is crossed out as not a state.
