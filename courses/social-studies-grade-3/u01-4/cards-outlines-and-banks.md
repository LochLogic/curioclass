# Outlines and name banks (days 12 and 13, teaching)

Invented for practice. Day 12 teaching: an outline of New England with Massachusetts marked, and a worked example: Massachusetts touches New Hampshire, Vermont, New York, Connecticut and Rhode Island. Pairs find the states that touch our own state on an outline of our region.

Day 13 teaching: an outline of our region and an outline of another region (the Midwest, or the Northeast if ours is the Midwest), each with a plain bank of that region's states only. Pairs label each outline from the bank with the key's list beside them.
