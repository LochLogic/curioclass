# Land and water picture cards (day 14)

Invented for practice. Eight cards, one picture each, labeled by one word: river, lake, mountains, shoreline, cape, bay, pass, harbor. A cape is land that sticks out into water; a bay is water that curves into the land; a pass is a low way between mountains; a harbor is a sheltered place for boats. Pairs match each card to a feature on the physical map.
