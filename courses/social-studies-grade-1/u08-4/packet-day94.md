# Day 94 packet: check 2, day one, maps (closed until day 94)

Nothing here is taught before day 94. Cover every completed teaching map in the room; leave blank map pages and the unit 5 key words if they are up. Read each prompt aloud once, again when asked. Say aloud: "Hollin Bay, Dunmore and Brindle Vale are made up. The world view is real." Print "invented for practice" on each made-up map. Thick lines, large print. Accept a point, token, drawing, dictation or familiar-language speech. Judge spatial meaning, not drawing skill.

## Map 1: Hollin Bay (items 1 and 2)

A town seen from above. Key box: a triangle is the station, a square is the school, a circle is the market, wavy lines are water. An arrow marked N points to the top of the page. In page terms: the triangle in the middle, the square to its right, the circle above it, and a wavy pond to its left. Only the key names the marks.

## Map 2: Dunmore (items 3 and 4)

A state outline, made up. Key box: a star is the capital, a big dot is a major city, a small dot is a town. A star labelled Marlow near the middle; a big dot labelled Quay City on the coast, with a small harbor drawing; a small dot labelled Pell. Read the record: "Quay City is the biggest city in Dunmore. Ships stop at the harbor in Quay City and bring goods from far away, and many shops opened there." The labels are read aloud.

## Map 3: a fresh world view (item 5)

A real flat world map, drawn fresh and centered on a different place from the views of days 50 and 54. Key box: gray is land, blue is water. The seven continents and five oceans are named on it. Natural Earth outlines (public domain, source 50E); names checked on sources 50A and 50C.

## Map 4: Brindle Vale (item 6)

A landform map, made up. Key box: brown rings are a mountain, a blue band with arrows is a river, a blue oval is a lake. The river starts at the foot of the mountain and runs down the page into the lake.

## Item 7: the day 52 map

Each child's day 52 map, revised on day 53 and saved for today. Look-fors: the land and water are where the photographs show; each symbol has one meaning in the key; north is shown; at least two places; one place is named east or west of another.

## How the day runs

Three groups of six are heard, about three minutes each, one ask a child chosen by the record sheet. A child not heard stays not yet seen on the spoken part and is heard on day 96. Mark each part shown, shown with help or not yet seen. All look-fors in a row are needed for shown.
