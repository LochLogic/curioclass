# Day 93: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet's zeros make at most four groups, most-missed letter first: H item 1; L item 2; M item 3; D item 4; E item 5; F item 6; P item 7; G item 8. If more than a third missed one letter, plan 4 minutes of whole-class teaching on it first and three groups after. Lay out the repair cards and the fresh-try cards. Keep the fresh-try cards closed. Say: "These people, shops and banks are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Return evidence

Day 92's assessment is marked: each child gets their records and packet pages back, marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, with a picture note. The class sees which letters were missed most, never named children. Error G from day 91 goes to its group if the record sheet shows it. **Ask.** "Which part will you try again?" Expect: a point to a part.

#### 10 min. Reteach

Review: each group's idea was taught on days 81 to 91; this is a second look, not new teaching. Each group of about seven meets the teacher for 2 minutes with its letter's repair card and an unused case; the rest work in pairs from a printed repair card. Pip makes the mistake on purpose. Never repeat a day 92 packet case or a fresh-try case. If a third of the class missed one letter, teach it to everyone for 4 minutes, then meet three groups for 2 minutes each.

#### 10 min. Fresh try

Each child gets the fresh-try card for their group's letter and marks the picture. [[s08-X93a]] [[s08-X93a2]] L, a flyer. [[s08-X93b]] M, what is given up. [[s08-X93c]] H, a salary. [[s08-X93d]] [[s08-X93d2]] D, two shops. [[s08-X93e]] E, saving reasons. [[s08-X93f]] F, a loan. [[s08-X93g]] P, nine tokens in jars. [[s08-X93h]] [[s08-X93h2]] G, a claim with a reason. Say: "These cards are made up." Hear up to six children whose explanation stayed missing, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), the card's spoken ask. A child not heard stays "not yet seen".

#### 5 min. Reflect

Each child marks one improvement on a picture card: "I can now ...". No one is named as an example. A child may pass. Update the record sheet.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the missed idea on paper and make up a new case.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard children.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at the marked work together. Your child picks one part.
- **20 min. Try again.** Make up a new made-up card for that part.
  **Say.** "What did you not show before? What is different now?" Expect: a detail pointed to.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Pick the row for the idea: [[s08-X93a]] [[s08-X93a2]] [[s08-X93b]] [[s08-X93c]] [[s08-X93d]] [[s08-X93d2]] [[s08-X93e]] [[s08-X93f]] [[s08-X93g]] [[s08-X93h]] [[s08-X93h2]]. Read it aloud; your child answers.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group's idea and one try. Longer: a second idea tried again.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: again, reason, closing, loan, salary. Frame: "I can now ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 94 is the course check, year-end, day one: reading a map not seen before. Unit 8's record sheet and pages stay with the unit.
