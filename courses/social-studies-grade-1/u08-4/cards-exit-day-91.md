# Exit card, day 91

Say aloud first: "This story is made up. This card is practice." Print "invented for practice" at the bottom. Keep closed until the day.

**Story, read aloud:** Ola pulls a small wagon to the market. When it rains, the wagon seat gets wet.

**Claim one:** "Buy the rain cover, because the story says the wagon seat gets wet." **Claim two:** "Buy it, because we should buy it." Closings: "So the rain cover keeps the seat dry." and "So yes."

**Mark:** circle the claim that has a reason. **Spoken (one ask, chosen by the record sheet):** "Why is that claim supported?" or "Which closing goes with it, So the rain cover keeps the seat dry, or So yes?"
