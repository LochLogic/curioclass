# Day 92: What we know about earning, buying and saving: the check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet closed until now. Cover the mat, the jar models and the claim boxes: only blank frames stay. From the record sheet pick up to ten children who still lack a spoken explanation for items 1 to 6, most missing first. If day 91's exit showed error G for many, note it; day 93 takes it in a group. Say: "The people, shops and banks in the packet are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reread the packet

Read the six parts once each, with their pictures: three workers, a flyer, Robin, two seed shop cards, three reasons to save, a bank card. Review the source question: who made it (we did, for this check), when (now), what for. No talk, no hints.

#### 15 min. Items 7 and 8

Tables of five, alone. 7 minutes: [[s08-E7]] each child places 11 tokens in the three jar outlines, each once, and draws or says what the save jar is for and what waits. 8 minutes: [[s08-E8]] each child draws, writes a word or dictates a claim, a reason from the packet and a closing for "Should Robin buy the kite now or save all the money?" Circulate; the only help is rereading a packet card. Hear at most ten children, 30 seconds each (5 minutes), where a picture is unclear or a child dictates; the rest answer on the page. Unfinished pictures end here.

#### 10 min. Missing explanations

The ten chosen children come one at a time, one minute each, one item each: the card is marked and the spoken ask heard. Items: [[s08-E1a]] pay cards, Dara and Lee; [[s08-E1b]] who earns by selling; [[s08-E2a]] [[s08-E2a2]] goods; [[s08-E2b]] the service; [[s08-E2c]] prices; [[s08-E3a]] enough tokens for Robin; [[s08-E3b]] [[s08-E3b2]] what Robin gives up; [[s08-E4]] two seed shops; [[s08-E5a]] the gift; [[s08-E5b]] [[s08-E5b2]] what cannot wait; [[s08-E6]] the bank card. A child with no revised record for an item does that item here; an unconfirmed part stays missing. The rest draw their plan quietly.

Collect every page and packet sheet at the end of each part. After class, about 20 minutes: score items 1 to 6 from the revised records of days 84, 86, 88, 90 and 91, and items 7 and 8 from today. Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Judge the economics, not the drawing.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: make up three cards with two prices and a short story.

**Skip.** The packet, the heard children and the record sheet.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Give no hints. Tell a made-up story of a child with ten paper tokens and three circles.
  **Say.** "Put the tokens in the circles. What is the save circle for, and what waits?" Expect: a later thing; something today waits.
  **Say.** "What would you choose, and what in the story says so?" Expect: a claim, a detail, a closing.
- **5 min. Respond.** Note one part that was hard; try it again on a new made-up story.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the jars and the claim only. Longer: a second claim with the other choice.

### Words and supports

Read every item aloud. Words: wages, salary, good, service, price, give up, save, bank, claim. Frame: "I think ___, because ___. So ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 93 acts on this check: marked work back, small groups by letter, H, L, M, D, E, F, P and G, most-missed first, then a fresh try. Error G from day 91 goes to its group.
