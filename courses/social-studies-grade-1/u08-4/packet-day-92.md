# Day 92 packet (reserved)

Held by the teacher. Never rehearsed, never shown before day 92. Read aloud in short parts with pictures. Say first: "These people and shops are made up." Print "invented for practice" on every card. Source questions: who made it, when, what for. Tokens are paper, each worth one dollar.

## Part 1: the story of three workers

Dara cuts hair at a salon and is paid by the hour, so her pay depends on her hours. Lee works at a library; his job agreement sets his pay for the year, and he is paid a part of it each month. Uma grows vegetables and sells them at a stall; nobody pays her wages or a salary.

## Part 2: the flyer

A pretend shop flyer: a kite, 6 dollars; a bicycle repair, 9 dollars; a packet of seeds, 11 dollars.

## Part 3: Robin

Robin has 11 dollars. Robin ranks the kite first and the bicycle repair second.

## Part 4: two seed shop cards, each made by its shop

One card: "We give our workers paid rest breaks. Our packet of seeds costs 11 dollars." The other: "We use less packaging. Our packet of seeds costs 11 dollars." Two buyer pictures stand between the shops: one buyer who cares how workers are treated, and one who cares about waste.

## Part 5: three reasons to save

One person saves for a picnic blanket. Another saves tokens to give to a community garden. Another keeps money for a broken window nobody expected.

## Part 6: a fresh bank card

A made-up bank card: "We accept savings and write down how much each saver has. We pay savers a little interest. We also lend money, and borrowers repay it." A picture of a record page, a picture of gifts handed out, a picture of money given to anyone.

## Item sheets

- **Item 7:** three jar outlines and 11 tokens. Place all 11, each once. Draw or say what the save jar is for and what is given up or waits now.
- **Item 8:** three boxes. Claim, reason from the packet, closing, for the question: should Robin buy the kite now or save all the money?
- **Packet questions** (marks by picture, one ask each, as items.csv lists): pay cards for Dara and Lee; circle who earns by selling; tokens on goods; circle the service; point to and compare prices; enough or not enough for Robin's 11; what Robin gives up; shops joined to buyers; the gift and what cannot wait; the bank record.

Scoring rides on items.csv, the revised records, and the answer key read by another worker.
