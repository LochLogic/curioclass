# Day 91: A claim, a reason and a closing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang three boxes labeled claim, reason, closing. Leave the jar outlines and the day 86 mat blank on the wall. Keep exit card 91 closed. Say: "Mira, Teo, these cards and flyers are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: return My Bank Page with a picture note. 1 minute: post "How should people choose what to buy and what to save?" 3 minutes: day 90's exit. If many made error P, all 3 minutes are the repair: Lia has 8 tokens and three jars; place each token in one jar, once. **Ask.** "How many tokens in each jar? Do they make 8?" Expect: any split that makes 8; each token once.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Review: in unit 3 and unit 7 you made a claim, a reason and a closing. Mira has 10 tokens. The flyer: a sketch pad 6, a paint set 12. The story card says Mira ranked the paint set first. Model, one box at a time: claim, "Mira should save for the paint set." Reason, "The flyer says it costs 12 and she has 10, and spending 6 now leaves 4." Closing, "So saving gets Mira the thing she wants most." Then cover the example. Pip says "Mira should save for the paint set, because she should save" (error G). **Ask.** "What does the card say that Pip could point to?" Expect: the paint set costs 12 and Mira has 10. Repeating the claim is not a reason.

#### 16 min. Supported work

5 minutes: each child revises My Bank Page, made on day 89, one change; circulate. 8 minutes: pairs get Teo's cards: Teo has 9 tokens and ranked a wind chime first and a lunch box second; the flyer shows the chime 4 and the lunch box 9. Each child draws, writes a word or dictates a claim, a reason from a card and a closing. Either choice can be defended. Hear the sixth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 8): "What do you choose, and what on the card says so? What closes it?" 3 minutes: partners read aloud "my reason is on the card". A child may pass. Practice, never scored.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 91 (1 minute). [[s08-X91]] Say: "This story is made up." The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No flyer: write two prices on paper.

**Skip.** The partner reading and the heard six.

- **5 min. Read.** Read Mira's cards: 10 tokens, a sketch pad 6, a paint set 12. All made up.
- **15 min. Claim.** Your child says a claim, a reason from a card and a closing.
  **Say.** "What do you choose? What on the card says so? What closes it?" Expect: a choice; a price or story detail; a closing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X91]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Mira only, then the exit. Longer: defend the other choice with a different reason.

### Words and supports

Words: claim, reason, closing, choose, save. Frames: "I think ___, because the card says ___." "So ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 92 is the check: a made-up packet is read aloud, then items 7 and 8. Nothing is taught that day. If the exit showed error G, day 93 takes it in a small group.
