# Day 94: Show what you know about maps

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 93 said today is the course check, year-end, day one, and teaches nothing. Unit 8's record sheet and pages stay with the unit. Keep the packet closed until now. Cover the teaching maps. Open the check 2 record sheet, one row a child, a column for each of items 1 to 7, and number three groups of six from children not yet heard on a spoken map part. Stack the day 52 maps, revised on day 53 and saved for today.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Say: "Today I find out what you can do with maps. I will not help you." Say: "Hollin Bay, Dunmore and Brindle Vale are made up. The world view is real." Read each prompt aloud once, again if asked. Pointing, drawing and dictation count.

#### 8 min. Hollin Bay

Paper: circle the station [[sc2-E1]] [[sc2-E1b]]; put a token east of the station [[sc2-E2]]. Group 1 comes to you for three minutes, half a minute each, one ask a child chosen by the record sheet from [[sc2-E1]] [[sc2-E1b]] or [[sc2-E2]]. Give no help.

#### 8 min. Dunmore

Read the Dunmore record aloud: Quay City is the biggest city, ships stop at its harbor and many shops opened there. Paper: circle the capital [[sc2-E3]]; circle the major city [[sc2-E4]] [[sc2-E4b]]. Group 2 comes for three minutes, one ask a child from [[sc2-E3]] or [[sc2-E4]] [[sc2-E4b]].

#### 8 min. World and landforms

Paper: put a token on a continent and circle an ocean on the fresh world view [[sc2-E5]]; on Brindle Vale put a token on the mountain, trace the river and circle where it ends [[sc2-E6]]. Group 3 comes for three minutes, one ask a child from [[sc2-E5]] or [[sc2-E6]].

#### 3 min. Hand in

Collect every sheet and every day 52 map. Mark each part shown, shown with help or not yet seen, about a minute a child after class. Read each child's day 52 map against its look-fors, scored from the work handed in [[sc2-E7]]; all look-fors are needed for shown. Three groups hear 18 children; the others are marked on paper only, and their spoken parts stay not yet seen for day 96.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a town map with a key, and a landform map, on scraps. No tokens: use buttons.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the groups.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Say these maps are made up. Draw a town: a triangle for the station, a square for the school, a pond to the left, a key, and an arrow for north pointing up. Your child finds the station by the key, and says which way to the pond. Draw a state with a star and a big dot beside a harbor; your child says why the star is the capital and what the harbor brought. Draw a river from a mountain into a lake; your child names each. Give no help.
  **Say.** "How did you find the station? Which way is the pond? What makes the oval a lake?" Expect: by the key; west; land all around it.
- **10 min. Respond.** Your child points to one part to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 1, 3 and 5 first; the rest wait for day 96. Longer: nothing is added.

### Words and supports

Read every prompt aloud. Pointing, drawing, dictation, a scribe, a private hearing and home-language words count. Frame: "The key says ___, so ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 95 is part two of the check: a letter read aloud, our Decision Record and a town problem. Day 94's results go to the record sheet and to day 96.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
