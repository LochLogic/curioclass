# Repair cards, day 93

Each group of about seven gets 2 minutes with its letter's repair and an unused case. The rest work in pairs from a printed repair card. Wrong ideas are spoken by Pip. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice". No case here repeats an exit, a fresh try or an earlier repair.

- **H:** Pip: "A salary comes in one lump on one day." Right: a salary is an agreed amount paid in regular parts. Retry: Pim, a zoo keeper, has a salary agreed for the year. The teacher lays pay envelope cards on January, February and March; the child says whether April brings another envelope, and why.
- **L:** Pip: "The 4 is the price of everything." Right: each tag belongs to its own offer. Retry: a flyer with a map at 10, a haircut at 4 and a puzzle at 8; point to the price of the haircut, then the price of the puzzle.
- **M:** Pip: "What I give up is the price." Right: the next best thing, not the tokens. Retry: Eli ranks a snow globe first, a pair of mittens second and a pencil third and buys the globe. Two label cards, "the price" and "given up": place each on the right card or tokens.
- **D:** Pip: "A buyer of the other shop is bad." Right: each buyer cares about something different; both are fine. Retry: Bakery card one, "We pay kitchen helpers extra on holidays"; Bakery card two, "We give day-old bread to a shelter." Say a reason a buyer might pick each.
- **E:** Pip: "Saving is for treats only." Right: later purchase, gift or emergency. Retry: a bicycle bell planned for May; a gift to the library; a flooded basement nobody expected. Put each in its hoop.
- **F:** Pip: "A bank gives free money." Right: a loan is repaid. Retry: the Corner Bank lends 3 tokens to Mina. Three picture cards, the bank lends, Mina uses the tokens, Mina repays: put them in order and say which step the bank waits for.
- **P:** Pip: "The same token is in two jars." Right: each token goes in one jar once. Retry: Ravi's three jars are drawn with 7 tokens, but one token card lies in both the spend jar and the save jar. Find the one that sits twice and fix it.
- **G:** Pip: "Buy a rug, because rugs are good." Right: a reason is a detail on a card, such as "the story says the reading corner floor is cold". Retry: a new story card, "the class paint pots are all dry"; Pip says "Buy new paint, because we should". The child finds the words on the card that can be the reason and says a claim and a closing.
