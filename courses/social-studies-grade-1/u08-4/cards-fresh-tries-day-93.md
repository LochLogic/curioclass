# Fresh-try cards, day 93

The teacher hands each child the card for the letter of their group. Say aloud: "This is made up." Print "invented for practice". Each card is read aloud and the child marks the picture. Up to six children whose explanation stayed missing are heard, one sentence each. Keep closed until the day.

- **L:** a lawn-mowing service at 7 dollars and a woolly scarf at 12 dollars on a flyer; join each tag to its offer. Spoken, with a new flyer, bread at 4 dollars and a wool hat at 11: "What does the hat cost?" or "Which offer has the 4?"
- **M:** Ana ranks a sun hat first, a pinwheel second and a ribbon third and buys the hat; circle the next best thing given up. Spoken: "Why that one?"
- **H:** Orla plans swimming classes at a town pool, her pay a salary agreed for the year; place a token card on each month of a twelve-month strip. Spoken: "When is Orla's pay for the year paid?"
- **D:** two lamp shops, each with its own card. Shop E: "We give workers paid sick days." Shop F: "Our lamps are made from old glass." Same price. Two buyers stand between the shops: one cares how workers are treated, and one likes things made from used materials. Join each card to a buyer who might pick it. Spoken: "Why might a buyer pick Shop F?"
- **E:** a class saves tokens to give to a food drive; a family saves for a plant stand; circle the saving reason that is a gift. Spoken: "What is the other reason to save?"
- **F:** Maple Bank lends 5 tokens to Wen, who needs a ladder; circle the arrow that shows what happens next, tokens going back, or tokens staying with Wen. Spoken: "Why?"
- **P:** Jo has 9 tokens and three jars. Jo wants a plant stand soon, and a leaking roof might need money someday. Place all 9 tokens, each once. Spoken: "What is the save jar for?"
- **G:** the class garden hose leaks and the beds stay dry. Claim one: "Buy a new hose, because the story says the old hose leaks." Claim two: "Buy a new hose, because we should." Circle the claim that has a reason. Spoken: "Which words are the reason?"
