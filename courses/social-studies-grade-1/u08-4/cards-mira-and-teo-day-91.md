# Mira and Teo: flyers and story cards (day 91)

Say aloud: "These people, shops and things are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print. Tokens are paper pieces, each worth one dollar; no real money.

## Mira (teaching, 7 minutes)

**Teaching flyer:** a sketch pad 6, a paint set 12. **Story card:** Mira has 10 tokens and ranked the paint set first. **Model:** "Mira should save for the paint set. The flyer says it costs 12 and she has 10, and spending 6 now leaves 4. So saving gets Mira the thing she wants most." Three labeled boxes: claim, reason, closing. The example is then covered.

## Teo (supported work, 8 minutes)

**Flyer:** a wind chime 4 and a lunch box 9. **Story card:** Teo has 9 tokens and ranked the wind chime first and the lunch box second. Each child draws, writes a word or dictates a claim, a reason from a card and a closing. Either choice can be defended. Partner read-aloud: "my reason is on the card".

## Pip

Pip says: "Mira should save for the paint set, because she should save." Right: the reason is a detail on a card, that the paint set costs 12 and Mira has 10. Pip gives one claim, never two to choose between. Pip is making a mistake on purpose.
