# Day 96 repair cards, fresh tries and exit cards

At most four teacher groups, most-missed letter first, about two minutes each at the teacher's table. The other children pair on a printed repair card. Say first: "These cards are made up." Wrong answers are voiced by "someone might say". Name each group by a picture, never by who missed. Never repeat a day 94 or day 95 case.

## Map group (errors M, N, S)

**Repair card:** "Trace the mark to the key. One mark, one meaning. North is the arrow's way." **New case:** a made-up map of Fenmoor with a key (a diamond is the museum, a cross is the clinic) and an arrow marked N pointing up. Children find the clinic by the key, say what the cross stands for and say which way to the museum.

## Places group (errors O, P)

**Repair card:** "A capital is where the government is centered. A city grows for a reason in the record." **New case:** a made-up state of Orrin, a star labeled Vale, a big dot labeled Port Orrin; the record: a railway meets the river at Port Orrin and many workers came. Children circle the capital and say why the big city grew.

## Land and water group (errors Q, R)

**Repair card:** "Gray is land, blue is water. A river flows. A lake has land all around." **New case:** a made-up landform map of Lantern Vale with a mountain, a river and a lake. Children trace the river and name each.

## Claim group (errors T, U, V)

**Repair card:** "Different and shared can both be true. Say the claim, then point to the detail." **New case:** a made-up letter from Heron Cove, read aloud: potters, drivers and gardeners, many festivals, and one town library everyone uses. Children sort different and shared and say a claim with one detail.

## Choice and problem group (errors W, Y, Z)

**Repair card:** "A group with limited time picks one. The one left is given up. A class helps safely, with a grown-up." **New case:** a made-up class has time for one of two jobs, a bulletin board or a book box; a made-up town's bench paint is worn. Children say what is given up and one safe step.

## Exit cards, day 96 (kept closed until the day)

Card a: the Pebble Cove map with a flag and a tent in its key ([[sc2-X96a]]). Card b: a Pebble Cove card, a lamp on the pier ([[sc2-X96b]]). Each child marks the card the record names; the spoken part is heard from the first six.
