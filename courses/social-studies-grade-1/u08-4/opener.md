# Section 8.4 opener: claim, reason, closing, and the check

## What the section does

Three days. Day 91 (application): answer the compelling question, "How should people choose what to buy and what to save?", with a claim, a reason from a card and a closing. Day 92 (check, observe): a withheld packet read aloud, the jar plan (item 7) and a claim, reason and closing (item 8); the revised records of items 1 to 6 are scored from earlier days. Day 93 (respond): small groups re-teach what the check showed, then a fresh try.

## How the days run

Day 91: reopen (5), Mira (7), supported work (16), exit (2). Day 92: reread the packet (5), items 7 and 8 (15), missing explanations (10). Day 93: return evidence (5), reteach (10), fresh try (10), reflect (5).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- A **claim** says what to do; a **reason** is a detail from a card or story that supports it; a **closing** says what the reason gives. "Buy it, because we should" only repeats the claim.
- Either affordable position can be defended: buy now, or save. The standard is a reason from the source, not agreement with the teacher.
- Day 92 is observation, not an exit. The packet is read aloud in short parts, said to be made up, and never rehearsed. The teacher gives no hints beyond rereading a card.
- Items 1 to 6 are scored from revised records only; nothing is repeated on the day.

## The errors, in plain words

- **G:** a claim with no reason. Beside it, a claim with a detail from the story.
- Day 93 repairs by letter: H, L, M, D, E, F, P and G, each with an unused case.

## Care

Nobody is ranked. Never post the record sheet. A child may pass. Accept pictures, a word, dictation or familiar-language speech.

## Supplies

Mira and Teo flyers and story cards, three-box sheet, exit card day 91, the reserved packet, jar sheets, 11 tokens each, fresh-try cards, repair cards.

## A room with nothing

A drawn flyer and story card, three boxes on paper, buttons for tokens.
