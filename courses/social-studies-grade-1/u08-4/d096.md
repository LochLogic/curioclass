# Day 96: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet's zeros make at most four groups by error letter, most missed first: map, places, land and water, claim, or choice and problem. If more than a third missed one letter, teach it to everyone for four minutes first. Others pair on a repair card. Keep fresh-try cards and exit cards closed. Say: "These cards are made up."

### The day

#### 4 min. Return evidence

Return day 94's and day 95's sheets. Each child gets their record: one thing shown, one next step. The class sees which letters were missed most, never named children. **Ask.** "Which part will you try again?" Expect: a point to a part.

#### 10 min. Reteach by group

If many missed day 94's items or day 95's items, this is a second look, not new teaching. Two minutes at your table per group with its repair card. Map group, errors M, N: trace the mark to the key; the arrow shows north; error S: one mark, one meaning. Places group, error O: the capital is where the state government is centered; error P: the record gives the reason. Land and water group, errors Q, R: read the key; the river flows to the lake. Claim group, errors T, U: different and shared are both true; error V: point to a detail. Choice and problem group, errors W, Y: the one left is given up, time is limited; error Z: safe steps with a grown-up.

#### 9 min. Fresh try

Each group gets its new made-up case on paper. Children draw, point or say the answer. Hear up to six children whose explanation stayed missing, half a minute each (3 minutes). The others stay not yet seen.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

All mark the exit card the record names. [[sc2-X96a]] Read aloud: "Circle the boat ramp." [[sc2-X96b]] [[sc2-X96c]] Read aloud: "Circle the evidence." Two minutes. The first six come to you, half a minute each: what the flag stands for, or the claim with because. Look for: the key, and a detail from the card (errors M, V). The rest draw what they will do next.

#### 3 min. Hand on

Finish the check 2 record sheet and file it with the unit records and each child's revised pages as one folder for the grade 2 teacher. The sheet is never posted where it ranks children.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the missed idea and make up a new case.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at the marked work together. Your child picks one part.
- **15 min. Try again.** Your child finds one mark on their own day 52 map by its key, and says what it stands for.
  **Say.** "What does this mark stand for? How do you know?" Expect: a real thing; the key says so.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc2-X96a]] [[sc2-X96b]] [[sc2-X96c]] Show the card. Your child marks it.
  **Say.** "I think ___, because ___." Expect: a claim joined to a detail by because.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group's idea and one try. Longer: a second idea tried again.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I think ___, because ___." Pointing, drawing and dictation count. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

There is no later day in this course. The record sheet and the revised pages go on to the grade 2 teacher.
