# Repair cards, section 8.3

Each repair puts a wrong idea beside a right one, spoken by Pip or a teacher card. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every card. At most four minutes. No card reuses an exit or the day 93 cases.

## E and O: three purposes, which can wait (opening of day 89, 4 minutes)

**Pip:** "Saving is only for treats." "Everything has a day you know." **Right:** people save for a later purchase, a gift to a cause or an emergency; an emergency has no known day. **Retry:** three new cards in three hoops: a bus fare for a museum trip next spring; a gift of warm hats to a coat drive; the class fish tank breaks. Ask "which can wait?".

## Q: the saver owns the savings (opening of day 90, 4 minutes)

**Pip:** "The bank owns the tokens." **Right:** the saver owns them; the bank keeps a record. **Retry:** a record card for a new saver, Wren, with 5 tokens; point to whose tokens they are.

## F and R: loans are repaid; the record stays (same 4 minutes)

**Pip:** "A bank gives free money." "When the bank lends, the savings disappear." **Right:** a loan is lent and repaid; the saver's amount stays on the record. **Retry:** Wren's record card kept beside a loan card, the Corner Bank lends 4 tokens to a baker, Odo; a loan arrow out and a repayment arrow back.

## P: each token in one jar once (opening of day 91, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "The same 3 tokens are in my save jar and I can buy a snack with them." **Right:** each token goes in one jar once; the snack waits. **Retry:** a new set of 8 tokens for a child, Lia; point to each jar and each token once.
