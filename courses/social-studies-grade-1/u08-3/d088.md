# Day 88: Why people save

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the mat from day 86 and three hoops. Lay out the cards. Keep exit card 88 closed. Say: "These people, cards and things are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: collect My Two Shops Page. 1 minute: return My Choice Mat with a picture note. 3 minutes: day 87's exit. If many made error D, all 3 minutes are the repair: Blue Shop's workers get a morning to rest; Green Shop collects rainwater. **Ask.** "A buyer might choose Blue because ...; another Green because ...?" Expect: a reason from each card; both fine. Post "Why and where save?"

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Review: in kindergarten you saved a token in a named envelope. Today: why people save. Three made-up cards, each told as a picture. Imo knows winter is coming, so Imo saves a little each week for boots. A class saves a gift for a town fund that builds a swing; the fund wants it by spring. A water pipe cracks and nobody knew it would, so saved money pays the repair. Words with pictures: save, saving reason, emergency (a need nobody planned for). **Ask.** "Which can wait, the boots or the pipe?" Expect: the boots a little; the pipe cannot. Review the day 86 mat: spend now, give up saving; save, give up the thing today. Pip says "Saving is only for buying something for yourself" (error E) and "Everything you save for has a day you know" (error O); the class puts Pip right.

#### 13 min. Supported work

4 minutes: each child revises My Choice Mat, one change; circulate. 4 minutes: tables of five sort their six cards into three hoops, later purchase, gift, emergency; review of sorting into hoops. Each table says "which can wait?". 5 minutes: My Saving Page (practice): draw one card in each box and circle the one that cannot wait. Hear the third six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 5): "Which cannot wait, and why?" Collected tomorrow as item 5's practice record.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 88 (1 minute). [[s08-X88]] [[s08-X88b]] Say: "These cards are made up." The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No hoops: draw three circles on paper.

**Skip.** The table sort and the heard six.

- **5 min. Read.** Tell the three reason cards: boots, a gift for a swing, a cracked pipe. All made up.
- **15 min. Sort.** Your child puts six new made-up cards in three circles.
  **Say.** "Which can wait? Which cannot?" Expect: boots or a plan can wait; the pipe cannot.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X88]] [[s08-X88b]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the three cards, then the exit. Longer: make up a fourth saving reason and say whether it can wait.

### Words and supports

Words: save, saving reason, emergency, can wait. Frame: "I save for ___ because ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 89 opens with three new reason cards in three hoops if the exit showed error E (a child says saving is only for wants) or error O (a child says every reason has a known day); then it asks what a bank does.
