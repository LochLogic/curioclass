# Section 8.3 opener: saving and banks

## What the section does

Three days, 88 to 90. Day 88: three reasons to save, a later purchase, a gift to a cause and an emergency, and which can wait. Day 89: what an invented bank does, a record of each saver's amount, a little interest, loans that are repaid. Day 90: three paper jars, spend, save and share, and what saving now gives up now.

## How the days run

Day 88: reopen (5), three reasons (10), supported work (13), exit (2). Day 89: reopen (5), the Corner Bank (12), supported work (11), exit (2). Day 90: reopen (5), three jars (8), supported work (15), exit (2).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Reasons to save:** a purchase later (the time is known), a gift to a cause (may have a deadline), an emergency (nobody knew the day, so it cannot wait).
- **A bank** keeps savers' money with a record of each saver's amount and lends money to others, who repay (sources 89A, 89B and 89C). The saver owns the savings; the bank keeps the record. Interest is a little extra for keeping savings there; it is not pay for work. A loan is repaid, so it is not free money. Lending does not make a saver's amount vanish.
- **Three jars** are optional planning categories, not a rule for any family. Sharing may be zero. Each token goes in one jar once. What is saved cannot also be spent now.
- Everything is invented. No real bank is named; deposit insurance is not taught.

## The errors, in plain words

- **E:** saving is only for treats. **O:** every saving reason has a known day. **Q:** the bank owns the savings. **F:** a bank gives free money. **R:** lending makes savings disappear. **P:** one token can sit in two jars.

## Care

Nobody is asked what their family saves or whether they have a bank. Paper tokens only. Read every card aloud.

## Supplies

Reason cards and three hoops, My Saving Page, Corner Bank cards (record, interest, loan, repayment), the credited extract, My Bank Page, three jar sheets, 12 tokens each, exit cards (88 to 90), repair cards.

## A room with nothing

Three bowls for jars, drawn record cards, buttons for tokens.
