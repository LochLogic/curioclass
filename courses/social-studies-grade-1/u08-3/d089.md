# Day 89: What a bank does

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set Ila's four cards on the board; copy a set for each table. Keep exit card 89 closed. Say: "Ila and the bank are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: collect My Saving Page. 4 minutes: day 88's exit. If many made error E or O, all 4 minutes are the repair: a bus fare for a museum trip next spring; a gift of warm hats to a coat drive; the class fish tank breaks. Put each in a hoop. **Ask.** "Which cannot wait?" Expect: the tank.

#### 12 min. Teaching first

Ila puts 6 tokens in the Corner Bank. The bank keeps them safe and writes on a record card that 6 are Ila's. It pays a little extra for keeping them, called interest. Review: pay for work is wages, from day 81; interest is not pay for work. The bank also lends money to another person, a loan, who must repay it: a loan arrow goes out, a repayment arrow comes back. Ila's amount stays on her record. Read one sentence of a government page for grown-ups: "Lots of people put money in a savings account to protect it and receive interest on their saving." Review the source question: who made it (a U.S. government office), when (December 2024), what for (to help grown-ups teach children about money). Words: bank, interest, loan. Pip says "The bank owns Ila's tokens" (error Q), "A bank gives borrowers free money" (error F) and "When the bank lends, Ila's savings disappear" (error R); the class puts Pip right.

#### 11 min. Supported work

5 minutes: tables move the cards to show what the bank does for a saver, then for a borrower. **Ask.** "Who owns the tokens in the bank?" Expect: Ila. 6 minutes: My Bank Page (practice): draw the saver, the record card and the loan with what comes back. Hear the fourth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 6): "What does the bank do for a saver?" Collected tomorrow; item 6's practice record.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 89 (1 minute). [[s08-X89]] Say: "These banks are made up." The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a record page, an arrow out and an arrow back.

**Skip.** The table cards and the heard six.

- **5 min. Read.** Tell Ila's story with ten paper tokens. All made up.
- **15 min. Bank.** You play the Corner Bank; your child is Ila. Write "Ila: 6" on a record page.
  **Say.** "Whose tokens are these? What comes back after a loan?" Expect: Ila's; the loan is repaid.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X89]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Ila's record and the loan only. Longer: Ila's record beside a second saver's.

### Words and supports

Words: bank, record, interest, loan, repay. Frame: "The bank keeps ___ safe." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 90 opens with a record card for a new saver if the exit showed error Q (a child says the bank owns the tokens), error F (free money) or error R (savings disappear); then it adds three paper jars.

### Credit

Ours. The sentence read aloud is from a U.S. government page, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, "Explore saving", changed December 12, 2024, public domain.
