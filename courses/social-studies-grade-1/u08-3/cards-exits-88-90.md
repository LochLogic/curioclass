# Exit cards, days 88 to 90

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the rotating six. Say aloud first: "These people and banks are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" at the bottom. Keep closed until the day.

## Day 88 exit card

**Pictures:** a garden gate broken by a storm; a puppet-show ticket for next month; a new shelf for spring. **Read aloud** each. **Mark:** circle the reason to save for something nobody planned. **Spoken (one ask):** "How are the two reasons different, the gate and the ticket?" or "Which of the three cannot wait?"

## Day 89 exit card

**Pictures:** card one, a bank record page with Kemal's tokens; card two, a bank handing out gifts without end; card three, a bank giving tokens to anyone and keeping no record. **Mark:** circle what a bank can do. **Spoken (one ask):** "Who owns the tokens Kemal keeps at the bank?" or "What is the record page for?"

## Day 90 exit card

**Pictures:** Pat with 10 tokens in three jars, 5 in spend, 3 in save, 2 in share; Pat is saving for a watering can; a snack costs 6 tokens today and the spend jar holds 5. **Mark:** circle the jar that holds the watering can money. **Spoken (one ask):** "Why does Pat not use the save jar for the snack today?" or "What does Pat give up today to keep saving?"
