# Day 90: Three jars

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang three jar outlines: spend, save, share. Count twenty tokens. Give each child 12 tokens and a jar sheet. Keep exit card 90 closed. Say: "These people and things are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: collect My Bank Page. 4 minutes: day 89's exit. If many made error Q, F or R, all 4 minutes are the repair: Wren has 5 tokens at the Corner Bank; the bank lends 4 to Odo, who bakes bread. Keep Wren's record card beside the loan card; draw an arrow out and an arrow back. **Ask.** "Whose 5 tokens are on the record? What comes back?" Expect: Wren's; Odo repays the 4.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Three paper jars: spend, save, share. Review: the choose-and-give-up mat from day 86, and Imo's boots from day 88. Model 20 tokens as one possible plan: 8 in spend, 8 in save, 4 in share. Review: adding within 20 from math. **Ask.** "8 and 8 and 4: how many in all?" Expect: 20, the tokens we had. Each token goes in one jar once. The save jar holds what waits, like Imo's boots, so a toy today is given up. Sharing may be zero. The jars are a way to plan; they are not a rule for any family. Pip says "The same 3 tokens are in my save jar and I can buy a toy with them" (error P); the class puts Pip right: one token, one jar.

#### 15 min. Supported work

5 minutes: each child revises My Saving Page (made on day 88) and My Two Shops Page (made on day 87), one change each; circulate. 7 minutes: each child splits 12 tokens into three jars, counting that the jars make 12, and says what the save jar is for and what waits. Hear the fifth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 7): "What does your save jar wait for?" 3 minutes: partners tell "What waits? Why?" A child may pass.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 90 (1 minute). [[s08-X90]] Say: "Pat is made up." The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No jar sheet: draw three circles and use ten scraps.

**Skip.** The revising and the heard six.

- **5 min. Show.** Split ten scraps into three circles. All made up.
- **15 min. Split.** Your child splits ten scraps their own way.
  **Say.** "What is the save circle for? What waits?" Expect: a later thing; something today waits.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X90]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the 20-token model, then the exit. Longer: split again with a different plan and say what waits.

### Words and supports

Words: spend, save, share, jar, waits. Frame: "I give up ___ when I save." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 91 opens with a new set of 8 tokens, one jar each, if the exit showed error P (a child says the spend jar holds the saved money, or a token sits in two jars); then it asks how people should choose what to buy and what to save.
