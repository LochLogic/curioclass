# The Corner Bank: Ila's record, interest, loan and repayment cards (day 89)

Say aloud: "These people, shops and things are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print. Tokens are paper pieces, each worth one dollar; no real money. No real bank is named. This rests on sources 89A, 89B and 89C, in our words.

## Ila and the bank (teaching, 12 minutes)

1. **Ila puts 6 tokens in the Corner Bank.** The bank keeps them safe and writes on a **record card** that 6 are Ila's. **Read aloud:** "The tokens are Ila's. The bank keeps a record."
2. **Interest.** The bank pays a little extra for keeping her tokens there. **Read aloud:** "That extra is called interest. Interest is not pay for work. Pay for work is wages."
3. **A loan.** The bank lends some money to another person, who must repay it. A **loan arrow** goes out and a **repayment arrow** comes back. **Read aloud:** "A loan is lent, and it is repaid."
4. **Ila's amount stays on her record the whole time.**

The credited extract of the federal page (extract-savings-page-day-89.md) is read aloud with pictures. Words: bank, save, interest, loan.

## Movable cards (supported work, 5 minutes)

Record, interest, loan and repayment cards on a strip: "What the bank does for a saver" and "for a borrower".

## Pip

Pip says: "When Ila puts tokens in the bank, the bank owns them." Right: Ila owns them; the bank keeps a record. Pip says: "A bank gives borrowers free money." Right: a loan is repaid. Pip says: "When the bank lends, Ila's savings disappear." Right: Ila's amount stays on her record. Pip is making a mistake on purpose.
