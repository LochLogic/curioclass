# Extract read aloud on day 89, with pictures

Source: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, "Explore saving" (Money as you grow, school-age children and preteens), consumerfinance.gov. The page was last modified Dec. 12, 2024, and read by Curio Class on 2026-11-20. A U.S. government page; no logos or third-party art are used.

Read aloud, one sentence at a time, each with a picture:

> "Lots of people put money in a savings account to protect it and receive interest on their saving."

> "To encourage your child to save money, it can be helpful for them to know exactly what they're saving for."

Teacher note: the second sentence is for a grown-up reader; say to children only: "A savings goal is what you are saving for." Ask: who made this page, when, and what for? Nothing else on the page is taught here.
