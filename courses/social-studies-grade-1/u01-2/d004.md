# Day 4: A rule, a duty and the reason

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the rule and purpose cards in an envelope for each pair. Choose today's six: the fourth six on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-3-5.md.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

If many missed, up to two 3-minute repairs. Error B (day 3's retrieval): show a doorway where children wait in line and one where they crowd.

**Ask.** Why is waiting a good rule at the doorway? Expect: so no one gets pushed. Error D (day 3's exit): show a sandcastle crew and a sing-along group of different ages and hats.

**Ask.** What makes each a group? Expect: they do something together. Otherwise small groups have them in the sort.

#### 7 min. A rule and its reason

Say the swim team is made up. Show the pool deck. Review: in kindergarten a rule has a reason. The rule: "Wait for the whistle before you jump in." Model a claim from the picture (kindergarten's frame): "I think the rule is to wait, because I see a swimmer in the water." The class says the frame: "The rule is ___. It helps because ___."

**Ask.** Which card says what the rule is for: "So no one jumps onto a swimmer" or "Because the coach said"? Point. Expect: the first.

#### 6 min. A duty

A rule says how we act; a duty is a job a member is expected to do for the group (review: a job in kindergarten). The equipment keeper puts the kickboards back so the team can find them. All mime it at once.

**Ask.** What does the equipment keeper's duty help the team do? Expect: find the kickboards.

#### 6 min. Rule cards on purpose cards

Review: in kindergarten rules kept us safe or were fair. Pairs set four rule cards on three purposes (stay safe, be fair, help us finish what we do together): walk on the pool deck; share the lane; count the swimmers before you go; line up when the coach calls. 15 pairs work at once; visit 5. Accept two purposes with a reason. "A grown-up says" is error B.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Read both pictures: "Circle the picture that shows a rule that helps the club finish its puzzle." (s01-X4, 1 minute.) Ask the fourth six, one each: "Why does the club keep that rule?" (about 40 seconds each, 4 minutes). The others draw a pool rule. "A grown-up says" is error B.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the rules on paper slips. No exit card: draw both pictures.

**Skip.** The repairs and the pair sort.

- **7 min. A rule and its reason.** Say the swim team is made up. The rule is "Wait for the whistle before you jump in."
  **Say.** "Which is the reason: so no one jumps onto a swimmer, or because the coach said?" Expect: the first.
- **6 min. A duty.** A duty is a job a member does for the group. Your child mimes putting kickboards in a bin.
  **Say.** "What does that duty help the team do?" Expect: find the kickboards.
- **7 min. Sort.** Read "Walk on the deck", "Share the lane", "Count the swimmers". Your child sorts each by purpose.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one rule and what it is for.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X4]] Show both pictures.
  **Say.** "Why does the club keep that rule?" Expect: so the pieces stay together and members can finish.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort three cards. Longer: a second reason.

### Words and supports

Frame as above. A pointed card is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 5 reopens with the exit: if many said a grown-up says (error B), three minutes on two cards. Then our own class rules.
