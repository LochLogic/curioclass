# Teacher key, exit cards, days 3 to 5

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-3-5.md; never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 3: members cooking together; help, friendship, learning or shared work. Choosing the matching shirts shows error D.
- Day 4: pieces on a mat; the pieces stay together so members can finish the puzzle. "Because a grown-up says" shows error B.
- Day 5: taking turns; everyone gets a turn to use the telescope. Choosing the cupboard, or giving no reason tied to joining, shows error I.
