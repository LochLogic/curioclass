# Pool deck, rule and purpose cards (days 4 and 5)

The swim team is the invented group from day 3. Say aloud: "This is made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, one word or a small picture clue only.

## The pool deck picture

**Picture:** The deck of a pool with six lanes. One swimmer in the water, a coach on the deck with a whistle, and four swimmers waiting on the edge in a line. A bin of kickboards stands against the wall; the kickboards have a label with a picture of a board.

## The case: the whistle rule

**Rule card:** Wait for the whistle before you jump in. **Reason card:** So no one jumps onto a swimmer. **Command-only card:** Because the coach said. Teach the frame "The rule is ___. It helps because ___." The class repeats it, then points to the card that says what the rule is for (error B).

## The duty

**Duty card:** The equipment keeper puts the kickboards back so the team can find them. **Picture:** one swimmer lifting a kickboard into the bin. Everyone mimes the duty and says what it helps.

## Four rule cards

Each shows one rule and a small picture.

1. Walk on the pool deck. Picture: a figure walking, with a splash crossed out.
2. Share the lane. Picture: two swimmers in one lane, one behind the other.
3. Count the swimmers before you go. Picture: a coach pointing at six dots.
4. Line up when the coach calls. Picture: four swimmers in a line by the pool edge.

## Three purpose cards: what the group is for

- **Stay safe.** Picture: a shield.
- **Be fair.** Picture: a balanced scale.
- **Help us finish what we do together.** Picture: two hands on one flag.

## Using the cards

Pairs set the four rule cards onto the three purpose cards; many fit more than one. Accept either when the pair gives a reason. Fifteen pairs work at once; visit five. A "because a grown-up says" answer joins error B; show two cards for one rule and ask which tells what the rule is for.
