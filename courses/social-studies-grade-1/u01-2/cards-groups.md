# Swim team and kite club (day 3, kept for day 4)

Two invented groups used to teach group, member and belong. Say aloud: "These are made up. The people in the pictures are pretend." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the single word on the back of each word card.

## The swim team

**Picture:** A pool with a lane rope. Six swimmers of different ages, from a young child with arm floats to a teenager, and a grown-up coach on the deck holding a stopwatch. Swimmers wear different caps. One swimmer uses a ramp and a chair lift to get in. A board of kickboards leans on the wall.

**Teacher says:** "This is a swim team. A group is people together for something. These people are together to swim and get better."

## The kite club

**Picture:** An open field with a path. Five members of different ages fly kites of different shapes: a diamond, a box, a long tube, a round one, a fish. One member sits on a blanket and holds the string. A big reel of string lies in the middle where everyone can reach it.

**Teacher says:** "This is a kite club. A member is one person in the group. Who are the members of the kite club?"

## Three word cards with motions

1. **group** (picture: a ring of small figures). Motion: draw a circle in the air with both hands.
2. **member** (picture: one figure standing out of a ring with an arrow). Motion: point a finger at yourself, then at a neighbor.
3. **belong** (picture: one figure stepping into the ring). Motion: step one foot in toward the middle.

## For the teacher

Ask: who are the members of each? Children point. If a child says "the ones in the same hat", note error D and plan the three-minute repair on day 4: two invented groups with different ages and hats doing one thing together; ask what makes each a group. Keep the three word cards on the wall; day 4 uses the swim team again.
