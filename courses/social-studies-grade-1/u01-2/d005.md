# Day 5: Our class rules and duties

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out six strips from the class's real rules and duties and the four mats at each of six tables. Seat the fifth six together: the observed table. Teacher key: key-exits-3-5.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen and repair

Read the board: "Why do groups need rules and duties?" If many missed day 4's exit (error B), 3 minutes: show two cards for one rule, "Put the lid on the paint." One says "So the paint does not dry out." One says "Because the teacher said."

**Ask.** Which card says what the rule is for? Point. Expect: the first. Otherwise a small group has it in the sort.

#### 6 min. Our own rules chart

Say: "This one is real. It is ours." Read the six strips from the class's dated chart, each with a picture. Examples: walk in the hall; use quiet voices at the tables; push in your chair; line leader holds the door; share the markers; put the lids on the markers. Use none that records one child. With fewer than six, add from the first weeks. The class echoes each.

**Ask.** Point to one rule or duty on our chart. Expect: any strip.

#### 10 min. Sort by purpose

Review: day 4's purposes. Each table of five sets the strips on the mats. Each child places one strip and says the frame: "The rule is ___. It helps because ___." Five children at about 30 seconds is 2.5 minutes a table. Stay at the observed table all through. The sixth strip is placed together. A strip may fit two mats with a reason; "not sure" is fine.

#### 5 min. Report and answer

**Ask.** What do we need to find out to answer "Why do groups need rules and duties?" Expect: what each rule is for; the words rule and duty, from day 2.

Each table tapes one strip on the class chart under its purpose (6 tables, about 40 seconds each). Then write the answer: "Groups need rules and duties so ___."

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Read both pictures: "A telescope club wants everyone to join. Circle the rule that helps." (s01-X5, 1 minute.) Ask the fifth six, one each: "Why does that rule help everyone join?" (about 40 seconds each, 4 minutes). The others draw a class rule and what it is for. The cupboard, or "it is a rule," is error I.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: write rules on paper slips. No mats: use four bowls. No exit card: draw both pictures.

**Skip.** The repair, the table sort and the class chart.

- **6 min. Made-up rules.** Write three rules for a pretend toy corner on slips.
  **Say.** "What is this one for?" Expect: safe, fair, or finishing together; any reason tied to the rule.
- **7 min. Sort.** Your child puts each slip on stay safe, be fair, or help us finish.
- **6 min. The answer.** Say "Groups need rules and duties so ___."
- **6 min. Draw.** Your child draws a rule and what it is for.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X5]] [[s01-X5b]] Show both pictures.
  **Say.** "Why does that rule help everyone join?" Expect: everyone gets a turn.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four strips a table. Longer: add a duty.

### Words and supports

Frame: "The rule is ___. It helps because ___." A pointed strip is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 6 reopens with the exit: if many chose the cupboard (error I), two minutes choosing between two rules by which helps the group. Then roles.
