# Exit cards, days 3 to 5

Each exit has a picture card that every child marks (circle or point) and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six (the third six on day 3, the fourth on day 4, the fifth on day 5). Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. None of these pictures is reused from the group, family, sort, pool or class strip cards.

## Day 3 exit card: a cooking club

**Picture:** Two ringed pictures side by side. On the left, five people of different ages at a kitchen counter, stirring, chopping and tasting together. On the right, five people in matching shirts standing in a row, looking in different directions, doing nothing together.

**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows a group. **Spoken:** "What could members get from belonging to the cooking club?"

## Day 4 exit card: a puzzle club

**Picture:** Two ringed pictures side by side. On the left, a table where a puzzle is half done and its box of pieces sits on a mat beside it. On the right, a grown-up standing and pointing a finger at a table where pieces are scattered on the floor.

**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows a rule that helps the club finish its puzzle. **Spoken:** "Why does the club keep that rule?"

## Day 5 exit card: a telescope club

**Picture:** Two ringed pictures side by side. On the left, five members in a line, one looking through a telescope while the next waits with a hand up. On the right, a tall cupboard with a telescope on its top shelf, shut, and five members looking up at it.

**Mark:** Circle the rule that helps everyone join. **Spoken:** "Why does that rule help everyone join?"
