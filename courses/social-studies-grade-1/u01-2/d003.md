# Day 3: What belonging gives

## Teacher page

### Before class

Envelope the eight belonging cards and three mats for each pair. Choose today's six: the third six on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-3-5.md.

### The day

#### 7 min. Return and repair, and retrieval

Review: in kindergarten you learned that a rule has a reason.

**Ask.** A club puts the scissors in a tray after use. Why? (s01-R2) Expect: so they stay safe or can be found. Hear four children. "Because the club said" joins error B on day 4.

If many missed day 2's exit (error F), 4 minutes: show a door before card and a door after card; which picture shows what the door was like before? That is history. Otherwise a small group has it in the sort.

#### 7 min. Group, member, belong

Say the swim team and kite club are made up. A group is people together for something; a member is one person in it; to belong is to be a member. Motions: a circle in the air; point to yourself, then a neighbor; step one foot in. Twice each.

**Ask.** Who are the members of the kite club? Point. Expect: the five who fly kites; they belong because they fly together.

#### 5 min. Families are groups too

Say before the first card and again at the end: "Families come in many kinds. These are made up. No one needs to tell about their own." Show the three mats, then the five family cards in order. For the first two, say the answer (help, shared work).

**Ask.** What does belonging give here: help, friendship or shared work? Point to a mat. Expect: any answer tied to the picture. Say foster and adoptive plainly, once. If a child tells about their own family, say "That is a family too" and go on.

#### 6 min. What belonging gives

Pairs sort the eight cards onto the mats. 15 pairs work at once; visit 5. Accept two mats for a card when the pair says why. Sorting by clothes or looks is error D: ask what the people do together.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Read both pictures: "Circle the picture that shows a group." (s01-X3, 1 minute.) Ask the third six, one each: "What could members get from belonging to the cooking club?" (about 40 seconds each, 4 minutes). The others draw a club they would like to belong to on the student page. "The shirts" is error D.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a swim team and a kite club. No mats: use three bowls. No exit card: draw two pictures.

**Skip.** The retrieval, the repair and the pair sort.

- **6 min. Group, member, belong.** Say a group is people together for something. Show the three motions.
  **Say.** "Who belongs to the kite club?" Expect: the people flying kites.
- **8 min. What belonging gives.** Say members can get help, friendship or shared work.
  **Say.** "A band member lends a drumstick. What does belonging give?" Expect: help.
- **6 min. Families.** Say families come in many kinds and these are made up. Draw a grandparent reading with a child; your child names what belonging gives.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a club they would like to join.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3]] Show both pictures.
  **Say.** "What could members get from belonging to the cooking club?" Expect: help, friendship or shared work.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort six cards. Longer: sort a second way.

### Words and supports

Frame: "Members get ___." A pointed card is an answer.

### Tomorrow
