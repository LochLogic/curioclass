# Belonging sort cards (day 3)

Eight picture cards, three mats. Pairs sort the cards onto the mats: help, friendship, shared work. Fifteen pairs work at once; the teacher visits five pairs. Say aloud: "These are made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, one picture on each card, no words.

## The three mats

- **Help.** Picture: one hand lifting another.
- **Friendship.** Picture: two figures sitting side by side laughing.
- **Shared work.** Picture: two figures carrying one basket.

## The eight cards

1. Two choir members share one song sheet. Fits: shared work, or help.
2. A band member lends a spare drumstick. Fits: help.
3. Two team members cheer for a teammate. Fits: friendship.
4. A soccer team passes the ball down the field. Fits: shared work.
5. A walking group waits for the slowest walker. Fits: help, or friendship.
6. Two puppet-show members paint one banner. Fits: shared work.
7. A neighbor group shares a bench and a story. Fits: friendship.
8. A rowing crew lifts one boat together. Fits: shared work.

## Using the cards

Many cards fit more than one mat; accept a mat when the pair gives a reason ("they help each other"). A pair that sorts by clothing or looks has made error D; ask what the people are doing together. Pairs may pass a card to the "not sure" corner.
