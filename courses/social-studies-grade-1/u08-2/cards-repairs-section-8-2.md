# Repair cards, section 8.2

Each repair puts a wrong idea beside a right one. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip or shown on a teacher card, never a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most three minutes. Nobody is asked to say, stand or sing. No card reuses an exit.

## C: not enough tokens (opening of day 86, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "I want both, so I can buy both." **Right:** "Count the prices against the tokens held; if the prices together are more, buy one." **Retry:** Sami has 9 tokens; a jump rope card 6 and a toy boat card 5. Put tokens on both cards, count against 9, then place tokens on the one Sami can pay for.

## M: price is not given up (opening of day 87, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "What I give up is the price." **Right:** "The price is the tokens; the given-up thing is the next best." **Retry:** a new ranked set: Reya has 5 tokens and ranks a kazoo first (price 5), a magnet second (4), a badge third (3); no two together are 5 or fewer. Put the price on the kazoo card and a ring on the thing given up.

## N: only the second best is given up (same 3 minutes as M)

**Pip:** "I give up everything I did not pick." **Right:** "Only the next best, the second, is given up." **Retry:** the Reya cards again, point to the one given up, not both.

## D: both buyers are fine (opening of day 88, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "A buyer of the other shop is bad." **Right:** "Each buyer cares about something different; both choices are fine." **Retry:** a new pair of cards. Blue Shop: "We give workers a morning to rest after long days." Green Shop: "We collect rainwater for our garden." Same price. Say "a buyer might choose Blue because ...; another Green because ...".
