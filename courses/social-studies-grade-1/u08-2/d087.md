# Day 87: Why a buyer might choose

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the shop cards. Keep exit card 87 closed. Say: "These shops, cards and prices are made up, and each card was made by its own shop."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: collect My Choice Mat. 4 minutes: day 86's exit. If many made error M or N, all 4 minutes are the repair: Reya ranks a kazoo first, priced 5 tokens, a magnet second and a badge third. Put 5 tokens on the kazoo card and a ring on the one given up. **Ask.** "What is the price? What is given up?" Expect: 5 tokens; the magnet, the second.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Shop A's card says its workers keep a steady schedule and take turns choosing days off; its book costs 9. Shop B's card says it recycles its paper bags and takes back old books for new readers; its book costs 8. Review: who made each card (the shop), when (now), what for (to get buyers). A shop's card is what the shop says about itself; it cannot prove what a real shop does. Model: "A buyer might choose A because workers get turns choosing days off; another might choose B because it costs 8." Both are fine, and nobody has to choose either. Model: "I think a buyer might pick B, because the card says it costs 8." Pip says "A person who buys from the other shop is bad" (error D); the class puts Pip right: each buyer cares about something different.

#### 13 min. Supported work

Pairs, Shop C's card (a safe, clean break room for workers; the book costs 9) and Shop D's (turns old cloth into new bags; 9). 4 minutes: each pair says "A buyer might choose C because ___; another might choose D because ___." 6 minutes: each child draws two buyers and their reasons on My Two Shops Page, practice. Hear the second six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 6). 3 minutes: **Ask.** "What does a shop's card tell? What can it not tell?" Expect: what the shop says and its price; whether it is true of a real shop.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 87 (1 minute). [[s08-X87]] Say: "These stalls and cards are made up." The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write two shop sentences on paper.

**Skip.** The pairs and the heard six.

- **5 min. Read.** Read two made-up shop cards. Each costs the same; each says one thing.
- **15 min. Buyers.** You play a second buyer.
  **Say.** "Why might one buyer pick this shop? Why might I pick the other?" Expect: a reason from each card; both fine.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X87]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Shops A and B, then the exit. Longer: a third shop, with its own reason.

### Words and supports

Words: shop, card, price, might choose. Frame: "A buyer might choose ___ because ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 88 opens with a new pair of shop cards if the exit showed error D (a child says a buyer of the other stall is not nice); then it asks why people save.
