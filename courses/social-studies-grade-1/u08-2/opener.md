# Section 8.2 opener: choosing with limited money

## What the section does

Three days, 85 to 87, of 30 minutes. Day 85: with limited tokens a person cannot buy everything, so a choice has a benefit and a cost. Day 86: what is given up is the next best thing not chosen, not the price and not everything left. Day 87: two invented shops, what each says about itself, and why different buyers might choose differently.

## How the days run

Day 85: reopen (5), Ama and the tokens (10), supported work (13), exit (2). Day 86: reopen (5), Kofi and the mat (10), supported work (13), exit (2). Day 87: reopen (5), the bookshops (10), supported work (13), exit (2).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Benefit** is what a choice gives; **cost** is the tokens it uses, and the other thing you can then no longer buy. "Not enough" is a fact about paper tokens in the room, never about anyone's family.
- **What is given up** (economists say opportunity cost, said once): rank the choices first, second, third. You pick the first and what you give up is the second only. Not the price, and not everything left.
- **Voluntary choice.** Nobody makes a buyer pick a shop. Buyers weigh what a shop does, its price and what they care about. No shop is the good one; the teacher stays neutral.
- **A shop card is a source.** The shop made it, for now, to get buyers. A card cannot prove what a real shop does. Every shop here is invented.
- Maths is review only: add and compare within 20.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** wants have no limit, so both can be bought. Count the price tokens against the tokens held.
- **M:** what is given up is the price. Put the price on one card and the given-up thing on another.
- **N:** you give up everything not chosen. Only the second best is given up.
- **D:** a buyer of the other shop is bad. Each buyer cares about something different; both are fine.

## Care

No child is asked what their home buys. Every child gets the same tokens. Read every card aloud; accept a point, a token, a drawing or dictation.

## Supplies

Paper tokens (9 each), price cards, two-box cards, ranked cards and the mat, shop cards, My Choice Mat, My Two Shops Page, exit cards (85 to 87), repair cards.

## A room with nothing

Buttons for tokens, ranked pictures on scrap paper, shop cards on index cards.
