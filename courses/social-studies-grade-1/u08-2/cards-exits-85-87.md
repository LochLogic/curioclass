# Exit cards, days 85 to 87

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the rotating six. Say aloud first: "These people and shops are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Keep closed until the day. No card reuses a teaching card or a repair card.

## Day 85 exit card

**Pictures:** Neri with 5 tokens; a show card, 3 tokens; a puzzle card, 4 tokens; a happy Neri with the puzzle. **Read aloud:** "Neri has 5 tokens. The show costs 3. The puzzle costs 4." **Mark:** circle yes or no, can Neri pay for both. **Spoken (one ask, chosen by the record sheet), with a new case, Nia with 6 tokens, a book at 2 and a game at 5:** "Can Nia pay for both? Why? Count the tokens." or "What does Nia get and what does it cost if Nia picks the book?"

## Day 86 exit card

**Pictures:** Dev ranks chalk first, a spinning top second and a marble third; Dev buys only the chalk. **Read aloud:** "Dev ranks the chalk first, the top second and the marble third. Dev buys the chalk." **Mark:** circle the next best thing given up. **Spoken (one ask):** "Why is that the one given up?" 

## Day 87 exit card

**Pictures:** two plum stalls, each with a card it made itself. Stall A: "Our price is 6 dollars. We plant trees." Stall B: "Our price is 4 dollars." A buyer who likes trees and a buyer who wants the lowest price stand between the stalls. **Read aloud** both cards and say the stalls and cards are made up. **Mark:** circle the buyer who might still pick Stall A. **Spoken (one ask):** "Why might another buyer pick Stall B?" or "Does Stall A's card prove it plants trees?"
