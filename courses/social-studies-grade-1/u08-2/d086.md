# Day 86: What you give up

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the mat, a strip with three places: first, second, third. Give each child 7 tokens and a blank My Choice Mat. Return each My Flyer Page with a picture note. Keep exit card 86 closed. Say: "These people and things are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: return My Flyer Page, made on day 84. 2 minutes: day 85's exit. If many made error C, repair it: Sami has 9 tokens; a jump rope costs 6, a toy boat 5. Put tokens on both cards, count against 9, then on the one Sami can pay for; the recall below then takes 1 minute. 2 minutes: post "What does choosing give and give up?" **Ask.** "Yesterday Ama chose the train. What did it give her, and what did it cost?" Expect: playing with it; 5 tokens.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Kofi has 6 tokens and ranks three things on the mat: a yo-yo first, 6 tokens; a paper fan second, 5; a bean bag third, 4 (no two fit in 6 tokens). He pays 6 for the yo-yo and has none left. What he **gives up** is the next best thing he did not choose: the fan. Not the bean bag, and not the 6 tokens; that is the price. Say once: grown-ups call this opportunity cost. **Ask.** "What does Kofi give up?" Expect: the fan, the second. Pip says "What I give up is the 6 tokens" (error M) and "I give up the fan and the bean bag" (error N); the class puts Pip right on the mat.

#### 13 min. Supported work

5 minutes: revise My Flyer Page, one change or addition each, tags joined to the right offers (error L); circulate. 8 minutes: Ines has 7 tokens; a bell costs 5, a button card 4, a sticker sheet 4. Each child ranks the three cards on My Choice Mat, chooses the first (Ines can pay for only one), and names the one given up, the second. Say "I choose ___ and give up ___." Hear the first six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 8): "What do you give up?" Practice and first try; collected tomorrow as item 3's record.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 86 (1 minute). [[s08-X86]] The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No mat: draw three boxes in a row and number them.

**Skip.** The tables and the heard six.

- **5 min. Rank.** Draw three made-up things; your child numbers them first, second, third.
- **15 min. Give up.** Say your child has tokens for only one.
  **Say.** "Which do you choose? What do you give up?" Expect: the first; the second, the next best.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X86]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Kofi only, then the exit. Longer: choose the second and name what is given up.

### Words and supports

Words: rank, first, second, third, give up. Frames: "I choose ___." "I give up ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 87 opens with a new ranked set if the exit showed error M (the child names the tokens) or error N (the child names more than one thing); then it compares two made-up shops.
