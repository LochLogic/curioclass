# Ama and Lena: price cards and the two-box card (day 85)

Say aloud: "These people, shops and things are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print. Tokens are paper pieces, each worth one dollar; no real money.

## Ama (teaching, 10 minutes)

**Cards:** Ama with 8 paper tokens; a toy train card, 5 tokens; a whistle card, 6 tokens. **Read aloud:** "Ama has 8 tokens. The train costs 5. The whistle costs 6. 5 and 6 make 11, which is more than 8, so Ama must choose." Ama picks the train: the benefit is playing with the train; the cost is 5 tokens, and 3 are left, not enough for the whistle. Words with pictures: benefit, cost, not enough, choose. **Model:** "I choose the train because the card shows Ama liking it."

## Lena (supported work, 5 minutes)

**Cards:** Lena, a puppet card 6 and a bird feeder card 5; each child holds 9 tokens. Put tokens on both cards to see that 11 is more than 9. Then each child buys one card from a set and fills the **two-box card**: "What I get" and "What it cost".

## Pilar returns (reminder only)

Pilar from day 81: 9 tokens, a drum 7, a clock 6, not enough for both. Never an exit.

## Pip

Pip says: "I want the puppet and the bird feeder, so I can buy both." Right: 6 and 5 make 11; 11 is more than 9 tokens, so only one. Pip is making a mistake on purpose.
