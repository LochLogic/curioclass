# Ranked card sets (day 86)

Say aloud: "These people, shops and things are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print. Tokens are paper pieces, each worth one dollar; no real money.

## Kofi (teaching, 10 minutes)

**Cards on the mat:** Kofi has 6 tokens. First: a yo-yo, 6 tokens. Second: a paper fan, 3 tokens. Third: a bean bag, 2 tokens. **Read aloud:** "Kofi ranks the yo-yo first, the fan second and the bean bag third. He can pay for the yo-yo, 6 tokens, and has none left. What Kofi gives up is the fan, the next best thing." Say once: grown-ups call this opportunity cost. Not the bean bag, and not the 6 tokens; 6 is the price.

## Ines (supported work, 8 minutes)

**Cards:** Ines has 7 tokens. A bell, a button card and a sticker sheet, with a price on each (bell 5, button card 4, sticker sheet 4); no two together are 7 tokens or fewer. Each child ranks the three cards on **My Choice Mat**, chooses one Ines can pay for, and names what is given up.

## Pip

Pip says: "What I give up is the 6 tokens." Right: 6 is the price; the fan is what is given up. Pip says: "I give up the fan and the bean bag." Right: only the second best is given up. Pip is making a mistake on purpose.
