# Day 85: Not enough for both

## Teacher page

### Before class

Give each child 9 tokens. Lay out Ama's cards and each table's price cards: a flute 8, a lantern 7, a yarn ball 4, a pinecone wreath 3. Keep exit card 85 closed. Say: "These people and things are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: collect My Flyer Page, made on day 84. 2 minutes: review from kindergarten, needs and wants. **Ask.** "A made-up traveler needs drinking water and wants a decoration. Which keeps the body well? Can limited money buy everything?" Expect: water; no. Recall Pilar's 9 tokens, a drum 7, a clock 6: not enough for both. 2 minutes: if many made error K or L on day 84's exit, the 2 minutes are the repair and retrieval takes 1: two new tags and pictures to match, a large spoon 3 and a small bell 6; follow each line from tag to picture.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Ama has 8 tokens. A toy train costs 5; a whistle costs 6. Count 5 and 6: 11, more than 8, so Ama must choose. Ama picks the train. Review from unit 5: **benefit**, what a choice gives you; here, playing with the train. New: **cost**, the tokens it uses, and what you then cannot buy: 5 tokens, and 3 are left, not enough for the whistle. Review from kindergarten: choose and not enough. Model: "I choose the train because the card shows Ama liking it." Pip says "I want the puppet and the bird feeder, so I can buy both" (error C). Put 6 and 5 tokens on those two cards and count against 9: 11, more than 9, so only one.

#### 13 min. Supported work

Tables of five, each child with 9 tokens. 5 minutes: Lena's cards, a puppet 6 and a bird feeder 5; each child puts tokens on both and says "11 is more than 9." 5 minutes: each child picks one price card, puts tokens on it and fills a two-box card: what I get, drawn; what it cost, in tokens. Say "I choose ___ because ___." Hear the fifth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes) while the others finish: "What did you get, and what did it cost?"

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 85 (1 minute). [[s08-X85]] [[s08-X85b]] The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: use 9 buttons or coins of paper.

**Skip.** The tables and the heard six.

- **5 min. Count.** Give your child 9 paper tokens and two made-up cards, a ball 7 and a drum 6.
- **15 min. Choose.** Your child puts tokens on both cards, then buys one.
  **Say.** "Can you buy both? What do you get, and what does it cost?" Expect: no, 13 is more than 9; the one picked, and its tokens.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X85]] [[s08-X85b]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Ama and one two-box card. Longer: a third card tried.

### Words and supports

Words: choose, benefit, cost, not enough. Frame: "I choose ___ because ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 86 opens with new cards and tokens if the exit showed error C (a child circles yes, or puts tokens on both); then it asks what is given up.
