# Day 37: Citizens choose again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Say: "This town and this pond-watch group are made up." The name card is text only: no pictures of people, no role play. Ask nothing about home or status.

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

If many missed error K on day 36's exit, count who shares the work on a town card: mayor, council, clerk, road crew. If error L, trace coins from many hands to the garden path. If error S, re-sort a pet-grooming shop and a snow-clearing crew.

#### 6 min. Citizens

Review: you learned citizen in unit 3, belong in unit 1. Show the belonging picture: everyone who lives here belongs and has rights, such as learning and being heard. A citizen is a member of a town, city or nation under its rules. Citizens old enough, following voting rules, choose leaders. Children cannot vote in government elections yet; neighbors who cannot vote still belong. Say it once (error E, error N).

#### 6 min. Terms

Review: you learned the calendar in unit 2. Lay three cards on the strip for pond-watch captain Wes: election day, end of term, voters choose again. A term is a set time a leader serves. When it ends, voters choose again, and may pick Wes or someone new; to choose Wes again is to re-elect. **Ask.** "What happens when a term ends?" Expect: voters choose again.

#### 7 min. Nations choose today

Review: you met the Cherokee Nation in unit 3. Say: "This is our own telling of what the Navajo Nation's pages say." Read: "The Navajo Nation has its own government today. The people of the Navajo Nation elect a President, a Vice President and delegates to the Navajo Nation Council. The delegates are elected from their districts. Other nations have their own governments, chosen in their own ways." Hear one thing from three. If a child says nations are only long ago, say once: "The Navajo Nation chooses its leaders today" (error G, error P).

#### 2 min. Tomorrow

Tomorrow we read a made-up leader's record and decide whether to choose again.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 37. [[s04-X37]] Read aloud: "Circle what happens next." The first six come to you: "Who decides whether the mayor keeps the job?" Look for: voters decide, in a new election, thinking about the work (error O). The rest draw.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: fold paper in three.

**Skip.** The nation and the six.

- **5 min. Belonging.** Say: everyone who lives in a town belongs. Voting has rules, such as age.
- **15 min. A term.** Fold paper in three and draw a made-up captain's election day, end of term, and voters choose again.
  **Say.** "What happens when the term ends?" Expect: voters choose again; they may pick the same leader or a new one.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X37]] Show the card. Your child circles what happens next.
  **Say.** "Who decides?" Expect: the voters, in a new election.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: terms and exit. Longer: compare two terms.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: citizen, term, re-elect. Frame: "Voters choose again because the term ends." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 judges a leader's record. Its warm-up repairs this exit: error O (a liked leader stays), mark the end of a term on the calendar.

Ours, all of it.
