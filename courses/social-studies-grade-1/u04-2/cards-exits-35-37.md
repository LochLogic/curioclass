# Exit cards, days 35 to 37

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day.

## Day 35 exit card: the icy roads

**Picture:** A story card with a snowy road and a person in a coat holding a paper, and the line the teacher reads: the governor asked everyone to stay off the icy roads. Three choice cards, each with a circle: a city street; the outline of a state; a globe.

**Mark:** Circle the place the governor is in charge of. **Spoken:** "A made-up town has a mayor and a librarian. Which one do voters choose? Say why."

## Day 36 exit card: the town pool

**Picture:** A made-up town card with a pool, a rules sign and a worker with a brush. Three choice cards, each with a circle: many coins from many people going into one jar; a pool with a coin purse of its own; one big coin held by the mayor.

**Mark:** Circle how the workers are paid. **Spoken:** "Tell one thing the town government does for the pool."

## Day 37 exit card: the mayor's term

**Picture:** A made-up city hall, a mayor waving, a calendar strip with an end-of-term card, and a crowd. Two choice cards, each with a circle: voters at a ballot box choosing again; a smiling mayor in the chair with a crowd holding a banner that says we like you, and no ballot box.

**Mark:** Circle what happens next. **Spoken:** "Who decides whether the mayor keeps the job?"
