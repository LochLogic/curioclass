# Repair cards, section 4.2

New cards for the warm-up parts of days 36 to 38, used only if many children missed that letter on the day before. Say first: "These cards are made up." Wrong answers are voiced by a puppet or "someone might say", never by a named child. None of these cases appears in an exit or end item.

## M and D: day 36 warm-up (4 minutes)

**M, every leader governs the same place.** Match three new office cards to three new place cards: a school-club captain, a mayor, a governor to a club, a city, a state. Say: "The governor leads the state; the President leads the country."
**D, every helper is elected.** Sort four new helper cards, voted or hired, for a made-up town: the mayor (voted), the crossing helper (hired), the garden-path worker (hired), the club president (voted by the club).

## K, L and S: day 37 warm-up (4 minutes)

**K, one official is the whole government.** Show a made-up town card with a mayor, a council, a clerk and a road crew. Count the helpers who share the work.
**L, government goods and services cost nothing.** Trace coins from many hands into a shared jar, then to the town garden path and its workers.
**S, any activity is a government service.** Re-sort two cards inside the K or L repair: a pet-grooming shop (not government) and a snow-clearing crew paid by the town (government).

## E, N, O, G and P: day 38 warm-up (3 minutes)

**E and N, voting and belonging.** Show the belonging picture from day 37 once and say: "Everyone who lives in a community belongs. Voting has rules, and people who cannot vote still belong." Ask no one about their own status.
**O, a leader keeps office forever.** Mark the end of a term on the class calendar; then the next card: voters choose again.
**G and P, nations today.** Re-read the nation card in the present tense: "The Navajo Nation chooses its leaders today. Other nations have their own governments." Say it once.
