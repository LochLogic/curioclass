# Section 4.2 opener: elected leaders, government and citizens

## What the section does

Three days, 35 to 37, of 30 minutes. Day 35 teaches four elected offices, each with its place and job: team captain, mayor, governor, President. Day 36 teaches what a government is and does, with a good and a service, paid for together through taxes. Day 37 teaches citizens, belonging and voting, terms and re-election, and that the Navajo Nation chooses its leaders today.

## How the days run

Day 35: warm-up and repair (4), four places (4), what each does (7), not every helper is voted for (5), match (5), exit (5). Day 36: warm-up and repair (4), teaching (8), paid for together (6), supported work (7), exit (5). Day 37: warm-up and repair (4), citizens (6), terms (6), nations choose today (7), tomorrow (2), exit (5). The teacher hears the spoken part from one rotating six.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Offices, not people.** No current office holder is named, praised or criticized. Name the office and its job. The governor is our own state's, with Colorado and Washington as examples; check the office on the state's directory page.
- **The President.** Leads the country and sees that its laws are carried out, for a term of four years. Do not teach how the vote is counted.
- **Not all leaders are elected.** In many cities voters choose the mayor; in some, the council chooses. A hired helper such as a bus driver works for the town too.
- **Government is shared work.** Leaders and workers make rules, carry them out and provide what everyone uses: a good (a thing) and a service (work done).
- **Taxes.** Money many people pay together for what everyone uses. No sums, no amounts, no questions about families.
- **Belonging is wider than voting.** Everyone who lives in a community belongs and has rights. Voting has rules, such as age. Say it once, and never ask any child's or family's status.
- **A term ends.** Voters choose again and may pick the same leader or a new one.
- **One nation, not all.** The Navajo Nation is named by its own name, in the present tense, with the offices its pages name. It is not every nation's way.

## The errors, in plain words

- **M, D:** every leader governs the same place; every helper is elected. Match offices to places; sort voted or hired.
- **K, L, S:** one official is the government; it costs nothing; any activity is government. Count the helpers; trace the coins; re-sort.
- **E, N:** every resident votes; only voters belong. Show belonging once.
- **O:** a leader stays forever. Mark the end of a term on the calendar.
- **G, P:** nations only long ago; all nations the same.

## Care

No child reports on home, family, money or status. The nation is shown by a name card only: no pictures of people, no role play. A child may pass. The sensitivity read of day 37 comes first.

## Supplies for the section

Place, office, job and helper cards; the town card, sort cards and jar; the term strip and belonging picture; the nation summary and name card; the printed exit cards; the repair cards.

## A room with nothing

Hand-drawn cards, a jar or box, the calendar on a sheet of paper.
