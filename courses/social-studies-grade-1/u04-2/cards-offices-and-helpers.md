# Day 35 cards: four offices and the helpers

Say first: "The teams, towns and helpers on these cards are made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. One person acts on each card. Print "made up" in small letters on each card. The teacher reads every card aloud. Offices only: no card names or shows a real office holder.

## Place cards (four)

A team of children with a ball; a city street with tall buildings; the outline of a state, with a line to write or draw our own state's name; a country shown as a globe. Two example states, Colorado and Washington, may be shown as outlines beside our own.

## Office cards (four)

Team captain, mayor, governor, President. Each is chosen by voters in an election, as the garden captain was. A note for the teacher: in many cities voters choose the mayor directly; in some, the city council chooses the mayor. Say "many" and never "all".

## Job pictures (four, read aloud)

- The team captain organizes the team's play.
- The mayor leads the work of a city.
- The governor leads the work of the state and sees that the state's laws are carried out.
- The President leads the country and sees that the country's laws are carried out, and serves a set term of four years.

The teacher says the last two in their own words. The four-year term and the duty to see that laws are carried out are from Article II, sections 1 and 3, of the Constitution. Our governor's title and state outline come from our state's directory page; the examples come from the directory pages for Colorado and Washington, offices only.

## Helper cards (voted or hired, four)

In this made-up town, voters choose the mayor and the team captain of the town soccer team; the town hires the bus driver and the park worker.
1. The mayor (voted).
2. The soccer team captain (voted by the team).
3. The bus driver (hired).
4. The park worker (hired).

Both the voted mayor and the hired bus driver work for the town. Sort the four cards, voted or hired.

## Pair match sets (fifteen envelopes)

Each envelope holds four office cards, four place cards and four job pictures. Pairs match each office to its place and its job; the class checks one match at a time by pointing.

## For the teacher
