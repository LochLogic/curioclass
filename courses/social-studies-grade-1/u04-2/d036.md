# Day 36: What a government does

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep exit card 36 closed. Say: "This town is made up." Never ask what a family pays.

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

If many missed error M on day 35's exit, match a club captain, a mayor and a governor to a club, a city and a state; two minutes. If error D, sort four helper cards, voted or hired: mayor, crossing helper, path worker, club president; two minutes.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review: you learned community in unit 1 and mayor on day 35. Show the town card and read it: the government makes rules, such as the park closes at dusk; town workers carry them out; the government provides a school, a mended road, a park bench and a fire station. Government is many leaders and workers sharing the tasks, not one person. A bench is a good, a thing. Mending the road is a service, work done. Words: government, good, service. **Ask.** "Is the mayor the whole government? Who else works for the town?" Expect: no; workers and other leaders share the tasks.

#### 6 min. Paid for together

Show the jar. Many grown-ups put coins in together; this is called taxes. The government uses it for what everyone uses. Children trace a coin from the jar to each thing on the town card. **Ask.** "Why can everyone use the bench?" Expect: many people paid together. No sums; no child says what any family pays.

#### 7 min. Supported work

Pairs sort six cards, provided by the government or not: a park bench, road mending, a fire station; a toy shop, a bakery, a neighbor's swing. Then name each provided card a good or a service: the bench is a good; road mending and the firefighters' work are services. Three minutes (error S). Then each child draws one thing everyone in a town uses, with a label word. Four minutes.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 36. [[s04-X36]] Read aloud: "Circle how the workers are paid." The fifth six come to you, half a minute each: "Tell one thing the town government does for the pool." Look for: many people paying together, called taxes; a job such as making rules or paying workers (error K, error L).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No jar picture: use a real jar and buttons.

**Skip.** The sort and the six.

- **5 min. The town.** Tell a made-up town: the government makes a rule, mends a road and keeps a bench in the park.
- **15 min. The jar.** Your child drops a button in a jar for each thing, then names a good or a service.
  **Say.** "Is the bench a good or a service? Who pays?" Expect: a good; many people pay together, called taxes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X36]] Show the card. Your child circles how the workers are paid.
  **Say.** "Tell one thing a town government does." Expect: makes rules, or pays workers.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the jar and the exit. Longer: another service.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: government, good, service, taxes. Frame: "The government ___." Pointing counts. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 teaches citizens and terms. Its warm-up repairs this exit: error K (the mayor is the whole government), count the helpers on a town card who share the work; error L (nobody pays), trace coins from many hands to the park.
