# Day 35: Who leads where

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep exit card 35 closed. Key: key-cards-offices-and-helpers.md. Write our state's name on the state card. Name offices only, never a person who holds one. Say: "The teams, towns and helpers on these cards are made up."

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

If many missed error B on day 34's exit, re-run a covered box with six ballots, two minutes. If error J, two puppets say "I disagree, and I accept the count" and "I may ask the counters to check," two minutes.

#### 4 min. Four places

Review: you learned election and ballot in days 33 and 34. Show four places: a team, a city, our state, a country. Who is in charge of each? Name the offices: team captain, mayor, governor, President. Voters choose each in an election, as for the garden captain; many cities elect their mayor.

#### 7 min. What each does

Say each job in your words: the captain organizes the team's play; the mayor leads the work of a city; the governor leads the state's work and sees that state laws are carried out; the President leads the country's work and sees that national laws are carried out, for four years at a time. Children say each back: "The ___ leads the ___." Start from the garden captain's job card: it says what the leader does; the rules still hold for the leader.

#### 5 min. Not every helper is voted for

Voters choose the mayor and the soccer team captain; the town hires the bus driver and the park worker. Hired means chosen for a job, not by an election. All four work for the town. Pairs sort four helper cards, voted or hired.

#### 5 min. Match

Fifteen pairs match four office cards to a place and a job picture. The class checks one match at a time by pointing (error M).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 35. [[s04-X35]] [[s04-X35b]] [[s04-X35c]] [[s04-X35b]] Read aloud: "Circle the place the governor is in charge of." The fourth six come to you, half a minute each: "A made-up town has a mayor and a librarian. Which one do voters choose? Say why." Look for: the state outline; the mayor, and the librarian is hired (error M, error D). The rest draw a leader's job.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw four boxes on a page.

**Skip.** The sort and the six.

- **5 min. Four places.** Draw a team, a city, your state and a country. Ask who leads each: team captain, mayor, governor, President.
- **15 min. Jobs.** Say each job. Your child points to the place it leads. Then ask about a made-up bus driver, who is hired.
  **Say.** "Who leads the state? Is the bus driver voted for?" Expect: the governor; no, the town hires the driver.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X35]] [[s04-X35b]] [[s04-X35c]] [[s04-X35b]] Show the card. Your child circles a place.
  **Say.** "Which helper do voters choose, and why?" Expect: the mayor; a helper is hired for the job.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three offices and the exit. Longer: compare two jobs.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: mayor, governor, President, hired. Frame: "The ___ leads the ___." Pointing counts. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow
