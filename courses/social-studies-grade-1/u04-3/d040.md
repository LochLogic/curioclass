# Day 40: Show what you know about choosing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet closed until now. Cover the models; leave the blank frames. Open the record sheet's heard column and number five groups of six, from children not yet heard on a spoken part. Check the day 34 tally first: if it was seven and four, use eight and five here, and change the marks and the key. Day 39's exit and revisions are done; nothing from it returns today. Never ask how anyone voted.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions and readings

Say: "Today I find out what you already know. I will not help you choose." Say: "These groups are made up." Read the cases from the packet aloud once, again if asked: the nature-display tally and the second group's vote, the rule card, Nell's record, the town card. Pointing, drawing and dictation count.

#### 5 min. Item 1

Paper: circle the winner [[s04-E1]], then the group that held an election [[s04-E2]] [[s04-E2b]]. Read the item aloud at its start. Group 1 comes to you, three minutes, for the spoken whys.

#### 6 min. Item 2

Paper: circle the fair rule cards and what to do about an extra vote [[s04-E7]]. Group 2 [[s04-E4]] [[s04-E5]] [[s04-E6]] and group 5 [[s04-E3]] each come for three minutes. Give no help.

#### 7 min. Item 3

Paper: match two office pictures to their jobs [[s04-E8]]. Group 3 comes for the spoken parts [[s04-E9]] [[s04-E10]] [[s04-E11]] [[s04-E12]] [[s04-E13]], three minutes.

#### 7 min. Item 4

Paper: circle re-elect Nell or choose again, then draw the reason from her record [[s04-E17]] [[s04-E18]]. Group 4 comes for [[s04-E14]] [[s04-E15]] [[s04-E16]] [[s04-E16b]], three minutes. Write each child's words. Collect each sheet at the end of its part. Mark every part shown, shown with help or not yet seen: a part neither marked nor heard is not yet seen and goes to day 41. A tally slip alone does not erase a sound answer about fairness. Either choice about Nell meets when it names a deed from her record and invents no fact. Also note each child's day 38 judgment, only to fill a part day 40 did not show. Mark after class, about a minute a child.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw the marks and the town on scrap paper.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the groups.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Say, made up: a group chose its captain; seven marks under Nell, four under Lio. A second group voted between a mosaic and a mural. Your child circles the winner and the election, then says why a private ballot is fair. Then read: Nell invited everyone's ideas and set out the materials; one display was late because the room was closed. Your child chooses and draws a deed. Give no help.
  **Say.** "Who won, and how do you know? Re-elect Nell, or choose again? Which deed?" Expect: Nell, longer row; either choice with a deed from the record.
- **10 min. Respond.** Note one part to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 1 and 4. Longer: the rest wait for day 41.

### Words and supports

Read every prompt aloud. Pointing, drawing, dictation, a scribe, a private hearing and home-language words count. Frame: "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 41 re-teaches from the record sheet, in groups by item: item 1 (errors A, Q, T, H), item 2 (errors B, C, I, J), item 3 (errors D, K, L, M, S), item 4 (errors E, F, N, O). Then each child tries a fresh case.
