# Section 4.3 opener: judging a leader, answering the question, and the check

## What the section does

Four days, 38 to 41, of 30 minutes. Day 38 asks: should we re-elect? Children judge a made-up library captain's term from the record of what the captain did. Day 39 answers the unit question, what is the fairest way for a group to decide, for new situations. Day 40 is an observation with unseen cases. Day 41 re-teaches what the observation showed and gives a fresh try.

## How the days run

Day 38: warm-up and repair (5), model (6), judge (10), partners (4), exit (5). Day 39: warm-up and repair (3), return and revise (5), two records (4), answer the unit question (10), share (3), exit (5). Day 40: directions and readings (5), item 1 (5), item 2 (6), item 3 (7), item 4 (7); spoken parts run alongside. Day 41: return evidence (5), reteach by group (10), fresh try (10), exit (5).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A term record shows deeds.** A deed is something the leader did. A reason the record gives for a slip, such as a delivery that came late, belongs to the record too.
- **Two judgments can both be sound.** Re-elect or choose again, so long as the child names a deed from the record and invents no fact.
- **Claim, reason, closing.** "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___." A say-so ("everybody says so", "my friend") is not evidence.
- **Different methods can be fair.** Talk to agreement, a vote or an election each fits some situations; the reason must fit.
- **Day 40 is unaided.** Models stay covered; blank frames stay. Four paper items, with spoken parts heard group by group. A part not marked and not heard is not yet seen, not failed.
- **Day 41 repairs by letter**, with the cards for that letter, then a fresh try.

## The errors, in plain words

- **F:** a say-so or a feeling stands in for a deed. Sort cards, a deed on the record or a say-so.
- **H:** voting is always best. Show a case where talk finds a way and one where only a vote can.
- **J:** keep quiet about the count. Tell the counters so they check.
- **O:** a leader stays forever. Mark the end of a term.

## Care

Every case is invented. No child is asked how they voted or about home. The teacher never announces a child's choice. Read all prompts aloud; accept pointing, drawing, dictation and speech in a familiar language. Judge content apart from reading and handwriting. Never supply a choice, reason or explanation.

## Supplies for the section

The two term records and deed cards, the unit 4 claim frames, the day 39 situation cards, the day 40 packet (closed until day 40), the printed exit cards, the repair cards, the move cards and fresh tries, the unit 4 record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Hand-drawn deed cards, a folded paper frame in three boxes, the teacher's voice for the read-alouds.
