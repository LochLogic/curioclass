# Day 39: The fairest way

## Teacher page

### Before class

Say: "These groups are made up." Cover the day 38 model with paper; leave the blank frames and Our Decision Record up. Keep exit card 39 closed. Third six: record sheet.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up and repair

If many missed error F on day 38's exit, use the repair card: sort three made-up reasons for a bird-feeder monitor, Juno: "The record shows Juno filled the feeder every week"; "Everybody says Juno is great"; "Juno is my cousin's friend." Ask: which can we check on the record? Expect: the first.

#### 5 min. Return and revise

Return each judgment with its feedback line. Each child revises once: adds a missing deed, or a closing that says why it matters. Note revised work; it fills a part only if day 40 does not show it.

#### 4 min. Two records

Review: you learned talk, vote and election in unit 4. Reread Our Decision Record: day 32's reading corner (we talked and agreed) and storybook (we voted), and day 34's garden election. Ask how each group decided.

#### 10 min. Answer the unit question

Read the unit question: What is the fairest way for a group to decide? Each table gets one made-up card, read aloud: four children want different buildings and the blocks fit all four; a group may sing one song on visitor day and members want different songs; a club needs a leader to hand out supplies all season. Each child picks talk, a vote or an election and fills the frame "The fairest way is ___ because ___. That is why ___." by drawing, pointing or dictating. Supply no choice or reason. The third six dictate to you, half a minute each.

#### 3 min. Share

Say: "Another way may be fair too, when the reason fits."

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 39. [[s04-X39]] Read aloud: "Look at the card and the tally. Circle how this club chose." Say: "Two members wanted the job, and they talked and were still apart." The third six come to you, half a minute each: "Why did this club vote and not talk until all agreed?" Look for: still apart after talking, so equal votes decide (errors H, R). The rest draw a ballot box.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a block tower and a song on scraps.

**Skip.** The records and the six.

- **5 min. Look back.** Draw three boxes: talk, vote, election.
- **15 min. Choose.** Say a made-up case: two children want different buildings and the blocks fit both. Your child picks a way and says the frame.
  **Say.** "The fairest way is ___ because ___." Expect: talk, because there is room for both.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X39]] Show the card. Your child circles how the club chose.
  **Say.** "Why did the club vote?" Expect: still apart after talking, so equal votes decide.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one situation card and the exit. Longer: a second way that could also be fair, with its reason.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: talk, vote, election, fair, reason. Frame: "The fairest way is ___ because ___. That is why ___." Pointing and dictation count. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 40 is the check: four paper items, with spoken parts heard group by group; nothing is taught. Day 41 repairs what it shows.
