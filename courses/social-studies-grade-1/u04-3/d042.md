# Day 42: Show what you know so far

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 41 sent the class here and teaches nothing today. Keep the packet closed until now. Cover the models; leave the blank frames and the question board. Open the check 1 record sheet, one row a child, a column for each of items 1 to 4, and number four groups of six from children not yet heard on a spoken part. Day 41's exit is done; nothing from it returns. Never ask how anyone voted.

### The day

#### 4 min. Directions and readings

Say: "Today I find out what you remember from this half of the year. I will not help you choose." Say: "These cases are made up." Read the four cases from the packet aloud once, again if asked: the tortoise cards, the porch photograph, Kesi's two pictures, the garden team's two pictures. Pointing, drawing and dictation count.

#### 8 min. Items 1 and 2

Paper: circle the two smaller questions [[sc1-E1]] [[sc1-E1b]], then circle the kind of source [[sc1-E2]]. Read each prompt aloud at its start. Group 1 comes to you for three minutes, a spoken fact each [[sc1-E1]] [[sc1-E1b]]; group 2 comes for three minutes, one thing the photograph shows [[sc1-E2]]. Give no help.

#### 9 min. Item 3

Paper: place Kesi's two pictures in order [[sc1-E3a]], then draw or write the claim about Kesi [[sc1-E3b]]. Group 3 comes for three minutes to tell first and next, half a minute each. In the other six minutes take dictation of the claim, a minute each, from up to six who ask. Write each child's words.

#### 7 min. Item 4

Paper: circle the secret ballot picture [[sc1-E4]]. Group 4 comes for three minutes, the spoken why, half a minute each. Give no help.

#### 2 min. Hand in

Collect each sheet. Four groups hear 24 children; in a larger class the others are marked on paper only, and their spoken parts stay not yet seen for day 43. Mark every part shown, shown with help or not yet seen, about a minute a child after class. A claim meets when its reason is a deed from Kesi's record. A part neither marked nor heard is not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a tortoise, a porch and two ballot pictures on scraps.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the groups.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Say these are made up. A class wants to take care of a class tortoise; your child names two smaller questions and one fact one needs. Show a drawn photograph of a porch with a rocking chair; your child says its kind and one thing it shows. Tell Kesi's deeds: asked each player their spot, then hung a card of spots. Your child tells them in order and says why Kesi leads. Last, a garden team picks its leader by folded slips or by calling names; your child says which is secret and why it is fairer. Give no help.
  **Say.** "Which is a smaller question? What does it show? Why is secret fairer?" Expect: what a tortoise eats; a rocking chair; each can choose without watching, and every slip counts.
- **10 min. Respond.** Your child picks one part to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 3 and 4 first; the rest wait for day 43. Longer: nothing is added.

### Words and supports

Read every prompt aloud. Pointing, drawing, dictation, a scribe, a private hearing and home-language words count. Frame: "I think ___, because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 43 re-teaches from day 42's items, in groups by item: item 1 (errors A, B, C), item 2 (errors J, K, L), item 3 (errors D, E, F, G), item 4 (errors H, I). Each child then tries a fresh case.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
