# Day 41: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 40's cells. Name four groups by a picture, never by who missed: the marks group (item 1), the covered-box group (item 2), the town group (item 3), the calendar group (item 4). A group over a third of the class gets whole-class teaching first. Say: "These cards are made up." Keep exit card 41 closed. Fourth six: record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return evidence

Return day 40's assessment. Give each child their record: one thing shown, one next step.

#### 10 min. Reteach by group

Two minutes at your table per group, using the letter's repair card from sections 4.1, 4.2 or 4.3; wrong answers voiced by "someone might say". Marks group, errors A, Q, T: line up the marks, the longer row wins, a person for a job is the election; error H: talk finds a way, or only a vote can. Covered-box group, errors B, C, I: private marking, equal votes, the job card; error J: "I tell the counters so they check." Town group, errors D, K, L, M, S: office to place, many helpers, coins in the shared jar, a shop is not government. Calendar group, errors E, N: belonging without voting; error F: a deed on the record or a say-so; error O: the term ends, voters choose again. The rest pair on bird-feeder monitor Juno's record: filled the feeder every week, forgot to ask the group once, a cold week froze the feeder one day. Each pair says two judgments, each with a deed.

#### 10 min. Fresh try

Each group gets its new made-up case on paper: a pond club's tally and banner vote; a seed club's snack-table leader and its three rules; a village path; a plant-swap club's leader. Children draw, point or say the answer. Hear up to eight children whose parts are not yet seen, one minute each. The others stay not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 41. [[s04-X41]] Read aloud: "Circle what to do." Two minutes. The fourth six come to you, half a minute each: "The club leader's term is ending after sharing turns fairly. Would you re-elect? Say one reason." Look for: tell the counters, and a deed, the shared turns (error J, error O). The rest draw what they will do next time.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the cases on scraps.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look back.** Pick one part from day 40 to try again.
- **15 min. Try again.** Say a made-up case: a bird-feeder monitor filled the feeder every week and forgot to ask the group once. Your child says one choice with a deed from it.
  **Say.** "Re-elect or choose again? Which deed?" Expect: either choice, naming a deed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X41]] Show the card. Your child circles what to do.
  **Say.** "The leader's term is ending after sharing turns fairly. Would you re-elect? One reason." Expect: tell the counters; a deed, the shared turns.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one part and the exit. Longer: say the other judgment too.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___." Pointing, drawing and dictation count. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 is the mid-year check: new questions, a photograph, a claim about a leader, and why a secret ballot is fair. Nothing is taught.
