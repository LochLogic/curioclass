# Day 43 repair cards, fresh tries and exit cards

Four teacher groups by item, about two minutes each at the teacher's table: item 1 (errors A, B, C), item 2 (J, K, L), item 3 (D, E, F, G), item 4 (H, I). The other children work in pairs on the pair card. Say first: "These cards are made up." Wrong answers are voiced by "someone might say". Name each group by a picture, never by who missed. Print "invented for practice" on every card.

## Item 1 group: smaller questions

**Repair card:** "A smaller question asks about one part. A liking is not a question." **New case:** a made-up class wants to answer How can we keep the class rabbit cage clean? Cards: Rabbits are soft. What cleans a cage best? How often does the cage need cleaning? Children sort question from liking, pick two smaller questions and name one fact each needs.

## Item 2 group: sources

**Repair card:** "A photograph shows one place on one day." **New case:** a made-up dated photograph of a market cart and a recording of a made-up cart driver. Children sort the two cards into photograph and recording, name one thing the photograph shows and one thing it cannot show.

## Item 3 group: two deeds and a claim

**Repair card:** "First, next. Because, a deed." **New case:** a two-picture record of Doro, a made-up team leader: first Doro asks each member for an idea, next Doro writes the ideas on a wall chart. Children lay the pictures in order and say Doro is a good leader because ___.

## Item 4 group: the secret ballot

**Repair card:** "Mark alone, fold, drop in the box, two counters count." **New case:** a made-up ring toss team picks its captain. Children act both ways with two puppets and say which way lets each member choose freely.

## Pair card

A made-up photograph card of a school gate and a made-up recording of a past pupil. In pairs, one child holds each card and says what kind of source it is and one thing it gives.

## Exit cards, day 43 (two, kept closed until the day)

Card a: a made-up tram stop photograph and a recording of a made-up tram driver ([[sc1-X43a]]). Card b: a made-up play corner with a cloth roof over the mats and a puzzle shelf ([[sc1-X43b]]). Each child marks the card the record names for them; the spoken part is heard from the first six.
