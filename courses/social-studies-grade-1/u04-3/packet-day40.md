# Day 40 packet: the observation (read aloud, closed until day 40)

Nothing here is taught before day 40. Cover completed teaching models; leave blank frames and rereading available. Read every card aloud once, and again when asked. Say first: "These groups are made up." Print "invented for practice" under each case. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. No tally in the unit's teaching or exits is seven and four (the repair cards use six and four, day 39 uses five and four), so the tally below stands as written.

## Case 1: the nature-display group

Picture: a table with leaf and shell displays, and two name cards, Nell and Lio. Under the names, eleven ballot marks in two rows: seven under Nell, four under Lio. Read: "A nature-display group elects its captain. Nell has seven votes. Lio has four." A second picture: another group at a wall with two choices, a mosaic and a mural. Read: "A second group votes between a mosaic and a mural."

## Case 2: the rule card (read aloud)

"Everyone in this group may vote once, privately. Each vote counts equally. The higher total wins. Two counters check the count. The captain organizes displays for one term, then the group chooses again."

## Case 3: Nell's term record

Three pictures. Read: "Nell invited everyone's ideas and arranged the materials. One display was late because the room was closed. The next election is now."

## Case 4: the town card

A made-up town with a council table, a bridge and workers collecting rubbish. Read: "A town council makes community rules. Town workers carry out the rules. The government provides a bridge, and workers collect rubbish. People help pay through taxes."

## The four item sheets

1. **Item 1 sheet.** The tally picture. Circle the winner. Circle the group that held an election. Pointing, drawing and dictation count for the spoken parts.
2. **Item 2 sheet.** The rule card as pictures: a covered box, a ballot, two counters. Circle the fair rule cards. Circle what to do about an extra vote.
3. **Item 3 sheet.** Two office pictures to match to their jobs; the town card with a circle for a good and a circle for a service.
4. **Item 4 sheet.** Circle re-elect or choose again, and draw the reason from Nell's record.

## Record sheet

Names down the side; a column for each look-for; a heard column for the spoken parts. Use the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Write the child's actual words.

## For the teacher

The spoken parts run alongside, five groups of six, about three minutes each: group 1 item 1, group 2 item 2, group 3 item 3, group 4 item 4, group 5 item 2. A part neither marked on paper nor heard is not yet seen and goes to day 41. A tally error alone does not erase a sound explanation of fairness. A different judgment about Nell meets when it names a deed from the record and invents no fact. Do not teach or rehearse any of these cases.
