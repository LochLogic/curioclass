# Day 38: Should we re-elect?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Say: "These leaders are made up." Keep exit card 38 closed. Never say a child's choice aloud. Second six: record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

If many missed error O on day 37's exit, mark the end of a made-up captain's term on the class calendar: voters choose again, even a leader we like (three minutes). Then show the blank frame: "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___." Review: you learned claim, reason and closing in unit 3.

#### 6 min. A term record

Review: you learned term and re-elect on day 37, source questions in unit 3. Ask them of Ivo's record, a made-up plant monitor: who made it (the group's recorder), when (the end of the term), how it helps (it lists what Ivo did). A deed is something a leader did. Show Ivo watering a row of pots. Read: "Ivo watered the plants every week." Beside it hold a bubble: "Everybody likes him." The record shows a deed; the bubble does not. Model the frame aloud: "I choose to re-elect Ivo because the record shows he watered every week. That matters because the plants stay alive."

#### 10 min. Judge

Read Rosa's record twice, a made-up library captain: she shelved the returned books every week; she asked the group for ideas once; the reading-corner sign was not painted, and the record does not say why. Each child chooses re-elect or choose again, points to a deed card, draws it and fills the frame by pointing. Supply no choice and no reason (error F). Six come to you, half a minute each, and dictate the frame.

#### 4 min. Partners

Pairs tell the choice and one deed, two minutes each. Say: "Two choices can both be sound, if each names a deed on the record."

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 38. [[s04-X38]] [[s04-X38b]] [[s04-X38c]] [[s04-X38b]] Read aloud: "Circle the reason card that says what the captain did." Two minutes. Six come to you, half a minute each: "Would you choose this captain again? Tell one thing the captain did." The rest draw a deed they weighed. Look for a deed from the record (error F). Write feedback after class.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw each deed on a scrap of paper.

**Skip.** The partners and the six.

- **5 min. A record.** Tell a made-up paper monitor: gave out paper every day; forgot to stack it once. Say what a deed is.
- **15 min. Judge.** Your child chooses re-elect or choose again, draws one deed, and says the frame.
  **Say.** "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___." Expect: a deed from the record, then why it matters.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X38]] [[s04-X38b]] [[s04-X38c]] [[s04-X38b]] Show the card. Your child circles the reason card that says what the captain did.
  **Say.** "Would you choose this captain again? Tell one thing the captain did." Expect: a deed from the record, such as the repaired bags.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Rosa's record and the exit. Longer: choose the other judgment and tell its deed.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: record, deed, re-elect, term, reason, closing. Frame: "I choose ___ because the record shows ___. That matters because ___." Pointing and dictation count. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 returns your feedback; each child revises once, then answers the unit question for new groups. Its warm-up repairs error F: sort three reason cards, a deed on the record or a say-so.
