# Day 38: two term records

Say first: "These leaders and groups are made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. One person acts on each card. Print "invented for practice" in small letters on each card. The teacher reads every card aloud twice.

## The model, covered on day 39: plant monitor Ivo

Source questions first. Who made this record? The group's recorder. When? At the end of the term. How does it help? It lists what Ivo did.
Record card: a picture of Ivo watering a row of pots. Read: "Ivo watered the plants every week." A say-so card beside it: a speech bubble. Read: "Everybody likes him." The record shows a deed; the say-so card does not.
Teacher models the frame with Ivo: "I choose to re-elect Ivo because the record shows he watered every week. That matters because the plants stay alive."

## The judging case, for each child: library captain Rosa

Record card (read aloud twice), three pictures:
1. Rosa puts returned books back on the shelves. Read: "Rosa shelved the returned books every week."
2. Rosa asks the group, with a hand raised to the room. Read: "Rosa asked the group for ideas once."
3. A reading-corner sign, still blank, with a paintbrush lying dry. Read: "The reading-corner sign was not painted. The record does not say why."

Deed cards (three, one for each picture) for the children to point to, draw and use in the frame. Each child chooses re-elect or choose again, points to a deed card, draws it and fills the frame by pointing. The teacher takes dictation from the rotating six.

## Words with pictures

re-elect (choose the same leader again after judging the work); term (a set time a leader serves); deed (something a leader did); record (what was written down); reason (what the record shows); closing (why it matters).

## For the teacher

Two judgments can both be sound: re-elect Rosa for the shelved books, or choose again because the group's ideas were asked only once. A say-so such as "everybody says so" or "she is my friend" is not evidence. The unpainted sign has no reason on the record, and no child may supply one. Never announce a child's choice.
