# Exit cards, days 38, 39 and 41

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day.

## Day 38 exit card: the equipment captain

**Picture:** A made-up equipment captain by a pile of bags, one being mended, and a meeting list with some boxes empty. Three reason cards, each with a circle: a mended bag; a friendship picture of two children with linked arms; a crowd with speech bubbles.

**Mark:** Circle the reason card that says what the captain did. **Spoken:** "Would you choose this captain again? Tell one thing the captain did."

## Day 39 exit card: the hiking club

**Picture:** A rule card and a tally. The rule card shows one folded ballot each, a covered box, and two counters. The tally shows five marks under Dara and four marks under Eli. Three choice cards, each with a circle: members at a covered box with one ballot each; one tall member standing and shouting; two ballots in the hands of a friend of the leader.

**Mark:** Look at the card and the tally. Circle how this club chose. **Spoken:** "Why did this club vote and not talk until all agreed?"

## Day 41 exit card: the reading club

**Picture:** A made-up reading club with twelve ballots in a box and a tally with thirteen marks. Two choice cards, each with a circle: a child telling the two counters; a child with a finger on their lips.

**Mark:** Circle what to do. **Spoken:** "The club leader's term is ending after sharing turns fairly. Would you re-elect? Say one reason."

## For the teacher

The answers are in the item data, not on this page. Do not use these cases in any teaching. Day 39's card carries the rule and tally as pictures, and the spoken part states that two members wanted the job and were still apart after talking.
