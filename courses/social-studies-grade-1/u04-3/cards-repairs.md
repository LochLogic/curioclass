# Repair cards, section 4.3

New cards for the warm-up parts of days 38 and 39, used only if many children missed that letter the day before. Say first: "These cards are made up." Wrong answers are voiced by a puppet or "someone might say", never by a named child. None of these cases appears in an exit or end item.

## F: day 39 warm-up (3 minutes)

**F, a say-so is evidence.** Sort three new reason cards for a made-up bird-feeder monitor named Juno: "The record shows Juno filled the feeder every week" (a deed on the record); "Everybody says Juno is great" (a say-so); "Juno is my cousin's friend" (a say-so). Ask: which one can we check on the record?

## H: day 41, in the item 1 group (2 minutes)

**H, voting always works better than agreement.** A made-up craft table has two children who both want the red paper, and there is plenty. A puppet says: "We must vote!" Show that when talk can find a way both accept, we may not need a vote. Then show a case where only one can happen and the group is still apart: a vote helps.

## J: day 41, in the item 2 group (2 minutes)

**J, we must keep quiet about the count.** A made-up game club counts its scores, and the total looks too big. Puppet one says: "Keep quiet." Puppet two says: "I tell the counters so they check." The class says which keeps the count fair.

## O: day 41, in the item 4 group (2 minutes)

**O, a leader we like stays forever.** Mark the end of a made-up term on a calendar strip. The next card says voters choose again. A puppet says: "But we like our leader!" Answer: voters may choose the same leader again after judging the work.
