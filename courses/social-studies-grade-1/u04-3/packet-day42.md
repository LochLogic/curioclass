# Day 42 packet: check 1, mid-year (read aloud, closed until day 42)

Nothing here is taught before day 42. Cover every completed model in the room; leave blank frames and the question board. Read each card aloud once, and again when asked. Say first: "These cases are made up." Print "invented for practice" under each case. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Pointing, drawing and dictation count.

## Case 1: the class tortoise (item 1)

Four question cards, each with a small picture: a tortoise eating a leaf, a tortoise being cute with a heart, a class around a tortoise, a tortoise by a warm lamp. Read: "What does a tortoise eat? Tortoises are cute. How can our class take care of a class tortoise? How warm should a tortoise home be?" The big question card is the third.

## Case 2: the porch photograph (item 2)

A drawn card that stands for a photograph: a porch, a rocking chair and a basket of apples, with a thin photograph border. Caption read aloud: "A porch at a made-up farmhouse, about 1910 (invented)." Three kind cards: a photograph (a camera), a map (a top view) and a recording (a speaker).

## Case 3: Kesi's record (item 3)

Two pictures. One: a captain, Kesi, asks each player at a field which spot they want to try. Two: Kesi hangs a card on a wall showing each player's spot. Read: "Kesi is the captain of a made-up kickball team."

## Case 4: the garden team ballot (item 4)

Two pictures of a made-up class garden team, a team like the one in unit 1. One: each member marks a slip alone, folds it and drops it in a covered box. Two: members call out a name while the whole team watches. Read: "The garden team picks its leader for the next row."

## How the day runs

Marks are made by every child on the paper. Four groups of six are heard on the spoken part of one item each; a child who is not heard stays not yet seen on that part and goes to day 43. Mark each part shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Either look-for in the claim is enough when the reason is a deed from Kesi's record.
