# Day 43: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 42's cells. Name four groups by a picture, never by who missed: the question cards (item 1), the camera card (item 2), Kesi's pictures (item 3), the covered box (item 4). If more than a third missed one item, teach it to everyone for four minutes first. Say: "These cards are made up." Keep exit cards a and b closed. Day 44 needs nothing from today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return evidence

Return day 42's sheets. Each child gets their record: one thing shown, one next step. The class sees which items were missed most, never named children. **Ask.** "Which part will you try again?" Expect: a point to a part.

#### 10 min. Reteach by group

If many missed day 42's items, this is a second look, not new teaching. Two minutes at your table per group with its repair card; wrong answers are voiced by "someone might say". Question cards, errors A, B, C: a liking is not a question, the big question is not smaller, a fact is what we find out, not who we ask. Camera card, errors J, K, L: sort photograph, recording and map; one photograph shows one place on one day; say only what is in it. Kesi group, errors D, E, F, G: first, next; two deeds; a reason is a deed, then because. Covered box, errors H, I: mark alone, counters count, watching presses a member. The rest pair on the pair card.

#### 10 min. Fresh try

Each group gets its new made-up case on paper: the rabbit cage questions, the market cart photograph, Doro's two pictures, the ring toss captain. Children draw, point or say the answer. Hear up to eight children whose parts are not yet seen, one minute each. The others stay not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark the exit card the record names. [[sc1-X43a]] Read aloud: "Put a token on the photograph." [[sc1-X43b]] Read aloud: "Circle the reason." Two minutes. The first six come to you, half a minute each: one thing the recording tells that a photograph cannot, or the claim frame with because. Look for: the card's own detail and a deed or detail, not a liking (errors F, G, J, K). The rest draw what they will do next time.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the cases on scraps.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at day 42's work and pick one part to try again.
- **15 min. Try again.** Make up a case: a photograph of a bridge, and a recording of a made-up bridge worker. Your child says which is the photograph and one thing it shows.
  **Say.** "Which is the photograph? What does it show?" Expect: the camera one; one thing in it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc1-X43a]] [[sc1-X43b]] Show the card. Your child marks it.
  **Say.** "I think the corner is shady, because ___." Expect: a cloth roof over the mats.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one part and the exit. Longer: say the other part too.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I think ___, because ___." Pointing, drawing and dictation count. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 opens unit 5: a map stands for a place. Nothing from today is handed on.
