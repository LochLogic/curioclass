# Day 39 situation cards

Say first: "These groups are made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. One person acts in each picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters on each card. One card for each of three tables. The teacher reads each card aloud.

## Card 1: the blocks

Picture 1: four children each hold a different drawing of a building, a tower, a bridge, a house, a barn. Picture 2: the block shelf, full, with room on the rug for all four. Read: "Four children want to build different buildings. The blocks and the rug fit all four."

## Card 2: the visitor's day song

Picture 1: a group of children on a rug, and a calendar page with a star for visitor day. Picture 2: three song pictures on the wall and a raised hand beside each. Read: "A group may sing one song on visitor day. Members want different songs, and only one will fit."

## Card 3: the supplies leader

Picture 1: a club shelf with paper, glue and scissors. Picture 2: a child with a clipboard and an empty chair. Read: "A club needs a leader to hand out supplies all season."

## For the teacher

Each child picks talk, a vote or an election, and completes "The fairest way is ___ because ___. That is why ___." by drawing, pointing or dictating. Likely: card 1 talk, card 2 a vote, card 3 an election. Another method may be fair when its reason fits (for example, the group could talk to choose the leader if all agree).
