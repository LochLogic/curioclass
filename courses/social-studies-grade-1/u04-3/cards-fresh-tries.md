# Day 41 move cards and fresh tries

Four teacher groups by item, about two minutes each at the teacher's table: item 1 (letters A, Q, T, H), item 2 (B, C, I, J), item 3 (D, K, L, M, S), item 4 (E, F, N, O). The other children work in pairs on two possible judgments of the new term record below. Say first: "These cards are made up." Wrong answers are voiced by "someone might say". Name each group by a picture, never by who missed.

## Item 1 group: tally and kind of vote

**Move card:** "Line up the marks. The longer row wins. Is it a person for a job, or a thing?" **New case:** A made-up pond club has four marks under Sol and three under Una for its captain, and also votes for a club banner, green or blue. Children line up the marks, say who wins and which vote is the election.

## Item 2 group: the secret ballot and the count

**Move card:** "Private marking, equal votes, two counters. What does each rule do?" **New case:** A made-up seed club picks its snack-table leader: each member marks one ballot alone, folds it and drops it in a covered box, and two counters count. Children match three rule pictures (the covered box, one ballot each, the two counters) to what each protects: choosing without pressure, every vote the same, a checked count.

## Item 3 group: offices and government

**Move card:** "Match the office to its place and job. Say what government provides and who pays." **New case:** A made-up village card: a council makes a rule that the path stays clear, workers clear snow from the path, and people help pay together. Children name a good, a service and how it is paid for.

## Item 4 group: citizens and the term

**Move card:** "The term ends. Voters judge the work. Then choose again." **New case:** A made-up plant-swap club's leader finished a term. Children say who belongs, who votes in a government election, and what happens when a term ends.

## New term record for pairs: bird-feeder monitor Juno

Record: Juno filled the bird feeder every week. Juno forgot to ask the group once. A cold week kept the feeder frozen one day. Pairs say two possible judgments, one for re-electing Juno and one for choosing again, each with a deed.

## Fresh try for everyone

Each group writes, draws or points to an answer on an unused made-up case on paper. The teacher hears up to eight children whose parts are not yet seen, one minute each. Any others stay not yet seen.
