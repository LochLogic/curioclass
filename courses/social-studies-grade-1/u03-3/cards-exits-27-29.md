# Exit cards, days 27 to 29

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses the Lost Coats, a chart row, a frame model or a repair card.

## Day 27 exit card: the broken bench

**Picture:** Three pictures out of order, labelled with circles instead of numbers. One: a repair group stands around a bench with a broken leg, one member pointing. Two: the group looks at a drawn plan on a paper. Three: the same bench, whole, and a child sitting on it.

**Mark:** Place the three pictures in order. **Spoken:** "Tell it in order: what did they do first, then, and so what happened?"

## Day 28 exit card: the cooking club

**Picture:** A club leader holds out a hand to a quieter member while other members stand around a table with bowls. Two reason cards read aloud, each with a circle: "She asks quieter members for ideas." and "She is a good leader." A claim card above reads "She listens."

**Mark:** Circle the reason that supports "She listens." **Spoken:** "Give one reason that supports 'She listens.'"

## Day 29 exit card: the reading space

**Picture:** A club leader speaks to seated members, then sets out cushions with them. Two action cards each with a circle: "asked members" and "is famous." A claim card above reads "The leader listened."

**Mark:** Circle the action card that supports "The leader listened." **Spoken:** "Say a closing that goes back to 'The leader listened.'"
