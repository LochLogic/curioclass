# Day 27: First, then, so

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the timeline strip; the action word strip stays on the wall. Make the pair cards, order, below. The six: third six on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

If many missed day 26's exit (error N), use the class-trip repair pictures (made up): a leader counting lunch bags, another holding a sign. Place the caring card under both: "Both look after the group, by different jobs."

#### 2 min. Words with pictures

Review: in kindergarten you said first, next, last. Today: **first**, **then**, **so**; so says the result. A **fact that matters** helps explain what happened.

#### 7 min. Model: The Lost Coats

Say "This story is made up." Read each card, twice. Then tell it, a finger on each picture: "First Ms. Dunn noticed the coats piling up. Then she asked helpers to sort them. So owners found their coats."

**Ask.** Which fact helps explain what happened: the box was full, or the coats were different colors? Expect: the box was full. Cross out the colors card. Someone might say "First she asked helpers, then she saw the box" (error C): can helpers sort before she sees it?

#### 5 min. Timeline

Review: the strip from days 22 to 25. Read its cards in order. The Cherokee Nation is here today; Huerta has died, and her card shows her start.

**Ask.** Which came first, Tubman's escape or Lincoln as President? Expect: Tubman's. Children say "First Tubman, then Lincoln."

#### 7 min. Pairs, and hearing the six

Made up. Case 1: a visitor asks the way; the helper points down the hall; the visitor finds the library; extra, a clock hangs on the wall. Case 2: a child plants seeds; waters them; sprouts come up; extra, a bird sat on the fence. Review from kindergarten: order three action pictures. Pairs alternate cases, cross out the extra card (error J) and say them with first, then, so. The six come to your table, a minute each, to mark exit card 27 and answer its spoken ask.

#### 4 min. Exit

The other twelve pairs mark exit card 27. [[s03-X27]] Read aloud: "Place the three pictures in order." Spoken to the six: "Tell it in order: what did they do first, then, and so what happened?" Look for: noticed, planned, fixed; a so naming the bench used; no extra detail.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: draw three boxes.

**Skip.** The timeline and the six.

- **5 min. Tell.** Say a made-up story, drawing as you go: Ms. Dunn sees a box full of coats by the door, asks helpers to sort the coats, and owners find their coats.
- **15 min. Order.** Mix the three drawings; your child puts them in order. Add a fourth, "the coats were different colors," for your child to cross out.
  **Say.** "Tell it in order. Which fact helps explain what happened?" Expect: First she saw the full box, then she asked helpers, so owners found coats; the full box.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X27]] Tell a made-up story in three drawings: a gardener sees weeds, pulls them, so the flowers can grow. Your child places them in order.
  **Say.** "Tell it in order." Expect: First she saw weeds, then she pulled them, so flowers can grow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the timeline. Longer: tell a toy a story from four pictures.

### Words and supports

Frame: "First ___, then ___, so ___." Pointing and home-language words count.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 opens with error C if many placed the pictures out of order (reorder three new cards), and error J if many told an unrelated detail (strike one card of four).
