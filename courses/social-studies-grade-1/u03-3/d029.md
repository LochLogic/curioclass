# Day 29: My own piece about a leader

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover the day 28 models with paper. The chart, the blank claim frames, the quality cards and two-column strip, the action word strip and the pair question cards, explanations stay up. The six: fifth six on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

If many missed day 28's exit, use the made-up repair cards. Error H: match three claim cards ("She organizes." "She cares." "She shares.") to three mural action cards, and say "She organizes because she gave each helper a job." Error M: answer three read-aloud questions with a word from each question and a picture.

#### 2 min. Frame

Say the three parts aloud once over the blank frames: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___." Ask the question: "What makes a good leader?"

#### 11 min. Write or dictate

Each child picks two leaders from the chart, says one claim, draws an action from each as the reason, and says a closing, with at most a word label. If a child picks Lincoln, the chart says his paper said people who were enslaved in the named states were free, not everywhere. The Cherokee Nation is here today; Huerta has died. The six come to your table one at a time and dictate the whole piece, a minute and a half each; write only their words, and take the spoken closing for exit 29. [[s03-X29b]] Others draw. Supply no claim, reason or closing (error P: the same words, a new name; error Q: a name instead of a quality).

#### 8 min. Pairs ask and answer

Each child tells the partner's piece back in order, asks one question from the pair cards and answers the partner's (error M). Move among the pairs. Collect the pieces.

#### 4 min. Exit

The other twenty-four mark exit card 29. [[s03-X29]] Read aloud: "Circle the action card that supports The leader was fair." Look for: gave each member a turn. For the six, look in the dictated piece for a closing in the child's own words that returns to the claim, not the day 28 closing copied (error P), and not left off (error I). Write a line of feedback on each piece after class.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: draw two leaders, a crossing guard who stops cars so children cross, and a team captain who gives each player a job.

**Skip.** The pair cards and the six.

- **5 min. Tell.** Say what each of the two leaders does. Fold a page in three for a claim, a reason and a closing.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child picks one leader, says a quality, draws the action, and says why it matters. You write their words.
  **Say.** "What makes a good leader? Why does it matter?" Expect: a quality, an action, a reason it matters.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X29]] [[s03-X29b]] Tell a made-up story: a pet-show leader draws names from a hat so every owner gets one turn. Your child circles "gave every owner a turn" or "is famous."
  **Say.** "Say a closing that goes back to The leader was fair." Expect: So fair leaders make sure no one is left out.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one leader and the exit. Longer: a second quality.

### Words and supports

Frame: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___." Pointing, drawing, dictation and home-language words count.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 is the check; nothing is taught. Day 31 repairs this exit: error I (a reason with no closing) adds a closing to a covered, unfinished piece; error P (the model's words copied) tries a new action record. Pieces come back on day 31.
