# Repair cards, section 3.3 (days 27 to 29)

New made-up cards for the reopen parts. They never appear in an exit or end item and never reuse the Lost Coats. Say first: "These cards are made up." Each is five to ten minutes at most, and only when many children missed the error the day before.

## Day 27 repair for N: different jobs, one quality

Two pictures of a made-up class trip. Left: a leader counts the lunch bags at the door. Right: another leader holds up a sign so the line stays together. Children place a quality card, caring, under both and say "Both look after the group, by different jobs."

## Day 28 repair for C: put the pictures in order

Three action cards laid in the wrong order for a made-up library shelf: sorted the books; saw books on the floor; put them back on the shelf. Children reorder them and say "First she saw the books on the floor, then she sorted them, so they were back on the shelf."

## Day 28 repair for J: strike the extra card

Four cards for a made-up sand table: the sand table was full of toys; the helper asked for tubs; the toys went into the tubs; the tubs were red. Children strike the card that does not tell what happened and say why.

## Day 29 repair for H: match a claim to an action

Three claim cards ("She organizes." "She cares." "She shares.") and three action cards for a made-up mural (gave each helper a job; brought a bandage for a scraped knee; gave the last crayons to a friend). Children match each claim to the action that supports it, and say "She organizes because she gave each helper a job."

## Day 29 repair for M: answer the question asked

Three question cards read aloud: "What did the leader do first?" "Who helped?" "What changed?" Children answer each with a word from the question and a picture: "She asked first." "Helpers helped." "The wall was painted."

## For the teacher

Wrong answers are voiced by "someone might say," never by a named child. Do not reuse a repair case as an exit case.
