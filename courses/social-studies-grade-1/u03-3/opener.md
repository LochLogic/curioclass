# Section 3.3 opener: explaining, a claim and a check

## What the section does

Five days, 27 to 31, of 30 minutes. Day 27 teaches telling what happened in order with first, then, so, and which facts matter. Day 28 teaches the claim, reason and closing, first as a model and then as a class piece. Day 29 asks each child to write or dictate their own piece from the leaders on the chart. Day 30 is an observation of unseen cases. Day 31 re-teaches what the observation showed and gives a fresh try.

## How the days run

Day 27: reopen (5), words (2), model (7), timeline (5), pairs and the six (7), exit (4). Day 28: reopen (5), words (2), model (7), shared writing (7), pairs ask and answer (5), exit (4). Day 29: reopen (5), frame (2), write or dictate (11), pairs (8), exit (4). Day 30: read-aloud (5), paper parts (14), item 4 and exchange (11). Day 31: return evidence (5), reteach by component (10), fresh try (10), self-check and exit (5).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Order and fit.** First, then, so tells what happened in order; a fact matters when it helps explain what happened.
- **Claim, reason, closing.** A claim says what a leader is; a reason is an action that shows it; a closing goes back to the claim and says why it matters.
- **The frame holds the shape, not the content.** The child chooses the action. Models are covered on day 29; blank frames stay.
- **Ask and answer.** A listener asks about a partner's explanation; the speaker answers the question asked.
- **Any quality that the action supports meets.** Courage, listening, organizing or caring all count when an action backs them.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C and J: order and extra facts.** Reorder, then strike the card that does not tell what happened.
- **H: the reason repeats the claim.** Match a claim to an action that supports it.
- **M: the answer does not answer.** Answer with a word from the question.
- **I, P, Q: the closing, the copy, the person.** Add a closing; use a fresh action; name a quality and an action.

## Care

Children write at most a word label. Everything else is spoken or dictated, and the teacher writes only what the child says. No child is asked to share about family or home. The day 30 texts stay closed until day 30. Heard evidence is counted in turns, and a child not yet heard is recorded as not yet seen, with no penalty.

## Supplies for the section

The Lost Coats, the three-box frame, pair question cards, the exit cards, repair cards, the day 30 packet, the move cards and fresh tries, the exit card for day 31, the strip, the chart, and the record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Stick pictures on scrap paper; a folded paper for the three boxes; the teacher's voice for the read-alouds.
