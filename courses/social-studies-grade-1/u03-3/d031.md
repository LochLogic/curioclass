# Day 31: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 30's assessment cells and day 29's exit. Four groups, named by a picture: one (errors A, F, G); two (errors B, H, I); three (errors C, D); four (errors E, M). A group over a third of the class goes first. The action word strip stays on the wall.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return evidence

Return the day 29 pieces and name one improvement per child on the record sheet. Error I: a covered, unfinished piece gets a closing; ask "Why does that matter?" Error P: cover the model and try a new action record from the chart.

#### 10 min. Reteach by component

The cards are made up. Two and a half minutes at your table per group; the others pair on their move card. One: a swim-lesson helper asks each child "Which float do you want?"; put the helper inside the ring, name the job, say asking is a duty, not a shout. Two: a bake-sale leader brings extra cups from the kitchen, claim "She cares"; cover the name, say the quality, then why it matters. Three: two helpers fill an empty bird feeder, so birds can eat; say it with first, then, so, and name the helpers. Four: a poster group paints signs; ask "What did the group paint?" and answer with a word from the question. Error Q goes to pairs on the written move card.

#### 10 min. Fresh try

A made-up park clean-up leader gives each helper a job, so the park is tidy. Each child says or draws a claim, a reason and a closing, then asks and answers one question with a partner. Hear four more pairs, a minute and a half each, from children not yet heard; then take exit 31's spoken asks from six of them, half a minute each.

#### 5 min. Self-check and exit

Everyone marks exit card 31. [[s03-X31a]] Read aloud: "Place the three pictures in order." Spoken to the six: "Tell it in order with first, then, so." [[s03-X31b]] [[s03-X31c]] Then: "Name a quality this leader shows and one action that shows it." Look for: an action as the reason (not error H) and a quality, not a name (error Q). Last, each child tells a partner one thing better than on day 29, and you write it.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the pictures on scraps.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard pairs.

- **5 min. Look back.** Pick one part to try again: the quality, the action or the closing.
- **15 min. Try again.** Say a made-up case: a park clean-up leader gives each helper a job. Your child says a claim, a reason that is an action and a closing; you write them. Your child asks one question about the leader.
  **Say.** "What did the leader do? Why does it matter?" Expect: gave each helper a job; so the park gets tidy.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X31a]] [[s03-X31b]] [[s03-X31c]] Tell a made-up story in three drawings: a story-time leader sees the rug is crowded, asks children to sit in two circles, so everyone can see. Your child places them in order and names a quality.
  **Say.** "Tell it in order with first, then, so. Name a quality and an action." Expect: First she saw the crowd, then she made two circles, so all could see; caring, because she made two circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one component and the exit. Longer: a second quality for the same action.

### Words and supports

Frame: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___." Pointing and home-language words count.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 begins unit 4, fair ways to decide. A miss today stays on the record sheet and returns in unit 4's reasons; day 32 has no repair time.
