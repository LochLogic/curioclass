# Exit card, day 31

A paper card every child marks and a spoken part with two asks, heard from the six. Say aloud first: "This card is made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep it closed until the day. No card reuses a day 31 fresh-try case, a repair card or a chart row.

## The pond walk

**Picture:** Three pictures out of order, marked with circles. One: a member points at a steep hill on a walking path and looks worried. Two: the leader draws a new path around the hill on a paper. Three: the leader stands on the new path and asks a small group, with a child in a wheelchair among them, if all can get by; they nod.

**Mark:** Place the three pictures in order. **Spoken, first ask:** "Tell it in order with first, then, so." **Spoken, second ask:** "Name a quality this leader shows and one action that shows it."

## For the teacher

The answers are in the item data, not on this page. Do not use this case in any teaching. A child may point and speak in a familiar language first.
