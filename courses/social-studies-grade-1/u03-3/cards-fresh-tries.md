# Day 31 component move cards and fresh tries

Four teacher groups, about two and a half minutes each at the teacher's table: A, F, G; B, H, I; C, D; E, M. The other children work in pairs from the written move for their component. Other letters go to pairs from the written repair cards. Say first: "These cards are made up." Wrong answers are voiced by "someone might say."

## Group A, F, G: the member, the job and the duty

**Move card:** "Put the leader inside the ring. Name the job. Ask: is it a duty or a shout?" **New case:** A made-up swim-lesson helper holds a rope for beginners, hands out floats and asks each child, "Which float do you want?" Children place the helper in the ring of lesson helpers, name the job, and say asking is the helper's duty, not a shout.

## Group B, H, I: reason and closing

**Move card:** "Cover the name. Read one action. Say the quality. Then say why it matters." **New case:** A made-up market leader sets out boxes so a neighbor with a cane can reach the stall. Claim: "She cares." Reason: the action. Closing: back to the claim.

## Group C, D: order and helpers

**Move card:** "First, then, so. Who else did a job?" **New case:** A made-up trail group sees a fallen branch on the path, moves it with two helpers, so walkers can pass. Children say it in order and name the helpers.

## Group E, M: asking and answering

**Move card:** "Ask what someone did. Answer with a word from the question." **New case:** A made-up poster group paints signs for a fair. Pairs ask "What did the group paint?" and answer "The group painted signs."

## Fresh try for everyone

Each child retries a piece about a leader from the chart on a new made-up case: a leader asks a quiet member what they think, then changes a plan. The heard pairs are chosen from the rotating six. Credit: all cases are made up and said to be made up.

## For the teacher

Do not use any of these cases in the exit card or the day 30 packet. The move cards never supply the child's content (P). A miss on day 31 stays on the record sheet for unit 4.
