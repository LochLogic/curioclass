# Pair question cards (days 28 to 30)

For asking and answering about a partner's explanation. Four large cards with a picture each, read aloud by the teacher. Say first: "These cards are our own."

## The cards

- **What did the leader do first?** (a hand pointing to the first picture)
- **What happened next?** (an arrow)
- **So what changed?** (a before and an after)
- **Who helped?** (two figures side by side)

## How a pair uses them

The listener picks one card and asks it. The speaker answers using a word from the question: "She noticed the coats first." "Then she asked helpers." The speaker may point to a picture. The teacher writes the child's actual words on the record sheet.

## Rehearse

"I heard ___. I wonder ___." "She did ___." A child may answer in a familiar language first.

## For the teacher

M: an answer that does not respond to the question ("I like blue") is not wrong because of the content; ask the question again and point to the picture it asks about. Any relevant question counts. The same cards are used on day 30 and are not new.
