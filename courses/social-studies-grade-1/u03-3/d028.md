# Day 28: Claim, reason, closing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the action chart; keep the Tubman summary, the quality cards and two-column strip and the action word strip near. Draw the claim frame large, an arrow curving from box three back to box one. The six: fourth six.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

If many missed day 27's exit, use the made-up repair cards. Error C: reorder three library-shelf cards and say them with first, then, so. Error J: strike the sand-table card that does not tell what happened.

#### 2 min. Words with pictures

Review: you have said "I think ..., because ..." since kindergarten. Now: a **claim** is what we say a leader is; a **reason** is an action that shows it; a **closing** says why it matters. Say together: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___."

#### 7 min. Model

Point to Tubman's row. Write large and say: "A good leader is brave. The source tells she went back to help others escape to freedom. This matters because brave leaders help others reach safety." Brave is the courage card. Think aloud: the reason is an action, not the claim again (error H); the closing follows the arrow back.

**Ask.** What did she do that shows brave? Expect: she went back to help others escape to freedom.

#### 7 min. Shared writing

Huerta has died: use the past tense. You hold the pencil and write only what children say. Claim: "A good leader organizes." Reason: children choose the action card.

**Ask.** Which action card shows organizing? Expect: she helped organize farm workers. Then ask for the closing; point to the arrow.

#### 5 min. Pairs ask and answer, and hearing the six

Each pair takes a question card and asks it about the Huerta sentence; the partner answers with a word from the question (error M). The six make three pairs and come to your table, a minute and a half each.

#### 4 min. Exit

The other twelve pairs mark exit card 28. [[s03-X28]] [[s03-X28b]] Read aloud: "Circle the reason that supports She listens." Spoken to the six, half a minute each: "Give one reason that supports She listens." Look for: an action, not the claim again (error H). Practice, not scored.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: draw three boxes on a page.

**Skip.** The pair cards and the six.

- **5 min. Show.** Write for your child: "A good leader is fair. The class leader gave every child a turn on the swing. This matters because fair leaders make sure no one is left out." Point to each box.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child picks a leader who helps, says a quality for box one, draws the action for box two, and says a closing for box three. You write their words.
  **Say.** "What did the leader do that shows it?" Expect: an action, not the quality again.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X28]] [[s03-X28b]] Tell a made-up story: a book-club leader asks each child which story to read next. Your child circles "She asks each child" or "She is a good leader" as the reason for "She listens."
  **Say.** "Give one reason that supports She listens." Expect: She asks each child.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the Tubman model and the exit. Longer: a second claim for the same action.

### Words and supports

Frame: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___." Pointing and home-language words count.

### Tomorrow

Day 29 asks for each child's own piece, models covered. It opens with error H if many gave the claim again as the reason (match a claim to an action), and error M if many did not answer the question asked.
