# Day 30: Show what you know about leaders

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet closed until now. Cover the models and the chart; leave the blank claim frames and the pair question cards, explanations. Open the record sheet with its "heard" column and mark six pairs, twelve children, from the children not yet heard in unit 3. Day 29's errors I and P wait for day 31.

### The day

#### 5 min. Source read-aloud and directions

Say "Today I find out what you already know. I will not help you choose." Read the three texts from the assessment packet aloud once, again if asked. Name each: the first is our summary of a government page about real people; the other two are made up. Say once, in the summary's words, that the neighbors include people who moved from other countries, and ask no child about their own family or home.

#### 14 min. Paper parts, items 1 to 3

Everyone works on the item sheets. Sheet 1: circle the puppet leader among the members and draw one thing the leader does [[s03-E1]]; then tell or dictate the bus monitor's job [[s03-E2]]. Sheet 2: name one quality all three leaders show and an action of each [[s03-E3]]; then the blank frame, a claim, a reason that is an action from the cases and a closing [[s03-E4]]. Sheet 3: put the three Addams pictures in order and tell what the group did first and what followed, with a fact from the summary [[s03-E5]]. Take dictation at your table in turn, three minutes each, at most four children; a further child is scribed by a helper or finishes on day 31, marked shown with help. Give no help with the answer. Collect each sheet at the end of its part.

#### 11 min. Item 4, and the exchange

Two minutes: everyone draws a question about Addams or her group [[s03-E6]]. Nine minutes: pairs exchange an ordered explanation and a question, then answer [[s03-E7]] [[s03-E7b]]. You hear one marked pair at a time, a minute and a half each, six pairs; the other pairs repeat the exchange, unheard, as practice. Write each child's words. Mark every part shown, shown with help or not yet seen; an answer that says the streets became clean is partly shown.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw the puppet club on a page.

**Skip.** The pairs and the record sheet.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read aloud, made up: "A puppet-club leader tells everyone they can help, sorts the tasks, listens to ideas, and guides the group to choose how to mend a torn puppet." Your child draws the club and circles the leader, then says what the leader does. Next, a claim, a reason that is an action and a closing; you write their words. Give no help.
  **Say.** "What makes a good leader? What did the leader do? Why does it matter?" Expect: a quality, an action from the case, and why it matters.
- **10 min. Respond.** Note one part to try again. Give no help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the circle, the drawing and the claim, reason and closing. The rest waits for day 31.

### Words and supports

Read every prompt aloud. Pointing, drawing, dictation, a scribe, a private hearing and home-language words count. Frame: "A good leader ___. The source tells ___. This matters because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 31 re-teaches by component from the record sheet. Group one: the leader inside the group, the job and the duty (errors A, F, G). Group two: the quality named from an action, the reason and the closing (errors B, H, I). Group three: order and helpers (errors C, D). Group four: asking and answering (errors E, M). Error Q goes to pairs on the written move card.

### Credit

Ours, all of it; the first text is our summary of a National Park Service page.
