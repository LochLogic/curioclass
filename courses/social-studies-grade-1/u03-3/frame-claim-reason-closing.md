# The three-box frame (days 28 to 31)

One page with three large boxes, each with a picture cue and a printed starter read aloud. Children write at most a word label in each box; claims, reasons and closings are spoken or dictated. The frame never supplies the child's content.

## The three boxes

1. **Claim** (a hand pointing): "A good leader ___." What we say a leader is.
2. **Reason** (an action card slot): "The source tells ___." An action that shows it.
3. **Closing** (an arrow curving back to box 1): "This matters because ___." Why it matters, going back to the claim.

## Day 28 model, written by the teacher

"A good leader is brave. The source tells she led others to freedom. This matters because brave leaders help others reach safety." The model is about Harriet Tubman, from the day 22 read-aloud and its chart row. Think aloud: the claim, an action as the reason, a closing that goes back.

## Day 28 shared piece

The class writes a second piece about Dolores Huerta together (organizing), with the teacher holding the pencil and children choosing the action card from the chart.

## Days 29 to 31

Cover both models on day 29. Blank frames stay, and so do the chart and rereading. The frame is the same on day 30 and day 31.

## For the teacher

P: a piece with the same words as the model and only the name changed is the frame supplying the content. Ask for a different action from the chart. I: a piece that stops after the reason has no closing; ask "Why does that matter?" and point at the arrow back to the claim. Q: "A good leader is Mankiller" names a person; ask for a quality and an action. Accept familiar-language explanations first.
