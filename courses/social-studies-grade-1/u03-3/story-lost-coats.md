# The Lost Coats (day 27 model, used again on day 28)

An invented picture story in four pictures, read aloud twice. Say first: "This story is made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. One person acts in each picture.

## Picture 1

Ms. Dunn stands by a big box by the school door. The box is full of coats, and some hang over the edge. A label on the box reads "lost and found."

**Teacher reads:** "Ms. Dunn sees the box. It is full of coats."

## Picture 2

Ms. Dunn holds up a card that reads "sort" in front of three helpers. Each helper holds one coat.

**Teacher reads:** "Ms. Dunn asks helpers to sort the coats."

## Picture 3

Three helpers lay coats out in rows on a long table. Ms. Dunn is not in the picture.

**Teacher reads:** "The helpers sort the coats into rows."

## Picture 4

A child holds up a coat with a smile. The long table is nearly empty.

**Teacher reads:** "Owners find their coats."

## The explanation, modeled in order

"First Ms. Dunn noticed the coats piling up. Then she asked helpers to sort them. So owners found their coats." Model the three words first, then, so, one at a time with a finger on each picture.

## A fact that matters, and one that does not

- **Matters:** the box was full. It is why she acted.
- **Does not matter to the order:** the coats were different colors. Cross out the card that shows the colors and say why: it does not tell what happened first, then, so.

## For the teacher

This is the day 27 model and the day 28 reference for how an action becomes a reason. Wrong answers are voiced by "someone might say," never by a named child. The story is made up and said to be made up. Do not use it in an exit or end item.
