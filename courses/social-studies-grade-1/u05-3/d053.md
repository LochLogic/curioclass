# Day 53: Why did people build towns and cities where they did?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out day 52 maps and cards. Hang the Harbor Map. Keep exit card 53 shut. Teacher keys: key-map-harbor.md, key-cards-cattail-crossing.md, key-map-harbor-lenape-layer.md, key-map-another-place-and-feedback-cards.md, key-pictures-park-set-and-map-page.md, key-sheet-claim-frame-unit5.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Spoken. Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Error O, 2 minutes: Pip's map has a star in the key for "park" and a star elsewhere for "dog": "Both are fine." **Ask.** "Can a symbol mean two things?" Expect: no, one meaning, written in the key. Error P, 1 minute: Pip adds a pond "because parks have ponds"; hold the photograph beside the map.

#### 5 min. Revise the map

Each child revises one thing from the feedback card and saves the map for day 54. A child may point.

#### 5 min. Step back

Review: a claim is what we say; a closing is why it matters, back to the claim (unit 3). Name each source: who made it, when. Model: "A city can grow beside a harbor." **Ask.** "What does the map show?" Expect: ships and docks by the water. **Ask.** "What does the account say?" Expect: ships brought goods; travelers came. **Ask.** "How did it help?" Expect: goods and a reason to settle near. Closing: "That is one reason a town can grow." Lay the Lenape layer: the nations' own accounts say Lenape people lived here first, and Lenape nations live today. Pip: "Cities grow because that is where cities are." Missing: a map detail and an account detail. A river crossing and a capital are other reasons; no one reason fits every city. Cover the finished example.

#### 13 min. Supported work

Each child answers the question on the claim frame: a map detail and an account detail, from the Harbor Map or the Cattail Crossing card, drawn, with labels from the word bank. Pairs tell the partner first. The second six tell you instead, two minutes each; you scribe. Look for: a claim, a map detail, an account detail, "this helped because", a closing of one reason. Practice; mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 53 (Alder is made up). [[s05-X53]] Read the record, then: "Circle the reason the record supports." The second six, 20 seconds each: "Why did people open offices in Alder?" Look for: a map detail, a record detail, a because (error T, error U).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No Harbor Map: draw a harbor with ships and docks. No claim frame: draw two boxes.

**Skip.** Pip, the pairs and the heard six.

- **5 min. Your map.** Read the feedback card with your child. They change one thing.
- **20 min. Why here?** Draw a harbor. Draw ships at the docks.
  **Say.** "What does the map show? What does an account say? How did it help?" Expect: ships and docks; ships brought goods and travelers came by ship; a reason a city grew there.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X53]] Show the card and read the record. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the map detail. Longer: a second reason.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: harbor, account, reason. A child may point.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 is the unit check on maps not seen before; the revised map is handed in. Error T or error U from this exit shows in item 6; day 55 repairs it.
