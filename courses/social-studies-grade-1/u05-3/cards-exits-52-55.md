# Exit cards, days 52, 53 and 55

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Day 54 has no exit card; the unit check is its evidence. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses the park photographs, Hollow Green, the Harbor Map, the packet maps or a repair or fresh-try card.

## Day 52 exit card: an observation card

**Picture:** an observation card, a small picture of a place from above. A pond drawn as a blue oval below a wooded rise (a patch of small tree shapes on a hill), and a path drawn between them as a dashed line. A north arrow in one corner. Beside the card, an empty key box with a line for each symbol.

**Mark:** Draw the symbol for the pond in the key box and circle where it goes on the card. **Spoken:** "How could a visitor use your key?"

## Day 53 exit card: Alder

**Picture:** a map of Alder from above. A council building drawn as a larger square with a flag, and a row of small squares beside it for offices. A key: a large square with a flag is the council building, a small square is an office. A record card read aloud: "Workers opened offices after the state government moved to Alder." Under the map, three small word-and-picture cards with a circle each: government work, fishing, farming. The teacher reads all three aloud.

**Mark:** Circle the reason the record supports, government work, fishing or farming. **Spoken:** "Why did people open offices in Alder?"

## Day 55 exit card: Larch village

**Picture:** a map of Larch village from above. A large direction indicator labelled N with its arrow pointing down the page. An oval is a well. A triangle is a clinic; one clinic is on the left of the well and one on the right. A key: a triangle is a clinic, an oval is a well, a thin strip across a stream is a footbridge. A stream runs along the left side of the page with the footbridge across it. A record card read aloud: "People crossed the footbridge to reach the new clinic." Three tokens are set beside the map.

**Mark:** Place a token on the clinic that is east of the well. **Spoken:** "Why does the footbridge matter?"
