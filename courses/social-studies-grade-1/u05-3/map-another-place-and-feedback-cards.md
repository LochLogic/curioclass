# Day 52 and 53 pieces: a map of another place and the feedback cards

## Teacher-made map of another place (invented)

Say aloud first: "Hollow Green is made up." Print "invented for practice" at the bottom. Thick lines, large print.

**Picture:** a small town green drawn from above. A key box: a triangle is a bandstand, a square is a bench, a wavy line is a brook, a rectangle is a stall. On the map: one triangle in the middle, four squares around it, a wavy line along the left and one rectangle near the top. No direction indicator on this map, on purpose. The title is Hollow Green.

**Task for children:** say what is missing. Children say, "There is no indicator." They may also notice the key is present and the water is shown. The teacher says: four things a good map has, once: where things are, land and water, symbols with a key, the four directions.

## Written feedback cards (one per child, written that evening)

A half-page card for each child's day 52 map, about a minute a map. Five rows, one for each look-for, and a box for each with the three marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The rows: places are where the photographs show them; land and water are drawn where they are; each symbol has one meaning; the key says what each symbol means; the north arrow points the right way and a direction is named. Under the rows, one line: "One next step:" for the teacher to fill in, such as "Add the pond to the key."
