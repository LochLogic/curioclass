# Claim frame and word bank, unit 5

Name ______________________

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom.

## The blank frame

Why did people build towns and cities where they did?

- Draw a map detail: ______________________ (a box)
- Draw an account detail: ______________________ (a box)
- The map shows ___. The account says ___. This helped because ___.
- Closing: That is one reason a town can grow.

Pairs tell their partner first. A child may point to the word bank, draw, or dictate; an adult scribes the rotating six.

## The word bank (one word each, with a picture)

harbor, ship, dock, river, bridge, road, market, shop, water, capital, government, city, map, account, reason.
