# Teacher key, sheet claim frame unit5

Teacher only. Keep apart from sheet-claim-frame-unit5.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### one model, covered afterward

Model once with the Harbor Map, the Lenape account and the capital account:

- Claim: A city can grow beside a harbor.
- Map detail: the Harbor Map shows ships and docks by the water.
- Account detail: the account says ships brought goods, and travelers came by ship, to the city; the Lenape account says Lenape people were here first.
- Reason: ships coming to the docks gave people goods and a reason to settle nearby. This helped because it shows why a city grew there.
- Closing: That is one reason a town can grow.

No single reason explains every city; a capital grows because government work gathers there, and a river crossing grows because people meet there. Cover the completed example after the demonstration on day 53, and keep the frame blank. On day 54 cover every model; only blank frames stay up.
