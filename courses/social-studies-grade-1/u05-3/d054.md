# Day 54: Maps and places, the check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet closed until now. Cover every completed model in the room; only blank frames stay. Lay out the tokens, the park photograph set to judge item 5 by, and each child's revised map. Add to the record sheet which of the five sixes is heard when. Teacher keys: key-packet-day54.md, key-maps-globes-and-nested-cards.md, key-maps-locator-and-state-cards.md, key-sheet-claim-frame-unit5.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions and sources, read aloud

Say: "Reed is made up. The United States map is real." Name each source: A and C are ours, B is a real locator redrawn by us. Read every prompt and source aloud, with no hints. A point, token, drawing, dictation or familiar-language speech counts. Group 1 is heard first, then a new group every five minutes, about 50 seconds a child, one or two asks from the record sheet. A child not heard is heard on day 55. Mark each part shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 4 min. Item 1

Map C and its key. [[s05-E1]] [[s05-E1b]] Circle Reed and the bridge; the double line. [[s05-E2]] What map C is a map of.

#### 5 min. Item 2

[[s05-E3]] A continent, map A. [[s05-E4]] [[s05-E4b]] An ocean, map A. [[s05-E5]] River, lake and mountain, map C. [[s05-E6]] The labeled city on the globe views. [[s05-E7]] The new paper relief: made by people, already there. [[s05-E8]] How people use the place on map C.

#### 4 min. Item 3

Map C, with its indicator. [[s05-E9]] A token on the lake; the mountain from the lake. [[s05-E10]] What lies north of Reed; how north was found. [[s05-E11]] Reed from the fields. [[s05-E12]] Map B: Los Angeles, Washington, D.C. and our state capital.

#### 3 min. Item 4

Map B. [[s05-E13]] Circle the stars that are capitals; why. [[s05-E14]] Washington, D.C. [[s05-E15]] Our state capital. [[s05-E16]] Read aloud that Los Angeles is one of the biggest cities in the country; is it always the capital?

#### 3 min. Item 5

Children hand in the revised day 52 map. [[s05-E17]] The land, the water and the place mapped. [[s05-E18]] The key and one symbol. [[s05-E19]] North and one place east of another.

#### 6 min. Item 6

Read the record for map C aloud twice. [[s05-E20]] Each child draws or dictates a claim, a reason and a closing on the student page. [[s05-E21]] One reason Reed became a place people go. Error T from day 53 may show here. Collect the pages and maps.

After class, about fifteen minutes: write each part on the record sheet, and group the errors for day 55: item 1 A, item 2 D, item 3 C, item 4 G, item 5 O, item 6 T. Judge spatial meaning, not drawing.

### Other rooms

The materials line names the stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a made-up map with a key, a river, a lake, a hill and a town. Say it is made up.

**Skip.** The state map, the relief and the heard groups.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Give no hints.
  **Say.** "Which direction is the hill from the lake? How do you know?" Expect: a direction from the north arrow, not the top of the page.
  **Say.** "Why did the town grow there?" Expect: a map detail, a note detail, a reason.
- **10 min. Respond.** Note one part that was hard; ask again on a new map.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 1, 3 and 6. Longer: ask a second reason.

### Words and supports

Read every prompt and source aloud. Words: map, key, north, capital, continent, river, lake, mountain. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 acts on this check: groups by item from the record sheet, item 1 error A, item 2 error D, item 3 error C, item 4 error G, item 5 error O, item 6 error T, with error U from day 53 if it shows. Children not heard are heard there.
