# Day 52: A map a stranger could use

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out a photograph set per pair and a map page with border and key box per child. Name the park or place. Write N on one edge of the wide photograph. Hang the wall signs N S E W. Keep exit card 52 closed. Teacher keys: key-map-another-place-and-feedback-cards.md, key-pictures-park-set-and-map-page.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Spoken. Day 51's exit: error J, 2 minutes, two cards, a map shows where water is, not whether to drink it; error E, 1 minute, picture cards. Hand back the labeled drawings. Show the six photographs. **Ask.** "What is land here? What is water?" Expect: grass, path, trees; the pond, stream or lake.

#### 5 min. Step back

Review: a key is a box that says what each small picture stands for; the direction indicator is the mark that shows where north is. Point to a key on one of the three maps with keys. Show the teacher-made map of another place, Hollow Green (made up): a key, no indicator. **Ask.** "What is missing?" Expect: the indicator. Say once the four things a good map has: places, land and water, symbols with a key, the four directions. Add a question board card.

#### 18 min. Supported work

Each child maps the park from the photographs: land and water where they show, two places or more, a symbol for each, a key, the north arrow. Name north first: the arrow points the way the N on the wide photograph does. Draw only what the photographs show. Frame: "My key says ___ means ___." Save as practice. You hear the first six, two minutes each. **Ask.** "What place did you map?" Expect: the park, and its water. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 52. [[s05-X52]] Read aloud: "Draw the symbol for the pond in the key box. Circle where it goes on the card." The first six, 20 seconds each: "How could a visitor use your key?" Look for: one meaning in the key; the circle where the card shows the pond (error O, error P).

That evening, a minute of feedback per map: positions, land and water, symbols, key, directions, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and one next step. A symbol in the wrong place is a spatial error, not a drawing one.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No photographs: use a picture of a small park with water that your child has not drawn. No crayons: pencil is fine.

**Skip.** The Hollow Green map, the pairs and the heard six.

- **5 min. What is here?** Show the picture. Your child names the land and the water.
- **20 min. Make the map.** On a page, draw a key box and a north arrow. Draw the park from above: land, water, two places, a symbol for each. Write nothing in the key but what each symbol means.
  **Say.** "How could someone who has never been here use your key?" Expect: each symbol means one thing, written in the key.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X52]] Show the card. Your child draws the symbol and circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one place and the water. Longer: one more place.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: map, key, symbol, north. A child may point.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 returns this exit and each map: error O, Pip's two-star map, 2 minutes; error P, the photograph beside the map, 1 minute. Each child revises one thing.
