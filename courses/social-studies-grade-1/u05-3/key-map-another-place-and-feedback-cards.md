# Teacher key, map another place and feedback cards

Teacher only. Keep apart from map-another-place-and-feedback-cards.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Teacher-made map of another place (invented)

**Teacher key.** This map has places, water (the brook) and a key; it lacks the four directions. Praise any true answer. Do not call a child's answer wrong; ask them to point to it.

### Written feedback cards (one per child, written that evening)

**Teacher key.** Feedback is about positions, land and water, symbols, key and directions. Two or three days of rotation is acceptable. The feedback card goes back with the map on day 53, and the child revises one thing from it. A map is not ranked.
