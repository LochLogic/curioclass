# Day 55: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet make at most four groups, the most missed item first: item 1 error A, item 2 error D, item 3 error C, item 4 error G, item 5 error O, item 6 error T. Lay the cards. Keep exit card 55 closed. Say: "These places are made up." Teacher key: key-pictures-park-set-and-map-page.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return evidence

Each child gets their own work from day 54's assessment back with the mark by part: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Nothing is posted or ranked. **Ask.** "Which part will you try again?" Expect: a point to a part.

#### 10 min. Reteach by item

Groups meet you about two minutes each; the rest work in pairs from the written repair card. Pip makes a mistake on purpose, on a new place each time. Error A: Pip says the squares are tiny real buildings; touch an object, then its mark, "stands for". Error D, with E, I and R: Pip says a continent is a country; find a country inside a continent. Error C: rotate a map, trace the indicator to the wall sign; retry on a map whose indicator points left. Error G: two dots on a new state card; the caption says where the government meets. Error O, with P: one meaning for each symbol; the photograph beside the map. Error T, with U: pair the claim with a source detail card; two cards, harbor and capital.

#### 10 min. Fresh try

Each child tries the part missed on a new invented card, one per item: Willow Bend, a world view, Tamarack Lane, a made-up state, a new park view, Pebble Ferry. Children not heard on day 54 come to you, a minute each, one ask the record sheet names.

#### 3 min. Reflect

Review: a claim, a reason and a closing (day 53). Pairs set the first work beside the fresh try; each says one improvement. **Ask.** "What changed between your first try and this one?" Expect: a detail now used, such as the indicator. A child may point.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 55. [[s05-X55]] Read the record aloud, then: "Place a token on the clinic that is east of the well." The third six, 20 seconds each: "Why does the footbridge matter?" Look for: the indicator, not the page right (error C); a clue from the record (error K).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a new small map with a north arrow that points sideways.

**Skip.** Pip, the groups and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at the marked work together. Your child picks one part.
- **20 min. Try again.** Draw a new map for that part. Say it is made up.
  **Say.** "What changed between your first try and this one?" Expect: one thing now done well, such as using the arrow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X55]] Show the card and read the record. Your child places a token.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group, one try. Longer: a second item.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: indicator, reason, closing. A child may point.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 begins unit 6 and reserves no check. Note error C and error K from this exit on the sheet; day 58 reviews the key and directions.

Ours, all of it.
