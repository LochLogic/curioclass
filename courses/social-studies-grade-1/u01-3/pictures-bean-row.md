# Bean Row

A picture sequence for day 6. Five scenes. Before you show it, say: "We made this story for our class. The children in it are made up." One actor a scene. The goal is a planted row of beans in the class garden. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

## Scene 1: Mara tells the order

**Picture:** A garden bed with a string stretched along it. Mara stands at one end holding a paper with four pictures in a row, pointing at the first. Ben, Aiko and Tomas stand together behind her looking at the paper.

**Read:** Mara tells the order of the work. First loosen the soil, then place the seeds, then water.

## Scene 2: Ben loosens the soil

**Picture:** Ben kneels at the bed with a small hand fork, turning up dark soil along the string. The seed packet sits on a nearby bench.

**Read:** Ben loosens the soil so the roots can grow.

## Scene 3: Aiko places the seeds

**Picture:** Aiko sits on a low stool at the bed and drops one bean seed into each small hole, one hand's width apart. A paper tray of seeds rests on her lap.

**Read:** Aiko places the seeds, one in each hole, in a row.

## Scene 4: Tomas waters

**Picture:** Tomas holds a small watering can and pours a thin stream over the covered row. A puddle shines at the end of the bed.

**Read:** Tomas waters the row so the seeds get what they need.

## Scene 5: the finished row

**Picture:** The bed, with a neat row of small green sprouts along the string. No people.

**Read:** The row is planted. Each part helped.

## For the teacher

Teach two words with motions. **Role:** the part a member plays in a group; hold up one finger and wiggle it. **Goal:** what the group wants to finish together; point to the finished row. Mara's part is the review of in charge from kindergarten. Ask: what was Ben's role? What was Aiko's? Never assign a role in the room by gender or ability.
