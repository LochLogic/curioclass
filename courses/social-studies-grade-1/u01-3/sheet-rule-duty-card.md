# Rule and duty card (day 8, kept for day 9)

One card for each child, large print, about half a sheet. The teacher writes the words; a child's own mark counts.

## Front

- **Our rule:** ____________________ (the teacher writes the agreed rule, for example "Leave space for others at the shelf").
- **My duty:** ____________________ (the role card: crayon sorter, lid closer, box carrier, shelf pointer or floor checker).
- **Feedback:** a box where the teacher draws a mark or a stamp after practice.

## Back

A large empty box with the heading **How my duty helps**. The child draws the duty and what it helps; the teacher writes a word label if asked. Children who finish their feedback early draw their duty here while others wait.

## For the teacher

Thirty cards at about fifteen seconds each is seven and a half minutes; a helper halves it. Score only the revised performance and its explanation, recorded on the tidy-up wall chart. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
