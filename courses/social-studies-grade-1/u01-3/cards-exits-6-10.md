# Exit cards, days 6 to 8 and 10

Each exit has a picture card that every child marks (circle or point) and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six (the first six on day 6, the second on day 7, the third on day 8, the children not heard on day 9 on day 10). Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. None of these pictures is reused from Bean Row, the fire station and library, the Trashbusters summary or the tidy-up cards. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

## Day 6 exit card: a class picnic

**Picture:** Two ringed pictures side by side. On the left, a picnic blanket with a guide pointing the way, a cup carrier handing paper cups to children, and children sitting and drinking. On the right, the same blanket with the guide pointing while two children stand by and only watch.

**Mark:** Circle the picture where the picnic gets done. **Spoken:** "How does the cup carrier help?"

## Day 7 exit card: a science night

**Picture:** Two ringed pictures side by side. On the left, a school hallway with a greeter pointing the way to the rooms and two children holding arrow signs. On the right, the same hallway with the greeter pointing and two children only watching.

**Mark:** Circle the picture where everyone has a part in getting visitors to the rooms. **Spoken:** "What is the task they share?"

## Day 8 exit card: a book fair table

**Picture:** Two ringed cards side by side. On the left, the words "The coin counter counts the coins," with a picture of a hand sorting coins into a tray. On the right, a badge with the words "Coin Counter" and no picture of work.

**Mark:** Circle the card that tells what a duty does. **Spoken:** "How does the coin counter's duty help?"

## Day 10 exit card: a bird-watching club

**Picture:** Two ringed rule cards side by side. On the left, "Keep voices low so the birds stay near," with a picture of two children whispering beside a bush. On the right, "Keep voices low," with the same picture and no reason.

**Mark:** Circle the rule card with a reason. **Spoken:** "Say a smaller question for How can a bird-watching club work well?"
