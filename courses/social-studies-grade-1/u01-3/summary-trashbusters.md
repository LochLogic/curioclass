# The Trashbusters (day 7)

Our short summary of a real account. Read it aloud. Do not read the page itself to the children. Credit it aloud: "This is a real group. It is from a page by the National Park Service. The summary is ours."

Source: National Park Service, Padre Island National Seashore, "Impacts Beyond the Beach: Trashbusters and Their Shared Mission", page updated April 27, 2025 (https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/impacts-beyond-the-beach-trashbusters-and-their-shared-mission.htm). Public domain; credited. No pictures of real people are used and no individual is named.

## Read aloud

There is a group of friends who volunteer at Padre Island National Seashore in Texas. They are called the Trashbusters. Every week they pick up trash and marine debris along the shores, beaches and dunes. They have done this since 2015. A park staff member supervises the work.

The Trashbusters say that being outdoors, seeing wildlife and seeing clean beaches feel good. They want visitors to enjoy the beach.

## Teach and ask

A volunteer is a person who chooses to help a group's work and is not paid. Ask: why do they do it? Any answer tied to what the summary says meets: being outdoors, seeing wildlife, clean beaches, visitors enjoying the beach. Then ask who guides (the park staff member) and who does a part (the volunteers). Say plainly that we know only what the page says.
