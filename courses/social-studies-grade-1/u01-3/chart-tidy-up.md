# Tidy-up wall chart (day 8, read on day 9)

This chart is real: it is the class's own record of day 8. It is not a story and not an invented card.

## Layout

A large chart on the wall with one row for each child. Columns: **Child**, **Duty**, **Rule kept after the retry**, **How my duty helps** (a small drawing the child pastes in or points to). The teacher fills in the columns during the three-minute retry and the five-minute draw.

## Marks

Use the three marks only: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Write nothing about how many reminders a child needed, and never post the chart where it ranks children.

## On day 9

Item E3 is scored from this chart, not from a new attempt: note whether the child did the agreed duty and kept the agreed rule, and what the child said or drew for how the duty helps. In E8 children may point to something on the chart as their second detail; show the chart without any child's mistake called out.
