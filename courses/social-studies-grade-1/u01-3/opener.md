# Section 1.3 opener: roles, common work and the check

## What the section does

Five days, 6 to 10, of 30 minutes. Day 6 teaches role and goal with Bean Row, an invented story of four children planting a row of beans. Day 7 shows how a community gets common work done: an invented fire station, an invented library and a real volunteer group, the Trashbusters, in our own summary of a National Park Service page. Day 8 is a real tidy-up where every child has a role and a duty, with picture feedback and a retry. Day 9 is the check: eight items on what the unit taught. Day 10 responds to what day 9 showed.

## How the days run

Day 6: reopen and retrieval, Bean Row, act the roles, what would be missing, exit. Day 7: return and repair, two pictures, the Trashbusters, who does what, exit. Day 8: return and plan, tidy-up practice (not scored), picture feedback, retry, explain and draw, exit. Day 9: source orientation (5), paper items (12), claim and hearing (13). Day 10: revisit, targeted teaching in at most four groups, a fresh try, self-check and exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A role is the part a member plays; a duty is the job inside it.** Every role helps the goal. Take one away and something is missing.
- **A leader is one member.** The others still matter. A person who is not in charge still has a part.
- **Authority is shown, not defined.** Day 7 pictures show who guides, who does a part and who checks.
- **Volunteers choose to help and are not paid.** The Trashbusters say why: being outdoors, seeing wildlife, clean beaches.
- **A claim has a because.** "Sharing jobs helps, because ..." with a detail from a picture or the tidy-up record.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C: only the guide matters.** Trace each member's part to the goal on three cards.
- **H: a badge or title instead of an action.** Act out a paper collector's job and say what it makes ready.
- **E: a liking, not a detail.** "I like groups" tells a feeling. Point to a detail and say what it shows.
- **G: a question with no fact to find.** "Is it fun?" has no fact. "Who gets a book next?" does.
- **I: a rule with no reason.** Choose between two rules by which helps what the group is for.

## Safety

The tidy-up uses crayons and boxes only: no lifting, no scissors, no cleaning products. The school's own safety guide governs. Wash hands as usual. No role by gender or ability; every table gives every child a role, including seated roles. No real person's picture is used on day 7. The Trashbusters summary names the group only.

## The photograph on day 9

The teacher saves one enlarged print of the Group Volunteering photograph from the National Park Service page (row 9A; NPS-made photographs are generally public domain unless marked; credit NPS Photo/H. Marquez). The caption is read aloud. Do not show it before day 9.

## A room with nothing

Draw four role cards on scrap paper and use a box of any light items to tidy; describe the photograph aloud with its caption.
