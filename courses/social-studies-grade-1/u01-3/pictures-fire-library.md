# Fire station and library (day 7)

Two invented pictures. Say first: "We made these pictures. The people in them are made up. We only say what is drawn." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

## Picture 1: the fire station

**Picture:** A fire station bay with a truck parked and a bulletin board on the wall. A firefighter in the middle points to a board with a list of the morning's jobs, drawn as three small pictures: a box with a tick, a rag and a bucket, a clipboard. A second firefighter checks a piece of equipment on a rack. A third cleans a piece of equipment with a cloth.

**Teacher says:** "One firefighter points to the list of the morning's jobs. One checks equipment. One cleans equipment." Real firefighters respond to emergencies and also clean, maintain and inspect their equipment (row 7D).

## Picture 2: the library

**Picture:** A library with tall shelves. A librarian stands at an end of a shelf and shows a visitor where the books go, pointing at a sign with a picture of an open book. Beside her a student helper, shorter, puts returned books on a cart with a row of numbered spines. This helper is invented.

**Teacher says:** "The librarian shows where books go so they are easy to find. A student helper puts returned books on a cart." Real librarians organize library materials so they are easy to find (row 7E).

## Questions

For each picture ask: who guides? who does a part? what is missing without each? Teach community: people who share a place and look after it together. Do not tell the children what anyone feels; say only what is drawn.
