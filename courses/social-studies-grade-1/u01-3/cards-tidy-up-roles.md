# Tidy-up role cards (day 8)

Five role cards for each table of five. Every child gets one; no table leaves a child out. No role by gender or ability. Light items only (crayons and boxes). Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

1. **Crayon sorter.** Picture: crayons in two small piles by color. Can be done seated.
2. **Lid closer.** Picture: a hand pressing a lid on a box.
3. **Box carrier.** Picture: a figure holding one light box in both arms. A child may slide the box instead of lifting.
4. **Shelf pointer.** Picture: an arm pointing at a shelf with a gap. Directs placement; can be done seated.
5. **Floor checker.** Picture: eyes over a patch of floor. Can be done seated with a pointer.

## Using the cards

At each table the teacher gives every child a role, then the table agrees one rule (for example, leave space for others at the shelf). The rule and the child's duty go on the rule and duty card. Children keep the role card at the table during practice. No lifting, no scissors, no cleaning products; hands are washed as usual.
