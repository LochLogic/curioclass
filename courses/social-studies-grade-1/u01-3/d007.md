# Day 7: How a community gets common work done

## Teacher page

### Before class

Envelope for each pair: six cards, three mats. Today's six: the second on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-6-10.md.

### The day

#### 7 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 6's exit (error C), 3 minutes: show Bean Row scenes 2, 3 and 4. **Ask.** What would the row lack without Aiko's part? Expect: any tie to the row.

If many gave a title only on day 6's retrieval (error H), 4 minutes: all mime a paper collector gathering papers. **Ask.** What does that job make ready? Expect: papers ready for the teacher.

#### 6 min. Two pictures

Say: "We made these pictures. The people in them are made up. We only say what is drawn." Show the fire station, then the library. Real firefighters clean and inspect equipment; real librarians organize books so they are easy to find (sources 7D, 7E).

**Ask.** In each picture, who guides? Who does a part? What is missing without each? Expect: the one pointing or showing guides; the others do parts; any tie to the picture.

Teach community: people who share a place and look after it together.

#### 7 min. The Trashbusters

Say: "This is a real group. It is from a page by the National Park Service. The summary is ours." Read the summary. Teach volunteer: a person who chooses to help a group's work and is not paid.

**Ask.** Why do they do it? Expect: any reason in the summary. Who guides? Who does a part? Expect: the park staff member; the volunteers. We know only what the page says.

#### 5 min. Who does what

Pairs set six cards on three mats: guides, does a part, helps the community. The cards: the pointing firefighter, the cleaning firefighter, the librarian, the student helper, the park staff member, the volunteers. 15 pairs work at once; visit 5. Accept two mats with a reason. "Only the first matters" is error C.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Say the science night is made up. Read both pictures: "Circle the picture where everyone has a part in getting visitors to the rooms." (s01-X7, 1 minute.) Ask the second six, one each: "What is the task they share?" (4 minutes). The others draw story helpers. "The greeter points" is error C.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: draw them. No cards: use paper slips. No exit card: draw both pictures.

**Skip.** The repair and the pair sort.

- **7 min. Two pictures.** Say these people are made up. Draw a fire station: one firefighter points at a list of jobs, one checks equipment, one cleans it.
  **Say.** "Who guides? Who does a part?" Expect: the one pointing guides; the others do parts.
- **7 min. The Trashbusters.** Say they are a real group of friends who pick up trash along beaches in Texas each week, and a park staff member supervises (National Park Service).
  **Say.** "Why do they do it?" Expect: being outdoors, or clean beaches.
- **6 min. Draw.** Your child draws the helpers in one story.
- **5 min. Sort.** Slips on guides or does a part.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X7]] Show both pictures; say they are made up.
  **Say.** "What is the task they share?" Expect: visitors find the right rooms.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one picture. Longer: name a school helper.

### Words and supports

Words: community, volunteer, guide. Frame: "___ guides. ___ does a part."

### Tomorrow

If many named only the greeter (error C), day 8 opens with one card tracing each child's part to the shared task. Then the class tidy-up.
