# Day 9 check cards

Kept closed until day 9. Paper parts of items E1, E2, E4, E5, E6 and E7. The teacher reads each item aloud, about three minutes each; children mark, circle, match or draw. Completed worked examples are withdrawn; picture supports stay on the wall. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

## Items E1 and E2: the school field day

**Question (read aloud):** How can a group make a school field day work well?

**E1:** Which part of social studies helps answer this question? Each child holds up one of the four subject cards (history, geography, civics, economics) and the teacher notes the card and the reason. Civics is the expected answer; economics (cones and ropes are limited) and geography (where on the field) also meet with a reason.

**E2:** Say two smaller questions. For each, what fact would we need, and what important word? Children draw or point to the picture cards below, then the teacher hears them.

**Question cards (pictures for support):**
1. A figure setting out cones along a line. Fits: Who sets out the cones? Fact: the setter's job. Word: role.
2. A starting flag and a row of runners. Fits: How do racers know when to start? Fact: the starting signal. Word: rule.
3. A ribbon at a finish line. Fits: Who hands out ribbons? Fact: who hands them. Word: duty.

## Items E4 to E8: the photograph and the invented park case

**The photograph.** The teacher's enlarged print of the Group Volunteering photograph, with its caption read aloud (row 9A). It is real and the summary is ours.

Before day 9 the teacher looks at the print and writes on the key the two actions the people in it show; the keys rest on the caption (volunteers take a close look on the trail) and on that look.

**E4:** What does belonging to this group mean? What can members gain? Suggest a useful rule and say its reason.

**E5:** What are two people in the photograph doing? How does each help the group?

**The invented park case (row 9B).** Read it aloud once for E6 and again for E7. Say first: "This is made up."

A park has an area where tall weeds are crowding out the small plants. A park worker takes three volunteers, Ivy, Sam and Noor, to the area. The park worker checks which patch is safe to work in and gives each volunteer an area to start in. Ivy pulls the weeds. Sam and Noor put them in bags. They work until the patch is clear.

**E6:** What does the park worker add? What do the volunteers add? Why do volunteers matter when they are not in charge?

**E7:** What task do they share? Who assigns the areas and checks that it is safe? What does each volunteer do?

## E8: the claim

**Question:** What makes a group work well? Use something in the photograph and caption and something from our tidy-up record to explain. Children draw the claim with its detail in three minutes at their seats; the teacher hears six at a time, about two minutes a group.
