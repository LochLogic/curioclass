# Day 8: Our tidy-up

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the rule on all 30 rule and duty cards. Per table: crayons loose in a tray, a box, five face-down role cards, a marked shelf space. Draw the chart rows. Today's six: the third on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-6-10.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and plan

If many missed day 7's exit (error C), 2 minutes: a made-up hallway display, one card each: tells where pictures go, hangs them, holds the tape. **Ask.** What does the tape holder help the display do? Expect: any tie to the display.

Read the board's third question. Review: a duty is a job a member is expected to do for the group (day 4). The goal: a tidy crayon shelf. Propose one rule, "Leave space for others at the shelf"; the class agrees by thumbs. Each child at a table of five draws a role card: crayon sorter, lid closer, box carrier, shelf pointer, floor checker. Any child may swap for a seated role, no reason asked. The card goes on the child's rule and duty card.

#### 9 min. Tidy-up practice

Six tables, six boxes, all at once. Light items only: a child may slide the box; no scissors, no cleaning products. Step back: one prompt a table at most. Not scored. Note who needs an arrow.

#### 8 min. Picture feedback

Draw a check where the child kept the rule or did the duty, an arrow where to try again. 30 cards at about 15 seconds is 7.5 minutes; a second adult halves it, and without one the retry shrinks to 2 minutes. Waiting children draw their duty on the back.

#### 3 min. Retry

Tip ten crayons out at each table. Each child redoes the duty. Record the revised performance on the wall chart: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. This is the evidence, scored on day 9 (s01-E3).

#### 5 min. Explain, draw and exit ticket

All draw "How my duty helps" on the back of the card. Say together: "Each role helps the goal because ___." Say the book fair is made up. Read the names and cards: "Give each helper one duty card with a line." (s01-X8, 1 minute.) Ask the third six, one each: "How does the coin counter's duty help?" (30 seconds each, 3 minutes). "The counter wears a badge" is error H.

### Other rooms

The materials line names stand-ins. Wash hands as usual.

### At home

**Swap.** No tables: use one crayon box. No exit card: draw both cards.

**Skip.** The repair and the six tables.

- **4 min. Plan.** Agree the rule "Leave space for others at the shelf." Your child draws one role: crayon sorter, lid closer, box carrier, shelf pointer or floor checker.
- **8 min. Tidy up.** Tip the crayons out; your child does the duty while you step back.
- **4 min. Feedback.** Draw a check where the rule was kept, an arrow where to try again.
- **4 min. Retry.** Tip out ten crayons; your child tries again.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws "How my duty helps."
  **Say.** "How does your duty help?" Expect: crayons are easy to find.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8]] [[s01-X8b]] Show the card and read the names and duties; say they are made up. Your child gives each helper one duty.
  **Say.** "How does the coin counter's duty help?" Expect: every buyer gets the right change.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: practice six minutes. Longer: name a second duty.

### Words and supports

Words: duty, rule, role. Frame: "My duty is ___. It helps because ___." Seated duties: crayon sorter, shelf pointer, floor checker.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 is the check; item s01-E3 is scored from the wall chart. If many named a badge (error H), day 10 has a group for it.
