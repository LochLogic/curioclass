# Day 10: Show it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Count day 9's items by part; no names. Draw six move cards: point, trace, act, ask, compare, choose. Keep cards A to I closed. Today: those not heard on day 9. Teacher key: key-exits-6-10.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Revisit the evidence

Return each child's own day 9 drawing, its mark on the back. Show the class counts as picture bars, parts only, beginning with what the class showed well. Never show one child's mistake.

#### 10 min. Targeted teaching

At most four groups, by the largest counts, each with its letter's repair on a made-up case; visit each about 2.5 minutes. The others work in pairs from the move cards.

- **C (E5, E6).** Toy shelf crew: one tells where toys go, one puts blocks in the bin, one animals in the basket. **Ask.** What is missing without each?
- **D (E4).** A snow-shoveling crew and a cookie-baking crew, different ages and hats. **Ask.** What makes each a group?
- **B or I (E4, X10).** Two cards: "Rinse the brushes so the next group has clean ones." "Rinse the brushes." **Ask.** Which has a reason?
- **E (E8).** The wall chart. Point to one detail and say what it shows.
- **F (E1).** "How did our playground slide change?" with an old and a new slide. **Ask.** Which subject asks about before?
- **G (E2).** "How can a puppet show crew work well?" Ask a smaller question, a fact and a word.
- **H (X8, E3).** A mat roller rolls up the story mats. **Ask.** What does that job make ready?

#### 10 min. Fresh try

Say: "These are made up." Each group tries the closed card of its letter (A to I); hear the four groups in turn, about 2.5 minutes each. Pairs try a card alone with their move cards. One child follows the same cycle with an adult.

#### 5 min. Self-check and exit ticket

Each child points to a wall picture of a part they can now show (1 minute). Say the bird-watching club is made up. Read both cards: "Circle the rule card with a reason." (s01-X10, 1 minute.) Ask the children not heard on day 9, one each: "Say a smaller question for How can a bird-watching club work well?" (about 40 seconds each, 3 minutes). The others draw a rule and its reason. "Keep voices low" is error I; "Is it fun?" is error G.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the cases on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the closed cards.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at the day 9 drawings. Pick one part to show again: a role, a rule with its reason, a smaller question or a detail.
- **10 min. Show it again.** Tell a made-up toy shelf crew: one tells where toys go, one puts blocks in a bin, one puts animals in a basket.
  **Say.** "What is missing without each?" Expect: toys would not be found.
- **5 min. Self-check.** Your child points to one part they can show now.
- **5 min. Draw.** A rule and its reason.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X10]] Show both cards; say the club is made up.
  **Say.** "Say a smaller question for How can a bird-watching club work well?" Expect: Where do we sit to watch?

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three groups. Longer: a second card.

### Words and supports

Words: role, rule, duty, goal. Frame: "I can show ___." A pointed card is an answer.
