# Day 6: Roles and a shared goal

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the five Bean Row scenes in order. Pair envelopes: four role cards and the finished row card. Today's six: the first on the record sheet. Mark four zones. Teacher key: key-exits-6-10.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and retrieval

Review: in kindergarten a job is your part of our class work. **Ask.** A class messenger carries a note to the office. What does that job help the class do? (s01-R3) Expect: talk to the office. A title alone is error H.

If many missed day 5's exit (error I), 2 minutes: a made-up singing group wants everyone to join. Show "Take turns picking the song" and "One child picks every song." Which rule helps? Expect: the first. Otherwise a small group has it in the pairs.

#### 8 min. Bean Row

Say: "We made this story for our class. The children in it are made up." Read the five scenes. Mara tells the order of the work. Review: in kindergarten you learned in charge, someone whose job it is to decide for the group; Mara is one member. Ben loosens the soil, Aiko places the seeds, Tomas waters.

Teach role: the part a member plays in a group (wiggle one finger). Teach goal: what the group wants to finish together (point to the finished row). Echo each twice.

**Ask.** What was Ben's role? Expect: loosens the soil. What is the group's goal? Expect: a planted row.

#### 6 min. Act the roles

Count off to the four zones. Everyone mimes that role for 30 seconds, then moves on, twice. All act at once; watch, hear no one. A seated mime counts. Pick no role by strength or speed.

#### 6 min. What would be missing?

Pairs take one role card away and show what the row lacks. 15 pairs work at once; visit 5.

**Ask.** What is missing without that role? Expect: any part tied to the row. "Mara matters most" is error C: ask what is missing without the others. Day 8 answers the board's third question.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Say the picnic is made up. Read both pictures: "Circle the picture where the picnic gets done." (s01-X6, 1 minute.) Ask the first six, one each: "How does the cup carrier help?" (4 minutes). The others draw a favorite role. "The guide matters" is error C.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the four roles on paper. No exit card: draw both pictures.

**Skip.** The retrieval, the repair, the zones and the pair work.

- **8 min. Bean Row.** Say these children are made up. Draw five small pictures as you tell it: Mara points at the order, Ben loosens soil, Aiko puts seeds in holes, Tomas waters, then a row of sprouts.
  **Say.** "What is Ben's role?" Expect: loosens the soil.
- **6 min. Act.** Mime each role together.
- **6 min. What is missing?** Cover one picture.
  **Say.** "What is missing without Aiko's part?" Expect: seeds in the holes.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a favorite role.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6]] Show both pictures; say they are made up.
  **Say.** "How does the cup carrier help?" Expect: cups let people drink.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: act two roles. Longer: take two roles away.

### Words and supports

Words: role, goal, guide (the member who leads). Frame: "Each role helps the goal because ___."

### Tomorrow

If many chose the guide (error C), three cards trace a member's part to the goal. If many gave a title only (error H), children act out a paper collector's job.
