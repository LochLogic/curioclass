# Bean Row role cards (days 6 and 8)

Pairs hold these on day 6 and the teacher keeps a wall set for day 8. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

## The four role cards

1. **Tells the order** (Mara). Picture: a figure pointing at a row of four small pictures.
2. **Loosens the soil** (Ben). Picture: a hand fork in dark soil.
3. **Places the seeds** (Aiko). Picture: one seed over a small hole.
4. **Waters the row** (Tomas). Picture: a watering can with a thin stream.

## The finished row card

**Picture:** The planted row of sprouts along a string, no people.

## What would be missing?

On day 6 pairs choose one role card to take away and show the finished row card with that part missing: no order, hard soil, no seeds in the holes or dry seeds. Fifteen pairs work at once; the teacher visits five pairs. Any role that is tied to the row meets. The day 8 question board answer is: Each role helps the goal because ___.
