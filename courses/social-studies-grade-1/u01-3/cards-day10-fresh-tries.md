# Day 10 fresh-try cards

Nine unused invented cards, A to I, one for each error letter. They are kept closed until day 10; no earlier day shows them. Say first: "These are made up." Each group tries the card for its letter; pairs try theirs alone. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

- **A (statement or question).** "Do ducks swim?" beside "Ducks swim." Ask: which one is a question?
- **B (command or reason).** "Wait at the door because someone said" beside "Wait at the door so no one is bumped." Ask: which says why?
- **C (only the guide matters).** A class parade: one child waves the flag, two carry the banner, one plays the drum. Ask: how does each part help the parade?
- **D (matching things make the group).** A chess club and a gardening group, different ages and hats, each doing one thing together. Ask: what makes each a group?
- **E (a liking, not a detail).** "I like the park" beside "The caption shows a worker with plants." Ask: which uses a detail?
- **F (a subject label with no idea).** The question "How did our school bell change?" with an old bell and a new bell. Ask: which picture helps, and what do we need to find out?
- **G (a question with no fact to find).** "How can a lost-and-found box work well?" Ask for a smaller question and a fact it needs.
- **H (a badge instead of an action).** The lights helper turns the lights off at recess. Ask: what does the job help the class do?
- **I (a rule with no purpose).** Two rules for one group, one that serves its purpose and one that works against it. Ask: which rule helps, and why?

## For the teacher

Hear one group at a time at about two and a half minutes. The key for each is in the unit plan (field 4). No card repeats an exit case or an end item.
