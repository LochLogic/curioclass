# Day 9: What makes a group work well? The check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Look at the saved print and write the two actions the people in it show beside item E5 on the record sheet. Keep it covered. Take down the worked examples; the wall keeps the question board, the word cards and day 8's chart.

### The day

#### 5 min. Source orientation

Uncover the print and read its caption word for word: "Group volunteers take a close look at non-native species in the Coastal Trail." Say: "This is real: a National Park Service photograph from Cabrillo National Monument, NPS Photo/H. Marquez. The park case is ours and made up." Give no hints.

#### 12 min. Paper items

Read each item once, one ask at a time. Children mark, point, circle or draw in the numbered box; write a word label if asked. About 2 minutes an item. Marks: shown, shown with help (after one content prompt), not yet seen.

- **E1, box 1.** Field day: hold up a subject card, then draw the idea.
- **E2, box 2.** Two smaller questions: point to two question cards, draw a fact, point to a word card.
- **E4, box 3.** The print: what belonging means, what members gain, a rule and its reason.
- **E5, box 4.** Two people and how each helps.
- **E6, E6b, E6c, box 5.** Read the park case: what the worker adds, what the volunteers add, why they matter.
- **E7, box 6.** Read it again: the shared task, who assigns and checks, what each volunteer does.

Items E3 and E3b are scored after class from day 8's chart. No class time.

#### 13 min. Claim and hearing

Review: in kindergarten you learned a claim, "I think ___, because ___." Ask (s01-E8): "What makes a group work well? Use something in the photograph and caption and something from our tidy-up record." Children draw the claim and both details for 3 minutes. Then hear six at a time at a side table, 2 minutes a group (about 20 seconds a child), asking one why where a box is unclear. The others finish drawing. Collect the pages. A child not heard is heard on day 10. "Groups are good because I like them" is error E. Fair rules, shared jobs and helpful leaders all meet with a detail.

### Other rooms

The materials line names the stand-in for the print.

### At home

**Swap.** No print: use a photograph of people working together that your child has not seen.

**Skip.** The park case, the subject cards and the hearing groups.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Show the photograph and read its caption. Add no hints.
  **Say.** "What are two people doing? How does each help?" Expect: looking, pointing; each helps the group learn together.
  **Say.** "Say two smaller questions about a school field day." Expect: Who sets out the cones?
  **Say.** After a made-up case where a worker checks a patch is safe and gives three volunteers a place to start: "Why do volunteers matter when they are not in charge?" Expect: the work is not done without them.
  **Say.** "What makes a group work well?" Expect: a claim, a detail and because.
- **10 min. Respond.** Note one part to practise: a role, a rule with its reason, or a detail.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: E5, E6 and E8 only. Longer: ask for a second rule with its reason.

### Words and supports

Words: role, rule, duty, goal, volunteer. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." Pointing, drawing and a familiar language all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 shows each child's marked work from day 9's items and the class counts, then forms at most four groups by the largest counts: error C (E5, E6), D (E4), E (E8), F (E1), G (E2), H (E3). Children not heard today are heard on the exit.
