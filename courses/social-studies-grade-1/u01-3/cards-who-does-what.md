# Who-does-what cards (day 7)

Six cards from the three stories of the day, set onto three mats by pairs. Fifteen pairs work at once; the teacher visits five pairs. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except where a card names one.

## The three mats

- **Guides.** Picture: a pointing hand.
- **Does a part.** Picture: a hand holding a tool.
- **Helps the community.** Picture: a ring of small houses.

## The six cards

1. The firefighter who points to the list of jobs. Fits: guides.
2. The firefighter who cleans equipment. Fits: does a part.
3. The librarian who shows where books go. Fits: guides.
4. The student helper who puts books on the cart. Fits: does a part.
5. The park staff member who supervises. Fits: guides.
6. The volunteers who pick up trash. Fits: does a part, or helps the community.

## Using the cards

Several cards fit two mats: all six also help the community. Accept a mat when the pair gives a reason. A pair that says only the first person matters shows error C; ask what would be missing without the others.
