# Teacher key, the blank strip, our town

Teacher only. Keep apart from sheet-strip-our-town.md; never hand to children.

## Key

**Teacher key.** Many is more than three squares of ten; some is one to three. The strip says nothing about what any neighbor believes or whether any neighbor is a citizen. If no published count is clear, use the Holly Run strip and say the class has no count of its own yet.
