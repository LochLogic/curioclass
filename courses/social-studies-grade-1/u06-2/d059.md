# Day 59: What holds us together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the timeline, Constitution page and Hazel Town cards. Keep exit card 59 closed. Say: "Hazel Town is made up." If asked whether everyone was always treated fairly: no; people worked to change unfair laws.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed error B on day 58, 5 minutes: two cards, "weather changes a coat" and "weather does not teach a word". The class sorts three new cards: a rain jacket, a word learned at school, a hat for sun. If few missed it, a small group while the rest see the unit 5 board.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: a holiday, from kindergarten, is a day a community sets aside to remember something. Show: "July 4 remembers the Declaration of Independence." Lay the two supplied dated cards on the timeline: "1776, the Declaration of Independence" and "1787, the Constitution". **Ask.** "What does this holiday remember? Does everyone celebrate in the same way?" Expect: independence; no. A miss joins the small group.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

United means joined together as one. Review: a timeline, read left to right, from kindergarten. The Constitution is the paper that says how our government is set up; read its opening words: "We the People of the United States." A law is a rule for everyone in a place. Show Hazel Town: Mr. Pell plays a drum in the park, Ms. Quill sings at her window, and both stop at a law card, "look both ways". Review: Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Pip says error C: "To be one town, everyone must do the same thing." Correct: "They share the day and the laws, and keep the day in their own ways." Words with pictures: united, law, holiday.

#### 9 min. Supported work

Pairs sort eight cards into "everyone shares" (the Constitution paper, Independence Day, a law card, a classroom flag) and "neighbors differ" (a kite, a food, a song, a way to say hello). 3 minutes, each pair saying why for two cards. Then pairs draw one thing people share. Meanwhile hear the fourth six at your table, 40 seconds each (4 minutes), card closed. Last 2 minutes: the retrieval small group.

#### 3 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 59. [[s06-X59]] [[s06-X59b]] Say: "These are practice pictures. Pine Town is made up."

#### 2 min. Look back

The class says one thing people share and one way they differ. "Tomorrow: how people in our country are different."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each word on a scrap of paper.

**Skip.** Pip, the pairs and the heard six.

- **10 min. Shared.** Say what united means. Show the Constitution words and a law, such as look both ways.
  **Say.** "What do neighbors share, even when they celebrate in different ways?" Expect: a day and the laws; they need not celebrate alike.
- **15 min. Sort.** Your child sorts the cards into "everyone shares" and "neighbors differ".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X59]] [[s06-X59b]] Show the card. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four cards. Longer: a second law.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: united, law, holiday. Frame: "They share ___, and they differ in ___."
