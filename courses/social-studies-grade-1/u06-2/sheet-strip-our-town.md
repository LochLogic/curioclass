# Blank strip: our town (day 60)

Name ______________________

A row of ten empty squares, a title line "Neighbors in ______________ who ______________", and a line "Where the number comes from: ______________" for the town's own public figure and the page it came from. Two blank answer lines: "Shaded: ___" and "That is: some / many".

**Use:** the teacher fills the strip from a published count for the town (a language spoken, or where people were born), never from the class, and writes a county label if the town's figure is missing. Read aloud, count, say "some" or "many". No percent sign. No child's name, home, language or birthplace is used or asked.
