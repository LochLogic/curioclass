# Repair cards and fresh-try cards, section 6.2

Each repair card puts a wrong idea beside a right one. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most five minutes, a small group when few missed it. No card reuses an exit or end case.

## C: belonging needs sameness (day 60 return and repair)

**Pip:** "To be one town, everyone must celebrate the same way." **Right:** "They share the day and the laws. They keep the day in their own ways." **Retry:** a fresh town, Thorn Cove, invented: one neighbor plays a fiddle and one picks apples; both follow a market law. "What is the same for both?"

## F: Native nations disappeared

**Pip:** "Native people lived long ago and are gone." **Right:** "The Cherokee Nation governs itself today." **Retry (day 61):** one new calm line from the nation's page in our words, "the nation makes its own laws today", and a fresh question: "What does the page say about today?"

## J: everyone arrived by choice

**Pip:** "They chose to go." **Right:** "In 1838 the United States sent soldiers to force the Cherokee people from their homelands. It was not a choice." Said plainly, once, with no picture of the removal.

## L: ancestry or a count tells belief

**Pip:** "The strip shows what each neighbor believes." **Right:** "A strip counts. An account told by the people themselves tells more." **Retry:** a new strip for Poplar Lane, invented, with four squares shaded, "neighbors who moved here this year"; "What does it show? What can it not show?"

## G: "I like it" is evidence

**Pip:** "I like Rowan Town, so it is united." **Right:** "The source shows ___." **Retry (day 62):** two new cards, one a liking and one a detail ("a sign says the town hall is open to all"); point to the detail.

## M: one detail shows both sides

**Pip:** "The park shows they are different too." **Right:** "Name a separate detail for each." **Retry (day 62):** a fresh card, Briar Fields, invented: neighbors speak many languages and share one fire station. "What shows united? What shows different?"
