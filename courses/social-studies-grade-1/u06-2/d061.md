# Day 61: A claim and a detail

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the cards and frames. Keep exit card 61 closed. Say: "Rowan Town and Tansy Town are made up." Look for: something shared; something different; a detail on the card; "I like it" kept apart from evidence. Teacher keys: key-sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md, key-cards-rowan-town-and-tansy-town.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed them on day 60: error F, 2 minutes: read a new calm line, "the nation makes its own laws today"; **Ask.** "What does the page say about today?" Error J, 2 minutes: say once, "The soldiers forced the Cherokee people away; it was not a choice," with no picture. Error L, 1 minute: a new strip, Poplar Lane, four of ten shaded, neighbors who moved here this year; "What can a count show? What can it not show?" Else a small group.

#### 5 min. Model a sourced claim

Nothing new is taught. Review: a claim is what you say is true; evidence is what we saw or read that shows it (unit 3); "I think ___, because ___" (kindergarten). Show Rowan Town: a crossing sign, and the caption "Neighbors in Rowan Town sing different songs. All follow the same crossing rule." Claim: "Rowan Town is united." Say: "The source shows everyone follows the same crossing rule. This supports united because they share a rule." Then point to a second detail: different songs. Review: Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Pip says error G: "I like Rowan Town, so it is united." Pip says error M: "The crossing rule shows they are different too." Correct: a liking is not evidence; each claim has its own detail. Words with pictures: evidence, claim.

#### 12 min. Supported work

Pairs have Tansy Town, a market drawing: two stalls with different foods and a board, "Keep the lane clear." No caption is read. Each pair points and fills the frame twice, for united and for different, and says it aloud: about 5 minutes. The pair swaps and listens. Hear the second six at the exit cards, 40 seconds each: 4 minutes. A child may draw or dictate.

#### 3 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 61. [[s06-X61]] Say: "These are practice pictures." Read the card aloud.

#### 5 min. Co-write

Answer "How can people in the United States be united and different?" in two sentences, each with a detail from day 59 or 60: the Constitution, a language, the nation's account. Write them on the board.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No frame: say the three sentence starters aloud.

**Skip.** Pip, the pairs and the heard six.

- **10 min. Model.** Show the Rowan Town card. Say: "The source shows ___. This supports united because ___."
  **Say.** "What does the card show? What does that support?" Expect: the same crossing rule; united.
- **15 min. Try.** Your child points to details on the Tansy Town card and says one claim for united and one for different.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X61]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one claim. Longer: a second detail.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: evidence, claim. Frame: "The source shows ___. This supports ___ because ___."
