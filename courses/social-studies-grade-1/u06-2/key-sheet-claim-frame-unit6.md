# Teacher key, the claim frame sheet, unit 6

Teacher only. Keep apart from sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md; never hand to children.

## Key

**Teacher key.** Box 1 names a detail that is on the card, not a liking. Box 2 is united or different. Box 3 joins the two. Four look-fors: names something shared; names something different; points to a detail on the card; keeps "I like it" apart from evidence. Drawing, pointing and dictation count.
