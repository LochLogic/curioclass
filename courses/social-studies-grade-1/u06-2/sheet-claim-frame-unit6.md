# Claim frame (days 61 and 66, unit 6)

Name ______________________

Four boxes, each with a small picture clue and a line. Box 1, a magnifying glass: "The source shows ______________." Box 2, an arrow: "This supports ______________." Box 3, a link: "because ______________." Box 4, a door: "So ______________." Beside the frame, a word bank with a picture for each word: united, different, law, nation, language, evidence.

**Use:** Read aloud. A child points to a card for box 1, says boxes 2 and 3, and says or draws a closing. An adult may scribe. On day 61 pairs hold a filled-in frame beside the card. On day 66 the frame is blank and no model is shown.
