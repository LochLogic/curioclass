# Class store flyers (day 84)

Say aloud: "These people, shops and things are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print. Tokens are paper pieces, each worth one dollar; no real money. Every price is made up. These flyers cannot tell the price of anything in a real shop.

## Flyer one: made this week by an invented class committee (teaching, 9 minutes)

A ruler, 1 dollar. A crayon box, 2 dollars. A glue stick, 3 dollars. A paper pad, 4 dollars. One service: a paper-folding show, 4 dollars. Each price tag is joined by a line to its picture.

**Source questions, aloud:** Who made it? The class committee. When? This week. What for? To show the store's prices. A second flyer made last year, with different prices (a ruler 2 dollars, a crayon box 3 dollars): which would you use today, and why? (The new one, because prices may change.)

## Flyer two: My Flyer Page practice (supported work, 7 minutes)

A stamp, 8 dollars. A notebook, 11 dollars. A clay lesson, 9 dollars. Sort each offer as a good or a service, point to each price, and mark the higher price.

## Pip

Pip says: "The big picture costs more." Right: the number on the tag is the price. Pip says: "The 4 on the flyer is the price of everything." Right: each price sits by one offer; follow the line from the tag to its picture. Pip is making a mistake on purpose.
