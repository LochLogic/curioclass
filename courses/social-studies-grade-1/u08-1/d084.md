# Day 84: Reading a price tag

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang flyer one; copy flyer two for each pair. Return each My Earning Page with a picture note. Keep exit card 84 closed. Say: "These flyers and prices are made up."

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

1 minute: return My Earning Page, finished on day 82. 3 minutes: if many made error B on day 83's exit, lay an object card beside an action card on two new pairs: boots and polishing boots for someone; a tin whistle and teaching a song. **Ask.** "Which is a good? Which is a service?" Expect: the boots and the whistle; the polishing and the teaching.

#### 9 min. Teaching first

Review from kindergarten: price, what a buyer gives, counted in tokens. New: a **flyer**, a page of offers with price tags; each tag is joined by a line to one offer. Flyer one, made this week by an invented class committee: a ruler 1 dollar, a crayon box 2, a glue stick 3, a paper pad 4, and a paper-folding show 4, a service. Read each tag with its picture. Review: who made it (the committee), when (this week), what for (to show prices). **Ask.** "A flyer made last year has a ruler at 2 dollars. Which would you use today?" Expect: this week's, because prices change. It is made up: it cannot tell the price of anything in a real shop. Pip says "The big picture costs more" (error K) and "The 4 is the price of everything" (error L); the class corrects Pip by following each line from tag to picture.

#### 15 min. Supported work

5 minutes: revise My Earning Page, one change or addition each. If many show error H, the first 2 minutes are a strip of twelve month boxes: Tavi, a harbor clerk with a yearly salary; one token card on each month. 7 minutes: pairs use flyer two, a stamp 8 dollars, a notebook 11, a clay lesson 9; each child fills My Flyer Page, sorting each offer to good or service, writing its price and circling the higher price. Hear the fourth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 7). 3 minutes: partners say "Which costs more? How do you know?"

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 84 (1 minute). [[s08-X84]] The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed flyer: write prices on scraps of paper.

**Skip.** The pairs and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look.** Make a made-up flyer: a spoon 6, a bell 9, a haircut 8.
- **15 min. Read.** Your child points to each price and circles the highest.
  **Say.** "Which costs more? How do you know?" Expect: the bell; 9 is more than 6 and 8.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X84]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: no partner talk. Longer: circle the lowest price too.

### Words and supports

Words: flyer, price, costs more. Frame: "The ___ costs more, because ___ is more than ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 85 opens with two new tags and pictures to match if the exit showed error K (size) or error L (one tag for everything); then it asks what a person does when tokens do not reach.
