# Exit cards, days 81 to 84

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the rotating six. Say aloud first: "These people and shops are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a teaching card, a repair card or a flyer.

## Day 81 exit card

**Pictures:** Suri on a ladder mending a roof, with a clock beside the picture; Bram raking leaves in a friend's yard, no clock. **Read aloud:** "Suri mends roofs for a building company and is paid by the hour. Bram rakes leaves in a friend's yard for fun, and no one pays him." **Mark:** circle the person who is employed. **Spoken (one ask, chosen by the record sheet):** "Why is Suri employed?" or "What is Suri's pay called?"

## Day 82 exit card

**Pictures:** Mara at a market table with clay bowls and a price tag; Jun stacking books on a library cart. **Read aloud:** "Mara makes clay bowls and sells them at a market table. Jun stacks books at a library for one afternoon as a volunteer and is not paid." **Mark:** circle who earns by selling something made. **Spoken (one ask):** "How does Mara earn?" or "Why does each of a mail carrier, a dentist and a tailor need a different skill?" (three small pictures)

## Day 83 exit card

**Pictures:** a hand-poured candle; a grown-up carrying boxes up a flight of stairs, with a neighbor at the top. **Read aloud:** "A hand-poured candle. A grown-up is paid to carry boxes up the stairs for a neighbor." **Mark:** circle the service. **Spoken (one ask):** "Why is it a service?" or "Why is the other card a good?"

## Day 84 exit card

**Picture:** a pretend shop flyer with a large pencil and a tag 5, and a small towel and a tag 7, each tag joined to its picture by a line. **Read aloud:** "The pencil costs 5 dollars. The towel costs 7 dollars." **Mark:** circle the higher price. **Spoken (one ask):** "Why is that the higher price?" or "Which price sits on the pencil?"
