# Day 81: Who is paid for work?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the cards and tokens. Staple the student page to My Earning Page: it is box one. Keep exit card 81 closed.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Day 80 ended unit 7 (s07-X80b); no readiness check. Hook (made up), 1 minute: Pilar holds 9 tokens; a drum costs 7, a clock 6: 13 together, more than 9. Post "How should people choose what to buy and what to save?" (day 85 returns to it). Review from kindergarten: work, skill and the "I think ___ because" frame. **Ask.** "A made-up grown-up cares for a baby; another builds a fence. Point to both kinds of work. What does each need to know?" Expect: both are work; each needs a skill. If many say caring is not work (error A), spend 2 of the 4 minutes setting a paid worker beside an unpaid one.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Pilar paints signs for 3 tokens an hour; 3 hours is 9 tokens: **wages**. Noor guides museum visitors; her job agreement sets her pay for the year, paid in the same part each month: **salary**. Hal makes hats and sells them at a stall; buyers pay him: he earns by selling. New, with pictures: **employed**, has a job and is paid for it; **income**, money you receive for work. Review: earn and pay, from kindergarten. Say once: this unit follows money from work; money can come other ways. Model: "Pilar is employed, because the card shows her paid for her hours." Pip says "A person who plants flowers at the school gate for fun is not working, because nobody pays" (error A) and "Noor gets all her year's pay on one day" (error H); the class corrects Pip.

#### 13 min. Supported work

Tables of five, six worker cards read aloud: a cashier and a carpenter paid by the hour; a ranger and a map-maker paid a salary; a woman who sells mittens she knits; a man who sells tomatoes he grows. 5 minutes: sort the cards to wages, salary or sells, saying "I think ___ because the card shows ___." 8 minutes: each child draws box one, a worker paid by the hour with the pay in tokens. Hear the first six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes): "How is your worker paid?" Practice, not scored.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 81 (1 minute). [[s08-X81]] [[s08-X81b]] Say: "These people are made up." The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw Pilar, Noor and Hal on paper.

**Skip.** The tables and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look.** Show a made-up picture of a sign painter paid 2 tokens an hour.
- **15 min. Draw.** Your child draws a worker paid by the hour, with the pay in tokens.
  **Say.** "Is this person employed? How is the pay counted?" Expect: yes, paid for the work; by the hour, wages.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X81]] [[s08-X81b]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the cards, then the exit. Longer: draw a salary worker.

### Words and supports

Words: employed, income, wages, salary. Frame: "I think ___ because the card shows ___." A child may pass.
