# Unit 8 record sheet: earning, buying and saving

Write each child's name down the left. The spoken why of each daily exit is heard from a rotating six: the first six on days 81 and 86, the second six on days 82 and 87, the third six on days 83 and 88, the fourth six on days 84 and 89, the fifth six on days 85 and 90, and the sixth six on day 91. Keep a column naming which six each child is in. Each X column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling, placing a token or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Mark what is seen on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child not heard stays "not yet seen", never guessed.

Also keep a column for each written record and its date: My Earning Page (item 1), My Flyer Page (item 2), My Choice Mat (item 3), My Two Shops Page (item 4), My Saving Page (item 5) and My Bank Page (item 6). For each, note the first version and the revised version apart. Only the revised record is scored, on day 92. The practice claim of day 91 is item 8's practice record and is never scored.

The End columns are the day 92 observation, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Record paper evidence and spoken evidence apart, and write evidence beside it, such as a dictated phrase. Items 1 to 6 are scored from the revised records of days 84, 86, 88, 90 and 91; items 7 and 8 use the day 92 packet. On day 92 the sheet names who still lacks a spoken explanation for items 1 to 6, most missing first, at most ten children.

For day 93, a zero column per error letter picks at most four groups, the most-missed letter first. Work of days 81 to 91 is practice, not scored. A child may pass. Never write a count of reminders, never post this sheet where it ranks children, and never record a child's home, family, money, body, belief or background; nobody is asked.
