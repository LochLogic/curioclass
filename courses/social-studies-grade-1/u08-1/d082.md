# Day 82: A chain of workers

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the bread cards on a strip. Give each table the jam cards. Keep exit card 82 closed. Say: "These people are made up."

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

If many made error A on day 81's exit, the 3 minutes are the repair: two new cards, a grown-up caring for a young child at home and a paid school cleaner. **Ask.** "Is it work? Is it paid?" Expect: both are work; only the cleaner is paid. 1 minute: post "How do people earn?" and show Pilar, Noor and Hal.

#### 9 min. Model the chain

Bread, made up. The baker bakes loaves with an oven and is paid wages. The carrier takes loaves on a hand cart to the stall and is paid wages. The seller sells at the stall and is paid a salary each month. Name each one's skill, tool and pay. Each learns one part well, so the work goes faster and better: they **specialize**. That is not a ranking of jobs. Review from kindergarten: skill and tool. Say: "An unpaid cook at home feeds people too; that work counts, with no pay." **Ask.** "How does the baker earn? How does the seller earn?" Expect: wages; a salary. Pip says "Everyone in the bread chain does the same work" (error I) and "Only people paid by the hour earn income" (error J); the class corrects Pip from the cards.

#### 15 min. Supported work

Tables of five. 7 minutes: three jam cards, read aloud: a grower who sells fruit; a jam maker paid wages; a shop seller paid a salary. Set them in order (3 minutes), name each skill, tool and way of earning (2), draw one worker and the tool on the student page (2). 8 minutes: finish My Earning Page: box two a salary worker, box three a worker who sells what they make, copying wages, salary and income from the board onto the right boxes. Hear the second six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes inside the 8): "How does your worker earn?" Collect the pages tomorrow; this is item 1's record.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 82 (1 minute). [[s08-X82]] The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three workers in a row on paper.

**Skip.** The tables and the heard six.

- **5 min. Chain.** Tell a made-up chain: a farmer grows wheat, a miller grinds it, a baker bakes.
- **15 min. Draw.** Your child draws one worker and the tool.
  **Say.** "What does this worker know how to do? How is the worker paid?" Expect: one skill; wages, a salary, or by selling.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X82]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the bread chain, then My Earning Page box two. Longer: a fourth worker in the chain.

### Words and supports

Words: specialize, skill, tool. Frame: "The ___ earns ___, because ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 83 opens in 2 minutes with a new set of three worker cards if the exit showed error I (they all do the same work) or error J (only hourly pay is income); then it sorts goods and services.
