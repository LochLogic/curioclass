# Day 83: Things you hold, things people do

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the cards and hoops. Keep exit card 83 closed. Say: "These things are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

1 minute: collect My Earning Page. 2 minutes: review from kindergarten, goods and services. **Ask.** "Sort a pear and a haircut. What does the buyer receive?" Expect: a pear is a good; a haircut is a service; the buyer gets a pear, or a haircut done. 2 minutes: if many made error I or J on day 82's exit, the 2 minutes are the repair and retrieval takes 1: a made-up corn chain: a farmer who sells corn, a mill worker paid by the hour, a stall seller paid a salary. Point to how each is paid, then each skill.

#### 9 min. Teaching first

Goods: a loaf, a hat, a wooden spoon. Services: a haircut, mending a shoe, a train ride. Review from kindergarten: goods, services, buyer and seller. New, with a picture each: the **maker**, who makes a good. Say: the thing a service uses is still a good, such as the scissors in a haircut. Model: "I think the train ride is a service, because the picture shows someone taking the rider along." Pip says "A haircut is a good, because I paid for it" (error B); the class corrects Pip: a loaf is held, a haircut is done for you.

#### 14 min. Supported work

Tables of five. Six goods (a clay jug, a bucket, a woolen sock, a bar of seed bread, a wooden comb, a paper fan) and six services (a drum lesson, washing a neighbor's car, a story hour, sharpening a knife, fixing a wheel, a boat ride). 6 minutes: each child sorts cards and says "I think ___ because the picture shows ___." 5 minutes: each draws a service they could do for a classmate, such as holding the door, and a good they could make from paper; no one is asked about home. Hear the third six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes) while the others finish.

#### 2 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 83 (1 minute). [[s08-X83]] The same six answer the spoken ask, 10 seconds each (1 minute).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No hoops: draw two circles on paper.

**Skip.** The tables and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look.** Lay out six made-up picture cards, three things and three things people do.
- **15 min. Sort.** Your child puts each card in a circle.
  **Say.** "Is it a thing you hold, or something someone does?" Expect: a good; a service.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X83]] Read the card aloud. Your child circles and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort six cards, not twelve. Longer: a good that a service uses, drawn.

### Words and supports

Words: good, service, maker, buyer, seller. Frame: "I think ___ because the picture shows ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 84 opens with a new object card beside an action card if the exit showed error B (a child circles the good, or says a paid job is a good); then it reads prices on a class flyer.
