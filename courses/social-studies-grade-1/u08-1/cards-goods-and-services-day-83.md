# Goods and services cards (day 83)

Say aloud: "These people, shops and things are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print. Tokens are paper pieces, each worth one dollar; no real money.

## Teaching cards (9 minutes)

Goods: a loaf, a hat, a wooden spoon. Services: a haircut, mending a shoe, a train ride. Words with pictures: goods, services, buyer, seller, maker. **Say:** the thing a service uses is still a good: the scissors in a haircut. **Model:** "I think the train ride is a service, because the picture shows someone taking the rider along."

## Twelve cards for each table (supported work, 14 minutes), two hoops

Goods: a clay jug, a bucket, a woolen sock, a bar of seed bread, a wooden comb, a paper fan. Services: a lesson on playing the drum, washing a neighbor's car, telling a story hour for visitors, sharpening a knife, fixing a wheel, a boat ride across a lake.

## Draw (5 minutes)

Each child draws one service they could do for a classmate, such as holding the door or helping carry, and one good they could make from paper. No one is asked about home.

## Pip

Pip says: "A haircut is a good, because I paid for it." Right: a haircut is a service, because someone does it for you; a loaf is a good, because you hold it. Pip is making a mistake on purpose.
