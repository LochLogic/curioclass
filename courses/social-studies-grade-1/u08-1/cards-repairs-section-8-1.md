# Repair cards, section 8.1

Each repair card puts a wrong idea beside a right one. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most three minutes (day 84, five revision minutes), or a small group when few missed it. Nobody is asked to say, stand or sing. No card reuses an exit or a flyer.

## A: is it work? is it paid? (opening of day 82, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "Unpaid work is not work." **Right:** "Work with no pay is still work; being employed means a job where someone pays you." **Retry:** two new cards, a grown-up caring for a young child at home and a paid cleaner at a school. For each, point to "is it work?" and "is it paid?".

## I and J: skills and pay (opening of day 83, 2 minutes)

**Pip:** "Everyone in the chain does the same work." "Only hourly pay is income." **Right:** "Each worker does a different part and needs a different skill; wages, a salary and selling what you make all bring income." **Retry:** one new set of three cards, a corn chain: a farmer who grows corn and sells it, a mill worker paid by the hour who grinds it, a stall seller paid a salary each month. Point to how each is paid, then to each one's skill.

## B: a thing or something done (opening of day 84, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "A haircut is a good, because I paid." **Right:** "A thing you hold is a good; something one person does for another is a service." **Retry:** two new pairs, an object card beside an action card: a pair of boots and polishing boots for someone; a tin whistle and teaching a song.

## H: a salary in parts (the first 2 of day 84's revision minutes)

**Pip:** "Noor gets all her year's pay on one day." **Right:** "A salary is an agreed amount paid in regular parts." **Retry:** a strip of twelve month boxes and one token card for each month; a new worker, Tavi, a clerk in a harbor office, agreed a salary for the year. Place a card on each month.

## K and L: tags (opening of day 85, 2 minutes)

**Pip:** "The big picture costs more." "The 4 is the price of everything." **Right:** "The tag number is the price; each tag belongs to one offer." **Retry:** a new flyer with a large spoon tagged 3, a small bell tagged 6 and a medium cup tagged 5, tags joined by lines. Point from each tag to its picture, then say which price is higher.
