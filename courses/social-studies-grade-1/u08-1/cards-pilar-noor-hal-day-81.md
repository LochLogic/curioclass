# Worker cards, day 81: Pilar, Noor and Hal

Say aloud: "These people, shops and things are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print. Tokens are paper pieces, each worth one dollar; no real money. Rests on sources 81A and 81B for the pay words; every person and amount is made up.

## The hook (1 minute)

**Picture:** Pilar, a sign painter, with 9 paper tokens in her hand. Beside her, two things: a drum with a tag of 7 and a clock with a tag of 6. **Read aloud:** "Pilar has 9 tokens. She wants the drum and she wants the clock. Can she pay for both?" The teacher counts 7 and 6 together as 13, which is more than 9, and says the problem comes back on day 85. Post the question "How should people choose what to buy and what to save?"

## Three picture cards (teaching, 10 minutes)

1. **Pilar paints signs for a shop.** The card shows Pilar with a brush and a clock face on the wall showing three hours, and 3 tokens beside each hour: 3 hours, 9 tokens. **Read aloud:** "Pilar is paid 3 tokens for each hour. She worked 3 hours, so she is paid 9 tokens. Pay counted by the hour is called wages." Label: wages.
2. **Noor guides visitors at a museum.** The card shows Noor with a group, and a calendar strip of twelve months with a token card on each month. **Read aloud:** "Noor's job agreement says her pay for the year. She is paid a part of it in the same amount on the same day every month. That is a salary." Label: salary.
3. **Hal makes hats and sells them at a stall.** The card shows Hal at a stall with hats and a tag on each hat. **Read aloud:** "No shop pays Hal. Hal earns by selling the hats he makes." Label: sells what he makes.

**Words with pictures:** employed (has a job and is paid for it), income (money you receive for work), wages, salary. Review: earn and pay. **Model:** "Pilar is employed, because the card shows her paid for her hours."

## Six worker cards for each table (supported work, 5 minutes)

Sort each card to wages, salary or sells what they make. Read each aloud: a cashier at a movie house paid by the hour; a park ranger paid a salary each month; a woman who knits mittens and sells them; a carpenter paid by the hour; a map-maker at a museum paid a salary each month; a man who grows tomatoes and sells them at a roadside table.

## Pip

Pip says: "A person who plants flowers by the school gate for fun is not working, because nobody pays." Right: that is work with no pay. Pip says: "Noor gets all her year's pay on one day." Right: a salary is paid in regular parts. Pip is making a mistake on purpose.
