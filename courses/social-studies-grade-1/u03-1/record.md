# Unit 3 record sheet: leaders

Write each child's name down the left. The spoken why or question of each daily exit is heard from a rotating six: the first six on days 20 and 25, the second six on days 21 and 26, the third six on days 22 and 27, the fourth six on days 23 and 28, the fifth six on days 24 and 29. Keep a column naming which six each child is in. Each E column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling, placing, joining or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard.

Day 20 also has a retrieval record: three marks apart for each child, judged the character, asked a question, gave a reason. Recheck the questions on day 21 and the reasons on day 22.

The End columns are the day 30 observation, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Every look-for in a row is needed for shown. Write evidence beside each code, such as a dictated phrase. Only the exchange (the last item) is heard group by group: six pairs, twelve children, on day 30; four more pairs and the E31 spoken ask on day 31. A child not heard stays not yet seen until the rotation reaches them.

Day 28 and day 29 pieces and exchanges are practice, not scored. Written feedback on the day 29 piece is returned on day 31, and one improvement is named for each child. The day 30 items are scored only from each child's own unaided answers on cases not seen before. Record paper evidence and spoken evidence apart. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders, and never post this sheet where it ranks children.
