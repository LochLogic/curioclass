# Day 21: What did the leader do?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the Muddy Path pictures in order. The six: second six on the record sheet. Four pairs each take record A, B or C.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

If many missed day 20's exit, lay the Ms. Hale cards in the team ring (error A) and name the shared task, keeping the playground ready; a small group takes the rest. Match the lunch, line and art helper cards to what each does and why (error F). Recheck wonders: show the bridge painter; each child says or points to one wonder.

#### 3 min. Words with pictures

Review: group, people together for something (unit 1); wonder and change, make something different (kindergarten). Question openers on the board: **who, what, how**.

#### 7 min. Model questioning

Say The Muddy Path is made up. Show the four pictures. Cover faces and ask each from one picture. Ms. Okoye is a neighbor and the leader.

**Ask.** What did the leader do? What did the group do? What changed? Expect: gave out jobs; laid the stones; the muddy path is now dry.

Say once: a question asks what someone did or what changed. "Do you like muddy shoes?" asks what we like, and the pictures cannot answer it.

#### 4 min. Co-write the supporting questions

Review: the question board and supporting questions (unit 1). Write: What responsibility does a leader take? What did each leader from history and their group change? Which actions show good leadership? Volunteers point to a picture that could help each. List a fact and a word beside each.

#### 7 min. Pairs, and hearing the six

Twelve pairs take a made-up record, ask one question about the leader and one about the group, and draw one, with at most a word label. You write each question. The six come to your table, a minute each, to mark exit card 21 and answer the spoken ask.

#### 4 min. Exit

The twelve pairs mark exit card 21. [[s03-X21]] [[s03-X21b]] Read aloud both questions. Spoken to the six: "Ask one question about what the leader did." Look for: the question about the leader's actions circled, and a question about what someone did.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: draw stick people.

**Skip.** The partner records.

- **5 min. The path.** Draw three pictures: a muddy path, neighbors laying stones, a dry path.
  **Say.** "What did the leader do? What did the group do? What changed?" Expect: gave out jobs; laid stones; the path is dry.
- **15 min. Your turn.** Draw a leader handing out jobs for a snowy sidewalk. Your child asks one question about the leader and one about the group.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X21]] [[s03-X21b]] Draw a leader giving out jobs and neighbors lifting a clock hand. Your child circles the question about the leader: "How did the leader organize the repairs?" or "Was the clock pretty?"
  **Say.** "Ask one question about what the leader did." Expect: a question about an action.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one record, one question. Longer: ask a third question, about what changed.

### Words and supports

Frames: "What did the leader do?" "What did the group do?" Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 22 meets Harriet Tubman. If many asked what we like, such as "Was the clock pretty?" (error E), it opens by sorting six questions into asks what someone did and asks what we like. Reasons are rechecked there.
