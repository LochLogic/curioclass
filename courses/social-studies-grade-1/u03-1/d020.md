# Day 20: A leader is in the team too

## Teacher page

### Before class

Post a wall strip for three words. Swap action card 5 for a table-list card: a helper writes who wipes which lunch table. Seat day 19 leftover-need children by your table. The six: first six on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook

Say they are made up. Show Ms. Reyes, a principal who listens and says who will carry what, then Captain Bo, who shouts while the others work. Review the four source questions in a breath. Post "What makes a good leader?" Nobody acts out Bo.

**Ask.** What do you notice?

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review: in kindergarten you asked "What did the character do? How do you know?" Tell The Play Leader; pairs answer, you mark what you hear.

**Ask.** Who is in charge? What shows care? What do you wonder? "I think ___ because ___" about Kit.

Three marks apart. If a third miss one, tell The Sandbox Leader.

#### 3 min. Words with pictures

Review: member, one person in a group (unit 1); in charge, someone whose job it is to decide for the group; responsible (kindergarten). New: **leader**, a member who takes on extra responsibility; **responsibility**, a job you take on that others count on.

#### 6 min. Worked example

Mr. Tran, a made-up crossing guard, stands in the team ring with two crossing team members: a member. He guides children across, encourages the shy ones and organizes the line. Post **encourage**, help others feel they can; **organize**, sort out who does what; **guide**, show the way or help the group choose. Lay Ms. Reyes in the ring: extra, not above. A vest only helps us see him; a shout is not the group's job.

**Ask.** What did he do? What shows that? Expect: guiding, encouraging, organizing.

#### 8 min. Sort, and hearing the six

Twelve pairs sort the action cards: 1 and 4 encourage, 2 and the table list organize, 3 and 6 guide. The six come to your table, a minute each, to mark exit card 20 and answer the spoken ask.

#### 4 min. Exit

The twelve pairs mark exit card 20. [[s03-X20]] Read aloud: "Circle the one who is a member of the team and a leader." Spoken to the six: "Why is that person both a member and a leader?" Look for: Mina, with the team, and the extra job of organizing.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw stick people in a ring.

**Skip.** The partner sort.

- **5 min. The ring.** Draw a ring of stick people and put a star on one. Say: "A leader is in the team and takes on extra."
- **10 min. Three actions.** Show with your hands: pat a back (encourage), give each toy a spot (organize), point the way (guide). Your child names one of each.
  **Say.** "What did the leader do? What shows that?" Expect: an action, such as pointing the way for guide.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child fills the student page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X20]] Draw two helpers: one works beside the team and hands out jobs, one stands apart. Your child circles the member and leader.
  **Say.** "Why is she a member and a leader?" Expect: she works with the team and takes an extra job.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four cards. Longer: a new card.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I think ___ because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled Dev (error F), day 21 opens with role cards matched to actions; if many said the leader is not on the team (error A), the leader card goes in the ring. Questions are rechecked on day 21, reasons on day 22.
