# The Play Leader (day 20 retrieval story)

Three made-up pictures, read aloud once. Say first: "This story is made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. One person acts in each picture.

## Picture 1

Kit, a play leader in a made-up class play, stands in front of a row of children holding paper masks. Pip, one child at the end of the row, holds no mask and looks down.

**Teacher reads:** "Kit is the play leader. Kit looks along the row. Pip has no part."

## Picture 2

Kit crouches beside Pip, a hand open and a question mark over Kit's head. A small thought bubble over Pip holds a drum.

**Teacher reads:** "Kit asks Pip, 'What would you like to do?' Pip says, 'I would like to play the drum.'"

## Picture 3

Pip stands at the side of the stage with a drum. Kit stands beside Pip with a thumb up. The row of children has masks on.

**Teacher reads:** "Kit finds a part with Pip. Now Pip has the drum, and the play can begin."

## Questions, and the three marks

Ask, one at a time, and wait for a pointed, drawn or spoken answer:

- "Who is in charge?" (judging the character)
- "What does Kit do that shows care?"
- "What do you wonder?" (a question; any relevant question counts)
- "Say 'I think ... because ...' about something Kit did." (a reason)

Record three marks apart on the record sheet: character judgment, question, reason. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## For the teacher

Kit notices, listens and includes. A miss is met in at most five minutes inside the hook and retrieval with a different made-up story from the repair cards: for more than a third of the class, as a whole class; otherwise a group at the board while the others explain the hook to each other. The kindergarten work behind this retrieval is the "who is in charge" and "I think ... because ..." routine; it is not a verified lesson, only a prior routine to recall.
