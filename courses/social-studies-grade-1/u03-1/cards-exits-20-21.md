# Exit cards, days 20 and 21

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a leader or action card, the Muddy Path, a pair record or a repair card.

## Day 20 exit card: two helper cards

**Picture:** Two cards side by side. Left: Mina, a helper, kneels beside a low table and sorts game pieces into three boxes, then holds out a box to one of two other helpers tidying at the same table. Label: Mina. Right: Dev stands alone at the end of the shelf with a badge on his shirt, his arms folded, no book in his hands. Label: Dev. Under each card, a small circle to mark.

**Mark:** Circle the one who is a member of the team and a leader. **Spoken:** "Why is that person both a member and a leader?"

## Day 21 exit card: the town clock

**Picture:** Three pictures in a row, labelled 1, 2 and 3. 1: a leader stands in front of a town clock with a broken hand hanging down. 2: the leader hands out job cards to three neighbors. 3: two neighbors lift the clock hand up while a third holds the ladder. Under the row, two question cards with a circle each: "How did the leader organize the repairs?" and "Was the clock pretty?" The teacher reads both aloud.

**Mark:** Circle the question that asks about the leader's actions. **Spoken:** "Ask one question about what the leader did."
