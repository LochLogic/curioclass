# Two Leaders (day 20 hook)

Two made-up pictures. Say first: "These pictures are made up. Nobody here is a real person." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. One person acts in each picture; the helpers are shown doing their own jobs. The teacher shows one picture at a time, holds it up for the whole room, and asks only "What do you notice?" Do not judge yet and do not name either picture good or bad.

## Picture A: Ms. Reyes

**Picture:** Ms. Reyes, a principal, stands in the middle of four helpers beside a table of boxes, a hand to her ear, listening to one helper who is pointing. In her other hand is a card with four small drawings: a box, a table, a mop and a book. Two helpers already carry boxes. A small speech mark over Ms. Reyes holds a picture of a box and an arrow to a table.

**Teacher says:** "This is Ms. Reyes. She is a principal in a made-up school. She listens to the helpers and says who will carry what."

## Picture B: Captain Bo

**Picture:** Captain Bo, a team captain with a whistle, stands on a box with one arm up and a wide-open mouth with sound lines. Four helpers carry boxes, push a cart, hold a ladder and sweep. Captain Bo carries nothing.

**Teacher says:** "This is Captain Bo. Captain Bo is a made-up team captain. Captain Bo shouts orders while the others do all the work."

## Then

Post the compelling question on the board with its picture: **What makes a good leader?** Children say what they notice, in any language. Write one or two words beside each picture. Leave both pictures up for day 21 and day 26.

## For the teacher

No child acts out Captain Bo, shouts orders or plays either scene. Captain Bo is a made-up contrast, never a real group or a real child. The picture is not a lesson about being loud; it only sets up the question. Error G: a leader's duty is the group's job (asking, organizing, guiding); a shout is not the job.
