# Repair cards, section 3.1 (days 20, 21 and 22)

New made-up cards for the reopen and retrieval parts. They never appear in an exit or end item. Say first: "These cards are made up." One acting person on each card.

## Day 20 retrieval repair: The Sandbox Leader

Three pictures. 1: Rae, a play leader, looks at Tom, who stands by the sandbox with no shovel. 2: Rae kneels and asks, "What would you like to do?" and Tom says, "I would like to carry the water." 3: Tom carries a bucket; Rae stands beside him. Ask: "Who is in charge? What shows care? What do you wonder? I think ... because ..."

## Day 21 repair for A: inside or outside the team

Two cards to lay on the team ring. Card 1: Ms. Hale, a recess helper, hands out balls inside a ring of three other recess helpers. Card 2: the same drawing with Ms. Hale outside the ring. Children lay the card where Ms. Hale belongs and name the shared task: keeping the playground ready. Ms. Hale is a member who also organizes.

## Day 21 repair for F: match a role to its action

Three role cards and three action cards for children to match, saying what the action is for: lunch helper (hands out trays, so everyone can eat), line helper (points to the end of the line, so no one is left out), art helper (sets out paper, so everyone can draw). Then a pair: one person wears a badge and stands with arms folded, one has no badge and points the way to the lost children. "Which one guides? The badge only helps us see."

## Day 21 recheck of questions: a wonder picture

A person on a ladder paints a bridge rail. Children say or point to one thing they wonder (any relevant question counts).

## Day 22 recheck of reasons: one new picture

Ms. Quill holds a door open for a line of children and smiles at the smallest. Children say "I think she ___ because ___."

## Day 22 repair for E: asks what someone did, or asks what we like

Six question cards read aloud. Children sort them into two boxes.

- Asks what someone did: "What did the helpers do to fix the fence?" "Who carried the water?" "How did the team plan the rows?"
- Asks what we like: "Was the fence pretty?" "Do you like the paint?" "Is the hat nice?"

## For the teacher

Each repair fits the part it lands in: day 21 reopen (A 2 minutes, F 2 minutes, recheck of questions 1 minute), day 22 reopen (recheck of reasons 2 minutes, E 2 minutes).
