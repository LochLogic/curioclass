# Pair record cards (day 21)

Three made-up records, three pictures each, one acting person in each picture. Say first: "These records are made up." Print "made up" on each. Each trio of pairs gets one record. Each pair asks one question about the leader and one about the group, then draws one of them (a word label at most). Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines.

## Record A: the creek clean-up

1. Mr. Bell stands at a creek edge with leaves and cans floating in it. A neighbor holds a rake.
2. Mr. Bell hands rakes and bags to three neighbors and points to the creek.
3. The neighbors pull leaves and cans from the water. The creek is clear.

## Record B: the snowy sidewalk

1. Ms. Park stands at the end of a sidewalk covered with deep snow. A neighbor holds a shovel.
2. Ms. Park writes a list of sidewalk squares and a name beside each.
3. Neighbors shovel their squares. The sidewalk is clear and a child walks on it.

## Record C: the birdhouses

1. Ms. Cruz stands in a bare park beside a pile of boards. A neighbor holds a hammer.
2. Ms. Cruz shows a drawing of a birdhouse and hands boards to three neighbors.
3. The neighbors hang birdhouses on a tree. A bird sits on one.

## For the teacher

The model questions are the Muddy Path questions: What did the leader do? What did the group do? What changed? A question that asks what someone did meets; a question about what is liked is repaired with the sort on day 22 (E). The teacher writes the question in words beside the pair's drawing. Do not use these records again in an exit or end item.
