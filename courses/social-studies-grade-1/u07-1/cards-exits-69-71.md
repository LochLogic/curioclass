# Exit cards, days 69 to 71

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the rotating six. Say aloud first: "These people are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a teaching card, a repair card, a fresh-try card or the day 79 packet.

## Day 69 exit card: Mia, Omar and Sol

**Picture:** three people side by side, one actor each. Read aloud: "Mia was born here. Omar followed the steps the law sets, as an adult, and became a citizen. Sol lives here, goes to our public school and is not a citizen. Sol helps hang cards for the school art show."

**Mark:** Circle who became a citizen later. **Spoken (one, chosen by the record sheet):** "Does Sol have rights at school? Why?" Or: "How did Omar become a citizen?"

## Day 70 exit card: boots at the door

**Picture:** a classroom door that will not open all the way because wet boots are lined up across the mat. No person is in the picture. Beside it, a second card that says "favorite color".

**Mark:** Circle the problem. **Spoken:** "Say one thing we need to find out about the boots."

## Day 71 exit card: the leaking tap

**Picture:** a classroom sink with a drip. A note, read aloud: "March 12 (invented). From the caretaker. Adults are checking the leaking tap." Under it two picture choices: adults checking the tap; a tap already fixed.

**Mark:** Circle what the note shows. **Spoken:** "Who is already working on this?" Or: "What does the note not tell us?"
