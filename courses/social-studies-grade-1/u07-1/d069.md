# Day 69: Who belongs in our community?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Shuffle the cards each time; keep exit card 69 closed. For you: born here means a citizen from birth (Source 69B); one born elsewhere may become one as an adult by following the steps the law sets (Source 69A); every child may learn at a public school (Source 69C, a 1982 court case). Never ask where anyone was born; if a child tells, say "Everyone here belongs."

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Day 68's exit and marks went to the record sheet. Post: "How can our class help our community?" Review from kindergarten: help means make a problem smaller. Review from unit 4: a citizen is a member of a town, city or nation under its rules. Say: "Everyone on these cards is made up."

#### 6 min. Hook: neighbors at a library

Show three cards: Ari shelves books; Bea reads to little children; Cy rolls the returns cart. **Ask.** "Who helps, and what do they do?" Expect: all three, with jobs. No route is named yet.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Lay a mat by each card. Ari was born here: a citizen from birth. Bea followed the steps the law sets, as an adult, and became a citizen. Cy lives here, goes to our public school and is not a citizen: a resident, a person who lives in a place. Say: "Two ways, not every way." Lay two rights (learn at school; be safe) and two responsibilities (respect others' rights; follow school rules), saying "the right a person has", never the right side. All three have all four and belong. If a child says citizens vote: adults vote in elections; our class votes are open to every child.

#### 5 min. Pairs sort

Fifteen pairs place six cards, about 50 seconds a card: Dee and Lia, born here; Teo and Joss, followed the steps; Uma and Rafa, live here, not citizens. Hear the first six pairs. Pip puts Uma off the mats: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose."

#### 3 min. Belonging talk

**Ask.** "What is one thing Cy could do at our school?" Expect: a job. Partners take 30 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 69 (2 minutes). [[s07-X69]] [[s07-X69b]] Say: "These people are made up." Hear the first six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw one way to help at a library.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three made-up people on scraps and three mats on paper. Never use your own family.

**Skip.** The pairs and the heard six.

- **10 min. Three mats.** Show made-up cards: one person born here, one who followed the steps the law sets as an adult, one who lives here and is not a citizen.
  **Say.** "Who belongs in our community?" Expect: all three.
- **15 min. Sort.** Your child sorts six more made-up cards onto the mats.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X69]] [[s07-X69b]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four sort cards. Longer: make a new card.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: citizen, resident, right, responsibility. Frame: "___ lives here and belongs." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow
