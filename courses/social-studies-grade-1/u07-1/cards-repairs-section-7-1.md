# Repair cards, section 7.1

Each repair card puts a wrong idea beside a right one. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most five minutes, or a small group when few missed it. Nobody is asked to say, stand or sing. No card reuses an exit, an end item or the day 79 packet.

## A, N and F: who belongs, who has rights, what helping is (day 70, 5 minutes)

**Pip:** "Only citizens belong." "Only citizens have rights." "Quinn helped so much that Quinn became a citizen." **Right:** "A resident who is not a citizen lives here and belongs. Every child at a public school may learn there. Helping is good, and it is a different thing from the steps the law sets." **Retry:** re-sort the three mats with a fresh trio, such as a card for a person who volunteers at a park, a card for a person who followed the steps the law sets and a card for a resident at the public school. Then a two-card sort, "steps the law sets" and "ways to help", with four new statements read aloud.

## M: a favorite question (day 71, 4 minutes)

**Pip:** "A question about our problem is: which snack do you like?" **Right:** "We ask for a fact we can look for." **Retry:** sort four new question cards onto "a fact we can look for" and "what someone likes".

## J and C: what a note shows, and who is already working (day 72, 5 minutes)

**Pip:** "The note says adults are sweeping the path, so the path is clean." "Nobody helped until we noticed." **Right:** "The note shows adults sweeping. It does not show the path is clean. The adults were already helping." **Retry:** a fresh note on the two-sided "shows / does not show" card, then add one more adult to the helper web.
