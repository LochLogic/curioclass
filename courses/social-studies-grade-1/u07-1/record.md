# Unit 7 record sheet: helping our community

Write each child's name down the left. The spoken why of each daily exit is heard from a rotating six: the first six on days 69 and 74, the second six on days 70 and 75, the third six on days 71 and 76, the fourth six on days 72 and 77, the fifth six on days 73 and 78. Keep a column naming which six each child is in. Each X column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling, placing, matching or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Mark what is seen on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child not heard stays "not yet seen", never guessed.

Also keep a column for each child's part in questioning (day 70), deliberating (days 72 and 73), acting (day 74) and reviewing (day 78), written as what the child did, never as agreement or how much help was given. The day 72 retrieval prompt goes here as a teaching note, one mark, never scored. The day 76 judgment sheet, the day 78 claim and the action log are practice and are not scored.

The End columns are the day 79 observation, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Record each part of items 1 to 6 separately and write evidence beside it, such as a dictated phrase. Spoken asks are heard in five groups of six, about three minutes a group. A child not heard, or with an unresolved explanation, is heard on day 80, about 30 seconds each, and the date goes in the cell.

Day 69 to 78 work is practice, not scored. The day 79 items are scored only from each child's own unaided answers. Record paper evidence and spoken evidence apart. A child may pass. Never write a count of reminders, never post this sheet where it ranks children, and never record a child's home, family, status, belief or background; nobody is asked.
