# Day 71: Who is already helping?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Ask one adult at school for a real, dated note or two lines about our problem; copy it with its date. Write four statements from it: two it shows, two it does not, one saying the work is finished. Keep exit card 71 closed.

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

Read the three questions. If many made error M on day 70's exit, Pip says: "A question about our problem is: which snack do you like?" Say Pip is making a mistake on purpose. Sort four question cards onto "a fact we can look for" and "what someone likes". If few, a small group meets during Our staff note.

#### 6 min. Teaching first: shows and does not show

Read the music-room note, dated March 6, which is made up. Review from unit 3: what kind it is, who made it, when, and what it is good for. Underway means being worked on now, not finished. On the two-sided card, "shows" holds a teacher mended two drums and the shelf drums are next; "does not show" holds whether every drum is mended. Read four statements: a teacher mended two split drums; the music teacher wrote this on March 6; every drum is now mended; no one else helps with the drums. **Ask.** "Does the note show this, or not?" Expect: shows, shows, does not, does not.

#### 8 min. Our staff note

Read our real note and its date; ask the same questions. Fifteen pairs sort your four statements, about 30 seconds a card. Listen to the third six pairs. If no adult wrote anything, say you asked one adult for two lines.

#### 5 min. Who already helps

Draw each adult the note names on the helper web, with a word for what they do. Use roles, such as caretaker, never a child's name. Say: "These adults were helping before we chose." The web stays on the wall.

#### 3 min. What is still unknown

Pairs name one thing our note leaves unknown, 20 seconds. Hear the third six pairs; add two to the question board.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 71 (1 minute). [[s07-X71]] Say: "This note is made up." Then hear the third six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw one adult helper.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed note: write two made-up lines on paper, with a date.

**Skip.** The pairs and the heard six.

- **10 min. A note.** Read a made-up dated note from a grown-up, such as one about a garden they tend.
  **Say.** "What does it show? What does it not show?" Expect: the work underway; whether it is finished.
- **15 min. Who helps.** Your child draws two helpers on a paper web, with a word each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X71]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two statements. Longer: one more thing the note leaves unknown.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: note, shows, underway, helper. Frame: "The note shows ___. It does not show ___." A child may pass.
