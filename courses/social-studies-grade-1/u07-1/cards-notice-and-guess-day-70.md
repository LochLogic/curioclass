# Notice and guess cards, three problem cards, question cards, day 70

Say aloud: "These pictures are made up." Print "invented for practice" on the plant picture and the statement cards. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines.

## The drooping plant (teaching picture)

A class plant in a pot on a sill. The leaves droop. The soil looks pale and dry. The water dish is empty. No person is in the picture.

## Six statement cards (sort with thumbs: up for "I see", down for "I guess")

1. I see drooping leaves. (notice)
2. I see pale soil. (notice)
3. I see an empty dish. (notice)
4. Someone forgot. (guess, and it names a person)
5. The plant is sad. (guess)
6. The plant was never any good. (guess)

Say: a problem is a place or a thing, never a child. A guess may be right, but we look first.

## Three problem cards (real, safe spots at school; the class chooses one)

1. **The ball bin** at recess is often empty.
2. **The art tubs** get mixed up.
3. **The jump ropes** are tangled.

Each child drops one block in a closed cup under a card, or passes. The teacher counts aloud with the class. A tie: agree which goes first; the other stays on the board. Never offer litter, books, or any case printed on an exit card.

## Four question cards (for the repair, error M)

Sort into "a fact we can look for" and "what someone likes".

- How many jump ropes are tangled? (a fact)
- How often is the bin empty on Monday? (a fact)
- Which game do you like best? (what someone likes)
- What is your best recess friend's name? (what someone likes, and it points at a child)
