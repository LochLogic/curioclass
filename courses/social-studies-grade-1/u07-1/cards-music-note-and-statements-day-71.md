# Music-room note and two-sided card, day 71

Say aloud: "This note is made up." Print "invented for practice" on it. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines.

## The note (read aloud, then read by its routine questions)

Dated: March 6 (invented). From: the music teacher. "Two drum skins split last week. I mended both on Tuesday. The shelf drums are next."

Routine questions, one at a time: Who made it? When? What is it good for? (kind, maker, date, use; review of unit 3.)

## Two-sided card (large)

- **Shows:** a teacher mended two drums, and the shelf drums are next (work underway).
- **Does not show:** whether every drum is mended.

Place four small statements on the card, read aloud, one at a time:

1. A teacher mended two split drums. (shows)
2. The music teacher wrote this on March 6. (shows)
3. Every drum is now mended. (does not show)
4. No one else helps with the drums. (does not show)

## Our staff note (the class's own)

The teacher reads a real, dated note or account from an adult at the school about the class's problem, with the date written on a printed copy. If none exists, the teacher asks one adult for two lines and says so aloud. Then fifteen pairs sort four statement cards, which the teacher writes from the note, onto "shows" and "does not show".
