# Library cards, three mats and sort cards, day 69

Say aloud: "Every person on these cards is made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters on each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, one person on each card. Shuffle the cards each time they are shown, and never let a name stand for a route.

## Hook cards (one actor each, read aloud in one sentence)

1. **Ari** shelves books at the library.
2. **Bea** reads a story to little children at the library.
3. **Cy** rolls the returns cart at the library.

Children point and say who helps and what they do. No route is named yet.

## Three mats (large paper, a picture and three words each)

- **A citizen from birth:** a child's footprint and a map of our country.
- **A citizen later:** a person following a path of steps toward a door, labeled "steps the law sets".
- **A resident who is not a citizen:** a person at a school door, labeled "lives here".

Teaching sentences, read aloud: Ari was born here and is a citizen from birth. Bea became a citizen later, as an adult, by following the steps the law sets. Cy lives here, goes to our public school and is not a citizen. We cannot tell by looking; the card tells us because we made it up. There are other routes too, and this class does not need to know any person's route.

## Right and responsibility cards (two each, picture and a word label)

- **A right** (a thing a person may have): learn at school; be safe.
- **A responsibility** (a thing a person does so others can count on them): respect others' rights; follow the school rules.

All three people have them, and all three belong. Say which "right" you mean every time: "the right a person has", never the right side.

## Six sort cards for the pairs (new people, each card one actor)

Each card is read aloud and placed on a mat. Two for each mat, shuffled:

- **Dee** was born here. Dee helps at the school fair.
- **Teo** followed the steps the law sets, as an adult, and became a citizen. Teo sweeps the hall after the play.
- **Uma** lives here, goes to our public school and is not a citizen. Uma helps carry the paint.
- **Lia** was born here. Lia reads to a younger child.
- **Joss** followed the steps the law sets, as an adult, and became a citizen. Joss waters the window plants.
- **Rafa** lives here, goes to our public school and is not a citizen. Rafa holds the door.
