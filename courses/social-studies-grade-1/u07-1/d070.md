# Day 70: What needs attention?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the plant picture, six statement cards and three problem cards. If a spot is not true here, draw a real, safe one, never litter or books. Keep exit card 70 closed. Say: "These pictures are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

If many missed errors A, N or F on day 69's exit, Pip says: "Only citizens belong." "Quinn helped so much that Quinn became a citizen." Say Pip is making a mistake on purpose. Re-sort the mats with a fresh trio: a park volunteer, one who followed the steps the law sets, a resident at the public school. Then sort four statements onto "steps the law sets" and "ways to help": an adult follows the steps; a child holds the door; an adult applies as the law says; a neighbor waters flowers. If few, a small group meets during Baseline.

#### 7 min. Teaching first: notice, then guess

Show the plant picture. Review from kindergarten: "I see ..., I think ..., because I see ...". Noticing: "I see drooping leaves." Guessing: "Someone forgot", which names a person. Review from kindergarten: a problem is a place that needs fixing, never a child. Read the six cards; thumbs up for "I see", down for a guess. **Ask.** "Is this noticing or guessing?" Expect: up for the first three cards, down for the rest.

#### 5 min. Choose our problem

Show: the ball bin is often empty; the art tubs get mixed up; the jump ropes are tangled. Review from kindergarten: a block in a closed cup, or pass. A row at a time, 10 seconds a child; count aloud. A tie: agree which goes first.

#### 4 min. Baseline

Draw what is there (2 minutes). Review from unit 2: write the date, month, number, year. Count one thing aloud, each child making a tally mark: balls in the bin, mixed tubs, or knotted ropes. Day 78 repeats it.

#### 5 min. Supporting questions

Review from unit 1: a supporting question is a smaller question that helps answer the big one. Write three asking for a fact: how many, how often, who uses it. **Ask.** "What do we need to find out about our place?" Expect: a fact, not a child. "What do you like" goes to the wonder wall.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 70 (1 minute). [[s07-X70]] Say it is made up. Hear the second six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others finish drawing.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cups: use pencil marks.

**Skip.** The vote and the heard six.

- **10 min. Notice.** Pick a small shared place, not a person. Say "I see ..." three times.
  **Say.** "What do you see? What do we need to find out?" Expect: a fact, such as how many.
- **15 min. Draw and count.** Your child draws the place, counts one thing and writes the date.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X70]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one question. Longer: a second fact.

### Words and supports

Words: problem, notice, guess. Frame: "I want to find out ___." A child may pass.
