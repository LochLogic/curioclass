# Exit cards, days 11 to 13

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "This is a pretend class." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a Room 4 card, the March page, the June page, the action cards or the year strip cards.

## Day 11 exit card: a pretend class and a November page

**Picture:** A November month page, large, seven columns headed by weekday names, the squares numbered 1 to 30 and empty. Beside it two small wind pictures to place, each the same: a tree bending in the wind. Under the page: "A class noted wind on November 4 and on November 11." Read aloud.

**Mark:** Place both wind pictures on the page, each on its date. **Spoken:** "What repeats?"

## Day 12 exit card: a pretend class and a strip

**Picture:** The past, present and future strip with its three parts left empty. Two squares under it, one headed "yesterday" and one headed "tomorrow", and one small picture to place, of two children clapping. Under it: "The class will clap." Read aloud.

**Mark:** Circle where the clapping picture goes, tomorrow's square or yesterday's square. **Spoken:** "A class sang, listens and will clap. Tell the three in order, using yesterday, today and tomorrow."

## Day 13 exit card: a pretend class and a year

**Picture:** A blank year strip with twelve month names, January at the left. Three cards to place, shown in this order: a drawing of three small figures walking to a door, "class visit, May 7"; a drawing of a paper boat, "boat drawing, August 19"; a drawing of a small chart with a sun and a cloud, "weather chart, February 12". Under them: "All in one year." Read the three aloud, in that order.

**Mark:** Place the three on the strip. **Spoken:** "Which comes first, and how do you know?"
