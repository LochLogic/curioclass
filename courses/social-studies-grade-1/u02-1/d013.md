# Day 13: A whole year

## Teacher page

### Before class

Choose from the real school calendar one event before today and one after, not a birthday. Exit six: the third six. Group A: the first six in the calendar column.

### The day

#### 3 min. Calendar corner

Date today's square. Group A each say one fresh sentence about the class's own days with yesterday, today or tomorrow, about 20 seconds each. Write it under a small drawing.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review day 12 and use its exit (s02-X12b). If many put the coming action on yesterday's square (error B), act a finished action and a coming action, wiping the board, and place each on its square, saying the verb form aloud together (3 minutes). If many used one form for every time (error H), say "wiped, wipes, will wipe" on the three-part strip and point at past, present, future (2 minutes).

#### 6 min. Tie: the year strip

Review: in kindergarten the timeline was a picture strip read left to right. Now it holds a year: twelve month pages in a row, January to December. Say the months together. Place Room 4's pretend events by month first: the bird feeder, January 14; the hats, March 6; the pond visit, June 12. The made-up helper puts the pond visit first because it was read first (error F). **Ask.** Which goes first? Expect: the bird feeder, January is first on the strip. Then move the today marker and place the real earlier and later events. A record is drawn with the solid pen, a plan with the dotted pen. Review: dotted means it has not happened. Dates do not tell us the weather.

#### 14 min. Supported work

Pairs lay six Room 4 cards on a blank strip, earliest first: February 3, crayons counted; March 20, paint colors mixed; March 27, a shelf swept; June 5, paper stars hung; September 9, shells sorted; November 18, a block tower built. Two fall in March: the day orders them. They say two sentences, "___ came before ___ because ___." Hear the third six at the side table, about 80 seconds each, with the exit card.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Say the class is pretend. Read the three cards in order. Children place them on the strip. [[s02-X13]] Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names them.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strip: fold paper into twelve and write the months.

**Skip.** The side table.

- **5 min. Date.** Say today's date as month, number, year.
- **8 min. A year.** Fold a paper into twelve and write January to December. Say them together.
- **9 min. Place cards.** Draw three made-up events with dates, in a mixed order. Your child places them by month.
  **Say.** "Which came first? How do you know?" Expect: the earlier month, not the first card read.
- **3 min. Dotted.** Draw one plan with dots.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X13]] Say the class is made up.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four cards. Longer: find a second month with an event more than a season away.

### Words and supports

Words: year, month, earlier, later. Frames: "___ came before ___ because ___." A point or home-language word counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 begins section 2.2, kinds of sources about the past. It opens with today's exit: error F, order four new dated cards by month, then by day.
