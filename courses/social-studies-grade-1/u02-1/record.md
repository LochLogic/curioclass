# Unit 2 record sheet: clues from the past

Write each child's name down the left. Two rotations run side by side. The spoken why of each daily exit is heard from a rotating six: the first six on days 11 and 16, the second six on days 12 and 17, the third six on day 13, the fourth six on day 14, the fifth six on day 15, and on day 19 the children not heard on day 18 first. The calendar sentences for item 2 are heard from four groups of six: group A on day 13, B on day 14, C on day 15, D on day 16, each revising one sentence from the teacher's picture feedback in the next day's calendar corner, and each group's revised sentence is final by day 17. Keep a column naming which group each child is in and a cell for the date the sentence was heard and the date it was revised. Each E column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling, placing or drawing, and a note when the spoken why was heard. The End columns are the day 18 observation, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. For item 2, a revised sentence from days 14 to 17 counts for the tense looks; a child with none is heard on the invented record of day 18. Record paper evidence and spoken evidence apart. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders, and never post this sheet where it ranks children.
