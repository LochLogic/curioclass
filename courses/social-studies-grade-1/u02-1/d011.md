# Day 11: Our calendar page

## Teacher page

### Before class

Draw our room and write today's date on it as month, number, year. Copy day 10's carry-forward notes to the record sheet, unit 2. Today's six: the first six.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook and question board

Hold the Johnston photograph beside our dated drawing. Say what you see. Read the caption word for word: "Classroom scenes in Washington, D.C. public schools: studying live dog, 5th Division." Say: "Frances Benjamin Johnston took this about 1899. It is another school, not ours. The caption tells which school." **Ask.** Could this photograph tell us about our own school's past? Any child may pass; answer nothing. Post the big question and wonders on the question board.

#### 6 min. Retrieval and repair

Say: "Room 4 is a pretend class." Review: in kindergarten you learned first, next, last, before and after. Show the three morning cards. **Ask.** What happened before opening the book? Expect: the class came in. If many miss, act the three repair cards together and reorder them, 3 minutes; if a few, guide them next part.

#### 9 min. Teaching first

Four minutes: review that kindergarten's calendar had day, week, month and year rows. A calendar is a page people made to keep track of days. Say today's date as month, number, year; the helper draws one real class event on today's square. Two minutes: name the season by what the window shows: "A season word is what we usually see, not a promise for a date." Three minutes: Room 4's March page, a sprout on three squares in one column. **Ask.** What repeats? Expect: the sprout, a week apart. **Ask.** How do you know? Expect: pointing to the drawn squares. The helper says "drawn every day" (error G): point to an empty square; say together "The record does not say."

#### 9 min. Supported work

Pairs lay four June cards on their June page and say "On June ___, Room 4 ___." A card on the first square is error A. Guide retrieval misses for 3 minutes. Then hear today's six at the side table, about 60 seconds each: they place both wind pictures and answer the spoken question; that placing is their mark.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Read the card aloud. The others place both pictures on their dates. [[s02-X11]]

### Other rooms

The materials line names them.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: draw squares on paper.

**Skip.** The photograph, question board and side table.

- **5 min. Look at a calendar.** Show any calendar. Say today's date as month, number, year. Your child draws what they are doing now on today's square.
- **9 min. A sprout page.** Draw a month page with a sprout on three squares one week apart; the rest empty. Say it is made up.
  **Say.** "What repeats? How do you know?" Expect: a sprout, a week apart, on these dates.
  **Say.** "Was it drawn every day?" Expect: the page does not say.
- **6 min. Look outside.** Name the season by what you see.
- **5 min. Four cards.** Draw four made-up events; your child puts each on its date.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X11]] Say the class is made up.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the March page and the exit. Longer: a picture in another column.

### Words and supports

Words: calendar, month, year, season, date. A point counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 teaches past, present and future words, and opens with today's exit: error A, point to a date's square and place two new cards; error G, a drawn square, then an empty one.
