# Action cards and the past, present, future strip (days 12 and 13)

Say first: "These children are made up." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, one actor on each card.

## The strip

A long strip of paper in three parts, left to right, each headed by one word and one small picture: **Past** (a closed door behind you), **Present** (a hand pointing down), **Future** (a dotted arrow ahead). Under the three, the words yesterday, today and tomorrow. A record is drawn with a solid line; a plan, something that has not happened yet, is drawn with a dotted line.

## The teaching triples (day 12, said three ways and acted)

1. **Go.** We went to the library (the real schedule's place, a picture of the door); we go; we will go.
2. **Build.** A child built a block wall; a child builds a block wall; a child will build a block wall.
3. **Paint.** A child painted a fence; a child paints a fence; a child will paint a fence.

The made-up helper then says "yesterday we will paint" and the class repairs it: "yesterday we painted."

## The six action cards (day 12, pairs)

Each card shows one named child and one action, finished, going on or planned. A finished action shows the result; an action going on shows the child doing it; a planned action is drawn with a dotted line.

1. Ana: an empty watering can beside a wet plant.
2. Ben: Ben sweeps the floor, the broom moving.
3. Cam: a dotted drawing of Cam reading at a table.
4. Dee: coats on their hooks, Dee stepping away.
5. Eli: Eli stacks chairs, one in his hands.
6. Fay: a dotted drawing of brushes in a sink.

Pairs lay the six cards on the strip and say "Yesterday ___. Today ___. Tomorrow we will ___." Two cards go in each part.
