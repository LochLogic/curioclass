# Room 4 cards (days 11 to 13)

Say first, every time: "Room 4 is a pretend class. We made it up." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, one actor in each picture, no printed words except a date or a month name. Room 4 never stands for a real class.

## The March page (day 11, the teacher's worked case)

A large month page for March. A small drawing of a bean sprout sits on three squares: March 2, March 9 and March 16. All three are in the same column. Every other square is empty. The teacher points to the drawn squares, then to an empty square, and says what the page tells and what it does not tell.

## The morning cards (day 11 retrieval and repair)

Three picture cards of one invented morning in Room 4: the class comes in through the door, a child opens a book, a child puts the book away. For a repair, three different actions on three new cards: a child hangs a coat, a child sits at a table, a child takes out a pencil.

## The recheck mornings (day 12)

A new set of three cards: the class lines up, the class walks down the hall, the class sits on the rug.

## The June page (day 11, pairs)

A month page for June with four dated cards to place: June 3, a child washes the paint tray; June 9, a child reads a story outdoors; June 17, a child matches buttons; June 24, a child waters the plants. Pairs lay each card on its date and say "On June ___, Room 4 ___."

## The year strip cards (day 13)

- **The teacher's three:** January 14, a bird feeder is hung; March 6, hats are drawn; June 12, the class visits the pond.
- **Six for pairs:** February 3, crayons are counted; March 20, paint colors are mixed; March 27, a shelf is swept; June 5, paper stars are hung; September 9, shells are sorted; November 18, a block tower is built. Two fall in March. Pairs lay them earliest first and say two sentences, "___ came before ___ because ___."

Plans are drawn with the dotted pen and records with the solid pen. The teacher also places today's marker and one earlier and one later real class event from the school calendar.
