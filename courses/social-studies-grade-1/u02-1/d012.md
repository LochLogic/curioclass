# Day 12: Yesterday, today, tomorrow

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick one real place the class went yesterday, goes today and goes tomorrow, such as the library. Today's six: the second six on the record sheet. Keep the exit card closed.

### The day

#### 2 min. Calendar corner

Review day 11: say today's date as month, number, year. The helper draws one real class event on today's square. This corner now runs every day.

#### 6 min. Reopen, hand-back and recheck

Use day 11's exit. If many put both wind pictures on one square (error A), say a date, children point to its square, then place two new picture cards (up to 2 minutes). If many said every day (error G), point to a drawn square, then an empty one, and say together "The record does not say" (1 minute). Recheck (review first, next, last): Room 4, a pretend class, lines up, walks down the hall, sits on the rug. **Ask.** What happened last? Expect: the class sat on the rug.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review: in kindergarten you learned yesterday, today and tomorrow. Show the strip: yesterday is the past, today the present, tomorrow the future. Say each action three ways and act it: "We went to the library. We go. We will go." Then "A child built a block wall. A child builds. A child will build." Then "painted, paints, will paint." Say: "Will tells us it has not happened yet; a plan is drawn dotted." The made-up helper says "Yesterday we will paint" (error H). **Ask.** What should we say? Expect: yesterday we painted.

#### 12 min. Supported work

Pairs lay the six action cards on the strip, two in each part, and say "Yesterday ___. Today ___. Tomorrow we will ___." Dotted drawings are things that have not happened. Hear today's six at the side table, about 80 seconds each, with the exit card.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Say the class is pretend. Read: "The class will clap." Children circle where the picture goes. [[s02-X12]] [[s02-X12b]] Score the time idea apart from the grammar; a point or home language shows it. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Yesterday's square is error B.

### Other rooms

The materials line names them.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw six small actions on paper.

**Skip.** The side table.

- **4 min. Date.** Say today's date as month, number, year.
- **8 min. Three ways.** Fold a paper in three: past, present, future. Walk across the room. Say it three ways.
  **Say.** "Tell me: yesterday we ___, today we ___, tomorrow we will ___." Expect: walked, walk, will walk.
- **8 min. Cards.** Draw six small actions, two done, two going on, two dotted for later. Your child sorts them.
- **5 min. A mix-up.** Say "Yesterday we will paint."
  **Say.** "What should I say?" Expect: yesterday we painted.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X12]] [[s02-X12b]] Say the class is made up.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two actions in supported work. Longer: pairs make up a new action.

### Words and supports

Words: past, present, future, yesterday, today, tomorrow, will. Frame: "Yesterday ___. Today ___. Tomorrow we will ___." A point or home-language word counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 lays dated events on a year strip. Its first minutes use today's exit: error B, act a finished and a coming action; error H, a new action with all three forms.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
