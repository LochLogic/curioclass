# Day 68: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet make at most four groups, the most shared error first: item 1 error A, item 2 errors B and I, item 3 errors D and K, item 4 errors C and E, item 5 errors D and E, item 6 errors C, G and M. Lay out the cards. Keep exit card 68 closed. Say: "These towns and cards are made up." Teacher key: key-sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return evidence

Each child gets their own sheet from day 67's assessment back, one part marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and one next step. No sheet names another child. **Ask.** "Which part will you try again?" Expect: a point to a part.

#### 10 min. Reteach by component

Review from kindergarten: a group re-teaches one move, then tries again. Groups meet you about 2 minutes each; the rest work in pairs from the written move card. Item 1, error A: say what a picture shows and what it leaves out. Item 2, errors B and I: sort weather from what people made or learned. Item 3, errors D and K: say what it stands for and point to the detail; the hope and the limit. Item 4, errors C and E: many different people, one country; saying is a choice. Item 5, errors D and E: say the words, find the detail, say what it expresses. Item 6, errors C, G and M, with error M from day 66's exit: a separate detail for united and for different; a liking is not evidence. Also hear any child not heard on day 67, one symbol or the closing, about 35 seconds each.

#### 10 min. Fresh attempt

Everyone marks exit card 68. [[s06-X68]] Say: "This town is made up." Read the card aloud. Then each child tries the part missed again on a new card, one for each item. Hear the unresolved children, 35 seconds each, up to six at a time in these 7 minutes. Others do a new pair task from the move card.

#### 5 min. Compare work

Pairs set their day 66 frame beside the new try and say one thing that improved. **Ask.** "What changed in your answer? What is your detail?" Expect: a detail now pointed to, kept apart for each side. "Tomorrow starts the next unit: who is a citizen."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a new made-up card for the part that was hard.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at the marked work together. Your child picks one part.
- **20 min. Try again.** Draw a new made-up card for that part.
  **Say.** "What changed in your answer? What is your detail?" Expect: one detail now pointed to.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X68]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group, one try. Longer: a second part tried again.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: claim, evidence, united, different. A child may pass. Frame: "The source shows ___. This supports ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 69 begins unit 7: who is a citizen. It reserves no check; day 68's results go to the record sheet and to unit 7's retrieval.
