# Source cards, day 66

Say aloud first: "These are the cards we know. Nothing new is taught today." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, large print.

Three source cards, read aloud, kept on the board while children work:

1. **The Constitution.** The Preamble's first words, "We the People of the United States," in large print, with the picture of the paper from day 59. Plain words: the paper that says how our government is set up (59A).
2. **The Cherokee Nation's account.** The card from day 60: "The Cherokee Nation governs itself today. It has its own constitution and laws." (60A, in our words)
3. **A symbol source.** The Great Seal's banner, "E pluribus unum", Out of many, one (62A, 64).

The teacher says: shared government, shared history or shared ideals are all good answers, and each needs a detail. The blank claim frame (sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md) is shown with all completed charts covered. No model is shown. Each child draws and labels, or dictates to a partner who listens and says it back: a claim, one piece of evidence (a card pointed to), a reason ("because") and a closing ("So ___").
