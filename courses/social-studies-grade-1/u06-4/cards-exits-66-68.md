# Exit cards, days 66 and 68

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the rotating six. Say aloud first: "These towns are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a teaching card, a repair card, a fresh-try card or any day 67 packet case.

## Day 66 exit card: Larkspur Lane

**Picture:** a drawn picture of the neighbors of Larkspur Lane, a made-up street: a row of houses, a stop sign, and three neighbors each at their own door with a different festival lantern. Caption read aloud: "Neighbors keep different festivals and follow the same national laws." Under it three picture choices: the laws; the festivals; the same clothes.

**Mark:** Circle what these neighbors share. **Spoken (one, chosen by the record sheet):** "What shows they are united? Point to it." Or: "What shows they are different? Point to it."

## Day 68 exit card: Aspen Town

**Picture:** a small courthouse with a flag flying outside it, and two neighbors greeting each other in two different ways, one at a time. Record read aloud: "Neighbors use different greetings. They share one courthouse. A flag flies outside it." Under it three picture choices: the country; a decoration; the courthouse.

**Mark:** Circle what the flag stands for. **Spoken:** "How are the Aspen Town neighbors united?" Or: "How are they different?" Only for a child still unresolved from day 67: "What does the flag stand for, and what shows it?"
