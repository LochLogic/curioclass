# Teacher key, fresh-try cards, day 68

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-repairs-and-fresh-tries-day-68.md; never hand to children.

## Key

- Item 1 (A): Answer: the first shows a busy stall and leaves out the quiet street; the second shows the street and leaves out the stall. Wrong: "the picture shows everyone in Larch Cove."
- Item 2 (B, I): Answer: chalk drawing and "good morning" are made or learned; snowflake and puddle are weather.
- Item 3 (D, K): Answer: the tree stands for shade and welcome; the detail is the bench under the branches; the hope is that everyone can rest, and the limit is that a sign alone does not make sure everyone is welcome.
- Item 4 (C, E): Answer: they are different in how they take part and one in belonging to the same country; saying the words is a choice, and listening quietly is a choice too. Wrong: "Tam is not part of the country."
- Item 5 (D, E): Answer: say the line; the detail is the lamp; it expresses waiting and welcome home. Wrong: "it is a real song everyone knows."
- Item 6 (C, G, M): Answer: united (one lantern) and different (many voices) each with its own detail. Wrong: "I like the lantern."
