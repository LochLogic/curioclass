# Day 66: What holds us together?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the three source cards on the board. Cover every completed class chart and every supplied reason. Lay out the blank frames. Keep exit card 66 closed. Say: "Nothing new is taught today. Nobody is asked to say or sing." Look for: a claim; a card pointed to; a reason; a closing. Teacher key: key-sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 65's exit, error D, 5 minutes, whole class: hum a tune and show the taught phrase card "land of the free and the home of the brave" with no meaning card. **Ask.** "What do the words say? What detail? What does the song express?" Expect: free and brave people; the people; the song says the people are free and brave. Retry with "purple mountain majesties". Else a small group.

#### 3 min. Reopen

Read aloud: "What holds a country of so many different people together?" Point to the three cards: the Constitution from day 59, the Cherokee Nation's account from day 60, and the seal's banner from day 64. Say: shared government, shared history or shared ideals are all good answers, and each needs a detail.

#### 4 min. Step back

Show the blank frame. Review: a claim, a reason and a closing from day 28, and evidence from day 61. The boxes are: claim; the card shows; because; "So ___". Show no model.

#### 12 min. Independent work

Each child draws and labels, or dictates to a partner who listens and says it back: a claim, one card pointed to, a reason and a closing. Hear the fourth six dictate, 40 seconds each: 4 minutes. The rest draw. A child may point.

#### 3 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 66. [[s06-X66]] [[s06-X66b]] Say: "This street is made up." Read the card aloud.

#### 3 min. Look back

Children tell their partner their closing. "Tomorrow: new pictures and a new story. Nothing new is taught. Nobody is asked to say or sing."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No frame: say the four words aloud: claim, card, because, so.

**Skip.** The pairs and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look.** Read the three source cards aloud: the Constitution, the Cherokee Nation, "Out of many, one".
- **20 min. Say and draw.** Your child draws a claim, a card, a reason and a closing; you listen and say it back.
  **Say.** "What holds a country of so many different people together? What shows it?" Expect: government, history or ideals; a detail from a card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X66]] [[s06-X66b]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the claim and one card. Longer: a second answer with a different card.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: claim, evidence, reason, closing. Frame: "The source shows ___. This supports ___ because ___. So ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 67 is a check on new pictures and a new story; nothing new is taught. Error M (one detail taken to show both sides) from this exit waits for day 68.
