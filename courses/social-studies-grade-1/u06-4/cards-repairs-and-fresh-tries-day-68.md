# Written moves and fresh-try cards, day 68

Each written move is a one-page card for a group working without the teacher: the wrong idea, the right idea and two cards: a reteach card for the group and a separate fresh-try card. Say aloud: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Groups come from day 67's record sheet by component, at most four, the most shared error first. One child follows this cycle with an adult. No fresh-try card reuses a day 67 case, an exit case, a reteach card or a teaching case.

## Item 1 (A): one picture shows everyone

Move: say what this picture shows, then what it leaves out, with a second view beside it. **Reteach card:** a harbor town, Pike Town, invented, with a fishing boat, and a second view of its empty beach. **Fresh-try card:** Larch Cove, invented: a busy market stall in one picture and a quiet side street in the second.

## Item 2 (B, I): weather and writing

Move: sort weather from what people made or learned. **Reteach card:** a sunny market with a painted sign, invented. Sort: a painted sign; a puddle; a wave hello; a cold wind. **Fresh-try card:** a school yard, invented. Sort: a chalk drawing; a snowflake; a spoken "good morning"; a rain puddle.

## Item 3 (D, K): naming and equal welcome

Move: say what it stands for and point to the detail; say the hope and the limit. **Reteach card:** a town sign at Cobb Hill, invented, with a bald eagle holding an olive branch (not a flag: the exit card shows a different symbol); the Statue of Liberty drawn small with a torch only, and the limit cards. **Fresh-try card:** an Elm Hollow town sign, invented: a tree with wide branches over a bench, the words "Sit and rest."

## Item 4 (C, E): sameness and saying the words

Move: many different people, one country; saying is a choice. **Reteach card:** Birch Ridge, invented, where two neighbors with different beliefs both help paint the town hall fence. **Fresh-try card:** Rae and Tam, invented, watch a parade. Rae says the pledge words; Tam listens quietly. Both are neighbors in the same country.

## Item 5 (D, E): recognizing a tune

Move: say the words, find the detail, say what it expresses. **Reteach card:** use the taught phrases "purple mountain majesties" and "land of the free and the home of the brave" with their title cards, and hum the tunes once more. **Fresh-try card:** a made-up song title, "Harbor Light," with one printed made-up line, "the lamp that waits for the boats."

## Item 6 (C, G, M): the claim

Move: the frame, a separate detail for united and for different, a liking is not evidence. **Reteach card:** the three source cards of day 66 (the Constitution, the Cherokee Nation's account, the seal's banner); the child chooses one and fills the frame. **Fresh-try card:** a fourth card, a Kestrel Town sign, invented: a lantern with the words "Many voices, one lantern" and four small speech bubbles in four different languages. The child fills the frame: a claim, the card shows, because, so. Frame: sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md.

## Fresh attempt (the exit)

Mark the Aspen Town card (cards-exits-66-68.md). Then each child tries again the component they missed on the fresh-try card for that item; the teacher hears up to ten children, 35 seconds each, in two sittings of five.
