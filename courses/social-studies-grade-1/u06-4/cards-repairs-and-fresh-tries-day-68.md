# Written moves and fresh-try cards, day 68

Each written move is a one-page card for a group working without the teacher: the wrong idea, the right idea and a new card. Say aloud: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Groups come from day 67's record sheet by component, at most four, the most shared error first. One child follows this cycle with an adult. No card reuses a day 67 case, an exit case or a teaching case.

## Item 1 (A): one picture shows everyone

Move: say what this picture shows, then what it leaves out, with a second view beside it. New card: a harbor town, Pike Town, invented, with a fishing boat, and a second view of its empty beach.

## Item 2 (B, I): weather and writing

Move: sort weather from what people made or learned. New cards: a sunny market with a painted sign, invented. Sort: a painted sign; a puddle; a wave hello; a cold wind.

## Item 3 (D, K): naming and equal welcome

Move: say what it stands for and point to the detail; say the hope and the limit. New cards: a town sign at Cobb Hill, invented, with a bald eagle holding an olive branch (not a flag: the exit card shows a flag); the Statue of Liberty drawn small with a torch only, and the limit cards.

## Item 4 (C, E): sameness and saying the words

Move: many different people, one country; saying is a choice. New card: Birch Ridge, invented, where two neighbors with different beliefs both help paint the town hall fence.

## Item 5 (D, E): recognizing a tune

Move: say the words, find the detail, say what it expresses. New cards: use the taught phrases "purple mountain majesties" and "land of the free and the home of the brave" with their title cards, and hum the tunes once more.

## Item 6 (C, G, M): the claim

Move: the frame, a separate detail for united and for different, a liking is not evidence. New card: the three source cards of day 66 again (the Constitution, the Cherokee Nation's account, the seal's banner); the child chooses a different card than on day 66 and fills the frame: a claim, the card shows, because, so. Not a made-up town record, because the exit card is one. Frame: sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md.

## Fresh attempt (the exit)

Mark the Aspen Town card (cards-exits-66-68.md). Then each child tries again the component they missed, on a new card above; the teacher hears the unresolved children, 35 seconds each.
