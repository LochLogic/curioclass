# Day 67: One country, many people: the check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet closed until now. Cover every completed model in the room; only blank frames stay. Plan the five groups of six from the record sheet.  Teacher key: key-packet-day-67.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Orientation

Say: "Today is a check. You may point, circle, draw and say. Nobody is asked to say the Pledge, stand or sing; you may listen quietly. Willow Town is made up. The two streets are drawings of real places. The symbols are real." Read each item aloud in its first minute, with no hints. Mark each part shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Item 6

Read the Willow Town record, a new Constitution line and a new seal view aloud twice. [[s06-E14]] Circle what shows these neighbors are united. [[s06-E15]] Circle what shows they are different. [[s06-E16]] Each child draws or dictates a claim, a detail, a reason and a closing on the student page.

#### 5 min. Item 3

Four new symbol views. [[s06-E6]] [[s06-E6b]] The flag. [[s06-E7]] The seal. [[s06-E8]] The White House. [[s06-E9]] The statue. Each child circles the part that shows what each stands for. 

#### 4 min. Item 1

Sources A and B. [[s06-E1]] Circle the weather on each picture. [[s06-E2]] The lunch words in A's caption; B's caption does not say. [[s06-E3]] [[s06-E3b]] Could everyone there live exactly like this picture? Hear the first group, 20 seconds each child: 2 minutes.

#### 3 min. Item 2

The globe view with its key. [[s06-E4]] A token on Sapporo and on Singapore; one made or learned feature in each picture. [[s06-E5]] One weather feature in each, and how weather might change daily life. Hear the second group: 2 minutes.

#### 4 min. Item 4

A new seal view and phrase cards. Read the Pledge once, as a text. [[s06-E10]] [[s06-E10b]] Out of many, one: what it means. [[s06-E11]] Liberty and justice for all; whether someone must say it to belong. Hear groups three and four: 4 minutes.

#### 4 min. Item 5

Hum a short passage of each tune once, one after the other, for the whole class. Nobody hums back. [[s06-E12]] Match the first tune and a phrase card, with its meaning. [[s06-E13]] [[s06-E13b]] Match the second. Hear the fifth group: 2 minutes. A child who cannot hear a tune matches title and printed words; record the tune as inaccessible, not incorrect.

After class, about twenty minutes: write each part on the record sheet and group the errors for day 68: item 1 A, item 2 B and I, item 3 D and K, item 4 C and E, item 5 D and E, item 6 C, G and M. Collect the pages at the end of item 6 and keep them for day 68. Judge meaning and evidence, not drawing.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a street in the snow and a street in the rain; make a flag and a seal on scrap paper. Say they are made up.

**Skip.** The tunes, the globe view and the heard groups.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Give no hints.
  **Say.** "What does this symbol stand for? Which part shows it?" Expect: the country, or the president's work, or liberty; a detail.
  **Say.** "How are these neighbors united? How are they different?" Expect: a shared law or road; languages or beliefs.
- **10 min. Respond.** Note one part that was hard; ask again on a new made-up card.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 3 and 6. Longer: a second claim.

### Words and supports

Read every item aloud. Words: symbol, motto, song, united, different, evidence. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 acts on this check: groups by component, with errors A, B, I, D, K, C, E, G and M, including error M from day 66's exit.
