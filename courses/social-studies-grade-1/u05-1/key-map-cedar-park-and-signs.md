# Teacher key, map cedar park and signs

Teacher only. Keep apart from map-cedar-park-and-signs.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Cedar Park map (invented)

**Teacher key (north points right).** North is the right edge, so the swing set at the left is south of the pond (the opposite of north), and the bench at the right is north of the pond. East is down the page here, so the gate below the pond is east of the pond, and west is up the page, so the slide above the pond is west of the pond. The model sentence is "The swing set is south of the pond", and the model reason is "I know because the indicator says north is this way, not up the page."

### Address cards (retrieval, unmarked)

**Teacher key.** This is review of the kindergarten address. Rebuild the nesting with the cards in a small group if a child misses a part.
