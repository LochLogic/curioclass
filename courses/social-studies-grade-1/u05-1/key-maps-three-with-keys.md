# Teacher key, maps three with keys

Teacher only. Keep apart from maps-three-with-keys.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Map 1: the school map (ours)

**Teacher key.** The symbols stand for the places in the photographs. Make the map from the real school before use. Do not show anyone's home or street. Name its kind, maker, date and purpose: a drawn map, made by us, this year, to find places at school.

### Map 2: Maple Park (invented)

**Teacher key.** The pairs place each card on its symbol, trace to the key and say "I found ___ because the key says ___." The square is a picnic table on this map only.

### Map 3: Linden (invented)

**Teacher key.** The square is a picnic table on Maple Park and a school on Linden. The same shape means different things; read each map's key. Never say a shape "always" means something.
