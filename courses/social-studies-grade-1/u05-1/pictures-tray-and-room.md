# Day 44 pictures: the tray, the classroom and the playground

Say aloud first: "These pictures are made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no color needed. These are the teaching pictures for day 44 and day 45's fresh tray. They are not the day 44 exit card.

## Picture 1: the tray from the side and from above

**Real objects:** a paper tray outline on the table with a cup, a block and a book on it. Hold the real tray at eye level (from the side) and then stand over it (from above).

**Picture, side view:** a flat row drawing of the cup, the block and the book standing in a line, the tall cup hiding part of the block. Label: from the side.

**Picture, from above:** the tray as a rectangle. Inside it: a circle for the cup, a square for the block, a rectangle for the book, each in the place it sits on the real tray. Label: from above. This is the map of the tray.

## Picture 2: our classroom from above

**Picture:** a rectangle for the room. Marks, flat and simple: a door (a gap in one wall), a large oval for the rug, small squares for tables in two rows, a long thin rectangle for the coat hooks, a small square for the teacher's desk.

**Source line to say aloud (for the tray photograph or drawing):** who made it (the teacher), when (this week), why (to show a place from above).

## Picture 3: the playground (retrieval, unmarked)

**Picture:** a playground drawn from above. A slide, a bench, a swing set and three trees. Place the bench next to the slide and put two trees on either side of the path between the swings and the slide. Label nothing.

**Three retrieval prompts, one at a time:** "What is between the trees?" (the path) "What is next to the slide?" (the bench) "Show what the bench picture stands for." (a child touches the bench mark, then the real bench in the room if there is one, or says it)
