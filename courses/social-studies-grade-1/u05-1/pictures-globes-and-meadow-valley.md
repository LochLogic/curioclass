# Day 46 pictures: paired globe views and Meadow Valley

Say aloud first: "Meadow Valley is made up." Print "invented for practice" on the Meadow Valley pieces. The globe views are the real Earth, drawn by us. Thick lines, large print. A globe is optional; these printed views serve.

## Paired globe views (retrieval and supported work)

**Picture:** two round views of the Earth side by side, labeled View 1 and View 2. View 1 shows a large area of land in tan with the ocean to its left and right in blue. View 2 is the opposite side of the Earth and is mostly blue water with a few small tan land areas. A small dot labeled Denver sits on the land in View 1. A key box: tan is land, blue is water, a dot is a city. No continent or ocean names are printed; those are met on day 50.

## Meadow Valley map (invented)

**Picture:** a valley drawn from above. A boxed key: stripes are a field, small squares are houses, brown rings are a hill, blue is water, a thick line is a road, a rectangle is a barn. On the map: a hill at the top, a blue stretch of water at the right, six small squares in a loose group near the road, three striped fields beside them and one barn. A title: Meadow Valley. A direction indicator.

**Eight picture cards** for the pair sort: house, field, barn, road, hill, water, a stand of trees, a patch of marsh. Two columns to sort into: made by people, was already there.

## The paper relief

One low folded-paper relief of Meadow Valley, shared by the six tables, two minutes per pair: a raised hill in folded brown paper, blue paper for the water, cut paper stripes for the fields and cut small squares for the houses glued flat. A different relief is used on day 51.
