# Day 47: North, south, east and west

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the four signs with the compass held away from steel; if it will not settle, use the school's site plan and its north arrow, and say which. Say: "Cedar Park is made up." Teacher keys: key-sheet-mini-map.md, key-map-cedar-park-and-signs.md, key-maps-three-with-keys.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Unmarked, spoken. Review: in kindergarten you learned that an address nests: street, town, state, country. Read the invented school address on four cards, "18 Birch Street, Milltown, Colorado, United States". **Ask.** "Point to the part that names the street." Then town, state, country, four children. Expect: the right card. Pairs put the four cards in order. If day 46's exit showed error S or R, that small group takes the repair card at the back table.

#### 11 min. Teaching first

All stand and point to each sign together, N, S, E, W; no child is singled out. A compass needle points toward north; the signs are set from it. On the school grounds map, the direction indicator is the mark that shows where north is. Turn the map: the indicator turns with it. North is where it points, not the top of the page. Show Cedar Park, north pointing right. **Ask.** "Which way is north here?" Expect: toward the right edge. Model: "The swing set is south of the pond. I know because the indicator says north is this way, not up the page." **Ask.** "Is the bench north or south of the pond?" Expect: north.

#### 8 min. Supported work

Pairs, a paper Cedar Park and tokens: put a token east of the pond, then say the direction of the swing set from the bench. Expect: south. You hear the fourth six, a minute each. **Ask.** "Which direction is the slide from the pond?" Expect: west.

#### 4 min. Written task and share

Each child writes one direction word beside the arrow on the mini map, or an adult scribes it. Collect the sheets. One child shows one place.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 47. [[s05-X47]] Read aloud: "Place a token on the shed that is east of the well." The fourth six come to you, 20 seconds each: "Which direction is the gate from the well?" Look for: the indicator, not the page right (error C).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No compass: label four table edges N, S, E and W with a phone compass held away from metal.

**Skip.** The pairs and the heard six.

- **5 min. The signs.** Point to each table edge and say its name.
- **15 min. Which way?** Draw a mat with a cup, a spoon and a book. Draw an arrow for north, pointing anywhere.
  **Say.** "Which direction is the spoon from the cup? How do you know?" Expect: a direction word, from the arrow, not the top of the page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X47]] Show the card. Your child places a token.
  **Say.** "Which direction is the gate from the well?" Expect: south, from the indicator.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the signs. Longer: two directions from one place.

### Words and supports

Frame: "The ___ is ___ of the ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 teaches what a capital is. Its Return acts on this exit: if many chose the page right (error C), rotate a map and trace the indicator to the N sign. It also returns the mini map.
