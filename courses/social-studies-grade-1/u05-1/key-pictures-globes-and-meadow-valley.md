# Teacher key, pictures globes and meadow valley

Teacher only. Keep apart from pictures-globes-and-meadow-valley.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Paired globe views (retrieval and supported work)

**Teacher key.** Children color one land area and circle one water area on each view and find the labelled city. A globe view can have a city named on it, and it shows land and water. The two views are two sides of one round Earth; View 2 has more water than land, and it still has some land. Compare with a flat map: it also shows land and water, but flat.

### The paper relief

**Teacher key.** On the map and on the relief: the fields, houses, barn and road were made by people. The hill, the water, the trees and the marsh were already there. A valley's land and water tell what a place is like: farmland and town where the fields and houses are, wild land where the hill and marsh are. Fields are not natural rectangles; people made them.
