# Teacher key, pictures tray and room

Teacher only. Keep apart from pictures-tray-and-room.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Picture 1: the tray from the side and from above

**Teacher key.** The picture from above shows where each thing sits, so it is the map. The side picture shows what the things look like but hides where they sit. Each mark stands for one object. Say "stands for" while touching the object and then its mark. On day 45 make a new arrangement of the same three objects and let children point to its drawing.

### Picture 2: our classroom from above

**Teacher key.** Children find the door and the rug. The model claim is "I think this map helps find the door, because the door mark is by the rug mark." Adjust the picture to the real room before use so that the door mark is beside the rug mark; if it is not, change the claim to match the picture.

### Picture 3: the playground (retrieval, unmarked)

**Teacher key.** These are review of position words and picture maps. A miss is repaired in the teaching part, not marked.
