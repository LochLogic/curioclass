# Day 45 maps: the school, Maple Park and Linden

Say aloud first: "Maple Park and Linden are made up." Print "invented for practice" at the bottom of those two. The school map is made from our own school. Large print, thick black lines on white, a boxed key on each map. Leave the key visible while the map is used.

## Map 1: the school map (ours)

**Picture:** the school grounds from above, drawn to match the real school. Four to six places only, each with a simple symbol: the front door (a small gap in the building), the playground (a triangle), the office (a square), the gym (a rectangle), the field (a large oval). A boxed key beside the map says what each symbol stands for, with a word label. For each symbol, show a photograph of the real place beside the key so children see the symbol stands for something they know.

## Map 2: Maple Park (invented)

**Picture:** a park drawn from above. A boxed key: a square is a picnic table, a triangle is a slide, a rectangle is a bench, a diamond is a swing set, a dashed line is a walking path, a wavy line is a stream. On the map: three squares near each other under the top edge, a triangle at the far right, two rectangles beside the dashed path, a diamond at the left, and a wavy line running along the bottom. A title: Maple Park. A direction indicator pointing up the page, labeled N.

**Six place cards** for the pairs, each a picture card with a word label read aloud: picnic table, slide, bench, swing set, path, stream.

## Map 3: Linden (invented)

**Picture:** a small town drawn from above. A boxed key: a square is a school, a triangle is a fire station, a rectangle is a shop, a gray stripe is a road. On the map: one square near the middle, a triangle at the left, three rectangles along the top, and two gray stripes that cross. A title: Linden. A direction indicator pointing up the page, labeled N.

## Model sentence for all three

"The map shows ___. I can find it because the key says ___."
