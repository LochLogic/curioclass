# Mini map: one direction word

Name ______________________

Say aloud first: "This mini map is made up." Print "invented for practice" at the bottom.

**Picture:** a small square map of a school yard from above. A key box: a triangle is a slide, a square is a flagpole, a circle is a drum for balls. A large direction indicator labeled N with its arrow pointing to the bottom edge of the page. A short arrow drawn from the flagpole to the slide, with a blank line beside it.

**Task:** Write one direction word beside the arrow to tell where the slide is from the flagpole: north, south, east or west. A word bank of the four words is printed at the bottom. An adult may scribe the word, or the sentence "The ___ is ___ of the ___."
