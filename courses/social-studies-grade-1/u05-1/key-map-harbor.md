# Teacher key, map harbor

Teacher only. Keep apart from map-harbor.md; never hand to children.

## Key

A harbor is a sheltered place by the water where ships can stop. This map shows a harbor at the southern tip of an island in New York State; the harbor is the calm water where the ships and docks are. The compelling question on day 44 is asked and not answered: "Why did people build towns and cities where they did?" On day 49 the Seaport account says ships brought goods, and travelers came by ship, to the city; the map shows the harbor, the ships and the docks, and the account says why they mattered.

Do not add real street names, building names or numbers. Do not draw people. Nothing here says who lived at the harbor first; that is day 49, told by the Lenape layer and the account of the Lenape nations, never by this map.
