# Repair cards, section 5.1

Each card puts a wrong idea beside a right one so the class can see the difference. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most five minutes, a small group when few missed it.

## A: the map is the place

**Pip:** holds up the day 44 classroom drawing from above and says, "This is the room. I can sit on it."

**Right:** the drawing stands for the room, drawn from above and small. The real room is the place.

**Repair:** on a tray of three objects beside their drawing from above, touch each object and then its mark and say "stands for." Then ask the group to say who could sit on the tray and who could sit on the drawing.

## N: a side drawing is a map

**Pip:** shows a side drawing of one cup and says, "This is a map of the table."

**Right:** a map looks straight down on the whole table.

**Repair:** draw the same cup and block from the side, then from above; children choose which shows where each sits on the table.

## B: a shape means the same on every map

**Pip:** points to a triangle on Maple Park and then on Linden and says, "A triangle is a slide everywhere."

**Right:** read this map's key. A triangle is a slide on Maple Park and a fire station on Linden.

**Repair:** trace from the triangle to the key and back on both maps, saying "on this map."

## S: the globe shows only water

**Pip:** holds up globe View 2 and says, "A globe is all water."

**Right:** find a land area and a water area on both views.

**Repair:** paired views again; each pair points to one land and one water on each, then compares with the flat map.

## R: fields and houses are natural

**Pip:** points to the Meadow Valley relief and says, "The fields and houses are natural, like the hill."

**Right:** people made the fields and houses; the hill was there.

**Repair:** sort picture cards into two piles, made by people and was already there: a fence, a sandy beach, a swing set, a rock, a path, a pond with a wild edge.

## I: a globe has no cities

**Pip:** looks at a globe view and says, "A globe has no cities, only land and water."

**Right:** a globe view can have a city named on it.

**Repair:** find the labeled city dot on View 1, say it is a city, and say what the dot stands for.

## C: north is the top of the paper

**Pip:** turns the school map on the table and says, "North is always at the top of the paper."

**Right:** north is where the indicator points. Turn the map and the indicator turns with it.

**Repair:** rotate the map on a table; trace the indicator to the N wall sign each time. Then try a new map, Pool Yard, with north pointing right: a map of a small pool yard with a key (a square is a towel rack, a triangle is a chair) and the indicator pointing to the right edge; place a token south of the towel rack.
