# Day 46: What a place is like

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fold the Meadow Valley relief; six tables pass it on your clap, one minute a table. Say: "Meadow Valley is made up." Teacher keys: key-maps-three-with-keys.md, key-pictures-globes-and-meadow-valley.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Unmarked, spoken. Review: in kindergarten a globe is a model of the round Earth, with land and water. **Ask.** "Which model shows the whole round Earth? Find land and water on both." Expect: the globe views. Review from unit 4: a term record is what a leader did in a set time. **Ask.** "What detail in the term record supported your judgment?" Expect: a deed from the record. Error B from day 45: trace one triangle on two keys, a slide on Maple Park and a fire station on Linden; one minute. A globe miss: compare a flat map.

#### 5 min. Step back

Model once with Meadow Valley, the views and the relief: land or water, town or farmland. "People put houses and fields here: town and farmland. The hill and water were already there." Two words for the sort: made by people, was already there.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs color a land area and circle a water area on the paired card, find the dot that stands for Denver (error I: a globe view can have a city), then read the Meadow Valley key. The relief passes on your clap. You hear the third six at their seats, a minute each. **Ask.** "Point to the hill on the relief. Made by people or already there?" Expect: already there.

#### 8 min. Pairs sort

Eight cards on the map and relief: house, field, barn, road, hill, water, a stand of trees, a patch of marsh. Pairs make two piles. Expect: house, field, barn, road made by people; the rest already there. You meet the group that missed retrieval for three minutes.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 46. [[s05-X46]] Read aloud: "Color one land area and circle one water area." The third six come to you, 20 seconds each: "On the island map and the relief, what did people make to grow food?" Look for: the field symbols, made by people (error S, "all water"; error R, "fields are natural").

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed views: draw an Earth with a land patch and a water patch.

**Skip.** The pairs, the relief and the heard six.

- **5 min. Land and water.** Look at a picture map with land and water. Your child colors the land and circles the water.
- **15 min. Made or there.** Sort picture cards: house, field, hill, water.
  **Say.** "Did people make this, or was it already there?" Expect: houses and fields were made; hill and water were already there.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X46]] Show the card. Your child colors and circles.
  **Say.** "What did people make to grow food?" Expect: the fields.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the sort. Longer: a fence and a beach.

### Words and supports

Frame: "People made the ___. The ___ was already there." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 teaches directions. Its Retrieval gives a small group the repair card for error S (paired views again) or error R (sort the two piles).
