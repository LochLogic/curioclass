# Teacher key, the mini map sheet

Teacher only. Keep apart from sheet-mini-map.md; never hand to children.

## Key

**Teacher key.** The arrow goes from the flagpole to the slide, and the slide sits at the page-top of the flagpole. North points down on this sheet, so south is up the page; the slide is south of the flagpole. A child who writes "north" followed the page top, not the indicator. Judge three look-fors, not a score: the place sits where it was drawn; the direction word fits the indicator, not the page top; the symbol matches the key.
