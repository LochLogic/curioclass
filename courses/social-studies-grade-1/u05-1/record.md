# Unit 5 record sheet: maps and places

Write each child's name down the left. The spoken why of each daily exit is heard from a rotating six: the first six on days 44, 48 and 52, the second six on days 45, 49 and 53, the third six on days 46, 50 and 55, the fourth six on days 47 and 51. Keep a column naming which six each child is in. Each E column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling, placing, coloring or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. On days 50 and 51 the sheet also names which of the two spoken asks each child in the six was given, so the other ask is used the next time.

The retrieval prompts of days 44, 46 and 47 go here as teaching notes, one mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. They are never scored. The day 47 mini map and the day 51 labelled drawing are judged on three look-fors each (the place sits where it was; the direction word fits the indicator; the symbol matches the key; or, for the drawing, the label matches the drawn feature) and are not scored.

The End columns are the day 54 observation, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Every look-for in a row is needed for shown. Write evidence beside each code, such as a dictated phrase. Items 1 to 6 are heard across the paper parts, group by group, five groups of six, about five minutes a group; a second adult hears a second group. A child not heard is heard on day 55, one minute each, one ask, and the date goes in the cell.

Day 52 and day 53 work is practice, not scored, except the revised day 52 map, which is scored on day 54 (item 5) after the written feedback and the day 53 revision. The day 54 items are scored only from each child's own unaided answers on maps not seen before. Record paper evidence and spoken evidence apart. A child may pass; try again another day. Never write a count of reminders, and never post this sheet where it ranks children.
