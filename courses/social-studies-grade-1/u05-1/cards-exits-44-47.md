# Exit cards, days 44 to 47

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a tray, classroom, Maple Park, Linden, Meadow Valley, Cedar Park or repair picture.

## Day 44 exit card: a reading room

**Picture:** two drawings side by side. Left: a reading room seen from straight above: a large rectangle with a round rug, four small squares for tables and three long thin rectangles along one wall for shelves. Right: one shelf seen from the side, tall, with books drawn standing in rows. Under each drawing, a small circle to mark.

**Mark:** Circle the drawing that is a map of the reading room. **Spoken:** "How could the map help someone find a shelf?"

## Day 45 exit card: Sunny Plot

**Picture:** a map of Sunny Plot from above, with a boxed key: a circle is a tree and a rectangle is a bed. On the map, two circles near the left edge and three rectangles in a row. Under the key, two small word-and-picture cards with a circle each: a tree and a pond. The teacher reads both words aloud.

**Mark:** Circle what a circle means, a tree or a pond. **Spoken:** "How can you tell what grows in Sunny Plot?"

## Day 46 exit card: new globe views and Marsh Island

**Picture:** two round views of the Earth side by side with a key box (tan is land, blue is water). Below them, a map of Marsh Island beside a low paper relief of the island. The island map has a key: stripes are a field, small squares are houses, brown rings are a hill. The relief shows cut paper stripes and small squares glued flat on a raised hill.

**Mark:** On the globe views, color one land area and circle one water area. **Spoken:** "On the island map and the relief, what did people make to grow food?"

## Day 47 exit card: Quince Orchard

**Picture:** a map of Quince Orchard from above. A large direction indicator labelled N with its arrow pointing to the left edge of the page. A round well in the middle. One shed is above the well and one shed is to the right of the well; the gate is farther to the right of the well. A key: a square is a shed, a circle is a well, a short thick line is the gate. Three tokens are set beside the map.

**Mark:** Place a token on the shed that is east of the well. **Spoken:** "Which direction is the gate from the well?"
