# Day 33: Choosing a leader

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep exit card 33 closed. The second six are heard. Bag eight ballots: six Mara, two Otis. Say: "These cards are made up."

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

Read the unit question. Day 32's exit, error Q (loudest picks), two minutes: puppet Loud shouts "We paint the fence my way!"; puppet Turns says everyone gets a turn, then each counts the same. Children point to the fair card. Error R (talk never works), two minutes: replay scenery and costumes.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review: you learned vote in kindergarten. A kite club picks a captain and a kite color. Picking Mara or Otis is an election: the club chooses a person for a job. Picking red or yellow is a vote on a matter. Read the rule card: "One ballot each. The same rules for all. Private. Two ballot counters." A puppet says: "Only members near the captain may mark a ballot." **Ask.** "Are the rules the same for all?" Expect: no, so it is not fair. Words: election, ballot, equality (each vote counts the same).

#### 6 min. Count the ballots

Two child counters call each ballot; you mark the tally. Review: you lined up marks in math unit 1. The class reads the longer row: six under Mara, two under Otis. **Ask.** "Why does Mara win?" Expect: six is more than two (error T). Add it to Our Decision Record. Words: tally, majority (the choice with more votes). The puppet who wanted Otis says: "I disagree, I accept the count, and I may ask the counters to check." (error J)

#### 7 min. Supported work

Pairs sort four cards, election or matter: a recycling group picks who carries the bin; picks blue bin or green bin; a bird-feeder club picks its leader; picks apples or pears. Three minutes; then each draws one rule that makes a vote fair, with one label word at most. Four minutes.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 33. [[s04-X33]] Read aloud: "Circle the election." The second six come to you, half a minute each: "A club chair lets her friends vote two times each. What is the problem?" Look for: the chair is an election; extra votes are not equal (error A, error I).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No bag or puppets: use a cup and paper slips.

**Skip.** The counters and the sort.

- **5 min. Two kinds of vote.** Tell a made-up case: a garden club picks its leader, and picks tulips or daisies. Ask which pick is the election.
  **Say.** "Which one chooses a person for a job?" Expect: the leader.
- **15 min. Count.** Mark eight slips, five for one name and three for another, and fold them. Your child draws a mark under each name as you call it, then reads the longer row.
  **Say.** "Who wins? How do you know?" Expect: the longer row; five is more than three.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X33]] Show the chess club card. Your child circles the election.
  **Say.** "A chair lets friends vote twice. What is the problem?" Expect: not equal; each vote counts the same.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the count and the exit. Longer: your own cards.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: election, ballot, tally. Frame: "I disagree, and I accept the count." Pointing counts. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 holds our election. Its warm-up repairs this exit: error A (both are elections), sort four cards, person or thing; error I (extra votes), line up one mark each under two names.
