# Day 34: the garden captain election

Say first: "These two candidates are made up. They are not anyone in our class." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. One person acts in each picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters on each card. The teacher reads every card aloud.

## The job card (read aloud)

"The garden captain organizes the watering sheet and asks the group for ideas. The captain follows the same rules as everyone." A drawing of a watering sheet, and a child asking a group.

## The deed cards (two candidates)

Suri. Picture 1: Suri hands a trowel to a friend. Read: "Suri shares tools." Picture 2: Suri walks past an empty watering can. Read: "Suri sometimes misses watering."
Tomas. Picture 3: Tomas pours water on a row of plants. Read: "Tomas waters when he is on the sheet." Picture 4: Tomas stands alone by the plants, and no one has been asked. Read: "Tomas sometimes forgets to ask for ideas."

In pairs, point to the deed that fits organize and the deed that fits ask for ideas. Nobody says who they will pick.

## The rule card (read aloud)

"One private ballot each. Each ballot counts the same. Two counters count. Everyone can see the tally. The higher total wins. If the totals are tied, talk for two minutes and use one new ballot; if still tied, the two made-up candidates share the job in turns."

## How the election runs

Children mark behind a folder, fold the ballot and drop it in one of two covered boxes. A child may drop a blank slip. Two counters call each ballot while the teacher marks the tally on a large sheet. The class lines up the two rows and says the result together. Nobody is asked how they voted, and the teacher never says a child's choice aloud.

## Look back

Why did our rule make this fair? Which deed might a voter weigh? Record the date, the job, the names, the tally and the result on Our Decision Record. Ask of the record: who made it, when, and how does it help?

## For the teacher

The candidates are made up so no child loses a vote. Note the final tally: the day 40 packet may need a different pair of totals.
