# Exit cards, days 32 to 34

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a hook case, a sort card, the kite club, the garden election or a repair card.

## Day 32 exit card: the drum group

**Picture:** A group of children around a table with a hand drum, two song pictures on the wall, and some children pointing at different pictures. Three choice cards, each with a circle: (1) children talking, then all raising one hand each; (2) one tall child standing with arms wide and the others sitting; (3) all hands up at once, with a crossed-out speech bubble.

**Mark:** Circle how the group decides. **Spoken:** "Why is that a fair way for them to decide?"

## Day 33 exit card: the chess club

**Picture:** Two small scenes. One: a club at a table with a chess board and a ballot box with a name card. Two: a different club at a table with a calendar and a ballot box. Three choice cards, each with a circle: choosing the chair; choosing the meeting day; both.

**Mark:** Circle the election. **Spoken:** "A club chair lets her friends vote two times each. What is the problem?"

## Day 34 exit card: the choir

**Picture:** A choir of children on risers, a covered box, and one child at the side with a thoughtful face. Two choice cards, each with a circle: a child with a hand raised politely, saying I disagree, then standing with the group; a child with a hand over their mouth.

**Mark:** Circle what that child may do. **Spoken:** "Why might private voting help the choir choose?"
