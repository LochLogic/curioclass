# Repair cards, section 4.1

New cards for the warm-up parts of days 33 to 35, used only if many children missed that letter on the day before. Say first: "These cards are made up." Wrong answers are voiced by a puppet or "someone might say", never by a named child. None of these cases appears in an exit card.

## Q and R: day 33 warm-up (4 minutes)

**Q, the loudest picks.** Puppet Loud stands up and shouts: "We paint the fence my way!" Puppet Turns says: "Everyone gets a turn to say, and then each of us counts the same." Show the loud card beside the equal-turns card; the class points to the one where every voice is heard.
**R, talk never works.** Replay the scenery and costumes case from day 32, where talking found a way to do both. Then say: "When talking finds a way both accept, we do not need a vote. When we are still apart and only one can happen, we vote."

## A and I: day 34 warm-up (3 minutes)

**A, every decision is an election.** Sort four cards, person or thing: who leads the recess line (person, election); which game to play (thing, vote on a matter); who carries the first-aid box (person, election); which ribbon color (thing, vote on a matter).
**I, some deserve extra votes.** Line up one mark each under two new names, Ola and Pat, six marks and four marks, and say: each mark is one vote and counts the same; no one's counts twice.

## B and J: day 35 warm-up (4 minutes)

**B, private ballots are never counted.** Re-run one covered box with six ballots: marked privately, dropped in, counted by two counters who call each ballot aloud.
**J, we must not say we disagree.** Two puppets say the pair of sentences: "I disagree, and I accept the count." "I may ask the counters to check."

## T: day 35 warm-up

**T, the name I like wins.** Line up marks for a new pair of names, Jo and Kim: the longer row wins, whichever name anyone likes.

## C: day 35 teaching starts from the job card (inside its office part, 4 minutes)

**C, winning permits any order.** Read the day 34 job card. A puppet says: "The captain says nobody else may touch the hose." The job card says what the captain does; the rules still hold for the captain. Show the two side by side.
