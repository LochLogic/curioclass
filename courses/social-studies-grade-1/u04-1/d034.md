# Day 34: Our garden captain election

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep exit card 34 closed. Note the third six in the election. Say: "These two candidates are made up, not anyone in our class."

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up and repair

Read the job card: "The garden captain organizes the watering sheet and asks the group for ideas, under the same rules as everyone." If many missed error A on day 33's exit, sort four cards, person or thing (recess leader, game, first-aid carrier, ribbon color). If error I, line up six marks under Ola, four under Pat.

#### 5 min. Two candidates

Review: you learned organize and guide in unit 3. Suri shares tools and sometimes misses watering. Tomas waters when on the sheet and sometimes forgets to ask for ideas. Fifteen pairs point to the deed that fits organize and the one that fits asking. Nobody says their pick.

#### 4 min. Rules and ballots

Read the rule card: "One private ballot each. Each ballot counts the same. Two counters count. Everyone sees the tally. The higher total wins." A tie: talk, one new ballot, then share the job. Two lines of fifteen mark behind a folder, fold and drop it in a covered box, six seconds each. A blank slip is allowed. Say: marking is private; counters count all (error B).

#### 6 min. Count and read

Two counters call each ballot as you mark the tally. The class lines up rows; the longer row wins (error T). A child who disagrees says: "I disagree, I accept the count, and I may ask the counters to check." (error J)

#### 7 min. Look back and record

**Ask.** "Why did our rule make this fair?" Expect: one private ballot each, counted the same, a tally all saw. **Ask.** "Which deed might a voter weigh?" Expect: a deed from the cards. Fill Our Decision Record: date, job, names, tally, result. Review: ask who made it, when, and how it helps, as in unit 3.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 34. [[s04-X34]] Read: "Circle what that child may do." The third six come to you, half a minute each: "Why might private voting help the choir choose?" Look for: kind disagreement and acceptance; no pressure, every ballot counted (error B, error J). The rest draw.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No boxes: use two cups. Slips can be scrap paper.

**Skip.** The two lines and the counters.

- **5 min. The job.** A made-up garden club needs a captain who organizes the watering and asks for ideas. Suri shares tools; Tomas waters well. Both are made up.
- **15 min. Vote.** Your child marks one slip in private and folds it; never ask which. Add four folded slips you marked, three for Suri and one for Tomas. Count together, drawing marks in two rows.
  **Say.** "Why was our rule fair?" Expect: one private ballot each, counted the same, counted in front of everyone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X34]] Show the choir card. Your child circles what the child may do.
  **Say.** "Why might private voting help?" Expect: people choose without pressure, and every ballot is still counted.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the count and the exit. Longer: a tie.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: ballot, tally, tie. Frame: "I disagree, and I accept the count." An adult may mark a ballot as the child directs. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow
