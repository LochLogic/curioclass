# Unit 4 record sheet: choosing together

Write each child's name down the left. The spoken why of each daily exit is heard from a rotating six: the first six on days 32 and 37, the second six on days 33 and 38, the third six on days 34 and 39, the fourth six on days 35 and 41, the fifth six on day 36. Keep a column naming which six each child is in. Each E column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling, placing, joining or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard.

Day 32 also has a retrieval record: one mark apart for each child, joined in the class decision talk (shown, shown with help, not yet seen). Day 34 also notes the six's actions in the class election. Never write down how any child voted.

The End columns are the day 40 observation, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Every look-for in a row is needed for shown. Write evidence beside each code, such as a dictated phrase. The spoken parts are heard group by group on day 40, five groups of six, about three minutes a group. A part neither marked on paper nor heard is not yet seen, and is heard in the day 41 fresh try, up to eight children, one minute each. Add the day 38 re-election judgment only to fill a part day 40 did not show.

Day 38 and day 39 pieces are practice, not scored. Written feedback on the day 38 judgment is returned on day 39 and one next step is named for each child. The day 40 items are scored only from each child's own unaided answers on cases not seen before. Record paper evidence and spoken evidence apart. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders, and never post this sheet where it ranks children.
