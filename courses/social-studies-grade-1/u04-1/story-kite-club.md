# Day 33: the kite club picks a captain and a color

Say first: "These cards are made up." Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. One person acts in each picture. Print "invented for practice" in small letters on each card. The teacher reads every card aloud.

## The kite club (four pictures)

Picture 1: a girl, Mara, holds a bag of kite string beside a name card that says Mara. Read: "A kite club picks a captain. The captain carries the supply bag."
Picture 2: a boy, Otis, holds a kite tail beside a name card that says Otis. Read: "Two members would like to be captain, Mara and Otis."
Picture 3: a table with a red kite and a yellow kite. Read: "The club also picks a kite color, red or yellow."
Picture 4: two cards side by side, a person and a kite. Read: "Picking the captain is an election, because the club chooses a person for a job. Picking the color is a vote on a matter, because the club chooses a thing."

## The rule card for the election (read aloud)

"One ballot each. The same rules for all. Private. Two ballot counters." A puppet gives a wrong example: "The oldest member's ballot counts twice." Ask: does every vote count the same? No, so it is not equal.

## The count (eight ballots, six to two)

Eight folded ballots in a bag. Two child counters take turns: one calls the name on each ballot and the other checks it. The teacher draws a mark under that name. The class lines up the two rows of marks and reads the longer row: six marks under Mara, two under Otis. Mara wins; the choice with more votes is the majority. A puppet who wanted Otis says: "I disagree, I accept the count, and I may ask the counters to check."

## Four cards: election, or vote on a matter

1. A walking group picks the leader who carries the map. (election)
2. The same walking group picks which path to walk today. (vote on a matter)
3. A bird-feeder club picks its leader for the spring. (election)
4. The bird-feeder club picks apples or pears for its snack. (vote on a matter)

## For the teacher

The brackets give the answers; the cards are for teaching. Counters rotate and are never the candidates, who are made up. None of these cases is used in an exit card.
