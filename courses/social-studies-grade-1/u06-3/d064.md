# Day 64: Out of many, one

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the cards. Keep exit card 64 closed. Say: "The quilt and the library are made up. Saying the Pledge is a choice. You may listen."

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 63's exit, error K, 5 minutes: two cards, "a hope" and "not equal for all". Read three statements, and the class places each: "The torch stands for liberty." "A law in 1882 kept Chinese workers from coming for ten years." "The poem welcomes tired people." Retry with one more.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Show the seal's banner: E pluribus unum, Latin for "Out of many, one". Review: two bins. The record says it joins the thirteen states into one nation; people say many people, one country. Show the made-up quilt, "Many patches, one quilt". **Ask.** "What are the many? What is the one?" Expect: patches; the quilt. Read the Pledge once, as a text, without standing: "I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one Nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all." Review the meanings from kindergarten: a promise of loyalty; one country together; freedom and fair treatment for all. "Under God" is part of the text; people believe different things, and no one is asked to agree. A court said in 1943 that students cannot be forced to say it. Review: Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Pip says error C: "To be in one country, everyone must believe the same." Pip says error E: "You must say it to belong." Correct: "Many people, different beliefs, one country. Saying it is a choice."

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs place two phrase cards, "Out of many, one" and "liberty and justice for all", with two meaning cards, "many joined as one" and "freedom and fair treatment", point to each detail and say "Out of many, one means ___." About 4 minutes. Show the made-up Fox Town library, sign "Everyone may borrow a book": 2 minutes. Hear the first six at the exit cards, 40 seconds each: 4 minutes.

#### 3 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 64. [[s06-X64]] Read the card aloud.

#### 4 min. Look back

Review: united and different. **Ask.** "What do the readers of the Fox Town library share? What could be different?" Expect: one library; their books. Saying the words is a choice. "Tomorrow: two songs."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each phrase on a scrap and draw a patchwork.

**Skip.** Pip, the pairs and the heard six.

- **10 min. Show.** Show the seal's banner. Say: "Out of many, one." Read the Pledge once, as a text. Nobody has to say it.
  **Say.** "What does out of many, one mean? Must everyone say the Pledge to belong?" Expect: many joined as one; no, it is a choice.
- **15 min. Place.** Your child places each phrase with its meaning and says: "Out of many, one means ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X64]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the motto only. Longer: draw a quilt.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: motto, belong, pledge. Frame: "Out of many, one means ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 65 repairs error C (belonging needs sameness): 3 minutes, and error E (belonging needs saying words): 2 minutes. Then two songs.
