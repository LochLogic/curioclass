# Neighbors over time, day 76

Say aloud: "This record is made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines.

## The record (read aloud)

Long ago, neighbors laid stepping stones across a muddy field so people could cross. Later, town helpers laid a smooth path. Both helped people walk.

## Two pictures

1. **Long ago:** stepping stones across a muddy field.
2. **Later:** a smooth path across the same field.

Place the two pictures in order. Say what changed (stones, then a smooth path; neighbors, then town helpers) and what stayed (someone making a way for people to walk across the field). A row on the chart is kept for "what stayed".

## A helper account (read aloud, day 76)

Say aloud: "A real account could not be checked, so this one is made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters.

Ms. Hale lives on Elm Street. Wet leaves made the sidewalk slippery. Ms. Hale asked the neighbors to help. One Saturday they swept it together. Now the sidewalk is safe to walk on.

Class says the problem (a slippery sidewalk), what she did (asked the neighbors, and they swept together) and a quality (cooperate or caring, with that detail).
