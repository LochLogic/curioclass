# Day 76: Helpers, long ago and now

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the founder and path pictures. Make a source card and sheet for each child, a third each of story, record and log line. Hang the then-and-now chart. Keep exit card 76 closed. Say which story is real.

### The day

#### 3 min. Action routine

One child draws today's dated action log line.

#### 3 min. Warm-up and repair

If many made error G or B on day 75's exit, Pip says: "Dax has the biggest bike, so Dax is the best helper." Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Sort card G's six cards onto what a person has and does (90 seconds). Pip: "She is good because I like her story." Pairs lay a quality card on a detail card and say "the detail is ___" (90 seconds).

#### 6 min. A library founder

Say: "This is a true story from long ago." Read the founder text twice and lay the strip: before, what he did, now. Partners whisper (1 minute); hear six pairs, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "What did Enoch Pratt do for his city, and what quality does it show?" Expect: made a free library so all could borrow; helpful, fair or caring, with that detail. His job is not why (error G).

#### 5 min. Neighbors over time

Read the made-up record: neighbors laid stepping stones across a muddy field; later town helpers laid a smooth path. Review from kindergarten: the then-and-now chart with a row for what stayed; it holds the two pictures. **Ask.** "What changed, and what stayed the same?" Expect: stones, then a path; neighbors, then town helpers; a way to walk stayed (error L). Draw one thing in the row.

#### 4 min. A real helper

Read the made-up helper account once; a real one could not be checked. **Ask.** "What was the problem, what did she do, and what quality?" Expect: a slippery sidewalk; she asked the neighbors and they swept together; cooperate, with that detail.

#### 5 min. Supported work for item 2

Show no frame. Each child circles a quality for the source card (1 minute), then draws the detail (3 minutes). Take dictation from up to six who ask, 30 seconds each. Collect the sheets; write a note on each after class (about 15 minutes). Nobody is rated.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 76 (1 minute). [[s07-X76]] [[s07-X76b]] Hear the third six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw one thing that stayed the same.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: fold a page in two, long ago and now.

**Skip.** The source card and the heard six.

- **10 min. Then and now.** Draw a made-up log seat long ago and a wood bench now.
  **Say.** "What changed, and what stayed the same?" Expect: the bench; someone made a place to sit.
- **15 min. A helper.** Tell a made-up story of a neighbor who plants flowers at the bus stop. Your child says the problem, the deed and a quality.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X76]] [[s07-X76b]] Show the card and read it. Your child places the cards and names a quality.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the founder and the exit. Longer: a second founder quality.

### Words and supports

Frame: "Long ago ___; now ___; the same is ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 77 repairs error L, then lays out near, region and far. Day 78 returns the sheets.
