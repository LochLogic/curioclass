# Section 7.3 opener: good members, long ago, and the same problem elsewhere

## What the section does

Three days, 75 to 77, of 30 minutes. Day 75: what makes a good community member. A quality needs a detail: we say what a person did, then what quality it shows. We judge deeds, never a classmate, and never what a person has. Day 76: people who made their communities better over time, with a library founder, neighbors who laid stepping stones and then a smooth path, and the class's own action log. Day 77: the same kind of problem near, in the region and far away, and the people already working on it at each.

## How the days run

Day 75: action routine (3), warm-up and repair (4), a quality needs a detail (8), a second story (6), our own action (4), exit (5). Day 76: action routine (3), warm-up and repair (3), a library founder (6), neighbors over time (5), a short record of a helper (4), supported work (5), exit (4). Day 77: action routine (3), warm-up and repair (4), near, region, far (6), two packets (8), people there already help (4), exit (5). Every child marks the exit card; the teacher hears the spoken part from one rotating six.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A quality is a way a person is, shown by what the person does.** Judge from a detail: "What did the person do?" before "Do I like it?" Caring, fair, helpful and respectful are review from kindergarten and unit 3. Honest (tells the truth) and cooperate (work together) are new on day 75.
- **A good member is judged by deeds, never by money, fame or status.** A good member may or may not be a citizen. We judge the class's action, never a child, and we do not say our action fixed or caused everything.
- **Long ago and now:** the way may change and the purpose may stay. The founder text and the stepping-stone record show a change and what stayed.
- **A plan or invitation is not a record of what happened.** A dated record shows what was done. Say "plan" or "record" for every card (error J).
- **Near, region, far.** Near is our school and the place on our baseline. A region is a big area made of many towns. Far means another country; invented cards name no real country. People at every place are already working on their own problems, and no place is shown as helpless. No child is asked to act abroad.
- **Facts rest on checked pages.** The founder read-aloud rests on the Enoch Pratt Free Library's own history page (source 76A): Enoch Pratt, a Baltimore businessman, wanted a free library open to all, and it opened in January 1886 with 32,000 volumes and four branch libraries. Nothing else about him is stated.
- **Real news and helper accounts are cut here.** No dated school bulletin or helper account was checked for this course, so none is shipped and none is invented. A teacher may print a dated page from the class's own school or district site, with its address and the date read, and use it beside the invented cards. Without one, the invented cards and the class's action log carry the day.
- **Day 77's packets are made after the class chooses its problem (day 70).** The page names the layout and the plan and record labels. The facts for each packet come from named, dated pages read for the class's problem and credited; reference-only pages are never quoted. If no checked page supports a fact, the fact is cut.

## The errors, in plain words

- **G:** a helper's wealth or fame decides who is good. Sort "what a person has" from "what a person does".
- **B:** liking supplies a judgment. Match a quality card to a detail card, then say "the detail is ___".
- **L:** efforts never change. Lay two then-and-now pictures and a row for what stayed.
- **C:** people far away cannot help themselves. Add the helper at each place.
- **O:** only our school has this problem. Place the three comparison cards on the strip.
- **J:** an invitation shows the work is done. Label four cards "plan" or "record".

A wrong idea is voiced by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child.

## Care

Judge deeds and the class's action, never a child. No place is shown as poor or helpless. Other places are named by their own names where a real place is used, in the present tense where people live there now.

## Supplies for the section

The Mo story cards, quality and detail cards, the second story cards, the founder read-aloud and strip, the stepping-stone record, the judgment sheet and source cards, the near, region and far strip, packet cards, the exit cards, the repair cards, the action log and the unit 7 record sheet.

## A room with nothing

Hand-drawn cards, three sheets of paper as the strip, and the teacher reading each source aloud.
