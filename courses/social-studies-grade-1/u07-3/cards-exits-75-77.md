# Exit cards, days 75 to 77

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the rotating six. Say aloud first: "These people, places and records are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a teaching card, a repair card or the day 79 packet.

## Day 75 exit card: the lunch bag and the crayon tub

**Picture A:** two scenes side by side, one actor each. Left: a character returns a lost lunch bag to a child. Right: a character stands beside the most toys. Read aloud: "One character returns a lost lunch bag. Another character owns the most toys."

**Picture B, a record read aloud:** "Class 1B put all the crayons in one shared tub so everyone can reach them, and wrote the day on the lid."

**Mark:** Circle the action that shows a good community member. **Spoken:** "Pick a quality in the record and say the detail."

## Day 76 exit card: the bike rack

**Picture:** three cards. Read aloud: "Years ago, neighbors built a new bike rack. Now, neighbors repaint its posts each spring." Cards: the bike rack built long ago; the posts painted now; a card that says "nothing ever changed".

**Mark:** Place the two cards that fit, in order. **Spoken:** "Say one quality their work shows, and point to the picture that shows it."

## Day 77 exit cards: three places

**Cards, read aloud:** "At a school near us, the line at the sink grows long, so staff add a turn chart." "Across a region, towns lack route information, so workers share walking maps." "At a school in another country, the gate is crowded, so staff arrange a waiting space."

**Mark:** Place each card on near, region or far. **Spoken:** "Say what the staff do at the far school."
