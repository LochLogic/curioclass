# Near, region and far strip, and the packet cards (day 77)

## The strip (three large paper sections laid out left to right)

- **Near:** our school, with our baseline picture.
- **Region:** a big area made of many towns; a small map with several dots.
- **Far:** another country; a globe sticker. Invented cards name no real country.

Under each section, a space for **who is already working on it**.

## A packet card (one for each place)

Place: near / region / far

The problem: ______________

Who is working on it: ______________

Label: **plan** / **record** (circle one)

Source and the date it was read: ______________

Say aloud for every card: a plan says what people hope to do; a record, with a date, says what was done.

## Making the packets

Curio Class makes day 77's two packets after the class chooses its problem on day 70, from named, dated pages that support each fact, credited. If no checked page supports a fact, the fact is cut and not replaced. Nothing is quoted from a reference-only page. The held-out day 79 packet is never used here.

## Fallback packets (use only when the two packets made for the class's problem are not in the folder)

Say aloud: "These places and people are made up." Print "invented for practice" on both cards. The class's problem is one of the day 70 choices (the ball bin, the art tubs or the jump ropes), so the fallback is about a shared box of play things that gets mixed up. No real country is named; the grown-ups there are the actors.

- **Region packet, label plan.** Read aloud: "Across a region, the play parks in many towns keep shared balls and ropes in one box, and the box gets mixed up. Park helpers plan to hang a hook for each rope and a sign for each ball."
- **Far packet, label record.** Read aloud: "At a school in another country, children share a box of play things, and it was often mixed up. A card dated March 9 says: the teachers sorted the box and put a picture on each shelf."

Say for each: a plan says what people hope to do; a record, with a date, says what was done. Who is working on it: park helpers (region); the teachers at that school (far).
