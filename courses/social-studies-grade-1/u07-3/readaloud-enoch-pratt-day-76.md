# A library founder, day 76 (read aloud, about 80 words, credited)

Written by Curio Class from the Enoch Pratt Free Library's own history page (source 76A). Say aloud: "This is a true story from long ago." Print the credit on the page: "Curio Class, from the Enoch Pratt Free Library history page."

## Read aloud

Long ago, a businessman named Enoch Pratt lived in the city of Baltimore. He wanted a library that was free, so that all the people in the city could borrow books. In January of 1886 the Enoch Pratt Free Library opened. It had thirty-two thousand books and four branch libraries in different parts of the city. Today the Enoch Pratt Free Library is still a library for the people of Baltimore.

## Three-picture strip (before, what the founder did, now)

1. **Before:** a city with no free library; people with empty hands.
2. **What he did:** Enoch Pratt and a new library with its door open; shelves of books.
3. **Now:** people borrowing books at a library in the same city.

Children say what the founder did for the town and which quality it shows (helpful, fair, caring).
