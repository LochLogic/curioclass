# Day 77: The same problem, near and far

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the strip with our baseline under near. Cut the two packet cards and the plan and record labels. If our packets are not ready, use the fallback pair in the strip file. Keep exit card 77 closed.

### The day

#### 3 min. Action routine

One child draws today's dated action log line.

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

If many made error L on day 76's exit, Pip says: "They always did it the same way." Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Lay two made-up pictures, mail carried by horse long ago and by truck now, and a row for what stayed. Children point to each (2 minutes). Partners say "Long ago ___; now ___; the same ___" (1 minute).

#### 6 min. Teaching first: near, region, far

Review from kindergarten: near is close; far is not close. New: a **region** is a big area made of many towns (a map with several dots). Far today means another country; made-up cards name no real country. Show the strip: our problem and baseline are near; each place has a space for who is already working on it. **Ask.** "Where is our problem?" Expect: near. **Ask.** "Could another place have a problem like ours?" Expect: yes, and people there work on it (error O).

#### 8 min. Two packets

Read the region packet, then the far packet; each is credited and carries a label. Say: a plan says what people hope to do; a record, with a date, says what was done. After each, a pair sets its card on the strip and says its label (1 minute each); the others show one finger for plan, two for record. **Ask.** "Does this page say the work is done?" Expect: the plan, no; the dated record, yes (error J).

#### 4 min. People there already help

Partners whisper one way people at each place work on it (1 minute). Hear six pairs, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Everyone draws one helper at one place on a copy of the strip (1 minute). Adults and groups there are already at work; no place is helpless (error C).

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 77 (2 minutes). [[s07-X77]] Say: "These places are made up." Hear the fourth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw one helper at a place.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: fold a page in three, near, region, far.

**Skip.** The packets and the heard six.

- **10 min. Three places.** Name a made-up problem, a wobbly park bench. Fold the page; say the same problem could be near, in a region or far.
  **Say.** "Who could be working on it at each place?" Expect: an adult or group at each.
- **15 min. A helper.** Your child draws one helper at one place and says what they do.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X77]] Show the card and read it. Your child places the cards and answers.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one packet. Longer: two helpers.

### Words and supports

Words: region, plan, record. Frame: "At ___, people ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 78 repairs error C (far away cannot help), error O (only here) and error J (a plan, not a record) on the strip, then looks back at what changed after we acted.
