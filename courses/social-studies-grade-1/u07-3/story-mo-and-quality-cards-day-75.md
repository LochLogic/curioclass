# Mo, the quality and detail cards, day 75

Say aloud: "Mo is made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters on each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, one actor in each scene.

## Mo, in two scenes

1. **Mo** carries a younger child's bag up the steps.
2. **Mo** tells the truth about a spilled paint cup: "I knocked it over."

## Quality cards (picture and one word)

Review: **caring, fair, helpful, respectful.** New: **honest** (tells the truth), **cooperate** (work together).

## Detail cards for the model

For Mo's first scene: "Mo carries the bag." (fits caring) and two that do not fit: "Mo has a red hat." "Mo is tall."

Model aloud: "I think Mo is caring because Mo carries the bag." Then match the second scene to honest. A good member is judged by what a person does, not by what a person has.

## Second story for the pairs: the reading buddies

Two classes meet each Friday as reading buddies. Print a date at the top of the record: Friday, May 15 (a made-up date, invented for practice). Read aloud once, one actor a scene:

1. **Ines** waits while a little child picks the book.
2. **Ines** and the little child take turns turning the pages.
3. **Ines** says thank you when the little child shares a story back.

Six cards for fifteen pairs to match, a quality card to a detail card: respectful, cooperate, helpful, caring, fair, honest with their details (for example "waits while a little child picks" to respectful; "take turns turning pages" to cooperate).

## The class's own action

Point to one line in the action log and say what it shows. Ask: "Is what we did what a good community member does?" The class judges the action, not a child. Practice, not scored.
