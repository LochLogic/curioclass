# Repair cards, section 7.3

Each repair card puts a wrong idea beside a right one. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most three or four minutes, or a small group when few missed it. Nobody is asked to say, stand or sing. No card reuses an exit, an end item or the day 79 packet.

## G: what a person has (day 76, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "Dax has the biggest bike, so Dax is the best helper." **Right:** "Dax held the door for a younger child. That is what a good member does." **Retry:** sort six cards onto "what a person has" and "what a person does": a big house; shares a ball; a fast car; says sorry and cleans up; a famous name; holds the gate.

## B: liking is a judgment (day 76)

**Pip:** "She is good because I like her story." **Right:** "She is honest: she told the truth about the spilled cup." **Retry:** match one quality card to one detail card, then say "the detail is ___".

## L: it never changed (day 77, 4 minutes)

**Pip:** "They always did it the same way." **Right:** "First by path stones, later a smooth path; both helped people walk." **Retry:** two then-and-now pictures, mail carried by horse long ago and by truck now, and a row for what stayed (the mail still reached people).

## C, O and J: far away, only here and an invitation (day 78, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "People far away cannot help themselves." "Only our school has this problem." "The page asks for helpers, so it is already fixed." **Right:** "Adults and groups there are already working on it. Other places have problems too, and people work on them. A plan says what people hope to do; a dated record shows what happened." **Retry:** add the helper at each place to the strip, say each place's problem, and label four new cards "plan" or "record": "A flyer asks neighbors to sweep the path on Saturday." "A dated photo shows the swept path." "A sign says the pond will be cleaned next spring." "A dated note says the pond was cleaned on May 3."
