# Day 75: What a good member does

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut Mo's scenes, the six quality cards and the detail cards. Copy the reading-buddies record onto three scene cards for each pair, dated. Keep exit card 75 closed. Say: "Mo and the buddies are made up."

### The day

#### 3 min. Action routine

One child draws today's dated action log line.

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

If many made error H on day 74's exit, Pip says: "I will go out alone and put up the signs." Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Sort the six step cards of card H onto safe and unsafe, the stop-and-tell rule said aloud.

#### 8 min. Teaching first: a quality needs a detail

Review from unit 3: a quality is a way a person is, shown by what they do. Review from kindergarten: caring, helpful, respectful, fair. New, with pictures: **honest**, tells the truth; **cooperate**, work together. Scene one: Mo carries a younger child's bag. Lay three detail cards: Mo carries the bag; Mo has a red hat; Mo is tall. **Ask.** "Which card shows what Mo did?" Expect: carries the bag. Lay caring above it and say: "I think Mo is caring because Mo carries the bag." Scene two: Mo says "I knocked it over" about a spilled paint cup. **Ask.** "Which quality fits?" Expect: honest. A red hat is what Mo has; a quality is judged by what a person does (error G).

#### 6 min. News

Read the dated, made-up reading-buddies record once: Ines waits while a little child picks the book; takes turns turning pages; says thank you when the child shares a story back. Pairs lay a quality card on each scene card (2 minutes). Hear six pairs, 30 seconds each (3 minutes): "I think Ines is ___ because ___." Accept any quality the detail shows (error B).

#### 4 min. Our own action

Six children each point to a log line and say what it shows, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). **Ask.** "Is what we did what a good community member does?" Expect: yes or not yet, with a log line. Judge the action, never a child; it did not fix everything. Practice, not scored.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 75 (2 minutes). [[s07-X75]] Say: "These people are made up." Hear the second six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw the quality they chose.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw six small pictures on paper.

**Skip.** The log, the pairs and the heard six.

- **10 min. A quality.** Tell a made-up story of a child who helps a neighbor with a heavy door. Name a quality and what the child did.
  **Say.** "What did the child do, and what quality does it show?" Expect: held the door; caring or helpful.
- **15 min. Match.** Your child draws three made-up deeds and writes caring, honest or cooperate by each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X75]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles and says the quality and the detail.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Mo and the exit. Longer: a second quality for one deed.

### Words and supports

Words: quality, honest, cooperate, detail. Frame: "I think ___ is ___ because ___." A child may pass.
