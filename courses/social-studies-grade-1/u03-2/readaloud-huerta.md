# Dolores Huerta read-aloud (day 24)

Read aloud by the teacher twice, in the past tense, with the four drawings. Say first: "This is our own short telling of two pages: one from the National Park Service and one from the foundation that her community made in her memory. It is about a real person who has died." Print the credit once in large type: "Our telling of a National Park Service page and the Dolores Huerta Foundation's page."

## The four sentences

1. Dolores Huerta lived from 1930 to 2026, and she died on October 28, 2026.
2. Many farm workers worked long hours in the fields for very low pay, and some fields were unsafe.
3. Starting in 1962, Huerta helped organize farm workers, with other organizers, so they could ask together for fair pay and safer work through a union.
4. Many workers joined, including Filipino farm workers who called the 1965 strike in Delano, California, and over the years union agreements brought better pay and safer work.

## The four drawings

Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, one person in each, no faces shown. Nothing shows hardship being done to a person.

1. A woman with a pencil beside two dates on a card, 1930 and 2026.
2. A wide field with rows of plants and a water jug at the end of a row; no workers drawn, a card reads "long hours, low pay."
3. A woman at a table with a stack of cards and a sign-up sheet, beside a card that reads "1962" and the word "union" with a picture of linked hands.
4. A line of workers seen from behind walking with signs that show a star; a card reads "many workers."

## Words with pictures

- **farm worker**: a person who works in a farm field growing food.
- **union**: workers who join together (heard once, not tested).
- **organize**: sort out who does what (review of day 20).

## Source questions

What kind of source is this? (Our telling of two pages.) Who made it? (Us, from a government page and her community's memorial.) Old or new? (New pages about events from 1962 on.) What can it help us find out? (What Huerta did and who joined her.)

## For the teacher

Past tense throughout: Dolores Huerta has died. If a child names Cesar Chavez, say one sentence: "Cesar Chavez started the farm workers' union with her; this class studies Huerta's work." The 2026 statements about him are not taught at this age. Tell the farm workers' story plainly, in a calm voice; no one acts out field work or hardship. Do not ask a child about money, a parent's work or home. The memorial's own numbers are not used. The 1962 card goes on the leader strip. The quality the class draws from the action is organizing.
