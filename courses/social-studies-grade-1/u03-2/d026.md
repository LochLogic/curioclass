# Day 26: Two leaders, one quality

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the four chart rows. Put five quality cards in each envelope, not the caring card. The six: second six on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

If many missed day 25's exit, set out a made-up choir: the leader holds a song card and the singers raise hands for each line; children name what the singers did (error D). Read the made-up line "Many people came to the sing-along. The record does not say if all liked it"; sort "the record says" from "we guess" (error O). Say once: "The Cherokee Nation governs itself today" (error L).

#### 4 min. Revisit the board

Point to "What responsibility does a leader take?" from day 20. Lincoln was President and Mankiller was Principal Chief, jobs in a government; the chart says what Tubman and Huerta took on. Open "Which actions show good leadership?" for the chart.

**Ask.** What did each leader take on? Expect: Tubman went back to help others; Lincoln issued the paper; Huerta helped workers organize; Mankiller worked with young citizens.

#### 10 min. Compare in pairs, and hearing the six

Twelve pairs take two chart rows, so each of the six pairings is worked twice. They set one action from each leader on the strip, join a quality card, say "I think both show ___ because ___," write one word and draw an action; you write their sentence. Any quality both actions support meets, such as courage for Tubman and Huerta. If a pair says the jobs must match (error N), ask "What does each one do for the group?" The six come to your table, a minute each, to mark exit card 26 and answer the spoken ask.

#### 7 min. Share

Three pairs show their joined cards. The class names the read-aloud each action came from and says whether the quality is the same though the jobs differ. The hook pictures come back; Ms. Reyes and Mr. Tran are not new.

#### 4 min. Exit

The twelve pairs mark exit card 26. [[s03-X26]] Read aloud: "Can both show the same quality, care? Join the pictures for yes or keep them apart for no." Spoken to the six: "Why can both show the same quality?" Look for: joined, with different jobs and care shown by what each does.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each quality word on a scrap.

**Skip.** The sharing.

- **5 min. Look back.** Draw four boxes: Tubman, Lincoln, Huerta, Mankiller. Your child draws one action in each.
- **15 min. Join.** Your child picks two boxes and a quality word: courage, taking responsibility, organizing, working together or listening.
  **Say.** "Why do both show it?" Expect: an action from each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X26]] Tell a made-up story: at a sports day one leader shuts the field gate and another sets out water bottles. Your child joins or separates them.
  **Say.** "Why can both show the same quality?" Expect: different jobs, both look after the group.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one pairing. Longer: a second quality for the same actions.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I think both show ___ because ___." Pointing and home-language words count.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 gives short explanations in order: first, then, so. If many kept the pictures apart because the jobs differ (error N), it opens by placing a new pair of different jobs under one quality card.
