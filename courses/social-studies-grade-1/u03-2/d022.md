# Day 22: Harriet Tubman and her helpers

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the chart and the strip with "long ago" and "today" cards. The six: third six on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

If many missed day 21's exit, sort the six question cards into asks what someone did and asks what we like (error E). Recheck reasons from day 20: Ms. Quill holds a door for a line; each pair says "I think she ___ because ___."

#### 3 min. Words with pictures

**quality**, a way a person is, shown by what they do. **evidence**, what we saw or read that shows it. **courage**, doing something hard to help others.

#### 7 min. Read-aloud with sourcing

Say: "This is our own telling of a National Park Service page. It is about real people." Read its four sentences twice with the drawings. Say the hard fact once, plainly: people were owned and forced to work, and it was wrong. Nobody acts it out.

Review the four source questions from unit 2.

**Ask.** What kind of source? Who made it? Old or new? What can it help us find out? Expect: our telling; us; new; what Tubman did.

#### 4 min. Action chart

Fill row one with pointed cards: did, others, changed. Add the 1849 card to the strip. Review: the strip from unit 2; it now runs past one year, so we only put cards in order. Quality: courage, with the action as evidence. Tubman led, and helpers gave a hand (error D); she shows courage by what she did, not because her name is known (error B).

#### 7 min. Questions, and hearing the six

Twelve pairs take two question cards, one about Tubman and one about her helpers, and say "I think she shows ___ because ___." Write their questions. The six come to your table, a minute each, to mark exit card 22 and answer the spoken ask.

#### 4 min. Exit

The twelve pairs mark exit card 22. [[s03-X22]] Read aloud: "Circle who helped." Spoken to the six: "What does the keeper's action show about him?" Look for: the keeper and the neighbors together, and a quality tied to the action.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: draw three boxes.

**Skip.** The partner questions.

- **5 min. Tell.** Say in your own words: long ago some people were owned by others and forced to work, which was called slavery, and it was wrong. Harriet Tubman escaped in 1849, went back about thirteen times and helped more than seventy people. Jacob Jackson helped her.
- **15 min. Chart.** Your child draws what she did, who helped and what changed.
  **Say.** "What does it show about her? What shows that?" Expect: courage, from going back to help.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X22]] Tell a made-up story: a lighthouse keeper warns boats while neighbors carry lanterns. Your child circles the keeper with the neighbors, or alone.
  **Say.** "What does his action show?" Expect: a quality from the action.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one chart row. Longer: a second quality from another action.

### Words and supports

Frames: "She did ___." "I think she shows ___ because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 meets Abraham Lincoln. If many circled the keeper alone (error D), it lays the leader and helper cards on the chart; if many said fame makes a good leader (error B), it covers the name and reads one action.
