# Action chart (days 22 to 26, kept for days 26 and 29)

One large sheet on butcher paper or a wall chart, with five columns of pictures and a few big words read aloud. One row is filled in each day by the class, with the teacher placing cards a child points to. Say first: "This chart is our own. It is made from the read-alouds."

## The five columns

1. **Leader** (a small drawing and the name card).
2. **What the leader did** (an action card).
3. **Who else acted** (a helper card).
4. **What changed** (a change card).
5. **A quality, and the action that shows it** (a quality card joined to the action card by a string or arrow).

## The four rows, one a day

- **Day 22, Harriet Tubman.** Did: escaped in 1849, then went back about thirteen times to lead others. Others: her brothers, and helpers such as Jacob Jackson. Changed: more than seventy people reached freedom. Quality: courage.
- **Day 23, Abraham Lincoln.** Did: as President, issued the proclamation on January 1, 1863. Others: many people worked for freedom. Changed: people held as slaves in the named states were free; slavery did not end everywhere that day. Quality: taking responsibility for a hard decision.
- **Day 24, Dolores Huerta.** Did: from 1962, helped organize farm workers. Others: other organizers and many workers, including Filipino farm workers. Changed: union agreements brought better pay and safer work. Quality: organizing.
- **Day 25, Wilma Mankiller.** Did: Principal Chief 1985 to 1995; young citizens and the Chief worked together in 1989. Others: young citizens of the Cherokee Nation. Changed: a youth council began; three rural health centers opened. Quality: working together with her nation.

## How it is used

The strip beside it holds the dates. On day 26 pairs take two rows. On day 29 the rows are the evidence for the class claim. Keep the chart up through day 31 with the quality column left blank for the children to fill.

## For the teacher

Every cell comes from the day's read-aloud and nothing else. If a child names something that is not in the read-aloud, say "the read-aloud does not tell us that" and add a question card to the board. Do not fill the quality column before the class has named the action. D: always fill "who else acted." K: the Lincoln row must say what did not change.
