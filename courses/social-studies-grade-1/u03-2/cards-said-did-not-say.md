# Said and did not say card (day 23)

One large split card the teacher holds up: **said** on the left, **did not say** on the right. Plain black line drawing on white. Pairs and the teacher place small picture cards on each side. Say first: "This card is our own, made from the old paper and a National Archives page."

## Said (left side, three cards)

1. A paper with a date, 1863. "The paper has a date."
2. A map with a shaded area. "It said people who were enslaved, owned and forced to work, in the places named were free."
3. A small flag over the shaded area. "It named the states fighting against the country."

## Did not say (right side, two cards)

1. A blank area of the map. "It did not name every place."
2. A long road with a star at the far end and a step marked "this paper." "It was a step on the road, not the end."

## For the teacher

Error K is also on the day 23 exit; if a child says "Lincoln's paper freed everyone," the teacher says the matching card once: "It said people who were enslaved in some places were free; slavery did not end everywhere that day." No score. The words the cards use are the same as the unit's: Lincoln, the proclamation, President.
