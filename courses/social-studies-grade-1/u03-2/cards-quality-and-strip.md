# Quality cards and the two-column strip (day 26; used again on days 28 and 29)

Say first: "These cards are our own."

## Six quality cards

Each has a large word, read aloud, and a small picture of a hand or a figure with no face.

1. **courage**: doing something hard to help others.
2. **taking responsibility**: taking on a job that others count on.
3. **organizing**: sorting out who does what.
4. **working together**: doing the job with others.
5. **listening**: hearing what others say before choosing.
6. **caring**: looking after others by what you do.

## The two-column strip

A long strip with a line down the middle. Each column has a picture slot at the top for an action card from one leader. Under both is a wide slot for one quality card. A pair places an action from each leader and joins them with one quality card.

## How the cards are used

A pair takes two of the four chart rows and finds an action in each. They place a quality card under both and say "I think both show ___ because ___." The action must be the evidence. Day 20's principal and crossing guard may stand in as a fifth and sixth example.

## For the teacher

N: two leaders with different jobs can show one quality. If a pair says the jobs must match, ask "What does each one do for the group?" One quality can be shown by more than one action, and one action can show more than one quality. For the written task, pairs write one word on the strip and draw the action under it; the teacher writes the sentence.
