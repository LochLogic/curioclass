# Leader timeline strip cards (days 22 to 25, used again on day 27)

An unscaled strip on a string with clothes pins, started with unit 2's year strip and stretched so it runs past one year. Each day one card is added to the right of the last, in time order. The cards are not placed to scale; say so: "We do not measure the space. We only put them in order."

## The four cards

1. **1849** (day 22). A woman walking on a road. Words: "Tubman escapes, 1849."
2. **1863** (day 23). A man in a tall hat at a desk. Words: "Lincoln issues the proclamation, January 1, 1863."
3. **1962** (day 24). A woman at a table with a sign-up sheet. Words: "Huerta begins to organize farm workers, 1962."
4. **1985 to 1995** (day 25). A woman at a long table. Words: "Mankiller is Principal Chief, 1985 to 1995."

Two end cards: one labelled "long ago" at the left and one labelled "today" at the right.

## For the teacher

Ask each day: "Is this before or after the last card?" and "Which cards are from long ago and which are close to today?" The class never reads a year; the teacher reads it. Huerta's card shows her start only. Do not put the exit or end cases on the strip.
