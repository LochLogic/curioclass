# Day 24: Dolores Huerta and the farm workers

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tell the story calmly; nobody acts out field work. The six: fifth six on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

If many missed day 23's exit (error K), say once beside the split card: slavery did not end everywhere that day. If many left out who else acted, set the lost-mitten leader and helper cards beside the Lincoln row (error D); if many wrote liking questions, sort six new ones (error E).

#### 3 min. Words with pictures

**farm worker**, a person who works in a farm field growing food. **union**, workers who join together (heard once). **strike**, workers stop work together to ask for a change (heard once). Review: organize, sort out who does what (day 20).

#### 7 min. Read-aloud with sourcing

Say: "This is our own telling of two pages, one from the National Park Service and one her community made in her memory. She was a real person who has died." Read it twice in the past tense with the drawings. If a child names Cesar Chavez, say: "He started the farm workers' union with her; this class studies Huerta's work." Ask no child about pay or a parent's job.

**Ask.** What kind of source? Who made it? Old or new? What can it help us find out? Expect: our telling of two pages; us; new; what Huerta did and who joined.

#### 4 min. Action chart

Fill row three: did (from 1962 helped organize farm workers), others (other organizers; many workers), changed (union agreements brought better pay and safer work). Add the 1962 card, her start only. Quality: organizing, with others (error D).

#### 7 min. Questions, and hearing the six

Twelve pairs take two question cards, one about Huerta and one about the workers. Write their questions. The six come to your table, a minute each, to mark exit card 24 and answer the spoken ask.

#### 4 min. Exit

The twelve pairs mark exit card 24. [[s03-X24]] [[s03-X24b]] Read aloud: "Circle who asked for the change." Spoken to the six: "Ask one question about what the neighbors did." Look for: the neighbors and the leader together, and a question about what they did.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: draw three boxes.

**Skip.** The partner questions.

- **5 min. Tell.** Say in your own words: Dolores Huerta lived from 1930 to 2026. Many farm workers worked long hours for low pay. From 1962 she helped organize them, with others, to ask together for fair pay and safer work.
- **15 min. Chart.** Your child draws what she did, who joined and what changed.
  **Say.** "What did she do? Who else acted?" Expect: helped workers organize; other organizers and many workers.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X24]] [[s03-X24b]] Tell a made-up story: neighbors and a leader ask a hall's owner for a safe stair. Your child circles who asked.
  **Say.** "Ask one question about what the neighbors did." Expect: a question about meeting or asking.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one question each. Longer: a second quality.

### Words and supports

Frames: "She did ___." "I think she shows organizing because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 meets Wilma Mankiller and the Cherokee Nation. If many circled the leader alone (error D), it lays the leader and helper cards with the Huerta row; if many asked what color the stair was (error E), it sorts six new questions.
