# Pair question cards, leaders (days 22 to 25)

Each pair gets two cards a day, one for the leader and one for the helpers or the group. A card has a large question word and a picture. The teacher reads each aloud. Say first: "These cards are our own."

## The cards

- **Who** (a picture of two people): "Who helped?"
- **What** (a picture of a hand holding a tool): "What did the leader do?"
- **How** (a picture of a path of steps): "How did the group help?"
- **What changed** (a picture of a before and an after): "What was different after?"

## Frame, said aloud first

"I wonder ___ about the leader." "I wonder ___ about the helpers." A pair may point instead of speaking.

## Sample asks, for the teacher to model

- Day 22: "What did Tubman do?" "Who helped her?"
- Day 23: "What did Lincoln decide?" "Who else worked for freedom?"
- Day 24: "What did Huerta do?" "What did the farm workers do?"
- Day 25: "What did Mankiller do?" "What did the young citizens do?"

## For the teacher

An ask about what a person or group did counts. An ask only about what we like goes in the other box (E). Write the pair's ask in words beside its drawing. No child is asked about their own family or work.
