# Exit cards, days 22 to 26

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a read-aloud, a chart row, a strip card or a repair card.

## Day 22 exit card: the lighthouse

**Picture:** A keeper stands at the top of a lighthouse beside a bell. Three boats sit on dark waves. On the shore, two neighbors carry lanterns. Two small circles: one under the keeper and the neighbors together, one under the keeper alone.

**Mark:** Circle who helped the boats. **Spoken:** "What does the keeper's action show about him?"

## Day 23 exit card: the pool paper

**Picture:** A town notice that reads "The east pool is open." Below it a map of three pools: the east pool drawn with waves, the north and west pools drawn empty. Two circles under the map: "opened the east pool" and "opened all three pools." Read aloud: "A town paper said: the east pool is open. The town has three pools."

**Mark (one ask, every child):** Circle what the paper did.

## Day 24 exit card: the hall stair

**Picture:** Three pictures. 1: neighbors stand with a leader outside a community hall. 2: they talk with the owner at the door. 3: the hall has a new stair with a rail. Two circles: the neighbors and the leader together; the leader alone.

**Mark:** Circle who asked for the change. **Spoken:** "Ask one question about what the neighbors did."

## Day 25 exit card: the garden club

**Picture:** Two pictures side by side. Left: a club leader holds up a plan while members point to places on it. Right: a club leader puts a flag on the plan while members stand with their hands down. Each has a circle.

**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows listening. **Spoken:** "Ask one question about what the members chose."

## Day 26 exit card: sports day

**Picture:** Two pictures. Left: a leader closes a field gate. Right: another leader sets out water bottles on a blanket. Between them, a short line with a circle at each end, one marked "join" and one marked "apart."

**Mark:** Can both show the same quality, care? Join the two pictures or keep them apart. **Spoken:** "Why can both show the same quality?"
