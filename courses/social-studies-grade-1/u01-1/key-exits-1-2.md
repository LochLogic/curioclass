# Teacher key, exit cards, days 1 and 2

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-exits-1-2.md; never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 1: the card that asks, "How do we stay together?"; a question such as "Where do we walk?" or "Who walks with us?" meets. Marking "Walks are fun." or saying "We walk" shows error A.
- Day 2 (mark): the civics card, because the question asks who does a job and how a group shares it. Showing the geography card because the question says "hallway" shows error F. Day 2 (spoken, the six): who has the job and how the jobs are shared, such as "Who changes it?" or "Do we take turns?" Saying only "the hallway" or "the wall" shows error F.
