# Question board cards (days 1 to 9)

The question board is a large sheet of chart paper that stays on the wall beside the wonder wall until day 9. The teacher holds the cards; children hold only the card they place.

## The board

**Layout:** A strip across the top for the big question. Below it, three rows, one for each supporting question, each with a day label to be added later. A fourth row for the smaller questions that come from the class. Cards tape on and off.

## The big question strip

**Strip text:** What makes a group work well?

Post it on day 1. Say that this is the big question for the whole unit.

## The three supporting question cards

Each card has a picture clue and is read aloud once, then echoed once by the class. The teacher says which day answers it.

1. **What does belonging give members?** Clue: a ring of hands. Answered on day 3.
2. **Why do groups need rules and duties?** Clue: a signpost beside a broom. Answered on day 5.
3. **How does each role help a shared goal?** Clue: two puzzle pieces fitting. Answered on day 8.

## Using the board

On day 1 the three cards go under the big question. On day 2 each card gets a fact card and a word card beside it. On days 3, 5 and 8 the card being answered is read aloud at the start. On day 9 the board stays up as a picture support; the examples worked in teaching are taken down.
