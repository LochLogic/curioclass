# Two Mural Panels

Two picture panels for day 1 (and again on day 2). Say first, before you show them: "We made these pictures for our class. The children in them are made up." Then show both side by side and post the big question, "What makes a group work well?". Ask the children to point to the panel where the group is working well. They point together and nobody speaks yet.

Drawing style for both panels: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. The same five invented children appear in both panels, drawn in many skin tones and hair styles. One child uses a wheelchair at a table set at the right height.

## Panel A: one sheet for everyone

**Picture:** One big sheet of paper lies on a table. All five children lean in and reach for the same sheet at once, hands piled on it. The paper is creased. One box of paint stands in the middle; a brush has rolled off the edge. Behind them a bare wall by the door has a tape mark where a mural could go. Nothing has been drawn yet.

**Teacher says:** "Everyone reaches for the same paper."

## Panel B: sharing the tasks

**Picture:** The same table and the same bare wall by the door. Each child has a different part. One child draws the outline in pencil. One paints the sky with a wide brush. One tears paper shapes at a low table; this is the child who uses a wheelchair. One glues the shapes on. One holds the sheet flat. The one box of paint sits in the middle with a lid on. The mural, half done, shows a river and a hill.

**Teacher says:** "Everyone has a part."

## For the teacher

After the children point, ask: "What do you wonder about the mural group?" (that is the wonder wall, five minutes). Keep both panels for day 2: the bare wall is what history asks about (what was here before), the tape mark by the door is what geography asks about (where), the way the children share the work is civics, and the one box of paint is economics. Say aloud that the pictures show only what is drawn; do not tell the children what anyone feels.
