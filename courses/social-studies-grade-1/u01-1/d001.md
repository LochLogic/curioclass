# Day 1: A big question and smaller ones

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape up the board and the wonder wall. Hang the two panels. Choose today's six: the first six on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-1-2.md.

### The day

#### 6 min. Hook: the two mural panels

Say first: "We made these pictures for our class. The children in them are made up." Show both panels and post the strip: "What makes a group work well?" It is our big question for the unit.

**Ask.** Which panel shows the group working well? Point. Expect: all point at once, most to the panel where each child has a part. No one speaks yet. 

#### 5 min. Wonder wall (review)

Review: in kindergarten a wonder is a question.

**Ask.** What do you wonder about the mural group? (s01-R1) Expect: any question. Write each beside a picture: eight at most, about 35 seconds each. A child may pass, or look at the gate picture with the painted sun. Say a statement back once as a question; note it for error A.

#### 7 min. Big and small questions

Say: "This is a pretend class and a pretend block corner." Hold the big question card with arms wide and read it. It is too big to answer at once. Hold the three smaller cards low, in small hands, and read each. These are supporting questions: smaller questions that help. Both motions twice.

**Ask.** Tell your partner one more smaller question about the block corner. 15 pairs talk at once; hear 3 pairs and tape two questions on the board. A statement gets "Can you ask that as a question?" (error A).

#### 6 min. Our supporting questions

Read the three unit cards once each; the class echoes each once. Tape them under the big question and name the day that answers each: days 3, 5 and 8. Explain no word.

**Ask.** Point to one smaller question under our big question. Expect: any of the three.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Review: the exit ticket from kindergarten. Read both cards aloud: "Circle the card that asks a smaller question about a class walk." (s01-X1, 2 minutes.) Then ask the first six, one each: "Say one smaller question of your own about a class walk." ([[s01-X1b]], about 40 seconds each, 4 minutes). The others draw on the student page. A statement is error A: thank the child, note it. A child may pass or point.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No panels: draw two tables, one with hands piled on one paper, one where each child holds something different. No exit card: write both cards on paper.

**Skip.** The wonder wall and the partner talk.

- **6 min. Two pictures.** Say the children are made up. The big question is: What makes a group work well?
  **Say.** "Which picture shows the group working well?" Expect: the one where each child has a part; pointing counts.
- **6 min. A wonder.** Turn a statement into a question.
  **Say.** "What do you wonder?" Expect: any question.
- **8 min. Big and small.** Say "A block corner: how can it work well for everyone? That is big. A smaller question is: Who uses the blocks?"
  **Say.** "What is another smaller question?" Expect: any question about the blocks.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a wonder.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X1]] [[s01-X1b]] Read both cards, your child circles one.
  **Say.** "Say one smaller question about a class walk." Expect: a question, such as "Where do we walk?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear one pair. Longer: tape on a second smaller question.

### Words and supports

Frame: "A smaller question is ...". A home language, a point or a drawing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 2 reopens with the exit: if many circled the statement or said one (error A), four minutes of repair. Then four ways to ask.
