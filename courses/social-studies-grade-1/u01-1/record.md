# Unit 1 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into rotating sixes: the first six are heard on days 1 and 6, the second six on days 2 and 7, the third six on days 3 and 8, the fourth six on day 4, the fifth six on day 5, and the children not heard on day 9 are heard on day 10. The R columns are the readiness looks (days 1, 3 and 6): no score, note the support a child used. Each E column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling or drawing, and a note when the spoken why was heard. The End columns are the day 9 assessment, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Item E3 is scored on day 9 from the day 8 tidy-up chart, not from a new attempt. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders.
