# Day 2: Four ways to ask

## Teacher page

### Before class

Envelope each child's four subject cards. Choose today's six: the second six on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-1-2.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Read the board and wall aloud. If many missed day 1's exit (error A), run 4 minutes: children stand for a question and sit for a statement. Cards: "Whose bag is red?" "The bag is on the hook." "Where does the bag go?" Review (kindergarten day 1): turn the statement into a question together. Otherwise a small group has it in the sort.

#### 8 min. Four ways to ask

Say the panels are made up. Hold each large card, say its word and what it asks, and show its motion; the class repeats it twice. History asks what it was like before (wave back over your shoulder). Geography asks where (point). Civics asks how a group works together (hold two hands together). Economics asks what we have and what we choose (hold up a few fingers). For each, ask the card's mural question. Say "four ways to ask," not kinds of children.

#### 7 min. Which way is this question?

Read the four mural question cards one at a time.

**Ask.** Which way of asking is this question? Show your card. Expect: history, geography, civics, economics in turn; either of two ways is right with a reason.

**Ask.** Tell your partner why. 15 pairs talk at once for 10 seconds; name two reasons heard.

#### 6 min. What we need to find out

Each supporting question is a civics question: it asks how a group works together. For each, hold up its fact card and word cards and say them; the class echoes the words; a child tapes the cards beside the question. Review: you learned rule in kindergarten. Only name the new words: belong, duty, role, goal.

**Ask.** What do we need to find out about a rule (review) and a duty? Expect: what a rule is for.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Cards stay shut. Read: "How did our hallway clock change? Circle the picture that helps." (s01-X2, 1 minute.) Ask the second six, one each: "What do we need to find out?" (about 30 seconds each, 3 minutes). The others set each subject card on its box on the student page. A place name alone, "the hallway," is error F.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: use the four hand motions. No exit card: draw an old clock, a new clock and a hallway map.

**Skip.** The repair and the pair talk.

- **6 min. Four motions.** Waving back asks before, pointing asks where, hands together ask how a group works, fingers up ask what we have.
  **Say.** "Which motion asks: where does the mural go?" Expect: pointing.
- **7 min. Which way?**
  **Say.** "We have three brushes and six painters. Which way of asking is that? Why?" Expect: economics, or civics with a reason such as "we have to share".
- **7 min. What to find out.**
  **Say.** "What do we need to find out about rules?" Expect: what a rule is for; give help if needed.
- **5 min. Show me.** Read the two questions about the mural wall; your child does a motion for each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2]] Show the old and new clock and the map; your child circles one.
  **Say.** "What do we need to find out?" Expect: what the clock was like before.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort two cards. Longer: a child makes a question for one way.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I need to find out ...". A pointed card is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 3 reopens with the exit: if many named only a place (error F), four minutes with a door before and after. Then group, member and belong.
