# Four subject cards (day 2, and again for the day 9 check)

Four ways to ask. Each way has a large card for the teacher and a small card for every child, so that all thirty can show a card at once. Say "These are four ways to ask", never "four kinds of children".

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, one word at the bottom of each card.

## History: a way of asking what it was like before

**Picture:** A short arrow that curves backward over a bare wall with a tape mark on it. **Word:** history. **Motion:** wave a hand back over the shoulder. **Example from the mural:** What was on this wall before the mural?

## Geography: a way of asking where

**Picture:** A pin standing on a simple drawn map of a classroom, with a door and a window. **Word:** geography. **Motion:** point a finger to a place. **Example from the mural:** Where on the wall does the mural go?

## Civics: a way of asking how a group works together

**Picture:** Two hands held together. **Word:** civics. **Motion:** hold two hands together. **Example from the mural:** Who does which job so the mural gets finished?

## Economics: a way of asking what we have and what we choose

**Picture:** A hand holding a few crayons. **Word:** economics. **Motion:** hold up a few fingers for what we have. **Example from the mural:** There is one box of paint. How do we share it?

## How the cards are used

The class does each motion twice. For the sort on day 2 the teacher reads a question, every child shows the card they think fits, and pairs say why for ten seconds. For the day 9 check each child has the same four cards.
