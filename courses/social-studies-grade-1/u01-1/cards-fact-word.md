# Fact and word cards (day 2)

For each of the three supporting questions the teacher models one fact to find out and one important word on picture-and-word cards. Tie each to civics. Children echo the word and place the card beside the question on the board. The words are only named here and are taught on days 3, 4 and 6.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, a large picture and one word.

## Question 1: What does belonging give members?

**Fact card:** What members get from the group. **Picture:** two hands handing something across. **Word card:** belong. **Picture:** one child stepping into a ring of children.

## Question 2: Why do groups need rules and duties?

**Fact card:** What a rule is for. **Picture:** a signpost with a small arrow. **Word cards:** rule (**picture:** a signpost) and duty (**picture:** a child putting a box on a shelf).

## Question 3: How does each role help a shared goal?

**Fact card:** What each member does. **Picture:** four hands each holding something different. **Word cards:** role (**picture:** one child with a small hat marked with a star) and goal (**picture:** a flag on a hilltop).

## For the teacher

Say each word aloud, children echo it, and the card goes on the board. Do not define the words today. Say "We will find out more on day 3" and the right day.
