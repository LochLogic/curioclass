# Mural question cards (day 2)

Four new questions about the mural group, read aloud one at a time. Each child shows the subject card that fits, then says why to a partner for ten seconds. The teacher names two reasons heard. Say aloud that the mural group is made up.

Drawing style: one small plain line picture on each card, one question under it.

## Card 1

**Question:** What did our art corner look like last year? **Picture:** A bare art corner with a table and no pictures. **Fits:** history (what it was like before). A child may also hold up geography with the reason "it is about a corner"; ask what the corner would tell us about before.

## Card 2

**Question:** Which side of the room is the best place to hang the mural? **Picture:** A room outline with an arrow pointing at one wall. **Fits:** geography (where). Civics is also fair when the reason is "we have to decide together".

## Card 3

**Question:** How do the painters take turns at the mural? **Picture:** Two painters, one painting and one waiting, with a line of children. **Fits:** civics (how a group works together).

## Card 4

**Question:** We have three brushes and six painters. What do we choose to do? **Picture:** Three brushes beside six small figures. **Fits:** economics (what we have and what we choose). Civics also fits with the reason "we have to share".

## Using the cards

Accept either subject when the child gives a reason. If a child names only a place name or a thing, ask what the question would find out (error F).
