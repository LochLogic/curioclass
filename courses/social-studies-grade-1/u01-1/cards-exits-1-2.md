# Exit cards, days 1 and 2

Each exit has a picture card that every child marks (circle, point or show a card) and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six, each a separate ask. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Neither card reuses a picture from the mural panels, the block corner cards, the subject cards or the mural question cards.

## Day 1 exit card: a class walk

**Picture:** Two question cards side by side. On the left, a card with the words "How do we stay together?" and a picture of a line of children walking behind a teacher. On the right, a card with the words "Walks are fun." and a picture of two smiling children walking. Each card is ringed by a thick circle so a child can mark it.

**Mark:** Circle the card that asks a smaller question about the walk. **Spoken:** "Say one smaller question of your own about a class walk."

## Day 2 exit card: who changes the clock?

**Picture:** At the top, the words "Who changes the hallway clock each week?" and a small drawing of a clock on a wall, labeled "a made-up school". Below, a row of the four small subject cards, ready to be shown. Do not draw any old and new clock pair or any map.

**Mark:** Show the subject card that fits. **Spoken (a separate ask, the second six):** "What do we need to find out?"
