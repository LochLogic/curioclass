# Chart: two places (day 58)

Name ______________________

Say aloud first: "These places are real. The pictures are drawings." A chart with two columns headed with the two names the pair works on (the teacher models Singapore and Nairobi; pairs fill Sapporo and Singapore) and three rows: "weather", "what people do about it", "one thing this picture cannot tell". Each square has room to draw and one word label. Below, one line: "Rain or snow can change ___. It does not decide ___."

**Task:** Pairs fill the chart for Sapporo and Singapore from the teaching views and place one token each on the map. Weather row: snow for Sapporo; warm days and a lot of rain for Singapore. Choice row: coats and boots; light clothes and a jacket. Third row: what the picture cannot tell, such as what the families eat, or what the school day is like.
