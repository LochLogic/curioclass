# Map: three cities and tokens (days 58, then the exit and packet use fresh views)

Say aloud first: "This map is real. The three dots are real cities." A flat world map redrawn from Natural Earth outlines (source 50E, public domain): the seven continents in gray, the oceans in blue. A key box: a gray shape is land, a blue shape is water, a dot with a name is a city. A direction indicator labelled N with its arrow pointing to the top of the page. Three dots, each labelled once in large print: Sapporo (on the island of Hokkaido, northeast Asia), Singapore (at the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula, southeast Asia), Nairobi (in east Africa, a little south of the equator). Three tokens per pair.

**Teaching ask (day 58):** "Find Sapporo with the key. Which of the three cities is farthest north?" Answer: Sapporo. Then each pair places a token on Singapore and one on Nairobi and says which direction each is from Sapporo (south for both, Nairobi also west).

This map is not used for the exit or the end items. Those use a new map of Asia and Africa and a new globe view.
