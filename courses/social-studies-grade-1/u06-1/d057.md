# Day 57: What people do and say

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the drawings up. Lay sort cards and bins for each pair. Keep exit card 57 closed. Error C and error K from day 55 stay on the sheet for day 58.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

1 minute: "What does one picture show?" Expect: one street. 4 minutes, if many missed error A on day 56 (s06-X56b): a new street of made-up Ridge Town with a market, then a second view with no market. **Ask.** "What does the first show? What does it leave out?" If few missed it, a small group.

#### 9 min. Teaching first

A custom is something people in a place do the same way, such as a song to start the day. A language is the words people use to talk or write. Show the made-up Holly Run card: a class sings with a drum. The speaker says: "We sing so everyone begins the day together." Review: two views side by side, from kindergarten. "Here, we start the day like this. There, they sing. For the people who use it, it makes sense because everyone begins together." Review: Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Pip says error H: "That is wrong. We do it differently." Correct: read the speaker first. Show the Nairobi sign: "In this picture, a sign in Nairobi has two languages, English and Kiswahili, official languages of Kenya." Two bins: "people made or learned it" (cultural) and "land, water, weather" (environmental). Song and sign go in the first, a pond in the second. Pip says error I: "The writing on the sign is part of the land." Correct: people made the writing.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs sort six cards: a made-up greeting, the song, the sign, a snow pile, a river, a sandy beach: 90 seconds. Then 30 seconds to say one custom or language and one land, water or weather feature for a named place. Hear the second six at the exit cards, 40 seconds each, one spoken ask: 4 minutes. A pair may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 57. [[s06-X57]] Say: "These are practice pictures." Read the three choices aloud.

#### 3 min. Look back

Pairs say "It makes sense to them because ___" for one custom. Add a question to the board.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No bins: use two plates.

**Skip.** Pip, the pairs and the heard six.

- **5 min. Two bins.** Read the song card. Your child sorts the cards into two piles.
  **Say.** "Did people make it or learn it, or is it land, water or weather?" Expect: made or learned for the song and sign; weather for snow.
- **20 min. Makes sense.** Say what the speaker says. Your child finishes: "It makes sense to them because ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X57]] Show the card. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four cards. Longer: add a street from day 56.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: custom, language. Frame: "It makes sense to them because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 58 repairs error H (a custom called wrong) and error I (a sign called land); error B (rain called a custom) is met in its model row. Error C and error K stay on the sheet for its map review.
