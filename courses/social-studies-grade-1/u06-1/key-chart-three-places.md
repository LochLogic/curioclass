# Teacher key, chart three places

Teacher only. Keep apart from chart-three-places.md; never hand to children.

## Key

**Teacher key.** Judge the sentence, not the drawing: a child says what is on the card before what they think. Accept "I see a jacket; I think it is chilly, because I see a jacket" as shown, and note the order. A guess about what people eat or say at home is "not yet seen" for the look-for and is met with "I cannot see that in the picture."
