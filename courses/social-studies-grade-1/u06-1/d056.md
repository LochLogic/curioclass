# Day 56: Three places today

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the three drawings. Lay a chart and nine cards at each table. Keep exit card 56 closed. Teacher key: key-chart-three-places.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook

Read each caption: Sapporo in winter, Nairobi, Singapore. "These are real places today; the pictures are drawings of one street." **Ask.** "What do you see in all three?" Expect: roads, shops, people, signs. Review: the question board. Post the big question and write with the class: "How do people live in these places?"

#### 2 min. Retrieval

Review: a tradition, from kindergarten. Show the made-up card "Neighbors tell stories together every spring." **Ask.** "What makes this a tradition? Must every neighbor join?" Expect: people repeat it and pass it on; no. A miss joins the small group.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review: "I see ..., I think ..., because I see ..." from kindergarten. Model on Nairobi: "I see jackets. I think it may be chilly, because I see jackets." Then Sapporo: "I see snow by the road. I think it is winter, because I see snow." Say: "I cannot see round the corner." Review: who made it, when and why, asked of the Sapporo winter page: an encyclopedia's editors, today, for readers; it says Sapporo has snowy winters, nothing about any family. Words with pictures: homes, clothes, street. Review: Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Pip says error A: "This picture shows how everyone in that city lives." Correct: "It shows this street today." Show the quiet side street: "A second picture shows something else."

#### 11 min. Supported work

Six tables of five. 4 minutes: each child places a card or two and says "I see ___; I think ___, because I see ___" (nine cards, 20 seconds a card). 4 minutes: each draws one way of living and says a word; an adult may scribe. Meanwhile hear the first six at the exit cards, 40 seconds each, one spoken ask. Last 3 minutes: a small group for a retrieval miss matches three repeated activities to captions. A guess about home: "I cannot see that."

#### 3 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 56. [[s06-X56]] [[s06-X56b]] Say: "These are practice pictures."

#### 2 min. Look back

Add one question to the board. "Tomorrow: what people say, and what they write on signs."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three streets.

**Skip.** The tables, Pip and the heard six.

- **5 min. Look.** Show a drawing. Your child says what they see.
  **Say.** "What do you see? What do you think, and how do you know?" Expect: "I see jackets; I think it is chilly, because I see jackets."
- **20 min. Draw.** Your child draws one thing they noticed, adds one word, and says what the picture cannot show.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X56]] [[s06-X56b]] Show the card. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one card each. Longer: model Singapore too.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: homes, clothes, street. A child may point.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 repairs error A if many circled the same clothes or lunch. Error C and error K from day 55 stay on the sheet; day 58 reviews the key and directions.
