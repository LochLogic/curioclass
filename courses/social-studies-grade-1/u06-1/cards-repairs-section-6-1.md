# Repair cards and fresh-try cards, section 6.1

Each repair card puts a wrong idea beside a right one so the class can see the difference. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most five minutes, a small group when few missed it. No card reuses an exit or end picture.

## A: one picture shows how everyone lives

**Pip:** looks at the Sapporo street and says, "This picture shows how everyone in that city lives."

**Right:** "This picture shows this street today. A second picture can show something else." Show a second drawing, a quiet Sapporo side street with bare trees and no one on it, beside the first.

**Retry (day 57, whole class):** a fresh street drawing of a made-up town, Ridge Town, with a market, then a second view of it with no market. "What does the first show? What does it leave out?" Fresh pair: a school yard and a school hall.

## H: an unfamiliar greeting is wrong

**Pip:** "That greeting is wrong. We do it differently."

**Right:** "It is a greeting. The speaker says it makes people feel welcome."

**Retry (day 58, 3 minutes):** read the speaker's words first, then say what it means to them. Fresh card: Thistle Market rings a bell, and the speaker says, "We ring the bell so everyone knows the market is open." Pip says, "That bell is wrong." Invented for practice.

## I: the writing on a sign is part of the land

**Pip:** "The writing on the sign is part of the land."

**Right:** "People made the writing. A hill is land."

**Retry (day 58, 2 minutes):** two bins, "people made or learned it" and "land, water, weather"; sort four new cards: a handshake; a river; a language lesson; a hot sun.

## B: weather decides language and every custom

**Pip:** "It is cold, so the people in Briar Town speak slowly."

**Right:** "Snow changes what we wear. Language is something people learn."

**Retry (day 59 return and repair, 5 minutes):** two cards, "weather changes a coat" and "weather does not teach a word"; the class sorts three new cards: a rain jacket; a word a child learns at school; a hat for sun. Fresh place: Briar Town, invented, with snow and a shared song (Pip's line is about Briar Town).
