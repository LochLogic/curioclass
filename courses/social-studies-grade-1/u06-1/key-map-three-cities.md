# Teacher key, map three cities

Teacher only. Keep apart from map-three-cities.md; never hand to children.

## Key

**Teacher key.** North is the top of the page on this map. Sapporo (about 43 degrees north) is the farthest north of the three; Singapore (about 1 degree north) is just north of the equator; Nairobi (about 1 degree south) is just south of it. Do not say these numbers to children. Sources: Natural Earth outlines (50E); the city positions rest on a standard reference.
