# Chart: three places (day 56)

Name ______________________

Say aloud first: "These are drawings. Each shows one home, one set of clothes, one street." A chart with three columns headed Sapporo, Singapore, Nairobi, and three rows with a small picture clue at the left: a home, clothes, a street. Nine empty squares. The nine chart cards (pictures-street-views-and-cards.md) are placed one in each square. Below the chart, a drawing box with the words "One way of living I noticed" and room for a one-word label.

**Task:** Each child places two cards and says "I see ___; I think ___, because I see ___." Then draws one way of living they noticed and says one word label. An adult may scribe the label.
