# Unit 6 record sheet: one country, many people

Write each child's name down the left. The spoken why of each daily exit is heard from a rotating six: the first six on days 56, 60, 64 and 68, the second six on days 57, 61 and 65, the third six on days 58 and 62, the fourth six on days 59, 63 and 66. Keep a column naming which six each child is in. Each E column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card, by pointing, circling, placing, matching or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Mark what is seen on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child not heard stays "not yet seen", never guessed.

The retrieval prompts of days 56, 59 and 62 go here as teaching notes, one mark each; they are never scored. The day 58 chart, the day 61 drawn or dictated claim and the day 66 frame are judged on four look-fors each, written in the section plan, and are not scored.

The End columns are the day 67 observation, one cell a look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Record every component of items 1 to 6 separately and write evidence beside each, such as a dictated phrase. Items 3, 4 and 6 are heard in five groups of six, about two minutes a group. A child not heard, or with an unresolved explanation, is heard on day 68, about 35 seconds each, and the date goes in the cell.

Day 62 to 66 work is practice, not scored. The day 67 items are scored only from each child's own unaided answers on the fresh packet. Record paper evidence and spoken evidence apart. A child may pass. Never write a count of reminders, and never post this sheet where it ranks children. Never record a child's home, family, belief or background; nobody is asked.
