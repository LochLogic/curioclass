# Day 58: Weather and a choice

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put out each pair's chart, map and tokens. Keep the drawings and unit 5's map up. Keep exit card 58 closed. Teacher keys: key-chart-two-places.md, key-map-three-cities.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

3 minutes, error H from day 57 (a custom called wrong): read the speaker first. Made-up Thistle Market: Pip says "That bell is wrong." The speaker says: "We ring the bell so everyone knows the market is open." Say what it means to them. 2 minutes, error I from day 57 (a sign called land): two bins; sort four new cards: a handshake, a river, a language lesson, a hot sun.

#### 3 min. Reopen

Review: the map key and the directions north, south, east and west from unit 5. Show the map of three cities. "Find Nairobi with the key. Which city is farthest north?" Expect: Sapporo. North is where the N arrow points. If day 55's sheet showed error C, trace the arrow; if error K, ask "What do you see that says so?"

#### 5 min. Model a row

Nothing here is new. On the two-place chart, fill the Singapore column: weather, warm days and a lot of rain; what people do about it, light clothes. Say: "Weather changes what we wear." Pip says error B: "It is cold, so the people in made-up Briar Town speak slowly." Correct: "Snow changes what we wear. Language is something people learn." Finish: "Weather can change what we wear. It does not decide a word or a custom."

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs fill the chart for Sapporo and Singapore from the drawings: weather, what people do about it, one thing the picture cannot tell. Each pair finds both cities on its map; each child places one token and says one row, 30 seconds a child. Hear the third six at the exit cards, 40 seconds each, one spoken ask: 4 minutes. A child may point.

#### 3 min. Exit

Everyone marks exit card 58. [[s06-X58]] Say: "These are practice pictures." Read: "Place one token on Sapporo and one on Nairobi."

#### 4 min. Co-write

A child says one sentence from the chart, and you write it, answering "How do people live in these places?" "Tomorrow: one country, many people."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: use two buttons.

**Skip.** The chart, Pip and the heard six.

- **10 min. Weather.** Look at a street drawing. Your child says what the weather changes there.
  **Say.** "What does the weather change here? What does it not change?" Expect: what people wear; not a word.
- **15 min. A map.** Find a place with the map key; say its direction.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X58]] Show the card. Your child places tokens.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one row. Longer: add Nairobi to the chart.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: weather, choice, key. Frame: "Weather changes ___. It does not decide ___." A child may point.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 repairs error B (weather taken as the cause of a word or custom) for the class: weather changes a coat; it does not teach a word. A swapped pair of tokens has no letter: read the key again.
