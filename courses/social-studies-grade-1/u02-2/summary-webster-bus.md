# Webster bus summary (day 16)

Read aloud by the teacher. Say first: "This is our own short summary of a caption from a historical society. We do not have its picture here."

In 1928, children going to school at Webster Consolidated School in Keokuk County, Iowa, rode a horse-drawn bus. The caption we read, from the State Historical Society of Iowa, does not say how long the ride took.

Print the two sentences and the credit once, in large type, under the first row of the chart: "Our summary of a caption, Courtesy of State Historical Society of Iowa, 1928." Do not print or copy the society's image.

## For the teacher

This is one school in one place in one year. Say "I think ___, because our summary says ___" and never "all schools". Ask what the summary does not tell us: how long the ride took, whether other schools did the same.
