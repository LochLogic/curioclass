# Exit cards, days 14 to 17 and 19

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say aloud first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each invented card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a kind card, a repair card, the chart, the Johnston print or a day 18 card.

## Day 14 exit card: two cards

**Picture:** Two cards side by side. Left: a drawn map of a made-up old town, a river, a bridge and six small houses. Right: a recording icon (a small speaker) beside a drawing of a made-up person with a speech mark. Under each card, three small circles to mark: a map picture, a camera picture and a mouth-and-sound picture, with the words map, photograph, story people tell.

**Mark:** Circle each card's kind. **Spoken:** "Is either card a real clue from the past?"

## Day 15 exit card: our school's yard

**Picture:** At the top, the words "How did our school's yard change?" and a drawing of a schoolyard with a fence. Below, two ringed choices. Left: three small old-looking photographs of a yard. Right: today's lunch menu with a few drawn items.

**Mark:** Circle the more helpful source. **Spoken:** "Why?"

## Day 16 exit card: two classrooms

**Picture:** Two drawings of a pretend classroom side by side. Left, "long ago": a hanging lamp on a chain, a teacher's table with a book on it, children's seats in rows. Right, "today": a flat ceiling light, a teacher's table with a book on it, children's seats in rows. The lamp and the table each have a ring around them.

**Mark:** Circle one thing that stayed the same. **Spoken:** "What changed between the two?"

## Day 17 exit card: Cedar School

**Picture:** Two cards side by side. Left: a drawing of a school gate with a post on each side and a date in the corner. Right: a recording icon beside a drawing of a made-up person with a speech mark, labelled "a former Cedar pupil." Under both: "Cedar School."

**Mark:** Circle the source for the question "What did Cedar's gate look like?" **Spoken:** "Which source could help us find out what lessons at Cedar were like long ago, and why?"

## Day 19 exit cards

**Card a.** A January month page, large, seven columns headed by weekday names, the squares numbered 1 to 31 and empty. Beside it two small pictures to place, each a book with a patch of tape on its cover. Under the page: "A class repaired books on January 5 and on January 12." Read aloud. **Mark:** Place both repair pictures on the page, each on its date. **Spoken:** "Describe the pattern."

**Card b.** Three small cards side by side: a camera picture headed "photograph," an envelope picture headed "letter" and a flat map picture headed "map." Under them, a drawing of an old folded paper with a stamp in one corner and a name written on its front, printed "invented for practice." Say "This card is made up." **Mark:** Circle its kind.
