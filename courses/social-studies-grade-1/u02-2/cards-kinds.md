# Kind cards and kind boxes (days 14 and 15)

Say first: "We made these cards. The people in them are made up." Every invented card is printed "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. A real source is printed with its credit.

## The six kind boxes

Six open boxes, each headed by one word and one small picture: **photograph** (a camera), **letter** (an envelope), **object** (a hand holding a thing), **map** (a flat drawing seen from above), **building** (a house front), **story people tell** (a mouth and a sound mark). Pairs say "This ___ is a ___."

## The teacher's six models (day 14, about 50 seconds each)

For each, say the four source questions in this order: what kind, who made it (known or not), old or new, what could it help us find out.

1. **Photograph:** the Johnston classroom print (real; the maker is known; old).
2. **Letter:** "Dear Room 4, the garden tools come on Tuesday. From Mo." (made up; the maker is known, Mo; new, we made it).
3. **Object:** a drawing of an old wooden ruler with its numbers worn away (made up; the maker is not known; old). If the school office keeps a real older school object, the teacher may hold it up instead and say where it came from.
4. **Map:** the teacher's drawing of today's reading corner seen from above (new; the maker is known, the teacher).
5. **Building:** a photograph of our own school building (the maker is known, the teacher; new).
6. **Story people tell:** a made-up school helper, Ms. Dale, tells the class how she drove a school bus long ago (read aloud by the teacher, made up; the maker is known; old in the telling).

## The twelve cards for pairs (day 14)

Pairs sort twelve cards into the six boxes. Each box gets two cards. The six models above are not used again.

- **Photograph:** a pretend photograph of a stone fountain in a park; a pretend photograph of a row of coat hooks.
- **Letter:** "Dear Room 4, your seeds came in. From Quinn." and "Dear Room 4, the paint is on the shelf. From Rae."
- **Object:** a drawn wooden spinning top; a drawn brass key.
- **Map:** a drawn map of a school garden; a drawn map of a made-up village green.
- **Building:** a drawing of an old barn; a drawing of a small brick post office.
- **Story people tell:** a recording of a made-up baker telling how she walked to school; a recording of a made-up gardener telling about a winter morning.

## The helper's mistake (day 14, error L)

The made-up helper looks at the Johnston print and says "The children are sad." The class repairs it: we say what we see; the caption does not tell feelings or who they are.
