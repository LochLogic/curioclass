# Day 18: Show what you know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet closed until now. Take down completed examples; leave the blank year strip, month pages and kind pictures up. Open the record sheet. Table: the third six, about 72 seconds in each item part. Day 17's exit errors M and J wait for day 19.

### The day

#### 5 min. Orient

Say "Today I find out what you already know. I will not help you choose." Read the packet aloud. Collect each paper part at the end of its item. Say the cards are made up, except the photograph: Frances Benjamin Johnston, Library of Congress, about 1899. Read its caption aloud: "Grade school children posed in classroom, with teacher standing in back of room, Washington, D.C."

#### 5 min. Item 1

Everyone places three leaf drawings on the October page: a pretend class drew leaves on October 6, 13 and 20. [[s02-E1]] The third six, in turn: "What repeats? Show the dates." Mark each part shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 6 min. Item 2

Everyone draws one classroom experience on today's real square. [[s02-E2]] The third six, in turn, over the three picture cards (story date October 21; say "children draw leaves, display the leaves, label the leaves"): "Tell what the children did yesterday, do today and will do tomorrow." Sentences revised on days 14 to 17 count for the tenses; a child with none is heard here.

#### 4 min. Item 3

Everyone places a music day on December 8, a first assembly on September 15 and a book swap on April 11 on the blank year strip, earliest at the left. [[s02-E3]] [[s02-E3b]] The third six: "Which came before the assembly?"

#### 5 min. Item 4

Everyone circles the kind of the new photograph and of the letter "Dear Teacher, our class used a bell to begin lessons. From Lee.", and ticks pretend or real for the letter. [[s02-E4]] The third six: "What does the photograph show about that classroom?" For a child who cannot see it, say: children sit at wooden desks in rows; a person stands at the back.

#### 5 min. Item 5

Say these are described sources, not records that exist. Everyone circles the more useful for finding out what our school's old front sign looked like: a dated photograph of the sign or a recording about another school's lunch, and draws a reason. [[s02-E5]] The third six: "To find out what lessons at our school were like long ago, name another kind of source and what it might tell us."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw an October page, a year strip and two sources.

**Skip.** The groups and the rotating six.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Draw an October page and a strip. Say "A class drew leaves on the 6th, 13th and 20th." Your child places them. Draw a photograph and a letter; say the letter is made up. Ask which source would show an old school sign.
  **Say.** "What repeats? What kind is each? Which source helps, and why?" Expect: once a week; photograph and letter; the sign photograph, because it could show the sign.
- **10 min. Respond.** Note one part to try again. Give no help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 1, 4 and 5; the rest wait for day 19.

### Words and supports

Read every prompt aloud. Pointing, drawing, home-language words and dictation count.

### Tomorrow

Day 19's respond part follows day 18's assessment. Its groups, from the record sheet: calendar and year strip (errors G, F), verb forms (errors B, H), kinds (errors C, I, L), choosing (errors D, J, M). The children not heard today come first.
