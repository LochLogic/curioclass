# Washboard and washing machine drawings (day 14, review only)

Kindergarten's then-and-now pair, shown again as a retrieval. Two original drawings, plain black line on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Say first: "We drew these pictures for our class."

- **Drawing 1:** a ridged wooden washboard standing in a tub of water, a cloth over the ridges.
- **Drawing 2:** a washing machine with a round door, a basket of clean cloth beside it.

The teacher asks "What differs?" and then "What work do both help people do?" Different tools help wash clothes. Do not suggest that washboards vanished.

## For a miss

Model change and continuity with a hand fan and an electric fan during teaching, then try another pair. If more than a third miss, model for everyone; otherwise guide a small group at supported work. Record observations, not a score.
