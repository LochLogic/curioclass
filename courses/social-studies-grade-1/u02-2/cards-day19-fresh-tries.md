# Day 19 fresh-try cards and answer picture

Kept closed until day 19. For the fresh try, one unused card for each error letter a group works on, and the answer picture for the self-check. Say first: "These cards are made up." Print "invented for practice" on each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading. Never an assessment case or an exit case.

## Fresh-try cards (about four minutes in each group)

- **G or A (the calendar):** a December page with a drawing of a snowman on December 1, 8 and 15, all in one column; empty squares between. Place the three on their dates and say what repeats and what the record does not say.
- **F (the year strip):** four dated cards, a pumpkin drawn on October 2, a bucket washed on July 14, a snow shovel put away on March 9, a scarf drive on November 20. Order them by month on the strip.
- **B or H (verb forms):** a child sweeps a porch: show three squares, one with a swept porch, one with a child sweeping and one with a dotted drawing of a clean porch; say did, do and will do.
- **C or I or L (kinds):** a pretend photograph of a covered bridge, a letter "Dear Room 4, your plants need water. From Sam.", a drawn old kettle, a drawn map of a village green, a drawing of a town hall and a recording of a made-up fisherman; sort them by kind and say which are made up.
- **D or J (choosing a source):** the question "What did the old bridge look like?" with a photograph of the bridge and a birthday invitation; and a caption card "Linden School, 1930", whose school is it?
- **M (a detail in a claim):** the Linden School front steps card with the claim frame "I think ___, because it shows ___."

## The answer picture (self-check)

A printed picture of the day 19 exit answers, kept face down until the end: a January page with the repair pictures on January 5 and January 12, and the letter card circled. Each child compares the try with it and marks "same" or "not the same yet". One child follows the same cycle with an adult.
