# Day 19: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 18's assessment cells and day 17's exit. Four groups, named by a picture: calendar and year strip (errors G, F); verb forms (errors B, H); kinds (errors C, I, L); choosing (errors D, J, M). A group over a third of the class goes first. Answer picture face down. Table: children not heard on day 18.

### The day

#### 5 min. Review the evidence

Show each group its own work and say what was shown, never what was missed. Items 1 and 3 go to the calendar group; item 2 to verb forms; item 4 to kinds; item 5 to choosing.

#### 10 min. Move groups

Review: in kindergarten you practiced what you still needed in move groups. About 2 and a half minutes at each table, with a new teaching case; the other groups pair on their move card. Calendar group: point to the dates a pretend class fed a fish, May 3 and May 17; an empty square does not say what happened, then order a puppet show, February 24, a lemonade stand, July 3, a bike parade, July 19 and a puzzle table, October 22, by month, then day. Verb forms: act a finished and a coming action and place each on its square; a child plants a seed; on the three-column strip say "planted, plants, will plant." Kinds: sort a pretend photograph of a swing set, a letter from Jo and a map of a pond path; read the label first for error I, look and name for error L. Choosing: match "How did the baker get flour?" to a recording of a baker rather than a photograph of a shop; read the caption "Pine Street Bakery, 1925" and ask "whose shop is this?"; add a detail to the claim frame, "it shows the big window."

#### 10 min. Fresh try

Everyone marks exit cards 19a and 19b (3 minutes). [[s02-X19a]] [[s02-X19b]] Then each group tries its own unused card from the fresh-try cards, 4 minutes. The children not heard on day 18 come first to the table for the spoken part of card 19a, about 60 seconds each (6 minutes). Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Self-check

Turn the answer picture over. Each child compares the try and says "same" or "not the same yet." One child does the same with an adult. Leftover needs go to unit 3.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No answer picture: draw a January page with the books on the 5th and 12th.

**Skip.** The groups and the side table.

- **5 min. Look back.** Name one part from day 18 to try again.
- **10 min. Move.** Re-teach it with a made-up case: events on a strip, did, do and will do, sort a photograph, a letter and a map, or pick the source that helps.
  **Say.** "What kind is it? Which source helps? Why?" Expect: a kind, a source that fits, a reason from what it shows.
- **5 min. Check.** Compare with a drawn answer.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X19a]] [[s02-X19b]] Draw a January page; your child places two books on the 5th and 12th, then circles the letter.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group, one fresh card. Longer: your child teaches a toy the move.

### Words and supports

Words: same, not the same yet. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." Pointing and home-language words count.

### Tomorrow

Unit 3 begins on day 20 with what a leader is, and a crossing guard. The source questions come back there; leftover needs from day 19 go with it.
