# Day 14: Clues from the past

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the Johnston dog classroom print large with credit and caption. Lay kind cards, kind boxes and the year strip at each pair. Open the class calendar. Table: the fourth six.

### The day

#### 3 min. Calendar corner

Date today's square. Group A revise a sentence from your picture feedback, group B say a fresh one, about 12 seconds each. The rest draw their square.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

If many ordered by the card read first on day 13 (error F), order four new dated cards, month then day: November 4, mitten; April 22, kite; August 25, sandcastle; April 9, seeds. Say "___ came before ___ because ___."

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Review: in kindergarten you drew a washboard and a washing machine. **Ask.** What differs? Expect: ridges and a tub; a round door. **Ask.** What work do both help people do? Expect: wash clothes. Washboards did not vanish. If over a third miss, show a hand fan and an electric fan.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Say "A source is something from the past that helps us find out." A card marked invented for practice is not a real clue. Photograph first. Review: you met this photograph on day 11, another school, by Frances Benjamin Johnston, Library of Congress, about 1899. Look first, name only what we see, read the caption aloud. Ask the four source questions: What kind is it? Who made it, known or not? Old or new? What could it help us find out? Expect: photograph; Johnston, known; old. The made-up helper says "The children are sad" (error L); the caption gives no feelings.

Ask the four of five more, about 50 seconds each (cards-kinds): letter, object, map, building, story people tell. Review: photograph, object and map are from kindergarten; the rest are new. If asked why old schools differ, say only: "In the past, some public schools kept children apart by race. That was unfair, and families went to court to change it."

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs sort twelve made-up cards into six boxes: "This ___ is a ___." Guide retrieval misses 3 minutes. The fourth six come to the table, about 70 seconds each, to mark exit card 14 and answer aloud (7 minutes).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Say the cards are made up. Everyone circles each card's kind on exit card 14. [[s02-X14]] Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names stand-ins.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: draw six boxes on scrap paper.

**Skip.** The pairs and the side table.

- **5 min. Washing.** Draw a washboard and a washing machine.
  **Say.** "What differs? What work do both help people do?" Expect: how they look; both wash clothes.
- **15 min. Six kinds.** Make up one card for each box: photograph, letter, object, map, building, story people tell. Say "This is made up." Your child says "This ___ is a ___."
  **Say.** "What kind is it? Who made it? Old or new?" Expect: the kind; you; new.
- **5 min. Real or made up.** Ask whether a made-up card is a real clue.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X14]] Draw a map and a mouth with a sound mark. Your child circles each kind.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four kinds. Longer: a second object.

### Words and supports

Frame: "This ___ is a ___." A point or home-language word counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 opens with today's exit: error C, error I and error L, then asks which kind helps our school question.
