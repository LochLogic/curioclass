# Source drawing sheet (day 17)

One sheet for each child, large print, about a page. The teacher reads the words aloud; a child's own mark counts.

## Front

- **Our big question** (read aloud, with its picture): How can we find out what our school was like long ago?
- **My source:** a big empty box to draw one source the class studied (the Johnston photograph, our classroom record or the bus summary) and, inside the box, one detail from it.
- **Its kind:** a line to copy one kind word, photograph, letter, map, object, building or story people tell.
- **My claim:** "I think we can find out ___ with a ___, because ___."

## Back

A blank box for a plan of sources to find. Another school's photograph cannot show our school's history. A plan of sources to find is a good answer.

## For the teacher

The teacher writes the child's spoken claim beside the drawing. The rotating six are heard at the table; their words go beside the drawing too. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Formal written claims begin in unit 3.
