# Day 17: I think we can find out

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the big question, the chart, the Johnston print and the bus summary in view. Table: the second six.

### The day

#### 3 min. Calendar corner

Date today's square on the class calendar. Group D revise a sentence from your picture feedback, about 15 seconds each. The rest draw one past event on their square and copy a time word from the board: yesterday, last week or last month. Collect the squares as the record.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Use day 16's exit and the day 17 repair card. Errors E and K: fill the chart for a made-up pair, a hand-turned then a plug-in pencil sharpener. Changed: how it is turned. Stayed: it sharpens a pencil. Not known: when the first was made; whether every school had one. Say "this is one pair, not every school."

#### 5 min. Reopen

Point to the posted big question: How can we find out what our school was like long ago? Say the four source questions. Review: the claim "I think ___, because ___" from kindergarten now names a source: "I think we can find out ___ with a ___, because ___." Give no model.

#### 16 min. Supported work

Each child draws one source on the source drawing sheet: the Johnston print, a page of our class calendar or the bus summary, with one detail, and copies the kind word. Then tell a partner the claim. Another school's photograph cannot show our school's history; a plan of sources to find is a good answer. The second six come to the table, about 80 seconds each, to mark exit card 17 and answer aloud (8 minutes); write their words beside the drawing. Look for: a source kind; a detail; a reason that links the detail to the question, not a liking. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Everyone circles the source for the question on exit card 17. [[s02-X17]]

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed sheet: fold a paper and draw one big box.

**Skip.** The pairs, the side table and the repair.

- **5 min. Question.** Say "How can we find out what our school was like long ago?"
- **5 min. Frame.** Say "I think we can find out ___ with a ___, because ___." Model nothing.
- **15 min. Draw.** Your child draws a clue from the unit and one detail, copies its kind word and says the claim.
  **Say.** "What can we find out? With which kind? Why?" Expect: a kind, a detail and a reason, not a liking.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X17]] Draw a photograph and a person talking. Your child circles one for a question you ask and says why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one claim. Longer: a plan of sources on the back.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I think we can find out ___ with a ___, because ___." Pointing, dictation and home-language words count.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 is the observation: nothing taught, no help. Errors M and J from today's exit go to day 19's move groups.
