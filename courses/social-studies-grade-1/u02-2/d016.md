# Day 16: Then and now

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the chart. Tape the bus summary and its credit under row one. Draw today's room with seats, board and wall pictures only. If the school office holds a dated photograph of our school, print it beside the Johnston print. Table: first six.

### The day

#### 3 min. Calendar corner

Date today's square on the class calendar. Group C revise a sentence from your picture feedback, group D say a fresh one, about 12 seconds each. The rest draw their square.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Use day 15's exit and the day 16 repair cards. Error D (one favorite kind for every question): match four new questions to the kind that fits, with the frame. Error J (another school's picture taken for ours): read two captions aloud and ask "whose school is this?"

#### 7 min. Model: then and now with a claim

Review: in kindergarten you filled a then-and-now chart with changed and stayed the same. Ours gains a column, what we do not know. Row one, getting to school. Read our two-sentence bus summary aloud, with its credit. Beside it, our bus drawing. Changed: how the bus moved. Stayed: children rode a bus to school. Not known: how long the ride took. Say "I think children rode a bus to school in 1928, because our summary says they rode a horse-drawn bus." One school, not every school (error K). If asked why schools differed, say only: "In the past, some public schools kept children apart by race. That was unfair, and families went to court to change it."

#### 14 min. Supported work

Pairs fill two rows, the room and what the person at the left wears, using the Johnston print (another school) beside today's drawing of our room, or our own school's photograph. Name what we see and cannot tell: "In the photograph ___, but today ___; both ___." Details: desks in rows, a chalkboard with writing, framed pictures, a long skirt, a light blouse. Never say who the children are or how they felt. The first six come to the table, about 80 seconds each, to mark exit card 16 and answer aloud (8 minutes).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Everyone circles one thing that stayed, exit card 16. [[s02-X16]] Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names stand-ins.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed chart: draw three columns on a paper.

**Skip.** The pairs, the side table and the repair.

- **5 min. Chart.** Draw the columns then, now and what we do not know, and a row for what stayed.
- **5 min. Model.** Read: "In 1928, children going to school at Webster Consolidated School in Keokuk County, Iowa, rode a horse-drawn bus. The caption does not say how long the ride took." Your child draws a school bus today.
  **Say.** "What changed? What stayed? What do we not know?" Expect: how the bus moved; a bus to school; how long the ride took.
- **15 min. A room.** Draw your room now and one from long ago in a picture book; say "In the picture ___, but today ___; both ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X16]] Draw two rooms that share one thing. Your child circles what stayed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one row. Longer: a third row.

### Words and supports

Frames: "In the photograph ___, but today ___; both ___." "I think ___, because ___." A point or home-language word counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 opens with today's exit: error E and error K, then each child tells a claim from a source.

### Credit

Ours, except the photograph (Frances Benjamin Johnston, Library of Congress) and our summary of a caption courtesy of the State Historical Society of Iowa, 1928.
