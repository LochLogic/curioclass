# Teacher key, the claim frame, day 78

Teacher only. Keep apart from sheet-claim-frame-day-78.md; never hand to children.

## Key

**Look-fors.** Box 1, a claim about change: something changed after we acted, or little did, and something remains. Box 2, a reason from a dated record (a dated drawing, the log or the tally), not a liking or one picture. Box 3, a closing that starts with so and says why it matters. Drawing, pointing and dictation count. Practice, not scored.
