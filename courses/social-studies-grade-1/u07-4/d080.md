# Day 80: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet make at most four groups, the most-missed item first: item 1 errors A, N, F; item 2 B; item 3 L; item 4 O, J; item 5 H, P; item 6 B. Lay out the move cards and the fresh-try cards. Keep exit card 80 closed. Say: "These people and places are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Each child gets day 79's assessment back, their own packet, each part marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, with a picture of the move for a part missed. If many made error E on day 78's exit, lay the baseline beside the day 78 drawing and say one change and one thing that remains (90 seconds). If many made error K, ask for the date on each picture and put both in order (90 seconds). **Ask.** "Which part will you try again?" Expect: a point to a part.

#### 10 min. Reteach

Review from kindergarten: a group re-teaches one move, then tries again. Meet each group about 2 minutes; the others pair and work from the move card, then an unused case. Item 1, errors A, N, F: a made-up resident who is not a citizen belongs, with a right and a responsibility. Item 2, B: a quality joined to a deed. Item 3, L: then-and-now pictures and a row for what stayed. Item 4, O, J: plan or record, and a problem near, in a region and far. Item 5, H, P: one small, safe part with an adult. Item 6, B: claim, reason from the source, closing. Never rehearse day 79's cases.

#### 10 min. Fresh try

Everyone marks exit card 80 (1 minute). [[s07-X80]] [[s07-X80b]] Say: "This record is made up." Hear six children, the ones not heard on day 79 first, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Each child tries the missed part on a fresh card, for its item only. Hear up to twelve fresh-try asks, 30 seconds each (6 minutes). A child not heard is heard in the next rotation, never by a whole-class wait.

#### 5 min. Review

Each group says its move aloud once. Update the record sheet: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, one column for the paper and one for the spoken part.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a new made-up card for the part that was hard.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard children.

- **5 min. Look back.** Look at the marked work together. Your child picks one part.
- **20 min. Try again.** Draw a new made-up card for that part.
  **Say.** "What changed in your answer? What is your reason?" Expect: a reason now pointed to.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X80]] [[s07-X80b]] Show the card and read it. Your child places the pictures and answers.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group, one try. Longer: a second part tried again.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: reason, change, stayed. Frame: "I think ___ because ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 81 begins unit 8: what it means to be employed. No readiness check is needed; day 80's results go to the record sheet.
