# Fresh-try cards, day 80

Say aloud: "These people and places are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" on each. Ask only the missed parts, with a paper part and a spoken part. No card reuses a day 79 case.

## Item 1: Kit, Lou and Max

Kit was born here. Lou became a citizen later, as an adult, by following the steps the law sets. Max lives here and is not a citizen. Paper: match each person to a route. Spoken: "Say a right Max has."

## Items 2, 3 and 6: a pantry delivery

A community pantry first sent a hand cart, and later a small truck, to bring food to neighbors. Ask only the missed item, so no card repeats an exit's shape. Item 2: paper, circle the deed that shows a quality, "brings food to neighbors" or "the cart is red"; spoken, "Name a quality and the deed." Item 3: paper, three boxes to draw in, long ago, later and the same; spoken, "What changed, and what stayed?" Item 6: paper, three boxes, claim, reason from the card and closing, about whether to keep bringing food to neighbors.

## Item 4: a garden invitation, a town and a region

A community garden invites neighbors to pull weeds on Saturday (a plan, not a record). Two invented cards: in a town, a path floods when it rains, so workers add a drain; across a region, towns share one trail, so workers plan one map. Paper: circle each problem. Spoken: "Does the invitation show the garden is tidy?"

## Item 5: another way to help

For each of the two cards, draw one safe, child-sized way to help, with an adult. Spoken: "Name one safe helping step."
