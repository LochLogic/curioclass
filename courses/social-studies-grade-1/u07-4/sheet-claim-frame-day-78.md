# Claim frame, day 78

Name ______________________

Three boxes, each with a small picture clue and a line. Box 1, a speech bubble: "I think ______________." Box 2, a small calendar: "because ______________." Box 3, the word "so": "so ______________." Beside box 3, the label "closing". Beside the frame, a word bank with a picture for each word: claim, reason, closing, remain.

**Use:** Read aloud. A child says or draws each box; an adult may scribe. The reason comes from a dated record. Practice, not scored.
