# Day 78: What changed after we acted?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the baseline sheet, staff note, log and both views. Lay out each judgment sheet with its note, the columns and the model pictures. Keep exit card 78 closed; claim frame blank. Teacher key: key-sheet-claim-frame-unit6.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Observe and action routine

Review from unit 2: write the date, month, number, year. Draw our baseline place and tally the same count the same way (2 minutes). One child draws today's log line. If a count cannot be repeated, compare drawings and the staff account, and say so.

#### 3 min. Warm-up and repair

If many made error C or O on day 77's exit, Pip says: "People far away cannot help themselves." "Only our school has this problem." Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Add a helper at each place on the strip (2 minutes). Label card J's four cards plan or record (1 minute, error J).

#### 5 min. Return and revise

Each child hears the note on the day 76 sheet read aloud, then changes or adds one thing, a quality or a detail (4 minutes). The revised sheet counts. A liking is not a detail (error B).

#### 3 min. Model a look back

Say this class is made up. Review from kindergarten: "Before, ___. After, ___. It helped, but ___ remains." Lay two dated pictures of a water-bottle shelf, May 4 and May 18: before, a jumble; after, a row; it helped, but the bottles have no names. Dates show change, not cause.

#### 5 min. Our look back

Lay the baseline beside today's drawing, the staff note and the log. Review from kindergarten, the four look-back questions: how did we decide, did it help, what shows that, what could we change. **Ask.** "What changed after we acted, and what remains?" Expect: a change or none, by a dated drawing or count; something remains (error E). Fill the columns, say our claim once, then cover them.

#### 5 min. Claim, reason, closing

Show the blank frame from unit 6: "I think ___ because ___ so ___." The reason comes from a dated record (error K: one picture is not enough). Each child writes, draws or dictates three boxes; hear the first six, 30 seconds each, meanwhile.

#### 2 min. Pair check and revise

Partners tick a speech bubble, a calendar and "so" on the checklist and add one thing. Practice, not scored.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 78 (1 minute). [[s07-X78]] Hear the fifth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw one thing that remains.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No columns: fold a page in two, what changed and what remains.

**Skip.** The pair check and the heard six.

- **10 min. Before and after.** Draw a made-up town park before a bench was added and after, with dates.
  **Say.** "What changed, and what remains?" Expect: a bench was added; something still needs care.
- **15 min. A claim.** Your child fills three boxes: I think, because, so, about whether neighbors should keep watering a flower bed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X78]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles and names a quality.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the model and claim. Longer: a second thing that remains.

### Words and supports

Words: claim, reason, closing, remain. Frame: "Before ___; after ___; still ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 79 is the check, with a fresh packet. Day 80 repairs errors E and K.
