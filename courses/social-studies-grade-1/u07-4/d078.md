# Day 78: What changed after we acted?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the baseline sheet, staff note, log and both views. Lay out each judgment sheet, the columns and the model pictures. Keep exit card 78 closed. Keys: key-sheet-claim-frame-day-78.md, key-exits-78-80.md.

### The day

#### 1 min. Observe and action routine

Review from unit 2: write the date. One child draws today's log line. If a count cannot be repeated, compare drawings and say so.

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

If many made error C or O on day 77's exit, Pip says: "People far away cannot help themselves." "Only our school has this problem." Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Add a helper at each place on the strip (2 minutes). Label card J's four cards plan or record (2 minutes, error J).

#### 5 min. Return and revise

Each child reads the stamp on the day 76 sheet: a calendar means add a detail, a swap arrow means change the quality. Children change or add one thing (4 minutes). The revised sheet counts. A liking is not a detail (error B).

#### 3 min. Model a look back

Say this class is made up. Review from kindergarten: "Before, ___. After, ___. It helped, or did not, because ___." New: "and what remains." Lay two dated pictures of a water-bottle shelf, May 4 and May 18: a jumble, then a row; it helped, but the bottles have no names. Dates show change, not cause.

#### 6 min. Our look back

Redraw our baseline place and tally the same way (2 minutes); lay it beside today's drawing, the staff note and the log. Review: the four look-back questions. **Ask.** "What changed after we acted, and what remains?" Expect: a change or none, by a dated drawing or count; something remains (error E). Fill the columns, say our claim once, then cover them.

#### 6 min. Claim, reason, closing

Show the blank day 78 frame. A closing says why it matters; today it starts with so. The reason comes from a dated record (error K). Each child writes, draws or dictates three boxes; hear the first six, 30 seconds each, meanwhile.

#### 2 min. Pair check and revise

Partners tick a speech bubble, a calendar and "so" on the checklist and add one thing. Practice, not scored.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Read exit card 78 aloud; say it is made up. [[s07-X78]] Everyone circles one answer. Collect the cards.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No columns: fold a page in two, what changed and what remains.

**Skip.** The pair check and the heard six.

- **10 min. Before and after.** Draw a made-up town park before a bench was added and after, with dates.
  **Say.** "What changed, and what remains?" Expect: a bench was added; something still needs care.
- **15 min. A claim.** Your child fills three boxes: I think, because, so, about whether neighbors should keep watering a flower bed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X78]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles one answer.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the model and claim. Longer: a second thing that remains.

### Words and supports

Words: claim, reason, closing (starts with so), remain (a picture of a half-empty box). Frame: "Before ___; after ___; still ___." A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 79 is the check, with a fresh packet. Day 80 repairs errors E and K.
