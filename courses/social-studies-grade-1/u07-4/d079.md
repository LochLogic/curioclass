# Day 79: What we know about helping: the check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet closed until now. Cover every completed model in the room: the strip, the columns, the quality cards. Only blank frames stay. Plan five groups of six from the record sheet. Say: "Nola, Paz and Ren are made up. Sources A and B are real places. The three place cards are made up."

### The day

#### 5 min. Hear the packet

Read Source A, a library's book wagon in Maryland, then Source B, a park's invitation to clean up, each twice, with pictures; the printed copies stay in view. Then read the three place cards, near, region and far, each with a picture. No talk.

#### 3 min. The six items

Read each paper item once, with no hints. [[s07-E1]] Nola, Paz and Ren. [[s07-E2]] A quality in Source A. [[s07-E3]] The wagon and the motor in order. [[s07-E4]] [[s07-E4b]] A problem at each place. [[s07-E5]] One safe way to help. [[s07-E6]] Claim, reason and closing about Source A.

#### 20 min. Paper parts and spoken groups

Each child does the six paper parts alone, about three minutes each, items 4 and 6 four; a child who finishes draws. Meanwhile call five groups of six, 3 minutes each, 30 seconds a child, one ask each: group one, item 1, "Does Ren belong? Why?"; group two, item 2, "Name a quality and one detail from A."; group three, item 4, "Name a problem near and what people do."; group four, item 5, "Name one safe helping step."; group five, item 1, "Say a right Ren has." A child who needs to dictate item 6 does it at that turn. Mark paper and spoken evidence apart: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child not heard is heard on day 80.

#### 2 min. Hand in

Collect the packets. After class, about 15 minutes: mark each part, and group the errors for day 80: item 1 A, N, F; item 2 B; item 3 L; item 4 O, J; item 5 H, P; item 6 B. Judge meaning and evidence, not drawing.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: make up three people and a short story about a helper.

**Skip.** The heard groups and the packet pictures.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Give no hints. Tell a made-up helper story twice, then ask.
  **Say.** "What did the helper do, and what quality does it show?" Expect: a deed; a quality with that detail.
  **Say.** "Name a made-up person who is not a citizen. Does the person belong?" Expect: yes; lives here, with rights.
- **5 min. Respond.** Note one part that was hard; ask again on a new made-up story.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 2 and 6. Longer: a second claim.

### Words and supports

Read every item aloud. Words: quality, region, plan, record, citizen. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 80 acts on this check: marked packets, groups by most-missed item, then a fresh try. Errors E and K from day 78 return first.
