# A model look back, day 78

Say aloud: "This class is made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines.

## The case: the water-bottle shelf

A different invented class, with two dated pictures of the same corner:

1. **May 4:** water bottles in a jumble by the door; no shelf.
2. **May 18:** a low shelf with a row of bottles. The bottles have no names on them.

Frame, read aloud: "Before, ___. After, ___. It helped, but ___ remains."

Model: "Before, the bottles were in a jumble by the door. After, they stand in a row on a shelf. It helped, but the bottles still have no names." Say aloud: dated pictures show change. They do not show that the shelf caused it, and two pictures do not show what will happen next week.
