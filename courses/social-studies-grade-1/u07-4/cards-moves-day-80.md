# Written moves, day 80

One card for each item. Each card is a picture, a few words and one move. The teacher meets each group for about two minutes; other children pair and work from the card, then try a fresh case. Do not use any day 79 case.

- **Item 1:** show a made-up resident who is not a citizen belonging, with a right and a responsibility. Move: "lives here, belongs, has rights."
- **Item 2:** link a quality to a deed. Move: "the quality is ___ because the person ___."
- **Item 3:** lay the then-and-now pictures and a row for what stayed. Move: "long ago ___, later ___, the same ___."
- **Item 4:** sort plan and record, then name a problem at each of near, region and far. Move: "a plan says hope; a dated record says what happened."
- **Item 5:** one small, safe part for children, with an adult. Move: "we do ___ with an adult; adults do ___."
- **Item 6:** claim, reason from the source, closing. Move: "I think ___ because the source says ___ so ___."

Errors E, K and B return here as well: E, one change and one thing that remains; K, the date on each picture; B, match a claim to a detail card.
