# Repair cards, section 7.2

Each repair card puts a wrong idea beside a right one. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most three or four minutes, or a small group when few missed it. Nobody is asked to say, stand or sing. No card reuses an exit, an end item or the day 79 packet.

## D: a different view is bad (day 73, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "The kitchen helper wants it his way, so his way is bad." **Right:** "Both reasons stay on the board: his, and ours." **Retry:** put each reason beside its view and say it back, on a fresh pair: one friend wants to kick the ball, one wants to throw it.

## I: only the winning side votes (day 74, 3 minutes)

**Pip:** "Only the side that is winning puts in a slip." **Right:** "Every child puts in one slip, then we count." **Retry:** rehearse one slip each with two paper boxes.

## P: we will fix everything (day 74 plan part)

**Pip:** "We will fix every problem in the whole school." **Right:** "We start with one small part that is safe and ours." **Retry:** sort four ideas onto "we can" and "adults do": we put our cups in the bin; adults build a new roof; we draw a reminder card; adults repair the road.

## H: a child may go alone (day 75, 4 minutes)

**Pip:** "I will go out alone and put up the signs." **Right:** "I make the signs with our teacher and we check the way." **Retry:** sort six step cards onto safe and unsafe, with the stop-and-tell rule: carry the book basket with the teacher down the hall; pick up a strange thing from the floor; tell a grown-up about a sharp thing; climb a ladder alone; draw a reminder at the table; walk to the road to look.
