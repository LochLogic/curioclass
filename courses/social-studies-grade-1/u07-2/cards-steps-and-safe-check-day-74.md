# Step cards and the safe-step check (day 74)

## Safe-step check (say it aloud before any step)

1. We stay inside the school.
2. An adult is with us.
3. Nothing unknown to touch.
4. If we find something unknown, we stop and tell an adult.

## Three step cards (the class fills them)

Each card: who does what, with which adult, in which place.
