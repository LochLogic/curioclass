# Day 73: Which way will we help?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang Our Decision Record and both views. Cut a three-picture slip for each child. Name four counters. Keep exit card 73 closed. If ideas run short: a counting card by the ball bin; a picture on each art tub; a hook for each rope.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Review from unit 4: a ballot is a paper one person marks; a secret ballot is marked in private. Say: one slip each, no names, counted together.

#### 3 min. Warm-up and repair

If many made error D on day 72's exit, Pip says: "The kitchen helper wants it his way, so his way is bad." Pip makes a mistake on purpose. Put each reason beside its view and say it back on a fresh pair: one friend wants to kick the ball, one to throw it.

#### 6 min. Ideas, then a filter

Review from kindergarten: an idea is something we could try. Partners whisper one idea (30 seconds); write ideas on cards. Filter aloud: small enough for us; inside school; an adult can help; nothing unknown to touch; no money. Ideas left become three proposal cards with a reason from the two views; others go on the board with the reason. We start with one small part, not everything (error P). Safety, rights and access are never voted.

#### 3 min. A vote, not an election

Review from unit 4: an election is voting to choose a leader for a job; a vote on a matter chooses a thing. Today's vote picks a plan; every child in our class may put in one slip. Show a slip: mark one picture, fold, drop it in the box. **Ask.** "Does this vote choose a plan or a leader?" Expect: a plan.

#### 5 min. Cast the ballots

Each child marks, folds and drops one slip, a row at a time, about 10 seconds a child. A child who cannot mark points; an adult marks, in private. Nobody shows a slip.

#### 4 min. Count

Four counters read marks aloud while the class counts the piles and you tally. A tie: a runoff between the top two. If still tied, try both in turn, as in kindergarten.

#### 2 min. Record

Fill Our Decision Record: date, problem, proposals, result, reasons. Unchosen proposals and both views stay up.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 73 (1 minute). [[s07-X73]] Hear the fifth six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw a folded slip going into the box.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No box: use a hat and scrap slips.

**Skip.** The count and the heard six.

- **10 min. A vote.** Name two made-up ways to help a park. Show a slip with two pictures.
  **Say.** "Is this a vote for a plan or a leader?" Expect: a plan.
- **15 min. Cast.** Your child and one grown-up each mark, fold and drop one slip. Count together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X73]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two proposals. Longer: explain one idea that failed the filter.

### Words and supports

Words: idea, vote, slip. A child may pass.
