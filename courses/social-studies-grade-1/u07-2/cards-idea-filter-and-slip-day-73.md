# Idea filter, proposal cards and the ballot slip (day 73)

## Idea filter (read aloud, one picture each)

An idea stays if it is: small enough for us; inside the school; something an adult can help with; nothing unknown to touch; no money. Ideas that fail go to the board with the reason. Safety, rights and access are never voted.

## Three proposal cards (the class fills them)

Each card: a picture, the idea in a few words, and a reason from the class's two views.

## A vote and an election (two cards)

- **A vote on a plan:** every child in the class, one slip, a plan is chosen.
- **An election:** voters choose a leader for a job (unit 4).
