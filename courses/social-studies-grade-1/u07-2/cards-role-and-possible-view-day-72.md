# Role cards for two real views (day 72)

Make one card for each adult. No face is needed, only a role.

Role: ______________ (for example a teacher, the caretaker, a lunch helper)

Said on (date): ______________

I see: ______________

I think ______________ because ______________.

## If only one adult view exists

Say aloud: "We have one adult view. Here is a second one, and it is made up." Print "possible view, invented for practice" at the top of the second card, and write a short reason the class can check against what it saw.
