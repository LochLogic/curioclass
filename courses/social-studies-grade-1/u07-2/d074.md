# Day 74: Our first step

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the chosen proposal from the Decision Record and cut three step cards (who, what, which adult, which place). Ask a second adult, such as an aide or a helper from the web, to be nearby. Keep exit card 74 closed. If our own way is making signs, use the alternate named in the exit cards file. Say: "These steps are ours, and adults help."

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up and repair

Read the Decision Record aloud. Day 73's exit decides. If many made error I, Pip says: "Only the side that is winning puts in a slip." Say: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Rehearse one slip each with two paper boxes, a row at a time, about 6 seconds a child.

#### 6 min. Plan the steps

Review from kindergarten: plan an action and take its first step together. Lay the three step cards: who does what, with which adult, in which place. Say the safe-step check aloud: we stay inside the school; an adult is with us; nothing unknown to touch; if we find something unknown, we stop and tell an adult. **Ask.** "Does this step pass the check?" Expect: yes or no, with the line it uses. Sort any child idea that fails with the reason (error H). If many made error P on day 73, take 2 minutes from the next part and sort four ideas onto "we can" and "adults do": we put our cups in the bin; adults build a new roof; we draw a reminder card; adults repair the road.

#### 14 min. Carry out the first step

Three groups of ten each take one pictured job, within sight of an adult. Seated, drawing and recording roles are offered. Move between groups for the safety check; hear no one in this part. A child who finds something unknown stops and tells an adult.

#### 2 min. Action log

Write the date, what we did, which groups, what is next, and add a picture. Say what we did, not what it caused.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 74 (1 minute). [[s07-X74]] Say: "This class is made up." Then hear the first six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes), while the others draw their own part of the job.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No step cards: draw three steps on a page.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard six.

- **10 min. Safe steps.** Say the check aloud, then plan one tiny, safe step for a shared place, with a grown-up.
  **Say.** "Does this step pass the check?" Expect: yes, a grown-up is with us.
- **15 min. First step.** Your child and a grown-up do it, then write one dated line.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X74]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one step card. Longer: your child sorts two more ideas onto "we can" and "adults do".

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words with pictures: step, adult, safe, log. Frame: "A grown-up is with us, so ___." A child may pass.
