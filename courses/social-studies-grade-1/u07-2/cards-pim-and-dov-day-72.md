# Pim and Dov, and the retrieval card, day 72

Say aloud: "Pim and Dov are made up." Print "invented for practice" in small letters on each card. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, one figure on each card.

## Retrieval card (the first minutes; never scored)

Two made-up friends, **Ivy** and **Joe**. Read aloud: "Ivy wants the quiet corner. Joe wants to talk about a book." Partners say each friend's reason, 20 seconds each. A child who names only one side joins the two-sides sort in "Return and repair".

## Teaching cards: the class rug

1. **Pim** wants the new class rug by the window, to read by.
2. **Dov** wants the new class rug by the shelf, to reach books.

Fill two columns on the sheet: **a view**, **its reason**, **what we saw**. Then teach two ways to help both: **turns** (one then the other) and **two spaces**. Say: a view that differs is not a bad view.

## Two-sides sort (for a child who named one side)

Read four statements aloud and place each beside Pim or Dov: "I want light to read." "I want books close." "The window is bright." "The shelf is near the books."
