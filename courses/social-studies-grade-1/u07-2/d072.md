# Day 72: Why do views differ?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Ask two adults who see our problem differently for a minute each, or a dated note. Fill a role card for each. If only one view exists, say so aloud and read a second card marked "possible view, invented for practice". Write a fresh dated note for the repair; keep exit card 72 closed.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

Post: "Why do views differ?" Review from kindergarten: a view is what one person thinks and why. Say Ivy and Joe are made up: Ivy wants the quiet corner; Joe wants to talk about a book. **Ask.** "What does each friend want, and why?" Partners, 20 seconds each; hear the fourth six.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many made errors J or C on day 71's exit, Pip says: "The note says adults are sweeping the path, so the path is clean." "Nobody helped until we noticed." Say Pip is making a mistake on purpose. Read a fresh note on the two-sided card; add one more adult to the helper web. A child who named one side joins the two-sides sort.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Pim and Dov are made up. Pim wants the new class rug by the window, to read by; Dov wants it by the shelf, to reach books. Review from unit 6: a perspective makes sense to the people who live it. Fill two columns: a view, its reason, what we saw (the window is bright; the shelf is near the books). **Ask.** "Does each one have a reason?" Expect: yes, both. Say: "A view that differs is not a bad view." Two ways help both: turns (window, then shelf) and two spaces (two small rugs).

#### 6 min. Two real views of our problem

Each adult speaks about a minute, or you read the note. Say who each is and when. Neither is ranked; no one says which is theirs.

#### 3 min. Fill the columns

Fill the two-views sheet: role and date, what they want, their reason, and what we saw from day 70's baseline. Read each column back together.

#### 3 min. Both helped?

Pairs find one way that helps both views, 40 seconds at once. Hear the fourth six pairs; keep two on the board.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Everyone marks exit card 72 (1 minute). [[s07-X72]] [[s07-X72b]] Hear the fourth six, 30 seconds each, while the others draw one reason.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: fold paper into two columns.

**Skip.** The adults' words and the heard six.

- **10 min. Two views.** Show two made-up cards: one wants the rug by the window, one by the shelf.
  **Say.** "What does each want, and why?" Expect: light to read; books close.
- **15 min. Both.** Your child draws two ways that help both: turns, two spaces.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X72]] [[s07-X72b]] Show the card and read it. Your child circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one adult's view and the invented one.

### Words and supports

Words with pictures: view, reason, turns, two spaces. A child may pass.
