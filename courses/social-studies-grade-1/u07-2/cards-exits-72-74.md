# Exit cards, days 72 to 74

Each exit has a paper card every child marks and a spoken question heard from the rotating six. Say aloud first: "These children and classes are made up. These cards are practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines. Keep these closed until the day. No card reuses a teaching card, a repair card or the day 79 packet.

## Day 72 exit card: Hana and Bo

**Picture:** the big floor mat, with Hana at one end holding a dance scarf and Bo at the other with a tower of blocks. Read aloud: "Hana wants the big mat to practice a dance step. Bo wants the big mat to build a block city." Under it two picture choices: turns or two spaces; the dancer's way only.

**Mark:** Circle a way that helps both. **Spoken:** "Tell me both reasons."

## Day 73 exit card: shelf labels or a return box

**Picture:** a group at a table with one box with a slot. Read aloud: "A group is choosing between two ways to help: shelf labels or a return box." Under it two picture choices: everyone puts one folded slip in the box; only one side puts in slips.

**Mark:** Circle the fair secret ballot. **Spoken:** "How can the ballot be fair?"

## Day 74 exit card: help younger pupils find a room

**Picture:** a class and a teacher making signs at a table; beside it a child alone at a door that leads outside. Read aloud: "A class chose to help younger pupils find a room." Under it two picture choices: making signs with the teacher; leaving school alone to put signs outside.

**Mark:** Circle a safe first step. **Spoken:** "Why is that step safe?"

If the class's action is signs, use the reserved alternate for day 74: an invented class helps a staff helper sort the lost-and-found box, or carries the box alone down a street.
