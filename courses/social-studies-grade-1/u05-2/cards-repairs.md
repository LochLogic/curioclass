# Repair cards, section 5.2

Each card puts a wrong idea beside a right one so the class can see the difference. The wrong idea is spoken by Pip the puppet or shown on a teacher card, never by a named child. Say aloud: "Pip is making a mistake on purpose." Print "invented for practice" on every invented card. Used only when the exit showed the error; at most five minutes, a small group when few missed it. The state used for retries is a new one, never Oregon, Texas or the class's own.

## G: the biggest city is the capital

**Pip:** holds a card with two city dots, one large and one small, and says, "The big one is the capital."

**Right:** the capital is where the government meets; the caption says which city.

**Repair:** two city dots on a new state card, one bigger, with a caption saying where the government meets; place the star by the caption. Then retry on another new state.

## L: the capital is a building

**Pip:** points to a building in a city and says, "This is the capital."

**Right:** the capital is a city; the building is in it.

**Repair:** a picture of a city with one building circled; say "a city" and "a building in the city"; retry with a new card.

## F: a map alone proves why a place grew

**Pip:** points to a river on a map and says, "The map shows a river, so shops grew."

**Right:** a map clue and a record detail together. The map suggests; the record says.

**Repair:** pair a map clue card with a record card; children join them with "The map shows ___. The record says ___."; retry on a new map.

## H: no one was here before

**Pip:** says, "Nobody was here before the ships came."

**Right:** Lenape people lived here, with towns and trails. Our sources name the place, Manahatta.

**Repair:** a small group hears the second read-aloud again beside the Lenape layer; each pair points to the name Manahatta.

## K: an opinion is a reason

**Pip:** says, "Shops grew there because people like shops."

**Right:** a reason names what a source shows.

**Repair:** replace the opinion with a clue; point to the clue first, then say the reason.

## M: the Lenape are gone

**Pip:** says, "The Lenape are all gone."

**Right:** Lenape nations live today and tell their own histories.

**Repair:** a small group looks at the three name cards, the Delaware Nation, the Delaware Tribe of Indians and the Stockbridge-Munsee Community, and says each name. No role play and no guessing about any family.

## Q: a state and a country are the same

**Pip:** says, "My state and my country are the same place."

**Right:** the state is a part inside the country.

**Repair:** nested outline cards; place the state card on the country card, then the town on the state.

## D: a continent is a country

**Pip:** points to a continent and says, "That is a country."

**Right:** a continent is a very large land area that holds many countries.

**Repair:** paired views: find a continent, then a country inside it; say "inside".

## E: river, lake and ocean are the same

**Pip:** points to a lake and says, "That is a river," and to an ocean and says, "That is a lake."

**Right:** a river flows; a lake is water with land all around it; an ocean is a huge connected body of salt water; a mountain is very high land.

**Repair:** match picture cards for river, lake, ocean and mountain to defining pictures; retry on a new map.

## J: the map says the water is safe

**Pip:** points to a lake and says, "The map shows the water is safe to drink."

**Right:** a map shows where water is; people clean water before they drink it.

**Repair:** two small cards, "where water is" and "whether to drink it"; the child says which one the map shows.
