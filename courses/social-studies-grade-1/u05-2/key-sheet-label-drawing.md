# Teacher key, the label a drawing sheet

Teacher only. Keep apart from sheet-label-drawing.md; never hand to children.

## Key

**Teacher key.** Judge three look-fors, not a score: the label matches the drawn feature (a river is drawn with flowing arrows, a lake as a closed shape with land around it, a mountain as high land); the symbol matches the key on the Heron Valley map; a need named is one water meets (drinking, food, travel). A child who draws a river as a closed oval has not yet shown the river; draw arrows with them and try again.
