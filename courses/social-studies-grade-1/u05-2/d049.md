# Day 49: Why a harbor city grew

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the Lenape layer apart until part two. Say: "Cattail Crossing is made up." Teacher keys: key-map-harbor.md, key-cards-cattail-crossing.md, key-map-harbor-lenape-layer.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Spoken. If day 48's exit showed error G (the bigger city), 2 minutes: a new state card, two dots, one bigger; the star goes by the caption. Error L: a small group in supported work. Review: in unit 4 you asked who made a source, when, and how it helps; ask it of the Harbor Map.

#### 14 min. Teaching first

Name each source before reading.

Part one, 5 minutes. A harbor is a sheltered place by the water where ships can stop. Read the first oral summary, a museum's history page. Frame: "The map shows ___. The account says ___. This helped because ___." Say the first blank: ships and docks. **Ask.** "What does the account say?" Expect: ships brought goods; travelers came by ship. **Ask.** "How did it help?" Expect: why a city grew there.

Part two, 5 minutes. Lay the layer on the map and read the second summary, from the nations' own pages; point to the approximate trail and the name Manahatta. **Ask.** "Does the Harbor Map tell who lived here before the ships?" Expect: no; the nations' accounts do. If a child asks "Are the Lenape gone?", read it once for everyone and answer: "No. Lenape nations live today." Point to the three name cards. No acting out, no family questions.

Part three, 4 minutes. Houses grew near the landings of Cattail Crossing, where people cross a river. A capital draws a government's workers (day 48).

#### 9 min. Supported work

Pairs put a token on the ship card (the map) and the ship-dock-market card (the account), draw a reason on the blank card, then point to something the map does not say. You hear the second six, a minute each. **Ask.** "What does the Lenape account tell about this place before the ships?" Expect: Lenape people lived here; their nations live today. The error L group meets you first, three minutes.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 49. [[s05-X49]] Read aloud: "Customers ride the ferry to the shops." The second six come to you, 20 seconds each: "Why did shops grow here?" Look for: the landing; the record says so; it does not say who was here before (error F, H, K).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No Harbor Map: draw a harbor with ships and docks, and a dashed trail beside it.

**Skip.** The pairs, the heard six and Cattail Crossing.

- **10 min. A harbor.** Read the first oral summary. Your child draws a ship and a dock.
  **Say.** "The map shows ships. What does the account say?" Expect: ships brought goods; travelers came by ship.
- **10 min. Who was here first?** Read the second oral summary. Point to the dashed trail.
  **Say.** "Does the map say who lived here before?" Expect: no; the Lenape nations' accounts do, and they live today.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X49]] Show the card and read it.
  **Say.** "Why did shops grow here?" Expect: the ferry brings customers; the record does not say who was here before.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Cattail Crossing. Longer: another reason.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: harbor, account, Lenape. A child may pass or point.
