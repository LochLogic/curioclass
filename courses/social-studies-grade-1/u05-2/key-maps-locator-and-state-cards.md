# Teacher key, maps locator and state cards

Teacher only. Keep apart from maps-locator-and-state-cards.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### United States locator

**Teacher key.** Washington, D.C. is the capital of the United States; the national government is centered in this city. The star stands for the city, not for one building. Ask: "What government meets in Washington, D.C.?" (the national government, for the whole country). The locator for the day 54 packet is a different picture and holds other places.

### Our state outline card

**Teacher key.** The star is the capital. Pairs put a token on the capital and say "___ is the capital because the state's government meets there." One direction word from the indicator: "The capital is ___ of the other city," with the pair chosen on the real card at production.

### Two example state cards

**Teacher key.** Use the two example states when the class's own state is not Colorado or Washington; if it is one of them, take the other and a third state chosen at production, never Oregon or Texas. The caption on each card says where the government meets, not which city is bigger. A capital may be the biggest city or not; the caption carries the answer.
