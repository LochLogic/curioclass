# Teacher key, cards cattail crossing

Teacher only. Keep apart from cards-cattail-crossing.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Cattail Crossing card (invented)

**Teacher key.** A river crossing is a place where people get across a river. A landing on each bank makes it a place people come to, so houses grew close to it. This is an invented case shown in two minutes after the Harbor Map; it is not about any real town. It does not say who lived here before.

### Claim cards for pairs

**Teacher key.** A good reason names what a source shows: ships stop at the harbor and bring goods, and travelers come by ship, so a city grew there. "Because people like shops" names nothing from a source. The map suggests the reason and the account supports it.
