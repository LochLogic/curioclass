# Teacher key, map harbor lenape layer

Teacher only. Keep apart from map-harbor-lenape-layer.md; never hand to children.

## Key

Lay the layer over the Harbor Map and say what each mark is. The name is approximate and comes from the nations' own pages, not from this map. The Harbor Map shows ships and docks; the layer shows that Lenape people were here first. Together they answer "who lived here before the ships?" with a source, not a guess. Ask each pair to point to one mark on the layer and say "The layer shows ___."

Do not remove the word "approximate". Do not draw the layer from memory of any other map. The layer is read beside the second read-aloud, never alone.
