# Teacher key, maps globes and nested cards

Teacher only. Keep apart from maps-globes-and-nested-cards.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Nested outline cards: town in state in country

**Teacher key.** The town is inside the state and the state is inside the country. Say "inside." Children place the state card on the country card and the town dot on the state card. Kindergarten's address rings (town, state, country) are the same idea in the same words. A state and a country are not the same place; the country is bigger and holds many states.

### The world view (continents and oceans named once)

**Teacher key.** Say each name once, with a finger on it. A continent is a very large land area. An ocean is a huge connected body of salt water. The count taught in the United States is seven continents and five oceans; some places count differently, and the oceans are really one connected ocean with named parts. Children do not need to remember the names or place them from memory; grade 2 owns that. Point only to the class's own continent and the nearest ocean. A continent holds many countries; our country is on a continent.

### Paired globe views (day 50)

**Teacher key.** On the views the class finds land and water again. The views show names; a child need not read them. Do not ask a child to find a continent or ocean that was not pointed to as our own.
