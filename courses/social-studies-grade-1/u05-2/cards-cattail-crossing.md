# Day 49 cards: Cattail Crossing and the claim cards

Say aloud first: "Cattail Crossing is made up." Print "invented for practice" on the card. Thick lines, large print.

## Cattail Crossing card (invented)

**Picture:** a wide river from above, drawn as a blue band across the page. A rope ferry, a flat boat on a rope, in the middle of the river. A landing on each bank, drawn as a short line sticking out. A footpath leading from each landing to a small group of six squares (houses) on the near bank and three squares on the far bank. A key: a flat boat is a ferry, a short line is a landing, a square is a house. A direction indicator labelled N pointing up.

## Claim cards for pairs

Three small picture cards for each pair, to join with "The map shows ___. The account says ___. This helped because ___."

- A map clue card: a ship in the harbor (from the Harbor Map).
- An account card: a ship, a dock and a market square together (from the first read-aloud).
- A reason card: a blank card to draw on.

Pairs lay a token on the Harbor Map clue and on the account picture, and draw the reason. Then each pair points to one thing the Harbor Map does not say, such as who lived here before the ships.
