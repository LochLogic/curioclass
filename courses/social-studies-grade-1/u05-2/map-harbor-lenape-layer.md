# The Harbor Map: the Lenape layer

A see-through layer (clear sheet or tracing paper) laid over the Harbor Map from section 5.1. It is used on day 49 and again on day 53. Print "approximate" in large letters on it. Thick lines, no color needed beyond the dashed line.

## What the layer shows

- The word "Manahatta" written once across the island in plain letters, with a small label under it: "a Lenape name for this place".
- A small box in the corner: "Lenape nations live today. The Delaware Nation, the Delaware Tribe of Indians and the Stockbridge-Munsee Community."
- No village sites are drawn. No houses, no people, no faces, no costume, no arrows of removal on this layer.
