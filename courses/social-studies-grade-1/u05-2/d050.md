# Day 50: Where in the world is our town?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark our continent and nearest ocean on the world view. Set out the address cards, Milltown. Say: "This is the real Earth." Keep exit card 50 closed. Teacher key: key-maps-globes-and-nested-cards.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Spoken. If day 49's exit showed error F, 3 minutes: join a map clue card to a record card, "The map shows ___. The record says ___." Errors H, K and M: a small group in supported work. Review: in kindergarten an address nests. Lay the four address cards, Milltown, in a row. **Ask.** "Say the rings of an address." Expect: street, town, state, country.

#### 11 min. Teaching first

Stack the nested place cards: the town inside the state, the state inside the country (error Q: they are not the same place). **Ask.** "Is our state inside our country, or our country inside our state?" Expect: the state is inside the country. Now the world view. Say each continent and ocean once, a finger on its name; no child repeats them. A continent is a very large land area. An ocean is a huge connected body of salt water. The count taught in the United States is seven and five. Pip points at our continent: "That is a country." Right: a continent holds many countries (error D). Point only to our continent and the nearest ocean.

#### 9 min. Supported work

Pairs put a token on our continent and the nearest ocean on the paired globe views, then lay the nested place cards in order. The error H, K and M group meets you first, three minutes. You hear the third six, a minute each. **Ask.** "Which continent is our town on?" Expect: ours, a very large land area.

#### 3 min. Share and question board

One child points to our state on the country card, one to our continent. Add a board card: "Where in the world is our town?"

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 50. [[s05-X50]] Read aloud: "Circle our state on the country outline." Then our continent on the world view. The third six come to you, 20 seconds each; ask the one the record sheet names, South America or the blue part named Indian. Look for: a continent is a very large land area, an ocean a huge body of salt water (error D, error E).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: draw three rings and a flat Earth with land and water.

**Skip.** The pairs, the heard six and the repair group.

- **5 min. The rings.** Say the rings of an address, town, state, country, and draw them inside each other.
- **15 min. Land and water.** Look at a world picture. Find our continent and the nearest ocean.
  **Say.** "Which continent is our town on?" Expect: ours, a very large land area.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X50]] Show the card. Your child circles.
  **Say.** "Is a continent a country?" Expect: no, a continent is a very large land area that holds many countries.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the rings only. Longer: other countries.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: continent, ocean, inside. A child may pass or point.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 acts on this exit: error Q, the state card on the country card; error D, a continent holds many countries; error E, picture cards for river, lake and ocean.
