# Teacher key, map heron valley

Teacher only. Keep apart from map-heron-valley.md; never hand to children.

## Key

### Heron Valley map (invented)

**Teacher key.** A river is water that flows from higher land toward lower water; here the arrows show it flowing down from the mountain toward the sea. A lake is water with land all around it; this one is separate from the river. A mountain is very high land. People settle near water because they need it, to drink after it is cleaned, for fish and to travel. A bridge gives one benefit, an easier crossing; a poorly planned road or crossing can also block fish. A map shows where water is. It does not say the water is safe to drink.
