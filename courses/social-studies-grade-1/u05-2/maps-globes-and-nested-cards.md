# Day 50 pieces: nested outlines, world views and the globe views

The world pictures show real places. Say aloud: "This is the real Earth." The outlines are redrawn by us from free public map outlines. Thick lines, large print, the key visible.

## Nested outline cards: town in state in country

**Picture:** three cards that stack. The largest is an outline of the United States, labeled United States. The middle card is an outline of the class's own state, labeled with its name, sized to sit on the country card where the state is. The smallest is a small shape for the class's town, a dot with the town's name, sized to sit on the state card. A key: a dot is a town. Make the three from real outlines before use.

## The world view (continents and oceans named once)

**Picture:** a flat world map. Seven continents in tan, each with its name printed: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia. Five oceans in blue, each with its name printed: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic. A key box: tan is land, blue is water. A direction indicator.

## Paired globe views (day 50)

The same printed views from day 46, now with names. Add the continent and ocean names beside the land and water. Pairs put a token on our continent and on the nearest ocean, and nested cards in order.
