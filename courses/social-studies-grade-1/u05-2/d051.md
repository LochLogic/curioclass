# Day 51: Rivers, lakes and mountains

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fold the relief: a mountain, a ribbon river, a low oval lake, a bridge strip. Cut label strips. Say: "Heron Valley is made up." Keep exit card 51 closed. Teacher keys: key-sheet-label-drawing.md, key-map-heron-valley.md, key-maps-globes-and-nested-cards.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Spoken. Repair what day 50's exit showed, if anything: error Q, the state card on the country card, 3 minutes; error D, our country is on a continent, 1 minute; error E, picture cards for river, lake and ocean, 1 minute. Review: in kindergarten you learned what people need to stay well. **Ask.** "What do people need to stay well?" Expect: food, water, shelter, clothing.

#### 11 min. Teaching first

Show Heron Valley beside the relief. A river is water that flows from higher land toward lower water; the arrows run away from the mountain. A lake is water with land all around it. A mountain is very high land. **Ask.** "Is the lake east or west of the river?" Expect: east. People settle near water because they need it. **Ask.** "How can a lake help people?" Expect: drinking water after it is cleaned, fish, travel. A bridge makes crossing easier, but a poorly planned road or crossing can block fish. Pip points at the lake: "The map shows the water is safe to drink." Right: a map shows where water is; people clean water before they drink it (error J).

#### 9 min. Supported work

Pairs put a token on the river, the lake and the mountain, then say "Water meets the need for ___." Each child draws one and copies its word from the word label strip; an adult may scribe. You hear the fourth six, a minute each, while others draw. **Ask.** "How could the river help people?" Expect: drinking water after it is cleaned, fish, travel. Judge, not scored: the word matches the drawing, the symbol matches the key, the need is one water meets.

#### 3 min. Share

Hold up two or three matching drawings; the class points to the feature on the relief.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

All mark exit card 51. [[s05-X51]] [[s05-X51b]] Read aloud: "Circle the lake." The fourth six come to you, 20 seconds each; ask the one the record sheet names. Look for: a need water meets, or the river flowing toward the lake, not safe water (error E, error J).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No relief: fold paper into a mountain and lay a blue crayon line down it.

**Skip.** The pairs, the heard six and the share.

- **5 min. Water.** Ask what people need to stay well.
- **15 min. River, lake, mountain.** Look at the Heron Valley map. Touch each one.
  **Say.** "How can a lake help people?" Expect: water after it is cleaned, fish, travel. Your child draws one and copies its word.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X51]] [[s05-X51b]] Show the card. Your child circles.
  **Say.** "Does the map say the water is safe to drink?" Expect: no, it shows where water is.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the bridge. Longer: another need.

### Words and supports

Read the student page aloud. Words: river, lake, mountain, flow. Frame: "Water meets the need for ___." A child may pass or point.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 acts on this exit: error J, two cards, where water is and whether to drink it; error E, picture cards. The labelled drawing comes back.
