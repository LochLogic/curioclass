# Label a drawing: river, lake or mountain

Name ______________________

Print "invented for practice" at the bottom.

**Picture:** a blank box for a drawing. Beside it, a strip with three word labels in large print, one word each: river, lake, mountain. A small pocket or glue spot beside the box for the copied label.

**Task:** Draw one of the three on a map of Heron Valley (children may copy from the map or the relief), then copy its word label beside the drawing. An adult may scribe a sentence such as "A ___ is ___."
