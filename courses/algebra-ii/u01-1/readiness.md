# Unit 1 start-of-year check

## Student

Work alone with a pencil. These three questions look back at Algebra I. They are practice: they are not a grade, and
they tell your teacher what to review first. Show your thinking in the space under each question.

## Teacher

Day 1, the last ten minutes, after the hook: one minute to hand out and collect, then item 1 (about 2 minutes),
item 2 (about 4) and item 3 (about 3). Students work unaided and silent. Do not score it as unit mastery. Mark each
item right, partly right or not yet on the record sheet, and write the error letter beside a miss: error G on item 1,
error B on items 2 and 3, error C on item 3. Day 2 opens with the repair: the whole class for any item more than a
third of the class missed, otherwise small groups while the rest do the warm-up, and a fresh independent retry for
every missed item.
