# Day 4: rule cards

Each card gives a start and a rule that repeats. Build a table, plot the points on graph paper, and give the last term
asked for. Cards 1 and 2 come with a table frame; cards 3 and 4 give only the start and the rule; cards 5 and 6 give
the rule in words.
