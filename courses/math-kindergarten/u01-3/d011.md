# Day 11: Get one for every place

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the plate exit card and all eight paper napkins covered. Cut the separate supply master into large pieces. Teacher-only answer key: d011-key.html. Bring day 10 hat-and-cup cards and practice with feedback. Prepare five places and exactly five squares per pair, with clear short routes. Select six rotating exit listeners on the class list. Use large intact paper pieces.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 10 complete pairs with [[s01-W11]]. Partners take one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Review [[s01-N11]] slowly together, supporting one through ten without speed pressure.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 10's exit and written task s01-P10 with feedback. If day 10's tickets showed error E, check fresh square-place pairs one by one before judging enough. Error B: cover each occupied place before supplying another. Choose one five-minute repair; whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise one small group while others revisit familiar pairs. No hats or cup-picture practice.

#### 8 min. Recall and demonstrate

Review day 10 single-piece trips with [[s01-K11]].

[[steps:s01-K11]]

Review the puppet repeat with [[s01-F11]], right before wrong. If appearance judgments persist, use [[s01-G11]] instead; both are invented for practice. Choose one demonstration so it fits. This applies known matching and moving actions, with no new math method.

#### 14 min. One item per trip

Fifteen pairs work at once. Carry one square per trip to five separate floor places, then reverse roles. Use exactly five squares, never whole-set fetching from an excess pool. Keep routes clear; a tabletop mat and a short reach to the tray replace walking. Two rounds per child make twenty single-piece trips per pair, about fifteen seconds each, five minutes; allow four more for exchange and checking and five for [[s01-P11]].

**Ask.** [[s01-Q11]] Expect checking every distinct pair; partners reply during the checking minutes. If many revisit occupied places, point to each supplied place before choosing the next empty one. Collect each paper record for feedback.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers with cubes, flat shapes, adjacent linked block pages or the same five-square trips. Keep plates, napkins and excess-supply fetching away from practice. Hear six practice children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; others use familiar partner actions. Record ordered words only when heard and distinguish an empty place from an observed repeat.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

At most two volunteers show a checked pair, 45 seconds each; children may pass. Give brief feedback. Table groups return pieces together; leave pencils ready and routes clear.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each new plate card and eight napkins only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X11]] exactly. Children select and place napkins independently, then trace each selected piece beside its plate and dot that trace to preserve the paper response. Any four napkins may be used. Do not add a counting ask or model the selection. Observe six rotating children for at most 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Directions, simultaneous work, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help and matching actions actually observed. Return all papers on day 12, Return and repair. Finish feedback after class: thirty children's two-paper sets at twenty seconds per set, ten minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and entire exit picture from the master, preserving spacing and choices. Keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; plain paper replaces mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace tools; hand-drawn places replace mats. Use the covered plate card and cut-paper napkin supply. A tabletop reach replaces walking.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.

- **3 min. Review.** Show a complete square-place pair.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check each place once, covering occupied places if helpful.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Repeat yesterday's single-piece trips to five places.
- **13 min. Practice.** Your child completes [[s01-P11]].
  **Say.** "How can you check that every place has one?" Expect checking each separate pair. Rehearse one through ten together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X11]] on the uncovered card and supply. Your child selects, places and records alone. Keep the response for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice supplies separate places one item per trip from exactly five pieces; the exit selects just enough napkins from an excess supply for new plates.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep demonstration, practice and exit. Longer: change the starting place or trip route while checking the same five distinct pairs.

### Words and supports

Review: place and enough, Object-place pairs and One item per trip from day 10; match from day 8; group and mark from day 6. Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner, from days 1 to 5. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Read every printed line aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile pictures and the child's communication aid. A child may direct an adult's marks; record help. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 counts lined-up collections to ten, with one word per object. Return day 11 papers in Return and repair. Error E: check fresh square-place partners before judging enough. Error B: cover each supplied place or counted object before moving on. Choose one action; keep unseen words not yet seen and carry unresolved needs to a later repair slot.
