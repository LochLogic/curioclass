# Day 10: Give every place a partner

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the hat-and-cup exit card; cut its bottom strip into three cup pieces and keep each set covered with its card. Teacher-only answer key: d010-key.html. Bring day 9 shell cards and linked practice with feedback. Prepare five-place mats and large squares. Select six rotating exit listeners on the class list. Reject small or damaged tools; use large paper.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 9 linked marks with [[s01-W10]], using the returned block pages. Review [[s01-N10]] slowly together. Fifteen pairs take 45-second turns each, simultaneously. Keep links visible.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 9's exit and written task s01-P9 with feedback. If day 9's tickets showed error A, dot each of five fresh squares once. Error B: cover each after its word. If practice showed error D, pause until the next touch. Choose one repair; more than ten affected means whole class, otherwise one small group while others revisit familiar pieces. Never rehearse shells or reverse-panel records.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Use [[s01-K10]] to introduce place and enough through actual paired pieces.

[[steps:s01-K10]]

Recall day 9 visible links before making the new object-place pairs. Then review omission with [[s01-F10]], right before wrong. Say the puppet scene is invented for practice. Keep hats, cups and enough-choice pictures covered.

#### 14 min. Build and repair matches

Pairs carry one square per trip from a nearby tray to build five complete square-place pairs, remove one square, locate its missing partner and restore it. Also repeat with one place.

**Ask.** [[s01-Q10]] Expect the empty place, shown by pointing. Each child has two 45-second turns, three minutes per pair, simultaneously. Complete [[s01-P10]] as a record of the restored pairs, using adjacent marks and links already taught. If many judge by size, align actual pieces one to each place before restoring the missing pair. Collect every page for feedback.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers: cubes, flat shapes, adjacent block records or restored square-place pairs. No target-card selections or place-setting supply tasks. Hear six practice children, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; others continue familiar paired work. Note words actually heard and whether each place has exactly one partner. Paper cannot prove unheard word order.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most indicate a completed physical pair, 45 seconds each; children may pass. Give a brief next step. Table groups return pieces together and keep pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the new hats and three cup pieces at each child's own turn. Read [[s01-X10]] exactly. Children move the cups, draw each matching link on the hat paper and mark the unmatched hat. This is one sufficiency response, with no added yes/no ask. Observe six rotating children at most 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Directions, simultaneous responses, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record observed repeats separately from appearance judgments. Return exit and practice on day 11, Return and repair. Finish feedback after class: thirty children's two-paper sets at twenty seconds per set, ten minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and entire exit picture from the master, preserving spacing and choices. Keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; plain paper replaces mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace tools; hand-drawn boxes replace mats. Copy and cover the exit card and cut cup pieces.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.

- **3 min. Review.** Follow yesterday's adjacent block links.
- **4 min. Repair.** Model reaching each of five squares once.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Put one square at each of five places. Remove and restore one partner.
- **13 min. Practice.** Your child completes [[s01-P10]].
  **Say.** "Which place still needs its partner?" Expect the place where the square was removed. Rehearse one through ten together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X10]] on the uncovered card. Your child moves cups and records matches alone. Keep the response for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice restores a removed partner in a completed physical layout; the exit uses new pictured hats and cups to show an unmatched location.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep demonstration, practice and exit. Longer: spread the same completed place pairs differently and check each once.

### Words and supports

New words: place, enough. Review: match from day 8; group and mark from day 6. Enough supplies one per place; extra supply pieces do not make it insufficient. Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner, from days 1 to 5. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Read every printed line aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile pictures and the child's communication aid. A child may direct an adult's marks; record help. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 11 applies place matching by carrying one item per trip. Return day 10 papers. Error E: match fresh squares to drawn places before judging enough. Error B: cover each place after supplying it. Choose one five-minute action; carry other needs forward without taking the next demonstration or exit minutes.
