# Day 119: I can show what my number record tells.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and unit 6 record and feedback private. Sort days 115 to 118 annotated products/conferences by child/component. Prepare familiar choices and optional frames/cards. Read everything aloud at 18 point. No new assessment sheets or exit. Say "Invented for practice" for games.

### The day

#### 10 min. Feedback revision

Return day 118's exit, conference annotations and written tasks s06-P118, s06-K118 and s06-P118-2 with written feedback, including all practice/count/game/retry paper. Children independently revise retained work, including error F replacements, from feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2; no new demonstration. Record help/unresolved components. Keep earlier revisions attached; no repeated secure conference.

#### 10 min. Close retained records

Review Close retained observation records, day 18, and Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. Children use familiar choices. Teacher privately reviews [[s06-A119-F1]] [[s06-A119-F2]] [[s06-A119-F3]] [[s06-A119-F4]] after revision: F1 three/F2 three/F3 three/F4 one teacher minutes, ten total. Check numeral/quantity, tracking/count-out, teen composition/decomposition/naming plus the retained day 114 s06-Q114 equation after day 115 revision, and supplied start respectively. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing evidence stays pending in scheduling. Preserve initial/help, feedback, revision and judgment dates. Finish records in thirty teacher minutes after class.

#### 20 min. Familiar counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Revisit corrected partial records or trace completed routes. Optional large counters/paired frames/numeral cards support familiar paper choices. Five untimed game minutes: [[s06-G119-1]] [[s06-G119-2]] [[s06-G119-3]] [[s06-G119-4]]. Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately; Rin alone acts. Partners alternate handling/checking, mark four boxes and get immediate object feedback. Collect for day 120.

#### 15 min. Paper choices and reflection

Continue familiar choices. **Ask.** [[s06-P119]] At most two voluntary 45-second reflections; passing welcome. Peers' words never establish another child's evidence. New paper gets written feedback, day 120 revision and day 121 judgment, never scoring today.

#### 5 min. Synthesis and tidy

Retain records. Prioritize one or two unresolved components for ten-minute Centers response slots days 120/121. Further needs pending. No new battery or reteaching.

### Other rooms

One optional shared screen enlarges teaching only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printed frames, handwritten cards replace printed cards, pencil replaces crayons. Supervise large adult-cut pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printing, handwritten cards replace numeral cards. Use pencil and retained annotated papers.

**Skip.** Rotations, public reflection and secure conferences.

- **10 min. Familiar choices.** Replay the square game; alternate handling/checking.
- **5 min. Record.** Look at earlier evidence; no new test. Keep spoken findings separate from marks.
- **10 min. Revise.** Return day 118 written feedback. Child corrects retained page independently before you close earlier records.
  **Say.** "Point to a change you made." Expect a corrected missing mark or label, indicated accessibly. Passing is welcome.
- **5 min. Tidy.** Keep work together; note first unresolved component for next response sitting.

No exit. New paper gets feedback now, revision next sitting, judgment the sitting after that. Missing evidence stays pending.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim optional choices. Longer: repeat familiar games; preserve revision before closeout.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; teen, ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Large tactile pictures, signs, home-language reasoning, broad pencils and directed adult marks support access. Scribing quantity never establishes independent numeral formation. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 120 composes flat shapes and counts pieces. Its ten-minute Centers slot receives today's games/reflection and retained unit 6 records, including day 118's page: prioritize one or two five-minute repairs/revisions. Day 121's ten-minute Centers slot checks a fresh selected-component transfer; judge earlier revised evidence afterward. New transfers get feedback/later revision before judgment. Further needs pending; never repeat secure components.
