# Day 117: I can keep track of every picture.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-117 and sealed counting-117, conference-117 and hooks-only exit-117. Copy only its first panel onto half slips; windows print on student Side 2 Exit box. Keep Side 2 covered until each child's exit turn. Conference roster 17-24; exit sample 19-24. Bring day 116 annotated exit, practice/retries, numeral/teen sheets and conference feedback. Read all print aloud at 18 point; say "Invented for practice." Frames and references remain optional; record help. Draw supplied partial repairs from item steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K117]], then [[steps:s06-K117]]. Echo six/seven/eight, eight/nine/ten, then tens together. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: children mark beats. No individual round; marks do not prove unheard words. Write no numbers above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s06-X110, s06-X114 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 116's exit tickets and annotated written tasks s06-P116 and s06-K116, with all practice/count/game/retry paper. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten, show error F, **Ask.** [[s06-F117-F]], then [[steps:s06-F117-F]] and fresh partial retry [[s06-F117-F-r]]. Error H gets [[s06-F117-H]] and fresh echo retry [[s06-F117-H-r]]. Five minutes per action/retry, maximum two; otherwise targeted repair/familiar review. Further needs pending. Keep picture structure and oral starts separate. Collect repairs for day 118 feedback.

#### 9 min. Connected work

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. First five minutes: revise day 116's F1/F3 products and conference annotations from written feedback before judgment. No repeated secure turn. Four minutes: Review Start and completed-visit marks for fixed pictures, day 25. **Ask.** [[s06-P117]], then [[steps:s06-P117]]. Use the supplied twelve-square route; restore its missing visit. Check one arrangement at a time, never a complete independent mixed-layout record. Read counting-117's shared stem once and point to each panel as children receive it. Do not answer.

#### 28 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Counting-117 takes eight independent paper minutes, staggered around each conference. Children mark visits and record quantities; dictation records quantity separately from formation. No peer keys, coaching or concurrent teacher repair. After paper use the familiar partial route; unfinished panels stay pending.

Review Four-ask counting and teen conference, day 114. Teacher hears roster 17-24 privately: [[s06-C117-F2-count]], [[s06-C117-F2-out]], [[s06-C117-F3-name]], [[s06-C117-F4-start]]. Reveal one reserved panel at its own turn; read each exact stem separately. Four 45-second asks take three minutes per child, 24 teacher minutes plus 4 for transitions/access. Children mark or direct paper traces. Record names, initial performance, help and access separately. This supplies fresh domain evidence for unheard day 106 starts, day 110 names and day 114 parts, never retroactive proof. Still-unheard components remain pending.

Five untimed game minutes: [[s06-G117-1]] [[s06-G117-2]] [[s06-G117-3]] [[s06-G117-4]]. Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read asks before rotations; Rin alone acts. Children use squares and mark four boxes. Partners alternate handler/checker with immediate object feedback, never help on reserved products. Collect all practice/count/game/repair paper for day 118 feedback. Each assigned child has 8 paper + 3 conference + 5 game = 16 minutes, leaving twelve for familiar choices/tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the hooks half slip and Side 2 Exit box only at each child's own turn; sampled papers stay covered until heard. Read [[s06-X117a]] and [[s06-X117b]] exactly, one at a time. Every child marks paper or directs adult marks. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Hear roster 19-24, first three on hooks, last three on windows; note names/help. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; pending is scheduling. Paper cannot prove unheard words. Every child returns both panels. A wrong total alone does not prove omission. After class: 30 teacher minutes for component feedback and records, including thirty exits. Return before later-day revision and judgment.

### Other rooms

One optional shared screen enlarges teaching only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printed frames, handwritten cards replace printed cards, pencil replaces crayons. Supervise large adult-cut pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use paper squares, drawn frames, handwritten cards and pencil; copy the partial route, hooks half slip and covered windows Exit box.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Rehearse.** [[s06-P117]] Point to supplied visits.
  **Say.** "Finish the missing visit on this supplied array route." Expect one new tick, twelve squares visited once.
- **8 min. Paper.** Use sealed counting-117 independently in a separate sitting if needed.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's feedback; touch ten separate ones or echo the start.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X117a]] [[s06-X117b]] Read separately. Child marks hooks on the half slip and windows in the Exit box; expect nine and twenty.

Give the assigned four-ask conference once in a separate three-minute sitting. Preserve feedback and next-sitting revision before judgment.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, keeping independent paper, repair and exit. Longer: check another supplied route through the same twelve-square array; count order changes, membership does not.

### Words and supports

Review Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible, day 108; optional by choice. Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; teen, ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Large tactile pictures, signs, home-language reasoning, broad pencils and directed adult marks support access. Scribing quantity never establishes independent numeral formation. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 118 returns day 117's hooks exit ticket, windows Exit box and all practice/retry paper in Return and repair. Observed omissions are error A: five-minute missing-visit correction with fresh partial retry. Use the second five minutes for one remaining need; further needs pending. First five Connected work minutes revise F2 and conference feedback. Roster 25-30 finishes conferences. Children complete partial twelve/fourteen book pages before the fresh blank final page.
