# Day 116: I can use ten and more and start from a number.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Seal numeral-116, teen-split-116, conference-116 and exit-116 until each child's turn. Conference roster 9-16; exit sample 13-18. Bring day 115 annotated exit, practice and final products. Read all print aloud; say "Invented for practice." Withdraw supplied frames/references; retain optional access and record help. Default practice uses the unframed student picture; practice-116 is the optional framed version. Draw supplied repairs from steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K116]], then [[steps:s06-K116]]. Echo six/seven/eight, eight/nine/ten, then tens together. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: children mark beats. No individual round; marks do not prove unheard words. Write no numbers above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s06-X110, s06-X114, s06-P115, s06-K115 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 115's exit tickets and all annotated practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Review Return and repair, day 2. Honor yesterday's error E handoff: **Ask.** [[s06-F116-E]], then [[steps:s06-F116-E]]; fresh partial retry [[s06-F116-E-r]]. If F1 shows error D, use [[s06-F116-D]] and [[s06-F116-D-r]]. Each action/retry takes five minutes. More than ten sharing a letter triggers its action; otherwise target affected children while others review. Maximum two actions, further needs pending. Collect retries for day 117 feedback.

#### 9 min. Connected work

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. First five minutes: day 115 written feedback guides revision of numeral/teen products and conference annotations before later judgment. Preserve first performance and help; no repeat secure conference.

Four minutes: rehearse taught ten structure, day 108. **Ask.** [[s06-P116]], then [[steps:s06-P116]]. Partners check the supplied fourteen total; ten stays ten individual ones. Count-all remains valid. Separately echo starts from Counting; never combine starts with a fresh picture in practice. Read F1/F3 shared stems as children receive sealed papers, pointing to each panel; do not answer. References remain optional, with help recorded.

#### 28 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Each child independently completes numeral-116 for eight minutes and teen-split-116 for eight. Ten F1 panels and nine F3 panels: one shared stem per sheet. Stagger blocks around conferences to preserve sixteen uninterrupted paper minutes. No peer keys, coaching or concurrent teacher repair. Unfinished panels stay pending.

Review Four-ask counting and teen conference, day 114. Teacher hears roster 9-16 privately: [[s06-C116-F2-count]], [[s06-C116-F2-out]], [[s06-C116-F3-name]], [[s06-C116-F4-start]]. Reveal one reserved panel at its own turn; read each exact stem separately. Four 45-second asks take three minutes per child, 24 teacher minutes plus 4 for transitions/access. Children mark or direct paper traces. Record names, initial performance, help and access separately. This supplies fresh domain evidence for unheard day 106 starts, day 110 names and day 114 parts, never retroactive proof. Still-unheard components remain pending.

Five untimed game minutes: [[s06-G116-1]] [[s06-G116-2]] [[s06-G116-3]] [[s06-G116-4]]. Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read asks before rotations; Rin alone acts. Children use squares and mark four boxes. Partners alternate handler/checker with immediate object feedback, never help on reserved products. Collect all practice/count/game/repair paper for day 117 feedback. Each assigned child has 16 paper + 3 conference + 5 game = 24 minutes, leaving four for choice/tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-116 at each child's own turn; sampled slips stay covered until heard. Read [[s06-X116]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs adult marks. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Hear roster 13-18; note names/help. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; pending is scheduling. Paper cannot prove unheard words. Record quantity and heard start separately; counting all remains valid for quantity. After class: 60 teacher minutes for component feedback and records, including thirty exits. Return before later-day revision and judgment.

### Other rooms

One optional shared screen enlarges teaching only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printed frames, handwritten cards replace printed cards, pencil replaces crayons. Supervise large adult-cut pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use paper squares, drawn frames, handwritten cards and pencil. Copy practice and sealed exit masters without keys.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repetition.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Rehearse.** [[s06-P116]] Point to the supplied fourteen picture.
  **Say.** "Check this supplied ten-and-more total." Expect fourteen: ten separate ones and four further.
- **8 min. Paper.** Partners check that supplied record; keep starts separate from pictures.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise day 115 feedback, correcting digit order or empty labels.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X116]] Read the exact stem; child marks the sealed picture. Note heard start separately.

In separate sittings preserve eight independent minutes per final sheet and the one assigned three-minute conference. Return written feedback next sitting; revise before judgment. Unfinished evidence stays pending.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer optional repetitions; retain both paper blocks, repair and exit. Longer: check the same supplied total on a differently spaced square picture, never a reserved panel.

### Words and supports

Review Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible, day 108; optional by choice. Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; teen, ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Large tactile pictures, signs, home-language reasoning, broad pencils and directed adult marks support access. Scribing quantity never establishes independent numeral formation. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 117 returns day 116's exit and all practice/retry paper in Return and repair. Error F gets visible-ten correction; error H gets echo-start correction, five minutes each with partial retries. First five Connected work minutes revise today's F1/F3 products and conference feedback. Roster 17-24 receives its fresh conference; unanswered oral components stay pending. Day 117 rehearses tracking and collects the separate fresh F2 sheet.
