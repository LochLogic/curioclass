# Day 118: I can make a teen-number picture book.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-118, student partial fourteen-page, crayons, and sealed conference-118/exit-118. Conference and exit roster 25-30. Bring day 117 annotated exits, practice/retries, F2 paper and conference feedback. Read all print aloud at 18 point; say "Invented for practice." Keep marks visible; no skin printing. Frames/cards remain optional. Draw partial visit repairs from steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K118]], then [[steps:s06-K118]]. Echo six/seven/eight, eight/nine/ten, then tens together. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: children mark beats. No individual round; marks do not prove unheard words. Write no numbers above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 117's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/retry paper. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten, visibly omitted objects, use error A: **Ask.** [[s06-F118-A]], then [[steps:s06-F118-A]] and fresh partial retry [[s06-F118-A-r]], five minutes total. For one first remaining need, error F uses [[s06-F118-F]]/[[s06-F118-F-r]]; error H uses [[s06-F118-H]]/[[s06-F118-H-r]], five minutes including retry. Maximum two actions; otherwise familiar review. Further needs pending; no letter from unexplained totals. Collect repairs for day 119 feedback.

#### 9 min. Connected work

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. First five minutes: revise day 117's F2 sheet and conference annotations from written feedback before later judgment. Four minutes: Review Make picture-book pages, day 9, and ten ones/further ones, day 108. **Ask.** [[s06-P118]], then [[steps:s06-P118]]. Complete only the missing second further mark. Then [[s06-P118-2]] completes missing fourth further mark. Match each supplied label to visible ones. No whole blank page or eighteen rehearsal. Pictures, labels and optional equations describe the same parts; review equation, plus and equals, day 78. Count-all valid.

#### 28 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Children complete/decorate supplied partial twelve/fourteen pages individually, then fold them into their book. Review part/whole, day 87. Keep individual marks visible; art detail is irrelevant. Familiar application, no new content or scored product. No peer coaching on conferences or final pages; no teacher repair during conferences.

Review Four-ask counting and teen conference, day 114. Teacher hears roster 25-30 privately: [[s06-C118-F2-count]], [[s06-C118-F2-out]], [[s06-C118-F3-name]], [[s06-C118-F4-start]]. Reveal one reserved panel at its own turn; read each exact stem separately. Four 45-second asks take three minutes per child, 18 teacher minutes plus 10 for transitions/access. Children mark or direct paper traces. Record names, initial performance, help and access separately. This supplies fresh domain evidence for unheard day 106 starts, day 110 names and day 114 parts, never retroactive proof. Still-unheard components remain pending.

Five untimed game minutes: [[s06-G118-1]] [[s06-G118-2]] [[s06-G118-3]] [[s06-G118-4]]. Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read asks before rotations; Rin alone acts. Children use squares and mark four boxes. Partners alternate handler/checker with immediate object feedback, never help on reserved products. Collect all practice/count/game/repair paper for day 119 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-118 at each child's own turn; sampled slips stay covered until heard. Read [[s06-X118]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs adult marks. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Hear roster 25-30; note names/help. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; pending is scheduling. Paper cannot prove unheard words. The final book page receives descriptive feedback, no application score. After class: 30 teacher minutes for component feedback and records, including thirty exits. Return before later-day revision and judgment.

### Other rooms

One optional shared screen enlarges teaching only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printed frames, handwritten cards replace printed cards, pencil replaces crayons. Supervise large adult-cut pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Pencil replaces crayons; use paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards. Copy partial practice pages; keep blank exit covered.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and decoration if unwanted.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Rehearse.** [[s06-P118]] Restore missing further mark on supplied twelve-page.
  **Say.** "Finish this partial twelve-page." Expect ten separate ones and two further ones.
- **8 min. Book.** [[s06-P118-2]] Complete partial fourteen-page; expect ten and four. Fold pages together.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's annotated paper; repair a missing visit if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X118]] Read exact stem. Child makes fresh final page; expect ten separate marks and eight further, with 18 or dictated label. Give descriptive feedback next sitting.

Give assigned conference once in a separate three-minute sitting. Later revision precedes judgment.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut decoration/repetition; keep mathematical marks, repair, assigned conference and exit. Longer: add patterns around partial pages, without hiding or adding counted marks.

### Words and supports

Review Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible, day 108; optional by choice. Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; teen, ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Large tactile pictures, signs, home-language reasoning, broad pencils and directed adult marks support access. Scribing quantity never establishes independent numeral formation. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 119 returns day 118's exit, conference annotations and all practice/retry paper with written feedback in ten-minute Feedback revision. An observed one-object replacement is error F; the child revises the retained page from feedback, without a new demonstration. Close earlier records only after revision. No new exit or repeated conference. Unresolved components go to ten-minute Centers response slots days 120/121; art is never scored.
