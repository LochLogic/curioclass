# Day 129: I can build solid shapes and show their parts.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Print this day's student drawing instead of practice-129's incomplete frame, plus sealed exit-129. Set out low adult-started cube/box frames, paper rods, soft joins, face panels and partial traces. Give every child loose exit paper/end pieces and ready-to-press tabs, hidden until exit; no step card. Pretest loose paper/end pieces for fit. Choose six observation names. Inspect models; no staples, residue, sharp edges, glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Paper replaces clay.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. Chorus gives no individual judgment; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 128's tickets, practice, arrow corrections and game packet with written feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many rejected square after turning, error C: five minutes [[s07-K129-turn]]. If many used color alone, error D: five minutes [[s07-K129-group]]. Include fresh partial retry and original-record revision. Error O, tube flat because its ends are flat, remains recorded for day 130's five-minute slot; do not squeeze in a third repair.

#### 10 min. Rehearsal

Apply yesterday's features to building, as promised. Review cube/solid, days 1/4; square, day 45; side/corner, day 48; equal sides and Build and compose closed quadrilaterals from supplied parts, day 51. Review Wide paper strips and folded paper joins, day 51. Adults handle joins as directed, applying that familiar connection to a low frame.

**Ask.** [[s07-K129-frame]] [[steps:s07-K129-frame]] A square panel fills one face; rods show boundaries, not surfaces. Review face/cylinder, day 127; curved, day 46. Show a finished known cylinder without constructing it: curved body, two circular ends, whole solid beyond either end. Do not demonstrate cylinder building or give its step card.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs repair partial oblong box frames. Read [[s07-P129]] using the corrected student drawing. The dashed rear-left rod is hidden but present; only front-left vertical rod is missing. Children add that rod on paper and position a physical paper rod in the same gap. Adult presses joins if needed. Match a supplied rectangular face panel to the frame and finish its adult-started trace on construction-record.

Hear two optional 45-second contributions on the started-frame task. If many call the frame flat, hold a panel at its front and feel the frame extending behind it. Judge shape decisions, not craftsmanship. No whole cylinder builds in practice.

#### 15 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Five minutes: game. Eight minutes: repeat partial cube/box rod repairs and supplied face traces; two minutes: tidy. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Keep constructions low on stable paper bases. Keep marked practice in folders and collect new records with tomorrow's feedback packet.

Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Show 2, 3, 4, 3 with pieces or game-record marks. Give immediate feedback: error I counts only arrivals; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-129 and fresh upright cylinder-like model only at each child's turn. Read [[s07-X129]] exactly. Everyone builds independently with their own loose paper/end pieces and broad tabs; a child may direct an adult's movements for access. Never hand out a premade curved body. Judge whether the result represents a curved body extending between circular ends, not neatness. A hollow model is valid.

Six observations at 45 seconds take 4.5 minutes; the others build simultaneously. Use 1.5 minutes for directions and collection preparation. Last two minutes: each child sketches/traces their build on the half slip and tidies; keep the actual build for inspection. After class count four minutes to inspect thirty builds/records at eight seconds each, then 7.5 minutes for written feedback, fifteen seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return on day 130; no inference of an unheard reason from a sketch.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Hand-draw masters without answers if no printer. Adult cuts and steadies models at child direction. Retain solids beside pictures; a drawing alone cannot show hidden depth. Large prepared tabs allow simultaneous independent joining. Adults can attach joins at child direction; record that access separately from shape hints.

### At home

**Swap.** Rolled paper, broad paper tabs and adult-cut panels replace sticks/clay. Clean models replace kits; hand-draw masters; crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise day 128 feedback.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Repair one rod in an adult-started box frame. Show a face panel.
- **4 min. Paper.** [[s07-P129]] Add its missing front-left rod. **Say.** "Finish this started frame." Expect one rod closing the indicated gap.
- **5 min. Game.** Repeat joining/removal with pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X129]] Reveal the new cylinder and exit-129 now. Child builds without a demonstration; attach joins only as directed. Child sketches/traces the result. Record help; give written feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated frame repairs, preserving feedback, game, whole-cylinder exit and record. Longer: repair another indicated rod on the known frame and trace its supplied face panel, without a cylinder rehearsal.

### Words and supports

Review whole, day 87; flat, day 45; face/cylinder, day 127. Informal "round ends" and "goes back" count as feature language. Read every word aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Offer tactile outlines, large stable models, home language, signing and directed adult marks. Record access separately from mathematical help; public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 130 describes classroom solids and positions. Return day 129's build, paper trace, practice and game records for revision in Return and repair. Error O, flat circle offered as the whole cylinder, joins day 128's same error in one five-minute action: feel beyond one flat end on a fresh known model. Other needs stay recorded for days 135/136; keep observation-window time intact.
