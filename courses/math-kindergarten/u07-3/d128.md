# Day 128: I can compare shape features after a turn.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Print two-sided student page, covering Side 2, and sealed exit-128. Prepare single turned-square label card, cube-face wrap, known upright cone, two differently colored cubes, oblong box and sphere. Patterns supplement colors. Display one practice case at a time. Select six exit observation names. Inspect models for sharp edges, staples and residue; no glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep solids low on stable paper bases.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W128-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W128-ones]] Count one through twenty together. Chorus gives no individual judgment; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 127's naming notes s07-X127a, feature tickets s07-X127b, practice, position arrows and game packet with written feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many said triangle for cone, error F: five minutes on [[s07-K128-cone]]. If many called whole cube square, error B: five minutes on [[s07-K128-cube]]. Each includes fresh partial retry and original-record revision; do not repeat the reserved diagonal cone.

#### 10 min. Teaching and model

Keep yesterday's promise to compare features after a turn. Review flat, square, rectangle and triangle, day 45; curved, day 46; side/corner, day 48; equal sides, day 51; sphere/cylinder/cone/face, day 127. Trace one square's indicated sides, then turn it. **Ask.** [[s07-K128-turn]] [[steps:s07-K128-turn]] Correct orientation-based error C after showing unchanged features.

Inspect solids separately. Touch-count six flat cube faces, turning to find hidden ones. Then put cube away and touch the known cone's one flat base; informal totals are not memorized targets. Show curved sphere surface separately. **Ask.** [[s07-K128-rule]] [[steps:s07-K128-rule]] Test the corrected feature rule after turning each known model. Color-only grouping is error D; an object's flat part does not make the whole object flat, error O.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs read [[s07-P128]]. With matching real cubes, repair the supplied flat-face group by moving the excluded cube across the line with an arrow. Color is irrelevant; on paper both drawings are uncolored. Hear two optional 45-second explanations on the supplied rule. If many use color, cover colors with plain paper while keeping geometry visible; repeat correction.

Keep flat and solid practice separate. On the known square, finish a supplied side trace through a turn; on the known cube, repeat indicated face-group correction. No large-solid/tiny-flat pair, no independent difference annotation. Individual paper corrections stay in folder.

#### 15 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Five minutes: game. Eight minutes: repeat supplied within-type group corrections across size/turn; two minutes: tidy. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Briefly review above/below/beside/next to/in front of/behind, day 45, by repeating day 127's partial cup moves as needed. Collect new corrections/game records for tomorrow's feedback packet.

Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately: [[s07-G128-1]] [[s07-G128-2]] [[s07-G128-3]] [[s07-G128-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Show 2, 3, 4, 3 with pieces or game-record marks. Give immediate feedback: error I counts only arrivals; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal both exit-master questions together; collect both in this slot.

Reveal exit-128 and its real cylinder only at the child's own turn. Read [[s07-X128a]] on the half ticket and wait. Read the second exit-master question, [[s07-X128b]]. Children separate the ticket pictures, then independently mark depth on the Exit box picture. Six children, three per ask, have one 45-second observation each, 4.5 minutes total. No added why question; record volunteered words.

Reserve one minute directions, half a minute collection and two minutes to scan thirty ticket/Exit-box pairs, four seconds each. A mark of depth beyond the circular end supports the difference; size alone does not. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Assign error D only from explicit color words, never the uncolored marks. After class write thirty feedback packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes; return on day 129.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Hand-draw masters without answers if no printer. Adult cuts and steadies models at child direction. Retain solids beside pictures; a drawing alone cannot show hidden depth.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-draw masters; boxes and paper models replace kits. Plain paper covers model colors; crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise day 127 feedback on another known model.
- **5 min. Model.** Turn one square without changing its sides. Turn known solids separately.
- **4 min. Paper.** [[s07-P128]] Correct the supplied group with an arrow. **Say.** "Correct this feature rule." Expect flat-faced cubes together, even after a turn.
- **5 min. Game.** Use familiar joining/removal stories and pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X128a]] [[s07-X128b]] Reveal exit-128, then Side 2's Exit box. Read prompts separately; child marks each paper alone. Record help; give written feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center corrections, preserving repairs, feature-rule discussion, game and exit. Longer: rotate the known cube and repair the same supplied group; never compare the reserved cylinder/circle pair.

### Words and supports

Review solid, day 4; cube, day 1; flat names, sides/corners/equal sides and new solid names as above. Use "Both have ___" only with indicated practice features. Read every word aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Offer tactile outlines, large stable models, home language, signing and directed adult marks. Record access separately from mathematical help; public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 129 applies known joining and shape features to partial solid frames. Return day 128's tickets, Exit boxes s07-X128b, practice and game records for revision. Error C, turned square rejected, gets five incremental-turn minutes; error D, color-only rule, gets five feature-group minutes. Error O, whole tube called flat for its ends, uses day 130's five-minute Return and repair slot. Other needs remain recorded for days 135/136.
