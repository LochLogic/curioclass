# Day 126: I can tell flat shapes from solid objects.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Print practice-126 through the student page and sealed exit-126 separately. Prepare single tube/box label cards, square outline and ten-part records. Choose six exit names and six unheard day 122 names. Inspect plain models for residue, staples and sharp edges: no glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep models low on stable paper bases. Withdraw supplied number frames; retain choice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. Chorus gives no individual judgment; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 125's tickets, Exit boxes, practice and game packet with written feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2; part/whole/split, day 87; partner of ten and Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97. If many called nine ten, use five minutes on [[s07-K126-ten]]. Use the other five for omitted-piece/duplicate-split notes, correcting supplied eight/two, six/four or five/five records. Revise the original record. Do not label swapped groups a new split.

#### 10 min. Teaching and model

Day 125 promised handling flat and solid shapes. Say today's goal. **Ask.** [[s07-K126-hook]] Revisit after the model. Review flat and square, day 45; solid, day 4; cube, day 1. State that a square drawing represents a flat shape. Show the whole cube extending beyond one square surface, without asking a whole/face comparison.

**Ask.** [[s07-K126-model]] [[steps:s07-K126-model]] Show correct before mistaken label, error A. Then **Ask.** [[s07-K126-picture]] [[steps:s07-K126-picture]] Contrast error M. No sole-flat selection display in teaching.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs handle a single oblong box beside its picture. Read [[s07-P126]]; children correct the supplied flat label by dictated solid or a mark linking picture to real box. Teacher scribes words, never judges reading. Place that corrected card on the teacher-labelled solid mat; show a square outline alone on the flat mat. Hear two optional 45-second contributions on the picture-label question. If many confuse paper with represented shape, hold the real box behind its picture and repeat the supplied correction.

#### 15 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Five minutes: privately hear six next unheard day 122 questions, [[s07-X122a]], 45 seconds each plus transition. Record exact words against retained solution; sign stays concealed from other children. Five minutes: game below. Five minutes: single-model corrections and tidy; fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Marked practice stays in the folder; return new paper in tomorrow's feedback packet.

Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Show 2, 3, 4, 3 with pieces or game-record marks. Give immediate feedback: error I counts only arrivals; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Unseal exit-126 and matching real models at each child's own turn; all start paper independently. Read [[s07-X126]] exactly. Six rotating children have one 45-second observation each, 4.5 minutes total; no extra spoken question. Record only volunteered explanation, never infer error M from a wrong circle alone.

Allow one minute for directions, half a minute to collect, two minutes to scan thirty marks at four seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. After class write feedback on thirty packets, fifteen seconds each, 7.5 minutes; return all judged paper on day 127 before later judgment.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Hand-draw masters without answers if no printer. Adult cuts and steadies models at child direction. Retain solids beside pictures; a drawing alone cannot show hidden depth.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-draw masters; paper pieces replace counters. Clean boxes/tubes replace kit solids; crayons replace pencils. Draw optional frames only by choice.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens, then ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise day 125 feedback on supplied ten-part records.
- **5 min. Model.** Hold a tube beside its drawing; correct its supplied flat label.
- **4 min. Paper.** [[s07-P126]] Dictate solid or link to the real box. **Say.** "Correct the picture label." Expect solid, referring to the box.
- **5 min. Game.** Alternate familiar joining/removal with pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X126]] Reveal exit-126 for the first time. Read the prompt; child marks alone. Record help; give written feedback for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated label corrections, preserving repairs, day 122 collection, game and exit. Longer: turn the same known tube again and repair its supplied label, without a mixed display.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; circle, square, rectangle, triangle and hexagon as shape names, day 45. Review flat, solid and cube as above. Read every word aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Offer tactile outlines, large stable models, home language, signing and directed adult marks. Record access separately from mathematical help; public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 127 names solid shapes and traces a cube face. Return day 126's exit, practice and new game records in Return and repair. Error M, picture material mistaken for represented shape, gets five model/picture minutes; error A, tube called flat after a turn, gets five thick-stack minutes. Each includes a fresh partial retry. Three last day 122 questions remain for centers.
