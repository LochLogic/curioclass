# Day 127: I can name solid shapes and show their parts.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Print student practice-127 and sealed exit-127. Keep unfamiliar diagonal cone hidden. Prepare cube, sphere, upright teaching cone and tube, square tracing paper, cup/shelf/block and six partial arrow scenes, displayed singly. Choose six exit names, three naming and three feature observations, plus three unheard day 122 names. Inspect models: no sharp edges, staples, residue, glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing; keep builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W127-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W127-ones]] Count one through twenty together. Chorus gives no individual judgment; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 126's written tasks with annotated feedback in this existing slot. Keep first marks beside later revisions; no extra interview or minutes.

Return day 126's exit, practice and game packet with written feedback for revision. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many tickets showed error M, use five minutes on [[s07-K127-picture]]. If many practice records showed error A, use five minutes on [[s07-K127-stack]]. Include fresh partial retry in each slot, not a second exit selection. Keep mathematical help separate from access.

#### 7 min. Teaching and model

Keep yesterday's promise: name solids and trace a cube face. Review flat, day 45; solid, day 4; cube, day 1. Introduce sphere, cylinder, cone and face with actual teaching models. State names first: sphere has a curved surface and no flat face; cylinder has two circular flat ends and a curved side; cone has a circular flat base, curved side and point. Face means flat surface here. Hollow paper models can represent solid shapes.

Review square, day 45; side/corner, day 48; equal sides, day 51. Trace a cube face, naming four equal sides. **Ask.** [[s07-K127-face]] [[steps:s07-K127-face]] Contrast error B after correct example. **Ask.** [[s07-K127-cone]] [[steps:s07-K127-cone]] Contrast error F on the known upright model, never select its feature independently.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Launch familiar supplied corrections: [[s07-P127-pos1]] [[s07-P127-pos2]] [[s07-P127-pos3]] [[s07-P127-pos4]] [[s07-P127-pos5]] [[s07-P127-pos6]] [[s07-P127]] Pairs continue independently while the teacher uses these twelve minutes for children 1 to 10, with naming-cards-127. Indicate one same-kind cube pair and read "Name this shape." Wait, then indicate the sphere pair and repeat that single ask. Two 45-second turns per child; five further Counting centers minutes complete seventeen minutes including launch/reset. No public teacher shares or inferred names from matching.

#### 18 min. Counting centers

Five teacher minutes finish the cube/sphere naming roster begun in Connected work. Three minutes hear the last three day 122 questions, s07-X122a, children 28 to 30, one 45-second turn each plus transitions. Five minutes return day 126 question feedback for later revision. Five minutes use the familiar within-five supplied-record game with immediate feedback, including tidy. [[s07-G127-1]] [[s07-G127-2]] [[s07-G127-3]] [[s07-G127-4]] Use each current row’s answer in teacher-key.md. Pairs work simultaneously; no extra interview or repeated secure name. Other children continue familiar supplied corrections while waiting.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's exit-127 and unfamiliar diagonal cone only at their turn. Read [[s07-X127a]] to three assigned children, then [[s07-X127b]] for everyone to mark independently. Three different children get feature observations. Six times 45 seconds is 4.5 minutes. Do not require writing for the spoken name; adult records exact names privately. Paper marks do not prove unheard naming.

Reserve one minute for separate directions, half a minute collection and two minutes to scan thirty papers, four seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component; unheard names stay not yet seen with observation date. After class: thirty feedback packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return on day 128.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Hand-draw masters without answers if no printer. Adult cuts and steadies models at child direction. Retain solids beside pictures; a drawing alone cannot show hidden depth.

### At home

**Swap.** Clean plain models replace kits; paper ball/cone and drawn shelf replace toys. Hand-draw masters and arrows; broad crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar tens and ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise day 126 feedback.
- **5 min. Models.** State cube, sphere, cylinder, cone; show their parts. Trace one cube face.
- **4 min. Paper.** [[s07-P127]] Finish the indicated trace. **Say.** "Correct the whole-object label." Expect cube; one square face belongs to it.
- **5 min. Game.** Repeat joining/removal with paper pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X127a]] [[s07-X127b]] Reveal the diagonal cone and exit-127 only now. Ask each prompt separately; adult records spoken name, child marks feature. Record help and give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center corrections, preserving repairs, positions, day 122 questions and exit. Longer: trace the indicated face after turning the known cube; keep all reserved models sealed.

### Words and supports

Review whole, day 87; circle and square, day 45. New spoken words: sphere, cylinder, cone, face. Accept "round end" for a circular base and "flat part" for face; repeat the precise name. Read every word aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Offer tactile outlines, large stable models, home language, signing and directed adult marks. Record access separately from mathematical help; public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 128 compares features across sizes and turns. Return day 127's naming notes, feature tickets, practice and game records for revision. If many said triangle for cone, error F gets five minutes with another round-base model; if many called cube square, error B gets five wrap-and-trace minutes. Unheard names enter days 130 to 133 domain conferences, without retroactive exit credit; remaining gaps go to days 135/136.
