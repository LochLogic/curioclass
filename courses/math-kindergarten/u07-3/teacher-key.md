# Teacher-only answer key

Keep private. Read all child print aloud. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Unheard components stay not yet seen with a scheduled date. No points.

## s07-X126

Mark the flat shape.

Answer: The circle drawing represents a flat shape; the real sphere, cube and cone are solid.

Real models beside matching drawings. A thin paper picture can represent a solid; do not classify represented shape by paper material. | Shape: Select sole flat form from mixed display; practice relabels one model at a time.

Shown: Marks circle. | Not yet seen: Marks sphere and says its picture is flat.

## s07-X127a

Name this shape.

Answer: Cone.

Three assigned speakers, 45 seconds each, real diagonally lying cone beside drawing. Remaining names pending. Usual communication accepted. | Shape: Name unfamiliar cone-shaped model; practice corrects supplied labels on known single models.

Shown: Cone. | Not yet seen: Triangle from its outline.

## s07-X127b

Mark one part that helps you know.

Answer: Circular flat base or pointed tip on cone picture.

All mark paper; three different children heard. A marked part shows selection, not an unheard spoken name. | Shape: Select diagnostic feature of unfamiliar cone; practice corrects supplied cube-face traces/indicated labels.

Shown: Marks circular base. | Not yet seen: Traces triangular silhouette and calls whole solid triangle.

## s07-X128a

Separate the pictures that are different shapes.

Answer: Line separates large cylinder model and tiny circle drawing. They are different shapes.

Paper separation establishes classification, not an unheard reason. Show real cylinder beside its picture. | Shape: Compare a large solid and tiny flat form directly; practice within-type feature sorts only.

Shown: Separates cylinder and circle. | Not yet seen: Groups both as flat because cylinder has flat ends.

## s07-X128b

Mark what makes them different.

Answer: Marks cylinder body extending beyond its circular end, unlike the flat circle.

A depth arrow, trace or directed adult mark is valid. Color alone is not geometric evidence. | Shape: Independently annotate dimension difference on mismatched-size pair; practice repairs indicated labels.

Shown: Marks thickness beyond cylinder end. | Not yet seen: Marks only color as difference.

## s07-X129

Build a solid like this one.

Answer: Cylinder-like body with curved side between two circular flat ends; paper sketch/trace records build.

Loose rollable paper, circular ends and soft paper joins. Adult joins at child direction. Judge shape, not craftsmanship. Open tube may model cylinder when both end boundaries/body are evident. | Shape: Independently construct whole cylinder-like model; practice repairs partial cube/box frames, no cylinder step card.

Shown: Builds curved body extending between round ends, records sketch. | Not yet seen: Builds only a flat circle and says whole tube is flat.

## s07-P126

Correct the supplied label for this model.

Answer: The single oblong box is solid; its picture represents that solid object.

Supplied partial practice only. Immediate feedback; record support. At most two optional 45-second public contributions. All children mark paper; no independent reading.

Shown: The single oblong box is solid; its picture represents that solid object. | Not yet seen: Calls represented box flat because its drawing is paper.

## s07-P127

Supplied partial practice only. Immediate feedback; record support. At most two optional 45-second public contributions. All children mark paper; no independent reading.

Shown: Complete boundary of indicated square cube face, distinguish face from whole. | Not yet seen: Calls whole cube square.
