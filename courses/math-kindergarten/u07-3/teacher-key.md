## s07-K126-hook

How are solid shapes different from flat ones?

Answer: A solid extends beyond a flat outline; a flat form does not have that depth.

Use the known square outline and one box, shown separately. Accept pointing or informal sticks out language; demonstrate again after listening. Paper is a thin object whose drawing may represent either type of shape.

Shown: A solid extends beyond a flat outline; a flat form does not have that depth. | Not yet seen: Says every pictured object is flat because it is on paper.

## s07-K126-model

Correct this label.

Answer: The sideways tube is solid; its picture represents a solid object.

Show one real tube upright, then sideways. State solid: it extends beyond a flat outline. Label its drawing solid, referring to what the picture represents. Then supply the mistaken flat label on that same sideways tube. Correct it without choosing among several objects. A paper drawing has slight thickness, but the shape it represents may be flat or solid.

Shown: The sideways tube is solid; its picture represents a solid object. | Not yet seen: Calls tube flat because it lies sideways.
