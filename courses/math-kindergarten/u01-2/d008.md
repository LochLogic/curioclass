# Day 8: Build a matching group

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover the star-and-button exit card. Teacher-only answer key: d008-key.html. Lay out large squares and strips for physical pairs. Bring day 7 art-corner cards and practice with feedback. Choose six rotating exit listeners; keep names heard, words and help on the class list. Keep pieces on trays; replace small or damaged tools with large paper.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 7 whole-group checking with [[s01-W8]], then review [[s01-N8]] slowly together. Partners take 45 seconds each, simultaneously. Keep the whole group visible.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 7's exit and written task s01-P7 with feedback. If day 7's tickets showed error A, reach every member of five fresh squares; if error B, cover each after its word. Error x: establish the physical group boundary before counting. Choose one repair, together if more than ten need it, otherwise in a small group while others revisit loose objects.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Introduce match through physical partners using [[s01-K8]]. Ask before building the second group.

[[steps:s01-K8]]

Connect physical pairs to an adjacent drawn record with visible links. This prepares tomorrow's use of the record. Review ordered words with [[s01-F8]], right before wrong; say the puppet scene is invented for practice.

#### 14 min. Build a matching group

Pairs build a second collection beside three loose partner pieces, then one, using squares and strips. Align physical pairs before making [[s01-P8]]. Both collections are produced together on one page; no card is matched to a supplied picture group.

**Ask.** [[s01-Q8]] Expect distinct physical pairs or careful touch-counts. Each partner has two 45-second turns, three minutes per pair, simultaneously; drawing and checking fill the remainder. If many rely on size, align each actual pair and check no member is left alone.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers: choose cubes, flat blocks or loose pairs. Keep stars, buttons and picture-group choice cards put away. Hear six practice children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, while partners check familiar actions. Note any ordered-word errors actually heard, separately from correct paper links. Collect every student page for feedback.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most show a physical pair and its recorded link, 45 seconds each. Children may pass. Give a brief next step; table groups return pieces and leave pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each new card at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X8]] exactly. Children draw one matching line independently; no further spoken ask. Observe six rotating children for at most 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, including spontaneous checking. Directions, simultaneous paper responses, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes. Record the selected quantity; do not infer number-word order from the line. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all papers on day 9, Return and repair. Finish feedback after class: thirty children's two-paper sets at twenty seconds per set, ten minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and entire exit picture from the master, preserving spacing and choices. Keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; plain paper replaces mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large squares and strips replace all blocks. Plain paper replaces the mat. Copy and cover the exit master.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.

- **3 min. Review.** Count the whole five-piece group once each.
- **4 min. Repair.** Rehearse one through five with one touch per piece.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Pair three squares with three strips, then draw the pairs together and link them.
- **13 min. Practice.** Your child makes [[s01-P8]].
  **Say.** "How could we check these groups?" Expect checking one partner per piece. Rehearse counting words through ten.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X8]] on the uncovered card. Your child draws the line alone. Keep the response for feedback before next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice produces physical pairs and their linked record; the exit selects a pictured quantity match among new alternatives.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep demonstration, practice and exit. Longer: change the spacing of loose groups, then verify each physical pair again.

### Words and supports

New word: match. Review: group and mark from day 6. Adjacent object-to-mark links connect each pictured pair; children produce the record rather than choose a picture card. Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner, from days 1 to 5. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Read every printed line aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile pictures and the child's communication aid. A child may direct an adult's marks; record help. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 applies adjacent links in classroom picture-book pages. Return day 8 papers. Observed error A: include every member of a new loose group; error B: cover each after its word. Error C heard during practice: rehearse ordered words before pairing them. Choose one five-minute action; unexplained wrong matching remains error x and gets a fresh physical-pair check.
