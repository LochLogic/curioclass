# Day 6: Notice a small group, then check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the small-group exit card covered. Teacher-only answer key: d006-key.html. Bring day 5 leaf cards and written task s01-P5-1 with feedback. Select six exit listeners not heard yesterday; add columns for names heard, exact words, help and next step to the class list. Set out large intact pieces on trays; replace small or damaged ones with paper.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 5 tool choice with [[s01-W6]]. Fifteen pairs work at once, 45 seconds each partner. Retain the support the child chose yesterday.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 5's exit and written task s01-P5-1 with private feedback. Distribute both papers and read feedback aloud, one minute. Select one observed practice difficulty or, if day 5's tickets showed error x, compare familiar touching, moving and plain-mat supports with fresh loose squares. Model one correction, one minute. More than ten affected: demonstrate together; otherwise meet one small group while others revisit familiar pieces. Partners retry [[s01-W6]] with five fresh squares, two minutes, working simultaneously. Collect papers for dated folders, one minute. Queue other errors; no leaf-tray practice.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Name a group as objects considered together. Use [[s01-K6]] with loose pieces, asking before touching.

[[steps:s01-K6]]

A quick answer is optional; leave or show the pieces again. Then review the puppet omission with [[s01-F6]]. Show right before wrong. Say the puppet scene is invented for practice.

#### 14 min. Notice then verify

Pairs show one, three and four loose shapes in turn, without a timer or target-card choice. Repeat [[s01-K6]] as actions, then complete [[s01-P6]] on the original block picture page. Mark means a pencil trace; review the familiar count-dot record.

**Ask.** [[s01-Q6]] Expect a quantity or a gesture encompassing the noticed pieces. Three 45-second notice-and-check turns per child take 4.5 minutes per pair, simultaneously. Allow two minutes for partner talk and 7.5 for dotting, checking and transitions. If many skip, point slowly to each separate object before the next round.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers from day 1: choose cubes, flat shapes or the original object-only block picture page. Use familiar touch or move actions, without leaf cards. Spend two minutes modeling [[s01-N6]], slowly, then repeat together. Hear six practice children for 45 seconds each during familiar counting, 4.5 minutes. Record actual words; unheard words remain not yet seen. Collect every student page for feedback.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most, 45 seconds each, show a familiar counting action. Children may pass. Give a brief next-step comment. Groups return pieces to trays together; keep pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Uncover each new card only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X6]] exactly; children mark the half slip independently. Do not add another spoken ask. Hear the counting of the rotating six, at most 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Directions, simultaneous paper work, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes. Record card selection, distinct dots and the words actually heard separately; unheard words remain not yet seen. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return exit and practice on day 7, Return and repair. Finish feedback after class: thirty children's two-paper sets at twenty seconds per set, ten minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and entire exit picture from the master, preserving spacing and choices. Keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; plain paper replaces mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper pieces replace all tools; plain paper replaces the mat. Copy and cover the exit master.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.

- **3 min. Review.** Count five loose pieces with yesterday's chosen support.
- **4 min. Repair.** Model reaching each square once.
- **5 min. Notice.** Show one, three, then four pieces; let your child notice, then touch to check.
  **Say.** "What did you notice before touching?" Expect the quantity or a gesture showing the group.
- **13 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P6]]; your child dots the block pictures. Rehearse counting words through ten together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X6]] on the uncovered card. Your child marks alone. Keep the response; give feedback before next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice notices a loose group and checks objects; the exit selects a quantity among unseen leaf cards.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep demonstration, practice and exit. Longer: show the same loose groups in another arrangement and check again, without claiming an invariance rule.

### Words and supports

New word: group. Name mark while recalling the familiar pencil dot. No written numeral is taught. Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner, from days 1 to 5. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Read every printed line aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile pictures and the child's communication aid. A child may direct an adult's marks; record help. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 7 finds classroom groups and checks by counting. Return day 6 papers. If error A was observed, dot each fresh square once; if error B, cover each after its word. An unexplained wrong choice, error x, calls for a fresh loose-group check. Choose one five-minute repair, not all. Keep unheard number words not yet seen.
