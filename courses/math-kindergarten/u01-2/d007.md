# Day 7: Find a group in the room

## Teacher page

### Before class

Place a closed book and five large blocks at each pair's reachable table. Keep the art-corner exit card covered. Teacher-only answer key: d007-key.html. Bring day 6 small-group cards and marked practice. Choose six exit listeners different from yesterday; keep names heard, exact words and help columns. Keep large intact pieces on trays and walkways clear.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 6 noticing then touching with [[s01-W7]]. Each partner takes one 45-second turn simultaneously across fifteen pairs. Rehearse [[s01-N7]] together slowly; no new sequence or timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 6's exit and written task s01-P6 with feedback. If day 6's tickets showed error A, dot five fresh squares once each. For error B, cover each after its word; for error x, notice a fresh loose group before checking. Choose only one needed action. Whole class if more than ten need it; otherwise a small group while others use familiar pieces.

#### 8 min. Recall and demonstrate

Review day 6 group and Notice then verify using [[s01-K7]].

[[steps:s01-K7]]

Then review the repeat error with [[s01-F7]], right before wrong. Say this puppet demonstration is invented for practice. Never use an art-corner search or ask for a target quantity.

#### 14 min. Classroom hunt

Pairs visit the safe, reachable book and block groups, taking turns to choose either whole group without a target-number request. Count objects, never children or their features. Each child makes [[s01-P7]] using the familiar drawing-and-dot actions. The prepared groups make the task runnable without other room supplies.

**Ask.** [[s01-Q7]] Expect a distinct-touch demonstration. Two 45-second turns per child, three minutes per pair, run simultaneously; the remaining time holds looking, drawing and transitions. If many lose the group boundary, surround the selected physical group with a plain mat and try again.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers: cubes, flat blocks, and the original block picture page from day 6. No art supplies or target-finding pictures. Repeat known counting actions; teacher hears six practice children, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, while others work with partners. Record actual word-object actions, not a guessed sequence from the drawing. Collect all student pages for feedback.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

At most two volunteers show a found group and its checking action, 45 seconds each. Children may pass. Give brief feedback; table groups return pieces together and leave pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the new art-corner picture at each child's own turn. Read [[s01-X7]] exactly. Count-dots identify the whole selected tray and record checking. Do not add another spoken ask or demonstrate the picture. Hear the counting from six rotating children, at most 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Directions, simultaneous paper work, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes. Record selection and any observed count separately; unheard ordered words remain not yet seen. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all papers on day 8, Return and repair. After class allow thirty times twenty seconds, ten minutes, for feedback.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and entire exit picture from the master, preserving spacing and choices. Keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; plain paper replaces mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace blocks; fold paper for a book. Plain paper replaces the mat. Copy and cover the exit picture.

**Skip.** Room rotations and public sharing.

- **3 min. Review.** Notice three loose pieces, then check each.
- **4 min. Repair.** Model covering a piece after its word.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Show a safe whole group, then its simple drawing.
- **13 min. Hunt.** Your child chooses either prepared group for [[s01-P7]].
  **Say.** "How did you check your group?" Expect touching each member once, with counting words. Rehearse one through ten together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X7]] on the uncovered picture. Your child marks alone. Keep the response and give feedback before next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice finds any physical classroom group and makes a record; the exit locates a specified whole group in a new art scene.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep demonstration, practice and exit. Longer: visit a second prepared group and compare the checking actions.

### Words and supports

Review: group and mark, named on day 6; no new math method today. Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner, from days 1 to 5. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Read every printed line aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile pictures and the child's communication aid. A child may direct an adult's marks; record help. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 builds matching loose-object collections. Return day 7 papers. Observed error A: reach every member of a fresh five-piece group. Error B: cover each after its word. Error x: establish the whole loose-group boundary. Choose the earliest needed action in Return and repair, keeping unheard word sequences not yet seen.
