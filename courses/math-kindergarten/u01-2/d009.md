# Day 9: Make a picture-book record

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the shell exit card front; place the empty panel on its reverse. Keep both covered. Teacher-only answer key: d009-key.html. Bring day 8 star-and-button cards and practice with feedback. Choose six rotating listeners on the class list. Put large intact tools on trays; pencils replace crayons. Keep five blocks visible for the picture-book record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 8 physical pairs with [[s01-W9]]. Partners take 45 seconds each, simultaneously across fifteen pairs. Slowly rehearse [[s01-N9]] together, continuing the sequence from day 6.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 8's exit and written task s01-P8 with feedback. If day 8's tickets showed error A, include every member of five fresh squares; error B, cover each after its word. If practice showed error C, rehearse one through five before touching. Error x gets a fresh physical-pair check. Choose one five-minute action; small group unless more than ten need it.

#### 8 min. Recall and demonstrate

Review adjacent links from day 8 with [[s01-K9]].

[[steps:s01-K9]]

Then use [[s01-F9]] to review one word per separate action, right before wrong. Say this puppet scene is invented for practice. The book page applies known drawing, dot and link actions; no new notation or number-writing task.

#### 14 min. Make picture-book pages

Children create [[s01-P9]] from five classroom blocks. Keep each mark adjacent to its drawing with a visible link, all on the same side. Partners follow each link and compare it with the real block.

**Ask.** [[s01-Q9]] Expect the connected block, shown by tracing its line. Fifteen pairs work at once; two 45-second turns per child take three minutes per pair, leaving eleven for drawing, checking and transitions. If many skip or repeat, point to each actual block as its linked mark is checked. Collect each page for feedback.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers with cubes, flat shapes and the adjacent-link page. Children may repeat the same five-block page; do not make separate-panel records or shell pictures. Hear six practice children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, while others use familiar partner actions. Note word-action coordination separately from drawing quality. No marks need to look like their objects.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

At most two volunteers follow one link in their pages, 45 seconds each; children may pass. Give a brief next step. Groups return pieces together and leave pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Uncover the new shell half slip at each child's own turn. Read [[s01-X9]] exactly. Children may turn it over as often as needed and make marks independently on the blank reverse. Do not add a spoken count or explanation ask. Observe source-to-mark actions for six rotating children, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Directions, simultaneous drawing, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help and observed skips or repeats. Return all papers on day 10, Return and repair. Finish feedback after class: thirty times twenty seconds, ten minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and entire exit picture from the master, preserving spacing and choices. Keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; plain paper replaces mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace blocks; a pencil replaces crayons. Plain paper replaces the mat. Copy the exit front with a blank reverse; cover it.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public sharing.

- **3 min. Review.** Make three physical square-strip pairs.
- **4 min. Repair.** Model one counting word per separate touch.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Draw five blocks with adjacent marks and visible links, as yesterday.
- **13 min. Practice.** Your child makes [[s01-P9]].
  **Say.** "Which block goes with this mark?" Expect following the link to its block. Rehearse one through ten together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X9]] on the uncovered card. Let your child flip freely and mark alone. Give feedback before next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice draws an adjacent linked record with its objects visible; the exit records new shells on the reverse without printed links.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep demonstration, practice and exit. Longer: make another adjacent linked record of the same five blocks in a new layout.

### Words and supports

Review: group and mark from day 6, match and adjacent links from day 8. Picture-book pages collect the familiar linked records; artistic likeness is unnecessary. Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner, from days 1 to 5. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Read every printed line aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile pictures and the child's communication aid. A child may direct an adult's marks; record help. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 uses object-place pairs to decide whether every place has a partner. Return day 9 papers. Observed error A: dot every fresh square once; error B: cover after its word; error D heard in practice: pause between separate touches. Choose one action in Return and repair. Unexplained record errors remain descriptive, error x.
