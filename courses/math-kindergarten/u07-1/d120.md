# Day 120: I can compose a shape and count its pieces.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately for exits and this page for practice keys. Print student page and half ticket. Print the complete two-question exit-120 half slip. Read one prompt at a time; reveal the next after the first response. Adult-cut pieces must fit the target at the same scale. Choose six observation names. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Inspect models; no sharp edges, staples, glass, metal cans or mouthing. Send family.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. No individual evidence from chorus; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 8 min. Hook

Whole class: show a square drawing, two adult-cut half-square triangles and a real cube. Review flat, square and triangle, day 45; cube, day 1. **Ask.** [[s07-K120-hook]] Accept a visible feature; wonder aloud about differences between flat and solid shapes. Keep that question open until day 126. Hear one optional 45-second contribution.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review compose, outline, gap and overlap, day 60. **Ask.** [[s07-K120-tri]] [[steps:s07-K120-tri]] Trace the repaired diagonal on construction-record. Review equation, plus and equals, day 78: [[s07-K120-eq]] [[steps:s07-K120-eq]] Hear at most two 45-second volunteers; everyone points or marks.

#### 10 min. Connected work

Launch the familiar supplied paper corrections before listening: [[s07-P120]] [[s07-F120-count]] Children continue these tasks independently; no new blank whole-build or whole-story solution. Demonstrate only the needed direction, then omit public shares. Use position-scenes and position-roster for cells 1 to 20, child first and relation second. One uncued relation per 45-second ask, then its separate target mark; no supplied relation or solid name. Retain genuine earlier matching evidence and skip that cell. The position block uses 10 minutes here and 6 in Counting centers, including launch/reset. Paper alone never proves speech.

#### 24 min. Counting centers

Return day 119's assessment and s06-P119 with written feedback in the existing ten-minute Unit 6 response allocation. Preserve original marks and later revisions.

Finish the position block in six teacher minutes. Then use ten separate teacher minutes for day 119 Unit 6 feedback, one assigned repair and later revision. A ten-and-further-ones gap uses the supplied fourteen-piece record, restoring its missing mark while ten individual ones stay visible. No simultaneous teacher-led repair during interviews. Other children continue the launched partial math records, including five independent prior-feedback revision minutes. Seven minutes familiar independent correction and one minute collection/tidy complete the block. The optional within-five game is omitted today. The familiar game rows remain available on a later funded day: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]].

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal both exit-master questions together; collect both in this slot.

Reveal exit-120 only at the child's own turn. Read [[s07-X120a]], then read the second exit-master question, [[s07-X120b]] Everyone marks independently. Cover the rectangle with one square and two half-square triangles, trace joins, then write 3. Accept an accurate count of an incomplete build; judge its uncovered space separately. Separate geometry and numeral formation; dictation records access, not independent formation. Diagnostic, never final evidence. Hear six rotating children, one 45-second observation each, three per ask across the ticket and Exit box: 4.5 minutes. Public talk may be passed.

One minute covers directions/collection; 2.5 minutes scan thirty tickets, five seconds each. Children work during observations. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately from mathematical help. Finish written feedback after class: thirty packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return every paper next day in Return and repair for revision before later judgment.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges teaching pictures only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adults cut pieces and steady the paper base at the child's direction. Draw optional number frames by choice.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces replace blocks/counters; hand-draw masters, frames and equations if no printer. Broad crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Look and repair.** Slide partly filled square and supplied strip pieces until sides meet.
- **5 min. Count record.** Touch each square of the supplied six-piece array once; correct its count.
- **5 min. Game.** Alternate familiar joining/removal stories.
- **7 min. Paper.** Revise day 119 feedback. **Say.** "Point to your repair." Expect a closed gap or corrected count.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X120a]] [[s07-X120b]] Reveal the fresh rectangle and fitted pieces. Read each stem separately; your child traces joins and writes the count.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center practice, preserving repairs, game and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial correction with pieces turned; keep reserved cases sealed until evidence collection.

### Words and supports

Review shape, day 45; rectangle, day 45; join, day 51; pattern block, day 2; counter, day 3. Review compose, outline, gap, overlap, day 60; count, day 1; numeral, day 30. Gap is uncovered space; overlap covers another piece. Read aloud; no independent reading. Large tactile shapes, home language, signs and directed adult marks support access. Record mathematical help separately; public speech may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 121 compares piece counts. Return day 120's annotated exit, practice and games in Return and repair: error K gaps, five minutes; error L revisits, five. Its ten-minute Counting centers slot receives day 119's unit 6 records revised today; check selected fresh transfer and judge earlier revised evidence afterward. New paper gets feedback and later revision. Further needs stay scheduled.
