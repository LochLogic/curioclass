# Position collection roster

Use one copy as the class roster. Replace class-list numbers with names privately. Each row is one 45-second ask; a child marks that target during the same turn. Keep the other panels covered. A home-language response or usual communication counts with its support recorded. A mark never establishes an unheard word.

Twenty turns on 120/121 take fifteen minutes plus one reset. Twenty-eight on each of 122 to 126 take twenty-one plus one reset. Connected work supplies ten minutes on 120 and twelve on 121 to 126; centers supply six, four, then ten daily. Launch familiar independent mathematics before interviewing. Unit 6 response and E122 question turns are sequential teacher blocks.

Written feedback returns next day for five independent revision minutes while another child is heard. No teacher-led repair runs concurrently. Keep the first response, exact words, help, feedback, revision and judgment date. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
