## s07-X120a

Fill this outline with these pieces.

Answer: One square and two half-square right triangles cover the two-square rectangle without gaps or overlaps. Square at either end and either diagonal are valid.

Look for complete coverage and traced joins on paper. | Shape: Independently complete an outline construction; practice repairs partial fills, never this rectangle or complete three-piece construction.

Shown: Fully covers the rectangle, traces joins. | Not yet seen: Leaves a visible gap.

Each piece counted once. Formation separate from quantity; dictated numeral records access, not independent formation. | Shape: Self-composed whole-to-numeral record; practice corrects a supplied count on a separate array.
