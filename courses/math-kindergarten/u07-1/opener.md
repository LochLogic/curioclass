# Section 7.1

Composition counts pieces, not area. Review Outer boundary and internal joins using an incomplete long strip: the outside boundary differs from joins inside. Full sides meet without gaps or overlap. A square is also a rectangle. Do not reuse unit 3's completed hexagon fills as an exit. Days 120/121 retain ten center minutes each for unit 6 response: first revision of day 119 feedback, then fresh transfer and judgment of earlier revised work. See ../u06-1/record.md for the first unresolved component. New transfers receive feedback and later revision. Send family.md on day 120.

Read every word aloud; children do not read independently. Use 18 point print, high contrast, large stable pieces and tactile models. Accept usual communication, home-language reasoning and directed adult movement. Scribe labels when needed; note access separately from mathematical help. A child may pass in public talk. Patterns supplement color. Every scene and quantity is invented for practice; say this aloud.

Review the established routines Counting centers, Partner turns, Paper count-dot record and Return and repair from unit 1, rather than assuming planned entries were taught. Demonstrate with fresh partial records. Review count and numeral from units 1/2, composition and flat names from unit 3, equation/plus/minus/equals from unit 4, part/whole/split/partner of ten from unit 5, and Oral tens to one hundred from unit 6. Keep the ledger wording. If earlier teaching is missing, demonstrate it before use; never call a planned record evidence.

Before class, print only today's practice and sealed reserved master. Open teacher-key.md privately; never display its answers or print them for children. Reserved pictures appear only when their named question is administered, including any later unheard-child turn. Put all other copies away. Teaching, centers, repairs and home practice use supplied partial corrections, never independent reserved formats. Draw masters by hand when printing is unavailable; same mathematical layout and blank response room. One shared screen may enlarge paper, with no queue.

Use large adult-cut paper shapes, drawn outlines and frames, paper squares instead of counters, clean plain boxes/tubes and paper balls/cones instead of kits. Soft paper joins and rolled paper replace sticks/clay. Inspect for staples, residue and sharp edges; no glass, metal cans, small loose joins, mouthing or throwing. Keep builds low, spheres loose on a mat. Paper is default so no clay handwashing queue. Adult attaches joins at child's direction.

The day is sixty minutes as its data budget states. Counting is simultaneous, five minutes; games use four familiar mixed asks and immediate feedback in five center minutes, untimed. Fifteen pairs alternate two 45-second turns simultaneously, 1.5 minutes. Hear at most two optional teaching contributions. Exit tickets are half sheets, at most two paper items; an extra judged paper item is a student-page Exit box. Six 45-second samples take 4.5 minutes, one ask per child. Paper marks never establish an unheard name or explanation.

Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Pending describes scheduling, not another score. Return written feedback to the named receiving part, revise on a later day, then judge earlier work. More than ten sharing a letter triggers its five-minute repair including fresh partial retry. Two actions maximum in ten minutes, one in five. Further needs stay recorded; day 134 does not reteach. For one child, count 3, demonstrate 5, repair 5, paper/building 9, exit 8; split evidence into another sitting if needed.
