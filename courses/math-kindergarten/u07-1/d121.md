# Day 121: I can compare builds by how many pieces they use.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md for exits and this teacher page's worked steps privately. Print practice-121 as the student-page picture and exit-121 as the sealed half-sheet ticket, without a second generated ticket. Choose six observation names on record. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Adult-cut exit pieces must fit the outline at the same scale. Inspect models for sharp edges, staples and residue; no glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. No individual evidence from chorus; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 120's tickets and Exit boxes, s07-X120a, s07-X120b, with written feedback for revision. Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 120's annotated exit, practice and games. If many left gaps, five minutes repair [[s07-K121-strip]] on the partial four-square strip, then retry on the six-square strip. If many revisited a piece, five minutes correct yesterday's supplied six-piece count by moving counted pieces across a boundary, then retry on the supplied four-piece row. Revise retained written feedback. Secure children check retained practice; never reuse the exit as a new test.

#### 10 min. Review and model

Say today's goal. Review compose, outline and gap, day 60; count, day 1. Repair each partial strip with [[s07-K121-strip]], covering it before the next. Review more and fewer, day 21; same, day 20. Keep builds covered: **Ask.** [[s07-K121-match]] [[steps:s07-K121-match]] Match once each, not by spread. Error G calls the wider four-piece row more. Count four and six to verify two extras. No completed-build comparison in teaching.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs finish [[s07-P121]] on practice-121; the first two links are supplied. Link each remaining upper square to one unused lower square, then mark the two extras. Extras establish more even when close together. Hear two optional 45-second explanations. Re-pair loose pieces if many use width; each child independently corrects their own record.

#### 15 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Ten minutes receive day 119's retained unit 6 records, including day 120 revisions. For an unresolved ten-and-further-ones component, use [[s07-P121-transfer]]: ten supplied squares, three further marks and an empty fourth place beneath 14. Review ten ones and further ones, day 108. Restore the missing mark, then count fourteen once each. Otherwise revise retained feedback; never repeat a secure interview. New paper gets feedback and later revision; judge earlier revised evidence after class. Further needs stay scheduled. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Remaining five minutes hold game and tidy.

Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Show 2, 3, 4, 3 with pieces or marks on game-record. Give immediate feedback; error I counts only arrivals, error H adds during removal. No timer. Collect new paper for next day's Return and repair packet; marked game-record stays in the folder.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-121 only at the child's own turn. Read separately: [[s07-X121]] Everyone marks independently. L-builds have equal outlines; lower rows show their loose pieces. Six squares use more than three rectangles despite row spread. Matching leaves three extras. Choice alone proves no unheard reason. Hear six rotating children, one 45-second observation each, three per ask on a two-item ticket: 4.5 minutes. Public talk may be passed.

One minute covers directions/collection; 2.5 minutes scan thirty tickets, five seconds each. Children work during observations. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately from mathematical help. Finish written feedback after class: thirty packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return every paper next day in Return and repair for revision before later judgment.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges teaching pictures only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adults cut pieces and steady the paper base at the child's direction. Draw optional number frames by choice.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces replace blocks/counters; hand-draw masters, frames and equations if no printer. Broad crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Revise day 120 feedback on partial strips.
- **5 min. Match.** Keep builds covered; pair four loose pieces with six.
- **7 min. Paper.** [[s07-P121]] Finish supplied links. **Say.** "Show the extra pieces." Expect two extras in six.
- **5 min. Game.** Alternate familiar joining/removal stories.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X121]] Reveal the fresh L-builds. Read the stem; your child marks independently. Record help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center practice, preserving repairs, game and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial correction with pieces turned; keep reserved cases sealed until evidence collection.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; numeral, day 30; match, day 8; join, day 51. Review more/fewer, day 21; same, day 20. More needs extras after unique matching, not greater spread. Read aloud; no independent reading. Large tactile shapes, home language, signs and directed adult marks support access. Record mathematical help separately; public speech may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 122 asks and solves counting stories. Return day 121's annotated exit/practice in Return and repair. Error G, choosing the wider smaller group, gets five minutes on fresh partial matching links. Unit 6 new transfers receive feedback and later revision before judgment.
