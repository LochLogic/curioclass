# Day 122: I can ask and solve a story about a shape build.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md for exits and this teacher page's worked steps privately. Print practice-122 as the student-page picture and exit-122 as the sealed half-sheet ticket, without a second generated ticket. Choose six observation names on record. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Adult-cut exit pieces must fit the outline at the same scale. Inspect models for sharp edges, staples and residue; no glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. No individual evidence from chorus; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 121's annotated exit and matching practice. If many used width, five minutes finish a fresh partial four/six matching record, marking two extras in six: [[s07-P121]] with rows turned vertically. Other five minutes address a retained join/tracking need on supplied strips. Revise original feedback; no new completed-build comparison.

#### 10 min. Rehearsal

Say today's goal. Review story, day 68; compose and outline, day 60. Show the adult-started eight-place strip with six occupied places. **Ask.** [[s07-K122-ending]] [[steps:s07-K122-ending]] Contrast a countable ending with an unrelated wondering. Then show practice-122, a six-place outlined strip; place four loose squares over the first four places, leaving two outlined places empty. This overlay makes the partial construction clear.

#### 12 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s07-P122]] Children finish the teacher-started question with a partner and mark the part they mean. Judge the ending by whether its pictured part is countable; an unrelated ending is error x. Accept already placed or still missing; four occupied/two empty on the overlaid record. Then **Ask.** [[s07-F122-whole]] [[steps:s07-F122-whole]] Show correct whole before the two-only error I contrast. Everyone corrects a supplied enclosure on construction-record. Hear two optional 45-second contributions. Never originate and solve a whole new question in practice.

#### 15 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Ten minutes repeat partial question/record corrections; five minutes play the game and tidy. Fifteen pairs alternate two 45-second roles simultaneously; each then marks independently. Keep the sign sealed. Note only heard wording; partner reports never establish an unheard question.

Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Show 2, 3, 4, 3 with pieces or marks on game-record. Give immediate feedback; error I counts only arrivals, error H adds during removal. No timer. Collect new paper for next day's Return and repair packet; marked game-record stays in the folder.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the sign at each child's own paper turn. Read separately: [[s07-X122a]] [[s07-X122b]] Everyone privately forms a question through usual communication and marks its answer. A partner may listen, never supply it. Hear three assigned questions and three different solution accounts, one 45-second ask each, 4.5 minutes. Never infer wording from marks. Twenty-seven questions get later centers turns; retain solution paper for correspondence. A total question gives seven, triangles five, squares two; accept another answerable question and correct solution. Error I is an added-part-only total; unrelated questions get descriptive notes.

One minute covers directions/collection; 2.5 minutes scan thirty tickets, five seconds each. Children work during observations. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately from mathematical help. Finish written feedback after class: thirty packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return every paper next day in Return and repair for revision before later judgment.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges teaching pictures only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adults cut pieces and steady the paper base at the child's direction. Draw optional number frames by choice.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces replace blocks/counters; hand-draw masters, frames and equations if no printer. Broad crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Revise day 121 feedback on partial matching links.
- **5 min. Question ending.** [[s07-P122]] Overlay four squares on the first four places. **Say.** "What ending fits?" Expect already placed or still missing.
- **7 min. Paper.** Correct supplied four-plus-two enclosure; no whole-question practice.
- **5 min. Game.** Alternate familiar joining/removal stories.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X122a]] [[s07-X122b]] Reveal the sign. Record your child's question exactly, then let them mark its answer.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center practice, preserving repairs, game and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial correction with pieces turned; keep reserved cases sealed until evidence collection.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; triangle, day 45; match, day 8. Review story, day 68; part/whole, day 87. The picture must hold an answer. Read aloud; no independent reading. Large tactile shapes, home language, signs and directed adult marks support access. Record mathematical help separately; public speech may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 123 connects groups to equations. Return day 122's annotated exit/practice in Return and repair; error I, only an added part as whole, gets five minutes on supplied enclosures. Centers hear six unheard questions each day 123 to 126, then three on 127: initial three plus 6/6/6/6/3 reaches thirty. Keep each question with its solution; unheard language is not yet seen.
