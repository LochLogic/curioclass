# Day 124: I can show joining and separating stories.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md for exits and this teacher page's worked steps privately. Print practice-124 as the student-page picture and exit-124 as the sealed half-sheet ticket, without a second generated ticket. Choose six observation names on record. Keep student Side 2 covered until the exit. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Adult-cut exit pieces must fit the outline at the same scale. Inspect models for sharp edges, staples and residue; no glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. No individual evidence from chorus; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 123's tickets and Exit boxes, s07-X123, with written feedback for revision. Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 123's annotated exit and equation practice. If many showed error N, repeating one group, five minutes link six/two groups to their own numerals using [[s07-P124-repair-equation]]. If many counted only an arriving part, five minutes repair [[s07-K124-join]]. Revise original feedback. Secure children trace corrected links; never turn the exit menu into practice.

#### 10 min. Rehearsal

Say today's goal. Review story, day 68; plus/minus/equals, day 78. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone handles pieces. **Ask.** [[s07-K124-join]] [[steps:s07-K124-join]] Then show six squares and physically remove two: four remain. Point to four remaining, two removed and six before removal. Show this correct action before contrasting error H, adding two and obtaining eight. Counting all is valid.

#### 12 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s07-P124]] on practice-124. Read the supplied action aloud: Rin had six pieces and removed two. Children trace the two removal marks and finish the missing quantity, four. Repeat the supplied joining-record enclosure, never a blank whole-story solution. Hear two optional 45-second explanations. If many add during removal, move two pieces off the base and recount only those still there. Retain each corrected record.

#### 12 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Five minutes collect the retained day 122 question, s07-X122a from six new unheard children: 4.5 minutes plus transition, sign private. Record exact wording and compare retained solution. Two minutes repeat a supplied correction and tidy; five minutes play the game. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously; each marks independently.

Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Show 2, 3, 4, 3 with pieces or marks on game-record. Give immediate feedback; error I counts only arrivals, error H adds during removal. No timer. Collect new paper for next day's Return and repair packet; marked game-record stays in the folder.

#### 11 min. Exit ticket

Reveal both exit-master questions together; collect both in this slot.

Reveal exit-124 only at the child's own turn. Read [[s07-X124]], then read the second exit-master question, [[s07-P124-exitbox-removal]] Everyone marks independently. Give blank paper and loose pieces. Seven marks or equivalent drawing represents five initial plus two arriving. Counting all is valid. Two alone as whole shows error I. For the removal Exit box, five remain: accept eight marks with three removed or equivalent evidence. Adding to get eleven shows error H; record removal separately from joining. Hear six rotating children, one 45-second observation each, three per ask across the joining ticket and removal Exit box: 4.5 minutes. Public talk may be passed.

Reserve 1.5 minutes for directions/collection and five minutes to scan sixty responses, five seconds each. Children work during observations. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately from mathematical help. Finish written feedback after class: thirty packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return every paper next day in Return and repair for revision before later judgment.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges teaching pictures only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adults cut pieces and steady the paper base at the child's direction. Draw optional number frames by choice.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces replace blocks/counters; hand-draw masters, frames and equations if no printer. Broad crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Revise day 123 feedback on incomplete links.
- **5 min. Action.** Act out supplied four-plus-two enclosure with one actor.
- **4 min. Paper.** [[s07-P124]] Read six pieces, two removed. **Say.** "Show what remains." Expect four uncrossed pieces; finish the record.
- **5 min. Game.** Alternate familiar joining/removal stories.
- **11 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X124]] [[s07-P124-exitbox-removal]] Read the new story. Give blank paper and pieces; your child independently represents the result.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center practice, preserving repairs, game and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial correction with pieces turned; keep reserved cases sealed until evidence collection.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; numeral, day 30. Review story, day 68; plus/minus/equals, day 78. Show the action, then count its result. Read aloud; no independent reading. Large tactile shapes, home language, signs and directed adult marks support access. Record mathematical help separately; public speech may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 125 works with parts of ten. Return day 124's annotated exit/practice in Return and repair: error I, arriving two as whole, five minutes; removal error H, adding instead, five. Centers hear six further day 122 questions. The ticket checks joining; the independent Exit box checks removal. Both return in day 125's repair slot.
