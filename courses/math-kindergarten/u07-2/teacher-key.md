# Teacher-only answer key

Before class only; keep private. Read child print aloud. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record heard words separately from paper; never infer a missing spoken component. Every quantity and scene here is invented for practice.

## s07-F123-repair-whole

Correct the total marked on this eight-piece record.

Answer: 8 marks enclosed together: six initial pieces and two arriving.

Invented for practice. Rin places six squares and adds two. Draw all eight but enclose only the arriving two as total; extend the supplied enclosure to all eight. Record support separately.

Shown: 8 marks enclosed together: six initial pieces and two arriving. | Not yet seen: Keeps only arriving two as total.

## s07-K123-link

Point to the group for the missing numeral.

Answer: Two triangles beside four squares; 6 = 4 + [ ] and 4 + [ ] = 6 each need 2.

Enclose all six; link each group to its own numeral. Complete one equation at a time; equals connects equal quantities in either order. Record access/help separately; shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Shown: Two triangles beside four squares; 6 = 4 + [ ] and 4 + [ ] = 6 each need 2. | Not yet seen: Uses four squares for both parts.

## s07-X123

Join each part of this build to its number in the matching equation.

Answer: Links three squares to 3 and four triangles to 4 in 7 = 3 + 4; 7 names the whole. The other true equations have different parts.

Read the shuffled cards 7 = 2 + 5, 7 = 3 + 4 and 7 = 1 + 6 neutrally. Look for each group linked to its own addend, not selection by total alone. | Shape: Match a complete composition to an equal-total equation and its addends; practice repairs an incomplete equation or missing link.

Shown: Links square group to 3 and triangle group to 4 in 7 = 3 + 4. | Not yet seen: Links both groups to the same addend.

## s07-P124-exitbox-removal

Rin makes a sign with eight pieces. Rin removes three pieces. Show how many pieces remain.

Answer: 5 pieces remain: eight initial marks with three removed, or five remaining marks tied to the removal.

Invented for practice. Reserved independent paper Exit box. Read aloud once; child draws eight and removes three or uses loose pieces and records five. One actor, one result ask. Earlier practice only repairs supplied records; never administer this whole story before the exit.

Shown: Eight marks with three removed and five remaining indicated. | Not yet seen: Adds three to eight and records eleven.

## s07-K125-whole

Show what stays the same when I move this piece.

Answer: Ten altogether: supplied six/four becomes five/five after one piece moves groups.

Show six squares and four triangles in supplied groups. Move one square into the other group; count five and five, then all ten. Groups now mix shapes, retaining ten. Record access/help separately; shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Shown: Ten altogether: supplied six/four becomes five/five after one piece moves groups. | Not yet seen: Leaves out one and calls nine ten.

## s07-P125

Supplied partial practice only. Immediate feedback; record support. At most two optional 45-second public contributions. All children mark paper; no independent reading.

## s07-P125-exitbox

Fill the empty part to make ten.

Answer: 3 new marks, since 7 + 3 = 10.

Independent student-page Exit box, not taught practice. Seven occupied spaces nonconsecutive. Each added mark fills one empty place. | Shape: Complete a novel interrupted ten-space row; practice uses supplied complete diagrams.

Shown: Adds three marks, all ten occupied. | Not yet seen: Adds two and calls nine ten.

## s07-X125a

## s07-X125b

Make a different split here.

Answer: Any pair totaling 10 different from first unordered pair. A swapped pair is not different.

Reserved independent second question on the separate exit master, not teaching practice. Second ten-tab copy. A repeated unordered pair gets a descriptive note, not J unless whole also incorrect. | Shape: Generate a second partition on a fresh copy; never complete a supplied split.

Shown: Seven and three after nine and one. | Not yet seen: Claims eight and one form all ten.
