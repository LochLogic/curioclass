## s07-X124

Rin has five pieces. Rin adds two pieces. Show how many pieces Rin has now.

Answer: 7. Five initial pieces and two arriving pieces make seven.

Invented for practice. One actor, one result ask. Blank paper and loose pieces. Accept counting all. | Shape: Independently solve a whole joining story on blank paper; practice repairs supplied action records.

Shown: Draws seven marks, enclosing initial five and added two. | Not yet seen: Shows only arriving two as the total.
