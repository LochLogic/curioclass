# Day 123: I can match shape pieces to an equation.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md for exits and this teacher page's worked steps privately. Print practice-123 as the student-page picture and exit-123 as the sealed half-sheet ticket, without a second generated ticket. Choose six observation names on record. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Adult-cut exit pieces must fit the outline at the same scale. Inspect models for sharp edges, staples and residue; no glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. No individual evidence from chorus; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 122's annotated exit and partial-story practice. If many treated an arriving part as total, five minutes repair [[s07-F122-whole]] on its supplied four/two drawing, then revise original feedback. Other five minutes complete its partial-question ending or a retained join repair. Unheard questions remain unresolved for correspondence; centers collect them separately.

#### 10 min. Rehearsal

Say today's goal. Review part/whole, day 87; equation/plus/equals, day 78. **Ask.** [[s07-K123-link]] [[steps:s07-K123-link]] Review Equal quantities linked to both equation orders, day 78. Read six equals four plus two, then four plus two equals six. Equal quantities stay equal with the whole on either side. Show correct correspondence before contrasting repeated-square error N and second-part-only error I.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Use [[s07-P123]] on practice-123. Cover the lower equation; fill one missing numeral. Uncover and repeat in the other order. Each child links the missing numeral to its triangle group. Hear two optional 45-second contributions. If many repeat four, cover each group in turn and count its own occupants. Keep puzzles partial; no menu of complete equations until the exit.

#### 15 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Five minutes collect [[s07-X122a]] from the next six unheard children: one 45-second question each, 4.5 minutes plus transition. Show the sign privately for that evidence turn; record exact wording, then compare retained solution paper. Never infer wording from marks. Five minutes finish incomplete equation links/partial fills; five minutes play game and tidy. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously.

Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Show 2, 3, 4, 3 with pieces or marks on game-record. Give immediate feedback; error I counts only arrivals, error H adds during removal. No timer. Collect new paper for next day's Return and repair packet; marked game-record stays in the folder.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-123 only at the child's own turn. Read separately: [[s07-X123]] Everyone marks independently. Read every equation in printed order without emphasis. A link to 7 = 3 + 4 matches three squares/four triangles. Assign error N only if repeated-part method is observed. Hear six rotating children, one 45-second observation each, three per ask on a two-item ticket: 4.5 minutes. Public talk may be passed.

One minute covers directions/collection; 2.5 minutes scan thirty tickets, five seconds each. Children work during observations. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately from mathematical help. Finish written feedback after class: thirty packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return every paper next day in Return and repair for revision before later judgment.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges teaching pictures only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adults cut pieces and steady the paper base at the child's direction. Draw optional number frames by choice.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces replace blocks/counters; hand-draw masters, frames and equations if no printer. Broad crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Revise day 122 feedback on supplied enclosures.
- **5 min. Groups.** Count four squares and two triangles.
- **7 min. Paper.** [[s07-P123]] Cover one equation at a time. **Say.** "Which group needs its numeral?" Expect two triangles; write 2.
- **5 min. Game.** Alternate familiar joining/removal stories.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X123]] Reveal the fresh build; read every equation in printed order. Your child joins independently, without hints.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center practice, preserving repairs, game and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial correction with pieces turned; keep reserved cases sealed until evidence collection.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; triangle, day 45; numeral, day 30. Review equation/plus/equals, day 78; part/whole, day 87. Each addend names its own group. Read aloud; no independent reading. Large tactile shapes, home language, signs and directed adult marks support access. Record mathematical help separately; public speech may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 124 solves joining and separating stories. Return day 123's annotated exit/practice in Return and repair: repeated-group error N, five minutes; second-part-only error I in practice, five. Centers receive six further day 122 questions. A correct link alone proves no unheard explanation.
