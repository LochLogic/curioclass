# Day 125: I can split ten pieces and record the parts.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately for exits and this page for practice keys. Print student page and half ticket. Print both stems and tab panels on the exit-125 half slip. Side 2 holds only the partner strip; keep it covered until its separate round. Choose six observation names. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Inspect models; no sharp edges, staples, glass, metal cans or mouthing. Keep builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104. Two minutes: [[s07-W120-tens]] Say tens first if needed; chorus echoes. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]] Count one through twenty together. No individual evidence from chorus; no written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

The existing two actions are error I for the joining/question-feedback packet, five minutes, and error H for the independent removal Exit box, five minutes. Each includes revision and its fresh partial retry.

Return day 124's written tasks with annotated feedback in this existing slot. Keep first marks beside later revisions; no extra interview or minutes.

Return day 124's tickets and Exit boxes, s07-X124, s07-P124-exitbox-removal, with written feedback for revision. Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 124's annotated exit and removal practice. If many counted only an arriving part, five minutes repair [[s07-K125-repair-join]]. If many added in the removal Exit box or practice, five minutes finish [[s07-P125-repair-removal]] on a supplied eight-piece row with two crossed out; retry by covering removed pieces and counting six remaining. Revise original feedback; no blank whole-story practice.

#### 7 min. Rehearsal

Say today's goal. Review part/whole/split, day 87; partner of ten and Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97. Use supplied eight/two, six/four and five/five groups only. **Ask.** [[s07-K125-whole]] [[steps:s07-K125-whole]] Moving a piece changes its group, not the whole count. Verify parts against all ten. Show correct eight/two before the eight/one error J contrast. No ungrouped whole or blank two-solution record in teaching.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Launch the familiar supplied paper corrections before listening: [[s07-P125]] [[s07-K125-whole]] Children continue these tasks independently; no new blank whole-build or whole-story solution. Demonstrate only the needed direction, then omit public shares. Use position-scenes and position-roster for cells 125 to 152, child first and relation second. One uncued relation per 45-second ask, then its separate target mark; no supplied relation or solid name. Retain genuine earlier matching evidence and skip that cell. The position block uses 12 minutes here and 10 in Counting centers, including launch/reset. Paper alone never proves speech.

#### 16 min. Counting centers

Finish the position block in ten teacher minutes. Then hear children 16 to 21 on their first day 122 question, s07-X122a: six 45-second turns plus thirty seconds transition, five minutes. Show the reserved sign only for that child; record the exact question beside the saved solution. One minute collection/tidy. Other children continue the launched mathematics and five independent picture-feedback revision minutes. Teacher-led follow-up is sequential, never simultaneous with position listening. The optional game is omitted today. The familiar game rows remain available on a later funded day: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]].

#### 10 min. Exit ticket

Reveal both exit-master questions together; collect both in this slot.

Reveal the ticket panel for [[s07-X125a]]. Read [[s07-X125b]] on the exit master, then uncover student Side 2 for practice [[s07-P125-exitbox]]. Read stems exactly, one at a time. Everyone marks independently. Any parts totaling ten are valid, including a clear empty group. The second unordered pair must differ; swapping groups is not different. Strip needs three new marks. Hear six children, two per ask, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; marks never prove unheard explanations.

Reserve 1.5 minutes for separate directions/collection and four minutes to scan thirty three-panel records, eight seconds each. Children work during observations. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately from mathematical help. Finish written feedback after class: thirty packets at fifteen seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return every paper next day in Return and repair for revision before later judgment.

### Other rooms

Paper is the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges teaching pictures only. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adults cut pieces and steady the paper base at the child's direction. Draw optional number frames by choice.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces replace blocks/counters; hand-draw masters, frames and equations if no printer. Broad crayons replace pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Return and repair.** Revise day 124 feedback on supplied records.
- **5 min. Parts.** Count supplied eight/two, six/four and five/five groups.
- **5 min. Paper.** [[s07-P125]] Correct supplied numeral. **Say.** "Show the whole." Expect all ten counted once.
- **5 min. Game.** Alternate familiar joining/removal stories.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X125a]] [[s07-X125b]] [[s07-P125-exitbox]] Reveal panels separately; read one stem at a time. Keep both partitions and strip; record help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trim repeated center practice, preserving repairs, game and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial correction with pieces turned; keep reserved cases sealed until evidence collection.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; triangle, day 45; numeral, day 30; decompose, day 87. Review part/whole/split, day 87; partner of ten, day 97. Keep the whole ten visible. Read aloud; no independent reading. Large tactile shapes, home language, signs and directed adult marks support access. Record mathematical help separately; public speech may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 126 handles and sorts flat shapes and solid objects. Return day 125's half ticket, Exit box and practice in Return and repair; error J, nine called ten, gets five minutes on supplied full-ten records. A repeated unordered pair gets a descriptive note and another partition opportunity; assign J only if the whole is wrong. Withdraw supplied practice frames, retaining choice. Centers hear six further day 122 questions; day 127 hears the last three.
