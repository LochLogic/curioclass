# Day 36: One more and the next number

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fold practice-36.md's 7 column under; supply 8, cover 9, matching the student row. Bring day 35 feedback. Hide heard-36.md, exit-36.md and tower-pieces.md. Keep teacher-key.md and d036-key.html private. Prepare conference-tabs.md, separate screened seven/ten trays. Counting roster: 11 to 15; heard/exit roster: 7 to 12. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review supported oral joining through twenty, day 25: [[s02-R36-oral]], adult leading beyond ten. Review day 20's unchanged total: [[s02-R36]]. One puppet spreads eight squares and calls them ten. Children correct a loose-paper diagram without changing membership. Say all pictures are invented for practice. Read every printed word aloud, 18 point.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Whole class with individual squares. Teach tower: equal squares stacked. Start with eight, join one: **Ask.** [[s02-K36-1]] before revealing the total. [[steps:s02-K36-1]] Repeat one to two. Link pieces, drawings, then numerals. One more means exactly one additional square; next means the following counting word. Children point and use each word.

Review more/fewer, day 21. **Ask.** [[s02-K36-2]] before counting the matched units. [[steps:s02-K36-2]] Greater means more; less means fewer, regardless of numeral print size. Order means arrange by count. Fewest is the smallest count; most is the largest. Read the already ordered 8/9/10 row, pointing from fewest to most. Count equal units rather than judging height alone.

#### 12 min. Connected work

First seven minutes: pairs fill one gap in supplied 1/2, [[s02-P36-2]], then supplied 8/9/10, [[s02-P36-1]]. Read supplied labels aloud; children count before writing the missing numeral. **Ask.** [[s02-P36-3]] before children mark the extra square on loose paper; then align eight and nine. Four concurrent 45-second turns take three minutes; four remain for modeling, paper corrections and feedback. For error H join one and connect the changed total to the next word; for error M reconnect the counted quantity and numeral.

Last five minutes: reveal heard-36.md and read [[s02-O36]]. Everyone marks the relevant feature; hear children 7 to 12, six 45-second asks plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Scribe words. Record names/help using shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Unheard reasons remain not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Hear children 11 to 15 using counting-conference.md: [[s02-M1a-36]], [[s02-M1b-36]], [[s02-M1c-36]], [[s02-M1d-36]], [[s02-M2a-36]]. Spread after the total; indicate opposite start before the fourth ask. Five 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes each, twenty total. Others repeat supplied-row corrections independently. Keep new one-more practice separate from M1/M2/M3. No teacher repair during conferences. After class, five teacher minutes provide five conference feedback notes, returned day 37. Later cohorts remain unassessed.

#### 5 min. Record return and repair

Return day 35 s02-X35, s02-P35, s02-O35, s02-R35, s02-R35-oral, s02-F35, s02-P35-1, s02-P35-2, s02-F35-route, M3, shared star routes and conference feedback. If many day 35 responses show error D use [[s02-F36]]; for errors K/L correct one returned route; for error M connect its counted quantity and numeral. Choose one blocking correction: return 1, model 1, concurrent revision 2, collect 1 minute. Above ten sharing it, repair together; otherwise a group while others revise. Preserve originals/revisions; queue other letters for days 37/38. Close only eligible earlier components after class, ten teacher minutes.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect originals and revisions separately; return pieces concurrently to table trays. After class, thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each give fifteen marking minutes. Return day 37 with feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Give unseen exit-36.md and tower-pieces.md when each child's work starts. Read [[s02-X36]] exactly, one minute. All arrange and outline on paper, two minutes. Observe the sole action for children 7 to 12, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes; collect 0.5. No reason follow-up; O36 supplies separate evidence. Use the same scale and record help. After class, thirty sets at thirty seconds each give fifteen marking minutes. Return in day 37 Mid-unit response; this is formative new-content evidence.

### Other rooms

Draw the two supplied rows and equal-square strips; hand-copy exact reserved pictures without showing them early. Use blank-frames.md for familiar counting support. No device turns. Adult movement follows the child's exact directions.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw towers, rows and cards; use scrap squares and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations and closing the class record.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words; lead beyond ten.
- **5 min. Model.** Show eight equal squares, join one, count nine, show 8 and 9. **Say.** "What changes when this square joins?" Expect one additional square and next number nine. Repeat one to two. Explain fewest to most with the supplied row.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-P36-1]] Fill one gap in the already ordered 8/9/10 row. **Say.** "Why does this tower come next?" Expect nine has one more square than eight.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise one returned day 35 component; put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X36]] on unseen exit-36.md. Your child arranges and outlines alone. Keep the paper and note help; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated gap corrections, retain new teaching, conferences, record revision and exit. Longer: revisit the same ordered displays, explaining adjacent quantities through matching.

### Words and supports

New words are defined and used in the model. Review numeral, zero, none and written numerals 0 to 10, day 30; more/fewer, day 21; count, number word, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. Enlarge or use tactile pictures. A scribe records reasons, never supplies writing-check numerals. Record motor help separately. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 adds one and names the next quantity without recounting. Return day 36 checks, practice and conference feedback in Mid-unit response. For error H join one square to a known set; for error M reconnect quantity and numeral. Use five minutes for one repair, five for fresh transfer. Unheard reasons remain not yet seen.
