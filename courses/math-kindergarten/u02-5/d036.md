# Day 36: One more and the next number

## Teacher page

### Before class

Select practice-36.md through practice-records.md. Hide exit-36.md and tower-pieces.md; keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-5/teacher-key.md and d036-key.html private. Prepare conference-tabs.md, separate seven/ten trays. Bring day 35 records with feedback; never display independent answers. Select counting roster 11 to 15, exit roster 13 to 18. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

[[s02-R33-oral]] Review day 25 supported oral joining through twenty. [[s02-R36]] Review day 20 unchanged totals: eight squares spread remain eight, none entered or left. Children correct a loose-paper diagram. All pictures are invented for practice. Read every printed word aloud, 18 point.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Introduce tower: equal squares stacked in a picture. Build two, then join one. **Ask.** [[s02-K36-1]] before revealing next total. [[steps:s02-K36-1]] Connect concrete pieces, drawn squares, then written numerals. Teach one more and next; repeat seven to eight. Align equal units, count rather than judging height alone. [[s02-K36-2]] Teach greater as more, less as fewer; review those quantity words from day 21. An order from fewest to most starts with the smallest count and ends with the largest. Demonstrate on the adjacent two/three pair, no scrambled stair or 4/5/6 sequence.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs correct one gap in supplied ordered 1/2: [[s02-P36-2]]. Then [[s02-P36-1]], supplied 7/8/9/10 with one missing label. Point to each numeral, read it, verify equal-square quantities; the missing numeral is written only after counting. **Ask.** [[s02-P36-3]] before matching units to reveal the extra. Two 45-second turns each take three minutes concurrently; remaining nine hold modelling, paper corrections and feedback. Error H: physically join one, then connect the changed total to next number. Error M: count, match, cover, write, check. No full scrambled row.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Hear roster 11 to 15 using counting-conference.md: [[s02-M1a-36]], [[s02-M1b-36]], [[s02-M1c-36]], [[s02-M1d-36]], [[s02-M2a-36]]. Spread after total; indicate opposite start before fourth ask. Five separate 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes each, twenty total. Others independently repeat supplied-row corrections; keep these new one-more papers separate from earlier mid-unit evidence. No teacher-led repair during conferences. Five after-class teacher minutes provide feedback, returned day 37. Later counting cohorts remain unassessed until days 37 to 39.

#### 5 min. Record return and repair

Return day 35 s02-X35, s02-O35, s02-R35, s02-F35, s02-P35-1, s02-P35-2 and s02-F35-route, conference feedback and M3 with shared star routes. If many day 35 responses show error D, use [[s02-F36]]; for errors K/L correct one route on the returned sheet; for error M link its counted quantity and numeral. One blocking action fills this slot, whole class above ten sharing a letter, otherwise group. Each child revises one flagged component; queue others for days 37/38 response. Preserve original and revision. Close only completed earlier components after class, ten teacher minutes; no complete thirty-child counting claim today.

#### 2 min. Tidy

After class, mark thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 37 with feedback. Collect original records and revisions separately; return pieces concurrently to table trays. Keep practice for day 37 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

One-minute launch: reveal each child's exit-36.md and scrambled tower-pieces.md with matching numeral labels. Read [[s02-X36]] exactly. Two paper minutes: place towers/labels in order and trace outlines with number marks on the stair mat. Hear roster 13 to 18: [[s02-O36]], six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Half-minute collection. This checks today's new content formatively, separate from M1/M2/M3. After class thirty papers at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 37 with practice. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen, names and help. Paper order cannot prove an unheard one-more explanation.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied rows and exact exits; draw equal-unit tower strips. Use blank-frames.md as a familiar counting support if needed. No device turns.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw towers, rows and cards; use scrap squares and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations and closing the class record.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words.
- **5 min. Model.** Start with two squares, join one, count three, show 2 and 3. **Say.** "What changes?" Expect one more square, next number three.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-P36-1]] Fill the missing 8 between supplied 7 and 9; count each square. **Say.** "Why does eight come next?" Expect one extra square beyond seven.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return one day 35 correction, then put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X36]] on fresh paper. Your child orders and outlines four, five, six with labels alone. [[s02-O36]] **Say.** "What changes at each step?" Expect one more square, next number. Keep paper.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated gap corrections, retain new teaching, conferences, record revision and exit. Longer: revisit the same ordered displays, explaining adjacent quantities through matching.

### Words and supports

New: tower, next, one more, order, fewest, most, greater, less. Review more/fewer, day 21; numeral, zero, none, written numerals 0 to 10, day 30; count, number word, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2. Enlarge/tactile squares; directed adult movement and dictated reasons allowed. Record motor help separately; adult never supplies writing-check numerals. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 adds one and names the next quantity without recounting. Return day 36 exits, practice and counting feedback in Mid-unit response. For error H join one square to a known set; for error M reconnect counted quantity and numeral. Use one five-minute repair then fresh transfer in the ten-minute response, not simultaneous conferences. Continue unfinished earlier evidence; leave unheard reasons not yet seen.
