# Counting conference prompts

Teacher only. Seven tabs on first tray; ten on separate second tray. Five assigned children daily. Five separate prompts, 45 seconds each and 15 seconds notes, four minutes per child. Spread first seven between prompts two and three; indicate opposite start before prompt four. Reset quietly. No coaching or result shown.
