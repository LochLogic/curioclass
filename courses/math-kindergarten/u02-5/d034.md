# Day 34: Number labels tell how many

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-records.md to select practice-34.md. Hide exit-34.md and all numeral-record sheets. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-5/teacher-key.md and d034-key.html private. Prepare conference-tabs.md: seven on one tray, ten on another, screened from centers. Select counting roster 1 to 5 and exit roster 1 to 6. Adult-cut large paper pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

[[s02-R34]] Review supported oral joining through twenty, day 25: adult leads beyond ten. [[s02-R34]] Review day 26's filled 5-frame using blank-frames.md. Children touch once and record a total with dots. All pictures are invented for practice. Read every printed word aloud, 18 point.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 33 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O33. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return s02-P33-1, s02-P33-2, s02-P33-3, s02-P33-4, s02-P33-5 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 33 s02-X33, s02-O33 and all corrected practice with feedback. If many day 33 tickets show error O, connect none to supplied 0 using [[s02-F34]]. For error D use the visible five-label bag in [[s02-P34-1]]; for error M trace its 5 after counting. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten share it, otherwise a group. Retain original and correction; queue other letters.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Show a paper bag labeled 5 containing three squares. **Ask.** [[s02-P34-1]] before demonstrating. [[steps:s02-P34-1]] Trace model 5. Review numeral: the written number label tells a quantity, not an object's name. On a separate empty bag show supplied 1: [[s02-F34]], then its corrected 0. Zero tells none; it does not request one object. Never pair these bags as an independent labeling task.

#### 12 min. Connected work

First three minutes: [[s02-P34]] on its separate Exit box picture. Read the single stem; launch 0.5, paper 2, collect 0.5. Pairs verify the five-label bag, tracing added squares on paper. [[s02-P34-2]] Show and guide each numeral separately, 0 through 10, about thirty seconds each, 5.5 minutes. Trace then copy each on a loose strip, keeping the model visible. These are formation rehearsals, not independent collection labels. [[s02-P34-3]] Cover only model 5 for one recall attempt, then uncover. Spread the completed bag's same five squares, adding or removing none: [[s02-P34-4]]. Review day 31's retained numeral; trace the changed positions on loose paper. Two 45-second partner checking turns take 1.5 minutes concurrently; remaining five minutes cover bag work, handling and feedback. Recognizable reversals get formation feedback; error M requires evidence of a different quantity label.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O34]] from heard-34.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 25 to 30: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Hear children 1 to 5 privately with counting-conference.md, in order: [[s02-M1a-34]], [[s02-M1b-34]], [[s02-M1c-34]], [[s02-M1d-34]], [[s02-M2a-34]]. Spread the first collection after the total; indicate the opposite start before the fourth ask. Five separate 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes each, twenty total. No coaching. Others independently repeat guided bag checks and formation; return practice paper. No bowl, feather, star, ticket or eleven-box practice. Never teach a repair while conferencing. Five teacher minutes after class provide conference feedback; return day 35.

#### 2 min. Tidy

After class, mark thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 35 with feedback. Concurrent table trays collect pieces and formation strips. Retain practice for day 35 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-34.md only now and read [[s02-X34]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 25 to 30, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact pictures and supplied partial bag; folded paper replaces bags. No device needed. Keep exit artwork unseen until the child's turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Fold scrap bags; draw frame and pictures; use paper squares, handwritten cards and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations and conferences.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **5 min. Together.** [[s02-P34-1]] Add two to three for supplied 5. **Say.** "What does this label request?" Expect five squares.
- **7 min. Form.** [[s02-P34-2]] Show one numeral at a time; trace, then copy.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-F34]] Show an empty bag labeled 1. **Say.** "What label tells none?" Expect 0; replace 1. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X34]] on the unseen exit-34.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, retain formation, conferences and exit. Longer: repeat the same supplied-label bag checks, explaining a correction.

### Words and supports

Review: count, number word, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; group, day 6; match, day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; total, day 19; same, day 20; numeral, number label, zero, none and written numerals 0 to 10, day 30. Enlarge pictures; tactile squares and directed adult movement are available. Record motor help separately. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 rehearses counting out and numeral formation, including zero. Return day 34 paper, practice and counting feedback in Return and repair. For error E use a supplied empty-bag correction; for error M reconnect counted five with model 5; for error D retain the requested stop. Choose one action. Independent eleven-box work follows; never rehearse its pictures.
