# Day 34: Number labels tell how many

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-records.md to select practice-34.md. Cover the student-page Exit box; hide exit-34.md, heard-34.md and numeral records until their turns. Keep teacher-key.md and d034-key.html private. Prepare conference-tabs.md: seven and ten on separate screened trays. Counting roster: 1 to 5; heard/exit roster: 25 to 30. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Pick up day 33's intentional empty group. Review supported oral joining through twenty, day 25: [[s02-R34-oral]]. Lead beyond ten. Review the filled 5-frame from day 26: [[s02-R34]], using blank-frames.md. Children touch once and make a Paper count-dot record. Say all pictures are invented for practice. Read every printed word aloud, 18 point.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 33 s02-X33, s02-O33, s02-P33, s02-F33, s02-R33, s02-R33-oral and s02-P33-1, s02-P33-2, s02-P33-3, s02-P33-4 and s02-P33-5 with feedback. If many day 33 tickets show error O, clear a fresh frame and connect none to supplied 0 with [[s02-F34]]. For error D use [[s02-P34-1]]; for error M count then trace its 5. Choose one priority: return 1, model 1, simultaneous fresh correction 2, collect 1 minute. Above ten sharing a letter, repair together; otherwise a group while others revise. Keep originals; queue other letters.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Whole class, then individual strips. A number label is a written mark telling how many. Children say the words while pointing to supplied 5 on the bag. Review numeral and zero/none from day 30. Show three squares in that bag. **Ask.** [[s02-P34-1]] before demonstrating. [[steps:s02-P34-1]] In a separate scene, one puppet labels an empty bag 1: [[s02-F34]]. Reveal 0 after attempts. These two corrections take 2.5 minutes. Spend 5.5 minutes guiding [[s02-P34-2]]: show 0 through 10 separately, thirty seconds each, trace then copy. Give formation feedback without confusing a recognizable reversal with error M.

#### 12 min. Connected work

First three minutes: uncover the Exit box only for [[s02-P34]]. Read its exact stem: directions 0.5, independent paper 2, collect 0.5. Next four: [[s02-P34-3]], cover only model 5 for one isolated recall, then uncover. [[s02-P34-4]], spread the completed bag's same five squares; children trace their positions on loose paper and retain its label. Two concurrent 45-second Partner turns take 1.5 minutes; 2.5 remain for recall, handling and feedback.

Last five minutes: reveal heard-34.md and read [[s02-O34]]. Everyone marks the relevant feature; hear only children 25 to 30, six 45-second asks plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Scribe their words; ask nothing further. Record names and help. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Teach the conference routine: come alone, follow each single prompt, then return to familiar work. Hear children 1 to 5 privately with counting-conference.md: [[s02-M1a-34]], [[s02-M1b-34]], [[s02-M1c-34]], [[s02-M1d-34]], [[s02-M2a-34]]. Spread after the total; indicate the opposite start before the fourth ask. Five 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes each, twenty total. No coaching. Others repeat guided bag checks and formation, returning paper. Review Counting centers and Counted-uncounted boundary. Keep reserved pictures hidden; give no group repair while conferencing.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect pieces and formation strips concurrently at table trays. Keep practice, conference notes and checks separate. After class: fifteen teacher minutes for thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each; five for five conference records at one minute each. Return all on day 35.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Give each child unseen exit-34.md when their work starts; read [[s02-X34]] exactly, one minute. Children label on paper, two minutes. Observe the sole action for children 25 to 30, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes; collect 0.5. No follow-up ask. Use the same scale; record names and help. After class, thirty sets at thirty seconds each give fifteen marking minutes. Return with s02-P34 and heard notes in day 35 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact pictures and supplied partial bag; folded paper replaces bags. No device needed. Keep exit artwork unseen until the child's turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Fold scrap bags; draw the frame and pictures; use paper squares, handwritten cards and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations and conferences.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words, with your help beyond ten.
- **5 min. Together.** [[s02-P34-1]] Draw three squares in a bag labeled 5. **Say.** "Fix the bag for its label." Expect two more squares.
- **7 min. Form.** [[s02-P34-2]] Show one numeral at a time for tracing and copying.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-F34]] Draw an empty bag labeled 1. **Say.** "Fix this empty bag's label." Expect 0. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X34]] on unseen exit-34.md. Your child labels it on paper alone. Keep the work and note help; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, retain formation, conferences and exit. Longer: repeat the same supplied-label bag checks, explaining a correction.

### Words and supports

Review: count, number word, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; group, day 6; match, day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; total, day 19; same, day 20; numeral, zero, none and written numerals 0 to 10, day 30. Enlarge or use tactile pictures. Directed adult movement is available; record motor help separately. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 rehearses counting out and numeral formation, including zero. Return day 34 checks, practice and conference feedback in Return and repair. For error E correct an empty-bag label; for error M reconnect counted five and 5; for error D retain the stop. Choose one repair. Independent eleven-box work follows.
