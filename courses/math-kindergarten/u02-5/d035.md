# Day 35: Rehearse counting and number writing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-records.md and practice-35.md for loose-paper corrections. Cover both numeral-record pictures and the Exit box until collection; hide exit-35.md, ticket-pieces.md and heard-35.md. Keep teacher-key.md and d035-key.html private. Prepare conference-tabs.md, separate screened seven/ten trays. Counting roster: 6 to 10; heard/exit roster: 1 to 6. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review supported oral joining through twenty, day 25: [[s02-R35-oral]], adult leading beyond ten. Retrieve day 34's five-label bag with three squares: [[s02-R35]]. Each child records the completion on loose paper; blank-frames.md is optional. Keep the supplied stop visible. Say all pictures are invented for practice. Read every word aloud at 18 point.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 34 s02-X34, s02-P34, s02-O34, s02-R34, s02-R34-oral, s02-F34 and s02-P34-1, s02-P34-2, s02-P34-3 and s02-P34-4, plus conference feedback. If many day 34 tickets show error E, use [[s02-F35]]; for error M use [[s02-P35-1]]; for error D retain the stop in [[s02-R35]]. Choose one action: return 1, model 1, simultaneous correction 2, collect 1 minute. Above ten sharing a letter, repair together; otherwise a group while others revise. Preserve originals and revisions; queue other letters.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review taught formation and tracking, with immediate teacher feedback. [[s02-P35-2]]: show 0, 5, 8, 10 separately for tracing, three minutes. [[s02-F35-route]]: one puppet omits a square, then separately revisits one, two minutes. Children repair each attempt with one mark per visit. Observe omission for error K or repetition for error L. Two concurrent 45-second Partner turns take 1.5 minutes; 1.5 remain for handling. No speed target or timer.

#### 12 min. Connected work

First three minutes: uncover [[s02-P35]] in the Exit box, read exactly; directions 0.5, independent paper 2, collect 0.5. Next two: pairs correct [[s02-P35-1]] on loose paper. **Ask.** Read its stem before feedback. Count actual squares. Repeat [[s02-R35]]: two 45-second turns, thirty seconds handling.

Minutes six and seven: reveal the numeral records, remove models/cards. Point to boxes 1, 3, 5, 7 and read [[s02-M2p-1]], [[s02-M2p-3]], [[s02-M2p-5]], [[s02-M2p-7]] separately. Point to each box and read [[s02-M3-1]], [[s02-M3-2]], [[s02-M3-3]], [[s02-M3-4]], [[s02-M3-5]], [[s02-M3-6]], [[s02-M3-7]], [[s02-M3-8]], [[s02-M3-9]], [[s02-M3-10]], [[s02-M3-11]]. Point to the shared mark/label reminders; show no answers. Cover until centers.

Last five minutes: reveal heard-35.md; read [[s02-O35]]. Everyone marks; hear children 1 to 6, six 45-second asks plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Scribe words. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names/help. Unheard language stays not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Everyone completes M3 alone for twelve working minutes, four included for star routes. No copying or coaching. Hear children 6 to 10 using counting-conference.md: [[s02-M1a-35]], [[s02-M1b-35]], [[s02-M1c-35]], [[s02-M1d-35]], [[s02-M2a-35]]. Spread after the total; indicate opposite start before the fourth ask. Five 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes per child, twenty total. Interviewees cover their work, then resume; each still has sixteen available paper minutes. Others finish with eight familiar formation minutes; interviewees have four. Keep one shared star record for M2/M3. Never repair during conferences.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect records separately from practice strips; return pieces concurrently to trays. After class: fifteen teacher minutes for thirty practice sets, fifteen for thirty M3 sheets, each at thirty seconds per set; five for five conference records at one minute each. Return all on day 36.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Give unseen exit-35.md and ticket-pieces.md when each child's work starts. Read [[s02-X35]] exactly, one minute. All select and outline on paper, two minutes. Observe the sole action for children 1 to 6, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes; collect 0.5. Use the same scale and record help; ask nothing further. After class: thirty sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen marking minutes. Return with s02-P35 and heard notes in day 36 Record return and repair.

### Other rooms

Copy exact eleven-box diagrams and shared prompts, preserving arrangements. Draw ticket outlines on adult-cut paper. All work has a paper path without devices.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy pictures; fold scrap bags; use paper squares and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words; lead beyond ten.
- **4 min. Rehearse.** Show 0, 5, 8, 10 separately for tracing.
- **5 min. Together.** Draw five squares beside 2. **Say.** "Fix the supplied number." Expect 5. Then show three squares under 5. **Say.** "Finish this five-label bag." Expect two added. Read the record's shared prompts, pointing to each box, before independent work.
- **12 min. Independent record.** Your child marks stars and labels all eleven boxes on the student page alone. Remove numeral models. Keep the work for next-day feedback.
- **1 min. Tidy.** Put practice pieces away.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X35]] on unseen exit-35.md. Your child selects and outlines tickets alone. Keep the work and note help; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, retain independent paper time, conferences and exit. Longer: repeat supplied-label repairs with feedback. Add no new content.

### Words and supports

Review number label, day 34; numeral, zero, none and written numerals 0 to 10, day 30; requested stop, day 32; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; count, number word, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2. Enlarge or use tactile pictures and broad pencils. Record motor help separately; a scribe never supplies a numeral on M3. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 teaches one more and number order. Return day 35 checks, practice, conference feedback and M3 in Record return and repair. For error D repair a partial group under 5; for errors K/L correct one route; for error M reconnect quantity and numeral. Choose one blocking repair; retain original and revision. Close only completed earlier components.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
