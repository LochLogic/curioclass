# Day 35: Rehearse counting and number writing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Select practice-35.md through practice-records.md. Student page embeds both numeral-record pictures with shared prompts printed once. Cover records until centers; hide exit-35.md and ticket-pieces.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-5/teacher-key.md and d035-key.html private. Prepare conference-tabs.md, separate seven/ten trays. Select counting roster 6 to 10, exit roster 7 to 12. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

[[s02-R33-oral]] Review day 25 supported oral joining through twenty. [[s02-R35]] Review day 34's visible five-label bag, three squares already placed; record the correction on loose paper using familiar blank-frames.md if helpful. No independent blank order mat. All pictures are invented for practice. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 34 s02-X34, s02-O34, s02-R34, s02-F34, s02-P34-1, s02-P34-2, s02-P34-3 and s02-P34-4 with conference feedback. If many day 34 tickets show error E, use [[s02-F35]], supplied 1 on an empty bag. For error M use [[s02-P35-1]], linking counted five to 5. For error D retain the visible stop in [[s02-R35]]. Choose one five-minute action, whole class above ten sharing a letter, otherwise a group. Preserve original and correction; queue other letters.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Fluency reviews taught work only. [[s02-P35-2]] Trace 0, 5, 8, 10 separately with immediate formation feedback. [[s02-F35-route]] One puppet omits a square, then separately revisits one. Children mark one visit per square; errors K and L require observed omission or repetition. Use two 45-second partner turns, 1.5 minutes concurrently; remaining time holds demonstration and practice. No speed target or timer.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs correct [[s02-P35-1]] on loose practice paper. **Ask.** Read its single stem before showing feedback. Check against private key; count actual squares rather than copying the wrong supplied 2. Repeat familiar partial bag completion, [[s02-R35]]. Two 45-second turns each, three minutes concurrently. Last two minutes: reveal number-record sides, remove numeral cards and models. Point to each star panel in turn and read [[s02-M2p-1]], [[s02-M2p-3]], [[s02-M2p-5]], [[s02-M2p-7]] separately. Then point and read each box's single label prompt: [[s02-M3-1]], [[s02-M3-2]], [[s02-M3-3]], [[s02-M3-4]], [[s02-M3-5]], [[s02-M3-6]], [[s02-M3-7]], [[s02-M3-8]], [[s02-M3-9]], [[s02-M3-10]], [[s02-M3-11]]. No answers shown.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Children work independently for twelve paper minutes, including four for star routes, on the same eleven boxes. No copying or coaching. Hear roster 6 to 10 with counting-conference.md: [[s02-M1a-35]], [[s02-M1b-35]], [[s02-M1c-35]], [[s02-M1d-35]], [[s02-M2a-35]]. Spread after total; indicate opposite start before fourth ask. Five 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes each, twenty total. Each interviewed child covers their sheet, then resumes, receiving twelve working minutes across the remaining sixteen. Others finish paper then use eight familiar formation/label-check minutes; interviewed children have four. Never double count shared M2/M3 stars or repair while conferencing. Retain sheets for day 36; five after-class teacher minutes provide conference feedback, fifteen mark thirty sheets at thirty seconds each.

#### 2 min. Tidy

After class, mark thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 36 with feedback. Collect independent records separately from practice strips; return pieces concurrently to trays. Keep originals and help notes.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

One-minute launch: each child first sees exit-35.md with ten ticket pieces. Read [[s02-X35]] exactly and read printed request 6. Two paper minutes: move the selected tickets onto mat, outline them and write the number label. Hear roster 7 to 12 with [[s02-O35]], six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Half-minute collection. After class mark thirty slips at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes. Return exits and practice day 36 alongside records. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names/help, leaving unheard stops not yet seen.

### Other rooms

Copy exact eleven-box diagrams and shared prompts, preserving arrangements. Draw ticket outlines on adult-cut paper. All work has a paper path without devices.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy pictures, fold scrap bags, use paper squares and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations; save the independent eleven-box sheet for its own sitting.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Write 0, 5, 8, 10 separately for your child to trace.
- **7 min. Together.** Draw five squares beside 2. **Say.** "What number fits?" Expect 5; replace 2.
- **5 min. Repair.** Draw five squares; skip one while counting. Your child fixes the count. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X35]] on a fresh slip. Your child makes and outlines six tickets, stops, labels 6 alone. **Say.** "Where did you stop?" Expect six. Keep paper for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, retain independent paper time, conferences and exit. Longer: repeat supplied-label repairs with feedback. Add no new content.

### Words and supports

Review day 34's number labels, numeral, zero, none, written numerals 0 to 10; day 32's requested stop; day 25's marking route; day 12's Counted-uncounted boundary; day 1's count, number word, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record; day 2's Return and repair. Enlarge/tactile pictures, broad pencils, directed adult movement; record motor help separately. A scribe never supplies the numeral on M3. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 teaches one more and orders quantities, separate from the mid-unit record. Return day 35 exits, practice, conference feedback and M3 in Record return and repair. For error D use a partial group under supplied 5; for errors K/L correct one marked route; for error M connect a counted group to its numeral. Pick one blocking repair; preserve original and revision. Close completed evidence only.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
