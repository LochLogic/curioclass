# Day 90: I can organize pairs and notice repeats.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare practice-90.md, ordered six-square starters and optional supplied ten-square records. Keep exit-90.md covered and key-teacher.md/generated key private. Bring annotated day 89 packets, older unresolved work, record and feedback. Set squares, mats, optional frames/covers, crayons and game cards. Select roster 19-24. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-K90-count]] then [[steps:s05-K90-count]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: everyone marks together, no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover can separate counted pieces; counting words remain within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 89 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with written picture feedback. If day 89 tickets show error A, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F90-A]] then [[steps:s05-F90-A]], fresh retry included. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group while others correct familiar starters.

Second five: one older unresolved component only, error F [[s05-F90-F]] OR error B [[s05-F90-B]], with the corresponding model/retry. With no unmet need, correct a familiar supplied record. Keep first marks and revision distinct; further needs remain pending.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review day 87 One-up one-down transfer, part, whole and pair. Demonstrate systematic work: move exactly one square, complete the next supplied record, then check all six. **Ask.** [[s05-K90-order]] after the final transfer, before naming the repeat. Then [[steps:s05-K90-order]]. Explain that for parts without category labels, exchanged counts repeat a pair. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; others follow with pieces. Show successive records singly, never an unordered selection list.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P90]] using practice-90.md. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker, exchange roles. Each child finishes the adult-started next step on paper afterward. Circulate for unchanged copies treated as new, error B. If many miss, compare the counts and transfer one loose square before a fresh supplied retry. Never choose matching records from a list in practice. Collect all papers.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: game-cards.md, [[s05-G90-1]], [[s05-G90-2]], [[s05-G90-3]], [[s05-G90-4]]. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check immediately, leave quantity marks. No speed demand.

Optional ten-minute choice: **Ask.** [[s05-C90-ten]] then use [[steps:s05-C90-ten]] privately to prepare successive starters. Others repeat familiar six-square corrections for those ten minutes. Never require finding every pair. Three minutes familiar corrections, two tidy. Children hold paper/pieces; teacher retains records. All independent evidence cases stay covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-90.md only now. Read the exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X90]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 19-24 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. Every child marks their slip; no hints, extra asks or public key. Listen to explanations volunteered with the same response; marks alone do not prove a reason. Record help and heard names; unheard reasons remain pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Systematic pair-finding is instructional work, not something this selection item demonstrates.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied starters and the identical fresh exit; no device needed. One shared screen enlarges teaching only. Every child keeps a paper path.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace counters; draw mats/frames, fold covers, and copy supplied starters and fresh slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and the optional ten-square extension.

- **3 min. Count.** Count six squares together; child marks six dots.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 89 feedback; revise one mark without erasing the original.
- **5 min. Model.** Move one at a time: one/five, two/four, three/three, four/two. Remove part labels.
  **Say.** "Tell why this pair repeats." Expect: four/two uses the same counts as two/four.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P90]] Supply the next first part as two; child completes four and checks all six.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X90]] Read the fresh slip exactly; child marks alone. Keep it for descriptive feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: omit optional extension; keep model, repairs and exit. Longer: continue the supplied ordered six-square record, stopping when counts begin to repeat.

### Words and supports

Review part, whole, split, pair, empty and repeat from day 87. Gesture one transferred square and the unchanged whole. Large tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing and directed adult marks preserve quantities and the ask. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers or child cutting.

### Tomorrow

Day 91 connects the same split to a drawing and a total-first equation. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game packets at 30 seconds each, including annotations. Return all in day 91 Return and repair; error B, rearrangement mistaken for new counts, gets five minutes and a fresh supplied-record retry. Optional extension papers join that packet; any extra annotation time is after class. Other needs remain pending.
