# Day 88: I can count the parts of a design.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print practice-88.md, fresh exit-88.md, game cards and feedback; keep teacher-key.md and generated key private. Prepare six-piece designs, including one enlarged square, and supplied wrong-count records from today's data. Bring day 87 packets. Set paper shapes, mats, optional frames/covers and crayons. Select roster 7-12. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review day 87 count, total and altogether: **Ask.** [[s05-K88-count]] then [[steps:s05-K88-count]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1: a dot records an object, never proves unheard counting. Review optional Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26; place six squares with one in the second row. A folded cover may separate counted/uncounted pieces. All mark together.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 87 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, plus retrieval, with written picture feedback. If day 87 tickets show error B, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F88-B]] then [[steps:s05-F88-B]], including fresh retry. More than ten of thirty means whole class; otherwise small group while others correct familiar starters.

Second five: first unresolved retrieval need, [[s05-F88-quantity]] OR [[s05-F88-action]], with model and fresh paper retry. For no remaining need, use familiar partial-count correction. Preserve initial work; record revision separately. A second diagnostic uses five center minutes, never extra oral turns.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review triangle and square, unit 3 day 45, and part/whole/pair from day 87. **Ask.** [[s05-K88-design]] then [[steps:s05-K88-design]]. Count pieces, not covered space. Show error A separately: **Ask.** [[s05-F88-A]] then [[steps:s05-F88-A]]. Show error F separately: **Ask.** [[s05-F88-F]] then [[steps:s05-F88-F]]. No complete new design is constructed; all models correct supplied records. At most three 45-second contributions; others point or mark simultaneously.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P88]] Use practice-88.md. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: handler counts/checker tracks, two one-minute turns with exchanged roles. Then each child completes their own missing count. Circulate: omission, error A, needs a one-to-one trace; double assignment, error F, needs one destination per piece. If many miss, re-demonstrate the relevant supplied record and retry, keeping the blank seven-slot designs covered. Collect papers.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: read four fixed rounds from game-cards.md, [[s05-G88-1]], [[s05-G88-2]], [[s05-G88-3]], [[s05-G88-4]]. Partners alternate maker/checker, compare collection immediately, leave quantity marks. No new facts or speed demand.

Thirteen minutes: supplied six-piece partial-count corrections, with up to two reserved five-minute actions for unmet day 87 needs, fresh retries included; otherwise repeat supported design checks. Children handle pieces/paper, teacher retains marks. Two minutes tidy. Do not rehearse the exit's independent production format.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-88.md only now. Read its exact one-ask stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X88]]. One minute launch, two simultaneous paper minutes, six roster 7-12 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, half-minute collection. Each child fills their own two design spaces; neutral access includes directed adult marks. No hints, extra questions or displayed key. Record names heard/help; unheard reasons remain pending. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

Draw the same supplied masters and fresh exit if no printer. Paper shapes replace blocks; all work is on paper. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult-cut paper shapes/squares replace blocks/counters. Draw mats/frames, fold covers, and copy supplied pictures and fresh slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated game rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Count six pieces once each; mark six dots.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 87 picture feedback; preserve first marks and revise one.
- **5 min. Model.** Show two triangles/four squares, including one large square. Count each piece once.
  **Say.** "Complete the missing square count." Expect: four; size does not add a piece.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P88]] Point to supplied shapes; child completes the square count. Swap handler/checker roles with the child.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X88]] Read the exact fresh prompt; child marks both spaces alone. Keep for feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated center corrections; keep repair, model and exit. Longer: recount the same supplied design after moving its pieces, without changing their types.

### Words and supports

Review triangle/square and day 87 part, whole, pair, empty. Distinguish type by outline as well as pattern; never color alone. Read every label aloud. Large tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing and directed adult marks are available; public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 89 rehearses changing parts while keeping the whole. Reserve thirty teacher minutes after class: thirty slips and thirty practice/game packets at 30 seconds each, including annotation. Return all in day 89 Return and repair: error A, omission, five minutes; error F, double assignment, five minutes. Each includes fresh supplied-record retry. Additional needs remain pending; no thirty-child oral round.
