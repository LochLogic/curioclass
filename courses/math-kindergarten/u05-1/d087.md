# Day 87: I can split one whole in different ways.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the named masters; keep key-teacher.md and generated key private. Cover exit-87.md until each child's turn. Label two mats; supply practice starters. Set six squares per pair, optional frames/covers, crayons, record and feedback. Select roster 1-6. Send family.md. Adult cuts large pieces; replace damaged or mouthed pieces with drawings. No required fingers, food or hidden children.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Day 86 promised equation retrieval and splitting numbers. Say "Invented for practice." Model [[s05-K87-hook]] with pieces before pictures. **Ask.** [[s05-K87-hook]] Then [[steps:s05-K87-hook]]. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; everyone follows the transfer on a started mat. Recount without adding or losing pieces.

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, units 1/2. Review Paper count-dot record and Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: handle, watch, swap. Model [[steps:s05-K87-count]]. **Ask.** [[s05-K87-count]] All mark paper together. Chorus four through ten, with teacher pointing; no thirty-child round. Review D82 supplied square model and correction: [[s05-K87-D82]] then [[steps:s05-K87-D82]].

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review expression, equation and equals from unit 4 day 78. Read signs aloud. Two separate paper asks on retrieval.md: **Ask.** [[s05-R87-quantity]] then **Ask.** [[s05-R87-action]]. All mark, unscored. Retain quantity and sign needs separately; no oral round. These are reminders, not evidence of new decomposition learning.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

**Ask.** [[s05-K87-four]] Then [[steps:s05-K87-four]]. Review zero, unit 2 day 30; empty means nothing in this part. Contrast the supplied unchanged and changed records: **Ask.** [[s05-F87-B]] then [[steps:s05-F87-B]]. Repeat means the same pair again. Objects first, matching partial drawings next; never two blank split collections.

#### 7 min. Connected work

Pairs use practice-87.md. **Ask.** [[s05-P87]] Two one-minute Partner turns: one finishes the supplied mat, one checks every square, then swap. Each child completes paper independently afterward. Circulate for omission, error A; trace every piece into exactly one part. If many omit, model one unused square joining the unfinished part, then retry the same supported action. Collect all papers.

#### 20 min. Centers and repair

Return sC-P86-count, sC-P86-compare, sC-P86-symbol, sC-X86-count, sC-X86-compare, sC-X86-symbol, sC-X86-move, sC-X86-next, sC-X86-stop, sC-X86-fewer with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Review Counting centers and Return and repair, unit 1. First five: return day 86's annotated practice and assigned transfer, read feedback, revise one mark, preserve original; judge eligible revised components afterward. Next five: quantity diagnostic repair [[s05-F87-quantity]] and its fresh retry. Next five: sign repair [[s05-F87-action]] and retry. More than ten misses means class repair; otherwise small group while others correct familiar supplied records.

Last five: review the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, unit 4 day 71. Model each action before its round using game-cards.md: [[s05-G87-1]], [[s05-G87-2]], [[s05-G87-3]], [[s05-G87-4]]. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check, leave quantity marks in numbered boxes. Immediate feedback; no speed target. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. Tidy within actions; extra needs wait for day 88.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-87.md, the actual half slip; its stem is exactly **Ask.** [[s05-X87]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous marking two, six roster 1-6 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. All thirty mark paper; no hints or extra asks. Do not display the key. Record help and heard names separately; unheard reasons stay pending. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the supplied masters and fresh exit; every task works on paper. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace counters; draw mats/frames and fold covers. Hand-copy the supplied record and fresh slip; no screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and extra repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Count six squares together; child marks six dots.
- **5 min. Model.** Move one square from five to one. Name two and four, still six.
  **Say.** "What stayed the same?" Expect: six altogether.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P87]] Use the supplied starter, child finishes four. Read the two retrieval prompts from retrieval.md if needed.
- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 86 feedback; preserve first marks and revise one.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X87]] Read the fresh slip exactly; child marks alone. Keep it for descriptive feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated center records; preserve repairs and exit. Longer: finish another supplied four-square starter, never a fresh independent split sheet.

### Words and supports

Part, whole, split, decompose, pair, empty, repeat: gesture while moving pieces. Review count, total, altogether and zero where used. Read every word aloud at 18 point. Large outlines, tactile mats, signing, pointing or adult marks exactly as directed preserve the goal; public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 88 counts shape pieces. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips at 30 seconds and thirty practice packets at 30 seconds, including annotations. Return all on day 88, Return and repair. Error B, unchanged counts treated as new, receives five minutes; first unresolved retrieval need receives five, second uses five within centers. Paper feedback reaches all thirty without another oral round.
