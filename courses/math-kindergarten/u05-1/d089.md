# Day 89: I can change parts and keep the whole.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare practice-89.md, separated six-piece starters, game cards, optional frames/covers and feedback. Keep fresh exit-89.md covered; keep key-teacher.md and generated key private. Bring annotated day 88 papers and record. Select roster 13-18. Paper strips replace cubes; squares remain loose during practice. Adult cuts large pieces; replace damaged/mouthed pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether from days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-K89-count]] then [[steps:s05-K89-count]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: all children mark quantities together; no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover can separate counted pieces. Sequence review stays within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 88 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with written picture feedback. If day 88 tickets show error A, use first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F89-A]] then [[steps:s05-F89-A]], fresh retry included. Error F uses second five: **Ask.** [[s05-F89-F]] then [[steps:s05-F89-F]]. More than ten of thirty with a letter means class repair; otherwise small group while others correct familiar records. Keep original marks and revision separately. Additional needs stay pending.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review day 87 One-up one-down transfer and Two labeled part mats: start with the already separated six-square groups. **Ask.** [[s05-K89-change]] after moving one, then [[steps:s05-K89-change]]. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; everyone traces the moved square on a supplied record. Both parts together keep all six. Rehearse only: no new method, no unbroken train to snap, no independent boundary choice.

#### 9 min. Connected rehearsal

**Ask.** [[s05-P89]] on practice-89.md. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker, swapping roles. Use loose pieces to verify the already separated record; each child completes their own missing part marks. Circulate for omitted pieces, error A, or double assignment, error F. If many miss, trace each square into exactly one existing group and retry the supplied record. Collect all papers; explaining every child's work aloud would not fit.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes use game-cards.md: [[s05-G89-1]], [[s05-G89-2]], [[s05-G89-3]], [[s05-G89-4]]. Partners alternate maker/checker, immediately compare action and collection, leave quantity marks. No new facts or speed demand.

Thirteen minutes correct familiar supplied six-piece partial records; use at most two five-minute actions for unresolved omission/double-assignment needs, each with its fresh retry. Others repeat supported records. Two minutes tidy. Children hold pieces and paper; teacher retains marks. Keep all reserved independent cards covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-89.md, the fresh half slip. Read its stem exactly: **Ask.** [[s05-X89]]. One minute launch/read, two simultaneous paper minutes, six roster 13-18 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, half-minute collection. Each child marks their own boundary. Offer the matching nine-cell paper strip for access, without supplying a split. No hints, new questions or displayed key. Record names heard/help; unheard part descriptions stay pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. This exit samples partitioning; continued-change evidence remains for the later portfolio.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters and fresh slip; paper replaces cubes and counters. One shared screen can enlarge rehearsal only. No device is required.

### At home

**Swap.** Loose paper squares replace cubes/counters. Draw mats/frames, fold covers, and copy supplied records and the fresh strip slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated game rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Count six squares together; child leaves six dots.
- **5 min. Return.** Read day 88 feedback and revise one mark; preserve the first version.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Start with one/five, move one into the first part.
  **Say.** "Why did the other part shrink?" Expect: one moved from it; still six altogether.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P89]] Use the already separated starter. Child completes four in the second part; take turns checking with pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X89]] Read the fresh slip exactly. Child marks alone; retain it for descriptive feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated center corrections; preserve repairs and exit. Longer: rehearse the same transfer backward and check the fixed total with pieces.

### Words and supports

Review part, whole, split, pair and empty from day 87. Point to the source and destination of the moved square. Large tactile outlines, nonslip mats, pointing, signs and adult marks exactly as directed preserve the task. Public speech may be passed. No fine cutting or required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 90 organizes pairs and considers repeats. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game packets at 30 seconds each, including annotations. Day 90 Return and repair returns all; error A, a cell omitted from the two parts, receives five minutes with a supplied six-piece retry. Second five serves one older unresolved component. Do not infer an omission letter from an unexplained numeral alone.
