# Day 71: I can show a story with objects.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare student pages from practice-71.md, familiar joining/removal game pictures, supplied seven-square/counter correspondence and partial-cue repair records, large circles/squares, frames, thirty covered exit-71.md half slips and record.md. Have day 70 feedback ready. Adult cuts large intact pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Use drawn marks if unsafe; never move children as story objects.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count

Review count, day 1; total, day 19; keep track, day 12. **Ask.** [[s04-K68-count]] Count together, then continue five to ten. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1. No individual round or required counting on to solve.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 70's exit and recorded practice feedback. If day 70's tickets showed only arrivals retained, error B, **Ask.** [[s04-F71-B]] Then [[steps:s04-F71-B]]. Five guided minutes, five fresh supplied-cue retry. If many, more than ten, share it, repair together; otherwise one teacher group while peers correct familiar supplied records. Unheard retelling stays pending. No puppet replay or additional group.

#### 10 min. Objects stand for story things

Say "Invented for practice." Review story/take away/subtract/remain, day 68, and retell/first, day 70. Rin alone moves objects. Hook: show a square drawing beside a circular counter. **Ask.** [[s04-K71-counter]] Then [[steps:s04-K71-counter]]. Each counter stands for one story object; its shape need not match. Review Original whole with separate moved region, day 68. Review 10-frame/five-and-some, day 26: lay seven counters as five then two beside the picture before moving three. Four remain; count all as needed.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1: fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; repeat with resets. Adult narrates events separately; children follow each or repair a partial movement. **Ask.** [[s04-P71]] Then [[steps:s04-P71]]. The supplied seven-square record has only two of the three removal marks. Restore the third; ring four remaining. Children retain their corrected paper. Hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds inside the block. If the moved three are selected as remaining, error E, point to the mat location and count its four. Do not independently retell while moving in practice.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. First five minutes begin a mixed untimed game within five, using only previously taught one-and-three joining and five-take-two removal. **Ask.** [[s04-P71-game]] Then [[steps:s04-P71-game]]. Supply three dots below the joining card before play. Partners give immediate feedback by matching pieces to marks; teacher observes, no second group. Next five minutes repeat the supplied seven-square partial correction. Corrected unscored paper stays in the folder; recorded judgments return day 72. Last two minutes tidy and count pieces in.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-71.md at each child's own turn. Read today's exact stimulus from teacher-key.md, then **Ask.** [[s04-X71]] Read exact stem. Each child independently moves counters across the printed regions while telling back, then retains traces on the half slip. Hear roster 13 to 18 only, six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent marking and collection get 3.5. No public answers, practice frame or visible key.

Today's same turn may establish aligned fresh components pending from day 67 to 70. No additional ask or repeated check; earlier unheard speech is never inferred from paper.

After class, thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher marking minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; log heard names, access and mathematical help separately. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 72 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters. Large paper circles/squares replace counters, folded paper replaces mats, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Offer tactile boundaries, broad pencils, usual communication aids and child-directed adult movement/scribing, recording mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large adult-cut circles/squares, folded-paper mats and drawn frames. Hand-copy original pictures.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count together to ten, then five to ten.
- **5 min. Return.** Use day 70 feedback with one supplied event correction.
- **7 min. Model.** [[s04-K71-counter]] Match each circle to one of seven story squares. Narrate each event separately; move three, four remain.
  **Say.** "What does this counter stand for?" Expect: one story square.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s04-P71]] Restore the missing third removal mark. Play one familiar joining/removal game round if time allows.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X71]] First read the exact new stimulus from the private key, then stem. Child acts, retells and traces alone. Keep paper/words; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated seven-square corrections; preserve correspondence teaching, repair, five-minute game and exit. Longer: explain the same model using a different large counter shape, one per story object.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; counter, day 3; story, day 68; retell/first, day 70. A counter may stand for something differently shaped. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Public talk may be passed. Access aids preserve child-selected locations and actions.

### Tomorrow

Day 72, section 4.3, returns this exit, then solves joining/separating stories with objects or drawings. Adding removed pieces, error C: move them off along supplied square paths. Reporting the moved part as outside, error E: identify the question location. Choose one ten-minute priority repair with fresh square records, never the truck scene.
