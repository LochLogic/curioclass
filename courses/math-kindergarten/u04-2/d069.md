# Day 69: I can tell what happens.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare student pages from practice-69.md, supplied five-square/seven-dot and four-square removal records, large squares, frames, thirty covered exit-69.md half slips and record.md. Have day 68 feedback ready. Adult cuts large intact pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Drawn marks replace unsafe pieces; never move children as story objects.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1; total, day 19; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. **Ask.** [[s04-K69-count]] Count together, then continue four to ten. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1. No individual round or required counting on to solve.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 68's exit and recorded practice feedback. Start on student side 1. If day 68's tickets showed adding the removed part, error C, or marking the moved part as remaining, error E, choose one priority. **Ask.** [[s04-F69-CE]] Then [[steps:s04-F69-CE]]. First five minutes include hand-back and guided correction; next five, a fresh supplied-record retry. If many, more than ten, share it, repair together; otherwise one teacher group while peers correct supplied records. Keep the bean check out of practice.

#### 10 min. Complete a story action

Say "Invented for practice." Review story, take away/subtract/remain, day 68, and put together/add/altogether, day 64. Turn to side 2. Rin alone moves objects. Say: "Rin has five squares. Rin puts two in a tray." Follow supplied outward paths; three remain on the mat. Review Original whole with separate moved region, day 68; retain both locations.

Apply the same action-to-words connection to joining. **Ask.** [[s04-P69]] Then [[steps:s04-P69]]. Adult starts: "Rin has one square. Rin brings three squares to the mat." Stop before moving; children complete this missing action. It ends with four on the mat. No complete stories from silent panels in practice.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1: fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap, with resets. One child supplies only the missing action words and completes the adult-started move; the other follows the supplied record, then exchange roles. Repeat joining and the already-moved separating correction. Each child keeps a corrected paper trace. Hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds inside this block. If arrivals alone are kept, error B, retain the starting square and enclose all four before recounting. Review 10-frame and Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, day 26, beside familiar seven squares, five then two.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. Repeat these supplied square corrections for ten minutes, alternating joining and separating. Teacher checks action direction and the requested location, giving immediate model feedback. Fingers, quiet sounds, imagined actions or pointing may accompany the same visible action; none is required. Corrected unscored paper stays in the folder; recorded judgments return day 70. Tidy/count pieces in for the last two minutes.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-69.md at each child's own turn. **Ask.** [[s04-X69]] Say "Invented for practice" and name Rin as the actor without supplying events. Read exact stem without narrating the pictures. Look for the starting group, arriving group and joining action; listen to the named six for their story words. Everyone makes independent paper action marks; then simultaneous partners enact and tell. Hear roster 1 to 6 only, one 45-second turn each, 4.5 minutes. Reading, concurrent marking and collection have 3.5 minutes. No chorus, supplied sentence frame or visible key.

Record an earlier missing component only if today supplies aligned fresh evidence. Keep earlier unheard speech pending with its next-needed component on record.md; paper marks cannot establish it. No additional ask today.

After class, mark thirty paper sets, exit and any recorded practice, at twenty seconds each: ten teacher minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard names, action, access and mathematical help separately. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 70 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters. Large paper squares replace counters, folded paper replaces mats, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Offer tactile boundaries, broad pencils, usual communication aids and child-directed adult movement/scribing.

### At home

**Swap.** Use adult-cut large paper squares, drawn frames and folded-paper mats. Hand-copy the original pictures.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count together to ten, then four to ten.
- **5 min. Return.** Use day 68 feedback for one supplied-square correction.
- **7 min. Action.** [[s04-P69]] Read the adult-started one-and-three story; your child finishes its move.
  **Say.** "Finish this missing joining action." Expect: all three join the starting one, four altogether.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s04-F69-CE]] Correct the supplied removal record; keep the paper.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X69]] First show the boat pictures now; read exact stem. Child marks, tells and acts without a supplied story. Keep paper/words; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated pair actions; preserve both action types, repair and exit. Longer: explain the direction of each supplied square move while the partner points.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; story, take away/subtract/remain, day 68: move some out and count what stays in the place named. Review add and altogether, day 64: keep both groups and count all. Children may use their own action words. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Public talk may be passed; provide usual communication aids without supplying the exit story.

### Tomorrow

Day 70 returns this exit, then models hearing and retaining a story before solving. Only arrivals retained, error B: restore the first group in a supplied square record. Use one ten-minute repair; never practice the boat scene again.
