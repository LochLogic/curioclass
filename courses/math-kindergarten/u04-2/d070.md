# Day 70: I can tell a story back.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare student pages from practice-70.md, a supplied two-and-two event cue, two-and-three joining repair records, large squares, frames, thirty covered exit-70.md half slips and record.md. Have day 69 feedback ready. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Use drawn marks if unsafe; move objects, never children.

### The day

The opening is Oral count, Return and repair and the next teaching part: 5 + 10 + 10 = 25 minutes.

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review count, day 1; total, day 19; keep track, day 12. **Ask.** [[s04-K70-count]] Count together, then continue three to ten. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1. No individual round or required counting on to solve.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 69's exit and recorded practice feedback. If day 69's tickets showed only arrivals as the whole, error B, **Ask.** [[s04-F70-B]] Then [[steps:s04-F70-B]]. Five guided minutes, five fresh supplied-record retry. If many, more than ten, share it, repair together; otherwise one teacher group while peers correct familiar supplied records. No boat replay or additional letter group.

#### 10 min. Hear and retain

Say "Invented for practice." Review story, day 68. Display the supplied two-and-two square event cue. Hook: tell its story before moving anything. **Ask.** [[s04-K70-who]] Then [[steps:s04-K70-who]]. **Ask.** [[s04-K70-first]] Then [[steps:s04-K70-first]]. Everyone points to the initial panel; hear one volunteer, at most 45 seconds.

**Ask.** [[s04-K70-retell]] Then [[steps:s04-K70-retell]]. Teach retell by completing an adult-started account, then jointly act and count. First retain what happened, then solve. Do not assume listening instruction from another subject. No complete unseen-story retelling in practice.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1: fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap. Adult supplies the starting clause each time. Partners finish only its missing event using the arrow cue. **Ask.** [[s04-W70]] Then [[steps:s04-W70]]. Two initial squares are printed; children restore two incoming marks in the blank event panel, then join pieces and retain four in all. Each child keeps the completed paper cue. If only arrivals are counted, error B, retain the starting two before recounting. Hear one more volunteer, at most 45 seconds inside this block. Review 10-frame/five-and-some, day 26, beside familiar seven squares, five then two.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. Ten minutes repeat the supplied missing-event cue and familiar joining-record correction. Teacher gives immediate feedback on the initial event and action direction. Never give a complete unseen retelling task, puppet scene or blank whole-story solution as practice. Corrected unscored paper stays in the folder; recorded judgments return day 71. Last two minutes tidy and count pieces in.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-70.md at each child's own turn. Read only today's exact stimulus from teacher-key.md, then **Ask.** [[s04-X70]] Read exact stem. Judge retained events and question here. Earlier supplied event cues are guided practice; independent object-action evidence comes from later reserved story tasks. Everyone draws sequence reminders. Hear roster 7 to 12 only, six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes, before any solving. Others quietly tell back to a partner after making their own marks. Reading, concurrent marking and collection get 3.5 minutes. Retelling includes the story's question; do not ask a second exit question or require its answer.

Return pending feedback for s04-X66, s04-X67, s04-X68, s04-X69 in today's same turn; it may supply aligned fresh evidence for pending day 66, day 67, day 68 and day 69 components. No extra ask or repeated check. Unheard speech stays pending; paper alone does not establish retelling.

After class, thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher marking minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard names, access and mathematical help separately. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 71 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters. Paper squares replace counters, folded paper replaces mats, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Offer raised boundaries, broad pencils, usual communication aids and child-directed adult scribing without supplying the missing event.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large adult-cut squares and folded paper. Hand-copy the original event cue and exit picture.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count together to ten, then three to ten.
- **5 min. Return.** Give day 69 feedback using a supplied square record.
- **7 min. Hear.** [[s04-K70-retell]] Read the two-and-two square story; keep its event cues visible. Begin its retelling and pause.
  **Say.** "Finish my retelling." Expect: two more join the starting two. Together act and count four afterward.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s04-W70]] Restore the two missing incoming marks; keep the cue.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X70]] First read the exact new stimulus from the private key, then the stem. Child records and retells before solving. Keep paper/words; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer cue repetitions; preserve explicit listening model, repair and exit. Longer: complete the same partial retelling with gestures in place of speech.

### Words and supports

Teach retell and first in the modeled sequence. Review square, day 45, and story, day 68. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Public talk may be passed. Use usual communication aids; remove practice language frames for the independent exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 71 returns this exit, then explicitly links one counter to one story object. If the initial group was lost, error B, restore it in a supplied square cue before counting. Keep unheard retellings pending; one ten-minute repair uses fresh square records, never the puppet scene.
