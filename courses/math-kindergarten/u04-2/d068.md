# Day 68: I can show taking away.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare student pages, supplied one-and-three/two-and-two repair records, five-square outward paths, frames, thirty covered exit-68.md half slips, large paper bean cutouts and record.md. Have day 67 feedback ready. Adult cuts large intact pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks; never move children as story objects.

### The day

The opening is Oral count, Return and repair and the next teaching part: 5 + 10 + 10 = 25 minutes.

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review count, day 1; total, day 19; keep track, day 12. **Ask.** [[s04-K68-count]] Review Paper count-dot record, day 1, with familiar squares. No individual counting round.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 67's exit and practice feedback. If day 67's tickets showed only incoming sounds represented, error B, **Ask.** [[s04-F68-B]] Then [[steps:s04-F68-B]]. Choose error F or G instead only from recorded practice evidence. Five guided minutes, five fresh supplied-record retry. If many, more than ten, share one error, repair as a class; otherwise one teacher group while peers correct supplied records. No replay of sounds or additional groups.

#### 10 min. Taking and partitioning

Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone moves objects. Review put together/add/altogether, day 64, by retaining both groups; today reverse the joining action. Hook: cover two of the five square outlines, then uncover the moved region. **Ask.** [[s04-K68-take]] Then [[steps:s04-K68-take]]. Contrast a whole partitioned into visible parts with the requested mat remainder. **Ask.** [[s04-K68-all]] Then [[steps:s04-K68-all]]. No operation signs.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; repeat with reset layouts. **Ask.** [[s04-P68]] Then [[steps:s04-P68]]. Erase the supplied ring around moved squares and ring only the mat group, error E contrast. Everyone retains a corrected paper trace. Hear one volunteer, 45 seconds within this block. Imagine the removal, then point to its visible model; quiet claps or fingers are optional. Review 10-frame/five-and-some, day 26, beside familiar seven squares, five then two; counting all is valid.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. Ten minutes repeat supplied partition paths and already-moved square corrections. Teacher watches the requested location and gives immediate feedback. No independent new-pile removal in practice. Corrected unscored paper stays in the folder; any recorded judgment returns day 69. Last two minutes tidy and count pieces in.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-68.md at each child's own turn. **Ask.** [[s04-X68]] Read exact stem, once. Children use ten large paper bean cutouts over the picture, move three off and retain their own traces/rings on the half slip. The moved three and remaining seven show two parts of the original ten. Mark both regions as the record of this one action, with no extra spoken question. Hear roster 25 to 30 only, six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; 3.5 for reading, concurrent marking and collection. No public answers or visible keys.

Return pending feedback for s04-X64, s04-X65, s04-X66, s04-X67 during this same roster-six turn only when today's action supplies aligned fresh evidence. No extra ask or repeated check; unheard speech stays pending.

After class, thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher marking minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; log heard names and mathematical help separately from access. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 69 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters. Paper squares/bean outlines replace counters, folded paper replaces mats, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Child-directed adult movement/scribing and tactile boundaries preserve the child's decisions.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult-cut large paper squares/beans replace counters; draw mats and supplied records.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count together one to ten, then five to ten.
- **5 min. Return.** Give day 67 feedback using the one selected supplied-record correction.
- **7 min. Model.** [[s04-K68-take]] Move two of five along drawn paths; distinguish the mat from the moved region.
  **Say.** "Where are the pieces that left?" Expect: two beside the mat; three remain.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s04-P68]] Correct the supplied ring with your child; keep paper.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X68]] Read exact stem over the fresh half slip. Child moves paper beans and marks alone. Give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer partner repeats, preserving repair, partition/removal contrast and exit. Longer: explain the same corrected picture by pointing to whole, moved part and remainder.

### Words and supports

Teach take away, subtract, remain, taking apart/from and story with the actions above. Review square, day 45, and zero/none, day 30. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point; independent reading is not required. Public talk may be passed; offer usual communication aids and objects instead of fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 69 returns this exit before completing joining/separating story actions. Adding removed pieces, error C: move them off along supplied square paths. Ringing moved pieces as remaining, error E: identify the question's mat. Choose one priority repair in ten minutes, using fresh square records, never the bean check again.
