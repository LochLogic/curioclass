# Day 64: I can join groups.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare guided square paths, student pages from retrieval.md/practice-64.md, thirty exit-64.md half slips and matching large paper cork pieces. Cover exits until each turn. Set family.md, record.md, numeral cards 7/8 and day 63's unit 3 feedback/closed records. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing; draw marks if unsafe. Send family.md today.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Day 63 promised joining groups and unit 3 response. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone moves objects. Pose our unit question: [[s04-K64-question]] One volunteer, 45 seconds; no answer about taking away required. **Ask.** [[s04-K64-hook]] Then [[steps:s04-K64-hook]]. Everyone follows supplied paths, never independent blank-mat combining.

#### 5 min. Oral count

Review count, day 1; keep track, day 12. Count together one to ten, then five to ten with simultaneous gestures. Starting mid-sequence rehearses words, not a required addition strategy. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: one dot per touched square. No individual round.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review 10-frame, day 26; numeral, day 30; total, day 19. Three minutes: **Ask.** [[s04-R64-frame]] Match numeral 8 afterward, no numeral writing. Two minutes: **Ask.** [[s04-R64-spread]] Set seven squares; record before/after spreading without adding or losing any. Unscored; note needs, demonstrate afresh where needed.

#### 7 min. Model

Review group and mark, day 6; counter, day 3; square, day 45. **Ask.** [[s04-K64-model]] Then [[steps:s04-K64-model]]. Both groups remain visible. Review Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12, and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, day 26. Counting all remains valid; no operation signs. Hear one volunteer, 45 seconds within this block.

#### 5 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously, 45 seconds each then swap; reset and repeat. **Ask.** [[s04-P64]] Link dots to squares on the supplied five-square/four-dot record; restore its omitted fifth dot. Each child keeps corrected paper unscored in the folder. Check: each square has one dot.

#### 22 min. Centers and response

Review Counting centers, day 1, and Return and repair, day 2. First ten minutes honor day 63: return day 62 s03-X62 and closed F1 to F6 records with written feedback. Five minutes: **Ask.** [[s04-P64-shape]] Turn over a tracing, track sides/corners, then use a fresh turned square in the next five minutes; hear roster 19 to 24, six times 45 seconds plus thirty seconds transition. Others repeat supplied square records. First-made day 63 work gets revision before judgment; missing evidence stays pending. Queue unresolved needs for day 65, no extra teacher group.

Next five minutes, if retrieval needs it, work with at most five on one-per-space or moving each square across a boundary; others correct records. Then five independent practice minutes; last two tidy. Unit 2 retry is tomorrow.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-64.md at each child's own turn. **Ask.** [[s04-X64]] Read exact stem; four striped/two plain paper corks, no guided paths. Each child moves pieces and leaves traces/dots on the half slip. Hear roster 1 to 6 only, one 45-second turn each: 4.5 minutes; 3.5 cover reading, concurrent marking and collection. Quiet work while waiting; no public totals or printed keys.

Mark thirty papers afterward, twenty seconds each = ten teacher minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names heard and help, leave unheard speech pending. Return feedback day 65 Return and repair. Later roster turns may establish aligned missing components with fresh cases, never earlier unheard speech.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy originals; large paper squares replace counters, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Child directs adult movement/scribing. Never remove children.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares and drawn frames replace counters and printing. Adult cuts.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers; use your child's earlier feedback.

- **5 min. Count.** Touch five squares once; count together to ten.
- **7 min. Join.** Follow drawn paths with two then three squares into one outlined mat.
  **Say.** "Which squares belong in our whole?" Expect: both groups, five.
- **5 min. Frame.** [[s04-R64-frame]] Place eight squares, one per space; show numeral 8 afterward.
- **5 min. Correct.** [[s04-P64]] Restore the fifth dot. Apply earlier shape feedback if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X64]] First reveal reserved paper corks. Read the stem; child moves and marks alone. Keep marks and note words. Return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; preserve response, models and exit. Longer: explain the corrected record by pointing to each match, within ten.

### Words and supports

Teach put together, add and altogether through actions. Review total: how many in the whole. Review side/corner, day 48. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Broad pencils, tactile boundaries, usual communication aids; separate access from math help. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 65 returns day 64's exit: excluding the first group suggests error B only with observed action. Enclose both groups using fresh squares. Retry retrieval unscored in Oral count; preserve ten Centers minutes for unit 3. Then combine pictures to find the total.
