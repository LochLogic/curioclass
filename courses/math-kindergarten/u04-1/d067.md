# Day 67: I can show joining.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare student pages from practice-67.md, supplied sound-dot/whole records, large squares/disks, frames, thirty covered exit-67.md half slips and record.md. Quiet mouth sounds replace a chime; offer visible beats or tactile taps. Have day 66 feedback ready. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing; replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count

Review count, day 1; total, day 19; keep track, day 12. Count one to ten together, then five to ten with simultaneous gestures. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1, using familiar squares. No individual round or required counting on to solve addition.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 66's exit with feedback. If day 66's tickets showed a visibly skipped boot, error A, **Ask.** [[s04-F67-A]] Then [[steps:s04-F67-A]]. Five minutes guided movement, five fresh supplied-route retry. If many, more than ten, share it, repair together; otherwise one teacher group while others correct familiar partial records. No boot replay, extra letter group or interview. Missing observations stay pending.

#### 10 min. Actions and zero/one

Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone moves objects. Review put together/add/altogether, day 64. **Ask.** [[s04-K67-action]] Then [[steps:s04-K67-action]]. Next **Ask.** [[s04-K67-sound]] Then [[steps:s04-K67-sound]]. Supply dots before claps; never practice independent sound reconstruction.

Review zero and none, day 30; one more, day 36. Explicitly teach the arrival effects: **Ask.** [[s04-P67]] Then [[steps:s04-P67]]. **Ask.** [[s04-P67-one]] Then [[steps:s04-P67-one]]. Four with no arrival stays four; four with one arrival becomes five. Teach correct actions before wrong/right contrasts, errors F/G. No signs or required fingers.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; reset/repeat. Jointly replay visible square actions and correct supplied zero/one records, retaining paper. **Ask.** [[s04-F67-B]] Then [[steps:s04-F67-B]]. Three arrivals alone omit the starting one; four includes both groups, error B contrast. One public volunteer, 45 seconds within this block; no children moved as story objects. For larger familiar totals, review 10-frame/five-and-some, day 26, beside three-and-four squares, seven. Counting all is valid.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. Ten minutes repeat these supplied corrections and joint actions, one dot per visible square. Teacher checks matches and gives immediate feedback. No independent blank-paper sound models in practice. Each child keeps corrected unscored paper in the folder. Last two minutes tidy and count pieces back in.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-67.md at each child's own turn. Make two quiet chime sounds, pause, then six; repeat identically if needed. **Ask.** [[s04-X67]] Read exact stem. Everyone represents both bursts with disks or fingers and retains dots on the half slip. Visible beats/tactile taps replace hearing as needed; record access, not a different quantity. Hear roster 19 to 24 only, six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent paper marking and collection get 3.5. Quiet waiting, no public answers or printed keys.

Return pending feedback from day 64 s04-X64 and day 65 s04-X65 to today's roster six. This same turn may establish fresh aligned whole-count evidence; earlier unobserved actions stay pending. No extra ask or repeated check card.

After class, thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher minutes to mark. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard names/help. Unheard speech stays pending. Today's fresh aligned evidence can establish an earlier missing component, never earlier unheard speech. Return exit and any recorded practice feedback day 68 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters; paper disks/squares replace counters/fingers, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Broad pencils, tactile boundaries, usual communication aids and child-directed adult movement/scribing; never remove children.

### At home

**Swap.** Cut large paper squares/disks; draw supplied records. Quiet mouth sounds, visible beats or tactile taps replace a chime.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **5 min. Count.** Count together to ten.
- **7 min. Return.** Give day 66 feedback; follow a supplied square route once per square.
- **10 min. Actions.** [[s04-P67]] [[s04-P67-one]] Keep four squares; move nothing from an empty mat, then start again and move one onto four. Correct supplied records.
  **Say.** "Correct this supplied arrival record." Expect: four after none join; five after one joins four.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X67]] First make two sounds, pause, six. Read exact stem; child acts and dots alone. Keep paper/words; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer pair repetitions; preserve repair, explicit zero/one teaching and exit. Longer: explain what changed in the same supplied arrival records.

### Words and supports

Teach adding to: more joining a group already there. Review square, day 45. Review zero/none and one more with actions. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading. Public talk may be passed. Separate communication access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 68, section 4.2, returns day 67's exit before taking apart/from. Only incoming sounds represented suggests error B; enclose both groups and count all. A recorded extra dot for none, error F, or unchanged four after one joins, error G, may take priority instead. One ten-minute repair, fresh squares only.
