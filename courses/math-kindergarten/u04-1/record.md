# Unit 4 record

Teacher only. One scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Leave an unobserved component blank and write pending in its follow-up cell. Pending is scheduling, not a fourth score. A numeral or selection alone does not establish an unheard explanation. Record communication/motor access separately from mathematical help. Keep original paper, written feedback and revision.

Everyone marks every exit on paper. Hear six names per teaching day, one ask at 45 seconds each: 4.5 of eight minutes, with 3.5 for reading, marking and collection. Roster 1 to 6: days 64/69/74/79; 7 to 12: 65/70/75/80; 13 to 18: 66/71/76/81; 19 to 24: 67/72/77/82; 25 to 30: 68/73/78. Later fresh evidence can establish a component when goals align, never prove earlier unheard speech. Handbacks schedule the next four available Exit parts with zero additional minutes. If the goal does not align, keep pending; do not add questions. Day 83 has at most two missing 45-second asks plus transitions in Finish and revise. Remaining components carry to day 84 record reuse.

Day 79: F2 paper ten minutes, F1 eight, collect two. No conference. Days 80/81/82: ten assigned children daily, four distinct 45-second asks each, thirty minutes; five minutes allow transition, pauses and tidy inside Conferences and centers 35. Hear F2a, F2b, F1a, F1b separately. Observe object action within the same turn when needed, never an extra station. Record all quantity/sign/equality components separately; a 45-second limit is a planning allowance, not a child timer. Incomplete explanations stay pending, never rushed.

Thirty teacher minutes after each window day annotate feedback. Days 80/81/82 Feedback and revision five minutes, day 83 Finish and revise five of ten, return preceding feedback. Score only earlier-made work after written feedback and revision. First-made or first-observed day 83 evidence stays pending until later feedback/revision. Day 83 Interpret existing records: F1 four teacher minutes, F2 six. Independent feedback application is unscored. Days 84/85 reuse secure records without another interview or score.
