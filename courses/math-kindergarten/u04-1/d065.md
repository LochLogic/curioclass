# Day 65: I can combine pictures.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare student pages, practice-65.md, supplied routes and correction records, large squares, frames, thirty covered exit-65.md half slips, record.md and day 64 feedback. Bring unresolved unit 3 records from day 63. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Adult cuts; count pieces in/out, no mouthing; substitute drawn marks if unsafe.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review count, day 1; total, day 19; 10-frame, day 26. **Ask.** [[s04-R65-count]] Then **Ask.** [[s04-R65-start]] Children speak or gesture simultaneously. For day 64 s04-R64-frame/s04-R64-spread needs, **Ask.** [[s04-R65-retry]] Use six squares in one row, matching marks and optional frame. Unscored, no new readiness judgment; no individual round.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 64's exit and feedback. If day 64's tickets showed first-group exclusion, error B, **Ask.** [[s04-F65-B]] Then [[steps:s04-F65-B]]. Five minutes guided correction; five fresh two-and-two retry. If more than ten show the same error, repair together; otherwise one teacher group while peers restore familiar records. Classify B only from exclusion, not a wrong total alone. All get feedback; no extra letter group.

#### 10 min. Pictures and whole

Review put together, add and altogether, day 64. **Ask.** [[s04-W65]] Then [[steps:s04-W65]]. Objects become pictures without losing members. Show correct counting before the supplied omission, error A: four marks versus five, with a visible skipped square. The enclosing loop is supplied; both groups belong. Only one volunteer, 45 seconds within the block. No separate animal groups or blank total panels in practice.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; keep track and Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. Fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; partners restore individual s04-W65 records. Next **Ask.** [[s04-P65-route]] Teacher supplies the path through three and three scattered squares. Touch and tick each once, then pairs follow the same route; no route invention. This teaches the supplied-route routine before tomorrow's rehearsal. Repeat familiar corrections with immediate feedback. Keep route practice unscored in folders; collect W65 for feedback.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. First ten minutes continue day 63's unit 3 handoff: five-minute priority turned-square repair from day 64, then five-minute fresh transfer. **Ask.** [[s04-P65-shape]] Hear roster 25 to 30, six times 45 seconds, thirty seconds transition. Others correct familiar supplied square records. Return unresolved day 62 s03-X62 components and day 63 records; revise first-made work before judgment, leave missing observations pending. No extra interview. Last two minutes tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-65.md at each child's own turn. Three fox pictures and six owl pictures remain separate; supply no intermediate totals or enclosing loop. **Ask.** [[s04-X65]] Exact stem read aloud; everyone dots the whole on the half slip. Hear roster 7 to 12 only, six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent paper marking and collection get 3.5. Quiet waiting, no public answers.

After class allow thirty times twenty seconds = ten minutes to mark paper, plus thirty times ten seconds = five minutes for W65 feedback. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; names heard/support recorded. Missing speech stays pending. Later roster cases may establish aligned earlier components without an added ask. Return exit/W65 day 66 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters; large paper squares replace counters, joined drawn frames replace printing. One shared screen may enlarge teaching, no device required. Offer raised outlines, broad pencils and child-directed adult movement/scribing; never remove children.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw masters and cut large paper squares instead of counters; drawn frames suffice.

**Skip.** Class rotations, repeated centers and extra interviews.

- **5 min. Count.** [[s04-R65-retry]] Match marks to six squares; count together to ten.
- **7 min. Return.** Give yesterday's feedback. Correct supplied first-group omissions with adult-guided squares.
- **10 min. Pictures.** [[s04-W65]] Match each square to one dot; restore the fifth.
  **Say.** "Restore the missing count mark." Expect: five dots, both groups included.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X65]] First show reserved animal pictures. Read the stem; child dots the whole alone. Keep paper and words; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated pair rounds, preserving both repair slots and exit. Longer: trace each square-to-dot match on the same supplied record, within ten.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; side/corner, day 48; numeral, day 30. Review total: whole count, and altogether: both groups. No independent reading. Usual communication aids and public passing allowed; record access separately from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 66 returns day 65's exit and s04-W65. Visible skipping suggests error A: move each square once, then fresh supplied-route retry. First-group exclusion suggests error B; choose one priority only. Tomorrow rehearses scattered tracking already taught today, no new strategy.
