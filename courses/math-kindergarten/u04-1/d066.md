# Day 66: I can keep track.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and today's generated answer key private. Prepare student pages from practice-66.md, three-and-four supplied route cards, large squares, frames, thirty covered exit-66.md half slips and record.md. Have day 65 exit/s04-W65 feedback ready. Adult cuts pieces, counts in/out and prohibits mouthing; replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review count, day 1; total, day 19; keep track, day 12. **Ask.** [[s04-R66-count]] Then **Ask.** [[s04-R66-start]] Children speak or gesture simultaneously. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: touch one familiar square and match one mark. No individual round, timed recall or new count range.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 65's exit and s04-W65 with feedback. If day 65's tickets showed skipping, error A, **Ask.** [[s04-F66-A]] Then [[steps:s04-F66-A]]. Five minutes guided boundary movement; five fresh supplied-route retry. If many, more than ten, share it, repair together; otherwise one teacher group while others correct familiar partial records. First-group exclusion, error B, takes priority instead only when observed; enclose both square groups. Never rehearse the animal exit. No additive repair groups.

#### 10 min. Tracking rehearsal

Review Follow supplied touch route before independent route, day 65. **Ask.** [[s04-P66]] Use practice-66.md: three and three scattered squares, five supplied ticks. Follow the adult's existing line; restore its one missing visit. Count six, one touch and one tick per square. Model only the familiar route action; teach nothing new. One volunteer, 45 seconds. Review 10-frame and five-and-some, day 26; place the same six beside the picture as five then one if helpful. No operation signs.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; reset/repeat. One follows the supplied route, partner points to matches without naming a total first. **Ask.** [[s04-P66-mix]] Alternate familiar six-visit and seven-visit corrections.

Check: one visit per square, none missing or counted twice.

Every child returns a corrected paper record to their own folder. Immediate feedback; no speed score, new strategy or self-invented practice route.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. Ten minutes repeat these familiar route corrections or move the same square collections once across a supplied boundary. Children keep count marks with their records. Teacher observes one group, no second interview. Both groups remain in the whole. Last two minutes tidy and count pieces back in.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-66.md at each child's own turn: one boot and seven scattered boots, no supplied route. **Ask.** [[s04-X66]] Read exact stem aloud. Everyone marks their chosen tracking on the half slip. Hear roster 13 to 18 only, six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent paper marking and collection get 3.5. Quiet work while waiting; no public counts or printed keys.

Return pending day 64 s04-X64 feedback to today's roster six. This same turn may establish a fresh counted-whole component; unobserved independent movement stays pending. No extra ask or repeated check card.

After class, thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher minutes to mark. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard names and help separately. Unheard speech stays pending. Today's fresh case may establish an aligned earlier missing component without another ask; it never proves earlier speech. Return exit paper/feedback day 67 Return and repair. Unscored practice stays in folders.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy original paths and exit masters. Large paper squares replace counters; drawn joined 5-frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Broad pencils, raised boundaries and child-directed adult movement/scribing support access. Never remove children.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw routes and cut large squares instead of counters; draw joined frames if needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **5 min. Count.** Count one to ten together, then five to ten.
- **7 min. Return.** Give day 65 feedback; move five squares once across a drawn boundary.
- **10 min. Rehearse.** [[s04-P66]] [[s04-P66-mix]] Follow supplied paths; repair omitted visits.
  **Say.** "Repair this supplied counting route." Expect: six visits, each square once; the other record has seven.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X66]] First show reserved boot pictures with no route. Read stem; child marks alone. Keep paper and words; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated pair rounds; retain repair, rehearsal and exit. Longer: follow the same supplied route again and check each visit, within ten.

### Words and supports

Review put together, add and altogether, day 64; square, day 45. Read all print aloud at 18 point, no independent reading. Counts may use speech, signs or usual communication aid. Passing public talk is allowed; record access separately from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 67 returns day 66's exit. A visibly untouched boot suggests error A; move every square once and retry a fresh supplied layout, not the boot card. Then show joining through actions and a supplied sound record; explicitly teach no arrival and one arrival.
