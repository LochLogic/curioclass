# Day 105: I can keep track.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and unit 5 key-teacher.md private. Set twelve/fourteen movable squares and supplied routes. Select roster 7-12 for the exit. Sort day 104 feedback and day 103 revised unit 5 records; assign just one matching response A-F per child. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K105]], then [[steps:s06-K105]]. Everyone echoes and marks beats, one short run per fresh paper line. No individual round or written numbers beyond twenty. Hear words before recording sequence evidence.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 104's exit tickets and written task packet, including practice, counting and game paper with written feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten of thirty, show error A, move counted squares to marked places. **Ask.** [[s06-F105-A]], then reveal [[steps:s06-F105-A]]. One five-minute action includes the fresh partial-route retry; use the other five for a remaining tracking need, otherwise familiar review. Further needs stay scheduled, not squeezed into oral turns.

#### 7 min. Connected work

Review day 104 tracking with twelve/fourteen movable squares. **Ask.** [[s06-M105-1]], then [[steps:s06-M105-1]]. **Ask.** [[s06-Q105-1]] before [[steps:s06-Q105-1]]. After a secure correct count, contrast omission with the twelve-square error A route, then circle repetition: **Ask.** [[s06-P105]], then [[steps:s06-P105]]. Thirteen visits to twelve squares repeats the start, error B. Practice corrects a supplied route; no blank circular sticker record. Partners each take 45 seconds, simultaneously across fifteen pairs.

#### 30 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. First ten minutes fulfill day 103's handoff: assign only the first unmet unit 5 component. A: [[s06-U5-105-A]]; B: [[s06-U5-105-B]]; C: [[s06-U5-105-C]]; D: [[s06-U5-105-D]]; E: [[s06-U5-105-E]]; F: [[s06-U5-105-F]]. Read only each child's assigned single stem from its response master. In D/E use the occupied left frame; cover the unused right frame. These letters belong to unit 5. Children mark independently; keep new transfers unscored beside the earlier product revised day 104. After class, rejudge only that earlier revised component if its look-fors are met. Other needs stay pending.

Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read [[s06-G105-1]], [[s06-G105-2]], [[s06-G105-3]], [[s06-G105-4]] separately. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone acts; children mark four game-record spaces. Partner touch-check after each.

Fifteen minutes including tidy rehearse the supplied line/row/circle routes with movable squares. Teacher observes at most six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes; others work simultaneously. Children hold models/paper; teacher records help. Collect practice, count, games and assigned transfer for day 106 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-105 only at the child's own turn. Read [[s06-X105]] exactly. All mark paper or direct adult marks. Budget: launch 1, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Keep the six copies covered until their turn; no extra ask. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names/help. After class allow 30 times 20 seconds, ten teacher minutes for exit marks. Unheard speech stays pending; do not infer a route from the total alone.

### Other rooms

No device needed. Copy original practice/response/exit pictures without keys if no printer. Paper squares, drawn mats/frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw mats, frames and numeral cards; use large paper squares. Copy the supplied practice and reserved exit picture.

**Skip.** Group rotations, public turns and extra response forms.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and starts from day 104; mark beats.
- **6 min. Rehearse.** Follow supplied routes through twelve/fourteen movable squares.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P105]] Child repairs the supplied repeat.
  **Say.** "Repair the repeated visit." Expect: cross out the second start visit; twelve visits remain.
- **5 min. Repair.** Use only the assigned unit 5 response, after yesterday's revision. Keep it beside the earlier product, unscored.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X105]] Read the exact stem. Child marks independently; adult hears one count and gives later picture feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions, preserving response, feedback and exit. Longer: repeat a taught route with the same movable collection.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; counted/uncounted, day 12; row/circle, day 25; numeral, day 30. Unit 5 response reviews part/whole, day 87, partner of ten, day 97, and equation/plus/equals, day 78. Pointing, tactile pictures, home-language speech, signs and directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 106 returns day 105 exit/practice/count/game and unit 5 transfer paper in Return and repair. Error B needs a flagged start and stop; error A needs counted squares moved to places, each with a fresh partial retry. Maximum two five-minute actions. Then count the same movable set from another end and rehearse supplied starts taught day 104.
