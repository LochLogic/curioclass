# Day 107: I can explain why moving keeps the count.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare twelve movable squares in two rows of six, twenty in four rows of five, supplied labels and repair routes. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Select roster 19-24; keep exit copies covered. Bring day 106 annotated two-panel exits and practice/count/game paper.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K107]], then [[steps:s06-K107]]. All echo and mark beats on fresh paper lines; no individual round. No written numerals beyond twenty. Marks do not prove unheard words.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 106 exit, practice, count, game and repair paper with written feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten, show error A, move counted squares to marked places: **Ask.** [[s06-F107-A]], then [[steps:s06-F107-A]]. Error H: echo supplied start before continuing: **Ask.** [[s06-F107-H]], then [[steps:s06-F107-H]]. If count-out work instead showed error C, say target/move the completed subset aside: **Ask.** [[s06-F107-C]], then [[steps:s06-F107-C]]. Select at most two five-minute actions, each including a fresh partial retry. Otherwise familiar review. Other needs stay pending; unheard sequences keep their assigned conference roster.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review same, day 20, and Quantity with a retained numeral across arrangements, day 31. Extend to twelve movable squares: **Ask.** [[s06-M107-1]], then [[steps:s06-M107-1]]. Show each square returning to its former place; none enters or leaves. The spaces changed, not membership. Hear at most two 45-second volunteer observations; other children point. Do not require independent causal picture explanations in practice.

After the correct demonstration, show the spread twelve with supplied 14, error I. **Ask.** [[s06-P107]], then [[steps:s06-P107]]. Children correct only the supplied label. Recounting remains acceptable. Finally teach writing twenty: **Ask.** [[s06-M107-2]], then [[steps:s06-M107-2]]. Objects first, 20 beside the four rows, then copy in the extra blank box; distinguish supported formation from independent evidence.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read [[s06-G107-1]], [[s06-G107-2]], [[s06-G107-3]], [[s06-G107-4]] separately. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone adds/removes squares. Children mark four game-record spaces; partners touch-check after each.

Twenty minutes including tidy: alternate moving twelve squares, checking supplied labels and copying 20 beside the counted four rows. Keep paired frames/numeral cards available as references. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second turns concurrently in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes; children hold models/paper, teacher records help. Collect all practice, counting, games and repair marks for day 108 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-107 only at each child's own turn. Read [[s06-X107]] exactly. All mark paper with their own reason drawing or directed adult marks; six may dictate the reason onto their slip. No supplied answer choices. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Keep the six copies covered until heard. Record names/help; no extra ask. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Paper must show a reason; tracing printed links alone does not prove one. Unheard words stay pending. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes to mark reasons.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy original pictures without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn mats/frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares, drawn mats/frames and handwritten cards. Copy the supported practice and reserved exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and taught starts; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** Spread twelve squares and return each to its place. Model 20 beside four rows of five.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P107]] Use the supplied wrong label.
  **Say.** "Correct this label after the move." Expect: twelve remains twelve; only spaces changed. Child corrects 14.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's picture note, preserving initial marks.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X107]] Read the exact stem. Child draws a reason independently or dictates while pointing; record their words without supplying a reason. Give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; keep repair, causal model, numeral formation and exit. Longer: another taught movable arrangement with the same squares.

### Words and supports

Review numeral, day 30; count, day 1; counted/uncounted, day 12; row/circle, day 25. Tactile outlines, broad pencils, signs, home-language speech, pointing and directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 108 returns day 107 exit/practice/count/game and repair paper in Return and repair. Error I, more because wider: match moved squares to former places, then retry a fresh supplied partial label; at most two five-minute actions. Day 108 counts arranged teen pictures and separates ten individual ones from the rest. Keep every one visible.
