# Day 106: I can count in another order.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare twelve/fourteen movable squares, supplied equal labels and partial routes. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Select roster 13-18: three for each exit panel. Copy only the leaf panel and its two answer spots from exit-106.md onto the half slip. Keep the student Exit box covered until the exit slot. Bring day 105 annotated paper and assigned unit 5 transfer feedback.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K106]], then [[steps:s06-K106]]. All echo/mark beats, starting a fresh paper line per run; no thirty-child round. Written numbers stay within twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 105's exit tickets and written task packet, including practice, count, games and assigned unit 5 transfer with written feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten, show error B, flag the start and stop before its repeat: **Ask.** [[s06-F106-B]], then [[steps:s06-F106-B]]. If many show error A, move counted squares to places: **Ask.** [[s06-F106-A]], then [[steps:s06-F106-A]]. Each selected action includes its fresh partial retry in five minutes; at most two. Otherwise familiar review. Unit 5 transfer stays unscored evidence of change; its packet return adds no interview or repair minutes.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review day 104 counting from either end: **Ask.** [[s06-M106-1]], then [[steps:s06-M106-1]]. Supplied totals remain beside movable squares. Children check the teacher's visits, not generate paired complete routes. Review supplied start: **Ask.** [[s06-F106-H]], then [[steps:s06-F106-H]]. Contrast restarting at one, error H, after the correct model.

Review stopping at a requested count: **Ask.** [[s06-P106]] on practice-106. Its supplied enclosure wrongly selects all fourteen for target twelve, error C. Reveal [[steps:s06-P106]] after children amend it: move two outside or cross them out of the selection, leaving twelve selected and two outside. Teacher states the target before partners take two 45-second handler/checker turns, simultaneously. No new method and no independent start strip.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read [[s06-G106-1]], [[s06-G106-2]], [[s06-G106-3]], [[s06-G106-4]] separately. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone acts; each child marks four game-record spaces. Partners touch-check after each.

Twenty minutes including tidy: alternate taught movable-square counts and supplied partial-record corrections. Keep paired frames/numeral cards available as references. No independently completed reserved formats. Teacher hears at most six 45-second practice turns, 4.5 minutes; fifteen pairs work concurrently. Children hold models/paper; teacher records support. Collect practice, counting, games and repair marks for day 107.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the exit-106 leaf half slip and student page Exit box at each child's own turn. Read [[s06-X106a]], then [[s06-X106b]], separately and exactly. Collect both: the leaf half slip and the student Exit box. Distinguish leaf passes by above/below ticks as the master shows. Launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, three private 45-second samples per item 4.5, collect 0.5. The six keep copies covered until heard; each hears one ask only. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record names/help separately from formation. Five marks cannot prove unheard words. Route unheard sequence evidence to the assigned days 115-118 conference roster; use fresh domain evidence there. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds for marking the half slip and Exit box, fifteen teacher minutes.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching; all work has paper. Copy original masters if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn mats/frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares, drawn mats/frames and handwritten cards. Copy the supported practice and reserved exit masters.

**Skip.** Class rotations and extra repetitions.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and taught starts; mark beats.
- **6 min. Rehearse.** Move twelve squares from each end beside supplied 12 labels.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P106]] Use the supplied enclosure.
  **Say.** "Check this count-out record." Expect: twelve selected, two outside; selecting all fourteen overshoots.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's picture feedback and revise one partial route. Keep original marks.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X106a]] [[s06-X106b]] Read the two stems separately. Child marks the leaf half slip and student Exit box independently; hear both counts without supplying words. Give picture feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated centers, preserving feedback, contrasts and the half slip and Exit box. Longer: another taught route through the same movable collection.

### Words and supports

Review same, day 20; numeral, day 30; counted/uncounted, day 12; row/circle, day 25. Supplied start and stopping rehearsal are review of day 104. Tactile pictures, broad pencils, pointing, signing, home-language speech and directed adult marks preserve each ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 107 returns day 106 exit/practice/count/game and repair paper. Error A: move counted squares to places; error H: echo the supplied first word, then continue. If center work showed error C, say the target and move the completed subset aside. Choose at most two five-minute actions with fresh partial retries in Return and repair; keep other needs pending. Then move twelve squares without changing their membership and connect twenty objects to 20.
