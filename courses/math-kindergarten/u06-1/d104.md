# Day 104: I can count bigger groups.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and unit 5 key-teacher.md private. Prepare twelve and fourteen movable squares, plus a row growing to twenty. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Select roster 1-6 for the exit; keep its picture covered. Pair day 103 written notes with prior products.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review count, day 1, and Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97. Whole class: **Ask.** [[s06-R104-1]], then [[s06-R104-2]], separately. Everyone points/makes quantity marks; reveal steps afterward. Review counted/uncounted, day 12. If tracking slips, move counted squares across the mat boundary; no new readiness score.

#### 8 min. Hook

Twelve movable squares beside the full frame. **Ask.** [[s06-Q104-1]] before [[steps:s06-Q104-1]]; then [[s06-Q104-2]], followed by [[steps:s06-Q104-2]]. Hear at most two volunteers, 45 seconds each; others point/draw a tracking idea. Public speech may be passed. Say, "Invented for practice."

#### 5 min. Counting

Teach Oral tens to one hundred by echo: [[s06-K104]], then [[steps:s06-K104]]. Say one word, children echo and mark a beat; start a fresh paper line each run. Model six/seven/eight, then eight/nine/ten, retaining the supplied first word. All act simultaneously. No written numerals above twenty; marks cannot prove unheard words.

#### 7 min. Model

Review row/circle and Start and completed-visit marks for fixed pictures, day 25, using movable squares. **Ask.** [[s06-M104-1]], then [[steps:s06-M104-1]]. **Ask.** [[s06-M104-2]], then [[steps:s06-M104-2]]. **Ask.** [[s06-M104-3]], then [[steps:s06-M104-3]]. Demonstrate before children imitate. Last, **Ask.** [[s06-M104-4]], then [[steps:s06-M104-4]]: growing row and supplied labels. This introduces arranged counts through twenty and supplies both orders before fluency rehearsal.

#### 2 min. Connected work

Pairs, practice-104 picture: **Ask.** [[s06-P104]]. Check by touching supplied visits; reveal [[steps:s06-P104]] afterward. Repair one missing visit, not a fresh complete picture-to-total task.

#### 25 min. Counting centers and unit 5 response

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1; Return and repair, day 2. Return day 103's assessment and written task packet, including games and counting records. First ten minutes receive day 103 feedback: revise the first unmet unit 5 component on its retained product, preserving original marks and secure evidence. Unit 5 errors A/B/C/D/E/F use the matching private key repair; no thirty-child interviews. Return day 103 game/count packets here too. Others follow supplied square routes.

Five untimed game minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Read [[s06-G104-1]], [[s06-G104-2]], [[s06-G104-3]], [[s06-G104-4]] separately; Rin alone adds/removes squares, invented for practice. Each child marks one game-record space per answer. Partners alternate handler/checker and touch-check immediately.

Ten minutes including tidy: repeat taught square moves or partial-route repair. Fifteen simultaneous pairs exchange two 45-second turns in 1.5 minutes. Children hold paper/models; teacher records support. Collect practice, count and game paper for day 105 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-104 at each child's own turn. Read [[s06-X104]] exactly; everyone marks paper or directs an adult mark. Launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Hear only today's six; keep their copies covered until heard. No extra ask. Record names/help. Mark after class: 30 children at 20 seconds, 10 teacher minutes. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Unheard speech stays pending; formation is separate.

### Other rooms

Copy original pictures without answers if no printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Draw mats/frames/cards; adult-cut squares replace counters. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw frames, mats and cards; use large paper squares. Copy supplied practice and sealed exit pictures if no printer.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public turns.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and the two supplied starts; child marks beats.
- **6 min. Show.** Move twelve squares across a line, rows and another starting end; flag fourteen around a circle. Select twelve from fourteen.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P104]] Use the supplied partial route.
  **Say.** "Finish this route." Expect: one missing visit repaired, twelve counted once.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's unit 5 product from its picture note; keep original marks.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X104]] Read the stem aloud. Child marks alone; hear the count, then add a picture note afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; keep models, unit 5 revision and exit. Longer: same movable squares with another taught tracking route.

### Words and supports

Review numeral, day 30; Paper count-dot record, day 1. Supplied start means begin with the number said. Tactile outlines, broad pencils, pointing, signing, home-language speech or directed adult marks preserve the ask. No required fingers.

### Tomorrow

Day 105 returns day 104 exit/practice/count/game paper in Return and repair. If many, more than ten, show error A by omission, move counted squares to places, then retry a fresh partial route. Circles and rows rehearse today's methods. Ten center minutes give one assigned unit 5 transfer from day 103's first gap; judge revised earlier products after day 105.
