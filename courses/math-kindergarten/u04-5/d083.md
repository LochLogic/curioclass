# Day 83: I can use feedback on my math.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private; generated answer key holds new practice steps. Prepare student pages, practice-83.md, counters, frames, mats, record.md, day 79 originals and one assigned card per child cut from teacher master transfer-83.md. Label card backs with roster names. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks. Never move children as objects. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading.

### The day

#### 10 min. Finish and revise

Review Feedback revision before closing component records, day 62. Return day 82 conference feedback for five revision minutes, retaining originals. The other five minutes hold prior completion, at most two missing 45-second asks and transitions. Never repeat secure interviews. First-made/first-heard evidence stays pending for later feedback/revision.

#### 10 min. Interpret existing records

Review Close retained observation records, day 18. Review day 79 originals, written feedback and dated revisions: F1 four teacher minutes, F2 six. Record eligible component judgments under [[s04-A83-F1]] and [[s04-A83-F2]]. These entries document retained work; children write nothing for them. Retain separate quantity/action/question/equality judgments: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Selections alone cannot establish unheard reasons. Children independently correct [[s04-P83]]; unscored paper stays in folders. Keep day 64 diagnostic separate.

#### 20 min. Independent feedback application

First ten minutes: return day 82 exit and recorded practice feedback, including s04-W82; children apply picture cues to retained familiar paper independently. Next ten: each child applies earlier F1/F2 feedback on one assigned full-size transfer-83.md card, selected for error A, B, C, D, E, F, G or H. All work concurrently. Read only assigned single stems: [[s04-T83-A]] [[s04-T83-B]] [[s04-T83-C]] [[s04-T83-D]] [[s04-T83-E]] [[s04-T83-F]] [[s04-T83-G]] [[s04-T83-H]]. Private key supplies exact scenes, look-fors and samples. No worked example, hints, reteaching or new score. Circulate for access; collect the assigned transfer cards at this block's close and retain support notes. Unheard reasons stay pending.

#### 20 min. Counting and closing

Review counting, day 1: five minutes, **Ask.** [[s04-K-count-83]] Then eight minutes familiar supplied correction: **Ask.** [[s04-P83]] Then [[steps:s04-P83]]. Five-minute synthesis: **Ask.** [[s04-K-close-83]] Two volunteers at most, 90 seconds. Two-minute tidy. Carry records to days 84/85 without recollection/rescoring. Reserve thirty teacher minutes after class to finish feedback/component records.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy original masters. Large paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printing, folded paper replaces mats/cards. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy only the assigned card; paper squares replace counters.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **5 min. Assessment record and revision.** Return day 82 conference feedback. Child revises retained paper; preserve original.
- **5 min. Interpret.** [[s04-A83-F1]] [[s04-A83-F2]] Review eligible earlier originals, feedback and revisions. First-seen work stays pending.
- **10 min. Feedback application.** Return exit/practice cues; child uses them on familiar paper, then marks one assigned transfer card independently. Keep unscored work.
- **10 min. Close.** [[s04-P83]] Familiar work, counting, reflection and tidy.
  **Say.** "What change did your feedback help you make?" Expect: a specific changed mark or group, including pointing. Carry pending components to day 84.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar closing repetitions, retain feedback/revision and existing-record interpretation. Longer: complete familiar unscored paper; no new check or reteaching.

### Words and supports

Review familiar count, keep track, group and mark words. Read every word aloud. Pointing, signs and child-directed scribing count. Separate access from mathematical help. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 84 reuses day 83's assessment records: existing F1/F2 originals, feedback, revisions and pending notes, plus unscored transfers. No secure interview repeated or record rescored. New day 83 evidence waits for later feedback/revision before judgment. Unresolved action, question and equality components remain pending. Course-level check samples earlier counting, comparing and turned shapes.
